# 牛肉 (ぎゅうにく) — beef

## Meaning

牛肉 means beef, the meat of cattle.

- 牛肉を買いました。 (ぎゅうにくを かいました。) — I bought beef.
- この料理には牛肉が入っています。 (この りょうりには ぎゅうにくが はいって います。) — This dish contains beef.

The word names an ingredient, not a particular cut or preparation.

## How the kanji combine

[牛](../kanji/牛.md), "cattle," identifies the source of [肉](../kanji/肉.md), "meat." It is a modifier-and-head compound: cattle meat.
Both readings are on: ギュウ + ニク → ぎゅうにく. Do not substitute the standalone native reading うし into this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- ビーフ is the English-derived word for beef; it can occur in food names as well.
- 豚肉 (ぶたにく) means pork, and 鶏肉 (とりにく) means chicken meat. These are alternative ingredients, not antonyms.
- There is no natural direct antonym of 牛肉.

## Distinctive meaning

牛肉 specifies which animal the meat comes from. [料理](料理.md) (りょうり) names cooking or a dish; a dish can contain 牛肉 without being named after it.
The word by itself says nothing about whether the beef is raw, cooked, expensive, or locally produced.
