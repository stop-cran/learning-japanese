# 雄勁 (ゆうけい) — vigorous style (art; prose)

## Meaning

雄勁 describes vigor or power, characteristically in calligraphy, painting, poetry or prose. It is a noun and a na-adjective: **雄勁な + noun**, or **雄勁です** as a polite predicate.

Digital Daijisen gives the phrase **雄勁な筆跡** (ゆうけいなひっせき), "vigorous handwriting." Here 筆跡 means handwriting, and な connects 雄勁 to that noun. The phrase praises forceful expression, not the physical strength of the writer's hand.

## How the kanji combine

雄 (ユウ) evokes imposing strength here; [勁](../kanji/勁.md) (ケイ) contributes strength. Their related meanings reinforce the idea of forceful expression. This is a meaning aid, not a historical reconstruction: the compound does not mean "male handwriting."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[力強い](力強い.md) (ちからづよい), "powerful; reassuring," overlaps in its powerful or vigorous sense, but is a broader i-adjective. Its separate reassurance sense is not the meaning taught by 雄勁.

[強い](強い.md) (つよい), "strong; powerful," is the ordinary broad adjective. 雄勁 is not a general substitute for it when describing a strong team, wind or resistance.

Weak or feeble expression gives a contextual contrast. There is no single opposite that works for every artistic description.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 雄勁 as descriptive vocabulary for a vigorous style, rather than as the everyday word for every kind of strength. Art and prose are characteristic contexts, not an absolute restriction on every dictionary use.

Use な before a noun, not the ending い of an i-adjective. Noun status alone does not make this a する verb. The word assesses force or vigor in the expression; it does not by itself establish the work's overall quality or the creator's personal character.
