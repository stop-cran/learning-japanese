# 名物 (めいぶつ) - local specialty; famous product

## Meaning

名物 is a specialty or product for which a place is known.
Food is a frequent example, but the word is not limited to food.

- この町の名物はうどんです。 (このまちのめいぶつはうどんです。) - This town's specialty is udon.
- 旅行先で名物を食べました。 (りょこうさきでめいぶつをたべました。) - I ate the local specialty at my travel destination.

Daijisen also records characteristic or well-known local things and people:
名物教師 (めいぶつきょうし) is a teacher well known as a distinctive figure,
not a teacher who is literally a product.

## How the kanji combine

[名](../kanji/名.md) contributes renown, and [物](../kanji/物.md) contributes a thing.
The modifier-plus-head compound uses メイ + ブツ.
Interpret the established word, rather than translating it as any "named thing."

## Synonyms and antonyms

特産品 (とくさんひん) is a specialty product of a region.
[土産](土産.md) (みやげ) and [お土産](お土産.md) (おみやげ) are souvenir gifts:
a local 名物 can be bought as a 土産, but it need not be a gift.
[特徴](特徴.md) (とくちょう) names a characteristic or feature more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

名物 presents something as notably associated with a place or community.
It does not mean every special item, nor does it guarantee exceptional quality.
The local-character use above is separately supported by Daijisen;
the card does not attempt to teach all historical or tea-ceremony uses.
