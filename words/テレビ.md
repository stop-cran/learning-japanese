# テレビ — television

## Meaning

テレビ means television: the appliance, the medium, or the programs being watched. It is a noun, and the surrounding verb helps identify which meaning is intended.

- 夜、テレビを見ます。 (よる、テレビをみます。) — I watch television at night.
- 部屋に小さいテレビがあります。 (へやにちいさいテレビがあります。) — There is a small television in the room.

## How the kanji combine

テレビ is a katakana abbreviation of テレビジョン. It has three morae, テ・レ・ビ, without a long-vowel mark. No kanji analysis is needed.

## Synonyms and antonyms

テレビ番組 (テレビばんぐみ) specifically means a television program. 画面 (がめん) is the screen or displayed image. ラジオ is radio, a related medium rather than an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

テレビを見る usually means watching the programming, not staring at the physical casing. テレビをつける and テレビを消す mean turning it on and off. The verb 消す here does not mean destroying or deleting the television.
