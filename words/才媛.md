# 才媛 (さいえん) — talented or accomplished woman

## Meaning

才媛 is a noun praising a woman's talent or learning. It identifies a person, not the talent itself.

- 彼女は若くして名を知られた才媛だった。 (かのじょはわかくしてなをしられたさいえんだった。) — She was an accomplished woman who became well known when young.
- その才媛は三か国語で詩を書いた。 (そのさいえんはさんかこくごでしをかいた。) — That talented woman wrote poetry in three languages.

## How the kanji combine

[才](../kanji/才.md) (さい) points to ability; [媛](../kanji/媛.md) (えん) refers to a woman. The second character does not mean the person is a princess here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[才能](才能.md) (さいのう) is talent: 才能がある (さいのうがある) can be said of anyone. [美人](美人.md) (びじん) praises appearance, not necessarily accomplishment.

## Distinctive meaning

If a profile calls someone a 才媛, look to the surrounding sentence for what she excels at. The word names neither a particular profession nor a noble rank.
