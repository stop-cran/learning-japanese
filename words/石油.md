# 石油 (せきゆ) - petroleum; oil

## Meaning

石油 is petroleum or oil in the energy-resource sense. It is a noun, used in expressions such as 石油を輸入する (せきゆをゆにゅうする), "import petroleum."

- この国は石油を輸入しています。 (このくにはせきゆをゆにゅうしています。) - This country imports petroleum.
- 石油の価格が上がりました。 (せきゆのかかくがあがりました。) - The price of oil rose.

JMdict also records a **colloquial** sense meaning kerosene or paraffin. In a home-heating context, 石油 can therefore refer to that fuel, not crude petroleum.

## How the kanji combine

[石](../kanji/石.md) contributes "stone; rock," and 油 contributes "oil." "Rock oil" is a useful component-based reminder of petroleum, not a description of cooking oil. The on readings are セキ + ユ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[油](油.md) (あぶら) is broader and can refer to oils used in cooking or lubrication. 灯油 (とうゆ) specifically names kerosene. [ガソリン](ガソリン.md) names gasoline, a different fuel; these terms should not be freely swapped.

## Distinctive meaning

The gloss "oil" depends on the context. A recipe calling for oil does not thereby call for 石油. Similarly, the dictionary's kerosene sense is register-qualified, not a reason to translate every discussion of petroleum markets as a discussion of household heating fuel.
