# ワンピース - dress; one-piece garment

## Meaning

ワンピース commonly names a dress whose upper and skirt portions form
one garment. It also describes a one-piece construction, as in swimwear.

- 夏用のワンピースを買いました。 (なつようのワンピースをかいました。) - I bought a summer dress.
- 彼女は青いワンピースを着ています。 (かのじょはあおいワンピースをきています。) - She is wearing a blue dress.

For the second use, ワンピースの水着 (ワンピースのみずぎ) is
"a one-piece swimsuit." The clothing context determines what is meant.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word comes from English "one-piece." It has no kanji components.
Keep the long ピー sound; the ordinary Japanese clothing word is not
a general substitute for every English phrase "one piece."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ドレス](ドレス.md) also means a dress or gown and overlaps with the dress use.
[スカート](スカート.md) is the lower-body garment alone.
[スーツ](スーツ.md) commonly has separate matching garments;
a dress and a skirt suit do not have the same construction.
[着る](着る.md) (きる) is the verb for putting on a dress.

## Distinctive meaning

The one-piece swimsuit use does not mean every swimsuit is a dress.
Conversely, an ordinary reference to buying a ワンピース need not mean
swimwear. The separately recorded manga/media name ワンピース is not
the clothing entry or a new sense derived from its examples.
