# 考え (かんがえ) - thought; idea; intention

## Meaning

考え is a noun for a thought, view, idea, or intention. **考えがある** means "have an idea/plan";
**考えを変える** (かんがえをかえる) means "change one's mind."
An intended action can be expressed with **dictionary-form verb + 考えだ**.

- いい考えが浮かびました。 (いいかんがえがうかびました。) - A good idea came to mind.
- 来年、留学する考えです。 (らいねん、りゅうがくするかんがえです。) - I intend to study abroad next year.

The first example introduces an idea. The second presents a current intention, not a prediction that the action is certain to happen.
私の考えでは (わたしのかんがえでは) introduces "in my view."

## How the kanji combine

[考](../kanji/考.md) supplies the idea of thinking. 考え is the noun related to [考える](考える.md) (かんがえる).
The final え distinguishes the noun form; do not attach する to it to mean "think."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意見](意見.md) (いけん) emphasizes an opinion about a matter.
[案](案.md) (あん) can mean a proposal or plan, and [つもり](つもり.md) overlaps with intention.
考え covers these areas without making every thought a developed proposal.

## Distinctive meaning

The construction guides the translation. 考えを聞く (かんがえをきく) asks for someone's views,
while 行く考えはない (いくかんがえはない) means "have no intention of going."
Learn the phrase around the noun rather than selecting one English gloss for every occurrence.
