# 政府 (せいふ) - government; administration

## Meaning

政府 is a government or administration: the institution that governs, rather than a particular office building. It is a noun.

- 政府は新しい計画を発表しました。 (せいふはあたらしいけいかくをはっぴょうしました。) - The government announced a new plan.
- 政府の説明を聞きました。 (せいふのせつめいをききました。) - I listened to the government's explanation.

Use **country name + 政府** in expressions such as 日本政府 (にほんせいふ), "the Japanese government." With another ordinary noun, の can express the relationship: 政府の方針 (せいふのほうしん), "government policy."

## How the kanji combine

[政](../kanji/政.md) contributes governing or political administration, and 府 contributes an administrative office or authority. Together they identify the governing institution. The on readings are セイ + フ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[政治](政治.md) (せいじ) concerns politics or governing as an activity. [国会](国会.md) (こっかい) is Japan's national legislature. These are related institutions and activities, not interchangeable names or antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

政府 can be the subject of an action even though individual officials carry it out, just as English says "the government announced." It does not mean every citizen of a country. When reading a news report, distinguish the administration's position from that of the legislature, a political party, or the public.
