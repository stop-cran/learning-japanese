# 適する (てきする) - suit; be suitable for

## Meaning

適する is an intransitive special する verb meaning "suit; be suitable for." Use **AはBに適している** to say that A is suited to B. B may be a purpose, activity, or person.

- この靴は山歩きに適しています。 (このくつはやまあるきにてきしています。) - These shoes are suitable for hiking.
- 初心者に適した本を探しています。 (しょしんしゃにてきしたほんをさがしています。) - I am looking for a book suitable for beginners.

適した + noun describes something suited to the stated need. The に marks what it suits; it is not an English-style direct object.

## How the kanji combine

[適](../kanji/適.md) supplies fitting or suitability, and する makes the verb. Learn the forms 適します (てきします), 適して (てきして), and 適した (てきした) as a set.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[合う](合う.md) (あう) also means "fit; suit," with a broad range of uses. A shoe can fit someone's foot in size yet not be 適している for hiking. These describe related but distinguishable kinds of fit.

## Distinctive meaning

適している evaluates suitability, not whether a person actually chooses or uses the item. The book in the second example remains suitable for beginners even before anyone buys it. For unsuitability, 適していない is the direct negative of that state; the evaluation still needs a purpose or standard supplied by context.
