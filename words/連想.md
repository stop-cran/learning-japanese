# 連想 (れんそう) - association of ideas

## Meaning

連想 is the association by which one thing brings another to mind. It is a noun and a transitive する-verb. In AからBを連想する, A is the trigger and B is the idea it evokes.

- 「夏」という言葉から海を連想します。 (「なつ」ということばからうみをれんそうします。) - The word "summer" makes me think of the sea.
- この色を見ると、春を連想します。 (このいろをみると、はるをれんそうします。) - When I see this color, I think of spring.

The speaker is doing the associating. The thing recalled takes を even though English may phrase it as "reminds me of."

## How the kanji combine

[連](../kanji/連.md) contributes linking or connecting, and [想](../kanji/想.md) contributes thought or an image in the mind. Together they concern connected ideas. Their on readings are レン + ソウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[想像](想像.md) (そうぞう) means imagining; it need not begin from an associative trigger. [思い出す](思い出す.md) (おもいだす) means remembering something previously known or experienced. An association may evoke a memory, but it can also evoke a general idea.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "suggestion" here means something being suggested to the mind, not giving someone advice. Likewise, "association" does not mean a club or organization. Different people can associate the same word with different images; 連想 names the mental connection, not an objective equivalence.
