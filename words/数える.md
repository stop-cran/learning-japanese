# 数える (かぞえる) - count

## Meaning

数える means to count or enumerate. It is a transitive ichidan verb: put を after
what you count. The polite form is 数えます (かぞえます), the past 数えた, and
the negative 数えない.

- 箱の中のりんごを数えました。 (はこのなかのりんごをかぞえました。) - I counted the apples in the box.
- 一から十まで数えてください。 (いちからじゅうまでかぞえてください。) - Please count from one to ten.

In the second example, から marks the starting number and まで the endpoint.
You can count aloud or silently; the verb itself does not specify which.

## How the kanji combine

[数](../kanji/数.md) concerns number or quantity. In this native verb, 数 is read
かぞ and える is written in kana. Compare the noun [数](数.md) (かず), "number;
quantity": its reading is not the verb's stem.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計算](計算.md) (けいさん)する means to calculate. Counting apples and calculating
their total price are different activities, although both use numbers.
数え直す (かぞえなおす) means to count again.

## Distinctive meaning

数える focuses on determining a number by counting items or proceeding through a
sequence. It is not a general translation of English "count on" meaning "rely on."
For counted objects, the appropriate counter still matters: counting people does
not remove the need for 人 when reporting how many people there are.
