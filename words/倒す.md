# 倒す (たおす) - knock down; defeat

## Meaning

倒す means to knock something down, put it on its side, or defeat an opponent.
It is a transitive godan verb: use を for what is brought down. The polite form
is 倒します (たおします), the past 倒した, and the negative 倒さない.

- うっかりコップを倒してしまいました。 (うっかりコップをたおしてしまいました。) - I accidentally knocked the glass over.
- 決勝で強い相手を倒しました。 (けっしょうでつよいあいてをたおしました。) - We defeated a strong opponent in the final.

The first example is physical; the second concerns winning a contest.
椅子の背を倒す (いすのせをたおす) means reclining a chair's back.

## How the kanji combine

[倒](../kanji/倒.md) concerns falling or bringing down. It is read たお in this
native verb, followed by す. There is no compound of separate kanji meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[倒れる](倒れる.md) (たおれる) is the intransitive partner: コップが倒れた,
"the glass fell over." Compare コップを倒した, "someone knocked the glass over."
負かす (まかす) also means defeating someone in a contest.

## Distinctive meaning

倒す does not require intentional action: the glass example is accidental.
Nor does defeating an opponent automatically mean physically knocking them
down. Keep the object and context with the verb, and do not replace it with
倒れる while leaving the object marked を.
