# 布 (ぬの) - cloth; fabric

## Meaning

布 is the noun "cloth" or "fabric." It can name textile material or a piece of cloth. Use 布で to identify a material or a cloth used as a tool, depending on the action.

- 柔らかい布で机を拭きました。 (やわらかいぬのでつくえをふきました。) - I wiped the desk with a soft cloth.
- この袋は丈夫な布でできています。 (このふくろはじょうぶなぬのでできています。) - This bag is made of strong fabric.

In the first example, the cloth is the wiping tool. In the second, it is the material from which the bag is made.

## How the kanji combine

布 is a single kanji read with the native reading ぬの here. It writes the whole noun; there is no compound to split or inflecting ending to remove.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[生地](生地.md) (きじ) overlaps when discussing fabric, particularly material used for making something. [紙](紙.md) (かみ), "paper," is another sheet-like material, not a synonym. [革](革.md) (かわ), "leather," also identifies a different material.

## Distinctive meaning

布 does not specify whether the fiber is cotton, wool, or synthetic. Nor does it name a finished garment merely because that garment is made of cloth. Add a modifier when the material or condition matters: 濡れた布 (ぬれたぬの), "a wet cloth." This page teaches the ぬの cloth sense, not senses restricted to other readings.
