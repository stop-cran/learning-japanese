# ステージ (ステージ) - stage; performance

## Meaning

ステージ names a stage or platform for a performance, and also the performance
or show itself. It can further name a phase of progress, or a stage/level
in a video game.

- 歌手がステージに立ちました。 (かしゅがステージにたちました。) - The singer stood on the stage.
- 来月、彼らのステージを見に行きます。 (らいげつ、かれらのステージをみにいきます。) - Next month, I am going to see their show.

The first names a place; the second names what the audience watches.
新しいステージに進む (あたらしいステージにすすむ) can describe moving to
a new phase. A game's 次のステージ (つぎのステージ) is its next stage or level.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword with no kanji components. The ー lengthens テ:
ステージ. Keep the voiced ジ at the end.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[舞台](舞台.md) (ぶたい) overlaps in the performance-stage and theatrical uses.
[公演](公演.md) (こうえん) is a public performance; [演奏](演奏.md)
(えんそう) specifically concerns musical performance.
段階 (だんかい) is useful for a phase of development.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every English "level" is a ステージ: [水準](水準.md) (すいじゅん)
can describe a standard of living or attainment. Nor does every ステージ
name a physical platform. Read the topic and the surrounding verb before
choosing between a place, an event, and a phase.
