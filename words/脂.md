# 脂 (あぶら) - fat; grease

## Meaning

脂 is a noun for fat or grease, including tallow or lard. It is useful
when discussing the fatty part of meat or fat in food.

- 肉の脂を少し取りました。
  (にくのあぶらをすこしとりました。) - I removed some of the fat from the meat.
- 冷めたスープの表面に脂が固まりました。
  (さめたスープのひょうめんにあぶらがかたまりました。) - Fat solidified on the surface of the cooled soup.

脂が多い肉 (あぶらがおおいにく) means meat with a lot of fat.
The word alone does not say how much fat there is or whether it is wanted.

## How the kanji combine

[脂](../kanji/脂.md) writes the complete native noun あぶら.
There is no multi-kanji reading to combine. The same character has the
on reading シ in 脂肪 (しぼう), "fat," and 樹脂 (じゅし), "resin."
Those compounds do not give this native noun every meaning of the character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[油](油.md) (あぶら) is the separate oil entry. Learn the spelling with
the substance and context; do not replace this distinction with an
absolute solid versus liquid or animal versus plant rule.

[石油](石油.md) (せきゆ) concerns petroleum and can also denote kerosene.
Its broad English oil label does not make it a normal Japanese word for
food fat. 脂肪 is another useful fat word, but it is read しぼう, not あぶら.

## Distinctive meaning

The fat in the second example becomes solid as the soup cools. That
example does not establish that everything called 脂 must always be solid.
Keep the whole lexical item separate from a broad character association,
and do not pronounce 樹脂 as though it were the standalone noun あぶら.
