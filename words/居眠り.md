# 居眠り (いねむり) - dozing; nodding off

## Meaning

居眠り is dozing or nodding off, especially while sitting. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb: **居眠りする**, "doze off." The person who dozes is the subject; で can mark the place.

- 電車で居眠りをしてしまいました。 (でんしゃでいねむりをしてしまいました。) - I accidentally dozed off on the train.
- 授業中に居眠りをしないでください。 (じゅぎょうちゅうにいねむりをしないでください。) - Please do not doze off during class.

The first example's てしまいました adds an unintended or regretted outcome. That nuance is not a separate meaning of 居眠り itself.

## How the kanji combine

[居](../kanji/居.md) contributes being or remaining in place; [眠](../kanji/眠.md) supplies sleep. The native elements い + ねむり form a word for dozing where one is, rather than an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[眠る](眠る.md) (ねむる) is the broader verb "sleep." [寝る](寝る.md) (ねる) can describe going to bed or lying down. 昼寝 (ひるね) is a daytime nap; it does not necessarily suggest nodding off while seated.

## Distinctive meaning

居眠り draws attention to a lapse into sleep during another setting or activity. It is not the general word for a full night's sleep. 居眠り運転 (いねむりうんてん), "drowsy driving; falling asleep at the wheel," names a dangerous situation, not a planned rest.
