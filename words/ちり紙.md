# ちり紙 (ちりがみ) - tissue paper; toilet paper

## Meaning

ちり紙 is paper used for wiping, such as tissue paper or toilet paper.
The dictionary also writes it チリ紙 and 塵紙, with the same reading ちりがみ.
Learn the hygiene use rather than assuming that any thin decorative paper is meant.

- 鼻をかむために、ちり紙を一枚取りました。
  (はなをかむために、ちりがみをいちまいとりました。) - I took a tissue to blow my nose.
- 母はかばんにちり紙を入れています。
  (はははかばんにちりがみをいれています。) - My mother keeps tissues in her bag.

Sheets can be counted with 枚 (まい).
The reading ちりし is also recorded; this card's primary reading is ちりがみ.

## How the kanji combine

[紙](../kanji/紙.md) means paper and appears as がみ, the voiced form of かみ.
In 塵紙, 塵 contributes dust or waste material.
The public dictionary describes coarse paper made from scraps, then the broader
use for nose-wiping or toilet paper. This is a lexical explanation, not a claim
that every modern tissue has that manufacturing process.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ティッシュペーパー, often shortened to ティッシュ, names tissue paper.
トイレットペーパー specifies toilet paper.
[紙](紙.md) (かみ) is paper more generally, including writing paper.
There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The source spelling 塵紙 does not mean that the paper is already dirty.
Distinguish its intended use from a manufacturing or cleanliness claim.
Nor does an English translation as "tissue" mean biological tissue.
