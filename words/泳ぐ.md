# 泳ぐ (およぐ) — swim

## Meaning

泳ぐ means to swim. It is an intransitive verb: the swimmer is the subject, and the verb describes
that swimmer's movement. Use で for the place where the swimming happens.

- 毎週、プールで泳ぎます。 (まいしゅう、プールでおよぎます。) — I swim in the pool every week.
- 魚が泳いでいます。 (さかながおよいでいます。) — A fish is swimming.

This is a godan verb, whose ending changes with its form: 泳ぎます (およぎます), 泳いで (およいで),
and 泳いだ (およいだ).

## How the kanji combine

There is one kanji, [泳](../kanji/泳.md), carrying the swimming meaning.
The native reading およ- combines with the written ending ぐ; the whole verb is およぐ.
The kana ending changes when the verb is conjugated.

## Synonyms and antonyms

水泳 (すいえい) names swimming as an activity and can become a verb with する.
[浮く](浮く.md) (うく), "float," is a contrast, not a synonym: a piece of wood can float without swimming.

## Distinctive meaning

泳ぐ describes swimming movement, not just being in water.
Use 泳いでいる when describing someone or something currently swimming; 水泳 is useful when naming the activity itself.
The form 泳いで is worth learning with the verb because the final ぐ changes to いで.
