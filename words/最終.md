# 最終 (さいしゅう) - final; last

## Meaning

最終 marks the final part or endpoint of a sequence. It is a noun that can modify another noun with の, and it appears directly in compounds such as 最終日 (さいしゅうび), "the final day," and 最終回 (さいしゅうかい), "the final episode."

- 明日は試験の最終日です。 (あしたはしけんのさいしゅうびです。) - Tomorrow is the final day of the examinations.
- 最終の電車に間に合いました。 (さいしゅうのでんしゃにまにあいました。) - I caught the last train.

When the transport service is understood, 最終 can itself mean the day's last train, bus, or flight: 最終に乗る (さいしゅうにのる), "take the last service."

## How the kanji combine

[最](../kanji/最.md) contributes the furthest or utmost position, and [終](../kanji/終.md) contributes ending. Together they identify the final point. The readings are サイ + シュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[最後](最後.md) (さいご) also means "last; end" and overlaps considerably. 最終 is useful in labels for a final stage, service, or decision. [最初](最初.md) (さいしょ), "first; beginning," is the corresponding contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

最終 does not mean "most recent." [最近](最近.md) (さいきん) concerns recent time; the latest episode available is not necessarily the 最終回. English "last week" likewise means the previous week, [先週](先週.md) (せんしゅう), not a week described with 最終.
