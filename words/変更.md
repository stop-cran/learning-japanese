# 変更 (へんこう) — change; revision

## Meaning

変更 is a change or revision to something specified, such as a plan or arrangement.
As a verb, 変更する takes the thing changed with を. Use に to introduce the replacement:
**AをBに変更する**, "change A to B."

- 会議の時間を三時に変更しました。 (かいぎのじかんをさんじにへんこうしました。) — We changed the meeting time to three o'clock.
- 予定の変更がありました。 (よていのへんこうがありました。) — There was a change of plans.

## How the kanji combine

[変](../kanji/変.md) contributes change, and [更](../kanji/更.md) contributes renewal or alteration.
These related meanings reinforce one another rather than naming an action and its object.
The on readings are ヘン + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[変化](変化.md) (へんか) also means "change," but 変化する describes something changing state.
Compare 予定を変更する (よていをへんこうする), "change the plan," with
状態が変化する (じょうたいがへんかする), "the state changes."
The を/が difference makes the roles clearer than translating both verbs simply as "change."

## Distinctive meaning

変更 is useful when an existing arrangement is revised: a new time can replace an old one.
It need not mean the event is canceled, and it does not say whether the new arrangement is better.
In 変更がある (へんこうがある), "there is a change," 変更 remains a noun rather than taking する.
