# 議長 (ぎちょう) - chairperson; assembly speaker

## Meaning

議長 is the person who chairs a meeting or presides over an assembly. Depending
on the institution, English may use "chair," "chairperson," or "speaker."
The Japanese noun does not specify the person's gender.

- 田中さんが会議の議長を務めます。 (たなかさんがかいぎのぎちょうをつとめます。) - Tanaka will chair the meeting.
- 議長は出席者に意見を求めました。 (ぎちょうはしゅっせきしゃにいけんをもとめました。) - The chair asked the attendees for their opinions.

議長を務める means to serve as chair; 議長になる means to become chair.

## How the kanji combine

[議](../kanji/議.md) contributes deliberation or discussion, and
[長](../kanji/長.md) contributes leader or head. It is a role compound: the
leader of the proceedings. The on readings are ギ + チョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) is the meeting, while [議会](議会.md) (ぎかい) is an
assembly or council. Neither names its presiding person. A participant is
not an antonym of 議長; the words identify different roles.

## Distinctive meaning

Being 議長 concerns presiding over the proceedings. It does not automatically
make someone the head of the entire organization. Translate the title using
the institution's context rather than treating English "chairman" as proof
of a male-only Japanese office.
