# 敬う (うやまう) - respect; revere

## Meaning

敬う is a transitive godan verb for respecting, honoring or revering someone.
The respected person takes を.

- 私は祖母を敬っています。 (わたしはそぼをうやまっています。) - I respect my grandmother.
- 多くの人がその医師を敬っています。 (おおくのひとがそのいしをうやまっています。) - Many people hold that doctor in esteem.

Learn 敬います (うやまいます), 敬って (うやまって) and 敬わない (うやまわない).
敬っている can describe an ongoing attitude, not just a single polite gesture.

## How the kanji combine

[敬](../kanji/敬.md) supplies the native stem うやま; う is okurigana.
The same character uses ケイ in [尊敬](尊敬.md) (そんけい);
that does not change the reading of this native verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

尊敬する overlaps closely in respect or esteem.
[敬意](敬意.md) (けいい) is a noun: 敬意を払う (けいいをはらう) means "pay respect."
[尊重](尊重.md) (そんちょう) particularly suits treating a person, opinion or right as worthy of consideration.
These English "respect" labels overlap, but the Japanese constructions differ.

## Distinctive meaning

Respect is not the same as obeying every instruction or agreeing with every opinion.
敬う names respect; it is not itself a special humble conjugation for one's own actions.
Whether an utterance uses respectful or humble language is a separate grammatical question
from whether the speaker personally holds someone in esteem.
