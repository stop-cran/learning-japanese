# いってまいります - I am off (formal departure greeting)

## Meaning

いってまいります announces that the speaker is going out, normally with
an expected return. It is a more formal, deferential counterpart of
いってきます, not a general farewell for every parting.

- それでは、いってまいります。 - Well then, I am off.
- 銀行へいってまいります。 (ぎんこうへいってまいります。) - I am off to the bank.

The person leaving says this. Someone seeing them off may reply
[いってらっしゃい](いってらっしゃい.md), "Take care."

## How the kanji combine

Dictionary spellings include 行ってまいります, 行って参ります, and
いって参ります. [行](../kanji/行.md)って is the て-form of
[行く](行く.md) (いく), and まいります is the polite form of
[参る](参る.md) (まいる), using [参](../kanji/参.md).
Learn the departure formula as a whole rather than translating each verb
independently.

## Synonyms and antonyms

いってきます is the everyday departure announcement. ただいま, "I am
back," belongs to the return; it is not a reply to a departing person.
いってらっしゃい is said from the other side of the exchange.

## Distinctive meaning

The formal wording presents the speaker's own departure deferentially;
it does not honor the departing speaker as a superior. English "see you
later" captures the situation, not a promise about an exact return time.
The short example without a destination still communicates going out.
