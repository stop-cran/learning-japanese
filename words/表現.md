# 表現 (ひょうげん) - expression; representation

## Meaning

表現 is expression or representation through language, art, movement, or another
medium. 表現する is transitive: use を for what is expressed and で for the
means of expression. As a noun, 表現 can also identify a particular wording.

- 自分の気持ちを言葉で表現しました。 (じぶんのきもちをことばでひょうげんしました。) - I expressed my feelings in words.
- この表現は少し分かりにくいです。 (このひょうげんはすこしわかりにくいです。) - This expression is a little difficult to understand.

The first example concerns an act of expressing; the second concerns the
expression that a reader or listener encounters.

## How the kanji combine

[表](../kanji/表.md) contributes showing or expressing, and [現](../kanji/現.md)
contributes appearing or making something manifest. Their meanings combine
into giving an idea a perceptible form. The readings are ヒョウ + ゲン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[言葉](言葉.md) (ことば) names words or language, one medium for 表現.
[文章](文章.md) (ぶんしょう) can name a passage of writing.
表現 is broader than either: music and gesture can express something without
forming a sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

An expression need not be emotional; a writer can express an opinion or describe
a situation. In mathematics, 表現 also has the technical sense "representation
of a group," as in 群の表現 (ぐんのひょうげん). That specialized sense does not
make every mathematical formula a 表現 in the same technical sense.
