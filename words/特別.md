# 特別 (とくべつ) — special, exceptional

## Meaning

特別 describes something set apart from the ordinary: special, exceptional, or receiving different treatment.
For a basic noun phrase, use 特別な + noun. 特別に can modify an action.

- 今日は特別な日です。 (きょうは とくべつな ひです。) — Today is a special day.
- このケーキは特別に作りました。 (この ケーキは とくべつに つくりました。) — I made this cake especially for the occasion.

## How the kanji combine

[特](../kanji/特.md) contributes "special," and [別](../kanji/別.md) contributes "separate, different."
The meanings reinforce the idea of something distinguished from the usual.
Both readings are on: トク + ベツ → とくべつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 特に (とくに), "especially," singles something out. It overlaps with some uses of 特別に but cannot replace 特別な before a noun.
- 特殊 (とくしゅ), "special, peculiar," emphasizes an unusual or particular kind; it need not suggest something personally meaningful.
- 普通 (ふつう), "ordinary," is a useful contrast: 普通の日 (ふつうのひ), an ordinary day, versus 特別な日.

## Distinctive meaning

特別 says that something differs from the ordinary in the relevant situation. It does not automatically mean expensive, excellent, or rare.
A simple day or gift can be 特別 because of its importance to a particular person.
