# 掻く (かく) - scratch; sweat

## Meaning

掻く is a transitive godan verb used for scratching and several related actions. These uses are usually written かく in kana.

- かゆい所をかかないでください。 (かゆいところをかかないでください。) - Please do not scratch the itchy spot.
- 暑くて汗をかきました。 (あつくてあせをかきました。) - It was hot, and I sweated.

In 汗をかく, [汗](汗.md) (あせ) means sweat; the phrase does not mean scratch the sweat. Other uses include 雪をかく (ゆきをかく), "shovel snow," and オールで水をかく (オールでみずをかく), "paddle the water with an oar."

## How the kanji combine

掻 is the written kanji of this single native verb, with く in kana. Learn the whole word and its object patterns rather than assigning every occurrence the single English translation "scratch."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぽりぽり](ぽりぽり.md) can depict light scratching, as well as crunching food; it describes a manner rather than naming every action of かく.

[書く](書く.md), also かく, means write. The same pronunciation and godan forms do not make it the same word as 掻く.

## Distinctive meaning

Forms include かきます, かかない, かいて and かいた. Keep the object with its expression: a scratched body part, sweat produced, snow shoveled or water paddled. English "scratch" does not justify using this verb for every scratch-like sound, mark or cancellation.
