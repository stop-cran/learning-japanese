# 具体 (ぐたい) - concreteness; embodiment

## Meaning

具体 is a **noun** meaning concreteness or embodiment: an idea's concrete form rather than abstraction alone. It is especially useful to recognize in the contrast 抽象と具体 (ちゅうしょうとぐたい), "abstraction and concreteness."

- この授業では抽象と具体の違いを学びます。 (このじゅぎょうではちゅうしょうとぐたいのちがいをまなびます。) - In this class, we study the difference between abstraction and concreteness.
- 具体的に説明してください。 (ぐたいてきにせつめいしてください。) - Please explain in concrete terms.

The second sentence uses **具体的に**, not bare 具体 as an adjective. With 的 (てき), the derived form gives 具体的な + noun and 具体的に + verb.

## How the kanji combine

[具](../kanji/具.md) contributes providing or being equipped with something, and [体](../kanji/体.md) contributes body or form. As a meaning aid, the combination suggests giving an idea a definite form. This is not a claim about the word's historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

抽象 (ちゅうしょう), "abstraction," is the dictionary's opposite. An [例](../kanji/例.md) (れい), "example," can make an abstract explanation concrete, but "example" and "concreteness" are not identical nouns.

## Distinctive meaning

English "concrete" can be an adjective, but that does not establish 具体な as the model Japanese construction. Learn the actual expansion 具体的な説明 (ぐたいてきなせつめい), "a concrete explanation." Here the intended meaning is specific and tangible in thought, not the building material concrete.
