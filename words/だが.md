# だが (だが) - but; however; nevertheless

## Meaning

だが is a conjunction meaning "but; however; nevertheless." It introduces a statement that contrasts with, limits, or goes against an expectation raised by the preceding statement.

- この方法は簡単だ。だが、時間がかかる。 (このほうほうはかんたんだ。だが、じかんがかかる。) - This method is simple. However, it takes time.
- 雨はやんだ。だが、まだ出発できない。 (あめはやんだ。だが、まだしゅっぱつできない。) - The rain has stopped. Nevertheless, we still cannot leave.

Both statements can be true. だが signals the contrast; it does not automatically deny the first statement.

## How the kanji combine

だが is written in kana. Its form contains だ and が, but here it functions as a sentence-linking expression. No kanji components or foreign-language origin are needed to explain it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しかし](しかし.md) also introduces a contrasting statement. [でも](でも.md) provides a conversational alternative in many situations. These expressions differ in tone and placement, rather than representing opposite meanings.

## Distinctive meaning

Sentence-initial だが can give prose or narration a firm, direct transition. It is not by itself a polite ending; surrounding sentences establish the style. The examples show complete statements followed by だが, not a rule for attaching だ to every verb. The meaning here is dictionary-supported; the community source row supplied no English gloss.
