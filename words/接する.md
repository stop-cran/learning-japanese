# 接する (せっする) - touch; border on; deal with

## Meaning

接する can mean touch or be next to something, border on a place, or deal with
people. It also covers encountering something or receiving news.
Learn the special-class する verb as a whole: 接します (せっします),
接して (せっして), 接しない (せっしない).

- この土地は道路に接しています。 (このとちはどうろにせっしています。) - This plot of land borders the road.
- 彼は誰にでも親切に接します。 (かれはだれにでもしんせつにせっします。) - He treats everyone kindly.
- その知らせに接して驚きました。 (そのしらせにせっしておどろきました。) - I was surprised on receiving that news.

These examples use に for the boundary, person dealt with, or news encountered.
The dictionary also records transitive bringing together, as in 二つの面を接する
(ふたつのめんをせっする), bringing two surfaces into contact, and tangency in geometry.

## How the kanji combine

[接](../kanji/接.md) contributes contact, connection or dealing with.
Its セツ reading appears as せっ before する; do not pronounce せつする.
This is not an invitation to detach a free noun 接 and then treat every use as
an ordinary noun + する construction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[触る](触る.md) (さわる) and [触れる](触れる.md) (ふれる) overlap in touching;
隣接 (りんせつ) concerns adjacency. [応対](応対.md) (おうたい)
and [応接](応接.md) (おうせつ) overlap in dealing with or receiving people.
[接近](接近.md) (せっきん), approaching, need not reach contact.

## Distinctive meaning

The object and construction matter: a road boundary, someone you treat kindly,
and news you receive are not all literal touching events.
Likewise, not every sense of the overlapping English words receive or meet
can be imported into 接する. Read the whole phrase before selecting the translation.
