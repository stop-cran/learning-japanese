# 効く (きく) - take effect; be effective

## Meaning

効く means to have an effect or work effectively. In this use it is an intransitive godan verb: 効きます, 効いた, 効かない. The medicine, device, or other effective thing takes が; the condition it helps with can take に.

- この薬は頭痛に効きます。 (このくすりはずつうにききます。) - This medicine works for headaches.
- 暖房がよく効いています。 (だんぼうがよくきいています。) - The heating is working well.

The first sentence illustrates the grammar of a claim about effectiveness, not medical advice about an identified medicine.

## How the kanji combine

効 represents effect or efficacy. Its native reading here is き, followed by く. The noun [効果](効果.md) (こうか), "effect," uses an on reading of the same character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

効果がある means "have an effect." [働く](働く.md) (はたらく) can mean work, but does not substitute for 効く in every medicine or heating example. 効かない is the direct negative: "does not work; has no effect."

## Distinctive meaning

The shared dictionary entry also includes 利く, preferred in expressions about faculties or senses, such as 鼻が利く (はながきく), "have a keen sense of smell." These are spelling tendencies, not unrelated conjugations. [聞く](聞く.md), "hear; ask," is a different きく and should not be inferred from pronunciation alone.
