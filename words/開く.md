# 開く (あく) - open, become open

## Meaning

**あく** is an intransitive godan verb: something opens, with が marking that thing. Its forms include あきます, あいた, and あかない.

- ドアが開きました。 (ドアがあきました。) - The door opened.

The same spelling also represents **ひらく**, another godan verb. For an event, it means "hold" or "organize," with the event marked by を.

- 来週、会議を開きます。 (らいしゅう、かいぎをひらきます。) - We will hold a meeting next week.

## How the kanji combine

[開](../kanji/開.md) expresses opening. These are two established native readings, not interchangeable pronunciations of every sentence. The polite form of ひらく is ひらきます; its past is ひらいた.

## Synonyms and antonyms

開ける (あける) is the ordinary transitive partner of あく: ドアを開ける, "open the door." 閉まる (しまる) means "close" intransitively.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the construction along with the reading: ドアがあく describes opening; 会議をひらく describes arranging an event. ひらく also has physical opening uses, such as 本をひらく, "open a book."
