# 約 (やく) - approximately; about

## Meaning

約 is an adverb meaning "approximately" or "about," placed before a numerical expression. It tells the reader that the amount is an estimate rather than an exact figure.

- 駅から歩いて約十分です。 (えきからあるいてやくじゅっぷんです。) - It is about ten minutes on foot from the station.
- 会場には約百人が集まりました。 (かいじょうにはやくひゃくにんがあつまりました。) - About a hundred people gathered at the venue.

The number still takes the appropriate counter or unit: 約三キロ (やくさんキロ), "about three kilometers."

## How the kanji combine

[約](../kanji/約.md) is a single kanji read ヤク. In this use it supplies approximation. Other words containing 約, such as 約束 (やくそく), "promise," do not force that promise meaning into an estimated quantity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[およそ](およそ.md) also expresses approximation. [大体](大体.md) (だいたい) can mean roughly or generally and has a wider range of uses.

[ほど](ほど.md) can follow a quantity, as in 十分ほど, "about ten minutes"; 約 goes before it.

## Distinctive meaning

約 is not the English preposition "about" meaning "on the subject of." Nor does it itself mark a lower or upper bound: 約百人 may be somewhat below or above one hundred. Avoid piling several approximation markers onto one short number unless the surrounding style genuinely calls for it.
