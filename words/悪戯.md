# 悪戯 (いたずら) - mischief; prank

## Meaning

悪戯 means "mischief; prank." It is usually written **いたずら**. The noun takes する for "play a prank; get up to mischief," and **いたずらな + noun** means "mischievous."

- 子供たちがいたずらをしました。 (こどもたちがいたずらをしました。) - The children played a prank.
- いたずらな猫が、紙を破りました。 (いたずらなねこが、かみをやぶりました。) - A mischievous cat tore the paper.

The verb can also describe tampering or fooling around with something: 機械にいたずらする (きかいにいたずらする), "tamper with a machine."

## How the kanji combine

[悪](../kanji/悪.md) suggests badness, and 戯 suggests play. Together they give a meaning-based spelling for いたずら; do not assign separate portions of that reading to the two characters. Kana spelling avoids this irregular reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冗談](冗談.md) (じょうだん) is a joke, including something said in jest. An いたずら may involve an action rather than words. [遊ぶ](遊ぶ.md) (あそぶ), "play," does not by itself imply mischief.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not guarantee harmlessness. In some reporting it is a euphemism for sexual misconduct, a separate, serious use rather than an ordinary playful prank. Judge the situation instead of assuming that calling an action いたずら makes it acceptable or minor.
