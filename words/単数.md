# 単数 (たんすう) - singular number; a single item

## Meaning

単数 means a number of one. In grammar, it names the singular,
contrasted with the plural.

- 英語の cat は単数形です。
  (えいごの cat はたんすうけいです。) - English "cat" is a singular form.
- 一つだけを指すので、単数で表します。
  (ひとつだけをさすので、たんすうであらわします。) - Since it refers to just one, we express it in the singular.

単数形 (たんすうけい) means "singular form."
Outside grammar, 単数 can simply identify a single item rather than several.
It is not the ordinary general-purpose counter used to order one apple.

## How the kanji combine

[単](../kanji/単.md), single, qualifies [数](../kanji/数.md), number.
The on readings タン + スウ give たんすう.
Keep the long う; たんす is a different word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

複数 (ふくすう), "multiple; plural," is the direct counterpart.
[一つ](一つ.md) (ひとつ) counts one item.
The words overlap in referring to one, but their constructions differ.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese nouns do not generally require the English cat/cats type of
singular/plural inflection. 本 (ほん) may refer to a book or books as context
requires. That does not mean Japanese cannot express number: counters,
quantities, and other expressions can make it explicit.
