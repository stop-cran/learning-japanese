# 景気 (けいき) - business conditions; economic climate

## Meaning

景気 is a noun describing business or economic conditions. Use 景気がいい/悪い (けいきがいい/わるい) for a good or poor economic climate. The word can concern a wider economy or how business is doing.

- 最近、景気がよくなってきました。 (さいきん、けいきがよくなってきました。) - Economic conditions have been improving lately.
- 景気が悪くて、店の売り上げが減りました。 (けいきがわるくて、みせのうりあげがへりました。) - Business conditions were poor, and the shop's sales fell.

The second sentence links the general business climate with one shop's sales.

## How the kanji combine

[景](../kanji/景.md) contributes a scene or appearance, and [気](../kanji/気.md) an atmosphere or spirit. Think of the overall business atmosphere as a mnemonic for the compound, not a historical derivation. Its on readings are ケイ + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[経済](経済.md) (けいざい) names the economy or economic activity more broadly. 景気 focuses on how business is doing. 好景気 (こうけいき), "prosperity," contrasts with 不景気 (ふけいき), "a business slump."

## Distinctive meaning

The English word "condition" needs qualification here. 景気 is not a general replacement for [状態](状態.md) (じょうたい): a machine's physical condition is not its 景気. Learning the business sense first prevents this misleading extension from a broad English gloss.
