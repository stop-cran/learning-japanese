# 加わる (くわわる) - join; be added

## Meaning

加わる is an intransitive godan verb: something is added, or someone joins a group or activity. The joining or added thing takes が; the group or existing whole takes に.

- チームに新しいメンバーが加わりました。 (チームにあたらしいメンバーがくわわりました。) - A new member joined the team.
- 料金に手数料が加わります。 (りょうきんにてすうりょうがくわわります。) - A service charge is added to the price.

It also describes an increase in degree or intensity: 暑さが加わる
(あつさがくわわる), "the heat intensifies." Here 暑さ is what increases,
rather than an outside force being applied to something.

Past 加わった (くわわった) and negative 加わらない (くわわらない) follow the godan pattern despite the final る.

## How the kanji combine

[加](../kanji/加.md) supplies the addition idea; わる completes the native verb. The spelling is related to [加える](加える.md) (くわえる), but the two verbs present different participants as the subject.

## Synonyms and antonyms

加える is transitive: the person doing the adding acts on an object with を. Compare メンバーを加える, "add a member," with メンバーが加わる, "a member joins."

[参加](参加.md) (さんか) names participation. 会議に参加する and 会議に加わる can both describe joining a meeting.

## Distinctive meaning

Intransitive does not mean unintentional: a person can choose to join. The same verb also describes force being applied, as in 圧力が加わる (あつりょくがくわわる), "pressure is applied." Focus on what joins, is added, increases, or acts on something, rather than translating every English "add" with 加わる.
