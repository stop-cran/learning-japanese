# 思い付く (おもいつく) - hit upon an idea; recall

## Meaning

思い付く means to think of or hit upon an idea. It also has a recall meaning. The spelling 思いつく has the same reading and meaning.

- いい方法を思い付きました。 (いいほうほうをおもいつきました。) - I thought of a good method.
- いい案が思い付きません。 (いいあんがおもいつきません。) - I can't think of a good idea.

JMdict records both transitive and intransitive uses. An idea can appear with を, as in the first example, or with が, as in the second. Do not impose an only-を or only-が rule.

## How the kanji combine

[思](../kanji/思.md)い comes from [思う](思う.md), "think," and [付](../kanji/付.md)く supplies つく. The combination suggests a thought coming to mind, not physically attaching an object. It is godan: 思い付きます, 思い付いた (おもいついた), 思い付かない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[考える](考える.md) (かんがえる) broadly covers thinking or considering. [浮かぶ](浮かぶ.md) (うかぶ) overlaps when an idea comes to mind. [思い出す](思い出す.md) (おもいだす) focuses on recalling something from memory.

## Distinctive meaning

Having an idea does not establish that it is new to the world, correct, or already carried out. The dictionary's "recall" gloss belongs to the broader idea of a thought coming to mind; it is not a blanket replacement for 思い出す in memory sentences. For ordinary recollection of a known name or past event, use 思い出す: 彼の名前が思い出せません (かれのなまえがおもいだせません), "I can't recall his name." The idea examples above instead concern a thought or solution coming to mind. Neither use means deliberately memorizing new information: compare [覚える](覚える.md) (おぼえる).
