# 国家 (こっか) - state; nation

## Meaning

国家 is a noun meaning a state, country, or nation, especially considered as a political entity. Use 国家の before another noun: 国家の役割 (こっかのやくわり), "the role of the state."

- 授業で国家の役割について話し合いました。 (じゅぎょうでこっかのやくわりについてはなしあいました。) - We discussed the role of the state in class.
- その国は独立国家になりました。 (そのくにはどくりつこっかになりました。) - That country became an independent state.

国家間 (こっかかん), "between states," also appears in discussions of international relations.

## How the kanji combine

[国](../kanji/国.md) contributes country or territory. [家](../kanji/家.md) contributes the idea of an established house or collective body within this fixed compound. 国家 names the political whole; it is not a literal "country house." The pronunciation has a small っ: こっか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[国](国.md) (くに) is a broader everyday word for a country and can be used when discussing travel or one's home country. 国家 emphasizes the state as an entity. [国民](国民.md) (こくみん) names its people or citizens, not the state itself.

## Distinctive meaning

国家 is not simply the current government or its leaders. It can remain the same state while its government changes. English "state" also has regional meanings, so the translation does not mean 国家 is the usual word for every province or state within a federation.
