# ボールペン - ballpoint pen

## Meaning

ボールペン is a ballpoint pen. It names the writing instrument, not every type of pen.

- ボールペンで名前を書いてください。 (ボールペンでなまえをかいてください。) - Please write your name with a ballpoint pen.
- 青いボールペンを一本買いました。 (あおいボールペンをいっぽんかいました。) - I bought one blue ballpoint pen.

で marks the writing tool. 一本 (いっぽん) counts one pen.

## How the kanji combine

This is an English-based katakana formation from "ball" and "pen"; JMdict marks it as Japanese-made English. ボール refers to the small ball at the writing tip. There are no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ペン is broader. 万年筆 (まんねんひつ) is a fountain pen, and 鉛筆 (えんぴつ) is a pencil; these are contrasting tools, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

The ordinary English equivalent is "ballpoint pen," not a word-for-word assumption about a pen for playing ball. Learn the Japanese compound as one established stationery term.
