# 失業 (しつぎょう) - unemployment; losing a job

## Meaning

失業 is unemployment. The intransitive verb 失業する means "lose one's job; become unemployed"; 失業している describes the resulting state of being unemployed.

- 会社が倒産して、失業しました。 (かいしゃがとうさんして、しつぎょうしました。) - The company went bankrupt, and I lost my job.
- 失業中なので、新しい仕事を探しています。 (しつぎょうちゅうなので、あたらしいしごとをさがしています。) - I am unemployed, so I am looking for a new job.

失業中 is a compact way to say "while unemployed." It is not the same as being off work for the day.

## How the kanji combine

[失](../kanji/失.md) contributes losing, and [業](../kanji/業.md) contributes work or occupation. The action-and-object relationship is "lose employment." The on readings are シツ + ギョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[就職](就職.md) (しゅうしょく), "getting a job," gives a practical contrast. [仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) is work or a job itself, not the state of unemployment.

## Distinctive meaning

失業 states a work situation without explaining every cause. It does not by itself say whether the person was dismissed, a company closed, or another circumstance ended the employment. Likewise, do not apply it mechanically to everyone not currently working, such as someone on holiday: the employment relationship and context matter.
