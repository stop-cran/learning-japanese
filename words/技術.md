# 技術 (ぎじゅつ) - technique; technology

## Meaning

技術 names technology or a practical technique or skill. The field may be industrial, artistic, or everyday.

- 新しい技術を学んでいます。 (あたらしいぎじゅつをまなんでいます。) - I am learning a new technique.
- この会社は高い技術を持っています。 (このかいしゃはたかいぎじゅつをもっています。) - This company has advanced technical expertise.

It is a noun. Use 技術の to connect it to another noun, or learn compounds such as 技術者 (ぎじゅつしゃ), "engineer; technical specialist."

## How the kanji combine

技, "skill," and [術](../kanji/術.md), "technique; art," reinforce the idea of practical expertise: ぎ + じゅつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

知識 (ちしき) is knowledge; 能力 (のうりょく) is ability more broadly. 技術 emphasizes methods and the ability to apply them.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss "art" means a practiced craft or technique here, not every work of fine art. Context determines whether English "technology," "technique," or "skill" fits best.
