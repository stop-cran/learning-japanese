# 将棋 (しょうぎ) - shogi; Japanese chess

## Meaning

将棋 is shogi, the Japanese board game often called Japanese chess in English. A characteristic feature is that captured pieces can be returned to play on the capturing side.

- 祖父と将棋を指しました。 (そふとしょうぎをさしました。) - I played shogi with my grandfather.
- 将棋の駒を並べてください。 (しょうぎのこまをならべてください。) - Please arrange the shogi pieces.

Learn 将棋を[指す](指す.md) (しょうぎをさす), "play shogi," as a complete expression. Here 指す does not mean merely pointing at the board.

## How the kanji combine

[将](../kanji/将.md) has a commander association, and 棋 a board-game association. They provide a memory aid for the game's name, not a historical account of its origin. The whole-word reading is しょうぎ; do not invent a standalone ギ reading for 棋 merely from this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

チェス names chess, and 囲碁 (いご), also called [碁](碁.md) (ご), is go: they are different games, not interchangeable translations of 将棋. The conventional verb for playing go is 囲碁を[打つ](打つ.md) (いごをうつ), contrasting with 将棋を指す.

## Distinctive meaning

"Japanese chess" helps identify shogi for an English-speaking learner, but does not mean the rules or pieces are identical to international chess. JMdict also records rare spellings such as 象棋, 象戯, and 将棊; they are lookup material, not required replacements for 将棋.
