# 人種 (じんしゅ) - race

## Meaning

人種 is the noun "race" in discussions of human groupings and racial identity.
It is used in expressions about equality and discrimination. 人種の can connect
it to a following noun, as in 人種の違い (じんしゅのちがい), "racial differences."

- 人種によって人を差別してはいけません。 (じんしゅによってひとをさべつしてはいけません。) - People must not be discriminated against on the basis of race.
- この授業では、人種差別の歴史を学びます。 (このじゅぎょうでは、じんしゅさべつのれきしをまなびます。) - In this class, we study the history of racial discrimination.

人種差別 (じんしゅさべつ) means racial discrimination or racism.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) contributes people, and [種](../kanji/種.md) contributes kind
or category. The compound is read ジン + シュ. This component explanation identifies
the vocabulary; it does not establish a biological theory of human categories.

## Synonyms and antonyms

民族 (みんぞく), "ethnic group; people," and 国籍 (こくせき), "nationality,"
answer different questions. [国民](国民.md) (こくみん) concerns a nation's people
or citizens. Nationality and race should not be treated as interchangeable labels.

## Distinctive meaning

The English word "race" also means a competition, but that is not this sense of
人種. When discussing people, use the term with attention to the actual social
or historical context; it does not justify assumptions about an individual's
abilities or character. There is no useful general antonym.
