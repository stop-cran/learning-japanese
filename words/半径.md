# 半径 (はんけい) - radius

## Meaning

半径 is a radius: a line segment from the center of a circle to its
circumference, or from a sphere's center to its surface.
It also means the length of that segment.

- この円の半径は五センチです。 (このえんのはんけいはごセンチです。) - This circle has a radius of five centimeters.
- 半径十メートルの円を描きました。 (はんけいじゅうメートルのえんをかきました。) - I drew a circle with a radius of ten meters.

半径 + measurement + の + noun is a useful size expression.
The noun does not mean an area measurement.

## How the kanji combine

[半](../kanji/半.md) contributes half, and 径 contributes a path or
distance across. A radius is half a diameter.
The on readings combine as ハン + ケイ: はんけい.
The missing 径 card does not remove that written character from the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[直径](直径.md) (ちょっけい) is the diameter: the segment through
the center from one boundary point to the other, or its length.
For a circle, the diameter is twice the radius.
[円周](円周.md) (えんしゅう) is the circumference, not the radius.
These are distinct measurements, not interchangeable labels.

## Distinctive meaning

The center matters: not every segment inside a circle is a radius.
In 行動半径 (こうどうはんけい), the word extends to someone's
range of activity. That expression need not describe a perfectly circular
territory, but it does not change the geometrical definition.
