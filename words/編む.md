# 編む (あむ) - knit; braid; compile

## Meaning

編む is a transitive godan verb. It can mean knit, plait or braid, and it
also means compile or edit material into a work such as a book. The thing
being made or compiled takes を.

- 母はマフラーを編んでいます。
  (はははマフラーをあんでいます。) - My mother is knitting a scarf.
- 先生は昔の詩を集めて本を編みました。
  (せんせいはむかしのしをあつめてほんをあみました。) - The teacher collected old poems and compiled a book.

The polite form is 編みます (あみます), the て-form is 編んで (あんで),
and the past form is 編んだ (あんだ). Do not form the て-form as あみて.

## How the kanji combine

[編](../kanji/編.md) writes the native stem あ, followed by the ending む.
This is one inflecting word, not a compound of two independently read kanji.
The same character appears in 手編み (てあみ), "hand knitting."

## Synonyms and antonyms

編集 (へんしゅう) concerns editing or compilation and takes する for the
corresponding action. It is not a replacement for knitting a scarf.
[組む](組む.md) (くむ) includes assembling or teaming up, but also has a
dictionary braiding sense. The two verbs therefore do not have a universal
textile versus non-textile boundary.

## Distinctive meaning

The object and context distinguish the uses. A scarf suggests textile work;
a book assembled from selected material suggests compilation. Do not
translate the book example literally as knitting, or assume 編む is a
general verb for every kind of craft.
