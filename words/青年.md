# 青年 (せいねん) — young man, youth

## Meaning

青年 is a young person, typically a young man in the teens to twenties: 好青年 こうせいねん (a fine young man), 青年期 せいねんき (adolescence and youth), 青年会 せいねんかい (a youth association).

## How the kanji combine

[青](../kanji/青.md) (blue-green, young, unripe) plus [年](../kanji/年.md) (year, age): "green years", the young. On readings セイ + ネン. 青春 せいしゅん (youth, "green spring") follows the same idea.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 若者 わかもの (young people, everyday word), 少年 しょうねん (boy).
- Related: 青少年 せいしょうねん (young people, boys and girls).

## Distinctive meaning

青年 has a somewhat formal or written tone and leans male; 若者 is the common everyday word.
