# 青年 (せいねん) — young man, youth

## Meaning

青年 is a noun for a young person or youth, often a young man: 好青年 こうせいねん
(a fine young man), 青年期 せいねんき (adolescence and youth), 青年会 せいねんかい
(a youth association). The word itself does not set an exact age boundary.

- 駅で一人の青年に道を聞きました。
  (えきでひとりのせいねんにみちをききました。) — I asked a young man for directions at the station.
- 町の青年たちが祭りの準備をしています。
  (まちのせいねんたちがまつりのじゅんびをしています。) — The town's young people are preparing for the festival.

## How the kanji combine

[青](../kanji/青.md) (blue-green, young, unripe) plus [年](../kanji/年.md) (year, age): "green years", the young. On readings セイ + ネン. 青春 せいしゅん (youth, "green spring") follows the same idea.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 若者 わかもの (young people, everyday word), [少年](少年.md) しょうねん (boy).
- Related: 青少年 せいしょうねん (young people, boys and girls).

## Distinctive meaning

青年 suits descriptions of a person or age group and can sound more written than
若者 in everyday conversation. In a description of one individual, "young man" is a useful
reading; the word does not establish an absolute male-only rule for every group or organization.
[成人](成人.md) (せいじん), "adult," identifies adulthood rather than youth.
