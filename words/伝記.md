# 伝記 (でんき) - biography; life story

## Meaning

伝記 is an account of a person's life and achievements: a biography or life story.

- 図書館で科学者の伝記を借りました。 (としょかんでかがくしゃのでんきをかりました。) - I borrowed a scientist's biography from the library.
- この伝記には、彼女の子供時代も詳しく書かれています。 (このでんきには、かのじょのこどもじだいもくわしくかかれています。) - This biography also describes her childhood in detail.

## How the kanji combine

[伝](../kanji/伝.md) suggests passing an account on, and [記](../kanji/記.md) recording it. The on readings are デン + キ. Together they name a recorded account of someone's life, not the lifetime itself.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[物語](物語.md) is a story or narrative and can recount a life. [歴史](歴史.md) is history and [記録](記録.md) a record more generally; neither is restricted to an individual's life. 自伝 (じでん) is an autobiography, written about one's own life.

## Distinctive meaning

A biography need not be fiction or cover every day of its subject's life. [小説](小説.md), "novel," identifies a different kind of work rather than an antonym. [電気](電気.md), "electricity," has the same でんき reading but is an unrelated word; homophony alone does not make it an alternative spelling of 伝記.
