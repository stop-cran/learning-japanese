# 少ない (すくない) — few, little, scarce

## Meaning

少ない is an い-adjective for small amounts or numbers: 人が少ない (there are few people), 雨が少ない (little rain), 少なくとも すくなくとも (at least).
Like 多い, it is often used as a predicate (数が少ない). For a simple "few + noun" phrase, 少数の (しょうすうの) or わずかな is often more natural.
A clause can also modify a noun: 人が少ない場所 (ひとがすくないばしょ), "a place with few people."

## How the kanji combine

[少](../kanji/少.md) with the okurigana ない: すく + ない. Compare 少し (すこ + し, [少し](少し.md)). Past: 少なかった; negative: 少なくない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [多い](多い.md) おおい.
- Close: わずかな (a mere, slight), まれな (rare).

## Distinctive meaning

少ない states that the number or amount is small, while 少し is an adverb for a little. 小さい ちいさい is about size, not amount ([小](../kanji/小.md)).
