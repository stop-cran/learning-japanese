# 前 (まえ) - before; in front

## Meaning

前 expresses a position in front or a time before something. With a noun, use noun + の前.

- 駅の前で待っています。 (えきのまえでまっています。) - I am waiting in front of the station.
- 寝る前に歯を磨きます。 (ねるまえにはをみがきます。) - I brush my teeth before going to bed.

Before an action, attach the dictionary form directly to 前に, without の. This form remains in a past account: 寝る前に歯を磨きました, "I brushed my teeth before bed."

## How the kanji combine

The single character [前](../kanji/前.md) writes まえ. There is no compound or okurigana here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

後ろ (うしろ) contrasts with physical "in front." 後 (あと), "after," contrasts with temporal "before." They are not one interchangeable opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

After a duration, 前 means "ago": 三年前 (さんねんまえ), "three years ago," without の. Distinguish this from an event + の前, such as 食事の前, "before a meal."
