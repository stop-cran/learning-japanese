# 要素 (ようそ) - element; component; factor

## Meaning

要素 is an element, component, or factor that helps make up a whole or shape a result. **Nの要素** identifies an element of N; 要素の一つ (ようそのひとつ) means "one of the factors."

- 安全は大切な要素です。 (あんぜんはたいせつなようそです。) - Safety is an important factor.
- この物語には冒険の要素があります。 (このものがたりにはぼうけんのようそがあります。) - This story contains an element of adventure.

An element need not be a physically separable piece. In the second example, it is a feature of the story.

## How the kanji combine

[要](../kanji/要.md) contributes importance or necessity, and 素 contributes a basic constituent. Together they suggest a constituent that matters to the makeup of something. The readings are ヨウ + ソ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[原因](原因.md) (げんいん) is a cause; a 要素 can contribute to a result without being its sole cause. [全体](全体.md) (ぜんたい) is the whole, contrasting with one constituent. 元素 (げんそ) is the precise word for a chemical element.

## Distinctive meaning

要素 can also mean an element of an array or similar data structure in computing. There it is an individual member, not necessarily the most important one. The kanji association with importance should not become a rule that every 要素 is indispensable.
