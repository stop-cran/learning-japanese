# さきおととい - three days ago

## Meaning

さきおととい means three days ago: the day before the day before yesterday. It functions as a noun or a time adverb.

- さきおととい、友達に会いました。 (さきおととい、ともだちにあいました。) - I met a friend three days ago.
- さきおとといから雨が続いています。 (さきおとといからあめがつづいています。) - It has been raining since three days ago.

The first example uses the time expression without に; the second adds から, "since."

## How the kanji combine

The dictionary spellings are 一昨昨日 and 一昨々日. They use [一](../kanji/一.md), one, [昨](../kanji/昨.md), previous, and [日](../kanji/日.md), day; 々 repeats the preceding character.

Learn さきおととい as a whole calendar expression rather than assigning each syllable to a separate kanji. The same family also records the whole-word readings さきおとつい and いっさくさくじつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[昨日](昨日.md) (きのう) is yesterday; [一昨日](一昨日.md) (おととい) is the day before yesterday. さきおととい is one day earlier still. These are distinct dates, not interchangeable synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's English "two days before yesterday" also means three days before today. Count from yesterday, not from today. If today is Friday, 昨日 is Thursday, 一昨日 is Wednesday, and さきおととい is Tuesday.
