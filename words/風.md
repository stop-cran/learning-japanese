# 風 (かぜ) - wind

## Meaning

風 is wind or a breeze. The common expression 風が吹く (かぜがふく) means "the wind blows."

- 今日は風が強いです。 (きょうはかぜがつよいです。) - The wind is strong today.
- 涼しい風が吹いています。 (すずしいかぜがふいています。) - A cool breeze is blowing.

風 is a noun. Use an adjective such as 強い or 冷たい to describe its strength or temperature.

## How the kanji combine

[風](../kanji/風.md) represents the whole native word かぜ. There is no okurigana and no second component in the written word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そよ風 (そよかぜ) is a gentle breeze. 空気 (くうき) is air, not necessarily air in motion. There is no single everyday opposite of wind.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish 風 from 風邪 (かぜ), "a cold" as an illness. Also distinguish the reading ふう, used in expressions about style or manner; that does not replace かぜ in ordinary weather sentences.
