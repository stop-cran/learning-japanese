# 棚 (たな) - shelf; rack

## Meaning

棚 is a shelf, rack, or shelving unit used to hold things.
The same Japanese noun can refer to one shelf or several; context supplies
the number.

- 棚にコップを並べました。 (たなにコップをならべました。) - I arranged the cups on the shelf.

に marks where the cups are placed, and を marks the cups.

## How the kanji combine

棚 writes the complete native noun たな, with no okurigana.
In some compounds its first consonant becomes voiced: 本棚
(ほんだな), "bookshelf," ends in だな rather than たな.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本棚](本棚.md) is shelving specifically for books.
引き出し (ひきだし), "drawer," names a sliding storage compartment, not an
open shelf. These are related furnishings, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

棚 identifies the supporting storage structure, not the objects stored there.
棚の上 (たなのうえ) can mean "on top of the shelving unit"; plain 棚に often
suffices when placing something on one of its shelves. Choose the phrase
that fits the physical arrangement.
