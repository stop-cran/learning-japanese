# 余分 (よぶん) - extra; surplus

## Meaning

余分 is an extra amount or something beyond what is needed or expected.
It is a noun and な-adjective: 余分な荷物 (よぶんなにもつ), "extra luggage."
Use 余分に with an action to mean doing or providing more than the baseline.

- 念のため、おにぎりを一つ余分に作りました。 (ねんのため、おにぎりをひとつよぶんにつくりました。) - I made one extra rice ball just in case.
- 余分な荷物は置いていきましょう。 (よぶんなにもつはおいていきましょう。) - Let's leave the unnecessary luggage behind.

The extra amount is useful insurance in the first sentence, but an unwanted
burden in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[余](../kanji/余.md) contributes what remains or exceeds an amount, and
[分](../kanji/分.md) contributes a portion. Together they name a surplus
portion. The on readings combine as よ + ぶん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[余る](余る.md) (あまる) is the verb "remain; be left over."
余計 (よけい) can also mean extra or unnecessary, with overlapping uses.
[必要](必要.md) (ひつよう), "necessary," contrasts when an extra item is
unneeded, but an emergency spare may be deliberately useful.

## Distinctive meaning

余分 does not itself mean wasteful. To understand the evaluation, ask "extra
compared with what?" It may be extra compared with the number of guests,
the planned amount, or what someone can carry.
一つ余分に makes that extra count explicit. Without a quantity such as
一つ, 余分 does not specify how much extra there is.
