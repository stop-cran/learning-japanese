# ぱりぱり — thin and brittle-crisp

## Meaning

Crispness that breaks with a sharp crackle, often thin food such as seaweed or a fried wrapper. It also describes ripping or cracking sounds.

- 焼きのりがぱりぱりしていて、おいしい。 — The toasted seaweed is crisp and delicious.
- 春巻き（はるまき）の皮（かわ）がぱりぱりに揚がった（あがった）。 — The spring-roll wrapper fried up crisp.
- 薄い氷がぱりぱりと割れた。 — The thin ice cracked with a brittle sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[さくさく](さくさく.md) often suggests a lighter, crumbly bite. [かりかり](かりかり.md) can emphasize hard crispness. [べちゃべちゃ](べちゃべちゃ.md) is a useful contrast when a wrapper becomes wet and soggy.

## Distinctive meaning

ぱりぱりののり, ぱりぱりした皮, and ぱりぱりに焼く are useful; と also occurs in sound descriptions such as ぱりぱりと割れる（われる）. Thinness is a prototype, not a strict restriction. JMdict additionally records vigorous people and crisp, high-quality or well-starched clothing; do not force the food translation into those uses.
