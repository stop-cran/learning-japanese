# 刊行 (かんこう) - publication; issuing a publication

## Meaning

刊行 means publication or the issuing of a publication.
It is a noun and a transitive する verb: 本を刊行する means publish a book.
When the publication is the subject, use 刊行される, be published.

- 新しい辞典を刊行する。
  (あたらしいじてんをかんこうする。) - Publish a new dictionary.
- この雑誌は毎月刊行される。
  (このざっしはまいつきかんこうされる。) - This magazine is published every month.

## How the kanji combine

[刊](../kanji/刊.md) contributes publishing, and [行](../kanji/行.md)
contributes carrying something out. Together they describe carrying out publication,
not physically walking with a book.
The on readings combine as かん + こう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出版](出版.md) (しゅっぱん), publication or publishing, overlaps directly.
[発行](発行.md) (はっこう) can also mean publication or issuance.
Do not manufacture a strict distinction that makes only one of these acceptable
for every book or magazine.

## Distinctive meaning

刊行 concerns bringing out a publication, not merely writing the manuscript
or [印刷](印刷.md) (いんさつ), printing the pages.
The related nouns in the synonym section can describe the same publishing event.
Choose from the actual context rather than assuming fixed one-word English equivalents.
