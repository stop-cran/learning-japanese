# 痩せる (やせる) — to lose weight; to become thin

## Meaning

痩せる means becoming thinner or losing weight. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 痩せます, 痩せない, 痩せて. The person undergoing the change takes が.

- 病気で少し痩せました。 (びょうきですこしやせました。) — I lost a little weight because of an illness.
- その猫は痩せています。 (そのねこはやせています。) — That cat is thin.

## How the kanji combine

痩 supplies や, and せる is okurigana. The full verb is やせる. Kana やせる is also a practical spelling when the kanji is unfamiliar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

太る (ふとる), "gain weight; become fat," is the change-of-state contrast. 細い (ほそい), "thin; slender," describes shape and applies to objects such as ropes as well as body parts.

## Distinctive meaning

As a sentence-ending past form, 痩せた reports losing weight; 痩せている commonly describes the resulting thin state. The word does not say whether weight loss was intended, healthy, or desirable. Comments on someone else's body can be sensitive, so do not assume it is automatically a compliment.
