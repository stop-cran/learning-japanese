# 断定 (だんてい) - definite judgment; assertion

## Meaning

断定 is making a definite judgment or stating a conclusion categorically.
断定する is transitive. Use **matter を断定する** or quote the judgment
with **plain clause + と断定する**.

- まだ原因を断定できません。
  (まだげんいんをだんていできません。) - We cannot yet determine the cause definitively.
- 警察は、その火事は事故だと断定しました。
  (けいさつは、そのかじはじこだとだんていしました。) - The police concluded definitively that the fire was accidental.

In 事故だと, retain だ after the noun predicate before quoting it with と.
断定的な言い方 (だんていてきないいかた) is a categorical way of speaking.

## How the kanji combine

[断](../kanji/断.md) contributes deciding or judging and
[定](../kanji/定.md) fixing or establishing.
Together they concern a settled judgment, not physically cutting something.
The on readings are ダン + テイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[判断](判断.md) (はんだん) is judging or assessing more generally.
[結論](結論.md) (けつろん) names a conclusion.
断定 highlights committing to a definite judgment, but it does not
guarantee that the conclusion is correct.
判断を保留する (はんだんをほりゅうする), "reserve judgment,"
provides a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

In Japanese grammar descriptions, 断定 also names the predicative
assertion associated with forms such as だ and です.
This technical use is not simply the positive-versus-negative polarity
distinction taught under [肯定](肯定.md) (こうてい).
Keep that terminology separate from the everyday act of declaring a conclusion.
