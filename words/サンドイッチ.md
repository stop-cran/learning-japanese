# サンドイッチ - sandwich

## Meaning

サンドイッチ means a sandwich: bread with a filling. It is an ordinary food noun and does not itself specify the filling, serving size, or number of pieces.

昼ご飯にサンドイッチを食べました。 (ひるごはんにサンドイッチをたべました。) - I ate a sandwich for lunch.

卵のサンドイッチを作ります。 (たまごのサンドイッチをつくります。) - I will make an egg sandwich.

The filling can be connected with の.

## How the kanji combine

The word is borrowed from English "sandwich" and written in katakana. Pay attention to the small ッ before チ. サンドウィッチ is another recorded spelling, but this page uses サンドイッチ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

サンド is a shortened form found in food names. パン means bread, which is an ingredient rather than a synonym for the finished sandwich. おにぎり is a different portable food, not an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that an English sandwich description predicts the exact bread, crusts, or filling of a Japanese shop's product. The noun gives the broad food category; menu names and the package provide the details.
