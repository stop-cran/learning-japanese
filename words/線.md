# 線 (せん) - line

## Meaning

線 is a line, such as a mark drawn on paper. It also occurs in words for wires, boundaries, and transport routes.

- 紙にまっすぐな線を引いてください。 (かみにまっすぐなせんをひいてください。) - Please draw a straight line on the paper.
- 黄色い線の内側で待ってください。 (きいろいせんのうちがわでまってください。) - Please wait inside the yellow line.

線を引く (せんをひく) is the usual expression for drawing a line. At a station, a marking called a 線 may show where passengers should stand.

## How the kanji combine

This is one kanji used with its on reading せん. There is no internal compound to separate. The character also follows other elements as a suffix naming a line or route.

## Synonyms and antonyms

直線 (ちょくせん) specifically means a straight line, while 曲線 (きょくせん) means a curve. 列 (れつ) is a row or queue of people or things.

## Distinctive meaning

English "line" covers both a drawn mark and people waiting in order. For the latter, use 列 rather than automatically translating it as 線. In a railway name, 線 identifies a route, not one individual train.
