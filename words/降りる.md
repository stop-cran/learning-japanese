# 降りる (おりる)

## Meaning

降りる means to get off a vehicle or descend. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 降ります (おります), 降りて (おりて), 降りない (おりない).

- 次の駅で電車を降ります。 (つぎの えきで でんしゃを おります。) — I will get off the train at the next station.
- 山から降りました。 (やまから おりました。) — I came down from the mountain.

With vehicles, を marks what someone leaves; it does not turn the verb into a transitive action on the vehicle. で marks the place of getting off.

## How the kanji combine

[降](../kanji/降.md) supplies お, followed by りる. The ichidan conjugation base is おり, not just the part written in kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

乗る (のる), "get on; ride," contrasts with getting off. 下りる has the same reading and is especially used for downward movement, such as going down stairs.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling 降りる is particularly useful for leaving transport. Do not confuse おりる with 降る (ふる), used for rain or snow falling.
