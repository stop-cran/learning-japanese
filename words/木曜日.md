# 木曜日 (もくようび) - Thursday

## Meaning

木曜日 is Thursday. It is a time noun and can mark when something happens.

- 木曜日に図書館へ行きます。 (もくようびにとしょかんへいきます。) - I will go to the library on Thursday.
- 次の木曜日は休みです。 (つぎのもくようびはやすみです。) - Next Thursday is a day off.

木曜日の授業 (もくようびのじゅぎょう) means "Thursday's class," using の before another noun.

## How the kanji combine

[木](../kanji/木.md) is もく, [曜](../kanji/曜.md) is よう, and [日](../kanji/日.md) is び. The last is a voiced native reading of 日, so this is an on-first mixed compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

木曜 (もくよう) is the shorter form. 水曜日 (すいようび), Wednesday, and 金曜日 (きんようび), Friday, are neighboring weekdays rather than opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

The standalone word 木 is き, "tree; wood," but Thursday is not きようび. Learn もくようび as a whole weekday name; calendar headings may abbreviate it to 木.
