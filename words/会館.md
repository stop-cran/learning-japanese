# 会館 (かいかん) - meeting hall; assembly hall

## Meaning

会館 is a meeting or assembly hall: a building or facility used for gatherings and activities. It appears in facility names such as 市民会館 (しみんかいかん), "civic hall."

- 市民会館で講演会があります。 (しみんかいかんでこうえんかいがあります。) - There will be a public lecture at the civic hall.
- 会館の入口で待っています。 (かいかんのいりぐちでまっています。) - I am waiting at the hall's entrance.

で marks the event location; の links the hall to its entrance.

## How the kanji combine

[会](../kanji/会.md), "meeting; gathering," modifies [館](../kanji/館.md), "hall; large building." Their on readings カイ + カン give かいかん. Keep the final ん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[講堂](講堂.md) (こうどう), "auditorium; assembly hall," overlaps for a hall used for talks or assemblies. [会場](会場.md) (かいじょう), "event venue," identifies where a particular event is held; it need not be a building called a 会館.

## Distinctive meaning

会館 is the facility, not the meeting itself. [会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) is a meeting or conference. A 会館 can contain more than one room and host different activities, so do not interpret every occurrence as one specific meeting room.
