# 昼休み (ひるやすみ) — lunch break

## Meaning

昼休み is a midday break at work or school, usually including time for lunch. It names the break, not the meal itself.

昼休みに、近くの公園を散歩します。 (ひるやすみに、ちかくのこうえんをさんぽします。) — I take a walk in a nearby park during my lunch break.

## How the kanji combine

[昼](../kanji/昼.md) is ひる, "daytime; noon," and [休](../kanji/休.md)み is やすみ, "rest; break." Both are native readings. The final み is written after the kanji in the noun 休み.

## Synonyms and antonyms

休憩 (きゅうけい) is a break in general and can occur at any time. 昼ご飯 (ひるごはん) is lunch as a meal. 夏休み (なつやすみ) is summer vacation, a much longer kind of 休み.

## Distinctive meaning

昼休み does not imply an exact start at twelve or an exact duration of one hour. Those depend on the schedule. 昼休みは一時までです (ひるやすみはいちじまでです) means "The lunch break lasts until one." Use に to locate another action during the break.
