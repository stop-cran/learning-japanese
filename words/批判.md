# 批判 (ひはん) - criticism; critical judgment

## Meaning

批判 is a noun for criticism or critical judgment. 批判する
is transitive, with the person, policy, or action criticized marked
by を. As a noun, Nへの批判 identifies criticism directed
toward N.

- 住民は市の計画を批判しました。 (じゅうみんはしのけいかくをひはんしました。) - The residents criticized the city's plan.
- その発言は多くの批判を受けました。 (そのはつげんはおおくのひはんをうけました。) - That statement received a great deal of criticism.

批判を受ける names receiving criticism, whereas 批判する
names giving it. Keep that difference in perspective clear.

## How the kanji combine

批 contributes criticism or evaluation, and [判](../kanji/判.md)
contributes judgment. The related ideas combine into critical
assessment. Their on readings are ひ + はん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[批評](批評.md) (ひひょう) also concerns critical appraisal,
for example of a work. [意見](意見.md) (いけん) is the
broader word "opinion"; an opinion need not criticize anything.
Praise is a contextual opposite, but criticism can also include
reasoned evaluation rather than simple personal hostility.

## Distinctive meaning

批判 is not a neutral substitute for every English "comment."
It frames the statement as criticism or judgment, often drawing
attention to problems. Nor does the word itself establish whether
the criticism is fair, well supported, or constructive; those are
separate evaluations of its content.
