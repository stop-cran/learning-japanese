# 書き取り (かきとり) - dictation; written copying

## Meaning

書き取り is writing down spoken words, as in dictation. It can also mean copying written material or writing kanji from a kana prompt. **書取** is another dictionary spelling, also read かきとり.

- 先生が読んだ文の書き取りをしました。 (せんせいがよんだぶんのかきとりをしました。) - We did a dictation exercise using sentences the teacher read aloud.
- 今日は漢字の書き取りの練習をしました。 (きょうはかんじのかきとりのれんしゅうをしました。) - Today we practiced writing kanji.

The second does not by itself say that an audio recording was used. Learn the fuller instruction to know whether you should listen, copy or produce kanji from kana.

## How the kanji combine

[書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing and [取](../kanji/取.md) taking something down. 書き and 取り are native verb stems, read かき + とり. This is not the on-reading combination ショ + シュ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

書き取る (かきとる) is the related verb, "write down; take dictation; copy out." [書く](書く.md) (かく) is the broader writing verb. [メモ](メモ.md) can be brief notes of the main points rather than a word-for-word transcription.

## Distinctive meaning

書き取り names a writing activity or exercise, not the notebook it is written in. Dictation is one important use, but treating listening as a requirement for every 書き取り would miss the written-copying and kanji-production uses.
