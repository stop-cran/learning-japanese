# 椎骨 (ついこつ) - vertebra

## Meaning

椎骨 is an anatomical noun for a **vertebra**, one of the bones making up the backbone. Japanese nouns do not mark singular and plural in the same way as English: context can make 椎骨 mean one vertebra or several vertebrae.

- 椎骨は背骨を構成する骨です。 (ついこつはせぼねをこうせいするほねです。) - Vertebrae are the bones that make up the backbone.
- この図は椎骨の形を示しています。 (このずはついこつのかたちをしめしています。) - This diagram shows the shape of a vertebra.

## How the kanji combine

[椎](../kanji/椎.md) (ツイ) supplies the vertebral meaning, and [骨](../kanji/骨.md) (コツ) means bone. This is a modifier-plus-head compound: a **vertebral bone**. The tree reading しい of 椎 is not used in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

脊椎骨 (せきついこつ) also means a vertebra. 背骨 (せぼね) normally names the backbone as a whole rather than one of its component bones. 脊椎 (せきつい) can name the spine, but the dictionary also records its use for a vertebra, so the distinction from 椎骨 is not absolute.

[骨](骨.md) (ほね) is the broader native word for bone. 椎間板 (ついかんばん), an intervertebral disc, is a different structure between vertebrae, not an antonym or another word for a vertebra.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 椎骨 when identifying these bones in anatomy or a medical explanation. It does not mean every bone, the entire back, or the spinal cord. For the ordinary word "backbone," 背骨 (せぼね) is useful; 椎骨 focuses on the bones that form it.
