# カーブ (カーブ) - curve; bend; curveball

## Meaning

カーブ is a curve or bend, for example in a road. 急なカーブ (きゅうなカーブ) means "a sharp bend." カーブする is intransitive: it describes something curving. In baseball, カーブ also names a curveball.

- この道は右にカーブしています。 (このみちはみぎにカーブしています。) - This road curves to the right.
- 投手がカーブを投げました。 (とうしゅがカーブをなげました。) - The pitcher threw a curveball.

In the first example, the road is the topic; in the second, を marks the pitch that is thrown.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "curve," not a kanji compound. Keep the long vowel in カー.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[曲がる](曲る.md) (まがる), "bend; turn," overlaps when describing a road's shape or turning along a route. 曲線 (きょくせん) names a curved line. Their sentence patterns are not automatically interchangeable with every noun use of カーブ.

## Distinctive meaning

The baseball sense names a particular kind of pitch, not any ball that changes direction. The road and baseball meanings share the curved-path idea; context tells you whether English "bend" or "curveball" is appropriate.
