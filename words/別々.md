# 別々 (べつべつ) - separate; individually

## Meaning

別々 describes things or people as separate, distinct or respective rather than
together. Use 別々に for "separately/individually," and 別々の + noun or
別々な + noun to describe separate things. A predicate can end 別々です.

- 会計は別々にお願いします。 (かいけいはべつべつにおねがいします。) - Separate bills, please.
- 二人は別々の会社で働いています。 (ふたりはべつべつのかいしゃではたらいています。) - The two work at different companies.

## How the kanji combine

[別](../kanji/別.md), read ベツ, is repeated: 々 stands for the preceding
character, giving べつべつ. 別別 is another spelling of the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[別](別.md) (べつ) can mean another, different or separate.
[別に](別に.md) (べつに) can mean separately, but also "not particularly" with
a negative predicate. [それぞれ](それぞれ.md) means each/respectively;
it need not emphasize keeping things apart.

## Distinctive meaning

別々に帰る (べつべつにかえる) is to go home separately. It does not by itself
mean a romantic breakup. [別れる](別れる.md) (わかれる) names parting from
someone and can mean breaking up in a relationship; context decides.
Do not use bare 別々 as though every English adverb attaches directly to a verb.
