# こっそり — secretly; without being noticed

## Meaning

Doing something discreetly or secretly, avoiding other people's notice.

- 友達へのプレゼントをこっそり用意（ようい）した。 — I secretly prepared a present for my friend.
- みんなを起（お）こさないように、こっそり部屋を出た。 — I slipped out of the room without waking everyone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こそこそ](こそこそ.md) often adds a furtive or sneaky impression. [そっと](そっと.md) overlaps in avoiding notice but also means gentle handling. [ひそひそ](ひそひそ.md) specifically evokes low voices.

## Distinctive meaning

こっそり用意する does not imply wrongdoing: a surprise gift is a useful neutral scene. Unlike a universal “quietly,” こっそり emphasizes concealment or lack of notice, not merely low sound volume.
