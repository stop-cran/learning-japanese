# ミルク (ミルク) - milk

## Meaning

ミルク is milk. It is a noun: **ミルクを飲む** means to drink milk, and ミルクを入れる means to add or put in milk. In a baby-feeding context, it can specifically mean infant formula.

- コーヒーにミルクを入れますか。 (コーヒーにミルクをいれますか。) - Would you like milk in your coffee?
- 赤ちゃんのためにミルクを作りました。 (あかちゃんのためにミルクをつくりました。) - I prepared formula for the baby.

In the second example, 作る means preparing the feed, not producing milk biologically.

## How the kanji combine

ミルク is the katakana loanword corresponding to English "milk." It has three kana and no long-vowel mark. There is no kanji decomposition for this spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[牛乳](牛乳.md) (ぎゅうにゅう) specifies cow's milk. Its meaning overlaps with ミルク, but ミルク is not limited to cow's milk in every context. 母乳 (ぼにゅう) specifically means breast milk; 粉ミルク (こなミルク) specifies powdered milk or formula.

## Distinctive meaning

The situation determines what product is meant. ミルク in a drink order does not by itself provide a full ingredient list, and a baby-feeding use should not automatically be translated as ordinary cow's milk. These are lexical distinctions, not feeding or dietary instructions.
