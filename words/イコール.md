# イコール - equal; equals sign

## Meaning

イコール expresses equality or equivalence. In **AはBとイコールだ**, A is
equal or equivalent to B. It also names the equals sign **=** and reads
the relation in an equation.

- この二つの値はイコールです。 (このふたつのあたいはイコールです。) - These two values are equal.
- 「二足す三イコール五」と読みます。 (「にたすさんイコールご」とよみます。) - We read it as "two plus three equals five."

イコールで結ぶ (イコールでむすぶ) means joining with an equals sign.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in katakana and has no kanji components. Keep the long
vowel in コー. Its equality use and its name for a written symbol belong to
the same word, but the surrounding construction shows which is intended.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[等しい](等しい.md) (ひとしい) is an い-adjective meaning equal or equivalent.
[相当](相当.md) (そうとう) in Nに相当する expresses correspondence or equivalence.
等号 (とうごう) is another name for the equals sign.

## Distinctive meaning

The equivalence can be figurative: 成功がイコール幸せとは限らない
(せいこうがイコールしあわせとはかぎらない), "Success does not necessarily
equal happiness." This is a claim about equivalence, not a claim that the
two words have identical dictionary definitions in every context.
