# 済ませる (すませる) - finish; get something done

## Meaning

済ませる is a transitive ichidan verb: someone finishes or gets something out of the way. The task takes を. Forms include 済ませます (すませます) and 済ませて (すませて).

- 出かける前に、宿題を済ませました。 (でかけるまえに、しゅくだいをすませました。) - I finished my homework before going out.
- 昼食はパンで済ませました。 (ちゅうしょくはパンですませました。) - I made do with bread for lunch.

The second example uses a related sense, **Xで済ませる**, "manage/make do with X." It presents bread as sufficient for the meal, not as an unfinished chore.

## How the kanji combine

[済](../kanji/済.md) contributes completion or settlement. The written ませる completes the native verb. Learn it alongside [済む](済む.md) (すむ), which describes something being finished or settled.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[終える](終える.md) (おえる) also means finishing something. Compare 用事を済ませる (ようじをすませる), "get an errand done," with 用事が済む, "the errand is done." The を/が difference separates action from the resulting state.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not gloss 済ませる simply as "be finished": its subject gets something finished. The verb can also describe resolving a matter. In both completion and making-do uses, the idea is that the requirement is dealt with, without necessarily explaining how thoroughly or elegantly.
