# 社長 (しゃちょう) - company president

## Meaning

社長 names the president or head of a company. It is a job title, not a general word for anyone who supervises you.

- 社長は今、会議中です。 (しゃちょうはいま、かいぎちゅうです。) - The company president is in a meeting now.

The title can follow a surname: 田中社長 (たなかしゃちょう), "President Tanaka." It can also be used by itself when the person and company are understood.

## How the kanji combine

[社](../kanji/社.md) has its company sense, and [長](../kanji/長.md) means a head or leader here. The on readings are シャ + チョウ. 長 does not describe the person's physical height.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上司 (じょうし) means one's superior or boss more broadly. 店長 (てんちょう) manages a shop; 部長 (ぶちょう) heads a department. These roles are not interchangeable with 社長.

## Distinctive meaning

English corporate titles do not always align exactly with Japanese ones. "Company president" is a useful default translation; do not assume 社長 always identifies the owner or every position translated "CEO."
