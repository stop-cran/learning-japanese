# 寺 (てら) -- Buddhist temple

## Meaning

寺 is a Buddhist temple. In everyday conversation it is also commonly said with the prefix お: お寺 (おてら).

日曜日に近くのお寺へ行きました。 (にちようびにちかくのおてらへいきました。) -- On Sunday I went to a nearby Buddhist temple.

Use に or へ for the destination of a visit, and で for an activity there.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 寺 writes the native noun てら. Temple names may instead use the on reading じ. Learn the pronunciation of each name separately rather than assuming every 寺 must be read てら.

## Synonyms and antonyms

神社 (じんじゃ) is a Shinto shrine, not simply another spelling of 寺. 教会 (きょうかい) can name a Christian church. They are related religious-place vocabulary, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

English "temple" can refer to places of worship in several religions. In ordinary Japanese, 寺 specifically directs the listener toward a Buddhist institution. A translation should preserve that distinction when comparing it with a shrine.
