# 繰り返す (くりかえす) - repeat; do again

## Meaning

繰り返す is a godan verb meaning to repeat or do something again. In the transitive pattern, the words, action, or mistake being repeated takes を.

- 同じ質問を繰り返さないでください。 (おなじしつもんをくりかえさないでください。) - Please do not repeat the same question.
- この練習を三回繰り返します。 (このれんしゅうをさんかいくりかえします。) - We will repeat this exercise three times.

The forms are 繰り返します, 繰り返した, and 繰り返さない. 繰り返して読む (くりかえしてよむ) means "read repeatedly." The repetition can be deliberate practice or an unwanted recurrence.

## How the kanji combine

繰り is connected with reeling or working through something; [返](../kanji/返.md) contributes returning or doing back. Learn the compound as a cycle of doing something again, not as two separate commands. The ending conjugates like [返す](返す.md) (かえす).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もう一度](もう一度.md) (もういちど), "once more," can request another performance of an action. [続ける](続ける.md) (つづける), "continue," emphasizes carrying on; 繰り返す emphasizes recurrence. [止める](止める.md) (やめる), "stop," can end repeated activity but is not its opposite in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

繰り返す does not necessarily mean correcting a failed attempt: you can repeat something that succeeded. It also has an intransitive use, as in 歴史は繰り返す (れきしはくりかえす), "history repeats itself." Here history recurs; nobody is named as making it happen again.
