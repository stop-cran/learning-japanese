# ひらひら — fluttering lightly

## Meaning

Light, thin things waving, flapping, or fluttering.

- 白いリボンが風にひらひら揺（ゆ）れている。 — A white ribbon is fluttering in the breeze.
- 花びらがひらひらと落ちた。 — The petals fluttered down.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[はらはら](はらはら.md) and [ちらちら](ちらちら.md) overlap in light falling things. [ゆらゆら](ゆらゆら.md) more broadly describes swaying, without necessarily suggesting something thin and light.

## Distinctive meaning

ひらひら舞（ま）う, “flutter about,” need not imply falling downward. A noun use also refers to a frill or thin piece of material. The inspected dictionary marks a separate flickering-light use archaic; that is not the primary lesson.
