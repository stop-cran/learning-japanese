# パーセント (パーセント) - percent; percentage

## Meaning

パーセント names a proportion out of one hundred and follows a number. It is written with % as well as in katakana. Fifty percent is 五十パーセント (ごじゅっパーセント), one half of the relevant total.

- 参加者の三十パーセントが学生です。 (さんかしゃのさんじゅっパーセントががくせいです。) - Thirty percent of the participants are students.
- この商品は二十パーセント引きです。 (このしょうひんはにじゅっパーセントびきです。) - This item is twenty percent off.

For 十 before パーセント, both じゅっ and じっ occur, as in じゅっパーセント/じっパーセント.

## How the kanji combine

The katakana loanword has no kanji components. The mark ー lengthens パ. Numerals may be written as digits, so 30% and 三十パーセント express the same percentage.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[割合](割合.md) (わりあい) is a proportion or ratio; パーセント expresses it on a per-hundred scale. 一割 (いちわり) is ten percent, not one percent. A higher or lower percentage is a numerical contrast, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Identify the base amount. A twenty-percent discount means paying eighty percent of the original price. Also distinguish percentage points from relative growth: an increase from 20% to 30% is ten percentage points, but a fifty-percent increase relative to the original 20%. パーセント alone does not resolve that comparison.
