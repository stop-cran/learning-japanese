# 恐縮 (きょうしゅく) - feeling obliged; apologetic gratitude

## Meaning

恐縮 expresses feeling obliged or grateful, or feeling sorry about troubling someone. It is a noun that can take する. In polite exchanges, gratitude and an awareness of the other person's trouble can occur together.

- ご丁寧なお手紙をいただき、恐縮しております。
  (ごていねいなおてがみをいただき、きょうしゅくしております。) - I am very grateful for your thoughtful letter.
- 恐縮ですが、もう一度説明していただけますか。
  (きょうしゅくですが、もういちどせつめいしていただけますか。) - Sorry to trouble you, but could you explain once more?

恐縮ですが can soften a request. 恐縮しております is a polite way of describing the speaker's feeling; it is not an instruction to the other person.

## How the kanji combine

[恐](../kanji/恐.md) contributes fear or apprehension, and 縮 shrinking. The literal association helps remember a feeling of shrinking modestly, but the modern courteous uses need not mean literal terror. The readings are キョウ + シュク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

恐れ入る (おそれいる) also has apology and gratitude uses. [感謝](感謝.md) (かんしゃ) directly names gratitude, while [すみません](すみません.md) is a useful everyday polite expression for apology, attention, or thanks.

## Distinctive meaning

恐縮 can acknowledge kindness, not just wrongdoing. It is also not a general replacement for every [恥ずかしい](恥ずかしい.md) (はずかしい), "embarrassed": embarrassment about performing on stage need not involve troubling or receiving kindness from someone. Literal shrinking in fear is an older dictionary sense, not the required interpretation of the request above.
