# うじうじ — hesitating; dwelling indecisively

## Meaning

うじうじ describes being unable to make up one's mind or continuing to worry without acting.

- 入部するかどうか、いつまでもうじうじ悩んでいた。
  — I kept hesitating over whether to join the club.
- うじうじしていないで、まず一度相談してみよう。
  — Instead of dwelling on it, let's try talking it over once.

## Synonyms and antonyms

うじうじ is critical or self-deprecating when applied to a person's indecision. It is not a neutral
word for taking time to consider a difficult choice.

## Distinctive meaning

The focus is indecision or lingering worry, not simply moving slowly. The situation should make
clear what decision or concern the person is avoiding.
