# 休憩 (きゅうけい) - rest break; intermission

## Meaning

休憩 is a break or rest during an activity. Use 休憩する for taking a break, and 休憩を取る (きゅうけいをとる) for the same practical action. A place to rest can take で.

- ここで少し休憩しましょう。 (ここですこしきゅうけいしましょう。) - Let's take a short break here.
- 会議の途中で十分間の休憩を取りました。 (かいぎのとちゅうでじゅっぷんかんのきゅうけいをとりました。) - We took a ten-minute break during the meeting.

休憩中 (きゅうけいちゅう) means "on a break" or "during the break," as on a workplace notice.

## How the kanji combine

[休](../kanji/休.md) and 憩 both concern resting. Their similar meanings reinforce each other in this compound, read キュウ + ケイ. The long vowel in きゅう is part of the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[休む](休む.md) (やすむ) is broader: it can mean rest, take time off, or be absent. [休暇](休暇.md) (きゅうか) refers to leave or vacation rather than a brief pause within the workday.

## Distinctive meaning

休憩 frames the stopping as a break in an activity, rather than saying that the activity has been canceled or completed. Its duration must come from context; the word does not prescribe a fixed number of minutes.
