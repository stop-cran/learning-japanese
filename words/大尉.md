# 大尉 (たいい) — captain in the army; lieutenant in the navy

## Meaning

大尉 is an officer rank. The English title depends on the service: for example, “captain” in an army context and “lieutenant” in a navy context. It is not a title for the leader of a sports team.

- 写真の説明には「陸軍大尉」とある。 (しゃしんのせつめいには「りくぐんたいい」とある。) — The photo caption reads “army captain.”
- 小説では、陸軍の中尉が大尉に昇進した。 (しょうせつでは、りくぐんのちゅういがたいいにしょうしんした。) — In the novel, an army first lieutenant was promoted to captain.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) is たい in たいい; [尉](../kanji/尉.md) is い. Say all three morae: た・い・い. The family also records だいい, used in the former Imperial Japanese Navy.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[キャプテン](キャプテン.md) can be a sports team captain. 大尉 says what rank someone holds, not that they are the captain of a team or the person in charge of a particular ship.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the branch in view. The first example actually supplies 陸軍 (りくぐん), “army”; translating 大尉 as “captain” in every branch would lose information.
