# 必ず (かならず)

## Meaning

必ず means without fail, certainly, or without exception. It can state a reliable pattern or emphasize a firm instruction or promise.

- 寝る前に必ず歯を磨きます。 (ねるまえに かならず はを みがきます。) — I always brush my teeth before bed.
- 明日、必ず電話してください。 (あした、かならず でんわしてください。) — Please be sure to call tomorrow.

It is an adverb: place it with the predicate, without な or の.

## How the kanji combine

[必](../kanji/必.md) suggests certainty or necessity. Learn the complete native adverb かならず; its final ず is not a negative ending that learners should remove.

## Synonyms and antonyms

きっと expresses strong confidence or expectation. 必ず more directly stresses that something will not be omitted or has no exceptions. [多分](多分.md), "probably," is less certain.

## Distinctive meaning

必ず does not by itself translate the obligation "must" in every sentence. Also learn 必ずしも〜ない as a separate pattern meaning "not necessarily"; it does not mean "certainly not."
