# 入口 (いりぐち) — entrance

## Meaning

入口 is where you go in: a door, a gate, a cave mouth, and figuratively the starting point of a subject.

- 入口で靴を脱ぐ — take shoes off at the entrance
- 駅の入口 — the station entrance
- 研究の入口 — the way in to a field of research

Also written 入り口.

## How the kanji combine

[入](../kanji/入.md) (go in) plus [口](../kanji/口.md) (opening; ぐち). 入り (いり) is the stem/noun form of 入る (いる), "go in", an older verb retained in literary language and fixed expressions such as 気に入る (きにいる, like something). It is not 居る (いる), "be; exist". The compound can be understood as "the opening of going in". Antonym: [出口](出口.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [出口](出口.md) でぐち (exit).
- Related: 玄関 げんかん (a building's main entrance or entrance hall), [出入口](出入口.md) (combined entrance and exit), 入場口 にゅうじょうぐち (admission gate).
- Figurative: きっかけ (opportunity or trigger), 入門 にゅうもん (introduction, a beginner course).

## Distinctive meaning

The reading taught here is いりぐち. JMdict also records はいりぐち, so it is not an impossible reading;
learn いりぐち for this card rather than deriving it mechanically from はいる. 入口 is the plain word,
while 玄関 means a building's main entrance or entrance hall, including public buildings.
In a Japanese home, it typically includes the area where shoes are removed; shoe removal is not part of the word's definition.
