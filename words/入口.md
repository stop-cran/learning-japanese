# 入口 (いりぐち) — entrance

## Meaning

入口 is where you go in: a door, a gate, a cave mouth, and figuratively the starting point of a subject.

- 入口で靴を脱ぐ — take shoes off at the entrance
- 駅の入口 — the station entrance
- 研究の入口 — the way in to a field of research

Also written 入り口.

## How the kanji combine

[入](../kanji/入.md) (go in; いり, the noun form of いる／はいる) plus [口](../kanji/口.md) (opening; ぐち). In the noun 入り (いり), the verb becomes a noun form, so the
word is literally "the opening of going in". Antonym: [出口](出口.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [出口](出口.md) でぐち (exit).
- Related: 玄関 げんかん (the formal entrance of a house), [出入口](出入口.md) (combined entrance and exit), 入場口 にゅうじょうぐち (admission gate).
- Figurative: きっかけ (opportunity or trigger), 入門 にゅうもん (introduction, a beginner course).

## Distinctive meaning

Reading: 入口 is いりぐち, not はいりぐち, even though the verb is はいる. 入口 is the plain word,
while 玄関 specifically means a house's main entrance with its footwear area.
