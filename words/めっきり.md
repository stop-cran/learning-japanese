# めっきり - noticeably; considerably

## Meaning

めっきり is an adverb emphasizing a readily perceptible degree,
especially a noticeable change. It can occur with or without と.

- 朝晩はめっきり涼しくなりました。 (あさばんはめっきりすずしくなりました。) - The mornings and evenings have become noticeably cooler.
- 最近は電話がめっきり減りました。 (さいきんはでんわがめっきりへりました。) - There have been considerably fewer phone calls lately.

めっきりと涼しくなった (めっきりとすずしくなった), "became noticeably
cooler," illustrates the optional と.

## How the kanji combine

This word is written in kana. Keep the small っ: it is めっきり, not めきり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[かなり](かなり.md) and [ずいぶん](ずいぶん.md) also express a
considerable degree. めっきり often draws attention to a change that
can clearly be felt; that tendency does not make the other adverbs unusable
with changes.

## Distinctive meaning

This is an adverb, not an adjective meaning "noticeable."
Its frequent association with change is useful, but not an absolute
requirement: the dictionary also records a sufficiently large amount or
degree. It does not mean that the change was necessarily sudden.
