# 幻 (まぼろし) — illusion; fleeting dream; fabled rarity

## Meaning

幻 can be something you seem to see that is not really there. It also describes something that vanishes like a dream or is so rare that people doubt its existence.

- 霧の中に人影が見えたが、幻だった。 (きりのなかにひとかげがみえたが、まぼろしだった。) — I saw a figure in the mist, but it was an illusion.
- 写真家は、ついに幻の鳥を撮影した。 (しゃしんかは、ついにまぼろしのとりをさつえいした。) — The photographer finally captured the elusive bird on camera.

## How the kanji combine

[幻](../kanji/幻.md) writes the whole word まぼろし. The same character is read げん in 幻想 (げんそう), “fantasy; illusion.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

[夢](夢.md) (ゆめ) can be a dream during sleep, or a hope for the future. Seeing a 幻 does not require the observer to be asleep. [空想](空想.md) (くうそう) is a fantasy one pictures in the mind.

## Distinctive meaning

A figure seen in the mist may turn out never to have been there. The bird in the second example is real: people call it 幻の鳥 because so few have seen it.
