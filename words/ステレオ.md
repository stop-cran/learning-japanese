# ステレオ - stereo sound or system

## Meaning

ステレオ means stereo sound and can also name a stereo player or sound system. Context distinguishes the audio format from the equipment.

- この曲はステレオで録音されています。 (このきょくはステレオでろくおんされています。) - This piece is recorded in stereo.
- 父は古いステレオを使っています。 (ちちはふるいステレオをつかっています。) - My father uses an old stereo system.

The first sentence concerns the recording format; the second clearly names a device.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword corresponds to English "stereo." There are no kanji components. The sequence is ス・テ・レ・オ, without a long-vowel mark.

## Synonyms and antonyms

モノラル, "monaural," contrasts with stereo as a sound format. オーディオ is broader audio-related vocabulary and can concern equipment as a whole, not just stereo reproduction.

## Distinctive meaning

ステレオ can modify another word in compounds, as in ステレオ放送 (ステレオほうそう), "stereo broadcasting." Do not assume that every use names a standalone music player; the same word may instead specify how the sound is recorded or reproduced.
