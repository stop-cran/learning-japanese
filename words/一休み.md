# 一休み (ひとやすみ) - short rest; breather

## Meaning

一休み is a short rest or breather.
一休みする is an intransitive expression: pause an activity and rest for a bit.

- ここで一休みしましょう。 (ここでひとやすみしましょう。) - Let's take a short rest here.
- 掃除の途中で一休みしました。 (そうじのとちゅうでひとやすみしました。) - I took a breather in the middle of cleaning.

The word suggests a break, not a specified number of minutes.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) contributes ひと and
[休](../kanji/休.md)み is the native rest noun.
Together they present a short bout of resting.
Learn ひとやすみ, not an on-reading combination.
一やすみ and ひと休み are recognized alternative spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[休憩](休憩.md) (きゅうけい) is a break or rest period.
[休息](休息.md) (きゅうそく) also concerns resting.
[休む](休む.md) (やすむ) is broader: it can mean resting, sleeping,
or being absent. [続ける](続ける.md) (つづける), continue, contrasts
with interrupting the activity for a breather.

## Distinctive meaning

一休み does not by itself mean a holiday, a whole day off, or retirement.
The first example invites a pause in the present activity; it does not
tell the listener to quit permanently.
In 一休みしてから (ひとやすみしてから), "after taking a short rest,"
the して form connects the rest to what follows.
