# 膨大 (ぼうだい) - huge; vast

## Meaning

膨大 describes an enormous size, extent or amount. As a な-adjective, use
膨大な + noun and 膨大です as a polite predicate.
膨大になる means to become enormous.

- 膨大な量の資料を読みました。 (ぼうだいなりょうのしりょうをよみました。) - I read an enormous amount of material.
- 必要な費用は膨大です。 (ひつようなひようはぼうだいです。) - The required cost is enormous.

## How the kanji combine

膨 contributes swelling/expansion, and [大](../kanji/大.md) largeness.
ボウ + ダイ gives ぼうだい. This association does not make the adjective a
claim that something must visibly be swelling now.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[莫大](莫大.md) (ばくだい) also describes enormous amounts.
[巨大](巨大.md) (きょだい) emphasizes immense size;
[大きい](大きい.md) (おおきい) is the broader "big" adjective.
膨大 often makes the scale striking, rather than merely saying something is large.
Compare [広々](広々.md) (ひろびろ) for a spacious, open feel rather than
sheer size or amount. Broad English descriptions of extent can overlap,
but these are not unrestricted substitutes.

## Distinctive meaning

膨大 also has a noun/intransitive する sense, swelling or expansion: something
becoming larger, rather than only the adjectival state. For straightforward
everyday "swell/expand," compare [膨らむ](膨らむ.md) (ふくらむ).
Recognize rare 厖大 and 尨大 as adjective spellings; the separate swelling/
expansion verb sense belongs to 膨大, not automatically to those spellings.
