# ダイヤ (ダイヤ) - diamond; railway timetable

## Meaning

ダイヤ represents two distinct words with the same pronunciation. One means a
diamond, shortened from ダイヤモンド. The other means a railway schedule or
timetable, and can also abbreviate "diagram." Both are nouns, but their contexts
are different.

- この指輪には小さなダイヤがついています。 (このゆびわにはちいさなダイヤがついています。) - This ring has a small diamond in it.
- 雪のため、電車のダイヤが乱れています。 (ゆきのため、でんしゃのダイヤがみだれています。) - The train timetable is disrupted because of snow.

ダイヤの指輪 (ダイヤのゆびわ) is a diamond ring; ダイヤの改正
(ダイヤのかいせい) is a timetable revision.

## How the kanji combine

These are katakana forms, not kanji compounds. The jewelry word is an abbreviation
of ダイヤモンド. The railway word comes from the "dia-" part of English
"diagram." The shared short form does not make train schedules a sense of the
diamond entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ダイヤモンド names the stone without abbreviation. 時刻表 (じこくひょう) is
a timetable listing departure and arrival times; railway ダイヤ can refer to
the operating schedule those times describe.
Neither meaning has a useful everyday antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Look for the topic: jewelry favors the diamond word, and trains or service
disruptions favor the timetable word. Do not infer a connection between the
objects from the shared spelling. The English occupation "dyer" is not a
supported meaning of either entry.
