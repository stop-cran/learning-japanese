# 掴む (つかむ) - grasp; seize

## Meaning

掴む is a transitive godan verb meaning "grasp," "grab," or "seize."
It also describes obtaining an opportunity or grasping an idea.
The thing physically or figuratively grasped takes を.

- 子供が母親の手を掴みました。 (こどもがははおやのてをつかみました。) - The child grabbed their mother's hand.
- 説明を聞いて、要点を掴みました。 (せつめいをきいて、ようてんをつかみました。) - I listened to the explanation and grasped the main point.

チャンスを掴む means "seize an opportunity." Useful forms are
掴みます, 掴んだ, 掴んで, and 掴まない.

## How the kanji combine

掴 contributes grasping. The native word is つかむ, with む
written as okurigana. The hand component is a useful visual cue
for the physical sense, but the word also has abstract uses.
JMdict marks it as usually written in kana: つかむ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[握る](握る.md) (にぎる) emphasizes gripping or clasping.
[捕まえる](捕まえる.md) (つかまえる) can mean catching someone
or something. [理解](理解.md) (りかい), with する, overlaps
with the understanding sense but not with grabbing a hand.

## Distinctive meaning

The physical and figurative uses share the idea of getting a hold
of something. This does not make 掴む a general replacement for
every English "get." Choose an object that fits grasping, seizing,
or understanding rather than translating the English verb alone.
