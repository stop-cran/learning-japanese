# 言い出す (いいだす) - begin to say; bring up; propose

## Meaning

言い出す means beginning to say something, bringing a matter up, or being the first
to suggest an idea. Use と for quoted words or を for the matter introduced.

- 彼は急に「帰りたい」と言い出しました。 (かれはきゅうに「かえりたい」といいだしました。) - He suddenly started saying that he wanted to go home.
- その話を言い出しにくかった。 (そのはなしをいいだしにくかった。) - It was hard to bring up that subject.
- 旅行の計画は姉が言い出しました。 (りょこうのけいかくはあねがいいだしました。) - My older sister was the one who first proposed the travel plan.

It is a transitive godan verb ending in す: 言い出します, 言い出さない and 言い出して.

## How the kanji combine

The stem 言い of [言う](言う.md) joins [出す](出す.md). [言](../kanji/言.md)
contributes saying; [出](../kanji/出.md) supports the onset or bringing-out sense
of this compound. The native reading is いいだす, not a pair of on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[提案](提案.md) (ていあん), "proposal; suggestion," overlaps with suggesting an idea.
[話す](話す.md) (はなす) broadly describes talking or speaking; it does not
necessarily identify who first introduced a matter.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every instance of saying something is 言い出す. The beginning, new topic or
first suggestion matters. The source gloss "break the ice" can describe a situation
where someone starts speaking, but the word does not always imply easing social tension.
