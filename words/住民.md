# 住民 (じゅうみん) - resident; inhabitant

## Meaning

住民 is a resident or the inhabitants of a place. It is a noun: 地域の住民 (ちいきのじゅうみん), "residents of the area." Japanese can use the same form for one resident or a whole local population.

- 近所の住民が公園を掃除しました。 (きんじょのじゅうみんがこうえんをそうじしました。) - Local residents cleaned the park.
- その建物には多くの住民がいます。 (そのたてものにはおおくのじゅうみんがいます。) - Many residents live in that building.

The place may be a district, municipality, or building, depending on context.

## How the kanji combine

[住](../kanji/住.md) contributes living or residing, and [民](../kanji/民.md) contributes people. The first element describes the people: those who reside in a place. The on readings are ジュウ + ミン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[国民](国民.md) (こくみん) concerns the people or citizens of a country. [市民](市民.md) (しみん) can identify citizens or inhabitants of a city. 住民 focuses on residence rather than automatically establishing nationality.

## Distinctive meaning

Living in an area and visiting it are different relationships. Someone passing through is not a 住民 just because they are physically present. Conversely, the English translation "citizen" must not be used to infer a resident's legal citizenship: the word itself supplies the residence connection, not every legal qualification.
