# 鍵 (かぎ) — key

## Meaning

鍵 is a key used to open a lock. It can also refer to the lock itself in everyday expressions.

家の鍵をなくしました。（いえのかぎをなくしました。） — I lost my house key.

鍵をかけてください。（かぎをかけてください。） — Please lock the door.

## How the kanji combine

鍵 is one kanji read with the native word かぎ. It is not a compound, and its reading should not be divided into separate parts.

## Synonyms and antonyms

錠（じょう） specifically names a lock. 合鍵（あいかぎ） is a duplicate key. There is no ordinary direct antonym for the object 鍵.

## Distinctive meaning

鍵をかける means "lock," whereas 鍵を開ける（かぎをあける） means "unlock." 鍵 can also mean a clue or key to success, but it is not the usual word for a piano key or keyboard key.
