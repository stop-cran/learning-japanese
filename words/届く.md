# 届く (とどく) - reach; arrive; be delivered

## Meaning

届く is an intransitive godan verb meaning to reach a point, arrive, or be delivered.
The thing reaching or arriving takes が; the destination can take に or まで.
It can describe a hand's reach, the carrying of a voice, or the arrival of mail.

- 高い棚には手が届きません。
  (たかいたなにはてがとどきません。) - I cannot reach the high shelf.
- 昨日、友達から手紙が届きました。
  (きのう、ともだちからてがみがとどきました。)
  - A letter from a friend arrived yesterday.

Forms include 届きます, 届いた, 届いて, and 届かない.
In the first example, Japanese makes 手, "hand," the thing that does not reach.

## How the kanji combine

届 supplies the reaching or delivery meaning with the reading とど.
く completes the written native verb. There is no multi-kanji compound to split.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[届ける](届ける.md) (とどける) is transitive: someone delivers something.
Compare 手紙を届ける, "deliver a letter," with 手紙が届く, "a letter arrives."
[着く](着く.md) (つく) also means arrive, especially when describing someone's arrival at a destination.

## Distinctive meaning

届く focuses on reaching the endpoint, not on the sender's act of dispatch.
A letter can be sent without yet having 届いた.
Do not copy English "reach the shelf" as 棚を届く; use 棚に手が届く for physical reach.
