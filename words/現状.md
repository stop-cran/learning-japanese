# 現状 (げんじょう) - current state; status quo

## Meaning

現状 is the present state of affairs: how something stands now. It is a noun, with の connecting it to a following noun, as in 現状の問題点 (げんじょうのもんだいてん), "problems with the current situation."

- まず現状を確認しましょう。 (まずげんじょうをかくにんしましょう。) - First, let's check the current situation.
- 現状では、計画を変える必要があります。 (げんじょうでは、けいかくをかえるひつようがあります。) - Under the present circumstances, we need to change the plan.

現状を[維持](維持.md)する (げんじょうをいじする) means "maintain the status quo."

## How the kanji combine

[現](../kanji/現.md) contributes the present or actual, and [状](../kanji/状.md) contributes condition or state. The first character specifies which state: the one that currently exists. The readings are ゲン + ジョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[状態](状態.md) (じょうたい) also means condition or state, without itself specifying the present time. [現在](現在.md) (げんざい) identifies the present time rather than necessarily describing its circumstances.

## Distinctive meaning

現状 does not mean that the situation is good, bad, or permanent. A speaker may want to preserve it or change it. Similarly, 現状では limits a statement to current circumstances, leaving open the possibility that the answer could change if those circumstances change.
