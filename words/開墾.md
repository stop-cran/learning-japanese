# 開墾 (かいこん) — clearing land for cultivation

## Meaning

開墾 is turning previously uncultivated land into farmland. It names the change of use, not simply a farmer's work on a field each season.

- 村の人たちは荒れ地を開墾した。 (むらのひとたちはあれちをかいこんした。) — The villagers cleared uncultivated land for farming.
- 開墾した土地に麦をまいた。 (かいこんしたとちにむぎをまいた。) — They sowed wheat on land they had brought into cultivation.

## How the kanji combine

[開](../kanji/開.md) suggests opening something up. [墾](../kanji/墾.md) specifies what is being opened up: land for farming.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[耕す](耕す.md) (たがやす) means to till the soil. You can till an established field every spring without carrying out a new 開墾.

## Distinctive meaning

One dictionary gloss is “reclamation.” Here it means bringing land into agricultural use; it need not involve the sea. The examples concern making farmland where there was none before.
