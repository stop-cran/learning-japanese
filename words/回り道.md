# 回り道 (まわりみち) - detour; roundabout route

## Meaning

回り道 is a detour or roundabout route instead of the direct way. 回り道をする means to take such a route.

- 工事中なので、回り道をしました。 (こうじちゅうなので、まわりみちをしました。) - I took a detour because of construction work.
- 少し回り道になりますが、この道は静かです。 (すこしまわりみちになりますが、このみちはしずかです。) - It is a little out of the way, but this road is quiet.

It can also describe an indirect method or path toward a goal, not only a physical road.

## How the kanji combine

[回](../kanji/回.md) supplies まわり, "going around," and [道](../kanji/道.md) supplies みち, "way." Learn まわりみち as a native compound. まわり道 is another spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

寄り道 (よりみち) can involve stopping somewhere on the way and can also overlap with a detour; it is not limited to one rigid English translation. [道](道.md) (みち) is a way or road without necessarily implying a deviation.

## Distinctive meaning

A 回り道 need not be an accidental wrong turn. You may deliberately take it because a road is closed or to avoid traffic. It does not by itself say that you stopped at a shop or visited someone.
