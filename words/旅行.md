# 旅行 (りょこう) — travel, trip

## Meaning

旅行 is travel or a trip: 旅行に行く (go on a trip), 海外旅行 かいがいりょこう (overseas travel), 旅行する (to travel), 旅行者 りょこうしゃ (traveller), 修学旅行 しゅうがくりょこう (school trip).

## How the kanji combine

旅 (journey) plus [行](../kanji/行.md) (go): two near-synonyms, "journey" and "go", form a pair of the same kind. The readings are on (リョ + コウ), with 行 as コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 旅 たび (journey, more poetic), 出張 しゅっちょう (business trip), 観光 かんこう (sightseeing).
- Related: [行く](行く.md) いく (to go).

## Distinctive meaning

旅行 is the neutral word for travel in general. 旅 たび is more literary and often has a wandering or lonely feel (旅に出る), and 出張 is a trip for work.
