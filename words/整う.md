# 整う (ととのう) - be ready; be in order; be settled

## Meaning

整う describes something being ready, arranged, or in proper order.
It also covers a balanced or well-proportioned state, adjustment,
and negotiations or arrangements being settled.
It is an intransitive godan verb; what reaches the state takes が.

- 出発の準備が整いました。 (しゅっぱつのじゅんびがととのいました。) - The preparations for departure are complete.
- 彼女は整った顔立ちをしています。 (かのじょはととのったかおだちをしています。) - She has well-proportioned features.
- ようやく契約が整いました。 (ようやくけいやくがととのいました。) - The contract arrangements have finally been settled.

Forms include 整います, 整って, 整った, and 整わない.
整った before a noun describes its resulting ordered or balanced state.

## How the kanji combine

整 contributes putting into order, with the native verb ととのう.
There is no second written kanji to translate as a compound.
The transitive partner is 整える (ととのえる):
準備を整える means get the preparations ready.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[揃う](揃う.md) (そろう) overlaps when necessary things are all present or ready.
[できる](できる.md) and [出来上がる](出来上がる.md) (できあがる)
also have finished or ready uses.
[決まる](決まる.md) (きまる) overlaps with matters being settled,
but not every decision describes an orderly shape.

## Distinctive meaning

Adjustment, readiness, balanced form, and a settled agreement are related uses,
not a rule that every finished event is now tidy.
調う is another recognized spelling, especially associated with readiness
or arrangements in the public dictionary; this is a tendency, not a spelling ban.
Learn the intransitive pattern alongside 整える rather than treating them as interchangeable forms.
