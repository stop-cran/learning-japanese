# 間隔 (かんかく) - spacing; interval

## Meaning

間隔 is the space or interval between things or events.
Use XとYの間隔 for the interval between X and Y, or
N分間隔で for something occurring at intervals of N minutes.

- 椅子の間隔を広くしてください。
  (いすのかんかくをひろくしてください。) - Please leave more space between the chairs.
- バスは十分間隔で来ます。
  (バスはじゅっぷんかんかくできます。) - Buses come every ten minutes.

間隔を空ける (かんかくをあける) means to leave a gap or space things out.
The context tells you whether the separation is physical or temporal.

## How the kanji combine

[間](../kanji/間.md) contributes a space or interval, and 隔 separation.
Their related meanings reinforce the idea of a gap between things.
The on readings カン + カク give かんかく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[間](間.md) (あいだ; ま) is a broader word for a space or interval.
距離 (きょり), "distance," is useful for how far apart things are;
間隔 also readily describes repeated timing or spacing.
There is no single opposite covering every kind of interval.

## Distinctive meaning

[感覚](感覚.md) is also read かんかく, but means a sense or sensation.
間隔 concerns the separation, not the ability to perceive it.
In computing, the dictionary also records a space character or whitespace;
the everyday chair and bus examples do not depend on that technical use.
