# 飢える (うえる) - starve; hunger for

## Meaning

飢える means to be hungry, famished, or starving. It can also describe
being starved of something figuratively, such as affection or knowledge.
It is an intransitive ichidan verb.

- 食べ物がなく、人々は飢えていました。 (たべものがなく、ひとびとはうえていました。) - There was no food, and the people were starving.
- その子は愛情に飢えています。 (そのこはあいじょうにうえています。) - That child is starved of affection.

In the figurative construction, に marks what is keenly lacking.
Forms include 飢えます, 飢えて, and 飢えない.

## How the kanji combine

飢 has hunger associations and writes う in this verb;
える is the written ending. For conjugation, drop the final る and
keep うえ-, as in うえます. 餓える is another dictionary spelling,
and 饑える is marked rare. The dictionary also lists かつえる,
restricted to 飢える and 餓える, not 饑える.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お腹が[空く](空く.md) (おなかがすく) is useful for everyday
"become hungry." お腹が[ぺこぺこ](ぺこぺこ.md) describes strong hunger
in a conversational way. 満腹 (まんぷく), "a full stomach," contrasts
with the physical hunger use, not every figurative lack.

## Distinctive meaning

The examples illustrate serious deprivation and strong figurative need.
The dictionary's broader "be hungry" gloss does not mean that every
ordinary lunchtime statement should use this word.
[植える](植える.md) also reads うえる but means "plant something";
the spelling and meaning are unrelated to hunger.
