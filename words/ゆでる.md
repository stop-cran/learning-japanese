# ゆでる - boil food in water

## Meaning

ゆでる means to boil something, typically food, in hot water.
It is a transitive ichidan verb: ゆでます, ゆでた, ゆでて,
ゆでない. The food takes を.

- 卵を十分ゆでました。 (たまごをじゅっぷんゆでました。) - I boiled the eggs for ten minutes.
- ほうれん草をゆでて、水にさらします。 (ほうれんそうをゆでて、みずにさらします。) - Boil the spinach, then rinse it in cold water.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses the kana spelling ゆでる.
茹でる is the recognized kanji spelling; the dictionary also lists
the rare spelling 煠でる and the alternative reading うでる.
These are not separate N2 assignments.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[煮る](煮る.md) (にる) overlaps in boiling or simmering, often with
the cooking liquid contributing to the finished dish.
[沸かす](沸かす.md) (わかす) heats a liquid until it boils;
[沸く](沸く.md) (わく) describes the liquid boiling.
[蒸す](蒸す.md) (むす) cooks with steam, while
[焼く](焼く.md) (やく) uses a different heating method.

## Distinctive meaning

The practical distinction is 卵をゆでる, "boil eggs," versus
お湯を沸かす (おゆをわかす), "boil water."
ゆでる and 煮る are not separated by an absolute "water only versus
seasoning" rule: their cooking uses overlap.
A separate dictionary use is treating a swelling or similar problem
with medicinal steam. It does not make ordinary food ゆでる
synonymous with 蒸す.
