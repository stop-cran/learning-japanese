# ぐるぐる — round and round; winding around

## Meaning

ぐるぐる describes repeated turning, going in circles, or winding something around something else.

- 犬が木の周りをぐるぐる回っている。 — The dog is going round and round the tree. (周り: まわり; 回る: まわる.)
- 箱にひもをぐるぐると巻いた。 — I wound string around the box several times. (巻く: まく.)

Use it directly with the action verb or with と. 頭の中で同じ考えがぐるぐる回る means that the same thought keeps circling in one's head.

## How the kanji combine

This native kana expression depicts repeated motion, not necessarily an audible noise. Its reduplicated form fits the repeated circuits or coils; the syllables are not separate kanji meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くるくる](くるくる.md) overlaps in rotation and winding, often presenting a lighter or nimbler movement. [ころころ](ころころ.md) particularly pictures rolling. These are tendencies, not rules based on an object's exact weight.

## Distinctive meaning

ぐるぐる can suggest circling without making progress, but frustration is not built into every use. With 巻く, the winding interpretation comes from the whole phrase. Learn ぐるぐる回る and ぐるぐる巻く rather than assuming that every mimetic freely takes する or な.
