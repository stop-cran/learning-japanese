# すたすた — walking briskly

## Meaning

すたすた describes brisk walking with little hesitation, often giving an impression that the walker knows where they are going.

- 彼女は駅に向かってすたすた歩いていった。 — She walked briskly toward the station. (向かう: むかう.)
- 先生は質問に答えると、すたすたと教室を出た。 — After answering the question, the teacher strode out of the classroom. (教室: きょうしつ.)

Use it with a walking or departure verb: すたすた歩く or すたすたと歩く. It is not an all-purpose substitute for "quickly."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とぼとぼ](とぼとぼ.md) contrasts with slow, heavy, often dejected steps. [のろのろ](のろのろ.md) describes sluggish progress more broadly. [うろうろ](うろうろ.md) suggests wandering rather than heading straight onward.

## Distinctive meaning

すたすた can imply that someone walks off without waiting for others, but rudeness is not inherent in the word. Nor does it necessarily mean running. Learn it as a description of a brisk gait rather than automatically adding する or な.
