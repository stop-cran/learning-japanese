# 支度 (したく) — preparations; getting ready

## Meaning

支度 is preparation, especially getting oneself or everyday things ready. It forms a する verb. Use noun + の支度 for preparations for something.

- 旅行の支度をしています。 (りょこうのしたくをしています。) — I'm getting ready for a trip.
- 朝ご飯の支度をしました。 (あさごはんのしたくをしました。) — I prepared breakfast.

支度する and 支度をする both occur; 身支度 (みじたく) specifically refers to getting oneself dressed and ready.

## How the kanji combine

[支](../kanji/支.md) and [度](../kanji/度.md) form the established word したく. The reading and meaning should be learned together rather than inferred from the separate characters. 仕度 is another recognized spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

準備 (じゅんび), "preparation," is broader and also covers plans, equipment, and large projects. 支度 is particularly useful for meals, clothing, and departure.

## Distinctive meaning

支度 is not simply a synonym for cooking. 出かける支度 (でかけるしたく), "getting ready to go out," may include dressing or packing. The noun or situation supplies what is being prepared.
