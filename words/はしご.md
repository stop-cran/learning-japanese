# はしご - ladder; visiting places in succession

## Meaning

はしご is a ladder: equipment with rungs for climbing to a higher place.
The dictionary also records stairs, and a separate use for visiting several
places in succession, including bar-hopping.

- はしごを使って屋根に上りました。 (はしごをつかってやねにのぼりました。) - I used a ladder to climb onto the roof.
- 友達と居酒屋を三軒はしごしました。 (ともだちといざかやをさんげんはしごしました。) - I went to three pubs in succession with friends.

はしごする has both transitive and intransitive uses.
In the second example, the places visited take を.
The first example concerns the physical tool, not three successive venues.

## How the kanji combine

JMdict marks はしご as usually written in kana. 梯子 is a recognized spelling
at the source reading はしご; 梯 and 階子 are also recorded, with rarity notes.
Learn the whole native word rather than reading it mechanically as on-reading ていし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[階段](階段.md) (かいだん) is stairs or a staircase.
The recorded stairs use of はしご overlaps; it does not make every staircase
the same object as a portable ladder. 段 (だん) can count individual steps.
There is no useful general antonym covering a ladder and venue-hopping alike.

## Distinctive meaning

The successive-venue use is not limited to alcohol.
The Japanese dictionary gives 映画のはしご (えいがのはしご), going to successive
films, as an example. In either setting the point is moving through multiple
visits or activities, not merely walking on a ladder.
