# 補償 (ほしょう) - compensation for loss

## Meaning

補償 is compensation that makes good a loss. It is a noun and forms a
transitive verb with する. With 補償する, the loss being compensated for takes を.

- 会社は事故による損害を補償しました。 (かいしゃはじこによるそんがいをほしょうしました。) - The company compensated for the damage caused by the accident.
- 私たちは補償を求めています。 (わたしたちはほしょうをもとめています。) - We are seeking compensation.

In the second example, 補償 is the noun object of 求める (もとめる), "seek."
Seeking compensation does not mean that it has already been paid.

## How the kanji combine

[補](../kanji/補.md) contributes supplying what is missing;
[償](../kanji/償.md) contributes recompense or making good a loss.
The near-synonymous parts reinforce the idea of making up for a loss.
補 supplies ホ and 償 supplies ショウ in ほしょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[償う](償う.md) (つぐなう) can mean compensating for a loss or making amends.
[補う](補う.md) (おぎなう) is broader: it can also mean supplementing an explanation.
[保証](保証.md) (ほしょう), "guarantee; warranty," is a different word despite
the shared pronunciation. A promise or warranty is not itself compensation paid for a loss.
There is no single everyday antonym covering every compensation context.

## Distinctive meaning

This article focuses on practical compensation for loss, not the separate
psychological or technical uses. Legal sources can distinguish particular
types of 補償 from damages, but ordinary use can be broader; do not infer
that 補償 always excludes damages or automatically establishes someone's liability.
Whether compensation is owed or paid depends on the actual circumstances and conditions.
