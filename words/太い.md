# 太い (ふとい) — thick; stout

## Meaning

太い means thick, stout, or fat in cross-section, especially for something elongated such as a rope, tree trunk, line, or body part.

- この木の幹は太いです。 (このきのみきはふといです。) — This tree's trunk is thick.
- 太い線を引いてください。 (ふといせんをひいてください。) — Please draw a thick line.

It is an i-adjective: 太くない (ふとくない), "not thick"; 太かった (ふとかった), "was thick."

## How the kanji combine

[太](../kanji/太.md) supplies ふと, and い is the adjective ending. The word is native, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

細い (ほそい), "thin; slender," is the direct contrast. 厚い (あつい) describes thickness through a layer or flat object, as in a thick book.

## Distinctive meaning

A 太い腕 (ふというで) is a thick or stout arm. For someone's overall build, 太っている (ふとっている) is the usual "be fat" expression, though personal comments can be insensitive. Do not use 太い for every English "thick": a thick book is 厚い本 (あついほん).
