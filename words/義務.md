# 義務 (ぎむ) - duty; obligation

## Meaning

義務 is a duty or obligation: something a person is required or expected to do under a rule, agreement, or moral responsibility. It is a noun. Put a plain verb before it to specify the action: 説明する義務, "an obligation to explain."

- 会社には安全を守る義務があります。 (かいしゃにはあんぜんをまもるぎむがあります。) - The company has a duty to protect safety.
- この活動への参加は義務ではありません。 (このかつどうへのさんかはぎむではありません。) - Participation in this activity is not compulsory.

義務を果たす (ぎむをはたす) means "fulfill one's duty." 義務がない means that there is no obligation, not that the action is forbidden.

## How the kanji combine

義 contributes what is right or owed, and [務](../kanji/務.md) contributes a task or duty to carry out. Together they identify work or conduct one is obliged to undertake. The on readings are ギ + ム.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[責任](責任.md) (せきにん) is responsibility or accountability and can concern the consequences of an action. 義務 focuses on what must be done. [権利](権利.md) (けんり), "right; entitlement," is a paired social concept, not a universal antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

義務 is stronger than a personal preference or casual plan. It does not, by itself, say who imposes the obligation or what happens if it is ignored. Name the rule or relationship when that matters; the word alone does not establish a legal requirement.
