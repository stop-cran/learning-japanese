# 始終 (しじゅう) - all the time; from beginning to end

## Meaning

始終 works as an adverb for something happening throughout a period or
all the time. It can also emphasize frequent, habitual recurrence:
"all the time" need not mean that an action literally occupies every second.
Put it before the predicate without に.

- 会議の間、彼女は始終笑顔でした。 (かいぎのあいだ、かのじょはしじゅうえがおでした。) - She was smiling throughout the meeting.
- 彼は始終仕事のことを考えています。 (かれはしじゅうしごとのことをかんがえています。) - He is thinking about work all the time.

The first gives a bounded period. The second can describe a recurring habit.

## How the kanji combine

[始](../kanji/始.md) contributes beginning and [終](../kanji/終.md) ending.
Their combination helps recall the span from start to finish.
The on readings are シ + シュウ; 終's シュウ is voiced to ジュウ here.
Do not turn the character image into a ban on habitual-frequency uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずっと](ずっと.md) can emphasize continuity through a period.
[いつも](いつも.md) and [常に](常に.md) (つねに) overlap in "always."
[しばしば](しばしば.md) and [たびたび](たびたび.md) foreground recurring occasions.
始終 does not specify an exact number or interval.

## Distinctive meaning

始終 also occurs as a noun for the full course of events:
事件の始終 (じけんのしじゅう), "the course of the incident from beginning to end."
The related noun 一部始終 (いちぶしじゅう) means "the whole story; all the details."
These noun uses are not an instruction to use 始終する for "begin and end."
This card focuses on the adverb, without treating all noun uses as historical.
