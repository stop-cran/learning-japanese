# 青 (あお) - blue

## Meaning

青 names the color blue. It is a noun: use 青です to identify a color and 青の before a noun.

このペンの色は青です。 (このペンのいろはあおです。) - This pen's color is blue.

青のシャツを買いました。 (あおのシャツをかいました。) - I bought a blue shirt.

## How the kanji combine

[青](../kanji/青.md) represents the whole native word あお. There is no compound to divide. The related adjective is 青い (あおい).

## Synonyms and antonyms

ブルー is a borrowed word for blue. 青い describes something as blue directly: 青い空 (あおいそら), "a blue sky." 赤 (あか), "red," is another color, not a universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

青 also occurs for things English calls green, especially traffic lights and some plant expressions. 信号が青です (しんごうがあおです) means "The light is green." This conventional usage does not make 青 a general replacement for 緑 (みどり), "green."
