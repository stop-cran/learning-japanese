# 確率 (かくりつ) - probability; likelihood

## Meaning

確率 is the probability or likelihood of an event. A clause can identify the event directly: 成功する確率 (せいこうするかくりつ), the probability of succeeding. 高い and 低い describe high and low probability.

- このくじで当たる確率は低いです。 (このくじであたるかくりつはひくいです。) - The probability of winning this lottery is low.
- 成功する確率を計算しました。 (せいこうするかくりつをけいさんしました。) - We calculated the probability of success.

## How the kanji combine

[確](../kanji/確.md) contributes certainty or assurance, and 率 a rate or proportion. The compound concerns how likely something is, not an assertion that the outcome is certain. The on readings are カク + リツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

可能性 (かのうせい), possibility or likelihood, and 見込み (みこみ), prospect or likelihood, overlap in suitable contexts. [機会](機会.md) (きかい) and [チャンス](チャンス.md) can instead mean an opportunity: having a chance to try and having a high chance of success are different questions.

## Distinctive meaning

確率 does not have to be high, and a likely result is not guaranteed. Keep the homophone 確立 (かくりつ), establishment, separate: 確立する is not the verb for calculating a probability. The character 確 alone cannot choose between these words.
