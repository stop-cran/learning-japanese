# 時速 (じそく) - speed per hour

## Meaning

時速 is speed expressed as distance per hour.
The distance unit still needs to be stated or understood:
時速六十キロ (じそくろくじゅっキロ) means sixty kilometres per hour.
時速六十キロで走る means "travel at sixty kilometres per hour."

- この車は時速六十キロで走っています。 (このくるまはじそくろくじゅっキロではしっています。) - This car is traveling at sixty kilometres per hour.
- 風の速さを時速二十マイルで表しました。 (かぜのはやさをじそくにじゅうマイルであらわしました。) - We expressed the wind's speed as twenty miles per hour.

The second example uses miles, not kilometres.
で marks the speed at which something moves or the numerical expression used.

## How the kanji combine

[時](../kanji/時.md) contributes the hour unit, and
[速](../kanji/速.md) contributes speed: speed per hour.
The on readings combine as ジ + ソク.
Do not read 時 here as the standalone native とき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[速度](速度.md) (そくど) is speed more generally.
秒速 (びょうそく) expresses distance per second, and 分速 (ふんそく)
distance per minute. They can describe the same motion in different units,
not opposing kinds of movement.

## Distinctive meaning

時速 is a rate, not the distance already traveled.
A vehicle need not travel for a full hour before its speed can be
expressed this way. Nor does the word itself mean "fast":
a low speed can also be stated as a 時速.
