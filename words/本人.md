# 本人 (ほんにん) - the person concerned

## Meaning

本人 is the person in question or the person themselves, rather than someone speaking or acting on their behalf. It is a noun and does not specify gender. The surrounding conversation identifies whose identity, wishes, or actions are at issue.

- 詳しいことは本人に聞いてください。 (くわしいことはほんにんにきいてください。) - Please ask the person themselves for the details.
- 申込書には本人が署名してください。 (もうしこみしょにはほんにんがしょめいしてください。) - The applicant themselves should sign the application form.

本人の希望 (ほんにんのきぼう) means "the wishes of the person concerned."

## How the kanji combine

[本](../kanji/本.md) contributes the actual or original subject, and [人](../kanji/人.md) contributes person. The compound points back to the actual person at issue, not to a representative.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[自分](自分.md) (じぶん) means oneself, with its reference depending on the sentence. 本人 identifies the person concerned and can refer to someone other than the speaker. A 代理人 (だいりにん), "representative/proxy," is a useful contrasting role.

## Distinctive meaning

本人確認 (ほんにんかくにん) is identity verification: checking that someone is the person they claim to be. 本人 does not mean "I" by default. It can also contrast a person's own view with what family members, colleagues, or officials have said about them.
