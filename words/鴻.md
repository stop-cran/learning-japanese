# 鴻 (ひしくい) — bean goose

## Meaning

鴻, read **ひしくい**, names the **bean goose**, a real waterbird.
The word is usually written **ヒシクイ** or **ひしくい**. Learn 鴻 as a
documented kanji spelling, not as the spelling required in a bird guide.

- 湖でヒシクイを見ました。 (みずうみでひしくいをみました。) — I saw a bean goose at the lake.
- 図鑑でヒシクイを調べました。 (ずかんでひしくいをしらべました。) — I looked up bean geese in an illustrated reference book.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun: [鴻](../kanji/鴻.md) writes **ひしくい**
as a whole. It is not a compound to split into separate character readings.
The on reading コウ belongs to other words, such as [鴻鵠](鴻鵠.md) (こうこく).
Writing this native animal name in katakana does not make it a loanword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鳥](鳥.md) (とり), "bird," and [動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," are
broader categories. 雁 (がん／かり), "wild goose," is broader than this bird name.
[鶴](鶴.md) (つる), "crane," and [鷹](鷹.md) (たか), "hawk; falcon,"
name other birds, not interchangeable species labels. There is no useful antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The same 鴻 spelling also has documented おおとり and おおがり readings.
おおとり concerns large birds; おおがり is another name for ヒシクイ and,
in a separate dictionary sense, ガチョウ (domestic goose).
Those readings do not make every use of 鴻 mean a bean goose.

This article teaches the **ひしくい** noun. Usually-kana spelling does not
mean the pronunciation is rare. The English gloss is a practical bird-name
anchor, not a lesson in current Latin taxonomy.
