# 相談 (そうだん) — consultation; asking for advice

## Meaning

相談 is talking something over or asking someone for advice. Add する to make the verb.
Two useful frames are person + に + 相談する for consulting someone, and person + と + 相談する for discussing something together.

- 進路について先生に相談しました。 (しんろについてせんせいにそうだんしました。) — I consulted my teacher about my future course of study or career.
- 旅行の計画を家族と相談します。 (りょこうのけいかくをかぞくとそうだんします。) — I will discuss the travel plans with my family.

The topic can be marked with について, "about," or appear as an を-object, as in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

[相](../kanji/相.md) contributes an interpersonal or mutual association, and [談](../kanji/談.md) contributes talking or discussion.
The combination is an exchange about a matter, not just the physical act of speaking.
The on readings are ソウ + ダン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[質問](質問.md) しつもん is a question or inquiry. Asking a specific factual question can be part of a consultation,
but 相談 often involves considering what to do.
[話す](話す.md) はなす is broader: people can talk without seeking advice or reaching a decision.

## Distinctive meaning

相談 does not require the participants to have equal expertise, nor does it guarantee that they agree.
先生に相談する can put a learner in the advice-seeking role.
For practical use, remember the topic and the person separately rather than translating the English "consult someone" word for word with を.
