# 黒板 (こくばん) - blackboard

## Meaning

黒板 is a blackboard or chalkboard. It is a noun naming the classroom object, not the writing on it.

- 先生が黒板に名前を書きました。 (せんせいがこくばんになまえをかきました。) - The teacher wrote a name on the blackboard.
- 黒板の字が小さくて読めません。 (こくばんのじがちいさくてよめません。) - The writing on the blackboard is too small for me to read.

Use 黒板に書く for writing on its surface, and 黒板の字 for the characters written there. に marks the writing destination, not the instrument.

## How the kanji combine

[黒](../kanji/黒.md) contributes black, and 板 contributes board. This is a modifier-plus-head compound: a black board. The on readings are コク + バン. The separate native noun [板](板.md) is read いた.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ホワイトボード is a whiteboard, normally written on with markers rather than chalk. It is a comparable classroom object, not an antonym formed by reversing the word's meaning. 板 by itself is much broader than either kind of writing board.

## Distinctive meaning

黒板 names a kind of object, so a green chalkboard can still be called 黒板. Do not use the literal color components as a rule that the surface must be black. In 黒板を消す (こくばんをけす), classroom context normally means erasing its writing, not making the board disappear.
