# 不思議 (ふしぎ) - mysterious; strange

## Meaning

不思議 is a na-adjective and noun for something mysterious, strange, or marvelous. It expresses wonder or puzzlement rather than simply saying that something is bad.

- 昨日、不思議な夢を見ました。 (きのう、ふしぎなゆめをみました。) - I had a strange dream yesterday.
- 不思議と今日は眠くありません。 (ふしぎときょうはねむくありません。) - Strangely enough, I am not sleepy today.

Use 不思議な before a noun and 不思議だ / 不思議です as a predicate. The second example uses the adverbial **不思議と**, "strangely enough; for some reason."

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) contributes negation, [思](../kanji/思.md) thought, and [議](../kanji/議.md) discussion or consideration. As a learning association, the whole suggests something that resists ordinary explanation. This is a guide to the meaning, not a historical derivation from three English glosses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[変](変.md) (へん), "odd; strange," overlaps but can more directly suggest that something is unusual or wrong. 不思議 can express fascination as well as suspicion. A predictable event offers a contextual contrast, not a fixed dictionary antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "curiosity" is ambiguous. 不思議 can describe a curious or puzzling thing, but it does not simply name someone's desire to learn. Likewise, 不思議と introduces an unexpectedly true observation; the speaker need not be claiming a supernatural explanation.
