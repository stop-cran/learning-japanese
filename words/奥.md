# 奥 (おく) - inner part; depths

## Meaning

奥 is the inner or farther part of a space: the back of a shop, the depths of a forest, or the inside of a drawer away from its opening. Use Nの奥 for "the inner part of N."

- 店の奥に席があります。 (みせのおくにせきがあります。) - There are seats at the back of the shop.
- 大切な手紙を引き出しの奥にしまいました。 (たいせつなてがみをひきだしのおくにしまいました。) - I put the important letter away at the back of the drawer.

奥に locates something or gives its destination; 奥で locates an activity.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 奥 represents this whole noun, read おく. Its core image is an interior reached by going farther in, not a fixed compass direction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中](中.md) (なか) broadly means "inside" or "middle." 奥 draws attention to depth or distance from an entrance or viewpoint. 手前 (てまえ), "this side; the nearer part," is a useful spatial contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

奥 is not simply the area outside and behind a building: 店の奥 normally directs you farther inside the shop. The idea also extends to hidden feelings: 心の奥 (こころのおく), "deep in one's heart." This figurative use concerns inner thoughts rather than a physical room.
