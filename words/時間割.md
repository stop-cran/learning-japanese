# 時間割 (じかんわり) - timetable; class schedule

## Meaning

時間割 is a timetable, especially one showing the sequence of school lessons. It is a noun: 時間割を作る means to make a timetable.

- 明日の時間割を確認しました。 (あしたのじかんわりをかくにんしました。) - I checked tomorrow's class schedule.
- 時間割では、一時間目は数学です。 (じかんわりでは、いちじかんめはすうがくです。) - According to the timetable, the first lesson is mathematics.

## How the kanji combine

[時間](時間.md) じかん supplies time, using [時](../kanji/時.md) ジ and [間](../kanji/間.md) カン. [割](../kanji/割.md) わり contributes dividing or allotting: allocating time to activities.

This is an on-first mixed reading, grouped under kango here, not three on readings. 時間割り is another dictionary spelling of the same noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予定](予定.md) よてい names a planned arrangement or schedule more broadly. 時刻表 (じこくひょう) lists times, especially for transport; [ダイヤ](ダイヤ.md) can mean a railway timetable. A school 時間割 tells you which lesson occupies each period.

## Distinctive meaning

時間割 is not the length of time itself. 一時間 (いちじかん) means one hour, while 一時間目 (いちじかんめ) in a school schedule means the first lesson period, not necessarily a full sixty minutes.
