# 人差し指 (ひとさしゆび) - index finger; forefinger

## Meaning

人差し指 is the index finger, between the thumb and middle finger.

- 右手の人差し指でボタンを押しました。 (みぎてのひとさしゆびでボタンをおしました。) - I pressed the button with my right index finger.
- 人差し指をけがしました。 (ひとさしゆびをけがしました。) - I injured my index finger.

The dictionary also records the second toe, counting from the big toe.
That is an additional recognition sense, not a separate N2 assignment.
The hand is intended in the first example.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md), ひと, combines with 差し, さし, and
[指](../kanji/指.md), ゆび.
[差](../kanji/差.md)し belongs to the pointing element here:
"the finger used for pointing" is a useful meaning association, not
an instruction to derive the whole word from English character glosses.
人指し指 is another dictionary spelling. The raw source's 人差指
is a search-only spelling in JMdict.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[指](指.md) (ゆび) is the broader finger/toe word.
[親指](親指.md) (おやゆび), thumb, and
[中指](中指.md) (なかゆび), middle finger, are adjacent digits.
They are not synonyms or antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

English finger numbering varies with whether the thumb is counted.
"Index finger" or its position between thumb and middle finger avoids
the ambiguity of a bare "first finger" or "second finger."
The word itself does not specify the right or left hand.
