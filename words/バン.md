# バン - three separate loanword identities

## Meaning

The source row combines three meanings that belong to separate dictionary entries, not three senses of one entry.

**Hair:** バン can name a bun hairstyle, with the hair gathered into a rounded arrangement. This is the supplied entry's meaning; its English "bun" does not mean a bread roll.

**Vehicle:** バン also means a van, including a delivery van.

- バンに荷物を積みました。 (バンににもつをつみました。) - We loaded the luggage into the van.

**Computing:** ＶＡＮ, also displayed as VAN, means "value-added network" and has バン among its readings. This is a technical network term, not a vehicle.

- この会社はＶＡＮを利用しています。 (このかいしゃはバンをりようしています。) - This company uses a value-added network.

## How the kanji combine

There are no kanji components in バン. Different source forms converge on the same katakana spelling: a hairstyle bun, a vehicle van, and the acronym VAN. The acronym also has the letter-name reading ブイ・エー・エヌ in the inspected entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[髪](髪.md) (かみ) supplies the hairstyle context; [自動車](自動車.md) (じどうしゃ) is the broader vehicle category. [パン](パン.md), "bread," begins with パ, not バ, and does not establish a food sense for this バン entry.
