# オーケストラ - orchestra

## Meaning

オーケストラ is an orchestra, the group of musicians who play
together. It is a noun, not the Japanese verb "perform."

- オーケストラが交響曲を演奏しました。
  (オーケストラがこうきょうきょくをえんそうしました。) - The orchestra performed a symphony.
- 姉はオーケストラでバイオリンを弾いています。
  (あねはオーケストラでバイオリンをひいています。) - My older sister plays the violin in an orchestra.

が identifies the performing group in the first example; で
identifies the group in which the sister plays in the second.

## How the kanji combine

This word is written entirely in katakana, with no kanji to divide.
The ー after オ marks a long vowel: オーケストラ, not オケストラ.
Learn the complete spelling with the noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

管弦楽団 (かんげんがくだん) is another word for an orchestra.
[演奏](演奏.md) (えんそう) is musical performance, the activity
rather than the group. [音楽](音楽.md) (おんがく) is music more
broadly; neither is simply a substitute name for the musicians.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not state the exact number of players or guarantee
a particular programme. Distinguish the orchestra from its concert,
the piece it performs, and the building where it plays.
In オーケストラの演奏, "the orchestra's performance," の
connects the performers with their performance.
