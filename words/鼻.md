# 鼻 (はな) - nose

## Meaning

鼻 is the nose. It is an ordinary noun for the body part in people and animals.

- 風邪で鼻がつまっています。 (かぜではながつまっています。) - My nose is blocked because of a cold.
- 象は鼻が長いです。 (ぞうははながながいです。) - Elephants have long trunks.

In the second sentence, Japanese uses 鼻 for an elephant's trunk even though English chooses a different noun. 鼻をかむ (はなをかむ) means "blow one's nose."

## How the kanji combine

鼻 is a single character writing the native word はな. There are no separate word components to combine. The same kana sequence also writes 花, "flower," but that is a different word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

口 (くち), "mouth," and 耳 (みみ), "ear," are neighboring body parts, not synonyms or opposites. 鼻水 (はなみず) refers to nasal mucus or a runny nose.

## Distinctive meaning

The sound はな alone does not distinguish a nose from a flower in writing; kanji and context do. Body-part sentences often use が for the affected part without adding an explicit possessive such as "my."
