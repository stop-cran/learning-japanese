# 立場 (たちば) - position; standpoint

## Meaning

立場 is a noun for someone's position or situation, including the standpoint from which they
understand an issue. It can concern responsibilities and circumstances rather than a physical place.
Use person + の立場 for "that person's position."

- 相手の立場で考えてみてください。 (あいてのたちばでかんがえてみてください。) - Try thinking from the other person's position.
- 私は意見を言える立場ではありません。 (わたしはいけんをいえるたちばではありません。) - I am not in a position to give an opinion.

The first example asks for a change of perspective. The second concerns the speaker's role or
circumstances, not their ability to form words.

## How the kanji combine

[立](../kanji/立.md) contributes standing, through the stem たち of [立つ](立つ.md) (たつ).
[場](../kanji/場.md) is ば, "place." The native compound evokes where one stands,
including a figurative position in a relationship or discussion.
It is read たちば, not an on-reading combination.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion: what someone thinks or says.
立場 concerns the position from which they speak and is not necessarily the opinion itself.
[場所](場所.md) (ばしょ) is a place; it does not generally replace 立場 in the examples.

## Distinctive meaning

立場 can explain why two people view the same event differently, even when neither has misunderstood
the facts. 親の立場から (おやのたちばから), "from a parent's standpoint," explicitly supplies a role.
The word alone does not tell you that someone's position is powerful or socially superior.
