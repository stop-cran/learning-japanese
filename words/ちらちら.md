# ちらちら — brief repeated glimpses; flickering

## Meaning

Something appearing and disappearing, repeated brief glances, or little flickering movements.

- 授業（じゅぎょう）中に時計（とけい）をちらちら見た。 — I kept glancing at the clock during class.
- 遠くで明かりがちらちらしていた。 — A light was flickering in the distance.
- 雪（ゆき）がちらちら降（ふ）ってきた。 — A few snowflakes began fluttering down.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ひらひら](ひらひら.md) overlaps in light fluttering things; [きらきら](きらきら.md) in twinkling. [じろじろ](じろじろ.md) more strongly suggests scrutinizing than fleeting looks.

## Distinctive meaning

ちらちら見る does not require the person to be hiding something. Conversely, falling snow need not involve anyone glancing at it. Keep the looking, visibility, and falling senses separate.
