# 凡庸 (ぼんよう) — mediocre; unremarkable

## Meaning

凡庸 describes someone or something that does not stand out for talent, originality, or another notable quality. Before a noun, use 凡庸な.

- 最初の案は凡庸だったが、二つ目の案には驚いた。 (さいしょのあんはぼんようだったが、ふたつめのあんにはおどろいた。) — The first idea was unremarkable, but the second surprised me.
- 評者は、その結末を凡庸なものだと感じた。 (ひょうしゃは、そのけつまつをぼんようなものだとかんじた。) — The reviewer found the ending mediocre.

## How the kanji combine

[凡](../kanji/凡.md) (ぼん) and [庸](../kanji/庸.md) (よう) both contribute the “ordinary” sense here. In 凡例 (はんれい), a key or guide to a book, 凡 has a different reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[平凡](平凡.md) (へいぼん) overlaps in meaning and can also describe an uneventful life. [普通](普通.md) (ふつう) may simply describe what is usual, without judging its merits.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling an idea 凡庸 judges its lack of distinction. It does not mean you computed an average score or established that most people had the same idea.
