# 国立 (こくりつ) - nationally established institution

## Meaning

国立 describes establishment and operation by the national or central
government, especially for an institution. It is a noun and の-modifier;
it also appears directly in compounds such as 国立大学
(こくりつだいがく), "national university."

- 彼女は国立大学で学んでいます。 (かのじょはこくりつだいがくでまなんでいます。) - She studies at a national university.
- この博物館は国立です。 (このはくぶつかんはこくりつです。) - This museum is a nationally established institution.

The point is institutional establishment or administration, not simply
being located somewhere inside the country.

## How the kanji combine

[国](../kanji/国.md) contributes the state or nation, and
[立](../kanji/立.md) has the establishment association.
国 specifies who establishes the institution.
The on readings are コク + リツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[公立](公立.md) (こうりつ) normally concerns a local public body in
the three-way classification 国立・公立・私立.
私立 (しりつ) is private establishment.
In a broader public-versus-private contrast, 公立 can also include
national institutions; the categories are not absolutely disjoint
in every use.

## Distinctive meaning

国立 does not guarantee free admission, unrestricted public access,
or a particular funding arrangement for every institution.
The place name 国立 is read くにたち; that separate proper-name
reading does not change こくりつ in the institution examples.
Learn the actual institution's category rather than inferring it
from the English adjective "national" alone.
