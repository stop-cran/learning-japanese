# 不通 (ふつう) - interruption of traffic or communication

## Meaning

不通 describes traffic or communication being cut off or unavailable. Roads, railway lines and telephone connections can become 不通.

- 大雪で鉄道が不通になりました。 (おおゆきでてつどうがふつうになりました。) - Heavy snow put the railway out of service.
- 台風の後、電話が不通になりました。 (たいふうのあと、でんわがふつうになりました。) - After the typhoon, the telephone connection was cut off.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md), not, negates [通](../kanji/通.md), passage or connection: traffic or contact does not get through. The on readings are フ + ツウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[停止](停止.md) (ていし) is a broader stoppage. [故障](故障.md) (こしょう) concerns a breakdown or malfunction, which can cause an interruption but is not the same description. [無沙汰](無沙汰.md) (ぶさた) concerns a long failure to keep in touch.

## Distinctive meaning

不通 and [普通](普通.md) share ふつう but not the meaning: 普通 means ordinary or usual. Do not merge the words because their kana match. 不通 can also concern an absence of correspondence; it is not limited to a damaged telephone. Ordinary slow traffic is not automatically a complete loss of passage.
