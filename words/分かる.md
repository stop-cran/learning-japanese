# 分かる (わかる) — to understand

## Meaning

分かる is to understand, to know, to become clear: 日本語が分かります (I understand Japanese), 分かりました (I understand / got it), 意味が分かる (the meaning is clear), 分からない (I don't understand).

It is an intransitive godan verb: 分かります (わかります), 分かって (わかって), and 分からない (わからない).
The source gloss "to be understood" is a possible English perspective on the meaning, not a Japanese passive conjugation.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) (divide) with the kun'yomi わ.かる. As a learning image, think of distinguishing a matter clearly into parts; this is not an established historical derivation. Related verbs: 分ける わける (divide, transitive), 分かれる わかれる (split, intransitive). The word can also be written in kana: わかる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 理解する りかいする (understand, formal), 知る しる (know).
- Antonym: 分からない (don't understand).

## Distinctive meaning

In the basic pattern taught here, 分かる marks the thing understood with が: 日本語が分かる. 知る is "come to know" (information), while 分かる is "grasp, make out". Both 分かる and 解る appear in print, but 分かる is standard.
