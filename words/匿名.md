# 匿名 (とくめい) — anonymity; using an assumed name

## Meaning

匿名 means not revealing one's real name, or using an assumed name.

- 匿名で寄付をした人がいます。 (とくめいできふをしたひとがいます。) — Someone made an anonymous donation.
- この調査は匿名で回答できます。 (このちょうさはとくめいでかいとうできます。) — You can answer this survey anonymously.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[匿](../kanji/匿.md) means hiding, and [名](../kanji/名.md) means name. Together, they mean hiding one's name.

## Synonyms and antonyms

実名 じつめい, “real name” (JMdict entry 1321470), is the opposite. [秘密](秘密.md) (ひみつ) is a secret in general (JMdict entry 1484150).

## Distinctive meaning

匿名 concerns a person's name or identity, not secrecy about other matters.
