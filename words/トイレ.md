# トイレ (トイレ) — toilet; restroom

## Meaning

トイレ means a toilet or restroom. It can refer to the facilities or to going to use them, not only to the fixture itself.

- すみません、トイレはどこですか。  
  すみません、トイレはどこですか。  
  "Excuse me, where is the restroom?"
- トイレに行ってきます。  
  トイレにいってきます。  
  "I'm going to the restroom." (And will come back.)

## How the kanji combine

トイレ is a katakana abbreviation of トイレット. The usual everyday form has three kana and no long-vowel mark. There are no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お手洗い (おてあらい) is a polite alternative for a restroom. 便所 (べんじょ) also means toilet, but can sound plainer or less polite. These differ in wording and register, not in being opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

American English "bathroom" can translate トイレ when it means a restroom. Do not infer that the room contains a bath: お風呂 (おふろ) concerns bathing, a different purpose.
