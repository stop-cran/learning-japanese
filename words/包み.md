# 包み (つつみ) - bundle; wrapped package

## Meaning

包み is a bundle, package, or parcel made by wrapping something. It is a noun: **包みを開ける** (つつみをあける) means to open a package, and 包みを渡す means to hand a package over.

- 友達から小さな包みをもらいました。 (ともだちからちいさなつつみをもらいました。) - I received a small package from a friend.
- 紙の包みを開けると、お菓子が入っていました。 (かみのつつみをあけると、おかしがはいっていました。) - When I opened the paper package, there were sweets inside.

The contents and wrapping can be specified separately; 包み alone does not tell you what is inside.

## How the kanji combine

包 contributes wrapping. 包み is related to the native verb [包む](包む.md) (つつむ), "wrap," with み giving the noun form used here. It is not a multi-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[荷物](荷物.md) (にもつ) includes luggage and parcels, whether wrapped this way or not. [箱](箱.md) (はこ) is a box; a 包み may instead be enclosed in paper or cloth. These words categorize an object by different features.

## Distinctive meaning

包み draws attention to something being wrapped together. It does not by itself imply that the package has been mailed, delivered, or bought. The recipient and means of delivery come from the surrounding sentence, as 友達から does above.
