# 農業 (のうぎょう) - agriculture; farming

## Meaning

農業 is agriculture or farming as an activity, occupation, or industry. It is a noun. Useful expressions include 農業をする, "farm," and 農業に従事する (のうぎょうにじゅうじする), "work in agriculture."

- 祖父は農業をしています。 (そふはのうぎょうをしています。) - My grandfather is a farmer.
- この地域では農業が盛んです。 (このちいきではのうぎょうがさかんです。) - Agriculture is thriving in this region.

The first example describes a person's work; the second concerns a regional industry. Neither names a particular crop.

## How the kanji combine

農 contributes farming or cultivation, while [業](../kanji/業.md) contributes work or an industry. 農 specifies which kind of work is meant. The on readings are ノウ + ギョウ, with a long vowel in each part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[農家](農家.md) (のうか) can name a farming household or farmer. 農業 names the work or industry, not the household. [工業](工業.md) (こうぎょう), "manufacturing industry," is a contrasting economic sector, not a direct opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

農業 is broader than the daily act of planting or harvesting. A discussion of 農業 can concern employment, machinery, policy, or production. Conversely, growing one plant at home does not by itself identify someone's occupation as 農業; use the word for the scope of activity actually intended.
