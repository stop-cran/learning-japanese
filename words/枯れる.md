# 枯れる (かれる) - wither (plants)

## Meaning

枯れる describes a plant withering or dying. It is an intransitive ichidan
verb: the plant is the subject, marked with が.

- 庭の花が枯れてしまいました。 (にわのはながかれてしまいました。) - The flowers in the garden have withered.
- 水をやらないと、この植物は枯れます。 (みずをやらないと、このしょくぶつはかれます。) - Without watering, this plant will wither.

Forms include 枯れます (かれます), 枯れて (かれて), and 枯れない
(かれない). 枯れている can describe the resulting withered state.

## How the kanji combine

[枯](../kanji/枯.md) contributes withering and supplies か; れる is okurigana.
The conjugation stem is 枯れ (かれ), found by removing only the final る,
not by removing all the written kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

枯らす (からす) is the transitive counterpart. Compare 花が枯れた
(はながかれた), the flowers withered, with 花を枯らした
(はなをからした), I let the flowers wither. 枯らす is godan:
枯らさない (からさない), not 枯れない.

[死ぬ](死ぬ.md) (しぬ) names dying more generally. [乾く](乾く.md)
(かわく) names becoming dry, as with laundry; it does not necessarily
involve a living thing dying.

## Distinctive meaning

The basic use taught here is about plants, not a person's death or the
ordinary drying of clothes. Withering can involve drying, but do not replace
洗濯物が乾く (せんたくものがかわく), the laundry dries, with 枯れる.
This spelling is not a general-purpose equivalent of every English dry up.
