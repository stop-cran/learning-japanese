# 組合 (くみあい) - union; association

## Meaning

組合 is an association or union formed around shared interests. In a workplace, it can abbreviate 労働組合 (ろうどうくみあい), "labor union." The noun fits **組合に入る** (くみあいにはいる), "join a union," and **組合の会員**, "an association's member."

- 私は労働組合に入っています。 (わたしはろうどうくみあいにはいっています。) - I belong to a labor union.
- 組合が会社と話し合いました。 (くみあいがかいしゃとはなしあいました。) - The union held discussions with the company.

入っています describes current membership in the first example, not the physical act of entering a room.

## How the kanji combine

[組](../kanji/組.md) contributes grouping or joining, and [合](../kanji/合.md) contributes coming together. The native readings くみ + あい evoke people joining together. Despite being written entirely in kanji, this is a native-reading word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[団体](団体.md) (だんたい) and [組織](組織.md) (そしき) are broader words for a group or organization. [会社](会社.md) (かいしゃ) is a company; it can be the other party in labor negotiations, but it is not a dictionary antonym of 組合.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume every 組合 is a labor union. The surrounding organization name may instead identify a cooperative or another association. The shortened workplace meaning becomes clear from context such as negotiations with an employer.
