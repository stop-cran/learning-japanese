# 別 (べつ) - separate; different

## Meaning

別 means separate, different, or another. Before a noun, 別の is a useful everyday pattern.

- 別の店に行きましょう。 (べつのみせにいきましょう。) - Let's go to another shop.
- 料金は別です。 (りょうきんはべつです。) - The fee is separate.

別の部屋 (べつのへや) means a different or separate room. As a predicate, use 別だ or 別です, not an i-adjective ending.

## How the kanji combine

[別](../kanji/別.md) alone uses the on reading べつ and carries the idea of separation or distinction. The native reading in 別れる (わかれる), "part; separate," belongs to a related but different word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ほかの also means "another" or "other." 同じ (おなじ), "the same," gives a useful contrast. 別々 (べつべつ) emphasizes things being separate from one another.

## Distinctive meaning

別 does not necessarily mean strange or unusual; two ordinary things can simply be different. Also learn 別に + negative as a whole pattern: 別に困っていません (べつにこまっていません), "I'm not particularly troubled." That adverbial expression does not literally describe a separate location.
