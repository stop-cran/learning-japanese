# 焼ける (やける) - burn, be baked

## Meaning

焼ける is an intransitive ichidan verb. Something burns, or food becomes cooked by baking, grilling, or similar heat. The affected thing takes が.

- パンが焼けました。 (パンがやけました。) - The bread is baked and ready.
- 火事で小屋が焼けました。 (かじでこやがやけました。) - The shed burned in the fire.

The first example is a successful cooking result, not necessarily burnt food. Forms include 焼けます and 焼けない (やけます、やけない).

## How the kanji combine

焼 writes や, followed by ける. The ichidan stem is やけ. This spelling supports both the burning and cooking senses taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

焼く (やく) is transitive: パンを焼く, "bake bread." 焦げる (こげる) specifically concerns scorching or charring, which 焼ける need not imply.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the pair as を焼く versus が焼ける. The form 焼ける can also be the potential of 焼く, so context may instead mean "can bake." The examples above teach the independent intransitive verb and its resulting condition.
