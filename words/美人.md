# 美人 (びじん) - beautiful woman

## Meaning

美人 is a noun for a beautiful woman or a beauty. It identifies a person rather than functioning as the general adjective "beautiful." Use **美人だ / 美人です** as a predicate.

- 母は若い頃、美人だったそうです。 (はははわかいころ、びじんだったそうです。) - I hear that my mother was a beauty when she was young.
- その絵には美人が描かれています。 (そのえにはびじんがえがかれています。) - A beautiful woman is depicted in that painting.

The first example uses the noun's past predicate だった. It reports what the speaker has heard; そうです does not express visual resemblance here.

## How the kanji combine

[美](../kanji/美.md), "beauty," modifies [人](../kanji/人.md), "person." The on readings are ビ + ジン. Although the components literally suggest a beautiful person, the inspected dictionary sense specifically identifies a beautiful woman.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[美しい](美しい.md) (うつくしい) is an adjective usable for people, scenery, music, and other beautiful things. きれいな人 is a descriptive phrase rather than the single noun 美人.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not replace every English "beautiful" with 美人: a beautiful mountain is not a 美人. The word expresses an appraisal of appearance, not intelligence or character. Such appraisals are subjective, and an unsolicited label about someone's looks may be unwelcome even when intended as praise.
