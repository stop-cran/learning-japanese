# 倍 (ばい) - double; times as much

## Meaning

倍 on its own commonly means double or twice as much. After a number, it expresses a multiplier: 二倍 (にばい), "twice," 三倍 (さんばい), "three times as much."

値段が倍になりました。 (ねだんがばいになりました。) - The price doubled.

この箱はあの箱の三倍の重さです。 (このはこはあのはこのさんばいのおもさです。) - This box is three times as heavy as that box.

## How the kanji combine

倍 is read バイ. It can stand as the noun "double" or attach to a number as a multiplier counter. There is no second written component.

## Synonyms and antonyms

二倍 explicitly means double; 半分 (はんぶん), "half," gives the reverse proportional comparison. 回 (かい) counts occurrences, so 三回 is three occasions, not three times the amount.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the reference amount clear: Aの三倍 is three times A, not A plus three more copies. As an ordinary numerical multiplier, 一倍 (いちばい) means one times the reference amount, not double. With a following noun, use の: 倍の値段 (ばいのねだん), "twice the price."

Learn 人一倍 (ひといちばい), "much more than others," as a fixed expression rather than applying that numerical rule to it.
