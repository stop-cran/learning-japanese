# ビル (ビル) - multistory building

## Meaning

ビル commonly means a multistory building, such as an office or commercial building.

- 駅の前に高いビルがあります。 (えきのまえにたかいビルがあります。) - There is a tall building in front of the station.

会社のビル (かいしゃのビル) means "the company's building." ビルに入る (ビルにはいる) means "enter the building."

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word is shortened from ビルディング, borrowed from English "building." It has no kanji components and no long vowel: ビル, not ビール.

## Synonyms and antonyms

建物 (たてもの) means a building more broadly, including structures not normally described as ビル. 家 (いえ) is a house or home, not a general synonym for an office block.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records a separate English-derived ビル for a bill, check, or invoice; this is a different lexical entry, not another meaning of the building abbreviation. Do not use that dictionary recognition as a reason to default to ビル when asking to pay: お会計 (おかいけい) is the useful restaurant expression, and 請求書 (せいきゅうしょ) means an invoice.
