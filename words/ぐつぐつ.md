# ぐつぐつ — bubbling and simmering

## Meaning

The bubbling sound and appearance of liquid cooking in a pot, whether simmering or boiling with visible bubbles.

- 鍋（なべ）の中でシチューがぐつぐつ煮えている。 — The stew is bubbling away in the pot.
- 火を弱めて、豆（まめ）をぐつぐつと煮た。 — I turned the heat down and simmered the beans.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ことこと](ことこと.md) often emphasizes a quieter, gentle simmer. [ぶくぶく](ぶくぶく.md) foregrounds bubbles or foam more generally, not necessarily cooking. [じゅうじゅう](じゅうじゅう.md) is the sizzle of food against a hot surface.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ぐつぐつ煮る（にる, simmer something）, ぐつぐつ煮える（にえる, be simmering）, and ぐつぐつと沸く（わく, boil）. It can suggest more noticeable bubbling than ことこと, but it does not prescribe a fixed temperature or always mean a violent rolling boil. The verb and recipe determine the cooking action.
