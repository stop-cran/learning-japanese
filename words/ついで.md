# ついで - opportunity while doing something else

## Meaning

ついで is an opportunity to do an additional thing while doing something else.
It is usually written in kana; 序で is the source's kanji spelling, read ついで.
Use noun + のついでに, or a dictionary-form or past-form verb + ついでに.

- 買い物のついでに、郵便局に寄りました。
  (かいもののついでに、ゆうびんきょくによりました。) - I stopped at the post office while I was out shopping.
- 図書館に行くついでに、この本を返してください。
  (としょかんにいくついでに、このほんをかえしてください。) - Please return this book when you go to the library.

The library visit supplies the occasion for returning the book.
A past plain form can also precede it: 東京に行ったついでに
(とうきょうにいったついでに), "while in Tokyo on that trip."

## How the kanji combine

In 序で, 序 has an occasion or order association.
This is a native word, not the on reading ジョ.
The kana-primary spelling on this page represents the same lexical identity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[機会](機会.md) (きかい) and [チャンス](チャンス.md) mean opportunity more broadly.
[きっかけ](きっかけ.md) can be a trigger or occasion that leads to an action.
ついで specifically connects a secondary action with another activity.
It has no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use ついで for every English "opportunity": a major independent ambition
need not be something done incidentally.
Also distinguish 次いで (ついで), "next; subsequently," related to
[次ぐ](次ぐ.md) (つぐ). That is a separate word, not the spelling for this
"while doing something else" construction.
The old order/sequence and successor senses of 序で are marked archaic in JMdict;
they are not the contemporary focus here.
