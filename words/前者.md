# 前者 (ぜんしゃ) - the former

## Meaning

前者 refers back to the first of two things, people, or options already mentioned: "the former." It is a noun and can take ordinary particles such as は, が, and を.

- 電車とバスなら、前者のほうが速いです。 (でんしゃとバスなら、ぜんしゃのほうがはやいです。) - Of the train and the bus, the former is faster.
- 二つの案を比べて、前者を選びました。 (ふたつのあんをくらべて、ぜんしゃをえらびました。) - I compared the two proposals and chose the former.

In the first example, the reference is explicitly 電車. The second requires the reader or listener to know the order in which the proposals were presented.

## How the kanji combine

[前](../kanji/前.md) contributes "before" or "earlier"; [者](../kanji/者.md) identifies the one under discussion. Here 者 is not limited to a human being. The readings are ゼン + シャ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[後者](後者.md) (こうしゃ), "the latter," refers to the second member of the pair. They contrast by order of mention, not by quality, age, or importance.

## Distinctive meaning

English "former" can mean "previous," as in a former president. 前者 is instead a reference back to an earlier-mentioned item. When the order is unclear, repeating the actual noun is more helpful than using 前者.
