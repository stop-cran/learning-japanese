# 幸い (さいわい) - good fortune; fortunately

## Meaning

幸い names good fortune or a fortunate circumstance. It also works adverbially as "fortunately," either alone or with に or にも. Despite the final い, it is not an ordinary い-adjective: use 幸いなこと (さいわいなこと), "a fortunate thing."

- 幸い、誰もけがをしませんでした。 (さいわい、だれもけがをしませんでした。) - Fortunately, nobody was injured.
- 早く出発したことが幸いしました。 (はやくしゅっぱつしたことがさいわいしました。) - Leaving early worked in our favor.

In the second example, 幸いする means to prove fortunate or produce a favorable outcome; it is not simply "feel happy."

## How the kanji combine

[幸](../kanji/幸.md) carries happiness or good fortune. The native word is written with this single kanji and final kana い. There is no pair of kanji meanings to combine.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[幸せ](幸せ.md) (しあわせ) overlaps with happiness and fortunate circumstances. [不幸](不幸.md) (ふこう), "misfortune; unhappiness," is a useful contrast. 幸い at the beginning of a sentence evaluates the outcome as fortunate rather than directly describing someone's emotion.

## Distinctive meaning

In a courteous request, ご連絡いただければ幸いです (ごれんらくいただければさいわいです) means "I would appreciate it if you could contact me." The conditional presents the requested action as welcome; it does not announce that contact has already happened.
