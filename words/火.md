# 火 (ひ) — fire; flame

## Meaning

火 is fire or flame, including a controlled flame used for cooking or lighting. It can refer to one flame or fire more generally.

- ろうそくの火が消えました。 (ろうそくのひがきえました。) — The candle flame went out.
- 料理ができたら、火を消してください。 (りょうりができたら、ひをけしてください。) — When the food is ready, turn off the heat.

In cooking instructions, 火 can refer to the burner or heat rather than requiring a visibly burning flame in every case.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [火](../kanji/火.md) writes the native noun ひ. Its on reading カ appears in compounds, but the standalone word here is not か.

## Synonyms and antonyms

炎 (ほのお) specifically emphasizes a flame or flames. [火事](火事.md) (かじ) means an uncontrolled, destructive fire incident. 水 (みず), "water," is a practical contrast, not a lexical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the action pair 火を消す (ひをけす), "extinguish the flame," and 火が消える (ひがきえる), "the flame goes out." 火 itself is a noun; the verb tells you what happens to it.
