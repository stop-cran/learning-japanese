# マスター - proprietor; master a skill

## Meaning

マスター has distinct uses within one dictionary entry. As a noun it can identify a proprietor or manager, particularly in a bar or coffee-shop setting. With する it means mastering a skill: **skill をマスターする**.

- 喫茶店のマスターにおすすめを聞きました。 (きっさてんのマスターにおすすめをききました。) - I asked the coffee-shop proprietor for a recommendation.
- 基本の操作をマスターしました。 (きほんのそうさをマスターしました。) - I mastered the basic operations.

In academic contexts, the noun also has the sense "master," as in a master's degree in arts or science; 修士 (しゅうし) makes that degree meaning explicit.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "master." There are no kanji components. The final ー is part of the assigned spelling マスター.

## Synonyms and antonyms

店長 (てんちょう) is a shop manager, but マスター is not a general job title for every manager. [習う](習う.md) (ならう) describes learning from instruction; マスターする emphasizes attaining command of the skill.

## Distinctive meaning

The proprietor noun and skill verb require different grammar. マスターに聞く asks a person; 操作をマスターする takes the skill as its object. Do not infer that being called a café's マスター certifies an academic degree or proves mastery of every activity associated with the business.
