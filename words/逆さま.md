# 逆さま (さかさま) - inverted; wrong way round

## Meaning

逆さま describes something inverted, upside down, reversed, or back to front. It is a noun and a な/の modifier.

- 地図を逆さまに持っていました。 (ちずをさかさまにもっていました。) - I was holding the map upside down.
- 順番が逆さまです。 (じゅんばんがさかさまです。) - The order is reversed.

逆さまに marks the reversed orientation; 逆さまな or 逆さまの can modify a following noun.

## How the kanji combine

[逆](../kanji/逆.md) contributes reversal. The rest of this native word is written in kana in the article's spelling.

The community source writes **逆様**, also read **さかさま**. JMdict marks that kanji spelling as rare. [様](../kanji/様.md) writes the final さま there; recognize the same whole word, not a person's name followed by an honorific.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[逆さ](逆さ.md) (さかさ) is a shortened form with overlapping meanings. [逆](逆.md) (ぎゃく) more broadly concerns reversal or oppositeness. [反対](反対.md) (はんたい) can mean opposite, but also opposition to a proposal; that opinion use is not automatically 逆さま.

## Distinctive meaning

The inversion need not be only vertical: order, front/back, and other orientations can be reversed. The second example concerns sequence, not a map physically hanging upside down. The source spelling 逆様 does not create a separate meaning or a different pronunciation.
