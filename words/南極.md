# 南極 (なんきょく) - South Pole; Antarctic

## Meaning

南極 names the South Pole. It also names the Antarctic region or
Antarctica more broadly; context determines whether the point or the
wider place is meant.

- 探検隊は南極点に到達しました。 (たんけんたいはなんきょくてんにとうたつしました。) - The expedition reached the South Pole.
- 南極でペンギンを観察しました。 (なんきょくでぺんぎんをかんさつしました。) - We observed penguins in Antarctica.

南極点 (なんきょくてん) explicitly specifies the polar point.
南極大陸 (なんきょくたいりく) specifies the continent.
The second sentence uses 南極 in the wider geographic sense.

## How the kanji combine

[南](../kanji/南.md) ナン, "south," modifies
[極](../kanji/極.md) キョク, "pole; extreme":
the southern pole. The established on-reading compound is なんきょく,
not みなみごく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

北極 (ほっきょく), the North Pole or Arctic, supplies the geographic
contrast. [南](南.md) (みなみ) is the general direction "south,"
not automatically the name of a polar region.
南極圏 (なんきょくけん) names the Antarctic Circle or the region within it.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not treat the South Pole, the Antarctic continent and every use of
"the Antarctic" as geographically identical. 南極 can cover more than
one of those referents in ordinary language, while longer terms make
the intended one explicit. Likewise, a scene somewhere in Antarctica
need not be at the exact South Pole.
