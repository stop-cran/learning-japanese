# 親友 (しんゆう) - close friend

## Meaning

親友 is a close friend: someone with whom a person has a particularly familiar
or trusted friendship. It is a noun. 私の親友 means "my close friend," and
親友になる means "become close friends."

- 彼女は高校時代からの親友です。 (かのじょはこうこうじだいからのしんゆうです。) - She has been a close friend since high school.
- 悩みを親友に相談しました。 (なやみをしんゆうにそうだんしました。) - I talked to a close friend about my worries.

In the second example, に marks the person consulted, and を marks the matter
discussed.

## How the kanji combine

[親](../kanji/親.md) contributes closeness, and [友](../kanji/友.md) means friend.
The first character describes the relationship with the friend named by the
second. Read the compound シン + ユウ, not おやとも.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[友達](友達.md) (ともだち) is the broader word "friend."
[親しい](親しい.md) (したしい) is an adjective, so 親しい友達 expresses a closely
related idea with different grammar. [仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) can identify people
sharing an activity or group without claiming a deeply personal friendship.

## Distinctive meaning

"Best friend" may be a natural translation in context, but 親友 does not require
that there be exactly one such person. It also does not imply romance.
The important distinction from an acquaintance is the closeness of the friendship,
not simply how long the people have known each other.
