# 発売 (はつばい) - release for sale

## Meaning

発売 is putting a product on the market or offering it for sale.
With する it is transitive; the product can take を.

- 来月、新しい本を発売します。 (らいげつ、あたらしいほんをはつばいします。) - We will release a new book for sale next month.
- この商品は発売中です。 (このしょうひんははつばいちゅうです。) - This product is on sale now.

発売日 (はつばいび) is the release date. 発売中 means currently available for purchase,
not necessarily that its price has been reduced.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) contributes sending out or starting, and [売](../kanji/売.md) concerns selling.
The compound focuses on making something available on the market.
ハツ + バイ remains はつばい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[販売](販売.md) (はんばい) is selling or sales more generally; [売る](売る.md) (うる) is the ordinary verb "sell."
[発行](発行.md) (はっこう) is issuing a publication or document, which need not involve a sale.
[特売](特売.md) (とくばい) specifically concerns a special, reduced-price sale.

## Distinctive meaning

English "on sale" can mean either available to buy or discounted.
発売 establishes availability, not a discount. Conversely, 発売中 can continue after the initial release day:
the word is not limited to a single moment when a product first appears.
