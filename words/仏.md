# 仏 (ほとけ) - Buddha; Buddhist image; the deceased

## Meaning

仏, read **ほとけ**, is a noun referring to Buddha or a Buddhist image. It can also refer to a deceased person or departed soul, and figuratively to a merciful person.

- 寺で仏を拝みました。 (てらでほとけをおがみました。) - I paid my respects to the Buddha image at the temple.
- あの人は仏のように優しいです。 (あのひとはほとけのようにやさしいです。) - That person is as kind as a Buddha.

The first example is understood here as reverence before an image. The second is a comparison expressing kindness, not a claim that the person is a religious figure.

## How the kanji combine

仏 is the whole noun, read ほとけ. There is no compound to split. This native-reading word is distinct from compounds using the on reading ぶつ, such as 仏教 (ぶっきょう), "Buddhism."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[神](神.md) (かみ) names a deity or god and is not an interchangeable label for every 仏. 仏像 (ぶつぞう), "Buddhist statue," explicitly identifies an image. These distinctions concern the referent, not a simple pair of opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

In discussion of death, 仏 may mean "the deceased" rather than a statue or Buddha himself. The dictionary also notes ホトケ for that sense. Context and sensitivity matter: the existence of this meaning does not make 仏 an appropriate substitute for a person's name in every condolence or conversation.
