# 傍観 (ぼうかん) — looking on; standing by and watching

## Meaning

傍観 means looking on from the side without taking part. JMdict glosses include “standing by and watching,” “sitting back and watching” and “remaining a spectator.” It can be used with する.

- 困っている人を傍観しているだけではいけません。 (こまっているひとをぼうかんしているだけではいけません。) — You must not just stand by and watch people in trouble.
- 彼は議論に加わらず、傍観していました。 (かれはぎろんにくわわらず、ぼうかんしていました。) — He stayed out of the discussion and just looked on.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[傍](../kanji/傍.md) means beside, and [観](../kanji/観.md) means viewing or observing. Together, they describe watching from the side.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[観察](観察.md) (かんさつ), “observation; survey; watching” (JMdict entry 1214900), does not by itself say whether the watcher takes part; 傍観 means watching without taking part. [見物](見物.md) (けんぶつ) is sightseeing or watching something as a spectator (JMdict entry 1260000).

## Distinctive meaning

The person who looks on is a 傍観者 ぼうかんしゃ, “onlooker; bystander” (JMdict entry 1518810).
