# 継続 (けいぞく) - continuation; persistence

## Meaning

継続 is continuation: an activity or state goes on, or someone keeps it going.
It can also be translated as maintenance or persistence in the appropriate context.
継続する has both transitive and intransitive uses.

- 調査を継続します。 (ちょうさをけいぞくします。) - We will continue the investigation.
- 雨のため、試合の継続は難しいです。 (あめのため、しあいのけいぞくはむずかしいです。) - Because of the rain, continuing the match is difficult.
- この状態が継続しています。 (このじょうたいがけいぞくしています。) - This state is continuing.

In the first example を marks what is continued; the third makes the continuing state the subject with が.

## How the kanji combine

継 contributes carrying on or succeeding; [続](../kanji/続.md) contributes continuing.
The related meanings reinforce carrying something onward.
The on readings combine as ケイ + ゾク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[続ける](続ける.md) (つづける) is a transitive native verb, and [続く](続く.md) (つづく) is its intransitive counterpart.
[続き](続き.md) (つづき) can mean a continuation or the rest of something.
[維持](維持.md) (いじ) emphasizes maintaining a condition.
[中止](中止.md) (ちゅうし), stopping or cancellation, is a useful contrast for an ongoing activity.

## Distinctive meaning

継続 is useful in reports and decisions about keeping an activity or state going.
It does not inherently mean improvement, success, or preservation without any change.
Do not transfer the transitivity of 続ける alone to every use of 継続する.
