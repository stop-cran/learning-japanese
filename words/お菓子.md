# お菓子 (おかし) — sweets; confectionery

## Meaning

お菓子 names sweets and snack foods such as candy, cakes, biscuits, and rice crackers. It is not limited to individually wrapped candy.

- お土産にお菓子を買いました。 (おみやげにおかしをかいました。) — I bought some sweets as a souvenir.
- このお菓子は甘くありません。 (このおかしはあまくありません。) — This snack isn't sweet.

## How the kanji combine

菓子 (かし) is a Sino-Japanese word for confectionery. 菓 has a confectionery association; [子](../kanji/子.md) is part of the established compound, not a literal child here. The native prefix お gives the everyday form お菓子; this page classifies the compound base as kango.

## Synonyms and antonyms

おやつ refers to a snack or snack occasion, while お菓子 names a food category. 飴 (あめ) is specifically candy.

## Distinctive meaning

The prefix お is conventional and does not mean that the sweets belong to an honored person. 菓子 without お is common in labels and compound words, such as 和菓子 (わがし), Japanese confectionery.
