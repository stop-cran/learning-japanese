# 鎖 (くさり) - chain

## Meaning

鎖 is a noun for a chain: connected links used for fastening, holding, or transmitting motion. **鎖でつなぐ** means "connect/tether with a chain"; で marks the means used.

- 門は鎖で閉じてあります。 (もんはくさりでとじてあります。) - The gate has been secured with a chain.
- 自転車の鎖が外れました。 (じてんしゃのくさりがはずれました。) - The bicycle chain came off.

The second example describes what happened to the chain, not someone deliberately removing it. 鎖を外す (くさりをはずす) would mean "remove/unfasten the chain."

## How the kanji combine

鎖 is one kanji read くさり in this native noun. Its metal component is useful for recognizing the character, but there is no two-word compound to split. Learn the full reading with the whole character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[紐](紐.md) (ひも) is a cord or string. It can also fasten things, but lacks the linked construction of a chain. チェーン is a loanword used for physical chains too; the words overlap in contexts such as bicycles.

## Distinctive meaning

English "chain" also describes a group of shops, but a business chain is normally called a チェーン, not simply a 鎖. Start with the physical linked object and judge figurative extensions from context. A chain can secure something or form part of machinery; the noun alone does not imply captivity.
