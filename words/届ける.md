# 届ける (とどける) - deliver; notify

## Meaning

届ける means to deliver or send something to a destination. It is transitive: mark the delivered item with を and the recipient or destination with に.

友達の家に荷物を届けました。 (ともだちのいえににもつをとどけました。) - I delivered a package to my friend's house.

It also means to report or notify an authority.

住所の変更を会社に届けました。 (じゅうしょのへんこうをかいしゃにとどけました。) - I notified my company of my change of address.

## How the kanji combine

届 represents とど, followed by okurigana ける. This is an ichidan verb: 届けます, 届けて, 届けない. The complete verb describes making something reach its destination or formally reporting something.

## Synonyms and antonyms

送る (おくる) emphasizes sending. 届く (とどく) is the intransitive partner: 荷物が届く (にもつがとどく), "the package arrives."

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 届ける simply as intransitive "reach" or "arrive." Contrast 荷物を届ける, "deliver a package," with 荷物が届く, "a package arrives." The reporting sense likewise involves someone actively submitting information, not information arriving by itself.
