# 丹念 (たんねん) — painstaking; meticulous

## Meaning

丹念 means doing something with great care: paying attention even to small details and putting your heart into the work. It is a noun and a na-adjective, so you can say 丹念な仕事 for careful work and 丹念に for doing something carefully.

- この人形は、丹念に作られています。
  (このにんぎょうは、たんねんにつくられています。)
  "This doll has been made with great care."
- 先生は、私の作文を丹念に読んでくれました。
  (せんせいは、わたしのさくぶんをたんねんによんでくれました。)
  "My teacher read my essay very carefully."

These are original usage examples, not quotations from a dictionary or other source.

## How the kanji combine

[丹](../kanji/丹.md) (タン) contributes sincerity, a true heart; [念](../kanji/念.md) (ネン) contributes attention or care. This is a modifier + head relation: attention given with a sincere heart. It explains the compound's meaning, not the historical origin of either character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[丁寧](丁寧.md) (ていねい), "polite; careful," is broader: it also covers politeness, while 丹念 is about care and effort, not manners. [慎重](慎重.md) (しんちょう), "cautious; prudent," is about being careful not to make mistakes or take risks. [苦心](苦心.md) (くしん), "painstaking effort; taking pains," names the hard effort itself, not the careful way of working. 入念 (にゅうねん), "careful; elaborate; scrupulous," is very close; Digital Daijisen gives it as a synonym.

There is no single fixed antonym. [粗末](粗末.md) (そまつ), "poor-quality; careless," can describe careless handling, but it is not a direct opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

丹念 is about care with every small detail, done with real feeling, such as checking materials one by one. Keep it apart from 丁寧, which can also describe polite words and manners.
