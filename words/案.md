# 案 (あん) - proposal; draft

## Meaning

案 is an idea, proposal, or plan put forward for consideration. It can also name a draft rather than a finished document. Use 案を出す (あんをだす) for putting forward an idea and 案を考える (あんをかんがえる) for thinking one up.

- 何かいい案はありますか。 (なにかいいあんはありますか。) - Do you have any good ideas?
- 会議で新しい案を出しました。 (かいぎであたらしいあんをだしました。) - I put forward a new proposal at the meeting.

Attached to a noun, 案 can identify a proposed version: 計画案 (けいかくあん), "a draft plan," or 予算案 (よさんあん), "a proposed budget."

## How the kanji combine

案 is a single character read with the on reading アン. Here it supplies the whole idea of a proposal or draft; there are no separate written words to combine.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計画](計画.md) (けいかく) is a plan, including a plan already adopted. [意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion and need not propose an action. [提案](提案.md) (ていあん) can name the act of proposing.

## Distinctive meaning

An 案 is something to consider, compare, amend, or accept. Calling a document an 案 does not say that its contents have already been approved. It has no single useful lexical opposite.
