# 乗車 (じょうしゃ) - boarding a vehicle

## Meaning

乗車 means boarding a train, bus, or similar vehicle, or getting into a vehicle such as a taxi. 乗車する is intransitive; the vehicle can take に.

- このバスには後ろのドアから乗車してください。 (このバスにはうしろのドアからじょうしゃしてください。) - Please board this bus through the rear door.
- 駅前でタクシーに乗車しました。 (えきまえでタクシーにじょうしゃしました。) - I got into a taxi in front of the station.

から marks the entrance used in the first example. 乗車券 (じょうしゃけん) is a ticket for travel on a vehicle, particularly rail transport.

## How the kanji combine

[乗](../kanji/乗.md) contributes boarding or riding, and [車](../kanji/車.md) a vehicle. The vehicle is what one boards. Read ジョウ + シャ, not the standalone native readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[乗る](乗る.md) (のる) is the broader everyday verb "board; ride." [下車](下車.md) (げしゃ) is getting off a vehicle. [運転](運転.md) (うんてん) concerns driving or operating it, not simply becoming a passenger.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list's "taking a train" is too narrow as an exclusive definition: buses and taxis also fit. Boarding does not mean driving, and 乗車した alone does not establish that the passenger reached a destination. The compound is useful in transport instructions and announcements.
