# お手伝いさん (おてつだいさん) - maid; domestic helper

## Meaning

お手伝いさん names someone employed to help with household work, traditionally a woman: a maid or domestic helper. It does not simply mean anyone who lends a hand.

- その家では、お手伝いさんが掃除や洗濯をしていました。 (そのいえでは、おてつだいさんがそうじやせんたくをしていました。) - In that household, a maid did the cleaning and laundry.

The noun names a person. Helping with a chore as a family member does not by itself make that person an お手伝いさん.

## How the kanji combine

The base [手伝い](手伝い.md) (てつだい) is the noun related to [手伝う](手伝う.md) (てつだう), "help." [手](../kanji/手.md) contributes a helping-hand association and [伝](../kanji/伝.md) belongs to that established native spelling. お and さん frame the person reference; adding them produces this established occupational expression, not a universal polite label for every assistant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

家政婦 (かせいふ), "housekeeper; maid," and メイド, "maid," overlap in domestic employment. [手伝い](手伝い.md) can mean help or a helper much more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

Dictionary recognition is not a recommendation to use this as a job title for everyone performing household services today. The term carries its established domestic-service meaning. In an actual employment setting, use the person's or service's stated title rather than inferring it merely from the presence of さん.
