# 致す (いたす) - do humbly

## Meaning

致す is a humble or deferential counterpart of する, "do." It presents the
speaker's action, or an action on the speaker's side, modestly in formal interaction.
It is a godan verb: いたします, いたした, いたして.

- 私がいたします。 (わたしがいたします。) - I will do it.
- 後でご連絡いたします。 (あとでごれんらくいたします。) - I will contact you later.

## How the kanji combine

致 writes the native verb with す as okurigana. In polite formulas and auxiliary
uses, hiragana いたす is usual; the examples use that spelling.
The polite base is いたし-, not いた-.

## Synonyms and antonyms

する is the ordinary verb, and します is its neutral polite form.
なさる honors the person doing the action instead; it is not a more formal
replacement for one's own いたす.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn conventional combinations such as お願いいたします (おねがいいたします),
"I would appreciate your help." Do not replace every する mechanically: the
relationship between speaker, actor, and listener matters, and honorific
prefixes belong to established constructions.
