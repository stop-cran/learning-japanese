# 欠伸 (あくび) - yawn

## Meaning

欠伸 means a yawn or yawning. The word is usually written あくび in kana.
Useful constructions are あくびが出る (あくびがでる), "a yawn comes out,"
and あくびをする, "yawn."

- 眠くて、あくびが出ました。 (ねむくて、あくびがでました。) - I was sleepy and yawned.
- 彼は大きなあくびをしました。 (かれはおおきなあくびをしました。) - He gave a big yawn.

あくびする also functions as an intransitive する verb.

## How the kanji combine

Learn あくび as a whole-word reading, not as 欠's ケツ plus 伸's シン.
As a memory aid, [欠](../kanji/欠.md) is also the yawning radical and
[伸](../kanji/伸.md) relates to stretching. This association is not a claim about
the historical derivation of the spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[眠い](眠い.md) (ねむい), "sleepy," describes a state, not the yawn itself.
ため息 (ためいき), "sigh," names a different action and is not an interchangeable label.
There is no useful lexical opposite of a yawn.

## Distinctive meaning

The kanji spelling is worth recognizing, but ordinary examples can use あくび.
Yawning often accompanies sleepiness; the word alone does not prove why someone yawned
or that they are bored with the speaker.
