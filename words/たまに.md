# たまに - occasionally

## Meaning

たまに means "occasionally" or "once in a while." It describes something that happens infrequently but does happen.

- たまに映画館に行きます。 (たまにえいがかんにいきます。) - I go to the cinema once in a while.
- 兄はたまに料理をします。 (あにはたまにりょうりをします。) - My older brother cooks occasionally.

It modifies the clause directly; no extra に is needed after the word.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The spelling 偶に is also recorded, but the complete adverb is たまに, including its final に. It is not a conjugated verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

時々 (ときどき), "sometimes," overlaps but can suggest more frequent repetition. よく means "often" in frequency statements. These are broad tendencies, not fixed numerical schedules.

## Distinctive meaning

たまに is compatible with an affirmative statement because it allows occasional occurrences. あまり followed by a negative verb instead says that something does not happen much. Both may describe infrequency, but their sentence patterns differ.
