# 賢い (かしこい) - wise; clever

## Meaning

賢い is an i-adjective meaning "wise; clever; smart." It can praise intelligence or good judgment. Before a noun, use 賢い directly; the adverbial form is 賢く (かしこく).

- それは賢い選択だと思います。 (それはかしこいせんたくだとおもいます。) - I think that is a wise choice.
- お金を賢く使いたいです。 (おかねをかしこくつかいたいです。) - I want to use my money wisely.

The second sentence describes how the money is used, not the money itself. The negative adjective is 賢くない, not 賢いではない.

## How the kanji combine

賢 supplies the idea of wisdom or intelligence, with the native reading かしこい. The final い changes in forms such as 賢かった (かしこかった), "was wise." This is a single-kanji adjective, not a Sino-Japanese compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頭](頭.md) (あたま) appears in 頭がいい (あたまがいい), "be smart." It overlaps with 賢い, but 賢い選択 evaluates a decision rather than identifying a person's general intellectual ability. 愚かな (おろかな), "foolish," is a useful contrast for judgment.

## Distinctive meaning

賢い is not limited to school achievement: a practical decision can be 賢い. The dictionary separately records "well-behaved; good" in Kansai child-directed language. That qualified use should not replace the general "wise; clever" meaning in every context.
