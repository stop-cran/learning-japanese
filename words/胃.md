# 胃 (い) - stomach

## Meaning

胃 is the noun for the stomach as a digestive organ. It is useful when describing where discomfort is felt or discussing digestion. 胃が痛い (いがいたい) means "my stomach hurts"; the body part is marked by が, not を.

- 朝から胃が痛いです。 (あさからいがいたいです。) - My stomach has hurt since this morning.
- 医者に胃の検査を勧められました。 (いしゃにいのけんさをすすめられました。) - The doctor recommended a stomach examination.

胃の薬 (いのくすり), "stomach medicine," uses の to connect the organ with something associated with it.

## How the kanji combine

胃 is a single character read with the on reading イ. It names the whole organ; there is no multi-kanji relationship to explain. Keep the short reading い distinct from longer words that begin with that sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[おなか](おなか.md) is an everyday word for the belly or abdominal area. [腹](腹.md) (はら) also refers to the belly. They overlap with English "stomach," but neither automatically identifies this specific internal organ.

## Distinctive meaning

English "stomach" can name either an organ or the front of the body. Japanese distinguishes these descriptions. For a general "I'm hungry," おなかがすいた is useful; knowing 胃 does not mean replacing おなか in every familiar expression. 胃 has no ordinary lexical opposite.
