# 糸 (いと) - thread; yarn

## Meaning

糸 is thread, yarn, or a fine string. It is a noun; context identifies the material and purpose.

- 白い糸を買いました。 (しろいいとをかいました。) - I bought white thread.
- 針に糸を通します。 (はりにいとをとおします。) - I thread a needle.

In the second example, を marks the thread being passed through, and に marks the needle.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 糸 writes the native word いと. There is no okurigana or compound to split. In 毛糸 (けいと), "wool yarn," 糸 retains this reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ひも is a useful contrast for cord or string, particularly something used to tie objects. The categories overlap in English; 糸 commonly names fine strands used in sewing or weaving.

## Distinctive meaning

糸 does not specify cotton, silk, or another material. Add that information when needed. There is no useful direct antonym to memorize.
