# 防犯 (ぼうはん) - crime prevention; security

## Meaning

防犯 means preventing crime. It is a noun, used in 防犯のために, "for crime prevention," and before nouns naming security measures or equipment.

- 防犯のために、夜は必ず鍵をかけます。 (ぼうはんのために、よるはかならずかぎをかけます。) - To prevent crime, I always lock up at night.
- 入口に防犯カメラがあります。 (いりぐちにぼうはんカメラがあります。) - There is a security camera at the entrance.

防犯カメラ (ぼうはんカメラ) is a security camera; 防犯対策 (ぼうはんたいさく) means crime-prevention measures. Learn these noun combinations rather than adding する to every 防犯 expression.

## How the kanji combine

[防](../kanji/防.md) contributes preventing or guarding against; [犯](../kanji/犯.md) concerns committing an offense. This is an action-and-object association: preventing crime. The on readings are ボウ + ハン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予防](予防.md) (よぼう) is prevention or precaution more broadly, including illness. [防止](防止.md) (ぼうし) is preventing an unwanted event. [警備](警備.md) (けいび) is guarding or security work.

[犯罪](犯罪.md) (はんざい), "crime," names what 防犯 aims to prevent, not a synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The 犯 limits this word to crime-related prevention. 防犯 does not mean every kind of safety, and a 防犯カメラ is not necessarily recording at every moment merely because it has that name.
