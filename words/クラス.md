# クラス — class; group

## Meaning

クラス means a class, particularly a group of students or an instructional class.

- 私たちは同じクラスです。 (わたしたちはおなじくらすです。) — We are in the same class.
- 夜、日本語のクラスに通っています。 (よる、にほんごのくらすにかよっています。) — I attend a Japanese class in the evenings.

Use の to connect it with a subject, as in 日本語のクラス, or to modify another noun: クラスの友達 (くらすのともだち), "a friend from class."

## How the kanji combine

クラス is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "class." There are no written kanji components and no long-vowel mark in this spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

授業 (じゅぎょう) emphasizes a lesson or the teaching session. 学級 (がっきゅう) refers to a school class as an organized group of pupils. These overlap with クラス without covering every use.

## Distinctive meaning

クラス can identify the group you belong to, not just an event on a timetable. Outside school, it can also name a category or rank; the surrounding topic determines which sense of "class" is meant.
