# まさに (まさに) - exactly; truly; just about to

## Meaning

まさに is an adverb emphasizing an exact match or a strong judgment: "exactly; precisely; truly; indeed." It can also locate an event just on the point of happening.

- これはまさに探していた本です。 (これはまさにさがしていたほんです。) - This is exactly the book I was looking for.
- 電車がまさに発車しようとしていました。 (でんしゃがまさにはっしゃしようとしていました。) - The train was just about to depart.

In the second example, the verb's volitional form + としている expresses being about to act; まさに emphasizes that immediate moment.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. 正に is a recorded spelling for the exactness or certainty uses. The written character connects with correctness, but that does not make まさに a general adverb meaning "in the correct manner."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本当](本当.md) (ほんとう) in 本当に can also strengthen a claim as "really." ちょうど overlaps when something matches exactly. 正しく (ただしく), "correctly," describes doing something the right way and has a different job.

## Distinctive meaning

まさに identifies something as the very thing intended or a striking realization of a description. It does not imply that an action was performed without errors. Use 正しく答える (ただしくこたえる) for "answer correctly"; まさに is not a replacement for 正しく there.
