# 遠慮 (えんりょ) — holding back out of consideration

## Meaning

遠慮 is restraint or hesitation out of consideration for other people. With する it means to hold back, refrain, or politely decline.

- 遠慮しないで、食べてください。  
  えんりょしないで、たべてください。  
  "Please don't hold back; help yourself."
- 今日は遠慮しておきます。  
  きょうはえんりょしておきます。  
  "I'll pass today." (Politely declining an offer.)

## How the kanji combine

[遠](../kanji/遠.md) means "far" and 慮 relates to thought or consideration. The on readings combine as えん + りょ. Thinking beyond one's immediate wishes is a useful meaning association, not a rule for translating each character literally.

## Synonyms and antonyms

我慢 (がまん), "endurance; putting up with something", emphasizes bearing discomfort. 遠慮 emphasizes holding back in relation to others. 遠慮なく (えんりょなく), "without hesitation", provides a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

ご遠慮ください (ごえんりょください), "please refrain", is a polite request not to do something, not encouragement to hesitate. On a sign, polite wording can still communicate a firm restriction.
