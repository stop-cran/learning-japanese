# 八百屋 (やおや)

## Meaning

八百屋 means a greengrocer or a fruit and vegetable shop. Context can refer to the business, premises, or person selling the produce.

- 八百屋でトマトを買いました。 (やおやで トマトを かいました。) — I bought tomatoes at the greengrocer's.

で marks the place of buying. 八百屋さん (やおやさん) is a familiar everyday way to refer to the shop or its keeper.

## How the kanji combine

[八](../kanji/八.md) and [百](../kanji/百.md) are written as 八百, here read やお, followed by [屋](../kanji/屋.md), や, a shop or trade ending. Learn the whole reading; it is not はっぴゃくや.

## Synonyms and antonyms

青果店 (せいかてん) is another term for a fruit and vegetable shop. スーパー is a supermarket, which sells more kinds of goods. Neither is a direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The written number does not claim that the shop stocks exactly eight hundred products. The ordinary meaning is a produce seller; do not use 八百屋 for any store merely because it sells many different things.
