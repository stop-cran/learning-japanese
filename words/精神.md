# 精神 (せいしん) — mind; spirit

## Meaning

精神 names the mind or spirit, including a person's mental life rather than just the physical body.
The related な-adjective 精神的 (せいしんてき) means mental, spiritual, or emotional.
Use 精神的な before a noun and 精神的に before a verb or adjective describing how something is affected.

- 精神的に疲れています。 (せいしんてきにつかれています。) — I am mentally tired.
- 精神の健康も大切です。 (せいしんのけんこうもたいせつです。) — Mental health matters too.

精神 itself is a noun; the 的 is part of the related adjective, not a conjugated ending of 精神.

It also describes an attitude or guiding spirit: 最後まであきらめない精神
(さいごまであきらめないせいしん), "the determination not to give up before the end."
法律の精神 (ほうりつのせいしん), "the spirit of the law," instead refers to
the law's underlying purpose or essence, not a person's mental health.

## How the kanji combine

[精](../kanji/精.md) contributes vitality or essence, while [神](../kanji/神.md) has its mind or spirit association here.
The meanings work together rather than describing a physical deity.
Both are on readings: セイ + シン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[心](心.md) こころ, "heart; mind; spirit," overlaps in meaning.
[気分](気分.md) (きぶん), "feeling; mood," is useful for a temporary state:
今日は気分がいい (きょうはきぶんがいい), "I feel good today."
Do not replace it mechanically with 精神 just because both concern inner experience.

[体](体.md) からだ, "body," provides a physical-versus-mental contrast, rather than an opposite like good versus bad.

## Distinctive meaning

The 神 in 精神 does not make every use religious.
精神的に疲れている can describe ordinary mental exhaustion without making a claim about gods or the soul.
Choose "mind," "spirit," or "mental" according to the whole expression instead of translating each kanji separately.
