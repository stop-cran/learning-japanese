# 番 (ばん) - number in a series; turn

## Meaning

番 can label a number in a series, and can mean someone's turn. In the first use it follows a number; in the second, Nの番 means "N's turn."

- 三番のバスに乗ってください。 (さんばんのバスにのってください。) - Please take the number 3 bus.
- 次は私の番です。 (つぎはわたしのばんです。) - It is my turn next.

A numbered label and an opportunity to act are related to ordering, but they do not require the same English translation. 私の番 does not mean "my telephone number."

## How the kanji combine

The single character [番](../kanji/番.md) supplies the on reading バン. It is a whole noun or a number-following element, not a compound needing another kanji to be complete.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[番号](番号.md) (ばんごう) names a number or identifying code. [順番](順番.md) (じゅんばん) emphasizes order or sequence and can also mean a turn. The expressions overlap, but their grammatical positions differ.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the pattern together with the intended meaning: 三番 for an assigned number, 私の番 for a turn. The source row gives no English gloss. These two senses are explicit dictionary-backed teaching choices; the blank source does not establish that every meaning of 番 belongs to a particular study level.
