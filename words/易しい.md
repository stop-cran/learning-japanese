# 易しい (やさしい) — easy

## Meaning

易しい describes something that is easy to do or understand. JMdict marks it as **usually written in kana**:
やさしい. The kanji spelling is useful for recognizing which meaning is intended.
It is an い-adjective: 易しい問題 (やさしいもんだい), "an easy problem," needs no な.

- この問題は易しいです。 (このもんだいはやさしいです。) — This problem is easy.
- もっとやさしい本を読みたいです。 (もっとやさしいほんをよみたいです。) — I want to read an easier book.

## How the kanji combine

[易](../kanji/易.md) represents ease here; しい completes the adjective's native reading.
There is no second kanji to combine with it. The negative is やさしくない, "not easy,"
and the past form is やさしかった, "was easy."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[難しい](難しい.md) (むずかしい), "difficult," provides the direct contrast.
[簡単](簡単.md) (かんたん), "simple; easy," overlaps when describing low difficulty.
[容易](容易.md) (ようい) also means "easy," but its grammar differs: 容易な問題 (よういなもんだい).
[優しい](優しい.md) is another やさしい, meaning "kind; gentle"; shared pronunciation does not
make kindness the meaning of 易しい.

## Distinctive meaning

易しい focuses on difficulty, not a person's considerate behavior.
When やさしい appears in kana, use the surrounding phrase to distinguish an easy explanation
from a kind person. Do not assume kana spelling is missing information that must be "corrected" into kanji.

### Neutral ease versus チョロい

易しい describes low difficulty; 簡単 can describe low difficulty or something uncomplicated or brief.
**ちょろい**, also written **チョロい**, is a colloquial い-adjective meaning "easy; easily handled,"
often translated as "a cinch" or "a piece of cake." Compare the same question:

- この問題は易しい。 (このもんだいはやさしい。) — This question is easy.
- この問題はチョロい。 (このもんだいはちょろい。) — This question is a cinch.

The difference is not a fixed scale on which チョロい always means "even easier."
チョロい presents something as little challenge to the speaker. It can sound playfully confident,
cocky or dismissive, depending on context; it is not automatically an insult.
易しい and 簡単 are safer choices for neutral explanations or formal conversation.

Applied to a person, チョロい can suggest an easy opponent or someone easy to fool or manipulate:
"a pushover" may fit the context. It does **not** mean "kind" or "easygoing."
Calling someone チョロい can therefore be belittling rather than complimentary.
