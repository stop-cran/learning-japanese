# 文芸 (ぶんげい) - literature; the arts

## Meaning

文芸 commonly concerns literature as an art: creative writing, literary works and the
activities surrounding them. It can also have the broader sense of literature and the arts.
It is a noun; 文芸の modifies another noun.

- この雑誌には文芸の評論が載っています。 (このざっしにはぶんげいのひょうろんがのっています。) - This magazine contains literary criticism.
- 彼女は文芸に関心があります。 (かのじょはぶんげいにかんしんがあります。) - She is interested in literature and the arts.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) contributes writing or literature; [芸](../kanji/芸.md) contributes
art or artistic accomplishment. The combination presents writing in its artistic setting.
These are on readings: ブン + ゲイ. 文藝 is an outdated-kanji spelling of the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[文学](文学.md) (ぶんがく) also means literature, including its study.
[芸術](芸術.md) (げいじゅつ) is the broader arts term; [芸能](芸能.md) (げいのう)
often concerns performance; 文芸 does not specifically denote stage entertainment.
[文献](文献.md) (ぶんけん) instead emphasizes
written sources used for reference or research.

## Distinctive meaning

In 文芸作品 (ぶんげいさくひん), "literary work," and 文芸雑誌 (ぶんげいざっし),
"literary magazine," the literary focus is clear. Do not translate every 文芸 as
"fine art" without checking whether the context is specifically about writing.
The broader dictionary sense is recognition guidance, not a claim that all art terms
are freely interchangeable.
