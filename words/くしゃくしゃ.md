# くしゃくしゃ — crumpled; wrinkled

## Meaning

くしゃくしゃ describes paper, clothing, or hair that is crumpled or untidy.

- かばんの中で、地図がくしゃくしゃになってしまった。
  — The map got crumpled in my bag.
- 風で髪がくしゃくしゃになった。
  — The wind messed up my hair.

## Synonyms and antonyms

くしゃくしゃ describes wrinkles or a disheveled appearance. It can also describe feeling
annoyed or upset, but the context must establish that emotional use.

## Distinctive meaning

The card focuses on crumpled and rumpled appearances. The same form can also describe chewing
noisily or being upset; those are separate uses.
