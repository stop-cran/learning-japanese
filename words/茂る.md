# 茂る (しげる) - grow thickly; be in full leaf

## Meaning

茂る describes plants growing thickly or trees being in full leaf. It is an intransitive godan verb: the vegetation takes が.

- 庭に草が茂っています。 (にわにくさがしげっています。) - Grass is growing thickly in the garden.
- 夏になると、木の葉が茂ります。 (なつになると、きのはがしげります。) - When summer comes, the trees fill out with leaves.

Forms include 茂ります, 茂った, 茂って, and 茂らない. 茂っている can describe an already dense, leafy state, not only growth happening at that instant.

## How the kanji combine

茂 contributes luxuriant growth and writes しげ; る completes the native verb. 繁る is another dictionary spelling. 滋る is marked obsolete and is not the spelling to imitate for these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[伸びる](伸びる.md) のびる can describe increasing length, while [育つ](育つ.md) そだつ concerns growing or developing more generally. 茂る highlights the thick growth or foliage. [枯れる](枯れる.md) かれる, wither, provides a useful plant-state contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The English idea here is luxuriant vegetation, not an expensive or luxurious lifestyle. No exact height, leaf count, or spacing threshold is built into 茂る. Dense growth can be welcome shade or unwanted weeds; the verb alone does not judge it as good or bad.
