# 速達 (そくたつ) - express delivery; express mail

## Meaning

速達 means rapid or express delivery. In everyday postal use, it names
a service that gives mail faster handling.
Use **速達で送る** (そくたつでおくる), "send by express mail."

- この書類を速達で送りたいです。 (このしょるいをそくたつでおくりたいです。) - I would like to send these documents by express mail.
- 速達の料金はいくらですか。 (そくたつのりょうきんはいくらですか。) - How much does express delivery cost?

It is a noun and is also listed as a transitive する verb for delivering rapidly.
The examples teach the practical postal noun construction, not a requirement
to use 速達する in every request.

## How the kanji combine

[速](../kanji/速.md) contributes speed, and [達](../kanji/達.md)
contributes reaching or delivery. The relationship is speedy arrival/delivery.
The on readings combine as そく + たつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[配達](配達.md) (はいたつ) is delivery more generally.
普通郵便 (ふつうゆうびん), ordinary mail, is a useful service comparison,
not a universal antonym in every delivery system.
[急行](急行.md) (きゅうこう), an express train in transport contexts,
does not name the same postal service.

## Distinctive meaning

English "express" has several uses. A 速達 request concerns rapid delivery,
not an express train, an expressed opinion or every action done quickly.
The word alone does not promise a particular number of hours;
the service's actual delivery conditions must be checked separately.
