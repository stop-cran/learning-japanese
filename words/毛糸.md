# 毛糸 (けいと) - knitting wool; woollen yarn

## Meaning

毛糸 is knitting wool or woollen yarn: animal-hair fibers made into yarn for knitting or fabric.
毛糸の can describe something made from this yarn.

- 赤い毛糸を買いました。 (あかいけいとをかいました。) - I bought red knitting wool.
- 祖母が毛糸の帽子を編んでくれました。 (そぼがけいとのぼうしをあんでくれました。) - My grandmother knitted me a woollen hat.

The word names the yarn, not an animal's fur still attached to its skin.

## How the kanji combine

[毛](../kanji/毛.md), "hair; wool," modifies [糸](../kanji/糸.md), "thread; yarn."
These are native readings: け + いと = けいと.
The first two kana do not represent an on reading ケイ of 毛.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[糸](糸.md) (いと) is broader and can refer to thread or yarn of different materials.
[ウール](ウール.md) means wool as a material; 毛糸 identifies yarn.
[毛皮](毛皮.md) (けがわ) is fur or a pelt, a different material form.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary-backed core here is wool/animal-hair yarn.
Do not infer from an English craft-shop label "yarn" that every synthetic knitting yarn must therefore be 毛糸 in every context.
Conversely, the spelling does not mean loose body hair.
