# じとじと — wet and clammy

## Meaning

Unpleasant, clinging dampness or wetness, including humid air and sweat-damp material.

- 汗（あせ）でシャツがじとじとして、気持ちが悪い。 — My shirt feels clammy with sweat, and it is uncomfortable.
- 雨の日は、この部屋の壁（かべ）までじとじとする。 — On rainy days, even the walls of this room feel damp.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[じめじめ](じめじめ.md) strongly overlaps, often foregrounding pervasive dampness or humidity. [ぐっしょり](ぐっしょり.md) emphasizes heavy saturation, and [しっとり](しっとり.md) can describe welcome moisture.

## Distinctive meaning

じとじとする, じとじとした服, and じとじとと湿る（しめる）are useful patterns. It is not restricted to tiny amounts of moisture: JMdict includes wet and soaked senses. The “clinging clamminess” contrast with じめじめ is a tendency of focus, not a strict division by object or humidity level.
