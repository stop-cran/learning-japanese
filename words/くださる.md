# くださる - give respectfully to us

## Meaning

くださる is the respectful counterpart of くれる: someone gives something to the speaker or someone on the speaker's side. It honors the giver, who is the subject. Mark the gift with を and an explicit recipient with に.

先生が私に本をくださいました。 (せんせいがわたしにほんをくださいました。) - My teacher gave me a book.

With a verb's て-form, it describes someone kindly doing something for us.

先生が説明してくださいました。 (せんせいがせつめいしてくださいました。) - My teacher kindly explained it to me.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual, especially in the auxiliary use. 下さる (くださる) is another spelling, using [下](../kanji/下.md). This special godan verb has くださいます, not くださります; its て-form is くださって. The plain past is くださった, and the plain negative is くださらない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くれる](くれる.md) is neutral. [いただく](いただく.md) humbly describes receiving the gift or favor instead, making the recipient the subject.

## Distinctive meaning

ください is the request form, as in 水をください (みずをください), "Water, please," and 待ってください (まってください), "Please wait." Learn this form alongside くださる rather than treating it as an unrelated word or a universal marker for giving in any direction.
