# 入社 (にゅうしゃ) - joining a company; starting employment

## Meaning

入社 is a noun and an intransitive suru verb for joining a company as an
employee. Use 会社に入社する (かいしゃににゅうしゃする), with に marking
the company joined.

- 来月、この会社に入社します。 (らいげつ、このかいしゃににゅうしゃします。) - I will join this company next month.
- 入社して三年になります。 (にゅうしゃしてさんねんになります。) - It has been three years since I joined the company.

The second sentence measures time since the joining event.
Useful noun combinations include 入社日 (にゅうしゃび), "date of joining,"
and 入社一年目 (にゅうしゃいちねんめ), "one's first year at the company."

## How the kanji combine

[入](../kanji/入.md), "enter," combines with [社](../kanji/社.md),
here "company": entering a company as an employee.
The on readings are ニュウ + シャ. 社's shrine meaning does not apply here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[就職](就職.md) (しゅうしょく), "finding employment; getting a job,"
overlaps when the employer is a company, but is not restricted to companies.
[勤める](勤める.md) (つとめる) describes working for an employer.
[辞める](辞める.md) (やめる), "resign; quit a job," gives a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

入社 is becoming an employee, not walking into an office every morning.
[出勤](出勤.md) (しゅっきん) describes reporting for work or attendance.
Someone can continue [働く](働く.md) (はたらく), "working," long after
their 入社; the joining event and the ongoing work are different.
