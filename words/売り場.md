# 売り場 (うりば) — sales area

## Meaning

売り場 is a place where goods or tickets are sold: a department, sales floor, or counter. It often follows the thing sold.

切符売り場はどこですか。（きっぷうりばはどこですか。） — Where is the ticket counter?

## How the kanji combine

[売](../kanji/売.md)り is the combining form of 売る（うる）, "sell." [場](../kanji/場.md), "place," is read ば here. The compound uses native readings. 売場 is an alternative spelling without り.

## Synonyms and antonyms

店（みせ） is a shop as a whole. 売り場 can be just one section within a shop. レジ is the checkout, not necessarily the entire sales area.

## Distinctive meaning

In 食品売り場（しょくひんうりば）, "food department," the first noun identifies the merchandise. Use 売り場で to describe an action there, and 売り場に to describe a destination.
