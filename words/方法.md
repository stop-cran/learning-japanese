# 方法 (ほうほう) — method

## Meaning

方法 is a method or way of doing something. It is a noun, so 方法で can identify the method used,
while 方法を can mark the method as something you choose, explain, or teach.

- この方法でやってみます。 (このほうほうでやってみます。) — I will try doing it this way.
- 日本語を勉強する方法を教えてください。 (にほんごをべんきょうするほうほうをおしえてください。) — Please tell me how to study Japanese.

In the second example, the verb phrase 日本語を勉強する directly describes 方法: "a method of studying Japanese."

## How the kanji combine

[方](../kanji/方.md) supplies a way or manner, and [法](../kanji/法.md) supplies a method or system.
Their meanings reinforce one another here; 法 does not mean that the method is a law.
Both characters have the on reading ホウ in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

やり方 (やりかた) is a close alternative meaning "way of doing."
このやり方, "this way of doing it," points to a procedure just as この方法 can.

## Distinctive meaning

方法 answers "How is it done?", not "Which physical direction?"
Although 方 can concern direction elsewhere, 方法 does not mean a road or route simply because English
"way" can have that meaning. Learn it with the action being carried out.
