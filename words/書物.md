# 書物 (しょもつ) - book; written volume

## Meaning

書物 is a noun meaning a book or volume. It is useful when discussing books as written works, sources of knowledge, or objects to collect and read. It does not grammatically mark singular versus plural.

- 歴史に関する書物を読んでいます。 (れきしにかんするしょもつをよんでいます。) - I am reading a book about history.
- この図書館には古い書物がたくさんあります。 (このとしょかんにはふるいしょもつがたくさんあります。) - This library has many old books.

In the first sentence, 書物を is the object of reading. In the second, たくさん and the setting make the plural interpretation clear.

## How the kanji combine

[書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing or written works, and [物](../kanji/物.md) contributes a thing. The writing-related first component specifies the kind of object. The whole word means "book," not every possible thing with writing on it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本](本.md) (ほん) is the straightforward word for a book and can replace 書物 in many book-related contexts. [書類](書類.md) (しょるい) refers to documents or paperwork, which need not be bound books.

## Distinctive meaning

書物 fits discussion of written works, but does not establish that a book is old, scholarly, or physically large. In the library example, 古い supplies "old." Nor does the English plural gloss "books" make the Japanese noun inherently plural: 一冊の書物 (いっさつのしょもつ) is one volume.
