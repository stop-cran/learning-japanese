# きちきち — jam-packed; tightly filled

## Meaning

きちきち describes a space packed so full that little room remains.

- 本を箱にきちきちに詰めた。
  — I packed the books tightly into the box.
- 旅行用のかばんに衣類をきちきちに詰め込んだ。
  — I packed the clothes tightly into the travel bag.

## Synonyms and antonyms

きちきち emphasizes crowding or a very tight fit. It is not simply a word for neat, orderly
arrangement.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records a grinding sound and a precise or correct sense. Those uses, along with a
homonymous locust name, are not the focus of these examples.
