# 夜行 (やこう) - night travel; night train

## Meaning

夜行 means traveling or going about at night. It also abbreviates 夜行列車 (やこうれっしゃ), a night train. In compounds it identifies transport operating at night, as in 夜行バス (やこうバス), a night bus.

- 夜行バスで大阪へ行きました。
  (やこうバスでおおさかへいきました。) - I went to Osaka by night bus.
- 夜行列車で朝早く着きました。
  (やこうれっしゃであさはやくつきました。) - I arrived early in the morning by night train.

The train abbreviation is a noun use; do not treat every occurrence as a verb meaning to walk.

## How the kanji combine

[夜](../kanji/夜.md) contributes night, and [行](../kanji/行.md) going or travel: travel at night. The on readings are ヤ + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) and [旅](旅.md) (たび) are broader travel words. [列車](列車.md) (れっしゃ) means a railway train without specifying its operating time.

[夜間](夜間.md) (やかん) identifies nighttime, not specifically a journey. [徹夜](徹夜.md) (てつや), staying up all night, concerns remaining awake; a passenger on a night train may sleep.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep やこう for the night-train sense. The same written family also has やぎょう, including the monster-procession sense in 百鬼夜行 (ひゃっきやぎょう); that is not the reading or sense of the transport examples.

Night travel need not last the entire night, and the word by itself does not say whether the traveler stays awake.
