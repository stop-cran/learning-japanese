# 習慣 (しゅうかん) — habit; custom

## Meaning

習慣 is a repeated personal habit or an established social custom. It is a noun: 習慣がある (しゅうかんがある), "have a habit," and 習慣になる (しゅうかんになる), "become a habit."

- 毎朝、散歩する習慣があります。 (まいあさ、さんぽするしゅうかんがあります。) — I have a habit of walking every morning.
- 国によって習慣が違います。 (くにによってしゅうかんがちがいます。) — Customs differ from country to country.

## How the kanji combine

[習](../kanji/習.md) suggests learning through practice, and [慣](../kanji/慣.md) becoming accustomed. Their on-readings, しゅう + かん, form a word for an established pattern of behavior.

## Synonyms and antonyms

癖 (くせ) can mean a habit or personal tendency, often one noticeable to others. 風習 (ふうしゅう) concerns social customs. 礼儀 (れいぎ) is etiquette or manners, not a general synonym for any repeated habit.

## Distinctive meaning

習慣 itself does not mean either good or bad behavior: 良い習慣 (よいしゅうかん) and 悪い習慣 (わるいしゅうかん) specify that. Do not confuse it with 週間, also しゅうかん, used for a duration in weeks.
