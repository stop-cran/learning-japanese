# 手拭い (てぬぐい) - traditional hand towel

## Meaning

手拭い is a traditional hand towel, usually a thin cotton cloth, used for wiping the hands, face, or body. It can also be worn as a headband or used for other practical purposes. 手ぬぐい and 手拭 are dictionary spellings of this word.

- 手拭いで顔の汗を拭きました。 (てぬぐいでかおのあせをふきました。) - I wiped the sweat from my face with a cotton towel.
- 旅行のお土産に、柄の入った手拭いを買いました。 (りょこうのおみやげに、がらのはいったてぬぐいをかいました。) - I bought a patterned cotton towel as a souvenir from my trip.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md) is て, and 拭い connects with 拭う (ぬぐう), "wipe." The native compound reading is てぬぐい. 拭く (ふく), used in the first example, is another reading of 拭; it does not make this towel てふい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[タオル](タオル.md) is a towel more generally, including thicker absorbent towels. [ハンカチ](ハンカチ.md) is a handkerchief and [雑巾](雑巾.md) a cleaning cloth. Their possible uses overlap, but those names do not specify the same traditional cloth.

## Distinctive meaning

Despite 手, the word is not restricted to drying hands. Decorative patterns or other uses do not stop it being a 手拭い. Older dictionary headings such as たなごい and てのごい are historical recognition information, not the modern reading practiced here.
