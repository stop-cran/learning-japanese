# くんくん — sniffing; an animal whimpering

## Meaning

Repeated sniffing, especially by an animal, or a small whining/whimpering voice.

- 犬がかばんのにおいをくんくんかいだ。 — The dog sniffed at the bag.
- 子犬（こいぬ）がドアの前でくんくん鳴（な）いている。 — The puppy is whimpering in front of the door.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[わんわん](わんわん.md) commonly suggests louder barking or wailing. [めそめそ](めそめそ.md) overlaps in a whimpering impression, usually when describing human tearfulness.

## Distinctive meaning

においをくんくんかぐ concerns smelling; くんくん鳴く concerns a voice. A picture of a dog's nose alone cannot tell whether sniffing, whimpering, or neither is happening.
