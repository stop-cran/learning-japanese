# ぶるぶる — trembling and shivering

## Meaning

Repeated shaking or quivering, often from cold, fear, or another strong emotion.

- 寒くて、体がぶるぶる震（ふる）えた。 — My body shivered with the cold.
- 子犬（こいぬ）が怖（こわ）がって、ぶるぶる震えている。 — The puppy is trembling with fear.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぞくぞく](ぞくぞく.md) often brings the shivery sensation into focus. [がたがた](がたがた.md) can also describe trembling, and [びくびく](びくびく.md) overlaps in fearful shaking.

## Distinctive meaning

ぶるぶる震える describes the movement, not one particular emotion. The same visible shaking can have different causes. It does not by itself mean that a person is timid or that something is making a loud noise.
