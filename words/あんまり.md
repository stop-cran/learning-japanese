# あんまり (あんまり) - not very; excessively

## Meaning

あんまり is a conversational form related to [あまり](あまり.md). With a negative predicate, it means "not very" or "not much." With a positive expression of degree, especially あんまりに or あんまりにも, it can mean "excessively."

- この料理はあんまり辛くないです。 (このりょうりはあんまりからくないです。) - This dish is not very spicy.
- あんまりにも暑くて、眠れません。 (あんまりにもあつくて、ねむれません。) - It is so terribly hot that I cannot sleep.

In それはあんまりだ, "That is too much," it expresses that something is excessive or unreasonable.

## How the kanji combine

This article uses kana. Although JMdict groups あまり and あんまり together under 余り, not every sense belongs to both readings. The noun meaning "remainder; leftovers" belongs to あまり, not the あんまり taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[全然](全然.md) (ぜんぜん) with a negative predicate expresses "not at all," a stronger denial than "not very." あまり provides a close alternative in the degree uses.

## Distinctive meaning

The negative ending matters: あんまり辛くない does not say that the dish is excessively spicy. Conversely, あんまりにも暑い has no negative predicate and expresses excess. Learn the complete pattern rather than assigning a single positive or negative meaning to the adverb alone.
