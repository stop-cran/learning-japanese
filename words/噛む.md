# 噛む (かむ) -- bite; chew

## Meaning

噛む means to bite something or to chew food. The object takes を.

- 食べ物をよく噛んでください。 (たべものをよくかんでください。) -- Please chew your food well.
- 犬がおもちゃを噛んでいます。 (いぬがおもちゃをかんでいます。) -- The dog is biting its toy.

This is a godan verb: 噛みます (かみます), 噛まない (かまない), 噛んで (かんで), and 噛んだ (かんだ). The て-form changes む to んで.

## How the kanji combine

噛 represents biting or chewing and supplies か in the native verb. The written む completes the dictionary form. This spelling boundary is not the full conjugation rule.

## Synonyms and antonyms

かじる emphasizes biting into or gnawing at something. 食べる (たべる) covers eating more generally. [飲む](飲む.md) (のむ), drinking or swallowing, describes a different action rather than a universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Chewing does not necessarily mean swallowing or finishing food: ガムを噛む (ガムをかむ) means "chew gum." Context determines whether a particular use describes a single bite or repeated chewing.
