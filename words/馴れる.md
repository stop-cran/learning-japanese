# 馴れる (なれる) - become tame

## Meaning

馴れる describes an animal becoming tame or accustomed to people,
rather than remaining wary of them. The dictionary also glosses this
sense as becoming domesticated. The animal takes が; the person it
becomes familiar with can take に.

- この猫は人によく馴れています。 (このねこはひとによくなれています。) - This cat is very used to people.
- 子犬が家族に馴れてきました。 (こいぬがかぞくになれてきました。) - The puppy has begun to become comfortable with the family.

It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 馴れます, 馴れない, 馴れて and
馴れた. 馴れている can describe the resulting familiar or tame state.
The animal, not an owner deliberately taming it, is the subject.

## How the kanji combine

馴 writes な, with れる in kana. The character's horse component can
help as a modern visual reminder of the animal sense; it does not
restrict the verb to horses or prove an etymology.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[慣れる](慣れる.md), also なれる, emphasizes getting used to something
or becoming experienced in the existing article.
The dictionary groups the spellings together but specifically favors
馴れる for the tame-animal sense and 慣れる for accustomed/skilled senses.
[おとなしい](おとなしい.md) can describe a tame or docile animal's
quality rather than the process of becoming familiar.

## Distinctive meaning

These spelling tendencies are not a rule that animals can never
慣れる or people can never 馴れる. This bounded article teaches the
source's tame/domesticated sense; it does not duplicate every use of
the shared dictionary family. An individual animal becoming comfortable
with people does not necessarily mean a species has undergone
biological domestication.
