# 倉庫 (そうこ) - storehouse; warehouse

## Meaning

倉庫 is a warehouse or storehouse: a building or space for keeping goods
or supplies. It is a noun. 倉庫に入れる (そうこにいれる)
means putting something into storage in the warehouse.

- 商品は倉庫にあります。 (しょうひんはそうこにあります。) - The goods are in the warehouse.
- 使わない机を倉庫に入れました。 (つかわないつくえをそうこにいれました。) - We put the unused desks in the storehouse.

に marks the storage location or destination in these examples.
The word alone does not specify ownership or the goods stored there.

## How the kanji combine

倉 and [庫](../kanji/庫.md) both contribute storage or storehouse meanings.
Their related ideas reinforce a place for keeping things.
The on readings are ソウ + コ.
Do not substitute the native reading くら inside this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[車庫](車庫.md) (しゃこ) specifically houses vehicles.
[冷蔵庫](冷蔵庫.md) (れいぞうこ) is a refrigerator.
Sharing 庫 does not make these unrestricted alternatives to 倉庫.
保管 (ほかん) names keeping something in safekeeping, not the building itself.

## Distinctive meaning

A warehouse can store goods without being a shop open to customers.
倉庫 also does not imply that the contents are unwanted or being discarded.
Use surrounding nouns to identify the goods, purpose, and kind of storage.
