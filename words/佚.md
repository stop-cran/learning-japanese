# 佚 (いつ) - ease; relaxation

## Meaning

佚, read いつ, is a dictionary-recorded noun for being at ease or relaxing.
Learn it here through an attested classical expression:

- 佚を以て労を待つ。 (いつをもってろうをまつ。) - Await a weary opponent while rested oneself.

In this expression, 佚 is the rested or comfortable condition, and 労
(ろう) is the opponent's fatigue. を以て (をもって) means "with; by means of."
The saying concerns meeting a tired enemy while one's own side is well rested.
It also has the spelling 逸を以て労を待つ.

## How the kanji combine

This is one character representing a Sino-Japanese noun, not a multi-character
compound. Keep the written form 佚 together with the reading いつ.
Other character-dictionary meanings of 佚 do not automatically become meanings
of this particular noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[楽](楽.md) (らく) also concerns ease or comfort.
[のんびり](のんびり.md) describes relaxing or an unhurried manner, as in
家でのんびりする (いえでのんびりする), "take it easy at home."
The expression above contrasts 佚 with 労, fatigue.

## Distinctive meaning

This page teaches recognition through the attested saying, not a general
pattern for inventing modern combinations such as replacing every 楽な
with 佚な. It is a different word from the time question
[いつ](いつ.md), "when," despite their identical pronunciation.
