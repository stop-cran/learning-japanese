# 位置 (いち) - position; location

## Meaning

位置 names where something is situated, often relative to other things. As a noun, use Nの位置, "the position of N." The intransitive expression Nに位置する means "be located at/in N."

- 地図で駅の位置を確かめました。 (ちずでえきのいちをたしかめました。) - I checked the station's location on a map.
- その町は島の北に位置しています。 (そのまちはしまのきたにいちしています。) - The town is located in the north of the island.

It can also name a position or standing in a group, not just a point on a map.

## How the kanji combine

[位](../kanji/位.md) contributes "place; rank," and [置](../kanji/置.md) contributes "put; place." The related ideas combine into the position occupied by something. The on readings are イ + チ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[場所](場所.md) (ばしょ) names a place or space. 位置 directs attention to its location or arrangement: changing a chair's 位置 changes where it stands. [立場](立場.md) (たちば) can describe a person's standpoint or circumstances.

## Distinctive meaning

位置 does not mean every kind of English "situation." To discuss the state of affairs rather than where something stands, consider [状態](状態.md) (じょうたい), "state; condition." Also distinguish 位置を変える, "change the position," from 位置する, "be located": the latter does not mean "put something there."
