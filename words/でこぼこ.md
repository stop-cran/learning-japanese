# でこぼこ — uneven and bumpy

## Meaning

An uneven arrangement of raised and sunken parts. It also describes an imbalance or mismatch, such as people of markedly different heights.

- この道はでこぼこしていて、自転車で走りにくい。 — This road is bumpy and hard to cycle along.
- パテで壁（かべ）のでこぼこを埋めた（うめた）。 — I filled the uneven spots in the wall with putty.
- 背の高い兄と小さい弟は、でこぼこコンビだ。 — The tall older brother and small younger brother make a mismatched pair.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ごつごつ](ごつごつ.md) emphasizes hard, angular projections. [ざらざら](ざらざら.md) is roughness at a finer scale. 平ら（たいら）means flat or level.

## Distinctive meaning

でこぼこの道, でこぼこした道, and でこぼこな道 occur; as a noun, でこぼこを直す means to fix unevenness. The mismatch sense is not automatically a criticism. 凹凸（おうとつ）is a related conventional term with the characters reversed, not another reading to substitute mechanically into でこぼこコンビ.
