# 魅力 (みりょく) - charm; appeal

## Meaning

魅力 is charm, appeal, or an attractive quality. It is a noun: 魅力がある, "have appeal," and Nに魅力を感じる (Nにみりょくをかんじる), "find N appealing."

- この町の魅力は静かな環境です。 (このまちのみりょくはしずかなかんきょうです。) - The appeal of this town is its quiet surroundings.
- その仕事に魅力を感じています。 (そのしごとにみりょくをかんじています。) - I find that job appealing.

In the second sentence, に identifies what attracts the speaker, and を marks 魅力 as what is felt.

## How the kanji combine

魅 contributes charming or captivating, and [力](../kanji/力.md) contributes power or capacity. Together they name the power to attract interest. The on readings are ミ + リョク; this is not physical strength.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[人気](人気.md) (にんき) is popularity, an indication that people like something. 魅力 concerns the appeal it has, which an individual may recognize even when it is not widely popular. [特徴](特徴.md) (とくちょう) is a characteristic and need not be attractive.

## Distinctive meaning

For an adjective, use 魅力的な (みりょくてきな), "appealing; attractive," or the phrase 魅力のある. Do not mechanically attach な to the noun 魅力. Its appeal may concern a person's character, a place's atmosphere, or a job's conditions, not just beauty or romantic attraction.
