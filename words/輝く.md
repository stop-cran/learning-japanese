# 輝く (かがやく) - shine; sparkle; glow

## Meaning

輝く is an intransitive godan verb meaning to shine, sparkle, or glitter. The shining thing takes が. It can also describe a face or eyes lighting up with happiness or hope.

- 夜空に星が輝いています。 (よぞらにほしがかがやいています。) - Stars are shining in the night sky.
- 合格の知らせを聞いて、彼女の目が輝きました。 (ごうかくのしらせをきいて、かのじょのめがかがやきました。) - Her eyes lit up when she heard that she had passed.

Forms include 輝きます, 輝いた, and 輝かない. 輝いている describes the continuing shining state in the first example.

## How the kanji combine

輝 is read かがや before the ending く. The single character supplies the radiance meaning; this is not a two-kanji compound. The dictionary records other spellings, but 輝く is the spelling taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[光る](光る.md) (ひかる) overlaps in meaning "shine" or "give off light." 輝く is useful when emphasizing radiance or brilliance. [明るい](明るい.md) (あかるい) describes brightness as an adjective rather than the shining action or state.

## Distinctive meaning

Literal and emotional radiance share the same verb, but the subject clarifies the intended image. 目が輝く describes visibly brightened eyes, not necessarily a physical source of light. The English command "shine the shoes" requires a polishing verb, not this intransitive verb for something shining.
