# 満悦 (まんえつ) — great delight; deep satisfaction

## Meaning

満悦 is the state of being fully satisfied and delighted. Digital Daijisen defines it as 心が満ち足りてよろこぶこと, “feeling fulfilled and glad,” and gives 手厚いもてなしに満悦する (てあついもてなしにまんえつする), “be delighted by warm hospitality,” and 満悦至極 (まんえつしごく), “highly delighted.”

- 父は久しぶりの温泉旅行に満悦していた。 (ちちはひさしぶりのおんせんりょこうにまんえつしていた。) — My father was delighted with his first hot-spring trip in a long time.
- 新しいおもちゃをもらって、その子はご満悦だった。 (あたらしいおもちゃをもらって、そのこはごまんえつだった。) — Having been given a new toy, the child was thoroughly pleased.

## How the kanji combine

[満](../kanji/満.md) (まん) means full or satisfied, and [悦](../kanji/悦.md) (えつ) means delight. 満悦する is the verb form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[満足](満足.md) (まんぞく) is satisfaction in general. [喜ぶ](喜ぶ.md) (よろこぶ) and [喜び](喜び.md) (よろこび) are the everyday verb and noun for being glad. 満悦 combines satisfaction and delight.

## Distinctive meaning

ご満悦 (ごまんえつ), in the second example, adds the polite prefix ご; [実用日本語表現辞典](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%81%94%E6%BA%80%E6%82%A6) has a heading for it, also written 御満悦, and calls it a polite form of 満悦. Other words with 悦 are 喜悦 (きえつ), “joy,” and 悦楽 (えつらく), “joy; pleasure.”
