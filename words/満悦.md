# 満悦 (まんえつ) — great delight; deep satisfaction

## Meaning

満悦 is the state of being fully satisfied and delighted. JMdict notes that it is usually used as ご満悦 (ごまんえつ), with the prefix ご. Digital Daijisen defines 満悦 as 心が満ち足りてよろこぶこと (こころがみちたりてよろこぶこと), “feeling fulfilled and glad,” and also gives the verb use 手厚いもてなしに満悦する (てあついもてなしにまんえつする), “be delighted by warm hospitality,” and 満悦至極 (まんえつしごく), “highly delighted.”

- 新しいおもちゃをもらって、その子はご満悦だった。 (あたらしいおもちゃをもらって、そのこはごまんえつだった。) — Having been given a new toy, the child was thoroughly pleased.
- 父は久しぶりの温泉旅行に満悦していた。 (ちちはひさしぶりのおんせんりょこうにまんえつしていた。) — My father was delighted with his first hot-spring trip in a long time.

## How the kanji combine

[満](../kanji/満.md) (まん) means full or satisfied, and [悦](../kanji/悦.md) (えつ) means delight. The usual form is ご満悦, as in the first example; the verb 満悦する, in the second example, is also used.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[満足](満足.md) (まんぞく) is satisfaction in general, as in 結果に満足している (けっかにまんぞくしている), “I am satisfied with the result.” 満悦 adds gladness: being fulfilled and glad at the same time. [喜ぶ](喜ぶ.md) (よろこぶ) and [喜び](喜び.md) (よろこび) are the everyday verb and noun for being glad.

## Distinctive meaning

The first example follows JMdict’s usual form in plain narration about a child. [実用日本語表現辞典](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%81%94%E6%BA%80%E6%82%A6) describes ご満悦 as a word for being glad and satisfied from the heart; its entry for 御満悦, the other spelling, calls it a polite expression of 満悦. Other words with 悦 are 喜悦 (きえつ), “joy,” and 悦楽 (えつらく), “joy; pleasure.”
