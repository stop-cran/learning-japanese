# 割引 (わりびき) - discount; tenths off

## Meaning

割引 is a discount or reduction from a price. The noun appears in
割引がある (わりびきがある), "there is a discount," and
割引料金 (わりびきりょうきん), "discounted price/rate."
Digital Daijisen also records 割引する, "give a discount."

- 学生には割引があります。 (がくせいにはわりびきがあります。) - There is a discount for students.
- この商品は二割引です。 (このしょうひんはにわりびきです。) - This item is 20 percent off.

In the numerical use, each 割 is one tenth: 二割引 means a reduction of
two tenths, leaving 80 percent of the original price, not a final price of 20 percent.

## How the kanji combine

[割](../kanji/割.md) contributes a share or proportion and
[引](../kanji/引.md) subtracting. The native elements わり + ひき give
わりびき, with ひ voiced to び. 割引き and 割り引き are alternate
spellings; the irregular-okurigana form 割り引 is not the primary teaching spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[減らす](減らす.md) (へらす) is reducing an amount more generally.
[低下](低下.md) (ていか) describes a level or quality falling;
a decline is not automatically a commercial discount.
[安い](安い.md) (やすい) describes a low price. An expensive item can
have a discount and still not be cheap.

## Distinctive meaning

Discounting a person's story means allowing for exaggeration rather than
literally changing its price. Digital Daijisen records that figurative use
and a specialized abbreviation for bill discounting; ordinary shopping
examples do not teach the financial procedure.

The pinned JMdict gives noun and counter senses. The する construction
above is supported separately by the public Japanese dictionary, not
silently attributed to a verb POS in that pinned record.
