# おじいさん (おじいさん) — grandfather or elderly man

## Meaning

おじいさん means a grandfather or an elderly man. The family relationship must come from context.

おじいさんは毎日散歩します。（おじいさんはまいにちさんぽします。） — Grandfather takes a walk every day.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. When kanji are used, お祖父さん normally means "grandfather," while お爺さん means "elderly man." お and さん are polite elements in this established word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

祖父（そふ） is the neutral term for one's own grandfather when speaking outside the family. おばあさん is the corresponding word for a grandmother or elderly woman.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the long いい: おじさん, with short い, means "uncle" or "middle-aged man." Calling an unfamiliar person おじいさん directly can feel overly familiar or emphasize their age; it is not automatically respectful in every situation.
