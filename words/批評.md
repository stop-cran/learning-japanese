# 批評 (ひひょう) - critique; review

## Meaning

批評 is a noun for a critique, review, or evaluative commentary. With する, it becomes a transitive verb: **Nを批評する**, "critique or review N."

- 新しい映画の批評を読みました。 (あたらしいえいがのひひょうをよみました。) - I read a review of the new film.
- 授業で互いの作品を批評しました。 (じゅぎょうでたがいのさくひんをひひょうしました。) - In class, we critiqued one another's work.

In 映画の批評, の identifies the subject of the review. In the second example, を marks the work being evaluated.

## How the kanji combine

批 contributes critical examination, and 評 contributes appraisal or comment. Their related meanings combine into evaluating and commenting on something. The reading is ひ + ひょう; keep the long vowel in ひょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[批判](批判.md) (ひはん) also involves critical judgment and can overlap. 批評 is useful for examining a work's qualities, not simply declaring dislike. [意見](意見.md) (いけん), "opinion," is broader and need not amount to a review.

## Distinctive meaning

A 批評 can identify strengths as well as weaknesses. Do not assume the English translation "criticism" means an insult or exclusively negative remarks. Nor is 批評 the same as retelling the plot: the evaluative viewpoint is important, whether the overall judgment is favorable or unfavorable.
