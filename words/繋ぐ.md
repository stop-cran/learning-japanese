# 繋ぐ (つなぐ) - connect; tie

## Meaning

繋ぐ is a transitive godan verb for connecting, linking, or tying things together. JMdict marks these uses as usually written in kana: **つなぐ**. Use **A を B につなぐ** for connecting A to B.

- パソコンをインターネットにつなぎました。 (パソコンをインターネットにつなぎました。) - I connected the computer to the internet.
- 担当者に電話をつないでください。 (たんとうしゃにでんわをつないでください。) - Please put the call through to the person in charge.

The second example uses the specific telephone-transfer sense. For tying, 船を岸につなぐ (ふねをきしにつなぐ) means "tie a boat to the shore." The past is つないだ, the て-form is つないで, and the negative is つながない.

## How the kanji combine

繋 contributes joining or fastening with the native reading つな-. The ending ぐ changes with conjugation. The kanji spelling and kana つなぐ represent the same verb here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結ぶ](結ぶ.md) (むすぶ) overlaps in tying or connecting. 接続 (せつぞく) names a connection, including technical connections. 繋がる (つながる) is intransitive: something is or becomes connected.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 電話をつなぐ, "put a call through," with 電話がつながる, "the call connects." The object marker を identifies what someone connects; が identifies what becomes connected. The English verb "connect" alone does not reveal this distinction.
