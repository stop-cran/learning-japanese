# 何時か (いつか) - sometime; someday

## Meaning

何時か, usually written いつか, is an adverb for an unspecified time. Depending on the surrounding tense and context, it can mean a future "someday" or a past "some time."

- いつか日本に住みたいです。 (いつかにほんにすみたいです。) - I want to live in Japan someday.
- その話はいつか聞いたことがあります。 (そのはなしはいつかきいたことがあります。) - I have heard that story at some point before.

The word leaves the date open; it does not itself promise that an event will happen soon.

## How the kanji combine

[何](../kanji/何.md) suggests an unspecified "what," and [時](../kanji/時.md) supplies time. The written combination 何時 is read いつ as a whole here; do not assemble it as なに + じ. Adding か makes the time indefinite. Kana いつか avoids confusion with 何時 (なんじ), "what time?"

## Synonyms and antonyms

[そのうち](そのうち.md) can also mean "before long; one of these days," locating an event in a vaguely anticipated period. [やがて](やがて.md) presents something as coming about in due course. Neither simply supplies a calendar date missing from いつか.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish いつか, "sometime," from いつ, "when?" Also distinguish its spelling from 五日 (いつか), "the fifth; five days." The same kana pronunciation does not make those expressions interchangeable.
