# 無事 (ぶじ) - safe; without incident

## Meaning

無事 is a noun and na-adjective for safety or being unharmed.
無事に describes an action happening safely or without incident.
Learn 無事です, "is safe," and 無事を祈る, "pray for safety."

- 家族はみんな無事です。 (かぞくはみんなぶじです。) - Everyone in my family is safe.
- 飛行機は無事に着陸しました。 (ひこうきはぶじにちゃくりくしました。) - The plane landed safely.

The first example reports people's condition. The second evaluates
how an event turned out. 無事に終わる (ぶじにおわる)
means ending without trouble or mishap.

## How the kanji combine

無 contributes absence, and [事](../kanji/事.md) contributes
an event or matter. In this established word, the absence concerns
trouble or an untoward incident, not the literal absence of all
activity. The readings combine as ぶ + じ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[安全](安全.md) (あんぜん) concerns safety from danger.
[安心](安心.md) (あんしん) concerns feeling reassured or free
from worry. A report that someone is 無事 may bring 安心,
but the person's condition and the listener's feeling are different.

## Distinctive meaning

"Quiet" in an English gloss should not be read as low sound
volume. A noisy celebration can finish 無事に if nothing goes
wrong. The word is especially useful for reassuring someone after
a journey, accident, difficult event, or period of uncertainty.
