# 電気 (でんき) — electricity, electric light

## Meaning

電気 is electricity: 電気代 でんきだい (electricity bill), 電気を使う (use electricity). In everyday speech it also means the light: 電気をつける (turn on the light), 電気を消す (turn off the light).
It also shows up in appliance names such as 電気ストーブ (electric heater).

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) (electricity) and [気](../kanji/気.md) combine with on readings: デン + キ.
As a mnemonic, think "electric energy"; this is a memory aid, not an explanation of the word's historical formation.
This is the same 気 as in [天気](天気.md), both read キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 電力 でんりょく (electric power), 電灯 でんとう (electric lamp, more formal).
- Antonym of 電気をつける: 電気を消す.

## Distinctive meaning

電気 is both the physical quantity and the light. For the light only, 電灯 でんとう and 明かり あかり exist, but 電気をつけて is the normal phrase.
