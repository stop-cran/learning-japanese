# 監督 (かんとく) - supervision; director; coach

## Meaning

監督 names supervision or direction, and also a person who directs: a supervisor, film director, or sports manager/coach. **監督する** is transitive; を marks the work or people supervised.

- 工事を監督する人が必要です。 (こうじをかんとくするひとがひつようです。) - We need someone to supervise the construction work.
- 監督は選手たちに指示を出しました。 (かんとくはせんしゅたちにしじをだしました。) - The coach gave the players instructions.

映画監督 (えいがかんとく) explicitly identifies a film director. The surrounding activity tells you which occupational translation fits.

## How the kanji combine

監 contributes watching over or overseeing, and 督 contributes directing or supervising. The two related ideas reinforce one another. カン + トク gives かんとく; the compound can name both the function and the person responsible.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[指導](指導.md) (しどう) is guidance or instruction and can be part of a supervisor's work. A sports [選手](選手.md) (せんしゅ) is a player or competitor, a different role rather than an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 監督 as "coach" without checking the setting: a film director is also a 監督. Nor does the title alone tell you exactly how authority is divided in every organization or sport. In 監督する, focus on the act of overseeing, not on an English job title.
