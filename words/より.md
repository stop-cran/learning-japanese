# より — than

## Meaning

より marks the comparison standard. In AはBより + description, A has the stated quality to a greater degree than B. Japanese does not need a special comparative adjective ending.

このかばんはあのかばんより軽いです。 (このかばんはあのかばんよりかるいです。) — This bag is lighter than that bag.

電車よりバスの方が安いです。 (でんしゃよりバスのほうがやすいです。) — The bus is cheaper than the train.

## How the kanji combine

より is written in kana. As a comparison particle, it follows the standard: あのかばんより, "than that bag." It does not go before the noun as English "than" does.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ほど with a negative gives another comparison pattern: AはBほど高くない, "A is not as expensive as B." It is not a direct substitution for より.

## Distinctive meaning

より can also be an adverb meaning "more," as in より良い方法 (よりよいほうほう), "a better method." Other uses include formal "from." Identify the construction rather than translating every より as "than."
