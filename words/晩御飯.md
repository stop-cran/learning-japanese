# 晩御飯 (ばんごはん)

## Meaning

晩御飯 means dinner or the evening meal.

- 今日の晩御飯はカレーです。 (きょうの ばんごはんは カレーです。) — Tonight's dinner is curry.
- 家族と晩御飯を食べました。 (かぞくと ばんごはんを たべました。) — I had dinner with my family.

It is a noun: use 晩御飯を食べる for eating dinner, not 晩御飯する as the basic learner pattern.

## How the kanji combine

[晩](../kanji/晩.md) specifies evening. [御](../kanji/御.md) contributes ご and [飯](../kanji/飯.md) contributes はん in 御飯, "rice; meal." The whole reading is ばん + ごはん. 晩ご飯 and 晩ごはん are also ordinary spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

夕飯 (ゆうはん) and 夕食 (ゆうしょく) also mean the evening meal. 朝御飯 (あさごはん) is breakfast, and 昼御飯 (ひるごはん) is lunch; these are meal-time contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

御飯 in this compound means the meal, not necessarily cooked rice. 晩御飯 can consist of noodles, bread, or another dish without rice. The word is everyday and does not imply a formal dinner party.
