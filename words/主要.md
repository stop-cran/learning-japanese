# 主要 (しゅよう) - main; principal

## Meaning

主要 means main, principal, or major. It is a na-adjective and takes な before a noun: 主要な原因 (しゅようなげんいん), "a major cause." It identifies something central among the relevant items.

- 会議では主要な問題を話し合いました。 (かいぎではしゅようなもんだいをはなしあいました。) - We discussed the main issues at the meeting.
- この道路は二つの主要な都市を結んでいます。 (このどうろはふたつのしゅようなとしをむすんでいます。) - This road connects two major cities.

The second example shows that several things can be 主要. The word does not necessarily select one unique winner.

## How the kanji combine

[主](../kanji/主.md) contributes main or leading, and [要](../kanji/要.md) contributes essential or important. Their related meanings reinforce the idea of principal importance. The on readings are シュ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

主な (おもな) is another useful form before a noun meaning "main." [重要](重要.md) (じゅうよう) means important, which need not mean principal within a set. 副次的 (ふくじてき), "secondary," supplies a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

You will also meet compact compounds such as 主要都市 (しゅようとし), "major cities." That compound form does not change the ordinary teaching pattern 主要な + noun. Nor does 主要 mean "necessary" in every context: an item can be [必要](必要.md) (ひつよう), necessary, without being a main feature.
