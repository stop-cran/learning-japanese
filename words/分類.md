# 分類 (ぶんるい) - classification; sorting

## Meaning

分類 is classification, categorization or sorting into groups. It is a noun and a
transitive する verb: things を分類する. The classification criterion can take
によって or による, and the resulting groups can take に.

- 本を分野別に分類しました。 (ほんをぶんやべつにぶんるいしました。) - I classified the books by subject area.
- ごみを種類によって分類してください。 (ごみをしゅるいによってぶんるいしてください。) - Please sort the rubbish according to its type.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) contributes dividing; [類](../kanji/類.md) contributes
kind or category. The compound concerns dividing things into kinds: ブン + ルイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[区分](区分.md) (くぶん) also concerns division or classification.
[整理](整理.md) (せいり) can include sorting while putting things in order.
[分ける](分ける.md) (わける) is a broader divide/separate verb that also has a
classifying use. [区別](区別.md) (くべつ) foregrounds distinguishing things;
[種類](種類.md) (しゅるい) names a kind, not necessarily the act of classifying.

## Distinctive meaning

分類 requires a way of grouping things, not merely making a pile physically smaller.
分類する can be based on purpose, subject, material or another relevant criterion.
It does not itself guarantee that the classification is scientifically correct or
that one group is superior to another.
