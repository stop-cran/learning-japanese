# 回復 (かいふく) - recovery; restoration

## Meaning

回復 is recovery or restoration of a condition. 回復する can describe something recovering or someone regaining something. Learn both が回復する and を回復する in their contexts.

- 父は病気から回復しました。 (ちちはびょうきからかいふくしました。) - My father recovered from his illness.
- 少し休んで体力を回復しました。 (すこしやすんでたいりょくをかいふくしました。) - I rested a little and regained my strength.

景気が回復する (けいきがかいふくする) means "the economy recovers." Here 景気 is what improves, not an object someone directly acts on.

## How the kanji combine

[回](../kanji/回.md) contributes turning or returning, and 復 contributes returning or restoring. Their related meanings combine around a return toward an earlier or better condition. The on readings are カイ + フク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[治る](治る.md) (なおる) describes an illness or injury healing. 回復 has wider applications, including strength and economic conditions. [改善](改善.md) (かいぜん) means improvement and need not involve regaining a previous condition.

## Distinctive meaning

回復 does not by itself specify that recovery is complete: 徐々に回復する (じょじょにかいふくする) means "recover gradually." The medical sense can also be written 快復, but 回復 works beyond illness. Choose the subject and particles for the actual recovery, rather than assuming every use is either transitive or intransitive.
