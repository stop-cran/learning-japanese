# 高校生 (こうこうせい)

## Meaning

高校生 means a senior high school student. It names a person, not the school itself.

- 妹は高校生です。 (いもうとは こうこうせいです。) — My younger sister is a high school student.

As a noun it takes の before another noun: 高校生の弟 (こうこうせいのおとうと), "my younger brother who is a high school student." Do not add な.

## How the kanji combine

高校 (こうこう) is the shortened form of 高等学校 (こうとうがっこう), "senior high school." [高](../kanji/高.md) means high, [校](../kanji/校.md) concerns school, and [生](../kanji/生.md) marks a student here. The on readings are こう + こう + せい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

学生 (がくせい) is broader. 中学生 (ちゅうがくせい) is a junior high school student, while 大学生 (だいがくせい) is a university student. These identify different educational stages, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

高校生 describes enrollment at a school level rather than an exact age. Keep 高校, the institution, separate from 高校生, the person attending it.
