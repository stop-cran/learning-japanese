# じろじろ — staring scrutinizingly

## Meaning

Looking closely or repeatedly in a scrutinizing way, often making another person feel observed.

- 知らない人にじろじろ見られて、落（お）ち着（つ）かなかった。 — Being stared at by a stranger made me uneasy.
- 店員（てんいん）は客（きゃく）の服をじろじろ見た。 — The clerk looked the customer's clothes up and down.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[じっと](じっと.md) can also describe a fixed gaze, often more neutrally. [ちらちら](ちらちら.md) emphasizes brief glances; [きょろきょろ](きょろきょろ.md) shifts attention around.

## Distinctive meaning

じろじろ見る frequently implies an intrusive or unwelcome stare, especially at people. That is a usage tendency, not proof of the observer's intention. It does not simply mean seeing something clearly.
