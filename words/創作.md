# 創作 (そうさく) - creative production; original work; fabrication

## Meaning

創作 is creating something original, especially a story or other artistic
work. It can name the creative activity or the resulting work.
創作する is transitive: the work created takes を.

- 授業で短い物語を創作しました。 (じゅぎょうでみじかいものがたりをそうさくしました。) - We created a short story in class.
- これは彼女の創作です。 (これはかのじょのそうさくです。) - This is an original work of hers.

There is also a fabrication or made-up-story use:
その話は創作だった (そのはなしはそうさくだった),
"That story was made up." Fiction as an art form need not be deceptive;
the context tells you whether a supposed fact is being challenged.

## How the kanji combine

創 contributes originating or creating, and [作](../kanji/作.md)
making. Their related ideas emphasize creation.
The on readings are ソウ + サク.
These associations are not a rule that every new object must be called 創作.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[制作](制作.md) (せいさく) concerns artistic production.
[作品](作品.md) (さくひん) names a creative piece, often the result.
[作る](作る.md) (つくる) is the broader everyday verb "make."
[小説](小説.md) (しょうせつ) is prose fiction;
創作 is not restricted to novels or even to literary writing.

## Distinctive meaning

English "work" here does not mean every job or task.
Likewise, the fabrication use overlaps with making something up,
but not every 創作 is a [嘘](嘘.md), lie.
[発明](発明.md) (はつめい), invention, is especially useful for
devising a new device or method rather than simply writing a story.
