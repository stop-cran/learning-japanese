# 明日 (あした) — tomorrow

## Meaning

明日 means the day after today. Both あした and あす mean "tomorrow"; あした is a useful everyday reading, while あす is also heard in announcements and forecasts. みょうにち is a more formal reading.

- 明日は休みです。 (あしたはやすみです。) — Tomorrow is a day off.
- 明日、雨が降るそうです。 (あす、あめがふるそうです。) — Apparently it will rain tomorrow.

## How the kanji combine

[明](../kanji/明.md), "bright," and [日](../kanji/日.md), "day," write the word as a whole in あした and あす. Do not divide either reading into separate on-readings. みょうにち uses on-readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

今日 (きょう) is today, 昨日 (きのう) is yesterday, and 明後日 (あさって) is the day after tomorrow. 翌日 (よくじつ) is "the following day," relative to a day already mentioned.

## Distinctive meaning

明日 is anchored to the speaker's present day. As a time expression it normally needs no に: 明日行きます (あしたいきます), "I will go tomorrow." With あす, it can also refer figuratively to the near future; that extension is not needed to understand the basic calendar meaning.
