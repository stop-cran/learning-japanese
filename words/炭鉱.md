# 炭鉱 (たんこう) - coal mine

## Meaning

炭鉱 is a coal mine: a place where coal is extracted from the ground.
It is a noun, not the name of the fuel itself.

- 昔、この町には炭鉱がありました。
  (むかし、このまちにはたんこうがありました。) - There used to be a coal mine in this town.
- 祖父は炭鉱で働いていました。
  (そふはたんこうではたらいていました。) - My grandfather used to work at a coal mine.

で marks the workplace. 炭鉱から石炭を運ぶ
(たんこうからせきたんをはこぶ) means "transport coal from the mine."

## How the kanji combine

[炭](../kanji/炭.md) contributes coal and
[鉱](../kanji/鉱.md) mineral extraction or ore.
The compound names a mine for coal. The on readings are タン + コウ;
こう has a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

鉱山 (こうざん) is the broader word for a mine, including mines for
resources other than coal. [石炭](石炭.md) (せきたん) is coal,
the material extracted. A mine and its product are not synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

炭坑 and 炭礦 are recorded spellings for the coal-mine meaning.
In the pinned dictionary, the separate "coal-mine shaft" sense is
specifically marked 炭坑; do not silently apply every sense to every spelling.
The workplace noun alone does not specify whether the mine is operating today.
