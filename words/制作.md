# 制作 (せいさく) - artistic production; creation

## Meaning

制作 concerns creating or producing a work, such as a film or an artistic
piece. It is a noun and takes する as a transitive verb.
The dictionary's English "work (film, book)" belongs to this creative
context, not unrestricted employment.

- 学生たちが短い映画を制作しました。 (がくせいたちがみじかいえいがをせいさくしました。) - The students created a short film.
- 今、新しい作品を制作しています。 (いま、あたらしいさくひんをせいさくしています。) - I am creating a new work now.

制作中 (せいさくちゅう) means in production or being created.
The noun can describe the creative undertaking, not only the act of
physically assembling material.

## How the kanji combine

[制](../kanji/制.md) has an organizing or regulating association, and
[作](../kanji/作.md) contributes making. Learn the established creative
production meaning rather than translating this literally as regulating work.
The on readings are セイ + サク, the same pronunciation as 製作.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[製作](製作.md) also applies to films and other media; artistic versus
industrial emphasis is a tendency, not a strict border.
[作成](作成.md) commonly concerns drawing up documents or other prepared
material. [作品](作品.md) names a creative work or piece, often the result,
whereas 制作する explicitly describes making it.

## Distinctive meaning

The precise scope of a production credit depends on the organization.
Do not derive a universal division between financing and hands-on creation
from the two せいさく spellings. Nor does 制作 replace [仕事](仕事.md)
for every job or [作業](作業.md) for every task simply because English
can call all of them "work."
