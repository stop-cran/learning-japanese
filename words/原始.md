# 原始 (げんし) - origin; primeval

## Meaning

原始 concerns an origin or beginning, and an early, primitive, or primeval
state. It is a noun; 原始の can modify a following noun.
Learn the beginning sense with this early-state association, not as a
replacement for every everyday "start."

- 原始の地球には、今とは違う環境がありました。 (げんしのちきゅうには、いまとはちがうかんきょうがありました。) - The primeval Earth had an environment different from today's.
- この森には原始の姿が残っています。 (このもりにはげんしのすがたがのこっています。) - This forest retains its primeval appearance.

原始時代 (げんしじだい) means a primitive or prehistoric age;
原始林 (げんしりん) is a primeval forest. A natural state need not be
an inferior one.

## How the kanji combine

[原](../kanji/原.md) contributes original or primitive, and [始](../kanji/始.md)
contributes beginning. Together they direct attention to an initial state.
The on readings combine as ゲン + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[始まり](始まり.md) (はじまり) and [初め](初め.md) (はじめ) are useful
general words for a beginning. [最初](最初.md) (さいしょ) concerns being
first or at the beginning. These are not restricted to primeval conditions.

## Distinctive meaning

Something is not 原始 merely because it is old. Nor does the word require
a judgment that ancient people were unintelligent. In the forest example
it concerns an original natural state, not an everyday event's starting time.
