# 骨折 (こっせつ) - bone fracture

## Meaning

骨折 is a bone fracture. With する, it means to break or fracture a bone; use body part + を骨折する to identify the injured part. This construction can describe an accident, not just an intentional action.

- 転んで腕を骨折しました。 (ころんでうでをこっせつしました。) - I fell and broke my arm.
- 医師は「骨折はしていません」と言いました。 (いしは「こっせつはしていません」といいました。) - The doctor said, "There is no fracture."

The second example specifically rules out a fracture, not every possible injury. 骨折した腕 (こっせつしたうで) means "the fractured arm."

## How the kanji combine

骨 means bone and [折](../kanji/折.md) contributes breaking. The compound describes a bone breaking. The reading has a small っ: こっせつ, from the on-reading combination, rather than こつせつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怪我](怪我.md) (けが) is injury more generally. A fracture is one kind of injury, not a synonym for every cut or bruise. [折る](折る.md) (おる) can mean break or fold; 骨を折る (ほねをおる) can describe breaking a bone in a literal context.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun names a particular type of injury. Pain or swelling alone is not what the word means, and the term does not specify severity or treatment. In language learning, keep the concrete medical meaning separate from figurative expressions involving 骨.
