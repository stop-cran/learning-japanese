# 会議室 (かいぎしつ) - meeting room

## Meaning

会議室 is a room used for meetings or conferences, for example in an office.

- 三時から会議室を使います。 (さんじからかいぎしつをつかいます。) - We will use the meeting room from three o'clock.

会議室で話す (かいぎしつではなす) means "talk in the meeting room": で marks the location of the activity. 会議室に入る (かいぎしつにはいる) instead uses に for entering the room.

## How the kanji combine

[会](../kanji/会.md) and [議](../kanji/議.md) form 会議 (かいぎ), "meeting; conference." [室](../kanji/室.md), シツ, adds "room." The compound uses on readings and names a room by its purpose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

会議 is the meeting itself, not the room. 教室 (きょうしつ) is a classroom; 事務室 (じむしつ) is an office room. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

An empty room can still be a 会議室. The word identifies its function; it does not say that a meeting is currently taking place.
