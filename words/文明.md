# 文明 (ぶんめい) - civilization

## Meaning

文明 is the noun "civilization." It can name a historical civilization or the developed systems, knowledge, and technologies of a society. Use 文明の before a noun, as in 文明の発展 (ぶんめいのはってん), "the development of civilization."

- 古代エジプトの文明について学びました。 (こだいエジプトのぶんめいについてまなびました。) - We learned about ancient Egyptian civilization.
- この博物館では、さまざまな文明を紹介しています。 (このはくぶつかんでは、さまざまなぶんめいをしょうかいしています。) - This museum presents various civilizations.

The noun can refer to one civilization or several; context provides the number.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) contributes writing or culture, and [明](../kanji/明.md) contributes brightness or enlightenment. The compound names civilization; the components provide an association, not a test for deciding which societies qualify.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[文化](文化.md) (ぶんか), "culture," overlaps but is useful for customs, arts, values, and ways of life. 文明 often brings a society's wider institutions and technical development into focus. This is a practical tendency, not an absolute material-versus-spiritual division.

## Distinctive meaning

文明 is not simply a synonym for modern technology: 古代文明 (こだいぶんめい) explicitly refers to ancient civilizations. The word also appears as the historical era name Bunmei, but that dated proper-name use is separate from the civilization meaning taught in the examples.
