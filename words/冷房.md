# 冷房 (れいぼう) - air cooling

## Meaning

冷房 means cooling indoor air, or air conditioning used for cooling.
Common everyday phrases are 冷房をつける, "turn on the cooling," and
冷房が効いている (れいぼうがきいている), "the cooling is working."

- 暑いので、冷房をつけました。 (あついので、れいぼうをつけました。) - It was hot, so I turned on the air conditioning.
- この部屋は冷房がよく効いています。 (このへやはれいぼうがよくきいています。) - This room is well air-conditioned.

## How the kanji combine

[冷](../kanji/冷.md) concerns cold or cooling; 房 contributes a room or chamber.
The on-readings レイ + ボウ give れいぼう: room cooling.
Keep both vowel sequences in the pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

暖房 (だんぼう), "heating," is the direct seasonal contrast.
エアコン names the air-conditioning appliance or system, which may offer
both cooling and heating. It is not restricted to 冷房 mode.

## Distinctive meaning

冷房 describes a function, not merely any device moving air. A fan circulates
air but is not normally called 冷房. The noun can form 冷房する,
"cool a room," although switching and operating-state phrases are especially
useful in daily conversation.
