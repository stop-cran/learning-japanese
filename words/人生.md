# 人生 (じんせい) - one's life; lifetime

## Meaning

人生 is a person's life considered as a course of experiences, choices, and events. It is a noun: **私の人生** (わたしのじんせい) means "my life," and 人生を変える means to change someone's life.

- その出会いが私の人生を変えました。 (そのであいがわたしのじんせいをかえました。) - That encounter changed my life.
- 人生にはいろいろな選択があります。 (じんせいにはいろいろなせんたくがあります。) - Life contains many different choices.

人生で初めて (じんせいではじめて) means "for the first time in one's life."

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) contributes a human being, and [生](../kanji/生.md) contributes life or living. 人 specifies whose life is being considered. The on readings are ジン + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[生活](生活.md) (せいかつ) concerns daily living and one's way or conditions of life. [命](命.md) (いのち) concerns life as what can be saved or lost. These overlap in English "life" but do not fit all the same sentences.

## Distinctive meaning

人生 is useful for reflecting on the direction or significance of a life, not just today's routine. Its lexical meaning does not establish a biological, philosophical, or legal starting point for life. The source list's "conception to death" wording should not be treated as part of the Japanese word's definition.
