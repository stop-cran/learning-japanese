# ハンカチ

## Meaning

ハンカチ means a handkerchief: a small cloth carried for wiping hands, sweat, or similar everyday uses.

- ハンカチで手を拭きました。 (ハンカチで てを ふきました。) — I wiped my hands with a handkerchief.
- かばんにハンカチを入れました。 (かばんに ハンカチを いれました。) — I put a handkerchief in my bag.

で marks the tool used for wiping; を marks the object put into the bag.

## How the kanji combine

This is a shortened katakana loan from "handkerchief." The ordinary form is ハンカチ; ハンケチ is a recognized variant but need not replace the basic spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

タオル is a towel. ティッシュ is tissue paper and is usually disposable, unlike a cloth handkerchief. These items may share uses without being exact synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

ハンカチ does not refer to any piece of fabric or every paper napkin. The word identifies a small personal cloth item. It has no natural antonym; useful contrasts concern material, size, and purpose rather than opposite meanings.
