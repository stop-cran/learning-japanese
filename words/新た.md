# 新た (あらた) - new; fresh

## Meaning

新た describes something new or fresh, including a new beginning, development,
or addition. It is a な-adjective: 新たな計画 (あらたなけいかく) is "a new plan."
The adverbial form 新たに means "newly" or "afresh."

- 新たな問題が見つかりました。 (あらたなもんだいがみつかりました。) - A new problem was found.
- 新たに三人がチームに加わりました。 (あらたにさんにんがチームにくわわりました。) - Three new people joined the team.

In the second example, 新たに marks an addition to the previous situation.

## How the kanji combine

[新](../kanji/新.md) carries the meaning "new." The native word is read あらた,
with た written in kana. This is a single-kanji word, not a compound whose
characters each supply a separate meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[新しい](新しい.md) (あたらしい) overlaps in meaning but is an い-adjective:
新しい計画, not 新しいな計画. [古い](古い.md) (ふるい), "old," can contrast
with something new, though a new problem is not necessarily opposed to an
old-fashioned problem.

## Distinctive meaning

新た is useful when presenting a development as a change from what came before.
It does not itself say whether that change is good: 新たな問題 can be unwelcome.
Learn the attachment difference alongside 新しい rather than treating あらた
as a shortened pronunciation of あたらしい.
