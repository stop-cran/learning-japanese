# 虫 (むし) — insect; bug

## Meaning

虫 is an insect or bug in everyday speech. Its range is broader than the strict biological category "insect": it can also refer to worms and other small creatures in context.

窓から虫が入ってきました。 (まどからむしがはいってきました。) — A bug came in through the window.

庭で虫を見つけました。 (にわでむしをみつけました。) — I found a bug in the garden.

## How the kanji combine

虫 alone writes the native word むし. There is no compound or kana ending. The older form 蟲 exists, but 虫 is the ordinary modern spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

昆虫 (こんちゅう) is the more specific word for insects as a biological group. 蚊 (か) is a mosquito, and 蝶 (ちょう) is a butterfly: these name particular creatures rather than the broad category.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 匹 (ひき) to count small creatures: 一匹 (いっぴき), 二匹 (にひき). The noun does not imply that the creature is harmful. 虫 also appears in figurative expressions and compound names, so a literal "insect" translation will not fit every word containing the character.
