# 少々 (しょうしょう) - a little; a short while

## Meaning

少々 expresses a small amount, a slight degree, or a short time. It can function as an adverb or quantity expression. The surrounding action shows what is small.

- 少々お待ちください。 (しょうしょうおまちください。) - Please wait a moment.
- 塩を少々加えてください。 (しおをしょうしょうくわえてください。) - Please add a little salt.

The first sentence is a useful polite request in shops or on the telephone. The second concerns quantity, not waiting time. 少々難しい (しょうしょうむずかしい) means "a little difficult."

## How the kanji combine

[少](../kanji/少.md) contributes small quantity. The mark 々 repeats the preceding kanji, producing しょうしょう. It is an iteration mark, not a separate kanji with an independent meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[少し](少し.md) (すこし) overlaps in amount, degree, and time. Compare 少し待ってください with the service-style request 少々お待ちください; the politeness also comes from お待ちください, not from 少々 alone.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not convert the translation "a minute" into a promise of exactly sixty seconds. Similarly, a recipe's 少々 gives a small amount rather than a universal weight. The expression is deliberately approximate; use a numerical duration or measurement when precision is required. It can soften an evaluation, but context still determines how serious the difficulty is.
