# 小学生 (しょうがくせい) - elementary school pupil

## Meaning

小学生 is an elementary or primary school pupil: someone attending 小学校 (しょうがっこう).

- 妹は小学生です。 (いもうとはしょうがくせいです。) - My younger sister is an elementary school pupil.
- この本は小学生向けです。 (このほんはしょうがくせいむけです。) - This book is intended for elementary school children.

In 小学生向け, 向け (むけ) identifies the intended audience. The noun names a learner, not the school building.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md), small, combines with [学](../kanji/学.md), learning, and [生](../kanji/生.md), here associated with a student. Learn 小学校 / 小学生 as an institutional pair rather than using "small" alone to infer someone's school level.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[学生](学生.md) and [生徒](生徒.md) are broader learner words whose usual range depends on context. [児童](児童.md) overlaps with children and elementary pupils; [子](子.md) and [子供](子供.md) are broader child words. None of these broader labels independently specifies elementary enrollment.

## Distinctive meaning

In Japan, elementary school normally has six school years. That contextual fact does not make a fixed age the definition of 小学生 in every country or circumstance. A middle-school or university student is not a 小学生 merely because all attend educational institutions.
