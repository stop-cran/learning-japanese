# みっともない (みっともない) - unsightly; shameful; unseemly

## Meaning

みっともない is an i-adjective judging an appearance or action as unsightly, shameful, indecent or unseemly.

- そんなみっともないことはやめなさい。 (そんなみっともないことはやめなさい。) - Stop doing something so unseemly.
- 人前で大声でけんかするのはみっともない。 (ひとまえでおおごえでけんかするのはみっともない。) - Arguing loudly in public is disgraceful.

Its forms include みっともなかった, "was unseemly," and みっともなくない, "is not unseemly."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[恥ずかしい](恥ずかしい.md) (はずかしい) can concern feeling embarrassed as well as something embarrassing or shameful. [下品](下品.md) (げひん) overlaps in indecency or vulgarity. 見苦しい (みぐるしい), "unpleasant to look at; unsightly," also overlaps; these are not rigidly separate categories.

## Distinctive meaning

The word is usually written in kana. It can be a sharp criticism of conduct, not just a comment about visual beauty. English "shameful" does not mean "shameless": [厚かましい](厚かましい.md) (あつかましい) instead concerns brazen impudence.
