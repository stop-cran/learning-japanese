# 消す (けす) — erase

## Meaning

消す can mean erase or delete. It is transitive: the writing or other thing removed takes を.
It is also used for turning off a light or putting out a fire, with the English translation changing to match the object.

- 黒板の字を消しました。 (こくばんのじをけしました。) — I erased the writing on the blackboard.
- 電気を消してください。 (でんきをけしてください。) — Please turn off the light.

This godan verb forms 消します (けします), 消して (けして), and 消した (けした).

## How the kanji combine

[消](../kanji/消.md) expresses extinguishing or making something disappear.
The native reading け- and the ending す form 消す.
Compare the different native reading in 消える (きえる); reading the shared kanji identically would give the wrong verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

消える (きえる) is not a synonym with the same grammar.
字が消える (じがきえる), "the writing disappears," describes what happens to the writing;
字を消す (じをけす), "erase the writing," describes acting on it.

## Distinctive meaning

The object guides the interpretation. Erasing blackboard writing does not mean destroying the blackboard,
and 電気を消す refers to turning off the light in the example.
Learn the verb with its object instead of treating "erase" as a fixed translation in every sentence.
