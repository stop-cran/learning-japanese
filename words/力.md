# 力 (ちから) — strength, power

## Meaning

力 is physical strength, ability or force: 力が強い (strong), 力を入れる (put effort in), 力になる (be of help), 日本語の力 (Japanese ability), 力持ち ちからもち (a strong person).
It also means influence and effect: 経済の力, 薬の力.

## How the kanji combine

A single kanji, [力](../kanji/力.md), with the kun reading ちから. The on readings リョク and リキ appear in compounds instead: 体力 たいりょく, 努力 どりょく, 力士 りきし.
The kun reading voices in a few compounds: 力強い ちからづよい, 底力 そこぢから.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 能力 のうりょく (ability), 体力 たいりょく (physical strength), 権力 けんりょく (political power), 勢い いきおい (momentum).
- Antonym: 弱さ よわさ (weakness).

## Distinctive meaning

力 is the general everyday word and covers physical and abstract power. 能力 stresses capability, 体力 stamina, 努力 is effort.
