# 船便 (ふなびん) - sea mail; shipping service

## Meaning

船便 means surface mail sent by ship. The word also has a boat or ferry service sense.

- 荷物を船便で送りました。 (にもつをふなびんでおくりました。) - I sent the parcel by sea mail.
- 船便では到着まで時間がかかります。 (ふなびんではとうちゃくまでじかんがかかります。) - Delivery by sea mail takes time.

## How the kanji combine

[船](../kanji/船.md), ship, modifies [便](../kanji/便.md), a transport or delivery service. ふなびん combines native ふな with on-reading ビン, a kun-first mixed reading. It is stored under wago because the format has no separate mixed category; that does not make both parts native readings. The dictionary also records せんびん, using on readings セン + ビン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[郵便](郵便.md) (ゆうびん) is mail without specifying this sea route. [便](便.md) (びん) is broader service or transport language. Sea mail is a transport method, not a promise that every parcel will arrive after the same number of days.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not interpret every transport use of 船便 as postal mail: the additional dictionary sense concerns sailings or a ferry/steamer service. Here the main learning examples teach the source list's mail sense. Keep ふなびん as the primary reading rather than silently replacing it with せんびん.
