# 複写 (ふくしゃ) - copying; duplication

## Meaning

複写 is copying, duplication, or a reproduced copy.
複写する is a transitive verb: the original being copied takes を.
The method can involve handwriting, photography, or a copying
device; the word is not limited to a particular machine.

- この書類を二部複写してください。 (このしょるいをにぶふくしゃしてください。) - Please make two copies of this document.
- 複写した資料を配りました。 (ふくしゃしたしりょうをくばりました。) - I distributed the copied materials.

二部 counts two copies of a document, not two individual kanji.

## How the kanji combine

[複](../kanji/複.md) contributes duplication or multiplicity,
and [写](../kanji/写.md) copying or reproducing.
Together they name making another representation of an original.
The on readings are フク + シャ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[コピー](コピー.md) also names a copy or the act of copying.
[写す](写す.md) (うつす) is a broader native verb with copying
and photographing uses. 複製 (ふくせい) also concerns reproduction;
the objects and method determine the most suitable expression.
[清書](清書.md) (せいしょ) specifically emphasizes a neat
or fair written version.

## Distinctive meaning

複写 concerns reproducing an original, not simply creating any new
document or expressing a similar idea.
複写機 (ふくしゃき) is a copying machine, but the presence of that
compound does not restrict the verb to photocopying.
A faithful copy is not necessarily a corrected or improved version.
