# 生徒 (せいと) — pupil; student

## Meaning

生徒 is a pupil or student: someone learning in a school or under instruction. It is a noun for the
learner, not for the act of studying.

- この中学校には三百人の生徒がいます。 (このちゅうがっこうにはさんびゃくにんのせいとがいます。) — This junior high school has three hundred pupils.

The number describes people, so 人 (にん) is the counter in 三百人.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) occurs in learner-related words, and [徒](../kanji/徒.md) has a people or pupil
use here. Together they name the person receiving instruction. The on readings are セイ + ト.
The walking sense of 徒 in 徒歩 (とほ), "on foot," is not the meaning to use in 生徒.

## Synonyms and antonyms

In the usual school distinction, 生徒 refers to junior-high and high-school pupils,
[学生](学生.md) (がくせい) to university or college students, and 児童 (じどう) to elementary-school pupils.
These are useful institutional distinctions, not absolute boundaries: 学生 can also be used broadly
for learners, including school pupils.
[教師](教師.md) (きょうし) is the teacher, a contrasting role rather than a lexical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Outside that school-level distinction, 生徒 also has a broader sense of someone receiving instruction;
the word by itself does not fix their age. It describes a learner in relation to teaching, rather than
just anyone who happens to be reading or researching something.
