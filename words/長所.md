# 長所 (ちょうしょ) - strong point; merit; advantage

## Meaning

長所 is a strong point, merit, or advantage of a person, thing, or method.
It is a noun: **Nの長所** identifies whose or what good points are meant.

- 彼女の長所は、最後まで諦めないことです。
  (かのじょのちょうしょは、さいごまであきらめないことです。) - Her strong point is that she does not give up.
- この方法には長所も短所もあります。
  (このほうほうにはちょうしょもたんしょもあります。) - This method has both advantages and disadvantages.

## How the kanji combine

[長](../kanji/長.md) contributes a positive or outstanding quality here,
and [所](../kanji/所.md) an aspect or point.
The on readings are チョウ + ショ.
This is not a physically long place.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[短所](短所.md) (たんしょ), shortcoming or disadvantage, is the direct contrast.
[得意](得意.md) (とくい) overlaps with a person's strong area;
長所 can also concern character or a product's design.
[利益](利益.md) (りえき) includes benefit or advantage, often an outcome
rather than an inherent good point. [長短](長短.md) (ちょうたん)
can name strengths and weaknesses together.

## Distinctive meaning

A [特徴](特徴.md) (とくちょう), characteristic, need not be good.
Calling something a 長所 evaluates it positively.
The dictionary gloss virtue need not mean moral goodness in the abstract:
[善](善.md) (ぜん) focuses on that moral idea.
[味噌](味噌.md) (みそ), primarily miso paste, also has a separate
dictionary sense for a key or good point; that is not a food meaning of 長所.
