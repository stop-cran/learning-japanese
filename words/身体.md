# 身体 (しんたい) - body; physical system

## Meaning

身体 is a noun for the body or physical system. This card teaches
しんたい, a reading useful in expressions concerning health, physical
ability, and the body as an object of study.

- 運動は身体の健康に役立ちます。 (うんどうはしんたいのけんこうにやくだちます。) - Exercise benefits physical health.
- 学校で身体測定を受けました。 (がっこうでしんたいそくていをうけました。) - I had my body measurements taken at school.

身体の + noun links the body to a feature or condition.
身体測定 (しんたいそくてい) is an established compound for
measuring bodily dimensions, such as height and weight.

## How the kanji combine

身 and [体](../kanji/体.md) both contribute the body or physical
person. The compound combines related meanings rather than opposing
them. Its on readings are しん + たい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[体](体.md) (からだ) is the familiar everyday word for the body.
[心](心.md) (こころ), "mind; heart," and [精神](精神.md)
(せいしん), "mind; spirit," provide contextual contrasts when discussing
physical and mental aspects of a person.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling 身体 is also read からだ; the dictionary explicitly
notes that reading. Do not assume that every printed 身体 must be
pronounced しんたい. Conversely, the valid reading しんたい should
not be dismissed because からだ is encountered in ordinary speech.
Learn fixed compounds with their full readings.
