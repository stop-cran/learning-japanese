# 女 (おんな) - woman; female

## Meaning

女 means "woman" or "female." It occurs alone and in expressions such as 女の人 (おんなのひと), "woman," and 女の子 (おんなのこ), "girl."

- あの女の人は先生です。 (あのおんなのひとはせんせいです。) - That woman is a teacher.
- 姉の子供は女の子です。 (あねのこどもはおんなのこです。) - My older sister's child is a girl.

It is a noun, not an adjective conjugating with い. The の in these expressions connects it to another noun.

## How the kanji combine

[女](../kanji/女.md) is a single character with the native reading おんな. The on reading じょ appears in other words, including 女性 (じょせい), "woman; female."

## Synonyms and antonyms

女の人 and 女性 are more courteous ways to refer to a woman. 男 (おとこ), "man; male," is the contrasting term.

## Distinctive meaning

Bare 女 can sound blunt when used to identify a particular person, especially in expressions such as あの女. The meaning is not inherently insulting in every context, but 女の人 is a safer beginner choice in everyday references to someone you do not know.
