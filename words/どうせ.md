# どうせ - anyway; whatever happens

## Meaning

どうせ presents an outcome as something that will happen regardless.
It often conveys resignation, dismissal or a low expectation.

- どうせ間に合わないから、走るのはやめよう。
  (どうせまにあわないから、はしるのはやめよう。) - We won't make it in time anyway, so let's stop running.
- どうせ行くなら、朝早く出よう。
  (どうせいくなら、あさはやくでよう。) - If we're going anyway, let's leave early in the morning.

The second pattern, どうせ + conditional, treats the action as already
accepted and suggests making the most of it. It need not express pessimism.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とにかく](とにかく.md) can mean "anyway; at any rate" when setting other
considerations aside. [結局](結局.md) (けっきょく) focuses on the eventual result.
[やはり](やはり.md) and [やっぱり](やっぱり.md) can confirm an expectation.
Their English translations overlap, but they do not always carry どうせ's dismissive stance.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling someone どうせ... can sound belittling, as if their efforts or prospects
do not matter. Do not insert it for every neutral English "anyway."
The inevitability is the speaker's assessment, not a grammatical guarantee
that the prediction is true.
