# 謙遜 (けんそん) - modesty; self-deprecation

## Meaning

謙遜 is modesty or humbling oneself, including speaking modestly about
one's achievements. It is a noun, an intransitive する verb, and a
na-adjective: 謙遜する and 謙遜な態度 (けんそんなたいど),
"a modest attitude."

- 彼は褒められても謙遜しています。 (かれはほめられてもけんそんしています。) - He remains modest even when praised.
- そんなに謙遜しなくてもいいですよ。 (そんなにけんそんしなくてもいいですよ。) - You do not need to be so modest.

In the second sentence, the speaker reassures someone who is downplaying
their own ability or contribution.

## How the kanji combine

謙 contributes humbling oneself or being modest, and 遜 contributes
humility or yielding. Their related meanings reinforce the idea of
lowering one's own claims. The on readings combine as ケン + ソン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[謙虚](謙虚.md) (けんきょ) also describes being modest or humble.
謙遜 can draw attention to modest self-presentation, but this is not a
strict outward-versus-inward division between the two words.
[遠慮](遠慮.md) (えんりょ) concerns holding back out of consideration;
[恐縮](恐縮.md) (きょうしゅく) concerns feeling obliged or apologetically grateful.

## Distinctive meaning

謙遜 need not be insincere, and it does not require claiming that one has
no ability. It is also not the name of the grammatical humble-language
system. The mixed spelling 謙そん is recorded as search-only in this
JMdict snapshot; 謙遜 is the teaching spelling here.
