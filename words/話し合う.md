# 話し合う (はなしあう) - discuss; talk together

## Meaning

話し合う is a godan verb meaning to discuss or talk something over together.
Use 人と話し合う (ひととはなしあう) for the discussion partner and Xについて話し合う for the topic.
The topic can also be a direct object: 問題を話し合う (もんだいをはなしあう), "discuss a problem."

- 家族と旅行の計画について話し合いました。 (かぞくとりょこうのけいかくについてはなしあいました。) - I discussed our travel plans with my family.
- この問題をみんなで話し合いましょう。 (このもんだいをみんなではなしあいましょう。) - Let's all discuss this problem.

The て-form is 話し合って (はなしあって), and the negative is 話し合わない (はなしあわない).

## How the kanji combine

[話](../kanji/話.md) appears in 話す (はなす), "speak."
Its stem 話し combines with [合う](合う.md) (あう), using [合](../kanji/合.md) to express mutual or joint action here.
Conjugate the final 合う portion, not the internal 話し.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話す](話す.md) is broader and does not by itself require a mutual discussion.
[相談](相談.md) (そうだん) emphasizes consultation or seeking advice.
[議論](議論.md) (ぎろん) can emphasize debating or examining competing views.

## Distinctive meaning

話し合う emphasizes exchange between participants rather than a one-way announcement.
It does not guarantee agreement: 長く話し合ったが、決まらなかった means "We discussed it for a long time, but nothing was decided."
An outcome needs to be stated separately.
