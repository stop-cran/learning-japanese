# 懲りる (こりる) — learn a lesson; be put off

## Meaning

懲りる is to have such a bad experience that you resolve not to repeat it. Digital Daijisen defines it as 失敗してひどい目にあい、もうやるまいと思う (しっぱいしてひどいめにあい、もうやるまいとおもう), “to fail, suffer badly and resolve never to do it again,” and gives 二度の失敗ですっかり懲りた (にどのしっぱいですっかりこりた), “after failing twice, I had completely learned my lesson.” JMdict lists “to learn by experience; to learn one’s lesson” and “to be discouraged (by); to have enough (of).”

- 一度ひどい船酔いをして懲りたので、もう船には乗らない。 (いちどひどいふなよいをしてこりたので、もうふねにはのらない。) — I got terribly seasick once and learned my lesson, so I don’t take boats anymore.
- 彼は何度遅刻しても懲りない。 (かれはなんどちこくしてもこりない。) — No matter how many times he is late, he never learns.

## How the kanji combine

[懲](../kanji/懲.md) alone writes こ, and りる is okurigana. 懲りる is an ichidan verb: 懲りた (こりた) is the past and 懲りない (こりない) the negative. The experience that taught the lesson can be marked with に, as in the proverb 羹に懲りて膾を吹く below, or with で, as in Digital Daijisen’s 二度の失敗で.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[反省](反省.md) (はんせい) is reflecting on one’s own mistakes, and [学ぶ](学ぶ.md) (まなぶ) is learning in general. [うんざり](うんざり.md) describes being fed up, and [飽きる](飽きる.md) (あきる) is growing tired of something. 懲りる is specifically being put off by a bad experience.

## Distinctive meaning

Related words are 懲らしめる (こらしめる), “to chastise; to punish,” 性懲りもなく (しょうこりもなく), “not having learned one’s lesson,” and こりごり (懲り懲り), “having had enough (of)”; JMdict marks こりごり as usually written in kana. JMdict glosses the proverb 羹に懲りて膾を吹く (あつものにこりてなますをふく) “a burnt child dreads the fire; once bitten twice shy.”
