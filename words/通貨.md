# 通貨 (つうか) - currency

## Meaning

通貨 is currency: money functioning as a means of payment or circulation.
It can identify a country's or region's currency, not just coins in someone's pocket.

- 日本の通貨は円です。
  (にほんのつうかはえんです。) - Japan's currency is the yen.
- 旅行の前に、現地の通貨に両替しました。
  (りょこうのまえに、げんちのつうかにりょうがえしました。) - Before the trip, I exchanged money for the local currency.

通貨に in the second sentence identifies the currency exchanged into.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes passing or circulating, and
[貨](../kanji/貨.md) contributes money or goods.
Together they point to money that circulates. The on readings are ツウ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お金](お金.md) (おかね), [金](金.md) (かね), and
[金銭](金銭.md) (きんせん) refer to money more broadly.
[現金](現金.md) (げんきん) specifies cash.
[硬貨](硬貨.md) (こうか) are coins; [紙幣](紙幣.md) (しへい)
and [札](札.md) (さつ) are banknotes.

## Distinctive meaning

通貨 is not restricted to physical cash: the public dictionary includes deposit
currency as well as cash currency. [円](円.md) (えん) is a monetary unit;
[銅貨](銅貨.md) (どうか) identifies a coin by its material.
Those distinctions do not make the broad money labels mutually exclusive quiz answers.
Also distinguish the homophone [通過](通過.md) (つうか),
"passage through; passing." Shared pronunciation does not give it a money meaning.
