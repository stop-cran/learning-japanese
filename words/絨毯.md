# 絨毯 (じゅうたん) - carpet; rug

## Meaning

絨毯 is a carpet or rug used as a floor covering, including a runner. The word does not require wall-to-wall installation. じゅうたん in kana is also useful for recognizing the word.

- 床に絨毯を敷きました。
  (ゆかにじゅうたんをしきました。) - I laid a carpet on the floor.
- この絨毯は柔らかいです。
  (このじゅうたんはやわらかいです。) - This rug is soft.

絨毯を敷く means laying a carpet or rug. The covering takes を, and に identifies where it is laid.

## How the kanji combine

絨 concerns wool cloth, and 毯 a wool rug. Their related fabric meanings combine in the name of the floor covering. The on readings are ジュウ + タン. These character associations do not require every modern product called 絨毯 to be made entirely of wool.

## Synonyms and antonyms

カーペット is another word for carpet. [畳](畳.md) (たたみ) is tatami, a different kind of floor covering, not a synonym for a carpet.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict recognizes 絨緞 as a rare spelling. Its search-only forms, including 絨氈, are not additional recommended spellings to memorize. The primary teaching spelling here is 絨毯, read じゅうたん, not じゅたん.
