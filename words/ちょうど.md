# ちょうど - exactly, just right

## Meaning

ちょうど indicates an exact match in time, quantity, size, or suitability: "exactly," "just," or "just right."

- 今、ちょうど三時です。 (いま、ちょうどさんじです。) - It is exactly three o'clock now.
- この靴はちょうどいいです。 (このくつはちょうどいいです。) - These shoes are just right.

In the second sentence, the context usually concerns fit or suitability, not a precise numerical value.

## How the kanji combine

The ordinary spelling is hiragana. 丁度 is also seen, but JMdict identifies its kanji as phonetic spelling; do not derive the expression's meaning from a literal character-by-character explanation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぴったり overlaps when something fits exactly. 約 (やく), "approximately," contrasts with ちょうど before a number. 少し (すこし), "a little," is not its general opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

ちょうど can also describe an opportune coincidence, such as arriving just as something begins. Its core is a close match to a relevant point or need, not simply a small quantity or a short duration.
