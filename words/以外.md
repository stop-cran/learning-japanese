# 以外 (いがい) - other than; except

## Meaning

以外 follows a noun to mean "other than" or "except for" it. The excluded item provides a boundary for what the statement covers.

- 日曜日以外は働いています。 (にちようびいがいははたらいています。) - I work every day except Sunday.
- 水以外の飲み物はありますか。 (みずいがいののみものはありますか。) - Do you have any drinks other than water?

Use noun + 以外の before another noun. 以外に can introduce something additional: 日本語以外に英語も話せます (にほんごいがいにえいごもはなせます), "I can also speak English besides Japanese."

## How the kanji combine

[以](../kanji/以.md) helps establish a reference or limit; [外](../kanji/外.md) means outside. Together they mark what lies outside the named category.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ほか can also mean "other" or "besides." だけ restricts the statement to the named item instead: 日曜日だけ means "only Sunday."

## Distinctive meaning

以外 is not just a physical "outside." It excludes an item from a set, so the surrounding positive or negative statement matters. 私以外は知っている (わたしいがいはしっている) means "Everyone except me knows."
