# ぐったり — limp; listless with exhaustion

## Meaning

ぐったり describes a limp, weary, listless state, often from exhaustion, heat, or illness.

- 暑さでぐったりして、何もする気になれない。 — The heat has left me so drained that I do not feel like doing anything.
- 長い散歩のあと、犬は床にぐったりと横になった。 — After the long walk, the dog lay limply on the floor. (床: ゆか; 横になる: よこになる.)

Use ぐったりする or ぐったりしている for the state, and ぐったりと before an appropriate action or posture verb. ぐったりした様子 (ようす) means "an exhausted, listless appearance."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[へとへと](へとへと.md) and [くたくた](くたくた.md) emphasize being worn out. ぐったり more vividly presents a lack of energy or limp posture. [ぴんぴん](ぴんぴん.md) can contrast with someone being full of life.

## Distinctive meaning

ぐったり does not identify the cause of the weakness or establish that someone has fainted. A person may still be awake and responsive. It is an everyday description, not a medical diagnosis or a synonym for every kind of sadness.
