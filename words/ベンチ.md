# ベンチ (ベンチ) - bench

## Meaning

ベンチ is a bench, a long seat such as one in a park or at a station. It is a noun. Use に with 座る (すわる), "sit," and で when the bench is the location of an activity.

- 公園のベンチに座りました。 (こうえんのべんちにすわりました。) - I sat on a park bench.
- ベンチで本を読んでいる人がいます。 (べんちでほんをよんでいるひとがいます。) - Someone is reading a book on the bench.

The different particles belong to the different predicates: に identifies the seat for sitting, while で locates the reading activity.

## How the kanji combine

ベンチ is written in katakana and has no kanji decomposition. Read ベ・ン・チ, including the nasal ン. The spelling does not contain a long-vowel mark or a small ッ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[椅子](いす.md) (いす) is a chair or seat more generally. A ベンチ is specifically the long-seat kind. [ソファー](ソファー.md) names a sofa, a different kind of seating.

These nouns classify objects; they are not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

ベンチ does not specify its material, whether it has a back, or exactly how many people it seats. Add a modifier when relevant: 木のベンチ (きのべんち), "a wooden bench." Nor does the word itself say that the bench is outdoors; 公園の in the first example supplies that location.
