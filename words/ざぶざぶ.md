# ざぶざぶ — sloshing and splashing

## Meaning

A substantial, repeated splashing or sloshing impression.

- 大きなたらいでシーツをざぶざぶ洗（あら）った。 — I washed the sheet in a large basin with a lot of splashing.
- 長靴（ながぐつ）で小川（おがわ）をざぶざぶ歩いた。 — I splashed through the stream in rubber boots.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[じゃぶじゃぶ](じゃぶじゃぶ.md) strongly overlaps in washing or wading. [ばしゃばしゃ](ばしゃばしゃ.md) can likewise describe repeated splashes; neither contrast is an exact water-volume threshold.

## Distinctive meaning

ざぶざぶ洗う and ざぶざぶと歩く（あるく）depict an activity. They do not mean the same thing as the resulting soaked state [びしょびしょ](びしょびしょ.md).
