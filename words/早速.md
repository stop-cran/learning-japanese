# 早速 (さっそく) — at once; without delay

## Meaning

早速 means at once, immediately or without delay. It often introduces taking action promptly after receiving something or getting an opportunity.

本が届いたので、早速読み始めた。（ほんがとどいたので、さっそくよみはじめた。） — The book arrived, so I started reading it right away.

早速のお返事、ありがとうございます。（さっそくのおへんじ、ありがとうございます。） — Thank you for your prompt reply.

## How the kanji combine

[早](../kanji/早.md) contributes early/quick and [速](../kanji/速.md) contributes fast. The two related meanings reinforce promptness. The compound is さっそく, with a small っ; it does not use the standalone adjective readings はやい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すぐに](すぐに.md) and [直ちに](直ちに.md) ただちに also mean immediately. [さっさと](さっさと.md) overlaps in prompt action but can also describe a brisk or indifferent manner. [速い](速い.md) はやい concerns speed rather than necessarily starting without delay.

## Distinctive meaning

早速 can modify an action directly, as in 早速読む, or a noun with の, as in 早速の返事. A train that travels fast is not thereby 早速: the word concerns prompt timing of action, not its top speed.
