# 宴会 (えんかい) - banquet; party

## Meaning

宴会 is a banquet or party where people gather to eat, drink and enjoy
themselves. It is a noun: 宴会を開く (えんかいをひらく) means to
hold a banquet or party.

- 仕事の後で宴会を開きました。
  (しごとのあとでえんかいをひらきました。) - We held a party after work.
- 宴会に二十人が参加しました。
  (えんかいににじゅうにんがさんかしました。) - Twenty people attended the banquet.

に marks the event someone participates in. The word does not by itself
specify the number of guests, a particular venue, or whether everyone
drinks alcohol.

## How the kanji combine

宴 contributes a feast or entertainment, and [会](../kanji/会.md) a
gathering. The first character specifies the kind of gathering:
エン + カイ. 宴 is retained in the spelling even though it has no
kanji card in this collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[パーティー](パーティー.md) also names a party; the words overlap
rather than separating all Japanese-style and Western-style occasions.
[会合](会合.md) (かいごう) is a meeting or gathering more broadly.
[会](会.md) (かい) can likewise name a party as well as other meetings.

## Distinctive meaning

宴会 foregrounds the social meal or celebration, not just any meeting.
English "reception" can name such an event, but
[応接](応接.md) (おうせつ) concerns receiving and attending to visitors,
not the banquet itself. Check what people are doing rather than matching
an English label alone.
