# 筆者 (ひっしゃ) - writer of a text; author

## Meaning

筆者 is the writer or author of a text.
A writer can use 筆者 to refer to themself: "the present writer" or,
in a natural English translation, "I."

- この文章の筆者は誰ですか。 (このぶんしょうのひっしゃはだれですか。) - Who wrote this passage?
- 筆者はこの意見に賛成です。 (ひっしゃはこのいけんにさんせいです。) - The present writer agrees with this opinion.

The second assumes that it appears in the writer's own text.
In another context, 筆者 can refer to a different person whose writing is under discussion.

## How the kanji combine

[筆](../kanji/筆.md) contributes writing;
[者](../kanji/者.md) identifies the person doing it.
The activity-plus-person relationship gives "writer."
ヒツ + シャ becomes ひっしゃ, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[著者](著者.md) (ちょしゃ) often identifies a book's author.
[作者](作者.md) (さくしゃ) is a work's creator, including works other than text.
[作家](作家.md) (さっか) can identify a writer's creative role.
[ライター](ライター.md) has a writer identity separate from its lighter identity.

## Distinctive meaning

筆者 connects someone with writing, not necessarily with a paid profession.
It is useful in discussing a passage's argument or viewpoint.
Do not treat its self-reference use as a general spoken replacement for
[私](私.md) (わたし) in introductions or everyday requests.
