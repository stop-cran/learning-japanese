# 犯罪 (はんざい) - crime; offense

## Meaning

犯罪 is a crime or criminal offense. It is a noun. The expression 犯罪を犯す (はんざいをおかす) means "commit a crime"; 犯罪が起こる (はんざいがおこる) means "a crime occurs."

- 犯罪を防ぐための対策が必要です。 (はんざいをふせぐためのたいさくがひつようです。) - Measures to prevent crime are necessary.
- この町では犯罪が減っています。 (このまちでははんざいがへっています。) - Crime is decreasing in this town.

犯罪 can refer to crime as a general phenomenon, not just one countable incident. The second sentence does not state that crime has disappeared entirely.

## How the kanji combine

[犯](../kanji/犯.md) contributes committing or violating, and [罪](../kanji/罪.md) contributes an offense or crime. The combination evokes committing an offense. The on readings are ハン + ザイ. Even with this action-like composition, the word taught here is a noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[事件](事件.md) (じけん) means an incident or case; not every incident is a crime. [犯人](犯人.md) (はんにん) identifies a culprit, whereas 犯罪 names the offense. 罪 can also have moral or religious uses.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 犯罪 as a general label for every mistake, rude act, or disagreement. Whether conduct constitutes a crime depends on the applicable law and facts. The vocabulary word itself neither determines guilt nor supplies a legal judgment about an individual.
