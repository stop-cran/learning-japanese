# 連れる (つれる) — take someone along

## Meaning

連れる means to take or bring someone along with you. It is a transitive ichidan verb: the person or animal accompanying you takes を.

- 子供を公園に連れて行きました。 (こどもをこうえんにつれていきました。) — I took the children to the park.
- 明日は友達を連れて来てもいいですか。 (あしたはともだちをつれてきてもいいですか。) — May I bring a friend tomorrow?

Its forms include 連れます and 連れない. The て-form 連れて commonly combines with 行く or 来る to specify taking away or bringing here.

## How the kanji combine

[連](../kanji/連.md) supplies an association with accompanying or connection. れる completes the native verb つれる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

持って行く (もっていく) means to take an object along. 案内する (あんないする), "guide/show around," emphasizes helping someone navigate rather than simply accompanying them.

## Distinctive meaning

English "lead" can cover leading a company or winning a race, but 連れる does not have those meanings. It concerns bringing a person or animal with you; it does not require walking in front of them.
