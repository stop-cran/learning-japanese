# 中央 (ちゅうおう) - center; middle

## Meaning

中央 is the center or middle of a space or arrangement. It is a noun; use **noun の中央** for the center of something, and **中央の + noun** for an item in the central position.

- 部屋の中央に机があります。 (へやのちゅうおうにつくえがあります。) - There is a desk in the center of the room.
- 写真の中央にいるのが姉です。 (しゃしんのちゅうおうにいるのがあねです。) - The person in the middle of the photograph is my older sister.

に marks the central location in both examples.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) contributes middle, and 央 also contributes center. Their meanings reinforce one another. The on readings are チュウ + オウ, with two long-vowel sequences in ちゅうおう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[真ん中](真ん中.md) (まんなか) also means the middle and fits many everyday spatial descriptions. [中](中.md) (なか), "inside," is broader: something inside a room need not be at its center. 端 (はし), "edge," provides a spatial contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

中央 can also refer to the capital or central seat of government, contrasted with local areas. That institutional center need not be the country's geographical midpoint. In a photograph or room description, however, the physical arrangement is the relevant frame.
