# 建物 (たてもの) — building

## Meaning

建物 is a building: a constructed structure such as a house, school, office, or museum.

あの白い建物が図書館です。 (あのしろいたてものがとしょかんです。) — That white building is the library.

古い建物を見に行きました。 (ふるいたてものをみにいきました。) — I went to see an old building.

## How the kanji combine

[建](../kanji/建.md) is related to 建てる (たてる), "build," and [物](../kanji/物.md) is もの, "thing." The native reading is たてもの. The standard spelling 建物 omits the て that is written as okurigana in the verb 建てる; pronounce it nonetheless.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ビル commonly refers to a substantial office, commercial, or similar building. 家 (いえ) is a house or home. 建物 is the broader physical-building term and does not by itself say what happens inside.

## Distinctive meaning

English "building" can also mean the activity of constructing something. 建物 names the finished or identifiable structure, not that activity. For a construction action, use a verb such as 建てる: 家を建てる (いえをたてる), "build a house."
