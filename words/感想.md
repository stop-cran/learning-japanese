# 感想 (かんそう) - impressions; personal reactions

## Meaning

感想 is one's impressions, thoughts, or reactions after experiencing something.
It is a noun: 感想を言う (かんそうをいう) means to give one's impressions,
and 感想を聞く (かんそうをきく) to ask for or hear them.

- 映画の感想を聞かせてください。
  (えいがのかんそうをきかせてください。) - Please tell me what you thought of the film.
- 本を読んで、感想を書きました。
  (ほんをよんで、かんそうをかきました。) - I read a book and wrote my impressions.

Nの感想 identifies what the reaction concerns.
感想文 (かんそうぶん) is a written account of one's impressions,
such as a response to a book.

## How the kanji combine

[感](../kanji/感.md) contributes feeling and
[想](../kanji/想.md) thought or conception.
The related meanings combine in a noun for what someone feels or thinks
about an experience. The on readings are カン + ソウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[印象](印象.md) (いんしょう) is an impression made or received.
[意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion, including a position or proposal.
The distinction is not absolute: a person's 感想 can contain an 意見.
[感じ](感じ.md) (かんじ) also overlaps in a subjective impression.

## Distinctive meaning

感想 does not automatically mean praise. A critical or mixed response
is still a 感想, and giving one does not establish an objective fact.
[乾燥](乾燥.md) is also read かんそう but means dryness or drying;
the shared pronunciation is not a shared meaning.
