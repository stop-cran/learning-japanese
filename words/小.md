# 小 (しょう) - small size; small item

## Meaning

小, read **しょう**, is a noun or prefix associated with smallness or a small item. As a size label, it can stand for "small," alongside 中 (ちゅう), "medium," and 大 (だい), "large."

- サイズは大・中・小から選べます。 (サイズはだい・ちゅう・しょうからえらべます。) - You can choose large, medium, or small.
- 小を一つください。 (しょうをひとつください。) - One small one, please.

The second sentence assumes the available items or portions are already clear. 小 is a label standing for the item, not an i-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) is the whole word here and uses the on reading しょう. The same reading contributes "small" in 小規模 (しょうきぼ), "small scale." Different words containing this character may use other readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[小さい](小さい.md) (ちいさい) is the ordinary adjective: 小さい箱 (ちいさいはこ), "a small box." Size-label 小 contrasts with 大; it does not replace 小さい freely before any noun.

## Distinctive meaning

The size-label use is a practical reading target even though a dictionary lists several other meanings for 小. This card focuses on smallness and small items; it does not treat every abbreviation written with the same character as the same use. In particular, learn the pronunciation しょう from the whole expression rather than assuming every standalone 小 must be read this way.
