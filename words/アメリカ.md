# アメリカ — America; United States

## Meaning

アメリカ commonly names the United States of America. It can also mean the Americas as a landmass when the geographical context makes that clear.

姉はアメリカに住んでいます。 (あねはアメリカにすんでいます。) — My older sister lives in the United States.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is katakana. Historical phonetic kanji spellings such as 亜米利加 exist but are not needed for ordinary writing. This is a borrowed place name, not a Japanese kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

アメリカ合衆国 (アメリカがっしゅうこく) is the fuller country name. 米国 (べいこく) also means the United States and is common in news and formal writing.

## Distinctive meaning

Use アメリカの before a following noun: アメリカの大学 (アメリカのだいがく), "a university in America." アメリカ人 (アメリカじん) means an American person. Country names have no meaningful lexical antonyms; comparisons with other countries concern geography, not opposites.
