# 王妃 (おうひ) — queen consort

## Meaning

A 王妃 is a royal consort, for example the wife of a king. “Queen” can work in English, but the point here is the relationship, not that she rules in her own right.

- 国王と王妃が式に出席した。 (こくおうとおうひがしきにしゅっせきした。) — The king and the queen consort attended the ceremony.
- これは若いころの王妃の肖像画だ。 (これはわかいころのおうひのしょうぞうがだ。) — This is a portrait of the queen consort when she was young.

## How the kanji combine

[王](../kanji/王.md) refers to the royal figure, [妃](../kanji/妃.md) to the consort. The second kanji has the on-reading ひ, not the standalone reading きさき, in this title.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[女王](女王.md) can mean a female sovereign; it also has a consort sense, so the two words are not exact opposites. [王女](王女.md) (おうじょ) is a king's daughter, not another word for his wife.

## Distinctive meaning

In a story where the queen herself inherits the throne, “queen” alone does not establish that 王妃 is the right translation. Look for the relationship expressed by the Japanese title.
