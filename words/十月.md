# 十月 (じゅうがつ) — October

## Meaning

十月 is October: 十月は紅葉の季節だ (October is the season of autumn leaves), 十月十日 じゅうがつとおか (October 10th).

## How the kanji combine

[十](../kanji/十.md) (ten) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month): the tenth month. ジュウ stays long before がつ.
"Ten months" is 十か月 じゅっかげつ／じっかげつ, with the contraction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 神無月 かんなづき (old name of the tenth month).
- Related: 十一月 じゅういちがつ, 十二月 じゅうにがつ.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not say じゅっがつ; the contraction is only for counters like か月. The date is [十日](十日.md) とおか.
