# 辰 (たつ) — Dragon zodiac sign

## Meaning

辰 is the **Dragon sign**, the fifth of the twelve zodiac signs.
Use it to identify a place in the cycle.

- 私の干支は辰です。 (わたしのえとはたつです。) — My zodiac sign is the Dragon.
- 十二支の五番目は辰です。 (じゅうにしのごばんめはたつです。) — The fifth of the twelve zodiac signs is the Dragon.

十二支 (じゅうにし) names the twelve-sign set. In the first example,
干支 (えと) has its everyday twelve-year zodiac sense. 干支 can also
mean the sixty-term calendar cycle combining stems and branches. The
reading かんし is documented for that sixty-term sense, not as a substitute
for えと's twelve-year zodiac sense.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [辰](../kanji/辰.md) writes the whole native sign noun
たつ, with no okurigana or second character to separate.

Recognize 辰年 (たつどし), **year of the Dragon**: 辰 identifies the sign
and [年](../kanji/年.md) the year. Learn the whole reading たつどし;
the separate noun [年](年.md) has the primary reading とし.
Daijisen also records 辰年 for a person born in that year.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The associated dragon is normally written 竜 or 龍, with the readings
りゅう and たつ among its documented readings. For the zodiac sign,
learn the spelling 辰 and reading たつ here. The association does not
make these spellings unrestricted substitutes. A zodiac sign has no
useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

辰 is a calendar sign associated with a dragon. Keep the context in
view: 辰年 labels a year in the cycle, not a mythical creature.

Older senses of 辰 concern an hour around 8 a.m., a traditional direction
labelled east-southeast, and the third lunar month. Dictionaries give
both 7–9 a.m. and 8–10 a.m. hour mappings, not one universal modern interval.
The traditional bearing is not an exact modern sixteen-point compass
bearing; the lunar month is not Gregorian March. These are recognition
notes, not ordinary modern scheduling or navigation vocabulary.
