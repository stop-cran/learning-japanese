# 鹿 (しか) — deer

## Meaning

鹿 means a deer or deer as a group; context supplies the number.
It can name deer generally, especially the sika deer. The noun itself does
not specify the animal's sex.

- 山で鹿を見ました。 (やまでしかをみました。) — I saw a deer in the mountains.
- あそこに鹿がいます。 (あそこにしかがいます。) — There is a deer over there.

In the first sentence, で marks where the sighting took place and を marks
what was seen. Living animals take いる in the second sentence.
You can also encounter the kana spellings しか and シカ.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [鹿](../kanji/鹿.md) writes the complete native noun しか.
The reference card also lists ロク and か, but those readings do not replace
しか for the ordinary standalone animal noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動物](動物.md) どうぶつ, "animal," is a broader category, not a precise
synonym. [馬](馬.md) うま means horse, a different animal rather than an
opposite of deer.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse the animal noun with [馬鹿](馬鹿.md) ばか, "fool; foolish."
That complete word has an ateji spelling; combining horse and deer meanings
does not explain its use. Learn its full reading separately from the animal
noun しか.
