# へらへら — laughing foolishly or frivolously

## Meaning

へらへら describes laughing or smiling in a frivolous, careless, or inappropriate way.

- 注意されても、彼はへらへら笑っていた。
  — Even when corrected, he kept laughing foolishly.
- 真剣な質問にへらへら答えて、彼女は相手を困らせた。
  — She answered a serious question frivolously and puzzled the other person.

## Synonyms and antonyms

へらへら often conveys a negative judgment, unlike a warm or sincere smile.

## Distinctive meaning

It can also describe careless or flippant speech; these examples focus on an unserious response.
