# 祝い (いわい) - celebration; congratulatory gift

## Meaning

祝い is a noun for a celebration or congratulations. It can also name a gift presented
to mark a happy occasion. The form [お祝い](お祝い.md) (おいわい) adds the polite prefix お.

- 卒業の祝いに家族で食事をしました。 (そつぎょうのいわいにかぞくでしょくじをしました。) - We had a family meal to celebrate graduation.
- 結婚祝いをもらいました。 (けっこんいわいをもらいました。) - I received a wedding gift.

An occasion can precede の祝い, as in 卒業の祝い, or form an established compound such as
結婚祝い. The surrounding action tells you whether a celebration or a present is meant.

## How the kanji combine

祝 carries the idea of celebrating or congratulating. 祝い uses the native reading いわい
and is related to [祝う](祝う.md) (いわう), "to celebrate; congratulate."
The final い belongs to the noun form; it is not the ending of an i-adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[プレゼント](プレゼント.md) is a present more generally. 祝い emphasizes the happy occasion
that a gift marks. A festival can involve celebration, but 祝い is not a universal replacement
for a festival's name. There is no useful single opposite covering both the celebration and gift senses.

## Distinctive meaning

祝いに explains the purpose or occasion: "in celebration of" or "as a congratulatory gift."
Use the verb 祝う when you need "celebrate" as a verb, rather than mechanically adding する
to every occurrence of 祝い. Both the event and the gift are positive responses to an occasion.
