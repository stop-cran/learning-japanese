# 中止 (ちゅうし) - cancellation; discontinuation

## Meaning

中止 is stopping an activity or calling off a planned event. With する, the activity being stopped takes を. 中止になる describes an event being canceled or discontinued without naming who makes the decision.

- 雨のため、試合は中止になりました。 (あめのため、しあいはちゅうしになりました。) - The match was canceled because of rain.
- 機械に問題が見つかり、作業を中止しました。 (きかいにもんだいがみつかり、さぎょうをちゅうししました。) - We found a problem with the machine and stopped work.

The first can concern an event that has not begun; the second concerns work that was underway.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) suggests the middle or course of something; [止](../kanji/止.md) means stopping. The established compound includes cancellation before the start, so "stop in the middle" is not a complete definition. The readings are チュウ + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[延期](延期.md) (えんき) is postponement to a later time. 中断 (ちゅうだん) emphasizes interruption. [続ける](続ける.md) (つづける), "continue," provides a practical contrast to stopping.

## Distinctive meaning

中止 does not promise a replacement date, but neither does it establish that the activity can never be held again. The announcement or situation must tell you whether anything will be rescheduled.
