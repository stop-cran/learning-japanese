# 漢和 (かんわ) - kanji dictionary; Chinese and Japanese

## Meaning

漢和 can mean China and Japan, or the Chinese and Japanese languages. In dictionary terminology, it abbreviates 漢和辞典 (かんわじてん): a dictionary explaining kanji and kanji compounds in Japanese.

- 知らない漢字を漢和辞典で調べました。
  (しらないかんじをかんわじてんでしらべました。) - I looked up an unfamiliar kanji in a kanji dictionary.
- この棚には国語と漢和の辞典があります。
  (このたなにはこくごとかんわのじてんがあります。) - This shelf has Japanese-language dictionaries and kanji dictionaries.

## How the kanji combine

[漢](../kanji/漢.md) relates here to Chinese characters or China, and [和](../kanji/和.md) to Japan or Japanese. Their on readings combine as カン + ワ. The characters help identify the relationship, not the contents of every dictionary.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[辞書](辞書.md) (じしょ), [辞典](辞典.md) (じてん), and [字引](字引.md) (じびき) are broader dictionary words. [英和](英和.md) (えいわ) concerns English and Japanese; an 英和辞典 explains English words in Japanese.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read 漢和辞典 as simply a modern Chinese-to-Japanese translation dictionary. The key focus is kanji and their compounds. Outside the dictionary context, the country or language meanings of 漢和 remain possible; context selects the use.
