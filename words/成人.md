# 成人 (せいじん) - adult; coming of age

## Meaning

成人 is an adult or grown-up. With する, it means to become an adult or come of age.
The verb is intransitive: the person becoming an adult takes が, not を.
成人の modifies a following noun.

- この調査は成人を対象にしています。
  (このちょうさはせいじんをたいしょうにしています。)
  - This survey is aimed at adults.
- 娘は成人してから、一人で暮らし始めました。
  (むすめはせいじんしてから、ひとりでくらしはじめました。)
  - After reaching adulthood, my daughter started living on her own.

The first uses the noun as the survey's target group; the second describes the transition to adulthood.

## How the kanji combine

[成](../kanji/成.md) contributes becoming or reaching maturity, and [人](../kanji/人.md)
contributes a person. The compound names a person who has reached adulthood.
The on readings are セイ + ジン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大人](大人.md) (おとな) is another word for an adult and can also describe mature behavior.
未成年 (みせいねん), "minor; underage person," contrasts with an adult in age-based classifications.

## Distinctive meaning

成人 does not itself specify one universal age threshold for every country, historical
period, or activity. Check the relevant rule rather than inferring a number from the word.
Do not confuse [青年](青年.md) (せいねん), "youth; young man," with 成人 (せいじん):
youth and adulthood are different classifications.
