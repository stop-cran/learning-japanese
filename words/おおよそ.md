# おおよそ - approximately; outline

## Meaning

おおよそ is an adverb meaning "about; approximately," and can also mean
"generally; on the whole." As a noun it can name an outline or gist.

- 駅までおおよそ十分かかります。 (えきまでおおよそじゅっぷんかかります。) - It takes approximately ten minutes to the station.
- 話のおおよそは分かりました。 (はなしのおおよそはわかりました。) - I understood the gist of the story.

The first sentence estimates a quantity; the second uses the noun sense.
An estimate does not specify an exact margin of error.

## How the kanji combine

大凡 is a dictionary spelling read おおよそ, but JMdict marks the written
forms as rare and the word as usually kana-written. Recognize
[大](../kanji/大.md) in that spelling without using its on reading ダイ here.
Learn おおよそ as a whole word, not as a character-by-character translation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[およそ](およそ.md), [大体](大体.md) (だいたい), and [約](約.md) (やく)
overlap in approximation. [あらすじ](あらすじ.md) is an outline or summary.
正確に (せいかくに), "exactly; accurately," contrasts with a rough estimate.

## Distinctive meaning

A separate emphatic use means "entirely" or, with a negative, "not at all":
おおよそ考えられない (おおよそかんがえられない), "quite unthinkable."
Do not interpret that as an approximate quantity.
おおよそ and およそ overlap; the extra お does not establish a different
degree of numerical accuracy.
