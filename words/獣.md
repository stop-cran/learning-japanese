# 獣 (けもの) — beast

## Meaning

獣 means a **beast or animal**, especially with mammals in mind rather than
the whole biological animal kingdom. The noun does not itself specify the
species, sex or number.

- 山で獣を見ました。 (やまでけものをみました。) — I saw a beast in the mountains.
- この山には獣がいます。 (このやまにはけものがいます。) — There are beasts in these mountains.

で marks where the sighting took place; に marks where the animals exist.
The second sentence uses いる for living animals.

**けだもの** is another dictionary-supported reading of 獣 with an overlapping
animal sense. Applied to a person, it can be an abusive, contemptuous word
for someone regarded as inhumane. This is a usage warning, not a claim that
けだもの can only refer to people or is an obsolete animal word.
The word-reading question for this article uses the primary けもの.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [獣](../kanji/獣.md) writes the whole native noun
けもの, without okurigana. Its on-reading ジュウ appears in compounds such
as [獣医](獣医.md) じゅうい, veterinarian; do not replace けもの with that
reading in the animal examples above.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動物](動物.md) どうぶつ is the broad neutral category "animal."
[熊](熊.md) くま, bear, and [鹿](鹿.md) しか, deer, specify kinds of animals;
獣 does not identify either species. [家畜](家畜.md) かちく emphasizes
domesticated animals or livestock, a different way of categorizing them.
These categories can overlap; they are not mutually exclusive meanings
or a set of antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 獣 as an automatic substitute for 動物 in every scientific or
everyday category statement. Conversely, the character does not make every
compound wild-animal-only: 獣医 names the veterinary profession.
Calling a person a "beast" can be dehumanizing; the animal examples do not
license using the word as a neutral description of someone's character.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
entry 1335590, sense 1. Its separate subculture sense is restricted to ケモノ,
not a general meaning assigned to every reading and spelling of 獣.
[Digital Daijisen, けもの](https://www.weblio.jp/content/けもの) and
[けだもの](https://www.weblio.jp/content/けだもの) corroborate the animal
overlap and the latter's abusive person use.
