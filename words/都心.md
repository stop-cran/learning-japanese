# 都心 (としん) - city center; downtown

## Meaning

都心 is a city's central area, especially central Tokyo in a Tokyo-related context.
It can name the downtown concentration of urban activity,
not necessarily a precisely measured geometric midpoint.

- 都心まで電車で三十分かかります。 (としんまででんしゃでさんじゅっぷんかかります。) - It takes thirty minutes by train to reach the city center.
- 都心には高いビルが多いです。 (としんにはたかいビルがおおいです。) - There are many tall buildings downtown.

まで marks the destination limit; に locates the buildings.
Context supplies which city's center is meant.

## How the kanji combine

[都](../kanji/都.md) contributes city or capital, and
[心](../kanji/心.md) contributes heart or center.
The first identifies the kind of center: the heart of the city.
Read ト + シン, not ミヤコ + ココロ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中心](中心.md) (ちゅうしん) is a center more generally.
[都市](都市.md) (とし) is a city, not just its central district.
[郊外](郊外.md) (こうがい), the outskirts or suburbs, provides a useful urban contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 都心 for every sort of center, such as the middle of a sheet of paper.
Nor does it mean the whole metropolitan area.
An unqualified 都心 can refer to central Tokyo in the relevant conversation,
but the word is not an absolute proper name that can only describe Tokyo.
