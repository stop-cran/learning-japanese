# 幼稚 (ようち) - childish; immature; infancy

## Meaning

幼稚 describes something childish, immature or insufficiently
developed. It is a na-adjective: 幼稚な before a noun,
幼稚に adverbially, and 幼稚だ as a predicate.

- 自分でも幼稚な考えだったと思います。 (じぶんでもようちなかんがえだったとおもいます。) - Even I think it was an immature idea.
- 大人でも幼稚なことを言う場合があります。 (おとなでもようちなことをいうばあいがあります。) - Adults sometimes say childish things too.

The dictionary also records the noun meaning infancy or early
childhood. The critical adjective is the practical focus here.

## How the kanji combine

[幼](../kanji/幼.md) and 稚 both contribute youth
or immaturity. Their on readings ヨウ + チ give ようち.
The reinforcing pair can describe immature thinking or methods,
not only a person's biological age.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[幼い](幼い.md) (おさない) overlaps in young or immature.
未熟 (みじゅく), not fully developed or inexperienced, is a related
comparison. A particular opposite depends on whether the criticism
concerns judgment, skill or another kind of development.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling an adult's proposal 幼稚 can be dismissive; it is not simply
stating that the speaker is young.
In [幼稚園](幼稚園.md) (ようちえん), the early-childhood element
is part of the institutional name, not an insult to the school.
Learn the whole construction rather than transferring the critical
adjective's tone to every compound.
