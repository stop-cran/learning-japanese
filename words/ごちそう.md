# ごちそう — feast; meal as a treat

## Meaning

ごちそう is a feast, special dish, or excellent meal. ごちそうする means to treat someone to food or drink.

- テーブルにごちそうが並んでいます。 (てーぶるにごちそうがならんでいます。) — A feast is laid out on the table.
- 今日は私が昼ご飯をごちそうします。 (きょうはわたしがひるごはんをごちそうします。) — I'll treat you to lunch today.

In the verb pattern, に can identify the recipient and を identifies the food or drink: 友達にコーヒーをごちそうする, "treat a friend to coffee."

## How the kanji combine

The kanji spelling is ご馳走, also 御馳走. The central on-reading compound ちそう takes the prefix ご. Kana spelling is useful for everyday study; do not split ちそう into modern standalone words with invented meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

食事 (しょくじ) is a meal without the special-treat emphasis. おごる also means to pay for someone else's food or drink.

## Distinctive meaning

ごちそうさまでした is said after eating to acknowledge the meal. Its use does not imply that every ordinary meal was a lavish banquet.
