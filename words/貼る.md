# 貼る (はる) — stick on; paste

## Meaning

貼る means to stick, paste, or affix something to a surface. The item attached takes を; the surface or destination takes に.

- 封筒に切手を貼ります。 (ふうとうにきってをはります。) — I put a stamp on the envelope.
- 壁にポスターを貼りました。 (かべにポスターをはりました。) — I put a poster on the wall.

## How the kanji combine

貼 writes は and る is okurigana. It is a godan verb, not ichidan: 貼ります (はります), 貼って (はって), 貼らない (はらない). The small っ appears in the て-form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

付ける (つける) is a broader "attach; put on." はがす means to peel or remove something stuck to a surface, a useful opposite in the poster or sticker context.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling 貼る makes the sticking/pasting sense clear. 張る, also はる, covers other meanings such as stretching or putting up a tent. Their dictionary treatment overlaps, but that does not mean every meaning of 張る should be written 貼る. For stamps and adhesive labels, learn 貼る with its を and に pattern.
