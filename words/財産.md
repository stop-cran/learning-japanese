# 財産 (ざいさん) - property; assets; wealth

## Meaning

財産 is a noun for property, assets, or accumulated wealth. It can include money, land, buildings, and other valuable possessions. Use 人の財産 for someone's assets and 財産を with an action affecting them.

- 祖父から財産を受け継ぎました。 (そふからざいさんをうけつぎました。) - I inherited property from my grandfather.
- この経験は私の大切な財産です。 (このけいけんはわたしのたいせつなざいさんです。) - This experience is a valuable asset to me.

The second sentence is figurative: the experience is something valuable to retain, not a bank balance.

## How the kanji combine

[財](../kanji/財.md) contributes wealth or valuables, and [産](../kanji/産.md) contributes property or assets in this compound. Their related meanings combine in a collective word for what someone owns or has accumulated.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お金](お金.md) (おかね) is money; 財産 is not limited to cash. [高価](高価.md) (こうか) describes something expensive, while 財産 names an asset or collection of assets rather than a price.

## Distinctive meaning

The English translation "fortune" here means wealth, not luck or a prediction of the future. Nor does the word necessarily imply that its owner is extremely rich. In an inheritance discussion, 財産 identifies what can be passed on; in the experience example, it emphasizes enduring value without making a literal claim of ownership.
