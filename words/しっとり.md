# しっとり — pleasantly moist; softly composed

## Meaning

Lightly moist or damp, often attractively so. It also describes a calm, soft, gracefully restrained atmosphere or manner.

- このケーキは、次の日もしっとりしている。 — This cake is still moist the next day.
- 雨上がりの土はしっとりしていた。 — The soil was moist after the rain.
- 彼女は静かな曲をしっとりと歌った。 — She sang the quiet song with gentle composure.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぱさぱさ](ぱさぱさ.md) is dried-out; [びしょびしょ](びしょびしょ.md) is thoroughly wet. [じめじめ](じめじめ.md) usually emphasizes unwelcome dampness. しっとり does not imply that all moisture is pleasant.

## Distinctive meaning

しっとりした肌（はだ）, しっとりする, and しっとりと歌う are useful. A cake's moisture and a singer's restrained mood are separate contextual senses. Do not automatically translate it as “wet,” or use it as a general praise word meaning stylish in every setting.
