# 境 (さかい) - boundary; turning point

## Meaning

境 is a noun for a boundary or dividing line. AとBの境 identifies
the boundary between A and B. It can also name a turning point:
出来事を境に means "with an event as the dividing point."

- この川が二つの町の境です。 (このかわがふたつのまちのさかいです。) - This river is the boundary between the two towns.
- 卒業を境に、生活が変わりました。 (そつぎょうをさかいに、せいかつがかわりました。) - My life changed after graduation, which marked a turning point.

The second pattern marks a before-and-after contrast. It does not
merely say that two events happened at approximately the same time.

## How the kanji combine

境 is a single character read さかい in these examples. The whole
native noun expresses a boundary; no second written component modifies
it. A temporal turning point extends the idea of a dividing line.

## Synonyms and antonyms

境界 (きょうかい) also means "boundary."
[間](間.md) (あいだ) names a space or interval between things;
境 instead identifies their division. There is no useful general
antonym covering both spatial and temporal uses.

## Distinctive meaning

The same dictionary entry includes "mental state," usually with the
reading きょう. That is a reading tendency, not a hard prohibition
on さかい. For practical study, associate さかい first with
boundaries and the を境に turning-point construction, rather than
using it as an unrestricted substitute for "mood."
