# 活躍 (かつやく) - energetic activity; active contribution

## Meaning

活躍 is energetic or successful activity in a role or field. It is a noun used with する; 活躍する often describes someone making an active contribution.

- 彼女は通訳として国際会議で活躍しています。 (かのじょはつうやくとしてこくさいかいぎでかつやくしています。) - She is making an active contribution as an interpreter at international conferences.
- 試合では若い選手が活躍しました。 (しあいではわかいせんしゅがかつやくしました。) - Young players played a prominent role in the match.

Xとして活躍する identifies the role; Xで活躍する can identify the field or setting.

## How the kanji combine

[活](../kanji/活.md) contributes activity or vitality, and 躍 leaping or lively movement. The two reinforce the idea of energetic action. The on readings are カツ + ヤク; learn the established word rather than translating every use as literal jumping.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[活動](活動.md) (かつどう) is activity more broadly. [努力](努力.md) (どりょく) is effort: someone can make an effort without yet standing out through their contribution. [活力](活力.md) (かつりょく) is the vitality or energy supporting action, not the action itself.

## Distinctive meaning

活躍 often has an appreciative tone, drawing attention to what someone does in a role. It does not simply mean being physically in motion, nor does it guarantee a prize or victory. The practical activity sense taught here is distinct from a dictionary's obsolete vigorous-walking sense.
