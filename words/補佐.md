# 補佐 (ほさ) — aide; assistance

## Meaning

補佐 means aid or assistance; it can also refer to an assistant or adviser. 補佐する means to assist.

- 彼は部長を補佐しています。 (かれはぶちょうをほさしています。) — He assists the department manager.
- 会議では、事務局が議長を補佐しました。 (かいぎでは、じむきょくがぎちょうをほさしました。) — At the meeting, the secretariat assisted the chair.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[補](../kanji/補.md) and [佐](../kanji/佐.md) together form the word for aid or assistance. The word is read ほさ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[援助](援助.md) also means aid or assistance, but is broader: 補佐 often describes helping a person carry out a role. The words overlap, so they are excluded from each other’s meaning quiz choices.

## Distinctive meaning

補佐 can name either the act of assisting or the person who provides assistance.
