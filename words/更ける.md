# 更ける (ふける) - grow late; night wears on

## Meaning

更ける describes the night advancing or growing late.
It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 更けます, 更けて.
The advancing night can be the subject: 夜が更ける
(よがふける).

- 話しているうちに、夜が更けました。 (はなしているうちに、よがふけました。) - While we were talking, the night grew late.
- 夜が更けても、店は開いていました。 (よがふけても、みせはあいていました。) - The shop was still open even late into the night.

This does not mean that the speakers or shop arrived late
for an appointment.

## How the kanji combine

[更](../kanji/更.md) contributes growing late in this native
verb. Use ふける, not its compound on reading コウ.
深ける is another dictionary-listed spelling, marked rare;
更ける is the main spelling here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暮れる](暮れる.md) (くれる) can describe daylight fading
or a period drawing to a close. Nightfall and the later night
wearing on are not the same event.
[遅れる](遅れる.md) (おくれる) concerns being late or delayed,
which does not by itself say that it is nighttime.

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen also records a season deepening, as in 秋が更ける
(あきがふける), autumn advances. That additional usage does not
make 更ける a general verb for every kind of progress or lateness.
老ける (ふける), grow old, and 耽る (ふける), be absorbed in
something, are different word identities despite the same reading.
