# 目印 (めじるし) - identifying mark; landmark

## Meaning

目印 is a mark used for quick identification or recognition.
It can also be a landmark or guide that helps someone find the intended place.

- 荷物に赤いリボンを目印として付けました。 (にもつにあかいりぼんをめじるしとしてつけました。) - I attached a red ribbon to the luggage as an identifying mark.
- 郵便局を目印に来てください。 (ゆうびんきょくをめじるしにきてください。) - Please use the post office as a landmark when coming here.

Nを目印に uses N as the recognition or navigation cue.
The post office need not have been built for that purpose.

## How the kanji combine

[目](../kanji/目.md) contributes visual recognition, and [印](../kanji/印.md)
contributes a mark or sign. The native reading is め + じるし:
the second part relates to しるし with a voiced initial sound in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[印](印.md) (しるし) is a mark or sign more generally.
[標識](標識.md) (ひょうしき) can be a sign or marker.
[目標](目標.md) (もくひょう) has goal/target uses and also a landmark sense,
so it is not categorically excluded from physical navigation.
[名所](名所.md) (めいしょ) is a famous place; a 目印 need not be famous.

## Distinctive meaning

What matters is the mark's identifying or guiding function, not merely that something is visible.
JMdict records 目標 as an alternate spelling for **めじるし**; that does not
merge this entry with 目標 read **もくひょう**.
It also records an archaic Edo-period "trademark" sense. That historical recognition
is not a recommendation to replace the modern term 商標 (しょうひょう) with 目印.
