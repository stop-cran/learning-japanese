# たっぷり (たっぷり) - plenty; amply

## Meaning

たっぷり describes an ample or generous amount. It works as an adverb, optionally
with と, and in noun phrases such as たっぷりの水 (たっぷりのみず), "plenty of water."

- 鍋に水をたっぷり入れてください。 (なべにみずをたっぷりいれてください。) - Please put plenty of water in the pot.
- 出発まで時間がたっぷりあります。 (しゅっぱつまでじかんがたっぷりあります。) - There is plenty of time before departure.

It can describe time, food, sleep, and other quantities, not just how full a
container looks.

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana-written mimetic word. There is no kanji decomposition or
foreign-language source to infer. Keep the small っ: たっぷり.
Learn the optional と with the whole adverb, as in たっぷりと休む.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[たくさん](たくさん.md) means a lot or many. たっぷり highlights abundance
or a generous amount. [十分](十分.md) (じゅうぶん), "sufficient," evaluates
whether an amount meets a need; the meanings can overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

たっぷり does not promise that the amount is objectively enough for every
purpose. It expresses an ample quantity in the situation. With a measured
duration, たっぷり二時間かかった (たっぷりにじかんかかった) means "took a
full two hours," emphasizing that the duration was substantial rather than
that time was left over.
