# いわゆる (いわゆる) - so-called; what is called

## Meaning

いわゆる introduces the name or category by which something is known.
It is a prenominal expression: place it directly before a noun, without な or の.

- これは、いわゆる省エネ住宅です。 (これは、いわゆるしょうエネじゅうたくです。) - This is what is called an energy-efficient home.
- 彼は、いわゆる天才です。 (かれは、いわゆるてんさいです。) - He is what people call a genius.

The expression points to an established label. Whether the speaker accepts,
questions, or merely explains that label depends on the surrounding message.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses the usual kana spelling. The dictionary also lists 所謂 with
the whole-word reading いわゆる. Do not try to obtain that pronunciation by
joining the ordinary individual readings of the two characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

...と呼ばれる (...とよばれる), "called...," can explain a name with a full verb
construction. [言わば](言わば.md) (いわば), "so to speak," introduces an analogy
or way of putting something; it is not the same prenominal word.

## Distinctive meaning

English "so-called" can sound dismissive, but いわゆる can neutrally identify a
familiar category. Do not automatically add skepticism to its translation.
Also avoid translating it as a freely placed sentence adverb "so to speak":
the noun immediately after いわゆる is central to its construction.
