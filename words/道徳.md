# 道徳 (どうとく) - morals; morality

## Meaning

道徳 is a noun for morals or morality: ideas about right and wrong conduct.
Use 道徳について考える (どうとくについてかんがえる), "think about morality."
道徳の授業 (どうとくのじゅぎょう) is a class on moral education.

- 道徳の授業で、人を助けることについて話し合いました。 (どうとくのじゅぎょうで、ひとをたすけることについてはなしあいました。) - In moral education class, we discussed helping other people.
- 法律と道徳は同じものではありません。 (ほうりつとどうとくはおなじものではありません。) - Law and morality are not the same thing.

For adjectival use, 道徳的な (どうとくてきな) means "moral" or "ethical"; do not automatically attach な to bare 道徳.

## How the kanji combine

[道](../kanji/道.md) contributes a way or path, here a way of conduct.
徳 contributes virtue.
Together they provide a useful image of principles for virtuous conduct.
The on-readings are ドウ + トク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

倫理 (りんり) also concerns ethics and moral principles; the terms overlap.
[法律](法律.md) (ほうりつ) concerns law, while [礼儀](礼儀.md) (れいぎ) concerns manners or etiquette.
Good manners, legal compliance, and moral judgment are related but not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

道徳 names the domain of moral standards; it does not by itself settle a disagreement about what is right.
People can discuss 道徳 while holding different views.
The word also does not mean "morale," the English term for confidence or enthusiasm within a group.
