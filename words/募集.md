# 募集 (ぼしゅう) - recruitment; taking applications

## Meaning

募集 is recruitment or a call for applicants, participants, or contributions. It is a noun and a transitive する-verb. What is being sought takes を: 参加者を募集する (さんかしゃをぼしゅうする), "seek participants."

- イベントの参加者を募集しています。 (イベントのさんかしゃをぼしゅうしています。) - We are recruiting participants for the event.
- アルバイトの募集に応募しました。 (アルバイトのぼしゅうにおうぼしました。) - I applied for the advertised part-time position.

応募する (おうぼする) is applying or responding to the call. In the second sentence, に marks the recruitment opportunity being answered.

## How the kanji combine

[募](../kanji/募.md) contributes soliciting or seeking people and contributions; [集](../kanji/集.md) contributes gathering. Together the related ideas describe seeking people or resources and bringing them together. The on readings are ボ + シュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[集める](集める.md) (あつめる), "collect; gather together," is broader and can also concern people or money. 募集 highlights seeking applications or contributions, not simply picking up objects.

[招待](招待.md) (しょうたい) is an invitation to someone as a guest. English "invitation" can also describe a call for applications, but the Japanese words are not freely interchangeable. 募集 and 応募 describe the seeking and responding sides, not opposites in value.

## Distinctive meaning

募集 is not limited to hiring employees. It can seek event participants or submissions, and the dictionary also records raising funds or contributions: 寄付を募集する (きふをぼしゅうする), "solicit donations." [集金](集金.md) (しゅうきん) concerns collecting money, often payments due; 募集 foregrounds the call for contributions.

A call for applicants does not itself mean that every applicant has been accepted. In finance, 株式の募集 (かぶしきのぼしゅう) concerns offering shares for subscription; this specialized recognition does not turn every use into employee selection.
