# 様 (よう) - way; manner; likeness

## Meaning

様, read よう, occurs in constructions meaning a way of doing something or a likeness. The attachment matters; it is not a freely interchangeable translation of English "way" or "kind."

For a method, use a verb's ます-stem + よう: 言います becomes 言いよう (いいよう), "way of saying." For similarity, use noun + のようだ, "be like N"; before another noun, use Nのような.

- 言いようで印象が変わります。 (いいようでいんしょうがかわります。) - The impression changes with how you say it.
- あの雲は綿のようです。 (あのくもはわたのようです。) - That cloud is like cotton.

## How the kanji combine

The single [様](../kanji/様.md) uses the on reading ヨウ here, though kana よう is useful in these constructions. Do not confuse this reading with the respectful name ending 様 (さま).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[方法](方法.md) (ほうほう) is a standalone noun for a method. Verb-stem + [方](方.md) (かた) also names a way of doing something. Neither replaces every similarity use of よう.

## Distinctive meaning

言いようがない (いいようがない) means "there is no way to express it." The method construction uses 言い, not the dictionary form 言う. By contrast, 綿のような雲 (わたのようなくも), "a cotton-like cloud," uses の and な to connect the comparison. Keeping the method and similarity patterns separate prevents errors caused by a single broad English gloss.
