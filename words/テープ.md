# テープ -- tape

## Meaning

テープ is tape. Context distinguishes adhesive tape used for fastening from recording tape and other tape-like strips.

- 箱をテープでとめました。 (はこをテープでとめました。) -- I fastened the box with tape.
- このテープには昔の歌が入っています。 (このテープにはむかしのうたがはいっています。) -- This tape contains songs from long ago.

In the first example, で marks the fastening material. In the second, テープ names a recording medium.

## How the kanji combine

The word comes from English "tape" and is written in katakana. The ー lengthens テ; it is part of the spelling, not punctuation separating two words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

セロハンテープ specifies cellophane adhesive tape. カセットテープ specifies a cassette recording tape. These are narrower terms that clarify which kind is intended.

## Distinctive meaning

テープ is a noun, not automatically the English verb "to tape." For sticking tape onto something, use テープを貼る (テープをはる). If someone asks for テープ without further context, the needed type may have to be established from the task at hand.
