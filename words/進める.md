# 進める (すすめる) - move forward; carry forward

## Meaning

進める is a transitive ichidan verb meaning to move something forward or carry work or a plan forward. Mark what is advanced with を.

- 会議を予定どおりに進めましょう。 (かいぎをよていどおりにすすめましょう。) - Let's proceed with the meeting as planned.
- 時計を一時間進めました。 (とけいをいちじかんすすめました。) - I set the clock forward one hour.

仕事を進める (しごとをすすめる) means "make progress with the work." The past is 進めた (すすめた); the negative is 進めない (すすめない).

## How the kanji combine

[進](../kanji/進.md) supplies the forward-progress idea, and める completes the native verb. Compare the related intransitive [進む](進む.md) (すすむ): 仕事が進む, "the work progresses."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[続ける](続ける.md) (つづける), "continue," emphasizes not stopping; 進める emphasizes advancing the activity.

[勧める](勧める.md) has the same reading すすめる but means to recommend or encourage. 本を勧める recommends a book; it does not mean moving it forward.

## Distinctive meaning

The object need not physically move: discussions, preparations, and research can be advanced. Conversely, 進める is not a general substitute for "promote" in every English sentence. Learn the object and verb together. The を/が contrast is especially useful: someone advances the plan with を進める, while the plan itself progresses with が進む.
