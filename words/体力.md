# 体力 (たいりょく) — physical strength; stamina

## Meaning

体力 is the body's strength, stamina, or ability to keep up physical activity. It is not limited to how much weight someone can lift.

- 毎日歩いて、体力をつけます。 (まいにちあるいて、たいりょくをつけます。) — I build up my stamina by walking every day.
- この仕事は体力がいります。 (このしごとはたいりょくがいります。) — This job requires physical stamina.

## How the kanji combine

[体](../kanji/体.md) (body) modifies [力](../kanji/力.md) (strength): bodily strength.
The on readings are タイ + リョク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[力](力.md) (ちから) is broader: strength or power in many senses.
筋力 (きんりょく) focuses on muscle strength.
[元気](元気.md) (げんき) means being well or energetic, which is related but does not measure endurance.
There is no ordinary single-word opposite; 体力がない (たいりょくがない) means lacking stamina.

## Distinctive meaning

体力 is useful when talking about exercise, tiring work, or the capacity to keep going.
体力をつける means to build it up; feeling cheerful alone does not necessarily mean having much 体力.
