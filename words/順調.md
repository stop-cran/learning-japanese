# 順調 (じゅんちょう) - going smoothly; doing well

## Meaning

順調 describes favorable, smooth progress. It is a na-adjective: use 順調な before a noun, 順調です as a predicate, and 順調に before a verb.

- 準備は順調です。 (じゅんびはじゅんちょうです。) - The preparations are going well.
- 工事は順調に進んでいます。 (こうじはじゅんちょうにすすんでいます。) - Construction is progressing smoothly.

The second example describes an ongoing process. 順調な回復 (じゅんちょうなかいふく) means "a smooth recovery," not necessarily a recovery already completed.

## How the kanji combine

順 contributes orderly or favorable progression, while [調](../kanji/調.md) contributes a harmonious or well-adjusted condition. Together they suggest a process proceeding well. The on readings are ジュン + チョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[調子](調子.md) (ちょうし) can describe condition or performance: 調子がいい means "be in good form/run well." 順調 emphasizes how a process is progressing. 不調 (ふちょう), "poor condition/not going well," can contrast with it, though the sentence pattern needs adjusting.

## Distinctive meaning

順調 is not a general-purpose "OK" for every conversation. It works when assessing progress or development, not simply agreeing to a request. 順調ではありません (じゅんちょうではありません) means things are not progressing smoothly; it does not say that success is impossible or that no progress has occurred.
