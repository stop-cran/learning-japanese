# 冒険 (ぼうけん) - adventure; risky venture

## Meaning

冒険 is an adventure or venture into something uncertain. 冒険する means to venture or have an adventure. It can also describe a risky attempt whose success is doubtful.

- 子供のころ、冒険の物語が好きでした。 (こどものころ、ぼうけんのものがたりがすきでした。) - As a child, I liked adventure stories.
- 準備なしで店を開くのは冒険です。 (じゅんびなしでみせをひらくのはぼうけんです。) - Opening a shop without preparation is a risky venture.

The first example suggests exploration and excitement; the second emphasizes risk. Calling something 冒険 does not promise an enjoyable outcome.

## How the kanji combine

冒 contributes braving or venturing, while [険](../kanji/険.md) contributes danger or difficulty. The relationship is roughly venturing into danger. The on readings are ボウ + ケン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) is travel, which need not involve the uncertainty associated with an adventure. [危険](危険.md) (きけん) means danger; it can exist without anyone undertaking a venture. Safety can contrast with the risk, but it is not a direct opposite of every adventure story.

## Distinctive meaning

冒険 can be literal or figurative. It may concern a journey, a business decision, or trying something outside one's usual experience. Check whether the speaker is presenting the uncertainty positively as adventure or negatively as a gamble. The noun itself does not settle that evaluation.
