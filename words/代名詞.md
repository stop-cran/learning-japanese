# 代名詞 (だいめいし) - pronoun; byword

## Meaning

代名詞 first names the grammatical category pronoun: a word used in place
of, or to refer to, a named person or thing. Japanese grammatical
classifications do not have to match English ones in every detail.

- 英語の「he」は代名詞です。
  (えいごの「he」はだいめいしです。) - English "he" is a pronoun.
- 彼の名前は成功の代名詞になりました。
  (かれのなまえはせいこうのだいめいしになりました。) - His name became a byword for success.

The second example is figurative: something becomes a characteristic
representative or classic example of another thing. Nの代名詞 can mean
a byword or representative example of N, not a grammatical pronoun for N.

## How the kanji combine

[代](../kanji/代.md) contributes substitution, [名](../kanji/名.md)
relates to naming, and [詞](../kanji/詞.md) to words or parts of speech.
ダイ + メイ + シ gives だいめいし. The grammatical term can be remembered
as a word standing in for a name; that mnemonic is not a full theory of pronouns.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[代表](代表.md) (だいひょう), [典型](典型.md) (てんけい) and
[例](例.md) (れい) overlap the representative-example side, not the
grammatical category. [型](型.md) (かた) has model or pattern uses, but its
physical mold and martial-arts-form uses are not definitions of 代名詞.
These comparisons do not make every example or pattern a byword.

## Distinctive meaning

The figurative sense is about characteristic association: a name can evoke
success, quality or some other trait. It need not be a literal synonym
usable in the same sentence position. Identify whether the passage concerns
grammar or a representative example before choosing an English translation.
