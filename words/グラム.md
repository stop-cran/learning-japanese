# グラム — gram

## Meaning

グラム is a gram, a metric unit of mass used for ingredients, food portions, and other quantities. Put the number immediately before it.

- 砂糖を百グラム使います。 (さとうをひゃくグラムつかいます。) — I'll use one hundred grams of sugar.
- この袋は何グラムですか。 (このふくろはなんグラムですか。) — How many grams does this bag weigh?

The unit does not gain an English-style plural ending.

## How the kanji combine

グラム is written in katakana as a borrowed measurement term. The dictionary also records the rare character spelling 瓦 for the unit, but ordinary writing uses グラム or the symbol g.

## Synonyms and antonyms

キログラム is a kilogram: one thousand grams. ミリグラム is a milligram: one thousandth of a gram. These are different-sized units, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

グラム measures mass, not volume. A recipe's グラム and ミリリットル are therefore not automatically interchangeable. Learn the full number-plus-unit expression, such as 五十グラム (ごじゅうグラム), rather than adding a separate counter between them.
