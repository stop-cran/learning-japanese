# 皆 (みな) — everyone; all

## Meaning

皆 means everyone or all of the relevant group. This article uses the reading みな.
JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana, so みな is not an inferior spelling of 皆.

- 皆、元気です。 (みな、げんきです。) — Everyone is well.
- 学生は皆、教室にいます。 (がくせいはみな、きょうしつにいます。) — All the students are in the classroom.

The group comes from context: the first sentence need not mean every person in the world.
In the second, 皆 applies to the students already named.

The reading [みんな](みんな.md) is also taught here, with the same basic meaning:

- 皆で写真を撮りましょう。 (みんなでしゃしんをとりましょう。) — Let's all take a photograph together.

In this example, で marks the group taking part together. The article keeps みな as its primary reading
and its N4 label; neither prevents learning this additional source reading.

## How the kanji combine

One character, [皆](../kanji/皆.md), writes the complete native word みな.
The reading みんな is also valid; it is not a mistake to be corrected to みな in every setting.
Neither reading should be replaced by the on reading カイ in these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

みんな has the same basic group meaning; JMdict describes it as less formal and more emphatic.
[全員](全員.md) (ぜんいん) explicitly refers to all members of a group.
[全部](全部.md) ぜんぶ emphasizes the whole or total amount; choose the expression that fits what is being counted or included.

## Distinctive meaning

皆さん (みなさん) is the honorific group expression, useful when addressing or referring to people respectfully.
Learn its reading as みなさん rather than attaching さん to みんな.
The bare word 皆 can refer to the group or apply across it, as the two examples show; it is not a suffix that must be attached to a person's name.
