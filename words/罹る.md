# 罹る (かかる) - contract a disease

## Meaning

罹る means to contract or suffer from a disease. It is an intransitive godan verb:
the illness takes に, not を. The polite form is かかります, the past かかった,
and the negative かからない.

- 去年、インフルエンザにかかりました。 (きょねん、インフルエンザにかかりました。) - I caught influenza last year.
- 兄は重い病気に罹っています。 (あにはおもいびょうきにかかっています。) - My older brother is suffering from a serious illness.

The past form can describe contracting an illness; かかっている can describe the
resulting state of being ill.

## How the kanji combine

罹 expresses being afflicted. The whole native verb is read かかる, with the final
る in kana. JMdict recognizes this kanji spelling and marks the sense as usually
kana-written. That preference does not make the spelling incorrect or establish
that the word itself is rare.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[病気](病気.md)になる (びょうきになる), "become ill," states the change more
generally. 病気が治る (びょうきがなおる), "recover from an illness," is a useful
opposing event, not another meaning of かかる.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the disease context. English "suffer from" also occurs with financial or
mechanical problems; this entry does not license those uses of 罹る.
The many other meanings of [掛かる](掛かる.md) (かかる), such as taking time,
belong to a separate lexical entry despite the shared pronunciation.
