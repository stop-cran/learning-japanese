# 出迎える (でむかえる) - go out to meet; greet an arrival

## Meaning

出迎える means going out to meet or greet someone who is arriving. It is a transitive ichidan verb: the arriving person takes を, and に can mark the place of the meeting.

- 母を駅に出迎えました。 (ははをえきにでむかえました。) - I met my mother on her arrival at the station.
- 玄関でお客様を出迎えます。 (げんかんでおきゃくさまをでむかえます。) - I greet the guests at the entrance.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) gives going out and [迎](../kanji/迎.md) receiving: one goes out to welcome an arrival. The related noun is [出迎え](出迎え.md). Forms include 出迎えます, 出迎えて, and 出迎えない; this is not a godan verb simply because it ends in る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[迎える](迎える.md) is broader and can describe welcoming an event or a new stage as well as a person. [会う](会う.md) means meeting someone more generally. 見送る (みおくる) can describe seeing someone off, the departure-side contrast to receiving an arrival.

## Distinctive meaning

The arrival and going-out-to-receive perspective matter. A scheduled discussion with a colleague is not automatically 出迎える, and merely saying hello does not always involve receiving someone arriving. Conversely, one can go just to the entrance rather than travel a long distance.
