# 娯楽 (ごらく) - entertainment; recreation

## Meaning

娯楽 is entertainment, amusement, or recreation: things people do or experience for enjoyment, especially outside work or study.

- この町には娯楽施設が少ないです。 (このまちにはごらくしせつがすくないです。) - This town has few entertainment facilities.
- 読書は私の大切な娯楽です。 (どくしょはわたしのたいせつなごらくです。) - Reading is an important recreation for me.

It is useful as a noun and before another noun, as in 娯楽施設, entertainment facilities, or 娯楽映画 (ごらくえいが), a film intended as entertainment.

## How the kanji combine

娯 contributes recreation or pleasure, and [楽](../kanji/楽.md) contributes enjoyment or ease. Their related meanings reinforce the enjoyment idea. The on readings are ゴ + ラク, not the ガク reading familiar from music vocabulary.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[遊び](遊び.md) (あそび) overlaps in play and recreation. [楽しみ](楽しみ.md) (たのしみ) can be enjoyment or something anticipated with pleasure. [趣味](趣味.md) (しゅみ) often names a person's hobby or taste; 娯楽 can cover entertainment without implying a continuing personal hobby.

## Distinctive meaning

娯楽 is not necessarily childish or frivolous. A book, film, or recreational activity can count, depending on its role for the person. Nor does the word claim that everyone enjoys that activity equally.

The practical noun use here does not require adding する to mean enjoying a film. The dictionaries' wider historical verb coverage is not a model for every modern sentence.
