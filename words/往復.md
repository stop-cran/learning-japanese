# 往復 (おうふく) - round trip; return ticket

## Meaning

往復 is going somewhere and returning: a round trip.
往復する describes making that journey. In a ticket-buying context,
往復 can also abbreviate 往復切符 (おうふくきっぷ), a round-trip
or return ticket.

- 駅まで往復で四十分かかります。
  (えきまでおうふくでよんじゅっぷんかかります。) - It takes forty minutes to go to the station and back.
- 東京と大阪を何度も往復しました。
  (とうきょうとおおさかをなんどもおうふくしました。) - I travelled back and forth between Tokyo and Osaka many times.

The forty minutes covers both directions, not forty minutes each way.
In the second example, を marks the route or stretch travelled.

## How the kanji combine

往 contributes going, and [復](../kanji/復.md) returning.
This is a paired combination of the two directions: オウ + フク.
往 is part of the written word even though it has no kanji card here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

片道 (かたみち), one way, contrasts with a round trip.
[帰り](帰り.md) (かえり) names the return or way back; it does
not itself include the outward journey. A return ticket in English
can cover both journeys, so its "return" is not just the Japanese 帰り.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records exchanging letters and visiting one
another. Those back-and-forth uses do not make every correspondence
a transport ticket. For travel, establish whether a duration, fare or
distance is for one direction or for the complete 往復.
