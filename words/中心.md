# 中心 (ちゅうしん) - center; core; focus

## Meaning

中心 is a noun for a center, core, or focus. Use Nの中心 for the center of something. Nを中心に means "centered on N" or "with N as the main focus."

- 町の中心に大きな広場があります。 (まちのちゅうしんにおおきなひろばがあります。) - There is a large square in the center of town.
- この授業では会話を中心に勉強します。 (このじゅぎょうではかいわをちゅうしんにべんきょうします。) - In this class, we study with a focus on conversation.

The first example is spatial. In the second, conversation is the focus of the activity, not a physical place.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) contributes middle or within, and [心](../kanji/心.md) contributes heart or core. Their reinforcing meanings form a word for the central part or organizing focus.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[真ん中](真ん中.md) (まんなか) emphasizes the middle of a space or arrangement. 中心 also extends naturally to a main focus or hub. [周り](周り.md) (まわり), "surroundings," gives a spatial contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

A town's 中心 need not be its mathematically exact midpoint; it may be the main active area. The suffix-like use in 野菜中心の食事 (やさいちゅうしんのしょくじ), "a vegetable-centered diet," likewise identifies emphasis, not necessarily exclusivity. Other foods may still be included.
