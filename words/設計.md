# 設計 (せっけい) - design; planning

## Meaning

設計 is a design or the work of planning how something will be constructed or arranged.
設計する is transitive: 家を設計する (いえをせっけいする), "design a house."
It can concern buildings, machines, systems, and other planned structures.

- 姉は建物を設計する仕事をしています。 (あねはたてものをせっけいするしごとをしています。) - My older sister designs buildings for a living.
- 使いやすいように、この装置を設計しました。 (つかいやすいように、このそうちをせっけいしました。) - We designed this device to be easy to use.

設計を変更する (せっけいをへんこうする) means "change the design."
設計図 (せっけいず) is a design drawing or plan.

## How the kanji combine

設 contributes establishing or arranging, and [計](../kanji/計.md) planning or calculating.
Together they suggest working out an arrangement before or during its creation.
The reading is せっけい: セツ + ケイ produces the small っ in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計画](計画.md) (けいかく) is a broader plan, including an itinerary or schedule.
デザイン overlaps with design and may emphasize appearance, though its scope is not limited to decoration.
[建設](建設.md) (けんせつ) concerns construction rather than the design alone.

## Distinctive meaning

Completing a 設計 does not necessarily mean the object has been built.
The word highlights the planned structure or arrangement, while manufacturing, construction, and installation are further activities.
A practical design can include function and usability, not just how something looks.
