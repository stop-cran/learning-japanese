# 揺れる (ゆれる) - shake; sway

## Meaning

揺れる describes shaking, swaying, rocking, or trembling. The moving thing is the subject; this is an intransitive ichidan verb.

- 風で木が揺れています。 (かぜできがゆれています。) - The tree is swaying in the wind.
- 電車が急に揺れました。 (でんしゃがきゅうにゆれました。) - The train suddenly jolted.

Useful forms are 揺れます, 揺れて, 揺れた and 揺れない. The movement can be gentle or strong; the verb alone does not specify its force.

## How the kanji combine

揺 expresses shaking or swaying and is read ゆ here. れる is okurigana; the ichidan stem includes れ: 揺れ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

揺らす (ゆらす) is transitive: to shake or rock something. 動く (うごく), "move," is broader and does not necessarily suggest back-and-forth motion.

## Distinctive meaning

揺れる can also describe uncertainty: 気持ちが揺れる (きもちがゆれる) means one's feelings or resolve waver. That figurative use is not physical trembling.
