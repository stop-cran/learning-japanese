# 着々 (ちゃくちゃく) - steadily; step by step

## Meaning

着々 describes something progressing steadily, with successive parts
being carried out in order. It is an adverb; 着々と is also possible.
着着 is another spelling of the same word.

- 準備は着々と進んでいます。 (じゅんびはちゃくちゃくとすすんでいます。) - The preparations are progressing steadily.
- 開店に向けて、着々と準備を進めました。 (かいてんにむけて、ちゃくちゃくとじゅんびをすすめました。) - We steadily moved the preparations forward toward opening the shop.

着々 modifies the progress or preparation. It is not an adjective
to attach to an arbitrary noun with な.

## How the kanji combine

[着](../kanji/着.md) is repeated with the on reading チャク:
ちゃく + ちゃく. The mark 々 repeats the character.
Settling successive parts of a task is a useful association for the
whole expression, not a verified historical derivation or a literal
instruction to put on clothes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[順調](順調.md)に (じゅんちょうに) describes favorable, smooth
progress. [だんだん](だんだん.md) emphasizes change by degrees.
They can overlap with 着々 in a developing process without sharing
all its emphasis on orderly progress.

## Distinctive meaning

着々 does not specify a numerical speed. Work can progress steadily
without being either slow or exceptionally fast.
Nor does progress toward a goal prove that the goal has already
been reached: 着々と準備している still describes preparation.
