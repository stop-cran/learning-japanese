# 細かい (こまかい) — fine; small in detail

## Meaning

細かい is an い-adjective for small pieces, fine particles, or minute details. It commonly focuses on the scale of individual parts rather than the overall size of an object.

- 細かい字が読めません。 (こまかいじがよめません。) — I cannot read the small print.
- 野菜を細かく切ります。 (やさいをこまかくきります。) — I cut the vegetables into small pieces.

## How the kanji combine

細 supplies こま; かい is the written okurigana. The whole adjective is こまかい, and its adverbial form is 細かく, not a form obtained by removing all the okurigana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

小さい (ちいさい) describes small size more generally. 詳しい (くわしい) means detailed or knowledgeable. 粗い (あらい), "coarse; rough," contrasts with fineness in suitable contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

細かいお金 (こまかいおかね) means small-denomination money or change. 細かい注意 (こまかいちゅうい) can indicate detailed care or instructions. English "fine" meaning "excellent" is not automatically 細かい.
