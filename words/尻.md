# 尻 (しり) - buttocks; bottom

## Meaning

尻 is a noun for the buttocks or rear. The form お尻 (おしり) adds お and is useful in ordinary conversation,
including talking about sitting, falls, and clothing.
Use 尻が痛い (しりがいたい) for "my bottom hurts."

- 転んで、お尻を打ちました。 (ころんで、おしりをうちました。) - I fell and landed hard on my bottom.
- 長く座っていたので、お尻が痛いです。 (ながくすわっていたので、おしりがいたいです。) - My bottom hurts because I was sitting for a long time.

The first example treats the body part as the object of 打つ; the second makes it the subject of the painful state.

## How the kanji combine

尻 is a single character representing the native noun しり.
The prefix お in お尻 is not part of a different kanji compound.
Its presence changes the expression's presentation, not the body part being named.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[腰](腰.md) (こし) concerns the waist or lower-back region, not exactly the same part.
[背中](背中.md) (せなか) is the back.
These are neighboring body areas, not antonyms or freely interchangeable translations of English "hips."

## Distinctive meaning

The noun also extends to a bottom or end, as in 尻尾 (しっぽ), "tail," although compounds have their own readings and meanings.
When describing pain or injury, name the actual location rather than choosing 尻 from a broad English gloss.
The examples here teach the body-part sense.
