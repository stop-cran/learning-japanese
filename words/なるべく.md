# なるべく - as far as possible

## Meaning

なるべく means as much as possible, as far as practical, or if possible. It modifies the desired action or degree rather than naming a fixed amount.

- なるべく早く来てください。 (なるべくはやくきてください。) - Please come as early as possible.
- なるべくお金を使わないようにしています。 (なるべくおかねをつかわないようにしています。) - I try not to spend money where possible.

No particle is required immediately after this adverb.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. Dictionary alternatives include 成るべく, but learners need not use kanji for this everyday expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

できるだけ also means "as much as possible" and often overlaps. 必ず (かならず), "without fail," expresses a stronger requirement rather than practical effort.

## Distinctive meaning

なるべく can modify avoiding something, not only doing more of it. It normally leaves room for practical limitations; do not treat it as a promise that the absolute maximum will always be achieved.
