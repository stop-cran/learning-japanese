# 講師 (こうし) - lecturer; instructor

## Meaning

講師 is someone who gives a lecture or teaches a class: a lecturer,
speaker, or instructor. It can also be an academic position.
講師を務める (こうしをつとめる) means "serve as a lecturer."

- 研修の講師を紹介します。 (けんしゅうのこうしをしょうかいします。) - I will introduce the instructor for the training.
- 彼女は大学の講師です。 (かのじょはだいがくのこうしです。) - She is a university lecturer.

The dictionary also records part-time schoolteachers and cram-school tutors.
The institution and employment context determine the intended role.

## How the kanji combine

[講](../kanji/講.md) contributes lecturing or explaining, and
[師](../kanji/師.md) contributes a teacher or specialist. Together they
name the person teaching, not the lecture itself. コウ + シ gives こうし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[教師](教師.md) (きょうし) is a broader occupational word for a teacher.
[先生](先生.md) (せんせい) can identify or address a teacher respectfully.
[講義](講義.md) (こうぎ) and [講演](講演.md) (こうえん) concern the
lecture or talk, not the person delivering it.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every 講師 as "professor," or infer that every lecturer
is part-time. University lecturers can be full-time or part-time, and a guest
講師 need not hold a university appointment. A job title and an honorific
way of addressing that person serve different purposes.
