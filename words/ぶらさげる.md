# ぶらさげる - hang down; dangle; carry hanging

## Meaning

ぶらさげる means to hang or suspend something so that it hangs down.
It also describes carrying something loosely in the hand.
The thing hung or carried takes を; に can mark its support.

- 壁にかばんをぶらさげました。 (かべにかばんをぶらさげました。) - I hung the bag on the wall.
- 彼は袋を片手にぶらさげています。 (かれはふくろをかたてにぶらさげています。) - He is carrying a bag dangling from one hand.

It is ichidan: ぶらさげます, ぶらさげた, and ぶらさげない.

## How the kanji combine

The dictionary spelling is **ぶら下げる**.
[下](../kanji/下.md)げる (さげる) contributes lowering or hanging;
ぶら adds the dangling association.
This page keeps the source list's kana form as its stable headword.
The alternative spelling does not change the verb's conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[吊るす](吊るす.md) (つるす), [吊る](吊る.md) (つる),
and [掛ける](掛ける.md) (かける) overlap in hanging uses.
[持つ](持つ.md) (もつ) is broader holding or carrying:
ぶらさげる describes a particular hanging manner, not every way to carry a bag.

## Distinctive meaning

[ぶらぶら](ぶらぶら.md) can describe dangling or swinging;
ぶらぶらさせる explicitly makes something dangle or swing.
ぶらさげる focuses on hanging the object or carrying it hanging,
without requiring continuous back-and-forth movement.
It is not a verb for aimlessly hanging around a place.
