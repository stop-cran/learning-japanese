# 苦い (にがい) — bitter-tasting

## Meaning

苦い describes a bitter taste, such as the taste of some medicines or unsweetened coffee.
It is an i-adjective: 苦い薬 (にがいくすり) means "bitter medicine."
This article focuses on the taste sense.

- このコーヒーは少し苦いです。
  (このコーヒーはすこしにがいです。)
  — This coffee is a little bitter.

## How the kanji combine

[苦](../kanji/苦.md) represents にが, and the written い completes the adjective.
The same character can express suffering in other words, but that does not make 苦い a
general-purpose adjective for everything difficult.
The past is 苦かった (にがかった); the negative is 苦くない (にがくない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

甘い (あまい), "sweet," is a useful taste contrast.
辛い (からい), "spicy; hot," names another taste sensation, not bitterness.
苦しい (くるしい), "painful; difficult," shares the kanji but describes distress rather than
the coffee's taste.

## Distinctive meaning

Bitterness does not itself say whether the speaker likes the drink or food.
Someone can enjoy coffee while describing it as 苦い.
Do not switch to 苦しい merely to say the coffee tastes very bitter; the two readings belong
to different adjectives.
