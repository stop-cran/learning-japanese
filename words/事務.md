# 事務 (じむ) — office work

## Meaning

事務 means clerical or administrative work: handling the business matters that keep an organization running.
Think of records, forms, and arrangements rather than every possible job performed inside an office.

- 大学で事務の仕事をしています。 (だいがくでじむのしごとをしています。) — I do administrative work at a university.
- 午前中に事務を済ませました。 (ごぜんちゅうにじむをすませました。) — I finished the administrative work during the morning.

In 事務の仕事, の connects the kind of work to 仕事. The noun names the work, not the employee.

## How the kanji combine

[事](../kanji/事.md) contributes matters or affairs, and [務](../kanji/務.md) contributes duties to be carried out.
Their combination points to attending to organizational affairs. The on readings are ジ + ム.
It does not literally mean a physical "office room."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと), "work; job," is broader: office administration is one possible kind of 仕事.
勤務 (きんむ), "work; service," concerns working or being on duty rather than specifying clerical tasks.
Neither word automatically replaces 事務 when the type of work matters.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 事務 to identify the administrative content of a job. A person working in an office might instead
design software, conduct research, or do other work that is not being described as 事務.
There is no single useful opposite covering all those other jobs.
