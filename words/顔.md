# 顔 (かお) — face; facial features

## Meaning

顔 (かお) is the face: the front of the head, including its visible features.
It is useful both when talking about the body and when describing someone's appearance.

- 顔を洗いました。 (かおをあらいました。) — I washed my face.
- 顔に何かついています。 (かおになにかついています。) — There is something on your face.

In the second example, に marks the surface where something is attached.
The owner of the face can be understood from context rather than stated every time.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [顔](../kanji/顔.md) writes the native noun かお.
There is no compound relation to unpack.
In 笑顔 (えがお), "smiling face; smile," 顔 becomes がお; learn the full reading えがお rather than reading it character by character from on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頭](頭.md) (あたま) is the head as a whole, not just the face.
表情 (ひょうじょう) focuses on facial expression: the look that a face conveys.
笑顔 specifies a smiling expression. Neither of these means that 顔 by itself implies smiling.

## Distinctive meaning

顔 identifies a body part or someone's facial appearance, not a general flat surface.
For a tabletop or another object's surface, 表面 (ひょうめん) is the relevant surface word rather than a mechanical substitution of 顔.
Some compounds have their own extended meanings, so keep the plain noun's face sense separate from what another whole word may mean.
