# 塞ぐ (ふさぐ) - block; plug; feel down

## Meaning

塞ぐ means to block, plug, cover or occupy something. A separate intransitive use means to feel depressed or downhearted.

- 穴を板でふさぎました。 (あなをいたでふさぎました。) - I covered the hole with a board.
- 大きな音がしたので、耳をふさぎました。 (おおきなおとがしたので、みみをふさぎました。) - There was a loud noise, so I covered my ears.
- その知らせを聞いて、気がふさいでいます。 (そのしらせをきいて、きがふさいでいます。) - I have been feeling down since hearing the news.

## How the kanji combine

塞 expresses blocking or closing; ぐ is the native verb ending. The character has no card yet. The physical uses do not by themselves explain the word's historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[塞がる](塞がる.md) (ふさがる) describes being blocked or occupied. [妨げる](妨げる.md) (さまたげる) overlaps in obstructing, without requiring a plugged opening. [沈む](沈む.md) (しずむ) also has a downhearted use.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the construction with the sense: 穴をふさぐ is transitive, but 気がふさぐ is intransitive. Do not treat all uses as an action performed on an object. This godan verb changes to ふさがない and ふさいで. The dictionary also records a duty-performing use; that does not make 塞ぐ the general replacement for "do" or "finish."
