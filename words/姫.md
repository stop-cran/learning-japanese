# 姫 (ひめ) — princess; young lady of noble birth

## Meaning

姫 names a daughter of high rank. In a fairy tale it can be translated as “princess”; the word itself does not say that her father is a king.

- 物語の姫は自分で城を出た。 (ものがたりのひめはじぶんでしろをでた。) — The princess in the story left the castle on her own.
- 劇で姫の役を演じた。 (げきでひめのやくをえんじた。) — I played the princess in the play.

## How the kanji combine

[姫](../kanji/姫.md) writes the whole noun ひめ, with no kana ending. お姫様 (おひめさま) adds お and 様 around that same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[王女](王女.md) (おうじょ) means the daughter of a king. A storyteller can call her 姫, but 姫 can also describe a nobleman's daughter. [王妃](王妃.md) (おうひ) names a king's wife.

## Distinctive meaning

Ask why the person has the title: “a lord's daughter” fits 姫 without making her a queen. For your own daughter in an ordinary introduction, use [娘](娘.md) (むすめ).
