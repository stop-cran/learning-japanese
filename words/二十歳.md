# 二十歳 (はたち) — twenty years old

## Meaning

二十歳 is the age twenty, conventionally read はたち. Use 二十歳です to state someone's age and 二十歳になる for turning twenty.

来月、二十歳になります。 (らいげつ、はたちになります。) — I turn twenty next month.

## How the kanji combine

[二](../kanji/二.md) and [十](../kanji/十.md) write twenty; [歳](../kanji/歳.md) marks age. はたち is a special whole-word reading, not a concatenation of the usual readings of these three characters. The spelling 20歳 is also common.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The same age can be pronounced にじゅっさい or にじっさい, also recorded for 二十歳. These are readings of the same expression, not different ages.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not add さい to はたち: it already expresses the complete age. For twenty objects, the ordinary number is にじゅう with an appropriate counter, not はたち. The word identifies an age; it does not by itself define the current legal age of adulthood.

二十歳 does not mean a calendar's twentieth year or a twentieth anniversary.
