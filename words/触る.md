# 触る (さわる) - touch; handle

## Meaning

触る means to touch or handle something. It is a godan verb despite ending in る: 触ります, 触って, 触った and 触らない.

- このボタンに触らないでください。 (このボタンにさわらないでください。) - Please do not touch this button.
- 猫の背中をそっと触りました。 (ねこのせなかをそっとさわりました。) - I gently touched the cat's back.

に commonly marks the point of contact. を is also possible when touching or handling something as the object of the action.

## How the kanji combine

触 expresses contact and is read さわ here; る is okurigana. The same character appears in 触れる (ふれる), a different verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

触れる also means to touch, with overlapping but not identical uses. 持つ (もつ), "hold," involves possession or support rather than mere contact.

## Distinctive meaning

触る does not necessarily mean pushing a button or moving an object. Contact alone can be enough, so warnings with 触らないで prohibit touching, not just operating something.
