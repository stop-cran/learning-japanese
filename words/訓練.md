# 訓練 (くんれん) - training; drill

## Meaning

訓練 is a noun for training, practice, or a drill intended to develop
a skill or reliable response. 訓練する means "train"; the person,
animal, or capacity being trained can take を.

- 犬を訓練しています。 (いぬをくんれんしています。) - I am training a dog.
- 学校で避難訓練を行いました。 (がっこうでひなんくんれんをおこないました。) - We held an evacuation drill at school.

避難 (ひなん) means evacuation. For the learner's side, 訓練を受ける
(くんれんをうける) means "receive training." This distinguishes the
person conducting training from someone undergoing it.

## How the kanji combine

訓 contributes instruction, and 練 contributes practice or refinement.
The two related ideas combine into training through instruction and
practice. Their on readings are くん + れん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[練習](練習.md) (れんしゅう) is practice, such as rehearsing a piece
or working on a skill. 訓練 often highlights developing a capability
or rehearsing a response. The words overlap; this is not a ban on
using either word in the other's contexts. [教育](教育.md)
(きょういく), "education," has a broader scope.

## Distinctive meaning

訓練 does not by itself imply military activity or harsh treatment.
The noun before it identifies the skill or scenario, as 避難 does
in the example. A drill can prepare people for an event that is not
actually taking place.
