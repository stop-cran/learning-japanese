# ぎくしゃく — awkward and stiff; strained

## Meaning

ぎくしゃく describes movements or interactions that feel awkward, stiff, or strained.

- 緊張して、彼はぎくしゃくした動きで前に出た。
  — Nervous, he stepped forward with stiff, awkward movements.
- 誤解が続き、二人の関係がぎくしゃくしてきた。
  — After a series of misunderstandings, their relationship became strained.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぎくしゃく often describes something that does not flow smoothly, whether movement or a
relationship. It does not by itself mean that people are openly hostile.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can describe a specific awkward action or a relationship that has become strained.
The surrounding sentence identifies which.
