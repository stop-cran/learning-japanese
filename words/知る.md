# 知る (しる) — know; find out

## Meaning

知る means knowing something or coming to know it. A useful first distinction is between 知りました (しりました),
"found out," and 知っています (しっています), "know."

- その人を知っています。 (そのひとをしっています。) — I know that person.
- きのう、そのことを知りました。 (きのう、そのことをしりました。) — I found out about that yesterday.

For "I don't know," the ordinary polite form is 知りません (しりません). The positive and negative do not
normally use matching ている forms in this basic exchange.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single native verb. [知](../kanji/知.md) supplies し; る is okurigana.
Despite the i-sound before る, it is a godan verb: 知ります (しります), 知って (しって), 知らない (しらない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [分かる](分かる.md) (わかる) often emphasizes understanding. 知っている can mean that you know a fact without fully understanding it.
- [習う](習う.md) (ならう) means learning under instruction, not simply finding out a piece of news.
- 忘れる (わすれる), "forget," means losing access to something you knew. Merely not knowing is 知らない (しらない), not necessarily forgetting.

## Distinctive meaning

知っている is useful for familiarity with people, places, and facts. The thing known normally takes を.
Do not turn the contrast with 分かる into a rigid rule: dictionary senses overlap, and the natural verb depends
on what kind of knowing or understanding the sentence describes.
