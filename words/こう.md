# こう (こう) — like this

## Meaning

こう means **in this way or like this**. It points to a manner the speaker is demonstrating or presenting. It is an adverb, so it can modify a verb without に.

- こう書いてください。 (こうかいてください。) — Please write it like this.
- 私はこう思います。 (わたしはこうおもいます。) — This is what I think.

The first sentence needs a demonstration or an already clear model; こう alone does not explain the method.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is kana. The dictionary also records the rare spelling 斯う, but it is not needed for everyday writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そう means “like that,” often referring to what the other person has said or shown. ああ points to “that way” more remotely; どう asks “how?” These form a demonstrative set rather than an antonym pair.

## Distinctive meaning

こう describes manner, not a physical route. For “come this way” when indicating a direction, こちら is often appropriate. こう言う (こういう), “say this,” also appears in こういう + noun, “this kind of ...”
