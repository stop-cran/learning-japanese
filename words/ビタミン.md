# ビタミン - vitamin

## Meaning

ビタミン is a vitamin. It is a noun, used in names such as
ビタミンC (ビタミンシー), "vitamin C," and in descriptions of food.

- この果物にはビタミンCが含まれています。 (このくだものにはビタミンシーがふくまれています。) - This fruit contains vitamin C.
- 食品のビタミンの量を調べました。 (しょくひんのビタミンのりょうをしらべました。) - I checked the amount of vitamins in the food.

ビタミンが豊富な食品 (ビタミンがほうふなしょくひん)
means food rich in vitamins.

## How the kanji combine

There is no kanji compound. The inspected dictionaries attribute the
Japanese loanword to German Vitamin. JMdict also records ヴィタミン
and バイタミン; these are recognition variants, not separate nutrients.

## Synonyms and antonyms

栄養素 (えいようそ) is the broader term "nutrient."
[栄養](栄養.md) (えいよう) is nutrition or nourishment.
[薬](薬.md) (くすり), medicine, is not an interchangeable name
for every vitamin or vitamin-containing food.

## Distinctive meaning

ビタミン can refer to a vitamin in food or in a prepared product;
it does not by itself mean a supplement tablet.
Knowing this word does not establish what amount someone needs,
whether a supplement is appropriate, or that more is better.
The examples teach vocabulary, not dietary or treatment advice.
