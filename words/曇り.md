# 曇り (くもり) — cloudy weather

## Meaning

曇り is cloudiness or cloudy weather. It is a noun, so use 曇りです to describe the weather and 曇りの before another noun.

- 明日は曇りです。 (あしたはくもりです。) — Tomorrow will be cloudy.
- 曇りの日も散歩します。 (くもりのひもさんぽします。) — I take walks on cloudy days too.

## How the kanji combine

曇 carries the clouding meaning. 曇り is related to the native verb 曇る (くもる), "become cloudy; cloud over." The final り is part of the noun; it does not make this an i-adjective.
The spelling 曇, without the final kana, is also recorded for the same noun and reading くもり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[晴れ](晴れ.md) (はれ), "clear weather," provides the useful weather contrast. [雨](雨.md) (あめ) means rain; cloudy weather does not necessarily involve rainfall.

## Distinctive meaning

A cloud itself is [雲](雲.md) (くも), not 曇り. The related expression 曇っています (くもっています) also describes cloudy conditions. Outside weather, 曇り can refer to fogging or a lack of clarity, such as on a mirror; this article's main sense is the weather report noun.

- 鏡の曇りを拭き取りました。 (かがみのくもりをふきとりました。) — I wiped the mist off the mirror.

This surface-fogging use is different from [霧](霧.md) (きり), atmospheric fog.
