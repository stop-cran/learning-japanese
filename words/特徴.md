# 特徴 (とくちょう) - characteristic; distinguishing feature

## Meaning

特徴 is a noun for a characteristic or feature that helps describe or distinguish something. It can concern appearance, behavior, design, or another noticeable property. Use Xの特徴, "a feature of X," or Xには特徴がある, "X has a distinctive feature."

- この鳥の特徴は赤いくちばしです。 (このとりのとくちょうはあかいくちばしです。) - This bird's distinguishing feature is its red beak.
- 二つの製品の特徴を比べました。 (ふたつのせいひんのとくちょうをくらべました。) - I compared the features of the two products.

特徴的な (とくちょうてきな) is the adjective-like expression "characteristic/distinctive."

## How the kanji combine

[特](../kanji/特.md) contributes special or distinguishing. 徴 contributes a sign or indication. Together they identify a distinguishing sign or property, without saying whether that property is good.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[違い](違い.md) (ちがい) is a difference between things; 特徴 can describe one thing without an explicit comparison. [特別](特別.md) (とくべつ), "special," is an adjective-like word, not a noun meaning a feature.

## Distinctive meaning

A 特徴 is not necessarily an advantage or a unique property found nowhere else. A red beak may distinguish a bird in the comparison at hand even though other species also have red beaks. State the actual feature rather than assuming that "distinctive" means superior or universally unique.
