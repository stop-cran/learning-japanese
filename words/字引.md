# 字引 (じびき) - dictionary

## Meaning

字引 means a dictionary: a reference used to look up words or characters.
The spelling 字引き is also valid.

- 字引で言葉の意味を調べました。 (じびきでことばのいみをしらべました。) - I looked up the meaning of a word in a dictionary.

Here で identifies the reference used, while を marks the information being checked.

## How the kanji combine

[字](../kanji/字.md) means written character, and [引](../kanji/引.md) contributes
引き, from 引く (ひく), used for looking something up.
The reading mixes on ジ with native ひき, voiced to びき. Under this repository's
classification it is on-first `kango`, but it is not an all-on compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

辞書 (じしょ) and 辞典 (じてん) also mean dictionary; 辞書 is a useful neutral
choice in everyday study instructions. A textbook teaches material in sequence,
whereas a dictionary provides reference entries.

## Distinctive meaning

Despite 字, the word is not restricted to a kanji-only dictionary.
The whole compound names the reference work; it does not mean the individual
act of pulling a character. There is no direct antonym.
