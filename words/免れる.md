# 免れる (まぬがれる) — escape harm; avoid an unwelcome outcome

## Meaning

免れる means escaping something unwelcome, such as injury or disaster,
or avoiding a burden such as punishment or responsibility. The unwelcome
event or responsibility takes を: **けがを免れる**, "escape injury,"
and **責任を免れる** (せきにんをまぬがれる), "evade responsibility."

- 幸い、けがを免れました。
  (さいわい、けがをまぬがれました。) — Fortunately, I escaped injury.
- 責任を免れることはできません。
  (せきにんをまぬがれることはできません。) — You cannot evade responsibility.

This is an ichidan verb: replace the final る to form 免れます
(まぬがれます), 免れた (まぬがれた), and 免れない (まぬがれない).
The alternative pronunciation **まぬかれる** is also valid, with forms
such as まぬかれます and まぬかれた. It is not a different written word.

## How the kanji combine

[免](../kanji/免.md) writes まぬが here, followed by れる as okurigana;
with the alternate pronunciation it writes まぬか. There is no second
kanji to combine. The ending れる is part of this verb, not a passive
conjugation added to a verb called まぬが.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[逃げる](逃げる.md) (にげる) can describe fleeing and also evading
responsibility. 免れる need not involve running or moving away.
[避ける](避ける.md) (さける) overlaps in avoiding an unwelcome situation;
the words are not unrestricted substitutes in every sentence.

[助かる](助かる.md) (たすかる) overlaps when someone survives or is spared
harm, but it also describes receiving practical help. [救う](救う.md)
(すくう) presents saving someone or something else; 免れる does not
itself identify a rescuer. Suffering the harm is a contextual contrast,
not a single fixed antonym for every use.

## Distinctive meaning

The injury example reports being spared harm; it does not say whether
that happened through deliberate action, another person's help, or luck.
Avoiding responsibility can instead be blameworthy. Do not infer either
physical escape or approval from the verb alone.

Dictionaries differ in classification: JMdict calls this verb transitive,
while Nikkoku calls it intransitive. Learn the attested **unwelcome event
を免れる** construction directly rather than changing を to match an
English gloss or treating one classification as universal.
