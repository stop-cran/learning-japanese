# 寝坊 (ねぼう) — oversleeping

## Meaning

寝坊 means sleeping late or oversleeping. Add する to express the action: 寝坊する, 寝坊した, 寝坊しない. The noun can also describe a late riser or sleepyhead.

- 今朝、寝坊して、朝ご飯を食べませんでした。 (けさ、ねぼうして、あさごはんをたべませんでした。) — I overslept this morning and did not eat breakfast.
- 明日は寝坊しないように、目覚ましをかけます。 (あしたはねぼうしないように、めざましをかけます。) — I will set an alarm so I do not oversleep tomorrow.

## How the kanji combine

[寝](../kanji/寝.md) contributes ね, related to 寝る, "sleep"; 坊 contributes ぼう. This is a mixed kun-plus-on formation, categorized here with native-first words rather than as an all-on compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

早起き (はやおき) is getting up early, a useful contrast. 遅刻 (ちこく) is being late for an appointment or event. Oversleeping can cause lateness, but they are not the same event.

## Distinctive meaning

寝坊 concerns waking or getting up late, not going to bed late. 夜更かし (よふかし) is staying up late. Nor does 寝坊 mean merely feeling sleepy: 眠い (ねむい) describes that sensation.
