# 西洋 (せいよう) - the Western world

## Meaning

西洋 means the West or Western countries, especially Europe and the Western cultural sphere. It is a broad cultural and historical label rather than the name of one country.

大学で西洋の歴史を勉強しています。 (だいがくでせいようのれきしをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying Western history at university.

西洋美術 (せいようびじゅつ) means "Western art."

## How the kanji combine

[西](../kanji/西.md), "west," combines with [洋](../kanji/洋.md), "ocean; Western." Their on readings form せいよう. In this word, the combination identifies a cultural-geographical sphere, not simply an ocean to the west.

## Synonyms and antonyms

欧米 (おうべい) refers to Europe and America. 東洋 (とうよう), "the East," is the traditional contrasting label. 西 (にし) is the compass direction west.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 西洋の before a noun, as in 西洋の文化 (せいようのぶんか), "Western culture." The category's boundaries depend on context, and it should not imply that all Western societies share one culture. For a specific country or region, its name is clearer.
