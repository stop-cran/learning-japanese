# 宛名 (あてな) - addressee; name and address

## Meaning

宛名 identifies the person or organization to whom something is addressed.
On an envelope, it can mean the recipient's name or the name-and-address information.
宛て名 is another dictionary spelling of the same word.

- 封筒に宛名を書いてください。 (ふうとうにあてなをかいてください。) - Please write the addressee's name and address on the envelope.
- 宛名の漢字を間違えました。 (あてなのかんじをまちがえました。) - I wrote a kanji in the recipient's name incorrectly.

## How the kanji combine

宛 contributes addressing or directing something to someone, and [名](../kanji/名.md)
means name. The native reading is あて + な, not an on-reading compound.
The name identifies the intended recipient.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[住所](住所.md) (じゅうしょ) is an address or residence, not specifically an addressee.
宛先 (あてさき) concerns the destination or recipient.
差出人 (さしだしにん), "sender," names the other side of the correspondence.

## Distinctive meaning

宛名 is not a general word for directions such as "turn left."
Nor does it mean only a street address: the recipient's name matters even when
several people receive mail at the same location.
