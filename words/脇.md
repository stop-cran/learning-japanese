# 脇 (わき) - side; underarm; beside

## Meaning

脇 is a noun for the side of the body or the area under the arm. It also names a position beside something, or a place off to the side.

- 本を脇に抱えて歩いていました。 (ほんをわきにかかえてあるいていました。) - I was walking with a book tucked under my arm.
- 道の脇に車を止めました。 (みちのわきにくるまをとめました。) - I parked the car at the side of the road.

Use **Nの脇に** to locate something beside N. 脇に置く (わきにおく) can mean "put to one side."

## How the kanji combine

脇 supplies the whole native noun わき. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide. The dictionary records other written forms, but the assigned spelling here is 脇.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[側](側.md) (そば), "close by; beside," overlaps with the proximity use of 脇.
[隣](隣.md) (となり) identifies a neighboring position or thing.
These are location comparisons, not the underarm sense.
[左](左.md) and [右](右.md) specify which side; 脇 alone does not.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun can shift from anatomy to relative position without losing its sideward focus. 道の脇 does not refer to the road's literal armpit. Conversely, in the book example the body context makes the underarm reading natural. English "side" is broad, so use the complete phrase to identify what position is intended.
