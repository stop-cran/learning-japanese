# 鬼 (おに) - oni; ogre

## Meaning

鬼 is a noun for an oni, a demon or ogre in Japanese stories. In a chasing game, it also names the person who is "it."

- この昔話には鬼が出てきます。 (このむかしばなしにはおにがでてきます。) - An oni appears in this folktale.
- 次は私が鬼です。 (つぎはわたしがおにです。) - I'll be it next.

The second sentence makes sense during a game such as 鬼ごっこ (おにごっこ), tag. It is not a claim that the speaker is a supernatural creature.

## How the kanji combine

鬼 alone writes the native word おに. There is no compound to divide into smaller words. Keep this reading distinct from キ, an on reading used in other vocabulary; the game role taught here is おに.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[悪魔](悪魔.md) (あくま), "devil/demon," overlaps in broad English translation, but it does not name the person who is it in tag. お化け (おばけ) refers broadly to a ghost or frightening supernatural being, rather than identifying this particular creature.

## Distinctive meaning

鬼 can figuratively describe a fierce or merciless person. Calling someone 鬼のようだ (おにのようだ), "like an ogre," can be criticism, so do not use it as a neutral job description. A story character, a harsh person's behavior, and a game role depend on different contexts.
