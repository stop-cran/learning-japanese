# 換える (かえる) - exchange; interchange

## Meaning

換える is a transitive ichidan verb used for exchanging or replacing something. Use AをBに換える for exchanging A for B. Its forms include 換えます, 換えた, and 換えない.

- 円をドルに換えました。 (えんをドルにかえました。) - I exchanged yen for dollars.
- 窓を開けて、部屋の空気を換えましょう。 (まどをあけて、へやのくうきをかえましょう。) - Let's open the window and let fresh air into the room.

The air example illustrates renewal rather than a trade between two people.

## How the kanji combine

換 contributes exchange or interchange. It is read か in this native verb, followed by える. The character is a spelling choice within the broader かえる vocabulary, not a separate conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

JMdict groups 換える, [替える](替える.md), and 代える in one entry. It notes tendencies: 替える for replacement or change, 換える for exchange, and 代える for substituting a person. Compare 電池を替える (でんちをかえる), "replace the battery," and 選手を代える (せんしゅをかえる), "substitute a player."

## Distinctive meaning

These are spelling preferences, not absolute boundaries between unrelated verbs. [変える](変える.md) emphasizes changing a condition or content, as in a plan. [交換](交換.md) (こうかん) is the noun/する-verb counterpart useful for an exchange. All require attention to what is changed and what replaces it, rather than relying on the English word "change" alone.
