# かぎり — limit; scope

## Meaning

かぎり names a limit or extent. In grammatical expressions it can restrict a claim to a scope or
make it depend on a continuing condition.

- 私が知るかぎり、その店は日曜日も開いています。  
  (わたしが しるかぎり、その みせは にちようびも あいています。)  
  — As far as I know, that shop is open on Sundays too.
- 生きているかぎり、学び続けたいです。  
  (いきているかぎり、まなびつづけたいです。)  
  — I want to keep learning as long as I live.
- 予約しないかぎり入れません。 (よやくしないかぎり はいれません。) — You cannot get in unless you make a reservation.

These clauses use plain verb forms before かぎり, including the negative ない-form. For noun
scope, compare 私の知識のかぎりでは (わたしのちしきのかぎりでは), "within the limits of my knowledge."
For a condition expressed with a noun, 学生であるかぎり (がくせいであるかぎり) means "as long as one is a student."

## How the kanji combine

The word is also written 限り, from [限](../kanji/限.md), which supplies かぎ. It is related to
[限る](限る.md) かぎる, "limit; restrict." The kana spelling is useful in grammatical constructions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ほど](ほど.md) and [くらい](くらい.md) can concern extent, but do not replace かぎり in every
"as long as" or "unless" construction. [だけ](だけ.md) overlaps with limiting a range; it is not
the same grammatical pattern. There is no single antonym for all these uses.

## Distinctive meaning

予約しないかぎり入れない says that a reservation is necessary; it does not promise admission to
everyone who reserves. Also distinguish the suffix use 今日かぎり (きょうかぎり), "ending today"
or "today only," whose meaning depends on the surrounding statement.
