# 白 (しろ) — white color

## Meaning

白 is the noun "white," used for the color or a white item understood from context. Before another noun, 白の is possible; the adjective 白い (しろい) is also very common.

- 好きな色は白です。 (すきないろはしろです。) — My favorite color is white.
- 白のシャツを選びました。 (しろのシャツをえらびました。) — I chose a white shirt.

## How the kanji combine

[白](../kanji/白.md) has the native noun reading しろ. Adding written い gives the related adjective 白い. These are different grammatical forms, not alternative pronunciations of an unchanged word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

黒 (くろ), "black," is the corresponding color contrast. 白色 (はくしょく) is a more formal color term. 白い directly modifies a noun: 白い紙 (しろいかみ), "white paper."

## Distinctive meaning

With the noun, 白です means "it is white" and 白ではない (しろではない) means "it is not white." The adjective 白い instead has the negative 白くない (しろくない). The noun also has figurative senses, such as innocence, which should not be inferred whenever a literal white object appears.
