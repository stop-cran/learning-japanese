# 食卓 (しょくたく) - dining table

## Meaning

食卓 is a noun meaning "dining table": a table viewed as the place where a meal is served or eaten. Useful patterns include 食卓に並べる (しょくたくにならべる), "set out on the dining table," and 食卓を囲む (しょくたくをかこむ), "gather around the dining table."

- 食卓に皿を並べました。 (しょくたくにさらをならべました。) - I set out the plates on the dining table.
- 家族で食卓を囲みました。 (かぞくでしょくたくをかこみました。) - The family gathered around the dining table.

The second expression naturally presents people sharing a meal, not merely standing around furniture.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) means eating or food; 卓 means table here. 食 identifies the table's purpose, giving a modifier-plus-head relationship. The readings are ショク + タク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[テーブル](テーブル.md) is the broader loanword for a table. [机](机.md) (つくえ) usually identifies a desk or working surface.

[食事](食事.md) (しょくじ) names a meal or eating, not the table itself.

## Distinctive meaning

食卓 focuses on the meal setting. It can be useful when talking about family life or the food laid before diners, not just buying furniture. 食卓の上 (しょくたくのうえ) explicitly means "on top of the dining table." There is no natural opposite: other furniture serves a different purpose.
