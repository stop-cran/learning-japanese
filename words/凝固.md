# 凝固 (ぎょうこ) — coagulation; solidification

## Meaning

凝固 names coagulation or solidification. With する, it describes a material
congealing or becoming solid; the material takes が.

- 血液が凝固する。（けつえきがぎょうこする。）— Blood coagulates.

It also names a change to the solid state, including water becoming ice.
The English translation depends on the material: blood **coagulates**;
a liquid can **solidify**. Not every 凝固 is water freezing.

## How the kanji combine

[凝](../kanji/凝.md) contributes congealing and [固](../kanji/固.md) firmness
or hardening. Their related meanings reinforce the solidifying idea.
Read ギョウ + コ; learn the established scientific uses as whole-word meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[固まる](固まる.md)（かたまる）, "harden; solidify," overlaps in a material
becoming firm. [凍る](凍る.md)（こおる）, "freeze," is useful for water or a
surface freezing over. [冷凍](冷凍.md)（れいとう）concerns freezing or frozen
storage, rather than every kind of coagulation.

## Distinctive meaning

凝固 is useful in descriptions of materials and blood. It is not the term for
mental concentration: compare [集中](集中.md)（しゅうちゅう）.
The physical process is not necessarily a change to ice, and this vocabulary
explanation does not provide medical advice.
