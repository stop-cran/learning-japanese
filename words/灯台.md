# 灯台 (とうだい) - lighthouse

## Meaning

灯台 is a lighthouse: a structure with a light used to help ships navigate
or recognize a coast or hazard.

- 岬の上に白い灯台があります。
  (みさきのうえにしろいとうだいがあります。) - There is a white lighthouse on the cape.
- 船から灯台の光が見えました。
  (ふねからとうだいのひかりがみえました。) - We could see the lighthouse's light from the ship.

Use 灯台の光 for its light, or 灯台を目印にする (とうだいをめじるしにする)
for using the lighthouse as a landmark.

## How the kanji combine

[灯](../kanji/灯.md) contributes a lamp or light, and [台](../kanji/台.md)
a supporting stand or structure. The on readings are トウ + ダイ.
The established compound is a lighthouse, not every stand holding a lamp.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[塔](塔.md) (とう) is a tower; a tower need not serve as a lighthouse.
[電灯](電灯.md) (でんとう) names an electric light.
Neither describes the lighthouse's navigational function by itself.
There is no natural general antonym of lighthouse.

## Distinctive meaning

The familiar modern use concerns guiding ships. The dictionary also records
an old indoor oil-lamp fixture under this word; that historical object is not
what the coastal examples describe. 燈台 is an older kanji spelling of 灯台.
