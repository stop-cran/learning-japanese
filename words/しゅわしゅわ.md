# しゅわしゅわ — sparkling with fine bubbles

## Meaning

しゅわしゅわ describes the lively bubbles and light fizz of a carbonated drink.

- グラスに注ぐと、炭酸水がしゅわしゅわ泡立った。
  — When I poured it into the glass, the sparkling water fizzed.
- しゅわしゅわした飲み物を一口飲んだ。
  — I took a sip of a fizzy drink.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぶくぶく](ぶくぶく.md) suggests larger, more forceful bubbles or vigorous bubbling. しゅわしゅわ
is a fine, lively fizz rather than a thick or sticky texture.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records a light, fluffy texture for foods such as soufflé or whipped cream. The
examples here teach the carbonated-drink use; a still picture of bubbles alone does not
establish a sound.
