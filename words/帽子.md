# 帽子 (ぼうし)

## Meaning

帽子 is a hat or cap worn on the head.

- 外に出るときは帽子をかぶります。 (そとに でるときは ぼうしを かぶります。) — I put on a hat when I go outside.
- 部屋で帽子を脱ぎました。 (へやで ぼうしを ぬぎました。) — I took off my hat in the room.

Use かぶる for putting it on and かぶっている for wearing it. 脱ぐ (ぬぐ) means to take it off.

## How the kanji combine

[帽](../kanji/帽.md) concerns a head covering; [子](../kanji/子.md) is a Chinese-derived nominal ending in this established noun, not a literal child. 帽 already names a head covering, so the ending need not turn a non-noun into a noun. The on readings are ぼう + し. Compare the ending in [椅子](いす.md) (いす), "chair," where 子 is read す.

## Synonyms and antonyms

キャップ often names a brimmed casual cap. ヘルメット is a protective helmet, a related but more specific head covering. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

帽子 is broader than one particular hat shape. Clothing verbs depend on the item: use 帽子をかぶる rather than automatically extending 着る (きる), used for much upper-body clothing, to everything worn.
