# 下町 (したまち) - low-lying urban district

## Meaning

下町 is a low-lying part of a city, often associated with commercial
or industrial neighborhoods. In Tokyo it also names the traditional
eastern-city area associated with places such as Asakusa and Nihonbashi.

- 下町の商店街を歩きました。 (したまちのしょうてんがいをあるきました。) - I walked through a shopping street in the city's traditional low-lying district.
- 東京の下町を訪ねたいです。 (とうきょうのしたまちをたずねたいです。) - I want to visit Tokyo's Shitamachi area.

下町の + noun describes something in or associated with such a district.
The Tokyo reference is a particular geographical use, not the only city
in which the general word can be used.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) contributes lower, and [町](../kanji/町.md)
contributes town or neighborhood.
The native readings した + まち give したまち.
The lower-ground association is more useful than mechanically translating
it as every English "downtown."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[町](町.md) (まち) is a broader town/neighborhood word.
[地区](地区.md) (ちく) names a district without specifying this
topography or urban character.
山の手 (やまのて), the higher-ground part of a city, is a useful
geographical contrast, especially in discussions of Tokyo.

## Distinctive meaning

下町 is not universally the city center, business district, or any old street.
"Downtown" may work in a particular translation, but it does not erase
the Japanese word's topographical and cultural associations.
Nor does the word certify that all buildings or businesses there are old.
