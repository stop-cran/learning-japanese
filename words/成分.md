# 成分 (せいぶん) - component; ingredient

## Meaning

成分 is a constituent, component or ingredient of something. It is useful for
what a substance contains, such as a food's nutrients or a medicine's ingredients.
Nの成分 identifies the components of N.

- 食品の成分を調べました。 (しょくひんのせいぶんをしらべました。) - I investigated the food's composition.
- この薬には同じ成分が含まれています。 (このくすりにはおなじせいぶんがふくまれています。) - This medicine contains the same ingredient.

成分表 (せいぶんひょう) is a table or listing of components.

## How the kanji combine

[成](../kanji/成.md) contributes making up or forming, and [分](../kanji/分.md)
contributes a part. Together they suggest parts that constitute a whole.
Read セイ + ブン, with ぶ rather than ふ in the second part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[材料](材料.md) (ざいりょう) and [原料](原料.md) (げんりょう) emphasize inputs
used to make something. 成分 emphasizes what it consists of; cooking ingredients
and the resulting food's chemical components need not be described at the same level.
[要素](要素.md) (ようそ) is an element or factor; [部分](部分.md) (ぶぶん) is a part more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

The substance examples are a learning focus, not an absolute limit.
Japanese dictionaries also use 成分 for sentence constituents and mathematical
vector components. Those technical contexts require their own definitions; they do
not make 成分 the default name for every physical spare part or piece of equipment.
