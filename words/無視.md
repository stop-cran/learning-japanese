# 無視 (むし) - ignoring; disregarding

## Meaning

無視 is a noun for disregard, and **無視する** is a transitive verb meaning "ignore." The person, warning, opinion, or requirement disregarded takes を.

- 大切な注意を無視しないでください。 (たいせつなちゅういをむししないでください。) - Please do not ignore the important warning.
- 彼は私の質問を無視しました。 (かれはわたしのしつもんをむししました。) - He ignored my question.

**無視される** (むしされる) is the passive, "be ignored": 意見が無視された, "the opinion was disregarded."

## How the kanji combine

無 contributes absence or negation, and 視 contributes looking at or regarding. Together, ム + シ suggests treating something as not worth attention. This is a meaning association, not a claim that ignoring requires literally looking away.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[気付く](気付く.md) (きづく) means noticing. Not noticing something is different from disregarding it, though the visible result can look similar. [認める](認める.md) (みとめる), "acknowledge; recognize," can provide a contrast when discussing whether someone's opinion receives consideration.

## Distinctive meaning

Ignoring is not identical to disagreeing. Someone may consider a proposal and reject it without 無視する. Conversely, a person can disregard a rule while knowing it exists. When describing another person's behavior, the word can imply a judgment about their attention; a missed message alone may not establish deliberate disregard.
