# ノート (ノート) — notebook

## Meaning

ノート commonly means a notebook or exercise book for writing in. It can also refer to notes taken during a lesson.

ノートに名前を書いてください。（ノートになまえをかいてください。） — Please write your name in the notebook.

授業でノートを取ります。（じゅぎょうでノートをとります。） — I take notes in class.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word is related to English "note"; the notebook sense is a shortening of ノートブック. The mark ー lengthens ノ. There are no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

メモ usually means a brief note or reminder. 手帳（てちょう） is a pocket notebook or planner. These overlap in use but are not all the same object.

## Distinctive meaning

ノートに書く uses に for the surface or place where you write. In the class example above, ノートを取る means "take notes." In a different context, the same words could describe physically taking a notebook.
