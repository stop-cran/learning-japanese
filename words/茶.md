# 茶 (ちゃ) - tea

## Meaning

茶 means tea. It can name the drink or the tea plant, depending on context. The familiar everyday form is [お茶](お茶.md) (おちゃ), with お.

- 食後に温かい茶を飲みました。 (しょくごにあたたかいちゃをのみました。) - I drank warm tea after the meal.
- この地域では茶を栽培しています。 (このちいきではちゃをさいばいしています。) - Tea is cultivated in this region.

飲む (のむ) selects the drink meaning, while 栽培する (さいばいする), "cultivate," selects the plant. 茶を入れる (ちゃをいれる) can mean "make tea."

## How the kanji combine

The single [茶](../kanji/茶.md) uses the on reading チャ in this word. There is no compound to split. Other words may use サ, as in 茶道 (さどう), the way of tea; learn those readings with their words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[紅茶](紅茶.md) (こうちゃ) specifically means black tea. 茶 is broader and should not be equated with that one variety. Tea and other drinks contrast as choices, not as antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

茶 also appears as a short form for the brown color, as in 茶の服 (ちゃのふく), "brown clothes." [茶色](茶色.md) (ちゃいろ) makes that color meaning explicit. Do not interpret every occurrence as a beverage, or assume that adding お changes tea into a different substance.
