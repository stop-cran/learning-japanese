# 曖昧 (あいまい) - vague; ambiguous

## Meaning

曖昧 describes something unclear, insufficiently definite, or open to different interpretations.
It is a な-adjective: 曖昧な返事 (あいまいなへんじ), "a vague answer,"
and 曖昧に答える (あいまいにこたえる), "answer vaguely."

- 彼は曖昧な返事をしました。 (かれはあいまいなへんじをしました。) - He gave a vague answer.
- その説明では、二つの違いが曖昧です。 (そのせつめいでは、ふたつのちがいがあいまいです。) - That explanation leaves the difference between the two unclear.

## How the kanji combine

曖 has a lack-of-clarity association, and 昧 a darkness association. The near-synonymous
components help connect the compound with something not made clear. The on readings
アイ + マイ give あいまい; this meaning-based aid is not a historical origin claim.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぼんやり](ぼんやり.md) overlaps in indistinctness but also describes absentmindedness.
[はっきり](はっきり.md), "clearly," provides a useful contrast:
はっきり答える (はっきりこたえる), "answer clearly."

## Distinctive meaning

Unclear wording need not be deliberately deceptive: uncertainty or an incomplete explanation
can also make an answer 曖昧. The dictionary separately records a shady or disreputable sense;
the examples above teach lack of clarity, not a rule that every vague speaker is dishonest.
