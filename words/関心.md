# 関心 (かんしん) - interest; concern

## Meaning

関心 is interest in or concern about a subject. It is a noun, used in
Nに関心がある, "be interested in N," and Nに関心を持つ, "take an interest in N."
The topic takes に.

- 日本の歴史に関心があります。
  (にほんのれきしにかんしんがあります。) - I am interested in Japanese history.
- 多くの人が環境問題に関心を持っています。
  (おおくのひとがかんきょうもんだいにかんしんをもっています。)
  - Many people take an interest in environmental issues.

関心を示す (かんしんをしめす) means to show interest.
Use these noun constructions rather than treating 関心 as a regular する verb.

## How the kanji combine

[関](../kanji/関.md) contributes involvement or connection, and [心](../kanji/心.md)
contributes the mind or heart. The compound concerns the mind's engagement with a subject.
The on readings are カン + シン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[興味](興味.md) (きょうみ) overlaps strongly in "interest," often highlighting curiosity
or attraction. [心配](心配.md) (しんぱい) is worry; 関心 need not involve anxiety.
無関心 (むかんしん), "indifference," supplies the opposite idea.

## Distinctive meaning

English "concern" can mean either attention or worry. 関心 is useful when an issue matters
to someone or receives their attention, without necessarily saying they are afraid of it.
The history example expresses intellectual interest, not concern that history is dangerous.
