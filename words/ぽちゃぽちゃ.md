# ぽちゃぽちゃ — splashing lightly in water

## Meaning

ぽちゃぽちゃ describes repeated small splashes in water.

- 子どもが浅い水たまりでぽちゃぽちゃ遊んでいる。
  — A child is playing with little splashes in a shallow puddle.
- 小魚が水面をぽちゃぽちゃ跳ねた。
  — Small fish made little splashes as they jumped at the surface.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぽちゃぽちゃ suggests light, small splashes; it is gentler than vigorous splashing.

## Distinctive meaning

The selected sense is water movement. The dictionary also records a separate use for a plump appearance.
