# 赤 (あか) — red

## Meaning

赤 is the noun naming the color red. It can stand alone or modify a noun with の: 赤のペン (あかのペン), "a red pen."

好きな色は赤です。 (すきないろはあかです。) — My favorite color is red.

## How the kanji combine

[赤](../kanji/赤.md) uses its native reading あか. There is no compound to divide. The related adjective 赤い (あかい) adds い and behaves differently grammatically.

## Synonyms and antonyms

赤い means "red" before a noun or as a description: 赤い花 (あかいはな), "a red flower." 白 (しろ), "white," and 青 (あお), "blue," are other color names, not strict opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose 赤 when naming or selecting the color itself: 赤にします (あかにします), "I'll choose red." Use 赤い for an adjective that conjugates: 赤くない (あかくない), "not red." Do not form an adjective by adding な to 赤.
