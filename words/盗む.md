# 盗む (ぬすむ) — steal

## Meaning

盗む means to steal. The stolen thing takes を.
It is a godan verb with polite form 盗みます (ぬすみます) and past form 盗んだ (ぬすんだ).

- 犯人が財布を盗みました。 (はんにんがさいふをぬすみました。) — The culprit stole a wallet.
- 昨日、自転車を盗まれました。 (きのう、じてんしゃをぬすまれました。) — My bicycle was stolen yesterday.

The second example uses the passive 盗まれる and leaves the thief unstated. It reports the theft from the affected person's perspective.

## How the kanji combine

[盗](../kanji/盗.md) is read ぬす in 盗む; む is written in kana.
This is not a compound of separate word meanings.
The character's on reading トウ belongs to compounds such as 盗難 (とうなん), "theft."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[取る](取る.md) とる is broader: it can mean take or pick up and can also describe stealing in context.
盗む makes the theft meaning explicit.
[借りる](借りる.md) かりる means borrow or rent; it is not a softer synonym for stealing.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 盗む when the taking is characterized as theft, not for every transfer of an object.
For a report that your property was stolen, the passive example is useful even when you cannot identify who did it.
The article focuses on literal theft; figurative expressions using 盗む need to be learned in their own contexts.
