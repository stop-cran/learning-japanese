# 分 (ぶ) - fractional unit; portion

## Meaning

This page distinguishes two dictionary identities: **ぶ**, a fractional or traditional unit, and **ぶん**, a portion or amount. They are not freely interchangeable readings in the examples.

For **ぶ**, the value depends on the scale. In body temperature, one 分 is 0.1 degree. In the 割・分 system for proportions, one 分 is 1%, one tenth of a 割 (わり). Thus 一割五分 (いちわりごぶ) is 15%.

- 熱は三十七度五分あります。 (ねつはさんじゅうななどごぶあります。) - My temperature is 37.5 degrees.

As a traditional length unit, one 分 is one tenth of a 寸 (すん), approximately 3 mm.

For **ぶん**, use Nの分 for someone's portion and amount + 分 for an amount's worth.

- これは私の分です。 (これはわたしのぶんです。) - This is my share.
- 二日分の食料を用意しました。 (ふつかぶんのしょくりょうをよういしました。) - I prepared two days' worth of food.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) concerns division or portions. This single character has the on readings ブ and ブン used for the distinct functions above. Its component meaning does not make either noun a verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

部分 (ぶぶん) names a part or section, overlapping with some ぶん uses. [分ける](分ける.md) (わける) is the verb "divide; distribute." Do not turn 分 into an unrestricted 分する meaning "divide."

## Distinctive meaning

五分 read ごぶ needs a scale; it is not automatically five percent in every context. 私の分 read わたしのぶん needs no measurement scale: it identifies a share. Preserve both the reading and the construction when interpreting the same written character.
