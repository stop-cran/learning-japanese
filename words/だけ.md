# だけ — only; as much as

## Meaning

だけ can limit something to a stated range, "only," or express an extent, "as much as."
The surrounding phrase determines which use is intended.

- 一つだけ選んでください。 (ひとつだけ えらんでください。) — Please choose just one.
- 好きなだけ食べてください。 (すきなだけ たべてください。) — Please eat as much as you like.

In the first example, だけ follows the counted amount directly. In the second, 好き is a
na-adjective, so it takes な before だけ. Plain verbs also attach directly:
できるだけ, "as much as possible."

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is usual. JMdict records 丈 read だけ for the particle, separately from the noun
丈 (たけ), "height; length." Do not infer a pronunciation from the character alone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ばかり](ばかり.md) overlaps with "only" but can emphasize concentration or repetition.
しか also limits a range, but normally requires a negative predicate:
一つしかない (ひとつしかない), "there is only one." It is not a drop-in replacement in
the affirmative 一つだけある (ひとつだけある), "there is just one."

## Distinctive meaning

だけ does not inherently require a negative sentence. It also need not mean a small amount:
好きなだけ can allow a large amount. Learn these uses separately from the additional meanings
of longer constructions such as だけに.

For だけ compared with ほど, くらい, and ばかり, see
[the particle-neighbor section](../articles/koto-wake-and-formal-nouns.md#particle-neighbors-ほど-くらいぐらい-だけ-and-ばかり).
