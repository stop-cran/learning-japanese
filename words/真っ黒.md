# 真っ黒 (まっくろ) - jet-black; deeply tanned; black with dirt

## Meaning

真っ黒 describes intense blackness. It can describe a black object,
deeply tanned skin, or something blackened by dirt or soot.

- その猫の毛は真っ黒です。 (そのねこのけはまっくろです。) - That cat's fur is jet-black.
- 外で遊んで、顔が真っ黒になりました。 (そとであそんで、かおがまっくろになりました。) - Playing outside left my face deeply tanned.
- 煙で壁が真っ黒になりました。 (けむりでかべがまっくろになりました。) - The smoke blackened the wall.

な can connect it to a noun: 真っ黒な服 (まっくろなふく), jet-black clothes.

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md) contributes the intensifying まっ, and
[黒](../kanji/黒.md) supplies くろ, black.
This is not the on-reading combination シン + コク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[黒い](黒い.md) (くろい) also describes blackness and tanning.
[真っ暗](真っ暗.md) (まっくら) foregrounds darkness, but the words can
overlap rather than forming an absolute color-versus-light partition.
[真っ白](真っ白.md) (まっしろ), pure white, contrasts in color.

## Distinctive meaning

The object and cause identify the use. A person's natural skin color is
not by itself evidence that this tanning expression is appropriate.
Likewise, a black garment need not be dirty: the dirt sense does not
apply automatically to every black object.
