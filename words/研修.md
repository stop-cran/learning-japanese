# 研修 (けんしゅう) - in-service training

## Meaning

研修 is training or study to develop knowledge and skills, especially
workplace, professional, or induction training. It is a noun and can form
研修する. The trainee need not already be an experienced employee.

- 新入社員は一週間の研修を受けます。 (しんにゅうしゃいんはいっしゅうかんのけんしゅうをうけます。) - New employees receive a week of training.
- 海外の病院で研修しました。 (かいがいのびょういんでけんしゅうしました。) - I trained at a hospital overseas.

研修を受ける means "receive training"; 研修に参加する
(けんしゅうにさんかする) means "participate in training."
JMdict also permits transitive 研修する; an object can identify the
knowledge or skills studied.

## How the kanji combine

[研](../kanji/研.md) contributes study or polishing, and 修 contributes
study or mastering. Their related learning associations reinforce one another.
The on readings are ケン + シュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[訓練](訓練.md) (くんれん) emphasizes training;
[練習](練習.md) (れんしゅう) emphasizes practice.
[稽古](稽古.md) (けいこ) is useful for practice in arts or other skills.
[学習](学習.md) (がくしゅう) and [勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) are
broader study words. These tendencies overlap rather than forming exclusive categories.

## Distinctive meaning

The title highlights a useful context, not an employees-only restriction.
A 研修 may involve observation, lectures, and practical work.
The noun does not guarantee a qualification or say who pays for the training.
