# 投書 (とうしょ) - letter to the editor; written complaint

## Meaning

投書 is a letter submitted to a newspaper, magazine or organization, often
expressing an opinion or complaint. It also names contributing such writing.
Use 投書する, with に for the recipient.

- 新聞に交通問題について投書しました。
  (しんぶんにこうつうもんだいについてとうしょしました。) - I wrote to the newspaper about traffic problems.
- 市役所に住民から投書が届きました。
  (しやくしょにじゅうみんからとうしょがとどきました。) - A letter from a resident reached city hall.

The noun can refer to the submitted text as well as the act.
Not every 投書 is a complaint; a reader may offer a suggestion or opinion.

## How the kanji combine

[投](../kanji/投.md) contributes submitting or sending in, and
[書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing: writing sent to a recipient.
The on readings are トウ + ショ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[手紙](手紙.md) (てがみ) is the broader word for a letter.
投稿 (とうこう) also concerns submitting material for publication or posting it.
[苦情](苦情.md) (くじょう) is a complaint, which need not be written.

## Distinctive meaning

The English "contribution" here concerns submitted writing, not donating money
or every form of [貢献](貢献.md) (こうけん), contribution to an achievement.
A personal letter to a friend is not normally called 投書 merely because it is posted.
