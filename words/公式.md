# 公式 (こうしき) - official; formula

## Meaning

公式 has two useful meanings: official or formally established status,
and a formula, especially in mathematics.
The official sense modifies nouns with の or in compounds:
公式の発表 (こうしきのはっぴょう), "an official announcement."

- 公式の発表を待っています。 (こうしきのはっぴょうをまっています。) - We are waiting for an official announcement.
- この公式を使って面積を求めます。 (このこうしきをつかってめんせきをもとめます。) - We use this formula to find the area.

The formal sense also concerns publicly established forms or procedures.
English "formality" needs that context; it is not a general word for good manners.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md) contributes public or official, and
[式](../kanji/式.md) contributes a form, procedure, or formula.
The combination suits an established public form and an established formula.
The on readings are コウ + シキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[正式](正式.md) (せいしき) overlaps in official or formal status.
[形式](形式.md) (けいしき) concerns form or format, and [式](式.md)
(しき) can name a mathematical expression or formula.
非公式 (ひこうしき), "unofficial," contrasts with the official sense.

## Distinctive meaning

An official statement need not be a mathematical formula, and a formula need
not be issued by a government. Identify the noun and setting before choosing
an English meaning. Official standing is not simply a speaker's polite style.
