# 年寄り (としより) - elderly person

## Meaning

年寄り is a noun for an older or elderly person. It can also modify a noun with の. The expression お年寄り (おとしより) adds a courteous prefix, but age labels still need care in context.

- バスでお年寄りに席を譲りました。 (バスでおとしよりにせきをゆずりました。) - I gave up my seat to an elderly person on the bus.
- この施設はお年寄りの生活を支えています。 (このしせつはおとしよりのせいかつをささえています。) - This facility supports older people's daily lives.

These refer to people; they are not instructions to address an individual as 年寄り.

## How the kanji combine

[年](../kanji/年.md) contributes years or age; [寄](../kanji/寄.md) connects with 寄る, as in the expression 年が寄る, "advance in age." Learn the native compound としより as a whole. 年寄 and 年より are also recognized spellings; the source's 年寄 is not an error.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[老人](老人.md) (ろうじん) is another age-related noun. 高齢者 (こうれいしゃ) is useful in administrative and service contexts. 若者 (わかもの), "young person," provides an age contrast, not an assessment of worth or ability.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict marks the old-person sense as sensitive. Neither dictionary inclusion nor adding お guarantees that the label will be welcome. Use a person's name or an appropriate form of address when speaking to them, and mention age when it is relevant rather than assuming a fixed personality or ability.
