# 法律 (ほうりつ) — law; legislation

## Meaning

法律 means law or legislation: legally established rules, or a particular law understood from context. It can also name law as a subject of study.

大学で法律を勉強しています。 (だいがくでほうりつをべんきょうしています。) — I am studying law at university.

## How the kanji combine

[法](../kanji/法.md) and 律 both contribute rule or law meanings. The on readings ほう + りつ form ほうりつ. Preserve the long vowel in ほう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

規則 (きそく) means rules or regulations, including those of a school or workplace. ルール is a broader everyday loanword. 法律 specifically concerns law rather than every informal rule.

## Distinctive meaning

法律を守る (ほうりつをまもる) means obeying the law; 法律に違反する (ほうりつにいはんする) means violating it. Note the different particles. The word itself does not mean a lawyer: that profession is 弁護士 (べんごし).
