# 何人 (なんにん) — how many people

## Meaning

何人 asks for a number of people: 何人家族ですか (How many are in your family?), 何人来ますか (How many people are coming?).
The same spelling is read **なにじん** and means "what nationality": あの人は何人ですか is "What nationality is that person?" The reading decides the meaning.

## How the kanji combine

[何](../kanji/何.md) (what, how many) plus [人](../kanji/人.md) (person, here the counter 人 にん); an on + kun mix (なん from the kun reading of 何, にん on). 何 becomes なん before a counter. The answers are 一人 ひとり, 二人 ふたり, then 三人 さんにん, 四人 よにん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 何人 vs 誰 だれ (who) and 何名 なんめい (how many people, polite, for reservations).
- Formal counterpart in restaurants: 何名様 なんめいさま.

## Distinctive meaning

なんにん is regular and common. For nationality, 何人 なにじん is rarely asked bluntly; どこの国の方ですか is more polite.
