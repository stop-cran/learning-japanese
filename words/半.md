# 半 (はん) — half; half past

## Meaning

半 means **half** and occurs in established quantity expressions. After a clock hour it means **half past** that hour.

- 授業は九時半に始まります。 (じゅぎょうはくじはんにはじまります。) — Class starts at half past nine.
- 一時間半待ちました。 (いちじかんはんまちました。) — I waited an hour and a half.

九時半 is 9:30; 一時間半 is a duration of one and a half hours.

## How the kanji combine

[半](../kanji/半.md) has the on reading ハン. As a prefix it appears in 半年 (はんとし), “half a year,” with native とし. As a following element, it adds a half in expressions such as 一年半 (いちねんはん), “a year and a half.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

半分 (はんぶん) is the ordinary noun “half,” useful for half of a cake or another object. 全部 (ぜんぶ), “all,” contrasts with a partial amount.

## Distinctive meaning

半 is not freely substituted for 半分 everywhere. Learn the attachment in common time and quantity expressions. In particular, 九時半 names a clock time, not nine and a half hours of elapsed time.
