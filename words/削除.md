# 削除 (さくじょ) - deletion; erasure

## Meaning

削除 is deletion or elimination, especially removing writing, entries, or data.
削除する is transitive: the item removed takes を.

- 重複した行を削除しました。 (ちょうふくしたぎょうをさくじょしました。) - I deleted the duplicate line.
- このファイルを削除してもいいですか。 (このファイルをさくじょしてもいいですか。) - May I delete this file?

削除されたファイル (さくじょされたファイル) is "a deleted file"; された is passive.

## How the kanji combine

削 contributes shaving or cutting away, and [除](../kanji/除.md) contributes removal or exclusion.
The related meanings reinforce taking something out. The on readings are サク + ジョ, with a small ょ.
Deleting a line is not a claim that paper was physically shaved.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[消す](消す.md) (けす) also covers erasing or deleting, as well as turning off lights.
[除く](除く.md) (のぞく) is broader removal or exclusion.
追加 (ついか), "addition," supplies a practical contrast when editing a list.

## Distinctive meaning

The legacy source's "cancellation" should not make 削除する a general verb for canceling an event.
[中止](中止.md) (ちゅうし) concerns calling off or discontinuing an activity.
Deleting a calendar entry and canceling the event itself are different actions.
The word alone also does not promise that deleted data can be recovered.
