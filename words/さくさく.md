# さくさく — lightly crisp; smoothly efficient

## Meaning

A light, dry crunch, often in pastry or cookies; also the crunch of walking on snow or similar material. Figuratively, work can proceed smoothly and efficiently.

- このクッキーはさくさくしている。 — These cookies have a light, crisp texture.
- 新雪（しんせつ）の上をさくさくと歩いた。 — I walked over the fresh snow with a crunch.
- 新しいパソコンでは、作業（さぎょう）がさくさく進む（すすむ）。 — Work goes smoothly on the new computer.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[かりかり](かりかり.md) often gives a harder crisp impression. [ぱりぱり](ぱりぱり.md) often describes thin, brittle layers. [しっとり](しっとり.md) describes moisture, which can be desirable in a different kind of cake.

## Distinctive meaning

さくさくしたパイ, さくさくと歩く, and さくさく進む have different subjects and verbs. The efficient-work sense is not literal chewing. JMdict also records archaic liquid-pouring and clear-speaking senses; this card prioritizes common learner uses without suggesting that they exhaust the entry.
