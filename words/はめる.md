# はめる - fit into; put on; entrap

## Meaning

はめる means to fit or insert something into a place, or put on something
such as a ring or gloves. It is a transitive ichidan verb: what is fitted
takes を, and the place it fits into can take に.

- 枠にガラスをはめました。 (わくにガラスをはめました。) - I fitted the glass into the frame.
- 指に指輪をはめています。 (ゆびにゆびわをはめています。) - I am wearing a ring on my finger.

Forms include はめます, はめて, and はめない.
Extended uses include forcing something into a fixed mold, throwing it
into something, and trapping or deceiving someone. 罠にはめる
(わなにはめる) means "entrap."

## How the kanji combine

This verb is usually written in kana. 嵌める is a dictionary spelling,
but it is not necessary to write the unfamiliar kanji in ordinary use.
The kana-primary article has no written-kanji metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入れる](入れる.md) (いれる) is broader "put in."
[挿す](挿す.md) (さす) often concerns inserting a slender object.
[着る](着る.md) (きる) is useful for putting on a shirt or coat;
English "put on" does not make はめる the verb for every garment.
外す (はずす), "remove; take off," contrasts with fitting or fastening
in an appropriate setting.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "make love" gloss requires a register warning.
JMdict also records a **vulgar, colloquial sexual use**, usually written
ハメる. It is not a neutral romantic synonym for "make love," and its
register must not be generalized to the everyday glass or ring examples.

The spelling or a shared English gloss alone does not identify the sense.
Learn the object and construction: fitting glass, wearing a ring, and
trapping a person are different uses of the same verb.
