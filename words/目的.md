# 目的 (もくてき) - purpose; objective

## Meaning

目的 is the purpose, aim, or intended objective of an action. It is a noun. Use **activity の目的** to ask or explain what the activity is meant to accomplish.

- この旅行の目的は何ですか。 (このりょこうのもくてきはなんですか。) - What is the purpose of this trip?
- この会の目的は、友達を作ることです。 (このかいのもくてきは、ともだちをつくることです。) - The purpose of this gathering is to make friends.

In the second example, こと turns the verb phrase into the noun-like complement required after 目的は.

## How the kanji combine

[目](../kanji/目.md) contributes an aim or focus, and 的 contributes a target. The two ideas reinforce the notion of an intended endpoint. The on readings are モク + テキ. 的 here is part of the noun, not a detachable adjective-making suffix.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[目標](目標.md) (もくひょう) also means a goal or target, often one used as a concrete benchmark. [意味](意味.md) (いみ) concerns meaning or significance. A reason for acting and the meaning of a word are not the same question.

## Distinctive meaning

Purpose and method differ: 目的 describes what you aim to accomplish; 手段 (しゅだん) describes the means. This is a conceptual contrast, not a pair of opposites. 目的を達成する (もくてきをたっせいする) means "achieve the objective."
