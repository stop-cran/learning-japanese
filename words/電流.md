# 電流 (でんりゅう) - electric current

## Meaning

電流 is electric current: the flow of electric charge.
Its magnitude is measured in amperes, A. It is a noun.

- この回路には電流が流れています。 (このかいろにはでんりゅうがながれています。) - Electric current is flowing through this circuit.
- 電流の大きさを測りました。 (でんりゅうのおおきさをはかりました。) - I measured the magnitude of the current.

電流が流れる (でんりゅうがながれる) is the ordinary pattern for current flowing.
を in 電流を測る marks what is measured.

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) contributes electricity and [流](../kanji/流.md) flow.
This modifier-plus-head compound identifies an electrical flow.
The on readings are デン + リュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[直流](直流.md) (ちょくりゅう), DC, and [交流](交流.md) (こうりゅう), AC,
are types of electric current distinguished by direction over time.
[電気](電気.md) (でんき) is broader "electricity."
[電力](電力.md) (でんりょく) is electric power, measured in watts, not amperes.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not interchange current, voltage, and power.
電圧 (でんあつ) is voltage; 電流 is charge flow, not the name of the potential difference driving it.
The vocabulary examples describe quantities, not instructions for handling an energized circuit.
