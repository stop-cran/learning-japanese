# マフラー - warm scarf; exhaust muffler

## Meaning

マフラー names a scarf worn for warmth. It also names a vehicle's
exhaust muffler or silencer.

- 寒いので、マフラーを巻きました。 (さむいので、マフラーをまきました。) - It was cold, so I wrapped a scarf around my neck.
- 車のマフラーを交換しました。 (くるまのマフラーをこうかんしました。) - I replaced the car's exhaust muffler.

The surrounding objects distinguish the garment from the vehicle part.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "muffler."
Keep the long final vowel, マフラー. There is no kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[スカーフ](スカーフ.md) also means scarf, often a lighter accessory.
Warmth and thickness are useful tendencies, not an absolute rule about
the wearer, season, or fabric. Neither clothing term is the opposite
of the other.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every マフラー as a neck accessory: a repair invoice
may concern the exhaust system. Conversely, "silencer" in the vehicle
sense does not automatically make this the word for any device that
reduces sound in any setting.
