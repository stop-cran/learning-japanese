# 証明 (しょうめい) - proof; verification

## Meaning

証明 is proof, demonstration, verification, or certification.
As a transitive する verb, it takes を for what is established.
For a complete claim, use **clause in noun-modifying form + ことを証明する**.
Before こと, plain verbs and i-adjectives attach directly.
Affirmative nonpast na-adjectives use な or である; noun predicates can use
である, as in 彼が学生であることを証明する
(かれががくせいであることをしょうめいする), "prove that he is a student."
学生のこと instead means matters concerning a student or students.
Past and negative predicates keep their endings: 学生だったこと,
学生ではないこと. See the [こと attachment guide](../articles/koto-wake-and-formal-nouns.md).

- この写真は、彼がそこにいたことを証明しています。
  (このしゃしんは、かれがそこにいたことをしょうめいしています。)
  - This photograph proves that he was there.
- 身分を証明する書類を見せてください。
  (みぶんをしょうめいするしょるいをみせてください。)
  - Please show a document proving your identity.

証明書 (しょうめいしょ) is a certificate or document of proof.
The surrounding words specify what it certifies.

## How the kanji combine

証 contributes evidence or attestation, and [明](../kanji/明.md) contributes making clear.
Their combination concerns establishing something clearly through support.
The on readings are ショウ + メイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[説明](説明.md) (せつめい) is explanation. An explanation may help someone understand
a claim without proving that it is true.
証拠 (しょうこ), "evidence," is material used in support; 証明 concerns the proof
or act of establishing the claim.

## Distinctive meaning

The word is not confined to mathematics: the identity-document example concerns
administrative verification. Conversely, merely saying 証明した, "proved it," does not
make an argument sound. Whether the offered evidence really proves a claim remains a separate judgment.
