# 濃度 (のうど) - concentration

## Meaning

濃度 is concentration: how much of a substance is present in a solution
or mixture relative to the whole. It is a noun.
濃度が高い (のうどがたかい) means high concentration;
濃度が低い (のうどがひくい) means low concentration.

- 食塩水の濃度を測りました。
  (しょくえんすいののうどをはかりました。) - I measured the concentration of the salt solution.
- 部屋の二酸化炭素の濃度が高くなっています。
  (へやのにさんかたんそののうどがたかくなっています。) - The carbon dioxide concentration in the room is rising.

The thing measured takes を.
The gas example shows that the word is not restricted to liquids;
the relevant units depend on what is measured.

## How the kanji combine

[濃](../kanji/濃.md) contributes concentration or intensity and
[度](../kanji/度.md) degree: the degree of concentration.
The on readings are ノウ + ド.
Compare the adjective [濃い](濃い.md) (こい), "concentrated; strong; dark."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[集中](集中.md) (しゅうちゅう) also has the English label "concentration,"
but concerns focusing or things gathering, not necessarily a chemical proportion.
密度 (みつど), "density," describes a different quantity in many scientific contexts.
薄める (うすめる), "dilute," can describe lowering a solution's concentration.

## Distinctive meaning

濃度 is not the general term for a person's concentration on a task,
nor is it the brightness of a light.
Concentration can be measured even when the mixture is colorless.
The dictionary's mathematical "cardinality" is a separate technical sense,
outside this article's practical chemical teaching.
