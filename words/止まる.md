# 止まる (とまる) — to stop, come to a halt

## Meaning

止まる means that something stops moving, operating, or continuing. It is intransitive: the thing that stops is normally marked with が.

- バスが止まりました。 (バスがとまりました。) — The bus stopped.
- 時計が止まっています。 (とけいがとまっています。) — The clock has stopped.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native verb with [止](../kanji/止.md) read と, followed by the okurigana まる. It is a godan verb: 止まります とまります, 止まって とまって, 止まらない とまらない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 止める とめる is the transitive partner: 車を止める くるまをとめる, "to stop the car," versus 車が止まる くるまがとまる, "the car stops."
- 停止する ていしする also means to stop or suspend operation, often in more formal or technical language.
- 動く うごく, "to move; operate," provides a useful contrast, though it is not the opposite in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

This article teaches とまる. The same spelling also has the separate reading とどまる, "to remain," which is not interchangeable with とまる in the examples above.
Do not confuse it with 泊まる とまる, "to stay overnight." 止まっています can describe the resulting stopped state, not an ongoing process of coming to a stop.

留まる is also a dictionary spelling of とまる, with [留](../kanji/留.md).
It can be used for perching or alighting:

- 小鳥が枝に留まっています。 (ことりがえだにとまっています。) — A small bird is perched on a branch.

This spelling is not an instruction to read every 留まる as とまる:
the separate [留まる](留まる.md) article teaches とどまる, remaining or staying.
The public dictionary also records things being fixed in place under とまる:

- 画鋲ではうまく留まらない。 (がびょうではうまくとまらない。) — It will not stay fastened properly with thumbtacks.

It also records 目に留まる (めにとまる), "catch one's eye."
Those broader uses are not all separately indexed in the pinned three-sense JMdict family.
