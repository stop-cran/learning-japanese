# アウト (アウト) - out in sport; unacceptable

## Meaning

アウト names an "out" in sports: a ball outside the line in tennis, for example, or an out in baseball. It also describes something as unacceptable or beyond an allowed limit.

- 今のボールはアウトです。 (いまのボールはアウトです。) - That ball was out.
- その発言はアウトだと思います。 (そのはつげんはアウトだとおもいます。) - I think that remark is unacceptable.

The first needs the game's context to identify the rule. The second is a judgment, not a statement about physical location. 野球で二つ目のアウト (やきゅうでふたつめのアウト) means "the second out in baseball."

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana and has no kanji decomposition. Pronounce the written sequence ア・ウ・ト rather than treating it as a Japanese verb stem.

## Synonyms and antonyms

セーフ contrasts with an out in baseball, while イン contrasts with an out-of-bounds ball. The appropriate contrast depends on the sport. [外](外.md) (そと), "outside," names a location and is not a general replacement for these sporting calls.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not transfer every English use of "out" into アウト. For example, ordinary "go outside" uses 外に出る (そとにでる). In the evaluative use, アウト can express strong disapproval or disqualification; it does not automatically name a formal rule or legal verdict.
