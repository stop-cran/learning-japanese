# 儲かる (もうかる) - be profitable; yield a profit

## Meaning

儲かる is an intransitive godan verb: something is profitable or yields
a profit. Useful forms are 儲かります (もうかります), 儲かって
(もうかって), and 儲からない (もうからない).

- この商売はよく儲かります。 (このしょうばいはよくもうかります。) - This business is very profitable.
- 売り上げが増えても、必ず儲かるとは限りません。 (うりあげがふえても、かならずもうかるとはかぎりません。) - Even if sales increase, you will not necessarily make a profit.

## How the kanji combine

儲 writes the native profit-related base, followed by かる.
Learn もうかる as a whole verb; this is not an on reading followed
by an independent word かる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[儲ける](儲ける.md) (もうける) is the transitive counterpart for
earning or gaining something. [利益](利益.md) (りえき) names profit
or benefit, and [有利](有利.md) (ゆうり) can describe profitable terms.
These overlaps do not give the words identical grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 商売が儲かる (しょうばいがもうかる), "the business is profitable,"
with お金を儲ける (おかねをもうける), "earn money."
Revenue or [収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう) is not automatically net profit.
The Japanese dictionary also recognizes an unexpected saving or advantage:
buying nothing and saving the expense can be described as 儲かった.
