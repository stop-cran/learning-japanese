# 首都 (しゅと) - national capital

## Meaning

首都 is a country's capital city. Use a country name with の to identify whose capital it is: フランスの首都, "the capital of France."

- パリはフランスの首都です。 (パリはフランスのしゅとです。) - Paris is the capital of France.
- その国の首都を地図で探しました。 (そのくにのしゅとをちずでさがしました。) - I looked for that country's capital on a map.

It is a noun, not a suffix to add automatically to a city's proper name.

## How the kanji combine

[首](../kanji/首.md) contributes the head or leading position, and [都](../kanji/都.md) contributes a city or capital. The first character specifies the city's leading political role. The on readings are シュ + ト.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[都市](都市.md) (とし) means a city in general. [市](市.md) (し) identifies a municipality or supplies the suffix in an official city name. Neither means "national capital" by itself. [地方](地方.md) (ちほう), "the regions; a locality," can contrast with the capital in context, but is not a precise antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

首都 concerns a city's political role, not merely its population or fame. A country's largest commercial city need not be its capital. English "capital" also refers to money and uppercase letters; those meanings are not covered by 首都.
