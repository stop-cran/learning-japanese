# 光線 (こうせん) - ray; beam of light

## Meaning

光線 is a ray or beam of light. It can also be translated simply as "light"
when the context concerns light traveling or shining in a direction.
It is a noun, not a verb meaning "shine."

- 窓から光線が差し込みました。 (まどからこうせんがさしこみました。) - A beam of light streamed in through the window.
- 光線が鏡で反射します。 (こうせんがかがみではんしゃします。) - The ray of light is reflected by the mirror.

## How the kanji combine

[光](../kanji/光.md) contributes light, and [線](../kanji/線.md)
contributes a line. Together they suggest light considered as a ray or line
of travel. This is a word-meaning aid, not a claim that every beam is one
infinitely thin physical line. The on readings are コウ + セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[光](光.md) (ひかり) is broader and can itself name a ray or beam.
[日光](日光.md) (にっこう) specifies sunlight.
[明かり](明かり.md) (あかり) concerns light or illumination and can also
name a lamp. The ranges overlap; the distinctions are not absolute barriers.

## Distinctive meaning

光線 does not specify sunlight, electricity, or a particular color.
Identify the source separately if it matters.
The English "beam" in [桁](桁.md) (けた) is a structural beam or girder,
not a 光線. Likewise, [軽い](軽い.md) (かるい), "light in weight,"
translates another meaning of English "light."
