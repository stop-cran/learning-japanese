# 農産物 (のうさんぶつ) - agricultural produce

## Meaning

農産物 means agricultural produce or products of farming.
It includes grains, vegetables, fruit and tea; the public dictionary
also includes livestock products. It is not restricted to fresh vegetables.

- この市場では地元の農産物を売っています。
  (このいちばではじもとののうさんぶつをうっています。) - This market sells local agricultural produce.
- 台風で農産物に被害が出ました。
  (たいふうでのうさんぶつにひがいがでました。) - The typhoon caused damage to agricultural produce.

The products take を with 売る, "sell."
農産物に被害が出る presents damage affecting the produce.

## How the kanji combine

[農](../kanji/農.md) contributes farming,
[産](../kanji/産.md) production and
[物](../kanji/物.md) things or goods.
農産 qualifies the products: things produced through agriculture.
The readings are ノウ + サン + ブツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作物](作物.md) (さくもつ), "crops; agricultural produce," overlaps,
especially for cultivated crops.
[収穫](収穫.md) (しゅうかく) can mean the harvest or what is harvested.
[製品](製品.md) (せいひん) generally concerns manufactured products;
not every product is agricultural.

## Distinctive meaning

The word identifies the products' agricultural origin, not whether they are
organic, pesticide-free, local or already for sale.
Those properties require further information.
Likewise, do not infer that all livestock products are excluded simply because
English "produce" often brings fruit and vegetables to mind.
