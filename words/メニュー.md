# メニュー - menu; planned program

## Meaning

メニュー is a noun for a food menu or the dishes planned or offered.
It also names a planned set of activities or services, and a computer menu.

- メニューを見せてください。 (メニューをみせてください。) - Please show me the menu.
- 今日の練習メニューを確認します。 (きょうのれんしゅうメニューをかくにんします。) - I will check today's training program.
- メニューから「保存」を選びます。 (メニューから「ほぞん」をえらびます。) - Select "Save" from the menu.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword, from French menu. The final ュ is small
and the last vowel is long: メニュー, not メニュ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[献立](献立.md) (こんだて) overlaps with a planned meal or menu.
[プログラム](プログラム.md) and [コース](コース.md) overlap with
particular organized activities or offerings. A training メニュー need
not be food, and not every broadcast [番組](番組.md) (ばんぐみ) is a メニュー.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not limit the word to the printed restaurant sheet. It can mean the
actual planned items or their contents, including exercises in a workout.
Conversely, the computing use is a list of available commands or choices,
not the computer program itself.
