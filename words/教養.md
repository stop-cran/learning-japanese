# 教養 (きょうよう) - culture; cultivated knowledge

## Meaning

教養 is a person's cultivated knowledge, cultural understanding, or refinement developed through learning and experience. It is a noun; English "culture" or "education" can be appropriate depending on context.

- 読書を通して教養を深めたいです。
  (どくしょをとおしてきょうようをふかめたいです。) - I want to deepen my cultural understanding through reading.
- 彼女は教養のある人です。
  (かのじょはきょうようのあるひとです。) - She is a cultured person.

教養がある and 教養のある both describe someone as having this cultivation. 教養を身に付ける (きょうようをみにつける) means to acquire it.

## How the kanji combine

[教](../kanji/教.md) contributes teaching or learning, and 養 nurturing or cultivation. Their related contributions suggest developing oneself through learning. The readings are キョウ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[文化](文化.md) (ぶんか) can describe a society's culture rather than one person's cultivation. [教育](教育.md) (きょういく) concerns education or teaching, and [知識](知識.md) (ちしき) knowledge more generally. 無教養 (むきょうよう) means uncultured or unrefined.

## Distinctive meaning

教養 is not simply the name of a diploma or university degree. Nor does knowing one isolated fact automatically establish broad cultural understanding. [常識](常識.md) (じょうしき), "common sense; common knowledge," brings shared everyday expectations into focus rather than exactly the same quality.
