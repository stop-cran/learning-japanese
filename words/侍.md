# 侍 (さむらい) — samurai

## Meaning

侍 is a historical word for a warrior, especially a military retainer serving a feudal lord.
JMdict also records a colloquial figurative sense for a person with a strong backbone or considerable talent.

- 物語に、主君を守る侍が登場します。 (ものがたりに、しゅくんをまもるさむらいがとうじょうします。) — A samurai who protects his lord appears in the story.
- その映画は、一人の侍の旅を描いています。 (そのえいがは、ひとりのさむらいのたびをえがいています。) — The film depicts the journey of a samurai.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji word. The character [侍](../kanji/侍.md) uses its kun reading さむらい here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

JMdict glosses [武士](武士.md) ぶし as “samurai; warrior” (entry 1583680); the historical sense of 侍 さむらい is a warrior, especially a military retainer of a daimyo in the Edo period.

## Distinctive meaning

The colloquial figurative sense describes a person, not a warrior from a historical period.
