# 洗濯 (せんたく) - laundry; washing clothes

## Meaning

洗濯 means washing clothes or doing laundry. As a noun it names the activity; 洗濯する is the verb.

- 日曜日に洗濯をします。 (にちようびにせんたくをします。) - I do the laundry on Sundays.
- このシャツは家で洗濯できます。 (このシャツはいえでせんたくできます。) - This shirt can be washed at home.

洗濯をする and 洗濯する are both useful patterns. Clothes being washed can take を.

## How the kanji combine

[洗](../kanji/洗.md), "wash," and 濯, "rinse; wash," combine as せん + たく. The two characters reinforce the washing idea.

## Synonyms and antonyms

洗う (あらう) is broader: it can refer to washing hands, dishes, or clothes. 掃除 (そうじ) is cleaning a room or similar space.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "washing" is too broad if used without context. Ordinary 洗濯 concerns laundry, not washing your face. 洗濯機 (せんたくき) is a washing machine; 洗濯物 (せんたくもの) is the laundry itself.
