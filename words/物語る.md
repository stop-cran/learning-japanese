# 物語る (ものがたる) - recount; tell of; indicate

## Meaning

物語る means to recount an event or experience.
It also describes facts, expressions, or evidence that tell of or indicate something.
It is a transitive godan verb: 物語ります, 物語って, 物語った.

- 祖父は若いころの経験を物語りました。 (そふはわかいころのけいけんをものがたりました。) - My grandfather recounted his experiences when he was young.
- この傷が事故の激しさを物語っています。 (このきずがじこのはげしさをものがたっています。) - This damage shows how severe the accident was.

を marks what is recounted or indicated.
In the second example, the damage is the subject: it reveals information
without literally speaking.

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md) contributes things or matters, and
[語](../kanji/語.md) contributes telling or relating.
The native combination is もの + かたる, voiced to がたる.
It is not an on-reading compound ending in ゴ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[語る](語る.md) (かたる) overlaps in narration and revealing information.
[話す](話す.md) (はなす) is a broader speaking/telling verb.
[示す](示す.md) (しめす) overlaps with showing or indicating;
[証明](証明.md) (しょうめい) emphasizes proof.

## Distinctive meaning

An expressive face or physical evidence can 物語る a state of affairs.
That does not mean the verb always establishes a formal proof.
Nor is it the default verb for every brief request to tell someone an address:
choose the construction and kind of information, not just English "tell."
