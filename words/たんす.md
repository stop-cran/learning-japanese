# たんす - chest of drawers

## Meaning

たんす is a chest or cabinet used for storing clothing and similar belongings.
"Chest of drawers" is a useful starting translation, although a たんす
can also include compartments with doors.

- 服をたんすにしまいました。
  (ふくをたんすにしまいました。) - I put the clothes away in the chest of drawers.
- 部屋の隅に古いたんすがあります。
  (へやのすみにふるいたんすがあります。) - There is an old chest in the corner of the room.

に marks the storage destination in the first example and location in the second.
The word names the furniture, not an individual drawer.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual; タンス and the kanji spelling 箪笥 are also recorded.
箪 and 笥 have container associations, with on readings タン + ス.
This kana-primary article has no written-kanji components in its metadata.
The older character form 簞 in 簞笥 is recognition information, not
a second preferred spelling to memorize.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[引き出し](引き出し.md) (ひきだし) is a drawer, which may be part of a たんす.
[棚](棚.md) (たな) is a shelf or rack.
[家具](家具.md) (かぐ) is the broader category, furniture.
These related items are not interchangeable names for the same structure.

## Distinctive meaning

たんす does not mean every storage space in a room.
An 押入れ (おしいれ) is a built-in closet, whereas たんす names a piece
of furniture. Check the actual object rather than assuming every English
"cabinet" requires this word.
