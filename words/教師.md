# 教師 (きょうし) — teacher; instructor

## Meaning

教師 names a teacher or instructor as a role or occupation.
Use it as a noun: 教師です (きょうしです), "is a teacher," or 教師になる (きょうしになる), "become a teacher."

- 母は高校の教師です。 (はははこうこうのきょうしです。) — My mother is a high-school teacher.
- 将来、教師になりたいです。 (しょうらい、きょうしになりたいです。) — I want to become a teacher in the future.

## How the kanji combine

[教](../kanji/教.md) contributes teaching, and [師](../kanji/師.md) contributes a teacher or skilled practitioner.
The first element specifies the person's activity; together they name someone who teaches.
The on readings are キョウ + シ, giving きょうし with a long first vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[先生](先生.md) (せんせい) can mean "teacher" and can also be a respectful title or form of address.
教師 identifies the role; when addressing a teacher, 先生 is the relevant title.
[生徒](生徒.md) (せいと), "pupil; student," names a different participant in the teaching relationship, not an antonym in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

Saying 私は教師です (わたしはきょうしです), "I am a teacher," identifies an occupation.
It is not the same act as addressing someone as 先生.
Keep the job label and the respectful address separate, even though English may translate both with "teacher."
