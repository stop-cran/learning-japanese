# 省く (はぶく) - omit; cut down on

## Meaning

省く means to omit or leave something out, or to save effort or expense by
cutting it down. It is a transitive godan verb: mark what is omitted or saved
with を.

- 時間がないので、詳しい説明は省きます。 (じかんがないので、くわしいせつめいははぶきます。) - Since there is no time, I will omit the detailed explanation.
- この道具を使うと、手間を省けます。 (このどうぐをつかうと、てまをはぶけます。) - Using this tool saves effort.

省きます (はぶきます) is polite nonpast; 省いて (はぶいて) is the て-form.
省ける (はぶける) is the potential form, "can omit/save."

## How the kanji combine

省 contributes omitting or economizing in this word. The native verb is
はぶく, not the on reading ショウ used in some compounds. The final く
changes with conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[除く](除く.md) (のぞく) overlaps in excluding or removing.
省略 (しょうりゃく) means omission or abbreviation and overlaps when leaving
part of an explanation out. Adding information is a contextual opposite,
not the meaning of one fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

省く often concerns reducing what a process requires, as in 手間を省く.
It does not automatically mean careless omission: the result may be an
efficient procedure. Conversely, omitting something does not prove that
it was unimportant; the surrounding explanation supplies the reason.
