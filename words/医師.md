# 医師 (いし) - physician; medical doctor

## Meaning

医師 is a noun meaning a medical doctor or physician. It identifies a profession, not someone who holds any kind of doctoral degree. Use 医師になる for becoming a doctor, or 医師に相談する for consulting one.

- 医師に薬の飲み方を聞きました。 (いしにくすりののみかたをききました。) - I asked the doctor how to take the medicine.
- 姉は病院で医師として働いています。 (あねはびょういんでいしとしてはたらいています。) - My older sister works as a doctor at a hospital.

として means "as" in the second example: it names the capacity in which she works.

## How the kanji combine

[医](../kanji/医.md) contributes medical treatment; [師](../kanji/師.md) identifies a skilled practitioner or professional. The first character specifies the field of the second. The on readings combine as イ + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[医者](医者.md) (いしゃ) also means a medical doctor. 医師 suits professional titles, notices, and descriptions of qualifications; 医者 is also useful in everyday conversation. These are overlapping terms, not different medical ranks. [歯医者](歯医者.md) (はいしゃ) refers to a dentist or dental practice.

## Distinctive meaning

医師 names the role when talking about someone. When addressing a doctor, [先生](先生.md) (せんせい) is a useful form of address; do not assume the job noun itself works like English "Doctor!" The contrast with 患者 (かんじゃ), "patient," is between roles, not lexical opposites.
