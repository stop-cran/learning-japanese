# 手頃 (てごろ) - handy; reasonably priced

## Meaning

手頃 describes something suitably sized, easy to handle, or appropriate for one's needs or means. With prices it often means reasonably priced or affordable. Use 手頃な + noun and 手頃だ as a predicate. 手ごろ is another spelling.

- 手頃な大きさのかばんを探しています。 (てごろなおおきさのかばんをさがしています。) - I am looking for a bag of a convenient size.
- この店では手頃な値段で食事ができます。 (このみせではてごろなねだんでしょくじができます。) - You can eat at this restaurant for a reasonable price.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md), て, evokes handling something. 頃 contributes the appropriate degree or extent in this word; learn the full native reading てごろ rather than substituting the time expression この頃 (このごろ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[便利](便利.md) describes convenience more generally. [適当](適当.md) and [適切](適切.md) can mean suitable. [安い](安い.md) means inexpensive; 手頃な値段 emphasizes fitting a budget or expectation, not simply being the lowest price.

## Distinctive meaning

What counts as 手頃 depends on the user and purpose. A handy-sized dictionary need not be pocket-sized, and a reasonably priced item need not be poor quality. The word can also describe a manageable job or other suitable choice; it is not restricted to things literally held in a hand.
