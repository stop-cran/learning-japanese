# 通行 (つうこう) - passage; traffic

## Meaning

通行 is the passage of people or vehicles along or through a place.
It is a noun and an intransitive する verb.
道を通行する means "travel along a road"; を marks the route, not an object being changed.

- 歩行者はこの道を通行できます。
  (ほこうしゃはこのみちをつうこうできます。) - Pedestrians can use this road.
- 工事のため、この橋は通行止めです。
  (こうじのため、このはしはつうこうどめです。)
  - This bridge is closed to traffic because of construction work.

通行止め (つうこうどめ) is a closure or prohibition on passage.
A notice may specify which vehicles or people it applies to.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes passing through, and [行](../kanji/行.md) contributes going.
The related motion meanings reinforce each other.
The on readings are ツウ + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通る](通る.md) (とおる) is the everyday verb for passing through or along a place.
[交通](交通.md) (こうつう) concerns traffic or transportation more broadly.
通行 focuses on the actual passage or its permission and restrictions.

## Distinctive meaning

通行 does not mean every English "passage": it is not a paragraph in a book.
It is especially useful on road signs and in access instructions.
Although the verb is intransitive, a route can still take を, just as it can with 通る.
