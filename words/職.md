# 職 (しょく) - job; employment; position

## Meaning

職 names employment, a job, or an occupational position. Learn it in combinations such as 職を探す (しょくをさがす), "look for work," and 職に就く (しょくにつく), "take up employment."

- 大学を卒業して、職に就きました。 (だいがくをそつぎょうして、しょくにつきました。) - After graduating from university, I took a job.
- 新しい職を探しています。 (あたらしいしょくをさがしています。) - I am looking for a new job.

It also appears as a suffix in terms for a kind of position, such as 管理職 (かんりしょく), "a managerial position; management staff."

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [職](../kanji/職.md) supplies the whole noun with the on reading ショク. It relates to one's occupation or assigned work; there is no second character to divide off in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) can describe work, a job, or a task. [職業](職業.md) (しょくぎょう) identifies an occupation or profession. 職 is particularly useful in expressions concerning obtaining, holding, or losing a position.

## Distinctive meaning

職 can also concern a trade or practical skill: 手に職をつける (てにしょくをつける) means "acquire a marketable skill." It is not a literal job placed in someone's hand. The surrounding construction tells you whether employment, a post, or occupational skill is in focus.
