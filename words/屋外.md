# 屋外 (おくがい) - outdoors; outside a building

## Meaning

屋外 means outdoors, outside a building. It is a location noun.
Use で for an activity and に with a location or destination construction.

- 屋外で運動しています。 (おくがいでうんどうしています。) - I exercise outdoors.
- この椅子は屋外に置いてください。 (このいすはおくがいにおいてください。) - Please put this chair outdoors.

屋外の活動 (おくがいのかつどう) means "outdoor activities."

## How the kanji combine

[屋](../kanji/屋.md), building or roof, identifies the boundary;
[外](../kanji/外.md) supplies outside.
The on readings オク + ガイ give おくがい.
This is not the native reading や of 屋 used in shop names.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[外](外.md) (そと) is broader: it can mean outside a box, a room,
or a social group as well as outdoors.
野外 (やがい) and 戸外 (こがい) overlap in outdoor settings.
屋内 (おくない), "indoors," is the direct contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

屋外 does not itself mean remote wilderness or require distance from buildings.
A space just outside a building can be 屋外.
It names the location, not an action of going outside;
particles and the verb supply that part of the sentence.
