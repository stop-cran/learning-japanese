# 天気予報 (てんきよほう) - weather forecast

## Meaning

天気予報 means a weather forecast, whether presented on television, in an app, or elsewhere.

- 出かける前に天気予報を見ます。 (でかけるまえにてんきよほうをみます。) - I check the weather forecast before going out.

明日の天気予報 (あしたのてんきよほう) is "tomorrow's weather forecast," normally a forecast for tomorrow. の connects the day with the forecast.

## How the kanji combine

[天](../kanji/天.md) and [気](../kanji/気.md) form 天気 (てんき), "weather." [予](../kanji/予.md), advance or beforehand, and [報](../kanji/報.md), report, form 予報 (よほう), "forecast." All four characters use on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

天気 is the weather itself. 予報 is broader than a weather forecast, although the context often makes weather understood. ニュース means news, which may include but is not limited to forecasts.

## Distinctive meaning

A forecast concerns expected conditions, not a guarantee. 天気予報を見る means looking at or checking the forecast; the verb 見る does not limit the information source to television.
