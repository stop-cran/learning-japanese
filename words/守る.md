# 守る (まもる) — protect; keep a promise

## Meaning

守る means to protect, guard, or defend. It also means keeping a promise or following rules.
It is **transitive**: the person, thing, or obligation being protected or kept can be marked with を.
It follows the **godan** conjugation pattern: its ending changes to give forms such as 守ります (まもります), 守って (まもって), and 守らない (まもらない).

- 子供を危険から守ります。 (こどもをきけんからまもります。) — I protect children from danger.
- 約束を守ってください。 (やくそくをまもってください。) — Please keep your promise.

In the first example, から marks what the children are protected from.

## How the kanji combine

[守](../kanji/守.md) supplies the protect-or-obey meaning, and る is the dictionary-form ending.
The native reading まもる belongs to this verb.
The meaning is not limited to physical guarding: a rule or promise can be what is kept intact.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[助ける](助ける.md) (たすける) means to help or rescue. It focuses on giving help or saving someone, while 守る presents the action as protection.
For a promise or rule, [破る](破る.md) (やぶる), "break; violate," provides the opposite action:
約束を破る (やくそくをやぶる), "break a promise."
That contrast does not make 破る an all-purpose opposite for every protective use.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose the English verb from the object: protect a child, defend a place, keep a promise, or obey a rule.
The particle を stays useful across these meanings.
Despite its final る, 守る does not conjugate like [食べる](食べる.md) (たべる), whose negative is 食べない (たべない).
For 守る, the negative is 守らない, not 守ない.
