# 卸す (おろす) - sell wholesale; grate; cut up fish

## Meaning

卸す has a commercial use, "sell wholesale," and cooking uses, "grate" vegetables or "cut up" a fish. It is a transitive godan verb; the goods or food take を.

- 問屋は商品を小売店に卸します。 (とんやはしょうひんをこうりてんにおろします。) - The wholesaler sells goods to retail shops.
- 大根を卸してください。 (だいこんをおろしてください。) - Please grate the daikon.
- 魚を三枚に卸しました。 (さかなをさんまいにおろしました。) - I filleted the fish into three sections.

In the last example, 三枚に names the result of the preparation; it does not mean selling three fish wholesale. Forms include 卸します (おろします), 卸した (おろした), and 卸さない (おろさない).

## How the kanji combine

卸 supplies おろ, and す is written in kana. The native verb inflects with its ending; do not replace the whole reading with the on reading シャ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[売る](売る.md) (うる) and [販売](販売.md) (はんばい) concern selling more broadly. [下ろす](下ろす.md) and [降ろす](降ろす.md), also おろす, cover lowering or unloading. The complete JMdict family for those spellings also records grating and cutting up fish; these cooking uses are not exclusive spelling compartments.

## Distinctive meaning

Wholesale selling, grating, and fish preparation must be distinguished from context. Recognition of 卸す does not make it the spelling for every action pronounced おろす, nor does it make the same-reading lowering spellings wrong for every cooking use. The English adjective "grated" corresponds to a prepared result, not the dictionary-form verb by itself.
