# サイン (サイン) - signature; signal

## Meaning

サイン means an autograph or signature, and also a sign, signal, or indication.
For signing, use 書類にサインする (しょるいにサインする), "sign a document."
For a signal, サインを出す (サインをだす) means "give a signal."

- ここにサインしてください。 - Please sign here.
- コーチが選手にサインを出しました。 (コーチがせんしゅにサインをだしました。) - The coach gave the player a signal.

There is also a **separate mathematical word** サイン meaning "sine," a trigonometric function.
For example, サイン三十度は二分の一です (サインさんじゅうどはにぶんのいちです),
"The sine of thirty degrees is one-half."

## How the kanji combine

The katakana spelling has three morae: サ・イ・ン.
The autograph/signal word and the mathematical sine word share this spelling and pronunciation,
but the dictionary treats them as separate lexical entries.
No kanji components distinguish them.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[署名](署名.md) (しょめい) overlaps with a written signature.
[合図](合図.md) (あいず) overlaps with a signal.
Neither comparison explains mathematical sine; that belongs with trigonometry rather than handwriting or communication.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the setting: documents and fans suggest signatures/autographs; coaching suggests signals; angles and formulas suggest sine.
サイン does not automatically mean every kind of physical English "sign," such as a shop's signboard.
The mathematical identity is not an additional sense of the autograph entry.
