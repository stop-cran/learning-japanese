# 不満 (ふまん) - dissatisfaction; discontent

## Meaning

不満 is a noun for dissatisfaction or a complaint. It also functions as a na-adjective.
Use Nに不満がある to say someone is dissatisfied with N.
不満です states dissatisfaction, and 不満な顔 describes a dissatisfied expression.

- 今の給料に不満があります。 (いまのきゅうりょうにふまんがあります。) - I am dissatisfied with my current pay.
- 彼は不満な顔をしていました。 (かれはふまんなかおをしていました。) - He looked dissatisfied.

不満を言う (ふまんをいう), "voice complaints," describes expressing dissatisfaction.
Having the feeling does not itself establish that someone has said anything about it.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) negates [満](../kanji/満.md), "full; satisfied."
The compound conveys not being satisfied, rather than a literal container that is not full.
The readings combine as フ + マン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[満足](満足.md) (まんぞく), "satisfaction," provides the direct contrast.
[不足](不足.md) (ふそく), "shortage; insufficiency," concerns something not being enough.
An objective shortage may cause 不満, but dissatisfaction can also arise from expectations or preferences.

## Distinctive meaning

The word centers on something failing to satisfy, not every kind of sadness or unhappiness.
The subject may be omitted when it is clearly the speaker, as in the first example.
不満がない means "have no complaints" or "not be dissatisfied"; it need not express enthusiastic praise.
This makes it useful in discussing work, service, and living conditions.
