# 月曜日 (げつようび) - Monday

## Meaning

月曜日 means Monday. Use it with に to locate an event, or with は when making Monday the topic.

- 月曜日は店が休みです。 (げつようびはみせがやすみです。) - The shop is closed on Mondays.

来週の月曜日 (らいしゅうのげつようび) means "next Monday." 月曜日の朝 (げつようびのあさ) is "Monday morning," with の connecting the two time nouns.

## How the kanji combine

[月](../kanji/月.md) is ゲツ, [曜](../kanji/曜.md) is ヨウ, and [日](../kanji/日.md) is び. This is an on-first compound with a final native reading, not an entirely on-read word. Do not read the first character つき here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

日曜日 (にちようび) is Sunday and 火曜日 (かようび) is Tuesday. 月曜 (げつよう) is a shortened form used in schedules and conversation.

## Distinctive meaning

The name does not itself mean "workday": holidays and individual schedules differ. The example describes the shop's schedule, not a rule that all shops close on Monday.
