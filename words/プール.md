# プール — swimming pool

## Meaning

プール is a swimming pool, including the pool at a school, gym, or public swimming facility.

夏休みにプールで泳ぎました。 (なつやすみにプールでおよぎました。) — I swam in a pool during summer vacation.

明日はプールに行きます。 (あしたはプールにいきます。) — I am going to the pool tomorrow.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word comes from English "pool." The long-vowel mark ー lengthens プ: write プール, not プル. There are no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

海 (うみ), "sea," and 川 (かわ), "river," can be other places to swim, but are natural bodies of water, not pools. 水泳 (すいえい) names swimming as an activity.

## Distinctive meaning

で marks the location of swimming, while に marks the destination in プールに行く. The word also has specialized senses, such as pooling money, but in an ordinary discussion of swimming or summer recreation the physical swimming pool is intended.
