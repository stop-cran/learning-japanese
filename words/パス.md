# パス - pass; computing path

## Meaning

パス represents two distinct dictionary identities. The **pass** word covers passing a test, an admission pass, passing a ball, and passing one's turn. The separate **path** word identifies a computing path, such as a file's location.

- 味方にボールをパスしました。 (みかたにボールをパスしました。) - I passed the ball to a teammate.
- 試験をパスしました。 (しけんをパスしました。) - I passed the exam.
- ファイルのパスを確認してください。 (ファイルのパスをかくにんしてください。) - Please check the file path.

For the ball, を marks what is passed and に the recipient. In computing, パス is a noun: パスを確認する checks the path; it does not mean "pass a test."

## How the kanji combine

Both identities are written パス in katakana. English "pass" and "path" converge on the same Japanese spelling and pronunciation, but that does not make the computing noun another sense of the pass entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[合格](合格.md) (ごうかく) overlaps with passing an exam, not passing a ball. [切符](切符.md) (きっぷ) overlaps with a transport ticket or pass. [道](道.md) (みち) names a physical path or road; the computing identity does not establish that generic outdoor meaning for パス.

## Distinctive meaning

In a game, パスします can mean "I pass my turn." With a ball and recipient, it instead describes a pass to another player. Context and particles distinguish these uses. The page's study level does not independently assign a level to every homograph or subsidiary sense.
