# やはり (やはり) — as expected; after all

## Meaning

やはり often says that something agrees with an expectation: "as expected" or "just as I thought." It can also mark a conclusion after reconsidering: "after all."

- やはり今日は寒いですね。 (やはりきょうはさむいですね。) — It is cold today, just as expected.
- 考えましたが、やはり行きません。 (かんがえましたが、やはりいきません。) — I thought about it, but I am not going after all.

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is usual. 矢張り is a rare ateji spelling, not a literal explanation involving arrows or stretching. Learn やはり as one adverb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

やっぱり is a conversational variant with similar meanings. 予想どおり (よそうどおり) explicitly means "as predicted." 意外に (いがいに), "unexpectedly," gives a useful contrast to the expectation-confirming use.

## Distinctive meaning

やはり can also mean still, as before, or likewise in context. It does not universally mean "absolutely" or guarantee certainty. The second example expresses the speaker's eventual choice, not an objective proof that going is impossible. The expectation or earlier possibility often comes from the preceding conversation.
