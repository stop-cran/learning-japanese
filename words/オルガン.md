# オルガン (オルガン) - organ (musical instrument)

## Meaning

オルガン is the musical instrument, including the organ heard in a church. Playing it is オルガンを弾く (おるがんをひく).

- 教会でオルガンの演奏を聞きました。 (きょうかいでおるがんのえんそうをききました。) - I listened to an organ performance at a church.
- 姉はオルガンを弾きます。 (あねはおるがんをひきます。) - My older sister plays the organ.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana, with no kanji combination. JMdict identifies Portuguese órgão as its source; resemblance to English "organ" is not evidence of an English borrowing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

パイプオルガン specifies a pipe organ. [ピアノ](ピアノ.md) is a piano: both are keyboard instruments, but they are not the same instrument. No antonym is needed for an instrument name.

## Distinctive meaning

English "organ" also means a body part such as the heart or liver. オルガン does not carry that bodily meaning; the Japanese word is 臓器 (ぞうき). Do not use the instrument name as a general translation of every English "organ."
