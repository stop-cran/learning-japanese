# いただく - receive humbly

## Meaning

いただく is the humble counterpart of [もらう](もらう.md), "receive." It presents the speaker's or their group's receiving action respectfully toward the giver. Mark the giver with に or から and the thing received with を.

先生に本をいただきました。 (せんせいにほんをいただきました。) - I received a book from my teacher.

After a verb's て-form, it means receiving the benefit of someone's action.

先生に作文を見ていただきました。 (せんせいにさくぶんをみていただきました。) - My teacher kindly looked over my composition.

## How the kanji combine

The usual kana spelling avoids a kanji decomposition. 頂く is another spelling, using [頂](../kanji/頂.md). The verb is godan: いただきます, いただいて, いただかない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もらう is neutral. [くださる](くださる.md) respectfully describes the giver's giving; いただく describes the recipient's receiving.

## Distinctive meaning

いただく also humbly or politely means "eat" or "drink." The customary いただきます before a meal uses this wider background. Humble language is not simply an all-purpose upgrade for every person's action: the relationship and whose action is described matter.
