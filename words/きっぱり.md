# きっぱり — clearly and decisively

## Meaning

Making one's position or action unambiguous and resolute.

- 無理（むり）な頼（たの）みをきっぱり断（ことわ）った。 — I firmly refused the unreasonable request.
- 彼女は「行きません」ときっぱり言った。 — She said decisively, “I'm not going.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

[はっきり](はっきり.md) overlaps in clarity but also applies to perception. [ずけずけ](ずけずけ.md) can suggest tactless bluntness, while [はきはき](はきはき.md) emphasizes clear, brisk delivery.

## Distinctive meaning

きっぱり断る is about firmness, not necessarily rudeness or high volume. A quiet refusal can be decisive. きっぱりした態度（たいど）means a clear, resolute attitude.
