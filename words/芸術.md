# 芸術 (げいじゅつ) - art; the arts

## Meaning

芸術 is a noun for art or the arts. It can cover creative fields such as painting, music, and sculpture rather than identifying one particular medium.

- 音楽も芸術の一つです。 (おんがくもげいじゅつのひとつです。) - Music is also one of the arts.
- この町では芸術を身近に感じられます。 (このまちではげいじゅつをみぢかにかんじられます。) - In this town, art feels close to everyday life.

Useful combinations include 芸術作品 (げいじゅつさくひん), "a work of art," and 芸術を学ぶ (げいじゅつをまなぶ), "study art."

## How the kanji combine

芸 contributes artistic skill or accomplishment, while [術](../kanji/術.md) contributes technique or art. The related ideas reinforce each other in ゲイ + ジュツ. The dictionary also records the older spelling 藝術.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[絵画](絵画.md) (かいが) names paintings or painting, a narrower field. 美術 (びじゅつ) is useful for fine or visual art. These words overlap in art-related settings, but 芸術 can include music and other forms not described as 絵画.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun identifies an artistic field or activity; it does not automatically praise every work as excellent. 芸術的な (げいじゅつてきな), "artistic," is a related adjective construction with 的な, not simply the bare noun placed before any other noun. There is no useful universal antonym for all of the arts.
