# 特色 (とくしょく) - distinctive feature; characteristic

## Meaning

特色 is a characteristic or distinguishing feature of a person, thing, or place.
It can highlight a good or outstanding point, but is not restricted to praise.
Nの特色 identifies what has the feature.

- この町の特色を紹介します。 (このまちのとくしょくをしょうかいします。) - I will introduce this town's distinctive features.
- 少人数の授業がこの学校の特色です。 (しょうにんずうのじゅぎょうがこのがっこうのとくしょくです。) - Small classes are a distinctive feature of this school.

## How the kanji combine

[特](../kanji/特.md) contributes special or distinctive, and
[色](../kanji/色.md) contributes color or character.
Together they suggest a distinguishing character, not necessarily a literal color.
The on readings are トク + ショク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[特徴](特徴.md) (とくちょう) is a characteristic or distinguishing feature,
whether favorable or unfavorable.
[特長](特長.md), also とくちょう, emphasizes a strong point or merit.
These words overlap: 特色 can also name an outstanding point.
[長所](長所.md) (ちょうしょ) is another useful word for a strength.

## Distinctive meaning

In printing, 特色 also means a spot color: a separately specified printing ink/color,
rather than simply every color visible in a picture.
That technical use is separate from describing a school's distinctive features.
The word does not by itself establish that a feature is unique in the world.
