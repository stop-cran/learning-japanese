# 胸 (むね) - chest; breast; feelings

## Meaning

胸 is a noun for the chest or breast area. It also appears in expressions about one's heart, thoughts, or feelings. The same word can therefore describe a physical location or an emotional experience.

- 胸に手を当てました。 (むねにてをあてました。) - I put my hand on my chest.
- うれしくて胸がいっぱいになりました。 (うれしくてむねがいっぱいになりました。) - I was overwhelmed with happiness.

The second sentence describes being filled with emotion, not a physical object filling the chest.

## How the kanji combine

胸 is one character with the native reading むね. The dictionary also records むな, used especially in compounds. Learn the reading of a compound separately rather than changing むね to むな in an ordinary standalone sentence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[腹](腹.md) (はら) refers to the belly, a neighboring but different body area. [心](心.md) (こころ), "heart/mind," overlaps with emotional uses, but it is not a replacement for the physical chest in the first example.

## Distinctive meaning

胸 can refer to someone's breasts in a suitable context, but it is not restricted to that meaning or to one gender. 胸が痛い (むねがいたい) can concern physical chest pain or emotional distress; surrounding information determines the meaning. Do not convert every figurative 胸 expression into an anatomical claim.
