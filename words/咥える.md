# 咥える (くわえる) - hold in the mouth

## Meaning

咥える means holding something in one's mouth, for example between the teeth or lips.
It is transitive: the thing held takes を.

- 犬がボールをくわえています。 (いぬがボールをくわえています。) - The dog is holding a ball in its mouth.
- 彼はペンをくわえたまま考えていました。 (かれはペンをくわえたままかんがえていました。) - He was thinking with a pen held in his mouth.

Holding is not the same as swallowing or eating the object.
This ichidan verb has forms くわえます, くわえて, and くわえない.
It is usually written in kana as くわえる.

## How the kanji combine

咥 is the single written character; える is okurigana.
Learn the whole native verb くわえる rather than trying to derive its pronunciation from the visible 口 component.
銜える is another recognized spelling of this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[含む](含む.md) (ふくむ) also has a mouth-holding sense, as well as inclusion and other uses.
There is overlap, not an absolute rule assigning lips to one verb and the inside of the mouth to the other.
[持つ](持つ.md) (もつ), "hold; carry," is broader.
The homophone [加える](加える.md) means adding something, not holding it in the mouth.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the surrounding sentence and the written form to distinguish the two くわえる verbs.
咥える in the examples tells us how the object is held, not that the subject added it to a collection.
