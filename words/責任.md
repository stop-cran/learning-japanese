# 責任 (せきにん) — responsibility

## Meaning

責任 concerns responsibility or duty, including responsibility for work or other people.
Learn 責任がある (せきにんがある), "have responsibility," and 責任を持つ (せきにんをもつ), "take responsibility."
The noun itself is not a verb meaning to perform a task.

- この仕事の責任は私にあります。 (このしごとのせきにんはわたしにあります。) — I am responsible for this work.
- 最後まで責任を持って仕事をします。 (さいごまでせきにんをもってしごとをします。) — I will carry out the work responsibly to the end.

## How the kanji combine

[責](../kanji/責.md) is associated with blame, and [任](../kanji/任.md) concerns an entrusted duty.
A useful way to connect them is being answerable for what has been assigned; the whole word does not require an actual accusation.
The on readings combine as セキ + ニン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[義務](義務.md) (ぎむ) is an obligation or duty: what someone is required to do.
[役割](役割.md) (やくわり) is a role or allotted part.
These overlap with responsibility, but identifying someone's role does not by itself settle how much responsibility that person bears.

## Distinctive meaning

責任 is not limited to blame after something goes wrong.
In the second example it describes taking care of work before its outcome is known.
When reading a sentence, ask what responsibility is involved and who has it, rather than treating 責任 as a label for a guilty person.
