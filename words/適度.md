# 適度 (てきど) - moderate; appropriate in degree

## Meaning

適度 describes a suitable or moderate amount or degree: neither excessive nor insufficient for the situation. Learn **適度な + noun** and **適度に + verb**.

- 適度な運動を続けています。 (てきどなうんどうをつづけています。) - I keep up a moderate amount of exercise.
- 長い作業の間は、適度に休んでください。 (ながいさぎょうのあいだは、てきどにやすんでください。) - During a long task, take an appropriate amount of rest.

The word evaluates the amount; the sentence does not supply a precise exercise or rest schedule.

## How the kanji combine

[適](../kanji/適.md) contributes suitability, and [度](../kanji/度.md) contributes degree or extent. The modifier-plus-head relation is "a suitable degree." The on readings are テキ + ド.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[適当](適当.md) (てきとう) can also mean suitable, but it has other uses, including casual or careless treatment. 適度 specifically emphasizes amount or degree. 過度 (かど), "excessive," contrasts with it on the too-much side.

## Distinctive meaning

適度 is relative to the purpose and circumstances. It does not necessarily mean the numerical midpoint between two extremes. 適度に冷えた飲み物 (てきどにひえたのみもの), "a pleasantly/appropriately chilled drink," evaluates how cold the drink is, not whether someone followed an exact mathematical proportion.
