# 属する (ぞくする) - belong to; come under

## Meaning

属する means to belong to a group or category, be affiliated with something,
or come under its authority. Use **member/item は group/category に属する**.
It is intransitive: に marks what something belongs to, not a direct object with を.

- この植物はバラ科に属します。 (このしょくぶつはバラかにぞくします。) - This plant belongs to the rose family.
- 彼は研究部に属しています。 (かれはけんきゅうぶにぞくしています。) - He belongs to the research department.

属します (ぞくします), 属して (ぞくして) and 属した (ぞくした)
are useful forms. 属している describes an existing affiliation.

## How the kanji combine

属 contributes belonging or affiliation; する supplies the verb form.
The Chinese-derived base is read ぞく here, followed by する.
Learn ぞくする as this complete verb, not as a native verb ending in る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

所属する (しょぞくする) also describes affiliation.
[入る](入る.md) (はいる) can describe joining a group, whereas 属している
describes being in it; an act of joining and an existing affiliation are not identical.
An opposite depends on the relationship: leaving a group is different from being
classified outside a category.

## Distinctive meaning

属する is useful in formal descriptions and classification.
The subject can be a person, an organization or an item; it need not be physically
inside a building. The dictionary also records a separate reading しょくする
with different uses; do not transfer those uses to every ぞくする sentence.
