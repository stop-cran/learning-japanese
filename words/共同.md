# 共同 (きょうどう) — joint activity; cooperation

## Meaning

共同 describes acting jointly or cooperating on something.
Use 共同で before an action to say it is done jointly.
共同の can modify a noun, presenting it as shared or joint.

- 友達と共同で本を作りました。 (ともだちときょうどうでほんをつくりました。) — I made a book jointly with a friend.
- これは二人の共同の作品です。 (これはふたりのきょうどうのさくひんです。) — This is a joint creation by the two of them.

The word also covers shared possession or communal use; it need not describe people doing exactly the same movement at the same time.

## How the kanji combine

[共](../kanji/共.md) contributes togetherness or sharing, and [同](../kanji/同.md) contributes sameness.
They reinforce the shared basis of an activity or arrangement.
Both readings are on readings: キョウ + ドウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

協力 きょうりょく also means cooperation and can focus on giving help or support.
[一緒](一緒.md) いっしょ is broader "together," especially in 一緒に:
一緒に帰る (いっしょにかえる), "go home together," need not be a jointly organized project.
These expressions overlap but do not mark exactly the same relationship.

## Distinctive meaning

共同 makes the joint character of the activity or ownership explicit.
In the book example, it says more than that the two people were in the same room.
It does not by itself specify equal workloads, equal shares, or how responsibilities were divided.
