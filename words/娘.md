# 娘 (むすめ) — daughter

## Meaning

娘 means "daughter." A daughter can be a child or an adult: the word names her relationship to a parent.
私の娘 (わたしのむすめ) makes "my daughter" explicit; context can supply the parent when の is absent.

- 娘は大学で働いています。 (むすめはだいがくではたらいています。) — My daughter works at a university.

The same dictionary entry also records "girl; young unmarried woman." That is a separate use, not an age limit on the daughter sense.

娘 is the ordinary unadorned word for your own daughter when speaking politely to outsiders,
but the noun itself is not grammatically humble and can name anyone's daughter.
For someone else's daughter, お嬢さん (おじょうさん) is a respectful alternative.

## How the kanji combine

[娘](../kanji/娘.md) is the sole written kanji and carries the whole native reading むすめ.
Its visible parts are not separately pronounced in this word.
In 一人娘 (ひとりむすめ), the same noun follows 一人 (ひとり), giving "only daughter."

## Synonyms and antonyms

息子 (むすこ), "son," is the family counterpart, not an opposite personality or age group.
女の子 (おんなのこ) means "girl" and can identify someone by age and gender without naming a particular parent.
When the point is the parent-child relationship, 娘 supplies that connection directly.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling someone your 娘 does not imply that she is young, unmarried, or financially dependent on you.
Keep the family sense separate from the young-woman sense.
Likewise, 一人娘 means "only daughter"; it does not exclude the possibility of brothers.
