# 欠如 (けつじょ) — lack; absence of something needed

## Meaning

欠如 means that something needed is lacking. You can put the missing quality before の, or use が欠如している to say what is lacking.

- 経験の欠如が問題だった。 (けいけんのけつじょがもんだいだった。) — Lack of experience was the problem.
- その説明には具体性が欠如している。 (そのせつめいにはぐたいせいがけつじょしている。) — That explanation lacks specifics.

## How the kanji combine

[欠](../kanji/欠.md) supplies the idea of something missing. [如](../kanji/如.md) is read じょ. Learn the meaning of the compound as a whole. Do not translate 欠如 as “like a lack.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不足](不足.md) also means a lack or shortage. 人手不足 (ひとでぶそく) describes being short of staff; 判断力の欠如 (はんだんりょくのけつじょ) calls out a lack of judgment. These examples illustrate usage, not mutually exclusive definitions.

## Distinctive meaning

A missing quality and a missing person are different kinds of absence. For not attending class, use [欠席](欠席.md) (けっせき); 経験の欠如 is not someone failing to turn up.
