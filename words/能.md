# 能 (のう) - talent; noh theatre

## Meaning

能 is a noun meaning talent, a gift, or function. It also names **noh**, the Japanese theatrical tradition. These meanings share the spelling and reading but require different contexts.

- 彼には人を笑わせる能があります。 (かれにはひとをわらわせるのうがあります。) - He has a talent for making people laugh.
- 京都で能を見ました。 (きょうとでのうをみました。) - I saw a noh performance in Kyoto.

In the first example, a verb phrase describes the talent and が marks 能 as what exists. In the second, を marks the performance being watched.

## How the kanji combine

[能](../kanji/能.md) itself contributes ability or capacity. The on reading のう is the whole word, with a long vowel. There is no second component to combine with it. The theatrical meaning must be learned as an established use, not deduced as a complete description of the art from "ability."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[能力](能力.md) (のうりょく) means ability or capacity more broadly; [才能](才能.md) (さいのう) emphasizes talent. Neither substitutes for 能 when the topic is noh theatre.

## Distinctive meaning

能 is not an adverb meaning "nicely" or "properly." To describe doing something well, the sentence needs an appropriate adverbial expression, not bare のう before a verb. Expressions such as 能がない (のうがない), "have no ability or talent," can be disparaging, so pay attention to context rather than treating every ability-related phrase as neutral praise.
