# 結う (ゆう) - do up hair; bind

## Meaning

結う means to do up or arrange hair. It also has a broader tying or binding use. It is a transitive godan verb: what is arranged or tied takes を.

- 姉は髪を結っています。 (あねはかみをゆっています。) - My older sister has her hair tied up.
- 母は娘の髪を結いました。 (はははむすめのかみをゆいました。) - The mother did her daughter's hair.

## How the kanji combine

[結](../kanji/結.md) supplies ゆ, and う is the written ending. Forms include 結います (ゆいます), 結って (ゆって), 結った (ゆった) and 結わない (ゆわない). Despite the spelling with う, the て-form is not ゆいて.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結ぶ](結ぶ.md) (むすぶ) overlaps in tying and has wider connection and agreement uses. [結わえる](結わえる.md) (ゆわえる) means tie up or fasten and is a different verb with different conjugation.

## Distinctive meaning

Doing up hair does not necessarily mean braiding it. Nor should every extended use of 結ぶ be transferred to 結う: learn the object and expression, not just the shared kanji.
