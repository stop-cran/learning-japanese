# 楽しむ (たのしむ) — to enjoy

## Meaning

楽しむ means "enjoy" or "have a good time." The activity or experience enjoyed can take を. It is a godan verb: 楽しみます (たのしみます), 楽しんで (たのしんで), 楽しんだ (たのしんだ).

週末は料理を楽しみます。 (しゅうまつはりょうりをたのしみます。) — I enjoy cooking on weekends.

旅行を楽しんでください。 (りょこうをたのしんでください。) — Enjoy your trip.

## How the kanji combine

[楽](../kanji/楽.md) carries the pleasure meaning, read たの here. The word is related to 楽しい (たのしい), but 楽しむ is a verb, not that adjective's conjugation.

Use 楽しむ in ordinary writing. JMdict retains 楽む as a search-only spelling, not an equally usual alternative.

## Synonyms and antonyms

楽しい describes something as enjoyable. 遊ぶ (あそぶ), "play" or "spend leisure time," names an activity rather than necessarily the feeling of enjoying it.

## Distinctive meaning

楽しむ usually concerns enjoying an experience. To express anticipation in everyday Japanese, use 楽しみにする or 楽しみにしている: 旅行を楽しみにしています, "I'm looking forward to the trip." Do not treat the dictionary's dated anticipatory sense of 楽しむ alone as the default modern pattern.
