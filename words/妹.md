# 妹 (いもうと) — younger sister

## Meaning

妹 is a younger sister. The age comparison is between siblings; an adult can still be someone's 妹.

- 妹は学生です。 (いもうとはがくせいです。) — My younger sister is a student.
- 田中さんの妹さんに会いました。 (たなかさんのいもうとさんにあいました。) — I met Tanaka's younger sister.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun: [妹](../kanji/妹.md) represents the whole word いもうと. There are no separately pronounced compound parts or okurigana.

In 姉妹 しまい, "sisters," 妹 instead has the on-reading マイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [姉](姉.md) あね means older sister, contrasting relative age.
- [弟](弟.md) おとうと means younger brother, contrasting gender within a similar age relationship.
- 姉妹 しまい refers to sisters collectively. These are related family terms, not interchangeable synonyms or strict logical antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

妹 is the usual neutral reference to your own younger sister when talking to someone outside the family. 妹さん (いもうとさん) is a polite reference to another person's younger sister. Adding さん does not change which sibling is younger, and family address habits vary.
