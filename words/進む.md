# 進む (すすむ) — move forward

## Meaning

進む means moving forward. It is intransitive: the subject moves or progresses, rather than acting on a direct object.
へ or に can indicate the direction or destination. Its polite form is 進みます (すすみます); its て-form is 進んで (すすんで).

- もう少し前へ進んでください。 (もうすこしまえへすすんでください。) — Please move a little farther forward.
- 仕事が進んでいます。 (しごとがすすんでいます。) — The work is progressing.

## How the kanji combine

[進](../kanji/進.md) represents advancing; む completes the native verb すすむ.
No second kanji is involved. The abstract use for work progressing extends the idea of forward movement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

進める (すすめる) is the related transitive verb: 仕事を進める (しごとをすすめる), "move the work forward."
With 進む the work progresses; with 進める someone advances it.
[戻る](戻る.md) (もどる), "go back; return," gives a useful directional contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

進む does not mean improvement in every context: a situation can advance without becoming better.
For the basic work example, focus on progress through a task, rather than assuming a successful result.
