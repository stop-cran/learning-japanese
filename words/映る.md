# 映る (うつる) - be reflected; appear on screen

## Meaning

映る is an intransitive godan verb: something is reflected, projected or displayed.
The thing seen takes が; the mirror or screen can take に.

- 鏡に顔が映っています。 (かがみにかおがうつっています。) - A face is reflected in the mirror.
- 画面に地図が映りました。 (がめんにちずがうつりました。) - A map appeared on the screen.

Learn 映ります (うつります), 映った (うつった) and 映らない (うつらない).
This article focuses on reflections and displayed images; the dictionary also records
photographic appearance and visual harmonizing uses.

## How the kanji combine

[映](../kanji/映.md) supplies うつ; る is the kana ending.
Its image/reflection association connects these uses.
[映す](映す.md) has a different ending and a transitive sentence pattern.

## Synonyms and antonyms

顔を鏡に映す (かおをかがみにうつす) presents someone's action of reflecting a face;
顔が鏡に映る presents the reflected face.
[写る](写る.md) teaches appearing in a photograph.
Both dictionary entries admit image-related overlap; they are not separated solely by whether a camera is involved.
[移る](移る.md), also うつる, concerns moving, not a reflection spelling.

## Distinctive meaning

For a practical spelling contrast, use 写 for recording a photographic subject and 映 for reflection or display.
The official guide permits 映る for video playback and for live camera images viewed as displays.
Seeing a map 映っている on a screen does not mean that the physical map moved into the device.
These examples do not teach every older or context-dependent visual use of 映る.
