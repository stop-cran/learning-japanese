# 受験 (じゅけん) - taking an examination

## Meaning

受験 is taking an examination, especially a school or university entrance examination. 受験する is transitive. The object can name the examination or the institution whose entrance examination one takes.

- 来年、この大学を受験します。
  (らいねん、このだいがくをじゅけんします。) - Next year I will take the entrance examination for this university.
- 受験しましたが、合格できませんでした。
  (じゅけんしましたが、ごうかくできませんでした。) - I took the exam but did not pass.

大学を受験する does not mean that the university itself is the examinee.

## How the kanji combine

[受](../kanji/受.md) contributes undergoing or taking, and [験](../kanji/験.md) testing. Together they identify the examinee's act of taking a test. Read ジュ + ケン, with a short じゅ, unlike the long じゅう in [重量](重量.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[試験](試験.md) (しけん) names an examination or test. [受ける](受ける.md) (うける) gives the parallel construction 試験を受ける, "take an exam."

[合格](合格.md) (ごうかく) is passing or meeting a standard. It is a possible result, not a synonym or opposite of taking the examination.

## Distinctive meaning

受験 does not guarantee passing, and preparing to take an exam is not the same event as actually taking it. 受験生 (じゅけんせい) is an examination candidate; the compound can include someone still preparing for the test.
