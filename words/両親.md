# 両親 (りょうしん) - both parents

## Meaning

両親 means both parents. It identifies the pair rather than leaving the number of parents unspecified.

- 両親は大阪に住んでいます。 (りょうしんはおおさかにすんでいます。) - My parents live in Osaka.
- 両親に手紙を書きました。 (りょうしんにてがみをかきました。) - I wrote a letter to my parents.

It is a noun; に in the second example marks the recipients.

## How the kanji combine

[両](../kanji/両.md), "both," and [親](../kanji/親.md), "parent," combine with on readings: りょう + しん. This differs from the independent native noun 親 (おや).

## Synonyms and antonyms

親 can mean one parent or parents according to context. 父 (ちち) and 母 (はは) specify father and mother individually. These are distinctions within a family relationship, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

両親 is a reference term, not a normal way to call directly to one's parents. ご両親 (ごりょうしん) adds respect when referring to someone else's parents.
