# 王 (おう) — king; sovereign

## Meaning

王 names a king or sovereign. It describes the ruler, not the country being ruled.
The reading おう has a long vowel: do not shorten it to お.

- 王は城に住んでいます。 (おうはしろにすんでいます。) — The king lives in a castle.
- この物語には王が出てきます。 (このものがたりにはおうがでてきます。) — A king appears in this story.

王 can stand as a noun, and the dictionary also records it as a suffix in compounds.

## How the kanji combine

This word consists of [王](../kanji/王.md) alone, read with the on reading オウ.
There is no two-part compound to unpack. Its classification here is kango, a word using a Sino-Japanese reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

国王 (こくおう) specifies the monarch of a country.
[王様](王様.md) (おうさま) adds the honorific 様 (さま), showing respect toward the king.
王国 (おうこく), "kingdom," names the realm instead of the person; it is related vocabulary, not a synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The basic word identifies a sovereign. It is not a general replacement for "boss" or anyone who is influential.
The honorific in 王様 changes how the person is referred to, not the kind of country they govern.
Learn extensions in their complete compounds rather than automatically translating every final 王 as a literal reigning king.
