# 友 (とも) - friend; companion

## Meaning

友 is a noun meaning "friend" or "companion." It can name someone
with whom one has a bond or shares an experience. Use 友と
for doing something with a friend, and 友から for receiving
something from one.

- 古い友から手紙が届きました。 (ふるいともからてがみがとどきました。) - A letter arrived from an old friend.
- 旅の友とまた会いました。 (たびのともとまたあいました。) - I met my traveling companion again.

In the first example, 古い describes a longstanding friendship,
not necessarily the friend's age. 旅の友 identifies a companion
through the shared experience of travel.

## How the kanji combine

[友](../kanji/友.md) represents the whole native noun とも.
The same character appears in longer words for friendship, but this
card's single-character form is a complete noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[友達](友達.md) (ともだち) is a useful everyday word for a
friend. [友人](友人.md) (ゆうじん) is another noun for a
friend. [仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) emphasizes belonging to a
shared group or activity and need not imply a close friendship.

## Distinctive meaning

友 works naturally in expressions such as 古い友 and 旅の友.
These phrases highlight the bond or companionship. Calling someone
a 友 does not by itself specify how long you have known them,
their age, or whether the relationship began through work, travel,
or school.
