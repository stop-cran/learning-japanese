# 手 (て) — hand

## Meaning

手 is the hand, and often the whole arm in casual speech: 手を洗う (wash one's hands), 手を上げる (raise a hand), 手をつなぐ (hold hands), 手が足りない (short-handed).
It can also mean a means, method, or trick: 新しい手 (あたらしいて), "a new approach"; 同じ手は使えない (おなじてはつかえない), "I can't use the same trick again."
In a game, 強い手 (つよいて) is "a strong move."

## How the kanji combine

A single kanji, [手](../kanji/手.md), with the kun reading て. Compounds mix readings: 手紙 てがみ (letter), 切手 きって (stamp), 上手 じょうず and 下手 へた (skilful, unskilful, special readings),
歌手 かしゅ (singer), 選手 せんしゅ (player), 相手 あいて (partner).
See [右手](右手.md) and [左手](左手.md) for the right and left hand.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 腕 うで (arm), 掌 てのひら (palm), 指 ゆび (finger).
- Antonym: [足](足.md) あし (foot), in body-part pairs and in 手足 てあし (hands and feet).

## Distinctive meaning

手 stands for the hand as a tool. Japanese uses 手 in many idioms: 手が空く (be free), 手に入る (get hold of), 手を貸す (lend a hand).
