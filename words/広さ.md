# 広さ (ひろさ) - spatial extent; width

## Meaning

広さ is how wide or extensive something is: its area, extent, size,
or breadth. It is a noun, useful when asking or stating how much space
something has.

- この部屋の広さは二十平方メートルです。 (このへやのひろさはにじゅっへいほうメートルです。) - This room has an area of twenty square meters.
- 庭の広さを比べました。 (にわのひろさをくらべました。) - We compared the sizes of the gardens.

Nの広さ identifies what has that extent.
The first example supplies a unit; the word itself does not specify one.

## How the kanji combine

[広](../kanji/広.md) supplies ひろ in [広い](広い.md)
(ひろい), wide or spacious. Replacing final い with さ forms the
noun 広さ, the extent of that quality.
This is adjective-to-noun formation, not a two-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[幅](幅.md) (はば) overlaps in width or breadth.
面積 (めんせき) specifically names area as a measurement;
広さ can also concern width and is not limited to square units.
[寸法](寸法.md) (すんぽう) names dimensions or measurements.
[範囲](範囲.md) (はんい) is a range or scope, which can be spatial
or abstract.

## Distinctive meaning

広さ names an extent, not a region merely because English calls
both an "area." It also does not say the space is large:
広さを聞く (ひろさをきく), ask how big it is, leaves the answer open.
Keep the noun apart from 広い, which directly describes something
as wide or spacious.
