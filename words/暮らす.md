# 暮らす (くらす) — live; lead a life

## Meaning

暮らす means to live or lead one's life. It can describe where, with whom, or how someone lives.
For the place of daily life, a useful frame is **place + で暮らす**.
In this living sense it is intransitive: the person leading the life is the subject, not an object marked を.

- 家族と東京で暮らしています。 (かぞくととうきょうでくらしています。) — I live in Tokyo with my family.
- 今は一人で暮らしています。 (いまはひとりでくらしています。) — I live on my own now.

## How the kanji combine

[暮](../kanji/暮.md) has a livelihood sense in this word. The kana らす complete the native verb,
which is wago rather than a compound of on readings.
It is a godan verb with final す: 暮らします (くらします) is polite, and 暮らして (くらして)
is its て-form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[住む](住む.md) (すむ) emphasizes residence. Compare 東京に住んでいます (とうきょうにすんでいます),
"I reside in Tokyo," with 東京で暮らしています, which presents Tokyo as the setting for daily life.
The meanings overlap; this is a useful focus and particle-pattern contrast, not a claim that one
sentence describes a completely different situation.

## Distinctive meaning

暮らす is useful when explaining one's living arrangements, not merely stating an address.
In 一人で暮らす (ひとりでくらす), で describes being on one's own, rather than a location.
暮らし (くらし) is the related noun, "day-to-day life; livelihood."
Do not confuse 暮らす with 暮れる (くれる), "grow dark; come to an end."
