# とんでもない (とんでもない) - outrageous; not at all

## Meaning

とんでもない is an i-adjective for something unthinkable, absurd, or outrageous.
It also works as a response: strongly rejecting a suggestion, or reassuring
someone that a favor was no trouble. Context and tone are essential.

- とんでもない間違いをしてしまいました。 (とんでもないまちがいをしてしまいました。) - I made a terrible mistake.
- 「全部一人で作ったの？」「とんでもない。みんなで作ったんだ。」 (「ぜんぶひとりでつくったの？」「とんでもない。みんなでつくったんだ。」) - "Did you make it all yourself?" "Not at all. We all made it together."

After thanks, a reassuring response is:
「手伝ってくれてありがとう。」「いえ、とんでもないです。」
(「てつだってくれてありがとう。」「いえ、とんでもないです。」)
- "Thank you for helping." "Not at all; it was no trouble."

## How the kanji combine

Use the kana spelling. This is an established expression; do not treat it as
the ordinary verb 飛ぶ (とぶ), "fly," merely because it starts with とんで.
Before a noun, use とんでもない directly, without な.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[普通](普通.md) (ふつう), "ordinary," can contrast with an extraordinary situation,
but it does not reverse every conversational use. English "no way" suits strong
rejection, not automatically a polite response to thanks.

## Distinctive meaning

The adjective need not accuse someone of being offensive. It can describe a
terrible mistake or an extraordinary situation. Conversely, the response after
thanks is not calling the favor absurd. Interpret the entire exchange rather
than assigning one emotional tone to the expression.
