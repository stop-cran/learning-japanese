# まっすぐ (まっすぐ) — straight ahead

## Meaning

まっすぐ means straight, straight ahead, or directly. It can describe a path without bends or movement without a detour.

この道をまっすぐ行ってください。（このみちをまっすぐいってください。） — Please go straight along this road.

今日はまっすぐ家に帰ります。（きょうはまっすぐいえにかえります。） — I will go straight home today.

背中をまっすぐにしてください。（せなかをまっすぐにしてください。） — Please straighten your back.
Here the word describes straight alignment rather than a direction of travel.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual; 真っ直ぐ is also recorded. Learn the whole spelling and pronunciation, including small っ, rather than treating it as a regular two-kanji on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

曲がる（まがる）, "turn; bend," contrasts with going straight. 寄り道する（よりみちする） means to make a stop or detour on the way.

## Distinctive meaning

Before a noun, use な: まっすぐな道（まっすぐなみち）, "a straight road." As an adverb it can appear with or without に. For a person, まっすぐ can figuratively suggest honesty or straightforwardness.
