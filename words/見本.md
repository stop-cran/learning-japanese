# 見本 (みほん) -- sample; model

## Meaning

A 見本 is a sample or specimen shown to indicate what something is
like. It can also be a model or example to follow. Use 見本の before
another noun, or 見本にする for making something a model.

- 布の見本を見せてください。 (ぬののみほんをみせてください。) -- Please show me a fabric sample.
- 先生が見本を示しました。 (せんせいがみほんをしめしました。) -- The teacher demonstrated a model to follow.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) supplies native み, seeing, and
[本](../kanji/本.md) supplies on-reading ホン, with its model or basis
association. This is a native-first mixed reading, not an all-on compound.
The metadata uses the repository's native-first `wago` convention.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [サンプル](サンプル.md) overlaps directly in sample or specimen.
- [標本](標本.md) (ひょうほん) often concerns a specimen for study, though its meanings also include sample or example.
- [例](例.md) (れい) and [実例](実例.md) (じつれい) can be examples without being a physical sample.
- [典型](典型.md) (てんけい) emphasizes a typical or representative example.

## Distinctive meaning

見本 is not limited to merchandise or a free promotional item. A written
model or a demonstrated action can also serve as a 見本. The word does
not by itself guarantee that a sample is free, scientifically
representative or the only correct model.
