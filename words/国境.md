# 国境 (こっきょう) - national border

## Meaning

国境 is the border between countries. It is a noun: 国境を越える (こっきょうをこえる) means "cross a national border,"
and 国境の近く (こっきょうのちかく) means "near the border."
The word does not itself specify whether the boundary follows a river, a mountain range, or an agreed line.

- この川が二つの国の国境になっています。 (このかわがふたつのくにのこっきょうになっています。) - This river forms the border between two countries.
- 電車で国境を越えました。 (でんしゃでこっきょうをこえました。) - We crossed the border by train.

In the second sentence, を marks the boundary crossed.

## How the kanji combine

[国](../kanji/国.md) contributes country, and 境 boundary.
Together they mean a boundary between countries.
The reading こっきょう has a small っ: do not pronounce it as こくきょう.
JMdict restricts this reading to the spelling 国境 among the entry's listed written forms.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[境](境.md) (さかい) is a boundary more generally.
[国内](国内.md) (こくない), "within the country," and 国外 (こくがい), "outside the country," identify sides of a national boundary rather than synonyms for it.

## Distinctive meaning

In an English gloss such as "state border," understand "state" as a sovereign country.
A boundary between provinces or subnational states is not automatically a 国境.
Choose the Japanese term from the administrative relationship, not simply from the English word "border."
