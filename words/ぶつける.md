# ぶつける - hit against; throw at; vent on

## Meaning

ぶつける means to hit something against a target, including accidentally bumping a body part, or to throw something at someone or something. Feelings and questions can also be directed at a person with this verb.

- 頭を棚にぶつけました。 (あたまをたなにぶつけました。) - I bumped my head against the shelf.
- ボールを壁にぶつけました。 (ボールをかべにぶつけました。) - I threw the ball against the wall.
- 彼に怒りをぶつけてしまいました。 (かれにいかりをぶつけてしまいました。) - I ended up venting my anger on him.

## How the kanji combine

The ordinary spelling is kana. JMdict records 打付ける as rare and 打つける as search-only; neither is the teaching spelling or a reason to add kanji to this card's metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぶつかる](ぶつかる.md) describes colliding intransitively. [当てる](当てる.md) (あてる) overlaps in hitting a target, while [投げる](投げる.md) (なげる) means throwing without itself requiring this targeted contact.

## Distinctive meaning

This is a transitive ichidan verb: ぶつけない, ぶつけます, ぶつけて. Learn the object and target: 頭を棚にぶつける. Physical impact need not be deliberate. The dictionary additionally records pitting someone against an opponent. A misleading source gloss such as "nail on" is not a general instruction to use this word for fastening a nail.
