# 湿気 (しっけ) - moisture; humidity

## Meaning

湿気 means moisture, dampness, or humidity.
It can concern moisture in the air or in an object.
**しっき** is another dictionary reading of the same word.
Both source-list readings, しっけ and しっき, are covered here.

- 雨の日は部屋に湿気がこもります。 (あめのひはへやにしっけがこもります。) - On rainy days, moisture builds up in the room.
- この箱は湿気を吸いやすいです。 (このはこはしっけをすいやすいです。) - This box readily absorbs moisture.

湿気が多い (しっけがおおい) means there is a lot of moisture;
湿気を取る (しっけをとる) means remove moisture.
The readings above use the article's primary しっけ.

## How the kanji combine

[湿](../kanji/湿.md) contributes dampness, and [気](../kanji/気.md)
contributes an air or vapor association.
シツ combines with 気's ケ or キ as しっけ or しっき, with a small っ.
These are readings of the established compound, not a reason to replace
every standalone 気 (き) with け.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[湿度](湿度.md) (しつど) is humidity as a measure.
[じめじめ](じめじめ.md) and [じとじと](じとじと.md) can emphasize
unpleasant dampness; [しっとり](しっとり.md) can describe welcome moisture.
[乾燥](乾燥.md) (かんそう), dryness or drying, provides a useful physical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

湿気 is not restricted to hot weather or to air alone.
Nor does the noun itself guarantee discomfort or say how much moisture is present.
Use a description such as 多い, or a measurement expressed as 湿度,
when the amount matters.
