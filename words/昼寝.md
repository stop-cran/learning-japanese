# 昼寝 (ひるね) - daytime nap; siesta

## Meaning

昼寝 is a daytime nap or sleeping during the day.
It is a noun and an intransitive する expression: 昼寝する,
take a nap.

- 昼ご飯の後、少し昼寝をしました。 (ひるごはんのあと、すこしひるねをしました。) - I took a short nap after lunch.
- 子供は公園のベンチで昼寝をしています。 (こどもはこうえんのベンチでひるねをしています。) - The child is napping on a park bench.

The first example describes one time for a nap, not a rule requiring
every 昼寝 to follow lunch.

## How the kanji combine

[昼](../kanji/昼.md), ひる, daytime, modifies
[寝](../kanji/寝.md), ね, sleeping.
The native combination identifies sleeping during the day.
昼寐 is a search-only spelling in the pinned dictionary, not the
recommended spelling for these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[居眠り](居眠り.md) (いねむり) concerns dozing or nodding off,
especially while sitting.
[寝る](寝る.md) (ねる) and [眠る](眠る.md) (ねむる) are broader
sleep verbs. [睡眠](睡眠.md) (すいみん) is the noun sleep.
[昼休み](昼休み.md) (ひるやすみ) is a lunch break, which need
not involve sleeping.

## Distinctive meaning

昼寝 is not restricted to one's home or bed.
The word does not set an exact starting hour or duration.
Conversely, resting with one's eyes open is not automatically a nap:
sleep, rather than merely a pause in work, is central here.
