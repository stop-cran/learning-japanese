# 親戚 (しんせき) - relative; kin

## Meaning

親戚 is a relative or relatives, including family connections through blood or marriage. The noun does not itself specify which relationship someone has to you.

- 正月に親戚が集まりました。 (しょうがつにしんせきがあつまりました。) - The relatives gathered at New Year.
- 大阪に住んでいる親戚を訪ねました。 (おおさかにすんでいるしんせきをたずねました。) - I visited a relative who lives in Osaka.

The first sentence describes a group; the second can concern one relative. Japanese does not require a plural ending on 親戚.

## How the kanji combine

[親](../kanji/親.md) contributes family connection or closeness. 戚 also contributes kinship or relatives. The two related meanings reinforce the family-relationship meaning. The on readings are シン + セキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

親類 (しんるい) also means relatives or kin and substantially overlaps with 親戚. [家族](家族.md) (かぞく) means family; in everyday descriptions, it can focus on one's immediate family rather than the wider relatives being discussed.

## Distinctive meaning

Being 親戚 is a relationship, not a measure of emotional closeness or how often people meet. 遠い親戚 (とおいしんせき), "a distant relative," can refer to a remote family connection rather than someone who lives far away. Specify おじ, おば, いとこ, or another relationship term when the exact connection matters.
