# 素質 (そしつ) - aptitude; innate qualities

## Meaning

素質 means aptitude or the makings of an ability: qualities that provide
a basis for development. It can also mean someone's underlying nature,
temperament or constitution, not only a special talent.
Useful patterns are **Nの素質がある** and **Nとしての素質がある**.

- 彼女には音楽家としての素質があります。 (かのじょにはおんがくかとしてのそしつがあります。) - She has the makings of a musician.
- 兄弟でも、生まれ持った素質は違います。 (きょうだいでも、うまれもったそしつはちがいます。) - Even siblings differ in their inborn qualities.

The first example recognizes potential; it does not say that she is already
an accomplished professional.

## How the kanji combine

素 contributes a basic or original state, and [質](../kanji/質.md)
contributes nature or quality. Together they point to underlying qualities.
The on readings combine as そ + しつ.
This is a meaning association for the compound, not a claim that each character
has only this meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[才能](才能.md) (さいのう) and [能力](能力.md) (のうりょく) overlap in
talent or ability. 素質 often highlights the basis or potential for development,
while [実力](実力.md) (じつりょく) concerns actual ability.
[性質](性質.md) (せいしつ) and [性格](性格.md) (せいかく) overlap with
the nature/temperament use. These are tendencies, not mutually exclusive boxes.

## Distinctive meaning

Having 素質 is not a guarantee of success and does not make practice unnecessary.
Nor should the word be restricted to praise for exceptional genius:
the nature/constitution sense is broader.
English "character" and "qualities" cross several dictionary entries;
quiz exclusions do not make [人物](人物.md) or [カラー](カラー.md)
freely interchangeable with 素質 in a sentence.
