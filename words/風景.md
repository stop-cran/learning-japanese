# 風景 (ふうけい) - scenery; scene

## Meaning

風景 is a noun for scenery, a landscape, or a scene viewed as a whole. It can include buildings and people as well as mountains, rivers, or fields.

- 電車の窓から田舎の風景を楽しみました。 (でんしゃのまどからいなかのふうけいをたのしみました。) - I enjoyed the countryside scenery from the train window.
- 子どもたちが遊ぶ風景を写真に撮りました。 (こどもたちがあそぶふうけいをしゃしんにとりました。) - I photographed the scene of children playing.

風景を眺める (ふうけいをながめる) means "look at the scenery." A preceding clause can identify the scene, as 子どもたちが遊ぶ does above.

## How the kanji combine

[風](../kanji/風.md) can contribute an appearance or character here, rather than literal wind. [景](../kanji/景.md) contributes a view or scene. Their combination names the scene's overall appearance. Read フウ + ケイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[景色](景色.md) (けしき) closely overlaps in the scenery sense. [眺め](眺め.md) (ながめ) names a view or prospect, often from a particular vantage point. These are useful comparisons, not categorical divisions between natural and artificial scenes.

## Distinctive meaning

風景 does not itself mean beautiful scenery; adjectives such as 美しい (うつくしい) add that evaluation. Familiar streets, a busy workplace, or an ordinary neighborhood can form a 風景. Do not restrict the word to untouched nature or tourist attractions.
