# 午後 (ごご) — p.m., afternoon

## Meaning

午後 is the time from noon to midnight: 午後三時 ごごさんじ (3 p.m.), 午後から (from the afternoon), 午後の会議 (afternoon meeting).

## How the kanji combine

[午](../kanji/午.md) (noon) plus [後](../kanji/後.md) (after): "after noon". Both on'yomi: ゴ + ゴ (the reading ゴ of 後, not コウ). It is the mirror of [午前](午前.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [午前](午前.md) ごぜん (a.m.).
- Related: 昼 ひる (daytime, noon), 夜 よる (night). 午後 includes the evening, such as 午後九時.

## Distinctive meaning

午後 is a clock term covering noon through midnight, while English "afternoon" ends around the evening. For natural parts of the day use 昼, 夕方 ゆうがた and 夜.
