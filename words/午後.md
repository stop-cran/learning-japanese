# 午後 (ごご) — p.m., afternoon

## Meaning

午後 means "afternoon" in everyday plans and descriptions. It also marks p.m. in clock times, covering the period from noon until midnight.

- 今日の午後は暇です。 (きょうのごごはひまです。) — I'm free this afternoon.
- 電車は午後九時に出ます。 (でんしゃはごごくじにでます。) — The train leaves at 9 p.m.

午後から (ごごから) means "from the afternoon," and 午後の会議 (ごごのかいぎ) means "an afternoon meeting."

## How the kanji combine

[午](../kanji/午.md) (noon) plus [後](../kanji/後.md) (after): "after noon". Both on'yomi: ゴ + ゴ (the reading ゴ of 後, not コウ). It is the mirror of [午前](午前.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [午前](午前.md) ごぜん (a.m.).
- Related: 昼 ひる (daytime, noon), 夕方 ゆうがた (early evening), 夜 よる (night). A clock label such as 午後九時 (ごごくじ) extends into the night.

## Distinctive meaning

As a natural part of the day, 午後 runs from noon toward evening; ordinary conversation does not impose one fixed clock cutoff. It is the normal word in expressions such as "this afternoon," not just a timetable label. 午後九時 means 9 p.m., but that does not make 9 p.m. "afternoon" in the everyday sense.
