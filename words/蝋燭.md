# 蝋燭 (ろうそく) - candle

## Meaning

蝋燭 is a candle. The word is usually written in kana:
ろうそく, ロウソク or ローソク.

- 蝋燭に火をつけました。 (ろうそくにひをつけました。) - I lit the candle.
- ろうそくの火が消えました。 (ろうそくのひがきえました。) - The candle flame went out.

In 火をつける (ひをつける), 火 is what is lit and に can
mark the candle receiving the flame.

## How the kanji combine

蝋 contributes wax and 燭 a candle or light.
The material qualifies the lighting object.
Read ロウ + ソク; the second character is ソク in this compound.
There are no separate component cards to link here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[灯](灯.md) (ひ) and [明かり](明かり.md) (あかり) concern
light or a source of illumination more broadly.
A candle can provide such light, but 蝋燭 names the candle itself,
not every kind of illumination. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The written kanji pair has reading ろうそく in this card.
The alternate katakana forms are kana spellings, not a license
to assign every kana-only dictionary reading to the written pair.
Do not confuse the candle with its flame: a candle remains an
object even while unlit.
