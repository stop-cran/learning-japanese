# 送別 (そうべつ) - farewell; send-off

## Meaning

送別 is a farewell or send-off for someone leaving.
It is a noun and can form the transitive verb 送別する:
the person being sent off takes を.
送別の言葉 (そうべつのことば) means words of farewell.

- 転勤する同僚の送別会を開きました。 (てんきんするどうりょうのそうべつかいをひらきました。) - We held a farewell party for a colleague who is transferring.
- 先生に送別の言葉を贈りました。 (せんせいにそうべつのことばをおくりました。) - We offered our teacher words of farewell.

送別会 (そうべつかい) adds 会, gathering or party.
送別 itself does not require an organized party.

## How the kanji combine

[送](../kanji/送.md) contributes sending someone onward, and
[別](../kanji/別.md) parting.
Together they suggest sending someone off at parting.
The on readings are ソウ + ベツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[見送り](見送り.md) (みおくり) also means seeing someone off,
but additionally has a separate postponement use.
[別れ](別れ.md) (わかれ) is parting more broadly;
[さようなら](さようなら.md) is a spoken farewell greeting.
[歓迎](歓迎.md) (かんげい), welcoming someone, contrasts at an arrival.

## Distinctive meaning

A send-off can acknowledge a departure without implying a quarrel or
permanent separation. The English "farewell" can name the occasion,
the act, or the words said, but those do not give every Japanese expression
the same grammatical behavior.
