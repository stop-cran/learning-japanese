# 反映 (はんえい) - reflection; taking effect

## Meaning

反映 names reflection of light or the way one thing reflects a condition,
attitude, or other influence. It is a noun and a する verb; the dictionary
records both transitive and intransitive uses.

- 水面が夕日の光を反映しています。 (すいめんがゆうひのひかりをはんえいしています。) - The water's surface reflects the light of the setting sun.
- 利用者の意見を新しい設計に反映させました。 (りようしゃのいけんをあたらしいせっけいにはんえいさせました。) - We incorporated users' opinions into the new design.

Compare AがBに反映する, "A is reflected in B," with
AをBに反映させる, "cause A to be reflected in B."
The first presents a reflected condition; the second explicitly makes
someone bring that reflection or incorporation about.

## How the kanji combine

[反](../kanji/反.md) contributes a returning/back association, and
[映](../kanji/映.md) contributes an image or reflection.
The compound connects reflected light with abstract things being
reflected in something else. Read ハン + エイ, with no long はん sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[映す](映す.md) (うつす) and [映る](映る.md) (うつる) overlap in
image/reflection uses. [影響](影響.md) (えいきょう) broadly concerns
an effect or influence. The source's "influence" gloss does not make
反映 a replacement for every construction with 影響.

## Distinctive meaning

In computing, 変更が反映される (へんこうがはんえいされる) means
that changes are applied or take effect. This is not necessarily a
visible mirror image; the displayed or stored state incorporates the change.

English "reflection" can also mean self-examination.
That is [反省](反省.md) (はんせい), not the optical or
condition-reflecting meaning taught here.
