# 入院 (にゅういん)

## Meaning

入院 means hospitalization: admission to or a stay in hospital as a patient. 入院する means to be admitted or hospitalized.

- 父は昨日入院しました。 (ちちは きのう にゅういんしました。) — My father was admitted to hospital yesterday.
- 一週間入院しています。 (いっしゅうかん にゅういんしています。) — I have been in hospital for a week.

The patient is the subject. This is an intransitive suru-verb, not "hospitalize someone" with the patient as its を-object.

## How the kanji combine

[入](../kanji/入.md) means enter, and [院](../kanji/院.md) identifies the institution, here a hospital. The on readings are にゅう + いん; pronounce the long ゅう before いん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[退院](退院.md) (たいいん) is discharge from hospital and ends the stay. 通院 (つういん) means attending hospital or a clinic for treatment without necessarily staying there.

## Distinctive meaning

Entering the hospital building as a visitor is not 入院. The word describes patient status, not merely physical entry. English may use a passive phrase, "was hospitalized," where Japanese uses ordinary 入院した.
