# 記号 (きごう) - symbol; sign; mark

## Meaning

記号 is a sign, symbol, or mark used to represent something. It is a noun. The meaning of a particular symbol depends on the system or context in which it is used.

- 地図の記号の意味を調べました。
  (ちずのきごうのいみをしらべました。) - I looked up the meanings of the map symbols.
- 正しい答えの記号を選んでください。
  (ただしいこたえのきごうをえらんでください。) - Please choose the symbol for the correct answer.

## How the kanji combine

[記](../kanji/記.md) concerns recording or marking, and [号](../kanji/号.md) a sign or designation. Together they suggest a mark that represents something. The on readings are キ + ゴウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[印](印.md) (しるし) also means a mark or sign. [コード](コード.md) can concern a code or coding system, but not every code is one 記号: a program, for example, can contain many symbols. The word 記号 does not take on コード's unrelated cord or musical-chord meanings.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list includes English "code." Treat that as a contextual translation connected with signs or notation, not permission to replace コード everywhere. A 記号 can be a single mark; the larger rules for interpreting marks are a different matter.
