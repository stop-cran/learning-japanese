# 加速 (かそく) - acceleration; speeding up

## Meaning

加速 is acceleration or speeding up. 加速する can describe something accelerating or someone speeding up a process; the construction identifies the viewpoint.

- 車が急に加速しました。 (くるまがきゅうにかそくしました。) - The car suddenly accelerated.
- 新製品の開発を加速します。 (しんせいひんのかいはつをかそくします。) - We will speed up development of the new product.

車が加速する describes the car's change. 開発を加速する describes speeding development up.

## How the kanji combine

[加](../kanji/加.md) contributes adding or increasing; [速](../kanji/速.md) contributes speed. Together they express adding speed. The on readings combine as カ + ソク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[加速度](加速度.md) (かそくど) names acceleration as a rate of change of velocity, especially a measured physical quantity. 減速 (げんそく), deceleration or slowing down, contrasts with the everyday speeding-up use.

## Distinctive meaning

Being fast and becoming faster are different claims: [速度](速度.md) (そくど) is speed or velocity, whereas 加速 in these examples concerns changing it. The practical contrast with 減速 does not mean that the physical quantity 加速度 only exists when speed increases.
