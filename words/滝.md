# 滝 (たき) - waterfall

## Meaning

滝 is a waterfall: water dropping over a steep fall in its course.

- 山の中に大きな滝があります。
  (やまのなかにおおきなたきがあります。) - There is a large waterfall in the mountains.
- 滝の近くで写真を撮りました。
  (たきのちかくでしゃしんをとりました。) - I took a photograph near the waterfall.

滝の近く identifies the nearby place; で marks where the action happens.
The word is a noun, not a verb meaning fall.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 滝 writes the native noun たき, so there is no compound
to split. Its water-related meaning is useful for recognition, but does
not by itself establish the character's historical construction.
瀧 is marked an outdated kanji form in JMdict.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[川](川.md) (かわ) is a river; it does not require a waterfall.
[流れ](流れ.md) (ながれ) concerns a flow or current more broadly.
A waterfall is a feature of the watercourse, not another name for every
river or current.

## Distinctive meaning

Falling water, rather than any fast-flowing stretch, is the practical
modern focus here. JMdict also records an archaic rapids sense; this article
does not teach that as an unrestricted modern replacement for rapids.
There is no single natural antonym for all waterfalls.
