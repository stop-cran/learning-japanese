# 拡充 (かくじゅう) - expansion; strengthening

## Meaning

拡充 is expanding something while making it more substantial or better equipped. It is useful for facilities, services, staffing, or organizational provision. 拡充する is transitive: what is expanded takes を.

- 図書館の設備を拡充しました。 (としょかんのせつびをかくじゅうしました。) - We expanded and improved the library's facilities.
- 子育て支援のサービスを拡充する予定です。 (こそだてしえんのサービスをかくじゅうするよていです。) - We plan to expand the services supporting child-rearing.

## How the kanji combine

拡 contributes widening, and 充 filling or making sufficient. Their related contributions suggest both broader provision and greater substance. The on readings are カク + ジュウ. This is a meaning aid, not a history of the characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[拡張](拡張.md) (かくちょう) emphasizes extending range or scale, and [拡大](拡大.md) (かくだい) enlargement. These overlap with 拡充; the emphasis on making provision more substantial is a useful tendency, not an exclusive division of objects. 縮小 (しゅくしょう), scaling down, can contrast in organizational scale.

## Distinctive meaning

Simply magnifying a photograph is normally described with 拡大, not by treating 拡充 as a general word for every size increase. Conversely, expanding support services may involve their range, personnel, and resources rather than a physically larger building.
