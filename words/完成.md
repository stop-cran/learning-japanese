# 完成 (かんせい) — completion

## Meaning

完成 names completion: a work or product has reached its finished form.
X が完成する describes X becoming complete.
X を完成させる explicitly presents someone bringing X to completion.

- 絵が完成しました。 (えがかんせいしました。) — The picture is finished.
- 今日、この絵を完成させます。 (きょう、このえをかんせいさせます。) — I will finish this picture today.

## How the kanji combine

[完](../kanji/完.md) contributes completeness and [成](../kanji/成.md) contributes becoming or reaching a state.
Together they express reaching completion, not merely stopping work partway through.
Both use on readings: カン + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

完了 (かんりょう) also means completion or conclusion. A useful learning contrast is a finished picture with 完成 and a completed procedure with 完了.
This is a contextual distinction, not a rule that their uses never overlap.
未完成 (みかんせい) means incomplete or unfinished.

## Distinctive meaning

完成 emphasizes a finished result. Simply putting down a brush does not establish that the picture is complete.
Nor does completion guarantee flawlessness: 完全 (かんぜん), "complete; perfect," is a related but different word.
Keep the finished work distinct from a judgment that it has no defects.
