# 早口 (はやくち) - rapid speech; fast talking

## Meaning

早口 is rapid speech or fast talking. It is a noun, with constructions
such as 早口で話す (はやくちではなす), "speak quickly," and
早口の人 (はやくちのひと), "a fast talker."

- 彼は早口で話します。 (かれははやくちではなします。) - He speaks quickly.
- 早口なので、聞き取れませんでした。 (はやくちなので、ききとれませんでした。) - Because the speech was fast, I couldn't catch it.

The dictionary also records はやぐち. The primary はやくち is not an
error that must be changed to a voiced ぐ.

## How the kanji combine

[早](../kanji/早.md) supplies はや, with a quickness association here,
and [口](../kanji/口.md) supplies くち, "mouth."
The native compound identifies a manner of speaking.
速口 is another listed spelling of the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[速い](速い.md) (はやい), "fast," can describe an action's speed more
broadly. 早口 specifically concerns speech, not a fast train.
ゆっくり話す (ゆっくりはなす), "speak slowly," gives a useful
contrast in a conversation.

## Distinctive meaning

The written 早 does not make this "speaking early in the morning."
Nor does the word mean that the speaker is necessarily persuasive,
dishonest, or fluent. English "fast talker" can suggest manipulation;
the Japanese noun by itself describes speed.
早口言葉 (はやくちことば) means a tongue twister.
