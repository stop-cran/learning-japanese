# 中古 (ちゅうこ) — second-hand, used

## Meaning

中古 refers to things that are used and resold: 中古車 ちゅうこしゃ (used car), 中古品 ちゅうこひん (second-hand goods), 中古住宅 ちゅうこじゅうたく (a pre-owned house).
In history 中古 is also the middle ancient period, especially the Heian period (中古文学 ちゅうこぶんがく).

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) (middle) plus [古](../kanji/古.md) (old): "the middle of old", neither new nor ancient. On readings チュウ + コ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: セカンドハンド (second-hand), 古い (old, [古い](古い.md)).
- Antonym: 新品 しんぴん (brand-new goods).

## Distinctive meaning

中古 is a neutral market term for used goods. 古い describes age, while 中古 describes the condition of having had a previous owner.
