# 中古 (ちゅうこ) — second-hand, used

## Meaning

中古 refers to things that are used and resold: 中古車 ちゅうこしゃ (used car), 中古品 ちゅうこひん (second-hand goods), 中古住宅 ちゅうこじゅうたく (a pre-owned house).
In history 中古 is also the middle ancient period, especially the Heian period (中古文学 ちゅうこぶんがく).

For used goods, it modifies a noun with の or appears directly in an established
compound such as 中古車.

- 中古の自転車を買いました。 (ちゅうこのじてんしゃをかいました。) - I bought a second-hand bicycle.
- この車は中古ですが、状態がいいです。 (このくるまはちゅうこですが、じょうたいがいいです。) - This car is used, but it is in good condition.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) contributes "middle" and [古](../kanji/古.md) "old."
Those components fit the historical-period sense, but do not define a fixed
middle age for a used product. Learn the modern second-hand meaning as a whole.
The on readings are チュウ + コ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: セカンドハンド (second-hand), 古い (old, [古い](古い.md)).
- Antonym: 新品 しんぴん (brand-new goods).

## Distinctive meaning

中古 is a neutral market term for used goods. 古い describes age, while 中古 describes the condition of having had a previous owner.
