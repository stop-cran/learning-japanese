# 診察 (しんさつ) - medical examination; consultation

## Meaning

診察 is a medical examination or consultation in which a clinician assesses a patient.
As a transitive する verb, the patient takes を.
From the patient's viewpoint, 診察を受ける (しんさつをうける) means to be examined.

- 医師が患者を診察しています。
  (いしがかんじゃをしんさつしています。) - The doctor is examining a patient.
- 病院で診察を受けました。
  (びょういんでしんさつをうけました。) - I had a medical consultation at the hospital.

診察室 (しんさつしつ) is an examination or consulting room.
診察時間 (しんさつじかん) can indicate consultation hours on a clinic's notice.

## How the kanji combine

診 contributes medical examination, and [察](../kanji/察.md) contributes observation
or investigation. The second character reinforces the careful examination expressed by the first.
The on readings are シン + サツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[検査](検査.md) (けんさ) is an examination, inspection, or test, including a laboratory test.
診察 specifically frames a clinician's examination of a patient.
Treatment may follow, but examining and treating are not identical actions.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the roles clear: a doctor 診察する the patient, while the patient 診察を受ける.
診察 can include asking about symptoms as well as examining the body; the word does not
by itself specify which tests or procedures are performed. It is not an academic examination.
