# どんなに (どんなに) - how much; no matter how

## Meaning

どんなに is an adverb about degree: "how much" or "to what extent." With a concessive ending such as ても or でも, it means "no matter how."

- どんなに心配したか、わかりますか。 (どんなにしんぱいしたか、わかりますか。) - Do you realize how worried I was?
- どんなに忙しくても、朝ご飯は食べます。 (どんなにいそがしくても、あさごはんはたべます。) - No matter how busy I am, I eat breakfast.

Use an い-adjective's くても form, a verb's ても form, or a な-adjective with でも: どんなに便利でも (どんなにべんりでも), "however convenient it is."

## How the kanji combine

There are no written kanji here. The expression is related to [どんな](どんな.md), "what kind of," but どんなに functions adverbially to ask or emphasize degree.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どれほど also concerns degree. [いくら](いくら.md) can mean "how much" and also appears with ても in concessive expressions.

[どうして](どうして.md), "why," asks for a reason, not a degree.

## Distinctive meaning

どんなに by itself does not supply "no matter." The following grammar creates that interpretation. Compare how worried someone was, with a clause in which the result holds despite any degree of busyness. It is not a general replacement for English "how" when asking the method of doing something.
