# 作製 (さくせい) - manufacture; production

## Meaning

作製 is making or producing something, such as an object, model, or drawing.
It forms the transitive verb 作製する: the thing produced takes を.

- 授業で町の模型を作製しました。 (じゅぎょうでまちのもけいをさくせいしました。) - We made a model of the town in class.
- 新しい部品の設計図を作製しています。 (あたらしいぶひんのせっけいずをさくせいしています。) - We are producing a design drawing for the new part.

Forms include 作製します, 作製した, and 作製して.

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) contributes making, and 製 contributes manufacture or production.
The near-related components reinforce making a product. Both use on readings: サク + セイ.
The reading is the same as 作成, but the second character is different.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作成](作成.md) is useful for drawing up reports and plans or creating digital records.
[製造](製造.md) (せいぞう) concerns manufacture, and [生産](生産.md) (せいさん) frames things as output or production.
[作る](作る.md) (つくる) is broader and useful in ordinary conversation.

## Distinctive meaning

作製 is not restricted to mass production in a factory: a classroom model also fits.
Nor does it mean only solid three-dimensional objects; the drawing example matters.
The contrast with 作成 is a practical usage tendency, not proof that every noun permits exactly one spelling.
The two spellings do not identify different pronunciations.
