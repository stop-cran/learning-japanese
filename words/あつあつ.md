# あつあつ — piping hot; passionately in love

## Meaning

Very hot, especially food just cooked or served. Figuratively, it describes a couple visibly in love.

- あつあつのスープで舌（した）をやけどした。 — I burned my tongue on the piping-hot soup.
- このたこ焼きは、あつあつのうちに食べてね。 — Eat these takoyaki while they are piping hot.
- あの二人は、結婚して十年たってもあつあつだ。 — Those two are still very much in love after ten years of marriage.

## How the kanji combine

A native expressive repetition of あつ, associated with 熱い（あつい, hot）. It is also written 熱々 or 熱熱; this card uses kana. This describes the visible word formation, not a reconstructed historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ほかほか](ほかほか.md) foregrounds inviting warmth, whereas あつあつ may be hot enough to burn. [ぽかぽか](ぽかぽか.md) is pleasant surrounding or bodily warmth. [ひんやり](ひんやり.md) is cool to the senses.

## Distinctive meaning

あつあつの料理（りょうり）and あつあつの二人 are useful noun-modifying patterns; あつあつだ also works. Do not mechanically turn it into あつあつする to mean “be hot.” The couple sense is informal and can be teasing. It is not the separate dictionary word 厚々, “thick/dense.”
