# 講義 (こうぎ) - lecture

## Meaning

講義 is a lecture: a structured explanation of a subject, especially in higher education. 講義する means to lecture or explain a topic.

大学で歴史の講義を受けています。 (だいがくでれきしのこうぎをうけています。) - I am attending history lectures at university.

先生が日本文学について講義しました。 (せんせいがにほんぶんがくについてこうぎしました。) - The teacher lectured on Japanese literature.

講義を受ける describes the learner's attendance; 講義する describes the lecturer's action.

## How the kanji combine

講 concerns lecturing or explaining, and 義 can concern meaning or principles. The on readings コウ and ギ form こうぎ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

授業 (じゅぎょう) is a class or lesson more broadly, including activities other than a lecture. 講演 (こうえん) is a lecture or address to an audience, often a public event.

## Distinctive meaning

講義 emphasizes the explanatory teaching format, not just any gathering at school. A practical exercise or discussion class can be a 授業 without primarily being a 講義. Be careful with the homophone 抗議 (こうぎ), "protest," which is a different word.
