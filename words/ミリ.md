# ミリ -- milli-; millimeter

## Meaning

As a metric prefix, ミリ means one thousandth, or 10^-3, of the named
unit. As a shortened noun in a length context, it means millimeter,
short for ミリメートル.

- 厚さは三ミリです。 (あつさはさんミリです。) -- The thickness is three millimeters.
- 1ミリグラム = 0.001グラム. -- One milligram is one thousandth of a gram.

The standalone length abbreviation does not make ミリグラム a unit
of length. The complete unit name determines what is measured.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [センチ](センチ.md) abbreviates centimeter or supplies centi-, one hundredth; it is not the same scale as ミリ.
- [メートル](メートル.md) is the meter: 1メートル = 1,000ミリメートル.
- [グラム](グラム.md) is the gram; attaching ミリ changes the scale, not mass into length.
- [キロ](キロ.md) supplies kilo-, one thousand, in metric compounds; it is not one thousandth.

## Distinctive meaning

The prefix and the abbreviation are related but not identical uses.
三ミリ on a ruler means 3 mm; a product's ミリグラム label concerns
mass. JMdict marks a French source for the prefix sense.
