# 名人 (めいじん) - master; expert

## Meaning

名人 is a noun for someone exceptionally skilled at an activity: a master or expert. Use the activity + の名人 to identify the field.

- 祖父は釣りの名人です。 (そふはつりのめいじんです。) - My grandfather is an expert angler.
- 彼女は料理の名人です。 (かのじょはりょうりのめいじんです。) - She is a master cook.

These sentences praise skill; they do not assert that the person holds an official certificate. 名人 also names the specific professional Meijin title in games such as shogi and go.

## How the kanji combine

[名](../kanji/名.md) contributes reputation or distinction, and [人](../kanji/人.md) identifies a person. Together they suggest a person distinguished by mastery. The reading is メイ + ジン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

専門家 (せんもんか) is a specialist or expert in a field; 名人 emphasizes outstanding skill. [上手](上手.md) (じょうず) describes being good at something, while 名人 names the person. 初心者 (しょしんしゃ), "beginner," gives a practical contrast, not an insult or a permanent category.

## Distinctive meaning

English "master" can mean an owner, employer, or degree holder; those meanings are not supplied by 名人. Likewise, a person praised as a 料理の名人 does not thereby hold a competitive Meijin title. When the word appears in a game report, distinguish its official-title use from ordinary praise by checking the event and context.
