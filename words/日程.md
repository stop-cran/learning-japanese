# 日程 (にってい) - schedule; agenda

## Meaning

日程 is a noun for the schedule or program of activities, including
the dates selected for an event or trip. It can concern a single day
or a plan extending over several days.

- 出張の日程を決めました。 (しゅっちょうのにっていをきめました。) - We decided the schedule for the business trip.
- 都合が合わないので、日程を変更します。 (つごうがあわないので、にっていをへんこうします。) - The dates do not work for us, so we will change the schedule.

Useful expressions are 日程を組む (にっていをくむ), "draw up a schedule,"
and 日程を調整する (にっていをちょうせいする), "coordinate the schedule/dates."

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) contributes days, and [程](../kanji/程.md) contributes
a course or extent. Together they name the planned course of activities
over time, rather than an arithmetic number of days.
Learn the on-reading compound にってい, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[スケジュール](スケジュール.md) overlaps strongly.
[予定](予定.md) (よてい) names plans or a schedule more broadly, while
[計画](計画.md) (けいかく) can include a plan's method and purpose.
[日時](日時.md) (にちじ) focuses on when an event occurs.
These distinctions do not prevent overlap when arranging a meeting.

## Distinctive meaning

日程 is not necessarily an hour-by-hour [時間割](時間割.md) (じかんわり),
nor only a collection of dates without activities.
English "agenda" can also mean a list of discussion topics; that English
meaning should not be transferred automatically to every use of 日程.
A [カレンダー](カレンダー.md) displays dates and can record a 日程,
but the calendar object and the planned activities are not identical.
