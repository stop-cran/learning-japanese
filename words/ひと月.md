# ひと月 (ひとつき) - one month

## Meaning

ひと月 means a period of one month. It expresses a duration or interval, not the name of January.

- 日本にひと月いました。 (にほんにひとつきいました。) - I stayed in Japan for one month.
- ひと月に一度、祖母に会います。 (ひとつきにいちど、そぼにあいます。) - I see my grandmother once a month.

In the second example, に establishes the interval over which the frequency is counted.

## How the kanji combine

ひと is the native one-element, and [月](../kanji/月.md) is read つき, "month." The spelling 一月 is also possible for ひとつき, but writing ひと月 makes the intended reading easier to recognize.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一か月 (いっかげつ) is another ordinary way to say "one month." 一月 read いちがつ means January; it is not the reading selected for this card.

## Distinctive meaning

ひと月 does not always mean exactly thirty days: calendar months vary in length, and context determines how the interval is reckoned. If a precise number of days matters, state that number rather than treating "month" as a fixed day count.
