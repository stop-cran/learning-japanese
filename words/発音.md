# 発音 (はつおん) — pronunciation

## Meaning

発音 is pronunciation: how a word or sound is spoken. It also forms the transitive verb 発音する, "pronounce."

- 日本語の発音を練習しています。 (にほんごのはつおんをれんしゅうしています。) — I'm practicing Japanese pronunciation.
- この言葉はどう発音しますか。 (このことばはどうはつおんしますか。) — How do you pronounce this word?

The word or sound being pronounced takes を: 名前を発音する (なまえをはつおんする), "pronounce a name."

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) suggests producing or sending out, and [音](../kanji/音.md) means sound. The on readings combine as ハツ + オン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

読み方 (よみかた) is how something is read, including the reading assigned to written characters. 発音 focuses on spoken sound. 意味 (いみ), "meaning," is another aspect of a word, not its pronunciation.

## Distinctive meaning

Knowing a word's kana reading and producing its sounds clearly are related but different skills. 発音 can concern vowels, consonants, and other spoken details; it is not a name for the word's spelling or English translation.
