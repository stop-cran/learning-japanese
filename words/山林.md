# 山林 (さんりん) - mountain forest; woodland

## Meaning

山林 means mountain forest, woodland, or forest land. It can also refer collectively to mountains and forests. It is a noun, useful both for landscapes and for discussing land.

- この地域には広い山林があります。 (このちいきにはひろいさんりんがあります。) - This region has extensive woodland.
- 山林を守るために、木の状態を調べています。 (さんりんをまもるために、きのじょうたいをしらべています。) - We are checking the condition of the trees to protect the forest.

## How the kanji combine

[山](../kanji/山.md) contributes mountain, and [林](../kanji/林.md) contributes woods or forest. The compound can bring mountain woodland into focus or combine mountains and forests as a landscape. The on readings are サン + リン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[林](林.md) はやし and [森](森.md) もり also name wooded areas. 森林 (しんりん) is another forest term. These words overlap; a simple rule assigning each an exact number or height of trees would be misleading.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not turn the character 山 into a universal high-altitude requirement. 山林 also occurs as a land category for woodland. Conversely, not every [山](山.md) やま, mountain, is forested. Let the landscape or land-use context guide the translation.
