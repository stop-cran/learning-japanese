# 空っぽ (からっぽ) - empty; hollow

## Meaning

空っぽ describes something empty, with no contents. Use 空っぽだ as a predicate and 空っぽの before a noun: 空っぽの箱 (からっぽのはこ), "an empty box."

- 箱の中は空っぽでした。 (はこのなかはからっぽでした。) - The inside of the box was empty.
- 空っぽの瓶を棚に戻しました。 (からっぽのびんをたなにもどしました。) - I put the empty bottle back on the shelf.

空っぽになる means "become empty."

## How the kanji combine

[空](../kanji/空.md) supplies the emptiness idea. Read the complete word からっぽ, including the small っ before ぽ; do not use そら or クウ here. Related [空](空.md) read から can also mean empty.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[からから](からから.md) has an empty or hollow use as well as a dryness use. [空き](空き.md) (あき) concerns available space or time, while [空く](空く.md) describes becoming empty or available. Their grammar and ordinary uses differ.

## Distinctive meaning

An empty bottle is 空っぽ even if nobody is offering it for use. An available appointment slot is normally discussed as 空き rather than as a container with no contents. For a filled container, いっぱい, "full," supplies a useful contextual contrast.
