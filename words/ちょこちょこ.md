# ちょこちょこ — small quick steps; every so often

## Meaning

Small, quick movements, or doing something frequently in little installments.

- 小鳥（ことり）が庭（にわ）をちょこちょこ歩いている。 — A little bird is taking quick small steps around the garden.
- 近くに住む友達が、ちょこちょこ遊びに来る。 — My friend who lives nearby often drops in.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[よちよち](よちよち.md) overlaps in a toddler's small steps but typically suggests uncertain balance. [ちびちび](ちびちび.md) focuses on small amounts, not necessarily frequent visits.

## Distinctive meaning

ちょこちょこ来る（くる）does not mean every visitor walks with tiny steps. The frequency use can mean “now and then” or “quite often” according to context, not a fixed number of visits.
