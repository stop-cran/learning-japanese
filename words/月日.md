# 月日 (つきひ／がっぴ) — time passing; the date

## Meaning

月日 has two readings and two meanings:

- **つきひ**: time, the months and days that pass, e.g. 月日が経つ つきひがたつ (time passes), 月日の流れ (the flow of time).
- **がっぴ**: a calendar date, month and day. Seen on forms: 生年月日 せいねんがっぴ (date of birth), 年月日 ねんがっぴ.

## How the kanji combine

[月](../kanji/月.md) (month, moon) plus [日](../kanji/日.md) (day, sun): "months and days", a coordinated pair that stands for the whole span of time or a date.
In the date reading がっぴ, 月 takes the on reading ガツ (as in ガッ + ひ) and 日 takes the kun reading ひ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close for time: 歳月 さいげつ (years, literary), 時間 じかん, 年月 ねんげつ／としつき (years and months).
- Close for date: 日付 ひづけ, 日にち ひにち.
- Contrast with 日月 じつげつ (the sun and the moon); the order of kanji changes the meaning.

## Distinctive meaning

月日 emphasises months and years going by, often with emotion: 月日が経つのは早い. 時間 (time) is neutral and counts hours.
