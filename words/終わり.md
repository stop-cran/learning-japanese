# 終わり (おわり) — end; conclusion

## Meaning

終わり is the end or conclusion of an activity, story, period, or other continuing thing. Use noun + の終わり for "the end of."

- 授業の終わりに質問しました。 (じゅぎょうのおわりにしつもんしました。) — I asked a question at the end of class.
- 今日はこれで終わりです。 (きょうはこれでおわりです。) — That's all for today.

## How the kanji combine

[終](../kanji/終.md) carries the ending idea. 終わり is the noun related to 終わる (おわる), "come to an end." The kana わり belong to the established noun spelling.

The source spelling 終 with reading おわり omits the okurigana and is marked irregular in JMdict.
This article retains 終わり as the ordinary teaching spelling for that same noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

始まり (はじまり), "beginning," is the direct contrast. 最後 (さいご), "last; final," often identifies the last item or stage, whereas 終わり names the endpoint.

## Distinctive meaning

終わりです announces an end; it is not the same grammatical form as 終わりました (おわりました), "it ended." To say you finished a task yourself, 終える (おえる) or 終わらせる (おわらせる) makes the action explicit.
