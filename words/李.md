# 李 (すもも) - Japanese plum tree; Japanese plum

## Meaning

李 names the Japanese plum tree and its fruit, *Prunus salicina*, in the botanical sense selected here.

- 李を二つ買いました。 (すももをふたつかいました。) - I bought two Japanese plums.
- 李の花が咲きました。 (すもものはながさきました。) - The plum blossoms have opened.

李の木 (すもものき) specifies the tree, and 李の実 (すもものみ) specifies the fruit. The noun does not require a separate plural form.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [李](../kanji/李.md) writes the whole native noun すもも. JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana: すもも and スモモ are alternatives to the kanji spelling.

酢桃 and 酸桃 are dictionary-recognized but rare kanji spellings of the same noun. Their [桃](../kanji/桃.md) does not make すもも another pronunciation of the standalone peach noun もも. The character 李 has the on reading リ in compounds; it does not replace すもも here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

プラム is a loanword for plum. It can overlap with すもも, but neither an English "plum" label nor the loanword alone fixes a species.

[梅](梅.md) (うめ), *Prunus mume*, is also called Japanese plum in English, but is a different plant. [桃](桃.md) (もも), peach, and [杏](杏.md) (あんず), apricot, are related fruit-tree names, not synonyms. [果物](果物.md) (くだもの), fruit as food, is broader. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Botanical scope depends on context. The reference encyclopedia also groups European plums with Japanese plums under the broader すもも category. ヨーロッパ李 (ヨーロッパすもも) specifically names the European plum; do not read every qualified plant name as the species selected by this page's primary definition.

The proverb 李下に冠を正さず (りかにかんむりをたださず) warns against actions that invite suspicion. Its written 李 is read リ in 李下, not すもも; that does not turn the native plant noun into a proverb or a general word for suspicion.
