# 今に (いまに) - before long; even now

## Meaning

今に is an adverb that can point forward: "before long," "one of these days," or "eventually." It often accompanies a prediction or a confident expectation.

- 今にきっと分かるよ。 (いまにきっとわかるよ。) - You will understand before long.
- この伝統は今に伝わっている。 (このでんとうはいまにつたわっている。) - This tradition has been handed down to the present day.

The second construction looks from the past toward the present: 今に伝わる means to survive or be transmitted down to today. It is not a prediction that the tradition will begin soon.

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) means "now"; the kana に completes this established adverbial expression. Learn the whole expression, since the forward-looking use cannot be translated by replacing each piece separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[今](今.md) by itself identifies the present. すぐ, "soon; immediately," can make a closer time connection, while 今に can leave the interval unspecified. [まだ](まだ.md), "still; not yet," overlaps with continuing situations but has different constructions.

## Distinctive meaning

The predicate determines the time direction: 今に分かる predicts understanding, while 今に伝わる describes transmission to the present. Do not confuse 今に with 今にも, used for something that seems about to happen.
