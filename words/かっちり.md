# かっちり — tightly; precisely fitted

## Meaning

かっちり describes a secure, exact fit or a neat, precise arrangement.

- ケースのふたがかっちり閉まった。
  — The case lid closed tightly.
- 部品をかっちり組み合わせた。
  — The parts were fitted together precisely.

## Synonyms and antonyms

かっちり emphasizes a snug or exact fit. It does not by itself describe a person's build or
physical strength.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can describe something fitting closely or being arranged with precision. Context
distinguishes a physical fit from exactness in a plan or schedule.
