# バター - butter

## Meaning

バター is butter, used as a spread or cooking ingredient. It names the food, not the action of spreading it.

- パンにバターを塗りました。 (パンにバターをぬりました。) - I spread butter on the bread.
- このケーキにはバターを使います。 (このケーキにはバターをつかいます。) - We use butter in this cake.

In the first example, the bread takes に and the substance applied takes を.

## How the kanji combine

The word is a katakana borrowing corresponding to English "butter." Keep the final long-vowel mark: バター. It is not written with kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

マーガリン means margarine, a related but different product. 油 (あぶら) means oil or fat more broadly and is not an exact substitute for バター in a recipe.

## Distinctive meaning

バター is normally treated as a substance rather than a count of individual objects. If a recipe specifies an amount, give a measure such as グラム. A package or portion may be counted separately, but that does not change the food's name.
