# 集合 (しゅうごう) - gathering; mathematical set

## Meaning

集合 is meeting up or assembling in one place. In the practical intransitive pattern **人が場所に集合する**, the people gather at the place. A notice such as 九時集合 (くじしゅうごう) means "meet at nine."

- 明日の朝、駅前に集合してください。
  (あしたのあさ、えきまえにしゅうごうしてください。) - Please meet in front of the station tomorrow morning.
- 集合時間は九時です。
  (しゅうごうじかんはくじです。) - The meeting-up time is nine.

In mathematics, 集合 is a set: a collection of elements with clearly determined membership. For example, {1, 2, 3} is a set with three elements.

## How the kanji combine

[集](../kanji/集.md) contributes gathering, and [合](../kanji/合.md) joining together. Their related meanings reinforce coming together. The on readings are シュウ + ゴウ, with long vowels in both parts.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[集まる](集まる.md) (あつまる) also means gathering. [集会](集会.md) (しゅうかい) names a shared-purpose gathering or meeting; 集合 can instead direct participants to assemble before an event. The words overlap, but the assembly instruction need not describe a discussion.

[解散](解散.md) (かいさん), dispersing or breaking up, gives the opposite stage of a group activity.

## Distinctive meaning

The mathematical sense is not limited to a package of things sold together. [セット](セット.md) also has a general "set" meaning, but the context must establish mathematics. Conversely, a gathering instruction does not become a mathematical statement merely because the people form a group.
