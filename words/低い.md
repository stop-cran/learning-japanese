# 低い (ひくい) — low; short in height

## Meaning

低い is an い-adjective describing a low position, level, or height. For a person's short stature, use 背が低い (せがひくい).

- この机は低いです。 (このつくえはひくいです。) — This desk is low.
- 弟は私より背が低いです。 (おとうとはわたしよりせがひくいです。) — My younger brother is shorter than I am.

## How the kanji combine

低 supplies ひく, followed by the adjective ending い. Its negative is 低くない (ひくくない), and its past is 低かった (ひくかった). The repeated く in the negative is correct.

## Synonyms and antonyms

高い (たかい), "high; tall," is the main contrast. 短い (みじかい), "short," concerns length or duration rather than height above the ground. 小さい (ちいさい) concerns size more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

低い can describe temperature, rank, probability, or voice pitch as well as physical height. 低い声 (ひくいこえ) can mean a low-pitched voice; it does not simply mean a short voice. For prices, 安い (やすい) is the ordinary adjective "cheap," while 価格が低い (かかくがひくい) describes the price level as low.
