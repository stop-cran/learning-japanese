# 公立 (こうりつ) — public institution

## Meaning

公立 usually describes an institution established and maintained by a local public body, such as a prefecture or city.
Common examples are schools and libraries.
Use 公立の before an ordinary noun phrase: 公立の学校 (こうりつのがっこう), "a public school."

- 妹は公立の学校に通っています。 (いもうとはこうりつのがっこうにかよっています。) — My younger sister attends a public school.
- この図書館は公立です。 (このとしょかんはこうりつです。) — This library is a public institution.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md) (public; official) qualifies [立](../kanji/立.md), which has an establishment sense here.
The combination concerns being publicly established, not physically "standing in public."
The on readings are コウ + リツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

私立 (しりつ) means a privately established institution and provides the useful contrast.
国立 (こくりつ) is the national-government category, normally distinguished from local-government 公立
in the three-way classification 国立・公立・私立.
Learn the institution's actual category rather than treating these labels as freely interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

公立 concerns an institution's establishment and public administration, not simply whether visitors may enter.
A privately run place can be open to everyone without becoming 公立.
