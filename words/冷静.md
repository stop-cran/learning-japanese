# 冷静 (れいせい) - calm; composed

## Meaning

冷静 is a noun for composure and a na-adjective describing someone or something as calm
and clear-headed. Use 冷静な before a noun, 冷静に before an action, and 冷静です as a predicate.

- 問題を冷静に考えましょう。 (もんだいをれいせいにかんがえましょう。) - Let's think about the problem calmly.
- 彼女は事故の後も冷静でした。 (かのじょはじこのあともれいせいでした。) - She remained composed even after the accident.

冷静な判断 (れいせいなはんだん) means "a calm, clear-headed judgment."
冷静になる means "become calm; regain composure."

## How the kanji combine

[冷](../kanji/冷.md) contributes coolness, and [静](../kanji/静.md) contributes quietness or stillness.
The two qualities combine figuratively in mental composure.
The on readings are レイ + セイ. The word does not describe low body temperature.

## Synonyms and antonyms

落ち着く (おちつく), "calm down; settle," overlaps in descriptions of composure but is a verb.
[冷たい](冷たい.md) (つめたい) can describe physical coldness or an unfriendly manner;
冷静 does not by itself mean either.
Panic or agitation contrasts with composure, depending on the situation.

## Distinctive meaning

冷静 emphasizes maintaining perspective and judgment rather than being controlled by an immediate
emotional reaction. A person can be concerned and compassionate while remaining 冷静.
Conversely, the word alone does not guarantee that their judgment is correct.
Keep the な and に forms distinct: 冷静な人 describes a person, while 冷静に話す describes speaking calmly.
