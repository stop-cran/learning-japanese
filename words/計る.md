# 計る (はかる) - measure; time; gauge

## Meaning

計る is a transitive godan verb meaning to measure, time, weigh, or gauge. The quantity being measured takes を.

- 料理にかかる時間を計りました。 (りょうりにかかるじかんをはかりました。) - I measured how long the cooking took.
- ストップウォッチで走る時間を計ります。 (ストップウォッチではしるじかんをはかります。) - I time the run with a stopwatch.

The instrument takes で. Forms include 計ります, 計った, and 計らない. The examples here focus on measuring elapsed time, not counting separate objects.

## How the kanji combine

[計](../kanji/計.md) contributes calculation or measurement. Here its native reading is はか, followed by る. The same dictionary entry includes 測る and 量る, also read はかる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

測る is useful for measurements such as dimensions or temperature; 量る is associated with amounts or weight. 計る suits the timing examples above. These are helpful spelling associations, not absolute divisions imposed by the dictionary. [数える](数える.md) (かぞえる) counts items rather than necessarily measuring a continuous quantity.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer that an English gloss like "weigh" creates a separate pronunciation or that every measurement must use 計る. Learn the preferred spelling with a concrete object and context. In the inspected entry, 計る, 測る, and 量る have no hard sense restrictions separating them; a rigid one-spelling-per-quantity rule would overstate the evidence.
