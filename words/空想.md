# 空想 (くうそう) - daydream; fantasy; imagined vision

## Meaning

空想 is a daydream, fantasy or imagined vision: something pictured in the mind rather than established as reality. 空想する means imagine or fantasize; the imagined content can take を.

- 子どものころ、月での生活を空想していました。 (こどものころ、つきでのせいかつをくうそうしていました。) - As a child, I used to imagine life on the moon.
- それは現実ではなく、私の空想です。 (それはげんじつではなく、わたしのくうそうです。) - That is not reality; it is my fantasy.

## How the kanji combine

[空](../kanji/空.md), empty or without substance here, qualifies [想](../kanji/想.md), thought or imagining. It helps suggest imagining without an established real object. The on readings are クウ + ソウ. This is a modern meaning aid, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[想像](想像.md) (そうぞう) is broader imagination: one can imagine a real situation not directly seen. 空想 more readily highlights fantasy or an imagined world. [夢](夢.md) (ゆめ) overlaps as a dream or aspiration, but 空想 does not require sleep. [現実](現実.md) (げんじつ), reality, supplies the contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

English "vision" here is an imagined scene or prospect, not eyesight. Nor does 空想 by itself diagnose a medical delusion or mean that an imagined future is logically impossible. 空想の世界 (くうそうのせかい) is "a world of fantasy."
