# こないだ - the other day; recently

## Meaning

こないだ refers to a recent past occasion: "the other day" or "recently." It is a shortened spoken form of このあいだ.

- こないだ、駅で先生に会ったよ。 (こないだ、えきでせんせいにあったよ。) - I ran into my teacher at the station the other day.
- こないだの話、覚えている？ (こないだのはなし、おぼえている？) - Do you remember what we talked about the other day?

It can introduce the time directly, without に. Before a noun, use の, as in こないだの話.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[このあいだ](このあいだ.md) is the fuller form. [先日](先日.md) (せんじつ) also points to a recent past occasion and suits more formal reference. [最近](最近.md) (さいきん) can cover an ongoing recent period as well as a completed event.

## Distinctive meaning

こないだ leaves the date to context; it does not mean exactly yesterday or a fixed number of days ago. It is useful in conversation, but the shortened form is not the default for a formal letter.

Do not transfer the separate このかん reading's "meanwhile" sense to this expression merely because both can be associated with この間.
