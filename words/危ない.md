# 危ない (あぶない) - dangerous; at risk

## Meaning

危ない is an i-adjective for danger or risk. It can describe something that causes danger or someone whose safety is threatened.

- ここで泳ぐのは危ないです。 (ここでおよぐのはあぶないです。) - Swimming here is dangerous.
- 危ない！車が来ます！ (あぶない！くるまがきます！) - Watch out! A car is coming!

Use 危ない道 (あぶないみち), "a dangerous road," without な. The negative is 危なくない.

## How the kanji combine

[危](../kanji/危.md) expresses danger. The written ending ない belongs to the adjective itself: 危ない is not a negative verb form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

危険 (きけん) also expresses danger but takes な before a noun. 安全 (あんぜん), "safety; safe," provides the opposite idea.

## Distinctive meaning

As a sudden warning, 危ない！ means "Look out!" It need not identify the danger explicitly. Outside immediate physical danger, context may instead suggest uncertainty or a close call.
