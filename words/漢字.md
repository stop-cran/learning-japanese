# 漢字 (かんじ) — kanji, Chinese character

## Meaning

漢字 names a Chinese character or the Chinese-derived characters used in Japanese writing. The same noun can refer to one character or to kanji collectively.

- この漢字は何と読みますか。 (このかんじはなんとよみますか。) — How do you read this kanji?
- 毎日、漢字を三つ書きます。 (まいにち、かんじをみっつかきます。) — I write three kanji every day.

## How the kanji combine

[漢](../kanji/漢.md) カン contributes "Chinese," and [字](../kanji/字.md) ジ means a written character. The first element specifies the kind of character: a modifier+head compound.

Both are on-readings: カン + ジ → かんじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 文字 もじ is broader: a written character, whether kanji, kana or another script.
- 字 じ can mean a character or handwriting; it is not limited to kanji.
- 仮名 かな refers to kana, including hiragana and katakana. It is a contrasting writing category, not an antonym in the ordinary sense.

## Distinctive meaning

漢字 names a writing unit, not necessarily a whole word. Some words use one kanji and others several; many also contain kana. Knowing a character does not automatically tell you which reading to use in every word.
