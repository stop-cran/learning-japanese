# 特売 (とくばい) - special sale; bargain sale

## Meaning

特売 is a special or bargain sale, with goods offered at reduced prices.
It is a noun and a する expression. 商品を特売する presents goods
being offered in such a sale.

- スーパーで卵の特売がありました。 (スーパーでたまごのとくばいがありました。) - The supermarket had a special sale on eggs.
- この店は週末に野菜を特売します。 (このみせはしゅうまつにやさいをとくばいします。) - This shop sells vegetables at special prices on weekends.

特売品 (とくばいひん) means goods offered in a special sale;
特売日 (とくばいび) is a sale day.

## How the kanji combine

[特](../kanji/特.md) qualifies [売](../kanji/売.md), selling:
special selling at a bargain price.
The on readings are トク + バイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[販売](販売.md) (はんばい) and [売る](売る.md) (うる) concern selling more generally.
They do not by themselves promise a price reduction.
通常価格 (つうじょうかかく), the regular price, supplies a useful price contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

A 特売 is not simply every occasion when something is for sale.
The usual bargain-sale use concerns a special price offer, often for a limited period.
It does not say that the goods are defective or that every item in the shop is discounted.
