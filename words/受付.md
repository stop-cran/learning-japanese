# 受付 (うけつけ) - reception; acceptance

## Meaning

受付 is a reception desk, the staff working there, or the act of accepting applications and requests.

- 受付で名前を書いてください。 (うけつけでなまえをかいてください。) - Please write your name at reception.
- 申し込みの受付は九時からです。 (もうしこみのうけつけはくじからです。) - Applications are accepted from nine o'clock.

In the second example, 受付 names the receiving or acceptance process, not a place. The related verb is 受け付ける (うけつける), "accept" or "handle."

## How the kanji combine

[受](../kanji/受.md) contributes receiving and [付](../kanji/付.md) attaching or handling. The noun comes from 受け付ける. The spelling 受付 especially favors the desk or staff, while 受け付け especially favors the act of acceptance. These are spelling preferences, not absolute divisions between meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

窓口 (まどぐち) is a service counter or contact point. フロント often names a hotel's front desk. 領収書 (りょうしゅうしょ) is a receipt proving payment.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "receipt" here means receiving something. Do not ask for an 受付 when you want a paper receipt after paying. 受付終了 (うけつけしゅうりょう) means that registration or acceptance has closed.
