# 役目 (やくめ) - duty; role; function

## Meaning

役目 is the duty, role, or function that someone or something has. It can concern a person's assignment or the purpose served by an object.

- 私の役目は会議の記録を取ることです。
  (わたしのやくめはかいぎのきろくをとることです。) - My role is to take the meeting minutes.
- この道具はもう役目を終えました。
  (このどうぐはもうやくめをおえました。) - This tool has already served its purpose.

役目を果たす (やくめをはたす) means to fulfill one's role or duty.

## How the kanji combine

[役](../kanji/役.md) supplies the role or duty element. [目](../kanji/目.md) is read め here; learn the whole noun rather than translating it literally as "role eye."

The reading mixes on やく with native め. Following this collection's on-first convention, it is stored as kango, with a mixed-reading tag; it is not two on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[役割](役割.md) (やくわり) and [役](役.md) (やく) overlap in role or allotted duty. [務め](務め.md) (つとめ) emphasizes a duty or responsibility, and [機能](機能.md) (きのう) can overlap when discussing what something does.

[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) can be work or a job more broadly. A 役目 need not be paid employment.

## Distinctive meaning

Context supplies whose role it is and what is expected. A role may be temporary; it need not be a permanent occupation or a legal obligation.

役目 and 役割 overlap. Do not turn usage tendencies into a rule that one is exclusively spoken and the other exclusively written.

English "function" here means a role or purpose, not the social event called [行事](行事.md) (ぎょうじ).
