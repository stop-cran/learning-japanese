# 溶かす (とかす) - melt or dissolve something

## Meaning

溶かす is a transitive verb for melting, thawing or dissolving something.
What is melted or dissolved takes を; a liquid used for dissolving can take に.

- 鍋でバターを溶かしました。
  (なべでバターをとかしました。) - I melted butter in a pan.
- お湯に砂糖を溶かします。
  (おゆにさとうをとかします。) - Dissolve sugar in hot water.

Melting turns the substance liquid; dissolving puts it into a solution.
Both use this verb. It is godan: 溶かします, 溶かした, 溶かして.

## How the kanji combine

[溶](../kanji/溶.md) contributes melting or dissolving. The native reading
is とかす, with かす in kana. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide.
JMdict also records the less usual spellings 融かす and 解かす.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[溶ける](溶ける.md) (とける) is intransitive:
バターが溶ける means the butter melts; バターを溶かす means someone melts it.
[溶く](溶く.md) (とく) overlaps when mixing or dissolving an ingredient in liquid.
固める (かためる), make solid, can contrast with melting in a suitable context.

## Distinctive meaning

Colloquially, お金を溶かす (おかねをとかす) can mean losing or squandering money,
for example through gambling. This is not literal heating and is not an
unrestricted replacement for [失う](失う.md) (うしなう), "lose."
The financial use is separate from the material examples.
