# 昨日 (きのう) — yesterday

## Meaning

昨日 is yesterday: the day before today. It can set the time of a sentence directly, without に.
With の, it describes something belonging to that day: 昨日の新聞 (きのうのしんぶん),
"yesterday's newspaper."

- 昨日、友達に会いました。 (きのう、ともだちにあいました。) — I met a friend yesterday.
- 昨日の映画は面白かったです。 (きのうのえいがはおもしろかったです。) — Yesterday's movie was interesting.

## How the kanji combine

[昨](../kanji/昨.md) contributes "previous" and [日](../kanji/日.md) contributes "day."
The meaning is transparent, but **きのう is read as a whole**: it is jukujikun, a special reading assigned
to the written word. Do not split it into a supposed き reading of 昨 and のう reading of 日.

## Synonyms and antonyms

昨日 also has the reading さくじつ, with the same basic meaning.
[昔](昔.md) (むかし), "the old days," and [最近](最近.md) (さいきん), "recently,"
are broader time expressions, not exact replacements for yesterday.

## Distinctive meaning

Yesterday fixes the reference day; the sentence still needs the appropriate verb or adjective form.
In 会いました (あいました), the verb says that meeting happened; 昨日 does not supply the verb's tense ending.
Remember the whole spoken form きのう alongside its two-character spelling.
