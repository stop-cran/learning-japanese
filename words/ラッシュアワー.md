# ラッシュアワー - rush hour

## Meaning

ラッシュアワー is rush hour: the period when many people travel at once,
especially the morning or evening commute. It is a noun.

- ラッシュアワーの電車はとても混んでいます。 (ラッシュアワーのでんしゃはとてもこんでいます。) - The trains are very crowded during rush hour.
- ラッシュアワーを避けて出発しました。 (ラッシュアワーをさけてしゅっぱつしました。) - We set out outside rush hour to avoid the crowds.

The spelling ラッシュ・アワー represents the same expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通勤](通勤.md) (つうきん) is commuting, not the crowded period itself.
[混雑](混雑.md) (こんざつ) is congestion or crowding;
[渋滞](渋滞.md) (じゅうたい) can describe traffic congestion.
These conditions can occur outside rush hour too.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn a time expression such as ラッシュアワーに, "during rush hour."
The word does not mean every traffic jam or every journey to work.
It is a borrowed noun, not an instruction to hurry.
