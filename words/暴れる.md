# 暴れる (あばれる) - act violently; thrash about

## Meaning

暴れる means to act violently or wildly, rage, or struggle physically. It can
describe a person behaving uncontrollably or an animal thrashing about. It is
an intransitive ichidan verb: the person or animal acting this way takes が.

- 馬が驚いて暴れました。 (うまがおどろいてあばれました。) - The horse was startled and thrashed about.
- そんなに暴れないでください。 (そんなにあばれないでください。) - Please do not act so wildly.

Its forms include 暴れます (あばれます), 暴れて (あばれて), and 暴れない
(あばれない). Remove only the final る to get the stem 暴れ.

## How the kanji combine

[暴](../kanji/暴.md) contributes violent or uncontrolled behavior and supplies
あば; れる is okurigana. This is a single native verb, not a compound or a
passive form made by adding れる to another verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[騒ぐ](騒ぐ.md) (さわぐ) can describe noisy commotion or agitation without
violent movement. [戦う](戦う.md) (たたかう) concerns fighting or competing;
not every competition is wild behavior. English fight and struggle can
overlap in descriptions of a violent physical struggle, but the Japanese
words are not unrestricted substitutes.

## Distinctive meaning

暴れる describes behavior rather than merely an angry feeling. The horse
example does not require a quarrel with an opponent. Conversely, an orderly
protest or a sports contest is not automatically 暴れる just because someone
resists or competes.
