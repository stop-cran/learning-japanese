# 外 (そと) - outside

## Meaning

外 means the outside or exterior, including outdoors. It is a location noun and takes particles according to the action.

- 外で遊びましょう。 (そとであそびましょう。) - Let's play outside.
- 猫が外にいます。 (ねこがそとにいます。) - The cat is outside.

で marks where an activity happens; に marks the cat's location with いる.

**外 also has the separate reading ほか**, meaning another place, something else, or the rest.
It is the same lexical identity as [ほか](ほか.md), not a pronunciation to substitute for every そと.

- この店になければ、外を探しましょう。 (このみせになければ、ほかをさがしましょう。) - If this shop doesn't have it, let's look elsewhere.
- 外の人にも聞いてみます。 (ほかのひとにもきいてみます。) - I will ask other people too.

外の人 read そとのひと instead concerns people outside a boundary or group.
The kana reading is therefore important when the written expression alone is ambiguous.

## How the kanji combine

[外](../kanji/外.md) represents the whole native word そと. Other readings, such as がい in compounds or ほか in a different use, are not freely interchangeable with そと.

## Synonyms and antonyms

中 (なか), "inside," is the useful basic contrast. 屋外 (おくがい) specifically means outdoors, whereas 外 can be outside a smaller boundary as well.

## Distinctive meaning

With another noun, use の to identify the boundary: 箱の外 (はこのそと), "outside the box." The word can also refer to people or places outside one's household or group; context decides whether the boundary is physical or social.
