# 有名 (ゆうめい) — famous, well-known

## Meaning

有名 is a な-adjective meaning famous or well-known. Use 有名な before a noun and 有名です as a polite predicate.

- ここは有名な店です。 (ここはゆうめいなみせです。) — This is a famous shop.
- この町はお茶で有名です。 (このまちはおちゃでゆうめいです。) — This town is famous for its tea.

## How the kanji combine

[有](../kanji/有.md) ユウ (have) + [名](../kanji/名.md) メイ (name): an on + on compound. "Having a name people know" is a useful meaning aid; simply having any name does not make someone 有名.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 人気 にんき means popularity. 人気がある にんきがある means "to be popular": being liked is not the same as being widely known.
- 無名 むめい can mean unknown or not famous, and is a useful contrast. It can also mean unnamed, depending on context.

## Distinctive meaning

有名 does not guarantee a good reputation. Someone can be widely known for something bad. The pattern Xで有名 means "famous for X"; X identifies the reason for the reputation.
