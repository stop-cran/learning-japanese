# 結婚 (けっこん) — marriage

## Meaning

結婚 names marriage; 結婚する means "get married."
Mark the person someone marries with と, not by copying English "marry someone" into an を-object pattern.
結婚している describes the resulting married state.

- 姉は去年結婚しました。 (あねはきょねんけっこんしました。) — My older sister got married last year.
- 田中さんと結婚しています。 (たなかさんとけっこんしています。) — I am married to Tanaka.

## How the kanji combine

結 has tying or joining meanings, and [婚](../kanji/婚.md) specifies marriage.
The combination can be understood as forming a marriage bond; it is the established word for marriage, not any sort of joining.
The on readings ケツ + コン become けっこん, with a small っ before こ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

結婚式 (けっこんしき) means "wedding ceremony." It is related but narrower: the ceremony and the marriage are not the same thing.
離婚 (りこん), "divorce," is the useful contrast for ending a marriage.
[夫婦](夫婦.md) (ふうふ) names the married couple rather than the event or relationship in the abstract.

## Distinctive meaning

結婚しました reports getting married; 結婚しています reports being married.
The latter does not normally mean that a wedding ceremony is currently in progress.
Keep these two time perspectives separate when answering questions about someone's marital status.
