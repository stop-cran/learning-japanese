# 安い (やすい) — cheap

## Meaning

安い is an い-adjective for low price: このシャツは安いです (this shirt is cheap), 安く買う (buy cheaply), 安くなる (become cheaper).
It can be positive ("a bargain"); 安っぽい やすっぽい means "cheap-looking", negatively.

## How the kanji combine

[安](../kanji/安.md) with okurigana い: やす + い. The same kanji with on reading アン gives [安心](安心.md) あんしん. Related: 安さ やすさ (cheapness), 安く (adverb), 安まる やすまる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [高い](高い.md) たかい.
- Close: お手頃 おてごろ (reasonably priced), 格安 かくやす (bargain price).

## Distinctive meaning

安い is about price only; "safe" is 安全 あんぜん and "peaceful" is 平和 へいわ or 安らか やすらか. In everyday speech, 安い is the common word.
