# 安い (やすい) — cheap

## Meaning

安い is an い-adjective for low price: このシャツは安いです (this shirt is cheap), 安く買う (buy cheaply), 安くなる (become cheaper).
It can be positive ("a bargain"); 安っぽい やすっぽい means "cheap-looking", negatively.

## How the kanji combine

[安](../kanji/安.md) with okurigana い: やす + い. The same kanji with on reading アン gives [安心](安心.md) あんしん. Related: 安さ やすさ (cheapness), 安く (cheaply).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [高い](高い.md) たかい.
- Close: お手頃 おてごろ (reasonably priced), 格安 かくやす (bargain price).

## Distinctive meaning

The everyday use taught here concerns price. Do not use it as a general replacement for "safe" (安全 あんぜん) or "peaceful" (平和 へいわ or 安らか やすらか).
The dictionary also records a calm or peaceful sense under the spelling 安い, but that does not make it interchangeable with those words in ordinary descriptions.

Do not confuse 安い with the suffix やすい (易い), "easy to," after a verb's ます-stem: 読みやすい (よみやすい), "easy to read."
