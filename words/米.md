# 米 (こめ) — rice as grain

## Meaning

米 refers to rice grains, especially rice as an ingredient before cooking.
It is the useful word when buying rice by weight or preparing it for the pot.

- 米を二キロ買いました。 (こめをにキロかいました。) — I bought two kilograms of rice.
- 米を洗ってから炊きます。 (こめをあらってからたきます。) — I wash the rice and then cook it.

The second example uses 炊く (たく), the verb used here for cooking rice.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [米](../kanji/米.md) is read こめ in this native noun.
Its compound readings are not interchangeable with that pronunciation:
白米 (はくまい), "polished white rice," uses マイ.
Learn each full word rather than replacing こめ with マイ in the examples above.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ご飯](ご飯.md) ごはん means cooked rice and can also mean a meal.
For a bowl of prepared rice, ご飯 is the useful contrast to rice bought as grain.
稲 (いね) instead names the growing rice plant.

These are different ways of identifying rice, not antonyms.
The distinction is what aspect is in focus, not an absolute ban on using 米 with a verb such as [食べる](食べる.md) たべる, "eat."

## Distinctive meaning

The character also occurs in 米国 (べいこく), "United States."
That is a separate use of 米, not another sense of the food noun pronounced こめ.
Seeing the same character does not mean every compound is about rice.
