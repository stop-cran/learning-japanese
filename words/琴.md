# 琴 (こと) - koto; Japanese zither

## Meaning

琴 names the koto, a Japanese plucked string instrument. The familiar traditional form has thirteen strings stretched over a long, low body: it is a **zither**, not an upright Western harp. Instruments with other numbers of strings also exist.

- 母は琴を弾きます。 (はははことをひきます。) - My mother plays the koto.
- 琴の音を聞きました。 (ことのおとをききました。) - I listened to the sound of a koto.

The verb for playing this string instrument is [弾く](弾く.md) (ひく).

## How the kanji combine

琴 writes the whole native noun こと; there is no compound to divide. 箏 is another established spelling for the instrument. The dictionary also records 筝 as a variant character form.

In older usage, こと could refer more broadly to string instruments. Do not assume that every historical occurrence identifies the modern thirteen-string instrument.

## Synonyms and antonyms

箏 (こと) is the alternative instrument spelling just discussed. The reading そう belongs to 箏/筝 in the cited family; do not transfer it to the spelling 琴.

The existing [こと](こと.md) article concerns a matter or thing done, not a musical instrument. The shared pronunciation does not make them the same word.

## Distinctive meaning

English descriptions sometimes loosely call the koto a "Japanese harp." For identifying its construction, "Japanese zither" is more precise. This article teaches 琴/こと, not every instrument or reading represented by 琴 in other vocabulary.
