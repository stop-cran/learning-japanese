# ぶよぶよ — soft, spongy, and mushy

## Meaning

ぶよぶよ describes a soft, yielding, or spongy texture.

- 熟しすぎた桃は、触るとぶよぶよしていた。
  — The overripe peach felt soft and mushy to the touch.
- 水を吸ったスポンジが、ぶよぶよに膨らんだ。
  — The sponge swelled into a soft, spongy mass after absorbing water.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぶよぶよ emphasizes a soft, yielding texture rather than firmness or crispness.

## Distinctive meaning

It may describe food, materials, or other things that have become soft and flabby.
