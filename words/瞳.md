# 瞳 (ひとみ) - pupil of the eye; eyes

## Meaning

瞳 names the pupil or dark central part of the eye.
It also means the eyes more broadly, especially when describing their
appearance or expression.

- 明るい所では瞳が小さくなります。 (あかるいところではひとみがちいさくなります。) - The pupils become smaller in a bright place.
- 子どもの瞳が輝いていました。 (こどものひとみがかがやいていました。) - The child's eyes were shining.

The first concerns the pupil's size; the second describes expressive eyes,
not a separate anatomical change.

## How the kanji combine

瞳 is a single character writing the native word ひとみ.
Its 目 component associates it with the eye; the other visible component
does not make ひとみ a compound meaning "child's eye."
JMdict also records rare 眸 and search-only 睛 as spellings of this word.
Those restrictions do not make each character an equally usual replacement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[目](目.md) (め) is the broader everyday eye word.
瞳 can overlap with it, but 目 also has uses such as a stitch, mesh opening,
or an ordinal marker that do not transfer to 瞳.
There is no useful ordinary antonym for the body part.

## Distinctive meaning

English "pupil" also means a school student, but that is not the meaning
of 瞳. Keep the eye context.
Everyday descriptions of the dark part of an eye can be broader than
strict anatomical terminology: do not infer that the pupil and iris
are medically identical structures.
