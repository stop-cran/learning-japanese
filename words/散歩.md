# 散歩 (さんぽ) — a stroll

## Meaning

散歩 is a walk or stroll, viewed as an activity in its own right. Add する to make 散歩する,
"take a walk." A place or route can take を: 公園を散歩する (こうえんをさんぽする),
"walk around a park." Here を marks the area traversed, not an object being acted on.

- 毎朝、公園を散歩します。 (まいあさ、こうえんをさんぽします。) — I take a walk in the park every morning.
- 散歩に行きましょう。 (さんぽにいきましょう。) — Let's go for a walk.

## How the kanji combine

[散](../kanji/散.md) suggests spreading out or moving about; [歩](../kanji/歩.md) supplies walking.
Together they name a stroll, not "scattering footsteps." Learn the compound's established meaning.
The reading is さんぽ, with ぽ for 歩 in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[歩く](歩く.md) (あるく) simply means "walk." 駅まで歩く (えきまであるく), "walk to the station,"
describes how you get there; it need not be a stroll.
散歩 puts the focus on going for a walk, so it is not a replacement for every occurrence of 歩く.

## Distinctive meaning

散歩 can be the noun in 散歩が好きです (さんぽがすきです), "I like taking walks,"
or join する as the verb. In 散歩に行く, に marks the purpose of going.
There is no need to supply an artificial antonym: sitting still and traveling by train are different activities,
not lexical opposites of this word.
