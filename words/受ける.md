# 受ける (うける) — receive; undergo

## Meaning

受ける describes receiving something, including help or an explanation. The received thing takes を;
a source can be marked with から.

- 先生から説明を受けました。 (せんせいからせつめいをうけました。) — I received an explanation from the teacher.

It also describes undergoing an experience:

- 明日、試験を受けます。 (あした、しけんをうけます。) — I will take an exam tomorrow.

Its forms include 受けます (うけます), 受けた (うけた), and 受けて (うけて).

## How the kanji combine

[受](../kanji/受.md) contributes receiving or undergoing. The kana ける completes the native verb.
The related 受かる (うかる) is a different verb, not another conjugation of 受ける.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もらう, usually written in kana, also means receiving. It readily describes receiving an item from someone.
受ける extends to experiences such as an examination or operation; it is not restricted to acquiring a possession.

Compare 試験を受ける (しけんをうける), "take an exam," with 試験に受かる (しけんにうかる),
"pass an exam." The latter states a successful result, not the reverse of receiving.

## Distinctive meaning

Although the subject receives or undergoes something, 受ける is not grammatically a passive verb form.
In the examples it takes an object with を. Taking the exam also says nothing about passing it.
This article selects receiving and undergoing; it does not cover every colloquial or specialized sense.
