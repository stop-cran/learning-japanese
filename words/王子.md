# 王子 (おうじ) - prince

## Meaning

王子 is a noun meaning "prince," especially a king's son. It appears in stories and descriptions of royal families. 王子様 (おうじさま) adds the respectful suffix 様 and is familiar in fairy-tale language.

- 物語の王子は城に住んでいます。 (ものがたりのおうじはしろにすんでいます。) - The prince in the story lives in a castle.
- 王子が町を訪れました。 (おうじがまちをおとずれました。) - The prince visited the town.

As a noun, it can take particles directly: 王子の名前 (おうじのなまえ), "the prince's name," or 王子に会う (おうじにあう), "meet the prince."

## How the kanji combine

[王](../kanji/王.md) means king, and [子](../kanji/子.md) contributes child. The relationship is "the king's child," with the established word referring to a prince. Learn おうじ as a whole: the 子 reading here is ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[王](王.md) (おう) and [王様](王様.md) (おうさま) mean king, not prince. 王女 (おうじょ), "princess," is a related royal-family term, not an antonym. [息子](息子.md) (むすこ) means son without specifying royalty.

## Distinctive meaning

A 王子 is not necessarily the heir to the throne. "Crown prince" specifies a succession role that the bare word does not establish. In stories, 王子様 may evoke an idealized romantic figure, but the basic noun does not itself mean "boyfriend."
