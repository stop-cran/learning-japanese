# 失敗 (しっぱい) — failure; mistake

## Meaning

失敗 is a failure, mistake, or blunder. Add する to describe an attempt going wrong: 失敗する (しっぱいする).
An activity or undertaking can be marked with に, as in 料理に失敗する, "make an unsuccessful attempt at cooking."

- 料理に失敗しました。 (りょうりにしっぱいしました。) — My attempt at cooking went wrong.
- 一度失敗しても、またやってみます。 (いちどしっぱいしても、またやってみます。) — Even if I fail once, I will try again.

## How the kanji combine

[失](../kanji/失.md) brings the idea of loss or error; [敗](../kanji/敗.md) brings failure or defeat.
The meanings reinforce an unsuccessful result rather than forming a literal object-and-action phrase.
シツ + ハイ becomes しっぱい: the small っ and the following ぱ are part of the word's pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

成功 (せいこう), "success," gives a direct contrast for an undertaking: 成功する versus 失敗する.
間違い (まちがい) means a mistake or error, such as an incorrect answer or calculation.
The words overlap, but an incorrect detail and the failure of an entire attempt are not necessarily the same event.

## Distinctive meaning

失敗 evaluates what happened against an intended result. It need not mean that a person is generally incapable.
For losing an object rather than failing at an attempt, 失う (うしなう), "lose," is a different verb despite the shared character.
Learn the construction with the situation, not just an English label such as "fail."
