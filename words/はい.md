# はい

## Meaning

はい is a polite "yes." It also acknowledges that you heard or understood someone, or answers when your name is called.

- 「学生ですか。」「はい、学生です。」 (「がくせいですか。」「はい、がくせいです。」) — "Are you a student?" "Yes, I am."
- 「明日は来ないんですか。」「はい、来ません。」 (「あしたは こないんですか。」「はい、きません。」) — "You're not coming tomorrow?" "That's right, I'm not."

## How the kanji combine

This interjection is normally written in kana. It has no kanji decomposition and does not conjugate. An elongated はーい is possible in casual speech but has a different tone from a short, clear はい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

うん is an informal acknowledgment. いいえ politely denies or rejects a proposition. ええ is another affirmative response.

## Distinctive meaning

はい can confirm a negative statement, as the second example shows. Do not mechanically translate English yes/no responses without checking the full proposition. During conversation, repeated はい may show attentive listening rather than agreement with every claim or acceptance of a commitment.
