# 方向 (ほうこう) - direction; course

## Meaning

方向 is a direction, orientation, or bearing. It can also name the course
of an activity or decision. Use **Nの方向に/へ** for movement toward N,
and 方向を変える (ほうこうをかえる), "change direction."

- 駅の方向へ歩きました。 (えきのほうこうへあるきました。) - I walked in the direction of the station.
- 話し合いはよい方向に進んでいます。 (はなしあいはよいほうこうにすすんでいます。) - The discussion is moving in a good direction.

The second example concerns progress toward an outcome, not a compass bearing.

## How the kanji combine

[方](../kanji/方.md) contributes a direction or side, and
[向](../kanji/向.md) contributes facing or being oriented toward something.
Their meanings reinforce orientation. The on readings are ほう + こう,
with a long vowel in each part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

方角 (ほうがく) also names direction or bearing.
向き (むき) can describe which way something faces.
[方法](方法.md) (ほうほう) is a method, so it matches English "way" in
"a way to do it," not automatically "which way to go."
反対の方向 (はんたいのほうこう) names the opposite direction in a context.

## Distinctive meaning

Moving in a place's 方向 does not guarantee arrival there. Someone can walk
toward the station and stop elsewhere. Likewise, a discussion moving in a
good direction need not already be resolved. The word describes orientation
or course, while a destination or completed result requires additional information.
