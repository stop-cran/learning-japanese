# まま — remaining unchanged

## Meaning

まま presents a state as continuing without being changed. Learn what precedes it: an action's
result, an action left undone, or a noun describing the unchanged state.

- 窓を開けたまま寝ました。 (まどを あけたまま ねました。) — I slept with the window left open.
- 朝ご飯を食べないまま出かけました。  
  (あさごはんを たべないまま でかけました。) — I went out without eating breakfast.
- この部屋は昔のままです。 (この へやは むかしのままです。) — This room is just as it used to be.

For these uses, compare **verb た-form + まま**, **verb ない-form + まま**, and **noun + の + まま**.
An affirmative nonpast na-adjective takes な, as in 静かなまま (しずかなまま), "remaining quiet";
an i-adjective attaches directly, as in 暗いまま (くらいまま), "remaining dark."

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is usual. JMdict lists 儘 and 侭 as rare kanji spellings of this word; there is no kanji
decomposition to learn for the kana form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そのまま means "as it is" or "without changing it." A phrase with ～ながら describes simultaneous
actions, which is a different focus: 開けたまま concerns the result of opening, not opening the
window continuously while sleeping. 変える (かえる), "change something," provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The past form in 開けたまま establishes a resulting state. It is not simply an instruction to
translate まま as English "while" after every verb.
This word is also separate from 間々 (まま), "occasionally," and ママ, "mom."
