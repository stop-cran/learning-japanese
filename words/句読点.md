# 句読点 (くとうてん) - punctuation marks

## Meaning

句読点 means punctuation marks, especially the sentence-ending 。 and the comma-like 、 in Japanese writing.

- 句読点を適切につけましょう。 (くとうてんをてきせつにつけましょう。) - Let us use punctuation appropriately.
- この文章には句読点がありません。 (このぶんしょうにはくとうてんがありません。) - This passage has no punctuation marks.

句点 (くてん) is the sentence-ending mark, usually 。; 読点 (とうてん) is the comma, usually 、.

## How the kanji combine

句 concerns a phrase or sentence; [読](../kanji/読.md) concerns reading, here at the on reading トウ; [点](../kanji/点.md) (テン) supplies mark or point. 句読 concerns breaks in reading; 点 identifies the marks that indicate them. There is no 句 card currently.

The whole word is くとうてん, not くどくてん. Do not automatically use the familiar ドク reading from 読書.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[記号](記号.md) (きごう), symbol or mark, is a wider category. 句読点 identifies punctuation rather than every written symbol.

[区切る](区切る.md) (くぎる) can describe dividing language into units or punctuating it; that verb is not the name of the marks.

## Distinctive meaning

The central Japanese examples are 。 and 、. Punctuation can be discussed more broadly, including other marks; this is not a claim that every written mark is a 句読点 or that a comma belongs after every spoken pause.
