# 混雑 (こんざつ) - crowding; congestion

## Meaning

混雑 is crowding or congestion. **混雑する** is intransitive: a station, road, or other place is the subject. **混雑している** describes a place as currently crowded.

- 朝は駅が混雑しています。 (あさはえきがこんざつしています。) - The station is crowded in the morning.
- 混雑を避けて、早く出発しました。 (こんざつをさけて、はやくしゅっぱつしました。) - We left early to avoid the crowds.

In the second sentence, 混雑 is a noun and the object of 避ける (さける), "avoid"; it is not a direct object of 混雑する.

## How the kanji combine

混 suggests mixing together, while [雑](../kanji/雑.md) suggests a miscellaneous or jumbled state. コン + ザツ names congestion from things or people being crowded together.

## Synonyms and antonyms

混む (こむ) is the everyday verb "be crowded." [渋滞](渋滞.md) (じゅうたい) focuses on blocked or slow movement, especially traffic. [空く](空く.md) (すく), "become less crowded," gives a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

A 混雑した店 (こんざつしたみせ) is a crowded shop, not necessarily a confusing shop layout. Although the word can also describe disorder, do not use the English gloss "confusion" to infer that a person who fails to understand something is 混雑している. The examples concern the state of a place.
