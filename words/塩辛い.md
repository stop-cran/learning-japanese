# 塩辛い (しおからい) - salty; briny

## Meaning

塩辛い describes a salty or briny taste. It is an i-adjective: 塩辛い料理 means salty food, 塩辛くない means not salty, and 塩辛かった means was salty.

- このスープは少し塩辛いです。 (このスープはすこししおからいです。) - This soup is a little salty.
- 塩辛い物を食べたので、水が飲みたくなりました。 (しおからいものをたべたので、みずがのみたくなりました。) - After eating something salty, I wanted some water.

## How the kanji combine

[塩](../kanji/塩.md) しお, salt, specifies the taste of [辛い](辛い.md) からい. [辛](../kanji/辛.md) contributes から, with い completing the adjective. Both parts use native readings: しお + からい, without voicing か to が.

## Synonyms and antonyms

しょっぱい also means salty. 辛い alone can mean salty as well as spicy; 塩辛い makes the saltiness explicit. [薄い](薄い.md) うすい describes weak flavor or concentration, not a universal opposite for every taste.

## Distinctive meaning

Saltiness does not itself mean spicy heat or physical temperature, nor does the adjective always mean too salty. JMdict also lists the rare spelling 鹹い. Its reading からい is restricted to that spelling in this entry; it is not a license to read 塩辛い as からい.
