# 出入口 (でいりぐち) — a door used for both entering and leaving

## Meaning

出入口 is "an opening used to go in and out": a doorway or gate without a separate entrance and exit. It is common on signs
and in building descriptions. Also written 出入り口.

## How the kanji combine

出入口 is 出入り (でいり, "going out and coming in") plus 口 (くち) "opening" in the voiced form ぐち.
[出](../kanji/出.md) and [入](../kanji/入.md) are in kun readings (で, いり), unlike 出入国 しゅつにゅうこく ("leaving and entering a country") which uses on readings.
The order is 出入, not 入出, here and in 出入り.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Parts: [出口](出口.md) (exit), [入口](入口.md) (entrance).
- Related: 玄関 げんかん (the main entrance of a house), 通用口 つうようぐち (service entrance), 非常口 ひじょうぐち (emergency exit).
- 出入口 has no true antonym; its component pair is 出口 ↔ 入口.

## Distinctive meaning

Where entrances and exits are separate, use 入口 and 出口. 出入口 stresses that one opening serves both, as in 出入口をふさがないでください
(please don't block the doorway). 出入り (でいり) also means "comings and goings" or "dealings": 人の出入りが多い.
