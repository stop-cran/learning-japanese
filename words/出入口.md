# 出入口 (でいりぐち) — entrance and exit

## Meaning

出入口 is an entrance and exit: a place for going in and out, such as a doorway or gate.
It can identify an opening used both ways, but the word does not establish that a building
has only one doorway or cannot have separate entrance and exit routes. Also written 出入り口.

- 出入口をふさがないでください。
  (でいりぐちをふさがないでください。) — Please do not block the entrance and exit.
- この建物には出入口が二つあります。
  (このたてものにはでいりぐちがふたつあります。) — This building has two entrances/exits.

## How the kanji combine

出入口 is [出入り](出入り.md) (でいり, "going out and coming in") plus [口](../kanji/口.md) (くち) "opening" in the voiced form ぐち.
[出](../kanji/出.md) and [入](../kanji/入.md) are in kun readings (で, いり), unlike 出入国 しゅつにゅうこく ("leaving and entering a country") which uses on readings.
The order is 出入, not 入出, here and in 出入り.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Parts: [出口](出口.md) (exit), [入口](入口.md) (entrance).
- Related: [玄関](玄関.md) げんかん (a building's main entrance or entryway), 通用口 つうようぐち (service entrance), 非常口 ひじょうぐち (emergency exit).
- 出入口 has no true antonym; its component pair is 出口 ↔ 入口.

## Distinctive meaning

入口 and 出口 distinguish the entry and exit functions when that matters.
出入口 names the access point or points without itself specifying their arrangement.
出入り instead names the movement or dealings: 人の出入りが多い
(ひとのでいりがおおい), "many people come and go."
