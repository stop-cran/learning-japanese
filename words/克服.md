# 克服 (こくふく) - overcoming; surmounting

## Meaning

克服 is overcoming or bringing a difficulty under control. **克服する** is transitive: the obstacle, fear, illness, or weakness being overcome takes を. It describes a challenge being surmounted, not merely noticed.

- 彼女は困難を克服しました。 (かのじょはこんなんをこくふくしました。) - She overcame the difficulties.
- 人前で話すことへの恐怖を克服したいです。 (ひとまえではなすことへのきょうふをこくふくしたいです。) - I want to overcome my fear of speaking in public.

The second sentence expresses a goal with たい; it does not claim the fear has already disappeared.

## How the kanji combine

克 contributes overcoming or mastering, and [服](../kanji/服.md) contributes submission or bringing under control here. The related ideas reinforce the meaning in コク + フク. 服 is not "clothes" in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[解決](解決.md) (かいけつ) is solving or settling a problem. It overlaps when a difficulty is resolved, but 克服 highlights getting past or mastering the obstacle. Giving up is a contextual contrast, not a fixed one-word antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "conquest" needs qualification: 克服 concerns difficulties, crises, illnesses, and similar challenges. It is not a general word for conquering territory or subjugating a population. Equally, a person can 克服 a weakness without claiming that every associated problem has vanished permanently.
