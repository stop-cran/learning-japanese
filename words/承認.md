# 承認 (しょうにん) - approval; recognition

## Meaning

承認 is a noun and transitive する verb for approval, acceptance, or recognition. In 計画を承認する (けいかくをしょうにんする), the plan being approved takes を.

- 委員会はその計画を承認しました。 (いいんかいはそのけいかくをしょうにんしました。) - The committee approved the plan.
- この変更には上司の承認が必要です。 (このへんこうにはじょうしのしょうにんがひつようです。) - This change requires the supervisor's approval.

承認を得る (しょうにんをえる) means "obtain approval." The passive 承認される means "be approved" or "be recognized"; it does not name who approves unless the sentence adds that information.

## How the kanji combine

承 contributes accepting or receiving, and [認](../kanji/認.md) contributes acknowledging or recognizing. Their combination expresses accepting something as valid, recognized, or approved. The readings are ショウ + ニン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[認める](認める.md) (みとめる) overlaps in recognizing or approving, but also has other uses such as noticing something. [許可](許可.md) (きょか) focuses on permission to do something. [賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい) expresses agreement or support; supporting a plan does not necessarily grant its required approval.

## Distinctive meaning

承認 is useful in procedures, applications, and organizational decisions. It may also concern acknowledgment rather than permission, so inspect the object. Simply recognizing a familiar face is not normally described as 顔を承認する. 不承認 (ふしょうにん), non-approval, directly contrasts with an approval decision.
