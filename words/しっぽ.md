# しっぽ (しっぽ) - animal tail; tail end

## Meaning

しっぽ is an animal's tail. It is a noun, also written **尻尾** (しっぽ). It can additionally name the tail end or tip of something.

- 犬がしっぽを振っています。
  (いぬがしっぽをふっています。) - The dog is wagging its tail.
- 猫の長いしっぽが見えました。
  (ねこのながいしっぽがみえました。) - I could see the cat's long tail.

For the extended end sense, しっぽの部分 (しっぽのぶぶん) identifies "the tail-end part." The object and situation must make clear which end is meant; this is not a universal replacement for every English "end."

## How the kanji combine

The primary spelling here is kana. In 尻尾, 尻 suggests the rear and 尾 means a tail. Learn the whole reading しっぽ; do not infer that 尾 independently reads ぽ.

JMdict also records しりお as an outdated or obsolete reading. That is recognition information, not a recommendation to replace しっぽ in the examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

尾 (お) also means a tail. [尻](尻.md) (しり) means buttocks or the rear/end, not specifically an animal's tail: a tail's position and the body part behind it are not the same thing.

[端](端.md) (はし) is an edge, end, or tip more generally. [最後](最後.md) (さいご) identifies the last part or final position, rather than necessarily a protruding tail.

## Distinctive meaning

An animal's しっぽ is not its wing or hind leg. The end extension does not remove that basic body-part meaning, and it does not license translating the end of any event or deadline as しっぽ. Use the surrounding noun and established expression to choose the appropriate end word.
