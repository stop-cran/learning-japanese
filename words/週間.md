# 週間 (しゅうかん) - week-long period; duration in weeks

## Meaning

週間 names a week or a period measured in weeks. Learn it in constructions such as 一週間 (いっしゅうかん), "one week," and 二週間 (にしゅうかん), "two weeks." The number specifies the duration.

- 京都に一週間滞在しました。 (きょうとにいっしゅうかんたいざいしました。) - I stayed in Kyoto for one week.
- 二週間後に試験があります。 (にしゅうかんごにしけんがあります。) - There is an exam in two weeks.

In the first example, the duration does not need に. In the second, 後に (ごに) locates the event after that interval.

## How the kanji combine

[週](../kanji/週.md) means week, and [間](../kanji/間.md) contributes an interval or span. Together they identify a weekly span. The reading is しゅうかん, with a small っ in 一週間.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[週](週.md) also names a week and appears in calendar expressions. [時間](時間.md) (じかん) can name time or a duration in hours. These are different units or perspectives, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

週間 does not by itself mean "every week." 毎週 (まいしゅう) expresses recurrence, while 三週間 (さんしゅうかん) expresses a three-week duration. Also distinguish 週間 from the same-sounding 週刊 (しゅうかん), used for weekly publication. A week-long event and an event repeated each week are different schedules.
