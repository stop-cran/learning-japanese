# 医者 (いしゃ) — doctor, physician

## Meaning

医者 is a medical doctor or physician.

- 父は医者です。（ちちは いしゃです。）— My father is a doctor.
- お医者さんに相談します。（おいしゃさんに そうだんします。）— I will consult a doctor.

お医者さん おいしゃさん is a common polite way to refer to a doctor.

## How the kanji combine

[医](../kanji/医.md) イ means medicine or medical practice; [者](../kanji/者.md) シャ identifies a person. This is a field-plus-person compound: a person who practices medicine. Both are on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 医師 いし also means physician and is common as a professional or institutional designation.
- [先生](先生.md) せんせい is a respectful title used when addressing a doctor, but also a teacher and certain other professionals.
- [病院](病院.md) びょういん is a hospital or clinic, a place rather than a person.
- There is no natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

医者 refers to a medical practitioner, not every holder of an academic doctorate. When speaking directly to a doctor, 先生 is a useful address; do not assume that the bare job noun 医者 is a polite form of address.

For 医者 compared with 医師 (same physician, different ending), see [the article on occupation and role endings](../articles/occupation-and-role-endings.md).
