# ぽかぽか — pleasantly warm

## Meaning

Pleasant, enveloping warmth, especially sunshine or a warmed body. A separate sound-symbolic use describes repeated blows.

- 日なたはぽかぽかしていて、眠くなる。 — It is pleasantly warm in the sun, and it makes me sleepy.
- お風呂から出たら、体がぽかぽかしてきた。 — After my bath, my whole body began to feel warm.
- 子どもがクッションをぽかぽかとたたいている。 — The child is thumping the cushion repeatedly.

## How the kanji combine

A native Japanese mimetic, normally written in kana. The repeated ぽか pattern is part of the word; it is not two kanji meanings or a plural.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ほかほか](ほかほか.md) overlaps in comfortable warmth, especially food or something freshly warmed. [ひんやり](ひんやり.md) describes a cool sensation. [あつあつ](あつあつ.md) is much hotter, not necessarily comfortable to touch.

## Distinctive meaning

ぽかぽかする and ぽかぽかした陽気（ようき, weather）are useful patterns. In ぽかぽかとたたく, と introduces the manner of striking; this does not mean “warmly.” The object and verb distinguish the senses. Warmth need not involve an audible sound.
