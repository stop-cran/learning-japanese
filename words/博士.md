# 博士 (はかせ) - expert; doctor

## Meaning

博士, read **はかせ**, can mean an expert or learned person.
It can also be used colloquially for a doctor/PhD or as a title "Dr."
The expert use does not by itself prove that someone holds a doctoral degree.

- 弟は恐竜に詳しくて、家では恐竜博士と呼ばれています。 (おとうとはきょうりゅうにくわしくて、いえではきょうりゅうはかせとよばれています。) - My younger brother knows a lot about dinosaurs, so we call him our dinosaur expert at home.
- 田中博士に話を聞きました。 (たなかはかせにはなしをききました。) - I spoke with Dr. Tanaka.

In the second example, はかせ follows a surname as a title.
Interpret it using context, not as the ordinary word for a medical practitioner.

## How the kanji combine

博 contributes breadth of learning, and 士 contributes a learned or professional person.
The assigned reading はかせ is a special whole-word reading, not a simple concatenation of the characters' on readings.
The same spelling also has the separately indexed reading **はくし**.

## Synonyms and antonyms

専門家 (せんもんか) means a specialist or expert.
[医者](医者.md) (いしゃ) identifies a medical doctor; English "doctor" does not make these words interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict marks the doctor/PhD use of **はかせ** colloquial.
Its separate **はくし** entry gives doctor/PhD without that qualification, as in 博士号 (はくしごう), "doctoral degree."
Retain both the assigned はかせ reading and this distinction rather than silently substituting はくし.
The knowledge-based nickname use and the academic title are related but not identical claims.
