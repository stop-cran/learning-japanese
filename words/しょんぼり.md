# しょんぼり — downcast and dejected

## Meaning

A subdued, dispirited state or manner, often apparent in someone's posture or behavior.

- 試合（しあい）に負けて、しょんぼりしている。 — They are downcast after losing the match.
- 雨で遠足（えんそく）が中止（ちゅうし）になり、子どもはしょんぼり帰った。 — With the excursion canceled by rain, the child went home dejectedly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[がっかり](がっかり.md) overlaps in disappointment. [うきうき](うきうき.md) gives a cheerful contrast. [しんみり](しんみり.md) can also be subdued, but may concern a reflective shared atmosphere.

## Distinctive meaning

しょんぼりする describes the state; しょんぼり帰る（かえる）describes the manner of going home. Visible downcast behavior does not tell the observer its precise cause.
