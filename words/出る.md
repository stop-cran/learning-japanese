# 出る (でる) — go out or appear

## Meaning

出る means to leave, go out, come out, or appear. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 出ます, 出て, 出ない.

八時に家を出ます。（はちじにいえをでます。） — I leave home at eight.

雲の間から月が出ました。（くものあいだからつきがでました。） — The moon came out from between the clouds.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) expresses coming or going out. Here it is read で, followed by る. The conjugation base is 出（で）.

## Synonyms and antonyms

入る（はいる）, "enter," contrasts with leaving. 出す（だす） is transitive: it describes someone taking or putting something out.

## Distinctive meaning

In 家を出る, を marks the place left; it does not make 出る transitive. Other familiar patterns include 会議に出る（かいぎにでる）, "attend a meeting," and 電話に出る（でんわにでる）, "answer the phone." Learn the meaning together with its noun and particle.
