# 郵送 (ゆうそう) - mailing; posting

## Meaning

郵送 means sending something through the postal service. It is a noun and forms the transitive verb 郵送する.

- 申込書を郵送しました。
  (もうしこみしょをゆうそうしました。) - I mailed the application form.
- 書類は明日郵送します。
  (しょるいはあしたゆうそうします。) - I will send the documents by post tomorrow.

を marks the item sent. 郵送で (ゆうそうで) expresses the method, by mail.

## How the kanji combine

[郵](../kanji/郵.md) specifies the postal service, and [送](../kanji/送.md) sending: send by post. The on readings are ユウ + ソウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[送る](送る.md) (おくる) is broader and does not by itself specify postal transport. [出す](出す.md) (だす) also has a letter-sending use, as in 手紙を出す (てがみをだす).

投函 (とうかん) focuses on posting an item in a mailbox. 発送 (はっそう) concerns dispatch or shipping more broadly. [郵便](郵便.md) (ゆうびん) can name the postal service or postal matter rather than the act of sending alone.

## Distinctive meaning

郵送 identifies a postal method. Sending an electronic message or handing a document directly to someone is not automatically 郵送.

Dispatch and arrival are different: 郵送した does not by itself guarantee that the item has already [届いた](届く.md) (とどいた), arrived. The word alone does not specify express service, registration, postage, or a delivery date.
