# 可能 (かのう) - possible; feasible

## Meaning

可能 means possible or feasible: something can be done under the circumstances.
It behaves as a na-adjective, with 可能な before a noun and 可能です as a predicate.
Use an action noun + が可能 or verb in dictionary form + ことが可能.

- 予約の変更は可能ですか。
  (よやくのへんこうはかのうですか。) - Is it possible to change the reservation?
- この機械で写真を印刷することが可能です。
  (このきかいでしゃしんをいんさつすることがかのうです。)
  - It is possible to print photographs with this machine.

可能な方法 (かのうなほうほう) means "a feasible method."
The negative is 可能ではない.

## How the kanji combine

可 contributes possibility or acceptability, and [能](../kanji/能.md) contributes ability.
The compound combines these related ideas in "able to be done." The on readings are カ + ノウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

できる can express being able to do something and often permits a simpler sentence:
予約を変更できますか, "Can I change the reservation?"
不可能 (ふかのう), "impossible," is the direct opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

可能 concerns possibility, not a promise that the action will happen.
An available method may be expensive or inconvenient and still be 可能.
Likewise, technical feasibility does not by itself grant permission: whether an action is
allowed may require a separate question.
