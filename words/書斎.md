# 書斎 (しょさい) - study; home office

## Meaning

書斎 is a study: a room used for reading, writing, or desk work, often in a home.
It can be translated as "home office" when that suits the room's use. It is a
place noun, not the activity of studying.

- 父は書斎で本を読んでいます。 (ちちはしょさいでほんをよんでいます。) - My father is reading a book in his study.
- 二階の小さな部屋を書斎にしました。 (にかいのちいさなへやをしょさいにしました。) - We made the small room upstairs into a study.

で marks where reading happens. In 部屋を書斎にする, を marks the room being
converted and に the resulting use.

## How the kanji combine

[書](../kanji/書.md) contributes books or writing. 斎 has a study or room sense
in this compound. Together they name a room associated with books and writing;
the readings are ショ + サイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[部屋](部屋.md) (へや) means a room generally; 書斎 identifies its purpose.
[勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) is the activity "study," not a room.
A public library is [図書館](図書館.md) (としょかん), not automatically a 書斎.

## Distinctive meaning

The presence of a desk or books can support a room's use as a 書斎, but the word
does not specify its size, ownership, or occupant's profession. Keep the place
meaning when translating English "study": studying Japanese can happen in a
書斎, but the action itself is not 書斎する.
