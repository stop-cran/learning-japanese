# 印象 (いんしょう) - impression

## Meaning

印象 is the impression a person, place, or experience leaves in someone's mind.
Useful noun constructions are 印象を受ける (いんしょうをうける), "get an impression,"
印象を与える (いんしょうをあたえる), "give an impression," and 印象に残る
(いんしょうにのこる), "remain in one's memory."

- 彼の話し方から、優しい人だという印象を受けました。
  (かれのはなしかたから、やさしいひとだといういんしょうをうけました。)
  - From his way of speaking, I got the impression that he was kind.
- 旅先で見た夕日が印象に残っています。
  (たびさきでみたゆうひがいんしょうにのこっています。) - The sunset I saw on my trip has stayed with me.

第一印象 (だいいちいんしょう) means "first impression."

## How the kanji combine

印 suggests a mark, and 象 an image or form. Together they suggest an image marked
in the mind, a useful meaning association rather than a claim about the word's historical origin.
The on readings are イン + ショウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion or view that someone holds or states.
印象 can instead be an immediate sense of how something seems.
良い印象 and 悪い印象 are favorable and unfavorable impressions, not separate lexical opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

An impression need not be a verified fact. 優しい人だという印象 reports how the person
seemed, not a guarantee of their character. The noun also concerns memorability, as the sunset example shows.
