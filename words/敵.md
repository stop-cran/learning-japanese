# 敵 (てき) - enemy; opponent

## Meaning

敵 is a noun for an enemy, opponent, or adversary. It can also
describe something harmful as an "enemy" or threat. Use 敵と戦う
for fighting an opponent, and 人の敵 for someone's enemy.

- 敵の動きをよく見てください。 (てきのうごきをよくみてください。) - Watch your opponent's movements carefully.
- 睡眠不足は健康の敵です。 (すいみんぶそくはけんこうのてきです。) - Lack of sleep is an enemy of good health.

The first example can fit a game or contest. The second uses 敵
figuratively for a harmful condition, not for a person with hostile
intentions.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 敵 supplies the whole noun through the on
reading テキ. There is no multi-kanji combination to analyze.
Its use for a threat extends the ordinary adversarial relationship.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[相手](相手.md) (あいて) is a broader counterpart or partner,
including an opponent without necessarily implying hostility.
[味方](味方.md) (みかた), "ally; someone on one's side," is
the direct contrasting side. A rival can be an 敵 in a contest
without being a personal enemy.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling someone 敵 frames them as being on the opposing side.
That framing can sound stronger than simply identifying the other
participant as 相手. Context determines whether the opposition is
military, competitive, personal, or metaphorical.
