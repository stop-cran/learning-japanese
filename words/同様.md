# 同様 (どうよう) - same; similar

## Meaning

同様 describes something as the same or similar in the respect being discussed. Use Nと同様 for "like N," 同様の/同様な before a noun, and 同様に for "similarly/in the same way."

- 前回と同様の方法で調べました。 (ぜんかいとどうようのほうほうでしらべました。) - We investigated using the same kind of method as last time.
- 他の学生と同様に、私も試験を受けます。 (ほかのがくせいとどうように、わたしもしけんをうけます。) - Like the other students, I will take the exam too.

と marks the comparison standard, not a companion performing an action together.

## How the kanji combine

[同](../kanji/同.md) means same, while [様](../kanji/様.md) contributes manner or appearance. Together they suggest the same manner or kind. The on readings are ドウ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ) also means same, but its noun attachment differs: 同じ方法 versus 同様の方法. [似る](似る.md) (にる), usually 似ている, describes resemblance. [違う](違う.md) (ちがう), "differ," supplies a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

同様 does not automatically claim that two things are identical in every detail. It may identify the same treatment, kind of method, or relevant property. The phrase 前回と同様に, "as last time," relies on context to say what is repeated. Keep the linking particle と and the following noun or verb construction visible when learning it.
