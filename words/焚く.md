# 焚く (たく) - burn fuel; stoke a fire

## Meaning

焚く is a transitive godan verb for burning or kindling a fire, burning
fuel or incense, and stoking or heating a bath, stove or boiler.
It also has the established camera-flash use フラッシュを焚く.

- 庭で火を焚きました。
  (にわでひをたきました。) - I lit a fire in the garden.
- 線香を焚きます。
  (せんこうをたきます。) - I burn incense.

The object takes を. The polite form is 焚きます, the て-form 焚いて,
and the past 焚いた. 風呂を焚く (ふろをたく) means heat the bath,
not set the bath itself on fire.

## How the kanji combine

焚 writes the native element た, with the inflecting く.
The practical reading selected here is たく.
[焼く](焼く.md) is やく; an outdated spelling record in that separate
family is not a rule that the two modern headwords freely exchange readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

焼く and [燃える](燃える.md) (もえる) overlap the broad English burn,
but 焚く commonly concerns deliberately tending or producing a fire.
[焼ける](焼ける.md) (やける), [焦がす](焦がす.md) (こがす) and
[焦げる](焦げる.md) (こげる) have burning or scorching labels without
all describing fire-tending.

[沸かす](沸かす.md) (わかす), [加熱](加熱.md) (かねつ) and
[暖める](暖める.md) (あたためる) overlap heating contexts.
[炊く](炊く.md) (たく) is the rice-cooking comparison, not an automatic
alternative spelling for every use above.

## Distinctive meaning

The object and context matter. 焚く a fire, incense, a bath and a flash
does not describe one identical physical process.
In particular, フラッシュを焚く means use or fire the flash, not destroy
the camera by burning it.
