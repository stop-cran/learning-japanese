# 領事 (りょうじ) - consul

## Meaning

領事 is a consul: an official working abroad on matters such as
protecting their country's citizens, promoting trade and handling
certifications. It is a noun naming the official.

- 領事がその会合に出席しました。 (りょうじがそのかいごうにしゅっせきしました。) - The consul attended the meeting.
- 領事に相談しました。 (りょうじにそうだんしました。) - I consulted the consul.

## How the kanji combine

[領](../kanji/領.md) contributes taking charge or managing here,
and [事](../kanji/事.md) concerns affairs.
The on readings are リョウ + ジ.
This character association does not mean that a consul owns foreign territory.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大使](大使.md) (たいし) is an ambassador, a different diplomatic role.
領事館 (りょうじかん) is a consulate, the institution or office;
[大使館](大使館.md) (たいしかん) is an embassy.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the official from the office: 領事 is a person,
whereas 領事館 includes 館 (かん), a building or institution.
Do not use 領事 as a general translation of every foreign representative.
