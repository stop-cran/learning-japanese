# 湧く (わく) - well up; gush out; arise

## Meaning

湧く describes water welling up or gushing out, and extends to ideas or
feelings arising. The thing that emerges takes が.
It is an intransitive godan verb: 湧きます, 湧いて, 湧いた, 湧かない.

- この泉から冷たい水が湧いています。 (このいずみからつめたいみずがわいています。) - Cold water is welling up from this spring.
- いい考えが湧いてきました。 (いいかんがえがわいてきました。) - A good idea came to me.

Other recorded uses include tears or sweat appearing, and insects breeding
or appearing in numbers: 虫が湧く (むしがわく).
These are not instructions to translate every appearance with this verb.

## How the kanji combine

湧 supplies the native verb わく; く is its written ending.
涌く is a rare alternate spelling of the same word.
Neither character currently has a kanji card, but 湧 still belongs in this
word's written-kanji metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[沸く](沸く.md), also わく, describes water boiling and also excitement.
水が湧く describes water emerging; 水が沸く describes it becoming hot enough
to boil. Shared pronunciation does not merge the two dictionary families.

[浮かぶ](浮かぶ.md) (うかぶ) also works with an idea coming to mind.
[生じる](生じる.md) (しょうじる) and [生ずる](生ずる.md) (しょうずる)
are broader arising/occurrence verbs, with their own constructions.

## Distinctive meaning

湧く is not limited to physical water: 喜びが湧く (よろこびがわく)
means joy wells up, and 拍手が湧く (はくしゅがわく) describes applause arising.
Compare 会場が沸く (かいじょうがわく), "the audience/venue erupts with excitement."
These separate words share the reading わく and have related figurative
contexts without making their literal springing and boiling uses interchangeable.

The community source mixes boiling/heating glosses into 湧く.
This card follows the supplied 湧く entry rather than importing those
literal senses from the separate 沸く entry.
