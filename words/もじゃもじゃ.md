# もじゃもじゃ — shaggy and unkempt

## Meaning

もじゃもじゃ describes hair, fur, or a beard that is thick, shaggy, and untidy.

- 彼はもじゃもじゃのひげを伸ばしている。
  — He has grown a shaggy beard.
- 庭の草がもじゃもじゃに生えている。
  — The grass is growing in a thick, tangled mass in the garden.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もじゃもじゃ suggests abundant, tangled growth, unlike a neat, closely trimmed appearance.

## Distinctive meaning

It describes dense, unruly growth rather than just hair that is slightly out of place.
