# 物語 (ものがたり) - story; narrative

## Meaning

物語 is a noun for a story, tale, or narrative. It presents events as something told or recounted, whether in a book, film, oral telling, or another form.

- 子どものころ、祖母が昔の物語を聞かせてくれました。 (こどものころ、そぼがむかしのものがたりをきかせてくれました。) - When I was a child, my grandmother told me old stories.
- この物語は小さな村から始まります。 (このものがたりはちいさなむらからはじまります。) - This story begins in a small village.

物語を読む (ものがたりをよむ) means reading a story, while 物語を語る (ものがたりをかたる) means telling one.

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md) contributes things or matters, and [語](../kanji/語.md) connects with [語る](語る.md) (かたる), "tell; narrate." The native compound is もの + がたり, with voicing in the second element. It is not read with the on readings ブツ and ゴ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話](話.md) (はなし) is broader: it can mean talk, a conversation, or a story. [小説](小説.md) (しょうせつ) names a novel or fictional literary work. A 物語 need not be a novel, and the word does not by itself prove that the events are invented.

## Distinctive meaning

The focus is a narrative, not every isolated piece of information. A timetable or a short factual notice is not automatically a 物語. Conversely, a person's life can be presented as a 物語 when its events are related as a connected account.
