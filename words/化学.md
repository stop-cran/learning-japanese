# 化学 (かがく) - chemistry

## Meaning

化学 is chemistry, the study of substances, their properties, and their transformations. It is a noun: 化学を勉強する means to study chemistry, while 化学の実験 (かがくのじっけん) is a chemistry experiment.

- 大学で化学を勉強しています。 (だいがくでかがくをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying chemistry at university.
- 化学の実験で色が変わりました。 (かがくのじっけんでいろがかわりました。) - The color changed in the chemistry experiment.

The word names a field of study, not a chemical substance by itself.

## How the kanji combine

[化](../kanji/化.md) contributes change or transformation, and [学](../kanji/学.md) means study or learning. The compound names the study associated with chemical substances and changes. Its on readings are カ + ガク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[科学](科学.md), also かがく, means science more broadly. Chemistry is a science, but a science class is not necessarily a chemistry class. [研究](研究.md) (けんきゅう) means research, an activity that can take place in chemistry or many other fields.

## Distinctive meaning

Because 化学 and 科学 sound alike, 化学 can be pronounced **ばけがく** in speech to distinguish it from 科学. This is a disambiguating reading of the same word, not a different academic discipline. Writing shows the distinction directly through 化 versus 科.
