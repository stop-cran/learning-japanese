# 学ぶ (まなぶ) - learn; study

## Meaning

学ぶ means to learn, study, or take lessons in something. It is a transitive
godan verb: the subject learned takes を. A source of learning can take から;
a teacher can take に.

- 大学で日本の歴史を学んでいます。 (だいがくでにほんのれきしをまなんでいます。) - I am studying Japanese history at university.
- 失敗から多くのことを学びました。 (しっぱいからおおくのことをまなびました。) - I learned many things from my mistakes.

The polite form is 学びます (まなびます), the past 学んだ (まなんだ), and the
negative 学ばない (まなばない). The ぶ ending changes to んで in 学んでいる.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) contributes learning. It is read まな in this native verb,
with ぶ written in kana. The on reading ガク belongs to compounds such as
[留学](留学.md) (りゅうがく), "study abroad."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう)する emphasizes study and overlaps in academic
contexts. [習う](習う.md) (ならう) readily describes learning through instruction
or practice. 学ぶ can also describe learning from experience, as in the second
example.

## Distinctive meaning

学ぶ does not categorically require deep or prolonged study. Nor does using the
verb prove that the learner has mastered the subject. It can describe an
ongoing educational activity or a particular lesson gained from an experience.
