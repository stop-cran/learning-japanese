# 結論 (けつろん) - conclusion

## Meaning

結論 is a noun for a conclusion reached through discussion, argument,
or study. Learn 結論を出す, "reach a conclusion," and
結論に達する, "arrive at a conclusion." 結論する can express
"conclude," with the conclusion quoted using と.

- まだ結論は出ていません。 (まだけつろんはでていません。) - No conclusion has been reached yet.
- 話し合った結果、延期するという結論になりました。 (はなしあったけっか、えんきするというけつろんになりました。) - After discussing it, we concluded that we would postpone it.

In the second example, という connects the content to the noun:
"the conclusion that we would postpone."

## How the kanji combine

結 contributes tying together or bringing to a close, while
[論](../kanji/論.md) contributes argument or discussion. Together,
the compound names the outcome to which reasoning or discussion is
brought. This explains the modern components without claiming an
exact historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結果](結果.md) (けっか) means "result," including outcomes that
involve no reasoning. [意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion;
an opinion may contribute to a discussion without being its conclusion.

## Distinctive meaning

A conclusion is not automatically correct, unanimous, or permanent.
結論を急ぐ (けつろんをいそぐ) means rushing to a conclusion.
The word identifies the role of a judgment in reasoning, not a guarantee
of the quality of the evidence behind it.
