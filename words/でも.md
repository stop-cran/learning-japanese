# でも — but; however

## Meaning

At the beginning of a statement, でも means "but" or "however." It introduces a contrast with what was just said.

この店は小さいです。でも、料理はおいしいです。 (このみせはちいさいです。でも、りょうりはおいしいです。) — This restaurant is small. But the food is delicious.

## How the kanji combine

でも is written in kana. The standalone conjunction and the particle attached after another word have related-looking forms but different jobs in a sentence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

しかし also introduces a contrast and is more suited to formal exposition. [そして](そして.md), "and" or "and then," adds information rather than contrasting it.

## Distinctive meaning

After a noun, でも need not mean "but": お茶でも飲みませんか (おちゃでものみませんか), "Would you like some tea or something?" It can also mean "even," as in 子どもでも分かります (こどもでもわかります), "Even a child can understand." Identify whether でも starts a new statement or is attached to the preceding expression.
