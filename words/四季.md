# 四季 (しき) - the four seasons

## Meaning

四季 names spring, summer, autumn, and winter collectively. It is a noun. 四季の変化 means the changes of the four seasons.

- この庭では四季の花を楽しめます。 (このにわではしきのはなをたのしめます。) - You can enjoy flowers from all four seasons in this garden.
- 四季を通じて多くの人が訪れます。 (しきをつうじておおくのひとがおとずれます。) - Many people visit throughout the four seasons.

## How the kanji combine

[四](../kanji/四.md) contributes four and [季](../kanji/季.md) contributes season. The number specifies the seasons as a set. The on readings シ + キ give しき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[季節](季節.md) きせつ names a season more generally. [春](春.md) はる, [夏](夏.md) なつ, [秋](秋.md) あき, and [冬](冬.md) ふゆ are the individual seasons. [シーズン](シーズン.md) also means season but can refer to an activity period or television season.

## Distinctive meaning

四季 names the four-season grouping, not a claim that every region experiences identical weather or equally long seasons. It is also not interchangeable with the homophone [式](式.md) (しき), a ceremony, formula, or style: context and kanji distinguish the words.
