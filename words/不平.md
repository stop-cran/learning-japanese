# 不平 (ふへい) - discontent; complaint

## Meaning

不平 is a noun for dissatisfaction, discontent, or a complaint. Two useful constructions are 不平がある, "have a grievance," and 不平を言う, "complain."

- 彼は仕事について不平を言っています。 (かれはしごとについてふへいをいっています。) - He is complaining about his work.
- 今の生活に不平はありません。 (いまのせいかつにふへいはありません。) - I have no complaints about my current life.

について or に can identify what the dissatisfaction is about, depending on the construction. 不平 is not itself a verb meaning "complain"; 不平を言う supplies the action.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) contributes negation, and [平](../kanji/平.md) contributes evenness or calm. Their combination offers a mnemonic of an unsettled feeling, but the established word means discontent, not every kind of physical unevenness. Read it フ + ヘイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不満](不満.md) (ふまん) overlaps strongly in dissatisfaction. [文句](文句.md) (もんく) also appears in 文句を言う, "complain." [満足](満足.md) (まんぞく), satisfaction, gives a useful opposite. These overlaps do not create a strict rule that 不平 must be spoken aloud.

## Distinctive meaning

不平 can refer to the feeling or its expression. The predicate determines whether someone actually voices it: 不平があっても言わない (ふへいがあってもいわない) means "have complaints but not voice them." Do not confuse 不平 with 不公平 (ふこうへい), "unfairness"; unfairness may cause a complaint, but the words name different things.
