# 同じ (おなじ) — same

## Meaning

同じ means the same, identical, or equal in the respect being compared.

- 私たちは同じ学校に通っています。（わたしたちは おなじ がっこうに かよっています。）— We go to the same school.
- この二つは同じです。（この ふたつは おなじです。）— These two are the same.

Use Aと同じ for "the same as A."

## How the kanji combine

This is one native word, not a compound. [同](../kanji/同.md) represents おな and the okurigana じ completes おなじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 似ている にている, from 似る にる, means "be similar" or "resemble." Similar things need not be the same.
- 違う ちがう, "differ; be different," is a useful opposite.
- 別の べつの, "another; a different," presents something as distinct or separate.

## Distinctive meaning

同じ has an unusual adjective pattern. Before an ordinary noun, use 同じ directly: 同じ本 おなじほん, "the same book," not 同じな本. As a predicate, use 同じだ or 同じです.

This shortcut is for ordinary nouns: explanatory のだ／のです takes な, as in 同じなのです（おなじなのです）, "it is the same." Do not generalize the missing な to every construction.
