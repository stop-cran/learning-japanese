# 大部分 (だいぶぶん) - greater part; most

## Meaning

大部分 is a noun meaning the greater part or most of something. **Nの大部分** identifies the larger share of N; **大部分のN** describes most members or portions of N.

- 仕事の大部分は終わりました。 (しごとのだいぶぶんはおわりました。) - Most of the work is finished.
- 大部分の学生は電車で通っています。 (だいぶぶんのがくせいはでんしゃでかよっています。) - Most of the students commute by train.

The first sentence divides work into completed and remaining parts. The second describes the majority within a group of students.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) means "large." [部](../kanji/部.md) and [分](../kanji/分.md) together form 部分 (ぶぶん), "part; portion." 大 modifies that whole noun: the large or greater portion. Keep both ぶ sounds in だいぶぶん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大半](大半.md) (たいはん) is a close alternative for "most; the majority." [全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ), "all," is stronger. A small part, 小部分 (しょうぶぶん), is the dictionary's contrasting idea.

## Distinctive meaning

大部分 does not mean every part, and it does not state an exact percentage. It works with divisible amounts such as time and work as well as countable groups. In 大部分は分かりました (だいぶぶんはわかりました), "I understood most of it," the statement leaves room for some details that remain unclear.
