# 拝見 (はいけん) — look at (humble)

## Meaning

拝見する is a humble way to say that you look at or see something associated with a person you are treating respectfully. Depending on the object, it can also translate as "read."

- 先生のお写真を拝見しました。 (せんせいのおしゃしんをはいけんしました。) — I looked at your photograph, teacher.
- 資料を拝見してもよろしいですか。 (しりょうをはいけんしてもよろしいですか。) — May I look at the materials?

The thing viewed takes を. 拝見します is polite; 拝見いたします is a more deferential form.

## How the kanji combine

拝 contributes a respectful or humble attitude, while [見](../kanji/見.md) concerns seeing. Both are on-readings: ハイ + ケン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

見る (みる) is the ordinary verb. ご覧になる (ごらんになる) respectfully describes the other person's looking; it has the opposite social direction, not the opposite visual action.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 拝見 for your own side's action, not to elevate the listener's action. Humble language does not mean the photograph or document is unimportant; it expresses respect toward the person connected with it.
