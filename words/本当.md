# 本当 (ほんとう) - truth; real

## Meaning

本当 means truth or reality, and describes something as true or genuine. 本当です means "It is true." Before a noun, 本当の is the useful everyday pattern.

その話は本当ですか。 (そのはなしはほんとうですか。) - Is that story true?

本当の理由を教えてください。 (ほんとうのりゆうをおしえてください。) - Please tell me the real reason.

## How the kanji combine

[本](../kanji/本.md) can suggest a basis or what is original; [当](../kanji/当.md) can suggest being right or fitting. Their on readings form ほんとう. The word's established truth meaning is more useful than a literal character-by-character translation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

事実 (じじつ) is a fact or actual state of affairs. 嘘 (うそ), "lie; falsehood," contrasts with truth. 本物 (ほんもの) refers to a genuine article rather than an imitation.

## Distinctive meaning

本当に (ほんとうに) means "really; truly": 本当にありがとう, "Thank you very much, truly." Casual speech often shortens ほんとう to ほんと. An incredulous 本当？ can mean "Really?" rather than asking for a formal explanation of truth.
