# 眼鏡 (めがね) — eyeglasses

## Meaning

眼鏡 means glasses or spectacles worn to help vision. Kana めがね and katakana メガネ are common spellings of the same word.

新しい眼鏡を買いました。 (あたらしいめがねをかいました。) — I bought new glasses.

父は眼鏡をかけています。 (ちちはめがねをかけています。) — My father is wearing glasses.

## How the kanji combine

眼 concerns the eye and 鏡 a mirror or optical device. めがね is the conventional special reading of the compound, not ガン + キョウ. Learn the written pair together with the whole reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

コンタクトレンズ are contact lenses. サングラス are sunglasses. These are related eyewear terms, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 眼鏡をかける for putting on glasses, and 眼鏡をかけている for wearing them. 眼鏡を外す (めがねをはずす) means taking them off. 着る (きる), the verb for putting on upper-body clothing, is not used for glasses. The noun does not require a plural form for its two lenses.
