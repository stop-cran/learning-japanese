# 前後 (ぜんご) - front and back; before and after; around

## Meaning

前後 can mean front and back in space, or before and after in time.
After a quantity, age or time, it also means "around; approximately."

- 車の前後を確認してください。 (くるまのぜんごをかくにんしてください。) - Please check in front of and behind the car.
- 試験の前後は忙しいです。 (しけんのぜんごはいそがしいです。) - I am busy before and after the exam.
- 参加者は三十人前後でした。 (さんかしゃはさんじゅうにんぜんごでした。) - There were around thirty participants.

In the first two patterns, Nの前後 identifies the reference object or event.
Approximate quantities attach directly: 三十人前後, not 三十人の前後.
The final amount may be somewhat below or above thirty.
九時前後 (くじぜんご) similarly means around nine o'clock.

前後 also concerns order, context or consequences:
話の前後 (はなしのぜんご) is the surrounding sequence or context of an account;
前後を考える (ぜんごをかんがえる) can concern considering the circumstances and consequences.
前後する can mean getting out of order:
説明の順序が前後しました (せつめいのじゅんじょがぜんごしました),
"The explanation got out of sequence."

## How the kanji combine

[前](../kanji/前.md), before/front, and [後](../kanji/後.md), after/back,
form a coordinate pair. Their on readings are ゼン + ゴ.
The established compound extends to the surroundings of a reference point;
its approximation use is not read まえあと.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[約](約.md) (やく) precedes an approximate quantity;
[ほど](ほど.md) follows one.
[頃](頃.md) (ころ; suffix ごろ) locates an approximate time.
[順序](順序.md) (じゅんじょ) names an order or sequence.
These comparisons concern particular senses, not every use of 前後.

## Distinctive meaning

前後して can describe events occurring at nearly the same time or in close succession:
二人は前後して到着しました (ふたりはぜんごしてとうちゃくしました),
"The two arrived almost together."
This need not mean the exact same instant, despite overlap with [同時](同時.md)
(どうじ), "same time." Around an event, 前後 can also locate its approximate time.
前後 does not always mean a reversed order;
the attached phrase and the presence of する matter.
