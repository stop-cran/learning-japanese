# くしゃみ (くしゃみ) - sneeze; sneezing

## Meaning

くしゃみ is a sneeze or sneezing. Use くしゃみをする or くしゃみが出る (くしゃみがでる); the person sneezing is not an object that someone sneezes.

- 花粉でくしゃみが出ます。 (かふんでくしゃみがでます。) - Pollen makes me sneeze.
- くしゃみをしたら、猫が驚きました。 (くしゃみをしたら、ねこがおどろきました。) - When I sneezed, the cat was startled.

くしゃみする is also a dictionary-listed suru use.

## How the kanji combine

The usual writing is kana, so this article has no kanji components. The dictionary also records rare kanji spellings 嚏 and 嚔; they are not needed to read the examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[咳](咳.md) (せき), cough, is a different bodily action. A cough, a sneeze and a runny nose may occur together, but are not synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

This article teaches the ordinary sneeze sense with the reading くしゃみ. A separate dictionary charm expression restricted to くさめ is not a meaning to transfer to くしゃみ.
