# 受け取る (うけとる) - receive; interpret

## Meaning

受け取る is a transitive godan verb: to receive or accept something, or
to interpret someone's words or behavior. The item or message takes を.
The source can take から, and an interpretation can take と.

- 受付で荷物を受け取りました。 (うけつけでにもつをうけとりました。) - I received the parcel at reception.
- 彼の言葉を冗談と受け取りました。 (かれのことばをじょうだんとうけとりました。) - I took his words as a joke.

Useful forms are 受け取ります, 受け取った, and 受け取らない. Although
the verb ends in る, its negative is not 受け取ない.

## How the kanji combine

[受](../kanji/受.md) contributes receiving, through [受ける](受ける.md),
and [取](../kanji/取.md) contributes taking, through [取る](取る.md).
The first verb's stem joins the second: うけ + とる. Together they focus
on taking something in.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もらう](もらう.md) presents receiving something from someone and can
also frame a beneficial action. 受け取る focuses on receipt or acceptance.
[送る](送る.md) (おくる) is the opposite side of a delivery, not an
antonym for every interpretive use.

## Distinctive meaning

Receiving a message does not guarantee agreement with it. The second example
shows a further distinction: 受け取る can name how a listener understands
something, not merely whether the words reached them.
