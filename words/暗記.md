# 暗記 (あんき) - memorization

## Meaning

暗記 is memorization or learning something by heart. It is a noun and a transitive
する verb: mark the material memorized with を in 暗記する.

- 明日までに、この詩を暗記します。 (あしたまでに、このしをあんきします。) - I will memorize this poem by tomorrow.
- 公式を暗記しましたが、使い方はまだ分かりません。 (こうしきをあんきしましたが、つかいかたはまだわかりません。) - I memorized the formula, but I still do not know how to use it.

暗記した is the completed action; 暗記している can describe having the material
memorized. 暗記が苦手 (あんきがにがて) means being poor at memorization.

## How the kanji combine

[暗](../kanji/暗.md) has a "without looking at the text" association here, rather
than describing a dark room. [記](../kanji/記.md) concerns recording or remembering.
Together they name retaining material so that it can be recalled without consulting
it. The on readings are アン + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[覚える](覚える.md) (おぼえる) covers remembering, memorizing, and acquiring skills;
暗記する focuses on committing material to memory. [記憶](記憶.md) (きおく) names
memory or remembering more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

暗記 does not itself guarantee understanding. The formula example deliberately
separates remembering the words or symbols from knowing how to apply them.
Conversely, memorization is not inherently meaningless: it can be one useful part
of learning a poem, vocabulary, or a procedure.
