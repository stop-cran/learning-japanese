# 栄える (さかえる) - prosper; flourish

## Meaning

栄える means to prosper, flourish, or thrive. It is an intransitive ichidan
verb: the place, business, or other thing that flourishes is the subject.

- この町は貿易で栄えました。 (このまちはぼうえきでさかえました。) - This town prospered through trade.
- 港の近くに商店街が栄えていました。 (みなとのちかくにしょうてんがいがさかえていました。) - A shopping district flourished near the port.

Forms include 栄えます (さかえます), 栄えて (さかえて), and
栄えない (さかえない). 栄えている can describe an ongoing flourishing state.

## How the kanji combine

[栄](../kanji/栄.md) contributes flourishing and supplies さか; える is
okurigana. The conjugation stem is 栄え (さかえ), not just the part
written with the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[盛ん](盛ん.md) (さかん) describes vigorous activity or a flourishing
state as a na-adjective. Compare 商業が盛んな町 (しょうぎょうがさかんなまち),
a town with thriving commerce, with 商業で栄えた町
(しょうぎょうでさかえたまち), a town that prospered through commerce.

[豊か](豊か.md) (ゆたか) is a na-adjective for abundance or prosperity.
Financial prosperity can overlap with 栄える, but abundant resources or
experience do not by themselves describe something flourishing over time.

## Distinctive meaning

This article teaches さかえる, not the separately listed 栄える はえる,
which shares an entry with 映える and can mean shine or look attractive.
Do not freely exchange the readings because the written forms match.

栄える is not a transitive verb meaning make a town prosper. In the first
example, で marks the means or basis of the town's prosperity, not a direct
object. The English idea flourishing also does not make this verb a direct
substitute for every adjective meaning active or abundant.
