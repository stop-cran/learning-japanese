# 重役 (じゅうやく) - company executive; important post

## Meaning

重役 can name a company director, executive, or top management. It also names an important post or a role carrying heavy responsibilities.

- 会社の重役が会議に出席しました。
  (かいしゃのじゅうやくがかいぎにしゅっせきしました。) - A company executive attended the meeting.
- 彼は重役を任されました。
  (かれはじゅうやくをまかされました。) - He was entrusted with a position of great responsibility.

The first example names a person; the second concerns the responsibility entrusted to someone.

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) contributes importance or heavy responsibility, and [役](../kanji/役.md) an office or role. The modifier identifies a weighty role, not a physically heavy actor. The on readings are ジュウ + ヤク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[社長](社長.md) (しゃちょう) specifies the company president. 重役 can include other executives rather than naming just that one post. [役](役.md) (やく) and [役割](役割.md) (やくわり) concern roles or responsibilities more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every department supervisor is a company director, and [監督](監督.md) (かんとく), such as a film director, is a different role. JMdict restricts the executive sense to じゅうやく. Its obsolete reading おもやく belongs to the important-role sense; it is not another reading to attach freely to the company-executive example.
