# 沖 (おき) - offshore; open sea

## Meaning

沖 is a noun for water away from the shore: offshore waters or the open sea. 沖に marks a location there; 沖へ marks movement toward it.

- 沖に船が見えます。 (おきにふねがみえます。) - I can see a boat offshore.
- 船は沖へ出ました。 (ふねはおきへでました。) - The boat headed out to sea.

In geographical descriptions, a place name can come before 沖: 千葉県沖 (ちばけんおき), "off the coast of Chiba Prefecture." The expression locates something relative to the coast.

## How the kanji combine

沖 is a single-kanji native noun read おき. There is no compound to divide into on readings. Its water-related written form can help you remember the sea setting, but the whole word supplies the offshore meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[海](海.md) (うみ) means "sea" broadly; 沖 specifically directs attention away from shore.

[岸](岸.md) (きし), "shore; bank," is a useful spatial contrast. 沖 is not merely another word for a beach.

## Distinctive meaning

English "open sea" can suggest a great distance, but 沖 does not specify a fixed number of kilometers. Context determines how far offshore is intended. Treat 沖 as a location noun, not an adjective inserted directly before an English-style noun phrase; 沖の船 (おきのふね) means "the boat offshore."
