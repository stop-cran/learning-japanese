# 余裕 (よゆう) - leeway; room to spare; composure

## Meaning

余裕 is spare capacity or leeway in time, money, space, or effort. It can also mean mental composure. Use 余裕がある, "have room to spare," and 余裕がない, "have no leeway."

- 出発までまだ時間に余裕があります。 (しゅっぱつまでまだじかんによゆうがあります。) - There is still plenty of time before departure.
- 忙しくて、気持ちに余裕がありません。 (いそがしくて、きもちによゆうがありません。) - I am so busy that I have no mental breathing room.

Verb dictionary form + 余裕 gives the capacity to do something: 休む余裕がない (やすむよゆうがない), "have no room to take a break."

## How the kanji combine

[余](../kanji/余.md) contributes what remains or is in excess. 裕 contributes plenty or roominess. Together they express having more capacity than the immediate demand consumes. The on readings are ヨ + ユウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暇](暇.md) (ひま) concerns free time. 余裕 is broader: someone may have financial leeway but little free time. 不足 (ふそく), "shortage," contrasts with enough capacity to spare in a particular resource.

## Distinctive meaning

余裕 does not always mean an idle period or unused money. It can describe calmness under pressure: 余裕のある態度 (よゆうのあるたいど), "a composed manner." Conversely, 余裕がない may explain emotional strain even when the sentence gives no measured shortage of minutes or money.
