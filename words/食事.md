# 食事 (しょくじ) — a meal

## Meaning

食事 is a meal, and as a する-verb, "to have a meal": 食事の時間 (mealtime), 食事をする (have a meal), 一緒に食事しませんか (shall we eat together?), 食事代 しょくじだい (the cost of a meal).

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) (eat) plus 事 (matter, affair, こと／ごと): "the matter of eating". Both are on readings (ショク + ジ), 事 is read ジ in compounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: ご飯 ごはん (rice, a meal; everyday).
- Specific meals: 朝食 ちょうしょく／朝ご飯 あさごはん, 昼食 ちゅうしょく／昼ご飯, 夕食 ゆうしょく／晩ご飯.
- Related verb: [食べる](食べる.md) たべる.

## Distinctive meaning

食事 is a polite or neutral noun for the act of having a meal, so it is used in invitations and notices (食事会 しょくじかい, a dinner party). In casual speech people say ご飯を食べる instead.
