# サービス (サービス) - service; complimentary extra

## Meaning

サービス can mean service, help, or an offered facility. In shops and restaurants it can also refer to a discount or something provided free as an extra.

- このホテルはサービスがいいです。 (このホテルはサービスがいいです。) - This hotel provides good service.
- このコーヒーはサービスです。 (このコーヒーはサービスです。) - This coffee is complimentary.

The first evaluates how guests are served; it does not say the hotel is free. In the second, the context identifies a no-charge item. サービスする can mean providing such an extra: 一個サービスします (いっこサービスします), "I'll give you one extra for free."

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana. Keep the long vowel mark in サー and the final ス. There are no written kanji components to interpret.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[無料](無料.md) (むりょう) and [ただ](ただ.md) explicitly express no charge. サービス has broader meanings and does not guarantee that everything offered is free. 割引 (わりびき) is a discount, which reduces a price rather than necessarily eliminating it.

## Distinctive meaning

Ask what kind of service is being discussed. 配達サービス (はいたつサービス), "delivery service," can be a paid offering; コーヒーはサービスです can offer a free drink. The word alone does not establish the price. Its sports sense, a serve, belongs to a different context from customer service.
