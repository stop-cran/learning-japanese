# 祭日 (さいじつ) - festival day; national holiday

## Meaning

祭日 can be a day on which a festival or religious observance is held. It is also used for a national holiday. Identify the calendar or observance being discussed.

- この神社の祭日は五月です。 (このじんじゃのさいじつはごがつです。) - This shrine's festival day falls in May.
- その店は日曜と祭日に休みます。 (そのみせはにちようとさいじつにやすみます。) - That shop closes on Sundays and national holidays.

The first example concerns a shrine's observance; it does not establish a nationwide day off.

## How the kanji combine

[祭](../kanji/祭.md) contributes festival or ritual observance, and [日](../kanji/日.md) day. The latter is the head: a day of observance. The on readings are サイ + ジツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

祝日 (しゅくじつ) also names a celebratory day or a national holiday. [休日](休日.md) (きゅうじつ) is a day off or holiday more broadly; a person's day off need not be a festival day. [祭](祭.md) (まつり), also written 祭り, names the festival itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer a strict present-day calendar split between 祭日 and 祝日 merely from their characters. Their holiday uses overlap. Equally, not every festival implies that schools, shops, or workplaces close: check the actual institution's schedule.
