# 主宰 (しゅさい) — directing a group; its leader

## Meaning

主宰する means to lead and direct a group or its work. The noun can also identify the person at its head. Use を for the group the person runs.

- 叔母は小さな劇団を主宰している。 (おばはちいさなげきだんをしゅさいしている。) — My aunt runs a small theater company.
- 雑誌の取材でその劇団の主宰に話を聞いた。 (ざっしのしゅざいでそのげきだんのしゅさいにはなしをきいた。) — For a magazine, I interviewed the head of that theater company.

## How the kanji combine

[主](../kanji/主.md) (しゅ) points to the person in the leading role; [宰](../kanji/宰.md) (さい) concerns directing affairs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

主催 (also しゅさい) is organizing or hosting an event. A group that a director leads (主宰) can in turn host (主催) a festival. [司会](司会.md) (しかい) is presiding over the proceedings, for example as an emcee. [監督](監督.md) (かんとく) can also name someone who directs, such as a film director.

## Distinctive meaning

The spoken word しゅさい does not decide the spelling. If a profile tells you who runs a theater company, think 主宰. If an event notice identifies its organizer, think 主催.
