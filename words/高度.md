# 高度 (こうど) - altitude; advanced level

## Meaning

高度 is a noun for altitude, height, or elevation. It also describes
a high degree or advanced level: 高度な技術 (こうどなぎじゅつ),
"advanced technology." Use な before a noun in this descriptive use
and に before a verb; の-modification is also recorded.

- 飛行機は高度を下げました。 (ひこうきはこうどをさげました。) - The airplane descended to a lower altitude.
- この装置には高度な技術が使われています。 (このそうちにはこうどなぎじゅつがつかわれています。) - Advanced technology is used in this device.
- 作業は高度に機械化されています。 (さぎょうはこうどにきかいかされています。) - The work is highly mechanized.

The degree sense can convey sophistication or strength, depending on
what is being described. It is not confined to physical height.

## How the kanji combine

[高](../kanji/高.md) contributes high; [度](../kanji/度.md) contributes
degree or extent. Their modifier-plus-head relationship points to
height or a high degree. The on readings are コウ + ド.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[高い](高い.md) (たかい) is the broad adjective high.
[高等](高等.md) (こうとう) concerns a high class or grade, and
[高級](高級.md) (こうきゅう) can describe superior quality.
Their ranges overlap without making every advanced technique a luxury.
Low altitude or a low degree provides the relevant contrast in context.

## Distinctive meaning

The altitude noun and the advanced-level description need different
English constructions. 高度を下げる lowers altitude; 高度な技術
does not describe technology physically positioned overhead.
