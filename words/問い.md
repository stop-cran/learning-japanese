# 問い (とい) - question; query

## Meaning

問い is a noun for a question or query. It can name something asked aloud, a written question, or a question considered in discussion. Learn 問いに答える (といにこたえる), "answer a question," with に.

- 次の問いに答えてください。 (つぎのといにこたえてください。) - Please answer the following question.
- 「幸せとは何か」という問いについて話しました。 (「しあわせとはなにか」というといについてはなしました。) - We discussed the question "What is happiness?"

問いを立てる (といをたてる) means "formulate a question." を marks the question being formulated, whereas に marks what is answered.

## How the kanji combine

[問](../kanji/問.md) carries the asking meaning. 問い is a noun related to 問う (とう), "ask; question." The written い belongs to this noun form; it is not an adjective ending here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[質問](質問.md) (しつもん) also means a question and can combine with する for the act of asking.

[問題](問題.md) (もんだい) may name an exercise, issue, or problem rather than specifically a request for an answer.

## Distinctive meaning

問い is useful in instructions and when framing an issue for thought, but it is not restricted to philosophical questions. 答え (こたえ), "answer," is the corresponding response, not a contradictory meaning. 問いかけ and questioning can invite reflection even when no immediate answer is available.
