# 広告 (こうこく) - advertisement; advertising

## Meaning

広告 is a noun for an advertisement or advertising, and **広告する** means advertise something.
Useful expressions include **広告を出す** (こうこくをだす), "place an advertisement,"
and **広告を見る** (こうこくをみる), "see an advertisement."

- 新聞に店の広告を出しました。 (しんぶんにみせのこうこくをだしました。) - We placed an advertisement for the shop in the newspaper.
- この広告を見て、商品に興味を持ちました。 (このこうこくをみて、しょうひんにきょうみをもちました。) - Seeing this advertisement made me interested in the product.

に identifies the medium where the advertisement appears in the first sentence;
の connects it with the shop being advertised.

## How the kanji combine

[広](../kanji/広.md) contributes "widely," and [告](../kanji/告.md) contributes announcing or informing.
The combination conveys making something widely known. The on readings コウ + コク give こうこく.
This helps recall the word without defining every public announcement as commercial advertising.

## Synonyms and antonyms

宣伝 (せんでん) names promotion or publicity.
[掲示](掲示.md) (けいじ) concerns posting information; a posted notice need not promote a product.
[知らせ](知らせ.md) (しらせ) is information or news being conveyed and is broader than an advertisement.

## Distinctive meaning

広告 can refer to a particular item someone reads or to advertising as an activity.
The context determines whether English needs "an advertisement" or the uncountable "advertising."
It is not limited to printed material: an advertisement displayed on a screen can also be 広告.
