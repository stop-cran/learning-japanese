# 関西 (かんさい) - Kansai region

## Meaning

関西 names the Kansai region of Japan, centered on Kyoto, Osaka and Kobe
and their surrounding area. It is a regional name, not just another name
for Osaka city.

- 来月、関西へ旅行します。
  (らいげつ、かんさいへりょこうします。) - I will travel to Kansai next month.
- 関西の友達が遊びに来ました。
  (かんさいのともだちがあそびにきました。) - A friend from Kansai came to visit.

関西の modifies another noun. 関西弁 (かんさいべん) refers to Kansai speech;
the regional label does not imply that everyone there speaks identically.

## How the kanji combine

[関](../kanji/関.md) contributes a barrier or checkpoint, and
[西](../kanji/西.md) west.
The historical association is land west of a barrier.
The on readings カン + サイ give かんさい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[関東](関東.md) (かんとう) is the region around Tokyo, a useful geographic
contrast rather than an antonym in every context.
近畿 (きんき) is a related regional name; do not assume that every
organization uses identical boundaries for the two labels.

## Distinctive meaning

Historical texts can use 関西 for a broader western area, with the relevant
barrier and extent varying by period. That does not make present-day 関西
the whole western half of Japan in every travel conversation.
The reading taught here is かんさい; the dictionary's older かんせい
reading is not a replacement for it in the examples above.
