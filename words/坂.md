# 坂 (さか) -- slope; hill

## Meaning

坂 is sloping ground, especially a road or path that rises or falls. English may call such a stretch "a hill."

駅までの道には急な坂があります。 (えきまでのみちにはきゅうなさかがあります。) -- There is a steep hill on the road to the station.

坂を上る (さかをのぼる) means going up a slope; 坂を下る (さかをくだる) means going down it. Here を marks the route traveled.

## How the kanji combine

坂 is a single character read with the native reading さか. Its meaning concerns an incline. The visible parts inside the character are not separate spoken words in this noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

斜面 (しゃめん) describes a sloping surface. 丘 (おか) is a hill as a raised landform. These meanings overlap in some landscapes, but 坂 often focuses on the incline one travels along.

## Distinctive meaning

The same 坂 can be uphill for one traveler and downhill for another. The noun does not itself give the direction of movement; the following verb or a word such as 上り (のぼり) supplies that information.
