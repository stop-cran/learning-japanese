# 歌謡 (かよう) - song; ballad

## Meaning

歌謡 is a song or vocal song form. The source's English "ballad" is one translation, not a rule that every 歌謡 must tell a narrative.

- 日本の歌謡について調べています。 (にほんのかようについてしらべています。) - I am researching Japanese songs.
- ラジオで昔の歌謡曲を聞きました。 (ラジオでむかしのかようきょくをききました。) - I heard an old popular song on the radio.

歌謡曲 (かようきょく) names kayokyoku, a form of Japanese popular music that developed in the Showa era. It is not simply every possible kind of music.

## How the kanji combine

[歌](../kanji/歌.md) and 謡 both concern singing or song. Their related meanings reinforce the song idea. The on readings are カ + ヨウ; keep the long よう in かよう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[歌](歌.md) (うた) is the everyday song noun and also has a poetry use. [曲](曲.md) (きょく) is a piece of music, including instrumental pieces. [音楽](音楽.md) (おんがく) names music more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 歌謡 as a song-category word rather than replacing every everyday 歌 with it. The overlap does not make an instrumental piano piece a vocal song, and the compound 歌謡曲 has its own established popular-music use.
