# 遅刻 (ちこく) — arriving late

## Meaning

遅刻 is lateness in arriving where you are expected, such as school, work, or a meeting.
Use **event or destination に遅刻する** for being late to it.
The amount of lateness can appear directly before the verb.

- 授業に十分遅刻しました。 (じゅぎょうにじゅっぷんちこくしました。) — I was ten minutes late for class.
- 明日は遅刻しないように、早く家を出ます。 (あしたはちこくしないように、はやくいえをでます。) — Tomorrow I will leave home early so that I am not late.

Here 十分 is じゅっぷん, "ten minutes," not じゅうぶん, "enough."

## How the kanji combine

[遅](../kanji/遅.md) contributes lateness, and [刻](../kanji/刻.md) contributes time.
Together they point to being late relative to an expected time, not to carving slowly.
The on readings are チ + コク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[遅れる](遅れる.md) おくれる is broader: it can mean being late or delayed, as in 電車が遅れました
(でんしゃがおくれました), "The train was delayed."
[欠席](欠席.md) けっせき means absence or non-attendance, not simply arriving after the start.
Arriving late and not attending are different facts.

## Distinctive meaning

遅刻 evaluates arrival against a schedule or expectation.
Leaving home late may cause 遅刻, but the two are not identical: you might still arrive on time.
In the class example, the ten minutes measure how late the arrival was, not how long the class lasted.
