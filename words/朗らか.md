# 朗らか (ほがらか) - cheerful; bright and clear

## Meaning

朗らか is a な-adjective describing a cheerful, merry, open disposition or a bright, pleasant sound. The dictionary also records bright, fine, clear skies or days.

- 彼女は朗らかな人です。 (かのじょはほがらかなひとです。) - She is a cheerful person.
- 子供たちは朗らかに笑った。 (こどもたちはほがらかにわらった。) - The children laughed cheerfully.

Use 朗らかな before a noun and 朗らかに to modify an action. 朗らかな笑い声 (ほがらかなわらいごえ) is cheerful laughter. The corresponding noun is 朗らかさ (ほがらかさ), cheerfulness.

## How the kanji combine

朗 contributes brightness or cheerfulness in the native form ほがらか. The following kana complete the adjective; there is no multi-kanji combination to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[明るい](明るい.md) (あかるい) and [陽気](陽気.md) (ようき) overlap in cheerful-person uses. [晴れ](晴れ.md) (はれ) names clear weather.

[明らか](明らか.md) (あきらか) commonly means clear or obvious. That does not make 朗らか the ordinary adjective for an unambiguous answer.

## Distinctive meaning

朗らか can present a person, voice, or scene as pleasantly bright. Its clear-sky sense does not establish that it can replace every "clear" adjective, such as "transparent" water or "precise" instructions. Cheerfulness also does not by itself mean good physical health.
