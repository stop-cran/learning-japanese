# 消費 (しょうひ) - consumption; expenditure

## Meaning

消費 means consumption or expenditure: using resources, goods, energy, or money. It is a noun and a transitive する-verb. The resource consumed takes を.

- このエアコンは電気をたくさん消費します。 (このエアコンはでんきをたくさんしょうひします。) - This air conditioner consumes a lot of electricity.
- 家庭の水の消費量を調べました。 (かていのみずのしょうひりょうをしらべました。) - We investigated household water consumption.

消費量 (しょうひりょう) names the amount consumed. As a noun, 消費 can be increased or reduced: 消費を減らす (しょうひをへらす), "reduce consumption."

## How the kanji combine

[消](../kanji/消.md) contributes disappearing or using up; [費](../kanji/費.md) contributes spending. The related ideas combine around resources being expended. Read ショウ + ヒ, with a long しょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[使う](使う.md) (つかう) is broader "use": using a chair does not normally consume the chair. 浪費 (ろうひ) adds the judgment of wastefulness. [生産](生産.md) (せいさん), "production," is an economic counterpart rather than a direct opposite in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

消費 is not inherently wasteful and does not require using up an entire supply. 消費する can describe necessary electricity use or ordinary household spending. Compounds such as 消費者 (しょうひしゃ), "consumer," connect the word to the people who use or buy goods and services.
