# 確かめる (たしかめる) - check; make sure

## Meaning

確かめる is a transitive ichidan verb meaning "check; make sure; verify." The thing checked takes を. A question clause ending in か can supply what needs verification. Forms include 確かめます, 確かめて, and 確かめた.

- 出発の時間を確かめました。 (しゅっぱつのじかんをたしかめました。) - I checked the departure time.
- ドアが閉まっているか確かめてください。 (ドアがしまっているかたしかめてください。) - Please check whether the door is closed.

In the second sentence, か marks an embedded question, not a question addressed directly to the listener.

## How the kanji combine

[確](../kanji/確.md) contributes certainty. The verb is related to [確か](確か.md) (たしか), "certain; reliable"; the kana completes たしかめる. It is a native verb, not a string of on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[確認](確認.md) (かくにん) with する overlaps with checking or confirming. [調べる](調べる.md) (しらべる) can describe investigating more broadly; 確かめる highlights settling whether something is so.

## Distinctive meaning

確かめる describes the checking action, not a guarantee that the expected answer will be confirmed. You may check and discover that the information was wrong. Both a tangible condition, such as a closed door, and information, such as a departure time, can be checked with this verb.
