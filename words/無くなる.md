# 無くなる (なくなる) — go missing; run out

## Meaning

無くなる means to become absent: go missing, disappear, or be used up. It is commonly written なくなる. The thing that is no longer present takes が.

- 鍵がなくなりました。  
  かぎがなくなりました。  
  "My keys have gone missing."
- 痛みがなくなりました。  
  いたみがなくなりました。  
  "The pain has gone away."

With supplies, it means "run out": 牛乳がなくなった (ぎゅうにゅうがなくなった), "The milk has run out".

## How the kanji combine

無 relates to absence. The expression combines ない in its なく form with なる, "become". Conjugation follows godan なる: なくなります, なくなって, なくならない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

なくす means to lose something: 鍵をなくした (かぎをなくした), "I lost my keys". [消える](消える.md) overlaps with disappearing but also describes a light going out. The words are not interchangeable in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

無くなる does not identify who lost or used something up. Also distinguish 亡くなる (なくなる), "pass away": it has the same pronunciation, but uses a different kanji and refers respectfully to a person's death.
