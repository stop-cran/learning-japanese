# エンジン (エンジン) - engine

## Meaning

エンジン is a noun for an engine, such as the power-producing unit in a car or boat. Useful expressions distinguish starting an engine from the engine starting or stopping by itself.

- 車のエンジンをかけました。 (くるまのエンジンをかけました。) - I started the car's engine.
- 途中でエンジンが止まりました。 (とちゅうでエンジンがとまりました。) - The engine stopped on the way.

を marks the engine in エンジンをかける; が marks it as the subject of 止まる. エンジンを止める (エンジンをとめる) means to stop or turn off the engine.

## How the kanji combine

エンジン is a katakana loanword, not a kanji compound. Read its written sequence エ・ン・ジ・ン. There is no long-vowel mark and no small ッ in the spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[機械](機械.md) (きかい), "machine," is broader: not every machine is an engine. モーター means a motor, particularly useful for electric motors. These are related mechanism words, not a guarantee that every technical device has interchangeable names.

## Distinctive meaning

The engine is not the whole vehicle. A car can have an engine problem even when its tires and doors are fine. Learn the noun with the operation verb: starting, stopping, or checking the engine requires an appropriate verb, not an invented エンジンする.
