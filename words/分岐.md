# 分岐 (ぶんき) — branching off; divergence

## Meaning

分岐 is the division of one course into different paths. In 道が分岐する, the road is what branches; no person needs to divide it.

- 橋を渡ると、道は左右に分岐する。 (はしをわたると、みちはさゆうにぶんきする。) — After the bridge, the road forks left and right.
- この物語は、選んだ答えによって結末が分岐する。 (このものがたりは、えらんだこたえによってけつまつがぶんきする。) — In this story the ending branches depending on the answer you choose.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) is ぶん, division; [岐](../kanji/岐.md) is き, a fork. Together they describe branching, not a particular number of roads.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[分かれる](分かれる.md) (わかれる) can describe roads branching, but also other kinds of separation. 分岐点 (ぶんきてん) is the *point* where the paths diverge.

## Distinctive meaning

A fork on a map is a physical example. In the story example, different answers lead to different possible endings; no physical road is involved.
