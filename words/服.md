# 服 (ふく) — clothes

## Meaning

服 means clothes or clothing, especially ordinary Western-style clothes in everyday conversation. Depending on context it can refer to an outfit or an individual item; Japanese does not mark singular and plural here.

- 新しい服を買いました。 (あたらしいふくをかいました。) — I bought some new clothes.
- 今日は白い服を着ています。 (きょうはしろいふくをきています。) — I am wearing white clothes today.

## How the kanji combine

There is only one kanji, [服](../kanji/服.md), read with its on reading フク. This is a single-kanji kango word, not a native kun-reading word or a two-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [洋服](洋服.md) ようふく explicitly means Western-style clothes, contrasting with 和服 わふく, Japanese-style clothes.
- 衣類 いるい means clothing or garments as a category, often in labels or more formal descriptions.
- 着物 きもの commonly means a kimono; it is not a replacement for 服 in every everyday clothing sentence. There is no simple direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

服 names the clothes, not the action of wearing them. Use [着る](着る.md) きる for putting on clothes worn from the shoulders down; 着ています きています expresses the resulting state, "is wearing."
