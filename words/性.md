# 性 (せい) - sex; gender; -ness (suffix)

## Meaning

性, read せい, is a noun that can mean sex or gender. Depending on context it can also concern sexuality, sexual attraction, or sexual activity. In grammar, 性 means grammatical gender; the subject determines which meaning is intended.

- 性による差別をなくしたいです。 (せいによるさべつをなくしたいです。) - I want to eliminate discrimination based on sex or gender.
- この製品の安全性を確認しました。 (このせいひんのあんぜんせいをかくにんしました。) - We checked this product's safety.

In the second example, **-性** is a noun-forming suffix indicating a quality or condition, similar to English "-ness" or "-ity." 安全性 means "safety," not a kind of sex or gender.

## How the kanji combine

[性](../kanji/性.md) contributes nature, character, or a defining property. The reading here is the on reading セイ. Recognize the whole compound before deciding which part of this meaning range is active.

## Synonyms and antonyms

性別 (せいべつ) specifically concerns classification by sex or gender. 性質 (せいしつ) describes a nature or property. These comparisons do not make every word ending in 性 interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

The suffix is productive but not a rule for translating every English abstract noun. Learn established combinations, such as 可能性 (かのうせい), "possibility." Also distinguish grammatical gender from a person's sex or gender: in a discussion of noun classes, 性 describes a grammatical category.
