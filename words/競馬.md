# 競馬 (けいば) - horse racing

## Meaning

競馬 is horse racing: races in which horses compete.
It can refer to the sport or to a horse-racing event.

- 日曜日に競馬を見に行きました。 (にちようびにけいばをみにいきました。) - I went to watch horse racing on Sunday.
- 競馬場には大勢の人がいました。 (けいばじょうにはおおぜいのひとがいました。) - There were many people at the racecourse.

競馬場 (けいばじょう) is a horse-racing venue or racecourse.

## How the kanji combine

[競](../kanji/競.md), "compete," combines with [馬](../kanji/馬.md), "horse": horses competing in races.
Learn ケイ + バ as this word's reading.
競 also has キョウ in other compounds; 競馬 is not きょうば.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[競争](競争.md) (きょうそう) is competition or a race more broadly.
[競技](競技.md) (きょうぎ) is a sporting event or competition.
Neither by itself specifies horses.
There is no useful single antonym for horse racing.

## Distinctive meaning

競馬 is associated with betting, but watching a race does not imply placing a bet.
The word is not a general synonym for all gambling or all equestrian activities.
The horses and the racing activity are central to its meaning.
