# 代理 (だいり) - representation; proxy

## Meaning

代理 means acting on another person's behalf, or the person serving as a representative or stand-in. Learn **person の代理で** and **person の代理として**.

- 部長の代理で会議に出席しました。 (ぶちょうのだいりでかいぎにしゅっせきしました。) - I attended the meeting on behalf of the department head.
- 今日は私が代理を務めます。 (きょうはわたしがだいりをつとめます。) - Today I will serve as the substitute.

The first example names the person represented; the second names the role undertaken. 代理する is also possible as a verb for representing someone.

## How the kanji combine

[代](../kanji/代.md) contributes substitution, and [理](../kanji/理.md) contributes handling or managing affairs here. The compound concerns handling matters in another's place. The on readings are ダイ + リ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[代わり](代わり.md) (かわり) is broader: a replacement or doing something instead of another. 代理 foregrounds the representative role. 本人 (ほんにん), "the person concerned," contrasts with a proxy acting for that person.

## Distinctive meaning

English "agency" here concerns representation, not automatically an office or company. A 代理 can attend a meeting or handle a matter without permanently taking over the original person's position. The noun alone also does not tell you the legal scope of that person's authority; the surrounding agreement or procedure supplies it.
