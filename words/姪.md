# 姪 (めい) - niece

## Meaning

姪 is a niece: a daughter of one's brother or sister.

- 姪は今年六歳になります。 (めいはことしろくさいになります。) - My niece turns six this year.
- 姉の娘は私の姪です。 (あねのむすめはわたしのめいです。) - My older sister's daughter is my niece.

The relationship depends on whose family connection is being described.
The same child is her mother's 娘 (むすめ), "daughter," and her aunt's or uncle's 姪.

## How the kanji combine

姪 is a single character read with the native word めい here.
It is not pronounced by adding a separate sound for each visible component.

## Synonyms and antonyms

姪っ子 (めいっこ) is another expression for a niece.
姪の子 does not mean "niece"; the の would make it "the niece's child."
甥 (おい), "nephew," is the corresponding brother's or sister's son.
[親類](親類.md) (しんるい) and [親戚](親戚.md) (しんせき) are broader words for relatives.

## Distinctive meaning

姪 identifies a particular family relationship, not any young female relative.
It does not mean one's own [娘](娘.md), nor does it mean "cousin."
Use a possessive or surrounding context when it matters whose niece she is.
