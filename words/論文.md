# 論文 (ろんぶん) - academic paper; thesis

## Meaning

論文 is a written paper presenting research or a sustained argument. Depending on context, it may be a thesis, scholarly article, or argumentative essay. Use 論文を書く (ろんぶんをかく), "write a paper," and 論文を提出する (ろんぶんをていしゅつする), "submit a paper."

- 日本語の歴史について論文を書いています。 (にほんごのれきしについてろんぶんをかいています。) - I am writing a paper on the history of Japanese.
- 卒業論文を先生に提出しました。 (そつぎょうろんぶんをせんせいにていしゅつしました。) - I submitted my graduation thesis to my teacher.

について marks the subject discussed. 卒業論文 identifies the work by its graduation requirement.

## How the kanji combine

[論](../kanji/論.md) contributes discussion or reasoned argument, and [文](../kanji/文.md) contributes writing or a text. The first character specifies the kind of writing. The on readings are ロン + ブン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作文](作文.md) (さくぶん) can be a composition or writing exercise. [記事](記事.md) (きじ) is an article or report, often in news contexts. These overlap in being written texts, but do not automatically have a 論文's research or argumentative purpose.

## Distinctive meaning

論文 names the kind of work, not the sheet of paper it is printed on. Its length, degree requirement, and publication status come from context. An unpublished student thesis and a published research paper can both be 論文.
