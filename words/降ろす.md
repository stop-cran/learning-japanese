# 降ろす (おろす) - lower; unload; drop off

## Meaning

降ろす is a transitive godan verb meaning "lower; take down," and especially "unload; let someone off a vehicle." The person or load takes を; から marks its starting place. Forms include 降ろします and 降ろして.

- トラックから荷物を降ろしました。 (トラックからにもつをおろしました。) - We unloaded the luggage from the truck.
- 次の角で降ろしてください。 (つぎのかどでおろしてください。) - Please drop me off at the next corner.

In the second example, the passenger being dropped off is understood. で identifies where the action should happen.

## How the kanji combine

[降](../kanji/降.md) carries the idea of descending. The written ろす completes the native verb おろす. Compare the related intransitive [降りる](降りる.md) (おりる), "get off; descend": 降ろす has someone cause that movement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下ろす](下ろす.md), also おろす, overlaps in lowering and taking down. 降ろす is particularly associated with passengers and cargo. [上げる](上げる.md) (あげる), "raise," is an opposite for vertical movement, not for every unloading situation.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 子供が車から降りる, "the child gets out of the car," with 子供を車から降ろす, "take the child out of the car." The shift from が to を identifies who moves and who causes the movement. English "drop off" here need not imply dropping someone from a height.
