# 結わえる (ゆわえる) - tie up; fasten

## Meaning

結わえる means to bind, tie up or fasten something. It is transitive: what is tied takes を. A cord or other tying material can take で.

- 古い新聞をひもで結わえました。 (ふるいしんぶんをひもでゆわえました。) - I tied up the old newspapers with string.
- 荷物をロープで結わえてください。 (にもつをロープでゆわえてください。) - Please tie up the luggage with a rope.

## How the kanji combine

[結](../kanji/結.md) supplies ゆ, and わえる is written in kana. This is an ichidan verb: 結わえます (ゆわえます), 結わえて (ゆわえて) and 結わえない (ゆわえない). Its grammatical stem ゆわえ includes kana after the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結ぶ](結ぶ.md) (むすぶ) and [縛る](縛る.md) (しばる) overlap in physical tying. [結う](結う.md) (ゆう) includes doing up hair and binding, but is a godan verb, not another conjugation of 結わえる.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 結わえて (ゆわえて), the て-form here, with 結って (ゆって), from 結う. Similar sounds and the shared kanji do not make the forms interchangeable. An extended phrase such as 契約を結ぶ (けいやくをむすぶ), "conclude a contract," is not a pattern to copy mechanically with every tying verb.
