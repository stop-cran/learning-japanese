# 堅い (かたい) -- hard; solid

## Meaning

堅い describes something hard or solid. It can also describe steadiness or dependability rather than physical hardness.

- この木は堅いので、切るのが大変です。 (このきはかたいので、きるのがたいへんです。) -- This wood is hard, so it is difficult to cut.
- 堅い仕事に就きたいです。 (かたいしごとにつきたいです。) -- I want to enter a dependable line of work.

This is an i-adjective: 堅くない (かたくない), "not hard," and 堅かった (かたかった), "was hard."

## How the kanji combine

堅 supplies かた and the idea of firmness. The written い completes the adjective. There is no second kanji or compound reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

硬い and 固い are overlapping spellings of かたい. 硬い is especially useful for hard metal or stone; 堅い is useful for solid wood or dependable character. These are spelling tendencies, not completely separate meanings.

## Distinctive meaning

柔らかい (やわらかい), "soft," contrasts with physical hardness. It is not automatically the opposite of every figurative use. Interpret 堅い仕事 through reliability, not as work made of hard material.
