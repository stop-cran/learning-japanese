# 勝負 (しょうぶ) - match; contest; victory or defeat

## Meaning

勝負 can name victory or defeat, or a match, contest, or showdown in which people compete. 勝負する means to compete; the opponent commonly takes と.

- 友達と将棋で勝負しました。 (ともだちとしょうぎでしょうぶしました。) - I competed against a friend at shogi.
- まだ勝負はついていません。 (まだしょうぶはついていません。) - The outcome has not been decided yet.

で identifies the game in the first example. 勝負がつく (しょうぶがつく) means that the issue of winning and losing is settled.

## How the kanji combine

[勝](../kanji/勝.md) contributes winning, and [負](../kanji/負.md) losing. Like [勝敗](勝敗.md) (しょうはい), it combines opposite outcomes, but the established whole word also names a competitive encounter. Read ショウ + ブ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[試合](試合.md) (しあい) names a match or game. 勝負 can emphasize the confrontation or what is at stake rather than an event's formal organization. 勝敗 concentrates on victory or defeat; [結果](結果.md) (けっか) is a result more generally. [将棋](将棋.md) (しょうぎ) is one activity people can compete at.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every 勝負 as the event itself: 勝負がつく concerns its resolution. Conversely, 勝負する is a useful verb for competing, whereas 勝敗する is not the ordinary equivalent. Winning and losing are possible outcomes, not two unrelated meanings to choose arbitrarily in a sentence.
