# 出 (で) - outflow; origin; graduate

## Meaning

出 is a noun, also used after other words, meaning coming out or the way something flows out. It can also identify a person's origin or educational background.

- この蛇口は水の出が悪いです。 (このじゃぐちはみずのでがわるいです。) - The water flow from this tap is poor.
- 彼女はこの大学の出です。 (かのじょはこのだいがくのでです。) - She is a graduate of this university.

水の出 names the flow itself, allowing descriptions such as 出が良い and 出が悪い. In 大学の出, the institution identifies the person's educational origin.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) is read で here, related to the stem of [出る](出る.md) (でる), "come out; leave." The noun is not the case particle で, even though the pronunciation matches. Kanji and sentence structure help distinguish them.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[流れ](流れ.md) (ながれ), "flow," overlaps when describing water, but does not mean a university graduate. [出身](出身.md) (しゅっしん) overlaps with origin or background. [卒業](卒業.md) (そつぎょう) names graduation; 大学の出 identifies a person through that background.

## Distinctive meaning

The preceding noun is essential to interpretation. 水の出 concerns output, while 地方の出 (ちほうので) concerns coming from a region. Do not translate every 出 as a physical exit or every "graduate" as 出 without the supporting construction. This card teaches these noun uses, not all meanings of the character.
