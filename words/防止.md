# 防止 (ぼうし) - prevention

## Meaning

防止 is prevention: keeping an unwanted event or development from occurring.
防止する is transitive; what is prevented takes を.

- この設備は事故を防止するためのものです。 (このせつびはじこをぼうしするためのものです。) - This equipment is for preventing accidents.
- 再発防止の対策を考えています。 (さいはつぼうしのたいさくをかんがえています。) - We are considering measures to prevent a recurrence.

## How the kanji combine

[防](../kanji/防.md) contributes warding off;
[止](../kanji/止.md) stopping. ボウ + シ gives ぼうし.
The same reading in [帽子](帽子.md), "hat," is a different word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[防ぐ](防ぐ.md) (ふせぐ) is the related verb "prevent/protect against."
[予防](予防.md) (よぼう) also means prevention or precaution, as in preventing
illness. These words overlap; do not invent an absolute disease-versus-accident boundary.

## Distinctive meaning

事故防止 (じこぼうし), accident prevention, and 再発防止 (さいはつぼうし),
recurrence prevention, use 防止 after the noun naming the unwanted event.
事故を防止する means to prevent accidents, not merely inspect them.
[チェック](チェック.md) often means checking/inspection, but can also concern
screening to prevent unsuitable entry; the words are not categorically unrelated.
