# 言葉遣い (ことばづかい) - wording; manner of speaking

## Meaning

言葉遣い is how someone uses words: their wording, phrasing, or manner of speaking. It often concerns whether an expression sounds considerate, polite, rough, or suitable for the listener.

- 丁寧な言葉遣いを心がけています。 (ていねいなことばづかいをこころがけています。) - I try to use polite language.
- その言葉遣いは少し乱暴に聞こえます。 (そのことばづかいはすこしらんぼうにきこえます。) - That wording sounds a little rough.

This is a noun: 言葉遣いが丁寧だ, "the person's language is polite," and 言葉遣いに気をつける, "be careful how one speaks."

## How the kanji combine

[言葉](言葉.md) (ことば), "words; language," modifies 遣い (づかい), use or handling. The existing [言](../kanji/言.md) and [葉](../kanji/葉.md) cards explain the characters in 言葉; their individual glosses are not a literal definition of the whole expression.

言葉使い is another dictionary spelling, using [使](../kanji/使.md). Both are read ことばづかい. Keep づ in the second part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[表現](表現.md) (ひょうげん) can name an expression, including a particular wording, but also expression through art or movement. [言語](言語.md) (げんご) names language as a system; 言葉遣い concerns someone's use of words, not the identity of that system.

[敬語](敬語.md) (けいご) concerns honorific or polite language. It can be part of appropriate 言葉遣い, but rough or casual 言葉遣い is still 言葉遣い.

## Distinctive meaning

The word evaluates or describes the **way words are used**, not just how many words someone knows. Nor does 言葉遣い by itself mean "polite speech": 丁寧な, 悪い, or another description supplies that judgment.
