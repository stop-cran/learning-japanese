# 訓 (くん) - native Japanese kanji reading

## Meaning

訓 is a native Japanese reading assigned to a kanji, also called 訓読み (くんよみ). It is a noun used when discussing how written characters are read.

- 「山」の訓は「やま」です。 (「山」のくんは「やま」です。) - The kun reading of 山 is やま.
- この漢字を訓で読んでください。 (このかんじをくんでよんでください。) - Please read this kanji using its kun reading.

訓で marks the reading system being used. A character may have several readings; the noun itself does not say how many there are.

## How the kanji combine

The single 訓 is read with the on reading クン in this word. That is not a contradiction: a word naming kun readings can itself use an on reading. There is no two-character compound to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

訓読み names the same reading category explicitly. [音](音.md) (おん), in discussions of 音読み (おんよみ), refers to the Sino-Japanese reading category. These are contrasting reading types, not good and bad pronunciations.

## Distinctive meaning

For [山](../kanji/山.md), compare kun やま with on サン. A kun reading connects the character to a native Japanese word. This distinction is about the reading being used, not a rule that every kanji has both kinds or that every multi-kanji word must use on readings.
