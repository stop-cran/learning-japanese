# スピーチ (スピーチ) - speech; short address

## Meaning

スピーチ is a noun for a speech or address delivered to an audience. Use スピーチをする, "give a speech," and スピーチを頼む (スピーチをたのむ), "ask someone to give a speech."

- 結婚式で短いスピーチをしました。 (けっこんしきでみじかいスピーチをしました。) - I gave a short speech at the wedding.
- 来週、英語のスピーチコンテストに出ます。 (らいしゅう、えいごのスピーチコンテストにでます。) - Next week I will enter an English speech contest.

The language can precede it with の: 日本語のスピーチ (にほんごのスピーチ), "a speech in Japanese."

## How the kanji combine

スピーチ is a katakana loanword from English "speech." The long-vowel mark in ピー matters: hold the vowel rather than reading it as a short ピ. There are no kanji components to translate.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[演説](演説.md) (えんぜつ) also means a public speech and fits contexts such as a political address. The terms overlap; length alone does not create an absolute boundary.

[話](話.md) (はなし) can mean talk or a story more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

スピーチ normally presents an organized address, not every instance of spoken language or the physical ability to speak. A conversation between friends is not automatically a スピーチ. The title "short address" describes a useful setting, not a fixed maximum duration imposed by the word.
