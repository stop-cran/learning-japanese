# しまう (しまう) - put away; finish; end up doing

## Meaning

As a standalone transitive verb, しまう means to put something away, finish something, or close a business. The object takes を: 店をしまう (みせをしまう), "close up the shop."

- おもちゃを箱にしまいました。 (おもちゃをはこにしまいました。) - I put the toys away in the box.
- 宿題はもう全部やってしまいました。 (しゅくだいはもうぜんぶやってしまいました。) - I have already finished all my homework.

After a verb's て-form, しまう is an auxiliary expressing completion or something happening unintentionally. 財布を忘れてしまいました (さいふをわすれてしまいました), "I accidentally left my wallet behind," illustrates the latter.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is the usual spelling. 仕舞う is a recorded but rare kanji form; do not build the meaning by separately translating those characters. This is a godan verb: しまいます, しまって, しまわない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[終える](終える.md) (おえる) means to finish something, while [終わる](終わる.md) (おわる) describes something ending. Neither replaces the storage meaning of おもちゃをしまう.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep lexical and auxiliary uses apart: 本をしまう (ほんをしまう) puts a book away; 本を読んでしまう (ほんをよんでしまう) finishes reading it. The auxiliary is not invariably regretful. Casual contractions include てしまう → ちゃう and でしまう → じゃう, as in 読んじゃった (よんじゃった), "finished reading it."
