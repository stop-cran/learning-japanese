# 無 (ぶ; む) - non-; absence; nothingness

## Meaning

The reading **ぶ** is a bound prefix, not the standalone noun "nothing." It contributes absence or negation, and in some words a bad or poor quality. Learn it through established words:

- [無事](無事.md) (ぶじ), "safe; without incident": 無事に帰りました (ぶじにかえりました), "I returned safely."
- 無作法 (ぶさほう), "ill-mannered": 無作法な態度 (ぶさほうなたいど), "an ill-mannered attitude."

The separate reading **む** can be a noun meaning nothing or nothingness, and also a negative prefix.

- 努力が無になりました。 (どりょくがむになりました。) - The effort came to nothing.
- [無料](無料.md) (むりょう) means "free of charge," with む expressing the absence of a charge.

## How the kanji combine

無 is one kanji associated with absence or nonexistence. This card covers two on readings used in distinct lexical entries. The prefix ぶ is also written 不 in some established words, including 不作法 (ぶさほう).

## Synonyms and antonyms

ない expresses absence in ordinary predicates, as in お金がない (おかねがない), "there is no money." It is not interchangeable with a bound prefix.

有 (ゆう), "existence; having," provides a contrast in compounds and abstract descriptions.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not freely attach ぶ or む to any noun, or switch readings inside a known word. The community list pairs ぶ with a mismatched "nothing" gloss; its historical intention is unresolved. Both ぶ and む have independently assigned source rows here, so their supported meanings are taught separately rather than silently replacing one with the other.
