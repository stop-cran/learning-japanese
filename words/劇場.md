# 劇場 (げきじょう) - theater; playhouse

## Meaning

劇場 is a noun for a theater or playhouse: a venue for performances. It can describe the building, not just the stage inside it.

- 駅の近くに新しい劇場ができました。 (えきのちかくにあたらしいげきじょうができました。) - A new theater has opened near the station.
- 昨日、その劇場で芝居を見ました。 (きのう、そのげきじょうでしばいをみました。) - Yesterday I saw a play at that theater.

劇場に行く marks the destination with に. 劇場で見る marks where the viewing happens with で. 劇場の入口 (げきじょうのいりぐち) means "the theater entrance."

## How the kanji combine

劇 means drama or a play; [場](../kanji/場.md) means a place or setting. The first element specifies the activity associated with the place: a venue for drama or performances. The readings are ゲキ + ジョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[映画館](映画館.md) (えいがかん) explicitly identifies a cinema. [舞台](舞台.md) (ぶたい) can mean the stage on which performers act, not the whole theater. [会場](会場.md) (かいじょう) is a venue more generally. None is a simple antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "theater" can refer to live performance, a cinema, or the art itself. 劇場 chiefly identifies the venue. A particular venue's name or programming may include films, so the distinction from 映画館 is not an absolute naming rule. Use the event and building context to understand which kind of theater is intended.
