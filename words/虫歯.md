# 虫歯 (むしば) - tooth decay; cavity

## Meaning

虫歯 is tooth decay, a dental cavity, or a tooth affected by decay. It is a noun. 虫歯がある means having a cavity, while 虫歯になる describes developing one.

- 歯医者で虫歯が見つかりました。 (はいしゃでむしばがみつかりました。) - A cavity was found at the dentist's.
- 虫歯の治療を受けています。 (むしばのちりょうをうけています。) - I am receiving treatment for tooth decay.

The phrase 虫歯の治療 makes the condition the target of treatment. You can also count affected teeth, for example 虫歯が一本 (むしばがいっぽん), "one decayed tooth."

## How the kanji combine

虫 means insect or worm, and [歯](../kanji/歯.md) means tooth. The spelling associates an eaten-away tooth with 虫; it is not a modern medical explanation involving actual worms. The native readings combine as むし + は, with は voiced to ば.

## Synonyms and antonyms

歯 is the tooth itself, whether healthy or decayed. [歯医者](歯医者.md) (はいしゃ) is a dentist or dental practice. 歯が痛い (はがいたい), "my tooth hurts," describes a symptom rather than diagnosing 虫歯.

## Distinctive meaning

English may require "a cavity," "tooth decay," or "a decayed tooth" where Japanese uses 虫歯. Select the translation from the sentence, and do not treat every toothache as proof of this condition.
