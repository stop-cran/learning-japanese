# 抜ける (ぬける) - come out; be missing

## Meaning

抜ける is an ichidan verb: 抜けます, 抜けて, 抜けない. In its come-out and missing senses, it is intransitive: **something が抜ける**.

- 子供の歯が抜けました。 (こどものはがぬけました。) - The child's tooth fell out.
- 名簿から私の名前が抜けています。 (めいぼからわたしのなまえがぬけています。) - My name is missing from the list.

The first describes something coming loose or out; the second describes an omission. から marks the place or set from which something is absent.

## How the kanji combine

[抜](../kanji/抜.md) contributes removal or coming out, and ける completes the native verb. Compare its form with [抜く](抜く.md) (ぬく), the related verb for pulling out or removing something.

## Synonyms and antonyms

歯を抜く means to pull a tooth out; 歯が抜ける describes the tooth coming out without naming an agent. [取れる](取れる.md) (とれる) overlaps when something comes off, but does not replace every omission use of 抜ける.

## Distinctive meaning

In other constructions, 会議を抜ける (かいぎをぬける) is to leave a meeting, and トンネルを抜ける is to pass through a tunnel and emerge. The particle を belongs to those constructions; it does not make 名前が抜ける a transitive removal statement. Learn the relevant meaning together with its particles.
