# 傾向 (けいこう) - tendency; trend

## Meaning

傾向 is a noun for a tendency or an overall direction of change. A useful pattern is a noun-modifying expression + 傾向がある, "there is a tendency to..." Plain verbs and i-adjectives attach directly; affirmative nonpast na-adjectives take な. A noun used as a modifier takes の. Do not carry an affirmative nonpast predicate's final だ straight into 傾向. For a trend in progress, use 傾向にある.

- 最近、朝食を食べない人が増える傾向にあります。 (さいきん、ちょうしょくをたべないひとがふえるけいこうにあります。) - Recently, the number of people who skip breakfast has been tending to increase.
- 私は緊張すると早口になる傾向があります。 (わたしはきんちょうするとはやくちになるけいこうがあります。) - I tend to speak quickly when I get nervous.

同じ傾向 (おなじけいこう) means "the same tendency."

## How the kanji combine

傾 contributes leaning or inclining, and [向](../kanji/向.md) contributes direction or facing. Together they describe a direction toward which things tend, literally as a meaning image rather than a claim about the word's origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[習慣](習慣.md) (しゅうかん) is an established habit or practice. A 傾向 may be a statistical pattern without anyone deliberately following a habit. [流行](流行.md) (りゅうこう) concerns something spreading or being fashionable, not every observable trend.

## Distinctive meaning

傾向 does not assert that every individual case behaves the same way. It suits qualified observations about data, behavior, or change. "The opposite tendency," 逆の傾向 (ぎゃくのけいこう), reverses a particular pattern; it is not a separate antonym meaning "no tendency."
