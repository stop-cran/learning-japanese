# 悲しむ (かなしむ) - grieve; be saddened

## Meaning

悲しむ is a godan verb meaning to feel sorrow or grieve over something.
The event or loss can take を: 別れを悲しむ (わかれをかなしむ), "grieve
over a parting." The て-form is 悲しんで, and the past is 悲しんだ.

- 彼女は友達との別れを悲しんでいます。 (かのじょはともだちとのわかれをかなしんでいます。) - She is grieving over parting from her friend.
- そんなに悲しまないでください。 (そんなにかなしまないでください。) - Please don't be so sad.

In the first example, ている describes the ongoing emotional state; it does not
make the verb a different part of speech.

## How the kanji combine

[悲](../kanji/悲.md) conveys sadness. In this native verb it is read かな-,
with しむ written as okurigana. The related adjective is [悲しい](悲しい.md)
(かなしい), but its adjective ending and the verb's ending behave differently.

## Synonyms and antonyms

悲しい describes feeling sad or something being sad: 別れは悲しい, "Parting
is sad." 悲しむ describes experiencing sorrow over it. [喜ぶ](喜ぶ.md)
(よろこぶ), "be glad; rejoice," provides an emotional contrast.
Crying may accompany sorrow, but sadness does not require tears.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "regret" here concerns sorrow about something. It does not
automatically mean admitting a mistake or apologizing. Keep the object pattern
visible: 別れを悲しむ is grammatical even though English "be sad" normally
needs an added preposition such as "about."
