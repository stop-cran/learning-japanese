# あびる — to shower; to bathe in

## Meaning

あびる means receiving water, light, or something similar over your body. It is a transitive ichidan verb: あびます, あびない, あびて. The thing received takes を, even though English often uses "in."

- 朝、シャワーをあびます。 (あさ、シャワーをあびます。) — I take a shower in the morning.
- 日光をあびました。 (にっこうをあびました。) — I basked in the sunlight.

## How the kanji combine

The usual kanji spelling is 浴びる. This card retains the kana headword; 浴 expresses bathing, with びる as okurigana. It is one native verb, not a compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お風呂に入る (おふろにはいる) means taking a bath, typically by getting into the tub. 洗う (あらう) means washing something, emphasizing cleaning rather than exposure to water.

## Distinctive meaning

シャワーをあびる does not mean immersing yourself in a bathtub. The same image extends beyond bathing: 注目をあびる (ちゅうもくをあびる) means attracting attention, not washing.
