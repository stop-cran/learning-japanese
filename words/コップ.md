# コップ (コップ) - drinking glass; tumbler

## Meaning

コップ is an everyday drinking vessel, often a glass or tumbler used for water or juice. It does not have to be made of glass.

- コップに水を入れてください。 (コップにみずをいれてください。) - Please put some water in the glass.
- 子供はプラスチックのコップを使っています。 (こどもはプラスチックのコップをつかっています。) - The child is using a plastic cup.

For the contents, コップ一杯の水 (コップいっぱいのみず) means "a glass of water."

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is katakana. JMdict gives Portuguese *copo* and Dutch *kop* as source forms. Rare kanji spellings exist, but they are not needed for ordinary modern writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

グラス also names a drinking glass. [カップ](カップ.md) often suggests a coffee or tea cup, but the boundaries overlap; material and handles do not create a universal naming rule.

## Distinctive meaning

The English translation "glass" names the vessel here, not its material. ガラス is the Japanese word for glass as a substance. コップ refers to what one drinks from, so プラスチックのコップ is not a contradiction.
