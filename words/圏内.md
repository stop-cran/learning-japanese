# 圏内 (けんない) — within a defined range

## Meaning

圏内 means within a defined area or range, including a physical zone or a qualifying range.

- 暴風雨の圏内に入る。 (ぼうふううのけんないにはいる。) — to enter the storm zone
- 合格圏内にある。 (ごうかくけんないにある。) — to be within the passing range

## How the kanji combine

[圏](../kanji/圏.md) gives the idea of a sphere or range; [内](../kanji/内.md) means inside. Together, 圏内 identifies the inside of a specified range.

## Synonyms and antonyms

圏外 (けんがい) is the corresponding “outside the range” form. [以内](以内.md) (いない) can also mean “within,” but commonly expresses a limit or boundary; the two are excluded from each other's quiz choices.

## Distinctive meaning

圏内 takes its boundary from context: it can be a physical zone or a qualifying range, as in 合格圏内. It does not specify one fixed measurement.
