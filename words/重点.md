# 重点 (じゅうてん) - emphasis; important point

## Meaning

重点 is an important point, emphasis, or priority: the part receiving particular attention or effort. Learn **Nに重点を置く**, "put emphasis on N." 重点 is a noun, not an instruction to attach する automatically.

- この授業は会話に重点を置いています。
  (このじゅぎょうはかいわにじゅうてんをおいています。) - This class puts emphasis on conversation.
- 復習に重点を置いて勉強しました。
  (ふくしゅうにじゅうてんをおいてべんきょうしました。) - I studied with an emphasis on review.

に marks the focus of attention, while を marks 重点 in this construction.

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) contributes importance or weight, and [点](../kanji/点.md) a point. The combination identifies a point carrying particular weight. The readings are ジュウ + テン; compare the different initial sound in [終点](終点.md) (しゅうてん), "terminus."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[要点](要点.md) (ようてん) is a main or essential point. [重視](重視.md) (じゅうし) is attaching importance to something, while [強調](強調.md) (きょうちょう) is emphasizing it. Their meanings overlap, but their grammatical constructions differ.

## Distinctive meaning

The pinned JMdict also records 重点 for a colon (:), and for an iteration mark, a sign indicating repetition. These are recognition notes, not the meaning of 重点を置く in the examples. The inspected Digital Daijisen entry confirms the important-point and iteration-mark uses but does not list the colon sense; its omission alone does not invalidate JMdict's record.
