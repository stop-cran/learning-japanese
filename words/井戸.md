# 井戸 (いど) - water well

## Meaning

井戸 is a well from which underground water is obtained. It names the
well, not simply a container of water or a natural river.

- 庭に古い井戸があります。 (にわにふるいいどがあります。) - There is an old well in the garden.
- 井戸から水をくみました。 (いどからみずをくみました。) - I drew water from the well.

から identifies the source of the water. 井戸水 (いどみず) is well water.
The word alone does not say whether that water is safe to drink.

## How the kanji combine

井 has a well association and the native reading い;
[戸](../kanji/戸.md) has the native reading と, voiced here to ど.
The established compound names the well. Do not infer that it must have
a literal door from 戸's standalone meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[水道](水道.md) (すいどう) is a water-supply system, rather than a well.
[池](池.md) (いけ), "pond," names a different kind of water feature.
These are useful comparisons, not lexical opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

[緯度](緯度.md), "latitude," has the same reading いど but a different
spelling and meaning. Neither word is an alternate spelling of the other.
In an expression such as 井戸の水, "water from the well," の connects
the source with the water being discussed.
