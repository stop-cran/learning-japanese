# 以下 (いか) — or less; the following

## Meaning

After a number or quantity, 以下 means "that amount or less," including the stated limit.

- 五人以下のグループで来てください。 (ごにんいかのグループできてください。) — Please come in groups of five or fewer.

It also introduces material below or following the current passage.

- 以下の質問に答えてください。 (いかのしつもんにこたえてください。) — Please answer the following questions.

## How the kanji combine

[以](../kanji/以.md) marks a reference point here, and [下](../kanji/下.md) supplies the downward direction. The on readings combine as イ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

以上 (いじょう), "or more," also includes its boundary. 未満 (みまん), "less than," excludes it: 五人未満 allows at most four people.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate numerical 以下 as strictly "less than." Outside numerical limits, however, 平均以下 (へいきんいか), "below average," can express a judgment against a standard. The "following" sense uses 以下の + noun, not a numerical limit.
