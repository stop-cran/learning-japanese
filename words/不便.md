# 不便 (ふべん) — inconvenient

## Meaning

不便 describes inconvenience or something impractical to use. It is a noun and a な-adjective: 不便な場所 (ふべんなばしょ), "an inconvenient location."

ここはバスが少なくて不便です。 (ここはバスがすくなくてふべんです。) — This place is inconvenient because there are few buses.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) supplies a negative meaning, and [便](../kanji/便.md) contributes convenience. The on readings ふ + べん form the compound. Do not read 便 as びん here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

便利 (べんり), "convenient," is the direct contrast and also takes な. 面倒 (めんどう) emphasizes trouble or bother. 難しい (むずかしい), "difficult," is broader and not always about practical convenience.

## Distinctive meaning

不便 does not necessarily mean unusable, impossible, or dangerous. A town with infrequent buses can still be pleasant and accessible; the word evaluates a practical limitation. 不便を感じる (ふべんをかんじる) means experiencing inconvenience.
