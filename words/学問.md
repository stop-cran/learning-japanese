# 学問 (がくもん) - scholarship; academic learning

## Meaning

学問 is a noun for learning or scholarly study and can take する. It can also name an academic discipline: a field of organized knowledge and inquiry.

- 大学で学問に励みたいです。 (だいがくでがくもんにはげみたいです。) - I want to devote myself to academic study at university.
- 歴史学は人間の過去を研究する学問です。 (れきしがくはにんげんのかこをけんきゅうするがくもんです。) - History is a discipline that studies the human past.

学問に励む means to apply oneself to study; 学問をする means to pursue learning. 学問の自由 (がくもんのじゆう) is "academic freedom."

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) contributes learning, and [問](../kanji/問.md) contributes asking or inquiry. Their meanings reinforce an image of learning through inquiry. The readings combine as ガク + モン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) is useful for everyday studying, including homework and exam preparation. [研究](研究.md) (けんきゅう) highlights investigating a subject. 学問 can describe the wider scholarly pursuit or the discipline itself; these are overlapping perspectives, not rigid boundaries.

## Distinctive meaning

English "scholarship" here means learning or scholarly achievement, not financial aid. A scholarship providing money is [奨学金](奨学金.md) (しょうがくきん). Also, 学問 need not mean natural science: history and philosophy are fields of 学問 too. It has no simple antonym that covers both study and academic disciplines.
