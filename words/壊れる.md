# 壊れる (こわれる) — break; stop working

## Meaning

壊れる means to break, become damaged, or stop working. It is intransitive: the affected object takes が. 壊れた reports that it broke; 壊れている commonly describes its resulting broken state.

- 椅子が壊れました。  
  いすがこわれました。  
  "The chair broke."
- この時計は壊れています。  
  このとけいはこわれています。  
  "This clock is broken."

## How the kanji combine

壊 relates to breaking or destruction. The spelling divides こわ + れる, but the ichidan stem is こわれ: 壊れます (こわれます), 壊れて (こわれて), 壊れない (こわれない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

壊す (こわす), "break something", is transitive: 椅子を壊した (いすをこわした), "I broke the chair". 直る (なおる), "be fixed", describes recovery of function. 割れる (われる), "crack; shatter", is more specific to splitting or breaking into pieces.

## Distinctive meaning

壊れる does not require visible fragments. A clock that no longer works can be 壊れている even if its case is intact. The verb also does not itself say who caused the damage.
