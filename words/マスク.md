# マスク - face mask; facial features

## Meaning

マスク is a mask worn over all or part of the face, including a sanitary face mask, protective equipment, or a costume mask. The word also has a facial-features or looks sense.

- 電車ではマスクを着けています。 (でんしゃではマスクをつけています。) - I wear a face mask on the train.
- 作業の前に防じんマスクを着けた。 (さぎょうのまえにぼうじんマスクをつけた。) - I put on a dust mask before starting work.

甘いマスク (あまいマスク) describes attractive, gentle-looking facial features. It does not mean a sweet-tasting protective mask.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[面](面.md) (めん) includes a mask as well as a surface or aspect. The appropriate word depends on the kind of mask and the expression, not simply the English gloss.

[顔](顔.md) (かお), "face; facial features," overlaps the looks sense. [表情](表情.md) (ひょうじょう) is facial expression, not automatically someone's underlying features.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary additionally records a technical mask used in electrical work, such as circuit etching. A mask in that sense controls which area is exposed; it is not necessarily worn on a face.

The reading マスク also occurs in unrelated place-name or fragrance entries. Those homographs do not turn the face-mask word into a name for musk perfume.
