# 項目 (こうもく) - listed item; heading

## Meaning

項目 is an item, heading, category, or clause treated as one unit in
an organized list or classification. It is a noun.

- 申込書のすべての項目に記入してください。 (もうしこみしょのすべてのこうもくにきにゅうしてください。) - Please fill in every item on the application form.
- 確認する項目を三つに分けました。 (かくにんするこうもくをみっつにわけました。) - I divided the items to check into three groups.

An additional dictionary use is a headword or entry in a dictionary
or encyclopedia: 辞書の項目 (じしょのこうもく), "a dictionary entry."

## How the kanji combine

項 contributes an item or clause, and [目](../kanji/目.md) contributes
a class or item rather than a literal eye. Their related itemizing
associations reinforce the organized-unit meaning.
The on readings are コウ + モク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[科目](科目.md) (かもく) can name a curricular subject and also an
item or heading outside education. [種類](種類.md) (しゅるい)
concerns a kind or category.
[品物](品物.md) (しなもの) is an item in the goods sense;
the English word "item" alone hides this difference.

## Distinctive meaning

An 項目 may be a field on a form, a heading, or one classified matter,
not necessarily a physical object for sale. A clause can be presented
as an 項目, but the noun does not by itself specify a complete sentence
or a particular legal provision. The dictionary-entry sense is additional
teaching, not a separately established N2 assignment.
