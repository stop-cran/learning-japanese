# 生年月日 (せいねんがっぴ) - full date of birth

## Meaning

生年月日 is the year, month and day of someone's birth. It is useful on forms
and in biographical records. 生年月日を書く means write one's date of birth.

- ここに生年月日を書いてください。 (ここにせいねんがっぴをかいてください。) - Please write your date of birth here.
- 本人の生年月日を確認しました。 (ほんにんのせいねんがっぴをかくにんしました。) - We checked the person's date of birth.

Here 本人の identifies whose date is meant; を marks what is checked.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) contributes birth; [年](../kanji/年.md), [月](../kanji/月.md)
and [日](../kanji/日.md) supply year, month and day. Together they specify the
complete birth date. Learn 生 / せい plus 年月日 / ねんがっぴ as the established
pronunciation, with a small っ in がっぴ, not ねんげつにち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[誕生日](誕生日.md) (たんじょうび) is birthday, often the recurring month-and-day
occasion. It can also identify the original birth day, so the words overlap.
[日付](日付.md) (ひづけ) is a calendar date generally, not specifically a birth date.

## Distinctive meaning

生年月日 explicitly includes the year. A birth month alone is not a complete
生年月日. The word does not itself require Western year numbering rather than
Japanese era years: follow the particular form's instructions for notation.
