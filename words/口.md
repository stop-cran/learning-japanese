# 口 (くち) — mouth

## Meaning

口 means a mouth. It also names an opening, such as the mouth of a bottle or bag.

口を開けてください。 (くちをあけてください。) — Please open your mouth.

袋の口をしっかり閉じます。 (ふくろのくちをしっかりとじます。) — I close the opening of the bag securely.

## How the kanji combine

[口](../kanji/口.md) is one character with the native reading くち. In compounds it can become ぐち, as in 入口 (いりぐち), "entrance"; that is not the standalone pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

唇 (くちびる) means lips, not the whole mouth. 穴 (あな) means a hole, which need not function as an entrance or opening. There is no useful direct antonym of 口.

## Distinctive meaning

The opening sense connects the body word to containers and entrances. Context is essential: 瓶の口 (びんのくち) is a bottle's mouth, not a literal body part. Expressions involving speech and taste also build on the mouth, but should be learned as complete phrases.
