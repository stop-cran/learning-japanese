# 眠る (ねむる) — sleep

## Meaning

眠る means to sleep. It is intransitive and focuses on the sleeper rather than an object affected by an action.
It is a godan verb: the polite form is 眠ります (ねむります) and the past is 眠った (ねむった).

- 赤ちゃんが静かに眠っています。 (あかちゃんがしずかにねむっています。) — The baby is sleeping quietly.
- 昨夜はよく眠れました。 (さくやはよくねむれました。) — I was able to sleep well last night.

眠っています describes being asleep; 眠れました is a potential form, describing being able to sleep.

## How the kanji combine

[眠](../kanji/眠.md) is read ねむ in 眠る; る is written in kana.
眠 also reads ねむ in the adjective 眠い (ねむい), "sleepy"; the adjective describes sleepiness rather than the act of sleeping.
The on reading ミン appears in 睡眠 (すいみん), the noun "sleep."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[寝る](寝る.md) ねる can also mean sleep, but includes going to bed or lying down.
The contrast is not an absolute ban on overlap: both verbs can describe sleep.
[起きる](起きる.md) おきる provides the waking contrast, and can also mean to get up.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 眠る when actual sleep is the point.
Lying in bed without falling asleep can be described with 寝る in its lying-down sense, but does not establish that someone 眠っている.
Likewise, 眠い reports sleepiness rather than sleep itself.
Remember 眠ります beside 寝ます (ねます): the similar meanings do not give the verbs the same conjugation pattern.
