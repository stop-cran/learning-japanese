# 秤 (はかり) - weighing scales

## Meaning

秤 is a device for weighing things: scales or a weighing machine.
It is a noun; で marks the instrument in 秤で量る (はかりではかる), "weigh with scales."

- 秤で小麦粉を量りました。 (はかりでこむぎこをはかりました。) - I weighed the flour with scales.
- 荷物を秤に載せてください。 (にもつをはかりにのせてください。) - Please put the luggage on the scales.

に in the second sentence marks the surface on which the luggage is placed.
The word does not specify a particular size or weighing mechanism.

## How the kanji combine

秤 writes the whole native noun はかり. There is no multi-kanji compound to split.
The related measuring verb はかる is taught under [計る](計る.md),
including the spelling 量る used in the first example.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[重量](重量.md) (じゅうりょう) names weight, not the instrument.
体重計 (たいじゅうけい) specifically measures body weight.
A ruler and a thermometer also measure things, but are not therefore 秤.
There is no useful general antonym for this tool.

## Distinctive meaning

English "scales" can be plural while Japanese 秤 names a single device.
Do not confuse はかり with the particle ばかり, whose first consonant and grammatical role differ.
The kanji spelling and the surrounding construction identify the weighing tool.
