# 鯛 (たい) — sea bream

## Meaning

鯛 means **sea bream**, especially red sea bream, 真鯛 (まだい). It names a fish, not the general category of seafood.

- 海に鯛がいます。 (うみにたいがいます。) — There are sea bream in the sea.
- 鯛を焼いて食べました。 (たいをやいてたべました。) — I grilled and ate sea bream.

In the first sentence, に marks the location and が identifies what is there. In the second, を marks the food; [焼く](焼く.md) やく appears as 焼いて, connecting cooking to [食べる](食べる.md) たべる, eat.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [鯛](../kanji/鯛.md) writes the whole native noun たい, with no compound to split or kana ending to add. **たい** and **タイ** are also spellings; JMdict labels the animal name usually written in kana.

In 真鯛 (まだい), たい becomes **だい** after the first part. This compound means red sea bream; the article's standalone reading stays たい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[魚](魚.md) さかな, fish, is a broader category, not a precise replacement for the animal name. [鯉](鯉.md) こい, carp, and [鮎](鮎.md) あゆ, ayu, name different fish.

[海](海.md) うみ, sea, names a habitat rather than the fish. There is no useful direct antonym for sea bream.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn longer fish names as whole words. **金目鯛** (きんめだい), splendid alfonsino, is not simply a variety of sea bream despite the shared character.

**たい焼き・鯛焼き** (たいやき) is a fish-shaped pancake filled with bean paste. The fish shape supplies the name; do not confuse the sweet with the grilled fish in the example above.

The character dictionary also records チョウ. That is not another pronunciation to use for this animal noun.
