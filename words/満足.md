# 満足 (まんぞく) — satisfaction

## Meaning

満足 means satisfaction or contentment.
With する it describes being or becoming satisfied; with 満足している it describes the resulting state.
Use に to identify what someone is satisfied with.

- この結果に満足しています。 (このけっかにまんぞくしています。) — I am satisfied with this result.
- 今日の食事に満足しました。 (きょうのしょくじにまんぞくしました。) — I was satisfied with today's meal.

## How the kanji combine

[満](../kanji/満.md) contributes fullness or having enough, and [足](../kanji/足.md) contributes sufficiency,
not its "foot" meaning. The two ideas reinforce the sense of being satisfied.
The on readings マン + ソク become まんぞく, with そ voiced to ぞ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不満](不満.md) (ふまん) means dissatisfaction or discontent.
Compare 結果に満足している (けっかにまんぞくしている), "be satisfied with the result,"
with 結果に不満がある (けっかにふまんがある), "have dissatisfaction with the result."
Notice the different constructions rather than replacing one noun mechanically inside the same phrase.

## Distinctive meaning

Being satisfied is not the same as something being physically full.
満員 (まんいん) describes a place full of people; 満足 describes satisfaction.
A person can be satisfied with a meal because it was enjoyable, not just because their stomach is full.
