# 物騒 (ぶっそう) - dangerous; unsafe; unsettled

## Meaning

物騒 describes a dangerous or unsafe situation, or an unsettled atmosphere in which trouble seems possible. It can describe a neighborhood, an incident or threatening talk.

- この辺りは夜になると物騒です。 (このあたりはよるになるとぶっそうです。) - This area is unsafe at night.
- 物騒なことを言わないでください。 (ぶっそうなことをいわないでください。) - Please do not say such threatening things.

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md), things or affairs, and 騒, disturbance, provide a modern association with troubled circumstances. The established reading is ぶっそう, with a small っ. 騒 has no card yet. This association is not an asserted historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[危険](危険.md) (きけん) and [危ない](危ない.md) (あぶない) overlap in danger. [騒がしい](騒がしい.md) (さわがしい) can concern noisy or unsettled surroundings, but noise alone does not establish danger. [安全](安全.md) (あんぜん) is the useful safety contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 物騒な before a noun and 物騒だ or 物騒です as a predicate. Do not translate the kanji mechanically as "a noisy object." The danger or troubled atmosphere matters; a lively, harmless conversation need not be 物騒.
