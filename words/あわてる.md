# あわてる - become flustered; hurry

## Meaning

あわてる means becoming flustered, disconcerted or panicked. It also means hurrying,
especially in the form あわてて. It is an intransitive ichidan verb:
あわてます, あわてない, あわてた and あわてて.

- 財布が見つからなくて、あわてました。 (さいふがみつからなくて、あわてました。) - I became flustered because I could not find my wallet.
- 遅刻しそうだったので、あわてて家を出ました。 (ちこくしそうだったので、あわてていえをでました。) - I hurried out of the house because I was about to be late.

あわてないで means "do not panic" or "do not rush," according to context.

## How the kanji combine

The verb is also written 慌てる, read あわてる. 周章てる is a rare spelling,
not a different pronunciation to apply to the ordinary word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[急ぐ](急ぐ.md) (いそぐ) means hurrying without necessarily being flustered.
[混乱](混乱.md) (こんらん) concerns confusion or disorder.
落ち着く (おちつく), "calm down; settle," contrasts with becoming flustered.

## Distinctive meaning

English "become confused" here concerns losing composure, not simply failing to
understand a difficult idea. [あわただしい](あわただしい.md) describes hectic
circumstances or activity; あわてる presents someone becoming flustered or rushing.
