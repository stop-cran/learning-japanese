# サラリーマン (サラリーマン) — salaryman; office worker

## Meaning

サラリーマン is a salaried company employee, typically pictured as an office worker. It is a noun for a person, not the salary itself.

父はサラリーマンです。（ちちはサラリーマンです。） — My father is a salaried company worker.

サラリーマンとして会社で働いている。（サラリーマンとしてかいしゃではたらいている。） — I work at a company as a salaried employee.

## How the kanji combine

There are no kanji in this spelling. サラリーマン is wasei-eigo, an expression coined in Japanese from English material associated with salary and man. It is not simply the ordinary English phrase for every employee. Pronounce the long vowel in リー: サラリーマン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

会社員（かいしゃいん） and 社員（しゃいん） also describe company employees; [会社](会社.md) かいしゃ itself means company. [公務員](公務員.md) こうむいん is a public employee, not automatically the same occupation label. [事務](事務.md) じむ names office work, not the person doing it.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not expand the word into "anyone who receives pay": [店員](店員.md) てんいん, shop employee, and [工員](工員.md) こういん, factory worker, have their own occupational focus. The office-company setting is a useful guide, not a claim that the word describes one precise job title or rank.
