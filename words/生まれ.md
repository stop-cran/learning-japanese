# 生まれ (うまれ) - birth; birthplace

## Meaning

生まれ is a noun concerning birth or birthplace. After a place or time expression, it means
"born in": 東京生まれ (とうきょううまれ), "born in Tokyo," or 四月生まれ (しがつうまれ), "born in April."
Use の to attach such an expression to another noun.

- 私は大阪生まれです。 (わたしはおおさかうまれです。) - I was born in Osaka.
- 四月生まれの人はいますか。 (しがつうまれのひとはいますか。) - Is anyone here born in April?

生まれはどこですか (うまれはどこですか), "Where were you born?", asks specifically about birthplace.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) supplies the birth meaning, with the kana ending まれ.
The noun is related to the stem of [生まれる](生まれる.md) (うまれる), "be born."
It is not a past-tense verb: the full verbal statement is 大阪で生まれました.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出身](出身.md) (しゅっしん) concerns where someone comes from and can include a place of upbringing or an institution.
生まれ identifies birth rather than every kind of background.
[誕生](誕生.md) (たんじょう) names birth or coming into existence.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun construction can describe a past birth with present です because birthplace remains a biographical fact.
It does not mean the person currently lives there.
Similarly, a birth month is not a complete [誕生日](誕生日.md) (たんじょうび), "birthday"; the day may still be unknown.
