# 研究 (けんきゅう) — research

## Meaning

研究 is research or a focused investigation. It is a noun and also forms the verb 研究する (けんきゅうする).
The topic being researched can take を.

- 大学で日本語を研究しています。 (だいがくでにほんごをけんきゅうしています。) — I research the Japanese language at university.
- これは私の研究です。 (これはわたしのけんきゅうです。) — This is my research.

The first example describes investigating the language as a subject, not merely taking Japanese lessons.

## How the kanji combine

[研](../kanji/研.md) contributes close study or polishing, while [究](../kanji/究.md) contributes thorough investigation.
The two related ideas reinforce each other: study a subject closely. Both readings are on readings,
ケン + キュウ → けんきゅう. Keep the long う at the end.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) is study or learning, such as preparing for a test; it need not involve research.
- 調べる (しらべる) means checking, looking up, or investigating. A quick lookup can be 調べる without being a research project.
- There is no single useful direct antonym of 研究. Not researching something is not a separate vocabulary item that must be memorized as its opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

研究 suggests examining a topic or problem in some depth. It is useful in academic and professional settings
but is not restricted to laboratories. For ordinary study of beginner Japanese, 日本語を勉強する
(にほんごをべんきょうする) is usually the intended expression.

研究者 (けんきゅうしゃ) adds [者](../kanji/者.md), "person," to mean "researcher."
