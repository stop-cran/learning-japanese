# 金額 (きんがく) - amount of money

## Meaning

金額 is an amount or sum of money. It is a noun: use 金額を確認する
(きんがくをかくにんする), "check the amount," or 金額を書く, "write the amount."
The surrounding phrase identifies what the amount is for.

- 振り込む前に、金額を確認してください。 (ふりこむまえに、きんがくをかくにんしてください。) - Please check the amount before making the transfer.
- 予想より大きな金額でした。 (よそうよりおおきなきんがくでした。) - It was a larger sum than I expected.

支払う金額 (しはらうきんがく) means "the amount to pay"; 合計金額
(ごうけいきんがく) is the total amount.

## How the kanji combine

[金](../kanji/金.md) means money here, and 額 means an amount or sum. 金 specifies
what is being measured by 額: a monetary amount. The readings are キン + ガク,
not the native reading ひたい used for "forehead."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[値段](値段.md) (ねだん) is a price, and [料金](料金.md) (りょうきん) is a fee or
charge. Both can be expressed as a 金額, but a donation or account balance also
has an amount without being a price.

## Distinctive meaning

金額 names the quantity, not the physical banknotes or coins. It does not specify
the currency, whether the amount is large, or whether it has already been paid.
Add those details explicitly when completing forms or discussing a transaction.
