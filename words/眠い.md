# 眠い (ねむい) — sleepy

## Meaning

眠い means sleepy or drowsy: feeling a desire or need to sleep. It is an い-adjective, not a verb meaning "sleep".

- 昨日はあまり寝なかったので、眠いです。  
  きのうはあまりねなかったので、ねむいです。  
  "I'm sleepy because I didn't sleep much yesterday."
- まだ眠くありません。  
  まだねむくありません。  
  "I'm not sleepy yet."

## How the kanji combine

[眠](../kanji/眠.md) supplies ねむ, followed by the adjective ending い. Useful forms are 眠くない (ねむくない), "not sleepy", and 眠かった (ねむかった), "was sleepy".

## Synonyms and antonyms

眠る (ねむる) is the verb "sleep". 疲れた (つかれた), "tired", can explain why someone wants rest, but physical fatigue and sleepiness are not identical. 眠くない provides the straightforward negative contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

眠い describes a feeling, not being asleep. Someone saying 眠いです is still awake. For a person who is sleeping, use a verb expression such as 寝ています (ねています), "is asleep".
