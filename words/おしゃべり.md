# おしゃべり - chatting; talkative

## Meaning

おしゃべり names chatting or casual talk. With する, it describes the activity; the person you chat with takes と. It also describes a talkative person, using な before a noun: おしゃべりな人 (おしゃべりなひと).

- 友達とおしゃべりをしました。 (ともだちとおしゃべりをしました。) - I chatted with a friend.
- 妹はとてもおしゃべりです。 (いもうとはとてもおしゃべりです。) - My younger sister is very talkative.

As a label for a person, it can suggest a chatterbox or someone who says too much. Whether that sounds affectionate or critical depends on the context.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana; お喋り is also recorded. The form contains お and しゃべり, the noun-like stem associated with しゃべる, "talk." The お is part of this established expression, not a guarantee that calling someone おしゃべり is complimentary.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会話](会話.md) (かいわ) is conversation more broadly. [話す](話す.md) (はなす) is the general verb "speak; talk." [静か](静か.md) (しずか), "quiet," contrasts with talkativeness in a particular situation.

## Distinctive meaning

おしゃべり is a good fit for a relaxed chat, but does not by itself describe a prepared speech or formal explanation. Keep the activity and personality uses separate when translating it.
