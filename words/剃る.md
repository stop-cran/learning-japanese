# 剃る (そる) - shave hair

## Meaning

剃る means to shave hair with a razor or similar tool.
It is a transitive godan verb: **hair/body area を剃る**.
Useful forms are 剃ります (そります), 剃って (そって),
剃った (そった) and 剃らない (そらない).

- 毎朝、ひげを剃ります。 (まいあさ、ひげをそります。) - I shave my beard every morning.
- かみそりで足の毛を剃りました。 (かみそりであしのけをそりました。) - I shaved the hair on my legs with a razor.

The object identifies what or where is shaved; で can mark the tool.

## How the kanji combine

剃 supplies shaving, and る is the written verb ending.
This is a native verb rather than an on-reading compound.
**剃る (する)** is an alternative dictionary reading of the same shaving verb.
In that reading its godan forms include 剃ります (すります) and 剃って (すって).
It is not the irregular verb [する](する.md), "do."
Use the primary そる reading consistently in the examples above.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[削る](削る.md) (けずる) can mean shave or scrape material off something,
such as sharpening a pencil; its English "shave" is not limited to grooming.
[切る](切る.md) (きる) is cutting more generally, while
[刈る](刈る.md) (かる) includes clipping or cutting hair and vegetation.
The actions overlap in removing material, but the tool and manner matter.

## Distinctive meaning

剃る is not a general word for scraping wood or cutting a sheet of paper.
Nor does shaving imply removing hair by the roots.
The shared alternative reading する does not turn this godan verb into
しました, して or しない.
