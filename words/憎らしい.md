# 憎らしい (にくらしい) - hateful; ironically darling

## Meaning

憎らしい is an i-adjective meaning hateful, odious or detestable.
It can express annoyance or resentment toward a person or their behavior.

- そんなことを言う彼が憎らしい。 (そんなことをいうかれがにくらしい。) - I resent him for saying something like that.
- 憎らしいほど上手ですね。 (にくらしいほどじょうずですね。) - You are so good it is almost annoying!

The second sentence illustrates an admiring, ironic use rather than a literal
statement of hatred. Dictionaries also record an ironic "darling" sense.
Affection or admiration must come from the context, not from らしい alone.

## How the kanji combine

[憎](../kanji/憎.md) contributes hatred in the core meaning.
Learn the whole adjective にくらしい; do not mechanically translate it as
"apparently hateful." Its adverbial form is 憎らしく (にくらしく),
and its negative is 憎らしくない (にくらしくない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[憎い](憎い.md) (にくい) and [憎む](憎む.md) (にくむ) share the hatred
meaning with different wording or grammar. [嫌う](嫌う.md) (きらう) also
expresses dislike. Their possible intensities overlap; 憎らしい is not
automatically milder or stronger than every use of 憎い.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that every 憎らしい remark is affectionate teasing, or that
every occurrence expresses serious hatred. An expression such as
憎らしいほど上手 can acknowledge skill while conveying the speaker's
frustration. It is not the safest unqualified compliment to an unfamiliar person.
