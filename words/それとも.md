# それとも (それとも) - or; or alternatively

## Meaning

それとも is a conjunction introducing an alternative, especially in a question. It asks the listener to choose between possibilities: **Aですか。それともBですか。**

- お茶にしますか。それともコーヒーにしますか。 (おちゃにしますか。それともコーヒーにしますか。) - Would you like tea, or coffee?
- 歩いて行きますか、それともバスに乗りますか。 (あるいていきますか、それともバスにのりますか。) - Shall we walk, or take the bus?

The alternatives can be whole actions, not just individual nouns. A speaker can also use the pattern when wondering which explanation is correct.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 其れとも is a recorded but rare kanji spelling. Learn それとも as a linking expression; there is no useful kanji decomposition in the spelling used here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[または](または.md) also means "or" and can list alternatives in statements or instructions. それとも particularly suits setting one possibility against another in a question. [それで](それで.md), "so; and then," expresses a different connection.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every English "or else" as それとも. In the examples it offers alternatives; it is not a threat meaning "otherwise something bad will happen." In a choice question, preserve the question endings or context that make the alternatives clear.
