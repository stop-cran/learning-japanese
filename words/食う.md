# 食う (くう) - eat; consume

## Meaning

食う is a transitive godan verb meaning "eat." The food takes を. Forms include 食います (くいます), 食わない (くわない), 食って (くって), and 食った (くった).

- 昼にパンを食った。 (ひるにパンをくった。) - I ate bread at lunchtime.
- この作業は時間を食う。 (このさぎょうはじかんをくう。) - This work eats up time.

The second example uses a separate resource-consuming sense: no actual eating is involved.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) represents eating. 食う is a native reading of the single character with okurigana, not a compound. The same character also appears in [食べる](食べる.md) (たべる), but the two verbs have different endings and conjugations.

## Synonyms and antonyms

食べる is the neutral starting point for a learner talking about meals. For eating, 食う sounds blunter and more colloquial; adding ます does not erase every difference between the verbs. Drinking is a different action, not the opposite of eating.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary marks the **eating** sense as masculine language. That is a register label, not a rule forbidding other speakers from using it. Its **vulgar** label belongs to a separate sexual sense, not to the eating sense itself. Do not treat every instance of 食う as obscene, or assume its time-consuming use carries all the labels of unrelated senses.
