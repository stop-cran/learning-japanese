# 増やす (ふやす) - increase something; add more

## Meaning

増やす means to increase a number or amount, or add more.
It is transitive: the quantity being increased takes を.

- 練習の時間を増やしました。 (れんしゅうのじかんをふやしました。) - I increased the amount of practice time.
- 店は従業員を二人増やします。 (みせはじゅうぎょういんをふたりふやします。) - The shop will increase its staff by two people.
- 店は従業員を十人に増やします。 (みせはじゅうぎょういんをじゅうにんにふやします。) - The shop will increase its staff to ten people.

It is godan: 増やします (ふやします), 増やした (ふやした),
and 増やさない (ふやさない).
In the second example, 二人 is the increase, not the resulting total.
In the third, 十人に gives the resulting total of ten people.

## How the kanji combine

[増](../kanji/増.md) contributes increase. The native reading is ふ,
followed by やす; there is no compound of separate nouns.

**殖やす (ふやす)** is another spelling in the same dictionary family,
marked rare in the pinned JMdict. The Japanese dictionary notes a tendency
to use 殖やす for increasing assets or living things.
That is a spelling tendency, not two exceptionlessly separate verbs or a new reading.
Both source-list rows are taught on this page.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[増える](増える.md) (ふえる) is intransitive:
仕事が増える, "the amount of work increases," versus
仕事を増やす, "increase the amount of work."
[増す](増す.md) (ます) has both transitive and intransitive uses.
[減らす](減らす.md) (へらす), reduce something, gives the opposite direction.

## Distinctive meaning

[足す](足す.md) (たす) and [加える](加える.md) (くわえる) focus on adding;
増やす focuses on making an amount greater.
Adding a name to a list can increase the number of names, but the different
constructions are not interchangeable merely because both involve more.
The verb itself does not say whether an increase is desirable.
