# 音 (おと) — sound; noise

## Meaning

音（おと）means **sound** or **noise**, from rain and footsteps to an instrument or machine.
It does not automatically mean an unpleasant noise.

- 雨の音が聞こえます。（あめのおとがきこえます。）— I can hear the sound of rain.
- テレビの音が大きいです。（テレビのおとがおおきいです。）— The television is loud.

With 音, 大きい commonly describes volume: "loud," not a sound with a large physical size.

音 also means a musical **note**, understood as a sound rather than its written symbol:
ピアノでドの音を出します。（ピアノでドのおとをだします。）— I play a C note on the piano.
音符（おんぷ）is the written musical notation.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun. [音](../kanji/音.md) is read おと as a whole;
there are no component words or okurigana to combine.
The on readings used in compounds do not replace おと in the examples above.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 声（こえ）usually means a person's voice or an animal's vocal sound; 音 is broader.
- 音楽（おんがく）means "music," a particular kind of organized sound, not all sounds.
- 音色（ねいろ）means "tone quality" or "timbre": how an instrument or voice sounds, rather than whether there is a sound.

There is no direct noun antonym that works in every setting; an absence of sound is usually described in context.

## Distinctive meaning

This article uses the reading **おと**. 音 can also be ね, as in 音色, or オン／イン in other words.
Those readings are word-specific, not interchangeable pronunciations of the same everyday expression.
For ordinary "the sound of X," Xの音（Xのおと）is a useful starting pattern.
