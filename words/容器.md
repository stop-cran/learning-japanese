# 容器 (ようき) - container; receptacle

## Meaning

容器 is a container or receptacle for holding something.
It can also be translated as packaging when a container performs
that function. Its material and shape are not fixed by the word.

- 残った料理を容器に入れました。 (のこったりょうりをようきにいれました。) - I put the leftover food in a container.
- この容器はふたをしっかり閉められます。 (このようきはふたをしっかりしめられます。) - The lid of this container can be closed tightly.

## How the kanji combine

[容](../kanji/容.md) contributes containing or accommodating;
器 is a vessel or utensil.
Their on readings ヨウ + キ give ようき.
The first character qualifies the vessel's holding function.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入れ物](入れ物.md) (いれもの) is a close everyday equivalent.
[ケース](ケース.md) overlaps in its container/case sense, not its
separate "instance" sense.
[箱](箱.md) (はこ), [瓶](瓶.md) (びん) and [袋](袋.md) (ふくろ)
name more particular container forms.

## Distinctive meaning

容器 names the thing that holds contents, not the contents themselves.
[包装](包装.md) (ほうそう) concerns wrapping or packaging, including
the wrapping itself; that is related but not a universal synonym
for a container.
The homophone [陽気](陽気.md) (ようき), cheerful or seasonal weather,
has a different identity and meaning.
