# 飛行 (ひこう) - flight; flying

## Meaning

飛行 is a noun for flight or flying and an intransitive する verb for traveling through the air. It describes the activity rather than naming the vehicle.

- 飛行機は海の上を飛行しています。 (ひこうきはうみのうえをひこうしています。) - The airplane is flying over the sea.
- 強い風のため、飛行を中止しました。 (つよいかぜのため、ひこうをちゅうししました。) - We canceled the flight because of strong winds.

In the first example, を marks the area traversed. In the second, 飛行 is a noun serving as the object of 中止する. 飛行時間 (ひこうじかん) means flight time.

## How the kanji combine

[飛](../kanji/飛.md) contributes flying, and [行](../kanji/行.md) contributes going or movement. Together they describe going through the air. The readings are ヒ + コウ, with a long final vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飛ぶ](飛ぶ.md) (とぶ) is the native verb for flying and can also cover jumping in suitable contexts. [飛行機](飛行機.md) (ひこうき) is an airplane. 着陸 (ちゃくりく), "landing," names a phase that ends airborne travel, not a general antonym of flight.

## Distinctive meaning

飛行 suits descriptions of an aircraft's movement or an operation involving flight. It does not by itself identify a scheduled passenger service, ticket, or flight number. When English "flight" means a particular booked service, the surrounding travel expression may instead use 便 (びん).
