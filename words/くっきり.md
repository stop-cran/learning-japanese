# くっきり — clearly; in sharp relief

## Meaning

くっきり describes a shape or detail standing out with a clear, distinct outline.

- 雨上がりの空に、山の輪郭がくっきり見えた。
  — After the rain, the mountain's outline stood out clearly against the sky.
- 写真には、窓辺の花がくっきり写っている。
  — The flower by the window appears distinctly in the photograph.

## Synonyms and antonyms

くっきり emphasizes a clear visual outline or contrast. It does not simply mean that the whole
scene is bright.

## Distinctive meaning

The word describes how clearly a feature stands out. It does not guarantee that the viewer can
identify every detail in the scene.
