# 就く (つく) - take up a position; study under

## Meaning

就く is an intransitive godan verb for taking up a job, position, or role. Mark the position with に: 職に就く (しょくにつく), "take up employment." It also describes studying under a teacher, again with に.

- 卒業後、教師の職に就きました。 (そつぎょうご、きょうしのしょくにつきました。) - After graduation, I took up a teaching position.
- 有名な先生に就いて、書道を学びました。 (ゆうめいなせんせいについて、しょどうをまなびました。) - I studied calligraphy under a well-known teacher.

The second example concerns instruction under someone, not merely standing or sitting near them.

For physical seating, dictionaries record both **席に着く** and **席に就く**
(せきにつく), "take one's seat." The seat is marked by に.
This overlap does not make the two spellings freely interchangeable in every construction.

## How the kanji combine

就 writes the native stem つ, and く completes the verb. Forms include 就きます (つきます), 就いた (ついた), and 就かない (つかない). The character also appears with an on reading in [就職](就職.md) (しゅうしょく), "finding employment."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[着く](着く.md) includes arriving at a destination, while [付く](付く.md) includes attachment. The shared sound つく is not enough to choose a spelling; learn the full construction.

## Distinctive meaning

仕事に就く describes entering employment, not simply doing a task at a given moment. Likewise, 先生に就く presents an instructional relationship. These constructions do not make every English "take" or "settle" a use of 就く.
