# パート -- part-time work

## Meaning

In employment contexts, パート means part-time work or a part-time worker.

- スーパーでパートとして働いています。 (スーパーでパートとしてはたらいています。) -- I work part-time at a supermarket.
- この店はパートを募集しています。 (このみせはパートをぼしゅうしています。) -- This shop is recruiting part-time staff.

パートの仕事 (パートのしごと) means a part-time job. Use の before a following noun, not な.

## How the kanji combine

The employment word is a shortened loanword related to パートタイム, "part-time," and パートタイマー, a part-time worker. The ー lengthens パ. There are no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

アルバイト overlaps with part-time work, but employers may use these labels differently. 正社員 (せいしゃいん) names regular employment status; it is not simply a mathematical opposite defined only by hours worked.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer a worker's gender, age, or exact schedule from パート. Nor does the label alone establish every contractual condition. This card teaches the employment senses, rather than the separate meaning "part" in contexts such as music or a divided task.
