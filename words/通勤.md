# 通勤 (つうきん) - commuting to work

## Meaning

通勤 is commuting to one's workplace. 通勤する is intransitive.
The workplace can take に or へ; the means of transport takes で.

- 毎日、電車で会社に通勤しています。
  (まいにち、でんしゃでかいしゃにつうきんしています。) - I commute to the office by train every day.
- 通勤に四十分かかります。
  (つうきんによんじゅっぷんかかります。) - My commute to work takes forty minutes.

通勤時間 (つうきんじかん) means commuting time.
The first example's ～ています describes an ongoing routine.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes going back and forth, and
[勤](../kanji/勤.md) contributes employment or working.
Together they identify travel associated with attending work.
The on readings are ツウ + キン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通う](通う.md) (かよう) is broader regular attendance or travel to a place.
[出勤](出勤.md) (しゅっきん) focuses on going or reporting to work.
[通学](通学.md) (つうがく) is commuting to school.
Work and school are different purposes, not antonyms of travel.

## Distinctive meaning

通勤 concerns the journey associated with work, not the work itself.
[働く](働く.md) (はたらく) means work, and
[勤める](勤める.md) (つとめる) can mean being employed somewhere.
Neither alone specifies a commute. 通勤する also does not require a train:
walking, cycling, or driving can all be means of commuting.
