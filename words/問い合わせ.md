# 問い合わせ (といあわせ) - inquiry; query

## Meaning

問い合わせ is an inquiry or query made to obtain information.
It is a noun. Use 問い合わせをする, "make an inquiry,"
and 問い合わせに答える (といあわせにこたえる), "answer an inquiry."

- ホテルに空室についての問い合わせをしました。 (ホテルにくうしつについてのといあわせをしました。) - I made an inquiry to the hotel about room availability.
- お客様からの問い合わせに答えました。 (おきゃくさまからのといあわせにこたえました。) - I answered a customer's inquiry.

に marks the destination of an inquiry; から marks its source.
問い合わせ先 (といあわせさき) is a contact point for inquiries.

## How the kanji combine

[問](../kanji/問.md) contributes asking and [合](../kanji/合.md) combines with it in
問い合わせる (といあわせる), "inquire." 問い合わせ is the related noun.
Learn the native sequence といあわせ, not モン + ゴウ.
問合せ, 問い合せ and 問合わせ are also recorded spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[質問](質問.md) (しつもん) and [問い](問い.md) (とい) can mean a question.
問い合わせ highlights asking a person or organization for information;
it need not be a philosophical question or an examination exercise.
[返事](返事.md) (へんじ) is a reply, the response side of the exchange.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun is useful in customer support, booking, and information services, but is not restricted to a technical computer command.
An English source gloss "ENQ" does not make every ordinary 問い合わせ a particular control character.
The noun pattern above is a practical model, not a claim that every other する construction is impossible.
