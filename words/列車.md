# 列車 (れっしゃ) - railway train

## Meaning

列車 is a train traveling on a railway. It is a noun:
列車に乗る (れっしゃにのる), "board a train," and 列車を降りる
(れっしゃをおりる), "get off a train."
The word does not specify whether the service is local or express.

- 次の列車は三番線から出発します。 (つぎのれっしゃはさんばんせんからしゅっぱつします。) - The next train departs from platform three.
- 特急列車で京都へ行きました。 (とっきゅうれっしゃできょうとへいきました。) - I went to Kyoto by limited-express train.

The second example explicitly shows that an express service can be a 列車.

## How the kanji combine

[列](../kanji/列.md) contributes a row or line, and [車](../kanji/車.md)
contributes vehicles. The combination suggests railway vehicles arranged as
a train. レツ and シャ combine as れっしゃ, with a small っ.
The meaning should not be turned into an invented minimum-car requirement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[電車](電車.md) (でんしゃ) identifies an electrically powered train and is
also used in everyday talk about train travel.
[汽車](汽車.md) (きしゃ) has associations with steam trains and other
context-dependent train references. 列車 does not specify the power source.

## Distinctive meaning

The community source's "(ordinary)" parenthetical is not a restriction to
local stopping services. 列車 can be further qualified as a passenger,
freight, or express train. A timetable or announcement needs additional
words to tell you the service class, stops, and destination; 列車 alone
does not provide them.
