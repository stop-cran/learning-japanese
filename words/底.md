# 底 (そこ) - bottom; sole

## Meaning

底 is a noun meaning the bottom or lowest surface of something. It can also name a shoe's sole.

- 箱の底に写真がありました。 (はこのそこにしゃしんがありました。) - There was a photograph at the bottom of the box.
- 靴の底に穴が開きました。 (くつのそこにあながあきました。) - A hole appeared in the sole of my shoe.

Use Xの底 for "the bottom of X." A location can then take に: 海の底に (うみのそこに), "at the bottom of the sea." The object whose bottom you mean can be omitted when context makes it clear.

## How the kanji combine

底 is a single-kanji native word read そこ. Learn that reading together with the physical idea of a bottom or base. The same pronunciation そこ can also mean "there," but that demonstrative is a different word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下](下.md) (した) broadly means below or underneath. 箱の下 is underneath the box, whereas 箱の底 is the box's bottom. [表面](表面.md) (ひょうめん), "surface," can contrast with a depth or bottom, but the bottom is itself a surface in some contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

底 identifies a part or boundary, not just a direction. 心の底から (こころのそこから), "from the bottom of one's heart," extends the depth image to sincere feeling. There is no single physical opposite that fits a box, shoe, sea, and heart alike; choose the contrast for the actual object.
