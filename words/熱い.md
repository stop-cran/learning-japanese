# 熱い (あつい) — hot to the touch

## Meaning

熱い describes something hot to the touch, such as tea, water, or a heated object.
It is an い-adjective, so it can directly describe a noun or finish a sentence.

- このお茶は熱いです。 (このおちゃはあついです。) — This tea is hot.
- 熱いお湯に気をつけてください。 (あついおゆにきをつけてください。) — Please be careful with the hot water.

Use 熱いお茶 (あついおちゃ), "hot tea," without な.
The negative form is 熱くない (あつくない), "not hot."

## How the kanji combine

[熱](../kanji/熱.md) contributes heat, with the native reading あつ- and the adjective ending い.
The related noun 熱 is ねつ, "heat; fever." Removing い therefore does not leave a noun pronounced あつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冷たい](冷たい.md) (つめたい), "cold to the touch," is the useful temperature contrast:
熱いお茶 versus 冷たいお茶, hot tea versus cold tea.
暑い (あつい) has the same pronunciation but is used in a weather statement such as
今日は暑いです (きょうはあついです), "It is hot today."

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling helps separate hot objects from hot weather.
Learn 熱い with something such as お茶 so that the distinction remains visible even when both adjectives sound あつい.
For "I have a fever," the noun construction is 熱がある (ねつがある), not a simple replacement of that noun with 熱い.
