# 水泳 (すいえい) — swimming

## Meaning

水泳 means swimming as an activity or sport. It is a noun and can combine with する to mean "swim; do swimming".

- 私は水泳が好きです。  
  わたしはすいえいがすきです。  
  "I like swimming."
- 毎週、プールで水泳をしています。  
  まいしゅう、プールですいえいをしています。  
  "I go swimming at the pool every week."

The location of the activity takes で. Both 水泳する and 水泳をする are possible.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md), "water", and [泳](../kanji/泳.md), "swim", use their on readings: すい + えい. Keep the final い when writing the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

泳ぐ (およぐ) is the ordinary verb "swim". 水泳 is convenient when naming a sport, lesson, or hobby, as in 水泳の授業 (すいえいのじゅぎょう), "swimming class". There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the word that fits the sentence: 魚が泳いでいる (さかながおよいでいる), "A fish is swimming", describes movement. 水泳 more naturally names the human activity or discipline than a fish's motion.
