# 構造 (こうぞう) - structure; framework

## Meaning

構造 is the structure, construction, or makeup of something: how its parts
are arranged and related. It can concern a physical object or an abstract
system. Nの構造 means "the structure of N."

- この建物の構造を調べています。 (このたてもののこうぞうをしらべています。) - We are examining this building's structure.
- 社会の構造は時代とともに変わります。 (しゃかいのこうぞうはじだいとともにかわります。) - The structure of society changes with the times.

English "construction" here can describe how something is put together,
not only the activity of building it.

## How the kanji combine

[構](../kanji/構.md) contributes arranging or building, and
[造](../kanji/造.md) contributes making or construction.
The related meanings reinforce how something is put together.
The on readings コウ + ゾウ give こうぞう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[構成](構成.md) (こうせい) overlaps in composition or arrangement of parts.
[組織](組織.md) (そしき) can also concern organization or structure.
Do not impose an absolute physical-versus-abstract division between these words.
[工事](工事.md) (こうじ) instead directs attention to construction work.

## Distinctive meaning

構造 need not be visible from the outside or made of building materials.
The word itself does not tell you that the arrangement is strong, efficient,
or desirable. Its "pattern" gloss can concern an arrangement of parts;
it is not an instruction to call every decorative design a 構造.
