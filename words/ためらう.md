# ためらう - hesitate; waver

## Meaning

ためらう means to hesitate, waver, or be reluctant to decide or
act. It is a godan verb: ためらいます, ためらった,
ためらって, ためらわない.

- 彼に本当のことを話すのをためらいました。
  (かれにほんとうのことをはなすのをためらいました。) - I hesitated to tell him the truth.
- 困ったときは、ためらわずに相談してください。
  (こまったときは、ためらわずにそうだんしてください。) - When you are in difficulty, please ask for advice without hesitation.

**Verb in plain form + のをためらう** identifies the contemplated
action. ためらわずに means "without hesitating."
It can also be used without an expressed object.

## How the kanji combine

The usual kana spelling is used here. 躊躇う is a dictionary
spelling with a special whole-word reading, not a sequence of
ordinary on readings. No kanji occur in this article's headword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[迷う](迷う.md) (まよう) overlaps when someone is undecided.
躊躇する (ちゅうちょする) is another expression for hesitating.
[決める](決める.md) (きめる), "decide," describes reaching
a choice rather than wavering.

## Distinctive meaning

Hesitation does not necessarily mean refusing: someone may hesitate
and then act. The verb is not limited to an objectless pattern;
the dictionary records both transitive and intransitive uses.
The first example's の turns the action into what is hesitated over.
