# センチ - centimetre; centi-; sentimental

## Meaning

センチ commonly abbreviates センチメートル, a centimetre:
one hundredth of a [メートル](メートル.md), metre.
It follows a number without の.

- このひもは三十センチです。 (このひもはさんじゅっセンチです。) - This cord is thirty centimetres long.
- 身長が二センチ伸びました。 (しんちょうがにセンチのびました。) - I grew two centimetres taller.

As a measurement prefix, センチ- means one hundredth, or 10^-2.
In センチメートル, it specifies one hundredth of a metre.
It is not an abbreviation for every unit of length.

There is also a separate colloquial abbreviation of センチメンタル,
"sentimental." センチな気分 (センチなきぶん) is a sentimental mood.
This additional use is not a measurement, nor a claim that every sense
has an independently established N2 level.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in katakana and has no written kanji components.
Context separates the shortened unit name from the shortened adjective.
The emotional use takes な before a noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

センチメートル is the full unit name.
[しんみり](しんみり.md) can describe a subdued, sentimental mood,
but also quiet, heartfelt interaction; it is not a unit of length.

## Distinctive meaning

A number followed by センチ normally concerns measurement.
センチになる, in a conversation about feelings, can instead mean
becoming sentimental. Do not infer emotional meaning from a ruler's label
or add an English plural ending to the unit.
