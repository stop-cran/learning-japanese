# こんばんは - good evening

## Meaning

こんばんは is a greeting used in the evening. As an interjection, it can stand alone.

- こんばんは、田中さん。 (こんばんは、たなかさん。) - Good evening, Tanaka.
- こんばんは。お元気ですか。 (こんばんは。おげんきですか。) - Good evening. How are you?

Pronounce the final は as **わ**. The standard spelling remains こんばんは, not こんばんわ.

## How the kanji combine

The dictionary spelling 今晩は contains [今](../kanji/今.md), now, and [晩](../kanji/晩.md), evening/night, followed by the particle は. The Sino-Japanese base is こん + ばん; the greeting as a whole is a fixed Japanese expression.

It is usually written in kana. Do not read the final は as a third on reading, or treat the greeting as an unfinished statement meaning simply "as for tonight."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こんにちは](こんにちは.md) is a daytime greeting; [おはよう](おはよう.md) is used in the morning. These are situational alternatives, not antonyms. [おやすみ](おやすみ.md) can be a good-night farewell, rather than a greeting on meeting someone.

## Distinctive meaning

[今晩](今晩.md) (こんばん) means tonight. Adding は in the fixed greeting changes its conversational function: こんばんは greets someone; 今晩の予定 (こんばんのよてい) concerns tonight's plans. The word's definition does not establish an exact clock boundary for every situation.
