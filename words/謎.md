# 謎 (なぞ) - mystery; riddle

## Meaning

謎 is a noun for a mystery, riddle, or puzzling matter whose explanation is not known. With の before a noun, it can mean "mysterious": 謎の人物 (なぞのじんぶつ), "a mysterious person."

- この事件にはまだ謎が残っています。 (このじけんにはまだなぞがのこっています。) - Mysteries still remain in this case.
- 最後に、謎の人物の正体が分かりました。 (さいごに、なぞのじんぶつのしょうたいがわかりました。) - At the end, we learned the identity of the mysterious person.

謎を解く (なぞをとく) means "solve a mystery/riddle"; 謎が解ける (なぞがとける) means "the mystery is solved."

## How the kanji combine

謎 is a single character read なぞ. It represents the whole native word rather than a combination of independently pronounced kanji. The kana spelling なぞ is also useful when the character is unfamiliar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[問題](問題.md) (もんだい) can be a problem, question, or exercise without any mysterious element. [不思議](不思議.md) (ふしぎ) concerns something strange or puzzling and overlaps, but uses different attachment: 不思議な versus 謎の.

## Distinctive meaning

Something can remain 謎 without being supernatural or deliberately concealed. The key point is the missing explanation. 謎の in a story creates an information gap; it does not itself establish that the person is dangerous, dishonest, or imaginary.
