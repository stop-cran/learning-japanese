# 預ける (あずける) - entrust; leave in someone's care

## Meaning

預ける is a transitive ichidan verb: leave something or someone in another's care. Use **person or place に + entrusted item を預ける**. It also covers depositing money.

- 受付に荷物を預けました。 (うけつけににもつをあずけました。) - I left my luggage at reception.
- 午後は母に子供を預けます。 (ごごはははにこどもをあずけます。) - I will leave my child in my mother's care this afternoon.

The recipient takes responsibility for keeping or looking after what is entrusted. Useful forms are 預けます, 預けて, and 預けない.

## How the kanji combine

預 carries the idea of entrusting or keeping something for someone. In the spelling 預ける, 預 is read あず and ける is written in kana. There is no two-kanji compound to unpack.

## Synonyms and antonyms

預かる (あずかる) describes the receiving side: 荷物を預かる means "look after someone's luggage." It is a change of viewpoint, not simply an opposite action. [任せる](任せる.md) (まかせる) emphasizes leaving a task or decision to someone.

## Distinctive meaning

預ける does not mean giving away ownership. Compare a bank deposit, 銀行にお金を預ける (ぎんこうにおかねをあずける), with a gift: the money is entrusted to the bank, not presented as a gift. In the luggage example, the expected next step is to collect it again.
