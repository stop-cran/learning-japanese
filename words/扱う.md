# 扱う (あつかう) - handle; deal with; treat

## Meaning

扱う is a transitive godan verb for handling something, dealing with a matter, or treating a person. The person or thing takes を. Its forms include 扱います, 扱って, and 扱わない.

- この機械は丁寧に扱ってください。 (このきかいはていねいにあつかってください。) - Please handle this machine carefully.
- 私を子供として扱わないでください。 (わたしをこどもとしてあつかわないでください。) - Please do not treat me as a child.

In the second example, AをBとして扱う means "treat A as B." Problems and topics can also be objects: この問題を扱う (このもんだいをあつかう), "deal with this problem."

## How the kanji combine

扱 carries the handling meaning and is read あつか before the ending う. This is one native verb, not a compound of several kanji. Learn its changing endings with the whole word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[使う](使う.md) (つかう) means "use": using a machine and handling it carefully emphasize different things. [処理](処理.md) (しょり) concerns processing or disposing of a matter; 扱う need not imply that the matter has been resolved.

## Distinctive meaning

The object and surrounding phrase determine the English translation. A shop that 商品を扱う (しょうひんをあつかう) deals in those goods, while an article that 問題を扱う covers a problem. The core idea is how someone engages with the person or thing, not simply physical contact.
