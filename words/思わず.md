# 思わず (おもわず) - involuntarily; in spite of oneself

## Meaning

思わず is an adverb for a spontaneous or involuntary reaction. It modifies an action without requiring a particle: 思わず笑う (おもわずわらう), "laugh in spite of oneself."

- その話を聞いて、思わず笑ってしまいました。 (そのはなしをきいて、おもわずわらってしまいました。) - Hearing that story, I could not help laughing.
- 大きな音に驚いて、思わず声を上げました。 (おおきなおとにおどろいて、おもわずこえをあげました。) - Startled by the loud noise, I cried out involuntarily.

The speaker reports that the reaction occurred before deliberate control or planning.

## How the kanji combine

[思](../kanji/思.md) belongs to [思う](思う.md) (おもう), "think." 思わず has the form 思わ + ず, with the negative ending ず. As this established adverb, it means acting without intending to, not simply the complete statement "I do not think."

## Synonyms and antonyms

つい can also introduce an unintended action, including giving in to a habit or temptation. [わざと](わざと.md), "deliberately; on purpose," contrasts with the lack of intention expressed here.

## Distinctive meaning

思わず does not mean that a person was unconscious. Someone can be fully awake but laugh, cry, or speak before considering it. Nor is the action necessarily regrettable: involuntary laughter can be pleasant. In the first example, てしまう adds the sense of an action happening beyond deliberate restraint.
