# 意識 (いしき) - consciousness; awareness

## Meaning

意識 is a noun meaning consciousness or awareness. 意識がある means "be conscious";
意識を失う (いしきをうしなう) means "lose consciousness."
With する, Nを意識する means to be aware of N or consciously pay attention to it.

- 倒れた人には意識がありました。
  (たおれたひとにはいしきがありました。) - The person who collapsed was conscious.
- 話すときは、発音を意識しています。
  (はなすときは、はつおんをいしきしています。) - When speaking, I pay conscious attention to my pronunciation.

The first concerns a person's state of consciousness; the second concerns what they
keep in mind. Do not use 意識する to mean simply "wake up."

## How the kanji combine

[意](../kanji/意.md) contributes mind or intention, and [識](../kanji/識.md) contributes
recognition or awareness. Together they concern mental awareness. The on readings are イ + シキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[気付く](気付く.md) (きづく) focuses on noticing or realizing something; 意識する can
describe continuing awareness after that realization.
無意識 (むいしき) contrasts with conscious awareness, as in 無意識に, "unconsciously."

## Distinctive meaning

意識 does not name the five sensory faculties collectively in every context where English
uses "the senses." Learn the constructions separately: 意識がある describes consciousness,
while Nを意識する identifies something a person is mentally attending to.
