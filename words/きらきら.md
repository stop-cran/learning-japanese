# きらきら — twinkling and glittering

## Meaning

Sparkling, twinkling, or glittering light, often with a bright, attractive impression. Eyes can figuratively sparkle with interest or excitement.

- 川の水面（みなも）がきらきらと輝いている。 — The surface of the river is sparkling.
- 子どもたちは目をきらきらさせて、話を聞いていた。 — The children listened with sparkling eyes.

## How the kanji combine

A native mimetic repeating きら, usually written in kana. The repetition depicts a visual impression, not a grammatical plural or a literal sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぴかぴか](ぴかぴか.md) also describes shine but readily suggests polishing or newness. [ぎらぎら](ぎらぎら.md) tends toward stronger, harsher glare. [つやつや](つやつや.md) focuses on a glossy surface.

## Distinctive meaning

きらきら光る, きらきらと輝く（かがやく）, and きらきらした目 are useful. 目をきらきらさせる describes someone's excited appearance. The favorable impression is a tendency, not a guarantee: context can make even glittering decoration seem excessive. Do not use the word for every kind of bright, uniformly lit room.
