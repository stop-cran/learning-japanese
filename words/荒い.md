# 荒い (あらい) - rough; wild; violent

## Meaning

荒い describes forceful, wild or rough movement and behavior. It also describes
heavy breathing and reckless or extravagant use of money. It is an i-adjective:
荒くなる (あらくなる), "become rough"; 荒かった, "was rough."

- 今日は波が荒いです。 (きょうはなみがあらいです。) - The waves are rough today.
- 階段を上った後、息が荒くなりました。 (かいだんをのぼったあと、いきがあらくなりました。) - My breathing became heavy after I climbed the stairs.
- 彼は金遣いが荒い。 (かれはかねづかいがあらい。) - He is reckless with money.

## How the kanji combine

[荒](../kanji/荒.md) contributes roughness or wildness. The native adjective is
あらい; final い changes with inflection. This is not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[激しい](激しい.md) (はげしい), "intense; violent," overlaps in force or intensity.
[穏やか](穏やか.md) (おだやか), "calm; mild," supplies a contextual contrast for the sea or temperament.
[荒れる](荒れる.md) (あれる) describes becoming rough or disordered rather than simply having the quality 荒い.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish [粗い](粗い.md), also あらい. Official spelling guidance associates 荒い
with strong force or rough behavior, and 粗い with lack of fineness or care.
Thus 波が荒い concerns rough waves, while 網の目が粗い (あみのめがあらい)
describes a net with a coarse, open mesh. English "rough" alone does not choose the spelling.
