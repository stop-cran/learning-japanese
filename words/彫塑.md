# 彫塑 (ちょうそ) — sculpture; carving and modeling

## Meaning

彫塑 names sculpture made by carving or by shaping a material such as clay. It can name the work as well as the practice.

- 美術学校で彫塑を学んだ。 (びじゅつがっこうでちょうそをまなんだ。) — I studied sculpture at art school.
- 展覧会で彫塑の作品を見た。 (てんらんかいでちょうそのさくひんをみた。) — I saw works of sculpture at the exhibition.

## How the kanji combine

[彫](../kanji/彫.md) points to carving material away. [塑](../kanji/塑.md) points to modeling a form, for example in clay. The characters put the two approaches side by side.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[彫刻](彫刻.md) (ちょうこく) also means sculpture. These are overlapping words, not two mutually exclusive classes of finished objects.

## Distinctive meaning

When 彫塑 names a clay model, picture the figure shaped in clay. When it names sculpture as a field of study, as in the art-school example, the word does not specify what every piece is made of.
