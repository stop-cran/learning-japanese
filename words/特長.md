# 特長 (とくちょう) - strong point; merit

## Meaning

特長 is a strong point, merit, or favorable feature.
It highlights what is good about someone or something, not an achievement alone.
Nの特長 identifies whose or what strong point is being discussed.

- この製品の特長は、軽くて丈夫なことです。 (このせいひんのとくちょうは、かるくてじょうぶなことです。) - This product's strong point is that it is light and durable.
- それぞれの方法には特長があります。 (それぞれのほうほうにはとくちょうがあります。) - Each method has its strengths.

## How the kanji combine

[特](../kanji/特.md) contributes distinctive or special, while
[長](../kanji/長.md) contributes a strength or superior point here.
The on readings are トク + チョウ; retain the long vowel in ちょう.
This is not a statement about physical length.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[長所](長所.md) (ちょうしょ) also means a strong point or merit.
The homophone [特徴](特徴.md) is broader: a characteristic need not be favorable.
[特色](特色.md) (とくしょく) can mean a distinctive feature or an outstanding point,
so the three words do not occupy completely separate compartments.
[短所](短所.md) (たんしょ), a weak point, provides an evaluative contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose the spelling from the intended meaning, not the sound とくちょう alone.
特長 emphasizes merit; 特徴 also works for neutral or unfavorable characteristics.
Several alternatives can each have a 特長: praise does not require that only one
person or product possess the quality.
