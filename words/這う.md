# 這う (はう) - crawl; creep

## Meaning

這う is an intransitive godan verb: crawl or creep along close to a surface. A person or animal can move on all fours, and a snake can slither without having limbs.

- 赤ちゃんが床を這っています。 (あかちゃんがゆかをはっています。) - The baby is crawling across the floor.
- 蛇が地面を這っていました。 (へびがじめんをはっていました。) - A snake was slithering along the ground.

The mover takes が. 床を and 地面を mark the surface traversed, not an object being changed. Forms include 這います, 這った, 這って, and 這わない.

An additional use concerns trailing or spreading plants: つたが壁を這っている (つたがかべをはっている), "ivy is creeping along the wall." The plant need not move around like an animal.

## How the kanji combine

這 represents は in this native verb, with う as okurigana. Kana はう is also useful to recognize. Do not read the past 這った as はうた; the godan form changes to はった.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[歩く](歩く.md) (あるく) ordinarily describes walking, rather than crawling close to the surface. For plants, [広がる](広がる.md) (ひろがる), "spread," and [伸びる](伸びる.md) (のびる), "grow longer; extend," can describe related aspects of growth without being replacements in every animal-motion sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

English "creep" can mean moving stealthily while upright, but 這う does not simply mean "sneak." Conversely, the plant use does not imply stealth. Keep the physical manner and the subject with the translation.
