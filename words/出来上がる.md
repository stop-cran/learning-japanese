# 出来上がる (できあがる) - be finished; be ready

## Meaning

出来上がる means that something has reached its finished, ready state. It is an intransitive godan verb: the finished thing takes が.

- 注文した服が出来上がりました。 (ちゅうもんしたふくができあがりました。) - The clothes I ordered are ready.
- 夕食が出来上がるまで、ここで待っていてください。 (ゆうしょくができあがるまで、ここでまっていてください。) - Please wait here until dinner is ready.

There is also a colloquial drinking sense: もうすっかり出来上がっている can mean someone is already thoroughly drunk. Context separates it from a finished product.

## How the kanji combine

This native compound combines [できる](できる.md), written [出](../kanji/出.md)[来](../kanji/来.md)る, with [上がる](上がる.md), whose [上](../kanji/上.md) contributes a completion/result idea here. Learn できあがる as a whole; it does not mean physically coming outside and going upstairs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕上がる](仕上がる.md) also describes being finished. [完成](完成.md) emphasizes completion, while [終わる](終わる.md) can simply mean ending without producing a finished object. [出来上がり](出来上がり.md) is the related noun for completion or the finished result. The drinking use overlaps [酔う](酔う.md), not every sense of that verb.

## Distinctive meaning

Forms include 出来上がります, 出来上がって, and 出来上がらない. To describe making something reach completion, use a suitable transitive construction rather than putting を before this intransitive verb. The source list's English "by definition" is not a general adverbial translation: dictionaries separately record inherent constitution or being made a certain way.
