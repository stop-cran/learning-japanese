# ぎらぎら — glaring and dazzling

## Meaning

An intense, insistent glare or shine, such as strong sun, reflective metal, or fiercely shining eyes.

- 真夏（まなつ）の太陽がぎらぎらと照りつける（てりつける）。 — The midsummer sun beats down with a fierce glare.
- 彼は賞金（しょうきん）の話を聞いて、目をぎらぎらさせた。 — His eyes gleamed intensely when he heard about the prize money.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[きらきら](きらきら.md) often has a lighter, more attractive sparkle. [ぴかぴか](ぴかぴか.md) can emphasize polished shine. [てかてか](てかてか.md) focuses on a conspicuously shiny surface, often oily.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ぎらぎら光る, ぎらぎらした光, or 目がぎらぎらしている. With eyes it can imply eagerness, aggression, or greed, depending on context; it does not itself name one specific emotion. It is a visual mimetic, not the sound the sun or metal makes.
