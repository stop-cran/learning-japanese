# ぜひ (ぜひ) - by all means; definitely

## Meaning

ぜひ strengthens a wish, invitation, or request. It expresses that someone really wants an action to happen, often translated "by all means," "definitely," or "without fail."

- ぜひ遊びに来てください。 (ぜひあそびにきてください。) - Please do come and visit.
- ぜひ京都へ行ってみたいです。 (ぜひきょうとへいってみたいです。) - I would really like to visit Kyoto.

It modifies the request or desire; it does not conjugate.

## How the kanji combine

ぜひ can also be written 是非, using the on readings ゼ + ヒ. The compound has a separate noun meaning concerning right and wrong or pros and cons. That is not the meaning of the invitations here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

必ず (かならず) emphasizes certainty or an action occurring without exception. ぜひ more naturally expresses eagerness or encouragement in the patterns above.

## Distinctive meaning

ぜひ is not a general prediction word: use it to invite, request, or express a strong wish, not simply to state that tomorrow's weather is certain.
