# 承知 (しょうち)

## Meaning

承知 means knowing or being aware of something, and also accepting, consenting, or acknowledging a request. Use 承知する as the verb.

- 父はその計画を承知しています。 (ちちは そのけいかくを しょうちしています。) — My father is aware of the plan.
- 日程の変更を承知しました。 (にっていの へんこうを しょうちしました。) — I agreed to the schedule change.

The object commonly takes を. Context distinguishes awareness from agreement.

## How the kanji combine

承 contributes receiving or accepting; [知](../kanji/知.md) contributes knowing. Their on readings are しょう + ち. Together they connect understanding a matter with accepting it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

了解 (りょうかい) overlaps with understanding or acknowledgment. 同意 (どうい) more specifically means agreement. 拒否 (きょひ), "refusal," contrasts with the acceptance sense.

## Distinctive meaning

承知しました is a polite acknowledgment of instructions or requests. 承知いたしました adds humility and is useful in formal service or business exchanges. Neither proves enthusiastic approval: the speaker may simply be confirming that the request has been understood and accepted.
