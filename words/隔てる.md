# 隔てる (へだてる) - separate; have between

## Meaning

隔てる is a transitive ichidan verb: separate or partition things, put or have
something between them, or estrange people. It can concern space, time or relationships.
The separating barrier can be the subject; an intervening distance can be the を phrase.

- 川が二つの町を隔てています。 (かわがふたつのまちをへだてています。) - A river separates the two towns.
- 二人は机を隔てて座りました。 (ふたりはつくえをへだててすわりました。) - The two people sat with a desk between them.

## How the kanji combine

隔 contributes separation or an interval; てる completes this native verb's
written form. Learn へだてる as a whole ichidan verb, with へだてない and
へだてた. The dictionary also records rare-kanji 距てる for the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[離す](離す.md) (はなす) separates or keeps things apart.
[分ける](分ける.md) (わける) is a broader divide/separate verb.
隔たる (へだたる) is the separate intransitive godan verb for being apart or
separated. Do not treat it as another conjugation of 隔てる.
[間](間.md) (あいだ) names an interval or the space between things.

## Distinctive meaning

Ten years can be described as 十年を隔てて (じゅうねんをへだてて), "with a
ten-year interval." The を phrase need not be a person physically pushed away.
The estrangement sense concerns creating distance between people.
An English gloss such as "be shut out" must not make you conjugate this as an
intransitive verb: へだてられる is its passive form.
