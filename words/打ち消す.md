# 打ち消す (うちけす) - deny; negate; drown out

## Meaning

打ち消す is a transitive godan verb: deny or contradict something,
or negate an effect, especially by drowning out a sound. The denied statement or obscured sound takes を.
打消す is another spelling, also read うちけす.

- 彼女はそのうわさを打ち消しました。 (かのじょはそのうわさをうちけしました。) - She denied the rumor.
- 大きな音が彼の声を打ち消しました。 (おおきなおとがかれのこえをうちけしました。) - A loud noise drowned out his voice.

Forms include 打ち消します (うちけします), 打ち消した (うちけした) and 打ち消さない (うちけさない).
The second example does not mean the voice made a false assertion.

## How the kanji combine

[打](../kanji/打.md)ち supplies うち, and [消](../kanji/消.md)す supplies けす.
The native compound connects with [消す](消す.md), "erase; extinguish,"
but its established denial and drowning-out meanings are learned as a whole.
There need not be a physical blow.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[否定](否定.md) (ひてい) overlaps in denial or negation and forms 否定する.
[肯定](肯定.md) (こうてい), "affirmation," contrasts with denying a claim,
but not with every act of drowning out a sound.

## Distinctive meaning

The object determines whether someone contradicts a claim or one sound masks another.
Denying a rumor does not prove that the denial is true.
Nor should the compound replace 消す for every ordinary action such as erasing writing.
