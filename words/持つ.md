# 持つ (もつ) — hold; have; carry

## Meaning

持つ means to hold or carry something, and also to have or own it. In these uses the object takes を.

- このかばんを持ってください。  
  このかばんをもってください。  
  "Please hold this bag."
- 車を持っています。  
  くるまをもっています。  
  "I own a car."

## How the kanji combine

This is one native verb, not a compound. [持](../kanji/持.md) supplies も, with つ as okurigana. It is godan: 持ちます もちます, 持って もって, 持たない もたない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

握る にぎる emphasizes gripping something in the hand. 運ぶ はこぶ emphasizes transporting it from one place to another. 放す はなす (let go) contrasts with physically holding, but is not a general opposite for every sense of 持つ.

## Distinctive meaning

持っている もっている describes a continuing state: either holding something now or possessing it. The car example does not mean carrying a car. For ordinary current ownership, 持っています is more useful than mechanically translating English "have" as the dictionary form 持つ.
