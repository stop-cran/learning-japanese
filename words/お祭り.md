# お祭り (おまつり) -- festival

## Meaning

お祭り is a festival or festive celebration. A neighborhood or shrine festival may have stalls, performances, or a procession, but the word does not require every one of those features.

今夜、友達と町のお祭りに行きます。 (こんや、ともだちとまちのおまつりにいきます。) -- Tonight I am going to the town festival with a friend.

Use に with 行く for the event being visited; use お祭りで for something that happens at the festival.

## How the kanji combine

お is the polite prefix attached to 祭り (まつり), festival. 祭 carries the festival or ritual meaning, and り is the written ending of the noun. This is a native word, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

祭り without お names the same kind of event. フェスティバル is a loanword used in event names; パーティー usually describes a party rather than the same cultural setting.

## Distinctive meaning

The prefix makes this a polite form, not a separate kind of festival. お祭り does not mean a public holiday in general: a festival may occur on a working day, and a holiday need not involve a festival.
