# 丈夫 (じょうぶ) - sturdy; durable

## Meaning

丈夫 describes something sturdy and durable, or a person with a robust constitution. It is a na-adjective.

- 丈夫なかばんが欲しいです。 (じょうぶなかばんがほしいです。) - I want a durable bag.
- 祖父は体が丈夫です。 (そふはからだがじょうぶです。) - My grandfather has a strong constitution.

Use 丈夫な before a noun and 丈夫です as a polite predicate. Do not add い.

## How the kanji combine

丈 and [夫](../kanji/夫.md) form an established compound with on readings ジョウ + ブ. Learn the modern adjective as a whole; the individual characters do not transparently describe a durable bag.

## Synonyms and antonyms

強い (つよい), "strong," is broader and can describe force or ability. 壊れやすい (こわれやすい), "easy to break," provides a practical contrast for objects; 病弱 (びょうじゃく), "sickly," concerns health.

## Distinctive meaning

丈夫 is about lasting strength or robustness, not a momentary cheerful mood. 大丈夫 (だいじょうぶ), "all right; okay," is a separate everyday word; do not shorten every reassurance to 丈夫.
