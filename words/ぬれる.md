# ぬれる - get wet

## Meaning

ぬれる means to get wet. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: ぬれます, ぬれて, ぬれた and ぬれない.

- 雨で服がぬれました。 (あめでふくがぬれました。) - My clothes got wet in the rain.
- 床がぬれています。 (ゆかがぬれています。) - The floor is wet.

The thing that becomes wet takes が. ぬれている commonly describes the resulting wet state, not an action that someone is deliberately performing.

## How the kanji combine

This page uses kana; the ordinary kanji spelling is 濡れる. In either spelling, the ichidan stem is ぬれ, obtained by removing る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぬらす is the transitive partner, "make wet": タオルをぬらす means to wet a towel. 乾く (かわく), "dry; become dry," is the useful opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

English "wet" can be an adjective or a transitive verb. Japanese distinguishes the change ぬれる, the state ぬれている, and the action ぬらす.
