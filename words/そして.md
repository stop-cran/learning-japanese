# そして — and then

## Meaning

そして connects statements with "and" or "and then." It can add another fact or present the next event in a sequence.

朝ご飯を食べました。そして、学校へ行きました。 (あさごはんをたべました。そして、がっこうへいきました。) — I ate breakfast. Then I went to school.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is kana. Dictionary kanji spellings such as 而して are rare; there is no need to use them when writing this everyday conjunction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

それから often introduces a later event or another item. でも, "but," instead introduces a contrast. と connects nouns in expressions such as パンと牛乳 (パンとぎゅうにゅう), "bread and milk."

## Distinctive meaning

そして links statements, not every pair of words that English can join with "and." Its added statement does not always come later in time: it can add a second characteristic or fact about the same situation. Do not assume it necessarily means "therefore."
