# 席 (せき) — seat

## Meaning

席 is a seat or a place for someone to sit, such as on a train or at a table.

この席は空いていますか。 (このせきはあいていますか。) — Is this seat available?

窓のそばの席に座りました。 (まどのそばのせきにすわりました。) — I sat in the seat by the window.

## How the kanji combine

[席](../kanji/席.md) is one kanji used with its on reading セキ. It also appears in 座席 (ざせき), "seat," and 出席 (しゅっせき), "attendance."

## Synonyms and antonyms

椅子 (いす) is a chair, the piece of furniture. 座席 more explicitly names seating, especially in vehicles or venues. 空席 (くうせき) means a vacant seat.

## Distinctive meaning

A 席 need not be a separate chair: a place at a shared bench or a floor-seating table can be a 席. Use 席に座る for sitting down in a seat and 席を立つ (せきをたつ) for leaving one's seat.
