# こっち (こっち) — this way

## Meaning

こっち is a casual form of こちら. It can mean this way, here, this one, or our side, depending on what the speaker points to or contrasts.

こっちに来て。（こっちにきて。） — Come over here.

こっちのかばんが好きです。（こっちのかばんがすきです。） — I like this bag.

## How the kanji combine

Write this word in kana; the dictionary spelling 此方 is rare. It belongs to the こ・そ・あ・ど demonstrative family; the small っ marks a brief consonant closure.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そっち is "that way; your side," あっち is "over there," and どっち asks "which way; which of two." こちら is the more polite alternative.

## Distinctive meaning

For politely introducing a person, use こちら, as in こちらは田中さんです（こちらはたなかさんです）, "This is Mr./Ms. Tanaka." Do not treat こっち as an equally respectful substitute for that person-introducing use.
