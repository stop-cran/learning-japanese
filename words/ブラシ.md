# ブラシ - brush

## Meaning

ブラシ is a brush: a tool used, for example, for cleaning or arranging hair.
It is a noun. で marks the tool used.

- ブラシで靴を磨きました。 (ブラシでくつをみがきました。) - I polished the shoes with a brush.
- 髪をとかすブラシを買いました。 (かみをとかすブラシをかいました。) - I bought a brush for brushing my hair.

The public dictionary also records an electrical brush used in a motor or similar equipment.
That technical use is not hair brushing.

## How the kanji combine

ブラシ is a katakana loanword with no kanji compound.
刷子 (さっし) is a dictionary-recorded Japanese equivalent,
not a reason to change this article's spelling or kanji metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[櫛](櫛.md) (くし) is a comb. A comb and a hairbrush can perform related tasks
but have different structures.
[筆](筆.md) (ふで) is a writing or painting brush.
Use the tool appropriate to the activity rather than treating every English
"brush" as one specialized implement.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list's English "brushy" does not make ブラシ a Japanese adjective.
For the action, use a construction such as ブラシで磨く, polish with a brush,
or 髪をとかす, brush or comb one's hair.
The noun alone does not specify the brush's material or purpose.
