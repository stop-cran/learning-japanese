# 真っ暗 (まっくら) - pitch-dark; utterly hopeless

## Meaning

真っ暗 describes complete darkness. It also describes a very bleak or
hopeless outlook. Use な before a noun and に with an appropriate verb.

- 停電で部屋が真っ暗になりました。 (ていでんでへやがまっくらになりました。) - The power outage left the room pitch-dark.
- その知らせを聞いて、目の前が真っ暗になりました。 (そのしらせをきいて、めのまえがまっくらになりました。) - When I heard the news, everything seemed hopeless.

The second sentence can describe shock and loss of hope, not necessarily
a physical change in the lighting.

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md) contributes the intensifying まっ, and
[暗](../kanji/暗.md) the darkness associated with 暗い (くらい).
Learn the whole form まっくら, including the small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暗い](暗い.md) (くらい) is less specifically total darkness and also
has gloomy uses. [真っ黒](真っ黒.md) (まっくろ) emphasizes blackness;
the two words can overlap in describing a completely black or dark view.
[明るい](明るい.md) (あかるい) gives a useful light or hopeful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

真っ暗 is a na-adjective: 真っ暗な道 (まっくらなみち), a pitch-dark road.
Its negative is 真っ暗ではない (まっくらではない).
A bleak-future use does not assert that the future has a literal black color.
