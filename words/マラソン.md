# マラソン (マラソン) - marathon

## Meaning

マラソン is a marathon. The standard race distance is 42.195 kilometres.

- 来年、マラソンに出ます。 (らいねん、マラソンにでます。) - I will take part in a marathon next year.
- 初めてマラソンを完走しました。 (はじめてマラソンをかんそうしました。) - I finished a marathon for the first time.

完走する (かんそうする) means to complete a race. マラソン大会 (マラソンたいかい) is a marathon event.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[走る](走る.md) (はしる), "run," names the action, not this particular race. [競争](競争.md) (きょうそう) is competition more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

Public usage also extends マラソン to other endurance-running events and long, continuous activities, as in a broadcast marathon. Do not infer an official 42.195-kilometre course from every event name containing the word.
