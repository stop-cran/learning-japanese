# 金融 (きんゆう) - finance; financing

## Meaning

金融 is a noun for finance: the lending, borrowing, and circulation of money and credit.
It also qualifies things as financial, either with **の** or in established compounds such as
金融機関 (きんゆうきかん), "financial institution."

- 兄は金融の仕事をしています。 (あにはきんゆうのしごとをしています。) - My older brother works in finance.
- 銀行は金融機関の一つです。 (ぎんこうはきんゆうきかんのひとつです。) - A bank is one kind of financial institution.

金融政策 (きんゆうせいさく) means monetary policy. The noun describes an economic activity or sector,
not a particular coin or banknote.

## How the kanji combine

[金](../kanji/金.md) contributes money; 融 contributes the idea of circulation or making funds available in this word.
The combination concerns money being supplied and circulated. Read キン + ユウ as きんゆう,
keeping the ん before ゆ distinct.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[経済](経済.md) (けいざい), "economy/economics," includes production, consumption, and other activity beyond finance.
[銀行](銀行.md) (ぎんこう) names a bank, an institution operating in finance.
[現金](現金.md) (げんきん) means cash and is not interchangeable with 金融.

## Distinctive meaning

English "financial" covers many contexts, but the Japanese noun points to finance and credit arrangements,
not simply anything involving a price. A question about the cost of a meal is not automatically a 金融問題.
Learn compounds in context instead of translating every occurrence of "money" as 金融.
