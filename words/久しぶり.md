# 久しぶり (ひさしぶり) - first time in a long while

## Meaning

久しぶり means that a long time has passed since the previous occasion. With に, it describes doing something again after that gap.

久しぶりに友達に会いました。 (ひさしぶりにともだちにあいました。) - I met my friend for the first time in a long while.

お久しぶりです。 (おひさしぶりです。) - It has been a long time.

The second example is a polite greeting when meeting someone again.

## How the kanji combine

久 contributes the idea of a long time. 久しぶり combines the native ひさし with ぶり, a form also found after a time interval. 久し振り is another spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

三年ぶり (さんねんぶり) names a specific gap: "for the first time in three years." しばらく means "for a while" and does not by itself specify a return after that interval.

## Distinctive meaning

久しぶり is about time since the previous occurrence, not how long the current action lasts. Use 久しぶりの before a noun: 久しぶりの旅行 (ひさしぶりのりょこう), "the first trip in a long while." In casual greetings, 久しぶり alone is common.
