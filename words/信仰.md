# 信仰 (しんこう) - religious faith

## Meaning

信仰 is religious faith or belief. It is a noun and a transitive する-verb. A religion or deity can take を with 信仰する; 信仰を持つ means "have religious faith."

- 祖母は仏教を信仰しています。 (そぼはぶっきょうをしんこうしています。) - My grandmother practices the Buddhist faith.
- この地域では山への信仰が今も残っています。 (このちいきではやまへのしんこうがいまものこっています。) - Religious reverence for mountains still survives in this region.

への marks the object toward which the faith or reverence is directed. 信仰している can describe someone's continuing religious adherence, not just an action taking place at this moment.

## How the kanji combine

[信](../kanji/信.md) contributes believing or trusting; 仰 contributes looking up to or revering. Together they suggest belief accompanied by reverence. The on readings are シン + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[宗教](宗教.md) (しゅうきょう) names religion, including an organized tradition. 信仰 focuses on faith or adherence. [信じる](信じる.md) (しんじる) is broader believing; [信用](信用.md) (しんよう) concerns trust or credibility and need not involve religion.

## Distinctive meaning

信仰 is not a neutral replacement for every English "belief." Believing a weather report or trusting a friend's statement does not ordinarily call for this religious vocabulary. The word describes faith without itself judging whether the belief is true or what degree of observance a person follows.
