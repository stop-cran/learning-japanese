# 大学生 (だいがくせい) - university student

## Meaning

大学生 means a university or college student. It is a noun identifying someone's student status.

- 姉は大学生です。 (あねはだいがくせいです。) - My older sister is a university student.

Before another noun, use の: 大学生の兄 (だいがくせいのあに), "my older brother who is a university student." It is not an adjective taking な.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) and [学](../kanji/学.md) form 大学 (だいがく), "university." [生](../kanji/生.md), セイ, identifies a student here. The complete compound uses on readings ダイ + ガク + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

学生 (がくせい) is broader, while 高校生 (こうこうせい) is a high-school student. 大学院生 (だいがくいんせい) specifically identifies a graduate student.

## Distinctive meaning

大学 is the institution; 大学生 is a person studying there. The final 生 is therefore essential. Neither the word's spelling nor its meaning says that the student must be a particular age.
