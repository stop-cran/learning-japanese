# 日時 (にちじ) - date and time; days and hours

## Meaning

日時 is a noun for the date and time of an event, meeting or departure.
It is useful when asking or announcing when something will take place.

- 会議の日時を教えてください。 (かいぎのにちじをおしえてください。) - Please tell me the date and time of the meeting.
- 面接の日時が決まりました。 (めんせつのにちじがきまりました。) - The date and time of the interview have been decided.

The word also has a "days and hours" sense referring to elapsed time.
For example, 日時を費やす (にちじをついやす) means "spend days and hours."
This is not a request for a calendar appointment.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md), "day," and [時](../kanji/時.md), "time; hour,"
form an additive pair: date/day plus time/hour.
The on readings combine as ニチ + ジ, giving にちじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[日付](日付.md) (ひづけ) emphasizes a calendar date.
[時刻](時刻.md) (じこく) emphasizes a time of day.
[時間](時間.md) (じかん) often concerns duration, but can also name an
appointed time; it is not restricted to duration in every sentence.
[日程](日程.md) (にってい) concerns a schedule or planned dates.

## Distinctive meaning

For an event's 日時, give enough information to identify when it happens:
for example, 六月三日の午後二時 (ろくがつみっかのごごにじ),
"June 3 at 2 p.m." This is an illustration, not a rule that the word
always requires both a printed date and a clock reading.
Context separates the appointment use from the elapsed-time sense.
