# 但し (ただし) - however; provided that

## Meaning

但し, usually written ただし, introduces a condition, restriction,
or exception to what was just said. It is a conjunction, not an
adjective meaning "correct."

- 入場は無料です。ただし、予約が必要です。
  (にゅうじょうはむりょうです。ただし、よやくがひつようです。) - Admission is free. However, a reservation is required.
- この部屋は使えます。ただし、飲食は禁止です。
  (このへやはつかえます。ただし、いんしょくはきんしです。) - You may use this room, provided that you do not eat or drink.

Put it before the qualifying statement. The first statement remains
applicable, subject to the added qualification.

## How the kanji combine

但 writes the native connective ただし, with し in kana.
There is no second character to combine. Recognize 但し in written
notices while learning the kana spelling alongside it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しかし](しかし.md) is a broader "however."
[もっとも](もっとも.md) can also add a qualification, and
[ただ](ただ.md) has a conjunction use besides "only" and "free."
These overlap, but do not have identical uses in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

ただし often narrows permission or a general statement, making it useful
in explanations and notices. It does not by itself create an ordinary
"if" clause of every kind. Also distinguish 正しい (ただしい),
"correct": that adjective has an extra final い.
