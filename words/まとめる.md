# まとめる - put together; summarize; finalize

## Meaning

まとめる means to collect or integrate things into a whole, summarize
material, or bring something to a conclusion. It also includes putting
things in order and establishing or deciding arrangements.
It is a transitive ichidan verb; what someone brings together takes を.

- 資料を一つのファイルにまとめました。 (しりょうをひとつのファイルにまとめました。) - I put the materials together in one file.
- 話の内容を三行にまとめてください。 (はなしのないようをさんぎょうにまとめてください。) - Please summarize the talk in three lines.
- 会議で今後の方針をまとめました。 (かいぎでこんごのほうしんをまとめました。) - We finalized our future policy at the meeting.

Forms include まとめます, まとめた, and まとめない.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. 纏める is a recorded but rare kanji spelling, not a
requirement for ordinary writing. Learn the complete native verb rather
than inventing a character-by-character origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[まとまる](まとまる.md) is intransitive: 話がまとまる
(はなしがまとまる), the discussion reaches a conclusion.
話をまとめる presents someone's action in bringing it to a conclusion.
[集める](集める.md) (あつめる) overlaps in collecting;
[整理](整理.md) (せいり) in organizing.

## Distinctive meaning

A summary need not reproduce every detail: it brings the important
content together. Conversely, gathering documents into one file need
not shorten their contents. The object and context decide whether
collecting, summarizing, settling, or deciding is meant.
