# ずらり - in a row; in a line

## Meaning

ずらり describes people or things lined up in an array, typically with
an impression of many extending along the line. It is a mimetic adverb,
not the sound made by the things.

- 店の前に客がずらりと並んでいます。 (みせのまえにきゃくがずらりとならんでいます。) - Customers are lined up in a long row outside the shop.
- 棚には本がずらりと並んでいます。 (たなにはほんがずらりとならんでいます。) - Books stand in a long row on the shelves.

Both ずらり and ずらりと are recorded. The と belongs to the adverbial
pattern, not a quotation in these examples. The related form ずらっと
also describes many people or things lined up.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[並ぶ](並ぶ.md) is the verb line up or stand in a row;
[並べる](並べる.md) means to arrange things in a line.
[列](列.md) names a row or line, and [行列](行列.md) can name a queue.
ずらり adds a description of the array rather than replacing all these
nouns and verbs in the same grammatical position.

## Distinctive meaning

This is not a word for an arbitrary drawn line. The image is people or
things arrayed along a line, not necessarily people waiting for service.
The book example therefore does not imply a queue of customers.
