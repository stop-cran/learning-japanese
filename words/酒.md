# 酒 (さけ) — alcoholic drink

## Meaning

酒 means alcoholic drink; depending on the context, it can refer specifically to sake.
It is a noun, so 酒を飲む (さけをのむ) means "drink alcohol."

- 私は酒を飲みません。 (わたしはさけをのみません。) — I do not drink alcohol.
- この店では酒を売っています。 (このみせではさけをうっています。) — This shop sells alcoholic drinks.

## How the kanji combine

There is only one kanji: [酒](../kanji/酒.md). Here it carries the native reading さけ,
not the on reading シュ found in 日本酒 (にほんしゅ). There is no compound relation to unpack.

## Synonyms and antonyms

日本酒 (にほんしゅ) names Japanese sake, a specific kind of alcoholic drink.
[飲み物](飲み物.md) (のみもの), "beverage," is broader: it also covers drinks such as water and tea.
These words form broader and narrower categories, not an antonym pair.

## Distinctive meaning

The English loanword "sake" can encourage too narrow a translation of Japanese 酒.
In a statement about whether someone drinks, 酒 can cover alcoholic drinks generally; use the context before translating it as rice wine.

For example, ビールも酒です (ビールもさけです), "Beer is also an alcoholic drink."
This includes beer in the broader category without calling it 日本酒.
