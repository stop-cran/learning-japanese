# 数字 (すうじ) - numeral; numerical figure

## Meaning

数字 is a noun for a numeral or digit, and also for numerical figures such as recorded amounts.
It can identify the written symbol or the numerical information being discussed.

- この数字は六ですか、八ですか。 (このすうじはろくですか、はちですか。) - Is this digit a six or an eight?
- 売上の数字を確認してください。 (うりあげのすうじをかくにんしてください。) - Please check the sales figures.

The first example concerns reading a written character. The second concerns the values recorded.
数字で書く (すうじでかく) means "write in numerals."

## How the kanji combine

[数](../kanji/数.md) contributes number, and [字](../kanji/字.md) contributes written character.
The modifier-plus-head relation gives "number character," with the on readings スウ + ジ.
Keep the long vowel in すうじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[数](数.md) (かず) names a number or quantity more broadly.
[文字](文字.md) (もじ) means written characters, including ones that are not numerals.
[番号](番号.md) (ばんごう) identifies a number assigned for labeling or reference, such as a phone number.
These overlap in use but do not name exactly the same thing.

## Distinctive meaning

数字 is not limited to Arabic digits: 漢数字 (かんすうじ) are Chinese-character numerals such as 一 and 二.
Conversely, "figures" in a business report can mean the numerical results, not just their shapes.
Pay attention to whether the conversation concerns writing, counting, or interpreting data.
