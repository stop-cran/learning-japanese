# 不自由 (ふじゆう) - restricted; inconvenienced

## Meaning

不自由 describes difficulty doing what one needs or wants, from inconvenience or lack of resources to restricted physical ability. It is a noun and na-adjective; 不自由する also means experiencing difficulty or doing without something.

- 車がないと、ここでの生活は不自由です。 (くるまがないと、ここでのせいかつはふじゆうです。) - Without a car, life here is inconvenient.
- お金には不自由していません。 (おかねにはふじゆうしていません。) - I am not short of money.

Use 不自由な before a noun. **Resource に不自由する** identifies what one has trouble obtaining or managing without.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) contributes negation; [自](../kanji/自.md) and [由](../kanji/由.md) form [自由](自由.md) (じゆう), freedom. 不 negates that whole base, producing a sense of restricted freedom or ease.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不便](不便.md) (ふべん) overlaps with inconvenience. 不自由 also describes restricted ability, as in 足が不自由 (あしがふじゆう), having difficulty using one's legs. 自由 contrasts with restriction, though it does not replace every negative construction.

## Distinctive meaning

The word is not limited to destitution or a total inability. The money example can simply mean having sufficient resources. When describing disability, be specific and respectful: a statement about mobility does not imply that a person's whole life lacks independence or satisfaction.
