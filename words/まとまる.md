# まとまる - come together; be settled; become coherent

## Meaning

まとまる means to come together into an organized whole. It describes
things being collected, an agreement being reached or work completed,
and ideas becoming coherent or well arranged.
It is an intransitive godan verb: the resulting whole takes が.

- 話し合いで意見がまとまりました。 (はなしあいでいけんがまとまりました。) - Through discussion, we reached a shared view.
- 旅行の費用がまとまったので、予約しました。 (りょこうのひようがまとまったので、よやくしました。) - Once we had the money together for the trip, we made a reservation.
- 考えがまとまってから、返事をします。 (かんがえがまとまってから、へんじをします。) - I will reply once my thoughts are organized.

Forms include まとまります, まとまった, まとまらない.
Do not conjugate it as an ichidan verb merely because it ends in る.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual in all three selected dictionary senses. 纏まる is the
recorded kanji spelling; there is no written kanji in this card's primary
spelling. This is a native verb, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[まとめる](まとめる.md) is transitive: 意見をまとめる
(いけんをまとめる), bring opinions together.
Compare 意見がまとまる, opinions come together.
[集まる](集まる.md) (あつまる) concerns gathering;
[整う](整う.md) (ととのう) includes becoming ordered or settled.
The words overlap without making every physical gathering coherent.

## Distinctive meaning

まとまる is not just the passive conjugation of まとめる.
The person organizing things can be unmentioned, and が presents what
comes together. A group can gather while its members' opinions still
fail to cohere: 人は集まったが、意見はまとまらない
(ひとはあつまったが、いけんはまとまらない).
