# 丸い (まるい) — round; circular

## Meaning

丸い describes a round, circular, or spherical shape. It is an i-adjective: 丸い形 (まるいかたち), "a round shape," and 丸くない (まるくない), "not round."

- 丸いテーブルを買いました。 (まるいてーぶるをかいました。) — I bought a round table.
- 生地を丸くしてください。 (きじをまるくしてください。) — Please make the dough round.

The adverbial form 丸く appears before する when describing making something round.

## How the kanji combine

丸 carries the roundness idea; い completes the native adjective まるい. 円い is another spelling, especially associated with flat circular shapes, but the spellings do not form a rigid everyday three-dimensional/two-dimensional division.

## Synonyms and antonyms

四角い (しかくい) means square or rectangular. 球 (きゅう), "sphere," and 円 (えん), "circle," are more geometrically specific nouns.

## Distinctive meaning

丸い does not promise mathematical perfection. A rounded face or table can be described this way without being a perfect circle. It can also describe an amiable, less sharp-edged personality.
