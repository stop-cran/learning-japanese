# 過去 (かこ) - the past

## Meaning

過去 names the past: time and events before the present. It can modify a noun with の, as in 過去の経験 (かこのけいけん), "past experience." 過去に means "in the past."

- 過去の失敗から学びましょう。 (かこのしっぱいからまなびましょう。) - Let's learn from past mistakes.
- 過去三年間、この町に住んでいました。 (かこさんねんかん、このまちにすんでいました。) - I lived in this town for the past three years.

Before a duration, 過去 can identify a period looking backward from the relevant reference point. It does not have to mean the distant past.

## How the kanji combine

[過](../kanji/過.md) contributes passing, and [去](../kanji/去.md) contributes going away. Together they name time that has passed. The on readings are カ + コ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[昔](昔.md) (むかし) points to former times or long ago. 過去 can be used for precisely measured recent periods as well. [以前](以前.md) (いぜん) concerns an earlier time or what came before a reference point.

[現在](現在.md) (げんざい), "the present," and [未来](未来.md) (みらい), "the future," provide the other positions on a time line.

## Distinctive meaning

In its basic time sense, 過去 is neutral about whether earlier events were happy, regrettable, or important. 過去の失敗 is negative because of 失敗, not because every use of 過去 implies a shameful history. Likewise, the word does not tell you how well someone remembers those events.
