# 適当 (てきとう) - suitable; careless

## Meaning

適当 has two important meanings: suitable or appropriate, and careless or haphazard. The context determines whether it is praise, a neutral instruction, or criticism.

- この仕事に適当な人を探しています。 (このしごとにてきとうなひとをさがしています。) - We are looking for a suitable person for this job.
- 適当なことを言わないでください。 (てきとうなことをいわないでください。) - Please do not say things carelessly.

It is a na-adjective: 適当な + noun, 適当に + action, and 適当です as a predicate.

## How the kanji combine

[適](../kanji/適.md) suggests suitability and [当](../kanji/当.md) fitting or corresponding. That combination helps with the "suitable" sense, but does not predict the whole range of modern usage.

## Synonyms and antonyms

適切 (てきせつ), "appropriate," makes the favorable meaning clearer. いい加減 (いいかげん) overlaps with the careless sense. 不適当 (ふてきとう) means unsuitable.

## Distinctive meaning

An instruction to do something 適当に may mean to use reasonable judgment without exact specifications, or to do it roughly. Do not assume "suitable" always means meticulous, or translate every occurrence as "careless." The two meanings are a practical reason to learn complete phrases.
