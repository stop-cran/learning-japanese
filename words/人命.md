# 人命 (じんめい) - human life

## Meaning

人命 is human life, especially life considered as something to protect,
save, or lose. It is a noun; English can use singular "human life"
or plural "human lives," depending on the sentence.

- 人命を守ることが最優先です。 (じんめいをまもることがさいゆうせんです。) - Protecting human life is the top priority.
- 事故で多くの人命が失われました。 (じこでおおくのじんめいがうしなわれました。) - Many human lives were lost in the accident.

The second sentence uses the passive 失われる (うしなわれる), "be lost."
It does not say that 人命 is a verb.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md), human, specifies whose [命](../kanji/命.md),
life, is meant. The on readings are ジン + メイ.
命 contributes life here, not its separate command or decree meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[命](命.md) (いのち) and [生命](生命.md) (せいめい) overlap in the life
that can be protected or lost; they are not restricted to humans.
[人生](人生.md) (じんせい) instead presents a person's life course or
experiences. English "life" can hide that difference in focus.

## Distinctive meaning

人命 does not mean someone's daily routine or standard of living;
compare [生活](生活.md) (せいかつ).
人命救助 (じんめいきゅうじょ), "saving human lives; rescue,"
is a useful compound. The noun does not itself specify the number
of people involved or a legal or philosophical definition of life.
