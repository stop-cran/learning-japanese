# 格好 (かっこう) - appearance; shape

## Meaning

格好 refers to outward appearance, shape, posture, or how someone is dressed.
Use a descriptive phrase before it to specify the appearance.

- 楽な格好で来てください。 (らくなかっこうできてください。) - Please come in comfortable clothes.

Here で describes the state or manner in which someone comes.

## How the kanji combine

[格](../kanji/格.md) relates to form or standard; [好](../kanji/好.md) has liking
and favorable senses. Learn 格好 as a whole word for appearance, not as
"liking a standard." The reading かっこう includes a small っ and a long final vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

姿 (すがた) also describes a visible figure or appearance. 服 (ふく) means the
clothes themselves; 格好 can include posture and overall presentation.
There is no universal opposite of "appearance."

## Distinctive meaning

格好がいい (かっこうがいい), often shortened to かっこいい, means "cool" or
"good-looking." 格好が悪い (かっこうがわるい) is the corresponding unfavorable
assessment. 格好 alone does not mean that an appearance is good.
