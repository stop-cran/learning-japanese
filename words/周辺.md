# 周辺 (しゅうへん) - surrounding area; periphery

## Meaning

周辺 names the area around something: its surroundings, vicinity, or outskirts. It can also refer to the boundary around a figure, or circumstances surrounding a matter.

- 駅の周辺には店がたくさんあります。
  (えきのしゅうへんにはみせがたくさんあります。) - There are many shops in the area around the station.
- 学校周辺の地図を見ました。
  (がっこうしゅうへんのちずをみました。) - I looked at a map of the area around the school.

In computing, 周辺 can mean a peripheral; 周辺機器 (しゅうへんきき) means peripheral equipment or devices.

## How the kanji combine

[周](../kanji/周.md) contributes the extent around something, and [辺](../kanji/辺.md) the surrounding area or boundary. Their related meanings reinforce the area around a reference point. Read シュウ + ヘン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[周り](周り.md) (まわり) and [周囲](周囲.md) (しゅうい) also name surroundings. [近く](近く.md) (ちかく) emphasizes nearness. In geometry, [円周](円周.md) (えんしゅう) specifically concerns a circle's circumference, whereas 周辺 is not limited to circles.

## Distinctive meaning

周辺 does not supply a fixed distance. A station's 周辺 may include several streets, while a geometric boundary is a line. The reference point and context separate these uses. Likewise, 周辺機器 is a technical compound, not a claim that every nearby object is a computer accessory.
