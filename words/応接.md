# 応接 (おうせつ) - receiving visitors; reception

## Meaning

応接 is receiving and attending to visitors. It is a noun also used
with する. A useful construction is 客に応接する
(きゃくにおうせつする), "attend to guests."

- 担当者が客に応接しました。
  (たんとうしゃがきゃくにおうせつしました。) - The person in charge attended to the guests.
- 客の応接で忙しいです。
  (きゃくのおうせつでいそがしいです。) - I am busy receiving visitors.

に identifies the people attended to in the first example.
応接室 (おうせつしつ) is a room for receiving visitors,
such as a reception room or parlour.

## How the kanji combine

応 contributes responding, and [接](../kanji/接.md) contact
or receiving. Their related ideas connect responding with meeting
the visitor: オウ + セツ. This is a memory aid, not a claim about
the historical formation of either character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[応対](応対.md) (おうたい) also covers attending to people and
overlaps with 応接. The words do not divide into an absolute
in-person versus telephone rule.
[受付](受付.md) (うけつけ) can name a reception desk or the
acceptance of requests, rather than all the subsequent attention
given to a visitor.

## Distinctive meaning

English "reception" is ambiguous:
[歓迎](歓迎.md) (かんげい) emphasizes a welcome, while
[宴会](宴会.md) (えんかい) can name a banquet or party.
応接 here is attending to visitors, not the banquet itself.
Nor does the word alone guarantee a warm or enthusiastic welcome.
