# 頂上 (ちょうじょう) — summit; top

## Meaning

頂上 (ちょうじょう) is the top or summit. A mountain summit is a clear concrete example:
山の頂上 (やまのちょうじょう).
Use に for the point reached and まで for the endpoint of a distance or journey.

- 山の頂上に着きました。 (やまのちょうじょうにつきました。) — We reached the mountain's summit.
- 頂上まであと少しです。 (ちょうじょうまであとすこしです。) — It is only a little farther to the top.

## How the kanji combine

[頂](../kanji/頂.md) means a top or summit here, and [上](../kanji/上.md) adds the upper position.
The two characters reinforce the idea of the topmost point.
Their on readings are チョウ + ジョウ: keep the long vowel in both halves of ちょうじょう.
頂's separate "receive" sense is not the one used in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

山頂 (さんちょう), "mountain summit," overlaps directly in a mountain context.
[上](上.md) (うえ) is broader: it can indicate above, an upper part, or a top.
An upper location is not necessarily the very highest point, so 上 is not a precise substitute in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

頂上 identifies the top as a destination or location, not the activity of climbing.
Reaching a high point along a route does not automatically mean reaching its 頂上.
The surrounding noun or conversation tells you what has a summit or top.
