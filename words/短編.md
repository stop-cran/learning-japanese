# 短編 (たんぺん) - short story; short film

## Meaning

短編 is a short work, such as a short story, film, or poem.
It is a noun; the medium comes from context or a following noun.

- 寝る前に短編を一つ読みました。
  (ねるまえにたんぺんをひとつよみました。) - I read a short story before bed.
- この短編映画は十分ほどです。
  (このたんぺんえいがはじゅっぷんほどです。) - This short film is about ten minutes long.

短編小説 (たんぺんしょうせつ) explicitly means a short story;
短編映画 (たんぺんえいが) explicitly identifies a short film.

## How the kanji combine

[短](../kanji/短.md), short, qualifies
[編](../kanji/編.md), a compiled or completed work.
The reading is **たんぺん**, with ぺ, not たんへん.
短篇 is another recorded spelling with the same reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

長編 (ちょうへん) is a long work, such as a full-length novel or film.
[小説](小説.md) (しょうせつ) can include both short and long prose fiction;
小 does not make every 小説 a 短編.
[映画](映画.md) (えいが) identifies the medium, not the length.

## Distinctive meaning

短編 is not the general adjective "short."
Use [短い](短い.md) (みじかい) for a short pencil or a short visit.
The word does not impose one universal word count or running time;
the relevant genre or classification supplies that boundary.
