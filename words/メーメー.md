# メーメー — sheep bleating

## Meaning

The conventional Japanese representation of a sheep's bleat.

- 羊（ひつじ）がメーメーと鳴（な）いた。 — The sheep bleated.
- 牧場（ぼくじょう）でメーメーという声を聞いた。 — I heard bleating at the farm.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こけこっこー](こけこっこー.md) evokes a rooster's crow, and [わんわん](わんわん.md) commonly evokes barking. These animal voices are comparisons, not synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

The inspected dictionary records メーメー and メエメエ. This card retains an attested katakana spelling rather than creating an extra hiragana identity. Katakana does not make this native sound expression a loanword.
