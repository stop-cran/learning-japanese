# 着物 (きもの) — kimono

## Meaning

着物 means a kimono: traditional Japanese clothing, especially the full-length garment worn with an obi. The dictionary also records the broader meaning "clothes", but "kimono" is the useful starting meaning in modern everyday conversation.

- お正月に着物を着ました。  
  おしょうがつにきものをきました。  
  "I wore a kimono at New Year."

## How the kanji combine

[着](../kanji/着.md) relates to wearing and [物](../kanji/物.md) means "thing". The native elements き, from 着る (きる), and もの give the transparent association "something to wear". That association does not mean every garment is normally called a 着物 today.

## Synonyms and antonyms

服 (ふく) is the general everyday word for clothes. 和服 (わふく) means Japanese-style clothing, contrasting with 洋服 (ようふく), Western-style clothing. 浴衣 (ゆかた) is a lighter, informal garment in this clothing tradition.

## Distinctive meaning

着物を着る (きものをきる) repeats 着 with different roles: first inside the noun, then in the verb "wear". Mark the garment with を.
