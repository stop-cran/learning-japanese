# 安全 (あんぜん) - safety; safe

## Meaning

安全 means safety or freedom from danger. As a na-adjective, it takes な before a noun and に when describing how something is done.

ここは安全な場所です。 (ここはあんぜんなばしょです。) - This is a safe place.

安全に運転してください。 (あんぜんにうんてんしてください。) - Please drive safely.

As a noun, 安全を確認する (あんぜんをかくにんする) means "check that it is safe."

## How the kanji combine

[安](../kanji/安.md) suggests ease or peace; [全](../kanji/全.md) means whole or complete. Together their on readings form あんぜん, a word for being protected from danger.

## Synonyms and antonyms

危険 (きけん), "danger; dangerous," is the direct contrast. 安心 (あんしん) means peace of mind or relief: it concerns how someone feels, whereas 安全 concerns the situation's safety.

## Distinctive meaning

Someone can feel reassured without actually being safe. Keep 安全 and 安心 distinct, and do not use the adjective form 安全い. The predicate is 安全です, "it is safe."
