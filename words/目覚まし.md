# 目覚まし (めざまし) - alarm clock; waking

## Meaning

目覚まし commonly abbreviates 目覚まし時計 (めざましどけい), "alarm clock."
目覚し, the source-list spelling, and 目ざまし are spellings of this same めざまし entry.

- 目覚ましを七時にセットしました。 (めざましをしちじにせっとしました。) - I set the alarm clock for seven.
- 目覚ましを掛け忘れました。 (めざましをかけわすれました。) - I forgot to set the alarm clock.

The entry also records waking up or keeping oneself awake.
For example, 目覚ましに濃いお茶を飲む
(めざましにこいおちゃをのむ) means "drink strong tea to wake oneself up."
A separate regional/traditional use is an early-morning treat, especially for children;
do not assume that use whenever someone mentions an alarm.

## How the kanji combine

[目](../kanji/目.md) contributes the eyes, and [覚](../kanji/覚.md) contributes waking.
目覚まし is the noun here; the related verb 目覚ます (めざます) concerns waking someone.
The shorter written form 目覚し does not remove ま from the pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時計](時計.md) (とけい) is a clock or watch in general.
[覚ます](覚ます.md) (さます) concerns waking someone or bringing them out of a state;
[覚める](覚める.md) (さめる) concerns waking or coming out of it.
These related verbs are not substitutes for the object noun in 目覚ましをセットする.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose the sense from the construction: setting a 目覚まし concerns an alarm,
while tea taken 目覚ましに serves a waking purpose.
This noun is not the ordinary verb "get up"; that is a use of [起きる](起きる.md) (おきる).
