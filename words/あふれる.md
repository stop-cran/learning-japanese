# あふれる - overflow; brim over

## Meaning

あふれる means to overflow or brim over. It is an **intransitive ichidan verb**:
あふれます, あふれて, あふれた.
What overflows can take が, with から identifying where it spills from.

- コップから水があふれました。 (コップからみずがあふれました。) - Water overflowed from the glass.
- 会場は人であふれていました。 (かいじょうはひとであふれていました。) - The venue was overflowing with people.

The second example uses で for what fills the place. It describes a crowded venue,
not people literally flowing as a liquid.

## How the kanji combine

溢れる is the dictionary kanji spelling; the entry marks the word as usually written in kana.
Learn あふれる as a whole verb. The れる ending here does not make it a passive verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[満ちる](満ちる.md) (みちる), "become full; brim with," overlaps in fullness.
あふれる can emphasize an excess beyond a boundary, as when water runs out of a glass.
There is no single opposite that covers overflowing water and an overflowing crowd equally well.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's English "flood" does not make あふれる a transitive verb for deliberately flooding a place.
Compare 水があふれる, "water overflows," not 水をあふれる.
The noun and context determine whether the overflow is physical or figurative.
