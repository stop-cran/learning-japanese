# 予備 (よび) - reserve; spare; preliminary preparation

## Meaning

予備 concerns keeping something ready for when it is needed, or
the spare item itself. It also has a preparation or preliminary
sense. Use の before a following noun.

- 予備の電池を持っていきます。 (よびのでんちをもっていきます。) - I'll take spare batteries.
- 予備に一本買っておきました。 (よびにいっぽんかっておきました。) - I bought one extra to keep in reserve.

予備の鍵 (よびのかぎ) is a spare key.
In 予備知識 (よびちしき), "background knowledge," 予備 concerns
knowledge acquired beforehand. 予備調査 (よびちょうさ) is a
preliminary survey or investigation.

## How the kanji combine

[予](../kanji/予.md) contributes beforehand, and
[備](../kanji/備.md) preparing or providing.
The on readings are ヨ + ビ. The alternative spelling 預備 is
recognized, but does not change the reading or the lesson's primary ID.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[準備](準備.md) (じゅんび), [用意](用意.md) (ようい), and
[支度](支度.md) (したく) overlap in preparation.
[余分](余分.md) (よぶん) concerns something extra or surplus;
予備 emphasizes having it ready for a possible need.
[代わり](代わり.md) (かわり) is a substitute or replacement.

## Distinctive meaning

A spare battery may never be used: reserve readiness is enough.
Not every use of 予備 names an extra physical object, however;
preliminary investigation and background knowledge concern prior
preparation. Do not form an ordinary な-adjective from this noun:
learn 予備の電池, not 予備な電池.
