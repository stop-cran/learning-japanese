# 雲 (くも) — cloud

## Meaning

雲 means a cloud in the sky. Like other Japanese nouns, it does not change form to mark singular or plural.

- 空に白い雲が浮かんでいます。 (そらにしろいくもがうかんでいます。) — White clouds are floating in the sky.
- 今日は雲が多いです。 (きょうはくもがおおいです。) — There are many clouds today.

雲が多い describes the amount of cloud; 雲の形 (くものかたち) means "the shape of a cloud."

## How the kanji combine

雲 is a single kanji representing the native noun くも. There is no compound or changing verb ending to analyze here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

曇り (くもり) means cloudy weather, not an individual cloud. 霧 (きり), "fog," is a related weather word. 晴れ (はれ), "clear weather," contrasts with cloudiness rather than being the opposite of a cloud.

## Distinctive meaning

The homophone 蜘蛛 (くも) means "spider." The characters and sentence context distinguish them; a shared kana reading is not evidence that the two meanings belong to one word.
