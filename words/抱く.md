# 抱く (だく) — hold in one's arms

## Meaning

抱く, read **だく** here, means holding someone or something in one's arms, embracing, or hugging.
The person or thing held takes を.

- 母は赤ちゃんを抱いています。 (はははあかちゃんをだいています。) — My mother is holding the baby in her arms.
- 子供を優しく抱きました。 (こどもをやさしくだきました。) — I gently held the child in my arms.

The て-form is 抱いて (だいて), the past is 抱いた (だいた), and the negative is 抱かない (だかない).

The separately recorded reading **いだく** also means to hold in one's arms, in a formal or literary use,
and to harbor a thought or feeling. It is a transitive godan verb too, but it is not labeled slang in the inspected entry.

- 母は幼い子を胸に抱いていた。 (はははおさないこをむねにいだいていた。) — The mother held her young child to her chest. This uses the literary physical sense.
- 将来に希望を抱いています。 (しょうらいにきぼうをいだいています。) — I have hopes for the future.

For this reading, the forms are 抱いた (いだいた), 抱いて (いだいて), and 抱かない (いだかない).
The feeling takes を; its subject matter can take に, as in the future example.

## How the kanji combine

[抱](../kanji/抱.md) supplies the embracing idea. The ending く completes one native verb.
The same written form also has the reading いだく; the headword's reading is therefore essential information.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[抱える](抱える.md) (かかえる) also describes carrying something in the arms or under an arm.
[持つ](持つ.md) (もつ) is broader: it can mean holding something in the hand or possessing it.
Owning a bag and holding a child in one's arms are not the same kind of "having."

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the だく entry separate from 抱く (いだく) in expressions for thoughts or feelings.
希望を抱く (きぼうをいだく) means "harbor hope." The shared spelling does not justify substituting だく
for every use of いだく. Physical holding can occur with either reading in the appropriate register,
but that overlap does not erase the reading distinction in the feeling construction.
