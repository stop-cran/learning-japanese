# 粗末 (そまつ) - poor-quality; careless

## Meaning

粗末 is a na-adjective describing something crude, shabby, or poor in quality. 粗末な + noun describes the thing itself. Nを粗末にする means treating N carelessly or without due regard.

- その小屋には粗末な机が一つありました。 (そのこやにはそまつなつくえがひとつありました。) - There was one crude table in the hut.
- 食べ物を粗末にしないでください。 (たべものをそまつにしないでください。) - Please do not waste food or treat it carelessly.

The second example evaluates treatment of the food, not its quality.

## How the kanji combine

粗 contributes roughness or coarseness. [末](../kanji/末.md) can suggest something minor or of little account. Together the established compound conveys poor quality or insufficient care. The readings are ソ + マツ; a literal "rough end" is not a useful translation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[簡単](簡単.md) (かんたん), "simple," need not be a criticism of quality. [丁寧](丁寧.md) (ていねい), "careful," contrasts with careless handling. [大切](大切.md) (たいせつ) appears in 大切にする, "take good care of."

## Distinctive meaning

Calling someone else's belongings 粗末 can be insulting. A host's お粗末さまでした (おそまつさまでした), said modestly after serving food, is a conventional self-deprecating expression, not a request for guests to judge the meal as bad.
