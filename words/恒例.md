# 恒例 (こうれい) — established practice; custom

## Meaning

恒例 is something that is always done in the same way or at a fixed time. Digital Daijisen notes that it is used mostly, though not only, of ceremonies and events, and also for such an event itself, as in 新春恒例の歌会 (しんしゅんこうれいのうたかい), “the poetry gathering customarily held at New Year.”

- 恒例の花火大会が、今年も八月に開かれた。 (こうれいのはなびたいかいが、ことしもはちがつにひらかれた。) — The customary fireworks festival was held in August again this year.
- 会議の後にみんなで食事をするのが、この会社の恒例になっている。 (かいぎのあとにみんなでしょくじをするのが、このかいしゃのこうれいになっている。) — Eating together after meetings has become an established practice at this company.

## How the kanji combine

[恒](../kanji/恒.md) (こう) means constant, and [例](../kanji/例.md) (れい) here means an established custom; [Digital Daijisen, 例](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E4%BE%8B) lists 恒例 under that sense, sense 3. Before a noun, use の: 恒例の花火大会.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[習慣](習慣.md) (しゅうかん) is a habit or custom, including a personal one. [行事](行事.md) (ぎょうじ) is an organized event, and [伝統](伝統.md) (でんとう) a tradition handed down over time. [毎年](毎年.md) (まいとし) only says “every year”; 恒例 says that something is an established practice.

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen notes that 恒例 was once also read ごうれい; this article teaches こうれい. Related words are 恒常 (こうじょう), “constancy,” and 恒久 (こうきゅう), “permanence.”
