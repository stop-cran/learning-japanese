# 元気 (げんき) — well; energetic

## Meaning

元気 describes being well, lively, or full of energy. It is a noun and a な-adjective:
元気な人 (げんきなひと) is "an energetic person," and 元気です means "I am well" or "I feel energetic," depending on context.

- お元気ですか。 (おげんきですか。) — How are you? / Have you been well?
- 子供たちは今日も元気です。 (こどもたちはきょうもげんきです。) — The children are full of energy today too.

## How the kanji combine

[元](../kanji/元.md) contributes an origin or foundation idea, while [気](../kanji/気.md) can concern spirit or energy.
Think of "basic vitality" as a meaning aid, not a literal translation or a historical claim.
The on readings are ゲン + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

健康 (けんこう) focuses on health.
[体力](体力.md) (たいりょく) concerns physical strength and stamina, not simply being cheerful or lively.
元気がない (げんきがない) means lacking one's usual energy or spirits; it does not necessarily mean being ill.

## Distinctive meaning

元気 can describe outward liveliness as well as health. A quiet or discouraged person may seem 元気がない without having a disease.
お元気ですか is useful when asking about someone's well-being, particularly after time apart; it is not required at every meeting.
