# くるくる — spinning; coiling around

## Meaning

くるくる depicts repeated spinning, revolving, or winding. It often gives an impression of light, smooth, nimble movement.

- 風車が風でくるくる回っている。 — The pinwheel is spinning in the wind. (風車: かざぐるま; 回る: まわる.)
- リボンを指にくるくると巻いた。 — I wound the ribbon around my finger. (巻く: まく.)
- 猫はくるくる変わる表情で私を楽しませる。 — The cat entertains me with its constantly changing expressions. (表情: ひょうじょう.)

It modifies the action directly or with と. In descriptions such as くるくるした髪 (かみ), した connects the curled quality to a noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぐるぐる](ぐるぐる.md) overlaps with rotation and winding, often sounding less light or nimble. [ころころ](ころころ.md) emphasizes rolling or frequent changes. None provides a strict size-based distinction.

## Distinctive meaning

The following verb matters: 回る identifies spinning; 巻く identifies winding; 変わる identifies change. A stationary object may spin in place, so くるくる does not itself mean travelling around a route.
