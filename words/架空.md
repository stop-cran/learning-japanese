# 架空 (かくう) - imaginary; overhead

## Meaning

架空 can describe something fictitious, imaginary or fabricated. In this sense, use **架空の + noun**.

- この物語の町は架空の町です。 (このものがたりのまちはかくうのまちです。) - The town in this story is an imaginary town.
- 架空の人物の名前を書きました。 (かくうのじんぶつのなまえをかきました。) - I wrote the name of a fictional person.

It also has a separate aerial or overhead sense, as in 架空線 (かくうせん), "overhead wire." Such a wire is not imaginary.

## How the kanji combine

架 contributes spanning or supporting aloft and [空](../kanji/空.md) sky/empty space. Those associations help remember the overhead sense: カ + クウ. They are not a historical derivation of every figurative use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[想像](想像.md) (そうぞう) is imagination or imagining; 架空の町 describes the imagined town, not the act of imagining it. 虚構 (きょこう) can mean fiction or fabrication.

実在する (じつざいする), "actually exist," provides a useful contrast to the fictitious sense. A fictional character need not be a deliberate lie: context distinguishes imaginative creation from fraudulent fabrication.

## Distinctive meaning

The checked entry also lists がくう, but its fictitious/imaginary sense is restricted to **かくう**. Do not use がくう as a freely interchangeable reading for 架空の人物. Recognition of the alternate reading in the overhead sense does not change this card's primary reading.
