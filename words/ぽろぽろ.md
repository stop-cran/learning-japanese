# ぽろぽろ — in separate falling drops

## Meaning

ぽろぽろ describes drops falling one after another, often tears or drops of water.

- 葉先から水滴がぽろぽろ落ちた。
  — Drops of water fell one by one from the tip of the leaf.
- 彼女の目から涙がぽろぽろこぼれた。
  — Tears fell in large drops from her eyes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぽつぽつ](ぽつぽつ.md) can describe scattered drops or spots, but ぽろぽろ presents the
drops as falling one by one. [ほろほろ](ほろほろ.md) can also describe a gentle fall and is
used for tender food crumbling apart.

## Distinctive meaning

The sound of the word does not specify whether the drops are tears or water: the subject and
surrounding sentence do. The examples here focus on drops falling, not on small spots already
scattered across a surface.
