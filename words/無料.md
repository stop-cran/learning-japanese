# 無料 (むりょう) - free of charge

## Meaning

無料 means that no fee is charged. It is a noun used in **無料です**, "it is free," **無料の + noun**, "a free ...," and **無料で + verb**, "do something free of charge."

- この美術館は今日は入場無料です。 (このびじゅつかんはきょうはにゅうじょうむりょうです。) - Admission to this art museum is free today.
- このアプリは無料で使えます。 (このアプリはむりょうでつかえます。) - You can use this app free of charge.

The first example specifies admission. It does not say that every item sold inside the museum is also free. **無料のサービス** is "a free service."

## How the kanji combine

無 contributes absence or "without"; [料](../kanji/料.md) contributes a fee or charge. 無 modifies 料: without a charge. The on readings are ム + リョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

有料 (ゆうりょう), "paid; requiring a fee," is the direct opposite. [ただ](ただ.md) can also mean free of charge in context. [自由](自由.md) (じゆう) concerns freedom or liberty, not the price.

## Distinctive meaning

English "free" has several meanings. A free afternoon or freedom to choose is not necessarily 無料. Also check the stated scope: free admission, free delivery, and a free trial remove different charges and may have conditions. The word itself says nothing about ownership or permission to redistribute something.
