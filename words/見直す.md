# 見直す (みなおす) - look again; review; think better of

## Meaning

見直す can mean to look at something again, re-examine it or review it with possible changes.

- 答えをもう一度見直しました。 (こたえをもういちどみなおしました。) - I checked my answers again.
- 費用が高いので、計画を見直します。 (ひようがたかいので、けいかくをみなおします。) - The costs are high, so we will review the plan.

It also means to form a better opinion of someone or something.

- 彼の仕事ぶりを見て、彼を見直しました。 (かれのしごとぶりをみて、かれをみなおしました。) - Seeing how he worked made me think more highly of him.

A further intransitive use concerns recovery or improvement, for example 景気が見直す (けいきがみなおす), "the economy picks up." This is distinct from a person reviewing something.

It is a godan verb: 見直します, 見直して, 見直さない. The looking/reviewing/opinion uses are transitive; the recovery use is intransitive.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md), "see," combines with [直](../kanji/直.md), associated with correcting or doing anew. Both use native readings: み + なおす. Learn the complete word's uses rather than assuming that it means only physically repairing what is seen.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[検討](検討.md) (けんとう) is consideration or examination, and [修正](修正.md) (しゅうせい) is correction or revision. [回復](回復.md) (かいふく) overlaps in recovery. [復習](復習.md) (ふくしゅう) specifically concerns reviewing learned material, not every reconsideration of a plan.

## Distinctive meaning

Reviewing a plan does not necessarily mean deciding it is good: it may lead to cuts or changes. The better-opinion sense is specifically a more favourable appraisal. The recovery sense does not make every improvement in a learned skill an interchangeable use of 見直す.
