# 猿 (さる) - monkey

## Meaning

猿 is a noun for a monkey, especially familiar species such as the Japanese macaque. The animal sense is often written in kana as **サル** or さる. It can also be used broadly for non-human primates.

- 動物園でサルを見ました。 (どうぶつえんでサルをみました。) - I saw monkeys at the zoo.
- 猿が木に登っています。 (さるがきにのぼっています。) - A monkey is climbing a tree.

The noun itself does not mark singular or plural. 日本猿 (にほんざる), "Japanese macaque," has a voiced ざる in the compound.

## How the kanji combine

猿 is a single kanji carrying the whole native reading さる. Its animal component helps with recognition, but it is not a compound of separately pronounced words. Kana and kanji can identify the same animal.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[人間](人間.md) (にんげん), "human being," is a useful species contrast, not a direct antonym. When a particular species matters, use its name instead of expecting the broad word 猿 to provide a precise zoological classification.

## Distinctive meaning

Animal names can become insults when applied to people. 猿 has derogatory uses for a sly or foolish person; those are not neutral ways to describe someone's personality. The animal examples here do not carry that judgment. A dictionary's wider range does not make every comparison with a person socially harmless.
