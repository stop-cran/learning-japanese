# 趣味 (しゅみ) — hobby; personal taste

## Meaning

趣味 means a **hobby or pastime**: an activity someone enjoys outside their obligations. It can also mean personal taste or preference.

- 私の趣味は写真を撮ることです。 (わたしのしゅみはしゃしんをとることです。) — My hobby is taking photographs.
- 服の趣味が違います。 (ふくのしゅみがちがいます。) — Our tastes in clothes differ.

In the first example, dictionary-form verb + こと makes the activity a noun phrase. 趣味 itself is a noun, not a verb meaning “enjoy.”

## How the kanji combine

趣 contributes interest or inclination, while [味](../kanji/味.md) contributes taste in the extended sense. The on readings シュ + ミ form しゅみ; the word does not refer to a food's flavor.

## Synonyms and antonyms

楽しみ (たのしみ) is enjoyment or something to look forward to. 興味 (きょうみ) means interest: having an interest in a subject does not necessarily make it an established hobby.

## Distinctive meaning

Context separates “hobby” from “taste.” 趣味がいい (しゅみがいい) often praises someone's taste rather than saying that their recreational activities are morally good.
