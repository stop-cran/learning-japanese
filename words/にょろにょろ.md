# にょろにょろ — slithering and wriggling

## Meaning

にょろにょろ describes a long, winding, wriggling motion, especially the movement of a snake or worm.

- 小さなヘビが、草むらをにょろにょろ進んでいった。
  — A small snake slithered through the grass.
- ミミズが土の上をにょろにょろ動いている。
  — An earthworm is wriggling across the soil.

## Synonyms and antonyms

にょろにょろ emphasizes winding, flexible movement rather than a straight or rigid path.

## Distinctive meaning

It most often describes a long body bending as it moves.
