# 為す (なす) - do; carry out

## Meaning

為す means doing, committing, carrying out or bringing about an action.
It is usually written なす. The action takes を; the verb does not
inherently say that the deed is good or successful.

- 善をなすのは簡単ではありません。 (ぜんをなすのはかんたんではありません。) - Doing good is not easy.
- 彼は悪をなした。 (かれはあくをなした。) - He committed evil.

This is a transitive godan verb: なします, なさない, なして and なした.
The potential なせる appears in なせる業 (なせるわざ), a feat or result
that something can bring about.

## How the kanji combine

為 writes な, with す in kana. Learn the whole native verb rather than
trying to pronounce an on-reading compound. 爲す is a search-only
old-character spelling in the pinned dictionary, not the teaching form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[する](する.md) is the general everyday "do."
[行う](行う.md) (おこなう) means carrying out or conducting an activity.
なす occurs in established expressions such as 善をなす; it cannot replace
する in every activity or noun + する construction.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not merge all meanings of 成す (なす) into this spelling.
The separately indexed 成す family includes forming or constituting
something and accomplishing something; it also explicitly overlaps with
為す in doing a deed. That overlap is not a rule that every use accepts
either kanji. Likewise, 為す is not the する reading of rare 為る.
