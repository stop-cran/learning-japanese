# 冒す (おかす) — brave danger; take a risk

## Meaning

冒す means braving danger or proceeding despite a known difficulty. In 危険を冒す (きけんをおかす), "take a risk," を marks the danger being faced.
It is a transitive verb, not a noun meaning an adventure.

- 危険を冒して山に登りました。 (きけんをおかしてやまにのぼりました。) — I climbed the mountain despite the danger.
- 危険を冒す必要はありません。 (きけんをおかすひつようはありません。) — There is no need to take a risk.

The second sentence says that taking the risk is unnecessary; it does not say that the situation is safe.

## How the kanji combine

[冒](../kanji/冒.md) writes おか, with す as okurigana. There is no second kanji to read as a compound.
This is a godan verb: 冒します (おかします), 冒して (おかして), 冒さない (おかさない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冒険](冒険.md) (ぼうけん) is the related noun "adventure; risky venture"; 冒険する means to venture or have an adventure.
[危険](危険.md) (きけん) names danger, whether or not anyone chooses to face it.
Avoiding danger contrasts with braving it, but 冒す has other senses too, so no single opposite covers every use.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every occurrence as voluntarily taking a risk. 病に冒される (やまいにおかされる), "be afflicted by a disease," uses the passive of the harm/affliction sense.
Here the person is affected by the illness, not choosing to brave it. This sense can also concern harmful chemicals.
The dictionary also records desecrating something sacred and assuming someone else's surname; these are separate senses, not explanations of 危険を冒す.

Two other verbs share the reading おかす. 犯す, written with [犯](../kanji/犯.md), is used in 罪を犯す (つみをおかす), "commit a crime."
侵す concerns invading or infringing and also has a harm/affliction use overlapping with 冒す.
Choose the spelling from the expression and meaning, not from the sound alone; this comparison is not an absolute division of every use.
