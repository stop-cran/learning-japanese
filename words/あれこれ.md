# あれこれ - this and that; one thing or another

## Meaning

あれこれ refers to various things or considerations: this and that, one thing or
another. It can be pronoun-like or modify an action directly, without adding に.

- 旅行の準備で、あれこれ買いました。 (りょこうのじゅんびで、あれこれかいました。) - I bought this and that to prepare for the trip.
- 一人であれこれ考えていました。 (ひとりであれこれかんがえていました。) - I was thinking about one thing and another by myself.

## How the kanji combine

Learn the kana expression as あれ + これ, "that and this." The dictionary marks
彼是 and 彼此 as rare spellings; [彼](../kanji/彼.md) is not read かれ by itself
to produce the reading あれこれ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いろいろ](いろいろ.md) also expresses variety. あれこれ can foreground the individual things being
considered or tried, without specifying their number. "Only one thing" contrasts
in a particular context, not as a fixed lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not transfer every sense of another reading in the same dictionary family.
The approximate-time use belongs to かれこれ, as in かれこれ十年 (かれこれじゅうねん),
"about ten years." The reading あれこれ in these examples means this and that,
not approximately a duration.
