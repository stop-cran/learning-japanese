# じめじめ — damp and muggy; gloomy

## Meaning

Uncomfortable dampness or humidity, often lingering in a room, clothing, or the weather. Figuratively, it can describe a gloomy, dispiriting mood.

- 梅雨（つゆ）は部屋の中までじめじめする。 — During the rainy season, even the room feels damp and muggy.
- 押し入れ（おしいれ）がじめじめして、かびが生えて（はえて）しまった。 — The closet was damp, and mold grew in it.
- 失敗しても、いつまでもじめじめしていたくない。 — Even after failing, I don't want to stay gloomy forever.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic repeating じめ, with a voiced initial consonant. There are no written kanji to divide; the dampness is not an audible sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[じとじと](じとじと.md) overlaps strongly in clinging, unpleasant dampness. [しっとり](しっとり.md) often describes milder or welcome moisture. [からから](からから.md) describes extreme dryness rather than comfortable ventilation.

## Distinctive meaning

じめじめする, じめじめした部屋, and じめじめとした天気 are useful. The mood sense can sound critical when applied to another person. This is not simply “raining”: a room may be じめじめ even when no rain is falling, and pleasant moist cake is usually not praised as じめじめ.
