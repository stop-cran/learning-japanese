# 家具 (かぐ) - furniture

## Meaning

家具 is a noun for furniture, such as tables, chairs, beds, and cabinets. It names the category without specifying how many pieces there are.

- 引っ越す前に家具を売りました。 (ひっこすまえにかぐをうりました。) - I sold my furniture before moving.
- この部屋には大きな家具を置けません。 (このへやにはおおきなかぐをおけません。) - We cannot put large furniture in this room.

Use 家具を買う (かぐをかう), "buy furniture," and 家具を置く (かぐをおく), "put furniture somewhere." 家具付きの部屋 (かぐつきのへや) means "a furnished room": 付き indicates that the furniture is included.

## How the kanji combine

[家](../kanji/家.md) means house or home, and [具](../kanji/具.md) contributes equipment or implements. 家 specifies the setting for the equipment. The established compound means furniture, not everything that happens to be inside a house.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[道具](道具.md) (どうぐ), "tools; implements," is a different category organized around use. [机](机.md) (つくえ), "desk," and [いす](いす.md), "chair," name individual kinds of furniture. They are examples, not interchangeable synonyms for the whole category.

## Distinctive meaning

Furniture can be used in an office as well as a home. Do not interpret the 家 literally enough to exclude office desks. When asking about one specific item, its name gives more information than 家具. There is no everyday antonym; 家具のない部屋 describes "a room without furniture."
