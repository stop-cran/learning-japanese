# 仕方 (しかた) — way of doing

## Meaning

仕方 means a way, method, or means of doing something. Noun + の仕方 specifies the activity: 仕事の仕方 (しごとのしかた), "how to do the work."

予約の仕方を教えてください。 (よやくのしかたをおしえてください。) — Please show me how to make a reservation.

## How the kanji combine

[仕](../kanji/仕.md) writes し, associated here with doing, and [方](../kanji/方.md) is かた, "way." Compare する → し + 方. The word is not read しほう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

方法 (ほうほう) is a general method or procedure. やり方 (やりかた) is an everyday way of doing something. These overlap; choosing different English glosses does not make them semantic opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

仕方がない (しかたがない) means "it can't be helped" or "there is no choice," not simply "I don't know how." For lack of knowledge, say 仕方が分からない (しかたがわからない). With an ordinary verb, use its ます-stem + 方, as in [泳ぎ方](泳ぎ方.md).
