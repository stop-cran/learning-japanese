# 兎 (うさぎ) - rabbit; hare

## Meaning

兎 means rabbit or hare. It is a noun; the word itself does not distinguish sex or specify that the animal is a pet.

- 庭に白い兎がいます。 (にわにしろいうさぎがいます。) - There is a white rabbit in the garden.
- 子供が兎に餌をあげました。 (こどもがうさぎにえさをあげました。) - The child fed the rabbit.

In the second sentence, に marks the animal receiving the food, and [餌](餌.md) (えさ) is the を-marked object. Use いる for a living rabbit's presence, as in the first sentence.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 兎 writes the native word うさぎ. There is no compound to split. JMdict marks this word as usually written in kana; うさぎ and ウサギ are useful spellings to recognize alongside 兎.

## Synonyms and antonyms

English "rabbit" and "hare" can both translate うさぎ, depending on the animal. [猫](猫.md) (ねこ), "cat," is another animal name, not a synonym or opposite. Animal names do not generally form antonym pairs.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read the three kana syllables as separate kanji components: one character represents the entire word. The spelling changes between a story, a sign, and an animal label need not signal different meanings. For a precise species, a fuller animal name may be necessary.
