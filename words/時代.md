# 時代 (じだい) — era; period

## Meaning

時代 is an era, an age, or a period characterized by a way of life. It can refer to history or a stage of someone's life.

- 学生時代は東京に住んでいました。 (がくせいじだいはとうきょうにすんでいました。) — I lived in Tokyo when I was a student.
- 時代が変わりました。 (じだいがかわりました。) — Times have changed.

## How the kanji combine

[時](../kanji/時.md) (time) and [代](../kanji/代.md) (age; generation) reinforce the idea of a period in time.
Both use on readings: ジ + ダイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時間](時間.md) (じかん) is time or a duration, often measured in hours.
期間 (きかん) is a bounded period or interval. Neither automatically carries 時代's sense of an era or phase of life.
There is no direct antonym; different eras are contrasted by naming them.

## Distinctive meaning

時代 is not a substitute for clock time. 学生時代 (がくせいじだい) means one's student days as a life period,
not the number of hours spent studying.
