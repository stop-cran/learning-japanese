# 通り過ぎる (とおりすぎる) - go past; pass by

## Meaning

通り過ぎる is an intransitive ichidan verb meaning to go past or pass by. A place passed can take **を**: this marks the route or point passed, not an object being changed.

- バスが家の前を通り過ぎました。 (バスがいえのまえをとおりすぎました。) - A bus went past the house.
- 店に気づかず、通り過ぎてしまいました。 (みせにきづかず、とおりすぎてしまいました。) - I passed the shop without noticing it.

Forms include 通り過ぎます, 通り過ぎて, and 通り過ぎない. The second example adds しまう to present an unintended outcome.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) comes from [通る](通る.md) (とおる), "pass; go through"; [過](../kanji/過.md) comes from [過ぎる](過ぎる.md) (すぎる), "go past." 通り + 過ぎる combines passage with continuing beyond a point. Preserve とお, not とう, in the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

通る can describe using a route or going through a place without emphasizing moving beyond it. 通り過ぎる highlights passing the relevant point. Stopping there is a contextual contrast, not a universal antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Passing a shop does not necessarily mean missing it by mistake: that implication comes from 気づかず and しまいました in the example. Nor does this compound merely mean "pass too much"; learn its established movement meaning.
