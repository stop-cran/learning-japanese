# 嫌 (いや) — unpleasant; unwanted

## Meaning

嫌 describes something **unpleasant or unwanted**, or someone's reluctance to accept it. It is a な-adjective: 嫌な音 (いやなおと), “an unpleasant sound,” and 嫌です, “I dislike it / I don't want to.”

- このにおいは嫌です。 (このにおいはいやです。) — I dislike this smell.
- 一人で行くのは嫌です。 (ひとりでいくのはいやです。) — I don't want to go alone.

In the second example, verb + の turns the action into something that can be evaluated.

## How the kanji combine

嫌 is a single kanji read いや. The related word 嫌い (きらい) is also a な-adjective, despite ending in い. Kana いや and katakana イヤ are also used.

## Synonyms and antonyms

嫌い (きらい) expresses dislike, often a general preference. 嫌 is useful for objecting to a particular situation or proposed action. 好き (すき), “liked,” supplies a preference contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

いや！ can be an emotional refusal, “No!” or “Stop!” It is not the neutral polite answer いいえ. Calling something 嫌な can sound strongly negative, so context and tone matter.
