# 軟らかい (やわらかい) - soft; pliant

## Meaning

軟らかい is an i-adjective meaning soft, tender, supple or flexible. It is another spelling of [柔らかい](柔らかい.md), not a separate word with a mechanically different definition.

この土は軟らかいです。 (このつちはやわらかいです。) - This soil is soft.

軟らかい布で拭いてください。 (やわらかいぬのでふいてください。) - Please wipe it with a soft cloth.

Before a noun it attaches directly, as in 軟らかい布. The negative is 軟らかくない (やわらかくない), and the past is 軟らかかった (やわらかかった).

## How the kanji combine

[軟](../kanji/軟.md) supplies やわ, while らかい is okurigana. This is a native adjective, not a two-kanji compound. Its inflectional base includes らか; the written kanji boundary is not the grammatical stem boundary.

## Synonyms and antonyms

柔らかい and 軟らかい share the same unrestricted dictionary family. [ソフト](ソフト.md) overlaps in softness but is a different loanword. [硬い](硬い.md) (かたい), hard, is a useful texture contrast.

[ふかふか](ふかふか.md) emphasizes a soft, fluffy texture, and [優しい](優しい.md) (やさしい) overlaps in some gentle, nonphysical uses. Such overlap does not make every construction interchangeable.

[もちもち](もちもち.md) adds springy elasticity to softness. [もこもこ](もこもこ.md) can describe fluffy bulk, but a lumpy or bulging object need not be soft. Neither word is interchangeable with every use of 軟らかい.

## Distinctive meaning

The family also includes gentle or mild qualities, light or informal topics, and flexible thinking. These are not claims that a soft material is physically light, warm or weak. In the topic sense, English light means non-serious or informal, not illumination.

The inspected entry does not reserve 軟らかい exclusively for physical softness and 柔らかい exclusively for figurative softness. Preferences in a particular phrase require usage evidence, not a rule invented from the two kanji.

軟らか (やわらか) has a separate na-adjective entry: 軟らかな takes な before a noun. That does not license 軟らかいな as the attributive form of this i-adjective.
