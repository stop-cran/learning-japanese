# 坊さん (ぼうさん) - Buddhist priest; monk

## Meaning

坊さん is a familiar, respectful way of referring to a Buddhist priest or monk.
お坊さん (おぼうさん) is another established form. It is not a formal title for
every kind of religious clergyperson.

- お寺で坊さんの話を聞きました。 (おてらでぼうさんのはなしをききました。) - I listened to a Buddhist priest's talk at the temple.
- お坊さんに道を尋ねました。 (おぼうさんにみちをたずねました。) - I asked a Buddhist priest for directions.

## How the kanji combine

坊 contributes ボウ; さん is the familiar honorific suffix, not another kanji.
Learn ぼうさん as a complete expression, and おぼうさん with its added お.

## Synonyms and antonyms

僧侶 (そうりょ) is a more formal Buddhist-clergy term. The English translations
"priest" and "monk" do not mean that the word's usual reference includes every
Christian priest or every person practicing religion.

## Distinctive meaning

坊さん also has a familiar "boy" sense. In that use, compare
[男の子](男の子.md) (おとこのこ); the person need not be a Buddhist monk.
The situation identifies which meaning is intended. Familiar respect does not
guarantee that this is the best address for every ceremonial setting.
