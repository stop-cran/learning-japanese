# 営業 (えいぎょう) - business operations; sales

## Meaning

営業 names business operations and, in a job context, sales. 営業する means to conduct business; 営業している can describe a shop being open for business.

- この店は日曜日も営業しています。 (このみせはにちようびもえいぎょうしています。) - This shop is open on Sundays too.
- 兄は営業の仕事をしています。 (あにはえいぎょうのしごとをしています。) - My older brother works in sales.

On a sign, 営業中 (えいぎょうちゅう) means "open." 営業時間 (えいぎょうじかん) gives business hours.

## How the kanji combine

営 contributes carrying on or running something, and [業](../kanji/業.md) contributes work or business. Together they describe carrying on commercial activity. The on readings are エイ + ギョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[経営](経営.md) (けいえい) emphasizes managing an enterprise. [商売](商売.md) (しょうばい) concerns trade or doing business. 営業 overlaps with these but also has the particular sales-job meaning illustrated above. 休業 (きゅうぎょう), suspension of business or closure for a period, contrasts with operating.

## Distinctive meaning

営業中 says that customers can do business with the establishment; it does not necessarily describe the physical position of its door. In company conversation, 営業 may instead identify a function or department. Use the context to distinguish "business operations" from "sales," rather than translating every occurrence as management.
