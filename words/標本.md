# 標本 (ひょうほん) - specimen; sample; example

## Meaning

標本 is a specimen or sample used for examination or study.
An insect specimen in a collection is a useful example.
The word also means a representative or classic example of a type.

- 博物館で昆虫の標本を見ました。 (はくぶつかんでこんちゅうのひょうほんをみました。) - I saw insect specimens at the museum.
- この標本を顕微鏡で観察します。 (このひょうほんをけんびきょうでかんさつします。) - We will observe this specimen under a microscope.

In statistics, 標本 is a sample selected from a larger population.
That use does not require a preserved object in a display case.

## How the kanji combine

標 contributes a mark or model, and [本](../kanji/本.md) a basis
or reference. Together they suggest something used as a reference
for observation or comparison, not necessarily a book.
The on readings are ヒョウ + ホン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[サンプル](サンプル.md) overlaps in sample or specimen.
[個体](個体.md) (こたい) can identify an individual organism or
specimen; 標本 instead highlights its role as material for study.
[典型](典型.md) (てんけい) overlaps with the representative-example
sense, while [例](例.md) (れい) is a broader example word.

## Distinctive meaning

標本 does not always mean a dead animal or a preserved biological object.
A statistical sample is not guaranteed to represent its population well
merely because it is called 標本.
Likewise, the classic-example sense does not necessarily praise the example
or recommend imitating it.
