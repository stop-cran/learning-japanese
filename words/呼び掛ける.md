# 呼び掛ける (よびかける) - call out to; appeal; urge

## Meaning

呼び掛ける means to call out to or address someone, getting their
attention. It also means to appeal to people or urge their cooperation.
It is an ichidan verb: 呼び掛けます, 呼び掛けた, 呼び掛けない.

- 道の向こうにいる友達に呼び掛けました。 (みちのむこうにいるともだちによびかけました。) - I called out to my friend on the other side of the road.
- 住民に節水を呼び掛けました。 (じゅうみんにせっすいをよびかけました。) - We urged residents to conserve water.

The person addressed can take に. In the appeal construction,
the requested cooperation or action can take を, as 節水 does above.

## How the kanji combine

[呼](../kanji/呼.md)び is the stem of 呼ぶ (よぶ), call.
[掛](../kanji/掛.md)ける joins it in this native compound.
The compound directs a call toward someone; the independent "hang"
meaning of 掛ける is not a literal translation of the whole verb.
呼びかける is another recognized spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話しかける](話しかける.md) (はなしかける) concerns addressing
someone or beginning to talk to them.
[訴える](訴える.md) (うったえる) overlaps in appealing, while
[勧める](勧める.md) (すすめる) can encourage an action.
[呼び出す](呼び出す.md) (よびだす) focuses on summoning or
calling someone up, rather than necessarily appealing for cooperation.

## Distinctive meaning

A call to conserve water does not mean the residents have already
agreed or acted. The appeal can be directed to a group rather than
one nearby listener. Conversely, calling to a friend need not
be a public speech or a request for a campaign.
Do not translate every English "address" with this verb:
an address written on an envelope is not an act of 呼び掛ける.
