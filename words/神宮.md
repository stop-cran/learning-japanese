# 神宮 (じんぐう) — imperial Shinto shrine

## Meaning

神宮 is a title borne by certain high-ranking Shinto shrines connected with the imperial family. It occurs at the end of shrine names. By itself, 神宮 can also refer specifically to the Ise Grand Shrine.

- 東京で明治神宮を訪れた。 (とうきょうでめいじじんぐうをおとずれた。) — In Tokyo I visited Meiji Shrine.
- 伊勢神宮までの道を教えてください。 (いせじんぐうまでのみちをおしえてください。) — Please tell me the way to the Ise Grand Shrine.

## How the kanji combine

[神](../kanji/神.md) (じん) refers to a deity; [宮](../kanji/宮.md) is ぐう here, in its shrine sense. The English names “Meiji Shrine” and “Ise Grand Shrine” need not translate the two characters separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[神社](神社.md) (じんじゃ) is the general word for a Shinto shrine. A 神宮 is a 神社, but not every 神社 bears the title 神宮. [寺](寺.md) (てら) means a Buddhist temple.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the name before 神宮. 明治神宮 (めいじじんぐう) and 伊勢神宮 (いせじんぐう) are different places, even though both end in じんぐう.
