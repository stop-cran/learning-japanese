# 店 (みせ) — shop, store

## Meaning

店 is a shop, restaurant or store: 店に入る (enter a shop), 店を開ける あける (open a shop), 店を出る でる, 駅前の店 (a shop in front of the station). With お it is more polite: お店 おみせ.

## How the kanji combine

[店](../kanji/店.md) alone with the kun reading みせ. Sino-Japanese compounds such as 書店 use テン; native compounds such as 店先 みせさき use みせ: [書店](書店.md) しょてん, 店員 てんいん, 本店 ほんてん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 商店 しょうてん (a shop, formal), ショップ (loan word), 〜屋 や (the specific shop, as in 本屋 ほんや).
- Related: 店員 てんいん (shop clerk), 店長 てんちょう (shop manager).

## Distinctive meaning

店 includes restaurants and cafés as well as stores: 店で食べる (eat at the place). The suffix ～店 てん is used in names of stores: 本店, 支店.
