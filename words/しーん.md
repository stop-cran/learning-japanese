# しーん — complete hushed silence

## Meaning

A striking absence of sound, as when a room falls completely quiet.

- みんなが帰ると、部屋はしーんとした。 — After everyone left, the room fell completely silent.
- 質問（しつもん）しても、教室はしーんとしていた。 — Even after I asked a question, the classroom remained silent.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[静か](静か.md)（しずか）is a broader neutral word for quietness. [ざわざわ](ざわざわ.md) and [がやがや](がやがや.md) can provide a noisy-crowd contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn しーんとする and しーんとしている, with と. しいん is also recorded. This mimetic evokes silence rather than imitating an actual sound; it is not the loanword シーン meaning a scene.
