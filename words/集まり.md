# 集まり (あつまり) - gathering; collection

## Meaning

集まり is a noun for people or things gathered together: a gathering, meeting, or collection. An event takes に with an attendance verb: 集まりに参加する (あつまりにさんかする), "attend a gathering." It can also describe how well people turn up.

- 明日は家族の集まりがあります。 (あしたはかぞくのあつまりがあります。) - There is a family gathering tomorrow.
- 今日は人の集まりが悪いです。 (きょうはひとのあつまりがわるいです。) - Not many people have turned up today.

The second sentence evaluates attendance, not the character of the people present.

## How the kanji combine

[集](../kanji/集.md) contributes "gather." 集まり is the noun form associated with [集まる](集まる.md) (あつまる), "gather; come together"; まり is okurigana, not a second word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) foregrounds a meeting for discussion. 集まり can be a social gathering without that purpose. [会合](会合.md) (かいごう) also names a meeting or gathering.

## Distinctive meaning

集まり names the gathering or resulting group, rather than the action expressed by 集まる. 人が集まる means "people gather"; 人の集まり can mean "a group of people." The noun can refer to objects too, so do not make "organized human meeting" its entire definition.
