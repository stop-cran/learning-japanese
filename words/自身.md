# 自身 (じしん) - oneself; self

## Meaning

自身 is a noun or suffix meaning "(one's) self." It can follow a person expression to emphasize that person: 私自身 (わたしじしん), "I myself," or 彼自身 (かれじしん), "he himself."

- 私自身もその会議に出ました。 (わたしじしんもそのかいぎにでました。) - I myself attended that meeting too.
- 彼自身の考えを聞きたいです。 (かれじしんのかんがえをききたいです。) - I want to hear his own ideas.

Particles follow the complete expression: 私自身が identifies the subject, and 私自身の identifies something as my own.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) means self, and 身 contributes the person or body. They reinforce reference to the person themselves. The reading is ジ + シン, not the native reading み for 身.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[自分](自分.md) (じぶん) also refers to oneself; 自分自身 (じぶんじしん) makes that reference emphatic. [本人](本人.md) (ほんにん) is the person concerned. [一人](一人.md) (ひとり), especially 一人で, can express being alone or acting without another person.

## Distinctive meaning

Bare 自身 is not an adverb meaning "without help." In 私自身で確認します (わたしじしんでかくにんします), "I will check personally," the construction includes で and emphasizes personal involvement. It does not by itself prove that nobody assists. The source's gloss "by oneself, personally" must therefore be understood through an actual construction, not substituted for the noun in every sentence.
