# 喫茶店 (きっさてん) — coffee shop; tearoom

## Meaning

喫茶店 is a coffee shop or tearoom: a place to sit and have coffee, tea, and often a light meal.

- 駅の近くの喫茶店で会いましょう。 (えきのちかくのきっさてんであいましょう。) — Let's meet at the coffee shop near the station.

Use で for an activity there, as above, and に for the destination in 喫茶店に入る (きっさてんにはいる), "go into a coffee shop."

## How the kanji combine

喫 concerns consuming, [茶](../kanji/茶.md) means tea, and [店](../kanji/店.md) means shop. Learn the compound reading きっさてん as a whole: 茶 is さ here, and the small っ closes the preceding syllable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

カフェ overlaps with 喫茶店. レストラン focuses on a place serving meals; a 喫茶店 may serve food without being described primarily as a restaurant.

## Distinctive meaning

喫茶店 can evoke a traditional Japanese coffee shop, but the name alone does not guarantee a particular menu or decor. Despite the character 茶, coffee is entirely normal there.
