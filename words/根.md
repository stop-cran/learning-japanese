# 根 (ね) - root; underlying source

## Meaning

根 is a noun for a plant's root. It also describes roots of teeth or hair, an underlying source or cause, and someone's basic nature. Use の to identify what the root belongs to: 木の根 (きのね), "a tree's roots."

- この木は深く根を張っています。 (このきはふかくねをはっています。) - This tree has put down deep roots.
- 彼は無口ですが、根は優しい人です。 (かれはむくちですが、ねはやさしいひとです。) - He is quiet, but he is kind at heart.

In the second example, 根は concerns the person's underlying nature, not an anatomical part.

## How the kanji combine

根 is one character with the native reading ね. It names the whole root. Compounds may use a different reading, as in 根本 (こんぽん), "root/foundation"; do not transfer こん to the standalone examples here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[原因](原因.md) (げんいん) explicitly names a cause. 根 can present a cause figuratively as something deep beneath the visible problem. Leaves and branches are contrasting plant parts, not lexical opposites of "root."

## Distinctive meaning

The image extends from what anchors and nourishes a plant to what underlies a situation or character. 歯の根 (はのね), "a tooth's root," is anatomical; 問題の根 (もんだいのね), "the root of the problem," is figurative. Context determines the relevant layer.
