# 切る (きる) — cut

## Meaning

切る means to cut something. It also describes ending a connection, such as hanging up a phone.

- 紙を切ります。（かみを きります。）— I cut the paper.
- 電話を切りました。（でんわを きりました。）— I hung up the phone.

The affected object takes を. This is a godan verb: 切ります きります, 切って きって, 切らない きらない.

## How the kanji combine

This is one native verb, not a compound. [切](../kanji/切.md) supplies the cutting sense and the reading き; る is okurigana, the kana ending that changes in conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 切れる きれる describes something snapping, being cut, or running out. It often presents the event without naming someone who causes it; it is not an antonym.
- There is no one opposite for all uses. For a switch, 切る means "turn off" and 入れる いれる means "turn on."
- [着る](着る.md) きる, "put on clothing," is a different verb, not a synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not conjugate 切る like 着る: "cut" is 切ります きります, while "put on" is 着ます きます. Learn nonliteral combinations such as 電話を切る as phrases rather than assuming that an object is physically cut.
