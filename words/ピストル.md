# ピストル - pistol

## Meaning

ピストル is a pistol, a type of handgun. It is a noun.

- 博物館で古いピストルを見ました。 (はくぶつかんでふるいピストルをみました。) - I saw an old pistol at the museum.
- 新聞の記事にピストルという言葉が出てきました。 (しんぶんのきじにピストルということばがでてきました。) - The word "pistol" appeared in the newspaper article.

These sentences identify an object or a word; they do not describe
instructions for using a firearm.

## How the kanji combine

精選版 日本国語大辞典 traces this katakana loanword to Dutch pistool.
There are no kanji components in its spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

拳銃 (けんじゅう) is the corresponding handgun term.
[銃](銃.md) (じゅう) and [鉄砲](鉄砲.md) (てっぽう)
can name firearms more generally, not just pistols.
There is no useful lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every gun as ピストル: a rifle is not a pistol
merely because both are firearms. The surrounding report or description
must establish what object is meant; the word alone does not establish
ownership, legality, or how it was used.
