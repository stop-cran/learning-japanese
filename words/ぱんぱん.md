# ぱんぱん — full to bursting; sharp claps

## Meaning

A bulging, tightly filled state, or repeated sharp clap-like sounds.

- 本を入れすぎて、かばんがぱんぱんだ。 — I put in too many books and the bag is bulging.
- 両手（りょうて）をぱんぱんとたたいた。 — I clapped my hands sharply.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぎゅうぎゅう](ぎゅうぎゅう.md) overlaps in tight packing. [ぱちぱち](ぱちぱち.md) also describes clapping, without the same bulging-state use.

## Distinctive meaning

ぱんぱんのかばん and 手をぱんぱんたたく need different English words. “Full to bursting” describes an impression of fullness, not a prediction that the bag must break. The sound use does not require an actual explosion.
