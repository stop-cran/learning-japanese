# 重たい (おもたい) - heavy; oppressive

## Meaning

重たい describes physical heaviness. It can also describe a heavy, gloomy, or serious feeling or atmosphere.

- この荷物は重たいです。 (このにもつはおもたいです。) - This luggage is heavy.
- 会議は重たい雰囲気で始まりました。 (かいぎはおもたいふんいきではじまりました。) - The meeting began in a heavy atmosphere.

It is an i-adjective: 重たい荷物 (おもたいにもつ), "heavy luggage"; 重たくない (おもたくない), "not heavy." No な is needed before a noun.

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) supplies the native おも element; たい is part of this adjective's written ending. This is not a verb followed by the desire ending ～たい: 重たい does not mean "want to be heavy."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[重い](重い.md) (おもい) overlaps in heaviness. [暗い](暗い.md) (くらい) can overlap in a gloomy mood, but its literal darkness sense concerns light. [軽い](軽い.md) (かるい), "light," contrasts with physical weight.

## Distinctive meaning

重たい雰囲気 is not a measurement of air's mass. Conversely, a heavy bag need not be emotionally depressing. Do not turn the overlap with 重い into a universal substitution rule or claim that one word is always objective and the other always subjective.
