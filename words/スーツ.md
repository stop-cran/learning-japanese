# スーツ (スーツ) — suit

## Meaning

スーツ is a suit of clothes, usually a matching jacket with trousers or a skirt. The word can describe clothing for people of any gender.

面接にはスーツを着て行きます。 (めんせつにはスーツをきていきます。) — I will wear a suit to the interview.

新しいスーツを買いました。 (あたらしいスーツをかいました。) — I bought a new suit.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana. The ー makes スー long, and the final ツ belongs to the Japanese word. Do not remove it to make a singular: スーツ can mean one suit or several.

## Synonyms and antonyms

背広 (せびろ) traditionally refers to a man's business suit or suit jacket. 洋服 (ようふく) is Western-style clothing more broadly. 制服 (せいふく) is a uniform and identifies a social function, not necessarily a particular suit design.

## Distinctive meaning

着る (きる) is the useful general verb for wearing a suit: スーツを着る. The noun does not by itself mean formal ceremonial dress, and not every workplace requires one. A single suit can be described as スーツ一着 (スーツいっちゃく), using the clothing counter 着 (ちゃく).
