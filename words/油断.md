# 油断 (ゆだん) - carelessness; letting one's guard down

## Meaning

油断 is a lapse of attention or preparedness: carelessness,
inattention or letting one's guard down.
油断する is an intransitive する verb.

- 慣れた道でも油断しないでください。 (なれたみちでもゆだんしないでください。) - Please do not let your guard down, even on a familiar road.
- 油断して、大事な説明を聞き逃しました。 (ゆだんして、だいじなせつめいをききのがしました。) - I was inattentive and missed an important explanation.

油断は禁物だ (ゆだんはきんもつだ) means "you must not let your guard down."

## How the kanji combine

[油](../kanji/油.md) has the on reading ユ, and
[断](../kanji/断.md) ダン. These give ゆだん.
Do not derive the modern meaning mechanically as "cutting off oil."
The historical explanations are disputed; recognizing the written
parts is not proof of one origin story.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[うっかり](うっかり.md) describes an inadvertent act through inattention.
[ぼんやり](ぼんやり.md) can describe an absentminded state.
[過失](過失.md) (かしつ) includes negligence or fault, not only a
moment of relaxed vigilance.
[用心](用心.md) (ようじん) and [注意](注意.md) (ちゅうい)
provide caution/attention contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

油断 evaluates a failure to stay alert or prepared; it does not
necessarily describe a generally careless personality.
It also need not result in an accident.
Do not confuse the negative 油断しない, "do not let your guard down,"
with an instruction not to relax under any circumstances.
