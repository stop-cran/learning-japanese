# 交際 (こうさい) - social relations; dating

## Meaning

交際 is a noun and intransitive する verb for association with other people. It can mean general social relations or a romantic relationship. Use 人と交際する: the other person takes と.

- 仕事を通じて交際の範囲が広がりました。 (しごとをつうじてこうさいのはんいがひろがりました。) - Through work, my circle of acquaintances grew.
- 二人は三年前から交際しています。 (ふたりはさんねんまえからこうさいしています。) - The two have been dating for three years.

The second example's 交際している presents an ongoing relationship, not a single outing. The first example does not imply that all the acquaintances are romantic partners.

## How the kanji combine

[交](../kanji/交.md) contributes mingling or exchanging, and [際](../kanji/際.md) contributes contact or a meeting point. Together they evoke contact between people, giving the established sense of social association.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[付き合い](付き合い.md) (つきあい) overlaps with social relationships and association. [デート](デート.md) focuses on a date or outing; 交際 can name the continuing relationship. [別れる](別れる.md) (わかれる), "break up," contrasts with romantic involvement, not every kind of social contact.

## Distinctive meaning

交際 can sound more matter-of-fact or report-like than everyday 付き合っている when discussing a couple. It does not inherently mean marriage or engagement. In business contexts, 交際費 (こうさいひ) refers to expenses associated with maintaining social or business relationships, not specifically the cost of romantic dates.
