# 近く (ちかく) — nearby; vicinity

## Meaning

近く means "nearby" or "the vicinity". Use place + の近く for "near a place". The rest of the sentence determines whether the location takes に, で, or another particle.

- 駅の近くに住んでいます。  
  えきのちかくにすんでいます。  
  "I live near the station."
- 近くの店でパンを買いました。  
  ちかくのみせでパンをかいました。  
  "I bought bread at a nearby shop."

## How the kanji combine

[近](../kanji/近.md) contributes nearness. The form ちかく is related to the い-adjective 近い (ちかい), with い changing to く. 近く also functions as a location noun, as in the examples above.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そば means "beside; near" and often suggests close proximity. 遠く (とおく), "far away; a distant place", is the useful spatial contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 近くの店 for "a nearby shop", not 近くな店. Outside the location sense, 近く can mean "nearly" after a quantity or "soon"; those uses require their own context.
