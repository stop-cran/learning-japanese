# 要る (いる) — to need

## Meaning

要る means "to be needed" or "to need." The necessary thing normally takes が. It is a godan verb: 要ります (いります), 要らない (いらない), 要った (いった).

この仕事には時間が要ります。 (このしごとにはじかんがいります。) — This job requires time.

袋は要りません。 (ふくろはいりません。) — I don't need a bag.

## How the kanji combine

[要](../kanji/要.md) expresses necessity, with る as okurigana. The word is also commonly written entirely in kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

必要 (ひつよう) is "necessary": 時間が必要です (じかんがひつようです). 欲しい (ほしい), "want," emphasizes desire rather than a requirement.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 要る with 居る (いる), "to be present" for people and animals. Their negative forms distinguish them: 要らない means "not need"; いない means "not be there." The final る does not make 要る an ichidan verb.
