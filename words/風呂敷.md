# 風呂敷 (ふろしき) - furoshiki; wrapping cloth

## Meaning

風呂敷 is a wrapping cloth used to wrap or carry things.
It is a noun naming the cloth, not the resulting parcel.
Use で for the wrapping material.

- 本を風呂敷で包みました。 (ほんをふろしきでつつみました。) - I wrapped the books in a furoshiki.
- 旅行の前に風呂敷を一枚買いました。 (りょこうのまえにふろしきをいちまいかいました。) - I bought one wrapping cloth before the trip.

一枚 (いちまい) counts one sheet-like cloth.

## How the kanji combine

[風呂](風呂.md) is the complete bath word ふろ, written with
[風](../kanji/風.md) and 呂. 敷 contributes しき, related to
spreading or laying something out.
Learn ふろ + しき as units, not "wind + spine" as a definition.

The wago label groups the word with this native-word construction.
It does not settle the disputed historical origin of 風呂 or claim that
each character supplies a separate productive native reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[布](布.md) (ぬの) and [生地](生地.md) (きじ) are broader cloth or fabric words.
[包み](包み.md) (つつみ) is a wrapped package;
[包む](包む.md) (つつむ) is the action of wrapping.
A cloth and the parcel made with it are not the same object.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not specify a fixed size, pattern, or fiber.
It can be reused for carrying different objects; it is not restricted
to bath goods in modern use. Recognize the whole reading ふろしき.
