# 割り算 (わりざん) - division (mathematics)

## Meaning

割り算 is mathematical division. It is a noun and can take する.
The source spelling 割算 and the alternate わり算 represent the same
word, also read わりざん.

- 今日は割り算を習いました。 (きょうはわりざんをならいました。) - Today I learned division.
- この問題は割り算で解けます。 (このもんだいはわりざんでとけます。) - You can solve this problem by division.

For an equation, 六割る二は三 (ろくわるにはさん) means "six divided by two is three."
In 六を二で割る, を marks the number divided and で the divisor.

## How the kanji combine

[割](../kanji/割.md), through the native stem わり, contributes dividing.
[算](../kanji/算.md) contributes calculation with the on reading サン,
voiced to ざん. This is a native-first mixed compound, わり + サン,
not two on readings. The app's `wago` grouping follows
[引き算](引き算.md) and [掛け算](掛け算.md) without claiming both elements are native.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[割る](割る.md) (わる) is the verb "divide" in this arithmetic construction.
[計算](計算.md) (けいさん) is calculation more broadly;
[算数](算数.md) (さんすう) is elementary mathematics/arithmetic.
[掛け算](掛け算.md) (かけざん), multiplication, is a contrasting operation.

## Distinctive meaning

This word does not mean physically breaking a plate or every organizational
division. A division can leave a remainder; dividing a whole number by another
does not promise a whole-number result. Division by zero is not defined in
ordinary arithmetic, so multiplication and division are not unrestricted undo buttons.
