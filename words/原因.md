# 原因 (げんいん) — cause

## Meaning

原因 is the cause or source of an event or condition: what brought it about.
Use の to identify the event whose cause you mean: 事故の原因 (じこのげんいん), "the cause of the accident."

- 事故の原因を調べています。 (じこのげんいんをしらべています。) — We are investigating the cause of the accident.

The investigation example does not yet identify the cause. 原因 can refer to something still unknown.

## How the kanji combine

[原](../kanji/原.md) contributes an original source, and [因](../kanji/因.md) contributes a cause or causal factor.
The related meanings point backward from an outcome to what produced it.
The on readings are ゲン + イン: keep the ん before い in げんいん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

理由 (りゆう), "reason," can explain a decision or give grounds for it. 原因 more directly asks what
produced the event or state. The words can overlap, so this is not a rule dividing all explanations into two fixed categories.

結果 (けっか), "result," is the other side of a cause-and-result relationship, not simply a synonym with opposite emotional tone.

## Distinctive meaning

原因 concerns causation, not necessarily blame. A cause might be weather, a mechanical condition, or an action.
The word alone neither identifies a responsible person nor proves that the proposed explanation is correct.
