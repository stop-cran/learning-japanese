# 論じる (ろんじる) - discuss; argue

## Meaning

論じる means to discuss or deal with a topic, or to argue a point. It is a transitive ichidan verb: 論じます, 論じて, 論じた. Mark the topic with を; について can also introduce what is being discussed.

- この本は教育の問題を論じています。 (このほんはきょういくのもんだいをろんじています。) - This book discusses problems in education.
- 委員たちは、その案の是非を論じました。 (いいんたちは、そのあんのぜひをろんじました。) - The committee members debated the merits of that proposal.

The first example describes the content of a book. A human speaker is not required as the grammatical subject.

## How the kanji combine

[論](../kanji/論.md) contributes argument or discussion and is read ロン. The ending じる turns this Sino-Japanese element into a conjugating verb. The related form 論ずる (ろんずる) has the same core discussion meaning but a different dictionary form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話し合う](話し合う.md) (はなしあう) emphasizes talking together. 論じる can instead describe one author's treatment of a subject. [議論](議論.md)する (ぎろんする) overlaps in reasoned discussion or debate.

## Distinctive meaning

English "argue" can mean quarreling, but 論じる need not imply anger or personal conflict. It focuses on treating an issue through statements or reasoning. Merely mentioning a topic, as with [触れる](触れる.md) (ふれる), does not necessarily amount to discussing it at length. There is no general opposite verb.
