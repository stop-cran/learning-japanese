# お待たせしました (おまたせしました) - thank you for waiting; sorry to have kept you waiting

## Meaning

お待たせしました acknowledges that the listener has had to wait. Depending on the situation, English can use "Thank you for waiting" or "Sorry to have kept you waiting."

- お待たせしました。こちらがご注文の品です。 (おまたせしました。こちらがごちゅうもんのしなです。) - Thank you for waiting. Here is your order.
- お待たせしました。では、始めましょう。 (おまたせしました。では、はじめましょう。) - Sorry to keep you waiting. Let's begin.

The expression is polite. It can introduce the next action after the wait; it need not be a dramatic apology for a serious mistake.

## How the kanji combine

[待](../kanji/待.md) appears in [待つ](待つ.md) (まつ), "wait." 待たせる (またせる) is its causative, "make someone wait"; お待たせしました is the complete polite formula. Learn またせ rather than reading the kanji with its on reading タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お待ちどおさま](お待ちどおさま.md) also acknowledges having kept someone waiting. [すみません](すみません.md) is a broader apology or acknowledgment of trouble; [ありがとう](ありがとう.md) is general thanks.

## Distinctive meaning

This addresses someone who has waited. It is not the instruction "please wait": that asks the listener to continue waiting, whereas お待たせしました acknowledges the wait already caused. Its thanks translation does not make it interchangeable with ありがとう for every favor.
