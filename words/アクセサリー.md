# アクセサリー (アクセサリー) — accessory; jewelry

## Meaning

アクセサリー is an accessory, especially an item of personal adornment such as a necklace or earrings. It also occurs for accessories used with equipment, cars, or cameras.

旅行のお土産にアクセサリーを買いました。 (りょこうのおみやげにアクセサリーをかいました。) — I bought some jewelry as a souvenir of the trip.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana, not kanji. The final ー lengthens リ to リー. アクセサリ is another dictionary form, but this card uses アクセサリー consistently.

## Synonyms and antonyms

宝石 (ほうせき) means a jewel or precious stone, so it does not cover all accessories. ネックレス is specifically a necklace. 飾り (かざり) is a broader decoration or ornament.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not imply precious metal or an expensive gemstone: inexpensive decorative items can also be アクセサリー. For equipment, name the relevant item or field, as in カメラのアクセサリー, "camera accessories." Context determines whether English "jewelry" or the broader "accessories" is a better translation. Japanese does not add an English plural ending.
