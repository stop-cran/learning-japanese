# 鉄砲 (てっぽう) - gun; firearm

## Meaning

鉄砲 is a gun or firearm. It often occurs in historical descriptions and ordinary expressions about guns; it is not limited to one modern firearm model.

- 博物館で昔の鉄砲を見ました。 (はくぶつかんでむかしのてっぽうをみました。) - I saw old firearms at the museum.
- 展示された鉄砲には説明が添えてありました。 (てんじされたてっぽうにはせつめいがそえてありました。) - An explanation accompanied the displayed gun.

## How the kanji combine

[鉄](../kanji/鉄.md) supplies iron/metal, and 砲 names artillery or a gun. The on readings テツ + ホウ undergo compound sound changes to give てっぽう. Do not invent separate basic readings テッ and ポウ from this one word. 鉄炮 is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[銃](銃.md) is another word for a gun. [武器](武器.md) is the broader category "weapon" and is not restricted to firearms. Historical and contemporary contexts may favor different gun terms; this article does not claim a universal interchangeability rule.

## Distinctive meaning

The complete dictionary family also records a sumo training pole and a colloquial name for pufferfish. These are separate specialized senses, not evidence that any pole or fish is a firearm. The main learner sense and examples here concern guns; historical dictionaries may list still more specialized uses.
