# 育てる (そだてる) — raise or nurture

## Meaning

育てる means to raise, bring up, or nurture. It can describe caring for a child, growing plants, or developing someone's abilities. It is transitive: what is raised takes を.

祖母は三人の子供を育てました。（そぼはさんにんのこどもをそだてました。） — My grandmother raised three children.

庭で野菜を育てています。（にわでやさいをそだてています。） — I am growing vegetables in the garden.

## How the kanji combine

[育](../kanji/育.md) supplies the idea of growth and care. This is an ichidan verb: 育てます, 育てて, 育てない. Its conjugation base is 育て（そだて）.

## Synonyms and antonyms

育つ（そだつ） is intransitive: 子供が育つ, "a child grows up." 教える（おしえる） means to teach, which can be part of raising or training someone.

## Distinctive meaning

育てる focuses on supporting development over time. It does not mean simply lifting someone up physically or feeding them once.
