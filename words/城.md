# 城 (しろ) - castle

## Meaning

城 is a noun meaning castle. Useful patterns include **城を見学する** (しろをけんがくする),
"tour a castle," **城に住む** (しろにすむ), "live in a castle," and **城の周り**
(しろのまわり), "around the castle."

- 旅行中に古い城を見学しました。 (りょこうちゅうにふるいしろをけんがくしました。) - I toured an old castle during my trip.
- 城の周りには大きな堀があります。 (しろのまわりにはおおきなほりがあります。) - There is a large moat around the castle.

The second example describes one castle's surroundings; a moat is not part of the definition of every 城.
お城 (おしろ) is the same noun with the prefix お.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 城 writes the native noun しろ.
There is no multi-kanji relation to divide. In castle names the character can instead be read じょう,
as in 大阪城 (おおさかじょう), Osaka Castle.
Learn the complete name rather than substituting しろ mechanically.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[建物](建物.md) (たてもの) is the broader category "building."
[塔](塔.md) (とう) means tower and may identify one structure within a castle.
宮殿 (きゅうでん), "palace," emphasizes a ruler's residence; the words are not interchangeable labels for every grand building.

## Distinctive meaning

城 can refer to the castle as a site or complex, not just the tallest tower visible in a photograph.
Context tells you whether a visitor means a particular structure or the grounds.
Its reading しろ also matches [白](白.md), "white," but the words are unrelated in meaning.
