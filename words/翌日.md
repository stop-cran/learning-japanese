# 翌日 (よくじつ) - following day

## Meaning

翌日 is the day after a reference day: the following day or next day.
That day can be in a past story or a future plan. The reference point is
not necessarily the day on which the speaker is talking.

- 京都に着いた翌日は雨でした。
  (きょうとについたよくじつはあめでした。) - The day after I arrived in Kyoto was rainy.
- 会議の翌日に報告書を送りました。
  (かいぎのよくじつにほうこくしょをおくりました。) - I sent the report on the day after the meeting.

An event can identify the reference point with の, as in 会議の翌日.
A preceding clause can also identify it, as in 京都に着いた翌日.

## How the kanji combine

[翌](../kanji/翌.md) contributes following in time and
[日](../kanji/日.md) means day. The first character specifies which day:
the one following the reference day. The on readings ヨク + ジツ form よくじつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[明日](明日.md) (あした) is tomorrow, usually relative to the speaking
day. 翌日 and 明日 can identify the same date when the reference day is
today, but they are not interchangeable throughout a story about the past.
翌年 (よくねん or よくとし) applies the following-period idea to a year.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not calculate 翌日 from today's date unless the context supplies today
as the reference. In the first example, arrival in Kyoto establishes that
reference, even if the arrival happened long before the present conversation.
