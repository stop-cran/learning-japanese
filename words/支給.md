# 支給 (しきゅう) - provision; payment of an allowance

## Meaning

支給 names providing money or supplies, typically by an organization to eligible recipients. It is a noun and a transitive する-verb. Use recipient に + money or goods を + 支給する.

- 会社は社員に交通費を支給します。 (かいしゃはしゃいんにこうつうひをしきゅうします。) - The company pays employees a travel allowance.
- 制服は学校から支給されます。 (せいふくはがっこうからしきゅうされます。) - Uniforms are provided by the school.

The passive 支給される focuses on what is provided or received. The second example also shows that 支給 is not limited to cash.

## How the kanji combine

[支](../kanji/支.md) contributes supporting or supplying; [給](../kanji/給.md) contributes giving or providing. Together they concern furnishing what a recipient is to receive. Their on readings are シ + キュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[払う](払う.md) (はらう) is a broader verb for paying, including a customer's bill. [給料](給料.md) (きゅうりょう) is pay for work, one kind of money that can be 支給される. 配る (くばる) focuses on distributing items.

## Distinctive meaning

支給 identifies the provision, not automatically a gift, loan, or reimbursement in every context. The accompanying noun and arrangement establish those details. A shop customer's payment and an employer's allowance involve different roles, even though both can be translated using English "pay."
