# 暖まる (あたたまる) - get warm; warm oneself

## Meaning

暖まる means to become warm or warm oneself. It is an **intransitive godan verb**:
the person, room or other thing becoming warm takes が.

- 暖房をつけると、部屋が暖まります。 (だんぼうをつけると、へやがあたたまります。) - The room warms up when you turn on the heating.
- 火のそばで体が暖まりました。 (ひのそばでからだがあたたまりました。) - My body warmed up beside the fire.

Forms include 暖まります (あたたまります), 暖まって (あたたまって),
and 暖まった (あたたまった). Do not treat it as an ichidan verb just because it ends in る.

## How the kanji combine

暖 contributes warmth; まる completes the native verb. The dictionary also records
温まる with [温](../kanji/温.md), read あたたまる. Learn the spelling with its context:
the two characters do not create different pronunciations.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暖める](暖める.md) (あたためる) is transitive: 部屋を暖める (へやをあたためる),
"warm the room," versus 部屋が暖まる, "the room gets warm."
[冷える](冷える.md) (ひえる), "grow cold," supplies a useful opposite.
[暖かい](暖かい.md) (あたたかい) describes a warm state rather than the change into it.

## Distinctive meaning

An English sentence such as "I warmed myself" can sound transitive, but the Japanese
pattern here presents becoming warm. Use 体が暖まる for the body warming up,
not 体を暖まる.
