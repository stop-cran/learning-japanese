# 速度 (そくど) - speed; rate

## Meaning

速度 is a noun for speed, pace, or the rate at which something moves or proceeds.
Useful expressions include **速度を上げる** (そくどをあげる), "increase speed,"
and **速度を落とす** (そくどをおとす), "reduce speed."

- カーブの前で車の速度を落としました。 (カーブのまえでくるまのそくどをおとしました。) - I slowed the car before the bend.
- 動画の再生速度を変えられます。 (どうがのさいせいそくどをかえられます。) - You can change the video's playback speed.

再生速度 names how fast recorded material plays. The word is therefore not limited to vehicles
traveling a distance.

## How the kanji combine

[速](../kanji/速.md) contributes speed or quickness; [度](../kanji/度.md) contributes degree or measure.
Together they name a measure of how fast something happens.
The on readings ソク + ド give そくど.

## Synonyms and antonyms

スピード overlaps with speed.
[速い](速い.md) (はやい) is an adjective, while 速度 is a noun: 速度が速い means "the speed is high."
[遅い](遅い.md) (おそい), "slow," contrasts with 速い, not with the existence of a speed itself.

## Distinctive meaning

速度 can be specified numerically or discussed comparatively.
車の速度 and 読む速度 (よむそくど), "reading speed," measure different activities but share the idea of rate.
Do not translate every English "rate" as 速度: an interest rate or percentage is not necessarily a speed.
Identify what is changing or progressing over time.
