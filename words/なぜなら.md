# なぜなら (なぜなら) - the reason is; because

## Meaning

なぜなら is a conjunction introducing the reason for a preceding statement. A useful full pattern is **statement. なぜなら + reason + からです**. Before から, verbs and i-adjectives use a plain form; a present affirmative noun or na-adjective takes だ.

- 今日は出かけません。なぜなら、雨が降っているからです。 (きょうはでかけません。なぜなら、あめがふっているからです。) - I am not going out today. The reason is that it is raining.
- この店が好きです。なぜなら、静かだからです。 (このみせがすきです。なぜなら、しずかだからです。) - I like this shop because it is quiet.

静か is a na-adjective, so the reason is 静かだから, not 静かなから.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is kana; 何故なら is also recorded. Recognize [なぜ](なぜ.md), "why," in the expression, but learn なぜなら as a conjunction rather than translating its parts independently.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[だから](だから.md), "therefore; so," introduces a consequence. なぜなら moves in the opposite explanatory direction: it supplies the reason for what was just stated.

## Distinctive meaning

なぜなら is not simply a particle attached to the end of any clause. It introduces an explanation, often with an explicit reason ending such as からです. In a shorter sentence, 雨だから出かけません already means "I won't go out because it is raining"; なぜなら is unnecessary there.
