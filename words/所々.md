# 所々 (ところどころ) - here and there; in places

## Meaning

所々 means here and there or in places: different points within an area or sequence.
It can be used adverbially or as a noun with a particle such as に.
It need not refer to distant towns.

- 道の所々に雪が残っています。 (みちのところどころにゆきがのこっています。) - Snow remains here and there along the road.
- この本は所々読みにくいです。 (このほんはところどころよみにくいです。) - This book is difficult to read in places.

The second example concerns parts of a text, not its location on a shelf.

## How the kanji combine

[所](../kanji/所.md) contributes place; 々 repeats the character.
The repeated native form is ところどころ, with ど in the second half.
所所 and kana ところどころ are also dictionary spellings.
The iteration mark is not a separate kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あちこち](あちこち.md) also means here and there.
The place word [方々](方々.md) is read ほうぼう; its separate
かたがた reading refers respectfully to people.
[点々](点々.md) (てんてん) overlaps with things scattered at different points.
一か所 (いっかしょ), one place, supplies a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

所々 distributes the relevant condition rather than saying it holds everywhere.
It does not specify a fixed number of places or require equal spacing.
[各地](各地.md) (かくち) is useful for places across an area;
the overlap does not make it fit every reference to portions of a book.
