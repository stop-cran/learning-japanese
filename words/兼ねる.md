# 兼ねる (かねる) - combine roles; cannot readily do

## Meaning

兼ねる is an ichidan verb with a combined-functions use and a concurrent-posts use. As a suffix after another verb's masu stem, ～かねる means to be unable or find it difficult to do something.

- この部屋は事務所と寝室を兼ねています。 (このへやはじむしょとしんしつをかねています。) - This room serves as both an office and a bedroom.
- 彼は社長と会長を兼ねています。 (かれはしゃちょうとかいちょうをかねています。) - He holds both the president's and the chairperson's positions.
- その条件には同意しかねます。 (そのじょうけんにはどういしかねます。) - I cannot agree to those terms.

In the last example, 同意する becomes 同意し + かねる. Similarly, [答える](答える.md) (こたえる) gives 答えかねる, find it difficult to answer. Attach to the masu stem, not to the dictionary form or the て-form.

## How the kanji combine

兼 concerns combining or serving in more than one capacity; ねる completes the native verb. The ordinary combined-role verb is written 兼ねる. The difficulty suffix is usually written in kana as ～かねる; its meaning is not obtained by translating the kanji literally.

## Synonyms and antonyms

兼任する (けんにんする) describes holding concurrent posts. [勤める](勤める.md) (つとめる) means to work for or be employed, not specifically to combine positions. ～できない also expresses inability, but ～かねる is useful for difficulty or reluctance in formal responses; it is not a universal replacement in every inability sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

The suffix is negative in meaning even without ない. Conversely, ～かねない expresses possibility: 事故につながりかねない (じこにつながりかねない), "might lead to an accident." Do not treat it as a simple positive "can do" counterpart. The combined-role verb and the difficulty suffix need separate constructions.
