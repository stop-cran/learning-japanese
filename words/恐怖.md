# 恐怖 (きょうふ) - fear; terror

## Meaning

恐怖 names fear, dread, or terror. Useful noun patterns are 恐怖を感じる
(きょうふをかんじる), "feel fear," and Nへの恐怖, "fear of N."
恐怖する is also a verb, "fear; be terrified," but the noun patterns give a
straightforward way to build sentences.

- 暗い道を一人で歩いていて、恐怖を感じました。 (くらいみちをひとりであるいていて、きょうふをかんじました。) - I felt fear while walking alone along a dark road.
- 彼は恐怖で声が出ませんでした。 (かれはきょうふでこえがでませんでした。) - He could not speak because of fear.

In 恐怖で, で marks the cause of the inability to speak.

## How the kanji combine

[恐](../kanji/恐.md) and [怖](../kanji/怖.md) both convey fear. Their related
meanings reinforce each other rather than forming a verb-object pair.
The on readings combine as きょう + ふ; keep the long vowel in きょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怖い](怖い.md) (こわい) is an adjective: 怖い話 (こわいはなし), "a scary
story." 恐怖 is the noun naming the fear. [心配](心配.md) (しんぱい) is worry
or concern and need not reach the experience described as terror.
Reassurance may counter fear, but it is not a grammatical negative of 恐怖.

## Distinctive meaning

The word lets you discuss fear as an experience or topic, including 恐怖の原因
(きょうふのげんいん), "the cause of the fear." It is not an い-adjective:
use a construction such as 恐怖を感じる rather than attaching adjective endings
directly to 恐怖.
