# 券 (けん) - ticket; coupon

## Meaning

券 is a noun for a ticket, coupon, or certificate. It also appears after another word to identify what the ticket permits or provides. 入場券 (にゅうじょうけん) is an admission ticket; 割引券 (わりびきけん) is a discount coupon.

- 入口で券を見せてください。 (いりぐちでけんをみせてください。) - Please show your ticket at the entrance.
- この割引券は今日まで使えます。 (このわりびきけんはきょうまでつかえます。) - This discount coupon can be used through today.

券を買う (けんをかう) means "buy a ticket." Context supplies the kind when 券 appears by itself.

## How the kanji combine

券 is a single kanji read with the on reading けん. In compounds, it functions as the head: 入場 explains admission, while 券 identifies the ticket. This article does not divide the character's visual parts into a word etymology.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[切符](切符.md) (きっぷ) also means a ticket, particularly useful in transport situations. 券 participates in a wider range of compound labels, including coupons and certificates. The words overlap rather than forming opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Look to the preceding element for what a 券 is valid for. A discount coupon and an admission ticket are both 券, but they do different jobs. The noun is not a general synonym for every paper document, and its reading けん should not be inferred from English "ticket."
