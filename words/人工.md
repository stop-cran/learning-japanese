# 人工 (じんこう) - artificial; human-made

## Meaning

人工 concerns something made through human work rather than occurring naturally. Use 人工の before a noun. It also forms compounds such as 人工衛星 (じんこうえいせい), "artificial satellite."

- これは人工の湖です。 (これはじんこうのみずうみです。) - This is a human-made lake.
- スキー場では人工の雪を使っています。 (スキーじょうではじんこうのゆきをつかっています。) - The ski resort uses artificial snow.

人工的な (じんこうてきな) is another adjective-like form, with な before a noun. Do not attach な directly to 人工 when using the basic 人工の pattern.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) supplies "human," and [工](../kanji/工.md) concerns work or making. Their modifier-and-head relationship gives the idea of human workmanship. The on readings are ジン + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[天然](天然.md) (てんねん), "naturally occurring," provides a direct contrast for materials or features. [自然](自然.md) (しぜん) is broader and can refer to nature or naturalness, including how behavior appears.

## Distinctive meaning

人工 describes origin or manufacture, not necessarily poor quality or deception. An artificial lake can be a real lake made by people. Likewise, 人工 alone does not mean that someone's smile is insincere: the intended sense of English "artificial" matters.
