# 宝石 (ほうせき) - gem; precious stone

## Meaning

宝石 is a noun meaning a gem, jewel, or precious stone. It can refer to a stone set in a ring or necklace, as well as to one considered on its own.

- この指輪には青い宝石がついています。 (このゆびわにはあおいほうせきがついています。) - This ring has a blue gemstone set in it.
- 店員は宝石を一つずつ見せてくれました。 (てんいんはほうせきをひとつずつみせてくれました。) - The shop assistant showed me the gems one by one.

宝石の指輪 (ほうせきのゆびわ) describes a gemstone ring. The object named by 指輪 is the ring, while 宝石 identifies its jeweled feature.

## How the kanji combine

宝 contributes treasure or something precious; [石](../kanji/石.md) contributes stone. 宝 modifies the kind or value of 石. The on readings are ホウ + セキ, giving ほうせき rather than a combination of the standalone native readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[石](石.md) (いし) is the broader word for a stone. [宝](宝.md) (たから), "treasure," can include precious possessions or people, not just gems. [アクセサリー](アクセサリー.md) names accessories more broadly and does not imply that each item contains a gemstone.

## Distinctive meaning

English "jewelry" can mean finished ornaments collectively, whereas 宝石 focuses on gems. A plain metal bracelet may be an accessory without containing 宝石. The word alone also does not establish a particular mineral, color, price, or proof of authenticity; those require further description.
