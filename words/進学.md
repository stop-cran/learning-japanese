# 進学 (しんがく) - advancing to further education

## Meaning

進学 is a noun and intransitive する verb for advancing to the next stage of education. It includes going on to high school, university, or another subsequent educational stage. It does not mean university alone.

- 中学校を卒業して、高校に進学します。 (ちゅうがっこうをそつぎょうして、こうこうにしんがくします。) - After graduating from junior high school, I will go on to high school.
- 卒業後は大学に進学したいです。 (そつぎょうごはだいがくにしんがくしたいです。) - After graduation, I want to continue to university.

The school or educational stage takes に. 進学先 (しんがくさき) is the institution or destination to which someone advances.

## How the kanji combine

[進](../kanji/進.md) means advance, and [学](../kanji/学.md) concerns learning or education. Together they express moving forward in education. This component relationship helps recall the meaning without limiting it to a particular school type.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入学](入学.md) (にゅうがく) focuses on entering an institution; 進学 focuses on educational progression. [卒業](卒業.md) (そつぎょう) is graduating from the preceding course or school, not a direct opposite. [就職](就職.md) (しゅうしょく), entering employment, can be a contrasting post-graduation path.

## Distinctive meaning

進学 describes the transition, not attending classes on an ordinary day. The community source's English gloss "going on to university" gives one example but is too narrow as the definition. The high-school example above is also dictionary-supported usage. Whether the student has already advanced or merely intends to depends on the verb form and context.
