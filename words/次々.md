# 次々 (つぎつぎ) - one after another; in succession

## Meaning

次々 is an adverbial expression meaning "one after another; in succession." Useful forms are **次々に** and **次々と**, followed by the event that keeps occurring.

- 客が次々に店に入ってきました。 (きゃくがつぎつぎにみせにはいってきました。) - Customers came into the shop one after another.
- 新しい問題が次々と見つかりました。 (あたらしいもんだいがつぎつぎとみつかりました。) - New problems were discovered one after another.

The expression emphasizes a succession of arrivals or discoveries, rather than simply saying there was more than one.

## How the kanji combine

[次](../kanji/次.md) means "next" and is read つぎ here. The repetition mark 々 repeats the preceding kanji, giving つぎつぎ. It is not an independently pronounced kanji; the word's distinct written kanji is 次.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[次](次.md) (つぎ) identifies the next item or occasion. 次々 expands that idea into a continuing sequence. [同時](同時.md) (どうじ), "at the same time," contrasts with succession in suitable contexts, though real events can overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

"One by one" should not suggest a rule that each event must finish before the next begins. Customers can arrive 次々 while earlier customers remain inside. Nor does the expression itself say whether the succession is welcome: new opportunities and new problems can both appear this way.
