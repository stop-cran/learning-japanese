# レジャー - leisure; recreational activities

## Meaning

レジャー means leisure: free time, and recreation or outings enjoyed
in that time. In compounds such as レジャー施設 (レジャーしせつ),
it concerns recreational facilities.

- 休日のレジャーを楽しみにしています。 (きゅうじつのレジャーをたのしみにしています。) - I am looking forward to recreational activities on my day off.
- この地域にはレジャー施設が多いです。 (このちいきにはレジャーしせつがおおいです。) - This area has many leisure facilities.

It is a noun, not a verb meaning to relax.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暇](暇.md) (ひま) includes free time or leisure.
[娯楽](娯楽.md) (ごらく) concerns entertainment or recreation;
[レクリエーション](レクリエーション.md) also concerns recreation.
[趣味](趣味.md) (しゅみ) often names a continuing hobby or personal taste,
not all free time.

## Distinctive meaning

The English source is leisure, but Japanese レジャー is useful for
recreational activities as well as time available for them.
It need not mean an expensive holiday or a particular outdoor sport.
Conversely, an available hour does not prove that someone spends it
on a leisure outing.
