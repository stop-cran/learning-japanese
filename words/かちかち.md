# かちかち — hard and stiff; clicking

## Meaning

Something very hard or stiff, or repeated small hard clicks such as a clock ticking.

- 冷凍庫（れいとうこ）のパンがかちかちになった。 — The bread in the freezer became rock-hard.
- 時計（とけい）がかちかちと音を立てている。 — The clock is ticking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[がちがち](がちがち.md) overlaps in stiffness and tension. [かたかた](かたかた.md) suggests light rattling; [ぐにゃぐにゃ](ぐにゃぐにゃ.md) offers a soft, pliable contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn かちかちのパン and かちかちに凍る（こおる）alongside the sound use. The word can also describe a rigid outlook or someone tense with nerves; it is not restricted to objects that make a clicking noise.

Also written カチカチ. The cited JMdict snapshot keeps かちかち as a search-only kana variant, not a separate word. That dictionary flag does not mean hiragana is incorrect.
