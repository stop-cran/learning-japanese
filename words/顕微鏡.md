# 顕微鏡 (けんびきょう) - microscope

## Meaning

顕微鏡 is a microscope, an instrument for examining very small things.
It is a noun. 顕微鏡で見る means "look through / examine with a microscope";
で marks the instrument used.

- 顕微鏡で細胞を観察しました。 (けんびきょうでさいぼうをかんさつしました。) - I observed cells with a microscope.
- この小さな生物は顕微鏡で見えます。 (このちいさないきものはけんびきょうでみえます。) - This tiny organism can be seen with a microscope.

顕微鏡をのぞく means "look through a microscope."
The word itself does not specify a particular magnification or microscope technology.

## How the kanji combine

顕 has associations with appearing or becoming manifest, 微 with minuteness,
and 鏡 with a mirror or optical instrument.
As a mnemonic, think of an instrument making minute things visible.
This component image is not a historical account of the word.
The on readings are ケン + ビ + キョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[見る](見る.md) (みる) is the general verb "see; look."
望遠鏡 (ぼうえんきょう), a telescope, is a useful contrasting instrument:
it concerns distant objects rather than simply very small ones.
The two instrument names are not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

A 顕微鏡 is an instrument, not the specimen or the act of observing.
Nor is every object that magnifies something called a 顕微鏡;
a simple magnifying glass is named differently.
