# ジャーナリスト (ジャーナリスト) - journalist

## Meaning

ジャーナリスト is a journalist: someone engaged in journalism, such as reporting or editing news and commentary. It names a person, not the article or broadcast.

- 彼女は海外で働くジャーナリストです。
  (かのじょはかいがいではたらくジャーナリストです。) - She is a journalist working overseas.
- そのジャーナリストは事件について取材しています。
  (そのジャーナリストはじけんについてしゅざいしています。) - That journalist is gathering information about the incident.

ジャーナリストとして働く (ジャーナリストとしてはたらく) means "work as a journalist."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[記者](記者.md) (きしゃ), "reporter; journalist," overlaps directly and often identifies a reporting role. ジャーナリスト is not restricted to a reporter employed by a printed newspaper; the public dictionary includes editors as well as reporters.

[作家](作家.md) (さっか) names a writer or creative author. Writing a novel does not by itself make someone a journalist. There is no useful general antonym for this occupation.

## Distinctive meaning

This loanword corresponds to English "journalist." Keep the long vowel in ジャー. The label itself does not guarantee impartiality, accuracy, independence, or a particular employer. Those are separate questions about the person's work.
