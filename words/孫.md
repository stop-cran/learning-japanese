# 孫 (まご) - grandchild

## Meaning

孫 is a grandchild: the child of one's child. The noun does not specify gender. Use の to identify the grandparent whose grandchild is being discussed.

- 祖母には孫が三人います。 (そぼにはまごがさんにんいます。) - My grandmother has three grandchildren.
- 週末に孫が遊びに来ます。 (しゅうまつにまごがあそびにきます。) - My grandchild is coming to visit this weekend.

The first example counts people with 人. In the second, the relationship to the speaker is understood from context; Japanese does not need an explicit "my" in every family reference.

## How the kanji combine

孫 is one character writing the native word まご. Learn the complete reading with the family relationship. Its visible 子 component is associated with a child, but a component-by-component English gloss is not needed to identify the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[子供](子供.md) (こども) means child and does not by itself specify the grandchild relationship. [祖父](祖父.md) (そふ), "grandfather," and [祖母](祖母.md) (そぼ), "grandmother," name the reciprocal generation, not opposing kinds of people.

## Distinctive meaning

孫 can mean either grandson or granddaughter; add detail if gender matters. お孫さん (おまごさん) is a respectful way to refer to someone else's grandchild. Do not assume that a sentence containing 孫 describes the speaker's own family: the possessor may be stated elsewhere or supplied by the conversation.
