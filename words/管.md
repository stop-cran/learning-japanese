# 管 (くだ) - pipe; tube

## Meaning

管 (くだ) is a pipe or tube: a hollow object through which something such as water or air can pass. Use **管を通る** (くだをとおる) for passing through a tube and 管が詰まる (くだがつまる) for a pipe becoming blocked.

- この管の中を水が流れています。 (このくだのなかをみずがながれています。) - Water is flowing through this pipe.
- 細い管を容器につなぎました。 (ほそいくだをようきにつなぎました。) - I connected a thin tube to the container.

The noun does not by itself specify the tube's material, diameter, or purpose.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 管 supplies the whole noun and is read くだ here. This is a native reading; it should not be replaced with the on reading かん simply because that reading appears in compounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

パイプ and チューブ also refer to pipes or tubes, with the choice depending on the object and established terminology. A 筒 (つつ) is a hollow cylindrical object; it need not be used to convey a liquid or gas.

## Distinctive meaning

The examples describe a passage inside the tube. Compare [流れる](流れる.md) (ながれる), which describes the water's movement, with 管, which names the object. The community list's original dictionary ID did not support くだ; the entry below explicitly does.
