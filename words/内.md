# 内 (うち) — inside; within

## Meaning

内 means the inside or within a boundary. It also appears in expressions of time and membership, often written うち.

- 今日のうちに終わらせます。 (きょうのうちにおわらせます。) — I will finish it before today is over.
- 三人のうち、二人が学生です。 (さんにんのうち、ふたりががくせいです。) — Of the three people, two are students.

## How the kanji combine

[内](../kanji/内.md) is a single kanji read うち here. It is not the on-reading ない used in compounds. Kana is especially useful for grammatical patterns such as のうち and うちに.

## Synonyms and antonyms

中 (なか) also means "inside." 外 (そと), "outside," contrasts with the spatial meaning. 間 (あいだ) can express an interval, but it does not automatically convey the opportunity or deadline suggested by うちに.

## Distinctive meaning

For "while a state still holds," attach うちに to a plain verb or い-adjective, to a な-adjective with な, or to a noun with の: 若いうちに (わかいうちに), "while young"; 元気なうちに (げんきなうちに), "while healthy." A separate familiar use of うち means one's home or own group.
