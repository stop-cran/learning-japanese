# 会社 (かいしゃ) — company

## Meaning

会社 is a company or a business corporation, and also the workplace: 会社に行く (go to work), 会社員 かいしゃいん (company employee), 会社を休む (take a day off), 会社を作る つくる (found a company).
In Japanese 会社 is also a legal term for a corporation: 株式会社 かぶしきがいしゃ (joint-stock company, K.K.), with the voiced がいしゃ.

## How the kanji combine

[会](../kanji/会.md) (meeting, association) + [社](../kanji/社.md) (company, association): "an association" of people. Both are on readings (カイ + シャ). Compare the reversed order, [社会](社会.md) しゃかい (society).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 企業 きぎょう (enterprise, formal), 職場 しょくば (workplace), 勤め先 つとめさき (place of work).
- Related: 会社員, 社長 しゃちょう (company president).

## Distinctive meaning

会社 is the generic word; 企業 stresses the business as an economic body. Speaking of one's workplace, 会社 is the usual word, even for a small firm.
