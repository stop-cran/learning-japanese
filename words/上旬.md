# 上旬 (じょうじゅん) - first ten days of a month

## Meaning

上旬 means the first ten days of a month: the 1st through the 10th, inclusive. Put the month directly before it, as in 四月上旬 (しがつじょうじゅん), "early April."

- 四月上旬に引っ越す予定です。 (しがつじょうじゅんにひっこすよていです。) - I plan to move in early April.
- 工事は来月の上旬に終わります。 (こうじはらいげつのじょうじゅんにおわります。) - The construction work will finish in the first ten days of next month.

The first sentence gives a ten-day period, not a specific moving date.

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md) contributes the earlier or first part, and 旬 a ten-day period here. The compound names the month's first such period. Read ジョウ + ジュン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

中旬 (ちゅうじゅん) is the 11th through the 20th. [下旬](下旬.md) (げじゅん) runs from the 21st to the end of the month. [初め](初め.md) (はじめ), "beginning," is less exact by itself.

## Distinctive meaning

"First third of a month" is a conventional translation, not an instruction to divide the number of days by three. February's 上旬 still ends on the 10th; the last period has a variable length. 上旬 is not a general term for the first ten days of any arbitrary event.
