# 駅前 (えきまえ) — in front of the station

## Meaning

駅前 means the area directly outside a station, often a commercial zone: 駅前のカフェ (a café in front of the station), 駅前で会おう (let's meet at the station square), 駅前広場 えきまえひろば (station plaza).

## How the kanji combine

[駅](../kanji/駅.md) (station) + [前](../kanji/前.md) (front): "station front". The first is an on reading (エキ) and the second a kun reading (まえ), an on + kun mixture (a 重箱読み じゅうばこよみ pattern), but it is still classed as kango here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 駅ビル えきビル (a building at a station), 駅構内 えきこうない (inside the station).
- Related: 駅裏 えきうら (the back side of a station).

## Distinctive meaning

駅前 is very common in addresses, shop names and as a meeting spot. In the sense of "in front of the station" it works both as a noun and attributively, as in 駅前の店.
