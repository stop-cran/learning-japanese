# 下駄 (げた) - wooden sandal

## Meaning

下駄 are traditional Japanese wooden sandals, typically with a thong and raised supports under the sole.
They are worn on the feet, not ordinary wooden indoor floorboards.

- 浴衣に下駄を合わせました。 (ゆかたにげたをあわせました。) - I paired geta with a yukata.
- 下駄を履いて歩きます。 (げたをはいてあるきます。) - I walk wearing geta.

The verb for putting them on is [履く](履く.md) (はく).

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) means below; 駄 has character meanings including burden or packhorse.
These can be memory associations for something supporting the feet, not a verified historical explanation of the name.
KANJIDIC2 records the on readings ゲ for 下 and タ for 駄: learn げた as a whole word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[サンダル](サンダル.md) means sandals more broadly.
[草履](草履.md) (ぞうり) are another kind of traditional Japanese footwear, not a spelling of 下駄.
[靴](靴.md) (くつ) names shoes or footwear more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

An English "clog" is not necessarily the same construction as a 下駄.
The wooden base is the useful identifying feature; not every design has exactly the same supports.
The dictionary's specialized printing and go-game senses are outside this card's selected footwear scope.
