# 注目 (ちゅうもく) - attention; notice

## Meaning

注目 is attention directed toward something. It is a noun and する verb. Use Nに注目する, "pay attention to N," and 注目を集める (ちゅうもくをあつめる), "attract attention."

- グラフの赤い線に注目してください。 (グラフのあかいせんにちゅうもくしてください。) - Please focus on the red line in the graph.
- その選手は世界中の注目を集めています。 (そのせんしゅはせかいじゅうのちゅうもくをあつめています。) - That athlete is attracting attention from around the world.

In the first pattern, に marks the object of attention. In the second, attention itself is what is gathered, so 注目 takes を.

## How the kanji combine

[注](../kanji/注.md) contributes directing or pouring toward something, and [目](../kanji/目.md) contributes the eyes. "Directing one's eyes" is a useful image for focused attention. The on readings are チュウ + モク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[確認](確認.md) (かくにん), "checking; confirmation," involves establishing information, not simply attending to it. 注目 can precede careful checking without guaranteeing that anything has been verified.

## Distinctive meaning

注目 does not necessarily mean praise. An unusual event or a problem can attract attention as readily as a successful athlete. It also differs from English "notice" meaning a posted announcement: 注目 names the attention paid, not the written notice on a wall.
