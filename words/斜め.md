# 斜め (ななめ) - slanting; diagonal

## Meaning

斜め describes a slant, tilt or diagonal direction. It functions as a
noun or na-adjective. 斜めに specifies orientation; 斜めの線 is
"a diagonal line."

- 紙に斜めの線を引きました。 (かみにななめのせんをひきました。) - I drew a diagonal line on the paper.
- 帽子を斜めにかぶっています。 (ぼうしをななめにかぶっています。) - They are wearing their hat at an angle.

The reference direction comes from context: the word does not specify
a fixed angle such as forty-five degrees.

## How the kanji combine

斜 writes なな, followed by め. This is a native word, not an on-reading
compound. The dictionary also records 斜 with irregular okurigana,
outdated 傾, and obsolete なのめ. Those are recognition information,
not additional primary readings to substitute freely into these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[傾く](傾く.md) (かたむく) describes tilting or inclining as a verb.
[縦](縦.md) (たて) and [横](横.md) (よこ) name vertical and horizontal
directions. A diagonal is described relative to such reference directions,
not as a third fixed compass point.

## Distinctive meaning

Figuratively, 斜め can describe a slanted outlook or something amiss.
The expression ご機嫌斜め (ごきげんななめ) means being in a bad mood;
it does not describe the person's physical posture. This is an additional
use, not permission to call every difficult personality 斜め.
