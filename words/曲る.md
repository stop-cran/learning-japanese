# 曲る (まがる) -- turn; bend

## Meaning

曲る is a dictionary-listed spelling of 曲がる, the ordinary spelling used below. The verb describes bending or curving, and turning while moving.

- この道は右に曲がっています。 (このみちはみぎにまがっています。) -- This road curves to the right.
- 次の角を左に曲がってください。 (つぎのかどをひだりにまがってください。) -- Please turn left at the next corner.

It is a godan verb: 曲がります (まがります), 曲がらない (まがらない), 曲がって (まがって), and 曲がった (まがった).

## How the kanji combine

[曲](../kanji/曲.md) contributes bending or curvature. In the usual spelling 曲がる, がる is written as okurigana. The shorter spelling 曲る does not change the pronunciation or conjugation class.

## Synonyms and antonyms

曲げる (まげる) is transitive: someone bends an object, which takes を. まっすぐ行く (まっすぐいく), go straight, contrasts with making a turn in directions.

## Distinctive meaning

曲がる is intransitive, but route instructions can still use を: 角を marks the point where one turns, not a corner being physically bent. The direction takes に. Keep the travel construction separate from bending an object with 曲げる.
