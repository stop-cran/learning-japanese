# 飛び込む (とびこむ) - jump in; plunge into; barge in

## Meaning

飛び込む means to jump, leap, or dive into something.
It also describes bursting or barging in.
It is an intransitive godan verb; the destination can take に.

- 選手がプールに飛び込みました。 (せんしゅがプールにとびこみました。) - The athlete dived into the pool.
- 子供が部屋に飛び込んできました。 (こどもがへやにとびこんできました。) - A child came rushing into the room.

Forms include 飛び込みます, 飛び込んで, and 飛び込まない.
In the second example, くる adds movement toward the relevant viewpoint.

## How the kanji combine

飛び, the stem of [飛ぶ](飛ぶ.md) (とぶ), combines with 込む (こむ).
[飛](../kanji/飛.md) contributes flying or leaping, while
[込](../kanji/込.md) contributes movement inward.
The established compound need not describe literal airborne flight.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入る](入る.md) (はいる) means enter more broadly, without requiring a rush or leap.
[突っ込む](突っ込む.md) (つっこむ) also has plunging or rushing-in uses.
[飛び出す](飛び出す.md) (とびだす) foregrounds coming out;
read the particles as well, since its destination can still take に.

## Distinctive meaning

The inward direction matters: this is not simply every jump or every quick movement.
Someone can barge into a room without literally jumping.
The verb alone does not say whether entering was welcome or permitted.
These examples teach entry and diving, not every extended use of the compound.
