# 全集 (ぜんしゅう) - complete works; collected series

## Meaning

全集 names a complete collected edition or series, especially an author's works.
It can also be a broadly collected series organized by a category such as
genre or period, rather than one person's entire output.

- 図書館で夏目漱石の全集を借りました。 (としょかんでなつめそうせきのぜんしゅうをかりました。) - I borrowed Natsume Soseki's collected works from the library.
- この全集は全部で十巻あります。 (このぜんしゅうはぜんぶでじっかんあります。) - This collected edition has ten volumes in all.

作者名 + の全集 identifies whose collected works are meant.
全集 can also follow an author's name or a category directly in a series title.
The noun itself does not say how many volumes there are.

## How the kanji combine

[全](../kanji/全.md) contributes complete or all, and
[集](../kanji/集.md) a collection.
The first qualifies the second: a complete or comprehensive collected edition.
The on readings are ゼン + シュウ; keep the long う in しゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

選集 (せんしゅう) is a selection or anthology.
[コレクション](コレクション.md) is a broader collection, including objects
such as stamps. [集まり](集まり.md) (あつまり) can name things gathered together
without identifying a published collected edition.

## Distinctive meaning

"Complete" is part of the established title/category, not a guarantee that the edition
contains every unpublished scrap its author ever wrote.
Nor must every 全集 collect only one author: a literature series can use the name.
Distinguish the contents and editorial scope of the particular publication
from a literal claim about everything ever produced.
