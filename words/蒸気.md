# 蒸気 (じょうき) - steam; vapor

## Meaning

蒸気 is vapor, including steam from water. It is not restricted to water: a substance that normally exists as a liquid or solid can produce vapor through evaporation or sublimation.

- やかんから蒸気が出ています。 (やかんからじょうきがでています。) - Steam is coming from the kettle.
- 蒸気でやけどをしないように気をつけてください。 (じょうきでやけどをしないようにきをつけてください。) - Please be careful not to burn yourself with the steam.

These examples concern water vapor. In ordinary speech the visible mist above a kettle is also called steam; the card is not claiming that a visible cloud consists only of gaseous water.

## How the kanji combine

[蒸](../kanji/蒸.md) concerns steam, and [気](../kanji/気.md) has air or gas associations. Keep the long じょう in じょうき. 蒸汽 is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[気体](気体.md) and [ガス](ガス.md) are broader gas words; not every gas is called 蒸気. [蒸す](蒸す.md) is a verb for steaming food or being hot and humid, not the noun naming the vapor.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict additionally records 蒸気 as an abbreviation for a steam locomotive, steam train, or steamship, overlapping with [汽車](汽車.md) in the train use. That context-dependent recognition is not a recommendation to translate every modern vehicle as 蒸気. The pinned entry marks this as an abbreviation, not explicitly as archaic; it supplies no frequency measurement.
