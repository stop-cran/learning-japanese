# 言う (いう) — say

## Meaning

言う means to say or utter something. It also appears in naming expressions: "be called..."
Use と after quoted words or the name being given.

- 先生は「おはよう」と言いました。（せんせいは「おはよう」といいました。） — The teacher said, "Good morning."
- 私は田中と言います。（わたしはたなかといいます。） — My name is Tanaka.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native verb, not a compound. [言](../kanji/言.md) contributes the い part of いう,
and う is okurigana. It is a godan verb: 言います（いいます）, 言わない（いわない）, 言って（いって）.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話す](話す.md) はなす means "talk; speak" and can describe a conversation or speaking a language.
言う more directly presents an utterance or what was said.
There is no single direct antonym; "not say" is 言わない. [聞く](聞く.md) きく, "listen; hear," is the listener's activity, not an interchangeable opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary kana spelling is いう, but the plain form is often pronounced ゆう; JMdict also records that reading.
Do not extend that pronunciation mechanically to every inflected form: the polite form is いいます.
For "speak English," use [英語](英語.md)を話す（えいごをはなす）, not 英語を言う as a general description of language ability.
