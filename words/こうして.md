# こうして (こうして) - thus; in this way

## Meaning

こうして means "thus; in this way." It points to a way of doing something
being demonstrated or described, or connects earlier events with the outcome
they brought about.

- こうして紙を折ります。 (こうしてかみをおります。) - Fold the paper in this way.
- 皆で協力しました。こうして計画は成功しました。 (みんなできょうりょくしました。こうしてけいかくはせいこうしました。) - Everyone worked together. In this way, the plan succeeded.

The first example assumes a demonstration or explanation of the method.
The second points back to the preceding events.

## How the kanji combine

The ordinary form is kana-only. It contains [こう](こう.md), "like this,"
and して, the て-form of する. 斯うして is a rare dictionary-recognized
kanji spelling, not the primary spelling taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[そうして](そうして.md) can mean "in that way" or connect what follows.
[そして](そして.md) commonly adds the next statement or event.
[それで](それで.md) can introduce a consequence.
Their English translations overlap, but not every sequence has the same
demonstrative or causal connection.

## Distinctive meaning

こうして is not an unrestricted replacement for every English "so."
It can highlight the manner or course of events producing an outcome,
where [だから](だから.md) more directly presents a reason-based conclusion.
Nor is "this way" automatically a direction toward [こちら](こちら.md)
or [こっち](こっち.md).
