# 手入れ (ていれ) - care; maintenance; grooming

## Meaning

手入れ is care, maintenance, repair, or grooming that keeps something in good condition or puts it in order. Use Xの手入れをする or Xを手入れする for attending to X.

- 毎週、庭の手入れをしています。
  (まいしゅう、にわのていれをしています。) - I tend the garden every week.
- 靴は手入れをすれば長く使えます。
  (くつはていれをすればながくつかえます。) - Shoes last longer if you take care of them.

髪の手入れ (かみのていれ) concerns hair care; 道具の手入れ (どうぐのていれ) concerns maintaining tools. The particular work depends on what receives the care.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md) contributes the hand, and [入](../kanji/入.md) entering or putting in, through 入れ (いれ). Learn the established native noun ていれ: literally translating it as "insert a hand" would miss the maintenance sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[整備](整備.md) (せいび) overlaps with maintenance or putting in order. [修理](修理.md) (しゅうり) focuses on repairing something damaged. [世話](世話.md) (せわ) overlaps in care, especially looking after someone or their needs.

## Distinctive meaning

手入れ need not wait until something breaks; regular cleaning, trimming, or tending can qualify. Nor does it mean every possible repair. Separately, it can name a police raid or search: that use is not an instruction to groom a suspect.
