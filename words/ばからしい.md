# ばからしい - absurd; foolish; ridiculous

## Meaning

ばからしい judges something foolish, absurd or not worth taking seriously.
It is an i-adjective: ばからしい話 (ばからしいはなし), "an absurd story,"
and ばからしくない, "not foolish."

- そんなうわさを信じるのはばからしいです。 (そんなうわさをしんじるのはばからしいです。) - It is foolish to believe a rumor like that.
- 同じことで何度も争うのがばからしくなりました。 (おなじことでなんどもあらそうのがばからしくなりました。) - I came to feel it was pointless to argue over the same thing again and again.

The judgment can be dismissive. Choose the situation and listener carefully.

## How the kanji combine

馬鹿らしい is a recognized spelling. [馬鹿](馬鹿.md) (ばか), "foolish; a fool,"
uses ateji: do not derive foolishness from the literal animal meanings.
The ending らしい belongs to this established adjective; the whole word does not
merely report that someone apparently looks like a fool.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くだらない](くだらない.md) overlaps with foolish or worthless things.
[無駄](無駄.md) (むだ) focuses on waste or futility.
[つまらない](つまらない.md) can concern boredom, but also trivial or pointless things;
that latter use overlaps without making all boredom absurdity.

## Distinctive meaning

Unlike the na-adjective 馬鹿な, use ばからしい directly before a noun, without な.
Its past is ばからしかった, and its adverbial form is ばからしく.
Do not assume that adding a polite です makes a harsh evaluation harmless.
