# 八 (はち)

## Meaning

八 is the number eight. はち is its ordinary on reading for reciting numbers and many numerical expressions.

- 答えは八です。 (こたえは はちです。) — The answer is eight.
- 八時に会いましょう。 (はちじに あいましょう。) — Let's meet at eight o'clock.

Counting objects normally requires a suitable counter, not just the number alone.

## How the kanji combine

[八](../kanji/八.md) stands alone here. It also has native や in words such as 八つ (やっつ), "eight things." The Arabic numeral 8 represents the same number without specifying a Japanese reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

八つ is useful for general counting. 八人 (はちにん) counts people, while 八日 (ようか) means the eighth of the month or eight days. These are different counting expressions, not synonyms in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

The pronunciation can change when a counter follows: 八分 (はっぷん), "eight minutes." Learn each counter pattern instead of assuming はち always remains unchanged. Numbers do not have natural antonyms.
