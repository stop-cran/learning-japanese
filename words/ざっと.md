# ざっと - roughly; cursorily

## Meaning

ざっと is an adverb with two useful applications: an approximate quantity, or a quick, broad treatment of something without attending to every detail. It can precede a number or an action.

- 会場にはざっと百人いました。 (かいじょうにはざっとひゃくにんいました。) - There were roughly a hundred people at the venue.
- 出かける前に新聞をざっと読みました。 (でかけるまえにしんぶんをざっとよみました。) - I skimmed the newspaper before going out.

The first sentence gives an estimate. The second describes the manner of reading, not an approximate number of newspapers.

## How the kanji combine

This kana-written adverb is labeled mimetic in JMdict. There are no kanji components to interpret. The small っ matters in ざっと, and the final と is already part of the expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[およそ](およそ.md) also expresses approximation. [大体](大体.md) (だいたい) overlaps with "roughly" or "in general." [詳しい](詳しい.md) in 詳しく読む (くわしくよむ), "read in detail," gives a contextual contrast to skimming.

## Distinctive meaning

"Roughly" here does not mean treating someone violently or rudely. ざっと説明する (ざっとせつめいする) is "give a brief overview." It may be an appropriate first pass before a detailed explanation, not necessarily careless work. Contrast [きちんと](きちんと.md) when the question is whether a task meets the required standard.
