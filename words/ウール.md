# ウール - wool

## Meaning

ウール is wool, used as a textile fiber or material. It is a noun.
ウールの + noun describes something made of wool.

- ウールのセーターを着ています。 (ウールのセーターをきています。) - I am wearing a wool sweater.
- このコートはウール百パーセントです。 (このコートはウールひゃくパーセントです。) - This coat is one hundred percent wool.

The material can be part of a blend; the word alone does not specify
the percentage. Use の, not adjectival な, in ウールのセーター.

## How the kanji combine

There are no kanji components. The ー lengthens ウ, followed by ル:
ウー・ル. The Japanese word has a final vowel even though English
"wool" ends in a consonant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

羊毛 (ようもう) names sheep's wool. [毛](毛.md) (け) is broader:
hair, fur, or animal wool depending on context.
[綿](綿.md) (めん), "cotton," is a different fiber, not an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Warm clothing is not automatically ウール: warmth does not establish
its fiber composition. A wool sweater is a finished garment; the material
word itself does not mean "sweater" or specify how to wash it.
