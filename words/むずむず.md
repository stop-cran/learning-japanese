# むずむず — feeling itchy; itching to act

## Meaning

An itchy, tickly, restless sensation, or an eager urge to do something.

- 鼻（はな）がむずむずして、くしゃみが出そうだ。 — My nose tickles and I feel a sneeze coming.
- 新しい靴（くつ）を見ると、早く外で走りたくてむずむずする。 — Seeing my new shoes makes me itch to run outside.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ちくちく](ちくちく.md) is often prickly rather than itchy. [わくわく](わくわく.md) describes excited anticipation, without necessarily conveying the restless urge of むずむず.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 鼻がむずむずする or 何かしたくてむずむずする, “itch to do something.” The second is figurative: no actual skin itch is required. Neither pattern tells the cause of a physical sensation.
