# 留守 (るす) — absence from home

## Meaning

留守 commonly means that someone is away from home or another expected place. It is a noun: 留守です, "is out/away," and 留守にする, "be away; leave the place unattended."

- 田中さんは今、留守です。 (たなかさんはいま、るすです。) — Tanaka is out at the moment.
- 午後は家を留守にします。 (ごごはいえをるすにします。) — I will be away from home this afternoon.

## How the kanji combine

[留](../kanji/留.md) suggests remaining, and [守](../kanji/守.md) guarding. Read る + す. The characters connect with the related house-sitting sense, while the common modern examples above describe absence; do not derive the meaning mechanically from "remain."

## Synonyms and antonyms

不在 (ふざい) is absence more generally. 在宅 (ざいたく) means being at home. 留守番 (るすばん) is looking after a home or place while others are away.

## Distinctive meaning

留守 does not necessarily mean an empty building: someone else may be there while the person sought is out. 留守の間 (るすのあいだ) means "during the absence." 留守番電話 (るすばんでんわ) is an answering machine or voicemail service associated with receiving messages while unavailable.
