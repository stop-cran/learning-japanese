# 交差 (こうさ) - crossing; intersection

## Meaning

交差 is a crossing or intersection. With する it describes lines, roads,
or similar things intersecting: AとBが交差する, "A and B intersect."
The crossing things are subjects, not an object followed by を.

- この地点で二本の道路が交差しています。 (このちてんでにほんのどうろがこうさしています。) - Two roads intersect at this point.
- 二つの線が直角に交差します。 (ふたつのせんがちょっかくにこうさします。) - The two lines intersect at a right angle.

交叉 is another dictionary-recognized spelling, with the same reading.

## How the kanji combine

[交](../kanji/交.md) contributes crossing or intersecting.
[差](../kanji/差.md) has a difference or discrepancy association in other
words. The crossing meaning belongs to this complete compound; do not
translate it as an arithmetic difference. The on readings are コウ + サ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[交差点](交差点.md) (こうさてん) names an intersection point, particularly
a road junction. [横断](横断.md) (おうだん) often concerns crossing from
one side to another, but also has an intersecting use; it is not categorically
excluded from descriptions of crossing lines.

## Distinctive meaning

English "cross" also describes a person's movement across a road.
For that construction, 道を[渡る](渡る.md) (みちをわたる), "cross a road,"
does not become 道を交差する. Distinguish things intersecting from someone
moving across them; translation overlap does not erase the particle patterns.
