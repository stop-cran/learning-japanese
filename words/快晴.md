# 快晴 (かいせい) - clear weather; nearly cloudless sky

## Meaning

快晴 is clear weather with little or no cloud. Use it as a noun with です or before の: 快晴です (かいせいです), "The weather is clear," and 快晴の空 (かいせいのそら), "a nearly cloudless sky."

- 今日は快晴です。 (きょうはかいせいです。) - Today the sky is clear.
- 快晴の空の下で写真を撮りました。 (かいせいのそらのしたでしゃしんをとりました。) - I took photographs under a nearly cloudless sky.

## How the kanji combine

[快](../kanji/快.md), "pleasant," modifies [晴](../kanji/晴.md), "clear skies." The on readings カイ + セイ give かいせい. This association is not a claim that everyone finds the same weather pleasant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[晴れ](晴れ.md) (はれ), "clear weather," is broader in everyday use. 晴天 (せいてん) also refers to fair or clear weather. [曇り](曇り.md) (くもり), "cloudy weather," is a useful sky-condition contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

快晴 is more specific than general "good weather": the low amount of cloud matters. It need not mean literally zero clouds. Japan Meteorological Agency terminology distinguishes 快晴 from 晴れ by observed cloud amount; everyday 晴れ can still cover a day someone describes as 快晴. The word does not specify a warm temperature.
