# 過ごす (すごす) - spend time

## Meaning

過ごす means to spend time or lead a life in a particular way. It is a transitive godan verb: 過ごします, 過ごして, 過ごした. Mark the period with を, the place with で, and companions with と.

- 週末を家族と家で過ごしました。 (しゅうまつをかぞくといえですごしました。) - I spent the weekend at home with my family.
- 休みの日は、どう過ごしていますか。 (やすみのひは、どうすごしていますか。) - How do you spend your days off?

The second sentence asks about the way time is spent. The period is the topic, so it need not also appear as a separate を phrase.

## How the kanji combine

[過](../kanji/過.md) contributes passing. In this native verb it writes す, followed by ごす. The full reading is すごす, not the on reading カ used in [過去](過去.md) (かこ), "the past."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[経つ](経つ.md) (たつ) describes time passing on its own: 時間が経つ. 過ごす instead presents how someone spends that time. [暮らす](暮らす.md) (くらす) overlaps when describing living in a place or leading a life.

## Distinctive meaning

English "spend" also takes money, but this use of 過ごす concerns time. For spending money, [使う](使う.md) (つかう) is the relevant basic verb. 過ごす does not inherently mean wasting time: the period can be productive, pleasant, difficult, or restful.
