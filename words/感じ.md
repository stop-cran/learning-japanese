# 感じ (かんじ) - feeling; impression

## Meaning

感じ is a noun for a feeling, sensation, or impression. Use 感じがする for "have a feeling; get the impression," and a description before 感じ to say what the experience is like.

- この部屋は落ち着いた感じがします。 (このへやはおちついたかんじがします。) - This room has a calm feel.
- なんだか変な感じがします。 (なんだかへんなかんじがします。) - Something feels strange.

感じのいい人 (かんじのいいひと) is "a pleasant person": someone who gives a favorable impression. In 変な感じ, 変 takes な before the noun.

## How the kanji combine

[感](../kanji/感.md) contributes feeling or perception and is read かん. The kana じ completes the noun, related to 感じる (かんじる), "feel." This is not a two-kanji compound; do not confuse it with the same-sounding 漢字, "Chinese character."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[印象](印象.md) (いんしょう) concerns an impression left by something. [気分](気分.md) (きぶん) concerns a person's mood or condition. [感覚](感覚.md) (かんかく) can describe sensation or perceptual sense. Their meanings overlap without identical grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

感じ often presents a subjective impression rather than a verified fact. 暑い感じがする (あついかんじがする), "it feels hot," reports an experience. It need not give a measured temperature or prove that everyone shares the sensation.
