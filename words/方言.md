# 方言 (ほうげん) - dialect

## Meaning

方言 is a dialect or a regional expression. Regional differences can involve
vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation, not pronunciation alone.

- 祖母は地元の方言で話します。 (そぼはじもとのほうげんではなします。) - My grandmother speaks in the local dialect.
- この言葉は大阪の方言です。 (このことばはおおさかのほうげんです。) - This expression is Osaka dialect.

## How the kanji combine

[方](../kanji/方.md) has a region/locality association here;
[言](../kanji/言.md) contributes speech or language. ホウ + ゲン gives
ほうげん. Do not read 方 as the separate native word かた in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[言葉](言葉.md) (ことば) is broader: a word, expression or language, and can
also mean dialect. [アクセント](アクセント.md) concerns accent/emphasis;
it is not a name for every vocabulary or grammatical feature of a dialect.
標準語 (ひょうじゅんご), standard language, is a useful comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

方言 is not automatically incorrect Japanese or a different national language.
The word can also be extended to language characteristic of a particular social
group; regional speech remains the starting point for learning this word.
