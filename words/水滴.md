# 水滴 (すいてき) - water droplet

## Meaning

水滴 is a drop of water. It names the small quantity or bead of liquid, not the
action of dropping something.

- 窓に水滴がついています。 (まどにすいてきがついています。) - There are water droplets on the window.
- 水滴が机の上に落ちました。 (すいてきがつくえのうえにおちました。) - A drop of water fell onto the desk.

Japanese nouns do not require an English-style singular/plural ending; context
determines whether "a drop" or "droplets" fits.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md), water, modifies [滴](../kanji/滴.md), drop:
a drop of water. The on readings combine as スイ + テキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

滴 (しずく), also written 雫, is a drop of liquid more generally.
[水](水.md) names water itself, not specifically a droplet. A water droplet and
the water it consists of are related, not two freely interchangeable nouns.

## Distinctive meaning

水滴 does not specify why the drop formed: rain, splashing, and condensation can
all leave droplets. The dictionary also has a separate sense for a vessel used
to add water to an inkstone; the examples here concern drops of water.
