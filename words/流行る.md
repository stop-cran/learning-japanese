# 流行る (はやる) - be popular; be prevalent; flourish

## Meaning

流行る means to be popular, fashionable, or in vogue. It also describes
a disease being prevalent and a business flourishing.
It is an intransitive godan verb.

- この歌が若い人の間で流行っています。 (このうたがわかいひとのあいだではやっています。) - This song is popular among young people.
- 今年はインフルエンザが流行っています。 (ことしはインフルエンザがはやっています。) - Influenza is prevalent this year.

The subject takes が. Forms include 流行ります (はやります),
流行った (はやった), and 流行らない (はやらない).
Despite its ending, it is not an ichidan verb.
流行っている店 (はやっているみせ) is a thriving or popular shop.

## How the kanji combine

[流](../kanji/流.md) and [行](../kanji/行.md) suggest something
circulating or spreading. はやる is a special whole-word reading,
not a regular combination of their individual on readings.
The kana ending does not make it りゅうこうる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[流行](流行.md) (りゅうこう) is the related noun and する word
for fashion or prevalence. [人気](人気.md) (にんき) concerns
popularity, while [栄える](栄える.md) (さかえる) overlaps with
prospering. These do not make disease prevalence a favorable judgment.

## Distinctive meaning

Being widespread does not imply being desirable or lasting.
For music or clothes, 流行る concerns popularity; for a disease, it
concerns prevalence rather than people liking it.
The separate same-reading verb 逸る/早る, はやる, concerns impatience
or eagerness. It is not an alternative spelling for every fashion example.
