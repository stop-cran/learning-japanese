# 減らす (へらす) - reduce; decrease

## Meaning

減らす is a transitive godan verb meaning to reduce an amount, number, or extent.
Use Xを減らす for the thing reduced.
A target amount can be marked with に, while a reduction amount can be stated separately.

- 料理に使う塩の量を減らしました。 (りょうりにつかうしおのりょうをへらしました。) - I reduced the amount of salt used in cooking.
- 会議の回数を月一回に減らします。 (かいぎのかいすうをつきいっかいにへらします。) - We will reduce the number of meetings to once a month.

Useful forms are 減らして (へらして), 減らした (へらした), and 減らさない (へらさない).

## How the kanji combine

減 contributes decreasing. In this native transitive verb, the kana ending is らす.
The related intransitive verb is [減る](減る.md) (へる).
Keep the complete pair in mind rather than deriving a verb solely from the character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

減る describes the decrease itself: 塩の量が減る, "the amount of salt decreases."
増やす (ふやす), "increase something," is the corresponding transitive opposite.
減少 (げんしょう), "decrease," is a related noun/する expression.

## Distinctive meaning

減らす tells you that someone or something brings the quantity down; it does not require reducing it to zero.
It also does not specify whether the change is beneficial.
In numerical instructions, distinguish "reduce to three" from "reduce by three": the final amount and the amount removed are different.
