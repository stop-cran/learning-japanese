# 諸侯 (しょこう) — feudal lords

## Meaning

諸侯 is a historical collective term for feudal lords, barons or princes.

- 歴史の本で、諸侯の名前を調べました。 (れきしのほんで、しょこうのなまえをしらべました。) — I looked up the names of the feudal lords in a history book.
- 王は諸侯を集めました。 (おうはしょこうをあつめました。) — The king gathered the feudal lords.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[諸](../kanji/諸.md) means various or many, and [侯](../kanji/侯.md) refers to a marquis or feudal lord. Together, they form a historical term for feudal lords as a group.

## Synonyms and antonyms

王侯 (おうこう) is a related term for the king and his feudal lords.
