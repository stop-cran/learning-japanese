# お先に (おさきに) - ahead; pardon me for leaving first

## Meaning

お先に means "before; ahead" politely. It can also be the abbreviated
leaving-first formula お先に失礼します (おさきにしつれいします).

- どうぞ、お先に。 - Please, go ahead.
- お先に失礼します。 (おさきにしつれいします。) - Pardon me for leaving before you.
- では、お先に。 - Well then, I'll be going ahead.

In the first example the listener is invited to go first.
In a departure context the last example can announce that the speaker
is leaving first. Context determines who goes ahead.

## How the kanji combine

お adds politeness to [先](先.md) (さき), "ahead; before,"
and に makes the relation adverbial.
The written kanji [先](../kanji/先.md) has the native reading さき here,
not the compound reading セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

先に (さきに) gives the basic priority relation without お.
[前](前.md) (まえ) and [以前](以前.md) (いぜん) also have "before" uses,
but are not interchangeable farewell formulas.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every お先に as historical "previously."
In the departure formula, the speaker is leaving before people who remain.
The full 失礼します makes the social purpose clearer; the shortened
お先に relies more on the situation.
