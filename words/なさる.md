# なさる (なさる) — do (honorific)

## Meaning

なさる is the **respectful equivalent of する**, “do.” It raises the person performing the action, not the action's object. Use it for someone to whom respect is appropriate, not to honor your own actions.

- 先生は何をなさっていますか。 (せんせいはなにをなさっていますか。) — What is the teacher doing?
- お客様は何をなさいますか。 (おきゃくさまはなにをなさいますか。) — What will the customer do?

This is a special godan verb. The ordinary polite form is **なさいます**, not なさります; the negative is なさらない, the past なさった, and the て-form なさって.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The dictionary records the rare spelling 為さる, but the kana form is sufficient. The written ending does not make なさる an ichidan verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

する is neutral. いたす is a humble counterpart used for one's own side's actions in appropriate contexts. They differ in social perspective, not the basic activity.

## Distinctive meaning

Respectful vocabulary and polite endings are separate: なさる itself is honorific, while なさいます additionally uses polite speech. The command なさい is familiar instructional language in modern use; do not assume it is a universally respectful request.
