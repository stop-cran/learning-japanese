# 鷹 (たか) - hawk; falcon

## Meaning

鷹 is a hawk or falcon, a bird of prey. The word is also written **たか** or **タカ**; the dictionary marks kana as usual.

- 空を鷹が飛んでいます。 (そらをたかがとんでいます。) - A hawk is flying through the sky.
- 鷹の写真を撮りました。 (たかのしゃしんをとりました。) - I took a photograph of a hawk.

These sentences identify the bird, not a precise species from its appearance.

## How the kanji combine

This is one native noun, not a multi-kanji compound. The whole character [鷹](../kanji/鷹.md) is read **たか** here. Do not substitute the character's on readings ヨウ or オウ for this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鳥](鳥.md) (とり), "bird," is the broader category. 鷲 (わし), "eagle," is a related bird-of-prey name, not an unrestricted synonym for 鷹. There is no useful antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English bird names and Japanese common names do not form a simple one-to-one species key. Learn たか for the documented hawk/falcon word without assuming it names every bird of prey. Writing an animal name in katakana does not make it a foreign loanword.
