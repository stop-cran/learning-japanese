# 書店 (しょてん) — bookstore

## Meaning

書店 is a bookshop: 駅前の書店 (a bookstore in front of the station), 大型書店 おおがたしょてん (a large bookstore), 書店で本を買う (buy a book at a bookstore).

## How the kanji combine

[書](../kanji/書.md) (here "book") modifies [店](../kanji/店.md) (shop): "a writing shop". Both are on readings (ショ + テン).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 本屋 ほんや (everyday word), ブックストア (loan).
- Related: 古本屋 ふるほんや (second-hand bookshop), 図書館 としょかん (library).

## Distinctive meaning

書店 is a little more formal than 本屋 and common in signs and written text; in conversation 本屋 is more usual.
