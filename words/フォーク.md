# フォーク - fork

## Meaning

フォーク means a fork, especially the utensil used for eating.
Use で when identifying it as the tool with which you eat.

- フォークでサラダを食べます。 (フォークでサラダをたべます。) - I eat salad with a fork.
- フォークを一本ください。 (フォークをいっぽんください。) - Please give me one fork.

The second example uses 本 (ほん), with the form 一本 (いっぽん), to count
one slender utensil.

## How the kanji combine

The English loanword is written in katakana. Small ォ combines with フ to
form フォ, and ー lengthens its vowel: フォーク.
There are no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ナイフ](ナイフ.md), "knife," cuts food; スプーン, "spoon," scoops it.
箸 (はし), "chopsticks," is another eating utensil.
These are useful contrasts in a place setting, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese does not add a plural ending for several forks.
In a baseball context, フォーク can shorten フォークボール, "forkball";
that separate use is not the utensil meaning in these examples.
