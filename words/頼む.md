# 頼む (たのむ) — request; ask a favor

## Meaning

頼む (たのむ) means to ask someone for something or request that they do something.
A useful pattern is person に + task を + 頼む: に marks the person asked, and を marks the requested task.

- 友達に手伝いを頼みました。 (ともだちにてつだいをたのみました。) — I asked a friend for help.
- 母に買い物を頼みました。 (ははにかいものをたのみました。) — I asked my mother to do the shopping.

For a requested action expressed as a clause, one pattern is 手伝ってくれるように頼む
(てつだってくれるようにたのむ), "ask someone to help."

## How the kanji combine

[頼](../kanji/頼.md) contributes requesting or relying on someone and writes たの in this verb.
む completes the native verb. Notice the changed ending in 頼んで (たのんで), the て-form,
and 頼んだ (たのんだ), the past.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[願う](願う.md) (ねがう) also has a request sense, but it can express a wish for an outcome without asking a particular person.
頼る (たよる), "rely on," concerns dependence; reliance does not necessarily involve making a new request.
[質問](質問.md) (しつもん), "question," is useful for the other English sense of "ask": seeking information.

## Distinctive meaning

頼む asks for something to be done or provided; it does not mean the person agreed or completed the task.
The plain form is not automatically a polite way to address someone.
The examples use 頼みました to report a request, rather than teaching bare 頼む as a universally suitable way to say "please."
