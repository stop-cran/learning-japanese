# ぼろ - rag; hidden flaw; worn-out

## Meaning

ぼろ names a rag or old cloth, tattered clothes, or a fault or weakness someone is trying to hide. It also describes something worn-out, shabby, or run-down.

- 古い布をぼろとして使いました。 (ふるいぬのをぼろとしてつかいました。) - I used the old cloth as a rag.
- 話しているうちに、ぼろが出た。 (はなしているうちに、ぼろがでた。) - As I kept talking, the flaws in my story came out.
- 靴がもうぼろになった。 (くつがもうぼろになった。) - My shoes have already become worn-out.

ぼろが出る (ぼろがでる) is a useful expression for hidden faults coming to light; ぼろを出す (ぼろをだす) is to give oneself away or reveal those faults. Before nouns, recognize both ぼろな and combinations such as ぼろ服 (ぼろふく), ragged clothes.

## How the kanji combine

Usually written ぼろ or ボロ, the word also has the spelling 襤褸. Its ぼろ reading is a special whole-word reading, not a reason to assign ぼ and ろ separately to the two characters. The actual kana headword has no written kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぼろぼろ](ぼろぼろ.md) describes a tattered or crumbling condition. [雑巾](雑巾.md) (ぞうきん) is a cleaning cloth; an old rag can serve that purpose, but not every cleaning cloth is worn-out.

[欠点](欠点.md) (けってん) is a flaw or weak point. The concealed-fault use of ぼろ highlights what becomes exposed, rather than every mistake or every cause of a bad result.

## Distinctive meaning

ぼろ can be a thing, a condition, or an exposed flaw; the construction tells you which. Calling someone's clothes or home ぼろ is disparaging. Do not confuse this word with every separate intensifying ぼろ- prefix or historical homograph.
