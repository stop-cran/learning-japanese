# ラジカセ - radio-cassette player

## Meaning

ラジカセ is a combined radio and cassette player or recorder. It names a physical audio device, not a radio program or a cassette tape alone.

古いラジカセで音楽を聞きました。 (ふるいラジカセでおんがくをききました。) - I listened to music on an old radio-cassette player.

このラジカセはまだ使えます。 (このラジカセはまだつかえます。) - This radio-cassette player still works.

## How the kanji combine

The katakana word shortens ラジオカセット: ラジ from ラジオ, "radio," plus カセ from カセット, "cassette." It is a Japanese shortened compound of borrowed elements, not a direct copy of one English word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ラジオ can mean a radio receiver or broadcasting. カセットプレーヤー is a cassette player without necessarily including a radio. A CD player is a different device category.

## Distinctive meaning

The word remains useful for older equipment and products with cassette functions. It does not mean any modern portable speaker or music player. Some units add other playback features, so the exact functions depend on the model.
