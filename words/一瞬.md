# 一瞬 (いっしゅん) - an instant

## Meaning

一瞬 is a noun and adverb meaning "an instant" or "for an instant." It presents a time interval as extremely brief. 一瞬の attaches to a noun; 一瞬で describes something happening or finishing in an instant.

- 一瞬、何が起きたのか分かりませんでした。 (いっしゅん、なにがおきたのかわかりませんでした。) - For an instant, I did not understand what had happened.
- 鳥は一瞬で飛び去りました。 (とりはいっしゅんでとびさりました。) - The bird flew away in an instant.

Compare 一瞬止まった (いっしゅんとまった), "stopped for an instant," with 一瞬で終わった (いっしゅんでおわった), "finished in an instant." The で construction highlights the very short time taken.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) contributes "one," and 瞬 carries the idea of a blink or moment: one tiny span of time. In いっしゅん, the small っ marks a brief consonant hold before しゅ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[瞬間](瞬間.md) (しゅんかん) also means a moment or instant. [しばらく](しばらく.md), "for a while," describes an unspecified stretch, not necessarily an instant. [永遠](永遠.md) (えいえん), "eternity," provides a contrast in duration.

## Distinctive meaning

一瞬 is not a fixed scientific unit such as one second. It expresses how brief an event seems. A request to wait "a moment" may use [ちょっと](ちょっと.md); translating every English "moment" as 一瞬 can overstate the brevity.
