# 奴 (やつ) — a guy; that one (familiar or rough)

## Meaning

やつ can refer informally to a person, with affection among people who know each other or with contempt. It can also pick out a thing. For these uses, やつ is often written in kana.

- 弟は本当にいいやつだ。 (おとうとはほんとうにいいやつだ。) — My younger brother is a really good guy.
- 箱は小さいやつにしよう。 (はこはちいさいやつにしよう。) — Let's go with the small box.

## How the kanji combine

[奴](../kanji/奴.md) writes the whole noun やつ. In the second example やつ carries over “box” from the first part of the sentence; it is not a description of a person.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[人](人.md) (ひと) is a safer neutral word for a person. [物](物.md) (もの) means a thing; やつ can stand for an object already clear from the context, but it is not neutral in tone.

## Distinctive meaning

The relationship and the speaker's tone matter. Calling a friend いいやつ can sound warm. Do not take that as a model for introducing a customer or a stranger.
