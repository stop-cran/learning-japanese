# 上 (うえ) — above; on top

## Meaning

上 names a position above something or on its top surface. Use noun + の上, then the particle needed by the sentence.

- 机の上に本があります。 (つくえのうえにほんがあります。) — There is a book on the desk.
- 棚の上に箱を置きました。 (たなのうえにはこをおきました。) — I put a box on top of the shelf.

に marks the location of existence or the destination of placement in these examples. 上 is a noun, not an English-style preposition that directly precedes "desk."

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md) represents the complete native word うえ here. Other words use readings such as ジョウ; those are not substitutes in 机の上.

## Synonyms and antonyms

下 (した), "below/under," gives the opposite vertical position. 中 (なか), "inside," and 横 (よこ), "beside," describe different spatial relations.

## Distinctive meaning

上 can mean either contact with a top surface or a position higher than it. It also extends to relative age and rank, as in 年が上 (としがうえ), "older."
