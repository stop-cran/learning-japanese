# 上 (うえ) — above; on top

## Meaning

上 names a position above something or on its top surface. Use noun + の上, then the particle needed by the sentence.

- 机の上に本があります。 (つくえのうえにほんがあります。) — There is a book on the desk.
- 棚の上に箱を置きました。 (たなのうえにはこをおきました。) — I put a box on top of the shelf.

に marks the location of existence or the destination of placement in these examples. 上 is a noun, not an English-style preposition that directly precedes "desk."

**Other readings belong to distinct words or constructions:**

- **うわ** is a noun/prefix expressing an upper, outer, or surface part. [上着](上着.md) (うわぎ), "jacket; outer garment," illustrates the outer-layer use: 上着を脱ぎました (うわぎをぬぎました), "I took off my jacket."
- **かみ** can mean a river's upper reaches, an upper part, or the upper half of the body. [川上](川上.md) (かわかみ), "upstream," illustrates the river meaning, not a position above the water. The entry also lists a respectful high-ranking-person sense and a government/imperial-court sense. お上 (おかみ), "the authorities," is a useful recognition expression for the latter context, not a general modern adjective meaning "governmental."
- **じょう** can identify the best/top grade or the first volume of a two- or three-volume set. 上・下 (じょう・げ) can label first and second volumes. この小説の上を読みました (このしょうせつのじょうをよみました) means "I read the first volume of this novel." [上等](上等.md) (じょうとう), "high quality," also illustrates the top-grade association.

As a suffix, **じょう** also means "in terms of/from the standpoint of": 安全上の問題 (あんぜんじょうのもんだい), "a safety-related problem." Attach 上 directly to the preceding noun, then の when modifying another noun.

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md) represents the complete native word うえ here. Other words use readings such as ジョウ; those are not substitutes in 机の上.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下](下.md) (した), "below/under," gives the opposite vertical position. [中](中.md) (なか), "inside," and [横](横.md) (よこ), "beside," describe different spatial relations.

## Distinctive meaning

上 can mean either contact with a top surface or a position higher than it. It also extends to relative age and rank, as in 年が上 (としがうえ), "older."

The reading is part of the vocabulary identity. First-volume and top-grade meanings belong to じょう here, while the government-related sense belongs to かみ. They do not license changing 机の上 from うえ to another reading. This article retains its primary うえ lesson and N5 label; the added readings do not each receive that level.
