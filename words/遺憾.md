# 遺憾 (いかん) — regrettable; deplorable

## Meaning

遺憾 describes something that did not turn out as it should have and is regrettable. Digital Daijisen defines it as 期待したようにならず、心残りであること。残念に思うこと (きたいしたようにならず、こころのこりであること。ざんねんにおもうこと), “things not turning out as hoped, leaving dissatisfaction; feeling it is a pity,” and gives 遺憾の意を表する (いかんのいをひょうする), “express regret.” JMdict glosses it “regrettable; unsatisfactory; deplorable; lamentable.”

- 政府は、隣国の一方的な発表に対し、遺憾の意を表明した。 (せいふは、りんごくのいっぽうてきなはっぴょうにたいし、いかんのいをひょうめいした。) — The government expressed regret over the neighboring country’s one-sided announcement.
- このような事故が起きたことは、誠に遺憾です。 (このようなじこがおきたことは、まことにいかんです。) — It is truly regrettable that such an accident has occurred.

## How the kanji combine

[遺](../kanji/遺.md) (い) here means to remain behind, and [憾](../kanji/憾.md) (かん) means to feel strongly that something is unsatisfactory; [Digital Daijisen, 遺](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E9%81%BA) lists 遺憾 under sense 1 of イ, 後に残る (あとにのこる), “remain behind.” JMdict classifies 遺憾 as both a na-adjective and a noun. Before a noun it takes な, as in 遺憾な点 (いかんなてん), “a regrettable aspect”; it is followed by の in the set phrase 遺憾の意 (いかんのい), “regret,” in the first example, and ends a sentence with です in the second.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[残念](残念.md) (ざんねん) is the everyday word for regrettable or disappointing, and [悔しい](悔しい.md) (くやしい) is the frustration of losing or failing. The [実用日本語表現辞典](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E9%81%BA%E6%86%BE) entry notes that 遺憾 is used mainly in formal settings, and that 遺憾の意を示す (いかんのいをしめす), “express regret,” is common in political and business statements.

## Distinctive meaning

Two set phrases are 遺憾なく (いかんなく), “fully; amply,” as in 遺憾なく力を発揮する (いかんなくちからをはっきする), “show one’s ability to the full,” and 遺憾ながら (いかんながら), “I regret to say; unfortunately.”
