# 落す (おとす) — drop something

## Meaning

落す is a spelling of 落とす, "drop; let fall". Prefer the usual fuller spelling 落とす in ordinary writing. It is transitive: the thing dropped takes を.

- 鍵を床に落としました。  
  かぎをゆかにおとしました。  
  "I dropped my keys on the floor."
- コップを落とさないでください。  
  コップをおとさないでください。  
  "Please don't drop the glass."

It can also describe losing something by dropping or leaving it somewhere.

## How the kanji combine

[落](../kanji/落.md) relates to falling. Both written forms are read おとす. This is a godan verb: 落とします (おとします), 落として (おとして), 落とさない (おとさない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

落ちる (おちる), "fall", is the intransitive partner: 鍵が落ちた (かぎがおちた), "The keys fell". [拾う](拾う.md), "pick up", describes a possible next action, not an opposite for every sense.

## Distinctive meaning

Transitive does not mean deliberate. 鍵を落とした can report an accident; it identifies the person's involvement without saying that they intended to drop the keys.
