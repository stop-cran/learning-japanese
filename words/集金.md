# 集金 (しゅうきん) - collecting payments

## Meaning

集金 is collecting money, especially payments, fees, or money due from customers. It can also name the collected money. 集金する describes collecting; an explicit payment object takes を, and the person paying can take から.

- 会員から会費を集金しました。
  (かいいんからかいひをしゅうきんしました。) - I collected membership fees from the members.
- 係の人が集金に来ました。
  (かかりのひとがしゅうきんにきました。) - The person responsible came to collect the payment.

In the second example, 集金に gives the purpose of coming. 集金する can also be used without naming a direct object.

## How the kanji combine

[集](../kanji/集.md) contributes gathering, and [金](../kanji/金.md) supplies what is gathered: money. Read シュウ + キン, not the separate native words あつめる and かね.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[集める](集める.md) (あつめる) is the broader verb "collect; gather." 集金 identifies money collection in particular. [貯金](貯金.md) (ちょきん) concerns saving money, not collecting payments from other people.

## Distinctive meaning

Receiving a payment and making a donation are different viewpoints: [寄付](寄付.md) (きふ) names a donation or contribution. [コレクション](コレクション.md) names a collection such as stamps or watches; collecting coins as a hobby does not automatically mean collecting bills or fees. Learn the payment situation as well as the broad English word "collection."
