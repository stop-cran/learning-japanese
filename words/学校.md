# 学校 (がっこう) — school

## Meaning

学校 is a school, both the institution and the building: 学校に行く (go to school), 学校が休みだ (there is no school), 小学校 しょうがっこう (elementary school), 学校の先生.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) (ガク, study) + [校](../kanji/校.md) (コウ, school): both on readings. The ク of ガク doubles into the small っ before コウ, so the word is がっこう, not がくこう.
Compare 高校 こうこう and 大学 だいがく: 校 refers to a school as an institution, 学 to learning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 学園 がくえん (school grounds or campus; often in names), 校舎 こうしゃ (school building), 学び舎 まなびや (literary).
- Related: 大学 だいがく (university), a more specific educational-institution term.

## Distinctive meaning

学校 is a general word; context or a more specific term such as 大学 tells you the kind of institution.
Ask 「どこの学校ですか」 about someone's school.
