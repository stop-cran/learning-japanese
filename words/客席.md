# 客席 (きゃくせき) - audience seating; audience

## Meaning

客席 names seating for guests or customers, such as the audience seating in a theater or stadium. It can also mean the audience occupying that area. Other contexts include passenger seating.

- 劇場の客席はほぼ満員でした。
  (げきじょうのきゃくせきはほぼまんいんでした。) - The theater's audience seating was almost full.
- 客席から大きな拍手が起こりました。
  (きゃくせきからおおきなはくしゅがおこりました。) - Loud applause came from the audience.

In the second sentence, the people are applauding, not the chairs.

## How the kanji combine

[客](../kanji/客.md), guest or customer, specifies whose [席](../kanji/席.md), seat or place, is meant. The on readings combine as キャク + セキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[座席](座席.md) (ざせき) names a seat or seating more generally. [観客](観客.md) (かんきゃく) directly names spectators, whereas 客席 can describe their seating area or, by extension, the audience there. [スタンド](スタンド.md) also names spectator stands.

## Distinctive meaning

At a performance, 客席 contrasts with the stage where performers appear. Do not assume that every occurrence identifies one numbered seat: 客席全体 (きゃくせきぜんたい) can mean the whole audience area.
