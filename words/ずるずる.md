# ずるずる — dragging; slurping; dragging on

## Meaning

Dragging or sliding movement, a slurping sound, or an unresolved situation continuing.

- そばをずるずるすすった。 — I slurped the soba noodles.
- 重い袋（ふくろ）をずるずる引きずった。 — I dragged the heavy bag along.
- 結論（けつろん）を出さずに、話し合いがずるずる続いた。 — The discussion dragged on without a conclusion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[するする](するする.md) emphasizes smooth movement without catching. [だらだら](だらだら.md) overlaps in drawn-out activity; [ぐずぐず](ぐずぐず.md) often highlights dawdling or hesitation.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb is essential: すする concerns slurping, 引きずる（ひきずる）dragging, and 続く（つづく）continuation. A repeated sound here does not imply quick or energetic movement.
