# 外科 (げか) - surgery; surgical department

## Meaning

外科 is a surgical department or the branch of medicine concerned with surgical treatment.
At a hospital, it can tell you which department to visit.

- 外科を受診しました。 (げかをじゅしんしました。) - I consulted the surgical department.
- 姉は外科の医師です。 (あねはげかのいしです。) - My older sister is a doctor in surgery.

Visiting 外科 does not by itself mean that an operation was performed.
外科医 (げかい) means a surgeon.

## How the kanji combine

[外](../kanji/外.md), "outside," qualifies [科](../kanji/科.md), "branch; department."
Use that as a memory aid for the named medical specialty, not a rule that surgeons only treat external body parts.
The on readings here are ゲ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[手術](手術.md) (しゅじゅつ) is an operation, rather than the department or specialty.
English "surgery" can refer to either, so check the Japanese noun.
内科 (ないか), internal medicine, is a contrasting specialty, not a claim that a patient can only ever need one of the two.

## Distinctive meaning

外科 concerns a medical field or department; 外科医 names the practitioner, and 手術 names an operation.
Do not say that someone "had 外科" when you mean that they underwent an operation.
The English translation needs the hospital or treatment context.
