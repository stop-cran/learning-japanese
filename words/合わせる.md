# 合わせる (あわせる) - bring together; match

## Meaning

合わせる is a transitive ichidan verb meaning to bring things together or make them match. In **AをBに合わせる**, A is adjusted and B is the reference.

- 時計を正しい時間に合わせました。 (とけいをただしいじかんにあわせました。) - I set the clock to the correct time.
- みんなで力を合わせましょう。 (みんなでちからをあわせましょう。) - Let's join forces.

顔を合わせる (かおをあわせる), literally bringing faces together, means meeting or seeing one another: 毎日顔を合わせます (まいにちかおをあわせます), "We see each other every day."

## How the kanji combine

[合](../kanji/合.md) contributes joining or fitting. The native verb is あわせる, with わせる written in kana. Its forms include 合わせて and 合わせない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[合う](合う.md) (あう) describes things fitting or matching; 合わせる describes making that happen. サイズが合う means "the size fits," whereas サイズを合わせる means "adjust the size to fit." [分ける](分ける.md) (わける), separating or dividing, contrasts with bringing things together in suitable contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

The object determines the practical action: adjusting a clock, coordinating efforts, or meeting someone. The "face" meaning belongs in constructions such as 顔を合わせる; it is not a general replacement for English "be opposite" when describing two buildings.
