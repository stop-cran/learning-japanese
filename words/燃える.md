# 燃える (もえる) - burn; be on fire

## Meaning

燃える is an intransitive ichidan verb: something burns or is on fire. Mark the burning thing with が. The verb does not itself name someone causing the fire.

- 暖炉で薪が燃えています。 (だんろでまきがもえています。) - Firewood is burning in the fireplace.
- 彼は研究への情熱に燃えています。 (かれはけんきゅうへのじょうねつにもえています。) - He is burning with enthusiasm for his research.

The second example is figurative: the person is fired up, not physically burning. Forms include 燃えます, 燃えて, and 燃えない.

## How the kanji combine

燃 supplies the idea of burning. The complete native verb is もえる, with える written as okurigana. It is not a multi-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

燃やす (もやす) is the transitive comparison: someone burns something. Compare 紙が燃える (かみがもえる), "paper burns," with 紙を燃やす (かみをもやす), "burn paper." [火](火.md) (ひ) names fire itself, rather than the action.

## Distinctive meaning

燃えている describes ongoing burning in the fireplace example. 燃えるごみ (もえるごみ), "burnable rubbish," uses 燃える to characterize material; it does not mean the rubbish is currently ablaze. Actual disposal categories depend on local rules, so the language label alone is not an instruction about how or where to burn something.
