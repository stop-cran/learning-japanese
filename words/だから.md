# だから (だから) — therefore

## Meaning

だから connects a preceding reason to its result: "so; therefore; that is why." It commonly begins a new sentence.

今日は休みです。だから、家にいます。（きょうはやすみです。だから、いえにいます。） — Today is my day off. So I am staying home.

## How the kanji combine

There are no kanji. The form contains だ and the reason-marking から. As a sentence connector, learn だから as a unit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ですから is a more polite alternative. でも, "but," introduces a contrast instead of the expected result.

## Distinctive meaning

Inside one sentence, the preceding word controls the form: 休みだから（やすみだから）, "because it is a day off," but 寒いから（さむいから）, "because it is cold," without an extra だ after the i-adjective. Said emphatically at the start of a reply, だから can sound impatient, like "I already told you."
