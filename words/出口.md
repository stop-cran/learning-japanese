# 出口 (でぐち) — exit

## Meaning

出口 is a way out: the exit of a building, station or highway, and figuratively a way out of a problem.

- 出口はこちらです。 (でぐちはこちらです。) — The exit is this way.
- 駅の出口はどこですか。 (えきのでぐちはどこですか。) — Where is the station exit?
- トンネルの出口が見えます。 (とんねるのでぐちがみえます。) — I can see the end of the tunnel.
- 出口が見えません。 (でぐちがみえません。) — I can't see an exit. / Figuratively: I see no way out.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) (go out; で) plus [口](../kanji/口.md) (opening; ぐち, voiced in compounds): "the opening you go out through". Compare [入口](入口.md),
the opposite.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [入口](入口.md) いりぐち (entrance).
- Related: [出入口](出入口.md) (one opening used both ways), 非常口 ひじょうぐち (emergency exit), 改札口 かいさつぐち (ticket gate).
- For "way out" of a problem: 解決策 かいけつさく (solution), 活路 かつろ (a way out, literary).

## Distinctive meaning

Where there are several exits, signs distinguish them by place or purpose: 東口 ひがしぐち (east exit), 非常口 (emergency exit).
Figuratively, 出口 pairs with 入口: the way out of a situation versus the way in.
