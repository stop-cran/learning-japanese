# 要点 (ようてん) - main point; gist

## Meaning

要点 is a noun for the main point or essential points of an explanation, text, or issue. Use Nの要点 to identify whose or what points are meant. It does not grammatically distinguish one point from several.

- 説明の要点をメモしました。 (せつめいのようてんをメモしました。) - I noted down the main points of the explanation.
- 要点を三つにまとめてください。 (ようてんをみっつにまとめてください。) - Please summarize the essentials in three points.

Useful combinations include 要点をつかむ, "grasp the main point," and 要点を押さえる (ようてんをおさえる), "cover or grasp the essentials."

## How the kanji combine

[要](../kanji/要.md) contributes essential or important, and [点](../kanji/点.md) contributes a point or item. The first component qualifies the second: an essential point.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中心](中心.md) (ちゅうしん) can mean the focus or core of a discussion. 要点 identifies the important points to take away from it. [メモ](メモ.md) is a note: it may record 要点, but the note and its essential content are different things.

## Distinctive meaning

A short statement is not necessarily a good summary of the 要点; it may omit what matters. Conversely, there can be several 要点 even when English uses the singular "gist." Learn the word with a source such as 話の要点 (はなしのようてん), "the main points of the talk," rather than translating 点 as a physical dot.
