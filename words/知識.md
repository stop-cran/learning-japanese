# 知識 (ちしき) — knowledge

## Meaning

知識 is knowledge or information that someone has. Use it as a noun, for example in
知識がある (ちしきがある), "have knowledge," or 知識を増やす (ちしきをふやす), "increase one's knowledge."

- 日本の歴史についての知識を増やしたいです。 (にほんのれきしについてのちしきをふやしたいです。) — I want to learn more about Japanese history.

Here についての connects the subject area, Japanese history, to the noun 知識.

## How the kanji combine

[知](../kanji/知.md), "know," and [識](../kanji/識.md), "knowing; awareness,"
reinforce each other. The compound names knowledge rather than the event of suddenly
noticing something. Its on readings are チ + シキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

情報 (じょうほう) means information, news, or reports. Such information can add to someone's
知識, but a newly received report and the knowledge a person already has are not the same focus.
経験 (けいけん) is experience: doing or living through something, not simply knowing facts about it.
Neither is a direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

知識 is not itself a verb meaning "know." Use a noun construction such as 知識がある,
not an invented 知識する. In the example, 増やす supplies the action, while 知識 names
what the learner wants to increase.
