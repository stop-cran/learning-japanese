# ぐしゃぐしゃ — crumpled; messy; soggy

## Meaning

Something out of shape or disordered, including crumpled paper, messy hair, or a sodden mess.

- 紙をぐしゃぐしゃに丸（まる）めた。 — I crumpled the paper into a ball.
- 雨でノートがぐしゃぐしゃになった。 — The rain turned my notebook into a soggy mess.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[びしょびしょ](びしょびしょ.md) emphasizes being soaked. [ぐにゃぐにゃ](ぐにゃぐにゃ.md) describes pliability, which does not necessarily mean messy or damaged.

## Distinctive meaning

ぐしゃぐしゃにする presents an action causing the state; ぐしゃぐしゃになる presents the change. A ぐしゃぐしゃの髪（かみ, hair）need not be wet, and crumpled paper need not have been torn into pieces.
