# 運転 (うんてん) — driving; operation

## Meaning

運転 means **driving a vehicle** or **running a machine**. Add する to use it as a verb.

- 父は毎日、車を運転します。（ちちはまいにち、くるまをうんてんします。）— My father drives a car every day.
- この機械は今、運転中です。（このきかいはいま、うんてんちゅうです。）— This machine is running now.

In 車を運転する, を marks the vehicle being driven. 運転中 means "in operation" or "while driving,"
depending on what is operating.

## How the kanji combine

[運](../kanji/運.md) contributes movement or carrying, and [転](../kanji/転.md) contributes turning.
Together they evoke setting something in motion or keeping it running; this is a meaning aid,
not a claim that driving is merely "turning a car."
The reading is on + on: ウン + テン → うんてん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 操作する（そうさする）means to operate or handle something, often by working its controls; it has a broader range of devices.
- 乗る（のる）means to get into or ride a vehicle. A passenger can 車に乗る without driving it.
- 運ぶ（はこぶ）means to carry or transport something; it does not specify who drives.

There is no single natural antonym covering both driving and every kind of machine operation.

## Distinctive meaning

運転 focuses on **driving or operating**, not merely traveling somewhere.
Do not confuse it with 運動（うんどう）, "exercise" or "movement."
This article focuses on the everyday vehicle and machine senses, not specialized financial uses.
