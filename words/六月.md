# 六月 (ろくがつ) — June

## Meaning

六月 is June: 六月は雨が多い (it rains a lot in June; the rainy season 梅雨 つゆ starts around then), 六月六日 ろくがつむいか (June 6th).

## How the kanji combine

[六](../kanji/六.md) (six) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month): the sixth month. ロク before ガツ does not contract; contraction (ろっ) happens before counters starting with k/s/t/p/h sounds, as in 六回 ろっかい.
"Six months" is 六か月 ろっかげつ with the contraction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 水無月 みなづき (old name of the sixth month).
- Related: 六日 むいか (the 6th day).

## Distinctive meaning

六月 is the calendar month; 六か月 is the duration.
