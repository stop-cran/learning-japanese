# 居間 (いま) — living room

## Meaning

居間 is a living room or sitting room, such as the living room in a Western-style home.
It names the room rather than the activity happening there.

- 家族は居間でテレビを見ています。 (かぞくはいまでテレビをみています。) — The family is watching television in the living room.
- 居間に大きな窓があります。 (いまにおおきなまどがあります。) — There is a large window in the living room.

## How the kanji combine

[居](../kanji/居.md) contributes being or staying, and [間](../kanji/間.md) has a room or space sense here.
The result names a room for spending time in.
Both readings are native Japanese: い + ま. Two written kanji do not automatically make a word a compound of on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[部屋](部屋.md) (へや) means a room more generally; it does not tell you that the room is a living room.
寝室 (しんしつ) means a bedroom and contrasts rooms by their use.
These are room labels, not a strict pair of opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

居間 has the same kana reading as [今](../kanji/今.md), "now," but they are different words.
In 居間でテレビを見る (いまでテレビをみる), で marks a location: watching television in the living room.
The spelling and the surrounding sentence clarify which いま is intended.
