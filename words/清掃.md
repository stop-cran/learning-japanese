# 清掃 (せいそう) - cleaning; cleanup

## Meaning

清掃 means cleaning or cleanup, such as removing dirt or rubbish from a place.
清掃する is transitive: the place cleaned takes を.

- 毎朝、駅の前を清掃しています。 (まいあさ、えきのまえをせいそうしています。) - We clean the area in front of the station every morning.
- 公園の清掃に参加しました。 (こうえんのせいそうにさんかしました。) - I took part in cleaning up the park.

清掃中 (せいそうちゅう) means cleaning is in progress. The dictionary also records
garbage collection; the context determines whether a wider cleanup service is meant.

## How the kanji combine

[清](../kanji/清.md) contributes making clean, and [掃](../kanji/掃.md) contributes
sweeping or brushing away. The related actions combine around removing dirt.
Read セイ + ソウ, keeping both long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[掃除](掃除.md) (そうじ) closely overlaps in cleaning.
[洗う](洗う.md) (あらう) specifies washing, while [片付ける](片付ける.md)
(かたづける) focuses on putting things in order or away. These activities can overlap,
but putting books away does not necessarily remove dust.

## Distinctive meaning

清掃 is useful in work descriptions and signs; it does not imply a particular cleaning
tool. It need not involve a broom, and it is not restricted to municipal rubbish collection.
Choose the translation from the service or activity being described.
