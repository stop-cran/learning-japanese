# 適用 (てきよう) - application of a rule or method

## Meaning

適用 means applying a rule, law, method, or similar standard to a case. **適用する** is transitive: **規則を事例に適用する** (きそくをじれいにてきようする), "apply a rule to a case."

- 新しい規則を全員に適用します。 (あたらしいきそくをぜんいんにてきようします。) - We will apply the new rule to everyone.
- この割引は学生に適用されます。 (このわりびきはがくせいにてきようされます。) - This discount applies to students.

The second sentence is passive: the discount is what is applied, and に identifies those it applies to.

## How the kanji combine

[適](../kanji/適.md) contributes fitting or suitability, and [用](../kanji/用.md) contributes use. テキ + ヨウ gives the idea of putting something into effect for an applicable case.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[使用](使用.md) (しよう) broadly means using something. 適用 is more specific about bringing a rule or method to bear. [適当](適当.md) (てきとう) shares 適 but is not another form of the verb 適用する.

## Distinctive meaning

English "application" also names a job request, software, or putting cream on skin. Those meanings are not automatically 適用. Ask what rule or method is being applied and to what target. The pattern を ... に makes those two roles clear.
