# 種類 (しゅるい) — kind; variety

## Meaning

種類 groups things by type. It can describe varieties of food, kinds of vehicles, or categories of other things.
A number before 種類 counts kinds, not the total number of individual items.

- この店では三種類のパンを売っています。 (このみせではさんしゅるいのパンをうっています。) — This shop sells three kinds of bread.
- どんな種類の本が好きですか。 (どんなしゅるいのほんがすきですか。) — What kinds of books do you like?

Use の between the type phrase and the things classified: 三種類のパン, "three kinds of bread."

## How the kanji combine

[種](../kanji/種.md) has its "kind" sense, not its physical "seed" sense.
[類](../kanji/類.md) also concerns a class or kind.
Their overlapping meanings form the category word 種類, read with シュ + ルイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

種 (たね) is a seed in the plant sense; it cannot simply replace 種類 in 三種類のパン.
To compare types, use [同じ](同じ.md) おなじ, "same," or [違う](違う.md) ちがう, "different":
同じ種類 (おなじしゅるい), "the same kind," versus 違う種類 (ちがうしゅるい), "different kinds."
These contrast the classification rather than supply an antonym for the noun itself.

## Distinctive meaning

三種類のパン says there are three varieties; the shop may have many loaves of each.
パンを三つ (パンをみっつ) instead counts three individual items.
This difference between types and items is more useful than translating 種類 as a vague "number."
