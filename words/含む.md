# 含む (ふくむ) - contain; include; hold in the mouth

## Meaning

含む is a transitive godan verb: something contains or includes something else. The content takes を. In 口に含む (くちにふくむ), it means holding something in the mouth.

- この料金は朝食を含みます。 (このりょうきんはちょうしょくをふくみます。) - This charge includes breakfast.
- 水を少し口に含みました。 (みずをすこしくちにふくみました。) - I took a little water into my mouth and held it there.

Forms include 含みます (ふくみます), 含まない (ふくまない), and 含んで (ふくんで).

## How the kanji combine

含 contributes containing or holding and is read ふく here. The final む belongs to the verb. The physical and abstract meanings concern something held within, but should be learned through their actual constructions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入れる](入れる.md) (いれる) means putting something in; 含む can describe the resulting contents without that action. 含める (ふくめる) means to include something in a group or calculation, with a different verb ending.

## Distinctive meaning

The "bear in mind; understand" sense appears in the formal expression お含みおきください (おふくみおきください), "please bear this in mind; please understand." It uses 含み置く (ふくみおく), not a freely interchangeable replacement for everyday [知る](知る.md), "know." Also, 口に含む does not by itself say that the liquid was swallowed.
