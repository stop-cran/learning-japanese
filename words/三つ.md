# 三つ (みっつ)

## Meaning

三つ means three in the native counting series, useful for counting things without naming a more specific counter.

- パンを三つください。 (パンを みっつ ください。) — Please give me three pieces of bread.
- 質問が三つあります。 (しつもんが みっつ あります。) — I have three questions.

It can also give a young child's age: 三つです (みっつです), "They are three," according to context.

## How the kanji combine

[三](../kanji/三.md) supplies the native numerical element, and つ belongs to the native counting series. Keep the small っ in みっつ; do not read it さんつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

三 (さん) is the number three. 三個 (さんこ) counts individual items, while 三人 (さんにん) counts people. [七つ](七つ.md) uses the same general counting series at seven.

## Distinctive meaning

The series works for some abstract things as well as objects, as 質問 shows. It does not replace all counters: 三人 is three people, and 三時 (さんじ) is three o'clock. There is no natural antonym.
