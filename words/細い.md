# 細い (ほそい) - thin; slender

## Meaning

細い describes something narrow or slender, especially a line, a long object, or part of the body. It is an i-adjective.

- 細い線を引いてください。 (ほそいせんをひいてください。) - Please draw a thin line.
- この道はとても細いです。 (このみちはとてもほそいです。) - This road is very narrow.

Its negative is 細くない (ほそくない), and its past is 細かった (ほそかった).

## How the kanji combine

細 represents ほそ, followed by い. This is a native adjective. The same kanji in 細かい (こまかい), "fine; detailed; small," belongs to a different word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

太い (ふとい), "thick," is the relevant contrast for a rope, line, or limb. 薄い (うすい) describes thinness through a flat object's thickness, as with thin paper, rather than a slender shape.

## Distinctive meaning

English "thin" covers both 細い and 薄い. Choose according to the dimension: a thin string is 細い, while a thin sheet is 薄い. The contrast is not simply one word for small things and another for large things.
