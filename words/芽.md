# 芽 (め) - sprout; shoot; bud

## Meaning

芽 is a noun for a plant's sprout, shoot, or bud.
Use 芽が出る (めがでる) when a shoot emerges, and 芽を出す (めをだす) when a plant or seed puts one out.
新しい芽 (あたらしいめ) means "a new shoot/bud."

- まいた種から小さな芽が出ました。 (まいたたねからちいさなめがでました。) - A small shoot emerged from the seed I sowed.
- 木の枝に新しい芽が出ています。 (きのえだにあたらしいめがでています。) - New buds are appearing on the tree's branches.

から marks the source in the first sentence; に marks the location in the second.
The exact English choice depends on the plant and growth stage.

## How the kanji combine

芽 represents the whole native noun め.
The plant-related upper component can help recognition, but the word is not a compound of two separately pronounced words.
Do not confuse the sound め with a different noun solely because the pronunciation matches.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[種](種.md) (たね) is a seed, from which a shoot may grow.
[葉](葉.md) (は) is a leaf; a bud or developing shoot is not necessarily an unfolded leaf.
[目](目.md) (め), "eye," is a homophone, not a plant synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

芽 names early growth rather than a fully grown plant.
It can appear on an existing tree as well as emerge from a newly germinating seed.
That distinction helps explain why translating every occurrence as "seedling" can be too narrow.
