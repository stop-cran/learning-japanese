# 体育 (たいいく) - physical education

## Meaning

体育 is a noun meaning physical education, PE, or gym class.
It names an educational subject or activity, not a particular sport.
**体育の授業** (たいいくのじゅぎょう) means "PE class," and **体育で** means "in PE."

- 今日の体育ではバスケットボールをしました。 (きょうのたいいくではバスケットボールをしました。) - We played basketball in PE today.
- 明日は体育の授業があります。 (あしたはたいいくのじゅぎょうがあります。) - We have PE class tomorrow.

The first sentence identifies basketball as an activity within the class, not as a definition of 体育.
The standard written reading is **たいいく**, with both い characters.

## How the kanji combine

[体](../kanji/体.md) contributes body, and [育](../kanji/育.md) contributes educating or developing.
The combination concerns physical development through education.
タイ + イク gives たいいく; do not silently shorten the dictionary reading to たいく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[運動](運動.md) (うんどう) means exercise or movement more broadly.
[スポーツ](スポーツ.md) names sports, whether or not they occur at school.
体操 (たいそう) can mean exercises or gymnastics; it is not an unrestricted synonym for the school subject 体育.

## Distinctive meaning

English "gym" can mean either a class or a building. 体育 corresponds to the class/physical-education sense,
not the building itself.
Likewise, "athletics" can name particular sports, while 体育 can include many different activities.
Use 体育の授業 when the educational setting needs to be explicit.
