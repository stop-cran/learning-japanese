# 修理 (しゅうり) - repair; mending

## Meaning

修理 is repairing or mending something damaged or not working properly.
It is a noun and a transitive する verb: **objectを修理する**.
**修理に出す** (しゅうりにだす) means "send/take something in for repair."

- 自転車のブレーキを修理しました。 (じてんしゃのブレーキをしゅうりしました。) - I repaired the bicycle's brakes.
- 時計を修理に出しています。 (とけいをしゅうりにだしています。) - My watch is away being repaired.

The second construction says the item has been entrusted for repair; it does not say the speaker is doing the work.
修理中 (しゅうりちゅう) means "under repair."

## How the kanji combine

修 contributes mending or putting right, and [理](../kanji/理.md) contributes bringing into order.
The related meanings combine into restoring something to working condition.
Read シュウ + リ as しゅうり, keeping the long う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[直す](直す.md) (なおす), "fix," is broader: it can concern objects, wording, or habits.
[修正](修正.md) (しゅうせい) concerns correction or revision, such as changing a document.
[壊す](壊す.md) (こわす), "break," provides the opposing action for a physical object.

## Distinctive meaning

修理 focuses on restoring an object or mechanism, not treating a patient's illness or correcting someone's pronunciation.
A 修理の依頼 (しゅうりのいらい) is a request for repair; it does not guarantee that the item can be fixed.
The noun names the work, not its successful completion.
