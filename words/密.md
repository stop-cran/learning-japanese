# 密 (みつ) - dense; close

## Meaning

密 describes things packed densely, or a close relationship or connection.
It is a な-adjective: 密な + noun. Use 密に before an action, or 密にする
for making a connection closer.

- 木が密に生えています。 (きがみつにはえています。) - The trees are growing densely.
- 担当者と連絡を密にしてください。 (たんとうしゃとれんらくをみつにしてください。) - Please maintain close contact with the person in charge.

The second example asks for closer communication, not for people to stand
physically close together.

## How the kanji combine

密 supplies the whole word with the on reading ミツ. Its density and closeness
meanings are useful anchors; there is no multi-kanji compound to split.
The reading is みつ, without a small っ at the end.

## Synonyms and antonyms

疎 (そ), "sparse," is a direct contrast for density.
[近い](近い.md) (ちかい), "near," concerns distance and can overlap with
closeness, but proximity alone does not establish 密な関係 (みつなかんけい),
"a close relationship."

## Distinctive meaning

密 can describe how closely things are spaced or how closely people coordinate.
It does not by itself state that the situation is good or bad.

dictionary-supported density and relationship senses, without treating the
blank as evidence for a particular historical source intention.
