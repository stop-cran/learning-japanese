# 斬る (きる) - cut down with a blade; slash

## Meaning

斬る describes cutting with a blade, particularly cutting a person down with a sword or another blade. It also covers slicing or lopping something off. A translation such as "kill" needs this blade-related context.

- 映画で、侍が敵を斬る場面を見ました。 (えいがで、さむらいがてきをきるばめんをみました。) - In the film, I saw a scene in which a samurai cut down an enemy.

This is a transitive godan verb: 斬ります (きります), 斬って (きって), 斬らない (きらない). を marks the person or thing cut.

## How the kanji combine

This is one kanji, 斬, with the native reading き and the ending る, not an on-reading compound. There is no 斬 kanji card in the current collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[切る](切る.md) (きる) is the general cutting verb, for example cutting paper. [殺す](殺す.md) (ころす), "kill," does not specify a blade. [倒す](倒す.md) (たおす) and [討つ](討つ.md) (うつ) can concern defeating an opponent without specifying this cutting action.

## Distinctive meaning

Beheading is one possible blade-killing context, not the definition of every occurrence of 斬る. Conversely, it is not a general verb for murder by any means. Do not transfer every use of 切る to this spelling. The unrelated [着る](着る.md), "wear," has the same reading but is ichidan: 着ない (きない), not 斬らない.
