# 同一 (どういつ) - identical; one and the same

## Meaning

同一 means identical, the same, or one and the same. **AとBは同一だ** identifies sameness. Before a noun, **同一のN** is useful; the dictionary also recognizes な-adjective use.

- この二つは同一の製品です。 (このふたつはどういつのせいひんです。) - These two are the same product.
- 二つの名前は同一人物を指しています。 (ふたつのなまえはどういつじんぶつをさしています。) - The two names refer to one and the same person.

The second statement identifies one person, not two people who merely resemble each other.

## How the kanji combine

[同](../kanji/同.md) contributes sameness, and [一](../kanji/一.md) contributes one. The combination reinforces "the same one" or identity. The readings are ドウ + イツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ) is a useful everyday equivalent in many contexts, with different attachment patterns: 同じ製品, without の. [似る](似る.md) (にる) means to resemble, which is weaker than being identical.

## Distinctive meaning

Specify what is the same. Two manufactured objects can be 同一の製品 as a product type while remaining two physical objects. 同一人物 identifies a single person. The source gloss "similarity" is therefore too loose: shared features alone do not establish the identity claimed by 同一.
