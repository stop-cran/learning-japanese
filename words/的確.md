# 的確 (てきかく) - accurate; apt

## Meaning

的確 describes an accurate, well-targeted judgment, description, or response. It takes な before a noun and に when describing how something is done.

- 的確な判断が必要です。 (てきかくなはんだんがひつようです。) - An accurate judgment is needed.
- 先生は問題点を的確に説明しました。 (せんせいはもんだいてんをてきかくにせつめいしました。) - The teacher explained the problem points precisely.

適確 is another spelling of this same word, with the same てきかく reading and meaning. Dictionaries also record てっかく; do not confuse that alternative whole-word reading with a different meaning.

## How the kanji combine

的 suggests hitting the target; [確](../kanji/確.md) contributes certainty or accuracy. The on readings are テキ + カク. In 適確, [適](../kanji/適.md), "suitable," highlights fitting the situation. These character associations help explain the spellings, not a historical derivation or a strict division into two separate words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[正確](正確.md) emphasizes accuracy, while [適切](適切.md) emphasizes appropriateness. Both can overlap 的確, especially in a good assessment or response. [明確](明確.md) means clear or definite: a clearly expressed claim can still be wrong.

## Distinctive meaning

The central idea is being on target, not merely sounding confident or providing many details. 的確な答え is an apt, accurate answer; its length alone does not establish 的確さ. Use the spelling variants for recognition without inferring their relative frequency from dictionary order.
