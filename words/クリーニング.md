# クリーニング (クリーニング) - cleaning; laundry service

## Meaning

クリーニング means cleaning, including dry cleaning and a laundry service.
For clothes, クリーニングに出す means sending an item to be professionally cleaned.
It is a noun and can form the transitive verb クリーニングする.

- このコートをクリーニングに出します。 (このコートをクリーニングにだします。) - I will send this coat to the cleaners.
- ソファーをクリーニングしました。 - I cleaned the sofa.

The word itself does not specify whether professional cleaners were involved.
クリーニング does not always mean a dry process.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[洗濯](洗濯.md) (せんたく) means washing clothes or laundry.
[掃除](掃除.md) (そうじ) means cleaning, such as cleaning a room.
[洗う](洗う.md) (あらう) is the verb "wash."
These meanings overlap; they are not divided simply into professional versus do-it-yourself work.

## Distinctive meaning

This loanword comes from English "cleaning," but its clothing-service use is especially useful to recognize.
クリーニング店 (クリーニングてん) is a cleaner's shop.
Choose the word with the object and activity in mind, rather than assuming every English use of "clean" calls for this loanword.
