# 幅 (はば) - width; breadth; range

## Meaning

幅 is a noun for width or breadth. It can also name a range or difference, such as a spread of prices. Use Nの幅 to specify what is being measured.

- この机の幅は一メートルです。 (このつくえのはばはいちメートルです。) - This desk is one meter wide.
- この店の商品は値段の幅が広いです。 (このみせのしょうひんはねだんのはばがひろいです。) - This shop's products span a wide range of prices.

幅が広い means "be wide" or "have a broad range"; 幅が狭い (はばがせまい) expresses a narrow width or range.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 幅 represents the native noun はば. There is no compound relationship to explain. Keep the noun separate from the adjective 広い: one names the dimension, and the other describes it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

長さ (ながさ), "length," names another dimension; it is not an antonym of width. [広い](広い.md) (ひろい), "wide; spacious," can describe width or a larger area depending on context. "Narrow" is the useful contrast when describing a small 幅.

## Distinctive meaning

A large 幅 need not be a physical distance. 値段の幅 describes variation rather than the width of a price tag. The same broader image helps with 声の幅 (こえのはば), "vocal range." Identify what varies before translating 幅 as a measurement in meters.
