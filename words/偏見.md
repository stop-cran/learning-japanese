# 偏見 (へんけん) — prejudice; bias

## Meaning

偏見 is a biased or distorted view of someone or something. JMdict glosses it as “prejudice; bias; distorted view.”

- 偏見を持たずに、相手の話を聞きましょう。 (へんけんをもたずに、あいてのはなしをききましょう。) — Let's listen to the other person without prejudice.
- 外見だけで人を判断するのは偏見です。 (がいけんだけでひとをはんだんするのはへんけんです。) — Judging people only by their appearance is prejudice.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[偏](../kanji/偏.md) means leaning to one side, and [見](../kanji/見.md) means seeing or a view. Together, they describe a view that leans to one side.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[差別](差別.md) (さべつ) is distinction or discrimination, including discrimination against people (JMdict entry 1291410); 偏見 is the biased view itself, not the unequal treatment. [片寄る](片寄る.md) (かたよる) is the verb for leaning to one side or being biased (JMdict entry 1590420). [意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion or view in general (JMdict entry 1156530); 偏見 is a view that leans to one side.

## Distinctive meaning

偏見 names a way of seeing or judging. It does not by itself say that anyone was treated unfairly.
