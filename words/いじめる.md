# いじめる (いじめる) — bully; pick on

## Meaning

いじめる means to bully, torment, or pick on someone. "Tease" can translate it when the teasing is unkind; do not assume it means friendly joking. It is transitive: the person targeted takes を.

- 弟をいじめないでください。  
  おとうとをいじめないでください。  
  "Please don't pick on my younger brother."
- 友達をいじめてはいけません。  
  ともだちをいじめてはいけません。  
  "You must not bully your friends."

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is hiragana. 苛める and 虐める are dictionary spellings, but neither is needed to use this word. It is ichidan: いじめます, いじめて, いじめない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

からかう means "tease" or "make fun of"; context determines whether that is playful or hurtful. 親切にする (しんせつにする), "treat kindly", gives a useful contrast, not a fixed lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The related noun いじめ names bullying. Learn the verb with the affected person, rather than treating it as a neutral word for having fun with someone.
