# 実習 (じっしゅう) - hands-on practical training

## Meaning

実習 is practical training or learning by actually working with the
subject, equipment, or field situation. It is a noun and forms 実習する.
実習をする and 実習に参加する are useful constructions.

- 看護学生は病院で実習をします。 (かんごがくせいはびょういんでじっしゅうをします。) - Nursing students do practical training at a hospital.
- 調理の実習でパンを焼きました。 (ちょうりのじっしゅうでパンをやきました。) - We baked bread in a practical cooking class.

教育実習 (きょういくじっしゅう) is teaching practice.
The noun can name the practical part of a course or a placement.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality, and [習](../kanji/習.md)
contributes learning or practice: learning through actual practice.
ジツ + シュウ becomes じっしゅう, with a small っ and a long final う.
Do not confuse the reading with [自習](自習.md) (じしゅう), self-study.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[練習](練習.md) (れんしゅう) is practice more generally;
[研修](研修.md) (けんしゅう) is training or professional study that may
include lectures and observation as well as practical work.
[見学](見学.md) (けんがく) emphasizes learning by visiting or observing.
These activities can occur in one course without being the same activity.

## Distinctive meaning

実習 emphasizes hands-on learning, not proof of mastery or a qualification.
It is not merely any event that happened in real life.
自習 concerns learning independently; 実習 concerns practical participation.
Those are different axes, not a rule that all 実習 requires a teacher
or that independent learning can never be practical.
