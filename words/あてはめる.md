# あてはめる - apply a rule; fit into

## Meaning

あてはめる means actively applying something, such as a rule or formula, to a case.
It can also mean fitting something into an appropriate place or category.
It is a **transitive ichidan verb**: あてはめます, あてはめて, あてはめた.

- この規則をすべての場合にあてはめることはできません。 (このきそくをすべてのばあいにあてはめることはできません。) - We cannot apply this rule to every case.
- 公式に数値をあてはめて計算します。 (こうしきにすうちをあてはめてけいさんします。) - We calculate by substituting numerical values into the formula.

In these patterns, を marks what is applied or fitted, and に marks the target.
The object is a rule in the first example and numerical values in the second.

## How the kanji combine

The dictionary spellings include 当てはめる and 当て嵌める.
[当](../kanji/当.md) provides a useful connection with fitting or matching;
learn the whole native verb rather than interpreting every kana segment as a separate word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あてはまる](あてはまる.md) describes something fitting or being applicable,
without making it the object of an applying action.
[合う](合う.md) (あう) likewise describes fitting or matching rather than the same transitive pattern.

## Distinctive meaning

English "apply" also covers applying paint, [塗る](塗る.md) (ぬる),
and applying for a course, [申し込む](申し込む.md) (もうしこむ).
Those are different actions, not automatic Japanese uses of あてはめる.
Whether a rule can appropriately be applied is separate from merely attempting to apply it.
