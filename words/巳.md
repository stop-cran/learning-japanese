# 巳 (み) — Snake zodiac sign

## Meaning

巳 is the **Snake sign**, the sixth of the twelve zodiac signs.
It identifies a place in the cycle, not a living snake.

- 私の干支は巳です。 (わたしのえとはみです。) — My zodiac sign is the Snake.
- 十二支の六番目は巳です。 (じゅうにしのろくばんめはみです。) — The sixth of the twelve zodiac signs is the Snake.

十二支 (じゅうにし) names the twelve-sign set. In the first example,
干支 (えと) has its everyday twelve-sign zodiac sense. 干支 can also
name the sixty-term calendar cycle combining stems and branches;
these are related systems, not identical lists.

## How the kanji combine

[巳](../kanji/巳.md) writes the whole native sign noun み, with no
okurigana or second character to divide off.

Recognize 巳年 (みどし), **year of the Snake**: the sign modifies
[年](../kanji/年.md), year. Learn the complete compound reading;
the separate noun [年](年.md) has the primary reading とし.
巳年 also has the documented whole-word reading へびどし.
That alternative does not make へび the reading to substitute into
every word containing 巳.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[蛇](蛇.md) へび is the animal noun. Zodiac expressions can also name
a sign by its associated animal, but this does not make 巳 み a general
word for a snake you saw. [亥](亥.md) い, the Boar sign, is a different
member of the cycle, not a synonym or an antonym.
A zodiac sign has no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Use み for the sign noun in this lesson, not for the living animal
in a sentence such as "I saw a snake." Keep its spelling 巳 distinct
from the similar-looking 己 and 已.

The on-reading シ is independently documented in 上巳 (じょうし),
a seasonal festival name associated with the Doll's Festival.
The alternative reading じょうみ is also documented. Neither example
changes this article's primary reading み.

Older texts also use 巳 for an hour around 10 a.m., a direction
approximately south-southeast, or the fourth lunar month.
These are historical senses, not ordinary modern scheduling or navigation
vocabulary. Dictionary hour conventions include both 9–11 a.m. and
10 a.m.–noon; lunar month four is not simply April.
For ordinary clock-time discussion, [時刻](時刻.md) じこく means time of day.
