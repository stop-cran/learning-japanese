# 万一 (まんいち) — by any chance, if the worst should happen

## Meaning

万一 refers to a very unlikely, usually bad possibility. As a noun, 万一の場合 (まんいちのばあい)
is "in the unlikely event"; 万一のために means "in preparation for an emergency/just in case."

- 万一のために保険に入ります。 (まんいちのためにほけんにはいります。) — I will take out insurance just in case.
- 万一遅れたら連絡します。 (まんいちおくれたられんらくします。) — If I should be late, I will contact you.

In the second example, 万一 adds the low-likelihood framing; 遅れたら supplies the actual "if" condition.
Other conditional constructions, such as ～場合は (ばあいは), can also follow it.

## How the kanji combine

[万](../kanji/万.md) (ten thousand) plus [一](../kanji/一.md) (one) suggests a very small chance.
Both are read with the on readings マン and イチ. The dictionary's literal numerical image is "10000 to 1";
this is not a measured probability asserted by an ordinary sentence using 万一.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もしも](もしも.md) introduces an "if" situation without necessarily stressing an emergency.
万が一 (まんがいち) is a close alternative with が. ひょっとして can mean "by any chance" when wondering
about a possibility. [必ず](必ず.md) (かならず), "certainly/without fail," contrasts in certainty but is not
a grammatical opposite that can replace 万一 in a conditional clause.

## Distinctive meaning

万一 and 万が一 often frame an unwelcome or serious contingency. Use もしも for ordinary hypotheticals
when that emphasis is not intended. 万一 does not itself mean that the feared event will happen.
