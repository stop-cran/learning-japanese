# 万一 (まんいち) — by any chance, if the worst should happen

## Meaning

万一 refers to a very unlikely, usually bad possibility: 万一のために保険に入る (take out insurance just in case), 万一遅れたら連絡します (if I should be late, I'll contact you).
As a noun, 万一の場合 まんいちのばあい is "in the unlikely event".

## How the kanji combine

[万](../kanji/万.md) (ten thousand) plus [一](../kanji/一.md) (one): "one in ten thousand", a tiny chance. Both are read with the on readings マン and イチ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: もしも (if), 万が一 まんがいち (same, spoken, with が), ひょっとして (by any chance).
- Antonym: 必ず かならず (certainly).

## Distinctive meaning

万一 and 万が一 usually carry a negative or serious tone. Use もしも for ordinary hypotheticals.
