# どろどろ — thick and sludgy; emotionally murky

## Meaning

A thick, viscous, mushy consistency, or something coated with mud or grime. Figuratively, it can describe ugly, tangled emotions or relationships.

- 煮つめすぎて（につめすぎて）、スープがどろどろになった。 — I reduced the soup too far, and it became very thick.
- 雨の中を歩いて、靴が泥（どろ）でどろどろだ。 — I walked in the rain, and my shoes are covered in mud.
- そのドラマは、どろどろした人間関係（にんげんかんけい）を描いて（えがいて）いる。 — That drama portrays ugly, tangled relationships.

## How the kanji combine

A native mimetic repeating どろ. JMdict also records uncommon 泥々 and 泥泥 spellings for the viscous/muddy word. The mud association does not mean that every thick liquid literally contains mud.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ねばねば](ねばねば.md) emphasizes sticky viscosity; [べちゃべちゃ](べちゃべちゃ.md) wet, soggy mush. [さらさら](さらさら.md) contrasts with a thick liquid by suggesting easy flow.

## Distinctive meaning

どろどろのスープ, どろどろした関係, and どろどろに汚れる（よごれる）have different grammatical settings. A separate mimetic entry describes rumbling thunder or drums, as in どろどろと鳴る（なる）. That audible sense should not be confused with the thick-liquid sense, and not every use can be replaced by “muddy.”
