# できる (できる) — be able to; be ready

## Meaning

できる means **to be able to do something**. Use a suitable activity noun + ができる, or dictionary-form verb + ことができる.

- 私は料理ができます。 (わたしはりょうりができます。) — I can cook.
- ここで切符を買うことができます。 (ここできっぷをかうことができます。) — You can buy tickets here.

It is an ichidan verb: できます, できない, できた. In the second example, 切符を is the object of 買う, not of できる. A bare dictionary-form verb does not attach directly to できる.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The alternative spelling is [出](../kanji/出.md)[来](../kanji/来.md)る: 出 is read で, as in 出る (でる), “go out,” and 来 is read き, as in 来ます (きます), “come.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

A verb's potential form can express similar ability: 買える (かえる), “can buy.” できない means unable or not possible. する, “do,” describes the action rather than ability.

## Distinctive meaning

できる also means something is completed or ready: ご飯ができました (ごはんができました), “The meal is ready.” Context separates ability from completion. できた therefore does not always mean “was able to.”
