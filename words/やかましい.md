# やかましい - noisy; strict; fussy

## Meaning

やかましい can describe unpleasant noise, strict demands, or being fussy
and particular about something.
It is an i-adjective: やかましい音 (やかましいおと), "a noisy sound";
やかましくない, "not noisy/strict/fussy."

- 工事の音がやかましくて、眠れません。 (こうじのおとがやかましくて、ねむれません。) - The construction noise is so loud that I cannot sleep.
- 父は食べ物にやかましいです。 (ちちはたべものにやかましいです。) - My father is particular about food.
- この学校は服装にやかましいです。 (このがっこうはふくそうにやかましいです。) - This school is strict about clothing.

In **Nにやかましい**, に identifies the matter about which someone is demanding.
The second example does not say that the food makes noise.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. 喧しい is the dictionary's kanji spelling.
The lesson does not require constructing the native adjective from individual characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[うるさい](うるさい.md) overlaps in noise, annoyance, and fussiness.
[厳しい](厳しい.md) (きびしい) overlaps in strictness.
[難しい](難しい.md) (むずかしい) also has a fussy or particular sense,
not only difficulty.
[静か](静か.md) (しずか) contrasts with noise, not with every demanding standard.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can also describe something much discussed, or procedures and complaints
that feel bothersome. Context selects the use.
Saying やかましい！ directly to someone can be a harsh demand for quiet;
the word is not automatically a neutral measurement of sound volume.
Being particular about food need not involve audible shouting.
