# 引き算 (ひきざん) - subtraction

## Meaning

引き算 is subtraction: finding what remains after taking one number
away from another. It is a noun and can take する.

- 今日は学校で引き算を習いました。 (きょうはがっこうでひきざんをならいました。) - Today I learned subtraction at school.
- この問題は引き算で答えが出ます。 (このもんだいはひきざんでこたえがでます。) - You can get the answer to this problem by subtraction.

For an equation, 五引く二は三 (ごひくにはさん) means
"five minus two is three."

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md), through the native stem ひき, contributes
subtracting; [算](../kanji/算.md) contributes calculation.
This is a native-first mixed compound: ひき + サン, with サン
voiced to ざん. As with [掛け算](掛け算.md) (かけざん),
the app's `wago` label does not claim both elements are native readings.
The source spelling 引算 represents the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計算](計算.md) (けいさん) is calculation more generally;
[差し引き](差し引き.md) (さしひき) includes subtraction or deduction
and a resulting balance. 足し算 (たしざん), addition, is the
contrasting arithmetic operation. [足す](足す.md) (たす) is "add."

## Distinctive meaning

The arithmetic verb [引く](引く.md) (ひく) also means subtract,
although its existing article starts with physical pulling.
引き算 does not mean pulling an object merely because 引 appears
in its spelling. [算数](算数.md) (さんすう) is the broader elementary
mathematics subject, not another name for subtraction alone.
