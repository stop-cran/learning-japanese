# 破片 (はへん) - fragment; shard; splinter

## Meaning

破片 is a fragment or broken piece: a shard of glass, a splinter, or a
piece left by something breaking. It is a noun.

- 床にガラスの破片が落ちています。 (ゆかにガラスのはへんがおちています。) - There are shards of glass on the floor.
- 割れた皿の破片を拾いました。 (われたさらのはへんをひろいました。) - I picked up the pieces of the broken plate.

ガラスの破片 (ガラスのはへん) identifies the material or source.
The noun itself does not specify how many fragments there are.

## How the kanji combine

[破](../kanji/破.md) contributes breaking, and [片](../kanji/片.md)
contributes a piece or fragment. The modifier and head together give
"broken piece." Read ハ + ヘン: はへん, without a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

欠片 (かけら) also means a fragment or small piece.
断片 (だんぺん) can name a fragment, including a fragment of information.
These overlap, but the glass example gives 破片 a concrete broken-object
setting. A whole object contrasts with its fragments; there is no single
everyday antonym that fits every material.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 破片 for every small object or everything left over.
The word points to a fragment of a larger thing, with a breaking or
fragmentation association. Nor does it guarantee a sharp edge: the
material and the way it broke determine that.
