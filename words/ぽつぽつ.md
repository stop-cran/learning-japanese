# ぽつぽつ — isolated drops or scattered occurrences

## Meaning

Separated drops, dots, or scattered instances. It can also mean doing something bit by bit or at intervals.

- 顔に雨がぽつぽつと当たり始めた。 — Isolated raindrops began to hit my face.
- 丘（おか）の上に家がぽつぽつと見える。 — I can see houses dotted here and there on the hill.
- 彼は昔のことをぽつぽつと話し始めた。 — He began talking about the past in slow, separated bits.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぱらぱら](ぱらぱら.md) overlaps in sparse distribution and light falling rain. [しとしと](しとしと.md) foregrounds sustained gentle rain. [ざあざあ](ざあざあ.md) is a contrast for a continuous, noisy downpour.

## Distinctive meaning

ぽつぽつ降る, ぽつぽつとある, and ぽつぽつ話す select different meanings. As a noun, ぽつぽつ can mean dots or spots. The rain use often occurs when rain is starting, but the word does not itself require that a heavier shower follow. The speaking use concerns intermittent delivery, not necessarily a quiet voice.
