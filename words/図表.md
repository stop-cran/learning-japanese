# 図表 (ずひょう) - chart; diagram; graph

## Meaning

図表 names charts, diagrams, graphs, and graphical or tabular presentations
of information. It can refer to figures and tables together or to such a
visual presentation individually.

- 結果を図表にまとめました。 (けっかをずひょうにまとめました。) - I organized the results into charts and tables.
- 報告書に図表を入れてください。 (ほうこくしょにずひょうをいれてください。) - Please include charts or diagrams in the report.

図表で示す (ずひょうでしめす) means to show something using a chart or diagram.
With の, it can modify another noun: 図表の説明 (ずひょうのせつめい),
"an explanation of the chart."

## How the kanji combine

[図](../kanji/図.md) contributes a figure or diagram, and [表](../kanji/表.md)
a table or chart. Their related presentation forms combine as ズ + ヒョウ.
表 here is the chart word ひょう, not おもて, "front; outward-facing side."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[図](図.md) (ず) is a visual representation more broadly.
[表](表.md), read ひょう for the table/chart noun, organizes information.
[図形](図形.md) (ずけい) focuses on a shape or geometric figure, not every
way of arranging data.

## Distinctive meaning

A 図表 need not be a realistic picture: lines, symbols, numerical values,
or table cells can convey the information. It does not name the explanation's
written prose merely because the prose refers to a chart.
There is no general antonym.
