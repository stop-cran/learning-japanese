# 民間 (みんかん) -- private sector; folk

## Meaning

民間 concerns ordinary society or people outside official government
activity. Depending on the contrast, it means private, non-governmental,
unofficial or civilian. A further use is folk or popular: something
circulating among ordinary people.

- 民間の会社で働いています。 (みんかんのかいしゃではたらいています。) -- I work for a private-sector company.
- これは民間に伝わる話です。 (これはみんかんにつたわるはなしです。) -- This is a story handed down among the people.

Use 民間の before a noun. 民間療法 (みんかんりょうほう) means a folk
remedy; the label describes its circulation, not evidence of efficacy.

## How the kanji combine

[民](../kanji/民.md), people, and [間](../kanji/間.md), among or
between, help recall the ordinary-people sphere. The on-readings are
ミン + カン, not the separate native noun あいだ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [社会](社会.md) (しゃかい) and [世間](世間.md) (せけん) overlap in society or the public, without always contrasting it with government.
- [私立](私立.md) (しりつ) concerns privately established institutions; it is not a general word for every civilian or folk practice.
- [公式](公式.md) (こうしき), official, contrasts with the non-official use.
- [公共](公共.md) (こうきょう) concerns the public or community. English "public" does not invariably mean government-owned.

## Distinctive meaning

"Private" here does not automatically mean secret, intimate or personally
owned by a single individual. Nor does "popular" automatically mean a
current commercial hit: 民間伝承 (みんかんでんしょう) concerns folklore
or traditions transmitted among people.
