# ぎょっと — taken aback; startled

## Meaning

A sudden startle reaction, often with alarm or an unsettling surprise.

- 暗い部屋で急に声がして、ぎょっとした。 — A sudden voice in the dark room startled me.
- 請求書（せいきゅうしょ）の金額（きんがく）を見て、ぎょっとした。 — I was taken aback by the amount on the bill.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[びっくり](びっくり.md) is a broader surprise expression, including pleasant surprise. [びくびく](びくびく.md) usually concerns an ongoing fearful state rather than one sudden reaction.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ぎょっとする for the reaction and ぎょっとさせる for startling someone. The alarmed tendency is useful, but a surprise does not have to involve actual danger.
