# 咳 (せき) - cough; coughing

## Meaning

咳 is a cough or coughing. Learn it with a verb: **咳が出る** (せきがでる) means to cough or have a cough, and 咳をする means to cough. 咳が止まらない means the coughing does not stop.

- 昨日から咳が出ています。 (きのうからせきがでています。) - I have had a cough since yesterday.
- 夜になると、咳がひどくなります。 (よるになると、せきがひどくなります。) - My cough gets worse at night.

The が construction describes the symptom occurring. 咳 is a noun, not an independently conjugating verb.

## How the kanji combine

咳 is a single kanji read せき here; kana せき is also a useful way to recognize the word. There is no compound relationship to explain. The dictionary additionally records しわぶき, but this card teaches the assigned せき reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

A sneeze is くしゃみ, not 咳. [喉](喉.md) (のど) names the throat; 喉が痛い describes a sore throat rather than coughing itself. These symptoms can occur together but are not synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

咳 describes a symptom, not its cause. A statement such as 咳が出る does not by itself identify a particular illness. In reporting symptoms, duration and severity can be added separately, as 昨日から and ひどくなる do above.
