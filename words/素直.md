# 素直 (すなお) - obedient; frank

## Meaning

素直 describes an unaffected, receptive, or obedient disposition. It also
describes being frank or honest about one's feelings. It is a na-adjective:
素直な before a noun, 素直に before an action.

- 彼は素直な性格で、助言をよく聞きます。 (かれはすなおなせいかくで、じょげんをよくききます。) - He has a receptive disposition and listens well to advice.
- 自分の気持ちを素直に話しました。 (じぶんのきもちをすなおにはなしました。) - I spoke frankly about my feelings.

The word also describes straight hair and an unforced or unpeculiar form,
such as neat, straightforward handwriting. The surrounding noun selects
the sense; hair is not being described as morally obedient.

## How the kanji combine

素 has plain or unadorned associations; [直](../kanji/直.md) contributes
straightness or directness. Together they help recall the unaffected,
straightforward quality. This is a component association, not a historical
derivation.

The reading combines on ス with native なお. This repository groups an
on-first mixed reading under kango; it is not two regular on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[正直](正直.md) (しょうじき) overlaps in honesty and frankness.
[利口](利口.md) (りこう), especially お利口, can overlap in praise for
well-behaved children or pets. 頑固 (がんこ), "stubborn," contrasts with
receptiveness in some situations, not with every sense of 素直.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not reduce the word to passive obedience. 素直に気持ちを伝える
(すなおにきもちをつたえる) can mean expressing one's feelings without
concealment. Nor does being 素直 prove that advice is correct or that obeying
it is always desirable.
