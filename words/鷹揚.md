# 鷹揚 (おうよう) - magnanimous; composed

## Meaning

鷹揚 describes a large-hearted, easygoing or composed manner, without fussing over
small things. It is a な-adjective: 鷹揚な + noun, 鷹揚に + action, and 鷹揚だ/です.

- 彼は小さな失敗には鷹揚です。 (かれはちいさなしっぱいにはおうようです。) - He is magnanimous about small mistakes.
- 彼女は鷹揚な態度で話を聞きました。 (かのじょはおうようなたいどではなしをききました。) - She listened with an unruffled manner.

## How the kanji combine

[鷹](../kanji/鷹.md) names a hawk, and 揚 contributes rising or lifting.
Japanese dictionary explanations associate the expression with a hawk flying broadly in the sky.
That image helps with the unhurried, dignified manner; the modern adjective does not
literally describe a bird. In おうよう, the on readings are オウ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冷静](冷静.md) (れいせい) overlaps in composure, with an emphasis on clear-headedness.
[のんき](のんき.md) overlaps in an easygoing attitude, sometimes with a criticism of insufficient concern.
鷹揚 need not imply carelessness. 慌てる (あわてる), "become flustered," provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the whole word: the noun [鷹](鷹.md) remains たか, not おう.
JMdict also recognizes 鷹揚 read おおよう and ようよう.
The alternative spelling 大様 shares おおよう, but the entry restricts おうよう and
ようよう to 鷹揚. Do not freely substitute every reading into every spelling.
Being 鷹揚 does not guarantee sound judgment or generosity with money in every situation.
