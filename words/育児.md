# 育児 (いくじ) - childcare; child-rearing

## Meaning

育児 means caring for and bringing up a child. It is a noun and can take する.
育児をする also expresses doing childcare.

- 育児をしながら働いています。 (いくじをしながらはたらいています。) - I work while raising a child.
- 夫婦で育児を分担しています。 (ふうふでいくじをぶんたんしています。) - The couple share childcare responsibilities.

育児休業 (いくじきゅうぎょう) is childcare leave; 育児の経験
(いくじのけいけん) means experience of raising children.

## How the kanji combine

[育](../kanji/育.md) contributes raising or nurturing, and [児](../kanji/児.md)
identifies a child. The relationship is action and its object: raising a child.
The on readings are イク + ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

子育て (こそだて) also means raising children. [児童](児童.md) (じどう) names
children, not the activity of caring for them. [教育](教育.md) (きょういく), education,
overlaps with aspects of upbringing but is not a replacement for every childcare task.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary gloss "nursing" concerns caring for children here; 育児 is not
a general word for nursing any patient. It covers more than feeding a baby,
and the word itself does not specify which parent or other caregiver does the work.
