# 交通 (こうつう) — traffic; transportation

## Meaning

交通 covers traffic and transportation: the movement of people and vehicles and the means used for that movement.
It is broader than the name of a particular vehicle.
交通機関 (こうつうきかん) means a means or system of transportation.

- この町は交通が便利です。 (このまちはこうつうがべんりです。) — This town has convenient transportation.
- 旅行では、電車などの交通機関を使います。 (りょこうでは、でんしゃなどのこうつうきかんをつかいます。) — When traveling, I use transportation such as trains.

## How the kanji combine

[交](../kanji/交.md) contributes crossing or interaction, while [通](../kanji/通.md) contributes passing through.
Together they provide a useful image of movement between places.
The on readings are コウ + ツウ, with a long vowel in each part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

通行 (つうこう) also translates as "traffic," but foregrounds passage through a place.
交通 is useful for the broader transportation topic, as in the town example.
These are overlapping words, not opposites, and English "traffic" alone does not decide which Japanese word fits.

## Distinctive meaning

The "transportation" sense is not limited to road congestion.
A sentence about 交通 can concern access by train, bus, or other means.
Do not use the word as though it named one bus or one train: those are vehicles within the transportation being discussed.
