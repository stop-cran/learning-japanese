# 画伯 (がはく) — master painter; honorific for a painter

## Meaning

画伯 means a master painter; as an honorific, it refers to a painter and is often attached after the person's name.

- 画伯の作品が美術館に展示されています。 (がはくのさくひんがびじゅつかんにてんじされています。) — The master painter's works are on display at the museum.
- 山田画伯の個展が来月開かれます。 (やまだがはくのこてんがらいげつひらかれます。) — A solo exhibition of master painter Yamada's work opens next month.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[画](../kanji/画.md) contributes ガ and [伯](../kanji/伯.md) contributes ハク; together they form 画伯 (がはく).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[画家](画家.md) (がか) is the neutral word for a painter; 画伯 is a respectful title.

## Distinctive meaning

画伯 can be used after a painter's name as a respectful title. It is not a neutral word for the profession; use 画家 for that.
