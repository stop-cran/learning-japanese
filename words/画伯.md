# 画伯 (がはく) — master painter; great artist

## Meaning

画伯 is an honorific term for a painter or artist.

- 画伯の作品が美術館に展示されています。 (がはくのさくひんがびじゅつかんにてんじされています。) — The master painter's works are on display at the museum.
- 彼は友人から画伯と呼ばれています。 (かれはゆうじんからがはくとよばれています。) — His friends call him a great artist.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[画](../kanji/画.md) contributes ガ and [伯](../kanji/伯.md) contributes ハク; together they form 画伯 (がはく).

## Synonyms and antonyms

画家 (がか) is the neutral word for a painter; 画伯 is an honorific term.

## Distinctive meaning

画伯 is an honorific term for a painter or artist.
