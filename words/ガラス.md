# ガラス - glass material; pane

## Meaning

ガラス means glass as a material, or a pane of glass.
Use ガラスの before a noun to say it is made of glass.

- 窓のガラスが割れました。 (まどのガラスがわれました。) - The windowpane broke.
- ガラスの皿を買いました。 (ガラスのさらをかいました。) - I bought a glass plate.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword comes from Dutch *glas* and is normally written in katakana.
The spelling 硝子 is also recorded, but ガラス is the useful everyday form.
It is not pronounced グラス in this material sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

グラス normally names a drinking glass; コップ also names a drinking vessel.
Those words identify an object and do not guarantee its material.
プラスチック, "plastic," is a contrasting material, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "glass" can mean either the substance or a drinking container.
Japanese commonly separates these as ガラス and グラス/コップ.
When describing a pane breaking, が marks the glass as the thing that
breaks; when buying a plate, を marks the purchased object.
