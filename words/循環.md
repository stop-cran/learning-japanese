# 循環 (じゅんかん) - circulation; cycle

## Meaning

循環 means circulation or movement through a repeating cycle. It is a noun and an intransitive する-verb.

- 血液は体の中を循環しています。 (けつえきはからだのなかをじゅんかんしています。) - Blood circulates through the body.
- このバスは市内を循環します。 (このバスはしないをじゅんかんします。) - This bus follows a circuit around the city.

The を in these examples marks the route or space traversed, not an object being changed by a transitive verb. 循環 can also concern recurring processes rather than the motion of a single physical object.

## How the kanji combine

循 has following or going-around associations, and 環 a ring or surrounding connection. These provide a learning association with a circuit, not proof of historical character formation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[回転](回転.md), [回る](回る.md), [回す](回す.md), and [回り](回り.md) have rotation or circulation uses. [通う](通う.md) also has a circulation sense, apart from its familiar commuting use. Learn the complete construction rather than replacing these words freely.

## Distinctive meaning

循環 emphasizes a circuit or recurring process. Merely repeating a sound or doing an action twice does not necessarily form a cycle. A circular bus route is not the same thing as a bus that simply goes to a final destination and stops.
