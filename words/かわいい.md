# かわいい (かわいい) — cute

## Meaning

かわいい means cute, adorable, or endearing. It can describe a person, an animal, clothing, or a small attractive object.

この猫はかわいいです。（このねこはかわいいです。） — This cat is cute.

かわいい帽子を買いました。（かわいいぼうしをかいました。） — I bought a cute hat.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The spelling 可愛い also occurs, but it should be learned as a whole rather than assembled from ordinary readings of 可 and 愛.

## Synonyms and antonyms

きれい means beautiful or clean; it does not necessarily suggest cuteness. 小さい（ちいさい） means small, which is a size description rather than a judgment of charm.

## Distinctive meaning

かわいい is an i-adjective: かわいくない, "not cute," and かわいかった, "was cute." Keep かわい in these forms; this word does not follow the irregular よい forms of いい, "good."
