# はっきり — clearly; distinctly

## Meaning

はっきり means clearly or distinctly, whether something is easy to hear, easy to see, or unambiguous in meaning.

- もう少しはっきり話してください。 (もうすこしはっきりはなしてください。) — Please speak a little more clearly.
- 山がはっきり見えます。 (やまがはっきりみえます。) — I can see the mountain clearly.

It modifies a verb directly, or with optional と: はっきりと話す. はっきりする means to be or become clear; はっきりした答え (はっきりしたこたえ) is a definite answer.

## How the kanji combine

This mimetic expression is written in kana. Keep the small っ in はっきり; there is no kanji compound to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

明確 (めいかく) means clear or definite, especially in statements and decisions. ぼんやり can describe a vague or blurred impression. Quiet speech can still be clear, so 大きな声 (おおきなこえ), "a loud voice," is not a synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

はっきり言う can mean to speak frankly, not just pronounce each sound distinctly. The object and situation tell you whether the issue is sensory clarity or directness of expression.
