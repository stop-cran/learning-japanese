# 時間 (じかん) — time, hour

## Meaning

- **Time as a quantity**: 時間がありません (I have no time), 時間がかかる (it takes time).
- **A duration in hours**: 二時間 にじかん (two hours), 一時間半 いちじかんはん (an hour and a half).
- **Time slot, period**: 授業の時間 じゅぎょうのじかん (class time), 時間割 じかんわり (timetable).

## How the kanji combine

[時](../kanji/時.md) (time, hour) plus [間](../kanji/間.md) (interval): "a stretch of time". The two kanji are near synonyms in the sense of time, joined into a compound that names an amount of time. Note the contrast with 時 alone, which names a point (三時 さんじ, "3 o'clock"), while 時間 is a length (三時間, "three hours").

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 時刻 じこく (a specific time of day), 期間 きかん (a period), 時 とき (time, when).
- See also 分 (minutes) and 秒 (seconds), the smaller units.

## Distinctive meaning

時間 is the general word for "time" as something you have or spend. For "point in time" use 時刻 or just 時. 何時 is "what time", while 何時間 is "how many hours".
