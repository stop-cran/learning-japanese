# 時間 (じかん) — time, hour

## Meaning

- **Time as a quantity**: 時間がありません (I have no time), 時間がかかる (it takes time).
- **A duration in hours**: 二時間 にじかん (two hours), 一時間半 いちじかんはん (an hour and a half).
- **Time slot, period**: 授業の時間 じゅぎょうのじかん (class time), 時間割 じかんわり (timetable).

## How the kanji combine

[時](../kanji/時.md) (time, hour) plus [間](../kanji/間.md) (interval): "a stretch of time" is a useful meaning aid. In numerical expressions, 時 marks a clock hour (三時 さんじ, "3 o'clock"), while 時間 counts hours of duration (三時間 さんじかん, "three hours"). This counter contrast is not a rule for every use of either word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 時刻 じこく (a specific time of day), 期間 きかん (a period), 時 とき (time, when).
- See also 分 (minutes) and 秒 (seconds), the smaller units.

## Distinctive meaning

時間 is the general word for "time" as something you have or spend, but it can also refer to an appointed time. 時刻 specifically identifies a time point. In clock-hour questions, 何時 (なんじ) asks "what time?", while 何時間 (なんじかん) asks "how many hours?" of duration.

- 出発の時間は九時です。 (しゅっぱつのじかんは くじです。) — The departure time is nine o'clock.
