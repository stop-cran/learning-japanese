# おおざっぱ - rough; broad-brush; careless

## Meaning

おおざっぱ describes something broad or approximate rather than detailed.
It can also criticize careless work or a person's lack of attention to detail.
Use おおざっぱな + noun and おおざっぱに + action.

- おおざっぱな計画を立てました。 (おおざっぱなけいかくをたてました。) - I made a rough plan.
- 費用をおおざっぱに計算しました。 (ひようをおおざっぱにけいさんしました。) - I calculated the cost roughly.

The first plan need not be a bad one: a broad outline may be enough at this stage.
In おおざっぱな仕事 (おおざっぱなしごと), the context may instead criticize sloppy work.

## How the kanji combine

大雑把 and 大ざっぱ are dictionary spellings, both read おおざっぱ.
[大](../kanji/大.md) and [雑](../kanji/雑.md) help recognize the written form;
do not construct its meaning by translating each character separately.
大 has native おお- here; this is not an all-on-reading compound.
Keep the doubled consonant represented by small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ざっと](ざっと.md) describes a rough estimate or a quick overall treatment.
[粗い](粗い.md) (あらい) also concerns roughness, including coarse grains and
rough workmanship. [丁寧](丁寧.md) (ていねい), "careful," contrasts with careless execution.

## Distinctive meaning

"Rough" here need not mean violent, harsh to the touch, or emotionally difficult.
Calling a person おおざっぱ can be critical; describing an initial estimate that
way does not automatically accuse its author of negligence.
