# 異なる (ことなる) - differ; be different

## Meaning

異なる is an intransitive godan verb meaning to differ or vary. Use AはBと異なる for "A differs from B." A source of variation can be marked with によって.

- 私の意見は彼の意見と異なります。 (わたしのいけんはかれのいけんとことなります。) - My opinion differs from his.
- 料金は季節によって異なります。 (りょうきんはきせつによってことなります。) - Prices vary depending on the season.

Forms include 異なります, 異なった, and 異ならない. Before a noun, use 異なる方法 (ことなるほうほう), "a different method," without な. 異なった方法 is another way to describe a differing method; it need not mean the difference has ended.

## How the kanji combine

異 carries the meaning "different" or "other," and なる completes the native verb ことなる. Learn the full word rather than trying to pronounce it with the on reading イ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[違う](違う.md) (ちがう) overlaps strongly for "be different" and fits ordinary conversation. 異なる is useful in explanations and comparisons. [同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ), "the same," provides the direct contrast, but has different grammar: 同じ方法, not 同じる方法.

## Distinctive meaning

異なる describes a difference without necessarily judging it a mistake. 意見が異なる means opinions differ; it does not by itself say that the people argued. To translate English "disagree," check whether the sentence describes differing views or an active expression of opposition.
