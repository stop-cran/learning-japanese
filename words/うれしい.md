# うれしい — glad; pleased

## Meaning

うれしい describes feeling glad or pleased about something. It is an i-adjective: うれしかった means "was glad," and うれしくない means "not glad."

- 会えてうれしいです。 (あえてうれしいです。) — I'm glad to see you.
- プレゼントをもらって、うれしかったです。 (ぷれぜんとをもらって、うれしかったです。) — I was happy to receive a present.

A preceding て-form can identify the occasion for the feeling. When describing another person's apparent feelings, うれしそうです means "looks pleased."

## How the kanji combine

The kanji spelling is 嬉しい. The hiragana spelling keeps the same word and conjugation; it is not a different kind of happiness. There is no compound to decompose.
嬉しい知らせ (うれしいしらせ), "welcome news," describes something that brings
pleasure, not a message that itself experiences an emotion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[楽しい](楽しい.md) (たのしい), "enjoyable/fun," emphasizes an enjoyable experience.
[悲しい](悲しい.md) (かなしい), "sad," is a useful emotional contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

うれしい commonly responds to welcome news, kindness, or a desired result. 楽しいパーティー is a fun party; パーティーに招かれてうれしい means being glad to have been invited.
