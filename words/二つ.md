# 二つ (ふたつ) — two things

## Meaning

二つ is the native-Japanese way to count two things: みかんを二つください (two mandarins, please), 二つある (there are two), 二つ目 ふたつめ (the second one).

## How the kanji combine

[二](../kanji/二.md) read with the kun reading ふた plus the okurigana つ, a general counter for things. The series is 一つ ひとつ, 二つ ふたつ, 三つ みっつ, up to 九つ ここのつ, and 十 とお (no つ).
For people the series is separate: ひとり, ふたり, さんにん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 二個 にこ (two pieces, on-reading counter).

## Distinctive meaning

つ-counting is an everyday general option for many things, including some that also have a more specific counter.
二個 is used for countable pieces or objects; 二つ can also include abstract things (二つの問題, two problems).
Use the separate people-counting form 二人 (ふたり), not 二つ, for two people.
