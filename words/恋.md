# 恋 (こい) - romantic love

## Meaning

恋 is a noun for romantic love. Learn 恋をする, "be in love,"
and 人に恋をする, "fall in love with someone." 恋に落ちる
(こいにおちる) describes falling in love.

- 彼女は同級生に恋をしています。 (かのじょはどうきゅうせいにこいをしています。) - She is in love with a classmate.
- これは初めての恋でした。 (これははじめてのこいでした。) - This was my first love.

In the first example, に identifies the person toward whom the feeling
is directed. 恋をしている describes the ongoing state rather than
requiring that the falling-in-love event is happening at that moment.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 恋 supplies the whole native noun, read こい.
It is not a compound to divide into separate words. The older written
form 戀 belongs to the same dictionary entry; learners can recognize
the modern 恋 without learning a different pronunciation for it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[愛](愛.md) (あい) covers love more broadly, including family affection.
[好き](好き.md) (すき) expresses liking and can convey romantic
interest, but has different grammar: 人が好き, compared with 人に恋をする.

## Distinctive meaning

恋 does not say that the feeling is mutual or that the people are
already a couple. It identifies romantic feeling, not every form of
care, affection, or commitment. Avoid treating it as a general replacement
for English "love" in sentences about hobbies or food.
