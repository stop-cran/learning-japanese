# 民謡 (みんよう) -- folk song

## Meaning

民謡 is a folk song: a song associated with a community or region,
often transmitted through work, festivals, rituals or other shared
activities. The term is not limited to a solo singer or one instrument.

- この地方には古い民謡が伝わっています。 (このちほうにはふるいみんようがつたわっています。) -- Old folk songs have been handed down in this region.
- 祭りで民謡を歌いました。 (まつりでみんようをうたいました。) -- We sang folk songs at the festival.

## How the kanji combine

[民](../kanji/民.md) concerns the people and 謡 singing or a song.
The on-readings are ミン + ヨウ. The compound means a folk song, not
every utterance by an ordinary person.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [歌](歌.md) (うた) is the broader word song.
- [歌謡](歌謡.md) (かよう) also covers songs or ballads without itself requiring this folk-transmission setting.
- [民間](民間.md) (みんかん) shares the ordinary-people or folk association but does not itself mean a song.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary translation "popular song" should not be read as "any
current pop hit." Traditional transmission is a useful starting point,
but not an absolute rule that a 民謡 must have an unknown author:
the broader category also includes 新民謡 (しんみんよう), newly
created songs associated with places or communities.
