# 乙 (おつ) — second rank; class B; stylish

## Meaning

As a noun, 乙 is the second label after 甲 (こう), like B after A: the second party, the second rank, or grade B. As a na-adjective, it describes something stylish or chic, different from the ordinary and with an interesting flavor. It can also mean strange or odd.

- 甲の次は乙です。
  (こうのつぎはおつです。)
  "After A comes B."
- 雪を見ながら飲むお茶も、乙なものですね。
  (ゆきをみながらのむおちゃも、おつなものですね。)
  "Drinking tea while watching the snow has a special charm, doesn't it?"

These are original usage examples, not quotations from a dictionary or other source.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [乙](../kanji/乙.md) uses the on reading オツ here. There is no multi-character compound to divide. The pair 甲乙 (こうおつ) means "first and second; A and B," and 甲乙丙丁 (こうおつへいてい) means "A, B, C and D; first, second, third and fourth."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[丙](丙.md) (へい), "third rank; class C," comes after 乙, and 甲 (こう), "1st in rank; grade A," comes before it; they are neighbors in one sequence, not synonyms or antonyms. [後者](後者.md) (こうしゃ), "the latter," also picks the second of two, but it is not a rank label. For the stylish sense, [おしゃれ](おしゃれ.md) (おしゃれ), "stylish; dressing up," is about looks and clothes. For the strange sense, [変](変.md) (へん), "strange; odd," and [妙](妙.md) (みょう), "odd; strange," are close.

There is no single fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

In contracts and similar documents, 甲 is the first party and 乙 the second party. The same character read きのと names the second sign of the Chinese calendar; do not read the label examples above as きのと.

JMdict also lists two senses marked abbreviation/slang, "thank you; good job" and "see you; goodbye; goodnight," with a cross-reference to お疲れ様 (おつかれさま).
