# 機嫌 (きげん) - mood; temper

## Meaning

機嫌 is a noun for someone's mood or temper, especially how pleased
or displeased they seem. Learn 機嫌がいい, "be in a good mood," and
機嫌が悪い, "be in a bad mood."

- 今日は父の機嫌がいいです。 (きょうはちちのきげんがいいです。) - My father is in a good mood today.
- 彼女は機嫌を直しました。 (かのじょはきげんをなおしました。) - She stopped being upset.

機嫌を直す describes recovering one's good mood. With the prefix ご,
ご機嫌 can itself describe being cheerful: ご機嫌な子供
(ごきげんなこども), "a cheerful child."

## How the kanji combine

[機](../kanji/機.md) has meanings including an occasion or opportunity;
嫌 contributes dislike or displeasure. The established compound means
"mood." These component associations help recognition, but mechanically
adding their English glosses does not derive the modern meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[気分](気分.md) (きぶん) can describe how someone feels, including
physical well-being. 機嫌 focuses on mood or temper.
[気持ち](気持ち.md) (きもち) covers feelings more broadly.
機嫌がいい and 機嫌が悪い form the useful positive-negative pair.

## Distinctive meaning

English "humor" in an older dictionary gloss means disposition here, not
comedy. Saying someone has a good 機嫌 does not say they tell good jokes.
It describes their current mood rather than a permanent personality trait.
