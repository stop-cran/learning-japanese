# 小遣い (こづかい) - pocket money; spending allowance

## Meaning

小遣い is money available for personal everyday spending,
including pocket money or an allowance.
The prefixed form お小遣い (おこづかい) is also useful.
It can describe an adult's spending money, not just a child's allowance.

- 毎月、子供に小遣いを渡しています。
  (まいつき、こどもにこづかいをわたしています。) - I give my child an allowance every month.
- 小遣いをためて、自転車を買いました。
  (こづかいをためて、じてんしゃをかいました。) - I saved my pocket money and bought a bicycle.

The recipient takes に with 渡す (わたす), "hand over," while
the money takes を.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) contributes the native prefix こ, small;
遣い is read づかい and relates to using or spending.
The whole word abbreviates 小遣い銭 (こづかいせん).
Keep づ in こづかい; the source dictionary's search-only
こずかい is not the spelling taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お金](お金.md) (おかね) and [金銭](金銭.md) (きんせん) mean
money more generally. [支給](支給.md) (しきゅう) concerns providing
money or supplies, including an allowance; it is not the name of
every recipient's personal spending fund.
[費用](費用.md) (ひよう) concerns the cost of an activity.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "personal expenses" should be understood here as money
for personal spending, not every amount spent or every incidental
business expense. The word does not specify the exact sum, payment
interval, or whether someone else supplied it.
Saving it, as in the second example, does not stop it from being 小遣い.
