# ばつ (ばつ) - cross mark for an incorrect answer

## Meaning

ばつ, also written バツ, names the cross-shaped mark × used for an incorrect answer or something disallowed.
The word is normally written in kana; × is the mark itself.
Dictionaries also record fullwidth ｘ for this cross-mark word, read ばつ, not エックス in that use.

- 間違った答えにばつを付けました。 (まちがったこたえにばつをつけました。) - I put a cross by the wrong answer.
- この印はばつです。 (このしるしはばつです。) - This mark is a cross.

## How the kanji combine

There are no kanji in the primary spelling. The name's origin is uncertain:
dictionaries tentatively connect it with ばってん (罰点), rather than establishing that derivation as fact.
The deck provisionally groups it as kango following that possible connection with 罰点.
This is a qualified classification, not a verified etymology; kana spelling alone does not establish a native Japanese origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ばってん and ぺけ can also name a cross mark.
[丸](丸.md) (まる) contrasts as a circle mark, for example marking an answer correct.
罰 (ばつ), "punishment," is not an unrestricted synonym for the mark name.

## Distinctive meaning

The alphabet letter X or ｘ is normally エックス when named as a letter.
It is not automatically read ばつ in names, algebra or every English use of "X."
The selected meaning here is specifically the cross mark, not the name of every X-shaped character.
