# 商人 (しょうにん) - merchant; trader

## Meaning

商人 is a noun for a merchant, trader, dealer, or shopkeeper: someone engaged in buying and selling goods.
The reading taught here is **しょうにん**. The same spelling also has the established reading **あきんど**.

- 商人たちが港に集まりました。 (しょうにんたちがみなとにあつまりました。) - Merchants gathered at the port.
- 彼は布を売る商人でした。 (かれはぬのをうるしょうにんでした。) - He was a merchant who sold cloth.

A preceding clause can specify the trade: 布を売る商人, "a merchant who sells cloth."
商人になる means "become a merchant."

## How the kanji combine

[商](../kanji/商.md) contributes commerce or trade; [人](../kanji/人.md) identifies a person.
It is an activity-plus-person compound, with the on readings ショウ + ニン.
The alternative reading あきんど is learned for the whole word rather than assembled from these on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[店員](店員.md) (てんいん) means a shop employee and does not by itself say the person runs a trading business.
[商売](商売.md) (しょうばい) names business or trade, not the person.
[客](客.md) (きゃく), "customer," names the other participant in a sale, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

商人 emphasizes the person's commercial role. It can occur in accounts of historical trade as well as descriptions of merchants.
Do not translate every English "businessperson" as 商人: a person's business may involve services or management rather than trading goods.
The surrounding field or activity supplies the useful detail.
