# 広がる (ひろがる) - spread out; extend

## Meaning

広がる is an intransitive godan verb for something spreading, extending, or reaching across a wider area. The thing that spreads takes が. Its forms include 広がります, 広がらない, and 広がった.

- 目の前に青い海が広がっています。 (めのまえにあおいうみがひろがっています。) - The blue sea stretches out before us.
- そのニュースは町中に広がりました。 (そのニュースはまちじゅうにひろがりました。) - The news spread throughout the town.

The first sentence describes an expanse already in view; the second describes information reaching more people.

## How the kanji combine

[広](../kanji/広.md) contributes breadth or spaciousness. The native verb reading is ひろがる, related in meaning to 広い (ひろい), "wide." The kana ending makes this a verb rather than the adjective itself.

## Synonyms and antonyms

広げる (ひろげる) is the transitive partner: 地図を広げる (ちずをひろげる), "spread out a map." 広がる instead presents the spreading thing as the subject. [増える](増える.md) (ふえる) concerns amount or number, not necessarily spatial reach.

## Distinctive meaning

The word works for physical space, news, interests, and possibilities. It does not by itself say that someone deliberately caused the expansion. For "we spread the news," a transitive construction is needed; simply replacing が with を does not make 広がる transitive.
