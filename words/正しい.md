# 正しい (ただしい) — correct, proper

## Meaning

正しい is an い-adjective meaning correct or right. It can also mean proper or just when judging behavior or a decision.

- この答えは正しいです。 (このこたえはただしいです。) — This answer is correct.
- 正しい名前を書いてください。 (ただしいなまえをかいてください。) — Please write the correct name.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native adjective with one kanji. [正](../kanji/正.md) supplies ただ, and しい is the okurigana. There is no second component to read as a compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 正確 せいかく means accurate or precise and is a な-adjective. It emphasizes accuracy or exactness rather than necessarily moral rightness.
- 間違い まちがい and 誤り あやまり mean a mistake or error. They express the contrasting idea but are nouns, not い-adjectives that can replace 正しい without changing the sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

正しい compares something with facts, a rule, or a standard of proper conduct. It is not a general substitute for every positive English "good." English "right" as a direction is [右](../kanji/右.md) みぎ, not 正しい.
