# テープレコーダー (テープレコーダー) - tape recorder

## Meaning

テープレコーダー is a tape recorder, a device that records sound on magnetic tape.

- 古いテープレコーダーで声を録音しました。 (ふるいテープレコーダーでこえをろくおんしました。) - I recorded my voice on an old tape recorder.

で marks the device used. 録音する (ろくおんする) means recording sound; テープレコーダー names the machine, not that action.

## How the kanji combine

The word combines two English-derived katakana elements: テープ, "tape," and レコーダー, "recorder." Keep the long vowels in both parts. There are no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

レコーダー is broader and does not necessarily specify tape. An ICレコーダー (アイシーレコーダー) is a digital audio recorder.

## Distinctive meaning

This remains an intelligible equipment name, but it is technology-specific. Do not call every phone recording app a テープレコーダー. Its appearance in an older beginner vocabulary list does not make tape the default recording medium today.
