# 半ば (なかば) - middle; halfway; partly

## Meaning

半ば is a noun or suffix for the middle or halfway point of a period or process. It also works adverbially as "half; partly."

- 来月の半ばに帰ります。 (らいげつのなかばにかえります。) - I will return in the middle of next month.
- 半ば冗談で言ったのです。 (なかばじょうだんでいったのです。) - I said it partly as a joke.

In the first example, の connects the month to its middle, and に marks the time. In the second, 半ば expresses degree, not a point on a calendar.

## How the kanji combine

[半](../kanji/半.md) supplies the idea of half. The native word is なかば, with ば written in kana. It is not read はんば; the on reading はん belongs to other words such as 半分.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[半分](半分.md) (はんぶん) is the useful comparison for one half of an amount. [中](中.md) (なか) can identify the inside or middle of something more broadly. These overlap with particular uses of 半ば, but do not share all its constructions.

## Distinctive meaning

In time expressions, 半ば indicates a middle region rather than necessarily one mathematically exact instant. Likewise, 半ば冗談 describes a mixture of joking and seriousness; it does not measure the joke as precisely fifty percent. The noun and adverbial uses share a halfway idea but attach differently.
