# 金持ち (かねもち) — wealthy person

## Meaning

金持ち is a rich or wealthy person. It is a noun, and does not specify gender despite sometimes being translated as "rich man." お金持ち (おかねもち) is also a familiar form.

- あの人はお金持ちです。 (あのひとはおかねもちです。) — That person is wealthy.
- 金持ちになりたいです。 (かねもちになりたいです。) — I want to become rich.

Use の before another noun: 金持ちの友達 (かねもちのともだち), "a wealthy friend."

## How the kanji combine

[金](../kanji/金.md) is かね, "money," and [持](../kanji/持.md) appears in 持ち, from 持つ (もつ), "have/hold." The compound identifies a person with wealth, rather than someone merely holding coins.

## Synonyms and antonyms

裕福 (ゆうふく), "well-off," is a na-adjective useful for describing financial circumstances. 貧乏 (びんぼう), "poverty/poor," contrasts with being wealthy.

## Distinctive meaning

金持ち concerns material wealth, not personal worth or generosity. Its noun grammar differs from an English adjective even when English naturally translates 金持ちです as "is rich."
