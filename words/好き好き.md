# 好き好き (すきずき) - matter of taste

## Meaning

好き好き means that something is a matter of individual taste: different people prefer different things. It is a noun, commonly used in 人の好き好き (ひとのすきずき).

- 服の色は人の好き好きです。 (ふくのいろはひとのすきずきです。) - The choice of clothing color is a matter of personal taste.
- どちらのデザインを選ぶかは、好き好きです。 (どちらのデザインをえらぶかは、すきずきです。) - Which design to choose is a matter of taste.

## How the kanji combine

The written form repeats 好き, associated with [好](../kanji/好.md), liking. In this established word, the second す is voiced to ず: すきずき. The alternative spelling 好きずき makes that reading explicit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[好み](好み.md) is a preference or personal taste; [好き嫌い](好き嫌い.md) is likes and dislikes. 好き好き foregrounds variation between individuals rather than listing one person's preferences. [趣味](趣味.md) overlaps in its taste sense, not because every instance means a hobby.

## Distinctive meaning

Read this lexical noun すきずき, not すきすき. It is not simply an emphatic repetition meaning "I really like it." English "taste" here concerns preference, not the physical [味](味.md) of food; the expression can concern food, clothing, design or other choices.
