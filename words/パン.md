# パン — bread

## Meaning

パン means bread and includes many bread-based buns and sweet bakery products. It is a noun; the exact kind is often specified by a compound or the situation.

朝ご飯にパンを食べます。 (あさごはんにパンをたべます。) — I eat bread for breakfast.

## How the kanji combine

パン is a loanword from Portuguese pão, not from English "pan." It is normally written in katakana. The two morae パ・ン have no long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

食パン (しょくパン) is loaf or sandwich bread. あんパン is a bun with sweet bean filling. ご飯 (ごはん), cooked rice, is another staple food, not an opposite of bread.

## Distinctive meaning

English cookware "pan" is not パン in this sense; a frying pan is フライパン. Counting depends on the item: a slice can take 枚 (まい), while an individual bun can take 個 (こ). パン itself does not specify a slice, loaf, or serving.
