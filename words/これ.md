# これ -- this; this one

## Meaning

これ points to something near the speaker. It can also refer to the speaker's action or the matter currently being presented.

- これは私のかばんです。 (これはわたしのかばんです。) -- This is my bag.
- これをください。 (これをください。) -- This one, please.

これ stands in place of a noun and takes particles such as は or を. To say "this bag," use このかばん, not これかばん.

## How the kanji combine

This demonstrative is ordinarily written in hiragana. Dictionary spellings such as 此れ are rare alternatives, not the spelling a beginner needs for normal conversation and messages.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[それ](それ.md) points to something near the listener or associated with what the listener has said. あれ points to something away from both speakers. どれ asks "which one?"

## Distinctive meaning

The choice depends on conversational perspective, not an absolute distance measurement. A speaker holding an object can call it これ while the listener calls the same object それ. Keep this demonstrative distinct from the separately recorded calling interjection also read これ.
