# 幼稚園 (ようちえん) - kindergarten; preschool

## Meaning

幼稚園 is a kindergarten or preschool.
In Japan, it is a noncompulsory educational institution for children
from age three until they begin primary school.

- 娘は四月から幼稚園に通っています。 (むすめはしがつからようちえんにかよっています。) - My daughter has been attending kindergarten since April.
- 幼稚園で友達と遊びました。 (ようちえんでともだちとあそびました。) - I played with friends at kindergarten.

## How the kanji combine

[幼](../kanji/幼.md) and 稚 contribute early
childhood; [園](../kanji/園.md) contributes an enclosed place
or institution. The on readings are ヨウ + チ + エン.
Here [幼稚](幼稚.md) refers to young age, not a judgment that
the institution is immature.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[学校](学校.md) (がっこう) is the broader school word.
保育園 (ほいくえん) and 保育所 (ほいくしょ) concern nursery/daycare
provision and are not automatically identical to a 幼稚園.
認定こども園 (にんていこどもえん) combines early education and
childcare functions under a different institutional category.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume every English "preschool" or "kindergarten" has
Japan's exact age range or institutional arrangements.
Within the Japanese sequence, [小学校](小学校.md) (しょうがっこう)
is the following primary-school stage, not an antonym.
Nor does every facility caring for small children become a 幼稚園.
