# 子 (こ) — child

## Meaning

子 means a child or young person, and also someone's offspring. Context supplies whose child is meant and whether there is one child or more.

- あの子は私の弟です。  
  あのこはわたしのおとうとです。  
  "That child is my younger brother."
- うちの子は今年、学校に入ります。  
  うちのこはことし、がっこうにはいります。  
  "My child starts school this year."

## How the kanji combine

[子](../kanji/子.md) has the native reading こ here. It also forms familiar expressions such as 女の子 (おんなのこ), "girl", and 男の子 (おとこのこ), "boy". Other compounds can use the on reading し; do not apply it to this standalone noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[子供](子供.md) (こども) is the general everyday word "child". 親 (おや), "parent", contrasts a family role, while 大人 (おとな), "adult", contrasts age.

## Distinctive meaning

In うちの子, 子 identifies the speaker's child through family relationship, not necessarily a small child. Expressions such as あの子 can sound familiar; they are not a neutral respectful way to address every adult.
