# 恐ろしい (おそろしい) - terrifying; dreadful

## Meaning

恐ろしい is an い-adjective describing something frightening or dreadful. Use 恐ろしい話 (おそろしいはなし), "a frightening story," and 恐ろしくない, "not frightening."

- 昨夜、恐ろしい夢を見ました。 (さくや、おそろしいゆめをみました。) - I had a terrifying dream last night.
- 車が恐ろしい速さで走っていきました。 (くるまがおそろしいはやさではしっていきました。) - The car sped away at a frightening speed.

The word also extends to a startling or tremendous degree. 恐ろしく速い (おそろしくはやい), "astonishingly fast," uses the adverbial form 恐ろしく and need not describe literal fear.

## How the kanji combine

[恐](../kanji/恐.md) contributes fear. In this native adjective it is read おそ before ろしい. The final い changes in forms such as 恐ろしかった, "was terrifying."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怖い](怖い.md) (こわい) overlaps in the frightening sense and can express being scared. [恐れる](恐れる.md) (おそれる) is a related verb, "fear," not an adjective that can be substituted without changing the grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

English "terrible" can merely evaluate something as very bad. 恐ろしい instead suggests fear, dread, or an extraordinary degree, so a disappointing meal is not automatically 恐ろしい. The dictionary's startling-degree sense is attached to the spelling 恐ろしい; there is no need to learn the rare alternative spellings to use these examples.
