# 勝ち (かち) - a win; victory

## Meaning

勝ち is a noun meaning a win or victory. It names the result rather than conjugating like the verb "win." Use person/team + の勝ち to say whose victory it is.

- この試合は私たちの勝ちです。 (このしあいはわたしたちのかちです。) - This game is a win for us.
- 最後まで勝ちをあきらめませんでした。 (さいごまでかちをあきらめませんでした。) - We did not give up on winning until the end.

The second example describes the aim of winning; it does not establish that the team eventually won. 勝ちが決まる (かちがきまる) means that the victory is decided.

## How the kanji combine

[勝](../kanji/勝.md) contributes winning. 勝ち is related to the connecting/stem form of [勝つ](勝つ.md) (かつ), but functions as a noun here. There is no second kanji to form a compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[負け](負け.md) (まけ), "a loss/defeat," is its direct counterpart. 勝利 (しょうり) also means victory. 勝つ is the verb: 私たちが勝ちました (わたしたちがかちました), "We won."

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the noun and verb patterns separate: 私たちの勝ちです versus 私たちが勝ちました. The former labels the outcome; the latter states what the team did. The result noun is also distinct from the suffix ～がち, used for tendencies, which has different pronunciation and grammar.
