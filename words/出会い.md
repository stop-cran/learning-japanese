# 出会い (であい) - meeting; encounter

## Meaning

出会い is a noun for a meeting or encounter, including meeting someone for the first time
or coming across something. Use person or thing + との出会い to identify what was encountered.

- 先生との出会いが人生を変えました。 (せんせいとのであいがじんせいをかえました。) - Meeting that teacher changed my life.
- 旅先では新しい出会いがあります。 (たびさきではあたらしいであいがあります。) - There are new encounters when traveling.

出会いがある means that an encounter occurs or an opportunity to meet someone exists.
It is a noun construction, not a する verb made by adding する to 出会い.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) contributes coming or going out, and [会](../kanji/会.md) contributes meeting.
出会い is related to the native verb [出会う](出会う.md) (であう).
The final い marks the noun form; it does not make the word an i-adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会う](会う.md) (あう) is the broader verb "meet."
[別れ](別れ.md) (わかれ), "parting," is a useful contrast in accounts of relationships.
[会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) is a meeting organized for discussion, not a direct replacement for an encounter.

## Distinctive meaning

出会い can matter because it introduces someone or something into a person's life.
It is not restricted to romantic meetings: teachers, friends, books, and ideas can figure in such accounts.
Nor does the word alone prove that the encounter was accidental or life-changing;
the first example states its importance explicitly.
