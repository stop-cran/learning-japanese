# 見事 (みごと) - splendid; admirable

## Meaning

見事 praises something as splendid, excellent, or admirable. It can modify
a noun with な, as in 見事な演奏 (みごとなえんそう), "a splendid
performance," or an action with に.

- 庭の桜は見事でした。 (にわのさくらはみごとでした。) - The cherry blossoms in the garden were magnificent.
- 彼女は難しい問題を見事に解決しました。 (かのじょはむずかしいもんだいをみごとにかいけつしました。) - She solved the difficult problem brilliantly.

The second example praises how the task was accomplished, not just its visual appearance.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) contributes seeing, and [事](../kanji/事.md) contributes
a thing or matter. "Something worth seeing" is a useful association and
is recorded as an archaic noun sense. The native reading is みごと,
with こと voiced to ごと.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[立派](立派.md) (りっぱ), "splendid; fine," overlaps in praise.
[上手](上手.md) (じょうず) focuses on skill, while 見事 can evaluate a
result, performance, or sight. A poor performance would be a contextual
contrast; the word has no single opposite covering every use.

## Distinctive meaning

見事に can also mean "completely" or "utterly," especially with a bad outcome:
見事に負けた (みごとにまけた), "was thoroughly beaten."
That does not praise losing. Let the predicate decide whether 見事に is
admiration or an emphatic description of completeness. Its positive dictionary
gloss should not override the rest of the sentence.
