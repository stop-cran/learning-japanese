# 扇風機 (せんぷうき) - electric fan

## Meaning

扇風機 is an electric fan used to move air, often for personal cooling.
It is a noun: 扇風機をつける means turning on the fan.

- 暑いので、扇風機をつけました。 (あついので、せんぷうきをつけました。) - I turned on the electric fan because it was hot.
- 寝る前に扇風機を消しました。 (ねるまえにせんぷうきをけしました。) - I turned off the electric fan before going to bed.

つける and 消す (けす) describe switching it on and off here,
not physically attaching the fan or erasing it.

## How the kanji combine

扇 contributes fanning, [風](../kanji/風.md) air or wind, and
[機](../kanji/機.md) a machine.
Together they suggest a machine that fans air.
Learn せんぷうき as a whole: the middle is ぷう, not ふう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[扇子](扇子.md) (せんす) is a folding hand fan.
[冷房](冷房.md) (れいぼう) concerns air cooling or air conditioning,
and [クーラー](クーラー.md) can name an air conditioner.
These are related ways of cooling, not interchangeable names for one device.

## Distinctive meaning

A 扇風機 moves air; it does not by itself name an air-conditioning system
that lowers the room's air temperature.
The word also does not specify whether the fan stands on a desk or the floor.
The ordinary spelling taught here is 扇風機.
