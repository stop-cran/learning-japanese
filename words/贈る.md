# 贈る (おくる) - give as a gift; bestow

## Meaning

贈る is a transitive godan verb for presenting something as a gift or
bestowing an award or honor. Use 人に物を贈る: に marks the recipient,
and を marks what is presented.

- 母に花を贈りました。 (ははにはなをおくりました。) - I gave my mother flowers.
- 優勝者に賞を贈ります。 (ゆうしょうしゃにしょうをおくります。) - We will award a prize to the winner.

The past form is 贈った (おくった), and the negative is 贈らない
(おくらない). The verb does not itself specify whether the gift is
handed over in person or delivered.

## How the kanji combine

贈 carries the idea of giving or presenting. The whole native verb is
おくる, with る written as okurigana. There is no multi-character
compound to split.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[送る](送る.md), also おくる, covers sending or dispatching.
A parcel can be 送る without being a gift. [与える](与える.md)
(あたえる) is broader "give," while [受け取る](受け取る.md)
(うけとる) describes the recipient's side of a transfer.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling distinguishes an important intention. 手紙を送る focuses
on sending a letter; 花を贈る presents flowers as a gift. Do not use
贈る as a generic spelling for sending a file or dispatching an employee.
The award use extends the same idea of presenting something to a recipient.
