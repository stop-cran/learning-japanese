# 壇 (だん) — raised platform; podium

## Meaning

壇 is a raised place such as a platform from which someone speaks. Here “podium” means the platform the speaker stands on, not merely a stand for notes.

- 先生は壇上で話した。 (せんせいはだんじょうではなした。) — The teacher spoke from the platform.
- 話が終わると、壇を下りた。 (はなしがおわると、だんをおりた。) — When the talk ended, the speaker stepped down.

## How the kanji combine

[壇](../kanji/壇.md) writes the whole noun だん. In 壇上 (だんじょう), the same reading points to the platform one is on.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[台](台.md) (だい) can support an object. [舞台](舞台.md) (ぶたい) can be a stage for a performance. A speaker's platform is an example of a 壇 without being a theatrical stage.

## Distinctive meaning

A physical platform is the sense in these examples. Do not force it into compounds about groups of people: 文壇 (ぶんだん), for instance, means literary circles.
