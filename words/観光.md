# 観光 (かんこう) - sightseeing; tourism

## Meaning

観光 is sightseeing or tourism: visiting a place to enjoy its sights and attractions. It is a noun and a transitive する-verb. A place toured can take を, while で can mark where the activity happens.

- 週末に京都を観光しました。 (しゅうまつにきょうとをかんこうしました。) - I went sightseeing in Kyoto over the weekend.
- この町には観光客がたくさん来ます。 (このまちにはかんこうきゃくがたくさんきます。) - Many tourists come to this town.

観光客 (かんこうきゃく) means a tourist; 観光地 (かんこうち) means a tourist destination. 観光に行く uses に to mark the purpose of going.

## How the kanji combine

[観](../kanji/観.md) contributes looking or viewing, and [光](../kanji/光.md) contributes light or splendor. "Viewing a place's splendor" is a component-based memory aid, not a complete account of the word's history. The on readings are カン + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) names travel or a trip; a trip can include sightseeing without having that as its purpose. [見物](見物.md) (けんぶつ) focuses on looking at a sight, event, or spectacle. These overlap rather than forming rigid categories.

## Distinctive meaning

観光 identifies an activity or travel purpose, not simply being away from home. A business traveler may spend an afternoon sightseeing; that afternoon can be 観光 even when the overall trip is for work.
