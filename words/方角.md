# 方角 (ほうがく) - direction; compass point

## Meaning

方角 is a direction, compass point or bearing: for example, north or the
direction in which a station lies.

- 駅はどちらの方角ですか。 (えきはどちらのほうがくですか。) - In which direction is the station?
- 出発する前に地図で方角を確かめました。 (しゅっぱつするまえにちずでほうがくをたしかめました。) - Before setting out, I checked the direction on a map.

## How the kanji combine

[方](../kanji/方.md) contributes direction; [角](../kanji/角.md) has an
angle/direction association. ホウ + カク becomes ほうがく, with voiced ガク.
It is not the corner word かど inserted into a compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[方向](方向.md) (ほうこう) also means direction, including physical directions.
方角 is useful for compass orientation, but there is no absolute rule making
方角 physical and 方向 abstract. [針路](針路.md) (しんろ) names a course or heading.

## Distinctive meaning

方角 can also concern the direction of thought, and has a method/means sense.
That broader use should be recognized, not transferred mechanically to every
compass example. [方法](方法.md) (ほうほう) is a straightforward method word;
[方針](方針.md) (ほうしん) concerns a policy or course of action.
