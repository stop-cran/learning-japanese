# そのため - hence; for that reason

## Meaning

そのため refers back to a reason and introduces its consequence:
for that reason, as a result, or hence.
**そのため、result** and **そのために result** are useful patterns.

- 大雪で道が通れません。そのため、今日は休校です。 (おおゆきでみちがとおれません。そのため、きょうはきゅうこうです。) - The roads are impassable because of heavy snow. For that reason, school is closed today.
- 電車が遅れました。そのために、会議に間に合いませんでした。 (でんしゃがおくれました。そのために、かいぎにまにあいませんでした。) - The train was delayed. As a result, I did not make it to the meeting on time.

In these examples the preceding situation is a cause, not a goal.

## How the kanji combine

This expression is usually written in kana; **その為 (そのため)** is
the dictionary's kanji spelling.
その refers back to the relevant matter, while [ため](ため.md) supplies
the reason/purpose relationship. The causal use, rather than a purpose construction,
is this article's main meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[だから](だから.md) and [それで](それで.md) can also introduce a consequence.
[従って](従って.md) (したがって) overlaps in "therefore."
[そこで](そこで.md) often introduces a response or next step prompted by
the situation; it is not a substitute for every unavoidable result.
The constructions overlap without having identical register or attachment.

## Distinctive meaning

The same components can be used for purpose when the context supplies a goal:
医者になりたいです。そのために勉強しています。
(いしゃになりたいです。そのためにべんきょうしています。)
means "I want to become a doctor. I am studying for that purpose."
That is a purpose use of ため, not evidence that causal "hence" means "in order to."
Do not decide between cause and purpose from the presence of に alone.
