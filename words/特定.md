# 特定 (とくてい) - specific; identifying; specifying

## Meaning

特定 describes something specific or designated. Before a noun, use **特定の**.
特定する means to specify, identify, or pinpoint something; the thing identified takes を.

- この割引は特定の商品にだけ適用されます。 (このわりびきはとくていのしょうひんにだけてきようされます。) - This discount applies only to specified products.
- 調査で事故の原因を特定しました。 (ちょうさでじこのげんいんをとくていしました。) - The investigation identified the cause of the accident.

The first sentence uses の, not an automatically interchangeable な.
Forms of the verb include 特定します and 特定した.

## How the kanji combine

[特](../kanji/特.md) contributes particular or special, and
[定](../kanji/定.md) contributes fixing or determining.
Together they concern singling something out or fixing what is meant.
The on readings are トク + テイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[指定](指定.md) (してい) overlaps in specifying or designating.
特定する can also identify something previously unknown, such as an accident's cause.
不特定 (ふとくてい), unspecified or not restricted to particular people or things,
provides a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

特定の人 (とくていのひと) means a particular person or specified people,
not necessarily someone exceptional or personally important.
Identifying a cause and deliberately designating a product are different situations;
context determines which use of 特定 is intended.
