# モデル - model; product version

## Meaning

モデル has several related noun uses. A physical model represents something;
a fashion model is a person who displays clothing.
For a product, モデル can identify its model or version.

- 新しいモデルの車を買いました。 (あたらしいもでるのくるまをかいました。) - I bought a new-model car.
- 彼女はファッションモデルとして働いています。 (かのじょはふぁっしょんもでるとしてはたらいています。) - She works as a fashion model.

A theoretical or mathematical model represents how something works.
Someone or something that inspires a fictional character or setting can also be its モデル:

- この小説の主人公のモデルは作者の祖父です。 (このしょうせつのしゅじんこうのもでるはさくしゃのそふです。) - The author's grandfather is the model for the main character in this novel.

モデルにする means to use something as a model; に identifies the model being used.
The word can also identify an example or standard to follow.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword with no kanji decomposition.
Do not assume that every occurrence names a person or a miniature object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[見本](見本.md) (みほん) can be a sample or model to follow.
[サンプル](サンプル.md) includes samples and display models.
[型](型.md) (かた) and [タイプ](タイプ.md) compare with product types,
but a model used to explain a theory is not necessarily a commercial product.

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding noun and construction select the sense.
モデルの仕事 can concern modeling work, whereas 数学のモデル
(すうがくのもでる) concerns a mathematical representation.
Neither expression licenses all uses of English "model" in every Japanese context.
