# ぴたっと — exactly or suddenly to a stop

## Meaning

ぴたっと describes a close, exact fit or an action that stops suddenly and completely.

- 車は線の手前でぴたっと止まった。
  — The car stopped precisely just before the line.
- 彼は話をぴたっとやめた。
  — He abruptly stopped talking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぴたっと emphasizes exact contact or a clean, immediate stop, rather than a gradual slowdown.

## Distinctive meaning

It can describe both close fit and sudden cessation; the verb and context select the sense.
