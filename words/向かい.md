# 向かい (むかい) - opposite; across from

## Meaning

向かい is a noun for a position opposite or facing something.
Use Nの向かい for "across from N," and 向かいの + noun
for the thing on the opposite side.

- 駅の向かいに銀行があります。 (えきのむかいにぎんこうがあります。) - There is a bank across from the station.
- 向かいの席に座ってください。 (むかいのせきにすわってください。) - Please sit in the seat opposite.

に marks the location in both examples. The first may describe
buildings across a street; the second can describe seats across
a table. A street is not required by the word.

## How the kanji combine

[向](../kanji/向.md) contributes facing or directing toward
something. 向かい has the form of the stem of 向かう
(むかう), but functions here as a location noun. The kana
かい belong to this native form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[向こう](向こう.md) (むこう) is a broader "over there; the
other side." [隣](隣.md) (となり) places something next door
or beside another thing. 向かい places it opposite, not beside.
[反対](反対.md) (はんたい) also means opposition but has
many nonspatial uses.

## Distinctive meaning

向かい does not express disagreeing with an opinion. Its useful
core is spatial: one location faces another. Identify the reference
point with の when the listener cannot tell what the location is
opposite to.
