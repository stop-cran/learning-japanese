# 勘定 (かんじょう) - reckoning; bill

## Meaning

勘定 is counting or reckoning, and 勘定する means to count or calculate something. It also names a bill or the settlement of a bill, especially in expressions with お.

- 人数を勘定しました。 (にんずうをかんじょうしました。) - I counted the people.
- お勘定をお願いします。 (おかんじょうをおねがいします。) - The bill, please.

In the first example, を marks what is counted. In the second, the speaker is asking to settle the bill, not requesting an abstract mathematics calculation.

## How the kanji combine

勘 contributes examining or reckoning, and [定](../kanji/定.md) contributes settling or determining. Together they suggest determining an amount or account. The on readings are カン + ジョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計算](計算.md) (けいさん) covers calculation more broadly. [会計](会計.md) (かいけい) overlaps in payment and accounting contexts. [支払う](支払う.md) (しはらう) directly means "pay," whereas 勘定 can name the amount or the counting process.

## Distinctive meaning

勘定に入れる (かんじょうにいれる) means "take into account"; 勘定に入れない means "leave out of the reckoning." This is the consideration sense, not a general replacement for every use of "think." Context distinguishes counting participants, paying a restaurant bill, and allowing for a factor in a decision.
