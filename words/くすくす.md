# くすくす — chuckling; giggling quietly

## Meaning

くすくす describes low, restrained laughter: chuckling or giggling rather than laughing loudly.

- 二人は写真を見て、くすくす笑っていた。 — The two of them were giggling quietly over a photo.
- 先生の言い間違いに、後ろの席で誰かがくすくすと笑った。 — Someone in a back seat chuckled at the teacher's slip of the tongue. (言い間違い: いいまちがい; 席: せき.)

Use くすくす笑う or くすくすと笑う. The expression modifies the manner of laughing rather than serving as a regular na-adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[げらげら](げらげら.md) contrasts with loud, hearty laughter. [にやにや](にやにや.md) describes a grin or smirk that need not make any sound.

## Distinctive meaning

Quiet giggling may be affectionate, embarrassed, or mocking; くすくす alone does not determine the motive. It is also not synonymous with whispering: [ひそひそ](ひそひそ.md) concerns low-voiced speech, not laughter.
