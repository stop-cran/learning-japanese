# レベル - level; standard; rank

## Meaning

レベル names a level, standard, grade or rank, such as someone's
skill or a product's quality. It can also describe the level of an amount.

- 自分のレベルに合った本を選びました。 (じぶんのレベルにあったほんをえらびました。) - I chose a book suited to my level.
- マイクの入力レベルを下げました。 (マイクのにゅうりょくレベルをさげました。) - I lowered the microphone's input level.

レベルが高い／低い (レベルがたかい／ひくい) means the level
is high or low. The relevant scale comes from the context.

## How the kanji combine

The English-derived word level is written in katakana.
There is no long-vowel mark: レベル, not レーベル,
which is a different loanword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[水準](水準.md) (すいじゅん) also means a level or standard.
[程度](程度.md) (ていど) concerns degree or extent.
[級](級.md) (きゅう) and [段](段.md) (だん) can name ranks
within particular systems; their numbers are not a universal scale.
[段階](段階.md) (だんかい) can distinguish stages or graded levels.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records a plane and a leveling or surveying
instrument. These physical uses differ from a skill level;
[水平](水平.md) (すいへい) concerns being horizontal.
The instrument is not every kind of measuring device, and a plane
here is not an aircraft.

Separate dictionary identities with the spelling レベル represent
English rebel and the name Revell. They are not additional senses
of the level word taught here.
