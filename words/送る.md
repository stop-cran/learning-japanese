# 送る (おくる) — send; escort

## Meaning

送る means to send something, including a letter or an electronic message.
With a person as its object, it can mean taking or accompanying that person to a destination.

- 友達にメールを送りました。（ともだちにメールをおくりました。） — I sent an email to a friend.
- 駅まで送ります。（えきまでおくります。） — I will take you to the station.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native verb with one kanji. [送](../kanji/送.md) is read おく, with る as okurigana.
It is a godan verb: 送ります（おくります）, 送らない（おくらない）, 送って（おくって）.

## Synonyms and antonyms

届ける（とどける）, "deliver," emphasizes getting something to its destination.
贈る（おくる） has the same pronunciation but means giving a gift or award.
受け取る（うけとる）, "receive," is the receiving side of sending an item, not the opposite of every sense of 送る.

## Distinctive meaning

For sending, a recipient is commonly marked with に: 友達にメールを送る.
For accompanying someone, まで marks how far: 駅まで送る.
The latter does not specify whether you walk or drive; the context supplies the means of travel.
