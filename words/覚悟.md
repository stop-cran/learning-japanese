# 覚悟 (かくご) - mental preparedness; resolve

## Meaning

覚悟 is readiness to face a consequence or difficulty, and can express firm resolve or acceptance of one's fate. As a noun, it appears in 覚悟がある, "be prepared." The transitive verb 覚悟する takes the anticipated outcome with を.

- 批判を受ける覚悟はあります。 (ひはんをうけるかくごはあります。) - I am prepared to face criticism.
- 失敗も覚悟して挑戦しました。 (しっぱいもかくごしてちょうせんしました。) - I made the attempt prepared for failure as well.

A plain-form verb phrase can modify the noun directly: 受ける覚悟, "readiness to receive/face."

## How the kanji combine

[覚](../kanji/覚.md) contributes awareness or awakening, and 悟 contributes understanding or realization. Their related meanings offer a reminder of clear-minded acceptance. This component explanation is not a claim that modern usage means literal awakening.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[準備](準備.md) (じゅんび) includes practical preparation such as packing supplies. 覚悟 focuses on mental readiness. [決心](決心.md) (けっしん) stresses making up one's mind; 覚悟 can additionally highlight accepting the consequences.

## Distinctive meaning

覚悟 is useful when the stakes matter. Being prepared for failure does not mean wanting failure or assuming it is certain. The expression can carry determination as well as resignation, so translating every occurrence as "giving up" would reverse its force.
