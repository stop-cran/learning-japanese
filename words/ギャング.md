# ギャング - criminal gang; gangster

## Meaning

ギャング is a noun for a criminal gang. It can also name an individual gangster, so context determines whether a group or a person is meant.

- その映画は、二つのギャングの争いを描いています。
  (そのえいがは、ふたつのギャングのあらそいをえがいています。) - The film depicts a conflict between two gangs.
- 彼は映画でギャングを演じました。
  (かれはえいがでギャングをえんじました。) - He played a gangster in a film.

The second sentence describes an acting role; it does not say that the actor belongs to a gang.

## Synonyms and antonyms

暴力団 (ぼうりょくだん) names a gangster organization or crime syndicate. [組](組.md) (くみ) has an organized-crime sense as well as ordinary group or set senses. [グループ](グループ.md) is a broader, neutral group word.

## Distinctive meaning

In ordinary crime-related use, ギャング is not simply any collection of friends. Do not transfer every friendly English use of "gang" into Japanese. Neither the group's nationality nor a particular crime is specified by the word alone.
