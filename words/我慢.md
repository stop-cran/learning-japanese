# 我慢 (がまん) - endurance; self-restraint

## Meaning

我慢 names enduring something unpleasant or restraining a desire. It is a noun and a する-verb. What someone puts up with can take を; a whole action can be expressed with verb + のを我慢する.

- 痛みを我慢しないで、医師に相談してください。 (いたみをがまんしないで、いしにそうだんしてください。) - Don't put up with the pain; consult a doctor.
- 今日は甘い物を食べるのを我慢しました。 (きょうはあまいものをたべるのをがまんしました。) - Today I resisted eating sweets.

The first example concerns bearing discomfort; the second concerns holding oneself back. 我慢できない means "cannot bear it" or "cannot resist," depending on the situation.

## How the kanji combine

我 concerns the self, and 慢 can suggest pride or arrogance. The modern meaning is not transparently obtained by adding those glosses. Learn 我慢 as the established compound がまん; the character meanings alone do not explain its semantic history.

## Synonyms and antonyms

耐える (たえる), "endure," overlaps with bearing difficulty. [苦しむ](苦しむ.md) (くるしむ) describes suffering, without itself saying that someone exercises restraint. 辛抱 (しんぼう) also concerns patient endurance.

## Distinctive meaning

我慢 is not automatically praise. 我慢して can ask someone to tolerate a difficulty, while 我慢しないで can encourage them to speak up or seek help. The situation determines whether restraint is useful; the vocabulary does not prescribe enduring every hardship.
