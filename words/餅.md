# 餅 (もち) - mochi; glutinous-rice cake

## Meaning

餅 is mochi, a rice cake made from glutinous rice.
It can be eaten in savory dishes as well as sweets; it is not automatically a dessert.
餅を焼く (もちをやく) means to grill or toast mochi.

- 正月に餅を食べます。 (しょうがつにもちをたべます。) - We eat mochi at New Year.
- 焼いた餅をお雑煮に入れました。 (やいたもちをおぞうににいれました。) - I put grilled mochi in New Year's soup.

お雑煮 (おぞうに) is a New Year's soup containing mochi; recipes vary.
These examples do not restrict mochi to that holiday.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 餅 writes the native noun もち.
Learn that whole reading rather than substituting an on reading.
餠 is a search-only variant in the pinned dictionary, not a required study spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[米](米.md) (こめ) is rice, the ingredient rather than the finished rice cake.
[菓子](菓子.md) (かし) is confectionery more broadly; some mochi preparations are sweets.
There is no useful general antonym for the food.

## Distinctive meaning

English "rice cake" can describe different foods, including crisp puffed-rice products.
Here it identifies Japanese mochi, not every food made from rice.
Likewise, a sweet whose name ends in 餅 need not have exactly the same ingredients
or preparation as plain mochi.
