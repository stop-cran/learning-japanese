# ポケット -- pocket

## Meaning

ポケット is a pocket in clothing, a bag, or a similar item. It is a small compartment for keeping things.

- 上着のポケットに鍵を入れました。 (うわぎのポケットにかぎをいれました。) -- I put the key in my jacket pocket.
- ポケットから財布を出しました。 (ポケットからさいふをだしました。) -- I took my wallet out of my pocket.

Use に for the destination when putting something in, and から for its source when taking it out.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword comes from English "pocket" and is written in katakana. The small ッ marks the consonant pause before ト. There are no kanji to break into component meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

袋 (ふくろ), a bag or sack, is broader and usually names a separate container. ポケット is typically a compartment belonging to another item. These are related storage words, not exact synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

ポケット does not mean the whole pair of trousers, jacket, or bag. Use の to identify which pocket: ズボンのポケット (ズボンのポケット), a trouser pocket, or かばんのポケット, a bag's pocket.
