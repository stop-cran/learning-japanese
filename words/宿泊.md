# 宿泊 (しゅくはく) - lodging; overnight stay

## Meaning

宿泊 is a noun for lodging or an overnight stay. 宿泊する is an intransitive verb:
the person stays, and the accommodation can take に.
It is useful in booking information and descriptions of travel arrangements.

- 駅の近くのホテルに宿泊します。 (えきのちかくのホテルにしゅくはくします。) - I will stay at a hotel near the station.
- 宿泊料金には朝食が含まれています。 (しゅくはくりょうきんにはちょうしょくがふくまれています。) - Breakfast is included in the accommodation charge.

宿泊料金 (しゅくはくりょうきん) is the charge for lodging.
宿泊した gives a past stay; 宿泊する予定です (しゅくはくするよていです) gives a planned one.

## How the kanji combine

[宿](../kanji/宿.md) contributes lodging or an inn, and 泊 contributes staying overnight.
The related meanings reinforce one another around accommodation.
Their on readings combine as シュク + ハク; the small ゅ belongs to しゅ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[泊まる](泊まる.md) (とまる) is the native verb for staying overnight and works in the same hotel example.
[旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) means travel, which may include lodging but need not do so.
A day trip contrasts with an overnight stay; it is not a lexical opposite for every use.

## Distinctive meaning

宿泊 emphasizes the accommodation aspect rather than the whole visit.
Being at a hotel restaurant for lunch does not itself establish 宿泊.
Likewise, the noun in 宿泊料金 describes what the charge is for; it does not say that every
meal or service is included. Those details require the booking conditions.
