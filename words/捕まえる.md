# 捕まえる (つかまえる) — catch; capture

## Meaning

捕まえる means catching, capturing, or arresting someone or something. It takes the caught person or thing with を.

- 子供が虫を捕まえました。 (こどもがむしをつかまえました。) — A child caught an insect.
- 警官が犯人を捕まえました。 (けいかんがはんにんをつかまえました。) — A police officer caught the culprit.

The past is 捕まえた (つかまえた), and the negative is 捕まえない (つかまえない).
The ending える does not make this a potential form: it is part of the dictionary form of the verb.

## How the kanji combine

[捕](../kanji/捕.md) supplies the capturing idea. The remaining kana complete one native verb.
Learn the whole spoken form つかまえる rather than guessing from the character's other readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

捕まる (つかまる) means "be caught" or "be arrested":
犯人が捕まった (はんにんがつかまった), "The culprit was caught."
It is an intransitive partner, describing what happens to the subject, not an alternative spelling of 捕まえる.

受け止める (うけとめる) can mean catching or stopping something coming toward you.
It provides another translation of English "catch," with a different focus from capturing an insect or culprit.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 犯人を捕まえた (はんにんをつかまえた), "caught the culprit,"
with 犯人が捕まった (はんにんがつかまった), "the culprit got caught."
The first presents an actor's action; the second presents the caught person's situation.
English "catch" has many uses, so do not apply 捕まえる to all of them automatically.
