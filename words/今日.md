# 今日 (きょう) — today

## Meaning

今日 is the current day: 今日は月曜日です (Today is Monday), 今日の天気 (today's weather), 今日中に (by the end of today).
The reading こんにち appears in formal or set phrases: 今日 こんにち (nowadays, formal), 今日は こんにちは (hello).

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) (now, this) plus [日](../kanji/日.md) (day): "this day". The reading きょう is a **jukujikun**: a whole-word reading assigned to the pair, not derived from いま+ひ or こん+にち. This is one of the special readings from the list of 日 compounds (like 昨日 きのう and 明日 あした).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Series: 昨日 きのう (yesterday), 今日 きょう, 明日 あした (tomorrow).
- 本日 ほんじつ is the formal, written or announcement equivalent.

## Distinctive meaning

Say きょう in everyday speech. こんにち is mostly in 今日は and in written phrases like 今日の社会 (today's society).
