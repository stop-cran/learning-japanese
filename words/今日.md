# 今日 (きょう) — today

## Meaning

今日 is the current day: 今日は月曜日です (Today is Monday), 今日の天気 (today's weather), 今日中に (by the end of today).
The reading こんにち can also mean "today; this day" in formal expressions,
and has a distinct "nowadays; these days" sense.

- 今日まで、この習慣が続いています。 (こんにちまで、このしゅうかんがつづいています。) - This custom has continued to this day.
- 今日の社会では、情報を確かめることが大切です。 (こんにちのしゃかいでは、じょうほうをたしかめることがたいせつです。) - In today's society, it is important to check information.

The first example covers time up to the present day; the second describes the
present era rather than one calendar day. The greeting こんにちは, "hello,"
is normally learned as its own kana-written expression.

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) (now, this) plus [日](../kanji/日.md) (day): "this day". The reading きょう is a **jukujikun**: a whole-word reading assigned to the pair, not derived from いま+ひ or こん+にち. This is one of the special readings from the list of 日 compounds (like 昨日 きのう and 明日 あした).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Series: 昨日 きのう (yesterday), 今日 きょう, 明日 あした (tomorrow).
- 本日 ほんじつ is the formal, written or announcement equivalent.

## Distinctive meaning

Use きょう as the everyday reading for the current day. Both readings belong
to the same inspected dictionary entry, but its "nowadays" sense is specifically
restricted to こんにち. Do not transfer that sense indiscriminately to きょう
or infer that こんにち cannot also refer to the present day.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1579110, senses 1-2; sense 2 is restricted to こんにち.
