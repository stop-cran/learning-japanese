# 招く (まねく) — invite

## Meaning

招く is a transitive godan verb meaning to invite in the sense taught here. A useful pattern is **person を + place に + 招く**:
invite someone to a place.

- 友達を家に招きました。 (ともだちをいえにまねきました。) — I invited a friend to my home.
- 夕食に先生を招きます。 (ゆうしょくにせんせいをまねきます。) — I will invite my teacher to dinner.

The past is 招いた (まねいた), and the negative is 招かない (まねかない).

## How the kanji combine

[招](../kanji/招.md) supplies the invitation idea. The final く is part of a native verb, not another component.
The on reading ショウ instead appears in 招待 (しょうたい), "invitation."

## Synonyms and antonyms

招待する (しょうたいする) also means inviting someone.
誘う (さそう) is useful for asking someone to join an activity:
友達を映画に誘う (ともだちをえいがにさそう), "invite a friend to a movie."
This is a useful contrast of situations, not a rule forbidding overlap between the verbs.

## Distinctive meaning

招く presents the invitation; it does not say the invited person accepted or arrived.
In the home example, the speaker is inviting someone as a guest, rather than merely proposing that they go somewhere together.
Other dictionary senses exist, but this article does not use them to blur the guest-invitation pattern.
