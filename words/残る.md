# 残る (のこる) — remain; be left

## Meaning

残る means **to remain or be left over**. The person or thing remaining is the subject, usually marked with が.

- パンが二つ残っています。 (ぱんがふたつのこっています。) — There are two pieces of bread left.
- 今日は会社に残ります。 (きょうはかいしゃにのこります。) — Today I will stay behind at the office.

It is a godan verb: 残ります, 残らない, 残った. 残っている commonly describes the resulting state, “still remaining.”

## How the kanji combine

[残](../kanji/残.md) writes のこ; る completes the native verb. The on reading ザン appears in compounds such as 残念 (ざんねん), but it does not replace のこ in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

残す (のこす) is transitive: パンを残す, “leave bread uneaten.” 残る presents the bread as what remains. なくなる, “be gone/run out,” offers a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

残る does not specify whether the remainder was intentional. Bread can remain because someone saved it or because nobody wanted it. With people, it can describe staying behind rather than leaving with everyone else.
