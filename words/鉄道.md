# 鉄道 (てつどう) - railway; rail transport

## Meaning

鉄道 is a noun for a railway or rail transport. It can refer to the transport system, its infrastructure, or railways as a field of interest. Use 鉄道で for "by rail" and 鉄道の before related nouns.

- この町には鉄道が通っています。 (このまちにはてつどうがとおっています。) - A railway runs through this town.
- 荷物は鉄道で運ばれます。 (にもつはてつどうではこばれます。) - The goods are transported by rail.

鉄道会社 (てつどうがいしゃ) means a railway company; 鉄道の歴史 (てつどうのれきし) means the history of railways.

## How the kanji combine

鉄 contributes iron, and [道](../kanji/道.md) contributes a road or route. "Iron road" is a useful component-based picture of rails forming a route. The word names the railway, not every road containing metal.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[電車](電車.md) (でんしゃ) names an electric train. 鉄道 names the railway or mode of transport rather than a particular electric vehicle. 線路 (せんろ) points more specifically to the track. [航空](航空.md), aviation, is another transport category.

## Distinctive meaning

鉄道 is not limited to passenger services or electrically powered trains. In ordinary travel talk, 電車で行く may describe taking a train; 鉄道で行く presents rail as the mode of travel. Select the word that matches whether you mean a vehicle, its track, or the larger system.
