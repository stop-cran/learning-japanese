# かわいがる (かわいがる) - cherish; dote on

## Meaning

かわいがる means to treat someone affectionately, cherish them, or dote on them. The person or animal receiving the attention takes を. It is also written 可愛がる.

- 祖母は孫をかわいがっています。 (そぼはまごをかわいがっています。) - My grandmother dotes on her grandchild.
- 家族みんなでこの猫をかわいがっています。 (かぞくみんなでこのねこをかわいがっています。) - Everyone in the family treats this cat affectionately.

Other dictionary uses include showing favoritism and petting or caressing. In colloquial, ironic speech, the word can instead describe rough treatment or harsh training. That use is a warning to interpret the context, not a recommendation.

## How the kanji combine

The formation uses the stem かわい- of [かわいい](かわいい.md) plus がる. Learn the resulting verb as affectionate treatment, not mechanically as "seems cute." It is godan despite ending in る: かわいがります, かわいがって, かわいがらない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[愛する](愛する.md) (あいする) is "love"; [愛情](愛情.md) (あいじょう) names affectionate feeling. かわいがる brings the way someone is treated into focus. [いじめる](いじめる.md), "bully; pick on," contrasts with the affectionate use but can overlap with the ironic rough-treatment use.

## Distinctive meaning

[かわいい](かわいい.md) describes someone or something as cute; かわいがる describes treatment. These are not interchangeable just because they share a stem. Nor does apparent affectionate wording guarantee fairness: favoritism is another possible interpretation.
