# 役に立つ (やくにたつ) — be useful; be helpful

## Meaning

役に立つ means to be useful or helpful. The useful thing takes が or は. To name a purpose or activity, use noun + の役に立つ.

- この辞書は勉強の役に立ちます。 (このじしょはべんきょうのやくにたちます。) — This dictionary is useful for studying.
- 教えてくれたことが役に立ちました。 (おしえてくれたことがやくにたちました。) — What you told me was helpful.

Conjugate 立つ as a godan verb: 役に立って (やくにたって), 役に立たない (やくにたたない).

## How the kanji combine

[役](../kanji/役.md), "role; service," uses on やく, while [立](../kanji/立.md) uses native た in 立つ. The particle に connects them. This is an on-first mixed expression, not a regular all-on compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

役に立たない means "not useful." 便利 (べんり), "convenient," overlaps but emphasizes ease or practicality rather than usefulness to a particular purpose.

## Distinctive meaning

Treat 役に立つ as one expression; the に inside it is required. A helpful dictionary does not literally stand up. Before a noun, use the plain form directly: 役に立つ本 (やくにたつほん), "a useful book," without な.
