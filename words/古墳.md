# 古墳 (こふん) — ancient burial mound

## Meaning

古墳 is an ancient burial mound. In Japanese history it refers to an archaeological tomb, not simply any old grave or any hill with an unusual outline.

- この古墳は上から見ると丸い。 (このこふんはうえからみるとまるい。) — This burial mound looks round from above.
- 古墳を調査しています。 (こふんをちょうさしています。) — They are investigating an ancient burial mound.

## How the kanji combine

[古](../kanji/古.md) means old or ancient and modifies [墳](../kanji/墳.md), a burial mound. Read 古 as こ here, not as the adjective 古い (ふるい).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[墓](墓.md) (はか) can be a grave of any age. [塚](塚.md) (つか) is a mound and need not be a grave at all.

## Distinctive meaning

The burial function matters. A round shape, an old date or a large pile of earth alone does not establish that a site is a 古墳.
