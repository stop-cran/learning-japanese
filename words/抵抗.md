# 抵抗 (ていこう) - resistance; opposition

## Meaning

抵抗 is resistance or opposition. For resisting a person, force, or measure, use Nに抵抗する. The thing opposed takes に, not the direct-object pattern を抵抗する.

- 住民はその計画に抵抗しました。 (じゅうみんはそのけいかくにていこうしました。) - The residents resisted that plan.
- この金属は電気抵抗が小さいです。 (このきんぞくはでんきていこうがちいさいです。) - This metal has low electrical resistance.

In physics, 抵抗 also names resistance or drag, as in 空気の抵抗 (くうきのていこう), "air resistance." 電気抵抗 can be shortened to 抵抗 in an electrical context.

## How the kanji combine

抵 contributes pressing against or opposing, and 抗 contributes resisting. Their related meanings reinforce the idea of acting against something. The on readings are テイ + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[反抗](反抗.md) (はんこう) emphasizes defiance or opposition to authority. 抵抗 also covers physical and electrical phenomena. [賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい), "agreement; support," contrasts in discussion of a proposal, but is not an electrical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

抵抗 can be psychological reluctance without open protest. 人前で話すことに抵抗がある (ひとまえではなすことにていこうがある) means "I feel reluctant to speak in front of people." Compare 抵抗する, actively resisting, with 抵抗がある, experiencing an inner barrier or reluctance.
