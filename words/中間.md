# 中間 (ちゅうかん) - middle; intermediate stage

## Meaning

中間 is the middle or an intermediate point between two ends or
stages. 中間の can describe something intermediate or interim;
established compounds also use 中間 directly before another noun.

- 駅と学校の中間に公園があります。 (えきとがっこうのちゅうかんにこうえんがあります。) - There is a park midway between the station and the school.
- 調査の中間報告を読みました。 (ちょうさのちゅうかんほうこくをよみました。) - I read the interim report on the investigation.

中間報告 (ちゅうかんほうこく) reports a stage before the final
result. It does not necessarily mean exactly half of the work is done.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) contributes middle, and [間](../kanji/間.md)
the space or interval between things. The reinforcing ideas identify
an intermediate position. The on readings are チュウ + カン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[真ん中](真ん中.md) (まんなか) emphasizes the middle or center.
[途中](途中.md) (とちゅう) places something partway through
a journey or activity. 中間 is useful when relating a position
or stage to the two sides or endpoints.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional sense is a moderate or neutral position between opposing
positions: 両者の中間の立場 (りょうしゃのちゅうかんのたちば),
"a position between the two sides."
That does not make every 中間 a political compromise.
The interim report example is about progress, not neutrality.
