# 出勤 (しゅっきん) - reporting for work; attendance at work

## Meaning

出勤 means going or reporting to work, and attendance at work. It is a noun and an intransitive する-verb: 会社に出勤する, "report to work at the company."

- 明日は九時に出勤します。 (あしたはくじにしゅっきんします。) - I will report for work at nine tomorrow.
- 今月の出勤日数を確認しました。 (こんげつのしゅっきんにっすうをかくにんしました。) - I checked the number of days I attended work this month.

The first example concerns reporting for duty, not necessarily the moment the journey begins.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md), going out, and [勤](../kanji/勤.md), work or duty, provide a learning association for going to work. Keep the small っ in しゅっきん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出席](出席.md) is attendance at an event or meeting. [通う](通う.md) emphasizes regular trips to a place, including commuting. [詰める](詰める.md) can also mean being stationed or on duty. 退勤 (たいきん), leaving work, contrasts with 出勤.

## Distinctive meaning

出勤 is not a general word for having a job or doing a particular task. A person may be employed without reporting to the workplace on a given day. In a schedule or attendance record, 出勤 contrasts with absence; context determines whether the emphasis is the arrival or the recorded attendance.
