# のびのび - freely; at ease

## Meaning

のびのび describes acting or feeling at ease without being constrained.
It is a mimetic description of a state or manner, not an imitation of a sound.

- 子どもたちは公園でのびのびと遊んでいます。
  (こどもたちはこうえんでのびのびとあそんでいます。) - The children are playing freely in the park.
- 試験が終わって、のびのびした気分です。
  (しけんがおわって、のびのびしたきぶんです。) - With the exam over, I feel relaxed and free.

Use it before a verb, with or without と, as in **のびのび遊ぶ／のびのびと遊ぶ**.
The expression **のびのびする** describes being at ease; **のびのびした気分** uses
した to modify 気分（きぶん）, "feeling."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[のんびり](のんびり.md) emphasizes being relaxed and unhurried; のびのび emphasizes
freedom from constraint. [のんき](のんき.md) describes an easygoing or unworried attitude,
which can also be critical. These words overlap but are not interchangeable in every context.

窮屈（きゅうくつ）, "constrained" or "cramped," can contrast with feeling free,
though it is not an opposite in every use.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling **伸び伸び** is used for the free, relaxed sense. Dictionaries also
record a use describing unhindered growth; the examples here focus on acting or
feeling freely. The word does not itself mean "slowly" or "a day off."

In the separate postponement use, often written **延び延び**, **のびのびになる**
means that something keeps being put off:

- 会議が延び延びになっています。
  (かいぎがのびのびになっています。) - The meeting keeps being postponed.

The spelling and context help distinguish postponement from feeling or acting freely;
the illustration on this card depicts only the latter.
