# 着替え (きがえ) - changing clothes; spare clothes

## Meaning

着替え names changing clothes or a change of clothes to put on. 着替えをする means "change clothes"; 着替えを持っていく (きがえをもっていく) means "take a change of clothes."

- 水泳の後で着替えをしました。
  (すいえいのあとできがえをしました。) - I changed clothes after swimming.
- 旅行のかばんに着替えを入れました。
  (りょこうのかばんにきがえをいれました。) - I put a change of clothes in my travel bag.

## How the kanji combine

[着](../kanji/着.md) concerns wearing clothes, and [替](../kanji/替.md) replacing something. The native forms combine as き + がえ, with か voiced to が. Learn 着替え as a whole word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

着替える (きがえる) is the related verb "change clothes." [着る](着る.md) (きる) means put on or wear clothing; [脱ぐ](脱ぐ.md) (ぬぐ) means take clothing off. A change can involve both actions, but neither verb alone says that one outfit replaces another.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the construction to distinguish an activity from replacement clothing: 着替えをする describes changing, while 着替えを用意する (きがえをよういする) prepares clothes for a change. The dictionary also recognizes 着換え, marked as a rare kanji spelling; that does not make it the recommended default here.
