# 怠る (おこたる) - neglect; fail to do

## Meaning

怠る means neglecting something one should do, or failing to give it
the required attention. In this use it is a transitive godan verb:
the duty or precaution takes を.

- 安全の確認を怠ってはいけません。 (あんぜんのかくにんをおこたってはいけません。) - You must not neglect the safety check.
- 忙しくても、毎日の練習を怠らないようにしています。 (いそがしくても、まいにちのれんしゅうをおこたらないようにしています。) - Even when busy, I try not to neglect my daily practice.

Forms include 怠ります (おこたります), 怠らない (おこたらない),
怠った (おこたった), and 怠って (おこたって).

## How the kanji combine

怠 carries neglect or laziness. The native reading here is おこたる,
with る as okurigana. The same character occurs in
[怠ける](怠ける.md), read なまける, but the verbs do not have the same
reading or conjugation class.

## Synonyms and antonyms

怠ける can describe laziness or neglecting work.
怠る focuses on the duty or care that was left undone; the words overlap,
rather than dividing all cases into accidental and deliberate failures.
[無視](無視.md) (むし), "ignoring; disregarding," is a related comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records an intransitive illness sense, "get better,"
but explicitly marks it dated. Do not use 怠る as an everyday modern
way to announce recovery from illness.
The ordinary neglect sense is negative even when the omitted task
seemed small to the person responsible.
