# 髭 (ひげ) — beard; facial hair

## Meaning

髭 means facial hair: a beard, moustache, or whiskers, depending on context. The kana spellings ひげ and ヒゲ are common and avoid an unfamiliar character.

- 父はひげを伸ばしています。  
  ちちはひげをのばしています。  
  "My father is growing a beard."
- 毎朝、ひげをそります。  
  まいあさ、ひげをそります。  
  "I shave every morning."

## How the kanji combine

髭 is one kanji read ひげ, not a compound. In careful distinctions, 髭 can specify a moustache, 鬚 a beard, and 髯 cheek whiskers. The dictionary marks the latter two spellings as rare; beginners need not enforce these distinctions in everyday kana writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

口ひげ (くちひげ) explicitly means a moustache. [毛](毛.md) (け), "hair; fur", is broader, while 髪 (かみ) means human head hair. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

ひげ also describes an animal's whiskers, as in 猫のひげ (ねこのひげ), "a cat's whiskers". English "beard" is therefore one contextual translation, not the complete range of the word.
