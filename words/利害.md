# 利害 (りがい) - advantages and disadvantages; interests at stake

## Meaning

利害 means advantages and disadvantages, or the interests that people
or organizations have at stake in a matter. It is a noun.
利害関係 (りがいかんけい) concerns such a relationship of interests.

- その計画の利害を考えました。 (そのけいかくのりがいをかんがえました。) - We considered the plan's advantages and disadvantages.
- 二つの会社は利害が一致しています。 (ふたつのかいしゃはりがいがいっちしています。) - The two companies' interests coincide.

## How the kanji combine

[利](../kanji/利.md) contributes benefit or advantage, while
[害](../kanji/害.md) contributes harm or disadvantage.
This opposite pair considers both favorable and unfavorable consequences.
The on readings are リ + ガイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[損得](損得.md) (そんとく) compares loss and gain.
[長短](長短.md) (ちょうたん) can mean merits and faults.
[利益](利益.md) (りえき) can mean profit, benefit or a party's interests;
the last use overlaps, although a benefit alone is not the whole 利害 pair.

## Distinctive meaning

English "interest" is ambiguous.
[興味](興味.md) (きょうみ) and [関心](関心.md) (かんしん) concern
curiosity or attention; 利害 concerns what someone stands to gain or lose.
It does not by itself mean that the person is behaving selfishly.
