# あなた - you

## Meaning

あなた is a second-person pronoun, "you." It is not a universal polite substitute for someone's name. Depending on the relationship and tone, it can sound distant, pointed, or rude.

- これはあなたの傘ですか。 (これはあなたのかさですか。) - Is this your umbrella?

This is grammatically ordinary, but in conversation the person's name plus さん is often a better choice than あなた.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is hiragana. Dictionary spellings include 貴方, but beginners need not use kanji for this pronoun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

私 (わたし) refers to the speaker, not the listener. A name plus さん or a role such as 先生 (せんせい) is often more appropriate when addressing a known person.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese commonly leaves an understood listener unspoken. あなた occurs naturally in questionnaires, general appeals, and some personal relationships; those settings do not make it neutral in every face-to-face exchange.
