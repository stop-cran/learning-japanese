# 工夫 (くふう) - ingenuity; devising a method

## Meaning

工夫 means thinking out a useful way of doing something, or the resulting idea, device or practical improvement. It is a noun and can take する.

- 時間を節約できるように工夫しました。 (じかんをせつやくできるようにくふうしました。) - I worked out a way to save time.
- この道具にはいろいろな工夫があります。 (このどうぐにはいろいろなくふうがあります。) - This tool incorporates various clever design ideas.

方法を工夫する (ほうほうをくふうする) means devise or improve a method. The focus is on finding a workable approach, not merely working harder.

## How the kanji combine

[工](../kanji/工.md) uses ク here and carries a craft/work association; [夫](../kanji/夫.md) uses フウ. Learn the compound's meaning as a whole. Adding the standalone English glosses of the characters does not explain ingenuity or prove a historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[考える](考える.md) (かんがえる) is the broader act of thinking or considering. [アイデア](アイデア.md) is an idea; 工夫 can describe both developing an effective approach and the practical idea embodied in it.

[発明](発明.md) (はつめい), invention, can overlap, but an everyday 工夫 need not be a wholly new invention.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse くふう with the separate homograph 工夫 (こうふ), a dated and sensitive term for a laborer. That worker meaning is not the meaning of this article's くふう. The source list mixes the worker gloss with the くふう reading; the reading-specific dictionary entry resolves the mismatch.
