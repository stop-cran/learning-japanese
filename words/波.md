# 波 (なみ) - wave; ups and downs

## Meaning

波 is a noun for a wave on water, from a ripple to a large swell. It also describes fluctuations or ups and downs.

- 今日は波が高いです。 (きょうはなみがたかいです。) - The waves are high today.
- 彼の成績には波があります。 (かれのせいせきにはなみがあります。) - His results fluctuate.

The second example's 波がある says the results are uneven, not that water is involved. 海の波 (うみのなみ) specifies sea waves; 波の音 (なみのおと) means the sound of waves.

## How the kanji combine

波 is a single-kanji native word read なみ. The visible water component 氵 is a useful reminder of the physical meaning. The dictionary also records 浪 and 濤 as rare spellings of this word; this card teaches 波.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[流れ](流れ.md) (ながれ), "flow; current," concerns flowing movement rather than necessarily a wave. [水](水.md) (みず) names the substance, not its wave form. [安定](安定.md) (あんてい), "stability," can contrast with fluctuations in performance, but is not the opposite of a sea wave.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese 波 does not name the hand gesture in English "give someone a wave." That action is 手を振る (てをふる). Keep the physical wave and the figurative rise-and-fall image separate from unrelated English uses. No particular wave height or time interval is built into the noun.
