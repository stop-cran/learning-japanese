# 売れ行き (うれゆき) - how well goods sell; sales

## Meaning

売れ行き concerns how goods are selling: their sales performance or
demand. Useful patterns are 売れ行きがいい, "sell well," and
売れ行きが悪い, "sell poorly."

- この本は売れ行きがいいです。 (このほんはうれゆきがいいです。) - This book is selling well.
- 新商品の売れ行きが気になります。 (しんしょうひんのうれゆきがきになります。) - I am concerned about how the new product is selling.

売行き is another encountered spelling, also read うれゆき. JMdict
marks it as a search-only variant; use 売れ行き as the learning form here.

## How the kanji combine

[売](../kanji/売.md) appears in 売れ (うれ), from
[売れる](売れる.md), "sell." [行](../kanji/行.md) appears in
行き (ゆき), suggesting the course or progress of those sales.
This native compound is read うれゆき, not うりいき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[売り上げ](売り上げ.md) (うりあげ) is the amount brought in by sales;
売れ行き focuses on how selling is going. [需要](需要.md) (じゅよう)
is demand or need, a related concept that does not itself say sales
have actually occurred.

## Distinctive meaning

売れ行き and 売り上げ can both be translated as "sales," but the
questions they answer differ. An amount such as 売り上げ五万円
(うりあげごまんえん), "fifty thousand yen in sales," names a sales
total; 売れ行きがいい evaluates sales performance. That is a useful
focus distinction, not a ban on using sales figures to discuss 売れ行き.
