# インキ - ink

## Meaning

インキ is ink used for writing or printing. It names the material, not
the pen or printing equipment.

- 印刷用のインキを注文しました。 (いんさつようのインキをちゅうもんしました。) - I ordered ink for printing.
- このインキは青いです。 (このインキはあおいです。) - This ink is blue.

インキの色 (インキのいろ) is the ink's color.
The word does not require the ink to be black.

## How the kanji combine

There are no kanji components. The pinned dictionary traces インキ to
Dutch inkt. Learn the Japanese ending キ rather than copying the
source word's final consonants.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[インク](インク.md) has the same material meaning. The dictionary gives
インキ its own entry and cross-references インク; that does not create
two different substances. 墨 (すみ) is traditional ink, for example in
calligraphy, with a more specific context.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer a strict printer-versus-pen boundary between インキ and
インク from these examples. The type, color, and use of an ink need
additional description. Ink has no natural lexical antonym.
