# じんわり — gradually; in a slow, spreading way

## Meaning

じんわり describes a sensation or change that spreads or develops gradually.

- 温かい飲み物を飲むと、体がじんわり温まった。
  — After drinking something warm, I gradually felt warmth spread through my body.
- 久しぶりに先生の声を聞き、胸がじんわり温かくなった。
  — Hearing my teacher's voice after a long time made a gentle warmth spread through my heart.

## Synonyms and antonyms

じんわり emphasizes a gradual, spreading effect. It does not describe an abrupt or sudden change.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can also describe sweat or tears slowly welling or seeping out; context indicates what is
spreading.
