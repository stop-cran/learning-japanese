# 幸せ (しあわせ) - happiness; happy

## Meaning

幸せ is a noun for happiness or good fortune and also behaves as a na-adjective:
**幸せな生活** (しあわせなせいかつ), "a happy life"; **幸せです**, "am/is happy."
Use **幸せを感じる** (しあわせをかんじる) for "feel happiness."

- 家族と食事ができて、幸せです。 (かぞくとしょくじができて、しあわせです。) - I am happy to be able to eat with my family.
- 二人の幸せを願っています。 (ふたりのしあわせをねがっています。) - I wish the two of them happiness.

The first sentence describes a happy state. In the second, 幸せ is the noun object of 願う, "wish for."
幸せになる means "become happy."

## How the kanji combine

[幸](../kanji/幸.md) represents happiness or good fortune in this native word.
Learn the whole reading しあわせ with the written ending せ.
Do not read it as こう, the on reading used in [幸福](幸福.md) (こうふく).

## Synonyms and antonyms

幸福 overlaps with happiness and well-being.
[うれしい](うれしい.md) describes feeling glad, often in response to something.
[不幸](不幸.md) (ふこう), "unhappiness/misfortune," supplies a useful opposite.
[幸運](幸運.md) (こううん) emphasizes good luck.

## Distinctive meaning

幸せ can describe a happy moment or a broader sense of well-being; it is not restricted to lifelong contentment.
The grammar distinguishes it from うれしい: say 幸せな時間 (しあわせなじかん),
but うれしい知らせ (うれしいしらせ), without な after the い-adjective.
