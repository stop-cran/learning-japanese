# 貸間 (かしま) - room for rent

## Meaning

貸間 means a room offered for rent. 貸し間 is another spelling of the same かしま.

- 駅の近くで貸間を探しています。 (えきのちかくでかしまをさがしています。) - I am looking for a room to rent near the station.
- 窓に「貸間あり」と書いてありました。 (まどに「かしまあり」とかいてありました。) - The window had a sign saying "Room for rent."

The word names the rental room, not the sum of money paid each month.

## How the kanji combine

[貸](../kanji/貸.md) supplies the lending/renting-out idea through 貸し (かし), the stem of [貸す](貸す.md). [間](../kanji/間.md) is read ま here and supplies the room or space. 貸し modifies 間: a room to let. The し remains in the pronunciation even when not written in 貸間.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[部屋](部屋.md) (へや) means a room more generally. [貸家](貸家.md) (かしや) names a house for rent, and [家賃](家賃.md) (やちん) is housing rent as a payment.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read this native compound as a string of on readings. A 貸間 is not automatically a hotel room, an entire house, or a particular legal tenancy arrangement; those details require context.
