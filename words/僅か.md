# 僅か (わずか) - slight; only a little

## Meaning

僅か describes a small amount, slight degree, or narrow margin. It is usually written わずか. Use わずかな before a noun and わずかに before a verb describing a slight change. わずか before a quantity means "only" or "just."

- 二人の点数にはわずかな差がありました。 (ふたりのてんすうにはわずかなさがありました。) - There was a slight difference between their scores.
- 駅までわずか五分です。 (えきまでわずかごふんです。) - It is only five minutes to the station.

気温がわずかに上がりました (きおんがわずかにあがりました) means "The temperature rose slightly."

## How the kanji combine

僅 carries the small-amount association, with か written as the ending of わずか. This is a native word, not an on-reading compound. The kana spelling is also appropriate in ordinary text.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[少し](少し.md) (すこし) and [ちょっと](ちょっと.md) overlap with "a little." わずか emphasizes how small the amount or difference is; its grammar is not identical to theirs.

## Distinctive meaning

わずか五分 evaluates five minutes as a short time; it does not change the number. This "only" use does not require a negative predicate. Do not automatically replace it with しか while leaving a positive ending unchanged.
