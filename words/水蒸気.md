# 水蒸気 (すいじょうき) - water vapor; steam

## Meaning

水蒸気 is water in its gaseous state: water vapor, also called steam. It is a noun, not the action of boiling or evaporating.

- 水が蒸発すると、水蒸気になります。 (みずがじょうはつすると、すいじょうきになります。) - When water evaporates, it becomes water vapor.
- 空気中には水蒸気が含まれています。 (くうきちゅうにはすいじょうきがふくまれています。) - The air contains water vapor.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md), water, specifies the substance in [蒸気](蒸気.md), vapor: [蒸](../kanji/蒸.md) concerns steaming, and [気](../kanji/気.md) contributes the gaseous sense here. The readings combine as スイ + ジョウ + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

蒸気 can refer to vapor more generally; 水蒸気 explicitly identifies water. [気体](気体.md) is the broader word for a gas or gaseous substance. [蒸発](蒸発.md) names evaporation, the process rather than the vapor itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Water vapor itself is colorless and invisible. The visible white cloud commonly called 湯気 (ゆげ), or "steam" in everyday English, consists of tiny liquid water droplets formed when vapor cools. The ordinary English translation "steam" is still useful; do not infer that gaseous water must look white.
