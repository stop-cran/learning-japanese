# 除く (のぞく) — remove; exclude

## Meaning

除く means removing something or excluding it from a set. It is transitive: を marks what is removed or left out.
The polite form is 除きます (のぞきます); its て-form is 除いて (のぞいて).

- 野菜の種を除きます。 (やさいのたねをのぞきます。) — I remove the seeds from the vegetables.
- 日曜日を除いて、毎日開いています。 (にちようびをのぞいて、まいにちあいています。) — It is open every day except Sunday.

## How the kanji combine

[除](../kanji/除.md) carries removal or exclusion, and く completes the native verb のぞく.
The same basic idea works for a physical object and for a member left out of a stated group.

## Synonyms and antonyms

取り除く (とりのぞく) also means taking something away.
[含む](含む.md) (ふくむ), "include; contain," provides the useful contrast for sets:
日曜日を含む (にちようびをふくむ) includes Sunday, while 日曜日を除く excludes it.

## Distinctive meaning

An exception does not imply destruction or rejection of the thing itself.
In a schedule, Xを除く changes which days the statement applies to. Check the noun before を carefully:
that is the exception, not the permitted group.
