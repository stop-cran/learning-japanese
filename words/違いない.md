# 違いない (ちがいない) - surely; there is no doubt

## Meaning

違いない expresses strong certainty, especially in **に違いない**:
"must be; surely." Use a plain-form verb or い-adjective before に.
For a present affirmative noun or な-adjective, omit だ: 学生に違いない
(がくせいにちがいない), "must be a student"; 静かに違いない
(しずかにちがいない), "must be quiet."

- 電気がついている。誰か家にいるに違いない。 (でんきがついている。だれかいえにいるにちがいない。) - The light is on. Someone must be home.
- このかばんは高かったに違いない。 (このかばんはたかかったにちがいない。) - This bag must have been expensive.

The polite ending is に違いありません (にちがいありません).

## How the kanji combine

[違](../kanji/違.md) contributes difference or error. 違い (ちがい) plus
ない suggests "no mistake"; learn the whole expression for certainty rather
than reading it as an ordinary comparison of two objects.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[きっと](きっと.md) also expresses confidence or a strong expectation.
[確か](確か.md) (たしか) has uses involving certainty, but its constructions
differ. かもしれない, "might," expresses possibility instead of the same
degree of commitment.

## Distinctive meaning

This is the speaker's confident conclusion, not a guarantee that the conclusion
is true. Tense and negation can stay inside it: 来なかったに違いない
(こなかったにちがいない), "must not have come"; 学生だったに違いない,
"must have been a student." The English "must" expresses inference here, not
an instruction or obligation.
