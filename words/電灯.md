# 電灯 (でんとう)

## Meaning

電灯 means an electric light or lamp. It identifies lighting powered by electricity, rather than light from any source.

- 暗いので、電灯をつけました。 (くらいので、でんとうを つけました。) — It was dark, so I switched on the light.

Use 電灯をつける to switch it on and 電灯を消す (でんとうをけす) to switch it off. The lamp takes を.

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) contributes electricity and 灯 contributes a lamp or light. The on readings are でん + とう. The older spelling 電燈 has the same reading, but 電灯 is the normal modern form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

明かり (あかり) is illumination more generally. 電気 (でんき) means electricity but also commonly refers to the lights in everyday requests. 電球 (でんきゅう) specifically names a light bulb.

## Distinctive meaning

電灯 identifies the lighting device or electric lighting. It is not a synonym for every electrical appliance. There is no direct antonym; switching on and switching off are contrasting actions involving the same light.
