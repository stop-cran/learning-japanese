# 平ら (たいら) - flat; level; even

## Meaning

平ら is a na-adjective meaning "flat; level; even." For this physical use, put **平らな** before a noun and **平らに** before an action that produces or uses a flat state.

- 平らな場所に箱を置いてください。 (たいらなばしょにはこをおいてください。) - Please put the box on a level surface.
- 土を平らにしました。 (つちをたいらにしました。) - I leveled the soil.

The second example's にする means "make it ..."; 平ら describes the resulting surface. The predicate can be 平らです, "is flat."

It can also describe a calm or composed state: 心を平らにする
(こころをたいらにする), "calm one's mind." Here the にする pattern concerns
a mental state, not flattening a physical surface.

## How the kanji combine

[平](../kanji/平.md) contributes levelness or evenness, with ら completing the native word たいら. Do not treat the final sound as an i-adjective ending: the noun connector is な.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[丸い](丸い.md) (まるい), "round," describes a different aspect of shape: a round tabletop can still have a flat surface. でこぼこ, "uneven; bumpy," is a useful contrast when discussing the surface.

## Distinctive meaning

Flatness is not the same as softness, straightness, or smoothness to the touch in every context. Specify the surface being described. A 平らな道 (たいらなみち) emphasizes level or even ground; it need not say that the road follows a straight route.
