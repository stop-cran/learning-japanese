# 何とか (なんとか) - somehow; one way or another

## Meaning

何とか is an adverb meaning "somehow" or "one way or another," especially when
managing a difficulty. With する, 何とかする means to do something about a problem
or find a way to handle it. The kana spelling なんとか is also usual.

- 急いだので、何とか間に合いました。
  (いそいだので、なんとかまにあいました。) - I hurried and somehow managed to make it in time.
- この問題を何とかしなければなりません。
  (このもんだいをなんとかしなければなりません。)
  - We have to do something about this problem.

何とかしてください asks someone to find a way to deal with the situation.
It does not specify the exact action they should take.

## How the kanji combine

[何](../kanji/何.md) contributes an unspecified element and is read なん here.
The kana とか complete the expression. Learn the whole use rather than translating
it word for word as an ordinary question beginning with "what."

## Synonyms and antonyms

どうにか also means somehow or with some effort.
[何か](何か.md) (なにか), "something," can name an unspecified thing, but does not
by itself express the managed success of 何とか間に合った.

## Distinctive meaning

何とか concerns finding or managing a way, not necessarily an unexplained cause.
In a completed-action sentence it can suggest succeeding despite difficulty;
in a request it expresses the wish for a solution, without guaranteeing success.
The phrase is therefore not limited to past-tense statements.
