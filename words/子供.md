# 子供 (こども) — child

## Meaning

子供 is a child, a young person: 子供の日 こどものひ (Children's Day, 5 May), 子供が二人いる (I have two children), 子供っぽい こどもっぽい (childish).
It can also mean "one's own children" regardless of age: 子供が大学生だ.

## How the kanji combine

[子](../kanji/子.md) (child) plus [供](../kanji/供.md), a kanji whose usual meanings are "accompany" and "offer" (とも, キョウ). In こども the ども is an old plural or collective
suffix (as in 私ども わたくしども), and 供 is commonly explained as an ateji (a kanji chosen for its sound or look rather than its meaning) for it that became standard in writing. The reading is a native Japanese word, not on readings.
The spelling 子ども is also common, partly because some people feel that 供 ("attendant") is an unfortunate character for children;
publishers, advocacy groups and some agencies use 子ども, but official usage is mixed (こども家庭庁 uses kana, and 子供 remains common).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 子 こ (a child, as in 女の子), 児童 じどう (a school-age child, official), 幼児 ようじ (young child; toddler or preschooler).
- Antonym: [大人](大人.md) おとな (adult).

## Distinctive meaning

子供 is the neutral everyday word and can be said of one's own child at any age, while 児童 and 幼児 are specific age terms.
