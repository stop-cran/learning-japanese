# 障害 (しょうがい) - obstacle; impairment

## Meaning

障害 is a noun for an obstacle or hindrance, and also for a disability, impairment, or dysfunction. The surrounding phrase identifies the sense. Use 障害になる (しょうがいになる) for becoming an obstacle, or 障害がある for having an impairment or obstruction in the relevant context.

- 資金不足が計画の障害になっています。 (しきんぶそくがけいかくのしょうがいになっています。) - A lack of funds is an obstacle to the plan.
- 彼は聴覚に障害があります。 (かれはちょうかくにしょうがいがあります。) - He has a hearing impairment.

通信障害 (つうしんしょうがい) refers to a communications disruption or malfunction, not a person's disability.

## How the kanji combine

障 concerns obstruction; [害](../kanji/害.md) contributes harm or interference. Together they name impediment or impaired functioning. The readings are ショウ + ガイ. Component associations are not a judgment about a person's value.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[問題](問題.md) (もんだい) is a broader problem or issue. [故障](故障.md) (こしょう) concerns breakdown or malfunction, especially of equipment, and should not replace 障害 indiscriminately when discussing people.

## Distinctive meaning

障害 is not a blanket translation for every kind of physical "damage." Specify the barrier, affected function, or system. The spelling 障がい is also used; changing the written form does not itself change the pronunciation.
