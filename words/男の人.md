# 男の人 (おとこのひと) - man

## Meaning

男の人 is a noun phrase meaning "man." It is useful when identifying or describing a person without knowing his name. The entire phrase behaves as a noun: 男の人が for the subject, 男の人に for a recipient.

- あの男の人は先生です。 (あのおとこのひとはせんせいです。) - That man is a teacher.
- 駅で男の人に道を聞きました。 (えきでおとこのひとにみちをききました。) - I asked a man for directions at the station.

In the second sentence, に marks the person asked and を marks what was asked about. The phrase itself does not specify one man versus several; context and numbers do that.

## How the kanji combine

[男](../kanji/男.md) (おとこ), "male; man," modifies [人](../kanji/人.md) (ひと), "person," through の. Both are native readings. Keep the connecting の: this is not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[男性](男性.md) (だんせい) also means a male person and suits classifications or descriptions. [男の子](男の子.md) (おとこのこ) means "boy." 女の人 (おんなのひと), "woman," is a gender contrast, not an evaluative opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

男の人 identifies a person; it does not mean "husband" or "boyfriend." It can be a less blunt way of referring to someone than bare [男](男.md), but it is not a respectful form of direct address. Use a person's name or appropriate title when addressing them.
