# 食べる (たべる) — to eat

## Meaning

食べる is to eat: ご飯を食べる (eat a meal), 朝ご飯を食べました, 何を食べますか (what will you eat?), 食べ物 たべもの (food).
The object takes を, and the place of eating takes で: 家で食べる.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) with the kun reading た plus okurigana べる. Ichidan verb (る-verb): polite 食べます, negative 食べない, て-form 食べて, past 食べた, potential 食べられる, volitional 食べよう.
The noun form 食べ (as in 食べ物, 食べ方 たべかた) is the stem. 食べ放題 たべほうだい is "all you can eat".

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Casual or rough: 食う くう (blunt, masculine), かじる (to nibble).
- Respectful: 召し上がる めしあがる; humble: いただく.
- Related: [飲む](飲む.md) のむ (drink), 食事 しょくじ (a meal).

## Distinctive meaning

食べる is the neutral word. Soup is usually 飲む, not 食べる, with exceptions (スープを飲む／食べる are both heard). 食う is rough in tone and 召し上がる is only for other people's eating.
