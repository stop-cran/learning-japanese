# 熱する (ねっする) - heat up; get worked up

## Meaning

熱する means heating something or something becoming hot.
It also means becoming excited or worked up.
It can be transitive or intransitive; do not assume every use takes an object.
Learn 熱します (ねっします), 熱した (ねっした),
熱して (ねっして) and 熱しない (ねっしない).

- フライパンをよく熱してから油を入れます。
  (フライパンをよくねっしてからあぶらをいれます。) - I heat the frying pan well before adding oil.
- 議論が熱してきました。
  (ぎろんがねっしてきました。) - The discussion has become heated.

The pan takes を as the thing heated.
In the second example, が marks the discussion becoming heated;
てきた presents the developing change.

## How the kanji combine

[熱](../kanji/熱.md) contributes heat, and する forms the verb.
The whole reading is ねっする, with a small っ.
This is not the adjective [熱い](熱い.md) (あつい), "hot."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[加熱](加熱.md)する (かねつする) concerns applying heat.
[暖める](暖める.md) (あたためる) overlaps with warming;
[沸かす](沸かす.md) (わかす) commonly brings liquid to a boil.
[あぶる](あぶる.md) concerns warming or toasting by exposure to heat.
[冷ます](冷ます.md) (さます), "let cool," contrasts with the transitive heating use.

## Distinctive meaning

熱する does not require reaching the boiling point, nor does it always refer
to cooking. Its emotional sense is not a claim about a measured body temperature.
熱しやすく冷めやすい (ねっしやすくさめやすい), "quick to get enthusiastic
and quick to lose interest," concerns changing enthusiasm in its figurative use.
[熱中](熱中.md) (ねっちゅう), absorption or enthusiasm, overlaps but does not
make the two words interchangeable in every construction.
