# 党 (とう) - political party

## Meaning

党 is a political party. It works as a noun and as the final element of party names. Use 党に入る (とうにはいる), "join a party," or 党を支持する (とうをしじする), "support a party."

- 彼は新しい党を作りました。 (かれはあたらしいとうをつくりました。) - He founded a new political party.
- この党は教育を重視しています。 (このとうはきょういくをじゅうししています。) - This party places importance on education.

支持する means supporting a party; it does not by itself establish formal membership.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 党 supplies the noun with the on reading トウ. In 政党 (せいとう), "political party," 政 makes the political domain explicit. 党 is not a verb meaning "hold a celebration."

## Synonyms and antonyms

政党 is a close political synonym. [パーティー](パーティー.md) refers to a social party or gathering, so English "party" does not identify the Japanese word without context. [政府](政府.md) (せいふ) is a government, not the same thing as a political party.

## Distinctive meaning

As a suffix, 党 can also indicate a person's liking: 甘党 (あまとう) is someone fond of sweets, not a political organization devoted to sugar. This is a separate use of the same entry. Neither political membership nor the fondness suffix implies an opposite word formed by simply removing 党.
