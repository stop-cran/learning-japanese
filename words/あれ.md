# あれ - that over there

## Meaning

あれ stands for something away from both speaker and listener: "that" or "that
thing over there." It is a pronoun, so it can take a particle without a following noun.

- あれは何ですか。 (あれはなんですか。) - What is that over there?
- あれを買いたいです。 (あれをかいたいです。) - I want to buy that one over there.

## How the kanji combine

Write this demonstrative in hiragana. Rare dictionary spellings with 彼 are not
needed for everyday writing and should not be confused with 彼 (かれ), "he."

## Synonyms and antonyms

これ is "this" near the speaker; それ is "that" near the listener or referring
back to something mentioned. あの must precede a noun: あの本 (あのほん), "that book."
These are contrasting demonstratives, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

あれ can also refer to something both people recognize without naming it.
Do not confuse this pronoun with the separate interjection あれ？, "Huh?",
which expresses surprise rather than pointing to an object.
