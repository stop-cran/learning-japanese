# 湾 (わん) - bay; gulf; inlet

## Meaning

湾 is a noun for a bay, gulf, or inlet: a part of the sea extending into the land. It also works as a suffix in place names, such as 東京湾 (とうきょうわん), "Tokyo Bay."

- この湾は波が穏やかです。 (このわんはなみがおだやかです。) - The waves in this bay are calm.
- 船は東京湾を出ました。 (ふねはとうきょうわんをでました。) - The ship left Tokyo Bay.

湾の入口 (わんのいりぐち) means "the entrance to the bay." In the second example, を marks the place being left.

## How the kanji combine

湾 is a single character with the on reading ワン. It supplies the whole geographic noun. In a name such as 東京湾, the preceding part identifies which bay is meant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

入り江 (いりえ) also describes an inlet. [海](海.md) (うみ) is the broader sea. A harbor is a related place used by boats; a 湾 is a coastal formation and need not be developed as a port.

## Distinctive meaning

English distinguishes names such as "bay" and "gulf," but 湾 can correspond to either. Use an established English place name where there is one rather than deciding the translation from size alone. The word also does not promise sheltered weather: the calm waves in the example are an observation, not part of its definition.
