# ずっと - the whole time; much more

## Meaning

ずっと is an adverb with two especially useful uses: continuity through a period, and a large difference in a comparison. Place it before the verb or description whose duration or degree it modifies.

- 昨日はずっと家にいました。 (きのうはずっといえにいました。) - I was at home all day yesterday.
- このかばんはあのかばんよりずっと軽いです。 (このかばんはあのかばんよりずっとかるいです。) - This bag is much lighter than that one.

The first example concerns uninterrupted time. In the second, より gives the comparison point and ずっと means "much," not "continuously."

## How the kanji combine

ずっと is written in hiragana. Keep the small っ, which marks the held consonant before と. The emphatic spelling ずーっと stretches the sound; it does not create a new part of speech.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いつも](いつも.md) means "always" or "usually" across occasions, whereas ずっと can describe continuity within one specified period. [もっと](もっと.md), "more," asks for or describes an increase without necessarily emphasizing such a large difference.

## Distinctive meaning

ずっと can also express distance in time or space: ずっと前 (ずっとまえ) is "long ago," and ずっと遠く (ずっととおく) is "far away." Do not translate every occurrence as "all the time." The following predicate and any comparison phrase determine whether duration, distance, or degree is intended.
