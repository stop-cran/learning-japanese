# 投票 (とうひょう) - voting; ballot

## Meaning

投票 is a noun for voting or a ballot, and **投票する** means "vote." Use **候補者に投票する** (こうほしゃにとうひょうする), "vote for a candidate." に marks the person or option chosen.

- 私は田中さんに投票しました。 (わたしはたなかさんにとうひょうしました。) - I voted for Mr./Ms. Tanaka.
- 明日は投票に行きます。 (あしたはとうひょうにいきます。) - I will go to vote tomorrow.

In the second example, に follows the noun 投票 to show the purpose of going. The setting determines whether the vote is in an election or another decision.

## How the kanji combine

[投](../kanji/投.md) contributes casting or putting in, and 票 contributes a ballot or vote. トウ + ヒョウ gives とうひょう: casting a ballot. Modern voting need not involve physically throwing anything.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい) means agreement or support; [反対](反対.md) (はんたい) means opposition. A vote can express either, so 投票 is not a synonym for agreeing. 選挙 (せんきょ) names an election, the broader process.

## Distinctive meaning

投票 says that a choice is formally registered; it does not reveal which side the voter supports unless the sentence supplies it. Distinguish 投票した, "voted," from 当選した (とうせんした), "was elected." One describes a voter's action, the other a candidate's result.
