# 枝 (えだ) - branch; twig

## Meaning

枝 names a branch or twig of a tree or plant. 木の枝 (きのえだ) means "a tree branch"; の connects the tree with its branch.

- 鳥が枝に止まっています。 (とりがえだにとまっています。) - A bird is perched on a branch.

Here に marks where the bird is perched. 一本の枝 (いっぽんのえだ) is "one branch," using the counter for long, slender things.

## How the kanji combine

枝 is one kanji read えだ in this native noun. Its tree component is a useful clue to the physical meaning, not a rule that every branching object must be called 枝.

## Synonyms and antonyms

幹 (みき) is a tree's trunk, 根 (ね) its roots, and 葉 (は) its leaves. 小枝 (こえだ) specifically names a small branch or twig.

## Distinctive meaning

English "branch" also names a local office or store. A bank branch is normally 支店 (してん), not simply 枝. Learn the plant sense before extending the English translation.
