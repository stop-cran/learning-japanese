# 領収 (りょうしゅう) - receipt of money; receive payment

## Meaning

領収 names receiving money. With する it is transitive:
代金を領収する (だいきんをりょうしゅうする),
"receive payment." を marks what is received.

- 代金を領収しました。 (だいきんをりょうしゅうしました。) - We have received payment.
- 会費を領収いたしました。 (かいひをりょうしゅういたしました。) - We have received your membership fee.

領収いたしました is a more deferential way to report the action,
using いたす rather than changing the noun's meaning.

## How the kanji combine

[領](../kanji/領.md) has a receiving sense in this compound, and
[収](../kanji/収.md) contributes taking in or collecting.
The two characters reinforce receiving, rather than naming a document.
The on readings are リョウ + シュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[受け取る](受け取る.md) (うけとる) is a broader receiving verb.
[受け取り](受け取り.md) (うけとり) can name either receiving or a
paper receipt; that second meaning must not be transferred automatically.
領収書 (りょうしゅうしょ), 領収証 (りょうしゅうしょう) and レシート
name receipt documents.

## Distinctive meaning

English "receipt" can mean an action or a piece of paper.
領収 here is the receiving action, not the source gloss's "voucher."
For "Please give me a receipt," ask for a 領収書 or レシート,
not simply 領収.
