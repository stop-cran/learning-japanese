# 廊下 (ろうか)

## Meaning

廊下 means a corridor or hallway connecting rooms or parts of a building.

- 廊下を走らないでください。 (ろうかを はしらないでください。) — Please do not run in the hallway.
- 廊下で先生に会いました。 (ろうかで せんせいに あいました。) — I met the teacher in the corridor.

を can mark the route traversed with a movement verb; で marks where an event occurs.

## How the kanji combine

廊 concerns a corridor or covered passage, and [下](../kanji/下.md) is read か in this compound. Learn ろう + か, keeping the long ろう. The word does not mean "downstairs" or literally "under the corridor."

## Synonyms and antonyms

通路 (つうろ) is a passage or route and is broader. 階段 (かいだん) means stairs; 部屋 (へや) means a room. These name other parts of a building, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

廊下 is the connecting passage, not every open area inside a building. English "hall" may also mean an entrance lobby or large room, so check the intended physical space rather than translating mechanically.
