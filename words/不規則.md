# 不規則 (ふきそく) - irregular; unsystematic

## Meaning

不規則 describes lacking a regular pattern, system, or rhythm.
It is a noun and na-adjective: 不規則な生活
(ふきそくなせいかつ), an irregular lifestyle.
Use 不規則に for doing something irregularly.

- 最近、食事の時間が不規則です。 (さいきん、しょくじのじかんがふきそくです。) - Recently, my mealtimes have been irregular.
- この動詞は不規則に変化します。 (このどうしはふきそくにへんかします。) - This verb changes irregularly.

The second concerns a grammatical pattern, not a verb behaving
dishonestly.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) negates
[規](../kanji/規.md) and [則](../kanji/則.md), the two
rule-related characters in [規則](規則.md) (きそく).
The compound describes an absence of regular order.
The on readings are フ + キ + ソク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

規則的 (きそくてき), regular or systematic, supplies a contextual
opposite. 不安定 (ふあんてい) concerns instability;
some irregular rhythms are unstable, but the words do not always
describe the same problem.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can also describe unsteadiness or disorderliness in a
pattern or arrangement. It does not automatically accuse someone
of deliberately breaking rules.
[不正](不正.md) (ふせい) can concern wrongdoing, dishonesty, or
an improper irregularity; an irregular work schedule alone does
not establish any of those accusations.
