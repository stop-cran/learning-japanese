# 伸ばす (のばす) - stretch; lengthen; grow out

## Meaning

伸ばす is a transitive godan verb for making something longer, stretching or straightening it, or letting it grow long. Mark the affected thing with を.

- 髪を伸ばしています。 (かみをのばしています。) - I am growing my hair out.
- 棚の上の本に手を伸ばしました。 (たなのうえのほんにてをのばしました。) - I reached out toward the book on the shelf.

足を伸ばす (あしをのばす) can mean stretching or straightening one's legs. A destination marked with に, as in the second example, tells you what someone is reaching toward.

## How the kanji combine

伸 carries the extension meaning; ばす completes the native verb のばす. Its forms include 伸ばします (のばします), 伸ばして (のばして), and 伸ばさない (のばさない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[伸びる](伸びる.md) (のびる) is the intransitive counterpart: 髪が伸びる, "hair grows longer," versus 髪を伸ばす, "grow one's hair out." 縮める (ちぢめる), "shorten; shrink," contrasts with physical extension.

## Distinctive meaning

延ばす has the same reading and shares the dictionary entry. It is particularly associated with postponement, as in 予定を延ばす (よていをのばす), "postpone the plan." These are spelling preferences within related meanings, not proof that every use belongs to a completely separate word.
