# 好み (このみ) - preference; personal taste

## Meaning

好み is a person's taste, liking, or preference. Use person + の + 好み to
identify whose taste it is, and 好みの + noun for a preferred kind of thing.

- この色は私の好みです。 (このいろはわたしのこのみです。) - This color is to my taste.
- 好みの味を選んでください。 (このみのあじをえらんでください。) - Please choose the flavor you prefer.

お好みで (おこのみで) means "according to your preference," for example when
letting someone decide how much seasoning to add.

## How the kanji combine

[好](../kanji/好.md) contributes liking. 好み is related to the native verb
好む (このむ), "like; prefer," and uses この rather than the す in 好き
(すき). This is one word with okurigana, not a multi-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[好き](好き.md) (すき) describes liking something: この色が好きです.
好み names the preference itself. [趣味](趣味.md) (しゅみ) can mean a hobby
or taste, but 好み is not a replacement for every use of "hobby."

## Distinctive meaning

好み is subjective. 好みが違う (このみがちがう), "have different tastes," need
not mean that one choice is wrong. Also distinguish personal "taste" from
the flavor of food: 味 (あじ) is the flavor; 味の好み (あじのこのみ) is a
preference about flavors.
