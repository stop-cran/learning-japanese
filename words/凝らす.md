# 凝らす (こらす) — concentrate; apply effort

## Meaning

凝らす directs concentrated attention or effort. Learn it with established
objects marked by **を**, rather than using it for every English "focus."

- 目を凝らして見ました。（めをこらしてみました。）— I looked closely, straining my eyes.
- 工夫を凝らしました。（くふうをこらしました。）— I exercised my ingenuity.

目を凝らす（めをこらす）concerns looking intently. 工夫を凝らす
（くふうをこらす）concerns inventive effort, not staring at an idea.
The verb is transitive and godan: 凝らします（こらします）,
凝らして（こらして）, 凝らさない（こらさない）.

## How the kanji combine

[凝](../kanji/凝.md) writes こ here, with らす as the written ending.
This is one native verb, not a compound of separate words for "concentration"
and "doing."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[集中](集中.md)（しゅうちゅう）overlaps in focusing attention.
[注ぐ](注ぐ.md)（そそぐ）can describe devoting energy or attention.
Their objects and particles must still fit the particular expression.

[凝る](凝る.md)（こる）instead describes stiffness, absorption, or elaborate
care: compare ゴルフに凝る, "be really into golf," with 工夫を凝らす,
"exercise one's ingenuity." The latter takes 工夫 as its object.

## Distinctive meaning

The object affects the translation. 息を凝らす（いきをこらす）means
"hold one's breath," not "stare at one's breath."

The same spelling has a separately recorded reading **こごらす**, including
"cause to congeal; freeze." This article teaches **こらす**.
凝らせる can be the potential form こらせる of this godan verb; the dictionary
verb 凝らせる **こごらせる**, "cause to congeal," is a different reading.
The homophone 懲らす（こらす）means "punish; chastise," not concentrate.
