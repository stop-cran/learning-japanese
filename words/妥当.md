# 妥当 (だとう) - reasonable; appropriate; valid

## Meaning

妥当 describes a judgment, amount, method, or conclusion as fitting
the circumstances and being reasonable. Learn 妥当な + noun,
妥当です, and 妥当ではない, "not reasonable/appropriate."

- この金額は妥当だと思います。
  (このきんがくはだとうだとおもいます。) - I think this amount is reasonable.
- その結論が妥当かどうか、もう一度考えましょう。
  (そのけつろんがだとうかどうか、もういちどかんがえましょう。) - Let's reconsider whether that conclusion is sound.

妥当な判断 (だとうなはんだん) is a reasonable or appropriate
judgment. な connects the adjective to a following noun.

## How the kanji combine

妥 contributes suitability or acceptability, and [当](../kanji/当.md)
contributes fitting or matching. These related meanings reinforce
an assessment of aptness. The on readings are ダ + トウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[適切](適切.md) (てきせつ) and the suitable sense of
[適当](適当.md) (てきとう) overlap. [もっとも](もっとも.md)
can mean reasonable or justified. 不適切 (ふてきせつ),
"inappropriate," is a useful contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

妥当 evaluates fit or soundness, not just personal liking.
It does not promise certainty or official validity in every setting:
a ticket being valid through tomorrow is naturally
[有効](有効.md) (ゆうこう). The dictionary also records a
する use for applying or fitting, but the な-adjective patterns
above are the focus here.
