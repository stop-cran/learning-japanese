# つまり (つまり) - in other words; in short

## Meaning

つまり is an adverb used to restate, explain, or summarize what has just been said:
"in other words," "that is," or "in short."
It can introduce a whole clause without a special attachment ending.

- 彼は私の母の弟、つまり私のおじです。 (かれはわたしのははのおとうと、つまりわたしのおじです。) - He is my mother's younger brother, in other words, my uncle.
- 時間もお金もありません。つまり、今は旅行できません。 (じかんもおかねもありません。つまり、いまはりょこうできません。) - I have neither time nor money. In short, I cannot travel now.

The first example gives an equivalent description; the second draws together the practical conclusion.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual for these explanatory uses. The dictionary also records 詰まり and 詰り.
Learn つまり as the connector in these examples, without forcing a literal kanji explanation.
The same entry has other noun senses, but they are not needed to interpret "in other words."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すなわち](すなわち.md) also introduces a restatement.
[要するに](要するに.md) (ようするに) emphasizes summing up the essential point.
[だから](だから.md), "therefore/because of that," stresses a consequence rather than necessarily rephrasing the same information.

## Distinctive meaning

つまり presents the next words as what the earlier information amounts to.
It is not just a pause-filler: listeners expect clarification or a conclusion.
In conversation, つまり、行かないんですね (つまり、いかないんですね), "So you mean you aren't going,"
checks an interpretation and may require the other person's confirmation.
