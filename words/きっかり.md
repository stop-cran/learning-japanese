# きっかり — exactly; right on time

## Meaning

きっかり marks an exact amount or a precise time.

- 集合時刻の九時きっかりに、全員が集まった。
  — Everyone gathered at exactly nine, the stated meeting time.
- 代金を五千円きっかり払った。
  — I paid exactly five thousand yen.

## Synonyms and antonyms

きっかり emphasizes an exact point or amount. It is not a description of a crowded space or a
tight physical fit.

## Distinctive meaning

The amount or time is made explicit by the sentence. Do not infer punctuality from きっかり when
the context instead gives an exact quantity.
