# どたどた — heavy, noisy footsteps

## Meaning

どたどた evokes heavy, noisy movement, especially footsteps thumping across a floor or on stairs.

- 子供たちが廊下をどたどた走っている。 — The children are running noisily down the hallway. (廊下: ろうか.)
- 兄がどたどたと階段を下りてきた。 — My older brother came thumping down the stairs. (階段: かいだん.)

Use どたどた走る, どたどたと歩く, or どたどたする when the context supplies the noisy movement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ばたばた](ばたばた.md) can describe noisy hurried movement but also flapping and being busy. [すたすた](すたすた.md) describes a brisk gait without necessarily heavy noise. [そっと](そっと.md) can contrast when someone moves quietly.

## Distinctive meaning

The focus is the noise and heaviness, not necessarily speed, anger, or a person's body weight. Even small children can run どたどた on a resonant floor. It is therefore not interchangeable with a neutral verb meaning "walk."
