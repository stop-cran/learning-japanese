# 従って (したがって) - therefore; consequently

## Meaning

従って is a conjunction introducing a conclusion or consequence of what was just stated. It is usually written したがって and fits explanations and reasoned statements. Place it before the resulting statement, rather than attaching it directly to a noun.

- 明日は祝日です。したがって、銀行は休みです。 (あしたはしゅくじつです。したがって、ぎんこうはやすみです。) - Tomorrow is a public holiday. Therefore, the bank is closed.
- 彼はまだ十歳です。したがって、車を運転できません。 (かれはまだじゅっさいです。したがって、くるまをうんてんできません。) - He is only ten. Consequently, he cannot drive a car.

## How the kanji combine

従 has a following or obeying association. The written form matches the て-form of [従う](従う.md) (したがう), but the conjunction has its own use linking statements.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[だから](だから.md) also introduces a reason-based conclusion and fits conversational explanations. [それで](それで.md) can describe what followed as a result. Their overlap does not make every discourse use interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 指示に従って作業します (しじにしたがってさぎょうします), "I work according to the instructions." Here 従って is the inflected verb 従う: 指示に supplies what is followed. That construction is not a second unrestricted meaning of the conjunction "therefore."
