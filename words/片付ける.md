# 片付ける (かたづける) — tidy up; put away

## Meaning

片付ける means to tidy up, put things away, or clear them away. It is transitive: use を for the room being tidied or the things being put away.

- 部屋を片付けます。  
  へやをかたづけます。  
  "I'll tidy my room."
- おもちゃを片付けてください。  
  おもちゃをかたづけてください。  
  "Please put away the toys."

## How the kanji combine

片 has the reading かた, while [付](../kanji/付.md) appears as づ in づける. The whole word is a native verb, also written 片づける. Keep づ in the kana spelling. It is ichidan: 片付けます, 片付けて, 片付けない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

掃除する (そうじする), "clean", often involves removing dirt or dust. 片付ける focuses on order or clearing things away. 散らかす (ちらかす), "make a mess", gives a useful opposite in a room-tidying context.

## Distinctive meaning

片付く (かたづく) is the intransitive partner: 部屋が片付いた (へやがかたづいた), "The room is tidy now". Tidying and cleaning can overlap, but putting books away does not necessarily clean the shelf.
