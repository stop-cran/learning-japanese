# 催し (もよおし) - event; social gathering

## Meaning

催し is an organized event, gathering, or function, often for entertainment
or a public activity. It is a noun, not itself a する-verb.
催しを開く (もよおしをひらく) means to hold an event.

- 図書館で子供向けの催しがあります。 (としょかんでこどもむけのもよおしがあります。) - There is an event for children at the library.
- 週末の催しに参加しました。 (しゅうまつのもよおしにさんかしました。) - I took part in the weekend event.

で gives the location of the event; に identifies the event participated in.
催し物 (もよおしもの) is another expression for an event or entertainment.

## How the kanji combine

催 contributes organizing or holding an event.
The native verb 催す (もよおす) has the stem もよおし, used here as a noun.
Do not read the noun from the character's on reading サイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[行事](行事.md) (ぎょうじ) overlaps with an organized event or observance.
[会合](会合.md) (かいごう) is a meeting or gathering.
[出来事](出来事.md) (できごと) is something that happens, not necessarily planned.
[事件](事件.md) (じけん) often identifies an incident or case.

## Distinctive meaning

English "event" also covers accidents and other occurrences.
催し normally directs attention to something people arrange, not an accidental incident.
The gloss "function" means a social event here, not a device's
[機能](機能.md) (きのう), function or capability.
