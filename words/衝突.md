# 衝突 (しょうとつ) - collision; clash

## Meaning

衝突 is a collision or a clash. 衝突する is intransitive: one thing collides with another, or two parties clash. Use に for a thing hit and と to identify the other participant.

- 車が壁に衝突しました。 (くるまがかべにしょうとつしました。) - The car collided with a wall.
- 意見の違いで同僚と衝突しました。 (いけんのちがいでどうりょうとしょうとつしました。) - I clashed with a colleague over a difference of opinion.

The second sentence describes conflict, not necessarily physical contact. 二台の車が衝突した (にだいのくるまがしょうとつした) means "two cars collided."

## How the kanji combine

衝 contributes striking or rushing against something, and [突](../kanji/突.md) contributes thrusting or hitting. Their action meanings reinforce the idea of collision. The on readings are ショウ + トツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぶつかる also describes bumping into something or clashing. 対立 (たいりつ) focuses on opposition between positions or sides; 衝突 presents the encounter as a clash. [攻撃](攻撃.md) (こうげき), "attack," names an attacking action rather than merely contact or disagreement.

## Distinctive meaning

A collision does not by itself establish intention. Likewise, a figurative 衝突 can be a sharp disagreement without violence. Preserve the intransitive pattern: 車が壁に衝突する, not treating the wall as a direct を object of 衝突する.
