# 直線 (ちょくせん) - straight line

## Meaning

直線 is a straight line, without bends.
It is a noun: 直線を引く (ちょくせんをひく) means draw a straight line.

- 定規で直線を引きました。
  (じょうぎでちょくせんをひきました。) - I drew a straight line with a ruler.
- 二つの点を直線で結んでください。
  (ふたつのてんをちょくせんでむすんでください。) - Please connect the two points with a straight line.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md), straight, modifies [線](../kanji/線.md), line.
Both use on readings: チョク + セン.
This is straightness, not 直's separate repair association.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[曲線](曲線.md) (きょくせん), curved line, is the shape contrast.
[線](線.md) (せん) is broader and does not require straightness.
[まっすぐ](まっすぐ.md) describes straight alignment or movement;
まっすぐな線 (まっすぐなせん) is a straight line.

## Distinctive meaning

In precise geometry, a line extends indefinitely in both directions.
The bounded part between two endpoints is a 線分 (せんぶん), line segment.
Everyday drawing instructions can still call a finite drawn stroke a 直線;
the practical use is not confined to an infinite physical object.
直線的な (ちょくせんてきな) is the related expression linear or straightforward,
not a reason to use bare 直線 as an unrestricted な-adjective.
