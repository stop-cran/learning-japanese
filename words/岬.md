# 岬 (みさき) - cape; headland

## Meaning

岬 is a cape or headland: a projecting part of the land extending into the sea or a lake.

- 岬の先に灯台があります。 (みさきのさきにとうだいがあります。) - There is a lighthouse at the tip of the cape.
- 船は岬を回りました。 (ふねはみさきをまわりました。) - The ship went around the cape.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-character word read みさき. 崎 is another recorded spelling for this reading. Learn 岬 as a whole geographic term rather than inventing its meaning from the modern shapes of its parts.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[半島](半島.md) (はんとう) is a peninsula, a related land projection but not an unrestricted substitute for 岬. [湾](湾.md) (わん) is a bay or inlet, where water extends into the land. [海岸](海岸.md) (かいがん) is the coast more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

岬 names the projecting land, not the water around it or an offshore island. It need not refer only to an ocean coast: the Japanese dictionary definition also includes a lake shore.
