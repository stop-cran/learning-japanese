# 面倒 (めんどう) - trouble; bother; care

## Meaning

面倒 is a noun and な-adjective for trouble, bother, or something troublesome. It also means care or attention in expressions such as Nの面倒を見る, "look after N."

- この手続きは面倒です。 (このてつづきはめんどうです。) - This procedure is a bother.
- 週末は妹の面倒を見ます。 (しゅうまつはいもうとのめんどうをみます。) - I look after my younger sister on weekends.

Use 面倒な作業 (めんどうなさぎょう), "a troublesome task," before a noun. In 面倒を見る, を belongs to the set expression and Nの identifies who or what receives the care.

## How the kanji combine

[面](../kanji/面.md) ordinarily has face or surface meanings, and [倒](../kanji/倒.md) has falling or toppling meanings. Here the spelling is **ateji**, assigned for sound: "a face falling over" is not the compound's derivation. The Japanese dictionary traces the expression to earlier native material, with details qualified as a proposed history.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[手間](手間.md) (てま) emphasizes the time and effort a task takes.

[世話](世話.md) (せわ) overlaps with the caregiving use. [迷惑](迷惑.md) (めいわく) emphasizes inconvenience or trouble caused to someone.

## Distinctive meaning

Looking after someone with 面倒を見る is not automatically complaining that they are a nuisance. Conversely, 面倒だ evaluates something as bothersome; it is not a general translation of "I care." The construction determines which sense is active.
