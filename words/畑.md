# 畑 (はたけ) - cultivated field; vegetable plot

## Meaning

畑 is a noun for cultivated land such as a field or vegetable plot. It can grow vegetables, wheat, or other crops; it is not a general label for any open space.

- 畑で野菜を育てています。 (はたけでやさいをそだてています。) - I grow vegetables in a field.
- 朝、畑に行きました。 (あさ、はたけにいきました。) - I went to the field in the morning.

で marks the setting of cultivation, while に marks the destination in the second sentence.

## How the kanji combine

畑 is the whole native noun, read はたけ here. The dictionary also records はた, but this article's examples teach はたけ. It is a single character, not two word components whose readings need to be combined.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[田](田.md) (た) is the useful comparison for a rice paddy. Both concern agricultural land, but a vegetable plot is naturally described as a 畑. [場所](場所.md) (ばしょ), "place," is much broader and says nothing about cultivation.

## Distinctive meaning

畑 also has a figurative "field of specialization" sense under the reading はたけ. 彼とは畑が違います (かれとははたけがちがいます) means "He and I work in different fields." In that context there need not be any farmland. The dictionary notes the suffix reading ばたけ for this specialized-field use; do not assume that every occurrence has the unchanged standalone pronunciation.
