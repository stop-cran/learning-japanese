# 悪魔 (あくま) - demon; devil

## Meaning

悪魔 is a noun for a demon, devil, or evil supernatural being. In stories, it can name a character rather than a particular religious figure. Use 悪魔の before another noun, or 悪魔のような for "like a demon."

- この物語には悪魔が出てきます。 (このものがたりにはあくまがでてきます。) - A demon appears in this story.
- 彼は悪魔のような顔をしていた。 (かれはあくまのようなかおをしていた。) - He had a devilish expression.

The second example makes a comparison; it does not say that he is literally a supernatural being.

## How the kanji combine

[悪](../kanji/悪.md) contributes "bad; evil," and 魔 refers to a demon or malign supernatural force. The first component characterizes the second: an evil supernatural being. The readings combine as あく + ま.

## Synonyms and antonyms

天使 (てんし), "angel," is a useful contrasting figure in stories. It is not a universal opposite across every religious tradition. [悪い](悪い.md) (わるい) simply means "bad"; it is an adjective, not the name of a creature.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling a person 悪魔 is a powerful figurative characterization, not a neutral description of a small mistake. The setting determines whether the word describes a fictional creature, a religious being, or someone's perceived cruelty.
