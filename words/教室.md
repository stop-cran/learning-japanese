# 教室 (きょうしつ) — classroom, class

## Meaning

教室 is a classroom or teaching room. It can also name a class or set of lessons, especially in an expression such as 料理教室 (りょうりきょうしつ), "cooking class."

- 教室に学生がいます。 (きょうしつにがくせいがいます。) — There are students in the classroom.
- 毎週、料理教室に通っています。 (まいしゅう、りょうりきょうしつにかよっています。) — I attend a cooking class every week.

## How the kanji combine

[教](../kanji/教.md) キョウ contributes "teach," and [室](../kanji/室.md) シツ means a room. The first element specifies the purpose of the second: a room for teaching.

Both are on-readings: キョウ + シツ → きょうしつ. The extended "class, lessons" sense is a meaning of the whole word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [部屋](部屋.md) へや is a room in general; it does not specify teaching.
- [授業](授業.md) じゅぎょう means a lesson or teaching activity, not the physical room.
- There is no ordinary direct antonym of 教室.

## Distinctive meaning

Context decides whether 教室 refers to a room or an organized class. In the first example, people are located inside a room; in 料理教室に通う, someone attends lessons. Do not translate every instance mechanically as a physical "classroom."
