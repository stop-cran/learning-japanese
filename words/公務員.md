# 公務員 (こうむいん) -- public employee

## Meaning

公務員 is a government or public-service employee. The word describes employment status, not one particular daily task.

姉は市役所で働く公務員です。 (あねはしやくしょではたらくこうむいんです。) -- My older sister is a public employee working at city hall.

Use 公務員です to state the occupation, and 公務員になる (こうむいんになる) for becoming a public employee. The role one enters takes に with なる.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md), read こう, concerns public affairs. [務](../kanji/務.md), read む, concerns duties or work. Together 公務 means public business; [員](../kanji/員.md), read いん, adds the person serving in that role.

## Synonyms and antonyms

会社員 (かいしゃいん), a company employee, is a useful employment-category contrast. 職員 (しょくいん), staff member, is broader and does not by itself identify a government employer.

## Distinctive meaning

公務員 does not mean only a politician or a senior official. Different occupations can fall within public employment. To learn someone's actual duties, ask what kind of work they do rather than assuming it from this label.
