# ダンス (ダンス) - dance; dancing

## Meaning

ダンス names dance or dancing. It is a noun and can form the verb **ダンスする**, "dance." Useful noun patterns include ダンスを習う (ダンスをならう), "learn dance," and ダンスが上手, "good at dancing."

- 週に一回、ダンスを習っています。 (しゅうにいっかい、ダンスをならっています。) - I take a dance lesson once a week.
- みんなで音楽に合わせてダンスしました。 (みんなでおんがくにあわせてダンスしました。) - We all danced to the music.

With a verb such as 習う or 練習する, ダンス is the activity being learned or practiced.

## How the kanji combine

ダンス is the katakana loanword corresponding to English "dance." Read the three kana ダ・ン・ス; there is no long-vowel mark or kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[踊る](踊る.md) (おどる) is a native verb meaning to dance. 踊り (おどり) is a related noun. [演技](演技.md) (えんぎ) is a performance or acting; not every performance is a dance.

## Distinctive meaning

ダンス does not by itself specify the style, whether it is a lesson or performance, or whether someone dances alone or with others. Those details need context. Likewise, ダンスを習う states that someone is learning dance, not that they are already a professional dancer.
