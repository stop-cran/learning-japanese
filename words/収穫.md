# 収穫 (しゅうかく) - harvest; fruitful result

## Meaning

収穫 is a harvest: the gathering of crops or what is gathered. With する it is transitive: **作物を収穫する**, "harvest crops." The noun also describes a valuable gain from an activity.

- 秋に米を収穫します。 (あきにこめをしゅうかくします。) - We harvest rice in autumn.
- 今回の旅行は収穫が多かったです。 (こんかいのりょこうはしゅうかくがおおかったです。) - We gained a lot from this trip.

In the second example, the gain could be useful experience or knowledge; no literal crop is implied. 収穫がある means that there is something worthwhile to show for an activity.

## How the kanji combine

[収](../kanji/収.md) contributes gathering or taking in; 穫 contributes reaping a crop. The components reinforce gathering the results of cultivation. The readings are シュウ + カク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作物](作物.md) (さくもつ) names the crops themselves, including while they are growing. [結果](結果.md) (けっか) is a result more generally and need not be a useful gain. [収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう) usually concerns income rather than harvesting.

## Distinctive meaning

収穫 draws attention to what an activity yields. A trip described as 収穫が多い was productive, not necessarily profitable in money. The figurative sense remains a noun in these examples; do not mechanically translate every "get a result" as 収穫する.
