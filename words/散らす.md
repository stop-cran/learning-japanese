# 散らす (ちらす) - scatter; disperse

## Meaning

散らす means to scatter things or disperse them over an area.
It is a transitive godan verb: the things scattered take を.
The distribution need not be accidental or untidy.

- 風が桜の花びらを散らしました。
  (かぜがさくらのはなびらをちらしました。) - The wind scattered the cherry blossom petals.
- サラダの上に刻んだねぎを散らします。
  (サラダのうえにきざんだねぎをちらします。) - I scatter chopped green onion over the salad.

気を散らす (きをちらす) means to distract someone or divert their attention.
Forms include 散らします, 散らした, 散らして, and 散らさない.

## How the kanji combine

[散](../kanji/散.md) contributes scattering, with the native reading ち here.
らす completes the written verb; the ます-stem is ちらし.
This is not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[散る](散る.md) (ちる) is intransitive: 花びらが散る describes petals falling
or scattering, while 花びらを散らす presents something causing that result.
[散らかす](散らかす.md) (ちらかす) focuses on scattering things around or making a mess.
[広がる](広がる.md) (ひろがる) can mean "spread out" as an intransitive change.
[配る](配る.md) (くばる) emphasizes distributing things to recipients.

## Distinctive meaning

散らす can describe deliberate arrangement as well as dispersal.
For a gathering breaking up, [解散](解散.md) (かいさん) is a useful comparison;
it also covers formally disbanding an organization.
After a verb's ます-stem, ～散らす can add a sense of doing something wildly
or all over the place: わめき散らす (わめきちらす), "rant and rave."
That suffix use is additional recognition, not a rule that every scattering is noisy.
