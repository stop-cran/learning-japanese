# 奪う (うばう) - take away; deprive

## Meaning

奪う is a transitive godan verb for taking something away, especially forcibly, or depriving someone of it. Use **person から + thing を奪う**. It can also describe something captivating a person's attention.

- 相手からボールを奪った。 (あいてからボールをうばった。) - I took the ball from my opponent.
- 美しい景色に目を奪われました。 (うつくしいけしきにめをうばわれました。) - I was captivated by the beautiful scenery.

奪った (うばった) is the past; 奪わない (うばわない) is the negative. The second example uses the passive 奪われる: the scenery figuratively captures the viewer's eyes.

## How the kanji combine

奪 supplies the native stem うば-, and う is the dictionary-form ending. This is one verb written with one kanji, not a compound of separate meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[取る](取る.md) (とる) is a broader "take." [盗む](盗む.md) (ぬすむ) focuses on stealing; 奪う also works for legal sporting competition or the loss of an opportunity. [与える](与える.md) (あたえる), "give," offers a contextual contrast to depriving someone.

## Distinctive meaning

The thing lost need not be physical: 自由を奪う (じゆうをうばう) means "deprive someone of freedom." In 心を奪う (こころをうばう), "capture someone's heart," the meaning is attraction, not literal theft. Let the object and context determine the English translation.
