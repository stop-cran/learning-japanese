# 改める (あらためる) - revise; reform; inspect

## Meaning

改める is a transitive ichidan verb for changing or revising something, or correcting
habits and behavior. Use を for what is revised: 文章を改める (ぶんしょうをあらためる),
"revise a piece of writing." The forms include 改めます, 改めない and 改めて.

- 文章の一部を改めました。 (ぶんしょうのいちぶをあらためました。) - I revised part of the text.
- 悪い習慣を改めたいです。 (わるいしゅうかんをあらためたいです。) - I want to correct my bad habits.

An additional inspection use appears in 切符を改める (きっぷをあらためる),
"inspect tickets." Here the tickets are checked, not rewritten.

## How the kanji combine

[改](../kanji/改.md) contributes changing or reforming. The native reading is
あらためる; do not read the written word as カイめる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[変える](変える.md) (かえる) broadly means changing something.
[修正](修正.md) (しゅうせい) concerns correction or revision;
[改善](改善.md) (かいぜん) explicitly concerns improvement.
[検査](検査.md) (けんさ) and [チェック](チェック.md) overlap with the inspection use.
These are contextual comparisons, not unrestricted substitutes.

## Distinctive meaning

改める is transitive; 改まる (あらたまる) describes something changing or becoming
renewed and can also concern formality. [改めて](改めて.md) is an established adverb
for again, anew or deliberately; its meaning is not limited to "having corrected."
The dictionary records further formal/proper-action uses beyond these examples.
