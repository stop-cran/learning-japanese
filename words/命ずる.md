# 命ずる (めいずる) - command; appoint

## Meaning

命ずる is a transitive verb meaning to order or command someone to do something,
or to appoint someone to a post. It is associated with authoritative or formal directions.

- 裁判所は支払いを命じました。 (さいばんしょはしはらいをめいじました。) - The court ordered payment.
- 彼を委員に命じました。 (かれをいいんにめいじました。) - They appointed him to the committee.

In the appointment pattern, 人を役職に命ずる, を marks the person and に marks the post.
The examples use 命じました, a form shared with 命じる.

## How the kanji combine

[命](../kanji/命.md) contributes a command here, not its other meaning "life."
The on reading メイ is followed by ずる.
Japanese dictionaries classify 命ずる as サ変; JMdict uses its "-zuru alternative
of -jiru verbs" category. For the forms used here, learn 命じます (めいじます),
命じた (めいじた), and 命じない (めいじない), not a regular godan ずる pattern.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[命じる](命じる.md) (めいじる) shares the command and appointment meanings.
[命令](命令.md) (めいれい) names an order and can form 命令する.
[指定](指定.md) (してい) can mean designate or specify; specifying a date need not appoint a person.

## Distinctive meaning

The English word "order" also describes a sequence, but 命ずる does not mean putting
things into [順序](順序.md) (じゅんじょ).
Similarly, "command a view" belongs to the view sense of [望む](望む.md) (のぞむ),
not to this verb's command meaning. Shared English wording does not make those Japanese uses interchangeable.
This card covers the pinned entry's command and appointment senses, not every historical use.
