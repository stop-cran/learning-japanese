# このあいだ - the other day

## Meaning

このあいだ commonly points to a recent past occasion: "the other day" or "recently." The exact date is left to context.

- このあいだ、駅で先生に会いました。 (このあいだ、えきでせんせいにあいました。) - I met my teacher at the station the other day.

It can stand directly before a clause without に. Use の when it modifies a noun: このあいだの話 (このあいだのはなし), "what we talked about the other day."

## How the kanji combine

The kana spelling corresponds to この間. この means "this," and あいだ concerns a span or interval. The recent-past use is an established expression, not a request to calculate a fixed interval.

## Synonyms and antonyms

先日 (せんじつ) also means "the other day" and is useful in more formal contexts. 最近 (さいきん) often refers to a recent period rather than one particular occasion.

## Distinctive meaning

The same written この間 can also be read このかん in interval-related uses. This card teaches このあいだ; the dictionary's "meanwhile" sense is restricted to このかん and should not be transferred to this reading.
