# 部首 (ぶしゅ) - kanji indexing radical

## Meaning

部首 is the radical used to group and look up a kanji in a dictionary. It is an indexing classification, not a name for every visible part of a character.

- この漢字の部首は何ですか。 (このかんじのぶしゅはなんですか。) - What is the radical of this kanji?
- 部首で漢字を調べました。 (ぶしゅでかんじをしらべました。) - I looked up the kanji by its radical.

## How the kanji combine

[部](../kanji/部.md), a section or group, and [首](../kanji/首.md), head, suggest the heading of a character group. The on readings are ブ + シュ. This describes the indexing idea, not the origin of every kanji under that heading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[部分](部分.md) (ぶぶん) means a part or portion, and [要素](要素.md) (ようそ) an element. A component can be discussed as a part without being that character's 部首.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume the largest, leftmost or most meaning-like component must be the radical. A dictionary's indexing key can have a changed positional shape in the character. Use the key recorded on the kanji card rather than inventing one from a mnemonic.
