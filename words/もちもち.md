# もちもち — springy and chewy

## Meaning

A soft but elastic, pleasantly resistant texture, especially noodles, bread, rice products, or dumpling dough.

- このうどんはもちもちしている。 — These udon noodles are springy and chewy.
- もちもちした生地（きじ）を薄くのばした。 — I rolled the elastic dough out thin.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic with the repeated もち pattern. It recalls the texture of 餅（もち, rice cake）, but this association is not proof of a particular historical derivation. The card contains no written kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぷにぷに](ぷにぷに.md) is soft, squishy resilience under a finger, not specifically the texture of chewing food. [ふわふわ](ふわふわ.md) is airy, while [ほくほく](ほくほく.md) is flaky or crumbly.

## Distinctive meaning

もちもちする, もちもちしたパン, and もちもちの食感（しょっかん, mouthfeel）are useful. The word is normally positive in food descriptions; it does not simply mean hard or difficult to chew. It also appears in descriptions of supple skin, where “chewy” would be a misleading literal translation.
