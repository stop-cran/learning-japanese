# 姿勢 (しせい) - posture; attitude

## Meaning

姿勢 is physical posture or the position in which a body is held.
Figuratively, it is an attitude, stance, or approach toward an issue.
It is a noun: 姿勢がいい describes good posture, while Nに対する姿勢
identifies an attitude toward N.

- 背筋を伸ばして、姿勢をよくしましょう。 (せすじをのばして、しせいをよくしましょう。) - Straighten your back and improve your posture.
- 会社は環境問題に取り組む姿勢を示しました。 (かいしゃはかんきょうもんだいにとりくむしせいをしめしました。) - The company showed its willingness to address environmental problems.

The second example concerns an approach to action, not the position of a body.

## How the kanji combine

姿 contributes figure or appearance; [勢](../kanji/勢.md) contributes
force, momentum, or manner in related uses.
Their established compound names bodily bearing and an adopted stance.
The on readings combine as シ + セイ; the character glosses are
associations, not a literal formula for the whole word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[態度](態度.md) (たいど) overlaps in attitude or manner.
[格好](格好.md) (かっこう) can concern appearance and posture.
[立場](立場.md) (たちば) emphasizes a position or standpoint.
姿勢 can instead focus on the approach someone adopts from that position.

## Distinctive meaning

姿勢 is not inherently favorable: a cooperative or uncooperative approach
can both be a 姿勢. Nor does a stated willingness prove that the promised
action has happened. Use modifiers and the surrounding sentence to separate
posture, attitude, and actual conduct.
