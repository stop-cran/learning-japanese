# 消耗 (しょうもう) - consumption; exhaustion

## Meaning

消耗 means consumption or depletion through use. It also describes exhausting
physical or mental reserves. It is a noun and a する-verb with transitive
and intransitive uses.

- 長い会議で気力を消耗しました。 (ながいかいぎできりょくをしょうもうしました。) - The long meeting drained my mental energy.
- 電池が消耗したので、交換しました。 (でんちがしょうもうしたので、こうかんしました。) - The battery was depleted, so I replaced it.

気力を消耗する presents someone expending energy; 電池が消耗する presents
the battery becoming depleted. The word need not mean that every resource
has been used up completely.

## How the kanji combine

[消](../kanji/消.md) contributes using up or disappearing, and 耗 contributes
decreasing or wearing down through use. Their related meanings reinforce
depletion. The established reading here is しょうもう; the dictionary also
records しょうこう. Learn the word's reading rather than predicting it from
another compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[消費](消費.md) (しょうひ) overlaps in consuming resources.
[疲れ](疲れ.md) (つかれ) names tiredness, and [疲れる](疲れる.md)
(つかれる) describes becoming tired. [へとへと](へとへと.md) vividly
describes an exhausted state; it does not name resource consumption.

## Distinctive meaning

消耗 is not restricted to mechanical abrasion or to human fatigue. It can
concern supplies, a battery, or a person's reserves. It does not itself judge
the expenditure useless: [無駄](無駄.md) (むだ) concerns waste or futility.
摩耗 (まもう) more specifically concerns wear through friction.
