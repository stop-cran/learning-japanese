# 包む (つつむ) - wrap; bundle

## Meaning

包む means to wrap or bundle something. It is transitive: the item wrapped
takes を. The wrapping material can be marked with で.

- プレゼントを紙で包みました。 (プレゼントをかみでつつみました。) - I wrapped the present in paper.
- このお皿を包んでください。 (このおさらをつつんでください。) - Please wrap this plate.

## How the kanji combine

包 conveys enclosing or wrapping; む completes the native verb.
It is godan, so its forms include 包みます (つつみます), 包んで (つつんで),
and 包んだ (つつんだ). The written kanji boundary does not determine the
conjugation base.

## Synonyms and antonyms

入れる (いれる), "put in," describes placement inside something, not necessarily
wrapping around it. 開ける (あける), "open," can describe opening the resulting
package, but is not an opposite for every use of 包む.

## Distinctive meaning

The wrapping is the focus, not the destination or transportation of the parcel.
The verb can also describe being enveloped by something, often in the passive:
霧に包まれる (きりにつつまれる), "be enveloped in fog."
This is an extension of enclosure, not literal packaging.
