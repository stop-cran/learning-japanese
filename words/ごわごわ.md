# ごわごわ — stiff and rough to the touch

## Meaning

ごわごわ describes a stiff, coarse texture, especially in fabric or hair.

- 洗ったタオルが乾いて、ごわごわになった。
  — The washed towel dried stiff and rough.
- このシャツは生地がごわごわしている。
  — This shirt's fabric feels stiff and coarse.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ごわごわ suggests stiffness and roughness, unlike soft, supple fabric.

## Distinctive meaning

It describes a tactile quality and commonly appears with する or になる.
