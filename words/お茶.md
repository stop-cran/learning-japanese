# お茶 (おちゃ) — tea

## Meaning

お茶 is the usual polite everyday word for tea, often green tea or barley tea depending on the setting.
It can also refer to a tea break; the drink need not be part of a tea ceremony.

- お茶を飲みます。（おちゃをのみます。） — I drink tea.
- お茶をどうぞ。（おちゃをどうぞ。） — Please have some tea.

## How the kanji combine

This is not a two-kanji compound. [茶](../kanji/茶.md) has the on-reading チャ, and the hiragana お is a
polite or beautifying prefix. The card's kango label describes the base 茶, not an imaginary on+on combination.
The only written kanji in お茶 is 茶.

## Synonyms and antonyms

茶（ちゃ） is the unprefixed word for tea; お茶 is natural in ordinary conversation.
紅茶（こうちゃ）, "black tea," names a specific kind rather than being an exact substitute in every context.
There is no direct antonym of tea.

## Distinctive meaning

The お is conventional, so お茶 is not unusually formal and can refer to your own tea.
In an ordinary household or restaurant, do not assume that unqualified お茶 specifically means black tea.
Context determines the kind, and asking for 紅茶 makes that choice explicit.
