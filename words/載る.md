# 載る (のる) - appear in print; be recorded

## Meaning

載る is an intransitive godan verb. An article, name, photograph, or item of information can 載る: appear in a publication, be listed, or be recorded.

- 私の名前が新聞に載りました。 (わたしのなまえがしんぶんにのりました。) - My name appeared in the newspaper.
- この情報はウェブサイトに載っています。 (このじょうほうはウェブサイトにのっています。) - This information is on the website.

Use **content が + medium に載る**. Forms include 載ります, 載った, and 載らない. The publication use extends beyond ink on paper.

## How the kanji combine

載 represents の in this native verb, with る as okurigana. Keep the spelling distinct from [乗る](乗る.md) (のる), "ride; get on": a newspaper article is not riding a vehicle.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The transitive partner is **載せる** (のせる), discussed in the [乗せる](乗せる.md) article. Compare 新聞に名前が載る, "a name appears in the newspaper," with 新聞に名前を載せる, "put a name in the newspaper." The publishing sense requires 載せる, not the spelling 乗せる. [記録](記録.md) (きろく), "record," is related, but recording something does not necessarily mean publishing it.

## Distinctive meaning

There is also a physical use, "be placed or loaded on": 台に品物が載っている (だいにしなものがのっている), "goods are sitting on the stand." Keep this additional sense separate from publication. A shared pronunciation does not transfer all of 乗る's riding, participation, or other meanings to 載る.
