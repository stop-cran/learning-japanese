# 左 (ひだり) — left side

## Meaning

左 means left or the left-hand side. It can describe a direction or the position of something.

次の角を左に曲がってください。（つぎのかどをひだりにまがってください。） — Please turn left at the next corner.

## How the kanji combine

[左](../kanji/左.md) alone is read ひだり, its native reading. The on reading サ belongs to compounds and is not the ordinary standalone direction word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

右（みぎ）, "right," is the opposite direction. 左側（ひだりがわ）, "left-hand side," makes the side explicit; 左手（ひだりて） names the left hand.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 左の before a noun: 左の道（ひだりのみち）, "the road on the left." Use 左に for a position or the direction of a turn. Left and right depend on the viewpoint, unlike compass directions such as north.
