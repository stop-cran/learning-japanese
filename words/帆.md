# 帆 (ほ) — sail (of a boat)

## Meaning

A 帆 catches the wind to move a boat. It is one part of the boat, not a name for the whole vessel in these examples.

- 港を出ると、船員が帆を上げた。 (みなとをでると、せんいんがほをあげた。) — As we left the harbor, a crew member raised the sail.
- 風が弱く、帆は垂れたままだった。 (かぜがよわく、ほはたれたままだった。) — The wind was light, and the sail hung limp.

## How the kanji combine

[帆](../kanji/帆.md) writes the entire noun ほ. In 帆船 (はんせん), “sailing ship,” its reading is はん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

A [旗](旗.md) (はた) can also flutter from a pole, but a flag is for display or signaling. A [船](船.md) (ふね) is the boat itself.

## Distinctive meaning

帆を上げる means to raise the sail; it does not mean to lift the boat. The verb describes what the sailor does to the rigging.
