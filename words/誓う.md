# 誓う (ちかう) - swear an oath; vow

## Meaning

誓う means to vow, pledge, or swear an oath.
It can express a solemn commitment to another person, a deity, or oneself.
Use **Nを誓う** or **plain clause + と誓う** for the commitment.

- 二度とうそをつかないと誓いました。
  (にどとうそをつかないとちかいました。) - I vowed never to lie again.
- 二人は永遠の愛を誓いました。
  (ふたりはえいえんのあいをちかいました。) - The two pledged eternal love.

This is a godan verb ending in う:
誓います (ちかいます), 誓って (ちかって), 誓わない (ちかわない).
The negative changes う to わ before ない.

## How the kanji combine

誓 supplies ちか in the native verb; う is okurigana.
Its character meaning is a vow or pledge.
盟う is another dictionary spelling, but this article practices 誓う.
Do not infer a productive ちか reading for every word containing 盟.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[約束](約束.md) (やくそく) is a promise or agreement.
誓う emphasizes a firm or solemn commitment; it is not merely
making an appointment.
[祈る](祈る.md) (いのる), "pray; wish for," concerns a desired outcome
rather than necessarily promising one's own conduct.

## Distinctive meaning

English "swear" can also mean using offensive language.
That is not the oath meaning taught by 誓う.
A sincere vow may still be broken later: the verb identifies the commitment,
not proof that the promised action occurred.
