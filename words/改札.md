# 改札 (かいさつ) - ticket inspection; ticket gate

## Meaning

改札 means checking tickets. It also abbreviates 改札口 (かいさつぐち), the ticket gate or barrier at a station. In everyday directions, the place sense is especially useful.

- 改札の前で待ち合わせましょう。 (かいさつのまえでまちあわせましょう。) - Let's meet in front of the ticket gate.
- 切符を持って、改札を通りました。 (きっぷをもって、かいさつをとおりました。) - I went through the ticket gate with my ticket.

In 改札を通る (かいさつをとおる), を marks the place passed through. It does not make 通る a verb meaning "inspect."

## How the kanji combine

[改](../kanji/改.md) has the checking or examining contribution here; [札](../kanji/札.md) supplies "ticket." Read カイ + サツ. The meaning is ticket examination, not changing a ticket into a different one.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[切符](切符.md) (きっぷ) is the ticket. [門](門.md) (もん), "gate," is broader and does not itself specify ticket checking. [検査](検査.md) (けんさ), "inspection," and [チェック](チェック.md), "check," cover broader checking activities.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records 改札する for conducting ticket checking. Do not treat the ticket-gate abbreviation as a verb meaning "become a gate." Nor is 改札 the word for buying a ticket: it concerns checking admission or the barrier where that happens.
