# 日曜日 (にちようび) — Sunday

## Meaning

日曜日 means Sunday, one of the seven days of the week. 日曜日に marks an event happening on Sunday.

日曜日に友達と会います。 (にちようびにともだちとあいます。) — I will meet a friend on Sunday.

## How the kanji combine

The first [日](../kanji/日.md) represents the sun and is read ニチ. [曜](../kanji/曜.md) is ヨウ, and the final 日 is び, the voiced form of the native reading ひ. The on-first word therefore mixes on and kun readings; the two occurrences of 日 are not pronounced alike.

## Synonyms and antonyms

日曜 (にちよう) is the shorter form. 土曜日 (どようび), "Saturday," is another weekend day, not an opposite. 休日 (きゅうじつ) means a holiday or day off.

## Distinctive meaning

日曜日 identifies the weekday, not whether a particular person has time off. Someone working on Sundays still calls that day 日曜日. 今度の日曜日 (こんどのにちようび) specifies an upcoming Sunday in context.
