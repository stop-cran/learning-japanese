# 遡る (さかのぼる) - go upstream; trace back

## Meaning

遡る means to go upstream, or to go back to an earlier time or origin. It is an intransitive godan verb: 遡ります (さかのぼります), 遡った (さかのぼった), and 遡らない (さかのぼらない).

- 船で川を遡りました。 (ふねでかわをさかのぼりました。) - We traveled upstream by boat.
- この祭りの歴史は江戸時代に遡ります。 (このまつりのれきしはえどじだいにさかのぼります。) - This festival's history dates back to the Edo period.

川を marks the route traveled in the first example. Its を does not contradict the dictionary's intransitive classification.

## How the kanji combine

The single 遡 writes the native stem さかのぼ; る is okurigana. Learn the whole reading rather than trying to substitute an on-reading compound. JMdict marks both selected meanings as usually written in kana: さかのぼる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[川上](川上.md) (かわかみ) names upstream or the upper part of a river, rather than the movement verb. [戻る](戻る.md) (もどる) more broadly means go back. [帰る](帰る.md) (かえる) specifically suits returning home or to one's base; it is not the default word for tracing an event's history.

## Distinctive meaning

The past-directed use also covers retroactive application. 四月に遡って適用する (しがつにさかのぼっててきようする) means "apply retroactively to April." The transitive action is expressed by 適用する; do not infer a transitive "make something retroactive" conjugation of 遡る from that English translation.
