# 通信 (つうしん) - communication; transmission

## Meaning

通信 names communication or transmission of information, including correspondence and telecommunications. 通信する means to communicate or transmit; と can identify the other party or device.

- この機器は無線で通信します。 (このききはむせんでつうしんします。) - This device communicates wirelessly.
- スマートフォンがサーバーと通信しています。 (スマートフォンがサーバーとつうしんしています。) - The smartphone is communicating with the server.

通信費 (つうしんひ) means communication expenses, such as phone or internet charges. 通信教育 (つうしんきょういく) is correspondence or distance education.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes passing through or connecting, and [信](../kanji/信.md) contributes a message or communication here. Together they concern messages passing between places or parties. The on readings are ツウ + シン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[連絡](連絡.md) (れんらく) focuses on making contact or passing on information. 通信 readily names the transmission process or infrastructure. [手紙](手紙.md) (てがみ) is a letter, which can be a means of correspondence rather than the entire process.

## Distinctive meaning

通信 is not limited to an internet connection, though it appears in many technical contexts. It can also concern correspondence and news dispatches; 通信社 (つうしんしゃ) means "news agency." For ordinary conversation, do not assume every exchange of spoken words should be called 通信: the word foregrounds communication across a channel or distance.
