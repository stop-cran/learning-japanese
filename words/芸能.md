# 芸能 (げいのう) - performing arts; cultivated accomplishments

## Meaning

芸能 means performing arts or public entertainment, including forms such as music, dance, and theatrical performance.

- 伝統芸能を紹介する番組を見ました。 (でんとうげいのうをしょうかいするばんぐみをみました。) - I watched a program introducing traditional performing arts.
- 芸能界で働いています。 (げいのうかいではたらいています。) - I work in the entertainment industry.

It also has an accomplishments/attainments sense: cultivated arts, skills, or abilities.
Here "accomplishments" means abilities someone possesses, not merely finished tasks or results.
For example, 芸能を身につける (げいのうをみにつける) means acquiring such accomplishments.

## How the kanji combine

[芸](../kanji/芸.md) contributes art or practiced skill, and [能](../kanji/能.md) contributes ability.
Their related meanings help recall artistic skill and performance.
The on readings combine as ゲイ + ノウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[芸術](芸術.md) (げいじゅつ), "art; the arts," is broader than entertainment or performance.
[演技](演技.md) (えんぎ) focuses on acting or performance; [演奏](演奏.md) (えんそう) on musical performance.
[能力](能力.md) (のうりょく) names ability more generally.
These distinctions guide context, not an absolute boundary between every art and every performance.

## Distinctive meaning

In 芸能界, 界 (かい) means a world or field: the entertainment world.
Do not translate every achieved goal as 芸能 just because an English dictionary gloss says "accomplishments."
The cultivated-ability sense is positive lexical teaching here, not a claim that it is obsolete or that every use concerns celebrities.
