# 激励 (げきれい) — encouragement; spurring on

## Meaning

激励 is encouragement that spurs someone on. It is a noun, and 激励する means "encourage" or "give a pep talk." The person or group encouraged can take を.

- 選手団を激励する。 (せんしゅだんをげきれいする。) — Encourage the team.
- 先生は学生たちを激励しました。 (せんせいはがくせいたちをげきれいしました。) — The teacher encouraged the students.

## How the kanji combine

激 contributes intensity or stirring up, and [励](../kanji/励.md) contributes encouraging. This is an intensifying combination, not a literal instruction to become violent. The readings are ゲキ + レイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[励ます](励ます.md) (はげます) is the native verb for encouraging or cheering someone on. [応援](応援.md) (おうえん) covers support and cheering, including practical backup as well as encouragement. The overlap is real: it is not a strict division between words and actions.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn both the noun 激励 and the verb 激励する. Encouraging someone to keep going is not the same as every kind of assistance, and the word does not guarantee success or an immediate improvement in the recipient's mood. Nor does every use require shouting or harsh treatment.
