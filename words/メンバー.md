# メンバー (メンバー) - member; participant

## Meaning

メンバー is a member of a group or a participant in an activity. It is a noun. Use Nのメンバー, "a member of N," and メンバーになる, "become a member."

- 私はこのチームのメンバーです。 (わたしはこのチームのメンバーです。) - I am a member of this team.
- 会議に参加するメンバーを決めました。 (かいぎにさんかするメンバーをきめました。) - We decided who would participate in the meeting.

The second use does not necessarily imply permanent membership in a formal organization.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana word with no kanji components. ン represents a mora of its own, and ー lengthens バ. メンバ is also listed in the dictionary, but this article preserves メンバー.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会員](会員.md) (かいいん) identifies a member of an association or membership organization. [仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) can emphasize companions or people sharing an activity. メンバー identifies participation or inclusion without necessarily conveying friendship.

## Distinctive meaning

メンバー can mean one member, several members, or a lineup, depending on context. Japanese does not require an English-style plural ending. 新しいメンバー (あたらしいメンバー) may be a newly joining person; it does not imply that every member of the group is new or that a new organization has been created.
