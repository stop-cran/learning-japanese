# 動物園 (どうぶつえん) - zoo

## Meaning

動物園 is a zoo, a place where animals are kept and visitors can see them.

- 日曜日に子供と動物園へ行きました。 (にちようびにこどもとどうぶつえんへいきました。) - I went to the zoo with my child on Sunday.

へ marks the destination. 動物園で写真を撮る (どうぶつえんでしゃしんをとる), "take photos at the zoo," instead uses で for the location of an activity.

## How the kanji combine

[動](../kanji/動.md) and [物](../kanji/物.md) form 動物 (どうぶつ), "animal." [園](../kanji/園.md), エン, adds a park or enclosed grounds. The complete compound uses on readings; keep the long vowel in どう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

水族館 (すいぞくかん) is an aquarium, while 植物園 (しょくぶつえん) is a botanical garden. They are related visitor destinations, not synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

動物 names an animal; 動物園 names the institution or place. Merely seeing animals in a park does not make that park a 動物園.
