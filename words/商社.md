# 商社 (しょうしゃ) - trading company

## Meaning

商社 is a trading company: a business dealing in the buying and selling of goods, often including import and export. Use 商社で働く (しょうしゃではたらく), "work at a trading company."

- 姉は商社で働いています。 (あねはしょうしゃではたらいています。) - My older sister works at a trading company.
- この商社は食品を輸入しています。 (このしょうしゃはしょくひんをゆにゅうしています。) - This trading company imports food products.

輸入する (ゆにゅうする) means to import. The second sentence supplies that particular company's activity; the word 商社 alone does not identify what goods it handles.

## How the kanji combine

[商](../kanji/商.md) contributes commerce, and [社](../kanji/社.md) company. 商 specifies the kind of company. The on readings are ショウ + シャ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会社](会社.md) (かいしゃ), "company," and [企業](企業.md) (きぎょう), "business enterprise," are broader. [商店](商店.md) (しょうてん) names a shop or store. [商業](商業.md) (しょうぎょう) names commercial activity rather than the organization.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not replace every English "firm" with 商社: a company need not be a trading company. Import/export is an important association, not a claim that every transaction of every 商社 must cross a national border. Nor does the word necessarily mean a neighborhood retail shop.
