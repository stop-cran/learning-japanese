# 彼女 (かのじょ) — she; girlfriend

## Meaning

彼女 has two everyday uses: the pronoun "she; her" and the noun "girlfriend." Context determines the meaning.

- 彼女は同じクラスの学生です。 (かのじょはおなじクラスのがくせいです。) — She is a student in the same class.
- 兄には彼女がいます。 (あににはかのじょがいます。) — My older brother has a girlfriend.

The particles show grammatical function: 彼女に (かのじょに), "to her"; 彼女の (かのじょの), "her" before a noun.

## How the kanji combine

[彼](../kanji/彼.md) contributes the native element かの, "that," and [女](../kanji/女.md) the on reading ジョ, "woman." This is a mixed native-first word rather than a regular all-on compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

彼 (かれ) can mean "he" or "boyfriend." 恋人 (こいびと), "romantic partner," does not specify gender.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese often uses a person's name or omits an understood subject instead of repeating pronouns. Do not mechanically replace every English "she" with 彼女. In expressions about having or introducing a 彼女, the girlfriend meaning is especially relevant.
