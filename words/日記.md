# 日記 (にっき) — diary; journal

## Meaning

日記 is a diary or journal: an account connected with the writer's days.
Use 日記を書く (にっきをかく) for writing in a diary.

- 寝る前に日記を書きます。 (ねるまえににっきをかきます。) — I write in my diary before going to bed.
- 昨日の日記を読み返しました。 (きのうのにっきをよみかえしました。) — I reread yesterday's diary entry.

The second sentence translates 日記 as an entry because that is the part being discussed.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md), "day," qualifies [記](../kanji/記.md), "written account."
The combination points to an account of days. Its on-reading compound is にっき,
with a small っ rather than a separately pronounced ち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

記録 (きろく) is the broader word for a record or recording. A diary can serve as a record,
but a list of measurements or a recording of an event need not be a 日記.
There is no useful direct antonym to force into this comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

日記 names the kind of writing, not a requirement that every day must have an entry.
It also does not by itself say whether the contents are private or published.
Learn the concrete pattern 日記を書く rather than trying to use 日記する for "keep a diary."
