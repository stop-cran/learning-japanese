# 程度 (ていど) — degree; approximate amount

## Meaning

程度 describes a degree, level, or amount. It can also follow a quantity to make it approximate.
Compare この程度 (このていど), "this level; this much," with 十分程度 (じゅっぷんていど), "about ten minutes."

- あと十分程度かかります。 (あとじゅっぷんていどかかります。) — It will take about another ten minutes.
- この程度の問題なら解けます。 (このていどのもんだいならとけます。) — I can solve problems at this level.

Before a following noun, use の in a phrase such as この程度の問題.
After a number and its unit, place 程度 directly: 十分程度, not 十分の程度 for this approximate-duration use.

## How the kanji combine

[程](../kanji/程.md) concerns extent, while [度](../kanji/度.md) contributes degree or measure.
The overlapping meanings form a word about how much or how far something extends.
The on readings are テイ + ド.

## Synonyms and antonyms

度合い (どあい) also means degree or extent, as in the degree of some condition.
約 (やく) expresses approximation before a quantity: 約十分 (やくじゅっぷん), "about ten minutes."
程度 follows it instead: 十分程度.

ほど, usually written in kana rather than 程, can also follow an amount:
十分ほど (じゅっぷんほど), "about ten minutes."
These expressions overlap in this example, not in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

程度 can refer to a qualitative level without giving a number at all.
In この程度の問題, the topic is the problem's level; in 十分程度, it is an approximate duration.
Do not translate every occurrence as the measurement unit "degrees."
