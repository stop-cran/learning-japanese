# 女性 (じょせい) — woman; female

## Meaning

女性 is a woman or the female sex/gender category, depending on context. It is a noun, and it can describe another noun with の.

女性の医師に相談したいです。 (じょせいのいしにそうだんしたいです。) — I would like to consult a female doctor.

会場には多くの女性がいました。 (かいじょうにはおおくのじょせいがいました。) — There were many women at the venue.

## How the kanji combine

[女](../kanji/女.md) means woman or female; [性](../kanji/性.md) concerns nature or sex/gender. The on-readings are じょ + せい. Together they form a general category word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

男性 (だんせい) means man or male and is the corresponding category contrast. 女の人 (おんなのひと) is another everyday expression for a woman. 女の子 (おんなのこ) normally means a girl rather than an adult woman.

## Distinctive meaning

女性 is useful in neutral descriptions, information, and public-facing language. It is not normally a way to call out to an individual, and it does not specify marital status. Say 女性の先生 (じょせいのせんせい), not 女性な先生: this noun uses の, not adjectival な.
