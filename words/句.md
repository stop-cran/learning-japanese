# 句 (く) - phrase; verse

## Meaning

句 is a noun for a portion of language. In linguistics, it means
"phrase." In broader textual discussion, it can mean a passage or
section, and context may justify English "sentence." It is not a
universal substitute for the grammatical term "sentence."

- 「赤い花」は名詞句です。 (「あかいはな」はめいしくです。) - "Red flower" is a noun phrase.
- この句は祖父が作りました。 (このくはそふがつくりました。) - My grandfather composed this haiku.

Poetry supplies additional uses: 句 can name a verse unit, such as a
five- or seven-mora segment, or a whole haiku. A mora is a Japanese
rhythmic timing unit, not necessarily an English-style syllable.

## How the kanji combine

句 is a single character with the on reading ク. It names a linguistic
or poetic unit. In 名詞句 (めいしく), "noun phrase," the preceding
名詞 specifies what kind of 句 it is.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[文](文.md) (ぶん) is the useful basic word for a sentence.
[文章](文章.md) (ぶんしょう) refers to connected writing or prose.
俳句 (はいく) specifies the poetic form that 句 can name in context.
These differ in scope rather than forming opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Identify the subject area before translating. A grammar lesson, discussion
of a passage, and poetry class need not use 句 for the same-sized unit.
The short reading く stays the same across these uses.
