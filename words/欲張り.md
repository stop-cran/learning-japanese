# 欲張り (よくばり) - greedy; a greedy person

## Meaning

欲張り describes greed or wanting too much. It can name a greedy
person or work as a na-adjective: 欲張りな人 (よくばりなひと).
The spelling 欲ばり represents the same word.

- そんなに一人で食べるなんて、欲張りですね。 (そんなにひとりでたべるなんて、よくばりですね。) - Eating that much all by yourself is greedy, isn't it?
- 一度に全部やろうとするのは欲張りな計画です。 (いちどにぜんぶやろうとするのはよくばりなけいかくです。) - Trying to do everything at once is an overambitious plan.

Its range is not limited to money: someone can want too much food,
too many possessions, or too many results at once.

## How the kanji combine

[欲](../kanji/欲.md) supplies the on reading ヨク and desire.
張り is native はり, related to 張る (はる), voiced to ばり here.
This is an on-first mixed formation, stored as kango, not two on
readings. The related verb is 欲張る (よくばる).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[欲しい](欲しい.md) (ほしい) expresses wanting something without
necessarily judging that desire excessive.
[わがまま](わがまま.md), selfish or self-willed, overlaps in criticism
but need not specifically concern wanting more.
[けち](けち.md), stingy, concerns reluctance to part with things;
greed and stinginess are not an obligatory pair.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling someone 欲張り can criticize their appetite for more.
In a plan or an affectionate remark, the force depends on context;
it is not a diagnosis of a permanent personality.
Do not turn the character association with stretching into a claim
about the historical origin of the word.
