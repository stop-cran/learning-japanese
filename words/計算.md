# 計算 (けいさん) - calculation

## Meaning

計算 is a calculation or the process of working something out numerically.
計算する is transitive: 費用を計算する (ひようをけいさんする), "calculate the cost."
The noun can also concern estimation or taking something into account.

- 旅行に必要な費用を計算しました。 (りょこうにひつようなひようをけいさんしました。) - I calculated the expenses needed for the trip.
- 電卓を使わずに計算してください。 (でんたくをつかわずにけいさんしてください。) - Please do the calculation without using a calculator.

The second sentence leaves the numbers understood from context; it does not change the verb into a different action.

## How the kanji combine

[計](../kanji/計.md) contributes counting or measuring, and 算 calculation.
Their meanings reinforce each other in the on-reading compound ケイ + サン.
The result names the calculation, not the device used to perform it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[数える](数える.md) (かぞえる) focuses on counting items.
計算 can involve addition, division, or other operations beyond counting one by one.
[予算](予算.md) (よさん) is a budget: calculation may be needed to prepare it, but it is not the same noun.

## Distinctive meaning

計算に入れる (けいさんにいれる), "take into account," can extend beyond arithmetic:
遅れる可能性も計算に入れる (おくれるかのうせいもけいさんにいれる), "allow for the possibility of delay too."
This use concerns planning or estimation, not necessarily entering a number into a calculator.
