# 日帰り (ひがえり) - day trip; same-day return

## Meaning

日帰り means going somewhere and returning the same day without staying
overnight. It is a noun and can form the intransitive verb 日帰りする.
日帰りで describes this arrangement for a journey.

- 京都へ日帰りで行きました。 (きょうとへひがえりでいきました。) - I made a day trip to Kyoto.
- 今回は泊まらずに日帰りします。 (こんかいはとまらずにひがえりします。) - This time I will return the same day rather than stay overnight.

日帰り旅行 (ひがえりりょこう) is a day trip; 日帰りの旅行
is another useful noun phrase.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) contributes the day, and
[帰](../kanji/帰.md), through [帰り](帰り.md), returning.
The native elements are ひ + かえり, with voicing to がえり.
The compound describes a return within that day.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) and [旅](旅.md) (たび) name trips
more broadly. [往復](往復.md) (おうふく) includes going and returning
but does not by itself require doing both on the same day.
[宿泊](宿泊.md) (しゅくはく), an overnight stay, is the relevant contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

日帰り describes the timing and lack of an overnight stay, not the purpose
or means of transport. It need not be a school outing, sightseeing, or
a short walk. A journey lasting only an hour is not automatically 日帰り
unless the going-and-returning arrangement is intended.
