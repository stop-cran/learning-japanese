# 担当 (たんとう) - being in charge

## Meaning

担当 means responsibility for an assigned area of work. It is a noun and a transitive する verb:
task + を担当する means "be in charge of that task."
With the noun construction, Nの担当です means "I am responsible for N," with the person supplied by context.

- 私は受付を担当しています。 (わたしはうけつけをたんとうしています。) - I am in charge of reception.
- この件の担当者は誰ですか。 (このけんのたんとうしゃはだれですか。) - Who is responsible for this matter?

担当者 (たんとうしゃ) explicitly names the person responsible.
担当している often describes a current assignment, not an action occurring for just a moment.

## How the kanji combine

担 contributes bearing or taking on a burden; [当](../kanji/当.md) contributes taking an assigned part
or being responsible. Together they describe taking charge of an area.
The on readings are タン + トウ, with a long final vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[係](係.md) (かかり) can also identify an assigned role or the person doing it.
[責任](責任.md) (せきにん), "responsibility," includes accountability and is broader than an assigned work area.
Being a 担当者 does not necessarily mean being everyone's manager.

## Distinctive meaning

担当 answers "Who handles this?" rather than automatically "Who has the highest authority?"
A person can be responsible for reception, a subject, or a customer account while reporting to someone else.
担当の先生 (たんとうのせんせい) identifies the teacher responsible for the relevant class or matter.
