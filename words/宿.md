# 宿 (やど) - lodging; inn

## Meaning

宿 is a noun for lodging or a place to stay, such as an inn or hotel. **宿を探す** (やどをさがす) means "look for accommodation"; **宿に泊まる** (やどにとまる) means "stay at a lodging."

- 駅の近くに宿を取りました。 (えきのちかくにやどをとりました。) - I arranged accommodation near the station.
- 今夜はこの宿に泊まります。 (こんやはこのやどにとまります。) - I will stay at this inn tonight.

宿を取る is a useful expression for securing a place to stay, not physically picking up a building.

## How the kanji combine

The single [宿](../kanji/宿.md) character carries the native reading やど. Its on reading シュク appears in related compounds, but the standalone noun in these examples is やど.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[旅館](旅館.md) (りょかん) and [ホテル](ホテル.md) name more specific accommodation categories. 宿 lets a speaker talk about the place to stay without making that distinction. [泊まる](泊まる.md) names the act of staying overnight.

## Distinctive meaning

宿 is not limited to a traditional Japanese inn; context can make "hotel" or simply "accommodation" the better English translation. In travel conversation, asking 宿は決まりましたか (やどはきまりましたか), "Have you decided where to stay?", concerns the overnight arrangement rather than someone's permanent address.
