# 着る (きる) — put on; wear

## Meaning

着る means putting on clothing that covers the upper body, such as a shirt, coat, or dress.
着ている (きている) commonly describes the resulting state, "be wearing."

- コートを着ます。 (コートをきます。) — I will put on a coat.
- 青いシャツを着ています。 (あおいシャツをきています。) — I am wearing a blue shirt.

The clothing takes を: [服](服.md)を着る (ふくをきる), "put on clothes."

## How the kanji combine

This is one native Japanese verb, not a compound. [着](../kanji/着.md) supplies き, and る is okurigana.
It is an ichidan verb: 着ます (きます), 着て (きて), 着た (きた), 着ない (きない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 履く (はく) is used for lower-body clothing and footwear, such as trousers and shoes; it is not a free replacement for 着る.
- 着せる (きせる) means putting clothes on someone else.
- 脱ぐ (ぬぐ), "take off," is the practical opposite for clothes.

## Distinctive meaning

English "wear" covers more objects than 着る. Learn the Japanese verb with the item, rather than using 着る
for every accessory or piece of clothing.

[切る](切る.md) (きる, cut) has the same dictionary-form reading but different conjugation:
切って (きって) versus 着て (きて). Do not add a small っ to 着て.
