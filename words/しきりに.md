# しきりに (しきりに) - repeatedly; insistently

## Meaning

しきりに describes something happening repeatedly or persistently. It can also
convey eagerness or insistence. It is an adverb; place it before the action
without adding another に.

- 犬がしきりにほえています。 (いぬがしきりにほえています。) - The dog keeps barking.
- 彼はしきりに旅行に行きたがっています。 (かれはしきりにりょこうにいきたがっています。) - He keeps showing how eager he is to go on a trip.

The second example uses 行きたがっている to describe someone else's displayed
desire. しきりに strengthens that persistent eagerness.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. 頻りに is also listed in the dictionary; 頻
associates with frequency. The word is not a compound of separately meaningful
kana, and there is no reason to invent a kanji for each syllable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

何度も (なんども), "many times," directly emphasizes repetition.
[ずっと](ずっと.md) can describe something continuing throughout a period.
しきりに highlights recurrence or intensity, rather than guaranteeing that
there were no pauses at all.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn it with an action that can recur or be pursued insistently, such as
barking, asking, or expressing a wish. It is not a count of occurrences,
and it need not express annoyance: whether the persistence is welcome depends
on the context.
