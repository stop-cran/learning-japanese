# 優れる (すぐれる) - excel; be superior

## Meaning

優れる is an intransitive ichidan verb meaning to excel, be excellent, or be superior. A field or quality can take に; a comparison can use より. Forms include 優れます, 優れた, and 優れている.

- この製品は安全性に優れています。 (このせいひんはあんぜんせいにすぐれています。) - This product excels in safety.
- 彼女は優れた研究者です。 (かのじょはすぐれたけんきゅうしゃです。) - She is an outstanding researcher.

優れた + noun is an established way to describe something as excellent. Its た form need not mean that the excellence existed only in the past.

## How the kanji combine

[優](../kanji/優.md) supplies the excellence meaning. The native verb is すぐれる, not the reading やさしい found in [優しい](優しい.md), "kind; gentle." The kana ending marks which word and conjugated form is being used.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上手](上手.md) (じょうず) describes skill at an activity; [得意](得意.md) (とくい) can name a person's strong area. 優れる also applies to products, qualities, and results. [劣る](劣る.md) (おとる), "be inferior," is a useful opposite in comparisons.

## Distinctive meaning

優れている presents an evaluation of excellence, which may be relative to other things or to an understood standard. The word does not itself supply the evidence or comparison group. Explain what is excellent with phrases such as 性能に優れている (せいのうにすぐれている), "have excellent performance."
