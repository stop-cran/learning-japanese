# 然る (しかる) — such

## Meaning

然る read しかる is a formal noun-modifying expression meaning "such; of that kind." For practical recognition, learn しかるべき, "appropriate; proper," as a whole expression.

しかるべき人に相談してください。（しかるべきひとにそうだんしてください。） — Please consult the appropriate person.

The reading さる has the separate sense "a certain; a particular."

さる人から聞きました。（さるひとからききました。） — I heard it from a certain person.

## How the kanji combine

[然](../kanji/然.md) contributes the meaning "so; such." Kana spelling is usual. Keep しかる and さる distinct rather than transferring every sense between them.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そのような is a more transparent everyday expression for "such." ある人（あるひと） means "a certain person," comparable to さる人.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss "a particular" belongs specifically to さる in JMdict, not to しかる. This word is also unrelated to 叱る（しかる）, "scold." Do not conjugate the noun-modifying 然る as though it were that verb.
