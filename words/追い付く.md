# 追い付く (おいつく) - catch up with; draw level

## Meaning

追い付く means to catch up with someone or reach a level that was ahead of you. It is an intransitive godan verb: the person or level caught up with takes に.

- 走って友達に追い付きました。 (はしってともだちにおいつきました。) - I ran and caught up with my friend.
- 練習して、先輩のレベルに追い付きたいです。 (れんしゅうして、せんぱいのレベルにおいつきたいです。) - I want to practice and reach my senior's level.

The past is 追い付いた (おいついた), and the negative is 追い付かない (おいつかない). Neither example says that the person then moved ahead.

## How the kanji combine

[追](../kanji/追.md) contributes chasing or following, while [付](../kanji/付.md) contributes reaching or joining in this compound verb. 追い is the connecting form of 追う (おう), followed by 付く (つく). 追いつく is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

追い越す (おいこす) means to pass or overtake: 友達を追い越す means moving ahead of the friend. Notice its を, compared with 友達に追い付く. [遅れる](遅れる.md) (おくれる), "fall behind/be late," provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The essential result is closing the gap. In a race it can be physical; in study or work it can concern ability or progress. English "overtake" is misleading if understood as necessarily passing the other person.
