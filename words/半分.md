# 半分 (はんぶん) — half

## Meaning

半分 is "half of something": ケーキを半分食べた (I ate half of the cake), 半分に切る (cut in half), 半分ずつ (half each), 半分くらい (about half).

## How the kanji combine

[半](../kanji/半.md) (half) plus [分](../kanji/分.md) (part, share): "a half share". The 分 is read ぶん here (not ふん/ぷん as in minutes), so it means "portion" rather than "minute".

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 半 (used with numbers: 三時半), 半ば なかば (halfway), 半数 はんすう (half the number).
- Antonym: 全部 ぜんぶ (all), 全体 ぜんたい (the whole).

## Distinctive meaning

半分 is a noun for half of a whole that can also be used adverbially. 半 alone goes after a number (一年半 one and a half years), but 半分 stands on its own. It also works adverbially: 半分寝ていた (I was half asleep).
