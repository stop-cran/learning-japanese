# 高校 (こうこう) — senior high school

## Meaning

高校 is a senior high school, the stage after junior high school in Japan. It is the ordinary shortened form of 高等学校（こうとうがっこう）.

妹は高校に通っています。（いもうとはこうこうにかよっています。） — My younger sister attends high school.

## How the kanji combine

[高](../kanji/高.md) means high, and [校](../kanji/校.md) identifies a school. Both are read こう here. Keep both long vowels: こうこう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

高等学校 is the full institutional name. 中学校（ちゅうがっこう） is junior high school, and 大学（だいがく） is a university. These are different educational stages.

## Distinctive meaning

高校生（こうこうせい） means a high-school student; 高校 itself names the school or school stage, not the student. Use 高校の before a noun, as in 高校の先生, "a high-school teacher."
