# 独身 (どくしん) - unmarried; single

## Meaning

独身 describes being unmarried. It is a noun that can modify another noun with の:
独身の人 (どくしんのひと), "an unmarried person."
Use 独身です to state the status.

- 兄は独身です。 (あにはどくしんです。) - My older brother is unmarried.
- 独身の友人と旅行に行きました。 (どくしんのゆうじんとりょこうにいきました。) - I went on a trip with an unmarried friend.

The word is not restricted to men. It says nothing by itself about sexual abstinence,
whether someone is dating, or whether they want to marry.

## How the kanji combine

独 contributes "alone; single," and 身 contributes "person; oneself."
Together they name unmarried status. The literal associations do not imply that an unmarried person
lives alone or has no close relationships. The on readings combine as ドク + シン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結婚](結婚.md) (けっこん), "marriage," gives the relevant contrast:
結婚している means "be married."
[夫婦](夫婦.md) (ふうふ) names a married couple.
一人暮らし (ひとりぐらし), "living alone," concerns a household arrangement rather than marital status.

## Distinctive meaning

English "single" can ambiguously suggest having no romantic partner; 独身 centers on being unmarried.
Likewise, translating it as "bachelorhood" can wrongly suggest a male-only category.
Choose "unmarried" when precision matters.
Questions about someone's status may be personal, so the appropriateness of asking is a separate issue
from whether the sentence is grammatically correct.
