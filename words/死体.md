# 死体 (したい) - dead body; corpse

## Meaning

死体 is a dead body: a human corpse or cadaver, or an animal carcass.
It is a noun, not a verb meaning to die.

- 警察が死体を発見しました。 (けいさつがしたいをはっけんしました。) - The police discovered a body.
- 公園で鳥の死体を見つけました。 (こうえんでとりのしたいをみつけました。) - I found a dead bird in the park.

The first is a matter-of-fact report; the second illustrates the animal use.
Neither sentence describes the cause of death.

## How the kanji combine

[死](../kanji/死.md) contributes death, and [体](../kanji/体.md)
contributes body: a body after death.
The on readings combine as シ + タイ.
屍体 is a rare dictionary spelling, also read したい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[体](体.md) (からだ) and [身体](身体.md) (しんたい) mean body
without inherently saying it is dead.
遺体 (いたい) is useful when respectfully referring to a deceased person's body.
生き物 (いきもの), a living thing, provides a contextual contrast,
not an interchangeable name for a body.

## Distinctive meaning

死体 can sound clinical or objectifying when referring to a person.
遺体 is commonly chosen in more person-respecting references.
This is not an absolute ban on human 死体: investigative and medical
contexts can use it. Choose wording with the situation and the people
affected in mind.
