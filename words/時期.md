# 時期 (じき) - time; period

## Meaning

時期 is a time, period, season, or stage associated with something happening. It is a noun and can form time expressions such as **この時期**, "at this time of year; during this period."

- この時期は雨が多いです。 (このじきはあめがおおいです。) - There is a lot of rain at this time of year.
- 引っ越しの時期を決めました。 (ひっこしのじきをきめました。) - We decided when to move.

**activity の時期** identifies the period for that activity. 時期が来る (じきがくる) means "the time comes," and 時期を待つ (じきをまつ) means "wait for the right time."

## How the kanji combine

[時](../kanji/時.md) contributes time; [期](../kanji/期.md) contributes a period or stage. The compound focuses on a stretch or point in the course of events. The on readings are ジ + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時間](時間.md) (じかん) can express duration, while [時刻](時刻.md) (じこく) identifies a clock time. [季節](季節.md) (きせつ) names a season. 時期 may concern a season, but can also refer to a stage of life or a suitable moment for action.

## Distinctive meaning

時期 asks "when in the larger course of events?" rather than necessarily "at what hour?" A suitable moving period could be spring or after graduation; specifying 時期 does not require choosing a precise date and time.
