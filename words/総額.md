# 総額 (そうがく) - total amount of money

## Meaning

総額 is a total monetary amount or sum. It is a noun, not the verb for
performing addition. The surrounding phrase identifies which payments or
amounts are included.

- 費用の総額は十万円です。
  (ひようのそうがくはじゅうまんえんです。) - The total cost is 100,000 yen.
- 支払う前に総額を確認してください。
  (しはらうまえにそうがくをかくにんしてください。) - Please check the total amount before paying.

総額で十万円 (そうがくでじゅうまんえん) means 100,000 yen in total.
The currency and number are supplied by the expression, not by 総額 alone.

## How the kanji combine

[総](../kanji/総.md) contributes whole or overall, and [額](../kanji/額.md) contributes an
amount or sum, especially money. The first character specifies the scope of
the amount: the total. The on readings ソウ + ガク give そうがく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[合計](合計.md) (ごうけい) also names a total and can take する for adding
amounts together. [計](計.md) (けい) has an in-total use before a quantity.

[金額](金額.md) (きんがく) and [額](額.md) (がく) can identify a monetary
amount without asserting that it includes the whole relevant sum. Their
meanings overlap when the amount under discussion is the total.

## Distinctive meaning

A total is only meaningful relative to its scope. 総額 does not automatically
promise that tax, delivery or every possible later charge is included.
Specify those conditions rather than treating the word itself as an
all-inclusive payment guarantee.
