# アクセント - accent; emphasis

## Meaning

アクセント can mean a pronunciation accent, word stress, or pitch accent.
It also covers intonation, a language or regional accent, and an emphasis or highlight
in design or music. The context determines which kind is meant.

- この単語のアクセントを確認しました。 (このたんごのアクセントをかくにんしました。) - I checked the accent of this word.
- 赤いバッグが服装のアクセントになっています。 (あかいバッグがふくそうのアクセントになっています。) - The red bag adds an accent to the outfit.

アクセントを置く (アクセントをおく) means "place an accent or emphasis."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[強調](強調.md) (きょうちょう), "emphasis," overlaps with making a feature stand out.
[発音](発音.md) (はつおん), "pronunciation," is broader than accent alone.
イントネーション refers to intonation, rather than making every use of アクセント identical.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese pitch accent is not simply an instruction to pronounce one syllable louder.
Conversely, the design use is not a pronunciation rule. The English label "accent" should
prompt you to identify the domain, not assume that every occurrence concerns a foreign accent.
