# 気温 (きおん) - air temperature

## Meaning

気温 is the temperature of the air, as in a weather forecast. It is a noun. 気温が高い (きおんがたかい) means the air temperature is high; 気温が低い (きおんがひくい) means it is low.

- 今日の最高気温は三十度です。 (きょうのさいこうきおんはさんじゅうどです。) - Today's maximum air temperature is thirty degrees.
- 夜になると気温が下がります。 (よるになるときおんがさがります。) - The air temperature drops at night.

最高気温 names the maximum, and 最低気温 (さいていきおん) names the minimum. Japanese weather reports normally give temperatures in degrees Celsius.

## How the kanji combine

[気](../kanji/気.md) contributes air here, and 温 contributes temperature or warmth. The first component specifies what is being measured: the temperature of the air. The readings キ + オン remain distinct in きおん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[温度](温度.md) (おんど) is the broader word for temperature, including that of water or objects. [体温](体温.md) (たいおん) is body temperature. They are related measurements, not alternative names for the same thing.

## Distinctive meaning

気温 does not mean how warm a person's body is or how hot bathwater is. [暑い](暑い.md) (あつい) and [寒い](寒い.md) (さむい) describe heat or cold as experienced in the surroundings; 気温 identifies the measurable air temperature. High and low temperatures are contrasts within the scale, not opposites of the noun 気温 itself.
