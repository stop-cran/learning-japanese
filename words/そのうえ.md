# そのうえ - moreover; on top of that

## Meaning

そのうえ adds a further fact: moreover, besides, or on top of that.
It commonly connects a new sentence or clause to what has just been said.
The additional fact can be favorable or unfavorable.

- この部屋は広いです。そのうえ、駅にも近いです。 (このへやはひろいです。そのうえ、えきにもちかいです。) - This room is spacious. Moreover, it is close to the station.
- 雨が降ってきました。そのうえ、風も強くなりました。 (あめがふってきました。そのうえ、かぜもつよくなりました。) - It started raining. On top of that, the wind grew stronger.

The written form **その上 (そのうえ)** also has a literal spatial use:

- 箱があります。その上に本を置いてください。 (はこがあります。そのうえにほんをおいてください。) - There is a box. Please put the book on top of it.

Here に marks a location; the phrase does not mean "moreover."

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual for the connector. In その上, その refers back to something
and [上](../kanji/上.md) (うえ) supplies above/on top.
其の上 is another dictionary spelling, with rare 其の.
The literal and connective uses belong to this family, but their sentence roles differ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[それに](それに.md), [更に](更に.md) (さらに) and [しかも](しかも.md)
also add information.
[そのほか](そのほか.md) can introduce something else in addition.
These overlaps do not establish unrestricted substitution in every construction.
For physical position, 上 contrasts with [下](下.md) (した).

## Distinctive meaning

The connective そのうえ does not itself mean "therefore":
an added fact is not necessarily a consequence of the first one.
Read the construction, not just the shared English phrase "on top of that,"
to distinguish addition from a physical location.
