# 自宅 (じたく) — one's home

## Meaning

自宅 means one's own home. "Own" identifies whose residence it is; it does not assert legal ownership of the building.
For an action at home, use 自宅で. For movement to the home, a verb such as 帰る can take 自宅に.

- 今日は自宅で仕事をします。 (きょうはじたくでしごとをします。) — I will work from home today.
- 友達は自宅に帰りました。 (ともだちはじたくにかえりました。) — My friend went back to their own home.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md), "self," modifies [宅](../kanji/宅.md), "residence."
The result is the residence belonging to the person under discussion in the sense of where they live.
Read the compound with ジ + タク, not the standalone readings of a different word for home.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[家](家.md) (いえ) is a broader word for a house or home and can also refer to a household.
実家 (じっか) means one's parents' home.
Someone's 自宅 and 実家 may be the same address, but the words identify it from different relationships.
住宅 (じゅうたく), "housing; residence," does not by itself identify whose home it is.

## Distinctive meaning

自宅 is not fixed to the speaker: the second example concerns the friend's home.
It also need not be a detached house; a rented apartment can be 自宅.
Use the surrounding sentence to identify the resident rather than translating every occurrence as "my house."
