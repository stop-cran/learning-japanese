# 散らかす (ちらかす) - scatter around; make a mess

## Meaning

散らかす means to scatter things around or leave a place untidy.
It is a transitive godan verb: the things scattered or place made messy take を.

- 部屋を散らかさないでください。
  (へやをちらかさないでください。) - Please do not make a mess of the room.
- 子供がおもちゃを散らかしました。
  (こどもがおもちゃをちらかしました。) - The child scattered the toys around.

Forms include 散らかします, 散らかした, 散らかして, and 散らかさない.
The す ending gives して in the て-form, not って.

## How the kanji combine

[散](../kanji/散.md) contributes scattering and is read ち here.
らかす completes the native verb.
The conjugation stem is ちらかし, as in 散らかします,
not simply the part written with the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[散らかる](散らかる.md) (ちらかる) is intransitive:
おもちゃが散らかる presents the toys as lying scattered.
[片付ける](片付ける.md) (かたづける), tidy up or put away,
is a practical contrast.
[汚す](汚す.md) (よごす), make dirty or stain, concerns dirt rather than necessarily disorder.

## Distinctive meaning

An untidy room can contain clean toys; messiness does not require stains.
散らかす foregrounds the scattered or disordered result, not simply any distribution.
[混乱](混乱.md) (こんらん), [荒れる](荒れる.md) (あれる),
and [混雑](混雑.md) (こんざつ) have broader disorder uses.
Those overlaps do not make physical clutter, mental confusion, and crowding identical.
