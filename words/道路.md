# 道路 (どうろ) — road

## Meaning

道路 is a road used for travel. It is useful when describing a road's width, condition, or traffic.
It does not by itself mean that the road is a highway.

- この道路は車が多いです。 (このどうろはくるまがおおいです。) — There are many cars on this road.
- 広い道路を渡りました。 (ひろいどうろをわたりました。) — I crossed a wide road.

## How the kanji combine

[道](../kanji/道.md) and [路](../kanji/路.md) both contribute road or way meanings.
This is a reinforcing pair of related meanings, read ドウ + ロ.
The compound names a road, rather than two separate routes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[道](道.md) (みち) also means a road or path, and can refer more broadly to a way, method, or course of life.
道路 keeps the road sense in focus; 道 has a wider range.
There is no useful direct antonym for the physical object "road."

## Distinctive meaning

If you are describing traffic on a road, 道路 fits.
Do not carry it over automatically to abstract English expressions such as "a way of thinking":
sharing the English gloss "way" does not make 道路 a general word for a method.
