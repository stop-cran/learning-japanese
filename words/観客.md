# 観客 (かんきゃく) - spectator; audience

## Meaning

観客 is a noun for someone watching a performance, sporting event, or other spectacle. Depending on context, it means one spectator or an audience collectively.

- 観客は試合を見ていました。 (かんきゃくはしあいをみていました。) - The spectators were watching the match.
- 劇場には大勢の観客がいました。 (げきじょうにはおおぜいのかんきゃくがいました。) - There was a large audience in the theater.

A number with a counter makes the count explicit: 一人の観客 (ひとりのかんきゃく), "one spectator." 観客席 (かんきゃくせき) means seating for the audience.

## How the kanji combine

[観](../kanji/観.md) contributes watching or viewing, and [客](../kanji/客.md) means guest or customer. 観 specifies what kind of guest: someone there to watch. Learn the full reading かんきゃく, including the small ゃ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[客](客.md) (きゃく) is broader: a guest or customer need not be watching anything. [選手](選手.md) (せんしゅ), "player; competitor," is a contrasting role at a match, not an antonym in every context. [俳優](俳優.md) (はいゆう), "actor," names a performer rather than the audience.

## Distinctive meaning

観客 describes a role in relation to an event, not whether a person paid admission. It also does not mean the same thing as "fan": someone may watch a match without supporting either team. An audience member at one event may be a performer at another.
