# 満員 (まんいん) - full of people; at capacity

## Meaning

満員 describes a place or vehicle full of people, including one that has reached its admission capacity. Use 満員だ or 満員の + noun.

- バスは満員でした。 (バスはまんいんでした。) - The bus was full.
- 満員の電車に乗りました。 (まんいんのでんしゃにのりました。) - I boarded a packed train.

満員御礼 (まんいんおんれい) is a notice thanking people for a full house.

## How the kanji combine

[満](../kanji/満.md) means "full," and [員](../kanji/員.md) concerns people or members. The compound uses on readings マン + イン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[混雑](混雑.md) (こんざつ) describes crowding or congestion; it does not necessarily mean that a formal capacity has been reached. [一杯](一杯.md) (いっぱい) can mean "full" more broadly, including containers. [空く](空く.md) (あく) can describe a vehicle becoming less crowded.

## Distinctive meaning

The relevant fullness is people, not any substance or product. A full glass is not 満員. A full-house or sold-out translation can fit admissions, but does not make 満員 the general expression for sold-out merchandise.
