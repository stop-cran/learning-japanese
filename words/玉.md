# 玉 (たま) - ball; jewel

## Meaning

玉 (たま) can name a ball or rounded object, and also a gem, jewel, or pearl.
Its meaning depends on the object and expression. The coin use is a suffix,
usually pronounced だま, rather than an unrestricted standalone meaning "coin."

- ガラスの玉が床を転がりました。 (ガラスのたまがゆかをころがりました。) - A glass ball rolled across the floor.
- 百円玉が二枚あります。 (ひゃくえんだまがにまいあります。) - I have two hundred-yen coins.

Each coin in the second example is worth one hundred yen. 玉 changes to だま in
百円玉; the coins are counted with 枚 (まい).

## How the kanji combine

玉 supplies the native word たま. It has associations with jewels and round
objects. The whole word must be read in context; its on reading ギョク appears
in other words, not in the glass-ball example.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[球](球.md) (たま) is another spelling in the same dictionary entry, associated
especially with sports balls and spherical forms. [ボール](ボール.md) also names
balls in ordinary contexts. 珠 is another spelling associated with gems and beads.

## Distinctive meaning

Shared dictionary membership does not mean every spelling is equally suitable
in every expression. Learn 百円玉 with its written 玉 and reading だま; do not
change it to 球 merely because both can mean a ball. Likewise, the jewel meaning
does not make an ordinary glass ball valuable.
