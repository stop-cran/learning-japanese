# 四角い (しかくい) - square; rectangular

## Meaning

四角い describes a square or rectangular shape. It is an i-adjective: use it directly before a noun, without な.

- 四角い箱に入れてください。 (しかくいはこにいれてください。) - Please put it in a rectangular box.
- このテーブルは四角くありません。 (このテーブルはしかくくありません。) - This table is not square or rectangular.

The adverbial form is 四角く, the plain negative 四角くない, and the past 四角かった.

## How the kanji combine

[四角](四角.md) しかく, a four-cornered shape, supplies the stem. [四](../kanji/四.md) シ means four and [角](../kanji/角.md) カク means corner or angle; the native adjective ending い makes 四角い.

The app's kango category follows the Sino-Japanese stem here. The whole adjective combines that stem with a native ending; it is not an adjective made exclusively from on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[丸い](丸い.md) まるい, round, is a useful shape contrast. 四角 is the related noun: compare 四角を描く (しかくをかく), draw a quadrilateral, with 四角い紙 (しかくいかみ), square or rectangular paper.

## Distinctive meaning

四角い does not require a mathematically perfect square with equal sides. For exact geometry, specify 正方形 (せいほうけい) or 長方形 (ちょうほうけい). Daijisen also records a figurative stiff or conventional manner; that is contextual additional usage, not another geometric property.
