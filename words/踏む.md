# 踏む (ふむ) - step on; tread on

## Meaning

踏む means to step or tread on something. It is a transitive godan verb: 踏みます, 踏んで, 踏んだ and 踏まない.

- 足を踏んでしまって、すみません。 (あしをふんでしまって、すみません。) - Sorry I stepped on your foot.
- 花を踏まないでください。 (はなをふまないでください。) - Please do not step on the flowers.

The thing stepped on takes を. ～てしまう in the first example expresses regret about the completed action.

## How the kanji combine

踏 represents stepping and is read ふ here, with む as okurigana. The て-form and past change む to んで and んだ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

歩く (あるく) means to walk; it does not by itself say what is stepped on. 蹴る (ける), "kick," describes a different foot action.

## Distinctive meaning

踏む may be accidental or deliberate. It does not necessarily mean trampling repeatedly or damaging the object. The basic physical use is a contact action, not simply traveling on foot.
