# 助ける (たすける) — help; save

## Meaning

助ける means helping someone or saving them from danger or difficulty. It is transitive: the person helped
can be marked with を. Its forms include 助けます (たすけます), 助けた (たすけた), and 助けて (たすけて).

- 困っている人を助けました。 (こまっているひとをたすけました。) — I helped someone who was in difficulty.
- 助けてください。 (たすけてください。) — Please help me.

## How the kanji combine

[助](../kanji/助.md) supplies the help or rescue meaning. The kana ける completes the native verb;
this is not a compound assembled from on readings.
Keep たすける distinct from 助かる (たすかる), which describes being saved or helped.

## Synonyms and antonyms

手伝う (てつだう) also means "help," especially when assisting with a task: 仕事を手伝う
(しごとをてつだう), "help with the work." 助ける can describe relief or rescue beyond sharing a task.
These uses overlap; do not infer that 助ける is limited to emergencies.

助かる is a viewpoint contrast, not an antonym: it presents the person's survival or relief rather than the helper's action.

## Distinctive meaning

The short request 助けて！ can be urgent, but the verb itself does not require a life-threatening situation.
Context tells the listener whether assistance, relief, or rescue is meant.
