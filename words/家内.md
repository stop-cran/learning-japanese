# 家内 (かない) — my wife

## Meaning

家内 is a modest way of referring to one's own wife when speaking to someone outside the family. It does not mean a housewife as an occupation.

家内は今、仕事に行っています。（かないはいま、しごとにいっています。） — My wife is at work now.

## How the kanji combine

[家](../kanji/家.md), "house; family," and [内](../kanji/内.md), "inside," use on readings. The literal household sense also appears in expressions such as 家内安全（かないあんぜん）, "safety of the household."

## Synonyms and antonyms

妻（つま） is a neutral word for one's wife. 主婦（しゅふ） means "housewife; homemaker" and describes a role, not the same relationship.

## Distinctive meaning

家内 is a traditional expression; 妻 avoids its household-centered wording. Do not use 家内 as a respectful title for someone else's wife, or assume that the person does not work outside the home.
