# 直 (じき) - soon; before long

## Meaning

直 (じき) expresses "soon; before long." For this time sense, learn the adverbial patterns
じき帰る (じきかえる), "return soon," and じきに終わる (じきにおわる), "finish before long."
Kana makes the intended reading clear.

- じきに雨がやむでしょう。 (じきにあめがやむでしょう。) - The rain will probably stop soon.
- もうじき春ですね。 (もうじきはるですね。) - Spring will be here before long.

This predicts a short wait; it does not promise that something happens instantaneously.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) is a single character with several vocabulary readings.
The primary reading here is じき. A separate dictionary entry reads **なお**:
it lists "straight" with an **archaic** label, plus separate "ordinary/common" and "doing nothing" senses.
These recognition meanings are not an instruction to replace modern adjectives with なお.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すぐ, also used as [すぐに](すぐに.md), overlaps with "soon" and "immediately," depending on context.
まもなく also means "before long."
For modern "straight," compare [まっすぐ](まっすぐ.md), rather than treating archaic 直 (なお) as an everyday equivalent.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the readings separate: じき in the examples is temporal, whereas the なお entry above has its own adjective senses.
Neither entry establishes "mischief" as a meaning.
The dictionary marks the なお "straight" sense archaic; that label should not be silently applied to every listed なお sense.
For a modern expression meaning "ordinary," learn [普通](普通.md) (ふつう) separately rather than assuming an everyday substitution pattern with なお.
