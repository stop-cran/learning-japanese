# 例外 (れいがい) - exception

## Meaning

例外 is an exception: a case outside the usual rule or general pattern.
It is a noun. 例外なく (れいがいなく) means "without exception."

- この規則には例外があります。 (このきそくにはれいがいがあります。) - There are exceptions to this rule.
- 全員が例外なく参加しました。 (ぜんいんがれいがいなくさんかしました。) - Everyone participated without exception.

例外として認める (れいがいとしてみとめる) means to allow something
as an exception.

## How the kanji combine

[例](../kanji/例.md) contributes a precedent or customary case here,
and [外](../kanji/外.md) outside: something outside the customary rule.
The on readings are レイ + ガイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[規則](規則.md) (きそく), a rule, is what an exception departs from.
[特別](特別.md) (とくべつ), special, is broader: something can be
special without being an exception to a particular rule.
[普通](普通.md) (ふつう), ordinary, offers a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

An exception needs a relevant generalization, not necessarily a written law.
例外的な (れいがいてきな) means exceptional; 例外的に means
exceptionally or as an exception. Bare 例外 is not an unrestricted
な-adjective replacing every use of 特別な.
