# 回 (かい) - times; occurrence counter

## Meaning

回 counts occurrences: how many times something happens. Attach it to a number, as in 二回 (にかい), "twice," or 三回 (さんかい), "three times." Learn the sound changes in 一回 (いっかい), 六回 (ろっかい), and 八回 (はっかい). 何回 (なんかい) asks "how many times?"

- この映画を三回見ました。 (このえいがをさんかいみました。) - I have seen this film three times.
- 一日に何回バスが来ますか。 (いちにちになんかいバスがきますか。) - How many times a day does the bus come?

The counter phrase can directly modify the action. 一日に sets the period over which occurrences are counted.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [回](../kanji/回.md) uses the on reading カイ. Its association with going around helps connect a cycle or occurrence with counting repetitions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[度](度.md) (ど) also counts occasions, as in [もう一度](もう一度.md), "once more." Their established expressions overlap, but one counter cannot replace the other in every compound.

## Distinctive meaning

回 measures occurrences, not elapsed time. 三回 is "three times"; 三時間 (さんじかん) is "three hours." Add 目 for order: 二回目 (にかいめ), "the second time." 一回も行っていない means "have not gone even once"; も combines with the negative to emphasize zero occurrences.
