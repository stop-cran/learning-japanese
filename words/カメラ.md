# カメラ — camera

## Meaning

カメラ is a camera, whether a separate device or a camera built into another device. It is a noun; use で to mark it as the instrument for taking a photograph.

このカメラで花の写真を撮りました。 (このカメラではなのしゃしんをとりました。) — I took a photograph of flowers with this camera.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana and has no kanji components. Read three morae, カ・メ・ラ; there is no long-vowel mark in the standard spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

デジタルカメラ specifies a digital camera, often shortened to デジカメ. 写真 (しゃしん) is a photograph, the image rather than the device. ビデオカメラ specifies a video camera.

## Distinctive meaning

カメラを買う means buying a camera, whereas 写真を撮る means taking a photograph. Do not use カメラ as the direct object of 撮る when you mean taking a picture with it: that would ordinarily mean photographing the camera itself.
