# ぐにゃぐにゃ — limp and pliable

## Meaning

Soft enough to bend or lose a firm shape: floppy, limp, or pliable.

- このゴムのおもちゃはぐにゃぐにゃ曲（ま）がる。 — This rubber toy bends floppily.
- ゆですぎて、麺（めん）がぐにゃぐにゃになった。 — I overcooked the noodles and they became limp.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぷにぷに](ぷにぷに.md) suggests squishy resilience under a finger. [かちかち](かちかち.md) and [がちがち](がちがち.md) offer hard or rigid contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

ぐにゃぐにゃした物（もの）has little firmness, but need not be liquid or sticky. In food it can criticize excessive softness; in a bendable toy it can be a neutral observation. It does not guarantee the pleasant chewiness of [もちもち](もちもち.md).
