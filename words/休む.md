# 休む (やすむ) — to rest, to take time off, to be absent

## Meaning

休む covers several senses that English separates:

- **Rest**: ちょっと休みましょう — let's rest a moment.
- **Be absent or take a day off**: 会社を休む, 学校を休む, 熱で休んだ.
- **Go to bed, sleep**: もうお休みになりました。 (もうおやすみになりました。) — They have already gone to bed.

Here お休みになる uses お + ます-stem + になる to honor the person whose action is described.
The example respectfully describes someone else's going to bed, not an ordinary way to describe your own action.
お休みなさい (おやすみなさい) is the separate greeting "good night."

## How the kanji combine

[休](../kanji/休.md) alone, with the kun reading やす + okurigana む. Related verbs: 休まる (be rested, intransitive: 心が休まる) and 休める (rest something, transitive: 体を休める).
For absence or taking time off, を can mark the work, school attendance, or activity being missed or suspended:
会社を休む (かいしゃをやすむ), "take time off work," and 学校を休む (がっこうをやすむ), "miss school."

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 休憩する きゅうけいする (take a break, formal), 休息する きゅうそくする (rest, relax the body), 欠席する けっせきする (be absent from class or meeting), 欠勤する けっきんする (be absent from work).
- Sleep: 寝る ねる (plain "sleep"); お休みなさい is a greeting ("good night").
- Antonym: 働く はたらく (work), 出勤する／出席する (attend).

## Distinctive meaning

休む is the everyday word for both resting and calling in absent. 欠席 and 欠勤 state the fact of absence more formally, and
don't suggest rest. For notifying an absence, 明日は休みます is the standard phrasing.
