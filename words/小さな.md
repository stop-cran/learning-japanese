# 小さな (ちいさな) — small; little before a noun

## Meaning

小さな means small or little and directly precedes a noun. It is a prenominal word: a word used to modify the following noun, rather than an ordinary な-adjective.

小さな店でパンを買いました。 (ちいさなみせでパンをかいました。) — I bought bread at a little shop.

小さな声で話してください。 (ちいさなこえではなしてください。) — Please speak in a quiet voice.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) conveys smallness and is read ちい; さな completes the written form. Learn ちいさな as a unit that must be followed by a noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

小さい (ちいさい) is an い-adjective and often overlaps before a noun: 小さい店 also means "small shop." 大きな (おおきな), "big; large," is the corresponding prenominal contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not say この店は小さなです. To make a statement, use この店は小さいです (このみせはちいさいです), "This shop is small." Likewise, the past and negative come from 小さい: 小さかった and 小さくない. The final な in 小さな does not make it conjugate like 静かな (しずかな).
