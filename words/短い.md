# 短い (みじかい) — short in length or time

## Meaning

短い means **short in length or duration**. It is an い-adjective and directly modifies a noun.

- この鉛筆は短いです。 (このえんぴつはみじかいです。) — This pencil is short.
- 短い休みを取りました。 (みじかいやすみをとりました。) — I took a short break.

The negative is 短くない (みじかくない), and the past is 短かった (みじかかった).

## How the kanji combine

[短](../kanji/短.md) writes みじか, followed by the adjective's final い. Learn the full native reading みじかい; the on reading タン appears in compounds such as 短所 (たんしょ), “weak point.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

長い (ながい), “long,” is the direct contrast for length and duration. 小さい (ちいさい) means small overall; 低い (ひくい) means low and is used for short stature in 背が低い (せがひくい).

## Distinctive meaning

短い is not a universal translation of English “short.” For a short person, say 背が低い, not simply 人が短い. A short explanation or visit, however, naturally uses 短い because the relevant dimension is length or time.
