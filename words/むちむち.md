# むちむち — plump and full-bodied

## Meaning

むちむち describes a person or body part as plump and full. It is an appearance description,
so use it with care and with a clear, appropriate context.

- 赤ちゃんのほっぺがむちむちしている。
  — The baby's cheeks are plump.
- 赤ちゃんは、むちむちした腕を元気に動かした。
  — The baby moved its chubby arms energetically.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぷにぷに](ぷにぷに.md) emphasizes a soft, yielding feel when touched. むちむち emphasizes a
full, plump shape; neither word is an appropriate default description for every person.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records a related spelling, むっちり. This card keeps the requested form むちむち
and does not treat the two spellings as separate meanings.
