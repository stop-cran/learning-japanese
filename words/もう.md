# もう — already; no longer

## Meaning

もう commonly means "already," presenting a change or completion as having happened by now.

- 宿題はもう終わりました。 (しゅくだいはもうおわりました。) — I've already finished my homework.
- もう食べません。 (もうたべません。) — I won't eat any more.

With a negative verb, もう often means "not any more/no longer," not "not yet." In questions, もう食べましたか (もうたべましたか) asks whether someone has eaten already.

With an event expected shortly, bare もう can mean "soon," even without すぐ. For example, while waiting for a booked taxi:

- タクシーはもう来るでしょう。 (たくしーはもうくるでしょう。) — The taxi should be here soon.

The arrival is still expected, not completed. This reading depends on context; a non-past verb with もう does not always mean "soon."

## How the kanji combine

This word is written in hiragana. The long vowel in もう matters; も by itself is a different grammatical item.

## Synonyms and antonyms

まだ means "still" or, with a negative, "not yet." Compare まだ食べていません (まだたべていません), "I haven't eaten yet," with もう食べません, "I won't eat any more."

## Distinctive meaning

もう also adds an amount or repetition: もう一つ (もうひとつ), "one more," and もう一度 (もういちど), "once again." もうすぐ means "soon." Do not force "already" into all of these established patterns.
