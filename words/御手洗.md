# 御手洗 (みたらし) - shrine purification font

## Meaning

御手洗, read みたらし here, names a shrine's purification-water place, often a basin near the entrance. Worshippers cleanse their hands and rinse their mouths before worship. English "font" here means a basin of water, not a style of lettering.

- 参拝する前に、御手洗で手を清めました。 (さんぱいするまえに、みたらしでてをきよめました。) - Before worshipping, I purified my hands at the purification place.

The place can also be a natural water source rather than a manufactured basin. The word's reading and context matter more than the isolated written characters.

## How the kanji combine

[御](../kanji/御.md), [手](../kanji/手.md), and [洗](../kanji/洗.md) suggest respectful handwashing. Learn the fixed word reading みたらし; do not derive it by substituting the ordinary readings お, て, and あらい. This is a native word, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

手水 (てみず・ちょうず) names water for washing the hands. Related shrine vocabulary does not make every handwashing place the same physical installation.

[洗う](洗う.md) (あらう) describes washing. 御手洗 here names the purification place, not the action of washing.

## Distinctive meaning

The identical spelling 御手洗 also occurs for おてあらい, the polite restroom word taught at [お手洗い](お手洗い.md). That is a different dictionary entry and reading. Do not silently read the shrine word as おてあらい or ask for the toilet using みたらし.

The community-list N2 label here identifies this shrine word, not every word written 御手洗 or every historical use.
