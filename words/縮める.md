# 縮める (ちぢめる) - shorten; reduce; shrink

## Meaning

縮める means to make something shorter or smaller, or to reduce
an amount or gap. It is a transitive ichidan verb: mark the thing
changed with を.

- 会議の時間を縮めました。 (かいぎのじかんをちぢめました。) - We shortened the meeting time.
- 長い説明を三行に縮めました。 (ながいせつめいをさんぎょうにちぢめました。) - I condensed the long explanation into three lines.

に marks the resulting length or amount in the second example.
Forms include 縮めます (ちぢめます), 縮めて (ちぢめて),
and 縮めない (ちぢめない).

## How the kanji combine

縮 contributes shrinking or shortening; める completes the native
verb ちぢめる. It is not pronounced with the on reading シュク
used in [縮小](縮小.md) (しゅくしょう).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[縮む](縮む.md) (ちぢむ) is intransitive: 服が縮む
(ふくがちぢむ), "clothes shrink," rather than someone shrinking them.
[詰める](詰める.md) (つめる) also has shortening and closing-gap
uses. [伸ばす](伸ばす.md) (のばす), "stretch; lengthen," provides
a contrast when the dimension is length.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb also describes drawing one's body or limbs inward:
足を縮めて座りました (あしをちぢめてすわりました),
"I sat with my legs drawn in." A separately recorded fabric use
means crumpling or wrinkling. Those additional senses do not make
every instance a reduction in numerical quantity.

The text example illustrates condensing an account. Do not take the
source list's English "boil down" as a cooking instruction by itself.
