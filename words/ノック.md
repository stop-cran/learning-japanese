# ノック (ノック) - knock; fielding-practice hitting

## Meaning

ノック is a noun for knocking; ノックする is a transitive verb. A door takes を. In baseball, ノック also means hitting balls to players for fielding practice, not knocking on a surface.

- 部屋に入る前にドアをノックしてください。 (へやにはいるまえにドアをノックしてください。) - Please knock on the door before entering.
- 選手たちは一時間ノックを受けました。 (せんしゅたちはいちじかんノックをうけました。) - The players spent an hour fielding practice hits.

In baseball, ノックを受ける describes the fielders receiving the practice balls; ノックをする can describe the hitting side of the drill.

## How the kanji combine

ノック is a katakana loanword associated with English "knock." The small ッ marks the consonant hold before ク. There are no kanji components, and the Japanese sports use should be learned as its own established expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[叩く](叩く.md) (たたく) means to hit or tap more broadly. ドアを叩く can describe the physical action, while ノックする identifies the familiar door-knocking action.

[打つ](打つ.md) (うつ) is a broader hitting verb relevant to the baseball sense.

## Distinctive meaning

The setting decides whether ノック concerns a door or a baseball drill. English "fungo hitting" describes hitting practice balls; a learner need not know that specialized English term to understand the Japanese use. Neither sense is an antonym of the other: they are separate uses of the same loanword.
