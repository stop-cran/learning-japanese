# 両替 (りょうがえ) - changing money

## Meaning

両替 is money exchange, either between currencies or between denominations.
両替する is transitive: XをYに両替する means "exchange X into Y."
Use で for the place where the exchange occurs.

- 空港でドルを円に両替しました。 (くうこうでドルをえんにりょうがえしました。) - I exchanged dollars for yen at the airport.
- この千円札を百円玉に両替できますか。 (このせんえんさつをひゃくえんだまにりょうがえできますか。) - Can you change this thousand-yen note into hundred-yen coins?

The second example stays within one currency. 両替所 (りょうがえじょ) is a currency-exchange counter or office.

## How the kanji combine

[両](../kanji/両.md) is read リョウ and can name a traditional monetary unit; its familiar "both" meaning alone is not a useful translation here.
替 contributes changing or replacing, with native かえ voiced to がえ.
The compound mixes an on-reading and a native reading rather than having two on-readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[交換](交換.md) (こうかん), "exchange," applies to many things besides money.
お釣り (おつり) is change returned after payment, not the act of exchanging money.
[払う](払う.md) (はらう), "pay," describes payment rather than conversion into another currency or denomination.

## Distinctive meaning

English "change" is ambiguous: receiving coins back after a purchase and asking to exchange a note are different transactions.
Use 両替 for the latter.
The word itself does not say that the service is free; fees and exchange rates must be checked separately.
