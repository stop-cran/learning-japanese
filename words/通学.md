# 通学 (つうがく) - commuting to school

## Meaning

通学 is commuting to school or the school commute. 通学する is intransitive. The school can take に or へ, and the means of transport takes で.

- 毎日、自転車で学校に通学しています。 (まいにち、じてんしゃでがっこうにつうがくしています。) - I commute to school by bicycle every day.
- 通学に一時間かかります。 (つうがくにいちじかんかかります。) - My commute to school takes an hour.

The first sentence uses ～ています to describe an ongoing routine, not necessarily a journey happening at the moment of speaking. 通学時間 (つうがくじかん) is commuting time.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes going back and forth or attending regularly, and [学](../kanji/学.md) contributes study or school. Together they identify regular travel to a place of learning. The reading is ツウ + ガク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通う](通う.md) (かよう) is the broader native verb for regularly going somewhere: 学校に通う, "attend school." 通勤 (つうきん) concerns commuting to work.

登校 (とうこう) focuses on going to or arriving at school, rather than describing the commute as a routine.

## Distinctive meaning

通学 does not mean studying a subject, passing an examination, or traveling to a school once as a visitor. A visitor can 学校に行く, "go to the school," without that trip being their 通学. The noun keeps the purpose and recurring school connection in view.
