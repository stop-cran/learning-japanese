# 限る (かぎる) — limit; restrict

## Meaning

限る means restricting a range or setting a limit. In XをYに限る, X is what is restricted and Y is the allowed range.
The shorter Yに限る often states a condition on a notice.

- 参加者を十人に限ります。 (さんかしゃをじゅうにんにかぎります。) — We will limit the participants to ten people.
- この割引は学生に限ります。 (このわりびきはがくせいにかぎります。) — This discount is restricted to students.

## How the kanji combine

[限](../kanji/限.md) represents a boundary; る completes the native verb かぎる.
This is a godan verb, a verb whose stem ending changes before suffixes: its polite form is 限ります (かぎります),
and its て-form is 限って (かぎって).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[制限](制限.md) (せいげん) is restriction or limitation and can form 制限する.
限定 (げんてい) also concerns limiting a range. These nouns help name a restriction;
限る gives the direct verb pattern for stating what is allowed.

## Distinctive meaning

The に phrase is the included range, not what is excluded.
学生に限る permits students; 学生を除く (がくせいをのぞく), using [除く](除く.md), means excluding students.
That change reverses who qualifies.
