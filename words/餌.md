# 餌 (えさ) - animal feed; bait

## Meaning

餌 is food given to animals, or bait used to attract them. It is a noun. In **animal に餌をあげる**, the animal receives the feed.

- 毎朝、猫に餌をあげます。 (まいあさ、ねこにえさをあげます。) - I feed the cat every morning.
- 針に餌をつけて、魚を釣ります。 (はりにえさをつけて、さかなをつります。) - I put bait on the hook and fish.

The first example concerns feeding; the second concerns attracting an animal to a hook. The same noun fits both purposes.

## How the kanji combine

餌 writes the whole native noun えさ. There are no separate compound elements to pronounce. You will also encounter the kana spellings えさ and エサ. This card teaches えさ rather than treating every dictionary reading as freely substitutable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[食べ物](食べ物.md) (たべもの) is the broader idea of food, while [食事](食事.md) (しょくじ) refers to a meal or eating. Neither is an exact replacement for fishing bait. There is no general opposite of 餌.

## Distinctive meaning

餌 can extend to an enticement offered to people: お金を餌にする (おかねをえさにする), "use money as bait." That image suggests using the reward to lure someone. For an ordinary human meal, 食事 or ご飯 is a safer neutral choice than describing it as animal feed.
