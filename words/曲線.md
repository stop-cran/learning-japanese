# 曲線 (きょくせん) - curved line; curve

## Meaning

曲線 is a curved line, such as one drawn on paper or shown on a graph.

- 紙に滑らかな曲線を描きました。 (かみになめらかなきょくせんをえがきました。) - I drew a smooth curve on the paper.
- このグラフには二本の曲線があります。 (このグラフにはにほんのきょくせんがあります。) - This graph has two curves.

## How the kanji combine

[曲](../kanji/曲.md), bending or curving, modifies [線](../kanji/線.md), line: a curved line. Both readings are on readings, キョク + セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[カーブ](カーブ.md) also means a curve or bend, especially in contexts such as roads; it additionally names a baseball pitch. [曲がる](曲る.md) (まがる) is the verb "bend; curve; turn." A straight line is 直線 (ちょくせん), a contrast rather than another curved line.

## Distinctive meaning

曲線 names a line's shape, not the action of turning at a corner. It can describe a mathematical curve without referring to a road or a curveball. [線](線.md) is the broader word "line"; a line need not be curved.
