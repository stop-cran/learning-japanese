# 店員 (てんいん) — shop employee

## Meaning

店員 is an employee of a shop or store, such as a shop assistant or sales clerk.

- 店員さんに聞いてください。（てんいんさんに きいてください。）— Please ask a shop assistant.
- 姉はこの店の店員です。（あねは この みせの てんいんです。）— My older sister works at this shop.

## How the kanji combine

[店](../kanji/店.md) テン specifies the workplace, a shop; [員](../kanji/員.md) イン identifies a member of its staff. This is a workplace-plus-worker compound with two on readings.

The reading is て・ん・い・ん: keep the い after the first ん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 会社員 かいしゃいん means a company employee; it does not specifically identify someone working in a shop.
- 店長 てんちょう is a shop manager, a particular role rather than a synonym for every employee.
- 客 きゃく, "customer," is the contrasting role in a purchase, not an absolute antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

店員 describes a person's job, not necessarily the owner or manager. 店員さん is a polite way to refer to a staff member; すみません, "excuse me," is a useful way to get their attention.

The dictionary also lists ていいん as an irregular reading. This card teaches the standard てんいん.
