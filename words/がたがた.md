# がたがた — rattling; shaky; grumbling

## Meaning

Rattling or shaking, a rickety condition, or grumbling complaints.

- 風で窓（まど）ががたがた鳴（な）っている。 — The window is rattling in the wind.
- この古い机（つくえ）はがたがただ。 — This old desk is rickety.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[かたかた](かたかた.md) often makes a rattle sound lighter. [ぐらぐら](ぐらぐら.md) overlaps in instability; [ぶるぶる](ぶるぶる.md) in trembling.

## Distinctive meaning

がたがた言う（いう）can mean complaining, rather than making a literal rattle. Telling someone がたがた言うな, “stop griping,” is brusque; do not treat it as a neutral request for an explanation.
