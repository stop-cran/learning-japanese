# べたべた — sticky; clingy

## Meaning

An unpleasantly sticky surface; also thick or extensive application of something, or people clinging closely to someone.

- ジュースをこぼして、机がべたべたする。 — I spilled juice, and the desk feels sticky.
- 壁にシールをべたべた貼らないで。 — Don't plaster stickers all over the wall.
- 人前でそんなにべたべたしないで。 — Don't be so clingy and affectionate in public.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic repeating べた. Its voiced consonant is an observable sound feature, not proof that all voiced mimetics are unpleasant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ねばねば](ねばねば.md) emphasizes viscous, often stringy stickiness. [ぬるぬる](ぬるぬる.md) is slippery slime or grease. [さらさら](さらさら.md) can contrast with sticky skin or hair.

## Distinctive meaning

べたべたする and べたべたした手 describe a state. べたべた塗る（ぬる）or 貼る（はる）can mean applying thickly or all over, not just creating a sticky surface. With people it can sound disapproving. JMdict also records a clichéd or hackneyed sense; do not infer physical stickiness from every figurative use.
