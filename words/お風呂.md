# お風呂 (おふろ) — bath

## Meaning

お風呂 means a bath, including the bathtub or the activity of bathing as context requires. お風呂に入る (おふろにはいる) means "take a bath"; use に, not を, with 入る.

- 夜、お風呂に入ります。 (よる、おふろにはいります。) — I take a bath at night.
- お風呂のお湯が熱いです。 (おふろのおゆがあついです。) — The bathwater is hot.

## How the kanji combine

The polite prefix お attaches to ふろ, "bath." [風](../kanji/風.md) and 呂 form the conventional spelling 風呂; learn ふろ as a whole word rather than translating the characters literally.

## Synonyms and antonyms

シャワー is a shower; [あびる](あびる.md) is the verb used in シャワーをあびる. 浴室 (よくしつ) specifically means the bathroom or bathing room.

## Distinctive meaning

お風呂 does not ordinarily mean the toilet. In a typical Japanese bathing routine, people wash before soaking in the tub; the word alone does not specify the washing method.
