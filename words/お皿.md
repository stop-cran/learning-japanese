# お皿 (おさら) - plate; dish

## Meaning

お皿 is a plate or dish used for serving food. The お makes the everyday noun 皿 (さら) sound polite or pleasantly familiar; it does not change the kind of object.

お皿を二枚ください。 (おさらをにまいください。) - Please give me two plates.

ケーキをお皿にのせます。 (ケーキをおさらにのせます。) - I put the cake on a plate.

## How the kanji combine

The prefix お is written in kana. 皿 represents the native noun さら. This is a prefixed word, not a compound with a separate reading for each piece of the character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

皿 is the same basic noun without お. 食器 (しょっき) means tableware more broadly; 茶碗 (ちゃわん) is a bowl or teacup rather than a flat plate.

## Distinctive meaning

English "dish" can mean a prepared food, but お皿 normally names the serving object. Flat plates are commonly counted with 枚 (まい). に marks the destination when food is placed on the plate, as in the second example.
