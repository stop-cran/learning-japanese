# 予防 (よぼう) - prevention; precaution

## Meaning

予防 is prevention: taking measures beforehand against an unwanted event or
condition. 予防する is transitive, with を marking what is to be prevented.

- 病気を予防する方法を調べています。 (びょうきをよぼうするほうほうをしらべています。) - We are looking into ways to prevent illness.
- 故障の予防のために、機械を点検します。 (こしょうのよぼうのために、きかいをてんけんします。) - We inspect the machine to prevent breakdowns.

The noun pattern is something + の + 予防. 予防のために means "for prevention"
or "in order to prevent it."

## How the kanji combine

[予](../kanji/予.md) contributes beforehand, and 防 contributes guarding
against or preventing. The first qualifies the preventive action: guarding
against something in advance. The on readings are ヨ + ボウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[防ぐ](防ぐ.md) (ふせぐ) is the native verb "prevent; defend against."
治療 (ちりょう), "treatment," provides a useful contrast when an illness
already exists, but prevention and treatment can both occur in the same
overall course of care.

## Distinctive meaning

予防 emphasizes measures taken before the unwanted event, not repairing
damage afterward. The word itself does not guarantee that the measures work.
予防策 (よぼうさく), "preventive measures," names the measures; their
effectiveness needs separate evidence. Keep prevention distinct from a
promise that a problem cannot happen.
