# 悠々 (ゆうゆう) - leisurely; composed; easily

## Meaning

悠々 describes a leisurely, unhurried or composed manner.
It also means doing something easily, with room to spare.
悠々と is the practical adverbial pattern.

- 退職後は田舎で悠々と暮らしています。 (たいしょくごはいなかでゆうゆうとくらしています。) - After retiring, I live leisurely in the countryside.
- 今から出れば、電車に悠々と間に合います。 (いまからでれば、でんしゃにゆうゆうとまにあいます。) - If we leave now, we can easily catch the train.

Before a noun, the literary pattern is 悠々たる:
悠々たる態度 (ゆうゆうたるたいど), "a composed manner."
Do not replace たる with an ordinary na-adjective's な.

## How the kanji combine

悠 is associated with remoteness and an unhurried
extent or manner. 々 repeats the character, including its on reading
ユウ: ゆうゆう. It is not a separately pronounced kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[のんびり](のんびり.md) and [ゆっくり](ゆっくり.md) overlap in taking
one's time. [冷静](冷静.md) (れいせい) emphasizes composure;
[余裕](余裕.md) (よゆう) is leeway or room to spare.
[容易](容易.md) (ようい) overlaps in ease, but has different grammar.
慌てて (あわてて), "in a fluster," contrasts with a composed manner.

## Distinctive meaning

Leisureliness and easy achievement are different uses: catching the
train 悠々と does not mean walking slowly enough to miss it.
The dictionary also records distant, boundless or enduring extent;
this is recognition-level literary scope, not a claim that 悠々
replaces every use of "wide," "far" or "forever."
悠悠 is another spelling. 悠々 and ゆうゆう are forms of one entry,
not two N2 assignments.
