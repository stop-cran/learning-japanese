# 派手 (はで) - flashy; showy

## Meaning

派手 is a na-adjective for something conspicuously showy or flashy.
It can describe clothes and colors, or extravagant behavior.
Use 派手な before a noun and 派手に to describe an action.

- 彼は派手なシャツを着ています。 (かれははでなシャツをきています。) - He is wearing a flashy shirt.
- 二人は派手にお金を使った。 (ふたりははでにおかねをつかった。) - The two of them spent money extravagantly.

## How the kanji combine

派 has the on-reading ハ; [手](../kanji/手.md) contributes native て, voiced to で in this word.
This is a mixed reading, grouped under kango in the deck's on-first convention, not an all-on compound.
The spelling does not literally mean "faction hand"; the word's historical derivation is not established here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[地味](地味.md) (じみ), plain or subdued, is the direct contrast.
[明るい](明るい.md) (あかるい) can describe a bright color or cheerful disposition without implying flashiness.
Something can be colorful and tasteful; 派手 does not necessarily mean ugly.

## Distinctive meaning

English "loud" here means visually or behaviorally conspicuous, not automatically a high sound volume.
The evaluation depends on context: 派手 can be appreciative, neutral or critical.
Do not confuse its な-adjective grammar with an i-adjective ending merely because its English gloss is an adjective.
