# 黄色い (きいろい) — yellow

## Meaning

黄色い is an い-adjective meaning yellow. It modifies a noun directly, without な.

黄色い花が咲いています。 (きいろいはながさいています。) — Yellow flowers are blooming.

For the negative, use 黄色くない (きいろくない), "not yellow"; the past is 黄色かった (きいろかった), "was yellow."

## How the kanji combine

黄 is read き and means yellow; [色](../kanji/色.md) is いろ, "color." Together 黄色 (きいろ) is the color noun, and 黄色い is the corresponding adjective. Pronounce both adjacent い sounds in きいろい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

黄色の花 (きいろのはな) also means "yellow flowers," using the noun 黄色 plus の. 赤い (あかい), "red," and 青い (あおい), "blue," are other color adjectives rather than true antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume every Japanese color noun forms an adjective by adding い. 黄色い is an established adjective, whereas many colors normally modify nouns with の. The extended expression 黄色い声 (きいろいこえ) describes a shrill, high-pitched voice, not a literal voice color.
