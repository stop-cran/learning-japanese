# エネルギー (エネルギー) - energy

## Meaning

エネルギー means energy. It names the physical quantity in science, and
also someone's energy, strength, or drive. In resource discussions it can
refer to energy sources; in nutrition it can mean food energy.

- 太陽のエネルギーを利用しています。 (たいようのエネルギーをりようしています。) - We are using energy from the sun.
- 今日はもう料理をするエネルギーがありません。 (きょうはもうりょうりをするエネルギーがありません。) - I have no energy left to cook today.

The first concerns an energy source; the second concerns a person's remaining
capacity to do something.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword, so it has no kanji decomposition.
JMdict identifies German *Energie* as its source. Keep the long final vowel:
エネルギー. Its English meaning is familiar, but its Japanese pronunciation
should not be replaced by the pronunciation of English "energy."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[力](力.md) (ちから) can mean strength or power, but technical physical
quantities need their own terms. [元気](元気.md) (げんき) concerns health,
liveliness, or being well; it is not a replacement for solar energy.
[電気](電気.md) (でんき) is electricity, not all forms of energy.

## Distinctive meaning

食品のエネルギー (しょくひんのエネルギー) concerns the energy supplied
by food, not whether the food is lively. The surrounding subject determines
whether you are discussing physics, resources, nutrition, or personal stamina.
Do not assume the everyday overlap among "energy," "power," and "strength"
makes those terms scientifically interchangeable.
