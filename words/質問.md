# 質問 (しつもん) — question, inquiry

## Meaning

質問 is a question asked to obtain information or clarification. 質問する means "ask a question."

- 質問があります。（しつもんが あります。）— I have a question.
- 先生に質問しました。（せんせいに しつもんしました。）— I asked the teacher a question.

The person being asked commonly takes に.

## How the kanji combine

[質](../kanji/質.md) シツ has a questioning or checking sense here; [問](../kanji/問.md) モン means ask. The two reinforce the idea of inquiry.

Both are on readings. Do not apply only the familiar "quality" sense of 質 and translate this as "quality question."

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 問い とい also means a question and often appears in written prompts.
- [聞く](聞く.md) きく can mean "ask" as well as "hear"; it is a common everyday alternative when the object of the question is stated.
- 答え こたえ, "answer," is the corresponding response, not a kind of question.

## Distinctive meaning

[問題](問題.md) もんだい can mean a test question, a problem, or an issue. 質問 focuses on an inquiry directed to someone. A difficult problem is not automatically a 質問, and 質問がある normally means "I have a question," not "I am in trouble."
