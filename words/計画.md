# 計画 (けいかく) — plan; planning

## Meaning

計画 is a **plan**; 計画する means **to plan something**. The verb takes the planned activity with を. 計画を立てる (けいかくをたてる) is another useful expression, “make a plan.”

- 夏の旅行を計画しています。 (なつのりょこうをけいかくしています。) — We are planning a summer trip.
- まず計画を立てましょう。 (まずけいかくをたてましょう。) — Let's make a plan first.

With する, the polite and past forms are 計画します and 計画した.

## How the kanji combine

[計](../kanji/計.md) contributes planning or reckoning; [画](../kanji/画.md) also contributes devising or planning here. The on readings ケイ + カク give けいかく. 画 does not mean a literal picture in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

予定 (よてい) is a schedule or intended arrangement. 計画 emphasizes devising how to accomplish something. 準備 (じゅんび), “preparation,” includes practical steps as well as planning.

## Distinctive meaning

A 計画 can be detailed, revised, or cancelled. It describes an intended course of action, not a promise that the action will happen. The noun alone is not the verb “plan”: add する or use 計画を立てる.
