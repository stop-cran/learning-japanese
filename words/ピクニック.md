# ピクニック (ピクニック) - picnic

## Meaning

ピクニック is a noun for a picnic, an outdoor leisure outing associated with eating a meal outside. Useful patterns are ピクニックに行く, "go on a picnic," and ピクニックをする, "have a picnic."

- 日曜日に公園へピクニックに行きます。 (にちようびにこうえんへピクニックにいきます。) - We are going to the park for a picnic on Sunday.
- 川のそばでピクニックをしました。 (かわのそばでピクニックをしました。) - We had a picnic beside the river.

へ identifies the destination in the first example, while the activity + に states the purpose. で marks where the picnic takes place in the second.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written entirely in katakana. The small ッ in ニック marks a brief closure before ク; it is not a full-sized ツ syllable. There is no kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[散歩](散歩.md) (さんぽ) is a walk and need not involve an outdoor meal. [旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) is travel or a trip more broadly. A picnic may be part of either kind of outing, but the words do not identify the same activity.

## Distinctive meaning

The inspected dictionary also records broader "outing/hike" meanings as dated. For present-day study, the picnic context is the useful starting point; do not assume every hike is called a ピクニック. The noun does not require a particular menu or a large organized group.
