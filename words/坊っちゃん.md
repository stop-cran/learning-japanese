# 坊っちゃん (ぼっちゃん) - another person's son; sheltered young man

## Meaning

坊っちゃん can respectfully refer to another person's son, a boy, or a young master. It can also suggest a sheltered, inexperienced young man, particularly one from a comfortable family.

- お宅の坊っちゃんはおいくつですか。 (おたくのぼっちゃんはおいくつですか。) - How old is your son?
- 彼はまだ世間知らずの坊っちゃんだ。 (かれはまだせけんしらずのぼっちゃんだ。) - He is still a sheltered young man who knows little of the world.

The second use is evaluative, not simply respectful. 世間知らず (せけんしらず) means inexperienced in the ways of the world.

## How the kanji combine

坊 contributes the boy/young-master association with a ボウ base; the familiar suffix ちゃん and the contracted pronunciation give ぼっちゃん. JMdict records both 坊っちゃん and 坊ちゃん for this same reading. Do not read 坊ちゃん as ぼうちゃん merely because it lacks written っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[息子](息子.md) (むすこ) is "son"; [男の子](男の子.md) (おとこのこ) is "boy." [坊や](坊や.md) (ぼうや) is familiar and may be affectionate toward a child or belittling toward an older male.

## Distinctive meaning

The respectful and sheltered-young-man uses must be distinguished by context. 坊っちゃん is not a neutral label for every adult son, nor a formal way to elevate one's own son. This article teaches the common noun, not the separate novel title.
