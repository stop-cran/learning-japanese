# 見慣れる (みなれる) -- become used to seeing

## Meaning

見慣れる means becoming accustomed to seeing something. 見慣れている
describes the resulting visual familiarity: a face, view or written form
no longer looks unfamiliar.

- これは見慣れた景色です。 (これはみなれたけしきです。) -- This is familiar scenery.
- まだ見慣れない文字が多いです。 (まだみなれないもじがおおいです。) -- There are still many characters I am not used to seeing.

JMdict classifies it as an intransitive ichidan verb. Forms include
見慣れます, 見慣れない and 見慣れて (みなれます・みなれない・みなれて).

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) supplies み, seeing, and
[慣](../kanji/慣.md) supplies な, becoming accustomed; れる completes
the verb. 見馴れる is a recorded rare spelling. The search-only form
見なれる is not a separate word or reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [慣れる](慣れる.md) (なれる) is broader: one can get used to a job or a routine, not just its appearance.
- [見つける](見つける.md) (みつける) usually concerns finding, but also has a dictionary sense of being accustomed to seeing.
- [親しい](親しい.md) (したしい) can mean familiar as well as personally close. 見慣れる does not by itself establish friendship.
- [知る](知る.md) (しる) covers knowing or familiarity more broadly; recognizing a familiar face is not knowing everything about the person.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the seeing component in view. A familiar-looking object need not be
well understood, and getting used to seeing something does not itself
mean learning how to use it. This card does not extend the word to every
kind of knowledge or interpersonal intimacy.
