# 道 (みち) — road, way

## Meaning

道 is a road, street, path or way: 道を渡る わたる (cross the road), 道に迷う まよう (get lost), 道を聞く きく (ask the way), 帰り道 かえりみち (the way home).
The expression 人の道 (ひとのみち) means "proper human conduct; moral principles".

## How the kanji combine

[道](../kanji/道.md) alone with the kun reading みち. The on readings are used in compounds: [水道](水道.md) すいどう, 柔道 じゅうどう, 北海道 ほっかいどう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 道路 どうろ (a road, formal), 通り とおり (street), 路 (literary).
- Related: 近道 ちかみち (shortcut), 回り道 まわりみち (detour).

## Distinctive meaning

みち is the everyday word, while 道路 どうろ is the formal and legal term (道路工事 road work). The suffix どう in names of arts (茶道, 武道) means a "way" of practice.
