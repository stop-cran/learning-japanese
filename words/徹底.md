# 徹底 (てってい) - thoroughness

## Meaning

徹底 is thoroughness or completeness, and also ensuring that a policy or practice
is carried out consistently. In Nを徹底する, the object is what is thoroughly
implemented. 徹底した before a noun means "thorough."

- 安全確認を徹底してください。 (あんぜんかくにんをてっていしてください。) - Please make sure safety checks are carried out thoroughly.
- 徹底した調査が必要です。 (てっていしたちょうさがひつようです。) - A thorough investigation is necessary.

徹底的に (てっていてきに), "thoroughly," is another useful form:
徹底的に調べる means investigating something thoroughly.

## How the kanji combine

徹 contributes going through or penetrating, and 底 means bottom. Together they
suggest reaching all the way through to the bottom, a helpful image for
thoroughness rather than a requirement for physical depth.
テツ + テイ becomes テッテイ, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[完全](完全.md) (かんぜん) concerns completeness or perfection.
徹底 emphasizes thorough execution or consistent application, not simply a
finished object with no missing parts. 中途半端 (ちゅうとはんぱ), "half-done;
halfhearted," is a useful contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

徹底 can describe both the depth of an investigation and the extent to which
people follow a practice. A request for 安全確認の徹底 is not merely a request
to announce the rules: it asks for thorough implementation. Neither use says
by itself whether the chosen policy is a good one.
