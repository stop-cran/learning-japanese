# 感心 (かんしん) - admiration; being impressed

## Meaning

感心 is admiration or being impressed by something. Use 感心する with に marking what impresses you. As a na-adjective, 感心な means "admirable; praiseworthy."

- 彼女の丁寧な説明に感心しました。 (かのじょのていねいなせつめいにかんしんしました。) - I was impressed by her careful explanation.
- 毎日練習するなんて、感心ですね。 (まいにちれんしゅうするなんて、かんしんですね。) - Practicing every day is admirable.

The first example describes the speaker's reaction. The second evaluates someone's behavior positively; it can function as praise rather than merely naming a feeling.

## How the kanji combine

[感](../kanji/感.md) contributes feeling or being affected, and [心](../kanji/心.md) contributes the heart or mind. Together they suggest a response in the mind. This is a component-based memory aid, not an account of the word's historical formation. The reading is カン + シン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[感動](感動.md) (かんどう) concerns being emotionally moved. [尊敬](尊敬.md) (そんけい) is respect or esteem. You can be impressed by one clever explanation without making a broad judgment about the speaker's character.

[関心](関心.md), also かんしん, means interest or concern, not admiration.

## Distinctive meaning

感心 can sound evaluative: the speaker is judging a performance or behavior. When praising a senior colleague, a specific comment about what helped you may fit better than an unqualified 感心しました. It is not a fixed interjection that automatically translates every English "Well done!"
