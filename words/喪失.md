# 喪失 (そうしつ) — loss; forfeiture

## Meaning

喪失 means losing something or the state of having lost it. It can be used with する.

- 事故で大切なデータを喪失しました。 (じこでたいせつなでーたをそうしつしました。) — We lost important data in the accident.
- 信頼を喪失すると、関係を修復するのは難しい。 (しんらいをそうしつすると、かんけいをしゅうふくするのはむずかしい。) — Once trust is lost, it is difficult to repair the relationship.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[喪](../kanji/喪.md) is read ソウ and [失](../kanji/失.md) is read シツ. Together they form 喪失, read そうしつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

失う (うしなう) is a verb meaning to lose; 喪失 names the loss or state of having lost something.

## Distinctive meaning

喪失 names the loss or state of losing something; the word alone does not specify whether the loss was intentional.
