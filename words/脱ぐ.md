# 脱ぐ (ぬぐ) - take off clothing

## Meaning

脱ぐ is a transitive godan verb for taking off clothing or footwear. Mark the item removed with を.

- ここで靴を脱いでください。 (ここでくつをぬいでください。) - Please take off your shoes here.
- 暑かったので、上着を脱ぎました。 (あつかったので、うわぎをぬぎました。) - It was hot, so I took off my jacket.

The polite form is 脱ぎます (ぬぎます), the te-form 脱いで (ぬいで), and the past 脱いだ (ぬいだ).

## How the kanji combine

脱 writes ぬ, followed by the godan ending ぐ. The voiced ending explains why the te-form ends in で, not て.

## Synonyms and antonyms

Putting clothing on uses different verbs: 着る (きる) for a jacket or shirt, and はく for shoes or trousers. 外す (はずす) is useful for removing items such as glasses.

## Distinctive meaning

脱ぐ covers both many clothes and shoes, even though their "put on" verbs differ. It is not a general verb for removing any object from any place; learn the clothing construction rather than translating every English "take off" with it.
