# 固い (かたい) - hard; firm

## Meaning

固い is an i-adjective meaning hard, firm, or solid. It can describe physical resistance or something firmly held.

- このパンは固いです。 (このパンはかたいです。) - This bread is hard.
- ふたが固くて開きません。 (ふたがかたくてあきません。) - The lid is tight and will not open.

The negative is 固くない (かたくない); 固かった (かたかった) is the past form.

## How the kanji combine

固 writes the native adjective's かた portion, with い as okurigana. 硬い and 堅い are other spellings of かたい with overlapping uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

柔らかい (やわらかい), "soft," is the basic contrast for texture. The spelling 硬い often highlights physical hardness; 堅い appears in uses involving solidity or reliability.

## Distinctive meaning

These spelling tendencies are not absolute divisions into separate meanings. Learn established phrases individually. 固い can describe a tight lid, but it is not the general equivalent of English "difficult," as in a difficult examination.
