# 続く (つづく) - continue; last

## Meaning

続く means that something continues, lasts, or goes on. It is intransitive: the event or state that continues takes が.

雨が三日間続きました。 (あめがみっかかんつづきました。) - The rain lasted for three days.

この道は駅まで続いています。 (このみちはえきまでつづいています。) - This road continues as far as the station.

The second example extends the idea to an unbroken route.

## How the kanji combine

[続](../kanji/続.md) supplies つづ, followed by okurigana く. This is a godan verb: 続きます (つづきます), 続いて (つづいて), 続かない (つづかない). Keep the spelling づ in the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

続ける (つづける) is transitive: 勉強を続ける (べんきょうをつづける), "continue studying." 終わる (おわる), "end," contrasts with continuation.

## Distinctive meaning

続く can appear at the end of an installment as "To be continued." That English passive wording does not make 続く a Japanese passive verb. Compare 会議が続く (かいぎがつづく), "the meeting continues," with 会議を続ける (かいぎをつづける), "continue the meeting."
