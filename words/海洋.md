# 海洋 (かいよう) - ocean; sea

## Meaning

海洋 means the ocean or sea. It is useful when discussing marine environments, geography, resources and research.

- 海洋の環境を守る必要があります。 (かいようのかんきょうをまもるひつようがあります。) - We need to protect the ocean environment.
- 大学で海洋について学んでいます。 (だいがくでかいようについてまなんでいます。) - I am studying the ocean at university.

海洋生物 (かいようせいぶつ) means "marine life"; 海洋汚染 (かいようおせん) is "marine pollution."

## How the kanji combine

[海](../kanji/海.md) contributes sea and [洋](../kanji/洋.md) ocean. The closely related water meanings reinforce one another: カイ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[海](海.md) (うみ) is the everyday sea/ocean word, as in 海に行く (うみにいく), "go to the sea." [沖](沖.md) (おき) focuses on the open sea away from shore.

## Distinctive meaning

海洋 is useful in technical and geographical descriptions, but it is not a fixed name for a zone beginning a particular distance offshore. Nor does 洋 make every use refer only to the Atlantic or Pacific. For an ordinary trip to the beach, 海 is a more useful starting point.
