# 臍 (へそ) - navel; central point

## Meaning

臍 is the navel or belly button, usually written へそ. It is a noun.
By extension, へそ can name a central bump or depression, or the center or main
point of something. The dictionary also records the reading ほぞ.

- 赤ちゃんのおへそが見えています。 (あかちゃんのおへそがみえています。) - The baby's belly button is showing.
- この町は国のへそと呼ばれています。 (このまちはくにのへそとよばれています。) - This town is called the navel, or center, of the country.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 臍 represents the whole native noun; it is not an on-reading
compound to split into independently pronounced pieces.
Learn the common へそ spelling and reading alongside recognition of 臍.
The kana form does not create a second, unrelated body part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中心](中心.md) (ちゅうしん), [中央](中央.md) (ちゅうおう),
[真ん中](真ん中.md) (まんなか) and [センター](センター.md) overlap with
the center extension. [要点](要点.md) (ようてん) helps with "main point."
[おなか](おなか.md) or [腹](腹.md) (はら) is the belly or abdomen as a whole,
not a synonym for its navel.

## Distinctive meaning

The central-bump/depression sense is restricted to へそ/ヘソ in the pinned
dictionary; do not freely substitute ほぞ for it.
The idiom へそを曲げる (へそをまげる) means become sulky or contrary, not
literally bend the navel. Separate ほぞ expressions require their own interpretation;
a dictionary explanation of ほぞ as resolve or one's true feelings is not a
general meaning to attach to every へそ.
