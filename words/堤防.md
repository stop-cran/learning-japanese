# 堤防 (ていぼう) — levee; embankment

## Meaning

堤防 is a raised structure along a river or coast that confines water or helps protect nearby land.

- 堤防を歩く。 (ていぼうをあるく。) — to walk along the levee

## How the kanji combine

[堤](../kanji/堤.md) means an embankment or bank, and [防](../kanji/防.md) relates to protection. Together they name a structure that controls or holds back water.

## Synonyms and antonyms

堤防 overlaps with words for embankments and barriers, but it is not interchangeable with every wall or breakwater. 防波堤 (ぼうはてい) is specifically a breakwater or seawall.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary describes river levees and coastal embankments. The word does not imply that every embankment is built from earth or that every water barrier is a 堤防.
