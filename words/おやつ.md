# おやつ (おやつ) - between-meal snack

## Meaning

おやつ is a snack between meals. It also has the more specific mid-afternoon snack or refreshment use, associated with around three o'clock.

- 三時におやつを食べましょう。 (さんじにおやつをたべましょう。) - Let's have a snack at three.
- おやつにおにぎりを食べました。 (おやつにおにぎりをたべました。) - I had a rice ball as a snack.

おやつに marks what something is eaten as; おやつを食べる uses the snack as the object of "eat."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お菓子](お菓子.md) (おかし) and [菓子](菓子.md) (かし) name confectionery and sweets. They can be eaten as おやつ, but a snack need not be sweet or belong to that food category.

## Distinctive meaning

The afternoon use does not impose a three-o'clock rule on every snack. The word names food or refreshment between meals, not necessarily a dessert served as part of a meal. There is no useful single antonym; a regular meal gives the practical contrast.
