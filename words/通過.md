# 通過 (つうか) - passage through; passing

## Meaning

通過 is passage through or past a place. 通過する is intransitive, but the
place traversed can take を, as with other movement verbs.

- この電車は次の駅を通過します。 (このでんしゃはつぎのえきをつうかします。) - This train will pass through the next station without stopping.
- 私たちのチームは予選を通過しました。 (わたしたちのチームはよせんをつうかしました。) - Our team got through the qualifying round.

The second example is an extended use: clearing a selection, inspection,
or preliminary stage rather than physically traveling through a place.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes going through, and [過](../kanji/過.md)
contributes passing beyond. The related actions combine into passage through.
The on readings are ツウ + カ, with a long vowel in つう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通る](通る.md) (とおる) also means pass through and has broad everyday uses.
[過ぎる](過ぎる.md) (すぎる) emphasizes going past or beyond.
In a train timetable, stopping contrasts with 通過, but is not an antonym
for every figurative use.

## Distinctive meaning

The object-like を in 駅を通過する marks the route or point passed; it does
not mean that the train changes the station. 通過 can also describe a bill
passing a legislative stage. Identify what is being passed before deciding
whether the English translation should be "transit," "clear," or "pass."
