# 冴える (さえる) — be clear-headed; be vivid

## Meaning

冴える describes mental clarity or alertness, and a crisp, vivid quality
in sights, colours or sounds. Learn the whole expression with its subject:
頭が冴える (あたまがさえる) means to be clear-headed; 目が冴える
(めがさえる) means to be wide awake, often when you would rather sleep.

- 今日は頭が冴えています。
  (きょうはあたまがさえています。) — I feel clear-headed today.
- 目が冴えて眠れません。
  (めがさえてねむれません。) — I'm wide awake and can't sleep.

This is an intransitive ichidan verb: the subject takes が.
Forms include 冴えます (さえます), 冴えた (さえた), 冴えて (さえて)
and 冴えない (さえない). 冴えている can describe an existing alert state.
The examples adapt dictionary examples to polite endings.

## How the kanji combine

[冴](../kanji/冴.md) supplies the clarity meaning in this single-kanji native verb.
In the written form 冴える, 冴 represents さ and the following える completes
さえる. The conjugation stem is さえ, including the written え.
It is not a compound assembled from on readings.

JMdict also records 冱える (さえる) as a rarely used kanji spelling.
That spelling flag does not make the pronunciation さえる rare.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[覚める](覚める.md) (さめる) focuses on waking or regaining awareness:
目が覚める (めがさめる) means "wake up," whereas 目が冴える describes wakefulness.
[澄む](澄む.md) (すむ) and [透き通る](透き通る.md) (すきとおる)
overlap for clear sound, but physical transparency is not a universal meaning of 冴える.

For an opposite evaluation, 冴えない can mean "lacklustre" or "unsatisfactory,"
depending on what is being described; it is not a single antonym for every sense.

## Distinctive meaning

冴える can describe deft technique or a well-executed performance, not only
mental alertness. It does not generally mean a permanent high intelligence score,
nor does 目が冴える mean eyes lighting up with happiness.

The negative 冴えない often concerns a disappointing result, an unappealing
appearance or a subdued condition rather than literal blur.
An older, somewhat old-fashioned sense of 冴える is "become intensely cold."
For ordinary cooling, learn [冷える](冷える.md) (ひえる);
for freezing, [凍る](凍る.md) (こおる). Those verbs are not alternative
pronunciations of the 冴える taught here.
