# 画家 (がか) - painter

## Meaning

画家 is a noun for a painter or artist who creates pictures. It names a person, not the activity of painting. Use 画家です, "is a painter," and 画家になる, "become a painter."

- 彼女は風景を描く画家です。 (かのじょはふうけいをえがくがかです。) - She is a painter who paints landscapes.
- 将来、画家になりたいです。 (しょうらい、がかになりたいです。) - I want to become a painter in the future.

画家の作品 (がかのさくひん), "a painter's work," uses の to connect the creator with the work.

## How the kanji combine

[画](../kanji/画.md) means a picture or painting. [家](../kanji/家.md), read カ here, identifies a practitioner or specialist rather than a house. The relation is field plus person: someone whose field is making pictures.

## Synonyms and antonyms

芸術家 (げいじゅつか), "artist," is broader and can include artists working in other media. [絵](絵.md) (え) is the picture itself. Neither is an opposite of 画家; they name a wider category and the resulting work, respectively.

## Distinctive meaning

English "painter" can also mean someone hired to paint walls. 画家 instead points to artistic picture-making. Conversely, calling someone a 画家 does not specify whether they use oil paint, watercolor, or another medium. Add that information separately when it matters.
