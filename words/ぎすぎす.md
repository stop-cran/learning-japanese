# ぎすぎす — tense and unfriendly; strained

## Meaning

ぎすぎす describes a cold, tense atmosphere or unfriendly manner.

- 意見がぶつかり、会議室の雰囲気がぎすぎすしていた。
  — The disagreement made the atmosphere in the meeting room tense.
- 忙しさで余裕がなくなり、会話までぎすぎすしてきた。
  — With everyone under pressure, even the conversations became strained.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぎすぎす emphasizes a cold or edgy atmosphere. It does not necessarily mean that a particular
pair of people has a specific unresolved misunderstanding.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records a thin, bony appearance. The examples here focus on social atmosphere and
interaction, not body shape.
