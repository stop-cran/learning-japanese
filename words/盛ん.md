# 盛ん (さかん) - thriving; active

## Meaning

盛ん describes vigorous activity, a flourishing industry, or something widely practiced. It is a na-adjective: 盛んな地域 (さかんなちいき), "an area where it flourishes." The activity must be clear from context.

- この町では野球が盛んです。 (このまちではやきゅうがさかんです。) - Baseball is widely played in this town.
- この地方は農業が盛んです。 (このちほうはのうぎょうがさかんです。) - Agriculture thrives in this region.

Use 盛んに (さかんに) to describe an action being done actively or repeatedly.

## How the kanji combine

盛 is associated with flourishing or abundance. 盛ん is read さかん as a native word; the final ん does not make it a verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

人気がある (にんきがある) means "be popular; be well liked." It overlaps in some contexts, but popularity alone does not express the sustained activity suggested by 盛ん.

## Distinctive meaning

盛ん describes a state; it is not a noun meaning "popularity." Use 野球が盛ん, not a literal noun-for-noun translation of "baseball's popularity." The focus is how actively something takes place.
