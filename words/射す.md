# 射す (さす) - shine in; shine onto

## Meaning

射す describes light shining into or onto a place.
It is an intransitive godan verb: the light is the subject, not an object someone is shooting.

- 窓から朝日が射してきました。 (まどからあさひがさしてきました。) - Morning sunlight began to stream through the window.
- 庭に夕日が射しています。 (にわにゆうひがさしています。) - The evening sun is shining onto the garden.

Forms include 射します, 射して, 射した, and 射さない.
から marks where the light comes through; に can identify the place it reaches.

## How the kanji combine

射 has a shining-into association as well as shooting-related uses in other words.
Here its native reading is さ, followed by す.
Do not read this verb as いる or infer its sentence pattern from English "shoot."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[差す](差す.md), also さす, supports the same sunlight construction; its umbrella use is separate.
[光る](光る.md) (ひかる) and [輝く](輝く.md) (かがやく) describe shining or brilliance more broadly.
[照らす](照らす.md) (てらす) is transitive: 月が海を照らす (つきがうみをてらす), "the moon illuminates the sea."

## Distinctive meaning

The legacy gloss "strike" can refer to light reaching a surface; it does not give this verb a general hitting sense.
Nor is 射す a generic verb for firing a weapon.
The examples describe the arrival or direction of light, rather than declaring that the garden itself emits light.
