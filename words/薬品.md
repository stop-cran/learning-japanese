# 薬品 (やくひん) - medicines; chemical agents

## Meaning

薬品 can mean medicinal drugs or chemical agents. Context determines whether the substances are medicines, laboratory chemicals, or other chemicals used for a purpose.

- 実験で使う薬品の名前を確認しました。
  (じっけんでつかうやくひんのなまえをかくにんしました。) - I checked the names of the chemicals used in the experiment.
- この棚には薬品が保管されています。
  (このたなにはやくひんがほかんされています。) - Chemicals are stored on this shelf.

薬品の取り扱い (やくひんのとりあつかい) means the handling of medicines or chemicals, as the setting requires.

## How the kanji combine

[薬](../kanji/薬.md) contributes drugs or chemical agents, and [品](../kanji/品.md) articles or products. The on readings are ヤク + ヒン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[薬](薬.md) (くすり) is the everyday word for medicine. 薬品 also has the chemical-agent sense, so not every 薬品 is something to take as medicine.

[物質](物質.md) (ぶっしつ), substance or matter, and [製品](製品.md) (せいひん), manufactured product, are broader categories rather than exact replacements. [化学](化学.md) (かがく) is chemistry, the field of study, not a chemical agent.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer safe consumption, a medical purpose, or a particular hazard just from this word. The medicinal and nonmedicinal senses must be distinguished from context.

The plural-looking English gloss does not impose a Japanese plural form: 薬品 can refer to one relevant agent or to chemicals collectively.
