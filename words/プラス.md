# プラス (プラス) - plus; positive factor

## Meaning

プラス names addition, a plus sign, or a positive value. It also describes something beneficial:
Nにとってプラスになる means "be a positive factor for N."
With する, AにBをプラスする means "add B to A."

- 二プラス三は五です。 (にプラスさんはごです。) - Two plus three is five.
- この経験は将来きっとプラスになります。 (このけいけんはしょうらいきっとプラスになります。) - This experience will surely be an asset in the future.

For a positive number, プラスの数 (プラスのかず) uses の before the noun.
The written symbol + is also called プラス.

## How the kanji combine

プラス is a katakana loanword, with no kanji to divide.
The small circle on プ distinguishes it from ブ.
Its mathematical and beneficial senses belong to the same dictionary entry;
the context tells you whether something is being added numerically or evaluated positively.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[足す](足す.md) (たす), "add," is a useful verb comparison for the arithmetic sense.
マイナス, "minus; negative factor," supplies the contrasting direction.
That contrast can concern numbers, gains, or benefits rather than someone's personality.

## Distinctive meaning

プラスになる does not necessarily mean receiving money: gaining useful experience can be a benefit.
Conversely, a positive number is mathematically above zero, not automatically a good result.
Keep the arithmetic sign, the adding action, and the favorable evaluation distinct when translating,
even though English "plus" conveniently overlaps with all three.
