# 曇る (くもる) - become cloudy or fogged

## Meaning

曇る is an intransitive godan verb. It describes the sky clouding over or a surface becoming foggy and less clear. The affected thing takes が.

- 午後から空が曇りました。 (ごごからそらがくもりました。) - The sky clouded over in the afternoon.
- 眼鏡が曇っています。 (めがねがくもっています。) - My glasses are fogged up.

Forms include 曇ります, 曇った, and 曇らない (くもります、くもった、くもらない).

## How the kanji combine

曇 supplies くも, and る completes the written word. Because this is godan, the polite form is くもります, not くもます; the kana boundary is not an ichidan stem rule.

## Synonyms and antonyms

晴れる (はれる), "clear up," contrasts with 曇る for weather. 曇り (くもり) is the noun used for cloudy weather, including in forecasts.

## Distinctive meaning

曇る does not mean that rain must fall. It can describe lost clarity without bad weather at all, as when warm moisture fogs a lens. Choose the English translation according to what is becoming obscured.
