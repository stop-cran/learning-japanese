# キログラム — kilogram

## Meaning

キログラム is the metric kilogram, equal to one thousand grams. In everyday conversation it measures weight, such as the weight of food or luggage. It follows a number directly.

米を二キログラム買いました。 (こめをにキログラムかいました。) — I bought two kilograms of rice.

この荷物は五キログラムです。 (このにもつはごキログラムです。) — This luggage weighs five kilograms.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is katakana. JMdict identifies a French source for the borrowing. キロ corresponds to "kilo-" and グラム to "gram"; the rare kanji 瓩 is not necessary for everyday writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

グラム is the smaller unit. キロ is a common shortening when the unit is clear, but it can also shorten キロメートル in a distance context.

## Distinctive meaning

The symbol is kg. Japanese does not add a plural ending after a number. 二キログラムの米 means "two kilograms of rice"; 米を二キログラム買う places the measured quantity with the buying action. Context must distinguish weight from distance when only キロ is used.
