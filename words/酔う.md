# 酔う (よう) - become intoxicated; get motion sick

## Meaning

酔う is an intransitive godan verb for becoming intoxicated.
It also means feeling sick from motion, such as in a vehicle.
The cause can take に: 酒に酔う, "get drunk on alcohol,"
or 車に酔う, "get carsick."

- 少し飲んだだけで酔いました。 (すこしのんだだけでよいました。) - I became tipsy after drinking just a little.
- 私はバスに酔いやすいです。 (わたしはバスによいやすいです。) - I get motion sick easily on buses.

The forms are 酔います (よいます), 酔った (よった),
酔って (よって), and 酔わない (よわない).
The negative uses わ, not お.

## How the kanji combine

酔 supplies the idea of intoxication. The native verb is よう,
with う as its kana ending. The same word extends to motion
sickness; it is not restricted to alcohol-related situations.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飲む](飲む.md) (のむ) is the action "drink."
Drinking [酒](酒.md) (さけ) can lead to 酔う, but the two
verbs do not describe the same action or guarantee the same result.
酔いがさめる (よいがさめる), "sober up," reverses the
intoxicated state.

## Distinctive meaning

酔っている can describe being drunk now, rather than actively
drinking at that moment. The cause matters: バスに酔った
reports motion sickness, not drinking alcohol on a bus.
