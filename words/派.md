# 派 (は) - faction; school of thought

## Meaning

派 is a group, faction, school or sect. It can be a noun or follow another expression to identify a camp or school.

- その政党には二つの派があります。 (そのせいとうにはふたつのはがあります。) - That political party has two factions.
- この二人の画家は同じ派に属しています。 (このふたりのがかはおなじはにぞくしています。) - These two painters belong to the same school.

The second example concerns an artistic school, not the building where someone studies.

## How the kanji combine

[派](../kanji/派.md) is a single character using its on-reading ハ. It supplies the group or school idea itself; there is no compound to split.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[グループ](グループ.md) is a broader group of people and can be temporary. A 派 often identifies a shared position, tradition or affiliation. This is a tendency, not a requirement that every 派 be a formal organization.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every "school" as 派: an ordinary educational institution is 学校 (がっこう). Conversely, a 派 need not be a hostile political faction; art and religion also have schools or sects.
