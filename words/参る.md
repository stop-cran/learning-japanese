# 参る (まいる) — to go or come humbly

## Meaning

参る is a humble or deferential word for going or coming, commonly used as 参ります in formal conversation and service language. It is a godan verb: 参ります, 参らない, 参って.

- 明日、そちらに参ります。 (あした、そちらにまいります。) — I will come to your place tomorrow.
- 私は毎朝、電車で会社に参ります。 (わたしはまいあさ、でんしゃでかいしゃにまいります。) — I go to work by train every morning. (formal)

## How the kanji combine

[参](../kanji/参.md) supplies まい, with る as okurigana. Kana まいる is also used. Do not confuse the spelling boundary with the conjugation class.

## Synonyms and antonyms

行く (いく) and 来る (くる) are the ordinary verbs. いらっしゃる respectfully describes another person's going, coming, or presence; it is not the humble equivalent.

## Distinctive meaning

参る generally lowers or presents the speaker's own side deferentially; it does not honor the subject. Use it according to whose action you are describing. Separate expressions such as 参った (まいった), "I give up; I am beaten," have different meanings and are not formal statements about travel.
