# 威嚇 (いかく) — threat; intimidation

## Meaning

威嚇 is threatening or intimidating someone by displaying force. It is a noun and is also used with する.

- 犬は牙をむいて相手を威嚇した。 (いぬはきばをむいてあいてをいかくした。) — The dog bared its teeth to intimidate the other.
- 「足元注意」は危険を知らせる警告で、人を怖がらせるための威嚇とは違う。 (あしもとちゅういはきけんをしらせるけいこくで、ひとをこわがらせるためのいかくとはちがう。) — “Watch your step” warns of danger; it is not intimidation meant to frighten people.

## How the kanji combine

[威](../kanji/威.md) is associated with force or authority that inspires fear, and [嚇](../kanji/嚇.md) with threatening or frightening. Together they form the established word 威嚇, intimidation by a display of force; learn it as a whole rather than as a fixed one-character-per-sense formula.

## Synonyms and antonyms

威嚇 overlaps with expressions for threatening or intimidating. A warning can inform or protect without trying to frighten; reassurance is a contextual contrast, not a fixed lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

威嚇 is intentionally intimidating through a display of force; it does not mean every warning.
