# 威嚇 (いかく) — threat; intimidation

## Meaning

威嚇 is threatening or intimidating someone by displaying force. It is a noun and is also used with する.

- 威嚇する。 (いかくする。) — to threaten; intimidate

## How the kanji combine

[威](../kanji/威.md) and [嚇](../kanji/嚇.md) both occur in the word, but the dictionary defines 威嚇 as a whole. Do not divide its meaning into a fixed one-character-per-sense formula.

## Synonyms and antonyms

威嚇 overlaps with expressions for threatening or intimidating. A warning can inform or protect without trying to frighten; reassurance is a contextual contrast, not a fixed lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

威嚇 is intentionally intimidating through a display of force; it does not mean every warning.
