# 戦争 (せんそう) — war

## Meaning

戦争 means war. It is a noun: 戦争が始まる (せんそうがはじまる) means "a war begins,"
and 戦争が終わる (せんそうがおわる) means "a war ends."
It can also take する to form a verb meaning to wage or engage in war.

- 私たちは学校で戦争の歴史を学びました。 (わたしたちはがっこうでせんそうのれきしをまなびました。) — We studied the history of war at school.

In 戦争の歴史, の connects "war" to "history"; 戦争 is not an adjective by itself.

## How the kanji combine

[戦](../kanji/戦.md) concerns fighting or battle, and [争](../kanji/争.md) concerns contention or dispute.
The related meanings reinforce one another in this established word for war.
The on readings combine as セン + ソウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[平和](平和.md) (へいわ), "peace," is the useful opposing concept.
争う (あらそう) is broader: it can mean competing or arguing without referring to war.
Two people disputing an answer are not, just because they 争う, engaged in a 戦争.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the whole word's meaning rather than adding up "fight" and "dispute" into any kind of disagreement.
The focus here is literal war; it does not teach every figurative use of the word.
For historical descriptions, the noun can name the event without identifying a particular participant as the subject.
