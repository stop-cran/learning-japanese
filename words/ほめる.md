# ほめる (ほめる) — praise; compliment

## Meaning

ほめる means **to praise or compliment** someone or something. The person or quality praised can take を.

- 先生は学生をほめました。 (せんせいはがくせいをほめました。) — The teacher praised the student.
- 友達が私の料理をほめてくれました。 (ともだちがわたしのりょうりをほめてくれました。) — My friend complimented my cooking.

It is an ichidan verb: ほめます, ほめない, ほめた. The passive is ほめられる, “be praised.”

## How the kanji combine

This page uses kana. The usual kanji form is 褒める, and 誉める is another recognized spelling. The native word is the same; no two-kanji compound needs to be decomposed.

## Synonyms and antonyms

認める (みとめる), “recognize/acknowledge,” does not always involve expressing praise. 叱る (しかる), “scold,” is a useful contrast when responding to someone's behavior, though praise and scolding are not exhaustive alternatives.

## Distinctive meaning

ほめる involves positive evaluation, often expressed to or about the person. Merely enjoying a meal is not the same as praising the cooking. With ほめてくれる, the speaker presents the compliment as welcome or beneficial.
