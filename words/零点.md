# 零点 (れいてん) - zero marks; mathematical zero

## Meaning

零点 means zero marks or a score of zero.
In mathematics, it also names a zero of a function: an input at which
the function's value is zero. Another dictionary sense is zero degrees
Celsius, the freezing point of water.

- テストで零点を取ってしまいました。 (テストでれいてんをとってしまいました。) - I ended up getting zero on the test.
- この関数の零点を求めます。 (このかんすうのれいてんをもとめます。) - We find the zeros of this function.

The context distinguishes a score from a mathematical zero;
the noun is not always an assessment of someone's performance.

## How the kanji combine

[零](../kanji/零.md), zero, qualifies [点](../kanji/点.md),
points or a point. The on readings are レイ + テン.
In the mathematical sense, learn the whole term rather than translating
it as an arbitrary dot labelled zero.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[零](零.md) (れい) and [ゼロ](ゼロ.md) mean zero more generally.
[点数](点数.md) (てんすう) is a score, not specifically zero.
[満点](満点.md) (まんてん), full marks, contrasts with the score use.

## Distinctive meaning

零点五 (れいてんご), "0.5," contains 零 plus the decimal-point
reading 点 plus 五; the written sequence 零点 there does not mean
"zero marks." Likewise, a function's 零点 is not its decimal point.
The temperature sense specifically concerns the Celsius zero, not
the zero of every possible temperature scale.
