# 支える (ささえる) - support; hold up

## Meaning

支える, read ささえる, is a transitive ichidan verb for supporting, holding up, or sustaining something. Mark the supported thing or person with **を**. The support may be physical, financial, or emotional.

- この柱が屋根を支えています。 (このはしらがやねをささえています。) - This pillar supports the roof.
- 家族が私を支えてくれました。 (かぞくがわたしをささえてくれました。) - My family supported me.

The past is 支えた (ささえた), and the negative is 支えない (ささえない). In the second example, くれました presents the support as something received with appreciation.

## How the kanji combine

[支](../kanji/支.md) supplies the support idea. In the spelling 支える, 支 is read ささ and える is written in kana. The ichidan stem used for conjugation is 支え (ささえ), formed by removing only the final る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[助ける](助ける.md) (たすける) broadly means helping or rescuing. 支える highlights what keeps a person, structure, or activity from failing or collapsing. [援助](援助.md) (えんじょ) names assistance and can overlap in material support.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read this support verb as "get blocked" or "choke." The separate verb つかえる means "get stuck" and is normally written in kana; its dictionary also lists 支える as a rare spelling. Shared written forms do not merge ささえる and つかえる into one reading or construction.
