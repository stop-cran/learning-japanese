# 災難 (さいなん) - calamity; misfortune

## Meaning

災難 is a calamity, misfortune, or adverse event that befalls someone. A useful construction is 災難に遭う (さいなんにあう), "suffer a misfortune."

- 旅行中に災難に遭いました。 (りょこうちゅうにさいなんにあいました。) - I suffered a misfortune during the trip.
- 財布をなくしたんですか。それは災難でしたね。 (さいふをなくしたんですか。それはさいなんでしたね。) - You lost your wallet? That was unfortunate.

The word can concern a personal mishap as well as a serious disaster. Context supplies its scale.

## How the kanji combine

災 contributes calamity or disaster, while [難](../kanji/難.md) contributes difficulty or trouble. The related negative associations reinforce the idea of misfortune. The on readings are サイ + ナン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不幸](不幸.md) (ふこう) can mean misfortune as well as unhappiness. [事故](事故.md) (じこ) can be an accident that causes a 災難, but not every misfortune is an accident. [悲劇](悲劇.md) (ひげき) can describe a real-life calamity and also has a theatrical meaning.

## Distinctive meaning

災難 does not identify a particular natural hazard or prove that someone is to blame. A response such as 災難でしたね acknowledges someone's unfortunate experience; it does not classify the incident for legal or insurance purposes.
