# 宝 (たから) - treasure

## Meaning

宝 is treasure: something of great value, whether material or personally precious. It is a noun. **私の宝** (わたしのたから) means "my treasure," and 宝を探す means to search for treasure.

- 島で宝を探しました。 (しまでたからをさがしました。) - We searched for treasure on the island.
- 家族は私の宝です。 (かぞくはわたしのたからです。) - My family is my treasure.

The first example permits a literal treasure such as valuables; the second expresses how deeply the speaker values their family.

## How the kanji combine

宝 is a single kanji using the native reading たから. No kana ending is needed. The complete noun supplies the treasure meaning; there is no multi-character compound to split.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お金](お金.md) (おかね) names money, which can be part of a treasure but does not cover everything treasured. [大切](大切.md) (たいせつ) describes something as important or precious; it is not a substitute noun in 宝を探す.

## Distinctive meaning

A 宝 need not have a high selling price. A memory, possession, or person can be treasured for personal reasons. 宝物 (たからもの), "treasured object; treasure," makes the thing being treasured explicit. Neither word guarantees a hidden chest or gold: that image depends on the story or situation.
