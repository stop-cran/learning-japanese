# 真似 (まね) - imitation; behavior

## Meaning

真似 is imitation or copying. **Nの真似をする** means to imitate N. 真似する also functions as a verb, as in 人を真似する, "imitate someone."

- 子供が先生の真似をしています。 (こどもがせんせいのまねをしています。) - The child is imitating the teacher.
- そんな危ない真似はしないでください。 (そんなあぶないまねはしないでください。) - Please do not do anything that dangerous.

In the second example, 真似 means an action or behavior; it does not require a person whose behavior is being copied.

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md) contributes an association with what is real, and [似](../kanji/似.md) with resemblance. These associations can help remember the spelling, but they do not derive the word historically. Learn the native word まね as a whole, not as シン + ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[似る](似る.md) (にる) means to resemble: two people can resemble each other without either imitating the other. 模倣 (もほう) also means imitation. Original creation can contrast with copying, depending on the activity.

## Distinctive meaning

The copying sense is not inherently criticism: imitation can be a learning method or entertainment. The behavior sense often appears when evaluating a particular action, as 危ない does above. Do not translate every 真似 as "pretense"; the construction may simply describe copying or conduct.
