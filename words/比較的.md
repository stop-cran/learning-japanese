# 比較的 (ひかくてき) - comparatively; relatively

## Meaning

比較的 is an adverb meaning comparatively or relatively.
It judges something against other things of its kind or a general standard,
which may be understood rather than stated.

- この店は比較的安いです。 (このみせはひかくてきやすいです。) - This shop is relatively inexpensive.
- 今日は道が比較的すいています。 (きょうはみちがひかくてきすいています。) - The roads are relatively clear today.

The first does not promise the lowest prices anywhere.
比較的 can go directly before the description; no に is needed in
these examples.

## How the kanji combine

[比](../kanji/比.md) and 較 form [比較](比較.md) (ひかく),
comparison. 的 adds a relation or manner element here, giving the
established adverb ひかくてき. The on readings are ヒ + カク + テキ.
Not every word ending in 的 has the same grammatical behavior.

## Synonyms and antonyms

割合 (わりあい), 割に (わりに), and 割と (わりと) also have
comparative adverbial uses. [比べる](比べる.md) (くらべる)
is the verb "compare."
[寧ろ](寧ろ.md) (むしろ) selects an alternative description;
it is not simply another degree adverb.

## Distinctive meaning

比較的 does not by definition mean "slightly" or "a little."
It supplies a relative assessment, not a fixed small quantity or percentage.
In 比較的簡単です (ひかくてきかんたんです), "It is relatively easy,"
the comparison standard still matters; the task need not be easy for everyone.
