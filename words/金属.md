# 金属 (きんぞく) - metal

## Meaning

金属 is the noun for metal as a material or class of materials. Use 金属の before a noun, or 金属製 (きんぞくせい) to mean "made of metal." It does not take な in the ordinary phrase "a metal box."

- この箱は金属でできています。 (このはこはきんぞくでできています。) - This box is made of metal.
- 金属製のスプーンを使っています。 (きんぞくせいのスプーンをつかっています。) - I use a metal spoon.

Here で marks the material. 金属を使う (きんぞくをつかう) means "use metal."

## How the kanji combine

[金](../kanji/金.md) contributes metal, not money in this compound. 属 contributes belonging to a class or category. Together they name the category of metallic materials; this is a component-based meaning aid, not an account of the word's historical creation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鉄](鉄.md) (てつ), iron, and [銀](銀.md) (ぎん), silver, are particular metals, not synonyms for the entire category. 非金属 (ひきんぞく), "nonmetal," is a category contrast. Plastic and wood are other materials, not individual lexical antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

金属 does not tell you which metal something contains, how valuable it is, or whether it is solid metal throughout. For a practical description, add the specific material when known. The reading is きんぞく; do not read 金 as かね inside this word.
