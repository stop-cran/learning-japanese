# 初旬 (しょじゅん) - first ten days of a month

## Meaning

初旬 means the first ten days of a month: the 1st through the 10th,
inclusive. A month can precede it directly.

- 四月初旬に桜が咲きました。 (しがつしょじゅんにさくらがさきました。) - The cherry blossoms bloomed in early April.
- 来月の初旬に出発します。 (らいげつのしょじゅんにしゅっぱつします。) - I will leave in the first ten days of next month.

The second sentence identifies a period, not the precise departure day.

## How the kanji combine

[初](../kanji/初.md) contributes the beginning, and 旬 a ten-day period
here. Together they identify the month's first such period.
The on readings are ショ + ジュン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上旬](上旬.md) (じょうじゅん) means the same calendar period.
中旬 (ちゅうじゅん) is the 11th through the 20th;
[下旬](下旬.md) (げじゅん) runs from the 21st to the month's last day.
[初め](初め.md) (はじめ) means beginning without that exact date range.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not calculate the interval by dividing a month's length by three.
February's 初旬 still ends on the 10th. 初旬 is a month division, not
a general label for the first ten days of any event.
