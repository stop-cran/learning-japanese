# 歩道 (ほどう) - sidewalk; footpath

## Meaning

歩道 is a sidewalk, pavement, or walkway for pedestrians. It is a noun naming a place to walk.

- 歩道を歩いてください。 (ほどうをあるいてください。) - Please walk on the sidewalk.
- この道には歩道がありません。 (このみちにはほどうがありません。) - This road has no sidewalk.

In the first sentence, を marks the route traversed, not an object changed by the walker. に in the second sentence identifies where a sidewalk does not exist.

## How the kanji combine

[歩](../kanji/歩.md) contributes walking, and [道](../kanji/道.md) contributes a road or path. The first specifies the use of the second: "walking path." The on readings are ホ + ドウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

車道 (しゃどう) is the part of a road used by vehicles, a useful contrast in street descriptions. [道](道.md) (みち) is broader and does not itself tell you whether a separate pedestrian path exists.

## Distinctive meaning

English "pavement" can mean a hard road surface generally, but 歩道 concerns the pedestrian walkway, not the material used to pave a road. The word alone does not settle all local traffic rules or whether bicycles are permitted on a particular section; signs and local regulations provide that information.
