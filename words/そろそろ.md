# そろそろ - about time; slowly and carefully

## Meaning

そろそろ often indicates that an appropriate or expected time is approaching: "soon," "any time now," or "it is about time."

そろそろ帰りましょう。 (そろそろかえりましょう。) - It is about time we went home.

It also describes slow, quiet, careful movement, often with と.

暗い廊下をそろそろと歩きました。 (くらいろうかをそろそろとあるきました。) - I walked gingerly along the dark corridor.

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana-written mimetic adverb with a repeated sound pattern. There are no written kanji to divide. The repetition belongs to the word, not to a grammatical plural.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もうすぐ means "very soon." ゆっくり means "slowly; without hurry." だんだん more directly describes a gradual change, such as something becoming warmer.

## Distinctive meaning

The two main uses must be distinguished in context: そろそろ帰る usually concerns when to leave, not walking home slowly. In a suggestion, it can soften the transition to a new activity; in other contexts, "about time" can convey impatience. It does not give an exact deadline.
