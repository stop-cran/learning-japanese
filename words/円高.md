# 円高 (えんだか) — a strong (high-valued) yen

## Meaning

円高 means the yen has risen against other currencies: 円高が進む (the yen continues to strengthen), 円高ドル安 えんだかドルやす (strong yen, weak dollar).
It is good for importers and travellers abroad, and bad for exporters.

## How the kanji combine

[円](../kanji/円.md) (yen) plus 高 (high, expensive, see [高](../kanji/高.md)): "high yen". 高 is read だか here: the kun reading たか is voiced to だか by rendaku (た→だ). So エン + たか is a mixed on-kun compound (jūbakoyomi); the format has no separate type for this, so it is filed under `kango` as the closest allowed value.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: 円安 えんやす (weak yen).
- Related: 為替 かわせ (exchange rate).

## Distinctive meaning

円高 describes the yen's value, not prices: 円高 does not mean high prices in Japan.
