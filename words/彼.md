# 彼 (かれ) — he; him

## Meaning

彼 refers to a male person as "he" or "him." Japanese does not change this word between subject and
object forms; particles show its job in the sentence.

- 彼は学生です。 (かれはがくせいです。) — He is a student.
- 昨日、彼に会いました。 (きのう、かれにあいました。) — I met him yesterday.

In the first example は marks the topic. In the second, に marks the person met.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [彼](../kanji/彼.md) writes the native word かれ. There is no compound to split.
Learn the whole pronoun, rather than trying to derive its use from the character's visible components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

あの人 (あのひと), "that person," can identify someone without the male reference of 彼.
彼女 (かのじょ), "she; her," is the corresponding female pronoun, not an antonym.
Which expression identifies the person clearly depends on the conversation.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not insert 彼 every time English supplies "he": when the person is already clear, a Japanese sentence
can leave them unstated. Also check context: JMdict records a separate noun sense "boyfriend."
The two examples here use the pronoun sense, not that relationship label.
