# そこで (そこで) - so; thereupon

## Meaning

そこで is a conjunction linking a situation to what happens or is done in response:
"so," "thereupon," or "accordingly." It can begin a new sentence without attaching to a noun
or conjugating the preceding verb.

- バスが来ませんでした。そこで、歩いて帰りました。 (バスがきませんでした。そこで、あるいてかえりました。) - The bus did not come. So I walked home.
- 説明だけでは分かりにくい。そこで、例を見てみよう。 (せつめいだけではわかりにくい。そこで、れいをみてみよう。) - The explanation alone is hard to understand. So let's look at an example.

The first situation motivates the following action. The second uses the same connection to organize
an explanation.

## How the kanji combine

The conjunction is usually written in kana; 其処で is a rare kanji spelling recorded by the dictionary.
There are no written kanji to decompose on this card.
Distinguish the conjunction from the ordinary location phrase [そこ](そこ.md) + で, "at that place."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[それで](それで.md) can connect a cause with its result.
[だから](だから.md) also gives a reason-based conclusion.
そこで is particularly useful when introducing a response to the situation just established;
these expressions are not interchangeable in every logical or conversational connection.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare そこで休みました, which may mean "I rested there" in a location context,
with バスが来ない。そこで、歩くことにした, "The bus isn't coming, so I decided to walk."
The written sequence alone does not settle whether it names a place or connects two developments.
