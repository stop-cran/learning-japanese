# 平凡 (へいぼん) - ordinary; unremarkable

## Meaning

平凡 describes something ordinary, commonplace or not especially remarkable.
It can suggest mediocrity, but an ordinary, uneventful life can also be valued.
Use 平凡だ/です, 平凡な + noun, and 平凡に when describing a manner of living.

- 彼は平凡な毎日を大切にしています。 (かれはへいぼんなまいにちをたいせつにしています。) - He values his ordinary everyday life.
- この作品の結末は少し平凡でした。 (このさくひんのけつまつはすこしへいぼんでした。) - The ending of this work was a little unremarkable.

## How the kanji combine

[平](../kanji/平.md) has an ordinary or level association here; 凡 (ボン)
contributes common or ordinary. The overlapping contributions reinforce the idea
of not standing out: ヘイ + ボン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[普通](普通.md) (ふつう), [一般](一般.md) (いっぱん) and [あたりまえ](あたりまえ.md)
overlap in ordinary or usual status. [地味](地味.md) (じみ) emphasizes being
plain or unshowy. [つまらない](つまらない.md) can be a stronger negative judgment
of interest or value. 非凡 (ひぼん), "exceptional," and [特別](特別.md)
(とくべつ), "special," provide useful contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

平凡 does not automatically mean boring, worthless or morally bad. Decide whether
the speaker welcomes an uneventful situation or criticizes a lack of originality.
平凡な人 (へいぼんなひと) evaluates how remarkable someone seems; it is not a
technical social rank or a statement that everyone has identical abilities.
