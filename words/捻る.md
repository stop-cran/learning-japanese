# 捻る (ひねる) - twist; sprain; devise

## Meaning

捻る, usually written ひねる, is a transitive godan verb.
It can mean twisting or turning something, or twisting and injuring a body part.

- 蛇口をひねりました。 (じゃぐちをひねりました。) - I turned the tap.
- 階段で足首をひねりました。 (かいだんであしくびをひねりました。) - I twisted my ankle on the stairs.

It also covers making something ingenious or intricate, including
composing a poem with effort. 頭をひねる (あたまをひねる) means to rack
one's brains: 頭をひねって答えを考えました
(あたまをひねってこたえをかんがえました), "I racked my brains to work out the answer."
Learn that whole expression, not an unrestricted replacement for "think."

## How the kanji combine

捻 is a single character writing the native verb ひねる.
Its hand component is a useful association with a manual action, not
evidence for a historical derivation of every figurative meaning.
Forms include ひねります, ひねった and ひねらない.
Despite ending in る, this is not ichidan.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[捩る](捩る.md) (ねじる) overlaps in physical twisting.
[回す](回す.md) (まわす) overlaps in turning something.
The shared kanji spelling 捻る can also represent the separate reading
ねじる; it does not make every injury or idiom equally natural with both readings.

## Distinctive meaning

The family additionally records wringing or strangling an animal,
defeating someone easily, and a dated money-gift use.
These are recognition senses, not instructions or models for unrestricted
modern collocations. Nor does the poem sense mean that every ordinary
"make" or "write" should be translated as ひねる.
