# 銃 (じゅう) - gun; firearm

## Meaning

銃 is a noun for a gun or firearm, including such types as rifles and pistols. **銃を持つ** (じゅうをもつ) means "carry/have a gun"; it does not itself say that the gun was used.

- 博物館に古い銃が展示されています。 (はくぶつかんにふるいじゅうがてんじされています。) - Old firearms are on display at the museum.
- 警察が銃を発見しました。 (けいさつがじゅうをはっけんしました。) - The police found a gun.

The second example reports discovery. It does not establish who owned the object or what happened before it was found.

## How the kanji combine

銃 is one character with the on reading じゅう. Its metal component can help identify the spelling. There is no compound to divide, and the long vowel is part of the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[武器](武器.md) (ぶき), "weapon," is broader than 銃. A gun is one kind of weapon; not every weapon is a gun. 拳銃 (けんじゅう) specifies a handgun, narrowing the type.

## Distinctive meaning

じゅう is also the pronunciation of 十, "ten," so context matters in speech. 銃 names the object, not a general act of violence. Read the accompanying verb carefully: finding, carrying, displaying, and using an object describe different events.
