# 海外 (かいがい) - overseas; abroad

## Meaning

海外 is a noun meaning overseas or abroad. Use **海外に／へ行く** for movement abroad,
**海外で** for an activity abroad, and **海外の + noun** for something associated with another country.

- 姉は海外で働いています。 (あねはかいがいではたらいています。) - My older sister works abroad.
- 海外の友人に手紙を送りました。 (かいがいのゆうじんにてがみをおくりました。) - I sent a letter to a friend overseas.

海外旅行 (かいがいりょこう), "overseas travel," is a direct compound without の.
It names the travel category, rather than a particular foreign country.

## How the kanji combine

[海](../kanji/海.md), "sea," combines with [外](../kanji/外.md), "outside."
The whole compound denotes the area abroad, associating it with being beyond the sea.
Its on readings are カイ + ガイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[外国](外国.md) (がいこく) means a foreign country; 海外 presents things from an abroad-versus-home perspective.
[国内](国内.md) (こくない), "within the country/domestic," is the practical contrast.
国内旅行 and 海外旅行 distinguish domestic and overseas trips.

## Distinctive meaning

The reference country comes from context. In Japanese discussions it is often Japan,
but the word itself is not the name of a fixed set of countries.
Nor does it say which route someone traveled: a person can go 海外 by plane.
As a noun modifier, learn 海外の rather than an invented 海外な.
