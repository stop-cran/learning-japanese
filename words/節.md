# 節 (ふし) - joint; tune; notable point

## Meaning

節 is a noun with several uses at the reading ふし. It can
name a joint or knuckle, a node on a plant stem or knot in wood,
a tune, or a notable point in someone's words or behavior.

- この竹には節がたくさんあります。 (このたけにはふしがたくさんあります。) - This bamboo has many nodes.
- その歌の節を覚えています。 (そのうたのふしをおぼえています。) - I remember the tune of that song.
- 彼の話には不思議な節があります。 (かれのはなしにはふしぎなふしがあります。) - There is something odd in his account.

指の節 (ゆびのふし) means a finger joint or knuckle.
The object or surrounding description identifies the intended sense.

## How the kanji combine

節 is a single character read ふし here. It represents the
whole native noun. The physical node and joint senses give a useful
recognition aid, but they are not proof of a historical derivation
for every abstract or musical use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[点](点.md) (てん) can mean a point in an argument.
[歌](歌.md) (うた) names a song; its 節 is the tune, not
necessarily the words or the complete performance. 関節 (かんせつ)
specifically names an anatomical joint.

## Distinctive meaning

This card teaches ふし, not every compound containing 節.
In 変な節 (へんなふし), "something odd," the word points to
a noteworthy aspect. It does not mean a physical knot or require
the speaker to identify a numbered section of text.
