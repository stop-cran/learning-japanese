# 行事 (ぎょうじ) - organized event; function

## Meaning

行事 is an event or function that people carry out, such as a school, community, or seasonal event. It is a noun.

- 運動会は学校の大切な行事です。
  (うんどうかいはがっこうのたいせつなぎょうじです。) - Sports day is an important school event.
- 地域の行事に家族で参加しました。
  (ちいきのぎょうじにかぞくでさんかしました。) - We took part in a local event as a family.

行事に参加する means to participate in an event; に marks what one participates in.

## How the kanji combine

[行](../kanji/行.md) contributes carrying out, and [事](../kanji/事.md) a matter or activity: something carried out as an event. The readings are ギョウ + ジ, rather than the こう reading found in some other 行 words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

催し (もよおし) and イベント also name events. [出来事](出来事.md) (できごと) can name a happening or occurrence without implying an organized function. [儀式](儀式.md) (ぎしき) foregrounds a ceremony or ritual; some 行事 include such ceremonies.

## Distinctive meaning

Annual and customary events are typical contexts, but the word does not require that every event recur each year. Conversely, an accidental happening is not automatically a 行事 simply because English calls it an "event."

Here English "function" means an organized social event, not the capability or operation called [機能](機能.md) (きのう).
