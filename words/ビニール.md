# ビニール - vinyl; plastic material

## Meaning

ビニール names vinyl or plastic material, and articles made from such material.
Learn it in everyday combinations such as ビニール袋 (ビニールぶくろ),
a plastic bag, and ビニールシート, a plastic sheet.

- ビニール袋に入れてください。 (ビニールぶくろにいれてください。) - Please put it in a plastic bag.
- 床にビニールシートを敷きました。 (ゆかにビニールシートをしきました。) - I spread a plastic sheet on the floor.

The everyday material label does not by itself identify the exact polymer
used in an individual bag or sheet.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana material word, not a kanji compound.
Keep the long vowel in ニー: ビニール.
The dictionary connects it with vinyl material; that does not establish
every step or language in the word's borrowing history.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ナイロン](ナイロン.md) is another material word, but nylon and vinyl
are not the same material. [袋](袋.md) (ふくろ) is the container:
a paper bag is still a 袋 without being a ビニール袋.
There is no ordinary material antonym that covers every context.

## Distinctive meaning

English "vinyl" can mean a phonograph record.
In this JMdict family, the record sense is restricted to the different
form バイナル; do not transfer that restriction away and teach ビニール
as a general replacement for [レコード](レコード.md).
This card teaches the material use supported for ビニール.
