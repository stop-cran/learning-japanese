# すんなり — smoothly; without resistance

## Meaning

すんなり describes something proceeding or being accepted without trouble or resistance.

- 必要な書類がそろい、申請はすんなり通った。
  — Once the required documents were ready, the application went through smoothly.
- 皆が賛成し、提案はすんなり受け入れられた。
  — Everyone agreed, and the proposal was accepted without resistance.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すんなり emphasizes the absence of obstacles or opposition. It can also describe a slender, supple
shape; that meaning is not used in these examples.

## Distinctive meaning

The process may still take time. すんなり indicates that it proceeds without the expected
difficulty or resistance.
