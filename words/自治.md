# 自治 (じち) - self-government; autonomy

## Meaning

自治 is self-government or autonomy: a community or institution handles
its own affairs on its own responsibility.
It can also abbreviate 地方自治 (ちほうじち), local self-government.
It is a noun; use 自治の + noun or constructions such as 自治を認める,
"recognize autonomy."

- 大学の自治は大切です。 (だいがくのじちはたいせつです。) - University autonomy is important.
- 住民が地域の自治について話し合いました。 (じゅうみんがちいきのじちについてはなしあいました。) - The residents discussed local self-government.

The university example shows that the word is not limited to sovereign countries.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) contributes oneself, and [治](../kanji/治.md)
contributes governing or ordering affairs.
The compound points to self-governance, not medical treatment.
The on readings combine as ジ + チ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[独立](独立.md) (どくりつ) concerns independence or self-reliance.
自治 can exist within a larger state or institution without complete
political independence.
[管理](管理.md) (かんり), management, does not by itself specify
that the people concerned govern their own affairs.

## Distinctive meaning

自治 identifies who has responsibility for governing affairs, not a guarantee
that every individual may ignore rules. The actual powers and limits depend
on the arrangement. It also does not mean that an organization needs no
cooperation or resources from outside.
