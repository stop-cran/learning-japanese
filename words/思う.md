# 思う (おもう) — think; believe; feel

## Meaning

思う expresses a thought, belief, or feeling. To say "I think that ...", put the thought before と: a plain-form clause + と思います.

- この本はおもしろいと思います。  
  このほんはおもしろいとおもいます。  
  "I think this book is interesting."
- 田中さんは学生だと思います。  
  たなかさんはがくせいだとおもいます。  
  "I think Tanaka is a student."

For a present affirmative noun or na-adjective, keep だ before と: 学生だと思う がくせいだとおもう (think someone is a student), 静かだと思う しずかだとおもう (think it is quiet). Do not add だ after an i-adjective such as おもしろい.

For an emotional response, an i-adjective can take its く-form: また会えてうれしく思います
(またあえてうれしくおもいます), "I am happy to see you again." This is more formal than simply saying うれしいです.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native single-kanji verb. [思](../kanji/思.md) supplies おも, and う is okurigana. It is godan: 思います おもいます, 思って おもって, 思わない おもわない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[考える](考える.md) かんがえる often emphasizes considering or thinking something through; it can also express a belief, so the meanings overlap. 感じる かんじる emphasizes feeling or sensing. There is no general lexical antonym; 思わない means "do not think".

## Distinctive meaning

と思います presents a view rather than an established fact. It does not necessarily mean uncertainty, and 思う is not restricted to emotions. The negative sentence この本は高くないと思います このほんはたかくないとおもいます means "I think this book isn't expensive": the negative belongs inside the thought.
