# 見舞い (みまい) - get-well visit; expression of sympathy

## Meaning

見舞い is a visit or inquiry concerning someone affected by illness or misfortune, or an expression of sympathy. It can also mean a get-well letter or a gift sent in sympathy. The form [お見舞い](お見舞い.md) (おみまい) adds お.

- 入院中の友達の見舞いに行きました。 (にゅういんちゅうのともだちのみまいにいきました。) - I went to visit a friend who was in hospital.
- 見舞いの品を送りました。 (みまいのしなをおくりました。) - I sent a get-well gift.

見舞いに行く uses に for the purpose of going. The second example names an item sent to convey concern; it does not imply an in-person visit.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) contributes seeing or looking, and [舞](../kanji/舞.md) has the independent meaning "dance." The latter does not give a useful literal explanation of this word. Learn 見舞い as a native whole-word expression, related to the verb 見舞う (みまう).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[訪問](訪問.md) (ほうもん) is a visit more generally. 問い合わせ (といあわせ) is an information inquiry; asking a shop its opening hours is not a 見舞い.

## Distinctive meaning

The motivating concern is essential to this meaning. A 見舞い can express care after misfortune as well as illness, and its form may be a visit, message, or gift. The noun alone does not guarantee recovery or specify what gift is appropriate. There is no useful direct antonym.
