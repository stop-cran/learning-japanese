# 妨げる (さまたげる) — hinder; obstruct

## Meaning

妨げる means to hinder, obstruct, disturb or prevent something. It is transitive: the activity or progress being interfered with takes を.

工事の音が勉強を妨げている。（こうじのおとがべんきょうをさまたげている。） — The construction noise is interfering with my studying.

大雪が列車の運行を妨げた。（おおゆきがれっしゃのうんこうをさまたげた。） — Heavy snow obstructed train operations.

## How the kanji combine

妨 contributes interference or obstruction in the native reading さまたげる. It is one verb, not a compound assembled from separate on-readings; the polite form is 妨げます（さまたげます）.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[じゃま](じゃま.md) and [差し支え](差し支え.md) さしつかえ name hindrance; じゃまする also expresses interfering. [障害](障害.md) しょうがい can name an obstacle. [防ぐ](防ぐ.md) ふせぐ overlaps in preventing something, often with a protective purpose.

## Distinctive meaning

妨げる does not guarantee that the obstructed activity becomes completely impossible: the noise example can describe making study difficult. Nor is every instance morally bad; something may prevent an undesirable development. The verb is ichidan: 妨げない, 妨げた. Keep the full reading さまたげる, not さまげる.
