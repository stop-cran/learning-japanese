# 仲 (なか) - interpersonal relationship

## Meaning

仲 is a noun for the relationship or terms between people.
仲がいい means "get along well"; 仲が悪い means "be on bad
terms." 人と仲がいい identifies the person one gets along with.

- 私は姉と仲がいいです。 (わたしはあねとなかがいいです。) - My older sister and I get along well.
- 二人はすぐに仲よくなりました。 (ふたりはすぐになかよくなりました。) - The two quickly became friendly with each other.

仲よく is an adverbial form used for friendly interaction.
仲よくする means treating one another in a friendly way, while
仲よくなる describes becoming friendly.

## How the kanji combine

仲 is a single character read なか in this noun. It names the
connection between people. The identically pronounced [中](中.md), "inside;
middle," is a different word and spelling, not a substitute here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[関係](関係.md) (かんけい) covers relationships and connections
more broadly, including connections between facts or organizations.
[友達](友達.md) (ともだち) names a person, a friend; 仲
names the relationship rather than one of its participants.

## Distinctive meaning

仲 does not inherently mean friendship or a good relationship.
The evaluation comes from いい, 悪い, or the context.
仲がいい can describe relatives, classmates, or colleagues without
claiming that they have a romantic relationship.
