# 点く (つく) - catch fire; come on

## Meaning

点く means that a flame is lit or starts burning, or that a light or appliance
comes on. It is usually written つく in kana.
It is an intransitive godan verb: the light or flame takes が.

- 暗くなると、街灯がつきます。
  (くらくなると、がいとうがつきます。) - The streetlights come on when it gets dark.
- ガスコンロの火がつきません。
  (ガスコンロのひがつきません。) - The gas burner will not light.

Forms include つきます, ついた, ついて, and つかない.
電気がついている (でんきがついている) describes the light being on.

## How the kanji combine

[点](../kanji/点.md) is read つ here, with く as okurigana.
The character's lighting use belongs to this verb; the noun reading テン,
"point; dot," does not replace it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[付ける](付ける.md) (つける) includes turning a light on:
電気をつける, "turn on the light," versus 電気がつく, "the light comes on."
[消える](消える.md) (きえる), "go out," is the useful opposite.
[燃える](燃える.md) (もえる) describes burning;
[光る](光る.md) (ひかる) describes shining or giving off light.

## Distinctive meaning

Starting to burn and continuing to burn are related but different viewpoints.
The dictionary also records the lighting sense under [付く](付く.md) (つく),
so its attachment-focused title does not erase that overlap.
[着く](着く.md), "arrive," and [就く](就く.md), "take up a position,"
are other つく verbs, not meanings of this lighting entry.
The verb does not itself identify who switched the light on.
