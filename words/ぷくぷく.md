# ぷくぷく — gently bubbling; round and chubby

## Meaning

Bubbles appearing in liquid, or something rising or sinking while releasing bubbles. It also describes a rounded, plump, puffed-up appearance.

- 水槽（すいそう）の底（そこ）から泡（あわ）がぷくぷくと上がってくる。 — Bubbles are rising from the bottom of the aquarium.
- 発酵中（はっこうちゅう）の生地（きじ）から、ぷくぷくと泡が出ている。 — Bubbles are forming in the fermenting dough.
- 赤ちゃんのほっぺは、ぷくぷくしている。 — The baby's cheeks are round and chubby.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぶくぶく](ぶくぶく.md) strongly overlaps in bubbles and swelling, sometimes with a heavier impression. [もこもこ](もこもこ.md) concerns bulky fluff or lumps; [ぷにぷに](ぷにぷに.md) concerns squishiness when pressed rather than roundness alone.

## Distinctive meaning

ぷくぷくと泡が出る and ぷくぷくしたほっぺ are useful patterns. The word often sounds small-scale or affectionate, but bubble size is not fixed by the consonant. Calling another person chubby can still be unwelcome; the sound pattern does not guarantee that a body comment is polite.
