# 実施 (じっし) - implementation; carrying out

## Meaning

実施 means implementation or carrying something out.
It is a noun and a transitive する verb, used with planned activities such as a survey,
test, drill, or policy: Nを実施する.
The passive Nが実施される means that N is carried out.

- 来週、避難訓練を実施します。
  (らいしゅう、ひなんくんれんをじっしします。) - We will hold an evacuation drill next week.
- 調査は四月に実施されました。
  (ちょうさはしがつにじっしされました。) - The survey was conducted in April.

実施するかどうか (じっしするかどうか) means "whether to carry it out."
The future form can describe an intention, not a completed action.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality, and 施 contributes carrying out or putting
into effect. The combination presents a plan becoming an actual operation.
The on reading is じっし, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[実行](実行.md) (じっこう) also means carrying out or putting into practice.
実施 fits organized activities and arrangements especially well.
[計画](計画.md) (けいかく), "planning; plan," names a related earlier step, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

実施 identifies the conduct or implementation of the activity, not its success.
A survey can be 実施された even if its results are inconclusive.
Choose the English verb for the object: "hold" a drill, "conduct" a survey,
or "implement" a policy.
