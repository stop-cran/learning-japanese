# 葉書 (はがき) — postcard

## Meaning

葉書 is a postcard: a card on which you write an address and message, normally mailed without an envelope. Kana spellings はがき and ハガキ are common.

旅行先から友達に葉書を送りました。 (りょこうさきからともだちにはがきをおくりました。) — I sent a postcard to a friend from my travel destination.

## How the kanji combine

[葉](../kanji/葉.md) is read は, and [書](../kanji/書.md) contributes がき, related to writing. The voiced が is part of this word's reading. The characters are useful spelling cues; a postcard is not literally a leaf covered in writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

手紙 (てがみ) means a letter. 絵葉書 (えはがき) is a picture postcard. 封筒 (ふうとう) is an envelope, which an ordinary postcard does not require.

## Distinctive meaning

葉書 names the card, not the postage stamp: that is 切手 (きって). Cards and other flat paper items can be counted with 枚 (まい): 葉書を二枚 (はがきをにまい), "two postcards."
