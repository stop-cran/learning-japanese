# 替える (かえる) - replace; change

## Meaning

替える is a transitive ichidan verb for replacing or changing something.
The item being replaced takes を; a replacement can take に.
Its forms include 替えます, 替えた, and 替えない.

- 電池を替えました。 (でんちをかえました。) - I replaced the battery.
- 古いタオルを新しいものに替えてください。 (ふるいタオルをあたらしいものにかえてください。) - Please replace the old towel with a new one.

These examples concern putting another item in place, not merely changing
the appearance of the original item.

## How the kanji combine

替 supplies the idea of replacement. It is read か in this native verb,
with える written in kana. This is a single-kanji verb, not a compound
whose characters supply separate parts of the meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[換える](換える.md) also reads かえる and tends toward exchanging or
interchanging, as in money exchange. 代える (かえる) tends toward
substituting a person, such as a player. JMdict treats these as spellings
within one entry and states tendencies, not absolute restrictions.
[変える](変える.md) (かえる) emphasizes changing a state, arrangement,
or property.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose a spelling with the intended action in mind, but do not turn the
spelling tendencies into three rigidly separate verbs. 替える makes a
useful starting point for replacing everyday items; the object and
context still do much of the work.
