# あぶる - toast; warm over a fire

## Meaning

あぶる means warming or drying something near heat, or toasting, grilling or scorching it.
It is a **transitive godan verb**: あぶります, あぶって, あぶった.
The thing exposed to heat takes を.

- 火で手をあぶりました。 (ひでてをあぶりました。) - I warmed my hands over the fire.
- のりを軽くあぶってから使います。 (のりをかるくあぶってからつかいます。) - I lightly toast the nori before using it.

The first example does not mean burning the hands. The object and degree of heating
determine whether English "warm," "toast" or "scorch" is appropriate.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 炙る and 焙る are dictionary spellings;
烘る is marked rare. They are not three different pronunciations.
This article retains the source's kana form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[焼く](焼く.md) (やく) is broader cooking or burning by heat, including baking.
[暖める](暖める.md) (あたためる) broadly means warming something;
あぶる makes exposure to heat more central.
冷ます (さます), "let something cool," can supply a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every occurrence as "scorch" just because that is the source list's gloss.
Lightly toasting seaweed and warming hands are useful ordinary uses.
Nor is あぶる the general verb for heating everything by any method.
