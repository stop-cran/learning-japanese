# 挟まる (はさまる) - get between; be caught between

## Meaning

挟まる describes something getting between two sides or being caught there.
It is intransitive: the thing caught takes が; the intervening location can take に.

- ドアに指が挟まりました。 (ドアにゆびがはさまりました。) - My finger got caught in the door.
- 本のページの間に紙が挟まっています。 (ほんのページのあいだにかみがはさまっています。) - A piece of paper is caught between the book's pages.

This is godan despite ending in る: 挟まります, 挟まった,
挟まって and 挟まらない. 挟まっている can describe the resulting position.

## How the kanji combine

[挟](../kanji/挟.md) contributes being held or positioned between.
It writes はさ before the kana まる. The complete native verb is はさまる.
挾まる is another recognized spelling, not a separate meaning to insert into these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[挟む](挟む.md) (はさむ) is transitive: 本にしおりを挟む,
"put a bookmark between the pages." Compare the inserted object with を
against the thing caught with が.
[捕まる](捕まる.md) (つかまる) can mean being caught or arrested,
without the same focus on an intervening position.

## Distinctive meaning

An English passive translation does not make 挟まる a passive conjugation of 挟む.
The physical examples concern a position between sides, not necessarily deliberate insertion.
A piece of paper can be between pages without injury; a caught finger can hurt.
Do not turn the verb into a claim that every occurrence is an accident.
