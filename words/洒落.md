# 洒落 (しゃれ) - joke; pun

## Meaning

洒落 means a joke, pun, witticism, or wordplay. Context determines whether the joke specifically plays on similar-sounding words; not every 洒落 must be a homophone pun.

- その洒落は面白いですね。
  (そのしゃれはおもしろいですね。) - That is an amusing joke.
- これは洒落ではありません。
  (これはしゃれではありません。) - This is no joke.

洒落を言う (しゃれをいう) means "make a joke or pun." Kana **しゃれ** and **シャレ** also occur; katakana does not turn the word into an English loanword.

## How the kanji combine

JMdict marks 洒落 as an **ateji** spelling: do not derive the joke meaning by mechanically adding character glosses or read it as シャ + ラク. [落](../kanji/落.md) is linked for character recognition, not because every pun means "fall."

The practical reading here is **しゃれ**. Separate public-dictionary headings such as しゃら and しゃらく are not alternate definitions to combine into this entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冗談](冗談.md) (じょうだん), "joke; jest," overlaps; 洒落 can foreground wit or wordplay. [ユーモア](ユーモア.md) names humor and can overlap with a humorous remark. Being serious contrasts with joking in a particular situation, not with every use of wit.

## Distinctive meaning

There is also a separately recorded stylish/refined sense. Recognize its connection with [おしゃれ](おしゃれ.md), including **おしゃれな服** (おしゃれなふく), "stylish clothes," and the related expression **洒落た店** (しゃれたみせ), "a stylish shop."

These expressions do not turn the joke noun into an unrestricted な-adjective. Use the construction appropriate to the meaning; the examples above focus on jokes. The additional style sense does not acquire an independent N2 classification here.
