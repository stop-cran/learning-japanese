# 刀 (かたな) - sword; katana

## Meaning

刀 is a noun meaning a sword, especially a Japanese single-edged sword. The reading taught here is かたな. It identifies the object, not a general category of every sharp cutting tool.

- 博物館で古い刀を見ました。 (はくぶつかんでふるいかたなをみました。) - I saw an old sword at the museum.
- この刀は江戸時代のものです。 (このかたなはえどじだいのものです。) - This sword is from the Edo period.

Use ordinary noun particles: 刀を見る, "look at a sword," or 刀の写真 (かたなのしゃしん), "a photograph of a sword."

## How the kanji combine

刀 is one character, with the native reading かたな for this standalone word. There is no compound to split. The character also has the on reading トウ, but another reading does not make all dictionary senses interchangeable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ナイフ](ナイフ.md), "knife," and 包丁 (ほうちょう), "kitchen knife," are useful contrasts for everyday cutting tools. They are not suitable general translations of 刀 when it is read かたな. A sword has no natural lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The community source's English gloss mixes in an engraving tool. The inspected dictionary places its scalpel and engraving-tool senses under とう, not かたな. Learn "sword/katana" for this card rather than extending it to kitchen knives or craft tools from that mixed gloss.
