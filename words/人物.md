# 人物 (じんぶつ) - person; figure; character

## Meaning

人物 is a noun for a person or figure, including a character in a story. It can also refer to someone's character or personal qualities. It does not specify gender.

- この小説には三人の人物が登場します。 (このしょうせつにはさんにんのじんぶつがとうじょうします。) - Three characters appear in this novel.
- 写真に写っている人物は誰ですか。 (しゃしんにうつっているじんぶつはだれですか。) - Who is the person in the photograph?

In 人物を評価する (じんぶつをひょうかする), "assess someone's character," the focus can be what the person is like rather than identifying their name.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) contributes person, and [物](../kanji/物.md) contributes an entity or figure in this compound. Learn the whole meaning rather than interpreting the second character as an assertion that a person is an inanimate object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[人](人.md) (ひと) is the broader everyday noun "person." [人間](人間.md) (にんげん) can emphasize a human being. 人物 is useful when identifying, describing, or evaluating an individual figure.

## Distinctive meaning

A 人物 may be real or fictional, famous or unknown. The dictionary also recognizes an evaluative sense, "an able or talented person," but that praise is not built into every occurrence. Neither the photograph example nor the noun itself tells us the person's ability, importance, or gender.
