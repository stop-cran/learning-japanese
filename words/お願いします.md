# お願いします (おねがいします) - please

## Meaning

お願いします is a polite request formula: "please" or "I would like to ask for this." A requested item or task can take を.

- お水をお願いします。 (おみずをおねがいします。) - Water, please.
- 書類の確認をお願いします。 (しょるいのかくにんをおねがいします。) - Please check the documents.

It can also stand alone when the request is clear from the situation. お願い is related to [願う](願う.md) (ねがう), "wish; request"; お願いします presents the request deferentially.

## How the kanji combine

[願](../kanji/願.md) supplies ねが in [願い](願い.md) (ねがい), "wish; request." The native prefix お and します form the complete expression. This is not an on-reading compound, and します is the polite form of する, not part of the kanji reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ください](ください.md) also makes requests, but its constructions differ: 水をください (みずをください), "please give me water," and 確認してください (かくにんしてください), "please check." [どうぞ](どうぞ.md) can invite or offer, as in "go ahead"; お願いします makes a request.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not attach お願いします mechanically wherever English has "please." For a task, 確認をお願いします names the task as a noun; 確認してください uses a verb's て-form with ください. [宜しく](宜しく.md) (よろしく) combines with it in よろしくお願いします, a formula whose translation depends on the introduction, cooperation, or favor being requested.
