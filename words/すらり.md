# すらり — slender and well-proportioned

## Meaning

すらり can describe a long, slender, well-proportioned shape or appearance.

- すらりとした体型で、どんな服もきれいに着こなす。
  — With a slender, well-proportioned figure, she wears any outfit elegantly.
- 彼女はすらりとした脚のラインを生かす服を選んだ。
  — She chose clothes that made use of her long, slender leg line.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すらり emphasizes a graceful, elongated proportion. ほっそり focuses more directly on being
slender or thin.

## Distinctive meaning

The word also has an adverbial use for smooth, continuous movement. These examples focus on
appearance, not motion.
