# 乾く (かわく)

## Meaning

乾く means to become dry. The drying thing is the subject, marked with が.

- 洗濯物が乾きました。 (せんたくものが かわきました。) — The laundry has dried.
- 髪がまだ乾いていません。 (かみが まだ かわいていません。) — My hair is not dry yet.

It is a godan verb: 乾きます (かわきます), 乾いて (かわいて), 乾かない (かわかない). 乾いている can describe the resulting dry state.

## How the kanji combine

乾 represents dryness and supplies かわ; く completes the dictionary form. The shared pronunciation with 渇く does not make their usual spellings interchangeable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

乾かす (かわかす) is transitive: 髪を乾かす, "dry one's hair." 濡れる (ぬれる), "get wet," provides the natural contrasting change.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 乾く for moisture leaving clothes, hair, paint, and similar things. For being thirsty, write 喉が渇く (のどがかわく), not the laundry-drying spelling. The particle contrast also matters: hair が乾く, but someone dries hair を乾かす.
