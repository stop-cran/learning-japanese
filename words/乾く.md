# 乾く (かわく)

## Meaning

乾く means to become dry. The drying thing is the subject, marked with が.

- 洗濯物が乾きました。 (せんたくものが かわきました。) — The laundry has dried.
- 髪がまだ乾いていません。 (かみが まだ かわいていません。) — My hair is not dry yet.

It is a godan verb: 乾きます (かわきます), 乾いて (かわいて), 乾かない (かわかない). 乾いている can describe the resulting dry state.

## How the kanji combine

[乾](../kanji/乾.md) represents dryness and supplies かわ; く completes the dictionary form. The shared pronunciation with [渇く](渇く.md) does not erase the useful distinction between ordinary drying and thirst.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[乾かす](乾かす.md) (かわかす) is transitive: 髪を乾かす, "dry one's hair." 濡れる (ぬれる), "get wet," provides the natural contrasting change.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 乾く for moisture leaving clothes, hair, paint, and similar things. For thirst, 喉が渇く (のどがかわく) is a useful teaching spelling. This is not an absolute prohibition of 乾: the dictionary also records 喉が乾く for "be thirsty." The particle contrast also matters: 髪が乾く (かみがかわく), "hair dries," but 髪を乾かす (かみをかわかす), "dry one's hair."
