# 海 (うみ) - sea; ocean

## Meaning

海 is the sea or ocean. It is the everyday word for the large body of salt water and can also suggest the seaside in a trip's context.

夏は海で泳ぎます。 (なつはうみでおよぎます。) - I swim in the sea in summer.

海に行きたいです。 (うみにいきたいです。) - I want to go to the sea.

Here で marks where swimming happens, while に marks the destination of going.

## How the kanji combine

[海](../kanji/海.md) writes the complete native noun うみ. Its on reading カイ occurs in compounds such as 海外 (かいがい), "overseas."

## Synonyms and antonyms

海洋 (かいよう) is a more technical word for the ocean or oceans. [湖](湖.md) (みずうみ) is a lake; [川](川.md) (かわ) is a river. 陸 (りく), "land," provides a useful environmental contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

English distinguishes "sea" and "ocean" in some names and geographical discussions, but both can translate ordinary 海. It does not specifically mean a sandy beach; that narrower location is a different idea.
