# 妻 (つま) — wife

## Meaning

妻 means "wife." It identifies one person by her marriage relationship, not by whether she has children or works outside the home.
Use a noun + の to make the relationship explicit, as in 私の妻 (わたしのつま), "my wife."

- 私の妻は教師です。 (わたしのつまはきょうしです。) — My wife is a teacher.
- 妻と買い物に行きました。 (つまとかいものにいきました。) — I went shopping with my wife.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [妻](../kanji/妻.md) writes the native noun つま.
There is no two-kanji relationship to unpack. The on reading サイ appears in other words, but it does not replace つま in these examples.
This article selects the wife sense attached specifically to the spelling 妻.

## Synonyms and antonyms

配偶者 (はいぐうしゃ) means "spouse" and does not specify wife rather than husband.
奥さん (おくさん) is an honorific expression for a wife, especially someone else's; it adds a social stance that the bare relationship label 妻 does not.
夫 (おっと), "husband," is the paired relationship term, not an antonym in the way "hot" and "cold" are.

## Distinctive meaning

妻 refers to one spouse, whereas [夫婦](夫婦.md) (ふうふ) refers to the married pair.
Do not automatically translate "woman" as 妻: marriage is part of the meaning.
The pronunciation つま also occurs in other dictionary entries; it does not make their unrelated meanings part of this wife sense.
