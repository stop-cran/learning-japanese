# デモ (デモ) - demonstration; demo

## Meaning

デモ is a noun with two useful contexts: a public demonstration or protest, and a demonstration showing how something works.
Use デモに参加する (デモにさんかする), "join a demonstration," or デモを見る (デモをみる), "watch a demo."

- 多くの人が平和を求めるデモに参加しました。 (おおくのひとがへいわをもとめるデモにさんかしました。) - Many people joined a demonstration calling for peace.
- 店で新しいソフトのデモを見ました。 (みせであたらしいソフトのデモをみました。) - I watched a demonstration of the new software at the shop.

デモを行う (デモをおこなう) can mean conducting either kind of demonstration; the topic resolves the ambiguity.

## How the kanji combine

デモ is an abbreviation of デモンストレーション, as explicitly recorded by JMdict.
It is written in katakana and has two morae, デ・モ.
The abbreviation preserves more than one sense of the longer word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

抗議 (こうぎ) is protest or an objection, which need not take the form of a public march.
実演 (じつえん) overlaps with showing a product or technique in action.
[でも](でも.md), "but; however," is a separate hiragana connector, not this noun.

## Distinctive meaning

A software デモ need not express political opposition, and a street デモ is not necessarily a product presentation.
Do not choose the meaning from the abbreviated spelling alone.
The participants, purpose, and object shown normally give the needed context.
