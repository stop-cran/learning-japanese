# 下降 (かこう) - descent; decline

## Meaning

下降 is downward movement, a fall, or a decline in level. 下降する is intransitive: the thing that descends or declines takes が, not an object with を.

- 飛行機がゆっくり下降しています。 (ひこうきがゆっくりかこうしています。) - The airplane is descending slowly.
- 気温が下降しました。 (きおんがかこうしました。) - The temperature fell.

The downward change can be controlled, as in an aircraft's descent; the word does not itself mean a crash.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) contributes down or below, and [降](../kanji/降.md) descending. Their related meanings reinforce downward movement. The on readings are カ + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下がる](下がる.md) (さがる) is an everyday verb for going down or becoming lower. 降下 (こうか) also means descent or a drop. [降りる](降りる.md) (おりる) includes descending and getting off a vehicle. 上昇 (じょうしょう), rising or ascending, is the corresponding upward contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

For "get off the bus," use バスを降りる (バスをおりる), not a mechanical replacement with 下降する. 下降 concerns a downward change, not every action translated into English with "down." The homophone [火口](火口.md) is a noun for a crater or boiler burner, not this movement word.
