# 推定 (すいてい) - estimation; inference

## Meaning

推定 is an estimate or inference drawn from available facts or clues rather than direct certainty. 推定する is a transitive suru verb: X を推定する means estimate or infer X, and X と推定する gives the conclusion.

- 足跡から動物の大きさを推定しました。 (あしあとからどうぶつのおおきさをすいていしました。) - We estimated the animal's size from its footprints.
- 調査の結果から、故障が原因だと推定しました。 (ちょうさのけっかから、こしょうがげんいんだとすいていしました。) - From the investigation results, we inferred that a malfunction was the cause.

推定三百人 (すいていさんびゃくにん) means "an estimated three hundred people."

## How the kanji combine

推 contributes inferring or reasoning, and [定](../kanji/定.md) contributes determining. Read スイ + テイ. This is a useful analysis of the compound, not a claim that the conclusion has been proved certain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予測](予測.md) concerns prediction, often of a future result; 推定 can infer a present or past fact. [仮定](仮定.md) sets up an assumption or hypothetical condition, which need not be a conclusion from evidence. [見当](見当.md) can be a rough estimate or idea. These words overlap in English without being freely interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

An estimate can be numerical, but 推定 is not limited to numbers. Nor does it require a future event or mean an evidence-free guess. The clue and the conclusion in the examples show why "inference" as well as "estimation" is useful.
