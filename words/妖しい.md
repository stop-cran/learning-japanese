# 妖しい (あやしい) — mysterious; bewitching

## Meaning

妖しい describes a mysterious, bewitching, or alluring quality. This spelling is stylistically
marked compared with everyday [怪しい](怪しい.md); it can suggest an uncanny atmosphere or captivating beauty.

- 妖しい光 (あやしい ひかり) — an eerie or mesmerizing light
- 妖しい魅力 (あやしい みりょく) — a bewitching charm

It is an i-adjective, with forms such as 妖しく (あやしく) and 妖しかった (あやしかった).

## How the kanji combine

[妖](../kanji/妖.md) supplies あや; しい completes the written adjective. The supernatural or alluring
association of the character helps explain the spelling choice; it does not make every occurrence
a claim about magic or sexuality.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怪しい](怪しい.md) also has mysterious and alluring senses, as well as the everyday suspicious and
doubtful ones. JMdict gives 妖しい its own entry and cross-references that overlapping sense.
Do not treat dictionary entry boundaries as proof that the spellings can never overlap.

There is no fixed antonym covering both mysterious atmosphere and seductive appeal.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 怪しい for an ordinary suspicious story; do not choose 妖しい merely because English uses
"mysterious" or "suspicious" for both. The spelling draws attention to the atmosphere or allure.

For 妖しい compared with 怪しい and [奇しい](奇しい.md), see
[strange, suspicious, and rare](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
