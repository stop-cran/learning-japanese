# 欠陥 (けっかん) - defect; flaw

## Meaning

欠陥 is a defect, flaw, or deficiency in something. It can concern a product, design, system, or argument. Use Nに欠陥がある for "N has a flaw," or Nの欠陥 for "a defect in N."

- この製品には欠陥があります。 (このせいひんにはけっかんがあります。) - This product has a defect.
- 検査で設計の欠陥が見つかりました。 (けんさでせっけいのけっかんがみつかりました。) - The inspection revealed a flaw in the design.

欠陥を直す (けっかんをなおす) means "correct a defect." The noun itself is not a verb for breaking down.

## How the kanji combine

[欠](../kanji/欠.md) contributes something missing or lacking, and 陥 contributes a fault or sunken part. Their meanings reinforce an inadequacy in what should be complete or sound. The reading けっかん has a doubled k sound, represented by small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[欠点](欠点.md) (けってん) is a shortcoming or weak point. 欠陥 can emphasize a flaw in how something is made or works. [故障](故障.md) (こしょう) concerns malfunction; a design defect may exist before a machine actually fails.

## Distinctive meaning

欠陥 is a substantial negative assessment, not just "something I dislike." Explain what is deficient rather than using it loosely about a person. [完全](完全.md) (かんぜん), "complete; perfect," is a contextual contrast, but lacking a detected defect does not prove that a product is perfect in every respect.
