# 重視 (じゅうし) - attaching importance to

## Meaning

重視 names treating something as important or giving it serious consideration. It combines with する as a transitive verb: the factor emphasized takes を.

- この会社は経験を重視します。 (このかいしゃはけいけんをじゅうしします。) - This company places importance on experience.
- 値段より安全性を重視して選びました。 (ねだんよりあんぜんせいをじゅうししてえらびました。) - I chose with more emphasis on safety than on price.

The second example shows a priority in decision-making. 重視 does not mean that the other factor is completely ignored.

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) contributes weight or importance, while 視 concerns viewing or regarding. Together they mean viewing something as weighty or important, not looking at a physically heavy object. The on readings are ジュウ + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[重要](重要.md) (じゅうよう) describes something as important: 経験が重要です. 重視する describes someone's stance toward it: 経験を重視します. [無視](無視.md) (むし), "ignore," is a useful contrast; 軽視 (けいし) more specifically means treating something lightly.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss "importance" can hide the action involved. 重視 is not simply a replacement for 重要性 (じゅうようせい), "importance." In 重視する, someone actively assigns weight to a consideration.
