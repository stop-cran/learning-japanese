# 愛 (あい) — love; affection

## Meaning

愛 names love, affection, or care. It is not restricted to romantic love.
Nへの愛 (Nへのあい) expresses love directed toward N.

- この手紙から家族への愛を感じます。 (このてがみからかぞくへのあいをかんじます。) — I can feel the love for the family in this letter.

Here から marks where the feeling is perceived, and への identifies the people toward whom the love
is directed.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [愛](../kanji/愛.md) supplies the whole noun and uses the on reading アイ.
There is no multi-kanji relationship to split. The related verb 愛する (あいする) means "to love."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[好き](好き.md) (すき) expresses liking or fondness and can also refer to romantic affection.
It is not just a weaker replacement in every sentence: it has different grammar.
恋 (こい) specifically names romantic love, whereas 愛 can describe the family affection in the example.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare the constructions: 家族への愛 (かぞくへのあい), "love for one's family";
家族を愛する (かぞくをあいする), "love one's family"; and 家族が好きです
(かぞくがすきです), "I am fond of my family."
The noun, verb, and 好き construction cannot be swapped while keeping all the same particles.
