# 縮れる (ちぢれる) - be wavy; be curly

## Meaning

縮れる describes becoming wavy or curled, especially hair.
It is an intransitive ichidan verb. 縮れている can describe
the resulting curled state, rather than an action happening now.

- 彼の髪は細かく縮れています。 (かれのかみはこまかくちぢれています。) - His hair is tightly curled.
- 縮れた髪をとかしました。 (ちぢれたかみをとかしました。) - I combed the curly hair.

縮れた modifies 髪 directly in the second sentence.
The polite form is 縮れます (ちぢれます); the て-form is
縮れて (ちぢれて).

## How the kanji combine

縮 has a contraction or wrinkling association here, but learn the
whole native verb ちぢれる. The kana れる is part of this verb,
not proof that it is the passive of [縮む](縮む.md) (ちぢむ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くるくる](くるくる.md) can describe curled hair in くるくるした髪
(くるくるしたかみ). That does not make its spinning examples literal
descriptions of hair rotating. Straight hair offers a contextual contrast
with curled hair; it is not a judgment about either hairstyle.

## Distinctive meaning

縮む often focuses on becoming smaller or shorter; 縮れる foregrounds
waves, curls, or crinkling. This is a useful contrast, not a claim
that their full dictionary ranges never overlap. The public dictionary
also records a wrinkling use for 縮れる, beyond the hair examples here.
