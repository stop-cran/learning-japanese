# ストーブ — room heater

## Meaning

ストーブ normally means a heater used to warm a room. It can describe heaters using different fuels; the word alone does not specify electricity, kerosene, gas, or wood.

寒いので、ストーブをつけました。 (さむいので、ストーブをつけました。) — I turned on the heater because it was cold.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword comes from "stove." The long vowel in トー is written with ー. There are no kanji components to interpret.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ヒーター is a broader word for a heater. 暖房 (だんぼう) names room heating or a heating system. エアコン may provide heating as well as cooling, but is a different appliance category.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not automatically use ストーブ for the cooking stove in an English sentence. A kitchen cooktop or burner is generally コンロ. Japanese ストーブ primarily points to space heating, even though some heaters can also be used to warm a kettle.
