# 悔やむ (くやむ) - mourn; regret

## Meaning

悔やむ means mourn or lament someone's death. The person or death lamented can be expressed with を.

- 親友の死を悔やんでいます。 (しんゆうのしをくやんでいます。) - I am mourning my close friend's death.

An additional important use is regret or feel sorry about a past action or result:

- あのとき何も言わなかったことを悔やんでいます。 (あのときなにもいわなかったことをくやんでいます。) - I regret not having said anything then.

It is godan: 悔やみます (くやみます), 悔やんで (くやんで), 悔やまない (くやまない).

## How the kanji combine

悔 carries a regret association, here in the native verb くやむ; む is the ending. No 悔 kanji card is currently available. 悔む is another dictionary spelling of the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[悲しむ](悲しむ.md) (かなしむ) means grieve or be saddened more broadly. 悔やむ can focus on lamenting a death or regretting what happened, rather than every experience of sadness.

[悔しい](悔しい.md) (くやしい) is the adjective for bitter frustration. The two share a character but do not have identical grammatical uses or emotional scope.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes mourning from regret. A sentence about a death can express lament without saying the speaker caused it. In the regret use, the speaker wishes things had gone differently; the bare verb is not a general-purpose spoken apology.
