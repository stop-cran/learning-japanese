# 帰る (かえる) — return home

## Meaning

帰る means to return home or to a place one belongs. Depending on the viewpoint, English uses "go home," "come home" or "go back." It can also describe a guest leaving to go home.

- 今日は早く家に帰ります。 (きょうははやくいえにかえります。) — I will go home early today.
- 友達はもう帰りました。 (ともだちはもうかえりました。) — My friend has already gone home.

## How the kanji combine

This is one kanji plus okurigana: [帰](../kanji/帰.md) supplies かえ, and る completes the dictionary form.

Despite its える ending, it is a **godan** verb: 帰ります かえります, 帰らない かえらない, 帰って かえって, 帰った かえった.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 戻る もどる is the broader "return, go back." It does not necessarily imply a home or base.
- 帰す かえす means to send someone home; the person sent is an object rather than the returning subject.
- [行く](行く.md) いく, "go," can contrast with returning in a particular trip, but it is not an opposite in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

帰る focuses on returning to a home or base, not on movement toward or away from the speaker. Mark the destination with に or へ: [家](家.md)に帰る (いえにかえる), "go home." The returning person is the subject; 家 is not an object being returned.
