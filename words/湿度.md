# 湿度 (しつど) - humidity

## Meaning

湿度 is humidity: a measure of moisture in the air. It is a noun.

- 今日は湿度が高いです。 (きょうはしつどがたかいです。) - The humidity is high today.
- 部屋の湿度を測りました。 (へやのしつどをはかりました。) - I measured the humidity in the room.

## How the kanji combine

[湿](../kanji/湿.md) contributes moisture and [度](../kanji/度.md) its degree or measure.
This is a modifier + head compound: a degree of humidity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[温度](温度.md) (おんど) is temperature. Temperature and humidity are separate measurements; one does not name the other.
湿る (しめる), "become damp," describes a change in something rather than the measurement itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 湿度が高い or 湿度が低い for high or low humidity. 湿度 alone does not mean "unpleasantly damp" or "muggy":
the amount of moisture and someone's reaction to it are different things.
