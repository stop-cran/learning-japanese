# 話し合い (はなしあい) - discussion; talking things over

## Meaning

話し合い is a discussion or talking something over together.
It is a noun and also permits する. The spelling 話合い represents the same word.

- 明日、家族で話し合いをします。 (あした、かぞくではなしあいをします。) - We will have a family discussion tomorrow.
- 話し合いで解決したいです。 (はなしあいでかいけつしたいです。) - I want to resolve it through discussion.

## How the kanji combine

[話](../kanji/話.md) appears in 話す (はなす), speak, and [合](../kanji/合.md) in 合う (あう).
Here the latter part contributes mutual action: talking with one another.
話し合い is the noun corresponding to [話し合う](話し合う.md), rather than a compound of on-readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[相談](相談.md) (そうだん) emphasizes consultation or advice.
[会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) can name an organized meeting, while [会話](会話.md) (かいわ) is conversation.
[議論](議論.md) (ぎろん) can emphasize examining or debating views;
[打ち合わせ](打ち合わせ.md) (うちあわせ) often concerns coordination or arrangements.
These overlap without forming a rigid formal/informal division.

## Distinctive meaning

話し合い emphasizes an exchange between participants, not a one-way announcement.
It does not guarantee agreement: 話し合いはしたが、決まらなかった
(はなしあいはしたが、きまらなかった) means "We did discuss it, but nothing was decided."
