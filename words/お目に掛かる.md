# お目に掛かる (おめにかかる) - meet someone humbly

## Meaning

お目に掛かる is a humble expression for meeting someone to whom respect is
directed. Use person + に + お目に掛かる. It presents the meeting from the
speaker's side, rather than honoring the other person's action.

- 先生にお目に掛かれて、うれしいです。 (せんせいにおめにかかれて、うれしいです。) - I am glad to have had the chance to meet you, Professor.
- 明日お目に掛かります。 (あしたおめにかかります。) - I will see you tomorrow.

掛かる conjugates as a godan verb: お目に掛かった (おめにかかった) is the past,
and お目に掛かります (おめにかかります) is polite nonpast.
お目に掛かれる (おめにかかれる), "be able to meet," is the potential form;
the first example uses its て-form, お目に掛かれて.

## How the kanji combine

[目](../kanji/目.md) is "eye," with the respectful prefix お; [掛](../kanji/掛.md)
appears in 掛かる. Learn the entire phrase as an idiom for meeting, not as
a literal instruction involving eyes. お目にかかる is another listed spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会う](会う.md) (あう) is the neutral verb "meet." お目に掛かる adds a humble
relationship between the speaker's action and the respected person. There is
no single opposite humble expression that covers all kinds of parting.

## Distinctive meaning

This is useful in courteous introductions or appointments. Adding お does not
turn it into a respectful verb for a superior's action: keep track of who is
doing the meeting. The teaching here is the dictionary's modern humble sense,
not its separate archaic sense of being seen or noticed.
