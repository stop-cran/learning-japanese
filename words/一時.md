# 一時 (いちじ) - temporarily; at one time

## Meaning

一時 can refer to a limited period, or to a situation that existed at one time. It works as a noun or adverb. The same reading also means "one o'clock," so the surrounding sentence matters.

- 電車は一時、運転を見合わせた。 (でんしゃはいちじ、うんてんをみあわせた。) - Train service was temporarily suspended.
- 一時、この町に住んでいました。 (いちじ、このまちにすんでいました。) - I lived in this town at one time.

By contrast, 午後一時に会いましょう (ごごいちじにあいましょう) means "Let's meet at one in the afternoon." Here に marks a clock time.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," modifies [時](../kanji/時.md), "time." The compound can identify one clock hour or a bounded period. It does not mean that every temporary event lasts exactly an hour.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しばらく](しばらく.md) means "for a while" and overlaps with the temporary-period use. [一度](一度.md) (いちど) counts an occurrence; 一時 is not a general replacement for "once" in "I tried it once."

## Distinctive meaning

一時の感情 (いちじのかんじょう) is "a passing emotion." 一時的な (いちじてきな) explicitly means "temporary" before a noun. The reading taught here is いちじ: do not infer a different reading merely because the English translation is "a while."
