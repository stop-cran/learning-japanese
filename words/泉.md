# 泉 (いずみ) - spring; source of water

## Meaning

泉 is a spring: a place where water emerges from the ground, or the water found there. It is a noun. 泉の水 (いずみのみず) is "spring water," and 泉から marks the spring as a source.

- 森の中に小さな泉があります。 (もりのなかにちいさないずみがあります。) - There is a small spring in the forest.
- 泉から水が湧いています。 (いずみからみずがわいています。) - Water is welling up from the spring.

The second sentence describes water emerging; it does not merely locate a pool.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 泉 represents the whole noun, using the native reading いずみ. The on reading セン occurs in words such as 温泉 (おんせん), "hot spring." Do not substitute セン for the standalone reading taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

A [池](池.md) (いけ) is a pond, whereas a [川](川.md) (かわ) is a river. These describe different water features, not opposites. 噴水 (ふんすい) is useful for an ornamental fountain that sprays water.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "fountain" can be misleading if it suggests every decorative fountain is a 泉. Start with the image of water arising from a source. The noun does not itself tell you that the water is hot, drinkable, or safe to use.
