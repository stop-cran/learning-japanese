# 結ぶ (むすぶ) - tie; connect

## Meaning

結ぶ is a transitive godan verb for tying, binding, or linking. Mark what is tied with **を**. Forms include 結びます, 結んで, and 結ばない.

- 靴のひもを結びました。 (くつのひもをむすびました。) - I tied my shoelaces.
- この鉄道は二つの町を結んでいます。 (このてつどうはふたつのまちをむすんでいます。) - This railway connects two towns.

The first example is a physical knot; the second is a connection between places. **AとBを結ぶ** is another way to specify the two connected endpoints.

## How the kanji combine

結 contributes tying or joining, with the native reading むすぶ and the kana ending ぶ. The same written character appears in [結婚](結婚.md) (けっこん), but that compound uses a different reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

つなぐ also means to connect or join and overlaps with the railway use. ほどく, "untie," is a useful opposite for a tied shoelace, but not a universal opposite for every connection or agreement.

## Distinctive meaning

結ぶ also appears in 契約を結ぶ (けいやくをむすぶ), "conclude a contract." The object selects the action: tying laces, connecting towns, and entering an agreement are not interchangeable procedures. A literal knot is therefore a helpful starting image, not a requirement in every sentence.
