# ひんやり — cool to the touch

## Meaning

A perceptible coolness against the skin or in the surrounding air. Often refreshing, but it can also feel chilly.

- 石の床（ゆか）に触る（さわる）と、ひんやりした。 — The stone floor felt cool when I touched it.
- 洞窟（どうくつ）の中は、夏でもひんやりしている。 — Inside the cave it feels cool even in summer.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぽかぽか](ぽかぽか.md) and [ほかほか](ほかほか.md) describe warm sensations. 冷たい（つめたい）is a broader adjective for cold things or cold treatment; ひんやり foregrounds the felt sensation.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ひんやりする, ひんやりした空気（くうき, air）, and ひんやりとした感触（かんしょく, sensation）. It is not an ordinary な-adjective: avoid treating ひんやりな as the default learner pattern. The word need not imply that something is unpleasantly cold, frozen, or low in temperature by measurement.
