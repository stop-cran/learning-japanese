# 株 (かぶ) - stock share; stump

## Meaning

株 is a share in a company or stock held as an investment. It is also used as a counter for shares. In a plant context, it can mean a stump or a rooted plant.

- その会社の株を百株買いました。 (そのかいしゃのかぶをひゃくかぶかいました。) - I bought one hundred shares in that company.
- 庭の切り株に座りました。 (にわのきりかぶにすわりました。) - I sat on the tree stump in the garden.

切り株 (きりかぶ) makes the stump left after cutting a tree explicit. 一株 (ひとかぶ) can count a share or a plant; context identifies what is being counted.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 株 carries the reading かぶ and the whole noun. Its tree component is a helpful association with the plant meanings. That visual association does not explain every step in the development of the financial meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

株式 (かぶしき) is another term for company stock or shares. It does not replace 株 in a description of a tree stump. 株価 (かぶか) means a stock's price, not the share itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every English "stock" as 株. Shop inventory and soup stock are different concepts. Similarly, a company's 株 and the price paid for it are distinct: buying shares gives an ownership interest, not a physical piece of the company building.
