# 電球 (でんきゅう) - light bulb

## Meaning

電球 is a light bulb: the light-producing component fitted into a lamp or other lighting equipment.
It is a noun.

- 廊下の電球が切れました。 (ろうかのでんきゅうがきれました。) - The bulb in the hallway burned out.
- 電球を交換しました。 (でんきゅうをこうかんしました。) - I replaced the light bulb.

電球が切れる describes a bulb burning out, not someone cutting its glass.
LED電球 (エルイーディーでんきゅう) is an LED bulb; the word is not limited to incandescent technology.

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) contributes electricity and [球](../kanji/球.md) a ball or rounded object.
The modifier-plus-head association helps recall an electric bulb, not a requirement that every bulb be perfectly spherical.
The on readings are デン + キュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[電灯](電灯.md) (でんとう) names an electric light or lamp more broadly.
[明かり](明かり.md) (あかり) can mean light or illumination.
[スタンド](スタンド.md) can name a desk or floor lamp; the whole fixture is not merely its 電球.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the replaceable light source from the equipment holding it and the illumination it produces.
A room can be 明るい (あかるい), "bright," without that adjective naming a bulb.
The noun 電球 alone does not specify its fitting, wattage, or compatibility with a fixture.
