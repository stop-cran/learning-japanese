# 規則 (きそく) — rules and regulations

## Meaning

規則 means a rule or set of regulations, such as those of a school, workplace, or activity. Japanese does not require a plural ending when several rules are meant.

学校の規則を守ってください。 (がっこうのきそくをまもってください。) — Please follow the school rules.

## How the kanji combine

[規](../kanji/規.md) suggests a standard or rule, and 則 also means a rule or principle. Their on readings combine as キ + ソク. Together they name the rules that govern conduct or a procedure.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ルール is an everyday loanword for "rule." 法律 (ほうりつ) means law, not merely any organization's rule. 約束 (やくそく) is a promise or arrangement between people.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 規則 with its verbs: 規則を守る (きそくをまもる), "obey the rules," and 規則を破る (きそくをやぶる), "break the rules." 規則 itself is a noun, not the adjective "regular."
