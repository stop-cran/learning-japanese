# 吸う (すう) — inhale; suck

## Meaning

吸う means to draw something in: inhale air, suck liquid, or absorb moisture.
The thing drawn in takes を. Start with the breathing use:

- ゆっくり息を吸ってください。 (ゆっくりいきをすってください。) — Please breathe in slowly.
- このタオルは水をよく吸います。 (このタオルはみずをよくすいます。) — This towel absorbs water well.

The polite form is 吸います (すいます), and the て-form is 吸って (すって), with a doubled consonant.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native Japanese verb, not a multi-kanji compound.
[吸](../kanji/吸.md) supplies the inward-drawing meaning and is read す in 吸う; う is written in kana.
The kanji's on reading キュウ appears in 呼吸 (こきゅう), "breathing," but not in the reading of 吸う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

For breathing, 吐く (はく), "breathe out," gives the directional contrast: 息を吸う versus 息を吐く.
[飲む](飲む.md) (のむ) means to drink or swallow. It suits drinking water without specifying suction.
These are not unrestricted opposites or substitutes across all their senses.

## Distinctive meaning

The same inward movement can be done by a person or by an absorbent material, as the examples show.
たばこを吸う (たばこをすう) means "smoke tobacco"; here English uses a different verb.
Do not translate 吸う simply as "breathe" when the inward direction matters.
