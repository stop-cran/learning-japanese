# 打つ (うつ) — hit; strike

## Meaning

打つ means to hit or strike in the physical sense taught here. The thing struck takes を;
an instrument can take で.

- バットでボールを打ちました。 (バットでボールをうちました。) — I hit the ball with a bat.
- 強く打たないでください。 (つよくうたないでください。) — Please do not hit it hard.

The past form is 打った (うった), and the negative is 打たない (うたない).
The polite form 打ちます (うちます) has ち where the dictionary form has つ.

## How the kanji combine

[打](../kanji/打.md) supplies the striking meaning; つ completes the native verb.
There are no two kanji meanings to add together. The reading here is うつ, not every reading listed on the character card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

叩く (たたく) overlaps in hitting, tapping, or knocking; both words can describe physical blows.
The particular object and expression matter, rather than a rule that one is always strong and the other weak.
[押す](押す.md) (おす), "push," describes applying pressure rather than necessarily delivering a blow.

## Distinctive meaning

Hitting a ball is 打つ; [投げる](投げる.md) (なげる) is throwing it.
These verbs distinguish actions performed on the same object.
打つ has many additional dictionary senses, so the physical "hit" meaning is a starting point, not a universal translation.
