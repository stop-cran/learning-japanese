# 南口 (みなみぐち) — south exit

## Meaning

南口 is the south exit or south entrance, mostly of a station: 南口で待ち合わせる（まちあわせる） (meet at the south exit), 駅の南口, 新宿駅南口.
Counterparts: 北口 きたぐち (north exit), 東口 ひがしぐち and [西口](西口.md) にしぐち.

## How the kanji combine

[南](../kanji/南.md) (south, kun みなみ) plus [口](../kanji/口.md) (mouth, here "opening, entrance", kun くち, voiced to ぐち by rendaku): a modifier-plus-noun compound. Both kanji have kun readings, so it is a wago compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: 北口 きたぐち.
- Related: 出口 でぐち (exit), 入口 いりぐち (entrance), 改札口 かいさつぐち (ticket gate).

## Distinctive meaning

Large stations often have exits labelled by compass direction. 口 here is any opening, not a mouth. Compare 南口 and 南側 みなみがわ ("south side"), which describes a side rather than a door.
