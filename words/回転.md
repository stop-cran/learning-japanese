# 回転 (かいてん) - rotation; turnover

## Meaning

回転 names rotation or spinning. 回転する describes something rotating; the rotating thing takes が.

- ファンがゆっくり回転しています。 (ファンがゆっくりかいてんしています。) - The fan is rotating slowly.
- この店は客の回転が速いです。 (このみせはきゃくのかいてんがはやいです。) - Customers come and go quickly at this shop.

The second is customer turnover, not customers physically spinning. 頭の回転が速い (あたまのかいてんがはやい) means "be quick-thinking." Money or goods can also circulate or turn over.

## How the kanji combine

[回](../kanji/回.md) contributes going around and [転](../kanji/転.md) turning. The related movement ideas reinforce one another: カイ + テン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[回る](回る.md) (まわる) is a native intransitive verb for turning or going around. [回す](回す.md) (まわす) means to turn something; compare ファンを回す (ファンをまわす), "run the fan." [回り](回り.md) (まわり) can also name rotation. [くるくる](くるくる.md) depicts spinning or going round and round.

## Distinctive meaning

The object pattern of 回す does not automatically transfer to 回転する. Start with 車輪が回転する (しゃりんがかいてんする), "the wheel rotates."

In mathematics, 回転 can name a rotation or curl, depending on the subject. In skiing it can abbreviate the slalom event. These are specialized uses, not reasons to translate every 回転 as a race or to read every "turnover" as employee replacement.
