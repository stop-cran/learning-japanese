# 組織 (そしき) - organization; tissue

## Meaning

組織 can be an organization or the structure in which its parts work together.
It can also describe an organized system, such as a transport system.
組織する means to organize or form a group: 委員会を組織する
(いいんかいをそしきする), "form a committee."

- 地域を支援する組織を作りました。 (ちいきをしえんするそしきをつくりました。) - We created an organization to support the community.
- 顕微鏡で筋肉の組織を調べました。 (けんびきょうできんにくのそしきをしらべました。) - We examined muscle tissue under a microscope.

The second sentence uses the biological noun sense, "tissue," not a group of people.

## How the kanji combine

[組](../kanji/組.md) contributes putting together or grouping, and 織 contributes
weaving. Their combination offers a useful image of parts arranged into a whole.
The on readings are そ + しき. This image connects meanings without making
biology a literal account of weaving cloth.

## Synonyms and antonyms

A [会合](会合.md) (かいごう) is a gathering; it need not be an enduring
organization. A [会社](会社.md) (かいしゃ) is a company, one possible kind
of organization. An organization can exist without being a business.
There is no single antonym that works for both social organizations and tissue.

## Distinctive meaning

会社の組織 (かいしゃのそしき) can mean a company's organizational structure,
not another company it owns. Check whether the sentence concerns the whole group,
its arrangement, a system, or biological tissue. English "tissue" here refers
to living material, not a disposable paper tissue.
