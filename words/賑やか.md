# 賑やか (にぎやか) — lively; bustling

## Meaning

賑やか describes a **lively or bustling** place, event, or atmosphere, often with people and sound. It is a な-adjective: 賑やかな町 (にぎやかなまち), “a lively town.”

- 駅の前は賑やかです。 (えきのまえはにぎやかです。) — The area in front of the station is bustling.
- 昨日は賑やかなパーティーでした。 (きのうはにぎやかなぱーてぃーでした。) — Yesterday's party was lively.

Use 賑やかに for manner, as in 賑やかに話す (にぎやかにはなす), “talk animatedly.”

## How the kanji combine

賑 writes にぎ, with やか in kana. The kana spelling にぎやか is also useful. The whole adjective is 賑やか; do not treat やか as a freely removable ending.

## Synonyms and antonyms

静か (しずか), “quiet,” is the practical contrast. うるさい evaluates noise as bothersome. 賑やか can be positive or neutral and does not automatically complain about the sound.

## Distinctive meaning

English “busy” can describe an overloaded schedule, but 賑やか describes liveliness. Use 忙しい (いそがしい) for having much to do. A person can be busy working alone in a quiet room.
