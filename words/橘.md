# 橘 (たちばな) - tachibana orange; tachibana tree

## Meaning

橘 names the **tachibana orange**, *Citrus tachibana*, or its tree. This is an evergreen citrus: the tree keeps its leaves through the seasons. Its white flowers and small fruit occur in botanical descriptions.

- 橘の花は白いです。 (たちばなのはなはしろいです。) - Tachibana flowers are white.
- 庭に橘の木があります。 (にわにたちばなのきがあります。) - There is a tachibana tree in the garden.

The wild tachibana's fruit is bitter and strongly sour. Older 橘 can instead refer to mandarins eaten as food; do not infer that every historical fruit reference concerns exactly the modern plant described here.

## How the kanji combine

[橘](../kanji/橘.md) writes たちばな as a whole, without okurigana. There is no compound to divide. In 橘の木 (たちばなのき), の connects the name to [木](木.md), tree.

柑橘類 (かんきつるい) means citrus or citrus fruit as a category, with 橘 using キツ. That on reading is not a replacement for たちばな in the independent plant noun. In 花橘 (はなたちばな), [花](花.md), flower, identifies a blooming tachibana or its flower.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[植物](植物.md) (しょくぶつ), plant, and [木](木.md) (き), tree, are broader categories. [果実](果実.md) (かじつ) and [実](実.md) (み) can describe fruit on a tree, without implying that it is suitable as food. There is no direct antonym.

みかん is a mandarin, especially the satsuma mandarin in the dictionary's first sense; its broader sense can also refer to citrus. [柚](柚.md) (ゆず), yuzu, is another specific citrus. Neither name should automatically replace 橘 when identifying the particular tachibana plant.

## Distinctive meaning

In older writing 橘 can also refer to its blossom or a crest designed from its leaves and fruit. The dictionary records fragrance-related historical uses and names separately. The examples here select the plant, not a crest, perfume or person.

Learn the precise name **tachibana** rather than treating the English label "orange" as a guarantee of an ordinary sweet dessert fruit. Context separates this plant, broader historical citrus naming and the family name Tachibana.
