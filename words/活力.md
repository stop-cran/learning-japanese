# 活力 (かつりょく) - vitality; energy

## Meaning

活力 is vitality, energy, or dynamism: the power to act vigorously. It is a noun and can describe a person, an organization, or a community.

- 十分な睡眠で活力を取り戻しました。 (じゅうぶんなすいみんでかつりょくをとりもどしました。) - I regained my energy through sufficient sleep.
- 新しい店が町に活力を与えています。 (あたらしいみせがまちにかつりょくをあたえています。) - New shops are bringing vitality to the town.

Useful patterns are 活力がある, have vitality, 活力を取り戻す, regain vitality, and 活力にあふれる, be full of energy.

## How the kanji combine

[活](../kanji/活.md) contributes activity or life, and [力](../kanji/力.md) strength or power. The compound names the power behind lively activity. The on readings are カツ + リョク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[活気](活気.md) (かっき) also describes liveliness or vigor, often apparent in a place or group. [元気](元気.md) (げんき) overlaps in a person's energy or well-being. [体力](体力.md) (たいりょく) emphasizes physical strength or stamina in its ordinary bodily use. These are tendencies, not mutually exclusive kinds of energy.

## Distinctive meaning

[エネルギー](エネルギー.md) can also mean energy in a technical physical sense. 活力 instead focuses on vitality or dynamism; it is not the general name for a quantity measured in joules. Compare [活躍](活躍.md) (かつやく), energetic activity or an active contribution.
