# 表彰 (ひょうしょう) — official commendation; public honoring

## Meaning

表彰 is the public recognition of someone’s achievements or good deeds, for example with a certificate, 表彰状 (ひょうしょうじょう). 表彰する means to honor someone in this way.

- 三十年働いた社員が、式で表彰された。 (さんじゅうねんはたらいたしゃいんが、しきでひょうしょうされた。) — An employee who had worked there for thirty years was honored at a ceremony.
- 川でおぼれた子どもを助けた男性に、警察が表彰状を渡した。 (かわでおぼれたこどもをたすけただんせいに、けいさつがひょうしょうじょうをわたした。) — The police presented a certificate of commendation to a man who had rescued a drowning child from the river.

## How the kanji combine

[表](../kanji/表.md) (ひょう) means to show or make public, and [彰](../kanji/彰.md) (しょう) means to make clear. Together they describe making someone’s merit known to others.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[賞](賞.md) (しょう) is the prize or award itself; 表彰 is the act of honoring, and a 賞 may be given as part of it. [ほめる](ほめる.md) (ほめる) is everyday praise and need not be public or official.

## Distinctive meaning

In the first example the passive 表彰された makes the employee the one who receives the honor.
