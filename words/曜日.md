# 曜日 (ようび) - day of the week

## Meaning

曜日 is a day of the week, such as Monday or Sunday. It is a noun. Ask 何曜日 (なんようび) to find out which weekday is meant; the answer is a weekday name, not a calendar date.

- 今日は何曜日ですか。 (きょうはなんようびですか。) - What day of the week is it today?
- 曜日によって営業時間が違います。 (ようびによってえいぎょうじかんがちがいます。) - Opening hours differ depending on the day of the week.

によって expresses variation according to the weekday in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

[曜](../kanji/曜.md) identifies the weekday category, and [日](../kanji/日.md) contributes "day." The reading is mixed: よう is an on reading, while び is the voiced form of the kun reading ひ. The repository groups this on-first mixed reading under kango.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[日付](日付.md) (ひづけ) is the date, not the weekday. [月曜日](月曜日.md) (げつようび), "Monday," and [日曜日](日曜日.md) (にちようび), "Sunday," are members of the weekday set, not synonyms for the whole category.

## Distinctive meaning

English "weekday" sometimes excludes Saturday and Sunday, but 曜日 covers the seven-day cycle. If someone asks 何曜日ですか, answering a month and day does not supply the requested information. Distinguish 何日 (なんにち), "what date/how many days," by its context.
