# 休暇 (きゅうか) - vacation; leave; time off

## Meaning

休暇 is a noun for time off, leave, or a vacation. **休暇を取る** (きゅうかをとる) means "take time off." **休暇中** (きゅうかちゅう) means "on leave; during a vacation."

- 来週、三日間の休暇を取ります。 (らいしゅう、みっかかんのきゅうかをとります。) - I will take three days off next week.
- 休暇中は家族と過ごしました。 (きゅうかちゅうはかぞくとすごしました。) - I spent my vacation with my family.

The noun names the period away from ordinary duties; it does not by itself specify whether the leave is paid.

## How the kanji combine

[休](../kanji/休.md) contributes rest or a break, while 暇 contributes free time or leisure. Together, キュウ + カ names time released from duties. Keep the long きゅう in the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[休み](休み.md) (やすみ) is a broader word for a break, rest, or day off. [休日](休日.md) (きゅうじつ) focuses on a non-working day. 休暇 can cover a planned stretch of leave rather than a single calendar day.

## Distinctive meaning

A vacation need not involve travel: the second example can describe staying at home. In workplace contexts, 休暇を申請する (きゅうかをしんせいする) means "apply for leave." Choose the English translation from the arrangement, rather than assuming that every 休暇 is a public holiday.
