# 内科 (ないか) - internal medicine

## Meaning

内科 is internal medicine, or the department providing that
medical care. It commonly concerns diagnosing and treating
internal or systemic illnesses, especially through nonsurgical
medical treatment.

- 熱があるので、内科を受診しました。 (ねつがあるので、ないかをじゅしんしました。) - Because I had a fever, I consulted the internal medicine department.
- この病院には内科と外科があります。 (このびょういんにはないかとげかがあります。) - This hospital has internal medicine and surgery departments.

内科を受診する means to consult that department or seek its
care. 内科医 (ないかい) is an internist, the doctor rather than
the field or department.

## How the kanji combine

[内](../kanji/内.md) contributes "inside; internal," and
[科](../kanji/科.md) contributes "department; branch."
The modifier-plus-head compound uses ナイ + カ.
It does not mean every department physically inside a building.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[外科](外科.md) (げか) is surgery or a surgical department.
[医療](医療.md) (いりょう) is medical care more broadly.
[病院](病院.md) (びょういん) is a hospital, which may contain
several departments, including 内科.

## Distinctive meaning

An 内科 sign can identify the specialty offered by a clinic.
The word itself is not the name of a doctor or a guarantee of a
large hospital. Which department is appropriate for a particular
symptom depends on the actual medical situation and services.
