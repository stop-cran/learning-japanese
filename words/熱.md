# 熱 (ねつ) - fever; heat

## Meaning

熱 means heat. In health-related conversation it commonly means a fever.

- 熱があるので、今日は休みます。 (ねつがあるので、きょうはやすみます。) - I have a fever, so I will take the day off.
- 朝、熱を測りました。 (あさ、ねつをはかりました。) - I took my temperature in the morning.

熱がある is the normal expression for having a fever. The person affected can be understood from context.

## How the kanji combine

[熱](../kanji/熱.md) is a single character used with the on reading ネツ. The related adjective 熱い (あつい), "hot," uses a different reading and grammar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

体温 (たいおん) names body temperature, whether normal or elevated. 発熱 (はつねつ) refers to developing a fever or generating heat, depending on context.

## Distinctive meaning

熱が下がる (ねつがさがる), "the fever goes down," concerns temperature. 熱 can also mean enthusiasm, but the health expressions above do not imply excitement or passion. The surrounding verb helps identify the intended sense.
