# 案外 (あんがい) - unexpectedly; surprisingly

## Meaning

案外 says that something differs from what was expected. It works directly as
an adverb, or with と: 案外難しい and 案外と難しい (あんがいとむずかしい)
both mean "surprisingly difficult."

- この問題は案外簡単でした。 (このもんだいはあんがいかんたんでした。) - This problem was surprisingly easy.
- 駅までは案外時間がかかりました。 (えきまではあんがいじかんがかかりました。) - Getting to the station took longer than I expected.

## How the kanji combine

案 can concern an idea or plan, and [外](../kanji/外.md) contributes "outside."
As a learning association, the result lies outside the expectation.
The on readings are アン + ガイ; this is not an etymological explanation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意外](意外.md) (いがい) also concerns the unexpected and has the adverbial forms
意外に and 意外と. 予想どおり (よそうどおり), "as expected," supplies a useful
contextual opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The surprise can be welcome or unwelcome. 案外 does not itself mean that an event
happened suddenly: an unexpectedly long journey can be gradual.
The dictionary also records na-adjective use; 案外な結果 (あんがいなけっか)
means an unexpected result. Do not add な to the ordinary adverbial pattern 案外難しい.
