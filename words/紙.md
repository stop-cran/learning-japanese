# 紙 (かみ) — paper

## Meaning

紙 means paper as a material or a piece of paper. Sheets are commonly counted with 枚 (まい), the counter for flat objects.

- 紙に名前を書いてください。 (かみになまえをかいてください。) — Please write your name on the paper.
- 紙を一枚ください。 (かみをいちまいください。) — Please give me one sheet of paper.

## How the kanji combine

This is one native word written with [紙](../kanji/紙.md), read かみ, with no okurigana.
In 手紙 (てがみ, letter), [手](../kanji/手.md) is followed by the voiced form がみ.
Learn 手紙 as the whole word "letter," not simply as any paper held in a hand.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 用紙 (ようし) is paper for a use or a blank form, a more specific word than the material 紙.
- 手紙 (てがみ) is a letter, and [新聞](新聞.md) (しんぶん) is a newspaper; these are related written items, not general synonyms for paper.
- Paper as a material has no natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

髪 (かみ, hair) and 神 (かみ, god or deity) have the same kana spelling as 紙, but are different words.
Context and kanji distinguish them in writing; the shared kana spelling is not a claim that their pitch patterns are identical.
This article selects かみ, not the suffix reading シ found in other uses of 紙.
