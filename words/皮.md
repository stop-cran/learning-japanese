# 皮 (かわ) - skin; peel; outer covering

## Meaning

皮 names an outer covering: skin or an animal's hide, a fruit's peel, tree bark, or a wrapping. Use **Nの皮** to identify what it covers, and 皮をむく for peeling something.

- みかんの皮をむきました。 (みかんのかわをむきました。) - I peeled the mandarin.
- 餃子の皮で具を包みます。 (ぎょうざのかわでぐをつつみます。) - Wrap the filling in a dumpling wrapper.

魚の皮 (さかなのかわ), "fish skin," and 木の皮 (きのかわ), "tree bark," illustrate how the covered thing determines the English noun.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 皮 uses the native reading かわ. There is no compound to decompose. Its central outer-covering idea connects the food, plant, and animal uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[革](革.md), also read かわ, specifically names leather. Distinguish skin or hide as an outer covering from leather as a processed material; do not assume identical readings make the spellings freely interchangeable. 皮膚 (ひふ) is the more anatomical word for skin.

## Distinctive meaning

皮 is broader than a person's skin but is not a universal translation of English "shell." Learn the actual object and collocation. The source list includes "leather" in its mixed gloss; the inspected dictionary separates the leather identity under 革.
