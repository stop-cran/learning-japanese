# 出す (だす)

## Meaning

出す means to take something out, put it out, or bring it into view. It also appears in everyday expressions for submitting, sending, or producing something.

- かばんから本を出しました。 (かばんから ほんを だしました。) — I took a book out of my bag.
- 明日、宿題を出します。 (あした、しゅくだいを だします。) — I will hand in my homework tomorrow.

The thing moved or submitted takes を. It is a godan verb: 出します (だします), 出して (だして), 出さない (ださない).

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) carries the outward movement association and supplies だ; す completes the native verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

出る (でる) is intransitive: something comes out or someone leaves. 入れる (いれる), "put in," contrasts with taking something out.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the object along with the verb: 手紙を出す (てがみをだす) means send a letter; 声を出す (こえをだす) means produce a voice or sound. English "put out a fire" instead uses 消す (けす), not 出す.
