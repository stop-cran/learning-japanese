# ピン (ピン) - pin

## Meaning

ピン is a noun for a pin, including a fastening pin.
It also names a bowling pin, and in golf it can mean the flagstick.
The surrounding activity identifies the object.
**ピンで留める** (ピンでとめる) means to fasten something with a pin.

- 布をピンで留めました。 (ぬのをピンでとめました。) - I fastened the cloth with pins.
- 最後のピンが倒れました。 (さいごのピンがたおれました。) - The last bowling pin fell over.

The first example uses で for the fastening tool.
The bowling context supplies the second example's sense; ピン alone does not specify its sport.
In golf, ピンまでの距離 (ピンまでのきょり) is "the distance to the flagstick."

## How the kanji combine

The assigned word is the katakana loanword ピン and has no kanji.
Its spelling ends in ン, with no long-vowel mark.
Do not infer a kanji origin or combine unrelated same-sounding expressions with this entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[針](針.md) (はり) is a needle and overlaps in shape or function with some pins.
安全ピン (あんぜんピン), "safety pin," is a specific kind.
These objects are not interchangeable in every task.

## Distinctive meaning

A small fastening pin, a bowling pin, and a golf flagstick are distinct objects sharing this word.
The entry does not make ピン a general translation for all English uses of "pin."
In particular, do not confuse these object senses with the English acronym PIN for an access code.
