# 外国人 (がいこくじん) — foreign national

## Meaning

外国人 means a foreign person or foreign national. In a Japanese setting it commonly means someone who is not Japanese. The relevant country comes from context, not from appearance or language ability.

この町には外国人も住んでいます。 (このまちにはがいこくじんもすんでいます。) — Foreign nationals also live in this town.

## How the kanji combine

[外](../kanji/外.md), がい, "outside," and [国](../kanji/国.md), こく, "country," form 外国, "foreign country." [人](../kanji/人.md), じん, adds the person: がいこくじん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

日本人 (にほんじん), "Japanese person," is the relevant contrast in a Japanese context. 外国の人 (がいこくのひと) is a less compact alternative. 外人 (がいじん) is shortened and can sound excluding or disrespectful.

## Distinctive meaning

外国人 does not mean "tourist": a foreign national may be a long-term resident. Use a person's name or a relevant nationality when possible rather than assuming this broad category is always the most helpful description.
