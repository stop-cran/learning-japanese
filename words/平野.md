# 平野 (へいや) - geographic plain

## Meaning

平野 is a geographic plain: a broad, relatively flat area of land.
It is a noun. The landscape can contain farms, towns or other land uses; it need
not be an empty or uncultivated field.

- 関東平野には多くの都市があります。 (かんとうへいやにはおおくのとしがあります。) - There are many cities on the Kanto Plain.
- 川に沿って平野が広がっています。 (かわにそってへいやがひろがっています。) - A plain extends along the river.

## How the kanji combine

[平](../kanji/平.md) contributes flatness; [野](../kanji/野.md) contributes
open land. This modifier-and-land association gives ヘイ + ヤ.
The whole word's reading here is へいや.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[原](原.md) (はら) and [野](野.md) (の) also concern open fields or plains.
平原 (へいげん) is another broad plain term; 平地 (へいち) emphasizes flat land.
[畑](畑.md) (はたけ) is a cultivated field, not the whole geographic landform.
山地 (さんち), "mountainous area," is a useful contrasting landscape.

## Distinctive meaning

This is a landform term, not [分野](分野.md) (ぶんや), a field of activity or
specialization. Nor does English "plain" here mean simple or unadorned.
平野 can also appear as ひらの in names; a surname or place-name heading does not
change the へいや reading of this common geographic noun.
