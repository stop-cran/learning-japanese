# 基づく (もとづく) - be based on; arise from

## Meaning

基づく is an intransitive godan verb meaning to be based on, grounded in, or in accordance with something.
It can also identify what something arises from.
Use **basis + に基づく**. Before another action, **に基づいて** means "on the basis of."

- この映画は実話に基づいています。 (このえいがはじつわにもとづいています。) - This film is based on a true story.
- 調査の結果に基づいて計画を変えました。 (ちょうさのけっかにもとづいてけいかくをかえました。) - We changed the plan on the basis of the survey results.

For a cause or origin, 誤解に基づく対立 (ごかいにもとづくたいりつ) is "conflict arising from a misunderstanding."
Conjugations include 基づきます, 基づかない, 基づいた, and 基づいて.

## How the kanji combine

基 contributes a foundation or basis, read もと here.
The complete verb is **もとづく**, with **づ**, not ず.
Preserve that spelling in forms such as 基づいて.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[基](基.md) (もと) names a basis in relevant contexts; 基づく states the relationship to it.
をもとにして is another way to express using something as a basis.
[従う](従う.md) (したがう) can overlap when acting according to a rule, but also has other uses.

## Distinctive meaning

The basis takes に, not を: facts, rules, experience, or evidence support what follows.
**に基づく + noun**, as in 事実に基づく説明 (じじつにもとづくせつめい), means "an explanation based on facts."
The expression signals a claimed foundation; it does not independently guarantee that the facts or conclusion are correct.
