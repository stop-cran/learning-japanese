# 過程 (かてい) - process; course of development

## Meaning

過程 is a process or the course through which something develops or reaches a result. It is a noun.

- 製品が完成するまでの過程を説明します。 (せいひんがかんせいするまでのかていをせつめいします。) - I will explain the process leading up to the product's completion.
- 議論の過程で、新しい問題が見つかりました。 (ぎろんのかていで、あたらしいもんだいがみつかりました。) - A new problem was discovered in the course of the discussion.

Xの過程で means "in the course of X"; 過程を経る (かていをへる) means to go through a process.

## How the kanji combine

[過](../kanji/過.md) contributes passing through, and [程](../kanji/程.md) a course or extent. Together they suggest the course passed through on the way to a result. The on readings are カ + テイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[流れ](流れ.md) (ながれ) can describe the flow or course of events. 手順 (てじゅん) focuses on an order of steps or procedure; [結果](結果.md) (けっか), a result, is a useful contrast to the process leading to it.

## Distinctive meaning

過程 concerns how something unfolds, not just the final outcome. [課程](課程.md) (かてい) is a curriculum or course of study, while [仮定](仮定.md) (かてい) is an assumption. The shared reading and English "course" do not make the written words interchangeable.
