# 片仮名 (かたかな) - katakana

## Meaning

片仮名 names katakana, one of the Japanese kana syllabaries. The word is usually written カタカナ; 片仮名 is its kanji spelling. Both are pronounced かたかな. Loanwords, many mimetic words, and some emphasis or naming uses are written with this script.

- 名前を片仮名で書いてください。 (なまえをかたかなでかいてください。) - Please write your name in katakana.
- 「コーヒー」は片仮名で書きます。 (「コーヒー」はかたかなでかきます。) - We write "coffee" as コーヒー in katakana.

The script name is not a statement that every word written with it is foreign.

## How the kanji combine

[片](../kanji/片.md) contributes a part or fragment; 仮名 (かな) is kana, a writing-system term. Within that term, 仮 relates to provisional or borrowed use and [名](../kanji/名.md) to naming; learn the contracted 仮名 reading かな as a unit rather than inventing freely reusable individual readings. Many katakana shapes developed from parts of kanji, but that is not an exceptionless account of every shape.

## Synonyms and antonyms

平仮名 (ひらがな) names hiragana, the other kana syllabary. [漢字](漢字.md) (かんじ) names kanji. These are different writing systems used together, not translations of one another.

## Distinctive meaning

The spellings 片仮名, カタカナ, and かたかな name the same script. The last spelling uses hiragana characters to write the name; it does not change the meaning to "hiragana."
