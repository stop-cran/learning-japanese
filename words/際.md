# 際 (さい) - occasion; time of an event

## Meaning

際 is a noun used for the occasion or time when something happens. The pattern ～際に or ～際は means "when..." or "on the occasion of..." It suits instructions and notices as well as descriptions of particular events.

Attach it to a noun with の, or directly to a verb's plain nonpast or past form: 登録の際, 利用する際, 訪れた際.

- 登録の際に、住所を書いてください。 (とうろくのさいに、じゅうしょをかいてください。) - When registering, please write your address.
- 電車を降りる際は、忘れ物に注意してください。 (でんしゃをおりるさいは、わすれものにちゅういしてください。) - When getting off the train, please take care not to leave anything behind.

## How the kanji combine

[際](../kanji/際.md) is a single kanji, read with the on reading サイ here. Learn this event-related word separately from other readings and boundary-related uses of the character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時](時.md) (とき) is a broadly useful "when; time" expression. [場合](場合.md) (ばあい), "case; situation," can emphasize a condition rather than the occasion of an action. They overlap but are not automatic replacements.

## Distinctive meaning

The tense of the verb before 際 helps establish timing; 際 itself does not conjugate. 来た際に (きたさいに) refers to the occasion of a visit expressed with 来た, while 来る際に (くるさいに) can introduce instructions for an upcoming visit.
