# もっとも (もっとも) - reasonable; but then

## Meaning

もっとも has two related practical uses in this entry. As a な-adjective it means reasonable, natural, or justified: もっともな意見 (もっともないけん), "a reasonable opinion." As a conjunction it introduces a qualification, "though/but then."

- それはもっともな質問です。 (それはもっともなしつもんです。) - That is a reasonable question.
- 歩いて行けます。もっとも、一時間かかります。 (あるいていけます。もっとも、いちじかんかかります。) - You can walk there. It does take an hour, though.

もっともです acknowledges that a point is reasonable. It does not mean that it has the highest degree of some quality.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses the usual kana spelling. 尤も, retained in the community source, is marked as a rare kanji spelling. Learn it as an alias of this entry rather than requiring kanji for everyday use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[最も](最も.md), also read もっとも, is the separate superlative adverb "most." Compare もっともな意見, "a reasonable opinion," with 最も重要な意見 (もっともじゅうようないけん), "the most important opinion."

## Distinctive meaning

The conjunction adds a caveat to what was just said; it need not reject that statement. In the walking example, walking remains possible, but its duration matters. "Reasonable" likewise means that a position makes sense, not that every factual detail has independently been proven.
