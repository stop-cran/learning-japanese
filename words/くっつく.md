# くっつく - stick to; keep close to

## Meaning

くっつく means stick or cling to something, or stay close beside someone. It is intransitive: the thing sticking takes が; what it sticks to takes に.

- ガムが靴の底にくっつきました。 (ガムがくつのそこにくっつきました。) - Gum stuck to the sole of my shoe.
- 子供がお母さんにくっついて歩いています。 (こどもがおかあさんにくっついてあるいています。) - The child is walking close beside their mother.

The godan forms include くっつきます, くっついて, くっつかない.

## How the kanji combine

This word is usually written in kana. The dictionary also records くっ付く, containing [付](../kanji/付.md), attach. This spelling is still read くっつく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くっつける](くっつける.md) is transitive: something is made to stick or is placed against something else. [付く](付く.md) (つく) also means be attached and has many other established uses.

[貼る](貼る.md) (はる), stick or paste something on, describes an action on an object. It is not the same grammatical construction as ガムがくっつく.

## Distinctive meaning

Keeping close need not involve glue or permanent attachment. An additional figurative use concerns becoming intimate or involved with someone. Context must distinguish physical contact, accompanying a person and a personal relationship.
