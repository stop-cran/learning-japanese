# 大戦 (たいせん) - major war; world war

## Meaning

大戦 is a great or large-scale war or battle. It also serves as a shortened reference to a world war, especially when the surrounding historical discussion identifies which one.

- 授業で第二次世界大戦について学びました。 (じゅぎょうでだいにじせかいたいせんについてまなびました。) - We studied the Second World War in class.
- その本は大戦後の社会を描いています。 (そのほんはたいせんごのしゃかいをえがいています。) - That book portrays society after the war.

In the second example, 大戦後 means "after the great/world war." The sentence alone does not identify which war.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes great or large-scale, and [戦](../kanji/戦.md) contributes war or battle. The first element specifies the scale of the second. The on readings here are タイ + セン, not ダイセン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[戦争](戦争.md) (せんそう) is the broader word "war." [戦い](戦い.md) (たたかい) can mean a battle or struggle. [平和](平和.md) (へいわ), "peace," contrasts with the state of war.

## Distinctive meaning

"Great" in this gloss concerns size or historical importance, not praise. Also distinguish 対戦 (たいせん), a contest or competing against an opponent. The shared pronunciation does not make a sports match a 大戦 in ordinary descriptive language.
