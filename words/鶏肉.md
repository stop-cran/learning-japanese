# 鶏肉 (とりにく) — chicken meat

## Meaning

鶏肉 is chicken as meat or food, rather than the living bird.

- 今日は鶏肉と野菜を炒めます。 (きょうはとりにくとやさいをいためます。) — Today I'll stir-fry chicken and vegetables.
- このスープには鶏肉が入っています。 (このスープにはとりにくがはいっています。) — This soup contains chicken.

Use 鶏肉を食べる (とりにくをたべる), "eat chicken," or 鶏肉の + noun for a dish or other noun associated with it.

## How the kanji combine

鶏 identifies chicken, and [肉](../kanji/肉.md) means meat. The reading combines native とり with on ニク. This native-first mixed compound is classified as wago here; that does not make にく a kun reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

チキン is also used for chicken, especially in dish names. 牛肉 (ぎゅうにく), "beef," and 豚肉 (ぶたにく), "pork," identify other meats.

## Distinctive meaning

鶏 (にわとり) is the chicken as a bird. 鳥肉 is another dictionary spelling of とりにく, but it also has a broader poultry or bird-meat sense. The spelling 鶏肉 specifically points learners to chicken.
