# 肌着 (はだぎ) - next-to-skin underclothes

## Meaning

肌着 is clothing worn next to the skin, such as an undershirt.
It is a noun, not the action of putting clothes on.

- 寒いので、暖かい肌着を着ました。 (さむいので、あたたかいはだぎをきました。) - Because it was cold, I put on warm underclothes.
- 肌着を毎日洗います。 (はだぎをまいにちあらいます。) - I wash my underclothes every day.

## How the kanji combine

[肌](../kanji/肌.md) はだ means skin; [着](../kanji/着.md) appears in the clothing word 着 き.
Here き becomes voiced ぎ: はだ + ぎ. The compound names what is worn against the skin,
not a reading assembled from the on-readings of the two characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下着](下着.md) (したぎ) is the broader overlapping word for underwear; it is not restricted to lower-body garments.
[シャツ](シャツ.md) can name an undershirt or an outer shirt, so not every シャツ is 肌着.
[上着](上着.md) (うわぎ) is an outer garment.

## Distinctive meaning

The next-to-skin function is the useful focus. Do not translate every item of underwear as a shirt,
or assume that 肌着 means any garment merely because it touches the body somewhere.
