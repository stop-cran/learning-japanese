# うじゃうじゃ — teeming; gathered in clusters

## Meaning

うじゃうじゃ describes many similar things gathered together or moving about in a crowded group.

- 水たまりの近くに小さな虫がうじゃうじゃ集まっていた。
  — Tiny insects had gathered in a swarm near the puddle.
- 夏の夜、草むらに蛍がうじゃうじゃ飛んでいた。
  — On the summer night, fireflies were flying all around the grass.

## Synonyms and antonyms

うじゃうじゃ emphasizes a dense, plentiful group. A line or procession of people is better
described by a word that focuses on their orderly movement rather than their number.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can sound negative when the speaker dislikes or is overwhelmed by the crowd. It can also
describe a large number without implying that the group is dangerous.
