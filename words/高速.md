# 高速 (こうそく) - high speed; expressway

## Meaning

高速 means high speed or high-speed. Useful forms include 高速の列車
(こうそくのれっしゃ), "a high-speed train," and 高速で, "at high speed."
As a noun in travel contexts, 高速 also abbreviates 高速道路
(こうそくどうろ), "expressway."

- この機械は高速で動きます。 (このきかいはこうそくでうごきます。) - This machine operates at high speed.
- 空港までは高速を使いました。 (くうこうまではこうそくをつかいました。) - We took the expressway to the airport.

The second example names a road, not an amount of speed.

## How the kanji combine

[高](../kanji/高.md) supplies "high" and [速](../kanji/速.md) supplies "speed;
fast." The first qualifies the second: speed at a high level.
The on readings combine as こう + そく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[速い](速い.md) (はやい) is the basic adjective "fast."
[速度](速度.md) (そくど) names speed without saying it is high.
低速 (ていそく), "low speed," is a direct contrast in the speed sense,
but it does not reverse the expressway abbreviation into a name for every other road.

## Distinctive meaning

Look at the predicate to distinguish meanings: 高速で走る describes speed,
while 高速に乗る (こうそくにのる) can mean entering an expressway.
The community gloss "high gear" should not be treated as a general replacement
for a vehicle's particular gear setting; this card teaches high speed and the
dictionary's expressway abbreviation.
