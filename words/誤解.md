# 誤解 (ごかい) — misunderstanding

## Meaning

誤解 is a misunderstanding: someone interprets something incorrectly.
It can be a noun or combine with する.

- 私の言葉を誤解しないでください。 (わたしのことばをごかいしないでください。) — Please do not misunderstand what I said.
- それは誤解です。 (それはごかいです。) — That is a misunderstanding.

In the first example, を marks the words being misunderstood.

## How the kanji combine

[誤](../kanji/誤.md), "mistaken," qualifies [解](../kanji/解.md), "understanding."
The error is in the interpretation. The on readings are ゴ + カイ, giving ごかい.
This is not simply a synonym for every kind of mistake.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[理解](../words/理解.md) (りかい) is understanding or comprehension. It gives the useful contrast,
but not understanding at all and misunderstanding are different situations:
誤解 suggests that the interpretation is wrong, rather than merely absent.

## Distinctive meaning

誤解 does not necessarily imply deliberate deception. It can arise even when nobody intended
to mislead anyone. A useful phrase is 誤解を解く (ごかいをとく), "clear up a misunderstanding":
解く here means resolving or dispelling it, not physically undoing an object.
