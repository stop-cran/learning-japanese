# 大体 (だいたい) - generally; approximately

## Meaning

大体 means "generally," "mostly," or "approximately." It gives a broad picture
rather than exact details. Hiragana だいたい is also usual.

- 宿題は大体終わりました。 (しゅくだいはだいたいおわりました。) - My homework is mostly finished.
- 駅まで大体十分かかります。 (えきまでだいたいじゅっぷんかかります。) - It takes about ten minutes to the station.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md), "large," and [体](../kanji/体.md), "body; whole," suggest
the broad whole rather than each detail. Their on-readings ダイ + タイ give
だいたい. This is a meaning aid, not a literal translation as "large body."

## Synonyms and antonyms

およそ also expresses approximation. 正確に (せいかくに), "exactly; accurately,"
is a useful contrast when discussing numbers. [全部](全部.md), "all," does not
leave the same room for unfinished parts.

## Distinctive meaning

As a noun modifier, use の: 大体の意味 (だいたいのいみ), "the general meaning."
The first example means that most work is done, not that it is certainly
complete. In a different conversational use, 大体 can introduce "to begin with."
