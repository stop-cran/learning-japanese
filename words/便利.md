# 便利 (べんり) — convenient; handy

## Meaning

便利 describes something convenient or handy for a purpose.
It is a な-adjective: use 便利な before a noun, 便利です at the end of a polite sentence,
and 便利に when describing how something is used.

- このアプリは買い物に便利です。 (このアプリはかいものにべんりです。) — This app is handy for shopping.
- 駅の近くに住むと便利です。 (えきのちかくにすむとべんりです。) — It is convenient to live near a station.

A useful noun phrase is 便利な道具 (べんりなどうぐ), "a handy tool."

## How the kanji combine

[便](../kanji/便.md) contributes convenience, and [利](../kanji/利.md) contributes benefit or advantage.
The related favorable meanings reinforce the idea of something being practical to use.
The on readings combine as ベン + リ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

不便 (ふべん), "inconvenient," is the direct contrast and also takes な before a noun.
役に立つ (やくにたつ), "be useful," overlaps with 便利 but can emphasize helping achieve a purpose.
Something may help solve a problem without being especially easy or convenient to use.

## Distinctive meaning

便利 evaluates practical convenience, not beauty, moral goodness, or price.
In the app example, 買い物に identifies the purpose for which it is handy.
When using it before a noun, keep the connector: 便利なアプリ (べんりなアプリ), not 便利のアプリ.
