# 唯一 (ゆいいつ) - only; sole; unique

## Meaning

唯一 expresses that there is just one relevant person, thing, or possibility. Before a noun, use 唯一の: 唯一の方法 (ゆいいつのほうほう), "the only method." It can also function adverbially.

- 読書が私の唯一の楽しみです。 (どくしょがわたしのゆいいつのたのしみです。) - Reading is my only pleasure.
- この問題を唯一解けたのは由美さんです。 (このもんだいをゆいいつとけたのはゆみさんです。) - Yumi was the only person who could solve this problem.

In the second example, 唯一 limits who succeeded. It does not mean that Yumi solved the problem just once.

## How the kanji combine

唯 contributes "only," and [一](../kanji/一.md) contributes "one." Together they emphasize a single qualifying instance. Learn the assigned reading ゆいいつ; ゆいつ is also an accepted dictionary reading, not a separate meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一つ](一つ.md) (ひとつ) counts one item. 唯一 adds the exclusion of other relevant candidates. [ただ](ただ.md) can express "just" in appropriate constructions, but has several other meanings and is not an unrestricted replacement.

## Distinctive meaning

The translation "unique" here means one of a kind within the relevant context, not merely unusual or interesting. 唯一の出口 (ゆいいつのでぐち) is the only exit, rather than an exit with an unusual design. Context supplies the set being limited; it need not mean the only such thing anywhere in the world.
