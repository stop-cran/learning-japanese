# 響く (ひびく) - resound; have an effect

## Meaning

響く means to resound or reverberate. It is an intransitive godan verb:
the sound or other thing resounding takes が. A place where the sound
is heard can take に.

- 鐘の音が町中に響きました。 (かねのおとがまちじゅうにひびきました。) - The bell's sound rang throughout the town.
- 先生の言葉が心に響きました。 (せんせいのことばがこころにひびきました。) - The teacher's words struck a chord with me.

Forms include 響きます, 響かない, 響いた and 響いて.
The verb can also describe vibration or something having an effect:
睡眠不足が仕事に響く (すいみんぶそくがしごとにひびく),
"Lack of sleep affects one's work."

## How the kanji combine

響 contributes resonance or vibration and is read ひび here;
く completes the native verb. [響き](響き.md) (ひびき) is the
related noun. This word is not read with the on reading キョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鳴る](鳴る.md) (なる) also describes sounding or ringing.
響く brings the sound's resonance or reach into focus.
[鳴らす](鳴らす.md) (ならす) instead describes making something sound:
鐘を鳴らす, ring a bell.
[影響](影響.md) (えいきょう)する overlaps in having an effect,
but uses its own construction.

## Distinctive meaning

心に響く concerns emotional impact or words that stay with someone.
The dictionary's English "come home" is figurative here, not returning
to one's house. The effect need not be favorable, as the sleep example shows.
Do not turn 仕事に響く into 仕事を響く by copying English transitive "affect."
The separate reading どよめく is not a free alternative in these examples.
