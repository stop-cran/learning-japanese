# アルバイト

## Meaning

アルバイト means a part-time or side job. It can also refer to a part-time worker. アルバイトする means to work part-time.

- 週末は喫茶店でアルバイトをしています。 (しゅうまつは きっさてんで アルバイトを しています。) — I work part-time at a cafe on weekends.
- アルバイトを探しています。 (アルバイトを さがしています。) — I am looking for a part-time job.

で marks the workplace in the first example.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loan comes from German Arbeit, "work." Japanese has developed a narrower everyday employment meaning; it is not the general word for all work.

## Synonyms and antonyms

バイト is the common shortened form. パート overlaps with part-time work, though employers' terminology varies. 正社員 (せいしゃいん), "regular employee," is a useful employment-status contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

An アルバイト is not necessarily a student's job or something done only on weekends. The label alone does not determine exact hours, duties, or employment rights. Use 仕事 (しごと) for work or a job in the broad sense.
