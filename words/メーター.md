# メーター - meter; gauge

## Meaning

メーター is a measuring instrument or gauge, including devices that display
electricity, gas, or water usage and a taxi's fare.
メータ is another recorded spelling.

- ガスのメーターを確認しました。 (がすのめーたーをかくにんしました。) - I checked the gas meter.
- タクシーのメーターに料金が表示されています。 (たくしーのめーたーにりょうきんがひょうじされています。) - The fare is displayed on the taxi meter.

The dictionary also records メーター for the length unit "meter."
It is therefore not correct to say that this spelling can only name an instrument.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[メートル](メートル.md) is the familiar length-unit word.
計器 (けいき) is a measuring instrument.
[計る](計る.md) (はかる) is a verb meaning measure or gauge in appropriate contexts,
not the name of the device.

## Distinctive meaning

As a practical tendency, メーター often suggests an instrument and メートル a unit of length.
Their recorded meanings overlap; this is not an absolute spelling rule.
A メーター need not be a [時計](時計.md) (とけい), "clock":
the gas meter measures usage and the taxi meter displays fare.
