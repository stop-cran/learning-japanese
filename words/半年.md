# 半年 (はんとし) — half a year

## Meaning

半年 is six months: 半年前 はんとしまえ (half a year ago), 半年かかる (it takes half a year), 半年間 はんとしかん／はんねんかん.

## How the kanji combine

[半](../kanji/半.md) (half) plus [年](../kanji/年.md) (year). The usual reading is はんとし, an on + kun mix (ハン + とし); はんねん also exists, mainly in compounds such as 半年間.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 六か月 ろっかげつ (six months, exact count), 半期 はんき (half of a fiscal year).
- Related: 一年半 いちねんはん (one and a half years).

## Distinctive meaning

半年 is the casual way to say six months. 六か月 is preferred when exactness matters.
