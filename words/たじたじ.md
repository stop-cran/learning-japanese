# たじたじ — faltering or overwhelmed

## Meaning

たじたじ describes faltering or feeling overwhelmed in the face of pressure or a formidable opponent.

- 強豪チームを前に、経験豊富な選手たちもたじたじだった。
  — Even the experienced players faltered in front of the powerhouse team.
- 質問が次々に飛んできて、彼はすっかりたじたじになった。
  — Questions came one after another, and he was completely overwhelmed.

## Synonyms and antonyms

たじたじ describes faltering under pressure, not simply being surprised or briefly unsure.

## Distinctive meaning

It describes a reaction to pressure, not ordinary hesitation about a small choice.
