# 浴衣 (ゆかた) - yukata; light cotton kimono

## Meaning

浴衣 is a light cotton kimono, worn in summer and also as a bathrobe
or informal garment at inns and similar places.

- 夏祭りに浴衣を着て行きました。 (なつまつりにゆかたをきていきました。) - I wore a yukata to the summer festival.
- 旅館の部屋に浴衣が用意されていました。 (りょかんのへやにゆかたがよういされていました。) - A yukata had been provided in the inn's room.

## How the kanji combine

[浴](../kanji/浴.md) evokes bathing and [衣](../kanji/衣.md) clothing.
ゆかた is the special reading of the whole compound, not separate
regular readings ゆ for 浴 and かた for 衣.
The historical connection is to 湯帷子 (ゆかたびら), a bathing garment.
The dictionary also recognizes the on-reading form よくい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[着物](着物.md) (きもの) is a broader kimono category;
浴衣 specifies this light, informal garment.
[衣服](衣服.md) (いふく) is clothing generally.
[寝間着](寝間着.md) (ねまき) describes sleepwear by its use,
not one particular garment construction.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every kimono as 浴衣.
The festival and inn examples show two uses of the same word,
not separate summer-only and bath-only lexical identities.
There is no useful universal garment antonym.
