# 南米 (なんべい) - South America

## Meaning

南米 is a noun meaning South America.
It is an abbreviated geographical name, not the southern part of the United States.

- 来年、南米を旅行します。 (らいねん、なんべいをりょこうします。) - Next year, I will travel around South America.
- ブラジルは南米にあります。 (ブラジルはなんべいにあります。) - Brazil is in South America.

南米の国々 (なんべいのくにぐに) means "the countries of South America."

## How the kanji combine

[南](../kanji/南.md) contributes "south," modifying the America-related
abbreviation [米](../kanji/米.md). The on readings are ナン + ベイ.
米 here does not mean rice. Its geographical use is associated with the
historical phonetic spelling 亜米利加 (アメリカ), not an agricultural description.

## Synonyms and antonyms

南アメリカ (みなみアメリカ) is the longer name.
北米 (ほくべい), "North America," provides a geographical contrast.
[アメリカ](アメリカ.md) can refer to America or the United States depending
on context; it is not an exact substitute for 南米.

## Distinctive meaning

In 米国 (べいこく), 米 refers to the United States, but that does not make
南米 "the southern United States." Interpret this established regional name
as a whole. Nor does 南米 by itself specify a particular country, language,
or political organization.
