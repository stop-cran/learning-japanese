# 服装 (ふくそう) - attire; manner of dress

## Meaning

服装 is clothing or attire, especially how someone is dressed as a whole.
It is a noun: 服装を変える means "change one's attire," and
Nの服装 means "N's clothing or manner of dress."

- 面接には、どんな服装で行けばいいですか。
  (めんせつには、どんなふくそうでいけばいいですか。)
  - What should I wear to the interview?
- 動きやすい服装で来てください。
  (うごきやすいふくそうできてください。) - Please come in clothes that are easy to move in.

In these examples, で describes the attire in which someone comes or goes.
It does not mark a tool used to travel.

## How the kanji combine

[服](../kanji/服.md) contributes clothes, and 装 contributes dressing or outfitting.
The compound combines the garments with the idea of being dressed.
The on readings are フク + ソウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[衣服](衣服.md) (いふく) names clothing or garments.
[洋服](洋服.md) (ようふく) concerns Western-style clothing, whereas 服装 does not
by itself select a Western or Japanese style.
Formal and casual attire are contrasting kinds of 服装, not opposites of the noun.

## Distinctive meaning

服装 is useful when the suitability or overall presentation of clothes matters.
An instruction about 服装 can concern the whole outfit, not just one shirt.
It is therefore not a counting word for individual garments, despite the English gloss "clothes."
