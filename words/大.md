# 大 (だい) - large; great

## Meaning

大 read だい expresses large size, great degree, or major importance. It appears as a prefix in words such as 大成功 (だいせいこう), "great success." As a noun or size label, 大 can also identify the large option.

- コンサートは大成功でした。 (こんさーとはだいせいこうでした。) - The concert was a great success.
- サイズは大を選びました。 (さいずはだいをえらびました。) - I chose the large size.

In the first example, 大 intensifies 成功. In the second, 大 is the option selected and takes を. Learn established combinations rather than assuming that 大 can be attached freely to any noun.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) itself means big or great. The single character is the whole written form, with the on reading ダイ. Other words use other readings, such as タイ in [大使](大使.md) (たいし), "ambassador."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大きい](大きい.md) (おおきい) is the ordinary i-adjective for describing something as big: 大きい箱, "a big box." It has different grammar from a size label or a compound prefix.

In size labels, [小](小.md) (しょう), "small," contrasts with 大.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read every occurrence of 大 as だい or replace 大きい with bare 大 in arbitrary sentences. This card teaches the large/great use and the large-option noun. The character has further dictionary uses and abbreviations; they are not all implied by these examples.
