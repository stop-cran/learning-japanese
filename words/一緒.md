# 一緒 (いっしょ) — together

## Meaning

For doing something together, use 一緒に (いっしょに) with an action.
The person accompanying you can be marked with と: 友達と一緒に (ともだちといっしょに),
"together with a friend."

- 友達と一緒に昼ご飯を食べました。
  (ともだちといっしょにひるごはんをたべました。)
  — I ate lunch with a friend.
- 一緒に帰りませんか。 (いっしょにかえりませんか。) — Shall we go home together?

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) means "one," while [緒](../kanji/緒.md) has cord and beginning senses.
The compound names togetherness as a whole; "one cord" is not its literal translation in these
sentences. The on readings イチ + ショ combine as いっしょ, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

別々に (べつべつに), "separately," contrasts directly in actions such as returning home:
別々に帰る (べつべつにかえる), "go home separately."
The particle と already identifies a companion; 一緒に makes the shared action explicit.

## Distinctive meaning

Remember に in this action pattern: 一緒に食べる (いっしょにたべる), "eat together."
It is the group doing something together that matters, not necessarily the participants eating
identical food or making identical choices.
