# 引く (ひく) — pull

## Meaning

引く means to pull or tug in the physical sense taught here.
It is transitive in this use: mark the thing being pulled with を.

- ドアを引いてください。 (ドアをひいてください。) — Please pull the door.
- 子供がロープを引いています。 (こどもがロープをひいています。) — A child is pulling the rope.

Useful forms are 引きます (ひきます), 引かない (ひかない), and 引いて (ひいて).

## How the kanji combine

There is one kanji, [引](../kanji/引.md), read ひ, followed by the verb ending く.
This is a native Japanese verb rather than a compound of on readings.
The kana ending changes during conjugation while the kanji remains: 引く → 引いて.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[押す](押す.md) (おす), "push," provides the physical contrast.
ドアを押す (ドアをおす) and ドアを引く (ドアをひく) describe different ways of moving a door.
The shared object does not make the actions interchangeable; follow the actual door's opening direction.

## Distinctive meaning

For this sense, identify what is being pulled before translating the verb.
The same written word has other dictionary senses, so "pull" is a starting point, not a translation rule for every phrase containing 引く.
The two examples here deliberately keep the concrete object-pulling pattern clear.
