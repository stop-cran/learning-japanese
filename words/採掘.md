# 採掘 (さいくつ) - mining

## Meaning

採掘 means mining or extracting material by digging. It is a noun and a transitive する verb.
The material extracted takes を.

- この鉱山では石炭を採掘しています。 (このこうざんではせきたんをさいくつしています。) - They are mining coal at this mine.
- 採掘には安全対策が必要です。 (さいくつにはあんぜんたいさくがひつようです。) - Mining requires safety measures.

The first example uses 採掘する as an action. The second uses the noun to name the activity.

## How the kanji combine

[採](../kanji/採.md) contributes taking or collecting, and [掘](../kanji/掘.md) contributes digging.
The combination concerns obtaining material by digging. The on readings are サイ + クツ, giving さいくつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

掘る ほる is the broader verb "dig": digging a hole need not be mining.
採る とる can mean picking or gathering, but that verb alone does not specify excavation.

## Distinctive meaning

The focus here is extraction by digging, not collecting or selecting something by any method.
The noun does not itself identify a particular mineral, digging tool, or method; the surrounding words supply those details.
