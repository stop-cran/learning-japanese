# 下車 (げしゃ) - getting off a vehicle

## Meaning

下車 means getting off a train or bus, or getting out of a car.
It is a noun and an intransitive する verb.

- 次の駅で下車します。 (つぎのえきでげしゃします。) - I will get off at the next station.
- 乗客はバスを下車しました。 (じょうきゃくはバスをげしゃしました。) - The passengers got off the bus.

で marks the place of getting off; を can mark the vehicle being left.
That を does not make the passengers objects being unloaded.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) contributes downward movement or getting off; [車](../kanji/車.md) identifies the vehicle.
Learn the whole reading げしゃ: the on readings are ゲ + シャ.
The word is not limited to a vehicle called くるま in ordinary speech.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[降りる](降りる.md) (おりる) is an everyday verb for getting off and also descending.
[下りる](下りる.md) (おりる) has overlapping uses.
乗車 (じょうしゃ), boarding a vehicle, is the dictionary's opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

下車 is useful in transport directions and announcements.
下車してください asks people to get off, not to drive downhill.
Leaving a building is not automatically 下車 just because English also says "get out."
