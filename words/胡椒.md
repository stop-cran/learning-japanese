# 胡椒 (こしょう) - pepper

## Meaning

胡椒 is pepper, especially the peppercorn spice used as a seasoning.
It is also written こしょう or コショウ; JMdict marks the word as
usually written in kana.

- スープに胡椒を少し入れました。
  (スープにこしょうをすこしいれました。) - I added a little pepper to the soup.
- 塩と胡椒で味を調えます。
  (しおとこしょうであじをととのえます。) - Season with salt and pepper.

に marks what receives the pepper in the first example; で identifies
the seasonings used in the second.

## How the kanji combine

The character dictionary associates 胡 with foreign or non-Han
peoples in historical usage, and 椒 with pepper.
Learn 胡椒 as the established spice name, with on readings コ + ショウ.
These character associations alone do not establish the compound's
historical route into Japanese or a literal modern definition
"foreign pepper."

## Synonyms and antonyms

塩 (しお), salt, is another seasoning, not a synonym.
唐辛子 (とうがらし) is chili pepper.
English "pepper" can cover more than one plant or spice;
胡椒 does not mean every vegetable called a pepper in English.
[故障](故障.md), also こしょう, means a malfunction.

## Distinctive meaning

黒胡椒 (くろこしょう), black pepper, and 白胡椒 (しろこしょう),
white pepper, are useful seasoning names.
The spelling コショウ does not make this a modern European loanword;
katakana is also used for plant names and other Japanese vocabulary.
Keep しょう as a long vowel.
