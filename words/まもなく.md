# まもなく - soon; shortly

## Meaning

まもなく means soon, shortly, or before long. It modifies a predicate
without adding に.

- まもなく電車が到着します。 (まもなくでんしゃがとうちゃくします。) - The train will arrive shortly.
- 会議はまもなく始まります。 (かいぎはまもなくはじまります。) - The meeting will begin soon.

A separate construction is **dictionary-form verb + 間もなく**,
"with no time to do that action":

- 休む間もなく、次の仕事が始まりました。 (やすむまもなく、つぎのしごとがはじまりました。) - The next job began without time for a rest.

Here 休む is the dictionary form. This does not mean "resting soon."

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual for the soon sense. 間もなく and 間も無く are recorded
spellings. [間](../kanji/間.md) is ま, an interval; もなく expresses
the absence of such an interval. Learn the complete expression rather
than substituting 間's reading あいだ into it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もうすぐ](もうすぐ.md) and [直](直.md) (じき) overlap with soon.
[すぐに](すぐに.md) can emphasize little or no delay.
[やがて](やがて.md) can narrate a much longer eventual development;
no fixed number of minutes distinguishes all their uses.

## Distinctive meaning

The announcement use predicts an approaching event; the verb-attached
construction denies time for an action. Distinguish them by attachment
and context rather than translating every occurrence simply as "soon."
