# 意義 (いぎ) - meaning; significance

## Meaning

意義 is the meaning or significance of something, including why an activity or
event matters. It is a noun: 活動の意義 (かつどうのいぎ), "the significance of an activity."

- この活動の意義を説明してください。 (このかつどうのいぎをせつめいしてください。) - Please explain the significance of this activity.
- この発見には大きな意義があります。 (このはっけんにはおおきないぎがあります。) - This discovery has great significance.

意義がある means having significance or a point; 意義がない denies that.

## How the kanji combine

[意](../kanji/意.md) concerns thought or meaning, and 義 contributes sense or
meaning here. Their related contributions form the on-reading compound イ + ギ.
Do not translate 義 mechanically as moral justice in every compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意味](意味.md) (いみ) overlaps in meaning and significance. It readily asks for
the meaning of a word; 意義 is useful when discussing a work's or activity's
significance. This is a practical emphasis, not a ban on either word's other uses.
[価値](価値.md) (かち), "value; worth," is related when discussing why something matters.

## Distinctive meaning

Explaining an event's 意義 can require more than translating its name.
The question is often what the event means in a broader context.
This does not make 意義 a word only for impressive achievements:
one can also question whether an activity has any significance.
