# 離婚 (りこん) - divorce

## Meaning

離婚 is divorce, the ending of a marriage. 離婚する means to divorce or get divorced. Use **spouse と離婚する** to identify the person from whom someone divorces.

- 二人は去年離婚しました。 (ふたりはきょねんりこんしました。) - The two of them divorced last year.
- 彼女は夫と離婚しました。 (かのじょはおっととりこんしました。) - She divorced her husband.

Although English can place "husband" directly after "divorced," the Japanese example uses と, not を. The noun also occurs in 離婚について話す, "talk about divorce."

## How the kanji combine

離 contributes separation, and [婚](../kanji/婚.md) contributes marriage. Together they identify separation from the marital relationship. The on readings are リ + コン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結婚](結婚.md) (けっこん) is marriage or getting married, the contrasting change in marital status. [別れる](別れる.md) (わかれる) can mean separating or breaking up and is broader: people need not have been married.

## Distinctive meaning

Living separately is not by itself the same as divorce, and a disagreement does not establish that a marriage has ended. 離婚 names that specific change in the relationship. The word does not explain whose decision it was, the reasons, or anyone's feelings; add those details only when the context supplies them.
