# 一応 (いちおう) - tentatively; roughly; just in case

## Meaning

一応 qualifies an action or assessment: it is adequate at a basic level,
provisional for now, or done as a precaution. The context determines which
English rendering fits.

- 一応、準備はできました。 (いちおう、じゅんびはできました。) - The preparations are more or less done.
- 一応、金曜日の席を予約しました。 (いちおう、きんようびのせきをよやくしました。) - I provisionally reserved seats for Friday.
- 一応、傘を持って行きます。 (いちおう、かさをもっていきます。) - I will take an umbrella just in case.

In the first example, the speaker leaves room for a qualification; the word
does not quantify what percentage is ready.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) contributes one, and 応 is associated with responding
or adapting. "An initial response" can be a mnemonic for some uses, not a
literal definition or a verified historical derivation of every sense.
The on readings are イチ + オウ. 一往 is another dictionary-listed spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ざっと](ざっと.md) can describe a rough first pass, while
[大体](大体.md) (だいたい) gives a broad or approximate assessment.
[一旦](一旦.md) (いったん) can describe a temporary step before a later
development. Their uses overlap, but 一応 can specifically leave a decision
provisional or express a precaution.

## Distinctive meaning

一応 is not a numerical promise that an action happens exactly once.
Use [一度](一度.md) (いちど) when simply counting one occurrence.
Nor does 一応 prove that a result is defective: a speaker can hedge an
otherwise adequate result. The qualification may concern certainty,
finality, or the limited purpose of an action.
