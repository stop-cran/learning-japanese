# ください - please give; please do

## Meaning

ください requests something from the listener. After a noun with を, it means
"please give me"; after a verb's te-form, it requests an action.

- 水をください。 (みずをください。) - Please give me some water.
- ここに名前を書いてください。 (ここになまえをかいてください。) - Please write your name here.

For a negative request, use the verb's ない-form plus でください:
入らないでください (はいらないでください), "Please do not enter."

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is usual, especially after a te-form. 下さい is another spelling.
Historically and grammatically, ください is the special imperative of
くださる, "give respectfully"; learn its modern request patterns as units.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お願いします (おねがいします) is another request expression, but it does not
attach to a verb's te-form in the same way. There is no antonym of "please."

## Distinctive meaning

ください makes a direct request, not an all-purpose translation of English
"please" that can be inserted anywhere. It is polite in form but may sound
instructive; a question such as 書いていただけますか (かいていただけますか),
"Could you write it?", asks more indirectly.
