# 禁止 (きんし) - prohibition; ban

## Meaning

禁止 is a noun meaning a prohibition or ban. 禁止する is transitive: use 行為を禁止する to prohibit an action. The passive 禁止されている describes an action that is prohibited.

- この図書館では飲食が禁止されています。 (このとしょかんではいんしょくがきんしされています。) - Eating and drinking are prohibited in this library.
- 学校は授業中の携帯電話の使用を禁止しています。 (がっこうはじゅぎょうちゅうのけいたいでんわのしようをきんししています。) - The school prohibits mobile phone use during lessons.

Notices can shorten this to a noun followed by 禁止: 撮影禁止 (さつえいきんし), "no photography."

## How the kanji combine

禁 contributes forbidding, and [止](../kanji/止.md) contributes stopping. Together they express preventing an action by a rule or ban. The reading is きんし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[許可](許可.md) (きょか), "permission," is a useful opposite in rules: 許可する permits what 禁止する forbids. [禁煙](禁煙.md) (きんえん) concerns not smoking; in signs it gives a specific smoking prohibition.

## Distinctive meaning

禁止 describes a restriction, not physical inability. A person may be able to take a photograph while photography is 禁止されている. Conversely, a broken camera does not make photography 禁止. Identify both the prohibited activity and the authority or setting that establishes the rule.
