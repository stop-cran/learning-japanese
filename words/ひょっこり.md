# ひょっこり — appearing suddenly or unexpectedly

## Meaning

ひょっこり describes someone or something appearing suddenly, unexpectedly, or by chance.

- 塀の向こうから、猫がひょっこり顔を出した。
  — A cat suddenly poked its head out from behind the fence.
- 旅行先で、昔の友人にひょっこり会った。
  — I unexpectedly ran into an old friend while traveling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ひょっこり emphasizes an unplanned, surprising appearance rather than a carefully arranged arrival.

## Distinctive meaning

It can describe both something popping into view and a chance encounter.
