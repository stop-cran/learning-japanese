# 指輪 (ゆびわ) — finger ring

## Meaning

指輪 is a ring worn on a finger. Use 指輪をする or 指輪をはめる for wearing or putting on a ring; 外す (はずす) means taking it off.

母は銀の指輪をしています。 (はははぎんのゆびわをしています。) — My mother is wearing a silver ring.

## How the kanji combine

[指](../kanji/指.md), ゆび, means finger, and 輪, わ, means ring or loop. The native readings combine directly as ゆびわ. This transparent formation specifies the place where the ornament is worn.

## Synonyms and antonyms

リング is a broader loanword for a ring, including shapes and sporting arenas. 結婚指輪 (けっこんゆびわ) specifies a wedding ring. 腕輪 (うでわ) is a bracelet worn around the wrist.

## Distinctive meaning

指輪 names the jewelry, not the finger. Nor does it automatically mean an engagement or wedding ring; those require context or a more specific compound. There is no ordinary lexical opposite to this object name.
