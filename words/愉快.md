# 愉快 (ゆかい) - enjoyable; amusing

## Meaning

愉快 describes something pleasant, enjoyable, cheerful, or amusing. It is a na-adjective: use 愉快な before a noun and 愉快に before a verb. As a predicate, use 愉快です or 愉快だ.

- 彼は愉快な話をしてくれました。 (かれはゆかいなはなしをしてくれました。) - He told us an amusing story.
- 友達と愉快に過ごしました。 (ともだちとゆかいにすごしました。) - I had an enjoyable time with my friends.

The first sentence describes the story; the second describes how the time was spent. The word can concern an experience as well as someone's cheerful character.

## How the kanji combine

愉 contributes pleasure or delight, and 快 contributes pleasantness or a good feeling. Their meanings reinforce each other in the compound. The reading is ユ + カイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[楽しい](楽しい.md) (たのしい) also means enjoyable, but it is an i-adjective: 楽しい話, without な. [面白い](面白い.md) (おもしろい) can mean interesting or funny, so its overlap depends on context.

不愉快 (ふゆかい), "unpleasant; disagreeable," is a direct contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

愉快 does not mean that every enjoyable experience makes someone laugh aloud. Its range includes cheerful enjoyment as well as amusement. When used about a person, 愉快な人 (ゆかいなひと) presents them as entertaining or enjoyable to be around, not merely as someone who is currently smiling.
