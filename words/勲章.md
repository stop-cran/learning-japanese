# 勲章 (くんしょう) — decoration; order; medal

## Meaning

勲章 is a decoration, order or medal. Here “order” means an order of merit, an honor awarded for service, not a command.

- 祖父は国から勲章を受けました。 (そふはくにからくんしょうをうけました。) — My grandfather received a decoration from the state.
- 胸に勲章をつけた兵士が並んでいます。 (むねにくんしょうをつけたへいしがならんでいます。) — Soldiers wearing medals on their chests are lined up.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[勲](../kanji/勲.md) means meritorious service, and [章](../kanji/章.md) can mean a badge. Together, they describe a badge of merit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[賞](賞.md) (しょう) is a prize or award in general (JMdict entry 1351910). [賞金](賞金.md) (しょうきん) is prize money.

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen lists 勲章 under the sense of 勲 meaning meritorious service to the state.
