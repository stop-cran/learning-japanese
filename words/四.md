# 四 (よん・し) — four

## Meaning

四 means four. Both よん and し are important readings, but the surrounding counter or fixed expression determines which is natural.

- りんごを四個買いました。 (りんごをよんこかいました。) — I bought four apples.
- 四月に学校が始まります。 (しがつにがっこうがはじまります。) — School starts in April.

よん is a useful standalone reading and occurs in 四個 (よんこ). 四月 is しがつ, not よんがつ.

## How the kanji combine

[四](../kanji/四.md) is one numeral. よん belongs to the native number family, while し is the on-reading. The wago label follows this card's primary よん reading, not a claim that し is native.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The digit 4 represents the same number. 四つ (よっつ) is the general native counting form; 四人 (よにん), "four people," uses よ rather than よん or し.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn numeral-counter combinations together. 四時 (よじ), "four o'clock," and 四日 (よっか), "four days/the fourth," demonstrate why one pronunciation cannot simply be inserted into every expression.
