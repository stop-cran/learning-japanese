# 混ぜる (まぜる) - mix; stir; blend

## Meaning

混ぜる means mix, stir, or blend things. It is a transitive ichidan verb: what someone mixes takes を. Use と for materials mixed together, or に for the material something is mixed into.

- 卵と牛乳を混ぜます。 (たまごとぎゅうにゅうをまぜます。) - I mix the eggs and milk.
- コーヒーに砂糖を混ぜた。 (コーヒーにさとうをまぜた。) - I mixed sugar into the coffee.
- よく混ぜてください。 (よくまぜてください。) - Please mix it well.

交ぜる is another spelling in the same entry. 雑ぜる is marked irregular in the pinned dictionary, not taught as the default spelling.

## How the kanji combine

[混](../kanji/混.md) contributes mixing; ぜる completes the native verb. Forms include 混ぜます (まぜます), 混ぜない (まぜない), and 混ぜた (まぜた).

The alternative [交](../kanji/交.md) also evokes mingling. Do not infer a rigid liquid-versus-solid restriction from the written character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[混ざる](混ざる.md) (まざる) and [混じる](混じる.md) (まじる) are intransitive godan verbs. Compare 砂糖を混ぜる, "mix in the sugar," with 砂糖が混ざる, "the sugar mixes in."

[混合](混合.md) (こんごう), "mixing; mixture," overlaps. [溶く](溶く.md) (とく) can mean mix something into liquid or beat eggs; that does not make every 混ぜる action an act of dissolving.

## Distinctive meaning

Mixing can blend materials without making them disappear. The objects and particles show who mixes what into what; do not substitute an intransitive verb merely because the English translation uses "mix" for both.
