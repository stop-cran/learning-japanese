# 努める (つとめる) - strive; make an effort

## Meaning

努める means to make an effort or devote oneself to a goal.
Use a noun with に, or a verb phrase with ように, to identify what one tries to achieve.
It does not say that the goal was necessarily reached.

- 健康の維持に努めています。
  (けんこうのいじにつとめています。) - I make an effort to maintain my health.
- 同じ間違いを繰り返さないように努めます。
  (おなじまちがいをくりかえさないようにつとめます。) - I will try not to repeat the same mistake.

## How the kanji combine

[努](../kanji/努.md) expresses exertion or effort. It supplies つと in this native verb; める is okurigana.
This is ichidan: 努めます, 努めた, 努めて, 努めない.
JMdict also records rare spellings 勉める and 力める; they are recognition forms, not the recommended spelling here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[努力](努力.md) (どりょく) is effort; 努力する overlaps with 努める.
[試す](試す.md) (ためす), "try out; test," concerns testing something, not necessarily striving toward a continuing goal.
[怠る](怠る.md) (おこたる), "neglect," contrasts with attending to a duty or effort.

[勤める](勤める.md) concerns employment, and [務める](務める.md) concerns fulfilling a role.
Their shared reading does not make their meanings interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

改善に努める (かいぜんにつとめる), "strive for improvement," describes the effort, not an employment relationship.
The community source repeats an employment gloss for this row; the pinned dictionary instead supports the effort meaning taught here.
