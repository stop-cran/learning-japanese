# 終点 (しゅうてん) - terminus; final stop

## Meaning

終点 is the endpoint of a route, especially the last station or stop reached by a train or bus. It names the destination point, not the act of arriving.

- 終点でバスを降ります。
  (しゅうてんでバスをおります。) - I will get off the bus at the final stop.
- この電車の終点はどこですか。
  (このでんしゃのしゅうてんはどこですか。) - Where does this train terminate?

で marks the place where someone gets off. The route or particular service determines which stop is its 終点.

## How the kanji combine

[終](../kanji/終.md) contributes ending, and [点](../kanji/点.md) a point. The first identifies the kind of point: the endpoint. The readings are シュウ + テン, not ジュウ + テン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[終わり](終わり.md) (おわり) is an end more broadly; [ゴール](ゴール.md) includes a finish line or goal. 起点 (きてん), a starting point, supplies the route contrast. [停留所](停留所.md) (ていりゅうじょ) can be any designated bus or tram stop, not necessarily the final one.

## Distinctive meaning

終電 (しゅうでん) is the last train of the day, not the final stop. A train earlier in the day still has a 終点. The public dictionary also records the endpoint of a directed line segment or vector; that mathematical recognition note is separate from the transport focus.
