# 正 (せい) - true; regular; positive

## Meaning

正 (せい) is a noun with context-dependent meanings. JMdict lists "(logical) true; regular,"
and separately the mathematical sense "positive; greater than zero."
It can function as a correctness label; in mathematics, use 正の数 (せいのすう), "a positive number."

- この表では、正しい答えを「正」で示します。 (このひょうでは、ただしいこたえを「せい」でしめします。) - In this table, correct answers are marked "correct."
- 三は正の数です。 (さんはせいのすうです。) - Three is a positive number.

In 正の数, の links the noun-like modifier to 数. Do not form an i-adjective by adding い to せい.

For the "regular" meaning, recognize the established compound **正会員** (せいかいいん), "regular member; full member." Here 正 indicates membership status, not how often the member visits.

## How the kanji combine

[正](../kanji/正.md) represents the whole word, using its on-reading セイ.
The same character occurs in [正しい](正しい.md) (ただしい), "correct," but that is a different grammatical form.
Knowing the character's general meaning does not determine the correct reading or attachment.

## Synonyms and antonyms

正しい is the ordinary adjective useful in statements such as 正しい答え, "a correct answer."
For mathematical 正, 負 (ふ), "negative," supplies the contrast.
[間違い](間違い.md) (まちがい), "mistake," contrasts with correctness, not with positive numbers.

## Distinctive meaning

A positive number is not a "correct number": sign and correctness are different properties.
Zero is neither positive nor negative in the greater-than-zero sense taught here.
The dictionary gloss "regular" also should not be extended mechanically to English phrases such as "a regular customer."
