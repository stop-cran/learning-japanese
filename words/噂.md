# 噂 (うわさ) - rumor; gossip

## Meaning

噂 is a noun for a rumor, hearsay, or gossip: something being talked about.
It can also combine with する to mean "talk about; gossip about."
A plain clause followed by という噂 gives the content of a rumor.

- あの店が閉まるという噂を聞きました。 (あのみせがしまるといううわさをききました。) - I heard a rumor that that shop is closing.
- 二人は新しい先生のことを噂していました。 (ふたりはあたらしいせんせいのことをうわさしていました。) - The two of them were talking about the new teacher.

噂を聞く means "hear a rumor"; 噂する describes doing the talking. These are different constructions,
not interchangeable placements of を.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 噂 writes the native word うわさ. Learn the reading as a whole,
rather than trying to assign one syllable to each visible component.
The kana spelling うわさ is also readily understandable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話](話.md) (はなし), "talk; story," is broader and need not involve hearsay.
[事実](事実.md) (じじつ), "fact," contrasts with unconfirmed information, but a rumor can turn out
to be true. [嘘](嘘.md) (うそ), "lie; falsehood," is therefore not an exact synonym for 噂.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling something 噂 identifies it as circulating talk, not independently established evidence.
It does not automatically accuse a speaker of deliberately lying. 噂の店 (うわさのみせ),
"the shop people are talking about," can introduce something attracting attention without implying a scandal.
