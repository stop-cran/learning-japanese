# 休養 (きゅうよう) - recuperative rest; relaxation

## Meaning

休養 is resting or relaxing to recover one's physical or mental energy. It is a noun and an intransitive する-verb. 休養を取る (きゅうようをとる) means to take recuperative rest.

- 週末は家でゆっくり休養しました。
  (しゅうまつはいえでゆっくりきゅうようしました。) - I rested and recuperated at home over the weekend.
- 疲れがひどいので、しばらく休養を取ることにしました。
  (つかれがひどいので、しばらくきゅうようをとることにしました。) - I decided to take some time to rest because I was very tired.

休養 need not follow a diagnosed illness. Restoring energy is the important perspective.

## How the kanji combine

[休](../kanji/休.md) contributes rest, and 養 nourishing or restoring strength. The meanings work together as restorative rest. The readings are キュウ + ヨウ, with both long vowels retained.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[休息](休息.md) (きゅうそく) is rest or respite more generally. [休憩](休憩.md) (きゅうけい) typically presents a pause in an activity. [回復](回復.md) (かいふく) names recovery or restoration, which rest may help bring about.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not prescribe hospitalization, bed rest, or a minimum duration. Recreation can contribute to 休養, but not every [遊び](遊び.md) (あそび), "play; recreation," is necessarily restorative.
