# ふざける - joke; fool around; make fun of

## Meaning

ふざける means to joke, fool around or behave playfully rather than seriously. It can also describe making fun of someone or playing a prank.

- 冗談だよ。ふざけて言っただけ。 (じょうだんだよ。ふざけていっただけ。) - It was a joke. I was only kidding when I said it.
- 授業中にふざけてはいけません。 (じゅぎょうちゅうにふざけてはいけません。) - You must not fool around during class.

## How the kanji combine

Use the ordinary kana spelling. The dictionary also records 巫山戯る as rare ateji, a conventional spelling not to be interpreted as a literal explanation of joking. Its characters are not the metadata for this kana-only card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冗談](冗談.md) (じょうだん) is a joke or jest. [からかう](からかう.md) overlaps in teasing, while [遊ぶ](遊ぶ.md) (あそぶ) is broader playing or recreation. Not every instance of playing is misbehavior.

## Distinctive meaning

This is an ichidan verb: ふざけない, ふざけます, ふざけて. ふざけるな is a sharp rebuke, not a neutral request for a joke; its force depends on context. The dictionary also records amorous fooling around, but that is not the meaning of every playful exchange.
