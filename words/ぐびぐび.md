# ぐびぐび — drinking with audible gulps

## Meaning

Vivid, repeated gulping, especially in descriptions of drinking alcohol. The word is not restricted to alcoholic drinks.

- 父はビールをぐびぐび飲んだ。 — My father gulped down his beer.
- 彼は冷たいお茶をぐびぐび飲んでいた。 — He was drinking cold tea in loud gulps.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ごくごく](ごくごく.md) and [がぶがぶ](がぶがぶ.md) overlap in the same drinking scenes. [ちびちび](ちびちび.md) contrasts with them by suggesting small amounts over time.

## Distinctive meaning

ぐびぐび飲む（のむ）describes the manner of drinking, not the drink's flavor or a fixed amount. The association with alcohol is a usage tendency, not an ingredient requirement.
