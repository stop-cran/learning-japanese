# きょろきょろ — looking around restlessly

## Meaning

Repeatedly looking this way and that, often searching or taking in unfamiliar surroundings.

- 駅で友達を探（さが）して、きょろきょろした。 — I looked around the station for my friend.
- 子どもは初めての店できょろきょろ辺（あた）りを見た。 — The child looked all around the unfamiliar shop.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[じろじろ](じろじろ.md) concerns scrutinizing or staring, not necessarily shifting attention around. [ちらちら](ちらちら.md) can describe repeated brief glances.

## Distinctive meaning

きょろきょろする does not prove that someone is lost or dishonest. It describes the visible manner of looking; the reason comes from context. It can suggest curiosity as well as nervous searching.
