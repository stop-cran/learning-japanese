# 隙間 (すきま) - gap; crevice

## Meaning

隙間 is a gap, crack, or narrow opening between things. It is a noun:
XとYの隙間 identifies the gap between X and Y.

- 窓の隙間から冷たい風が入ってきます。 (まどのすきまからつめたいかぜがはいってきます。) - Cold air comes in through a gap in the window.
- 本棚と壁の隙間にペンが落ちました。 (ほんだなとかべのすきまにペンがおちました。) - A pen fell into the gap between the bookcase and the wall.

から marks where the air comes through; に marks where the pen ends up.
隙間を[埋める](埋める.md) (すきまをうめる) means to fill or plug a gap.

## How the kanji combine

隙 contributes a gap or opening, and [間](../kanji/間.md) an intervening space.
The native readings すき + ま reinforce the space-between idea. The spelling
すき間 represents the same noun with its first element in kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[間隔](間隔.md) (かんかく) concerns spacing or intervals; 隙間 draws attention
to an opening left between things. [穴](穴.md) (あな) is a hole or hollow,
which need not lie between two separate objects. These descriptions can overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

A 隙間 need not be large enough for a person to pass through. The window example
can concern a very narrow opening. This lesson leads with physical gaps; it is
not a rule forbidding figurative uses or a translation of every English "gap,"
such as a difference in price.
