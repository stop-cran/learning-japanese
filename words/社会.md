# 社会 (しゃかい) — society

## Meaning

社会 is society, the community of people: 社会に出る (enter the working world), 社会人 しゃかいじん (a working adult), 社会問題 しゃかいもんだい (a social problem), 社会科 しゃかいか (the school subject of social studies), 日本社会 (Japanese society).

## How the kanji combine

[社](../kanji/社.md) (association) + [会](../kanji/会.md) (meeting, gathering): a group of people gathered together. It is the reverse of [会社](会社.md) かいしゃ; the two words are different despite sharing the same characters. Both are on readings (シャ + カイ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 世間 せけん (the social world and people around you), 世の中 よのなか.
- Related: 社会人 (adult in working life), 社会主義 しゃかいしゅぎ (socialism).

## Distinctive meaning

社会 is the abstract society, while 会社 is a concrete company. Mixing them up is a common mistake for learners.
