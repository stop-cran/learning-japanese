# あてはまる - be applicable; fit conditions

## Meaning

あてはまる means that a rule or description applies, or that something fits a category
or meets conditions. It is an **intransitive godan verb**:
あてはまります, あてはまって, あてはまった.

- この規則は外国人にもあてはまります。 (このきそくはがいこくじんにもあてはまります。) - This rule also applies to foreign nationals.
- 条件にあてはまる人は応募できます。 (じょうけんにあてはまるひとはおうぼできます。) - People who meet the conditions can apply.

に marks what the rule applies to in the first example, and the conditions met in the second.
Learn each complete pattern instead of assigning に one English translation.

## How the kanji combine

当てはまる is a dictionary spelling, with [当](../kanji/当.md).
The dictionary marks the word as usually written in kana; 当て嵌まる is a rare kanji spelling.
The article retains the source's kana form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あてはめる](あてはめる.md) (あてはめる) is transitive:
applying a rule or fitting an item into a category.
[合う](合う.md) (あう) is broader fitting or matching, including physical fit.
あてはまらない means "does not apply; does not fit the conditions."

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 規則があてはまる (きそくがあてはまる), "the rule applies,"
with 規則をあてはめる (きそくをあてはめる), "apply the rule."
The English verb "apply" hides this difference in participants and particles.
