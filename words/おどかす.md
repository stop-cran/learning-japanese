# おどかす (おどかす) - threaten; startle

## Meaning

おどかす is a transitive godan verb meaning to threaten or intimidate, or to startle someone. The person affected takes を.

- 大きな声で子供をおどかさないでください。 (おおきなこえでこどもをおどかさないでください。) - Please do not frighten the child with a loud voice.
- 秘密をばらすと言って、相手をおどかした。 (ひみつをばらすといって、あいてをおどかした。) - They threatened the other person by saying they would reveal the secret.

The forms include おどかします, おどかして, and おどかさない. The first example can concern frightening someone; the second clearly supplies a threat.

## How the kanji combine

脅かす is the written form of おどかす. There is only one kanji, not a multi-character compound. The kana headword keeps `kanji: []`; the alternative spelling does not change that metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

脅す (おどす) overlaps in threatening. [驚かす](驚かす.md) (おどろかす) or 驚かせる (おどろかせる) can mean to surprise or frighten.

[驚く](驚く.md) (おどろく) is intransitive: 子供が驚いた (こどもがおどろいた), "the child was startled," describes the child's reaction. [怖い](怖い.md) (こわい) and [恐ろしい](恐ろしい.md) (おそろしい) describe something frightening rather than the act of frightening someone.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling 脅かす also represents a different dictionary entry read おびやかす, including threatening or endangering something such as peace. Do not silently replace おどかす with that reading or assume all uses of the two entries are identical. Conversely, おどかす is not restricted to a serious criminal threat: the startle sense can describe an unexpected scare.
