# 翼 (つばさ) - wing

## Meaning

翼 is a noun for a wing. It can describe a bird's wing or an aircraft's wing; the surrounding noun identifies which.

- 鳥が翼を広げました。 (とりがつばさをひろげました。) - The bird spread its wings.
- 窓から飛行機の翼が見えます。 (まどからひこうきのつばさがみえます。) - I can see the airplane's wing from the window.

The first example uses を for the wings being spread. In the second, が marks what is visible. Japanese does not require a separate plural form when both wings are meant.

## How the kanji combine

翼 writes the entire native noun つばさ. There is no multi-kanji compound. The character also has the reading ヨク in other vocabulary, but the standalone wing examples here are read つばさ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[羽](羽.md) (はね) can mean a feather or wing. 翼 focuses on the wing as the structure, whereas a detached feather can be 羽 without being an entire 翼. The words overlap in some bird descriptions; they are not exact substitutes in every use.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer that a 翼 must be covered in feathers: aircraft wings provide a clear counterexample. Likewise, the word does not itself assert that the wing is moving or that flight is possible. Learn it with practical phrases such as 翼を広げる rather than translating the English plural mechanically.
