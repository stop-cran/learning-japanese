# 郵便 (ゆうびん) - mail; postal service

## Meaning

郵便 is a noun for the postal service or mail sent through it.
郵便で means "by post," with で identifying the means of sending.
The word can also name the delivered items.

- 書類を郵便で送りました。 (しょるいをゆうびんでおくりました。) - I sent the documents by post.
- 今日の郵便はもう届きましたか。 (きょうのゆうびんはもうとどきましたか。) - Has today's mail arrived yet?

The first example concerns the delivery method. The second concerns the mail itself,
a use also expressed by the fuller noun 郵便物 (ゆうびんぶつ), "postal items."

## How the kanji combine

郵 contributes postal delivery, and [便](../kanji/便.md) contributes a delivery or service.
The first specifies the kind of service. The on readings combine as ユウ + ビン.
Keep the long ゆう; ゆびん omits a vowel beat.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[手紙](手紙.md) (てがみ), "letter," can be one item sent by 郵便.
[郵便局](郵便局.md) (ゆうびんきょく) is a post office, not the mail itself.
Electronic mail is a different delivery medium; it is a practical contrast rather than an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every English occurrence of "mail" identically: it may name an item, a service,
or the action of sending. 郵便 is a noun, so use a verb such as 送る with it when describing
the sending action. The delivery method does not by itself specify speed, tracking, or the item's contents.
