# 飼う (かう) - keep; raise an animal

## Meaning

飼う is a transitive godan verb meaning to keep or raise an animal. Mark the animal with を. 飼っている (かっている) describes keeping one as an ongoing situation.

- 家で猫を二匹飼っています。 (いえでねこをにひきかっています。) - We keep two cats at home.
- このアパートでは犬を飼えません。 (このアパートではいぬをかえません。) - You cannot keep a dog in this apartment.

The negative is 飼わない (かわない), and the past is 飼った (かった). The verb can apply to livestock as well as pets.

## How the kanji combine

飼 supplies the animal-keeping idea; う is the dictionary-form ending. Learn the native reading かう with this spelling rather than identifying the word by sound alone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[買う](買う.md) (かう), "buy," is a different word with the same pronunciation. 犬を買う describes purchasing a dog; 犬を飼う describes keeping it.

[育てる](育てる.md) (そだてる), "raise; nurture," has a wider range, including children and plants.

## Distinctive meaning

Keeping an animal includes responsibility for its [世話](世話.md) (せわ), "care." For the particular act of giving food, 餌をやる (えさをやる) or 餌をあげる is clearer than assuming 飼う names each feeding. It is not a general translation of English "keep" in "keep a receipt" or "keep a promise."
