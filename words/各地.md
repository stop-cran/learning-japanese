# 各地 (かくち) - various places; all parts

## Meaning

各地 refers to various places or all parts of a stated or understood area. 日本各地 (にほんかくち) means places throughout Japan; 世界各地 (せかいかくち) means places throughout the world. It is a noun: 各地で locates an activity, and 各地の connects the places to a following noun.

- 日本各地で雪が降りました。 (にほんかくちでゆきがふりました。) - Snow fell in various parts of Japan.
- これは各地の祭りを紹介する本です。 (これはかくちのまつりをしょうかいするほんです。) - This is a book introducing festivals from various places.

The first sentence distributes the snowfall across places; it does not say that every point in Japan received snow.

## How the kanji combine

[各](../kanji/各.md) contributes each or every, and [地](../kanji/地.md) contributes place or land. Together they describe places across an area. The on readings are カク + チ, giving かくち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あちこち](あちこち.md) (あちこち), "here and there," also distributes attention across places, including scattered spots within one room. 各地 is useful for places across an area, as in 日本各地; the overlap does not make every construction interchangeable.

[方々](方々.md) has a place word read ほうぼう, "here and there; all over," which also overlaps. Its separate respectful people reading かたがた does not mean various places. 一か所 (いっかしょ), "one place," gives a contextual contrast, not a universal antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the area in view: 各地 need not mean places throughout the whole world. The surrounding expression can narrow the relevant set, as 日本 does in 日本各地. English "all parts" can describe broad distribution without a literal assertion about every possible location.
