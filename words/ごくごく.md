# ごくごく — drinking in big gulps

## Meaning

Repeated substantial gulps, often suggesting that a thirsty person drinks readily.

- 運動のあと、水をごくごく飲んだ。 — After exercising, I drank water in big gulps.
- 子どもが牛乳（ぎゅうにゅう）をごくごく飲んでいる。 — The child is gulping down milk.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[がぶがぶ](がぶがぶ.md) often emphasizes copious drinking; [ぐびぐび](ぐびぐび.md) makes the gulps especially vivid. Their uses overlap. [ちびちび](ちびちび.md) suggests small amounts instead.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn ごくごく飲む（のむ）, not a general verb ごくごくする. This sound-and-manner word is distinct from the same-sounding ごくごく meaning “extremely,” as in ごくごく普通（ふつう）, “quite ordinary.”
