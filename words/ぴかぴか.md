# ぴかぴか — sparkling clean; shiny and new

## Meaning

Brightly shining or flashing; also sparkling clean or brand-new and shiny.

- 靴を磨いて（みがいて）、ぴかぴかにした。 — I polished my shoes until they shone.
- 新しい自転車が日を受けてぴかぴか光っている。 — My new bicycle is gleaming in the sunlight.
- 弟は今年、ぴかぴかの一年生になった。 — My little brother became a brand-new first grader this year.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic repeating ぴか. There is no written kanji compound. Its appearance-related meaning does not imply an actual sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[きらきら](きらきら.md) often suggests many small sparkles. [ぎらぎら](ぎらぎら.md) can be harshly glaring. [つやつや](つやつや.md) concerns surface gloss rather than flashing light or newness.

## Distinctive meaning

Useful patterns include ぴかぴか光る, ぴかぴかに磨く, and ぴかぴかの靴. A “ぴかぴかの一年生” is newly starting school, not literally luminous. With dishes, ぴかぴか can emphasize a thoroughly cleared or cleaned plate; context determines whether the point is shine, cleanliness, or finishing the food.
