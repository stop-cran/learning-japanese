# 水曜日 (すいようび) — Wednesday

## Meaning

水曜日 means **Wednesday**. Use に to mark when an event happens, or は to make Wednesday the topic.

水曜日に日本語を勉強します。 (すいようびににほんごをべんきょうします。) — I study Japanese on Wednesday.

毎週水曜日 (まいしゅうすいようび) explicitly means “every Wednesday.” 水曜日 alone may refer to one Wednesday or a recurring schedule, depending on context.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md) is スイ here, not みず. [曜](../kanji/曜.md) is ヨウ, and [日](../kanji/日.md) becomes び in 曜日 (ようび), “day of the week.” This combines on readings with the native 日 reading, rather than three on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

水曜 (すいよう) is a shortened form. 火曜日 (かようび), Tuesday, comes before it; 木曜日 (もくようび), Thursday, follows it. These are neighboring weekdays, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

A timetable may abbreviate Wednesday to 水. In a weekday heading this identifies the day, not water. The full spoken form 水曜日 is a useful starting point for making appointments clearly.
