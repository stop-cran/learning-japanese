# 新聞社 (しんぶんしゃ) -- newspaper company

## Meaning

新聞社 is a company that produces a newspaper. It names the organization, not an individual copy of the paper.

兄は新聞社で働いています。 (あにはしんぶんしゃではたらいています。) -- My older brother works at a newspaper company.

Here で marks the workplace. 新聞社に勤める (しんぶんしゃにつとめる) also describes being employed by one.

## How the kanji combine

[新](../kanji/新.md), read しん, and [聞](../kanji/聞.md), read ぶん here, form 新聞, newspaper. [社](../kanji/社.md), read しゃ, adds company or organization. The structure is newspaper + company, not three unrelated descriptions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

出版社 (しゅっぱんしゃ) is a publisher, commonly of books or magazines. 新聞 (しんぶん) is the newspaper itself. These are related media terms rather than interchangeable names for the same thing.

## Distinctive meaning

A 新聞社 employs people in different jobs, not only reporters. 記者 (きしゃ) names a reporter or journalist; saying someone works at a newspaper company does not establish that they write the articles.
