# 転ぶ (ころぶ) - fall over; stumble and fall

## Meaning

転ぶ is an intransitive godan verb meaning "fall down" or "fall over."
The person who falls takes が; the place where it happens can take で.
It does not take a person with を to mean knocking that person over.

- 子供が道で転びました。 (こどもがみちでころびました。) - A child fell over on the road.
- 転んで、膝を打ちました。 (ころんで、ひざをうちました。) - I fell and hit my knee.

Its forms are 転びます, 転んだ, 転んで, and 転ばない.
Notice the んだ/んで forms for this verb ending in ぶ.

## How the kanji combine

[転](../kanji/転.md) contributes turning, tumbling, or falling.
The native word is ころぶ, with ぶ written in kana. There is no
multi-kanji compound to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[倒れる](倒れる.md) (たおれる) covers falling over or collapsing,
including objects such as trees. [落ちる](落ちる.md) (おちる)
focuses on falling from a position or height. These can describe
related accidents but do not select exactly the same scene.

## Distinctive meaning

転ぶ is useful for a person losing balance and falling, including a
trip or slip. The verb does not itself identify the cause. In
滑って転ぶ (すべってころぶ), "slip and fall," 滑って supplies
that extra information.
