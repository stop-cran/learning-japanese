# 鉱石 (こうせき) - ore; mineral

## Meaning

鉱石 names ore or mineral material. In mining, it concerns material from which a useful substance such as a metal is obtained.

- この鉱石には銅が含まれています。 (このこうせきにはどうがふくまれています。) - This ore contains copper.
- 鉱石を[採掘](採掘.md)します。 (こうせきをさいくつします。) - We mine ore.

It is a noun: 鉱石を identifies the material in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

[鉱](../kanji/鉱.md) contributes ore or mineral material, and [石](../kanji/石.md) stone. This is a modifier-and-head compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[石](石.md) (いし), stone, is broader: an arbitrary stone is not necessarily useful ore. [銅](../kanji/銅.md) (どう), copper, names a metal rather than every piece of ore that contains it.

鉱業 (こうぎょう) names the mining industry, not the extracted material itself.

## Distinctive meaning

The inspected entry also includes the gloss crystal. Context determines whether ore, mineral or crystal is the appropriate translation; that list is not a claim that all crystals contain a recoverable metal.
