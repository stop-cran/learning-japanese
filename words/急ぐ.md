# 急ぐ (いそぐ) — hurry; hasten

## Meaning

急ぐ means to hurry or make something happen sooner. It can describe hurried movement or working quickly on a task.

- 駅へ急ぎます。  
  えきへいそぎます。  
  "I'll hurry to the station."
- もう時間がありません。急ぎましょう。  
  もうじかんがありません。いそぎましょう。  
  "There's no time left. Let's hurry."

## How the kanji combine

This is a native verb with one kanji. [急](../kanji/急.md) supplies いそ; ぐ is okurigana. It is godan: 急ぎます いそぎます, 急いで いそいで, 急がない いそがない. Notice ぐ becoming いで in the て-form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[走る](走る.md) はしる means "run", which names a way of moving rather than urgency itself. 慌てる あわてる can mean rushing in a fluster; 急ぐ does not require panic. ゆっくり (slowly; unhurriedly) gives a practical contrast, as in ゆっくり行く ゆっくりいく (go without hurrying), rather than a one-word verb antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

急いでください いそいでください means "Please hurry"; 急がなくてもいいです いそがなくてもいいです means "You don't have to hurry". A person can hurry without running, so 急ぐ and 走る are not interchangeable.
