# 科学 (かがく) — science

## Meaning

科学 means science, the field of inquiry rather than a single school lesson.
As a noun it can be the subject studied, as in 科学を学ぶ (かがくをまなぶ), "study science."
Use の to relate it to another noun: 科学の本 (かがくのほん), "a book about science."

- 科学の本を読んでいます。 (かがくのほんをよんでいます。) — I am reading a book about science.
- 子供のころから科学に興味があります。 (こどものころからかがくにきょうみがあります。) — I have been interested in science since childhood.

## How the kanji combine

[科](../kanji/科.md) contributes the idea of a branch or department, and [学](../kanji/学.md) concerns study or learning.
The combination names science; it is not a literal label for every school department.
Its on readings are カ + ガク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

理科 (りか) also translates as "science," notably the school subject.
For a science class on a school timetable, 理科 is a useful word to recognize;
科学 is useful for discussing science as a field.
This is a practical distinction, not a claim that 理科 has no broader dictionary senses.

化学 (かがく) means chemistry. It has exactly the same pronunciation as 科学,
but its first kanji and scope differ: chemistry is a particular science.

## Distinctive meaning

An English gloss alone cannot distinguish 科学 from the homophone 化学.
In writing, check 科 versus 化; in speech, use the topic to work out whether the speaker means science generally or chemistry.
Neither word is the opposite of the other.
