# 日にち (ひにち) - calendar date; number of days

## Meaning

日にち means a date or an appointed day.
It also means the number of days something takes or requires.

- 会議の日にちを決めましょう。 (かいぎのひにちをきめましょう。) - Let's decide the date of the meeting.
- この修理には日にちがかかります。 (このしゅうりにはひにちがかかります。) - This repair will take some days.

In the first, one identifies a calendar day.
In the second, the focus is the amount of time, not a particular date.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) contributes the day element; learn the complete
native reading ひにち.
The dictionary also writes this word 日日, the raw source spelling.
Do not automatically read every repeated 日 spelling as ひにち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[日付](日付.md) (ひづけ) overlaps with a calendar date.
[日時](日時.md) (にちじ) specifies a day and time;
[日程](日程.md) (にってい) concerns a schedule.
日数 (にっすう) explicitly names a number of days.
There is no single opposite covering both date and duration.

## Distinctive meaning

The source row pairs 日日 and ひにち with the glosses "every day, daily,
day after day." Those glosses do not describe this reading's dictionary
family. Daily/day-after-day belongs to 日々 or 日日 read ひび or にちにち,
a different family.
This card preserves recognition of the source spelling while teaching
the date/day-count meanings for ひにち.
