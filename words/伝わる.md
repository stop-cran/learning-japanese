# 伝わる (つたわる) - be transmitted; be handed down

## Meaning

伝わる describes something reaching others or passing from one place or generation to another.
It is intransitive: the news, feeling, sound or tradition takes が; the recipient or destination can take に.

- ニュースが町中に伝わりました。
  (ニュースがまちじゅうにつたわりました。) - The news spread throughout the town.
- この技術は親から子へ伝わってきました。
  (このぎじゅつはおやからこへつたわってきました。) - This technique has been handed down from parent to child.
- 音が壁を通して伝わります。
  (おとがかべをとおしてつたわります。) - Sound travels through the wall.

The same verb describes a custom or technology being introduced from elsewhere.
気持ちが伝わる (きもちがつたわる) means that one's feelings get across, not necessarily that someone states them explicitly.

## How the kanji combine

[伝](../kanji/伝.md) contributes transmission. This native verb has the reading つたわる;
わる is okurigana, not a separate word. It is godan: 伝わります, 伝わった, 伝わって.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[伝える](伝える.md) (つたえる) is transitive: someone conveys something.
知らせを伝える (しらせをつたえる), "convey the news," contrasts with 知らせが伝わる, "the news gets around."
[広がる](広がる.md) (ひろがる) emphasizes spreading; [通じる](通じる.md) (つうじる) can emphasize getting through or being understood.

For the transmission of a signal, 途絶える (とだえる), "be interrupted; die out," can describe the reverse outcome.
It is not a universal opposite for every inherited custom or conveyed feeling.

## Distinctive meaning

An intentional sender is not required. A rumor can spread, heat or electricity can travel, and a feeling can come across.
Distinguish what is being transmitted from the person deliberately transmitting it.
