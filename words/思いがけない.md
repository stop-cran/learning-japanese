# 思いがけない (おもいがけない) - unexpected; unforeseen

## Meaning

思いがけない describes something not expected or anticipated. It can concern a welcome surprise or an unwelcome event.

- 思いがけない贈り物をもらいました。 (おもいがけないおくりものをもらいました。) - I received an unexpected gift.
- 旅先で思いがけない出会いがありました。 (たびさきでおもいがけないであいがありました。) - I had an unexpected encounter while traveling.

Use it directly before a noun. 思いがけなく + predicate gives "unexpectedly," as in 思いがけなく再会した (おもいがけなくさいかいした), "met again unexpectedly."

## How the kanji combine

[思](../kanji/思.md) contributes thinking or expectation. Also recognize 思い掛けない (おもいがけない), a same-entry spelling using [掛](../kanji/掛.md); JMdict marks that fuller kanji spelling as rare. The primary spelling here leaves がけない in kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意外](意外.md) (いがい) overlaps in unexpectedness but uses な before a noun. [案外](案外.md) (あんがい) can work as an adverb. [偶然](偶然.md) (ぐうぜん) and [たまたま](たまたま.md) highlight chance rather than necessarily the same person's expectations.

## Distinctive meaning

An unexpected event need not happen suddenly. The ending ない belongs to this established adjective; do not interpret 思いがけない as a general sentence meaning "I do not think." Its chance-encounter use also does not mean "casual" in the sense of informal clothes or a relaxed manner.
