# 対象 (たいしょう) - target; object of an activity

## Meaning

対象 is the person, thing, or group that an activity, rule, or attention is directed toward. Learn Nを対象にする, "make N the target," and Nが対象になる, "N comes within the scope."

- この講座は初心者を対象にしています。 (このこうざはしょしんしゃをたいしょうにしています。) - This course is intended for beginners.
- その商品は割引の対象ではありません。 (そのしょうひんはわりびきのたいしょうではありません。) - That product is not eligible for the discount.

研究の対象 (けんきゅうのたいしょう) is "the object of research": what is being studied.

## How the kanji combine

[対](../kanji/対.md) contributes facing or being directed toward something. 象 contributes an object or phenomenon here, not an elephant. Together they identify what an activity faces or addresses. The on readings are タイ + ショウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[相手](相手.md) (あいて) is a partner, opponent, or person one deals with. [目的](目的.md) (もくてき) is an aim or purpose. A research 対象 is what is studied; its 目的 is why the research is done.

## Distinctive meaning

対象 can be people without implying hostility: beginners are the intended audience in the first example. 対象外 (たいしょうがい), "outside the scope; ineligible," is a useful contrast for coverage or eligibility. Identify the particular rule or activity before translating the noun as a generic English "target."
