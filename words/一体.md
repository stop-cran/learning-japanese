# 一体 (いったい) - what on earth; one body

## Meaning

As an adverb before a question word, 一体 adds surprise, puzzlement, or frustration: "what on earth," "why in the world," and similar expressions. It does not supply the question by itself.

- 一体何が起きたんですか。 (いったいなにがおきたんですか。) - What on earth happened?

As a noun, it means one body or a unified whole. 一体になる means "become one" or "unite."

- 選手と観客が一体になりました。 (せんしゅとかんきゃくがいったいになりました。) - The players and spectators became united.

The dictionary also records the adverbial meaning "in general; on the whole," including 一体に (いったいに). That is distinct from the emphatic-question construction.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," modifies [体](../kanji/体.md), "body": one body or unit. The compound is いったい, with a small っ. This component image explains the noun more directly than the question-emphasizing adverb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一緒](一緒.md) (いっしょ) means being or doing something together; 一体 emphasizes unity as a whole. In a question, simply omitting 一体 keeps the basic question but removes this particular emphasis.

## Distinctive meaning

Context decides which meaning is intended; the pronunciation いったい stays the same. 一体どうして asks "why on earth," while 一体となって働く (いったいとなってはたらく) means "work as one." Emphatic questions can sound challenging, so consider tone and the situation.
