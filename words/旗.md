# 旗 (はた) - flag

## Meaning

旗 is a noun meaning "flag." It can identify a group, mark a
place, or convey a signal. Learn 旗を振る, "wave a flag,"
and 旗を立てる, "put up a flag."

- 応援する人たちが旗を振っています。 (おうえんするひとたちがはたをふっています。) - The supporters are waving flags.
- 入口に赤い旗を立てました。 (いりぐちにあかいはたをたてました。) - We put a red flag at the entrance.

を marks the flag being handled, while に marks where it is
placed. A color or group name can specify which flag is meant.

## How the kanji combine

旗 represents the whole native noun はた. There is no
multi-character compound to divide. The same character also occurs
with an on reading in compounds such as 国旗 (こっき),
"national flag"; do not transfer that compound reading to the
standalone はた.

## Synonyms and antonyms

国旗 is a particular kind of flag, not a synonym covering every
旗. [印](印.md) (しるし), "mark; sign," is broader: a flag can function
as a sign, but a sign need not be a flag. There is no useful
general antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun identifies the object; the situation supplies its message.
A red 旗 is not automatically a danger signal in every setting.
It may identify a team or mark a location, so interpret the flag
alongside its local use.
