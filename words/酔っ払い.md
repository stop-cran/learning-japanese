# 酔っ払い (よっぱらい) - a drunk; drunkenness

## Meaning

酔っ払い can name a drunk person or the state of being drunk.
It is a noun, not an i-adjective despite the final い.

- 酔っ払いが大声で話していました。 (よっぱらいがおおごえではなしていました。) - A drunk person was talking loudly.
- 酔っ払い運転 (よっぱらいうんてん) - drunk driving.

The spelling 酔っぱらい is also recognized.
The word can sound disparaging when used to label someone.

## How the kanji combine

酔 contributes intoxication, and [払](../kanji/払.md) appears in
the native verb 酔っ払う (よっぱらう), "get drunk."
酔っ払い is its noun form. Keep the small っ and the ぱらい
sequence; do not interpret this as an on-reading compound or as
literally paying someone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[酔う](酔う.md) (よう) is a verb for becoming intoxicated; it also
has a separate motion-sickness use. 酔っ払い here concerns alcohol,
not a person merely feeling sick on a bus.
しらふ, sober, provides a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

English "a drunk" can suggest a habitual heavy drinker, but this
Japanese word does not require a continuing habit or dependence.
It can label a person who is drunk on this occasion.
Do not equate drinking some alcohol, being drunk and having an
alcohol-use disorder.
