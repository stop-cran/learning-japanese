# 打ち合わせ (うちあわせ) - preparatory meeting; advance arrangements

## Meaning

打ち合わせ is a preparatory meeting, briefing or discussion to coordinate details in advance.
It is a noun and can be used with する.
The spelling 打合せ, also read うちあわせ, writes the same word.

- 明日の打ち合わせは三時からです。 (あしたのうちあわせはさんじからです。) - Tomorrow's planning meeting starts at three.
- 資料について、担当者と打ち合わせをしました。 (しりょうについて、たんとうしゃとうちあわせをしました。) - I discussed the materials with the person responsible to coordinate the details.

人と打ち合わせをする names the person consulted; 内容について names the topic.
The meeting can be brief or remote; the word does not set a minimum group size.

## How the kanji combine

The native parts are 打ち (うち), related to [打つ](打つ.md), and 合わせ (あわせ),
related to 合わせる (あわせる), "bring together; coordinate."
[打](../kanji/打.md) and [合](../kanji/合.md) form a lexical combination for arranging matters together;
the meeting is not a literal act of hitting someone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) overlaps in meeting and discussion.
打ち合わせ particularly highlights preparation or coordination, not an absolute informal/formal division.
[相談](相談.md) (そうだん) is talking something over or seeking advice; not every consultation is preparation for a shared event.

## Distinctive meaning

Making arrangements is not the same as every promise or service booking.
[予約](予約.md) (よやく) names a reservation; 打ち合わせ need not reserve a seat or room.
This article teaches the coordination sense, not the separate clothing-overlap or archaic matching senses.
