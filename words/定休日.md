# 定休日 (ていきゅうび) - regular closing day; fixed day off

## Meaning

定休日 is a regularly designated day off or closing day, especially for a shop or business. It identifies the established closure schedule rather than an unexpected closure.

- この店の定休日は月曜日です。
  (このみせのていきゅうびはげつようびです。) - This shop's regular closing day is Monday.
- 明日は定休日なので、店は開いていません。
  (あしたはていきゅうびなので、みせはあいていません。) - The shop will not be open tomorrow because it is its regular day off.

定休日なし (ていきゅうびなし) means "no regular closing day"; it does not promise that the business can never close for another reason.

## How the kanji combine

[定](../kanji/定.md) contributes setting or fixing, [休](../kanji/休.md) resting, and [日](../kanji/日.md) a day. 定休 identifies scheduled time off, and 日 identifies the day. The reading is ていきゅうび, with 日 read び.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[休日](休日.md) (きゅうじつ) and [休み](休み.md) (やすみ) are broader day-off expressions. [祝日](祝日.md) (しゅくじつ) is a public holiday; it need not coincide with a shop's 定休日. 営業日 (えいぎょうび) names a day on which the business operates.

## Distinctive meaning

A closure for emergency repairs is not automatically the regular closing day. Conversely, a 定休日 can fall on an ordinary weekday. The important point is its place in that business's recurring schedule, not whether the whole country has the day off.
