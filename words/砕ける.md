# 砕ける (くだける) - break into pieces; become less formal

## Meaning

砕ける means break into pieces or be smashed. It is intransitive: the thing breaking is the subject, normally marked with が.

- 落とした氷が砕けました。 (おとしたこおりがくだけました。) - The ice I dropped broke into pieces.
- 岩に当たって波が砕けます。 (いわにあたってなみがくだけます。) - The waves break against the rocks.

An additional useful use is 砕けた話し方 (くだけたはなしかた), a relaxed or informal way of speaking. Here 砕けた describes reduced formality, not damaged speech.

The ichidan forms include 砕けます (くだけます), 砕けて (くだけて), 砕けない (くだけない).

## How the kanji combine

砕 carries the breaking/crushing association; ける completes this native verb. No 砕 kanji card is currently available. The shared character helps connect the physical meanings, not every expression containing it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[砕く](砕く.md) (くだく) names breaking something: 氷を砕く. 砕ける names what happens to it: 氷が砕ける.

[壊れる](壊れる.md) (こわれる) is broader breakage or loss of function. [崩れる](崩れる.md) (くずれる) emphasizes collapse or loss of a coherent shape. The physical uses overlap; they are not separated by an exact fragment-size rule.

## Distinctive meaning

Intransitive does not mean that no one caused the breakage. Distinguish physical fragments from the figurative relaxation in 砕けた態度 (くだけたたいど), an informal manner. Whether that manner is appropriate depends on the situation.
