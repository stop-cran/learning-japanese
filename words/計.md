# 計 (けい) - plan; total

## Meaning

計 has several construction-dependent uses. As a noun it can mean a plan, as in
一年の計 (いちねんのけい), "a plan for the year." Before a quantity, it means
"in total." As a suffix, it can name a measuring instrument.

- 一年の計は元旦にあり。 (いちねんのけいはがんたんにあり。) - A year's plan is made on New Year's Day.
- 大人三人、子供二人で、計五人です。 (おとなさんにん、こどもふたりで、けいごにんです。) - Three adults and two children make five people in total.

The first is a saying about planning early, not an instruction that plans may
be made on no other day. 温度計 (おんどけい), "thermometer," illustrates the
instrument suffix.

## How the kanji combine

[計](../kanji/計.md) concerns reckoning, measuring, or planning. This single-kanji
word uses the on reading ケイ. Its role changes with its position in an expression;
the meanings are not interchangeable translations of every occurrence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計画](計画.md) (けいかく) is a straightforward word for a plan in everyday discussion.
[合計](合計.md) (ごうけい) also means a total and can take する for totaling things.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 計五人 as "five people altogether," not "five plans." Conversely, the 計
in 一年の計 does not mean a total. The number pattern and the instrument suffix
help identify the intended sense without relying on a single English gloss.
