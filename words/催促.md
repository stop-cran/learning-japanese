# 催促 (さいそく) - pressing for action; urging

## Meaning

催促 is urging someone to do something promptly, or pressing for something expected. 催促する is transitive: the requested response or item can take を, and the person approached can take に.

- 友達に返事を催促しました。 (ともだちにへんじをさいそくしました。) - I pressed my friend for a reply.
- 支払いの催促が来ました。 (しはらいのさいそくがきました。) - A payment reminder arrived.

催促の電話 (さいそくのでんわ) is a phone call pressing for action. Context determines how insistent or courteous the reminder is.

## How the kanji combine

催 has prompting or bringing-about associations in this compound; 促 contributes urging or pressing. The characters reinforce getting an action to happen. The on readings are サイ + ソク. This is not an instruction to translate every 催 compound as a demand.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[要求](要求.md) (ようきゅう) is a demand or firm request; [請求](請求.md) (せいきゅう) can concern a claim or bill. 催促 focuses on pressing for the response or action, rather than merely stating the amount owed. [頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ) is a broader asking/requesting verb.

## Distinctive meaning

An initial request is not automatically a 催促. The word highlights getting someone to act without further delay, often when a reply, payment, or other action is awaited. It does not by itself establish threats, legal enforcement, or an angry tone.
