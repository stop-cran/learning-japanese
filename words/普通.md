# 普通 (ふつう)

## Meaning

普通 means ordinary, normal, or usual. As an adverb it means "usually" or "normally." On railway signs, it can mean a local train stopping at every station.

- 普通の靴で大丈夫です。 (ふつうの くつで だいじょうぶです。) — Ordinary shoes are fine.
- 普通、七時に起きます。 (ふつう、しちじに おきます。) — I usually get up at seven.
- 急行ではなく、普通に乗ってください。 (きゅうこうではなく、ふつうに のってください。) — Please take the local train, not the express.

## How the kanji combine

普 concerns generality, and [通](../kanji/通.md) concerns passing through or common usage. The on readings are ふ + つう; keep the long final vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

通常 (つうじょう) is a more formal "usual; normally." 特別 (とくべつ), "special," contrasts with ordinary. In railway use, 各駅停車 (かくえきていしゃ) explicitly means an all-stations service.

## Distinctive meaning

普通の + noun is a useful basic pattern; the dictionary also recognizes adjectival 普通な. Bare 普通 can modify a whole statement. 普通に can mean "normally," so the train sense depends on transport context, as in the third example. Service labels vary by operator.
