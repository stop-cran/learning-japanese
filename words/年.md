# 年 (とし) — year; age

## Meaning

年 read とし means a year. It can also mean a person's age or years of life. Learn it in complete expressions rather than using とし after every number.

- 今年はいい年でした。  
  ことしはいいとしでした。  
  "This was a good year."
- 兄とは年が三つ違います。  
  あにとはとしがみっつちがいます。  
  "My older brother and I are three years apart in age."

## How the kanji combine

[年](../kanji/年.md) is one character with the native reading とし here. It has the on reading ねん in counted years: 一年 (いちねん), "one year". 今年 (ことし), "this year", has its own whole-word reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

年齢 (ねんれい) explicitly means "age". 年間 (ねんかん) concerns a span of years or a yearly period, depending on the construction. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun とし and the year-counting reading ねん are related but not interchangeable. For a person's numbered age, use an age expression such as 九歳 (きゅうさい), not 九年.
