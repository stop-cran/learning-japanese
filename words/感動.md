# 感動 (かんどう) - being deeply moved

## Meaning

感動 names a strong emotional response, such as being moved by a story, music, or someone's actions. With する, the person experiences the feeling; the thing that moves them can take に.

- 映画の最後の場面に感動しました。 (えいがのさいごのばめんにかんどうしました。) - I was moved by the final scene of the film.
- みんなの優しさに感動しました。 (みんなのやさしさにかんどうしました。) - I was deeply touched by everyone's kindness.

感動的な話 (かんどうてきなはなし) is "a moving story." This describes the story's emotional effect, while 感動した describes someone's response.

## How the kanji combine

[感](../kanji/感.md) concerns feeling, and [動](../kanji/動.md) concerns movement. Together they suggest the emotions being stirred. This is a useful meaning connection, not a claim about the word's historical origin. Both readings are on readings: カン + ドウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[感心](感心.md) (かんしん) often expresses admiration for an action, ability, or achievement. It can overlap with 感動, but admiring someone's efficiency is not necessarily the same as being emotionally moved.

## Distinctive meaning

English "impressed" can describe either admiration or emotional impact. 感動 emphasizes the latter. It does not simply mean noticing something, and need not imply that the person actually cried.
