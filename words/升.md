# 升 (ます) — measuring box

## Meaning

升 is a measuring container or measuring box. JMdict also gives a second sense: a box, as in box seating at a theatre and similar places. The compound 升席 ますせき is a tatami “box seat” for four people at sumo, kabuki and similar shows (JMdict entry 1706800).

- 升で米の量をはかります。 (ますでこめのりょうをはかります。) — We measure the amount of rice with a measuring box.
- 木の升に日本酒を注いでもらいました。 (きのますににほんしゅをついでもらいました。) — They poured sake for me into a wooden masu (measuring box).

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji word. The character [升](../kanji/升.md) is read ます here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[箱](箱.md) (はこ) is a box in general; a 升 is a box used for measuring. [容器](容器.md) (ようき) is any container. [腰掛け](腰掛け.md) (こしかけ) is a seat or bench (JMdict entry 1288370). The second sense of 升 is a whole box of seating, such as a 升席, not a single seat or bench.

## Distinctive meaning

Read しょう, the same character is the volume unit shō, about 1.8 litres (JMdict entry 2248100). JMdict also lists 枡 and 桝 as spellings of ます.
