# 升 (ます) — measuring box; box seat

## Meaning

升 is a measuring container or measuring box. JMdict also gives a box seat at a theatre and a square on a grid.

- 升で米の量をはかります。 (ますでこめのりょうをはかります。) — We measure the amount of rice with a measuring box.
- 木の升に日本酒を注いでもらいました。 (きのますににほんしゅをついでもらいました。) — They poured sake for me into a wooden measuring box.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji word. The character [升](../kanji/升.md) is read ます here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[箱](箱.md) (はこ) is a box in general; a 升 is a box used for measuring. [容器](容器.md) (ようき) is any container.

## Distinctive meaning

Read しょう, the same character is the volume unit shō, about 1.8 litres (JMdict entry 2248100). JMdict also lists 枡 and 桝 as spellings of ます.
