# 止める (とめる・やめる)

## Meaning

Two verbs share this spelling. **とめる** stops motion, operation, or an ongoing process. **やめる**, often written in kana, means to discontinue an activity, quit, or abandon a plan.

- ここで車を止めてください。 (ここで くるまを とめてください。) — Please stop the car here.
- 夜遅く食べるのをやめました。 (よるおそく たべるのを やめました。) — I stopped eating late at night.

Both are transitive ichidan verbs: とめます・とめて・とめない and やめます・やめて・やめない. A stopped thing takes を; for stopping an action, a plain verb + のをやめる is useful.

The とめる entry also covers fastening something in place and detaining someone, especially written **留める**.

- 紙をクリップで留めてください。 (かみをクリップでとめてください。) — Please fasten the papers with a clip.
- 警察はその男を署に留めました。 (けいさつはそのおとこをしょにとめました。) — The police detained the man at the station.

Here を marks what or who is held; で marks the fastening tool and に the place of detention.

## How the kanji combine

[止](../kanji/止.md) carries the stopping meaning. The shared める ending does not establish one interchangeable reading: learn とめる and やめる separately. The conjugation bases include め.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[止まる](止まる.md) (とまる) is intransitive: 車が止まる, "the car stops." [続ける](続ける.md) (つづける), "continue," contrasts with discontinuing an activity.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 音楽をとめる (おんがくをとめる), "stop the music," with 練習をやめる (れんしゅうをやめる), "stop practicing." For resigning from a job, [辞める](辞める.md) is the usual more specific spelling of やめる.

For stopping or switching off, as in 機械を止める (きかいをとめる), "stop the machine,"
the dictionary favors 止める or 停める. It favors 留める for fastening and detaining.
These are spelling preferences within the とめる entry, not hard exclusions assigning each spelling
an entirely separate verb.
