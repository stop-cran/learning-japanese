# 標識 (ひょうしき) - sign; marker

## Meaning

標識 is a sign, mark, or marker used to identify something or give
information, such as a road sign. It can also refer to a beacon.
道路標識 (どうろひょうしき) means a road or traffic sign.

- 道路標識をよく見てください。 (どうろひょうしきをよくみてください。) - Please look carefully at the road signs.
- 入口に案内の標識があります。 (いりぐちにあんないのひょうしきがあります。) - There is a direction sign at the entrance.

The noun does not itself specify what the sign says.

## How the kanji combine

標 contributes a mark or signpost, and [識](../kanji/識.md)
recognition or identification. Together they concern an identifying
or informative mark. The on readings are ヒョウ + シキ.
This meaning association is not a historical derivation of the characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[印](印.md) (しるし) is a mark or sign more broadly;
[記号](記号.md) (きごう) is a symbol or notation.
[看板](看板.md) (かんばん) is a signboard.
[信号](信号.md) (しんごう) can mean a transmitted signal or
traffic light, rather than a fixed road sign.

## Distinctive meaning

A 標識 need not be a board, and a traffic sign need not be a traffic light.
The shared English "sign" does not erase those distinctions.
[兆候](兆候.md) (ちょうこう) and [気配](気配.md) (けはい)
concern indications of a condition or presence, not the ordinary name
for a road marker.
The dictionary additionally records a linguistic marker sense;
do not restrict every use to roads or physical signposts.
