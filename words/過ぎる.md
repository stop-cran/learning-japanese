# 過ぎる (すぎる) — pass; go beyond

## Meaning

過ぎる describes passing a place or going beyond a point; it also describes time passing.
It is an intransitive verb. In a route expression, を marks the place passed, not an object being changed.

- 駅を過ぎてから、右に曲がってください。 (えきをすぎてから、みぎにまがってください。) — After passing the station, please turn right.
- 一時間が過ぎました。 (いちじかんがすぎました。) — An hour passed.

It can also mean exceeding an age or other reference point:
三十歳を過ぎてから、日本語を習い始めました。
(さんじゅっさいをすぎてから、にほんごをならいはじめました。)
— I began learning Japanese after I turned thirty.

## How the kanji combine

[過](../kanji/過.md) contributes going beyond; ぎる completes the native word すぎる.
The polite form is 過ぎます (すぎます), and the て-form is 過ぎて (すぎて).
It is an ichidan verb: remove final る to obtain the base すぎ-.

## Synonyms and antonyms

通る (とおる) can describe going along or through a route.
過ぎる draws attention to being beyond a reference point.
過ごす (すごす), "spend time," instead takes the time spent with を: [休日](休日.md)を過ごす (きゅうじつをすごす), "spend a day off."

## Distinctive meaning

A related excessive-use suffix is usually written すぎる in kana.
Attach it to a verb's ます-stem: 食べます → 食べすぎる (たべすぎる), "eat too much."
For an ordinary い-adjective, remove い: 高い → 高すぎる (たかすぎる), "too high/expensive."
This suffix use is distinct from passing a station.
