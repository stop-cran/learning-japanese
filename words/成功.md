# 成功 (せいこう) - success; achievement

## Meaning

成功 is success in achieving an intended result. 成功する is intransitive.
Use an undertaking + が + 成功する, or a person + が + undertaking + に +
成功する.

- 実験が成功しました。 (じっけんがせいこうしました。) - The experiment succeeded.
- 彼女は新しい技術の開発に成功しました。 (かのじょはあたらしいぎじゅつのかいはつにせいこうしました。) - She succeeded in developing a new technology.

The second construction puts に, not を, after the achievement.
成功を喜ぶ (せいこうをよろこぶ), "rejoice at success," instead uses 成功 as
the object of a different verb.

## How the kanji combine

[成](../kanji/成.md) contributes accomplishing or becoming, and 功 contributes
achievement or merit. Together they convey accomplishment. The on readings
are セイ + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[失敗](失敗.md) (しっぱい), "failure," is a direct contrast.
[結果](結果.md) (けっか) means a result whether favorable or unfavorable;
a result is not automatically a success.

## Distinctive meaning

成功 can refer to one completed task or broader success in life and work:
事業で成功する (じぎょうでせいこうする), "succeed in business."
What counts as success comes from the goal and context. It is not a verb
meaning to hit something physically, even when English calls a successful
work "a hit."
