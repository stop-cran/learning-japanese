# 指導 (しどう) - guidance; coaching

## Meaning

指導 is guidance, instruction, or coaching that directs someone's learning or activity.
指導する is transitive: 学生を指導する (がくせいをしどうする), "instruct students."
From the learner's side, 指導を受ける (しどうをうける) means "receive instruction."

- コーチは若い選手を指導しています。 (コーチはわかいせんしゅをしどうしています。) - The coach is training young athletes.
- 先生から作文の指導を受けました。 (せんせいからさくぶんのしどうをうけました。) - I received guidance on essay writing from my teacher.

In the second sentence, から marks the source of the guidance, and 作文の identifies its subject.
丁寧に指導する (ていねいにしどうする) means "instruct carefully."

## How the kanji combine

[指](../kanji/指.md) contributes pointing or indicating, and 導 guiding or leading.
Together they suggest showing someone a direction and helping them follow it.
The on-readings are シ + ドウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[教える](教える.md) (おしえる) is the broader verb "teach; tell."
[案内](案内.md) (あんない) can mean guiding someone around a place, rather than coaching their performance.
助言 (じょげん), "advice," may be one part of 指導.

## Distinctive meaning

指導 can cover an ongoing educational or supervisory relationship, not just one piece of information.
It need not imply harsh discipline: the manner of instruction must be stated separately.
The learner receives 指導; the instructor carries it out.
