# 見出し (みだし) - heading; headline; index

## Meaning

見出し is a heading, headline, title or caption that helps identify a piece of text. It also has an index use and can abbreviate 見出し語 (みだしご), "headword."

- 新聞の見出しを読みました。 (しんぶんのみだしをよみました。) - I read the newspaper headlines.
- 各節に短い見出しを付けました。 (かくせつにみじかいみだしをつけました。) - I added a short heading to each section.

In dictionary discussion, 見出し語 is the word under which an entry is listed. The index use concerns a way of locating material, not the whole content of the text.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md), "see," and [出](../kanji/出.md), "put out," are associated with 見出す (みいだす), "find; discover." Learn the noun's actual reading みだし; do not mechanically read it みいだし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[題名](題名.md) (だいめい) is a title or heading. [項目](項目.md) (こうもく) can be a listed item or heading, and [索引](索引.md) (さくいん) is an index. [要点](要点.md) (ようてん) is the main point or gist, which is not necessarily printed as a heading.

## Distinctive meaning

A 見出し labels or helps locate material; it need not reproduce a full explanation. Do not infer that the word means every kind of subtitle, especially translated dialogue displayed on a screen.
