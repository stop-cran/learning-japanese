# 発揮 (はっき) - exercise or display an ability

## Meaning

発揮 is the exercise or display of power, ability or a characteristic.
With する it is transitive: 能力を発揮する (のうりょくをはっきする), "exercise one's ability."

- 試合で実力を発揮しました。 (しあいでじつりょくをはっきしました。) - I showed my true ability in the match.
- 彼女は指導力を発揮しました。 (かのじょはしどうりょくをはっきしました。) - She demonstrated leadership.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) contributes bringing forth; 揮 concerns wielding or displaying here.
Think of bringing an ability into effective action, not exhibiting an object in a museum.
The on-reading ハツ is shortened before キ: はつ + き becomes はっき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[現す](現す.md) (あらわす), [表す](表す.md) (あらわす) and [示す](示す.md) (しめす)
overlap with showing or manifesting something, but have broader uses.
[活用](活用.md) (かつよう) emphasizes putting something to useful work.
[デモ](デモ.md) includes demonstration, as well as the distinct protest sense.

## Distinctive meaning

The object is typically an ability, power or characteristic being brought out.
An English source gloss "exhibition" does not make 発揮 a general word for an [展覧会](展覧会.md) (てんらんかい).
[実力](実力.md) (じつりょく) can mean actual ability or force; the intended quality depends on context.
