# のこぎり (のこぎり) - saw

## Meaning

のこぎり is a saw: a cutting tool with a toothed blade. Saws are used for wood and other materials; the appropriate blade depends on the material. The word is usually written in kana, and ノコギリ and 鋸 are also recognizable spellings.

- のこぎりで板を切りました。 (のこぎりでいたをきりました。) - I cut a board with a saw.
- こののこぎりはよく切れます。 (こののこぎりはよくきれます。) - This saw cuts well.

In the first example, で marks the tool used.

## How the kanji combine

鋸 represents the whole word のこぎり. This article uses the usual kana spelling; do not split its syllables into invented kanji meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

A [ナイフ](ナイフ.md) is a knife, another cutting tool, but not another name for a saw. [切る](切る.md) (きる), "cut," names the action rather than the tool.

## Distinctive meaning

The word identifies the kind of tool, not a particular power source or a guarantee that it can cut every material. Use のこぎりで切る for cutting with a saw; のこぎり itself is a noun, not the Japanese verb corresponding to English "saw."
