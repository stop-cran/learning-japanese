# 本部 (ほんぶ) - headquarters; head office

## Meaning

本部 is the headquarters or head office directing an organization or an operation. Depending on context, it can refer to the central organization, its staff, or the place where it works.

- 本部に報告してください。 (ほんぶにほうこくしてください。) - Please report to headquarters.
- 大会の本部は入口の近くにあります。 (たいかいのほんぶはいりぐちのちかくにあります。) - The event headquarters is near the entrance.

## How the kanji combine

[本](../kanji/本.md) contributes main or central; [部](../kanji/部.md) contributes section or organizational unit. This is a modifier-plus-head compound: the main directing unit. Read ほん + ぶ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[支店](支店.md) (してん), a branch office or shop, is a useful contrast to a central office. 支部 (しぶ) is a branch of an organization.

[事務所](事務所.md) (じむしょ) means an office. A 本部 may occupy an office, but 本部 specifies its central organizational role, not simply a room used for administration.

## Distinctive meaning

The word is not restricted to a company's permanent building. A temporary 本部 can coordinate a competition or emergency response. Identify the organization or operation from context rather than translating every office as "headquarters."
