# あっ (あっ) - ah!; oh!

## Meaning

あっ is an interjection for a sudden realization, recollection, or surprise. It stands outside the sentence's ordinary particle pattern; it is not a verb or a word meaning agreement.

- あっ、鍵を忘れた！ (あっ、かぎをわすれた！) - Oh, I forgot my keys!
- あっ、田中さん！ (あっ、たなかさん！) - Oh, Tanaka!

The second example can both register recognizing someone and attract that person's attention. Tone and the following words tell the listener what prompted the reaction.

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana-only exclamation, with no kanji components. Small っ represents an abrupt cutoff here, rather than a separately pronounced *tsu*. The spelling アッ is also recorded. [あ](あ.md) is a related spelling in the same dictionary entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ええ](ええ.md) can answer affirmatively, whereas the surprised あっ in the first example does not mean "yes." Interjections have conversational functions rather than a useful single antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

あっ signals the immediate reaction; the sentence after it supplies the content. It can precede an apology or warning, but it does not itself mean "sorry" or "watch out." Avoid treating an attention-getting exclamation as a complete polite request.
