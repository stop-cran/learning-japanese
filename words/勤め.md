# 勤め (つとめ) - work; duty; religious service

## Meaning

勤め is a noun connected with work, service, duties, or responsibilities. It also names Buddhist religious services. The setting is essential: an office and a temple suggest different kinds of 勤め.

- 今日の勤めを終えて、家に帰りました。 (きょうのつとめをおえて、いえにかえりました。) - I finished today's work and went home.
- お寺で朝のお勤めに参加しました。 (おてらであさのおつとめにさんかしました。) - I attended the morning Buddhist service at the temple.

The polite prefix お occurs in お勤め. It does not by itself decide whether the topic is employment or religion.

## How the kanji combine

[勤](../kanji/勤.md) contributes service or diligent work. 勤め is related to the native verb [勤める](勤める.md) (つとめる), "work/be employed." The kana め is part of the noun's written form, not a second component.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) broadly names work or a job. [義務](義務.md) (ぎむ) emphasizes obligation. The dictionary also records 務め (つとめ), a useful spelling when discussing a responsibility such as 親の務め (おやのつとめ), "a parent's duty."

## Distinctive meaning

Do not impose an absolute spelling division that the dictionary does not give: both 勤め and 務め belong to the inspected entry without hard sense restrictions. At the same time, learn established phrases in context. The temple example is a religious observance, not simply an employee's shift at a temple.
