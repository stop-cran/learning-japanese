# 一流 (いちりゅう) - first-rate; leading

## Meaning

In its quality or rank use, 一流 means first-rate, top-class, or leading.
Use **一流の + noun**, and 一流だ or 一流です as a predicate.

- 一流の料理人から学びたいです。 (いちりゅうのりょうりにんからまなびたいです。) - I want to learn from a first-rate chef.
- あの選手の技術は一流です。 (あのせんしゅのぎじゅつはいちりゅうです。) - That athlete's technique is first-rate.

These statements evaluate quality or standing, rather than identifying
who arrived first.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) supplies one or first, and [流](../kanji/流.md) can
refer to a school or style as well as flow. Think of a first-ranked class
as a meaning aid for the use taught here, not a literal translation of
every 一流 expression. The on readings are イチ + リュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[トップ](トップ.md) can identify a leading position.
[最高](最高.md) (さいこう) expresses the highest level or "best."
二流 (にりゅう), second-rate, contrasts with the quality ranking;
it is not necessarily the person in second place in a particular race.

## Distinctive meaning

一流 does not guarantee a unique number-one position or an objective ranking.
Several people can be 一流 in the same field. This article covers the
first-rate meaning; the word also has other dictionary senses, such as
a school or someone's characteristic style.
