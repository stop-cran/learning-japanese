# 選手 (せんしゅ) - athlete; sports player

## Meaning

選手 is a noun for an athlete or a player in a sport. It can refer to a team member or a competitor in an individual event. The sport can come before it: サッカー選手, "soccer player."

- あの選手は走るのが速いです。 (あのせんしゅははしるのがはやいです。) - That athlete runs fast.
- 兄は学校のサッカーチームの選手です。 (あにはがっこうのサッカーチームのせんしゅです。) - My older brother is a player on the school's soccer team.

A number plus 人 counts the people: 三人の選手 (さんにんのせんしゅ), "three players."

## How the kanji combine

[選](../kanji/選.md) contributes choosing or selecting, and [手](../kanji/手.md) can identify a person engaged in an activity. This gives a useful association with a selected participant. Read セン + シュ; do not translate the word as a literal "chosen hand."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[チーム](チーム.md) names the collective team, not one player. [参加](参加.md) (さんか) means participation; 参加者 (さんかしゃ) is a participant more broadly, including in non-sporting activities. An athlete and a spectator occupy contrasting roles rather than being exact antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

選手 does not itself mean professional: school and amateur athletes can also be 選手. The source list includes "team," but this card teaches the dictionary-supported person or team-member meaning, not the whole team as an independent sense.
