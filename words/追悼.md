# 追悼 (ついとう) — mourning (for the dead)

## Meaning

追悼 is remembering a person who has died and grieving for them. Digital Daijisen gives 追悼の辞 (ついとうのじ), “a memorial address,” and 故人を追悼する (こじんをついとうする), “mourn the deceased.”

- 事故で亡くなった人々を追悼する式が、駅前で行われた。 (じこでなくなったひとびとをついとうするしきが、えきまえでおこなわれた。) — A ceremony to mourn the people who died in the accident was held in front of the station.
- その歌手が亡くなり、テレビ局は追悼番組を放送した。 (そのかしゅがなくなり、てれびきょくはついとうばんぐみをほうそうした。) — When the singer died, the TV station broadcast a memorial program.

## How the kanji combine

[追](../kanji/追.md) (つい) here means looking back on the past; Digital Daijisen lists 追悼 under that sense of 追. [悼](../kanji/悼.md) (とう) means to grieve or mourn.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[悲しむ](悲しむ.md) (かなしむ) is to feel sad about anything. [弔問](弔問.md) (ちょうもん) is a condolence visit to a bereaved family, and a [葬式](葬式.md) (そうしき) is the funeral itself. A 追悼式 (ついとうしき) is a memorial service.

## Distinctive meaning

In the first example, 追悼する takes the people who died as its object with を. 哀悼 (あいとう), “condolence,” also contains 悼; 哀悼の意 (あいとうのい) means “one’s condolences.”
