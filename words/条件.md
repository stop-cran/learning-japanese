# 条件 (じょうけん) - condition; requirement; terms

## Meaning

条件 is a noun for a condition, requirement, or agreed term. Useful patterns include 条件を満たす (じょうけんをみたす), "meet a requirement," and 条件に合う (じょうけんにあう), "fit the conditions."

- 応募には三年の経験が条件です。 (おうぼにはさんねんのけいけんがじょうけんです。) - Three years of experience is a requirement for applying.
- 毎月掃除することを条件に、部屋を貸します。 (まいつきそうじすることをじょうけんに、へやをかします。) - I will rent out the room on condition that it is cleaned every month.

In the second sentence, a verb clause is made noun-like with こと, then followed by を条件に.

## How the kanji combine

条 can refer to an item or clause, while [件](../kanji/件.md) refers to a matter or item. Together they identify specified conditions or provisions. Read ジョウ + ケン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[約束](約束.md) (やくそく) is a promise or arrangement; a promise may contain conditions but is not identical to a requirement. [規則](規則.md) (きそく) names rules. 無条件 (むじょうけん), "unconditional," contrasts with imposing conditions.

## Distinctive meaning

English "condition" also means a thing's physical state. For the condition of a broken machine, [状態](状態.md) (じょうたい) is useful instead. 条件 concerns requirements, terms, or circumstances affecting what is possible, as in working conditions or conditions for participation.
