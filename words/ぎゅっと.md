# ぎゅっと — tightly; with a firm squeeze

## Meaning

ぎゅっと describes holding, pressing, or squeezing something firmly.

- こぼれないように、スポンジをぎゅっと絞った。
  — I squeezed the sponge firmly so the water would not spill.
- 彼女は子どもの手をぎゅっと握った。
  — She held the child's hand tightly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぎゅっと emphasizes a firm, concentrated squeeze or hold. It does not describe the resulting
shape or texture by itself.

## Distinctive meaning

The action and object determine whether the word means squeezing, pressing, or holding tightly.
It can also describe forceful pushing; these examples focus on a firm grip.
