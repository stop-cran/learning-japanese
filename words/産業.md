# 産業 (さんぎょう) - industry

## Meaning

産業 means industry or an economic sector: organized activity producing goods or providing services.

- 観光はこの町の大切な産業です。 (かんこうはこのまちのたいせつなさんぎょうです。) - Tourism is an important industry in this town.

自動車産業 (じどうしゃさんぎょう) means "the automobile industry." The word can name a sector as a whole, rather than one company in it.

## How the kanji combine

[産](../kanji/産.md) contributes producing, and [業](../kanji/業.md) contributes work or business. The on readings are サン + ギョウ. Keep the long vowel in ぎょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

工業 (こうぎょう) focuses on manufacturing. 会社 (かいしゃ) is a company, an organization that may operate within an industry. 仕事 (しごと) is work or a job, not necessarily a whole sector.

## Distinctive meaning

産業 is broader than factories: the example concerns tourism and its services. English "industry" can also mean personal diligence, but that is not the practical meaning of 産業 taught here.
