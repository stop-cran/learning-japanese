# 取り消す (とりけす) - cancel; retract; revoke

## Meaning

取り消す means to cancel, withdraw, retract, or revoke something
previously arranged, stated, or granted. It is a transitive godan
verb: the reservation, statement, or permission takes を.

- ホテルの予約を取り消しました。 (ホテルのよやくをとりけしました。) - I canceled the hotel reservation.
- 先ほどの発言を取り消します。 (さきほどのはつげんをとりけします。) - I retract what I said earlier.

Forms include 取り消します (とりけします), 取り消して
(とりけして), and 取り消さない (とりけさない).
The written form 取消す has the same reading とりけす.

## How the kanji combine

[取](../kanji/取.md) contributes the 取り element, and
[消](../kanji/消.md) contributes 消す (けす), "erase; cancel."
Learn the established compound rather than translating each
element as a separate action of taking and then erasing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中止](中止.md) (ちゅうし) concerns cancellation or discontinuation
of an activity. 取り消す also works with retracting words or
revoking permission. [断る](断る.md) (ことわる) can mean refusing;
refusing a request is not necessarily withdrawing an earlier
agreement.

## Distinctive meaning

A retracted statement need not disappear from a recording:
取り消す concerns taking it back, not physically erasing every
trace. Whether canceling a reservation incurs a fee depends on
the arrangement, not on the verb itself.
