# もう一度 (もういちど) — once more

## Meaning

もう一度 means "once more" or "again." It modifies an action directly and is particularly useful when asking someone to repeat something.

- もう一度言ってください。 (もういちどいってください。) — Please say it again.
- この映画をもう一度見たいです。 (このえいがをもういちどみたいです。) — I want to see this film once more.

## How the kanji combine

Native もう supplies the additional repetition, while [一](../kanji/一.md) + [度](../kanji/度.md) gives 一度 (いちど), "one time." This is a mixed expression, filed under wago for its native first element, not an all-native compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もう一回 (もういっかい) also means "one more time." また means "again" more broadly and need not specify a single additional occasion.

## Distinctive meaning

もう by itself has several uses, including "already," but もう一度 asks for or describes another occurrence. Do not interpret it as "already one time." In a request, the phrase can be softened further with すみません, but the politeness mainly comes from the complete request form.
