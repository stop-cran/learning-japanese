# 得る (える) - obtain; gain

## Meaning

得る (える) means "obtain; gain." It is a transitive ichidan verb: what is gained takes を. Forms include 得ます (えます), 得ない (えない), and 得た (えた).

- この仕事で経験を得ました。 (このしごとでけいけんをえました。) - I gained experience through this work.
- 彼の協力を得ることができました。 (かれのきょうりょくをえることができました。) - We were able to secure his cooperation.

The reading **うる** also exists. For acquiring something, it is formal or literary: 知識を得る (ちしきをうる), "acquire knowledge." Do not treat it as a freely interchangeable everyday conjugation of える.

## How the kanji combine

[得](../kanji/得.md) supplies gaining or obtaining, with る completing the verb. The same spelling covers both readings, so a kanji-only sentence may not resolve the reading by itself.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もらう](もらう.md) emphasizes receiving from someone; 得る also covers acquiring experience or securing results. [失う](失う.md) (うしなう), "lose," is a useful opposite when discussing something gained or held.

## Distinctive meaning

After a verb's ます-stem, 得る can express possibility: 起こり得る (おこりうる / おこりえる), "can happen." This is not literally obtaining an event. The negative is 起こり得ない (おこりえない), "cannot happen," not おこりうらない. The うる pattern retains older conjugational behavior; do not conjugate it like an ordinary godan verb ending in る.
