# テント - tent

## Meaning

テント is a tent. It is a noun used for camping shelters and tents at events or other temporary sites. **テントを張る** (テントをはる) means "pitch a tent," and **テントを畳む** (テントをたたむ) means "fold up a tent."

- 湖の近くにテントを張りました。 (みずうみのちかくにテントをはりました。) - We pitched a tent near the lake.
- 夜はテントの中で寝ました。 (よるはテントのなかでねました。) - We slept inside the tent at night.

In the first example, に marks the site where the tent is set up. In the second, で marks the location of sleeping. **二人用のテント** (ふたりようのテント) is "a tent for two people."

## How the kanji combine

テント is a katakana loanword, with no kanji decomposition. Its three written units are テ・ン・ト. Keep ン distinct from a small ッ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[小屋](小屋.md) (こや) is a hut or shed, rather than necessarily a fabric shelter. [キャンプ](キャンプ.md) names camping or a camp; it is an activity or site, not the tent itself.

## Distinctive meaning

A テント need not be a tiny sleeping tent. An event can use a larger tent for shade, registration, or exhibits. The noun does not specify size or purpose, so add modifiers when the distinction matters. There is no useful single antonym.
