# 文 (ぶん) - sentence

## Meaning

文 (ぶん) is a sentence. It is a noun used in instructions about reading,
writing, translation, and grammar. Put を after the sentence when it is what
you read, write, or translate.

- 次の文を英語に訳してください。 (つぎのぶんをえいごにやくしてください。) - Please translate the following sentence into English.
- この文は少し長すぎます。 (このぶんはすこしながすぎます。) - This sentence is a little too long.

短い文 (みじかいぶん) is "a short sentence"; 文を作る (ぶんをつくる)
means making or composing a sentence.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) supplies the whole noun, read with the on reading ブン.
It concerns written language. Other readings of the character in other words
do not change how to read the classroom examples here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[文章](文章.md) (ぶんしょう) refers to writing or a passage, often comprising
several sentences. [言葉](言葉.md) (ことば) can mean words or language, rather
than specifically a sentence. A sentence can contain several words, but a
word list is not automatically a connected sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep a sentence distinct from a printed line. One long sentence may wrap onto
several lines without becoming several 文. Conversely, a line can contain more
than one short sentence. The word has broader writing-related uses, but
"sentence" is the useful sense for instructions such as 次の文を読んでください,
"Please read the following sentence."
