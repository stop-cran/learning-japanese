# カレー -- curry

## Meaning

カレー is curry. In a Japanese meal context it can also be short for カレーライス, curry served with rice.

昼ごはんにカレーを食べました。 (ひるごはんにカレーをたべました。) -- I had curry for lunch.

Use カレーを作る (カレーをつくる) for making curry. カレー by itself does not specify a serving size or number of plates.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword comes from English "curry" and is normally written in katakana. The ー lengthens レ. Dictionary-listed kanji spellings such as 咖哩 are not needed for ordinary writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

カレーライス explicitly names the rice-and-curry dish. カレー粉 (カレーこ) is curry powder, an ingredient rather than the finished meal.

## Distinctive meaning

In Japan, カレー often evokes Japanese-style curry, but the word is also used for other regional kinds. It does not guarantee a particular meat, spice level, or recipe. If a menu needs to distinguish the sauce from the rice, the fuller dish name or context makes that clear.
