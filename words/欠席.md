# 欠席 (けっせき) — absence; non-attendance

## Meaning

欠席 means absence from, or non-attendance at, a class, meeting, or other gathering.
欠席する (けっせきする) is "be absent; not attend."
The person who is absent is the subject; the event can be marked with を.

- 風邪で授業を欠席しました。 (かぜでじゅぎょうをけっせきしました。) — I missed class because of a cold.
- 明日の会議は欠席します。 (あしたのかいぎはけっせきします。) — I will not attend tomorrow's meeting.

## How the kanji combine

[欠](../kanji/欠.md) contributes lack or absence, and [席](../kanji/席.md) contributes a seat or place at a gathering.
Together they express someone's absence from that occasion, not the absence of the furniture itself.
ケツ + セキ becomes けっせき, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

出席 (しゅっせき), "attendance," is the direct contrast.
Both words can take する: 出席する means to attend; 欠席する means not to attend.
Do not reverse the meaning because both contain 席.

## Distinctive meaning

欠席 is about participation in an occasion, not merely being away from a location.
It is also classified as intransitive in the dictionary, although the event can appear with を, as in 授業を欠席する.
That を phrase identifies the class missed; it does not make the absent person a direct object.
