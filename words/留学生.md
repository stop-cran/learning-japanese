# 留学生 (りゅうがくせい) - international student

## Meaning

留学生 is a student studying abroad. Depending on context, English may say "international student," "overseas student," or "exchange student."

- 私は日本で勉強している留学生です。 (わたしはにほんでべんきょうしているりゅうがくせいです。) - I am an international student studying in Japan.
- この大学には留学生がたくさんいます。 (このだいがくにはりゅうがくせいがたくさんいます。) - There are many international students at this university.

It is a noun for the person. 留学する (りゅうがくする) describes the activity of studying abroad.

## How the kanji combine

[留](../kanji/留.md) contributes staying, [学](../kanji/学.md) studying, and [生](../kanji/生.md) the student role. 留学 + 生 gives someone engaged in overseas study. Preserve the long りゅう at the beginning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

学生 (がくせい) is a student generally. 交換留学生 (こうかんりゅうがくせい) specifically identifies an exchange student. 外国人 (がいこくじん) means a foreign person, not necessarily a student.

## Distinctive meaning

留学生 does not by itself guarantee participation in a reciprocal exchange program. It also covers people who arrange an overseas course or degree independently. The defining situation is study abroad, not tourism or merely holding a different nationality.
