# ああ (ああ) — like that over there

## Meaning

ああ means "like that" or "in that way", referring to a manner or situation distant from both speaker and listener. It modifies what someone does or how something turns out.

- ああやって作るんですね。  
  ああやってつくるんですね。  
  "So that's how they make it." (Watching someone over there.)
- 私もああなりたいです。  
  わたしもああなりたいです。  
  "I want to be like that too."

## How the kanji combine

This demonstrative adverb is written in hiragana; there are no kanji to separate. Learn it with the manner series こう, そう, ああ, どう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

こう means "like this"; [そう](そう.md) means "like that", often referring back to what was said. どう asks "how?" These are viewpoint contrasts, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

ああ points to a way of doing or being, whereas [あそこ](あそこ.md) points to a place. The same sound can also be an exclamation such as "Ah!", but that is not the adverb taught here.
