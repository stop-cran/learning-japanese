# いい

## Meaning

いい means good, nice, or satisfactory. It also expresses acceptance or permission.

- これはいい本です。 (これは いいほんです。) — This is a good book.
- ここに座ってもいいですか。 (ここに すわっても いいですか。) — May I sit here?

For permission, attach もいい to a verb's te-form: 座ってもいい. This is not a construction with the dictionary form.

## How the kanji combine

The everyday form is written in kana here. 良い is a related spelling, especially associated with よい. Although the plain positive form is いい, standard conjugated forms use よ: よくない, "not good"; よかった, "was good"; よく, "well."

## Synonyms and antonyms

よい has the same central meaning and often sounds more formal. 悪い (わるい), "bad," is the usual contrast for quality.

## Distinctive meaning

いいです can accept something, but in an offer it can also mean "No, I'm fine." Context and tone decide; it is not an unambiguous equivalent of "yes."
