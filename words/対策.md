# 対策 (たいさく) - countermeasures; strategy

## Meaning

対策 names measures, a strategy, or preparations directed at a problem,
threat, or task. It is a noun in useful patterns such as 対策を立てる
(たいさくをたてる), "devise measures," and 対策を練る
(たいさくをねる), "work out a strategy."

- 事故を防ぐための対策を立てました。 (じこをふせぐためのたいさくをたてました。) - We devised measures to prevent accidents.
- 試験対策の本を買いました。 (しけんたいさくのほんをかいました。) - I bought a test-preparation book.

試験対策 is preparation aimed at succeeding in a particular exam; it does
not mean a plan to stop the exam from happening.

## How the kanji combine

[対](../kanji/対.md) contributes facing or responding to something;
策 is a plan or measure. The first element qualifies the plan's directed
purpose. The on readings are タイ + サク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計画](計画.md) (けいかく) is a plan more broadly.
[手段](手段.md) (しゅだん) names a means or method.
[準備](準備.md) (じゅんび) overlaps in preparation contexts.
対策 emphasizes what is done or planned in response to the relevant issue,
but this is not a rigid defensive-versus-positive distinction.

## Distinctive meaning

対策 can be preventive or taken after trouble begins. Having a 対策 does
not prove that it has been implemented or will succeed. JMdict labels the
entry a noun, while Daijisen also permits する; the practical noun patterns
above are not a claim that every occurrence of 対策する is wrong.
