# 貿易 (ぼうえき) — international trade

## Meaning

貿易 means international trade: importing and exporting goods between countries. 貿易する means to engage in such trade.

この会社は外国と貿易をしています。（このかいしゃはがいこくとぼうえきをしています。） — This company trades with foreign countries.

## How the kanji combine

貿 concerns trading; [易](../kanji/易.md) contributes exchange here. Both use on readings: ぼう + えき. 易 does not mean "easy" in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

輸出（ゆしゅつ） means exports, while 輸入（ゆにゅう） means imports. Both are parts of 貿易. 商売（しょうばい） is business or commerce more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

貿易 is not the ordinary word for buying something at a neighborhood shop or swapping objects with a friend. Its central scope is commerce across national borders. In 貿易会社（ぼうえきがいしゃ）, "trading company," it specifies the company's business.
