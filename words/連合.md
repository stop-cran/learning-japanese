# 連合 (れんごう) - union; coalition; association of ideas

## Meaning

連合 is a union, alliance, coalition or combination.
連合する can describe groups uniting or combining; the dictionary
recognizes both transitive and intransitive uses.

- いくつかの団体が連合して活動しています。 (いくつかのだんたいがれんごうしてかつどうしています。) - Several organizations are working in an alliance.
- 二つの政党が連合を組みました。 (ふたつのせいとうがれんごうをくみました。) - The two political parties formed a coalition.

In psychology, 連合 also means association: a connection formed
between ideas, experiences or responses.

## How the kanji combine

[連](../kanji/連.md) contributes linking and [合](../kanji/合.md)
joining together. Their related meanings reinforce combination.
Read レン + ゴウ, retaining the long ごう.
聯合 is a rare recorded spelling, not this card's primary metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[組合](組合.md) (くみあい) is a union or association.
[統一](統一.md) (とういつ) emphasizes unification or consistency;
forming an alliance does not automatically make every member identical.
[連想](連想.md) (れんそう) concerns association of ideas,
not a coalition of organizations.

## Distinctive meaning

The organizational and psychological meanings must be distinguished
from their context; one is not an explanation of the other.
連合 can also abbreviate the Japanese Trade Union Confederation,
RENGO. That proper-name use does not mean every alliance is that body.
A coalition need not erase the identities of its participating groups.
