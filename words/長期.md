# 長期 (ちょうき) - long term; long period

## Meaning

長期 is a long period or the long term. As a noun modifying another noun, use **長期の**, as in 長期の休暇 (ちょうきのきゅうか), "an extended holiday." It also forms compounds such as 長期滞在 (ちょうきたいざい), "a long stay."

- 夏に長期の休暇を取る予定です。 (なつにちょうきのきゅうかをとるよていです。) - I plan to take an extended holiday in summer.
- 工事は長期にわたりました。 (こうじはちょうきにわたりました。) - The construction work continued over a long period.

**長期にわたる** expresses extending over a long time. The adjectival form 長期的 (ちょうきてき) appears in 長期的な計画, "a long-term plan."

## How the kanji combine

[長](../kanji/長.md), "long," modifies [期](../kanji/期.md), "period." The on readings are チョウ + キ. The length is temporal, not the physical length of an object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

短期 (たんき), "short term," is the direct opposite. [長い](長い.md) (ながい) is the broader adjective "long." [期間](期間.md) (きかん) names a period without specifying its length.

## Distinctive meaning

長期 has no fixed number of days or years. What counts as long depends on the activity: a long holiday and a long investment horizon are not measured against the same expectations. Avoid treating 長期 as a な-adjective; learn 長期の and 長期的な separately.
