# 事実 (じじつ) - fact; in fact

## Meaning

事実 is a noun for a fact or what actually happened. It also works adverbially as "in fact."
A clause followed by という事実 names the content of a fact.

- まず事実を確認しましょう。 (まずじじつをかくにんしましょう。) - First, let's check the facts.
- 彼は料理が上手だ。事実、彼の料理はとてもおいしい。 (かれはりょうりがじょうずだ。じじつ、かれのりょうりはとてもおいしい。) - He is good at cooking. In fact, his food is very tasty.

The first example treats 事実 as an object with を. In the second, it introduces support for
the preceding statement. それは事実です means "That is a fact."

## How the kanji combine

[事](../kanji/事.md) contributes a matter or event; [実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality or truth.
Together they identify an actual matter rather than merely a proposed one.
The on readings are ジ + ジツ, giving じじつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[現実](現実.md) (げんじつ) is reality or the actual situation.
[噂](噂.md) (うわさ), "rumor," contrasts in evidential status: circulating talk may or may not prove factual.
[嘘](嘘.md) (うそ), "falsehood; lie," contrasts with truth but can add an implication of deception.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling a statement 事実 presents it as factual; the word itself does not supply the proof.
Distinguish reporting the facts from giving an [意見](意見.md) (いけん), "opinion."
The adverbial use needs no に in the example, unlike some other noun-based adverbs.
