# 床の間 (とこのま) - tokonoma; display alcove

## Meaning

床の間 is a tokonoma: a display alcove in a Japanese-style room,
typically with a raised floor and a place for a hanging scroll, flowers, or ornaments.
It names the alcove, not the whole room.

- 床の間に掛け軸がかかっています。 (とこのまにかけじくがかかっています。) - A hanging scroll is displayed in the tokonoma.
- 床の間に花を飾りました。 (とこのまにはなをかざりました。) - I arranged flowers in the display alcove.

に marks the place of display. The noun does not itself identify what is displayed.

## How the kanji combine

[床](../kanji/床.md) is read とこ here; の connects it to
[間](../kanji/間.md), read ま, a space.
Learn the complete architectural name とこのま.
The separate floor noun [床](床.md) is read ゆか; it does not supply ゆか in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[部屋](部屋.md) (へや) is a room, whereas 床の間 is a particular feature within one.
[隅](隅.md) (すみ) is a corner more generally; not every corner is a tokonoma.
There is no single useful antonym for this architectural fixture.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 床の間 as just a floor, bed, or storage closet from its components.
Its display function distinguishes it from ordinary storage space.
The word names an architectural arrangement, not a requirement that every
Japanese-style room contain one.
