# 物理 (ぶつり) - physics

## Meaning

物理 is a noun meaning "physics," shortened from 物理学 (ぶつりがく), and can also refer to physical laws or the laws of nature. As a school subject, it takes the ordinary noun constructions **物理を勉強する** and **物理の授業**.

- 大学で物理を勉強しています。 (だいがくでぶつりをべんきょうしています。) - I study physics at university.
- 明日は物理の試験があります。 (あしたはぶつりのしけんがあります。) - There is a physics exam tomorrow.

The word names the field, not a scientist; 物理学者 (ぶつりがくしゃ) means "physicist."

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md), "things; matter," connects with [理](../kanji/理.md), "principle; reason." ブツ + リ gives ぶつり, principles governing the physical world. This is a meaning association, not a claim that the field consists of a literal two-word phrase.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[化学](化学.md) (かがく) is chemistry, and [生物](生物.md) (せいぶつ) can name biology as a subject. These are neighboring fields, not synonyms or antonyms; their areas of study can overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

The adjective-like form 物理的 (ぶつりてき), "physical," is a related expression, but bare 物理 is a noun. "Physical education" is not a translation of 物理. In a timetable or exam discussion, read it as the science subject.
