# 犯人 (はんにん) — culprit

## Meaning

犯人 identifies the culprit, offender, or person who committed an offense.
It is a noun referring to the person, not to the offense itself.

- 警察は犯人を探しています。 (けいさつははんにんをさがしています。) — The police are looking for the culprit.
- 犯人はまだ分かりません。 (はんにんはまだわかりません。) — We still do not know who the culprit is.

The second sentence does not require knowing a person's identity before using 犯人:
it refers to whoever committed the act.

## How the kanji combine

[犯](../kanji/犯.md) concerns committing an offense, and [人](../kanji/人.md) supplies the person.
The first character qualifies the second: a person responsible for the offense.
The on readings are ハン + ニン, not ハン + ジン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

容疑者 (ようぎしゃ), "suspect," is an important contrast rather than a synonym.
It identifies someone under suspicion; 犯人 identifies the perpetrator.
[犯罪](犯罪.md) (はんざい), "crime," names the offense, so it cannot replace 犯人 when the intended meaning is a person.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling a specific person 犯人 presents that person as the culprit, not merely as someone being investigated.
That lexical distinction is not a claim that the word can only be used after a court judgment.
Choose wording that matches what is actually known, especially when discussing a real person.
