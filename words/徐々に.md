# 徐々に (じょじょに) - gradually; by degrees

## Meaning

徐々に is an adverb meaning gradually, little by little, or by degrees. It describes a process developing over time rather than happening in one sudden step.

- 痛みが徐々に弱くなりました。 (いたみがじょじょによわくなりました。) - The pain gradually became weaker.
- 徐々に仕事に慣れてきました。 (じょじょにしごとになれてきました。) - I have gradually become accustomed to the job.

The に is already part of the expression. Put 徐々に before the predicate without adding another に or turning it into an i-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

徐 carries a slow or gradual-progress idea. 々 repeats the preceding kanji, so 徐々 represents the repeated element じょじょ; it is not a second distinct kanji. に gives the adverbial form. 徐 is not 除, the character associated with removing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[だんだん](だんだん.md) similarly describes progressive change. [急に](急に.md) (きゅうに), "suddenly," gives a useful contrast in how the change happens. A gradual change can still lead to a large final difference.

## Distinctive meaning

徐々に does not mean quietly in the sense of low sound or secrecy. A noise may 徐々に大きくなる (じょじょにおおきくなる), "gradually grow louder." That example concerns increasing volume over time, directly showing why "quietly" is not a reliable gloss.
