# 交差点 (こうさてん) — intersection

## Meaning

交差点 is a crossing or intersection, especially a place where roads meet. It is useful when giving directions.

- 次の交差点を右に曲がってください。 (つぎのこうさてんをみぎにまがってください。) — Please turn right at the next intersection.
- 交差点で車が止まりました。 (こうさてんでくるまがとまりました。) — The car stopped at the intersection.

In the first example, を marks the point along the route where the turn is made. In the second, で marks where the action takes place.

## How the kanji combine

[交](../kanji/交.md) and [差](../kanji/差.md) form 交差 (こうさ), "crossing/intersecting." [点](../kanji/点.md) adds "point": an intersecting point.

## Synonyms and antonyms

横断歩道 (おうだんほどう) is a pedestrian crossing, which may be at an intersection but is not the entire junction. 道 (みち) means a road or path more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

交差点 does not promise four roads, traffic lights, or a marked crosswalk. The important idea is roads meeting, not a particular traffic-control arrangement.
