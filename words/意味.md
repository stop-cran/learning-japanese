# 意味 (いみ) — meaning; significance

## Meaning

意味 is the meaning or significance of a word, expression, action, or situation. 意味する means "to mean" or "to signify".

- この言葉の意味が分かりません。  
  このことばのいみがわかりません。  
  "I don't understand the meaning of this word."
- それはどういう意味ですか。  
  それはどういういみですか。  
  "What does that mean?"

## How the kanji combine

The on readings are イ + ミ. [意](../kanji/意.md) concerns thought or intention; [味](../kanji/味.md) has the concrete association "taste" and a figurative association "sense". As a learning aid, think of two related contributions to the sense something conveys, not a literal food flavor.

## Synonyms and antonyms

内容 ないよう is the content or substance of something; 意味 is what it means. 意図 いと is an intention or aim, which may differ from what another person understands. There is no single direct antonym; 意味がない いみがない means "has no meaning" or, in context, "there is no point".

## Distinctive meaning

Use Xの意味 for "the meaning of X". Meaning and purpose can overlap: それをする意味がない それをするいみがない means "There is no point in doing that", not necessarily that the action is impossible to understand.
