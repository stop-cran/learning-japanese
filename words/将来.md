# 将来 (しょうらい) — future; prospects

## Meaning

将来 is the future, often considered through a person's plans, opportunities, or prospects. It can work directly as a time expression or as a noun.

- 将来、医者になりたいです。 (しょうらい、いしゃになりたいです。) — I want to become a doctor in the future.
- 子供の将来について話しました。 (こどものしょうらいについてはなしました。) — We talked about our child's future.

Use 将来の + noun, as in 将来の夢 (しょうらいのゆめ), "a dream for the future."

## How the kanji combine

将 has an impending or future association here, and [来](../kanji/来.md) means "come." Together they point to what lies ahead. The on readings are ショウ + ライ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

未来 (みらい) also means "future" and can frame future time more broadly. 過去 (かこ), "the past," gives the time contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

将来 is useful for life plans and longer-term development, not a replacement for every "next" or "later." If you mean next month or tomorrow, name that time. Its everyday future sense is a noun, not a verb meaning "to future."
