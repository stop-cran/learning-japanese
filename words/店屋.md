# 店屋 (みせや) - store; shop

## Meaning

店屋 means a store or shop.

- この通りには店屋が並んでいます。 (このとおりにはみせやがならんでいます。) - Shops line this street.
- 近くの店屋で買いました。 (ちかくのみせやでかいました。) - I bought it at a nearby shop.

## How the kanji combine

[店](../kanji/店.md) supplies みせ, "shop," and [屋](../kanji/屋.md) supplies や, used for shops or businesses. This is a native compound, not テン + オク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[店](店.md) (みせ), [商店](商店.md) (しょうてん) and [ショップ](ショップ.md) overlap in the shop/store meaning. [売店](売店.md) (ばいてん) is often a smaller shop or kiosk within a facility; [売り場](売り場.md) (うりば) can be a sales area within a shop.

## Distinctive meaning

The headword here is みせや. A separate てんや reading has its own dictionary heading, including an eating-place use; do not transfer that restriction to every みせや. The inspected entry does not label this word archaic.
