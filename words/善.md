# 善 (ぜん) - moral good; virtue

## Meaning

善 is a noun for good, goodness, or virtue, especially as a moral idea. It can name good conduct as well as the concept of what is right.

- 善と悪について話し合いました。 (ぜんとあくについてはなしあいました。) - We discussed good and evil.
- 善を行うことは簡単ではありません。 (ぜんをおこなうことはかんたんではありません。) - Doing good is not easy.

In the second sentence, 善を行う treats good as something put into action. 善 is not functioning as an adjective before another noun.

## How the kanji combine

善 is a single-kanji noun with the on reading ぜん. The whole character conveys goodness or virtue; there are no separate word components to combine here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

悪 (あく), "evil," is the direct moral contrast. [良い](良い.md) (よい) is an adjective with a broader range of positive evaluations. A 良い天気, "good weather," is not automatically an example of moral 善.

## Distinctive meaning

The English translation "good" hides an important grammatical difference. 善 can be the object in 善を行う or one side of 善と悪. It does not replace 良い freely in phrases meaning that food tastes good or a tool works well. Discussion of 善 concerns goodness as an idea or action, while the standard of goodness may still be debated.
