# 角度 (かくど) - angle; viewpoint

## Meaning

角度 is an angle, including how much two directions diverge or the inclination of something. It also has a figurative use for an angle or viewpoint from which to consider a matter.

- この角度を測ってください。 (このかくどをはかってください。) - Please measure this angle.
- 別の角度から問題を考えましょう。 (べつのかくどからもんだいをかんがえましょう。) - Let's consider the problem from another angle.

In the second pattern, から marks the viewpoint. No geometrical measurement is being requested.

## How the kanji combine

[角](../kanji/角.md) contributes angle, and [度](../kanji/度.md) degree or measure. Together they concern an angle's extent. The on readings are カク + ド. The figure-of-speech use extends the viewpoint image rather than naming a separate unit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

観点 (かんてん), 視点 (してん), and 見方 (みかた) overlap with the viewpoint use. The existing [角](角.md) word article primarily teaches かど, corner; that is not the reading of 角 in 角度.

## Distinctive meaning

角度 names the angle, not automatically the measuring unit. 度 (ど), degrees, is one unit used to express its size. A street corner and the angular measurement of its turn are related ideas, but not every English "corner" is an interchangeable translation of 角度.
