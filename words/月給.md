# 月給 (げっきゅう) - monthly salary

## Meaning

月給 is a monthly salary: pay stated on a monthly basis.
It can refer to the monthly amount or the arrangement of being paid that way.

- 月給は二十五万円です。 (げっきゅうはにじゅうごまんえんです。) - The monthly salary is 250,000 yen.
- 新しい仕事では月給が上がりました。 (あたらしいしごとではげっきゅうがあがりました。) - My monthly salary increased in the new job.

## How the kanji combine

[月](../kanji/月.md) specifies a month; [給](../kanji/給.md) contributes pay.
The first character identifies the pay period.
ゲツ + キュウ gives げっきゅう, with small っ before きゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[給料](給料.md) (きゅうりょう) and [給与](給与.md) (きゅうよ) refer to pay more generally.
[収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう) is income and is not limited to salary.
時給 (じきゅう), hourly pay, is a useful contrast in the basis of calculation.

## Distinctive meaning

月給 specifies a monthly basis, not automatically take-home pay after deductions.
Do not infer bonuses, working hours, or payment dates from this word alone.
A monthly amount of investment income is not necessarily a 月給.
