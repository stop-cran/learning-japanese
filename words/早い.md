# 早い (はやい) — early

## Meaning

早い is an い-adjective meaning early, earlier than expected, or too soon. It can also describe something done quickly or promptly, depending on context.

- 今日は早く起きました。 (きょうははやくおきました。) — I got up early today.
- まだ早いです。 (まだはやいです。) — It is still too early.

The adverbial form is 早く はやく; the negative is 早くない はやくない.

## How the kanji combine

This is one native word, not a compound. [早](../kanji/早.md) supplies はや, and い is the okurigana completing the adjective. The dictionary notation はや.い marks that boundary.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 速い はやい emphasizes speed: a fast vehicle or a rapid movement. The two spellings overlap in some "quick" uses, but 早い is the standard choice for being early.
- 遅い おそい can mean late or slow. In a timing comparison, it is the opposite of 早い.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish an early arrival from a fast journey: 早い focuses on when something happens, while 速い focuses on speed. A train can arrive early without being especially fast. Do not assume that every English "fast" should be written 早い.
