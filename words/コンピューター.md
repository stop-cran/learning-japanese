# コンピューター (コンピューター) - computer

## Meaning

コンピューター means a computer. It can refer to computers broadly, not only the personal machine on someone's desk.

- コンピューターで日本語を勉強しています。 (コンピューターでにほんごをべんきょうしています。) - I study Japanese using a computer.

で identifies the tool or means. コンピューターを使う (コンピューターをつかう) means "use a computer," with を marking the object of 使う.

## How the kanji combine

The word is borrowed from English "computer" and written in katakana. ピュ uses small ュ; the following ー lengthens its vowel. コンピュータ, without the final long-vowel mark, is another established spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

パソコン specifically means a personal computer and is very useful for everyday laptops and desktops. ノートパソコン names a laptop.

## Distinctive meaning

コンピューター and パソコン often identify the same device in conversation, but the broader category includes computers that are not personal computers. Do not interpret the alternate spelling コンピュータ as a different kind of machine.
