# 酒場 (さかば) - bar; pub; tavern

## Meaning

酒場 is a bar, pub, or tavern: an establishment where people drink alcohol.

- 駅の近くに小さな酒場があります。 (えきのちかくにちいさなさかばがあります。) - There is a small bar near the station.
- 仕事のあと、友達と酒場で話しました。 (しごとのあと、ともだちとさかばではなしました。) - After work, I talked with friends at a bar.

In the second example, で marks where the conversation takes place.

## How the kanji combine

[酒](../kanji/酒.md) contributes alcohol and [場](../kanji/場.md) place: a place for drinking. The native reading combines the さか- form of 酒 with ば. It is not シュジョウ or さけば.

## Synonyms and antonyms

バー, 居酒屋 (いざかや), and 飲み屋 (のみや) name overlapping kinds of drinking establishment. Their atmosphere and service can differ. [店](店.md) (みせ) is a shop or establishment more generally, not specifically somewhere to drink.

## Distinctive meaning

酒場 does not specify a Western-style counter, a particular beverage, or whether food is served. It is not simply a shop selling unopened bottles to take home. The drinking-establishment meaning, not just the presence of alcohol, is the key.
