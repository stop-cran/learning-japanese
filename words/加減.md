# 加減 (かげん) - degree; adjustment; condition

## Meaning

加減 names a degree, balance, or condition. 加減する means to adjust or moderate
something, with を marking what is adjusted.

- 火加減を見てください。 (ひかげんをみてください。) - Please check the heat.
- 塩の量を加減してください。 (しおのりょうをかげんしてください。) - Please adjust the amount of salt.
- お加減はいかがですか。 (おかげんはいかがですか。) - How are you feeling?

火加減 concerns the degree of heat in cooking. In the last example, お加減
politely asks about someone's health, not about an amount of an ingredient.

## How the kanji combine

[加](../kanji/加.md) means add, and 減 means reduce. These opposing operations
form カ + ゲン. 加減 can literally name addition and subtraction, but everyday
uses extend to getting a level or balance right.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[具合](具合.md) (ぐあい) overlaps in "condition," including health and how
something is working. 調節 (ちょうせつ), "adjustment," overlaps with 加減する
when regulating a quantity. Neither replaces every sense of 加減.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every occurrence as arithmetic. お加減 and 火加減 are useful
whole expressions. The related suffix also appears in 疲れ加減
(つかれかげん), "rather tired": here 加減 indicates a slight state, not the
act of adjusting one's fatigue. This suffix attaches to the verb stem 疲れ.
