# ゼミ - seminar; small-group study

## Meaning

ゼミ is a seminar, especially a small-group university class in which students
research a topic, give reports and discuss it under a teacher's guidance.
The word can identify the seminar group as well as its sessions.

- 大学で経済学のゼミに入っています。 (だいがくでけいざいがくのゼミにはいっています。) - I belong to an economics seminar group at university.
- 今日のゼミで研究の結果を発表しました。 (きょうのゼミでけんきゅうのけっかをはっぴょうしました。) - I presented my research results in today's seminar.

ゼミに入る identifies joining the group; ゼミで identifies the setting of the presentation.
The format is not restricted to listening silently to a lecturer.

## How the kanji combine

ゼミ abbreviates ゼミナール, a borrowing from German Seminar.
The full form also covers courses using this seminar format outside the university setting.
There are no kanji components; do not expand ゼミ by inventing an English spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

セミナー is another seminar word.
[講義](講義.md) (こうぎ) means a lecture, while [授業](授業.md)
(じゅぎょう) is the broader word for a class or lesson.
A ゼミ can be a 授業, but the words do not identify the same teaching format in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

The university setting is a useful learning focus, not a rule that every seminar must
be a degree course or that every university lesson is a ゼミ.
For research itself, [研究](研究.md) (けんきゅう) names the activity;
ゼミ identifies its seminar setting or group rather than every act of studying.
