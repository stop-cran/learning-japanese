# アルコール (アルコール) — alcohol

## Meaning

アルコール means alcohol as a substance and can also mean alcoholic drinks. Context distinguishes a chemical ingredient from a beverage.

この飲み物にはアルコールが入っていません。（こののみものにはアルコールがはいっていません。） — This drink contains no alcohol.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword, recorded by JMdict as coming through Dutch. The long-vowel mark ー lengthens コ; there is no kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お酒（おさけ） is the everyday word for alcoholic drinks. アルコール is also used in technical or product descriptions, where お酒 would not be equivalent.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every product containing アルコール is a drink: it can occur in descriptions of disinfectants and other non-food products. アルコール度数（アルコールどすう） means alcohol content or strength. The word itself does not identify one particular kind of beverage.
