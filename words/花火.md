# 花火 (はなび) — fireworks

## Meaning

花火 is fireworks: 花火大会 はなびたいかい (a fireworks festival), 花火を見る (watch fireworks), 花火を打ち上げる うちあげる (set off fireworks), 手持ち花火 てもちはなび (handheld sparklers).
Fireworks are a summer tradition in Japan, often seen in yukata.

## How the kanji combine

[花](../kanji/花.md) (flower) + [火](../kanji/火.md) (fire): "flower of fire", describing the blooming bursts. The reading is regular kun reading: はな + び, with び the voiced form of ひ in compounds (rendaku).
It is not a jukujikun, since each kanji keeps its own native reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 打ち上げ花火 うちあげはなび (aerial shells), 線香花火 せんこうはなび (sparklers), 花火大会.
- Related: 花 はな, 火 ひ.

## Distinctive meaning

花火 is about the object and the event; the verb is 打ち上げる for shells and 遊ぶ for hand-held sparklers (花火で遊ぶ). The reversed order 火花 ひばな means sparks.
