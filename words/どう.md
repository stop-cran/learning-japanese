# どう (どう) - how; in what way

## Meaning

どう asks about a manner, condition, opinion, or choice: "how?" or "how about ...?"

- この言葉はどう読みますか。 (このことばはどうよみますか。) - How do you read this word?
- 新しい仕事はどうですか。 (あたらしいしごとはどうですか。) - How is your new job?

The first asks how to do something; the second invites an evaluation. A noun followed by はどうですか can also introduce a suggestion: お茶はどうですか (おちゃはどうですか), "How about some tea?"

## How the kanji combine

どう is normally written in kana. 如何 is a rare kanji spelling for this reading; it is not a pair of ordinary character readings to assemble independently.

## Synonyms and antonyms

いかが is a more polite counterpart in questions and offers. こう, そう, and ああ indicate "in this/that way" rather than asking which way.

## Distinctive meaning

どう is not used directly before a noun to mean "what kind of." Use どんな + noun for that: どんな仕事 (どんなしごと), "what kind of job." どうして is the separate question expression "why" or, in context, "how."
