# 学力 (がくりょく) - academic ability

## Meaning

学力 is ability acquired through learning, especially the knowledge and skills involved in school study. It is a noun: 学力が高い means academic ability is high; 学力を伸ばす (がくりょくをのばす) means to develop it.

- 毎日の学習で学力を伸ばしたいです。 (まいにちのがくしゅうでがくりょくをのばしたいです。) - I want to develop my academic ability through daily study.
- このテストで学生の学力を調べます。 (このテストでがくせいのがくりょくをしらべます。) - This test assesses the students' academic ability.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) narrows the field to learning, and [力](../kanji/力.md) contributes capacity or ability. The modifier-plus-head compound uses ガク + リョク, not the native ちから reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[能力](能力.md) (のうりょく) is broader ability or capability. [知識](知識.md) (ちしき) concerns knowledge; [成績](成績.md) (せいせき) an assessed performance record. A school result can provide evidence about 学力, but the mark and the ability it attempts to assess are not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary's "scholarship" concerns learning and scholarly ability here, not money supplied to a student. [奨学金](奨学金.md) (しょうがくきん) names financial support. 学力 is not a blanket judgment about someone's intelligence or worth outside the learning being discussed.
