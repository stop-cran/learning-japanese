# 幾分 (いくぶん) - somewhat; to a degree

## Meaning

幾分 expresses a limited amount or degree: "somewhat" or "to some extent."
As an adverb it modifies the following description without に.

- 今日は昨日より幾分涼しいです。 (きょうはきのうよりいくぶんすずしいです。) - Today is somewhat cooler than yesterday.
- 休んだので、気分が幾分よくなりました。 (やすんだので、きぶんがいくぶんよくなりました。) - After resting, I felt somewhat better.

The noun use refers to some portion: 利益の幾分かを寄付しました
(りえきのいくぶんかをきふしました), "I donated some of the profit."
か leaves the amount unspecified.

## How the kanji combine

[幾](../kanji/幾.md) contributes an unspecified quantity, and [分](../kanji/分.md)
contributes a part or share. Together they suggest an unspecified portion or degree.
Learn the whole reading いくぶん; it is not a question asking for a number of minutes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[多少](多少.md) (たしょう), [やや](やや.md), and [少し](少し.md) (すこし)
overlap in degree descriptions. [僅か](僅か.md) (わずか) can emphasize how small
an amount or difference is, with different attachment forms.

## Distinctive meaning

幾分よくなった means an improvement to some extent, not necessarily a complete
recovery. Conversely, 幾分か in the profit example leaves a share unspecified;
it does not supply a numerical fraction. Use measurements when an exact amount matters.
