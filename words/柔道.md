# 柔道 (じゅうどう) - judo

## Meaning

柔道 names judo, the Japanese martial art and sport associated with throws, holds, and grappling. Use 柔道をする for "do judo" and 柔道を習う (じゅうどうをならう) for "learn judo."

- 兄は高校で柔道を習っています。 (あにはこうこうでじゅうどうをならっています。) - My older brother is learning judo in high school.
- 日曜日に柔道の試合を見ました。 (にちようびにじゅうどうのしあいをみました。) - I watched a judo match on Sunday.

## How the kanji combine

柔 contributes softness or flexibility, and [道](../kanji/道.md) a way or discipline. The compound uses the on readings じゅう + どう, with a long vowel in both parts. These character associations are not a complete explanation of the sport's rules.

## Synonyms and antonyms

武道 (ぶどう) is the broader category of Japanese martial disciplines. 空手 (からて), "karate," is a different martial art, not another name for judo.

## Distinctive meaning

Although English borrowed "judo" from Japanese, the Japanese word remains a Sino-Japanese compound, not a foreign loanword normally written in katakana. 柔道の先生 means a judo instructor.
