# 牧畜 (ぼくちく) - livestock farming

## Meaning

牧畜 means livestock farming or breeding livestock. It is a noun naming an agricultural activity.

- この地方では牧畜が盛んです。 (このちほうではぼくちくがさかんです。) - Livestock farming is thriving in this region.
- 彼は牧畜を営んでいます。 (かれはぼくちくをいとなんでいます。) - He runs a livestock-farming business.

Learn the noun constructions 牧畜を営む (ぼくちくをいとなむ), "engage in livestock farming," and 牧畜業 (ぼくちくぎょう), "the livestock industry."

## How the kanji combine

牧 concerns herding or tending; [畜](../kanji/畜.md) concerns domestic animals. The action-and-object association is tending livestock. The on readings are ボク + チク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[農業](農業.md) (のうぎょう) is agriculture or farming more broadly. [飼う](飼う.md) (かう) means to keep or raise an animal and can concern a pet, not only agricultural production.

[牧場](牧場.md) (ぼくじょう) names a livestock farm or pasture, while [家畜](家畜.md) (かちく) names livestock. These distinguish the activity, place, and animals.

## Distinctive meaning

牧畜 is not limited to cattle, despite the English gloss "cattle breeding." It concerns keeping livestock as a farming activity, not every case of caring for an animal at home.
