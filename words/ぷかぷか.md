# ぷかぷか — floating lightly and buoyantly

## Meaning

ぷかぷか describes something floating lightly on a surface or bobbing buoyantly.

- 木の葉が池にぷかぷか浮かんでいる。
  — Leaves are floating lightly on the pond.
- 小さな船が波の上でぷかぷか揺れていた。
  — A small boat bobbed on the waves.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぷかぷか suggests buoyant floating; it does not imply sinking or being submerged.

## Distinctive meaning

It also has other established uses, including puffing on a pipe; these examples focus on floating.
