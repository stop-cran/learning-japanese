# ぎっしり - tightly packed; densely

## Meaning

ぎっしり describes things packed closely together with little room left. It is an adverb, used with or without と.

- 棚には本がぎっしり詰まっています。
  (たなにはほんがぎっしりつまっています。) - The shelves are packed with books.
- 箱に荷物をぎっしりと詰めました。
  (はこににもつをぎっしりとつめました。) - I packed the box tightly with belongings.

The first sentence describes a packed state; the second describes packing something tightly. ぎっしり itself does not decide whether the surrounding verb is transitive.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぎゅうぎゅう](ぎゅうぎゅう.md) can likewise describe cramming, often bringing pressure or squeezing into focus. [一杯](一杯.md) (いっぱい) is broader: being full need not emphasize closely packed individual things. びっしり also describes dense coverage or arrangement; these expressions overlap rather than dividing all objects into exclusive classes.

## Distinctive meaning

ぎっしり conveys density, not simply a large total number. A room with many books could still have plenty of empty shelf space. Do not translate it as a sound made by the books: it is a descriptive mimetic expression.
