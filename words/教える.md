# 教える (おしえる) — teach; tell

## Meaning

教える means to teach a skill or subject, or to tell someone information. The recipient takes に and what is taught or told takes を.

- 弟に英語を教えます。  
  おとうとにえいごをおしえます。  
  "I teach English to my younger brother."
- 名前を教えてください。  
  なまえをおしえてください。  
  "Please tell me your name."

## How the kanji combine

This is a native single-kanji verb. [教](../kanji/教.md) supplies おし, followed by the okurigana える. It is ichidan: 教えます おしえます, 教えて おしえて, 教えない おしえない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

伝える つたえる emphasizes conveying information or a message. [習う](習う.md) ならう and 教わる おそわる describe learning from someone: they reverse the viewpoint of teaching, rather than being strict antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

教える is not limited to classrooms or professional teachers. A person giving directions or telling you a name can use it. Compare 先生が日本語を教えます せんせいがにほんごをおしえます (the teacher teaches Japanese) with 先生に日本語を教わります せんせいににほんごをおそわります (I learn Japanese from the teacher).
