# バス — bus

## Meaning

バス is a bus used for transporting passengers.

- バスで学校に行きます。 (バスでがっこうにいきます。) — I go to school by bus.
- 次のバスに乗りましょう。 (つぎのバスにのりましょう。) — Let's take the next bus.

Use で for the means of transport, に with 乗る (のる), "get on; ride," and を with 降りる (おりる), "get off."

## How the kanji combine

バス is a katakana borrowing of English "bus." There are no kanji components, and neither syllable has a long-vowel mark.

## Synonyms and antonyms

電車 (でんしゃ), "train," and タクシー, "taxi," are other transport choices. バス停 (バスてい) is a bus stop, not the vehicle.

## Distinctive meaning

バス does not by itself specify a city bus, coach, or school bus; additional words or the situation do that. Other unrelated loanwords can share this spelling, including one from English "bath." In this article, the selected word is the transport noun.
