# 松竹梅 (しょうちくばい) - pine-bamboo-ume grouping; three-tier labels

## Meaning

松竹梅 names the auspicious grouping of pine, bamboo and ume. It also names a conventional set of three ranking labels, from 松, the highest, through 竹 to 梅.

- この皿には松竹梅の模様があります。 (このさらにはしょうちくばいのもようがあります。) - This plate has a pine, bamboo and ume design.
- この店のコースは松竹梅に分かれています。 (このみせのコースはしょうちくばいにわかれています。) - This restaurant's courses are divided into the Matsu, Take and Ume tiers.

The first example describes a design. The second describes course labels, not three required ingredients.

## How the kanji combine

[松](../kanji/松.md), pine, [竹](../kanji/竹.md), bamboo, and [梅](../kanji/梅.md), ume, are coordinated plant names: one does not modify another. Their on readings are ショウ + チク + バイ.

When identifying the individual tiers aloud, the separate plant names are [松](松.md) (まつ), [竹](竹.md) (たけ) and [梅](梅.md) (うめ). Keep those readings distinct from the compound しょうちくばい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

In the ranking sense, high, middle and low describe the three positions. [最高](最高.md) (さいこう), "highest; best," can evaluate one option; it does not name this entire three-label set. There is no single lexical antonym of the plant grouping.

## Distinctive meaning

The grouping can be decorative without ranking the plants against each other. In a menu or service list, the conventional order does not guarantee particular contents, prices or standardized quality. Read what each option actually offers; 松竹梅 is not a general replacement for any three items.
