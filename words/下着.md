# 下着 (したぎ) — underwear

## Meaning

下着 means underwear or underclothes. It is a general noun for garments worn beneath outer clothing, not the name of one specific garment.

- 旅行の前に、新しい下着を買いました。 (りょこうのまえに、あたらしいしたぎをかいました。) — I bought new underwear before the trip.
- 下着を洗いました。 (したぎをあらいました。) — I washed my underwear.

Japanese does not make this noun plural by changing its ending. Context tells you whether one item or several items are meant.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) is した, "under/below." [着](../kanji/着.md) contributes the clothing element き, voiced to ぎ in this compound. The reading is したぎ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

服 (ふく) means clothing more generally. 上着 (うわぎ) is an outer or upper garment, such as a jacket, and provides a useful clothing-layer contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Although 下 can mean "lower," 下着 is not simply clothing for the lower half of the body. An undershirt can also be 下着; the important distinction is the underlayer.
