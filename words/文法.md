# 文法 (ぶんぽう) — grammar

## Meaning

文法 means grammar: the patterns and rules by which words and forms combine into sentences. It applies to spoken language as well as writing.

日本語の文法を勉強しています。（にほんごのぶんぽうをべんきょうしています。） — I am studying Japanese grammar.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) means a sentence or writing; [法](../kanji/法.md) means a rule or method. Both use on readings. In this compound the pronunciation is ぶんぽう, with ぽう rather than ほう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

単語（たんご） means a word or vocabulary item. 発音（はつおん） means pronunciation. These are different aspects of language learning, not opposites of grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

文法 is a noun: use 文法の before another noun, as in 文法の本（ぶんぽうのほん）, "a grammar book." Knowing individual words does not by itself supply the patterns needed to combine them.
