# 夏 (なつ) — summer

## Meaning

夏 is summer, the season.

- 日本の夏は暑いです。 (にほんのなつはあついです。) — Summers in Japan are hot.
- 夏に海へ行きます。 (なつにうみへいきます。) — I will go to the seaside in summer.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun, not a compound. [夏](../kanji/夏.md) represents the whole word なつ, with no okurigana.

The character's on-readings belong to other words and uses. The ordinary season word here is なつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 夏休み なつやすみ means summer vacation: a break during the season, not the whole season itself.
- [冬](冬.md) ふゆ, winter, provides the contrasting season.
- [春](春.md) はる and [秋](秋.md) あき are spring and autumn, not synonyms of 夏.

## Distinctive meaning

夏 names a time of year, not a temperature. 暑い (あつい), "hot" for weather, describes a condition; 夏 does not replace that adjective. The noun can stand on its own or modify another noun with の, as in 夏の海 (なつのうみ), "the sea in summer."
