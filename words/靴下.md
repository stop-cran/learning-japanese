# 靴下 (くつした) — socks

## Meaning

靴下 means a sock or socks. Japanese does not normally mark the noun itself as singular or plural.

白い靴下をはいています。（しろいくつしたをはいています。） — I am wearing white socks.

## How the kanji combine

[靴](../kanji/靴.md) means shoes, and [下](../kanji/下.md) means below or underneath. Both use native readings: くつ + した. The compound names what is worn underneath the shoes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ソックス is a loanword for socks. 靴 are shoes, while ストッキング usually refers to stockings or hosiery. These related items are not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Use はく for putting on socks and 脱ぐ（ぬぐ） for taking them off. A pair can be counted with 足: 靴下一足（くつしたいっそく）, "one pair of socks." The reading here is not the standalone reading あし, "foot."
