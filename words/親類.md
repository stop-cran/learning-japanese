# 親類 (しんるい) - relative; kin

## Meaning

親類 means a relative or relatives connected through blood or marriage.
It is a noun and can refer to one person or to a group without changing form.
In ordinary descriptions, it often contrasts with one's immediate family.

- 近くに親類が住んでいます。 (ちかくにしんるいがすんでいます。) - A relative lives nearby.
- 親類の家に泊まりました。 (しんるいのいえにとまりました。) - I stayed at a relative's house.

親類の + noun identifies a relative's possession or something associated
with relatives, as in the second sentence.

## How the kanji combine

[親](../kanji/親.md) contributes family connection or closeness;
[類](../kanji/類.md) contributes a related group or kind.
The on readings are シン + ルイ.
The word names kinship, not necessarily emotional intimacy.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[親戚](親戚.md) (しんせき) substantially overlaps in referring to relatives.
[家族](家族.md) (かぞく) means family and can focus on immediate family
rather than the wider relatives being discussed.
The people included depend on context, not simply who shares one address.

## Distinctive meaning

A marriage connection can establish 親類; it is not limited to blood relatives.
遠い親類 (とおいしんるい), "a distant relative," can describe a remote
family connection rather than geographical distance.
The dictionary also records a figurative same-kind resemblance use;
that is separate from the family examples taught here.
