# 不運 (ふうん) - bad luck; misfortune

## Meaning

不運 is bad luck or misfortune. It is a noun and a na-adjective:
不運な出来事 (ふうんなできごと), an unfortunate event.
The reading is ふ + うん; keep both vowel morae.

- 不運にも、旅行の日に台風が来ました。 (ふうんにも、りょこうのひにたいふうがきました。) - Unfortunately, a typhoon arrived on the day of our trip.
- 事故に遭うとは不運でした。 (じこにあうとはふうんでした。) - It was bad luck to have an accident.

不運にも introduces an unfortunate circumstance; it does not
establish that somebody acted carelessly.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) negates what follows, and
[運](../kanji/運.md) contributes luck or fortune here:
not having good fortune. The on readings are フ + ウン.
運's carrying sense does not explain this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不幸](不幸.md) (ふこう) overlaps in misfortune and can also
concern unhappiness. [災難](災難.md) (さいなん) can name a
calamity or unfortunate trouble. [幸運](幸運.md) (こううん),
good luck, is the useful opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

English "fate" alone is too neutral: 不運 specifically concerns
unfavorable luck. It does not mean that every difficult event was
predestined, nor is it simply a statement that someone feels sad.
[運](運.md) (うん) is the broader word for luck or fortune.
