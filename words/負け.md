# 負け (まけ) - defeat; loss in a contest

## Meaning

負け is a defeat or loss in a game or contest. It is a noun:
私の負け (わたしのまけ), "my loss," and 負けを認める (まけをみとめる),
"admit defeat."

- 今回は私の負けです。 (こんかいはわたしのまけです。) - This time, I lose.
- 彼は素直に負けを認めました。 (かれはすなおにまけをみとめました。) - He readily admitted defeat.

The first example can concede the result of a contest without using a past
verb form.

## How the kanji combine

[負](../kanji/負.md) contributes losing in this word. 負け is related to the
native verb [負ける](負ける.md) (まける), "lose." The noun is まけ, not the
on reading フ heard in compounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勝ち](勝ち.md) (かち), "victory; win," is the direct opposite for a contest.
敗北 (はいぼく) also means defeat.
[失敗](失敗.md) (しっぱい), "failure," can concern an unsuccessful attempt
without any opponent.

## Distinctive meaning

負け here concerns a competitive result, not losing a key or being unable to
find something. Compare 私の負けです, "it is my defeat," with 私が負けました,
"I lost." The first uses the noun with の and です; the second conjugates
the related verb. Do not add する to make the ordinary verb "lose."
