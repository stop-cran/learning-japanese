# 地理 (ちり) — geography

## Meaning

地理 means geography: the study of places, land, climate, and human activity in relation to location. It also refers to knowledge of the layout of an area.

学校で地理を勉強しています。 (がっこうでちりをべんきょうしています。) — I am studying geography at school.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md), "land; earth," and [理](../kanji/理.md), "principle; order," form the on-reading compound ち + り. It names a subject, not a particular patch of land.

## Synonyms and antonyms

地図 (ちず) is a map, a tool used in geography. 歴史 (れきし) is history, a neighboring school subject rather than an opposite. 地学 (ちがく) refers to earth science.

## Distinctive meaning

この辺の地理に詳しい (このへんのちりにくわしい) means "know this area's layout well." This practical use need not imply academic training. Do not confuse 地理 with the homophone 塵 (ちり), "dust."
