# 涼しい (すずしい) — pleasantly cool

## Meaning

涼しい describes pleasant coolness, especially of air, weather, or a place. It is an i-adjective: 涼しかった means "was cool."

今日は涼しいです。（きょうはすずしいです。） — It is pleasantly cool today.

木の下は涼しいです。（きのしたはすずしいです。） — It is cool under the tree.

## How the kanji combine

涼 supplies the coolness meaning, and しい completes the native adjective. For conjugation, remove only the final い: 涼しくない（すずしくない）, "not cool."

## Synonyms and antonyms

寒い（さむい） describes cold weather or feeling cold. 冷たい（つめたい） usually describes coldness to the touch, such as cold water. 暑い（あつい）, "hot," is a weather contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

"Refreshing" here concerns coolness, not every kind of refreshment. A refreshing idea or an enjoyable break does not automatically take 涼しい. Nor is 涼しい the general word for a cold drink.
