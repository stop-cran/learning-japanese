# 珍しい (めずらしい)

## Meaning

珍しい means rare, uncommon, or unusual. It can describe something not often encountered, including an unexpected action by someone familiar.

- 珍しい鳥を見ました。 (めずらしい とりを みました。) — I saw an unusual bird.
- 彼が遅れるのは珍しいです。 (かれが おくれるのは めずらしいです。) — It is unusual for him to be late.

It is an i-adjective: negative 珍しくない (めずらしくない), past 珍しかった (めずらしかった).

## How the kanji combine

[珍](../kanji/珍.md) supplies めずら; しい completes the written adjective. The conjugating final い changes in forms such as 珍しく. Do not treat the kanji boundary as the full grammatical base.

## Synonyms and antonyms

まれ also means rare. [普通](普通.md), "ordinary; usual," offers a useful contrast. 変 (へん), "strange," can suggest oddness; 珍しい primarily highlights unfamiliarity or low frequency.

## Distinctive meaning

珍しい does not automatically mean suspicious, bad, or valuable. Something can be 珍しい only relative to the speaker's experience or one person's habits, without being rare everywhere.

For 珍しい compared with 変な, 怪しい and 疑わしい, see [the article on 怪しい and related words](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
