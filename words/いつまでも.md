# いつまでも - forever; indefinitely

## Meaning

いつまでも is an adverb describing continuation without a definite endpoint. Depending on the situation, it means "forever," "indefinitely," or simply "for a very long time." It does not conjugate.

- いつまでも友達でいよう。 (いつまでもともだちでいよう。) - Let's stay friends forever.
- いつまでも待つことはできません。 (いつまでもまつことはできません。) - I cannot wait indefinitely.

The first is a wish for lasting friendship. The second sets a limit on continuing to wait; it does not mean "I can never wait."

## How the kanji combine

This is normally a kana expression. You can recognize [いつ](いつ.md), "when," followed by まで and も. Learn the whole expression as open-ended continuation, rather than translating the pieces as a question. The dictionary also records 何時までも.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずっと](ずっと.md) can describe continuous duration too. [いつも](いつも.md) means "always" or "habitually," often concerning repeated occasions rather than one uninterrupted continuation.

## Distinctive meaning

いつまでも can be affectionate, patient, or critical. In a complaint it may suggest "still, after all this time." The feeling comes from the predicate and situation, not from an inherently positive or negative meaning. With a negative possibility such as 待てない (まてない), it denies the ability to continue indefinitely.
