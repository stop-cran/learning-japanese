# 周り (まわり) - surroundings, around

## Meaning

周り means the surrounding area, the people around someone, or a circumference. Use noun + の + 周り to identify the reference point.

- 駅の周りに店がたくさんあります。 (えきのまわりにみせがたくさんあります。) - There are many shops around the station.
- 池の周りを歩きました。 (いけのまわりをあるきました。) - I walked around the pond.

The を in the second example marks a route, not an object changed by walking.

## How the kanji combine

周 writes まわ, and り completes the noun. The character suits surrounding space or a perimeter. Learn this spelling separately from words involving the act of rotating.

## Synonyms and antonyms

近く (ちかく) means "nearby" without necessarily suggesting an area around something. 中 (なか), "inside," contrasts spatially with the surroundings, but is not an opposite in every use.

## Distinctive meaning

周りの人 (まわりのひと) can mean the people in one's social environment, not just those physically standing nearby. English "around" has many uses; 周り is a noun and generally needs the appropriate の and location or route particles.
