# 現れる (あらわれる) - appear; become apparent

## Meaning

現れる is an intransitive ichidan verb: someone or something appears, becomes visible, or becomes apparent. The appearing thing takes が; a place where it appears can take に.

- 雲の間から月が現れました。 (くものあいだからつきがあらわれました。) - The moon appeared from between the clouds.
- 練習の効果が現れてきました。 (れんしゅうのこうかがあらわれてきました。) - The effects of practice have begun to show.

Forms include 現れます (あらわれます), 現れない (あらわれない), and 現れて (あらわれて).

## How the kanji combine

[現](../kanji/現.md) contributes appearing or becoming present. It is read あらわ here, with れる completing the verb. The spelling 表れる (あらわれる) is also used for something being expressed or manifested, such as emotion showing on a face.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出る](出る.md) (でる) can overlap with appearing, but also means leaving or coming out. [表す](表す.md) (あらわす) is transitive: 気持ちを表す (きもちをあらわす), "express one's feelings." These have different subject and object patterns.

## Distinctive meaning

気持ちが顔に表れる (きもちがかおにあらわれる) means "feelings show on one's face." It describes feelings becoming perceptible, not someone deliberately expressing an opinion. Keep this intransitive meaning even when an English translation uses "be expressed."
