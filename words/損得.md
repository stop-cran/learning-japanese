# 損得 (そんとく) - loss and gain; advantages and disadvantages

## Meaning

損得 concerns what is lost or gained, financially or in a broader assessment
of advantage. It is a noun, not a verb meaning only "make a profit."

- 損得をよく考えてから決めましょう。 (そんとくをよくかんがえてからきめましょう。) - Let's weigh the advantages and disadvantages before deciding.
- 彼は損得を抜きにして友人を助けました。 (かれはそんとくをぬきにしてゆうじんをたすけました。) - He helped his friend without considering personal gain or loss.

損得を抜きにして means leaving that calculation aside. It does not say that
the action necessarily causes a financial loss.

## How the kanji combine

[損](../kanji/損.md) is loss or disadvantage; [得](../kanji/得.md) is gain
or benefit. The opposite pair covers the two sides of an evaluation.
The on readings are ソン + トク, with no voicing of とく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[長短](長短.md) (ちょうたん) includes strengths and weaknesses or pros and
cons, as well as length. 利害 (りがい) also concerns benefit and harm or
interests. [利益](利益.md) (りえき), "profit; benefit," names the favorable
side rather than the whole pair.

## Distinctive meaning

損得 need not describe money alone. It can concern whether a choice benefits
someone, but it does not by itself decide whether that choice is ethical,
kind, or fair. 損得だけで判断する (そんとくだけではんだんする), "judge
only by gain and loss," makes that limited basis explicit.
