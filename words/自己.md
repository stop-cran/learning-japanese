# 自己 (じこ) — self; oneself

## Meaning

自己 refers to the self: the person doing the examining, introducing, or judging is also its object.

- 旅は自己を見つめ直すきっかけになった。 (たびはじこをみつめなおすきっかけになった。) — The trip gave me a chance to take a fresh look at myself.
- 授業の最初に自己紹介をした。 (じゅぎょうのさいしょにじこしょうかいをした。) — I introduced myself at the beginning of class.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) (じ) and [己](../kanji/己.md) (こ) both contribute the idea of oneself. In 自己紹介 (じこしょうかい), 自己 specifies whom you introduce.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[自分](自分.md) (じぶん) can refer back to a person: 自分の部屋 is one’s own room. [自身](自身.md) (じしん) can emphasize the person in question, as in 私自身, “I myself.”

## Distinctive meaning

自己 is not a drop-in conversational replacement for 私 (わたし), “I.” In the first example 自己を is what the speaker reexamines; in the second 自己 forms part of a compound.
