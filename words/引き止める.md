# 引き止める (ひきとめる) - detain; hold someone back

## Meaning

引き止める means to stop someone, keep a departing person from leaving,
or hold someone back from an intended action. It is a transitive
ichidan verb; the person stopped takes を.

- 帰ろうとする友達を引き止めました。 (かえろうとするともだちをひきとめました。) - I stopped my friend from leaving.
- 道を尋ねるために、通りかかった人を引き止めました。 (みちをたずねるために、とおりかかったひとをひきとめました。) - I stopped a passerby to ask the way.

Forms include 引き止めます, 引き止めて, and 引き止めない.
Calling out or persuading can accomplish this; physical grabbing is
not required.

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) supplies the 引き element, and
[止](../kanji/止.md) the stopping association through
[止める](止める.md) (とめる). Learn the whole compound
ひきとめる. 引き留める is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

止める (とめる) is the broader verb "stop something."
[捕まえる](捕まえる.md) (つかまえる) can also include stopping
or detaining a person; it is not restricted to catching an insect.
[妨げる](妨げる.md) (さまたげる) concerns hindering or obstructing.
Letting someone leave is a contextual contrast, not an interchangeable
verb made by changing this word's ending.

## Distinctive meaning

English "check" here concerns restraining or stopping, not checking an
answer for accuracy. Nor is every mechanical stop naturally 引き止める:
the useful core is preventing a person from continuing, leaving, or
taking an action. The assigned reading is ひきとめる; public dictionaries
also record the related ひきとどめる, but the readings must not be
swapped mechanically in these forms.
