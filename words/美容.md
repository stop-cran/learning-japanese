# 美容 (びよう) - beauty care; beautification

## Meaning

美容 concerns beauty or an attractive appearance, and also the care
and treatments used to improve appearance. In practical expressions,
it often concerns beauty care rather than simply calling an object beautiful.

- 美容に関心があります。 (びようにかんしんがあります。) - I am interested in beauty care.
- この雑誌には美容についての記事が多いです。 (このざっしにはびようについてのきじがおおいです。) - This magazine has many articles about beauty care.

美容院 (びよういん) is a beauty or hair salon.
The dictionary records both beautiful appearance/features and
beautification, beauty treatments, or cosmetics.

## How the kanji combine

[美](../kanji/美.md) contributes beauty and
[容](../kanji/容.md) appearance or form.
The related ideas combine around an attractive appearance and its care.
The on readings are ビ + ヨウ; retain the long final vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[化粧](化粧.md) (けしょう) concerns makeup or applying it.
美容 is broader: hair or skin care need not involve makeup.
[美しい](美しい.md) (うつくしい) and [きれい](きれい.md)
can describe something as beautiful; 美容 is not that adjective.

## Distinctive meaning

美容 is not restricted to body shape, despite the source gloss
"beauty of figure or form." Nor is every beautiful landscape a
subject of beauty treatment.
The noun does not guarantee a treatment's effectiveness, safety,
or a shared objective standard of attractiveness.
