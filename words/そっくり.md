# そっくり (そっくり) - exactly alike; entirely

## Meaning

そっくり has two useful constructions. As a な-adjective, it describes striking
resemblance: AはBにそっくりだ, "A looks just like B." As an adverb, it can mean
that something is included or transferred in its entirety.

- 娘は母親にそっくりです。 (むすめはははおやにそっくりです。) - The daughter looks just like her mother.
- もらったお金をそっくり寄付しました。 (もらったおかねをそっくりきふしました。) - I donated all the money I received.

Before a noun, use な: 母親にそっくりな娘 (ははおやにそっくりなむすめ),
"a daughter who looks just like her mother."

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana-written mimetic word, not a kanji compound or a foreign loan.
The small っ matters: そっくり has a held consonant before く.
Its whole shape carries the learned meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[似る](似る.md) (にる), especially 似ている, describes resemblance.
そっくり emphasizes a close match. [全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ), "all," overlaps
with the entirety sense rather than the resemblance construction.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate the donation example as money "looking alike." The particles
and predicate distinguish the uses. Equally, saying that two people are
そっくり is an ordinary assessment of resemblance, not a scientific claim
that they are identical in every characteristic.
