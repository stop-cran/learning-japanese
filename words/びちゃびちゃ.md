# びちゃびちゃ — splashing and soaked

## Meaning

びちゃびちゃ describes repeated wet splashes or something made very wet by splashing.

- 子どもが水たまりを歩き、靴がびちゃびちゃになった。
  — The child walked through puddles, and the shoes became soaked.
- 泥水が跳ねて、ズボンの裾がびちゃびちゃに濡れた。
  — Muddy water splashed up and soaked the trouser cuffs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[べちゃべちゃ](べちゃべちゃ.md) can describe something wet and mushy or sticky, while
[びしょびしょ](びしょびしょ.md) emphasizes being thoroughly wet. びちゃびちゃ foregrounds
wet splashing or the resulting soaked state.

## Distinctive meaning

The scene determines whether the word describes the splashing action or the wet result. The
examples show both, but a quiet, damp surface alone does not establish a splashing sound.
