# 悔しい (くやしい) - bitterly frustrated; mortified

## Meaning

悔しい expresses bitter frustration or disappointment over a failure, humiliation or unfair treatment. It can describe the feeling or the frustrating situation.

- 試合に負けて悔しかったです。 (しあいにまけてくやしかったです。) - I was bitterly disappointed that I lost the match.
- 努力が認められなくて、悔しいです。 (どりょくがみとめられなくて、くやしいです。) - I feel frustrated that my efforts are not recognized.

It is an i-adjective: 悔しく (くやしく), 悔しかった (くやしかった), 悔しくない (くやしくない).

## How the kanji combine

悔 supplies the regret/frustration association in the native adjective くやしい. There is no 悔 card in the current collection. The dictionary also records 口惜しい as a rare kanji spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[残念](残念.md) (ざんねん) more broadly describes something disappointing or regrettable. 悔しい particularly foregrounds the bitter feeling of wanting a better result or not accepting what happened.

[悔やむ](悔やむ.md) (くやむ) is a verb for mourning or regretting something. Feeling 悔しい after a defeat does not necessarily mean repenting a wrongdoing.

## Distinctive meaning

English bitter here describes an emotion, not the flavor in [苦い](苦い.md) (にがい). Nor is 悔しい a neutral word for every sad event: the frustration, wounded pride or sense of unfairness matters.
