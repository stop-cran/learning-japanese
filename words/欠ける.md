# 欠ける (かける) - to be chipped; be lacking

## Meaning

欠ける is an intransitive ichidan verb. It can describe a piece breaking off, something missing
from a whole, or a person or thing lacking a needed quality.

- 茶わんの縁が欠けています。 (ちゃわんのふちがかけています。) - The rim of the bowl is chipped.
- この説明は具体性に欠けます。 (このせつめいはぐたいせいにかけます。) - This explanation lacks specificity.

With physical damage, the affected part takes が. In Nに欠ける, に names the quality that is
insufficient. 欠けている can describe the resulting incomplete state, not just an action happening now.
Forms include 欠けた and 欠けない.

## How the kanji combine

[欠](../kanji/欠.md) supplies the idea of something missing or incomplete.
The native verb is かける, with ける written as okurigana. There is no two-kanji relationship
to split and no reason to substitute the on reading ケツ in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[足りる](足りる.md) (たりる), "be sufficient," gives a useful contrast; 足りない also describes a shortage.
[割れる](割れる.md) (われる), "break; split," can describe a larger break rather than a chipped edge.
These words overlap in situations, not in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

Focus on what makes a whole incomplete. 全員そろう contrasts with 一人欠ける
(ひとりかける), "one person is missing." A bowl may still be usable while 欠けている.
The same pronunciation appears in unrelated verbs, so the spelling and particles help identify this one.
