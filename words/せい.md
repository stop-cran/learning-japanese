# せい (せい) - fault; cause of an adverse result

## Meaning

せい attributes a result, often an undesirable one, to a cause. Use noun + のせい, a plain verb or い-adjective + せい, or a な-adjective + なせい. せいで introduces the consequence; せいだ identifies what is to blame.

- 雨のせいで、試合が中止になりました。 (あめのせいで、しあいがちゅうしになりました。) - The match was canceled because of the rain.
- 寝るのが遅かったせいか、今日は眠いです。 (ねるのがおそかったせいか、きょうはねむいです。) - Perhaps because I went to bed late, I am sleepy today.

せいか presents the cause as a possibility, not an established explanation.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses the usual kana form. 所為 (せい), the community source spelling, is marked as a rare kanji form in the dictionary. It represents the same assigned identity, not another word that needs a separate meaning.
The Japanese dictionary cited below tentatively derives せい from the Sino-Japanese reading しょい.
The `kango` classification follows that analysis without treating the proposed origin as certain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[原因](原因.md) (げんいん) neutrally identifies a cause. [おかげ](おかげ.md) typically attributes a welcome result, although context can make it ironic. These constructions convey an evaluation beyond merely naming a reason.

## Distinctive meaning

あなたのせいです means "It's your fault," so it can sound accusatory. The pattern does not require an intentional action: weather or illness can also be blamed. In 気のせい (きのせい), "just one's imagination," the phrase attributes an impression to the perceiver rather than confirming an external event.
