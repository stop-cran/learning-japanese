# 副詞 (ふくし) - adverb

## Meaning

副詞 is the grammatical term adverb. In Japanese grammar, these
words generally do not conjugate and commonly modify a predicate,
give a degree, or frame how a statement is made.
The term 副詞 itself is a noun naming the category.

- 「ゆっくり」は副詞です。 (「ゆっくり」はふくしです。) - "Yukkuri" is an adverb.
- 「とても」は程度を表す副詞です。 (「とても」はていどをあらわすふくしです。) - "Totemo" is an adverb expressing degree.

Compare ゆっくり歩く (ゆっくりあるく), walk slowly, with
とても寒い (とてもさむい), very cold. The second modifies
an adjective rather than a verb.

## How the kanji combine

[副](../kanji/副.md) contributes an accompanying or secondary
role, and [詞](../kanji/詞.md) occurs in grammatical category
names. The on readings are フク + シ.
This character association is not a complete definition of an
adverb and does not mean an assistant person.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動詞](動詞.md) (どうし), verb, and
[形容詞](形容詞.md) (けいようし), adjective, name other
categories. A word's grammatical category is not determined simply
by whether its English translation ends in "-ly."

## Distinctive meaning

Do not limit 副詞 to "a word that modifies a verb."
Degree adverbs can modify adjectives and other adverbs, and some
adverbs work with the form of the whole statement.
Analyses can differ at category boundaries; this card introduces
the term, not an exhaustive classification of every expression.
