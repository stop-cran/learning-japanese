# 有効 (ゆうこう) - valid; effective

## Meaning

有効 is a な-adjective and noun-like expression meaning valid or effective. Use 有効な before a noun and 有効です as a predicate. The context decides whether something is officially usable or produces a useful result.

- この切符は明日まで有効です。 (このきっぷはあしたまでゆうこうです。) - This ticket is valid through tomorrow.
- この方法は時間の節約に有効です。 (このほうほうはじかんのせつやくにゆうこうです。) - This method is effective for saving time.

有効に使う (ゆうこうにつかう) means "use effectively."

## How the kanji combine

[有](../kanji/有.md) contributes having or possessing. 効 contributes effect or efficacy. Together they suggest "having effect," extended to documents or permissions that are in force.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[効果](効果.md) (こうか) is an effect or result. 有効 says something is effective; the noun 効果 names the effect. 無効 (むこう), "invalid/ineffective," is the direct opposite recorded in the dictionary.

## Distinctive meaning

Validity and effectiveness are related but different applications. A valid ticket meets the conditions for use; an effective method achieves its purpose. 有効 does not generally mean that an item is in stock or a person is free. 有効期限 (ゆうこうきげん) is an expiry date or period of validity, not a promise that something will remain available for sale.
