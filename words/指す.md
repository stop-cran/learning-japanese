# 指す (さす) - point; indicate

## Meaning

指す is a transitive godan verb meaning to point to or indicate something.
Use Xを指す for the thing indicated. It also means "play" specifically with shogi:
将棋を指す (しょうぎをさす).

- 先生は地図の上の町を指しました。 (せんせいはちずのうえのまちをさしました。) - The teacher pointed to the town on the map.
- 時計の針は三時を指しています。 (とけいのはりはさんじをさしています。) - The clock's hands show three o'clock.
- 祖父と将棋を指します。 (そふとしょうぎをさします。) - I play shogi with my grandfather.

Useful forms are 指して (さして), 指した (さした), and 指さない (ささない).

## How the kanji combine

[指](../kanji/指.md) contributes the pointing meaning and also appears in the noun 指 (ゆび), "finger."
The verb's native reading is さす. An actual finger is not required: clock hands and words can also indicate something.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[示す](示す.md) (しめす), "show; indicate," overlaps with the indication sense.
The homophone [差す](差す.md) (さす) is a different written verb in 傘を差す (かさをさす), "put up/hold up an umbrella."
[遊ぶ](遊ぶ.md) (あそぶ), "play," is not interchangeable with the shogi construction.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the object and spelling together. The umbrella expression is not evidence for writing 傘を指す,
and the shogi sense does not make 指す a general verb for playing any game.
