# さあ (さあ) - well; come on

## Meaning

さあ is an interjection whose effect depends on the situation and intonation. It can express hesitation or uncertainty, or encourage someone to begin.

- さあ、分かりません。 (さあ、わかりません。) - Well, I'm not sure.
- さあ、行きましょう。 (さあ、いきましょう。) - Come on, let's go.

A speaker can also use it to mark their own resolve: さあ、始めよう (さあ、はじめよう), "Right, let's start."

## How the kanji combine

This spoken expression is written in kana. The long あ sound matters; さあ is not a kanji compound requiring a character-by-character explanation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あの](あの.md) can introduce a hesitant approach or question. ええと often fills a thinking pause. These overlap with only some uses of さあ, not its encouraging "come on."

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary gloss "well" does not mean "in a good manner" here. It is a conversational signal. A drawn-out, uncertain さあ in response to a question can imply that the speaker does not know; an energetic さあ before a suggestion pushes the activity forward.
