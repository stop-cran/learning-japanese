# 評論 (ひょうろん) - critique; critical essay

## Meaning

評論 is critical discussion or evaluation of a work, issue, or other
subject, and also a text expressing that discussion.
It is a noun and a transitive する verb: Nを評論する,
critique or discuss N critically.

- 新聞で映画の評論を読みました。 (しんぶんでえいがのひょうろんをよみました。) - I read a critique of a film in the newspaper.
- この本には文学の評論が収められています。 (このほんにはぶんがくのひょうろんがおさめられています。) - This book contains literary criticism.

評論家 (ひょうろんか) names a critic or commentator.

## How the kanji combine

評 contributes appraisal or comment, and [論](../kanji/論.md)
discussion or argument. The related meanings combine in discussing
and assessing a subject. The on readings are ヒョウ + ロン.
The word is not simply any text containing an opinion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[批評](批評.md) (ひひょう) also means a critique or review.
[批判](批判.md) (ひはん) overlaps in critical judgment, while
[評価](評価.md) (ひょうか) concerns assessment or appraisal.
Their uses overlap; do not invent an absolute rule assigning every
film review, political comment, or literary essay to only one term.

## Distinctive meaning

"Critical" here means examining and assessing, not necessarily
attacking someone or giving an unfavorable verdict.
評論 can discuss strengths as well as weaknesses.
A mere plot summary or a personal reaction is not automatically
a developed critical discussion.
