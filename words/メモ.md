# メモ - note; memo; jot down

## Meaning

メモ is a noun for a note or memo. メモする is a transitive verb meaning to note something down: the information takes を. メモを取る (メモをとる) is another useful phrase for taking notes.

- 電話番号をメモしました。 (でんわばんごうをメモしました。) - I wrote down the phone number.
- 忘れないように、メモを残してください。 (わすれないように、メモをのこしてください。) - Please leave a note so that we do not forget.

In the first sentence, the number is the information recorded. In the second, メモ names the note itself, which can be left for someone.

## How the kanji combine

メモ is a katakana loanword corresponding to "memo" or "memorandum." There are no written kanji or native components to unpack. Adding する makes the note-taking verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[記録](記録.md) (きろく) is a record and can cover a wider range of documentation. [要点](要点.md) (ようてん) names main points; those may be the content of a メモ, rather than the note itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Despite the English gloss "memorandum," メモ need not be an official business document. A shopping reminder, a phone number, or a short digital note also fits. The verb refers to recording information, not simply remembering it mentally; writing a メモ can help you remember, but those are different actions.
