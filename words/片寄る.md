# 片寄る (かたよる) - lean to one side; be biased

## Meaning

片寄る means leaning or moving to one side, being unevenly distributed or concentrated, or being biased. 偏る is another spelling of the same かたよる.

- 荷物が片方に片寄っています。 (にもつがかたほうにかたよっています。) - The luggage is unevenly concentrated on one side.
- 仕事が一人に片寄らないようにしてください。 (しごとがひとりにかたよらないようにしてください。) - Please make sure the work does not all fall on one person.
- 片寄った見方をしないことが大切です。 (かたよったみかたをしないことがたいせつです。) - It is important not to take a biased view.

The godan forms are 片寄ります, 片寄って, and 片寄らない. The first two examples concern distribution, not necessarily an unfair personal opinion.

## How the kanji combine

[片](../kanji/片.md), かた, contributes one side; [寄](../kanji/寄.md), よる, contributes approaching or gathering toward it. The parts combine as a native verb. The alternative 偏る uses a different written character, but the cited entry does not impose a strict physical-versus-mental sense partition between the spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[傾く](傾く.md) (かたむく) overlaps in inclination. [寄る](寄る.md) (よる) is broader: approaching or stopping by need not involve imbalance. [公平](公平.md) (こうへい), fair or impartial, provides a useful contrast for biased judgment, not for every physical concentration.

## Distinctive meaning

Look at what is uneven: luggage, workload, or a viewpoint. It is misleading to translate every 片寄る as moral prejudice, or to assume that only the spelling 偏る can describe opinions. Learn the source spelling 片寄る while recognizing the shared lexical identity.
