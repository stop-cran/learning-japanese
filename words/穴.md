# 穴 (あな) - hole; opening

## Meaning

穴 is a noun for a hole, opening, or hollow, including a hole through fabric and a pit in the ground.
Use Xに穴がある for "X has a hole," and 穴を開ける (あなをあける) for making an opening.

- 靴下に穴が開いています。 (くつしたにあながあいています。) - There is a hole in my sock.
- 庭に穴を掘りました。 (にわにあなをほりました。) - I dug a hole in the garden.

開いている describes the resulting open state in the first example. 掘る (ほる), "dig," tells how the second hole was made.

## How the kanji combine

穴 is a single-kanji native noun, read あな. The character represents the whole word; it is not a compound to divide into separate word meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[空き](空き.md) (あき) can mean a vacant space or opening, but a hole in a sock is 穴.
[欠点](欠点.md) (けってん), "shortcoming," overlaps with a figurative use of 穴.
There is no general opposite noun: closing or filling the hole describes the contrasting action.

## Distinctive meaning

A hole need not pass all the way through something: a hollow in the ground also qualifies.
The image extends to an omission or weakness, as in 計画に穴がある (けいかくにあながある), "there is a flaw in the plan."
Read the surrounding noun before assuming that a physical opening is intended.
