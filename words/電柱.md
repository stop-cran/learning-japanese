# 電柱 (でんちゅう) - utility pole

## Meaning

電柱 is a utility pole supporting overhead wires, such as power or telephone lines.
It is a noun, not the name of every tall post beside a road.

- 道の脇に電柱が立っています。 (みちのわきにでんちゅうがたっています。) - A utility pole stands beside the road.
- 電柱の上に鳥が止まっています。 (でんちゅうのうえにとりがとまっています。) - A bird is perched on top of the utility pole.

The word identifies the pole by its function, not by whether it is wooden or concrete.

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) contributes the electrical association and [柱](../kanji/柱.md) a post or pillar.
This modifier-plus-head compound names the post used for overhead lines.
The on readings are デン + チュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[柱](柱.md) (はしら) is a pillar or supporting post more generally.
電信柱 (でんしんばしら) is a related name for a telegraph or telephone pole.
電線 (でんせん) names an electric wire, not the structure holding it up.

## Distinctive meaning

A pole carrying a streetlight may also be a utility pole, but a freestanding lamp post is not
automatically 電柱 merely because it provides light.
Likewise, [棒](棒.md) (ぼう), "rod; pole," does not by itself identify the overhead-wire function.
