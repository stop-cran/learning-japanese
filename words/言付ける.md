# 言付ける (ことづける) - send word through someone; entrust for delivery

## Meaning

言付ける means asking someone to pass on a message or deliver an item. The person entrusted with it can take に; a message can be quoted with と, and an item can take を.

- 田中さんは留守だったので、受付の人に「また電話します」と言付けました。 (たなかさんはるすだったので、うけつけのひとに「またでんわします」とことづけました。) - Tanaka was out, so I left a message with the receptionist saying that I would call again.
- 母へのお土産を友達に言付けました。 (ははへのおみやげをともだちにことづけました。) - I entrusted a friend with a souvenir to deliver to my mother.

It is a transitive ichidan verb: 言付けます, 言付けて, 言付けない.

## How the kanji combine

[言](../kanji/言.md) supplies the word/message association, while [付](../kanji/付.md) contributes attaching or entrusting. Learn the established native verb as ことづける, with **づ**, not ず. The related spelling 託ける is also recorded for this reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[伝える](伝える.md) (つたえる), "convey; tell," does not itself require an intermediary. [送る](送る.md) (おくる) is broader sending. [預ける](預ける.md) (あずける) emphasizes leaving something in someone's care; 言付ける specifically supplies the onward message or delivery task.

[任せる](任せる.md) (まかせる) also concerns entrusting, but can leave the handling of a matter or decision to someone. It does not itself specify passing a message or item onward.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish this reading from 言い付ける (いいつける), which can mean ordering someone or telling on them. The shorter written 言付ける can appear under that other dictionary heading too, so the reading and situation matter.

The cited family also has an archaic pretext sense. It is not the delivery meaning illustrated here.
