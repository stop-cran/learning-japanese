# 右手 (みぎて) — right hand

## Meaning

右手 is the right hand (or arm): 右手を上げる (raise your right hand), 右手で書く (write with the right hand). It can also mean "the right side, to the right": 右手に見えるのが駅です (what you can see on the right is the station).

## How the kanji combine

[右](../kanji/右.md) (right) plus [手](../kanji/手.md) (hand), both with kun readings: みぎ + て. Note that 右手 is read みぎて, not うしゅ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [左手](左手.md) ひだりて.
- Close: 右側 みぎがわ (the right side), 右 みぎ (right), 利き手 ききて (dominant hand).

## Distinctive meaning

右手 is concrete: the hand, or the direction as seen from where you stand. For the side as a whole, 右側 or just 右 is more common.
