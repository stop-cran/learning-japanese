# 強気 (つよき) - confident; bullish

## Meaning

強気 describes confidence, firmness, or an assertive attitude. Use 強気な before a noun and 強気に when describing how someone acts.

- 彼女は交渉で強気な態度を見せました。
  (かのじょはこうしょうでつよきなたいどをみせました。) - She took a firm stance in the negotiations.
- 店は強気の価格をつけています。
  (みせはつよきのかかくをつけています。) - The shop is setting its prices confidently high.

In markets, 強気 means bullish: expecting prices to rise. 相場に強気だ (そうばにつよきだ) means "be bullish about the market." The confidence need not turn out to be justified.

## How the kanji combine

[強](../kanji/強.md) contributes strength and [気](../kanji/気.md) spirit or attitude. Here they concern mental stance, not the physical strength of a body or material. Learn the complete reading つよき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[積極的](積極的.md) (せっきょくてき) overlaps in assertiveness but also concerns taking an active or proactive approach. 弱気 (よわき), "timid; bearish," provides the opposite stance.

## Distinctive meaning

強気に出る (つよきにでる) means to take a firm or assertive line. It is not automatically praise for courage: context can present the stance as excessive or unrealistic. Do not use this word as a general replacement for [強い](強い.md) (つよい), "strong."
