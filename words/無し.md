# 無し (なし) - without; none

## Meaning

無し is a noun or suffix-like expression meaning "without" or "none." It is often written なし. Useful patterns are Nなしで, "without N," and Nなしの + noun, "a noun without N."

- 砂糖なしでコーヒーを飲みます。 (さとうなしでコーヒーをのみます。) - I drink coffee without sugar.
- 予約なしの客は入れません。 (よやくなしのきゃくははいれません。) - Customers without reservations cannot enter.

Brief labels can omit the rest of the sentence: 変更なし (へんこうなし), "no change." なし does not take the ordinary adjective ending changes of ない.

## How the kanji combine

無 writes the absence meaning, with し completing this established word. The kana spelling なし is also normal. Although related in meaning to ない, this noun-like expression has its own constructions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

Compare 砂糖がない, "there is no sugar," with 砂糖なしで, "without sugar." The former is a predicate; the latter modifies an action. あり gives a corresponding "present; included" label in pairs such as 予約あり・予約なし.

## Distinctive meaning

Conversational それはなしです can mean "that is not acceptable" or "that option is out." This evaluative use is different from literal absence. [不足](不足.md) (ふそく), "shortage," is also different: not enough of something is not necessarily none of it. Learn the whole construction rather than substituting なし for every negative word.
