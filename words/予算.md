# 予算 (よさん) - budget; cost estimate

## Meaning

予算 is a budget or estimate of costs.
Learn it as a noun in 予算を立てる (よさんをたてる), "draw up a budget,"
and 予算を超える (よさんをこえる), "exceed the budget."
It can concern personal spending as well as organizations or governments.

- 旅行の予算は五万円です。 (りょこうのよさんはごまんえんです。) - The budget for the trip is fifty thousand yen.
- 予算内で必要な家具を買いました。 (よさんないでひつようなかぐをかいました。) - I bought the necessary furniture within the budget.

予算内 (よさんない) means "within budget"; 予算が足りない (よさんがたりない) means "the budget is insufficient."

## How the kanji combine

[予](../kanji/予.md) contributes advance preparation, and 算 calculation.
Together they suggest calculating in advance.
The on-readings are ヨ + サン.
This image explains the planning aspect without implying that every estimate will match the final expense exactly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[費用](費用.md) (ひよう) is cost or expense.
[計算](計算.md) (けいさん) is calculation, a process used to prepare or check a budget.
[収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう) is income, which may constrain spending but is not itself the same thing as a budget.

## Distinctive meaning

A 予算 is planned or allocated money, not necessarily money already spent.
When someone asks 予算はいくらですか, they are asking what amount is available or intended,
not automatically the final price already paid.
