# 来年 (らいねん) — next year

## Meaning

来年 is the year after this one: 来年日本へ行きます (I'm going to Japan next year), 来年の春 (next spring), 来年こそ (surely next year).

## How the kanji combine

来 (come) plus [年](../kanji/年.md) (year): "the year that is coming". 来 appears the same way in 来月 らいげつ (next month) and 来週 らいしゅう (next week). The reading is the on'yomi ライ + ネン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Series: 一昨年 おととし (the year before last), 去年 きょねん (last year), [今年](今年.md) ことし (this year), 来年 らいねん, 再来年 さらいねん (the year after next).
- Antonym: 去年 きょねん (last year).

## Distinctive meaning

Neutral and everyday. Greeting-card language uses 明年 (formal), but ordinary speech uses 来年.
