# 人造 (じんぞう) - artificial; man-made

## Meaning

人造 concerns making something through human work, especially producing
something the same as or resembling a naturally occurring material.
It can also name the resulting product. Use 人造の before a noun;
the word also forms compounds.

- これは人造の宝石です。 (これはじんぞうのほうせきです。) - This is an artificial gemstone.
- この道具には人造ダイヤモンドを使っています。 (このどうぐにはじんぞうダイヤモンドをつかっています。) - This tool uses synthetic diamond.

人造ダイヤモンド is read じんぞうダイヤモンド.
The word describes human manufacture, not automatically poor quality.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md), human, qualifies [造](../kanji/造.md), making or
manufacture. Their on readings are ジン + ゾウ.
The component relationship is "made by people," not a claim that every
object made by a person is ordinarily labelled 人造.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[人工](人工.md) (じんこう) substantially overlaps and can describe
human intervention or artificially produced features more broadly.
[自然](自然.md) (しぜん), nature or natural, provides a contrast;
天然 (てんねん) is useful when contrasting naturally occurring materials.

## Distinctive meaning

人造の is the basic noun-modifying pattern here; do not infer 人造な
from its English adjective gloss.
"Synthetic" need not mean fraudulent: an openly described human-made
material is not automatically a counterfeit sold as natural.
Conversely, copying a person's behavior is not this manufacturing sense.
