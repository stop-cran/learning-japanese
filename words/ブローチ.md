# ブローチ - brooch

## Meaning

ブローチ is a brooch: an ornament fastened to clothing, typically with a pin.
It is a noun, and ブローチを付ける means to put on or attach a brooch.

- コートにブローチを付けました。 (コートにブローチをつけました。) - I attached a brooch to my coat.
- 母から花の形のブローチをもらいました。 (ははからはなのかたちのブローチをもらいました。) - My mother gave me a flower-shaped brooch.

## How the kanji combine

ブローチ is a katakana loanword. It has a long ロー sound and no kanji
compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[アクセサリー](アクセサリー.md) is broader, including personal accessories.
[飾り](飾り.md) (かざり) means a decoration or ornament.
A [ピン](ピン.md) may fasten clothing or hair without being a decorative brooch.
The categories overlap but are not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not require a gemstone or an expensive metal.
The same katakana spelling also has a separate dictionary entry for
English "broach," a cutting tool. That is not another
purpose of the jewelry taught here.
