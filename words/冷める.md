# 冷める (さめる) — cool down; lose enthusiasm

## Meaning

冷める means to cool or get cold, often of something previously hot. It is intransitive: the cooling thing takes が.

お茶が冷めてしまった。（おちゃがさめてしまった。） — The tea has gone cold.

Enthusiasm, affection or anger can also subside.

その話を聞いて、旅行への熱が冷めた。（そのはなしをきいて、りょこうへのねつがさめた。） — Hearing that took away my enthusiasm for the trip.

The word also describes a cold or composed expression, as in 冷めた目（さめため）, a detached or unimpressed look.

## How the kanji combine

[冷](../kanji/冷.md) supplies the cooling meaning in the native verb さめる. Compare [冷ます](冷ます.md) さます, the transitive counterpart: お茶を冷ます, let the tea cool, versus お茶が冷める, the tea cools.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冷える](冷える.md) ひえる also means grow cold/cool down and is useful for bodies, air and objects. [冷たい](冷たい.md) つめたい describes a cold state, rather than itself naming the change. [落ち着く](落ち着く.md) おちつく can describe calming down, with wider settling uses.

## Distinctive meaning

This ichidan verb becomes 冷めます, 冷めない and 冷めた. Emotional cooling need not be physical temperature change. [覚める](覚める.md), also さめる, means wake up or regain awareness; do not choose its spelling solely because the pronunciation matches.
