# 目標 (もくひょう) - goal; target; landmark

## Meaning

目標 is a noun for a goal, target, or objective. **目標を立てる** (もくひょうをたてる) means "set a goal"; **目標を達成する** (もくひょうをたっせいする) means "achieve a goal."

- 毎日三十分読むことを目標にしました。 (まいにちさんじゅっぷんよむことをもくひょうにしました。) - I set a goal of reading for thirty minutes every day.
- チームは今年の目標を達成しました。 (チームはことしのもくひょうをたっせいしました。) - The team achieved this year's target.

**Xを目標にする** identifies X as the aim. X can be a noun or a nominalized action ending in こと.

## How the kanji combine

[目](../kanji/目.md) contributes seeing or directing one's attention, and 標 contributes a mark or sign. モク + ヒョウ evokes a mark to aim toward. That association links concrete markers with abstract goals.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[目的](目的.md) (もくてき) also means a purpose or objective. 目標 often presents a target to work toward, while 目的 can explain what an activity is for. The words overlap; they are not rigidly separated into measurable and unmeasurable aims.

## Distinctive meaning

目標 can also be a visible mark or landmark used for direction: 赤い屋根を目標に進む (あかいやねをもくひょうにすすむ), "head toward the red roof as a landmark." A target need not be a numerical performance figure, and setting a 目標 does not claim it has been achieved.
