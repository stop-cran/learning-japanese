# 申し上げる (もうしあげる) - say or express humbly

## Meaning

申し上げる is a humble ichidan verb meaning "say," "tell," or "express." It presents the speaker's side's words deferentially toward the recipient.

- 先生にお礼を申し上げました。 (せんせいにおれいをもうしあげました。) - I expressed my thanks to the teacher.
- 心からお祝い申し上げます。 (こころからおいわいもうしあげます。) - I offer my heartfelt congratulations.

The recipient can take に. Forms include 申し上げます and 申し上げた (もうしあげます、もうしあげた).

## How the kanji combine

[申](../kanji/申.md) belongs to 申す (もうす), "say" humbly. Its connecting form 申し combines with [上](../kanji/上.md)げる (あげる). The complete expression is not a literal instruction to speak upward.

## Synonyms and antonyms

言う (いう) is neutral. 申す is another humble or formal expression for saying. おっしゃる is honorific: it respects the person speaking, rather than lowering the speaker's side.

## Distinctive meaning

申し上げる is especially useful in formal thanks, apologies, and announcements. Do not use it for a respected person's speech merely because it sounds formal; the direction of respect is part of the construction.
