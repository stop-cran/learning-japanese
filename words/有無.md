# 有無 (うむ) - presence or absence; yes or no

## Meaning

有無 names whether something exists or is present: presence versus absence.
It also means consent versus refusal, "yes or no."
It is a noun, not a verb meaning to create something.

- アレルギーの有無を教えてください。 (アレルギーのうむをおしえてください。) - Please tell us whether you have any allergies.
- 有無を言わせずに仕事を押し付けました。 (うむをいわせずにしごとをおしつけました。) - They imposed the work without allowing any objection.

In the second example, 有無を言わせず means not letting someone consent or refuse:
the person is not given a say.

## How the kanji combine

[有](../kanji/有.md), read ウ here, concerns having or existing; 無, read ム, concerns absence.
The opposing components name the two alternatives, not "having nothing."
Learn the compound reading うむ. The pinned dictionary marks ゆうむ as outdated.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[存在](存在.md) (そんざい) names existence or presence.
有無 explicitly considers both possibilities: whether the thing is there or not.
Presence and absence are alternatives inside this compound rather than an external antonym pair.

## Distinctive meaning

On a form, Nの有無 asks whether N applies; it does not assert that N exists.
This is a general word, not a special computer term for every kind of flag.
The compound's "yes or no" use is not a general substitute for saying はい or いいえ on their own.
