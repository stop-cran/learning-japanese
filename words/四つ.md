# 四つ (よっつ) — four things

## Meaning

四つ counts four things with the native series: りんごを四つ買う (buy four apples), 四つ目 よっつめ (the fourth one).

## How the kanji combine

[四](../kanji/四.md) with the kun reading よ plus っつ; the full series is ひとつ, ふたつ, みっつ, よっつ, いつつ, むっつ, ななつ, やっつ, ここのつ, とお. In this series the っ is a sound change; the okurigana is つ.
Compare 四人 よにん, 四日 よっか and 四月 しがつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 四個 よんこ (four pieces).
- Related: 四 し／よん／よ — three readings of the same number.

## Distinctive meaning

四つ is the soft, everyday count for things without a special counter. For counting people use 四人 よにん.
