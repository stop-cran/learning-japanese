# ラジオカセット - radio-cassette player

## Meaning

ラジオカセット is a combined radio and cassette-tape player/recorder.
It names the equipment, not a particular radio program or the cassette
tape alone.

- 古いラジオカセットで音楽を聞きました。 (ふるいラジオカセットでおんがくをききました。) - I listened to music on an old radio-cassette player.

で marks the device used for listening.

## How the kanji combine

The katakana compound joins ラジオ, "radio," and カセット, "cassette."
The alternative spelling ラジオ・カセット separates the parts with a middle
dot. Neither form has kanji components; keep the small ッ in カセット.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ラジカセ is the familiar shortened name. ラジオ alone names a radio,
while カセットプレーヤー specifically names a cassette player and does not
necessarily imply a radio receiver.

## Distinctive meaning

This is vocabulary for a particular physical audio format, useful when
discussing older equipment or recordings. Do not extend it to every modern
music player. A combined unit may offer recording functions, but its exact
features depend on the device rather than the name alone.
