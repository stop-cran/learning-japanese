# 動作 (どうさ) - movement; behavior; operation

## Meaning

動作 names a bodily movement, action, or gesture, and can describe someone's bearing or manner of moving.
It also means the operation of a machine or software.

- 一つ一つの動作をゆっくり確認しました。 (ひとつひとつのどうさをゆっくりかくにんしました。) - We slowly checked each movement.
- この機械は正常に動作しています。 (このきかいはせいじょうにどうさしています。) - This machine is operating normally.

The machine use takes intransitive 動作する: the thing operating is marked by が or made the topic with は.
For a person's movement, 動作が速い (どうさがはやい), "quick in one's movements,"
is a useful noun pattern; do not mechanically replace every bodily action verb with 動作する.

## How the kanji combine

[動](../kanji/動.md) contributes movement and [作](../kanji/作.md) doing or acting.
Together they associate movement with an action or functioning.
The on readings are ドウ + サ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[行動](行動.md) (こうどう) concerns action or conduct, often what someone chooses to do.
動作 can focus more closely on the physical movement itself.
[操作](操作.md) (そうさ) concerns handling or controlling something:
an operator 操作する, while the machine 動作する.
[機能](機能.md) (きのう) concerns function or functioning.

## Distinctive meaning

Context decides whether 動作 concerns a body, a manner of acting, or equipment.
動作確認 (どうさかくにん) is checking operation, not necessarily an exercise routine.
Some word lists gloss 動作 as "manners"; that does not mean that every 動作 is an etiquette rule.
Likewise, computer operation does not imply that the machine is physically moving across the room.
