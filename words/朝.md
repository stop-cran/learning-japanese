# 朝 (あさ) — morning

## Meaning

朝 means morning, the early part of the day.

- 朝はパンを食べます。 (あさはパンをたべます。) — I eat bread in the morning.
- 毎朝六時に起きます。 (まいあさろくじにおきます。) — I get up at six every morning.

## How the kanji combine

[朝](../kanji/朝.md) represents the complete native word あさ, without okurigana. In 毎朝 まいあさ, "every morning," the on reading まい of [毎](../kanji/毎.md) combines with native あさ; that related word is mixed on + kun, not all-on.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [午前](午前.md) ごぜん means before noon or a.m., useful for clock times. Its boundaries are more precise than the everyday idea of 朝.
- [夕方](夕方.md) ゆうがた is late afternoon or early evening; [夜](夜.md) よる is night. They contrast with 朝 but name different parts of the day, not exact logical opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Use あさ for the everyday word "morning." 朝食 ちょうしょく, "breakfast," uses the on reading ちょう, while 朝ご飯 あさごはん retains あさ. The 朝 in dynasty names is a different use; do not read those automatically as あさ.
