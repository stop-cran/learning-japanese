# 再来年 (さらいねん) - year after next

## Meaning

再来年 is the calendar year after next year. It identifies a year relative to the present, not a date exactly two years from today.

- 再来年、日本へ行く予定です。 (さらいねん、にほんへいくよていです。) - I plan to go to Japan the year after next.
- 再来年の春に卒業します。 (さらいねんのはるにそつぎょうします。) - I will graduate in the spring of the year after next.

It normally works as a time expression without に. Add の when it modifies another time noun, as in 再来年の春.

## How the kanji combine

再 contributes recurrence, [来](../kanji/来.md) coming, and [年](../kanji/年.md) year. 来年 means "next year"; adding 再 advances the reference by another year. Learn さらいねん, not さいらいねん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

来年 (らいねん) is next year, and 今年 (ことし) is this year. 二年後 (にねんご) means "two years later" and can be measured from another reference point.

## Distinctive meaning

再来年 is the ordinary full spelling. JMdict treats the source-list spelling さ来年 as a search-only variant. Prefer 再来年 in normal writing; the variant does not represent another reading or another year.
