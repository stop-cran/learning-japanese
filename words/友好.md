# 友好 (ゆうこう) - friendship; friendly relations

## Meaning

友好 is friendship or friendly relations. It is especially useful when discussing relations between countries, communities, or groups, without being restricted exclusively to them.

- 両国は友好関係を深めています。
  (りょうこくはゆうこうかんけいをふかめています。) - The two countries are deepening their friendly relations.
- この交流は友好を目的としています。
  (このこうりゅうはゆうこうをもくてきとしています。) - This exchange aims to promote friendship.

友好関係 (ゆうこうかんけい) means friendly relations; 友好を深める (ゆうこうをふかめる) means deepen friendship or friendly ties.

## How the kanji combine

[友](../kanji/友.md) contributes friendship, and [好](../kanji/好.md) a favorable or friendly relationship. The on readings are ユウ + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[友情](友情.md) (ゆうじょう) also names friendship, often in personal relationships. [仲良し](仲良し.md) (なかよし) has a close-friendship sense as well as a close-friend sense.

[交際](交際.md) (こうさい) and [付き合い](付き合い.md) (つきあい) concern association or social relations more broadly; they do not inherently assert friendly international relations.

## Distinctive meaning

友好 describes the relationship, not the individual friend. [友人](友人.md) (ゆうじん) and [友達](友達.md) (ともだち) name people.

The same-sounding [有効](有効.md) (ゆうこう), valid or effective, is a different word. Shared pronunciation is not shared meaning.
