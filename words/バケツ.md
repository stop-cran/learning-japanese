# バケツ - bucket; pail

## Meaning

バケツ is a bucket or pail, such as a handled container for carrying water.
It is a noun and need not refer to a metal container.

- バケツに水を入れてください。 (バケツにみずをいれてください。) - Please put water in the bucket.
- 掃除に使うバケツを買いました。 (そうじにつかうバケツをかいました。) - I bought a bucket to use for cleaning.

に marks the destination of the water in the first sentence.
The noun itself does not specify how much the bucket holds.

## How the kanji combine

This borrowed word is written in katakana; there is no kanji compound to split.
JMdict also records rare ateji 馬穴 and 馬尻. Those are spelling-recognition
information, not reasons to explain a bucket through the literal character meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

容器 (ようき), "container," is broader.
コップ is a drinking glass or cup, not a general substitute for a cleaning bucket.
There is no useful everyday antonym for バケツ.

## Distinctive meaning

The everyday focus here is a pail for carrying or holding things.
Do not infer a fixed material, capacity or permitted contents from the word alone.
Unlike a verb meaning "fill," バケツ only names the container; the action needs its own verb.
