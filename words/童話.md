# 童話 (どうわ) - children's story; fairy tale

## Meaning

童話 is a story for children, including fairy tales and other imaginative
stories. It need not contain literal fairies or come from anonymous folklore.

- 寝る前に子どもに童話を読みました。
  (ねるまえにこどもにどうわをよみました。) - I read a children's story to my child before bed.
- 彼女は動物が話す童話を書いています。
  (かのじょはどうぶつがはなすどうわをかいています。) - She writes children's stories in which animals talk.

童話を読む and 童話を書く describe reading and writing such stories.
Adults can read or write 童話; the word concerns the kind of story, not the reader's age alone.

## How the kanji combine

[童](../kanji/童.md) contributes children, and [話](../kanji/話.md)
a story or tale. The first element identifies the intended audience or kind
of the story. The on readings are ドウ + ワ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[物語](物語.md) (ものがたり) is a broader story or narrative, and
[話](話.md) (はなし) has a story sense alongside talk and other uses.
[神話](神話.md) (しんわ) is myth; it does not mean every story told to children.
There is no general antonym of 童話.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 童話 as "children's conversation" by combining the characters
mechanically. Nor does "fairy tale" require a happy ending, a particular length,
or a supernatural character in every example. Learn the established literary category.
