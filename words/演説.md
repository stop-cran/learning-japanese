# 演説 (えんぜつ) - speech; public address

## Meaning

演説 is a speech or address delivered to an audience. It is a noun and forms the intransitive verb 演説する, "give a speech." 演説をする is also possible; the audience or topic can be supplied separately.

- 市長が駅前で演説しました。 (しちょうがえきまえでえんぜつしました。) - The mayor gave a speech in front of the station.
- 平和についての演説を聞きました。 (へいわについてのえんぜつをききました。) - I listened to a speech about peace.

Here で gives the speaking location, while について gives the subject of the speech.

## How the kanji combine

[演](../kanji/演.md) contributes presenting or performing; [説](../kanji/説.md) contributes explaining or stating. Together they describe presenting ideas in speech. Learn the compound reading えんぜつ, with ぜつ rather than せつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[講演](講演.md) (こうえん) is a lecture or talk, particularly useful for an informative presentation. 演説 suits an address putting forward views, including a political speech. These are overlapping contexts, not an absolute division.

## Distinctive meaning

[話す](話す.md) (はなす) simply means "speak; talk." 演説 does not name every conversation, nor does it mean a person's faculty of speech. The focus is an address to listeners, rather than an exchange of remarks between conversational partners.
