# がっかり (がっかり) - disappointed; let down

## Meaning

がっかり describes disappointment or feeling let down. Use がっかりする as a verb.
The disappointing result can take に, or a preceding clause can explain what happened.

- 試合の結果にがっかりしました。 (しあいのけっかにがっかりしました。) - I was disappointed with the match result.
- 店が閉まっていて、がっかりした。 (みせがしまっていて、がっかりした。) - I was disappointed to find the shop closed.

がっかりした gives a past reaction; がっかりしている describes someone being in that disappointed state.
Before a noun, がっかりした顔 (がっかりしたかお) means "a disappointed face."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[残念](残念.md) (ざんねん) expresses regret or disappointment, but its grammar differs:
残念です versus がっかりしました.
[失望](失望.md) (しつぼう) also names disappointment or loss of hope.
[しょんぼり](しょんぼり.md) emphasizes a downcast state or manner; the words can describe the same reaction.
Pleasure at a good result is a contextual opposite, not another meaning of がっかり.

## Distinctive meaning

The expression is useful when reality falls short of an expectation: a hoped-for result did not happen,
or an anticipated outing cannot proceed. It reports the person's reaction, not an objective judgment
that the whole situation is hopeless. がっかりさせる means "disappoint someone," a different construction
from personally feeling disappointed.
The dictionary also records feeling drained or exhausted, beyond the main disappointment sense taught here.
Keep the small っ; the recorded spelling ガッカリ is not a separate loanword.
