# 跳ねる (はねる) - jump; leap; splash

## Meaning

跳ねる means to jump, hop, or leap. It also describes liquid splashing or
spattering. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: the jumping or splashing thing
is the subject.

- 魚が水面で跳ねました。 (さかながすいめんではねました。) - A fish leaped at the surface of the water.
- フライパンの油が跳ねています。 (フライパンのあぶらがはねています。) - Oil is spattering from the frying pan.

Forms include 跳ねます (はねます), 跳ねて (はねて), and
跳ねない (はねない). Do not conjugate it as a godan verb.

## How the kanji combine

跳 writes は, and ねる is okurigana. This is a native verb, not an
on-reading compound. The character contributes a jumping association;
it does not predict every extended use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飛ぶ](飛ぶ.md) (とぶ) includes flying and, especially with the spelling
跳ぶ, jumping. [飛び込む](飛び込む.md) (とびこむ) specifies going into
something by a leap or rush; 跳ねる does not itself specify that destination.
There is no single opposite for jumping and splashing.

## Distinctive meaning

Other recorded uses include something bursting or popping and a performance
or day's business finishing: 芝居が跳ねる (しばいがはねる),
"the show ends." That last use overlaps [終わる](終わる.md) (おわる);
it is not a general replacement in every finishing expression.
In Go, ハネる can name the hane move.

Do not merge this entry with 撥ねる, also はねる, which includes hitting
or rejecting something, or 刎ねる, "behead." Shared pronunciation does not
make their objects and meanings interchangeable.
