# ドア (ドア) — door

## Meaning

ドア means **door**, commonly a door on a building, room, car, or train.

- ドアを閉めてください。 (どあをしめてください。) — Please close the door.
- 電車のドアが開きました。 (でんしゃのどあがあきました。) — The train doors opened.

With 閉める (しめる), the door takes を because someone closes it. With 開く (あく), が marks the door that opens.

## How the kanji combine

ドア is an English loanword written in katakana. There are no kanji components. The standard spelling here is ドア, without a final long-vowel mark.

## Synonyms and antonyms

戸 (と) and 扉 (とびら) also mean kinds of doors or door panels. 窓 (まど), “window,” is a different opening, not an opposite. 開く and 閉まる describe opposite changes of state.

## Distinctive meaning

The learner gloss “Western-style door” is a helpful contrast with traditional partitions, not an absolute rule that a ドア must swing on hinges. Train doors and automatic sliding doors are also naturally called ドア.
