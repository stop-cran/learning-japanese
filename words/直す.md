# 直す (なおす) — repair; correct

## Meaning

直す means to repair something or put it right, including correcting a mistake.
It is transitive: を marks what someone fixes.
The polite form is 直します (なおします), and the past is 直した (なおした).

- 時計を直しました。 (とけいをなおしました。) — I repaired the clock.
- 作文の間違いを直してください。 (さくぶんのまちがいをなおしてください。) — Please correct the mistakes in the composition.

The object can be a physical device or something that needs correction; the English translation changes with it.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) carries なお-, with す completing this native verb.
Its putting-right sense connects the two examples.
Do not replace なおす with the on reading チョク when reading this spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

直る (なおる) is the intransitive partner: 時計が直りました (とけいがなおりました) means "The clock is fixed."
That is a change of focus, not an opposite meaning.
治す (なおす) shares the pronunciation but means cure or heal; illnesses and wounds call for attention to this different spelling.

## Distinctive meaning

直す puts the repairer or corrector behind an action on something.
直る reports that the thing becomes right or functional, without itself identifying who fixed it.
Compare を直す and が直る rather than memorizing both as an undifferentiated English "fix."
This article covers the standalone verb; suffix uses in longer verbs are a separate construction.
