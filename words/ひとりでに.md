# ひとりでに - by itself; automatically

## Meaning

ひとりでに means by itself, of its own accord, or automatically:
the event happens without someone deliberately bringing it about in
the situation described.

- ドアがひとりでに開きました。 (ドアがひとりでにあきました。) - The door opened by itself.
- 傷はひとりでに治りました。 (きずはひとりでになおりました。) - The wound healed on its own.

The speaker does not have to know the mechanism behind the event.
The word does not assert that it literally has no cause.

## How the kanji combine

This adverb is usually written in kana.
独りでに and 一人でに are recognized dictionary spellings.
Learn the complete adverb: its meaning is not obtained by treating
every use of 一人, one person, as an automatic process.
The source has a blank written-form field and the reading ひとりでに.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[自然](自然.md) (しぜん) in 自然に or 自然と can overlap in spontaneous
or natural occurrence. [自動](自動.md) (じどう) concerns automatic operation,
but a naturally healing wound need not involve an automatic machine.
おのずから also means naturally or of itself; distinguish its reading
from みずから, which concerns oneself acting.

## Distinctive meaning

一人で作りました (ひとりでつくりました), "I made it alone," can describe
deliberate work without help. ひとりでにできました, "It came about by itself,"
does not simply praise the same person for working unaided.
勝手に (かってに) can sometimes mean by itself too, but its "without permission"
uses are not a general definition of ひとりでに.
