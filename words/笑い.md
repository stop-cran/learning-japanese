# 笑い (わらい) - laughter; smile

## Meaning

笑い is a laugh, laughter, or a smile. It is a noun: **笑いが起こる** (わらいがおこる) means laughter breaks out, while 笑いを浮かべる describes showing a smile.

- 彼の話に、みんなの笑いが止まりませんでした。 (かれのはなしに、みんなのわらいがとまりませんでした。) - No one could stop laughing at his story.
- 先生は笑いを浮かべてうなずきました。 (せんせいはわらいをうかべてうなずきました。) - The teacher nodded with a smile.

The first example concerns laughter; the second can describe a facial expression without an audible laugh.

## How the kanji combine

[笑](../kanji/笑.md) contributes laughing or smiling. 笑い is the noun form associated with [笑う](笑う.md) (わらう), with い as its written ending. It does not conjugate like the verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[微笑む](微笑む.md) (ほほえむ) is a verb meaning to smile. [笑顔](笑顔.md) (えがお) names a smiling face. [泣く](泣く.md) (なく), "cry," supplies a contextual contrast, though people can laugh and cry together.

## Distinctive meaning

笑い does not itself explain what prompted the reaction or whether it was friendly. The surrounding words distinguish amusement, a smile, and other attitudes. Do not use the noun as though it were the plain verb: "I laughed" is 笑った, not 笑い by itself.
