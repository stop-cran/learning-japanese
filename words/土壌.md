# 土壌 (どじょう) — soil; ground for growth

## Meaning

土壌 is soil considered as the ground in which plants grow. The same word can also describe conditions in which something develops.

- 畑の土壌を調べた。 (はたけのどじょうをしらべた。) — We examined the soil in the field.
- この町には新しい音楽が育つ土壌がある。 (このまちにはあたらしいおんがくがそだつどじょうがある。) — This town has the right conditions for new music to flourish.

## How the kanji combine

[土](../kanji/土.md) and [壌](../kanji/壌.md) both point to earth or soil. The second example keeps the idea of ground for growth without referring to physical dirt.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[土](土.md) (つち) is the ordinary word for earth or dirt. [土地](土地.md) (とち) can mean a piece of land: buying a plot is different from studying its soil.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the surroundings to choose the translation. A farmer may literally improve the soil; music growing in a town points instead to a favorable environment.
