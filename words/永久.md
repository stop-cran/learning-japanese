# 永久 (えいきゅう) - permanence; eternity

## Meaning

永久 expresses permanence or an unending duration. It functions as a noun and adjectival word. Learn 永久に, "forever; permanently," and 永久の, "eternal; permanent," before a noun.

- この思い出は永久に残るでしょう。 (このおもいではえいきゅうにのこるでしょう。) - This memory will probably remain forever.
- この資料は永久に保存します。 (このしりょうはえいきゅうにほぞんします。) - We will preserve this material permanently.

The first sentence expresses the speaker's expectation; the second states a preservation policy or intention.

## How the kanji combine

永 contributes enduring or lasting, and 久 contributes a long time. The two related ideas reinforce lasting duration. Their on readings combine as エイ + キュウ; keep both long vowels in えいきゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[永遠](永遠.md) (えいえん) also means eternity and overlaps substantially, especially in talk about lasting memories or feelings. [長期](長期.md) (ちょうき) means a long period, without saying it has no endpoint. 一時的 (いちじてき), "temporary," is a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

永久 is stronger than simply "for a long time." It can appear in technical descriptions, policies, promises, and emotional statements. In a promise or product description, the word does not by itself establish that something is physically indestructible: context determines what is meant to continue. It is not a general noun for an immortal person.
