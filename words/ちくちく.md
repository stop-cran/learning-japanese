# ちくちく — prickling and stinging

## Meaning

Repeated little pricks or a scratchy, stinging sensation, as from something touching the skin.

- このセーターは首がちくちくする。 — This sweater feels prickly around my neck.
- 指（ゆび）の小さな傷（きず）がちくちく痛む。 — The little cut on my finger stings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ひりひり](ひりひり.md) often suggests a raw or burning sting, while [ぴりぴり](ぴりぴり.md) can describe a sharp tingle. Their boundaries are subjective, not diagnostic categories.

## Distinctive meaning

ちくちくする and ちくちく痛む（いたむ）describe the feeling. The expression need not mean that an actual needle is present. Figuratively, ちくちく嫌味（いやみ）を言う means making repeated needling remarks.
