# 話 (はなし) — talk; story

## Meaning

話 can mean a conversation, what someone says, or a story. It is a noun. 話をする means having a talk or talking about something; 話を聞く means listening to someone or their account.

- 先生の話を聞きました。 (せんせいのはなしをききました。) — I listened to what the teacher said.
- 子供に短い話をしました。 (こどもにみじかいはなしをしました。) — I told the child a short story.

## How the kanji combine

[話](../kanji/話.md) carries the whole noun reading はなし without okurigana. The related verb 話す (はなす) writes す outside the kanji. Do not mechanically write the noun as the verb's ます-stem.

## Synonyms and antonyms

会話 (かいわ) emphasizes conversation between people. 物語 (ものがたり) emphasizes a narrative. 声 (こえ) is the voice as sound rather than the content of the talk.

## Distinctive meaning

話 does not by itself establish that an account is fictional. It can be a real report, casual conversation, or invented story. Use the surrounding context to choose the English translation.
