# ぶくぶく — bubbling and foaming; swelling

## Meaning

Bubbles rising or bursting in liquid, often with a gurgling sound. It can also describe swelling or a bulky, bloated appearance.

- ストローで水に息を吹く（ふく）と、泡（あわ）がぶくぶく出た。 — When I blew into the water through a straw, bubbles rose up.
- 排水口（はいすいこう）から水がぶくぶくと湧き上がった（わきあがった）。 — Water bubbled up from the drain.
- 水を吸った豆がぶくぶく膨らんだ。 — The beans swelled up as they absorbed water.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic repeating ぶく, with a voiced initial consonant. Compare the sound shape of [ぷくぷく](ぷくぷく.md), without treating voicing as an exact bubble-size rule.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぷくぷく](ぷくぷく.md) also concerns bubbles and rounded swelling. [ぐつぐつ](ぐつぐつ.md) especially evokes cooking liquid. [もこもこ](もこもこ.md) describes bulky fluff or bulges without implying bubbles.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ぶくぶくと泡が出る, ぶくぶく泡立つ（あわだつ）, or ぶくぶく膨らむ（ふくらむ）. With a person's weight or shape, ぶくぶく太る can be insulting. The swelling sense also extends to baggy clothing; do not translate it as “bubbling” regardless of the object.
