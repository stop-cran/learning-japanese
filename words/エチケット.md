# エチケット (エチケット) - etiquette; good manners

## Meaning

エチケット is etiquette, courtesy or good manners: considerate behavior
and conventions appropriate to a social situation. It is a noun.

- 食事のエチケットを学びました。 (しょくじのエチケットをまなびました。) - I learned dining etiquette.
- 公共の場所ではエチケットを守りましょう。 (こうきょうのばしょではエチケットをまもりましょう。) - Let's observe good manners in public places.

エチケットを守る (エチケットをまもる) is a useful pattern,
"observe etiquette." JMdict identifies French *étiquette* as the source
of this courtesy sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[礼儀](礼儀.md) (れいぎ) also means manners or courtesy.
[作法](作法.md) (さほう) can emphasize the proper way of behaving,
and [行儀](行儀.md) (ぎょうぎ) concerns behavior or manners.
[丁寧](丁寧.md) (ていねい) describes being polite or careful; its
politeness sense overlaps, but it is a na-adjective rather than an
automatic grammatical substitute for this noun.

## Distinctive meaning

Etiquette depends on the situation and community; using this word does
not establish one universal rule for every country or setting.
It also does not by itself mean a law or a compulsory technical
regulation. Check what behavior is being discussed.
