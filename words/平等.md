# 平等 (びょうどう) — equality

## Meaning

平等 concerns equality, including equal treatment or an even distribution.
It can be a noun or a な-adjective: 平等な扱い (びょうどうなあつかい), "equal treatment."
Use 平等に to describe doing something equally.

- 子供たちにお菓子を平等に分けました。 (こどもたちにおかしをびょうどうにわけました。) — I divided the sweets equally among the children.
- 私たちは平等を求めています。 (わたしたちはびょうどうをもとめています。) — We are seeking equality.

## How the kanji combine

[平](../kanji/平.md) contributes the idea of evenness, and [等](../kanji/等.md) contributes equality.
The related meanings reinforce each other.
Learn the on-reading compound as びょうどう, including the voiced どう; it is not へいとう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

公平 (こうへい), "fairness; impartiality," emphasizes being fair or unbiased.
It overlaps with 平等, but equal shares and a fair decision are not automatically the same question.
The words do not impose a universal philosophical rule about what is fair.

不平等 (ふびょうどう), "inequality; unfairness," is the direct negative counterpart.
[同じ](同じ.md) おなじ, "same," is broader: the same color or the same book does not by itself express equal treatment.

## Distinctive meaning

平等 does not say that people or objects are identical in every respect.
In the sweets example, the distribution is equal; the children can still differ in age or preferences.
Identify what is being made equal instead of translating the word as "everything is the same."
