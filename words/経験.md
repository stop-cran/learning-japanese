# 経験 (けいけん) - experience

## Meaning

経験 is experience gained by actually doing or undergoing something.
経験する is a transitive する verb: the event experienced takes を.
経験がある means "have experience."

- 日本で働いた経験があります。 (にほんではたらいたけいけんがあります。) - I have experience working in Japan.
- 初めて地震を経験しました。 (はじめてじしんをけいけんしました。) - I experienced an earthquake for the first time.

## How the kanji combine

[経](../kanji/経.md) contributes passing through; [験](../kanji/験.md) relates to
testing or verification. Together they suggest what one has personally been
through. The on-readings ケイ + ケン give けいけん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

体験 (たいけん) also emphasizes firsthand experience, often a particular
encounter or hands-on activity. 知識 (ちしき), "knowledge," need not come from
doing something yourself. These overlap in practice, rather than forming opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

経験 can describe a single event or accumulated background, including unpleasant
events. It does not necessarily mean expert skill. To specify a field, use
の: 仕事の経験 (しごとのけいけん), "work experience." A preceding clause,
as in 働いた経験, needs no extra の.
