# 値段 (ねだん) — price

## Meaning

値段 is the price of something: the amount asked or paid for it.
Use 商品の値段 (しょうひんのねだん), "the price of a product," or ask 値段はいくらですか,
"How much is it?"
Prices are 高い たかい, "high," or 安い やすい, "low; inexpensive."

- このかばんの値段はいくらですか。 (このかばんのねだんはいくらですか。) — What is the price of this bag?
- 去年より値段が高くなりました。 (きょねんよりねだんがたかくなりました。) — The price has gone up since last year.

## How the kanji combine

[値](../kanji/値.md) supplies price or value; [段](../kanji/段.md) has a grade or step sense.
"Price level" can serve as a learning association, but the established whole word simply means price.

The reading is mixed: native ね + on ダン. Following the repository's native-first mixed-word convention,
it is filed under `wago`; this does not mean that both components have kun readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

料金 りょうきん names a fee, charge, or fare, making it useful for the amount charged for a service.
価値 かち is value or worth, which need not be a sum of money.
Neither is a mechanical substitute for 値段 in every phrase.

## Distinctive meaning

A high 値段 tells you that something costs a lot, not that it is valuable to you or well made.
Use 値段 for the price question and 価値 when the point is worth or merit.
There is no general opposite noun; the contrast is between different prices.
