# 中学 (ちゅうがく) - junior high school; middle school

## Meaning

中学 is a noun for junior high school or lower secondary school, shortened from **中学校** (ちゅうがっこう). It can describe the institution or that stage of education.

- 弟は来年、中学に入ります。 (おとうとはらいねん、ちゅうがくにはいります。) - My younger brother will enter junior high school next year.
- 中学の時、テニスを始めました。 (ちゅうがくのとき、テニスをはじめました。) - I started playing tennis when I was in junior high school.

中学一年生 (ちゅうがくいちねんせい) means a first-year junior high school student. Here the year is within that school stage.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md), "middle," qualifies [学](../kanji/学.md), "learning; school." チュウ + ガク gives ちゅうがく. The shortened form omits 校 from 中学校; do not carry the full form's がっこう ending into 中学.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中学校](中学校.md) is the full name. [高校](高校.md) (こうこう) is high school, the later educational stage in the Japanese system. [学校](学校.md) (がっこう) is the broader word "school."

## Distinctive meaning

English "middle school" covers different grade ranges in different countries. 中学 identifies the Japanese lower-secondary stage when that system is under discussion; a translation does not guarantee that a foreign institution has identical ages or grades. Context determines whether the speaker means the building, school, or period of life.
