# 申す (もうす) - to say humbly

## Meaning

申す is a humble or deferential counterpart of 言う (いう), "say." It is particularly useful for introducing oneself formally: name + と申します means "my name is ..."

- 田中と申します。 (たなかともうします。) - My name is Tanaka.
- もう一度申します。 (もういちどもうします。) - I will say it once more.

It is a godan verb ending in す: 申します, 申さない, 申して. The quoted name or words take と, not を.

## How the kanji combine

[申](../kanji/申.md) carries a saying or stating meaning and is read もう here. The written す is part of the native verb, whose polite form is もうします.

## Synonyms and antonyms

言います is neutral polite speech. [おっしゃる](おっしゃる.md) is respectful toward the person whose words are reported. 申す is not the respectful choice for elevating that person.

## Distinctive meaning

と申します is not a passive form meaning that someone else performs a naming action. It is a conventional deferential way to state one's name. In everyday introductions among peers, といいます is also natural; the extra formality of 申します is not required in every conversation.
