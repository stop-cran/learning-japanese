# 池 (いけ) - pond

## Meaning

池 means a pond: a body of standing water, such as one in a garden or park. It is an ordinary noun.

- 公園の池に魚がいます。 (こうえんのいけにさかながいます。) - There are fish in the park's pond.

Here に marks where the fish are. 池の周り (いけのまわり) means "around the pond"; の connects the pond to its surroundings.

## How the kanji combine

池 is a single kanji representing the native word いけ. Its water component provides a useful visual association. This word has no kana ending to conjugate.

## Synonyms and antonyms

湖 (みずうみ) means a lake, generally a larger body of water. 川 (かわ) is a river, with flowing water. These are useful contrasts, not direct antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

池 does not itself say whether the pond formed naturally or was made by people. A garden pond is still a 池; do not limit the word to wild landscapes.
