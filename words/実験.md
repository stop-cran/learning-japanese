# 実験 (じっけん) - experiment

## Meaning

実験 is an experiment: trying something under conditions that let you observe
what happens or check an idea. Use 実験する or 実験をする for "conduct an experiment."

- 理科の授業で実験をしました。 (りかのじゅぎょうでじっけんをしました。) - We did an experiment in science class.
- 実験の結果をノートに書きました。 (じっけんのけっかをノートにかきました。) - I wrote the experiment's results in my notebook.

実験の + noun links the experiment to its results, method, or purpose.
In the first example, で identifies the setting in which it took place.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality or practical reality, and
[験](../kanji/験.md) contributes testing or verification. Together they suggest
checking through an actual trial. The reading is ジッケン, with a small っ,
not じつけん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[試験](試験.md) (しけん) is a test or examination. It can overlap with 実験
when equipment is being tested. [経験](経験.md) (けいけん) is experience: something one undergoes
or learns through doing, not necessarily a planned experiment.

## Distinctive meaning

An 実験 can have an unexpected result and still provide useful information.
The word itself does not imply [成功](成功.md) (せいこう), "success."
Learn 実験をする as the basic action pattern rather than treating every
occurrence of "try" in English as an experiment.
