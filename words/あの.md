# あの (あの) - um; excuse me

## Meaning

あの is a hesitation or attention-getting expression: "um," "well," or a tentative "excuse me." Speakers use it while finding their words or approaching someone.

- あの、駅はどこですか。 (あの、えきはどこですか。) - Excuse me, where is the station?
- あの、ちょっと聞いてもいいですか。 (あの、ちょっときいてもいいですか。) - Um, may I ask you something?

## How the kanji combine

This interjection is written in kana. A drawn-out pronunciation may be written あのう or あのー. It is not a kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ええと often fills a pause while thinking. すみません is a more explicit way to get attention or apologize. Neither has exactly the same conversational role.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse the interjection with あの before a noun, meaning "that ... over there": あの店 (あのみせ), "that shop." The hesitation stands outside the sentence's grammar and commonly has a pause after it. It does not itself express an apology or carry the full politeness of すみません.
