# 容易 (ようい) - easy; readily done

## Meaning

容易 is a na-adjective and noun expressing ease. It describes something that can be done without much difficulty. Use **容易な + noun**, **容易に + verb**, and **容易だ / 容易です**.

- この問題を解くのは容易ではありません。 (このもんだいをとくのはよういではありません。) - Solving this problem is not easy.
- この情報は容易に手に入ります。 (このじょうほうはよういにてにはいります。) - This information is readily available.

The negative 容易ではない denies ease; it does not necessarily mean something is impossible.

## How the kanji combine

[容](../kanji/容.md) has senses involving accommodating or allowing, and [易](../kanji/易.md) contributes ease. In this established compound, 易 provides the clearest clue to the overall meaning. Do not interpret 容 as an object's physical appearance in these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[簡単](簡単.md) (かんたん), "simple; easy," overlaps. A procedure may have few steps but still be hard to perform, so structural simplicity and ease are related rather than identical ideas. [難しい](難しい.md) (むずかしい), "difficult," supplies the practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

容易に focuses on how readily an action or result is possible. It is not the noun [用意](用意.md), also read ようい, meaning preparation. The kanji and construction separate 容易にできる (よういにできる), "can be done easily," from 用意する (よういする), "prepare."
