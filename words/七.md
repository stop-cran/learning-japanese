# 七 (なな) — seven

## Meaning

七 means seven. Both なな and しち are important readings; which one is used depends on the expression.

番号は七です。（ばんごうはななです。） — The number is seven.

七月に旅行します。（しちがつにりょこうします。） — I will travel in July.

## How the kanji combine

[七](../kanji/七.md) is one numeral. なな is its native reading, while しち is its on reading. This page uses なな as its primary reading but teaches both.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The Arabic numeral 7 represents the same number. 六（ろく）, six, and 八（はち）, eight, are neighboring numbers, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not choose a reading freely in every compound: 七月 is normally しちがつ, and 七つ, "seven things," is ななつ. 七日 is なのか for the seventh day of the month or a period of seven days. Learn counters and calendar expressions as whole forms.
