# 仲直り (なかなおり) - reconciliation; making up

## Meaning

仲直り means making up or repairing relations after a quarrel
or falling-out. It is a noun and forms the intransitive verb
仲直りする. と can mark the person one makes up with.

- けんかした友達と仲直りしました。 (けんかしたともだちとなかなおりしました。) - I made up with the friend I had quarreled with.
- 二人はもう仲直りしています。 (ふたりはもうなかなおりしています。) - The two have already made up.

仲直りをする is also possible. The word concerns restoring
relations, not simply meeting someone or being introduced.

## How the kanji combine

[仲](../kanji/仲.md) contributes relations between people;
[直](../kanji/直.md) contributes putting right in 直り
(なおり), related to [直る](直る.md).
The native elements なか + なおり suggest relations becoming
right again, rather than a physical object being repaired.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仲](仲.md) (なか) can name the relationship itself.
[親しい](親しい.md) (したしい) describes being close;
it does not require a preceding quarrel.
[喧嘩](喧嘩.md) (けんか), a quarrel or fight, provides a
useful contrast with making up afterward.

## Distinctive meaning

仲直り reports reconciliation, not a particular legal settlement
or a guarantee that every disagreement has disappeared.
Saying sorry and making up can occur together, but one apology
alone does not necessarily establish 仲直りした.
