# ぼんやり (ぼんやり) - vaguely; absentmindedly

## Meaning

ぼんやり describes something indistinct or a person acting absentmindedly or idly.
It can modify a verb directly or with と, and **ぼんやりする** describes a state of vagueness or inattention.

- 霧の中に山がぼんやり見えます。 (きりのなかにやまがぼんやりみえます。) - A mountain is faintly visible in the mist.
- ぼんやりしていて、駅で降りるのを忘れました。 (ぼんやりしていて、えきでおりるのをわすれました。) - I was distracted and forgot to get off at the station.

ぼんやりと空を眺める (ぼんやりとそらをながめる) means to gaze idly at the sky.
A した form can modify a noun: ぼんやりした記憶 (ぼんやりしたきおく), "a vague memory."

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana mimetic expression, representing a state rather than requiring a literal sound source.
There is no kanji combination to decode.
Learn the stable form ぼんやり and its constructions instead of inventing meanings for its separate syllables.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[はっきり](はっきり.md), "clearly/distinctly," contrasts with the visual or mental vagueness.
[集中](集中.md) (しゅうちゅう), "concentration," contrasts with inattention in suitable contexts.
[怠ける](怠ける.md) (なまける) judges laziness or neglect; ぼんやり does not always make that judgment.

## Distinctive meaning

Ask whether the sentence describes the perceived object or the person's attention.
A mountain can look indistinct even when the observer is concentrating carefully.
Conversely, a person can be absentminded while the view is perfectly clear.
Do not turn these descriptive uses into an unrestricted insult about someone's intelligence.
