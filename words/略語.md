# 略語 (りゃくご) - abbreviation; acronym

## Meaning

略語 is an abbreviation or shortened word or expression. It is a noun for
the resulting form, not the verb abbreviate.

- スマホはスマートフォンの略語です。 (スマホはスマートフォンのりゃくごです。) - Sumaho is a shortened form of the Japanese word for smartphone.
- この文章には略語が多いです。 (このぶんしょうにはりゃくごがおおいです。) - There are many abbreviations in this text.

In XはYの略語です, X is the short form and Y is the fuller expression.
The first example demonstrates shortening within a word, not selecting
the first letter of every word.

## How the kanji combine

[略](../kanji/略.md) contributes shortening or omission; [語](../kanji/語.md)
contributes word or expression. The first character qualifies the second,
giving a shortened expression. The on readings are リャク + ゴ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

略す (りゃくす) is the transitive godan verb abbreviate, shorten or omit.
[省く](省く.md) (はぶく) also concerns omission or saving effort, rather than
naming an abbreviated word. A full expression contrasts with its short
form; there is no need to treat every word with fewer syllables as a 略語.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary gloss acronym is one possibility, not a rule that every
略語 must be formed from initials. Learn what the short form stands for
and where it is understood. Calling a form a 略語 does not by itself
establish that it is informal, new or suitable for every audience.
