# ノー - no; without

## Meaning

ノー is an interjection meaning "no." It can express a negative answer or refusal. It also appears as a prefix in established expressions meaning absence, lack, or prohibition; the compound determines the precise interpretation.

- 答えはイエスですか、ノーですか。 (こたえはイエスですか、ノーですか。) - Is the answer yes or no?
- 彼はノーネクタイで会議に出ました。 (かれはノーネクタイでかいぎにでました。) - He attended the meeting without a tie.

In the first example, the response word is quoted as an answer category. In the second, ノー is part of ノーネクタイ, not a separate spoken refusal.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword has no kanji decomposition. The ー marks the long オ vowel. Do not confuse ノー with the grammatical particle の.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いいえ](いいえ.md) is a Japanese negative response. イエス is the contrasting loanword "yes"; [はい](はい.md) is an affirmative response whose interpretation depends on the question and situation.

## Distinctive meaning

ノー is not a general replacement for the negative ending ない, nor does its prefix use authorize arbitrary new compounds. ノーコメント means "no comment," for example. The source list supplied no English gloss; these interjection and prefix uses are dictionary-supported teaching choices, not a recovered account of what its compiler intended.
