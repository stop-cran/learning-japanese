# 警察 (けいさつ) - police

## Meaning

警察 means the police as an organization or the police involved in a situation. Context can also make it refer to a police officer or police station.

警察に電話しました。 (けいさつにでんわしました。) - I called the police.

警察が来ました。 (けいさつがきました。) - The police came.

It is a noun; it does not conjugate like a verb meaning "police an area."

## How the kanji combine

[警](../kanji/警.md) concerns warning or vigilance, and [察](../kanji/察.md) concerns observing or examining. Together their on readings form けいさつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

警察官 (けいさつかん) explicitly means a police officer. 警察署 (けいさつしょ) is a police station, while 交番 (こうばん) is a local police box.

## Distinctive meaning

English "the police" looks plural, but Japanese 警察 does not encode a number of officers. If the distinction between the organization, a person, and a building matters, use the more specific noun instead of relying on context. There is no natural single antonym.
