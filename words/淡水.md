# 淡水 (たんすい) - fresh water; nonsaline water

## Meaning

淡水 is fresh water: water containing very little salt, rather than seawater.
Here "fresh" concerns salinity, not how recently the water was obtained.

- この湖には淡水魚がいます。
  (このみずうみにはたんすいぎょがいます。) - Freshwater fish live in this lake.
- この植物は淡水で育ちます。
  (このしょくぶつはたんすいでそだちます。) - This plant grows in fresh water.

淡水の湖 (たんすいのみずうみ) means "a freshwater lake."
淡水魚 is たんすいぎょ; the fish character is voiced to ぎょ here.

## How the kanji combine

淡 contributes faintness or low concentration and
[水](../kanji/水.md) water. Together the established compound concerns
water with little salt, not necessarily pale-colored water.
The on readings are タン + スイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

真水 (まみず) also means fresh water.
海水 (かいすい), seawater, and 塩水 (しおみず), salt water or brine,
provide useful contrasts. [水](水.md) (みず) is the broader everyday word.

## Distinctive meaning

淡水 does not guarantee that water is clean or safe to drink.
飲料水 (いんりょうすい) specifically concerns drinking water.
Likewise, a water supply being interrupted is [断水](断水.md)
(だんすい), with a voiced initial だ and a different meaning.
