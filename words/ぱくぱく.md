# ぱくぱく — mouth opening and closing; eating heartily

## Meaning

A mouth repeatedly opening and closing, or someone readily eating mouthful after mouthful.

- 金魚（きんぎょ）が口をぱくぱくさせている。 — The goldfish is opening and closing its mouth.
- 子どもたちはカレーをぱくぱく食べた。 — The children ate the curry heartily.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もぐもぐ](もぐもぐ.md) brings chewing into focus; [むしゃむしゃ](むしゃむしゃ.md) suggests hearty munching. These are overlapping descriptions, not separate stages that every meal must follow.

## Distinctive meaning

口をぱくぱくさせる does not necessarily involve food: someone can gasp or try to speak. The dictionary also records things flapping open and shut, such as a loose shoe sole. Use the subject and verb to find the scene.
