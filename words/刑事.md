# 刑事 (けいじ) - police detective; criminal matters

## Meaning

刑事 has two related uses: a noun for a police detective, and "criminal" in legal expressions concerning criminal matters.

- 刑事が目撃者に話を聞いています。 (けいじがもくげきしゃにはなしをきいています。) - A detective is talking to a witness.
- これは刑事事件です。 (これはけいじじけんです。) - This is a criminal case.

In the first example, 刑事 is a person. In 刑事事件, it qualifies 事件 (じけん), "case/incident"; it does not mean "a detective's incident." Another useful combination is 刑事裁判 (けいじさいばん), "criminal trial."

## How the kanji combine

刑 contributes criminal punishment, and [事](../kanji/事.md) contributes a matter or affair. The legal meaning is therefore a helpful guide to the compound. The on readings are ケイ + ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[警官](警官.md) (けいかん) means a police officer, a broader role than detective. 探偵 (たんてい) can refer to a detective such as a private investigator. For legal matters, 民事 (みんじ), "civil," contrasts with 刑事; it is not an opposite kind of police officer.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the surrounding noun and sentence role to separate the two senses. 刑事が来た (けいじがきた) means a detective came. 刑事事件 concerns criminal law. Neither use means "criminal" as a noun naming the person who committed an offense.
