# 当日 (とうじつ) - day in question; day of the event

## Meaning

当日 is the particular day being discussed, often the day an event takes place.
It is a noun that can also locate an action in time without に.

- 試験当日は、九時までに来てください。
  (しけんとうじつは、くじまでにきてください。) - On the day of the exam, please arrive by nine.
- チケットは当日でも買えます。
  (チケットはとうじつでもかえます。) - You can buy tickets on the day itself, too.

当日の予定 (とうじつのよてい) is the schedule for that day.
The event or previous discussion supplies which day is meant.

## How the kanji combine

[当](../kanji/当.md) points to the relevant or specified thing here;
[日](../kanji/日.md) is the day. The on readings are トウ + ジツ.
This compound identifies the day in question, not a day being physically struck.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[今日](今日.md) (きょう) is today, relative to the time of speaking.
[当時](当時.md) (とうじ) concerns the time or period in question, not necessarily one day.
前日 (ぜんじつ), the preceding day, gives a useful event-relative contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

当日 can refer to a future event or a past one. It does not automatically mean
"today," and the English "appointed day" does not require a personal appointment.
当日券 (とうじつけん) are tickets sold for use on the day itself.
