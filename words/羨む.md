# 羨む (うらやむ) - envy; be envious of

## Meaning

羨む is a transitive godan verb meaning "envy; be envious of."
The person, ability or situation envied takes を. Useful forms are
羨みます, 羨んだ and 羨まない.

- 彼女の才能を羨んでいます。 (かのじょのさいのうをうらやんでいます。) - I envy her talent.
- 他人の成功を羨むだけでは何も変わりません。 (たにんのせいこうをうらやむだけではなにもかわりません。) - Nothing will change if you only envy other people's success.

## How the kanji combine

羨 writes the native element うらや, followed by the verb ending む.
There is no multi-kanji compound. The related adjective is
[羨ましい](羨ましい.md) (うらやましい).

## Synonyms and antonyms

Compare 彼女の才能を羨む with 彼女の才能が羨ましい:
both can express envy of her talent, but the verb and adjective use
different constructions. [憎む](憎む.md) (にくむ), "hate," is a different
attitude; envy does not necessarily involve hatred.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb identifies wishing for someone else's advantage or good situation.
It does not by itself say that the speaker intends to deprive that person
of it. Learn the full reading うらやむ, distinct from [恨む](恨む.md) (うらむ),
"resent; hold a grudge."
