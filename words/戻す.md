# 戻す (もどす) - put back; restore

## Meaning

戻す is a transitive godan verb meaning to put something back or restore it to an earlier place or state. The thing restored takes を, and the destination or resulting state can take に.

- 読んだ本を棚に戻しました。 (よんだほんをたなにもどしました。) - I put the book I had read back on the shelf.
- 設定を元に戻してください。 (せっていをもとにもどしてください。) - Please restore the settings to how they were.

Forms include 戻します, 戻した, and 戻さない. 元に戻す (もとにもどす) is useful for restoring an earlier condition, not just physically moving an object.

## How the kanji combine

[戻](../kanji/戻.md) supplies returning or restoring. In this native verb it is read もど, followed by す. The same stem appears in [戻る](戻る.md) (もどる), the intransitive partner.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[返す](返す.md) (かえす) overlaps with giving something back, especially to its owner or source. 戻す emphasizes a previous location or condition, but the two are not mutually exclusive. [出す](出す.md) (だす), "take out," can contrast with putting something back.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 本を戻す, "put the book back," with 本が戻る, "the book returns." 戻す names an action performed on something; 戻る names its return. Simply going home yourself normally calls for an intransitive verb, not 家に戻す with no object. Other dictionary senses exist, but this card focuses on restoration and return.
