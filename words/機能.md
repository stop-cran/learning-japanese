# 機能 (きのう) - function; feature

## Meaning

機能 names a function or capability of something, including a device, an organ,
or a system. 機能する is intransitive: a thing が機能する, "functions; works."
As a noun, a device can have several 機能, or features.

- このカメラには便利な機能があります。 (このカメラにはべんりなきのうがあります。) - This camera has a useful feature.
- ブレーキが正常に機能していません。 (ブレーキがせいじょうにきのうしていません。) - The brakes are not functioning properly.

The first sentence identifies a capability; the second describes whether a mechanism
is performing its job.

## How the kanji combine

[機](../kanji/機.md) suggests a mechanism, and [能](../kanji/能.md) contributes
ability. Together they provide a guide to what something can do or how it works.
The on readings are き + のう. The meaning is not limited to mechanical machines.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[役割](役割.md) (やくわり) is a role or assigned part.
[働く](働く.md) (はたらく) can describe working, but is broader and also
describes people doing work. A role and a function can overlap without having
identical grammar. A malfunction contrasts with successful functioning.

## Distinctive meaning

機能 does not tell you how impressive a feature is: it may be useful, unnecessary,
or broken. English "faculty" here means a capacity, not the teachers at a college.
Also distinguish きのう from [昨日](昨日.md) (きのう), "yesterday"; their written forms
and sentence roles separate them.
