# おだいじに (おだいじに) - take care of yourself; get well soon

## Meaning

おだいじに is a wish that someone take care of themselves or recover soon. It is useful when speaking to someone who is unwell or injured.

- A: 風邪をひいてしまいました。 (かぜをひいてしまいました。) - I've caught a cold.
- B: それは大変ですね。おだいじに。 (それはたいへんですね。おだいじに。) - That's too bad. Take care of yourself.

お大事に is the written form, also read おだいじに. The longer お大事になさってください (おだいじになさってください) makes the wish more explicit and respectful.

## How the kanji combine

The base [大事](大事.md) (だいじ) concerns importance or preciousness, with [大](../kanji/大.md) and [事](../kanji/事.md) read ダイ + ジ. The Japanese prefix お and ending に belong to the formula. The kango grouping concerns the base; this kana headword has no written kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[元気でね](元気でね.md) (げんきでね) wishes someone well at parting without implying illness. [いってらっしゃい](いってらっしゃい.md) sees someone off with an expected return. English "take care" overlaps across these expressions, but their situations differ.

## Distinctive meaning

This is not a general warning to watch out for immediate danger. It also does not announce that the speaker is healthy. Saying it to a healthy person simply because you are leaving can suggest an illness-related context that you did not intend.
