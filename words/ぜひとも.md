# ぜひとも - by all means; absolutely

## Meaning

ぜひとも strengthens a wish, invitation or request:
"by all means," "very much," or "absolutely."
It is an emphatic form of [ぜひ](ぜひ.md), not a verb.

- ぜひとも参加したいです。 (ぜひともさんかしたいです。) - I would very much like to participate.
- ぜひともお出かけください。 (ぜひともおでかけください。) - Please do come.

Place the adverb with the predicate expressing the wish or request.
The second example is an invitation, not proof that the speaker will reject a refusal.

## How the kanji combine

The spellings 是非とも and 是非共 are recorded for the same adverb.
是非 supplies ぜひ; とも strengthens the expression.
Recognize [非](../kanji/非.md) in 是非 and [共](../kanji/共.md)
in the alternative spelling, without translating this invitation as
an argument about "right and wrong together."
This article keeps kana as its primary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぜひ](ぜひ.md) expresses the underlying eagerness.
[どうしても](どうしても.md) can emphasize determination, but also has
negative inability uses that ぜひとも does not automatically share.
[必ず](必ず.md) (かならず) emphasizes certainty or fulfillment without fail.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "not taking no for an answer" gloss can suggest strong insistence,
but is not a rule about every use.
A courteous invitation can contain ぜひとも.
Nor is it a general weather-prediction adverb: eagerness for an event to happen
is different from evidence that it certainly will.
