# しゃっくり (しゃっくり) - hiccup

## Meaning

しゃっくり is a hiccup or hiccups. Useful patterns are **しゃっくりが出る** (しゃっくりがでる), "get the hiccups," and **しゃっくりが止まる** (しゃっくりがとまる), "the hiccups stop."

- 急にしゃっくりが出ました。
  (きゅうにしゃっくりがでました。) - I suddenly got the hiccups.
- しゃっくりがなかなか止まりません。
  (しゃっくりがなかなかとまりません。) - My hiccups will not stop.

JMdict also recognizes intransitive **しゃっくりする**, "hiccup." The が出る pattern is not a rule that する is forbidden.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. JMdict records 吃逆 and 噦 as rare kanji spellings; they are recognition information, not characters to substitute mechanically into the examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[咳](咳.md) (せき), a cough, and [くしゃみ](くしゃみ.md), a sneeze, are different bodily actions, not synonyms or antonyms. English "hiccough" is another spelling of "hiccup," not a different Japanese symptom.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the small っ in しゃっくり. This word names the bodily phenomenon; it does not itself express a cause, diagnosis, or treatment. English can use "a hiccup" figuratively for a minor setback, but this page does not establish that extension for Japanese しゃっくり.
