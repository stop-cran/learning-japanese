# 通じる (つうじる) - lead to; get across

## Meaning

通じる is an ichidan verb with several related constructions. A road can lead somewhere, and a meaning or intention can get across to someone.

- この道は駅に通じています。 (このみちはえきにつうじています。) - This road leads to the station.
- 私の日本語は相手に通じました。 (わたしのにほんごはあいてにつうじました。) - The other person understood my Japanese.

In the second example, the language is what gets across; 相手に identifies the listener. This is not the same structure as "I understand Japanese."

A person can also be well versed in a subject: 彼は法律に通じています (かれはほうりつにつうじています), "He is well versed in law." Here the person is the subject and the field takes に.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes passage or connection; じる supplies the verb ending associated with the on reading ツウ. The past is 通じた (つうじた), and the negative is 通じない (つうじない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通る](通る.md) (とおる) describes passing through. It is not a replacement for every communication use of 通じる.

[届く](届く.md) (とどく), "reach; arrive," overlaps in the idea of arrival but does not by itself say that words were understood.

## Distinctive meaning

There is also a transitive conveying use, as in 意を通じる (いをつうじる), "convey one's intentions." Keep this を construction separate from 意味が通じる (いみがつうじる), "the meaning gets across," and Nに通じている, "be knowledgeable about N." The source's bare English "understand" obscures these differences.
