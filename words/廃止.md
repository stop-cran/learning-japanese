# 廃止 (はいし) — abolition; discontinuation

## Meaning

廃止する means to abolish or discontinue something previously in effect, such as a rule, system, or service.

- 学校は来年から制服の制度を廃止する。 (がっこうはらいねんからせいふくのせいどをはいしする。) — The school will abolish its uniform policy starting next year.
- この路線は利用者が減ったため廃止された。 (このろせんはりようしゃがへったためはいしされた。) — The route was discontinued because the number of users had fallen.

## How the kanji combine

[廃](../kanji/廃.md) (はい) contributes ending or doing away with something; [止](../kanji/止.md) (し) means stop. In the active construction を marks what is discontinued.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中止](中止.md) (ちゅうし) can mean calling off a particular event. Canceling today’s bus trip need not mean discontinuing the route itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 制度を廃止する, “abolish the system,” with 言葉が廃れる (ことばがすたれる), “a word falls out of use.” The latter need not involve an official decision.
