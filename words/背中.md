# 背中 (せなか) — back of the body

## Meaning

背中 is the back of a person's body: the area you might touch, wash, or feel pain in.
It is a body-part noun, not the general location word "behind."

- 長く座っていたので、背中が痛いです。
  (ながくすわっていたので、せなかがいたいです。)
  — My back hurts because I was sitting for a long time.

## How the kanji combine

[背](../kanji/背.md), read せ, supplies "back," and [中](../kanji/中.md), read なか,
helps name the bodily area. Both are kun readings, so the compound is `wago`.
The established word means the back, not only a mathematically exact point in its middle.

## Synonyms and antonyms

背 (せ) can itself mean "back," but also means height or stature.
[後ろ](後ろ.md) (うしろ) is the useful location contrast:
家の後ろ (いえのうしろ) means "behind the house."
It does not name a bodily surface in the way 背中 does.

## Distinctive meaning

背中 is more specific than English "back." Use it for the body region in 背中が痛い,
not automatically for the back of a book or the space behind a building.
Likewise, 背が高い (せがたかい), "be tall," is a height expression with 背,
not a reason to translate 背中 as "height."
