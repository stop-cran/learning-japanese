# 破産 (はさん) - bankruptcy; insolvency

## Meaning

破産 is bankruptcy or insolvency. With する, it means to go bankrupt.
The verb is intransitive: the person or company going bankrupt takes が.
Use 破産の with a following noun, as in 破産の手続き (はさんのてつづき),
"bankruptcy proceedings."

- 借金を返せず、会社は破産しました。
  (しゃっきんをかえせず、かいしゃははさんしました。)
  - Unable to repay its debts, the company went bankrupt.
- 彼は破産の手続きについて相談しました。
  (かれははさんのてつづきについてそうだんしました。)
  - He sought advice about bankruptcy proceedings.

The term can concern an individual or a company; it is not restricted to personal bankruptcy.

## How the kanji combine

[破](../kanji/破.md) contributes breaking or ruin, and [産](../kanji/産.md) contributes
property or assets in this compound.
Together they suggest financial ruin. The on readings are ハ + サン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

倒産 (とうさん) concerns business failure and overlaps in some company contexts.
[失敗](失敗.md) (しっぱい), "failure," is much broader: an unsuccessful project does
not necessarily mean bankruptcy.

## Distinctive meaning

破産 is stronger than merely having little cash available today.
In legal or financial writing it may refer to a specific process or status, whose details
depend on the applicable rules. The vocabulary itself does not establish which debts are
affected or what procedure has been completed.
