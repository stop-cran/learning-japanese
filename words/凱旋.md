# 凱旋 (がいせん) — triumphant return

## Meaning

凱旋 is a **triumphant return** after victory or success. It is a noun
and forms the intransitive verb 凱旋する: the person or team returns.
Although associated with returning after victory in battle, it also
describes returns after sporting or performing success.

- 大会で優勝した選手が故国に凱旋した。
  (たいかいでゆうしょうしたせんしゅがここくにがいせんした。)
  — The athlete who won the championship returned to their homeland in triumph.
- 選手の凱旋を祝った。 (せんしゅのがいせんをいわった。)
  — We celebrated the athlete's triumphant return.

In the first example, the returning athlete is marked with が and the
destination with に. A destination can also take へ. 凱旋する is not a
verb for returning an object to its owner. In the second example,
凱旋 is a noun: the return is what people celebrated.

## How the kanji combine

[凱](../kanji/凱.md) contributes victory celebration or victory music;
旋 (せん) contributes returning **in this compound**. Together, they
describe a return characterized by triumph, not just an ordinary trip back.
The on-readings are ガイ + セン. This explanation does not make "return"
the only meaning of 旋 in other words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[帰る](帰る.md) (かえる), "return home," and [戻る](戻る.md)
(もどる), "go back," can describe the same movement without expressing
triumph. [帰宅](帰宅.md) (きたく), "returning home," likewise does not
imply success.

[優勝](優勝.md) (ゆうしょう), "championship victory," names the winning,
not the subsequent return. A person can win without having returned yet.
There is no need to treat every unsuccessful return as a fixed opposite
word of 凱旋.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 凱旋 when the return is presented as a success worth celebrating,
not merely for coming home after work. The word does not require that
the person won a championship: success in a performance can also
motivate a triumphant homecoming.

For recognition, 凱旋公演 (がいせんこうえん) is a performance given after
a successful tour abroad. This is not a label for every local concert
or every performance after a journey.
