# ボタン — button

## Meaning

ボタン means a clothing button or a button that you press to operate something. Use the accompanying action to distinguish the two.

- シャツのボタンを留めてください。 (シャツのボタンをとめてください。) — Please fasten the buttons on your shirt.
- このボタンを押すと、ドアが開きます。 (このボタンをおすと、ドアがあきます。) — Pressing this button opens the door.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is katakana. The clothing word comes from Portuguese botão. Rare kanji spellings such as 釦 exist, but are not needed for ordinary writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ファスナー is a zipper, a different clothing fastener. スイッチ is a switch and may be operated by a button, lever, or other mechanism; not every switch is physically a push-button.

## Distinctive meaning

For clothing, 留める (とめる) means fastening and 外す (はずす) unfastening. For a control, 押す (おす) means pressing. The homophone 牡丹 (ぼたん), "peony," is a different word and does not explain the borrowed button word.
