# きちんと - properly; neatly

## Meaning

きちんと is an adverb for doing something properly, accurately, or in an orderly way. It can describe meeting an expected standard or arranging things neatly. Place it before a verb, as in **きちんと説明する**, "explain properly."

- 名前をきちんと書いてください。 (なまえをきちんとかいてください。) - Please write your name properly.
- 靴をきちんと並べました。 (くつをきちんとならべました。) - I arranged the shoes neatly.

The exact standard comes from context: clear writing, a complete explanation, punctuality, or tidy arrangement. **きちんとしている** describes an orderly or proper state; **きちんとした服装** (きちんとしたふくそう) means "neat, appropriate clothing."

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana-written mimetic expression. It conveys a manner or state rather than naming a literal sound. The final と is already part of the expression; do not add a second と.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ちゃんと](ちゃんと.md) overlaps in "properly" or "as expected." [正確](正確.md)に (せいかくに) specifically emphasizes accuracy. [ざっと](ざっと.md), "roughly; cursorily," contrasts with careful completion in contexts such as checking a document.

## Distinctive meaning

きちんと does not simply mean "beautifully." A plain but orderly room can be きちんとしている. In a request, it can imply that the task should not be neglected or done carelessly, so tone and context matter when addressing someone.
