# 小さい (ちいさい) - small; little

## Meaning

小さい is an i-adjective meaning small in size. It goes directly before a noun, without な.

- 小さいかばんを使っています。 (ちいさいかばんをつかっています。) - I use a small bag.
- この靴は少し小さいです。 (このくつはすこしちいさいです。) - These shoes are a little small.

Its negative is 小さくない (ちいさくない); its past is 小さかった (ちいさかった). It can also describe low volume: 声が小さい (こえがちいさい), "have a quiet voice."

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) writes ちい, followed by さい in kana. The written kanji boundary is not the adjective's conjugation boundary: replace the final い when forming 小さくない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

大きい (おおきい), "big," is the main size contrast. 小さな (ちいさな) also modifies nouns, but cannot replace the predicate in この靴は小さい.

## Distinctive meaning

Small size is not the same as a small number. 少ない (すくない) describes a low quantity; 小さい describes size or degree. A small room can still contain many people.
