# 占う (うらなう) - tell fortunes; predict

## Meaning

占う is a transitive godan verb: tell someone's fortune or predict an
outcome. The person, fortune or outcome being considered takes を.
Useful forms are 占います, 占った and 占わない.

- カードで将来を占いました。 (カードでしょうらいをうらないました。) - I read the future with cards.
- この試合は優勝の行方を占う大切な一戦です。 (このしあいはゆうしょうのゆくえをうらなうたいせつないっせんです。) - This is an important match that will give an indication of who will win the championship.

The second example is prediction from an event, not necessarily divination.
占い (うらない) is the related noun "fortune-telling."

## How the kanji combine

[占](../kanji/占.md) writes the native portion うらな, followed by う.
There is no multi-kanji compound to divide. The same character also appears
in [占める](占める.md) (しめる), "occupy; account for," a different verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予想](予想.md) (よそう) and [予測](予測.md) (よそく) also concern
prediction. [予報](予報.md) (よほう) concerns a forecast. 占う has the
particular fortune-telling use, but its prediction sense is not limited
to supernatural methods.

## Distinctive meaning

占う describes attempting to read a future or outcome; it does not
guarantee accuracy. In commentary, a result can be said to 占う a later
development because it offers clues. Do not replace every weather
forecast or numerical estimate mechanically with this verb.
