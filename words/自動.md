# 自動 (じどう) - automatic operation

## Meaning

自動 is a noun used for automatic operation. It also modifies nouns with の and appears in compounds. **自動で + verb** means that something happens automatically, rather than requiring a person to perform each step.

- このドアは自動で開きます。 (このドアはじどうでひらきます。) - This door opens automatically.
- 設定を自動に変えました。 (せっていをじどうにかえました。) - I changed the setting to automatic.

自動ドア (じどうドア) means "automatic door." In the second example, に marks the setting selected as the result.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) contributes "self," and [動](../kanji/動.md) contributes "movement; operation." The combination describes operation proceeding on its own. This does not mean that a machine never needs a person to start or configure it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

手動 (しゅどう), "manual operation," is the direct contrast in equipment settings. [動く](動く.md) (うごく) merely says that something moves or operates; it does not specify whether the operation is automatic.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the actual patterns 自動で and 自動の, rather than importing English adjective grammar and assuming 自動な. The dictionary also records 自動 as a grammatical abbreviation for 自動詞 (じどうし), "intransitive verb." That specialized label is a different use from an automatic door and should be interpreted within its grammar context.
