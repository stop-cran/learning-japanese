# 豪華 (ごうか) - lavish; luxurious

## Meaning

豪華 is a な-adjective describing something lavish, luxurious, or splendid. Use **豪華な + noun**, **豪華です** as a predicate, and **豪華に + verb** for doing something lavishly.

- 豪華なホテルに泊まりました。 (ごうかなホテルにとまりました。) - We stayed at a luxurious hotel.
- 誕生日の食事はとても豪華でした。 (たんじょうびのしょくじはとてもごうかでした。) - The birthday meal was very lavish.

It can describe impressive furnishings, rich clothing, an elaborate meal, or an exceptional lineup of performers. The negative is 豪華ではない, "not lavish," rather than an い-adjective ending.

## How the kanji combine

豪 contributes grandeur or magnificence; 華 contributes splendor or brilliance. The two reinforce the impression of richness and display. Their on readings are ゴウ + カ. This component explanation is not a claim about the word's historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[贅沢](贅沢.md) (ぜいたく) emphasizes luxury or extravagance and can imply more than is necessary. [美しい](美しい.md) (うつくしい), "beautiful," does not require lavishness. A simple object can be beautiful without being 豪華.

## Distinctive meaning

豪華 does not merely mean "good" or "wonderful" in any context. It points to an impressive richness of appearance, provision, or personnel. It can be admiring, while the surrounding sentence may also criticize excessive display or spending.
