# 望み (のぞみ) - wish; hope; prospect

## Meaning

望み is a noun for a wish, desire, or hope. It can also mean a prospect of something happening. **望みがかなう** means "a wish comes true"; **望みがある** means "there is hope/a chance."

- 留学するという望みがかないました。 (りゅうがくするというのぞみがかないました。) - My wish to study abroad came true.
- まだ成功の望みがあります。 (まだせいこうののぞみがあります。) - There is still hope of success.

The first sentence identifies what someone wanted. The second evaluates the possibility of a favorable result.

## How the kanji combine

[望](../kanji/望.md) contributes hoping or desiring. The noun 望み relates to the verb [望む](望む.md) (のぞむ), "wish for; desire." The kana み completes the noun form, not a separate compound element.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[希望](希望.md) (きぼう) overlaps with "hope" and "wish." [願い](願い.md) (ねがい) can also name a wish or request. Their constructions differ: learn 望みがかなう and 成功の望み as complete patterns rather than substituting mechanically.

## Distinctive meaning

望みがない can mean that there is no prospect of the desired outcome; it need not mean the person has no wishes at all. Check whether the sentence concerns desire or likelihood. **望みを捨てる** (のぞみをすてる), "give up hope," describes the person's response, not proof that success is objectively impossible.
