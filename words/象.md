# 象 (ぞう) - elephant

## Meaning

象 is an elephant. It is a noun and does not itself indicate whether one animal or several are meant. Both 象 and the katakana spelling ゾウ occur; the reading here is ぞう.

- 動物園で象を見ました。 (どうぶつえんでぞうをみました。) - I saw an elephant at the zoo.
- 象が長い鼻で水を飲んでいます。 (ぞうがながいはなでみずをのんでいます。) - The elephant is drinking water using its long trunk.

In the second sentence, 鼻 (はな), normally "nose," refers to the elephant's trunk. で marks the means used.

## How the kanji combine

象 is a single-kanji word using the on reading ゾウ. There is no compound relationship to divide. Other words containing this character can concern images or phenomena; that does not change the animal meaning of the standalone noun here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," is a broader category, not a synonym specifically for elephant. ゾウ is a spelling alternative, not a different species or a contrasting meaning.

## Distinctive meaning

The kana form can be useful in animal labels and beginner reading, while 象 identifies the same animal in kanji. Neither the word nor its spelling says that the animal is male, adult, or a particular elephant species. Those details require additional words or context.
