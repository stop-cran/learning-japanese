# 集める (あつめる) — collect; gather together

## Meaning

集める means to collect or bring things or people together. It is a transitive ichidan verb: mark what you collect with を. Its forms include 集めます, 集めて, and 集めない.

- 古い切手を集めています。 (ふるいきってをあつめています。) — I collect old stamps.
- 先生がみんなの作文を集めました。 (せんせいがみんなのさくぶんをあつめました。) — The teacher collected everyone's compositions.

## How the kanji combine

[集](../kanji/集.md) expresses gathering. める completes the native verb あつめる; the ichidan conjugation base is あつめ-, not simply the part written in kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

集まる (あつまる) describes people or things gathering: 人が集まる (ひとがあつまる), "people gather." 集める instead presents someone as bringing them together. 配る (くばる), "distribute," is a useful contrasting action rather than an exact opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The collected items need not form a hobby collection. You can gather information, money, or participants, and 注目を集める (ちゅうもくをあつめる) means to attract attention.
