# 遠く (とおく) — far away; distant place

## Meaning

遠く can mean "far away" or a distant place. It is related to the く-form of the adjective 遠い, but also works as a place noun with particles.

- 遠くに山が見えます。 (とおくにやまがみえます。) — I can see mountains in the distance.
- ボールを遠くまで投げました。 (ボールをとおくまでなげました。) — I threw the ball a long way.

## How the kanji combine

[遠](../kanji/遠.md) contributes distance. 遠い (とおい) changes its final い to く. Keep the spelling とおく, with おお rather than おう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

近く (ちかく), "nearby," is the direct contrast. [遠い](遠い.md) directly describes a noun: 遠い町, "a distant town." 遠方 (えんぽう) is a more formal noun for a distant place.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun use allows 遠くの山 (とおくのやま), "mountains in the distance," and 遠くから (とおくから), "from far away." Do not attach 遠く directly to an ordinary noun without の.
