# 忘れ物 (わすれもの) — forgotten item; thing left behind

## Meaning

忘れ物 is something forgotten or left behind. It also occurs in 忘れ物をする, "leave something behind," where する describes the act rather than making an object.

- 電車に忘れ物をしました。 (でんしゃにわすれものをしました。) — I left something on the train.
- 忘れ物はありませんか。 (わすれものはありませんか。) — Have you left anything behind?

## How the kanji combine

[忘](../kanji/忘.md) appears in 忘れる (わすれる), "forget." Its ます-stem 忘れ joins [物](../kanji/物.md), もの, "thing." The compound uses native readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

落とし物 (おとしもの) is something dropped or lost. 忘れ物 emphasizes failing to take an item with you; 落とし物 emphasizes losing or dropping it. The categories can overlap at a lost-property counter.

## Distinctive meaning

A forgotten item is not necessarily permanently lost. Leaving your homework at home is also a 忘れ物 situation. The word does not mean forgetting an appointment or a fact; it normally concerns an item that should have been brought or taken along.
