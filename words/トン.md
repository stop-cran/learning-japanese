# トン (トン) - ton; metric tonne

## Meaning

トン names a ton, especially a metric tonne. **One metric トン is 1,000
kilograms**, not 1,000 tons. Put the number directly before the unit:
一トン (いっトン), "one tonne," and 二トン (にトン), "two tonnes."

- この荷物の重さは二トンです。 (このにもつのおもさはにトンです。) - This load weighs two tonnes.
- 一トンは千キログラムです。 (いっトンはせんキログラムです。) - One metric tonne is one thousand kilograms.

The second equation uses the metric meaning, not every historical unit
translated into English as "ton."

## How the kanji combine

The assigned teaching spelling is katakana. JMdict lists 屯, 噸, and 瓲
as rare ateji spellings for this unit, and marks it as usually written in
kana. Those characters are spelling alternatives here, not pieces of a
kanji-based numerical definition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

キログラム is another mass unit: one metric tonne equals 1,000 of those
units. It is not an antonym. English *tonne* makes the metric unit explicit,
whereas *ton* can name other units depending on the system.

## Distinctive meaning

The community source wrote 屯 and glossed it "1000 ton." That magnitude
is erroneous; this card preserves the lexical identity while using トン
and the supported metric conversion.
For a specification imported from another measurement system, verify which
ton is intended. The dictionary's "especially metric" qualification does
not justify treating every foreign ton as exactly 1,000 kilograms.
