# 気圧 (きあつ) - air pressure; atmosphere unit

## Meaning

気圧 is air or atmospheric pressure. It is a noun, used in weather reports and descriptions of pressure.

- 午後から気圧が下がりました。
  (ごごからきあつがさがりました。) - The air pressure fell in the afternoon.
- この計器で気圧を測ります。
  (このけいきできあつをはかります。) - We measure air pressure with this instrument.

気圧 also names the pressure unit "atmosphere": 一気圧 (いちきあつ), "one atmosphere." That is a measurement unit, not a description of a room's mood.

## How the kanji combine

[気](../kanji/気.md) contributes air, and [圧](../kanji/圧.md) pressure. Their on readings are キ + アツ. Keep the vowels of きあつ; this word is not pronounced きゃつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[圧力](圧力.md) (あつりょく) is pressure more generally, including figurative pressure. [大気](大気.md) (たいき) is the atmosphere or air itself; 気圧 is its pressure. [天気](天気.md) (てんき) is weather, not this particular measured quantity.

## Distinctive meaning

English "atmosphere" can mean surrounding air, a pressure unit, or a mood. 気圧 covers the pressure meaning; [雰囲気](雰囲気.md) (ふんいき) concerns mood or ambience. Do not translate every occurrence of "atmosphere" as 気圧.
