# 手袋 (てぶくろ) - gloves; mittens

## Meaning

手袋 means gloves or mittens. It can describe winter gloves, work gloves, or other coverings worn on the hands.

- 寒いので手袋をしました。 (さむいのでてぶくろをしました。) - I put on gloves because it was cold.
- 手袋を片方なくしました。 (てぶくろをかたほうなくしました。) - I lost one glove.

手袋をする and 手袋をはめる are useful ways to describe putting on or wearing gloves.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md) is て, "hand," and 袋 is ふくろ, "bag." In this native compound, ふ becomes voiced ぶ: て + ぶくろ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ミトン more specifically identifies mittens. 靴下 (くつした), "socks," is another clothing item, but it covers the feet rather than the hands.

## Distinctive meaning

手袋 does not itself say whether there is one glove or a pair. Context or expressions such as 片方, "one of a pair," supply that information. There is no useful direct antonym.
