# 礼 (れい) - thanks; bow

## Meaning

礼 can mean thanks or gratitude, and it can also name a bow.
For verbal thanks, use **礼を言う** (れいをいう).
For the bodily gesture, **礼をする** means to bow.
The prefixed form [お礼](御礼.md) (おれい, also written 御礼) is useful when expressing thanks.

- 手伝ってくれた友人にお礼を言いました。 (てつだってくれたゆうじんにおれいをいいました。) - I thanked the friend who helped me.
- 試合の前に相手に礼をしました。 (しあいのまえにあいてにれいをしました。) - I bowed to my opponent before the match.

Both examples mark the recipient with に, but 言う and する distinguish speaking thanks from making a bow.

## How the kanji combine

[礼](../kanji/礼.md) is a single character associated with respectful conduct or acknowledgment.
Its on reading here is レイ.
The thanks and bow senses are related uses, not separate readings that must be memorized for these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[感謝](感謝.md) (かんしゃ) names gratitude and forms 感謝する.
お辞儀 (おじぎ) specifically names bowing.
[礼儀](礼儀.md) (れいぎ) concerns manners or etiquette more generally.
None is an unrestricted substitute in every expression containing 礼.

## Distinctive meaning

A bow may express greeting or respect rather than thanks for a favor.
Conversely, saying お礼 does not necessarily describe a physical bow.
Interpret the accompanying verb and situation, rather than assuming all respectful gestures have the same purpose.
This card emphasizes practical thanks and bowing, not every specialized use of the character.
