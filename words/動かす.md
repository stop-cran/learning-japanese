# 動かす (うごかす) - move something; set in motion

## Meaning

動かす is a transitive godan verb: someone moves, shifts, or operates something. Mark what is moved with を. Its forms include 動かします, 動かして, and 動かした.

- 椅子を少し右に動かしてください。 (いすをすこしみぎにうごかしてください。) - Please move the chair a little to the right.
- この機械を動かすには電気が必要です。 (このきかいをうごかすにはでんきがひつようです。) - Electricity is needed to run this machine.

The second use means operating or setting a machine in motion, not merely carrying it somewhere.

## How the kanji combine

[動](../kanji/動.md) supplies the meaning "move." The native reading and ending make 動かす, not an on-reading compound. Learn it alongside the intransitive [動く](動く.md) (うごく).

## Synonyms and antonyms

Compare 車が動く (くるまがうごく), "the car moves," with 車を動かす (くるまをうごかす), "move the car." [移動](移動.md) (いどう) focuses on changing location; 動かす can also describe moving a finger or running machinery.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb also extends to feelings: 人の心を動かす (ひとのこころをうごかす) means "move people's hearts; influence them." Here the object is affected emotionally rather than physically displaced. The transitive pattern remains the same. English "move" can hide the difference between 動く and 動かす, so choose from who or what causes the motion.
