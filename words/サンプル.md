# サンプル - sample; specimen

## Meaning

サンプル is a sample, specimen, or example. It can be a small quantity offered for testing, an item examined in an investigation, or an example showing what something is like.

- この化粧品のサンプルをもらいました。 (このけしょうひんのサンプルをもらいました。) - I received a sample of this cosmetic product.
- 店の前に料理のサンプルが並んでいます。 (みせのまえにりょうりのサンプルがならんでいます。) - Food display models are lined up outside the restaurant.

The second example uses the dictionary's display-model sense, such as imitation food shown to customers. Such a model is not a portion to eat.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English sample, with no constituent kanji. Keep ン in サンプル; it is not a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

見本 (みほん) is a sample or model to show what something is like; 標本 (ひょうほん) is a specimen. [例](例.md) れい is an example more broadly. [典型](典型.md) てんけい emphasizes a typical example: a sample need not represent everything in its category perfectly.

## Distinctive meaning

サンプル names what is shown, tried, or examined. It does not by itself mean free of charge, scientifically representative, or edible. Learn the surrounding noun and purpose rather than assuming all samples are promotional giveaways.
