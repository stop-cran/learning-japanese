# など — and so on

## Meaning

など follows an example or list and means "and so on," "and the like," or "such as." It signals that the mentioned items are not necessarily the whole set.

店ではパンや果物などを売っています。 (みせではパンやくだものなどをうっています。) — The shop sells bread, fruit, and other things.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. It can also be written [等](../kanji/等.md), read など in this use, rather than the character's on reading トウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

や helps connect examples within an incomplete list. と more typically connects the specified items themselves. など can follow a single example as well as a や-list.

## Distinctive meaning

The particle showing the list's sentence role follows など: パンなどを買う (パンなどをかう), "buy bread and things like that." など can also downplay the preceding item, sometimes dismissively; not every occurrence is a neutral "etc." Learn its ordinary listing use first.
