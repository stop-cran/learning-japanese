# 強調 (きょうちょう) - emphasis; highlighting

## Meaning

強調 is a noun and transitive する verb meaning "emphasis" or "emphasize." Mark the point being emphasized with を. A speaker can emphasize an idea, and written presentation can make words stand out.

- 先生は復習の大切さを強調しました。 (せんせいはふくしゅうのたいせつさをきょうちょうしました。) - The teacher emphasized the importance of review.
- 重要な言葉を太字で強調しました。 (じゅうようなことばをふとじできょうちょうしました。) - I highlighted the important words in bold.

A quoted point can use と: 「安全が第一だ」と強調した (あんぜんがだいいちだときょうちょうした), "emphasized that safety comes first."

## How the kanji combine

[強](../kanji/強.md) means strong, and [調](../kanji/調.md) contributes tone or manner. A "strong tone" is a useful connection to giving something prominence. The word can describe visual emphasis too, so it does not require a loud voice.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[主張](主張.md) (しゅちょう) is asserting a position; 強調 concerns how prominently a point is presented. [重視](重視.md) (じゅうし) means attaching importance to something, which need not involve telling anyone about it. The ideas overlap without being identical.

## Distinctive meaning

To emphasize a claim is not to prove it. 強調 tells us that the speaker or writer draws attention to a point, not that the point is true. English "stress" here means emphasis, not psychological strain. Repetition, wording, and formatting can all supply the emphasis.
