# 先日 (せんじつ) - the other day

## Meaning

先日 refers to a recent past occasion: "the other day" or "a few days ago."
It works as a time noun and adverb. Place it before a past event, or use
先日の + noun for something from that occasion.

- 先日、駅で先生に会いました。 (せんじつ、えきでせんせいにあいました。) - I met my teacher at the station the other day.
- 先日の会議について質問があります。 (せんじつのかいぎについてしつもんがあります。) - I have a question about the meeting the other day.

It leaves the date unspecified; the shared situation may identify which
occasion the speaker means.

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md) contributes prior or preceding here, and
[日](../kanji/日.md) contributes day. The first qualifies the second: a prior
day. The on readings are セン + ジツ, not the reading にち for 日.

## Synonyms and antonyms

この間 (このあいだ) can also mean "the other day."
[昨日](昨日.md) (きのう) specifies yesterday, whereas 先日 does not identify
one fixed number of days back.

## Distinctive meaning

先日 is useful when referring back politely, for example 先日はありがとうございました
(せんじつはありがとうございました), "Thank you for the other day."
Although 先 can point ahead in other words, 先日 is not "a day in the future."
Use an exact date when the timing matters rather than expecting this word
to supply one.
