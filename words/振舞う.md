# 振舞う (ふるまう) - behave; treat to food or drink

## Meaning

振舞う is also written **振る舞う**, the full-okurigana spelling used
in the examples. It has two practical constructions.

For **behaving or conducting oneself**, it is intransitive;
a manner can be expressed with に.

- 彼は落ち着いて振る舞いました。 (かれはおちついてふるまいました。) - He behaved calmly.
- 客の前では丁寧に振る舞ってください。 (きゃくのまえではていねいにふるまってください。) - Please behave politely in front of the guests.

For **treating someone to food or drink**, it is transitive:
the recipient takes に, and what is served takes を.

- 客にお茶を振る舞いました。 (きゃくにおちゃをふるまいました。) - I treated the guests to tea.

Both uses are godan: 振る舞います (ふるまいます),
振る舞った (ふるまった), and 振る舞わない (ふるまわない).

## How the kanji combine

振 has waving or shaking associations, and
[舞](../kanji/舞.md) dancing or moving associations.
Learn the complete native verb ふるまう: the established meanings do not
require a literal dance. 振舞う and 振る舞う differ in written kana,
not pronunciation or conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[行動](行動.md) (こうどう) concerns action or behavior more broadly;
[行儀](行儀.md) (ぎょうぎ) concerns manners.
[ごちそう](ごちそう.md)する overlaps with treating someone to a meal.
The hospitality use is not the same as [扱う](扱う.md) (あつかう),
"treat; handle," in treating someone as a child.

## Distinctive meaning

Behaving politely does not necessarily mean offering refreshments,
and serving tea does not by itself evaluate someone's general behavior.
Read the participants and particles rather than assigning one English
"treat" or "conduct" gloss to every occurrence.
