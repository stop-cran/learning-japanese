# 確実 (かくじつ) - certain; reliable

## Meaning

確実 describes something definite, dependable, or securely established. It is a な-adjective: 確実な方法 (かくじつなほうほう), "a reliable method." The adverbial form 確実に can mean "certainly" or "reliably," depending on the action.

- この方法なら、確実に保存できます。 (このほうほうなら、かくじつにほぞんできます。) - With this method, you can save it reliably.
- 彼が来ることは確実です。 (かれがくることはかくじつです。) - It is certain that he will come.

The noun-like form occurs in expressions such as 確実さ (かくじつさ), "reliability/certainty."

## How the kanji combine

[確](../kanji/確.md) contributes firmness or certainty, and [実](../kanji/実.md) contributes reality or substance. The two reinforce the idea of something solidly grounded rather than merely hoped for.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[確か](確か.md) (たしか) overlaps when something is reliable or certain. As an adverb, however, 確か can introduce a recollection such as "if I remember correctly"; 確実に does not express that same hesitation. 不確実 (ふかくじつ), "uncertain," is a direct contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

確実 makes a strong claim about certainty or dependable results. [きっと](きっと.md) can express the speaker's confident expectation without establishing a guarantee. Likewise, [安全](安全.md) concerns safety; a reliable method and a safe method may coincide, but these words do not mean the same thing.
