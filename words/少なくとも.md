# 少なくとも (すくなくとも) - at least

## Meaning

少なくとも means "at least": it gives a lower estimate or the minimum that
can be said or expected. すくなくとも is the kana spelling of the same adverb.
Put it before the quantity or statement it qualifies.

- この作業には少なくとも二時間かかります。 (このさぎょうにはすくなくともにじかんかかります。) - This task will take at least two hours.
- 少なくとも、名前は覚えています。 (すくなくとも、なまえはおぼえています。) - At least I remember the name.

The first gives a minimum amount; the second limits the speaker's assurance
to something they do remember. The expression is not restricted to counting.

## How the kanji combine

[少](../kanji/少.md) appears in [少ない](少ない.md) (すくない), "few; little."
少なく is its adverbial form. Learn 少なくとも as the established lower-bound
expression, not as a statement that the final amount is necessarily small.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[最低](最低.md) (さいてい), especially 最低でも, overlaps when giving a minimum.
[以上](以上.md) (いじょう) follows a quantity: 二時間以上, "two hours or more."
[せめて](せめて.md) emphasizes a minimum wish or hope, so it is not a replacement in every
factual estimate.

## Distinctive meaning

せいぜい, "at most; at best," can set an upper limit instead. Do not reverse
these directions: 少なくとも二時間 allows the task to take longer than two
hours, not less. The adverb itself does not guarantee an exact final figure.
