# 紙屑 (かみくず) - wastepaper; paper scraps

## Meaning

紙屑 means wastepaper or scraps of paper. It is also written 紙くず, with the same reading and meaning.

- 床に紙屑が落ちています。 (ゆかにかみくずがおちています。) - There are paper scraps on the floor.
- 紙くずを拾って、ごみ箱に入れました。 (かみくずをひろって、ごみばこにいれました。) - I picked up the paper scraps and put them in the rubbish bin.

The two examples demonstrate the two spellings, not two different kinds of waste.

## How the kanji combine

[紙](../kanji/紙.md), read かみ, identifies paper, and 屑 (くず) contributes scraps or refuse. The first part specifies the material of the second. Both parts use native readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[塵](塵.md) (ごみ) is broader rubbish or garbage; it need not be paper. [紙](紙.md) (かみ) simply means paper and does not say that it is unwanted. [無駄](無駄.md) (むだ) concerns wastefulness or pointlessness, not specifically discarded paper.

## Distinctive meaning

A clean sheet of usable paper is not automatically 紙屑. Conversely, calling something 紙屑 does not tell you its local recycling category or collection day. Those practical rules come from the locality, not the word.
