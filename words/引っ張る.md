# 引っ張る (ひっぱる) - pull; tug

## Meaning

引っ張る is a transitive godan verb for pulling, tugging, or hauling something.
Use thing + を + 引っ張る. Its past form is 引っ張った (ひっぱった), and its
て-form is 引っ張って (ひっぱって).

- ロープを強く引っ張ってください。 (ロープをつよくひっぱってください。) - Please pull the rope hard.
- 子供が私の袖を引っ張りました。 (こどもがわたしのそでをひっぱりました。) - A child tugged at my sleeve.

The object can also be something towed: 車を引っ張る (くるまをひっぱる),
"pull/tow a car."

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) contributes pulling, and 張 contributes stretching or
drawing taut. The native compound joins these ideas as ひっぱる, with a
small っ and ぱ. Do not pronounce the written parts as two separate verbs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[引く](引く.md) (ひく) also means pull and has many additional uses.
[押す](押す.md) (おす), "push," is the opposite direction of force in the
physical pulling examples.

## Distinctive meaning

In baseball, 引っ張る also means to "pull" a batted ball toward the batter's
pull side: toward left field for a right-handed batter, right field for a
left-handed batter. That is a sports use, not physically tugging the ball.
Keep this context distinct from pulling a rope or someone's sleeve.
