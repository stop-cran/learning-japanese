# 引き出し (ひきだし) - drawer; cash withdrawal

## Meaning

引き出し commonly means a drawer. It also names a withdrawal of money from an account.

- 鍵は机の引き出しにあります。 (かぎはつくえのひきだしにあります。) - The key is in the desk drawer.
- 現金の引き出しにはカードが必要です。 (げんきんのひきだしにはカードがひつようです。) - A card is needed to withdraw cash.

It is a noun. The second example uses の to connect the money to the withdrawal.

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) contributes pulling and [出](../kanji/出.md) taking out. 引き出し is related to 引き出す (ひきだす), "pull out; withdraw," using its ます-stem as a noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

棚 (たな), "shelf," is different furniture storage. In banking, 預け入れ (あずけいれ), "deposit," contrasts with withdrawal.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "drawing out" is most useful here as withdrawing money, not a claim that every pulling action is ordinarily called 引き出し. Context distinguishes the movable furniture compartment from the financial transaction.
