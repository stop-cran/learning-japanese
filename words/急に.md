# 急に (きゅうに) - suddenly; rapidly

## Meaning

急に is an adverb meaning suddenly or abruptly. Depending on the event, it can also describe rapid change. It modifies the following predicate without another particle.

- 急に雨が降り出しました。 (きゅうにあめがふりだしました。) - It suddenly started raining.
- 川の水位が急に上がった。 (かわのすいいがきゅうにあがった。) - The river's water level rose rapidly.

In the second example, both the speed and abruptness of the rise may be relevant. Do not assume that English "quickly" and "suddenly" identify completely separate situations.

## How the kanji combine

[急](../kanji/急.md) contributes suddenness, haste, or steepness. に forms the adverbial expression from 急 (きゅう). Compare 急な変化 (きゅうなへんか), "a sudden change," with 急に変わる, "change suddenly."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[突然](突然.md) (とつぜん) also highlights a sudden occurrence. [ゆっくり](ゆっくり.md), "slowly," contrasts with rapid movement or change, while 徐々に (じょじょに), "gradually," contrasts with abruptness. These are contextual contrasts, not interchangeable opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

急に can also describe a sharp bend or steep change in slope, rather than a surprise. This card focuses on sudden events and rapid changes. The community source left its English gloss blank; the meanings here come from the dictionary, not from a recovered explanation by that compiler.
