# ピアノ (ピアノ) — piano

## Meaning

ピアノ is the musical instrument. The ordinary verb for playing it is 弾く (ひく), not する.

妹は毎日ピアノを弾きます。 (いもうとはまいにちピアノをひきます。) — My younger sister plays the piano every day.

子供のころ、ピアノを習っていました。 (こどものころ、ピアノをならっていました。) — I took piano lessons when I was a child.

## How the kanji combine

The loanword is written in katakana. JMdict identifies Italian as the source language. Pronounce ピ・ア・ノ as three morae; the modern spelling is ピアノ, not the older variant ピヤノ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

電子ピアノ (でんしピアノ) is a digital piano. キーボード can refer to an electronic keyboard instrument, though it also has nonmusical uses. 楽器 (がっき), "musical instrument," is the broader category.

## Distinctive meaning

ピアノを習う describes receiving piano instruction; ピアノを弾く describes playing. Neither expression says by itself that the player is a professional. In music terminology, ピアノ can also name the dynamic marking meaning "softly"; that is a separate use from the instrument taught here.
