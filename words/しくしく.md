# しくしく — sobbing softly; dull aching

## Meaning

しくしく describes quiet, sustained crying. In a separate bodily use, it describes a lingering dull or griping pain.

- 子供は布団の中でしくしく泣いていた。 — The child was sobbing softly under the bedding. (布団: ふとん.)
- 朝からお腹がしくしく痛む。 — I have had a dull ache in my stomach since this morning. (お腹: おなか; 痛む: いたむ.)

Use しくしく泣く, optionally with と, for crying. For pain, use しくしく痛む or お腹がしくしくする. The constructions are sense-specific.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[めそめそ](めそめそ.md) also describes tearful crying, but more readily conveys impatience or disapproval from the speaker. [むかむか](むかむか.md) describes nausea rather than the same kind of pain.

## Distinctive meaning

The pain sense does not mean that the stomach is literally sobbing. Nor does しくしく by itself diagnose the cause or seriousness of pain. The crying sense emphasizes quietness and persistence, not necessarily an attempt to manipulate someone.
