# 兆候 (ちょうこう) - sign; indication

## Meaning

兆候 is a sign or indication of a state or of something developing. It can also mean a symptom.
The sign need not be unfavorable: recovery can have 兆候 too.

- 回復の兆候が見られる。
  (かいふくのちょうこうがみられる。) - Signs of recovery can be seen.
- 今のところ、悪化の兆候はない。
  (いまのところ、あっかのちょうこうはない。) - So far, there are no signs of deterioration.

## How the kanji combine

[兆](../kanji/兆.md) contributes the idea of a sign or omen.
[候](../kanji/候.md) here participates in the related indication/condition meaning,
not a literal weather report. Treat 兆候 as a compound with related meanings reinforcing
the idea of an observable indication; 兆 is not the numerical unit "trillion" here.
Both characters use on readings: ちょう + こう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

兆し (きざし) also means a sign or indication. [現れ](現れ.md) (あらわれ)
is a manifestation or outward indication, while [印](印.md) (しるし) can mean a mark or sign.
Their broad English labels overlap, but that does not make every construction interchangeable.
[症状](症状.md) (しょうじょう) overlaps in the symptom sense.
[サイン](サイン.md) also has a sign or indication sense, alongside its signature and signal uses.

## Distinctive meaning

Use this word for an indication of a condition or development, rather than treating it as
a general name for any written symbol or signboard. It does not establish that the expected
event is certain to happen. Neither the kanji nor the English word "omen" makes 兆候
an inherently negative prediction.
