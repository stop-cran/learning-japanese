# 範囲 (はんい) - scope; range; extent

## Meaning

範囲 is the range or scope within which something applies, occurs, or is possible. It can concern a physical area, a set of topics, or the limits of an ability.

- 試験の範囲を確認しました。 (しけんのはんいをかくにんしました。) - I checked what the exam would cover.
- 分かる範囲で答えてください。 (わかるはんいでこたえてください。) - Please answer to the extent that you know.

範囲内 (はんいない) means "within the range," and 範囲外 (はんいがい) means "outside the range."

## How the kanji combine

範 contributes a model, standard, or bounds, and 囲 contributes enclosing or surrounding. Together they name a bounded extent. This component association is a memory aid, not an account of the word's historical formation. The on readings are ハン + イ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[限界](限界.md) (げんかい) focuses on a limit: the boundary beyond which something cannot go. 範囲 concerns the extent on one side of such boundaries. [対象](対象.md) (たいしょう) identifies what is targeted or covered rather than the scope itself.

## Distinctive meaning

範囲 need not be visible on a map. 試験の範囲 is the material covered, not the room where an exam takes place. In できる範囲で (できるはんいで), "as far as you can," the boundary is practical ability rather than distance.
