# くれる — to give to me or us

## Meaning

くれる presents giving toward the speaker or someone the speaker treats as on their side. The giver takes が, the gift を, and an explicit recipient に. The ordinary forms are くれます, くれない, くれて.

- 友達が本をくれました。 (ともだちがほんをくれました。) — A friend gave me a book.
- 兄が料理を作ってくれました。 (あにがりょうりをつくってくれました。) — My older brother cooked for me.

## How the kanji combine

Use kana in ordinary writing. The dictionary spelling 呉れる is rare; it is not necessary to write this basic giving verb in kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上げる](上げる.md), usually あげる for giving, presents giving outward. もらう means receiving: 私が本をもらった (わたしがほんをもらった), "I received a book." The grammatical subject changes.

## Distinctive meaning

After a verb's て-form, くれる presents an action as done for the speaker's benefit. It is not simply a polite ending. The direct imperative is irregular くれ, not くれろ; it is blunt. A polite request uses 〜てください or an appropriate request construction.
