# 生える (はえる) - sprout; grow

## Meaning

生える describes plants sprouting or growing, and hair or teeth coming through.
It is an intransitive ichidan verb: the thing that grows takes が.

- 庭に草が生えています。 (にわにくさがはえています。) - Grass is growing in the garden.
- 赤ちゃんに歯が生え始めました。 (あかちゃんにはがはえはじめました。) - The baby's teeth have started coming through.

Forms include 生えます, 生えて, 生えた and 生えない.
に identifies where the growth appears; 生えている can describe what is already growing there.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) contributes life or growth and writes は before える.
This is the native verb はえる, not the different 生きる (いきる), "live."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[育つ](育つ.md) (そだつ) concerns growing or being brought up.
[伸びる](伸びる.md) (のびる) can describe increasing length:
髪が生える (かみがはえる) concerns hair growing, while 髪が伸びる concerns its getting longer.
These growth descriptions can overlap, rather than dividing every stage into exclusive boxes.
[枯れる](枯れる.md) (かれる), "wither," contrasts in plant contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

English "cut teeth" here means that teeth emerge through the gums, not that
someone cuts them with a tool. 生える does not mean raising a child or deliberately
planting something. Keep the growing thing as the subject.
