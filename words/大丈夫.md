# 大丈夫 (だいじょうぶ) — all right; okay

## Meaning

大丈夫 means "all right; okay; safe" or "not a problem". What is okay depends on the situation: a person's condition, a proposed time, or the safety of something.

- 転んだけれど、大丈夫です。  
  ころんだけれど、だいじょうぶです。  
  "I fell, but I'm all right."
- 明日なら大丈夫です。  
  あしたならだいじょうぶです。  
  "Tomorrow works for me."

It is a na-adjective: 大丈夫です as a polite predicate, 大丈夫な + noun when directly modifying a noun.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) is read だい, 丈 じょう, and [夫](../kanji/夫.md) ぶ. Learn the whole established expression; the modern "okay" meaning is not obtained by translating the characters separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

問題ない (もんだいない), "no problem", overlaps in many settings. 危ない (あぶない), "dangerous", contrasts only the safety sense.

## Distinctive meaning

In response to an offer, 大丈夫です can mean "No thanks; I'm fine". Avoid treating it as an unambiguous yes. Adding いいえ or explaining what you need helps: いいえ、大丈夫です, "No, thank you".
