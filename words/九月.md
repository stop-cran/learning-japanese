# 九月 (くがつ) — September

## Meaning

九月 is September: 九月に新学期（しんがっき）が始まる (the new term begins in September in some countries), 九月九日 くがつここのか (September 9th).

## How the kanji combine

[九](../kanji/九.md) (nine) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month): the ninth month, read with the on reading ク, not キュウ: くがつ, not きゅうがつ.
"Nine months" is 九か月 きゅうかげつ, with キュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 長月 ながつき (old name of the ninth month).
- Related: [九日](九日.md) ここのか (the 9th).

## Distinctive meaning

Fixed words use ク (九月, 九時 くじ) and counts use キュウ (九人 きゅうにん). Both readings are standard, but each belongs to particular words.
