# 写生 (しゃせい) - sketching; depiction from life

## Meaning

写生 is sketching or depicting something from direct observation. It is a noun and a transitive する verb: **花を写生する** (はなをしゃせいする), "draw flowers from life."

- 公園で木を写生しました。
  (こうえんできをしゃせいしました。) - I sketched a tree from life in the park.
- 花瓶の花を見ながら写生しています。
  (かびんのはなをみながらしゃせいしています。) - I am drawing the flowers in the vase from observation.

The subject depicted takes を; で can mark where the activity takes place.

## How the kanji combine

[写](../kanji/写.md) contributes copying or depicting, and [生](../kanji/生.md) the living or actual subject. "Depict from life" is a meaning aid for the compound, not a rule that only living organisms can be depicted. Read シャ + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[描く](描く.md) (えがく / かく) is broader: draw or depict. [写す](写す.md) (うつす) concerns copying or reproducing and also photography; it is not a blanket substitute for every 写生する construction.

[絵](絵.md) (え) and [絵画](絵画.md) (かいが) can name a drawing or painting. 写生 foregrounds depicting an observed subject, not simply owning the resulting picture.

## Distinctive meaning

English "sketching" must not impose a pencil-only, rough-draft-only, or outdoor-only rule. Painting an observed subject indoors also fits. The public dictionary additionally applies 写生 to depicting what is observed in tanka, haiku, and prose; a literary use need not involve drawing a picture.

The source list's "drawing from nature" means observation of the subject, not that every subject must be an untouched natural landscape.
