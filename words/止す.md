# 止す (よす) - stop doing; give up

## Meaning

止す means to stop doing something, drop an idea, or decide not to proceed. It is a transitive godan verb, usually written よす. An activity can take を; a verb phrase can be turned into the object with のを.

- 冗談はよしてください。 (じょうだんはよしてください。) - Please stop joking.
- 雨なので、出かけるのはよそう。 (あめなので、でかけるのはよそう。) - Since it is raining, let's not go out.

Here は presents the activity as the topic in place of を. よそう is the volitional form, "let's stop/not do it." Other forms include よした, "stopped/dropped it," and よさない, "do not stop."

## How the kanji combine

[止](../kanji/止.md) writes the native stem よ, followed by す. The kanji expresses stopping, but the whole word must be read よす, not inferred from other verbs using 止.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[止める](止める.md), read やめる in the stopping-an-activity use, is close. [辞める](辞める.md) (やめる) specifically suits leaving a job or position. [続ける](続ける.md) (つづける), "continue," contrasts with abandoning an activity.

## Distinctive meaning

The outing example cancels an intended action before it starts; よす does not require that the action be underway. It is not a universal equivalent of "abolish" or "resign," despite broad English glosses in some lists. Learn the activity construction and the everyday request よしてください.
