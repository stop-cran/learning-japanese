# おめでたい (おめでたい) - auspicious; naive

## Meaning

おめでたい is an i-adjective for something worthy of celebration or auspicious. It can also describe someone as naive, too good-natured, or foolish; that use is ironic or critical, not a congratulation.

- それはおめでたい話ですね。 (それはおめでたいはなしですね。) - That's happy news.
- そんな話を信じるなんて、おめでたいね。 (そんなはなしをしんじるなんて、おめでたいね。) - You're naive to believe a story like that.

It goes directly before a noun: おめでたい話, not おめでたいな話. おめでたくない is the negative form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[おめでとう](おめでとう.md) is the expression "congratulations," not this adjective. [単純](単純.md) (たんじゅん) can overlap in describing a naive or simple-minded person; [馬鹿](馬鹿.md) (ばか) also has a foolishness meaning.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the adjective おめでたい from the noun おめでた, a happy event, especially marriage, pregnancy, or a birth. おめでたい does not itself mean the noun "pregnancy." Nor does a celebratory sense make the ironic personal description polite praise. [幸せ](幸せ.md) (しあわせ) describes happiness or good fortune more broadly.
