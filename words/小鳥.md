# 小鳥 (ことり) — small bird

## Meaning

小鳥 means a small or little bird. It describes size, not a particular species and not necessarily a baby bird.

- 庭で小鳥が鳴いています。 (にわでことりがないています。) — A little bird is singing in the garden.
- 木に小鳥が二羽います。 (きにことりがにわいます。) — There are two small birds in the tree.

Birds are normally counted with 羽 (わ), as in 二羽 (にわ). Use いる, rather than ある, for a living bird's presence.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) has the native combining reading こ, "small," and [鳥](../kanji/鳥.md) is とり, "bird." Together they form ことり, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

鳥 (とり) is the broader word. ひな refers to a young bird or chick, so it adds an age distinction that 小鳥 does not.

## Distinctive meaning

An adult sparrow can be a 小鳥. Do not infer that a bird is newly hatched simply because English translates the word as "little bird."
