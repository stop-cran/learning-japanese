# 茶色い (ちゃいろい) - brown

## Meaning

茶色い is an い-adjective meaning brown. It modifies a noun directly:
茶色い靴 (ちゃいろいくつ), "brown shoes."
The pinned dictionary glosses include light brown or tawny;
the public dictionary gives the broader brown-color description.

- 茶色い犬が庭にいます。 (ちゃいろいいぬがにわにいます。) - A brown dog is in the garden.
- 葉が茶色くなりました。 (はがちゃいろくなりました。) - The leaf turned brown.

The adverbial form before なる is 茶色く, without the final い.
The past adjective is 茶色かった (ちゃいろかった), and the
negative is 茶色くない (ちゃいろくない).

## How the kanji combine

[茶](../kanji/茶.md), tea, and [色](../kanji/色.md), color,
form [茶色](茶色.md) (ちゃいろ), the brown color noun.
Adding the adjective ending い gives 茶色い.
The base combines on チャ with native いろ; this on-first mixed
formation is grouped under kango here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

茶色の靴 and 茶色い靴 both mean "brown shoes."
Their grammar differs: noun 茶色 takes の, while the adjective
茶色い directly precedes the noun. ブラウン is a loanword
comparison for brown, often seen in color descriptions.

## Distinctive meaning

Say 茶色い犬 or 茶色の犬, not 茶色な犬.
The adjective does not establish a precise color value, nor does the
tea association mean that every kind of tea is brown.
