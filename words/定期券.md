# 定期券 (ていきけん) - commuter pass; season ticket

## Meaning

定期券 is a commuter or season pass, short for 定期乗車券 (ていきじょうしゃけん). In train and bus travel, it permits repeated journeys over a specified section during a fixed validity period.

- 通勤のために電車の定期券を買いました。
  (つうきんのためにでんしゃのていきけんをかいました。) - I bought a train commuter pass for traveling to work.
- この定期券は今月末まで有効です。
  (このていきけんはこんげつまつまでゆうこうです。) - This commuter pass is valid through the end of this month.

定期券を更新する (ていきけんをこうしんする) means "renew a commuter pass." The pass's actual route and validity must be checked, not inferred from the word alone.

## How the kanji combine

[定期](定期.md) combines [定](../kanji/定.md), fixing, with [期](../kanji/期.md), a period. This modifies [券](../kanji/券.md), a ticket. Read テイ + キ + ケン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

In transport conversation, 定期 can abbreviate the same pass. [切符](切符.md) (きっぷ) and [パス](パス.md) are broader ticket or pass words. [回数券](回数券.md) (かいすうけん) instead concerns an allotment of uses.

## Distinctive meaning

The defining contrast with 回数券 is a period-based pass versus a fixed number of uses, not paper versus an electronic card. Do not assume that every reusable travel card is a 定期券, or that a pass is valid on every route.
