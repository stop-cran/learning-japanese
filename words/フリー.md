# フリー - free; unrestricted; freelance

## Meaning

フリー can mean free or unrestricted, free of charge, or freelance.
The object and construction decide which meaning applies.
It can work as a noun or な-adjective; フリーの also modifies nouns,
as in フリーの記者 (フリーのきしゃ), "a freelance journalist."

- フリーの記者として働いています。 (フリーのきしゃとしてはたらいています。) - I work as a freelance journalist.
- このソフトはフリーです。 (このソフトはフリーです。) - This software is free of charge.

In the second sentence, フリー concerns the price in this context.
For available time, 午後はフリーです (ごごはフリーです) can mean
"I am free this afternoon," not that the afternoon has no price.

## How the kanji combine

フリー is a katakana loanword with a long final vowel.
There are no kanji to combine. It does not inherit every English
construction containing "free."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[自由](自由.md) (じゆう) concerns freedom; [無料](無料.md) (むりょう)
and [ただ](ただ.md) overlap in the no-charge sense.
[暇](暇.md) (ひま) and [空き](空き.md) (あき) concern unoccupied time.
These words are not interchangeable across all those meanings.

## Distinctive meaning

フリー can also abbreviate a freelancer, フリーサイズ (one-size clothing),
or the free program in figure skating. Context is essential for these
shortened uses. As a suffix, it can mean "free of" a substance, as in
カフェインフリー, caffeine-free.

No charge does not automatically grant every copyright or redistribution
permission, and freelance does not mean unpaid work. State the intended
sense rather than learning one English gloss as an unrestricted rule.
