# 教わる (おそわる) - be taught; learn from someone

## Meaning

教わる describes receiving instruction or learning something from someone. What is learned takes を; the person providing it can take に or から.

- 先生に漢字の書き方を教わりました。 (せんせいにかんじのかきかたをおそわりました。) - I learned how to write kanji from the teacher.
- 友達から駅への行き方を教わった。 (ともだちからえきへのいきかたをおそわった。) - A friend told me how to get to the station.

It is a transitive godan verb ending in る: 教わります (おそわります), 教わって (おそわって), 教わった (おそわった), 教わらない (おそわらない).

## How the kanji combine

[教](../kanji/教.md) supplies おそ, followed by わる. The same character has a different native reading in [教える](教える.md) (おしえる), "teach; tell." Do not copy that verb's ichidan conjugation onto 教わる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[習う](習う.md) (ならう) closely overlaps in learning through instruction or lessons. [学ぶ](学ぶ.md) (まなぶ) is broader and also describes learning from experience. 教わる can concern a particular explanation or piece of information, not only a continuing course.

教える presents the teacher's side: 先生が教える (せんせいがおしえる), "the teacher teaches," versus 先生に教わる (せんせいにおそわる), "learn from the teacher."

## Distinctive meaning

The English translation "be taught" looks passive, but 教わる is its own verb, not a passive conjugation of 教える. The learner is the subject, and the material learned is an を-marked object. A teacher can be a friend or colleague; professional status is not required.
