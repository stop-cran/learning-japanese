# むく -- peel; bare teeth

## Meaning

むく means removing an outer covering: peeling, skinning, paring,
hulling or stripping. 剥く, the source-list spelling, and 剝く are
recorded kanji spellings; both senses here are usually written in kana.

- りんごの皮をむきます。 (りんごのかわをむきます。) -- I peel an apple.

A further use is exposing teeth or opening the eyes wide:

- 犬が歯をむきました。 (いぬがはをむきました。) -- The dog bared its teeth.
- 目をむく (めをむく) -- open one's eyes wide.

This is a transitive godan verb: むきます, むかない, むいて and
むいた. The object or covering being removed takes を.

## How the kanji combine

In 剥く or 剝く, the character supplies む and く completes the verb.
The kana-primary card does not pretend the source has no kanji spelling.
Do not confuse it with homophonous 向く (むく), facing a direction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [はがす](はがす.md) overlaps in peeling off a covering, including bark; it is not restricted to adhesive labels.
- [削る](削る.md) (けずる) can mean paring or shaving away, with emphasis on cutting or reducing material.
- [取る](取る.md) (とる) and [除く](除く.md) (のぞく) cover removal more broadly.
- [向く](向く.md) has the same reading but a different meaning and dictionary identity.

## Distinctive meaning

Peeling a fruit and baring teeth share the idea of uncovering, but
目をむく does not mean peeling an eye. Learn the whole expression;
the presence of 剥 in another dictionary heading does not license
mixing its other readings with this むく entry.
