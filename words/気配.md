# 気配 (けはい) - sign; hint; market tone

## Meaning

気配 is a perceptible sign, hint, or sense of something that may not be clearly visible.
For example, a noise or atmosphere can give someone a sense that another person is nearby.

- 部屋には人の気配がありません。 (へやにはひとのけはいがありません。) - There is no sign of anyone in the room.
- 雨の気配を感じます。 (あめのけはいをかんじます。) - I sense signs of rain.

In finance, 気配 also means the tone or state of the market:
市場は強い気配を示しています。 (しじょうはつよいけはいをしめしています。) - The market is showing a firm tone.
This is not a separate word meaning every kind of trend.

## How the kanji combine

The spelling contains [気](../kanji/気.md) and [配](../kanji/配.md).
Daijisen identifies 気配 as an ateji spelling, characters applied to the word, and connects けはい with the older form けわい.
It also notes the spelling's influence on the pronunciation.
Do not derive the native word as an ordinary on-reading compound or interpret it literally as distributing 気.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[兆候](兆候.md) (ちょうこう) also names signs or indications.
[様子](様子.md) (ようす) can include outward signs as well as appearance or state.
[雰囲気](雰囲気.md) (ふんいき) concerns atmosphere and can overlap with a sense of presence.
[心配](心配.md) (しんぱい) is worry, not an unrestricted replacement for 気配.

## Distinctive meaning

気配 concerns what seems present or developing, not proof that it exists.
The source list's raw "worry" was not established as a meaning by the inspected entries; learn 心配 for worry instead.
JMdict also records きはい, marked rare; this card uses けはい.
The separate market word 気味 きあじ must not be transferred to the existing [気味](気味.md) きみ card.
