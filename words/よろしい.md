# よろしい (よろしい) — all right

## Meaning

よろしい is a formal or respectful way to say "good; all right; acceptable." It is common when checking permission or whether an arrangement suits someone.

ここに座ってもよろしいですか。（ここにすわってもよろしいですか。） — May I sit here?

明日でもよろしいですか。（あしたでもよろしいですか。） — Would tomorrow be all right?

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual; 宜しい is also recorded. This is an i-adjective, with forms such as よろしく and よろしければ, "if it is all right."

## Synonyms and antonyms

いい and よい are less formal alternatives. だめ means "not acceptable; not allowed" in a permission context, but it is not an appropriate opposite in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

Polite wording does not remove the effect of the situation: declaring よろしい, "very well," can sound like a superior granting approval. The question よろしいですか is useful for asking rather than imposing that judgment.
