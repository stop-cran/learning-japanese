# 編集 (へんしゅう) - editing; compilation

## Meaning

編集 is editing or compilation: selecting, arranging and preparing material for
a book, magazine, film or other finished work. 編集する is transitive; the material
being edited takes を.

- 旅行の動画を編集しました。 (りょこうのどうがをへんしゅうしました。) - I edited the travel video.
- 姉は雑誌の編集をしています。 (あねはざっしのへんしゅうをしています。) - My older sister works on editing a magazine.

## How the kanji combine

[編](../kanji/編.md) contributes arranging/compiling, and
[集](../kanji/集.md) gathering: ヘン + シュウ gives へんしゅう.
This is a compound association, not a claim that editing is only collecting material.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[編む](編む.md) (あむ) also means compiling or editing a book, besides knitting.
[修正](修正.md) (しゅうせい) is correction/revision; correcting an error can
be part of editing, but 編集 is not limited to error correction.
[社説](社説.md) (しゃせつ) is an editorial opinion article, not the editing process.

## Distinctive meaning

編集者 (へんしゅうしゃ) is an editor, and 編集委員会 (へんしゅういいんかい)
an editorial committee. The adjective-like English "editorial" there does not
turn 編集 into the opinion piece itself. 編修 and 編輯 are rare spellings to
recognize; use 編集 for ordinary production.
