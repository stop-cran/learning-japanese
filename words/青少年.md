# 青少年 (せいしょうねん) - young people; youth

## Meaning

青少年 means young people or the younger generation, including both boys and girls.
It is useful when discussing youth as a group, their activities, or their welfare.

- 青少年のための図書館を作りました。 (せいしょうねんのためのとしょかんをつくりました。) - We created a library for young people.
- この施設では青少年がスポーツを楽しめます。 (このしせつではせいしょうねんがスポーツをたのしめます。) - Young people can enjoy sports at this facility.

Nのための + noun means something intended for N. The word itself does not require an English plural ending.

## How the kanji combine

[青](../kanji/青.md) contributes youth here, [少](../kanji/少.md) being young,
and [年](../kanji/年.md) years or age. The established compound brings together
[青年](青年.md) (せいねん) and [少年](少年.md) (しょうねん), rather than naming a blue-colored child.
Read セイ + ショウ + ネン, keeping both long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

青年 concerns youth or a young person, often a young man. 少年 ordinarily means boy and
also has a broader juvenile sense. [少女](少女.md) (しょうじょ) focuses on girls;
[子供](子供.md) (こども), child, is a broader everyday comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer a male-only group from 少年 inside this compound. Nor is there one age
cutoff for every use of 青少年: ordinary descriptions and particular legal or organizational
categories need not coincide. The dictionary describes roughly ages 12-25 in its
general sense and under 18 in its youth-protection-ordinance context. These are
source definitions, not a universal current legal age range. Consult the relevant
definition when an exact age matters.
