# 台風 (たいふう) — typhoon

## Meaning

台風 means a typhoon, a type of tropical cyclone. It is not a general word for any windy day.

- 台風が近づいています。（たいふうが ちかづいています。）— A typhoon is approaching.
- 台風で学校が休みになりました。（たいふうで がっこうが やすみに なりました。）— School was closed because of the typhoon.

## How the kanji combine

The readings are [台](../kanji/台.md) タイ and [風](../kanji/風.md) フウ, both on readings. 風 gives a useful "wind" cue, but 台's ordinary "stand" meaning does not explain this word.

Learn 台風 as a fixed weather term. "Stand + wind" is neither its meaning nor a verified account of its origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 嵐 あらし means a storm more generally; it need not be a typhoon.
- 強風 きょうふう means strong wind and describes a condition rather than a particular kind of cyclone.
- There is no direct lexical opposite of 台風.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the final long vowel: たいふう, not たいふ. The full reading has four morae, た・い・ふ・う. The word commonly appears in weather reports and explanations of cancellations or delays. An ordinary storm and a typhoon should not be treated as interchangeable categories.
