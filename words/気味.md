# 気味 (きみ) - sensation; feeling

## Meaning

気味, read きみ, is a noun meaning a feeling or sensation. A useful expression is 気味が悪い (きみがわるい), "creepy; unsettling." It describes an uneasy impression rather than simply disliking a flavor.

- 誰もいないのに声がして、気味が悪かったです。 (だれもいないのにこえがして、きみがわるかったです。) - Hearing a voice when nobody was there felt creepy.

The related suffix 気味 is read **ぎみ**, not きみ. It attaches to nouns or verb stems to express a tendency or slight state.

- 最近、少し疲れ気味です。 (さいきん、すこしつかれぎみです。) - Lately I've been a little tired.

## How the kanji combine

[気](../kanji/気.md) suggests feeling or disposition; [味](../kanji/味.md) contributes a quality or flavor association. The compound concerns a felt quality, not necessarily a literal taste.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[気持ち](気持ち.md) (きもち) broadly describes feelings. [気分](気分.md) (きぶん) concerns mood or condition. Their expressions overlap, but replacing 気味 mechanically can change the meaning: 気分が悪い can mean feeling physically unwell.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list supplies きみ but gives a suffix-style English gloss. This card preserves the supported noun reading and distinguishes the separately recorded suffix -ぎみ. The compiler's historical intent remains uncertain, not the readings themselves. The suffix contrast is not an additional N3 assignment.
