# 直通 (ちょくつう) - direct connection; through service

## Meaning

直通 is a direct connection or through service to a destination or person.
直通する is intransitive: what connects directly takes が,
and the destination can take に.

- この電車は空港に直通しています。
  (このでんしゃはくうこうにちょくつうしています。) - This train runs through to the airport.
- こちらは担当者への直通番号です。
  (こちらはたんとうしゃへのちょくつうばんごうです。) - This is the direct number for the person in charge.

直通の電車 (ちょくつうのでんしゃ) describes a through train.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) contributes directness, and
[通](../kanji/通.md) passing through or connecting.
The on readings are チョク + ツウ.
The compound concerns the connection, not simply high speed.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[直接](直接.md) (ちょくせつ) and [直に](直に.md) (じかに)
express direct access more broadly.
[通信](通信.md) (つうしん) names communication or transmission;
[連絡](連絡.md) (れんらく) includes contact and connection.
直通 specifies the direct connection rather than every act of communicating.

## Distinctive meaning

A through train can stop at intermediate stations.
直通 does not invariably mean a nonstop journey or an express service.
[急行](急行.md) (きゅうこう) and [特急](特急.md) (とっきゅう)
are different service labels.
Likewise, [経由](経由.md) (けいゆ), via, can identify a place passed
without requiring a change of vehicles. Check the actual route and stops.
