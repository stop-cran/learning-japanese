# 人類 (じんるい) - humankind; humanity

## Meaning

人類 names humankind collectively. It is a noun, useful when discussing human history, the future of humanity, or problems affecting people as a whole. 人類のN means "humanity's N" or "N of humankind."

- 人類の歴史について学んでいます。 (じんるいのれきしについてまなんでいます。) - I am learning about the history of humankind.
- 気候の変化は人類全体に関わる問題です。 (きこうのへんかはじんるいぜんたいにかかわるもんだいです。) - Climate change is an issue affecting humanity as a whole.

The collective scope comes from the word, not from adding a Japanese plural ending.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) contributes "human; person," and [類](../kanji/類.md) contributes "kind; category." Together they identify the human kind collectively. The on readings are ジン + ルイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[人間](人間.md) (にんげん) can mean a human being or humans generally. [人](人.md) (ひと) is useful for an individual person. 人類 directs attention to humankind as a whole, rather than the particular people in a room.

## Distinctive meaning

English "humanity" can also mean kindness or compassion. 人類 does not carry that character-trait meaning: praising someone's humanity does not normally mean praising their 人類. Likewise, it is not the ordinary word for a local population; [住民](住民.md) (じゅうみん) identifies residents of a place.
