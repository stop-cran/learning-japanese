# 散る (ちる) - fall like blossoms; scatter

## Meaning

散る describes blossoms or leaves falling, or things scattering and dispersing.
It is an intransitive godan verb: what falls or scatters takes が.

- 桜の花が散り始めました。
  (さくらのはながちりはじめました。) - The cherry blossoms have begun to fall.
- 割れたガラスが床に散りました。
  (われたガラスがゆかにちりました。) - The broken glass scattered over the floor.

Forms include 散ります, 散った, 散って, and 散らない.
Despite ending in る, this is not an ichidan verb: the て-form is ちって.

## How the kanji combine

[散](../kanji/散.md) supplies ち and the scattering association; る is okurigana.
This is the complete native verb, not a shortened form of 散らかる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[散らす](散らす.md) (ちらす) is transitive: 風が花を散らす,
"the wind scatters the blossoms."
[落ちる](落ちる.md) (おちる) broadly means fall or drop;
散る is especially useful for blossoms and for dispersal.
[散らかる](散らかる.md) (ちらかる) focuses on a scattered or untidy state.

## Distinctive meaning

Falling blossoms need not make a place untidy.
[散らかす](散らかす.md) (ちらかす) names causing scattered disorder.
[広がる](広がる.md) (ひろがる), "spread," is broader about expansion.
Additional dictionary senses include clouds dispersing or disappearing, and ink
spreading or blurring. Compare [消える](消える.md) (きえる), "disappear,"
and [解散](解散.md) (かいさん), a gathering's dispersal.
These extensions do not make 散る the default verb for every object falling.
