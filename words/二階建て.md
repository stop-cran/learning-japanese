# 二階建て (にかいだて) — two-story building

## Meaning

二階建て means a building with two stories. It can stand as a noun or describe a building with の: 二階建ての家 (にかいだてのいえ), "a two-story house".

- この家は二階建てです。  
  このいえはにかいだてです。  
  "This house has two stories."
- 二階建ての家に住んでいます。  
  にかいだてのいえにすんでいます。  
  "I live in a two-story house."

## How the kanji combine

[二](../kanji/二.md), "two", plus 階, "floor", gives にかい. [建](../kanji/建.md) relates to building; 建て is read だて here. This mixes the on-reading count with a native element from 建てる (たてる), rather than using only on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

平屋 (ひらや) means a single-story building. 三階建て (さんがいだて) is a three-story building. These are contrasts in floor count, not general antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

二階 (にかい) alone means the second floor. 二階建て describes the whole building's number of stories, not a room's location on the second floor. Use の, not な, before the building noun.
