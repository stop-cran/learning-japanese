# 明後日 (みょうごにち) - day after tomorrow

## Meaning

明後日, read みょうごにち here, means "the day after tomorrow." It is a time noun that can modify a sentence without an added particle, or connect to another noun with の.

- 明後日、改めてご連絡します。 (みょうごにち、あらためてごれんらくします。) - I will contact you again the day after tomorrow.
- 明後日の会議は午前十時からです。 (みょうごにちのかいぎはごぜんじゅうじからです。) - The meeting the day after tomorrow starts at ten in the morning.

Both examples deliberately teach the assigned reading みょうごにち.

## How the kanji combine

[明](../kanji/明.md) contributes the coming-day idea, [後](../kanji/後.md) means after, and [日](../kanji/日.md) means day. Together they identify the day after tomorrow. This reading uses ミョウ + ゴ + ニチ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あさって](あさって.md) names the same day and is another reading of 明後日. Unlike みょうごにち, あさって is a whole-word reading that cannot be divided among the three characters.

[明日](明日.md) (あした) is tomorrow, one day earlier.

## Distinctive meaning

明後日 is relative to the day of speaking. For a day two days after some other reference date, check that the intended reference point is clear.

The dictionary's figurative "wrong direction" sense is restricted to あさって. Do not carry it over to みょうごにち simply because the written form matches.
