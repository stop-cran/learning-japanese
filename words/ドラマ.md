# ドラマ (ドラマ) - television drama; dramatic event

## Meaning

ドラマ is a noun for a television drama or acted fictional program.
In this use it can abbreviate テレビドラマ.
It can also describe drama in life: a striking or emotionally eventful incident.

- 毎週、家族とこのドラマを見ています。
  (まいしゅう、かぞくとこのドラマをみています。)
  - I watch this television drama with my family every week.
- 決勝戦には、最後までドラマがありました。
  (けっしょうせんには、さいごまでドラマがありました。)
  - The final match was dramatic right up to the end.

The first concerns a program; the second figuratively presents the match as an eventful story.
It does not say that the players were acting from a script.

## How the kanji combine

ドラマ is a katakana loanword corresponding to "drama."
There are no written kanji to explain, and the kana do not form separate Japanese
meaning components. テレビ specifies television in the longer expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[小説](小説.md) (しょうせつ) is a novel, a written work rather than an acted program.
[舞台](舞台.md) (ぶたい) can concern the theater stage or a theatrical production.
A documentary is a contrasting program type, not an antonym of every sense of ドラマ.

## Distinctive meaning

The television use is not confined to solemn tragedy: a fictional series can include
comedy, romance, or suspense. Conversely, the real-life "drama" use describes an
event's dramatic character, not the medium through which someone watched it.
