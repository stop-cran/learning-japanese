# 通帳 (つうちょう) - passbook; bankbook

## Meaning

通帳 is a passbook or bankbook recording account transactions.
It is a noun, not a verb for depositing money.

- 銀行で通帳を作りました。
  (ぎんこうでつうちょうをつくりました。) - I got a passbook at the bank.
- 通帳で預金の残高を確認しました。
  (つうちょうでよきんのざんだかをかくにんしました。) - I checked my deposit balance in the passbook.

In the second example, で marks the record used to check the balance.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes going back and forth or circulation;
帳 contributes an account book or register.
Learn the established banking word rather than treating it as any book that travels.
The on readings are ツウ + チョウ, with both long vowels retained.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[銀行](銀行.md) (ぎんこう) is the bank, not the passbook.
口座 (こうざ) is an account; 通帳 is its transaction record.
A [ノート](ノート.md) is a notebook more generally.
There is no natural direct antonym for a passbook.

## Distinctive meaning

A 通帳 is not itself the money in an account or a bank card.
The public dictionary also records books for transactions on credit;
the practical focus here is a deposit passbook.
The word does not imply that every bank account must have a paper passbook.
