# 取り入れる (とりいれる) - take in; adopt; harvest

## Meaning

取り入れる means to take something in, adopt an idea or method,
or gather in a harvest. It is a transitive ichidan verb; を marks
what is brought in or adopted.

- 雨が降る前に洗濯物を取り入れました。 (あめがふるまえにせんたくものをとりいれました。) - I brought the laundry inside before it rained.
- 新しい方法を取り入れました。 (あたらしいほうほうをとりいれました。) - We adopted a new method.
- 秋に米を取り入れます。 (あきにこめをとりいれます。) - We gather in the rice harvest in autumn.

Forms include 取り入れます (とりいれます), 取り入れて
(とりいれて), and 取り入れない (とりいれない).
Advice, techniques, and useful features can be taken into an
existing activity rather than physically carried indoors.

## How the kanji combine

[取](../kanji/取.md) supplies と in 取り, the linking form of
[取る](取る.md); [入](../kanji/入.md) supplies い in 入れる,
"put in." Together they suggest taking something into a place or
practice. Adoption can also be written 採り入れる, with
[採](../kanji/採.md); harvesting can be written 穫り入れる.
These recognized spellings do not make every dictionary search
variant a recommended everyday form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入れる](入れる.md) (いれる) is broader putting in and also has
adoption uses. [採る](採る.md) (とる) can mean adopting a method.
[収穫](収穫.md) (しゅうかく) names harvesting or its yield.
[取り出す](取り出す.md) moves something out rather than in.

## Distinctive meaning

Taking someone's advice into account and adopting it are related
but not identical: the sentence must establish what was actually
incorporated. Nor does 取り入れる always mean harvesting; the
object distinguishes laundry, a technique, and a crop.
