# 人文科学 (じんぶんかがく) - humanities

## Meaning

人文科学 means the humanities: fields concerned with human culture,
such as history, philosophy, and the study of language.
It is an academic-category noun, not simply a name for artistic practice.

- 大学で人文科学を学んでいます。 (だいがくでじんぶんかがくをまなんでいます。) - I study the humanities at university.
- 人文科学の本を探しています。 (じんぶんかがくのほんをさがしています。) - I am looking for books on the humanities.

Use 人文科学の before a related noun.
English may also call these academic fields "the arts"; here that means
humanistic study, not only [芸術](芸術.md) (げいじゅつ), artistic practice.

## How the kanji combine

人文 (じんぶん), human culture, qualifies [科学](科学.md) (かがく),
organized study or science. The written components are
[人](../kanji/人.md), human; [文](../kanji/文.md), writing or culture;
[科](../kanji/科.md), a branch; and [学](../kanji/学.md), study.
The reading is ジン + ブン + カ + ガク.
科学 here does not restrict the subject to laboratory natural science.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[社会科学](社会科学.md) (しゃかいかがく), social science, and
[自然科学](自然科学.md) (しぜんかがく), natural science, are useful
neighboring categories. [歴史](歴史.md) (れきし), history, and
[文学](文学.md) (ぶんがく), literature, illustrate humanistic study.
An individual discipline is not a synonym for the whole humanities category.

## Distinctive meaning

This card starts with "humanities," the pinned JMdict meaning.
Daijisen also records a broad cultural-study use encompassing social
subjects, alongside a narrower humanities use distinguished from
社会科学 and 自然科学. The categories therefore depend on the framework;
neither "always identical to social science" nor "can never include it"
is a safe universal rule.
