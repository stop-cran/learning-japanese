# 地下 (ちか) - underground; basement level

## Meaning

地下 is a noun for the area below ground. In a building it can refer to the basement level or levels.
Use **地下に** for an underground location or destination, **地下で** for an activity there,
and **地下の + noun** for something underground.

- 食品売り場は地下にあります。 (しょくひんうりばはちかにあります。) - The food department is in the basement.
- 地下の駐車場に車を止めました。 (ちかのちゅうしゃじょうにくるまをとめました。) - I parked in the underground parking area.

地下二階 (ちかにかい) means the second basement level, two levels below ground level.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) contributes ground or earth, and [下](../kanji/下.md) contributes below.
The first specifies what the second is relative to: below the ground.
The on readings チ + カ give ちか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

地下室 (ちかしつ) specifically names a basement room.
地上 (ちじょう), "above ground/on the surface," is the spatial opposite.
[地下鉄](地下鉄.md) (ちかてつ) names a subway system; 地下 by itself is not the name of that form of transport.

## Distinctive meaning

地下 can describe a location even when it is not an enclosed room.
It also has a figurative "underground" sense for secret activities, as in 地下活動 (ちかかつどう).
In that use the activity need not literally take place beneath the street.
Context separates a building location from concealment or secrecy.
