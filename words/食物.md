# 食物 (しょくもつ) - food; foodstuffs

## Meaning

食物 is a noun meaning food or foodstuffs: things eaten for nourishment. It can describe food collectively without specifying a particular meal or prepared dish.

- 食物をよくかんで食べましょう。 (しょくもつをよくかんでたべましょう。) - Let's chew our food well when we eat.
- 胃の中で食物が消化されます。 (いのなかでしょくもつがしょうかされます。) - Food is digested in the stomach.

The first sentence gives advice; the second describes what happens to food. Neither uses 食物 as the name of a meal such as lunch.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) contributes eating, and [物](../kanji/物.md) means a thing or substance. The first character identifies the role of the second: things for eating. The reading is しょく + もつ, not しょくぶつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[食べ物](食べ物.md) (たべもの) is a close equivalent using a native verb-based expression. Both can refer to food. 食物 fits explanatory contexts such as digestion or nutrition, but the difference is not that one denotes edible things and the other does not.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 食物 (しょくもつ) with 植物 (しょくぶつ), "plants." The sound and first kanji differ. Some plants are food, but 食物 names what is eaten, not a biological category. Remembering the complete reading helps keep these similar-looking compounds apart.
