# 回数 (かいすう) - number of times; frequency

## Meaning

回数 is the number of times something happens: a count of occurrences. Depending on context, English may translate it as "frequency." Use 回数が多い (かいすうがおおい), "happens many times," and 回数が少ない (かいすうがすくない), "happens few times."

- 練習の回数を増やしました。 (れんしゅうのかいすうをふやしました。) - I increased the number of practice sessions.
- 薬を飲む回数は一日三回です。 (くすりをのむかいすうはいちにちさんかいです。) - The medicine is taken three times a day.

The second sentence supplies the period 一日, "one day"; 回数 alone does not specify a time interval.

## How the kanji combine

[回](../kanji/回.md), "times; occurrences," modifies [数](../kanji/数.md), "number." The on readings カイ + スウ give かいすう. Keep the long う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[数](数.md) (かず) is number or quantity more generally. The counter [回](回.md) (かい) appears after a number, as in 三回 (さんかい), "three times"; 回数 is the noun naming that count. 頻度 (ひんど) concerns how often something happens and can overlap with a count over a stated period.

## Distinctive meaning

回数 is not elapsed duration. 練習の回数が多い says there are many practice occasions, not necessarily that each one lasts a long time. Nor is 回数 a finite verb meaning "count": [数える](数える.md) (かぞえる) names that action.
