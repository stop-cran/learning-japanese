# 甲乙 (こうおつ) — first and second; A and B; relative merit

## Meaning

甲乙 is a noun for the first and the second, like A and B. The word also means relative merit: which of two things is better. JMdict lists the set phrase 甲乙つけがたい (こうおつつけがたい) as its own entry: "difficult to say which of the two is better."

- 姉妹の演奏は、甲乙を争う出来ばえだった。
  (しまいのえんそうは、こうおつをあらそうできばえだった。)
  "The two sisters' performances were evenly matched in quality."
- 二つの作品はどちらもすばらしく、甲乙をつけがたい。
  (ふたつのさくひんはどちらもすばらしく、こうおつをつけがたい。)
  "Both works are excellent, and it is hard to say which is better."

These are original usage examples, not quotations. Both use the second sense, in phrases from Digital Daijisen's own examples for that sense: 甲乙を争う出来ばえ and 甲乙をつけがたい.

## How the kanji combine

Digital Daijisen derives the word from 甲 and 乙, the first two of the ten calendar signs (十干). Here 甲 (こう) is the first label and [乙](../kanji/乙.md) the second. The on readings are コウ + オツ, with no sound change.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[優劣](優劣.md) (ゆうれつ), "superiority or inferiority; relative merits," is the everyday word for the second sense; Digital Daijisen defines that sense of 甲乙 with 優劣. [前者](前者.md) (ぜんしゃ), "the former; the first mentioned," and [後者](後者.md) (こうしゃ), "the latter," pick one of two things by the order they were mentioned, not by a label. [丙](丙.md) (へい), "third rank; class C," continues the sequence, as in 甲乙丙丁 (こうおつへいてい), "A, B, C and D."

There is no single fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

In JMdict, one sense of 甲 is the A party or first party, for example in a contract, and one sense of 乙 is the second party, rank or grade. 乙 on its own, read おつ, can also mean stylish or chic; neither JMdict nor Digital Daijisen gives that sense for 甲乙.

The same spelling read かるめる is a different word. Digital Daijisen lists it under the spellings 上下 and 甲乙 as a term of traditional Japanese music: the high pitch (かる) and the low pitch (める). JMdict does not list this music term: its only entry spelled 甲乙 is read こうおつ.
