# ワイシャツ - dress shirt

## Meaning

ワイシャツ is a dress or business shirt, typically with a collar and front buttons. It is the sort of shirt commonly worn with a suit.

- 仕事用のワイシャツを買いました。 (しごとようのワイシャツをかいました。) - I bought a dress shirt for work.
- 青いワイシャツを着ています。 (あおいワイシャツをきています。) - I am wearing a blue dress shirt.

Use 着る (きる) for putting it on and 着ている for wearing it.

## How the kanji combine

The word comes from English "white shirt," but its established Japanese meaning is not restricted to white shirts. The alternative spelling Ｙシャツ reflects the sound ワイ rather than a Y-shaped garment.

## Synonyms and antonyms

シャツ is broader. Ｔシャツ (ティーシャツ) names a T-shirt and is not an interchangeable clothing category. There is no natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The source expression explains the history, not a present-day color requirement. A blue or striped business shirt can be a ワイシャツ. In English, translate it as "dress shirt" or "business shirt" when that is the garment intended, not automatically "white shirt."
