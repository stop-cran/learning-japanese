# 不味い (まずい) — bad-tasting; unpalatable

## Meaning

不味い means bad-tasting or unpalatable. It is usually written まずい. The word can also describe an awkward or problematic situation, but it is not a neutral general-purpose translation of every English "unpleasant".

- このスープはまずいです。  
  このスープはまずいです。  
  "This soup tastes bad."
- このままではまずいです。  
  このままではまずいです。  
  "It will be a problem if things stay like this."

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) suggests negation and [味](../kanji/味.md) means taste. The whole spelling is assigned the native adjective reading まずい, not a combination of regular on readings. It is an い-adjective: まずくない, "not bad-tasting", and まずかった, "tasted bad".

## Synonyms and antonyms

おいしい, "delicious", is the food-related opposite. おいしくない, "not tasty", is often a less direct negative assessment. Neither contrast automatically applies to every nonfood use of まずい.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling someone's cooking まずい is blunt, even with です. For learners, recognizing the word and understanding its strength matters as much as knowing its literal food meaning.
