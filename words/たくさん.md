# たくさん (たくさん) - many; a lot

## Meaning

たくさん expresses a large number or amount. It can modify an action directly or come before a noun with の.

- 公園に人がたくさんいます。 (こうえんにひとがたくさんいます。) - There are many people in the park.
- たくさんの本を読みました。 (たくさんのほんをよみました。) - I read a lot of books.

It works with amounts as well as countable objects: 水をたくさん飲む (みずをたくさんのむ), "drink a lot of water." For ordinary "many + noun," learn たくさんの, not たくさんな.

## How the kanji combine

沢山 is the kanji spelling, but kana is usual. This is a Sino-Japanese word even when written without kanji; its everyday meaning is quantity, not a literal description of mountains.

## Synonyms and antonyms

多い (おおい) means numerous or plentiful, but has different sentence patterns. 少し (すこし), "a little," contrasts with a large amount.

## Distinctive meaning

たくさん does not itself mean "too much." In もうたくさんです, however, it means "I've had enough." That expression may firmly reject any more, so it is not automatically a polite way to praise a generous serving.
