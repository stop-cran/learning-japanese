# ブラウス - blouse

## Meaning

ブラウス is a blouse, a type of upper-body garment.
It is a noun; wear it with 着る (きる), not a verb meaning to put on shoes.

- 白いブラウスを着ています。 (しろいブラウスをきています。) - I am wearing a white blouse.
- このブラウスは手で洗ってください。 (このブラウスはてであらってください。) - Please wash this blouse by hand.

The second sentence gives a care instruction for this garment,
not a rule that all blouses require hand washing.

## How the kanji combine

ブラウス is a katakana loanword. There is no kanji compound or native
verb ending to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[シャツ](シャツ.md) is a shirt; its garment category overlaps with a blouse.
[上着](上着.md) (うわぎ) is broader upper clothing, including jackets.
Neither label makes every shirt or jacket a ブラウス.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the actual garment name rather than translating every English "top"
as ブラウス. The word does not itself fix a fabric, sleeve length, color,
or dress code. A modifier such as 白い supplies those details.
