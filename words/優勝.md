# 優勝 (ゆうしょう) - championship victory

## Meaning

優勝 means an overall victory or winning a championship title.
優勝する is intransitive: 大会で優勝する (たいかいでゆうしょうする), "win the tournament."
Use で for the competition setting, not a direct-object を.

- 私たちのチームは全国大会で優勝しました。 (わたしたちのチームはぜんこくたいかいでゆうしょうしました。) - Our team won the national tournament.
- 今年は優勝を目指しています。 (ことしはゆうしょうをめざしています。) - This year we are aiming for the championship.

In the second sentence, 優勝 is a noun and the object of 目指す (めざす), "aim for."
優勝者 (ゆうしょうしゃ) is the champion or winner.

## How the kanji combine

[優](../kanji/優.md) contributes excellence or superiority, and [勝](../kanji/勝.md) winning.
Together they express taking the top result in a competition.
The on-readings are ユウ + ショウ; both contain long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勝つ](勝つ.md) (かつ) means "win" more broadly, including a single match.
勝利 (しょうり) is victory.
準優勝 (じゅんゆうしょう), "runner-up; second place," is a neighboring result, not simply a synonym for losing every match.

## Distinctive meaning

Winning one round does not necessarily mean 優勝した. The overall title is central to this competition sense.
Conversely, the exact competition format can vary: the champion may be decided by a final match or by accumulated results.
The word alone does not tell you the format.
