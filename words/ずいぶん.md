# ずいぶん (ずいぶん) — considerably; surprisingly much

## Meaning

ずいぶん means **quite, considerably, or very**, often with a feeling that the degree is noteworthy or greater than expected. It modifies the following description or action.

- ずいぶん寒くなりましたね。 (ずいぶんさむくなりましたね。) — It has become quite cold, hasn't it?
- ずいぶん待ちました。 (ずいぶんまちました。) — I waited a very long time.

ずいぶんと is also possible as an adverbial form. No に is needed.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The kanji spelling is 随[分](../kanji/分.md), read ズイ + ブン. It is best learned as an established expression rather than literally adding individual character meanings to predict its modern degree sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とても](とても.md) is a broadly useful “very.” [かなり](かなり.md) means “quite/considerably” and overlaps with ずいぶん. [少し](少し.md) (すこし), “a little,” contrasts in degree, but does not necessarily convey surprise.

## Distinctive meaning

ずいぶん is not tied to one exact numerical amount. The speaker's expectations matter. When directed at someone, the suggestion “more than expected” can sound admiring, surprised, or critical, depending on what follows and the tone.

The spelling 随分 (ずいぶん) represents this same adverb, not a separate N3-only meaning.
