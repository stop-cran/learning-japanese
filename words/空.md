# 空 (そら) — sky

## Meaning

空 (そら) is the sky: the space above us where we see clouds, the sun, and flying birds.
That is this article's primary reading; the separate reading から is explained below.

- 空が青いです。 (そらがあおいです。) — The sky is blue.
- 空に鳥がいます。 (そらにとりがいます。) — There is a bird in the sky.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single native noun written with [空](../kanji/空.md), read そら. There is no compound or okurigana.
In 青空 (あおぞら, blue sky), [青](../kanji/青.md) describes the colour and そら becomes ぞら.
That voicing does not make ぞら an on reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 天 (てん) overlaps with "sky" and "heavens," but also has religious and other senses; it is not a universal replacement for そら.
- 空気 (くうき) is air or atmosphere, not the same thing as the visible expanse of sky.
- There is no single direct antonym of そら needed for ordinary use.

## Distinctive meaning

The same kanji writes a different everyday word, 空 (から), meaning "empty":
箱は空です (はこはからです), "The box is empty." Do not read that sentence with そら.
Before a noun, use の: 空の箱 (からのはこ), "an empty box." 空になる (からになる)
means "become empty," as in 箱が空になりました (はこがからになりました),
"The box became empty."
The reading クウ also appears in compounds such as 空港 (くうこう, airport).

The N3 source row gives から but glosses it as "sky." Here から deliberately retains its
dictionary-supported empty meaning; the list compiler's original intention is not established.
