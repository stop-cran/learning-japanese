# 鶴 (つる) — crane (bird)

## Meaning

鶴 means a **crane**, the bird. It can name one crane or cranes generally; the noun itself does not mark plural. The kana spelling ツル is also used.

- 鶴を見ました。 (つるをみました。) — I saw a crane.
- あの鳥は鶴です。 (あのとりはつるです。) — That bird is a crane.

These examples identify the bird without identifying its species.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun: [鶴](../kanji/鶴.md) is read **つる** as a whole. It has no okurigana. The character's on reading カク is used in other words, not as a substitute reading of this noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鳥](鳥.md) (とり), "bird," and [動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," are broader categories. [鷹](鷹.md) (たか), "hawk; falcon," names another kind of bird, not a synonym. There is no useful antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

An English lifting-machine "crane" is クレーン (くれーん), not this bird word. コウノトリ (こうのとり), "stork," is a different bird name; do not infer interchangeability from a broad English character gloss.

In 折り鶴 (おりづる), "origami crane," the bird name becomes づる in the compound. The poetic name たず (historically たづ) also exists, but つる is the reading taught here.
