# 中小 (ちゅうしょう) — small and medium-sized

## Meaning

中小 means "medium and small": the group of businesses or sizes below the large ones. The standard compound is 中小企業 ちゅうしょうきぎょう (small and medium-sized enterprises, SMEs).
Other combinations: 中小零細 ちゅうしょうれいさい (medium, small and micro businesses).

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) (middle) plus [小](../kanji/小.md) (small): a coordinated pair meaning "middle and small", an inclusive range. It is in a series:
大中小 だいちゅうしょう (large, medium, small), [大小](大小.md) (large and small), 中小.

Reversing the order gives a different word: 小中 しょうちゅう, as in 小中学校 しょうちゅうがっこう ("elementary and junior high schools").
Here 中 is "middle school" and 小 is "elementary school", so 小中 is school stages, not size.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 零細 れいさい (tiny, micro business).
- Antonym: 大企業 だいきぎょう (large enterprise), 大手 おおて (a major company).

## Distinctive meaning

中小 is a size category, usually of business (employees or capital thresholds vary by law and industry). 小規模 しょうきぼ means "small scale" and doesn't include medium.
