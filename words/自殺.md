# 自殺 (じさつ) - suicide

## Meaning

自殺 is the noun "suicide." It also forms the intransitive verb 自殺する, "die by suicide."
This is sensitive vocabulary used in news, public health, and discussions of loss.
The person is the subject of する; the verb does not take a person as a direct object.

- 学校で自殺予防について学びました。 (がっこうでじさつよぼうについてまなびました。) - We learned about suicide prevention at school.
- この記事は若者の自殺を防ぐ取り組みを紹介しています。 (このきじはわかもののじさつをふせぐとりくみをしょうかいしています。) - This article introduces efforts to prevent suicide among young people.

自殺予防 (じさつよぼう) means suicide prevention. In the second example, を belongs to 防ぐ,
"prevent," not to the verb 自殺する.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) refers to oneself, and [殺](../kanji/殺.md) contributes killing.
The first component identifies the self-directed nature of the act.
The on readings ジ + サツ give じさつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[死亡](死亡.md) (しぼう) means death without specifying its cause.
[亡くなる](亡くなる.md) (なくなる) means pass away, again without identifying suicide.
These can be relevant in discussing a loss but are not exact synonyms for its cause.

## Distinctive meaning

Use this word carefully and without making assumptions about a person's motives.
It identifies a serious cause of death, not an ordinary mistake or a synonym for feeling unhappy.
In English translations, "died by suicide" is a neutral way to describe a death without implying a criminal judgment.
