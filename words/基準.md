# 基準 (きじゅん) - standard; criterion; basis

## Meaning

基準 is a standard, criterion, or reference used to measure, judge, or compare something. It can also name a requirement that must be met. It is a noun.

- この製品は安全基準を満たしています。
  (このせいひんはあんぜんきじゅんをみたしています。) - This product meets the safety standards.
- 何を基準にして選びましたか。
  (なにをきじゅんにしてえらびましたか。) - What did you use as the basis for choosing?

## How the kanji combine

基 contributes a base or foundation; [準](../kanji/準.md) a standard or conformity to a reference. The on readings are キ + ジュン. The related spelling 規準 uses [規](../kanji/規.md), a rule or standard, in place of 基.

## Synonyms and antonyms

**規準**, also read **きじゅん**, is recognized in the same JMdict family. Daijisen explains 規準 through a norm to follow in thought or conduct, whereas 基準 emphasizes a reference or requirements. For example, 行動の規準を話し合いました (こうどうのきじゅんをはなしあいました), "We discussed standards of conduct."

[水準](水準.md) (すいじゅん) concerns a level or standard; [基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) a foundation or basis. Their English glosses overlap without making every construction interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

基準 and 規準 overlap in "standard; criterion." Their dictionary explanations highlight different emphases, not an exceptionless rule assigning every technical term to one spelling. Keep the conventional spelling of the particular expression rather than mechanically swapping the characters.
