# 腰掛ける (こしかける) - sit down; take a seat

## Meaning

腰掛ける means to sit down or take a seat, commonly on a chair,
bench, or similar support. It is an intransitive ichidan verb:
腰掛けます, 腰掛けた, 腰掛けて, 腰掛けない.
The seat takes に.

- ベンチに腰掛けて、少し休みました。
  (ベンチにこしかけて、すこしやすみました。) - I sat on a bench and rested for a while.
- 窓のそばの椅子に腰掛けています。
  (まどのそばのいすにこしかけています。) - I am sitting on the chair by the window.

腰掛けている can describe the seated state, not repeated acts
of sitting down.

## How the kanji combine

[腰](../kanji/腰.md) (こし), waist or lower back, combines with
[掛](../kanji/掛.md)ける (かける), placing or resting here.
It is a native compound verb. The noun [腰掛け](腰掛け.md)
can name the seat itself.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[座る](座る.md) (すわる) is a broader sitting verb and also readily
describes sitting on the floor.
[おかけください](おかけください.md) is a polite seating invitation.
[立つ](立つ.md) (たつ), "stand up," contrasts with taking a seat.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 椅子に腰掛ける, not 椅子を腰掛ける in this sitting construction.
The usual chair or bench association is not a requirement that
the seat be a particular piece of manufactured furniture:
an appropriate ledge or stone can also support someone sitting.
The verb does not itself say that the person sits only briefly.
