# 性質 (せいしつ) - nature; inherent properties

## Meaning

性質 names a person's disposition or temperament, or the qualities and
inherent properties of something. It is a noun, not a statement that
the quality is good.

- 彼は穏やかな性質です。 (かれはおだやかなせいしつです。) - He has a gentle disposition.
- この金属には熱を伝えやすい性質があります。 (このきんぞくにはねつをつたえやすいせいしつがあります。) - This metal has the property of conducting heat easily.

The second uses 性質がある, have a property, with a clause describing
that property. It concerns the material's behavior, not ownership.

## How the kanji combine

[性](../kanji/性.md) contributes nature, and [質](../kanji/質.md)
quality or inherent substance. Their related meanings reinforce the
idea of what someone or something is characteristically like.
The on readings are セイ + シツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[性格](性格.md) often concerns personality or character, but it also
applies to things or events. 性格 is not limited to people, and 性質
is not limited to things.
[質](質.md) overlaps in quality or inherent nature.
[特徴](特徴.md) emphasizes a characteristic or distinguishing feature,
not necessarily every property of a material.

## Distinctive meaning

[自然](自然.md) can mean the natural world or being natural; that is
not the same as a material's inherent nature. English "property" here
also does not mean [財産](財産.md), owned assets.
A temporary mood is not automatically a person's 性質, and the noun
alone does not judge that disposition as admirable or objectionable.
