# 他 (た) - other

## Meaning

他, read **た**, means "other." It functions as a noun and modifies another noun with **の**. Useful expressions include **他の方法** (たのほうほう), "another method," and **他に例がない** (たにれいがない), "there is no other example."

- 他の方法を考えましょう。 (たのほうほうをかんがえましょう。) - Let's consider another method.
- 自分の意見を他に押しつけてはいけません。 (じぶんのいけんをたにおしつけてはいけません。) - You must not force your opinions on others.

The second sentence uses 他 as a noun for others, with に marking the people subjected to the action.

## How the kanji combine

[他](../kanji/他.md) supplies the idea of someone or something other than the reference point. The reading taught here is the on reading タ. This is a complete single-kanji word, not a missing half of a compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

他 also occurs with the reading ほか, so written 他の can require attention to context or supplied reading. [別](別.md) (べつ), "different; separate," overlaps in 別の方法, "another method." [同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ), "same," provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not memorize a rigid rule that た is for places and things, or that it excludes them. JMdict's qualification emphasizes people and abstract matters, but is not an exclusive grammatical restriction. Learn the actual construction and preserve the intended reading.
