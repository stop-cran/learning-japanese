# 似合う (にあう) - suit; look good on

## Meaning

似合う is an intransitive godan verb for something suiting a person or fitting their appearance or character. The item takes が, and the person it suits can take に.

- その帽子はあなたによく似合います。 (そのぼうしはあなたによくにあいます。) - That hat looks very good on you.
- 彼女には青い服が似合います。 (かのじょにはあおいふくがにあいます。) - Blue clothes suit her.

似合っている (にあっている) describes something looking suitable in its current use. Forms include 似合わない (にあわない), "does not suit," and 似合って (にあって).

## How the kanji combine

[似](../kanji/似.md) contributes resemblance or suitability, and [合](../kanji/合.md) contributes fitting or matching. The native elements に + あう combine into one verb. Do not interpret it simply as the separate instruction "resemble and meet."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[合う](合う.md) (あう) has broader matching and fitting uses. Clothes can fit in size without being flattering. [似る](似る.md) (にる) means to resemble: resembling someone and having a hat suit you are different relationships.

## Distinctive meaning

An English gloss may say "become," as in "That color becomes you." Here it means looking good on someone, not changing into another state. For a compliment, 似合っています is useful; it evaluates the match between wearer and item, not necessarily the item's price or size.
