# 方 (かた・ほう) — person; way; comparison side

## Meaning

**かた** politely refers to a person. As a suffix, it also means "way of doing": attach it to the verb's ます-stem, as in 読み方 (よみかた), "how to read."

- あの方は先生です。 (あのかたはせんせいです。) — That person is a teacher.
- この字の読み方を教えてください。 (このじのよみかたをおしえてください。) — Please tell me how to read this character.

**ほう** is a separate reading used to identify one side of a comparison:

電車の方がバスより速いです。 (でんしゃのほうがバスよりはやいです。) — The train is faster than the bus.

## How the kanji combine

[方](../kanji/方.md) has the native reading かた and on reading ほう. The frontmatter records かた, but both readings are taught here; the meaning and construction determine the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

人 (ひと) is the ordinary word for "person." 方法 (ほうほう) means a method. Neither replaces every use of 方.

## Distinctive meaning

For comparison, use noun + の方が, but a verb clause attaches directly: 歩く方が速い (あるくほうがはやい), "walking is faster." Do not read comparison 方 as かた or attach the method suffix to a dictionary-form verb.
