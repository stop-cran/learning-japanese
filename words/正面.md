# 正面 (しょうめん) - front; frontage

## Meaning

正面 is the front or directly facing side of something. 正面の入口 (しょうめんのいりぐち) is the front entrance; 建物の正面 (たてもののしょうめん) is the front of a building.

- 駅の正面で待っています。 (えきのしょうめんでまっています。) - I am waiting at the front of the station.
- 建物の正面に大きな窓があります。 (たてもののしょうめんにおおきなまどがあります。) - There is a large window on the front of the building.

に marks the window's location; で marks where the speaker waits. Use noun + の正面 to identify whose front is meant.

## How the kanji combine

[正](../kanji/正.md) contributes straight or direct, and [面](../kanji/面.md) a face or side. Together they name the directly facing side. Read ショウ + メン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[前](前.md) (まえ) can indicate a position in front or a time before; 正面 does not have that general temporal meaning. [表](表.md) (おもて) is a front or outward-facing side. 背面 (はいめん), "back; rear face," offers a contrast in a physical orientation context.

## Distinctive meaning

正面から取り組む (しょうめんからとりくむ) means to tackle an issue directly or head-on. That extension does not make しょうめん an unrestricted noun for "honesty": [正直](正直.md) (しょうじき) concerns being honest or frank. A dictionary page may also have a separate 正面 heading read まとも; do not transfer that reading's descriptions indiscriminately to しょうめん.
