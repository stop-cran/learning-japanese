# 有利 (ゆうり) - advantageous; favorable

## Meaning

有利 describes having an advantage or being favorable, including financially profitable terms. It functions as a noun and a な-adjective: 有利な条件 means "favorable terms." Use に to identify who or what benefits.

- この規則は経験のある選手に有利です。 (このきそくはけいけんのあるせんしゅにゆうりです。) - This rule favors experienced players.
- 有利な条件で契約できました。 (ゆうりなじょうけんでけいやくできました。) - We were able to sign a contract on favorable terms.

有利に進める (ゆうりにすすめる) means to advance something to one's advantage, for example a negotiation.

## How the kanji combine

[有](../kanji/有.md) contributes having or possessing, and [利](../kanji/利.md) contributes benefit or advantage. The relation is "having an advantage." Their on readings are ユウ + リ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不利](不利.md) (ふり), "disadvantageous," is a direct contrast. [便利](便利.md) (べんり) concerns convenience: something convenient may help, but convenience and competitive advantage are not identical. 有益 (ゆうえき) describes something beneficial or useful.

## Distinctive meaning

An advantage is relative to a person, purpose, or situation. A rule favorable to one player may disadvantage another. 有利 does not promise certain success, and the financial sense does not mean that every use concerns money: height can be advantageous in a sport without being a monetary profit.
