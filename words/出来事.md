# 出来事 (できごと) - occurrence; happening

## Meaning

出来事 is a noun for something that happens: an occurrence, event, or incident. It can concern a memorable public event or an ordinary experience in someone's day. The word is not inherently negative.

- 今日の出来事を日記に書きました。 (きょうのできごとをにっきにかきました。) - I wrote about today's events in my diary.
- 旅先で不思議な出来事がありました。 (たびさきでふしぎなできごとがありました。) - Something strange happened while I was traveling.

出来事がある presents an occurrence; 出来事を話す (できごとをはなす) means telling someone about what happened.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) and [来](../kanji/来.md) together form 出来, related here to something coming about. [事](../kanji/事.md) names a matter or event. The native compound is でき + ごと, with the voiced ご of こと in this combination.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[事件](事件.md) (じけん) can focus on a significant incident or case, including a crime. [事故](事故.md) (じこ) identifies an accident. Both can be kinds of 出来事, but neither should be substituted for every pleasant or uneventful happening.

## Distinctive meaning

出来事 names what occurred, not the act of scheduling or organizing an event. "Today's events" in a diary can be 今日の出来事; a notice advertising a planned festival may need the festival's name or an event word suited to that organized activity.
