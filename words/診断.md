# 診断 (しんだん) - diagnosis

## Meaning

診断 is a diagnosis: an assessment of someone's health or the nature
of an illness after examining the patient. It is a noun and a transitive
する verb. A conclusion can be followed by と診断する.

- 医師はインフルエンザと診断しました。 (いしはインフルエンザとしんだんしました。) - The doctor diagnosed it as influenza.
- 診断の結果を聞きました。 (しんだんのけっかをききました。) - I heard the results of the diagnosis.

From the patient's perspective, 風邪と診断されました
(かぜとしんだんされました) means "I was diagnosed with a cold."
The passive describes receiving the diagnosis.

## How the kanji combine

診 contributes examining a patient, and [断](../kanji/断.md) contributes
judgment or a decision. Together they connect examination with judging
the condition. The on readings are シン + ダン.
断 is not literal cutting in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[診察](診察.md) (しんさつ) focuses on a clinician's examination or consultation.
[検査](検査.md) (けんさ) can name a test or inspection.
These can contribute to a 診断, but examining, testing, and reaching
a diagnostic judgment are not identical steps.

## Distinctive meaning

診断 does not itself mean treatment, and it does not guarantee that the
judgment is correct. Daijisen also records broader assessment of a thing's
condition, as in 経営診断 (けいえいしんだん), "business diagnosis."
That extension does not turn every school examination into a 診断.
