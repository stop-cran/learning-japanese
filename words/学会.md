# 学会 (がっかい) - academic society; academic conference

## Meaning

学会 can name an academic society or an academic meeting or conference. Context distinguishes the organization from its event.

- 私は言語学の学会に入っています。 (わたしはげんごがくのがっかいにはいっています。) - I belong to an academic society for linguistics.
- 来月の学会で研究の成果を発表します。 (らいげつのがっかいでけんきゅうのせいかをはっぴょうします。) - I will present my research findings at next month's academic conference.

Membership is central in the first example; a presentation event is central in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) contributes scholarship or study, and [会](../kanji/会.md) a gathering or association. The academic setting specifies the kind of association or meeting. ガク + カイ becomes がっかい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) is a meeting or conference more broadly. [会合](会合.md) (かいごう) is a meeting or gathering, and [団体](団体.md) (だんたい) an organization or group. Neither term by itself specifies academic research.

## Distinctive meaning

A 学会 need not be a university department, and an ordinary school lesson is not automatically a 学会. Compare [学科](学科.md) (がっか), a subject or department. English "society" here means an association, not society at large.
