# サラダ (サラダ) — salad

## Meaning

サラダ means **salad**. It names a dish, not a particular vegetable or one fixed recipe.

昼ご飯にサラダを食べました。 (ひるごはんにさらだをたべました。) — I ate a salad for lunch.

Other nouns can form familiar dish names: ポテトサラダ (ぽてとさらだ), “potato salad,” and 野菜サラダ (やさいさらだ), “vegetable salad.”

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana, with no kanji components. JMdict records Portuguese *salada* and Dutch *salade* as source-language forms. Do not assume every familiar katakana food word came directly from English.

## Synonyms and antonyms

野菜 (やさい) means vegetables, whether raw or cooked. ドレッシング is salad dressing, not the salad itself. Neither word is an exact synonym, and salad has no ordinary lexical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

サラダ does not guarantee a dish made only of raw leafy vegetables. Potato salad and other mixtures also carry the name. Ingredients, preparation, and dressing depend on the particular dish.
