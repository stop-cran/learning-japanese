# 副作用 (ふくさよう) - side effect; adverse reaction

## Meaning

副作用 is a side effect or adverse reaction, particularly an unwanted effect of a medicine alongside its intended action. It is a noun: 副作用がある means "have side effects," and 副作用で can identify what caused a further result.

- この薬の副作用で、眠くなりました。 (このくすりのふくさようで、ねむくなりました。) - This medicine's side effects made me sleepy.
- 薬を飲む前に、副作用について説明を受けました。 (くすりをのむまえに、ふくさようについてせつめいをうけました。) - Before taking the medicine, I received an explanation of its side effects.

The examples illustrate language, not advice about a particular medicine.

## How the kanji combine

[副](../kanji/副.md) contributes secondary or subsidiary. [作](../kanji/作.md), doing, and [用](../kanji/用.md), use or function, occur together in 作用 (さよう), action or effect. 副 modifies that unit: a secondary effect. The on readings are フク + サ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[効果](効果.md) (こうか) means effect or effectiveness more broadly; 副作用 specifies a side effect. [影響](影響.md) (えいきょう) is influence or impact and does not by itself identify a medicine's side effect.

The intended effect and a side effect are different aspects, not exact antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

副作用 does not mean that a medicine failed to work. A medicine can have its intended effect and also produce 副作用.
