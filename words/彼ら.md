# 彼ら (かれら) - they; them

## Meaning

彼ら refers to a group as "they" or "them," usually males or a mixed-gender
group. Particles, rather than a changed pronoun form, show its role.

- 彼らは同じ学校に通っています。 (かれらはおなじがっこうにかよっています。) - They attend the same school.
- 昨日、彼らに会いました。 (きのう、かれらにあいました。) - I met them yesterday.

## How the kanji combine

[彼](../kanji/彼.md) writes かれ, "he," and the kana suffix ら adds a group
meaning. The spelling 彼等 is also recorded, but 彼ら keeps the suffix transparent.

## Synonyms and antonyms

あの人たち (あのひとたち), "those people," can identify a group more explicitly.
[彼](彼.md) is the corresponding singular reference, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not mechanically translate every English "they" as 彼ら. Japanese often
leaves a known group unstated or uses names and roles. This word refers to
people, not the ordinary plural equivalent of "it" for a set of objects.
The suffix ら is not a general plural ending for every Japanese noun.
