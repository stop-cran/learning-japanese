# 攻撃 (こうげき) - attack; offensive

## Meaning

攻撃 is an attack or offensive. 攻撃する is a transitive verb with する: the target takes を. It can concern physical action, an attacking phase in sport, or verbal criticism.

- 後半は私たちのチームの攻撃が続きました。 (こうはんはわたしたちのチームのこうげきがつづきました。) - Our team kept attacking in the second half.
- その記事は市長の方針を攻撃しています。 (そのきじはしちょうのほうしんをこうげきしています。) - That article attacks the mayor's policy.

In the second example, the attack is criticism, not physical contact. 攻撃を受ける (こうげきをうける) means "come under attack."

## How the kanji combine

攻 contributes attacking, and 撃 contributes striking. The two action meanings reinforce each other. Their on readings are コウ + ゲキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

守備 (しゅび), "defense," is a counterpart in sports. [批判](批判.md) (ひはん) means criticism and can describe evaluating a position without the same attacking characterization. Calling criticism 攻撃 presents it as an attack; it is not a neutral substitute in every discussion.

## Distinctive meaning

The target and setting determine the English translation. A team's 攻撃 may be its offense, while a newspaper's 攻撃 may be a verbal attack. The noun does not itself tell you whether the action was justified, successful, or physical.
