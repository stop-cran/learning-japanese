# 文房具 (ぶんぼうぐ) - stationery; writing supplies

## Meaning

文房具 is stationery or writing supplies: pens, pencils, erasers and similar tools
used for writing or study. It is a noun and can refer collectively to such items.

- 駅前の店で文房具を買いました。 (えきまえのみせでぶんぼうぐをかいました。) - I bought stationery at the shop in front of the station.
- この引き出しには文房具が入っています。 (このひきだしにはぶんぼうぐがはいっています。) - This drawer contains writing supplies.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) contributes writing; 房 has a room or chamber association
in 文房 (ぶんぼう), a study or writing room. [具](../kanji/具.md) contributes
tools or implements. Think of 文房 + 具 as tools for the writing room, not three
unrelated modern object labels. 房 is read ボウ in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

文具 (ぶんぐ) is a shorter synonym.
[道具](道具.md) (どうぐ) and [器具](器具.md) (きぐ) are broader tool or implement
terms. [事務](事務.md) (じむ) is office work or administration, not a synonym
for the supplies themselves.

## Distinctive meaning

文房具屋 (ぶんぼうぐや) is a stationery shop.
Do not limit the word to decorative letter paper, or expand it to every item found
in an office: an office chair is not thereby a writing implement.
English "stationery" names supplies; "stationary," meaning not moving, is unrelated.
