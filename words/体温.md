# 体温 (たいおん) - body temperature

## Meaning

体温 is a noun meaning body temperature. It names the measured property, not a fever by itself.

- 朝、体温を測りました。 (あさ、たいおんをはかりました。) - I took my temperature in the morning.
- 運動の後、体温が上がりました。 (うんどうのあと、たいおんがあがりました。) - My body temperature rose after exercise.

体温を測る uses を for the quantity being measured. 体温が高い (たいおんがたかい) and 体温が低い (たいおんがひくい) describe a high and low body temperature. 体温計 (たいおんけい) is a thermometer for measuring body temperature.

## How the kanji combine

[体](../kanji/体.md) means body, and 温 concerns temperature or warmth. 体 specifies whose temperature is meant. The on readings combine as タイ + オン; keep the vowel sequence in たいおん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[温度](温度.md) (おんど) is temperature in general. [気温](気温.md) (きおん) is air temperature. [熱](熱.md) (ねつ) can mean heat or fever; 熱がある is not a neutral substitute for saying that a body has a temperature.

## Distinctive meaning

A statement about 体温 needs context before it indicates health or illness. The word itself does not specify a measurement site, method, or a diagnostic threshold. For vocabulary purposes, the important distinction is between the neutral quantity 体温 and an expression describing a fever. High and low are contrasting values, not antonyms of the noun.
