# 最近 (さいきん) — recently; these days

## Meaning

最近 refers to the recent past or the period around now. The sentence determines whether English
"recently" or "these days" fits better: a completed event and a current habit need not have the same translation.
It can introduce the time directly, without adding に.

- 最近、新しい自転車を買いました。 (さいきん、あたらしいじてんしゃをかいました。) — I recently bought a new bicycle.
- 最近、日本語の本をよく読みます。 (さいきん、にほんごのほんをよくよみます。) — These days I often read books in Japanese.

## How the kanji combine

[最](../kanji/最.md) supplies "most" and [近](../kanji/近.md) supplies "near."
In this time expression, the nearness is to the present.
The on readings combine as サイ + キン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[昔](昔.md) (むかし), "the old days," contrasts an earlier period with 最近.
[昨日](昨日.md) (きのう), "yesterday," is much more exact: 最近 does not identify one particular day.
These words are not interchangeable merely because they can all introduce past events.

## Distinctive meaning

最近 is not restricted to completed past actions; the second example describes a current pattern.
Its time window depends on what is being discussed rather than a fixed number of days.
Before a noun, use の: 最近の出来事 (さいきんのできごと), "recent events."
For a precise appointment date, name the date instead.
