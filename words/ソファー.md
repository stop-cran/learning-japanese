# ソファー - sofa; couch

## Meaning

ソファー is a sofa or couch, a piece of seating furniture. It is a noun.

- ソファーに座ってください。 (ソファーにすわってください。) - Please sit on the sofa.
- 猫がソファーの下にいます。 (ねこがソファーのしたにいます。) - The cat is under the sofa.

Use に for the place someone sits. With a location noun such as 下 (した), connect it with の: ソファーの下, "under the sofa."

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to "sofa." There are no kanji elements. ファ combines フ with small ァ, and the final ー marks a long vowel. JMdict records both ソファ and ソファー; the shorter spelling does not name a different kind of furniture.

## Synonyms and antonyms

椅子 (いす) means chair and is a useful nearby furniture term, but does not necessarily mean a sofa. ベンチ is a bench. Neither is an opposite: the distinction concerns the kind of seat.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun does not specify the sofa's material or capacity. 二人掛けのソファー (ふたりがけのソファー) means "a two-person sofa." English "couch" is another useful translation of the same word, not evidence that Japanese has separate senses for every English furniture label.
