# 埋める (うめる) - bury; fill a gap

## Meaning

埋める is a transitive ichidan verb: someone buries something, fills a space or plugs a gap. The thing buried or the space filled takes を; material used to fill it can take で.

- 犬が骨を庭に埋めました。 (いぬがほねをにわにうめました。) - The dog buried a bone in the garden.
- 穴を土で埋めました。 (あなをつちでうめました。) - I filled the hole with soil.
- 空欄を埋めてください。 (くうらんをうめてください。) - Please fill in the blanks.

Forms include 埋めます うめます, 埋めた うめた and 埋めない うめない. The related intransitive verb is 埋まる うまる: 穴が埋まった (あながうまった), "the hole was filled."

## How the kanji combine

[埋](../kanji/埋.md) supplies the native stem う; める is written in kana. This article teaches the reading うめる. JMdict has a separate 埋める entry read うずめる, including burying one's face or covering something; the identical spelling does not make the readings freely interchangeable in every expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[詰める](詰める.md) (つめる) overlaps when packing a space or closing a gap, but also means shortening something or working out details.

[記入](記入.md) (きにゅう) focuses on entering written information: 空欄に名前を記入してください。 (くうらんになまえをきにゅうしてください。) - Please enter your name in the blank field. Here the information 名前 takes を and the field 空欄 takes に, unlike 空欄を埋める, where the blank itself takes を. 記入する does not mean burying a bone.

Digging something up reverses a burial, but is not the opposite of every abstract gap-filling use.

## Distinctive meaning

The object controls the interpretation: filling 空欄 くうらん, a blank field, is not burying a document underground. Do not translate every 埋める as bury, or use the 空欄を埋める example to infer that it replaces 記入する in every construction.
