# 下りる (おりる) — go down or get off

## Meaning

おりる means to go down or get off. 下りる is especially used for descending; 降りる is the normal choice for getting off a vehicle.

階段を下ります。（かいだんをおります。） — I go down the stairs.

次の駅で電車を降ります。（つぎのえきででんしゃをおります。） — I will get off the train at the next station.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) expresses downward movement. 下りる is an ichidan verb: 下ります, 下りて, 下りた. Its conjugation base is 下り（おり）. This is not the godan verb 下る（くだる）.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上る（のぼる）, "go up," contrasts with descending. 乗る（のる）, "get on," contrasts with getting off a vehicle.

## Distinctive meaning

おりる is intransitive even in 電車を降りる: を marks the vehicle left, not an object that someone moves. The spelling distinction helps keep descent and disembarking clear without treating them as unrelated readings.
