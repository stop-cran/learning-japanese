# 壊す (こわす) — break; damage

## Meaning

壊す means to break, damage, or destroy something. It is transitive: the affected object takes を. It does not require that the damage be intentional.

- 弟がおもちゃを壊しました。 (おとうとがおもちゃをこわしました。) — My younger brother broke the toy.
- 古い建物を壊します。 (ふるいたてものをこわします。) — They will demolish the old building.

## How the kanji combine

壊 writes こわ and す is okurigana. It is a godan verb: 壊します (こわします), 壊して (こわして), 壊さない (こわさない). The kana spelling こわす is also useful when the kanji is unfamiliar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

壊れる (こわれる) is the intransitive partner: おもちゃが壊れた, "the toy broke." 直す (なおす), "repair," is a useful opposite in the damaged-object sense. 割る (わる) specifically suggests breaking or splitting something into pieces.

## Distinctive meaning

壊す can describe loss of function without visible fragments, as with a machine. It also extends to spoiling something abstract. お腹を壊す (おなかをこわす) means to get an upset stomach, not physically smash one's abdomen.
