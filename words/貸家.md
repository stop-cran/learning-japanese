# 貸家 (かしや) - house for rent

## Meaning

貸家 is a house offered for rent. The noun identifies the rental property, not the rent payment.

- この貸家には小さな庭があります。 (このかしやにはちいさなにわがあります。) - This house for rent has a small garden.
- 駅の近くで貸家を探しています。 (えきのちかくでかしやをさがしています。) - I am looking for a house to rent near the station.

貸し家, 貸屋, and 貸し屋 also have the reading かしや. The alternative reading かしいえ applies to 貸家 and 貸し家 in the cited entry.

## How the kanji combine

[貸](../kanji/貸.md) supplies 貸し (かし), renting out, from [貸す](貸す.md). [家](../kanji/家.md) is the native や, "house," in the main reading かしや. The first part modifies the second: a house for letting. In the alternative かしいえ, 家 is read いえ instead; neither reading is カ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[貸間](貸間.md) (かしま) is a room for rent. [家賃](家賃.md) (やちん) is the payment for rented housing. 借家 (しゃくや) is another relevant housing term; it can describe a rented house or a house for rent, so the character difference does not establish mutually exclusive property categories.

## Distinctive meaning

貸 points toward letting a property out, but 貸家 is the property itself, not a verb meaning "rent." Learn かしや as the main reading. If you encounter かしいえ, recognize it with 貸家 or 貸し家, not with every alternative spelling listed above.
