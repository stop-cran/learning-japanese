# 危険 (きけん) — danger; dangerous

## Meaning

危険 names danger and also works as a な-adjective. Before a noun, use 危険な:
危険な場所 (きけんなばしょ), "a dangerous place." At the end of a polite sentence, use 危険です.

- この川で泳ぐのは危険です。 (このかわでおよぐのはきけんです。) — Swimming in this river is dangerous.

The verb phrase 泳ぐ followed by の treats swimming as the activity being evaluated.

## How the kanji combine

[危](../kanji/危.md) contributes danger or precariousness.
[険](../kanji/険.md) likewise has a dangerous or perilous sense here.
The related meanings reinforce each other; the on readings are キ + ケン.
The compound does not require a physically steep location.

## Synonyms and antonyms

危ない (あぶない) is a related い-adjective: 危ない場所 (あぶないばしょ), "a dangerous place."
It attaches directly to the noun, without な.
安全 (あんぜん), "safety; safe," gives the opposite evaluation and also uses な before a noun.

## Distinctive meaning

危険 identifies the possibility of harm, not necessarily harm that has already occurred.
It can evaluate an action, as in the river example, as well as a place or object.
Choose the adjective construction correctly rather than transferring 危ない's final い to 危険.
