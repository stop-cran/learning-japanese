# 窓 (まど) — window

## Meaning

窓 is a window in a building or vehicle.
Use it with an action verb when someone opens or closes it, and with の to describe its surroundings:
窓の外 (まどのそと), "outside the window."

- 窓を開けてください。 (まどをあけてください。) — Please open the window.
- 窓の外に鳥がいます。 (まどのそとにとりがいます。) — There is a bird outside the window.

In the first example, 窓 takes を because it is the object being opened.

## How the kanji combine

There is just one written kanji, [窓](../kanji/窓.md), read with the native reading まど.
Its visible internal parts are not separate spoken words in this noun.
Do not try to pronounce it by adding readings for the shapes inside the character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

戸 (と) can mean a door or shutter; it is related building vocabulary, not a universal synonym for 窓.
There is no useful everyday "opposite noun" to window.
For actions, [開ける](開ける.md) あける, "open," contrasts with [閉める](閉める.md) しめる, "close."

## Distinctive meaning

窓口 (まどぐち) is a separate compound meaning a service counter or point of contact.
At a station, 窓口 can be where you speak to staff; simply saying 窓 does not convey that service function.
Likewise, 窓 names the window, not only the material of its pane.
