# 棒 (ぼう) - stick; rod; pole

## Meaning

棒 is a noun for a long, relatively narrow object such as a stick, rod, pole, or baton.
The word does not specify its material: 木の棒 (きのぼう) is a wooden stick, and 金属の棒 (きんぞくのぼう) a metal rod.
Use 棒で to mark it as an instrument.

- 長い棒で地面に線を引きました。 (ながいぼうでじめんにせんをひきました。) - I drew a line on the ground with a long stick.
- 箱の中に短い棒が三本あります。 (はこのなかにみじかいぼうがさんぼんあります。) - There are three short rods in the box.

本 counts these long objects: 三本 is さんぼん.

## How the kanji combine

棒 represents the whole noun with the on-reading ボウ.
Although many sticks are wooden, the written 木 component is not a restriction to wood.
The full word can describe rods made from other materials.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[枝](枝.md) (えだ) is a branch, defined by its relation to a tree.
A detached branch may serve as a 棒, but the words focus on different properties.
[線](線.md) (せん) is a line; 棒 also has a separate line/dash sense in suitable written or drawn contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

English chooses "stick," "rod," or "pole" according to size, material, and use.
Japanese 棒 alone may leave those details unstated.
Use modifiers to make the intended object clear rather than assuming that every 棒 is a small wooden stick.
