# 水平線 (すいへいせん) - water horizon; horizontal line

## Meaning

水平線 is the horizon where the surface of a sea or other large body of water appears to meet the sky. Digital Daijisen also records the geometric sense "horizontal line."

- 海の向こうの水平線を眺めました。 (うみのむこうのすいへいせんをながめました。) - I gazed at the horizon across the sea.
- 紙に水平線を引いてください。 (かみにすいへいせんをひいてください。) - Please draw a horizontal line on the paper.

The second example uses the geometric sense, not a request to draw a seascape.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md) and [平](../kanji/平.md) form [水平](水平.md), horizontal or level; [線](../kanji/線.md) contributes line. Read スイ + ヘイ + セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[地平線](地平線.md) ordinarily identifies the land horizon; 水平線 identifies the water horizon. Both can be "horizon" in English. [線](線.md) is a line more generally, without specifying that it is horizontal.

## Distinctive meaning

The water-horizon sense is the selected JMdict sense. The horizontal-line use is separately supported by Digital Daijisen, not a second sense invented for this JMdict entry. Keep the distinction between 水平, an orientation, and 水平線, a line or horizon.
