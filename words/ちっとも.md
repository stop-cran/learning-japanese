# ちっとも - not in the least

## Meaning

ちっとも emphasizes a negative: "not at all," "not a bit," or "not in the
least." Use it with a negative verb or adjective, not an ordinary positive
statement.

- この問題はちっとも分かりません。 (このもんだいはちっともわかりません。) - I do not understand this problem at all.
- 今日はちっとも寒くないです。 (きょうはちっともさむくないです。) - It is not at all cold today.

## How the kanji combine

Write it in hiragana, including the small っ. The rare spelling 些とも is
not needed in ordinary writing. The complete expression intensifies the
negative rather than naming a measurable quantity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

少しも (すこしも) with a negative has a similar "not even a little" meaning.
あまり with a negative usually means "not very/much," which is weaker.
[それほど](それほど.md) can qualify a particular degree rather than deny it entirely.

## Distinctive meaning

ちっとも can convey the speaker's disappointment or insistence, but it need
not express anger. The negative ending is essential: ちっとも by itself does
not turn a positive predicate into a negative one.
