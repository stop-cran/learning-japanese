# 待合室 (まちあいしつ) - waiting room

## Meaning

待合室 is a waiting room, such as one at a station or clinic. It names the
room, not the act of waiting or a promise to meet someone.

- 待合室で名前を呼ばれるまで待ちました。 (まちあいしつでなまえをよばれるまでまちました。) - I waited in the waiting room until my name was called.
- 駅の待合室には椅子があります。 (えきのまちあいしつにはいすがあります。) - There are chairs in the station's waiting room.

Use で for an activity there, and に for its location or contents.

## How the kanji combine

[待](../kanji/待.md) contributes waiting, [合](../kanji/合.md) meeting
or coming together, and [室](../kanji/室.md) a room. 待合 (まちあい)
modifies 室 (しつ): a room for waiting.
This is a native-first mixed reading, まちあい + シツ, not タイ + ゴウ + シツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[部屋](部屋.md) (へや), room, is broader.
[会議室](会議室.md) (かいぎしつ) is a meeting room; its purpose is not
simply to accommodate people awaiting their turn.

## Distinctive meaning

A 待合室 need not be reserved for people waiting for one another.
Patients waiting to be called and passengers waiting for transport also
use one. Keep the room distinct from [待ち合わせる](待ち合わせる.md)
(まちあわせる), meeting by prior arrangement.
