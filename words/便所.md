# 便所 (べんじょ) - toilet; restroom

## Meaning

便所 is a toilet or restroom: a place for relieving oneself.
The reading here is べんじょ.

- 公園の便所はあそこです。 (こうえんのべんじょはあそこです。) - The park's restroom is over there.
- 便所に行ってきます。 (べんじょにいってきます。) - I'm going to the toilet; I'll be back.

## How the kanji combine

[便](../kanji/便.md) has an excretion association in this word;
[所](../kanji/所.md) contributes place. ベン + ショ becomes べんじょ,
with voiced ジョ. Do not translate it mechanically as a place for convenience.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[トイレ](トイレ.md) names the same kind of facility.
[お手洗い](お手洗い.md) (おてあらい) is a courteous alternative when asking
where the restroom is; [手洗い](手洗い.md) (てあらい) also has a restroom sense
besides handwashing.

## Distinctive meaning

便所 is a plain, direct name. Choosing お手洗い when asking a stranger is a
useful polite option; that does not make every occurrence of 便所 an insult.
English "bathroom" can mean a toilet room, but 便所 does not thereby mean
a room for taking a bath.
