# ヘリコプター - helicopter

## Meaning

ヘリコプター is a helicopter, an aircraft with powered rotating blades providing lift.
ヘリ is an established short form.

- ヘリコプターが山の上を飛んでいます。 (ヘリコプターがやまのうえをとんでいます。) - A helicopter is flying over the mountain.
- 救助のヘリコプターが到着しました。 (きゅうじょのヘリコプターがとうちゃくしました。) - The rescue helicopter arrived.

## How the word is formed

This katakana loanword has a long final vowel: ヘリコプター.
Its written parts do not form a Japanese kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飛行機](飛行機.md) (ひこうき) is an airplane.
Both are aircraft, but a helicopter and an airplane are not the same vehicle.
[交通機関](交通機関.md) (こうつうきかん) is the broader transport-system term.

## Distinctive meaning

The familiar abbreviation ヘリ is useful in expressions such as 救助ヘリ
(きゅうじょヘリ), rescue helicopter. Neither the full word nor the abbreviation
means rescue equipment in general.
