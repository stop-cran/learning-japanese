# 以後 (いご) - after; from then on

## Meaning

以後 refers to the time after a reference point. That point can be an earlier
event, a stated date or time, or the present. Used on its own, it can mean
"from now on."

- 卒業以後、彼には会っていません。 (そつぎょういご、かれにはあっていません。) - I have not seen him since graduation.
- 以後、気をつけます。 (いご、きをつけます。) - I will be more careful from now on.

それ以後 (それいご) means "since then; after that." Before another noun,
以後の予定 (いごのよてい) means "plans from that point onward."

## How the kanji combine

[以](../kanji/以.md) marks a reference point in this expression, and
[後](../kanji/後.md) contributes the later side of time. The on readings are
イ + ゴ. 已後 is a dictionary-listed rare spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[以降](以降.md) (いこう) overlaps in "from a point onward."
[今後](今後.md) (こんご) explicitly starts from now.
[以来](以来.md) (いらい) is useful for what has held or happened since an earlier event.
[以前](以前.md) (いぜん), "before; previously," supplies the opposite temporal direction.

## Distinctive meaning

For a date or clock time, 以後 normally includes the stated boundary:
三時以後 (さんじいご) is from three o'clock onward. Context can also use 以後
for the period following an event without including the event itself.
Do not turn the usual boundary convention into an absolute rule for every context.
