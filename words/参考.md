# 参考 (さんこう) - reference

## Meaning

参考 is reference or consultation: using information to help think, decide, or
do something. Learn it as a noun in the constructions Nを参考にする, "use N as
a reference," and 参考になる, "be helpful as a reference."

- 先輩の意見を参考にしました。 (せんぱいのいけんをさんこうにしました。) - I used my senior colleague's opinion as a guide.
- この説明はとても参考になりました。 (このせつめいはとてもさんこうになりました。) - This explanation was very helpful.

The first sentence describes using a resource. The second evaluates how useful
the information was.

## How the kanji combine

[参](../kanji/参.md) contributes consulting or referring here, and
[考](../kanji/考.md) contributes thinking or considering. Together they describe
bringing information into one's consideration. The readings are サン + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

参照 (さんしょう) focuses on referring to or consulting something, for example
a specified page. 参考 can include taking another person's experience as guidance.
[勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) is studying; a 参考書 (さんこうしょ), "reference book
or study guide," is one possible resource for it.

## Distinctive meaning

Using something as 参考 does not mean copying it exactly or agreeing with
everything in it. 参考までに (さんこうまでに), "for your reference," offers
information for consideration. Do not turn the English gloss "consultation" into
an assumed medical appointment or treat 参考 as a direct substitute for a
conversation with an adviser.
