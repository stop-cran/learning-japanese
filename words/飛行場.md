# 飛行場 (ひこうじょう) - airfield; airport

## Meaning

飛行場 is an airfield or airport: a place equipped for aircraft to take off
and land. The word does not by itself promise a large passenger terminal.

- 飛行場で小さな飛行機を見ました。 (ひこうじょうでちいさなひこうきをみました。) - I saw a small airplane at the airfield.

で marks the location of the observation.

## How the kanji combine

[飛](../kanji/飛.md), "fly," and [行](../kanji/行.md), "go," form 飛行
(ひこう), "flight." [場](../kanji/場.md), read ジョウ here, adds "place."
Thus 飛行 + 場 gives ひこうじょう, a place for flight operations.

## Synonyms and antonyms

空港 (くうこう), "airport," is the ordinary choice for a passenger's airport
journey. 飛行場 has a broader airfield focus; their reference can overlap.
駅 (えき), "station," belongs to a different transport system, not an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep 飛行場 separate from 飛行機 (ひこうき), "airplane": the final element
distinguishes a place from a machine. Neither the spelling nor the meaning
requires a specifically military airfield.
