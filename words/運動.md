# 運動 (うんどう) — physical exercise

## Meaning

運動 is physical exercise or activity. Add する for "exercise": 運動します, 運動しない, 運動して. The noun also appears with をする.

- 毎朝、公園で運動します。 (まいあさ、こうえんでうんどうします。) — I exercise in the park every morning.
- 最近、運動が足りません。 (さいきん、うんどうがたりません。) — I have not been getting enough exercise recently.

## How the kanji combine

[運](../kanji/運.md) conveys carrying or movement, and [動](../kanji/動.md) means moving. The on readings うん + どう form a word associated with movement and activity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

スポーツ commonly refers to sports, often organized games or athletic disciplines. 運動 is broader: walking for fitness can count. 休息 (きゅうそく), "rest," provides a useful activity contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The same word can mean a social campaign or movement, as in 平和運動 (へいわうんどう), "peace movement," or motion in science. These are related dictionary senses, not claims that every 運動 involves working out.
