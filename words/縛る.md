# 縛る (しばる) - tie up; restrict freedom

## Meaning

縛る is to tie, bind, or fasten something with a rope or cord. It also means to restrict someone's freedom, as with rules or obligations. It is a transitive godan verb: **物を縛る**, "tie something up."

- 古い新聞をひもで縛りました。
  (ふるいしんぶんをひもでしばりました。) - I tied up the old newspapers with string.
- 細かい規則で人を縛りたくありません。
  (こまかいきそくでひとをしばりたくありません。) - I do not want to tie people down with detailed rules.

In the first example, を marks what is tied and で the material used. Polite 縛ります is しばります; the て-form is **縛って** (しばって), not しばりて.

## How the kanji combine

縛 is the single character for the native verb しばる. The ending る is okurigana; there is no multi-character compound to split. Its on reading バク occurs in 束縛 (そくばく), "restraint," but does not replace しば in this verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結ぶ](結ぶ.md) (むすぶ) includes tying or making a connection. 縛る especially suggests binding something together or preventing free movement; the verbs overlap when tying a bundle.

[制限](制限.md) (せいげん), "restriction; limit," overlaps with the figurative sense. 縛られる (しばられる), "be bound/restricted," can describe being constrained by time or rules, not only someone being physically tied.

## Distinctive meaning

縛る can apply to ordinary bundles, so it does not always imply imprisonment or violence. Conversely, a figurative statement about being tied down does not imply literal rope. Do not use the godan ending as though this were an ichidan verb: the negative is 縛らない (しばらない).
