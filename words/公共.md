# 公共 (こうきょう) - public; communal

## Meaning

公共 concerns society, the community, or the public as a whole,
rather than only an individual's private affairs.
It is a noun and can modify another noun with の; it also occurs
in compounds such as 公共施設 (こうきょうしせつ), "public facilities."

- 図書館は公共の施設です。 (としょかんはこうきょうのしせつです。) - The library is a public facility.
- 公共の利益を考えて判断しました。 (こうきょうのりえきをかんがえてはんだんしました。) - We made the decision with the public interest in mind.

公共交通機関 (こうきょうこうつうきかん) means public transport.
The association is with shared or public use, not a claim that every
operator is government-owned.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md) contributes public, and [共](../kanji/共.md)
contributes together or shared.
Their related meanings reinforce public or common concerns.
The on readings combine as コウ + キョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[社会](社会.md) (しゃかい) is society, and [世間](世間.md) (せけん)
can be the social world or public. These help explain the noun association.
[公立](公立.md) (こうりつ) identifies public establishment or operation.
[共同](共同.md) (きょうどう) concerns acting or working jointly.
Private interests provide a useful contrast to public ones.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not automatically translate every standalone "community" as 公共.
The source gloss is useful for society-wide or common concerns, not
as the unrestricted name of any neighborhood group.
公共 also does not mean that a facility is free of charge or open without rules.
