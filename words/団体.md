# 団体 (だんたい) - group; organization

## Meaning

団体 is a noun for a group considered as a unit, or an organization/association. It can describe a travel party as well as a continuing body with a shared purpose.

- 博物館を団体で見学しました。 (はくぶつかんをだんたいでけんがくしました。) - We visited the museum as a group.
- 地域のボランティア団体に参加しています。 (ちいきのボランティアだんたいにさんかしています。) - I participate in a local volunteer organization.

団体で means "as a group." 団体に入る (だんたいにはいる) can mean joining an organization.

## How the kanji combine

団 contributes a group or collective, and [体](../kanji/体.md) contributes a body or unified whole. The compound presents people as a collective body, not as the physical bodies of its members.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会](会.md) (かい) overlaps when it names an association. [組織](組織.md) (そしき) can emphasize organization or structure. 個人 (こじん), "individual," is a useful contrast in expressions such as individual versus group applications.

## Distinctive meaning

団体 does not guarantee legal incorporation, a commercial purpose, or a permanent membership. Check the modifier and setting: 団体料金 (だんたいりょうきん) is a group rate, while 団体の代表 (だんたいのだいひょう) is a group's representative. A discount for a 団体 may refer simply to visitors arriving together.
