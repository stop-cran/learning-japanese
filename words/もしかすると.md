# もしかすると - perhaps; maybe

## Meaning

もしかすると is an adverb introducing a tentative possibility:
perhaps, possibly, or maybe.

- もしかすると、彼はもう帰ったかもしれません。 (もしかすると、かれはもうかえったかもしれません。) - Perhaps he has already gone home.
- もしかすると、この道が近道ではないですか。 (もしかすると、このみちがちかみちではないですか。) - Might this road be a shortcut?

It can be used with かもしれない, but it is not restricted to that ending.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. 若しかすると is a rare recognized spelling, not a
reason to interpret 若 as "young" in this expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もしかしたら](もしかしたら.md) is a close alternative, not a
categorically different degree of probability.
[かもしれない](かもしれない.md) expresses possibility at the predicate;
もしかすると introduces the tentative guess earlier in the sentence.
[おそらく](おそらく.md) often corresponds to "probably."

## Distinctive meaning

The presence of すると does not make this a report that someone actually
did something. Read the whole established expression.
"By some chance" here can express uncertainty; it does not make every
use a statement of an already established coincidence.
