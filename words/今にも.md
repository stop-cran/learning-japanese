# 今にも (いまにも) - at any moment; on the verge of

## Meaning

今にも is an adverb indicating that something seems about to happen. It is useful with verb-stem + そうだ, "looks as though it will," to describe an imminent event.

- 今にも雨が降りそうです。 (いまにもあめがふりそうです。) - It looks as though it could start raining any moment.
- その子は今にも泣きそうでした。 (そのこはいまにもなきそうでした。) - The child looked on the verge of tears.

For these verbs, use 降りそう and 泣きそう, not the dictionary forms followed by そう. The second example describes an impression at a past moment; it does not assert that the child actually cried.

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) is read いま, "now." The following にも is part of the established adverbial expression. Learn the phrase as a unit rather than translating each particle separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もうすぐ](もうすぐ.md), "soon," can describe an expected event on a schedule. 今にも emphasizes being right on the verge of an event. [今](今.md) alone identifies the present time without expressing imminence.

## Distinctive meaning

The translation "at any moment" does not mean "whenever you like." 今にも describes how close an event appears to be, not permission to choose a time. Dark clouds or a child's expression provide natural contexts because they suggest something is about to occur.
