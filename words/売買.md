# 売買 (ばいばい) - buying and selling; trade

## Meaning

売買 is buying and selling, or trade in something. It is a noun that also takes する; with 売買する, を marks what is traded.

- この会社は中古車を売買しています。 (このかいしゃはちゅうこしゃをばいばいしています。) - This company buys and sells used cars.
- 土地の売買には契約が必要です。 (とちのばいばいにはけいやくがひつようです。) - A contract is needed for buying and selling land.

It can describe an individual transaction or buying and selling as an activity. The word by itself does not say whether the transaction is legal, profitable, or international.

## How the kanji combine

[売](../kanji/売.md) means sell, and [買](../kanji/買.md) means buy. This is an opposite-action pair covering the two sides of trade. Both use the on reading ばい, giving ばい + ばい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[売る](売る.md) (うる) means sell, while [買う](買う.md) (かう) means buy. 売買 combines the two sides. [商売](商売.md) (しょうばい) and [商業](商業.md) (しょうぎょう) overlap in business and commerce; [貿易](貿易.md) (ぼうえき) specifically concerns international trade.

## Distinctive meaning

売買 is not limited to a shop's physical goods: shares and land can also be traded. Keep it separate from [バイバイ](バイバイ.md), the casual goodbye with the same pronunciation. In 売買契約 (ばいばいけいやく), "sale/purchase contract," the kanji identify the commercial meaning.
