# 滞在 (たいざい) - stay; temporary residence

## Meaning

滞在 is a stay in a place for a period of time, for example during travel or
work away from home. 滞在する is intransitive. Mark the place with に and
put the duration before the verb.

- 京都に三日間滞在しました。 (きょうとにみっかかんたいざいしました。) - I stayed in Kyoto for three days.
- 日本に滞在している間、日本語を勉強します。 (にほんにたいざいしているあいだ、にほんごをべんきょうします。) - I will study Japanese while staying in Japan.

滞在中 (たいざいちゅう) means "during one's stay" or "currently staying."

## How the kanji combine

滞 contributes remaining or staying, and [在](../kanji/在.md) contributes being
present in a place. Their related meanings describe remaining somewhere.
The on readings are タイ + ザイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[泊まる](泊まる.md) (とまる) and [宿泊](宿泊.md) (しゅくはく) focus on an
overnight stay or lodging. 滞在 focuses on the period spent in a place,
such as a city or country, rather than merely the night's accommodation.

## Distinctive meaning

滞在 does not specify the purpose of the stay or a particular duration.
A person may stay for business, study, or sightseeing. Learn 場所に滞在する
as a complete pattern; the place is not a direct を-object, even though
English may say someone "spent three days in" it.
