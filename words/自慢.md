# 自慢 (じまん) - pride; boasting

## Meaning

自慢 is pride in something connected with oneself, or boasting about it. 自慢する is a transitive suru verb: **thing を自慢する**. 自慢の + noun describes something one is proud of.

- 彼は新しい車を自慢しています。 (かれはあたらしいくるまをじまんしています。) - He is showing off his new car.
- この庭は祖母の自慢です。 (このにわはそぼのじまんです。) - This garden is my grandmother's pride and joy.

The first example describes behavior; the second names a source of pride. Neither construction needs な before a following noun.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) contributes self, and 慢 contributes self-satisfaction or boastfulness here. Together they concern pride associated with oneself. The on readings are ジ + マン; the components are a meaning aid, not a claim about the word's historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[得意](得意.md) (とくい) can describe being good at something or feeling proud. 自信 (じしん) is confidence in one's ability; it need not involve telling others about an achievement. Neither is a mechanical replacement for 自慢する.

## Distinctive meaning

自慢 can sound critical when someone repeatedly boasts, but it also appears positively: 自慢の料理 (じまんのりょうり), "a dish one is proud of." Let the construction and speaker's attitude guide the English translation rather than translating every occurrence as objectionable bragging.
