# 粒 (つぶ) - grain; bead; drop

## Meaning

粒 is a small grain, bead or drop. It can also count small round objects:
一粒 (ひとつぶ) means one grain, bead or drop, with the material identified
by context.

- 机の上に米が一粒落ちています。
  (つくえのうえにこめがひとつぶおちています。) - There is a grain of rice on the desk.
- 一粒のぶどうを食べました。
  (ひとつぶのぶどうをたべました。) - I ate one grape.

The first sentence counts a unit of rice; the second uses 一粒の before the
thing counted. A grape does not become a cereal grain just because the same
counter is suitable.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [粒](../kanji/粒.md) writes the native word つぶ.
There is no compound or okurigana to divide. Its on reading リュウ appears
in 粒子 (りゅうし), particle, and is also recorded as a counter reading.
That does not make the ordinary noun つぶ freely replaceable by りゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[米](米.md) (こめ) names rice, and [穀物](穀物.md) (こくもつ) names cereal
grains as a category. 粒 instead identifies an individual small unit or
counts it. English grain can cover both kinds of expression.

[水滴](水滴.md) (すいてき) specifically names a drop of water. 粒 is not
limited to water or even to liquid. [粉](粉.md) (こな), powder, describes
finely divided material rather than necessarily one visible grain.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the object and counting pattern together. 一粒の米
(ひとつぶのこめ) is one grain of rice; 米一粒 (こめひとつぶ) is another
way to place the quantity with the noun. The character does not specify a
fixed diameter, a cereal crop or a particular substance.
