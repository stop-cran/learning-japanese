# パターン - pattern; type; recurring behavior

## Meaning

パターン is a pattern, model or type. Depending on context it can concern
a design, a classified form, or a recurring way of acting.
It is a noun, not a verb made merely by borrowing English "pattern."

- 問題をいくつかのパターンに分けました。 (もんだいをいくつかのパターンにわけました。) - I divided the problems into several types.
- 毎回同じ行動パターンを繰り返しています。 (まいかいおなじこうどうパターンをくりかえしています。) - They repeat the same pattern of behavior every time.

行動パターン (こうどうパターン) is a pattern of behavior.
The first example classifies types without requiring a decorative picture.

## How the kanji combine

This is a borrowed katakana word, with no kanji to decompose.
パタン is also a recognized form. Keep the long vowel in the primary
パターン spelling and reading rather than treating the two written forms as different meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[型](型.md) (かた) overlaps for a model or prescribed form.
[模様](模様.md) (もよう) and [柄](柄.md), read がら for a design,
overlap for visual patterns.
[習慣](習慣.md) (しゅうかん) concerns a habit or custom, while
[癖](癖.md) (くせ) can identify an individual tendency.
These overlaps depend on the intended sense, not a universal replacement rule.

## Distinctive meaning

A パターン need not be visual, and a decorative pattern is not automatically a bad habit.
The public dictionary also records a dressmaking pattern or template.
English words that can be verbs do not automatically become Japanese する verbs;
use the construction required by the actual expression.
