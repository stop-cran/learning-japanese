# 全部 (ぜんぶ) — all; the whole

## Meaning

全部 includes the whole amount or every item in the set being discussed.
It can stand as a noun, modify another noun with の, or describe the extent of an action without a particle.

- お菓子を全部食べました。 (おかしをぜんぶたべました。) — I ate all the sweets.
- 全部の箱を開けてください。 (ぜんぶのはこをあけてください。) — Please open all the boxes.
- 全部で千円です。 (ぜんぶでせんえんです。) — It is one thousand yen in total.

The last pattern, 全部で, gives a total across the relevant items.

## How the kanji combine

[全](../kanji/全.md) means whole or all; [部](../kanji/部.md) contributes parts or portions.
Think "all the parts taken together." The on readings combine as ゼン + ブ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すべて, also written 全て, likewise means all or everything; JMdict marks this word as usually written in kana.
一部 いちぶ, "one part; a portion," provides the useful contrast.
全員 ぜんいん specifically takes in all the members of a group, as in 全員来ました
(ぜんいんきました), "Everyone came."

## Distinctive meaning

The scope comes from context. 全部食べました means eating all the food under discussion, not every food in existence.
In the box example, 全部の modifies 箱; in the sweets example, 全部 tells how much was eaten.
Keep these patterns distinct from treating 全部 as a な-adjective.
