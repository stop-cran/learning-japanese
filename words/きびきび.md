# きびきび — brisk and efficient

## Meaning

Lively, crisp, purposeful action rather than sluggish or hesitant movement.

- 店員（てんいん）がきびきび動いている。 — The shop staff are moving briskly and efficiently.
- 彼女はきびきびした動作（どうさ）で道具（どうぐ）を片付（かたづ）けた。 — She put the tools away with brisk, efficient movements.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[はきはき](はきはき.md) overlaps in briskness, often focusing on clear speech. [のろのろ](のろのろ.md) offers a sluggish-movement contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

きびきび動く（うごく）and きびきびした動作 are useful patterns. The impression is often positive, but “efficient” is a manner description here, not a measured productivity result.
