# 警官 (けいかん) — police officer

## Meaning

警官 means a police officer. It names a person rather than the whole police organization.
The same noun can refer to one or more officers when context supplies the number.

- 警官に道を聞きました。 (けいかんにみちをききました。) — I asked a police officer for directions.

Here に marks the person asked, and 道 (みち) is the route or way being asked about.

## How the kanji combine

[警](../kanji/警.md) has a warning or admonition sense and identifies the policing role in this compound.
[官](../kanji/官.md) identifies an official.
The combination names an official in policing; it does not mean any person who gives a warning.
Read the on readings ケイ + カン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

警察官 (けいさつかん) is another word for a police officer.
警察 (けいさつ) principally names the police as an organization, though the dictionary also records abbreviated uses for an officer or a station.
For an unambiguous person label, 警官 or 警察官 is useful.
There is no necessary antonym for this occupation.

## Distinctive meaning

English "the police" may refer to an organization or to officers at a scene. Japanese word choice should follow that intended reference.
警官 points directly to an officer and does not itself specify a particular rank.
It also does not identify a person merely because they are wearing a uniform.
