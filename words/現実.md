# 現実 (げんじつ) — reality

## Meaning

現実 means reality or actuality: what actually exists or happens, rather than what is only imagined or desired.
Use 現実になる for something becoming real.

- 夢が現実になりました。 (ゆめがげんじつになりました。) — My dream became reality.
- 現実を見てください。 (げんじつをみてください。) — Please look at the reality of the situation.

## How the kanji combine

[現](../kanji/現.md) contributes "present; existing; actual" and [実](../kanji/実.md) contributes "reality; truth."
The two reinforce actuality; this word does not use 実 in its fruit sense.
The on readings are ゲン + ジツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

事実 (じじつ), "fact," overlaps with reality but can identify a particular thing that is true.
現実 is useful for the actual situation as a whole.
理想 (りそう), "ideal," supplies a contrast between how things are and how one would like them to be; an ideal can sometimes be realized, so the two are not inherently incompatible.

## Distinctive meaning

現実 can appear in discouraging observations, but it does not itself mean "bad news" or "disappointment."
The dream example shows a welcome reality.
Choose it for actual circumstances, not simply as a replacement for every English use of "really."
