# 収入 (しゅうにゅう) — income

## Meaning

収入 is money received as income, earnings, or revenue. It can describe a person's income or an organization's receipts,
depending on context.

- 去年より収入が増えました。 (きょねんよりしゅうにゅうがふえました。) — My income increased compared with last year.

The sentence reports an increase, but does not say where the money came from.
月の収入 (つきのしゅうにゅう) means "monthly income."

- この仕事は収入が安定しています。 (このしごとはしゅうにゅうがあんていしています。) — This job provides a stable income.

収入がある／ない means "have income/have no income"; it does not by itself identify a particular employer.

## How the kanji combine

[収](../kanji/収.md) contributes taking in or collecting, while [入](../kanji/入.md) contributes coming in.
The compound presents income as money coming in. Its on readings are シュウ + ニュウ;
both syllable sequences contain a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[給料](給料.md) (きゅうりょう) means salary or wages. It identifies pay for employment,
while 収入 can include income from other sources.
支出 (ししゅつ), "expenditure," is the useful opposite direction: money going out.

## Distinctive meaning

収入 is not automatically the money left after expenses.
Someone may receive a substantial income and also have substantial 支出.
In financial material, check the surrounding definitions before treating an income figure as profit or disposable money.
In ordinary conversation, the word can simply answer how much comes in.
