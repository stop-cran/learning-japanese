# 転がる (ころがる) - roll; tumble; lie around

## Meaning

転がる describes rolling or tumbling, falling over, or lying on a surface. It is an intransitive godan verb: 転がります, 転がって, 転がった.

- ボールが坂を転がっていきました。 (ボールがさかをころがっていきました。) - The ball rolled down the slope.
- 畳の上に転がって、本を読みました。 (たたみのうえにころがって、ほんをよみました。) - I lay down on the tatami and read a book.
- 床に鉛筆が転がっています。 (ゆかにえんぴつがころがっています。) - A pencil is lying on the floor.

In the last sentence, 転がっている describes where the pencil lies; it does not require continued motion.

## How the kanji combine

[転](../kanji/転.md) supplies the rolling or turning association and writes ころ here. がる completes the native word. Despite ending in る, 転がる is godan, not ichidan.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[転がす](転がす.md) (ころがす) means causing something to roll or tip over: ボールを転がす. [転ぶ](転ぶ.md) (ころぶ) overlaps in falling or tumbling; [倒れる](倒れる.md) (たおれる) covers falling over or collapsing.

[ごろごろ](ごろごろ.md) can describe rolling or lounging, while [ころころ](ころころ.md) often depicts repeated rolling. The manner words and verb have different grammatical jobs.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every 転がっている as "is rolling." It can mean lying casually or being scattered about. The dictionary also records changes in an outcome, outside the practical selection here. An easy-availability use does not require literal wheels or a round object.

For example, うまい話はそう転がっていない (うまいはなしはそうころがっていない) means "Opportunities that good aren't just lying around." It is not a claim that a story physically rolls.
