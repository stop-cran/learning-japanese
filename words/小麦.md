# 小麦 (こむぎ) - wheat

## Meaning

小麦 is wheat, the cereal crop and its grain.
It is a noun: 小麦を育てる (こむぎをそだてる) means to grow wheat, and
小麦から作る (こむぎからつくる) means to make something from wheat.

- この畑では小麦を育てています。
  (このはたけではこむぎをそだてています。) - We grow wheat in this field.
- このパンには小麦が使われています。
  (このパンにはこむぎがつかわれています。) - Wheat is used in this bread.

小麦粉 (こむぎこ) is wheat flour. The added 粉, "powder; flour," matters:
a bag of flour and the crop it comes from are related, but are not the same form of the ingredient.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) means "small," and 麦 names cereal grains such as wheat or barley.
The native readings are こ + むぎ. Learn 小麦 as the established name of wheat,
not as a description of any small grain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

大麦 (おおむぎ) is barley, a different crop, not an antonym.
[米](米.md) (こめ) is rice. [パン](パン.md) is a food that can be made with wheat flour,
not another name for wheat itself.

## Distinctive meaning

The character 麦 alone does not establish that a product contains wheat rather than
another cereal. The complete word 小麦 identifies wheat specifically.
In ingredient information, retain that distinction instead of translating all grain names alike.
