# 赤ん坊 (あかんぼう)

## Meaning

赤ん坊 means a baby or infant. It is an ordinary noun, not a precise medical age category.

- 赤ん坊が眠っています。 (あかんぼうが ねむっています。) — The baby is sleeping.

Use 赤ん坊の for a noun modifier, as in 赤ん坊の服 (あかんぼうのふく), "baby clothes."

## How the kanji combine

[赤](../kanji/赤.md) contributes the association "red"; 坊 is an ending found in words for people, including children. Learn あかんぼう as a whole expression. The ん is part of the word, not a particle.

## Synonyms and antonyms

赤ちゃん (あかちゃん) is an affectionate everyday alternative. 子供 (こども), "child," covers a wider age range. [大人](大人.md), "adult," provides a useful life-stage contrast rather than a strict antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

赤ん坊 identifies a very young child without itself expressing the tenderness of ちゃん. For addressing a baby affectionately, 赤ちゃん or the child's name is often more suitable.
