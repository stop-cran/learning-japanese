# いろいろ

## Meaning

いろいろ expresses variety: various kinds of things or different ways of doing something.

- いろいろな国の料理を食べました。 (いろいろな くにの りょうりを たべました。) — I ate dishes from various countries.
- いろいろ考えました。 (いろいろ かんがえました。) — I thought about various things.

Use いろいろな before a noun. Adverbially, いろいろ or いろいろと can modify a verb.

## How the kanji combine

This common kana spelling also appears as 色々. The repeated 色 is associated here with kinds or varieties, not just literal colors. 々 is a repetition mark, not a separately pronounced word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

さまざま also describes variety. たくさん emphasizes a large quantity; いろいろ emphasizes different kinds. Neither guarantees the other.

## Distinctive meaning

いろいろな本 means books of various kinds, not merely a large pile of identical books. In conversation, いろいろありました can leave the details unstated: "Various things happened."
