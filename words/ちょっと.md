# ちょっと (ちょっと) - a little; somewhat

## Meaning

ちょっと means "a little," "somewhat," or "for a moment." It often reduces the apparent size of a request or softens an evaluation.

- このかばんはちょっと重いです。 (このかばんはちょっとおもいです。) - This bag is a little heavy.
- ちょっと待ってください。 (ちょっとまってください。) - Please wait a moment.

It modifies the following adjective or action without に. With a noun, ちょっとの時間 (ちょっとのじかん) means "a little time."

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. 一寸 and 鳥渡 are rare written forms for this word; there is no need to interpret their characters literally when using everyday ちょっと.

## Synonyms and antonyms

少し (すこし) is another word for a small amount or degree. たくさん means a lot. [しばらく](しばらく.md) means for a while, with a context-dependent duration.

## Distinctive meaning

ちょっと does not always measure a genuinely small amount. 明日はちょっと… (あしたはちょっと…), "Tomorrow is a bit difficult...", may function as a soft refusal even when the problem is substantial. Conversely, an abrupt ちょっと! can be a sharp call for attention; the word alone does not guarantee politeness.
