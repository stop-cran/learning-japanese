# 台所 (だいどころ) - kitchen

## Meaning

台所 is a kitchen, the room or area where food is prepared. It is an everyday household word.

母は台所で料理をしています。 (はははだいどころでりょうりをしています。) - My mother is cooking in the kitchen.

台所に冷蔵庫があります。 (だいどころにれいぞうこがあります。) - There is a refrigerator in the kitchen.

Use で for an action performed there and に for where something is located.

## How the kanji combine

[台](../kanji/台.md), "stand; platform," combines with [所](../kanji/所.md), "place." This is a mixed reading: on-reading ダイ plus native ところ, voiced to どころ. The repository places on-first mixed compounds in `kango`.

## Synonyms and antonyms

キッチン is another common word for a kitchen. 厨房 (ちゅうぼう) often refers to a cooking facility in a restaurant or other professional setting. 食堂 (しょくどう) is a dining hall or eating place, not the kitchen itself.

## Distinctive meaning

台所 names a function, not a particular size or layout. It also has a figurative financial sense in some expressions, but a household sentence about cooking normally refers to the physical kitchen.
