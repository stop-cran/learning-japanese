# 特に (とくに) -- especially; in particular

## Meaning

特に singles something out as especially relevant or noticeable. It modifies the statement that follows rather than naming an object.

- 果物の中では、特にいちごが好きです。 (くだもののなかでは、とくにいちごがすきです。) -- Among fruits, I especially like strawberries.
- 今日は特に寒いです。 (きょうはとくにさむいです。) -- It is especially cold today.

No extra particle is required after the adverb itself: 特に寒い, not 特にが寒い.

## How the kanji combine

[特](../kanji/特.md), read とく, contributes specialness or distinction. The following に is part of the established adverbial expression. It is not the destination-marking に used after a place.

## Synonyms and antonyms

とりわけ also means especially. とても intensifies degree, as in "very cold," but does not necessarily single the situation out against other cases.

## Distinctive meaning

With a negative expression, 特に can mean "particularly" or "in particular": 特にありません (とくにありません), "Nothing in particular." This is not an affirmative statement of special importance; the negative predicate determines the whole message.
