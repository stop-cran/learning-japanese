# 晩 (ばん) — evening; night

## Meaning

晩 refers to the evening or night part of the day. A preceding noun can identify which evening: 土曜日の晩 (どようびのばん), "Saturday evening."

昨日の晩は家にいました。 (きのうのばんはいえにいました。) — I was at home last night.

## How the kanji combine

[晩](../kanji/晩.md) is a single kanji used with its on reading バン. Familiar compounds include 今晩 (こんばん), "this evening," and 毎晩 (まいばん), "every evening/night."

## Synonyms and antonyms

夜 (よる) is a broad everyday word for night. 夕方 (ゆうがた) points more specifically to late afternoon or early evening. 朝 (あさ), "morning," provides a practical time-of-day contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The English choice between "evening" and "night" depends on context; 晩 does not establish a precise clock boundary. It also occurs in 晩ご飯 (ばんごはん), "evening meal," but 晩 alone should first be learned as a time word.
