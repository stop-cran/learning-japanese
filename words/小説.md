# 小説 (しょうせつ) — novel; fiction

## Meaning

小説 is a novel or a work of prose fiction. Depending on context, it can refer to a short story as well as a long novel.

寝る前に小説を読みます。 (ねるまえにしょうせつをよみます。) — I read fiction before going to sleep.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) is "small" and [説](../kanji/説.md) relates to telling or explanation. The on readings are ショウ + セツ. The established literary meaning is not "a small explanation," and 小 does not require the work to be short.

## Synonyms and antonyms

物語 (ものがたり), "story" or "narrative," is broader and need not designate a novel. 本 (ほん), "book," describes a publication, which can contain fiction or nonfiction.

## Distinctive meaning

短編小説 (たんぺんしょうせつ) is a short story; 長編小説 (ちょうへんしょうせつ) is a long novel. Thus 小説 alone does not settle the work's length. 小説家 (しょうせつか) adds 家 to mean a novelist or fiction writer.
