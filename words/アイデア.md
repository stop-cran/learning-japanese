# アイデア - idea

## Meaning

アイデア is an idea, such as a possible solution, suggestion, or creative approach.
Use アイデアが浮かぶ (アイデアがうかぶ), "an idea comes to mind," and
アイデアを出す (アイデアをだす), "put forward an idea."

- いいアイデアが浮かびました。 (いいアイデアがうかびました。) - A good idea came to mind.
- 会議で新しいアイデアを出しました。 (かいぎであたらしいアイデアをだしました。) - I put forward a new idea at the meeting.

It is a noun, not a verb meaning "think"; do not add する to it as a general translation of thinking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[アイディア](アイディア.md) is another spelling of the same word, not a different kind of idea.
[考え](考え.md) (かんがえ) is broader, also covering views and intentions.
[案](案.md) (あん) often presents an idea as a proposal for consideration.

## Distinctive meaning

Having an アイデア does not imply that a detailed [計画](計画.md) (けいかく), "plan,"
has been prepared or approved. The word can refer to a small practical suggestion as well as
an inventive concept; it does not guarantee originality.
