# 微笑む (ほほえむ) - smile

## Meaning

微笑む means to smile. It is an intransitive godan verb ending in む: 微笑みます, 微笑んで, 微笑んだ. **Person に微笑む** means to smile at someone.

- 母は子供に優しく微笑みました。 (はははこどもにやさしくほほえみました。) - The mother smiled gently at her child.
- 彼は写真を見て微笑んでいました。 (かれはしゃしんをみてほほえんでいました。) - He was smiling as he looked at the photograph.

In the second example, 写真を belongs to 見る, "look at"; it is not a direct object of 微笑む.

## How the kanji combine

微 contributes slightness or subtlety, and [笑](../kanji/笑.md) contributes smiling or laughing. The spelling suits a smile, but learn the complete native reading ほほえむ, not a concatenation of on readings. The ending む changes in conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[笑う](笑う.md) (わらう) can mean either laugh or smile, so it overlaps with 微笑む. [笑顔](笑顔.md) (えがお) names a smiling face rather than the action of smiling. Crying is a possible contextual contrast, not an exact opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

微笑む specifies a smile rather than an audible laugh. A smile does not automatically prove happiness or kindness: 優しく in the first example supplies the gentle manner explicitly. The dictionary also records ほおえむ; this card teaches ほほえむ.
