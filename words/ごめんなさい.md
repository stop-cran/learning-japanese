# ごめんなさい (ごめんなさい) - I am sorry

## Meaning

ごめんなさい is an apology: "I'm sorry" or "Please forgive me."
It can stand alone or follow an action in the て-form to identify what you
are apologizing for.

- 遅れてごめんなさい。 (おくれてごめんなさい。) - I'm sorry I'm late.
- A: 約束を忘れてごめんなさい。 (やくそくをわすれてごめんなさい。) - I'm sorry I forgot our arrangement.
- B: 大丈夫ですよ。 (だいじょうぶですよ。) - It's all right.

The reply belongs to the other speaker; ごめんなさい itself does not mean
that the apology has been accepted.

## How the kanji combine

This expression is usually written in kana. Spellings such as 御免なさい also
occur in the dictionary. The Sino-Japanese element ごめん is combined with the
native ending なさい, so this is a mixed expression, not a loan from English.
Learn it as a conventional apology rather than constructing it afresh as a command.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すみません](すみません.md) also apologizes and can additionally call for
someone's attention. Short ごめん is more casual.
[謝る](謝る.md) (あやまる) is the verb "apologize," not the apology formula
you substitute directly in this exchange.

## Distinctive meaning

The English "excuse me" is broader than this expression. ごめんなさい naturally
acknowledges something regrettable; it is not a universal phrase for attracting
a server's attention. It has a polite ending, but a formal professional apology
may call for wording such as 申し訳ありません (もうしわけありません).
Relationship and seriousness matter alongside the ending.
