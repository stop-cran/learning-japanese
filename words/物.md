# 物 (もの) — thing; object

## Meaning

物 means a **thing, object, or substance**. It is useful when an object's specific name is unimportant or unknown.

- 重い物を持たないでください。 (おもいものをもたないでください。) — Please don't carry heavy things.
- これは私の物です。 (これはわたしのものです。) — This is mine.

In the second example, person + の + 物 identifies a possession. The word does not itself distinguish one object from several.

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md) is a single character read with the native word もの. Other vocabulary uses on readings, as in 動物 (どうぶつ), “animal”; those readings do not replace もの here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

こと refers to an event, matter, or fact rather than ordinarily naming a physical object. 品物 (しなもの) often means goods or articles. 者 (もの) is a different word for a person, despite sharing the pronunciation.

## Distinctive meaning

The physical-object contrast with こと is a starting point, not a rule for every construction. Kana もの also occurs in grammatical expressions such as ものだ. Those uses need their own explanation rather than being translated mechanically as “object.”
