# 巡る (めぐる) - go around; recur; concern

## Meaning

巡る is a godan る verb for going around or making a circuit.
Its related uses include surrounding something, coming around again or circulating,
touring places, and concerning a matter.

- 池の周りを巡りました。 (いけのまわりをめぐりました。) - I went around the pond.
- 古い寺を巡りました。 (ふるいてらをめぐりました。) - I toured old temples.
- 春がまた巡ってきました。 (はるがまためぐってきました。) - Spring has come around again.
- 環境問題をめぐって話し合いました。 (かんきょうもんだいをめぐってはなしあいました。) - We discussed environmental issues.

For the surrounding use, 城の周りを堀が巡る
(しろのまわりをほりがめぐる) means "a moat surrounds the castle."
For circulation, 血が全身を巡る (ちがぜんしんをめぐる) means "blood circulates throughout the body."

## How the kanji combine

巡 carries the going-around association in this native verb.
The ending る is godan: 巡ります (めぐります), 巡って (めぐって),
巡った (めぐった), 巡らない (めぐらない).
The matter-related use is usually written in kana, as in 問題をめぐって.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[回る](回る.md) (まわる) overlaps in going around and making rounds.
[循環](循環.md) (じゅんかん) overlaps in circulation or cycles.
[関する](関する.md) (かんする) and [関わる](関わる.md) (かかわる)
can overlap in concerning a matter.
There is no single antonym covering movement, recurrence, and the matter-related construction.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records rare spellings 回る, 廻る, and 繞る for めぐる.
That does not turn the existing word 回る, read まわる, into the same lexical entry.
In Nをめぐって, the issue is the focus of a discussion or dispute; no physical circuit is required.
The を in the pond and moat examples marks the area traversed or encircled;
it does not by itself make this an ordinary transitive "move an object" verb.
