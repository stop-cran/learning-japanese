# じゃま — hindrance; interference

## Meaning

じゃま is a hindrance or something in the way. It is a noun and na-adjective: じゃまな物 (じゃまなもの), "something obstructing the way." じゃまする means to interrupt or interfere with something.

- この箱はじゃまです。 (このはこはじゃまです。) — This box is in the way.
- 勉強のじゃまをしないでください。 (べんきょうのじゃまをしないでください。) — Please don't interrupt my studying.

Use noun + のじゃまをする or noun + をじゃまする for the activity being disturbed.

## How the kanji combine

The kanji spelling is 邪魔, with on-readings ジャ + マ. 邪 contributes wrongness or evil, and 魔 a demon or obstructing force. The dictionary records an original Buddhist sense of a demon hindering practice. The modern everyday meaning is obstruction; you do not need to translate its individual characters in ordinary conversation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

妨げる (さまたげる) means to hinder or obstruct. [手伝う](手伝う.md) (てつだう), "help," contrasts with interfering, though it is not an exact opposite in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

おじゃまします is a conventional expression when entering someone's home: approximately "Excuse my intrusion." It does not announce that you intend to cause trouble. This visitor expression is distinct from calling a person or object a nuisance.
