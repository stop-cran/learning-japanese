# いちいち - one by one; every single

## Meaning

いちいち considers items separately, one by one, or stresses attending to
every single detail without omission. It normally works directly before
the action it qualifies.

- 先生は質問にいちいち答えました。 (せんせいはしつもんにいちいちこたえました。) - The teacher answered the questions one by one.
- 小さな間違いをいちいち指摘しないでください。 (ちいさなまちがいをいちいちしてきしないでください。) - Please do not point out every single little mistake.

The first describes complete individual attention. The second can convey
irritation at repeated or overly detailed criticism.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. 一々 and 一一 are recognized written forms.
[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," repeats to suggest considering items individually.
The mark 々 repeats the preceding kanji; it is not a second independent character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[それぞれ](それぞれ.md), "each; respectively," also distributes attention
among items. [一人一人](一人一人.md) (ひとりひとり) specifically concerns people.
[一度に](一度に.md) (いちどに), "all at once; in one go," offers a useful
contrast to processing things separately.

## Distinctive meaning

いちいち does not inherently mean annoyance: answering every question carefully
can be helpful. In a complaint it can emphasize the burden of doing something
every time or attending to tiny details. Read the whole sentence and tone instead
of treating it as either an always-rude expression or a precise numerical counter.
