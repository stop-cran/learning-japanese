# 叙情 (じょじょう) — lyricism; description of one's feelings

## Meaning

叙情 means expressing one's own feelings. JMdict glosses it as “lyricism; description of one's feelings,” and Digital Daijisen defines it as stating and expressing one's own feelings. JMdict also lists the spelling 抒情.

- 彼女の歌は叙情にあふれています。 (かのじょのうたはじょじょうにあふれています。) — Her songs are full of lyricism.
- 秋の景色を見ていると、叙情的な気分になります。 (あきのけしきをみていると、じょじょうてきなきぶんになります。) — Looking at the autumn scenery puts me in a lyrical mood.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[叙](../kanji/叙.md) means stating or expressing, and [情](../kanji/情.md) means feelings. Digital Daijisen lists 叙情 under the sense of 叙 used in place of 抒, expressing one's thoughts. Together, they mean expressing one's feelings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[感情](感情.md) (かんじょう) and [気持ち](気持ち.md) (きもち) are the feelings themselves; 叙情 is the expressing of them. [表現](表現.md) (ひょうげん) is expression in general. 叙情詩 じょじょうし is a lyric poem (JMdict entry 1344890), and 叙情的 じょじょうてき means lyrical or poetic (JMdict entry 2871136).

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen cross-refers 叙情 to 叙事 じょじ, which JMdict glosses as “narration; description of things and facts” (entry 1344840).
