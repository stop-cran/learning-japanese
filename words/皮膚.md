# 皮膚 (ひふ) - skin

## Meaning

皮膚 is skin, the body's covering. It is a noun used in anatomical
descriptions and in ordinary discussion of skin conditions.
皮膚の病気 (ひふのびょうき) means "a skin disease."

- 皮膚が乾燥しています。 (ひふがかんそうしています。) - My skin is dry.
- この薬は皮膚に塗ります。 (このくすりはひふにぬります。) - Apply this medicine to the skin.

In the second example, に marks the surface receiving the medicine.
皮膚 itself does not specify a particular disease or treatment.

## How the kanji combine

[皮](../kanji/皮.md) and [膚](../kanji/膚.md) both contribute skin
or covering associations. The related meanings reinforce each other;
the on readings combine as ヒ + フ. This is not the native noun かわ
with another reading added to its end.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[肌](肌.md) (はだ) overlaps in the skin sense and is also used for
surface texture or disposition. [皮](皮.md) (かわ) has a broader
range of coverings, including animal skin and fruit peel.

These distinctions do not create an absolute division in every
description of human skin. There is no direct antonym required for
this body-covering noun.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the complete word in its context rather than treating each
character as a separate technical layer of the skin. 皮膚 does not
mean fruit peel simply because 皮 can have that meaning on its own.
Conversely, 肌 is not forbidden from anatomical contexts.
