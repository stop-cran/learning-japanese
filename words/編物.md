# 編物 (あみもの) - knitting; knitted work

## Meaning

編物, also written 編み物, means knitting or the material or work made by knitting.
The dictionary also includes crochet, so it is not restricted to work with two knitting needles.

- 趣味は編み物です。 (しゅみはあみものです。) - My hobby is knitting.
- 一週間かけて編み物を仕上げました。 (いっしゅうかんかけてあみものをしあげました。) - I spent a week finishing my knitting.

編み物をする means "do some knitting." The second example can refer to the piece being made,
not just the abstract activity.

## How the kanji combine

[編](../kanji/編.md) connects with [編む](編む.md) (あむ), "knit,"
whose connecting stem is 編み (あみ). [物](../kanji/物.md) is もの, "thing."
The full reading is あみもの even in the spelling 編物, which omits the written み.
This is a native stem-plus-noun formation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[編む](編む.md) is the verb: マフラーを編む (マフラーをあむ), "knit a scarf."
縫う (ぬう), "sew," concerns joining with stitches rather than the same process.
These crafts are related, but their words are not unrestricted substitutes.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list's English "web" is not a reason to use 編物 for the internet or any spider's web.
The teaching sense is knitting, knitted material or crochet.
Keep the noun 編物 separate from 編む's additional book-compilation meaning.
