# 品物 (しなもの) — item, goods

## Meaning

品物 refers to an item or goods, often something handled, delivered, or sold.

- この店にはいろいろな品物があります。（この みせには いろいろな しなものが あります。）— This shop has a variety of goods.
- 品物は明日届きます。（しなものは あした とどきます。）— The goods will arrive tomorrow.

## How the kanji combine

[品](../kanji/品.md) しな means an article or goods; [物](../kanji/物.md) もの means a thing. Their related senses reinforce the idea of a tangible item.

Both are native kun readings, so this is a native compound, not an on-plus-on word. There is no okurigana between the two kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 商品 しょうひん emphasizes goods as merchandise for sale.
- 物 もの is the broader everyday word "thing" and also has abstract or grammatical uses.
- 製品 せいひん emphasizes a manufactured product.
- There is no natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

品物 is more specific than English "thing": it commonly refers to a concrete article, not a situation or abstract matter. It need not already be for sale, so 商品 is not always an exact replacement.

Japanese does not mark singular or plural here; context decides whether 品物 means one item or several goods.
