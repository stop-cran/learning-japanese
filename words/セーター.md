# セーター - sweater; jumper

## Meaning

セーター is a sweater, called a jumper in British English.
It names a knitted upper-body garment, not every warm piece of clothing.

- 寒いので、セーターを着ました。 (さむいので、セーターをきました。) - I put on a sweater because it was cold.

Use 着る (きる) for putting it on or wearing it.
セーターを着ています (セーターをきています) describes having it on.

## How the kanji combine

This English-derived word is written in katakana.
Both long-vowel marks matter: セーター. The dictionary records other
transcriptions, but セーター is the normal form to learn and use here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

カーディガン names a cardigan, usually opening at the front.
コート is a coat, a different outer garment.
ニット can describe knitwear more broadly; these are clothing categories,
not strict antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Describe material with の: 毛のセーター (けのセーター), "a wool sweater."
The loanword follows Japanese grammar, so it does not change for plural
reference. For taking a sweater off, use 脱ぐ (ぬぐ).
