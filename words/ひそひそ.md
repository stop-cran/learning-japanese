# ひそひそ — whispering; in low voices

## Meaning

ひそひそ describes whispering or speaking in undertones. It can also convey secrecy or stealth.

- 図書館で二人がひそひそ話している。 — Two people are talking in whispers in the library.
- 会議のあと、同僚がひそひそと相談を始めた。 — After the meeting, my colleagues began conferring in low voices. (同僚: どうりょう; 相談: そうだん.)

Use ひそひそ話す or ひそひそと話す. The compound ひそひそ話 (ひそひそばなし) means a whispered conversation.

## How the kanji combine

The repeated kana form evokes subdued human speech. Its extension to secretive behavior is not restricted to a literal sound, so voice-imitation and manner tags both apply.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こそこそ](こそこそ.md) emphasizes avoiding notice, whether or not someone speaks. [がやがや](がやがや.md) contrasts with a crowd's noisy hubbub. [くすくす](くすくす.md) is quiet laughter, not speech.

## Distinctive meaning

A whisper can be considerate, private, or conspiratorial. ひそひそ does not by itself establish gossip or wrongdoing, although listeners may interpret it that way. It describes the manner of speaking rather than making a noun into a universal ひそひそな expression.
