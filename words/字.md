# 字 (じ) - written character; handwriting

## Meaning

字 means a written character or letter, and can also refer to handwriting or written text.

- この字はどう読みますか。 (このじはどうよみますか。) - How do you read this character?
- もう少し大きな字で書いてください。 (もうすこしおおきなじでかいてください。) - Please write in slightly larger letters.

字がきれい (じがきれい) commonly praises someone's neat handwriting, not the meaning of what they wrote.

## How the kanji combine

This is one kanji, [字](../kanji/字.md), used with its on reading ジ. There is no second component word or kana ending in this noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

文字 (もじ) also means written characters or letters. 漢字 (かんじ) specifically names kanji; 字 is not restricted to that script. 言葉 (ことば) means words or language and is not a unit of writing in the same way.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish a character from a word: 日本 (にほん) is one country name written with two kanji. 字 concerns the writing units or their appearance, not necessarily whole vocabulary items.
