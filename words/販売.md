# 販売 (はんばい) - sales; selling

## Meaning

販売 is a noun for selling or sales. 販売する is transitive:
the goods sold take を. The place or channel can take で,
and the customer can take に.

- この店では地元の野菜を販売しています。 (このみせではじもとのやさいをはんばいしています。) - This shop sells locally grown vegetables.
- チケットの販売は明日からです。 (チケットのはんばいはあしたからです。) - Ticket sales start tomorrow.

販売中 (はんばいちゅう) means "on sale; available for purchase."
It indicates availability, not necessarily a reduced price.

## How the kanji combine

販 contributes dealing in goods, and [売](../kanji/売.md)
contributes selling. The related meanings reinforce the commercial
activity. Their on readings are はん + ばい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[売る](売る.md) (うる) is the straightforward verb "sell."
販売 names the selling activity and combines with する.
[買う](買う.md) (かう), "buy," describes the customer's side
of the same transaction. Advertising may support 販売 but is not
identical to making goods available for sale.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not import every English sense of "sale." A shop's 販売 can
take place at the regular price. Likewise, 販売 does not by itself
describe the entire process of market research and promotion sometimes
included in English "marketing." The focus is the commercial selling
of goods or services.
