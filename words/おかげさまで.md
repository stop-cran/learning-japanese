# おかげさまで - thankfully; thanks to your support

## Meaning

おかげさまで is a polite expression acknowledging a favorable situation
with gratitude: "thankfully" or "thanks to your support."
It can be a conventional response even when no specific help is being named.

- A: お元気ですか。 (おげんきですか。) - How have you been?
- B: おかげさまで、元気です。 (おかげさまで、げんきです。) - I'm well, thank you.
- おかげさまで、試験に合格できました。 (おかげさまで、しけんにごうかくできました。) - Thanks to your support, I was able to pass the exam.

In the last example, the surrounding conversation can identify whose
assistance the speaker acknowledges.

## How the kanji combine

The word is normally written in kana. Dictionary spellings include
お陰様で and お蔭様で; [様](../kanji/様.md) is read さま here.
Learn the complete formula rather than
interpreting "shadow" literally or changing its ending at random.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[おかげ](おかげ.md) appears in 先生のおかげで (せんせいのおかげで),
"thanks to my teacher." [ありがとう](ありがとう.md) directly expresses thanks.
[幸い](幸い.md) (さいわい) can mean "fortunately" without the same formula
of grateful acknowledgment.

## Distinctive meaning

The phrase does not necessarily refer to a god or religious belief.
It can acknowledge people's kindness or simply answer an inquiry appreciatively.
It is not a substitute for every thank-you: choose a formula that fits
the exchange rather than mechanically replacing ありがとうございます.
