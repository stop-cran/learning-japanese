# 詰める (つめる) - pack; shorten; work out details

## Meaning

詰める is an ichidan verb. It can mean packing something into a space, shortening or closing a gap, or working out remaining details.

- 箱に本を詰めました。 (はこにほんをつめました。) - I packed books into a box.
- ズボンの丈を詰めてもらいました。 (ズボンのたけをつめてもらいました。) - I had my trousers shortened.
- 会議で計画の細部を詰めます。 (かいぎでけいかくのさいぶをつめます。) - We will work out the plan's details at the meeting.

These uses are transitive: the contents, length, or details take を. Forms include 詰めます, 詰めた, and 詰めない. もう少し詰めてください can ask people to move closer together and make room, with no object stated.

## How the kanji combine

詰 is read つ in this native verb, with める as its ending. Packing tightly and reducing open space provide a useful memory connection; that image is not a substitute for learning each construction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入れる](入れる.md) (いれる) broadly means putting something in, without the packing sense. [短い](短い.md) (みじかい), "short," describes a state rather than the action of shortening. 広げる (ひろげる), "spread out," can contrast with closing space, but not with every sense.

## Distinctive meaning

細部を詰める is about resolving the remaining particulars, not physically filling a container. The object guides the interpretation. Likewise, packing a bag does not automatically mean forcing in more than it can hold: 詰める need not imply careless overpacking.
