# ころ — approximate time; around

## Meaning

ころ locates something around a time or within a period. It often leaves the boundaries less
exact than simply naming an occasion.

- 子供のころ、よくここで遊びました。  
  (こどものころ、よく ここで あそびました。) — I often played here when I was a child.
- 駅に着いたころ、雨が降り出しました。  
  (えきに ついたころ、あめが ふりだしました。) — It started raining around the time I arrived at the station.

Use **noun + の + ころ**, as in 子供のころ, or a noun-modifying clause, as in 駅に着いたころ.
After a clock time, the suffix form is **ごろ**: 三時ごろ (さんじごろ), "around three o'clock."

## How the kanji combine

頃 is a dictionary kanji spelling; hiragana is also usual. JMdict lists ころ and suffix ごろ
under the same entry. There is no kanji decomposition in this kana spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

とき names a time or occasion. Both 学生のとき and 学生のころ can refer to one's student years;
the contrast is not simply a short time versus a long time.
[くらい](くらい.md) and [ほど](ほど.md) approximate quantities such as durations:
三時間くらい (さんじかんくらい), "about three hours," differs from 三時ごろ, a clock time.

## Distinctive meaning

ころ can describe an approximate arrival time as well as a broad period of life. Do not change
every ころ to ごろ: 子供のころ and 三時ごろ have different attachment patterns.

For とき, ころ, and ごろ compared, see
[こと, わけ, and formal nouns](../articles/koto-wake-and-formal-nouns.md#とき-versus-ころ).
