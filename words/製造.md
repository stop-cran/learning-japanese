# 製造 (せいぞう) - manufacturing

## Meaning

製造 means manufacture or production of goods. As a noun it names the process; 製造する is a transitive する verb. Mark the product made with を and the manufacturing location with で.

- この工場では自動車の部品を製造しています。 (このこうじょうではじどうしゃのぶひんをせいぞうしています。) - This factory manufactures car parts.
- この食品は日本で製造されました。 (このしょくひんはにほんでせいぞうされました。) - This food product was manufactured in Japan.

製造される is passive: the product is manufactured rather than doing the manufacturing itself.

## How the kanji combine

製 contributes making or manufacturing, and 造 contributes making or constructing. The related meanings reinforce the production sense. The on readings are セイ + ゾウ. The compound names an activity, not a specific kind of machine.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作る](作る.md) (つくる) is a broad everyday verb for making. [生産](生産.md) (せいさん) means production and can cover areas such as agriculture as well as manufactured goods. [発明](発明.md) (はつめい), invention, concerns creating a new idea or device, not merely producing copies.

## Distinctive meaning

製造 describes making a product, not automatically selling or designing it. On a package, 製造日 (せいぞうび) is the manufacturing date; the expression itself does not mean an expiry date. 製造元 (せいぞうもと) identifies the manufacturer or manufacturing source.
