# 照る (てる) - shine; be sunny

## Meaning

照る describes the sun or moon shining, and also sunny weather. It is an intransitive godan verb.

- 太陽が照っています。 (たいようがてっています。) - The sun is shining.
- 月が明るく照っていました。 (つきがあかるくてっていました。) - The moon was shining brightly.

## How the kanji combine

[照](../kanji/照.md) has the native reading て.る here. The written boundary is て + る, but the whole godan verb gives てります before ます and てって in the て-form: 照ります and 照って. It is not the ichidan pattern てます.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[光る](光る.md) and [輝く](輝く.md) also describe shining. [照らす](照らす.md) is transitive: 月が照る is "the moon shines," whereas 月が海を照らす is "the moon illuminates the sea." [晴れる](晴れる.md) concerns becoming or being clear/sunny; [曇る](曇る.md) concerns clouding over.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the light source as the subject rather than treating the illuminated object as a direct object. Forms include 照らない and 照った. In a separate specialized Noh-theater sense, a mask turning slightly upward is said to 照る; this is not an ordinary substitute for every action of looking up.
