# スカーフ - scarf

## Meaning

スカーフ is a scarf, typically a relatively light piece of fabric worn around the neck or over the head, often as an accessory.

- 首にスカーフを巻きました。 (くびにスカーフをまきました。) - I wrapped a scarf around my neck.
- 母に絹のスカーフを贈りました。 (ははにきぬのスカーフをおくりました。) - I gave my mother a silk scarf.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "scarf," not a kanji compound. The long vowel is written ー.

## Synonyms and antonyms

マフラー (まふらー) usually suggests a scarf worn for warmth, often a thicker one. This is a practical contrast, not a rule that every scarf can be classified solely by thickness or season.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict's "especially a lightweight summer scarf worn by women" describes a prototype, not a ban on other wearers or seasons. English "scarf" is broader than that prototype. Use the actual garment and context rather than assuming スカーフ means every warm knitted winter scarf.
