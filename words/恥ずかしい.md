# 恥ずかしい (はずかしい) — embarrassed; embarrassing

## Meaning

恥ずかしい describes embarrassment, shame, or shyness. It can describe someone's feeling or an
embarrassing situation; it need not mean that a serious wrong has been committed.

- みんなの前で間違えて、恥ずかしかったです。 (みんなのまえでまちがえて、はずかしかったです。) — I felt embarrassed after making a mistake in front of everyone.

The past form 恥ずかしかった replaces the adjective's final い with かった.

## How the kanji combine

[恥](../kanji/恥.md) contributes shame or embarrassment and writes は in this spelling.
The following kana are ずかしい. Learn the complete written form 恥ずかしい rather than transferring
the noun 恥 (はじ) unchanged into the adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

恥 (はじ) is the noun "shame; embarrassment." 恥じる (はじる) is a verb meaning "feel ashamed":
自分の行動を恥じる (じぶんのこうどうをはじる), "feel ashamed of one's conduct."
These are related word forms, not replacements with identical grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

Match the English translation to the situation. "I am embarrassed" may fit an ordinary public mistake
better than "I am disgraced." Before a noun, the adjective attaches directly:
恥ずかしい思い出 (はずかしいおもいで), "an embarrassing memory."
There is no な between 恥ずかしい and the noun.
