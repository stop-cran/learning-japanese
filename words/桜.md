# 桜 (さくら) - cherry tree; cherry blossom

## Meaning

桜 is a noun naming a cherry tree or its blossoms. The surrounding verb or noun tells you whether the focus is the tree, the flowers, or the flowering scene.

- 公園の桜が咲きました。 (こうえんのさくらがさきました。) - The cherry trees in the park have blossomed.
- 庭に桜の木を植えました。 (にわにさくらのきをうえました。) - We planted a cherry tree in the garden.

桜の花 (さくらのはな) explicitly names the blossoms, while 桜の木 (さくらのき) explicitly names the tree. 桜が満開です (さくらがまんかいです) means the cherry blossoms are in full bloom.

## How the kanji combine

桜 is one kanji read with the native reading さくら. The entire character writes the tree name; it is not a compound whose parts are separately pronounced. Kana さくら and サクラ can also represent the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[花](花.md) (はな) is the broader noun "flower." [梅](梅.md) (うめ) names a different flowering tree, the Japanese apricot, not another reading or spelling of 桜.

## Distinctive meaning

For the edible cherry fruit, learn さくらんぼ rather than assuming 桜 names any cherry-related item. Also be aware that the dictionary records a different contextual use, usually written サクラ, for a fake customer or planted audience member. That usage is not a description of a tree and should be recognized separately from the blossom examples.
