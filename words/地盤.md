# 地盤 (じばん) - ground; supporting base

## Meaning

地盤 is the ground, especially the underlying ground that supports a building or other structure. It is a noun. **地盤が弱い** (じばんがよわい) means that the ground is weak.

- 雨で地盤が緩みました。
  (あめでじばんがゆるみました。) - The rain loosened the ground.
- 家を建てる前に、地盤を調べます。
  (いえをたてるまえに、じばんをしらべます。) - We will investigate the ground before building a house.

It also means a foundation or base, including a political support base or constituency. その候補者は、この地域に強い地盤を持っています (そのこうほしゃは、このちいきにつよいじばんをもっています) means "That candidate has a strong support base in this area." This is about support, not unusually solid soil.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) contributes ground and 盤 a board or supporting surface. Together they provide a meaning aid for the ground as a supporting base, not a geological derivation from the shapes. The on readings here are ジ + バン: じばん.

JMdict also records ちばん for the same entry. The primary reading taught here is the source's じばん; the alternate is not evidence that this reading is wrong.

## Synonyms and antonyms

地面 (じめん) names the ground surface. 地盤 particularly invites attention to the ground's supporting condition. [土地](土地.md) (とち) is land or a plot.

[基盤](基盤.md) (きばん) and [基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) also concern a supporting foundation. Their figurative uses overlap with 地盤; do not turn the soil association into an absolute physical-versus-abstract division.

## Distinctive meaning

地盤 is not simply a synonym for a room's [床](床.md) (ゆか), floor. Nor does an electoral 地盤 mean every resident agrees with a candidate. A strong support base describes established support or influence, not a guaranteed election result.
