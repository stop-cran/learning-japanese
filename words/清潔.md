# 清潔 (せいけつ) - clean; hygienic

## Meaning

清潔 describes being clean or hygienic. It is a な-adjective and can also name
cleanliness. Use 清潔な before a noun and 清潔に with an action such as keeping
something clean.

- 清潔なタオルを使ってください。 (せいけつなタオルをつかってください。) - Please use a clean towel.
- 台所を清潔に保ちましょう。 (だいどころをせいけつにたもちましょう。) - Let's keep the kitchen clean.

In the second example, を marks what is maintained and に marks the condition
it is kept in.

## How the kanji combine

清 contributes clarity or purity, and 潔 contributes cleanliness or purity.
The related meanings reinforce one another. Their on readings combine as
せい + けつ. This explains the compound's meaning, not the historical
development of either character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[きれい](きれい.md) can also mean clean, but additionally describes beauty.
A きれいな景色 (きれいなけしき), "beautiful view," is not therefore a
"hygienic view." [汚い](汚い.md) (きたない), "dirty," and 不潔
(ふけつ), "unclean; unhygienic," provide useful contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

清潔 focuses on a clean condition rather than visual attractiveness. A towel
can be plain-looking yet 清潔. The word is not a technical guarantee that
something is sterile or free of every microorganism.
For the negative, say 清潔ではない (せいけつではない); do not attach
an い-adjective ending directly to 清潔.
