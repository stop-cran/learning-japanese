# 潰れる (つぶれる) - be crushed; go out of business

## Meaning

潰れる describes something being crushed or losing its shape or function.
It is intransitive: the affected thing takes が. A business can 潰れる when it fails or closes down.

- 箱が重さで潰れました。
  (はこがおもさでつぶれました。) - The box was crushed by the weight.
- 近所の店が潰れてしまいました。
  (きんじょのみせがつぶれてしまいました。) - The neighborhood shop went out of business.

Other practical uses include a plan falling through, a chance being lost, a voice giving out, or time being used up.
声が潰れる (こえがつぶれる) describes losing one's voice; 一日が潰れた (いちにちがつぶれた) can mean that a whole day was taken up.
The conversational expression 酔って潰れる (よってつぶれる) means becoming incapacitated with drink.

## How the kanji combine

潰 supplies the crushing idea; れる is okurigana. This native verb is ichidan:
潰れます, 潰れた, 潰れて, 潰れない.
There is no separate 潰 card in this collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[潰す](潰す.md) (つぶす) is transitive: 箱を潰す, "crush a box," versus 箱が潰れる, "a box is crushed."
[壊れる](壊れる.md) (こわれる) broadly describes breaking or ceasing to work.
[破産](破産.md) (はさん) is bankruptcy; a shop going out of business need not establish that a particular legal bankruptcy procedure occurred.

For a business, 持ち直す (もちなおす), "recover," can contrast with failure.
It is not a universal opposite for a crushed object.

## Distinctive meaning

English "be crushed" looks passive, but Japanese 潰れる is an ordinary intransitive verb, not the passive conjugation of 潰す.
Physical flattening, business failure and losing one's voice are related extensions, not claims that each event literally smashes an object.
