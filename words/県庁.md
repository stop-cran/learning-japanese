# 県庁 (けんちょう) - prefectural office

## Meaning

県庁 is a prefectural government office: the administration of a 県,
or its offices as a place. It is a noun.

- 県庁に書類を提出しました。 (けんちょうにしょるいをていしゅつしました。) - I submitted the documents to the prefectural office.
- 姉は県庁で働いています。 (あねはけんちょうではたらいています。) - My older sister works for the prefectural government.

に marks the destination in the first sentence; で marks the workplace
in the second. Context can focus on the organization or its premises.

## How the kanji combine

[県](../kanji/県.md), prefecture, specifies the jurisdiction of
[庁](../kanji/庁.md), government office or agency.
The on readings combine as ケン + チョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[官庁](官庁.md) (かんちょう) is broader government offices or authorities.
[事務所](事務所.md) (じむしょ) and [オフィス](オフィス.md) are broader
office words and do not identify a prefectural government.
府庁 (ふちょう) is the corresponding prefectural office for Osaka or Kyoto.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not call every local government office a 県庁: a city's municipal
office is a different administrative body. Tokyo, Hokkaido, Osaka, and
Kyoto use different prefectural-category names rather than 県 in their
administrative titles.
