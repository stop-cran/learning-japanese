# 図 (ず) - diagram; figure

## Meaning

図 is a drawing, diagram, figure, or other visual representation. It is a noun used in explanations and captions. **図で説明する** means to explain using a diagram; 図を見る means to look at one.

- 次の図を見てください。 (つぎのずをみてください。) - Please look at the following figure.
- 機械の仕組みを図で説明します。 (きかいのしくみをずでせつめいします。) - I will explain how the machine works with a diagram.

A label such as 図1 (ずいち), "Figure 1," identifies a numbered illustration, not a numerical quantity.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [図](../kanji/図.md) supplies the whole word, using the on reading ズ. It needs no second character or kana ending. The older form 圖 is recorded, but 図 is the spelling used here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[地図](地図.md) (ちず) specifically means a map. 絵 (え), "picture; painting," can overlap with a drawing, but need not organize information as a diagram does. These categories can overlap; a diagram can also be attractively drawn.

## Distinctive meaning

図 is useful for visual information that accompanies an explanation. It need not depict something realistically: lines, arrows, and labels can make a 図. Do not read it as と simply because that reading appears in [図書](図書.md) (としょ).
