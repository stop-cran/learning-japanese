# 能率 (のうりつ) - efficiency; work rate

## Meaning

能率 concerns how efficiently work proceeds or how much work gets done
in a given time. It is a noun.
能率が上がる (のうりつがあがる) means efficiency improves;
能率を上げる (のうりつをあげる) means improving efficiency.

- 道具を変えたら、作業の能率が上がりました。
  (どうぐをかえたら、さぎょうののうりつがあがりました。) - Changing the tools improved the work rate.
- 休憩を取って能率を上げましょう。
  (きゅうけいをとってのうりつをあげましょう。) - Let's take a break to improve efficiency.

In the first example, が marks what improved.
In the second, を marks what people aim to improve.
The sentence suggests taking a break; it is not a universal productivity formula.

## How the kanji combine

[能](../kanji/能.md) contributes capability or performance and
率 a rate or proportion. Together they concern the rate or effectiveness
of work, not merely possessing an ability.
The on readings are ノウ + リツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

効率 (こうりつ), "efficiency," overlaps; it can emphasize output in relation
to resources or effort.
[性能](性能.md) (せいのう) includes performance and efficiency.
[能力](能力.md) (のうりょく) concerns ability or capability;
being capable does not automatically establish a high work rate.
[効果](効果.md) (こうか) concerns an effect or effectiveness.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not impose a fixed rule that 能率 is only for people and 効率 only for machines.
The work and the comparison being made matter.
The dictionary also records the physics term "moment";
that separate technical sense is not the meaning of an ordinary office
sentence about 作業の能率.
