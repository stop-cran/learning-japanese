# 高等 (こうとう) - high class; high grade

## Meaning

高等 describes a high class, grade, or level. It is a noun and
can modify with な or の; it also appears directly in compounds.
高等な技術 (こうとうなぎじゅつ) means advanced or high-level skills.

- この仕事には高等な技術が必要です。 (このしごとにはこうとうなぎじゅつがひつようです。) - This work requires advanced skills.
- 高等教育を受ける機会が増えました。 (こうとうきょういくをうけるきかいがふえました。) - Opportunities to receive higher education have increased.

高等教育 (こうとうきょういく) is higher or tertiary education.
[高等学校](高等学校.md) (こうとうがっこう) is the separate established
term for senior high school; do not translate it as a university.

## How the kanji combine

[高](../kanji/高.md) contributes high, and [等](../kanji/等.md)
contributes class, grade, or quality. 高 qualifies 等.
The on readings are コウ + トウ; keep both long vowels in こうとう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上等](上等.md) (じょうとう) and [高級](高級.md) (こうきゅう)
overlap in high quality. [高度](高度.md) (こうど) can describe a high
degree of development or sophistication.
下等 (かとう), inferior or lower-grade, supplies a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

High grade is not automatically luxury, high price, or greater moral
worth. Educational compounds have their own established meanings.
Do not infer the level of an institution merely by translating 高 as
"high" and ignoring the complete word.
