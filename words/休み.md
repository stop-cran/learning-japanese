# 休み (やすみ) - rest; day off

## Meaning

休み is a rest, break, day off, or holiday from normal activities. It can also indicate that a person is absent or a business is closed.

- 少し休みを取りましょう。 (すこしやすみをとりましょう。) - Let's take a short break.
- 明日は仕事が休みです。 (あしたはしごとがやすみです。) - I have the day off work tomorrow.

The noun combines with 取る (とる), "take," for taking a break or leave. 今日は店が休みです (きょうはみせがやすみです) means "The shop is closed today."

## How the kanji combine

[休](../kanji/休.md) supplies resting and is read やす; み completes the noun. It is related to the verb 休む (やすむ), "rest; take time off." The noun and verb have different endings and grammar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

休憩 (きゅうけい) is a break during an activity. 休日 (きゅうじつ) specifies a day off. 祝日 (しゅくじつ) is a public holiday.

## Distinctive meaning

A 休み need not be a national holiday or a vacation trip. It can be one person's free day, a school recess, or a shop's regular closure. Context determines what activity is paused.
