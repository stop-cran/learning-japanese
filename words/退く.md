# 退く (どく) - step aside; move out of the way

## Meaning

退く, read どく here, means to step aside or move out of someone's way.
It is intransitive: the person moving is the subject.
Kana どく is usual in this sense.

- 人が通れるように、少し横にどきました。
  (ひとがとおれるように、すこしよこにどきました。) - I moved a little to the side so someone could pass.
- すみません、そこをどいていただけますか。
  (すみません、そこをどいていただけますか。) - Excuse me, could you move out of that spot?

を can mark the place vacated, as in そこをどく; it does not make this a verb
for moving another person. It is godan: どきます, どいた, どいて, どかない.

## How the kanji combine

[退](../kanji/退.md) contributes moving away or withdrawing.
In this word the native reading is どく, with く in kana.
The character also writes other words and readings; its whole meaning range
does not automatically belong to どく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[譲る](譲る.md) (ゆずる) can mean yielding a place or letting someone go first.
[避ける](避ける.md) (さける) concerns avoiding a situation or question.
The same spelling read よける can mean physically avoiding contact or moving
out of the way; that reading is distinct from the linked さける card.
[動く](動く.md) (うごく) is broader movement, without necessarily clearing a path.

## Distinctive meaning

Direct どいて, "move!", can sound abrupt; the question above asks more politely.
Do not learn "resign; retire" as another どく sense: in this dictionary family,
that sense is restricted to のく. The same written 退く also has separate
しりぞく and ひく entries. A shared spelling does not merge their readings or uses.
