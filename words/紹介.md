# 紹介 (しょうかい) - introduction; referral

## Meaning

紹介 is an introduction or referral. 紹介する means to introduce a person,
place, product, or other subject to someone. What is introduced takes を;
the person receiving the introduction takes に.

- 友達に姉を紹介しました。 (ともだちにあねをしょうかいしました。) - I introduced my older sister to a friend.
- この本は京都の店を紹介しています。 (このほんはきょうとのみせをしょうかいしています。) - This book introduces shops in Kyoto.

## How the kanji combine

紹 has connecting or continuing associations; 介 concerns acting between
parties. Together they fit an introduction that connects people or makes
something known. The on-readings ショウ + カイ give しょうかい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

説明 (せつめい), "explanation," tells how or why something works; 紹介 makes
someone or something known. 推薦 (すいせん), "recommendation," more explicitly
endorses a choice. These functions can overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

紹介する does not always mean meeting face to face: a book or website can
introduce a place. 自己紹介 (じこしょうかい) is a self-introduction.
Keep the roles clear in AにBを紹介する: A hears about or meets B.
