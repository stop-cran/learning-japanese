# 缶詰 (かんづめ) - canned food; being confined

## Meaning

缶詰 is canned or tinned food. The spelling 缶詰め is also recorded,
with the same reading かんづめ.
Use Nの缶詰 to identify the contents.

- 魚の缶詰を買いました。
  (さかなのかんづめをかいました。) - I bought canned fish.
- 缶詰を開けて、皿に移しました。
  (かんづめをあけて、さらにうつしました。) - I opened the can of food and transferred the contents to a plate.

The noun also has a figurative confinement sense.
部屋に缶詰になる (へやにかんづめになる) means being stuck or shut
in a room; 人を缶詰にする means keeping someone confined.

## How the kanji combine

[缶](../kanji/缶.md) writes かん, "can," while
[詰](../kanji/詰.md) contributes packing or stuffing.
The second element is related to 詰める (つめる); here it is づめ.
This is a mixed formation, not an all-on-reading compound.
The container word かん itself has recorded foreign loan sources,
so its kanji spelling does not establish a wholly Chinese origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[缶](缶.md) (かん) names the container and can also abbreviate canned food.
An empty can is still a 缶, but it is not food.
瓶詰め (びんづめ), "bottled or jarred goods," uses a different container;
it is a related packaging word rather than a universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

- 締め切り前は、ホテルに缶詰になって原稿を書きました。
  (しめきりまえは、ホテルにかんづめになってげんこうをかきました。) - Before the deadline, I stayed shut up in a hotel writing the manuscript.

This figurative example does not involve an actual can.
Compare になる, entering the confined situation, with にする,
putting someone into it. Learn those complete expressions rather than
using 缶詰 as a free-standing verb meaning "imprison."
