# 西暦 (せいれき) - Common Era year numbering; Western calendar

## Meaning

西暦 is Common Era/Anno Domini year numbering, as in 2026, rather than a
Japanese era year. It also names the Western calendar, especially the Gregorian calendar.

- 生年月日を西暦で書いてください。 (せいねんがっぴをせいれきでかいてください。) - Please write your full date of birth using Common Era year numbering.
- これは西暦二千年に建てられた橋です。 (これはせいれきにせんねんにたてられたはしです。) - This bridge was built in the year 2000 CE.

西暦で specifies the system used to write the year.

## How the kanji combine

[西](../kanji/西.md) contributes Western here, and 暦 contributes calendar.
The modifier specifies the calendar or dating system. Read セイ + レキ;
暦 has no card here but remains part of the spelling and metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

和暦 (われき) uses Japanese era names and year numbers, such as 令和 (れいわ).
[カレンダー](カレンダー.md) is a calendar, including a calendar one looks at.
[生年月日](生年月日.md) (せいねんがっぴ) is a date of birth, which can be
expressed using either year system according to the context.

## Distinctive meaning

The English era in Common Era does not mean a historical period like
[時代](時代.md) (じだい). A request to write 西暦 normally concerns year notation,
not bringing a printed calendar. Distinguish year numbering from the rules of
a calendar; neither use alone explains how a historical date was converted.
