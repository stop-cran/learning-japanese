# 合流 (ごうりゅう) - confluence; joining

## Meaning

合流 describes separate rivers or streams flowing together.
It also describes people, groups, or traffic joining or linking up.
It is a noun and intransitive する-verb.

- この川は下流で大きな川に合流します。 (このかわはかりゅうでおおきなかわにごうりゅうします。) - This river joins a larger river downstream.
- 駅で友達と合流しました。 (えきでともだちとごうりゅうしました。) - I met up with my friend at the station.

In AがBに合流する, A joins B. 人と合流する presents people joining
up with one another. The friend example concerns linking up after
being apart, not necessarily a first introduction.

## How the kanji combine

[合](../kanji/合.md) contributes joining, and
[流](../kanji/流.md) contributes flowing. Together they suggest
flows coming together, extended to people and groups.
The on readings are ゴウ + リュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[加わる](加わる.md) (くわわる) can mean joining a group.
[合同](合同.md) (ごうどう) concerns combination or joint arrangements.
[会う](会う.md) (あう), meet, is broader and need not imply joining
previously separate movements. 分流 (ぶんりゅう), branching of a flow,
provides a contextual contrast for rivers.

## Distinctive meaning

English "merge" does not make this a transitive verb in the patterns
above. The river joins another river with に; do not transfer an English
object directly as 川を合流する.
People can 合流する without forming a permanent organization or
legally merging their employers.
