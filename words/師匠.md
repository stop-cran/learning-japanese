# 師匠 (ししょう) — master; teacher

## Meaning

師匠 is a master or teacher, especially of an art or skill. In sumo, it also means a stable master, the head of a sumo stable.

- 師匠から料理の技術を教わりました。 (ししょうからりょうりのぎじゅつをおそわりました。) — I learned cooking techniques from my master.
- 落語家は、師匠のもとで何年も修業します。 (らくごかは、ししょうのもとでなんねんもしゅぎょうします。) — Rakugo (traditional Japanese comic storytelling) performers train under a master for many years.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[師](../kanji/師.md) means teacher, and [匠](../kanji/匠.md) means a master craftsman. Together, they mean a master who teaches.

## Synonyms and antonyms

弟子 でし, “pupil; disciple; apprentice” (JMdict entry 1581960), is the counterpart of 師匠. [先生](先生.md) (せんせい) is a broader word for a teacher, and [名人](名人.md) (めいじん) means a master or expert (JMdict entry 1531680).

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen lists 師匠 under the sense of 匠 meaning a person accomplished in an art or skill.
