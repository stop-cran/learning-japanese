# 完全 (かんぜん) - complete; perfect

## Meaning

完全 describes completeness or freedom from a missing part or defect. Learn **完全な + noun**, **完全に + predicate**, and **完全だ／です**.

- これは完全な解決ではありません。 (これはかんぜんなかいけつではありません。) - This is not a complete solution.
- けがは完全に治りました。 (けがはかんぜんになおりました。) - The injury has healed completely.

The first example judges the solution as a whole. The second uses に to say that the recovery is complete, rather than partial.

## How the kanji combine

[完](../kanji/完.md) contributes completion, and [全](../kanji/全.md) contributes wholeness or entirety. Their meanings reinforce one another in a compound about completeness. The on readings are カン + ゼン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

不完全 (ふかんぜん), "incomplete; imperfect," is a direct negative counterpart. [十分](十分.md) (じゅうぶん), "sufficient," asks whether something is enough for a purpose. A solution can be sufficient for today's needs without being 完全.

## Distinctive meaning

English "perfect" can simply express enthusiastic approval. 完全 is especially useful when the intended point is that nothing is lacking or that an action has reached its full extent. In 完全に忘れました (かんぜんにわすれました), "I completely forgot," it intensifies the extent of forgetting; it does not praise the quality of the memory.
