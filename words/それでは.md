# それでは (それでは) — well then; in that case

## Meaning

それでは means "well then" or "in that case". It takes the situation just mentioned as the basis for a comment, decision, or next action.

- もう時間ですね。それでは、始めましょう。  
  もうじかんですね。それでは、はじめましょう。  
  "It's time now, isn't it? Well then, let's begin."
- 電車が止まっていますか。それでは、バスで行きます。  
  でんしゃがとまっていますか。それでは、バスでいきます。  
  "Have the trains stopped? In that case, I'll take the bus."

## How the kanji combine

Use hiragana. The expression combines それ with では, referring back to the situation. The final は is the particle, pronounced わ; write それでは, not それでわ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

それじゃ and じゃあ are conversational alternatives. [すると](すると.md) can introduce a following event or inference, but it does not fit every decision-making or conversation-closing use of それでは.

## Distinctive meaning

それでは can also close an exchange: それでは、また (それでは、また), "Well then, see you". It is not always a literal description of being "in that situation"; it often organizes the conversation itself.
