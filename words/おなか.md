# おなか

## Meaning

おなか means the belly or abdomen, often translated as "stomach" in everyday English. It need not refer specifically to the internal stomach organ.

- おなかがすきました。 (おなかが すきました。) — I am hungry.
- おなかが痛いです。 (おなかが いたいです。) — My stomach hurts.

These expressions use が: the belly is what becomes empty or hurts. おなかがいっぱい means being full after eating.

## How the kanji combine

The ordinary kanji spelling is お腹, but kana is common and clear. The initial お is a polite element within this established everyday word; it does not make the whole sentence honorific.

## Synonyms and antonyms

腹 (はら) also means belly and can sound plainer or rougher. 胃 (い) is the stomach as an anatomical organ. These overlap in English translation but are not identical in scope.

## Distinctive meaning

Use おなか for ordinary talk about hunger, fullness, or abdominal discomfort. There is no natural direct antonym; hungry and full are contrasting conditions of the same body area.
