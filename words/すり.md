# すり (すり) - pickpocket

## Meaning

すり means a pickpocket: someone who steals belongings from a person's pocket or bag without being noticed.

- 人が多い所では、すりに気をつけてください。 (ひとがおおいところでは、すりにきをつけてください。) - Please watch out for pickpockets in crowded places.

In the expression に気をつける (にきをつける), に marks what to be careful about.

## How the kanji combine

The ordinary spelling is kana, either すり or スリ. The source spelling 掏摸 is a rare kanji spelling of the same word. It is not necessary to learn separate character readings to use the everyday noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

泥棒 (どろぼう) means a thief more generally. A すり is one particular kind of thief. 落とし物 (おとしもの), "lost property," concerns an item someone dropped or lost, not a person who steals.

## Distinctive meaning

Use this noun to identify the person or danger in a warning. It does not describe every disappearance of a wallet: losing something does not by itself establish that a pickpocket took it.
