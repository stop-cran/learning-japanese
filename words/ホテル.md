# ホテル (ホテル) - hotel

## Meaning

ホテル is a hotel: accommodation where guests pay to stay. The usual expression for staying at one is ホテルに泊まる (ホテルにとまる).

- 駅の近くのホテルに泊まりました。 (えきのちかくのホテルにとまりました。) - I stayed at a hotel near the station.
- ホテルで朝ご飯を食べました。 (ホテルであさごはんをたべました。) - I ate breakfast at the hotel.

Notice the particles: に marks the lodging with 泊まる, while で marks the place where an activity such as eating occurs.

## How the kanji combine

ホテル is a katakana loanword from English "hotel." Its spelling has three ordinary kana and no long-vowel mark. There is no kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

旅館 (りょかん) typically suggests a Japanese-style inn. 宿 (やど) is a broader word for lodging or an inn.

## Distinctive meaning

The label ホテル does not itself promise luxury. A business hotel, resort hotel, and budget hotel may differ greatly in facilities and price. Add more information when a particular style of accommodation matters.
