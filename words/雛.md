# 雛 (ひな) — young bird; hina doll

## Meaning

**ひな** has two useful noun senses: a young bird or chick, and a traditional
doll displayed for ひな祭り (ひなまつり), the Dolls' Festival.

- 巣に雛が二羽います。 (すにひながにわいます。) — There are two young birds in the nest.
- ひな祭りにお雛様を飾ります。 (ひなまつりにおひなさまをかざります。) — We display hina dolls for the Dolls' Festival.

The second example uses お雛様 (おひなさま), a familiar way of referring to
the festival dolls. It is not talking about birds. 雛人形 (ひなにんぎょう)
also explicitly names a hina doll.

The same kanji also writes **ひよこ**, a young bird, especially a chicken.

- 雛がぴよぴよ鳴いています。 (ひよこがぴよぴよないています。) — A chick is cheeping.

This example reads 雛 as ひよこ; its kanji spelling alone does not determine
which bird reading is intended. In their bird senses, both ひな and ひよこ
are often written in kana. Do not automatically extend that spelling guidance
to the doll sense of ひな.

## How the kanji combine

[雛](../kanji/雛.md) writes the whole native noun ひな or ひよこ,
without okurigana. The longer reading is not built by adding a separately
written 子 (こ) to 雛. The character's on readings belong to other uses,
not alternative pronunciations of these whole native nouns.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鳥](鳥.md) とり is the broader bird category; [鶏](鶏.md) にわとり is the
domestic chicken, whether young or adult. [小鳥](小鳥.md) ことり concerns
size, not necessarily age. A small adult bird is not therefore a ひな.

[人形](人形.md) にんぎょう is a broader word for a doll, puppet or modeled figure;
ひな is not the general name for every toy doll. There is no single antonym
covering both the bird and festival-doll senses.

## Distinctive meaning

**ひよこ**, also ひよっこ, can figuratively mean an inexperienced person,
a novice or greenhorn who is not yet fully competent.

- この仕事では、私はまだひよこです。 (このしごとでは、わたしはまだひよこです。) — In this work, I am still a novice.

This self-description concerns experience, not age. Used about someone else,
ひよこ can sound belittling; do not assume it is automatically affectionate.
[後輩](後輩.md) こうはい and [目下](目下.md) めした concern relative
seniority or position, not necessarily lack of competence.

The dictionaries' ひな/ひよこ cross-reference applies to the **bird sense**:
do not use ひよこ as the reading for a hina doll, or automatically transfer
the figurative novice meaning to ひな. The frozen JMdict marks both senses
of ひよこ as usually kana; its ひよっ子 spelling is search-only, not a
recommendation for ordinary writing.
