# さじ - spoon

## Meaning

さじ is a spoon: a utensil for scooping liquids, powders, or food.
The source spelling 匙 has the same reading.

- さじで砂糖を加えました。 (さじでさとうをくわえました。) - I added sugar with a spoon.
- 薬をさじで量りました。 (くすりをさじではかりました。) - I measured the medicine with a spoon.

で marks the utensil used.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual; 匙 and 匕 are dictionary spellings for this word.
Digital Daijisen explains さじ as coming from the Sino-Japanese reading of 茶匙.
This origin is the basis for the card's kango classification, not an invented division of さ and じ between written components.
The dictionary also records しゃじ and an obsolete かい reading; these are not instructions to change the reading of a recipe.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[スプーン](スプーン.md) is the English borrowing for spoon.
箸 (はし), chopsticks, is another utensil rather than an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Recipes use 大さじ (おおさじ), "tablespoon," and 小さじ (こさじ), "teaspoon."
A measuring-spoon quantity is not automatically the capacity of any dining spoon.
Learn the complete measuring expression rather than interpreting every さじ as one fixed volume.
