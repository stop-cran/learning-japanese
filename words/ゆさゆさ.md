# ゆさゆさ — swaying with large, slow movements

## Meaning

ゆさゆさ describes something heavy or large swaying or shaking with broad, slow movements.

- 大きな枝が風でゆさゆさ揺れている。
  — A large branch is swaying in the wind.
- 彼は荷物をゆさゆさ揺らしながら歩いた。
  — He walked with his luggage swinging heavily.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ゆさゆさ suggests broad, weighty motion, unlike a fine tremor or quick flutter.

## Distinctive meaning

It often describes repeated movement of something substantial.
