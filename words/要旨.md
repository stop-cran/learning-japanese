# 要旨 (ようし) - gist; main points; abstract

## Meaning

要旨 is the gist or main points of a text, speech or argument.
It can also be a summary or abstract presenting those points.

- 講演の要旨を三行にまとめました。 (こうえんのようしをさんぎょうにまとめました。) - I summarized the main points of the lecture in three lines.
- 論文の要旨を先に読みました。 (ろんぶんのようしをさきによみました。) - I read the paper's abstract first.

## How the kanji combine

[要](../kanji/要.md) contributes what is important or essential;
旨 the purport or central point.
The on readings are ヨウ + シ: the essential purport.
This 旨 is not read うま or translated as delicious here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[要点](要点.md) (ようてん) is a main point or gist.
[あらすじ](あらすじ.md) overlaps in an outline or plot summary;
[概論](概論.md) (がいろん) is a general outline or introduction.
[内容](内容.md) (ないよう) is contents or substance, which may
include far more detail than a 要旨.

## Distinctive meaning

要旨 selects what is essential, not every sentence of the original.
It is not limited to a fictional plot, and an abstract need not
reproduce a lecture's or paper's original wording.
[大体](大体.md) (だいたい), [およそ](およそ.md) and
[おおよそ](おおよそ.md) have outline/gist uses as well as their
better-known approximation uses; those senses should not be
confused with an approximate number.
