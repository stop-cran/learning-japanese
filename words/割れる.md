# 割れる (われる) - break; crack apart

## Meaning

割れる describes something breaking, cracking, or splitting. It is intransitive: the affected thing takes が.

- コップが落ちて、割れました。 (コップがおちて、われました。) - The glass fell and broke.
- この皿は割れやすいです。 (このさらはわれやすいです。) - This plate breaks easily.

It is an ichidan verb: 割れます (われます), 割れない (われない), 割れた (われた). 割れやすい combines its stem with やすい, "easy to."

## How the kanji combine

[割](../kanji/割.md) contributes splitting or division. The written ending is れる, but the conjugation stem is 割れ: keep れ when adding ます.

## Synonyms and antonyms

割る (わる) is the transitive partner: 皿を割る (さらをわる), "break a plate." 壊れる (こわれる) is broader, including a machine that stops working without cracking apart.

## Distinctive meaning

割れる does not specify who caused the break. The glass can be the subject even if someone dropped it. This verb describes the resulting change, not a passive conjugation meaning that someone necessarily broke it intentionally.
