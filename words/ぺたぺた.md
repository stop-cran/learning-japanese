# ぺたぺた — repeated flat pats or sticking

## Meaning

Repeated flat contact, such as bare feet pattering against a floor; also repeatedly sticking, stamping, or daubing something onto a surface.

- 子どもが裸足（はだし）でぺたぺたと廊下（ろうか）を歩いた。 — The child walked down the hallway barefoot with soft slapping footsteps.
- ノートにシールをぺたぺた貼った。 — I stuck stickers all over the notebook.
- スポンジで絵の具（えのぐ）をぺたぺた塗った。 — I dabbed paint on with a sponge.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[べたべた](べたべた.md) can also describe pasting things all over, but additionally describes sticky states and clingy people. [ぽつぽつ](ぽつぽつ.md) concerns separated drops or scattered occurrences, not flat contact.

## Distinctive meaning

Use an action verb: ぺたぺた歩く, 貼る（はる）, or 塗る（ぬる）. It does not automatically mean that the finished surface is sticky; stickers can be ぺたぺた貼られている without leaving glue on your hand. Avoid using ぺたぺたする as an all-purpose replacement for the state べたべたする.
