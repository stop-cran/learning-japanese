# 駅 (えき) — station

## Meaning

駅 is a railway or train station: 駅で待つ (wait at the station), 駅に着く つく (arrive at a station), 次の駅 つぎのえき (the next station), 駅員 えきいん (station staff), 東京駅 とうきょうえき.

## How the kanji combine

[駅](../kanji/駅.md) is used on its own with the on reading エキ. It was once a post station, where horses were changed, which is why the kanji has 馬.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: [駅前](駅前.md) えきまえ, 停留所 ていりゅうじょ (stop for buses), バス停 バスてい.
- Place names ending with 駅 are read えき: 新宿駅 しんじゅくえき.

## Distinctive meaning

駅 is used for train and metro stations; a bus stop is not 駅 but バス停. 駅 is a free noun (駅で待つ), and in names it follows the station name (新宿駅).
