# 約束 (やくそく) — promise; agreement

## Meaning

約束 is a promise or agreement that commits someone to something. 約束する means "make a promise."
A verb can describe its content: 会う約束 (あうやくそく), "an agreement to meet."

- 友達と、明日また会う約束をしました。 (ともだちと、あしたまたあうやくそくをしました。) — I promised my friend that we would meet again tomorrow.
- 約束を守ります。 (やくそくをまもります。) — I will keep my promise.

Use person + と for the other party to an agreement. 約束を守る (やくそくをまもる) means to keep a promise;
約束を破る (やくそくをやぶる) means to break one.

## How the kanji combine

[約](../kanji/約.md) contributes the idea of an agreement and [束](../kanji/束.md) that of binding together.
Think of people being bound by an agreement, not of a literal bundle.
The on readings combine as ヤク + ソク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

契約 (けいやく) is a contract or agreement; it is useful when the commitment is a contract.
予約 (よやく) is a reservation or booking, such as securing a restaurant table.
Those ideas can overlap with an arrangement, but a personal promise is not automatically a booking.
[守る](守る.md) (まもる) and [破る](破る.md) (やぶる) provide the practical contrast "keep" versus "break" a promise.

## Distinctive meaning

約束 carries a commitment between people, not just an event that might happen.
約束がある (やくそくがある) can therefore mean that you have an arrangement to meet someone; context supplies the details.
The word does not by itself establish whether the agreement is legally enforceable.
