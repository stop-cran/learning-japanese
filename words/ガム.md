# ガム (ガム) - chewing gum

## Meaning

ガム means chewing gum in the everyday food use taught here. It is a shortened form of チューインガム.

- ガムを噛んでいます。 (ガムをかんでいます。) - I am chewing gum.
- ガムを一つもらいました。 (ガムをひとつもらいました。) - I received a piece of chewing gum.

[噛む](噛む.md) (かむ) is the verb "chew"; ガム names what is chewed.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word has no kanji to split. Keep the written form ガム, without adding a long-vowel mark or reading it like the English spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

チューインガム is the fuller name. ゴム usually concerns rubber or an elastic material rather than something to chew. The dictionary also records a material sense for ガム, so context matters; that is not the snack sense in the examples.

## Distinctive meaning

English "gum" can also refer to the tissue around teeth. Do not import that meaning merely because the English source word looks familiar. The practical expression here is ガムを噛む, "chew gum."
