# 寛大 (かんだい) — lenient; generous in judgment

## Meaning

寛大 describes someone broad-minded or lenient, or a response that shows such tolerance. In 失敗に寛大だ (しっぱいにかんだいだ), に marks what someone is lenient about. Before a noun, use 寛大な.

- 先生は私たちの失敗に寛大だった。 (せんせいはわたしたちのしっぱいにかんだいだった。) — The teacher was forgiving about our mistakes.
- 寛大な対応に感謝しています。 (かんだいなたいおうにかんしゃしています。) — I appreciate your generous response.

## How the kanji combine

[寛](../kanji/寛.md) (かん) points to giving someone leeway; [大](../kanji/大.md) (だい) is “large.” Think of room for another person’s mistake, not the physical dimensions of a room.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[優しい](優しい.md) (やさしい) describes someone kind or gentle more generally. [厳しい](厳しい.md) (きびしい), “strict,” is a useful contrast when judging how a mistake is handled.

## Distinctive meaning

In English, “generous” can mean offering a lot of money. A 寛大な判断 (かんだいなはんだん) is a generous *judgment*: no payment or gift is implied.
