# 追加 (ついか) - addition; supplement

## Meaning

追加 is adding something to what is already there, or the additional thing itself.
追加する is transitive: what is added takes を, and に can mark what receives it.

- 名簿に新しい名前を追加しました。
  (めいぼにあたらしいなまえをついかしました。) - I added a new name to the list.
- 料理をもう一皿追加しました。
  (りょうりをもうひとさらついかしました。) - I ordered one more dish.

追加の説明 (ついかのせつめい) is an additional explanation.
追加注文 (ついかちゅうもん) is an additional order.

## How the kanji combine

[追](../kanji/追.md) contributes following after, and [加](../kanji/加.md)
contributes adding. Together they suggest adding something afterward.
The on readings are ツイ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[加える](加える.md) (くわえる) broadly means add;
[足す](足す.md) (たす) is useful for quantities or adding more.
[補う](補う.md) (おぎなう) emphasizes supplying something missing or insufficient.
[付ける](付ける.md) (つける) also has adding uses, beyond physical attachment.

## Distinctive meaning

追加 does not require an earlier mistake or shortage.
[増加](増加.md) (ぞうか) concerns an increase in amount or number;
[加わる](加わる.md) (くわわる) describes something being added or joining.
In 追加する, a person can explicitly add an item.
The source's "appendix" points to appended material; 追加 is not the anatomical appendix.
For a book's supplementary section, 付録 (ふろく) is a useful comparison.
