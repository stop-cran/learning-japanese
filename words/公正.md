# 公正 (こうせい) - fairness; impartiality

## Meaning

公正 means fairness, justice, or impartiality: judging or acting without
unfair bias. It is a noun and な-adjective.
公正な判断 (こうせいなはんだん) means "a fair judgment";
公正に describes acting fairly.

- 審査員は公正に判断しなければなりません。 (しんさいんはこうせいにはんだんしなければなりません。) - The judges must make their decision fairly.
- 公正な取引が求められています。 (こうせいなとりひきがもとめられています。) - Fair dealings are required.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md) contributes public-mindedness rather than private
favor, and [正](../kanji/正.md) contributes rightness or propriety.
Together they reinforce impartial rightness. The on readings are コウ + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[公平](公平.md) (こうへい) directly overlaps in fairness and impartiality.
[平等](平等.md) (びょうどう) concerns equality and can also express
impartial treatment; equality and fairness are not sealed-off meanings.
[不正](不正.md) (ふせい), "wrongdoing; dishonesty," provides a contrast
when the issue is improper conduct.

## Distinctive meaning

公正 does not guarantee that everyone receives an identical outcome.
Conversely, do not define it as excluding equal treatment.
[正しい](正しい.md) (ただしい), "correct; proper; just," is broader:
an accurate numerical answer is not therefore an act of impartial judgment.
