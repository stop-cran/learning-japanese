# 訪ねる (たずねる) — visit; call on

## Meaning

訪ねる means to visit or call on someone. It can also take the place visited as its object.
Use を for the person or place in these patterns.

- 日曜日に友達の家を訪ねました。 (にちようびにともだちのいえをたずねました。) — I visited my friend's house on Sunday.
- 昔の先生を訪ねたいです。 (むかしのせんせいをたずねたいです。) — I want to visit my former teacher.

This is an ichidan verb: 訪ねます (たずねます), 訪ねた (たずねた).

## How the kanji combine

[訪](../kanji/訪.md) gives the visiting association. The following ねる completes the
native verb たずねる; it is not a separate word meaning "sleep."
Read the kanji and following kana together.

## Synonyms and antonyms

訪問する (ほうもんする) also means to visit.
The important spelling contrast is 尋ねる (たずねる), "ask; inquire":
先生に意味を尋ねる (せんせいにいみをたずねる) means "ask a teacher the meaning,"
not "visit the meaning."

## Distinctive meaning

The two verbs たずねる sound alike, but their spellings distinguish visiting from asking.
The particles can help: 先生を訪ねる is visiting the teacher, whereas
先生に尋ねる is asking the teacher. Both full patterns matter more than the sound alone.
