# 来る (くる) — to come

## Meaning

来る is to come toward the speaker or the place the speaker is focused on: 友達が来る (a friend comes), 春が来た (spring has come), 日本に来ました (I came to Japan).
It also appears as an auxiliary: 帰って来る (come back), 持って来る (bring), 暑くなって来た (it has been getting hot).

## How the kanji combine

[来](../kanji/来.md) is one of Japanese's two irregular verbs (the other is する). The stem changes by form, but the kanji stays the same:

| Form | Reading | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dictionary | く | 来る |
| Polite / past / te-form | き | 来ます, 来た, 来て |
| Negative / potential | こ | 来ない, 来られる |
| Imperative | こ | 来い／こい (rough) |
| Polite request (te-form) | き | 来てください／きてください |

So 来 is read く, き or こ. The on reading ライ is used in compounds such as [来年](来年.md) らいねん (next year) and 来週 らいしゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [行く](行く.md) いく (go).
- Close: いらっしゃる (honorific for 来る／行く／いる), 参る まいる (humble), お越し (honorific noun).

## Distinctive meaning

Compare the point of view: 来る is toward the speaker, 行く away from them. ここに来てください asks the listener to come to where you are.
Because the kanji hides the change, learn the three kana forms together: くる, きます, こない.
