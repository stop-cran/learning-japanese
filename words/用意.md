# 用意 (ようい) - preparation; getting ready

## Meaning

用意 means preparation or arrangements. 用意する means to prepare or have something ready; the prepared thing takes を.

- お客さんのためにお茶を用意しました。 (おきゃくさんのためにおちゃをよういしました。) - I prepared tea for the guests.
- 出かける用意はできましたか。 (でかけるよういはできましたか。) - Are you ready to go out?

The second example attaches the plain verb 出かける directly to 用意 to identify what the preparation is for.

## How the kanji combine

[用](../kanji/用.md) concerns use, and [意](../kanji/意.md) concerns intention or thought. The established compound is read ヨウ + イ, ようい. Keep the long よう before the final い.

## Synonyms and antonyms

準備 (じゅんび) also means preparation and often overlaps. 支度 (したく) commonly concerns getting oneself or necessary things ready. Do not assume a rigid boundary between them in every situation.

## Distinctive meaning

用意する emphasizes making what is needed available or ready. 用意ができた means the preparations are ready; it does not necessarily claim that the main activity has started.
