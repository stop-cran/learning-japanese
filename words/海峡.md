# 海峡 (かいきょう) — strait; sea channel

## Meaning

A 海峡 is a narrow passage of sea between land masses that connects two bodies of water.

- 船で海峡を渡った。 (ふねでかいきょうをわたった。) — We crossed the strait by boat.
- 海峡の向こうに島が見える。 (かいきょうのむこうにしまがみえる。) — An island is visible across the strait.

## How the kanji combine

[海](../kanji/海.md) (かい) supplies the sea; [峡](../kanji/峡.md) (きょう) evokes a narrow passage. A sea strait is water, not a strip of land.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[運河](運河.md) (うんが) is a canal built as a waterway. [湾](湾.md) (わん) is a bay or gulf; it need not connect two bodies of water.

## Distinctive meaning

Picture the two shores on either side and the water that runs between them. A narrow strip of *land* connecting two larger land areas is a 地峡 (ちきょう), an isthmus.
