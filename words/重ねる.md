# 重ねる (かさねる) - stack; repeat

## Meaning

重ねる means to put things on top of one another, or to repeat and accumulate an activity or experience. It is a transitive ichidan verb: what is stacked or repeated takes を. A surface or lower layer can take に.

- 皿を重ねて、棚に置きました。 (さらをかさねて、たなにおきました。) - I stacked the plates and put them on the shelf.
- 練習を重ねて、上手になりました。 (れんしゅうをかさねて、じょうずになりました。) - I improved through repeated practice.

Forms include 重ねます, 重ねた, 重ねて, and 重ねない.

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) contributes layering or accumulation. ねる completes the native verb かさねる; this is not the adjective 重い (おもい), heavy, plus a separate verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[積む](積む.md) (つむ) overlaps with stacking and accumulating; it also has a vehicle-loading use. [繰り返す](繰り返す.md) (くりかえす) means repeat or do again. 重ねる can emphasize a succession building up, but repetition and accumulation are overlapping perspectives, not strict compartments.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the grammatical viewpoint separate from [重なる](重なる.md) (かさなる): 皿を重ねました means someone stacked the plates; 皿が重なっています describes their layered state. 重ねる is not a replacement for every arithmetic "add": for adding numbers, use [足す](足す.md) (たす).
