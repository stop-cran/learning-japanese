# 付く (つく) -- be attached; stick

## Meaning

付く describes something attaching, sticking, or being connected to something else. It is intransitive: attached thing が + surface or object に + 付く.

- 靴にガムが付きました。 (くつにガムがつきました。) -- Gum stuck to my shoe.
- このシャツにはボタンが六つ付いています。 (このシャツにはボタンがむっつついています。) -- This shirt has six buttons attached.

This is a godan verb: 付きます (つきます), 付かない (つかない), 付いて (ついて), and 付いた (ついた). 付いている often describes the resulting attached state.

## How the kanji combine

[付](../kanji/付.md) contributes attachment and supplies つ. The written く completes the native verb; it changes when the verb conjugates.

## Synonyms and antonyms

付ける (つける) is the transitive partner: someone attaches an object with を. 取れる (とれる), "come off," can contrast with attachment in a suitable context.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse this spelling with 着く (つく), "arrive." A destination sentence such as 駅に着く (えきにつく), "arrive at the station," concerns reaching a place, not sticking to its surface.
