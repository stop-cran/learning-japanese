# 捕鯨 (ほげい) — whaling

## Meaning

捕鯨 means **whaling**, the activity of hunting or catching whales. It is a noun, not the name of the whale itself.

- 捕鯨について話しました。 (ほげいについてはなしました。) — We talked about whaling.
- 捕鯨に関する本を読みました。 (ほげいにかんするほんをよみました。) — I read a book about whaling.

について introduces the topic in the first example. に関する modifies 本 in the second: a book concerning whaling. Neither sentence says that the speaker caught a whale.

## How the kanji combine

[捕](../kanji/捕.md), capture, takes its on reading ホ; [鯨](../kanji/鯨.md), whale, takes ゲイ. The relation is **verb + object**: catching whales. Together they form the activity noun ほげい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鯨](鯨.md) くじら names the animal; 捕鯨 names the activity. [漁業](漁業.md) ぎょぎょう, fishing industry/fishery, and [水産](水産.md) すいさん, aquatic products/fisheries, are broader field words, not precise replacements for whaling.

[釣り](釣り.md) つり concerns fishing by angling; it does not mean whaling. There is no useful direct antonym for this activity noun.

## Distinctive meaning

The word specifies whales, not every kind of fishing, animal capture or sea-related work. It does not by itself identify a particular method, country or legal status. Describing or discussing 捕鯨 is different from saying that someone carries it out.

Use the whole-word reading **ほげい**, not ほくじら. The native reading くじら belongs to the animal noun in the linked article.
