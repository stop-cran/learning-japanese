# 一杯 (いっぱい) — one cupful

## Meaning

Here 一杯 means one serving in a cup, glass, or bowl. The container depends on what is being served.
It is a counter expression, not a fixed volume measurement.

- コーヒーを一杯ください。 (コーヒーをいっぱいください。) — One cup of coffee, please.
- 一杯の水を飲みました。 (いっぱいのみずをのみました。) — I drank a glass of water.

The quantity can follow the object, as in コーヒーを一杯, or precede the noun with の, as in 一杯の水.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) supplies "one"; [杯](../kanji/杯.md) counts cupfuls, glassfuls, or bowlfuls.
The on-reading combination is pronounced いっぱい, not いちはい.
Learn the small っ and the ぱ sound as part of this complete counter expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

English "one cup," "one glass," and "one bowl" can all translate this same expression, depending on the drink or food.
コップ (a drinking glass) names a container; 一杯 counts a serving, so the two are not interchangeable.
A quantity of one does not need an invented lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The same pronunciation also has "full" and "a lot" senses, usually written いっぱい in kana.
Those are separate uses: the 一杯 in a one-cup coffee order is a count, not a claim that there is a lot of coffee.
This article focuses on that countable-serving meaning.
