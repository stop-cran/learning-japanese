# すみません - excuse me; I am sorry

## Meaning

すみません is a polite expression used to apologize or attract someone's attention. It can also acknowledge a kindness or the trouble someone has taken for you, functioning as thanks.

- すみません、駅はどこですか。 (すみません、えきはどこですか。) - Excuse me, where is the station?
- 遅れてすみません。 (おくれてすみません。) - I am sorry I am late.

For gratitude, imagine someone carrying your bag: すみません、助かります (すみません、たすかります), "Thank you; that's a great help." The circumstances make clear that this is appreciation rather than an admission of wrongdoing.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana; 済みません is also recorded. In form it is the polite negative associated with 済む (すむ), but these social uses should be learned as an expression, not translated word by word as "does not finish."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ごめんなさい](ごめんなさい.md) expresses an apology, but is not the general choice for calling a server's attention. [ありがとう](ありがとう.md) directly expresses thanks, without necessarily foregrounding an inconvenience.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose the translation from the interaction. An opening すみません may introduce a request rather than apologize for it. For a completed mishap, すみませんでした is also useful: "I am sorry about what happened."
