# 絶滅 (ぜつめつ) - extinction

## Meaning

絶滅 is extinction: a kind of living thing dies out. 絶滅する is an intransitive する verb, with the species or population disappearing as its subject.

- その鳥はすでに絶滅しました。 (そのとりはすでにぜつめつしました。) - That bird species has already become extinct.
- この動物は絶滅の危機にあります。 (このどうぶつはぜつめつのききにあります。) - This animal is in danger of extinction.

絶滅の危機 (ぜつめつのきき) means "danger of extinction." The second sentence describes a risk, not extinction that has already happened.

## How the kanji combine

[絶](../kanji/絶.md) contributes coming to an end or being cut off; 滅 contributes perishing or disappearing. Together they emphasize dying out, rather than a small reduction. The on readings are ゼツ + メツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[減る](減る.md) (へる) means decrease: a population can decrease without becoming extinct. [死ぬ](死ぬ.md) (しぬ) describes dying and can refer to one individual; 絶滅 concerns disappearance of a kind or population in the relevant scope.

## Distinctive meaning

The broad English gloss "destruction" can mislead. A damaged building is not normally described with this species-extinction pattern. In ecological discussion, check the stated scope: disappearance from one area and worldwide extinction are not automatically the same claim. The word supplies the idea of dying out; surrounding information identifies what and where.
