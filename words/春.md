# 春 (はる) — spring

## Meaning

春 is spring, the season between winter and summer. This article focuses on the ordinary seasonal meaning.

- 春に花が咲きます。 (はるにはながさきます。) — Flowers bloom in spring.
- 春休みに旅行します。 (はるやすみにりょこうします。) — I will travel during spring vacation.

## How the kanji combine

[春](../kanji/春.md) represents the complete native word はる. There is no second kanji or okurigana. In 春休み はるやすみ, "spring vacation," 春 modifies 休み やすみ, "break."

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [夏](夏.md) なつ, [秋](秋.md) あき, and [冬](冬.md) ふゆ are the other seasons, not interchangeable synonyms.
- 秋 is the season on the opposite side of the yearly cycle, but it is not an antonym in the same sense as "hot" and "cold."

## Distinctive meaning

春 means the season, not a water source or a metal coil, both also called "spring" in English. In other vocabulary the character can suggest youth, as in 青春 せいしゅん, but that does not change the ordinary reading of the season to しゅん.
