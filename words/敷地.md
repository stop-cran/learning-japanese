# 敷地 (しきち) - site; plot; grounds

## Meaning

敷地 is a site, plot, or area of land used for a building, facility, road, or similar purpose. It can include the grounds around a building, not just the part directly underneath it.

- この学校の敷地は広いです。 (このがっこうのしきちはひろいです。) - This school has extensive grounds.
- 家を建てるための敷地を探しています。 (いえをたてるためのしきちをさがしています。) - We are looking for a plot on which to build a house.

It is a noun. 敷地内 (しきちない) means within the grounds or site.

## How the kanji combine

敷 contributes しき, the native stem related to [敷く](敷く.md), lay or spread underneath. [地](../kanji/地.md) contributes land with the on reading チ. The combination points to land serving as a site.

This is a kun-first mixed reading, grouped under wago as with [場所](場所.md). It does not mean both components are native readings or establish a complete historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[土地](土地.md) とち is land more broadly. [現場](現場.md) げんば is the actual scene of an event or work. A construction site's 敷地 and 現場 can refer to the same location while emphasizing the land and the ongoing activity differently.

## Distinctive meaning

敷地 is not only land on which a finished building already stands: the planned house site in the example is also 敷地. Nor is it a synonym for the building itself. Its boundaries and permitted use depend on the particular property, not on the word alone.
