# 坊や (ぼうや) - little boy; son

## Meaning

坊や is a familiar word for a boy or someone's son. It can be affectionate when referring or speaking to a small boy.

- かわいい坊やですね。 (かわいいぼうやですね。) - What a cute little boy.
- 坊や、名前は何というの？ (ぼうや、なまえはなんというの？) - Little boy, what is your name?

Used about an older male, it can instead be belittling: a mere boy, inexperienced youngster, or greenhorn. The relationship and tone matter.

## How the kanji combine

坊 supplies ボウ; や is a familiar suffix here. This on-reading base plus a Japanese suffix is not a two-kanji compound. The character is also found in [坊さん](坊さん.md) (ぼうさん), whose usual priest/monk meaning is different.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[男の子](男の子.md) (おとこのこ) is a more neutral "boy"; [息子](息子.md) (むすこ) is "son." [坊っちゃん](坊っちゃん.md) (ぼっちゃん) can respectfully refer to another person's son and can also suggest a sheltered young man.

## Distinctive meaning

坊や is not the neutral choice for every boy in a formal report. Affection toward a child can become condescension toward an adult. Do not infer that every occurrence is either an insult or praise from the word alone.
