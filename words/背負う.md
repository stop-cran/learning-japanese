# 背負う (せおう) - carry on one's back; bear a burden

## Meaning

背負う is a transitive godan verb: carry something on one's back, or bear a
burden such as responsibility or debt. What is carried or borne takes を.
Forms include 背負います (せおいます), 背負って (せおって), 背負わない (せおわない).

- 大きなリュックを背負っています。 (おおきなリュックをせおっています。) - I am carrying a large backpack on my back.
- 彼は家族の生活を背負っています。 (かれはかぞくのせいかつをせおっています。) - He bears responsibility for supporting his family's daily life.

The verb can also describe having something behind one, as in 山を背負った村
(やまをせおったむら), a village with a mountain behind it.

## How the kanji combine

[背](../kanji/背.md) contributes back, and [負](../kanji/負.md) contributes bearing
or carrying. Read the primary form せおう, with う as the okurigana.
The same spelling also has the reading しょう; do not mechanically replace every
reading on the card with it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[担ぐ](担ぐ.md) (かつぐ) emphasizes carrying on a shoulder or shoulders;
[抱える](抱える.md) (かかえる) can concern carrying in one's arms or having a
problem or burden. [持つ](持つ.md) (もつ) is broader holding or having.
These overlaps do not erase the physical difference between back, shoulders and arms.

## Distinctive meaning

Recognize the colloquial conceit use しょっている or しょってる, acting full of oneself.
That recorded sense belongs to the reading しょう, not to a general rule that
せおう means be conceited. The literal and figurative carrying senses do not imply
that a burden is willingly accepted.
