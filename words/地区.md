# 地区 (ちく) - district; designated area

## Meaning

地区 is a noun for a district, area, or zone considered as a section of a larger place. The boundaries may be administrative or established for a particular activity, purpose, or local description.

- この地区には古い家が多いです。 (このちくにはふるいいえがおおいです。) - There are many old houses in this district.
- 来週、地区の運動会があります。 (らいしゅう、ちくのうんどうかいがあります。) - There will be a district sports day next week.

この地区 identifies the area under discussion; 地区の connects that area with its event. The word does not itself specify a population or physical size.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) contributes land or a place, and 区 contributes a division or section. Together they identify a section of an area. The on readings combine as チ + ク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[地域](地域.md) (ちいき) also means an area or region and often overlaps. [地方](地方.md) (ちほう) can name a region or a non-central locality. Avoid memorizing an absolute size ranking: the intended boundaries come from context.

## Distinctive meaning

地区 is not automatically an official municipal ward just because it contains 区. A shopping district, school-related district, or event district can be defined for its own purpose. Learn the full designation rather than assuming every 地区 has the same legal status.
