# 近頃 (ちかごろ) - lately; nowadays

## Meaning

近頃 is a time noun used adverbially for "lately," "recently," or "nowadays." It points to a recent period around the present, with its exact length left to context.

- 近頃、よく雨が降ります。 (ちかごろ、よくあめがふります。) - It has been raining a lot lately.
- 近頃は忙しくて、あまり本を読んでいません。 (ちかごろはいそがしくて、あまりほんをよんでいません。) - I have been busy lately and have not been reading much.

It can appear without a particle, with は to frame the topic, or before a noun with の: 近頃の天気 (ちかごろのてんき), "the weather lately."

## How the kanji combine

[近](../kanji/近.md) contributes nearness, and 頃 contributes an approximate time or period. Here the nearness is temporal. 頃 is read ごろ in the compound. 近ごろ is another spelling of the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[最近](最近.md) (さいきん) overlaps broadly with "recently." [このごろ](このごろ.md) also points to the current recent period. [昔](昔.md) (むかし), "long ago," is a useful temporal contrast, not a precise opposite covering every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

近頃 works well with a recent tendency, condition, or repeated event; it is not an exact timestamp such as "three minutes ago." Avoid treating the near-synonyms as rigidly separated time windows. The dictionary also records a dated intensifying use, but the everyday time expression is the sense taught here.
