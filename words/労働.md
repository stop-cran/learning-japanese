# 労働 (ろうどう) — labor

## Meaning

労働 is labor or work considered as an activity requiring effort.
It is a noun and can form the verb 労働する (ろうどうする), "work; labor."
It is useful when discussing working time, the effort involved, or conditions of work.

- 長時間の労働で疲れました。 (ちょうじかんのろうどうでつかれました。) — I became tired from working long hours.
- 労働時間を短くする必要があります。 (ろうどうじかんをみじかくするひつようがあります。) — We need to shorten working hours.

## How the kanji combine

[労](../kanji/労.md) contributes toil or effort, and [働](../kanji/働.md) contributes working.
These related meanings reinforce the idea of labor rather than naming a worker and an object.
The on readings are ロウ + ドウ; 働 is not read はたら here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕事](仕事.md) しごと can mean a job, occupation, assignment, or work.
作業 さぎょう focuses on an operation or task being carried out.
働く はたらく is the verb "work," as in 店で働く (みせではたらく), "work at a shop."
These overlap with 労働 but offer different ways to frame the activity.

## Distinctive meaning

労働 does not name a particular profession. In the first example, the point is the effort and duration
of working, not whether the speaker is a teacher, builder, or shop worker.
When asking what someone's job is, 仕事 asks a different question from discussing that person's 労働時間.
