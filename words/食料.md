# 食料 (しょくりょう) - food; food supplies

## Meaning

食料 is a noun for food, especially useful when discussing food as supplies to obtain, store, or distribute. **食料を買う／用意する** means "buy/prepare food supplies."

- キャンプに必要な食料を買いました。 (キャンプにひつようなしょくりょうをかいました。) - We bought the food needed for the camping trip.
- 水と食料を三日分用意しました。 (みずとしょくりょうをみっかぶんよういしました。) - We prepared three days' worth of water and food.

三日分 marks an amount sufficient for three days; it does not mean that the preparation took three days.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md), "eat; food," qualifies [料](../kanji/料.md), "material; supplies." ショク + リョウ gives しょくりょう, material for eating. Here 料 does not mean a fee.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[食べ物](食べ物.md) (たべもの) is a broad everyday word for food or things to eat. [食品](食品.md) (しょくひん) emphasizes food items or products. [食事](食事.md) (しょくじ) is a meal or the act of eating.

## Distinctive meaning

食料 does not by itself say that the food is raw, cooked, delicious, or ready to serve. It is useful for discussing availability and supply. 食料が足りない (しょくりょうがたりない) means "there is not enough food," whereas 食事が終わった means a meal has finished.
