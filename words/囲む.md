# 囲む (かこむ) - surround; encircle

## Meaning

囲む means to surround, enclose, or form a circle around something.
It is a transitive godan verb: what is surrounded takes を. A surrounding material or
boundary can take で. Its forms include 囲みます, 囲んで, and 囲まない.

- 家族でテーブルを囲んで夕食を食べました。
  (かぞくでテーブルをかこんでゆうしょくをたべました。)
  - The family gathered around the table and ate dinner.
- 庭を塀で囲みました。
  (にわをへいでかこみました。) - We enclosed the garden with a wall.

The first describes people arranged around a shared center; 家族で identifies
the people acting together, "as a family." In the second, 塀で identifies the
surrounding boundary.
Neither requires a perfectly circular shape.

## How the kanji combine

囲 is the single kanji carrying the surrounding or enclosing meaning.
It is read かこ in 囲む; む is the written verb ending.
There is no compound of separate kanji meanings here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

取り囲む (とりかこむ) also means to surround.
囲まれる (かこまれる) is the passive of 囲む, not an opposite:
山に囲まれた町 (やまにかこまれたまち) is "a town surrounded by mountains."

## Distinctive meaning

囲む can describe a friendly arrangement, as around a dinner table, or a physical enclosure.
It does not inherently imply hostility or captivity.
Keep the viewpoints clear: people 囲む the table; the table is 囲まれる by the people.
