# 一月 (いちがつ) — January

## Meaning

一月 is January, the first month: 一月一日 いちがつついたち (New Year's Day), 一月に雪が降る (it snows in January).

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) (one) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month): "first month". The month names are the number plus 月 read がつ (ガツ, a different on reading from ゲツ in 月曜日 げつようび). Numbers read as on readings: いち.
The related word 一か月 いっかげつ means "one month (duration)", with a different reading of 月.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 正月 しょうがつ (the New Year period; not just January 1), 睦月 むつき (old name of the first month).
- Antonym: 十二月 じゅうにがつ (December).

## Distinctive meaning

一月 names the calendar month, 一か月 counts a length of time. Also compare 一日 ついたち (the first of the month) with 一日 いちにち (one day).
