# 資料 (しりょう) — reference materials; data

## Meaning

資料 is material used for learning, investigating, or discussing something: documents, data, or other information to consult.
It need not refer to a single sheet of paper.

- 会議の前に、この資料を読んでください。 (かいぎのまえに、このしりょうをよんでください。) — Please read these materials before the meeting.
- 研究に必要な資料を集めています。 (けんきゅうにひつようなしりょうをあつめています。) — I am collecting the materials needed for my research.

## How the kanji combine

[資](../kanji/資.md) suggests resources that support an activity; [料](../kanji/料.md) contributes the materials sense.
The combination names resources to draw information from. Its on readings are シ + リョウ.
Do not import 料's "fee" sense into this particular word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

情報 (じょうほう) names information; 資料 often presents it as material to examine or consult.
材料 (ざいりょう) can mean ingredients or raw materials, but also grounds or data for a decision.
The words overlap in some informational uses, so "資料 is information and 材料 is always physical" would be misleading.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 資料 for the materials supporting the task at hand: for example, documents distributed for a meeting.
In cooking, 材料 is the word for ingredients; calling the vegetables 資料 would shift to the wrong sense of "materials."
