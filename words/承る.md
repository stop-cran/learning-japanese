# 承る (うけたまわる) - hear; accept (humble)

## Meaning

承る humbly describes hearing or being told something. It also describes accepting an order,
undertaking a request or taking a reservation in deferential service language.
It is a transitive godan verb: the information or request takes を.

- 先生からお話を承りました。 (せんせいからおはなしをうけたまわりました。) - I heard about it from my teacher.
- ご予約を承ります。 (ごよやくをうけたまわります。) - We accept reservations.

Learn 承ります (うけたまわります), 承った (うけたまわった) and 承らない (うけたまわらない).
The long reading is うけたまわる, not an on-reading compound.

## How the kanji combine

[承](../kanji/承.md) writes the native stem うけたまわ; る is okurigana.
Its receiving/accepting association connects the hearing and undertaking uses.
The single spelling does not require dividing the pronunciation among several imagined characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[聞く](聞く.md) (きく) is neutral; [伺う](伺う.md) (うかがう) is another humble verb for asking or hearing,
and also means visiting. [承知](承知.md) (しょうち) names awareness or acceptance;
承知しました acknowledges a request rather than simply reporting that a sound was heard.
[いただく](いただく.md) humbly describes receiving, including gifts, but is not an automatic substitute in every reservation formula.

## Distinctive meaning

Use humble language for the speaker's side's action toward the person concerned.
Do not describe an honored customer's hearing as 承る just to elevate that customer.
Accepting a reservation does not say that the stay or service has already happened.
The hearing use can imply that the speaker knows something because they have been informed.
