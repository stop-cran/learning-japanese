# 着く (つく) - arrive; reach a destination

## Meaning

着く means to arrive at a destination. It is intransitive: the arriving person or vehicle is the subject, and the destination normally takes に.

- 電車は九時に駅に着きます。 (でんしゃはくじにえきにつきます。) - The train arrives at the station at nine.
- 家に着いたら、電話してください。 (いえについたら、でんわしてください。) - Please call when you get home.

It is a godan verb: 着きます (つきます), 着かない (つかない), 着いて (ついて), 着いた (ついた).

## How the kanji combine

[着](../kanji/着.md) has its arrival meaning here and is read つ, followed by く. The same kanji in 着る (きる), "wear," belongs to a different verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

到着する (とうちゃくする) also means "arrive," often in announcements or reports. 出発する (しゅっぱつする), "depart," marks the beginning rather than the end of a journey.

## Distinctive meaning

English "reach the station" has a direct object, but Japanese uses 駅に着く, not 駅を着く. Do not confuse this spelling with 付く, another つく with attachment-related meanings.
