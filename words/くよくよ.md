# くよくよ — brooding; fretting over things

## Meaning

くよくよ describes repeatedly worrying or dwelling on something unpleasant, especially a mistake or an event one cannot now change.

- 小さな失敗をいつまでもくよくよしている。 — I keep brooding over a small mistake. (失敗: しっぱい.)
- 昨日のことをくよくよと考えて、眠れなかった。 — I could not sleep because I kept fretting about yesterday.

Use くよくよする or くよくよと考える. The topic may be marked with を: 失敗をくよくよする. くよくよしないで means "don't dwell on it."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もやもや](もやもや.md) emphasizes unresolved unease; くよくよ emphasizes continuing to fret. [めそめそ](めそめそ.md) describes tearful whimpering or weeping. One may brood without crying.

## Distinctive meaning

The word often implies that dwelling on the problem is unhelpful. It is therefore not a neutral equivalent of careful planning or appropriate concern for someone else's safety. Telling a distressed person くよくよするな, "stop brooding," can sound dismissive even when intended as encouragement.
