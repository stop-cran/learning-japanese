# カーテン (カーテン) - curtain

## Meaning

カーテン means a curtain or curtains, commonly those covering a window.

- 朝、カーテンを開けます。 (あさ、カーテンをあけます。) - I open the curtains in the morning.
- 夜はカーテンを閉めます。 (よるはカーテンをしめます。) - I close the curtains at night.

を marks the curtains as the object of 開ける (あける), "open," or 閉める (しめる), "close."

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "curtain." The mark ー lengthens the vowel after カ. There are no kanji components to break down.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ブラインド means blinds, a related window covering rather than the same object. 窓 (まど) is the window itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese does not add a plural ending to カーテン. Context can identify one curtain, a pair, or several sets. Opening a curtain does not mean opening the window; the two actions have different objects.
