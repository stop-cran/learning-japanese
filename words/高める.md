# 高める (たかめる) - heighten; enhance

## Meaning

高める is a transitive ichidan verb: raise something's level or degree,
heighten it, or enhance it. The thing affected takes を.

- 練習で技術を高めます。
  (れんしゅうでぎじゅつをたかめます。) - I improve my skills through practice.
- 安全性を高める必要があります。
  (あんぜんせいをたかめるひつようがあります。) - We need to improve safety.

The polite form is 高めます, the て-form 高めて, and the past 高めた.
The verb can also concern raising a voice or reputation; the level or
intensity need not be a physical height.

## How the kanji combine

[高](../kanji/高.md) supplies high, using the native element たか.
The written ending is める. The ichidan stem used before ます is たかめ,
not simply the part written with the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

高まる (たかまる) is the intransitive counterpart: a level rises.
Compare 関心が高まる (かんしんがたかまる), interest grows, with
関心を高める (かんしんをたかめる), raise interest.

[上げる](上げる.md) (あげる) is broader; [挙げる](挙げる.md) also has
raising/enhancing uses. [強化](強化.md) (きょうか), [改善](改善.md)
(かいぜん), [伸ばす](伸ばす.md) (のばす), [伸びる](伸びる.md)
(のびる) and [育てる](育てる.md) (そだてる) overlap particular strengthening
or development contexts.
[増す](増す.md) (ます), [増大](増大.md) (ぞうだい) and
[増加](増加.md) (ぞうか) concern increase; not every increase is an
improvement. 低める (ひくめる), lower, provides a degree contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose the construction for the intended change: something becomes higher
with 高まる, while an agent raises its level with 高める.
The practical core is heightening a level, degree or quality, not an
unrestricted instruction to translate every act of physically lifting
an object with 高める.
