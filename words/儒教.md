# 儒教 (じゅきょう) — Confucianism

## Meaning

儒教 is Confucianism, the tradition of teachings associated with
孔子（こうし）, Confucius. It is a noun naming that particular tradition,
not religion or education in general.

- 歴史の授業で儒教について学びました。
  (れきしのじゅぎょうでじゅきょうについてまなびました。)
  — I learned about Confucianism in history class.
- この本は儒教の考え方を紹介しています。
  (このほんはじゅきょうのかんがえかたをしょうかいしています。)
  — This book introduces Confucian ideas.

儒教について学ぶ（じゅきょうについてまなぶ）means "learn about Confucianism";
studying a tradition does not itself mean following it.

## How the kanji combine

[儒](../kanji/儒.md) contributes Confucian thought or its scholars, and
[教](../kanji/教.md) contributes teaching or doctrine.
This is a modifier + head compound: Confucian teachings.
The on readings combine as ジュ + キョウ → じゅきょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

儒学（じゅがく）also means Confucianism, often emphasizing its scholarly aspect.
The words overlap; they are not an absolute division between religion and
secular philosophy.
[宗教](宗教.md) しゅうきょう means religion more broadly, and
[思想](思想.md) しそう means thought or ideology, not specifically Confucianism.
There is no simple everyday antonym for the name of this tradition.

## Distinctive meaning

教 does not make 儒教 a word for school teaching in general.
Use 儒教の思想（じゅきょうのしそう）, "Confucian thought," when describing its ideas.
The word names a tradition; it does not by itself state a person's nationality,
beliefs or degree of commitment.
