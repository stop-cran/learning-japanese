# 実は (じつは) - actually; to tell the truth

## Meaning

実は is an introductory expression meaning "actually," "as a matter
of fact," or "to tell you the truth." It presents information the
listener may not know, often correcting an assumption or revealing
background to the current conversation.

- 実は、明日引っ越します。 (じつは、あしたひっこします。) - Actually, I am moving tomorrow.
- 実は、その映画はもう見ました。 (じつは、そのえいがはもうみました。) - As a matter of fact, I have already seen that film.

It stands before the statement rather than changing the following
verb's form. It can introduce a small correction as well as a serious
personal disclosure.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md), read じつ here, contributes truth or actuality.
は is the topic particle, pronounced わ. Thus the written reading is
じつは, but the final spoken sound is wa. This card groups the
on-based expression under kango; は is not part of a kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本当](本当.md) (ほんとう) names truth or reality.
[ところで](ところで.md) often introduces a change of topic.
English may sometimes render 実は as "by the way," but its useful
core is revealing the actual situation, not simply announcing an
unrelated topic.

## Distinctive meaning

実は does not inherently mean "I lied earlier." The new fact may
merely be unexpected or previously unmentioned. Its effect depends on
what the listener was likely to assume before the disclosure.
