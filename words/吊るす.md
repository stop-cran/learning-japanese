# 吊るす (つるす) - hang up; suspend

## Meaning

吊るす means to hang something up so that it is supported from above. It is a transitive godan verb: use を for what is hung and に for the support or location.

- 軒先に風鈴を吊るしました。
  (のきさきにふうりんをつるしました。) - I hung a wind chime under the eaves.
- 洋服をハンガーに吊るしてください。
  (ようふくをハンガーにつるしてください。) - Please hang the clothes on a hanger.

Forms include 吊るします (つるします), 吊るした (つるした), and 吊るさない (つるさない). **吊す** is another recorded spelling of the same つるす, not a different reading.

## How the kanji combine

吊 contributes hanging or suspending. The full native verb is つるす; the difference between the spellings 吊るす and 吊す does not change its godan conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[吊る](吊る.md) (つる) overlaps with hanging or suspending. [掛ける](掛ける.md) (かける) also works for hanging various objects. [干す](干す.md) (ほす) focuses on drying or airing: hanging laundry may serve that purpose, but a hanging wind chime is not being dried.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the actual supported-object construction rather than treating every English "hang" as this verb. 吊るす does not mean hanging around a place or a computer becoming unresponsive. The hanging action alone also says nothing about how long the object will remain there.
