# 与える (あたえる) — give; grant

## Meaning

与える means to give, grant, or bestow something. It is a transitive verb: the thing granted takes を,
and the recipient can take に. The selected dictionary sense especially includes giving to someone of lower status.
Do not treat it as a socially neutral replacement for every English "give."

- 先生は生徒に考える時間を与えました。 (せんせいはせいとにかんがえるじかんをあたえました。) — The teacher gave the students time to think.

The verb changes to 与えます (あたえます) in the polite form and 与えた (あたえた) in the plain past.

## How the kanji combine

This is one native verb, not a compound of two kanji. [与](../kanji/与.md) supplies the giving meaning and is read あた;
える completes the written dictionary form. The on reading ヨ belongs to other words, such as 給与 (きゅうよ), "pay."

## Synonyms and antonyms

あげる also means give. For an ordinary present to a friend, 友達に本をあげた
(ともだちにほんをあげた), "I gave a friend a book," is a useful model.
与える can instead frame the action as granting a recipient something, as in the teacher example.
The choice involves the relationship and situation, not just whether an object changes hands.

## Distinctive meaning

Time and opportunities can be granted as well as physical things. In the example, the teacher allows the students time to think;
no object needs to be handed over. Learn this granting use without extending it to every giving construction.
