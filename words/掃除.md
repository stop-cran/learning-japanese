# 掃除 (そうじ) - cleaning

## Meaning

掃除 means cleaning, including sweeping, dusting, or scrubbing.
Add する to make a transitive verb: the place or object cleaned takes を.

- 毎朝、部屋を掃除します。 (まいあさ、へやをそうじします。) - I clean my room every morning.
- 今日は掃除をしたくありません。 (きょうはそうじをしたくありません。) - I do not want to do the cleaning today.

The second example uses 掃除 as the noun object of する.

## How the kanji combine

掃 means sweeping; [除](../kanji/除.md) contributes removing or getting rid of
something. Together they describe removing dirt. Their on-readings give
ソウ + ジ, with a long first vowel in そうじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

洗う (あらう), "wash," specifies cleaning with water or another liquid.
片付ける (かたづける), "tidy up," focuses on putting things in order.
A room can be tidy but still need dusting, so these verbs are not interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

掃除 does not specify the tool or method: a broom, cloth, or vacuum may be used.
掃除機 (そうじき) is a vacuum cleaner. For clothes washing, 洗濯
(せんたく) is the ordinary activity name.
