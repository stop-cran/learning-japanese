# 自分 (じぶん) - oneself

## Meaning

自分 refers to a person's own self. Depending on context, English may use "myself," "yourself," "himself," or "herself." Use 自分の for "one's own" and 自分で for doing something oneself.

自分の名前を書いてください。 (じぶんのなまえをかいてください。) - Please write your own name.

この料理は自分で作りました。 (このりょうりはじぶんでつくりました。) - I made this dish myself.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) concerns oneself, and [分](../kanji/分.md) can concern a part or share. Their on readings form じぶん. Learn the established pronoun rather than translating the characters separately in a sentence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

私 (わたし) explicitly identifies the speaker. 自分 can refer back to another person, so it is not simply a substitute for 私. 他人 (たにん), "another person," contrasts with one's own self.

## Distinctive meaning

Who 自分 refers to depends on the sentence and viewpoint. 自分で emphasizes personal agency, not necessarily being physically alone; 一人で (ひとりで) more directly means "alone." 自分 can also mean "I" in some speaking styles, but beginners should not assume that every occurrence identifies the speaker.
