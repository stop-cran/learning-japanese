# 弁当 (べんとう) - bento; packed meal

## Meaning

弁当 is a noun for a bento: a meal packed in a container, often eaten away from home. It may be homemade or bought.

- 毎朝、弁当を作ります。 (まいあさ、べんとうをつくります。) - I make a packed meal every morning.
- 駅で弁当を買って、電車の中で食べました。 (えきでべんとうをかって、でんしゃのなかでたべました。) - I bought a bento at the station and ate it on the train.

弁当を持っていく (べんとうをもっていく) means taking a packed meal along. The food, rather than just its container, is the focus.

## How the kanji combine

弁 can carry senses such as handling matters or speech, while [当](../kanji/当.md) can mean fitting, hitting, or the relevant item. Adding those modern component glosses does not transparently explain this food word. Learn 弁当 as an established whole, read ベン + トウ, rather than inventing a literal "speech-hit" etymology.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お弁当](お弁当.md) (おべんとう) adds お but refers to the same kind of meal. [昼御飯](昼御飯.md) (ひるごはん) means lunch, whether packed or not. [食事](食事.md) (しょくじ) is a meal more generally. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "box lunch" is convenient but can be too narrow: 弁当 does not specify noon. 弁当箱 (べんとうばこ) names the lunchbox or meal container itself. An empty 弁当箱 is not already a prepared 弁当.
