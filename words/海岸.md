# 海岸 (かいがん) - seashore; coast

## Meaning

海岸 is the coast or seashore, where the land meets the sea. It does not have to be sandy.

- 海岸を歩きました。 (かいがんをあるきました。) - I walked along the coast.
- 海岸で写真を撮りました。 (かいがんでしゃしんをとりました。) - I took photographs at the seashore.

With a movement verb such as 歩く, を can mark the route traversed. で marks where an activity happens.

## How the kanji combine

[海](../kanji/海.md), "sea," combines with 岸, "shore; bank": かい + がん. Together they identify the shore of the sea rather than a riverbank.

## Synonyms and antonyms

浜辺 (はまべ) and ビーチ commonly refer to a beach. 内陸 (ないりく), "inland area," is a useful geographic contrast, not an interchangeable place label.

## Distinctive meaning

海岸 can describe rocky stretches as well as recreational beaches. Translate it according to the setting rather than assuming sand, swimming, or a resort.
