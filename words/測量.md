# 測量 (そくりょう) - surveying; measurement

## Meaning

測量 means measurement and, especially in practical modern use, land surveying:
measuring positions, distances, heights or areas to describe land.
It is a noun and a transitive する verb: **土地を測量する**
(とちをそくりょうする), "survey land."

- 道路を造る前に土地を測量します。 (どうろをつくるまえにとちをそくりょうします。) - We survey the land before building a road.
- 測量の結果を地図にまとめました。 (そくりょうのけっかをちずにまとめました。) - We compiled the survey results into a map.

The examples concern physical measurements, not asking people a questionnaire.

## How the kanji combine

[測](../kanji/測.md) and [量](../kanji/量.md) both contribute measuring
or gauging; 量 also concerns quantity. Their related meanings reinforce
the measuring activity. The on readings combine as そく + りょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[測定](測定.md) (そくてい) is measurement of quantities, often with instruments.
It overlaps with 測量 rather than forming an absolute rival category.
[調査](調査.md) (ちょうさ) is investigation or a survey more generally;
English "survey" does not make every 調査 a land measurement.
[寸法](寸法.md) (すんぽう) names dimensions rather than the surveying operation.

## Distinctive meaning

測量 does not simply mean "look around."
The practical land-surveying sense involves establishing spatial information.
At the same time, the broad dictionary gloss "measurement" should not be
turned into a rule that 測量 can only ever concern land.
Use the whole situation to choose between overlapping measuring words.
