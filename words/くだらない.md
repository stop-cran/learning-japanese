# くだらない - worthless; trivial; foolish

## Meaning

くだらない judges something as not worth taking seriously: trivial, worthless, useless, stupid or nonsensical. It is an i-adjective and is usually written in kana.

- くだらないことでけんかしないでください。 - Please do not quarrel over something so trivial.
- くだらない冗談を言ってしまいました。 (くだらないじょうだんをいってしまいました。) - I ended up telling a silly joke.

The adverbial form is くだらなく; the past form is くだらなかった.

## How the kanji combine

下らない is a dictionary spelling, containing [下](../kanji/下.md). Learn the evaluative adjective as a word, rather than interpreting every use as literally not descending.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[つまらない](つまらない.md) overlaps in trivial, worthless and foolish uses, as well as having a boring/uninteresting use. This is not a strict distinction between boredom and worthlessness.

[無駄](無駄.md) (むだ) foregrounds waste or futility. A joke can be くだらない because it is silly, without the speaker making a precise judgment about wasted resources.

## Distinctive meaning

This is a dismissive evaluation, not a neutral way to say small or simple. Applied to a person's work or concerns, it can sound insulting. In a friendly setting, calling one's own joke くだらない can instead be self-deprecating.
