# 火曜日 (かようび) - Tuesday

## Meaning

火曜日 means Tuesday. A weekday can take に to mark when an event happens.

- 火曜日に図書館へ行きます。 (かようびにとしょかんへいきます。) - I will go to the library on Tuesday.

火曜日の授業 (かようびのじゅぎょう) means "Tuesday's class." 毎週火曜日 (まいしゅうかようび) means "every Tuesday," rather than one particular Tuesday.

## How the kanji combine

[火](../kanji/火.md) is カ, [曜](../kanji/曜.md) is ヨウ, and [日](../kanji/日.md) is び here. The word begins with on readings but ends with the voiced native reading of 日, so the complete reading is mixed, not all-on.

## Synonyms and antonyms

月曜日 (げつようび) is Monday; 水曜日 (すいようび) is Wednesday. In schedules 火曜 (かよう) is a shortened form of 火曜日.

## Distinctive meaning

The first character identifies the weekday. 火曜日 does not mean any day associated with fire; learn it as a fixed calendar name. Context determines which week's Tuesday is intended.
