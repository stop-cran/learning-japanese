# 札 (さつ) - banknote; paper money

## Meaning

札 read さつ means a banknote or bill: paper money rather than a coin.
It also follows a denomination, as in 千円札 (せんえんさつ), "a
1,000-yen note." Individual notes can be counted with 枚 (まい).

- 千円札を一枚出しました。 (せんえんさつをいちまいだしました。) - I took out one 1,000-yen note.
- 財布には札が二枚入っています。 (さいふにはさつがにまいはいっています。) - There are two banknotes in my wallet.

The number of notes and their monetary value are different quantities.

## How the kanji combine

札 is one character, using the on reading サツ for this money word.
There is no compound to divide. The same character also writes a different
word read ふだ; the spelling alone does not settle the meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

紙幣 (しへい) also means banknotes or paper currency.
[お金](お金.md) (おかね) is money more generally, including coins.
硬貨 (こうか), "coins," contrasts by form of currency rather than being an
opposite of having money.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep 札 (ふだ) separate: that dictionary entry covers tickets or tokens, labels,
cards, and talismans. A tag or charm is not the ordinary meaning of さつ.
The community source pairs さつ with those ふだ glosses; this card retains
the assigned reading and teaches the supported banknote identity instead.
This correction does not establish what the historical compiler intended.
