# 用語 (ようご) - term; terminology; wording

## Meaning

用語 can mean a term or the terminology used in a field or context.
It also concerns wording or the choice of words, so it is not
restricted to obscure technical jargon.

- この本には医学の用語がたくさん出てきます。 (このほんにはいがくのようごがたくさんでてきます。) - Many medical terms appear in this book.
- 説明書の用語を統一しました。 (せつめいしょのようごをとういつしました。) - We made the terminology in the manual consistent.

## How the kanji combine

[用](../kanji/用.md), use, qualifies [語](../kanji/語.md), words
or language: the words used for a purpose or in a context.
The on readings are ヨウ + ゴ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[言葉](言葉.md) (ことば) is the broad word/expression term.
[単語](単語.md) (たんご) focuses on a vocabulary item, while
[語](語.md) (ご) can mean a word or occur as a language suffix.
[言葉遣い](言葉遣い.md) (ことばづかい) overlaps in wording and manner
of speaking; [表現](表現.md) (ひょうげん) is an expression or
representation more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

Context can make 用語 collective, "terminology," or refer to an
individual term. It is not the same thing as a whole language,
nor does calling something 用語 guarantee that it is specialist,
difficult or unfamiliar.
There is no useful direct antonym across all these uses.
