# モノレール - monorail

## Meaning

モノレール is a monorail: a railway system in which a single rail or beam
supports or guides the train.
モノレールに乗る (ものれーるにのる) means to take a monorail.

- 空港までモノレールで行きました。 (くうこうまでものれーるでいきました。) - I went to the airport by monorail.
- 次の駅でモノレールに乗り換えます。 (つぎのえきでものれーるにのりかえます。) - I will transfer to the monorail at the next station.

で marks the means of transport in the first example;
に marks the service transferred to in the second.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana borrowing corresponding to "monorail," with a long vowel in レール.
It is not a Japanese 物 + レール compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鉄道](鉄道.md) (てつどう) is railway more broadly.
[電車](電車.md) (でんしゃ) identifies an electric train;
[地下鉄](地下鉄.md) (ちかてつ) is a subway.
These categories concern different features and are not general synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Some monorails straddle the supporting beam, while others hang beneath it.
Do not define the word as requiring only one of those designs.
An elevated railway is not automatically a monorail merely because it runs above a road.
