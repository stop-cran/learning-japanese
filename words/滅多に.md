# 滅多に (めったに) - rarely; seldom

## Meaning

滅多に combines with a negative predicate to say that something happens rarely.
Learn the complete pattern 滅多に...ない, not just a positive English adverb.

- この町では雪は滅多に降りません。 (このまちではゆきはめったにふりません。) - It rarely snows in this town.
- 忙しくて、友達には滅多に会えません。 (いそがしくて、ともだちにはめったにあえません。) - I am busy and rarely get to see my friends.

降りません is the negative of "fall," while 会えません is the negative
potential "cannot meet." Both provide the required negative construction.

## How the kanji combine

JMdict marks 滅多 as an ateji spelling: do not derive the word's meaning by
translating 滅 and [多](../kanji/多.md) separately. This is a native expression,
also written めったに. The small っ and the final に are part of the learned form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[たまに](たまに.md), "occasionally," and [時々](時々.md) (ときどき),
"sometimes," can describe infrequent or intermittent events with affirmative
predicates. 滅多に...ない presents them as rare from a negative perspective.

## Distinctive meaning

滅多に会わない does not mean "never meet"; exceptions remain possible.
Conversely, dropping ない from that pattern does not simply preserve
"rarely." Frequency depends on the activity and context, so the word does
not assign a fixed number of occurrences per month or year.
