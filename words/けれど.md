# けれど - but, although

## Meaning

けれど connects contrasting clauses: "but" or "although." Attach it to a plain verb or i-adjective; after a present affirmative noun or na-adjective, include だ.

- 雨が降っているけれど、出かけます。 (あめがふっているけれど、でかけます。) - It is raining, but I am going out.
- この店は便利だけれど、高いです。 (このみせはべんりだけれど、たかいです。) - This shop is convenient but expensive.

It can also follow polite endings: ですけれど, ますけれど.

## How the kanji combine

This connecting expression is written in hiragana. けれども is a longer form, while けど is common in casual conversation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

が can also connect contrasting clauses. しかし introduces a contrast as a separate connective, "however," rather than attaching in exactly the same way.

## Distinctive meaning

Sentence-final けれど can leave the rest implied or soften an introduction to a request. It therefore does not always announce an explicit opposing clause. Its politeness depends partly on the surrounding sentence; plain-form けれど is not automatically formal speech.
