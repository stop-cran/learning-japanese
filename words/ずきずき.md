# ずきずき — throbbing with pain

## Meaning

Pain felt in repeated throbs or pulses.

- 奥歯（おくば）がずきずき痛む。 — My back tooth is throbbing.
- 昨日ぶつけた足が、まだずきずきする。 — The foot I bumped yesterday is still throbbing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[がんがん](がんがん.md) also describes a pounding headache. [じんじん](じんじん.md) overlaps in throbbing or tingling; [ちくちく](ちくちく.md) often suggests small needle-like pricks.

## Distinctive meaning

Both ずきずきする and ずきずき痛む（いたむ）are useful. The word describes someone's sensation, not its cause or a diagnosis. It is not simply another spelling of [どきどき](どきどき.md), the familiar heart-pounding expression.
