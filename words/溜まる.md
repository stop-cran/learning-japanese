# 溜まる (たまる) - accumulate; build up

## Meaning

溜まる means that things collect, build up, or accumulate.
It is an intransitive godan verb, usually written たまる.
The accumulating thing takes が; a location can take に.

- バケツに雨水がたまっています。
  (バケツにあまみずがたまっています。) - Rainwater has collected in the bucket.
- 忙しくて、仕事がたまってしまいました。
  (いそがしくて、しごとがたまってしまいました。) - I was busy, and work piled up.

たまっている often describes the resulting accumulation.
Forms include たまります, たまった, たまって, and たまらない.

## How the kanji combine

溜 contributes collecting or accumulating. In 溜まる it writes
た, followed by まる. The kana spelling is useful alongside the
written form; no on-reading compound is being assembled.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[溜める](溜める.md) (ためる) is transitive: 水をためる,
"collect/store water," versus 水がたまる, "water collects."
[積もる](積もる.md) (つもる) overlaps with a buildup,
often in layers such as snow or dust.

## Distinctive meaning

Accumulation may be welcome or unwelcome: money, water, work,
or stress can build up. 貯まる, also たまる, is separately
recorded for money being saved up. Do not infer an absolute spelling
ban from that distinction; public dictionaries also recognize
financial accumulation under 溜まる.
