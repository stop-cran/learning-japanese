# 嫌がる (いやがる) - show dislike; seem unwilling

## Meaning

嫌がる means to show dislike or reluctance, or to appear uncomfortable
with something. It is a transitive godan verb; the unwanted thing or
action can take を.

- 子供は苦い薬を飲むのを嫌がっています。 (こどもはにがいくすりをのむのをいやがっています。) - The child is showing reluctance to take the bitter medicine.
- 彼女は写真を撮られるのを嫌がりました。 (かのじょはしゃしんをとられるのをいやがりました。) - She objected to having her photograph taken.

Verb + の makes the action into something that can take を.
The second example reports her response, not just the observer's own
dislike of taking photographs.

## How the kanji combine

[嫌](嫌.md) (いや), "unpleasant; unwanted," combines with がる,
which here describes displaying or seeming to have that feeling.
Forms include 嫌がります, 嫌がって, and 嫌がらない.
厭がる is a dictionary-listed rare spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[嫌い](嫌い.md) (きらい) states dislike with な-adjective grammar;
[嫌う](嫌う.md) (きらう) is a verb meaning dislike or shun.
嫌がる makes an outward reaction or apparent reluctance especially
relevant. [喜ぶ](喜ぶ.md) (よろこぶ), "be pleased," offers a contrasting
reaction in a suitable context.

## Distinctive meaning

It is often useful when describing someone else's observable reaction.
That is a usage tendency, not an absolute ban on self-reference.
Distinguish disliking a thing from displaying reluctance toward it;
the constructions can overlap without being identical.
