# 先程 (さきほど) - a short while ago

## Meaning

先程 means a short while ago or just now. 先ほど is another spelling of the same word.
It can introduce the time directly, without に.

- 先程、先生から電話がありました。 (さきほど、せんせいからでんわがありました。) - The teacher called a little while ago.
- 先ほどの説明をもう一度お願いします。 (さきほどのせつめいをもういちどおねがいします。) - Please explain again what you explained a little while ago.

Use の before a noun, as in 先ほどの説明.

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md) contributes earlier or before, and [程](../kanji/程.md) contributes an extent.
The native readings さき + ほど form this recent-past expression. Writing ほど in kana does not change its pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[さっき](さっき.md) also means a short while ago and is useful in ordinary conversation.
[最近](最近.md) (さいきん), "recently; these days," can cover a current period or habit.
[こないだ](こないだ.md), "the other day," points to a recent occasion rather than necessarily the immediately preceding part of this conversation.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss "some time ago" does not license an arbitrarily distant past.
先程 keeps the reference relatively close to now, without specifying an exact number of minutes.
It is not simply another reading for every use of 先 (さき).
