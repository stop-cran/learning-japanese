# 反対 (はんたい) — opposition; opposite

## Meaning

反対 names opposition or disagreement. Use に for what someone opposes, either with 反対する
or with 反対です.

- 私はその計画に反対です。 (わたしはそのけいかくにはんたいです。) — I am against that plan.

A separate sense means the opposite or reverse: 反対の方向 (はんたいのほうこう), "the opposite direction."
Here の connects 反対 to the noun.

## How the kanji combine

[反](../kanji/反.md) contributes reversal or opposition, and [対](../kanji/対.md) contributes facing or standing against something.
Together they can express an opposing position, whether an opinion or a direction.
The on readings are ハン + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい), "approval; support," contrasts with opposition to a proposal:
計画に賛成する (けいかくにさんせいする), "support the plan."
It is not the opposite of every use of 反対: a road does not have a "賛成 direction."

## Distinctive meaning

In an opinion, 反対 says where someone stands; it does not by itself tell us how strongly or angrily they object.
In a spatial description, it need not involve disagreement at all.
Learn the noun connection 反対の方向 separately from the verbal pattern 計画に反対する.
