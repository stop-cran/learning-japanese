# 何でも (なんでも) - anything; whatever

## Meaning

何でも means anything, whatever, or everything, depending on the sentence. It presents the choice or range without restricting it to a particular thing. The kana spelling なんでも is also used.

- 分からないことは何でも聞いてください。 (わからないことはなんでもきいてください。) - Please ask anything you do not understand.
- 彼は何でもよく知っています。 (かれはなんでもよくしっています。) - He knows a lot about everything.

There is a separate sentence-initial hearsay use: 何でも、来月引っ越すそうです (なんでも、らいげつひっこすそうです), "I hear they are moving next month." Here 何でも introduces reported information, not an unrestricted object.

## How the kanji combine

[何](../kanji/何.md) contributes "what" and is read なん here. Combined with でも, the expression extends beyond a particular answer: "whatever it is." Do not read this headword as なにでも merely by borrowing 何's reading from another construction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[何か](何か.md) (なにか) means something, whereas 何でも leaves the choice unrestricted. 何も with a negative predicate means nothing: 何も食べない, "eat nothing." That is a different construction, not simply the same expression with a negative ending.

## Distinctive meaning

何でもいい (なんでもいい) means "anything is fine." It does not turn standalone 何でも into an assent meaning "by all means." Also distinguish unrestricted choice from literal omniscience: the second example can be conversational praise rather than a claim that someone knows every fact.
