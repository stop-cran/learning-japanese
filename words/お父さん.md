# お父さん (おとうさん) — father, dad

## Meaning

お父さん is how one refers to or addresses someone's father, and how children address their own father: お父さんは会社にいます (Dad is at the office), 田中さんのお父さん (Mr Tanaka's father).

## How the kanji combine

お (honorific prefix) + [父](../kanji/父.md) + さん (respectful suffix). The 父 is read とう here, a reading that is not one of the standard on or kun readings (フ, ちち) but belongs to the word とうさん.
This is a native Japanese word with a special reading of the kanji, not a jukujikun (a whole word whose reading is not built from the kanji's own readings) of a kanji pair. Short forms: 父さん とうさん; the spelling お父様 おとうさま is more respectful.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Own father, to outsiders: 父 ちち; more informal: 親父 おやじ (rough, male speech); パパ (child-like).
- Antonym: [お母さん](お母さん.md) おかあさん (mother).

## Distinctive meaning

Use 父 ちち for your own father when speaking to people outside the family, and お父さん for someone else's father or when talking to your father directly.
