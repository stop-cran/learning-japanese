# 鉱物 (こうぶつ) - mineral

## Meaning

鉱物 is a mineral. It is a noun used when discussing natural materials,
their properties, or specimens.

- この博物館には多くの鉱物が展示されています。 (このはくぶつかんにはおおくのこうぶつがてんじされています。) - Many minerals are displayed in this museum.
- この鉱物の名前を調べています。 (このこうぶつのなまえをしらべています。) - I am looking up this mineral's name.

JMdict also recognizes 礦物 with the same reading and marks that
written form as rarely used. That flag does not measure its frequency
in a particular scientific text.

## How the kanji combine

[鉱](../kanji/鉱.md) contributes mineral or ore, and
[物](../kanji/物.md) contributes material or thing.
鉱 qualifies 物: a mineral substance. The on readings are コウ + ブツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鉱石](鉱石.md) (こうせき) often concerns ore from which useful material
can be obtained; its dictionary meanings also include mineral.
[岩](岩.md) (いわ), rock, and [金属](金属.md) (きんぞく), metal,
are related material terms, not unrestricted substitutes.
There is no useful single antonym for every mineral.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that every mineral is a metal, gemstone, or economically
useful ore. This card identifies the vocabulary item rather than giving
an exhaustive scientific classification of minerals and rocks.
