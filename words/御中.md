# 御中 (おんちゅう) - organization address honorific

## Meaning

御中 is a respectful suffix written after the name of a company, department, public office, or other organization receiving a letter. It is not attached to an individual person's name.

- 「営業部 御中」と書きます。 (「えいぎょうぶ おんちゅう」とかきます。) - I write "To the Sales Department," addressing the department respectfully.
- 個人宛てなら、「田中様」のように書きます。 (こじんあてなら、「たなかさま」のようにかきます。) - For an individual addressee, write something like "Mr./Ms. Tanaka."

These illustrate address lines, not ways to call out to someone in conversation.

## How the kanji combine

[御](../kanji/御.md) supplies the respectful prefix おん; [中](../kanji/中.md), read チュウ, contributes the within/group element. Learn the established address suffix as おんちゅう rather than translating the characters literally. This combines the native prefix reading おん with the on reading チュウ; it is not an all-kun or ordinary ゴ + チュウ reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The personal-name suffix [様](様.md) (さま, distinct from the linked article's primary reading よう) is the practical contrast. Compare 営業部 御中, addressed to the department, with 営業部 田中様 (えいぎょうぶ たなかさま), addressed to Tanaka in that department.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not stack 御中 and 様 for the same addressee: choose the organizational or individual address form. Dictionary translations such as "Messrs." or "... and Company" describe its correspondence function; 御中 is not a general translation of English "and company" in ordinary sentences.
