# 地位 (ちい) — social position

## Meaning

地位 means position, status, or standing in a society or organization.
The "position" here concerns where someone stands in a social ordering, not where their body is located.
Use の when connecting it to a following noun, and phrases such as 地位が高い (ちいがたかい), "have high status."

- 彼は会社で高い地位にあります。 (かれはかいしゃでたかいちいにあります。) — He holds a high position in the company.
- 社会的な地位だけで人を判断してはいけません。 (しゃかいてきなちいだけでひとをはんだんしてはいけません。) — You should not judge people by social status alone.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) has a ground or place association, while [位](../kanji/位.md) supplies rank or position.
Think of a place in a social ordering, not literally a patch of ground.
The on readings are チ + イ: pronounce both vowels in ちい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[位置](位置.md) (いち) can mean a location or position and also has figurative standing senses.
For the location of a desk in a room, 位置 is the relevant word, not this social-status use of 地位.
The words overlap for some figurative positions; the contrast is not "位置 is always physical."

## Distinctive meaning

地位 describes a person's standing, not their personal worth or exact job duties.
High and low status are expressed with modifiers such as 高い (たかい) and 低い (ひくい);
there is no need to invent a single opposite word for 地位 itself.
