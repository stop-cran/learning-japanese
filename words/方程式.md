# 方程式 (ほうていしき) - equation; formula

## Meaning

方程式 is an equation: an equality involving an unknown whose values are to be
found. For example, x + 2 = 5 is true when x = 3.

- この方程式を解いてください。 (このほうていしきをといてください。) - Please solve this equation.
- 方程式の答えが分かりました。 (ほうていしきのこたえがわかりました。) - I found the answer to the equation.

## How the kanji combine

Read [方](../kanji/方.md) ホウ + [程](../kanji/程.md) テイ +
[式](../kanji/式.md) シキ as ほうていしき.
式 contributes a mathematical expression/formula association. Learn 方程式
as the established technical word, not a literal instruction about direction or distance.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[式](式.md) (しき) is broader: a mathematical expression need not be an equation.
[公式](公式.md) (こうしき) can be a mathematical formula or standard rule;
not every formula is an equation to solve. [解く](解く.md) (とく) has the
relevant "solve" sense in 方程式を解く.

## Distinctive meaning

Figuratively, 方程式 can be an established method for solving a problem or
achieving a result. 成功の方程式 (せいこうのほうていしき), "a formula for
success," need not contain numbers or an equals sign.
Compare [方法](方法.md) (ほうほう), a general method word; the figurative
方程式 suggests a set way of getting the result rather than every possible approach.
