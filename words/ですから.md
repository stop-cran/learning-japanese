# ですから (ですから) - therefore; for that reason

## Meaning

ですから is a polite conjunction meaning "so; therefore; for that reason." It links an earlier reason or situation to a consequence, conclusion, or recommendation. It can begin a new sentence.

- 明日は試験です。ですから、今夜は勉強します。 (あしたはしけんです。ですから、こんやはべんきょうします。) - I have an exam tomorrow. Therefore, I will study tonight.
- この道は危険です。ですから、別の道を通りましょう。 (このみちはきけんです。ですから、べつのみちをとおりましょう。) - This road is dangerous. For that reason, let's take another road.

No additional に or を is needed after the conjunction.

## How the kanji combine

ですから is written in hiragana. Its recognizable parts are polite です and reason-marking から. Here the complete expression connects statements; there are no kanji components to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[だから](だから.md) gives the corresponding plain-style connection. [それで](それで.md) can also link a situation to what follows, while [しかし](しかし.md), "however," introduces a contrast rather than the same causal relationship.

These are discourse alternatives, not interchangeable words in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

Polite form does not guarantee a gentle tone. Repeating ですから can sound insistent when the speaker feels a reason has already been explained. Also distinguish the standalone connector from a clause ending such as 学生ですから (がくせいですから), "because I am a student." In that clause, the reason is stated within the same grammatical unit.
