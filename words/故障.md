# 故障 (こしょう) - breakdown; malfunction

## Meaning

故障 is a fault or breakdown that prevents a machine from working properly.
故障する is intransitive in this use: the machine takes が, not を.

- エアコンが故障しました。 (エアコンがこしょうしました。) - The air conditioner broke down.
- このコピー機は故障しています。 (このコピーきはこしょうしています。) - This photocopier is out of order.

The second sentence describes the resulting state, not necessarily a breakdown
happening at that moment.

## How the kanji combine

故 can concern a cause or trouble, and 障 suggests an obstruction.
The compound names a functional problem; its on-readings コ + ショウ give こしょう.
It is not related to the seasoning こしょう, "pepper."

## Synonyms and antonyms

壊れる (こわれる), "break," is broader and can describe a smashed object.
修理 (しゅうり), "repair," names the response to a fault, not the opposite state.

## Distinctive meaning

A machine can 故障する without visibly breaking into pieces.
The sign 故障中 (こしょうちゅう) means "out of order." This article teaches
equipment failure, rather than the word's additional uses for injuries or obstacles.
