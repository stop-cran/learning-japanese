# ジャム

## Meaning

ジャム is jam, the sweet fruit preserve spread on bread or used in food.

- パンにジャムを塗りました。 (パンに ジャムを ぬりました。) — I spread jam on the bread.
- いちごのジャムを作ります。 (いちごの ジャムを つくります。) — I will make strawberry jam.

In 塗る (ぬる), を marks the substance spread and に marks the surface receiving it.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana. ジャ combines ジ with small ャ to make one syllable; it is not pronounced as separate ジ and ヤ. No kanji decomposition is needed.

## Synonyms and antonyms

マーマレード is marmalade, a related preserve. バター and はちみつ are other possible spreads, not synonyms or antonyms of jam.

## Distinctive meaning

The beginner food sense does not make ジャム the default translation of every English "jam." A traffic jam is 渋滞 (じゅうたい). Learn technical or musical uses separately rather than transferring all English meanings to this food word.
