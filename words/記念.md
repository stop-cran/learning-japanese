# 記念 (きねん) - commemoration; keepsake

## Meaning

記念 is a noun for marking or remembering a significant occasion. Nを記念する means
"commemorate N"; Nの記念に means "to commemorate N" or "as a memento of N."
The noun can also refer to a keepsake.

- 卒業の記念に写真を撮りました。 (そつぎょうのきねんにしゃしんをとりました。) - We took a photograph to commemorate graduation.
- この切符は旅の記念です。 (このきっぷはたびのきねんです。) - This ticket is a keepsake from the trip.

The first example describes an action that preserves a memory. The second identifies an object
kept for its association with an experience.

## How the kanji combine

[記](../kanji/記.md) concerns recording or noting, and [念](../kanji/念.md) concerns thought or remembrance.
Together they suggest keeping an event in mind through a deliberate act or object.
The on readings are キ + ネン; the reading is きねん, not きんねん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[記憶](記憶.md) (きおく) is memory or remembering as a mental function.
[思い出](思い出.md) (おもいで) is a recollection or remembered experience.
[祝い](祝い.md) (いわい) focuses on celebration; an act of 記念 can celebrate an occasion while also preserving it.

## Distinctive meaning

記念 does not serve as the general word for every memory in one's head.
Useful combinations include 記念写真 (きねんしゃしん), "commemorative photograph,"
and 記念日 (きねんび), "anniversary; commemorative day."
The object in the second example need not be expensive: its connection to the trip makes it meaningful.
