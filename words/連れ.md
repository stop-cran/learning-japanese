# 連れ (つれ) - companion; accompanying person

## Meaning

連れ is a companion: someone accompanying a person on an outing or in a
particular situation. It is a noun, as in 私の連れ (わたしのつれ),
"the person with me."

- 連れをここで待っています。 (つれをここでまっています。) - I am waiting here for my companion.
- 私の連れは先に店に入りました。 (わたしのつれはさきにみせにはいりました。) - The person with me went into the shop first.

In customer service, お連れの方 (おつれのかた) is a courteous way to refer
to the person accompanying a customer.

## How the kanji combine

[連](../kanji/連.md) contributes accompanying or linking. 連れ is related to
the native verb 連れる (つれる), "take someone along." The noun uses つれ,
not the on reading レン found in compounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[友達](友達.md) (ともだち) identifies a friend.
[仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) identifies a fellow member or companion in a group.
連れ focuses on being together in the situation, without necessarily
specifying the longer-term relationship.

## Distinctive meaning

English "company" here means companionship or the people with someone,
not a business organization. A 連れ could be a friend, relative, or partner;
the noun alone does not establish marriage or romance. It is also not
automatically a polite direct form of address to that person.
