# 住宅 (じゅうたく) - housing; residence

## Meaning

住宅 is a residence, residential building, or housing collectively. It is a noun for where people live, not for the people themselves.

- 駅の近くに新しい住宅が建ちました。 (えきのちかくにあたらしいじゅうたくがたちました。) - New housing has been built near the station.
- 市は住宅の不足を調べています。 (しはじゅうたくのふそくをしらべています。) - The city is investigating the housing shortage.

住宅を建てる (じゅうたくをたてる) means to build a residence. The second example concerns the availability of places to live, not a shortage of residents.

## How the kanji combine

[住](../kanji/住.md) contributes living or residing, and [宅](../kanji/宅.md) contributes a dwelling. The components reinforce residential use. The readings are ジュウ + タク; じゅう has a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[家](家.md) (いえ) can name a house or home in everyday life. 住宅 is useful when discussing buildings and housing as a category. 住民 (じゅうみん) means residents, the people living in a place, and is not a synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list's English "resident" is misleading here: **residence** is the intended dictionary-supported teaching meaning. Compounds such as 住宅地 (じゅうたくち), "residential area," preserve the housing sense. A residence can be occupied by residents, but the two nouns identify different things.
