# しょうがない - cannot be helped; hopeless; unbearably

## Meaning

しょうがない is a conversational expression meaning that something cannot be helped or there is no other way. It also appears when something is pointless, someone or something is troublesome or hopeless, or a feeling is extremely strong.

- 雨だから、今日はしょうがない。 (あめだから、きょうはしょうがない。) - It is raining, so it cannot be helped today.
- 今さら後悔してもしょうがない。 (いまさらこうかいしてもしょうがない。) - There is no point regretting it now.
- 眠くてしょうがない。 (ねむくてしょうがない。) - I am unbearably sleepy.
- しょうがない人だね。 (しょうがないひとだね。) - What a troublesome person.

For the intense-feeling construction, attach しょうがない to a verb's て-form, an い-adjective's くて-form, or a な-adjective with で: 会いたくてしょうがない (あいたくてしょうがない), "I want to see you terribly"; 心配でしょうがない (しんぱいでしょうがない), "I cannot stop worrying." This is not a rule allowing any noun followed by でしょうがない.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is normal. しょうがない is a shortened form of しようがない, also recorded as a reading in the same dictionary family. The rare spelling 仕様がない supplies a connection with 仕様 (しよう), a way or means, plus がない. It is not an expression about ginger, 生姜 (しょうが).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕方](仕方.md)がない (しかたがない) overlaps in the cannot-be-helped expression. [たまらない](たまらない.md) overlaps with intense feelings; [無駄](無駄.md) with pointlessness. [面倒](面倒.md), [厄介](厄介.md), and [うるさい](うるさい.md) overlap with troublesome or annoying evaluations, not with every use above.

## Distinctive meaning

In 眠くてしょうがない, the expression intensifies sleepiness rather than neutrally declaring that an event is inevitable. Said about a person, it may be critical or affectionate depending on tone. Choose a more formal formulation when necessary rather than treating this colloquial expression as a neutral label in every register.
