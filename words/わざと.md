# わざと (わざと) - deliberately; on purpose

## Meaning

わざと is an adverb meaning "deliberately; on purpose." It modifies an action and identifies it as intentional, rather than accidental.

- わざと間違えたのではありません。 (わざとまちがえたのではありません。) - I did not make the mistake on purpose.
- 子供にも分かるように、わざとゆっくり話しました。 (こどもにもわかるように、わざとゆっくりはなしました。) - I deliberately spoke slowly so the children could understand too.

In the first example, the speaker denies intention, not necessarily the mistake itself. The second shows that a deliberate action can have a helpful purpose.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 態と is a recorded rare kanji spelling, but わざと is the straightforward form to learn. There is no multi-kanji compound in this spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[偶然](偶然.md) (ぐうぜん), "by chance," contrasts with intention in suitable contexts. わざわざ means taking the trouble to do something; it is not simply another spelling or a stronger version of わざと.

## Distinctive meaning

わざと can sound accusatory when it attributes a bad action to someone's intention, but the adverb does not inherently mean maliciously. Context supplies the purpose. Saying わざとではない, "not intentional," also does not automatically remove responsibility for an outcome.
