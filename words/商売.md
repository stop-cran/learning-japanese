# 商売 (しょうばい) - trade; business

## Meaning

商売 is business or trade as an activity. 商売をする or 商売する means to
do business. Useful combinations include 商売を始める
(しょうばいをはじめる), "start a business," and 商売がうまくいく,
"business goes well."

- 祖父はこの町で長く商売をしていました。 (そふはこのまちでながくしょうばいをしていました。) - My grandfather did business in this town for many years.
- 最近は商売がうまくいっています。 (さいきんはしょうばいがうまくいっています。) - Business has been going well recently.

The word focuses on commercial activity, not automatically a legally registered company.

## How the kanji combine

[商](../kanji/商.md) contributes commerce, and [売](../kanji/売.md) contributes
selling. The related commercial ideas combine to name doing business.
The on readings are しょう + ばい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) is work or a job more broadly; not all work is trade.
[店](店.md) (みせ) is a shop, a place or business where trading can occur,
rather than the activity itself. Buying and selling are parts of commerce,
so "buying" is not the opposite of 商売.

## Distinctive meaning

商売 can also colloquially mean someone's occupation or line of work:
商売柄 (しょうばいがら), "because of the nature of one's work," reflects
that extension. This does not make it a neutral replacement for every job title.
In 商売がうまくいく, the focus is how the business is doing, not how pleasant
one particular task feels.
