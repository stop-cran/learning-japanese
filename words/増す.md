# 増す (ます) - increase; grow greater

## Meaning

増す is a godan verb meaning to increase or grow greater. It has intransitive and transitive uses: a quantity can increase with が, or something can gain a quality expressed with を.

- 雨で川の水が増しました。 (あめでかわのみずがましました。) - The river's water increased because of the rain.
- 台風は勢いを増しています。 (たいふうはいきおいをましています。) - The typhoon is gaining strength.

The second construction is 勢いを増す (いきおいをます), "gain momentum/strength." It is not an instruction to supply more water.

## How the kanji combine

[増](../kanji/増.md) supplies the increase idea, with す as the native verb ending. Despite the reading ます, this is a lexical verb, not the polite auxiliary attached to another verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[増える](増える.md) (ふえる) is intransitive, "increase"; 増やす (ふやす) is transitive, "increase something."

[減る](減る.md) (へる), "decrease," provides the quantity contrast. Which verb fits depends on the noun and construction, not just an English transitivity label.

## Distinctive meaning

増す is useful for growing intensity as well as amount: 不安が増す (ふあんがます), "anxiety increases." Its polite form is 増します (まします), past 増した (ました), and negative 増さない (まさない). Keep those forms distinct from a sentence's ordinary polite ます ending.
