# 狭い (せまい) — narrow; cramped

## Meaning

狭い describes limited width or space: narrow, small, or cramped. It is an i-adjective, so use 狭い道 (せまいみち), "a narrow road," without な.

- この道は狭いです。 (このみちはせまいです。) — This road is narrow.
- 前の部屋は狭かったです。 (まえのへやはせまかったです。) — My previous room was cramped.

The negative is 狭くない (せまくない), "not narrow/cramped."

## How the kanji combine

狭 carries the limited-space meaning, with い completing the native adjective せまい. There are no separately pronounced compound parts.

## Synonyms and antonyms

広い (ひろい), "wide/spacious," is the direct spatial contrast. 小さい (ちいさい) describes small size more generally: a small object is not necessarily 狭い.

## Distinctive meaning

狭い commonly evaluates usable space or width, not simply an object's overall dimensions. It can also describe limited outlook or scope, but 狭い部屋 and 狭い道 are useful starting patterns.
