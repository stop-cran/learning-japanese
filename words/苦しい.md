# 苦しい (くるしい) - painful; distressing

## Meaning

苦しい is an い-adjective describing suffering, distress, or a hard situation.
It can concern the body, emotions, or strained finances. Use 苦しい生活
(くるしいせいかつ), "a hard life," or 生活が苦しい, "life is financially hard."
Its negative is 苦しくない and its past is 苦しかった.

- 坂を走って上ったので、息が苦しいです。 (さかをはしってのぼったので、いきがくるしいです。) - I ran up the hill, so I am struggling for breath.
- 仕事がなくなって、生活が苦しくなりました。 (しごとがなくなって、せいかつがくるしくなりました。) - After losing my job, I began struggling financially.

The second sentence describes hardship, not literal pain in an object.

## How the kanji combine

[苦](../kanji/苦.md) conveys suffering or difficulty. It writes くる- in
this native adjective, followed by しい. The same character appears in
[苦い](苦い.md) (にがい), "bitter," but that is a different word and reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[痛い](痛い.md) (いたい) directly describes pain, such as a toothache.
[辛い](辛い.md) (つらい) overlaps in painful or emotionally difficult situations.
[楽](楽.md) (らく), "comfortable; easy," can contrast with 苦しい.
These are useful comparisons, not fixed boundaries between physical and mental suffering.

## Distinctive meaning

苦しい emphasizes pressure or suffering rather than merely a puzzle being hard
to solve. 苦しい立場 (くるしいたちば), "a difficult position," extends the
same idea to circumstances. Choose the surrounding noun to show what kind of
hardship is meant; the adjective alone does not specify its cause.
