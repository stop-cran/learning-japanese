# 機関車 (きかんしゃ) - locomotive; train engine

## Meaning

機関車 is a locomotive: the engine vehicle of a train. It is a noun, not the name for every engine or for every complete train.

- 古い機関車を博物館で見ました。
  (ふるいきかんしゃをはくぶつかんでみました。) - I saw an old locomotive at a museum.
- 機関車が貨車を引いています。
  (きかんしゃがかしゃをひいています。) - A locomotive is pulling freight cars.

## How the kanji combine

[機](../kanji/機.md) and [関](../kanji/関.md) form [機関](機関.md) (きかん), here "engine" or "mechanism"; [車](../kanji/車.md) adds a vehicle. The on readings are キ + カン + シャ. The engine sense of 機関 matters here, not its institution sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[エンジン](エンジン.md) is an engine more generally. [汽車](汽車.md) (きしゃ) includes a steam locomotive or steam train. [列車](列車.md) (れっしゃ) names a railway train; it does not necessarily identify just its locomotive.

## Distinctive meaning

機関車 does not itself specify steam power. 蒸気機関車 (じょうききかんしゃ) specifies a steam locomotive; 電気機関車 (でんききかんしゃ) specifies an electric locomotive. Do not turn the connection with 汽車 into a rule that every 機関車 is steam-powered.
