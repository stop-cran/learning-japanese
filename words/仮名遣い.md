# 仮名遣い (かなづかい) - kana spelling; kana orthography

## Meaning

仮名遣い is kana spelling or orthography: conventions for choosing kana to write words and expressions. It is a noun.

- 正しい仮名遣いを辞書で確認しました。 (ただしいかなづかいをじしょでかくにんしました。) - I checked the correct kana spelling in a dictionary.
- 仮名遣いが間違っていたので、書き直しました。 (かなづかいがまちがっていたので、かきなおしました。) - I rewrote it because the kana spelling was wrong.

現代仮名遣い (げんだいかなづかい) names modern kana conventions; 歴史的仮名遣い (れきしてきかなづかい) names historical kana conventions.

## How the kanji combine

[仮名](仮名.md) (かな), kana, modifies 遣い (つかい), use. The beginning of the second element is voiced: かな + つかい becomes かなづかい. Keep づ in this spelling. [名](../kanji/名.md) is part of the contracted 仮名 unit, not an independently added な here.

仮名遣, かな遣い, かな使い, and 仮名使い are additional dictionary spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

綴り (つづり) also means spelling or orthography and is not restricted to kana. [送り仮名](送り仮名.md) (おくりがな) concerns the kana endings written after kanji, not every rule for kana spelling.

## Distinctive meaning

Kana spelling is not always a direct transcription of pronunciation. Modern conventions retain は, へ, and を for the corresponding particles, even when pronounced わ, え, and お. The official conventions include exceptions; these examples are not a complete spelling algorithm.
