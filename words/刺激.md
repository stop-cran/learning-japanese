# 刺激 (しげき) - stimulus; stimulation

## Meaning

刺激 is something that produces a physical or mental response: a stimulus, irritation, or motivating influence. 刺激する is transitive, with を marking what is stimulated. 刺激を受ける means "receive stimulation; be inspired."

- この石けんは肌への刺激が強いです。 (このせっけんははだへのしげきがつよいです。) - This soap is strongly irritating to the skin.
- 友達の作品から刺激を受けました。 (ともだちのさくひんからしげきをうけました。) - I was inspired by my friend's work.

In the second example, から identifies the source of the motivating influence. The noun need not refer to physical contact.

## How the kanji combine

刺 contributes pricking or piercing, and 激 contributes intensity or a strong effect. Together they provide an image of something provoking a response. This is a component-based explanation, not a claim that every stimulus is painful. The reading is シ + ゲキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[影響](影響.md) (えいきょう) is influence or effect more broadly. 刺激 highlights what prompts a response. [感動](感動.md) (かんどう) is being emotionally moved, rather than the provoking input itself.

There is no single opposite that covers both skin irritation and creative motivation.

## Distinctive meaning

刺激 is not automatically good or bad. Skin-care descriptions may aim for less 刺激, while a new artistic experience may offer welcome 刺激. 強い刺激 means a strong stimulus; whether that is desirable depends on what is being stimulated and why.
