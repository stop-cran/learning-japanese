# ぶうぶう — oinking; honking; grumbling

## Meaning

A low, repeated sound such as oinking or honking, and by extension vocal complaints.

- 豚（ぶた）がぶうぶう鳴（な）いている。 — The pig is oinking.
- 弟（おとうと）は順番（じゅんばん）を待ちながら、ぶうぶう文句（もんく）を言った。 — My younger brother grumbled while waiting for his turn.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぐずぐず](ぐずぐず.md) and [がたがた](がたがた.md) also have complaining uses. None is a neutral substitute for politely stating every disagreement.

## Distinctive meaning

ぶうぶう鳴る（なる）can concern a horn or a vibrating device, not an animal. A child-directed noun use can mean a car; Digital Daijisen also records a pig. The surrounding subject distinguishes these scenes.

Also written ブーブー or ぶーぶー. The cited JMdict snapshot keeps ぶうぶう as a search-only kana variant, not a separate word. That dictionary flag does not mean hiragana is incorrect.
