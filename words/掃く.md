# 掃く (はく) - sweep; brush clean

## Meaning

掃く means to sweep or brush a place clean, or sweep dirt away.
It is a transitive godan verb: the place cleaned or material swept can take を.

- ほうきで玄関を掃きました。 (ほうきでげんかんをはきました。) - I swept the entrance with a broom.
- 床のごみを掃いて集めます。 (ゆかのごみをはいてあつめます。) - I sweep up the rubbish on the floor.

で marks the tool in the first example. Forms include 掃きます, 掃いて,
掃いた and 掃かない; keep the いて te-form.

## How the kanji combine

[掃](../kanji/掃.md) contributes sweeping or brushing away and writes は before く.
The same pronunciation occurs in different verbs such as
[履く](履く.md), "put on footwear or lower-body clothing," and [吐く](吐く.md), "vomit."
Sound alone does not determine the spelling or action.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[掃除](掃除.md) (そうじ) and [清掃](清掃.md) (せいそう), with する,
name cleaning more broadly; neither requires a broom.
[払う](払う.md) (はらう) can describe brushing something off, besides its payment use.
[磨く](磨く.md) (みがく) focuses on rubbing or polishing, including brushing teeth.

## Distinctive meaning

掃く is not a general translation of every English "brush" or "gather."
Sweeping rubbish together in the example does not make it the verb for gathering people.
The dictionary's specialized silkworm use is separate from the household focus here;
the same-reading 刷く associated with applying paint is not the spelling taught by this card.
