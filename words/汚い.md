# 汚い (きたない)

## Meaning

汚い means dirty or unclean. It can also describe untidiness, messy handwriting, or unfair behavior.

- このタオルは汚いです。 (この タオルは きたないです。) — This towel is dirty.
- 字が汚くて、読めません。 (じが きたなくて、よめません。) — The handwriting is so messy that I cannot read it.

It is an i-adjective: 汚い靴 (きたないくつ), "dirty shoes"; negative 汚くない (きたなくない).

## How the kanji combine

汚 supplies きたな, followed by the adjective ending い. Although the word ends in ない, that sequence is not a grammatical negative here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

きれい contrasts with 汚い when it means "clean" or "neat." 汚れている (よごれている) describes being soiled. 散らかっている (ちらかっている) specifically describes things being scattered or a place being untidy.

## Distinctive meaning

汚い is evaluative and can sound insulting when applied directly to a person or their work. It is broader than visible dirt: 汚い手 (きたないて) can mean an underhanded tactic, not simply an unwashed hand.
