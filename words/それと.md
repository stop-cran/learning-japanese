# それと - and also; in addition

## Meaning

それと is a conjunction used to add another item or point: "and also" or "in addition to that." It is useful when adding something to a spoken list.

- パンを買ってきてください。それと、牛乳もお願いします。 (パンをかってきてください。それと、ぎゅうにゅうもおねがいします。) - Please buy some bread. And milk too, please.
- 明日は休みです。それと、来週も一日休みます。 (あしたはやすみです。それと、らいしゅうもいちにちやすみます。) - Tomorrow is a day off. Also, I will take one day off next week.

The added point has its own sentence grammar; それと introduces it rather than taking a noun-ending such as な.

## How the kanji combine

This expression is written in kana here. それ points back to what was just mentioned, and と provides the additive connection. Learn the whole linking expression rather than inventing a kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[そして](そして.md) also connects statements, while [それから](それから.md) can add another item or mark a later step. それと in these examples adds information without necessarily placing it later in time.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse それと with それとも, which introduces an alternative, often "or" in a question. The community source supplies no English gloss for this row; the additive conjunction taught here is supported by the dictionary, not a reconstructed claim about the list compiler's intended examples.
