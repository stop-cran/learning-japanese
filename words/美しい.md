# 美しい (うつくしい) — beautiful

## Meaning

美しい describes beauty: scenery, a voice, or something else one appreciates as beautiful.
It is an i-adjective, so it goes directly before a noun without な:
美しい景色 (うつくしいけしき), "beautiful scenery."

- 山から見た景色は美しかったです。
  (やまからみたけしきはうつくしかったです。)
  — The scenery seen from the mountain was beautiful.

## How the kanji combine

[美](../kanji/美.md) supplies the beauty meaning. In the written word, 美 represents うつく,
followed by しい in kana. This native adjective uses a kun reading.
Its past form is 美しかった (うつくしかった); its negative is 美しくない (うつくしくない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

きれい overlaps in the "beautiful" sense, but it is a na-adjective:
きれいな景色 (きれいなけしき). It can also mean clean or tidy.
For that cleanliness sense, 汚い (きたない), "dirty," is the useful opposite; do not turn
this into a rule that 美しい simply means "not dirty."

## Distinctive meaning

美しい evaluates beauty rather than cleanliness alone. A room can be tidy without being
especially beautiful. The word can also praise nonvisual qualities, as in 美しい声
(うつくしいこえ), "a beautiful voice"; it is not restricted to someone's appearance.
