# 境界 (きょうかい) - boundary; border; dividing limit

## Meaning

境界 is a boundary or border separating places, areas, or categories. It is a noun and need not describe a national border.

- ここが二つの町の境界です。
  (ここがふたつのまちのきょうかいです。) - This is the boundary between the two towns.
- 仕事と私生活の境界をはっきりさせたいです。
  (しごととしせいかつのきょうかいをはっきりさせたいです。) - I want to make the boundary between work and private life clear.

A boundary can be conceptual rather than a visible fence or painted line.

## How the kanji combine

[境](../kanji/境.md) and [界](../kanji/界.md) both contribute a boundary or division. Their similar meanings reinforce each other. The on readings are キョウ + カイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[境](境.md) (さかい) is another boundary word, and [国境](国境.md) (こっきょう) specifically identifies a national border. [限界](限界.md) (げんかい) often brings an extent or limit of capacity into focus, rather than the division between two areas.

## Distinctive meaning

English "limit" can translate several different perspectives: separation, an imposed restriction, or the greatest extent possible. Do not assume that every such use calls for 境界. The same-reading [教会](教会.md) (きょうかい), "church," is a different word.
