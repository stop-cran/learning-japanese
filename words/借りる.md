# 借りる (かりる) — borrow; rent

## Meaning

借りる means to receive something for temporary use, either by borrowing it or renting it.
Mark the item with を and the lender with から or に.

- 友達から本を借りました。 (ともだちからほんをかりました。) — I borrowed a book from a friend.
- 駅の近くで部屋を借りています。 (えきのちかくでへやをかりています。) — I rent a room near the station.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native verb: [借](../kanji/借.md) supplies か; りる is okurigana.
It is an ichidan verb: drop the final る for 借ります (かります) and 借りて (かりて).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[貸す](貸す.md) (かす, lend; rent out) describes the same arrangement from the other side:
友達は私に本を貸しました (ともだちはわたしにほんをかしました), "My friend lent me a book."
返す (かえす, return) is what the borrower does afterward, not another word for borrowing.

## Distinctive meaning

Borrowing and renting are both 借りる; payment is determined by context.
Do not reverse 借りる and 貸す: the borrower receives temporary use, while the lender provides it.
借りています can describe a continuing rental or a borrowed item still in one's possession.
