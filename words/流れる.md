# 流れる (ながれる) — flow

## Meaning

流れる describes something flowing or streaming. The thing that flows is the subject, marked with が
when it is stated in a basic sentence.
This article starts with liquid flow; the word also has uses beyond literal water.

- 水がゆっくり流れています。 (みずがゆっくりながれています。) — The water is flowing slowly.
- この川は町の中を流れています。 (このかわはまちのなかをながれています。) — This river flows through the town.

In the second sentence, を marks the space the river passes through, not an object being made to flow.
流れる is an ichidan verb: remove る to form 流れます (ながれます) or 流れて (ながれて).

## How the kanji combine

[流](../kanji/流.md) supplies the flow meaning and is read なが here; れる is written in kana.
That spelling boundary is not the conjugation boundary: the stem used with ます is 流れ (ながれ), formed by removing only the final る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

流す (ながす) is the useful transitive partner: 水を流す (みずをながす) means "let the water run."
It describes acting on the water, whereas 水が流れる (みずがながれる) describes the flow.
[浮く](浮く.md) (うく), "float," is a different property: something can float on still water.

## Distinctive meaning

流れる does not identify who caused the flow. A tap may have been opened by someone, but
水が流れている still focuses on the water's movement.
The contrast with 流す is about sentence structure and perspective, not whether a human cause exists.
