# うなる (うなる) - groan; growl; hum

## Meaning

うなる describes a low sound: a person's groan, an animal's growl, or the hum of an engine. In these uses it is an intransitive godan verb. The sound-maker takes が; its forms include うなります, うならない, and うなった.

- 彼は痛みでうなっていた。 (かれはいたみでうなっていた。) - He was groaning in pain.
- 犬が低い声でうなった。 (いぬがひくいこえでうなった。) - The dog gave a low growl.

エンジンがうなっている means "the engine is humming/roaring." The English choice depends on the sound and context.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses hiragana. The dictionary also records 唸る (うなる); the ending る belongs to the inflecting verb. There is no kanji compound or loanword formation to analyze in the assigned spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鳴く](鳴く.md) (なく) broadly describes an animal's call. うなる identifies the low growling quality more specifically. [叫ぶ](叫ぶ.md) (さけぶ), "shout/cry out," need not describe a low sound. Silence is a contextual contrast, not a special antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

うなる can also describe an admiring vocal reaction: 見事な演奏にうなった (みごとなえんそうにうなった), "I let out a sound of admiration at the splendid performance." It does not necessarily express pain or hostility. Despite ending in る, its negative is うならない, not うなない.
