# 絵 (え) — picture; drawing

## Meaning

絵 is a drawing, painting, or sketch. The word can describe a picture without deciding whether
it counts as fine art. A useful phrase is 絵を描く (えをかく), "draw or paint a picture."

- 子供が猫の絵を描きました。
  (こどもがねこのえをかきました。)
  — The child drew a picture of a cat.

## How the kanji combine

There is just one character: [絵](../kanji/絵.md), carrying the whole picture meaning.
エ is classified as an on reading, so this article uses `kango` even though the word is short
and stands alone. In 絵本 (えほん), "picture book," it combines with 本, "book."

## Synonyms and antonyms

絵画 (かいが) also means a painting or picture.
[写真](写真.md) (しゃしん) is a photograph. English calls both drawings and photographs
"pictures," but Japanese does not make 絵 and 写真 interchangeable labels for them.

## Distinctive meaning

In this drawing/painting sense, 絵 tells you that you are looking at a drawn or painted representation, not what material was
used to make it. 猫の絵 means a picture *of* a cat in the example; the noun before の can supply
the subject represented. A camera photograph of the same cat would be 猫の写真 (ねこのしゃしん).
