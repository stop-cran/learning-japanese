# 外国 (がいこく) — foreign country

## Meaning

外国 is a foreign country, or abroad: 外国に住む (live abroad), 外国語 がいこくご (foreign language), 外国人 がいこくじん (foreigner), 外国旅行 (foreign travel).

## How the kanji combine

[外](../kanji/外.md) (outside) plus [国](../kanji/国.md) (country): "outside country", i.e. a country other than one's own. Both on'yomi: ガイ + コク. The pair is the opposite of [国内](国内.md) (within the country).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 海外 かいがい (overseas), 外地 がいち (a place outside the homeland, dated), 異国 いこく (a foreign land, literary).
- Antonym: [国内](国内.md) こくない (domestic), 自国 じこく (one's own country).

## Distinctive meaning

外国 is the everyday neutral word. 海外 emphasizes "overseas" and is common in travel and business (海外旅行). 外人 がいじん is a shortened form for foreigner and can sound blunt; 外国人 is more neutral.
