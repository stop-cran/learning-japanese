# 攻める (せめる) - attack; take on; act boldly

## Meaning

攻める means attacking or taking the offensive, including in a game or sport.
It can also mean challenging or taking on something.
These uses are transitive: the target takes を.

- 後半は相手のゴールを積極的に攻めました。 (こうはんはあいてのゴールをせっきょくてきにせめました。) - In the second half, we attacked the opposing goal aggressively.
- 難しい課題を攻めるには、別の方法が必要です。 (むずかしいかだいをせめるには、べつのほうほうがひつようです。) - We need a different method to tackle the difficult task.

A colloquial intransitive use describes taking a bold or proactive approach.
攻めてるファッション, "bold, daring fashion," is a dictionary-attested example;
it does not describe clothes physically attacking someone.
The ichidan forms include 攻めます, 攻めて, and 攻めない.

## How the kanji combine

攻 is read せ here, with める written as okurigana.
The ichidan verb stem is せめ, not just せ.
Conjugation changes the final る, not the whole written める.
There is no second kanji to read as a compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[攻撃](攻撃.md) (こうげき) names an attack or offensive.
[積極的](積極的.md) (せっきょくてき) describes an active or proactive attitude.
[守る](守る.md) (まもる), "defend," provides a contrast in a game.

## Distinctive meaning

[責める](責める.md), also せめる, is useful for blaming or reproaching someone.
Do not choose a spelling from the sound alone.
The public dictionary notes a shared origin, so the practical attack/blame comparison
is not a claim that the two words have no historical or contextual overlap.
Attacking also does not guarantee winning.
