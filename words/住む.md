# 住む (すむ) — live in a place

## Meaning

住む means to reside or have one's home somewhere. Mark the place with に.
住んでいます (すんでいます) normally describes where someone currently lives.

- 東京に住んでいます。 (とうきょうにすんでいます。) — I live in Tokyo.
- 来年はこの町に住みたいです。 (らいねんはこのまちにすみたいです。) — I want to live in this town next year.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native verb, not a compound. [住](../kanji/住.md) supplies す and the residence meaning; む is okurigana.
It is a godan verb: 住みます (すみます), 住んで (すんで).

## Synonyms and antonyms

暮らす (くらす) focuses on leading one's life or getting by, not just one's address.
泊まる (とまる) is staying at a place such as a hotel, rather than making it one's home.
There is no single direct antonym of residing somewhere.

## Distinctive meaning

English "live" also means "be alive," but 住む specifies residence.
東京に住んでいます answers "Where is your home?" rather than "Are you alive?"
