# 優秀 (ゆうしゅう) - excellent; outstanding

## Meaning

優秀 is a な-adjective for excellence or outstanding ability, performance, or quality. Use **優秀な + noun** and **優秀です** as a predicate. It can evaluate people, results, or products.

- 彼女は優秀な研究者です。 (かのじょはゆうしゅうなけんきゅうしゃです。) - She is an outstanding researcher.
- このクラスには優秀な学生が多いです。 (このクラスにはゆうしゅうながくせいがおおいです。) - There are many excellent students in this class.

**優秀な成績** (ゆうしゅうなせいせき) describes excellent results or grades. The past predicate is 優秀だった; the negative is 優秀ではない.

## How the kanji combine

[優](../kanji/優.md) contributes excellence or superiority, and 秀 contributes outstanding quality. The components reinforce the positive evaluation. Their on readings are ユウ + シュウ; both contain a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[優れる](優れる.md) (すぐれる) is the related verb "excel; be superior." [上手](上手.md) (じょうず) emphasizes being skillful at an activity. [立派](立派.md) (りっぱ) can praise something as fine or admirable more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

優秀 does not simply mean kind, attractive, or morally good. Calling someone an 優秀な研究者 evaluates their ability or work as a researcher. The evaluation also need not mean that they are uniquely best; several members of a group can all be 優秀.
