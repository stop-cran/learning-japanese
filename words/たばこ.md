# たばこ (たばこ) — tobacco or cigarettes

## Meaning

たばこ means tobacco or a cigarette; context can also make it refer to tobacco products more generally. The noun itself does not mark singular or plural.

ここでたばこを吸わないでください。（ここでたばこをすわないでください。） — Please do not smoke here.

## How the kanji combine

たばこ is a loanword from Portuguese *tabaco*. It is also written タバコ or 煙草. The kanji spelling represents the word as a whole, not ordinary separate readings that add up to たばこ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

喫煙（きつえん） means smoking; 禁煙（きんえん） means no smoking or giving up smoking. These name an activity or restriction, rather than the tobacco itself.

## Distinctive meaning

The ordinary expression for smoking is たばこを吸う（たばこをすう）, literally using the verb "draw in; inhale." A 禁煙 sign describes a smoking restriction, not simply a place where tobacco is unavailable.
