# 澄む (すむ) - become clear; resonate clearly

## Meaning

澄む describes water, air, or a sky becoming clear or unclouded.
It also describes a clear, resonant voice or sound and an unclouded,
serene state of mind.

- 川の水が澄んでいます。 (かわのみずがすんでいます。) - The river water is clear.
- 彼女は澄んだ声で歌います。 (かのじょはすんだこえでうたいます。) - She sings in a clear voice.

This is an intransitive godan verb: 澄みます (すみます), 澄んで (すんで).
澄んでいる can describe an existing clear state, not only an ongoing change.
The dictionary additionally records a pronunciation use concerning an
unvoiced sound, rather than merely easily understood speech.

## How the kanji combine

澄 carries the clarity meaning; the native reading is すむ.
The same dictionary family also records 清む (すむ), using
[清](../kanji/清.md). Recognize that spelling without treating it as a
different conjugation or confusing it with 清い (きよい).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[透き通る](透き通る.md) overlaps for transparent water and clear sound.
[清い](清い.md) can concern purity; visible clarity does not by itself
prove that water is safe to drink. [穏やか](穏やか.md) overlaps in calmness,
but does not automatically describe transparent water.

## Distinctive meaning

[晴れる](晴れる.md) concerns clearing weather or becoming sunny.
A 澄んだ空 (すんだそら), clear sky, emphasizes clarity; do not use 澄む
as an unrestricted replacement for every improvement in the weather.
Nor does a clear voice necessarily mean the same thing as a
[はっきり](はっきり.md) understood explanation.
[住む](住む.md) and [済む](済む.md) are different verbs with the same reading.
