# いきなり - suddenly; without warning

## Meaning

いきなり means abruptly, all of a sudden or without warning. It modifies an action
directly, without adding に. Before a noun, use の, as in いきなりの質問
(いきなりのしつもん), "an unexpected question."

- いきなり大きな音がしました。 (いきなりおおきなおとがしました。) - Suddenly there was a loud noise.
- 彼はいきなり本題に入りました。 (かれはいきなりほんだいにはいりました。) - He went straight into the main topic without any preamble.

## How the kanji combine

The dictionary marks this expression as usually written in kana. 行き成り is
a rare spelling; the ordinary learning form here remains いきなり.
The presence of [行](../kanji/行.md) in that spelling does not make the examples
literal statements about going somewhere.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[突然](突然.md) (とつぜん) and [急に](急に.md) (きゅうに) overlap in suddenness.
[徐々に](徐々に.md) (じょじょに), "gradually," supplies a contrast in some situations.
The contrast depends on whether onset, change or preparation is at issue.

## Distinctive meaning

The focus can be the lack of warning or a preparatory step, not simply speed.
Going straight into the main topic can be いきなり even if the speaker proceeds slowly.
The rare reading ゆきなり in the dictionary is not a reason to change the usual
reading いきなり in these examples.
