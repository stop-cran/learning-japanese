# 土 (つち) - earth; soil

## Meaning

土 is earth or soil, including the material in which plants grow or the dirt
that sticks to shoes. It is a noun.

- 植木鉢に土を入れました。 (うえきばちにつちをいれました。) - I put soil in the flowerpot.
- 靴に土がついています。 (くつにつちがついています。) - There is dirt on my shoes.

In the first example, 土を marks the material being put somewhere and 植木鉢に
marks the destination. In the second, 土が names what is attached, while 靴に
identifies where it is attached.

湿った土 (しめったつち) is damp soil; 土を掘る (つちをほる) means digging
in the earth.

## How the kanji combine

[土](../kanji/土.md) supplies the entire native word つち. In compounds it also
has on readings, as in 土地 (とち). A character's different readings are not
interchangeable ways to pronounce the standalone soil noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[土地](土地.md) (とち) is land as a plot, terrain, or locality. Buying 土地
usually concerns land, whereas buying 土 for a flowerpot concerns the material.
地面 (じめん) is the ground surface. These meanings can overlap in a scene,
but their focus differs.

## Distinctive meaning

English "earth" can name soil or the planet. 土 is a useful word for the material,
not the ordinary name of planet Earth, 地球 (ちきゅう).
Similarly, the fact that soil becomes dirt on a shoe does not make 土 an
inherently negative word: the same material can be useful in a garden.
