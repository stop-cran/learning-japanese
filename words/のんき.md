# のんき (のんき) - carefree; easygoing

## Meaning

のんき describes an easygoing, carefree, or optimistic attitude. Depending on the situation, the same lack of worry can be welcome or can imply carelessness about something that deserves attention.

- 兄はのんきな性格です。 (あにはのんきなせいかくです。) - My older brother has an easygoing personality.
- 締め切りは明日なのに、のんきに構えています。 (しめきりはあしたなのに、のんきにかまえています。) - Despite the deadline being tomorrow, they are taking things very casually.

Use **のんきな + noun**, **のんきに + predicate**, and **のんきだ／です**. The word is usually written in kana. **呑気** and **暢気** are recognized spellings, also read のんき.

## How the kanji combine

The dictionary treats 呑気 and 暢気 as ateji spellings. Do not explain the word by combining modern "swallow" and "[気](../kanji/気.md), spirit." Digital Daijisen traces のん to a historical Chinese-derived reading of 暖; the present word is best learned as a whole, not as a freely predictable modern reading of either written variant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[のんびり](のんびり.md) overlaps in relaxed, unhurried behavior. のんき particularly evaluates the lack of concern or worry; the context determines whether that is reassuring or irresponsible. [心配](心配.md) (しんぱい), "worry," provides a useful contrast, not an automatic replacement.

## Distinctive meaning

のんき does not guarantee good judgment or a favorable outcome. Do not translate it as "reckless" in every sentence, or assume that an easygoing person never takes care over anything. The second example criticizes a relaxed attitude in that situation, not the person's physical speed.
