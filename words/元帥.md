# 元帥 (げんすい) — marshal; fleet admiral

## Meaning

元帥 is a very senior military title or rank. The English equivalent depends on the service and historical setting; a naval context may call for “fleet admiral” instead of “field marshal.”

- 展示室には、陸軍元帥の肖像画がある。 (てんじしつには、りくぐんげんすいのしょうぞうががある。) — There is a portrait of an army field marshal in the gallery.
- 資料には、この人物の肩書が海軍元帥と記されている。 (しりょうには、このじんぶつのかたがきがかいぐんげんすいとしるされている。) — The document gives this person’s title as fleet admiral.

## How the kanji combine

[元](../kanji/元.md) is げん and [帥](../kanji/帥.md) is すい. Here 帥 points to command; do not replace it with the similar-looking 師.

## Synonyms and antonyms

総帥 (そうすい) names the head of an organization or a commander in chief; it does not by itself specify the rank 元帥. [大尉](大尉.md) (たいい) names a different officer rank.

## Distinctive meaning

The examples show why the characters immediately before 元帥 matter. 陸軍 identifies the army and 海軍 the navy. Neither example sets a universal equivalence between national rank systems.
