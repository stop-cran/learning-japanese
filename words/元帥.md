# 元帥 (げんすい) — marshal; fleet admiral

## Meaning

元帥 is a very senior military title or rank. The English equivalent depends on the service and historical setting; a naval context may call for “fleet admiral” instead of “field marshal.”

- 展示室には、元帥の肖像画がある。 (てんじしつには、げんすいのしょうぞうががある。) — There is a portrait of a marshal in the gallery.
- この伝記は、彼が元帥になるまでの歩みを描いている。 (このでんきは、かれがげんすいになるまでのあゆみをえがいている。) — This biography follows his career up to the time he became a marshal.

## How the kanji combine

[元](../kanji/元.md) is げん and [帥](../kanji/帥.md) is すい. Here 帥 points to command; learn 元帥, not the irregular spelling 元師 recorded by JMdict.

## Synonyms and antonyms

総帥 (そうすい) names the head of an organization or a commander in chief; it does not by itself specify the rank 元帥. [大尉](大尉.md) (たいい) names a different officer rank.

## Distinctive meaning

The same Japanese word can require different English titles. If a document identifies the person as a naval officer, check the navy and period before translating 元帥; the word alone does not specify the service.
