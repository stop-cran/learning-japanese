# 伝染 (でんせん) - infection; contagion

## Meaning

伝染 is infection or contagion, with attention to transmission from one to another.
伝染する is intransitive: 病気が伝染する (びょうきがでんせんする), "a disease spreads."
に can identify whom it spreads to.

- その病気は人から人へ伝染します。 (そのびょうきはひとからひとへでんせんします。) - That disease spreads from person to person.
- あくびが伝染しました。 (あくびがでんせんしました。) - The yawn was contagious.

The second example is figurative: a yawn prompting another yawn is not a medical infection.

## How the kanji combine

[伝](../kanji/伝.md) contributes transmission; 染 contributes an association with becoming affected or permeated.
Together they help recall something spreading to another.
The on readings are デン + セン, not the native readings used in verbs for dyeing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[移る](移る.md) (うつる) also has a disease-transmission use, besides moving to another place.
感染 (かんせん) means infection; it can focus on becoming infected, whereas
伝染 makes the transmission aspect prominent. This is a useful focus difference, not a claim that their uses never overlap.
[流行](流行.md) (りゅうこう) concerns prevalence or a vogue rather than necessarily identifying transmission.

## Distinctive meaning

伝染 does not mean that every disease is contagious or specify its route of transmission.
The figurative use also does not justify replacing every occurrence of English "spread" with 伝染する.
Learn what spreads and to whom, rather than treating the verb as transitive "infect someone."
