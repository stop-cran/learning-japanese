# 木綿 (もめん) - cotton material

## Meaning

木綿 is cotton as a material, especially cotton fabric.
Use の to describe something made of it.

- 夏は木綿のシャツをよく着ます。 (なつはもめんのシャツをよくきます。) - I often wear cotton shirts in summer.

The material phrase 木綿の modifies シャツ; it is not a na-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[木](../kanji/木.md) has a tree or woody-plant association, and 綿 concerns
cotton or wadding. Learn the established reading もめん as a whole:
do not guess きめん from the standalone reading of 木.
The word names cotton, not a fabric made from timber.

## Synonyms and antonyms

コットン is the loanword used for the same material, and 綿 (めん) is common
in material descriptions and labels. 絹 (きぬ), "silk," is a contrasting
fiber, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary's reading きわた has an additional tree-name sense that must
not be transferred to もめん. The reading taught here is for cotton material.
木綿豆腐 (もめんどうふ) is a tofu variety name, not tofu containing cotton fiber.
