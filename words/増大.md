# 増大 (ぞうだい) - enlargement; increase

## Meaning

増大 is an increase in amount, size, or degree.
増大する can describe something becoming greater,
and also an action making an amount greater.
Use Xが増大する for X increasing.

- 仕事の負担が増大しています。 (しごとのふたんがぞうだいしています。) - The workload is increasing.
- この変更は費用を増大させます。 (このへんこうはひようをぞうだいさせます。) - This change will increase the costs.

The second sentence uses the causative 増大させる:
the change causes the costs to grow.
Direct Xを増大する is also recorded, as in increasing a budget;
the causative is not the only possible way to express an increase caused.

## How the kanji combine

[増](../kanji/増.md) contributes increasing, and
[大](../kanji/大.md) becoming large or great.
Their related ideas reinforce growth in scale or amount.
The on readings are ゾウ + ダイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[増加](増加.md) (ぞうか) concerns an increase in number or amount.
[拡大](拡大.md) (かくだい) can concern size, extent, or magnification.
[増える](増える.md) (ふえる) is an everyday intransitive increase verb.
[減る](減る.md) (へる), decrease, contrasts in quantity.

## Distinctive meaning

The thing increasing need not be beneficial: costs, burdens, and dissatisfaction
can grow. Conversely, 増大 does not itself specify a rapid rate or an exact
percentage. [増減](増減.md) (ぞうげん) allows both upward and downward
changes; 増大 focuses on becoming greater.
