# 響き (ひびき) - resonance; sound; impression

## Meaning

響き is an echo or reverberation, and also the distinctive sound of
something. It can describe the quality or impression of a voice,
phrase, or other sound rather than simply its volume.

- このホールは音の響きがいいです。 (このホールはおとのひびきがいいです。) - Sound resonates well in this hall.
- その言葉には温かい響きがあります。 (そのことばにはあたたかいひびきがあります。) - Those words have a warm ring to them.

The second example concerns how the words come across, not a measured
temperature. The dictionary also includes the feeling inspired by
something heard or read.

## How the kanji combine

響 contributes sounding or resonating. The noun ひびき is related to
[響く](響く.md) (ひびく), resound; き completes its written form.
響 alone is also recorded for the noun with irregular okurigana usage.
That recognition does not make every bare 響 readable as ひびき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[音](音.md) (おと) is the broader sound word; [声](声.md) (こえ)
identifies a voice. [物音](物音.md) (ものおと) reports a sound from
something happening. [印象](印象.md) (いんしょう) and
[感じ](感じ.md) (かんじ) overlap in impression or feel, without
requiring a sound.

## Distinctive meaning

響き can be pleasant or unpleasant; it does not inherently mean
[騒音](騒音.md) (そうおん), disturbing noise, or
[雑音](雑音.md) (ざつおん), unwanted noise or interference.
声の響き (こえのひびき) draws attention to how a voice sounds,
not necessarily what its speaker believes.
Keep ひびき separate from the different word どよめき, even where
a dictionary page also lists that reading.
