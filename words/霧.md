# 霧 (きり) - fog; mist

## Meaning

霧 is a noun meaning "fog" or "mist." Useful combinations are
霧が出る, "fog forms," 霧が濃い, "the fog is thick," and
霧が晴れる, "the fog clears."

- 今朝は霧が濃くて、山が見えません。 (けさはきりがこくて、やまがみえません。) - The fog is thick this morning, so I cannot see the mountains.
- 昼ごろには霧が晴れました。 (ひるごろにはきりがはれました。) - The fog cleared around noon.

霧で can mark the cause of poor visibility. The noun itself does
not mean that rain is falling; fog and rain are different weather conditions.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 霧 represents the whole native word きり.
The visible rain element helps associate it with atmospheric moisture.
There is no pair of written words whose meanings must be added together.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[雲](雲.md) (くも) means "cloud." [曇り](曇り.md) (くもり)
describes cloudy weather, not specifically fog near the ground.
靄 (もや), "haze; mist," overlaps in everyday description; technical
weather terminology can draw finer distinctions.

## Distinctive meaning

For practical use, pair the noun with the change or condition you mean.
霧が出た says fog appeared, whereas 霧が晴れた says it cleared.
The latter is a contrast in the fog's state, not a separate antonym
for the noun 霧.
