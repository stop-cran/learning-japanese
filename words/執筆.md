# 執筆 (しっぴつ) - writing; authoring

## Meaning

執筆 is writing or authoring a text, such as an article, essay, or book. It is a noun and a transitive する verb: **記事を執筆する** (きじをしっぴつする), "write an article."

- 旅行についての記事を執筆しています。
  (りょこうについてのきじをしっぴつしています。) - I am writing an article about travel.
- その本の執筆には二年かかりました。
  (そのほんのしっぴつにはにねんかかりました。) - Writing that book took two years.

執筆中 (しっぴつちゅう) means "in the process of writing." The noun can identify the activity without asserting that the text is finished or published.

## How the kanji combine

執 contributes taking hold of something, and [筆](../kanji/筆.md) a writing brush. "Taking up the brush" is a useful meaning aid for the writing activity, not a requirement to use a brush.

The on readings シツ + ヒツ combine as **しっぴつ**: note the small っ and the change from ヒ to ピ. ピツ is the compound realization here, not an additional independent reading to substitute everywhere.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[書く](書く.md) (かく) is the broader everyday verb "write." It covers writing a name or a shopping list as well as composing a book. 執筆 foregrounds producing a text and has a more formal or professional feel; it does not require a paid professional writer.

[著す](著す.md) (あらわす) overlaps in authoring a work. [作文](作文.md) (さくぶん) concerns composition or essay writing and can also name the composition itself.

## Distinctive meaning

The literal brush association does not limit 執筆 to handwriting: someone can 執筆する on a computer. Nor does 執筆 itself mean printing, publishing, or simply copying an existing page. Keep the authoring activity separate from the later production and distribution of a book.
