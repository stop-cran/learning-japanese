# 先生 (せんせい) — teacher, master

## Meaning

先生 is the word for a teacher, and an honorific for doctors, lawyers, politicians and other masters: 日本語の先生 (a Japanese teacher), 田中先生 (Teacher/Professor Tanaka), 先生に聞く (ask the teacher).
It is used as a title after a name and also for direct address: 先生、質問があります (Teacher, I have a question).
For a doctor, 先生 is a respectful title or form of address, not a general name for the profession.

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md) (ahead, earlier) plus [生](../kanji/生.md) (born, life): "one born earlier", that is, a person who came before you and has more experience. The relation is modifier plus head. Both parts use on'yomi: セン + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 教師 きょうし (teacher as an occupation), 教員 きょういん (teacher; member of the teaching staff), 師匠 ししょう (master of an art).
- Antonym: 生徒 せいと／学生 がくせい (student).

## Distinctive meaning

先生 is a respectful term for addressing or referring to others; people normally do not call themselves 先生 (a teacher introduces themself as 教師 or by name and job).
教師 is a neutral job description and is not used as a title.
