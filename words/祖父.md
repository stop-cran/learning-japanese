# 祖父 (そふ) — grandfather

## Meaning

祖父 names a grandfather: the father of one's mother or father.
It identifies a family relationship, rather than just an elderly man.
Use a noun plus の to identify whose grandfather is meant, as in 私の祖父 (わたしのそふ), "my grandfather."

- 祖父は毎朝散歩します。 (そふはまいあささんぽします。) — My grandfather takes a walk every morning.
- これは祖父の写真です。 (これはそふのしゃしんです。) — This is a photograph of my grandfather.

In a sentence about one's own family, the owner can be understood without repeating 私の.

## How the kanji combine

[祖](../kanji/祖.md) points back a generation, and [父](../kanji/父.md) identifies the male parent.
Together they name a grandfather. Both readings are on readings: ソ + フ.
The whole word is そふ, not a combination of そ and the standalone word ちち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

おじいさん is another word for grandfather. Its kanji spelling is お祖父さん, but JMdict marks it as usually written in kana.
Learn おじいさん as a whole word rather than trying to pronounce that spelling おそふさん.

祖母 (そぼ), "grandmother," is the corresponding family term, not an antonym.
先祖 (せんぞ), "ancestor," is broader: it does not identify specifically a grandfather.

## Distinctive meaning

祖父 tells you the relationship but not which side of the family.
母の父 (ははのちち), "my mother's father," and 父の父 (ちちのちち), "my father's father," make that distinction explicit.
An unrelated older man is not automatically someone's 祖父 just because of his age.
