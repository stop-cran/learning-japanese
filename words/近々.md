# 近々 (ちかぢか) - in the near future; close by

## Meaning

近々 commonly points to the near future: "soon; before long."
It modifies a predicate directly.

- 近々、引っ越す予定です。
  (ちかぢか、ひっこすよていです。) - I plan to move in the near future.
- 近々お会いしたいです。
  (ちかぢかおあいしたいです。) - I would like to meet you soon.

It also has a spatial use, particularly with と:
近々と寄って見る (ちかぢかとよってみる), "move close and look."
This describes proximity rather than a future appointment.

## How the kanji combine

[近](../kanji/近.md) contributes nearness. 々 repeats the preceding
character; it is not a separate kanji meaning "again."
The native repeated form is ちか + ぢか, with voicing in the second half.
Keep ぢ in the standard spelling, not じ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もうすぐ](もうすぐ.md), [やがて](やがて.md), and
[そのうち](そのうち.md) overlap in future timing, without promising
the same fixed interval.
[近く](近く.md) (ちかく) also has spatial and temporal uses.
[遠く](遠く.md) (とおく), "far away," contrasts in distance.

## Distinctive meaning

きんきん is another dictionary reading for the near-future sense.
The spatial sense in the pinned entry is restricted to **ちかぢか**;
do not transfer it automatically to きんきん.
近近 is also recorded. The search-only form 近ぢか is not a parallel
recommended practice spelling.
