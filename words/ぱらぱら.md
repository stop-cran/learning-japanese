# ぱらぱら — scattered pattering; loose separate grains

## Meaning

Light, scattered falling or sprinkling; also quickly flipping pages, sparse distribution, or crumbly, separate food particles.

- 雨がぱらぱらと降り始めた。 — A light patter of rain began to fall.
- 待っている間、雑誌（ざっし）をぱらぱらとめくった。 — While waiting, I flipped through a magazine.
- ご飯がぱらぱらになるまで炒めた（いためた）。 — I stir-fried the rice until the grains separated.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぽつぽつ](ぽつぽつ.md) emphasizes individual separated drops or occurrences. [しとしと](しとしと.md) often suggests sustained gentle rain. [べちゃべちゃ](べちゃべちゃ.md) contrasts with loose rice grains by describing a soggy result.

## Distinctive meaning

ぱらぱらと降る, ぱらぱらめくる, and ぱらぱらのチャーハン are useful. Loose grains are not necessarily unpleasantly dry like [ぱさぱさ](ぱさぱさ.md). 人がぱらぱらいる means people are sparsely scattered; the word does not always refer to an audible patter or things falling.
