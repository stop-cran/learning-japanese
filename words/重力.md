# 重力 (じゅうりょく) - gravity; gravitation

## Meaning

重力 is gravity or gravitation, a physics noun. It is not limited to Earth: other astronomical bodies also have gravity.

- 理科の授業で重力について学びました。
  (りかのじゅぎょうでじゅうりょくについてまなびました。) - I learned about gravity in science class.
- 月にも重力があります。
  (つきにもじゅうりょくがあります。) - The Moon has gravity too.

重力について means "about gravity." The にも in the second example adds the Moon to the bodies having gravity.

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) contributes weight or heaviness, and [力](../kanji/力.md) force. The compound names the physical force associated with weight. The readings are ジュウ + リョク; keep りょく distinct from the りょう in [重量](重量.md) (じゅうりょう), "weight."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[引力](引力.md) (いんりょく) includes gravitational attraction but also attractive forces more broadly. Not every 引力 is gravity; its article also records figurative appeal.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 重力 as the ordinary adjective "heavy": that is 重い (おもい). Gravity, an object's weight, and its mass are related concepts, not interchangeable names for the same quantity. This card introduces the word, not a full treatment of physical measurement.
