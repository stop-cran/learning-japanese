# 抽象 (ちゅうしょう) - abstraction

## Meaning

抽象 is abstraction: mentally selecting a property or aspect from
particular things and considering it apart from their other details.
It is a noun and a transitive する-verb.

- この授業では抽象と具体の違いを学びます。 (このじゅぎょうではちゅうしょうとぐたいのちがいをまなびます。) - In this class, we study the difference between abstraction and concreteness.
- 複数の事例から共通の特徴を抽象しました。 (ふくすうのじれいからきょうつうのとくちょうをちゅうしょうしました。) - We abstracted common features from several cases.

In the second sentence, から marks the cases from which the features
are selected, and を marks what is abstracted.

## How the kanji combine

抽 contributes drawing or pulling out, and [象](../kanji/象.md)
contributes a form or aspect here. As a meaning aid, the combination
suggests drawing out an aspect for consideration. It is not about
a literal elephant or a claim about historical character formation.
The on readings are チュウ + ショウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[具体](具体.md) (ぐたい), concreteness or embodiment, provides the
dictionary contrast. 抽象的 (ちゅうしょうてき) is a related
な-adjective: 抽象的な説明 (ちゅうしょうてきなせつめい),
"an abstract explanation," and 抽象的に考える
(ちゅうしょうてきにかんがえる), "think abstractly."

## Distinctive meaning

Do not turn the English adjective "abstract" into unrestricted 抽象な.
Learn the derived adjective with 的な or 的に.
Abstraction is also not automatically vagueness or poor explanation.
An English "abstract" meaning a summary of a paper is a different
sense of the English word, not the definition taught here.
