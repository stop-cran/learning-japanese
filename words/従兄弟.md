# 従兄弟 (いとこ) - male cousin

## Meaning

従兄弟 is a noun for a male cousin: a son of one's aunt or uncle. The
reading taught here is いとこ. The kana word いとこ is useful when the
written distinction between male and female cousins is unnecessary.

- 従兄弟と旅行に行きました。 (いとことりょこうにいきました。) - I went on a trip with my male cousin.
- 彼は母の姉の息子で、私の従兄弟です。 (かれはははのあねのむすこで、わたしのいとこです。) - He is my mother's older sister's son, so he is my cousin.

Identify the person before の, as in 私の従兄弟, "my cousin." The relationship is not
the same as being a brother, even if the cousins grew up together.

## How the kanji combine

従 has meanings including "follow"; [兄](../kanji/兄.md) and
[弟](../kanji/弟.md) mean older and younger brother. Here the whole
spelling names a cousin relationship. The reading いとこ is a whole-word
reading, not three separate on readings added together.

## Synonyms and antonyms

従姉妹 (いとこ) is a corresponding spelling for a female cousin.
[兄弟](兄弟.md) (きょうだい) means siblings, and [親戚](親戚.md)
(しんせき) covers relatives more broadly. Family relationships have contrasts,
not useful lexical opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Read いとこ as the whole word; do not assign each syllable to an individual kanji.
The dictionary marks this entry as usually written in kana; recognizing
従兄弟 remains useful when a text specifies the male relationship.
