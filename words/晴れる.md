# 晴れる (はれる) — clear up; be sunny

## Meaning

晴れる describes the sky clearing or sunny weather. It is intransitive: the sky or weather clears,
rather than someone "clearing" an object with を.
晴れています (はれています) describes the resulting sunny or clear condition.

- 空が晴れました。 (そらがはれました。) — The sky cleared.
- 午後は晴れるでしょう。 (ごごははれるでしょう。) — It will probably be sunny in the afternoon.

## How the kanji combine

[晴](../kanji/晴.md) carries the clear-weather meaning; れる completes the native verb.
It is an ichidan verb, meaning its endings attach after dropping the final る:
晴れます (はれます), "is sunny; clears up," and 晴れない (はれない), "does not clear up."

## Synonyms and antonyms

晴れ (はれ), "fine weather," is the related noun: 今日は晴れです (きょうははれです),
"It is sunny today." 晴天 (せいてん) also names fair weather.
曇る (くもる), "become cloudy," gives a useful weather contrast.
The nouns and verbs describe related conditions but do not attach to the rest of a sentence in the same way.

## Distinctive meaning

晴れる can report a change, as in 空が晴れました, or predict sunny weather without telling us
whether rain came first. It is not restricted to "the rain has just stopped."
This article concentrates on weather rather than treating every figurative use of 晴れる as identical.
