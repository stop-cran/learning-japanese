# シャッター (シャッター) - camera or security shutter

## Meaning

シャッター means a shutter. Two useful senses are a camera shutter, which controls exposure, and a shutter that closes a shopfront, door, or window.

- カメラのシャッターを切りました。
  (カメラのシャッターをきりました。) - I released the camera shutter.
- 店のシャッターを下ろしました。
  (みせのシャッターをおろしました。) - I lowered the shop's shutter.

シャッターを切る (シャッターをきる) belongs to the camera expression; it does not mean physically cutting the mechanism. 下ろす (おろす), "lower," suits a downward-closing shutter.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[雨戸](雨戸.md) (あまど) is a storm shutter used to protect an opening from weather. It overlaps in the building-shutter setting, but does not name a camera's exposure mechanism.

[戸](戸.md) (と) and [ドア](ドア.md) concern doors more broadly. The words are not interchangeable for every door mechanism. There is no single direct antonym covering both shutter senses.

## Distinctive meaning

For buildings, シャッター often refers to a rolling or similar closing barrier; context identifies what it protects. It is not a blanket name for every curtain or decorative blind.

Keep both the small ッ and long ー: **シャッター**. The noun alone does not tell you whether someone is taking a photograph or closing premises.
