# 終了 (しゅうりょう) - ending; termination

## Meaning

終了 is the end or termination of an activity, event, or process. 終了する has both intransitive and transitive uses: **活動が終了する**, "an activity ends," and **活動を終了する**, "bring an activity to an end."

- 試合は五時に終了しました。
  (しあいはごじにしゅうりょうしました。) - The match ended at five.
- これで説明を終了します。
  (これでせつめいをしゅうりょうします。) - This concludes the explanation.

終了しました can announce that an event or operation has ended. It does not by itself certify that the intended result was successful.

## How the kanji combine

[終](../kanji/終.md) contributes ending, and [了](../kanji/了.md) finishing. Their related meanings reinforce bringing something to its end. The on readings are シュウ + リョウ, with two long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[終わる](終わる.md) (おわる) and [終える](終える.md) (おえる) overlap with ending and finishing. [完了](完了.md) (かんりょう) presents a task or process as complete; [完成](完成.md) (かんせい) emphasizes a finished work or whole. An activity ending is not sufficient evidence that a product is complete.

開始 (かいし), beginning or commencement, is the event-stage contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

終了 is not the physical action of shutting every door or book. [閉じる](閉じる.md) (とじる) can nevertheless overlap when bringing a meeting to a close. Also distinguish [結論](結論.md) (けつろん), a conclusion reached by reasoning, from merely ending a discussion.
