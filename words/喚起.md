# 喚起 (かんき) — arousal; awakening; stimulation

## Meaning

喚起 means awakening or arousing a feeling, awareness, or response. It is often used in the expression 注意を喚起する, “to call attention to something.”

- 警察は住民に注意を喚起しました。 (けいさつはじゅうみんにちゅういをかんきしました。) — The police alerted residents.
- その広告は環境問題への関心を喚起します。 (そのこうこくはかんきょうもんだいへのかんしんをかんきします。) — The advertisement raises interest in environmental issues.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[喚](../kanji/喚.md) is read カン and [起](../kanji/起.md) is read キ. Together they form 喚起, read かんき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

呼び起こす (よびおこす) can mean to call forth or evoke; 喚起 is a formal noun used for arousing attention, awareness, or a response.

## Distinctive meaning

喚起 is used for bringing attention, awareness, or a reaction to the fore; it does not mean physically waking someone from sleep.
