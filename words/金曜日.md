# 金曜日 (きんようび) — Friday

## Meaning

金曜日 is Friday: 金曜日に会いましょう (let's meet on Friday), 今週の金曜日 (this Friday). Abbreviated 金曜 きんよう, written 金 in calendars.

## How the kanji combine

[金](../kanji/金.md) (metal, from 金星 きんせい, Venus) plus [曜](../kanji/曜.md) (day of the week) plus [日](../kanji/日.md) (day): "Venus-day". The reading is きん + よう + び (日 is び, the voiced form of ひ). 金 is read きん, not かね, here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Neighbours: 木曜日 もくようび (Thursday) and [土曜日](土曜日.md) どようび (Saturday).
- Related: 花金 はなきん (colloquial, Friday night before the weekend), 週末 しゅうまつ (weekend).

## Distinctive meaning

The weekday names follow the sun, moon and five elements: 日月火水木金土. 金曜日 pairs the metal element 金 with Venus.
