# 夜明け (よあけ) - dawn; daybreak

## Meaning

夜明け is a noun for dawn or daybreak, when the night gives way to daylight. Use 夜明け前 (よあけまえ), "before dawn," and 夜明けに (よあけに), "at dawn."

- 夜明け前に家を出ました。 (よあけまえにいえをでました。) - I left home before dawn.
- 夜明けとともに鳥が鳴き始めました。 (よあけとともにとりがなきはじめました。) - The birds began to sing as day broke.

夜明けを待つ (よあけをまつ) means "wait for daybreak." The noun identifies a transition in the day, not a fixed clock time.

## How the kanji combine

[夜](../kanji/夜.md) means night and is read よ here. [明](../kanji/明.md) appears in 明け, related to 明ける (あける), the ending of night and arrival of daylight. The compound presents the night giving way to day.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[朝](朝.md) (あさ), "morning," covers a broader part of the day.

日の出 (ひので), "sunrise," concerns the sun appearing above the horizon. It is closely related to dawn but not identical to the broader brightening of the sky.

## Distinctive meaning

夕暮れ (ゆうぐれ), "dusk," provides the opposite daily transition toward darkness. 夜明け can also supply an image of a new beginning, but this card's examples describe literal daybreak. Do not infer a universal hour: the timing changes with season and location.
