# 務め (つとめ) - duty; responsibility

## Meaning

務め is a duty, task, or responsibility that someone is expected to fulfill. It is a noun: Nの務め means "N's duty," and 務めを果たす (つとめをはたす) means "fulfill one's duty."

- 子どもを守るのは大人の務めです。 (こどもをまもるのはおとなのつとめです。) - Protecting children is adults' responsibility.
- 自分の務めを果たしました。 (じぶんのつとめをはたしました。) - I fulfilled my duty.

The same dictionary entry also includes Buddhist religious services. Recognize this use in 朝のお勤め (あさのおつとめ), "morning Buddhist devotions," with the spelling 勤め.

## How the kanji combine

[務](../kanji/務.md) contributes duty or performing a role; め completes the native noun つとめ. The dictionary lists both 務め and [勤め](勤め.md), without a hard spelling restriction separating its two senses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[義務](義務.md) (ぎむ) emphasizes an obligation, while [責任](責任.md) (せきにん) can emphasize responsibility or accountability. 務め directs attention to the duty or role to be carried out.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that every つとめ is paid employment. Looking after someone can be described as a duty, and the Buddhist use concerns worship rather than an office job. The surrounding words, especially お勤め in a religious setting, help identify which meaning is intended.
