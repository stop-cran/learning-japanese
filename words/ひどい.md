# ひどい (ひどい) - awful; severe

## Meaning

ひどい describes something very bad, severe, harsh, or cruel. It can evaluate a situation or emphasize the unpleasant degree of an illness or weather condition.

- 今日はひどい雨です。 (きょうはひどいあめです。) - The rain is terrible today.
- 友達にそんなことを言うなんて、ひどいです。 (ともだちにそんなことをいうなんて、ひどいです。) - It is cruel to say something like that to a friend.

It is an i-adjective: ひどい風邪 (ひどいかぜ), "a bad cold"; past ひどかった; adverbial ひどく.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual; 酷い is another spelling. Historically the adjective derives from 非道 (ひどう), "inhumanity; wrongdoing." Its stem is Sino-Japanese in origin, but the modern word uses Japanese i-adjective inflection, not na-adjective grammar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

悪い (わるい) is the broader "bad." むごい emphasizes cruelty. 優しい (やさしい), "kind," contrasts with cruel behavior, but is not an opposite for every weather or illness use.

## Distinctive meaning

ひどい is a strong negative evaluation. A ひどい人 is cruel or badly behaved, not merely someone whose appearance you dislike. With rain or a cold, it highlights severity rather than moral character.
