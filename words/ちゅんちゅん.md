# ちゅんちゅん — birds chirping

## Meaning

Repeated small bird chirps, commonly imagined as sparrows.

- 朝、すずめがちゅんちゅん鳴（な）いていた。 — Sparrows were chirping in the morning.
- 窓（まど）の外から、ちゅんちゅんという声が聞こえる。 — I can hear chirping outside the window.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぴよぴよ](ぴよぴよ.md) overlaps in little bird cheeps, often with a chick-like impression. [かあかあ](かあかあ.md) instead evokes a crow's caw.

## Distinctive meaning

ちゅんちゅん鳴く describes a sound, not the noun “sparrow.” A Japanese description may use it for a small bird without making a precise biological identification. English “chirp” and “cheep” do not divide the Japanese words perfectly.
