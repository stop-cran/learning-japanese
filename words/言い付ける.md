# 言い付ける (いいつける) - tell to do; tell on

## Meaning

言い付ける means telling or ordering someone to do something, or reporting
someone's misbehavior. It is also written 言いつける. It is a transitive ichidan
verb: 言い付けます, 言い付けない and 言い付けて.

- 母は子供に早く寝るように言い付けました。 (はははこどもにはやくねるようにいいつけました。) - The mother told the child to go to bed early.
- 友達のいたずらを先生に言い付けた。 (ともだちのいたずらをせんせいにいいつけた。) - I told the teacher about my friend's mischief.

For an instruction, person + に identifies who is told, and verb + ように states
what to do. For telling on someone, person + に identifies who receives the report,
and を can mark the misbehavior reported.

## How the kanji combine

The native compound joins 言い, the stem of [言う](言う.md), and
[付ける](付ける.md). [言](../kanji/言.md) contributes saying; [付](../kanji/付.md)
can serve as a learning association with directing words toward someone.
The established meanings must be learned as a whole, not as literal attachment.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[命令](命令.md) (めいれい) names a command; 命令する means ordering.
[報告](報告.md) (ほうこく) concerns reporting and need not involve telling on someone.
[伝える](伝える.md) (つたえる) more broadly conveys information.

## Distinctive meaning

The telling-on use can sound disapproving, as English "snitch" can.
The instruction use is not necessarily an accusation. Do not infer which use is
intended from the recipient particle に alone; inspect the content and situation.
