# 瓦 (かわら) - tile; roof tile

## Meaning

瓦 is a tile, especially a roof tile. 屋根の瓦 (やねのかわら) identifies a tile on a roof.

- 台風で屋根の瓦が割れました。 (たいふうでやねのかわらがわれました。) - A roof tile broke in the typhoon.
- 古い瓦を新しいものに替えました。 (ふるいかわらをあたらしいものにかえました。) - We replaced the old tiles with new ones.

The noun does not by itself mark whether one tile or several are involved.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 瓦 represents the whole reading かわら. There is no compound to split, and no written kana ending to conjugate.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[屋根](屋根.md) (やね) is the roof as a whole, not an individual tile. [替える](替える.md) (かえる), "replace," describes the action in the second example; it is not part of the noun 瓦.

## Distinctive meaning

A roof may use tiles or another covering. Calling something a 屋根 does not establish that it has 瓦, and 瓦 is not a general word for every building material. Learn the familiar roof-tile use without turning it into a claim that every tile must have one particular material or shape.
