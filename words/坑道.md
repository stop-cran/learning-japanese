# 坑道 (こうどう) — mine passage; mining gallery

## Meaning

坑道 is an underground passage, especially one used in a mine.

- 坑道を歩く。 (こうどうをあるく。) — to walk through a mine passage

## How the kanji combine

[坑](../kanji/坑.md) identifies the pit or mine context, and [道](../kanji/道.md) means a way or passage. The whole word is more specific than an ordinary tunnel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[トンネル](トンネル.md) (tunnel) overlaps when describing an underground passage, but is the more general term. This article focuses on 坑道 as a mining passage; the two words are not interchangeable in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

This article focuses on the specialist mining-passage sense.
