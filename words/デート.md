# デート (デート) - date; romantic outing

## Meaning

デート is a date or social outing for two, especially a romantic one. デートする means to go on a date; use person + と for the person you go with.

- 土曜日に恋人とデートします。 (どようびにこいびととデートします。) - I am going on a date with my partner on Saturday.
- 彼女をデートに誘いました。 (かのじょをデートにさそいました。) - I asked her out on a date.

The second sentence states an invitation, not that she accepted it. デートの約束 (デートのやくそく) means an arrangement to go on a date.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana. The long vowel mark makes デー longer than デ. There are no kanji components to break apart.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[約束](約束.md) (やくそく) means a promise or arrangement and is not necessarily romantic. [日付](日付.md) (ひづけ) is the straightforward word for a calendar date. The dictionary also records a calendar-date sense of デート, so the distinction is a practical guide, not a claim that the loanword can never mean that.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling an outing a デート presents it as a particular kind of social encounter. It does not mean every business appointment or every meeting between two people. Learn the two particle patterns separately: Nとデートする, "date with N," and Nをデートに誘う, "invite N on a date."
