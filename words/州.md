# 州 (しゅう) - state; province; continent

## Meaning

州 is a noun and suffix for a state, province, or comparable administrative region. Its English translation depends on the country's system. It can also name a continent, as in ヨーロッパ州 (ヨーロッパしゅう), "Europe."

- 彼はアメリカの別の州に住んでいます。 (かれはアメリカのべつのしゅうにすんでいます。) - He lives in another US state.
- 州の法律を確認してください。 (しゅうのほうりつをかくにんしてください。) - Please check the state's laws.

After a place name, it joins directly: カリフォルニア州 (カリフォルニアしゅう), "the state of California."

## How the kanji combine

The single character 州 supplies the on reading しゅう. It can stand as a noun or attach to a place name; there is no two-kanji relationship to divide. Keep the long vowel in しゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[国](国.md) (くに) names a country in this comparison. [県](県.md) (けん) names a prefecture in modern Japan. These terms identify different territorial units, not higher and lower degrees of the same adjective.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that every region called 州 has the same powers or size. The word covers several administrative systems, and its continent use is broader still. The reading taught here is しゅう for a region or continent, not a word for a sandbank.
