# 大好き (だいすき) — like very much; love

## Meaning

大好き expresses **liking very much or loving someone or something**. Despite the English verb, Japanese uses a な-adjective: Xが大好きです and 大好きな + noun.

- 私は猫が大好きです。 (わたしはねこがだいすきです。) — I love cats.
- これは私の大好きな歌です。 (これはわたしのだいすきなうたです。) — This is a song I love.

For an activity, use verb + の: 泳ぐのが大好きです (およぐのがだいすきです), “I love swimming.”

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md), read ダイ, intensifies [好](../kanji/好.md)き (すき), “liked.” This is an on-first mixed formation: Sino-Japanese 大 plus native 好き. The `kango` metadata follows the repository's convention for mixed on-first words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[好き](好き.md) is less emphatic. 大嫌い (だいきらい), “strongly dislike/hate,” supplies the strong negative contrast. 好きではない simply denies liking.

## Distinctive meaning

大好き reports someone's affection or preference, not an objective property of being “very likeable.” It can concern food, animals, activities, or people. When said about a person, the relationship and context determine whether the affection is romantic.
