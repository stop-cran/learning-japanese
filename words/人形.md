# 人形 (にんぎょう) — doll; puppet

## Meaning

人形 is a doll, puppet, or modeled figure, often resembling a person. It can be a toy, a display piece, or something used in a performance.

- 子供が人形で遊んでいます。 (こどもがにんぎょうであそんでいます。) — The child is playing with a doll.
- 棚に人形が飾ってあります。 (たなににんぎょうがかざってあります。) — A doll is displayed on the shelf.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md), "person," combines with [形](../kanji/形.md), "shape," to suggest a human-shaped figure. The readings are ニン + ギョウ, not ひと + かた in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぬいぐるみ is a stuffed toy, such as a plush animal. フィギュア often names a collectible modeled figure. These categories can overlap, but they highlight different kinds of objects.

## Distinctive meaning

人形 is not limited to a doll for children. A traditional decorative doll or a puppet in a show can also be 人形. In figurative language it may describe a person controlled by someone else, but that is not the basic toy or display sense.
