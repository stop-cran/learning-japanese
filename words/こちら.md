# こちら — this way; this person

## Meaning

こちら points toward the speaker's side: "this way," "here," or "this one." It can also politely identify a person near the speaker.

こちらへどうぞ。 (こちらへどうぞ。) — This way, please.

こちらは田中さんです。 (こちらはたなかさんです。) — This is Mr. or Ms. Tanaka.

## How the kanji combine

こちら is normally written in kana. 此方 is a rare dictionary spelling, not a spelling needed for everyday use. こっち is the casual counterpart for direction and side.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ここ primarily identifies a place; これ identifies a thing. そちら indicates the listener's side, while あちら points away from both participants.

## Distinctive meaning

こちら is useful for introductions because it can refer politely to a person; これ normally cannot do that. It can also mean "our side" or "we" in a conversation between two parties. Its interpretation depends on what the participants are pointing to or contrasting, not just physical distance.
