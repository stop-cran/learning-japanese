# 下旬 (げじゅん) - last third of a month

## Meaning

下旬 is the period from the 21st to the last day of a month.
"Last third" is a calendar division, not an instruction to divide each month's days into three equal groups.

- 四月下旬に引っ越します。 (しがつげじゅんにひっこします。) - I will move in late April.
- 二月下旬は二十一日から月末までです。 (にがつげじゅんはにじゅういちにちからげつまつまでです。) - Late February runs from the 21st through the end of the month.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) identifies the later section; 旬 contributes a roughly ten-day calendar period.
Together they identify the final section of a month.
The on readings combine as ゲ + ジュン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上旬 (じょうじゅん) is the 1st through the 10th, and 中旬 (ちゅうじゅん) the 11th through the 20th.
[月末](月末.md) (げつまつ) is the end of the month, rather than the entire precisely defined 下旬 interval.

## Distinctive meaning

四月下旬 does not mean the last third of a year or the final ten days of any arbitrary period.
It gives a useful approximate date without choosing a particular day.
The final section can have fewer or more than ten days because months differ in length.
