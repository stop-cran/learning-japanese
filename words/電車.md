# 電車 (でんしゃ) — electric train

## Meaning

電車 is an electric train, and in everyday speech any commuter train or tram: 電車で行く (go by train), 電車に乗る (get on a train), 満員電車 まんいんでんしゃ (a packed train), 終電 しゅうでん (the last train).

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) (electricity) modifies [車](../kanji/車.md) (vehicle): "an electric vehicle". Both are on readings (デン + シャ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 汽車 きしゃ (a steam train, old-fashioned), 列車 れっしゃ (a train, general or formal), 新幹線 しんかんせん (bullet train).
- Not a synonym: 地下鉄 ちかてつ (subway) is its own word, though the trains are 電車 too.

## Distinctive meaning

電車 emphasises the commuter train; 列車 is used in announcements and for long-distance trains.
