# 列島 (れっとう) - archipelago; chain of islands

## Meaning

列島 is an archipelago or chain of islands, especially islands
arranged in a long line. It names the group, not one island.

- 地図で日本列島を見ました。 (ちずでにほんれっとうをみました。) - I looked at the Japanese archipelago on a map.
- その列島には多くの島があります。 (そのれっとうにはおおくのしまがあります。) - There are many islands in that archipelago.

日本列島 (にほんれっとう) is a useful established compound.

## How the kanji combine

[列](../kanji/列.md) contributes a row or line and
[島](../kanji/島.md) island: islands considered in a chain.
レツ + トウ becomes レットウ, with small っ before と.
Do not read it れつしま.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[諸島](諸島.md) (しょとう) also names a group of islands;
列島 highlights the chain-like arrangement.
[島](島.md) (しま) is an island.
[半島](半島.md) (はんとう) is a peninsula connected to other land,
not the opposite kind of island chain.

## Distinctive meaning

The arrangement is a geographical description, not a requirement for
a perfectly straight line. Learn conventional geographical names as
whole expressions rather than freely replacing 列島 with 諸島
inside every name.
