# かわいらしい (かわいらしい) - adorable; sweet-looking

## Meaning

かわいらしい is an い-adjective meaning cute, sweet, lovely, or adorable. It can describe a person, animal, object, or an endearing detail such as a smile. Attach it directly to a noun: かわいらしい花 (かわいらしいはな), "a lovely little flower."

- 庭にかわいらしい花が咲いています。 (にわにかわいらしいはながさいています。) - Lovely flowers are blooming in the garden.
- その子はかわいらしく笑った。 (そのこはかわいらしくわらった。) - The child smiled sweetly.

The adverbial form is かわいらしく. The past form is かわいらしかった, and the negative is かわいらしくない.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses hiragana. The dictionary spelling 可愛らしい has the same reading and meaning. Learn かわいらしい as an established adjective; its ending does not make it a report meaning "I hear that it is cute."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[かわいい](かわいい.md) is a close comparison and can describe the same flower or child. [きれい](きれい.md) and [美しい](美しい.md) concern beauty or attractiveness without necessarily suggesting endearing cuteness. There is no single opposite that suits every use.

## Distinctive meaning

かわいらしい presents the subject as charming or endearing, not merely small. A small object need not be かわいらしい. When describing another adult, consider the relationship and setting: praise framed as "cute" can sound affectionate but may not be the compliment intended.
