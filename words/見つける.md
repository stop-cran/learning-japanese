# 見つける (みつける) — find; discover

## Meaning

見つける means to find, discover, or spot something. It is transitive: what is found takes を.

- なくした鍵を見つけました。 (なくしたかぎをみつけました。) — I found the key I had lost.
- 駅の近くで安い店を見つけました。 (えきのちかくでやすいみせをみつけました。) — I found an inexpensive shop near the station.

It is an ichidan verb: 見つけます (みつけます), 見つけて (みつけて), 見つけない (みつけない).

A further dictionary sense is "be used to seeing; be familiar with" in a visual setting. The attested phrase いつも見つけている景色 (いつもみつけているけしき) means "scenery one is accustomed to seeing." Here 見つけている describes visual familiarity, not repeatedly discovering a new view. It is not a general replacement for knowing a fact or being familiar with any subject; 見慣れる (みなれる) is a useful comparison for being accustomed to seeing.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md), "see," supplies み. The rest is written in kana in this headword; 見付ける is another standard spelling of the same native verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

探す (さがす), "look for," describes the search, not necessarily success. 見つかる (みつかる) is the intransitive partner: 鍵が見つかった (かぎがみつかった), "the key turned up."

## Distinctive meaning

見つける need not follow a deliberate search: you can come across a shop by chance. Compare 鍵を見つけた, "I found the key," with 鍵が見つかった, which presents the key's being found without naming the finder.
