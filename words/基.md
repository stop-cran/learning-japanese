# 基 (もと) - basis; foundation

## Meaning

基 (もと) is a basis, base, or foundation.
A particularly useful construction is Xを基に, "on the basis of X" or "using X as a basis."
Use Xを基にしたY to describe a Y based on X.

- この映画は実際の出来事を基にしています。 (このえいがはじっさいのできごとをもとにしています。) - This film is based on actual events.
- 調査の結果を基に、計画を立てました。 (ちょうさのけっかをもとに、けいかくをたてました。) - We made a plan based on the survey results.

In both, を marks what serves as the basis. The complete phrase を基に then connects that basis to the resulting work or decision.

## How the kanji combine

基 expresses a foundation, using the native reading もと here.
JMdict groups several spellings under もと, but specifically notes 基 as a spelling used for the basis/foundation sense.
That preference is not a rule that every occurrence of もと must use 基.

## Synonyms and antonyms

基礎 (きそ) also means a foundation or basic groundwork.
[原因](原因.md) (げんいん) is a cause; the basis for a plan need not be the cause of an event.
[元](元.md) (もと) overlaps in source/origin uses, but spelling depends on the sense.

## Distinctive meaning

Sharing a dictionary entry does not make all spellings interchangeable.
In particular, the entry restricts some senses to 元 or 本; this card does not assign those senses to 基.
A film based on events can still adapt them: を基に states a foundation, not an exact reproduction.
