# 煮る (にる) - boil; simmer; stew

## Meaning

煮る means cooking something in water or another liquid: boiling, simmering
or stewing it. It is a transitive ichidan verb:
煮ます (にます), 煮た (にた), 煮て (にて), 煮ない (にない).
The food or liquid being heated takes を.

- 野菜を柔らかくなるまで煮ます。
  (やさいをやわらかくなるまでにます。) - I simmer the vegetables until they become tender.
- 豆を弱火でゆっくり煮ました。
  (まめをよわびでゆっくりにました。) - I cooked the beans slowly over low heat.

弱火で specifies low heat. 煮る does not require a vigorous rolling boil:
gentle simmering also fits.

## How the kanji combine

煮 supplies に and る is okurigana in this native verb.
It is not the homophone [似る](似る.md) (にる), "resemble."
The spelling distinguishes two different verbs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[煮える](煮える.md) (にえる) is intransitive:
野菜が煮えた (やさいがにえた), "the vegetables cooked through."
[沸かす](沸かす.md) (わかす) and [沸く](沸く.md) (わく) commonly concern
bringing liquid to a boil and liquid boiling.
[炊く](炊く.md) (たく) is useful for cooking rice;
its broader simmering use has regional qualifications.
[料理](料理.md)する (りょうりする) is broader cooking.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss "cook" does not license every cooking method.
Baking or grilling is normally [焼く](焼く.md) (やく), not 煮る.
Keep the liquid-cooking method and the を construction together:
煮る is something a cook does, whereas food が煮える describes cooking
or becoming cooked. Neither verb should be reduced to a fixed English tense.
