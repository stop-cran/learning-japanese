# トンネル (トンネル) - tunnel

## Meaning

トンネル is a noun for a tunnel: a passage through or beneath something, such as a mountain, the ground, or water. This card focuses on the physical passage sense.

- 電車は長いトンネルを通りました。 (でんしゃはながいトンネルをとおりました。) - The train passed through a long tunnel.
- トンネルを出ると、海が見えました。 (トンネルをでると、うみがみえました。) - When we came out of the tunnel, we could see the sea.

In 通る, を marks the route traversed. With 出る, を marks the place left. トンネルに入る (トンネルにはいる) instead uses に for the place entered.

## How the kanji combine

トンネル is a katakana loanword, with no kanji to decompose. Preserve the ン before ネ: ト・ン・ネ・ル. The spelling is not obtained by assigning Japanese meanings to individual syllables.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[道](道.md) (みち) is a road or path more generally. [地下鉄](地下鉄.md) (ちかてつ) is a subway system or train, not the tunnel itself. A tunnel and a bridge are contrasting structures, not lexical opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume every トンネル is part of a railway or lies entirely underground. The surrounding phrase identifies what passes through it and where it is built. Specialized dictionary uses exist in other fields, but the travel examples here concern a physical passage.
