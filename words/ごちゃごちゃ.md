# ごちゃごちゃ — jumbled; cluttered

## Meaning

ごちゃごちゃ describes things mixed together in a disorderly way, or an explanation that is hard
to follow.

- 机の上に書類や文具が重なり、ごちゃごちゃしている。
  — Papers and stationery are piled on the desk, making it cluttered.
- ごちゃごちゃした説明では、要点がつかみにくい。
  — It is hard to grasp the main point from a jumbled explanation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ごちゃごちゃ emphasizes things being jumbled or confused. ぐちゃぐちゃ can instead suggest a
crushed, pulpy condition; ごたごた often names troublesome complications.

## Distinctive meaning

The word also has an adverbial use for nagging or complaining about several things. These examples
use its disorderly or confused sense.
