# 部長 (ぶちょう) — department head

## Meaning

部長 means the head or director of a department or section. In a school club, it can mean the club leader. The exact English job title depends on the organization's structure.

- 部長に予定を確認しました。  
  ぶちょうによていをかくにんしました。  
  "I checked the schedule with the department head."
- 田中部長は会議中です。  
  たなかぶちょうはかいぎちゅうです。  
  "Department Head Tanaka is in a meeting."

## How the kanji combine

[部](../kanji/部.md) means a division or group, and [長](../kanji/長.md) contributes "head; leader". The on readings are ぶ + ちょう. Here 長 identifies leadership, not physical length.

## Synonyms and antonyms

課長 (かちょう) heads a 課, often a smaller section within a department. 社長 (しゃちょう) heads a company. These are related roles, not interchangeable translations or antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

部長 can function as a title after a name, as in 田中部長. Do not infer one universal corporate rank from an English gloss such as "section chief"; the Japanese unit being headed and the local organization matter.
