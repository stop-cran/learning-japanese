# こと

## Meaning

こと means a matter, event, fact, or thing someone does, rather than primarily a physical object. It can turn a clause into a noun phrase.

- 大事なことを忘れました。 (だいじな ことを わすれました。) — I forgot something important.
- 日本語を話すことは楽しいです。 (にほんごを はなすことは たのしいです。) — Speaking Japanese is enjoyable.

For this noun-forming use, attach こと to a plain-form verb clause or an i-adjective. A na-adjective takes な, as in 静かなこと (しずかなこと), "being quiet"; a noun predicate commonly takes である.

## How the kanji combine

The word can be written 事, but kana is usual in grammatical constructions. There is no kanji decomposition in this spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もの often refers to a physical thing; こと commonly concerns an action or matter. This is a useful starting contrast, not a rule covering every abstract use of either word. の also forms noun phrases, but is not interchangeable in all constructions.

## Distinctive meaning

こと lets an action function as a topic or object: in 話すことは, は follows the entire noun phrase. It does not automatically mean "the fact that" in every sentence.
