# 氷 (こおり) - ice

## Meaning

氷 is ice: water in its frozen, solid state. It is a noun, used for ice in a drink as well as ice on a lake or road.

- コップに氷を入れてください。 (コップにこおりをいれてください。) - Please put ice in the glass.
- 池の氷が溶け始めました。 (いけのこおりがとけはじめました。) - The ice on the pond began to melt.

In the first sentence, 氷 is the object of 入れる. In the second, it is what starts melting.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 氷 represents the native noun こおり. Write the long vowel with お: こおり, not こうり. There is no multi-kanji relationship to analyze. The related verb [凍る](凍る.md) (こおる) means "freeze."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[水](水.md) (みず) names water, often contrasted with ice when liquid and solid states matter. [雪](雪.md) (ゆき) is snow, and 雹 (ひょう) is hail. These are related frozen-water words, not interchangeable names for an ice cube.

## Distinctive meaning

こおり should not be learned as a general translation of "hail." The ordinary word for that precipitation is 雹. 氷 concerns the substance and can occur in many settings, including food and drinks. In an order such as 氷なし (こおりなし), "without ice," the requested omission is clear from the drink context.
