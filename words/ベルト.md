# ベルト (ベルト) - belt

## Meaning

ベルト is a belt, especially one worn around the waist. It is a noun.
Useful actions include ベルトを締める (ベルトをしめる), "fasten/tighten a belt,"
and ベルトを外す (ベルトをはずす), "undo/remove a belt."

- ズボンに合うベルトを買いました。 (ズボンにあうベルトをかいました。) - I bought a belt that goes with my trousers.
- ベルトを少し緩めました。 (ベルトをすこしゆるめました。) - I loosened my belt a little.

The verb identifies what happens to the belt; ベルト itself does not mean
fastening or tightening.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "belt."
It has no kanji decomposition. The final ト belongs to the Japanese form,
which is used as a whole noun rather than conjugated like a verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[帯](帯.md) (おび) can name a sash or belt, notably with traditional clothing.
The words overlap, but a kimono sash and a trouser belt have different forms
and typical contexts. Neither is the opposite of the other.

## Distinctive meaning

The clothing meaning is not the word's entire range: 機械のベルト
(きかいのベルト) is a machine belt. In シートベルト, the compound identifies
a seat belt. Context distinguishes something worn around the waist from a
mechanical or safety belt, rather than the script alone settling its use.
