# すっと — swiftly; smoothly

## Meaning

すっと describes a quick, direct, or smooth movement, often without hesitation or resistance. Depending on the action, English may use "straight," "quietly," or "all of a sudden."

- 彼はすっと立ち上がりました。 (かれはすっとたちあがりました。) — He stood up in one swift movement.
- 紙がすっと箱の中に入りました。 (かみがすっとはこのなかにはいりました。) — The paper slid smoothly into the box.

It normally modifies a verb directly; do not add に after it.

## How the kanji combine

This mimetic word is written in kana. The small っ is part of the word. すうっと and スーッと are elongated variants that can emphasize the movement or sensation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

急に (きゅうに), "suddenly," focuses on an abrupt change. まっすぐ focuses on straight direction. Neither necessarily adds the smooth, unobstructed quality of すっと.

## Distinctive meaning

すっとする can describe feeling refreshed or relieved. Also distinguish ずっと, with voiced ず, which can mean "all along" or "much more." The single sound change produces a different word.
