# 市民 (しみん) — citizen; city resident

## Meaning

市民 means a citizen or the citizenry. It can also refer specifically to residents of a city.
The surrounding discussion tells you whether the focus is the wider civic public or a particular city.

- 多くの市民が集まりました。 (おおくのしみんがあつまりました。) — Many citizens gathered.
- 市民の声を聞くことが大切です。 (しみんのこえをきくことがたいせつです。) — It is important to listen to citizens' voices.

## How the kanji combine

[市](../kanji/市.md) contributes the city, and [民](../kanji/民.md) contributes its people.
The modifier-plus-head combination is transparent in the city-resident sense.
The word also has the broader civic sense above, so translating the characters as "city people" does not cover every context.
The readings are シ + ミン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

住民 (じゅうみん), "resident; inhabitant," focuses on living in a place.
市民 can overlap with it for a city's population, while also presenting people as members of civic society.
Neither word supplies a universal opposite category: a non-resident is not automatically an opponent or outsider in every sense.

## Distinctive meaning

English "citizen" can suggest a specific nationality status.
Because 市民 also has a city-resident sense, the word alone does not settle that legal question.
Read it together with the place and social context rather than treating one English gloss as a complete definition.
