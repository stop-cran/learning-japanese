# 明かり (あかり) - light; illumination

## Meaning

明かり is a noun for light or a glow that illuminates the surroundings. It can also refer to a light or lamp as a source of illumination. 明かりをつける means "turn on a light"; 明かりを消す means "turn it off."

- 月の明かりで道が見えます。 (つきのあかりでみちがみえます。) - I can see the path by moonlight.
- 部屋の明かりを消しました。 (へやのあかりをけしました。) - I turned off the room's light.

The first example concerns illumination, while the second treats the light as something that can be switched off.

## How the kanji combine

[明](../kanji/明.md) contributes brightness. This is a native noun, not a two-kanji compound; かり is part of its written form. Compare [明るい](明るい.md) (あかるい), "bright."

灯り is another spelling used for a light or lamp. That does not make every dictionary sense interchangeable across the spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[光](光.md) (ひかり) is a broader word for light, including light as a physical phenomenon. [電灯](電灯.md) (でんとう) specifies an electric light. 暗闇 (くらやみ), "darkness," is a contextual opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

明かり directs attention to light that makes a place visible or produces a glow. It does not by itself specify electricity: moonlight, candlelight, and a room's lighting can all fit. For a person's cheerful disposition, use an appropriate adjective such as 明るい rather than this noun.
