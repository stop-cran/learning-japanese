# 再来週 (さらいしゅう) - week after next

## Meaning

再来週 is the week after next: the calendar week following 来週 (らいしゅう). It does not mean exactly fourteen days from the moment of speaking.

- 再来週、京都に行きます。 (さらいしゅう、きょうとにいきます。) - I am going to Kyoto the week after next.
- 試験は再来週の月曜日です。 (しけんはさらいしゅうのげつようびです。) - The exam is on Monday of the week after next.

The week expression can stand without に; の connects it to a weekday.

## How the kanji combine

再 adds another recurrence to [来](../kanji/来.md), "coming," and [週](../kanji/週.md), "week." Learn the reading さ・らい・しゅう; 再 is さ here, not さい.

JMdict marks the mixed spelling さ来週 as search-only. Use 再来週 as the ordinary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

来週 is next week, 今週 (こんしゅう) is this week, and 先週 (せんしゅう) is last week. These locate different weeks rather than forming an absolute opposite pair.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 再来週 when the relevant calendar week matters. 二週間後 (にしゅうかんご), "two weeks later," instead measures an interval from a reference time and need not pick out the same intended date.
