# 嫌い (きらい) - disliked, dislike

## Meaning

嫌い expresses dislike and, in stronger contexts, hatred. Despite ending in い, it behaves as a **na-adjective**, not an i-adjective. The disliked thing commonly takes が.

- 私は納豆が嫌いです。 (わたしはなっとうがきらいです。) - I don't like natto.
- 嫌いな食べ物はありますか。 (きらいなたべものはありますか。) - Are there any foods you dislike?

The negative is 嫌いではない (きらいではない), "do not dislike," not きらくない.

## How the kanji combine

嫌 writes きら, followed by い. That written ending does not determine the grammatical class. Learn the complete word with its な attachment.

## Synonyms and antonyms

好き (すき), "liked," is the basic opposite. 苦手 (にがて) can mean being poor at something or finding it uncomfortable and often softens a statement about disliked food or activities.

## Distinctive meaning

嫌い can sound quite direct, especially about a person. Conversely, 嫌いではない only denies dislike; it need not be an enthusiastic statement that you love something.
