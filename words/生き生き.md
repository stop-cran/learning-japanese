# 生き生き (いきいき) - lively; vividly; energetically

## Meaning

生き生き describes lively, animated or vivid activity and expression.
Use 生き生きと before a verb, or 生き生きしている for an animated state.
The noun-modifying pattern 生き生きした表情 (いきいきしたひょうじょう)
means "an animated expression."

- 子供たちは生き生きと遊んでいます。 (こどもたちはいきいきとあそんでいます。) - The children are playing energetically.
- この小説は私の故郷の様子を生き生きと描いています。 (このしょうせつはわたしのふるさとのようすをいきいきとえがいています。) - This novel vividly depicts my hometown.

The second example concerns vivid portrayal, not whether the novel is literally alive.

## How the kanji combine

The expression repeats a native form associated with [生](../kanji/生.md) and life.
Learn いきいき as a whole expression; it is not the on reading セイ repeated.
活き活き, with [活](../kanji/活.md), is another spelling recorded for the same reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[元気](元気.md) (げんき) overlaps in energy and vitality.
[ぴんぴん](ぴんぴん.md) can describe someone vigorously well.
[活気](活気.md) (かっき) is liveliness or vigor.
[ぐったり](ぐったり.md), "limp; exhausted," gives a contextual contrast, not a universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

生き生き can describe eyes, expressions, actions or a vivid description, not just
physical health. Do not mechanically replace it with 生きている (いきている),
"is alive"; the expression adds an impression of animation or vividness.
