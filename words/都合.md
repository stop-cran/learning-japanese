# 都合 (つごう) — circumstances; availability

## Meaning

都合 means **circumstances or convenience**, especially whether someone's schedule allows something. Useful patterns are 都合がいい, “be convenient/available,” and 都合が悪い, “be inconvenient/unavailable.”

- 土曜日は都合がいいですか。 (どようびはつごうがいいですか。) — Does Saturday work for you?
- 仕事の都合で行けません。 (しごとのつごうでいけません。) — I can't go because of work commitments.

In the second example, noun + の + 都合 names the relevant circumstances, and で marks the reason.

## How the kanji combine

[都](../kanji/都.md) is ツ and [合](../kanji/合.md) is ゴウ here. Learn the established on-reading compound つごう; its meaning is not obtained by literally translating 都 as “capital.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

予定 (よてい) is a plan or schedule. 都合 concerns whether arrangements fit the situation. 便利 (べんり), “convenient/useful,” often evaluates a tool, location, or service rather than someone's availability.

## Distinctive meaning

都合が悪い need not mean that someone is ill or morally bad: it often simply means the timing does not work. 都合 can leave the exact reason unspecified without asserting a particular excuse.
