# 固まる (かたまる) - harden; solidify

## Meaning

固まる means becoming hard or solid. It also describes a plan becoming firm, people gathering closely together, or a computer freezing and becoming unresponsive.

- 冷えると、チョコレートが固まります。 (ひえると、チョコレートがかたまります。) - The chocolate solidifies when it cools.
- 来年の計画が固まりました。 (らいねんのけいかくがかたまりました。) - The plan for next year has taken definite shape.
- 子どもたちは一か所に固まって座っていました。 (こどもたちはいっかしょにかたまってすわっていました。) - The children were sitting huddled together in one place.
- パソコンが固まって、動きません。 (パソコンがかたまって、うごきません。) - The computer has frozen and will not respond.

It is an intransitive godan verb: 固まります, 固まって, 固まらない.

## How the kanji combine

[固](../kanji/固.md) writes the native stem かた; まる is the remaining written ending. Its firmness association extends from materials to plans or a closely packed group. The whole verb's established senses, not a literal hard-material explanation, license the computing use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

固める (かためる) is the transitive partner, "make firm; solidify something." [凍る](凍る.md) (こおる) specifically concerns freezing. [集まる](集まる.md) (あつまる) means gathering more generally, and [決まる](決まる.md) (きまる) overlaps in something becoming settled.

## Distinctive meaning

A firm plan is not a physically hard object. Likewise, パソコンが固まる describes unresponsiveness, not the machine turning to ice. A frozen program is not necessarily permanently broken; [壊れる](壊れる.md) (こわれる) has the broader breakdown meaning.
