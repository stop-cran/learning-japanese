# 県 (けん) - prefecture

## Meaning

県 is a prefecture, an administrative area in Japan. It works as a noun and as a suffix attached to a prefecture's name.

- 私は長野県に住んでいます。 (わたしはながのけんにすんでいます。) - I live in Nagano Prefecture.
- この県には大きな湖があります。 (このけんにはおおきなみずうみがあります。) - There is a large lake in this prefecture.

With a name, attach 県 directly: 長野県, not 長野の県. In the second example, この県 refers back to a prefecture already identifiable from context.

## How the kanji combine

県 is a single-kanji noun with the on reading ケン. There is no two-kanji meaning relationship to explain. The reading remains けん in the place-name example.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[市](市.md) (し), "city," names a different administrative unit. [都](都.md), read と in 東京都 (とうきょうと), belongs to the broader set 都道府県 (とどうふけん), the collective term for Japan's prefectural-level divisions. These are administrative categories, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not add 県 to every Japanese place name: Tokyo is 東京都, and Osaka is 大阪府 (おおさかふ). In translations about other countries, 県 may label a county, department, or similar division. Its precise institutional meaning depends on the country, not just the English gloss "prefecture."
