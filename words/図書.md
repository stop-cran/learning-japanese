# 図書 (としょ) - books

## Meaning

図書 means books, especially as reading materials or a collection being
selected, kept, or lent. It is a noun: 図書を借りる (としょをかりる),
"borrow books," and 図書の返却 (としょのへんきゃく), "returning books."

- この図書は来週まで借りられます。 (このとしょはらいしゅうまでかりられます。) - This book can be borrowed until next week.
- 学校で新しい図書を購入しました。 (がっこうであたらしいとしょをこうにゅうしました。) - The school purchased new books.

The first example refers to one item. The English gloss "books" does not
make the Japanese noun obligatorily plural.

## How the kanji combine

[図](../kanji/図.md) contributes diagrams or pictorial material, and
[書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing or books. Together they name books
as written materials; they do not require every book to contain diagrams.
The on readings here are と + しょ, not ず + しょ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本](本.md) (ほん) is the broad everyday word for a book.
[図書館](図書館.md) (としょかん) is a library, the place or institution,
not the books themselves. There is no useful antonym of 図書; another medium
is a comparison, not an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

図書 is useful in school and library contexts, including labels and descriptions
of collections. That does not make it restricted to official documents.
Distinguish 図書を借りる, borrowing a book, from 図書館で借りる,
borrowing at a library: one names the object and the other the location.
