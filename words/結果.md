# 結果 (けっか) - result; outcome

## Meaning

結果 is the result or outcome of an action, event, or investigation. Use noun +
の + 結果 for "the result of..." A completed action can precede it in the past
plain form: 調べた結果 (しらべたけっか), "as a result of checking."

- 試験の結果が出ました。 (しけんのけっかがでました。) - The exam results are out.
- 調べた結果、原因が分かりました。 (しらべたけっか、げんいんがわかりました。) - After investigating, we found the cause.

The first example uses 結果 as a noun. The second connects an investigation
to what was learned through it.

## How the kanji combine

結 contributes tying or bringing together, and [果](../kanji/果.md) contributes
fruit or outcome. The combination connects bringing something to fruition with
its result. The reading is ケッカ, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[原因](原因.md) (げんいん), "cause," is the other side of a cause-and-result
relationship, not an opposite value judgment. [成績](成績.md) (せいせき)
concerns performance or grades; 結果 has wider applications.

## Distinctive meaning

結果 does not mean success by itself. 良い結果 (よいけっか) is a good result,
but 悪い結果 (わるいけっか) is a bad one. In the connective pattern, use a
completed action to show what led to the outcome rather than assuming any
two events occurring in sequence have a causal relationship.
