# 指定 (してい) - designation; specification

## Meaning

指定 is specifying or designating a particular person, thing, place, time, or other choice. It is a noun and a transitive する verb: **場所を指定する** (ばしょをしていする), "specify the place."

- 集合場所を指定してください。
  (しゅうごうばしょをしていしてください。) - Please specify the meeting place.
- 指定された時間までに来てください。
  (していされたじかんまでにきてください。) - Please come by the specified time.

指定された is passive, "was specified/designated." The second sentence uses までに for a deadline, not a requirement to remain until that time. 指定の時間 (していのじかん) also means "the designated time."

## How the kanji combine

[指](../kanji/指.md) contributes pointing out, and [定](../kanji/定.md) determining or fixing. Together they suggest identifying a particular choice and settling it. The on readings are シ + テイ: してい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[選択](選択.md) (せんたく) is selection among options; [決定](決定.md) (けってい) is a decision or determination. 指定 particularly identifies the person, place, time, or item selected for a purpose. These actions can overlap in the same arrangement.

[指す](指す.md) (さす) can mean point or designate. The source gloss "pointing at" does not mean that 指定 always describes a physical finger gesture; verbally specifying a time is also 指定.

## Distinctive meaning

指定 is not automatically a reservation or a promise. 指定席 (していせき), "reserved/designated seat," is an established compound, but specifying a meeting place does not itself book the place. Administrative uses can also designate a formal status, such as a protected cultural property; the precise designation comes from the surrounding words.
