# 選ぶ (えらぶ) — choose

## Meaning

選ぶ means choosing or selecting. Mark what is chosen with を, and the set of options with から when needed.
Its polite form is 選びます (えらびます); its て-form is 選んで (えらんで).

- この中から一つ選んでください。 (このなかからひとつえらんでください。) — Please choose one from these.
- 妹のために本を選びました。 (いもうとのためにほんをえらびました。) — I chose a book for my younger sister.

## How the kanji combine

[選](../kanji/選.md) carries selection, and ぶ completes the native verb えらぶ.
The ending changes to んで in 選んで, just as in 遊んで (あそんで), the て-form of [遊ぶ](遊ぶ.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

選択 (せんたく) is the noun "selection" and forms 選択する, "select."
[決める](決める.md) (きめる) emphasizes deciding or settling something. Choosing a book and deciding which book to buy can describe the same event
from different angles; these are not mutually exclusive actions.

## Distinctive meaning

選ぶ does not mean buying. You can choose a book, a route, or a team member without making a purchase.
The useful pattern is selection from alternatives, whether or not those alternatives are explicitly listed.
