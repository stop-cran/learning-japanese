# 崩れる (くずれる) - collapse; crumble; lose shape

## Meaning

崩れる means collapse or crumble: something loses its coherent form or arrangement. It is intransitive; the collapsing thing takes が.

- 積み木の塔が崩れました。 (つみきのとうがくずれました。) - The tower of building blocks collapsed.
- 雨で髪型が崩れました。 (あめでかみがたがくずれました。) - My hairstyle lost its shape in the rain.

It also describes order breaking down, as in 秩序が崩れる (ちつじょがくずれる). 天気が崩れる (てんきがくずれる) means the weather turns bad; it need not mean a physical object falls.

The ichidan forms include 崩れます (くずれます), 崩れて (くずれて), 崩れない (くずれない).

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native verb: 崩 with the ending れる. There is no 崩 kanji card in the current collection. Learn くずれる alongside the related transitive くずす.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[崩す](崩す.md) (くずす) makes the affected thing an object: 塔を崩す (とうをくずす), knock down the tower. The pair describes a difference in sentence structure, not a guarantee that every use has an equally usual counterpart.

[壊れる](壊れる.md) (こわれる), break, can describe a machine failing without losing its shape. [倒れる](倒れる.md) (たおれる) can describe something falling over while remaining in one piece. [荒れる](荒れる.md) (あれる) overlaps in bad or rough weather, but there is no fixed severity boundary that makes the two weather expressions interchangeable in every case.

## Distinctive meaning

Intransitive does not mean "without a cause": rain or another person's action can cause something to lose its shape. The sentence still presents the thing whose condition changes as the subject. The hairstyle and weather uses are additional to the basic collapse/crumble meaning.
