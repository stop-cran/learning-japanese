# ベテラン - veteran; experienced hand

## Meaning

ベテラン is someone with considerable experience in a particular field: a veteran,
old hand or seasoned worker. Use ベテランの + occupation or role.

- ベテランの看護師に相談しました。 (ベテランのかんごしにそうだんしました。) - I consulted an experienced nurse.
- 彼はこの仕事のベテランです。 (かれはこのしごとのベテランです。) - He is an old hand at this work.

## How the word is formed

This is a katakana loanword, written ベテラン. There is no kanji compound to
analyze. Learn the Japanese field-experience meaning rather than importing every
meaning of English "veteran."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[プロ](プロ.md), "professional," concerns professional standing or expertise;
a professional can still be new to a particular job. [名人](名人.md) (めいじん)
emphasizes mastery. [素人](素人.md) (しろうと) is an amateur or nonexpert,
while 新人 (しんじん) is a newcomer.

## Distinctive meaning

An experienced teacher, driver or craft worker can be a ベテラン without having
served in the military. Conversely, the word does not automatically say someone
has retired, holds a degree, or is the best person in the field.
