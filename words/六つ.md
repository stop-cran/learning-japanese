# 六つ (むっつ) — six things

## Meaning

六つ means **six items** in the native general counting series. It is useful when a more specific counter is not being used.

りんごを六つ買いました。 (りんごをむっつかいました。) — I bought six apples.

The counting expression can follow the object and its particle, as above. Before a noun, use 六つの: 六つの質問 (むっつのしつもん), “six questions.”

## How the kanji combine

[六](../kanji/六.md) supplies the native number element, followed by つ. The whole form is **むっつ**, with small っ. Do not combine the on reading ロク with つ to make ろくつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

六 (ろく) is the number used with many specific counters: 六人 (ろくにん), “six people,” and 六本 (ろっぽん), “six long cylindrical items.” Five and seven in the general series are 五つ (いつつ) and 七つ (ななつ).

## Distinctive meaning

六つ is not a universal replacement for every number-plus-counter expression. It can also give a child's age, “six years old.” It is not the ordinary modern way to say six o'clock, which is 六時 (ろくじ).
