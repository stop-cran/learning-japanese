# わんわん — barking; loud wailing

## Meaning

A dog's barking, or loud, uninhibited human crying. It can also evoke loud resounding noise.

- 犬が門（もん）の前でわんわんほえた。 — The dog barked loudly in front of the gate.
- 子どもがわんわん泣（な）いている。 — The child is bawling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[にゃあにゃあ](にゃあにゃあ.md) represents meowing. [しくしく](しくしく.md) usually suggests quieter sobbing, while [めそめそ](めそめそ.md) can overlap in tearful behavior.

## Distinctive meaning

犬がわんわん鳴く（なく）and 子どもがわんわん泣く use different verbs in writing. In child-directed speech, わんわん can itself mean “doggy”; it is not the neutral adult animal name in every situation.
