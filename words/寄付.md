# 寄付 (きふ) - donation; contribution

## Meaning

寄付 is a donation or contribution, such as money or goods offered to an
organization or cause. 寄付する is transitive: recipient + に + thing + を +
寄付する.

- 図書館に本を寄付しました。 (としょかんにほんをきふしました。) - I donated books to the library.
- この団体は寄付を集めています。 (このだんたいはきふをあつめています。) - This organization is collecting donations.

The first example shows that 寄付 need not be cash. In the second, 寄付 is a
noun and the object of 集める, "collect."

## How the kanji combine

[寄](../kanji/寄.md) contributes bringing or sending toward, and
[付](../kanji/付.md) contributes giving or attaching. Together the word names
contributing something to a recipient. The on readings are キ + フ.
寄附 is another dictionary-listed spelling of the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[贈る](贈る.md) (おくる), "give as a gift," covers presents and other giving.
寄付 identifies a contribution, rather than an ordinary personal present.
There is no single antonym that covers donating both money and goods.

## Distinctive meaning

寄付 does not itself describe payment for a purchased item or service.
寄付金 (きふきん) specifically means donated money, while 寄付 can include
books or other goods. Keep the particles straight: the library receives に,
and the books given receive を.
