# 元気でね (げんきでね) - take care; keep well

## Meaning

元気でね is a friendly farewell wishing the other person well:
"take care" or "keep well."

- じゃあ、元気でね。また会おう。 (じゃあ、げんきでね。またあおう。) - Well then, take care. Let's meet again.
- 引っ越しても元気でね。 (ひっこしてもげんきでね。) - Take care after you move, too.

It is a wish addressed to the other person, not a statement that
the speaker is currently healthy.

## How the kanji combine

The base [元気](元気.md) (げんき), "well; energetic," contains
[元](../kanji/元.md) and [気](../kanji/気.md), read ゲン + キ.
The Japanese ending でね belongs to the complete farewell.
The kango grouping reflects the base, not a claim that で and ね
are Chinese-derived syllables.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お元気で (おげんきで) is a related farewell.
お大事に (おだいじに), "take care; get well soon," is useful when
someone is ill. [いってらっしゃい](いってらっしゃい.md) sees someone
off with an expected return and can also be translated "take care."

## Distinctive meaning

The bare ね ending makes this familiar rather than a generally formal formula.
Wishing someone continued well-being does not imply that they are sick.
English "take care" can also warn of immediate danger; that is not what
the farewell automatically means.
