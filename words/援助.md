# 援助 (えんじょ) - aid; assistance; support

## Meaning

援助 is a noun for aid or support, including money, supplies, and practical assistance. 援助する is a transitive verb: 人を援助する means to assist someone. With the noun, 人への援助 identifies aid directed to that person.

- 両親が留学の費用を援助してくれました。 (りょうしんがりゅうがくのひようをえんじょしてくれました。) - My parents helped pay for my studies abroad.
- 被災地への援助が必要です。 (ひさいちへのえんじょがひつようです。) - Aid for the disaster-hit area is needed.

援助を受ける (えんじょをうける) means "receive assistance"; the receiver need not be the person who performs 援助する.

## How the kanji combine

援 contributes support, and [助](../kanji/助.md) contributes help. The two related meanings reinforce one another in a noun read えんじょ. This component explanation is a guide to the modern word, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[手伝う](手伝う.md) (てつだう) describes helping with an activity. 援助 can instead supply the resources that make it possible. [救助](救助.md) (きゅうじょ) focuses on rescue or relief from danger.

## Distinctive meaning

援助 is not limited to governments, charities, or emergencies: family financial support also fits. Conversely, it is not automatically a loan. If repayment matters, the surrounding explanation must say so. The word names assistance, not its precise financial conditions.
