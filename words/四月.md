# 四月 (しがつ) — April

## Meaning

四月 is April: 四月に入学する (enter school in April; the Japanese school and fiscal year begin in April), 四月一日 しがつついたち (April 1st).

## How the kanji combine

[四](../kanji/四.md) (four) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month): the fourth month. The month always uses the on reading し, never よん: しがつ, not よんがつ.
"Four months" (duration) is 四か月 よんかげつ, with よん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 卯月 うづき (old name of the fourth month).
- Related: 四月 しがつ vs 四時 よじ — one is し, the other よ.

## Distinctive meaning

The calendar month needs し; the duration uses よん. Learn these as separate words.
