# 委嘱 (いしょく) — entrusting; commissioning

## Meaning

委嘱 is the formal act of entrusting someone with a task or commissioning them to perform a role. It can be used with する.

- 市は専門家に調査を委嘱しました。 (しはせんもんかにちょうさをいしょくしました。) — The city commissioned an expert to conduct an investigation.
- 委員会は外部の研究者に分析を委嘱しています。 (いいんかいはがいぶのけんきゅうしゃにぶんせきをいしょくしています。) — The committee has entrusted the analysis to an outside researcher.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[委](../kanji/委.md) means to entrust or leave something to another person. [嘱](../kanji/嘱.md) is read ショク, giving 委嘱 (いしょく).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[依頼](依頼.md) (いらい) is a more general request and can also commission work. 委嘱 is a formal entrusting or commissioning of a task or role.

## Distinctive meaning

委嘱 is formal and commonly used for assigning work, research, or an official role.
