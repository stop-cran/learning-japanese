# こってり — rich and heavy; thickly

## Meaning

Rich, heavy, or thick application, especially a rich-tasting food or a substantial coating.

- このラーメンのスープはこってりしている。 — This ramen broth is rich and heavy.
- トーストにバターをこってり塗（ぬ）った。 — I spread a thick layer of butter on the toast.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あっさり](あっさり.md) and [さっぱり](さっぱり.md) offer lighter-food contrasts. [どろどろ](どろどろ.md) describes thick, flowing consistency, which is not the same as richness of flavor.

## Distinctive meaning

こってりしたスープ can be welcome or too heavy depending on the eater. The word also intensifies a severe scolding in こってり叱られる（しかられる）; that use is not about a physical coating.
