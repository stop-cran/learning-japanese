# 見せる (みせる) — to show

## Meaning

見せる means "show" or "display." Use person + に and thing + を to say who sees what. It is an ichidan verb: 見せます (みせます), 見せて (みせて), 見せた (みせた).

友達に旅行の写真を見せました。 (ともだちにりょこうのしゃしんをみせました。) — I showed my friend photos from my trip.

切符を見せてください。 (きっぷをみせてください。) — Please show me your ticket.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) supplies み and the seeing connection. The following せる belongs to this established verb. The ます-stem is 見せ (みせ), not just 見.

## Synonyms and antonyms

見る (みる) is to look at or see something oneself. 見せる makes it available for someone else to see. 隠す (かくす), "hide," is a useful contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not automatically translate 見せる as "force someone to look." The ordinary showing sense is neutral about compulsion. English may omit the recipient, and Japanese can too when the person being shown something is clear from context.
