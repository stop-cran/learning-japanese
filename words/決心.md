# 決心 (けっしん) - resolve; determination

## Meaning

決心 is a firm decision or resolve. 決心する means to make up one's mind. A chosen action can be expressed with a dictionary-form verb followed by ことを決心する, or with a quoted clause and と決心する.

- 留学することを決心しました。 (りゅうがくすることをけっしんしました。) - I made up my mind to study abroad.
- もう諦めないと決心しました。 (もうあきらめないとけっしんしました。) - I resolved not to give up anymore.

The second example shows that the resolution can concern not doing something. 決心がつく (けっしんがつく) means reaching the point of having made up one's mind.

## How the kanji combine

[決](../kanji/決.md) contributes deciding, and [心](../kanji/心.md) contributes the mind or heart. The combination suggests settling one's mind. ケツ + シン becomes ケッシン, with a small っ before し.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[決める](決める.md) (きめる) broadly means deciding or choosing. [決定](決定.md) (けってい) can concern a settled decision by an organization. 決心 places particular emphasis on a person's resolve.

## Distinctive meaning

決心 names the commitment, not proof that the intended action has already happened. 留学を決心した can be said before any journey begins. It is therefore useful for the turning point between hesitation and a chosen course, rather than as a synonym for successfully completing that course.
