# 険しい (けわしい) - steep; rugged; stern

## Meaning

険しい describes steep, rugged terrain that is hard to traverse.
It also describes a grim, stern, or tense expression.
It is an i-adjective: 険しくない, not steep/stern; 険しかった, was steep/stern.

- この山道は険しいです。 (このやまみちはけわしいです。) - This mountain path is steep and rugged.
- 彼女は険しい顔で黙っていました。 (かのじょはけわしいかおでだまっていました。) - She remained silent with a stern expression.

Daijisen also supports difficult or unsafe prospects, as in 険しい道のり (けわしいみちのり), a difficult road ahead.
Whether the road is literal or figurative depends on context.

## How the kanji combine

[険](../kanji/険.md) contributes steepness or peril.
The native reading boundary is けわ.しい: しい is written outside the kanji.
Learn the whole adjective, not the on reading ケン followed by しい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[急](急.md) (きゅう) overlaps for steep slopes.
[厳しい](厳しい.md) (きびしい) overlaps in severity, but also has uses involving strict standards.
[難しい](難しい.md) (むずかしい) describes difficulty and can also concern a serious or glum look.
緩やかな坂 (ゆるやかなさか), a gentle slope, supplies a terrain contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The raw source gloss "inaccessible place, sharp eyes" must be understood adjectivally.
険しい is not the noun "place" and does not describe keen eyesight.
An angry or stern look is different from the ability to see fine details.
JMdict marks 嶮しい rare and 峻しい search-only; neither flag makes those forms recommended everyday spellings.
