# 憤慨 (ふんがい) — indignation; resentment

## Meaning

憤慨 is getting very angry. Digital Daijisen defines it as ひどく腹を立てること (ひどくはらをたてること), “getting extremely angry,” and gives 身勝手なふるまいに憤慨する (みがってなふるまいにふんがいする), “be indignant at selfish behavior.” JMdict glosses it “indignation; resentment”; with する, it is a verb.

- 住民たちは、市の一方的な決定に憤慨した。 (じゅうみんたちは、しのいっぽうてきなけっていにふんがいした。) — The residents were indignant at the city’s one-sided decision.
- 約束を破られて、彼は「もう信用できない」と憤慨していた。 (やくそくをやぶられて、かれは「もうしんようできない」とふんがいしていた。) — After the promise to him was broken, he was indignant, saying, “I can’t trust them anymore.”

## How the kanji combine

[憤](../kanji/憤.md) (ふん) means to be angry or indignant, and [慨](../kanji/慨.md) (がい) means to be filled with stirring feelings; [Digital Daijisen, 慨](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E6%85%A8) lists 憤慨 under that sense. に marks the cause of the anger: 決定に憤慨した, 身勝手なふるまいに憤慨する.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怒る](怒る.md) (おこる) is the everyday verb for getting angry. [恨む](恨む.md) (うらむ) is to bear a grudge against someone. [不満](不満.md) (ふまん) is dissatisfaction, which need not involve anger. [嘆く](嘆く.md) (なげく) is to lament or deplore; 憤慨 is anger rather than sorrow.

## Distinctive meaning

Related words are 憤り (いきどおり), “resentment; indignation,” and 義憤 (ぎふん), “righteous indignation.”
