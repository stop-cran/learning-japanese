# 一度に (いちどに) - all at once

## Meaning

一度に is an adverb meaning "all at once" or "in one go." It gathers several things or actions into a single occasion, rather than spreading them across separate occasions. Put it before the action it describes; do not add another に.

- 一度に全部食べないでください。 (いちどにぜんぶたべないでください。) - Please do not eat it all at once.
- 質問を一度に三つしないでください。 (しつもんをいちどにみっつしないでください。) - Please do not ask three questions at once.

The second request asks the listener to separate the questions, not to stop asking questions altogether.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," counts [度](../kanji/度.md), an occasion or time. The on-reading base 一度 (いちど) combines with the particle に to form this adverbial expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[同時](同時.md)に (どうじに) emphasizes things happening at the same time. 一度に can instead mean completing a batch in one sitting, without every part occurring at precisely the same instant. 少しずつ (すこしずつ), "a little at a time," provides a useful contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

[一度](一度.md) by itself means "once" in 一度行った, "went once." 一度に focuses on grouping: 一度に運ぶ (いちどにはこぶ), "carry everything in one trip." The extra に changes the practical question from how many occasions to how the work is grouped.
