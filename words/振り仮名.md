# 振り仮名 (ふりがな) - furigana; kana pronunciation guide

## Meaning

振り仮名 are kana indicating how kanji are read, usually printed above
the characters in horizontal writing or beside them in vertical writing.
The word itself is usually written **ふりがな**.

- 難しい漢字に振り仮名を付けてください。 (むずかしいかんじにふりがなをつけてください。) - Please add furigana to the difficult kanji.
- 振り仮名を見て、名前を読みました。 (ふりがなをみて、なまえをよみました。) - I read the name by looking at the furigana.

Use 漢字に振り仮名を付ける for adding a pronunciation guide to kanji.

## How the kanji combine

振り supplies ふり; [仮名](仮名.md) (かな) is the
kana writing-system term. Its beginning is voiced here: ふり + がな.
[名](../kanji/名.md) remains part of the contracted 仮名 unit;
do not invent freely reusable individual character readings from かな.
The wago label follows this native-word construction, not an all-on reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ルビ also names pronunciation annotations.
[読み](読み.md) (よみ) is the reading itself, not necessarily a printed guide.
[送り仮名](送り仮名.md) (おくりがな) are kana endings after kanji,
such as べる in 食べる (たべる), not the annotation above or beside them.

## Distinctive meaning

Furigana describes the kana's explanatory function, not a third kana syllabary.
[平仮名](平仮名.md) (ひらがな) and [片仮名](片仮名.md) (かたかな)
name scripts; kana used as a pronunciation guide have a different role
from kana used as ordinary spelling.
The source spelling 振り仮名 and usual ふりがな name the same device.
