# 厳しい (きびしい) - strict; harsh

## Meaning

厳しい is an i-adjective describing strict standards, severe treatment, or harsh conditions.

- この先生は宿題に厳しいです。 (このせんせいはしゅくだいにきびしいです。) - This teacher is strict about homework.
- 今年の冬は寒さが厳しいです。 (ことしのふゆはさむさがきびしいです。) - The cold is severe this winter.

に can identify what someone is strict about. Use 厳しい先生 directly before a noun; the adverbial form is 厳しく (きびしく).

## How the kanji combine

厳 represents the native adjective, followed by しい. Learn the whole reading きびしい; it is not a combination of on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

優しい (やさしい), "kind; gentle," can contrast with strict treatment, though a teacher may be both kind and strict. 難しい (むずかしい) focuses on difficulty.

## Distinctive meaning

厳しい is not limited to a person's character. It can describe demanding circumstances or severe weather, where English "strict" would be unnatural.
