# ボート - rowing boat; boat

## Meaning

ボート names a rowing boat and also appears with the broader meaning "boat," especially in compounds. It is a noun. Use ボートに乗る (ボートにのる), "get into or ride in a boat," and ボートをこぐ, "row a boat."

- 湖でボートを借りました。 (みずうみでボートをかりました。) - I rented a rowing boat at the lake.
- 二人でボートをこぎました。 (ふたりでボートをこぎました。) - The two of us rowed the boat.

に marks what you board or ride, whereas を marks the boat you row or rent.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in katakana with no kanji to divide. The long-vowel mark ー extends ボ's vowel. In モーターボート, "motorboat," the added element identifies a different means of propulsion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[船](船.md) (ふね) is a broader word for boats and ships. ボート is useful for the rowing-boat situation here but is not the automatic label for every large passenger vessel.

## Distinctive meaning

The community source specifically lists "rowing boat." That is a supported standalone use, not a rule that every compound ending in ボート must be rowed. Learn the noun with its activity and any modifier. ボートに乗る says you are aboard; it does not necessarily say that you personally do the rowing.
