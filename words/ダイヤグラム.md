# ダイヤグラム - diagram; transport operating chart

## Meaning

ダイヤグラム is a diagram: a graphic representation of relationships,
structure or movement. ダイアグラム is another spelling recognized in
the same JMdict family.

- 関係をダイヤグラムで示しました。
  (かんけいをダイヤグラムでしめしました。) - I showed the relationships in a diagram.
- 列車のダイヤグラムを見ながら説明しました。
  (れっしゃのダイヤグラムをみながらせつめいしました。) - I explained while looking at the train operating chart.

Digital Daijisen also explicitly describes the transport use: a chart of
train or bus operations. A railway diagram can show time against distance
or stations. This is additional public-dictionary-backed teaching, not an
invented second sense in the pinned one-sense JMdict entry.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword comes from English diagram.
JMdict lists both ダイアグラム and ダイヤグラム without a rarity flag.
That recognizes the spellings; it does not measure their relative frequency.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[図](図.md) (ず) and [図表](図表.md) (ずひょう) overlap the graphic/chart
side. [表](表.md) primarily teaches おもて, but its article also explicitly
distinguishes ひょう, table or chart.
[ダイヤ](ダイヤ.md) can abbreviate the transport word; it also has a
separate diamond meaning.

[予定](予定.md) (よてい) and [計画](計画.md) (けいかく) concern schedules
or plans more generally. A graphic operating chart is not every personal
plan, even though English schedule can describe the transport use.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the topic: a scientific diagram need not be a timetable, and a train
operating chart is more specific than an unspecified plan.
ダイヤグラム does not mean the gemstone [ダイヤモンド](ダイヤモンド.md).
The shared beginning of these loanwords is not proof of shared meaning.
