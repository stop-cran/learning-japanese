# 銀行 (ぎんこう) — bank

## Meaning

銀行 is a bank: 銀行に行く (go to the bank), 銀行でお金をおろす (withdraw money at the bank), 銀行員 ぎんこういん (bank clerk), 銀行口座 ぎんこうこうざ (bank account).

## How the kanji combine

銀 (silver) plus [行](../kanji/行.md) (here "business house, firm", Chinese háng, as also in 洋行 ようこう, a foreign trading firm; in 旅行 and 発行 the 行 means "go" or "carry out"): "silver firm". According to the Bank of Japan (https://www.boj.or.jp/about/education/oshiete/history/j09.htm), the word was coined in 1872 for the national banks, from 金行 and 銀行 chosen partly for sound (euphony), with 行 being the Chinese word for a shop. A link to the silver standard is sometimes suggested but is not established here. 銀行 is in on readings (ギン + コウ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 郵便局 ゆうびんきょく (post office, also offers banking), 信用金庫 しんようきんこ (credit union), ATM.
- Related kanji sense: 行 as "business house" in 洋行 ようこう.

## Distinctive meaning

銀行 reads ぎんこう, never ぎんぎょう: 行 as ギョウ appears in words such as 一行 いちぎょう ("one line"; いっこう is a different word, "a party") and 行事 ぎょうじ (event). It is a good example of 行 with the meaning "business house".
