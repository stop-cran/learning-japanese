# 銀行 (ぎんこう) — bank

## Meaning

銀行 is a bank: 銀行に行く (go to the bank), 銀行でお金をおろす (withdraw money at the bank), 銀行員 ぎんこういん (bank clerk), 銀行口座 ぎんこうこうざ (bank account).

## How the kanji combine

銀 (silver) plus [行](../kanji/行.md) (only in this word "business house, firm", Chinese háng; in 旅行 and 発行 the 行 means "go" or "carry out"): "silver firm". The word is usually explained as a Meiji-era coinage tied to the silver standard and the Chinese word 行 for a shop or trading house (compare 洋行 ようこう, a foreign trading firm). 銀行 is in on readings (ギン + コウ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 郵便局 ゆうびんきょく (post office, also offers banking), 信用金庫 しんようきんこ (credit union), ATM.
- Related kanji sense: 行 as "business house" in 洋行 ようこう.

## Distinctive meaning

銀行 reads ぎんこう, never ぎんぎょう: 行 as ギョウ is for "line" and "event" (一行, 行事). It is a good example of 行 with the meaning "business".
