# 捕る (とる) - catch; capture

## Meaning

捕る means to catch or capture, for example an animal, fish,
insect, or a baseball. It is a transitive godan verb; what is
caught takes を.

- 川で魚を捕りました。 (かわでさかなをとりました。) - I caught fish in the river.
- 外野手がボールを捕りました。 (がいやしゅがボールをとりました。) - The outfielder caught the ball.

Forms include 捕ります (とります), 捕って (とって), and
捕らない (とらない). The second example shows why this
spelling cannot be defined as catching only living things.

## How the kanji combine

[捕](../kanji/捕.md) contributes catching or capturing, using
the native reading と before る. The same character appears in
[捕える](捕える.md) (とらえる) and
[捕まえる](捕まえる.md) (つかまえる), but those are not
conjugated forms of 捕る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[釣る](釣る.md) (つる) emphasizes catching fish by angling;
捕る does not specify that method.
[受ける](受ける.md) (うける) also has a ball-catching use.
[採る](採る.md) (とる) can overlap when collecting insects or
specimens. These are overlapping uses, not exclusive compartments.

## Distinctive meaning

捕った reports a successful catch in these examples.
Trying to catch a fish does not by itself establish 魚を捕った.
Do not confuse the spelling with [撮る](撮る.md), "take a
photograph," merely because both are read とる.
