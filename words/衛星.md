# 衛星 (えいせい) - satellite

## Meaning

衛星 is a noun meaning "satellite." It can describe a natural satellite, such as a moon, or an artificial satellite. 人工衛星 (じんこうえいせい) makes the artificial meaning explicit; 衛星 alone can abbreviate it when the context is clear.

- 月は地球の衛星です。 (つきはちきゅうのえいせいです。) - The Moon is a satellite of Earth.
- この衛星は天気を観測しています。 (このえいせいはてんきをかんそくしています。) - This satellite is observing the weather.

Use Xの衛星 for "a satellite of X." In the second example, the observing equipment makes the artificial sense clear.

## How the kanji combine

衛 has guarding or protecting associations; 星 means a star or celestial body. Learn the compound as the astronomical word for a satellite, not as a claim that it literally protects a planet. The on readings are エイ + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[月](月.md) (つき) names our Moon and can also mean a month. 衛星 names a category rather than that one object.

[星](星.md) (ほし) is a broader everyday word for stars and visible celestial points. It is not an interchangeable technical label.

## Distinctive meaning

衛星 identifies an orbiting object's relationship, not its brightness or size. In technology contexts, 衛星放送 (えいせいほうそう), "satellite broadcasting," uses the artificial-satellite sense. A planet is a contrasting astronomical category, not an antonym.
