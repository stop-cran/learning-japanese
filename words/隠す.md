# 隠す (かくす) - hide; conceal

## Meaning

隠す is a transitive godan verb: someone hides or conceals something. Mark the hidden thing with **を** and a hiding place with **に**. It works for physical objects and for information or feelings.

- 子供が箱の中に手紙を隠しました。 (こどもがはこのなかにてがみをかくしました。) - The child hid the letter in a box.
- 彼女は驚きを隠せませんでした。 (かのじょはおどろきをかくせませんでした。) - She could not hide her surprise.

The past is 隠した (かくした); the negative is 隠さない (かくさない). 隠せない is the negative potential, "cannot conceal," not simply "does not conceal."

## How the kanji combine

隠 carries the hiding idea and is read かく- in this native verb. The kana ending す changes with conjugation. There is no second kanji component.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[隠れる](隠れる.md) (かくれる) is intransitive: 子供が隠れる, "the child hides." With 隠す, the child might instead hide an object. [見せる](見せる.md) (みせる), "show," is a useful opposite when visibility is intended.

## Distinctive meaning

隠す need not imply theft or wrongdoing: someone can conceal a birthday present. For information, 事実を隠す (じじつをかくす) means "conceal the facts." The physical and informational uses share keeping something from being seen or known, rather than merely putting it away neatly.
