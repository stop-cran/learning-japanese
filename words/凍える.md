# 凍える (こごえる) - be chilled; become numb with cold

## Meaning

凍える describes a person or body part becoming intensely chilled
or numb with cold. It is an intransitive ichidan verb:
凍えます, 凍えた, 凍えて, 凍えない.
The body or person is the subject; で can identify the cause.

- 寒さで手が凍えました。
  (さむさでてがこごえました。) - My hands became numb with cold.
- 外で長く待っていたので、凍えそうです。
  (そとでながくまっていたので、こごえそうです。) - I feel as though I'm freezing after waiting outside so long.

凍えそう expresses an impending state, not a claim that a body
has already turned into ice.

## How the kanji combine

[凍](../kanji/凍.md) concerns freezing. Here its native stem is
こご, followed by える. The complete verb is こごえる;
do not substitute the こお pronunciation of 凍る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[凍る](凍る.md) (こおる) describes freezing, as in 水が凍る
(みずがこおる), "water freezes." [冷える](冷える.md) (ひえる)
more broadly describes cooling or becoming cold, including body parts.
温まる (あたたまる), "get warm," is a practical reverse change.

## Distinctive meaning

English "I'm freezing" often describes an intensely cold person.
凍える fits that bodily experience; it does not necessarily assert
literal solidification. For an icy road or frozen water, learn
凍る instead. Merely becoming cooler need not reach the intensity
suggested by 凍える.
