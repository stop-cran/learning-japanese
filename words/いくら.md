# いくら -- how much

## Meaning

いくら asks about an amount, especially a price or cost. A useful shopping pattern is item は + いくらですか.

- このかばんはいくらですか。 (このかばんはいくらですか。) -- How much is this bag?
- 全部でいくらになりますか。 (ぜんぶでいくらになりますか。) -- How much does it come to altogether?

The answer may be a money amount such as 二千円 (にせんえん), "two thousand yen." いくら does not specify a currency by itself.

## How the kanji combine

This word is usually written in hiragana. The dictionary also lists 幾ら, but this card keeps the ordinary kana spelling. There are no written kanji components to combine.

## Synonyms and antonyms

何円 (なんえん) asks specifically how many [yen](円.md). いくつ asks how many things, so it is not a replacement for いくら when asking a price.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the question word from イクラ, salmon roe. They have similar sounds but different meanings and dictionary identities. Here いくら requests an amount; it does not name a food.
