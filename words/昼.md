# 昼 (ひる) — daytime, midday

## Meaning

昼 means daytime or the middle of the day. In conversation it can also stand for lunch.

- 昼は家にいます。 (ひるはいえにいます。) — I am at home during the day.
- 昼ご飯を食べました。 (ひるごはんをたべました。) — I ate lunch.

## How the kanji combine

[昼](../kanji/昼.md) represents the whole native word ひる, with no okurigana. In 昼ご飯 ひるごはん, 昼 specifies the midday meal; it retains its native reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 昼間 ひるま emphasizes daytime rather than specifically the lunch hour.
- 正午 しょうご is noon, exactly 12:00; 昼 can be less precise.
- [夜](夜.md) よる, "night," is the usual contrast when 昼 means daytime.

## Distinctive meaning

Context decides whether 昼 means the daytime period, around noon, or lunch. お昼 おひる is a common conversational form and retains that ambiguity. It is not a word for a whole calendar day including the night.
