# 地下鉄 (ちかてつ) — subway; metro

## Meaning

地下鉄 means a **subway, metro, or underground railway**. It can refer to the railway service or, in context, the train used for a journey.

地下鉄で会社に行きます。 (ちかてつでかいしゃにいきます。) — I go to work by subway.

地下鉄に乗る (ちかてつにのる) means “take/board the subway.” Use で for the means of transport and に with 乗る.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md)[下](../kanji/下.md), “underground,” combines with 鉄, “iron,” as an abbreviation of 地下鉄道 (ちかてつどう), “underground railway.” The on readings are チ + カ + テツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

電車 (でんしゃ) means an electric train and includes many subway trains, but is not limited to underground services. バス is another means of public transport, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The name identifies a railway system or service, not a guarantee that every part of the ride is underground. An above-ground section of a subway route does not require a different everyday word for the service.
