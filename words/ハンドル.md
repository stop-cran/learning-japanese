# ハンドル - handle; steering wheel

## Meaning

ハンドル is a handle or grip used to operate something.
In a car it is the steering wheel; on a bicycle it can mean the handlebars.
It is a noun, not the English verb "handle."

- 運転中はハンドルから手を離さないでください。 (うんてんちゅうはハンドルからてをはなさないでください。) - Please do not take your hands off the steering wheel while driving.
- 自転車のハンドルの高さを調節しました。 (じてんしゃのハンドルのたかさをちょうせつしました。) - I adjusted the height of the bicycle's handlebars.

ハンドルを切る (ハンドルをきる) means to turn the steering wheel,
not cut it. The dictionary also records an Internet handle or username,
related to ハンドルネーム.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword from English "handle" is written in katakana.
There is no kanji compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[柄](柄.md), read え for a handle, overlaps with handles on tools or
umbrellas. A car's [車輪](車輪.md) (しゃりん) is a vehicle wheel,
not its steering control; [タイヤ](タイヤ.md) is the tire.
An online handle is a kind of [名前](名前.md) (なまえ), not a vehicle part.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer that ハンドル always means steering wheel, or translate every
English use of "handle" with it. Identify the object and setting:
a machine's grip, a door handle, handlebars, and an online name select
different uses. English "handle a problem" requires a suitable Japanese
verb, not simply ハンドルする.
