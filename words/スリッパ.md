# スリッパ (スリッパ) - slippers

## Meaning

スリッパ means slippers, typically footwear slipped on indoors.

- 玄関でスリッパを履きました。 (げんかんでスリッパをはきました。) - I put on slippers at the entrance.

Use 履く (はく) for putting them on and 脱ぐ (ぬぐ) for taking them off. スリッパを脱いでください (スリッパをぬいでください) means "Please take off your slippers."

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword corresponds to English "slipper." Small ッ marks a pause before パ; the ordinary spelling is スリッパ, without a final long-vowel mark.

## Synonyms and antonyms

靴 (くつ) means shoes more generally. サンダル are sandals; their shape may resemble some slippers, but the words do not have identical uses.

## Distinctive meaning

Indoor footwear rules depend on the place. Slippers are commonly removed before stepping onto tatami, and designated toilet slippers should remain in that area. The word does not itself mean that the footwear may be worn everywhere indoors.
