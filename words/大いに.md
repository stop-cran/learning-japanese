# 大いに (おおいに) - greatly; a great deal

## Meaning

大いに is an adverb indicating a large degree: "greatly," "very much," or
"a great deal." It modifies the following action or evaluation without an
extra に.

- その説明は大いに役立ちました。 (そのせつめいはおおいにやくだちました。) - That explanation helped a great deal.
- 今日は大いに楽しんでください。 (きょうはおおいにたのしんでください。) - Please enjoy yourselves to the full today.

The second example is encouragement to do something wholeheartedly, not an
instruction to count a large number of enjoyable things.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes largeness, here applied to degree rather than
physical size. The native word is read おおいに as a whole. Do not confuse its
spelling with 多い (おおい), "numerous."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とても](とても.md), "very," is another degree adverb, but the words do not fit
every construction equally. [たくさん](たくさん.md) emphasizes quantity.
大いに役立つ concerns how helpful something is, not how many things there are.

## Distinctive meaning

大いに suits emphatic evaluations and encouragement. The adjective or verb
supplies the positive or negative judgment: 大いに問題がある
(おおいにもんだいがある) means there is a serious problem, not praise.
Treat the final に as part of the learned adverb, rather than adding another
adverb-forming ending.
