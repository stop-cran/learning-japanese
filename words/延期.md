# 延期 (えんき) - postponement

## Meaning

延期 means postponement: moving a planned event or action to a later time.
延期する is transitive. Mark the event with を and the new date with に.

- 雨のため、試合を来週に延期しました。 (あめのため、しあいをらいしゅうにえんきしました。) - We postponed the match until next week because of rain.
- 会議は延期になりました。 (かいぎはえんきになりました。) - The meeting has been postponed.

延期になる presents the resulting schedule change without naming who made it.
延期された, "was postponed," is another way to describe the event.

## How the kanji combine

延 contributes extending or putting off, and [期](../kanji/期.md) contributes a
scheduled time or period. Together they concern moving the appointed time later.
The on readings are エン + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中止](中止.md) (ちゅうし) means stopping or canceling; it does not itself promise
a later date. 延長 (えんちょう) is extension, such as making a meeting last longer.
[遅れる](遅れる.md) (おくれる) describes being late or delayed without necessarily
expressing an arranged change of date.

## Distinctive meaning

Postponement leaves the event planned for later, although the new date need not
already be known. 延期 does not imply a specific number of days. Compare moving
tomorrow's meeting to Friday with extending tomorrow's meeting by thirty minutes:
the first concerns 延期, the second 延長.
