# 種 (たね) - seed; source; material

## Meaning

種, read たね, is a noun for a seed, pip, or fruit stone. It also names a source of something or material for a story or discussion.

- 庭に花の種をまきました。 (にわにはなのたねをまきました。) - I sowed flower seeds in the garden.
- その旅行はいい話の種になりました。 (そのりょこうはいいはなしのたねになりました。) - That trip provided good material for conversation.

心配の種 (しんぱいのたね), "a source of worry," uses the cause/source sense rather than referring to a plant.

## How the kanji combine

[種](../kanji/種.md) supplies the whole native noun たね. There are no other written components to combine. In [種類](種類.md) (しゅるい), "kind; variety," the same character has the on reading シュ and a different construction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[原因](原因.md) (げんいん) means cause. 種 in 心配の種 presents something as the source from which concern develops, rather than being an unrestricted replacement for 原因.

[実](実.md) (み), "fruit," is related to seeds but not identical: a fruit may contain seeds.

## Distinctive meaning

The following or preceding noun guides the sense. 花の種 concerns plants; 話の種 concerns conversational material. The material meaning is not a universal translation for every physical substance. Learn these expressions as units rather than assuming all English uses of "seed," "source," or "material" share one Japanese equivalent.
