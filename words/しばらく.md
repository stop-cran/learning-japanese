# しばらく (しばらく) - for a while

## Meaning

しばらく means "for a little while," "for some time," or "for the time being." It gives an approximate interval rather than a fixed number of minutes.

- ここでしばらく待ってください。 (ここでしばらくまってください。) - Please wait here for a little while.
- しばらく日本に住む予定です。 (しばらくにほんにすむよていです。) - I plan to live in Japan for a while.

しばらくして means "after a while." The interval may be short in a request to wait but considerably longer in a statement about living somewhere.

## How the kanji combine

The normal everyday spelling is kana. 暫く is an alternative kanji spelling; the word is a native adverb, not a compound assembled from multiple character meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

少しの間 (すこしのあいだ) explicitly means "for a short time." ずっと can emphasize a much longer or continuous stretch, depending on context.

## Distinctive meaning

しばらく会っていません (しばらくあっていません) means "I haven't seen them for a while." The word does not guarantee that the interval feels short. In a greeting such as しばらくですね, it acknowledges that some time has passed since meeting.
