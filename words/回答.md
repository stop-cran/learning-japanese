# 回答 (かいとう) - reply; response

## Meaning

回答 is an answer or response to a question, inquiry or request. It can name the response itself or form 回答する, "respond; answer."

- アンケートに回答しました。 (アンケートにかいとうしました。) - I responded to the questionnaire.
- 会社から回答がありました。 (かいしゃからかいとうがありました。) - There was a reply from the company.

The first uses に for what is being answered. A 回答 may be an opinion, refusal or explanation; it need not solve an exercise.

## How the kanji combine

[回](../kanji/回.md) suggests returning something and [答](../kanji/答.md) an answer. Think of an answer sent back: カイ + トウ. This is a meaning aid, not a claim about the word's historical formation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[返事](返事.md) (へんじ) is a reply, including everyday replies to calls and messages. [答える](答える.md) (こたえる) is a native verb for answering. [解答](解答.md), also かいとう, is especially useful for an answer obtained by solving a problem.

Compare 質問への回答 (しつもんへのかいとう), "a response to a question," and 問題の解答 (もんだいのかいとう), "the solution to a problem." This is a useful contrast, not a ban on overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

回答 fits questionnaires and institutional or business inquiries, but is not restricted to writing or business. Do not choose the kanji from the sound かいとう alone. Asking someone for a response and marking the solution to a mathematics exercise place different emphasis on the answer.
