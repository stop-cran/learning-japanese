# 脱線 (だっせん) - derailment; digression

## Meaning

脱線 is derailment, and also a digression or deviation from the
main subject or course. 脱線する is intransitive: the train or
conversation that goes off course is the subject.

- 列車が脱線しました。
  (れっしゃがだっせんしました。) - The train derailed.
- 話が脱線したので、本題に戻しましょう。
  (はなしがだっせんしたので、ほんだいにもどしましょう。) - The conversation has gone off topic, so let's bring it back to the main subject.

脱線しない, "not digress/derail," and 脱線した話,
"a digression; a discussion that has gone off topic," are useful forms.

## How the kanji combine

脱 contributes leaving or escaping, and [線](../kanji/線.md)
contributes a line or track: leaving the track supplies a useful
image for both railway derailment and conversational digression.
ダツ + セン becomes だっせん, with small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[逸れる](逸れる.md) (それる) overlaps in 話がそれる,
"the conversation strays." 戻る (もどる), "return," contrasts
when the discussion returns to its main subject.

## Distinctive meaning

The figurative use does not require a train or any physical track.
Conversely, talking about a railway line is not automatically 脱線:
the wheels must leave the rails for the literal sense. The noun
names the departure from course, not the track itself.
