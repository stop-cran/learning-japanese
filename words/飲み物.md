# 飲み物 (のみもの) — a drink

## Meaning

飲み物 is a drink or beverage: 飲み物は何がいいですか (what would you like to drink?), 冷たい飲み物 (a cold drink), 飲み物を買う (buy a drink).

## How the kanji combine

[飲](../kanji/飲.md) in its noun form 飲み (from 飲む) plus 物 (もの, thing): "a thing to drink", a verb-stem plus noun compound.
Its counterpart is 食べ物 たべもの (food). In writing, okurigana may be dropped: 飲物 is seen on signs and menus.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: 食べ物 たべもの (food).
- Formal: 飲料 いんりょう (beverages, in the product category 清涼飲料水 せいりょういんりょうすい, soft drinks).
- Related verb: [飲む](飲む.md) のむ.

## Distinctive meaning

飲み物 is everyday and covers any drink. 飲料 is a business or labelling term, and ドリンク (from English) is the menu and ordering word.
