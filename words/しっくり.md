# しっくり — fitting just right; feeling natural

## Meaning

しっくり describes something that fits well, feels suitable, or seems to make sense in context.

- この靴は幅が広すぎず、足にしっくりくる。
  — These shoes fit my feet just right—not too wide.
- いくつも案を比べたが、この説明が一番しっくりくる。
  — I compared several ideas, but this explanation feels the most fitting.

## Synonyms and antonyms

しっくり emphasizes a good fit or a sense of suitability. It does not merely mean that something
is physically close or tightly packed.

## Distinctive meaning

The common pattern しっくりくる describes a sense of fitting or feeling right; the context may be
physical or conceptual.
