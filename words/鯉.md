# 鯉 (こい) — carp

## Meaning

鯉 means **carp**, a freshwater fish. It includes the colorful ornamental varieties, but does not mean only those varieties.

- 池に鯉がいます。 (いけにこいがいます。) — There are carp in the pond.
- 鯉が泳いでいます。 (こいがおよいでいます。) — A carp is swimming.

In the first sentence, に marks the location and が identifies what is there. In the second, [泳ぐ](泳ぐ.md) およぐ, swim, appears as 泳いでいます to describe ongoing movement.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [鯉](../kanji/鯉.md) writes the whole native noun こい; there is no compound to split or kana ending to add. **こい** and **コイ** are also spellings of the animal name.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[魚](魚.md) さかな, fish, is a broader category. 錦鯉 (にしきごい) names colored ornamental carp, a narrower term rather than a replacement for every 鯉.

[池](池.md) いけ, pond, is a possible location, not another name for the fish. There is no useful direct antonym for a carp.

## Distinctive meaning

The homophone [恋](恋.md) こい means romantic love. The shared reading does not make the written words interchangeable.

**鯉のぼり** (こいのぼり) names carp-shaped streamers traditionally flown for Children's Day in May, not living fish. Learn its complete meaning rather than translating every 鯉-containing expression as an animal.

This article practices the fish noun **こい**. The character's on-reading リ in 養鯉 (ようり), carp breeding, does not make り an alternative answer for this word.
