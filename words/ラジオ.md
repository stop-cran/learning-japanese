# ラジオ (ラジオ) — radio

## Meaning

ラジオ means **radio**, either the broadcasting medium or a radio receiver. The verb and context distinguish the device from what you listen to.

- 朝、ラジオを聞きます。 (あさ、らじおをききます。) — I listen to the radio in the morning.
- 小さいラジオを買いました。 (ちいさいらじおをかいました。) — I bought a small radio.

Use 聞く (きく) for listening, not 見る (みる), “watch.”

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana with no kanji components. Learn the Japanese pronunciation ラジオ rather than reproducing the English vowel pattern. ラジオ番組 (らじおばんぐみ) means “radio program.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

テレビ is television, with pictures as well as sound. 放送 (ほうそう), “broadcasting/broadcast,” is broader and can refer to television or radio. These are related media terms, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

ラジオを聞く does not necessarily identify a physical receiver: people can listen to radio programming through other devices. Conversely, ラジオを買う normally concerns the equipment. The same noun covers both uses without changing its spelling.
