# 疑わしい (うたがわしい) — doubtful; questionable

## Meaning

疑わしい describes something whose truth, reliability, or effectiveness is open to doubt.
It can also describe a person who arouses suspicion.

- その説明は疑わしいです。 (その せつめいは うたがわしいです。) — That explanation is questionable.
- 彼が本当に知っていたかどうかは疑わしいです。  
  (かれが ほんとうに しっていたかどうかは うたがわしいです。)  
  — It is doubtful whether he really knew.

It is an i-adjective: 疑わしくない (うたがわしくない), "not doubtful."

## How the kanji combine

[疑](../kanji/疑.md) supplies うたが, also seen in [疑う](疑う.md) うたがう, "doubt; suspect."
The written kana ending わしい completes the adjective; this is a native word, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怪しい](怪しい.md) overlaps in suspicion and doubt, but also covers eerie or ominous impressions.
[いかがわしい](いかがわしい.md) more readily suggests disreputability or impropriety.
確か (たしか), "certain; reliable," gives a useful contrast for information.

## Distinctive meaning

疑わしい often sounds more analytical than 怪しい, but it does not require proof or a formal
investigation. Conversely, it is not the default word for a noise that merely sounds strange:
変な音 (へんなおと) or 怪しい音 (あやしいおと) is more natural for that impression.

For 疑わしい compared with 怪しい and いかがわしい, see
[strange, suspicious, and rare](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
