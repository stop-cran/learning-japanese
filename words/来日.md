# 来日 (らいにち) - coming to Japan; visit to Japan

## Meaning

来日 means coming to or visiting Japan. It is a noun and an intransitive
する verb: 来日する, 来日した. The person coming is marked with が or は.

- その歌手は来月来日します。 (そのかしゅはらいげつらいにちします。) - That singer is coming to Japan next month.
- 初めての来日は十年前でした。 (はじめてのらいにちはじゅうねんまえでした。) - My first visit to Japan was ten years ago.

It appears in accounts of visitors, performers and other people arriving in Japan.
The destination is built into the word; it is not a general verb for visiting any country.

## How the kanji combine

[来](../kanji/来.md) contributes coming, and [日](../kanji/日.md) stands
for Japan here, not a calendar day. The on readings are ライ + ニチ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[来る](来る.md) (くる) is the general verb "come."
[訪問](訪問.md) (ほうもん) means a visit and does not specify Japan.
訪日 (ほうにち) also means a visit to Japan.

## Distinctive meaning

来日 names the arrival or visit, not a person's entire stay.
The reading here is らいにち. A separate dictionary word 来日 (らいじつ),
"a future day," is not an alternative reading to insert into these examples.
