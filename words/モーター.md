# モーター - motor; engine

## Meaning

モーター is a noun for a motor or engine that supplies mechanical power.
An electric motor is a particularly useful everyday example.

- このおもちゃは小さなモーターで動きます。 (このおもちゃはちいさなモーターでうごきます。) - This toy runs on a small motor.
- モーターが止まったので、機械を調べました。 (モーターがとまったので、きかいをしらべました。) - The motor stopped, so I checked the machine.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word comes from English motor. Both long-vowel marks
belong to モーター. The variant モータ is also recorded.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[エンジン](エンジン.md) overlaps with engine; in everyday descriptions
it often refers to an internal-combustion engine, whereas モーター
often brings an electric motor to mind. [機関](機関.md) (きかん)
also has an engine sense, besides its institution and system senses.

## Distinctive meaning

The electric-motor association is not an absolute definition:
the dictionaries also recognize a prime mover or engine more generally.
A [機械](機械.md) (きかい), "machine," or [装置](装置.md)
(そうち), "device," may contain a motor without the entire machine
being called a motor.
