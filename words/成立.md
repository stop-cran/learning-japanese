# 成立 (せいりつ) - formation; conclusion; validity

## Meaning

成立 is the formation or establishment of something, the conclusion or completion
of an agreement or other process, and the validity of an argument.
成立する is intransitive: what is established or holds takes が.

- 話し合いの結果、契約が成立しました。 (はなしあいのけっか、けいやくがせいりつしました。) - As a result of the discussions, an agreement was concluded.
- その議論は成立しません。 (そのぎろんはせいりつしません。) - That argument does not hold.

会社が成立する concerns a company's formation; 法律が成立する (ほうりつがせいりつする)
can concern a law's passage or enactment. Translate according to what has been established.

## How the kanji combine

[成](../kanji/成.md) contributes coming into being, and [立](../kanji/立.md)
contributes standing or becoming established. Together they suggest something
coming to stand as established. Read セイ + リツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[完成](完成.md) (かんせい) focuses on completion; [承認](承認.md) (しょうにん)
on approval; [有効](有効.md) (ゆうこう) on effectiveness or validity.
Which comparison is relevant depends on the object: a contract being concluded
is not the same event as a building being finished.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use を as though 成立する itself meant make something:
契約が成立する describes the agreement being concluded.
A law's 成立 does not by itself specify when it comes into force.
That separate timing needs the relevant 施行 (しこう) or 発効 (はっこう) context.
