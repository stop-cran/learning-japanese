# 売れる (うれる) - sell; sell well

## Meaning

売れる is an intransitive ichidan verb: something sells or finds a buyer. The item takes が. よく売れる means "sell well"; 売れない means "does not sell."

- この本はよく売れています。 (このほんはよくうれています。) - This book is selling well.
- 古い自転車が昨日売れました。 (ふるいじてんしゃがきのううれました。) - My old bicycle sold yesterday.

The forms are 売れます, 売れた, and 売れない. 売れている can describe ongoing commercial success. With a performer, 売れている歌手 (うれているかしゅ) means "a successful, popular singer."

## How the kanji combine

[売](../kanji/売.md) contributes selling; れる completes the native verb うれる. It is related to [売る](売る.md) (うる), "sell something," but its subject is the thing that sells.

## Synonyms and antonyms

Compare 店が本を売る (みせがほんをうる), "the shop sells books," with 本が売れる, "books sell." [買う](買う.md) (かう), "buy," describes the buyer's action, not a grammatical opposite of 売れる. [人気](人気.md) (にんき), "popularity," overlaps with the successful-performer use.

## Distinctive meaning

The form 売れる can also be the potential of 売る, "can sell." Context decides: ここで売れる can mean "can sell it here." Do not equate every 売れる with a passive: the passive of 売る is 売られる (うられる). The examples above focus on sales results rather than naming a seller.
