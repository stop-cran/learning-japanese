# 区別 (くべつ) - distinction; differentiation

## Meaning

区別 is the act or result of distinguishing things. As a transitive する verb, it appears in AとBを区別する, "distinguish A and B," or AをBと区別する, "distinguish A from B."

- この二つの音を区別できますか。 (このふたつのおとをくべつできますか。) - Can you tell these two sounds apart?
- 仕事と私生活を区別しています。 (しごととしせいかつをくべつしています。) - I keep my work and private life separate.

区別がつかない (くべつがつかない) means "cannot tell the difference." The subject is the distinction, rather than a person performing an action.

## How the kanji combine

区 contributes dividing into sections, and [別](../kanji/別.md) contributes separation or difference. The related ideas reinforce treating things as distinct rather than merging them.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[違い](違い.md) (ちがい) names a difference. 区別 emphasizes recognizing or maintaining that difference. [分ける](分ける.md) can involve actually dividing things into groups. 混同する (こんどうする), "confuse/conflate," is a useful contrasting action.

## Distinctive meaning

English dictionaries may gloss 区別 as "discrimination" in the sense of telling things apart. It does not inherently mean unfair treatment. [差別](差別.md) (さべつ) is the important comparison for discriminatory treatment. The context and purpose of separating categories still matter; the word 区別 alone does not establish that a distinction is justified.
