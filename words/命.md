# 命 (いのち) — life

## Meaning

命 is life as the state or force of being alive: something precious that can be saved or lost.
It is a noun, not the verb "live."

- 医者が子供の命を救いました。 (いしゃがこどものいのちをすくいました。) — The doctor saved the child's life.
- 命を大切にしてください。 (いのちをたいせつにしてください。) — Please cherish life.

In 子供の命, の identifies whose life is meant; を marks it as the object of 救う (すくう), "save."

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native Japanese word.
[命](../kanji/命.md) carries the complete reading いのち; there is no separate reading for each syllable.
Its other meaning, "command," appears in compounds such as 命令 (めいれい), not in the life meaning taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[生活](生活.md) (せいかつ) is life in the sense of living, daily activities, or livelihood.
Saving someone's 命 and improving their 生活 concern different things.
寿命 (じゅみょう) is lifespan, the length of a life; it can also describe the useful life of a device.
Neither word is a drop-in replacement for 命 in every phrase.

## Distinctive meaning

The English word "life" is broader than this one Japanese noun.
Use the context to distinguish being alive from a way of living or the duration of a life.
生命 (せいめい) is another word for "life; existence."
JMdict also notes occasional use of the spelling 生命 for いのち; do not assume that reading whenever you see 生命.
