# ひくひく — twitching in small movements

## Meaning

ひくひく describes a small repeated twitch, such as a nose or muscle moving involuntarily.

- 頬がひくひく動いている。
  — His cheek is twitching.
- ウサギはにおいを嗅ぐように、鼻をひくひくさせた。
  — The rabbit twitched its nose as if smelling something.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ひくひく suggests small repeated movements, not a large shake or deliberate gesture.

## Distinctive meaning

It often describes a body part moving in a subtle, involuntary way.
