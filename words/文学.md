# 文学 (ぶんがく) - literature

## Meaning

文学 means literature: writing considered as an artistic or cultural field. It can also identify literature as an academic subject.

大学で日本文学を勉強しています。 (だいがくでにほんぶんがくをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying Japanese literature at university.

文学に興味があります。 (ぶんがくにきょうみがあります。) - I am interested in literature.

The second example uses に to mark the subject of interest.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md), "writing; text," combines with [学](../kanji/学.md), "learning; study." The on readings ブン and ガク form ぶんがく. The word can name literary works collectively as well as their study.

## Synonyms and antonyms

小説 (しょうせつ), "novel; fiction," is one kind of literary writing. 詩 (し) means poetry. 文章 (ぶんしょう) means a piece of writing or prose more generally, without necessarily classifying it as literature.

## Distinctive meaning

文学 is broader than a single book or genre, but not every written instruction or shopping list is normally called 文学. Use の before a following noun: 文学の授業 (ぶんがくのじゅぎょう), "a literature class."
