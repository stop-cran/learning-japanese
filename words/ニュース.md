# ニュース - news

## Meaning

ニュース means news: newly reported information or a news report or broadcast. It can concern public events or a person's news.

毎朝テレビでニュースを見ます。 (まいあさテレビでニュースをみます。) - I watch the news on television every morning.

何かいいニュースはありますか。 (なにかいいニュースはありますか。) - Is there any good news?

## How the kanji combine

This word comes from English "news" and is written in katakana. The small ュ combines with ニ, and ー lengthens the vowel: ニュース. The final sound is ス, not the English voiced ending.

## Synonyms and antonyms

知らせ (しらせ) is news or a notification, often about a particular matter. 報道 (ほうどう) emphasizes news reporting. 新聞 (しんぶん) is a newspaper, one medium that carries news rather than a synonym for all ニュース.

## Distinctive meaning

English "news" looks plural, but ニュース does not encode singular or plural number. Context can identify one report, a program, or news collectively. It is a noun for reports or information, not a replacement for the adjective 新しい (あたらしい), "new," before another noun.
