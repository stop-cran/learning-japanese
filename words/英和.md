# 英和 (えいわ) - English-Japanese; English-Japanese dictionary

## Meaning

英和 means "English-Japanese," especially in a dictionary label:
English entries are explained or translated in Japanese.
英和辞典 (えいわじてん) is an English-Japanese dictionary.

- 英和辞典でこの単語を調べました。 (えいわじてんでこのたんごをしらべました。) - I looked up this word in an English-Japanese dictionary.
- 英和を一冊買いました。 (えいわをいっさつかいました。) - I bought an English-Japanese dictionary.

The second sentence uses 英和 as an abbreviation for the dictionary itself.
It is a noun or compound element.

## How the kanji combine

[英](../kanji/英.md) identifies English and [和](../kanji/和.md)
identifies Japanese here. The ordered pair indicates the two languages
and, in the dictionary use, the English-to-Japanese direction.
The on readings are エイ + ワ; 和 does not mean "peace" here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[辞書](辞書.md) (じしょ) and [辞典](辞典.md) (じてん) are broader
words for a dictionary. The reverse-direction label is 和英 (わえい),
"Japanese-English"; compare 和英辞典 (わえいじてん).

## Distinctive meaning

Do not reverse the two languages because English sometimes writes
"Japanese and English" without indicating lookup direction. An 英和辞典
starts from English headwords. A bilingual publication can contain both
directions, but 英和 and 和英 still identify different lookup sections.
