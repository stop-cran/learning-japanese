# 観察 (かんさつ) — observation

## Meaning

観察 is observation: watching something with attention to its features, behavior, or changes.
Add する to make the verb 観察する (かんさつする), "observe."
The thing observed is marked with を.

- 子供たちは鳥を観察しています。 (こどもたちはとりをかんさつしています。) — The children are observing birds.
- 毎日、花の様子を観察します。 (まいにち、はなのようすをかんさつします。) — I observe how the flowers are doing every day.

## How the kanji combine

[観](../kanji/観.md) contributes looking or viewing, while [察](../kanji/察.md) contributes examining and finding things out.
These related meanings reinforce attention to what is being observed.
The on readings combine as カン + サツ; neither character is a separate verb in the compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[見る](見る.md) (みる), "see; look at," is broader.
鳥を見る (とりをみる) says that you look at birds; 鳥を観察する (とりをかんさつする) presents that looking as observation.
察する (さっする), "infer; sense," concerns drawing a judgment from clues and is not a replacement for every use of 観察する.
There is no single useful opposite that covers all observation.

## Distinctive meaning

観察 identifies the activity of watching closely, not a guarantee that the observer's conclusion is correct.
The flower example emphasizes following what can be noticed from day to day; it does not itself say that an experiment was performed.
