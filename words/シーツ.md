# シーツ - bed sheet

## Meaning

シーツ is a bed sheet, used with a bed or futon. It is a noun; 枚 (まい) can count individual sheets.

- ベッドのシーツを替えました。 (ベッドのシーツをかえました。) - I changed the bed sheet.
- 洗ったシーツを外に干しました。 (あらったシーツをそとにほしました。) - I hung the washed sheet outside to dry.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English sheet in the bedding sense. There are no kanji. Keep the long vowel シー and final ツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

敷布 (しきふ) is another word for a sheet laid underneath. [毛布](毛布.md) もうふ is a blanket, and [布団](布団.md) ふとん is bedding such as a futon mattress or quilt. These are related items, not interchangeable names.

## Distinctive meaning

シーツ is not a general word for every sheet of paper. シート can represent the separate English loans sheet and seat; the extra ツ in シーツ identifies this bedding word rather than a seat.
