# テスト - test

## Meaning

テスト is a test of knowledge, ability, performance, or quality. It can name a school test or a practical check of something.

- 明日、漢字のテストを受けます。 (あした、かんじのテストをうけます。) - I will take a kanji test tomorrow.
- 新しいアプリをテストしました。 (あたらしいアプリをテストしました。) - I tested the new app.

テストする means to test something; its object takes を. For being the examinee, テストを受ける is the clear pattern.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword from English "test" is written in katakana. There is no kanji compound to analyze. Japanese verb endings attach through する, not directly to テスト.

## Synonyms and antonyms

試験 (しけん) overlaps with an examination or test and often appears in formal exam names. 練習 (れんしゅう), "practice," is a related activity but does not necessarily evaluate performance.

## Distinctive meaning

テスト is not restricted to school grades. Testing equipment or software uses the same word, with the object and context identifying what is being evaluated. It also does not automatically mean a multiple-choice quiz.
