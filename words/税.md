# 税 (ぜい) - tax

## Meaning

税 is a tax. It functions as a noun and as an ending in names of particular taxes.
Useful constructions include 税を納める (ぜいをおさめる), "pay tax,"
and 税がかかる (ぜいがかかる), "tax applies; tax is charged."

- 商品の価格には税が含まれています。
  (しょうひんのかかくにはぜいがふくまれています。)
  - Tax is included in the price of the product.
- 所得税を納めました。
  (しょとくぜいをおさめました。) - I paid income tax.

In 所得税 (しょとくぜい), 所得 identifies income as the basis of the tax.
消費税 (しょうひぜい) is consumption tax. Learn the complete tax name when the distinction matters.

## How the kanji combine

税 is a single character with the on reading ゼイ.
There is no multi-kanji relationship within this word.
In longer names it contributes the tax meaning to what precedes it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

税金 (ぜいきん) is another noun for tax or tax money.
[料金](料金.md) (りょうきん) is a fee or charge, such as payment for a service;
it is not automatically a tax. [値段](値段.md) (ねだん) is a price.

## Distinctive meaning

Whether tax is included is a separate question from how much the item costs.
税込み (ぜいこみ) means tax included; 税抜き (ぜいぬき) means tax excluded.
These describe how a quoted price is presented, not two kinds of tax.
