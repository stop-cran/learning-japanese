# 慶弔 (けいちょう) — congratulations and condolences

## Meaning

慶弔 covers happy and sad occasions together. Digital Daijisen defines it as 結婚・出産などの喜ぶべきことと、死などの悲しむべきこと (けっこん・しゅっさんなどのよろこぶべきことと、しなどのかなしむべきこと), “joyful events such as marriage or childbirth, and sad events such as death,” and also お祝い事と不幸 (おいわいごととふこう), “celebrations and bereavements.” JMdict glosses it “congratulations and condolences.”

- 会社の規定で、慶弔の際には休暇が取れる。 (かいしゃのきていで、けいちょうのさいにはきゅうかがとれる。) — Under company rules, employees can take leave for celebrations and bereavements.
- この会社には、社員の結婚や家族の不幸に慶弔見舞金を出す制度がある。 (このかいしゃには、しゃいんのけっこんやかぞくのふこうにけいちょうみまいきんをだすせいどがある。) — This company has a scheme that pays an allowance when an employee marries or loses a family member.

## How the kanji combine

[慶](../kanji/慶.md) (けい) here means a happy occasion, sense 2 in [Digital Daijisen, 慶](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E6%85%B6), and [弔](../kanji/弔.md) (ちょう) means to mourn a death and offer condolences; [Digital Daijisen, 弔](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E5%BC%94) lists 慶弔 under that sense. The word pairs the two opposite kinds of occasion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お祝い](お祝い.md) (おいわい) is a celebration or a congratulatory gift, and [弔問](弔問.md) (ちょうもん) is a condolence call. 慶弔 names both kinds of occasion at once; JMdict lists 慶弔休暇 (けいちょうきゅうか), “special leave for births, marriages or deaths in the family,” and 慶弔見舞金 (けいちょうみまいきん), in the second example, “congratulatory and condolence allowances.”

## Distinctive meaning

The [実用日本語表現辞典](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E6%85%B6%E5%BC%94) entry explains 慶弔 as 慶事 (けいじ), happy events, and 弔事 (ちょうじ), sad events: weddings and births are 慶事, and funerals are 弔事. 弔事 is a different word from 弔辞 (ちょうじ), “memorial address; eulogy,” which has the same reading.
