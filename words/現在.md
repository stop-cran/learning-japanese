# 現在 (げんざい) - the present; currently

## Meaning

現在 is the present time, or "now; currently" when used adverbially. 現在の before a noun means "current": 現在の住所 (げんざいのじゅうしょ), "current address."

After a date or time, 現在 instead means **as of** that reference point.

- 現在、東京に住んでいます。 (げんざい、とうきょうにすんでいます。) - I currently live in Tokyo.
- 四月一日現在、会員は百人です。 (しがつついたちげんざい、かいいんはひゃくにんです。) - As of April 1, there are one hundred members.

The second example gives a snapshot of the membership on that date. It does not say that all those people have belonged since an earlier date.

## How the kanji combine

[現](../kanji/現.md) contributes what is present or actual, and [在](../kanji/在.md) contributes being or existing. Together they point to the state that exists now. The on readings are ゲン + ザイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[今](今.md) (いま) is the everyday word for "now." 現在 fits reports, status descriptions, and explanations of one's current situation. [過去](過去.md) (かこ), "the past," and [未来](未来.md) (みらい), "the future," are time-line contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

現在 alone does not express a duration "up to now." 今まで (いままで), "until now," explicitly supplies that endpoint. Likewise, 現在 is not the name of the modern historical era; [現代](現代.md) (げんだい) serves that broader purpose.
