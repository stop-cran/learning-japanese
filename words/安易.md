# 安易 (あんい) - easy; simplistic

## Meaning

安易 is a na-adjective and noun. It can mean easy or simple, but it also describes
an overly easy, careless or insufficiently considered approach. Use 安易な before
a noun and 安易に before an action.

- 安易な方法を選ぶのは、よくないこともあります。 (あんいなほうほうをえらぶのは、よくないこともあります。) - Choosing an easy way can sometimes be a bad idea.
- 安易に引き受けないでください。 (あんいにひきうけないでください。) - Please do not agree to take it on too lightly.

The criticism in the second example is inadequate consideration, not the mere fact
that agreeing is physically easy.

## How the kanji combine

[安](../kanji/安.md) can suggest ease or being at ease, and [易](../kanji/易.md)
contributes ease or lack of difficulty. Their related contributions form アン + イ.
The established word includes an evaluative "too easy" sense; the characters do
not establish that every use is praise.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[容易](容易.md) (ようい) and [易しい](易しい.md) (やさしい) overlap in ease.
[簡単](簡単.md) (かんたん) concerns simplicity or ease.
[慎重](慎重.md) (しんちょう), "cautious; careful," contrasts with a thoughtless decision.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "easy-going" is not a reliable instruction to praise a relaxed,
pleasant personality with 安易. In expressions such as 安易な考え (あんいなかんがえ),
"simplistic thinking," the speaker can be criticizing inadequate thought.
Context determines whether plain ease or that criticism is intended.
