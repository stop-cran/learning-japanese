# 両方 (りょうほう) — both

## Meaning

両方 includes both members of a pair, both sides, or both parties. The pair can be supplied by the conversation.
Before a noun, use 両方の: 両方の店 (りょうほうのみせ), "both shops."

- 赤いのと青いのを両方買いました。 (あかいのとあおいのをりょうほうかいました。) — I bought both the red one and the blue one.
- 両方の店が開いています。 (りょうほうのみせがあいています。) — Both shops are open.

## How the kanji combine

[両](../kanji/両.md), "both," qualifies [方](../kanji/方.md), understood here as a side or party.
The on readings combine as リョウ + ホウ. The word can refer to two choices as readily as to two physical sides.

## Synonyms and antonyms

片方 (かたほう) selects one of a pair, making it the useful contrast.
[全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ), "all," does not itself limit the set to two.
If there are three books and you want every one, 全部 expresses that without forcing them into a pair.

## Distinctive meaning

両方 is not simply the number two. It includes the two already relevant alternatives.
買った本が二冊 (かったほんがにさつ), "the books bought are two in number," counts books;
両方買った (りょうほうかった), "bought both," says that neither of the two choices was left out.
