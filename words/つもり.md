# つもり (つもり) — intention

## Meaning

つもり expresses an intention or plan. Use a verb's dictionary form + つもりです for what you intend to do; use its ない-form + つもりです for what you intend not to do.

来年、日本に行くつもりです。（らいねん、にほんにいくつもりです。） — I intend to go to Japan next year.

今日は出かけないつもりです。（きょうはでかけないつもりです。） — I intend not to go out today.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual in this construction. The dictionary also records 積もり, but the familiar planning expression is normally written つもり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

予定（よてい） emphasizes a schedule or arrangement. つもり emphasizes someone's intention, even when no booking or definite timetable exists.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not put a polite verb form directly before it: 行くつもりです, not 行きますつもりです. 行くつもりはありません means "I have no intention of going"; it denies an intention rather than literally stating a plan not to go.
