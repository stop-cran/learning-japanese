# たいてい — usually; most

## Meaning

たいてい means "usually" or "generally" when describing a repeated situation. Before a noun, たいていの means "most."

- 日曜日はたいてい家にいます。 (にちようびはたいていいえにいます。) — I'm usually at home on Sundays.
- たいていの店はもう閉まっています。 (たいていのみせはもうしまっています。) — Most of the shops are already closed.

## How the kanji combine

The kana spelling is usual for the common "usually" sense. The kanji spelling 大抵 is also used; the Sino-Japanese reading たいてい gives this kana headword its kango classification. There are no written kanji in this card's headword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

いつも means "always" in a habitual statement, while ときどき means "sometimes." たいてい allows exceptions. 普通 (ふつう) can describe what is normal or usual, rather than simply how frequently something happens.

## Distinctive meaning

たいてい does not supply an exact percentage. Use it for a broad tendency, not a precise count. Distinguish its adverb use before a predicate from たいていの + noun; no な is needed in the ordinary "most shops" pattern.
