# 基本 (きほん) - basics; foundation

## Meaning

基本 names the basics, fundamentals, or underlying basis of something. It is a noun that can modify another noun with の: 基本の動き (きほんのうごき), "basic movements."

- まず文法の基本を学びましょう。 (まずぶんぽうのきほんをまなびましょう。) - First, let's learn the basics of grammar.
- 相手の話を聞くことが会話の基本です。 (あいてのはなしをきくことがかいわのきほんです。) - Listening to the other person is a fundamental part of conversation.

Nを基本にする means "make N the basis." The related form 基本的な (きほんてきな) means "basic; fundamental."

## How the kanji combine

基 contributes a base or foundation, and [本](../kanji/本.md) contributes the root or underlying part. Their related meanings reinforce what supports the rest. The on readings are キ + ホン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

基礎 (きそ) also means foundation or basics and overlaps in learning contexts. 応用 (おうよう), "application," is a practical contrast: applying principles is not their logical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Basic does not necessarily mean easy. A fundamental principle can take time to master.

In colloquial speech, 基本 can also work adverbially: 基本、毎日来ます (きほん、まいにちきます), "Basically, I come every day." It states the normal pattern, allowing contextual exceptions. 基本的に (きほんてきに) is another useful form for "basically; in principle."
