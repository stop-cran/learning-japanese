# 哀れ (あわれ) - pity; pitiable

## Meaning

哀れ is a noun for pity, sorrow, or pathos, and a na-adjective meaning "pitiable; pitiful." Use **哀れな + noun** to describe someone or something that evokes pity. **哀れを感じる** (あわれをかんじる) means "feel pity/pathos."

- その話を聞いて、哀れを感じました。 (そのはなしをきいて、あわれをかんじました。) - Hearing that story, I felt pity.
- 哀れな子猫を見つけました。 (あわれなこねこをみつけました。) - I found a pitiful little kitten.

The adjective presents an evaluation of someone's situation; it does not simply state that the person cannot do something.

## How the kanji combine

哀 carries the idea of sorrow. With れ, it writes the native word あわれ. This is not an on-reading compound. The spelling and meaning belong to the whole word; れ is not a separate word for a kind of sadness.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[かわいそう](かわいそう.md) also describes someone as deserving sympathy. [悲しい](悲しい.md) (かなしい), "sad," can describe the speaker's own feeling, whereas 哀れな人 presents the person as pitiable. These meanings overlap without identical grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling a person 哀れ can sound distancing or belittling, depending on context. It is not a neutral translation of every English "helpless." In descriptions of a story or scene, the noun can emphasize sorrowful emotional appeal rather than practical inability.
