# 遅れる (おくれる) — be late; be delayed

## Meaning

遅れる means to be late or delayed. It is intransitive: the late person or delayed vehicle takes が. The appointment or event one is late for takes に.

- 電車が十分遅れています。 (でんしゃがじゅっぷんおくれています。) — The train is running ten minutes late.
- 授業に遅れないでください。 (じゅぎょうにおくれないでください。) — Please do not be late for class.

## How the kanji combine

[遅](../kanji/遅.md) supplies おく and the lateness meaning; れる is okurigana. The verb is ichidan: 遅れます, 遅れて, 遅れない. Its grammatical stem is おくれ, not merely the portion written with kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

遅刻する (ちこくする) specifically concerns arriving late. 遅い (おそい) is an adjective meaning "late" or "slow." 間に合う (まにあう), "be in time," is a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

遅れる describes falling behind a schedule, expected time, or others. It does not mean deliberately delaying an object; 遅らせる (おくらせる) does that. A clock can also 遅れる when it runs slow.
