# 著者 (ちょしゃ) - author

## Meaning

著者 is the author or writer, especially of a particular book.
Use **book or work の著者** to identify whose authorship you mean:
この本の著者 (このほんのちょしゃ), "the author of this book."

- この本の著者に会ったことがあります。 (このほんのちょしゃにあったことがあります。) - I have met the author of this book.
- 著者は序文で研究の目的を説明しています。 (ちょしゃはじょぶんでけんきゅうのもくてきをせつめいしています。) - The author explains the purpose of the research in the preface.

Once the work is understood, 著者 can stand alone without repeating its title.

## How the kanji combine

著 contributes writing or authoring, and [者](../kanji/者.md) means a person
in a particular role. The relationship is activity plus person: the person
who authors the work. The on readings combine as ちょ + しゃ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作家](作家.md) (さっか) names a writer or creative author as an occupation
or role. 作者 (さくしゃ) also means a work's creator and extends to other
kinds of work. These overlap, but 著者 is useful when connecting someone
to a specific written work. A reader is a contrasting role, not an opposite person.

## Distinctive meaning

著者 does not itself say that writing is the person's full-time profession.
Someone can be the 著者 of a technical book while working in another field.
It also does not mean the publisher or the person who sold you the book.
Pay attention to the role being credited rather than treating everyone associated
with a publication as its author.
