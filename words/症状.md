# 症状 (しょうじょう) - symptoms

## Meaning

症状 is a noun for symptoms or a patient's condition as manifested by those symptoms. It does not mark singular or plural, so context determines whether English needs "a symptom" or "symptoms."

- いつから症状が出ていますか。 (いつからしょうじょうがでていますか。) - When did the symptoms start?
- 医者に症状を説明しました。 (いしゃにしょうじょうをせつめいしました。) - I described my symptoms to the doctor.

**症状が出る** describes symptoms appearing. **症状が重い** (しょうじょうがおもい) means the symptoms are severe, while **症状が軽い** (しょうじょうがかるい) means they are mild. These are descriptions, not diagnoses.

## How the kanji combine

症 contributes illness or a medical condition; [状](../kanji/状.md) contributes state or appearance. Together they point to how a condition manifests. The on readings are ショウ + ジョウ; both syllables have a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[病気](病気.md) (びょうき) is illness or disease, whereas 症状 refers to its manifestations or the patient's symptomatic condition. [状態](状態.md) (じょうたい) is a broader "state; condition" and is not limited to health.

## Distinctive meaning

A symptom such as a cough does not by itself identify a disease. Use 症状 to communicate what is happening, rather than as a substitute for a specific diagnosis. The noun has no useful single antonym; mild and severe describe degrees of symptoms.
