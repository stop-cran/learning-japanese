# 作家 (さっか) - writer; creative artist

## Meaning

作家 names a writer or author, especially in a literary context, and can also name an artist who creates works.
It is a noun: **作家になる** means "become a writer/artist," and **作家の作品**
(さっかのさくひん) means "a writer's or artist's work."

- 将来、作家になりたいです。 (しょうらい、さっかになりたいです。) - I want to become a writer in the future.
- この展覧会では若い陶芸作家の作品を紹介しています。 (このてんらんかいではわかいとうげいさっかのさくひんをしょうかいしています。) - This exhibition introduces works by young ceramic artists.

The field named before 作家 makes the second meaning explicit. Do not assume that every 作家 writes novels.

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) contributes creating or making; [家](../kanji/家.md) here denotes a person associated with a skill or occupation.
It does not mean a house in this compound. サク + カ contracts to さっか, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[著者](著者.md) (ちょしゃ) identifies the author of a particular written work.
[作者](作者.md) (さくしゃ) is the creator of a particular work.
[画家](画家.md) (がか) specifies a painter.
小説家 (しょうせつか) is a novelist and is narrower than 作家.
These are overlapping roles, not opposing categories.

## Distinctive meaning

作家 identifies someone as a creator of works, rather than merely someone who once wrote a message.
When discussing a particular book's authorship, 著者 is useful; when discussing a literary career or artistic practice,
作家 can present the person's creative role.

The label does not guarantee fame, commercial success, or a license.
