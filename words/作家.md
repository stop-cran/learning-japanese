# 作家 (さっか) — writer; creative artist

## Meaning

作家 commonly means a writer, especially a novelist, but can also refer to a creative practitioner
in another art. Context identifies the field.

- 将来は作家になりたいです。 (しょうらいは さっかに なりたいです。) — I want to become a writer in the future.
- 彼女は陶芸作家です。 (かのじょは とうげいさっかです。) — She is a ceramic artist.

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) サク contributes creating; [家](../kanji/家.md) カ identifies a practitioner
in a field. This is activity + practitioner. The established pronunciation is **さっか**, with
small っ, not さくか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作者](作者.md) (さくしゃ) is the creator of a particular work. 小説家 (しょうせつか), "novelist," specifies
a narrower field than 作家 can cover. There is no general direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

作家 is not literally a "making house": 家 has its person-denoting use here. The label does not
guarantee fame, commercial success, or a license, and it does not restrict the person to novels.
