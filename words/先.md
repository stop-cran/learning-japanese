# 先 (さき) — ahead; earlier or future

## Meaning

先 places something ahead in space, sequence, or time. It can mean the future, but it can also identify something earlier or previous. The surrounding expression and context determine which direction in time is intended.

- この先のことは、まだ分かりません。  
  このさきのことは、まだわかりません。  
  "I don't yet know what lies ahead."
- 先の会議で、この問題について話しました。  
  さきのかいぎで、このもんだいについてはなしました。  
  "We discussed this issue at the previous meeting."

The second use is more formal than everyday この前の会議 (このまえのかいぎ), "the other day's meeting".

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md) has the native reading さき here. The same character is read せん in [先生](先生.md), but that compound reading does not replace さき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

未来 (みらい) explicitly means "future". 前 (まえ) overlaps with "before; earlier". Neither replaces 先 in every setting.

## Distinctive meaning

先にどうぞ (さきにどうぞ) means "Please go first". Here 先に marks priority, not the distant future. Likewise, 先の + noun does not inherently mean "previous": a forward-looking context can make it refer to something still ahead.
