# 外れる (はずれる) - come undone; miss the mark

## Meaning

外れる describes something coming off, getting disconnected or moving out of place.
It is an intransitive ichidan verb: the detached thing takes が.
It also describes missing a target or a prediction proving wrong.

- シャツのボタンが外れました。 (シャツのボタンがはずれました。) - My shirt button came undone.
- 予想が外れました。 (よそうがはずれました。) - My prediction was wrong.

Forms include 外れます, 外れて, 外れた and 外れない.
In the first example, the button is no longer fastened; the sentence does not
require that it has been torn away from the shirt.

## How the kanji combine

[外](../kanji/外.md) contributes being outside or apart and writes はず before れる.
This native verb is distinct from the transitive [外す](外す.md) (はずす).
Compare ボタンを外す, "undo a button," with ボタンが外れる, "a button comes undone."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[取れる](取れる.md) (とれる) can describe something coming off;
[抜ける](抜ける.md) (ぬける) can describe coming out or being missing.
[逸れる](逸れる.md) (それる) overlaps for veering off or missing a target.
The exact object and construction distinguish these verbs; they are not replacements in every example.
当たる (あたる), "hit; be right," contrasts with a missed target or wrong prediction.

## Distinctive meaning

Additional uses include being excluded and departing from a standard:
メンバーから外れる (メンバーからはずれる), "be excluded from the members,"
and 常識から外れる (じょうしきからはずれる), "depart from common sense."
から can mark the group or standard left behind.
The verb does not by itself guarantee that a physical detachment was accidental
or that an exclusion was voluntary.
