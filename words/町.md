# 町 (まち) — town, neighborhood

## Meaning

町 means a town, neighborhood, or block. In everyday speech it can describe an inhabited area without making a precise statement about municipal status.

- この町に住んでいます。 (この まちに すんで います。) — I live in this town.
- 私の町には駅があります。 (わたしの まちには えきが あります。) — There is a station in my town.

## How the kanji combine

There is only one kanji, [町](../kanji/町.md), read with the native reading まち. This is a wago, not an on-reading compound, and there is no okurigana.
The same written character also has the on reading ちょう in other words and names. That does not change this article's selected reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 街 (まち) shares the reading and some meanings, but particularly suits downtown or main-street contexts. JMdict restricts its "downtown, main street" sense to 街, not 町.
- 村 (むら), "village," names a related kind of settlement. It is not an antonym.
- There is no single natural direct opposite of 町.

## Distinctive meaning

町 is useful for the town or neighborhood as a place where people live. Do not automatically rewrite every まち as 街.
In actual place names, the reading may be まち or ちょう; check the name rather than assuming that the everyday standalone reading always applies.
