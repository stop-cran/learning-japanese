# 生ずる (しょうずる) - arise; produce; cause

## Meaning

生ずる means to produce or cause something, or for something to arise or be generated. It is a more literary counterpart of [生じる](生じる.md) (しょうじる). Learn the particles with the intended use.

- 計画に問題が生ずることがあります。 (けいかくにもんだいがしょうずることがあります。) - Problems sometimes arise in the plan.
- この表現は誤解を生ずるおそれがあります。 (このひょうげんはごかいをしょうずるおそれがあります。) - This expression may cause misunderstanding.

The first has 問題が, the problem arising. The second has 誤解を, the misunderstanding produced. おそれがある means that there is a risk or possibility of an undesirable result.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) contributes coming into being or producing. ショウ is the on reading in しょうずる; ずる supplies the verbal ending. This is not the native verb [生きる](生きる.md) (いきる), "live."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[起こる](起こる.md) (おこる) describes something happening or arising. [起こす](起す.md) (おこす) can describe causing it. 生ずる covers both the arising and producing constructions; this does not make those two other verbs interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep しょうずる and しょうじる distinct as dictionary forms. The pinned JMdict calls 生ずる a ずる alternative of an ichidan じる verb; Digital Daijisen instead classifies 生ずる as サ変 and relates 生じる to it as an ichidan form. These are different descriptive frameworks, not a reason to conjugate 生ずる as an ordinary る-ending godan verb. Useful forms include 生じます (しょうじます), 生じて (しょうじて), and 生じた (しょうじた).
