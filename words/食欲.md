# 食欲 (しょくよく) - appetite for food

## Meaning

食欲 is the desire to eat: appetite for food. It is a noun. Learn 食欲がある, "have an appetite," and 食欲がない, "have no appetite."

- 今日は食欲がありません。 (きょうはしょくよくがありません。) - I have no appetite today.
- 運動したら、食欲が出ました。 (うんどうしたら、しょくよくがでました。) - After exercising, I developed an appetite.

が marks the appetite itself in both constructions. 食欲が出る describes appetite appearing or returning, not food coming out.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) contributes eating or food, and [欲](../kanji/欲.md) contributes desire. 食 specifies what the desire is for. The on readings are ショク + ヨク, giving しょくよく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お腹がすく (おなかがすく) describes becoming hungry. 食欲 focuses on wanting to eat, which is not identical to how full the stomach is. 食欲がない is the direct negative construction rather than a separate antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English uses "appetite" figuratively for many desires, but 食欲 specifically concerns food. It does not by itself measure how much someone actually eats. In conversation about health, it can describe a person's present condition without naming a diagnosis or explaining why the appetite has changed.
