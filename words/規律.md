# 規律 (きりつ) - discipline; rules; orderly conduct

## Meaning

規律 is discipline or orderly conduct maintained by following standards. It can also name the rules or regulations that maintain that order, especially within a group or organization.

- この学校では、規律を大切にしています。 (このがっこうでは、きりつをたいせつにしています。) - This school values discipline.
- 寮の規律を守って生活してください。 (りょうのきりつをまもってせいかつしてください。) - Please follow the dormitory's rules while living there.

規律を守る (きりつをまもる) means observe the rules or maintain discipline; 規律が乱れる (きりつがみだれる) means discipline breaks down.

## How the kanji combine

[規](../kanji/規.md), standard or rule, combines with [律](../kanji/律.md), regulation or law. The related meanings reinforce the idea of regulated order. The on readings are キ + リツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[規則](規則.md) (きそく) and [決まり](決まり.md) (きまり) overlap as rules. 規律 particularly connects the rules with discipline and orderly behavior. [法律](法律.md) (ほうりつ) is law in a legal system; not every organization's 規律 is legislation.

## Distinctive meaning

English "order" here can mean an orderly state, not only a command or an order for goods. English "law" in a broad gloss likewise does not require a national statute. Do not reduce 規律 either to obedience alone or to a list of written rules alone.
