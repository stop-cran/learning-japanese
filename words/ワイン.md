# ワイン (ワイン) - wine

## Meaning

ワイン is a noun meaning "wine." It takes ordinary noun particles:
ワインを飲む means "drink wine," and ワインの瓶 (ワインのびん) means "a bottle of wine."
The noun itself does not specify a color, quantity, or place of production.

- 料理に白ワインを少し使います。 (りょうりにしろワインをすこしつかいます。) - I use a little white wine in the dish.
- この店には赤ワインもあります。 (このみせにはあかワインもあります。) - This shop also has red wine.

白ワイン (しろワイン) and 赤ワイン (あかワイン) specify white and red wine.
The first example shows wine as an ingredient rather than a drink being ordered.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana as ワ + イ + ン.
There is no long-vowel mark and no kanji compound to analyze.
In 赤ワイン and 白ワイン, the preceding native color words modify the kind of wine;
the katakana noun remains unchanged.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[酒](酒.md) (さけ) can refer broadly to alcoholic drinks.
[ビール](ビール.md), "beer," and [ウイスキー](ウイスキー.md), "whisky," name different drinks,
not alternative translations of ワイン.
There is no useful lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ワイン for the drink category, then add the information needed for the situation.
ワインを一本 (ワインをいっぽん) can request one bottle when that unit is understood.
The noun alone does not mean one glass, one bottle, or a particular serving style.
