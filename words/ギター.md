# ギター

## Meaning

ギター means a guitar, the stringed musical instrument.

- 毎日ギターを弾きます。 (まいにち ギターを ひきます。) — I play the guitar every day.
- このギターは友達のです。 (この ギターは ともだちのです。) — This guitar belongs to my friend.

Use 弾く (ひく) for playing it, with the instrument marked by を. ギターが好きです means "I like guitars" or "I like guitar music," depending on context.

## How the kanji combine

The loanword is written in katakana. ギ has a voiced consonant, and ー lengthens the final vowel: ギター, not ギタ. There is no kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

アコースティックギター specifies an acoustic guitar, and エレキギター an electric guitar. ピアノ is another instrument, not an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

ギター names the instrument, not automatically the person playing it. ギタリスト is a guitarist. English "play" has several Japanese equivalents; the basic instrument expression here is ギターを弾く rather than using a general verb for games or recreation.
