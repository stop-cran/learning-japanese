# 億 (おく) — hundred million

## Meaning

億 is the number unit 100,000,000. Japanese groups large numbers by powers of ten thousand: one 億 is ten thousand 万. Normally say 一億 (いちおく) for one hundred million, 二億 (におく) for two hundred million.

この建物は一億円です。 (このたてものはいちおくえんです。) — This building costs one hundred million yen.

## How the kanji combine

億 is a single kanji read with its on reading おく. It functions as part of a number; it is not a counter for a particular kind of object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

万 (まん) is ten thousand. 一千万 (いっせんまん) is ten million, one tenth of 一億. Ten 億 make 十億 (じゅうおく), one billion.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 億 as "million." For amounts, place the currency after the complete number: 三億円 (さんおくえん), "three hundred million yen." For people, add 人: 一億人 (いちおくにん).
