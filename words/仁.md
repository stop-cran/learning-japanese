# 仁 (じん) — benevolence; compassion

## Meaning

仁 is kindness and loving care for other people: benevolence, compassion and charity. In Confucian teaching it is the highest virtue: getting close to others and living together with them with a caring heart.

- 仁は、人を思いやる心です。
  (じんは、ひとをおもいやるこころです。)
  "Benevolence is a heart that cares for others."
- この本は、儒教の仁について書いています。
  (このほんは、じゅきょうのじんについてかいています。)
  "This book writes about benevolence in Confucianism."

These are original usage examples, not quotations from a dictionary or other source.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [仁](../kanji/仁.md) uses the on reading ジン here. There is no multi-character compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[親切](親切.md) (しんせつ), "kind; considerate," and [優しい](優しい.md) (やさしい), "kind; gentle," describe kind people or kind acts; 仁 names kindness as a moral virtue. [善](善.md) (ぜん), "moral good; virtue," is goodness in general, and [道徳](道徳.md) (どうとく), "morals; morality," covers right conduct as a whole; 仁 is one particular virtue.

There is no single fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

仁 is especially the highest virtue of [儒教](儒教.md) (じゅきょう), "Confucianism." JMdict also gives the senses "human" and "kernel"; the kernel sense, the seed inside a fruit, can also be read にん. Even so, 仁 is not the ordinary word for a person: use [人間](人間.md) (にんげん), "human being."
