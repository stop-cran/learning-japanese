# 食器 (しょっき) - tableware; dishes

## Meaning

食器 means tableware: containers and implements used for eating, such as
bowls, plates, and chopsticks. It is not restricted to ceramic dishes.

- 食事のあとで食器を洗います。 (しょくじのあとでしょっきをあらいます。) - I wash the dishes after the meal.
- 新しい食器を買いました。 (あたらしいしょっきをかいました。) - I bought new tableware.

The context supplies whether 食器 refers to one item or several. Japanese
does not require a plural ending here.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) contributes eating or food, and 器 a vessel or
implement. The first character identifies the purpose of the second.
ショク + キ becomes しょっき, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[皿](皿.md) (さら) and [お皿](お皿.md) (おさら) name plates or dishes,
a narrower category. [箸](箸.md) (はし) are chopsticks and can also be 食器.
[道具](道具.md) (どうぐ) means tools or implements more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

In 食器を洗う, the dishes are objects to wash, not prepared food.
[料理](料理.md) (りょうり) can mean a dish of food. English dish covers
both, but 食器 does not mean the meal itself or every piece of kitchen
equipment used to prepare it.
