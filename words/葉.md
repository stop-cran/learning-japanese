# 葉 (は) — leaf

## Meaning

葉 means a leaf or leaves. It includes different plant leaf forms, such as blades of grass and pine needles, rather than only broad flat leaves.

この木の葉は大きいです。（このきのははおおきいです。） — This tree's leaves are large.

## How the kanji combine

[葉](../kanji/葉.md) is one kanji read with the native noun は. Its on reading ヨウ appears in compounds, but that is not this standalone word's reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

葉っぱ（はっぱ） is a familiar everyday alternative. 花（はな）, "flower," and 枝（えだ）, "branch," name other plant parts, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

葉 does not change form for plural; the situation tells you whether one leaf or many are meant. Pronounce the noun は with an h sound. In 葉は, the following topic particle は is pronounced わ, unlike the noun.
