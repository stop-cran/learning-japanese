# ぴちぴち — lively; leaping energetically

## Meaning

ぴちぴち describes a fish jumping energetically in the water. In other contexts it can describe
youthful energy, tight clothing or splattering oil; the subject selects the meaning.

- 池の小魚が水面でぴちぴち跳ねた。
  — Small fish in the pond leapt energetically at the surface.
- 生け簀の魚が、餌を見てぴちぴちと跳ねた。
  — The fish in the holding tank saw the food and leapt about energetically.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぱちぱち](ぱちぱち.md) often describes a series of sharp little sounds, such as clapping or
crackling. Here ぴちぴち describes lively fish movement, not the sound of applause.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records ぴちぴち for energetic youth, especially young women, and for tightness
or splattering. These examples use the fish sense to avoid turning an age- or gender-marked
description into a general compliment.
