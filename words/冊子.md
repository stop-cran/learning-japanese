# 冊子 (さっし) - booklet; pamphlet

## Meaning

冊子 is a book or booklet, including a pamphlet. The "booklet" gloss is useful,
but it does not establish a fixed page count or require a particular binding method.

- 受付で案内の冊子をもらった。
  (うけつけであんないのさっしをもらった。) - I received an information booklet at reception.
- この冊子には地図も載っている。
  (このさっしにはちずものっている。) - This booklet also contains a map.

## How the kanji combine

[冊](../kanji/冊.md) supplies the book or bound-volume idea.
[子](../kanji/子.md) functions as a noun-forming ending here, not as a literal child.
The resulting compound names a publication. Read it さっし, with a small っ:
the 冊 reading サツ combines with シ in this word, rather than remaining さつし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本](本.md) (ほん) and [書物](書物.md) (しょもつ) are broader words for books.
Their meanings overlap with 冊子; a different English label does not make them mutually
exclusive categories. A booklet can also be a book.

## Distinctive meaning

冊子 names an object, not the act of publishing or printing it.
It is useful for information booklets and similar publications, but the dictionary
also allows the broader book sense. Do not make "small" or "few pages" an absolute rule.
