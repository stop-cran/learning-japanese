# 主役 (しゅやく) - leading role; leading actor

## Meaning

主役 is the leading part in a performance, or the actor or actress who plays it. The same word can therefore name either the role or its performer.

- この映画で主役を演じています。 (このえいがでしゅやくをえんじています。) - I am playing the leading role in this film.
- 主役の俳優に会いました。 (しゅやくのはいゆうにあいました。) - I met the actor playing the lead.

There is also a figurative use for the central person or thing in a situation, not necessarily a stage performance. This extension is corroborated by the Japanese dictionary linked below.

## How the kanji combine

[主](../kanji/主.md) contributes main or central, and [役](../kanji/役.md) a role or part. The combination points to the leading role; it does not identify every person in charge of a workplace.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[役](役.md) and [役割](役割.md) are broader role words. [俳優](俳優.md) and [女優](女優.md) name performers without requiring a leading part. 脇役 (わきやく), a supporting role or performer, contrasts with 主役.

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding construction distinguishes role from performer. 主役を演じる describes playing the lead, while 主役が到着した (しゅやくがとうちゃくした), "the lead performer arrived," refers to a person. Neither reading entails that this person directed or wrote the production.
