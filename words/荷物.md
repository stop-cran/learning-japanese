# 荷物 (にもつ) — luggage

## Meaning

荷物 means luggage, baggage, or a package being carried or delivered. It can refer to several bags together without a plural ending.

荷物をここに置いてください。（にもつをここにおいてください。） — Please put your luggage here.

## How the kanji combine

荷, read に, means a load; [物](../kanji/物.md), read もつ, means a thing. This is a mixed native-plus-on reading, not two native readings. The first part gives the idea of something carried.

## Synonyms and antonyms

かばん is a bag, one possible container for 荷物. 小包（こづつみ） specifically means a parcel. 荷物 is broader than either one.

## Distinctive meaning

The word covers belongings in transit, not only suitcases on a journey. It can also mean a burden figuratively, but that does not mean ordinary luggage is necessarily troublesome. Context determines whether a package, bags, or baggage collectively is intended.
