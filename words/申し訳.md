# 申し訳 (もうしわけ) - apology; excuse

## Meaning

申し訳 names an excuse or apologetic explanation. It is especially useful in negative expressions of apology: 申し訳ない, 申し訳ありません, and 申し訳ございません. These acknowledge that there is no adequate excuse.

- 遅れて、申し訳ありません。 (おくれて、もうしわけありません。) - I am sorry for being late.
- ご迷惑をおかけして、申し訳ございません。 (ごめいわくをおかけして、もうしわけございません。) - I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.

The て-form connects the regretted action to the apology. 申し訳ございません is a courteous expression suited to acknowledging inconvenience or a mistake.

## How the kanji combine

[申](../kanji/申.md) belongs to [申す](申す.md) (もうす), the humble verb "say." Its stem 申し combines with [訳](../kanji/訳.md) (わけ), "reason; explanation." The whole native expression concerns an explanation offered, not the separate on-reading word 訳 (やく), "translation."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すみません](すみません.md) and [ごめんなさい](ごめんなさい.md) also apologize, with their own situational uses. 言い訳 (いいわけ) means an excuse or explanation and can suggest self-justification.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun 申し訳 alone is not the complete apology shown above. Learn the full negative expression instead of replacing English "sorry" with the bare noun. Although the dictionary also permits a する use for making excuses, these negative formulas are the practical patterns taught here; 申し訳する is not a direct replacement for them.
