# 財布 (さいふ) — wallet; purse

## Meaning

財布 is a wallet or purse for carrying money. It can refer to a coin purse as well as a wallet for notes.
English "purse" can also mean a handbag, so the money-container meaning is the one to keep in mind.

- 財布を家に忘れました。 (さいふをいえにわすれました。) — I left my wallet at home.
- 財布に千円入っています。 (さいふにせんえんはいっています。) — There is a thousand yen in the wallet.

## How the kanji combine

[財](../kanji/財.md) contributes the money or valuables association; 布 means cloth.
Together they name the object 財布, read with サイ + フ.
Use those associations to remember the spelling, not to conclude that a modern wallet must be made of cloth.

## Synonyms and antonyms

小銭入れ (こぜにいれ), "change purse," specifies a container for coins and overlaps with the coin-purse sense of 財布.
財産 (ざいさん), "property; assets," shares 財 but names possessions or wealth, not a wallet.
There is no useful everyday opposite to the name of this object.

## Distinctive meaning

財布 names the container, not the sum of money inside it.
In 財布に千円入っています, に locates the money in the wallet; the sentence is not saying that the wallet itself costs a thousand yen.
