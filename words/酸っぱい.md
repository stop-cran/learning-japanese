# 酸っぱい (すっぱい) - sour

## Meaning

酸っぱい describes a sour taste. It is an i-adjective, written すっぱい
in kana or 酸っぱい with kanji.

- このレモンはとても酸っぱいです。 (このレモンはとてもすっぱいです。) - This lemon is very sour.
- このヨーグルトはあまり酸っぱくありません。 (このヨーグルトはあまりすっぱくありません。) - This yogurt is not very sour.

Before a noun, use 酸っぱい directly: 酸っぱい果物 (すっぱいくだもの),
"sour fruit." The negative is 酸っぱくない; the past is 酸っぱかった.

## How the kanji combine

酸 is associated with acid and sourness. Learn the complete native adjective
すっぱい, including the small っ; it is not a compound read with サン.
The written っぱい completes this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[酸性](酸性.md) (さんせい) describes acidity as a chemical property, rather
than simply naming a taste. 甘い (あまい), "sweet," contrasts with sourness
in discussions of flavor, although a food can be both sweet and sour.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not substitute 苦い (にがい), "bitter," for sour. English "acid" in the
dictionary gloss concerns this taste use; the adjective is not a chemical
measurement or a general label for everything classified as 酸性.
