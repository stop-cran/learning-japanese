# 大臣 (だいじん) - cabinet minister

## Meaning

大臣 is a noun for a cabinet minister, a senior member of a government. It can stand alone when the person's portfolio is known, or appear as part of a specific title.

- 大臣が記者会見で説明しました。 (だいじんがきしゃかいけんでせつめいしました。) - The minister explained it at a press conference.
- 新しい外務大臣が任命されました。 (あたらしいがいむだいじんがにんめいされました。) - A new foreign minister was appointed.

外務大臣 (がいむだいじん) is the minister of foreign affairs. 大臣になる (だいじんになる) means "become a cabinet minister."

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes greatness or high status; 臣 has the meaning of a minister or retainer. The modifier-plus-head compound identifies a high ministerial office. Learn ダイ + ジン, not a reading assembled from 大きい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[首相](首相.md) (しゅしょう), "prime minister," names the head of government in the relevant system, not every cabinet member.

[政府](政府.md) (せいふ), "government," names the governing body rather than one officeholder.

## Distinctive meaning

大臣 is a government role, not the religious sense of English "minister." It also does not mean a company department manager merely because that person is senior. Specific portfolios come before 大臣, so identifying the whole title helps you see which policy area the person is responsible for.
