# 分数 (ぶんすう) - fraction (mathematics)

## Meaning

分数 is a mathematical fraction, such as 1/2 or 3/4. It is a noun.
In 二分の一 (にぶんのいち), "one half," the denominator comes before 分の and
the numerator follows it.

- 二分の一は分数です。 (にぶんのいちはぶんすうです。) - One half is a fraction.
- 分数の計算を練習しています。 (ぶんすうのけいさんをれんしゅうしています。) - I am practicing calculations with fractions.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) contributes division into parts; [数](../kanji/数.md) contributes
number. The compound names a number expressed through divided parts. Its on readings
are ブン + スウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[小数](小数.md) (しょうすう) is a decimal in the relevant mathematical sense:
1/2 and 0.5 can represent the same value in different notation.
[割合](割合.md) (わりあい) is a ratio or proportion; it can be expressed with a
fraction, but the relationship being measured is not identical to the notation.

## Distinctive meaning

分数 does not mean every small quantity or every English "fraction" in figurative
phrases. An improper fraction is still a 分数; a numerator larger than the denominator
does not invalidate the term. Remember the reading ぶんすう, not ふんすう.
