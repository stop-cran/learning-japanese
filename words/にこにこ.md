# にこにこ — smiling cheerfully

## Meaning

にこにこ depicts a friendly, cheerful smile, often maintained during an interaction.

- 店員はにこにこしながら注文を聞いた。 — The shop assistant took my order with a cheerful smile. (店員: てんいん; 注文: ちゅうもん.)
- 孫の話を聞いて、祖母はにこにこ笑っていた。 — My grandmother was smiling happily as she listened to her grandchild. (孫: まご; 祖母: そぼ.)

Use にこにこする or にこにこ笑う, optionally にこにこと笑う. にこにこした顔 (かお) means "a smiling face"; use した, not a general にこにこな rule.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[にっこり](にっこり.md) often presents a distinct smile that appears at a particular moment; にこにこ more readily pictures continued smiling. [にやにや](にやにや.md) suggests a grin or smirk that may strike the observer as self-satisfied.

## Distinctive meaning

にこにこ笑う need not involve laughter aloud. The word gives the visible smile a positive, friendly framing, but it cannot guarantee the person's sincerity. Its repeated form suggests a continuing manner without requiring a countable series of separate smiles.
