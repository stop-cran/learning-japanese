# 可決 (かけつ) - approval; passage of a bill

## Meaning

可決 is approving or passing a motion, bill, or similar proposal through a decision. 可決する is transitive: the proposal takes を. The passive 可決される means that it is approved or passed.

- 議会はその法案を可決しました。 (ぎかいはそのほうあんをかけつしました。) - The assembly passed the bill.
- その議案は全会一致で可決されました。 (そのぎあんはぜんかいいっちでかけつされました。) - The proposal was approved unanimously.

The second sentence specifies unanimity; 可決 alone does not say that every voter agreed.

## How the kanji combine

可 contributes acceptance or approval, and [決](../kanji/決.md) deciding. Together they describe deciding in favor of a proposal. The on readings are カ + ケツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[承認](承認.md) (しょうにん) is broader approval or recognition. [賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい) expresses agreement or support; one person's support does not establish that a motion passed. [通過](通過.md) (つうか) also describes a bill passing. 否決 (ひけつ), voting down a proposal, is the direct contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

可決 is not the general word for every permitted everyday action: [許可](許可.md) (きょか) concerns permission. Nor does a bill passing one stage necessarily mean that all remaining legal steps are complete. Identify the proposal and the body or stage making the decision.
