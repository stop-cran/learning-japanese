# 羨ましい (うらやましい) - envious; enviable

## Meaning

羨ましい is an i-adjective expressing envy or describing something
enviable. In the useful pattern 人のNが羨ましい, N is what the speaker
wishes they also had. English often translates the whole sentence with
"I envy ..." rather than "N is envious."

- 彼の自由な生活が羨ましいです。 (かれのじゆうなせいかつがうらやましいです。) - I envy his free lifestyle.
- それは本当に羨ましい話ですね。 (それはほんとうにうらやましいはなしですね。) - That really is something to be envious of.

The negative is 羨ましくない and the past is 羨ましかった.

## How the kanji combine

羨 writes the native base うらや; the whole adjective is うらやましい.
Compare [羨む](羨む.md) (うらやむ). These are related words, not
two different on readings of the character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

羨む is the verb "envy": 彼の生活を羨む (かれのせいかつをうらやむ).
Compare the を in that verb phrase with が in 彼の生活が羨ましい.
[恨む](恨む.md) (うらむ), "resent," instead concerns bitter feeling
toward a target, not simply wishing to share someone's good situation.

## Distinctive meaning

羨ましい can sound admiring or friendly; it does not automatically
express hatred or an accusation. Although "jealous" can translate it,
do not assume that it specifically describes possessiveness about a partner.
The envied situation and context determine the tone.
