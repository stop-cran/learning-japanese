# 試合 (しあい) — match; contest

## Meaning

試合 means a **match, game, or bout** in a competitive activity. It is a noun and can take する: 相手と試合する (あいてとしあいする), “play a match against an opponent.”

- 昨日の試合は面白かったです。 (きのうのしあいはおもしろかったです。) — Yesterday's match was interesting.
- 明日、隣の学校と試合をします。 (あした、となりのがっこうとしあいをします。) — Tomorrow we will play a match against the neighboring school.

## How the kanji combine

[試](../kanji/試.md) suggests testing, and [合](../kanji/合.md) suggests coming together. These associations help with remembering a contest, but JMdict marks 試合 as an **ateji spelling**. Do not present it as an ordinary on-reading compound; learn the native word しあい as a whole.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ゲーム also means a game, including non-sport games. 大会 (たいかい) can be a tournament containing many matches. 練習 (れんしゅう), “practice,” contrasts with competition, though a practice match is possible.

## Distinctive meaning

試合 is a competitive encounter, not every kind of play. It does not itself say whether the match is professional, amateur, official, or informal.
