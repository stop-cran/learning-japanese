# 助かる (たすかる) - be saved; be helped; survive

## Meaning

助かる describes someone surviving danger, something being spared damage,
or a person being helped or saved trouble. It is an intransitive godan
verb: 助かります, 助かった, 助かって, 助からない.

- 事故にあいましたが、命は助かりました。
  (じこにあいましたが、いのちはたすかりました。) - I had an accident, but survived.
- 荷物を運んでくれると助かります。
  (にもつをはこんでくれるとたすかります。) - It would help me if you carried the luggage.

The second is a useful indirect request: doing the action would reduce
the speaker's burden. It is not necessarily a request for rescue.
家が助かった (いえがたすかった) can mean that a house escaped damage.

## How the kanji combine

[助](../kanji/助.md) contributes help or rescue, read たす here.
かる completes the native verb. Despite the English "be saved,"
助かる is not the passive conjugation of 助ける.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[助ける](助ける.md) (たすける) presents someone's action: 人を助ける,
"help/save a person." 助かる presents the recipient's survival or relief.
[救う](救う.md) (すくう) also concerns rescue; [生存](生存.md)
(せいぞん) focuses on being alive.

## Distinctive meaning

助かった！ can express gratitude or relief after practical help.
For a helpful thing, Nがあると助かる means "having N helps."
Do not turn the intransitive verb into 人を助かる; the person helped
is not its direct object.
