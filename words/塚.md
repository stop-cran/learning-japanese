# 塚 (つか) — mound; burial mound

## Meaning

塚 is a raised mound. It can be a heap or a mound built over a grave; the word by itself does not tell you that someone is buried there.

- 畑のそばに小さな塚がある。 (はたけのそばにちいさなつかがある。) — There is a small mound beside the field.
- ここには昔の人を葬った塚が残っている。 (ここにはむかしのひとをほうむったつかがのこっている。) — A mound where people were buried long ago still stands here.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [塚](../kanji/塚.md) writes the whole native noun つか. In 貝塚 (かいづか), a shell mound, the initial sound of つか is voiced.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[丘](丘.md) (おか) is a hill or rise. [墓](墓.md) (はか) identifies a grave, whatever its shape. [古墳](古墳.md) (こふん) identifies an ancient burial mound.

## Distinctive meaning

In the examples above, 塚 draws attention to a mound; “burial mound” fits the second example because it mentions burial. A separate dictionary sense is a standing stone or roadside stone marker, so not every occurrence refers to a heap of earth.
