# 学期 (がっき) - school term; semester

## Meaning

学期 is a school term: one of the teaching periods into which an academic year
is divided. "Semester" fits a two-term system, but 学期 does not itself require
exactly two terms. Use 学期の初め (がっきのはじめ), "the beginning of the term."

- 来週から新しい学期が始まります。 (らいしゅうからあたらしいがっきがはじまります。) - A new school term starts next week.
- 今学期は日本語を勉強しています。 (こんがっきはにほんごをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying Japanese this term.

今学期 names the current term, not the current day or school year.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) contributes study or school, and [期](../kanji/期.md)
contributes a period or term. School-related learning modifies the time period.
The combination of ガク and キ produces がっき, with a small っ rather
than an extra full syllable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[期間](期間.md) (きかん) is a general period of time; 学期 specifies an
academic one. 学年 (がくねん) can identify an academic year or grade.
A year and one of its terms are related units, not interchangeable synonyms.
A vacation is a contrasting part of the schedule, not an antonym of "term."

## Distinctive meaning

When translating a school calendar, check its divisions rather than assuming
an English calendar model. 一学期 (いちがっき) can name the first term in a
school's sequence. The word identifies a curricular period, not the length of
a single lesson or the building where lessons occur.
