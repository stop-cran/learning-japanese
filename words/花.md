# 花 (はな) — flower

## Meaning

花 is a flower or blossom: 花が咲く さく (the flowers bloom), 花を買う かう (buy flowers), 花を飾る かざる (decorate with flowers), 花屋 はなや (flower shop), 花見 はなみ (cherry-blossom viewing).

## How the kanji combine

[花](../kanji/花.md) alone with the kun reading はな. In a few words the on reading カ is used: 花瓶 かびん (vase), 花壇 かだん (flower bed). [花火](花火.md) uses はな as a prefix.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 桜 さくら (cherry blossom, often meant by 花見), 草花 くさばな (flowering plants).
- Homophone: 鼻 はな (nose), 端 はな (edge, tip); they are different words with different pitch and written differently.

## Distinctive meaning

花 can also mean "the best part" as in 花形 はながた (star, a leading figure) and 花より団子 (food over beauty). In spring "花" often means cherry blossoms specifically.
