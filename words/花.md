# 花 (はな) — flower

## Meaning

花 is a flower or blossom: 花が咲く さく (the flowers bloom), 花を買う かう (buy flowers), 花を飾る かざる (decorate with flowers), 花屋 はなや (flower shop), 花見 はなみ (cherry-blossom viewing).

## How the kanji combine

[花](../kanji/花.md) alone with the kun reading はな. In a few words the on reading カ is used: 花瓶 かびん (vase), 花壇 かだん (flower bed). [花火](花火.md)（はなび）, "fireworks," uses はな as its first element; 火 is read び here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 桜 さくら (cherry blossom, often meant by 花見), 草花 くさばな (flowering plants).
- Same kana reading: 鼻 はな (nose), 端 はな (edge, tip); distinguish these words by their spelling and meaning.

## Distinctive meaning

花 can mean "(the) best" in set expressions such as 言わぬが花（いわぬがはな）, "some things are better left unsaid." 花形（はながた） means "a star; a leading figure."

In 花より団子（はなよりだんご）, literally "dumplings over flowers," 花 represents blossoms and aesthetic enjoyment, not "the best part." The proverb contrasts practical benefit with beauty. In spring, 花 often means cherry blossoms specifically.
