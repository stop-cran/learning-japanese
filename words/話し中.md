# 話し中 (はなしちゅう) - busy on the phone; in conversation

## Meaning

話し中 means busy on the phone, or in the middle of talking.
It can stand before です or modify a noun with の.
話中 is another recognized spelling at the same reading はなしちゅう.

- 電話をかけましたが、話し中でした。 (でんわをかけましたが、はなしちゅうでした。) - I phoned, but the line was busy.
- 先生は話し中なので、少し待ちましょう。 (せんせいははなしちゅうなので、すこしまちましょう。) - The teacher is talking, so let's wait a little.

## How the kanji combine

[話](../kanji/話.md) supplies the native noun/stem 話し はなし;
[中](../kanji/中.md) has the on-reading チュウ, "in progress" here.
This is a mixed native-plus-on formation, grouped under wago by the deck's native-first convention,
not an all-kun reading and not なか in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話](話.md) (はなし) is talk or a story, without the "currently in progress" part.
[会話](会話.md) (かいわ) names conversation.
[忙しい](忙しい.md) (いそがしい) is busy more generally; a busy telephone line is not described by simply substituting that adjective.

## Distinctive meaning

The phone sense means the line is engaged.
Outside the phone context, the word can describe an ongoing conversation.
Do not use 話し中 merely because someone has a lot of work to do.
