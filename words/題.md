# 題 (だい) - title; topic; test question

## Meaning

題 is a noun for a title, subject, theme, or assigned topic.
It can also mean a test question and serves as a counter for questions.
題をつける means "give something a title"; Nという題 means "the title or topic N."

- 作文に「私の夢」という題をつけました。
  (さくぶんに「わたしのゆめ」というだいをつけました。)
  - I titled my composition "My Dream."
- 数学の問題を三題解きました。
  (すうがくのもんだいをさんだいときました。) - I solved three mathematics problems.

一題 (いちだい), 二題 (にだい), and 三題 (さんだい) count questions or problems,
not the sheets of paper on which they are printed.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [題](../kanji/題.md) supplies the title or subject meaning.
Its on reading is ダイ.
In [問題](問題.md) (もんだい), it combines with 問, "question," to name a problem or question.

## Synonyms and antonyms

タイトル overlaps with the title sense.
[名前](名前.md) (なまえ) means a name more broadly.
問題 is useful for the actual exercise or issue; 題 can instead identify what a piece of writing is about.

## Distinctive meaning

A title and a topic need not be identical. The assigned 題 might be "your hometown,"
while the finished composition has a more specific title.
Look at the construction: 題をつける names a work, whereas 三題解く counts completed problems.
