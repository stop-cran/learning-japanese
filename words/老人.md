# 老人 (ろうじん) — elderly person

## Meaning

老人 means an old or elderly person. It does not specify a man rather than a woman,
and the noun alone does not supply an exact age cutoff.

- 公園で老人が犬と歩いていました。
  (こうえんでろうじんがいぬとあるいていました。)
  — An elderly person was walking with a dog in the park.
- その老人は毎朝ここで新聞を読みます。
  (そのろうじんはまいあさここでしんぶんをよみます。)
  — That elderly person reads the newspaper here every morning.

## How the kanji combine

[老](../kanji/老.md), "old age; grow old," qualifies [人](../kanji/人.md), "person."
This is a description-plus-person compound. The on readings are ロウ + ジン.
The meaning concerns the person's age, not how long you have known them.

## Synonyms and antonyms

高齢者 (こうれいしゃ) also means an elderly person.
若者 (わかもの), "young person," is an age-group contrast, not a synonym.
[古い](古い.md) (ふるい) describes old things; it is not the ordinary adjective to substitute
for a person's advanced age.

## Distinctive meaning

English "old friend" may mean a long-standing friend without saying that the friend is elderly.
老人 specifically concerns old age, so it cannot supply that relationship meaning by itself.
Likewise, the presence of 人 does not mean "male": the word's focus is age, not gender.
