# 不利 (ふり) - disadvantageous; unfavorable

## Meaning

不利 describes a disadvantage or an unfavorable position. It is a noun and
な-adjective: 不利な条件 (ふりなじょうけん), "unfavorable conditions."
Use **person に不利だ** to identify who is disadvantaged, and 不利になる
for becoming disadvantaged.

- このルールは初心者に不利です。 (このルールはしょしんしゃにふりです。) - This rule puts beginners at a disadvantage.
- 不利な状況でも、最後まで頑張りました。 (ふりなじょうきょうでも、さいごまでがんばりました。) - Even in an unfavorable situation, we kept trying until the end.

に identifies the affected side; it does not mark the rule's author.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) contributes negation, and [利](../kanji/利.md) contributes
benefit or advantage. The first negates the second: a lack of advantage or
an unfavorable position. The on readings combine as ふ + り.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[有利](有利.md) (ゆうり), "advantageous; favorable," is the direct counterpart.
[不便](不便.md) (ふべん), "inconvenient," concerns practical difficulty and
is not identical: a rule may be easy to use yet favor one side unfairly.
Likewise, unfavorable does not automatically mean illegal.

## Distinctive meaning

不利 evaluates conditions relative to a person's aim or another side.
It does not state the final result: someone can win despite a disadvantage.
Clarifying who is affected avoids ambiguity, since the same arrangement may
be 不利 to one participant and 有利 to another. For "disadvantageously,"
use 不利に rather than an い-adjective ending.
