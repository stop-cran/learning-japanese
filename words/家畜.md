# 家畜 (かちく) - livestock; domestic animals

## Meaning

家畜 is a noun for domesticated animals kept by people, especially in farming.
The particular animals and their purpose depend on context; the word is not
limited to cattle.

- この農場では家畜を飼っています。 (こののうじょうではかちくをかっています。) - Livestock are raised on this farm.
- 牛や豚は家畜です。 (うしやぶたはかちくです。) - Cattle and pigs are domesticated farm animals.

With [飼う](飼う.md), keep or raise an animal, 家畜 takes を. It names the animals,
not the farm or the act of breeding them.

## How the kanji combine

[家](../kanji/家.md) contributes home or household, and [畜](../kanji/畜.md)
concerns domesticated animals. The first component qualifies the second:
animals kept by people, rather than every animal found near a building.
Both use on readings, カ + チク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[牛](牛.md) (うし) names cattle, one kind of 家畜; 家畜 can also include pigs
and other domesticated animals. [動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), animal, is broader.
[ペット](ペット.md) is the ordinary term for a pet and emphasizes a different
relationship with its keeper. These are useful category contrasts, not a
claim that every context sorts animals into mutually exclusive groups.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary includes the English gloss cattle, but do not choose it
automatically: a passage about pigs or several kinds of farm animals needs
the broader livestock or domestic animals. Conversely, 家畜 does not mean
all animals, and merely seeing an animal in a house does not establish the
intended farming or domestication sense.
