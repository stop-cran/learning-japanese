# どっち - which one; which way

## Meaning

どっち asks "which one?", especially between two alternatives, or "which way?"
It is conversational. The alternatives are usually already visible or named.

- お茶とコーヒー、どっちがいい？ (おちゃとコーヒー、どっちがいい？) - Which would you like, tea or coffee?
- 駅はどっちですか。 (えきはどっちですか。) - Which way is the station?

## How the kanji combine

This word is normally written in hiragana. It is the shorter conversational
counterpart of どちら. Rare 何方 spellings are unnecessary for everyday writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どちら is the more polite choice. どれ asks which item from a set, often
three or more, while どこ asks for a location rather than a direction.
こっち, そっち, and あっち are corresponding pointing expressions.

## Distinctive meaning

Because it is a pronoun, どっち takes particles: どっちを買う？
(どっちをかう？), "Which one will you buy?"
Before an explicit noun, use の, as in どっちの道 (どっちのみち),
"which road." Adding です makes a sentence polite but retains the casual
flavor of どっち itself.
