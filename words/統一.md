# 統一 (とういつ) - unification; uniformity

## Meaning

統一 is unification, unity, or making things consistent.
統一する is transitive: Nを統一する means to unify or standardize N.
に can identify the standard adopted.

- 書類の形式を統一しました。 (しょるいのけいしきをとういつしました。) - We standardized the document format.
- 表記を一つの方式に統一しました。 (ひょうきをひとつのほうしきにとういつしました。) - We made the notation consistent with one system.

統一された形式 (とういつされたけいしき) is a standardized or unified format.
The noun can concern political unity as well as consistency of forms or methods.

## How the kanji combine

統 contributes bringing together or governing a whole, and [一](../kanji/一.md) one.
Together they suggest making a unified whole or a common standard.
The on readings are トウ + イツ; keep the full reading とういつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一致](一致.md) (いっち) can express agreement, correspondence, or unity.
[揃える](揃える.md) (そろえる) can mean making things uniform.
[合同](合同.md) (ごうどう) can concern a merger or joint undertaking,
but doing something jointly does not automatically standardize all the participants' practices.

## Distinctive meaning

統一 can bring distinct parts together or make their treatment consistent;
the relevant dimension must come from context.
Simply [集める](集める.md) (あつめる), "collecting," need not produce unity.
Do not treat an English gloss such as "compatible" as evidence that 統一 is an unrestricted adjective for equipment compatibility.
