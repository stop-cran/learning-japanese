# 場所 (ばしょ) — place, location

## Meaning

場所 is a place or location: where something is or where an activity happens. It can also mean the space available for something.

- ここは静かな場所です。 (ここはしずかなばしょです。) — This is a quiet place.
- 会う場所を決めましょう。 (あうばしょをきめましょう。) — Let's decide where to meet.

## How the kanji combine

[場](../kanji/場.md) ば and 所 しょ both contribute the idea of a place. They reinforce that meaning rather than naming two separate places.

The reading is **mixed kun+on**: native ば + on しょ, a pattern called *yutoyomi*. The app's `wago` category here groups a native-first mixed word; it does not mean that both readings are kun-readings. 所 has no card in the current deck.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- ところ (also 所) is another everyday word for a place, but it also has grammatical uses that 場所 cannot replace.
- 会場 かいじょう is the venue of an event, not any location.
- There is no general direct antonym of "place."

## Distinctive meaning

場所 is a neutral, concrete choice when identifying a location or finding space. It does not by itself say whether that place is a building, an outdoor spot or a seat. In sumo contexts it can also name a tournament; that specialized sense should not be imported into ordinary location sentences.
