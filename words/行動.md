# 行動 (こうどう) - action; behavior

## Meaning

行動 is action, conduct, or behavior. As an intransitive する-verb, 行動する means to act or take action. It can describe one person's conduct or people moving and acting together.

- よく考えてから行動してください。 (よくかんがえてからこうどうしてください。) - Please think carefully before you act.
- 旅行中は三人で行動しました。 (りょこうちゅうはさんにんでこうどうしました。) - During the trip, the three of us went around together.

The noun also works in 行動を起こす (こうどうをおこす), "take action." Here 起こす supplies the transitive verb and 行動 is its object.

## How the kanji combine

[行](../kanji/行.md) contributes going or carrying out, and [動](../kanji/動.md) contributes movement. Their related action meanings reinforce each other. Read コウ + ドウ, with a long vowel in each part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[活動](活動.md) (かつどう) often names an activity or a continuing field of effort. 動作 (どうさ) focuses on a movement or operation. 行動 can focus more broadly on what someone does and how they behave.

## Distinctive meaning

行動 is not limited to moving from place to place: contacting a friend or reporting a problem can count as action. Words or plans alone do not necessarily establish that the proposed action occurred. 言葉ではなく行動で示す (ことばではなくこうどうでしめす) means "show it through actions, not words."
