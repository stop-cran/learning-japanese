# 仮名 (かな) - kana; Japanese syllabaries

## Meaning

仮名, read かな here, names kana: hiragana and katakana. The word can also be written in hiragana as かな.

- 平仮名と片仮名は、どちらも仮名です。 (ひらがなとかたかなは、どちらもかなです。) - Hiragana and katakana are both kana.
- この辞書では、漢字の読みを仮名で示しています。 (このじしょでは、かんじのよみをかなでしめしています。) - This dictionary gives the readings of kanji in kana.

The script category is separate from the choice to write its name as 仮名 or かな.

## How the kanji combine

仮 contributes provisional or borrowed status, and [名](../kanji/名.md) naming. The native term かな is a contraction of かりな; learn 仮名/かな as a unit, not as a freely reusable 仮 = か plus 名 = な reading rule. The rare spelling 仮字 and older 假名 belong to this same かな entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

平仮名 (ひらがな) and [片仮名](片仮名.md) (かたかな) name the two kana syllabaries. [漢字](漢字.md) (かんじ) names kanji; it is a contrasting writing category, not a synonym. [文字](文字.md) (もじ), a written character or writing, is broader than either category.

## Distinctive meaning

The same spelling 仮名 also writes a different word read かめい, meaning an assumed name or alias. That meaning does not belong to the かな script word. [送り仮名](送り仮名.md) (おくりがな) are kana endings after kanji, and [仮名遣い](仮名遣い.md) (かなづかい) concerns kana spelling conventions.
