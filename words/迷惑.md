# 迷惑 (めいわく) - nuisance; inconvenience

## Meaning

迷惑 is trouble, a nuisance, or inconvenience experienced by someone.
It is a noun and な-adjective: 迷惑な電話 (めいわくなでんわ),
"a nuisance call." The key expression **person に迷惑をかける** means
"cause someone trouble."

- 夜中に大きな音を出すと、近所の人に迷惑をかけます。 (よなかにおおきなおとをだすと、きんじょのひとにめいわくをかけます。) - Making loud noises late at night causes trouble for the neighbors.
- 隣の部屋の騒音に迷惑しています。 (となりのへやのそうおんにめいわくしています。) - I am being bothered by the noise from the next room.

迷惑する means **be inconvenienced**, not "inconvenience someone."

## How the kanji combine

[迷](../kanji/迷.md) contributes being lost or confused, and 惑 contributes
perplexity or bewilderment. Their related ideas combine, but the modern
word here names unwanted trouble, not simply uncertainty about directions.
The on readings are めい + わく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[面倒](面倒.md) (めんどう) can concern trouble or a troublesome task.
邪魔 (じゃま) can mean obstruction or being in the way.
[困る](困る.md) (こまる) describes being troubled. They overlap but place
different emphasis on the task, obstruction, or affected person.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the two sides apart: 迷惑をかける causes inconvenience, while
迷惑する experiences it. ご迷惑をおかけしてすみません
(ごめいわくをおかけしてすみません) means "I'm sorry for causing you trouble."
The inconvenience need not be deliberate, so the word does not establish
someone's intention to annoy.
