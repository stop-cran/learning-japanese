# カロリー (カロリー) - calorie

## Meaning

カロリー is a calorie, a unit of energy. In everyday food talk it also refers to food energy or calorie content.

- このお菓子はカロリーが高いです。 (このおかしはカロリーがたかいです。) - This snack is high in calories.
- 袋には「一個当たり100 kcal」と書いてあります。 (ふくろには「いっこあたりひゃくキロカロリー」とかいてあります。) - The bag says "100 kcal per piece."

一個当たり identifies the amount per piece, not necessarily the amount in the whole bag.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana word with no kanji to divide. Keep the final long vowel: カロリー. キロカロリー is "kilocalorie," written kcal; 1 kcal equals 1,000 cal.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[エネルギー](エネルギー.md) means energy, including food energy. カロリー names an energy unit or food-energy amount, not every scientific use of energy. [活力](活力.md) (かつりょく) concerns vitality; it is not a technical unit.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the actual unit and the stated serving or piece size on a label. The word is not the food's weight and does not itself tell you how much someone should eat. カロリーが低い, "low in calories," contrasts with 高い in a particular comparison.
