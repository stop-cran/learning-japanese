# 廉価 (れんか) — low price; inexpensive

## Meaning

廉価 describes a low or moderate price. Use 廉価な before a noun, or 廉価で to say at what price something is offered.

- 学校では、辞書を廉価で販売している。 (がっこうでは、じしょをれんかではんばいしている。) — The school sells dictionaries at a low price.
- 旅行には廉価なカメラを持っていった。 (りょこうにはれんかなかめらをもっていった。) — I took an inexpensive camera on the trip.

## How the kanji combine

[廉](../kanji/廉.md) (れん) expresses low cost here; [価](../kanji/価.md) (か) means price or value. 廉 in 清廉 (せいれん), “upright; honest,” has a different sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[安い](安い.md) (やすい) is the corresponding い-adjective: 安いカメラ, not 安いなカメラ. [高価](高価.md) (こうか), expensive, contrasts with 廉価.

## Distinctive meaning

廉価 concerns the price, not whether the item works well. It also does not say that an original price was reduced: [割引](割引.md) (わりびき) specifically refers to a discount.
