# 恋しい (こいしい) - longed for; missed

## Meaning

恋しい is an i-adjective describing someone or something one longs for
or misses. The person, place, or thing longed for can take が.

- 海外にいると、家族が恋しくなります。 (かいがいにいると、かぞくがこいしくなります。) - When I am overseas, I begin to miss my family.
- 故郷の料理が恋しいです。 (ふるさとのりょうりがこいしいです。) - I miss the food from my hometown.

The adverbial form is 恋しく (こいしく), as in 恋しくなる,
"come to miss / long for." Before a noun, use 恋しい directly:
恋しい故郷 (こいしいふるさと), "my longed-for hometown."

## How the kanji combine

[恋](../kanji/恋.md) carries longing or affection, followed by しい.
Learn the native adjective reading こいしい as a whole.
It is not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[憧れる](憧れる.md) (あこがれる) can express yearning or admiration.
[好き](好き.md) (すき) expresses liking, and [愛する](愛する.md)
(あいする) expresses love. Affection can accompany 恋しい, but
neither liking nor love by itself requires longing for an absent person or thing.

## Distinctive meaning

恋しい is not restricted to romance, and it need not concern only a
place one has previously visited. English "dear" or "beloved" can work
when longing is present; do not use those glosses to erase that nuance.
