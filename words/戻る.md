# 戻る (もどる) — go back

## Meaning

戻る means to return or go back. This article starts with returning to a place:
**person が + destination に + 戻る**. The person returns; the verb does not take the thing being put back as an object.

- すぐに部屋に戻ります。 (すぐにへやにもどります。) — I will go back to the room right away.
- 先生はもう教室に戻りました。 (せんせいはもうきょうしつにもどりました。) — The teacher has already returned to the classroom.

The past form is 戻った (もどった); the negative is 戻らない (もどらない).

## How the kanji combine

[戻](../kanji/戻.md) contributes the idea of returning to an earlier place or condition.
The written る is the ending of a native verb, not another component of a compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[帰る](帰る.md) (かえる) also means returning, especially going home or back to one's base.
戻る emphasizes going back to where one was; that location need not be home.
Both may describe a return home, depending on the perspective.

戻す (もどす), "put back," is the useful transitive partner: 本を戻す (ほんをもどす) means "put the book back."
It is not the verb for the book's own return.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the subject and object separate: 私が戻る (わたしがもどる), "I go back,"
contrasts with 私が本を戻す (わたしがほんをもどす), "I put the book back."
English uses "return" for both patterns, but Japanese distinguishes them.
