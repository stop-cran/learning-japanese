# 星 (ほし) -- star

## Meaning

星 is a star or star-like heavenly body seen in the sky. In everyday language it can also include a visible planet, rather than making a strict astronomical classification.

今夜は星がよく見えます。 (こんやはほしがよくみえます。) -- The stars are clearly visible tonight.

The noun does not itself mark singular or plural. 星が一つ (ほしがひとつ) specifies one star.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 星 has the native reading ほし. Its visible internal parts are not separate spoken elements in this word. The on reading せい occurs in compounds such as 惑星 (わくせい), planet.

## Synonyms and antonyms

恒星 (こうせい) is the astronomical term for a star that emits its own light. 惑星 identifies a planet. 月 (つき), the moon, is related sky vocabulary rather than an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The everyday word is less technical than the English scientific distinction between stars and planets. Calling a bright point in the night sky 星 does not by itself identify which kind of object it is.
