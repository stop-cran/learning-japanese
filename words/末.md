# 末 (すえ) - end; after a process

## Meaning

末 is a noun read すえ, meaning an end or tip, including the end of a period. It also appears in constructions meaning "after" a process or effort.

- 今月の末に引っ越します。 (こんげつのすえにひっこします。) - I will move at the end of this month.
- よく考えた末に、留学を決めました。 (よくかんがえたすえに、りゅうがくをきめました。) - After thinking it over carefully, I decided to study abroad.

For the latter meaning, use a past-form clause + 末に or a noun + の末に, as in 話し合いの末に (はなしあいのすえに), "after discussion."

## How the kanji combine

[末](../kanji/末.md) is the whole written word here, with the native reading すえ. Its "end" meaning connects a spatial extremity with a temporal endpoint. Compounds can instead use マツ, as in 月末 (げつまつ), "month-end."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[終わり](終わり.md) (おわり) broadly means an end or finish. [後](後.md) (あと) marks later time without necessarily highlighting an intervening process. [始め](始め.md) (はじめ) contrasts with the end of a period.

## Distinctive meaning

末に presents an outcome reached after the preceding process; it is not just a replacement for every "then." The source also lists "powder," but that belongs to the separate 末/まつ identity, not this すえ entry. That different-reading contrast is not an additional N3 assignment on this card.
