# 部品 (ぶひん) - parts; components; accessories

## Meaning

部品 means parts or components used in a machine, device or other assembled product. The dictionary's "accessories" gloss belongs to this equipment/component setting, not jewelry or personal adornment in general.

- 車の部品を交換しました。 (くるまのぶひんをこうかんしました。) - I replaced a part of the car.
- この部品がないと、機械は動きません。 (このぶひんがないと、きかいはうごきません。) - Without this component, the machine will not work.

## How the kanji combine

[部](../kanji/部.md), part or section, qualifies [品](../kanji/品.md), an item: an item forming part of an assembly. The on readings are ブ + ヒン. The public dictionary identifies 部品 as a shortening of 部分品 (ぶぶんひん).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[部分](部分.md) (ぶぶん) is a broader part or portion, including a region of a whole rather than a separate component. [要素](要素.md) (ようそ) is an element or factor, and [成分](成分.md) (せいぶん) a constituent or ingredient. Their English "part/component" labels can overlap without making every use interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

A replacement component is a useful 部品 scene. Do not automatically use it for every section of a text, every body part or every chemical ingredient. Japanese does not require an English plural ending: context tells you whether one part or several parts are meant.
