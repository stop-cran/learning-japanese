# ところが (ところが) - however; contrary to expectation

## Meaning

ところが is a conjunction introducing a result or fact that runs against the expectation raised by the preceding statement.
As a sentence connector, it comes before the contrasting statement: "However, ..." or "Yet ..."
It does not conjugate.

- 店は開いていると思いました。ところが、休みでした。 (みせはあいているとおもいました。ところが、やすみでした。) - I thought the shop was open. However, it was closed.
- 十分に練習しました。ところが、本番で失敗しました。 (じゅうぶんにれんしゅうしました。ところが、ほんばんでしっぱいしました。) - I practiced thoroughly. Yet I made a mistake in the actual performance.

Each second sentence reverses an expectation rather than merely adding an unrelated fact.

## How the kanji combine

The conjunction is usually written in kana; JMdict also records 所が.
Learn the complete connector ところが rather than translating ところ as a physical place and が as a subject marker in these sentences.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しかし](しかし.md) also introduces contrast.
ところが particularly helps present an unexpected turn in a narrative.
[ところで](ところで.md) can change the topic: it is not a freely interchangeable spelling of this conjunction.
[だから](だから.md), "therefore," instead presents a consequence that follows from the preceding information.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the connector for an actual contrary development in the pattern taught here.
An isolated English gloss such as "even if" does not establish that ところが can replace any hypothetical concessive construction.
Likewise, "while" is misleading if it suggests two simultaneous activities rather than a contrast in expectations.
