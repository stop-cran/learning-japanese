# 待ち合わせる (まちあわせる) - meet by arrangement; arrange to meet

## Meaning

待ち合わせる means to meet at a prearranged place and time, or arrange to
meet. It is ichidan: 待ち合わせます, 待ち合わせた, 待ち合わせない.
The dictionary records both transitive and intransitive uses.

- 三時に駅の前で友達と待ち合わせます。 (さんじにえきのまえでともだちとまちあわせます。) - I am meeting a friend in front of the station at three.
- 私たちは図書館で待ち合わせることにしました。 (わたしたちはとしょかんでまちあわせることにしました。) - We decided to meet at the library.

In this useful pattern, 人と marks the other person, 場所で the meeting
place, and 時刻に the time.

## How the kanji combine

[待](../kanji/待.md) supplies まち, associated with waiting, and
[合](../kanji/合.md) supplies あわ in 合わせる.
The established native compound is まちあわせる, not a string of on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会う](会う.md) (あう) is broader and can describe a planned meeting or
an encounter. [出会う](出会う.md) (であう) often concerns a chance or
significant encounter. [待つ](待つ.md) (まつ) means wait; one can wait
for a train without arranging to meet another person.

## Distinctive meaning

The prior arrangement matters. This is not simply a synonym for every
gathering or discussion. [打ち合わせ](打ち合わせ.md) (うちあわせ)
is preparatory discussion or advance coordination, not another spelling
of this verb.
