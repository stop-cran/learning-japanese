# 嫡子 (ちゃくし) — heir to a house; legitimate child

## Meaning

In a family history, 嫡子 can identify the heir to the house. JMdict gives a second sense, “legitimate child.” Digital Daijisen illustrates this distinction in terms of a child born to the principal wife and refers readers to 嫡出子 (ちゃくしゅつし), a child born to married parents. Describing someone's parentage does not say who eventually inherited.

- 次男が嫡子に選ばれ、家を継いだ。 (じなんがちゃくしにえらばれ、いえをついだ。) — The second son was chosen as heir and succeeded to the house.
- 彼は王の嫡子として生まれたが、王位は継がなかった。 (かれはおうのちゃくしとしてうまれたが、おういはつがなかった。) — He was born the king's legitimate child but did not succeed to the throne.

## How the kanji combine

[嫡](../kanji/嫡.md) is ちゃく here, referring to the principal family line. [子](../kanji/子.md) is し, “child.” Learn ちゃくし as a whole, not as ちゃくこ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[嗣子](嗣子.md) (しし) names an heir, so it fits the succession sense, not the separate parentage sense. [相続](相続.md) (そうぞく) means inheritance or succession, the process rather than the person.

## Distinctive meaning

The heir was often an eldest son, but “eldest son” alone does not translate both senses. Read for whether the passage is about succession or parentage; do not infer present-day legal rights from a historical title.
