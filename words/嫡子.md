# 嫡子 (ちゃくし) — heir to a house; legitimate child

## Meaning

In a family history, 嫡子 can identify the heir to the house. A separate dictionary sense is “legitimate child,” referring to parentage within marriage. In historical accounts this can mean a child born to the principal wife. It need not identify who succeeded to the house.

- 次男が嫡子に選ばれ、家を継いだ。 (じなんがちゃくしにえらばれ、いえをついだ。) — The second son was chosen as heir and succeeded to the house.
- この系図では、正妻の子を「嫡子」と呼んでいる。 (このけいずでは、せいさいのこを「ちゃくし」とよんでいる。) — In this family tree, the children of the principal wife are called *chakushi*.

## How the kanji combine

[嫡](../kanji/嫡.md) is ちゃく here, referring to the principal family line. [子](../kanji/子.md) is し, “child.” Learn ちゃくし as a whole, not as ちゃくこ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[嗣子](嗣子.md) (しし) overlaps in the sense “heir”; it need not specify who the mother was. [相続](相続.md) (そうぞく) means inheritance or succession, the process rather than the person.

## Distinctive meaning

The heir was often an eldest son, but “eldest son” alone does not translate both senses. Read for whether the passage is about succession or parentage; do not infer present-day legal rights from a historical title.
