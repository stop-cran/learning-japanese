# 等しい (ひとしい) - equal; equivalent

## Meaning

等しい is an い-adjective meaning equal or equivalent. It compares quantities, values, or effects. Use AとBは等しい, "A and B are equal," or AはBに等しい, "A is equivalent to B."

- この二つの線は長さが等しいです。 (このふたつのせんはながさがひとしいです。) - These two lines are equal in length.
- その行為は、約束を破ることに等しいです。 (そのこういは、やくそくをやぶることにひとしいです。) - That action is equivalent to breaking a promise.

等しく分ける (ひとしくわける) means "divide equally." The negative is 等しくない (ひとしくない).

## How the kanji combine

[等](../kanji/等.md) supplies the equality idea; しい completes the native adjective. Learn the whole reading ひとしい rather than substituting the on reading トウ used in compounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ) means the same more broadly. 等しい is useful when specifying equality in a relevant property rather than claiming two things are identical in every respect.

[平等](平等.md) (びょうどう) often concerns equality of treatment or status. [違う](違う.md) (ちがう), "differ," provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Equivalence can be an evaluation, not just a mathematical measurement. In the second example, the speaker judges an action to have essentially the same significance as breaking a promise. State the property being compared when necessary: equal length does not imply equal color or material.
