# 実力 (じつりょく) - real ability; force

## Meaning

実力 is actual ability, competence, or true strength: what someone or something can really do, rather than reputation or appearances alone. It is a noun.

- 試合で実力を発揮しました。
  (しあいでじつりょくをはっきしました。) - I showed my real ability in the match.
- 自分の実力を知ることが大切です。
  (じぶんのじつりょくをしることがたいせつです。) - It is important to know your actual ability.

実力がある (じつりょくがある) means "have real ability"; 実力をつける (じつりょくをつける) means "develop one's ability."

A second sense is physical or coercive **force**, including armed force. 実力で阻止する (じつりょくでそしする) means "stop/prevent by force"; 実力行使 (じつりょくこうし) is the exercise or use of force. The surrounding action distinguishes this from succeeding through competence.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality and [力](../kanji/力.md) ability or power. Together they emphasize real, effective capacity. The on readings are ジツ + リョク: じつりょく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[能力](能力.md) (のうりょく) is ability or capacity more broadly. [才能](才能.md) (さいのう) concerns talent, and [腕](腕.md) (うで) can mean skill as well as a physical arm. These overlap with the competence sense, but context determines the particular ability.

[実績](実績.md) (じっせき) is an actual record of results. A record can provide evidence of 実力, but the past results and the ability itself are not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

実力 does not necessarily mean outstanding ability: 実力不足 (じつりょくぶそく) means insufficient ability. The source gloss "efficiency" belongs to the competence sense, not a universal replacement for a process-efficiency measure. Its "arms" gloss belongs to force or weaponry, not the limbs called [腕](腕.md).
