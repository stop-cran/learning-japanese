# 家事 (かじ) - housework; domestic chores

## Meaning

家事 is a noun for housework or domestic chores, such as cleaning, cooking, and laundry. **家事をする** means "do housework"; **家事を分担する** (かじをぶんたんする) means "share the household chores."

- 家族で家事を分担しています。 (かぞくでかじをぶんたんしています。) - We share the household chores as a family.
- 今日は家事に二時間かかりました。 (きょうはかじににじかんかかりました。) - Housework took two hours today.

The second example uses に to mark the activity that takes time. 家事 can also refer more broadly to household or family matters.

## How the kanji combine

[家](../kanji/家.md), "house; household," qualifies [事](../kanji/事.md), "matter; activity." カ + ジ gives かじ: things that concern the household. The compound is not restricted to repairing the physical building.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) is work or a job and can include many activities outside housework. [家庭](家庭.md) (かてい) names the home or family setting, not the chores themselves.

## Distinctive meaning

家事 identifies the work, not who is expected to do it. Nothing in the noun assigns those tasks to a particular gender or family member. Also distinguish the homophone [火事](火事.md) (かじ), "fire": 家事をする is doing chores, while 火事が起きる describes a fire breaking out.
