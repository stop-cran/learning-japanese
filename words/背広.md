# 背広 (せびろ)

## Meaning

背広 means a business suit, traditionally a man's Western-style jacket and matching trousers.

- 父は仕事に背広を着ていきます。 (ちちは しごとに せびろを きていきます。) — My father wears a suit to work.

Use 着る (きる) for wearing it. 背広の上着 (せびろのうわぎ) specifies the suit jacket.

## How the kanji combine

[背](../kanji/背.md) means back and [広](../kanji/広.md) means wide. The wago grouping follows Encyclopedia Nipponica's preferred explanation from Japanese tailoring terminology for a wide-backed jacket. The origin is not settled here: JMdict labels the spelling ateji and notes possible English connections, including "civil clothes" and "Savile Row." Do not present either borrowing story as established fact.

## Synonyms and antonyms

スーツ is the broader contemporary loanword for a suit. 制服 (せいふく) means a uniform; a business suit is not necessarily a uniform. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

背広 refers to this particular kind of clothing, not every formal outfit or every jacket. In shopping, スーツ is often the more broadly useful term; the source word remains worth recognizing.
