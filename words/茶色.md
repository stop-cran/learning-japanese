# 茶色 (ちゃいろ) - brown

## Meaning

茶色 names the color brown. As a noun, it takes の before another noun and です as a polite predicate.

茶色の靴を買いました。 (ちゃいろのくつをかいました。) - I bought brown shoes.

この犬は茶色です。 (このいぬはちゃいろです。) - This dog is brown.

## How the kanji combine

[茶](../kanji/茶.md), "tea," combines with [色](../kanji/色.md), "color." The reading mixes チャ with native いろ, so the repository uses its on-first mixed category, `kango`. The established color word means brown; it does not imply that every kind of tea has that color.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ブラウン is a loanword for brown, especially in product and fashion descriptions. 茶色い (ちゃいろい) is the related i-adjective: 茶色い靴 also means "brown shoes."

## Distinctive meaning

茶色 and 茶色い use different grammar even when their meaning overlaps. Say 茶色の犬 or 茶色い犬, not 茶色な犬. More specific shade words can distinguish dark or pale browns, but 茶色 is the ordinary broad color name.
