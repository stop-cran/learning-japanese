# 感情 (かんじょう) — emotion; feelings

## Meaning

感情 names emotion or feelings, such as anger or sadness. It is a noun for the emotional experience,
not by itself a statement about which emotion someone has.

- 彼は感情を顔に出しません。 (かれはかんじょうをかおにだしません。) — He does not show his emotions on his face.
- 自分の感情を言葉で伝えるのは難しいです。 (じぶんのかんじょうをことばでつたえるのはむずかしいです。) — It is difficult to put my feelings into words.

The pattern 感情を出す (かんじょうをだす) presents emotion as something expressed;
顔に (かおに) specifies where it becomes visible.
The noun can modify another noun with の: 感情の変化 (かんじょうのへんか), "a change in feelings."

## How the kanji combine

[感](../kanji/感.md) concerns feeling or sensing; [情](../kanji/情.md) contributes the emotion sense.
They reinforce each other in a word for feelings. The on readings are カン + ジョウ.
The circumstances meaning of 情 in other words is not the focus here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

気持ち (きもち) overlaps with "feeling" and can also describe sensations or a state of mind.
[性格](性格.md) (せいかく), "personality," is a different concept: not showing an emotion at one
moment does not by itself tell you someone's overall character.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese does not require a plural ending here; English may use "emotion" or "emotions" depending on
the sentence. The example says the person does not display feelings, not that they lack feelings.
Choose a specific emotion word when the identity of that feeling matters.
