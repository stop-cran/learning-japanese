# だらしない - sloppy; undisciplined; weak-willed

## Meaning

だらしない describes a sloppy appearance, poor self-discipline,
or careless conduct. It can also criticize a lack of resolve
or stamina.

- シャツが出ていて、だらしない格好でした。
  (シャツがでていて、だらしないかっこうでした。) - My shirt was untucked, and I looked sloppy.
- お金にだらしない人には貸したくありません。
  (おかねにだらしないひとにはかしたくありません。) - I do not want to lend money to someone who is irresponsible with it.

It is an i-adjective: だらしない服装 (だらしないふくそう),
"sloppy dress"; だらしなく見える (だらしなくみえる),
"look sloppy"; だらしなかった, "was sloppy."

## How the kanji combine

The kana spelling is primary here, with no written-kanji components.
The dictionary's だらし無い is search-only. Although the word
ends in ない, the dictionary form is not simply the grammatical
negative of an adjective だらしい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[汚い](汚い.md) (きたない) overlaps with untidiness but also
means dirty. [弱い](弱い.md) (よわい) is broader weakness.
[きちんと](きちんと.md), "properly; neatly," supplies a
contrast in appearance or conduct.

## Distinctive meaning

This is a negative judgment, not a neutral report about clothes,
money, or health. Calling a person だらしない can sound harsh.
Nにだらしない identifies the area of poor discipline, as in
お金にだらしない; it is not praise for relaxed flexibility.
The weakness sense is also evaluative, not a medical diagnosis.
