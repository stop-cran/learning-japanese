# 設備 (せつび) - equipment; facilities

## Meaning

設備 is a noun for equipment or facilities provided for a place or activity.
It can refer to a set of installed systems rather than one portable device.
Useful constructions include **設備がある**, "have facilities," and **設備を整える**
(せつびをととのえる), "provide/put facilities in order."

- この病院には新しい設備があります。 (このびょういんにはあたらしいせつびがあります。) - This hospital has new equipment.
- 工場の設備を点検しています。 (こうじょうのせつびをてんけんしています。) - We are inspecting the factory's equipment.

設備する is also a transitive verb meaning to equip or install facilities.
The noun patterns above are useful for describing what a place has and how it is maintained.

## How the kanji combine

設 contributes establishing or installing; [備](../kanji/備.md) contributes furnishing or preparing.
The actions combine into setting up what is needed for use.
The on readings are セツ + ビ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[装置](装置.md) (そうち) identifies a device or apparatus.
[道具](道具.md) (どうぐ) refers to a tool used to do something.
[機械](機械.md) (きかい) is a machine; a machine can form part of a building's 設備.
These are related categories, not interchangeable size labels.

## Distinctive meaning

設備 describes the provisions supporting an activity, such as a hospital's medical equipment or a factory's installations.
The building itself and its 設備 are not necessarily the same thing.
設備が整っている (せつびがととのっている) means that suitable facilities are in place,
not merely that loose objects have been tidied away.
