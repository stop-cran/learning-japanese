# 痛み (いたみ) - pain

## Meaning

痛み is a noun for pain, aching, or soreness. It can also describe emotional
distress. Use a body part plus の to locate the pain, or 痛みがある to say that
pain is present.

- 膝の痛みが続いています。 (ひざのいたみがつづいています。) - The pain in my knee is continuing.
- 大切な人を失う痛みは、簡単には消えません。 (たいせつなひとをうしなういたみは、かんたんにはきえません。) - The pain of losing someone important does not disappear easily.

痛みを感じる (いたみをかんじる) means "feel pain"; 痛みがなくなる means "the
pain goes away."

## How the kanji combine

[痛](../kanji/痛.md) carries the idea of pain. 痛み is a native noun related to
痛む (いたむ), "hurt; ache," with み written in kana. This is not an on-reading
compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[痛い](痛い.md) (いたい) is an adjective: 膝が痛い, "My knee hurts."
痛み makes the sensation a noun that can be described, felt, or relieved.
[苦痛](苦痛.md) (くつう) also describes pain or suffering and can emphasize distress.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict associates pain especially with 痛み, and damage or wear especially with
the same-reading spelling 傷み. These are spelling preferences, not proof of an
absolute division. For ordinary descriptions of a painful knee, 痛み is a clear
choice; do not translate every occurrence of いたみ as physical injury.
