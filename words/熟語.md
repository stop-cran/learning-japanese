# 熟語 (じゅくご) - kanji compound; idiom

## Meaning

熟語 can mean a kanji compound, a word formed by combining kanji. It also means an idiom or idiomatic phrase. The setting tells you whether someone is discussing written compounds or established expressions.

- 「学校」は二字の熟語です。
  (「がっこう」はにじのじゅくごです。) - The word 学校, "school," is a two-character kanji compound.
- 英語の熟語を三つ覚えました。
  (えいごのじゅくごをみっつおぼえました。) - I learned three English idioms.

The first example concerns how a word is formed; the second concerns expressions in English.

## How the kanji combine

As a modern meaning aid, associate 熟, "mature," with an established combination, and [語](../kanji/語.md) with a word or expression. This is an association for the compound, not a claim that its meaning is literally "ripe speech." Read ジュク + ゴ, with a short じゅ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[単語](単語.md) (たんご) is a word considered as a vocabulary unit. A kanji compound can be one 単語 even when it contains several characters. [言葉](言葉.md) (ことば) covers words and expressions more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that every kanji compound is a nonliteral idiom. Nor does a compound's character count tell you how many vocabulary words it contains. 四字熟語 (よじじゅくご) identifies a four-character expression; the broader word 熟語 does not require four characters.
