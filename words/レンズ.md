# レンズ - lens

## Meaning

レンズ is a lens, such as one in a camera or a pair of glasses.
It can also name the crystalline lens in the eye.

- カメラのレンズを拭きました。 (カメラのレンズをふきました。) - I wiped the camera lens.
- この眼鏡のレンズは軽いです。 (このめがねのレンズはかるいです。) - The lenses in these glasses are light.

It is a noun; Japanese need not mark a plural just because the
English translation uses lenses.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword comes from Dutch lens, not an English
source merely because English also has lens.
It has no kanji components and no long-vowel mark.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[眼鏡](眼鏡.md) (めがね) names eyeglasses as a whole,
not just their lenses. [鏡](鏡.md) (かがみ) is a mirror;
[ガラス](ガラス.md) is glass as material.
A lens is an optical element, not a synonym for all glass.
There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

コンタクトレンズ specifies a contact lens.
Not every lens is worn on the eye, and not every camera component
is a レンズ. Keep the final ズ distinct from ス in pronunciation
and spelling.
