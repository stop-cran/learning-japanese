# ちゃっかり — shrewdly making sure

## Meaning

ちゃっかり describes astutely securing an advantage or taking care of one's own interests, often with a hint of cheekiness.

- 彼はちゃっかり窓際の席を取った。
  — He shrewdly made sure to get the window seat.
- みんなで分けるお菓子の中から、彼女はちゃっかり自分の分も確保した。
  — From the snacks everyone was sharing, she cleverly made sure to save a portion for herself.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ちゃっかり emphasizes astute self-interest, often with a playful edge, rather than outright deceit.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can sound admiring or mildly critical depending on context; it does not by itself mean dishonest.
