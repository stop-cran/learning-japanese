# 紐 (ひも) - string; cord

## Meaning

紐 is a noun for string or cord, usually written **ひも**. Useful patterns are **ひもを結ぶ** (ひもをむすぶ), "tie a string," and **ひもで縛る** (ひもでしばる), "tie something up with string."

- 箱をひもで縛りました。 (はこをひもでしばりました。) - I tied the box with string.
- 靴のひもがほどけています。 (くつのひもがほどけています。) - Your shoelace is undone.

The first sentence's で marks the material used. In the second, the lace is the subject of the resulting state.

## How the kanji combine

紐 is one character read ひも. Its thread component helps associate the spelling with a cord; there is no two-kanji compound to divide. Kana spelling represents the same ordinary object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[糸](糸.md) (いと) is thread, while [鎖](鎖.md) (くさり) is a chain of links. All can connect or secure things, but their construction and uses differ. [結ぶ](結ぶ.md) names the tying action.

## Distinctive meaning

There is also a colloquial person sense, especially written **ヒモ**: a man financially dependent on a woman. It can be disparaging; it is not a neutral label for every household with one earner. English "pimp" is too narrow as its general definition. This person sense and the physical cord belong to the same dictionary entry, but context keeps them distinct.
