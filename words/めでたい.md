# めでたい - auspicious; joyous; naive

## Meaning

めでたい is an i-adjective for something happy or auspicious that is
worthy of celebration.

- 今日はめでたい日です。 (きょうはめでたいひです。) - Today is a joyous day.
- 結婚が決まったとは、めでたいですね。 (けっこんがきまったとは、めでたいですね。) - Your marriage has been arranged? That is wonderful news.

It also has a naive or too-trusting sense, often in おめでたい:
そんな話を信じるなんて、おめでたいね。
(そんなはなしをしんじるなんて、おめでたいね。)
- How naive of you to believe a story like that.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. 目出度い and the rare 芽出度い are ateji; 愛でたい is
also recognized. Do not infer the word's meanings by separately translating
eyes, sprouts, or degrees.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[おめでたい](おめでたい.md) shares the celebratory and naive uses.
[うれしい](うれしい.md) describes being pleased or glad; めでたい
often evaluates an occasion or piece of news as celebration-worthy.
[単純](単純.md) (たんじゅん) can overlap in the simple-minded sense.

## Distinctive meaning

めでたい is not the natural replacement for every statement of personal
pleasure. The naive use can be critical or sarcastic, not a compliment
about someone's good luck. Before a noun, use めでたい directly, without な.
