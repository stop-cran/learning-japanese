# 担ぐ (かつぐ) - carry on the shoulder; nominate

## Meaning

担ぐ is a transitive godan verb. Its physical use is to carry something on the shoulder. It also means to put someone forward as a representative or leader, to fool someone, or to be concerned with omens and superstitions.

- 祭りで神輿を担ぎました。 (まつりでみこしをかつぎました。) - We carried a portable shrine on our shoulders at the festival.
- 彼を候補者として担ぎました。 (かれをこうほしゃとしてかつぎました。) - We put him forward as a candidate.
- 縁起を担いで、試験の日には同じ鉛筆を使います。 (えんぎをかついで、しけんのひにはおなじえんぴつをつかいます。) - For good luck, I use the same pencil on exam days.

The past is 担いだ (かついだ), and the negative is 担がない (かつがない). In the fooling sense, 担がれた (かつがれた) can mean "was taken in," not physically carried.

## How the kanji combine

[担](../kanji/担.md) concerns shouldering or taking on a burden; ぐ completes the native verb. Learn かつぐ as the word's reading. The same kanji also appears in the distinct verb 担う (になう), shoulder or bear.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[運ぶ](運ぶ.md) (はこぶ) is carrying or transporting more generally. [持つ](持つ.md) (もつ) includes holding or carrying. Neither specifies the shoulder. 担う overlaps physically and also describes bearing a responsibility; its figurative uses do not all transfer to 担ぐ.

## Distinctive meaning

縁起を担ぐ (えんぎをかつぐ) is about acting with regard to good or bad luck, not lifting a physical object. A representative, a load, and a superstition belong to different established uses; a literal shoulder translation will not fit them all.
