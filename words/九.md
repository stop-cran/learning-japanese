# 九 (きゅう) — nine

## Meaning

九 means **nine**. Learn both **きゅう** and **く**: the reading depends on the expression, not simply the writer's preference.

- 学生が九人います。 (がくせいがきゅうにんいます。) — There are nine students.
- 九時に始まります。 (くじにはじまります。) — It starts at nine o'clock.

きゅう is useful when saying the number on its own. 九月 (くがつ), “September,” and 九時 (くじ), “nine o'clock,” require く in ordinary use.

## How the kanji combine

[九](../kanji/九.md) is one numeral with two important on readings, キュウ and ク. It can also be written with the digit 9. Native forms such as 九つ (ここのつ), “nine things,” must be learned separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

八 (はち) is eight; 十 (じゅう) is ten. Numerals have neighboring values, not useful lexical antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese normally combines a number with a counter suited to what is counted. Do not substitute く for きゅう in every counter phrase. This article teaches both source readings through common fixed combinations rather than treating them as universally interchangeable.
