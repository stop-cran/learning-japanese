# 使う (つかう) — use; spend

## Meaning

使う means to use something, including tools, methods, and language. With time or money it can mean to spend.
The thing used is marked with を.

- このペンを使ってもいいですか。 (このペンをつかってもいいですか。) — May I use this pen?
- 今日はお金をたくさん使いました。 (きょうはおかねをたくさんつかいました。) — I spent a lot of money today.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native verb, not a compound. [使](../kanji/使.md) supplies つか and the use meaning; う is okurigana.
It is a godan verb: 使います (つかいます), 使って (つかって).

## Synonyms and antonyms

使用する (しようする) is a more formal way to say "use."
利用する (りようする) often concerns making use of a service, facility, or opportunity.
There is no single opposite covering every use of 使う; 使わない (つかわない) simply means "not use."

## Distinctive meaning

使う does not mean buying or owning something: a borrowed pen can still be used.
In お金を使う (おかねをつかう), the English verb is normally "spend," not "use up"; using all the money is not implied.
