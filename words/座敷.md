# 座敷 (ざしき) - tatami room; formal Japanese room

## Meaning

座敷 is a tatami-floored Japanese room, especially a room for receiving guests.
It names the room, not an individual tatami mat.

- お客さんを座敷に案内しました。 (おきゃくさんをざしきにあんないしました。) - I showed the guests into the tatami room.
- 座敷でお茶をいただきました。 (ざしきでおちゃをいただきました。) - We had tea in the Japanese-style room.

に marks the destination in the first sentence; で marks the activity's location in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[座](../kanji/座.md) contributes seating, and 敷 contributes spreading or laying something out.
The association is a room with its floor laid with tatami.
ザ + native しき makes an on-first mixed reading, classified as kango in this repository.
It is not two on readings. 坐敷 is a dictionary-listed irregular spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[部屋](部屋.md) (へや) is the general room word.
[客間](客間.md) (きゃくま) foregrounds receiving guests without specifying tatami.
[居間](居間.md) (いま) is a living room; a room's use and flooring are different descriptions.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional sense is a dinner or drinking party in a tatami room, especially one attended by a geisha or maiko.
In 座敷が長引いた (ざしきがながびいた), "The gathering ran long," it is the occasion that lasts.
Do not infer from the room sense that every 座敷 involves such a party or professional entertainers.
