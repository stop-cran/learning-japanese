# 銀 (ぎん) - silver

## Meaning

銀 is silver, the metal. It can also name the silver color or, in a competition, a silver medal. As a noun modifying another noun, it uses の.

- この指輪は銀でできています。 (このゆびわはぎんでできています。) - This ring is made of silver.
- 彼女は大会で銀を取りました。 (かのじょはたいかいでぎんをとりました。) - She won silver at the competition.

In the first sentence, で marks the material. The competition context makes the medal meaning clear in the second.

## How the kanji combine

The single [銀](../kanji/銀.md) supplies the whole noun with the on reading ギン. There is no multi-kanji relationship to analyze. 銀色 (ぎんいろ) explicitly names the color.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[金](金.md) (きん) is gold, another metal and medal category. Gold and silver contrast but are not logical opposites. The loanword シルバー also occurs in material and color descriptions; its other uses need their own context.

## Distinctive meaning

銀の指輪 (ぎんのゆびわ) suggests a silver ring, while 銀色の指輪 explicitly describes its appearance without establishing the material. JMdict also records a historical silver-coin sense; that does not make 銀 the everyday word for any money. In [銀行](銀行.md) (ぎんこう), "bank," learn the complete compound rather than translating the financial institution as a piece of silver.
