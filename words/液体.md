# 液体 (えきたい) - liquid

## Meaning

液体 is a liquid: a substance in a liquid state. It is a noun, not an adjective meaning "wet."

- この液体を瓶に入れてください。 (このえきたいをびんにいれてください。) - Please put this liquid in the bottle.
- 液体が床にこぼれた。 (えきたいがゆかにこぼれた。) - Liquid spilled onto the floor.

## How the kanji combine

[液](../kanji/液.md) identifies liquid; [体](../kanji/体.md) expresses a physical form or state here.
The relation is modifier + head, not a reference to a person's body.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[水](水.md) means water and [油](油.md) means oil. These name substances that can be liquids; 液体 names the broader category,
not one particular substance. A wet object is not therefore itself a liquid.

## Distinctive meaning

液体 does not mean only drinking water. Use it when the liquid state or an unidentified liquid matters.
The noun can modify another noun with の, as in 液体の薬 (えきたいのくすり), "liquid medicine."
