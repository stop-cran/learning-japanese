# 濠 (ほり) - moat

## Meaning

濠, read ほり, names a moat. It is a noun. In a castle setting, 城の濠 (しろのほり) identifies the moat associated with the castle, and 濠に or 濠の周りに locates something in or around it.

- 城の濠に水が流れています。 (しろのほりにみずがながれています。) - Water is flowing in the castle moat.
- 濠の周りを歩きました。 (ほりのまわりをあるきました。) - I walked around the moat.

The first example describes a water-filled moat; its water is supplied by the sentence, not by an added word meaning "river."

## How the kanji combine

濠 is a single character writing the native noun ほり. There is no multi-kanji relationship to divide. JMdict also records 堀 and 壕 under this reading and lexical entry; retain 濠 when recognizing this article's spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[川](川.md) (かわ), "river," and [池](池.md) (いけ), "pond," describe other landscape features. They are comparisons, not synonyms that can replace a castle's moat without changing the description.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary entry also includes ditch and canal senses across its spellings, without hard spelling restrictions. This card foregrounds the source-assigned moat sense. Recognizing an alternative spelling does not justify a rule that every use of ほり must contain water or belong to a castle.
