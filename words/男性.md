# 男性 (だんせい) - man; male

## Meaning

男性 means a man or male person. It is a neutral-to-formal term used in descriptions, surveys, announcements, and ordinary courteous speech.

- 受付に男性が一人います。 (うけつけにだんせいがひとりいます。) - There is a man at reception.
- 男性の先生に聞きました。 (だんせいのせんせいにききました。) - I asked a male teacher.

It is a noun. To modify another noun in the ordinary pattern, use の: 男性の客 (だんせいのきゃく), "a male customer," not 男性な客.

## How the kanji combine

[男](../kanji/男.md) contributes male and [性](../kanji/性.md) sex or a category-defining characteristic. The on readings combine as だん + せい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

男の人 (おとこのひと) is an everyday equivalent. Bare 男 (おとこ) is more direct and can sound blunt when identifying someone. 女性 (じょせい), "woman; female," is the corresponding contrasting term.

## Distinctive meaning

男性 identifies a person or category; it does not itself mean "masculine-looking" or assert a personality trait. In descriptions of some languages, it can additionally name grammatical masculine gender, a separate specialized use.
