# 信ずる (しんずる) - believe; trust

## Meaning

信ずる means to believe something, believe in someone, or place trust in them.
It is the ずる counterpart of [信じる](信じる.md) (しんじる).
The person or claim believed can take を; a proposition can take と.

- 彼の説明を信ずる。 (かれのせつめいをしんずる。) - I believe his explanation.
- 必ず成功すると信じています。 (かならずせいこうするとしんじています。) - I believe that we will definitely succeed.

The second example shows the familiar じ form within the same verb family.
Useful inflected forms include 信じます (しんじます), 信じた (しんじた),
and 信じない (しんじない).

## How the kanji combine

[信](../kanji/信.md) contributes belief or trust with the on reading シン.
ずる supplies the verbal ending. This article groups the on-based verb under
kango; it is not a noun that takes an additional する.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[信用](信用.md) (しんよう) and [信頼](信頼.md) (しんらい) also concern trust.
[思う](思う.md) (おもう) can express a belief or opinion.
[疑う](疑う.md) (うたがう), doubt or distrust, offers a contrasting attitude.
信ずる can also concern religious belief, not only trusting factual reports.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep 信ずる and 信じる distinct as dictionary forms without inventing
different belief-versus-trust meanings for them.
JMdict labels 信ずる a ずる alternative of an ichidan じる verb.
Daijisen classifies it as サ変 and describes 信じる as its ichidan development.
These are different descriptive frameworks, not a regular godan paradigm
with forms such as 信ずります.
