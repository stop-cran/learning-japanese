# はあ (はあ) - well; huh; sigh

## Meaning

はあ is an interjection whose meaning depends strongly on intonation and context. It can acknowledge what someone says, ask "what?", or represent a sigh. These are uses of the same dictionary entry, not interchangeable translations in every exchange.

- 「説明は聞きましたか。」「はあ、聞きました。」 (「せつめいはききましたか。」「はあ、ききました。」) - "Did you hear the explanation?" "Well, yes, I did."
- 「もう一度やって。」「はあ？」 (「もういちどやって。」「はあ？」) - "Do it again." "Huh?"
- はあ、やっと終わった。 (はあ、やっとおわった。) - Sigh... finally finished.

The first response may sound hesitant; the second, with a questioning rise, can sound incredulous or irritated.

## How the kanji combine

There is no kanji decomposition. Kana represents the voiced reaction. はぁ and はー are also dictionary-recorded spellings; the written marks help suggest sound but do not fully specify tone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[はい](はい.md) is a clearer affirmative acknowledgment in many routine exchanges. For a polite request for repetition, もう一度お願いします (もういちどおねがいします), "Please say that again," avoids relying on a potentially abrupt はあ？

## Distinctive meaning

A sigh may express relief, fatigue, or disappointment; surrounding context supplies the emotion. The source vocabulary row has no English gloss, so these dictionary-supported uses are teaching selections, not a recovered account of what the list compiler intended.
