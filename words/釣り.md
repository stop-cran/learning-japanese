# 釣り (つり) - fishing; angling

## Meaning

釣り is a noun meaning fishing or angling. Use 釣りをする for doing the activity and 釣りに行く for going fishing. In the second construction, に marks the purpose of going.

- 日曜日に父と釣りに行きます。 (にちようびにちちとつりにいきます。) - On Sunday I will go fishing with my father.
- 川で釣りをしましたが、魚は釣れませんでした。 (かわでつりをしましたが、さかなはつれませんでした。) - I went fishing in the river, but I didn't catch any fish.

The activity noun does not guarantee a successful catch.

## How the kanji combine

釣 carries the native fishing sense. 釣り is related to the stem of [釣る](釣る.md) (つる), "catch fish by angling." The dictionary also recognizes the spelling 釣 for this noun; this card uses 釣り.

## Synonyms and antonyms

釣る names the action directed at catching a fish, while 釣り names the activity. 漁 (りょう), "fishing; fishery," can cover other ways of catching aquatic animals. [魚](魚.md) (さかな) names the fish, not the pastime.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also has other senses of 釣り, but this article selects fishing as its teaching focus. The community source used 釣 with an empty gloss. Fishing is a supported editorial choice, not evidence that the source assigned all the word's later senses to N3 or that its precise intended sense has been recovered.
