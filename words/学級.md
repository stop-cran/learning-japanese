# 学級 (がっきゅう) - school class group

## Meaning

学級 is an organized school class: a group of pupils or students taught together. It is a noun.

- 私たちの学級には二十八人の生徒がいます。 (わたしたちのがっきゅうにはにじゅうはちにんのせいとがいます。) - There are twenty-eight students in our class.
- 学級で話し合って、遠足の行き先を決めました。 (がっきゅうではなしあって、えんそくのいきさきをきめました。) - We discussed it as a class and decided where to go on our school outing.

学級担任 (がっきゅうたんにん) is the teacher responsible for a class.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) contributes schooling, and 級 a class or grouping. Together they name a class within a school. ガク + キュウ becomes がっきゅう; keep both the small っ and the long ゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[クラス](クラス.md) overlaps in this school-group use, and [組](組.md) (くみ) identifies a class in names such as 三年二組 (さんねんにくみ), class 2 in year 3. [授業](授業.md) (じゅぎょう) is the teaching or lesson; [教室](教室.md) (きょうしつ) can name the physical classroom.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not equate 学級 with an entire [学年](学年.md) (がくねん), school year or grade. One year can contain several class groups. The room, the lesson, and the group of students are related but different referents.
