# 酸性 (さんせい) — acidity; acidic

## Meaning

酸性 is acidity, or the property of being acidic. As a predicate use 酸性だ; before a noun use 酸性の.

この水溶液は酸性だ。（このすいようえきはさんせいだ。） — This aqueous solution is acidic.

液体が酸性かどうかを調べた。（えきたいがさんせいかどうかをしらべた。） — I checked whether the liquid was acidic.

## How the kanji combine

酸 サン contributes acid and [性](../kanji/性.md) セイ contributes nature/property. The head 性 turns the acid-related meaning into a property; it does not mean sex or gender in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

酸（さん） is an acid; 酸性 is acidity or being acidic. アルカリ性（アルカリせい）, alkalinity/alkaline, gives a chemical contrast. [化学](化学.md) かがく is chemistry, the broader field.

## Distinctive meaning

This names a chemical property, not simply the taste adjective 酸っぱい（すっぱい）, sour. 酸性の水溶液（さんせいのすいようえき） means an acidic aqueous solution; do not substitute 酸性な here. [賛成](賛成.md), also さんせい, means agreement/support and is a different word despite the matching pronunciation.
