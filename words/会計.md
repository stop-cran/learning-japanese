# 会計 (かいけい) - accounting; bill

## Meaning

会計 is a noun for accounting or managing financial accounts. Depending on context, it can also mean the person handling the money, a restaurant bill, or settling that bill with 会計する.

- クラブの会計を担当しています。 (クラブのかいけいをたんとうしています。) - I am responsible for the club's accounts.
- お会計をお願いします。 (おかいけいをおねがいします。) - The bill, please.

クラブの会計 can also mean "the club's treasurer" when it identifies a person. レジで会計する means "pay at the register." The surrounding sentence tells you whether 会計 refers to the work, the role, or payment.

## How the kanji combine

[会](../kanji/会.md) has a gathering association, and [計](../kanji/計.md) concerns counting or calculation. Bringing figures together and calculating accounts is a useful meaning-based connection, not a historical derivation from "meeting."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勘定](勘定.md) (かんじょう) overlaps with calculation and paying a bill. [支払](支払.md) (しはらい), "payment," focuses on paying rather than the broader work of accounting. [計算](計算.md) (けいさん) is calculation, including arithmetic unrelated to money.

## Distinctive meaning

At a restaurant, お会計 is a practical polite expression; it does not ask for an accountant. Conversely, a club's 会計 need not be a professionally qualified accountant. Avoid translating the English word "account" mechanically: an online user account is not this accounting sense.
