# 現場 (げんば) - scene; work site

## Meaning

現場 is the actual place where something happens or work is carried out. It can be the scene of an accident or crime, a construction site, or the front line of practical work. It is a noun.

- 警察が事故の現場に来ました。 (けいさつがじこのげんばにきました。) - The police came to the scene of the accident.
- 工事の現場で働いています。 (こうじのげんばではたらいています。) - I work at a construction site.

に marks the destination in the first example; で marks where the work happens in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[現](../kanji/現.md) contributes the actual or present, and [場](../kanji/場.md) contributes a place. The compound names the place of the actual event or activity. Its reading mixes on ゲン with native ば, an on-first mixed reading (jūbakoyomi) filed under `kango` here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[場所](場所.md) (ばしょ) is a general place. 現場 ties the place to a particular event or activity. A [工場](工場.md) (こうじょう) is a factory; it may be someone's work site, but 現場 is not limited to factories.

## Distinctive meaning

現場の声 (げんばのこえ), "voices from the field," means the views of people doing the work directly. The word need not suggest a crime or emergency. The reading taught here is げんば, including the work-site sense; do not confuse it with 現状 (げんじょう), "current situation."
