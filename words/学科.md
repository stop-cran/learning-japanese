# 学科 (がっか) - school subject; university department

## Meaning

学科 is a subject or course of study. It can also name a university department organized around a field.

- 入学試験の学科は学校によって違います。 (にゅうがくしけんのがっかはがっこうによってちがいます。) - The subjects on the entrance examination differ from school to school.
- 兄は大学の機械工学科で学んでいます。 (あにはだいがくのきかいこうがっかでまなんでいます。) - My older brother studies in a university's department of mechanical engineering.

The second example names an organizational unit, not one individual lesson.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) contributes study, and [科](../kanji/科.md) a division or branch. Together they identify an academic branch. ガク + カ becomes がっか, with a small っ; this is not がくか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[科目](科目.md) (かもく) also names a school subject or course. [学部](学部.md) (がくぶ) is a university faculty or division; a 学科 is often a more specific unit within it. [専攻](専攻.md) (せんこう) focuses on a student's major or specialization. These descriptions can concern the same field without naming exactly the same thing.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes the subject from the department. Institutional structures and English translations vary: "department" alone does not establish a universal 学部/学科 hierarchy. Do not confuse this word with [学会](学会.md) (がっかい), an academic society or conference, or [学級](学級.md) (がっきゅう), a school class group.
