# 性能 (せいのう) - performance; capability

## Meaning

性能 is performance or capability, especially how well a machine, tool or device
can do its job. 性能が高い (せいのうがたかい) means high-performance or highly
capable; 性能を比べる means compare performance.

- このカメラは性能がいいです。 (このカメラはせいのうがいいです。) - This camera performs well.
- 二台のパソコンの性能を比べました。 (にだいのパソコンのせいのうをくらべました。) - I compared the performance of two computers.

性能 is a noun, not a verb meaning to perform an action.

## How the kanji combine

[性](../kanji/性.md) contributes a characteristic or property, and [能](../kanji/能.md)
contributes ability. Together they concern the capabilities inherent in the thing.
The on readings are セイ + ノウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[能力](能力.md) (のうりょく) is ability or capacity more broadly, including people.
[機能](機能.md) (きのう) concerns a function or feature: what something does.
性能 readily concerns how well it does it. This is a focus difference, not a rule
that function and performance can never overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

For a person's ordinary skills, 能力 or [実力](実力.md) (じつりょく) is a more useful
starting comparison than treating 性能 as the general word for human ability.
Japanese dictionaries also record an older innate-ability use; the device focus
here is not an absolute historical restriction. A performance evaluation needs
context: 性能 alone does not tell us which metric was measured or whether the result is good.
