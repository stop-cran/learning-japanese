# 正方形 (せいほうけい) - square (geometric shape)

## Meaning

正方形 is a square: a four-sided figure whose sides are all equal and whose
four angles are right angles. It is a noun; 正方形の + noun describes something square.

- 正方形を一つ描いてください。 (せいほうけいをひとつかいてください。) - Please draw one square.
- 正方形の紙を使います。 (せいほうけいのかみをつかいます。) - We use a square sheet of paper.

一辺 (いっぺん) means one side of the figure.

## How the kanji combine

[正](../kanji/正.md) contributes regularity, [方](../kanji/方.md) a square form here,
and [形](../kanji/形.md) shape. The established compound specifies the regular square.
Read セイ + ホウ + ケイ, keeping the long vowels in せい and ほう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[四角](四角.md) (しかく) is a square or four-cornered figure more broadly;
[四角い](四角い.md) (しかくい) is the corresponding adjective.
長方形 (ちょうほうけい) means rectangle and does not by itself require all
four sides to be equal.

## Distinctive meaning

Four corners alone are not enough to establish a 正方形. Nor are four equal sides
enough without right angles: a slanting rhombus need not be a square.
Rotating a square on the page does not change these properties.
