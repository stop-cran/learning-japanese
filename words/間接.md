# 間接 (かんせつ) - indirectness; indirect connection

## Meaning

間接 is indirectness: a connection or influence through something else
rather than directly. It is a noun and can modify a noun with の.
The useful forms 間接的な (かんせつてきな) and
間接的に (かんせつてきに) mean "indirect" and "indirectly."

- この出来事は仕事に間接的な影響を与えました。
  (このできごとはしごとにかんせつてきなえいきょうをあたえました。) - This event had an indirect effect on my work.
- 友人を通じて間接的に知らせを聞きました。
  (ゆうじんをつうじてかんせつてきにしらせをききました。) - I heard the news indirectly through a friend.

間接的な modifies the following noun; 間接的に modifies how an action
or connection occurs. The second example identifies the intermediary.

## How the kanji combine

[間](../kanji/間.md) contributes something intervening, and
[接](../kanji/接.md) contact or connection.
Together they suggest a connection with something in between.
The on readings are カン + セツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[直接](直接.md) (ちょくせつ), "direct," is the central contrast.
本人から直接聞く (ほんにんからちょくせつきく) means hearing directly
from the person concerned, rather than through a friend.

## Distinctive meaning

Indirect does not mean false, weak, or unimportant.
An indirect effect can be substantial, and indirectly received news can
be correct. 間接 describes the route or relationship; other words
are needed to judge the evidence or size of the effect.
