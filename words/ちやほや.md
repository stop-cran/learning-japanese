# ちやほや — pampered with attention

## Meaning

ちやほやする means to lavish someone with attention, praise, or indulgence.

- 新入りの子猫は、家族みんなにちやほやされている。
  — The new kitten is being doted on by the whole family.
- ほめられても、ちやほやされるのは少し苦手だ。
  — Even when people praise me, I am not very comfortable being fussed over.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ちやほや implies unusually abundant attention or praise, which may be welcome or excessive.

## Distinctive meaning

It can describe affectionate pampering or excessive flattery; context determines whether the attention is welcome.
