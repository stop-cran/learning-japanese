# きれい (きれい) - beautiful; clean

## Meaning

きれい describes something beautiful or something clean and tidy. Context supplies the intended meaning.

- きれいな花ですね。 (きれいなはなですね。) - What beautiful flowers.
- 部屋をきれいにしました。 (へやをきれいにしました。) - I cleaned up the room.

Despite its final い, this is a na-adjective: きれいな before a noun, きれいに before an action, and きれいです as a polite predicate. Its negative is きれいではありません, not きれくない.

## How the kanji combine

The word is also written 綺麗. The kana spelling is common and especially useful for the clean or tidy sense. Kana spelling does not make it an i-adjective or change its Sino-Japanese origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

美しい (うつくしい) also means beautiful. 清潔 (せいけつ) emphasizes cleanliness or hygiene. 汚い (きたない), "dirty," contrasts with the clean sense.

## Distinctive meaning

A きれいな部屋 may be clean, attractive, or both. The word alone does not guarantee that something is sterile or medically hygienic. With に, it can also describe doing something neatly or completely, extending beyond physical beauty.
