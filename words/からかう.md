# からかう (からかう) - tease; make fun of

## Meaning

からかう means to tease, kid, or make fun of someone. It is transitive: the person targeted takes を. It may describe playful banter or hurtful mockery; the word alone does not guarantee that the target enjoys it.

- 兄は私の新しい髪形をからかいました。 (あにはわたしのあたらしいかみがたをからかいました。) - My older brother teased me about my new hairstyle.
- からかわないでください。 (からかわないでください。) - Please do not tease me.

It is a godan verb: からかいます, からかって, からかった, からかわない.

## How the kanji combine

The card uses the usual kana spelling. The dictionary also records 揶揄う as a rarely used kanji spelling; it is not necessary to write that form to use the verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いじめる](いじめる.md) means bully or pick on and emphasizes unkind treatment. It can overlap with hurtful teasing. [冗談](冗談.md) (じょうだん) is a joke or jest; telling a joke does not necessarily target anyone. [笑う](笑う.md) (わらう) can also mean laugh at or ridicule.

## Distinctive meaning

The speaker's intention and the other person's reaction can differ. Calling an action からかう does not excuse it as harmless, but neither should every friendly exchange of teasing automatically be described as sustained bullying.
