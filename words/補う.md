# 補う (おぎなう) - make up for; supplement

## Meaning

補う means supplying something missing, making up a shortage or loss,
or supplementing something insufficient. It is a transitive godan verb.
The shortage, lack, or material being supplemented takes を.

- 足りない生活費をアルバイトで補っています。 (たりないせいかつひをアルバイトでおぎなっています。) - I make up the shortfall in living expenses with a part-time job.
- 図を使って説明を補いました。 (ずをつかってせつめいをおぎないました。) - I supplemented the explanation with a diagram.

Forms include 補います (おぎないます), 補わない (おぎなわない),
補った (おぎなった), and 補って (おぎなって).

## How the kanji combine

[補](../kanji/補.md) contributes supplying what is missing.
おぎな is represented by the kanji and う is okurigana.
The compound reading ホ does not replace おぎな in this verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[埋める](埋める.md) (うめる) also covers filling a gap or making up a loss.
[足す](足す.md) (たす) and [加える](加える.md) (くわえる) describe adding;
補う makes the insufficiency or missing part important.
補充 (ほじゅう) is replenishment; 補足 (ほそく) is supplementation.

## Distinctive meaning

The supplement need not be money or a physical patch.
The diagram example adds information that the explanation lacks.
English "compensate" here does not automatically mean paying legal damages:
補償する (ほしょうする) is the relevant comparison for compensation or reparation.
