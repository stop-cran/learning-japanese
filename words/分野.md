# 分野 (ぶんや) - field; area of activity

## Meaning

分野 is a field, sphere, or branch of activity or knowledge. It is a noun. Use Nの分野, "the field of N," and 分野で for the field in which someone works or acts.

- 医学の分野で研究をしています。 (いがくのぶんやでけんきゅうをしています。) - I do research in the field of medicine.
- これは私の専門分野ではありません。 (これはわたしのせんもんぶんやではありません。) - This is not my area of expertise.

専門分野 identifies a specialized field; 分野 alone need not imply that someone is an expert.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) contributes division or a part, and [野](../kanji/野.md) contributes a field or domain. Together they identify a particular domain within a broader range. The on readings are ブン + ヤ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

専門 (せんもん) concerns a specialty; 分野 can describe an area of work whether or not it is anyone's specialty. [畑](畑.md) (はたけ) names a cultivated field in its physical sense, not this ordinary abstract use of 分野.

## Distinctive meaning

分野 does not name an open patch of land simply because English calls it a "field." It organizes topics, research, or activities into areas. 新しい分野 (あたらしいぶんや), "a new field," may be new to the speaker rather than newly invented for everyone.
