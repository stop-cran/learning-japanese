# 集会 (しゅうかい) - meeting; assembly; rally

## Meaning

集会 is a gathering, meeting, assembly, or rally for a shared purpose. It need not be a discussion meeting: listening to announcements or demonstrating support for a cause can also be the purpose.

- 住民の集会に参加しました。
  (じゅうみんのしゅうかいにさんかしました。) - I attended a residents' meeting.
- 公園で平和を願う集会が開かれました。
  (こうえんでへいわをねがうしゅうかいがひらかれました。) - A rally calling for peace was held in the park.

**集会を開く** (しゅうかいをひらく) means "hold a meeting or assembly"; 集会に参加する (しゅうかいにさんかする) means "participate in one." JMdict also records intransitive 集会する, "assemble; gather."

## How the kanji combine

[集](../kanji/集.md) contributes gathering, and [会](../kanji/会.md) meeting. The characters reinforce the gathering idea. Read シュウ + カイ as **しゅうかい**; keep the long しゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会合](会合.md) (かいごう) and [集まり](集まり.md) (あつまり) overlap in "meeting; gathering." [会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) more specifically foregrounds discussing or deliberating on matters; [打ち合わせ](打ち合わせ.md) (うちあわせ) concerns coordinating details. [学会](学会.md) (がっかい) can name an academic meeting as well as an academic society.

A [パーティー](パーティー.md) is a party or social gathering. Its celebratory or social focus is not what 集会 itself specifies. These distinctions do not remove all overlap between the broad English answer labels.

## Distinctive meaning

集会 is the gathering or event, not automatically the group as a permanent institution or the hall where it meets. [集団](集団.md) (しゅうだん) names a group or collective, and [議会](議会.md) (ぎかい) a legislative assembly as a body. An accidental crowd is not necessarily a 集会 with a common purpose.

Use **しゅうかい** here. Separate historical Buddhist readings in the public dictionary are not the practical reading taught by this card.
