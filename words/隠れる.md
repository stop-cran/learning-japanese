# 隠れる (かくれる) - hide; be hidden

## Meaning

隠れる is an intransitive ichidan verb: hide oneself, take cover, or become hidden from view.
The person or thing disappearing takes **が**, and the hiding place or covering can take **に**.
Forms include 隠れた (かくれた), 隠れている (かくれている), and 隠れない (かくれない).

- 子供が木の後ろに隠れました。 (こどもがきのうしろにかくれました。) - The child hid behind a tree.
- 月が雲に隠れています。 (つきがくもにかくれています。) - The moon is hidden by clouds.

The first subject acts deliberately; the second does not. 隠れる itself does not require an intention to hide.

## How the kanji combine

隠 carries the hiding meaning. The native verb is written 隠れる, with れる as its ending.
It is not a passive conjugation simply because English sometimes translates it as "be hidden."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[隠す](隠す.md) (かくす) is transitive: 秘密を隠す (ひみつをかくす), "hide a secret."
[消える](消える.md) (きえる) means disappear, without specifying that something remains behind a covering.
[現れる](現れる.md) (あらわれる), "appear," provides a visibility contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

隠れた才能 (かくれたさいのう), "hidden talent," extends the idea to something not yet recognized.
For a visible scene, distinguish 隠れた, "went out of sight," from 隠れている, "is hidden."
Do not give 隠れる an object with を when you mean deliberately hiding something else; choose 隠す instead.
