# 旅 (たび) - journey; trip

## Meaning

旅 is a noun for a trip, journey, or travel. Useful constructions include 旅に出る (たびにでる), "set off on a journey," and 旅をする (たびをする), "travel; make a journey."

- 来月、一人で旅に出ます。 (らいげつ、ひとりでたびにでます。) - Next month, I will set off on a trip alone.
- 旅の途中で友達に会いました。 (たびのとちゅうでともだちにあいました。) - I met a friend during my journey.

旅の途中 (たびのとちゅう) locates something within the progress of the trip. It does not tell you the means of transport.

## How the kanji combine

[旅](../kanji/旅.md) supplies the whole native noun たび. There is no okurigana and no multi-kanji relationship to split. The on reading りょ appears in related compounds, rather than replacing たび in these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) also means travel or a trip and has the convenient verb 旅行する. Both words can describe the same outing. A journey need not be long, solitary, or emotionally transformative simply because it is called 旅.

## Distinctive meaning

旅 presents the trip as an experience or course of travel and combines readily with words such as 途中, "on the way." Context supplies its purpose, length, and destination. Distinguish the noun from 度 (たび), "occasion; each time," which shares the sound but not the travel meaning.
