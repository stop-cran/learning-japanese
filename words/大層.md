# 大層 (たいそう) - very; grand; exaggerated

## Meaning

大層 is an adverb meaning "very" or "greatly," and a な-adjective for
something grand, considerable, or exaggerated. Kana たいそう is usual;
大そう is also recorded.

- 祖母はたいそう喜びました。 (そぼはたいそうよろこびました。) - My grandmother was very pleased.
- 大層な贈り物をいただきました。 (たいそうなおくりものをいただきました。) - I received a splendid gift.
- 大層な人数が集まりました。 (たいそうなにんずうがあつまりました。) - A great many people gathered.

The adverb directly modifies 喜びました. Before 贈り物 or 人数, the
adjective-like use takes な. Do not infer the same attachment pattern from
the English words "very" and "grand."

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes large degree; [層](../kanji/層.md) supplies
the established ソウ component of this word. Learn タイ + ソウ as the degree
expression たいそう. It does not describe physical layers, and this
association is not a historical derivation of the modern meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とても](とても.md), [非常に](非常に.md) (ひじょうに), and
[大変](大変.md) (たいへん) overlap with the degree use.
[立派](立派.md) (りっぱ) overlaps with the splendid evaluation;
[オーバー](オーバー.md) overlaps when the intended meaning is exaggerated.
Those overlaps do not transfer all of each word's other senses to 大層.

## Distinctive meaning

大層 can sound more formal or old-fashioned than everyday とても.
ご大層な話 (ごたいそうなはなし), "a grandiose story," can be ironic,
but ordinary 大層 is not always sarcastic. It can genuinely praise a gift
or report strong pleasure. Use context and tone rather than treating
ご大層 as a universal polite compliment.
