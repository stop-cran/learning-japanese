# 工業 (こうぎょう) — manufacturing industry

## Meaning

工業 means industry in the sense of manufacturing: processing materials and making products, especially in factories.

この町では工業が盛んです。 (このまちではこうぎょうがさかんです。) — Manufacturing is thriving in this town.

## How the kanji combine

[工](../kanji/工.md) relates to making or working on things, and [業](../kanji/業.md) to a trade or business. The on readings are コウ + ギョウ. Keep both long vowels in こうぎょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

産業 (さんぎょう) is broader: an industry or economic sector, including activities outside manufacturing. 商業 (しょうぎょう) concerns commerce and trade. 農業 (のうぎょう) is agriculture, a contrasting sector rather than an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "industry" is not always 工業: the tourism industry is not manufacturing. 工場 (こうじょう) names a factory, while 工業 names the industrial activity or sector. Use 工業の before a following noun when expressing a general connection to manufacturing.
