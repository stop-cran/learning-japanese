# コンセント - electrical outlet; socket

## Meaning

コンセント is an electrical outlet or socket: the connection that receives a plug.

- この部屋にはコンセントが二つあります。 (このへやにはコンセントがふたつあります。) - This room has two electrical outlets.
- プラグをコンセントに差し込んでください。 (プラグをコンセントにさしこんでください。) - Please insert the plug into the outlet.

In the second example, を marks the plug being inserted and に marks the socket receiving it.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana word, not a kanji compound. JMdict records the electrical borrowing as Japanese-made English from **concent(ric plug)**. That source form does not make コンセント an ordinary Japanese adjective meaning "concentric."

## Synonyms and antonyms

English *outlet*, *socket*, and *power point* can name this connection. プラグ is the plug inserted into it, not the receiving socket. Distinguish the two objects when describing what you insert or unplug.

## Distinctive meaning

There is also a separately recorded borrowing from English **consent**, meaning agreement or consent. Recognize it in インフォームド・コンセント, informed consent after an explanation, especially in medical care. Digital Daijisen records this under a separate heading; it is not an extra sense in the cited current JMdict electrical entry.

Context therefore matters: a room's コンセント is not its permission or agreement. [承知](承知.md) (しょうち) and [賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい) have acceptance/agreement uses, but neither names the electrical socket.
