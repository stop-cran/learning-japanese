# 推薦 (すいせん) - recommendation; endorsement

## Meaning

推薦 is a recommendation or endorsement of a person or thing as suitable.
推薦する is transitive: Xを推薦する means "recommend X."
A role can be marked with に, as in 会長に推薦する (かいちょうにすいせんする), "recommend for chairperson."

- 私は田中さんを会長に推薦します。 (わたしはたなかさんをかいちょうにすいせんします。) - I recommend Tanaka for chairperson.
- 先生からこの本を推薦されました。 (せんせいからこのほんをすいせんされました。) - My teacher recommended this book to me.

The passive in the second sentence presents the recommendation from the recipient's viewpoint.
推薦を受ける (すいせんをうける) means "receive a recommendation."

## How the kanji combine

推 contributes putting forward or supporting, and 薦 recommending.
Together they convey putting someone or something forward for consideration.
The on-readings are スイ + セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勧める](勧める.md) (すすめる) is a broader everyday verb for recommending or encouraging.
[選ぶ](選ぶ.md) (えらぶ) means choosing; recommending a candidate does not mean the candidate has already been selected.
[紹介](紹介.md) (しょうかい) can introduce someone without endorsing them for a role.

## Distinctive meaning

推薦 carries a positive judgment of suitability, but it is not a guarantee of acceptance or success.
In educational or employment contexts, 推薦状 (すいせんじょう) is a letter of recommendation.
Check who recommends whom and for what: the particles make those relationships explicit.
