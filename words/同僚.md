# 同僚 (どうりょう) - coworker; colleague

## Meaning

同僚 is a noun for a coworker or colleague.
It describes a relationship through work, not necessarily a personal friendship.
**会社の同僚** (かいしゃのどうりょう) is a colleague at one's company;
**同僚と** means "with a colleague."

- 同僚と昼ご飯を食べました。 (どうりょうとひるごはんをたべました。) - I had lunch with a colleague.
- 困ったときは同僚に相談します。 (こまったときはどうりょうにそうだんします。) - When I have a problem, I consult a coworker.

The second example uses に for the person consulted.
Japanese does not require a plural ending: context can make 同僚 refer to one colleague or several.

## How the kanji combine

[同](../kanji/同.md) contributes sameness or sharing, while 僚 contributes an associate or fellow worker.
Together they identify people connected by their work.
The on readings ドウ + リョウ give どうりょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) is a companion or member of the same group more broadly.
[友人](友人.md) (ゆうじん) is a friend, whether or not the person works with you.
上司 (じょうし), "superior," specifies a hierarchical relationship instead.

## Distinctive meaning

同僚 presents someone as a fellow worker. It does not establish their exact age,
job title, length of service, or level of friendship.
Avoid treating "same rank" as a rigid requirement derived from 同.
When hierarchy is the relevant point, use the appropriate role word rather than relying on 同僚 to explain it.
