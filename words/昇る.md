# 昇る (のぼる) - rise (sun/moon); be promoted

## Meaning

昇る describes natural rising, such as the sun, and also rising to a higher rank.
It is an intransitive godan verb: 昇ります のぼります, 昇った のぼった, 昇らない のぼらない.

- 日が東から昇ります。 (ひがひがしからのぼります。) - The sun rises in the east.
- 彼は部長の地位に昇りました。 (かれはぶちょうのちいにのぼりました。) - He rose to the position of department manager.

が marks what rises in the first example. に marks the position reached in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[昇](../kanji/昇.md) supplies the rising idea and the reading のぼ; the ending る is written in kana.
Its on reading ショウ belongs to other words, such as 昇格 しょうかく, promotion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上る のぼる shares the natural-rising sense in the dictionary. [登る](登る.md) のぼる instead supplies the physical-climbing
contrast: 山に登る やまにのぼる means climbing a mountain. [上がる](上がる.md) あがる is a broader verb for rising or going up.
The similar English glosses do not license using every spelling in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict assigns natural rising to 上る and 昇る, and promotion to 昇る.
Its physical-climbing sense is assigned to 上る and 登る, not 昇る.
Keep the spelling with the example: the sun rising and a person climbing a mountain are not the same use of のぼる.
