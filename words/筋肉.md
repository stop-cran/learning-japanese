# 筋肉 (きんにく) - muscle

## Meaning

筋肉 is a noun for muscle, whether discussing a particular muscle or muscular tissue generally. Use a body part + の筋肉 to specify where it is.

- 腕の筋肉が痛いです。 (うでのきんにくがいたいです。) - The muscles in my arm hurt.
- 運動で筋肉を鍛えています。 (うんどうできんにくをきたえています。) - I am strengthening my muscles through exercise.

In the first example, が marks what hurts. In the second, を marks what is being trained and で marks the means. 筋肉がつく (きんにくがつく) describes gaining muscle.

## How the kanji combine

筋 contributes the idea of muscle or sinew, and [肉](../kanji/肉.md) means flesh. Together they name muscular tissue. The readings are キン + ニク, producing きんにく. The broader meanings of an individual character do not all become meanings of the compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[肉](肉.md) (にく) is broader flesh or meat, depending on context. [骨](骨.md) (ほね) is bone, a different body structure rather than an antonym. A tendon is 腱 (けん); it should not be treated as the same anatomical object as a muscle.

## Distinctive meaning

Use "muscle" as the core English meaning, not an unrestricted "sinew." 筋肉の痛み (きんにくのいたみ) identifies muscle pain; it does not by itself explain what caused it. The word describes tissue, not a diagnosis or a particular exercise.
