# 嗣子 (しし) — heir; successor

## Meaning

嗣子 is an heir or successor, especially one who is to inherit a family or line.

- 彼は家の嗣子として育てられました。 (かれはいえのししとしてそだてられました。) — He was raised as the heir of the family.
- 嗣子がいなかったため、親族が後継者を決めました。 (ししがいなかったため、しんぞくがこうけいしゃをきめました。) — Since there was no heir, the relatives chose a successor.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[嗣](../kanji/嗣.md) is read シ and [子](../kanji/子.md) is read シ here, giving 嗣子 (しし).

## Synonyms and antonyms

後継者 (こうけいしゃ) is a more general modern word for a successor; 嗣子 is formal and often refers to an heir in a family or line.

## Distinctive meaning

嗣子 is a formal, literary word; 後継者 (こうけいしゃ) is a more general modern term for a successor.
