# 嗣子 (しし) — heir

## Meaning

嗣子 means the child who is to carry on a family, or heir; it is narrower than a general successor.

- 彼は家の嗣子として育てられました。 (かれはいえのししとしてそだてられました。) — He was raised as the heir of the family.
- 嗣子がいなかったため、親族が後継者を決めました。 (ししがいなかったため、しんぞくがこうけいしゃをきめました。) — Since there was no heir, the relatives chose a successor.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[嗣](../kanji/嗣.md) is read シ and [子](../kanji/子.md) is read シ here, giving 嗣子 (しし).

## Synonyms and antonyms

後継者 (こうけいしゃ) is a more general word for someone who succeeds to a role or position; 嗣子 specifically means an heir.

## Distinctive meaning

嗣子 means the child who is to carry on a family; use 後継者 when referring more generally to a successor.
