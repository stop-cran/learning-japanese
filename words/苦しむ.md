# 苦しむ (くるしむ) - suffer; struggle with distress

## Meaning

苦しむ describes suffering from pain, hardship, or emotional distress. It is an intransitive godan verb: the sufferer is the subject, and a cause can take に or で. Forms include 苦しみます, 苦しんだ, and 苦しんで.

- 祖父は長い間、腰の痛みに苦しんでいました。 (そふはながいあいだ、こしのいたみにくるしんでいました。) - My grandfather suffered from back pain for a long time.
- 彼女は仕事の問題で苦しんでいます。 (かのじょはしごとのもんだいでくるしんでいます。) - She is suffering because of problems at work.

苦しんでいる presents an ongoing state. The word can cover distress without implying an audible groan.

## How the kanji combine

[苦](../kanji/苦.md) contributes suffering or hardship. The native verb is くるしむ; the kana ending participates in conjugation. There is no compound relationship to divide into several kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[苦しい](苦しい.md) (くるしい) is the related adjective describing a painful or difficult state. [悩む](悩む.md) (なやむ) emphasizes being troubled or worrying over a matter. [我慢](我慢.md) (がまん) focuses on enduring or restraining oneself, not suffering alone.

## Distinctive meaning

苦しむ does not name the action of making another person suffer. For that, the related verb 苦しめる (くるしめる) takes the affected person as an object. Compare 彼が苦しむ, "he suffers," with 彼を苦しめる, "cause him distress."
