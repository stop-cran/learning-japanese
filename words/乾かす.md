# 乾かす (かわかす) - dry something

## Meaning

乾かす means to dry something or cause it to become dry. It is transitive: the item being dried takes を.

- ドライヤーで髪を乾かしました。 (ドライヤーでかみをかわかしました。) - I dried my hair with a hair dryer.
- 濡れた服を乾かしています。 (ぬれたふくをかわかしています。) - I am drying the wet clothes.

Compare 髪が乾きました (かみがかわきました), "My hair dried," with 髪を乾かしました, "I dried my hair."

## How the kanji combine

[乾](../kanji/乾.md) supplies the dryness idea and the native かわ part; かす completes this godan verb. Learn 乾かす with the intransitive counterpart [乾く](乾く.md) (かわく), without treating their particle patterns as identical.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[干す](干す.md) (ほす) can mean hang out to dry or air out; 乾かす is not restricted to that method. [乾燥](乾燥.md) (かんそう) names dryness or drying, and 乾燥させる can describe causing something to dry.

## Distinctive meaning

Forms include 乾かします, 乾かして, and 乾かさない. The dryer example uses で for the means and を for the object. [渇く](渇く.md), also pronounced かわく, concerns thirst rather than the ordinary laundry-drying use.
