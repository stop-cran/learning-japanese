# 実感 (じっかん) - real feeling; feel firsthand

## Meaning

実感 is a real, personally felt sense of something: it feels concrete
or real through experience rather than remaining an abstract idea.
実感する means to actually feel or experience something.
The thing felt can take を, or a clause can take と.

- 初めて働いて、お金を稼ぐ大変さを実感しました。 (はじめてはたらいて、おかねをかせぐたいへんさをじっかんしました。) - Working for the first time, I experienced firsthand how hard earning money is.
- 合格したと聞いても、まだ実感がありません。 (ごうかくしたときいても、まだじっかんがありません。) - Even after hearing that I passed, it still does not feel real.

実感がある and 実感がない concern having or lacking that felt reality.
Knowing a fact and emotionally taking it in need not happen at the same moment.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality, and [感](../kanji/感.md)
contributes feeling. The first qualifies the felt experience as concrete or real.
ジツ + カン combines as じっかん, with a small っ.
It does not use 実's native fruit reading み.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[感じ](感じ.md) (かんじ) and [感じる](感じる.md) (かんじる)
cover feeling or impression more broadly.
[経験](経験.md) (けいけん) concerns what one has undergone;
実感 highlights its felt quality or reality.
実感がない is the useful negative construction, not a claim that nothing happened.

## Distinctive meaning

"Real feeling" does not automatically mean an honest opinion or objective truth.
A vivid account can also convey a sense as though one experienced something.
The word concerns how an experience or situation is felt; it does not independently
verify every belief the experiencer forms.
