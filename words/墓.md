# 墓 (はか) - grave; tomb

## Meaning

墓 is a noun for a grave, gravesite, or tomb. The form お墓 (おはか) adds the prefix お and is useful in respectful everyday discussion.

- 祖父の墓に花を供えました。 (そふのはかにはなをそなえました。) - I placed flowers at my grandfather's grave.
- 家族でお墓を掃除しました。 (かぞくでおはかをそうじしました。) - Our family cleaned the grave.

Nの墓 identifies whose grave it is. 墓に花を供える places flowers at the grave, while 墓を掃除する makes the grave the object being cleaned.

## How the kanji combine

墓 writes the entire native noun はか. There is no second kanji to analyze. The prefix in お墓 does not change that reading or identify a different kind of grave.

## Synonyms and antonyms

墓地 (ぼち) is a cemetery or burial ground containing graves; 墓 can identify an individual grave or tomb. 墓石 (ぼせき) is a gravestone. A site and its stone marker are related but not identical objects. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

墓 names the place or structure associated with the deceased, not the deceased person. Its English translation depends on the physical setting: "grave" and "tomb" can both fit. The word alone does not specify a burial method or require that each family member have a separate grave. Learn the social context without treating one custom as universal.
