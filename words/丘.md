# 丘 (おか) - hill; rising ground

## Meaning

丘 is a hill or raised area of land. It is a noun: 丘の上 (おかのうえ) means "on top of the hill," and 丘に登る (おかにのぼる) means "to climb a hill."

- 丘の上に小さな家があります。 (おかのうえにちいさないえがあります。) - There is a small house on the hill.
- あの丘に登ると、海が見えます。 (あのおかにのぼると、うみがみえます。) - If you climb that hill, you can see the sea.

The first example locates a house; the second treats the hill as the destination of a climb.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 丘 represents the whole native noun おか. There is no compound to divide into components. 岡 is another spelling in the dictionary, and it also appears in many names; a name's reading should be learned separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[山](山.md) (やま) means mountain and can also describe a hill in context. These everyday labels do not give a universal numerical height boundary. [坂](坂.md) (さか) is a slope or inclined road: a hill may have a 坂 leading up it.

## Distinctive meaning

丘 identifies a landform, not height as an abstract measurement. An English description of "higher ground" may fit it, but the height of a person or a building is not 丘. Think of an area rising above its surroundings rather than a general word for "high."
