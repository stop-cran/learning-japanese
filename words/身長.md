# 身長 (しんちょう) - a person's height

## Meaning

身長 is a person's height or stature. It is a noun used for measuring, comparing, or reporting how tall someone is.

- 私の身長は百七十センチです。 (わたしのしんちょうはひゃくななじゅっセンチです。) - I am 170 centimeters tall.
- 去年より身長が伸びました。 (きょねんよりしんちょうがのびました。) - I have grown taller since last year.

Use **person の身長** to identify whose height is meant. 身長を測る (しんちょうをはかる) means "measure someone's height."

## How the kanji combine

身 contributes body, and [長](../kanji/長.md) contributes length. The compound specifies bodily length measured as standing height. The on readings are シン + チョウ. The final vowel in ちょう is long.

## Synonyms and antonyms

背が高い (せがたかい) means "be tall," and 背が低い (せがひくい) means "be short." These evaluate the measurement rather than naming a different kind of height. [高い](高い.md) and [低い](低い.md) can also describe many nonhuman objects.

## Distinctive meaning

身長 is not the general word for every vertical measurement. A building's height is 高さ (たかさ), not normally its 身長. The noun itself is neutral: both a tall person and a short person have a 身長. Asking for it seeks a measurement, not necessarily an evaluation.
