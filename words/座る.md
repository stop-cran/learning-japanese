# 座る (すわる) — sit down

## Meaning

座る means to sit down, be seated, or sit on the floor.
It is intransitive: the person sits, and に can mark the place where they sit.

- ここに座ってください。 (ここにすわってください。) — Please sit here.
- 子供が床に座っています。 (こどもがゆかにすわっています。) — A child is sitting on the floor.

In the second example, 座っています describes the seated state, not repeated movements of sitting down.

## How the kanji combine

[座](../kanji/座.md) is read すわ, with る written after it.
This is a native Japanese verb using a kun reading.
Although it ends in る, it conjugates with a changing final sound: 座ります (すわります), 座らない (すわらない),
and 座って (すわって). Do not form the polite verb as すわます.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[立つ](立つ.md) (たつ), "stand up; stand," is the direct posture contrast.
座席 (ざせき), "seat; seating," is a related noun, not another verb for sitting.
椅子 (いす) is a chair; 座る can also describe sitting without one.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 椅子に座る (いすにすわる), "sit on a chair," rather than making the chair an object of 座る with を.
The verb describes what the person does or the posture they take.
The floor example shows why translating it only as "take a chair" would be too narrow.
