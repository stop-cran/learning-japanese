# いる - be present; exist for living beings

## Meaning

いる says that a person or animal exists or is present. Use place + に and person or animal + が. With a known subject, は often replaces が.

庭に猫がいます。 (にわにねこがいます。) - There is a cat in the garden.

私には妹がいます。 (わたしにはいもうとがいます。) - I have a younger sister.

The second pattern expresses a relationship, not ownership of a person. いる is an intransitive ichidan verb: います, いない, いて.

## How the kanji combine

Usually written in kana, it also has the spelling 居る, with [居](../kanji/居.md); the dictionary labels this kanji form rarely used. Learn the complete verb rather than treating る as an independent meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ある normally expresses the existence of inanimate things. いない means "is not here" or "does not exist"; it is the negative of いる, not a separate opposite word.

## Distinctive meaning

After a verb's て-form, いる can express an ongoing action or a continuing state: 読んでいる (よんでいる), "is reading." This auxiliary use is distinct from locating a living being. It is also different from 要る (いる), "need."
