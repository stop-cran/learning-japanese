# それ -- that; it

## Meaning

それ refers to something near the listener or associated with what the listener has said or done.

- それはあなたの傘ですか。 (それはあなたのかさですか。) -- Is that your umbrella?
- 明日は休みです。それはいいですね。 (あしたはやすみです。それはいいですね。) -- Tomorrow is a day off. That's nice.

In the second exchange, the response points back to the news just given. それ can refer to information, not only a visible object.

## How the kanji combine

This demonstrative is ordinarily written in hiragana. The dictionary's rare spelling 其れ is not needed for everyday use. There are no kanji components in this card's spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[これ](これ.md) points toward the speaker's side; あれ commonly points away from both speaker and listener. These are contrasts in perspective, not opposites in value.

## Distinctive meaning

それ stands in place of a noun: それをください (それをください), "That one, please." Before a noun use その instead, as in その傘 (そのかさ), "that umbrella." This is the demonstrative, not the unrelated encouraging or attention-getting interjection also written それ.
