# 講演 (こうえん) - lecture; public talk

## Meaning

講演 is a lecture, address, or talk given to an audience. 講演する means to give such a talk; it does not mean to attend one. A topic can be introduced with について, and the venue with で.

- 先生は環境問題について講演しました。 (せんせいはかんきょうもんだいについてこうえんしました。) - The teacher gave a talk on environmental issues.
- 図書館で作家の講演を聞きました。 (としょかんでさっかのこうえんをききました。) - I listened to an author's talk at the library.

講演をする is another way to express giving a talk. 講演会 (こうえんかい) names an event organized around a lecture or talks.

## How the kanji combine

講 concerns explaining or lecturing, while [演](../kanji/演.md) contributes presenting or delivering something before others. Together they name a spoken presentation. The on readings are コウ + エン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[講義](講義.md) (こうぎ) fits instructional lectures, such as those in a university course. [授業](授業.md) (じゅぎょう) is a class or lesson more broadly. A visiting speaker's public 講演 need not be part of a course.

## Distinctive meaning

Pay attention to the accompanying verb: 講演する identifies the speaker's activity, while 講演を聞く identifies the listener's. Neither construction says whether admission is free or paid.
