# 三日 (みっか) — the 3rd of the month; three days

## Meaning

三日 is both a date and a duration: 三月三日 さんがつみっか (March 3rd, Girls' Day), 三日間 みっかかん (for three days), 三日坊主 みっかぼうず (someone who gives up after three days).

## How the kanji combine

[三](../kanji/三.md) plus [日](../kanji/日.md). The reading みっか is the native day-counting series: ふつか (2), みっか (3), よっか (4), いつか (5), むいか (6), なのか (7), ようか (8), ここのか (9), とおか (10). Here み comes from the kun reading of 三 and か from the counter for days; the っ is a sound change.
It is not the sum of the on readings サン + ニチ. The days of the month are a closed set of such special words, so they are learnt as whole words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 三日月 みかづき (crescent moon, "third-day moon", reads み without っ), 三日目 みっかめ (the third day).

## Distinctive meaning

For both "the 3rd" and "three days" the same word is used, so context decides. Ordinary on-reading counting resumes at 十一日 じゅういちにち, except 十四日 じゅうよっか, 二十日 はつか and 二十四日 にじゅうよっか.
