# 実現 (じつげん) - realization; becoming reality

## Meaning

実現 is a noun for a plan, goal, or possibility becoming real. 実現する can be intransitive or transitive: 夢が実現する, "a dream comes true," versus 夢を実現する, "realize a dream."

- 長年の夢が実現しました。 (ながねんのゆめがじつげんしました。) - My long-held dream came true.
- みんなでこの計画を実現しましょう。 (みんなでこのけいかくをじつげんしましょう。) - Let's make this plan a reality together.

実現に向けて (じつげんにむけて) means "toward realization," often describing work before the goal is achieved.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality or reality; [現](../kanji/現.md) contributes appearing or becoming present. Together they express something becoming actual rather than remaining an idea. The readings are ジツ + ゲン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[実行](実行.md) (じっこう) emphasizes carrying out an action or plan. 実現 emphasizes the desired situation coming into existence.

[夢](夢.md) (ゆめ), "dream," names one possible goal, not a synonym for its realization.

## Distinctive meaning

English "realization" can mean suddenly understanding a fact. 実現 does not express that mental discovery; here it means making something real. 実現しなかった means "did not materialize," not "did not notice." A plan may be discussed at length without 実現, so distinguish preparation, execution, and the attained result rather than treating them as identical stages.
