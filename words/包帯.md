# 包帯 (ほうたい) - bandage; dressing

## Meaning

包帯 is a medical bandage or dressing, such as a strip of cloth wrapped around
an injured part. 包帯する means to bandage; the body part being bandaged can take を.

- 看護師が腕に包帯を巻いてくれました。 (かんごしがうでにほうたいをまいてくれました。) - The nurse wrapped a bandage around my arm.
- 汚れた包帯を取り替えました。 (よごれたほうたいをとりかえました。) - I changed the dirty bandage.

## How the kanji combine

[包](../kanji/包.md) contributes wrapping;
[帯](../kanji/帯.md) a band or strip. ホウ + タイ gives ほうたい.
The medical compound does not mean an ordinary clothing sash.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[傷](傷.md) (きず) is a wound; 包帯 is something used to protect or support
an injured area, not the injury itself. [巻く](巻く.md) (まく), "wind/wrap,"
is useful for describing how a bandage is applied.

## Distinctive meaning

In 腕に包帯を巻く, に marks the part around which the bandage is wound, and
を marks the bandage. This is a different construction from 腕を包帯する
(うでをほうたいする), "bandage the arm."
繃帯 is a rare alternative spelling. English "dressing" here is medical,
not salad dressing or the act of putting on clothes.
