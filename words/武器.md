# 武器 (ぶき) - weapon; asset

## Meaning

武器 is a weapon or weaponry. It is a noun. It also has a figurative meaning: an ability or advantage that helps someone succeed.

- 博物館には昔の武器が展示されています。 (はくぶつかんにはむかしのぶきがてんじされています。) - Old weapons are displayed in the museum.
- 語学力は仕事で大きな武器になります。 (ごがくりょくはしごとでおおきなぶきになります。) - Language skills can be a major asset at work.

武器になる means "become or serve as an asset" in the second example. It does not suggest using a physical weapon at work.

## How the kanji combine

武 contributes military or martial activity, and 器 contributes an implement or instrument. Together they name an implement for armed conflict. The on readings are ブ + キ. This component relationship explains the compound's sense, not the historical shape of either character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[道具](道具.md) (どうぐ) is a tool or implement more generally. 武器 narrows the physical use to weapons. Figuratively, [能力](能力.md) (のうりょく), "ability," may be the asset being described.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's English "ordinance" is a spelling error: an ordinance is a law or regulation. The relevant English word is "ordnance," weaponry. 武器 does not mean [法律](法律.md) (ほうりつ), "law." In figurative use, identify what gives the advantage rather than interpreting every 武器 as military equipment.
