# たえる — withstand; endure

## Meaning

たえる means to bear a hardship or withstand an external force. In the formal pattern Nに堪える, it can also mean to be worthy of N.

- 厳しい訓練に耐える。 (きびしいくんれんにたえる。) — to endure rigorous training
- この作品は鑑賞に堪える。 (このさくひんはかんしょうにたえる。) — This work is worthy of appreciation.

## How the kanji combine

JMdict entry 1211310 lists 耐える and 堪える as spellings of たえる. Both are read たえる here; this kana-headed article is mapped to 堪える for [堪](../kanji/堪.md). The spelling 堪える also has a separate reading こらえる in JMdict entry 2827352.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こらえる](こらえる.md) can also mean endure, but often focuses on a person bearing hardship or holding back a feeling: 寒さをこらえる (さむさをこらえる) contrasts with 高温に耐える (こうおんにたえる), which describes withstanding a condition. [我慢](我慢.md) (がまん), often with する, also overlaps in bearing discomfort or restraining a desire, as in 痛みを我慢する (いたみをがまんする). [凌ぐ](凌ぐ.md) (しのぐ) can mean getting through a difficulty, as in 風雨を凌ぐ (ふううをしのぐ); the meanings overlap, but 凌ぐ emphasizes getting through or protecting against the difficulty.

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen centers たえる on resisting external pressure and notes that it can describe objects as well as people. In Nに堪える, it can instead mean “be worthy of N,” as in 鑑賞に堪える. “Hold back laughter” calls for こらえる, not たえる. These are usage patterns, not unrestricted substitution rules.
