# たえる — withstand; endure

## Meaning

たえる means to withstand or bear an external force or condition. It can also mean to be fit or worthy for something.

- 高温に堪える。 (こうおんにたえる。) — to withstand high temperatures

## How the kanji combine

JMdict entry 1211310 gives both 耐える and 堪える for たえる. This kana-headed article is mapped to the spelling 堪える for [堪](../kanji/堪.md). The same spelling can also be read こらえる in a separate JMdict entry; here it is the reading たえる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こらえる](こらえる.md) can also mean endure, but often focuses on a person bearing hardship or holding back a feeling. [凌ぐ](凌ぐ.md) overlaps in surviving or enduring difficulty, with different usages.

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen centers たえる on resisting external pressure and notes that it can describe objects as well as people: 高温に堪える is natural, while “hold back laughter” calls for こらえる. These are useful patterns, not an unrestricted substitution rule.
