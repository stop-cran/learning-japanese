# 急 (きゅう) - sudden; urgent; steep

## Meaning

急 describes something sudden, urgent, or steep. As a na-adjective it takes
な before a noun and に when modifying an action.

- 急な用事ができました。 (きゅうなようじができました。) - Something urgent has come up.
- この坂は急です。 (このさかはきゅうです。) - This slope is steep.
- 急に雨が降り始めました。 (きゅうにあめがふりはじめました。) - It suddenly began to rain.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [急](../kanji/急.md) carries ideas of haste and abruptness.
Its on-reading キュウ forms this word; there is no compound to split.

## Synonyms and antonyms

突然 (とつぜん), "suddenly," overlaps with the unexpected-event use.
緩やか (ゆるやか), "gentle; gradual," contrasts with a steep slope or rapid change.
No single opposite covers all uses.

## Distinctive meaning

Urgency, suddenness, and steepness share an abrupt or pressing quality, but
English chooses different words for them. In particular, 急に normally means
"suddenly," not simply "quickly"; for doing an action at high speed, 速く
(はやく) may be the intended expression.
