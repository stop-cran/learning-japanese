# 目 (め) — eye

## Meaning

目 is the eye and the sense of sight: 目が悪い (have poor eyesight), 目を閉じる (close one's eyes), 目が覚める (wake up), 目に入る (come into view), 目を通す (look over).
It also names experience, as in ひどい目にあう, and works as an ordinal suffix: 二番目 にばんめ (second).

## How the kanji combine

A single kanji, [目](../kanji/目.md), with the kun reading め. The on reading is モク in 目的 もくてき (purpose), 目次 もくじ (contents) and 注目 ちゅうもく (attention).
Related: 目玉 めだま (eyeball; "a feature"), 目覚まし時計 めざましどけい (alarm clock).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 眼 め (eye, more literary or technical), 瞳 ひとみ (pupil), 視力 しりょく (eyesight).
- Related: [耳](耳.md) みみ (ear).

## Distinctive meaning

目 is the ordinary word for "eye". Do not confuse it with 日 (sun, day), which is the same shape with one inner stroke; the on readings of 目 are モク and ボク, those of 日 are ニチ and ジツ.
