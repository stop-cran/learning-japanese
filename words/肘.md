# 肘 (ひじ) - elbow

## Meaning

肘 is the elbow, the joint area where the upper arm meets the forearm.
It is a noun. Kana ひじ is also useful.

- 転んで肘を打ちました。 (ころんでひじをうちました。) - I fell and bumped my elbow.
- 肘を曲げてください。 (ひじをまげてください。) - Please bend your elbow.

を marks the body part affected by the action.
肘が痛い (ひじがいたい) instead describes the elbow as hurting.

## How the kanji combine

肘 is a single character writing the whole native noun ひじ.
There is no multi-kanji compound to divide.
JMdict also records rare 肱 and 臂 spellings; recognize them
without assuming their readings in every other word are ひじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[腕](腕.md) (うで) is the arm more broadly.
[手首](手首.md) (てくび) is the wrist, a different joint.
These body parts are related, not antonyms or interchangeable names.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep ひじ, elbow, distinct from [膝](膝.md) (ひざ), knee or lap.
Their similar sounds do not merge their meanings.
No separate 肘 kanji card is needed to learn the vocabulary article;
the complete written character remains in its metadata.
