# 渡す (わたす) - hand over; pass

## Meaning

渡す means to hand or pass something over. It is a transitive godan verb: 渡します, 渡して, 渡した and 渡さない.

- 先生に宿題を渡しました。 (せんせいにしゅくだいをわたしました。) - I handed my homework to the teacher.
- この手紙を母に渡してください。 (このてがみをははにわたしてください。) - Please give this letter to my mother.

The thing handed over takes を, and the recipient takes に.

## How the kanji combine

[渡](../kanji/渡.md) contributes passing across or transfer; す is okurigana. The native reading is わたす.

## Synonyms and antonyms

あげる can mean giving something as a gift or benefit. 渡す focuses on the transfer itself, which need not change ownership. 受け取る (うけとる), "receive," describes the recipient's action.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish 渡す from 渡る (わたる), "cross": 橋を渡る means crossing a bridge yourself. In the handover use, 渡す needs the transferred object, not a route across a place.
