# ペンキ (ペンキ) - paint for coating surfaces

## Meaning

ペンキ is paint used for coating surfaces, such as a wall, fence, or piece of furniture.
It is a noun. Use Nにペンキを塗る (Nにペンキをぬる), "apply paint to N,"
or Nをペンキで塗る, "paint N with paint."

- 壁に白いペンキを塗りました。
  (かべにしろいペンキをぬりました。) - I applied white paint to the wall.
- ペンキがまだ乾いていないので、触らないでください。
  (ペンキがまだかわいていないので、さわらないでください。)
  - The paint is not dry yet, so please do not touch it.

ペンキ塗りたて (ペンキぬりたて) is a useful warning meaning "wet paint" or
"freshly painted."

## How the kanji combine

The assigned spelling is katakana, so there are no kanji to divide.
JMdict traces ペンキ to Dutch *pek*. That source-language note does not mean
that present-day ペンキ should be translated by guessing from the Dutch spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

絵の具 (えのぐ) names colors or paints used for making pictures.
塗料 (とりょう) is a broader term for coating material.
[色](色.md) (いろ) is the color itself, not the substance applied to the surface.

## Distinctive meaning

English "paint" covers both coating a fence and making a watercolor picture.
ペンキ is a useful choice for the coating context illustrated here.
It does not by itself specify every technical property of the product, such as its
exact ingredients or whether it is suitable for a particular surface.
