# 速力 (そくりょく) - speed; rate of motion

## Meaning

速力 is speed, especially how fast a moving body travels.
It is a noun. Useful patterns include 速力を上げる (そくりょくをあげる),
"increase speed," and 速力を落とす (そくりょくをおとす), "reduce speed."

- 船は速力を上げました。 (ふねはそくりょくをあげました。) - The ship increased its speed.
- 霧のため、船は速力を落としました。 (きりのため、ふねはそくりょくをおとしました。) - Because of the fog, the ship reduced its speed.

These examples do not give a numerical speed or a unit of measurement.

## How the kanji combine

[速](../kanji/速.md) contributes fast movement, and [力](../kanji/力.md)
contributes force or capacity. As a whole, 速力 names speed, not a separate
physical force that must be added to motion.
The on readings combine as そく + りょく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[速度](速度.md) (そくど) also means speed or rate.
速力 is particularly useful for moving vehicles or bodies; this is a tendency,
not a rule that the two can never describe the same motion.
[時速](時速.md) (じそく) expresses speed per hour.
[速い](速い.md) (はやい), fast, is an adjective rather than this noun.

## Distinctive meaning

速力 does not by itself mean "high speed."
Increasing and decreasing the same vehicle's 速力 are both possible.
Do not replace every abstract "rate" with 速力 merely because English uses
the word "speed"; the moving-body use is the practical focus here.
