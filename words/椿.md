# 椿 (つばき) - camellia tree; camellia flower

## Meaning

椿 names the camellia tree or shrub, particularly *Camellia japonica*. In a flowering scene the word can focus on its blossoms.

- 庭の椿が咲きました。 (にわのつばきがさきました。) - The camellia in the garden has blossomed.
- 椿の花を見ています。 (つばきのはなをみています。) - I am looking at the camellia flowers.

椿の木 (つばきのき) explicitly identifies the tree; 椿の花 (つばきのはな) identifies its flowers.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [椿](../kanji/椿.md) writes the whole native noun つばき. The word can also appear in kana as つばき or ツバキ. Do not read its visible 木 and 春 shapes as two spoken words.

JMdict also lists 山茶 as a spelling for つばき. Its other reading さんちゃ is restricted to 山茶, and the archaic "tea produced in the mountains" sense is restricted to さんちゃ. Neither is permission to pronounce 椿 さんちゃ or translate this plant noun as tea.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[木](木.md) (き), tree, [植物](植物.md) (しょくぶつ), plant, and [花](花.md) (はな), flower, are broader words. 山茶花 (さざんか) names sasanqua, *Camellia sasanqua*, rather than supplying another reading of 椿. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

椿油 (つばきあぶら) means camellia oil, not the tree or blossom itself. The shared plant name does not make every plant product interchangeable.

The same kanji also appears in 椿事 (ちんじ), a grave, unexpected incident. That compound uses a different reading and meaning; it does not make つばき an ordinary word for an incident.
