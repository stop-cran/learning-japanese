# 黒 (くろ) -- black

## Meaning

黒 is the name of the color black. It is a noun, not the i-adjective 黒い (くろい).

- 好きな色は黒です。 (すきないろはくろです。) -- My favorite color is black.
- 黒のかばんを買いました。 (くろのかばんをかいました。) -- I bought a black bag.

Before another noun, 黒 can take の: 黒のかばん. 黒いかばん is also possible, using the related adjective instead.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [黒](../kanji/黒.md) writes the native color noun くろ. There is no ending to remove from 黒 itself. Adding い gives the related adjective, not a longer reading of the noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

白 (しろ), white, is a contrasting color. 黒色 (くろいろ) explicitly means black as a color. 暗い (くらい), "dark," can describe low light rather than black coloration.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep noun and adjective patterns separate: 黒ではありません (くろではありません), "It is not black," is a noun predicate. 黒くない (くろくない) is the negative form of 黒い. The meanings overlap, but the grammar differs.
