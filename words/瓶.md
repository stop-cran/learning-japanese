# 瓶 (びん) - bottle; jar

## Meaning

瓶 is a noun for a bottle or jar. It can name a container with a narrow neck or a wider opening, depending on the contents and context.

- 空の瓶に水を入れました。 (からのびんにみずをいれました。) - I put water into an empty bottle.
- ジャムの瓶のふたが開きません。 (ジャムのびんのふたがあきません。) - The lid of the jam jar will not open.

瓶に marks the container receiving something. 瓶の中 (びんのなか) means "inside the bottle," and 空き瓶 (あきびん) is an empty bottle or jar.

## How the kanji combine

瓶 is a single-kanji word read with the on reading ビン. JMdict also records standalone ビン as a kana spelling. That katakana form does not make the word an English loanword. The older-looking forms 壜 and 罎 are marked as rare spellings in the inspected entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

容器 (ようき) is the broader word "container." [缶](缶.md) (かん) means a can or tin. [コップ](コップ.md) is a drinking cup or glass rather than a storage bottle. Containers have contrasting types, not a natural antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not force the English distinction between "bottle" and "jar" onto every 瓶. ジャムの瓶 naturally translates as a jam jar. The noun is associated with glass containers, but the actual material, size, and closure should be established by the description rather than assumed from the English gloss alone.
