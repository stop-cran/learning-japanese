# ペンチ - combination pliers

## Meaning

ペンチ are combination or cutting pliers, used for gripping and bending wire
and for cutting it with the cutting section.

- ペンチで針金を切りました。 (ペンチではりがねをきりました。) - I cut the wire with combination pliers.
- この針金はペンチで曲げられます。 (このはりがねはペンチでまげられます。) - This wire can be bent with pliers.

## How the word is formed

This katakana tool name comes from English "pinchers." It is ペンチ,
not an ordinary Japanese noun for pinching in general; there is no kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ニッパー are wire cutters or diagonal cutting pliers; プライヤー is a broader
pliers term. [鋏](鋏.md) (はさみ), scissors, has a different usual cutting tool
as its referent. [道具](道具.md) (どうぐ) is the broader word for a tool.

## Distinctive meaning

English usually uses the plural "pliers" or "a pair of pliers" for one tool.
Japanese ペンチ does not mark that English plural. The wire word in the examples
is [針金](針金.md) (はりがね), not a sewing needle.
