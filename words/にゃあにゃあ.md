# にゃあにゃあ — meowing

## Meaning

A cat's repeated meows.

- 猫が玄関（げんかん）でにゃあにゃあ鳴（な）いている。 — The cat is meowing at the entrance.
- 子猫（こねこ）が母猫を探（さが）して、にゃあにゃあ鳴いた。 — The kitten meowed as it looked for its mother.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[わんわん](わんわん.md) commonly represents a dog's bark. These are conventional Japanese representations, not exact recordings of every animal's voice.

## Distinctive meaning

Use にゃあにゃあ鳴く. ニャーニャー is a recorded spelling of the same expression. The word can also mean a cat in child-directed speech; that noun use should not be confused with the ordinary word [猫](猫.md)（ねこ）in every register.

See [Japanese onomatopoeia](../articles/japanese-onomatopoeia.md) and [motion and sound](../articles/onomatopoeia-motion-and-sound.md).
