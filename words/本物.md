# 本物 (ほんもの) - genuine article; real thing

## Meaning

本物 is the genuine article or the real thing, contrasted with an imitation or something pretending to be it. It is a noun: **本物です** means "it is genuine," and **本物の + noun** means "a genuine ...".

- この時計は本物ですか。 (このとけいはほんものですか。) - Is this watch genuine?
- 動物園で本物のパンダを見ました。 (どうぶつえんでほんもののパンダをみました。) - I saw a real panda at the zoo.

The first question concerns authenticity. The second contrasts a live animal with representations such as photographs or toys, not with a counterfeit brand.

## How the kanji combine

[本](../kanji/本.md) contributes the original or genuine, and [物](../kanji/物.md) contributes a thing. The reading mixes on-reading ホン with native もの. The card uses the repository's kango category for this on-first mixed compound; it is not two on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

偽物 (にせもの), "fake; imitation," is the direct contrast. [本当](本当.md) (ほんとう) concerns truth or reality more broadly. A true statement is 本当, but it is not ordinarily an "authentic article" in the same way as a watch.

## Distinctive meaning

本物 does not simply mean expensive or high quality. A modest, inexpensive item can be genuine, while an impressive-looking item can be an imitation. Keep the noun pattern 本物の; do not automatically turn it into 本物な because English uses the adjective "genuine."
