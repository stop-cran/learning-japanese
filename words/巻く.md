# 巻く (まく) - wind; coil; roll up

## Meaning

巻く means to wind, coil or roll something. In this use it is transitive:
the thing being wound or rolled takes を.

- 紙を巻いた。 (かみをまいた。) - I rolled up the paper.
- 首にマフラーを巻いた。 (くびにマフラーをまいた。) - I wrapped a scarf around my neck.

The second example focuses on putting the scarf around the neck, not merely
owning or carrying it.

## How the kanji combine

[巻](../kanji/巻.md) supplies ま, and く completes the native verb. It is a
godan verb: 巻きます (まきます), 巻いて (まいて), 巻いた (まいた).
The written kanji boundary is not the same as the conjugation base.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[包む](包む.md) (つつむ), wrap or bundle, focuses on enclosing something;
巻く focuses on winding or rolling in the examples above. Their meanings can
overlap, so this is not a rule that one English word always chooses the verb.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the object and construction together. 紙を巻く rolls paper, while
首にマフラーを巻く places the scarf around a body part. The verb has further
uses; the physical action taught here does not account for every expression
containing 巻く.
