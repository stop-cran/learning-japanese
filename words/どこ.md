# どこ (どこ) — where

## Meaning

どこ asks "where?" or "what place?" It stands in for a location. Keep the particle appropriate to that location's role in the sentence.

- 駅はどこですか。  
  えきはどこですか。  
  "Where is the station?"
- どこで昼ご飯を食べますか。  
  どこでひるごはんをたべますか。  
  "Where will you eat lunch?"

Use どこに for where something exists or a destination, and どこで for where an action happens. The word どこ does not remove the need for those distinctions.

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is the usual spelling. 何処 and 何所 are dictionary spellings, but beginner writing does not need them. The ordinary reading taught here is どこ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どちら can politely ask "where?" or ask about direction or a choice, depending on context. ここ, そこ, and [あそこ](あそこ.md) answer with "here", "there", and "over there".

## Distinctive meaning

どこか means "somewhere". A negative expression such as どこにもありません (どこにもありません), "It isn't anywhere", uses も with a negative verb; どこ alone does not mean "nowhere".
