# 辛い (からい) — spicy; hot-tasting

## Meaning

辛い describes a spicy or pungently hot taste. It is an い-adjective: 辛い料理 (からいりょうり), "spicy food"; 辛くない (からくない), "not spicy."

このカレーは辛いですが、おいしいです。 (このカレーはからいですが、おいしいです。) — This curry is spicy, but delicious.

## How the kanji combine

辛 writes から here, followed by the adjective ending い. The same spelling 辛い can also represent つらい, "painful; emotionally hard." That is a different reading and should not replace からい in the spicy-food example.

## Synonyms and antonyms

甘い (あまい), "sweet," is a useful taste contrast, not the only possible opposite. 熱い (あつい) concerns high temperature rather than spicy flavor. 塩辛い (しおからい) explicitly means salty.

## Distinctive meaning

からい can also describe a salty taste or strict criticism, depending on context. Thus the single English word "hot" is too ambiguous as a complete usage guide: a dish may be 辛い even when it is physically cold. Learn the flavor sense first and use context to distinguish the extensions.
