# 三日月 (みかづき) - crescent moon; young moon

## Meaning

三日月 is a young crescent moon: the slim, curved moon associated with the third night of the lunar month and nearby nights.

- 西の空に三日月が見えます。 (にしのそらにみかづきがみえます。) - A crescent moon is visible in the western sky.
- 三日月の形の飾りを買いました。 (みかづきのかたちのかざりをかいました。) - I bought a crescent-shaped ornament.

## How the kanji combine

[三](../kanji/三.md), "three," and [日](../kanji/日.md), "day," modify [月](../kanji/月.md), "moon." Learn the whole native reading みかづき, including づ. The date/duration word [三日](三日.md) is separately read みっか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[月](月.md) (つき) is the moon more generally. 満月 (まんげつ) is a full moon, not this crescent phase.

## Distinctive meaning

An English gloss "new moon" may refer loosely to a young moon. Here the useful image is a visible crescent, not the invisible astronomical conjunction. Do not interpret 三日 as the third day of every modern calendar month.
