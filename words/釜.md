# 釜 (かま) - cooking pot; kettle

## Meaning

釜 is a pot used for cooking rice, boiling water, or similar work. A traditional iron cooking pot or tea kettle is a useful image, but the word does not require every vessel to be made of iron.

- 釜でご飯を炊きました。 (かまでごはんをたきました。) - I cooked rice in a cooking pot.
- 茶釜でお湯を沸かします。 (ちゃがまでおゆをわかします。) - We boil water in a tea kettle.

茶釜 (ちゃがま) is a kettle associated with making tea; かま becomes がま in this compound.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 釜 writes the whole noun かま. There is no compound to divide. Its cooking-vessel meaning is a recognition aid, not a claim about the historical origin of the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鍋](鍋.md) (なべ) also names a cooking pot and can name the hot-pot dish itself. Learn the vessel and expression rather than assuming that English "pot" always selects the same Japanese word. やかん is a familiar word for a water-boiling kettle.

## Distinctive meaning

釜 is not the word for every saucepan or cooking appliance. The Japanese encyclopedia descriptions include both metal and ceramic vessels, so the source gloss "iron pot" is a prototype, not an absolute material rule. No particular heating method is specified by the noun alone.
