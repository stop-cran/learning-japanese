# 空中 (くうちゅう) - midair; in the air

## Meaning

空中 names the space in the air, above the ground, rather than air as a substance. 空中に describes a position or destination in that space; 空中で locates an action there.

- 紙飛行機が空中をゆっくり飛んでいます。 (かみひこうきがくうちゅうをゆっくりとんでいます。) - A paper airplane is flying slowly through the air.
- ボールが空中で止まって見えました。 (ボールがくうちゅうでとまってみえました。) - The ball looked as if it had stopped in midair.

In the first example, を marks the space traversed, not an object that is flown.

## How the kanji combine

[空](../kanji/空.md), sky or airspace, is followed by [中](../kanji/中.md), within: within the air. The on readings are クウ + チュウ; both contain a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[空](空.md) (そら), sky, overlaps when describing the space overhead. [空気](空気.md) (くうき) and [大気](大気.md) (たいき) concern air or the atmosphere, not simply an object's position off the ground.

## Distinctive meaning

空中 is not the normal noun for the air one breathes. It also does not mean outer space merely because something is high above the ground. In translations such as "in the air," the appropriate Japanese particle still depends on the sentence.
