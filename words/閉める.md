# 閉める (しめる) — close something

## Meaning

閉める describes someone closing a door, window, or similar opening. It is transitive: mark what is closed with を.
The polite form is 閉めます (しめます), and the て-form is 閉めて (しめて).

- 窓を閉めてください。 (まどをしめてください。) — Please close the window.
- 寒いので、ドアを閉めました。 (さむいので、ドアをしめました。) — I closed the door because it was cold.

## How the kanji combine

[閉](../kanji/閉.md) supplies shutting; める completes the native verb しめる.
The related 閉まる (しまる) uses different kana and a different sentence pattern.

## Synonyms and antonyms

Compare ドアを閉める (ドアをしめる), "close the door," with ドアが閉まる (ドアがしまる), "the door closes."
[開ける](開ける.md) (あける), "open something," is the opposite action.
閉じる (とじる) is another closing verb, useful in 本を閉じる (ほんをとじる), "close a book."

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 閉める with the same-sounding 締める (しめる), "tighten; fasten."
閉める focuses on closing an opening; the shared pronunciation alone does not choose the right spelling.
