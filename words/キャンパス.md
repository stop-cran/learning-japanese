# キャンパス - campus

## Meaning

キャンパス means a campus: the grounds or premises of a university or similar institution. It is a noun.

- 昼休みにキャンパスを散歩しました。
  (ひるやすみにキャンパスをさんぽしました。) - I took a walk around the campus during the lunch break.
- この大学には二つのキャンパスがあります。
  (このだいがくにはふたつのキャンパスがあります。) - This university has two campuses.

The second sentence distinguishes the institution from its separate locations.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana, from English "campus"; there are no constituent kanji to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

構内 (こうない) means premises or grounds and can also describe a station or other compound. [大学](大学.md) (だいがく) names a university; キャンパス particularly brings its grounds or site into focus. Extended uses can refer to university life or the university rather than a literal patch of land.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish キャンパス, with パ, from キャンバス (canvas), with バ. The small ャ belongs to キャ; it is not a separate full-sized ヤ syllable.
