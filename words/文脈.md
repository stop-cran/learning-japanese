# 文脈 (ぶんみゃく) - context

## Meaning

文脈 is the context or connected flow that helps an expression make sense.
It primarily concerns surrounding text or discourse, and can also mean the broader
context or circumstances of something. It is a noun.

- この言葉の意味は文脈によって変わります。 (このことばのいみはぶんみゃくによってかわります。) - The meaning of this word changes with the context.
- 前後の文脈を読んで判断してください。 (ぜんごのぶんみゃくをよんではんだんしてください。) - Please read the surrounding context before deciding.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) contributes text or discourse. 脈 (ミャク) has a vein,
pulse or connected-line association. Connecting passages as though following a
line is a useful association for the established word ブン + ミャク, not a
claim that a sentence literally has blood vessels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[前後](前後.md) (ぜんご) can mean the surrounding context, not just before and after.
[事情](事情.md) (じじょう), [状況](状況.md) (じょうきょう) and [次第](次第.md)
(しだい) can concern circumstances; 文脈 foregrounds how surrounding elements
make something intelligible. 背景 (はいけい), "background," is also useful in
the broader contextual sense.

## Distinctive meaning

文脈から意味を推測する (ぶんみゃくからいみをすいそくする) means infer a meaning
from context. The relevant evidence is more than the isolated dictionary gloss.
The broader use permits 社会的な文脈 (しゃかいてきなぶんみゃく), "social context";
do not insist that every 文脈 must be one adjacent written sentence.
