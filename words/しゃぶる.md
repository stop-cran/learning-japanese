# しゃぶる (しゃぶる) - suck; lick in the mouth

## Meaning

しゃぶる means to suck or lick something held in the mouth, such as candy or a finger. It is a **transitive godan verb**: しゃぶります, しゃぶって, しゃぶらない.

- 子どもが飴をしゃぶっています。
  (こどもがあめをしゃぶっています。) - The child is sucking on a candy.
- 赤ちゃんが指をしゃぶっています。
  (あかちゃんがゆびをしゃぶっています。) - The baby is sucking a finger.

The thing sucked takes を. しゃぶっている describes the ongoing action; the final る does not make this an ichidan verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[吸う](吸う.md) (すう) overlaps in sucking, but also covers inhaling or drawing in something. しゃぶる foregrounds the mouth's action on the object, not breathing generally.

[ぺろぺろ](ぺろぺろ.md) depicts repeated licking, and [ぺろり](ぺろり.md) can depict a quick lick. They overlap in licking imagery without being interchangeable verb forms.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list also gives "chew." Do not learn that as the central action: sucking a candy does not require biting it into pieces. [噛む](噛む.md) (かむ) is the useful comparison for biting or chewing with the teeth.

しゃぶる is not the ordinary verb for sucking air into a vacuum cleaner or absorbing water into paper. Identify what is happening in the mouth rather than translating every English "suck" mechanically.
