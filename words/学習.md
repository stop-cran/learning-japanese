# 学習 (がくしゅう) - learning; study

## Meaning

学習 names learning or study. With する it becomes a transitive verb:
**subject matter + を学習する**. It can describe learning knowledge or skills, including learning through experience.

- このアプリで日本語を学習しています。 (このアプリでにほんごをがくしゅうしています。) - I am learning Japanese with this app.
- 毎日の学習時間を決めました。 (まいにちのがくしゅうじかんをきめました。) - I decided how much time to spend studying each day.

In the first sentence, で identifies the means used. 学習時間 means time devoted to learning;
学習したこと (がくしゅうしたこと) means "what one has learned."

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) means learn or study; [習](../kanji/習.md) contributes learning through practice.
The related meanings reinforce one another rather than identify a learner and a separate object.
The on readings ガク + シュウ give がくしゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) overlaps with study.
[練習](練習.md) (れんしゅう) focuses on practicing an activity, while [学ぶ](学ぶ.md) (まなぶ)
is a native verb for learning. Repeating an exercise can be 練習 within a wider process of 学習.

## Distinctive meaning

学習 is useful when discussing learning as a process: plans, materials, time, or outcomes.
It does not guarantee mastery simply because someone has studied something.
Compare 学習している, "is studying/learning," with 学習した, "studied/learned":
the surrounding context establishes what knowledge was actually acquired.
