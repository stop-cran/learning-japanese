# 広める (ひろめる) - disseminate; broaden

## Meaning

広める means to spread or disseminate something, making it more
widely known or used. It also means to broaden or extend.
It is a transitive ichidan verb: what is spread or broadened takes を.

- この活動を多くの人に広めたいです。 (このかつどうをおおくのひとにひろめたいです。) - I want to make this activity known to many people.
- 本を読んで知識を広めました。 (ほんをよんでちしきをひろめました。) - I broadened my knowledge by reading books.

The second example concerns increasing one's own range of knowledge,
not necessarily teaching it to other people.
Forms include 広めます, 広めない, 広めて and 広めた.

## How the kanji combine

[広](../kanji/広.md) contributes breadth and is read ひろ;
める completes the native verb.
弘める is another dictionary spelling of ひろめる.
It does not change the primary article's metadata to include 弘.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[広げる](広げる.md) (ひろげる) also means to extend or spread.
広める often highlights dissemination or widening knowledge;
広げる readily describes unfolding an object or extending its area.
These are overlapping tendencies, not an exceptionless
information-versus-physical-space rule.
[伝える](伝える.md) (つたえる) focuses on conveying something,
which need not make it widely known.

## Distinctive meaning

広まる (ひろまる) is intransitive: うわさが広まる,
the rumor spreads, versus うわさを広める, spread a rumor.
[宣伝](宣伝.md) (せんでん)する concerns publicity or promotion;
広める does not inherently mean advertising or propaganda.
The noun object and the context determine whether the verb concerns
dissemination or broader extent.
