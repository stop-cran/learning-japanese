# 上着 (うわぎ) — jacket; upper-body outerwear

## Meaning

上着 is an upper-body garment, especially a jacket or another outer layer. It is a broad clothing word, not the name of one particular style.

- 寒いので、上着を着ます。 (さむいので、うわぎをきます。) — It's cold, so I'll put on a jacket.
- ここで上着を脱いでもいいですか。 (ここでうわぎをぬいでもいいですか。) — May I take off my jacket here?

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md) is read うわ, suggesting an upper or outer layer. [着](../kanji/着.md) contributes き, voiced to ぎ in this native compound. Learn うわぎ as a whole.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ジャケット names a jacket more specifically; コート usually means a coat. 下着 (したぎ), "underwear," contrasts with an outer garment rather than meaning trousers.

## Distinctive meaning

上着 need not be heavy winter clothing. A light jacket or suit jacket can also be 上着. Use 着る (きる) for putting it on and 脱ぐ (ぬぐ) for taking it off.
