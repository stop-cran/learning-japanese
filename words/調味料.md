# 調味料 (ちょうみりょう) - seasoning; condiment

## Meaning

調味料 is a seasoning or condiment used to adjust a dish's flavor.
It is a category, not the name of one particular spice or sauce.

- この料理には、どんな調味料を使いますか。
  (このりょうりには、どんなちょうみりょうをつかいますか。) - What seasonings do you use in this dish?
- 調味料を少しずつ加えてください。
  (ちょうみりょうをすこしずつくわえてください。) - Please add the seasonings a little at a time.

## How the kanji combine

[調](../kanji/調.md) contributes adjusting,
[味](../kanji/味.md) flavor, and
[料](../kanji/料.md) a material.
調味 (ちょうみ), seasoning or flavoring, modifies the material:
something used for seasoning. Read チョウ + ミ + リョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[塩](塩.md) (しお), [砂糖](砂糖.md) (さとう),
[味噌](味噌.md) (みそ), and [胡椒](胡椒.md) (こしょう)
are examples of seasonings, not interchangeable ingredient names.
醤油 (しょうゆ), soy sauce, is another example.
香辛料 (こうしんりょう) concerns spices; 調味料 is broader.

## Distinctive meaning

A 調味料 need not be spicy, dry, or added only at the table.
Sugar and liquid soy sauce illustrate why seasoning cannot be reduced
to pepper-like spices. Conversely, identifying something as a seasoning
does not tell you its amount or whether it suits a particular recipe.
