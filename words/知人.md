# 知人 (ちじん) - acquaintance

## Meaning

知人 is an acquaintance or someone one knows. It is a noun:
私の知人 (わたしのちじん), "an acquaintance of mine."

- 駅で知人に会いました。 (えきでちじんにあいました。) - I met an acquaintance at the station.
- 知人の紹介で、この店を知りました。 (ちじんのしょうかいで、このみせをしりました。) - I learned about this shop through an acquaintance's introduction.

に marks the person met in the first sentence. 知人の紹介 identifies
who provided the introduction in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[知](../kanji/知.md) contributes knowing, and [人](../kanji/人.md)
identifies a person. The combination names a person one knows.
The on readings are チ + ジン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知り合い](知り合い.md) (しりあい) is a close everyday comparison.
[友人](友人.md) (ゆうじん) and [友達](友達.md) (ともだち) identify
friends. These categories can overlap, but 知人 by itself does not
claim a close friendship.

## Distinctive meaning

Dictionary English may include "friend," but do not infer "close friend"
from 知人 alone. Conversely, it need not mean a stranger barely recognized
by sight. The word leaves the relationship's closeness unspecified.
For an explicitly close friend, compare [親友](親友.md) (しんゆう).
