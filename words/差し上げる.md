# 差し上げる (さしあげる) - give humbly

## Meaning

差し上げる is the humble counterpart of あげる, "give." It presents giving respectfully toward the recipient. The giver is the subject, the recipient takes に, and the gift takes を.

先生に写真を差し上げました。 (せんせいにしゃしんをさしあげました。) - I gave my teacher a photograph.

こちらの地図を差し上げます。 (こちらのちずをさしあげます。) - You may have this map.

It is an ichidan verb: 差し上げます, 差し上げて, 差し上げない.

## How the kanji combine

[差](../kanji/差.md) appears in 差し, and [上](../kanji/上.md) in 上げる. Learn さしあげる as a complete compound verb. The dictionary also records a literal "raise; hold up" sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

あげる is neutral. [いただく](いただく.md) humbly describes receiving. [くださる](くださる.md) honors someone giving to the speaker's side, so the direction and viewpoint differ.

## Distinctive meaning

Verb て-form + 差し上げる means doing something for someone. Merely using humble grammar does not make an unsolicited favor tactful: explicitly announcing a favor can sound presumptuous. Learn it in context rather than replacing every あげる automatically.
