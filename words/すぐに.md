# すぐに - immediately; right away

## Meaning

すぐに means immediately, at once, or after very little delay. It modifies an action rather than naming a length of time.

すぐに戻ります。 (すぐにもどります。) - I will be back right away.

電話が鳴ったので、すぐに出ました。 (でんわがなったので、すぐにでました。) - The phone rang, so I answered immediately.

## How the kanji combine

The phrase uses すぐ followed by に. It is normally written in kana; 直ぐに is another spelling, with [直](../kanji/直.md). Learn the whole adverbial expression for its time meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すぐ can also modify a verb without に. もうすぐ means "very soon," with an event approaching. 後で (あとで), "later," is a useful contrast when something will not be done immediately.

## Distinctive meaning

すぐに is relative to the situation: a promise to come back "right away" does not specify an exact number of seconds. In a request, it conveys urgency. The word is not a general adjective for something fast; use an appropriate speed adjective when describing a vehicle or movement.
