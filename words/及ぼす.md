# 及ぼす (およぼす) - exert an influence; cause an effect

## Meaning

及ぼす is a transitive godan verb for causing an effect to reach someone
or something. Learn it with an effect noun: 影響を及ぼす means "exert
an influence," and 被害を及ぼす means "cause damage."
The affected target takes に.

- 睡眠不足は仕事に悪い影響を及ぼします。 (すいみんぶそくはしごとにわるいえいきょうをおよぼします。) - Lack of sleep has a bad effect on work.
- 台風は広い地域に被害を及ぼしました。 (たいふうはひろいちいきにひがいをおよぼしました。) - The typhoon caused damage across a wide area.

The polite form is 及ぼします; the past is 及ぼした. It does not
translate the physical-exercise meaning of English "exercise."

## How the kanji combine

及 contributes the idea of reaching or extending. The native verb
およぼす describes making an effect extend to a target. The related
及ぶ (およぶ) describes reaching or extending without the same
transitive を-marked effect pattern.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[影響](影響.md) (えいきょう) names influence and can take する:
仕事に影響する, "affect work." Compare that with
仕事に影響を及ぼす. The meaning overlaps, but the grammar changes.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb does not inherently mean a bad effect; the effect noun and its
modifiers tell you that. 悪い影響 explicitly supplies "bad influence."
Learn the complete target + effect + verb pattern rather than treating
及ぼす as a substitute for every English "cause."
