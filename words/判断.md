# 判断 (はんだん) — judgment; assessment

## Meaning

判断 is a judgment, assessment, or decision about something.
判断する means to judge or reach a conclusion.
Use **matter を判断する** for what you assess, or **conclusion と判断する** for the conclusion reached.

- これは危険だと判断しました。 (これはきけんだとはんだんしました。) — I judged that this was dangerous.
- 自分で判断してください。 (じぶんではんだんしてください。) — Please judge for yourself.

In the first sentence, 危険だ is the conclusion and と introduces it.
The だ belongs to the plain predicate 危険だ, "is dangerous."

## How the kanji combine

[判](../kanji/判.md) contributes judging, and [断](../kanji/断.md) has a decision or judgment sense here.
The two reinforce reaching a judgment; 断 is not describing physically cutting an object in this word.
The on readings are ハン + ダン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

決断 けつだん means a decision or determination, useful when the focus is committing to a course of action.
判断 can instead report an assessment, such as judging that something is dangerous, without stating what action follows.
The meanings overlap, so this is a practical contrast rather than a ban on translating either as "decision."

## Distinctive meaning

判断 does not guarantee that the conclusion is correct.
It identifies the act or result of judging; the evidence and the person making the judgment still matter.
In the danger example, we learn the speaker's assessment, not automatically a final plan or an established fact.
