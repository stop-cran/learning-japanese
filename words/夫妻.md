# 夫妻 (ふさい) - husband and wife; married couple

## Meaning

夫妻 refers somewhat formally to a married couple other than one's own. It commonly follows a name when referring to the two spouses together.

- 田中夫妻を夕食に招きました。 (たなかふさいをゆうしょくにまねきました。) - We invited Mr. and Mrs. Tanaka to dinner.
- その夫妻には二人の子供がいます。 (そのふさいにはふたりのこどもがいます。) - The couple have two children.

## How the kanji combine

[夫](../kanji/夫.md), husband, and [妻](../kanji/妻.md), wife, form a coordinated pair: husband and wife together. The on readings are フ + サイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[夫婦](夫婦.md) (ふうふ) also means a married couple, without the same restriction against referring to one's own marriage. 私たち夫婦 (わたしたちふうふ), my spouse and I, is the useful self-reference.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not turn "formal" into a rule that 夫妻 is the polite version to use for yourself. Use 田中夫妻 for the Tanakas, but not 私たち夫妻 as the ordinary way to introduce your own couple. 夫妻 names the pair, not just one person's spouse.
