# 各々 (おのおの) - each; individually

## Meaning

各々 directs attention to each member of a group separately. It can work as a noun or adverb; 各々の + noun means "each one's" or "each ...," depending on the phrase.

- 各々の意見を聞きました。 (おのおののいけんをききました。) - I listened to each person's opinion.
- 参加者は各々、資料を持ち帰りました。 (さんかしゃはおのおの、しりょうをもちかえりました。) - The participants each took the materials home.

It is not restricted to people: 各々の方法 (おのおののほうほう) can refer to each method.

## How the kanji combine

[各](../kanji/各.md) contributes "each." The mark 々 repeats the written character; it is not a separate kanji or an instruction to pronounce おのおの twice. Learn the whole word as おのおの. The dictionary also records 各各 and single-character 各 with this reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[それぞれ](それぞれ.md) also distributes attention among members. [一人一人](一人一人.md) (ひとりひとり) specifically concerns people. [いちいち](いちいち.md), "one by one; every single," can emphasize dealing with every item and may express annoyance in context.

## Distinctive meaning

The focus is the individual members, not simply the total group. [あらゆる](あらゆる.md), "every; all kinds of," highlights a whole range and goes before a noun. Neither the shared English "every" nor the character 各 makes all these sentence patterns interchangeable.
