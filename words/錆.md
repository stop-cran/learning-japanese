# 錆 (さび) — rust

## Meaning

錆 is rust: the corrosion product appearing on metal. It is a noun, also written 錆び.

自転車のチェーンに錆が出ている。（じてんしゃのチェーンにさびがでている。） — There is rust on the bicycle chain.

道具の錆を落とした。（どうぐのさびをおとした。） — I removed the rust from the tools.

## How the kanji combine

This is the single-character native noun さび, not an on-reading compound. 錆び writes the same noun with a final kana; it is not a different pronunciation. 銹 and 鏽 are rare alternative spellings, not the writing models used here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[錆びる](錆びる.md) さびる is the corresponding intransitive verb, rust/become rusty. [腐る](腐る.md) くさる concerns rotting or deterioration and has broader corrosion uses, but is not a reason to call food spoilage 錆.

## Distinctive meaning

The practical meaning here is the substance or surface corrosion, not merely a color. 錆色（さびいろ） is a separate expression for a rust color. In 錆を落とす, 錆 is what is removed; compare the changing condition expressed by 錆びる.
