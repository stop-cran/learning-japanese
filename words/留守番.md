# 留守番 (るすばん) - house-sitting; house-sitter

## Meaning

留守番 is looking after a home or place while others are away.
It can name the person doing so. 留守番する is an intransitive
する-verb; 家で留守番する means to stay at home and mind the place.

- 今日は私が留守番をします。 (きょうはわたしがるすばんをします。) - Today I will stay home and look after the house.
- 留守番の人に鍵を渡しました。 (るすばんのひとにかぎをわたしました。) - I handed the key to the person minding the house.

The person need not be a professional or paid house-sitter.

## How the kanji combine

[留](../kanji/留.md) contributes remaining, [守](../kanji/守.md)
guarding, and [番](../kanji/番.md) a watch or duty.
[留守](留守.md) (るす) supplies the situation of someone's absence;
番 adds looking after the place. Read ル + ス + バン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

留守 commonly means being out or absent, but its dictionary family
also includes house-sitting and the house-sitter.
[世話](世話.md) (せわ) is care or assistance more generally.
The words are related without making every kind of care 留守番.

## Distinctive meaning

留守番 emphasizes minding the place during another person's absence,
not merely spending time at home. In 留守番電話 (るすばんでんわ),
the complete expression names an answering machine or voicemail service;
bare 留守番 is not the name of a telephone.
