# ええ -- yes

## Meaning

ええ is a conversational affirmative response: "yes," "right," or "that's correct." It can acknowledge what another person says.

- もう昼ごはんを食べましたか。ええ、食べました。 (もうひるごはんをたべましたか。ええ、たべました。) -- Have you eaten lunch yet? Yes, I have.
- ここに座ってもいいですか。ええ、どうぞ。 (ここにすわってもいいですか。ええ、どうぞ。) -- May I sit here? Yes, please do.

It stands as a response rather than taking a particle or conjugating.

## How the kanji combine

This interjection is written in kana. The two え characters represent the sustained vowel in ええ; there are no hidden kanji components to explain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

はい is a useful, more straightforward choice in formal responses. うん is a casual "yes." いいえ gives a negative response, though natural refusals may be phrased less directly.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume every prolonged え sound means agreement. Hesitation and surprise can be written えー and interpreted through intonation. This card teaches affirmative ええ, not those reactions or the regional adjective meaning "good."
