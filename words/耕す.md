# 耕す (たがやす) - till; plough

## Meaning

耕す is a transitive godan verb for tilling, ploughing or cultivating
land. The soil or field being worked takes を.

- 春に畑を耕します。
  (はるにはたけをたがやします。) - I till the field in spring.
- 種をまく前に土をよく耕しました。
  (たねをまくまえにつちをよくたがやしました。) - I worked the soil well before sowing the seeds.

The polite form is 耕します (たがやします), the て-form is 耕して
(たがやして), and the past form is 耕した (たがやした).

## How the kanji combine

[耕](../kanji/耕.md) writes the native stem たがや, with the inflecting
ending す. The whole word describes working agricultural land; it is not
assembled from separate on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

耕作 (こうさく) is cultivation or tillage and can take する.
耕地 (こうち) is cultivated land or farmland, a noun for the land rather
than this inflecting action.

There is no single ordinary opposite that covers every context. Planting,
growing and harvesting are related farming activities, not automatic
translations of 耕す.

## Distinctive meaning

English cultivate can also mean develop a talent or relationship.
Do not infer that every such figurative use can be translated with 耕す.
This lesson concerns working agricultural soil, not the whole process
of producing a crop.
