# どんどん — rapidly; one after another

## Meaning

どんどん describes vigorous progress, rapid change, or things happening one after another. With an increasing quantity, English often uses "more and more."

- お客さんがどんどん来ます。 (おきゃくさんがどんどんきます。) — Customers keep coming one after another.
- 町に新しい店がどんどん増えています。 (まちにあたらしいみせがどんどんふえています。) — More and more new shops are appearing in town.

It modifies the verb directly. どんどん質問してください (どんどんしつもんしてください) encourages someone to go ahead and ask plenty of questions.

## How the kanji combine

This repeated mimetic word is written in kana. It also imitates repeated banging or pounding sounds, as in ドアをどんどんたたく, "bang on the door."

## Synonyms and antonyms

次々に (つぎつぎに) emphasizes succession. だんだん emphasizes gradual change. Both can overlap with どんどん, but they present the pace differently.

## Distinctive meaning

どんどん does not itself require an increase: どんどん減る (どんどんへる) means to decrease rapidly. The following verb determines the direction of change, while どんどん gives the sense of momentum.
