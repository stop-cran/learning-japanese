# 酉 (とり) — Rooster zodiac sign

## Meaning

酉 is the **Rooster sign**, the tenth of the twelve zodiac signs.
It identifies a place in the cycle, not a bird standing in front of you.

- 私の干支は酉です。 (わたしのえとはとりです。) — My zodiac sign is the Rooster.
- 十二支の十番目は酉です。 (じゅうにしのじゅうばんめはとりです。) — The tenth of the twelve zodiac signs is the Rooster.

十二支 (じゅうにし) names the twelve-sign set. In the first example,
干支 (えと) has its everyday twelve-sign zodiac sense. 干支 can also mean
the sixty-term calendar cycle combining stems and branches; that is not
the same list as the twelve signs. The reading かんし is documented for
the sixty-term sense, not as a substitute for えと's twelve-sign sense.

## How the kanji combine

[酉](../kanji/酉.md) writes the whole native sign noun とり, with no
okurigana or second character to separate. Its on-reading ユウ appears
in 辛酉 (しんゆう), Metal Rooster, term 58 of the sixty-term cycle.
That expression is also read かのととり; the standalone sign here is とり.

Recognize 酉年 (とりどし), **year of the Rooster**: 酉 identifies the sign
and [年](../kanji/年.md) the year. Learn the complete reading とりどし;
the separate noun [年](年.md) has the primary reading とし.
Daijisen also records 酉年 for a person born in that year.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鳥](鳥.md) とり is the ordinary bird noun. [鶏](鶏.md) にわとり,
also read とり, names the domestic chicken, the animal associated with
this sign. These animal nouns are not unrestricted substitutes for 酉.
The zodiac sign has no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the spelling tied to the meaning: 酉 for the sign, 鳥 for a bird,
and 鶏 for chicken specifically. In food contexts とり can also mean
chicken meat, but that does not make 酉 a meat spelling. English descriptions
may call the sign the Rooster, Cock, Chicken or Bird; this does not make
every Japanese とり a male chicken.

Older texts also use 酉 for an hour around 6 p.m., west, or the eighth
lunar month. Dictionary hour conventions include both 5–7 p.m. and
6–8 p.m.; no universal conversion is intended. Lunar month eight is not
simply Gregorian August. For ordinary directions, use [西](西.md) にし,
west; [時刻](時刻.md) じこく names a time of day.
