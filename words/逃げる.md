# 逃げる (にげる) — run away; escape

## Meaning

逃げる means to flee or get away. The escaping person or animal is the subject;
から can mark what or where they escape from.

- 鳥がかごから逃げました。 (とりがかごからにげました。) — The bird escaped from the cage.
- 猫は犬から逃げました。 (ねこはいぬからにげました。) — The cat ran away from the dog.

This is an ichidan verb: replace the final る to make 逃げます or 逃げた.

## How the kanji combine

[逃](../kanji/逃.md) carries the getting-away meaning.
The native reading is にげる, with げる written in kana. No second kanji is involved.

## Synonyms and antonyms

逃走 (とうそう) is a noun meaning flight or escape, and 逃走する describes escaping.
逃がす (にがす) is a related but different verb: 鳥を逃がす (とりをにがす) means to let a bird go or let it get away.
The change from 鳥が逃げる to 鳥を逃がす changes whose action is being described.

## Distinctive meaning

逃げる presents movement as getting away from something, not just going to another place.
It does not require that the subject has done anything wrong: the cat in the example may simply be avoiding danger.
