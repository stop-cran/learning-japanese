# 署名 (しょめい) - signature; signing

## Meaning

署名 is a signature, and 署名する means to sign one's name. A useful construction is **document に署名する**, with に marking where the signature is placed.

- 契約書に署名しました。 (けいやくしょにしょめいしました。) - I signed the contract.
- ここに署名をお願いします。 (ここにしょめいをおねがいします。) - Please sign here.

The second example asks for a signature using the noun with お願いします. It does not use を to mark the document being signed.

## How the kanji combine

署 contributes writing or signing, while [名](../kanji/名.md) means name. The relationship is writing one's name as a signature. The readings are ショ + メイ; distinguish しょ from a long しょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

サイン can mean a signature or autograph, but also has other uses such as a signal. [名前](名前.md) (なまえ) is a person's name itself, not necessarily a signature. 記名 (きめい) is putting a name on something and does not by itself specify the same signing action.

## Distinctive meaning

署名 appears in document and petition contexts. 署名を集める (しょめいをあつめる), "collect signatures," concerns multiple people's signed support. A name merely mentioned in prose is not automatically someone's 署名, and the word alone does not establish a document's legal validity.
