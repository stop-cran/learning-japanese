# 稽古 (けいこ) - practice; training

## Meaning

稽古 is practice, training, or study, particularly useful for an art, performance, or martial art.
It is a noun and can form 稽古する; an activity being practiced can take を.

- 毎週、剣道の稽古をしています。 (まいしゅう、けんどうのけいこをしています。) - I practice kendo every week.
- 舞台に立つ前に、せりふを稽古しました。 (ぶたいにたつまえに、せりふをけいこしました。) - I practiced my lines before going on stage.

The noun can name a practice session as well as the activity.

## How the kanji combine

稽 includes associations with considering or thinking; [古](../kanji/古.md) contributes "old."
As a mnemonic, imagine learning by considering established models.
That association is not a verified account of the word's history.
The on readings combine as ケイ + コ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[練習](練習.md) (れんしゅう) also means practice and can describe many of the same activities.
[訓練](訓練.md) (くんれん) emphasizes training.
[勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) and [学習](学習.md) (がくしゅう) are useful study/learning words.
Do not turn these tendencies into an absolute arts-versus-non-arts partition.

## Distinctive meaning

稽古 is not simply attending a show: it describes working on the skill or material.
The specific art or activity comes from context, as 剣道 and せりふ do above.
