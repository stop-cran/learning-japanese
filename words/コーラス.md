# コーラス - chorus; choral singing

## Meaning

コーラス concerns choral singing. It can name the singing, a choral
piece, or the choir that performs it. It is a noun borrowed from
English "chorus."

- 学校のコーラスに参加しています。 (がっこうのコーラスにさんかしています。) - I take part in the school's chorus.
- コーラスの練習は土曜日です。 (コーラスのれんしゅうはどようびです。) - Chorus practice is on Saturday.

The first example concerns participating in a group activity;
the second identifies its practice.

## Synonyms and antonyms

合唱 (がっしょう) names choral singing; 合唱団 (がっしょうだん)
specifically names a choir. 独唱 (どくしょう), solo singing, contrasts
with the choral activity rather than with every possible use of コーラス.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not automatically carry every English use of "chorus" into Japanese.
For a song's hook or climactic refrain, サビ is a separate dictionary word.
This card focuses on the positively recorded choral meanings, not an
exhaustive glossary of song structure or audio effects.
Keep the long vowel in コーラス; it is not [コース](コース.md).
