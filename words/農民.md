# 農民 (のうみん) - farmer; farming people

## Meaning

農民 is a noun for a farmer or people who live by farming. It can refer to an individual or to farming people collectively; the noun does not have a compulsory plural ending.

- 農民たちは雨を待っていました。 (のうみんたちはあめをまっていました。) - The farmers were waiting for rain.
- この本は江戸時代の農民の生活を紹介しています。 (このほんはえどじだいののうみんのせいかつをしょうかいしています。) - This book introduces the lives of farmers in the Edo period.

農民の生活 means "farmers' lives." の connects the people with the aspect being discussed.

## How the kanji combine

農 concerns farming or agriculture; [民](../kanji/民.md) means people. The modifier-plus-head relationship gives "farming people." The on readings are ノウ + ミン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[農家](農家.md) (のうか) can identify a farming household or farmer and is useful when discussing a farm as a livelihood or household.

[農業](農業.md) (のうぎょう) names agriculture itself, not the people doing it.

## Distinctive meaning

"Peasant" can fit some historical or social-class contexts, but do not import its possible insulting English tone into every occurrence of 農民. The Japanese word can describe contemporary agricultural people as well. It does not specify land ownership, wealth, or the crop grown. Other occupations are contrasting categories, not direct antonyms.
