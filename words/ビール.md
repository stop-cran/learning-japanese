# ビール (ビール) - beer

## Meaning

ビール is a noun meaning beer.
Use ビールを飲む (ビールをのむ), "drink beer," or ビールが好き (ビールがすき), "like beer."
When ordering, the quantity can follow the noun with an appropriate counter.

- ビールを一杯お願いします。 (ビールをいっぱいおねがいします。) - One glass of beer, please.
- 冷蔵庫にビールが二本あります。 (れいぞうこにビールがにほんあります。) - There are two bottles of beer in the refrigerator.

一杯 (いっぱい) counts one glass or serving; 二本 (にほん) counts two bottles in the second context.
The counter describes the container or serving, not a different kind of beer.

## How the kanji combine

The katakana word has a long vowel: ビー・ル.
JMdict records Dutch *bier* as its source, so it should not be presented simply as an English borrowing.
The assigned spelling contains no kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[酒](酒.md) (さけ) can mean alcoholic drinks more broadly; ビール identifies one category.
[ワイン](ワイン.md) is wine, an alternative drink rather than an antonym.
[飲み物](飲み物.md) (のみもの), "beverage," includes both alcoholic and nonalcoholic drinks.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the long vowel distinct from ビル, "building."
The noun itself does not specify a brand, container, or serving temperature; modifiers and counters provide those details.
For example, 冷たいビール (つめたいビール) explicitly means "cold beer."
Compare ビールを二杯 (ビールをにはい), "two glasses of beer," with the bottle-counting example above.
