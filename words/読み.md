# 読み (よみ) - reading; interpretation

## Meaning

読み is a noun for reading, a word or character's reading, or an interpretation of a situation. It is related to the verb [読む](読む.md) (よむ), but the noun does not conjugate like that verb.

- この漢字の読みを教えてください。 (このかんじのよみをおしえてください。) - Please tell me how to read this kanji.
- 私の読みが当たりました。 (わたしのよみがあたりました。) - My prediction proved right.

読みの練習 (よみのれんしゅう) means "reading practice." In the first full example, 読み asks for pronunciation; in the second, it names someone's assessment or prediction.

## How the kanji combine

[読](../kanji/読.md) contributes reading and writes よ in this native word. The final み corresponds to the stem form of 読む. Learn 読み as a noun with its established meanings, rather than treating every verb stem as freely interchangeable with a noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

読み方 (よみかた) means how to read something and overlaps when asking for a pronunciation. [予想](予想.md) (よそう) overlaps with the prediction sense, not the pronunciation of a character.

書き (かき), "writing," is a paired literacy activity rather than a strict opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

A situational 読み involves judgment about what something means or what will happen. It need not involve written text. Conversely, asking for 漢字の読み is not asking for a character's full definition or historical origin; it asks how the written form is read in the relevant context.
