# 非常 (ひじょう) - emergency; extraordinary

## Meaning

非常 is a noun meaning an emergency. Use 非常の場合
(ひじょうのばあい), "in an emergency," or 非常時 (ひじょうじ),
"at a time of emergency." It also has a な-adjective use meaning extraordinary
or extreme, as in 非常な努力 (ひじょうなどりょく), "extraordinary effort."

- 非常の場合は、この階段を使ってください。 (ひじょうのばあいは、このかいだんをつかってください。) - In an emergency, please use these stairs.
- 彼は完成までに非常な努力をしました。 (かれはかんせいまでにひじょうなどりょくをしました。) - He made extraordinary efforts to complete it.

The second sentence is about degree, not a declaration of an emergency.

## How the kanji combine

[非](../kanji/非.md) contributes "not," and [常](../kanji/常.md) contributes
"usual; ordinary." Something outside the usual state can be an emergency
or something extraordinary. The on readings combine as ひ + じょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[異常](異常.md) (いじょう) concerns an abnormal condition, which is not
necessarily an emergency. [大変](大変.md) (たいへん) overlaps in serious
situations and as an intensifier. 平常 (へいじょう), "normal conditions,"
provides a contrast to the emergency noun.

## Distinctive meaning

[非常に](非常に.md) (ひじょうに) is the adverbial form meaning "very;
extremely": 非常に便利 (ひじょうにべんり), "extremely convenient."
Do not translate that as "emergency convenience."
[非常口](非常口.md) (ひじょうぐち), "emergency exit," instead preserves
the emergency meaning. Attachment and the following word help separate the uses.
