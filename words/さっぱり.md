# さっぱり (さっぱり) - refreshed; not at all

## Meaning

さっぱりする describes feeling refreshed or relieved. It can also describe a neat, clean appearance.
With a negative predicate, さっぱり means "not at all," a separate use that must be learned with its grammar.

- シャワーを浴びて、さっぱりしました。 (シャワーをあびて、さっぱりしました。) - I felt refreshed after taking a shower.
- 説明を聞いても、さっぱり分かりません。 (せつめいをきいても、さっぱりわかりません。) - Even after hearing the explanation, I do not understand at all.

For appearance, 髪を切ってさっぱりした (かみをきってさっぱりした) can mean "got a haircut and looked neater."
For food, さっぱりした味 (さっぱりしたあじ) is a light, refreshing taste.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すっきり](すっきり.md) overlaps in descriptions of feeling clear or refreshed.
[あっさり](あっさり.md) overlaps in light food; [こってり](こってり.md) suggests a rich or heavy quality.
[全然](全然.md) (ぜんぜん) overlaps with さっぱり in negative patterns such as 全然分からない,
"do not understand at all." That does not make it a substitute for feeling refreshed after a shower.

## Distinctive meaning

Polarity and the following predicate are crucial. さっぱりした after a shower is positive,
while さっぱり分からない expresses a complete lack of understanding.
The food use describes a light quality, not necessarily a lack of flavor.
Read the whole phrase rather than assigning one English gloss to every occurrence.
Preserve the small っ. The descriptive use also allows さっぱりとした味（あじ）;
do not treat と as necessary in every construction.
