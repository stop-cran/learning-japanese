# 広げる (ひろげる) - spread out; widen; unfold

## Meaning

広げる means to make something wider or more extensive, or spread
something out. It is a transitive ichidan verb: the affected thing takes を.
It also means unfolding or opening something.

- 机の上に地図を広げました。 (つくえのうえにちずをひろげました。) - I spread out a map on the desk.
- 道を広げる工事が始まりました。 (みちをひろげるこうじがはじまりました。) - Work to widen the road has begun.

Forms include 広げます, 広げない, 広げて and 広げた.
The verb also covers spreading things around and extending a business's
activity or prosperity; not every use concerns a flat object.

## How the kanji combine

[広](../kanji/広.md) contributes breadth and is read ひろ here;
げる completes the native verb.
拡げる is another recorded spelling. 展げる is marked search-only
in the pinned dictionary, with a note connecting it to unfolding.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[広がる](広がる.md) (ひろがる) is intransitive:
範囲が広がる (はんいがひろがる), the range expands,
versus 範囲を広げる, expand the range.
[広める](広める.md) (ひろめる) often highlights spreading
knowledge or making something widely known.
The distinction is not an absolute physical-versus-abstract division.

## Distinctive meaning

[開く](開く.md), read ひらく in 本を開く (ほんをひらく),
can overlap in opening or unfolding.
[敷く](敷く.md) (しく) focuses on laying something out underneath,
whereas 広げる need not place it beneath anything.
[散らかす](散らかす.md) (ちらかす) highlights a scattered,
untidy result. 広げる does not inherently criticize tidiness.
Ideas, interests, and activities can also be 広げる's objects;
learn their actual constructions rather than limiting it to physical width.
