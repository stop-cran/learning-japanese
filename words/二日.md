# 二日 (ふつか) — two days; second of the month

## Meaning

二日 means either two days or the second day of a month. The sentence tells you whether it is a duration or a calendar date.

- 二日に会いましょう。 (ふつかにあいましょう。) — Let's meet on the second.
- 旅行には二日かかります。 (りょこうにはふつかかかります。) — The trip takes two days.

二日間 (ふつかかん) explicitly marks a two-day period. 二日後 (ふつかご) means "two days later" or "in two days."

## How the kanji combine

[二](../kanji/二.md), "two," combines with [日](../kanji/日.md), "day," in the native day-counting reading ふつか. Do not construct the everyday date or duration by reading the characters as に + にち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一日 (ついたち) is the first day of a month, while 一日 (いちにち) is a one-day duration. 二日 does not have that particular split: ふつか serves both uses.

## Distinctive meaning

A bare date often takes に for when an event happens, while a duration directly modifies the action. Learning both example patterns prevents confusing "on the second" with "for two days."
