# 田植え (たうえ) - rice-seedling planting

## Meaning

田植え is planting or transplanting rice seedlings into a paddy.
It names the farming activity, not the seedlings or the field itself.
田植 is another written form in the same dictionary family.

- 家族の田植えを手伝いました。
  (かぞくのたうえをてつだいました。) - I helped my family with rice planting.
- この地域では五月に田植えが始まります。
  (このちいきではごがつにたうえがはじまります。) - Rice planting starts in May in this region.

The second example describes the stated region; it does not establish a
single planting month for all of Japan.

## How the kanji combine

[田](../kanji/田.md) supplies rice paddy, using the native reading た.
[植](../kanji/植.md) appears in 植え (うえ), the noun/stem form related to
[植える](植える.md) (うえる), plant. The combination is た + うえ,
not an on-reading compound with ショク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

植える is the broader verb for planting.
[田んぼ](田んぼ.md) (たんぼ) names a rice paddy, while
[耕す](耕す.md) (たがやす) means till or plough the land.
Tilling, planting and harvesting are related stages, not the same action.
稲刈り (いねかり), rice harvesting, is a useful later-stage comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

田植え concerns setting rice seedlings into paddies, not every kind of
sowing or gardening. The examples use the noun with 手伝う and 始まる;
the inspected dictionary entries do not supply a suru-verb classification
to generalize into a conjugation lesson here.
