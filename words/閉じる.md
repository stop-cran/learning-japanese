# 閉じる (とじる) - close; shut

## Meaning

閉じる means to close or shut, as with a book, eyes, or an opening. It is an
ichidan verb: 閉じます (とじます), 閉じて (とじて), 閉じない (とじない).
It has both transitive and intransitive uses.

- 本を閉じてください。 (ほんをとじてください。) - Please close the book.
- エレベーターのドアが静かに閉じました。 (エレベーターのドアがしずかにとじました。) - The elevator doors closed quietly.

を marks the book someone closes; が marks the doors that close.
目を閉じる (めをとじる), "close one's eyes," is another useful phrase.

## How the kanji combine

[閉](../kanji/閉.md) supplies the closing meaning with the native reading とじる.
The same character appears in 閉める and 閉まる, but their different readings
and verb patterns must be learned separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[閉める](閉める.md) (しめる) is transitive, and [閉まる](閉まる.md) (しまる)
is intransitive. They overlap with door-closing uses.
[開く](開く.md), read ひらく for opening a book, provides an opposite action.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not mechanically replace 本を閉じる or 目を閉じる with a different closing
verb just because the English is the same. 閉じる also extends to bringing
a meeting or event to a close. Its transitivity comes from the construction,
not from choosing a separate verb ending.
