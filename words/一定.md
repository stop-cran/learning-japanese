# 一定 (いってい) - constant; fixed; prescribed

## Meaning

一定 describes something fixed, constant, or prescribed. Before a noun,
use **一定の + noun**. 一定にする means making something constant;
一定している describes it as settled or stable.

- 温度を一定に保ってください。 (おんどをいっていにたもってください。) - Please keep the temperature constant.
- 毎月、一定の額を払います。 (まいつき、いっていのがくをはらいます。) - I pay a fixed amount each month.

一定の条件 (いっていのじょうけん) can mean specified conditions, and
一定の効果 (いっていのこうか) can mean a certain degree of effect.
The surrounding noun determines whether constancy or a defined extent is relevant.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) contributes one or uniformity, and [定](../kanji/定.md)
contributes fixing or settling. Together they suggest something kept at
a settled value or extent. Learn いってい with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

固定 (こてい), fixing or securing, overlaps in some fixed-state contexts.
[変化](変化.md) (へんか), change, supplies a contrast when the issue is whether a value
remains constant. [確実](確実.md) (かくじつ) concerns certainty or
dependability, not automatically an unchanging amount.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 一定の条件, not an automatically substituted 一定な条件.
The noun-modifying の pattern differs from an ordinary な-adjective.
"Certain" in a phrase such as 一定の効果 can mean a defined or limited
extent; it does not itself promise that an outcome is guaranteed or that
all values are identical in every respect.
