# 秋 (あき) — autumn; fall

## Meaning

秋 is the season autumn, called "fall" in American English. It is a noun.

- 秋が好きです。 (あきがすきです。) — I like autumn.
- 来年の秋に日本へ行きます。 (らいねんのあきににほんへいきます。) — I will go to Japan next autumn.

In the second example, 来年の秋 specifies which autumn, and に marks when the trip happens.

## How the kanji combine

This native word is written with the single character [秋](../kanji/秋.md), read あき.
There is no compound to split and no okurigana. The on reading シュウ belongs to other words,
such as 秋分 (しゅうぶん, autumnal equinox).

## Synonyms and antonyms

"Autumn" and "fall" are two English translations of the same seasonal word, not two Japanese meanings.
The related seasons are [春](春.md) (はる, spring), [夏](夏.md) (なつ, summer), and [冬](冬.md) (ふゆ, winter).
Spring can be a seasonal contrast, but it is not a strict lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

秋 names a season, not a fixed set of dates everywhere or a guarantee about the weather.
Read the ordinary seasonal word あき. The dictionary also recognizes 秋 read とき in a different "time" sense;
that does not license とき for the season in these examples.
