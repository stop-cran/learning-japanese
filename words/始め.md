# 始め (はじめ) - beginning; start

## Meaning

始め is the beginning or start of something, especially an activity. A preceding
noun normally takes の: 物事の始め (ものごとのはじめ), "the start of something."
Compare the spellings in these examples:

- 何事も始めが大切です。 (なにごともはじめがたいせつです。) - The beginning matters in everything you do.
- 秋の初めに旅行します。 (あきのはじめにりょこうします。) - I will travel in early autumn.
- 初めからもう一度読みます。 (はじめからもういちどよみます。) - I will read it again from the beginning.

## How the kanji combine

[始](../kanji/始.md) contributes beginning; め is okurigana.
The noun corresponds to the base of 始める (はじめる), "begin something."
初め is also read はじめ. Official guidance recommends 初め for the early
part of a period or first in a sequence, and 始め for commencement.
These are spelling recommendations, not a rule that every alternative is
ungrammatical.

## Synonyms and antonyms

最初 (さいしょ) also means the beginning or first item.
終わり (おわり), "end," is a useful opposite when discussing a bounded activity.

## Distinctive meaning

For "first" in instructions, prefer 初めに (はじめに). It does not automatically mean
"for the first time": use [初めて](初めて.md) for a first experience.
Also distinguish this noun from 始めて (はじめて), the te-form of 始める; the final
kana changes its grammatical role.
