# 支払 (しはらい) - payment

## Meaning

支払 is a noun meaning payment. 支払い is another spelling of the same word, with the final い written explicitly. Both are read しはらい.

- 支払期限は来週の月曜日です。 (しはらいきげんはらいしゅうのげつようびです。) - The payment deadline is next Monday.
- 支払いは現金でお願いします。 (しはらいはげんきんでおねがいします。) - Please pay in cash.

支払方法 (しはらいほうほう) is the payment method. 支払いを済ませる (しはらいをすませる) means to finish making a payment. The related verb is [支払う](支払う.md) (しはらう), "pay," with the amount or charge marked by を.

## How the kanji combine

This noun is formed from 支払う. JMdict identifies [支](../kanji/支.md) as ateji in that verb: a character used for its sound, not a literal "support" component. [払](../kanji/払.md) corresponds to はらい, from 払う (はらう), "pay."

The spelling combines シ with the native はらい. Like 支払う, this mixed reading is grouped under `kango` here; that display category does not make it an ordinary all-on-reading compound or establish its historical origin. Learn 支払 and 支払い as alternative written forms of one noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会計](会計.md) (かいけい) can refer to settling a restaurant bill, but also to accounting. [料金](料金.md) (りょうきん) is the charge to be paid, not the payment itself. [収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう), income, describes money coming in rather than directly negating 支払.

## Distinctive meaning

支払 identifies payment, not a promise that it has already happened. 支払予定 (しはらいよてい) is a payment schedule or plan, while 支払済み (しはらいずみ) means paid. The surrounding expression tells you the status.
