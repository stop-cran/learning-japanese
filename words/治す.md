# 治す (なおす) - cure; heal

## Meaning

治す means to cure or heal an illness or injury. It is a
transitive godan verb: を marks the condition being cured.
The subject may be a person, medicine, or another cause of healing.

- 風邪を治すために、今日は休みます。 (かぜをなおすために、きょうはやすみます。) - I will rest today to recover from my cold.
- この薬で病気を治しました。 (このくすりでびょうきをなおしました。) - I cured the illness with this medicine.

Forms include 治します (なおします), 治して (なおして),
and 治さない (なおさない). A purpose such as 治すために
states an aim; it does not say that recovery has already occurred.

## How the kanji combine

[治](../kanji/治.md) supplies なお, followed by the ending す.
This native verb uses the character's healing association,
not its governing sense or the on readings ジ and チ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[治る](治る.md) (なおる) is intransitive:
風邪が治った (かぜがなおった), "The cold cleared up."
Compare 病気を治す, curing an illness, with 病気が治る,
the illness getting better.
[直す](直す.md), also なおす, means repairing or correcting,
as with a broken clock or a written mistake.

## Distinctive meaning

The spellings matter even though 直す and 治す share a reading.
Do not extend this medical 治す entry to every English "fix" or
"repair." Also distinguish the healing aim from a guaranteed
outcome: discussing treatment does not itself establish a cure.
