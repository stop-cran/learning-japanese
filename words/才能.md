# 才能 (さいのう) — talent

## Meaning

才能 means talent or ability. A useful pattern is **field の + 才能がある**, "have talent in a field."
It is a noun: use ある to say someone has it, rather than treating 才能 as an adjective.

- 彼女には音楽の才能があります。 (かのじょにはおんがくのさいのうがあります。) — She has musical talent.
- 子供の才能を大切にしたいです。 (こどものさいのうをたいせつにしたいです。) — I want to value my child's talent.

## How the kanji combine

[才](../kanji/才.md) points to talent, and [能](../kanji/能.md) to ability.
The related meanings reinforce each other rather than naming two separate possessions.
The on readings combine as サイ + ノウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[能力](能力.md) (のうりょく) also means ability or capacity. 天才 (てんさい), "genius; prodigy," can name a gifted person:
彼女は天才です (かのじょはてんさいです), "She is a genius."
That differs grammatically and in strength from saying she has 才能.

才能がない (さいのうがない) means "have no talent"; there is no need to invent a separate opposite noun.

## Distinctive meaning

才能 describes an ability, not a qualification, job title, or completed achievement.
Saying someone has musical talent does not itself say they work as a musician.
The word alone also does not settle how much that ability comes from aptitude, learning, or practice.
