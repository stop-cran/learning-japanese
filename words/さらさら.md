# さらさら — silky and free-flowing

## Meaning

Dry, light smoothness or a freely flowing consistency; also a soft rustle or murmur, and an easy, fluent manner of doing something.

- 洗った髪が乾いて、さらさらになった。 — My washed hair dried and became silky.
- 小川（おがわ）の水がさらさらと流れている。 — The stream is flowing with a gentle murmur.
- 彼女は筆（ふで）で名前をさらさらと書いた。 — She wrote her name fluently with a brush.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic repeating さら. It can depict texture, movement, or sound. This is not the unrelated 更々, “not in the least,” despite the identical reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すべすべ](すべすべ.md) emphasizes smooth touch; [つるつる](つるつる.md) may foreground slipperiness. [ねばねば](ねばねば.md) and [どろどろ](どろどろ.md) contrast with a light, free-flowing liquid.

## Distinctive meaning

さらさらした髪, さらさらの砂, and さらさらと流れる are useful. It need not mean “dry” when describing flowing water. With writing it foregrounds ease and fluency, not the texture of the paper. JMdict also records rustling and slurping down ochazuke; choose the meaning from the accompanying noun and verb.
