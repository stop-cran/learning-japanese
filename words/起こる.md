# 起こる (おこる) - occur; happen

## Meaning

起こる is an intransitive godan verb meaning that an event happens or occurs. The event takes が; the sentence need not name a person causing it. Useful forms are 起こります, 起こって, and 起こった.

- 駅の近くで事故が起こりました。 (えきのちかくでじこがおこりました。) - An accident happened near the station.
- 同じ問題がまた起こるかもしれません。 (おなじもんだいがまたおこるかもしれません。) - The same problem might happen again.

で marks where the event occurs. The second example presents a possibility, not a claim that the problem has already returned.

## How the kanji combine

[起](../kanji/起.md) expresses rising or beginning. Here the native verb uses that character for the arising of an event. The ending こる belongs to the complete word おこる; this is not a compound of separate nouns.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[起きる](起きる.md) (おきる) can also describe an event happening, as well as someone getting up. 起こす (おこす) is the transitive contrast: 事故を起こす (じこをおこす), "cause an accident." Causing and happening are different perspectives, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

起こる is useful when the event itself is central: a problem, change, or accident arises. It is not the verb for a person's becoming angry; 怒る has the same reading おこる but a different spelling and meaning. Keep both the kanji and the subject in view.
