# 大きな (おおきな) — big

## Meaning

大きな means big or large and must come before a noun. It can describe physical size or abstract importance.

大きな犬がいます。（おおきないぬがいます。） — There is a big dog.

それは大きな問題です。（それはおおきなもんだいです。） — That is a major problem.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) supplies the idea of largeness. 大きな is a native word, read おおきな as a whole.

## Synonyms and antonyms

大きい（おおきい） also means "big" and can modify a noun. 小さな（ちいさな）, "small," is the corresponding noun-modifying contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Despite its final な, 大きな does not behave like a regular na-adjective. To end a sentence, say 犬は大きいです, "the dog is big," not 大きなです or 大きだ. Use 大きくない for "not big."
