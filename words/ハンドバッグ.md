# ハンドバッグ — handbag; purse

## Meaning

ハンドバッグ is a handbag: a bag carried for personal belongings. In American English, "purse" can translate this sense.

- ハンドバッグに財布を入れました。 (はんどばっぐにさいふをいれました。) — I put my wallet in my handbag.
- 母は黒いハンドバッグを持っています。 (はははくろいはんどばっぐをもっています。) — My mother has a black handbag.

Use に for putting something into the bag and を when the bag itself is the object of carrying or buying.

## How the kanji combine

The katakana word corresponds to English "handbag." Keep the final voiced グ: バッグ, not バック. The small ッ marks the consonant closure.

## Synonyms and antonyms

かばん and バッグ are broader words for a bag. 財布 (さいふ) is a wallet or coin purse, usually an item you might put inside a handbag rather than the handbag itself.

## Distinctive meaning

The English word "purse" varies by region. Use the object distinction rather than assuming every small container for money is a ハンドバッグ.
