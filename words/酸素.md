# 酸素 (さんそ) - oxygen

## Meaning

酸素 is oxygen. It is a noun used in scientific, environmental, and medical contexts. **酸素が必要** (さんそがひつよう) means oxygen is necessary; 酸素を含む (さんそをふくむ) means to contain oxygen.

- 空気には酸素が含まれています。 (くうきにはさんそがふくまれています。) - Air contains oxygen.
- 魚も酸素を必要とします。 (さかなもさんそをひつようとします。) - Fish need oxygen too.

The second example does not mean that fish breathe air in the same way people do. The noun identifies the substance, not the mechanism of taking it in.

## How the kanji combine

酸 contributes the character meaning acid or sourness; 素 contributes an element or basic constituent. Together they form the established scientific name 酸素, read サン + ソ. These component associations are a memory aid, not a claim that oxygen itself tastes sour.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[空気](空気.md) (くうき) is air, a mixture containing oxygen, not a synonym for pure oxygen. 二酸化炭素 (にさんかたんそ) is carbon dioxide, a different substance rather than an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

In [化学](化学.md), oxygen is the element represented by O; the oxygen gas in air consists mainly of O₂ molecules. Ordinary 酸素 can name that gas without explicitly saying "molecules." Keep substance names distinct rather than translating every breathing-related noun as "air."
