# 以来 (いらい) - since; ever since

## Meaning

以来 connects an earlier starting point with what has held or happened since. Attach it to a time or event noun, as in 卒業以来 (そつぎょういらい), "since graduation," or to a verb's て-form: 会って以来, "since meeting."

- 卒業以来、彼には会っていません。 (そつぎょういらい、かれにはあっていません。) - I haven't seen him since graduation.
- 日本に来て以来、毎日日記を書いています。 (にほんにきていらい、まいにちにっきをかいています。) - Ever since coming to Japan, I have written a diary every day.

It can also stand adverbially after a starting event has been mentioned: 以来、連絡がない (いらい、れんらくがない), "There has been no contact since."

## How the kanji combine

[以](../kanji/以.md) marks a reference point in expressions of time, and [来](../kanji/来.md) contributes "coming." Together they point from that reference point toward the later period under discussion. This is a meaning aid, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

てから also means "after/since doing" and can describe a simple sequence. 以来 particularly suits a continuing state or repeated activity. [今後](今後.md) (こんご), "from now on," looks forward instead.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not treat the dictionary gloss "henceforth" as permission to replace every future expression with 以来. These examples anchor the period in a past event. The later situation may be positive, negative, continuing, or repeatedly renewed.
