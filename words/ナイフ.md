# ナイフ - knife

## Meaning

ナイフ means a knife, including a table knife or a small cutting knife.
Its precise shape and use come from context.

- ナイフとフォークを使います。 (ナイフとフォークをつかいます。) - I use a knife and fork.
- ナイフでパンを切りました。 (ナイフでパンをきりました。) - I cut the bread with a knife.

を marks the utensils as objects of "use"; で marks the knife as the tool
used for cutting.

## How the kanji combine

This English loanword uses katakana: ナ・イ・フ.
There is no long-vowel mark and no kanji decomposition. Its form remains
the same when more than one knife is meant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

包丁 (ほうちょう) is the ordinary word for a kitchen knife used in food
preparation. [フォーク](フォーク.md), "fork," and スプーン, "spoon," are related
table utensils, not synonyms or antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

The broad English word "knife" may correspond to ナイフ or a more specific
Japanese term. In a restaurant place setting, ナイフ is natural; do not
assume it replaces 包丁 in every cooking context.
