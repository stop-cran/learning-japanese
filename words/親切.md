# 親切 (しんせつ) - kind; considerate

## Meaning

親切 describes kindness and consideration for others. It works as a noun and a na-adjective: 親切な人 (しんせつなひと), "a kind person."

- 店の人はとても親切でした。 (みせのひとはとてもしんせつでした。) - The shop staff were very helpful.
- 近所の人に親切にしてもらいました。 (きんじょのひとにしんせつにしてもらいました。) - My neighbors treated me kindly.

親切に describes how someone acts; 親切です is the polite predicate.

## How the kanji combine

[親](../kanji/親.md) suggests closeness; [切](../kanji/切.md) contributes earnestness in this established compound. The reading is しん + せつ, not a literal phrase about cutting a parent.

## Synonyms and antonyms

優しい (やさしい) overlaps with kind or gentle. 不親切 (ふしんせつ) means unkind or unhelpful.

## Distinctive meaning

親切 often concerns helpful treatment, not simply a pleasant mood. A stranger can be 親切 without being a close friend. Retain な before a noun rather than treating it as an i-adjective.
