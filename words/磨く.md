# 磨く (みがく) — polish; brush teeth

## Meaning

磨く means to polish or clean a surface by rubbing or brushing. The affected object takes を. 歯を磨く (はをみがく) is the ordinary expression for brushing one's teeth.

- 寝る前に歯を磨きます。 (ねるまえにはをみがきます。) — I brush my teeth before bed.
- 週末に靴を磨きました。 (しゅうまつにくつをみがきました。) — I polished my shoes at the weekend.

## How the kanji combine

磨 supplies みが, followed by く. This is a godan verb: 磨きます (みがきます), 磨いて (みがいて), 磨かない (みがかない). Notice the いて ending in its て-form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

洗う (あらう) means to wash, usually with water or another liquid. 拭く (ふく) is to wipe. The actions can contribute to cleaning but do not have the same focus as polishing.

## Distinctive meaning

磨く is not a general translation for every use of English "brush": brushing teeth fits, but brushing hair needs a different expression. Figuratively, 技術を磨く (ぎじゅつをみがく) means to hone a skill. There is no single opposite covering both cleaning and skill improvement.
