# 映画 (えいが) — movie, film

## Meaning

映画 means a movie or film. It names the work, not the building where it is shown.

- 友達と映画を見ました。 (ともだちとえいがをみました。) — I watched a movie with a friend.
- 家で日本の映画を見ます。 (いえでにほんのえいがをみます。) — I watch Japanese films at home.

## How the kanji combine

[映](../kanji/映.md) エイ (project, reflect) + [画](../kanji/画.md) ガ (picture): an on + on compound. Think of a projected picture as a guide to the meaning, not a rule that a film must actually be projected to count as 映画.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 動画 どうが means video or moving images, often a digital clip; it is broader than a feature film.
- 映画館 えいがかん means a cinema or movie theater, the place rather than the movie.
- There is no natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

A movie watched at home is still 映画. Conversely, a short video on a phone is not automatically called 映画 just because its pictures move. The category of work matters more than the viewing device.
