# 代わる (かわる) - take someone's place; be replaced

## Meaning

代わる is an intransitive godan verb for taking another person's place,
being replaced, or changing over. The source spelling **代る (かわる)**
represents this same word; this article uses 代わる as its main spelling.

- 私が田中さんに代わって説明します。
  (わたしがたなかさんにかわってせつめいします。) - I will explain in Mr. Tanaka's place.
- 電話を母に代わります。
  (でんわをははにかわります。) - I will put my mother on the phone.

人に代わって identifies the person whose place someone takes.
Forms include 代わります, 代わって, and 代わった.
The telephone expression is an established use: the speaker changes over
to another person, rather than exchanging the telephone itself.

## How the kanji combine

[代](../kanji/代.md) contributes substitution or acting in someone's place.
The native verb is read かわる, not with an on reading.
The same dictionary family includes 替わる, 換わる and the shorter spellings
替る, 代る and 換る.

[替](../kanji/替.md) is especially associated with replacement or relief;
代 with taking someone's place or representing them; and
[換](../kanji/換.md) with exchange or switching places.
These are spelling tendencies, not mutually exclusive definitions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[代わり](代わり.md) (かわり) is the related noun, "substitute; replacement."
[替える](替える.md) and [換える](換える.md), both かえる, are transitive:
someone replaces or exchanges something. Their English glosses overlap,
but the participant patterns differ.

## Distinctive meaning

[変わる](変わる.md) (かわる) means changing or becoming different.
It is a different dictionary identity: a situation changing is not
automatically one person replacing another.
See the [change and replacement comparison](../articles/kaeru-change-replace-and-return.md)
for the overlapping spellings and transitive/intransitive distinction.
