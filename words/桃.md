# 桃 (もも) - peach tree; peach

## Meaning

桃 names the peach tree and its fruit, *Prunus persica*. The surrounding expression tells you whether a tree, fruit or flowering scene is intended.

- 桃を二つ買いました。 (ももをふたつかいました。) - I bought two peaches.
- 庭の桃が咲きました。 (にわのももがさきました。) - The peach tree in the garden has blossomed.

桃の木 (もものき) specifies the tree, 桃の実 (もものみ) the fruit, and 桃の花 (もものはな) the blossoms.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [桃](../kanji/桃.md) writes the native noun もも. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide. The on reading トウ appears instead in [白桃](白桃.md) (はくとう), white peach.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[果物](果物.md) (くだもの) is the broader food category "fruit." [梅](梅.md) (うめ), ume, and すもも, Japanese plum, name different plants; they are not alternative readings of 桃. There is no direct botanical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

桃色 (ももいろ) means pink, and dictionaries also record 桃 as an abbreviation of that colour name. Context matters: a fruit purchase and a colour label need not use the same sense. Do not infer the fruit's flesh colour from 桃色 or use 桃 as a replacement for every English colour expression involving "peach."
