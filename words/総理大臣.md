# 総理大臣 (そうりだいじん) - prime minister; head of the cabinet

## Meaning

総理大臣 means prime minister, the head of a cabinet government.
In Japan, 内閣総理大臣 (ないかくそうりだいじん) is the full office title.
総理 (そうり) is a shortened form used in the same setting.

- 総理大臣が新しい政策を発表しました。 (そうりだいじんがあたらしいせいさくをはっぴょうしました。) - The prime minister announced a new policy.
- 総理大臣は国会で質問に答えました。 (そうりだいじんはこっかいでしつもんにこたえました。) - The prime minister answered questions in the Diet.

The title names an office, not a particular officeholder.

## How the kanji combine

[総](../kanji/総.md) contributes overall control, and [理](../kanji/理.md)
contributes managing or putting in order. Together, 総理 concerns directing
the whole. [大](../kanji/大.md) + [臣](../kanji/臣.md) forms
[大臣](大臣.md) (だいじん), minister.
総理 modifies the office: the minister directing the government as a whole.
Learn the complete on-reading sequence そう + り + だい + じん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[首相](首相.md) (しゅしょう) also means prime minister.
大臣 is a minister more generally; not every cabinet minister is the prime minister.
[大統領](大統領.md) (だいとうりょう), president, names a different office.
These offices are not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer an unrestricted power to decide everything from 総, "overall."
The responsibilities and powers of an office depend on the country's system.
Likewise, the title does not mean monarch or every kind of political leader.
