# 穀物 (こくもつ) - grain; cereals

## Meaning

穀物 is grain or cereals, such as rice and wheat. It is a noun used when discussing
crops, food supplies, or grain as a category. Connect it to another noun with の:
穀物の生産 (こくもつのせいさん), "grain production."

- この地域では、米や麦などの穀物を育てています。 (このちいきでは、こめやむぎなどのこくもつをそだてています。) - People in this region grow grains such as rice and wheat or barley.
- 穀物を湿気の少ない場所に保管します。 (こくもつをしっけのすくないばしょにほかんします。) - We store grain in a place with little moisture.

The first example names members of the category; the second treats grain as stored
material.

## How the kanji combine

穀 supplies the grain meaning, and [物](../kanji/物.md) contributes things or
products. The first character identifies the kind of material named by the
compound. Read it コク + モツ, not こくぶつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[米](米.md) (こめ) is rice, one kind of 穀物 rather than a synonym for the whole
category. 麦 (むぎ) covers grains such as wheat and barley.
[野菜](野菜.md) (やさい), "vegetables," is a contrasting food category, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "cereal" may suggest a prepared breakfast food, but 穀物 does not specify
that product. Likewise, an English dictionary's "corn" can mean grain generally;
do not narrow 穀物 to maize. Name the particular crop when that distinction
matters.
