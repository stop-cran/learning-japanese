# 普段 (ふだん) - usually; everyday

## Meaning

普段 names ordinary, everyday circumstances and works adverbially as "usually" or "normally." Use **普段は** to introduce a normal habit, and **普段の + noun** for an everyday or usual thing.

- 普段は自転車で会社に行きます。 (ふだんはじてんしゃでかいしゃにいきます。) - I usually go to work by bicycle.
- 今日は普段の服で来てください。 (きょうはふだんのふくできてください。) - Please come in your everyday clothes today.

The first sentence describes the usual pattern without ruling out exceptions. **普段から** means "regularly; in everyday life," and **普段より** sets up comparison with what is usual.

## How the kanji combine

普 has associations with generality, and [段](../kanji/段.md) with a step or stage. However, JMdict marks 普段 as **ateji** and notes the earlier spelling 不断. Do not derive "everyday" by literally combining "general" and "step." Learn the established spelling and reading ふだん together.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[普通](普通.md) (ふつう) overlaps in what is ordinary or normal. [いつも](いつも.md) can describe what usually or repeatedly happens. [特別](特別.md) (とくべつ), "special," provides a contextual contrast to everyday circumstances.

## Distinctive meaning

普段 is about a baseline habit or ordinary setting, not a mathematically exact frequency. 普段は歩く can remain true even if the speaker sometimes takes a bus. The の in 普段の服 belongs to noun modification; do not replace it mechanically with な.
