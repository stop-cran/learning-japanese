# 媒体 (ばいたい) — medium for carrying information

## Meaning

A 媒体 is something through which information or something else is conveyed. When a newspaper carries an advertisement, the newspaper is the medium, not the message.

- 知らせを広める媒体として新聞を選んだ。 (しらせをひろめるばいたいとしてしんぶんをえらんだ。) — We chose newspapers as the medium for spreading the news.
- 同じ広告を複数の媒体に載せた。 (おなじこうこくをふくすうのばいたいにのせた。) — We placed the same advertisement in several media.

## How the kanji combine

[媒](../kanji/媒.md) (ばい) supplies the idea of an intermediary. [体](../kanji/体.md) (たい) helps name the thing serving in that role. In the advertising example it does not mean a person's body.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[新聞](新聞.md) is one possible medium. [手段](手段.md) is a means or method more broadly: a means of transport need not be a medium of communication.

## Distinctive meaning

Separate the roles: in the second example 広告 (こうこく) is what readers see, while 媒体 is where it appears. 媒体 need not be printed: a website can serve the same role.
