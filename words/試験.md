# 試験 (しけん) — examination; test

## Meaning

試験 is an examination or test, such as a school exam. It can also mean a trial that checks performance or quality.
The academic sense is a useful starting point.

- 明日は英語の試験があります。（あしたはえいごのしけんがあります。） — There is an English exam tomorrow.
- 来週、試験を受けます。（らいしゅう、しけんをうけます。） — I will take an exam next week.

## How the kanji combine

[試](../kanji/試.md) シ means trying or testing, and [験](../kanji/験.md) ケン concerns testing or verification.
This on+on compound joins related ideas: a test that checks knowledge, ability, or results.

## Synonyms and antonyms

テスト overlaps strongly and is also common for school tests and practical checks; it is not restricted to small quizzes.
試す（ためす）, "try out; test," is a verb for trying something, not the noun for an exam.
There is no direct antonym of 試験.

## Distinctive meaning

A student **takes** an exam with 試験を受ける（しけんをうける）.
Although 試験 can form a する verb for testing something, 試験する is not the normal way to say
that you sit an exam. Learn the noun together with the phrase appropriate to the participant.
