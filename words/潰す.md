# 潰す (つぶす) - crush; wreck; waste

## Meaning

潰す means to crush, squash or ruin something. It is transitive: what someone crushes or ruins takes を.

- 空き缶を潰しました。
  (あきかんをつぶしました。) - I crushed an empty can.
- せっかくの才能を潰してはいけません。
  (せっかくのさいのうをつぶしてはいけません。) - You must not waste such talent.

The verb can also describe ruining a business, wrecking a plan or blocking a chance.
時間を潰す (じかんをつぶす) is "kill time": occupying a waiting period does not necessarily mean wasting it regrettably.

- バスが来るまで、喫茶店で時間を潰しました。
  (バスがくるまで、きっさてんでじかんをつぶしました。) - I killed time at a cafe until the bus came.

## How the kanji combine

潰 writes the native つぶ in this verb; す is okurigana.
It is godan: 潰します, 潰した, 潰して. There is no separate 潰 card in this collection.
Learn it with [潰れる](潰れる.md) (つぶれる), the intransitive partner for several of these uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[砕く](砕く.md) (くだく) emphasizes breaking into pieces; crushing something with 潰す need not produce separate fragments.
[壊す](壊す.md) (こわす) is broader breaking or damaging.
[無駄](無駄.md) (むだ) concerns waste or futility, but 暇を潰す (ひまをつぶす), "kill time," need not criticize the activity.

直す (なおす), "repair," or 立て直す (たてなおす), "rebuild; restore," can contrast in appropriate contexts.
They are not universal reversals of crushing.

## Distinctive meaning

The physical core suggests loss of shape or usability, with extensions to opportunities, businesses and abilities.
Do not translate every occurrence as "smash into pieces," and do not interpret every time-filling use as an accusation of waste.
