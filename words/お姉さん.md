# お姉さん (おねえさん) — older sister; respectful form

## Meaning

お姉さん is a respectful or familiar way to refer to an older sister, especially someone else's, and a common way to address one's own older sister.

- お姉さんはどこに住んでいますか。 (おねえさんはどこにすんでいますか。) — Where does your older sister live?
- お姉さん、これを見て。 (おねえさん、これをみて。) — Sis, look at this.

When talking to an outsider about your own older sister, 姉 (あね) is the usual neutral choice.

## How the kanji combine

[姉](../kanji/姉.md) is read ねえ in this word, surrounded by the respectful prefix お and suffix さん. Keep the long え sound: おねえさん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

妹 (いもうと) means "younger sister." お兄さん (おにいさん) is the corresponding term for an older brother. These are family-role contrasts, not general opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

お姉さん can also address or describe a young woman or older girl who is not a relative. That use depends on the setting and relationship; it is not a universally appropriate substitute for a stranger's name.
