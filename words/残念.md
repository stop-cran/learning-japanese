# 残念 (ざんねん) — disappointing; regrettable

## Meaning

残念 expresses disappointment or regret about an unfortunate outcome. Use 残念です as a complete
reaction, or 残念な before a noun.

- 一緒に行けなくて残念です。 (いっしょにいけなくてざんねんです。) — It is a pity that I cannot go with you.
- それは残念な結果です。 (それはざんねんなけっかです。) — That is a disappointing result.

The first sentence leaves the companion to context; its English translation assumes the speaker is addressing that companion.
The Japanese does not specify who caused the problem.

## How the kanji combine

[残](../kanji/残.md) has a remaining/left-over meaning; [念](../kanji/念.md) concerns a thought or feeling.
A "feeling that remains" can help you remember the established word for regret or disappointment.
That is a learning association, not a historical derivation. The readings are ザン + ネン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[悲しい](悲しい.md) (かなしい) describes sadness. You can be both sad and disappointed,
but 残念 highlights the regrettable outcome rather than simply naming sadness.
There is no one opposite that fits every 残念な situation.

## Distinctive meaning

残念です can express sympathy when someone reports disappointing news. It does not by itself say
that you caused the problem or accept responsibility, so it is not a general substitute for an apology.
Remember the attachment: 残念な結果, but 結果は残念です (けっかはざんねんです).
