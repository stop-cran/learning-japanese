# 何々 (なになに) - such and such; what?

## Meaning

何々 can stand for unspecified things or names: "such and such,"
"so-and-so" or "something or other." In a question it can ask about
multiple unspecified things.

- 「何々を買う」とメモに書いてください。 (「なになにをかう」とメモにかいてください。) - Write "buy such and such" in the note.
- 「何々、もう一度言って。」 (「なになに、もういちどいって。」) - "What? Say that again."

The first example deliberately uses a placeholder rather than an actual
shopping list. The second uses an interjection asking for confirmation,
not a noun naming an object.

## How the kanji combine

[何](../kanji/何.md), "what," is repeated: なに + なに.
何何 is another recorded spelling. 々 repeats the preceding character;
the whole word is なになに, not なんなん or なになん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[何か](何か.md) (なにか) means "something" or asks whether there is
anything; it does not by itself supply a repeated listing placeholder.
[あれこれ](あれこれ.md) concerns this and that, various things.
It can overlap in vague reference without having every 何々 function.

## Distinctive meaning

As an interjection, なになに can also introduce reading a document aloud,
roughly "let's see," or brush aside another person's words or concerns.
Those additional uses depend on the exchange and delivery; not every
なになに is a surprised question. Do not confuse a placeholder name with
the ordinary name of a person, or treat repetition as a plural ending
that can be attached to any Japanese word.
