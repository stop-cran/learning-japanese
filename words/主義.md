# 主義 (しゅぎ) - principle; doctrine

## Meaning

主義 is a noun for a principle, doctrine, or guiding approach. It also appears in compounds corresponding to English "-ism," such as 民主主義 (みんしゅしゅぎ), "democracy."

- 私は約束を守る主義です。 (わたしはやくそくをまもるしゅぎです。) - I make a point of keeping my promises.
- 彼は自分の主義を変えませんでした。 (かれはじぶんのしゅぎをかえませんでした。) - He did not change his principles.

In the first example, a plain-form clause before 主義 states the policy someone follows. A negative clause also works: 無理をしない主義 (むりをしないしゅぎ), "a policy of not overdoing things."

## How the kanji combine

[主](../kanji/主.md) contributes what is main or guiding; 義 contributes a principle or meaning. Together, they suggest the guiding principle of a position or approach. The compound is シュ + ギ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[考え](考え.md) (かんがえ) is a thought or view, not necessarily a settled principle. 方針 (ほうしん) is a policy or direction for action and can overlap in practical contexts. [規則](規則.md) (きそく) is a rule; 主義 need not be a rule imposed on other people.

## Distinctive meaning

主義 can name a large political doctrine or a modest personal practice. Saying 早く寝る主義です (はやくねるしゅぎです), "I make a point of going to bed early," does not create a political ideology. Context determines the scale and seriousness of the principle; there is no universal antonym.
