# 舞台 (ぶたい) — performance stage

## Meaning

舞台 is the stage in a theater, concert hall, or other performance setting.
The focus here is the place where performers appear, not a step in a process.

- 子供たちが舞台で歌っています。
  (こどもたちがぶたいでうたっています。)
  — The children are singing on the stage.
- 舞台に立つのは初めてです。
  (ぶたいにたつのははじめてです。)
  — This is my first time standing on stage.

## How the kanji combine

[舞](../kanji/舞.md), "dance," qualifies [台](../kanji/台.md), "stand; platform."
The combination identifies a platform for performance, though actual performances there need
not be dances. The on readings are ブ + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ステージ overlaps in the physical-platform sense.
段階 (だんかい), "stage; phase," is a different word for a stage of progress or development.
The English word "stage" therefore does not tell you by itself which Japanese noun to use.

## Distinctive meaning

Notice the particles in the examples: 舞台で歌う (ぶたいでうたう) locates the singing activity;
舞台に立つ (ぶたいにたつ) places the person on the stage.
舞台 names the performance space, not simply any raised object that someone can stand on.
