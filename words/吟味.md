# 吟味 (ぎんみ) — close examination; careful selection

## Meaning

吟味 means examining something carefully, or choosing carefully after close examination. JMdict glosses it as close examination, careful investigation, careful selection and scrutiny, and Digital Daijisen defines it as examining things carefully, or examining them carefully and choosing. It can be used with する.

- 店長は仕入れる野菜を一つ一つ吟味しています。 (てんちょうはしいれるやさいをひとつひとつぎんみしています。) — The store manager examines each vegetable the store buys in, one by one.
- 言葉を吟味して、手紙を書きました。 (ことばをぎんみして、てがみをかきました。) — I chose my words carefully and wrote the letter.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[吟](../kanji/吟.md) can mean reciting poetry, but here it has its sense of examining contents closely: Digital Daijisen lists 吟味 under that sense of 吟. [味](../kanji/味.md) means taste or savoring. Both sources mark poetry recitation as the original meaning of 吟味: Digital Daijisen sense 3 is reciting a poem and savoring its flavor, and JMdict sense 4 (archaic) is “reciting and appreciating traditional poetry”.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[検討](検討.md) (けんとう) is consideration or examination (JMdict entry 1258000), and [検査](検査.md) (けんさ) is an inspection or test (JMdict entry 1257890). [選択](選択.md) (せんたく) is choosing; 吟味 adds the careful examination before the choice.

## Distinctive meaning

Besides careful examination, JMdict lists a dated sense, investigation of a crime (sense 2), which Digital Daijisen also gives, and an archaic sense, reciting and appreciating traditional poetry, noted as the original meaning (sense 4).
