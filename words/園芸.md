# 園芸 (えんげい) - horticulture; gardening

## Meaning

園芸 is horticulture or gardening: growing and caring for plants such
as flowers, vegetables or fruit. It can concern a hobby or a field of
cultivation; the noun does not itself identify a professional gardener.

- 母の趣味は園芸です。
  (ははのしゅみはえんげいです。) - My mother's hobby is gardening.
- 園芸の本で花の育て方を調べました。
  (えんげいのほんではなのそだてかたをしらべました。) - I looked up how to grow flowers in a gardening book.

園芸の本 means a book about gardening. の connects the activity
with the noun it describes.

## How the kanji combine

[園](../kanji/園.md) contributes a garden, and
[芸](../kanji/芸.md) a skill or cultivated practice. Think of a
garden-related skill: エン + ゲイ. This is a memory aid for the
established compound, not an account of the characters' historical origins.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ガーデニング also means gardening.
[農業](農業.md) (のうぎょう), agriculture, concerns farming more broadly;
the fields can overlap, rather than dividing every plant by a simple rule.
[庭](庭.md) (にわ) names a garden or yard, not the activity of gardening.

## Distinctive meaning

園芸 is not restricted to flowers, and the word alone does not require
a large outdoor garden. Learn it as the cultivation activity, not as
another name for a park or for artistic performance.
