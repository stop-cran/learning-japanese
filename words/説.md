# 説 (せつ) - theory; view

## Meaning

説 is a noun for a theory, explanation, or view. It can also refer to an unconfirmed account or rumor. The construction clause + という説 introduces what the theory says.

- この説を支持する証拠があります。 (このせつをしじするしょうこがあります。) - There is evidence supporting this theory.
- その建物は百年前に建てられたという説があります。 (そのたてものはひゃくねんまえにたてられたというせつがあります。) - One theory is that the building was constructed a hundred years ago.

The second sentence reports an explanation; it does not establish the building's date as a fact. 人の説 can identify whose view it is.

## How the kanji combine

[説](../kanji/説.md) alone writes this noun with the on reading セツ. It also acts as an ending in names of theories or doctrines. There is no second written component to analyze here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion and need not be an explanatory theory. 理論 (りろん) suggests a body of reasoning or theory. [事実](事実.md) (じじつ), "fact," is a useful contrast, but a theory can be well supported; 説 does not mean "false."

## Distinctive meaning

Evaluate the wording around 説. 有力な説 (ゆうりょくなせつ) is a strong or leading theory, while 単なる説 (たんなるせつ) presents something as merely a theory. The bare noun supplies neither proof nor disproof, and its rumor sense does not make every scientific use speculative gossip.
