# 白髪 (しらが) - white or gray hair

## Meaning

白髪 means white or gray hair. It can refer to white hairs individually
or collectively. It is a noun and can modify another noun with の.

- 最近、白髪が増えました。 (さいきん、しらががふえました。) - Recently, I have more gray hair.
- 白髪の男性が入ってきました。 (しらがのだんせいがはいってきました。) - A man with gray hair came in.

The first example describes a change in hair, not a change of hairstyle.
The noun alone does not state the person's age.

## How the kanji combine

[白](../kanji/白.md) supplies white, and [髪](../kanji/髪.md) supplies
head hair. The first character qualifies the second. Learn the native
reading しらが as a whole; it does not establish が as a general reading
of 髪.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[髪](髪.md) (かみ) and [髪の毛](髪の毛.md) (かみのけ) name head hair
without specifying its color. [毛](毛.md) (け) is broader hair or fur.
Black hair is a color contrast, not a requirement that everyone without
白髪 must have black hair.

## Distinctive meaning

The same dictionary entry also records はくはつ for white or gray hair;
the examples here use しらが. The source list's extra gloss about fashionable
bleaching is not a separate meaning established by the inspected entry.
白髪 is not a verb meaning bleach one's hair or a word that by itself
identifies a fashion trend.
