# 辞書 (じしょ) — dictionary

## Meaning

辞書 is a dictionary: a reference for checking words and their meanings.
Use 辞書で to identify it as the resource used to look something up.

- 辞書で言葉の意味を調べました。 (じしょでことばのいみをしらべました。) — I looked up the meaning of the word in a dictionary.
- この辞書を借りてもいいですか。 (このじしょをかりてもいいですか。) — May I borrow this dictionary?

## How the kanji combine

[辞](../kanji/辞.md) supplies the word or expression sense, and [書](../kanji/書.md) the written-work association.
Think "a reference work about words." The readings are ジ + ショ.
辞's resigning sense is not active in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

辞典 (じてん) also means a dictionary or lexicon.
For learning the basic object, treat it as a close synonym; do not invent a strict division that makes one word mean translations
and the other definitions.
There is no direct everyday antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

A 辞書 is identified by its reference function, not simply by containing written language.
In the example 辞書で言葉の意味を調べる, the word's meaning is what you investigate and the dictionary is the tool.
That explains the different roles of を and で.
