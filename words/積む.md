# 積む (つむ) — stack; load

## Meaning

積む means stacking objects, and also loading them onto a vehicle or accumulating something such as experience.
It is transitive: the thing stacked or loaded takes を.
For loading, に can mark where the load goes.

- 箱を三つ積みました。 (はこをみっつつみました。) — I stacked three boxes.
- トラックに荷物を積みました。 (トラックににもつをつみました。) — I loaded the luggage onto the truck.

It is a godan verb: 積みます (つみます), 積んだ (つんだ), 積んで (つんで).

## How the kanji combine

[積](../kanji/積.md) carries the idea of building up or loading.
む is the native verb ending, not a separate word.
The visible ending changes in forms such as 積んだ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

重ねる (かさねる), "put one on top of another; stack," overlaps when arranging layers.
積む also has a loading sense: loading luggage into a vehicle need not mean building a tall pile.

For accumulation happening rather than someone stacking an object, compare 積もる (つもる):
雪が積もりました (ゆきがつもりました), "Snow accumulated."
In this sense, 積もる is intransitive; the accumulating thing takes が rather than being an object with を.

## Distinctive meaning

経験を積む (けいけんをつむ) means "gain experience."
It treats experience as something accumulated through doing, not as objects physically placed in a stack.
The concrete stacking sense, the loading sense, and this abstract use share a verb, but their English translations differ.
