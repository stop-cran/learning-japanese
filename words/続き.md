# 続き (つづき) - continuation; rest of a story or task

## Meaning

続き is a noun for the continuation or remaining part of a story, task, or other sequence. Use Nの続き for "the rest of N." It can refer to the next installment without implying a separately published sequel.

- 昨日の話の続きを聞かせてください。 (きのうのはなしのつづきをきかせてください。) - Please tell me the rest of yesterday's story.
- 仕事の続きは明日やります。 (しごとのつづきはあしたやります。) - I will do the rest of the work tomorrow.

続きから読む (つづきからよむ) means to resume reading from where you left off. から marks the starting point.

## How the kanji combine

[続](../kanji/続.md) contributes continuation and is read つづ before き. This noun is related to the verb [続く](続く.md) (つづく), "continue." The spelling uses づ, not ず.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[続ける](続ける.md) (つづける) is a transitive verb: 仕事を続ける means "continue the work." 仕事の続き instead names the remaining portion. Changing between them requires changing the sentence structure.

## Distinctive meaning

The English "rest" here means what remains, not relaxation. Likewise, "sequel" is just one possible translation: a conversation's 続き may simply be the next thing someone says. The noun points backward to an already established sequence, so context supplies what is being continued.
