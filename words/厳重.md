# 厳重 (げんじゅう) - strict; rigorous; secure

## Meaning

厳重 describes strict, rigorous, or carefully secured handling.
It is a noun and a na-adjective: 厳重な before a noun and 厳重に
when modifying an action.

- 貴重品は厳重に保管してください。 (きちょうひんはげんじゅうにほかんしてください。) - Please keep valuables securely stored.
- 警察は会場を厳重に警備しています。 (けいさつはかいじょうをげんじゅうにけいびしています。) - The police are keeping the venue under tight security.

厳重な注意 (げんじゅうなちゅうい) can be a stern warning;
厳重な抗議 (げんじゅうなこうぎ) is a strong protest.
English "firm" or "strong" concerns that seriousness or forcefulness here,
not physical hardness or muscular strength.

## How the kanji combine

厳 contributes strictness, and [重](../kanji/重.md) contributes weight or
importance. As a mnemonic, imagine treating a matter with weighty seriousness;
this is not a historical derivation. The on readings are ゲン + ジュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[厳しい](厳しい.md) (きびしい) overlaps in strictness, but can also
describe harsh weather or a strict person.
[徹底](徹底.md) (てってい) concerns thoroughness.
[警備](警備.md) (けいび) is security or guarding, an activity that can
be carried out 厳重に.

## Distinctive meaning

厳重 is useful for precautions, control, warnings, or protection.
Do not automatically replace 厳しい先生 with 厳重な先生.
Learn げんじゅう for these current uses; the dictionary's obsolete
げんじょう and げんちょう are not alternative everyday pronunciations taught here.
