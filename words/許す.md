# 許す (ゆるす) — allow; forgive

## Meaning

許す means to permit or allow. It also has a distinct forgiveness sense.
For permission to do an action, a verb phrase followed by ことを can name what is allowed.

- 先生は辞書を使うことを許しました。 (せんせいはじしょをつかうことをゆるしました。) — The teacher allowed us to use a dictionary.
- 私の間違いを許してください。 (わたしのまちがいをゆるしてください。) — Please forgive my mistake.

It is a transitive godan verb: 許します (ゆるします), 許した (ゆるした),
and negative 許さない (ゆるさない). Both examples mark the thing permitted or forgiven with を.

## How the kanji combine

[許](../kanji/許.md) carries the permission association in this native verb.
The final す belongs to its inflection. There is no second kanji to interpret as a separate object:
what is permitted or forgiven is supplied by the sentence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[許可](許可.md)する (きょかする) means to authorize or give permission. It fits the permission sense,
not automatically the forgiveness sense.
[認める](../words/認める.md) (みとめる) can mean approve or allow, but also recognize or admit;
admitting that a mistake happened is not the same as forgiving it.

## Distinctive meaning

The same spelling covers allowing an action and forgiving a fault. Do not assume that
許してください always asks for permission to begin something: it can be an appeal for forgiveness.
The object and situation decide which interpretation fits.
