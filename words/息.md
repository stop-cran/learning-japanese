# 息 (いき) — breath; breathing

## Meaning

息 is breath or breathing. Learn it with the verbs for taking air in and letting it out.

- ゆっくり息を吸ってください。 (ゆっくりいきをすってください。) — Please breathe in slowly.
- 次に、息を吐いてください。 (つぎに、いきをはいてください。) — Next, please breathe out.

息 is the を-marked noun in both sentences.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [息](../kanji/息.md) writes the native noun いき. This is a whole word,
not an abbreviation of a two-kanji expression. The reading ソク belongs to other words, not to the
two example sentences above.

## Synonyms and antonyms

呼吸 (こきゅう) also names breathing, and 呼吸する (こきゅうする) means "breathe."
[吸う](吸う.md) (すう), "inhale," and 吐く (はく), "exhale" in this context,
contrast the two directions of the action; they are not antonyms of the noun 息.

## Distinctive meaning

息をする (いきをする) means "to breathe":

- ゆっくり息をしてください。 (ゆっくりいきをしてください。) — Please breathe slowly.

Unlike 息を吸う (いきをすう), "breathe in," and 息を吐く (いきをはく), "breathe out,"
息をする describes breathing without selecting one direction. 呼吸する is another way to say "breathe."
