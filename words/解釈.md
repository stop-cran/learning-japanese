# 解釈 (かいしゃく) - interpretation

## Meaning

解釈 is an interpretation: an understanding of what words, behavior, or information
mean. 解釈する is transitive. Use something + を + 解釈する, or AをBと解釈する
for "interpret A as B."

- この文をどう解釈しますか。 (このぶんをどうかいしゃくしますか。) - How do you interpret this sentence?
- 私はその返事を断りと解釈しました。 (わたしはそのへんじをことわりとかいしゃくしました。) - I interpreted that reply as a refusal.

The second example reports the speaker's understanding; it does not prove
that the other person intended to refuse.

## How the kanji combine

[解](../kanji/解.md) contributes unraveling or understanding, and 釈 contributes
explaining. The related meanings combine into making sense of something.
The on readings are カイ + シャク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[説明](説明.md) (せつめい) is an explanation given to make something clear.
解釈 focuses on the meaning someone takes from it. An explanation can present
an interpretation, but the two words name different aspects of that activity.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 文の解釈 (ぶんのかいしゃく), "interpretation of a sentence," for the noun
construction. Different people can offer 異なる解釈 (ことなるかいしゃく),
"different interpretations," without the word itself deciding which is correct.
It is not a general term for spoken language interpreting between two languages.
