# 手品 (てじな) - magic trick; conjuring

## Meaning

手品 is a magic trick or conjuring performed as entertainment. It also names the activity as a whole. Useful patterns include 手品をする, "perform magic tricks," and 手品を見せる (てじなをみせる), "show someone a magic trick."

- 兄はカードの手品が得意です。 (あにはカードのてじながとくいです。) - My older brother is good at card tricks.
- 子どもたちに手品を見せました。 (こどもたちにてじなをみせました。) - I showed the children a magic trick.

The performer creates the appearance of something surprising happening; the word does not assert that supernatural powers are involved.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md) supplies the hand association, and [品](../kanji/品.md) can suggest an item or kind. Read the established word as てじな: しな becomes じな in the compound. These associations can aid recognition, but "hand goods" is not a definition or a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

マジック overlaps in its performance sense. 魔法 (まほう) refers to magic or sorcery, for example in a fantasy story. Neither comparison supplies a direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list includes the older English gloss "juggling." This card teaches illusion and sleight of hand, not the modern activity of repeatedly tossing and catching objects. A stage performer may do both, but the skills are not identical.
