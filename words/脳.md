# 脳 (のう) - brain; mind

## Meaning

脳 is the brain as an organ. It is a noun; 脳の働き (のうのはたらき)
means "the functioning of the brain." It also has an extended brains/mind
sense, so not every occurrence is a strictly anatomical description.

- 脳は体の動きを制御します。 (のうはからだのうごきをせいぎょします。) - The brain controls the body's movements.
- 研究者は脳の働きを調べています。 (けんきゅうしゃはのうのはたらきをしらべています。) - Researchers are studying how the brain functions.

大脳 (だいのう) is the cerebrum, a particular part of the brain.
It is not a synonym for every part of 脳.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [脳](../kanji/脳.md) uses the on reading ノウ.
There is no compound to divide. In 脳波 (のうは), "brain waves; EEG,"
脳 contributes brain and 波 contributes waves.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頭](頭.md) (あたま) is the head, the body part containing the brain.
It also has a brains/intellect sense. [知能](知能.md) (ちのう)
names intellectual ability rather than the physical organ.

[心](心.md) (こころ) and [精神](精神.md) (せいしん) concern the
mind or spirit in their relevant uses. Their overlap with 脳's
mental extension does not make them names for the anatomical brain.
There is no useful direct antonym for a brain.

[気](気.md) (き) also has a mind sense. The body nouns [胸](胸.md)
(むね) and [腹](腹.md) (はら) likewise have figurative mental uses.
Those uses can overlap with an English mind label without naming the
brain organ or licensing arbitrary substitution in Japanese.

## Distinctive meaning

Start with the organ meaning, then interpret mental extensions in their
whole expressions. Do not translate every English "mind" as 脳 or
assume that 頭 and 脳 have no figurative overlap. The direct quiz
exclusions protect overlapping labels, not unrestricted substitution.
