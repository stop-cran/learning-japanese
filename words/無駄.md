# 無駄 (むだ) - waste; futility; pointlessness

## Meaning

無駄 is a noun or な-adjective for waste, futility, or something serving no useful purpose. Use 無駄な before a noun, 無駄だ as a predicate, and Nを無駄にする for wasting a resource.

- 時間を無駄にしないでください。 (じかんをむだにしないでください。) - Please do not waste time.
- この経験は無駄ではありませんでした。 (このけいけんはむだではありませんでした。) - This experience was not wasted.

無駄な出費 (むだなしゅっぴ) means unnecessary or wasteful expenditure. 無駄に adds the sense "in vain" or "wastefully" to an action.

## How the kanji combine

The written characters are 無 and 駄, but the dictionary identifies 無駄 as ateji: an established spelling rather than a transparent kanji-by-kanji derivation. Learn the native word むだ as a whole. A story about an unloaded packhorse is not a reliable explanation of its origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[無理](無理.md) (むり) concerns impossibility, unreasonableness, or excessive strain. Something may be possible but 無駄 because it achieves nothing useful. [役に立つ](役に立つ.md) (やくにたつ), "be useful," provides a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

An unsuccessful attempt is not necessarily 無駄: the speaker may value what was learned. The second example explicitly rejects a wasteful interpretation. The word expresses a judgment about usefulness or resources, not merely whether an action reached its original goal.
