# 圧力 (あつりょく) - pressure

## Meaning

圧力 is a noun for physical pressure or pressure exerted on a person or organization.

- 容器の中の圧力が高くなりました。 (ようきのなかのあつりょくがたかくなりました。) - The pressure inside the container increased.
- 会社は政府に圧力をかけました。 (かいしゃはせいふにあつりょくをかけました。) - The company put pressure on the government.

圧力をかける (あつりょくをかける) means to apply or exert pressure. In the second example, the organization receiving the pressure takes に.

## How the kanji combine

[圧](../kanji/圧.md) expresses pressing or pressure; [力](../kanji/力.md) contributes force or power. This modifier-and-head combination names pressure. Learn the on readings アツ + リョク as あつりょく, not あつちから.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[力](力.md) (ちから) is broader strength, power or force, not specifically pressure. 圧力を上げる (あつりょくをあげる), "raise the pressure," and 圧力を下げる (あつりょくをさげる), "lower the pressure," give opposing changes; there is no single noun that is the opposite of every use of 圧力.

## Distinctive meaning

Political or organizational 圧力 does not require physical contact. Conversely, pressure inside a container does not imply that anyone is being intimidated. The surrounding construction identifies which meaning is intended.
