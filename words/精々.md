# 精々 (せいぜい) - at most; as much as possible

## Meaning

精々 is usually written せいぜい. This adverb can set an upper limit, "at most; at best," or mean doing as much as one can.

- 参加者はせいぜい十人でしょう。 (さんかしゃはせいぜいじゅうにんでしょう。) - There will probably be ten participants at most.
- せいぜい頑張って。 (せいぜいがんばって。) - Do the best you can.

The second expression needs care: depending on tone and the relationship, it can sound dismissive, as though the speaker has low expectations. It is not an automatically warm encouragement.

## How the kanji combine

[精](../kanji/精.md) has associations with energy or effort. 々 repeats that character, so 精々 is a repeated-character spelling, not two different kanji. Learn the established readings せい + ぜい and the whole adverb; a literal component translation does not predict both uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[最高](最高.md) (さいこう) can name a maximum or highest level, but its grammar differs. せいぜい directly limits the following estimate.

少なくとも (すくなくとも), "at least," sets a lower bound and provides a useful contrast to the upper-limit sense.

## Distinctive meaning

The two senses share the idea of a limit: the greatest amount expected, or the utmost effort possible. Context decides which is intended. To encourage someone without the possible limiting implication, a plain 頑張って can be clearer. Do not translate every occurrence as either sarcastic or supportive.
