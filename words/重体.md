# 重体 (じゅうたい) - critical medical condition

## Meaning

重体 is a serious or critical medical condition, with danger to life. It can result from an illness or an injury; it does not mean only "seriously ill."

- 事故にあった人は重体です。
  (じこにあったひとはじゅうたいです。) - The person involved in the accident is in critical condition.
- 彼はまだ重体です。
  (かれはまだじゅうたいです。) - He is still in critical condition.

重体になる means entering a critical condition. 重態 is another spelling of the same word and reading.

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) contributes severity rather than literal weight, and [体](../kanji/体.md) concerns the body. Together they name a grave bodily condition. In 重態, 態 instead explicitly contributes condition or state. The reading is ジュウ + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[病気](病気.md) (びょうき) is illness, while [症状](症状.md) (しょうじょう) concerns symptoms or how a condition manifests. 重体 specifies critical severity; it is not a name for every illness or symptom.

## Distinctive meaning

[渋滞](渋滞.md) has the same reading じゅうたい but means traffic congestion or stagnation. An accident can lead to both a traffic jam and an injured person in critical condition; the shared scene does not make the words synonyms.
