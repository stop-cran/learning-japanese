# 見解 (けんかい) - view; considered opinion

## Meaning

見解 is a view or opinion about a matter. It is useful when presenting or comparing how people interpret an issue. Use matter + についての見解 for "a view on a matter," and 見解を示す (けんかいをしめす) for stating a view.

- この問題についての見解を聞かせてください。 (このもんだいについてのけんかいをきかせてください。) - Please tell me your view on this issue.
- 二人の見解は違います。 (ふたりのけんかいはちがいます。) - The two people have different views.

政府の見解 (せいふのけんかい) means "the government's view"; the holder of the opinion can be an organization as well as an individual.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) concerns seeing or viewing, while [解](../kanji/解.md) contributes understanding or interpretation. Together they frame a way of seeing and understanding a matter. The readings are ケン + カイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意見](意見.md) (いけん) is a broad word for an opinion. [考え](考え.md) (かんがえ) can mean a thought or idea more generally. 見解 fits an explicitly formulated position, including in reports and discussions, without being restricted to official statements.

## Distinctive meaning

A 見解 is someone's interpretation, not automatically an established fact or a decision. Two experts can have different 見解 even when discussing the same evidence.
