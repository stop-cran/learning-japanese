# 捨てる (すてる) — throw away; discard

## Meaning

捨てる means to throw away or discard something no longer wanted. It is a transitive ichidan verb: the discarded thing takes を.

- 古い新聞を捨てました。 (ふるいしんぶんをすてました。) — I threw away the old newspapers.
- この箱はまだ捨てないでください。 (このはこはまだすてないでください。) — Please don't throw this box away yet.

Useful forms are 捨てます (すてます), 捨てて (すてて), and 捨てない (すてない).

## How the kanji combine

捨 supplies the discarding idea and is read す here. The written ending is てる; the conjugating stem is 捨て (すて).

## Synonyms and antonyms

残す (のこす), "leave; keep," can provide the practical contrast. 投げる (なげる) means physically throwing something through the air, whether or not it is being discarded.

## Distinctive meaning

捨てる describes giving something up or disposing of it, not a particular throwing motion. Carefully placing rubbish in a bin can still be ごみを捨てる (ごみをすてる). Conversely, throwing a ball to someone is normally 投げる, not 捨てる.
