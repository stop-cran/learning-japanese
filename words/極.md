# 極 (ごく) - very; extremely

## Meaning

極, read ごく, is an adverb meaning "very" or "extremely." Kana is usual for this use.
It directly strengthens the following expression; do not add に after ごく.
Useful combinations include ごく普通 (ごくふつう), "quite ordinary," and ごく少し (ごくすこし), "very little."

- ごく普通の家庭で育ちました。 (ごくふつうのかていでそだちました。) - I grew up in a quite ordinary household.
- 塩を入れるのは、ごく少しで十分です。 (しおをいれるのは、ごくすこしでじゅうぶんです。) - A very small amount of salt is enough.

The second sentence emphasizes how small the amount is. ごく does not invariably make the
quantity large; it intensifies the expression that follows.

## How the kanji combine

極 is associated with an extreme or limit. ごく is an on-reading-based word written with
this single character, not a two-kanji compound. The dictionary marks the adverb as usually kana;
the assigned written form remains useful for recognition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とても](とても.md) also intensifies a description. [非常](非常.md) (ひじょう) appears in
非常に, "extremely," with different attachment.
[少し](少し.md) (すこし) names a small degree or amount and can itself be strengthened by ごく.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn ごく in complete phrases instead of treating it as a replacement for every English "very."
ごく一部 (ごくいちぶ), "a very small part," is another useful pattern.
Other readings and technical meanings of 極 do not determine the meaning of this adverb.
