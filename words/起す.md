# 起す (おこす) — wake someone up

## Meaning

起す is a spelling of 起こす, "wake someone up". Use the usual fuller spelling 起こす in your own writing. The person being awakened takes を; the time can take に.

- 明日、七時に起こしてください。  
  あした、しちじにおこしてください。  
  "Please wake me up at seven tomorrow."
- 母が弟を起こしました。  
  ははがおとうとをおこしました。  
  "My mother woke my younger brother."

## How the kanji combine

[起](../kanji/起.md) relates to rising. The whole verb is おこす in either spelling. Its godan forms are 起こします (おこします), 起こして (おこして), and 起こさない (おこさない); the shorter spelling does not change the conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

起きる (おきる), "wake up; get up", describes the sleeper's own change. Compare 弟が起きた (おとうとがおきた), "My younger brother woke up", with 弟を起こした, "I woke my younger brother".

## Distinctive meaning

English "wake up" can describe either pattern, but Japanese distinguishes them. 起こす also means "cause; bring about" in other contexts; a person being awakened is the beginner sense here.
