# 欠点 (けってん) - flaw; weak point

## Meaning

欠点 is a flaw, weakness, shortcoming, or drawback. It is a noun and can describe a person, an object, or a plan. Use Nの欠点 for "N's weak point" and 欠点がある for "have a flaw."

- この計画には欠点があります。 (このけいかくにはけってんがあります。) - This plan has a drawback.
- 彼は自分の欠点を認めました。 (かれはじぶんのけってんをみとめました。) - He acknowledged his own shortcomings.

The first example presents the plan as the setting for a flaw with には. The second uses を because the shortcoming is what he acknowledges.

## How the kanji combine

[欠](../kanji/欠.md) contributes lack or deficiency, and [点](../kanji/点.md) contributes a point or aspect. Together they name an aspect that is deficient. The reading is けってん, with a small っ before てん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

短所 (たんしょ) also names a weak point, often in a person's abilities or character. 長所 (ちょうしょ), "strong point," is a useful evaluative contrast. [特徴](特徴.md) (とくちょう) means a characteristic and need not be negative.

## Distinctive meaning

A 欠点 is not necessarily a mistake someone just made. A heavy device or a plan with a high cost may have a drawback even if nobody has acted incorrectly. Identify the specific limitation rather than treating the noun as a general accusation of blame.
