# 諺 (ことわざ) - proverb; saying

## Meaning

諺 is a noun for a proverb, saying, or maxim: an established expression that conveys
an observation, lesson, or piece of practical wisdom.
The kana spelling ことわざ is usual and useful to recognize.

- 日本のことわざを一つ覚えました。 (にほんのことわざをひとつおぼえました。) - I learned one Japanese proverb.
- このことわざはどんな意味ですか。 (このことわざはどんないみですか。) - What does this proverb mean?

Use Nのことわざ to identify a language or tradition, and ことわざの意味
(ことわざのいみ) for "the meaning of a proverb."
一つ counts one expression in the first example.

## How the kanji combine

諺 writes the complete native word ことわざ. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide.
The [言](../kanji/言.md) component can help you recognize its association with language,
but it does not assign separate readings to pieces of ことわざ.
Kana and kanji spellings identify the same word here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[言葉](言葉.md) (ことば), "word; expression; language," is much broader.
A phrase can be someone's memorable remark without being an established proverb.
There is no useful everyday antonym: a proverb is a kind of expression, not one end of an opposite pair.

## Distinctive meaning

A proverb's practical point may not be obvious from its literal wording.
Learning the situation in which it is used is therefore as important as learning its translation.
The noun 諺 names that category; it does not claim that every proverb is universally true
or appropriate advice in every situation.
