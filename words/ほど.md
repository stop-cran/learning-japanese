# ほど (ほど) — extent; approximately

## Meaning

ほど expresses extent or degree. Depending on its construction, it can mark an approximate amount, a degree described by a clause, or a comparison standard.

- 三十分ほど待ちました。  
  さんじゅっぷんほどまちました。  
  "I waited about thirty minutes."
- 歩けないほど疲れました。  
  あるけないほどつかれました。  
  "I was so tired that I couldn't walk."
- 今日は昨日ほど寒くありません。  
  きょうはきのうほどさむくありません。  
  "Today isn't as cold as yesterday."

In the first example, attach ほど directly to the counted quantity. In the second, a plain verb clause describes the degree. In the third, noun + ほど supplies the standard for a negative comparison.

## How the kanji combine

ほど is usually written in hiragana. 程 is its dictionary kanji spelling; the kana page and 程 read ほど represent the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

くらい overlaps with the approximate-quantity sense: 三十分くらい, "about thirty minutes". That overlap does not make every ほど construction interchangeable with くらい.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate ほど as an independent object called "extent" in every sentence. It often works as a particle attached to a preceding phrase. The surrounding grammar, especially a negative predicate in comparisons, is essential to the meaning.
