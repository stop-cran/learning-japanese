# ぷりぷり — plump and springy, or angry

## Meaning

ぷりぷり has distinct uses for plump, springy texture and for acting angrily or in a huff.

- えびはぷりぷりしていて、おいしい。
  — The shrimp are plump and springy, and delicious.
- 彼女は約束を忘れられて、ぷりぷり怒っていた。
  — She was fuming because someone forgot their promise.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The food use suggests a resilient, plump texture; the behavior use conveys visible irritation.

## Distinctive meaning

Context separates the two senses. Some spellings also occur in related dictionary entries, so this card retains the selected ぷりぷり identity.
