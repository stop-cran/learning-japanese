# 抜く (ぬく) — pull out

## Meaning

抜く means pulling something out or extracting it in the physical sense taught first here.
Mark the removed object with を. A place it comes out of can take から.

- コンセントからプラグを抜きました。 (コンセントからプラグをぬきました。) — I pulled the plug out of the socket.
- 庭の草を抜きました。 (にわのくさをぬきました。) — I pulled up the weeds in the garden.

The past form is 抜いた (ぬいた), and the negative is 抜かない (ぬかない).

Two other useful transitive senses are omission and overtaking:

- 忙しくて朝食を抜きました。 (いそがしくてちょうしょくをぬきました。) — I was busy and skipped breakfast.
- 最後の直線で前の選手を抜きました。 (さいごのちょくせんでまえのせんしゅをぬきました。) — I overtook the runner ahead of me on the final straight.

Here を marks the omitted meal or the person passed, not something physically extracted.

## How the kanji combine

[抜](../kanji/抜.md) supplies the extraction idea. The written く completes a native verb.
For this use, think of removal from an inserted, attached, or rooted position rather than movement in any direction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[引く](引く.md) (ひく) means "pull," but pulling does not necessarily remove the object.
抜く makes extraction central.
抜ける (ぬける) is the corresponding intransitive verb in this physical use:
プラグが抜けました (プラグがぬけました), "The plug came out."

## Distinctive meaning

プラグを抜いた (プラグをぬいた), "pulled the plug out," presents someone removing it.
プラグが抜けた (プラグがぬけた), "the plug came out," presents the result without naming a remover.
Do not translate every occurrence of English "pull" as 抜く: pulling a door need not extract it from anything.
