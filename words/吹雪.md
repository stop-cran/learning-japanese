# 吹雪 (ふぶき) - blizzard; snowstorm

## Meaning

吹雪 is a blizzard or snowstorm in which snow is blown fiercely by the wind. The snow can be falling or already on the ground and lifted into the air.

- 吹雪で前がよく見えません。 (ふぶきでまえがよくみえません。) - I cannot see ahead well because of the blizzard.
- 吹雪が収まるまで待ちました。 (ふぶきがおさまるまでまちました。) - We waited until the snowstorm subsided.

## How the kanji combine

[吹](../kanji/吹.md), blow, and [雪](../kanji/雪.md), snow, fit the whole word's meaning. Learn the established whole-word reading ふぶき. The jukujikun label marks that reading relationship here; it is not an instruction to give 雪 a productive reading ぶき or to pronounce the word as an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[嵐](嵐.md) (あらし) is a broader storm. [雪](雪.md) (ゆき) is snow itself; snow falling gently is not automatically a 吹雪.

## Distinctive meaning

Wind-driven snow is central, not just a large quantity of snowfall. The dictionary also records the rare spelling 乱吹 for ふぶき; this card teaches the source spelling 吹雪. The spelling association is not a claimed historical derivation.
