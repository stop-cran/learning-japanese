# 場 (ば) - place; setting; field

## Meaning

場, read **ば**, is a noun for a place, space, or setting. It can also describe an occasion or situation rather than just a physical location.

- その場で返事をしました。 (そのばでへんじをしました。) - I gave my reply on the spot.
- ここは意見を交換する場です。 (ここはいけんをこうかんするばです。) - This is a place for exchanging opinions.

The first example can emphasize the immediate situation. In the second, the clause before 場 explains the activity for which the setting exists.

## How the kanji combine

[場](../kanji/場.md) supplies the whole native noun ば. There is no multi-kanji compound here. The character also has the on reading じょう in other words; do not replace ば with it in その場.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[場所](場所.md) (ばしょ) is a close comparison for "place; location." 場 is especially useful when the occasion or activity matters, as in 話し合いの場 (はなしあいのば), "a setting for discussion." The words overlap, rather than contrasting as opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

In physics, 場 also means a **field**, as in 電場 (でんば), "electric field." That technical use does not mean a crop field such as [畑](畑.md) (はたけ). English "field" spans different ideas; the scientific context or the surrounding compound identifies which one is intended.
