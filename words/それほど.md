# それほど - to that extent

## Meaning

それほど means "that much" or "to that degree." It points to an amount or
degree supplied by the conversation. With a negative, it commonly means
"not that much" or "not so."

- この問題はそれほど難しくありません。 (このもんだいはそれほどむずかしくありません。) - This problem is not that difficult.
- それほど好きなら、もう一度見ましょう。 (それほどすきなら、もういちどみましょう。) - If you like it that much, let's watch it again.

## How the kanji combine

The usual kana spelling combines それ, "that," with ほど, which marks degree
or extent. The alternative それ程 writes the second part with kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そんなに also means "that much." あまり with a negative often means "not very,"
without as explicitly pointing to an already suggested degree.
These are useful comparisons, not absolute opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Unlike ちっとも, which is normally paired with a negative, それほど is not
inherently negative. The first example softens a degree; the second accepts
that degree as the premise for a suggestion.
