# プラスチック - plastic

## Meaning

プラスチック is plastic as a material or class of materials.
Use の before a noun, or 製 (せい), "made of," in プラスチック製.

- この容器はプラスチックでできています。 (このようきはプラスチックでできています。) - This container is made of plastic.
- プラスチックのごみを分けてください。 (プラスチックのごみをわけてください。) - Please separate the plastic waste.

プラスティック is another dictionary spelling of the same word.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword, not a kanji compound.
Keep the small ッ in プラスチック; the alternative ティ spelling is
recognition of the same material word, not a new chemical category.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ビニール](ビニール.md) refers to vinyl and also has broader everyday
plastic-material uses. The terms can overlap, but not every plastic is
specifically vinyl.
[ゴム](ゴム.md), rubber, is another material word, not a universal substitute.

## Distinctive meaning

Here English "plastic" is the material noun, not every figurative use
of the English adjective. The word alone does not guarantee that an item
is transparent, flexible, disposable, or recyclable.
Follow the actual material and local collection instructions.
