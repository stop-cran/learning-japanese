# めちゃくちゃ - absurd; messy; incredibly

## Meaning

めちゃくちゃ describes something unreasonable, incoherent, extreme, or
in a chaotic mess. Its noun/adjective-like uses take な before a noun and
に when describing a manner or resulting state.

- めちゃくちゃな話をしないでください。 (めちゃくちゃなはなしをしないでください。) - Please do not talk nonsense.
- 部屋の中はめちゃくちゃです。 (へやのなかはめちゃくちゃです。) - The room is a complete mess.

There is also an adverbial intensifier use: このゲームはめちゃくちゃ楽しい。
(このゲームはめちゃくちゃたのしい。) - This game is incredibly fun.
Here it directly modifies 楽しい, without に.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. 滅茶苦茶 and 目茶苦茶 are recognized ateji spellings:
do not derive the modern meanings from literal tea, eyes, or bitterness.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[でたらめ](でたらめ.md) overlaps in nonsense or haphazardness, and
[混乱](混乱.md) (こんらん) in disorder. [とても](とても.md) provides a
degree comparison. These are separate overlaps, not one set of interchangeable constructions.

## Distinctive meaning

The disorder and unreasonable-behavior uses are often negative, but the
intensifier can be favorable, as the game example shows. Do not translate
every occurrence as "terrible," or insert な into the direct adverbial use.
