# よこす - send or hand over toward me

## Meaning

よこす means to send or hand something over toward the speaker or
the speaker's side. The thing sent takes を. It is a godan verb:
よこします, よこした, よこさない.

- 友達が写真をよこしました。 (ともだちがしゃしんをよこしました。) - A friend sent me some photos.
- その本をよこして。 (そのほんをよこして。) - Hand that book over here.

The second sentence is a blunt request, not a courteous formula.
A polite ending alone does not make every use suitable for
addressing a superior.

After another verb's て-form, よこす marks an action directed at
the speaker, often with a one-sided or unwelcome nuance:
彼は文句を言ってよこした (かれはもんくをいってよこした),
"He directed his complaints at me."

## How the kanji combine

This word is usually written in kana. 寄越す is a recognized spelling:
[寄](../kanji/寄.md) and [越](../kanji/越.md) occur in it, but learn
the whole reading よこす rather than inventing an on-reading compound.
JMdict also records rare 遣す; it marks 寄こす as a search-only form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[送る](送る.md) (おくる) is broader sending.
[渡す](渡す.md) (わたす) is handing over without the same built-in
speakerward viewpoint. [くれる](くれる.md) concerns giving toward
the speaker's side and has an overlapping auxiliary construction.

## Distinctive meaning

よこす highlights the direction toward "here," not simply a transfer
between any two unrelated people. Its auxiliary often sounds less
welcoming than ～てくれる, but "よこす is always unwelcome" and
"くれる is always beneficial" would both be overstatements.
The speaker's attitude and the surrounding construction matter.
