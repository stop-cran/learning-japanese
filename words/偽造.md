# 偽造 (ぎぞう) — forgery; counterfeiting

## Meaning

偽造 means forgery or counterfeiting. JMdict also glosses it as fabrication or falsification. It can be used with する.

- パスポートを偽造した男が逮捕されました。 (パスポートをぎぞうしたおとこがたいほされました。) — A man who forged a passport was arrested.
- この一万円札は偽造されたものです。 (このいちまんえんさつはぎぞうされたものです。) — This 10,000-yen note is a counterfeit.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[偽](../kanji/偽.md) means false or fake, and [造](../kanji/造.md) means making. Together, they mean making a fake.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[模倣](模倣.md) (もほう), “imitation; copying” (JMdict entry 1533700), and [真似](真似.md) (まね) are broader words for imitating. [コピー](コピー.md) and [複写](複写.md) (ふくしゃ) mean copying, and [創作](創作.md) (そうさく) is original creation.

## Distinctive meaning

偽造 is the act of forging; 偽物 にせもの, “spurious article; counterfeit” (JMdict entry 1577800), is the counterfeit article itself.
