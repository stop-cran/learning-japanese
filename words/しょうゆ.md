# しょうゆ — soy sauce

## Meaning

しょうゆ means soy sauce, used as a seasoning or dipping sauce. It is a noun, so use the appropriate verb to say what you do with it.

- ご飯にしょうゆを少しかけました。 (ごはんにしょうゆをすこしかけました。) — I put a little soy sauce on the rice.
- しょうゆを取ってください。 (しょうゆをとってください。) — Please pass me the soy sauce.

In the first example, を marks the sauce and に marks the food receiving it.

## How the kanji combine

The usual kanji spelling is 醤油. Its on-readings combine as ショウ + ユ. The kana spelling on this page represents the same word; preserve the long しょう and short ゆ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

塩 (しお), "salt," and みそ, "miso," are other seasonings, not interchangeable names for soy sauce. ソース is the loanword "sauce" and does not automatically mean しょうゆ.

## Distinctive meaning

English "sauce" is broad, while しょうゆ names a particular kind of seasoning. When asking for it at a table or checking a recipe, use the specific Japanese name.
