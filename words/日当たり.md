# 日当たり (ひあたり) - sun exposure; sunny place

## Meaning

日当たり concerns sunlight reaching a place: its exposure to the sun,
how well it receives sunlight, or a sunny place.
日当たりがいい (ひあたりがいい) means that a place gets good sunlight.

- この部屋は日当たりがいいです。 (このへやはひあたりがいいです。) - This room gets plenty of sunlight.
- 日当たりのいい場所に鉢を置きました。 (ひあたりのいいばしょにはちをおきました。) - I put the plant pot in a sunny place.

In 日当たりのいい部屋, の connects the noun with the description
いい; the whole phrase describes a room with good sun exposure.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) supplies sunlight, and [当](../kanji/当.md),
through 当たる (あたる), concerns its striking or reaching a place.
The native elements are ひ + あたり. 日当り and 陽当り are
dictionary spellings of the sun-exposure word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[日光](日光.md) (にっこう) and [日差し](日差し.md) (ひざし)
name sunlight itself. 日当たり particularly suits describing a room's
or location's access to it. [日陰](日陰.md) (ひかげ), "shade,"
provides a relevant environmental contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Good 日当たり does not mean that every part of the room is sunny all day.
The direction, season, and surrounding buildings can matter.
JMdict separately records "per day" for 日当たり and 日当り;
that spelling-restricted sense must not be transferred automatically to
the family's 陽 spellings.
