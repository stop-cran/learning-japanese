# 摩擦 (まさつ) - friction; rubbing; discord

## Meaning

摩擦 means physical friction or rubbing and chafing. Figuratively it means friction or discord between people, groups, or countries.

- 摩擦で熱が発生します。 (まさつでねつがはっせいします。) - Friction generates heat.
- タオルで皮膚を摩擦した。 (タオルでひふをまさつした。) - I rubbed my skin with a towel.
- 二つの部署の間に摩擦が生じた。 (ふたつのぶしょのあいだにまさつがしょうじた。) - Friction arose between the two departments.

The rubbing sense also takes する. Learn what is rubbing against what: Aと摩擦する describes contact with A; an object can also be rubbed, with を.

## How the kanji combine

摩 and 擦 both contribute rubbing. This is a reinforcing pair of related meanings, read ま + さつ, not a historical-origin claim based on the modern shapes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[擦る](擦る.md) (こする), "rub; scrub; scrape," describes the action directly. [ごしごし](ごしごし.md) emphasizes vigorous scrubbing rather than every degree of friction.

[衝突](衝突.md) (しょうとつ) includes a physical collision and a figurative clash. [対立](対立.md) (たいりつ) emphasizes opposing positions. Their conflict uses can overlap social 摩擦 without making every disagreement a physical collision.

## Distinctive meaning

貿易摩擦 (ぼうえきまさつ) is trade friction. The figurative word does not imply literal scraping, and physical 摩擦 does not by itself imply that either surface is being polished clean.
