# 役割 (やくわり) - role; allotted duties

## Meaning

役割 is a role, part, or set of duties within an activity or system. It is a noun. Useful patterns are 役割を果たす (やくわりをはたす), "fulfill a role," and 役割を決める (やくわりをきめる), "decide the roles."

- 会議の前に、それぞれの役割を決めました。 (かいぎのまえに、それぞれのやくわりをきめました。) - Before the meeting, we decided each person's role.
- 心臓は血液を送る役割を果たします。 (しんぞうはけつえきをおくるやくわりをはたします。) - The heart fulfills the role of pumping blood.

The second example shows that a role can belong to a body part, not just a person.

## How the kanji combine

[役](../kanji/役.md) contributes a duty or part, and [割](../kanji/割.md) contributes dividing or allotting. Together they suggest an allotted part. The reading mixes on やく with native わり, an on-first mixed reading (jūbakoyomi). The repository files that pattern under `kango`.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[担当](担当.md) (たんとう) concerns responsibility for a particular area or task. [仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) means work or a job more broadly. A role describes the part someone or something plays within that work.

## Distinctive meaning

役割 need not be an official title or a paid occupation. It can describe temporary duties in a group or the function of a component. Saying someone has an important role does not by itself identify their rank or authority.
