# 窓口 (まどぐち) - service counter; point of contact

## Meaning

窓口 is a service window or counter, for example at a bank or station.
It also names the person, department, or channel serving as a point of contact.

- 駅の窓口で切符を買いました。 (えきのまどぐちできっぷをかいました。) - I bought a ticket at the station's ticket counter.
- この件の窓口は田中さんです。 (このけんのまどぐちはたなかさんです。) - Tanaka is the contact person for this matter.

The second example does not say that Tanaka is a physical window.

## How the kanji combine

[窓](../kanji/窓.md) supplies まど, window, and
[口](../kanji/口.md) an opening or interface.
くち becomes ぐち in this native compound: まど + ぐち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[受付](受付.md) (うけつけ), reception or acceptance, overlaps with
service-desk uses. [窓](窓.md) (まど) is a window more generally.
[連絡](連絡.md) (れんらく) concerns contact or communication; 窓口
can identify whom to contact rather than the act of making contact.

## Distinctive meaning

English "counter" needs context: a kitchen worktop or an item-counting
word is not automatically 窓口. Likewise, the point-of-contact sense
does not require a building with a literal service window.
