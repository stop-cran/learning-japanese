# 立ち止まる (たちどまる) - stop in one's tracks; pause

## Meaning

立ち止まる means to stop walking or moving forward and stand still.
It is an intransitive godan verb: 立ち止まります, 立ち止まった,
立ち止まって, 立ち止まらない.

- 名前を呼ばれて、立ち止まりました。
  (なまえをよばれて、たちどまりました。) - I stopped when someone called my name.
- 店の前で立ち止まって、看板を読みました。
  (みせのまえでたちどまって、かんばんをよみました。) - I stopped in front of the shop and read the sign.

で can identify the stopping place. The person who stops is the
subject, not an object being stopped.

## How the kanji combine

[立](../kanji/立.md) contributes standing, through 立ち (たち),
and [止](../kanji/止.md) contributes stopping. The latter part is
どまる in this compound, compared with standalone 止まる (とまる).
Learn the complete reading たちどまる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[止まる](止まる.md) is broader: a clock or vehicle can stop.
立ち止まる highlights coming to a standstill rather than every
kind of suspended operation. 歩き続ける (あるきつづける),
"keep walking," gives a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb can also frame a reflective pause: 一度立ち止まって考える
(いちどたちどまってかんがえる), "pause and think."
Use the primary reading たちどまる here; the dictionary also records
たちとまる as outdated. It is not a freely interchangeable modern
pronunciation to infer from the two component verbs.
