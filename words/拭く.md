# 拭く (ふく) - wipe; dry by wiping

## Meaning

拭く means wiping something, for example with a cloth, towel,
or tissue. Wiping can remove dirt or moisture and can dry a surface.
It is a transitive godan verb: 拭きます, 拭いて, 拭いた.

- ぬれた手をタオルで拭きました。 (ぬれたてをタオルでふきました。) - I dried my wet hands with a towel.
- 布で机を拭いてください。 (ぬのでつくえをふいてください。) - Please wipe the desk with a cloth.

The thing wiped takes を, and the tool can take で.

## How the kanji combine

拭 is the single written kanji, associated with wiping.
The native verb reading is ふく; く is part of its written
inflection. The ending behaves like other godan く verbs,
not like an ichidan verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

拭う (ぬぐう) also concerns wiping away.
[乾かす](乾かす.md) (かわかす) means drying something without
specifying wiping as the method.
[洗う](洗う.md) (あらう), washing, does not mean the same
action as wiping with a cloth.

## Distinctive meaning

Wiping need not make an object completely dry or perfectly clean.
The same reading occurs in [吹く](吹く.md), blow, and
[服](服.md), clothes, but those are different word identities.
Do not choose the kanji from the pronunciation alone.
