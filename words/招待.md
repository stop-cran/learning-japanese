# 招待 (しょうたい) — invitation

## Meaning

招待 means an invitation. 招待する means to invite someone as a guest. The person invited takes を; the event or destination takes に.

友達を家に招待しました。（ともだちをいえにしょうたいしました。） — I invited a friend to my home.

結婚式に招待されました。（けっこんしきにしょうたいされました。） — I was invited to a wedding.

## How the kanji combine

[招](../kanji/招.md) suggests inviting or beckoning, and [待](../kanji/待.md) contributes waiting or receiving. Both use on readings: しょう + たい. Learn the compound as inviting and receiving guests.

## Synonyms and antonyms

誘う（さそう） is broader: it can mean asking someone to join an activity. 招待 emphasizes treating them as a guest.

## Distinctive meaning

招待する is not simply telling someone to come. It presents an invitation to a hosted occasion or place. 招待状（しょうたいじょう） is a written invitation; 招待された uses the passive to describe receiving one.
