# 科目 (かもく) - school subject; course

## Meaning

科目 is a subject or course treated as one item in a curriculum or examination. It is a noun.

- 得意な科目は数学です。 (とくいなかもくはすうがくです。) - The subject I am good at is mathematics.
- 来学期は三つの科目を選びます。 (らいがっきはみっつのかもくをえらびます。) - I will choose three subjects next term.

The first example identifies a field of study; the second counts selected subjects. 試験科目 (しけんかもく) means an examination subject.

## How the kanji combine

[科](../kanji/科.md) contributes a division or branch of study, and [目](../kanji/目.md) contributes an item or heading. Together they present a subject as a categorized item. The on readings are カ + モク; 目 is not a literal eye in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

教科 (きょうか) also names a school subject or curricular field. [授業](授業.md) (じゅぎょう) concerns teaching or a class session: one subject can have many lessons. These terms overlap in educational settings but do not identify the same unit in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

科目 is useful when choosing, listing, counting, or being tested on subjects. It can also mean an item or heading outside education, as in accounting classifications. Do not assume the whole curriculum is one 科目 simply because English sometimes uses "course" broadly.
