# 望む (のぞむ) — hope for; desire

## Meaning

望む means to want, wish for or hope for something. It is a transitive verb: the desired outcome
can take を. To make a whole clause the object, use **plain clause + ことを望む**.
For example, 彼が来ること (かれがくること) means "his coming" in the second sentence.

- 私たちは平和を望んでいます。 (わたしたちはへいわをのぞんでいます。) — We hope for peace.
- 彼が来ることを望んでいます。 (かれがくることをのぞんでいます。) — I hope that he will come.

## How the kanji combine

[望](../kanji/望.md) carries the hope or desire meaning, and む completes the native verb.
It is a godan verb: のぞみます is the polite form, while the て-form is のぞんで,
as in 望んでいます (のぞんでいます).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[願う](願う.md) (ねがう) overlaps in "wish; hope" and also has request or prayer uses.
[欲しい](欲しい.md) (ほしい) expresses wanting with different grammar:
本が欲しい (ほんがほしい), "I want a book," uses が with the desired thing.
Do not transfer that particle pattern mechanically to the verb 望む.

## Distinctive meaning

望む presents an outcome as desired, not guaranteed or already arranged.
In the example about someone coming, こと turns the clause into something that can be hoped for;
it is not an extra tense marker.
This article teaches the desire sense, rather than merging all of the verb's dictionary senses.
