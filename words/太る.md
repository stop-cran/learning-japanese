# 太る (ふとる) - gain weight

## Meaning

太る is an intransitive godan verb meaning to gain weight or become heavier in build. The person or animal changing is the subject.

- 最近、少し太りました。 (さいきん、すこしふとりました。) - I have gained a little weight recently.
- 太ったので、このズボンは少しきついです。 (ふとったので、このズボンはすこしきついです。) - Since I gained weight, these trousers are a little tight.

Forms include 太った and 太らない (ふとった、ふとらない).

## How the kanji combine

[太](../kanji/太.md) writes ふと, and る completes the native verb. The related adjective 太い (ふとい) means "thick" or "broad," but it is a different grammatical word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

やせる means "lose weight" or "become thin." 体重が増える (たいじゅうがふえる) explicitly says that body weight increases.

## Distinctive meaning

太っている (ふとっている) often describes a person's present build rather than an ongoing process of gaining weight at that instant. Comments about another person's body can be intrusive; the word's grammar does not make such remarks socially neutral.
