# すっかり

## Meaning

すっかり means completely, entirely, or thoroughly. It often describes a completed change or a state that has fully developed.

- 約束をすっかり忘れていました。 (やくそくを すっかり わすれていました。) — I had completely forgotten our appointment.
- 外はすっかり暗くなりました。 (そとは すっかり くらくなりました。) — It has become completely dark outside.

It is an adverb and directly modifies the predicate. It can also occur as すっかりと.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in kana. Preserve the small っ: it creates a pause before か. There is no kanji combination to explain in this spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

全部 (ぜんぶ), "all," often totals things or parts. すっかり emphasizes completeness of a state or change. 少し (すこし), "a little," can contrast in degree.

## Distinctive meaning

すっかり忘れる means forgetting completely, not simply forgetting frequently. It is especially useful with changes such as becoming dark, getting well, or changing one's appearance; the surrounding verb supplies what has become complete.
