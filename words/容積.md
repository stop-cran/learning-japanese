# 容積 (ようせき) - capacity; volume

## Meaning

容積 is capacity or three-dimensional volume.
It often concerns how much a container can hold, but dictionary
usage also includes the volume of a solid.

- この容器の容積を調べました。 (このようきのようせきをしらべました。) - I checked this container's capacity.
- 大きな箱に替えて、容積を増やしました。 (おおきなはこにかえて、ようせきをふやしました。) - We switched to a bigger box to increase the capacity.

## How the kanji combine

[容](../kanji/容.md) contributes containing or accommodating;
[積](../kanji/積.md) accumulation or a measure of extent.
The on readings are ヨウ + セキ.
This association helps remember capacity, but does not limit
the whole word to empty space inside a vessel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[体積](体積.md) (たいせき) is three-dimensional volume and genuinely
overlaps with 容積.
[量](量.md) (りょう) and [分量](分量.md) (ぶんりょう) are broader
quantity/amount words.
[面積](面積.md) (めんせき) is two-dimensional area, not volume.

## Distinctive meaning

An interior-capacity versus object-volume distinction is useful
in some teaching and technical contexts, not an absolute dictionary
wall between 容積 and 体積.
[定員](定員.md) (ていいん) also translates as "capacity," but concerns
a prescribed headcount; it is not a measurement of cubic space.
[能力](能力.md) (のうりょく), ability, is another meaning of English
"capacity," not the sense taught here.
