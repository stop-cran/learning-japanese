# 初め (はじめ) — beginning; outset

## Meaning

初め means **the beginning or outset**. Use noun + の + 初め to identify the beginning of a period.

- 四月の初めに引っ越します。 (しがつのはじめにひっこします。) — I will move at the beginning of April.
- 初めは難しかったです。 (はじめはむずかしかったです。) — It was difficult at first.

初めは often contrasts the initial situation with what happened later.

## How the kanji combine

[初](../kanji/初.md) writes はじ, followed by め. 始め is another spelling of はじめ. 初め is especially useful for an initial time or stage; 始め often highlights the start of an activity, but the meanings overlap.

## Synonyms and antonyms

最初 (さいしょ) means “first/the very beginning.” 終わり (おわり), “end,” provides the practical opposite. 初めて (はじめて) means “for the first time,” not simply “at the beginning.”

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 初め with the dictionary-form verb 始める (はじめる), “begin something.” 初めは日本語が分かりませんでした means “At first I did not understand Japanese”; it describes an initial stage, not necessarily someone's first-ever encounter with the language.
