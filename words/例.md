# 例 (れい) — example

## Meaning

例 is a noun meaning an example, instance, or illustration.
It names a particular case that helps explain a point.
Use 例を with a verb that acts on the example, or 例として (れいとして), "as an example."

- 例を一つ書いてください。 (れいをひとつかいてください。) — Please write one example.
- 東京を例として説明します。 (とうきょうをれいとしてせつめいします。) — I will explain using Tokyo as an example.

## How the kanji combine

This word consists of [例](../kanji/例.md) alone, using its on reading レイ.
There are no separate written components to combine into a compound meaning.
In 例文 (れいぶん), "example sentence," the same 例 specifies what kind of sentence is meant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

見本 (みほん) can be a sample or model, such as something shown to demonstrate a product or pattern.
例 can instead be a case chosen for an explanation, as Tokyo is above.
The words overlap in "example," but an illustrative case need not be an object presented as a sample.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the noun 例 from the expression 例えば (たとえば), "for example."
例を一つ, "one example," counts a noun; 例えば introduces an illustration in a sentence.
The shared kanji does not mean the two forms have the same grammatical job or reading.
The focus here is the example sense, not every use involving a precedent or customary practice.
