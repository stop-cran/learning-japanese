# 採点 (さいてん) - marking; grading; scoring

## Meaning

採点 is marking, grading, or assigning points. 採点する is transitive: the test answers, performance, or other material being scored takes を.

- 先生が答案を採点しています。 (せんせいがとうあんをさいてんしています。) - The teacher is marking the answer papers.
- 審査員が演技を採点しました。 (しんさいんがえんぎをさいてんしました。) - The judges scored the performance.

採点基準 (さいてんきじゅん) means scoring criteria. Digital Daijisen also records the assigned score itself, alongside the act of marking.

## How the kanji combine

[採](../kanji/採.md) contributes taking or selecting, and [点](../kanji/点.md) points or marks. Learn the established combination as assigning or assessing points, not as physically gathering dots. The on readings are サイ + テン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[評価](評価.md) (ひょうか) is assessment or appraisal more broadly. It need not produce a numerical score. [成績](成績.md) (せいせき) concerns grades or a performance record, rather than only the marking activity that produces them.

## Distinctive meaning

採点 is not merely looking over a page. It concerns evaluating answers or a performance for marks or a score. The word does not say whether the result is good, whether the marking is strict, or whether every criterion is numerical.
