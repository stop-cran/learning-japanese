# 乗り換える (のりかえる) - transfer between vehicles

## Meaning

乗り換える means to change trains, buses, or other vehicles during a journey. Mark the transfer location with で and the new vehicle or line with に.

次の駅で急行に乗り換えます。 (つぎのえきできゅうこうにのりかえます。) - I will change to an express train at the next station.

ここで電車からバスに乗り換えてください。 (ここででんしゃからバスにのりかえてください。) - Please transfer from the train to a bus here.

電車を乗り換える (でんしゃをのりかえる), "change trains," is also possible.

## How the kanji combine

乗り, from [乗](../kanji/乗.md)る (のる), "ride," combines with 換える (かえる), "exchange." The compound conjugates as an ichidan verb: 乗り換えます, 乗り換えて, 乗り換えない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

乗る means boarding or riding; 降りる (おりる) means getting off. A transfer includes changing what you ride, not just either action alone.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun 乗り換え (のりかえ) appears on travel information and route planners. 乗り換える can also mean switching to a different system or service, but in station directions its transport meaning is the relevant one. The destination particle に names the new service, not the transfer station.
