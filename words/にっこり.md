# にっこり (にっこり) - with a warm or broad smile

## Meaning

にっこり describes smiling sweetly or broadly. It is a mimetic expression: it evokes a visible manner rather than imitating an audible sound. Use **にっこり笑う**, **にっこりと笑う**, or **にっこりする**.

- 子供はプレゼントを見て、にっこり笑いました。 (こどもはプレゼントをみて、にっこりわらいました。) - The child smiled broadly on seeing the present.
- 彼女は私ににっこりしました。 (かのじょはわたしににっこりしました。) - She gave me a warm smile.

In the second example, に marks the person toward whom the smile is directed. The optional と in にっこりと introduces the manner.

## How the kanji combine

にっこり is written in kana; ニッコリ is another recorded spelling. Keep the small っ in this form. There is no kanji decomposition and no need to invent a foreign origin for the expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[笑う](笑う.md) (わらう) can describe smiling or laughing more generally. [微笑む](微笑む.md) (ほほえむ) means "smile." にっこり adds a picture of the smile's warm or broad appearance rather than simply naming the action.

## Distinctive meaning

A smile need not be accompanied by laughter. にっこり笑う therefore need not mean laughing aloud. The expression describes what the face does; context still determines why someone smiles and whether the smile reflects their full feelings.
