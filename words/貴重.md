# 貴重 (きちょう) - precious; valuable

## Meaning

貴重 is a な-adjective for something precious or valuable. The value can concern an object, information, time, or an experience; it need not be a market price. Use 貴重な before a noun and 貴重です as a predicate.

- 貴重な資料を見せていただきました。 (きちょうなしりょうをみせていただきました。) - I was shown valuable materials.
- 今日は貴重な経験ができました。 (きょうはきちょうなけいけんができました。) - I gained a valuable experience today.

貴重な時間 (きちょうなじかん), "precious time," presents time as something worth using carefully.

## How the kanji combine

貴 contributes preciousness or high value. [重](../kanji/重.md) contributes weight or importance, figuratively rather than physical heaviness here. Together they reinforce the assessment that something deserves to be valued.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[重要](重要.md) (じゅうよう) emphasizes importance or significance. [大切](大切.md) (たいせつ) can emphasize that something matters and should be cared for. [珍しい](珍しい.md) (めずらしい) means unusual or rare; rarity may make something valuable, but does not guarantee it.

## Distinctive meaning

貴重品 (きちょうひん) means "valuables," but the adjective is not restricted to possessions. 貴重なご意見 (きちょうなごいけん), "your valuable opinion," acknowledges useful input. Do not replace 貴重 with "expensive" automatically: an inexpensive photograph or a short conversation can be 貴重 to someone.
