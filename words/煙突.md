# 煙突 (えんとつ) - chimney; smokestack

## Meaning

煙突 is a chimney or smokestack, including a stove's smoke pipe
or a ship's funnel. It names the structure through which smoke or
exhaust passes, not the smoke itself.

- 工場の煙突から煙が出ています。
  (こうじょうのえんとつからけむりがでています。) - Smoke is coming from the factory's chimney.
- 屋根の上に煙突が見えます。
  (やねのうえにえんとつがみえます。) - I can see a chimney above the roof.

から marks where the smoke comes from. 工場の煙突 identifies
the factory's chimney without requiring a particular height or shape.

## How the kanji combine

[煙](../kanji/煙.md) contributes smoke, and
[突](../kanji/突.md) thrusting or projecting out. Remember a
projecting smoke outlet: エン + トツ. This component-based
memory aid is not a historical explanation of the compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[煙](煙.md) (けむり) is smoke.
[管](管.md) (くだ) is a pipe or tube more generally.
A smoke pipe can be a 煙突, but not every tube has that function.
There is no useful everyday opposite of the structure itself.

## Distinctive meaning

The same noun can cover a house chimney and an industrial smokestack;
it does not mean only a large factory tower. The inspected dictionary
also has a separate taxi-meter slang sense, which is not the building
meaning taught here. 烟突 is a dictionary-recognized rare spelling,
not the spelling used in this card.
