# ささやく - whisper; murmur

## Meaning

ささやく means speaking very quietly: whisper or murmur.
It is a godan verb classified as intransitive in the cited dictionary.
Forms include ささやきます, ささやいた, ささやいて, and ささやかない.

- 赤ちゃんを起こさないように、二人でささやいていました。 (あかちゃんをおこさないように、ふたりでささやいていました。) - The two of us were whispering so as not to wake the baby.
- 彼女は「大丈夫」とささやきました。 (かのじょは「だいじょうぶ」とささやきました。) - She whispered, "It's all right."

と marks the quoted words in the second example; it is not a direct object marked with を.

## How the kanji combine

囁く is the written source form, also read ささやく. Kana is usual.
囁 writes ささや and く is the changing ending; it is not a series of separate kana meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ひそひそ](ひそひそ.md) describes speaking in low voices: ひそひそ話す (ひそひそはなす).
[話す](話す.md) (はなす), "speak; talk," does not itself specify the volume.
[噂](噂.md) (うわさ) names rumor or gossip and also has a talking-about use.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional rumor use commonly appears in the passive:
あの店が閉まるとささやかれています (あのみせがしまるとささやかれています),
"It is rumored that that shop will close."
That reports circulating talk, not independent proof.
Ordinary whispering need not be gossip or an attempt to deceive.
