# 驚かす (おどろかす) - surprise; frighten

## Meaning

驚かす means to surprise or frighten someone, or cause a stir. It is a transitive godan verb: the person surprised takes を.

- 大きな音で子供を驚かしてしまいました。 (おおきなおとでこどもをおどろかしてしまいました。) - I accidentally frightened the child with a loud noise.
- その発表は多くの人を驚かしました。 (そのはっぴょうはおおくのひとをおどろかしました。) - That announcement surprised many people.

Forms include 驚かします (おどろかします), 驚かした (おどろかした), and 驚かさない (おどろかさない).

## How the kanji combine

驚 supplies おどろ in this native verb; かす is written in kana. There is only one kanji, not a compound to read with on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[おどかす](おどかす.md) overlaps in frightening or startling, but also has an explicit threatening sense. [驚く](驚く.md) (おどろく) describes experiencing surprise: 子供が驚いた (こどもがおどろいた), "the child was surprised."

## Distinctive meaning

The surprise need not be frightening or unwelcome: an unexpected announcement can also 驚かす people. Keep the viewpoints distinct: 人を驚かす causes surprise in someone; 人が驚く describes that person's reaction. 驚かす is not the passive form of 驚く.
