# 都市 (とし) - city; urban center

## Meaning

都市 is a noun for a city or urban center. It is useful when discussing settlements, population, development, and urban life.

- この都市には大きな駅が三つあります。 (このとしにはおおきなえきがみっつあります。) - This city has three large stations.
- 多くの人が地方から都市へ移りました。 (おおくのひとがちほうからとしへうつりました。) - Many people moved from regional areas to cities.

Before another noun, use の or a compound: 都市の生活 (としのせいかつ), "city life," and 都市計画 (としけいかく), "urban planning." The English adjective "urban" does not make 都市 a な-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[都](../kanji/都.md) contributes a metropolis or capital, and [市](../kanji/市.md) contributes a city or market center. Together they name an urban settlement. The reading is ト + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[町](町.md) (まち) can mean a town or an inhabited neighborhood and fits many everyday references to a place. [都会](都会.md) (とかい) emphasizes an urban setting. [田舎](田舎.md) (いなか), "countryside," provides a contextual contrast rather than a legal classification.

## Distinctive meaning

都市 is not restricted to a national capital: 都 in the spelling does not impose that condition. Nor does the word itself settle an area's administrative status. A sentence about 都市の問題 (としのもんだい), "urban problems," is discussing city life or organization, not necessarily a municipality's formal legal category.
