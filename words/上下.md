# 上下 (じょうげ) — up and down, top and bottom

## Meaning

上下 combines [上](../kanji/上.md) (up, above) and [下](../kanji/下.md) (down, below). It means the vertical axis as a whole.

- 上下に動く（うごく） — move up and down
- 株価（かぶか）が上下する — the share price fluctuates
- 上下関係（じょうげかんけい） — a hierarchy (senior and junior)
- 上下セット — top-and-bottom set (such as a suit or pyjamas)

## How the kanji combine

Opposites in a pair produce the dimension they define (like [大小](大小.md) for size): the vertical, the range between high and low, or rank in a
hierarchy. As a verb, 上下する means "go up and down" literally (an elevator) and "rise and fall" for prices and values.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 上がり下がり あがりさがり (native, informal), 増減 ぞうげん (rise and fall of amounts).
- For stairs and slopes: 上り下り のぼりおり／のぼりくだり.
- Contrast: 左右 さゆう (left and right, horizontal), 前後 ぜんご (before and after, front and back).

## Distinctive meaning

Reading matters. 上下 usually reads じょうげ; the reading うえした also exists in some phrases ("up and down", "upside down"), but learn じょうげ first.
The hierarchy sense 上下関係 is a central notion in Japanese social language.
