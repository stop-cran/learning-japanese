# 書類 (しょるい) - documents; papers

## Meaning

書類 refers to documents or papers, such as forms, applications, and records. It is a noun; the surrounding phrase supplies the action. 書類を出す means "submit the documents," while 書類に名前を書く means "write one's name on the document."

- 必要な書類を明日までに出してください。 (ひつようなしょるいをあしたまでにだしてください。) - Please submit the necessary documents by tomorrow.
- この書類に住所を書いてください。 (このしょるいにじゅうしょをかいてください。) - Please write your address on this document.

を marks what is submitted; に marks the surface or document where information is entered.

## How the kanji combine

[書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing or written material, and [類](../kanji/類.md) contributes kind or category. The compound groups written items as documents. The on readings are ショ + ルイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[紙](紙.md) (かみ) is paper as a material or sheet; a blank sheet is not automatically a 書類. [資料](資料.md) (しりょう) concerns reference material or information, which may include documents.

## Distinctive meaning

書類 need not mean a government document specifically. It suits business and personal paperwork too. Japanese does not require a plural ending here: context can make 書類 one document or a set. For counting individual sheets, 一枚 (いちまい) refers to the sheet, not necessarily a whole multi-page document.
