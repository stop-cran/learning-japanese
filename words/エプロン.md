# エプロン (エプロン) - apron

## Meaning

エプロン is an apron worn over clothing, for example while cooking,
cleaning or working. It is a noun. エプロンをつける means "put on
an apron," and エプロンを外す (エプロンをはずす) means "take off an apron."

- 料理をする前にエプロンをつけました。 (りょうりをするまえにエプロンをつけました。) - I put on an apron before cooking.
- このエプロンには大きなポケットがあります。 (このエプロンにはおおきなポケットがあります。) - This apron has a large pocket.

## Synonyms and antonyms

前掛け (まえかけ) names a waist apron. It overlaps with some エプロン
designs, but エプロン is not restricted to waist-down styles.
[服](服.md) (ふく) is the broader word "clothes"; an apron is a
particular item worn over them, not a synonym for all clothing.

## Distinctive meaning

An エプロン need not identify a particular gender or occupation.
The word alone also does not guarantee protection from chemicals,
heat or every other hazard; the product and task determine what protection
it provides. The clothing use is the focus of this article.
