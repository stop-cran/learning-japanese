# 診る (みる) -- examine medically

## Meaning

診る means medically examining someone, including checking a patient's
condition or pulse. The patient or body part examined takes を.

- 医師が患者を診ています。 (いしがかんじゃをみています。) -- A doctor is examining a patient.
- 脈を診てください。 (みゃくをみてください。) -- Please check my pulse.

It is a transitive ichidan verb: 診ます (みます), 診ない (みない),
診て (みて) and 診た (みた). This is not the godan conjugation
pattern of a verb such as 実る.

## How the kanji combine

診 writes み and る completes the native verb. Its medical association
distinguishes this spelling from ordinary 見る (みる); there is no
multi-kanji compound here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [見る](見る.md) (みる) has much broader seeing, watching and checking uses. Not every 見る can be rewritten 診る.
- [診察](診察.md) (しんさつ) is a medical examination or consultation.
- [診断](診断.md) (しんだん) concerns diagnostic assessment; examining a patient and reaching a diagnosis are related, not identical steps.
- [検査](検査.md) (けんさ) includes inspections or tests, including medical ones; [調べる](調べる.md) (しらべる) is broader investigating or checking.

## Distinctive meaning

診る marks the medical use, not simply looking at a person. A doctor
watching television still テレビを見る, not テレビを診る. English
"examine" also appears in academic or investigative contexts, so the
English label alone does not make every examination word a medical verb.
