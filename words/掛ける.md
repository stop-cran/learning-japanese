# 掛ける (かける) — hang something up

## Meaning

This article teaches 掛ける in the sense "hang something up." The hung object takes を;
the surface or support can take に.
JMdict marks this sense as **usually written in kana**, so the examples use かける.

- 絵を壁にかけました。 (えをかべにかけました。) — I hung a picture on the wall.
- コートをハンガーにかけてください。 (コートをハンガーにかけてください。) — Please hang the coat on a hanger.

The past is かけた, and the negative is かけない.

## How the kanji combine

[掛](../kanji/掛.md) represents the hanging sense in this spelling.
The ending ける completes a native verb; this is not a two-character compound.
Recognizing the kanji is useful even though the kana spelling is normal for this sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

掛かる (かかる), also usually written in kana, is the intransitive partner for hanging:
絵が壁にかかっています (えがかべにかかっています), "A picture is hanging on the wall."
It describes the picture's situation rather than someone's action of putting it there.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep **something を かける** separate from **something が かかる**.
The two differ in who or what is the subject, not merely in politeness.
かける has many other senses and spellings; this article is an entry point for hanging, not a rule that every かける means "hang."
The phrase 壁にかける (かべにかける) is independently recorded in JMdict.
