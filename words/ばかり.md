# ばかり — only; just finished; about

## Meaning

ばかり has several construction-dependent uses: concentration on something, a recently completed
action, or an approximate quantity.

- 甘いものばかり食べています。 (あまいものばかり たべています。) — I keep eating nothing but sweet things.
- ご飯を食べたばかりです。 (ごはんを たべたばかりです。) — I have just eaten.
- 一時間ばかり待ちました。 (いちじかんばかり まちました。) — I waited about an hour.

Compare **noun + ばかり**, **verb た-form + ばかり**, and **quantity + ばかり**.
For repeated activity, **verb て-form + ばかりいる** is useful:
遊んでばかりいる (あそんでばかりいる), "do nothing but play."

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is usual. 許り and 許 are dictionary spellings marked rare by JMdict. They do not
turn the particle into the verb 許す (ゆるす), "permit."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[だけ](だけ.md) overlaps with limiting a range. [くらい](くらい.md) and [ほど](ほど.md) overlap
with approximate quantities. None replaces ばかり in all the constructions above.
There is no one antonym spanning these different uses.

## Distinctive meaning

食べたばかり describes recent completion from the speaker's perspective, not continual eating.
Conversely, 食べてばかりいる emphasizes repeated or concentrated activity. Keep the verb form
with the meaning instead of memorizing "just" as a universal translation.

For ばかり compared with だけ, くらい, and ほど, see
[the particle-neighbor section](../articles/koto-wake-and-formal-nouns.md#particle-neighbors-ほど-くらいぐらい-だけ-and-ばかり).
