# 決して (けっして) — never; by no means

## Meaning

決して strongly reinforces a negative expression. Depending on the predicate, it means "never," "by no means," or "not at all."

- あなたの親切は決して忘れません。 (あなたのしんせつはけっしてわすれません。) — I will never forget your kindness.
- この問題は決して簡単ではありません。 (このもんだいはけっしてかんたんではありません。) — This problem is by no means easy.

## How the kanji combine

[決](../kanji/決.md) supplies the firm or decisive association. The expression is related in form to 決する (けっする), but learn 決して as the established adverb けっして, including the small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

絶対に (ぜったいに), "absolutely," can also strengthen a negative statement, but it can occur with affirmative statements too. あまり + negative means "not very," a weaker claim.

## Distinctive meaning

The negative ending does essential work: 決して alone does not turn an affirmative predicate into "never." It is also not limited to frequency over time; 決して簡単ではない denies that something is easy, rather than saying it is easy zero times.
