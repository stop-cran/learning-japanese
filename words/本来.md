# 本来 (ほんらい) - originally; by nature; properly

## Meaning

本来 refers to something's original or inherent nature, or to how something properly ought to be. It is not limited to an event that happened earlier in time.

- これは本来の目的ではありません。 (これはほんらいのもくてきではありません。) - This is not the original purpose.
- 本来なら、今日は休みです。 (ほんらいなら、きょうはやすみです。) - Normally, today would be a day off.
- この植物は本来、暖かい地方に生えます。 (このしょくぶつはほんらい、あたたかいちほうにはえます。) - This plant naturally grows in warm regions.

Use 本来の before a noun, or 本来 as an adverb. 本来なら introduces what would normally or properly be the case; the actual situation may differ. 本来の所有者 (ほんらいのしょゆうしゃ) is the rightful owner.

## How the kanji combine

[本](../kanji/本.md) suggests the original basis; [来](../kanji/来.md) contributes the idea of what has come down from that basis. Treat this as a learning association for ほん + らい, not a proof of the word's historical formation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

元来 (がんらい), "originally; by nature," overlaps, but it does not automatically replace 本来 in statements about what rightfully ought to happen.

[普通](普通.md) (ふつう), "usual; ordinary," can overlap a normal-state translation. 本来の目的 focuses on the intended or original purpose, not merely an ordinary purpose.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 本来の姿 (ほんらいのすがた), the original or true-to-nature form, with 本来ならできるはずだ, "one ought normally to be able to do it." The second expresses an expectation, not simply "originally" as a past-time adverb.
