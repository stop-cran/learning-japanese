# 訳 (わけ) - reason; sense

## Meaning

訳 means a reason, an explanation behind something, or its meaning or sense. The kana spelling わけ is also common.

- 遅れた訳を教えてください。 (おくれたわけをおしえてください。) - Please tell me why you were late.
- この説明はわけが分かりません。 (このせつめいはわけがわかりません。) - This explanation makes no sense to me.

In the first example, the plain past verb 遅れた directly modifies 訳: "the reason for being late." In the second, わけが分からない is the useful expression "not understand what is going on" or "make no sense."

## How the kanji combine

訳 is a single character read わけ here. The same character read やく can mean a translation, but that is a different word and not the reading taught on this page.

## Synonyms and antonyms

理由 (りゆう) also means reason. 意味 (いみ) is a more general word for meaning, especially the definition of a word.

## Distinctive meaning

訳 often concerns how a situation makes sense or why it happened. It is not a replacement for 意味 in every vocabulary question. 訳がある (わけがある), "there is a reason," can suggest circumstances not yet explained rather than an arbitrary dictionary definition.
