# 訳 (わけ) - reason; sense

## Meaning

訳 means a reason, an explanation behind something, or its meaning or sense. The kana spelling わけ is also common.

- 遅れた訳を教えてください。 (おくれたわけをおしえてください。) - Please tell me why you were late.
- この説明はわけが分かりません。 (このせつめいはわけがわかりません。) - This explanation makes no sense to me.

In the first example, the plain past verb 遅れた directly modifies 訳: "the reason for being late." In the second, わけが分からない is the useful expression "not understand what is going on" or "make no sense."

In **わけだ**, a clause is presented as an understandable conclusion:
窓が開いている。寒いわけだ。 (まどが あいている。さむいわけだ。) — The window is open. No wonder it is cold.
Use plain verb or i-adjective forms before わけ. In the affirmative nonpast, na-adjectives and noun
predicates take な, as in 静かなわけだ (しずかなわけだ) and 学生なわけだ (がくせいなわけだ).
Past and negative forms keep their endings: 学生だったわけだ, not 学生だったなわけだ.

**A separate reading, やく:** 訳 is also a noun or suffix meaning "translation; version." This does not change the primary わけ reading above.

- この訳は分かりやすいです。 (このやくはわかりやすいです。) - This translation is easy to understand.

日本語訳 (にほんごやく) means "Japanese translation," and 英語訳 (えいごやく) means "English translation." The language before -訳 identifies the language of the translated version.

## How the kanji combine

[訳](../kanji/訳.md) is a single character. The native reading わけ names reasons or explanatory sense; the on reading ヤク names a translation or version. These are distinct dictionary entries sharing a written character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[理由](理由.md) (りゆう) also means reason. [意味](意味.md) (いみ) is a more general word for meaning, especially the definition of a word.
[翻訳](翻訳.md) (ほんやく) compares with the やく identity: 翻訳する is "translate," while この訳 names a translated version.
[こと](こと.md) presents an action or fact as something to discuss; わけ often concerns its explanation.

## Distinctive meaning

訳 often concerns how a situation makes sense or why it happened. It is not a replacement for 意味 in every vocabulary question. 訳がある (わけがある), "there is a reason," can suggest circumstances not yet explained rather than an arbitrary dictionary definition.
Those reason and explanation constructions use わけ, not やく. The article retains its N4 label and primary わけ metadata; that article-level label does not assign a level separately to every reading.

Keep the negative constructions separate: わけがない rejects a possibility; わけではない rejects an
interpretation; わけにはいかない concerns circumstances or obligations that prevent an action.
