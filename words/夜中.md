# 夜中 (よなか) - middle of the night

## Meaning

夜中 is a time noun, also used adverbially, meaning the middle or dead of night. It identifies a nighttime period rather than an exact minute.

- 夜中に目が覚めました。 (よなかにめがさめました。) - I woke up in the middle of the night.
- 昨日は夜中まで本を読んでいました。 (きのうはよなかまでほんをよんでいました。) - Yesterday I was reading until late into the night.

夜中に places an event within that period. 夜中まで gives the limit of an activity, and 夜中の電話 (よなかのでんわ) means a phone call in the middle of the night.

## How the kanji combine

[夜](../kanji/夜.md) is read よ here, and [中](../kanji/中.md) is read なか. Their native readings combine transparently as "night's middle." This image is a broad part of the night, not a formula calculating its precise midpoint.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[夜](夜.md) (よる) covers night more broadly. 真夜中 (まよなか) emphasizes the middle or depths of the night. [昼間](昼間.md) (ひるま), "daytime," gives a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's English "midnight" can mislead if taken as precisely 00:00. 夜中の二時 (よなかのにじ), "two in the morning," is perfectly compatible with this word. If an exact time matters, state the time. Nighttime expectations also vary with situation, so avoid assigning the noun a universal fixed range of hours.
