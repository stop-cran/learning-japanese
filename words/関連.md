# 関連 (かんれん) - connection; relevance

## Meaning

関連 is a noun for a connection or relevance between things. 関連する means "be related": use AはBに関連する or AとBは関連している. The thing being connected is the subject, not an object marked with を.

- この二つの問題は関連しています。 (このふたつのもんだいはかんれんしています。) - These two problems are related.
- その事件に関連する資料を集めています。 (そのじけんにかんれんするしりょうをあつめています。) - I am collecting material related to that incident.

関連がある (かんれんがある), "there is a connection," and 関連がない, "there is no connection," are useful noun constructions.

## How the kanji combine

[関](../kanji/関.md) contributes involvement or connection, and [連](../kanji/連.md) contributes linking. Their related meanings combine in a word about things being connected. The reading is かんれん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[関係](関係.md) (かんけい) also means a relationship or connection and has broad uses, including personal relationships. 関連 is useful for connected information, matters, or events. The words overlap; they are not separated by an absolute people-versus-things rule.

## Distinctive meaning

関連 establishes a connection, not necessarily a cause. Saying that two problems are 関連している does not explain which caused which. In compounds such as 関連記事 (かんれんきじ), "related article," 関連 identifies material connected to the topic without a following な.
