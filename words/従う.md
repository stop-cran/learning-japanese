# 従う (したがう) - obey; follow; comply

## Meaning

従う is an intransitive godan verb for obeying, following, or acting in accordance with something. **人・指示・規則に従う** means "follow a person/instructions/rules." The person or standard followed takes に.

- 係員の指示に従ってください。 (かかりいんのしじにしたがってください。) - Please follow the staff member's instructions.
- 私たちは案内人に従って歩きました。 (わたしたちはあんないにんにしたがってあるきました。) - We walked following the guide.

Forms include 従います (したがいます), 従わない (したがわない), and 従って (したがって). The second example describes following a person, not merely agreeing with an instruction.

## How the kanji combine

従 carries the idea of following or complying. The written う completes the native verb したがう; there is no two-kanji compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[守る](守る.md) (まもる) can mean observing rules: 規則を守る. Notice its を pattern beside 規則に従う. [逆らう](逆らう.md) (さからう), "go against; disobey," contrasts with compliance and also uses に.

## Distinctive meaning

English "follow" covers many actions; 従う highlights going with a person or complying with a guide, instruction, or standard. It is not automatically the verb for following an account online. Keep the に attachment even where an English translation takes a direct object.
