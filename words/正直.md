# 正直 (しょうじき) - honest; frankly

## Meaning

正直 describes honesty or frankness. As a na-adjective, use 正直な人, "an honest person"; as an adverbial expression, use 正直に話す, "speak honestly." Bare 正直 can introduce a frank comment.

- 間違えた理由を正直に話しました。 (まちがえたりゆうをしょうじきにはなしました。) - I honestly explained why I made the mistake.
- 正直、この仕事は難しいです。 (しょうじき、このしごとはむずかしいです。) - Frankly, this job is difficult.

The second example signals that the speaker is giving a candid assessment, not describing the job as honest.

## How the kanji combine

[正](../kanji/正.md) contributes "right; correct," and [直](../kanji/直.md) contributes "straight; direct." Together they suggest uprightness and straightforward speech. Learn しょうじき as the compound reading, not せいちょく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[嘘](嘘.md) (うそ), "a lie," provides a contrast in behavior: 正直に話す versus 嘘をつく (うそをつく), "tell a lie." 嘘 is a noun, not an adjective that can replace 正直 with the same grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

Being 正直 does not guarantee that a statement is tactful, nor that the speaker's information is correct. The focus is sincerity or lack of concealment. 正直に言うと (しょうじきにいうと), "to be honest," is useful before an admission or an opinion that may be awkward to express.
