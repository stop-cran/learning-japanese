# もちろん (もちろん) - of course

## Meaning

もちろん presents something as natural, expected, or readily accepted: "of course" or "certainly."

- 「明日も来ますか。」「もちろん、来ます。」 (「あしたもきますか。」「もちろん、きます。」) - "Will you come tomorrow too?" "Of course I will."
- もちろん、予約は必要です。 (もちろん、よやくはひつようです。) - Of course, a reservation is necessary.

もちろんです can also stand as a polite reply. The adverb itself does not change for tense.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. Its kanji spelling is 勿論, an established Sino-Japanese expression. Learn もちろん as a whole, rather than trying to derive its conversational force from individual characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

当然 (とうぜん) emphasizes that something is natural or to be expected. [ぜひ](ぜひ.md) expresses eagerness or encouragement, and is not interchangeable with every "of course" reply.

## Distinctive meaning

Tone matters. もちろん can reassure a listener, but treating an answer as obvious can sound dismissive in some situations. It expresses the speaker's stance; it is not evidence that a claim must be true.
