# 漁師 (りょうし) - fisherman

## Meaning

漁師 is a fisherman: a person whose work is catching fish or other
seafood. It is a noun naming the person, not the fishing activity.

- 祖父は漁師です。 (そふはりょうしです。) - My grandfather is a fisherman.
- 漁師たちは朝早く船で出掛けました。 (りょうしたちはあさはやくふねででかけました。) - The fishermen went out by boat early in the morning.

## How the kanji combine

[漁](../kanji/漁.md) contributes fishing, and
[師](../kanji/師.md) a person skilled in an occupation.
The on readings are リョウ + シ. Although 漁 has ギョ in 漁業,
do not transfer that reading to 漁師.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[漁業](漁業.md) (ぎょぎょう) names fishing as an industry or occupation.
[釣り](釣り.md) (つり) is angling; it is not the noun for a fisherman.
猟師 (りょうし) is a hunter, a different word with the same pronunciation.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the person, activity and catch separate:
漁師 is the worker, 漁業 the industry, and [魚](魚.md) (さかな)
can be what is caught.
The homophone 猟師 concerns hunting rather than fishing.
