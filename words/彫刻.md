# 彫刻 (ちょうこく) - carving; engraving; sculpture

## Meaning

彫刻 concerns carving or engraving material such as wood, stone,
or metal. It also names sculpture as an art or the resulting work.
It is a noun and a する-verb; the dictionary records both transitive
and intransitive verb uses.

- 美術館で石の彫刻を見ました。 (びじゅつかんでいしのちょうこくをみました。) - I saw a stone sculpture at the art museum.
- 木に模様を彫刻しました。 (きにもようをちょうこくしました。) - I carved a pattern into the wood.

The first uses the noun for a work. In the second, を marks
the pattern carved, and に marks the material receiving it.
彫刻する can also name the activity without specifying an object.

## How the kanji combine

彫 contributes carving or sculpting, and [刻](../kanji/刻.md)
carving or engraving marks. The related meanings reinforce the
carving idea. The on readings are チョウ + コク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[刻む](刻む.md) (きざむ) also means engraving, besides its
mincing and rhythm uses. [芸術](芸術.md) (げいじゅつ),
"the arts," is broader than sculpture or carving.
Not every work of art is a 彫刻.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun can identify an activity, art form, or finished work;
the action around it helps distinguish them. Seeing a 彫刻
does not mean that the viewer made it.
The word is not limited to carved letters: a three-dimensional
sculpture also belongs to its meaning range.
