# はらはら — anxious suspense; fluttering down

## Meaning

はらはら has two useful everyday uses: anxious suspense about what may happen, and light things falling or scattering downward.

- 子供が高い木に登るのを見て、はらはらした。 — I watched anxiously as the child climbed a tall tree.
- 桜の花びらがはらはらと落ちてくる。 — Cherry petals come fluttering down. (桜: さくら; 花びら: はなびら.)
- 彼女の目から涙がはらはらとこぼれた。 — Tears trickled from her eyes. (涙: なみだ.)

Use はらはらする for suspense and はらはらと落ちる for falling. These are distinct constructions, not interchangeable senses of する.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[どきどき](どきどき.md) focuses on a pounding heart. [わくわく](わくわく.md) usually highlights eager anticipation rather than concern. [びくびく](びくびく.md) suggests ongoing fear or timidity.

## Distinctive meaning

An observer can はらはらする about someone else's risky behavior. It is not limited to fearing for oneself. In the falling sense, petals and tears need not fall noisily; the visual motion is central. Neither use means simply "laughing."
