# 言葉 (ことば) — word; expression; language

## Meaning

言葉 can mean a word or expression, or language as a means of speaking and communicating.
Context determines whether English needs a singular "word," plural "words," or the broader "language."

- この言葉の意味を教えてください。 (このことばのいみをおしえてください。) — Please tell me what this word means.
- 優しい言葉をありがとう。 (やさしいことばをありがとう。) — Thank you for your kind words.

## How the kanji combine

[言](../kanji/言.md) contributes the association with speech; [葉](../kanji/葉.md) is the
character for a leaf. Learn ことば as the established native word, with ば at the end.
The written leaf image can help memory, but "leaves of speech" is not a literal definition
or a historical derivation established here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[単語](../words/単語.md) (たんご) means a word as a vocabulary item. It fits counting or learning individual
items in a vocabulary list. 言葉 also covers an expression or someone's wording, so
the kind words in the second example need not be one 単語. There is no single useful opposite
for all these uses.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that one written string called 言葉 must be a single grammatical word.
A phrase can also be someone's 言葉. Conversely, when the context is language or dialect,
translating it mechanically as "a word" is too narrow.
