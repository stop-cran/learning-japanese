# 水筒 (すいとう) - water bottle; canteen

## Meaning

水筒 is a portable container for carrying something to drink: a water bottle, canteen or flask. It need not contain only plain water.

- 水筒にお茶を入れました。 (すいとうにおちゃをいれました。) - I put tea in my water bottle.
- 遠足には水筒を持っていきます。 (えんそくにはすいとうをもっていきます。) - I take a water bottle on an excursion.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md), water, modifies [筒](../kanji/筒.md), a tube or cylinder. The readings combine as スイ + トウ. Learn the established container meaning rather than treating every tube carrying water as a 水筒.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[瓶](瓶.md), bottle or jar, names a broader kind of vessel rather than specifically a portable drinking container. 魔法瓶 (まほうびん) is a vacuum-insulated flask; an insulated 水筒 can be one, but insulation is not required by the word 水筒.

## Distinctive meaning

Water, tea and other drinks can go in a 水筒. Neither its material nor its ability to keep a drink hot or cold is fixed by the word alone. English "canteen" here means the container, not a cafeteria.
