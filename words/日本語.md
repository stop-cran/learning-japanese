# 日本語 (にほんご) — the Japanese language

## Meaning

日本語 is the Japanese language: 日本語を勉強する (study Japanese), 日本語が話せる (can speak Japanese), 日本語の先生 (a Japanese teacher).

## How the kanji combine

[日本](日本.md) (Japan, にほん) + [語](../kanji/語.md) (ゴ, language): "Japan's language". The suffix 語 forms language names: 英語 えいご (English), 中国語 ちゅうごくご (Chinese), 外国語 がいこくご (foreign language).
Kanji: [日](../kanji/日.md), [本](../kanji/本.md), [語](../kanji/語.md). With 日本 the reading is にほん, so the word is にほんご, though にっぽんご is not normally used.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 国語 こくご (the national language; the school subject Japanese), 和語 わご (native Japanese words, as opposed to Sino-Japanese words).
- Related: 外国語 がいこくご (foreign language).

## Distinctive meaning

日本語 is the language seen from outside or as something to learn. 国語 is the school subject in Japan and also "the national language" as seen from inside. 日本語学校 is a school that teaches Japanese to foreigners.
