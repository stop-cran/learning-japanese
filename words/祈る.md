# 祈る (いのる) — pray; wish for

## Meaning

祈る means to pray, or earnestly wish for a good outcome. It is a godan verb despite ending in る: 祈ります (いのります), 祈って (いのって), 祈らない (いのらない).

- 家族の健康を祈ります。 (かぞくのけんこうをいのります。) — I pray for my family's health.
- 明日、晴れるように祈っています。 (あした、はれるようにいのっています。) — I am hoping and praying that it will be sunny tomorrow.

## How the kanji combine

祈 writes the native root いの; る is okurigana. This is a single-kanji verb, not a combination of separate words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

願う (ねがう) also means to wish or hope, and can include requests. 頼む (たのむ) means to ask someone to do something. A prayer need not be an actionable request to another person.

## Distinctive meaning

Mark the desired outcome with を, as in 成功を祈る (せいこうをいのる), "wish for success." 神に祈る (かみにいのる) marks the deity addressed with に. The word can be religious, but wishing someone success does not necessarily refer to religious belief.
