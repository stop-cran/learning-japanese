# 引き出す (ひきだす) -- withdraw money; pull out

## Meaning

引き出す means pulling something out. In banking, it means withdrawing money from an account.

- 口座から五千円を引き出しました。 (こうざからごせんえんをひきだしました。) -- I withdrew five thousand yen from my account.
- 机の下からいすを引き出しました。 (つくえのしたからいすをひきだしました。) -- I pulled the chair out from under the desk.

The item or amount takes を; its source takes から. This is a transitive godan verb ending in す: 引き出します, 引き出して, and 引き出した.

An additional use is bringing out an ability or eliciting information:

- 先生は生徒の力を引き出しました。 (せんせいはせいとのちからをひきだしました。) -- The teacher brought out the student's abilities.

This is not physically removing the student. The ability or information
still takes を.

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) appears in 引き, the combining form of 引く, pull. [出](../kanji/出.md) appears in 出す, put out. Together they describe drawing something out; the final 出す part controls conjugation.
The pinned source's 引出す is a search-only spelling in JMdict,
not a new article identity or a reason to replace 引き出す.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お金を[下ろす](下ろす.md) (おかねをおろす) is another everyday way to say withdraw money.
[預ける](預ける.md) (あずける), deposit or entrust, contrasts with withdrawal in an account context.
[出す](出す.md) (だす) and [取り出す](取り出す.md) (とりだす) overlap with taking something out.

## Distinctive meaning

English "withdraw" can mean retreating or removing an application. This card's banking sense does not make 引き出す a universal translation for all those uses. State the money, account, or physical object clearly.
