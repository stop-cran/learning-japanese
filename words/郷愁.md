# 郷愁 (きょうしゅう) — nostalgia; homesickness

## Meaning

郷愁 is the longing for one’s home while living away from it. Digital Daijisen also gives a second sense, being drawn to things of the past or to long-ago times, as in 古き良き時代への郷愁 (ふるきよきじだいへのきょうしゅう), “nostalgia for the good old days.”

- 外国で暮らしていると、ときどき強い郷愁を感じる。 (がいこくでくらしていると、ときどきつよいきょうしゅうをかんじる。) — Living abroad, I sometimes feel strong homesickness.
- 古い商店街の写真を見て、昭和の時代への郷愁を覚えた。 (ふるいしょうてんがいのしゃしんをみて、しょうわのじだいへのきょうしゅうをおぼえた。) — Looking at photos of an old shopping street, I felt nostalgia for the Showa era.

## How the kanji combine

[郷](../kanji/郷.md) (きょう) is a hometown, and [愁](../kanji/愁.md) (しゅう) is sorrow or melancholy.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[懐かしい](懐かしい.md) (なつかしい) is an adjective for something fondly remembered; 郷愁 is a noun for the feeling of longing. [故郷](故郷.md) (こきょう) is the hometown itself, and [恋しい](恋しい.md) (こいしい) means longed for. Digital Daijisen gives 郷愁を覚える, “feel nostalgia,” and 郷愁にかられる (きょうしゅうにかられる), “be seized by nostalgia.”

## Distinctive meaning

The second example uses Daijisen’s second sense. There 覚える means “to feel,” not “to memorize.” A related word for looking back on the past is 懐古 (かいこ), which JMdict glosses “reminiscence; nostalgia.”
