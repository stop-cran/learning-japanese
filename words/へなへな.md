# へなへな — limp, weak, or easy to bend

## Meaning

へなへな describes something limp or flimsy, or a person becoming weak and unable to stand firmly.

- 長い散歩のあと、足がへなへなになった。
  — After the long walk, my legs felt weak and wobbly.
- 薄い紙袋は、雨にぬれてへなへなになった。
  — The thin paper bag became limp after getting wet in the rain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

へなへな suggests loss of firmness or strength, rather than a sturdy or resilient condition.

## Distinctive meaning

It can describe either a physical object or a person's weakened state.
