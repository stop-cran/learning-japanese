# 肯定 (こうてい) - affirmation

## Meaning

肯定 is affirmation: treating a statement, fact or idea affirmatively.
肯定する is transitive; the thing affirmed can take を. The word does
not automatically establish liking, formal permission or legal approval.

- 彼はその事実を肯定しました。
  (かれはそのじじつをこうていしました。) - He affirmed that fact.
- 相手の意見をすべて肯定する必要はありません。
  (あいてのいけんをすべてこうていするひつようはありません。) - You do not need to affirm all the other person's views.

The noun can also have the logic-related meaning affirmative, in contrast
to a negative or negation. The context determines whether this technical
use is intended.

## How the kanji combine

[肯](../kanji/肯.md) contributes affirming or consenting, and
[定](../kanji/定.md) contributes determining or establishing.
Together they concern an affirmative determination, not two separate
actions that must occur in sequence. The on readings コウ + テイ give こうてい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[否定](否定.md) (ひてい), denial or negation, supplies a direct contrast.
[賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい) concerns agreement or support.
[認める](認める.md) (みとめる) can mean recognize or acknowledge.
These uses can overlap with affirmation without permitting substitution
in every construction.

[承認](承認.md) (しょうにん) can concern approval or recognition, while
[承知](承知.md) (しょうち) includes knowing or consenting.
[一致](一致.md) (いっち) can concern agreement or correspondence.
Their broad English labels are not precise definitions of 肯定.

## Distinctive meaning

Affirming that something exists or is true is not necessarily approving
of it. Conversely, agreement between two accounts does not itself mean
that someone performed an act of 肯定. Establish what is being affirmed
instead of treating every yes-like response as the same Japanese word.
