# ダブル - double; double bed or room

## Meaning

ダブル means double or twofold. ダブルの + noun is useful;
it also appears at the beginning of compounds.

- ダブルの喜びを味わいました。
  (ダブルのよろこびをあじわいました。) - I experienced a double joy.
- ホテルでダブルを一部屋予約しました。
  (ホテルでダブルをひとへやよやくしました。) - I booked a double room at the hotel.

In hotel terminology, ダブル can abbreviate a double bed or a
room with one. It does not simply mean any room for two people:
ツイン ordinarily identifies a room with two separate beds.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "double." There are no
written kanji components. W sometimes represents ダブル in labels
such as W受賞 (ダブルじゅしょう), a double award.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[倍](倍.md) (ばい) can mean double or express a numerical
multiplier. シングル, "single," contrasts with ダブル in
appropriate room, clothing, or quantity contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

Other domain uses include double-breasted clothing, double cuffs,
double-width cloth, and doubles in sports. The object and setting
identify the intended meaning; not every use specifies a room or
an exact physical size. ダブル is not the ordinary conjugatable
verb ダブる, "overlap/be duplicated." The rare identity-label
sense is outside this article's practical teaching.
