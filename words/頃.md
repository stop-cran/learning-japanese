# 頃 (ころ) - time; around a time

## Meaning

頃 names a time or period, often without a sharp boundary. The reading ころ occurs in 子供の頃 (こどものころ), "when I was a child," and 若い頃 (わかいころ), "when I was young."

- 子供の頃、この町に住んでいました。 (こどものころ、このまちにすんでいました。) - I lived in this town as a child.
- 三時頃に着きます。 (さんじごろにつきます。) - I will arrive around three o'clock.

Notice the reading change: as an approximate-time suffix after 三時, 頃 is ごろ, not ころ. Both readings belong to this dictionary entry.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 頃 represents the word. Kana spellings ころ and ごろ are also used. In 子供の頃 a noun connects through の; in 若い頃 an い-adjective directly modifies 頃.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時](時.md) (とき) also means "time; when." 頃 can frame a period or an approximate point rather than a sharply specified moment. 三時ちょうど (さんじちょうど), "exactly three," contrasts with 三時ごろ.

## Distinctive meaning

頃 can also indicate a suitable stage: 食べ頃 (たべごろ), "ready to eat; at the right stage for eating." Learn these whole expressions with their readings. The source reading ころ is not a license to pronounce every suffix occurrence that way, and "around" here concerns time, not position around an object.
