# 記入 (きにゅう) - filling in; entry on a form

## Meaning

記入 is a noun and transitive する verb for entering written information in a form, register, or similar record. Use 場所に内容を記入する: enter the information marked with を in the space or document marked with に.

- この欄に名前を記入してください。 (このらんになまえをきにゅうしてください。) - Please enter your name in this field.
- 申込書に住所を記入しました。 (もうしこみしょにじゅうしょをきにゅうしました。) - I filled in my address on the application form.

Instructions may say ご記入ください (ごきにゅうください), a polite "please fill in." 記入済み (きにゅうずみ) means that the entry or filling-in has been completed.

## How the kanji combine

[記](../kanji/記.md) means write down or record; [入](../kanji/入.md) contributes putting something in. Together they describe writing information into a designated place, rather than entering a room.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[書く](書く.md) (かく) is the broader verb "write." [記録](記録.md) (きろく), "record," can cover preserving information in many forms. 記入 focuses on filling in written particulars. 消す (けす), "erase," can reverse a written entry, but is not an opposite for every administrative use.

## Distinctive meaning

The English noun "entry" is ambiguous. 記入 concerns entering information, not admission to a venue. A form's 記入例 (きにゅうれい), "example of how to fill it in," shows the intended contents and arrangement. Learn the に/を pattern so that the field and the information do not get reversed.
