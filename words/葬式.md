# 葬式 (そうしき) - funeral

## Meaning

葬式 is a funeral: a ceremony associated with someone's death.
The form お葬式 (おそうしき) adds the prefix お.
Use 葬式に出る (そうしきにでる) for attending a funeral.

- 昨日、親戚のお葬式に出ました。 (きのう、しんせきのおそうしきにでました。) - I attended a relative's funeral yesterday.
- 葬式は午後に行われます。 (そうしきはごごにおこなわれます。) - The funeral will take place in the afternoon.

に marks the event attended in the first example.
行われる (おこなわれる), "be held," describes the ceremony taking place.

## How the kanji combine

葬 contributes funeral or burial associations, and
[式](../kanji/式.md) a ceremony.
The first component specifies the kind of ceremony.
The on readings are ソウ + シキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

葬儀 (そうぎ) also names a funeral.
[儀式](儀式.md) (ぎしき) and [式](式.md) (しき)
are broader words for a ceremony.
通夜 (つや), a wake, is related but not an unrestricted substitute:
it can identify a separate observance.

## Distinctive meaning

葬式 does not specify one religion, burial method, or fixed program.
Customs and the relationship between a funeral, wake, and farewell ceremony
depend on the setting. The word names the event, not the grave itself;
compare [墓](墓.md) (はか), grave or tomb.
