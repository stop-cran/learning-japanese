# もくもく — smoke or clouds billowing

## Meaning

Smoke, clouds, or similar masses rising in puffs or billows.

- 煙突（えんとつ）から煙（けむり）がもくもく上がっている。 — Smoke is billowing from the chimney.
- 空に大きな雲（くも）がもくもく湧（わ）いてきた。 — Large clouds began billowing up in the sky.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もこもこ](もこもこ.md) can suggest a puffy, bulky appearance; it does not necessarily describe rising smoke. [めらめら](めらめら.md) concerns flaring flames rather than smoke itself.

## Distinctive meaning

This visual mimetic is different from 黙々（もくもく）と働く（はたらく）, “work silently.” They share a pronunciation, but the smoke word does not mean that the chimney is concentrating quietly.
