# 縁 (ふち) - rim; edge

## Meaning

縁 (ふち) is a noun meaning a rim, brim, edge, or brink.
It names the boundary around an object or opening.
Use Xの縁 for "the rim/edge of X," and 縁に for a location on that boundary.

- コップの縁が欠けています。 (コップのふちがかけています。) - The rim of the glass is chipped.
- 池の縁に石が並んでいます。 (いけのふちにいしがならんでいます。) - Stones are lined up around the edge of the pond.

The first example describes a damaged rim; the second locates objects along a boundary.
帽子の縁 (ぼうしのふち) can refer to a hat's brim.

## How the kanji combine

縁 represents the whole native noun ふち here.
One written character can belong to several words, so the character alone does not establish its reading.
Learn ふち with physical rims and edges rather than importing an unrelated definition of 縁.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[端](端.md) (はし) is an end or edge more broadly.
[境](境.md) (さかい) focuses on a boundary between areas.
[中心](中心.md) (ちゅうしん), "center," provides a useful spatial contrast, but is not a universal antonym for every rim.

## Distinctive meaning

縁 can follow the outline of a cup, pond, or similar object rather than identifying just one endpoint.
That outline need not be circular.
The supported meaning of **ふち** here is rim/edge; "a means of living" does not belong to this reading and should not be learned from a conflicting source gloss.
