# 沈める (しずめる) - sink; submerge something

## Meaning

沈める means to sink or submerge something. It is a transitive ichidan
verb. Mark the affected object with を; に can mark where it is submerged.

- 実験で、小さな石を水に沈めました。 (じっけんで、ちいさないしをみずにしずめました。) - In the experiment, I submerged a small stone in water.
- この重りを水の底に沈めます。 (このおもりをみずのそこにしずめます。) - I will sink this weight to the bottom of the water.

Forms include 沈めます (しずめます), 沈めて (しずめて), and
沈めない (しずめない).

## How the kanji combine

[沈](../kanji/沈.md) supplies しず and the sinking idea; める is written
in kana. The conjugation stem is 沈め (しずめ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[沈む](沈む.md) (しずむ) is the intransitive counterpart:
石が沈む (いしがしずむ), a stone sinks. Compare 石を沈める
(いしをしずめる), sink a stone.

[浮く](浮く.md) (うく), float or rise to the surface, contrasts with the
object's sinking. It is intransitive, so it is not a word-for-word
replacement in the を construction.

## Distinctive meaning

沈める is a separate transitive verb, not the grammatical causative form
of 沈む. The causative 沈ませる (しずませる) belongs to that other verb's
conjugation; learn the ordinary transitive/intransitive pair first.

The examples here concern movement below a surface. The dictionary also
recognizes lowering one's body into a seat. The presence of める does not
by itself make this verb about calming a person or quieting a room.
