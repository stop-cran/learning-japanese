# 怖い (こわい) — scary; frightening

## Meaning

怖い describes something frightening and can express the speaker's fear of it. The English wording
depends on what the sentence is presenting.

- この映画は怖いです。 (このえいがはこわいです。) — This film is scary.
- 私は犬が怖いです。 (わたしはいぬがこわいです。) — I am afraid of dogs.

In the second example, は marks the person experiencing the fear, and が marks what is frightening
to that person.

## How the kanji combine

[怖](../kanji/怖.md) writes こわ and contributes the fear meaning; い makes the adjective form.
There is only one kanji, so no compound relation needs to be invented.

## Synonyms and antonyms

恐ろしい (おそろしい) also means frightening or dreadful.
[恐れる](恐れる.md) (おそれる) is a verb: 犬を恐れる (いぬをおそれる), "fear dogs."
These expressions overlap in meaning, but 怖い takes adjective grammar rather than that verb's を pattern.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 怖い only as "scary" and miss a statement about someone's own fear.
The related verb 怖がる (こわがる) describes being afraid: 子供が犬を怖がっています
(こどもがいぬをこわがっています), "The child is afraid of the dog."
For the adjective's negative, use 怖くない (こわくない), "not scary; not afraid."
