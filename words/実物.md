# 実物 (じつぶつ) - actual object; real thing

## Meaning

実物 is the actual thing or object, as opposed to a photograph, model, description, or representation of it. It is a noun; **実物を見る** (じつぶつをみる) means "see the actual thing."

- 写真だけでなく、実物も見たいです。
  (しゃしんだけでなく、じつぶつもみたいです。) - I want to see the actual thing, not just photographs.
- 実物は写真で見るより小さかったです。
  (じつぶつはしゃしんでみるよりちいさかったです。) - The actual object was smaller than it looked in the photograph.

実物の大きさ (じつぶつのおおきさ) is "the size of the actual object." 実物大 (じつぶつだい), "actual size; life-size," can describe a representation matching that size; the representation is not thereby the original object.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes reality or actuality and [物](../kanji/物.md) a thing. The first modifies the second: the actual thing. The on readings are ジツ + ブツ, with no small っ: じつぶつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本物](本物.md) (ほんもの), "genuine article; real thing," overlaps. 本物 especially invites an authenticity contrast with an imitation; 実物 is especially useful when contrasting the thing itself with an image or account. These are emphases, not mutually exclusive categories.

[写真](写真.md) (しゃしん), a photograph, can depict the 実物 without being that object.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary's English "original" means the thing itself in this contrast. It does not make 実物 a general adjective for a novel or creative idea. Likewise, 実物 does not by itself guarantee that a displayed object is an authentic antique: that is a separate question about what the object is.
