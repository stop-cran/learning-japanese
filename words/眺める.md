# 眺める (ながめる) - gaze at; look out over

## Meaning

眺める means to look at or gaze at something, including looking out over a view.
It is a transitive ichidan verb: the scene or object takes を.
The polite form is 眺めます, the past 眺めた, and the negative 眺めない.

- 窓から海を眺めていました。 (まどからうみをながめていました。) - I was gazing at the sea through the window.
- 古い写真をしばらく眺めました。 (ふるいしゃしんをしばらくながめました。) - I looked at the old photograph for a while.

から marks the viewpoint in the first example. 眺めている describes looking
in progress, rather than a single moment of noticing something.

## How the kanji combine

In the spelling 眺める, 眺 is read なが and める is written in kana. The character concerns
looking or gazing. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide into separate
meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[見る](見る.md) (みる) is the broader verb "see; look; watch."
見つめる (みつめる) emphasizes fixing one's gaze on something.
眺める works naturally with a landscape, but the photograph example shows that
it does not require a distant or panoramic object.

## Distinctive meaning

眺める often presents looking as an ongoing activity rather than a quick check.
It does not by itself guarantee admiration or enjoyment: someone can gaze at
an uninteresting scene. Let the context establish the emotion instead of
translating every use as "admire."
