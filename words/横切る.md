# 横切る (よこぎる) - cross; traverse

## Meaning

横切る is a transitive godan verb for crossing a road, space, or another path. The crossed area takes **を**. It emphasizes moving across, rather than following the area's length.

- 猫が道を横切りました。 (ねこがみちをよこぎりました。) - A cat crossed the road.
- 人の前を横切るときは気をつけてください。 (ひとのまえをよこぎるときはきをつけてください。) - Please be careful when passing in front of someone.

The past is 横切った (よこぎった), the negative is 横切らない (よこぎらない), and the て-form is 横切って. Despite ending in る, it is not an ichidan verb.

## How the kanji combine

[横](../kanji/横.md) contributes across or sideways; [切](../kanji/切.md) contributes cutting. The native compound よこ + きる becomes よこぎる through voicing. "Cut across" is a useful English parallel, but no literal cutting instrument is implied.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[渡る](渡る.md) (わたる) overlaps in crossing a road or river and can emphasize getting to the other side. [通る](通る.md) (とおる) is broader passing through or along a route.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate "cross one's arms" with 横切る. That English expression means folding or arranging the arms, not traversing a space; 腕を組む (うでをくむ) expresses it. The object of 横切る is the area or path being crossed.
