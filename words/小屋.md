# 小屋 (こや) - hut; shed; cabin

## Meaning

小屋 is a hut, shed, cabin, or similar small building. It can shelter people, animals, or equipment. The purpose is often made explicit by a preceding word.

- 庭の小屋に道具をしまいました。 (にわのこやにどうぐをしまいました。) - I put the tools away in the garden shed.
- 山小屋で一晩休みました。 (やまごやでひとばんやすみました。) - I rested for one night in a mountain hut.

犬小屋 (いぬごや) means "doghouse." In these compounds, こや changes to ごや; learn the reading with each expression.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) contributes smallness and [屋](../kanji/屋.md) a building or shelter. The first modifies the second. Both use native readings, こ + や, rather than forming an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[家](家.md) (いえ) is a house or home. 小屋 emphasizes the kind of structure, not a household or family. [部屋](部屋.md) (へや) is a room within a building, so a room and a shed are not the same unit.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling something 小屋 does not establish that it is abandoned, temporary, or unfit for use. A mountain hut can be maintained and provide accommodation. Nor should every small house automatically be called 小屋: the word can suggest a simple shelter rather than an ordinary residence.
