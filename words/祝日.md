# 祝日 (しゅくじつ) - public holiday; national holiday

## Meaning

祝日 is a public or national holiday. It names a calendar day, not simply any day when one person is free from work. Use 祝日の before a following noun.

- 祝日なので、学校は休みです。
  (しゅくじつなので、がっこうはやすみです。) - School is closed because it is a public holiday.
- この店は祝日も営業しています。
  (このみせはしゅくじつもえいぎょうしています。) - This shop is open on public holidays too.

The second example shows why a public holiday does not guarantee that every workplace closes.

## How the kanji combine

[祝](../kanji/祝.md) contributes celebration, and [日](../kanji/日.md) a day. The first identifies the kind of day. Read シュク + ジツ, not しゅうじつ or しゅくにち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[祭日](祭日.md) (さいじつ) also has a national-holiday use, as well as a festival or observance day. Do not infer completely separate present-day holiday calendars from the different kanji.

[休日](休日.md) (きゅうじつ) is a day off more broadly, and [休み](休み.md) (やすみ) can be a break, absence, or day off. Someone's personal day off need not be a 祝日.

## Distinctive meaning

A birthday or private celebration does not automatically become a public holiday. Likewise, [連休](連休.md) (れんきゅう) means consecutive days off; it does not say that every day in the stretch is a national holiday.
