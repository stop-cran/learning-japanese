# 随筆 (ずいひつ) - personal essay; literary jottings

## Meaning

随筆 is an essay or informal literary writing built around the writer's experiences, observations and thoughts. It need not follow a rigid argumentative structure.

- 日常の出来事について随筆を書きました。 (にちじょうのできごとについてずいひつをかきました。) - I wrote an essay about everyday events.
- この随筆には、作者の子供時代の思い出が書かれています。 (このずいひつには、さくしゃのこどもじだいのおもいでがかかれています。) - This essay describes the author's childhood memories.

## How the kanji combine

随 contributes following along, and [筆](../kanji/筆.md) contributes writing or the writing brush. Read ズイ + ヒツ. "Following the brush" is a helpful way to recall the freely developing writing, not a requirement to use a brush.

## Synonyms and antonyms

エッセイ (えっせい) overlaps substantially with 随筆. [作文](作文.md) can be composition or essay writing, including school assignments. [文章](文章.md) is written text more broadly. [論文](論文.md) focuses on an argument or research; not every paper called an "essay" in English is a 随筆.

## Distinctive meaning

Free-form does not mean careless or unedited. The emphasis is the writer's reflections rather than a mandatory academic proof structure. Do not make 随筆 and エッセイ artificially disjoint categories. JMdict also records 隨筆 as a search-only spelling; 随筆 is the teaching form here.
