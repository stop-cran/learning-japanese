# 社会科学 (しゃかいかがく) - social science

## Meaning

社会科学 is social science: systematic study of social phenomena, institutions, and human social relationships. Fields such as sociology, economics, and political science provide examples.

- 大学で社会科学を学んでいます。
  (だいがくでしゃかいかがくをまなんでいます。) - I study social science at university.
- この図書館には社会科学の本がたくさんあります。
  (このとしょかんにはしゃかいかがくのほんがたくさんあります。) - This library has many books on the social sciences.

The Japanese noun can refer to the field collectively; English may use "social science" or "the social sciences."

## How the kanji combine

[社会](社会.md) (しゃかい), society, modifies [科学](科学.md) (かがく), science. [社](../kanji/社.md) and [会](../kanji/会.md) form the society part; [科](../kanji/科.md) and [学](../kanji/学.md) form the science part. The reading is シャ + カイ + カ + ガク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[自然科学](自然科学.md) (しぜんかがく), natural science, is a contrasting field, not a synonym. 科学 is broader. 社会学 (しゃかいがく), sociology, is one social-science discipline, not a shorter interchangeable name for all 社会科学.

## Distinctive meaning

社会科学 does not simply mean knowing how to behave socially or taking part in society. It names a field of inquiry. Boundaries among social sciences, humanities, and other fields can vary by institution and framework; the examples above are not a universal administrative classification.
