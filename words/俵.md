# 俵 (たわら) — straw bag; bale

## Meaning

俵 is a straw bag, sack or bale.

- 重い俵を二人で運びました。 (おもいたわらをふたりではこびました。) — Two people carried a heavy bale.
- 倉庫には、米を入れた俵が積んであります。 (そうこには、こめをいれたたわらがつんであります。) — Bales filled with rice are stacked in the storehouse.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji word. The character [俵](../kanji/俵.md) is read たわら here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The card focuses on the object sense; the separate counter reading is described below.

## Distinctive meaning

俵 can also be used as a counter for sacks. JMdict lists ひょう for that counter sense; the selected word reading in this article is たわら.
