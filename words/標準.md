# 標準 (ひょうじゅん) - standard; norm; average

## Meaning

標準 is a standard, criterion, or norm used as a reference.
It also names an average or ordinary level.
Xを標準にする means to use X as the standard.

- この大きさを標準にしています。 (このおおきさをひょうじゅんにしています。) - We use this size as the standard.
- 私の身長はほぼ標準です。 (わたしのしんちょうはほぼひょうじゅんです。) - My height is about average.

標準的な (ひょうじゅんてきな) means standard or typical,
as in 標準的な大きさ, a standard size.

## How the kanji combine

標 contributes a mark or reference, and [準](../kanji/準.md)
conformity to a standard. Their related ideas combine in a reference
or norm for comparison. The on readings are ヒョウ + ジュン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[基準](基準.md) (きじゅん) also means a standard or criterion,
often a reference or requirement for judging something.
標準 often highlights an ordinary or typical model.
This is an emphasis, not an absolute boundary: the words overlap
in standards used for comparison.
[水準](水準.md) (すいじゅん) concerns a level or standard.

## Distinctive meaning

[平均](平均.md) (へいきん) can name a calculated average.
標準 need not be the arithmetic mean of measured values:
a standard can be chosen or agreed upon.
[普通](普通.md) (ふつう) overlaps in ordinary or normal, but
does not mean that every ordinary thing is a formal standard.
Neither 標準 nor an average level is automatically the best possible level.
