# 下ろす (おろす) - lower; take down

## Meaning

下ろす is a transitive godan verb: lower something or bring it down. Mark the thing moved with を, its starting point with から, and its destination with に when needed.

- 棚から箱を下ろしました。 (たなからはこをおろしました。) - I took the box down from the shelf.
- 手を下ろしてください。 (てをおろしてください。) - Please lower your hand.

The polite form is 下ろします, the te-form 下ろして, and the negative 下ろさない. Another practical use is お金を下ろす (おかねをおろす), "withdraw money."

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) contributes the downward direction; ろす completes the native verb おろす. The spelling 下す with reading おろす is recorded as irregular okurigana usage. Learn 下ろす as the full spelling here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[降ろす](降ろす.md), also おろす, shares the dictionary entry and is especially associated with unloading goods or dropping off passengers. [降りる](降りる.md) (おりる) describes the passenger getting off, rather than someone letting the passenger off.

## Distinctive meaning

The source spelling 下す does not turn this assignment into a different reading such as くだす. For おろす, choose spelling with the use in mind: taking a box down and dropping off a passenger belong to the same broad verb family, but that does not make all spellings equally suitable in every expression.
