# 立てる (たてる) — stand something upright

## Meaning

立てる means to stand something up, put it upright, or erect it. It is transitive: the object being positioned takes を.

- 庭に旗を立てました。  
  にわにはたをたてました。  
  "I put up a flag in the garden."
- 本を立てて並べます。  
  ほんをたててならべます。  
  "I arrange the books standing upright."

The word has other established uses, such as 計画を立てる (けいかくをたてる), "make a plan"; not every object is physically raised.

## How the kanji combine

[立](../kanji/立.md) relates to standing. The ichidan verb is たてる: 立てます (たてます), 立てて (たてて), 立てない (たてない). Its grammatical stem is たて, not just the kanji's written portion た.

## Synonyms and antonyms

立つ (たつ), "stand; be standing", is intransitive: 旗が立っている (はたがたっている), "A flag is standing". 横にする (よこにする), "lay sideways", contrasts the upright position.

## Distinctive meaning

建てる is also read たてる, but means to construct a building. Use 家を建てる (いえをたてる), "build a house", rather than assuming the two spellings are interchangeable.
