# ごしごし — scrubbing vigorously

## Meaning

Repeated, forceful rubbing or scrubbing, usually with a clear back-and-forth effort.

- ブラシで靴（くつ）の泥（どろ）をごしごし落とした。 — I scrubbed the mud off the shoes with a brush.
- タオルで床（ゆか）をごしごしこすった。 — I rubbed the floor vigorously with a towel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぽりぽり](ぽりぽり.md) can suggest little scratches, whereas ごしごし emphasizes sustained rubbing. [そっと](そっと.md) offers a gentle-handling contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

ごしごしこする and ごしごし洗う（あらう）describe the effort, not a guarantee that the surface becomes clean. [ぴかぴか](ぴかぴか.md) instead describes a gleaming result or appearance.
