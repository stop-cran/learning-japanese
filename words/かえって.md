# かえって - instead; contrary to expectation

## Meaning

かえって introduces a result contrary to what was expected: "instead," "rather" or "on the contrary." It can also suggest that something happens all the more as a contrary result.

- 休むつもりでしたが、かえって疲れました。 (やすむつもりでしたが、かえってつかれました。) - I meant to rest, but ended up more tired instead.
- 急ぐと、かえって失敗しやすくなります。 (いそぐと、かえってしっぱいしやすくなります。) - If you rush, you can become more likely to make a mistake instead.

The second reverses the expectation that hurrying will help. Neither example merely replaces one object with another.

## How the kanji combine

This adverb is usually written in kana. Dictionary spellings include 却って and 反って, both read かえって. [反](../kanji/反.md) supplies a useful visual association with reversal, not proof of the adverb's historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[寧ろ](寧ろ.md) (むしろ) selects a more fitting description or alternative; it need not report a surprising result. [ところが](ところが.md), "however; contrary to expectation," connects a following statement, whereas かえって works inside the clause describing the contrary result.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every English "instead" with かえって. Choosing tea instead of coffee need not contradict an expectation. Likewise, the same sound かえって can be the te-form of [帰る](帰る.md) or [返る](返る.md); those are conjugated verbs, not this adverb.
