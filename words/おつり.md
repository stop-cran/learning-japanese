# おつり - change from a payment

## Meaning

おつり is the money returned when you pay more than the price. It is a noun.

- おつりは二百円です。 (おつりはにひゃくえんです。) - Your change is two hundred yen.
- おつりを確認しました。 (おつりをかくにんしました。) - I checked my change.

It can refer to coins, banknotes, or both; it does not mean only small coins.

## How the kanji combine

This page uses kana. A normal alternative is お釣り. お is a polite prefix attached to つり; learn the whole word as the shopping expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

小銭 (こぜに) means small change or coins regardless of how they were received. 代金 (だいきん) is the amount paid for a purchase, not the money returned.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's English "balance" means the remainder returned from a payment here, not a bank-account balance or an amount still owed.
