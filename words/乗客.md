# 乗客 (じょうきゃく) - passenger

## Meaning

乗客 is a noun meaning passenger, especially in descriptions of transport services. It applies to people traveling on trains, buses, ships, aircraft, and similar vehicles.

- バスには乗客が十人いました。 (バスにはじょうきゃくがじゅうにんいました。) - There were ten passengers on the bus.
- 乗客は順番に飛行機を降りました。 (じょうきゃくはじゅんばんにひこうきをおりました。) - The passengers got off the plane in order.

電車の乗客 (でんしゃのじょうきゃく) means "the train's passengers." Japanese does not require a plural ending: the same noun can describe one passenger or many.

## How the kanji combine

[乗](../kanji/乗.md) means ride or board, and [客](../kanji/客.md) means guest or customer. 客 identifies the person, and 乗 specifies their transport role. Learn the assigned reading じょうきゃく; the dictionary also records じょうかく for this same entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[客](客.md) (きゃく) is broader: a guest or customer need not be traveling. [運転手](運転手.md) (うんてんしゅ), "driver," names a contrasting role rather than an antonym. [観客](観客.md) (かんきゃく) means spectators or an audience, not passengers.

## Distinctive meaning

乗客 identifies the person's role as a traveler being carried, not merely their physical presence inside a vehicle. A driver and passengers can be on the same bus without having the same role. A notice addressing passengers may use this word even when the English translation uses the friendlier "customers."
