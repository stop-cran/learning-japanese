# 患者 (かんじゃ) - patient

## Meaning

患者 is a noun for a medical patient: a person receiving examination or treatment. It is not the adjective "patient" meaning able to wait calmly.

- 医師が患者に薬の説明をしました。 (いしがかんじゃにくすりのせつめいをしました。) - The doctor explained the medicine to the patient.
- 待合室に患者が三人います。 (まちあいしつにかんじゃがさんにんいます。) - There are three patients in the waiting room.

患者に identifies the recipient of an explanation or treatment; 患者を診る (かんじゃをみる) means "examine a patient."

## How the kanji combine

患 concerns illness or suffering from illness; [者](../kanji/者.md) means a person associated with the preceding idea. The compound identifies the person in the medical-care role. カン + ジャ gives かんじゃ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

病人 (びょうにん) means a sick person and focuses on being ill rather than the relationship to medical care.

[医者](医者.md) (いしゃ), "doctor," is the corresponding caregiver role, not a true opposite. A doctor can also become a patient.

## Distinctive meaning

患者 does not tell you whether someone is staying in a [病院](病院.md) (びょういん), "hospital," or visiting briefly. 入院患者 (にゅういんかんじゃ) specifies an inpatient. In direct interaction, 患者さん adds a respectful さん; the bare noun is useful when discussing patients as a category.
