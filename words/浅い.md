# 浅い (あさい) - shallow

## Meaning

浅い is an i-adjective meaning shallow, with little depth. It also describes something superficial or not deeply developed.

- この川は浅いです。 (このかわはあさいです。) - This river is shallow.
- 私はこの仕事の経験が浅いです。 (わたしはこのしごとのけいけんがあさいです。) - I have little experience in this work.

Before a noun, use 浅い directly: 浅い川, "a shallow river." The negative is 浅くない (あさくない).

## How the kanji combine

浅 writes the native adjective あさい; い is okurigana. There is no compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[深い](深い.md) (ふかい), "deep," is the central opposite. 薄い (うすい), "thin; weak," concerns thickness or concentration rather than a river's depth.

## Distinctive meaning

The metaphor depends on the noun: 経験が浅い concerns limited experience, while 眠りが浅い (ねむりがあさい) means sleeping lightly. Do not translate every occurrence as physically shallow.
