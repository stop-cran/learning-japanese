# 待つ (まつ) — wait; await

## Meaning

待つ means to wait, including waiting for a person or an event. The person or thing awaited can take を; the place where you wait takes で.

- 駅で友達を待ちます。  
  えきでともだちをまちます。  
  "I'll wait for my friend at the station."
- ちょっと待ってください。  
  ちょっとまってください。  
  "Please wait a moment."

## How the kanji combine

This is one native verb. [待](../kanji/待.md) supplies ま and つ is okurigana. It is a godan verb: 待ちます まちます, 待って まって, 待たない またない. The て-form has small っ, not つ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

待ち合わせる まちあわせる means to meet at an agreed place and time; simply waiting does not imply such an arrangement. [急ぐ](急ぐ.md) いそぐ (hurry) is a useful situational contrast, not an exact antonym. There is no single direct opposite covering every use of 待つ.

## Distinctive meaning

待っています まっています means "am waiting" and is useful when telling someone you are awaiting them. Unlike English "wait for", the basic Japanese pattern directly marks the awaited person with を: 友達を待つ ともだちをまつ.
