# 大切 (たいせつ) — important; precious

## Meaning

大切 means important, valuable, or dear to someone. It is a na-adjective: 大切な人 (たいせつなひと), "someone precious to me," and 大切です, "is important."

- これは大切な書類です。 (これはたいせつなしょるいです。) — This is an important document.
- この時計を大切にしています。 (このとけいをたいせつにしています。) — I treasure this watch and take good care of it.

大切にする means to value, cherish, or handle something with care; the object takes を.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) has the reading タイ here, and [切](../kanji/切.md) is セツ. The compound means importance or preciousness; "big cutting" is not a usable translation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

大事 (だいじ) closely overlaps in importance and careful treatment. 重要 (じゅうよう) often emphasizes significance or practical importance. 粗末にする (そまつにする), "treat carelessly," contrasts with 大切にする.

## Distinctive meaning

大切 can concern either practical importance or personal attachment. A cherished inexpensive watch can be 大切 without being financially valuable.
