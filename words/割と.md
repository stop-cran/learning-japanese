# 割と (わりと) - relatively; fairly

## Meaning

割と is an adverb for a relative assessment: comparatively, relatively,
fairly or pretty. The comparison can be with what was expected.

- このかばんは割と軽いです。 (このかばんはわりとかるいです。) - This bag is fairly light.
- 試験は割と簡単でした。 (しけんはわりとかんたんでした。) - The exam was relatively easy.

The second sentence can suggest that it was easier than expected,
but does not state that no effort was needed. 割りと is another recognized spelling.

## How the kanji combine

[割](../kanji/割.md) supplies the native proportion/share association わり.
と belongs to the established adverb; this is not the character's on reading カツ.
Learn the whole form rather than adding と to arbitrary nouns to make comparison adverbs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[割合に](割合に.md) (わりあいに) and [比較的](比較的.md) (ひかくてき)
overlap closely. [かなり](かなり.md) expresses a considerable degree;
these expressions do not form a fixed percentage scale.
[寧ろ](寧ろ.md) (むしろ) instead chooses a better alternative description.

## Distinctive meaning

English "rather" can express degree or a preference between alternatives.
割と has the comparative/degree use here, not a general "rather than" meaning.
Its relative standard can be implicit, and the assessment can be favorable
or unfavorable depending on what follows.
