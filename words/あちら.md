# あちら - over there; that way

## Meaning

あちら points to a place or direction away from both speaker and listener. It is useful in polite directions, often corresponding to "over there" or "that way."

出口はあちらです。 (でぐちはあちらです。) - The exit is over there.

あちらでお待ちください。 (あちらでおまちください。) - Please wait over there.

## How the kanji combine

Normally written in hiragana, あちら belongs to the こちら・そちら・あちら・どちら demonstrative set. The dictionary spelling 彼方 is not needed for ordinary directions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

あそこ also means "over there," focusing on a place. あっち is a more casual counterpart. こちら points toward the speaker's side; そちら toward the listener's side or a contextually identified place.

## Distinctive meaning

あちら can politely refer to a person or the other party, not only a location. Particles still express the relationship: あちらへ indicates direction, while あちらで marks where an action happens. It is not an adjective placed directly before a noun.
