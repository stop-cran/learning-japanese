# 吸収 (きゅうしゅう) - absorption

## Meaning

吸収 is absorption or taking something in. It becomes a transitive verb with する: **something を吸収する**. What is absorbed can be a physical substance, energy, or knowledge.

- この布は水をよく吸収します。 (このぬのはみずをよくきゅうしゅうします。) - This cloth absorbs water well.
- 子供は新しい知識を吸収しています。 (こどもはあたらしいちしきをきゅうしゅうしています。) - The child is absorbing new knowledge.

The passive 吸収される describes something being absorbed. 水が吸収される means "water is absorbed," with the water now marked by が.

## How the kanji combine

[吸](../kanji/吸.md) contributes drawing or sucking in, while [収](../kanji/収.md) contributes taking in or receiving. Their meanings reinforce intake into something. The readings are キュウ + シュウ; both parts have long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[吸う](吸う.md) (すう) is a broader native verb for sucking in or inhaling. [理解](理解.md) (りかい) means understanding; absorbing knowledge suggests taking it in, rather than naming every step of understanding it. Releasing or emitting something can contrast with absorption, depending on the process.

## Distinctive meaning

Absorption implies intake, not merely attraction from a distance. The source gloss "attraction" should therefore not be read as a general word for personal attractiveness. The water and knowledge examples share an intake image without describing identical physical mechanisms.
