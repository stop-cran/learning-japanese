# 全く (まったく) - completely; not at all

## Meaning

全く is an adverb emphasizing completeness: "completely" or "entirely." With a negative predicate, it gives "not at all."

- 二つの意見は全く違います。 (ふたつのいけんはまったくちがいます。) - The two opinions are completely different.
- この説明は全く分かりません。 (このせつめいはまったくわかりません。) - I do not understand this explanation at all.

The negative comes from 分かりません, not from 全く itself. No extra に is needed before the predicate.

## How the kanji combine

[全](../kanji/全.md) contributes completeness. It writes the native adverb まったく, with く as okurigana. Compare the same character in [全て](全て.md) (すべて), which means all or everything; the reading and grammatical role differ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[完全](完全.md) (かんぜん) becomes 完全に when used adverbially and overlaps with "completely." [全然](全然.md) (ぜんぜん) also appears with negatives meaning "not at all," but its wider conversational uses should be learned separately.

## Distinctive meaning

全く can strengthen agreement: 全くそのとおりです (まったくそのとおりです), "That is absolutely right." As a separate exclamation, まったく！ can express exasperation, "Good grief!" Tone and placement distinguish this reaction from the degree adverb inside a sentence. It is not a word that requires a negative every time.
