# 毎月 (まいつき／まいげつ) — every month

## Meaning

毎月 means "every month, monthly": 毎月一回 (once a month), 毎月の給料 (monthly salary), 毎月東京へ行く (I go to Tokyo every month).

## How the kanji combine

[毎](../kanji/毎.md) (every) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month). The usual reading is まいつき, an on + kun mix using the kun'yomi つき; まいげつ with the on'yomi ゲツ is also given in dictionaries, but is less common. Compare 毎年 まいとし／まいねん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 月々 つきづき (month after month), 毎月 is the plain frequency word.
- Related: [毎日](毎日.md), [毎年](毎年.md), 毎週 まいしゅう.

## Distinctive meaning

毎月 simply states a monthly repetition. 月々 stresses each month as a unit, as in 月々の支払い (monthly payments).
