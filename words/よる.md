# よる (よる) - depend on; be due to; be based on

## Meaning

よる is an intransitive godan verb. With Nに, it can mean to depend on N, be caused by N, or be based on N. Several useful constructions grow from these meanings.

- 結果は努力によります。 (けっかはどりょくによります。) - The result depends on the effort.
- 大雨による被害が出ました。 (おおあめによるひがいがでました。) - Heavy rain caused damage.
- 天気予報によると、明日は晴れるそうです。 (てんきよほうによると、あしたははれるそうです。) - According to the weather forecast, it will be sunny tomorrow.

**Nによる + noun** connects a cause or basis to the following noun. **Nによると + report** introduces the source of reported information.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. Dictionary spellings include 依る, 因る, 拠る, and 由る, with preferences for particular senses rather than rigid one-to-one rules. The community source's 因る is retained in provenance; this card teaches よる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頼る](頼る.md) (たよる) can mean relying on a person or support. よる in these examples instead establishes a dependency, cause, or information source.

[寄る](寄る.md) (よる), "stop by; approach," is a different verb.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use bare よる as an English-style preposition meaning "according to." The particles and following form matter. Compare 努力による結果 (どりょくによるけっか), "a result due to effort," with 予報によると, "according to the forecast." The source-list gloss alone does not teach that attachment.
