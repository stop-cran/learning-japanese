# 退院 (たいいん) — discharge from hospital

## Meaning

退院 is leaving hospital as a patient when the stay ends.
With する it becomes 退院する, "be discharged from hospital."

- 母は昨日退院しました。 (はははきのうたいいんしました。) — My mother was discharged from hospital yesterday.
- 来週、退院する予定です。 (らいしゅう、たいいんするよていです。) — I am scheduled to leave hospital next week.

The patient is the subject of 退院する; the Japanese construction is not a passive verb just because English uses "be discharged."

## How the kanji combine

[退](../kanji/退.md) contributes withdrawal or leaving, and [院](../kanji/院.md) names the institution.
In this established word, that institution is a hospital, not any organization containing 院 in its name.
The readings are タイ + イン: keep both い sounds in たいいん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

入院 (にゅういん), "hospitalization," is the paired contrast.
入院する begins the hospital stay; 退院する ends it.
[帰る](帰る.md) (かえる), "go home," describes movement home rather than the patient's hospital status.

## Distinctive meaning

A visitor simply walking out of a hospital is not thereby 退院する.
The word concerns ending a patient's stay, and does not itself assert that every symptom has disappeared or that all treatment has ended.
