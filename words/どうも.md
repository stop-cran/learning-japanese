# どうも (どうも) — thanks; very much

## Meaning

どうも can be a brief "thanks." With ありがとう it strengthens the gratitude: どうもありがとうございます, "Thank you very much." On its own it is abbreviated and can be too brief for a situation requiring a fuller expression of thanks.

- 手伝ってくれて、どうもありがとう。 (てつだってくれて、どうもありがとう。) — Thank you very much for helping.
- どうもすみません。 (どうもすみません。) — I am very sorry.

## How the kanji combine

This word is written in hiragana. There is no kanji combination to analyze. Learn the whole expression and its conversational setting rather than assigning a single English equivalent to each kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ありがとう directly expresses thanks. すみません expresses an apology or gets someone's attention, though it can also accompany gratitude. These expressions are not interchangeable in every situation.

## Distinctive meaning

どうも is not always a thank-you. It can intensify an apology, act as a casual greeting, or mean "somehow" in a statement such as どうも分かりません (どうもわかりません), "Somehow I just don't understand." The surrounding words tell you which function it has. There is no single direct opposite.
