# 係 (かかり) - person in charge; assigned duty

## Meaning

係 names a person or group assigned to a task, or the duty itself. Use noun + の係 to identify the responsibility. In compounds such as 受付係 (うけつけがかり), "reception attendant," かかり becomes がかり.

- 詳しくは係の人に聞いてください。 (くわしくはかかりのひとにきいてください。) - Please ask the person in charge for details.
- 私は掃除の係です。 (わたしはそうじのかかりです。) - I am responsible for the cleaning.

The second example can describe an assigned classroom or household task, not just paid employment.

## How the kanji combine

[係](../kanji/係.md) writes the whole native noun かかり. Although the spelling has no kana ending, it is not read with the on reading ケイ here. 係り is another recorded spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[担当](担当.md) (たんとう) also concerns being assigned responsibility: 受付を担当する, "handle reception." [責任](責任.md) (せきにん) is responsibility or accountability, not automatically the name of the staff member doing the job.

## Distinctive meaning

係 identifies an assigned function. An English translation such as "official" can fit a public-facing role, but does not mean every 係 is a government official or a manager. The task or setting supplies the scope.
