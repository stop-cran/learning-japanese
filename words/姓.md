# 姓 (せい) - surname; family name

## Meaning

姓 is a noun for a surname or family name, not the whole of a person's name.

- 私の姓は田中です。 (わたしのせいはたなかです。) - My surname is Tanaka.
- この欄に姓を書いてください。 (このらんにせいをかいてください。) - Please write your family name in this field.

Use の to connect it with a person: 私の姓 (わたしのせい), "my surname." A form requesting 姓 is asking for the family-name part.

## How the kanji combine

The single [姓](../kanji/姓.md) uses the on reading セイ. There are no separate written components to translate as a compound here. In 姓名 せいめい, "full name," 姓 contributes the family-name part and [名](../kanji/名.md) the personal-name part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

名字 みょうじ and 苗字 みょうじ also mean family name. [名前](名前.md) (なまえ) and [名](名.md) (な) are broader words for a name. 下の名前 したのなまえ, "given name," is a contrast within a person's name, not an antonym meaning nameless.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not equate 姓 with first name or last name purely by position: the intended part is the family name. 姓名 せいめい refers to a full name, so it is not an unrestricted replacement when a form separates the family-name and given-name fields.
