# 卯 (う) — Rabbit zodiac sign

## Meaning

卯 is the **Rabbit or Hare sign**, the fourth of the twelve zodiac signs.
It identifies a place in the cycle, not a living rabbit.

- 私の干支は卯です。 (わたしのえとはうです。) — My zodiac sign is the Rabbit.
- 十二支の四番目は卯です。 (じゅうにしのよんばんめはうです。) — The fourth of the twelve zodiac signs is the Rabbit.

十二支 (じゅうにし) names the twelve-sign set. In the first example,
干支 (えと) has its everyday twelve-year zodiac sense. 干支 can also mean
the sixty-term calendar cycle combining stems and branches, a different
set of labels. The reading かんし is documented for that sixty-term sense,
not as a substitute for えと's twelve-year zodiac sense.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [卯](../kanji/卯.md) writes the whole native sign noun う,
with no okurigana or second character to separate.

Recognize 卯年 (うどし / うさぎどし), **year of the Rabbit**: 卯 identifies
the sign and [年](../kanji/年.md) the year. Learn these complete readings;
the separate noun [年](年.md) has the primary reading とし. The compound's
うさぎどし does not make うさぎ the standalone reading taught for 卯 here.
Daijisen also records 卯年 for a person born in that year.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[兎](兎.md) うさぎ, usually written in kana, is the ordinary animal noun
for rabbit or hare. It names the animal associated with the sign, but is
not an unrestricted substitute for the sign spelling 卯. The sign itself
has no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the spelling tied to the context: 卯 (う) for the sign, and 兎 or
うさぎ for a living rabbit. The animal noun also has an obsolete reading
う restricted to 兎 in JMdict; that does not make the current sign noun
obsolete or erase the difference in meaning.

Older uses of 卯 include an hour around 6 a.m., east, and the second lunar
month. Dictionary hour conventions include both 5–7 a.m. and 6–8 a.m.;
no universal conversion is intended. For ordinary directions, use
[東](東.md) ひがし, east; [時刻](時刻.md) じこく names a time of day.

Do not confuse standalone 卯's old second-lunar-month sense with 卯月
(うづき), the **fourth lunar month**. Lunar month numbers are not simply
Gregorian month numbers; modern [四月](四月.md) しがつ means April.
