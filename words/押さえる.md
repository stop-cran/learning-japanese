# 押さえる (おさえる) - hold down; secure; grasp

## Meaning

押さえる is a transitive ichidan verb. It can mean holding something down
or in place, covering a body part with a hand, or securing something.
It also means grasping an important point.
押える is another dictionary spelling, with the same reading おさえる.

- 風で飛ばないように、紙を手で押さえました。 (かぜでとばないように、かみをてでおさえました。) - I held the paper down with my hand so it would not blow away.
- 痛むお腹を押さえました。 (いたむおなかをおさえました。) - I clutched my aching stomach.
- 会議室を押さえておきました。 (かいぎしつをおさえておきました。) - I secured a meeting room in advance.
- 説明の要点を押さえましょう。 (せつめいのようてんをおさえましょう。) - Let's grasp the main points of the explanation.

The object takes を. Forms include 押さえます, 押さえた, and 押さえない.
The dictionary also includes catching or seizing someone and suppressing
an opposing force; the object determines the interpretation.

## How the kanji combine

[押](../kanji/押.md) carries pushing or pressure; さえる completes
the written native verb in this spelling.
[押す](押す.md) (おす) is a related but separate verb, not its conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[掴む](掴む.md) (つかむ) overlaps in grasping physically or understanding.
[予約](予約.md) (よやく), with する, overlaps with securing a room in advance.
[持つ](持つ.md) (もつ) is broader holding or possessing.
These comparisons do not license substitution in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

抑える, also おさえる, is useful for controlling feelings, spending,
or a rise in something. This is not an absolute physical-versus-mental split:
押さえる itself has nonphysical understanding, reservation, and suppression uses.
JMdict cross-references the suppression senses of the two spellings.
Learn 要点を押さえる and 費用を抑える (ひようをおさえる), "keep costs down,"
as complete combinations rather than choosing a character from English alone.
