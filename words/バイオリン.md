# バイオリン - violin

## Meaning

バイオリン is a violin. It is a noun naming the instrument, not the action of playing it.

- 妹はバイオリンを弾きます。 (いもうとはバイオリンをひきます。) - My younger sister plays the violin.
- 毎週、バイオリンを習っています。 (まいしゅう、バイオリンをならっています。) - I take violin lessons every week.

Use 弾く (ひく) for playing this instrument, and [習う](習う.md) (ならう) for learning it through instruction. The instrument takes を in both examples.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to "violin," with no kanji components. ヴァイオリン is another dictionary-supported spelling of the same word. The spelling variation does not identify a separate instrument or a different musical style.

## Synonyms and antonyms

楽器 (がっき) means musical instrument, the broader category. A ビオラ is a viola and a チェロ is a cello; these are related instruments, not synonyms for バイオリン. Instrument names do not need an antonym pair.

## Distinctive meaning

Playing an instrument does not use the same verb in every case: the violin takes 弾く, not a generic Japanese equivalent of English "play" used for games. バイオリンの音 (バイオリンのおと), "the sound of a violin," refers to its sound rather than to a person playing it.
