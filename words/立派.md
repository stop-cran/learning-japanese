# 立派 (りっぱ) - splendid, admirable

## Meaning

立派 praises something as impressive, fine, or worthy of respect. It is a na-adjective: use 立派な before a noun and 立派に before a suitable verb.

- 立派な建物ですね。 (りっぱなたてものですね。) - What an impressive building.
- 最後まで立派に役目を果たしました。 (さいごまでりっぱにやくめをはたしました。) - They fulfilled their role admirably to the end.

The subject of the second sentence is understood from context.

## How the kanji combine

[立](../kanji/立.md) suggests standing, and 派 can suggest a group or school. These associations do not literally define the adjective. Learn the established on-reading compound りっぱ, including its small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すばらしい overlaps with "wonderful" or "splendid." 偉い (えらい) can praise a person, but also concerns rank or importance. No single opposite fits every use of 立派.

## Distinctive meaning

立派 can praise a building's impressive appearance or a person's conduct and achievement. It is not limited to physical beauty, wealth, or social rank. A modest-looking act may still be 立派 because it is commendable.
