# 封筒 (ふうとう) - envelope

## Meaning

封筒 is an envelope used to enclose a letter, documents, or other flat contents. It names the container, not what is written inside.

手紙を封筒に入れました。 (てがみをふうとうにいれました。) - I put the letter in an envelope.

封筒に住所を書いてください。 (ふうとうにじゅうしょをかいてください。) - Please write the address on the envelope.

In both examples, に marks where something is put or written.

## How the kanji combine

封 concerns sealing or enclosing; 筒 can name a tube or container. Their on readings form ふうとう. The established word is an envelope, not a requirement that its shape be cylindrical.

## Synonyms and antonyms

手紙 (てがみ) is a letter. はがき is a postcard, normally sent without an envelope. 袋 (ふくろ) means a bag or sack more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

An empty or unsealed envelope is still a 封筒. Flat empty envelopes are commonly counted with 枚 (まい): 封筒を三枚 (ふうとうをさんまい), "three envelopes." The word does not indicate whether postage has already been attached.
