# 合同 (ごうどう) - combination; joint

## Meaning

合同 concerns previously separate things combining or joining.
It is a noun and する-verb. 合同で describes doing something jointly;
合同の and compounds such as 合同練習 (ごうどうれんしゅう),
"joint practice," describe the combined arrangement.

- 二つの会社が合同で説明会を開きました。 (ふたつのかいしゃがごうどうでせつめいかいをひらきました。) - Two companies held a joint information session.
- 三つの団体が合同して大会を開きます。 (みっつのだんたいがごうどうしてたいかいをひらきます。) - Three organizations will join together to hold an event.

An additional mathematical sense is congruence: figures have the same
shape and size and can be made to coincide.
この二つの三角形は合同です (このふたつのさんかくけいはごうどうです),
"These two triangles are congruent."

## How the kanji combine

[合](../kanji/合.md) contributes joining or fitting together, and
[同](../kanji/同.md) contributes sameness. The related ideas support
combining things or their matching relationship. The on readings are ゴウ + ドウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[共同](共同.md) (きょうどう) concerns joint activity or shared use.
[組み合わせ](組み合わせ.md) (くみあわせ) concerns a combination or pairing.
[合流](合流.md) (ごうりゅう) describes streams, people, or groups coming
together. These words overlap but foreground different arrangements.

## Distinctive meaning

A joint event does not by itself mean that the participating companies
have legally merged. Conversely, the source's incorporation or merger
glosses are not restricted to merely cooperating while staying separate.
Mathematical congruence is a separate technical use, not just agreement
with someone's opinion. The joint and mathematical senses are additional
teaching, not separate N2 assignments.
