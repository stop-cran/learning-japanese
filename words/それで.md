# それで (それで) — so; because of that

## Meaning

それで connects an earlier situation to its consequence: "so," "therefore," or "because of that." It can begin a new sentence.

電車が止まりました。それで、バスで帰りました。 (でんしゃがとまりました。それで、バスでかえりました。) — The trains stopped. So I went home by bus.

## How the kanji combine

The ordinary spelling is hiragana. それ points back to what was just mentioned, and で contributes the connection. The kanji spelling 其れで is recognized but rare; it is not needed here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

だから also introduces a reasoned consequence, and そのため means "for that reason." そして often simply adds the next event rather than highlighting a causal connection. それでも, "even so," expresses a different relationship, not a spelling variant.

## Distinctive meaning

In conversation, それで？ can mean "And then?" or "So what happened?" It invites the speaker to continue: それで、どうなりましたか (それで、どうなりましたか), "And what happened after that?" The dictionary therefore includes both consequence and continuation uses. Context decides whether English "because of that" or "and then" fits.
