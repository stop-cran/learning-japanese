# 複数 (ふくすう) - multiple; plural

## Meaning

複数 means more than one or multiple, and also names grammatical
plural number. It is a noun; use 複数の before another noun,
not an ordinary na-adjective pattern.

- 複数の会社に問い合わせました。 (ふくすうのかいしゃにといあわせました。) - I contacted more than one company.
- この英語の名詞を複数にしてください。 (このえいごのめいしをふくすうにしてください。) - Please put this English noun into the plural.

The first concerns how many companies; the second concerns a
grammatical form.

## How the kanji combine

[複](../kanji/複.md) contributes multiplicity, and
[数](../kanji/数.md) number. The first character modifies the
number idea: more than a single one.
The on readings are フク + スウ, with a long vowel in すう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[単数](単数.md) (たんすう), singular number or a single item,
is the useful opposite. 多数 (たすう) means a large number;
[多い](多い.md) (おおい) means many or much.
Those are not exact replacements: two items can already be 複数.

## Distinctive meaning

複数 does not establish a large quantity, different kinds of things,
or a particular exact count.
Nor does the grammatical term mean that every Japanese noun
requires an English-style plural ending.
Use the spelling 複数; the pinned dictionary's search-only 復数
form is not a recommended spelling alternative.
