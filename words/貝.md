# 貝 (かい) - shellfish; seashell

## Meaning

貝 can name shellfish or their shells. Context distinguishes the animal from a shell collected on a beach.
Here, "shellfish" refers to molluscs such as clams and sea snails, not to shrimp or crabs.

- 夕食に貝を食べました。 (ゆうしょくにかいをたべました。) - I ate shellfish for dinner.
- 海岸で白い貝を拾いました。 (かいがんでしろいかいをひろいました。) - I picked up a white seashell on the shore.

## How the kanji combine

This single-kanji native noun uses the kun reading かい of [貝](../kanji/貝.md).
There is no second character to supply a modifier or object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

貝殻 (かいがら) specifies a shell rather than the living animal; it overlaps with the shell sense of 貝.
貝類 (かいるい) is a category word for shellfish.
These are related words, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

The kanji also writes a different noun, 貝 ばい, for the Japanese Babylon sea snail, usually written バイ.
That specific name does not make ばい an alternative general reading of this かい article.
Nor does the broad English label "shellfish" make every animal included in that English category a 貝 in Japanese.

[皮](皮.md) かわ names an outer covering more generally, not specifically a shellfish.
The ばか family represented by [馬鹿](馬鹿.md) also has a usually-kana abbreviation for a particular clam;
that is separate from the "fool" meaning taught on that page.
