# ずらす - shift; reschedule

## Meaning

ずらす means to shift something's position or timing. It is transitive:
the object being shifted takes を. It often concerns an adjustment away
from a previous or aligned position, not just any journey to another place.

- 机を少し右にずらしてください。 (つくえをすこしみぎにずらしてください。) - Please shift the desk a little to the right.
- 会議を一時間早くずらしました。 (かいぎをいちじかんはやくずらしました。) - We moved the meeting one hour earlier.
- 昼休みの時間をずらしています。 (ひるやすみのじかんをずらしています。) - We stagger our lunch-break times.

It is a godan verb: ずらします, ずらして, ずらさない.
The scheduling sense includes moving earlier, moving later, and staggering;
it does not mean only postponing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずれる](ずれる.md) is intransitive: something shifts or is out of alignment.
Compare 机をずらす, shift the desk, with 机がずれる, the desk shifts.
[動かす](動かす.md) is a broader word for moving something.
[スライド](スライド.md) can also concern sliding or shifting a schedule.

## Distinctive meaning

[延期](延期.md) specifically postpones something.
[伸ばす](伸ばす.md), also written 延ばす in its postponement use,
can overlap when an event moves later. Neither explains the earlier move
in the second example. State 早く, 遅く (おそく), or the new time rather
than assuming that ずらす itself gives a direction.
