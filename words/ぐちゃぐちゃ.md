# ぐちゃぐちゃ — soggy and pulpy; messy

## Meaning

ぐちゃぐちゃ describes something crushed, pulpy, or badly disordered.

- 雨にぬれた紙袋の中で、パンがぐちゃぐちゃになっていた。
  — The bread had turned into a soggy mess inside the rain-soaked paper bag.
- 何度も書き直したノートのページが、消し跡でぐちゃぐちゃになった。
  — The notebook page became a mess from all the erasing and rewriting.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぐちゃぐちゃ emphasizes a crushed, pulpy, or badly muddled condition. ごちゃごちゃ more often
describes things jumbled together; ぐしゃぐしゃ often describes wrinkling or crumpling.

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding noun and cause distinguish a pulpy texture from general disorder. Do not treat
ぐちゃぐちゃ and ごちゃごちゃ as interchangeable in every context.
