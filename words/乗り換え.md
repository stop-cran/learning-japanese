# 乗り換え (のりかえ) - transfer; changeover

## Meaning

乗り換え is a transfer or connection between trains, buses, or other vehicles. **乗換** and **乗換え** have the same reading and meaning; recognize these shorter spellings on travel information.

- 次の駅で乗り換えをします。 (つぎのえきでのりかえをします。) - I will transfer at the next station.
- この電車なら乗り換えなしで行けます。 (このでんしゃならのりかえなしでいけます。) - With this train, you can get there without transferring.

乗り換え can also describe switching to a different system, method, or provider, not physically boarding another vehicle. That is an additional use of the same word, not another train connection.

## How the kanji combine

[乗](../kanji/乗.md) contributes riding or getting aboard, and [換](../kanji/換.md) contributes exchanging or replacing. The native compound is related to [乗り換える](乗り換える.md) (のりかえる), "transfer between vehicles; switch." The noun ends in え, while the verb ends in える.

## Synonyms and antonyms

Compare 次の駅で乗り換える, "transfer at the next station," with 次の駅で乗り換えをする, the noun-plus-する construction. [スイッチ](スイッチ.md) also has a switching/changeover use, though its physical-device sense is a different use. 乗り換えなし means "without a transfer."

## Distinctive meaning

A transfer concerns changing vehicles or services, not merely getting on one for the first time. Keep the noun and verb forms distinct: 乗り換えます is the polite form of 乗り換える, while 乗り換えが必要です (のりかえがひつようです), "a transfer is necessary," uses the noun with が.
