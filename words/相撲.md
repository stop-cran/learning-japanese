# 相撲 (すもう) - sumo wrestling

## Meaning

相撲 is sumo, the wrestling sport. 相撲を取る (すもうをとる) means to
wrestle a sumo bout; 相撲を見る (すもうをみる) means to watch it.

- 昨日、相撲を見に行きました。 (きのう、すもうをみにいきました。) - Yesterday I went to watch sumo.
- 子どもたちが相撲を取っています。 (こどもたちがすもうをとっています。) - The children are wrestling sumo.

The dictionary also records an abbreviated use for a sumo wrestler.
相撲取り (すもうとり) and 力士 (りきし) explicitly identify the wrestler.

## How the kanji combine

[相](../kanji/相.md) has a mutual or facing-one-another association;
撲 has a striking association. These can help remember the spelling,
but do not establish the word's historical origin.
Learn すもう as a whole-word irregular reading, not 相 = す and 撲 = もう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

レスリング names wrestling as a sport, but is not an unrestricted
replacement for this particular Japanese sport. 力士 names a participant
rather than the sporting activity.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling 角力 is also recorded, with a rare-kanji flag.
The old reading すまい is not the main modern reading taught here and
should not be confused with [住まい](住まい.md), a dwelling.
Keep the long ending of すもう.
