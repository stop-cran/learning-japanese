# 芝居 (しばい) - play; drama

## Meaning

芝居 is a noun for a play or theatrical drama. Use 芝居を見る (しばいをみる) for watching
a play, and 芝居をする (しばいをする) for performing or acting.
The surrounding verb distinguishes the audience's activity from the performer's.

- 週末に友達と芝居を見ました。 (しゅうまつにともだちとしばいをみました。) - I saw a play with a friend at the weekend.
- 学生たちが舞台で芝居をしています。 (がくせいたちがぶたいでしばいをしています。) - The students are performing a play on stage.

芝居の台詞 (しばいのせりふ) means "the lines of a play."
The word is not the general verb "play" used for children playing a game.

## How the kanji combine

芝 is しば, "grass; turf," and [居](../kanji/居.md) is associated with being or staying,
as in いる. The established word combines native readings as しばい.
Historically, 芝居 referred to spectators' seating on grass; its meaning later widened to theatre
and drama ([historical explanation](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%8A%9D%E5%B1%85-74675)).
It does not mean sitting on grass in the modern examples above.

## Synonyms and antonyms

演劇 (えんげき) also names theatre or drama.
[演技](演技.md) (えんぎ) focuses on acting or performance, which is part of presenting a play.
Neither names an opposite of 芝居.

## Distinctive meaning

English "drama" can refer to an upsetting personal situation, but 芝居 here concerns theatrical
performance. Choose a complete phrase such as 芝居を見る rather than carrying over every English
use of "play" or "drama." The examples also show that the same noun can occur in descriptions
of watching and performing.
