# ごぼごぼ — water gurgling or glugging

## Meaning

A bubbling, gurgling sound made by liquid and air moving together.

- 排水口（はいすいこう）から、ごぼごぼという音がした。 — A gurgling sound came from the drain.
- 瓶（びん）から水がごぼごぼと出てきた。 — Water came glugging out of the bottle.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぶくぶく](ぶくぶく.md) and [ぷくぷく](ぷくぷく.md) overlap in bubbles. [ぐつぐつ](ぐつぐつ.md) commonly concerns simmering; ごぼごぼ need not involve heating.

## Distinctive meaning

The sound can suggest bubbles or interrupted flow without proving that a drain is blocked. The dictionary also records forms such as こぽこぽ; this card teaches one stable written form, not a rule for inventing variants.
