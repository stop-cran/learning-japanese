# 放送 (ほうそう) — broadcast

## Meaning

放送 means broadcasting, a broadcast, or an announcement. Add する for the verb "broadcast":
the material broadcast can take を.

- このラジオ局はニュースを放送しています。 (このラジオきょくはニュースをほうそうしています。) — This radio station broadcasts news.
- 学校の放送が聞こえます。 (がっこうのほうそうがきこえます。) — I can hear the school's announcement.

The second example concerns an announcement over the school's audio system, not necessarily a radio or television program.

## How the kanji combine

[放](../kanji/放.md) suggests sending something out or emitting it, and [送](../kanji/送.md) contributes sending or transmitting.
The related actions reinforce the idea of broadcasting outward, rather than naming an object followed by an action.
The on readings are ホウ + ソウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[送る](送る.md) (おくる) is the broader verb "send" or "transmit."
メールを送る (メールをおくる) means "send an email"; that ordinary private message is not thereby a 放送.
放送する identifies a broadcast or announcement, rather than every act of sending information.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not limit 放送 to television: the noun also covers radio broadcasts and announcements over an audio system.
It can name the broadcast itself, as in 放送を聞く (ほうそうをきく), "listen to a broadcast,"
or the activity through 放送する. The sentence tells you which role it has.
