# これら (これら) - these

## Meaning

これら is a demonstrative pronoun meaning "these." It explicitly groups several things near the speaker or several items being presented in the discussion. It can stand alone as a noun phrase; before another noun, use これらの.

- これらはすべて手作りです。 (これらはすべててづくりです。) - These are all handmade.
- これらの資料を読んでください。 (これらのしりょうをよんでください。) - Please read these materials.

The first sentence uses これら itself as the topic. The second uses これらの to identify which materials.

## How the kanji combine

This usual kana spelling combines [これ](これ.md), "this," with the grouping suffix ら. The dictionary records kanji spellings such as これ等, but there are no written kanji to analyze in this card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

これ can refer to contextually understood material without marking number. これら makes the set explicit rather than changing every Japanese demonstrative into a compulsory singular/plural pair.

それら means "those," corresponding to [それ](それ.md). This is a contrast in reference, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "these materials" does not justify dropping の: use これらの資料, not これら資料. Also distinguish この資料 (このしりょう), "this material/these materials": この directly modifies a noun, while これら behaves as a pronoun. Japanese often leaves number to context; use これら when identifying the group is helpful.
