# ほろほろ — tenderly crumbly; softly falling

## Meaning

ほろほろ describes something tender breaking apart easily, such as well-cooked food. It can
also describe tears or petals falling quietly, one after another.

- 煮込んだ肉が、箸で触れるとほろほろと崩れた。
  — The braised meat crumbled at the touch of my chopsticks.
- 風が吹くと、花びらがほろほろと舞い落ちた。
  — When the wind blew, petals drifted down one after another.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぼろぼろ](ぼろぼろ.md) can also describe something falling apart, but often suggests damage,
wear or scattered pieces rather than tender food. For a person, ほろほろ may instead describe
tears falling; the surrounding scene determines which use is intended.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse ほろほろ with [ぽろぽろ](ぽろぽろ.md): both can describe falling drops, but
ぽろぽろ more strongly presents separate, often larger drops, while ほろほろ can suggest a
quiet, gentle fall. JMdict also records a bird sound and a regional dish; those are not the
focus of these examples.
