# 汚染 (おせん) - pollution; contamination

## Meaning

汚染 is pollution or contamination. It is a noun and a する verb, used for an
environment or substance affected by pollutants, germs, or other contaminants.
In a transitive construction, the affected thing takes を.

- 工場の排水が川を汚染しています。 (こうじょうのはいすいがかわをおせんしています。) - Wastewater from the factory is polluting the river.
- この水は細菌に汚染されています。 (このみずはさいきんにおせんされています。) - This water is contaminated with bacteria.

The second example is passive: 汚染される means "be contaminated." The dictionary
also recognizes intransitive use of 汚染する.

## How the kanji combine

汚 contributes being dirty or defiled; 染 contributes staining or permeation.
Their meanings combine into contamination rather than decorative coloring.
The on readings are オ + セン; the first vowel is short.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[汚れる](汚れる.md) (よごれる) is a broader everyday verb for becoming dirty, such
as getting mud on clothes. 公害 (こうがい) concerns public or environmental harm
from pollution and similar causes, rather than every contaminated object.
Cleaning or purification can counter contamination, but there is no single opposite
that suits every context.

## Distinctive meaning

汚染 does not require visible dirt. The water example can describe water that looks
clear. Conversely, a harmless stain on a sleeve need not be described as 汚染.
Choose the word for the contamination being discussed, not merely because something
looks untidy.
