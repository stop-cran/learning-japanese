# 材木 (ざいもく) - lumber; timber

## Meaning

材木 is wood used as material for building or making things: lumber or timber.

- 家を建てるために材木を買いました。 (いえをたてるためにざいもくをかいました。) - I bought timber to build a house.
- 倉庫に材木が積んであります。 (そうこにざいもくがつんであります。) - There is lumber stacked in the warehouse.

The word presents the wood as usable material, not simply a living tree.

## How the kanji combine

[材](../kanji/材.md) contributes material or timber, while [木](../kanji/木.md) contributes wood. The combination specifies wooden material. The on readings are ザイ + モク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

木材 (もくざい) also means wood material or timber; the order of both characters and readings is reversed. [木](木.md) (き) is broader tree/wood, and [材料](材料.md) (ざいりょう) means materials or ingredients without restricting them to wood.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read 材木 as もくざい. Both 材木 and 木材 concern wooden material, but neither word specifies the tree species, exact dimensions, or whether a particular piece is suitable for a construction project.
