# だいぶ — considerably

## Meaning

だいぶ means "considerably," "quite a lot," or "to a substantial degree." It is an adverb, often describing a noticeable change, difference, or amount.

風邪はだいぶよくなりました。 (かぜはだいぶよくなりました。) — My cold has improved considerably.

駅まではまだだいぶあります。 (えきまではまだだいぶあります。) — It is still quite a way to the station.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana; the kanji spelling is 大分. It has a Sino-Japanese form, also recorded as だいぶん. Do not confuse this word with the place name 大分 (おおいた).

## Synonyms and antonyms

かなり also means "considerably" and overlaps strongly. 少し (すこし), "a little," provides a useful degree contrast. とても is a more general intensifier.

## Distinctive meaning

だいぶ does not mean "completely." Someone who is だいぶよくなった has improved a lot but may not be fully recovered. It often invites comparison with an earlier state or an expected amount, without supplying a precise measurement.
