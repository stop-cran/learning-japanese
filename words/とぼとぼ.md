# とぼとぼ — trudging with heavy steps

## Meaning

とぼとぼ describes slow, heavy walking, commonly suggesting tiredness, discouragement, or dejection.

- 試合に負けて、とぼとぼ家に帰った。 — After losing the game, I trudged home. (試合: しあい.)
- 長い一日の終わりに、父はとぼとぼと坂を上った。 — At the end of a long day, my father trudged up the slope. (坂: さか.)

It modifies a walking or travelling verb, directly or with と: とぼとぼ歩く, とぼとぼと帰る.

## How the kanji combine

This is a repeated kana mimetic of gait and mood, not a kanji compound or a precise transcription of footsteps.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[のろのろ](のろのろ.md) emphasizes slowness and sluggishness, including vehicles or work. とぼとぼ particularly pictures a person's steps. [すたすた](すたすた.md), brisk walking, makes a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

"Slowly" alone loses the heavy, dispirited impression. A relaxed sightseeing walk is more naturally described with [のんびり](のんびり.md) or [ぶらぶら](ぶらぶら.md), not automatically とぼとぼ. Context can suggest the reason for the gait, but the word does not identify a specific emotion or medical cause.
