# 樹脂 (じゅし) - resin

## Meaning

樹脂 means **resin**. It can concern natural resin, such as that from a pine tree, or synthetic resin in a manufactured material. 天然樹脂 (てんねんじゅし) is natural resin; 合成樹脂 (ごうせいじゅし) is synthetic resin.

- 松から樹脂が出ています。 (まつからじゅしがでています。) - Resin is coming out of the pine tree.
- これは合成樹脂でできています。 (これはごうせいじゅしでできています。) - This is made of synthetic resin.

The first example concerns a tree's secretion. In the second, で marks the material; the word does not say that it was collected from a tree.

## How the kanji combine

[樹](../kanji/樹.md) supplies ジュ and the tree association; [脂](../kanji/脂.md) supplies シ and the resin association in this compound. The relation is modifier plus head: "tree resin" is a useful cue for the natural-material use. It is not a definition restricting all modern 樹脂 to tree products.

## Synonyms and antonyms

やに names tree resin in one dictionary sense, but also has tobacco-tar and eye-discharge senses. The spelling 脂 can write やに; the existing [脂](脂.md) article instead teaches あぶら, fat or grease. Learn the reading with the lexical item, not from the spelling alone.

[プラスチック](プラスチック.md), plastic, overlaps with synthetic-resin usage. [ビニール](ビニール.md) has vinyl and broader plastic-material uses. [ナイロン](ナイロン.md) names a specific synthetic material used in fibers and other products; nylon molding material can be a synthetic resin. These labels do not mean that every resin is vinyl or nylon. There is no general material antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

樹脂 is useful in material descriptions; 樹脂製 (じゅしせい) means "made of resin." The bare noun does not guarantee hardness, transparency, flexibility or heat resistance. Nor does it identify every tree fluid as the same substance: 樹液 (じゅえき) is the sap word, while 樹脂 specifically names resin.
