# 住まい (すまい) - dwelling; residence

## Meaning

住まい is a dwelling, home, or place of residence. Depending on the question,
it can also identify where someone lives rather than the building alone.

- 新しい住まいを探しています。 (あたらしいすまいをさがしています。) - I am looking for a new place to live.
- お住まいはどちらですか。 (おすまいはどちらですか。) - Where do you live?

The second uses polite お住まい. It does not necessarily request a complete
postal address. In combinations such as 一人住まい (ひとりずまい), living
alone, the ending is ずまい and concerns a way of living.

## How the kanji combine

[住](../kanji/住.md) contributes residing. The noun is associated with
住まう (すまう), dwell; do not read the word as ジュウまい.
The dictionary also lists 住い and 住居 for すまい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[住居](住居.md), with its primary reading じゅうきょ, also means a dwelling
or residence. Its article is not a reason to pronounce every 住まい
じゅうきょ. [住宅](住宅.md) emphasizes housing or residential buildings;
[家](家.md) can mean a house or home more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

[住所](住所.md) (じゅうしょ) specifically names an address. A request for
お住まい may ask only for an area, so supply the detail the situation requires.
住まい is a noun, unlike [住む](住む.md), live in a place.
Neither a particular building type nor home ownership is built into it.
