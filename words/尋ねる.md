# 尋ねる (たずねる) - ask; inquire

## Meaning

尋ねる means to ask for information or inquire. It is a transitive ichidan verb: 尋ねます, 尋ねて, 尋ねた and 尋ねない.

- 駅員に道を尋ねました。 (えきいんにみちをたずねました。) - I asked a station employee for directions.
- 先生に試験がいつか尋ねました。 (せんせいにしけんがいつかたずねました。) - I asked the teacher when the examination was.

に marks the person asked. を can mark the information sought; an embedded question with か can also follow the person.

## How the kanji combine

尋 is read たず, with ねる as okurigana. Remove only る to form the ichidan stem 尋ね.

## Synonyms and antonyms

聞く (きく) is another ordinary way to say "ask," as well as "hear; listen." 答える (こたえる), "answer," names the other side of the exchange.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish 尋ねる, "ask," from 訪ねる, "visit." They share the reading たずねる but are not interchangeable spellings in these examples.
