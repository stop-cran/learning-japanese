# 大陸 (たいりく) - continent; mainland

## Meaning

大陸 is a continent: a major landmass. It is a noun, and names can be attached directly, as in アフリカ大陸 (アフリカたいりく), "the African continent." 大陸の modifies another noun.

- 地図でアフリカ大陸を探してください。 (ちずでアフリカたいりくをさがしてください。) - Please find the African continent on the map.
- 船で大陸から島へ渡りました。 (ふねでたいりくからしまへわたりました。) - We crossed from the mainland to the island by boat.

In a suitable regional context, 大陸 can mean the Asian mainland, especially mainland China, or continental Europe. The intended region is not fixed by the noun alone.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes large size, while 陸 means land. 大 modifies 陸: a great landmass. The reading is タイ + リク, with たい rather than だい in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[陸](陸.md) (りく) is land as opposed to water, without requiring continental scale. [島](島.md) (しま) is an island. That contrast is useful geographically, but not every distinction is determined by size alone.

## Distinctive meaning

大陸 names a geographical landmass, not a country or government. One continent can contain many countries. In translations using "the mainland," identify whose geographical perspective is being used.
