# 足 (あし) — foot, leg

## Meaning

足 is the foot and the leg: 足が痛い (my foot hurts), 足が速い あしがはやい (be fast on one's feet), 足を組む (cross one's legs), 足音 あしおと (footsteps).
It is also the means of travel in 足がない (have no transport), and a table's leg is 足 as well.

## How the kanji combine

A single kanji, [足](../kanji/足.md), with the kun reading あし. The on reading ソク appears in 満足 まんぞく, 遠足 えんそく and the shoe counter 一足 いっそく.
The same kanji is read たりる in 足りる (be enough) and たす in 足す (add).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 脚 あし／きゃく (leg; a table's legs or a person's long legs), 足首 あしくび (ankle).
- Antonym: [手](手.md) て (hand).

## Distinctive meaning

足 covers both "foot" and "leg" in everyday speech. 脚 is used for tables, chairs and for a slim, long leg, as in 脚が長い.
