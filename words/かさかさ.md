# かさかさ — dry and rough; rustling

## Meaning

Dry, rough, moisture-deprived texture, particularly skin; also a light, dry rustling sound, such as paper or dead leaves.

- 冬は手がかさかさするので、クリームを塗る（ぬる）。 — My hands get dry and rough in winter, so I put cream on them.
- 風で枯れ葉（かれは）がかさかさと鳴っている。 — The dead leaves are rustling in the wind.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぱさぱさ](ぱさぱさ.md) often concerns dry food or hair. [ざらざら](ざらざら.md) is roughness without necessarily implying dryness. [しっとり](しっとり.md) contrasts with moisture-deprived skin.

## Distinctive meaning

かさかさする and かさかさの肌（はだ）describe a condition; かさかさと鳴る（なる）describes sound. Neither use automatically implies that something is brittle enough to snap. “Dry” here is a sensory description, not a claim that a particular skin condition has been diagnosed.
