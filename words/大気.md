# 大気 (たいき) - atmosphere; air

## Meaning

大気 names the atmosphere or air, especially when discussing the environment,
weather, or a planet's surrounding gases. It is a noun: 地球の大気
(ちきゅうのたいき) is "Earth's atmosphere."

- 地球の大気には酸素が含まれています。 (ちきゅうのたいきにはさんそがふくまれています。) - Earth's atmosphere contains oxygen.
- 今日は大気の状態が不安定です。 (きょうはたいきのじょうたいがふあんていです。) - Atmospheric conditions are unstable today.

The second sentence concerns weather conditions, not an uncomfortable social mood.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes "large; great," and [気](../kanji/気.md)
contributes air or vapor here. Together they suggest the broad body of air
around us. The on readings are たい + き. 気 does not mean personal
feelings in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[空気](空気.md) (くうき) also means air and works in everyday expressions
about a room or breathing. It overlaps with 大気 but also has figurative
social uses. [雰囲気](雰囲気.md) (ふんいき), "atmosphere; mood," suits the feeling of
a place. A pleasant cafe's "atmosphere" is not its 大気 in that sense.

## Distinctive meaning

大気汚染 (たいきおせん), "air pollution," shows the environmental use.
The dictionary also records a dated adjective meaning "magnanimous," but that
is not the ordinary atmospheric noun taught here.
Use the physical meaning to interpret weather and environmental reports,
without assuming every English occurrence of "atmosphere" has the same Japanese
translation.
