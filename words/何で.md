# 何で (なんで) - why; by what means

## Meaning

何で asks "why?" or "what for?" It is often written なんで in this use.
The same form can also ask "by what means?" Context separates the questions.

- 何で昨日休んだんですか。 (なんできのうやすんだんですか。) - Why were you absent yesterday?
- A: 駅まで何で行きますか。 (えきまでなんでいきますか。) - How will you get to the station?
  B: バスで行きます。 (バスでいきます。) - By bus.

The reply in the exchange establishes that the question is about transport,
not the reason for going to the station.

## How the kanji combine

[何](../kanji/何.md) contributes the question "what," followed by で.
For the means reading, this connects to the ordinary で of an instrument
or method. Learn the reason-question use as なんで, not by translating
each written part in isolation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[なぜ](なぜ.md) and [どうして](どうして.md) also ask for a reason.
なぜ can help make a reason-question clear where 何で might be understood
as asking about means.

## Distinctive meaning

なんで？ alone can sound surprised or challenging, depending on tone and
context. It is not inherently an accusation, but adding a polite ending
does not remove every implication of questioning someone's choice.
The reading なにで can also make an instrument question explicit; it is
not a replacement for なんで in the reason examples here.
