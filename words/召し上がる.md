# 召し上がる (めしあがる) — eat or drink honorifically

## Meaning

召し上がる is the respectful form of 食べる, "eat," and 飲む, "drink." It raises the person doing the eating or drinking, so use it for a guest, teacher, customer, or another person being treated respectfully.

先生は何を召し上がりますか。（せんせいはなにをめしあがりますか。） — What will you have, teacher?

どうぞ召し上がってください。（どうぞめしあがってください。） — Please help yourself.

## How the kanji combine

召し combines with [上](../kanji/上.md)がる in this established native honorific verb. Do not interpret it literally as moving upward. It is godan: 召し上がります and 召し上がって.

## Synonyms and antonyms

食べる and 飲む are neutral. いただく is a humble counterpart used for one's own eating or drinking in appropriate contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

This is more than simply adding polite ます. 食べます is polite toward the listener; 召し上がります also honors the eater. Do not use it to elevate your own action when speaking to a guest.
