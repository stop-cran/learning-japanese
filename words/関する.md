# 関する (かんする) - concern; relate to

## Meaning

関する is a special する verb meaning "concern; relate to." Two especially useful patterns are **Nに関する + noun**, "a noun concerning N," and **Nに関して + clause**, "regarding N."

- 日本の歴史に関する本を読みました。 (にほんのれきしにかんするほんをよみました。) - I read a book about Japanese history.
- この問題に関して質問があります。 (このもんだいにかんしてしつもんがあります。) - I have a question concerning this issue.

に marks the subject matter. The book takes を because it is the object of 読みました, not the object of 関する.

## How the kanji combine

[関](../kanji/関.md) contributes connection or involvement; する makes the verb. There is one kanji followed by a verbal ending, rather than a two-kanji compound. Learn 関して (かんして) as the te-form used in the second pattern.

## Synonyms and antonyms

Nについて also means "about N." Before a noun, use Nについての本, compared with Nに関する本. [関係](関係.md) (かんけい) names a relationship or connection and is useful for related ideas.

## Distinctive meaning

English "concern" can mean "make someone worried"; 関する here means "have to do with," not "worry." It works well for specifying the scope of a document, question, or discussion. Keep the に: the English phrase "a book concerning history" does not justify 歴史を関する本.
