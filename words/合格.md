# 合格 (ごうかく) - passing; qualification

## Meaning

合格 is passing an examination or meeting a required standard.
合格する is intransitive: 試験に合格する (しけんにごうかくする) means "pass an exam."
Use に, not a direct-object を, for the examination or inspection passed.

- 日本語の試験に合格しました。 (にほんごのしけんにごうかくしました。) - I passed the Japanese exam.
- この製品は安全検査に合格しています。 (このせいひんはあんぜんけんさにごうかくしています。) - This product has passed the safety inspection.

合格発表 (ごうかくはっぴょう) is the announcement of successful candidates.
合格した人 (ごうかくしたひと) means "a person who passed."

## How the kanji combine

[合](../kanji/合.md) contributes fitting or matching, and [格](../kanji/格.md) a standard or qualification.
Together they suggest fitting the required standard.
The on-readings are ゴウ + カク; keep the long vowel in ごう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[成功](成功.md) (せいこう) is success more generally.
合格 specifies success against an examination or qualifying standard.
不合格 (ふごうかく), "failure to pass," is the direct result contrast.
[優勝](優勝.md) (ゆうしょう) means winning a championship, not merely qualifying.

## Distinctive meaning

Passing does not say that someone earned the highest score. Several or many candidates may all 合格する.
Likewise, a product can pass an inspection without being the best product available.
The relevant requirement, rather than superiority over everyone else, is central to this word.
