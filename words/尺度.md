# 尺度 (しゃくど) — yardstick; criterion; scale of measurement

## Meaning

尺度 is a measure used to judge or compare things. The examples below illustrate this figurative sense. Dictionaries also list length or size and a measuring rule or scale. For the ruler on your desk, say 物差し (ものさし) or 定規 (じょうぎ).

- 成功を測る尺度は一つではない。 (せいこうをはかるしゃくどはひとつではない。) — There is more than one yardstick for measuring success.
- 二つの作品を同じ尺度で評価した。 (ふたつのさくひんをおなじしゃくどでひょうかした。) — We assessed the two works by the same standard.

## How the kanji combine

[尺](../kanji/尺.md) (しゃく) occurs in measurements of length; [度](../kanji/度.md) (ど) can express degree. The comparison may be figurative, so 尺度 does not prescribe a unit such as centimeters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[物差し](物差し.md) (ものさし) can be a physical ruler or a figurative yardstick. [基準](基準.md) (きじゅん) means the standard you judge by. A passing score might be a 基準 for admission; the 尺度 is what you measure performance against. Neither use requires a ruler on your desk.

## Distinctive meaning

In 同じ尺度で (おなじしゃくどで), the で marks what you use to judge. The two works need not have the same dimensions or receive the same score.
