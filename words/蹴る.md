# 蹴る (ける) - kick; reject

## Meaning

蹴る is a transitive verb meaning to kick.
In modern Japanese it follows the godan pattern: 蹴ります, 蹴って, 蹴らない.
Despite its short ける form, do not conjugate it as an ichidan verb.

- ボールを強く蹴りました。 (ボールをつよくけりました。) - I kicked the ball hard.

Additional uses include rejecting an offer and pressing the feet firmly against something:

- 彼はその申し出を蹴りました。 (かれはそのもうしでをけりました。) - He rejected that offer.
- 壁を蹴って泳ぎ始めました。 (かべをけっておよぎはじめました。) - I pushed off the wall and started swimming.

These additional meanings do not receive independent N2 assignments from the source row for "kick."

## How the kanji combine

蹴 is a single character with the native reading け.る.
The 足 element can remind you of a foot action; that is a mnemonic, not a complete historical analysis.
The written る belongs to the verb ending.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[断る](断る.md) (ことわる) overlaps with the refusal use.
[踏む](踏む.md) (ふむ) means stepping or treading on something; foot pressure and kicking can overlap, but they are not identical actions.
There is no useful single opposite for all three uses.

## Distinctive meaning

An offer that is 蹴られた was rejected, not literally struck with a foot.
Conversely, do not translate every ordinary step as 蹴る.
JMdict records 蹶る as a rare kanji form; this article uses 蹴る.
