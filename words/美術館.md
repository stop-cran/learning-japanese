# 美術館 (びじゅつかん) — art museum

## Meaning

美術館 is an **art museum or public art gallery**, a place where works of visual art are displayed.

日曜日に美術館で絵を見ました。 (にちようびにびじゅつかんでえをみました。) — I looked at paintings at an art museum on Sunday.

The destination takes に with 行く (いく), while で marks where an activity takes place: 美術館に行く, but 美術館で絵を見る.

## How the kanji combine

[美](../kanji/美.md), “beauty,” and [術](../kanji/術.md), “art/technique,” form 美術 (びじゅつ), “fine art.” [館](../kanji/館.md) adds an institution or building. The on readings are ビ + ジュツ + カン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

博物館 (はくぶつかん) is a museum, often of history, science, or other collections. 画廊 (がろう) is an art gallery, often a venue exhibiting or selling paintings. These categories can overlap in English translation.

## Distinctive meaning

美術館 emphasizes an art institution, not just any room containing pictures. The English gloss “gallery” should not make learners assume it is necessarily a commercial shop where the displayed works are for sale.
