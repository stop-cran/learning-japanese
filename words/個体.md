# 個体 (こたい) - individual organism; specimen

## Meaning

個体 is an individual considered as one separate entity, especially
one living organism rather than a whole species or group.
In biology it can refer to an individual animal or plant,
including a specimen being observed.

- この種には、白い個体もいます。
  (このしゅには、しろいこたいもいます。) - Some individuals of this species are white.
- 二つの個体を比べました。
  (ふたつのこたいをくらべました。) - We compared two specimens.

個体差 (こたいさ) means differences between individuals.
The word itself does not specify whether an individual is alive,
preserved, or being discussed abstractly.

## How the kanji combine

[個](../kanji/個.md) contributes an individual unit, and
[体](../kanji/体.md) a body or entity.
Together they identify one entity considered separately.
The on readings are コ + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[個人](個人.md) (こじん) is an individual person.
個体 is not restricted to people and commonly treats its subject
as an organism or specimen. 種 (しゅ), a species, and 集団
(しゅうだん), a group, describe different levels of grouping;
they are useful contrasts, not simple antonyms in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling a person 個体 frames them as an individual entity or organism,
not simply as a private citizen; 個人 is the ordinary personal label.
The homophone 固体 (こたい) means solid matter and is a different word.
An organism is not classified as a solid merely because 個体 and 固体
sound alike.
