# 並ぶ (ならぶ) — line up; stand in a row

## Meaning

並ぶ means to line up, stand in a queue, or be arranged in a row. It is intransitive: the people or objects forming the line take が.

- 入口に人が並んでいます。 (いりぐちにひとがならんでいます。) — People are lined up at the entrance.
- ここに並んでください。 (ここにならんでください。) — Please line up here.

It is a godan verb ending in ぶ: 並びます (ならびます), 並んだ (ならんだ), 並ばない (ならばない).

## How the kanji combine

並 carries the idea of things side by side. Its reading here is なら, followed by the verb ending ぶ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

並べる (ならべる) is transitive: 机を並べる (つくえをならべる), "arrange desks in a row." 待つ (まつ) means "wait" but does not by itself imply a line.

## Distinctive meaning

並んでいる can describe an existing arrangement of objects, not just people actively queuing: 店が並んでいる (みせがならんでいる), "shops stand in a row." The verb tells you about the arrangement; it does not require waiting for service.
