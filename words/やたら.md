# やたら - indiscriminately; excessively

## Meaning

やたら describes acting indiscriminately, randomly, or recklessly, or doing or experiencing something to an excessive degree. It often suggests lack of restraint or an unusually high degree, rather than merely a large quantity.

- やたらにボタンを押さないでください。
  (やたらにボタンをおさないでください。) - Please do not press the buttons indiscriminately.
- 今日はやたらと眠い。
  (きょうはやたらとねむい。) - I am unusually sleepy today.

The adverb occurs alone or with に or と. The adjectival use takes な before a noun, as in やたらな行動 (やたらなこうどう), indiscriminate behavior.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is kana. The rare spelling 矢鱈 is ateji: do not derive the meaning from a literal combination of arrow and codfish. This kana-only card has an empty kanji list.

## Synonyms and antonyms

むやみ (indiscriminate; reckless) and むやみやたら (at random; recklessly) are close comparisons. [でたらめ](でたらめ.md) overlaps in haphazardness, but also means nonsense.

[あんまり](あんまり.md) can overlap in excessive degree. [過剰](過剰.md) (かじょう), excess, expresses too much rather than the whole range of random or reckless behavior.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish indiscriminate manner from excessive degree. In the sleepy example, やたらと does not mean that sleepiness was deliberately chosen at random.

Not every instance is a condemnation, and not every degree word is interchangeable. [ずっと](ずっと.md) meaning "much more" requires a comparison; やたら does not itself establish that comparison.
