# コース - route; course

## Meaning

コース is a route or course to follow, and also an organized course
of study or program. It is a noun borrowed from English "course."

- 散歩のコースを変えました。 (さんぽのコースをかえました。) - I changed my walking route.
- 日本語のコースに申し込みました。 (にほんごのコースにもうしこみました。) - I signed up for a Japanese-language course.

Additional uses include a full-course meal and a plan or option offered
as a service:

- この店では三千円のコースを頼みました。 (このみせではさんぜんえんのコースをたのみました。) - At this restaurant, we ordered the three-thousand-yen set-course meal.
- 基本コースと上級コースから選べます。 (きほんコースとじょうきゅうコースからえらべます。) - You can choose between the basic and advanced options.

In the meal use, コース can abbreviate フルコース.
The service or lesson description determines what each option includes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[道](道.md) (みち) can mean a road or way; a walking コース need not be
one named road. [課程](課程.md) (かてい) is a course of study,
while [科目](科目.md) (かもく) can name a subject or course.
[プラン](プラン.md) overlaps with an offered plan or package.
There is no single antonym shared by all these uses.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that every English "course" has one Japanese meaning.
A study program, route, and complete meal are different uses of the
same borrowing. In particular, a restaurant's コース need not mean
only one dish served during the meal; the dictionary also separately
records that individual-course use.
This card selects route and study senses for the broad source gloss.
Meal and service-plan senses are additional; sports and other specialist
uses are not exhaustively taught or independently assigned N2.
