# 移す (うつす) - move; transfer

## Meaning

移す is a transitive godan verb: someone moves or transfers something. Use AをBに移す for "move A to B"; から can identify its starting place. Its forms include 移します, 移して, and 移した.

- 机を窓のそばに移しました。 (つくえをまどのそばにうつしました。) - I moved the desk beside the window.
- 家族に風邪をうつさないように気をつけています。 (かぞくにかぜをうつさないようにきをつけています。) - I am taking care not to pass my cold to my family.

The disease-transmission sense is usually written うつす in kana. The illness takes を and the recipient takes に.

## How the kanji combine

移 supplies the native stem うつ-, with す completing the verb. The central idea is transfer; this spelling is not a compound of two kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[移る](移る.md) (うつる) describes something moving or passing to another place: 風邪がうつる, "catch a cold from someone." [写す](写す.md) also reads うつす but concerns copying or photographing, not moving a desk.

## Distinctive meaning

Transfer need not be physical: 計画を実行に移す (けいかくをじっこうにうつす) means "put a plan into action," moving it to the implementation stage. Do not interpret the English gloss "remove" as necessarily throwing something away.
