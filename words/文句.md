# 文句 (もんく) - complaint; phrase

## Meaning

文句 has two useful noun meanings: a complaint or objection, and a phrase or set of words.
For complaining, use 文句を言う (もんくをいう).
文句がある means to have an objection; 文句はない means to have no complaint.

- 彼は食事に文句を言いました。
  (かれはしょくじにもんくをいいました。) - He complained about the meal.
- この広告の文句は覚えやすいです。
  (このこうこくのもんくはおぼえやすいです。)
  - The wording of this advertisement is easy to remember.

In the first example, に marks what the complaint is about.
In the second, 広告の文句 identifies advertising wording, not an objection to the advertisement.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) contributes a text or sentence, and 句 contributes a phrase.
The related language components support the wording sense; the complaint sense belongs
to the established whole word. The on readings here are モン + ク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不満](不満.md) (ふまん) means dissatisfaction.
Someone can feel 不満 without expressing a 文句.
苦情 (くじょう) also means a complaint, including one submitted about a service or problem.

## Distinctive meaning

文句を言う can sound like "grumble" or "complain," depending on context.
It is not a neutral description of every comment.
Conversely, the wording sense is not inherently negative: an advertising phrase can be memorable or effective.
The collocation resolves the ambiguity.
