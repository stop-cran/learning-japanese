# 全 (ぜん) - all; entire

## Meaning

全 is a prefix meaning "all," "whole," or "entire." It attaches
directly to a noun: [全員](全員.md) (ぜんいん), "everyone," and
全世界 (ぜんせかい), "the whole world." It can also introduce
the total number of volumes in a complete set.

- 全社員が会議に参加しました。 (ぜんしゃいんがかいぎにさんかしました。) - All employees attended the meeting.
- この小説は全三巻です。 (このしょうせつはぜんさんかんです。) - This novel is complete in three volumes.

全三巻 describes the complete work's total length, not merely three
volumes selected from a longer series.

## How the kanji combine

[全](../kanji/全.md) contributes entirety or completeness through its
on reading ゼン. In compounds, it specifies the scope of what
follows: the entire membership, world, or set, rather than one part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ) and [全て](全て.md) (すべて) also
express "all." Their grammar differs from a bound prefix:
全部の本, "all the books," uses の, whereas 全社員 joins
the prefix directly to 社員. 一部 (いちぶ), "a part," provides
a useful contrast to completeness.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn established 全 compounds instead of inserting a free-standing
全 wherever English has "all." The word defines the relevant whole;
it does not by itself tell you how large that group or collection is.
