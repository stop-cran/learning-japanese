# 足りる (たりる) — to be enough

## Meaning

足りる means that an amount meets a need. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 足ります, 足りない, 足りて. The resource whose sufficiency is judged takes が.

- 時間が足りません。 (じかんがたりません。) — There is not enough time.
- 千円で足りますか。 (せんえんでたりますか。) — Will a thousand yen be enough?

## How the kanji combine

[足](../kanji/足.md) supplies た; りる is okurigana. It has the sufficiency meaning here, not the body-part reading あし. The conjugating base for forms such as 足ります is たり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[十分](十分.md) (じゅうぶん) means sufficient. 不足する (ふそくする) means being short of something. [足す](足す.md) is the transitive verb "add," an action that may remedy a shortage.

## Distinctive meaning

が identifies the available resource; で can identify an amount or means adequate for the purpose. 足りる does not mean there is a lot in absolute terms: even a small quantity may be enough for a small task.
