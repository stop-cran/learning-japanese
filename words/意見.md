# 意見 (いけん) — opinion

## Meaning

意見 is an opinion or view about a subject. Useful combinations are 意見を言う (いけんをいう), "state an opinion," and 意見を聞く (いけんをきく), "ask for/listen to an opinion."

この計画について、意見を聞かせてください。 (このけいかくについて、いけんをきかせてください。) — Please tell me your opinion about this plan.

## How the kanji combine

[意](../kanji/意.md) concerns thought or intention, and [見](../kanji/見.md) concerns seeing or a view. Together they suggest a considered view of something. Both use on-readings: い + けん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

考え (かんがえ) is broader: a thought, idea, or plan. 感想 (かんそう) is an impression or reaction after an experience. Neither is always interchangeable with an opinion on a proposal.

## Distinctive meaning

The ordinary noun does not imply either agreement or disagreement. 私の意見 (わたしのいけん) means "my opinion." Be careful with 意見する: it can mean to admonish or remonstrate with someone, not simply the neutral act of expressing any opinion. For beginners, 意見を言う is the clearer neutral pattern.
