# 全国 (ぜんこく) - the whole country; nationwide

## Meaning

全国 is a noun meaning "the whole country." With particles it gives useful English expressions such as "nationwide" or "from across the country": **全国で**, **全国に**, **全国から**.

- 全国から学生が集まりました。 (ぜんこくからがくせいがあつまりました。) - Students gathered from across the country.
- この商品は全国で販売されています。 (このしょうひんはぜんこくではんばいされています。) - This product is sold nationwide.

全国の学校 (ぜんこくのがっこう) means "schools across the country"; の connects the geographical scope to the following noun.

## How the kanji combine

[全](../kanji/全.md), "all; whole," modifies [国](../kanji/国.md), "country." ゼン + コク directly expresses the whole-country scope. The reading is ぜんこく, not ぜんごく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[全体](全体.md) (ぜんたい) means the whole of something more generally; 全国 specifies a country. [国](国.md) (くに) can simply name a country without emphasizing coverage across it. A local area is a contextual contrast, not a universal antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

In Japanese domestic news, the country is often understood to be Japan, but the word's meaning is not inherently "Japan." Context supplies the country. Nationwide availability also does not promise an item in every individual shop: distinguish broad geographical coverage from an exhaustive claim about every location or person.
