# ばたばた — flapping; bustling hurriedly

## Meaning

ばたばた describes repeated flapping or clattering, noisy hurried movement, and being busy or flustered. It also occurs with things falling or happening in quick succession.

- 鳥が羽をばたばたさせた。 — The bird flapped its wings. (羽: はね.)
- 出かける前は、いつも準備でばたばたする。 — I am always rushing around getting ready before going out. (準備: じゅんび.)
- 強い風で看板がばたばたと倒れた。 — Signs toppled one after another in the strong wind. (看板: かんばん; 倒れる: たおれる.)

Use ばたばたする for bustling or flapping, and ばたばた（と） before a suitable action verb. ばたばたさせる makes something flap or move noisily.

## How the kanji combine

This repeated kana mimetic can imitate physical sounds or depict movement and a hurried state without actual noise.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[どたどた](どたどた.md) particularly evokes heavy, noisy footsteps. [のんびり](のんびり.md) contrasts with the hurried, busy sense; it does not contrast with every flapping sound.

## Distinctive meaning

ばたばたしていて返事が遅れた means "I was rushed off my feet, so my reply was late." No literal flapping is implied. The sound, busyness, and succession interpretations depend on the following verb, not a single English equivalent.
