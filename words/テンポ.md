# テンポ - tempo; pace

## Meaning

テンポ is musical tempo: how quickly or slowly a piece proceeds.
It also describes the pace of an activity or development.

- この曲はテンポが速いです。 (このきょくはテンポがはやいです。) - This piece has a fast tempo.
- 話がテンポよく進みました。 (はなしがテンポよくすすみました。) - The discussion moved along at a good pace.

テンポを落とす (テンポをおとす) means to slow the tempo or pace.
テンポよく describes a good, lively flow; it does not merely measure a vehicle's speed.

## How the kanji combine

テンポ is a katakana loanword from Italian *tempo*, with no kanji components.
Keep the small distinction between ポ and ボ: the spelling is テンポ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[速度](速度.md) (そくど) means speed or rate more broadly.
リズム, "rhythm," concerns the pattern of beats or movements, not just how fast they occur.
A piece can keep the same rhythm while its テンポ changes.

## Distinctive meaning

The musical sense concerns the speed of the beat, not volume or pitch.
In conversation or narrative, テンポ concerns how things proceed.
The same sound appears in unrelated Japanese words, such as 店舗 (てんぽ), "shop premises";
that spelling does not name musical tempo.
