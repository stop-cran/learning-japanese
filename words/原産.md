# 原産 (げんさん) - native origin

## Meaning

原産 identifies where a plant, animal, or product originally comes from.
It is a noun, often used in 地域名 + 原産, "native to / originating in that region,"
or 原産の before the thing described.

- この植物は南米原産です。 (このしょくぶつはなんべいげんさんです。) - This plant is native to South America.
- この果物の原産地を調べています。 (このくだもののげんさんちをしらべています。) - I am looking up this fruit's place of origin.

原産地 (げんさんち) explicitly adds "place." The relevant origin may be
biological or concern a product; the word alone does not establish a legal
country-of-origin certification.

## How the kanji combine

[原](../kanji/原.md) contributes original or source, and [産](../kanji/産.md)
contributes production or native origin. The combination points to original
provenance, not simply the current location. The on readings are ゲン + サン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出身](出身.md) (しゅっしん) concerns a person's place or background of origin.
[故郷](故郷.md) (こきょう) is a hometown or native place.
[天然](天然.md) (てんねん) concerns nature or natural origin rather than
identifying which region something originally came from.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary gloss "habitat" needs its origin qualification here.
A plant grown in a Japanese garden is not thereby 日本原産 if it originated
elsewhere. Likewise, 原産 is not the ordinary way to give one's hometown.
