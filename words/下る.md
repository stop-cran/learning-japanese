# 下る (くだる) - descend; go downstream

## Meaning

下る means travel down from a higher place or proceed downstream. With a route such as a slope or river, を marks the path traversed, not an object being lowered.

- 坂をゆっくり下りました。 (さかをゆっくりくだりました。) - I went slowly down the slope.
- 船で川を下りました。 (ふねでかわをくだりました。) - I traveled downstream by boat.

An additional use is an order or judgment being handed down: 判決が下る (はんけつがくだる), a judgment is delivered.

Despite ending in る, it is godan: 下ります (くだります), 下って (くだって), 下らない (くだらない).

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) contributes the down/below association and supplies くだ here. る is the verb ending. Do not substitute its other readings into this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下りる](下りる.md) (おりる) and [降りる](降りる.md) (おりる) overlap in descending; they also cover getting off a vehicle. 下る is useful for movement down a route or downstream.

[下がる](下がる.md) (さがる) can mean become lower or move back. Not every decrease is naturally described by 下る.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records 降る at くだる, but the existing weather word [降る](降る.md) is read ふる. Shared written characters do not make those readings or uses interchangeable. The adjective [くだらない](くだらない.md), worthless or foolish, also needs its own lexical meaning.
