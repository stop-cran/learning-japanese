# あ (あ) — ah; oh

## Meaning

あ is a short reaction when you notice, remember, or realize something. It stands outside the sentence rather than taking a particle.

- あ、電車が来た。 (あ、でんしゃがきた。) — Oh, the train is here.
- あ、忘れていた。 (あ、わすれていた。) — Ah, I had forgotten.

## How the kanji combine

This interjection is written in kana, with no kanji to divide. あっ represents an abruptly cut-off reaction; アッ is another written form. The small っ is not pronounced as a separate syllable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ああ can represent a longer realization or response. えっ often expresses surprise or a request to repeat something. These overlap in conversation but are not fixed translations of every English "oh."

## Distinctive meaning

Tone and context supply much of the meaning. あ can also introduce an attempt to get someone's attention, but it is not a polite substitute for すみません when addressing a stranger. There is no meaningful direct antonym.
