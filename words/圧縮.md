# 圧縮 (あっしゅく) - compression; condensing

## Meaning

圧縮 is compression: making something occupy less space or reducing its size.
It is a noun and a transitive する-verb; what is compressed takes を.
The word applies to physical material, data and condensed content.

- 空気を圧縮します。 (くうきをあっしゅくします。) - We compress the air.
- 写真のファイルを圧縮しました。 (しゃしんのファイルをあっしゅくしました。) - I compressed the photo file.
- 説明を三行に圧縮しました。 (せつめいをさんぎょうにあっしゅくしました。) - I condensed the explanation into three lines.

The file example concerns data size, not physically squeezing the computer.

## How the kanji combine

[圧](../kanji/圧.md) contributes pressure or pressing, and 縮 contributes shrinking
or shortening. Together they suggest reducing something by compression.
The on readings アツ and シュク combine as あっしゅく, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[詰める](詰める.md) (つめる) can mean packing or shortening, but placing things
in a box does not necessarily compress them. [圧力](圧力.md) (あつりょく) names
pressure rather than the compression process.
For a compressed file, 解凍する (かいとうする) can mean decompressing or extracting it.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use the source list's broad English "pressure" as the definition of every occurrence.
Pressure can cause compression, but they are not the same quantity or action.
Compression alone also does not tell you whether a data format loses information.
