# 味 (あじ) — taste, flavor

## Meaning

味 is the taste or flavor of something. It can also mean a distinctive charm or appeal.

- このスープはいい味です。（この スープは いい あじです。）— This soup tastes good.
- この店の料理は味がいいです。（この みせの りょうりは あじが いいです。）— The food at this restaurant tastes good.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun. [味](../kanji/味.md) represents the whole word あじ, with no okurigana.

The related verb 味わう あじわう means "taste" or "savor"; わう is its okurigana. Do not substitute the compound reading ミ for the standalone noun あじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 風味 ふうみ also means flavor, often highlighting a food's characteristic flavor or aroma.
- 匂い におい is smell, not taste; the senses are related but not interchangeable.
- There is no direct antonym. 味がない あじがない means "has no flavor," a phrase rather than an opposite noun.

## Distinctive meaning

味 is a noun, not the adjective "delicious." A food can have a good, bad, strong, or weak 味.

The dictionary contains separate entries for み, including suffix uses. This article teaches the independent word あじ, not those separate items.
