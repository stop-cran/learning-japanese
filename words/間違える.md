# 間違える (まちがえる) — to get wrong

## Meaning

間違える means making a mistake in something or getting it wrong. It is a transitive ichidan verb: 間違えます (まちがえます), 間違えた (まちがえた), 間違えない (まちがえない).

電話番号を間違えました。 (でんわばんごうをまちがえました。) — I got the phone number wrong.

出口を入口と間違えました。 (でぐちをいりぐちとまちがえました。) — I mistook the exit for the entrance.

## How the kanji combine

[間](../kanji/間.md) writes ま and [違](../kanji/違.md) writes ちが, followed by える. Learn まちがえる as the whole verb; its ます-stem is 間違え, not simply the written kanji portion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

間違う (まちがう) overlaps in making mistakes but has godan forms such as 間違った. [違う](違う.md) (ちがう) also means "differ," without necessarily implying an error.

## Distinctive meaning

XをYと間違える means mistaking X for Y. The を-marked item is what was misidentified; と introduces what it was taken to be. A mistake need not involve confusing two objects: a number, answer, or procedure can simply be wrong.
