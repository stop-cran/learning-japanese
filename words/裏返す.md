# 裏返す (うらがえす) - turn over; turn inside out

## Meaning

裏返す is a transitive godan verb: turn something over or inside out.
The object turned takes を. A card exposes its other face; a garment exposes its inside.

- カードを裏返してください。 (カードをうらがえしてください。) - Please turn the card over.
- 洗濯する前に、シャツを裏返しました。 (せんたくするまえに、シャツをうらがえしました。) - I turned the shirt inside out before washing it.

Forms include 裏返します (うらがえします), 裏返した (うらがえした) and 裏返さない (うらがえさない).
The example describes one washing preparation, not a care rule for every garment.

## How the kanji combine

[裏](../kanji/裏.md), read うら, supplies the reverse/inside;
[返](../kanji/返.md)す supplies かえす, voiced to がえす in this native compound.
The parts suggest bringing the other side outward or upward.

## Synonyms and antonyms

裏返し (うらがえし) names a reversed or inside-out state.
[逆](逆.md) (ぎゃく), "reverse; opposite," is broader and can concern order or direction,
not specifically turning a surface over.
[返す](返す.md) (かえす) also means giving something back; 裏返す does not mean returning a borrowed book.

## Distinctive meaning

The object determines whether "turn over" or "turn inside out" is appropriate.
Turning an object to its original side can undo a particular action;
there is no single opposite that fits every reversal.
The reading is うらがえす, not two on readings joined together.
