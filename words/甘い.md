# 甘い (あまい) -- sweet

## Meaning

甘い describes a sweet taste, whether the sweetness comes from fruit, sugar, or another ingredient.

- このいちごは甘いです。 (このいちごはあまいです。) -- This strawberry is sweet.
- 甘い物が好きです。 (あまいものがすきです。) -- I like sweet things.

It is an i-adjective: 甘くない (あまくない), "not sweet," and 甘かった (あまかった), "was sweet." Before a noun, use 甘い directly, without な.

## How the kanji combine

甘 carries the sweetness meaning and is read あま in this word. The written い completes the adjective; there is no multi-kanji compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

苦い (にがい), "bitter," and 酸っぱい (すっぱい), "sour," are useful taste contrasts. Neither is the one universal opposite: food can combine sweetness with bitterness or sourness.

## Distinctive meaning

甘い evaluates taste, not whether something is delicious. A drink may be too sweet for someone's preference: 甘すぎます (あますぎます), "It is too sweet." Use おいしい when the intended judgment is "tasty," rather than a particular flavor.
