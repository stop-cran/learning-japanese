# 漫画 (まんが) — comics; manga

## Meaning

漫画 means comics, cartoons, or manga: stories or commentary conveyed through drawings, often with words. It is a noun and can refer to the medium or an individual work.

電車で漫画を読んでいます。 (でんしゃでまんがをよんでいます。) — I am reading a comic on the train.

## How the kanji combine

漫 has associations with free or unrestrained expression, while [画](../kanji/画.md) means picture. The on readings まん + が form the established word. Kana まんが and マンガ are also used.

## Synonyms and antonyms

コミック is a related loanword, especially for comic publications. アニメ is animation, a different medium. 小説 (しょうせつ) is a prose novel; it is a useful comparison rather than an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

In Japanese, 漫画 is not restricted to works made in Japan. A 漫画 can be funny, serious, educational, or dramatic; English "comic" should not be interpreted as guaranteeing humor. Use 冊 (さつ) when counting bound volumes.
