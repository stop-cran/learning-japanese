# ごたごた — trouble; complications

## Meaning

ごたごた can refer to a troublesome dispute or a complicated situation.

- 契約をめぐるごたごたが続き、担当者は対応に追われた。
  — Complications over the contract continued, keeping the person in charge busy.
- 引っ越しのごたごたが片付き、ようやく普段の生活に戻れた。
  — The moving-related upheaval was settled, and I could finally return to everyday life.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ごたごた emphasizes troublesome complications or disputes. ごちゃごちゃ more often describes
things being mixed up or physically cluttered.

## Distinctive meaning

Here ごたごた is a noun naming the trouble itself; the surrounding particles show how it fits into
the sentence.
