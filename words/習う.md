# 習う (ならう) — learn under instruction

## Meaning

習う means learning from a teacher or taking lessons. The subject or skill takes を;
the teacher can be marked by に or から.

- 先生に日本語を習っています。 (せんせいににほんごをならっています。) — I am learning Japanese from a teacher.
- 毎週、ピアノを習っています。 (まいしゅう、ピアノをならっています。) — I take piano lessons every week.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native verb, not a compound. [習](../kanji/習.md) supplies なら, and う is okurigana.
It is godan: 習います (ならいます), 習って (ならって), 習った (ならった), 習わない (ならわない).
Notice the small っ in 習って.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 学ぶ (まなぶ) is a broader "learn" or "study"; the sentence need not present it specifically as taking lessons.
- [勉強](勉強.md)する (べんきょうする) means studying, which can be done independently.
- 練習する (れんしゅうする) means practising a skill; it does not itself say that a teacher is giving a lesson.
- [教える](教える.md) (おしえる, teach) describes the other side of instruction, rather than the absence of learning.

## Distinctive meaning

習う foregrounds instruction or training. It is not the usual verb for simply finding out some news:
[知る](知る.md) (しる) covers coming to know information.
習っている often describes ongoing lessons, not proof that the learner has already mastered the skill.
