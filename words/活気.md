# 活気 (かっき) - liveliness; vigor

## Meaning

活気 is a noun for liveliness, vigor, or an energetic atmosphere.
Useful patterns are **活気がある**, "be lively," **活気のある + noun**, "a lively ...,"
and **活気にあふれる**, "be full of life."

- この市場は朝から活気があります。 (このいちばはあさからかっきがあります。) - This market is lively from the morning.
- 学生たちが戻り、町は活気を取り戻しました。 (がくせいたちがもどり、まちはかっきをとりもどしました。) - The students returned, and the town regained its vitality.

In the second example, 活気を取り戻す treats vitality as something that can return after a quiet period.

## How the kanji combine

[活](../kanji/活.md) contributes activity or life, and [気](../kanji/気.md) contributes spirit or energy.
Together they describe an active, lively spirit. カツ + キ produces かっき, with the small っ marking a held consonant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[元気](元気.md) (げんき) can describe a person's health or energy.
[賑やか](賑やか.md) (にぎやか) describes a lively or bustling scene and overlaps with 活気のある.
[静か](静か.md) (しずか), "quiet," is a contextual contrast, not proof that a quiet place lacks vitality.

## Distinctive meaning

活気 draws attention to a sense of activity and engagement, not merely to the volume of noise.
A workplace can have 活気 because people are involved in their work.
Use the noun construction 活気がある or 活気のある rather than assuming it behaves like a na-adjective.
