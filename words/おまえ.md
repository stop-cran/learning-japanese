# おまえ (おまえ) - you (very informal)

## Meaning

おまえ is a very informal second-person pronoun, "you," also written お前.
It can convey familiarity or sound rough and insulting, depending on the
relationship and tone. It is associated with masculine speech, not restricted
by a rule about the speaker's gender.

- おまえも来るの？ (おまえもくるの？) - Are you coming too?
- これはおまえのかばん？ - Is this your bag?

These could occur between people who already accept that style of address.
They are recognition examples, not safe default questions for a new acquaintance.

## How the kanji combine

The assigned spelling uses kana. In お前, [前](../kanji/前.md) means "front."
JMdict records that the pronoun was formerly honorific; that history does not
make modern おまえ respectful.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あなた](あなた.md) and [君](君.md) (きみ) also address someone, with their
own social implications. A person's [名前](名前.md) (なまえ), appropriate title,
or no pronoun at all may suit the situation better. These are choices of address,
not a simple polite-to-rude conversion chart.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records a noun おまえ meaning "before/in front of a god,
nobleman, or similar figure." That is a separate sense within the same entry,
not permission to use the modern pronoun respectfully. The source list's
"presence of a high personage" gloss must not be read as the pronoun's current
register. Recognizing a line in fiction and adopting its address style are
different learning tasks.
