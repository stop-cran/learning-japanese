# パソコン (パソコン) - personal computer

## Meaning

パソコン is a personal computer, including a laptop or desktop computer. It is the normal short everyday term.

- パソコンで日本語の文章を書いています。 (パソコンでにほんごのぶんしょうをかいています。) - I am writing Japanese text on a computer.
- 新しいパソコンを買いました。 (あたらしいパソコンをかいました。) - I bought a new computer.

で marks the computer as the tool used for an activity; を marks it as the object bought or used.

## How the kanji combine

パソコン abbreviates パーソナルコンピューター, borrowed from English "personal computer." It combines shortened parts of the longer expression. There are no written kanji, and the abbreviation does not retain the long vowel of パーソナル.

## Synonyms and antonyms

コンピューター is the broader word "computer." ノートパソコン specifies a laptop. スマートフォン, usually shortened to スマホ, names a smartphone.

## Distinctive meaning

A smartphone is a computer in a technical sense, but ordinary Japanese does not usually call it a パソコン. The everyday category centers on the laptop or desktop used for work, study, and similar computing tasks.
