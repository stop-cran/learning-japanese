# 名作 (めいさく) - masterpiece; masterwork

## Meaning

名作 is an outstanding or celebrated work, for example a novel, film, or painting.
It evaluates the work highly rather than merely saying that something was created.

- この映画は名作です。 (このえいがはめいさくです。) - This film is a masterpiece.
- 世界の名作を読んでいます。 (せかいのめいさくをよんでいます。) - I am reading great works of world literature.

## How the kanji combine

[名](../kanji/名.md) contributes being distinguished or famous, and
[作](../kanji/作.md) contributes a created work.
The modifier-plus-head compound uses the on readings メイ + サク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[傑作](傑作.md) (けっさく) also means a masterpiece.
[作品](作品.md) (さくひん) is the broader word for a work or creation, without
the same built-in praise. A literary [古典](古典.md) (こてん), "classic,"
can also be a 名作; the words emphasize different qualities.

## Distinctive meaning

名作 does not require that the work be ancient or the creator's only great achievement.
Calling something a 名作 expresses an evaluation, which people can disagree about.
Fame and quality are associated here, not a guarantee that every famous work is excellent.
