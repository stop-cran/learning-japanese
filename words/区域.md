# 区域 (くいき) - defined area; zone; district

## Meaning

区域 is an area or zone considered as a delimited extent, for example a construction zone or a delivery area. Depending on context, English "district," "section," or the "limits" of an area also fits.

- この区域は工事中なので、入れません。 (このくいきはこうじちゅうなので、はいれません。) - This area is under construction, so you cannot enter.
- 配達できる区域は、この地図で確認できます。 (はいたつできるくいきは、このちずでかくにんできます。) - You can check the delivery area on this map.

区域内 (くいきない) means within the area; 区域外 (くいきがい) means outside it.

## How the kanji combine

[区](../kanji/区.md), a division or district, combines with [域](../kanji/域.md), area or extent. The related elements describe a marked-out area. The on readings are ク + イキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[地区](地区.md) (ちく) and [地域](地域.md) (ちいき) also name areas or districts. 区域 brings the delimited extent into focus; this is an emphasis, not an exceptionless size-based division between the words. [範囲](範囲.md) (はんい) can be a range or scope beyond physical geography.

## Distinctive meaning

An 区域 need not be an official municipal ward. Nor is it always just the dividing line: [境界](境界.md) (きょうかい) focuses on a boundary, while 区域 can name the entire area within its limits. A broad English gloss such as "domain" or "territory" does not by itself make this a word for Internet domain names.
