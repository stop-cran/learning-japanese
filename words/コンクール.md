# コンクール - competition; contest

## Meaning

コンクール is a competition or contest, especially one comparing artistic or performance skills. It often appears with the activity being judged.

- ピアノのコンクールに出ました。 (ピアノのコンクールにでました。) - I entered a piano competition.
- 写真コンクールで賞をもらいました。 (しゃしんコンクールでしょうをもらいました。) - I received a prize in a photography contest.

に出る identifies the event entered; で can mark the event where someone receives an award.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana borrowing from French **concours**, not a kanji compound. Keep the long vowel in クール: コンクール.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[競争](競争.md) (きょうそう) is competition or rivalry more broadly. [競技](競技.md) (きょうぎ) is useful for a competitive sport or event; [試合](試合.md) (しあい) often identifies a match or bout. A [大会](大会.md) (たいかい) can be a tournament or convention, not necessarily an arts competition.

## Distinctive meaning

The arts examples show a useful tendency, not a rule that only classical music can have a コンクール. Nor is every competitive situation called one: ordinary business rivalry is not automatically an organized judging event.

Entering a コンクール does not mean winning it. The prize in the second sentence comes from 賞をもらう, not from the noun alone.
