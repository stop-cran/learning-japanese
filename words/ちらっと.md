# ちらっと — briefly or at a glance

## Meaning

ちらっと describes looking at something briefly, often only for an instant.

- 彼は資料をちらっと見て、すぐ顔を上げた。
  — He glanced at the document and immediately looked up.
- 窓の外をちらっと見たら、雪が降り始めていた。
  — When I glanced outside, it had begun to snow.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ちらっと emphasizes the brevity of a look rather than sustained or careful observation.

## Distinctive meaning

It emphasizes the short duration of the look, not careful inspection.
