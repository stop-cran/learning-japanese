# 教科書 (きょうかしょ) - textbook

## Meaning

教科書 is a textbook or coursebook used to teach a subject. It is a noun: **subject の教科書** identifies the subject, and **教科書で勉強する** means to study using a textbook.

- 数学の教科書を忘れました。 (すうがくのきょうかしょをわすれました。) - I forgot my mathematics textbook.
- 教科書の十ページを開いてください。 (きょうかしょのじゅっページをひらいてください。) - Please open your textbook to page ten.

In the second example, 教科書の connects the book to its page. To count physical textbooks, use the book counter 冊: 教科書を二冊買った (きょうかしょをにさつかった), "bought two textbooks."

## How the kanji combine

[教](../kanji/教.md) contributes teaching, [科](../kanji/科.md) a subject or branch of study, and [書](../kanji/書.md) a book or written work. 教科, "school subject," modifies 書: a book for teaching a subject. The readings are キョウ + カ + ショ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本](本.md) (ほん) is the broader word "book." [テキスト](テキスト.md) can mean a course text or teaching material. [辞書](辞書.md) (じしょ) is a dictionary for looking things up, rather than necessarily a coursebook.

## Distinctive meaning

教科書 identifies a book's teaching function, not simply its subject matter. A novel studied in class is still a novel; being read at school does not automatically make every book a 教科書. There is no single useful opposite.
