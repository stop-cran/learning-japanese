# 大使館 (たいしかん)

## Meaning

大使館 means an embassy: the diplomatic mission representing a country in another country, or its premises.

- 大使館に電話しました。 (たいしかんに でんわしました。) — I called the embassy.

Country names can identify which embassy is meant: 日本大使館 (にほんたいしかん), "the Japanese embassy." The name identifies the represented country, not necessarily the country where the building stands.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) and [使](../kanji/使.md) form 大使 (たいし), "ambassador." [館](../kanji/館.md) adds a building or institutional base. The compound is therefore ambassador + institution, not simply any "large building."

## Synonyms and antonyms

領事館 (りょうじかん) is a consulate, a related but different diplomatic institution. Both may handle some services for travelers; the appropriate office depends on the country and service.

## Distinctive meaning

大使館 is not a general word for a foreign government office or tourist information center. It can refer to the institution's staff and work as well as its physical building. There is no natural direct antonym.
