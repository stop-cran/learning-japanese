# 分ける (わける) - divide; share out

## Meaning

分ける is a transitive ichidan verb: divide, separate, classify, or share something out. Use **thing を + resulting groups に分ける** when stating the division.

- 学生を二つのグループに分けました。 (がくせいをふたつのグループにわけました。) - We divided the students into two groups.
- お菓子を子供たちに分けました。 (おかしをこどもたちにわけました。) - I shared out the sweets among the children.

In the first example, に marks the resulting groups. In the second, it marks the recipients. The forms are 分けます, 分けて, and 分けない.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) contributes division or separation. The native verb is わける, with ける as okurigana. A different verb using the same kanji, 分かる (わかる), means understand; do not confuse their endings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

分かれる (わかれる) describes something splitting or people separating, without the same explicit object pattern. [合わせる](合わせる.md) (あわせる), "bring together," contrasts with division in suitable contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

分ける need not mean physically cutting something. Sorting rubbish into categories or dividing people into teams also counts. When sharing food, the verb says it is distributed, but equal shares require an additional expression such as 同じ量に (おなじりょうに), "into equal amounts."
