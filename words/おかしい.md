# おかしい - strange or amusing

## Meaning

おかしい can mean "funny" in the amusing sense or "strange, wrong" when something does not seem right. It is an i-adjective: おかしかった is past, and おかしくない is negative.

- その話がおかしくて、笑ってしまいました。 (そのはなしがおかしくて、わらってしまいました。) - The story was funny, and I couldn't help laughing.
- この時計はおかしいです。 (このとけいはおかしいです。) - Something is wrong with this clock.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is the usual spelling. 可笑しい is an established kanji spelling, but the word's meanings should not be limited to laughter on the basis of 笑.

## Synonyms and antonyms

変な (へんな) overlaps with "strange." 面白い (おもしろい) can mean "interesting" as well as "funny." 正しい (ただしい), "correct," contrasts with おかしい when a statement or calculation is wrong.

## Distinctive meaning

Context decides whether the reaction is amusement or concern. Calling someone's behavior おかしい may criticize it rather than compliment their sense of humor; it is not automatically a friendly description.
