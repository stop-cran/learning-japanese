# 大勢 (おおぜい)

## Meaning

大勢 means a large number of people. Use 大勢の before a noun referring to people, or use it adverbially with a verb.

- 大勢の人が来ました。 (おおぜいの ひとが きました。) — Many people came.
- 学生が大勢います。 (がくせいが おおぜい います。) — There are many students.

大勢で means "in a large group," as in 大勢で食べる (おおぜいでたべる), "eat in a large group."

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) supplies native おお, "large"; 勢 contributes ぜい. This is a mixed native-plus-on reading rather than an entirely native compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

たくさん can describe quantities of objects as well as people. 大勢 is specifically useful for people. 少人数 (しょうにんずう), "a small number of people," is a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 大勢の本 for "many books." Also distinguish the same spelling read たいせい, "general trend" or "overall situation": that is another word, not the crowd reading taught here.
