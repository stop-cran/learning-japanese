# 家族 (かぞく) — family

## Meaning

家族 means a family or its members. It refers to people rather than the house itself; family members need not all live together.

- 私の家族は四人です。 (わたしのかぞくはよにんです。) — There are four people in my family.
- 家族と日本に住んでいます。 (かぞくとにほんにすんでいます。) — I live in Japan with my family.

## How the kanji combine

[家](../kanji/家.md) カ (home, family) + [族](../kanji/族.md) ゾク (family or kin group): an on + on compound. 家 specifies the household or family connection of the group represented by 族.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 家庭 かてい also means home, household, or family, often with attention to home life rather than a list of family members.
- 親戚 しんせき means relatives and can include a wider circle than the immediate family.
- [家](家.md) いえ can mean house or home; it is not always interchangeable with 家族. There is no simple direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 家族 when talking about family members: 家族と, "with my family." A question about 家族 is not asking what kind of building you live in. The exact people included depend on the speaker and context, not a fixed dictionary head count.
