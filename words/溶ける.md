# 溶ける (とける) - melt; dissolve

## Meaning

溶ける is an intransitive ichidan verb: something melts, thaws, or dissolves.
The changing substance takes が; a liquid in which it dissolves can take に.

- 氷が溶けました。 (こおりがとけました。) - The ice melted.
- 砂糖が水に溶けます。 (さとうがみずにとけます。) - Sugar dissolves in water.

Forms include 溶けます, 溶けて, and 溶けない. Melting and dissolving are different
physical processes, but this Japanese verb can describe either.

## How the kanji combine

[溶](../kanji/溶.md) contributes melting or dissolving. The written division is
と + ける, but the grammatical stem before ます is とけ. This is a native verb,
not a compound of independent words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

溶かす (とかす) is transitive: 氷を溶かす (こおりをとかす), "melt ice."
Compare 氷が溶ける, "ice melts." The existing [解ける](解ける.md) article teaches
coming untied or being solved. However, JMdict also includes 解ける in the
melting/dissolving spelling family, so the teaching contrast is not an absolute
rule that 解ける can never describe melting.

## Distinctive meaning

溶ける does not identify who caused the change. Informal uses also describe money
or time being used up or melting away. That use can overlap with
[無くなる](無くなる.md), run out or disappear, [切れる](切れる.md), run out,
and [消える](消える.md), disappear; it does not make every use of those verbs
interchangeable. The examples above teach physical changes.
