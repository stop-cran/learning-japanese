# コレクション - collection; collecting

## Meaning

コレクション names a collection, such as stamps or artworks, and the activity of collecting them. コレクションする can take the collected items with を.

- これは私の切手のコレクションです。 (これはわたしのきってのコレクションです。) - This is my stamp collection.
- 古い時計をコレクションしています。 (ふるいとけいをコレクションしています。) - I collect old clocks and watches.

In fashion, it also names a designer's new clothing collection or its presentation. 春のコレクション (はるのコレクション) means "the spring collection" in that setting.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word borrows English **collection**. There is no kanji compound to unpack. Keep the Japanese spelling コレクション rather than reconstructing it from the English letters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[集める](集める.md) (あつめる) more broadly means collecting or bringing things together; it can concern information, money, or participants without a hobby collection. [集まり](集まり.md) (あつまり) can name a gathering or collection without the collecting interest suggested by the examples here.

## Distinctive meaning

The community source also lists **"correction."** Digital Daijisen has a separate コレクション heading borrowed from English *correction*, meaning correcting an error. That is a distinct borrowing, **not** another sense in the cited current JMdict collection family.

For ordinary discussion of a correction or revision, [修正](修正.md) (しゅうせい) is useful; 訂正 (ていせい) specifically concerns correcting an error. Do not read "the spring collection" as an act of correcting something merely because the katakana homographs coincide.
