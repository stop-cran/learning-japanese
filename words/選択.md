# 選択 (せんたく) - choice; selection

## Meaning

選択 is choosing, a selection, or an option. 選択する is transitive: the selected item takes を. Use から for the set from which the choice is made.

- 次の三つから一つを選択してください。 (つぎのみっつからひとつをせんたくしてください。) - Please select one of the following three.
- どちらを選ぶかは、あなたの選択です。 (どちらをえらぶかは、あなたのせんたくです。) - Which one to choose is your choice.

選択肢 (せんたくし) is a selectable option, such as an answer in a multiple-choice question. 選択 itself can refer to either the act of choosing or the choice made.

## How the kanji combine

[選](../kanji/選.md) means choosing, and 択 likewise contributes selecting. The two components reinforce the idea rather than describing separate stages of a decision. The reading is セン + タク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[選ぶ](選ぶ.md) (えらぶ) is the native verb "choose." It overlaps with 選択する, whose noun form is convenient in instructions and discussions of options.

[決定](決定.md) (けってい) is a decision or determination. A choice may lead to a decision, but the words emphasize different parts of the process.

## Distinctive meaning

選択 does not promise a good or successful choice. 正しい選択 (ただしいせんたく), "the right choice," adds that evaluation. Do not confuse the homophone [洗濯](洗濯.md) (せんたく), "laundry": the reading alone cannot tell you which activity is intended.
