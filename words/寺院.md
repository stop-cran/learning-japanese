# 寺院 (じいん) - temple; religious building

## Meaning

寺院 names a temple, especially a Buddhist temple. It can also be used more broadly for a religious building, including a church, cathedral, or mosque. It is a noun.

- 京都の古い寺院を訪ねました。 (きょうとのふるいじいんをたずねました。) - I visited an old temple in Kyoto.
- この寺院には長い歴史があります。 (このじいんにはながいれきしがあります。) - This temple has a long history.

## How the kanji combine

[寺](../kanji/寺.md) contributes temple, and [院](../kanji/院.md) contributes an institutional building or establishment. Together they name the religious establishment. The on readings ジ + イン give じいん; pronounce both vowel positions rather than shortening it to じん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[寺](寺.md) てら, often お寺 (おてら), is the everyday Buddhist-temple word. [教会](教会.md) きょうかい normally directs attention to a Christian church. [神社](神社.md) じんじゃ identifies a Shinto shrine. These distinctions are useful without making 寺院 an exclusively Buddhist word in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

The compound itself does not specify the temple's age, size, denomination, or whether visitors may enter. A Buddhist default is not a rule that 寺院 can never refer to another religion's building. Use the named religion and setting to interpret the broader sense.
