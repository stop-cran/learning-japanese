# ころり — with one light roll; plink

## Meaning

ころり can describe a small, light object rolling or falling with one brief movement.

- 坂の上から小さな木の実がころりと転がり、葉の上で止まった。
  — A small nut rolled down the slope and came to rest on a leaf.
- ボタンが机の端から床へころりと落ちた。
  — A button fell with a light roll from the edge of the desk to the floor.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ころり suggests one short, light movement. ころころ more often evokes repeated or continuous
rolling; くるり commonly describes a quick turn.

## Distinctive meaning

The context makes the single, brief movement clear. The dictionary also records adverbial uses
meaning easily or suddenly, as well as a noun sense for a light falling or rolling sound.
