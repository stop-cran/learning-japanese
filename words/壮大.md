# 壮大 (そうだい) — grand in scale; magnificent

## Meaning

壮大 describes something whose scale is impressive: a mountain panorama, for example, or an ambitious plan. Use 壮大な before a noun.

- 目の前に壮大な景色が広がった。 (めのまえにそうだいなけしきがひろがった。) — A magnificent landscape opened up before us.
- 計画は壮大だが、まだ始まっていない。 (けいかくはそうだいだが、まだはじまっていない。) — The plan is grand in scale, but it hasn't started yet.

## How the kanji combine

[壮](../kanji/壮.md) contributes the impression of grandeur; [大](../kanji/大.md) contributes size or scale. The compound evaluates the impression that scale makes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[立派](立派.md) (りっぱ) can praise quality, conduct or appearance without saying anything is vast. 壮大 draws attention to impressive scale.

## Distinctive meaning

The word is not limited to physical size. A plan can be 壮大 before anything has been built; a small, finely made object can be admirable without being 壮大.
