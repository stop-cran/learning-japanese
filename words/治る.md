# 治る (なおる) — recover; heal

## Meaning

治る means to heal, get better, or recover from an illness. It is intransitive: the illness or injury takes が.

- 風邪が治りました。 (かぜがなおりました。) — I've recovered from my cold.
- この傷はもう治っています。 (このきずはもうなおっています。) — This wound has already healed.

It is a godan verb: 治ります (なおります), 治って (なおって), 治らない (なおらない).

## How the kanji combine

[治](../kanji/治.md) supplies the healing association. It is read なお in this native verb, followed by the ending る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

治す (なおす) is transitive: 病気を治す (びょうきをなおす), "cure an illness." 悪くなる (わるくなる), "get worse," gives a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

直る has the same reading but normally concerns repair or correction, such as a broken machine working again. Use 治る for recovery from illness or injury. In the second example, 治っている describes the healed result, not necessarily healing still in progress.
