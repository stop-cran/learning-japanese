# 賛成 (さんせい) — agreement; support

## Meaning

賛成 expresses being in favor of an opinion, proposal, or course of action.
Use 賛成する as a verb, or 賛成です to state a position. Mark what you support with に.
Before a noun, 賛成の意見 (さんせいのいけん) means "an opinion in favor."

- 私はその計画に賛成です。 (わたしはそのけいかくにさんせいです。) — I am in favor of that plan.
- みんなが私の意見に賛成しました。 (みんながわたしのいけんにさんせいしました。) — Everyone agreed with my opinion.

## How the kanji combine

[賛](../kanji/賛.md) carries approval, while [成](../kanji/成.md) is associated with coming about or being accomplished.
The compound as a whole means approval or support. Do not turn those component associations into a claim that the plan has already succeeded.
Both readings are on readings: サン + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

賛同 (さんどう) is approval or endorsement.
[反対](反対.md) (はんたい) provides the opposite stance: その計画に反対です (そのけいかくにはんたいです), "I am opposed to that plan."
Both 賛成 and this use of 反対 take に for the proposal.

## Distinctive meaning

賛成 is taking a supportive position, not merely understanding what someone said.
It also differs from 称賛 (しょうさん), "praise": supporting a proposed action and praising an achievement are different responses.
