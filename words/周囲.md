# 周囲 (しゅうい) - surroundings; circumference

## Meaning

周囲 names the surroundings of a place or object. It can also mean the distance
around something: its circumference or perimeter. It is a noun; use Nの周囲
for "the area around N."

- 学校の周囲には木がたくさんあります。 (がっこうのしゅういにはきがたくさんあります。) - There are many trees around the school.
- この池の周囲は約一キロです。 (このいけのしゅういはやくいちキロです。) - This pond is about one kilometer around.

周囲の人 (しゅういのひと) means people around someone, including the people in
their social environment rather than just those physically standing nearby.

## How the kanji combine

周 contributes going around a circuit, and 囲 contributes surrounding or enclosing.
Their related meanings combine into the area or boundary around something.
The readings are シュウ + イ; keep the long vowel before い.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[回り](回り.md) (まわり) also refers to the area around something.
周辺 (しゅうへん) concerns the surrounding vicinity. In a measurement such as
the pond example, 周囲 identifies the distance all the way around, not just a
nearby neighborhood.

## Distinctive meaning

Context separates surroundings from circumference. Trees in the 周囲 are in an
area; a 周囲 measured in meters is a length. Do not interpret every instance as
a mathematical term or assume that 周囲の人 must be positioned in a literal
circle.
