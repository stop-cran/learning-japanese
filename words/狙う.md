# 狙う (ねらう) - aim at; aim for

## Meaning

狙う means aiming at a target, seeking something or setting a goal to achieve.
It is a transitive godan verb:
狙います (ねらいます), 狙った (ねらった),
狙って (ねらって), 狙わない (ねらわない).
The target takes を.

- カメラで鳥を狙いました。
  (カメラでとりをねらいました。) - I aimed the camera at the bird.
- 次の大会では優勝を狙っています。
  (つぎのたいかいではゆうしょうをねらっています。) - I am aiming to win the next tournament.

The first describes directing the camera toward the bird.
The second describes pursuing a result, not pointing a weapon at a trophy.
獲物を狙う (えものをねらう), "be after prey," illustrates seeking a target.

## How the kanji combine

狙 supplies ねら and う is okurigana.
The related noun [狙い](狙い.md) (ねらい) names the aim, target or intention.
The verb's って form has the godan consonant change.

## Synonyms and antonyms

目指す (めざす), "aim for," overlaps in pursuing a goal.
[望む](望む.md) (のぞむ), "wish for; desire," and
[希望](希望.md)する (きぼうする), "hope for; wish for,"
can concern the same desired outcome, but wishing does not necessarily
describe directing one's efforts toward it.
[諦める](諦める.md) (あきらめる), "give up," contrasts with pursuit in a suitable context.

## Distinctive meaning

The same verb covers literal aiming, being after something and aiming for success.
Choose the interpretation from the object.
狙っている does not assert that the goal has been achieved,
and it does not automatically make the intention suspicious or dishonest.
The desired-result uses can overlap with hoping or planning without being
interchangeable in every construction.
