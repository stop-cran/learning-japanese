# 予報 (よほう) - forecast

## Meaning

予報 is a forecast or prediction communicated in advance, especially about weather. It is a noun and a transitive する-verb. 天気予報 (てんきよほう) means a weather forecast.

- 天気予報によると、明日は雨です。 (てんきよほうによると、あしたはあめです。) - According to the weather forecast, it will rain tomorrow.
- 気象台は強い風を予報しています。 (きしょうだいはつよいかぜをよほうしています。) - The meteorological observatory is forecasting strong winds.

によると identifies the information source in the first example. In the second, を marks what is forecast.

## How the kanji combine

[予](../kanji/予.md) contributes beforehand, and [報](../kanji/報.md) contributes reporting or informing. Together they suggest informing people in advance. Read ヨ + ホウ, with a long ほう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予想](予想.md) (よそう) is an expectation or prediction and can be a personal guess. [予測](予測.md) (よそく) also concerns predicting what will happen. [報告](報告.md) (ほうこく) means a report and need not concern the future.

## Distinctive meaning

A 予報 states what is expected, not what is guaranteed. 予報が当たる (よほうがあたる) means a forecast proves accurate; 予報が外れる (よほうがはずれる) means it is wrong. The weather context makes the word particularly useful, but its meaning is not the same as the actual weather observed afterward.
