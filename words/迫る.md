# 迫る (せまる) - approach; press for

## Meaning

迫る can mean drawing near or becoming imminent, physically or in time.
It can also mean pressing someone strongly for something or compelling them.
The approach sense is intransitive; the pressing sense can take the demanded thing with を.

- 締め切りが迫っています。 (しめきりがせまっています。) - The deadline is approaching.
- 彼は私に返事を迫りました。 (かれはわたしにへんじをせまりました。) - He pressed me for an answer.

In the first, the deadline takes が. In 人に返事を迫る, に identifies the person pressed,
and を identifies the answer demanded.
This is a godan verb despite ending in る:
迫ります (せまります), 迫って (せまって), 迫らない (せまらない).

## How the kanji combine

迫 supplies せま in this native verb, followed by the written ending る.
There is no two-kanji compound to split.
逼る is a rare alternative spelling; the dictionary's obsolete reading せる
does not replace the primary せまる taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[接近](接近.md) (せっきん) and [寄る](寄る.md) (よる) overlap in drawing near.
[催促](催促.md) (さいそく) emphasizes urging an awaited action;
[要求](要求.md) (ようきゅう) names a demand or requirement.
[責める](責める.md) (せめる) also has an urging/pressing use besides blame.

## Distinctive meaning

A deadline approaching and a person demanding an answer use different constructions,
not one literal act of pushing.
必要に迫られる (ひつようにせまられる) means being compelled by necessity.
That passive phrase does not require another person physically pressing against someone.
Likewise, an approaching event has not necessarily happened yet.
