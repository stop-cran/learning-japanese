# 形式 (けいしき) — form; format

## Meaning

形式 is form or format: how something is arranged or presented, as distinct from its substance.
It can refer to a document's format, the way an activity is organized, or the outward form something takes.

- この形式で書いてください。 (このけいしきでかいてください。) — Please write it in this format.
- 形式より内容が大切です。 (けいしきよりないようがたいせつです。) — The content matters more than the form.

## How the kanji combine

[形](../kanji/形.md) contributes shape or form, and [式](../kanji/式.md) contributes a style, pattern, or method.
The related meanings combine into the form in which something is organized or expressed.
The on readings are ケイ + シキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

形 (かたち) also means "shape; form," including physical shape.
形式 is useful for an arrangement or format, as in the writing instruction above; it is not the default word for the outline of every object.
[内容](内容.md) (ないよう), "content; substance," is the useful contrast when discussing form versus what is expressed.
It is not an opposite in every possible sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

形式 does not inherently criticize something as empty or superficial.
The first example simply asks for a particular format.
In the second, the speaker explicitly values content more highly; that judgment comes from the sentence, not from 形式 alone.
