# 受話器 (じゅわき) - telephone receiver; handset

## Meaning

受話器 is a telephone receiver, including the handset held to one's ear on a conventional telephone.

- 受話器を取って、名前を言いました。 (じゅわきをとって、なまえをいいました。) - I picked up the receiver and said my name.
- 話が終わったら、受話器を置いてください。 (はなしがおわったら、じゅわきをおいてください。) - When the conversation ends, please put down the receiver.

受話器を取る and 受話器を置く describe handling that device; the surrounding situation establishes answering or ending the call.

## How the kanji combine

[受](../kanji/受.md) concerns receiving, [話](../kanji/話.md) speech, and 器 a device or implement. Together they provide an association with the device that receives speech. Read じゅわき, not a guessed reading based on the independent word 話 (はなし).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[電話](電話.md) can name a telephone or a phone call. 受話器 specifically identifies the receiving device or handset, not the entire telephone system or the call itself. A smartphone is not automatically called a 受話器.

## Distinctive meaning

The English word "receiver" has uses in radio, deliveries, and other fields; 受話器 is not a general translation of all of them. Dictionary search spellings do not justify replacing the normal final 器 with 機 in ordinary writing.
