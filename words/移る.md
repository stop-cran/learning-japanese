# 移る (うつる) — move to another place

## Meaning

移る means to move or transfer to a different place, organization, or state. In its basic intransitive pattern, the person or thing moving takes が, the starting point can take から, and the destination takes に.

- 家族が大阪に移りました。  
  かぞくがおおさかにうつりました。  
  "My family moved to Osaka."
- 来月、別の部署に移ります。  
  らいげつ、べつのぶしょにうつります。  
  "I'll transfer to another department next month."

## How the kanji combine

移 carries the native reading うつ; る is okurigana. It is godan: 移ります (うつります), 移って (うつって), 移らない (うつらない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

引っ越す (ひっこす) specifically describes moving one's home or premises. 移す (うつす) is the transitive partner: 席を移す (せきをうつす), "move one's seat". No single opposite covers every kind of transfer.

## Distinctive meaning

移る emphasizes a change from one location or setting to another, not movement of any kind. 歩く (あるく), "walk", describes a manner of movement; 移る does not say how the move was made.
