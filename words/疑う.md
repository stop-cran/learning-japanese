# 疑う (うたがう) — doubt; suspect

## Meaning

疑う is to doubt a claim, distrust a person, or suspect that something is wrong.
It is transitive: を marks the person or thing treated with doubt.
The polite form is 疑います (うたがいます); the past is 疑った (うたがった).

- 私はその説明を疑っています。 (わたしはそのせつめいをうたがっています。) — I doubt that explanation.
- 友達を疑いたくありません。 (ともだちをうたがいたくありません。) — I do not want to distrust my friend.

The second example shows why this can be a stronger interpersonal statement than merely having a question.

## How the kanji combine

[疑](../kanji/疑.md) is read うたが in 疑う; the final う is written in kana.
This is one native verb, not a compound pronounced with the on reading ギ.
When conjugating, keep the kanji and change the ending: 疑わない (うたがわない), "do not doubt."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[信じる](信じる.md) しんじる, "believe; trust," offers a direct contrast in attitude.
疑問 (ぎもん) is a noun for a doubt or question.
[質問](質問.md) しつもん is a question or inquiry; asking one does not necessarily mean that you distrust the answerer.

## Distinctive meaning

疑う reports suspicion or lack of belief, not proof that the suspicion is correct.
疑っている describes an ongoing attitude of doubt; it is not limited to repeated acts of asking questions.
Choose the object carefully: doubting an explanation and distrusting the person giving it are related but different claims.
