# 耳 (みみ) — ear

## Meaning

耳 is the ear and the sense of hearing: 耳が痛い (idiom: "that hits home", a remark is uncomfortably true), 耳がいい (have good hearing), 耳を澄ます みみをすます (listen closely), 耳にする (hear about).
It also means the rim or margin of something: パンの耳 (bread crust).

## How the kanji combine

A single kanji, [耳](../kanji/耳.md), with the kun reading みみ. The on reading ジ is found in 耳鼻科 じびか (ENT clinic) and 中耳炎 ちゅうじえん (middle-ear infection).
The kanji is also a part of [聞](../kanji/聞.md) (hear), which has 耳 inside the gate 門.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 聴覚 ちょうかく (sense of hearing), 耳たぶ みみたぶ (earlobe).
- Related: [目](目.md) め (eye).

## Distinctive meaning

耳 is the ordinary word for the ear. 耳が痛い is normally the idiom "that hits home"; for an aching ear say 耳が痛む.
