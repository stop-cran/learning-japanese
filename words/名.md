# 名 (な) - name; renown

## Meaning

名 means a name and can also mean fame or reputation. The reading taught here is な. It is a noun: 名を聞く (なをきく), "ask a name," or 名が知られている (ながしられている), "the name is known."

- その花の名を知っていますか。 (そのはなのなをしっていますか。) - Do you know the name of that flower?
- 彼は作家として名を上げました。 (かれはさっかとしてなをあげました。) - He made a name for himself as a writer.

名を上げる (なをあげる) is a reputation expression, not literally raising written letters.

## How the kanji combine

The single [名](../kanji/名.md) uses the native reading な. On readings occur in other constructions, such as 三名 (さんめい), "three people." That counting use is not read さんな.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[名前](名前.md) (なまえ) is a useful general word for a name. [評判](評判.md) (ひょうばん) concerns reputation or what people say about someone or something. It does not replace 名 when asking a flower's name.

## Distinctive meaning

名 alone does not mean that someone is famous: every named person has a name. Expressions such as 名を上げる supply the fame meaning. [有名](有名.md) (ゆうめい), "famous," is a different grammatical form, so it cannot be substituted into 名を聞く while preserving the sentence.
