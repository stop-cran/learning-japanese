# 観測 (かんそく) - observation; prediction

## Meaning

観測 is observation or measurement, for example of stars or weather.
観測する is transitive: the object observed takes を.
The noun also names a view, assessment, or prediction about a situation.

- 研究者たちが星を観測しています。
  (けんきゅうしゃたちがほしをかんそくしています。) - Researchers are observing the stars.
- 来年は売り上げが伸びるという観測もあります。
  (らいねんはうりあげがのびるというかんそくもあります。) - There is also a prediction that sales will grow next year.

In the second example, clause + という観測 identifies the prediction's
content. It does not say that next year's sales have already been measured.

## How the kanji combine

[観](../kanji/観.md) contributes observing, and
[測](../kanji/測.md) measuring or estimating.
The related actions combine in a word for observation and assessment.
The on readings are カン + ソク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[観察](観察.md) (かんさつ) emphasizes watching something's features
or behavior. [調査](調査.md) (ちょうさ) is investigation or survey.
[予測](予測.md) (よそく) overlaps in prediction.
These are contextual distinctions, not a rule that only one word can
describe a given research activity.

## Distinctive meaning

A scientific 観測 can provide evidence; a political or commercial
観測 can be someone's assessment of what will happen.
Do not translate every occurrence as instrument measurement or treat
every reported 観測 as an established fact.
The topic and surrounding construction identify the intended sense.
