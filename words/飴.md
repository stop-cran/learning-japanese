# 飴 (あめ) — candy

## Meaning

飴 is candy, especially a piece of hard candy that you suck. It is a noun; count individual pieces with 個 (こ) when a counter is needed.

飴を一つください。 (あめをひとつください。) — Please give me one candy.

## How the kanji combine

飴 is a single kanji read あめ here, not a compound. The kana spelling あめ is also easy to recognize. In words such as 水飴 (みずあめ), the meaning concerns a thick sweet syrup rather than an individually wrapped candy.

## Synonyms and antonyms

キャンディー overlaps with 飴. お菓子 (おかし) is broader: it includes cakes, biscuits, and other snacks as well as candy. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 飴 with 雨, "rain," which is also written あめ in kana. Kanji and context distinguish the words in writing; their pitch patterns also differ in standard Tokyo speech. 飴 is not the general word for every sweet food.
