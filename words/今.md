# 今 (いま) — now

## Meaning

今 means "now" or "the present time". Its exact span comes from context: the current moment, the current situation, or something about to happen.

- 今、何をしていますか。  
  いま、なにをしていますか。  
  "What are you doing now?"
- 今、行きます。  
  いま、いきます。  
  "I'm coming now." (About to move toward the listener.)

As a time adverb it normally needs no に. Use の before a noun: 今の仕事 (いまのしごと), "my current job".

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) is a single kanji with the native reading いま. Do not carry this reading automatically into compounds: [今日](今日.md), "today", is read きょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

現在 (げんざい), "the present; currently", is a more formal alternative in many contexts. 昔 (むかし), "long ago", contrasts the present with the past.

## Distinctive meaning

今 does not itself choose a tense. The verb and situation tell you whether the sentence concerns what is happening now, just happened, or will happen immediately.
