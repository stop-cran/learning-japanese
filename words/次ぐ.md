# 次ぐ (つぐ) - follow; rank next

## Meaning

次ぐ means to follow, come next after, or rank next to something.
It is an intransitive godan verb. Use Nに次ぐ for following or ranking
immediately after N.

- 東京に次ぐ大都市です。
  (とうきょうにつぐだいとしです。) - It is a major city ranking next to Tokyo.
- 開会式に次いで、最初の試合が始まりました。
  (かいかいしきについで、さいしょのしあいがはじまりました。) - After the opening ceremony, the first match began.

Forms include 次ぎます, 次いだ, 次いで, and 次がない.
The ぐ ending gives いで in the て-form, not って.

## How the kanji combine

[次](../kanji/次.md) contributes nextness and is read つ here;
ぐ is okurigana. The related noun [次](次.md) is read つぎ.
The readings belong to different word forms, not interchangeable pronunciations.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[続く](続く.md) (つづく) can also mean follow or come next, besides continuing.
[従う](従う.md) (したがう) includes following, but also obeying.
次ぐ highlights sequence or rank rather than compliance with an instruction.
Being first rather than following is a contextual contrast, not a universal antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The second sentence uses 次いで to connect events in sequence.
The dictionary also records 次いで (ついで), "next; subsequently,"
as its own adverbial entry. Do not confuse it with
[ついで](ついで.md), also written 序で, an opportunity to do something
while doing something else.
Ranking next to one city in the relevant comparison does not independently
establish a city's rank under every population or area measure.
