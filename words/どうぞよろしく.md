# どうぞよろしく - pleased to meet you; my regards

## Meaning

どうぞよろしく is a greeting used in introductions and in sending regards.
Its English translation depends on whom you are addressing.

- 初めまして、田中です。どうぞよろしくお願いします。
  (はじめまして、たなかです。どうぞよろしくおねがいします。) - How do you do? I'm Tanaka. I look forward to getting to know you.
- ご家族にどうぞよろしくお伝えください。
  (ごかぞくにどうぞよろしくおつたえください。) - Please give my regards to your family.

In the second example, に marks the people who should receive the regards.
お願いします extends the formula in the introduction; it is not necessary
to translate each part with a separate English word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[宜しく](宜しく.md) (よろしく) supplies the core favorable-regards expression.
[どうぞ](どうぞ.md) adds a polite invitation or encouragement;
it does not mean "meet" on its own. [お願いします](お願いします.md)
(おねがいします) is a request formula also used in the longer greeting.
Greetings have no single useful lexical opposite here.

## Distinctive meaning

An introduction use does not make this expression a description of meeting
someone physically. Nor does the regards example mean "nice to meet your family":
it asks the listener to pass on a greeting. Kana is usual; どうぞ宜しく is
also recorded, with the same reading.
