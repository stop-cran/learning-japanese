# 弱点 (じゃくてん) - weak point; weakness

## Meaning

弱点 is a weak point, weakness, or shortcoming in a person, plan, system, or other thing. It can identify an area of poor ability or a vulnerability that someone could exploit.

- この計画には大きな弱点があります。
  (このけいかくにはおおきなじゃくてんがあります。) - This plan has a major weakness.
- 相手の弱点を見つけました。
  (あいてのじゃくてんをみつけました。) - I found the opponent's weak point.

弱点を克服する (じゃくてんをこくふくする) means "overcome a weakness." 弱点がある states that there is a weak point; it is not a な-adjective construction.

## How the kanji combine

[弱](../kanji/弱.md) contributes weakness, and [点](../kanji/点.md) a point or aspect. The relation is modifier plus head: a weak point. Read ジャク + テン as **じゃくてん**, not じゃってん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[欠点](欠点.md) (けってん), "fault; shortcoming," overlaps, while [欠陥](欠陥.md) (けっかん) often identifies a defect or deficiency. 弱点 need not be a manufacturing defect: a strategy can have a vulnerable point.

[苦手](苦手.md) (にがて) concerns something one is poor at or finds difficult, and can identify a personal weakness. An advantage or strong point contrasts with a 弱点; simply being physically strong does not eliminate every possible weakness.

## Distinctive meaning

The "point" is not necessarily a visible dot or a location on the body. An opponent's weak subject, a security vulnerability, and a flaw in an argument can all be 弱点. The word does not by itself say whether the weakness is permanent or easy to fix.
