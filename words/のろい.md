# のろい (のろい) - slow; slow-witted

## Meaning

のろい describes slow, sluggish movement or action. It can also mean slow-witted or obtuse. It is an i-adjective, usually written in kana; **鈍い** is a recognized spelling for this reading too.

- この車は動きがのろいです。 (このくるまはうごきがのろいです。) - This vehicle moves sluggishly.
- 彼は反応がのろいです。 (かれははんのうがのろいです。) - He is slow to react.

Forms include のろく動く (のろくうごく), "move slowly," のろかった, and のろくない. Calling a person's movement or understanding のろい can sound critical; it is not a neutral compliment about taking time.

## How the kanji combine

The spelling [鈍](../kanji/鈍.md)い associates the word with dullness or slow responsiveness. It does not mean that every use of the separate reading にぶい belongs to のろい. The kana primary spelling keeps these two article identities distinct.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[遅い](遅い.md) (おそい) can concern either slowness or lateness; のろい emphasizes sluggishness rather than simply arriving late. [のろのろ](のろのろ.md) describes the sluggish manner of movement or progress. [鈍い](鈍い.md) (にぶい) overlaps in slow perception or response but also has other senses.

## Distinctive meaning

A knife's blunt edge is taught under **鈍い（にぶい）**, not imported into this のろい article merely because the kanji match. Nor is のろい the noun 呪い (のろい), "curse." Learn the spelling, grammatical role, and context together rather than treating every のろい as one word.
