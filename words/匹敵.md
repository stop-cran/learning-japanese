# 匹敵 (ひってき) - be a match for; equal

## Meaning

匹敵する means to be a match for, rival, or equal someone or something in a relevant quality. It is an intransitive する-verb. In AはBに匹敵する, B is the comparison standard; do not replace に with a direct-object を.

- 彼に匹敵する選手はほかにいません。 (かれにひってきするせんしゅはほかにいません。) - No other player is a match for him.
- この小さな機械は、大きな機械に匹敵する性能を持っています。 (このちいさなきかいは、おおきなきかいにひってきするせいのうをもっています。) - This small machine has performance to match that of a large machine.

In 匹敵する選手, the verb phrase modifies 選手: a player who can match the person being discussed.

## How the kanji combine

[匹](../kanji/匹.md) contributes matching or equality; 敵 (テキ) contributes an opponent or rival. The combined idea is being a match for a counterpart. ヒツ + テキ becomes ひってき, with a small っ; the animal-counter reading ひき is not used here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[等しい](等しい.md) (ひとしい) and [相当](相当.md) (そうとう) can express equality or equivalence, with different constructions and additional senses. [同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ), "same," can also express equality in a specified respect; a match in performance does not mean identity in every property.

[同様](同様.md) (どうよう) describes sameness or similarity in a relevant respect; [同一](同一.md) (どういつ) identifies sameness or identity. Their broader English equality labels can overlap, but matching someone in ability does not make the two people identical.

[劣る](劣る.md) (おとる), "be inferior," provides a contrast when comparing the same quality. [勝つ](勝つ.md) (かつ), "win," describes a result that 匹敵する does not assert.

## Distinctive meaning

匹敵する compares quality, ability, value, or another relevant property. It does not require an actual contest, and being a match for someone does not say that one has defeated them. State the dimension of comparison when context does not make it clear.
