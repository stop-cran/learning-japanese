# 時計 (とけい) — clock; watch

## Meaning

時計 is a device that tells the time: a clock or a watch. The word alone does not specify whether it is
on a wall, on a desk, or on someone's wrist.

- この時計は新しいです。（このとけいはあたらしいです。） — This clock is new.
- 時計を見てください。（とけいをみてください。） — Please look at the clock.

## How the kanji combine

[時](../kanji/時.md) contributes the idea of time and [計](../kanji/計.md) that of measuring:
the spelling suggests a time-measuring device. However, とけい is **not a regular on+on reading**.
ケイ is a regular reading of 計, but と is not the regular on-reading of 時.

Learn the whole word as とけい. JMdict marks this spelling as ateji; the card uses the app's jukujikun category
to group this irregular whole-word reading, not to claim that と is a general reading you can transfer to other words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

腕時計（うでどけい） specifically means a wristwatch and is narrower than 時計.
There is no direct antonym. [時間](時間.md) じかん means time or duration, not the device that measures it.

## Distinctive meaning

English distinguishes "clock" and "watch," but 時計 can cover both.
If the wrist-worn form matters, say 腕時計. Note the voiced ど in うでどけい, compared with とけい on its own.
