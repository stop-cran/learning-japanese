# 翻訳 (ほんやく) — translation

## Meaning

翻訳 means translation. It forms the verb 翻訳する, "translate." The text takes を and the language translated into takes に.

この手紙を日本語に翻訳してください。 (このてがみをにほんごにほんやくしてください。) — Please translate this letter into Japanese.

英語から日本語に翻訳します。 (えいごからにほんごにほんやくします。) — I translate from English into Japanese.

## How the kanji combine

翻 contributes turning or changing, and [訳](../kanji/訳.md) contributes translating or interpreting meaning. The on readings are ホン + ヤク. Learn the established compound rather than treating translation as a literal physical turning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

訳す (やくす) is the everyday verb "translate." 通訳 (つうやく) is interpreting, especially spoken communication between people using different languages.

## Distinctive meaning

翻訳 is the ordinary choice for translating written material. It can name the process or the resulting translation: 翻訳を読む (ほんやくをよむ), "read a translation." Translating conveys meaning across languages; it is not the same as merely transcribing sounds into another writing system.
