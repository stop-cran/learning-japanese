# 重要 (じゅうよう) - important; significant

## Meaning

重要 is a な-adjective for something important, significant, or central to a result. Use 重要な before a noun, as in 重要な問題 (じゅうようなもんだい), "an important issue," and 重要です as a predicate.

- これは重要な情報です。 (これはじゅうようなじょうほうです。) - This is important information.
- 約束を守ることが重要です。 (やくそくをまもることがじゅうようです。) - Keeping promises is important.

A clause in noun-modifying form + ことが重要です lets you describe an action or principle as important.
Before こと, plain verbs and i-adjectives attach directly. Affirmative nonpast na-adjectives
use な or formal である; noun predicates can use である, not unchanged だ.
Past and negative predicates keep their endings. See the [こと attachment guide](../articles/koto-wake-and-formal-nouns.md).
重要ではない is the corresponding negative.

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) contributes weight in the figurative sense of importance. [要](../kanji/要.md) contributes a key point or necessity. Together they reinforce significance; the word does not describe physical heaviness.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大切](大切.md) (たいせつ) overlaps with "important" and can emphasize cherishing or caring for something. [貴重](貴重.md) (きちょう) emphasizes preciousness or value. An important routine step need not be rare or irreplaceable.

## Distinctive meaning

重要 suits explaining why something matters to a decision, task, or system. It is not limited to impersonal facts, but it does not by itself express affection. For "someone important to me," the choice between 重要 and 大切 changes the emphasis. 重要性 (じゅうようせい) is the noun "importance/significance."
