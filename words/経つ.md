# 経つ (たつ) - elapse; pass of time

## Meaning

経つ means that time passes or elapses. It is an intransitive godan verb: 経ちます, 経って, 経った. Time can be the subject with が, or a duration can appear directly before the verb.

- あれから三年が経ちました。 (あれからさんねんがたちました。) - Three years have passed since then.
- しばらく経ってから、もう一度電話しました。 (しばらくたってから、もういちどでんわしました。) - After a while had passed, I called again.

～てから marks the starting point when attached to an earlier event: 日本に来てから (にほんにきてから), "since coming to Japan."

## How the kanji combine

[経](../kanji/経.md) contributes passing through or undergoing. Here its native reading た combines with the ending つ. The past form 経った sounds like たった; the dictionary spelling keeps the meaning visible.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[過ぎる](過ぎる.md) (すぎる) also describes time passing, among other uses. [過ごす](過ごす.md) (すごす) is transitive and describes how someone spends time: 週末を過ごす, "spend the weekend."

## Distinctive meaning

経つ does not describe a person passing a building or passing an examination. Its subject here is time. Also distinguish [立つ](立つ.md), "stand," and 発つ, "depart," both read たつ. The expression 時間が経つ (じかんがたつ) is about elapsed time, not money spent or a person's movement.
