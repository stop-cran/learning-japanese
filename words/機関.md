# 機関 (きかん) - institution; engine; system

## Meaning

機関 is a noun with several connected but distinct uses: an engine, an agency or institution, and an organized system or facility.

- 近くの医療機関に相談してください。 (ちかくのいりょうきかんにそうだんしてください。) - Please consult a nearby medical institution.
- 通勤には公共交通機関を使います。 (つうきんにはこうきょうこうつうきかんをつかいます。) - I use public transportation to commute.

蒸気機関 (じょうききかん), "steam engine," illustrates the mechanical meaning. 医療機関 concerns an institution providing care; 公共交通機関 concerns public transportation services and systems.

## How the kanji combine

[機](../kanji/機.md) contributes machinery or a functioning mechanism. [関](../kanji/関.md) contributes connection or involvement. Together they name an operating mechanism or an organized body performing a function. This is a semantic guide, not a historical derivation. The readings are キ + カン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[機械](機械.md) (きかい) means a machine, but does not substitute for a government agency. [組織](組織.md) (そしき) means an organization or structure and overlaps with the institutional use.

## Distinctive meaning

An English dictionary may gloss 機関 as "organ." Here that means an institutional body, such as an organ of government, not a heart or lung. The surrounding compound identifies which meaning is intended; do not read 医療機関 as a bodily organ merely because it concerns medicine.
