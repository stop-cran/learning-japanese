# 垣根 (かきね) - hedge; fence; barrier

## Meaning

垣根 is a hedge or fence forming an enclosure or boundary. It can also name a figurative barrier or dividing line.

- 庭の垣根を直しました。 (にわのかきねをなおしました。) - I repaired the garden fence.
- 国籍の垣根を越えて交流しました。 (こくせきのかきねをこえてこうりゅうしました。) - We interacted across barriers of nationality.

The second concerns a social division, not necessarily a physical fence at a border.

## How the kanji combine

垣 contributes a fence or enclosure; [根](../kanji/根.md) supplies the final ね of this established native compound. The reading is かき + ね. 根 often means "root" elsewhere, but 垣根 is not simply a literal name for the roots of hedge plants; learn the complete word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[塀](塀.md) (へい) is a boundary wall or fence. A 垣根 can consist of plants, but the word is not restricted to living hedges.

[境](境.md) (さかい) is a boundary or dividing line more generally. A fence can mark that boundary without being identical to every kind of boundary.

## Distinctive meaning

In figurative uses such as 分野の垣根を越える (ぶんやのかきねをこえる), "cross the boundaries between fields," identify what is being separated. Do not interpret the phrase as physical jumping merely because 越える can describe crossing a fence.
