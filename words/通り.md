# 通り (とおり) - street; as stated

## Meaning

通り (とおり) is a street or road. It also follows an expression to mean
"the same way as" or "as stated." These uses need different sentence patterns.

- この通りには店がたくさんあります。 (このとおりにはみせがたくさんあります。) - There are many shops on this street.
- 説明した通りに、紙を折ってください。 (せつめいしたとおりに、かみをおってください。) - Please fold the paper as explained.

For the second use, attach とおり to a plain verb, such as 言うとおり or
説明したとおり, or to noun + の: 説明のとおり (せつめいのとおり).
に connects the pattern to how an action is performed.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) concerns passing through. In this word it is read とお,
followed by り. Keep とおり distinct from the related voiced suffix どおり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[道](道.md) (みち) and [道路](道路.md) (どうろ) also refer to roads.
They do not replace the "as explained" construction.
そのとおりです means "That is right; exactly so," agreeing with what was said.

## Distinctive meaning

The suffix どおり follows nouns directly in forms such as 予定通り
(よていどおり), "as planned," and 大通り (おおどおり), "main street."
Compare 説明のとおり with 説明どおり: do not simply pronounce every written
通り the same way. JMdict records the どおり suffix separately; it is a
construction comparison here, not a substitute for the assigned とおり reading.
