# ポスター - poster

## Meaning

ポスター is a poster, such as a printed sheet displayed to advertise, announce, or decorate.

- 駅に映画のポスターが貼ってあります。 (えきにえいがのポスターがはってあります。) - A film poster is displayed at the station.
- 部屋の壁にポスターを貼りました。 (へやのかべにポスターをはりました。) - I put a poster on my room's wall.

Use ポスターを貼る (ポスターをはる), "put up a poster," or ポスターを作る (ポスターをつくる), "make a poster." JMdict also records ポスタ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[広告](広告.md) (こうこく) is an advertisement or advertising; a poster can be one medium for it. [掲示](掲示.md) (けいじ) concerns posting or displaying a notice.

[看板](看板.md) (かんばん) is a signboard and has additional figurative meanings. It need not be a printed poster.

## Distinctive meaning

ポスター names the displayed item, not every act of advertising or every notice. A decorative poster need not advertise a product, and a notice board is not automatically a poster.
