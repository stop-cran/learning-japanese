# しゃきしゃき — fresh and crisp; brisk

## Meaning

A fresh, firm crunch, especially in vegetables. It can also describe brisk, precise, energetic movement or manner.

- このもやしは、しゃきしゃきしている。 — These bean sprouts are crisp and crunchy.
- レタスのしゃきしゃきした食感（しょっかん）が好きだ。 — I like lettuce's fresh, crisp texture.
- 彼女は朝からしゃきしゃきと働いている。 — She has been working briskly since morning.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic repeating しゃき. The small ゃ is part of しゃ, not a separate full-sized syllable. There are no written kanji to combine.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[さくさく](さくさく.md) often suggests dry, light crispness. [こりこり](こりこり.md) emphasizes firmer resistance, and [ぱりぱり](ぱりぱり.md) brittle layers. These are useful prototypes, not exclusive food categories.

## Distinctive meaning

しゃきしゃきした野菜（やさい）, しゃきしゃきする, and しゃきしゃきと働く are useful. The food sense often suggests freshness, but the word is not an objective guarantee of freshness or safety. With people, it concerns briskness rather than literal crunching or simply moving fast without purpose.
