# お代わり (おかわり) - another helping; refill

## Meaning

お代わり means another helping of food or a refill of a drink.
It is usually written おかわり. おかわりする means to have another helping.

- ご飯をおかわりしてもいいですか。 (ごはんをおかわりしてもいいですか。) - May I have another helping of rice?
- コーヒーのおかわりをお願いします。 (コーヒーのおかわりをおねがいします。) - I'd like a refill of coffee, please.

ご飯 takes を with おかわりする. In the second example,
コーヒーの identifies what the requested refill is of.

## How the kanji combine

お precedes [代わり](代わり.md) (かわり).
[代](../kanji/代.md) helps recognize the written form.
お替わり is another dictionary spelling of the same word.
Learn the established food-and-drink use rather than translating it
as any possible "replacement."

## Synonyms and antonyms

もう一杯 (もういっぱい), "another cup/glass," is useful for drinks,
while もう一度 (もういちど) means "once again" more generally.
Neither expression says that a refill is free.

## Distinctive meaning

おかわり is not restricted to exactly the second helping.
In a different setting it is a dog command meaning "other paw!"
That interjection is a separate dictionary sense, not the meaning
of a food request at the table.
