# 尊重 (そんちょう) - respect; due regard

## Meaning

尊重 is a noun for respect or regard. 尊重する is a transitive verb meaning to treat someone or something as important and worthy of consideration. Mark the respected person, view, choice, or right with を.

- 相手の意見を尊重しましょう。 (あいてのいけんをそんちょうしましょう。) - Let's respect the other person's opinion.
- 両親は私の決定を尊重してくれました。 (りょうしんはわたしのけっていをそんちょうしてくれました。) - My parents respected my decision.

尊重される is the passive, "be respected." It describes how something is treated, not simply whether it is admired.

## How the kanji combine

尊 contributes honoring or valuing, and [重](../kanji/重.md) contributes weight or importance. Together they suggest giving something proper weight. Here 重 is read ちょう, producing そんちょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[尊敬](尊敬.md) (そんけい) concerns esteem or admiration, especially for a person. 尊重 is particularly useful for acknowledging opinions, rights, and decisions. [無視](無視.md) (むし), "ignoring," provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Respecting an opinion does not necessarily mean agreeing with it. 意見を尊重する can mean taking it seriously and allowing it due consideration while retaining a different view. This makes 尊重 useful in discussions of disagreement, cooperation, and personal choice rather than just praise for impressive achievements.
