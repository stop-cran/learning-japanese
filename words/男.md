# 男 (おとこ) - man; male

## Meaning

男 means a man or a male person. In ordinary polite descriptions, 男の人 (おとこのひと), "a man," is often a more comfortable choice than referring to someone as simply 男.

- あの男の人は先生です。 (あのおとこのひとはせんせいです。) - That man is a teacher.

男の子 (おとこのこ) means a boy. The whole expression matters: 子 supplies the child meaning.

## How the kanji combine

[男](../kanji/男.md) is a single character representing the native word おとこ. In compounds such as 男性 (だんせい), the character uses a different reading; do not substitute だん for the standalone word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

男性 (だんせい) is a more formal term for a man or male. 女 (おんな) and 女の人 (おんなのひと) provide the corresponding female-person contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

男 is descriptive vocabulary, not a general way to address a stranger. Bare 男 can sound blunt in reference to a person; use a name, role, or context-appropriate expression when speaking to someone.
