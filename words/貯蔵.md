# 貯蔵 (ちょぞう) - storage; stockpiling

## Meaning

貯蔵 is storage or stockpiling: keeping goods or other resources in reserve.
貯蔵する is transitive: what is stored takes を,
and the storage place can take に.

- 冬に備えて食料を貯蔵します。
  (ふゆにそなえてしょくりょうをちょぞうします。) - We store food in preparation for winter.
- このタンクには水を貯蔵しています。
  (このタンクにはみずをちょぞうしています。) - We store water in this tank.

貯蔵庫 (ちょぞうこ) is a storehouse or storage facility.

## How the kanji combine

[貯](../kanji/貯.md) contributes storing up, and
[蔵](../kanji/蔵.md) keeping in storage.
The related meanings reinforce keeping a stock.
Read チョ + ゾウ, not the separate native storehouse word くら.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[保存](保存.md) (ほぞん) overlaps with preservation and storage.
[蓄える](蓄える.md) (たくわえる) and
[溜める](溜める.md) (ためる) overlap with storing up.
[しまう](しまう.md), [収める](収める.md) (おさめる),
and [納める](納める.md) (おさめる) have putting-away or storage uses.
[残す](残す.md) (のこす) can mean setting something aside.
The object and construction determine the best word.

## Distinctive meaning

貯蔵 does not itself guarantee correct preservation conditions.
[預かる](預かる.md) (あずかる) emphasizes custody or responsibility,
while [維持](維持.md) (いじ) emphasizes preserving a condition.
[貯金](貯金.md) (ちょきん) specifically concerns saving money.
The dictionary additionally records unproductive hoarding of goods or money;
that is not a necessary implication of ordinary food or water storage.
