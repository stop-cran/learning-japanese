# 裁判 (さいばん) - trial; judicial judgment

## Meaning

裁判 is a noun for a trial or judicial judgment. 裁判する means to try or judge a case. In everyday learning, useful noun patterns include 裁判を受ける (さいばんをうける), "stand trial," and 裁判が始まる (さいばんがはじまる), "a trial begins."

- 裁判は来月始まります。 (さいばんはらいげつはじまります。) - The trial begins next month.
- その事件は裁判で争われました。 (そのじけんはさいばんであらそわれました。) - That case was contested in court.

裁判で identifies the judicial setting or process; it does not name the courthouse building.

## How the kanji combine

裁 contributes judging or adjudicating; [判](../kanji/判.md) contributes deciding or passing judgment. The meanings reinforce the judicial decision-making idea. The on readings are サイ + バン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[判断](判断.md) (はんだん), "judgment; decision," is broader and can describe everyday reasoning. Choosing which coat to wear is a 判断, not a 裁判.

[法律](法律.md) (ほうりつ) means law: the rules involved, not the trial itself.

## Distinctive meaning

裁判 is not limited to deciding whether someone committed a [犯罪](犯罪.md) (はんざい), "crime." It also occurs in civil disputes. A trial, a court building, and the final ruling are related but distinct ideas; context determines whether English "trial" or "judgment" best conveys this word. There is no direct everyday antonym.
