# 以上 (いじょう) — at least; the above

## Meaning

After a number or quantity, 以上 means that amount or more. For an exact numerical rule, the stated boundary is included:
十人以上 (じゅうにんいじょう) means ten people or more, including exactly ten.
It can also refer to what has just been stated; 以上です is a way to finish a report or explanation.

- 十人以上で行きます。 (じゅうにんいじょうでいきます。) — We will go with ten or more people.
- 質問は以上です。 (しつもんはいじょうです。) — Those are all my questions.

## How the kanji combine

[以](../kanji/以.md) can be learned here as taking a stated point as a basis; [上](../kanji/上.md) gives the upward direction.
Together they mark the stated level and above. The on readings are イ + ジョウ.
The meaning of the complete word, not 上 alone, establishes that the numerical boundary is included.

## Synonyms and antonyms

以下 (いか) means the stated amount or less in precise numerical limits; it also includes the boundary.
未満 (みまん) means strictly less than it. Thus 十人未満 (じゅうにんみまん), not 十人以下, is the exact complement of 十人以上.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate numerical 以上 simply as "more than" if that would exclude the boundary.
Its discourse use, "the above; that's all," is different from counting. This article does not cover the separate clause-ending construction meaning "now that."
