# 涙 (なみだ) - tear; tears

## Meaning

涙 is a noun for a tear or tears from the eyes.
Use 涙が出る (なみだがでる), "tears come out," and 涙を流す (なみだをながす), "shed tears."
The Japanese noun does not require a plural ending.

- 玉ねぎを切ると、涙が出ます。 (たまねぎをきると、なみだがでます。) - When I cut onions, my eyes water.
- 彼女はうれしくて涙を流しました。 (かのじょはうれしくてなみだをながしました。) - She shed tears of joy.

The first example concerns a physical stimulus. The second explicitly gives happiness as the reason.
Neither makes sadness a necessary part of the noun's meaning.

## How the kanji combine

涙 writes the complete native word なみだ.
It is a single-kanji word, not a compound whose on readings need to be assembled.
Its water component can help with recognition, but the whole word specifically concerns tears,
not liquid in general.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[泣く](泣く.md) (なく), "cry," is the related action, not a synonym with the same grammar.
[汗](汗.md) (あせ), "sweat," names a different bodily fluid.
Smiling can contrast with crying in a scene, but is not a lexical antonym of 涙.

## Distinctive meaning

Pay attention to particles: 涙が出る describes the tears emerging, while 涙を拭く
(なみだをふく), "wipe away tears," describes someone's action on them.
The noun does not decide whether the person is grieving, moved, laughing, or reacting to irritation;
the surrounding words supply that information.
