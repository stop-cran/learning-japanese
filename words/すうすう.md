# すうすう — with a soft draft; wheezing

## Meaning

すうすう can describe a thin stream of air passing through a gap, or a wheezing sound.

- 窓を閉めたはずなのに、すき間から風がすうすう入ってくる。
  — Even though I thought I closed the window, a draft keeps coming through the gap.
- ドアの下から冷たい風がすうすう通り、足元が寒い。
  — Cold air slips through under the door, making my feet cold.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すうすう evokes a light, continuous passage of air or a wheeze. It is not the loud, forceful sound
of a strong wind.

## Distinctive meaning

This card focuses on the air-through-a-gap sense. The dictionary also records a wheezing use, which
needs a breathing context.
