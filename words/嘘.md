# 嘘 (うそ) -- lie; falsehood

## Meaning

嘘 is a lie or falsehood. The expression for telling a lie is 嘘をつく (うそをつく), with を marking what is told.

- 嘘をついてはいけません。 (うそをついてはいけません。) -- You must not tell lies.
- その話は嘘でした。 (そのはなしはうそでした。) -- That story was not true.

嘘 is a noun, so use 嘘です rather than trying to conjugate it as an adjective. Before another noun it can take の.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 嘘 writes the native word うそ. There is no compound reading to assemble. The kana spellings うそ and ウソ are also used; using katakana does not turn it into a foreign loanword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

本当 (ほんとう), truth or what is real, is a useful contrast. 間違い (まちがい), a mistake, need not involve deliberately misleading anyone.

## Distinctive meaning

As an exclamation, 嘘！ (うそ！) can express startled disbelief, like "No way!" It is not necessarily an accusation of lying. Tone and context distinguish that reaction from a serious statement that someone has told a lie.
