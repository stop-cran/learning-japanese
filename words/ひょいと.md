# ひょいと — nimbly; unexpectedly

## Meaning

A light, easy movement, or something happening casually or unexpectedly.

- 水たまりをひょいと飛（と）び越（こ）えた。 — I lightly hopped over the puddle.
- 友達がひょいと店に現（あらわ）れた。 — A friend unexpectedly appeared at the shop.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぴょんぴょん](ぴょんぴょん.md) emphasizes repeated hopping. [ばったり](ばったり.md) overlaps in a chance encounter, but also has sudden stopping or falling uses.

## Distinctive meaning

The と is already part of this card's written form: do not add another と. ひょいと持（も）ち上げる can describe lifting something effortlessly; the word does not always mean jumping or surprise.
