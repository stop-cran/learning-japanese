# 病気 (びょうき) — illness, sickness

## Meaning

病気 means illness, sickness, or disease. It is a noun: 病気です means "be ill," and 病気の人 (びょうきのひと) means "a sick person."

- 病気で学校を休みました。 (びょうきで がっこうを やすみました。) — I was absent from school because I was ill.
- 母は今、病気です。 (ははは いま、びょうきです。) — My mother is ill now.

The で in the first example gives the reason for the absence.

## How the kanji combine

[病](../kanji/病.md) contributes illness, and [気](../kanji/気.md) contributes a person's state or condition here.
Think "a state of illness," with 病 specifying the condition. Do not translate 気 mechanically as "air."
Both readings are on: ビョウ + キ → びょうき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 病 (やまい) also means illness or disease.
- 健康 (けんこう), "health," gives the contrasting idea; 健康な (けんこうな) means "healthy."
- [元気](元気.md) (げんき) can mean well or healthy, but also energetic. It is not always a precise statement about the absence of disease.

## Distinctive meaning

病気 often suggests more than a minor ailment. For a common cold, people normally name it as 風邪 (かぜ) rather than choosing the broad label 病気.
This is a usage tendency, not a rule that a cold cannot count as illness.
