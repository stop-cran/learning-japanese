# いただきます - before starting a meal

## Meaning

いただきます is the expression said before beginning a meal. It acknowledges receiving the food; English may render it as "Thank you for the meal" or simply leave the mealtime formula untranslated. It is not a command telling someone else to enjoy their food.

- A: どうぞ、食べてください。 (どうぞ、たべてください。) - Please, help yourself.
- B: いただきます。 - Thank you; I'll start eating.

It can also accompany accepting food or drink: では、いただきます, "Well then, I'll have some." The situation supplies what is being received.

## How the kanji combine

This expression is usually written in kana. It has the polite ます form of [いただく](いただく.md), a humble verb used for receiving and for eating or drinking. The dictionary also records 頂きます. This card's kana spelling has no written kanji to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ありがとう](ありがとう.md) is general thanks, not specifically the before-meal formula. ごちそうさまでした expresses thanks after a meal; it is a paired expression, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the expression as something the eater says. Translating it as "Bon appetit" may convey the mealtime setting, but can obscure who says it and when.
