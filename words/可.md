# 可 (か) - acceptable; permitted; passing grade

## Meaning

可 is a noun or suffix-like label meaning acceptable or permitted. It is useful in brief notices and classifications. It also names a passing grade in systems that use labels such as 優・良・可 (ゆう・りょう・か).

- この部屋はペット可です。 (このへやはペットかです。) - Pets are allowed in this room.
- この科目の成績は可でした。 (このかもくのせいせきはかでした。) - My grade in this subject was a pass.

The grading example reports a category, not a universal percentage or a fixed equivalent in another country's grading system.

## How the kanji combine

可 is a single kanji read with its on reading か. It supplies the acceptability or permission idea seen in [可能](可能.md) (かのう), "possible," and [許可](許可.md) (きょか), "permission." Those compounds have their own grammar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不可](不可.md) (ふか) provides the opposing label "not permitted; unacceptable" or a failing grade. 許可 refers to permission or authorization, whereas 可 can simply mark a condition as allowed.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 可 as a compact label, not as a general-purpose adjective meaning "good." A notice saying 撮影可 (さつえいか) permits photography; it does not praise someone's photographic skill. Similarly, the grade 可 means the specified passing category rather than outstanding performance. Read the item before 可 to identify what is being allowed or assessed.
