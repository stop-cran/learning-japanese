# レコード (レコード) — phonograph record

## Meaning

レコード commonly means a phonograph record: a disc played on a record player. It also has specialized uses for a sports record or a computer data record.

父は古いレコードを聞いています。（ちちはふるいレコードをきいています。） — My father is listening to an old record.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "record." The long-vowel mark follows コ: レコード. There is no kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

CD is another music medium, not the same physical format. 記録（きろく） is the broader Japanese word for a record or documentation.

## Distinctive meaning

For everyday beginner use, associate レコード with recorded music on a disc. Do not replace every English "record" with レコード: recording information, keeping records, and making an audio recording require the appropriate Japanese noun or verb for that activity.
