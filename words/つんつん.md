# つんつん — poking repeatedly

## Meaning

つんつん describes repeatedly poking or prodding something with a fingertip or pointed object.

- 彼女は木の葉を指でつんつんとつついた。
  — She gently prodded the leaf with her finger.
- 猫は前足でおもちゃをつんつんした。
  — The cat poked at the toy with its front paw.

## Synonyms and antonyms

つんつん suggests repeated, light prods, not one forceful push.

## Distinctive meaning

The word also describes a standoffish manner, a pungent smell, or something sticking up sharply; these examples focus on poking.
