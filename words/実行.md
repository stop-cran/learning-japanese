# 実行 (じっこう) - carrying out; execution

## Meaning

実行 is carrying something out or putting it into practice. 実行する is transitive: mark the plan or action with を. In computing, it also means running or executing a program.

- 来月、この計画を実行します。 (らいげつ、このけいかくをじっこうします。) - We will carry out this plan next month.
- プログラムを実行すると、結果が表示されます。 (ぷろぐらむをじっこうすると、けっかがひょうじされます。) - When you run the program, the result is displayed.

実行に移す (じっこうにうつす) means "put into action." Here に marks the state of implementation into which the plan is moved.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality or realization, and [行](../kanji/行.md) contributes doing or carrying out. Together they emphasize making something an actual action. The reading is じっこう, with a small っ before こう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[実施](実施.md) (じっし) also means implementation, especially of an organized measure, survey, or event. [練習](練習.md) (れんしゅう) is practice for developing a skill; it is not the same "practice" as putting a plan into practice.

[計画](計画.md) (けいかく), "planning; plan," is an earlier stage rather than a true opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

実行 emphasizes doing rather than merely proposing or intending. It does not guarantee that the attempt succeeds. The computing meaning likewise says that a program runs, not that its output is correct or useful.
