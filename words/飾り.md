# 飾り (かざり) - decoration; ornament

## Meaning

飾り is a decoration or ornament: something that adorns an object, person, or place. It is a noun, and it also appears as the final element of words naming decorations.

- 部屋にクリスマスの飾りを付けました。 (へやにクリスマスのかざりをつけました。) - I put up Christmas decorations in the room.
- この帽子には小さな飾りが付いています。 (このぼうしにはちいさなかざりがついています。) - This hat has a small ornament on it.

## How the kanji combine

飾 contributes adornment. The native noun かざり is related to [飾る](飾る.md) (かざる), decorate or display. The final り is part of the noun's written form, not a second kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

装飾 (そうしょく) also means ornament or decoration and can take する. [アクセサリー](アクセサリー.md) covers personal adornments such as jewelry, as well as equipment accessories. A decorative necklace can be both an アクセサリー and a 飾り, but a camera accessory need not be decorative.

## Distinctive meaning

飾り names the decoration; 飾る names an action. In 飾りを付ける (かざりをつける), "attach a decoration," the verb is 付ける, not an invented conjugation of the noun. Decorations need not be expensive or made of precious materials.
