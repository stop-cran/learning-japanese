# 坪 (つぼ) — traditional land-area unit

## Meaning

坪 is a traditional unit of area for land and buildings. One 坪 is about 3.3 square metres.

- 坪は面積の単位です。 (つぼはめんせきのたんいです。) — A tsubo is a unit of area.

## How the kanji combine

The character [坪](../kanji/坪.md) is a single-character spelling of the unit つぼ; there is no multi-kanji compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

平方メートル is the modern metric unit used for comparison, not a synonym: one 坪 is approximately 3.3 square metres, not one square metre.

## Distinctive meaning

坪 also has historical and specialist senses for other kinds of area and for volume. This article uses only the land/building area unit.
