# 回す (まわす) - turn; pass around

## Meaning

回す is a transitive godan verb for turning or rotating something. It also means passing something around. The object takes **を**; a recipient or direction can take **に**.

- ドアのノブを右に回してください。 (ドアのノブをみぎにまわしてください。) - Please turn the doorknob to the right.
- 資料を隣の人に回しました。 (しりょうをとなりのひとにまわしました。) - I passed the handout to the person next to me.

The past is 回した (まわした), the negative is 回さない (まわさない), and the て-form is 回して. **洗濯機を回す** (せんたくきをまわす) means to run a washing machine, not to rotate the entire appliance by hand.

## How the kanji combine

[回](../kanji/回.md) contributes going around or rotating. Here it has the native reading まわ-, followed by the verb ending す. The same character's counter reading かい belongs to a different word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[回る](回る.md) (まわる) is intransitive: something turns or goes around. [渡す](渡す.md) (わたす), "hand over," overlaps with passing an item but does not itself emphasize circulation.

## Distinctive meaning

The object determines whether movement is literal rotation or transfer onward. Compare 車輪が回る (しゃりんがまわる), "the wheel turns," with 車輪を回す, "turn the wheel." English uses "turn" for both, so the Japanese particle and verb pairing are especially useful.
