# 沸かす (わかす) — boil; heat water

## Meaning

沸かす means to boil or heat a liquid, especially to prepare hot water. It is transitive: what is heated takes を.

- お茶を入れるために、お湯を沸かします。 (おちゃをいれるために、おゆをわかします。) — I'll boil water to make tea.
- お風呂を沸かしました。 (おふろをわかしました。) — I heated the bathwater.

It is a godan verb: 沸かします (わかします), 沸かして (わかして), 沸かさない (わかさない).

## How the kanji combine

沸 supplies the heating or boiling idea, read わ here. The written かす completes the native verb; it should not be treated as a separate word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

沸く (わく) is intransitive: お湯が沸きました (おゆがわきました), "the water has boiled." 冷ます (さます), "cool something," gives a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The required temperature depends on the object. お湯を沸かす commonly involves boiling water for use, but お風呂を沸かす means heating bathwater to bathing temperature, not making a bath boil. 温める (あたためる) is the broader verb for warming something.
