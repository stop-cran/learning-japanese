# 寄せる (よせる) - bring near; gather; send; add

## Meaning

寄せる is an ichidan verb with several related constructions.
It commonly brings something closer or gathers it, with the thing
taking を and a destination taking に.

- 椅子を窓のそばに寄せました。 (いすをまどのそばによせました。) - I moved the chair closer to the window.
- ご意見をお寄せください。 (ごいけんをおよせください。) - Please send us your opinions.

The second example is sending or contributing information, not
physically moving an opinion. Other useful combinations include:

- 信頼を寄せる (しんらいをよせる) - place trust in someone; the person can take に.
- 友人の家に身を寄せる (ゆうじんのいえにみをよせる) - take temporary shelter or stay in a friend's care.
- 思いを寄せる (おもいをよせる) - have feelings for someone.
- 皿を脇に寄せる (さらをわきによせる) - move the plates aside.

Forms include 寄せます, 寄せた and 寄せない.
There is also an intransitive approach use: 波が岸に寄せる
(なみがきしによせる), "waves roll toward the shore."
Do not force an object with を into that wave construction.

## How the kanji combine

[寄](../kanji/寄.md) contributes approaching or drawing near.
This native verb is related to [寄る](寄る.md) (よる).
せる is part of the established dictionary form 寄せる; it is not
an instruction to turn every sense mechanically into a causative.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[近付ける](近付ける.md) (ちかづける) overlaps with bringing close;
[集める](集める.md) (あつめる) with collecting; and
[送る](送る.md) (おくる) with sending.
[離す](離す.md) (はなす), separate or move apart, contrasts with
bringing things near, but is not an opposite for every use.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the object and construction together. 寄せる can also mean
adding numbers; ordinary [足す](足す.md) (たす) is a useful comparison.
Dictionaries additionally record letting someone visit, putting
aside, pressing, using something as a pretext, and a Kansai use
for including someone in a group. These are contextual uses, not
permission to substitute 寄せる for any occurrence of "put" or "push."
The visiting pattern 寄せてもらう means being allowed to visit.

The chair, opinion, feelings and wave constructions do not all
share one transitivity rule or one literal English translation.
