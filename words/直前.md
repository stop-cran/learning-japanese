# 直前 (ちょくぜん) - just before; right in front

## Meaning

直前 means just before an event. Use **noun + の直前** or
**dictionary-form verb + 直前**, followed by に when marking the time.

- 出発の直前に電話がありました。
  (しゅっぱつのちょくぜんにでんわがありました。) - There was a phone call just before departure.
- 家を出る直前に鍵を探しました。
  (いえをでるちょくぜんにかぎをさがしました。) - I looked for my keys just before leaving home.

The dictionary form 出る remains appropriate in that past account:
the leaving was still ahead of the key-searching.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) contributes immediacy or directness, and
[前](../kanji/前.md) before or in front.
The on readings are チョク + ゼン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[前](前.md) (まえ) is broader: it does not require immediate proximity.
[以前](以前.md) (いぜん) also means before or previously,
without requiring immediacy.
[先](先.md) (さき) has earlier/front uses but also future uses.
[直後](直後.md) (ちょくご), immediately after, is the temporal contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

直前 also has a spatial sense, right in front:
車の直前を横断しました (くるまのちょくぜんをおうだんしました),
"I crossed right in front of the car."
The situation determines whether the proximity is temporal or spatial.
For time, just before does not promise a fixed number of minutes.
The source list anchors the temporal sense; the spatial sense is additional teaching.
