# 市場 (いちば) - marketplace

## Meaning

市場 (いちば) is a physical marketplace where people buy and sell goods.
It is a noun: 市場で marks the place of a transaction, and 市場へ or 市場に can mark a destination.

- 朝、市場で魚を買いました。 (あさ、いちばでさかなをかいました。) - I bought fish at the market in the morning.
- この市場には野菜の店がたくさんあります。 (このいちばにはやさいのみせがたくさんあります。) - This market has many vegetable stalls.

The examples concern a place to visit, not an abstract economic system.

## How the kanji combine

[市](../kanji/市.md) (いち) contributes "market," and [場](../kanji/場.md) (ば) means a place.
Together they describe a market site. Both parts use native readings here.
The same written compound also has the on-reading しじょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[店](店.md) (みせ) is an individual shop; a 市場 can contain many shops or stalls.
市場 (しじょう) names markets in economic or financial discussion, as in 国内市場 (こくないしじょう), "domestic market."
The readings' uses overlap for some physical markets; this is not an absolute physical-versus-abstract spelling rule.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn いちば with the shopping-place meaning. Do not assign it the economic sense merely because English uses "market" for both.
In a market's proper name, check the name's actual reading rather than deciding from the characters alone.
