# 功績 (こうせき) - achievement; meritorious contribution

## Meaning

功績 is an achievement, meritorious deed, or distinguished contribution.
It concerns something accomplished that is valued, rather than effort alone.
Nの功績 identifies whose achievement or contribution is meant.

- 彼女の功績を高く評価しています。 (かのじょのこうせきをたかくひょうかしています。) - We highly value her achievements.
- 彼は医学の発展に大きな功績を残しました。 (かれはいがくのはってんにおおきなこうせきをのこしました。) - He made a major contribution to the development of medicine.

功績を残す (こうせきをのこす) means to leave achievements or make
a lasting contribution.

## How the kanji combine

功 contributes merit or successful accomplishment, and [績](../kanji/績.md)
contributes achievements or results. The related meanings reinforce a
meritorious accomplishment. The on readings are コウ + セキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[成功](成功.md) (せいこう) is success or achievement.
[貢献](貢献.md) (こうけん) concerns contributing to a cause.
[成績](成績.md) (せいせき) concerns assessed performance or results,
which need not be favorable. These meanings overlap without making every
result a 功績.

## Distinctive meaning

功績 highlights recognized or valued accomplishment. It need not involve
an official award, and the word alone does not identify who deserves credit.
English "contribution" can also mean a donation, as in [寄付](寄付.md)
(きふ); 功績 is not a general instruction to give money.
The unrelated homophone [鉱石](鉱石.md) (こうせき) means ore or mineral.
