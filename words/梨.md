# 梨 (なし) - pear tree; pear

## Meaning

梨 names a pear tree or its fruit. In food contexts, なし often refers to Japanese or Asian pears. The pinned JMdict entry particularly identifies *Pyrus pyrifolia*; the Japanese dictionary also permits a broader use covering European pears and wild species.

- 梨を二つ買いました。 (なしをふたつかいました。) - I bought two pears.
- 庭の梨の木に花が咲きました。 (にわのなしのきにはながさきました。) - The pear tree in the garden has blossomed.

梨の木 (なしのき) specifies the tree, and 梨の実 (なしのみ) specifies the fruit.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [梨](../kanji/梨.md) writes the whole native noun なし. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide. JMdict also records the rare spelling 梨子 for this noun; that is not a reason to pronounce its characters separately.

The dictionary marks the noun as usually written in kana. なし or ナシ can therefore be plant spellings, not evidence that the kanji spelling has a different fruit meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[果物](果物.md) (くだもの) is the broader food category "fruit." 洋梨 (ようなし) and 西洋梨 (せいようなし) specify European pear. They help make a variety distinction, but do not prove that the bare word 梨 can never include European pears. [木](木.md) (き), tree, and [植物](植物.md) (しょくぶつ), plant, are broader still. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes the tree from the fruit. The same pronunciation なし also occurs in the unrelated word [無し](無し.md), "without; none." A shared pronunciation does not give 梨 that negative meaning.
