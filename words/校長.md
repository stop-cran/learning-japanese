# 校長 (こうちょう) - school principal

## Meaning

校長 is the principal or head teacher of a school. It names a position, not a particular person's name or a teacher's length of service.

- 校長先生がお話をしました。 (こうちょうせんせいがおはなしをしました。) - The principal gave a talk.
- この学校の校長は田中先生です。 (このがっこうのこうちょうはたなかせんせいです。) - The principal of this school is Mr. or Ms. Tanaka.

校長先生 is a respectful school-context expression.

## How the kanji combine

[校](../kanji/校.md), こう, refers to school, and [長](../kanji/長.md), ちょう, means the head or leader here. The compound identifies the person heading the school.

## Synonyms and antonyms

先生 (せんせい) is broader and can address or refer to teachers. 教師 (きょうし) names the teaching occupation. A principal may be a teacher, but these terms do not designate the same role.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse こうちょう with 工場 (こうじょう), "factory." The consonant and both written characters differ. 校長 is gender-neutral; English translations such as "headmaster" reflect the particular person or school, not a male restriction in the Japanese word.
