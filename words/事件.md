# 事件 (じけん) — incident; case

## Meaning

事件 refers to an incident, affair, or case. It can name something that becomes a matter for attention or investigation.
The word by itself does not specify who was responsible or establish that a crime occurred.

- 警察がその事件を調べています。 (けいさつがそのじけんをしらべています。) — The police are investigating that case.
- 昨日、この町で事件が起きました。 (きのう、このまちでじけんがおきました。) — An incident occurred in this town yesterday.

With 事件が起きる (じけんがおきる), "an incident occurs," the incident is the subject.

## How the kanji combine

[事](../kanji/事.md) supplies "matter; affair," and [件](../kanji/件.md) treats a matter as a case or item.
The two related meanings combine into a word for an identifiable incident or case.
The on readings are ジ + ケン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[事故](事故.md) (じこ) foregrounds an accident or mishap.
[出来事](出来事.md) (できごと) is a broader word for a happening or event.
The categories can overlap: do not turn the comparison into a rule that every 事件 must be deliberate and every 事故 must be blameless.

## Distinctive meaning

For a simple account of what happened during the day, 出来事 can avoid presenting each event as a notable case.
事件 is useful when the event itself is the matter under discussion, as in an investigation.
The dictionary's English gloss "event" is therefore not a reason to translate every scheduled event as 事件.
