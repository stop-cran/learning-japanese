# プラン (プラン) - plan; package

## Meaning

プラン is a plan or proposed arrangement. It can also name a service,
payment, or accommodation package. Use プランを立てる
(プランをたてる), "make a plan," and プランを選ぶ
(プランをえらぶ), "choose a plan/package."

- 週末の旅行のプランを立てました。 (しゅうまつのりょこうのプランをたてました。) - I made a plan for the weekend trip.
- 朝食付きのプランを選びました。 (ちょうしょくつきのプランをえらびました。) - I chose the package that includes breakfast.

The first is an arrangement someone makes; the second is an option being offered.

## How the kanji combine

プラン is written in katakana and corresponds to English "plan."
There are no kanji components to explain. The final ン is part of the
Japanese word; no long-vowel mark or extra syllable follows it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計画](計画.md) (けいかく) overlaps strongly with the planning meaning.
[予定](予定.md) (よてい) concerns what is scheduled or expected.
[案](案.md) (あん) is a proposal or idea. These are not fixed stages that
every project must pass through, and プラン need not be less detailed
than 計画.

## Distinctive meaning

In service descriptions, compare what each プラン includes rather than
assuming the word means a schedule. 料金プラン (りょうきんプラン),
"pricing plan," concerns the charging arrangement.
The noun itself does not promise that a proposal is approved, affordable,
or already being carried out. Those details come from the surrounding
description, not from using a loanword instead of 計画.
