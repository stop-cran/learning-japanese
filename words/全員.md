# 全員 (ぜんいん) - all members; everyone

## Meaning

全員 means every member of a relevant group of people. It works as a noun and can indicate that the whole group takes part.

- 全員がそろいました。 (ぜんいんがそろいました。) - Everyone is here.
- 全員で部屋を掃除しました。 (ぜんいんでへやをそうじしました。) - We all cleaned the room together.

が marks the group as subject; で presents all its members as the participants acting together. 学生全員 (がくせいぜんいん) means "all the students."

## How the kanji combine

[全](../kanji/全.md) contributes all or entire, and [員](../kanji/員.md) contributes members. The modifier-plus-head combination means "all members." The reading is ゼン + イン: spell it ぜんいん, keeping the ん before い.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[皆](皆.md) (みな) also means everyone. [全て](全て.md) (すべて) and [全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ) can cover things as well as people; 全員 specifically counts group members. 一部の人 (いちぶのひと), "some of the people," contrasts with the whole group.

## Distinctive meaning

全員 does not itself mean unanimous. 全員が集まった (ぜんいんがあつまった) says everyone gathered, not that everyone agreed. To express unanimity, add the agreement: 全員が賛成した (ぜんいんがさんせいした), "Everyone agreed." The predicate, not 全員 alone, supplies that conclusion.
