# 次 (つぎ) — next; following

## Meaning

次 identifies the next item, event, or turn in a sequence.
It is a noun that can modify another noun with の, not an い-adjective.

- 次の電車に乗ります。 (つぎのでんしゃにのります。) — I will take the next train.
- 次は私の番です。 (つぎはわたしのばんです。) — It is my turn next.

Use 次の + noun for "the next ..."; use 次に (つぎに) to introduce what happens next.
With 次は, the next item or turn becomes the topic.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [次](../kanji/次.md) uses its kun reading つぎ.
There are no separate written components contributing different words.
The reading ジ in 次回 (じかい), "next time," belongs to another word, not a free alternative pronunciation of standalone 次.

## Synonyms and antonyms

次回 (じかい) specifies the next occasion.
[今度](今度.md) (こんど) can mean "this time" or "next time," depending on context, so it is not always a substitute for 次.
隣 (となり), "adjoining; next door," concerns physical adjacency rather than the order expressed by 次.

## Distinctive meaning

English "next" covers both "next in line" and "next to me." 次 is the useful choice for the sequence meaning.
It also needs a reference point: the next train is the one following the relevant current train or moment, not a permanently named train.
