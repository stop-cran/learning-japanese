# ほどく - untie; unfasten; unravel

## Meaning

ほどく means to undo a fastening, untie, loosen, unravel, or unpack something. It is a transitive godan verb; the thing undone takes を.

- 靴のひもをほどいた。 (くつのひもをほどいた。) - I untied my shoelaces.
- 結び目をゆっくりほどいてください。 (むすびめをゆっくりほどいてください。) - Please undo the knot slowly.
- 荷物をほどいて、服を出しました。 (にもつをほどいて、ふくをだしました。) - I unpacked my luggage and took out the clothes.

The forms are ほどきます, ほどいて, and ほどかない. The corresponding intransitive ほどける describes something coming undone: ひもがほどけた, "the cord came untied."

## How the kanji combine

Usually written in kana, ほどく can also be written 解く. The kana headword therefore has `kanji: []`; recognizing the spelling does not change the reading to とく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[解く](解く.md) (とく) also includes untying, but has additional solving, dispelling, and canceling uses. 数学の問題を解く (すうがくのもんだいをとく), "solve a mathematics problem," uses とく, not the ordinary unpacking/unknotting use taught here.

[外す](外す.md) (はずす) can mean unfasten or remove. [結ぶ](結ぶ.md) (むすぶ), "tie," reverses the direction of the knot example.

## Distinctive meaning

An object being untied takes を with ほどく, while something coming untied takes が with ほどける. The shared spelling 解く does not mean that every とく construction permits ほどく; neither does this practical contrast forbid every figurative use of ほどく.
