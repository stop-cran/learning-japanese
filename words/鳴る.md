# 鳴る (なる) — to ring; to sound

## Meaning

鳴る describes something producing a sound: a bell rings, a telephone sounds, or a stomach rumbles. It is an intransitive godan verb: 鳴ります, 鳴らない, 鳴って.

- 電話が鳴っています。 (でんわがなっています。) — The telephone is ringing.
- おなかが鳴りました。 (おなかがなりました。) — My stomach rumbled.

## How the kanji combine

[鳴](../kanji/鳴.md) supplies な, and る is okurigana. The same kanji appears in 鳴く (なく), but the two verbs are not interchangeable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

鳴らす (ならす) is transitive: ベルを鳴らす, "ring a bell." 鳴く describes the calls of animals such as birds or cats. 聞こえる (きこえる) means being audible to a listener.

## Distinctive meaning

Use が for the thing sounding, not を as though someone were ringing it. Kana-only なる also represents the unrelated verb "become"; writing 鳴る makes the sound meaning clear. 鳴る does not imply that anyone answered the ringing phone.
