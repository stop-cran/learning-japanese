# 石 (いし) — stone; pebble

## Meaning

石 is a stone, rock, or pebble. It can identify a stone as an object without saying what mineral it is made of.
Context supplies its size and number.

- 道に小さな石があります。 (みちにちいさないしがあります。) — There is a small stone on the road.
- この石は重いです。 (このいしはおもいです。) — This stone is heavy.

小さな in the first sentence supplies "small"; that meaning is not built into every use of 石.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [石](../kanji/石.md) writes the native noun いし.
Its on reading セキ appears in compounds, such as 宝石 (ほうせき), "precious stone."
An on reading is not needed merely because the word is written in kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

岩 (いわ) can name a rock, boulder, or crag and is useful when a substantial rock mass is meant.
The English word "rock" overlaps both entries; there is no exact size boundary that converts every 石 into 岩.
宝石 narrows the category to gems or precious stones. There is no ordinary opposite of "stone" to memorize.

## Distinctive meaning

石 is the material-object word, not a promise that the object is valuable or polished.
Use added description when shape, size, or value matters.
Compounds must still be learned as whole words: 石油 (せきゆ) means petroleum, not a loose stone covered in oil.
