# 雑巾 (ぞうきん) - cleaning cloth; dust cloth

## Meaning

雑巾 is a cloth or rag for wiping and cleaning, such as wiping a desk
or floor. It is a noun. 雑巾で拭く (ぞうきんでふく)
means wiping something with a cleaning cloth.

- 雑巾で机を拭きました。 (ぞうきんでつくえをふきました。) - I wiped the desk with a cleaning cloth.
- 掃除のあと、雑巾を洗いました。 (そうじのあと、ぞうきんをあらいました。) - I washed the cleaning cloth after cleaning.

In the first example, で marks the tool and を the surface being wiped.

## How the kanji combine

[雑](../kanji/雑.md) contributes miscellaneous or rough-use associations,
and 巾 cloth. Learn the established word for a cleaning cloth rather than
translating it as an arbitrary mixture of fabrics.
The on readings here are ゾウ + キン; 雑 is not read ざつ in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[布](布.md) (ぬの) is cloth or fabric more broadly.
[タオル](タオル.md) is a towel, and [ハンカチ](ハンカチ.md)
a handkerchief. Their possible use for wiping does not make every
towel or handkerchief a 雑巾.

## Distinctive meaning

The cleaning use identifies the object; it does not have to be a
particular color or have been bought as a finished cleaning product.
Conversely, 雑巾 is not the name for every piece of fabric in a house.
The spelling taught here is 雑巾.
