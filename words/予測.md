# 予測 (よそく) - prediction; estimation

## Meaning

予測 is a noun for a prediction or estimation.
**予測する** is transitive: **event or quantity + を予測する** means to predict it.
For a predicted statement, use **clause + と予測する**.

- 来月の売り上げを予測しています。 (らいげつのうりあげをよそくしています。) - We are predicting next month's sales.
- 来年は利用者が増えると予測されています。 (らいねんはりようしゃがふえるとよそくされています。) - The number of users is predicted to increase next year.

The second example is passive: 予測されています, "is predicted."
予測が外れる (よそくがはずれる) means that a prediction turns out to be wrong.

## How the kanji combine

[予](../kanji/予.md) contributes doing something beforehand; 測 contributes measuring or estimating.
Together they suggest estimating in advance.
The on readings ヨ + ソク give よそく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予想](予想.md) (よそう) overlaps with expectation or prediction.
[予報](予報.md) (よほう) emphasizes an advance forecast or report, such as a weather forecast.
The boundaries are contextual: it is too rigid to say 予測 always uses data while 予想 is always an unsupported guess.

## Distinctive meaning

予測 describes what is expected to happen, not a promise to make it happen.
A predicted result can be uncertain and can change when the information changes.
It often fits analytical discussion, but the word alone does not identify a particular statistical method.
Distinguish the prediction from a plan, a target, or a guaranteed result.
