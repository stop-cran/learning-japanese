# 酌む (くむ) - serve sake; drink together

## Meaning

酌む means pour or serve sake, and can describe drinking it together. The drink takes を; context distinguishes serving from sharing a drink.

- 客に酒を酌みました。 (きゃくにさけをくみました。) - I poured sake for the guest.
- 友人と酒を酌みながら話しました。 (ゆうじんとさけをくみながらはなしました。) - I talked with a friend over sake.

The godan forms include 酌みます (くみます), 酌んで (くんで), 酌まない (くまない).

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 酌 supplies the native stem く; む is the verb ending. There is no 酌 kanji card in the current collection. Its sake-serving association helps distinguish this written word from other words pronounced くむ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[汲む](汲む.md) (くむ) foregrounds drawing or scooping liquid, especially water, but it also has pouring/drinking uses. For sake-serving, 酌む is the spelling explicitly identified in the inspected Japanese dictionary note.

[飲む](飲む.md) (のむ) simply means drink; it does not itself specify serving someone or drinking together. [注ぐ](注ぐ.md) (そそぐ) means pour more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional use is 相手の気持ちを酌む (あいてのきもちをくむ), understand or consider the other person's feelings. 汲む also supports this use. Do not force a rule that 酌む is always literal sake or that only one spelling can express consideration.
