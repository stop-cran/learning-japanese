# 勘 (かん) - intuition; hunch

## Meaning

勘 is a noun for intuition, a hunch, or an intuitive feel for something. It can concern a quick guess or an ability developed through experience. Use **勘で** for doing something by intuition rather than through an explicit calculation.

- 答えが分からなかったので、勘で選びました。 (こたえがわからなかったので、かんでえらびました。) - I did not know the answer, so I chose by intuition.
- 私の勘が当たりました。 (わたしのかんがあたりました。) - My hunch was right.

勘が当たる (かんがあたる) says the hunch proves right; 勘が外れる (かんがはずれる) says it proves wrong.

## How the kanji combine

勘 is a single kanji used as the whole noun, with the on reading カン. There is no multi-kanji compound to break down.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予想](予想.md) (よそう) is an expectation or prediction and need not come from intuition. [判断](判断.md) (はんだん) names a judgment; 勘 can be one basis for making it. Neither word is an exact opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

勘がいい, "have good intuition," and 勘が鋭い (かんがするどい), "have sharp intuition," describe sensitivity to clues. They do not assert supernatural powers or guarantee correctness. 久しぶりで勘が鈍った (ひさしぶりでかんがにぶった) means one's feel for an activity has become rusty after a long break.
