# がっちり — firmly; tightly secured

## Meaning

がっちり describes a firm, secure hold or a tight fastening.

- クランプで板をがっちり固定した。
  — I secured the board firmly with a clamp.
- 荷物が動かないように、ベルトでがっちり縛った。
  — I tied the luggage tightly with a belt so it would not move.

## Synonyms and antonyms

がっちり emphasizes a strong, secure hold. It differs from [がっしり](がっしり.md), which
usually describes a sturdy build or construction.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records a well-built sense and a shrewd or calculating sense. The examples here teach
the secure-fastening use; they do not imply those other meanings.
