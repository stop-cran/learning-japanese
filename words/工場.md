# 工場 (こうじょう) — factory

## Meaning

工場 is a factory, manufacturing plant or workshop.

- 父は工場で働いています。 (ちちはこうじょうではたらいています。) — My father works at a factory.
- この工場では車を作っています。 (このこうじょうではくるまをつくっています。) — Cars are made at this factory.

## How the kanji combine

[工](../kanji/工.md) コウ concerns making or manufacturing, and [場](../kanji/場.md) ジョウ is a place. The first element specifies the activity associated with the place: a manufacturing site.

The selected reading こうじょう is on+on. **こうば** is another established reading of 工場; that one mixes on コウ with native ば. It is not a misspelling of こうじょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [会社](会社.md) かいしゃ is a company or organization. One company can operate factories, but the organization and a production site are not the same thing.
- 工業 こうぎょう is manufacturing industry, not a particular factory building.
- There is no general direct antonym of 工場.

## Distinctive meaning

工場 identifies a place where things are made or processed. It does not simply mean any workplace or office. The examples here use こうじょう; both dictionary readings refer to manufacturing places, so do not invent a rigid size boundary between them.
