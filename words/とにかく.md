# とにかく (とにかく) - anyway; in any case

## Meaning

とにかく is an adverb used to set other considerations aside and return to a main point or immediate action: "anyway; at any rate; in any case."

- 詳しいことは後で話します。とにかく、今は出発しましょう。 (くわしいことはあとではなします。とにかく、いまはしゅっぱつしましょう。) - I will explain the details later. For now, let's get going.
- 値段はとにかく、品質はいいです。 (ねだんはとにかく、ひんしつはいいです。) - Leaving the price aside, the quality is good.

In the second pattern, Nはとにかく names what is being set aside. The rest of the sentence supplies the point the speaker wants to make.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The spelling 兎に角 is recorded as rare ateji: its characters are not a literal rabbit-and-horn explanation of the meaning. Learn とにかく as a complete native expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ともかく can also set a matter aside. [何とか](何とか.md) (なんとか) means "somehow" in accomplishing something by some means; it is not the same discourse move as とにかく.

## Distinctive meaning

とにかく can sound insistent when it cuts past objections or details. It does not by itself explain how a difficult action was accomplished. In とにかく行こう, "Let's go anyway," the speaker prioritizes going, not an unknown method of travel.
