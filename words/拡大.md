# 拡大 (かくだい) - expansion; enlargement

## Meaning

拡大 is enlargement or expansion. With する it can be transitive, Xを拡大する, "enlarge X,"
or intransitive, Xが拡大する, "X expands." It covers image magnification as well as a growing scale or extent.

- 写真を拡大してください。 (しゃしんをかくだいしてください。) - Please enlarge the photograph.
- 事業の規模が拡大しています。 (じぎょうのきぼがかくだいしています。) - The scale of the business is expanding.

The first sentence asks someone to act on an image. The second describes growth without naming an agent.

## How the kanji combine

拡 contributes widening or extending, while [大](../kanji/大.md) contributes largeness.
The combined idea is increasing size or extent. The on-readings are カク + ダイ.
This is a meaning aid, not a claim about the historical construction of either character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[増加](増加.md) (ぞうか) concerns an increase in number or amount; 拡大 highlights size, range, or scale.
[広がる](広がる.md) (ひろがる) is an everyday intransitive word for spreading.
縮小 (しゅくしょう), "reduction; shrinking," contrasts directly with 拡大 in image size and organizational scale.

## Distinctive meaning

Expansion is not automatically desirable. 被害が拡大する (ひがいがかくだいする) means that damage spreads or grows.
Conversely, enlarging a photograph does not necessarily reveal extra detail: 拡大 names the size change,
not an improvement in the underlying image.
