# 俸給 (ほうきゅう) — salary; pay

## Meaning

俸給 is salary or pay for work, especially that of public employees.
The association with official employment is useful, but the word can also
describe company employees' pay. It is not a government-only synonym of salary.

- 俸給を受け取っています。
  (ほうきゅうをうけとっています。) — I receive a salary.
- 俸給が上がりました。
  (ほうきゅうがあがりました。) — My salary went up.

The first example names the money received; を marks it as the object.
The second reports an increase, not a change of occupation.

## How the kanji combine

[俸](../kanji/俸.md) contributes salary or a stipend, and
[給](../kanji/給.md) contributes giving or paying. Together they identify pay
provided for work. The on readings are ホウ + キュウ: ほうきゅう.
Keep both long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[給料](給料.md) (きゅうりょう), "salary; wages," and
[給与](給与.md) (きゅうよ), "pay," overlap this employment-pay sense.
[月給](月給.md) (げっきゅう) specifies a monthly salary.
[収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう), "income," is broader and can include money from
sources other than employment.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the official-pay association as a guide to recognizing 俸給 in descriptions
of employment, not as a rule forbidding its use for company employees.
The word alone does not tell you the payment interval, take-home amount, or
which allowances are included. A particular document may define its payroll
terms more narrowly; check that context rather than forcing every use of
俸給, 給料 and 給与 into separate categories.
