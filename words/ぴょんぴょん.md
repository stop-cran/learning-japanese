# ぴょんぴょん — hopping repeatedly

## Meaning

Light, repeated jumps or hops.

- うさぎが庭（にわ）をぴょんぴょん跳（は）ねている。 — A rabbit is hopping around the garden.
- 子どもがうれしそうにぴょんぴょん跳（と）んだ。 — The child jumped up and down happily.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ひょいと](ひょいと.md) can describe one nimble movement, including a hop over something. [よちよち](よちよち.md) instead describes toddling steps.

## Distinctive meaning

ぴょんぴょん跳ねる depicts the movement; it does not inherently mean happiness or identify a rabbit. The same repeated hopping can be described without any animal or joyful emotion being present.
