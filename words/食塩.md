# 食塩 (しょくえん) - table salt

## Meaning

食塩 is table salt, used in cooking or seasoning food. In chemical usage it
also names sodium chloride. It is a noun, not an adjective meaning salty.

- スープに食塩を少し加えます。 (スープにしょくえんをすこしくわえます。) - I add a little table salt to the soup.
- この料理には食塩を使っていません。 (このりょうりにはしょくえんをつかっていません。) - I have not used table salt in this dish.

を marks the salt added or used. 少し specifies the amount in the first
example; the word 食塩 itself does not specify a quantity.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) contributes food or eating, and [塩](../kanji/塩.md)
contributes salt. The first character narrows the kind of salt.
The on readings are ショク + エン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[塩](塩.md) (しお) is the everyday salt word and overlaps directly.
[しょうゆ](しょうゆ.md), soy sauce, is another seasoning, not another
name for 食塩. A sweet seasoning is a contrast in taste, not an antonym
for this substance.

## Distinctive meaning

The chemical noun 塩 (えん) covers a broader class of salts. It is not
a promise that every chemical salt is edible or is sodium chloride.
Conversely, 食塩 is not a general name for everything that tastes salty.
