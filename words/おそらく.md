# おそらく (おそらく) - probably; most likely

## Meaning

おそらく is an adverb expressing a likely inference or prediction: "probably" or "in all likelihood." It does not merely introduce any imaginable possibility.

- おそらく明日は雨でしょう。 (おそらくあしたはあめでしょう。) - It will probably rain tomorrow.
- 彼はおそらくもう家に着いています。 (かれはおそらくもういえについています。) - He has most likely already arrived home.

It can occur with だろう or でしょう, but these endings are not required. The second example is an inference about the present result of a past journey.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. 恐らく, with [恐](../kanji/恐.md), is also recorded. The spelling does not make this adverb a statement that the speaker is frightened; learn its current inference meaning as a whole.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[多分](多分.md) (たぶん) also means "probably." [きっと](きっと.md) can convey a confident expectation. [必ず](必ず.md) (かならず), "without fail; certainly," is not an interchangeable label for an uncertain prediction.

## Distinctive meaning

おそらく is useful when presenting what seems likely rather than directly observed. It can introduce unwelcome predictions, but is not limited to bad outcomes. These expressions do not define fixed numerical probability ranges; the evidence and context still matter.
