# はじめまして - nice to meet you

## Meaning

はじめまして is a polite greeting when meeting someone for the first time.
It often opens a self-introduction.

- はじめまして。田中です。 (はじめまして。たなかです。) - Nice to meet you. I am Tanaka.
- はじめまして。どうぞよろしくお願いします。 (はじめまして。どうぞよろしくおねがいします。) - Nice to meet you. I look forward to getting to know you.

The second example pairs the first-meeting greeting with a conventional request
for a good relationship. The English rendering is contextual, not a literal promise.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 初めまして and 始めまして
are recognized spellings, using the beginning-related characters
[初](../kanji/初.md) and [始](../kanji/始.md).
Learn the whole greeting; its spelling variants are not separate greetings to memorize.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こんにちは](こんにちは.md) is a daytime hello, not specifically a first-meeting formula.
[宜しく](宜しく.md), commonly in よろしくお願いします (よろしくおねがいします),
has a different role in the introduction and many uses beyond first meetings.
The expressions can occur together without being interchangeable everywhere.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use はじめまして as the ordinary greeting every time you see the same person.
It marks the first encounter in the situation, not merely the first thing said each morning.
Unlike [はじめに](はじめに.md), "first of all," it directly greets a person.
