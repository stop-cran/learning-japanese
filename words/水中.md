# 水中 (すいちゅう) — in the water, underwater

## Meaning

水中 is the space inside water: 水中写真 (underwater photography), 水中メガネ (swimming goggles), 水中で息を止める (hold one's breath underwater).

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md) (water) plus [中](../kanji/中.md) (inside): "in the water". The pattern noun + 中 (ちゅう) means "in, during, in the midst of" — 空中 くうちゅう (in the air), 地中 ちちゅう (underground), 勉強中 (studying).
This is different from the ジュウ reading, "throughout": 一日中 いちにちじゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 海中 かいちゅう (in the sea), 水面下 すいめんか (below the surface).
- Contrast: 水上 すいじょう (on the water), 水面 すいめん (the water surface), 水底 みなぞこ (the bottom).
- Antonym: 水上 is the usual opposite, with 陸上 りくじょう (on land) as a wider contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

水中 refers to any water, from a pool to a river. 海中 is specifically the sea, and 水底 is the bed. For "underwater", 水中 works both in compound nouns (水中カメラ) and with particles (水中で息を止める);
水の中で is a more conversational alternative.
