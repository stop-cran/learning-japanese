# 紅葉 (こうよう) - autumn leaves; autumn colors

## Meaning

Read **こうよう**, 紅葉 concerns leaves turning red in autumn, the
colored leaves, or autumn colors. It is a noun and an intransitive
する-verb: 木が紅葉する (きがこうようする), "a tree's leaves turn red."
The seasonal-color use can include yellow or brown foliage too.

- 山の紅葉を見に行きました。 (やまのこうようをみにいきました。) - We went to see the autumn colors in the mountains.
- 庭の木が紅葉しました。 (にわのきがこうようしました。) - The garden tree's leaves turned red.

The same spelling has a separate reading, **もみじ**.
That dictionary family includes both autumn foliage and maple:

- 庭に紅葉を植えました。 (にわにもみじをうえました。) - We planted a maple tree in the garden.

Here もみじ names the tree. Do not read the maple-tree example
こうよう merely because that is this card's primary reading.

## How the kanji combine

[紅](../kanji/紅.md) contributes crimson or red, and
[葉](../kanji/葉.md) contributes leaves. The modifier-plus-head image
is red leaves. The regular on readings are コウ + ヨウ.
The additional whole-word reading もみじ is learned as a unit;
do not divide it into invented character readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[葉](葉.md) (は) is the broader leaf or foliage noun.
黄葉 (こうよう) specifically concerns yellow autumn leaves;
楓 (かえで) names maple. Neither spelling difference nor the shared
season makes every leaf or tree a maple.

## Distinctive meaning

The community source pairs こうよう with "(Japanese) maple."
In the pinned dictionary, maple belongs to the separate もみじ family,
not the こうよう entry. This article preserves that distinction while
teaching both readings; the compiler's historical intention is unknown.
The もみじ senses are additional teaching, not separate N2 assignments.
