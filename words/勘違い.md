# 勘違い (かんちがい) - mistaken assumption; misunderstanding

## Meaning

勘違い is a mistaken understanding, assumption, or guess.
勘違いする means to get the wrong idea.
Use clause + と勘違いする to state what someone mistakenly believed.

- 会議は明日だと勘違いしていました。
  (かいぎはあしただとかんちがいしていました。) - I had mistakenly thought the meeting was tomorrow.
- 私の勘違いでした。
  (わたしのかんちがいでした。) - I had the wrong idea.

In the first example, と follows the full mistaken claim, 明日だ.
XをYと勘違いする means mistaking X for Y.

## How the kanji combine

勘 contributes judgment or intuition, and
[違](../kanji/違.md) contributes being different or mistaken.
The word combines カン with native 違い (ちがい), related to
[違う](違う.md). This is a mixed on-first compound, not two on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[誤解](誤解.md) (ごかい) overlaps in a misunderstanding or wrong
interpretation. [間違い](間違い.md) (まちがい) is broader:
a copying error need not involve a mistaken belief about the situation.
These distinctions do not make every use mutually exclusive.

## Distinctive meaning

勘違い does not necessarily imply deliberate deception.
The speaker may genuinely have assumed the wrong date, person, or meaning.
Do not infer that 勘違い is always harmless or only concerns another
person's words; context determines the consequences and what was mistaken.
