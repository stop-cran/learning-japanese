# 楽 (らく) - ease; comfortable

## Meaning

楽 means ease, comfort, or relief, and describes something that requires little effort or causes little trouble. It is a noun and na-adjective: **楽な + noun**, **楽に + verb**, **楽だ／です**.

- この椅子に座ると楽です。 (このいすにすわるとらくです。) - It is comfortable to sit in this chair.
- 荷物が少ないので、移動が楽です。 (にもつがすくないので、いどうがらくです。) - Getting around is easy because I have little luggage.

楽になる means becoming more comfortable or relieved; 楽にする means making something easier or more comfortable.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [楽](../kanji/楽.md) supplies the whole word with on reading ラク. Its other readings belong to other words, such as 楽しい (たのしい), "enjoyable," and 音楽 (おんがく), "music."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[簡単](簡単.md) (かんたん) emphasizes simplicity or lack of difficulty. 楽 often emphasizes the effort or burden experienced by a person. [苦しい](苦しい.md) (くるしい), "painful; distressing," provides a contextual contrast to comfort.

## Distinctive meaning

楽 and 楽しい are not the same adjective. A task may be easy but boring, or enjoyable but demanding. 気が楽になる (きがらくになる), "feel relieved," describes a reduction in mental burden rather than the discovery of entertainment.
