# 踊る (おどる) — to dance

## Meaning

踊る means "to dance." It is a godan verb: 踊ります (おどります), 踊って (おどって), 踊った (おどった). A place takes で; a companion takes と.

祭りでみんなと踊りました。 (まつりでみんなとおどりました。) — I danced with everyone at the festival.

音楽に合わせて踊ります。 (おんがくにあわせておどります。) — I dance to the music.

## How the kanji combine

踊 is the dance character, read おど in 踊る. The final る belongs to the verb's inflection. Do not classify it as ichidan merely because it ends in る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ダンスをする also means "dance." 舞う (まう) can describe dancing, with associations that differ by dance tradition and context; it is not the default replacement in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

踊る names the dancer's activity and is classified as intransitive. A dance name can nevertheless take を: ワルツを踊る, "dance a waltz." This does not mean causing another person to dance.
