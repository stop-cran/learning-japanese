# 主に (おもに) - mainly; primarily

## Meaning

主に is an adverb meaning "mainly," "primarily," or "chiefly."
It identifies the main part of an activity, group, or situation while leaving room for exceptions.
Place it before the phrase or action being emphasized.

- この店では主に野菜を売っています。
  (このみせではおもにやさいをうっています。) - This shop mainly sells vegetables.
- 休日は主に家で過ごします。
  (きゅうじつはおもにいえですごします。) - I spend my days off mainly at home.

The first shop may also sell other goods. The second speaker may sometimes go out.
Neither sentence supplies an exact percentage.

## How the kanji combine

[主](../kanji/主.md) means "main; principal" here, with the native reading おも.
The ending に makes the adverbial expression. Compare 主な仕事 (おもなしごと),
"one's main work": 主な modifies a noun, while 主に modifies the statement about what happens.

## Synonyms and antonyms

おもに and 主に are kana and kanji spellings of the same expression.
[必ず](必ず.md) (かならず), "without fail," is not a synonym: it expresses certainty,
not the main share of something. だけ, "only," is more restrictive in the shop example.

## Distinctive meaning

Translate according to scope. 主に野菜 means vegetables are the main goods;
主に家で means home is the main location. The expression does not inherently mean
"most importantly," which would rank importance rather than describe what predominates.
