# カレンダー - calendar

## Meaning

カレンダー is a calendar used to show dates and organize plans. It may be printed or digital.

- カレンダーで日付を確認しました。 (カレンダーでひづけをかくにんしました。) - I checked the date on the calendar.
- カレンダーに予定を書きます。 (カレンダーによていをかきます。) - I write my plans on the calendar.

In the second example, に marks where the information is entered.

## How the kanji combine

This is an English-derived loanword written in katakana. There are no kanji components. The final ー lengthens ダ: カレンダー.

## Synonyms and antonyms

暦 (こよみ) is the native word for a calendar or calendrical system. 手帳 (てちょう) is a notebook or planner, which may include calendar pages.

## Distinctive meaning

A カレンダー primarily organizes dates; it is not automatically a timetable listing departure times or a diary recording what happened. There is no useful direct antonym.
