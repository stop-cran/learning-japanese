# 冬 (ふゆ) — winter

## Meaning

冬 is the ordinary word for winter.

- 冬は日が短いです。（ふゆは ひが みじかいです。）— The days are short in winter.
- 冬に日本へ行きます。（ふゆに にほんへ いきます。）— I will go to Japan in winter.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native word, not a compound. [冬](../kanji/冬.md) represents the whole word ふゆ; there is no okurigana.

In 冬休み ふゆやすみ, "winter vacation," 冬 specifies the season of the break.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 冬季 とうき means "winter season" and is useful in schedules or seasonal notices; 冬 is the everyday choice.
- [夏](夏.md) なつ, "summer," is the usual contrasting season, not a logical negation of winter.
- [春](春.md) はる, "spring," and [秋](秋.md) あき, "autumn," complete the four-season set.

## Distinctive meaning

冬 names a season, not coldness itself. A cold day outside winter is not automatically 冬. Use 冬の before a noun, as in 冬の朝 ふゆのあさ, "a winter morning."
