# おめでとう (おめでとう) - congratulations; best wishes

## Meaning

おめでとう is a congratulatory expression used for happy milestones or good news. It is an interjection, not a noun meaning "an auspicious occasion." おめでとうございます is the polite version.

- A: 試験に合格したよ。 (しけんにごうかくしたよ。) - I passed the exam!
- B: おめでとう！ - Congratulations!

For a birthday, say お誕生日おめでとうございます (おたんじょうびおめでとうございます), "Happy birthday." A speaker can name the occasion before the expression; no を is needed to make it an object.

## How the kanji combine

Use the kana spelling おめでとう. Dictionaries record rare kanji spellings, but their individual characters are not a useful meaning-by-meaning decomposition of this greeting. The source list's お目出度う is retained as provenance, not recommended as the default spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ありがとう](ありがとう.md) expresses thanks: it is an appropriate response to congratulations, not another congratulatory formula. [あいさつ](あいさつ.md) names greetings and social addresses as a category. There is no general opposite greeting that simply reverses おめでとう.

## Distinctive meaning

Use it when sharing someone's happiness or marking a celebratory event. It is not a general replacement for praising any action: congratulating someone on passing an exam differs from thanking them for lending a book. The polite ending changes the level of courtesy, not the underlying celebratory meaning.
