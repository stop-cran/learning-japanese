# 七日 (なのか) — the 7th of the month; seven days

## Meaning

七日 is a date or a duration: 七月七日 しちがつなのか (July 7th), 七日間 なのかかん (seven days), 七日目 なのかめ (the seventh day), 初七日 しょなのか (the seventh day after a death).

## How the kanji combine

[七](../kanji/七.md) read なの plus [日](../kanji/日.md), the day counter か. This なの is a kun form of 七 found in 七日 and a few other words. See [三日](三日.md) for the series.
It is not しちにち; なぬか is an older variant reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 一週間 いっしゅうかん (one week).
- Contrast: [七月](七月.md) しちがつ (July).

## Distinctive meaning

Date and duration share one word. Compare 七つ ななつ (seven things).
