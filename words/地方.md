# 地方 (ちほう) - region; provincial areas

## Meaning

地方 is a noun for a region, district, or locality. It can also refer collectively to provincial areas in contrast with a central metropolis or center of national activity.

- 東北地方では雪が多いです。 (とうほくちほうではゆきがおおいです。) - There is a lot of snow in the Tohoku region.
- 東京から地方へ移住しました。 (とうきょうからちほうへいじゅうしました。) - I moved from Tokyo to a provincial area.

地方の + noun gives "regional" or "local," as in 地方の新聞 (ちほうのしんぶん), "a regional newspaper."

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) concerns land or place; [方](../kanji/方.md) concerns a direction or area. Together they identify a geographical area. The on readings are チ + ホウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[地域](地域.md) (ちいき), "area; region," overlaps with the geographical sense.

[田舎](田舎.md) (いなか) can mean the countryside or one's home area. 地方 is not necessarily farmland: a regional [都市](都市.md) (とし), "city," is still a city.

## Distinctive meaning

The contrast with [都会](都会.md) (とかい), "city; urban life," is contextual, not a claim that every 地方 is rural. A named region such as 東北地方 includes different kinds of settlements. This reading does not mean "the coast"; that English gloss in the community source is not supported by the inspected dictionary entry.
