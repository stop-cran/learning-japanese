# 七月 (しちがつ) — July

## Meaning

七月 is July: 七月七日 しちがつなのか (July 7th, the Tanabata festival), 七月に夏休みが始まる (summer holidays begin in July).

## How the kanji combine

[七](../kanji/七.md) (seven) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month): the seventh month, read with the on reading しち. The date 七日 is the special なのか.
Duration "seven months" is 七か月 ななかげつ (or しちかげつ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 文月 ふみづき (old name of the seventh month).
- Related: [七日](七日.md) なのか (the 7th day).

## Distinctive meaning

The month is しちがつ, while counting things is ななつ.
