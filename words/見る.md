# 見る (みる) — to see, to look, to watch

## Meaning

見る is to see with the eyes and covers "look" and "watch": 映画を見る (watch a movie), テレビを見ます, 地図を見る (look at a map), 先生に見てもらう (have the teacher look at it).
It also means "to examine, to try": 食べてみる (try eating), 様子を見る (see how things go), 面倒を見る めんどうをみる (look after).

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) with the kun reading み plus okurigana る. An ichidan verb: polite 見ます, negative 見ない, て-form 見て, past 見た, potential 見られる.
Related verbs: 見える みえる (be visible), 見せる みせる (show), 見つける みつける (find).
Spelling variants of the same word: 観る (watch a film or sport, appreciate), 視る (inspect, diagnose), 看る (nurse). 見る is the safe general spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 眺める ながめる (gaze at a view), 覗く のぞく (peek), ご覧になる (honorific), 拝見する はいけんする (humble).
- Related: [見物](見物.md) けんぶつ (sightseeing), 聞く (hear, [聞](../kanji/聞.md)).

## Distinctive meaning

〜てみる means "try doing": 着てみる (try on). 見る is intentional (you look), where 見える is "can be seen" and takes が: 山が見える.
