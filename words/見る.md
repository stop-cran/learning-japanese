# 見る (みる) — to see, to look, to watch

## Meaning

見る is to see with the eyes and covers "look" and "watch": 映画を見る（えいがをみる）, "watch a movie"; テレビを見ます（テレビをみます）, "I watch TV"; 地図を見る（ちずをみる）, "look at a map"; 先生に見てもらう（せんせいにみてもらう）, "have the teacher look at it."
Other uses include 食べてみる（たべてみる）, "try eating"; 様子を見る（ようすをみる）, "see how things go"; and 面倒を見る（めんどうをみる）, "look after."

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) with the kun reading み plus okurigana る. An ichidan verb: polite 見ます, negative 見ない, て-form 見て, past 見た, potential 見られる.
Related verbs: 見える みえる (be visible), 見せる みせる (show), 見つける みつける (find).
JMdict groups 見る, 観る (watch a film or sport, appreciate), and 視る (look closely; inspect) in one entry. For example, 映画を観る（えいがをみる） means "watch a movie." 見る is the safe general spelling.
Related みる verbs listed separately in JMdict are 診る（みる）, "examine medically," and 看る（みる）, "look after; nurse." The specialized medical spelling is 診る, not 視る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 眺める ながめる (gaze at a view), 覗く のぞく (peek), ご覧になる (honorific), 拝見する はいけんする (humble).
- Related: [見物](見物.md) けんぶつ (sightseeing), 聞く (hear, [聞](../kanji/聞.md)).

## Distinctive meaning

〜てみる means "try doing": 着てみる（きてみる）, "try on." In the basic visual contrast, 見る describes deliberately looking at or watching something, while 見える describes visibility: 山が見える（やまがみえる）, "The mountain is visible." が marks what is visible. This contrast does not describe every use of 見る.
