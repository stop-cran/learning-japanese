# 実 (み) - fruit; substance

## Meaning

実 (み) is a noun for a plant's fruit, nut, or seed. It can also refer to substance or meaningful content.

- 庭の木に赤い実がなりました。 (にわのきにあかいみがなりました。) - The tree in the garden bore red fruit.
- 実のある話し合いができました。 (みのあるはなしあいができました。) - We had a substantive discussion.

The second example means the discussion had worthwhile content, not literal fruit. 実がなる (みがなる) describes fruit developing on a plant.

The expression 努力が実を結ぶ (どりょくがみをむすぶ), "effort bears fruit," conveys a good result. Learn that whole expression rather than substituting 実 for every English "result."

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) alone writes the native noun み. The same character has ジツ readings elsewhere; the plant and substance examples here use み.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[果物](果物.md) (くだもの) names fruit as a food category. 実 can describe fruit or nuts on a plant without implying that they are food for people. [種](種.md) (たね) specifies a seed, whereas 実 has a broader range.

## Distinctive meaning

A plant description and a discussion description use different senses of the same noun. Neither justifies translating 実 as any kind of "content" without context. 実のある is a useful fixed pattern for something substantive; the fruit-bearing image also appears in the separately recorded expression 実を結ぶ.
