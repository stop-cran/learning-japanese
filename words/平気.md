# 平気 (へいき) - untroubled; all right

## Meaning

平気 describes being untroubled, unconcerned or composed. In conversation it can
also mean all right, fine or able to cope. Use 平気だ/です, 平気な + noun,
and 平気で + action.

- 怖い話を聞いても、彼は平気な顔をしていました。 (こわいはなしをきいても、かれはへいきなかおをしていました。) - Even after hearing the scary story, he looked untroubled.
- 「痛くない？」「うん、平気。」 (「いたくない？」「うん、へいき。」) - "Doesn't it hurt?" "No, I'm all right."

## How the kanji combine

[平](../kanji/平.md) contributes levelness or calm; [気](../kanji/気.md)
contributes a person's spirit or state of mind. The compound evokes an undisturbed
state: ヘイ + キ, not a literal measurement of flatness.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冷静](冷静.md) (れいせい) and [落ち着く](落ち着く.md) (おちつく) overlap in
composure. [大丈夫](大丈夫.md) (だいじょうぶ) overlaps in reassuring someone
that things are all right. [安心](安心.md) (あんしん) concerns freedom from worry;
平気 can also imply simply not caring about something.

## Distinctive meaning

平気でうそをつく (へいきでうそをつく) means lie without compunction or concern,
not necessarily praise for remaining calm. Context decides whether the lack of concern
is admirable, reassuring or objectionable.
Being 平気 about an injury reports the person's reaction; it is not a medical
guarantee that no injury exists.
