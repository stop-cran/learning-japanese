# 佳作 (かさく) — fine work; honorable mention

## Meaning

佳作 means a fine or excellent work. In a contest, it can also mean a work that receives an honorable mention.

- この作品は、絵画コンクールで佳作に選ばれました。 (このさくひんは、かいがこんくーるでかさくにえらばれました。) — This work received an honorable mention in an art contest.
- この短編は、読み返す価値のある佳作です。 (このたんぺんは、よみかえすかちのあるかさくです。) — This short story is a fine work worth rereading.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[佳](../kanji/佳.md) means excellent or pleasing, and [作](../kanji/作.md) means a work or creation. Together they form a word for a fine work, read かさく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[傑作](傑作.md) and [名作](名作.md) can both describe outstanding works. 佳作 means a fine work and can also name an honorable mention in a contest.

## Distinctive meaning

In a contest, 佳作 can mean a work that receives an honorable mention.
