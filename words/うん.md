# うん - yeah, informal yes

## Meaning

うん is an informal affirmative response, used with friends, family, and others with whom casual speech is appropriate.

- 「明日も来る？」「うん、来るよ。」 (「あしたもくる？」「うん、くるよ。」) - "Are you coming tomorrow too?" "Yeah, I am."

It can also acknowledge that you are listening. Such a response does not always promise agreement with everything the other person has said.

## How the kanji combine

This spoken response is written in kana. It is unrelated to 運 (うん), "luck," despite the identical short reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

はい is the usual polite affirmative. ううん is an informal negative response, "no"; the extra vowel matters. いいえ is a more formal negative.

## Distinctive meaning

Length and intonation affect interpretation. A drawn-out うーん often expresses thought or hesitation rather than a clear yes. In situations requiring deference, choose はい instead of assuming that adding a friendly tone makes うん polite.
