# 木曜 (もくよう) - Thursday

## Meaning

木曜 is Thursday, the shorter form of [木曜日](木曜日.md) (もくようび). It is a time noun used in calendars, schedules, and statements about a day.

- この店は木曜が休みです。 (このみせはもくようがやすみです。) - This shop is closed on Thursdays.
- 木曜に荷物が届きます。 (もくようににもつがとどきます。) - The parcel will arrive on Thursday.

The shop schedule supports a recurring interpretation in the first sentence. The second refers to a particular Thursday established by context. 毎週木曜 (まいしゅうもくよう) explicitly means "every Thursday."

## How the kanji combine

[木](../kanji/木.md), "wood; tree," identifies this member of the weekday series. [曜](../kanji/曜.md) is the shared weekday element. The on readings モク + ヨウ give もくよう. The word is a calendar name, not a description of a wooden object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

木曜日 names the same weekday. [火曜](火曜.md) (かよう), Tuesday, is another weekday, not an opposite. Both shorter names omit the final 日 without changing the day they identify.

## Distinctive meaning

On a calendar, 木 may abbreviate Thursday further. Outside that setting, 木 can simply mean a tree or wood, so the single-character abbreviation depends on context. 木曜 itself does not specify a date or year.
