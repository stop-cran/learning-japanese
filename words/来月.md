# 来月 (らいげつ) — next month

## Meaning

来月 means the next calendar month relative to now. If it is October, 来月 is November, regardless of today's date.

- 来月、引っ越します。 (らいげつ、ひっこします。) — I'm moving next month.
- 来月の予定を教えてください。 (らいげつのよていをおしえてください。) — Please tell me your plans for next month.

As a time expression, 来月 normally does not need に. Use の before a following noun.

## How the kanji combine

[来](../kanji/来.md), "come," qualifies [月](../kanji/月.md), "month": the coming month. The on readings are ライ + ゲツ, not つき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

今月 (こんげつ) is this month, and 先月 (せんげつ) is last month. [再来月](再来月.md) (さらいげつ) is the month after next.

## Distinctive meaning

来月 does not necessarily mean a full month from today. 一か月後 (いっかげつご), "one month later," measures an interval from a reference point. Use 来月 when identifying the next month on the calendar rather than an exact duration.
