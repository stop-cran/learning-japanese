# 統計 (とうけい) - statistics

## Meaning

統計 is statistics: numerical information compiled from observations or surveys,
or the activity of compiling such information.
As a verb, Nを統計する means to compile statistics on N.

- 人口の統計を調べました。 (じんこうのとうけいをしらべました。) - I looked up population statistics.
- 調査結果を統計しました。 (ちょうさけっかをとうけいしました。) - We compiled statistics from the survey results.

統計を取る (とうけいをとる), "gather or compile statistics," is another useful noun pattern.
統計学 (とうけいがく) names the academic field of statistics.

## How the kanji combine

統 contributes bringing information together as a whole; [計](../kanji/計.md) contributes counting or measurement.
The on readings are トウ + ケイ.
The association is compiling numerical information, not scattering objects.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計算](計算.md) (けいさん) is calculation.
[調査](調査.md) (ちょうさ) is an investigation or survey that may provide the data.
[資料](資料.md) (しりょう) means materials or data more broadly.
Neither an individual calculation nor every piece of reference material is necessarily statistics.

## Distinctive meaning

Statistics may be figures, summaries, or results compiled from many observations.
Do not confuse the word with English "scattering" or "dispersion":
the source list's gloss is erroneous for 統計.
Statistical analysis can study dispersion, but that does not make it this word's general meaning.
