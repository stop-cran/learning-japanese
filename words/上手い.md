# 上手い (うまい) - skillful; tasty

## Meaning

うまい is an i-adjective meaning "good at something" or "delicious."
For taste, write うまい, 旨い, or 美味い rather than making 上手い your default.
The food use is casual and can sound blunt in a formal setting.

- 兄は料理が上手い。 (あにはりょうりがうまい。) - My older brother is good at cooking.
- このスープはうまいね。 (このスープはうまいね。) - This soup is delicious.

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md), "up," and [手](../kanji/手.md), "hand," associate this spelling
with skill. The native reading うま- is assigned to the combination, not obtained
by adding ordinary readings of the two characters; い marks the adjective ending.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上手 (じょうず) is a na-adjective for skill; おいしい is the safer neutral
choice for delicious food. 下手 (へた), "unskillful," contrasts with the skill
sense, while まずい contrasts with good taste.

## Distinctive meaning

うまくできた means "it turned out well." This useful adverbial form expresses
successful performance, not necessarily deliciousness. The negative is
うまくない, and the past is うまかった.
