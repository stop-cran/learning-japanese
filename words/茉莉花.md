# 茉莉花 (まつりか) - Arabian jasmine

## Meaning

茉莉花 names **Arabian jasmine (Jasminum sambac)**. The dictionary also records 茉莉 (まつり) for the same plant.

- 茉莉花の花が咲きました。 (まつりかのはながさきました。) - The Arabian jasmine flowers bloomed.
- 庭で茉莉花を育てています。 (にわでまつりかをそだてています。) - I am growing Arabian jasmine in the garden.

These examples name the plant. They do not claim that every plant called "jasmine" in English is this species.

## How the kanji combine

Treat **茉莉** as the plant-name unit まつり, not as two independently meaningful everyday nouns. Within it, [莉](../kanji/莉.md) contributes リ; [花](../kanji/花.md) contributes カ, "flower," in the longer name **まつりか**. The whole word names the plant, not only a cut blossom.

## Synonyms and antonyms

茉莉 (まつり) names the same plant. ジャスミン is a broader jasmine name; identifying a plant specifically as 茉莉花 requires the intended species, not merely a pleasant floral smell. [花](花.md) (はな), "flower," is not a synonym for this particular plant. There is no useful antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Read this entry **まつりか**. Do not confuse the shorter plant name まつり with 祭り (まつり), "festival." The shared sound does not give the plant a festival meaning or establish an etymology.
