# 運 (うん) - luck; fortune

## Meaning

運 is luck or fortune: how events turn out when they are not entirely under
someone's control. It is a noun. 運がいい means "be lucky," and 運が悪い
(うんがわるい) means "be unlucky."

- ちょうどバスが来た。今日は運がいい。 (ちょうどバスがきた。きょうはうんがいい。) - The bus came just then. I'm lucky today.
- 試験に受かるかどうかを運に任せたくない。 (しけんにうかるかどうかをうんにまかせたくない。) - I don't want to leave passing the exam to luck.

運に任せる means "leave to chance"; に marks what is entrusted with the outcome.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [運](../kanji/運.md) uses its on reading ウン for this noun.
It also occurs in words about carrying or movement, but 運 alone here means
fortune, not transportation. Learn that whole-word meaning rather than translating
every occurrence of the character identically.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[幸運](幸運.md) (こううん) specifically identifies good fortune.
[幸せ](幸せ.md) (しあわせ) concerns happiness or a fortunate condition; it is
not a substitute for chance in every construction. Good and bad luck are expressed
by changing the description of 運, not by treating 運 itself as necessarily good.

## Distinctive meaning

運 can describe a single lucky break or a broader run of events. Saying 運がよかった,
"I was lucky," does not necessarily deny effort; it highlights favorable
circumstances. In speech, distinguish this noun from the unrelated casual response
うん, "yeah."
