# 向こう (むこう) - over there; the other side

## Meaning

向こう names the far side, opposite side, or a place over there. It is a location noun.

- 川の向こうに学校があります。 (かわのむこうにがっこうがあります。) - There is a school on the other side of the river.
- 向こうで少し待ちましょう。 (むこうですこしまちましょう。) - Let's wait over there for a little while.

Noun + の向こう identifies what lies between the speaker and the far side. に locates something; で marks where an action happens.

## How the kanji combine

[向](../kanji/向.md) is associated with facing or direction. In 向こう it writes む, followed by こう. The final vowel is long; this is a noun, not a verb ending in う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

あそこ also means "over there" but need not emphasize a far side. こちら (this side) and 手前 (てまえ, the near side) provide useful spatial contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

向こう depends on the speaker's viewpoint. Across a river, beyond a building, and a distant part of a room can all be 向こう in the appropriate situation. It does not specify a fixed compass direction.
