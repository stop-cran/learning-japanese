# 順々 (じゅんじゅん) - in order; one after another

## Meaning

順々 expresses proceeding in sequence or taking turns. A useful adverbial pattern is 順々に.

- 一人ずつ、順々に名前を呼びます。 (ひとりずつ、じゅんじゅんになまえをよびます。) - I will call the names one by one, in order.
- 先生は生徒の質問に順々に答えました。 (せんせいはせいとのしつもんにじゅんじゅんにこたえました。) - The teacher answered the students' questions one after another.

順順 is another dictionary spelling. Learn the whole expression rather than treating the repeated character as two independent nouns.

## How the kanji combine

[順](../kanji/順.md) concerns order or sequence. 々 is an iteration mark repeating the preceding kanji, not a separate kanji requiring its own character card. The reading is じゅんじゅん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[順](順.md), [順番](順番.md), and [番](番.md) concern order or a turn. [順序](順序.md) names a sequence or procedure. [次々](次々.md) also means one after another, but does not by itself require an assigned order.

## Distinctive meaning

順々に emphasizes orderly progression through the people or things concerned. It does not inherently mean that the action happens quickly, that everyone acts simultaneously, or that the sequence repeats indefinitely.
