# 責める (せめる) - to blame; reproach

## Meaning

責める is a transitive ichidan verb meaning "blame," "reproach," or "criticize."
The person blamed takes を. A phrase with ことで can explain what they are blamed for.

- 失敗したことで彼を責めないでください。 (しっぱいしたことでかれをせめないでください。) - Please do not blame him for failing.
- 彼女は自分を責めていました。 (かのじょはじぶんをせめていました。) - She was blaming herself.

Useful forms include 責めます, 責めた, and 責めない.
In the passive, 彼に責められた (かれにせめられた) means "I was blamed by him";
に identifies the person doing the blaming.

## How the kanji combine

[責](../kanji/責.md) carries the association of blame or responsibility.
The native verb reads せめる, with める written as okurigana.
Compare [責任](責任.md) (せきにん), "responsibility," which uses the on reading セキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[叱る](叱る.md) (しかる), "scold," emphasizes a reprimand; 責める emphasizes holding someone
at fault or reproaching them. The same event may involve both.
攻める (せめる), "attack," shares the pronunciation but is a different written word.
Praising or forgiving someone can contrast contextually with blaming, without being an exact opposite in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

Responsibility and blame are related but not identical.
Saying that someone 責めた reports their reproach, not proof that its target was actually at fault.
The reflexive pattern 自分を責める is useful for describing self-blame without confusing it
with the passive "be blamed by someone else."
