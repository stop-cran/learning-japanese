# ほんのり — faintly or subtly

## Meaning

ほんのり describes something perceptible but gentle or slight, such as a scent, color, or flavor.

- 部屋に花の香りがほんのり漂っている。
  — A faint floral scent is drifting through the room.
- 寒さで頬がほんのり赤くなった。
  — The cold made my cheeks turn slightly red.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ほんのり suggests a delicate trace; it is weaker than a strong or vivid effect.

## Distinctive meaning

It commonly modifies a sensory impression or a change that is only slight.
