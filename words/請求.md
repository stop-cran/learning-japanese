# 請求 (せいきゅう) - claim; billing; request

## Meaning

請求 is a noun for requesting or claiming something, including billing someone for payment.
With する it is transitive: person or organization + に + requested item or amount + を請求する.

- 店は客に修理代を請求しました。 (みせはきゃくにしゅうりだいをせいきゅうしました。) - The shop billed the customer for the repair cost.
- 大学に資料を請求しました。 (だいがくにしりょうをせいきゅうしました。) - I requested information materials from the university.

The second example shows that 請求 need not concern money.
請求書 (せいきゅうしょ) is a bill or invoice, the document requesting payment.

## How the kanji combine

請 contributes asking or requesting, and [求](../kanji/求.md) contributes seeking or demanding.
The two related meanings reinforce a request directed toward obtaining something.
The on readings are セイ + キュウ; keep the long vowels in both parts.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[要求](要求.md) (ようきゅう), "demand; requirement," overlaps but is broader in some contexts.
[頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ), "ask; request," works in many interpersonal requests.
[支払う](支払う.md) (しはらう), "pay," is the responding action in a billing situation,
not a synonym for issuing the request.

## Distinctive meaning

請求 presents an explicit request or claim, often in administrative or business procedures.
It does not guarantee that the recipient has paid, supplied the materials, or accepted the claim.
A [料金](料金.md) (りょうきん), "charge," can be the amount requested, while 請求 is
the act of requesting it.
