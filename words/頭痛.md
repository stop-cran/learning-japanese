# 頭痛 (ずつう) - headache

## Meaning

頭痛 is the noun "headache." Useful expressions are 頭痛がする, "have a headache," and 頭痛がある, "have a headache/be experiencing headaches." Although する occurs in the first expression, learn the whole pattern with が.

- 朝から頭痛がします。 (あさからずつうがします。) - I have had a headache since this morning.
- 頭痛がひどいので、少し休みます。 (ずつうがひどいので、すこしやすみます。) - My headache is bad, so I will rest for a while.

から marks when the symptom started, and ので gives the reason for resting.

## How the kanji combine

[頭](../kanji/頭.md) identifies the head, and [痛](../kanji/痛.md) identifies pain. This body-part-plus-symptom compound means pain in the head. Learn the on reading ずつう, not あたまいた.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頭](頭.md)が[痛い](痛い.md) (あたまがいたい), "my head hurts," describes the same symptom with a noun and adjective. [苦痛](苦痛.md) (くつう), "pain; suffering," is broader and does not locate the pain in the head.

## Distinctive meaning

頭痛 names a symptom, not its cause or a specific diagnosis. The noun is useful when reporting how one feels, but the word itself does not identify migraine or another particular condition. Keep the whole compound separate from the standalone reading 頭 (あたま).
