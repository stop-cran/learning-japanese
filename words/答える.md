# 答える (こたえる) — answer; reply

## Meaning

答える means answering or replying. The question being answered normally takes に;
the content of a quoted answer takes と.

- 質問に答えてください。 (しつもんにこたえてください。) — Please answer the question.
- 「はい」と答えました。 (「はい」とこたえました。) — I answered, "Yes."

## How the kanji combine

This is one native verb. [答](../kanji/答.md) supplies こた, and える is okurigana.
It is an ichidan verb: 答えます (こたえます), 答えて (こたえて), 答えない (こたえない).
The related noun is 答え (こたえ), "answer."

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 返事する (へんじする) means replying or responding; it can be as simple as responding when someone calls your name.
- 回答する (かいとうする) also means answering, often in a more formal or written context.
- [質問](質問.md)する (しつもんする), "ask a question," is the complementary conversational action, not a strict antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

答える is a natural everyday verb for supplying an answer. English "answer a question" has a direct object,
but the corresponding Japanese pattern is 質問に答える, not a mechanical replacement of English word order with を.
In 答えを書く (こたえをかく, write the answer), を instead belongs to the verb "write."
