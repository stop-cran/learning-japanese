# 浴びせる (あびせる) - pour over; shower someone with

## Meaning

浴びせる means pouring or showering something over a target. It is a transitive ichidan verb: 浴びせます, 浴びせない, 浴びせて.
The material takes を; the person or thing receiving it takes に.

- 子どもたちは互いに水を浴びせた。 (こどもたちはたがいにみずをあびせた。) - The children splashed water over one another.

The same verb can describe directing a stream of questions or emotional words at someone.

- 記者たちは選手に質問を浴びせた。 (きしゃたちはせんしゅにしつもんをあびせた。) - The reporters bombarded the athlete with questions.

## How the kanji combine

[浴](../kanji/浴.md) connects the verb with bathing or exposure. 浴びせる is one native verb with びせる as okurigana, not a compound of separate words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[注ぐ](注ぐ.md) (そそぐ) also covers pouring, including pouring into a vessel; 浴びせる emphasizes directing something over a target.
The receiving viewpoint is taught in [あびる](あびる.md), usually written 浴びる: 水を浴びる means receiving water over oneself.

かける also covers pouring or splashing something onto a target, usually written in kana in that sense.
The linked [掛ける](掛ける.md) article introduces its hanging sense, not every meaning of the verb.

[流す](流す.md) (ながす) also has a physical use for washing by pouring water over the body.
Their uses overlap, but they are not unrestricted synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

浴びる and 浴びせる are **both transitive** in Japanese. With 浴びる, the subject receives the water or attention.
With 浴びせる, the subject directs it at a target: 選手が質問を浴びた, "the athlete was showered with questions,"
versus 記者が選手に質問を浴びせた, "the reporter showered the athlete with questions."
They are not an intransitive/transitive pair, and 浴びせる does not simply mean taking a shower.
