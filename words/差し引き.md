# 差し引き (さしひき) - deduction; balance; ebb and flow

## Meaning

差し引き names subtraction or deduction, and the resulting balance.
差し引きする is transitive in this sense: the amount deducted takes を.

- 前払いの分を差し引きしました。 (まえばらいのぶんをさしひきしました。) - I deducted the amount paid in advance.
- 差し引きで三千円の黒字です。 (さしひきでさんぜんえんのくろじです。) - The net result is a surplus of three thousand yen.

It also names ebb and flow, or rise and fall:
潮の差し引き (しおのさしひき), "the ebb and flow of the tide,"
and 体温の差し引き (たいおんのさしひき), "rises and falls in body temperature."
The dictionary classifies the する verb as intransitive for this second sense.

## How the kanji combine

[差](../kanji/差.md) and [引](../kanji/引.md) form the native compound noun さしひき,
related to 差し引く (さしひく), "deduct."
引く supplies the subtracting association in the financial use.
Do not read the compound サイン from its isolated on readings.
差引き and dictionary-listed 差引 are spelling variants.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[引く](引く.md) (ひく) also means subtract in arithmetic, alongside its physical pulling uses.
[残り](残り.md) (のこり) is what remains, including a financial balance.
[足す](足す.md) (たす), "add," contrasts with subtraction, not with every ebb-and-flow use.

## Distinctive meaning

A balance need not be a surplus: the result may be a deficit or zero.
This noun does not mean physical balance when standing on one foot.
Do not carry the object-taking grammar of deduction into the tide's own rise and fall.
