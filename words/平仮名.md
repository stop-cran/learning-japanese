# 平仮名 (ひらがな) - hiragana

## Meaning

平仮名 names hiragana, one of the Japanese kana syllabaries.
Its name is usually written ひらがな; 平仮名 is the kanji spelling.
Hiragana is used for particles, inflectional endings, and many words
written without kanji.

- 名前を平仮名で書いてください。 (なまえをひらがなでかいてください。) - Please write your name in hiragana.
- 「さくら」は平仮名で書かれています。 (「さくら」はひらがなでかかれています。) - "Sakura" is written in hiragana.

で identifies the script used, not the language into which a name is translated.

## How the kanji combine

[平](../kanji/平.md) supplies ひら in this script name;
[仮名](仮名.md) (かな) is the kana writing-system term.
Its beginning is voiced here: ひら + がな.
[名](../kanji/名.md) remains part of that unit, not a separately
added reading. Learn 仮名/かな as a whole rather than inventing
freely reusable individual readings from this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[片仮名](片仮名.md) (かたかな), usually written カタカナ,
is the other kana syllabary.
仮名 is the broader term covering both scripts.
[五十音](五十音.md) (ごじゅうおん) concerns the conventional kana
sound arrangement and ordering, not exclusively hiragana.

## Distinctive meaning

The script name does not mean "47 syllables": historical kana,
modern basic characters, voiced forms, and combinations are different
things to count. Nor does writing a word in hiragana make its origin
native Japanese. The script used and the word's origin are separate.
The kanji spelling 平仮名 and kana spelling ひらがな name the same script.
