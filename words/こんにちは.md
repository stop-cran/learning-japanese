# こんにちは (こんにちは) - hello; good afternoon

## Meaning

こんにちは is a daytime greeting, translated as "hello," "good day," or "good afternoon." It is an interjection, so it can stand alone rather than needing a following verb.

- A: こんにちは、田中さん。 (こんにちは、たなかさん。) - Hello, Tanaka.
- B: こんにちは。今日はいい天気ですね。 (こんにちは。きょうはいいてんきですね。) - Hello. Nice weather today, isn't it?

In the greeting, the final は is pronounced **わ**. The standard written form remains こんにちは.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses the usual kana spelling. In the dictionary spelling 今日は,
今日 is read こんにち, with on readings コン + ニチ, followed by the topic particle は.
The complete greeting is a fixed Japanese expression, not an ordinary statement about today.
Kana helps distinguish it from [今日](今日.md) (きょう), "today," followed by は,
as in the second example.

## Synonyms and antonyms

おはようございます is a morning greeting; こんばんは is an evening greeting. These are alternatives selected by the situation and time, not antonyms. Saying goodbye has a different conversational function.

## Distinctive meaning

こんにちわ follows the pronunciation but is marked irregular in the dictionary. Learn こんにちは for standard writing. There is no exact clock boundary built into this word's definition, and a suitable greeting also depends on the relationship and situation. Do not read the final は as *ha* simply because this is a standalone greeting.
