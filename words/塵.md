# 塵 (ごみ) — rubbish; garbage

## Meaning

ごみ means rubbish, garbage, or unwanted waste. Although the headword preserves the spelling 塵, ordinary notices and everyday writing commonly use ごみ or ゴミ.

- ごみはここに捨てないでください。 (ごみはここにすてないでください。) — Please don't throw rubbish away here.
- ごみを袋に入れました。 (ごみをふくろにいれました。) — I put the garbage in a bag.

## How the kanji combine

塵 is a single character associated with dust and refuse. The reading selected here is ごみ; ちり is another word written with this character. Do not assume the written character always has this card's reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ほこり means dust; ごみ covers a broader range of discarded material. ごみ箱 (ごみばこ) is a rubbish bin. There is no direct opposite noun.

## Distinctive meaning

ごみを出す (ごみをだす) means putting rubbish out for collection, while ごみを捨てる (ごみをすてる) means discarding it. Local rules determine when and where to put it out; the word itself does not specify a waste category.
