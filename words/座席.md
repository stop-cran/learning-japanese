# 座席 (ざせき) - seat; seating

## Meaning

座席 is a noun for a seat, especially in a public place or a setting with assigned seating, such as a train, aircraft, theater, or classroom. It can refer to the seat itself or the place allocated to a person.

- 窓側の座席を予約しました。 (まどがわのざせきをよやくしました。) - I reserved a window seat.
- 座席の番号を確認してください。 (ざせきのばんごうをかくにんしてください。) - Please check your seat number.

座席に座る (ざせきにすわる) means "sit in a seat"; に marks the place occupied.

## How the kanji combine

[座](../kanji/座.md) contributes sitting or a seated position. [席](../kanji/席.md) contributes a seat or allocated place. Their related meanings reinforce a place provided for sitting.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[席](席.md) (せき) overlaps closely and is useful in everyday requests such as 席を予約する, "reserve a seat." [いす](いす.md) names a chair as furniture. A seat can be part of a bench or vehicle rather than a separate chair.

## Distinctive meaning

座席 does not by itself mean "reserved seat": a vehicle can have 座席 without assigning them to individual passengers. 指定席 (していせき) explicitly identifies reserved/assigned seating. Standing space is a practical contrast, not a direct lexical opposite of 座席.
