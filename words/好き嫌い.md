# 好き嫌い (すききらい) - likes and dislikes; preferences

## Meaning

好き嫌い is a person's likes and dislikes or preferences. In food contexts, it often concerns pickiness or the foods a person will and will not eat.

- 食べ物の好き嫌いはありますか。 (たべもののすききらいはありますか。) - Do you have any food likes and dislikes?
- 彼は人の好き嫌いがはっきりしています。 (かれはひとのすききらいがはっきりしています。) - He has clear likes and dislikes when it comes to people.

The noun can take がある or が激しい (がはげしい), "be pronounced/strong"; it is not an i-adjective just because it ends in い.

## How the kanji combine

This pairs [好き](好き.md), liking, and [嫌い](嫌い.md), dislike: an opposite pair expressed through native readings. [好](../kanji/好.md) is す in 好き, and 嫌 is きら in 嫌い. The whole reading is すききらい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[好み](好み.md) is a preference or personal taste, often emphasizing what someone favors. [趣味](趣味.md) can also concern personal taste, besides its hobby meaning. [好き好き](好き好き.md) emphasizes that the choice is a matter of individual taste.

## Distinctive meaning

好き嫌い can simply identify preferences, but warnings about 好き嫌い in food often criticize fussiness. It is not limited to food, nor is "taste" here necessarily [味](味.md), the flavor of something.
