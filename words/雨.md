# 雨 (あめ) — rain

## Meaning

雨 is rain: 雨が降る ふる (it rains), 雨がやむ (the rain stops), 雨の日 あめのひ (a rainy day), 大雨 おおあめ (heavy rain), 小雨 こさめ (light rain).
Compounds use あま-: 雨傘 あまがさ (umbrella for rain), 雨戸 あまど (storm shutter), 雨宿り あまやどり (taking shelter from rain).

## How the kanji combine

[雨](../kanji/雨.md) alone with the kun reading あめ. The on reading ウ is used in formal compounds: 雨天 うてん (rainy weather), 豪雨 ごうう (torrential rain).
The special word 梅雨 つゆ (rainy season) uses the same character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 雪 ゆき (snow), 雲 くも (cloud), 傘 かさ (umbrella).
- Antonym: 晴れ はれ (fine weather).
- Homophone: 飴 あめ (candy), distinguished by the pitch accent (雨 is あ↘め, 飴 is あ↗め in Tokyo).

## Distinctive meaning

雨 is the plain noun. The verb is 降る (the rain falls), and passive 雨に降られる means "to get caught in the rain".
