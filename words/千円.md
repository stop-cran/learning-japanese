# 千円 (せんえん) — 1,000 yen

## Meaning

千円 is 1,000 yen: 千円札 せんえんさつ (1,000-yen note), 千円ください (1,000 yen, please), 二千円 にせんえん (2,000 yen).

## How the kanji combine

[千](../kanji/千.md) (thousand) plus [円](../kanji/円.md) (yen). The number is not written 一千円 in ordinary use; but 一万円 keeps the 一 (see [一万円](一万円.md)).
Sound change: 三千円 さんぜんえん, 八千円 はっせんえん (see [三千](三千.md)).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: [百円](百円.md) ひゃくえん (100 yen), 一万円 いちまんえん (10,000 yen).

## Distinctive meaning

千円 is the lowest banknote value in common use today (the 1,000-yen note).
