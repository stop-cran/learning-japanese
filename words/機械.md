# 機械 (きかい) - machine; mechanism

## Meaning

機械 is a noun for a machine or mechanism: equipment whose parts work together to
perform a task. Useful combinations include 機械を使う, "use a machine," and
機械が動く (きかいがうごく), "a machine operates."

- この機械は紙を小さく切ります。
  (このきかいはかみをちいさくきります。) - This machine cuts paper into small pieces.
- 機械が故障したので、作業を止めました。
  (きかいがこしょうしたので、さぎょうをとめました。)
  - The machine broke down, so we stopped working.

The word identifies the equipment, not the person operating it.

## How the kanji combine

[機](../kanji/機.md) contributes machinery or a mechanism, and 械 contributes an implement
or apparatus. The related meanings reinforce one another. The on readings are キ + カイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[道具](道具.md) (どうぐ) is a tool or implement, including things without a working mechanism.
器械 (also きかい) is another spelling in the dictionary entry, particularly associated
with the instrument or apparatus sense. This is a spelling tendency, not an absolute division.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 機械 with [機会](機会.md) (きかい), "opportunity." They sound alike
but name different things: 機械を使う uses equipment, while 機会を利用する
(きかいをりようする) takes advantage of an opportunity. Context and kanji distinguish them.
