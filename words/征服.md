# 征服 (せいふく) — conquest; overcoming

## Meaning

征服 is conquering a country or people by force and bringing them under control. It can also mean overcoming a difficulty or conquering something hard to reach, such as a mountain.

- その国は、周りの小さな国々を次々に征服した。 (そのくには、まわりのちいさなくにぐにをつぎつぎにせいふくした。) — That country conquered the small neighboring countries one after another.
- 彼らは三日かけて、ついにその山を征服した。 (かれらはみっかかけて、ついにそのやまをせいふくした。) — After three days, they finally conquered the mountain.

## How the kanji combine

[征](../kanji/征.md) (せい) means to subdue by force, and [服](../kanji/服.md) (ふく) here means to submit, not clothes. 征服 is bringing an opponent to submission.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[克服](克服.md) (こくふく) is overcoming a difficulty or a weakness. 征服 can also be used of a difficulty, but its first sense is military conquest. [支配](支配.md) (しはい) is control or rule, which may follow a conquest.

## Distinctive meaning

征服 and 制服 (せいふく), “uniform,” sound the same. The context and the first kanji tell them apart.
