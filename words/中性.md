# 中性 (ちゅうせい) - neutrality; neuter gender

## Meaning

中性 is a neutral property or condition, including chemical or
electrical neutrality. In chemistry it contrasts with acidity and
alkalinity; in an electrical description it can mean having no net
charge. The technical context determines which property is neutral.

- この洗剤は中性です。 (このせんざいはちゅうせいです。) - This detergent is neutral.
- ドイツ語には中性名詞があります。 (ドイツごにはちゅうせいめいしがあります。) - German has neuter nouns.

The second sentence uses the grammatical sense, neuter gender.
中性 is a noun; 中性の + noun and compounds such as 中性名詞
are useful patterns, not an unrestricted 中性な rule.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) contributes an intermediate or neutral
position, and [性](../kanji/性.md) a property or category.
The combination identifies a neutral property or gender category,
depending on context. Read チュウ + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

酸性 (さんせい), acidic, and アルカリ性 (アルカリせい),
alkaline, contrast with chemical neutrality.
[男性](男性.md) (だんせい) and [女性](女性.md) (じょせい)
can name grammatical masculine and feminine in language descriptions.
Those are noun classes, not claims about an object's human identity.

## Distinctive meaning

Additional dictionary uses concern sexlessness or androgyny,
including qualities not assigned to either a male or female category.
Do not take the community list's "sterility" as a medical claim of
infertility, or apply the word as an automatic label for a person's
gender identity.

The dictionary also uses the gloss "indifference" in its neutrality
sense. Context is still necessary: 中性 is not a general replacement
for saying that one does not care about something.
