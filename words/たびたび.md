# たびたび - repeatedly; often

## Meaning

たびたび is an adverb meaning repeatedly, often, or on many occasions. Put it before the predicate without に.

- この店にはたびたび来ます。 (このみせにはたびたびきます。) - I come to this shop often.
- たびたび連絡してすみません。 (たびたびれんらくしてすみません。) - Sorry for contacting you repeatedly.

The first example describes recurring visits. The second acknowledges several contacts, which may be inconvenient to the recipient. The word does not state an exact count or interval.

## How the kanji combine

This page uses kana. The corresponding spelling 度々 repeats 度, read たび in the sense of an occasion or time; 々 is the repetition mark. The repeated form expresses recurrence. Do not confuse that occasion/time meaning with 旅 (たび), "journey."

## Synonyms and antonyms

よく can mean often, but it has other uses such as doing something well. 時々 (ときどき), "sometimes," normally suggests a different frequency emphasis. These words describe tendencies, not fixed numerical ranges.

## Distinctive meaning

たびたび highlights separate recurring occasions, not one continuous action. Repeatedly visiting a shop differs from staying in it for a long time. It fits both neutral descriptions and polite apologies, so the word itself does not prove that the repeated event is welcome or unwelcome.
