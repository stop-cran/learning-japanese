# 最初 (さいしょ) - beginning; first

## Meaning

最初 names the beginning or first part of a sequence. Use 最初の before a noun and 最初に to identify the first action.

- 最初の問題は簡単でした。 (さいしょのもんだいはかんたんでした。) - The first question was easy.
- 最初に名前を書いてください。 (さいしょになまえをかいてください。) - First, please write your name.

最初から (さいしょから) means "from the beginning"; 最初は can introduce how things were initially.

## How the kanji combine

[最](../kanji/最.md), "most; utmost," combines with [初](../kanji/初.md), "beginning": さい + しょ. Together they pick out the very first part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

始め (はじめ) also means beginning. 最後 (さいご), "last; end," is the direct contrast in a sequence.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish 最初に, "first in this sequence," from 初めて (はじめて), "for the first time." Being first to do something today does not mean you have never done it before.
