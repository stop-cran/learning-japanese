# 更に (さらに) - further; furthermore

## Meaning

更に, usually written **さらに**, is an adverb or conjunction adding another step, amount, or point: "further; even more; furthermore."

- 雨がさらに強くなりました。 (あめがさらにつよくなりました。) - The rain became even heavier.
- この部屋は広いです。さらに、駅にも近いです。 (このへやはひろいです。さらに、えきにもちかいです。) - This room is spacious. Furthermore, it is near the station.

In the first example, さらに increases an already relevant degree. In the second, it connects an additional advantage to the preceding statement.

## How the kanji combine

[更](../kanji/更.md) is associated with change or renewal; the established adverb is read さらに. There is no two-kanji compound to divide. The kana spelling is appropriate, and the dictionary explicitly marks kana usage as usual.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もっと](もっと.md) also means "more" and overlaps in degree expressions. さらに can additionally organize a sequence of points, as in the room example. A contrasting connector such as [しかし](しかし.md), "however," changes the relationship between statements rather than merely negating さらに.

## Distinctive meaning

Look for what is being added: a greater degree, an extra quantity, or another argument. さらに三人 (さらにさんにん) means "three more people." The word does not independently identify the starting amount, so that must come from context. Do not treat every English connector involving "more" as interchangeable.
