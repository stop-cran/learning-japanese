# 前進 (ぜんしん) - advance; progress

## Meaning

前進 is forward movement or progress. 前進する is intransitive: a person,
vehicle, or undertaking moves forward rather than taking a direct object
that it pushes.

- 車がゆっくり前進しました。 (くるまがゆっくりぜんしんしました。) - The car moved forward slowly.
- 問題の解決に向けて、一歩前進しました。 (もんだいのかいけつにむけて、いっぽぜんしんしました。) - We took a step forward toward solving the problem.

前進している can describe continuing movement or progress. The second example
uses a physical step as a way of describing progress toward a goal.

## How the kanji combine

[前](../kanji/前.md) contributes front or forward, and [進](../kanji/進.md)
contributes advancing. The first specifies the direction of the movement named
by the second. Read the compound ゼン + シン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[進む](進む.md) (すすむ) means to advance or proceed and covers overlapping
movement and development. 後退 (こうたい), "retreat; move backward," supplies a
direct contrast to forward movement and can also describe a setback.

## Distinctive meaning

Progress need not be physical: negotiations, research, or work toward a solution
can make a 前進. The word does not say that the goal has been reached or that
all problems are resolved. 一歩前進 is a useful way to describe a limited
improvement without claiming complete success.
