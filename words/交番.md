# 交番 (こうばん) - neighborhood police box

## Meaning

交番 is a small neighborhood police station, often called a police box or koban in English. It is a place where people can ask for directions or report a problem.

- 交番で道を聞きました。 (こうばんでみちをききました。) - I asked for directions at the police box.
- 駅の前に交番があります。 (えきのまえにこうばんがあります。) - There is a police box in front of the station.

## How the kanji combine

[交](../kanji/交.md) contributes exchange or alternation, and [番](../kanji/番.md) a turn or watch: こう + ばん. The everyday place name is an established compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

警察署 (けいさつしょ) is a police station at a larger organizational scale. 警察官 (けいさつかん) names an officer, not the building.

## Distinctive meaning

A 交番 is not a telephone booth or simply a box containing police equipment. Learn it as a staffed local police facility, without assuming an officer will always be present when you visit.
