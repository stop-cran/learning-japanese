# 反抗 (はんこう) - defiance; rebellion

## Meaning

反抗 is a noun for resistance or defiance. 反抗する is an intransitive する-verb; the authority, person, or demand resisted takes に.

- 彼は親の命令に反抗しました。 (かれはおやのめいれいにはんこうしました。) - He defied his parents' order.
- 反抗的な態度を取らずに、理由を説明してください。 (はんこうてきなたいどをとらずに、りゆうをせつめいしてください。) - Please explain your reasons rather than taking a defiant attitude.

反抗的 (はんこうてき) is the related な-adjective, "defiant." It is not formed by putting な directly after the verb 反抗する.

## How the kanji combine

[反](../kanji/反.md) contributes going against; 抗 contributes resisting. The meanings reinforce opposition or resistance. The readings are ハン + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[反対](反対.md) (はんたい) can simply express opposition to an idea or proposal. 反抗 highlights defying something that seeks compliance.

[抵抗](抵抗.md) (ていこう), "resistance," overlaps but has broader physical and technical uses. [従う](従う.md) (したがう), "obey; follow," provides a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling behavior 反抗 presents it as defiance; it is not a neutral label for every disagreement. It need not involve physical force. 反抗期 (はんこうき), "a rebellious phase," is a useful compound in discussions of growing up, but the word 反抗 itself is not limited to children or teenagers.
