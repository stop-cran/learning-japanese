# 流す (ながす) - let flow; shed; play or broadcast

## Meaning

流す is a godan verb whose central transitive uses involve letting something flow, sending it along, or shedding liquid. The thing moved takes を. Forms include 流します (ながします) and 流して (ながして).

- 水を流して、洗面台を洗いました。 (みずをながして、せんめんだいをあらいました。) - I ran water and washed the washbasin.
- 彼女はうれしくて涙を流しました。 (かのじょはうれしくてなみだをながしました。) - She shed tears of happiness.

It also covers floating something along: 川に木を流す (かわにきをながす), "float wood down the river." **音楽を流す** (おんがくをながす) means "play music," for example over speakers.

## How the kanji combine

[流](../kanji/流.md) contributes flowing; す completes the native verb ながす. The physical movement image helps connect liquid and transmitted sound without making every object interchangeable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[流れる](流れる.md) (ながれる) describes something flowing: 水が流れる, "water flows." 水を流す instead describes causing or allowing the water to flow. [涙](涙.md) (なみだ) identifies what is shed in the second example.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every sense is transitive. **タクシーが街を流す** (タクシーがまちをながす) means "a taxi cruises around town looking for passengers." Here the taxi moves, and を marks the area traveled, not an object being made to flow. Keep this customer-seeking use separate from the liquid and broadcasting patterns.
