# がやがや — hubbub of voices

## Meaning

がやがや evokes the noisy mixture of people talking, such as in a busy room or crowd.

- 休み時間の教室では、みんなががやがや話していた。 — Everyone was chattering noisily in the classroom during the break.
- 会場の外ががやがやしていて、放送が聞こえなかった。 — There was such a hubbub outside the venue that I could not hear the announcement. (会場: かいじょう; 放送: ほうそう.)

Use がやがや話す, がやがやと話す, or がやがやする. がやがやした場所 (ばしょ) describes a place full of noisy chatter.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ひそひそ](ひそひそ.md) depicts low-voiced speech. [ばたばた](ばたばた.md) can describe noisy bustle, but its noise is not specifically a crowd's overlapping voices.

## Distinctive meaning

がやがや can sound lively or bothersome depending on context. It does not necessarily mean people are quarrelling or shouting angrily. For the noise of machinery alone, choose a description appropriate to that sound rather than assuming がやがや means any loud noise.
