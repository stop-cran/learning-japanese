# 出迎え (でむかえ) - meeting an arrival; pickup

## Meaning

出迎え is going out to meet or receive someone arriving. It can also refer to the person doing the meeting.

- 父の出迎えに空港へ行きました。 (ちちのでむかえにくうこうへいきました。) - I went to the airport to meet my father on his arrival.
- 駅には出迎えの人が大勢いました。 (えきにはでむかえのひとがおおぜいいました。) - Many people were at the station to meet the arrivals.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) contributes going out, and [迎](../kanji/迎.md) receiving someone. 出迎え is the noun related to [出迎える](出迎える.md), not a する-verb lesson based only on an English "meet" gloss.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[迎え](迎え.md) also means meeting or picking someone up; 出迎え makes going out to receive the arrival explicit. [歓迎](歓迎.md) emphasizes a favorable welcome, while [あいさつ](あいさつ.md) includes greetings in many other situations. 見送り (みおくり) is seeing someone off rather than receiving an arrival.

## Distinctive meaning

出迎えに行く and 出迎えをする are useful noun constructions. They do not imply that every other verbal construction is impossible. The meeting need not involve a car, and it is not simply any chance encounter or a clerical reception desk.
