# せっけん (せっけん) — soap

## Meaning

せっけん means soap, used for washing the hands, body, or other things. It names the cleaning substance, not the action of washing.

せっけんで手を洗います。（せっけんでてをあらいます。） — I wash my hands with soap.

## How the kanji combine

The word is also written 石けん or 石鹸. This page keeps the kana spelling. It is a Sino-Japanese word despite being written in hiragana; the small っ is part of the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

洗剤（せんざい） means detergent or cleaning agent more broadly. シャンプー is shampoo, used for hair. They are related cleaning products, not interchangeable names for every kind of soap.

## Distinctive meaning

で marks the means used in せっけんで洗う. For using soap as the object of an action, use を: せっけんを買う（せっけんをかう）, "buy soap."
