# 見学 (けんがく) - study visit; observation

## Meaning

見学 means visiting or observing something to learn about it.
It can describe an inspection, field trip, or tour with this observational purpose.
It is a noun and a する verb, with transitive and intransitive uses.

- 工場を見学しました。 (こうじょうをけんがくしました。) - We toured the factory to learn about it.
- 明日は博物館へ見学に行きます。 (あしたははくぶつかんへけんがくにいきます。) - Tomorrow we will go on a study visit to the museum.

An additional use is sitting out an activity while watching:
今日は体育を見学します。 (きょうはたいいくをけんがくします。) - I will sit out PE today.
This does not necessarily mean leaving the class, and is not a separate N2 assignment.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) contributes seeing; [学](../kanji/学.md) contributes learning.
The combination helps recall learning by looking rather than necessarily participating.
The on readings combine as ケン + ガク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[観察](観察.md) (かんさつ) is observation more generally.
[遠足](遠足.md) (えんそく) can be a school outing or field trip, without defining every trip as an inspection.
[観光](観光.md) (かんこう) emphasizes sightseeing;
[見物](見物.md) (けんぶつ) concerns going to see something.
These activities can overlap rather than forming mutually exclusive trip categories.

## Distinctive meaning

見学 does not require a school group, nor does every visit involve a formal safety examination.
In 工場を見学する, を marks the site observed.
In 見学に行く, に marks the purpose of going.
The PE use means observing instead of taking part, not being absent from all schoolwork.
