# 通う (かよう) — attend regularly; commute

## Meaning

通う describes repeated trips to and from a place: attending school, commuting to work, or regularly visiting a facility.
Mark the place attended with に.

- 毎日、学校に通っています。（まいにち、がっこうにかよっています。） — I go to school every day.
- 週に二回、病院に通っています。（しゅうににかい、びょういんにかよっています。） — I go to the hospital twice a week.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native verb, not a compound. [通](../kanji/通.md) supplies かよ, and う is okurigana.
It is a godan verb: 通います（かよいます）, 通わない（かよわない）, 通って（かよって）.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[行く](行く.md) いく means "go" and does not by itself emphasize repeated attendance.
通る（とおる） means passing through or along a place, not attending it regularly.
通学（つうがく） is the related noun for commuting to school. There is no single direct antonym of 通う.

## Distinctive meaning

学校に行きます（がっこうにいきます） can describe a particular trip to school.
学校に通っています（がっこうにかよっています） describes ongoing attendance.
病院に通う means making outpatient visits, not staying in the hospital; repeated visits are the key idea.
