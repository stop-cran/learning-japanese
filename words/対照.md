# 対照 (たいしょう) - contrast; comparison against

## Meaning

対照 is comparison or contrast, including checking one thing against
another and a striking difference between them. It is a noun and a
transitive する-verb.

- 訳文を原文と対照しました。 (やくぶんをげんぶんとたいしょうしました。) - I compared the translation against the original.
- 二人の性格は対照的です。 (ふたりのせいかくはたいしょうてきです。) - Their personalities are contrasting.

In the first example, を marks what is checked and と the comparison
partner. 対照的な + noun and 対照的に supply useful adjective-like and
adverbial forms: 対照的な性格 means "contrasting personalities."

## How the kanji combine

[対](../kanji/対.md) contributes facing or setting alongside;
[照](../kanji/照.md) contributes comparison or checking here, not literal
illumination. Together they evoke examining things against one another.
The on readings are タイ + ショウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[比較](比較.md) (ひかく) and [比べる](比べる.md) (くらべる) describe
comparison broadly. 対照 often highlights contrast, but the translation
example shows that it is not restricted to pointing out differences.
[照らす](照らす.md) (てらす) also has a comparison/reference use.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the homophone [対象](対象.md) (たいしょう), "target; object of an
activity," separate. 対称 (たいしょう), "symmetry," is another word.
Matching pronunciation does not make either spelling an alternative for
対照. A contrast also need not be an exact logical opposite.
