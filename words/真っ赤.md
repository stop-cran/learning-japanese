# 真っ赤 (まっか) - bright red; completely red

## Meaning

真っ赤 is a na-adjective meaning vividly or thoroughly red. It can describe a bright-red object or a flushed face. Use **真っ赤な + noun** and **真っ赤になる**, "turn red."

- 真っ赤な花が咲いています。 (まっかなはながさいています。) - Bright-red flowers are blooming.
- 恥ずかしくて顔が真っ赤になりました。 (はずかしくてかおがまっかになりました。) - My face went bright red with embarrassment.

The second sentence supplies the cause of the redness. 真っ赤 alone would not tell us whether the cause was embarrassment, heat, or something else.

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md) acts as an intensifying element, while [赤](../kanji/赤.md) supplies red. Learn the whole pronunciation **まっか**, including the small っ; it is not pronounced as a sequence of independent dictionary readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[赤い](赤い.md) (あかい) is the basic i-adjective "red." Compare 赤い花 with 真っ赤な花: the latter emphasizes the redness and uses な. A different color is a contrast, not automatically an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

真っ赤 also intensifies a few non-color expressions. 真っ赤な嘘 (まっかなうそ) is "an outright lie," not a red-colored lie. This is an established use; it does not license placing 真っ赤 before any noun to mean "complete." Learn that phrase alongside [嘘](嘘.md) (うそ).
