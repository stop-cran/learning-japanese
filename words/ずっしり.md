# ずっしり — with a heavy, weighty feel

## Meaning

ずっしり describes the substantial weight of something, often as it is lifted or held. It can
also describe a weighty impression or effect.

- 箱を持ち上げると、思ったよりずっしり重かった。
  — When I lifted the box, it felt heavier than I expected.
- 本を詰めたかばんが、肩にずっしりとのしかかった。
  — The book-filled bag weighed heavily on my shoulder.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ごつごつ](ごつごつ.md) describes a rough, lumpy surface, not weight. The lighter feel
[ふわふわ](ふわふわ.md) describes softness or floating movement rather than a substantial load.

## Distinctive meaning

ずっしり is about perceived weight or impact, not merely a large object's dimensions. The
related form ずしり can express a similar heavy feeling; this card focuses on ずっしり.
