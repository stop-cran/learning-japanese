# 金魚 (きんぎょ) — goldfish

## Meaning

金魚 is a goldfish: 金魚を飼う かう (keep goldfish), 金魚鉢 きんぎょばち (a goldfish bowl), 金魚すくい (a festival game of scooping goldfish).

## How the kanji combine

[金](../kanji/金.md) (gold) modifies [魚](../kanji/魚.md) (fish): "gold fish", named after the colour. Both are on readings (キン + ギョ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 鯉 こい (carp), 熱帯魚 ねったいぎょ (tropical fish), [魚](魚.md) さかな.

## Distinctive meaning

金魚 is a pet fish, a staple of summer festivals; it is not normally eaten. The compound uses the on reading ギョ of 魚, not さかな.
