# 嗅ぐ (かぐ) - sniff; smell

## Meaning

嗅ぐ means to sniff or smell something. It is a transitive godan verb: what is smelled takes を.

- 犬が地面のにおいを嗅いでいます。 (いぬがじめんのにおいをかいでいます。) - The dog is sniffing the ground.
- 花の香りを嗅ぎました。 (はなのかおりをかぎました。) - I smelled the fragrance of the flowers.

Learn 嗅ぎます (かぎます), 嗅がない (かがない), 嗅いで (かいで) and 嗅いだ (かいだ). The ぐ ending gives いで/いだ, not the いて/いた of a typical く verb.

## How the kanji combine

嗅 writes か in this native verb, followed by ぐ. This is a single-kanji word, not a compound whose parts are pronounced separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[におい](におい.md) is the smell or scent itself. Compare においを嗅ぐ (においをかぐ), "sniff a smell," with いいにおいがする, "it smells good." The latter does not say that someone is actively sniffing.

[くんくん](くんくん.md) can depict an animal sniffing. [香り](香り.md) (かおり) often presents a pleasant fragrance; 嗅ぐ itself can concern pleasant or unpleasant smells.

## Distinctive meaning

The thing giving off a smell and the person or animal smelling it are different participants. To say that flowers smell good, use a pattern such as 花はいいにおいがする, not 花が嗅ぐ. Kana かぐ can represent the same verb; unrelated [家具](家具.md) (かぐ), "furniture," is a separate word.
