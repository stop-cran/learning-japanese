# ドレス (ドレス) - dress; gown

## Meaning

ドレス is a dress or gown. It is a noun for the garment, not a verb meaning to get dressed. Use ドレスを着る (ドレスをきる), "wear a dress," and ドレスを選ぶ (ドレスをえらぶ), "choose a dress."

- パーティーで青いドレスを着ました。 (パーティーであおいドレスをきました。) - I wore a blue dress at the party.
- このドレスはあなたによく似合います。 (このドレスはあなたによくにあいます。) - This dress looks very good on you.

ウェディングドレス names a wedding dress. The surrounding description identifies the occasion and style.

## How the kanji combine

ドレス is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "dress." It has no kanji components. Unlike スター, this spelling contains no long vowel mark; pronounce the written sequence ド・レ・ス.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[洋服](洋服.md) (ようふく) means Western-style clothing more broadly. [着物](着物.md) (きもの) can identify a kimono. A dress and a kimono are contrasting garments, not opposites in the sense of good and bad.

## Distinctive meaning

The examples concern a party garment and how it looks on its wearer. Do not extend ドレス to every English use of "dress": "dress warmly" describes how someone puts on clothing, not a particular dress. Likewise, the noun alone does not tell you a dress's color, length, or whether it is suitable for a particular event.
