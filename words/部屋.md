# 部屋 (へや) — room

## Meaning

部屋 is a room, such as a room in a house or hotel. In housing contexts it can also refer to an apartment or rented living space.

- この部屋は広いです。 (このへやはひろいです。) — This room is spacious.
- 部屋に机があります。 (へやにつくえがあります。) — There is a desk in the room.

## How the kanji combine

[部](../kanji/部.md) contributes the written idea of a section or portion, while [屋](../kanji/屋.md) relates to a building or enclosed space. As a meaning aid, picture a divided-off space within a building.

This native word has a spelling whose reading must be learned with the word: **へや**, not ぶや. The へ is not predictable from the ordinary on-reading ブ of 部; 屋 has its familiar native や. It is not a regular on+on compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [室](../kanji/室.md) シツ appears in more specific room names, such as [教室](教室.md) きょうしつ, "classroom."
- [家](家.md) いえ means a house or home, rather than just one room.
- There is no general direct antonym of 部屋.

## Distinctive meaning

部屋 is the ordinary conversational choice for a room. It does not specify the room's purpose. The same spelling has a specialized sumo sense, a training stable; that is not the intended meaning in the everyday examples above.
