# 宿題 (しゅくだい) — homework

## Meaning

宿題 is homework or an assignment. 宿題をする means "do homework"; 宿題を出す (しゅくだいをだす) can describe a teacher assigning it.
The word names assigned work, not every kind of studying.

- 図書館で宿題をしました。 (としょかんでしゅくだいをしました。) — I did my homework at the library.
- 今日の宿題はもう終わりました。 (きょうのしゅくだいはもうおわりました。) — I have already finished today's homework.

## How the kanji combine

[宿](../kanji/宿.md) has lodging and home associations; [題](../kanji/題.md) means a topic or subject.
For learning, connect the pair with assigned problems taken away from class.
That is a memory aid for the established word, not a historical derivation or a rule about where the work must be done.
The on readings are シュク + ダイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

課題 (かだい) can mean an assignment, but also a topic, task, or problem to be addressed.
It is broader than school homework.
[勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) means study: doing homework can be studying, but voluntary study need not be 宿題.
There is no useful single-word antonym for homework here.

## Distinctive meaning

The first example is still 宿題 even though it is done in a library.
Focus on the assignment, not on a literal translation of 宿 as "home."
Use 宿題をする for doing it; the noun is not itself a verb meaning "study."
