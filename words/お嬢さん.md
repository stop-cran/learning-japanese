# お嬢さん (おじょうさん) — daughter; young lady

## Meaning

お嬢さん is a polite reference to someone else's daughter. It can also mean a young lady, without identifying whose daughter she is.

- お嬢さんはお元気ですか。 (おじょうさんはおげんきですか。) — Is your daughter well?
- あのお嬢さんをご存じですか。 (あのおじょうさんをごぞんじですか。) — Do you know that young lady?

Use it as a noun. The respectful wording does not determine the daughter's age in every context.

## How the kanji combine

嬢 has the on-reading ジョウ and refers to a daughter or young woman. The respectful prefix お and suffix さん surround it. The kango label describes the central Sino-Japanese element, not the origin of every attached part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

娘 (むすめ) is the ordinary word for one's own daughter; 娘さん (むすめさん) is another polite reference to someone else's. 息子さん (むすこさん) means someone else's son.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not mechanically use お嬢さん for every female person or as a neutral workplace title. The "young lady" use draws attention to youth and gender; a person's name with さん is often more appropriate when known.
