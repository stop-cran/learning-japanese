# 武士 (ぶし) — samurai; warrior

## Meaning

武士 refers to a samurai or warrior in Japanese history. It is not the general modern word for
anyone serving in an army.

- この本には武士の生活が書かれています。  
  (この ほんには ぶしの せいかつが かかれています。)  
  — This book describes the lives of samurai.

## How the kanji combine

武 ブ supplies the martial or military meaning; [士](../kanji/士.md) シ identifies the warrior.
It is a modifier + person compound, using on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

侍 (さむらい), also written 士, overlaps with 武士 in ordinary descriptions of Japanese warriors.
Historical distinctions depend on the period and social context; the words are not universally
opposites or perfectly interchangeable technical labels.

## Distinctive meaning

The reading taught here is ぶし. JMdict also records rare さむらい for 武士 and a separate poetic
もののふ entry. Recognize those as special readings, not freely interchangeable pronunciations
of every occurrence.

For the warrior and professional uses of 士 compared with other person-denoting endings, see
[occupation and role endings](../articles/occupation-and-role-endings.md).
