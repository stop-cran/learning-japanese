# センター (センター) - center; central facility

## Meaning

センター is a noun meaning "center." It can identify a central position or a facility organized around a service or activity. In baseball, it also names the center fielder.

- スポーツセンターで泳ぎました。 (スポーツセンターでおよぎました。) - I swam at the sports center.
- 今日のセンターは田中選手です。 (きょうのセンターはたなかせんしゅです。) - Today's center fielder is Tanaka.

The second sentence assumes a baseball context. The same sound does not tell you whether a building, location, or player is meant without that context.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in katakana and corresponds to English "center." There are no kanji components to explain. The final ー lengthens the vowel in ター; it is part of this card's spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中心](中心.md) (ちゅうしん) also means center or central point. [真ん中](真ん中.md) (まんなか) identifies the middle. These overlap for position, but a service facility called a センター is not necessarily the geographic midpoint of a town.

## Distinctive meaning

In facility names, the preceding word explains the activity: スポーツセンター is a sports center, not any building in the middle. Do not replace every センター with 真ん中 simply because both can be translated "center." The baseball role is another established use, not an abbreviation for a building.
