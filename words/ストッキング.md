# ストッキング (ストッキング) - stockings

## Meaning

ストッキング means stockings or stocking-like hosiery. Thin stockings worn
on the legs are a familiar use. The word also occurs for longer sports
stockings, so it is not restricted to sheer clothing.

- 薄いストッキングをはきました。 (うすいストッキングをはきました。) - I put on thin stockings.
- ストッキングに穴が開いてしまいました。 (ストッキングにあながあいてしまいました。) - I ended up with a hole in my stocking.

Use はく for putting them on and 脱ぐ (ぬぐ) for taking them off. Japanese
does not require a different noun form for one stocking and several.

## How the kanji combine

This is written in katakana, with a small ッ before キ and a voiced final グ:
ストッキング. There is no kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[靴下](靴下.md) (くつした) broadly covers socks and stockings.
ソックス is another loanword for socks. These terms overlap, but the particular
item and its name matter; do not assume every pair of ordinary ankle socks
will be called ストッキング.

## Distinctive meaning

Stockings are not necessarily thigh-high, sheer, or worn only by women.
Sports usage is one reason a rigid material-and-gender definition would fail.
The noun itself does not tell you the exact length, thickness, or number of pairs.
