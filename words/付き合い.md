# 付き合い (つきあい) - association; socializing

## Meaning

付き合い is association with other people, social contact, or an ongoing relationship. It is a noun. Use **person と／との付き合い** to identify the people involved.

- 彼とは長い付き合いです。 (かれとはながいつきあいです。) - I have known him for a long time.
- 近所の人との付き合いを大切にしています。 (きんじょのひととのつきあいをたいせつにしています。) - I value my relationships with my neighbors.

The first example describes a long association, not necessarily a long conversation or a romantic relationship.

## How the kanji combine

[付](../kanji/付.md) contributes joining or accompanying, and [合](../kanji/合.md) contributes togetherness. 付き合い is the noun related to 付き合う (つきあう), with native readings rather than on readings. Treat the components as a meaning aid, not a word-for-word translation rule.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[交際](交際.md) (こうさい) also concerns social relations and can include dating. [友達](友達.md) (ともだち) names a friend, whereas 付き合い names the association itself. Lack of contact is a contextual contrast, not one fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

付き合い can involve enjoyment, obligation, or both. 付き合いで参加する (つきあいでさんかする) means attending for social reasons or to maintain relationships. Do not assume romance whenever the word appears: neighbors, colleagues, and longstanding acquaintances can all have 付き合い.
