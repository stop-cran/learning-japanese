# 暑い (あつい)

## Meaning

暑い means hot when describing weather, a room's atmosphere, or how the surrounding temperature feels.

- 今日は暑いですね。 (きょうは あついですね。) — It is hot today, isn't it?
- この部屋は暑くないです。 (このへやは あつくないです。) — This room is not hot.

It is an i-adjective: 暑い日 (あついひ), "a hot day"; past 暑かった (あつかった).

## How the kanji combine

暑 represents heat in the surroundings. The native reading is あつ, followed by the adjective ending い. Do not infer that every English use of "hot" takes this spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

寒い (さむい), "cold," contrasts with 暑い for weather. 熱い (あつい) describes hot objects or liquids, such as hot tea. 暖かい (あたたかい) describes warmth rather than stronger heat.

## Distinctive meaning

The same pronunciation あつい has different spellings. Use 暑い for a hot afternoon, but 熱い for tea that could burn your mouth. 暑い does not itself say whether the speaker enjoys the heat.
