# 柚 (ゆず) - yuzu tree; yuzu

## Meaning

柚 names the yuzu tree or its fruit, a citrus used for its aroma and sourness. Its peel is used to flavour food. 柚子, ゆず and ユズ also write the same plant name.

- 柚の皮を料理に使います。 (ゆずのかわをりょうりにつかいます。) - I use yuzu peel in cooking.
- 庭に柚の木を植えました。 (にわにゆずのきをうえました。) - I planted a yuzu tree in the garden.

柚の木 (ゆずのき) specifies the tree. A food context may instead select the fruit, its peel or juice.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [柚](../kanji/柚.md) writes the whole native noun ゆず. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide. The alternative spelling 柚子 has the same whole-word reading; it does not establish a freely reusable ず reading for [子](../kanji/子.md).

JMdict marks the noun as usually written in kana. This does not make 柚 or 柚子 a different species.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[果物](果物.md) (くだもの), fruit, and [木](木.md) (き), tree, are broader categories. レモン, lemon, and すだち, sudachi, name other citrus fruits; shared sourness is not a rule allowing free substitution in recipes. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the specific English name **yuzu**. Some dictionaries use "citron" in English glosses, but citron can also name *Citrus medica*, a different citrus. This article is about yuzu, not every plant called citron.

JMdict flags standalone ゆ and ゆう as outdated reading forms of 柚. Recognize them in older material; this article's primary reading and the linked kanji question use ゆず.
