# 交通機関 (こうつうきかん) - means of transport; transport system

## Meaning

交通機関 means transportation facilities, a transport system, or means
of transportation. Depending on context, it can concern services and facilities
collectively or particular forms of transport such as trains and buses.

- 公共の交通機関を利用してください。 (こうきょうのこうつうきかんをりようしてください。) - Please use public transport.
- 台風のため、多くの交通機関が止まりました。 (たいふうのため、おおくのこうつうきかんがとまりました。) - Many transport services stopped because of the typhoon.

交通機関を利用する means "use a means of transport"; を marks what is used.

## How the kanji combine

The useful grouping is [交通](交通.md) (こうつう), traffic or transport,
plus [機関](機関.md) (きかん), here a system or facilities.
[交](../kanji/交.md) contributes interaction, [通](../kanji/通.md)
passing through, [機](../kanji/機.md) a mechanism, and
[関](../kanji/関.md) connection or involvement.
Transport qualifies the system; do not translate 機関 here as an engine.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[乗り物](乗り物.md) (のりもの) can name vehicles or means of transport.
[交通](交通.md) is broader transport or traffic wording.
公共交通機関 (こうきょうこうつうきかん) specifically concerns public
transport; 交通機関 alone does not supply every public-service qualification.

## Distinctive meaning

This is not a vehicle-only definition excluding all infrastructure.
The broader dictionary account can include facilities enabling transport
and communication, although transport facilities are a central use.
A particular [便](便.md) (びん), "service; flight," can be operated within
such a system without being the entire system.
