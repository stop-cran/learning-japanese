# 活字 (かつじ) - printing type; printed text

## Meaning

活字 names printing type, including movable character units used in letterpress printing. It also means the printed word or printed matter.

- 活字を組んで、本を印刷しました。 (かつじをくんで、ほんをいんさつしました。) - We set the type and printed a book.
- 小さい活字は読みにくいです。 (ちいさいかつじはよみにくいです。) - Small print is difficult to read.

The first example concerns arranging the type; the second concerns reading printed characters.

## How the kanji combine

[活](../kanji/活.md) contributes activity or life, and [字](../kanji/字.md) a written character. As a meaning-based aid, picture character units that can be moved and reused; this is not a claim about the historical formation of the kanji. The on readings are カツ + ジ: かつじ, without a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[文字](文字.md) (もじ) and [字](字.md) (じ) are broader character terms, not limited to print. [印刷](印刷.md) (いんさつ) concerns printing; 活字 can name the type or what is read in print. [文章](文章.md) (ぶんしょう) focuses on the written text rather than its printing method.

## Distinctive meaning

English "type" here is not simply "kind" or "style," as in [タイプ](タイプ.md). In reading-related expressions, 活字 need not mean the physical blocks handled by a printer. Do not turn its technical origin into a rule that every occurrence names a piece of metal.
