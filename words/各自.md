# 各自 (かくじ) - each person; individually

## Meaning

各自 directs attention to each person in a group separately. It can be a noun or adverb. 各自で means each person doing something individually; 各自の + noun means each person's own something.

- 昼食は各自で用意してください。 (ちゅうしょくはかくじでよういしてください。) - Please arrange your own lunch.
- 各自の意見を聞きました。 (かくじのいけんをききました。) - I listened to each person's opinion.

## How the kanji combine

[各](../kanji/各.md) contributes each, and [自](../kanji/自.md) self. Together they point to each individual person. The on readings are カク + ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一人一人](一人一人.md) (ひとりひとり) also concerns each person. [各々](各々.md) (おのおの) and [それぞれ](それぞれ.md) can distribute attention over people or things. [全員](全員.md) (ぜんいん), all members, emphasizes the whole group rather than each person's separate involvement.

## Distinctive meaning

In the lunch request, everyone is included, but each person makes their own arrangements. 各自 does not mean that one representative supplies lunch for the whole group. [個人](個人.md) (こじん) names an individual or private person; it does not by itself distribute a task among every member.
