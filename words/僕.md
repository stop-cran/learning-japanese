# 僕 (ぼく) — I; me (masculine)

## Meaning

僕 is a first-person pronoun strongly associated with boys and men. It can occur in casual speech and in some polite conversations, but is not the safest choice for every formal setting.

- 僕は大学生です。 (ぼくはだいがくせいです。) — I'm a university student.
- これは僕のかばんです。 (これはぼくのかばんです。) — This is my bag.

Like a noun, it takes particles: 僕が, 僕に, and 僕の. It does not change into separate subject and object forms like English "I/me."

## How the kanji combine

僕 is one kanji with the on-reading ボク. The modern pronoun should be learned as a whole word, not translated through the character's historical servant meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

私 (わたし) is more broadly usable in polite introductions. 俺 (おれ) is a more strongly casual masculine choice. These differ in social voice, not grammatical person.

## Distinctive meaning

Pronoun choice expresses persona and relationship, not a strict biological rule. Japanese also often omits an understood "I." Repeating 僕 in every sentence can sound unnecessarily emphatic.
