# 飽くまで (あくまで) - persistently; purely

## Meaning

あくまで can emphasize persisting through to the end or maintaining a position.
It can also set a limit on how something should be understood: "purely," "only,"
or "it must be remembered that."

- あくまで自分の意見を通そうとしました。 (あくまでじぶんのいけんをとおそうとしました。) - I tried to insist on my own view to the end.
- これはあくまで私個人の意見です。 (これはあくまでわたしこじんのいけんです。) - This is purely my personal opinion.

The source spelling 飽くまで is a **rare kanji spelling**. Both uses above are usually
written in kana; rarity of the spelling does not mean the spoken expression is rare.

## How the kanji combine

飽 has associations with being sated or having enough. Learn the fixed adverb あくまで
as a whole rather than translating the character literally. It does not mean
"until bored" in every sentence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[単に](単に.md) (たんに), "merely; simply," overlaps with the limiting use.
[どうしても](どうしても.md) can express determination, but also has negative inability uses
that あくまで does not automatically reproduce.

## Distinctive meaning

The second example limits the status of an opinion, not its quantity or duration.
Do not translate every occurrence as "stubbornly," or treat the English gloss "after all"
as a universal replacement for [やはり](やはり.md).
The predicate and the speaker's intended boundary determine the interpretation.
