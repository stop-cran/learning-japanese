# 空く (あく) — become vacant; become less crowded

## Meaning

空く has two useful readings. **あく** means that a place, time slot, or resource becomes empty or available. **すく** describes a place becoming less crowded, or a stomach becoming empty. Both are intransitive godan verbs: 空きます (あきます／すきます), 空いた (あいた／すいた).

- 席が空きました。 (せきがあきました。) — A seat has become available.
- この電車は空いています。 (このでんしゃはすいています。) — This train is not crowded.
- お腹が空きました。 (おなかがすきました。) — I'm hungry.

## How the kanji combine

[空](../kanji/空.md) suggests emptiness; く is okurigana. Learn the reading with the expression, not from the character alone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

込む (こむ), "become crowded," contrasts with すく. 空ける (あける) is transitive: 席を空ける (せきをあける), "free up a seat."

## Distinctive meaning

The あく meaning "open" is normally written 開く for doors and shops: ドアが開く (ドアがあく), "the door opens." An available seat is 空く; do not assume every English "open" uses that spelling.
