# 夕飯 (ゆうはん) — evening meal

## Meaning

夕飯 is the evening meal: dinner or supper. It is an everyday word and does not specify a formal meal or restaurant setting.

七時に夕飯を食べます。（しちじにゆうはんをたべます。） — I eat dinner at seven.

## How the kanji combine

[夕](../kanji/夕.md), read ゆう, means evening; [飯](../kanji/飯.md), read はん, means a meal or cooked rice. This is mixed native-plus-on reading, rather than two on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

晩ご飯（ばんごはん） and 夕ご飯（ゆうごはん） are close everyday alternatives. 夕食（ゆうしょく） is a more formal meal name. 朝ご飯（あさごはん）, breakfast, is a contrasting meal.

## Distinctive meaning

夕飯 identifies the meal by its place in the day, not by its ingredients. It can contain bread or noodles rather than rice. The reading ゆうめし also exists, but this page teaches ゆうはん.
