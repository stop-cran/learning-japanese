# 梅雨 (つゆ) - East Asian rainy season

## Meaning

梅雨 has two readings, **つゆ** and **ばいう**. Both can name the East Asian
rainy season or the rain associated with that season. In Japan, the season
is typically around early summer, but its timing and extent vary by region
and year.

- もう梅雨に入りました。 (もうつゆにはいりました。) - The rainy season has already begun.
- 天気予報で梅雨前線の位置を確認しました。 (てんきよほうでばいうぜんせんのいちをかくにんしました。) - I checked the position of the rainy-season front in the weather forecast.

梅雨前線 uses ばいう. Learn the reading of the whole compound rather than
automatically inserting the page's primary reading つゆ.

## How the kanji combine

梅 means "plum," and [雨](../kanji/雨.md) means "rain."
The reading つゆ belongs to the whole written word; it cannot be divided
into separate regular readings of these two characters. In ばいう, the
on readings バイ and ウ combine directly. The character meanings are not
by themselves proof of a historical naming story.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[雨](雨.md) (あめ) names rain generally, including rain outside this season.
[季節](季節.md) (きせつ) is the broader word "season."
梅雨入り (つゆいり), the start, contrasts with 梅雨明け (つゆあけ),
the end of the rainy season.

## Distinctive meaning

梅雨 does not mean that it rains continuously every day. 梅雨の晴れ間
(つゆのはれま), "a sunny break in the rainy season," is compatible with
the season continuing. Keep the regional seasonal term distinct from any
random stretch of wet weather.
