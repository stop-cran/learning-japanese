# 関東 (かんとう) - Kanto region

## Meaning

関東 names the Kanto region of Japan, around Tokyo and the surrounding
prefectures. It is not another name for Tokyo city alone.

- 関東に住んでいます。
  (かんとうにすんでいます。) - I live in Kanto.
- 関東の天気予報を見ました。
  (かんとうのてんきよほうをみました。) - I watched the weather forecast for Kanto.

関東地方 (かんとうちほう) explicitly says "the Kanto region."
Use 関東の to modify another noun, as in the weather example.

## How the kanji combine

[関](../kanji/関.md) contributes a barrier or checkpoint, and
[東](../kanji/東.md) east.
The historical association is land east of a barrier.
The on readings カン + トウ give かんとう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[関西](関西.md) (かんさい) is the region centered on Kyoto, Osaka and Kobe.
It is a useful regional contrast, not a label for every place outside Kanto.
東日本 (ひがしにほん), "eastern Japan," is broader than the modern
Kanto region.

## Distinctive meaning

Historical uses of 関東 can cover a broader eastern area, depending
on the period and the relevant barrier.
Do not carry the dictionary's historical "eastern half of Japan"
gloss over as the exact present-day boundary.
In a modern travel or weather conversation, identify the named region
rather than treating 関東 as every location east of where you stand.
