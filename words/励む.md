# 励む (はげむ) — work hard; strive

## Meaning

励む means working hard or applying oneself to an activity. In this useful modern pattern, mark the activity with に: 勉強に励む (べんきょうにはげむ), "work hard at one's studies."

- 毎日、勉強に励んでいます。 (まいにち、べんきょうにはげんでいます。) — I work hard at my studies every day.
- 研究に励みました。 (けんきゅうにはげみました。) — I applied myself to research.

## How the kanji combine

[励](../kanji/励.md) writes はげ, and む is the written ending. This is one native verb, not a compound of separate on-reading words.

It is a godan verb ending in む. Useful forms are 励みます (はげみます), 励んで (はげんで), 励んだ (はげんだ) and 励まない (はげまない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[努める](努める.md) (つとめる), "strive; make an effort," and [努力](努力.md) (どりょく), "effort," overlap with 励む.

Contrast [励ます](励ます.md) (はげます), "encourage; cheer someone on":

- 自分が勉強に励む。 (じぶんがべんきょうにはげむ。) — I myself work hard at my studies.
- 友達を励ます。 (ともだちをはげます。) — I encourage a friend.

These are different roles: applying oneself to an activity versus encouraging someone.

## Distinctive meaning

励む describes effort, not guaranteed success. The activity + に pattern above is the modern teaching model here; it is not a claim that historical dictionaries contain no other constructions.

Do not mistake 励ます for a polite form of 励む. 励む becomes 励みます; 励ます is a separate verb whose polite form is 励まします (はげまします).
