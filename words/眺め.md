# 眺め (ながめ) - view; outlook

## Meaning

眺め is the view or scene that can be looked out upon. It is a noun: 眺めがいい (ながめがいい) means "have a good view," while 眺めを楽しむ (ながめをたのしむ) means "enjoy the view."

- この部屋は海の眺めがいいです。 (このへやはうみのながめがいいです。) - This room has a good view of the sea.
- 山の上からの眺めを楽しみました。 (やまのうえからのながめをたのしみました。) - We enjoyed the view from the mountaintop.

Place + からの眺め identifies the viewpoint. The sea or landscape is what is visible from it.

## How the kanji combine

眺 concerns looking out over something. 眺め is related to the native verb [眺める](眺める.md) (ながめる), "look at; gaze at." The kana め is part of this noun, not a separate word meaning "eye."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[景色](景色.md) (けしき) also names scenery. 眺め is particularly useful for the view available from a window, room, or lookout, while 景色 can describe the scenery itself. Their meanings overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

English "view" can also mean an opinion. For someone's position on an issue, [見解](見解.md) (けんかい) or [意見](意見.md) (いけん) is appropriate instead of treating the visible-view sense as interchangeable.
