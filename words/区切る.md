# 区切る (くぎる) - mark off; divide into sections; punctuate

## Meaning

区切る means divide or mark something off into sections. With language, it means separate units by punctuation or pauses, including when reading aloud.

- カーテンで部屋を二つに区切りました。 (カーテンでへやをふたつにくぎりました。) - We divided the room into two sections with a curtain.
- 文を意味のまとまりで区切って読んでください。 (ぶんをいみのまとまりでくぎってよんでください。) - Please read the sentence in meaningful chunks, with pauses between them.

This is a transitive godan verb: 区切ります (くぎります), 区切って (くぎって), 区切らない (くぎらない). The divided material takes を.

## How the kanji combine

[区](../kanji/区.md), division or section, combines with [切](../kanji/切.md), cutting or separating. The whole reading is くぎる, not くきる.

The app groups this on-first mixed form under kango: ク is on, while the voiced ぎる element is related to native 切る (きる). This is a modern reading explanation, not an all-on compound or a claim about the word's historical formation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[分ける](分ける.md) (わける), divide or separate, is broader; 区切る emphasizes boundaries between sections. [止める](止める.md) (とめる), stop, need not involve such divisions.

## Distinctive meaning

In a "stop" or "put an end to" translation, think of setting a stopping point or break: ここでいったん話を区切りましょう (ここでいったんはなしをくぎりましょう), "Let's pause the discussion here for now." It is not an unrestricted verb for ending anything.

句切る is a dictionary-listed rare spelling, especially recognizable in language-related contexts; it is not a second pronunciation.
