# ばきばき — cracking with sharp snaps

## Meaning

Repeated strong cracks or snaps, such as dry branches breaking.

- 強い風で枯（か）れ枝（えだ）がばきばき折（お）れた。 — The strong wind snapped the dead branches with sharp cracks.
- 足元（あしもと）で小枝（こえだ）がばきばき鳴（な）った。 — Twigs cracked underfoot.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ばりばり](ばりばり.md) overlaps in rough, forceful sounds. [ぱちぱち](ぱちぱち.md) can suggest smaller crackles or snaps. The distinction depends on the described sound, not a fixed decibel boundary.

## Distinctive meaning

ばきばき折れる describes something breaking; ばきばき鳴る only reports the sound. The expression can concern joints as well as branches, but hearing it does not establish damage.
