# タイプ (タイプ) — type or style

## Meaning

タイプ means a type, kind, or style. It can classify a product or describe the kind of person someone is or likes.

このタイプのかばんは軽いです。（このタイプのかばんはかるいです。） — This type of bag is light.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "type." It has no kanji parts. The final プ is part of the Japanese word's pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

種類（しゅるい） means kind or category and often overlaps with タイプ for products. 型（かた） can mean a model or pattern. None is an exact substitute in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

Use タイプの before a noun, not タイプな: このタイプの車（このタイプのくるま）, "this type of car." 私のタイプ（わたしのタイプ）, "my type," can describe someone the speaker finds attractive, rather than a neutral classification.
