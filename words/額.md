# 額 (がく) - frame; amount

## Meaning

額 (がく) is a noun meaning a picture frame or framed picture. It also means an amount or sum, particularly money,
and occurs as a suffix in 金額 (きんがく), "sum of money."
The same character read **ひたい** is a separate noun meaning "forehead."

- この絵を額に入れましょう。 (このえをがくにいれましょう。) - Let's put this picture in a frame.
- 支払う額を確認してください。 (しはらうがくをかくにんしてください。) - Please check the amount to be paid.
- 額の汗を拭きました。 (ひたいのあせをふきました。) - I wiped the sweat from my forehead.

In the first example, に marks the destination of the picture; in the third, の connects the forehead with its sweat.

## How the kanji combine

額 is one character, not a compound. ガク is its on-reading here; ひたい is the native body-part word.
Do not transfer the forehead meaning to がく simply because both use 額.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[値段](値段.md) (ねだん) is a price, whereas 額 can be a payment, donation, or other sum.
[顔](顔.md) (かお), "face," is broader than the forehead.
Neither meaning has a single useful opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes two questions at once: which reading is intended, and which がく meaning applies.
Pictures suggest the frame reading; payments suggest an amount; body descriptions can require ひたい.
Learn these as distinct vocabulary uses rather than a single English definition.
