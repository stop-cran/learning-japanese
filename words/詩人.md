# 詩人 (しじん) - poet

## Meaning

詩人 is a noun meaning "poet," a person who writes poetry. It identifies the creator, not an individual poem or the act of reading one.

- 彼女は自然について書く詩人です。 (かのじょはしぜんについてかくしじんです。) - She is a poet who writes about nature.
- 図書館で詩人の話を聞きました。 (としょかんでしじんのはなしをききました。) - I listened to a poet's talk at the library.

Use 詩人になる (しじんになる) for "become a poet." Like other role nouns, it takes です without な when used as the predicate.

## How the kanji combine

詩 means poetry; [人](../kanji/人.md) means person. The modifier-plus-head compound identifies a person associated with creating poetry. The readings are シ + ジン, not しひと.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[詩](詩.md) (し) is the work or literary form, whereas 詩人 is the person.

[作家](作家.md) (さっか), "writer; author," is a broader related role. A person can be both a poet and a novelist; these labels are not mutually exclusive.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling someone 詩人 does not itself say that poetry is their paid occupation, that they are famous, or that they have published a book. It names their creative role. There is no useful opposite corresponding to "non-poet"; compare different kinds of writers rather than inventing an antonym.
