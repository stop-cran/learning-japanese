# ぺこぺこ — very hungry; bowing repeatedly

## Meaning

ぺこぺこ describes strong hunger, repeated deferential bowing, or a thin surface that dents or gives when pressed.

- 朝から何も食べていなくて、お腹がぺこぺこだ。 — I have eaten nothing since morning and am starving. (お腹: おなか.)
- 彼は何度もぺこぺこと頭を下げた。 — He bowed his head repeatedly.
- このふたは押すとぺこぺこする。 — This lid flexes in and out when pressed. (押す: おす.)

For hunger, use お腹がぺこぺこだ or ぺこぺこのお腹. For bowing, use ぺこぺこする or ぺこぺこと頭を下げる. The surface sense also takes する.

## How the kanji combine

This repeated kana mimetic depicts a bodily state or repeated movement. It is not a kanji compound, and hunger need not be accompanied by a stomach noise.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぺらぺら](ぺらぺら.md) can describe flimsy material, but does not itself describe repeated bowing or hunger. お腹がいっぱい, "full," contrasts with the hunger use.

## Distinctive meaning

The bowing sense can imply excessive submission or fawning, not merely polite thanks. Context determines whether the description is critical. Keep the three uses separate: being hungry does not make someone submissive, and a flexible lid is not hungry.
