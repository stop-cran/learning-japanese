# スチュワーデス (スチュワーデス) - stewardess; female flight attendant

## Meaning

スチュワーデス means a stewardess or female flight attendant. It is a
gender-specific occupation word, useful to recognize in older descriptions
of air travel.

- 祖母は若いころ、スチュワーデスとして働いていました。 (そぼはわかいころ、スチュワーデスとしてはたらいていました。) - My grandmother worked as a stewardess when she was young.
- この古い映画では、主人公がスチュワーデスとして働いています。 (このふるいえいがでは、しゅじんこうがスチュワーデスとしてはたらいています。) - In this old film, the main character works as a stewardess.

として means "as; in the role of" in these examples.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword. Keep the small ュ in チュ and the long vowel
after ワ: スチュワーデス. It has no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

客室乗務員 (きゃくしつじょうむいん), "cabin crew member," and
フライトアテンダント, "flight attendant," are gender-neutral alternatives.
キャビンアテンダント is also used. None is an antonym of the older word.

## Distinctive meaning

Current descriptions often use these gender-neutral alternatives. That does
not make スチュワーデス itself gender-neutral, nor mean that nobody understands
it today. Choose the institution's current job title rather than assuming
every member of the cabin crew is a スチュワーデス.
