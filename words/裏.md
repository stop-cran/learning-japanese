# 裏 (うら) — reverse side

## Meaning

裏 is the reverse, back, or hidden side of something. Use noun + の裏 for "the back of": 紙の裏 (かみのうら), "the reverse of the paper."

紙の裏に名前を書いてください。 (かみのうらになまえをかいてください。) — Please write your name on the back of the paper.

## How the kanji combine

裏 is one kanji with the native reading うら. Its range includes the side facing away from view and the area behind a building.

## Synonyms and antonyms

表 (おもて) is the front or outward-facing side. 後ろ (うしろ) describes a position behind something; it need not be a surface or a concealed side.

## Distinctive meaning

家の裏 (いえのうら) is the back of, or area behind, a house. 裏 can also suggest what is hidden, as in a concealed motive or behind-the-scenes activity. These are extensions of "the side not openly visible," not reasons to translate every 裏 as "secret."
