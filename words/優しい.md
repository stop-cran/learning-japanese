# 優しい (やさしい) — kind; gentle

## Meaning

優しい describes kindness, tenderness, or a gentle manner.
Use **person に優しい** for being kind to someone, and 優しい directly before a noun.
The form 優しく (やさしく) describes how an action is done.

- 姉は子供に優しいです。 (あねはこどもにやさしいです。) — My older sister is kind to children.
- 優しく話してください。 (やさしくはなしてください。) — Please speak gently.

## How the kanji combine

[優](../kanji/優.md) carries the gentleness sense here, followed by the adjective ending しい.
This is a native word with the reading やさしい, not a compound using ユウ.
The character also has excellence-related uses, but this adjective does not mean "superior to someone."

## Synonyms and antonyms

親切 しんせつ overlaps in kindness and consideration, but it is a な-adjective:
親切な人 (しんせつなひと), compared with 優しい人 (やさしいひと).
[冷たい](冷たい.md) つめたい provides a contrast when it describes emotionally cold behavior.
厳しい きびしい means strict or severe; someone can be strict about rules while still being kind.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse it with [易しい](易しい.md) やさしい, "easy," which JMdict marks as usually written in kana.
A 優しい先生 (やさしいせんせい) is a kind teacher; an 易しい問題 (やさしいもんだい) is an easy problem.
The sound alone is identical, so context and spelling separate kindness from low difficulty.
