# 切符 (きっぷ) - ticket

## Meaning

切符 is a ticket, especially one for travel by train or another form of transport.

- 駅で切符を買いました。 (えきできっぷをかいました。) - I bought a ticket at the station.
- 東京までの切符を二枚ください。 (とうきょうまでのきっぷをにまいください。) - Two tickets to Tokyo, please.

Paper tickets are counted with 枚 (まい), the counter for flat objects. The destination can precede までの切符, "a ticket as far as ..."

## How the kanji combine

[切](../kanji/切.md) contributes cutting and 符 a token or sign. This is a mixed native/Sino-Japanese reading: the first part relates to 切る (きる), while 符 has the on reading ふ. Learn the combined sound きっぷ, with a small っ and ぷ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

チケット is a broad loanword for tickets, including concerts and events. 乗車券 (じょうしゃけん) is the more formal word for a ticket authorizing travel.

## Distinctive meaning

切符 readily suggests a travel ticket in everyday conversation, but it does not by itself specify a reserved seat or a particular fare category. Those details require additional information.
