# 五月 (ごがつ) — May

## Meaning

五月 is May: 五月五日 ごがついつか (May 5th, Children's Day), 五月に旅行する (travel in May), 五月晴れ さつきばれ (clear weather in May; reads さつき, not ごがつ).

## How the kanji combine

[五](../kanji/五.md) (five) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month): the fifth month. Number on reading ご plus がつ.
"Five months" is 五か月 ごかげつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 皐月 さつき (the old name of the fifth month, also the azalea).
- Related: 五日 いつか — the 5th day.

## Distinctive meaning

五月 is the calendar month; 五か月 counts duration. 五月病 ごがつびょう (May blues) is a common phrase for lack of motivation after the new term.
