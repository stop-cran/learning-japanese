# 塾 (じゅく) — cram school; tutoring school

## Meaning

A 塾 is a privately run place for lessons. A student can attend one for extra instruction after regular school; preparation for an exam is one possible purpose.

- 放課後は塾に通っている。 (ほうかごはじゅくにかよっている。) — I go to a tutoring school after my regular classes.
- 塾で数学を習っています。 (じゅくですうがくをならっています。) — I take math lessons at a tutoring school.

## How the kanji combine

[塾](../kanji/塾.md) alone writes the noun. Its on-reading ジュク (じゅく) is the whole word here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[学校](学校.md) (がっこう) is the broader word for a school. In 学校のあとで塾に行く, the student goes from regular school to additional lessons.

## Distinctive meaning

“Cram school” fits many contexts, but does not mean that every 塾 teaches only test-taking. Use “tutoring school” when the point is supplementary lessons.
