# 水 (みず) - water

## Meaning

水 means water, especially cool or cold water in everyday drink and household
contexts. It is not limited to drinking water.

- 水を一杯ください。 (みずをいっぱいください。) - Please give me a glass of water.
- 花に水をやります。 (はなにみずをやります。) - I water the flowers.

In the second example, に marks what receives the water and を marks what is given.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [水](../kanji/水.md) writes the whole native noun みず.
The on-reading スイ appears in compounds such as 水道 (すいどう), "water
supply," not as a substitute for みず in these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お湯 (おゆ) specifically names hot water. 氷 (こおり), "ice," names its frozen
form. These are distinctions of temperature or state, not unrelated substances.

## Distinctive meaning

In a request for a drink, 水 normally means water rather than hot water;
お水 (おみず) is a polite everyday form. In scientific or general discussion,
水 can name the substance without specifying a serving temperature.
