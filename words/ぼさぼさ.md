# ぼさぼさ — tousled and unkempt

## Meaning

ぼさぼさ describes hair or bristles that are messy, ruffled, or unkempt.

- 寝起きで髪がぼさぼさだ。
  — My hair is tousled after waking up.
- 風で犬の毛がぼさぼさになった。
  — The wind left the dog's fur ruffled.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぼさぼさ describes a disordered appearance, unlike neatly combed hair.

## Distinctive meaning

It can describe a person's general unkempt look as well as hair and fur.
