# 時々 (ときどき) — sometimes, from time to time

## Meaning

時々 is an adverb meaning "now and then": 時々映画を見ます (I sometimes watch movies), 時々雨 (rain at times), 時々電話する (call from time to time).

## How the kanji combine

[時](../kanji/時.md) (とき, time) repeated, with 々 as the iteration mark. Repetition makes it "time after time", and the second と becomes ど (rendaku, voicing). The reading ときどき is entirely kun'yomi. It can also be written 時時 or in kana (ときどき).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: たまに (occasionally, less often), 時折 ときおり (now and then, a bit formal).
- Frequency scale (rough): いつも (always) > よく (often) > 時々 (sometimes) > たまに (occasionally) > あまり〜ない (not often) > 全然〜ない.

## Distinctive meaning

時々 is more frequent than たまに, which implies "rarely, once in a while". It is also used in weather forecasts (曇り時々雨, "cloudy, occasionally rain").
