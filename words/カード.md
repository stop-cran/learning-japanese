# カード (カード) - card; curd

## Meaning

The ordinary card word covers information cards, greeting cards, playing cards, and payment cards. It is a noun; カードで払う means "pay by card."

- 代金はカードで払えますか。 (だいきんはカードではらえますか。) - Can I pay by card?
- 友達に誕生日のカードを送りました。 (ともだちにたんじょうびのカードをおくりました。) - I sent a birthday card to a friend.

**A separate food word** with the same spelling and reading means "curd," the coagulated milk material involved in cheesemaking. カードからチーズを作ります (カードからチーズをつくります) means "Cheese is made from curd." This is a separate dictionary identity, not another payment-card sense.

## How the kanji combine

Both words are written in katakana. The long vowel mark ー belongs to カード. The card and curd identities correspond to different English words despite their identical Japanese spelling; there is no kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[手紙](手紙.md) (てがみ), "letter," can share a greeting card's communicative purpose but is not the same format. [切符](切符.md) (きっぷ), "ticket," gives access to transport or an event; being card-shaped does not make every card a ticket.

## Distinctive meaning

At a checkout, カード is understood through the payment context. In a cheesemaking explanation, the food identity matters instead. Some baking texts also use the card entry for a dough scraper; that tool meaning is not evidence that "curd" belongs to the same entry.
