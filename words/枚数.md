# 枚数 (まいすう) - number of sheets or other flat items

## Meaning

枚数 is the number of items counted with 枚, such as sheets, tickets, photographs, or plates. It names the count, not an identifying number printed on an item.

- コピーの枚数を確認してください。 (コピーのまいすうをかくにんしてください。) - Please check the number of copied sheets.
- 写真の枚数が増えました。 (しゃしんのまいすうがふえました。) - The number of photographs increased.

## How the kanji combine

[枚](../kanji/枚.md) (まい) counts thin, flat objects; [数](../kanji/数.md) (すう) contributes number. This is the number of 枚-counted items: まい + すう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[数](数.md) (かず), "number; amount," is more general. [点数](点数.md) (てんすう) can count items, as well as mean a score; 枚数 specifically follows the counting convention for 枚.

The counter [枚](枚.md) follows a numeral: 三枚 (さんまい), "three sheets." 枚数 is the noun in 枚数を数える (まいすうをかぞえる), "count the sheets/items."

## Distinctive meaning

Ask 枚数は何枚ですか (まいすうはなんまいですか), "How many sheets/items are there?" Do not confuse this with asking for a ticket's serial number.

The dictionary separately records a sumo sense: a win-loss difference affecting a wrestler's ranking. That specialized use is not the everyday sheet-counting pattern.
