# 瀬戸物 (せともの) - ceramic ware; crockery

## Meaning

瀬戸物 names ceramic ware, including pottery and porcelain.
It is useful for crockery such as ceramic bowls and plates.
It also retains the original meaning of ceramic ware from Seto in Aichi Prefecture.

- 瀬戸物の茶碗を買いました。 (せともののちゃわんをかいました。) - I bought a ceramic bowl.
- 瀬戸物は割れやすいので、丁寧に包んでください。 (せとものはわれやすいので、ていねいにつつんでください。) - Ceramic ware breaks easily, so please wrap it carefully.

Use 瀬戸物の before a noun naming an item.
The general usage does not establish that the item was manufactured in Seto.

## How the kanji combine

瀬戸 (せと) is the place name in this product name;
[物](../kanji/物.md), read もの, contributes things or goods.
[戸](../kanji/戸.md) is と within せと.
Learn the place-derived word rather than translating the characters as
"rapids-door things." 瀬戸もの and セトモノ are recorded search variants,
not a requirement to replace the primary 瀬戸物 spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

陶磁器 (とうじき) also names pottery and porcelain.
[食器](食器.md) (しょっき) means tableware and includes nonceramic items;
[皿](皿.md) (さら) is a plate or dish, not the name of every ceramic object.
There is no useful single opposite noun.

## Distinctive meaning

Ceramic ware is a material/product category, not simply anything used at a meal.
A metal spoon can be 食器 without being 瀬戸物.
Conversely, a ceramic vase need not be tableware.
The dictionary's original Seto-ware sense and its broader usage should not be
collapsed into a rule that all Japanese ceramics come from that city.
