# 代金 (だいきん) - price; payment due

## Meaning

代金 is the money paid or owed for goods or services. Use 商品の代金 (しょうひんのだいきん), "payment for the goods," and 代金を払う or 代金を支払う, "pay the amount due."

- 商品の代金をカードで支払いました。 (しょうひんのだいきんをカードでしはらいました。) - I paid for the goods by card.
- 代金は配達の時に払ってください。 (だいきんははいたつのときにはらってください。) - Please pay when the goods are delivered.

を identifies the payment as the object of paying; で can identify the payment method.

## How the kanji combine

[代](../kanji/代.md) contributes substitution or exchange, and [金](../kanji/金.md) contributes money. Together they describe money given in exchange for something. The on readings are ダイ + キン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[料金](料金.md) (りょうきん) commonly concerns a charge for a service or use. [費用](費用.md) (ひよう) is a cost or expense. These overlap with 代金, but their focus is not identical: 代金 readily identifies the sum owed in a purchase.

## Distinctive meaning

代金 names the sum, not necessarily the act of paying it. A sentence may say that the 代金 has not yet been paid. It also does not say whether tax, delivery, or other charges are included; that information must come from the surrounding terms of the transaction.
