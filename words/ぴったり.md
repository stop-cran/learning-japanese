# ぴったり (ぴったり) - exactly; perfectly fitting

## Meaning

ぴったり describes an exact match, a close fit, or something ideally suited to a purpose.
It can be an adverb, combine with する, or appear as ぴったりだ.
Use Nにぴったり for "just right for N."

- この靴は私の足にぴったりです。 (このくつはわたしのあしにぴったりです。) - These shoes fit my feet perfectly.
- 時計は十時ぴったりを指していました。 (とけいはじゅうじぴったりをさしていました。) - The clock showed exactly ten o'clock.

The first example concerns fit; the second concerns precision.
旅行にぴったりのかばん (りょこうにぴったりのかばん) is "a bag just right for traveling."

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana-written mimetic word, with no kanji to decompose.
Keep the small っ. The dictionary also records ぴたり and katakana forms,
but that variation does not make the expression a foreign loanword.
ぴったりと can be used before a verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ちょうど](ちょうど.md) overlaps in "exactly" and "just right" contexts.
[合う](合う.md) (あう), "fit; match," is a verb: ぴったり合う emphasizes how closely something fits.
A mismatch is the contextual contrast, not a fixed opposite with identical grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

Exactness is relative to the relevant target: a time, size, need, or purpose.
The shoe example does not mean the shoes are uncomfortably tight.
ぴったり閉じる (ぴったりとじる), "close tightly," instead describes a close seal or contact.
Let the verb and situation determine which kind of fit is intended.
