# 共に (ともに) - together; both

## Meaning

共に, also written **ともに**, is an adverb meaning "together; jointly." **Nとともに** can mean "with; along with N," and 共に can also mean "both."

- 共に働き、共に学びましょう。 (ともにはたらき、ともにまなびましょう。) - Let us work together and learn together.
- 家族とともに新しい町へ移りました。 (かぞくとともにあたらしいまちへうつりました。) - I moved to a new town along with my family.

For "both," compare 両親はともに医者です (りょうしんはともにいしゃです), "Both my parents are doctors."

## How the kanji combine

[共](../kanji/共.md) contributes sharing or being together. The established adverb is ともに; it is not a two-kanji compound. The dictionary marks kana spelling as usual, so ともに is appropriate in the same constructions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一緒](一緒.md) (いっしょ) appears in 一緒に, "together." 家族と一緒に overlaps with 家族とともに in the family example. Neither expression by itself is a verb meaning "participate."

## Distinctive meaning

共に modifies the action or statement: 働く says what people do, and 共に says they do it together. Do not conjugate it as though the English source phrase "participate in" were its verb meaning. Also distinguish joint action from "both": two people can both be doctors without working together.
