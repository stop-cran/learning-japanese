# 休講 (きゅうこう) - cancellation of a class or lecture

## Meaning

休講 means that a scheduled class or lecture is not being held. It is a noun and can take する. 休講になる presents a class as canceled.

- 今日の日本語の授業は休講です。
  (きょうのにほんごのじゅぎょうはきゅうこうです。) - Today's Japanese class is canceled.
- 先生の都合で、明日の講義は休講になりました。
  (せんせいのつごうで、あしたのこうぎはきゅうこうになりました。) - Tomorrow's lecture has been canceled because of the instructor's circumstances.

The word identifies the class's status, not a student's decision to skip it.

## How the kanji combine

[休](../kanji/休.md) contributes suspension or a break, and [講](../kanji/講.md) a lecture: the lecture is not held. The on readings are キュウ + コウ, giving きゅうこう with two long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

休校 (きゅうこう) means a temporary school closure. It sounds the same but has a different scope: one lecture can be 休講 while the school remains open. [欠席](欠席.md) (けっせき) is absence or non-attendance, and [講義](講義.md) (こうぎ) names the lecture itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer that a canceled session has been permanently removed from the course or that a replacement session will definitely be arranged. 休講 alone gives neither conclusion.
