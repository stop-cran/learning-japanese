# 夢 (ゆめ) — dream

## Meaning

夢 is a dream during sleep. It can also be a hope or ambition for the future.
The surrounding expression tells you which sense is intended.

- 昨夜、変な夢を見ました。 (さくや、へんなゆめをみました。) — I had a strange dream last night.
- 私の夢は医者になることです。 (わたしのゆめはいしゃになることです。) — My dream is to become a doctor.

For a sleeping dream, Japanese uses 夢を見る (ゆめをみる), literally "see a dream," where English says "have a dream."

## How the kanji combine

[夢](../kanji/夢.md) carries the entire native Japanese reading ゆめ.
This single-kanji noun is not split into separate readings for ゆ and め.
The on reading ム appears in 悪夢 (あくむ), "nightmare."

## Synonyms and antonyms

悪夢 (あくむ) is a bad dream, so it is a narrower kind of 夢, not an opposite.
[現実](現実.md) (げんじつ), "reality," contrasts with an imagined experience or situation.
That contrast does not imply that every future 夢 is impossible: the second example describes an ambition that might be realized.

## Distinctive meaning

夢 is a noun, so do not attach する and assume it means "dream" in the sleeping sense.
Use the full pattern 夢を見る.
In the ambition example, 医者になること means "becoming a doctor"; こと makes the action into the thing identified as the speaker's dream.
