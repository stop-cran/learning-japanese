# 何か (なにか) — something, anything

## Meaning

何か is an indefinite pronoun and adverb: 何か飲みますか (Will you drink something?), 何か問題がありますか (Is there some problem?), 何か食べたい (I want to eat something).
It can also mean "somehow, for some reason" (何かおかしい, "something is off").

## How the kanji combine

[何](../kanji/何.md) (なに, what) plus the particle か (indefinite or question marker): "what-or-other". The kanji is read なに with the kun'yomi, not なん, since か is not a counter and does not start with t/d/n.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 何も〜ない (nothing, with a negative), 何でも なんでも (anything), 誰か だれか (someone), どこか (somewhere).
- Antonym: 何も (nothing at all, with negative verb).

## Distinctive meaning

何か (something) in a positive or question sentence is the counterpart of 何も (nothing) in a negative one: 何か食べた, 何も食べなかった.
