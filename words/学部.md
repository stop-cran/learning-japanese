# 学部 (がくぶ) - university faculty; undergraduate division

## Meaning

学部 is a university faculty, school, or major organizational division, such as 法学部 (ほうがくぶ), faculty of law. It can also refer to the undergraduate division in contrast with 大学院 (だいがくいん), graduate school.

- 姉は大学の文学部で学んでいます。 (あねはだいがくのぶんがくぶでまなんでいます。) - My older sister studies in a university's faculty of letters.
- この授業は学部の学生向けです。 (このじゅぎょうはがくぶのがくせいむけです。) - This class is intended for undergraduate students.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) contributes academic study, and [部](../kanji/部.md) a section or division. The academic setting modifies the division. The on readings are ガク + ブ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大学](大学.md) (だいがく) names the university; a 学部 can be a division within it. [学科](学科.md) (がっか) can name a subject or a university department, often a more specific unit within a 学部. Institutional names and their English translations vary, so "department" does not determine one universal hierarchy.

## Distinctive meaning

"Faculty" here is an organizational unit, not simply the university's teaching staff. 学部 does not by itself name an individual undergraduate student: 学部生 (がくぶせい) does. The undergraduate/graduate contrast and the division-by-field use are related uses, not a claim that every occurrence means one person.
