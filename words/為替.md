# 為替 (かわせ) - financial exchange; money order

## Meaning

為替 concerns financial exchange or settlement without physically transporting the cash between the parties. In foreign-exchange contexts it concerns different currencies. It can also name a money order or financial draft.

- 毎朝、為替レートを確認します。 (まいあさ、かわせレートをかくにんします。) - I check the exchange rate every morning.
- 代金を郵便為替で送りました。 (だいきんをゆうびんかわせでおくりました。) - I sent the payment by postal money order.

為替レート (かわせレート) is an exchange rate. 郵便為替 (ゆうびんかわせ) identifies the money-order context rather than a draft of a letter.

## How the kanji combine

The written form contains 為 and [替](../kanji/替.md). 替's replacing or exchanging association is useful, but かわせ is a special whole-word reading. Do not invent separate readings か and わせ for these characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[両替](両替.md) (りょうがえ) is changing money, including denominations within one currency. [交換](交換.md) (こうかん) is broader exchange or replacement. [換える](換える.md) (かえる) is a verb that can describe exchanging money. These overlap in financial discussion without being unrestricted replacements.

## Distinctive meaning

為替 is not every exchange of objects or opinions, and financial exchange is not restricted to handing foreign notes over a counter. The English "draft" here is a financial instrument, not a proposed text or [案](案.md) (あん). The noun alone does not establish fees, rates, or current service availability.
