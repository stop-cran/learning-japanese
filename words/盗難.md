# 盗難 (とうなん) - theft; having property stolen

## Meaning

盗難 is theft, especially an incident in which someone's property is stolen.
Use 盗難に遭う (とうなんにあう), "suffer a theft," and
盗難の被害 (とうなんのひがい), "loss or damage from theft."

- 旅行中に荷物が盗難に遭いました。
  (りょこうちゅうににもつがとうなんにあいました。) - Our luggage was stolen during the trip.
- 自転車の盗難を警察に届けました。
  (じてんしゃのとうなんをけいさつにとどけました。) - I reported the bicycle theft to the police.

The noun does not identify who stole the property.

## How the kanji combine

[盗](../kanji/盗.md) contributes stealing, and [難](../kanji/難.md)
trouble or misfortune. Together they frame theft as an incident suffered.
The on readings are トウ + ナン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[盗む](盗む.md) (ぬすむ) is the verb "steal."
[泥棒](泥棒.md) (どろぼう) can name a thief or theft.
[強盗](強盗.md) (ごうとう) concerns robbery involving force or threats;
盗難 does not require that feature.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 盗難する as the ordinary way to say someone steals.
The property or victim can suffer 盗難, while the thief 盗む.
The broad English gloss "robbery" must not be read as a requirement that
every 盗難 involves a confrontation.
