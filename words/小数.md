# 小数 (しょうすう) - decimal; decimal fraction

## Meaning

小数 in mathematics means a decimal: a number expressed with digits after a decimal point, such as 0.5 or 1.23. 小数点 (しょうすうてん) is the decimal point.

- 0.5は小数です。 (れいてんごはしょうすうです。) - 0.5 is a decimal.
- 答えを小数で書いてください。 (こたえをしょうすうでかいてください。) - Please write the answer as a decimal.

で marks the form in which the answer should be written. The whole-number part can be greater than zero; a decimal need not be smaller than one.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) contributes small, and [数](../kanji/数.md) number. The character association helps recall parts of a unit, but the mathematical term is an established whole word. Read ショウ + スウ, keeping both long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

分数 (ぶんすう) is a fraction written as one number over another: 1/2 can be expressed as the decimal 0.5. [整数](整数.md) (せいすう) is an integer. [数字](数字.md) (すうじ) concerns numerals or numerical figures, not specifically decimal notation.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not treat the source gloss "fraction" as permission to call every fraction's written form 小数. In ordinary school mathematics, 1/2 as written is a 分数 and 0.5 a 小数. JMdict separately records "small number; small amount"; that recognition does not erase the standard spelling 少数 (しょうすう) for a small number or minority.
