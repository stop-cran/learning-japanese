# ぽたぽた — dripping drop by drop

## Meaning

Liquid falling in repeated, separate drops.

- 蛇口（じゃぐち）から水がぽたぽた落ちている。 — Water is dripping from the faucet.
- ぬれた傘（かさ）から、しずくがぽたぽた落ちた。 — Drops fell from the wet umbrella.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぽつぽつ](ぽつぽつ.md) overlaps in scattered drops. [だらだら](だらだら.md) can suggest a more continuous trickle; these are not fixed measurements of flow.

## Distinctive meaning

ぽたぽた落ちる describes the drops, not the cause of a leak. The inspected entry groups ぼたぼた with this word, but its wet-heavy snow/clay and archaic slow-motion senses are restricted to ぼたぼた forms, not this ぽたぽた card.

See [Japanese onomatopoeia](../articles/japanese-onomatopoeia.md) and [motion and sound](../articles/onomatopoeia-motion-and-sound.md).
