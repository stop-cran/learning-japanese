# 手紙 (てがみ) - letter, written message

## Meaning

手紙 is a letter or written note addressed to someone. It normally suggests a written message on paper rather than an email.

- 祖母に手紙を書きました。 (そぼにてがみをかきました。) - I wrote a letter to my grandmother.
- 友達から手紙が届きました。 (ともだちからてがみがとどきました。) - A letter arrived from my friend.

Use に for the recipient and から for the sender in these patterns.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md), て, means "hand," and [紙](../kanji/紙.md), かみ, means "paper." The second element is voiced to がみ in the compound. These native readings make てがみ, not the on-reading sequence of the two characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

メール is the usual word for email. はがき is a postcard, which names a particular format rather than every letter. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

手紙 refers to the message or document, not paper as a raw material; that is 紙 (かみ). Nor does 手 require every letter to be handwritten: a typed or printed personal letter can still be 手紙.
