# クリスマス (クリスマス) - Christmas

## Meaning

クリスマス is a noun meaning Christmas. It can name the occasion in a time expression or modify another noun with の.

- クリスマスに友達と食事をしました。 (クリスマスにともだちとしょくじをしました。) - I had a meal with friends at Christmas.
- クリスマスのプレゼントを買いました。 (クリスマスのプレゼントをかいました。) - I bought a Christmas present.

に marks the occasion in the first example, while と identifies the people sharing the meal. クリスマスの + noun identifies something associated with Christmas.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "Christmas." It contains no written kanji, so there is no kanji decomposition. Learn the Japanese sound sequence クリスマス rather than trying to pronounce an English consonant cluster unchanged.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お祝い](お祝い.md) (おいわい) is celebration or congratulations more generally; it does not specifically mean Christmas. [誕生日](誕生日.md) (たんじょうび), "birthday," is another occasion on which presents may be given, not a synonym or an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun names the occasion, not a universal set of customs or a guaranteed day off work. The examples state what particular people did without assuming that everyone celebrates in the same way. If a schedule, religious observance, or specific date matters, provide that information separately rather than relying on the noun to establish it.
