# 作者 (さくしゃ) — creator of a work

## Meaning

作者 is the person who created a work: an author, artist, composer, or other maker, depending on
what the work is. It is not limited to written texts.

- この小説の作者は誰ですか。 (この しょうせつの さくしゃは だれですか。) — Who is the author of this novel?
- この絵の作者は分かりません。 (この えの さくしゃは わかりません。) — We do not know who created this picture.

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) サク contributes making or creating; [者](../kanji/者.md) シャ identifies
the person. It is action + person: the creator.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作家](作家.md) さっか commonly means a writer or creative practitioner. 作者 relates someone to a
particular work; 作家 describes a creative activity or identity. The same person may fit both.
読者 (どくしゃ), "reader," is a counterpart for a text, not an opposite in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

Being the 作者 of a work does not establish a career, income, qualification, or reputation.
Do not turn the contrast with 作家 into a rule that 作者 is an amateur and 作家 a professional.

For 作者 compared with 作家 and the endings 者 and 家, see
[occupation and role endings](../articles/occupation-and-role-endings.md).
