# 長短 (ちょうたん) — length; strengths and weaknesses

## Meaning

長短 pairs [長](../kanji/長.md) (long) and 短 (short, たん; みじかい, not a card yet). Two senses:

1. **Length** — how long or short something is: 髪の長短 (hair length), 時間の長短 (length of time).
2. **Merits and faults** — long point and short point: 人の長短を知る (know a person's strengths and weaknesses).

The four-character 一長一短 いっちょういったん, "one merit and one defect", means that something has both pros and cons.

## How the kanji combine

Same pattern as [大小](大小.md): opposites joined into an abstract dimension. Here the dimension is either physical length, or by extension the
spectrum of good points (長所 ちょうしょ) and bad points (短所 たんしょ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- For length: 長さ ながさ (native noun).
- For pros and cons: 長所と短所, メリット・デメリット, 一長一短, 利点と欠点.
- The word has no antonym; 長 ↔ 短 are its components.

## Distinctive meaning

In daily speech Japanese uses 長さ for measuring and 長所／短所 for personality or product points; 長短 is more formal and idiomatic.
