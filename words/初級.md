# 初級 (しょきゅう) - beginner level; elementary level

## Meaning

初級 is an elementary or beginner level. It is a noun and can modify another
noun with の. It also appears directly in compounds such as 初級コース
(しょきゅうコース), a beginner course.

- 初級のクラスに入りました。 (しょきゅうのクラスにはいりました。) - I joined the beginner class.
- この本は初級の学習者向けです。 (このほんはしょきゅうのがくしゅうしゃむけです。) - This book is intended for elementary-level learners.

The model is 初級の + noun, not an automatically inferred 初級な.

## How the kanji combine

[初](../kanji/初.md) contributes beginning, and 級 contributes a level or
grade. The first character identifies the beginning level in a graded scale.
The on readings are ショ + キュウ: the long vowel is in きゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

中級 (ちゅうきゅう) is intermediate level, and [上級](上級.md)
(じょうきゅう) advanced level. 初心者 (しょしんしゃ) names a beginner,
the person rather than the level.

[基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) and [基本](基本.md) (きほん) concern foundations
or basics, which are not always easy or confined to a beginner course.

## Distinctive meaning

初級 classifies a level; it does not promise that every learner will find
the material easy. The relevant scale comes from the course or activity.
Do not infer a person's age or school grade solely from 初級.
