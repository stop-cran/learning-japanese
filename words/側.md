# 側 (そば) — close by; beside

## Meaning

そば is the nearby place beside someone or something. It is a noun, so use noun + のそば, followed by the appropriate location particle.

- 駅のそばに住んでいます。 (えきのそばにすんでいます。) — I live near the station.
- 私のそばに座ってください。 (わたしのそばにすわってください。) — Please sit beside me.

## How the kanji combine

[側](../kanji/側.md) is a dictionary spelling of そば, but kana is usual for this word. 傍 is another spelling. The written character also occurs in words read がわ or そく; those readings are not freely interchangeable here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

近く (ちかく) broadly means nearby. 隣 (となり) identifies the adjacent place or item. そば stresses proximity without necessarily claiming that two items are immediate neighbors in a row.

## Distinctive meaning

そば does not specify a fixed distance: near a station and beside a person involve different scales. It is also unrelated to the food そば, "buckwheat noodles," despite the identical kana spelling.
