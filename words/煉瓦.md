# 煉瓦 (れんが) - brick

## Meaning

煉瓦 is a brick, typically a building block made by shaping and
firing clay. The word is usually written in kana: れんが or レンガ.

- 煉瓦でできた古い建物を見ました。 (れんがでできたふるいたてものをみました。) - I saw an old building made of brick.
- 庭にレンガを並べました。 (にわにレンガをならべました。) - I laid out bricks in the garden.

でできた identifies what something is made of.

## How the kanji combine

煉 has refining or kneading-over-fire associations, and
瓦 concerns tile. Think of fired building material as a meaning aid,
not a proven historical derivation.
The on readings are レン + ガ. The whole written word is れんが.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[瓦](瓦.md) (かわら) names a tile, especially a roof tile.
[石](石.md) (いし) is stone.
These are related building materials, not interchangeable names
for every brick or tile. Neither is an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The katakana spelling レンガ is useful to recognize, but this card
keeps 煉瓦 with reading れんが as its primary written identity.
The dictionary's kana-only レンガ reading is not an instruction
to pronounce the written kanji in a different way.
A brick need not have one fixed color or size.
