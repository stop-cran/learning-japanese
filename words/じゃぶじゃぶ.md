# じゃぶじゃぶ — splashing about in water

## Meaning

Repeated splashing, often while washing something or moving around in water.

- 子どもたちが浅（あさ）い川でじゃぶじゃぶ遊んだ。 — The children splashed about in the shallow river.
- バケツの水で靴下（くつした）をじゃぶじゃぶ洗（あら）った。 — I washed the socks with plenty of splashing in a bucket.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ざぶざぶ](ざぶざぶ.md) and [ばしゃばしゃ](ばしゃばしゃ.md) can fit overlapping splashing scenes. A picture of washing alone will not necessarily distinguish them.

## Distinctive meaning

じゃぶじゃぶ洗う describes abundant or vigorous water use. Figurative uses can concern pouring or spending generously; they do not require literal water. The word is not itself a guarantee that something becomes clean.
