# 虹 (にじ) - rainbow

## Meaning

虹 is a noun meaning a rainbow, the colored arc seen in the sky.
Useful combinations include 虹が出る (にじがでる), "a rainbow appears,"
and 虹がかかる (にじがかかる), "a rainbow spans the sky."

- 雨のあと、虹が出ました。 (あめのあと、にじがでました。) - A rainbow appeared after the rain.
- 空に大きな虹がかかっています。 (そらにおおきなにじがかかっています。) - A large rainbow stretches across the sky.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native word, read にじ as a whole.
Do not try to obtain the reading by pronouncing the visible parts separately.
The dictionary also records the rare spelling 霓; it is not needed to read
ordinary 虹.

## Synonyms and antonyms

There is no useful everyday antonym. [雨](雨.md) (あめ), "rain," is related
to the weather in which a rainbow may be seen, but is not a synonym.
虹色 (にじいろ) describes rainbow colors rather than naming another weather event.

## Distinctive meaning

Write and read this word as 虹 にじ. Other kanji or readings associated
with 虹 in names and literary compounds do not change this noun's reading.
Seeing a rainbow in an example does not imply that every period of rain
will produce one.
