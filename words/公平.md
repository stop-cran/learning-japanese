# 公平 (こうへい) - fair; impartial

## Meaning

公平 describes fairness without favoring one side. It is a な-adjective and
also a noun for fairness: 公平な判断 (こうへいなはんだん), "a fair judgment";
公平に扱う (こうへいにあつかう), "treat fairly."

- 審判には公平な判断が求められます。 (しんぱんにはこうへいなはんだんがもとめられます。) - Referees are expected to make fair judgments.
- 全員を公平に扱ってください。 (ぜんいんをこうへいにあつかってください。) - Please treat everyone fairly.

As a noun, 公平を保つ (こうへいをたもつ) means "maintain fairness."
The adjective's negative is 公平ではない, "not fair."

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md) contributes public or impartial, and [平](../kanji/平.md)
contributes even or level. Their meanings work together in the idea of
even-handed treatment. The on readings are コウ + ヘイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[平等](平等.md) (びょうどう) emphasizes equality. 公平 emphasizes fairness or
impartiality; what counts as fair may depend on relevant differences between
people's situations. 不公平 (ふこうへい), "unfair," is a direct opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

公平 does not automatically require identical amounts or outcomes in every
situation. It characterizes the treatment or judgment as fair. The word can
be used to argue about fairness, so a speaker's use of it is an evaluation,
not independent proof that all parties agree.
