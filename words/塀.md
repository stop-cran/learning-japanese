# 塀 (へい) - boundary wall; fence

## Meaning

塀 is a noun for a wall or fence that encloses or separates an area, such as the boundary around a house or garden. It can refer to different materials; the noun alone does not specify concrete, stone, or wood.

- 家の周りに高い塀があります。 (いえのまわりにたかいへいがあります。) - There is a high wall around the house.
- 猫が塀の上に座っています。 (ねこがへいのうえにすわっています。) - A cat is sitting on top of the wall.

塀の向こう (へいのむこう) means "the other side of the wall/fence."

## How the kanji combine

塀 is a single character read へい. It supplies the whole noun, so there is no multi-kanji relationship to explain. In speech, へい may sound like a long "he," but the written reading remains へい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[壁](壁.md) (かべ) is a broader word for a wall, including the walls of a room or building. 塀 is useful when the boundary stands around a property rather than enclosing an interior room. The distinction concerns function, not a universal material rule.

## Distinctive meaning

English "wall" does not identify which Japanese noun is intended. Compare a picture on a room's 壁 with a 塀 around a garden. A gate provides an opening through a boundary; it is a related structure, not an antonym of 塀.
