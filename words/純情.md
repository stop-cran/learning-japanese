# 純情 (じゅんじょう) - innocence; pure-heartedness; naivety

## Meaning

純情 describes an innocent or unworldly heart: pure feelings, guilelessness, or naivety. It is a noun and a な-adjective.

- 彼は純情な青年です。 (かれはじゅんじょうなせいねんです。) - He is an innocent, pure-hearted young man.
- 彼女の純情をからかわないでください。 (かのじょのじゅんじょうをからかわないでください。) - Please do not make fun of her innocence.

Use 純情な before a noun or 純情だ / 純情です as a predicate. The word can sound affectionate, admiring, or suggestive of a lack of worldly experience, depending on context.

## How the kanji combine

[純](../kanji/純.md) contributes purity, and [情](../kanji/情.md) feelings or emotional disposition. This provides a reminder of pure-heartedness, not a literal recipe for every word containing either character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[純粋](純粋.md) overlaps in purity or innocence but also describes unmixed materials. [清い](清い.md), [清潔](清潔.md), and [きれい](きれい.md) have moral-purity uses as well as physical ones. [単純](単純.md) and [おめでたい](おめでたい.md) can also suggest naivety, with different evaluative tones.

## Distinctive meaning

純情 concerns a person's feelings or disposition, not whether a room is hygienic or a chemical contains impurities. It does not independently mean sacrificing oneself for another person: devotion or self-sacrifice requires additional context. Do not infer that every innocent person is unintelligent or that the word always refers to romantic love.
