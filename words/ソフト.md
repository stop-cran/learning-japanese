# ソフト — soft

## Meaning

ソフト describes a soft, gentle, or mild quality. In the adjective use, ソフトな goes before a noun and ソフトに describes how an action is done.

先生はソフトな声で話します。 (せんせいはソフトなこえではなします。) — The teacher speaks in a gentle voice.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word is based on English "soft." There are no kanji components. It also serves as an abbreviation of several longer loanwords, so not every ソフト is an adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

柔らかい (やわらかい) overlaps with the physical and gentle senses of "soft." ハード is a contrast in some established contexts, but is not a universal replacement for "hard."

## Distinctive meaning

As a noun in computing, ソフト means software: 新しいソフトを使っています (あたらしいソフトをつかっています), "I use new software." In a food setting it may abbreviate ソフトクリーム, soft-serve ice cream. The dictionary's adjective sense and these abbreviated noun senses share the same spelling; the surrounding topic determines which is intended.
