# のんびり - at leisure; relax

## Meaning

のんびり describes a relaxed, unhurried manner. Use it directly before a verb or with と. のんびりする is an intransitive expression meaning to relax or take it easy.

- 休日は家でのんびりしています。 (きゅうじつはいえでのんびりしています。) - I take it easy at home on my days off.
- 川のそばをのんびりと歩きました。 (かわのそばをのんびりとあるきました。) - We walked leisurely beside the river.

To modify a noun, のんびりした休日 (のんびりしたきゅうじつ) means "a relaxing day off." The した form connects the expression to the noun.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in kana. The dictionary treats its adverbial use as mimetic: it depicts a relaxed manner or state rather than naming an actual sound. There is no kanji compound or foreign source to reconstruct.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ゆっくり](ゆっくり.md) also describes taking time or proceeding slowly. のんびり particularly emphasizes being unhurried or at ease. [急ぐ](急ぐ.md) (いそぐ), "hurry," gives a contrasting action.

## Distinctive meaning

Slow movement is not necessarily relaxed: someone carrying something fragile may move slowly while feeling tense. のんびり adds the relaxed manner. Depending on context, that can be approving or critical; のんびりしている場合じゃない means "this is no time to take it easy," not that relaxing is inherently bad.
