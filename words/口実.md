# 口実 (こうじつ) - excuse; pretext

## Meaning

口実 is an excuse or pretext offered to justify an action or avoid something.
It often suggests that the stated reason is convenient rather than the whole
real motive. Nを口実に means "using N as a pretext."

- 忙しさを口実に、返事を先延ばしにしました。 (いそがしさをこうじつに、へんじをさきのばしにしました。) - I used being busy as an excuse to put off replying.
- 参加しない口実を探しています。 (さんかしないこうじつをさがしています。) - I am looking for an excuse not to participate.

## How the kanji combine

[口](../kanji/口.md) contributes speaking or what is said, while
[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes substance or grounds. As a learning aid,
connect them with the grounds put forward in speech; the modern word does
not guarantee that those grounds are the actual motive.
The on readings are コウ + ジツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

言い訳 (いいわけ) and 弁解 (べんかい) also concern excuses or explanations
in defense of oneself. [理由](理由.md) (りゆう) and [訳](訳.md) (わけ)
are broader words for a reason; they can also be used of an excuse.
[申し訳](申し訳.md) (もうしわけ) appears in apologetic explanations.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling something a 口実 can cast doubt on the speaker's stated motive,
but the underlying circumstance need not itself be invented.
Being genuinely busy can still be used as a convenient pretext.
This noun is not the standalone polite phrase "excuse me."
