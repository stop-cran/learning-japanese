# 殻 (から) - shell; husk

## Meaning

殻 is a shell, husk, hull, or pod: an outer casing of something such as an egg, nut, or seed. Use Nの殻 to identify what it belongs to.

- 卵の殻を捨てました。 (たまごのからをすてました。) - I threw away the eggshells.
- くるみの殻は硬いです。 (くるみのからはかたいです。) - Walnut shells are hard.

The noun does not itself distinguish one shell from several.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 殻 writes the native noun から. There is no second character or kana ending to divide. It is a different word from [空](空.md) read から, "empty."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[皮](皮.md) (かわ) also names outer coverings, such as skin, peel, or a wrapper. [貝](貝.md) (かい) can name shellfish or a seashell; 殻 is not restricted to shellfish. Learn the object and collocation rather than assuming every English "shell" has the same Japanese translation.

## Distinctive meaning

An eggshell can be a 殻 without being a 貝. Conversely, the presence of a covering does not automatically select 殻: a book's [カバー](カバー.md) is not normally described as its eggshell-like casing. The examples concern the outer casing, not the contents.
