# 血圧 (けつあつ) - blood pressure

## Meaning

血圧 means blood pressure.
It is a noun; familiar combinations include 血圧を測る, measuring blood pressure, and 血圧が高い, having high blood pressure.

- 毎朝、血圧を測ります。 (まいあさ、けつあつをはかります。) - I measure my blood pressure every morning.
- 医師に血圧が高いと言われました。 (いしにけつあつがたかいといわれました。) - The doctor told me my blood pressure was high.

## How the kanji combine

[血](../kanji/血.md) specifies blood; [圧](../kanji/圧.md) supplies pressure.
The modifier identifies the kind of pressure.
The on readings combine as ケツ + アツ, with no small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[圧力](圧力.md) (あつりょく) is pressure more generally.
[血液](血液.md) (けつえき) names the blood itself, not its pressure.
[気圧](気圧.md) (きあつ) is air pressure, a different measurement.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 血圧 with a blood sample or the amount of blood.
高い and 低い describe the pressure reading; the word itself is not a diagnosis.
The examples teach language, not thresholds for medical decisions.
