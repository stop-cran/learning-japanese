# 桐 (きり) - paulownia tree

## Meaning

桐 is paulownia, *Paulownia tomentosa*, also called the empress tree or foxglove tree. These English names identify the same tree here, not three different readings.

- 庭に桐の木があります。 (にわにきりのきがあります。) - There is a paulownia tree in the garden.
- 桐の花が咲いています。 (きりのはながさいています。) - The paulownia is in bloom.

桐の木 きりのき explicitly names the tree. Its light wood is used for objects such as chests; 桐材 きりざい means paulownia wood.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [桐](../kanji/桐.md) writes the native noun きり. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide. Botanical writing also uses キリ; JMdict marks that katakana form as kana-only, not as another pronunciation of 桐.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[木](木.md) き is the broader word for a tree or wood. [杉](杉.md) すぎ, Japanese cedar, and [柳](柳.md) やなぎ, willow, name different trees rather than opposites of 桐.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not treat every plant name containing 桐 as the same species. 梧桐 ごとう / あおぎり is the Chinese parasol-tree, *Firmiana simplex*, not the paulownia taught here. Likewise, 桐油 とうゆ is tung oil from アブラギリ and related trees, not oil from キリ.

The character's name-use classification does not make this plant noun a person's name. Learn actual personal names separately.
