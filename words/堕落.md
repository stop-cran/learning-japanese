# 堕落 (だらく) — moral decline; degradation

## Meaning

堕落 is a fall into a worse state, especially in moral or social conduct. It can also describe something losing a normal or healthy condition.

- 彼は堕落した生活を送っていた。 (かれはだらくしたせいかつをおくっていた。) — He led a dissolute life.

## How the kanji combine

[堕](../kanji/堕.md) and [落](../kanji/落.md) both occur in words about falling or decline. 堕落 is a whole lexical item and can describe moral or social deterioration, not only a physical fall.

## Synonyms and antonyms

堕落 can overlap with words for corruption or deterioration, but its context determines whether the focus is conduct, condition, or decline. Improvement or recovery can be a contextual contrast; there is no single fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Unlike [低下](低下.md), which can describe a neutral measured drop such as temperature, 堕落 frames a change as falling into a worse state, often involving conduct or morality. JMdict records noun, intransitive, and する-verb uses; the cited Kotobank page's 精選版 日本国語大辞典 entry also records the literal sense “fall.”
