# 堕落 (だらく) — moral decline; degradation

## Meaning

堕落 is a fall into a worse state, especially in moral or social conduct.

- 彼は堕落した生活を送っていた。 (かれはだらくしたせいかつをおくっていた。) — He led a dissolute life.

## How the kanji combine

[堕](../kanji/堕.md) is associated with a lapse into a worse state, while [落](../kanji/落.md) also has “fall; drop” meanings. Together they form the established word 堕落 for deterioration, especially moral or social decline; do not extend it to any physical fall or neutral decrease.

## Synonyms and antonyms

堕落 can overlap with words for corruption or deterioration. Its evaluation is negative; improvement or recovery is a contextual contrast, not a single fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Unlike [低下](低下.md), which can describe a neutral measured drop such as temperature, 堕落 frames a change as falling into a worse state, often involving conduct or morality. JMdict records noun, intransitive, and する-verb uses.
