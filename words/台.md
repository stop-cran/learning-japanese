# 台 (だい) - stand; supporting base

## Meaning

台 is a stand, base, or platform that supports something. As a noun, it can
take ordinary particles: 台に置く (だいにおく), "put on a stand."

- この台に花瓶を置きます。 (このだいにかびんをおきます。) - I will put a vase on this stand.
- 車が二台あります。 (くるまがにだいあります。) - There are two cars.

The second example shows a separate function of the same entry: 台 is a
counter for machines and vehicles. 一台 (いちだい), 二台 (にだい), 三台
(さんだい) count units, not supporting platforms.

## How the kanji combine

[台](../kanji/台.md) supplies the whole noun with its on reading ダイ. There
is no compound to divide. The support/platform meaning is useful for learning
the noun, but does not mean every item counted with 台 has a separate stand.

## Synonyms and antonyms

A テーブル is a table with its own everyday uses; 台 focuses on a supporting
base and need not be a dining table. [机](机.md) (つくえ) is a desk, not a
general replacement for every 台.

## Distinctive meaning

After a rounded numerical value, 台 can also indicate a range: 三十度台
(さんじゅうどだい), "temperatures in the thirties." This is not a count of
thirty machines. The surrounding unit and noun distinguish the support,
counter, and range uses.
