# 年度 (ねんど) - fiscal year; school year

## Meaning

年度 is an annual period established for administration, accounting or schooling.
It can be a fiscal year or school year, with boundaries set by the institution.
今年度 (こんねんど) means the current such year;
来年度 (らいねんど) means the next one.

- 来年度の予算を話し合いました。
  (らいねんどのよさんをはなしあいました。) - We discussed the budget for next fiscal year.
- この学校の新年度は四月に始まります。
  (このがっこうのしんねんどはしがつにはじまります。) - This school's new school year begins in April.

の attaches the period to 予算 in the first example.
The second states this school's schedule; it is not a rule about every school.

## How the kanji combine

[年](../kanji/年.md) contributes a year and
[度](../kanji/度.md) a period or occurrence: an established annual span.
The on readings are ネン + ド.
This is not the homophone 粘土 (ねんど), "clay."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[年](年.md) (とし; ねん) is broader "year";
[年間](年間.md) (ねんかん) can express a year or yearly duration.
[学年](学年.md) (がくねん) includes a school year and also a school grade.
[学期](学期.md) (がっき), a school term or semester, need not span a whole year.
Previous and following periods can be 前年度 (ぜんねんど) and 翌年度 (よくねんど).

## Distinctive meaning

Japan's government fiscal year ordinarily runs from April 1 to March 31.
Do not extend that calendar to every company or institution.
An 年度 can cross a calendar-year boundary without becoming two 年度.
The dictionary also records a product-year use; it is recognition information,
not a reason to read every occurrence as a product model.
