# 農薬 (のうやく) - agrochemical; pesticide

## Meaning

農薬 means agricultural chemicals, including pesticides and other agents
used in managing crops. The category includes insecticides, fungicides,
herbicides and growth regulators; it is not only insect spray.
It is a noun, not a general word for every material used on a farm.

- 農薬を使わずに野菜を育てています。
  (のうやくをつかわずにやさいをそだてています。) - I grow vegetables without using agricultural chemicals.
- この農薬は決められた量を守って使ってください。
  (こののうやくはきめられたりょうをまもってつかってください。) - Please use this agrochemical in the specified amount.

使わずに means "without using."
The second example is language practice, not product-specific safety instructions.

## How the kanji combine

[農](../kanji/農.md) contributes agriculture and
[薬](../kanji/薬.md) a chemical agent or medicine.
Agriculture qualifies the agent: ノウ + ヤク.
Do not infer that 農薬 is medicine for people from 薬 alone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[薬](薬.md) (くすり) commonly names medicine, but its dictionary family
also includes efficacious chemical agents and pesticides.
殺虫剤 (さっちゅうざい), "insecticide," is one kind of 農薬.
肥料 (ひりょう), "fertilizer," supplies nutrients;
do not treat fertilizer and crop-protection chemicals as synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

The category does not by itself establish a particular product's hazard,
legal use, dose or effect.
Nor does the word alone establish whether produce is safe or unsafe.
Use the wider gloss "agrochemical" alongside "pesticide" so that growth regulators
are not silently excluded from the category.
