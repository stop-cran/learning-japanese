# 寮 (りょう) - dormitory; hostel

## Meaning

寮 is a dormitory or shared residential accommodation, such as a
student or employee dormitory. It is a noun:
学生寮 (がくせいりょう), "student dormitory," and
社員寮 (しゃいんりょう), "employee dormitory."

- 大学の寮に住んでいます。 (だいがくのりょうにすんでいます。) - I live in a university dormitory.
- 会社の寮では台所を共同で使っています。 (かいしゃのりょうではだいどころをきょうどうでつかっています。) - We share the kitchen in the company dormitory.

## How the kanji combine

This is the single character 寮, read with the on reading リョウ.
The modern housing word here is not a combination of separate characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下宿](下宿.md) (げしゅく) concerns boarding or lodging, often in
a private house. It can overlap in housing situations, but does not
automatically mean an institution's dormitory.
[宿](宿.md) (やど) is a broader lodging or dwelling word.

## Distinctive meaning

The housing sense does not mean that every hotel or apartment is a 寮.
The dictionary also records archaic bureau, tea-room and villa senses;
this card selects modern residential accommodation.
Do not confuse it with the homophone [量](量.md), "quantity; amount."
