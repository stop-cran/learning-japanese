# 父母 (ふぼ) - father and mother; parents

## Meaning

父母 refers to father and mother together: parents.

- 父母に感謝しています。 (ふぼにかんしゃしています。) - I am grateful to my parents.
- 父母の健康を願っています。 (ふぼのけんこうをねがっています。) - I hope my parents stay healthy.

## How the kanji combine

[父](../kanji/父.md), father, and [母](../kanji/母.md), mother, form a coordinated pair. The primary reading uses on readings フ + ボ. The dictionary also records the native reading ちちはは for the same written form; it does not replace ふぼ in this card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[両親](両親.md) (りょうしん) overlaps directly in both parents. [親](親.md) (おや) is broader parent/parents language, with the number and reference supplied by context.

## Distinctive meaning

This word refers to the parents as a pair; it is not a title for calling out to one parent. Do not infer a fixed singular or plural rule for every Japanese family noun from the English translation. The dictionary's historical readings are recognition data, not alternative everyday pronunciations taught here.
