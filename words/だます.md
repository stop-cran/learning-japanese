# だます - deceive; trick; coax

## Meaning

だます means to deceive or trick someone, including cheating or
swindling them. It is a transitive godan verb: the person deceived
takes を. Forms include だまします, だました, だまして,
and だまさない.

- うその話で人をだましてはいけません。
  (うそのはなしでひとをだましてはいけません。) - You must not deceive people with a false story.
- 偽物だと知らずに、だまされて買ってしまいました。
  (にせものだとしらずに、だまされてかってしまいました。) - Not knowing it was a fake, I was tricked into buying it.

だまされる is passive, "be deceived." The verb also has a
coaxing or soothing use, for example distracting a child to settle
them down. It does not always describe financial fraud.

## How the kanji combine

騙す is a dictionary spelling of だます. This article uses the
source's kana spelling, so its written-kanji array is empty.
The final す changes to し before ます.

## Synonyms and antonyms

欺く (あざむく) overlaps with deceiving or betraying someone's
trust. [信じる](信じる.md) (しんじる), "believe; trust,"
describes a different participant's attitude, not a direct antonym:
someone may trust the very person deceiving them.

## Distinctive meaning

Telling any untrue statement and successfully misleading someone
are not identical descriptions. だます focuses on deception of
the person. The soothing use must be read in context; do not
translate an accusation of fraud merely as friendly persuasion.
