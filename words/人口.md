# 人口 (じんこう) — population

## Meaning

人口 is the number of people living in a place: 日本の人口は約一億二千万人です (Japan's population is about 120 million), 人口が増える／減る (the population
grows or shrinks), 人口密度 じんこうみつど (population density).

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) (person) plus [口](../kanji/口.md) (mouth): people counted as "mouths", the same way a household is counted by mouths to feed.
So 人口 is not "person's mouth" but "people, by head count". In the idiom 人口に膾炙する じんこうにかいしゃする
("to be on everyone's lips"), 人口 still has its literal sense of people's mouths.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 人数 にんずう (the number of people in a group, e.g. 参加人数), 総人口 そうじんこう (total population).
- Contrast: 過密 かみつ (overcrowded) vs 過疎 かそ (depopulated), both of a region's density.
- No true antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

人口 is for the population of a defined area, as a statistic. 人数 is how many people are in a particular group or at an event.
