# 月末 (げつまつ) - end of the month

## Meaning

月末 is the end of a month. Depending on the expression, it can concern the final day or the closing part of the month.
月末までに sets a deadline: by the end of the month.

- 月末までに家賃を払います。 (げつまつまでにやちんをはらいます。) - I will pay the rent by the end of the month.
- 月末は仕事が忙しいです。 (げつまつはしごとがいそがしいです。) - Work is busy at the end of the month.

## How the kanji combine

[月](../kanji/月.md) identifies the month; [末](../kanji/末.md) identifies its end.
The second element is the head of the compound: the end of a month.
The on readings combine as ゲツ + マツ, without changing つ to small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下旬](下旬.md) (げじゅん) precisely covers the 21st through the last day; 月末 does not define that whole interval.
[末](末.md) (すえ) can refer more generally to an end.
月初 (げっしょ), the beginning of the month, supplies the temporal contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

月末 does not itself mean the end of this particular month: the surrounding date or conversation supplies which month.
The dictionary also records つきずえ for 月末; this card's primary reading is the source list's げつまつ.
Do not read the compound as げっまつ by analogy with 月給.
