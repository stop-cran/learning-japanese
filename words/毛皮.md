# 毛皮 (けがわ) - fur; animal pelt

## Meaning

毛皮 is an animal's fur or a pelt, including fur-bearing skin used as a material.
It can refer to the covering or to that material in an article of clothing.

- このコートは毛皮でできています。 (このコートはけがわでできています。) - This coat is made of fur.
- 動物の毛皮に触れました。 (どうぶつのけがわにふれました。) - I touched the animal's fur.

The material sense includes the hair-bearing skin, not just a loose individual hair.

## How the kanji combine

[毛](../kanji/毛.md), "hair; fur," modifies [皮](../kanji/皮.md), "skin; hide."
The native readings are け + かわ, with the second part voiced to がわ: けがわ.
The dictionary also records もうひ for 毛皮.
The rare alternative spelling 毛革, with [革](../kanji/革.md), accepts けがわ, but the recorded もうひ reading is restricted to 毛皮.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[毛](毛.md) (け) is broader for hair or fur.
[皮](皮.md) (かわ) is broader for skin, hide, and other coverings.
[革](革.md) (かわ) refers to processed leather; it is not freely interchangeable with a fur-bearing pelt.
[毛糸](毛糸.md) (けいと) is woollen yarn, not a pelt.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose 毛皮 when fur or the hair-bearing animal skin is relevant.
It is not the ordinary term for a person's bare skin, and its meaning does not extend to every peel or outer covering named 皮.
The shared material vocabulary should not obscure what form the material takes.
