# 音楽 (おんがく) - music

## Meaning

音楽 is music as an art, activity, or something to listen to. It is a noun, not a verb meaning "play an instrument."

- 毎日、音楽を聞きます。 (まいにち、おんがくをききます。) - I listen to music every day.
- どんな音楽が好きですか。 (どんなおんがくがすきですか。) - What kind of music do you like?

Use を with 聞く, but が with the adjective 好き in the basic preference pattern.

## How the kanji combine

[音](../kanji/音.md) contributes "sound," and [楽](../kanji/楽.md) has a music-related reading がく here. Do not read the second character らく in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

歌 (うた) means a song or singing, while 音 (おと) is sound more generally. Music can be instrumental, so 音楽 is not limited to songs with words.

## Distinctive meaning

音楽 names the broad category. To say what someone performs, use a suitable expression such as ピアノを弾く (ピアノをひく), "play the piano," rather than treating 音楽 as the name of an instrument.
