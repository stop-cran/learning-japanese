# 榛 (はしばみ) - Asian hazel shrub

## Meaning

榛 (はしばみ) names **Asian hazel**, a deciduous shrub. The pinned dictionary identifies *Corylus heterophylla var. thunbergii*. This article teaches the plant noun, not a general word for every nut.

- 榛の葉は丸い形をしています。 (はしばみのははまるいかたちをしています。) - Hazel leaves have a rounded shape.
- 榛の実は食べられます。 (はしばみのみはたべられます。) - Hazel nuts are edible.

The first example describes the overall leaf shape, not a perfectly smooth edge. In the second, [実](実.md) (み) identifies the plant's nuts, and 食べられます means "can be eaten" here. This botanical description is not advice to identify or eat an unknown wild plant.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [榛](../kanji/榛.md) writes the whole native noun はしばみ. There is no compound to divide. The dictionary marks this plant name as usually written in kana; はしばみ and ハシバミ are spellings to recognize, not different species determined by the script.

西洋榛 (せいようはしばみ), Eurasian hazel, is a separately named related species, *Corylus avellana*. Do not infer that every plant name ending in 榛 identifies the same plant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[植物](植物.md) (しょくぶつ), plant or vegetation, and [木](木.md) (き), tree or woody plant, are broader terms. [栗](栗.md) (くり), chestnut, is another nut-bearing plant, not a synonym for hazel.

榛の木 (はんのき) names Japanese alder, *Alnus japonica*. The archaic single-character 榛 (はり) also names alder; it is a different lexical identity from this article's はしばみ. There is no fixed direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling 榛 alone does not settle which plant an older text names. This article and its reading question use はしばみ; read はり as the old alder name when that reading is specified.

ヘーゼルナッツ means the nut. 榛の実 (はしばみのみ) can likewise name hazelnuts, but the dictionary marks that expression as a rare term. The plant noun and a nut sold as food are related referents, not unrestricted replacements for each other.
