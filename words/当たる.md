# 当たる (あたる) — hit; make contact

## Meaning

当たる describes hitting or striking in the contact sense taught here.
It is intransitive in this sense: the thing that hits is the subject, and に marks what it hits.

- ボールが壁に当たりました。 (ボールがかべにあたりました。) — The ball hit the wall.
- 石が窓に当たりました。 (いしがまどにあたりました。) — A stone hit the window.

Forms include 当たります (あたります), 当たらない (あたらない), and 当たって (あたって).

## How the kanji combine

[当](../kanji/当.md) contributes hitting or reaching a target and is read あ in this verb.
The written ending たる completes the native Japanese word.
The source notation あ.たる marks where the kanji reading ends and the following kana begin; the dot is not pronounced.

## Synonyms and antonyms

当てる (あてる) is the transitive counterpart for causing contact:
ボールを壁に当てました。 (ボールをかべにあてました。) — I made the ball hit the wall.
The ball now takes を because a person is directing it.

[打つ](打つ.md) (うつ), "hit; strike," is also transitive in its basic hitting sense.
Do not exchange these verbs while leaving the particles unchanged.

## Distinctive meaning

English "hit" can describe either the ball's collision or a person's action.
当たる in these examples reports the collision without making someone the agent who causes it.
It does not by itself say whether that contact was accidental; intention is a separate question.
