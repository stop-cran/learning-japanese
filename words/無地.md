# 無地 (むじ) -- plain; patternless

## Meaning

無地 means plain or patternless, for example fabric or paper without
a printed design. With colors it can mean solid-colored. It is a noun
used with の before another noun.

- 無地のシャツを買いました。 (むじのシャツをかいました。) -- I bought a plain shirt.
- この布は無地です。 (このぬのはむじです。) -- This fabric has no pattern.

Learn 無地のシャツ, not an automatically substituted 無地なシャツ.

## How the kanji combine

無 contributes absence, while [地](../kanji/地.md) can concern the
ground or base material. Together they help recall a surface without
a pattern. The compound uses ム + ジ, not the 地 reading チ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [地味](地味.md) (じみ) means plain or unshowy. A 地味な garment may still have a subdued pattern; a vivid solid color can be 無地.
- [模様](模様.md) (もよう) and [柄](柄.md) (がら) concern patterns or designs, rather than their absence.
- [単純](単純.md) (たんじゅん) means simple or uncomplicated; a simple repeated pattern is not necessarily 無地.

## Distinctive meaning

無地 does not mean white, colorless, boring or inexpensive. Pattern
and color are separate questions. English "plain" can also mean
ordinary, unshowy or a geographic plain; those English meanings do
not make every corresponding Japanese word a synonym for 無地.
