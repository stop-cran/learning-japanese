# 委員 (いいん) - committee member

## Meaning

委員 is a committee member: a person entrusted with a role in a committee or similar organized group. It is a noun, not the name of the whole committee. Useful patterns are **委員になる**, "become a committee member," and **委員を選ぶ** (いいんをえらぶ), "choose committee members."

- 新しい委員を選びます。 (あたらしいいいんをえらびます。) - We will choose new committee members.
- 私は図書委員です。 (わたしはとしょいいんです。) - I am a library committee member.

The second example can describe a school role involving the library. The noun does not by itself imply a paid position.

## How the kanji combine

委 contributes entrusting a task; [員](../kanji/員.md) identifies a member or person in a role. Together they name someone entrusted with committee duties. The on readings are イ + イン: retain both い sounds in いいん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会員](会員.md) (かいいん) is a member of an association or club; membership alone does not make someone a 委員. [議員](議員.md) (ぎいん) is a member of an assembly, such as a legislature.

## Distinctive meaning

委員会 (いいんかい) is the committee, while 委員 is a person serving on it. 委員長 (いいんちょう) adds the chairperson's role. These related nouns distinguish the organization, its members, and its chair rather than degrees of politeness.
