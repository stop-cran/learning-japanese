# 矛盾 (むじゅん) -- contradiction; inconsistency

## Meaning

矛盾 is a contradiction or inconsistency: claims, facts or actions do
not fit together. As an intransitive suru verb, 矛盾する means to
contradict or be inconsistent with something.

- 二つの説明は矛盾しています。 (ふたつのせつめいはむじゅんしています。) -- The two explanations contradict each other.
- 彼の発言は事実と矛盾しています。 (かれのはつげんはじじつとむじゅんしています。) -- His statement is inconsistent with the facts.

Use AがBと矛盾する for A being inconsistent with B. Forms include
矛盾しない, 矛盾して and 矛盾した (むじゅんしない・むじゅんして・むじゅんした).

## How the kanji combine

矛 means spear and 盾 shield; the readings here are ム + ジュン.
The dictionary traces the expression to a story in 韓非子 (かんぴし):
a seller praises a spear that pierces anything and a shield that nothing
can pierce, then cannot answer what would happen if the spear struck
that shield. This is an attributed explanatory story, not a report of
an actual tested weapon.

矛楯 is a recorded rare spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [相違](相違.md) (そうい) and [違い](違い.md) (ちがい) concern differences or discrepancies; not every difference is contradictory.
- [対立](対立.md) (たいりつ) and [衝突](衝突.md) (しょうとつ) include opposition or conflict, not necessarily a logical inconsistency.
- [一致](一致.md) (いっち) concerns agreement or correspondence.

## Distinctive meaning

Two opinions may differ without being mutually incompatible. 矛盾
points to their failure to fit together, not merely different tastes,
a quarrel between people or any isolated mistake. It is also not a
transitive template *Bを矛盾する for refuting an opponent.
