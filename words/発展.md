# 発展 (はってん) - development; expansion

## Meaning

発展 is development, growth, or expansion. With する it is intransitive: a town, industry, or situation develops. Xに発展する means developing into X, which need not be a positive outcome.

- この町は観光地として発展しました。 (このまちはかんこうちとしてはってんしました。) - This town developed as a tourist destination.
- 小さな意見の違いが大きな問題に発展しました。 (ちいさないけんのちがいがおおきなもんだいにはってんしました。) - A small difference of opinion developed into a major problem.

発展させる (はってんさせる) means causing something to develop or expanding it.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) contributes starting or coming forth, and 展 contributes unfolding or spreading. Together they describe an unfolding development. The sound changes to はってん, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[発達](発達.md) (はったつ) overlaps in development, often concerning growing capacities or developed functions. [成長](成長.md) (せいちょう) concerns growth. [増加](増加.md) (ぞうか) specifically names an increase in quantity; development can involve more than numbers.

## Distinctive meaning

発展 can describe broad progress, but "develop into" also covers escalation into a problem. Avoid reading praise into every occurrence. In 経済の発展 (けいざいのはってん), "economic development," the noun names the process; evaluation of its effects requires further context.
