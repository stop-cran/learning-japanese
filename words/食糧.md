# 食糧 (しょくりょう) - food supply; provisions

## Meaning

食糧 names food considered as provisions or a supply needed to sustain people, especially staple foods such as rice or wheat. It is useful in discussions of shortages, storage, and distribution.

- 災害に備えて食糧を保存しています。 (さいがいにそなえてしょくりょうをほぞんしています。) - We store food in preparation for disasters.
- 食糧が不足しています。 (しょくりょうがふそくしています。) - Food supplies are running short.

食糧不足 (しょくりょうぶそく) is a food shortage. The noun itself does not specify a military ration, an individual meal, or a particular package size.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) concerns eating or food, and 糧 means provisions or sustenance. Their related meanings form a supply-focused food word, read ショク + リョウ. The long vowel in りょう is part of the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[食料](食料.md) has the same reading and means food or foodstuffs broadly. The distinction is a tendency, not a rule that every item must fit exactly one spelling. [食品](食品.md) (しょくひん) is useful for food products or items.

## Distinctive meaning

食糧 directs attention to feeding people and having enough supplies. It is therefore useful when discussing quantities and availability, rather than describing how delicious a particular dish tastes. There is no ordinary direct antonym.
