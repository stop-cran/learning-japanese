# 味わう (あじわう) - savor; experience

## Meaning

味わう means tasting or savoring something, appreciating it, or experiencing a feeling
or event. It is a transitive godan verb ending in う; the object takes を.
Forms include 味わいます (あじわいます), 味わって (あじわって), and 味わった (あじわった).

- ゆっくり料理を味わいました。 (ゆっくりりょうりをあじわいました。) - I slowly savored the food.
- 初めて大きな失敗を味わいました。 (はじめておおきなしっぱいをあじわいました。) - I experienced a major failure for the first time.

It also applies to appreciating things such as music: 音楽を味わう (おんがくをあじわう),
"savor or appreciate music."

## How the kanji combine

[味](../kanji/味.md) supplies the taste association through あじ, followed by わう.
The established verb extends beyond the mouth to appreciation and lived experience;
it is not a two-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[楽しむ](楽しむ.md) (たのしむ) overlaps in enjoying something.
[経験](経験.md) (けいけん)する also means experiencing something, without necessarily
emphasizing its felt quality. [味](味.md) (あじ) is the noun "taste; flavor."

## Distinctive meaning

Do not force the pleasant English word "savor" into every context.
苦しみを味わう (くるしみをあじわう), "experience suffering," is not enjoying pain.
The object determines whether the sentence concerns food, appreciation, or undergoing an experience.
