# 東 (ひがし) — east

## Meaning

東 is the compass direction east. It is a noun: use 東の before another noun, 東に for a location, and 東へ for movement toward the east.

学校は駅の東にあります。 (がっこうはえきのひがしにあります。) — The school is east of the station.

## How the kanji combine

[東](../kanji/東.md) carries the native reading ひがし. Its on reading とう appears in 東京 (とうきょう), "Tokyo." Do not substitute that compound reading for the standalone direction in the example.

## Synonyms and antonyms

西 (にし), "west," is the opposite direction. 北 (きた), "north," and 南 (みなみ), "south," complete the four main compass points. 東側 (ひがしがわ) specifically means the east side.

## Distinctive meaning

東 is not the same as "right": which way is right depends on which direction you face. Use a landmark plus の東 to make a relative geographical position clear, as in 駅の東.
