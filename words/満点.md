# 満点 (まんてん) - full marks; perfect score

## Meaning

満点 is the maximum possible score: full marks.

- 試験で満点を取りました。 (しけんでまんてんをとりました。) - I got full marks on the exam.
- このテストは五十点満点です。 (このテストはごじゅってんまんてんです。) - This test is marked out of fifty.

It also expresses excellence or complete satisfaction: 栄養満点の食事 (えいようまんてんのしょくじ), "a very nutritious meal."

## How the kanji combine

[満](../kanji/満.md), "full," modifies [点](../kanji/点.md), "points." Read the on compound マン + テン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[点数](点数.md) (てんすう) is a score, not necessarily the maximum. [完全](完全.md) (かんぜん), "complete; perfect," overlaps with the extended quality sense, but does not itself specify an exam score.

## Distinctive meaning

満点 is not always one hundred points: the scoring scale determines the maximum. The extended sense also occurs outside numerical grading, so 栄養満点 is not a claim about a literal nutrition test.
