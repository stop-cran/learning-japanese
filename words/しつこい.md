# しつこい - insistent; overly rich

## Meaning

しつこい describes persistent, insistent, or tenacious behavior, often
felt to be excessive or bothersome.
It also describes an overly rich, heavy, or greasy taste.
It is an i-adjective: しつこい質問 (しつこいしつもん),
"a persistent question"; しつこくない, "not persistent/overly rich."

- しつこく聞かれて、困りました。 (しつこくきかれて、こまりました。) - I was troubled by being asked insistently.
- このソースは少ししつこいです。 (このソースはすこししつこいです。) - This sauce is a little too rich.

しつこく is the adverbial form and しつこかった the past.
The first sentence describes the manner of asking, not an exact question count.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. JMdict also lists 執拗い, containing characters associated
with holding fast and stubbornness, but the primary kana word has no written
kanji components to assign.
The alternate reading **しつっこい** is marked kana-only;
do not automatically attach it to 執拗い.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くどい](くどい.md) overlaps in tiresome insistence and excessive flavor.
[こってり](こってり.md) describes richness or heaviness that can be welcome;
しつこい more readily presents it as too much.
[あっさり](あっさり.md) and [さっぱり](さっぱり.md) can contrast
in lighter, refreshing food.

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen also describes overstrong or lingering color and smell.
The word is not confined to people, and persistence does not always mean
someone repeated the same words.
Likewise, the food use is an evaluation of flavor or heaviness, not a
measurement of physical weight.
