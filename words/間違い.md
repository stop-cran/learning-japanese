# 間違い (まちがい) - mistake; error

## Meaning

間違い is a noun meaning "mistake" or "error." Use 間違いがある
for containing a mistake, and 間違いを直す for correcting one.
間違いです identifies something as wrong.

- この文には間違いがあります。 (このぶんにはまちがいがあります。) - There is a mistake in this sentence.
- 名前に間違いがないか、確認してください。 (なまえにまちがいがないか、かくにんしてください。) - Please check whether the name is correct.

The second example literally asks whether there is no mistake in
the name. 間違いない can also express certainty: "there is
no doubt" or "it is definitely so."

## How the kanji combine

[間](../kanji/間.md) contributes an interval or relation, and
[違](../kanji/違.md) contributes difference or deviation.
間違い is the noun associated with 間違う (まちがう).
The established word means an error; "wrong interval" is not a
literal definition or a verified historical explanation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[誤り](誤り.md) (あやまり) also names an error.
[間違える](間違える.md) (まちがえる) is a verb for making
a mistake or confusing things. [正しい](正しい.md)
(ただしい), "correct," supplies a contrasting evaluation but is
an adjective, not a noun synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun does not identify the cause or seriousness of an error.
A typo and a mistaken decision can both be 間違い.
Specify the object or action when that distinction matters, rather
than assuming every mistake implies deliberate misconduct.
