# 友情 (ゆうじょう) - friendship

## Meaning

友情 is a noun for friendship, fellowship, or the affectionate bond between friends. It names the relationship or feeling, not one of the people.

- 二人の友情は今も続いています。 (ふたりのゆうじょうはいまもつづいています。) - The friendship between the two of them continues to this day.
- 私は彼との友情を大切にしています。 (わたしはかれとのゆうじょうをたいせつにしています。) - I value my friendship with him.

Nとの友情 means "friendship with N." The と links the other person to the relationship; の connects that phrase to 友情. 友情を深める (ゆうじょうをふかめる) means "deepen a friendship."

## How the kanji combine

[友](../kanji/友.md) contributes friend, and [情](../kanji/情.md) contributes feeling or affection. The first specifies the kind of feeling: affection between friends. The on readings are ユウ + ジョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[友達](友達.md) (ともだち) and [友人](友人.md) (ゆうじん) name a friend, not friendship itself. [仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) identifies a companion or member of a group; sharing a group does not automatically establish a close friendship.

## Distinctive meaning

友情 can be discussed without describing romance. The noun focuses on the bond, so 友情があります describes the presence of friendship, while 友達がいます means having a friend or friends. Do not choose between these merely by translating both loosely as "friendly relations."
