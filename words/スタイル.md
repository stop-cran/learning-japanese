# スタイル (スタイル) - style; figure

## Meaning

スタイル is a noun meaning style, manner, or form. It can also mean someone's figure, build, or physique. The surrounding noun or adjective determines which sense is intended.

- 自分に合う仕事のスタイルを探しています。 (じぶんにあうしごとのすたいるをさがしています。) - I am looking for a style of working that suits me.
- 彼女はスタイルがいいです。 (かのじょはすたいるがいいです。) - She has a good figure.

The second expression evaluates body shape; it does not merely say that her clothing is fashionable. Be considerate about whether commenting on someone's body is welcome.

## How the kanji combine

スタイル is written in katakana and has no kanji components. Its reading is ス・タ・イ・ル. Unlike words with ー, this spelling has no long-vowel mark; do not replace アイ with an invented prolonged syllable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[形式](形式.md) (けいしき) overlaps with form or format. [方法](方法.md) (ほうほう) concerns a method, while スタイル can emphasize an individual or characteristic manner.

[姿](姿.md) (すがた) concerns appearance or form but is not an exact replacement for every body-shape use.

## Distinctive meaning

スタイルの違い (すたいるのちがい), "a difference in style," need not be a judgment that one option is better. By contrast, スタイルがいい explicitly evaluates someone's figure. Do not let the familiar-looking word obscure this established Japanese meaning. There is no single antonym covering both senses.
