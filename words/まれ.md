# まれ (まれ) - rare; seldom

## Meaning

まれ describes something rare or an event that seldom happens. It is a na-adjective: まれな before a noun, まれだ as a predicate, and まれに for "rarely."

- これはまれな例です。 (これはまれなれいです。) - This is a rare example.
- この地方では、雪が降ることはまれです。 (このちほうでは、ゆきがふることはまれです。) - It seldom snows in this region.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 稀 and [希](../kanji/希.md) are recorded kanji spellings, including the source-list form 稀. These spellings do not change the reading まれ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[珍しい](珍しい.md) (めずらしい) overlaps in rarity or unusualness. [たまに](たまに.md), "occasionally," and [滅多に](滅多に.md) (めったに), normally with a negative predicate, describe infrequency with different constructions.

## Distinctive meaning

まれ does not assign a numerical probability. まれに起こる (まれにおこる), "happen rarely," has positive 起こる; do not add a negative merely because English "seldom" is negative in meaning.
