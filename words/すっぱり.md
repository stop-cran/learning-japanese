# すっぱり — decisively; cleanly and completely

## Meaning

すっぱり describes ending, quitting, or cutting something cleanly and decisively.

- 迷いをすっぱり捨て、留学を決めた。
  — I let go of my hesitation decisively and chose to study abroad.
- 彼は無理な依頼をすっぱり断った。
  — He flatly refused the unreasonable request.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すっぱり emphasizes a clean, complete break or a firm decision. きっぱり also describes a clear,
decisive response, often emphasizing an unambiguous manner.

When something is cut off, [ばっさり](ばっさり.md) can also fit: it draws attention to the
boldness of the cut or how much comes off, while すっぱり draws attention to a clean break.
That overlap in cutting does not make ばっさり a general substitute for “decisively.”

## Distinctive meaning

The word can also describe a clean cut made in one stroke. These examples use its decisive
ending/refusal sense.
