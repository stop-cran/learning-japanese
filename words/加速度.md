# 加速度 (かそくど) - acceleration

## Meaning

加速度 is acceleration: how velocity changes over time. In physics, velocity includes direction, so acceleration can exist even when the numerical speed stays the same.

- この実験では、車の加速度を測ります。 (このじっけんでは、くるまのかそくどをはかります。) - In this experiment, we measure the car's acceleration.
- 加速度の単位はメートル毎秒毎秒です。 (かそくどのたんいはメートルまいびょうまいびょうです。) - The unit of acceleration is metres per second per second.

The unit is commonly written m/s^2. Acceleration is not the same quantity as the car's current speed.

## How the kanji combine

[加](../kanji/加.md) adds, [速](../kanji/速.md) concerns speed, and [度](../kanji/度.md) supplies degree or measure. The compound builds on [加速](加速.md), adding 度 to name the measure of acceleration. The on readings are カ + ソク + ド.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[速度](速度.md) (そくど) is speed or velocity, not the rate at which velocity changes. 加速 can name accelerating as a process and also translates as "acceleration"; the English labels alone do not separate the two nouns.

## Distinctive meaning

The characters are a learning aid, not a substitute for the physical definition. A change of direction changes velocity too. For ordinary driving, "speeding up" is a useful first example, but not an exhaustive account of 加速度.
