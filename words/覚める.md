# 覚める (さめる) - wake up; regain alertness

## Meaning

覚める means to wake up or come out of a state such as sleep or intoxication. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 覚めます, 覚めた, 覚めない. A central expression is 目が覚める (めがさめる), "wake up."

- 朝六時に目が覚めました。 (あさろくじにめがさめました。) - I woke up at six in the morning.
- 大きな音で目が覚めました。 (おおきなおとでめがさめました。) - A loud noise woke me up.

In the second sentence, で marks the cause. Japanese still uses the intransitive expression 目が覚める even though the English translation has "woke me."

## How the kanji combine

[覚](../kanji/覚.md) concerns awareness here. It is read さ, followed by める. The same character in 覚える (おぼえる), "remember; learn," represents a different verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[起きる](起きる.md) (おきる) can mean waking or getting up. 目が覚める focuses on becoming awake, not necessarily leaving bed. [覚ます](覚ます.md) (さます) is the transitive partner: 目を覚ます.

## Distinctive meaning

酔いが覚める (よいがさめる) means intoxication wears off or someone sobers up. 冷める, also さめる, concerns cooling down and is a different word. Identical kana do not make the spellings interchangeable for sleep and temperature.
