# 谷 (たに) - valley; ravine

## Meaning

谷 is a valley or ravine: lower ground between hills or mountains. It is a noun, with の connecting it to another noun, as in 谷の底 (たにのそこ), "the bottom of the valley."

- 谷の底を川が流れています。 (たにのそこをかわがながれています。) - A river runs along the bottom of the valley.
- 道は深い谷に沿って続いています。 (みちはふかいたににそってつづいています。) - The road continues along a deep valley.

In the first example, を marks the route the river follows, not an object that it acts on.

## How the kanji combine

谷 represents the whole native noun たに. It is a single character, not a compound. The spelling is also found inside place names, where readings may differ; knowing this standalone word does not settle every name's pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

谷間 (たにま) also describes a valley or space between mountains. [山](山.md) (やま), "mountain," provides a topographical contrast: 谷 names the low area between rises, not the summit. These are related landforms rather than grammatical opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

The English translation depends on the landscape. A narrow, steep-sided 谷 may be a ravine or gorge, while a wider one is a valley. The Japanese word alone does not specify an exact width or depth, and a river need not be mentioned whenever 谷 is used.
