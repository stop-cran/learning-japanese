# おどおど — timidly and hesitantly

## Meaning

おどおど describes timid, nervous, hesitant behavior, often visible in someone's speech, gaze, or manner.

- 初めての面接で、おどおどしてしまった。 — I ended up acting nervously at my first interview. (面接: めんせつ.)
- 少年はおどおどと名前を答えた。 — The boy hesitantly gave his name.

Use おどおどする for acting timidly, or おどおどと before a suitable verb. おどおどした態度 (たいど) means "a timid manner"; した links the description to the noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[びくびく](びくびく.md) highlights fear or apprehensive trembling. おどおど highlights the hesitant behavior others can notice. [しっかり](しっかり.md) can contrast when it describes composure, but its other senses, such as fastening firmly, are unrelated.

## Distinctive meaning

Someone can feel nervous without visibly acting おどおど. The word can sound judgmental or belittling, especially when describing a person's character. It is not a neutral synonym for politeness, modesty, or careful speech.
