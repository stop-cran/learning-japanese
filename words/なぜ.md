# なぜ - why

## Meaning

なぜ asks for a reason: "why?" It modifies the following question without
needing a case particle such as を or に.

- なぜ日本語を勉強しているんですか。 (なぜにほんごをべんきょうしているんですか。) - Why are you studying Japanese?
- なぜ遅れたのか分かりません。 (なぜおくれたのかわかりません。) - I do not know why they were late.

The second sentence embeds the question with か rather than asking the listener
directly.

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is usual. The alternative spelling 何故 associates "what" and "reason,"
but なぜ is a whole-word reading, not regular readings added character by character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どうして also asks why and is useful in conversation. なんで is more casual
and, depending on pronunciation and context, can also be a question about means.
There is no direct antonym of a reason question.

## Distinctive meaning

なぜ does not itself accuse anyone, but a direct "why?" can sound challenging
depending on tone and situation. The explanatory んですか in the first example
requests background; it is not part of the word なぜ.
