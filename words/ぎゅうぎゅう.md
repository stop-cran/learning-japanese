# ぎゅうぎゅう — crammed tightly; squeezing hard

## Meaning

Tight packing or forceful squeezing, with little room left.

- かばんに服をぎゅうぎゅう詰（つ）めた。 — I crammed clothes tightly into the bag.
- 朝の電車は人でぎゅうぎゅうだった。 — The morning train was packed tightly with people.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぱんぱん](ぱんぱん.md) overlaps when a container is full to bursting. ぎゅうぎゅう foregrounds pressure and crowding; ぱんぱん often foregrounds fullness or bulging.

## Distinctive meaning

ぎゅうぎゅうに詰める is an action, while ぎゅうぎゅうだ describes the state. The word also has creaking and forceful-pressure uses. It does not automatically mean that an object actually bursts.
