# 生産 (せいさん) — production; manufacture

## Meaning

生産 names production or manufacture. Add する for the action "produce," and mark what is produced with を.
It can describe agricultural output as well as manufactured goods.

- この工場では車を生産しています。 (このこうじょうではくるまをせいさんしています。) — This factory produces cars.
- この地域では米を生産しています。 (このちいきではこめをせいさんしています。) — Rice is produced in this region.

The second sentence leaves the producers unstated; Japanese does not need an English-style passive to describe the region's activity.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) contributes bringing into being, and [産](../kanji/産.md) contributes producing or yielding.
The related meanings reinforce the idea of output. The readings are セイ + サン.
This compound is about production, not a literal translation of 生 as "life" followed by 産 as "childbirth."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作る](作る.md) つくる is a broader verb for making things, including preparing food.
消費 (しょうひ), "consumption," is a useful economic contrast: producing goods versus using them.
The words describe different activities, not necessarily different people.

## Distinctive meaning

生産 is useful when treating goods as output from a process. For making your own dinner, ご飯を作る (ごはんをつくる)
is the straightforward expression; 生産する would frame the activity as production.
Do not use 生産 for giving birth to a baby just because 産 also appears in childbirth vocabulary.
