# 車輪 (しゃりん) - vehicle wheel

## Meaning

車輪 is a wheel on a vehicle, such as a bicycle, car, or train. It names the wheel, not the entire vehicle.

- 自転車の車輪が回っています。
  (じてんしゃのしゃりんがまわっています。) - The bicycle's wheel is turning.
- 車輪に泥が付いています。
  (しゃりんにどろがついています。) - There is mud on the wheel.

車輪が回る (しゃりんがまわる) describes a wheel turning. The word does not specify whether it is a front or rear wheel.

## How the kanji combine

[車](../kanji/車.md) supplies the vehicle, and [輪](../kanji/輪.md) the wheel or ring. The vehicle modifies the wheel. Read シャ + リン as **しゃりん**, not くるまわ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[輪](輪.md) (わ) also has a wheel sense, alongside rings, loops, and circles. [車](車.md) (くるま) normally identifies a vehicle in beginner examples, but its recorded broader meanings include a wheel. These overlaps do not make either word a universal replacement for 車輪.

タイヤ is a tire, the part around a wheel's rim in relevant vehicles; it is not identical to the entire wheel. There is no useful general antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not limit 車輪 to private motorcars merely because the source list says "(car) wheel." Bicycle and railway wheels also fit. Conversely, a circular ornament or finger ring is not a 車輪 simply because 輪 appears in its name.
