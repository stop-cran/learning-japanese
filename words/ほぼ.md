# ほぼ - almost; approximately

## Meaning

ほぼ is an adverb meaning "almost," "nearly," or "approximately." It describes something close to completion, a quantity, or a stated condition without claiming exact identity.

- 準備はほぼ終わりました。 (じゅんびはほぼおわりました。) - The preparations are almost finished.
- 二つの箱はほぼ同じ大きさです。 (ふたつのはこはほぼおなじおおきさです。) - The two boxes are approximately the same size.

In the first example, some work may remain. In the second, small differences in size are compatible with the description.

## How the kanji combine

ほぼ is usually written in hiragana. The dictionary records 略 and 粗 as rare kanji spellings for this adverb. They are spelling alternatives for the same inspected identity, not evidence that the everyday kana word has a different meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[約](約.md) (やく) is useful before numerical amounts, such as 約三十人, "about thirty people." ほぼ also modifies descriptions such as 同じ. [大体](大体.md) (だいたい) overlaps in rough approximation but has other uses as well.

## Distinctive meaning

ほぼ does not necessarily mean "slightly less than." Two things described as ほぼ同じ may differ in either direction. With completion, however, "almost finished" naturally suggests something remains. Interpret the approximation relative to the following expression rather than applying one mathematical boundary to all uses.
