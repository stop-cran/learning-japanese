# 自転車 (じてんしゃ) — bicycle

## Meaning

自転車 means a bicycle. Use に with the act of riding it and で when it is the means of transportation.

- 自転車に乗ります。（じてんしゃにのります。） — I ride a bicycle.
- 自転車で学校に行きます。（じてんしゃでがっこうにいきます。） — I go to school by bicycle.

## How the kanji combine

The readings are on+on+on: [自](../kanji/自.md) ジ, self; [転](../kanji/転.md) テン, turn;
and [車](../kanji/車.md) シャ, vehicle. 車 is the head, with the preceding characters identifying the kind of vehicle.
These meanings help recall the spelling, but the established word means "bicycle," not any vehicle whose wheels turn.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[自動車](自動車.md) じどうしゃ means "car; automobile," not bicycle.
バイク usually means a motorcycle in ordinary conversation, though it can refer to a bicycle in some compounds.
It is therefore not a safe general replacement for 自転車. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn じてんしゃ, with てん. JMdict records じでんしゃ as an irregular kana usage, not the form selected for this card.
Keep 自転車 separate from 自動車: the middle character changes both the pronunciation and the vehicle named.
