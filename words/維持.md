# 維持 (いじ) - maintenance; preservation

## Meaning

維持 names maintaining or preserving a condition. 維持する is transitive: the state, standard, or relationship kept in place takes を.

- 健康を維持するために毎日歩いています。 (けんこうをいじするためにまいにちあるいています。) - I walk every day to maintain my health.
- 今の生活を維持するのは難しいです。 (いまのせいかつをいじするのはむずかしいです。) - Maintaining my present way of life is difficult.

維持のために (いじのために) means "for the purpose of maintenance." In 現状維持 (げんじょういじ), the thing preserved is the current situation.

## How the kanji combine

維 contributes holding together or sustaining; [持](../kanji/持.md) contributes holding or keeping. Their related meanings reinforce the idea of keeping something in existence or in its present condition. The on readings are イ + ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[続ける](続ける.md) (つづける) focuses on continuing an action. 維持する focuses on what is kept at a certain level or in a certain state. [改善](改善.md) (かいぜん), "improvement," contrasts with simply preserving the present state, though maintenance and improvement can be combined.

## Distinctive meaning

維持 does not mean that nothing requires effort. A person may need to act continually to keep a standard unchanged. Nor does it necessarily mean repairing equipment: context may concern health, a relationship, or living conditions rather than physical maintenance work.
