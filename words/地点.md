# 地点 (ちてん) - geographical point; spot

## Meaning

地点 is a particular point or spot in a geographical space.
It is useful when identifying a location on a map, route, or measured
course. It is a noun.

- 地図に集合する地点を示しました。 (ちずにしゅうごうするちてんをしめしました。) - I marked the meeting point on the map.
- スタートから五キロの地点で休みました。 (スタートからごキロのちてんでやすみました。) - We rested at the point five kilometers from the start.

で marks where the rest happened. 五キロ is a distance here,
not the number of points.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) contributes land or place, and
[点](../kanji/点.md) a point. The combination specifies a point
in geographical space. The on readings are チ + テン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[場所](場所.md) (ばしょ) and [所](所.md) (ところ) are broader
place words. [位置](位置.md) (いち) names where something is
situated. 地点 particularly brings an identifiable spot into focus.

## Distinctive meaning

A 地点 need not be a visible ink dot or a place with a building.
It can be identified through distance or surrounding landmarks.
English "point" also covers a score or a point in an argument;
do not transfer all of those meanings from 点 into 地点.
