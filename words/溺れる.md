# 溺れる (おぼれる) - to be drowning; indulge excessively

## Meaning

溺れる is an intransitive ichidan verb for struggling in water and becoming unable to breathe.
It describes drowning as an event or predicament; it does **not** by itself establish that the person dies.
The person takes が, and the place can take で.

- 川で溺れていた子供は助かりました。 (かわでおぼれていたこどもはたすかりました。) - The child who was drowning in the river survived.
- 彼は酒に溺れてしまった。 (かれはさけにおぼれてしまった。) - He became consumed by drinking.

The figurative pattern is Nに溺れる: indulge excessively in something or lose oneself in it.
It is not a neutral description of enjoying a hobby.

## How the kanji combine

In the spelling 溺れる, 溺 is read おぼ and れる is written in kana.
Learn 溺れる as its own ichidan verb, not as a passive made by attaching れる to おぼる.
Conjugate it as 溺れます, 溺れた, and 溺れない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[沈む](沈む.md) (しずむ), "sink," concerns movement below a surface and can describe objects.
[泳ぐ](泳ぐ.md) (およぐ), "swim," describes an activity, not the dangerous loss of control expressed here.
Neither is an exact opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

English "drowned" can suggest a fatal result, so translate with care when rescue or survival
is part of the context. The figurative use belongs to the same lexical entry:
酒に溺れる evokes being overwhelmed by alcohol, rather than literally being underwater.
