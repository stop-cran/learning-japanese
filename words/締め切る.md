# 締め切る (しめきる) - close up; close applications

## Meaning

締め切る means to close something up completely or keep openings closed. It also means to stop accepting applications or submissions at a cutoff. It is a **transitive godan verb**, despite ending in る: 締め切ります, 締め切って, 締め切らない.

- 寒いので、窓を全部締め切りました。
  (さむいので、まどをぜんぶしめきりました。) - Because it was cold, I closed all the windows.
- 参加の申し込みは明日締め切ります。
  (さんかのもうしこみはあしたしめきります。) - We will close applications to participate tomorrow.

The object takes を. 申し込みを締め切った means that acceptance has closed, not that every applicant has completed a form.

## How the kanji combine

締め supplies closing, and [切](../kanji/切.md), read き, adds completion: close up or cut off further acceptance. This is a native compound verb, not a string of on readings.

**閉め切る**, with [閉](../kanji/閉.md), is another recorded spelling, particularly useful for recognizing the physical closing sense. The application cutoff is usually written 締め切る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[閉める](閉める.md) (しめる) is the broader "close something"; 締め切る emphasizes complete or sustained closure. [閉じる](閉じる.md) (とじる) also overlaps in closing. [閉まる](閉まる.md) (しまる) describes something closing rather than an agent closing it.

The noun [締め切り](締め切り.md) (しめきり) is the deadline or closing. Reopening acceptance is a contextual contrast, not a second meaning of 締め切る.

## Distinctive meaning

The source-list gloss "shut up" means closing up an opening or room here. It is **not** an instruction to make someone stop talking. Nor is 締め切る interchangeable with [締める](締める.md) (しめる), "fasten; tighten," in every belt or necktie example.
