# 噴水 (ふんすい) - fountain; water jet

## Meaning

噴水 is water spouting upward or outward, or the apparatus that produces that display:
an ornamental fountain, for example. It is a noun, not the usual name for a drinking-water tap.

- 公園の噴水の前で会いましょう。 (こうえんのふんすいのまえであいましょう。) - Let's meet in front of the fountain in the park.
- 噴水から水が高く上がっています。 (ふんすいからみずがたかくあがっています。) - Water is rising high from the fountain.

## How the kanji combine

噴 (フン) contributes spouting or jetting out; [水](../kanji/水.md) (スイ)
is water. The compound concerns spouting water and, by extension, its fountain apparatus.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[泉](泉.md) (いずみ) is a spring or source of water. English "fountain" can overlap,
but a natural spring need not shoot water into the air.
[噴火](噴火.md) (ふんか) shares the emission association but concerns a volcanic
eruption, not an alternative name for a decorative fountain.

## Distinctive meaning

A park's 噴水 is often ornamental. English "drinking fountain" instead needs the
appropriate drinking-water expression, such as 水飲み場 (みずのみば), according to the
installation. Neither the kanji 水 nor the English word "fountain" establishes that
water from a particular 噴水 is safe to drink.
