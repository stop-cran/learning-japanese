# ぐっすり - soundly asleep

## Meaning

ぐっすり is an adverb describing deep, sound sleep. Use it with a sleep verb, either directly or with と: ぐっすり眠る／ぐっすりと眠る (ぐっすりねむる), "sleep soundly."

- 昨夜はぐっすり眠れました。 (さくやはぐっすりねむれました。) - I was able to sleep soundly last night.
- 赤ちゃんはぐっすり寝ています。 (あかちゃんはぐっすりねています。) - The baby is fast asleep.

眠れました is the potential form, reporting that good sleep was possible. 寝ています describes the baby's present sleeping state. The adverb itself does not carry tense.

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana-written mimetic word, describing a state rather than imitating a literal noise. There are no kanji components or foreign source words to decode. The small っ is part of the word's pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[眠る](眠る.md) (ねむる) and [寝る](寝る.md) (ねる) provide the sleep action; ぐっすり adds its quality. うとうと describes dozing and offers a useful contrast, though it is not a grammatical negative of ぐっすり.

## Distinctive meaning

English "fast asleep" does not mean sleeping quickly. Likewise, ぐっすり concerns depth or soundness of sleep, not how soon someone falls asleep or the precise number of hours slept. Keep a sleep verb in the sentence rather than treating it as an ordinary na-adjective.
