# 文章 (ぶんしょう) — written text

## Meaning

文章 means writing, a passage, a composition, or a piece of prose. It can also mean a sentence, but its scope is not restricted to one grammatical sentence.

この文章を読んで、質問に答えてください。 (このぶんしょうをよんで、しつもんにこたえてください。) — Read this passage and answer the questions.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) relates to writing or sentences, and 章 to an organized passage or chapter. Their on readings combine as ブン + ショウ. Keep the long final vowel in ぶんしょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

文 (ぶん) is the more precise word when counting grammatical sentences. 作文 (さくぶん) means a composition or the activity of composing. 文字 (もじ) names written characters rather than the text they form.

## Distinctive meaning

文章が上手です (ぶんしょうがじょうずです) means someone's writing is skillful, not necessarily that their handwriting is neat. 文章 can concern both a text and its style; identify which is meant before translating it as simply "sentence."
