# 見送り (みおくり) - seeing off; postponement

## Meaning

見送り is a noun for seeing someone off or giving a send-off. In a different use, it means deferring, shelving, or deciding not to proceed with something for now.

- 友達の見送りに駅へ行きました。 (ともだちのみおくりにえきへいきました。) - I went to the station to see my friend off.
- 新しい機械の購入は見送りになりました。 (あたらしいきかいのこうにゅうはみおくりになりました。) - The purchase of the new machine was put on hold.

見送りに行く uses に for purpose. 見送りになる describes the decision or outcome of not proceeding. The corresponding verb is 見送る (みおくる).

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) contributes seeing, and [送](../kanji/送.md) contributes sending someone onward. 見送り is the noun formed from the native compound verb 見送る. The physical send-off image helps with the first use but does not mean that a postponed plan literally travels away.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[迎え](迎え.md) (むかえ), meeting someone on arrival, contrasts with a send-off. [延期](延期.md) (えんき) overlaps with postponement, while [中止](中止.md) (ちゅうし) means stopping or canceling. The exact consequences depend on the decision being described.

## Distinctive meaning

見送り does not inherently promise a new date: a proposal may be left unadopted rather than rescheduled. Conversely, a friend's 見送り is a departure-related action, not rejection of the friend. The noun that precedes 見送り and the surrounding predicate separate these meanings.
