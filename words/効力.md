# 効力 (こうりょく) - efficacy; validity; potency

## Meaning

効力 is the power to produce an effect, or the force or validity of
something such as an agreement. It is a noun.
効力を発揮する (こうりょくをはっきする) means to exercise or show
its effectiveness; 効力を失う (こうりょくをうしなう) means to lose
effect or validity.

- この契約は来月から効力を持ちます。 (このけいやくはらいげつからこうりょくをもちます。) - This contract takes effect next month.
- その規則は効力を失いました。 (そのきそくはこうりょくをうしないました。) - That rule has lost its force.

In a discussion of a substance or treatment, potency or efficacy may
be the relevant English rendering rather than legal validity.

## How the kanji combine

[効](../kanji/効.md) contributes efficacy, and
[力](../kanji/力.md) contributes power or capability.
Together they name the power to have an effect.
The on readings are コウ + リョク; keep the small ょ in りょく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[効果](効果.md) (こうか) names an effect or effectiveness.
[有効](有効.md) (ゆうこう) describes something as valid or effective;
[効く](効く.md) (きく) is the verb work effectively or take effect.
Their meanings overlap, but their constructions differ.

## Distinctive meaning

Validity is not physical strength, and effectiveness need not mean
success in every circumstance. A statement that a contract has 効力
does not explain its legal conditions or tell a reader whether a
particular real agreement is enforceable.
Use 効力がある or 効力を持つ, not an assumed 効力する verb.
