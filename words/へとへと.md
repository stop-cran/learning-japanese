# へとへと — completely exhausted

## Meaning

へとへと describes being thoroughly worn out, especially after sustained work or exertion.

- 一日中引っ越しを手伝って、へとへとになった。 — I became completely exhausted after helping with the move all day. (引っ越し: ひっこし.)
- 今日はへとへとだから、早く寝たい。 — I am worn out today, so I want to go to bed early.

Use へとへとだ for the state and へとへとになる for becoming exhausted. へとへとの体 (からだ) describes an exhausted body. This is adjective-like grammar, not a universal mimetic + する pattern.

## How the kanji combine

The repeated kana expression depicts physical depletion without imitating a necessary sound. There are no kanji components to explain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くたくた](くたくた.md) overlaps closely in exhaustion but can also describe worn-out material or softened food. [ぐったり](ぐったり.md) pictures a limp, listless state. [ぴんぴん](ぴんぴん.md), full of life, offers a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

へとへと is stronger and more conversational than a neutral report of slight tiredness. It does not mean merely bored or sleepy. Even when work caused the fatigue, the expression does not itself tell us whether the work was physical or mentally draining.
