# 水産 (すいさん) - aquatic products; fisheries

## Meaning

水産 concerns products obtained from water, such as fish, shellfish,
and seaweed, and the fisheries field. It is a noun.
The water can be a sea, river, or lake; "marine products" is too narrow
if understood as excluding freshwater products.

- 水産物を扱う会社で働いています。 (すいさんぶつをあつかうかいしゃではたらいています。) - I work for a company that handles aquatic products.
- 大学で水産について学んでいます。 (だいがくですいさんについてまなんでいます。) - I am studying fisheries at university.

水産物 (すいさんぶつ) explicitly names the products.
水産業 (すいさんぎょう) names the fisheries industry.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md), water, qualifies [産](../kanji/産.md), products
or production. The on readings are スイ + サン.
The compound concerns products from an aquatic setting, not everything
manufactured using any amount of water.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[漁業](漁業.md) (ぎょぎょう) focuses on the fishing industry, including
catching, gathering, or farming aquatic animals.
海産物 (かいさんぶつ) specifically names marine products.
[魚](魚.md) (さかな) names fish; 水産 is not limited to that one kind
of animal or product.

## Distinctive meaning

The second example presents 水産 as a field of study, not a plate of fish.
Likewise, fisheries need not mean only angling with a fishing rod.
Use the complete expression to distinguish the products, industry,
and study of the field.
