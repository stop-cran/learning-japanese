# 八日 (ようか) — the 8th of the month; eight days

## Meaning

八日 is a date or a duration: 八月八日 はちがつようか (August 8th), 八日間 ようかかん (eight days), 八日目 ようかめ (the eighth day).

## How the kanji combine

[八](../kanji/八.md) read よう plus the day counter か. This よう is a kun reading of 八 used for the 8th day. See [三日](三日.md) for the series.
It is not はちにち. The 18th is regular: 十八日 じゅうはちにち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 八つ やっつ (eight things), 八日 and 四日 よっか (4th) are easy to confuse (よう vs よっ).
- Contrast: [八月](八月.md) はちがつ (August).

## Distinctive meaning

よう (八日) and よっ (四日) differ by one sound, so take care when listening.
