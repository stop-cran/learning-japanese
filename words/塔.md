# 塔 (とう) - tower; pagoda

## Meaning

塔 is a noun meaning "tower," including structures such as a steeple
or spire. It can also name a Buddhist pagoda or stupa.
It appears both as a noun and as the final part of compounds.

- 丘の上に高い塔が見えます。 (おかのうえにたかいとうがみえます。) - A tall tower is visible on the hill.
- その寺には五重の塔があります。 (そのてらにはごじゅうのとうがあります。) - That temple has a five-story pagoda.

五重の塔 is read ごじゅうのとう. The surrounding setting tells
you whether a religious structure or another kind of tower is intended.

## How the kanji combine

塔 is a single character with the on reading トウ. In
時計塔 (とけいとう), "clock tower," the preceding 時計
specifies the tower's function. The single-word card has no
second kanji contributing another part of the meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

タワー is a loanword meaning "tower."
[建物](建物.md) (たてもの) is the broader word "building."
[寺](寺.md) (てら) names a Buddhist temple; a 塔 can be one
structure within its grounds rather than the whole temple.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every tall building is called a 塔. The word identifies a
type of structure, not merely a height measurement. Conversely, the
pagoda use does not mean that every 塔 has a religious function.
Learn compound names and the architectural context together.
