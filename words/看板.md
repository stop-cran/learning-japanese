# 看板 (かんばん) - signboard; shop's reputation

## Meaning

看板 is a signboard, such as a shop sign.
It can also refer to what attracts customers or represents a business,
and to a name or reputation presented to the public.

- 店の前に看板を出しました。
  (みせのまえにかんばんをだしました。) - We put a signboard in front of the shop.
- この店の看板料理はカレーです。
  (このみせのかんばんりょうりはカレーです。) - This restaurant's signature dish is curry.

In 看板料理, 看板 modifies the dish that represents or draws people
to the restaurant; it does not mean a dish made from a board.
The dictionary also records an outward show or appearance.

## How the kanji combine

看 contributes looking or watching, while
[板](../kanji/板.md) contributes a board.
Together they suggest a board for people to look at.
The established on-reading compound is かんばん, with ばん for 板 here.
Modern signs need not literally be wooden boards.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[掲示](掲示.md) (けいじ) is a notice or posting information.
[評判](評判.md) (ひょうばん) concerns reputation or what people say.
Those comparisons relate to different 看板 senses, not to every use
of a physical shop sign.
[サイン](サイン.md) can be a signature or signal; do not translate
every English "sign" as the same Japanese word.

## Distinctive meaning

看板 can also mean closing time or the end of business for the day.
もう看板です (もうかんばんです) means "We're closed for the day"
in an appropriate shop or restaurant setting; it is not a comment
that a sign is visible.

Distinguish an actual board, a business's public face, and this
closing-time expression from their context.
None of these meanings guarantees that the shop has a good reputation.
