# ぱさぱさ — dried-out and lacking moisture

## Meaning

Unpleasantly dry and lacking needed moisture, especially food or hair. Food may feel crumbly or difficult to swallow.

- このパンは古くて、ぱさぱさしている。 — This bread is stale and dry.
- 髪の毛がぱさぱさなので、トリートメントを使った。 — My hair was dry, so I used a conditioning treatment.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しっとり](しっとり.md) contrasts with agreeable moisture in cake or hair. [かさかさ](かさかさ.md) often emphasizes dry roughness of skin or a dry rustle. [からから](からから.md) is thorough dryness or emptiness.

## Distinctive meaning

ぱさぱさする, ぱさぱさした肉, and ぱさぱさの髪 are useful patterns. It is usually an undesirable dryness, unlike the pleasant crispness of [さくさく](さくさく.md). A food can lose moisture without becoming crunchy, so English “dry” and “crispy” should not be collapsed.
