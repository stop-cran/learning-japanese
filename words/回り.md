# 回り (まわり) - rotation; rounds; spreading

## Meaning

回り is a noun connected with going around: rotation, making rounds, or spreading.
It can describe how readily a wheel turns or how something circulates.
Place names followed by 回り can indicate a route "via" that place.

- この車輪は回りが悪いです。 (このしゃりんはまわりがわるいです。) - This wheel does not turn smoothly.
- 今日は酒の回りが早いです。 (きょうはさけのまわりがはやいです。) - The alcohol is taking effect quickly today.
- 大阪回りで帰ります。 (おおさかまわりでかえります。) - I will return via Osaka.

回りが悪い evaluates the turning, not the condition of the area surrounding the wheel.

## How the kanji combine

[回](../kanji/回.md) contributes turning or going around.
The noun まわり relates to [回る](回る.md) (まわる), "turn; go around."
The kana ending り helps identify the noun rather than the dictionary-form verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[周り](周り.md) (まわり), a different written identity, means circumference, surroundings, or people around someone:
家の周り (いえのまわり), "around the house."
回り concerns rotation, rounds, or circulation in the uses above.
回転 (かいてん) overlaps with physical rotation.

## Distinctive meaning

The same pronunciation does not make 回り and 周り interchangeable in every context.
The alcohol expression combines the ideas of spreading and effect; it is not about a drink's circumference.
Use the intended relationship, not the English word "around" alone, to select the spelling.
