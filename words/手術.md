# 手術 (しゅじゅつ) — surgical operation

## Meaning

手術 is a surgical operation or procedure. It can be a noun, or combine with する
to describe operating. For the patient's experience, 手術を受ける (しゅじゅつをうける),
"undergo an operation," is a useful clear pattern.

- 来週、手術を受けます。 (らいしゅう、しゅじゅつをうけます。) — I will have an operation next week.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md), "hand," qualifies [術](../kanji/術.md), "technique."
The established compound names surgery, not any skilled activity performed by hand.
Its on readings are シュ + ジュツ: しゅじゅつ. Keep both small ゅ characters when writing the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

治療 (ちりょう) is medical treatment or therapy. It is broader: discussing 治療 does not
by itself specify an operation. 手術 names the surgical procedure in particular.
There is no direct antonym needed to understand this distinction.

## Distinctive meaning

手術 is not a general translation of English "operation." A machine's operation or a business
operation is not a 手術. The medical context belongs to the Japanese word itself, while
受ける in the example makes clear that the speaker is receiving the procedure.
