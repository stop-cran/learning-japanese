# 会合 (かいごう) - meeting; gathering

## Meaning

会合 is a gathering or meeting of people. It can name the event, and 会合する
means to meet or assemble. Use 会合に出る (かいごうにでる), "attend a meeting,"
or 会合を開く (かいごうをひらく), "hold a meeting."

- 明日は町内の会合に出ます。 (あしたはちょうないのかいごうにでます。) - I will attend a neighborhood meeting tomorrow.
- 代表者が東京で会合しました。 (だいひょうしゃがとうきょうでかいごうしました。) - The representatives met in Tokyo.

In the verb example, が marks the people who gather and で marks the location.

## How the kanji combine

[会](../kanji/会.md) conveys meeting, while [合](../kanji/合.md) conveys
coming together. Their related meanings reinforce the idea of assembling.
The on readings are かい + ごう; this is not the native reading あう of 会う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) focuses on a meeting for discussion or deliberation.
[集まり](集まり.md) (あつまり) broadly names a gathering. Both overlap with
会合; an organized gathering can also be a discussion meeting.
Dispersing afterward is a contrasting action, not a competing noun for the event.

## Distinctive meaning

会合 names people coming together without itself specifying a conference agenda,
size, or degree of ceremony. It works for community groups as well as official
representatives. To describe merely running into a friend, [会う](会う.md)
(あう) or [出会う](出会う.md) (であう) can express the encounter without
presenting it as an organized gathering.
