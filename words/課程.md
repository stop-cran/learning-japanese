# 課程 (かてい) - curriculum; course of study

## Meaning

課程 is a curriculum or organized course of study. It can name a program at a particular educational stage, rather than one individual lesson.

- 大学の修士課程を修了しました。 (だいがくのしゅうしかていをしゅうりょうしました。) - I completed a university master's program.
- この課程では、法律の基礎を学びます。 (このかていでは、ほうりつのきそをまなびます。) - In this course of study, we learn the basics of law.

修士課程 (しゅうしかてい) is a master's program, and 博士課程 (はくしかてい) a doctoral program. 課程を修了する (かていをしゅうりょうする) means to complete a program.

## How the kanji combine

[課](../kanji/課.md) contributes lessons or assigned study, and [程](../kanji/程.md) a course or extent. Together they name a course of organized study. The on readings are カ + テイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[科目](科目.md) (かもく) is a subject or course counted within a curriculum. [授業](授業.md) (じゅぎょう) concerns teaching or a class session. [学科](学科.md) (がっか) can name a subject or university department. Educational uses overlap, but they do not always identify the same unit.

## Distinctive meaning

English "course" can mean several things. 課程 is not a route through town, and it is not simply every [過程](過程.md) (かてい), process. A 課程 can have a completion requirement; [仮定](仮定.md) (かてい), an assumption, is a different homophone.
