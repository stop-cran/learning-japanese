# 別れ (わかれ) - parting; separation

## Meaning

別れ is a parting, separation, or farewell between people. In a romantic context it can mean a breakup. It is a noun: 人との別れ (ひととのわかれ) names parting from a person.

- 空港で友達に別れを告げました。 (くうこうでともだちにわかれをつげました。) - I said goodbye to my friend at the airport.
- 恋人との別れはつらい経験でした。 (こいびととのわかれはつらいけいけんでした。) - Breaking up with my partner was a painful experience.

In 別れを告げる, "say farewell," に marks the person addressed and を marks the farewell conveyed.

## How the kanji combine

[別](../kanji/別.md) contributes separation or parting. The noun is related to [別れる](別れる.md) (わかれる), "part; separate," and retains its stem わかれ. It is not the on-reading noun 別 (べつ), "another; different," pronounced differently.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出会い](出会い.md) (であい), "an encounter; meeting," provides a useful contrast with parting. さようなら is something said when leaving; 別れ names the parting itself.

## Distinctive meaning

別れ need not end a relationship permanently: friends can part at an airport and meet again later. Context supplies the stronger meanings of a romantic breakup or a final farewell. お別れする (おわかれする) can mean saying goodbye, but the noun by itself does not say who initiated the separation.
