# 分布 (ぶんぷ) - distribution across an area

## Meaning

分布 is the distribution or spread of things across an area or range. It is a noun
and a する verb. A useful pattern is things が + area に + 分布する.
It also applies to statistical distributions, not only maps.

- この植物は山地に分布しています。 (このしょくぶつはさんちにぶんぷしています。) - This plant is distributed across mountainous areas.
- この地図は人口の分布を示しています。 (このちずはじんこうのぶんぷをしめしています。) - This map shows the distribution of the population.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) contributes division or dispersal; [布](../kanji/布.md)
contributes spreading, not cloth in this compound. Together they concern how things
are spread out. The word is ブン + プ; learn this pronunciation rather than ぶんふ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[広がる](広がる.md) (ひろがる) can describe spreading more generally.
[普及](普及.md) (ふきゅう) often concerns adoption or something becoming widespread.
配布 (はいふ) is handing out materials, while 分配 (ぶんぱい) is allocating shares.
Those are not the default meanings of the population-distribution example.

## Distinctive meaning

分布 describes a pattern of presence: where organisms occur or how observations fall
across numerical values. The usual descriptive pattern above is intransitive.
This practical pattern is not an absolute claim that every dictionary-recognized use
must exclude an agent spreading or arranging things.
