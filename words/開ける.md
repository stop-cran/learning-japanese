# 開ける (あける) — open something

## Meaning

開ける（あける）means **to open something**, such as a door, window, or box.
The object being opened is commonly marked with を.

- ドアを開けてください。（ドアをあけてください。）— Please open the door.
- 箱を開けました。（はこをあけました。）— I opened the box.

It is an ichidan verb: 開けます（あけます）, 開けない（あけない）, 開けて（あけて）.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native verb, not a compound. [開](../kanji/開.md) writes あ in this word, and ける is
the **okurigana**, the following kana that helps identify and inflect the verb.
The dictionary reading notation is あ.ける.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 開く（あく）is the intransitive counterpart: ドアが開きます（ドアがあきます）, "the door opens."
- 閉める（しめる）is the ordinary opposite for doors and windows: "close something."
- 開く（ひらく）also means "open" or "unfold" and is used in expressions such as 本を開く（ほんをひらく）, "open a book."

These are not freely interchangeable: あける describes acting on an object, whereas あく describes its opening.

## Distinctive meaning

This article selects the reading **あける**. The same spelling also represents **ひらける**, a different verb
used for such meanings as a view opening out or an area developing. Do not transfer those meanings or
their intransitive grammar to あける.

For an ordinary door, keep the basic contrast clear: 人がドアを開ける（ひとがドアをあける）,
"a person opens the door," versus ドアが開く（ドアがあく）, "the door opens."
