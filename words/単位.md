# 単位 (たんい) - unit; academic credit

## Meaning

単位 is a unit used for measurement or counting. It also names an academic credit earned toward completing a course or qualification.

- 長さの単位はメートルです。 (ながさのたんいはメートルです。) - The unit of length is the meter.
- この授業の単位を取りました。 (このじゅぎょうのたんいをとりました。) - I earned the credits for this class.

単位を取る means to earn credits; 単位を落とす (たんいをおとす) means to fail to obtain the credits for a class. These are not descriptions of physically picking up or dropping objects.

N単位で expresses grouping or increments: 一時間単位で予約する (いちじかんたんいでよやくする), "book in one-hour units."

## How the kanji combine

[単](../kanji/単.md) contributes a single unit, and [位](../kanji/位.md) contributes a position or level. Together they name what is treated as one unit. The on readings are タン + イ; pronounce the ん before い.

## Synonyms and antonyms

点数 (てんすう) is a score. A student's score and the credits earned are related but not the same quantity. [種類](種類.md) (しゅるい), "kind/type," classifies things instead of specifying the unit used to measure them.

## Distinctive meaning

An amount without its unit can be unclear. 単位は何ですか (たんいはなんですか), "What is the unit?", asks how to interpret the number, not necessarily to recalculate it. The same principle of an established unit underlies the academic-credit use.
