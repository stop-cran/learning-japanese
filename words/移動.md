# 移動 (いどう) - movement; relocation

## Meaning

移動 is movement from one position or place to another. It is a noun and a する verb. People or things can move, and someone can move an object: the surrounding particles show the roles.

- 次の教室に移動してください。 (つぎのきょうしつにいどうしてください。) - Please move to the next classroom.
- この机を窓のそばに移動しました。 (このつくえをまどのそばにいどうしました。) - I moved this desk beside the window.

に marks the destination; を marks the desk being moved in the second example. 移動中 (いどうちゅう) means "on the move; in transit."

## How the kanji combine

移 contributes "shift; transfer," and [動](../kanji/動.md) means "move." These related components together emphasize a change of position. The compound does not specify a vehicle or a distance.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動く](動く.md) (うごく) covers movement more broadly, including a small motion without going to another location. [引っ越す](引っ越す.md) (ひっこす) specifically describes moving home or premises. Moving between classrooms is 移動, not normally a change of residence.

## Distinctive meaning

移動 is useful for routes, schedules, equipment, and groups changing locations. It does not by itself imply that something is being discarded: a machine's 移動 can mean relocating it while continuing to use it. Translate the whole situation rather than reading English "removal" as "throwing away."
