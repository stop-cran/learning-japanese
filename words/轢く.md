# 轢く (ひく) - run over with a vehicle

## Meaning

轢く is a transitive godan verb meaning to run over or knock someone down with a vehicle. The person or animal affected takes を. Reports also use the passive 轢かれる (ひかれる), "be run over."

- 歩行者が車に轢かれました。 (ほこうしゃがくるまにひかれました。) - A pedestrian was hit by a car.
- 運転手は動物を轢かないように車を止めました。 (うんてんしゅはどうぶつをひかないようにくるまをとめました。) - The driver stopped the car to avoid running over an animal.

The negative is 轢かない (ひかない); the past is 轢いた (ひいた). In the passive example, が marks the affected person and に identifies the vehicle.

## How the kanji combine

轢 writes the native stem ひ, followed by く. There is no multi-kanji compound. The dictionary marks this word as usually written in kana, so 車にひかれた is also a useful spelling to recognize.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[衝突](衝突.md) (しょうとつ), "collision," is broader and does not require a person to be run over. [引く](引く.md), "pull," and [弾く](弾く.md), "play a stringed or keyboard instrument," are different ひく words.

## Distinctive meaning

English "knock down" needs the vehicle context here; 轢く is not a general verb for knocking over any object. It also does not by itself establish that the accident was fatal. Read the rest of the report rather than adding a consequence that the verb does not state.
