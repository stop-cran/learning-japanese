# 旅館 (りょかん) -- traditional Japanese inn

## Meaning

旅館 is a Japanese-style inn. It is associated with traditional accommodation, often including tatami rooms and futon bedding, though facilities differ.

海の近くの旅館に一泊しました。 (うみのちかくのりょかんにいっぱくしました。) -- I stayed one night at a Japanese inn near the sea.

Use 旅館に泊まる (りょかんにとまる) for staying at one. に marks the accommodation; 一泊 (いっぱく) specifies one night.

## How the kanji combine

[旅](../kanji/旅.md), read りょ, concerns travel; [館](../kanji/館.md), read かん, names a substantial building or establishment. Together they identify an establishment accommodating travelers.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ホテル is the broader loanword for hotel, often associated with Western-style facilities in this contrast. 宿 (やど) can name accommodation more generally. Neither is an exact opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

旅館 does not mean every hotel located in Japan. It points to a style of accommodation. Nor does the name guarantee a hot spring, private bath, or meals in every booking; those features must be checked separately.
