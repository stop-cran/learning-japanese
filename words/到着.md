# 到着 (とうちゃく) — arrival

## Meaning

到着 is arrival at a destination. 到着する means "arrive" and is intransitive:
the person, vehicle, or item arriving is the subject, not an object acted on.
Use **destination に到着する**.

- 電車は三時に到着しました。 (でんしゃはさんじにとうちゃくしました。) — The train arrived at three o'clock.
- ホテルに到着したら、電話してください。 (ホテルにとうちゃくしたら、でんわしてください。) — Please call when you have arrived at the hotel.

The first sentence's に marks a time; the second's に marks a destination.

## How the kanji combine

[到](../kanji/到.md) contributes reaching, and [着](../kanji/着.md) contributes arriving.
They reinforce arrival at an endpoint. 着 is not using its clothing sense here.
The on readings are トウ + チャク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

着く つく also means to arrive and fits the same destination pattern: 駅に着く
(えきにつく), "arrive at the station."
[出発](出発.md) しゅっぱつ is departure, the opposite end of the journey.
到達 とうたつ also means reaching or attaining and can concern a goal or level, not just a travel destination.

## Distinctive meaning

到着 focuses on reaching the destination, not the time spent traveling there.
You can arrive early, late, or on time; the noun alone does not choose among them.
Similarly, a parcel can arrive even though it is not a person deliberately traveling.
