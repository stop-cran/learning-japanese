# 通知 (つうち) - notice; notification

## Meaning

通知 is a notice or notification, or informing someone of something.
通知する is transitive: the information takes を and the recipient takes に.

- 学校は保護者に日程の変更を通知しました。
  (がっこうはほごしゃににっていのへんこうをつうちしました。) - The school notified parents of the schedule change.
- スマートフォンの通知を切りました。
  (スマートフォンのつうちをきりました。) - I turned off notifications on my smartphone.

The computing use names a notification on a phone or computer.
通知が届く (つうちがとどく) means that a notification arrives.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes conveying or passing through, and
[知](../kanji/知.md) contributes knowing.
Together they suggest making information known. The readings are ツウ + チ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知らせ](知らせ.md) (しらせ) and [知らせる](知らせる.md) (しらせる)
are the related native noun and verb for news or informing.
[連絡](連絡.md) (れんらく) emphasizes contact or passing on information;
[伝える](伝える.md) (つたえる) is broader conveying.
[掲示](掲示.md) (けいじ) focuses on displaying a notice for people to read.
[届ける](届ける.md) (とどける) can also mean notifying an authority,
not just delivering an item.

## Distinctive meaning

Organizations often send 通知, but the word is not restricted to official senders.
[報告](報告.md) (ほうこく), [リポート](リポート.md), and
[レポート](レポート.md) also have reporting uses.
[案内](案内.md) (あんない) can provide information or an invitation.
These overlaps do not mean every report, guide, or notice has the same purpose.
English "notice" as attention or awareness is different:
[気付く](気付く.md) (きづく) means notice or become aware, not send a notification.
