# 頭脳 (ずのう) - brains; intellect

## Meaning

頭脳 means the head or brains, and especially intellectual capacity or
understanding. It is a noun: 頭脳を使う means to use one's brains.

- 頭脳を使う仕事が好きです。 (ずのうをつかうしごとがすきです。) - I like work that uses my intellect.
- 彼女はこのチームの頭脳です。 (かのじょはこのチームのずのうです。) - She is the brains of this team.

The second describes someone directing or contributing the intellectual work;
it does not identify a literal body part. This organizational use is also
recorded in the public dictionary.

## How the kanji combine

[頭](../kanji/頭.md) contributes head, and [脳](../kanji/脳.md) brain.
Their related meanings reinforce the brains/intellect idea. The on readings
are ズ + ノウ: ずのう, not the あたま reading of 頭 by itself.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[脳](脳.md) (のう) directly names the brain organ and also has a mental
extension. [頭](頭.md) (あたま) names the head and also has a brains/intellect
sense. [知能](知能.md) (ちのう) names intellectual ability, not the organ.
The overlap is real; it is not an absolute physical-versus-mental partition.

## Distinctive meaning

[情報](情報.md) (じょうほう) can be English "intelligence" in the sense of
information, not this intellectual capacity. Conversely, 頭脳 does not by
itself specify a test score or a person's worth. Use the whole expression
to distinguish the organ, ability, and figurative organizational role.
