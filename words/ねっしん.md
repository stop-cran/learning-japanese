# ねっしん -- enthusiastic; devoted

## Meaning

ねっしん, ordinarily written 熱心, describes earnest enthusiasm or committed attention to an activity. It is a na-adjective.

- 彼は日本語の勉強に熱心です。 (かれはにほんごのべんきょうにねっしんです。) -- He is devoted to studying Japanese.
- 学生たちは熱心に話を聞いていました。 (がくせいたちはねっしんにはなしをきいていました。) -- The students were listening attentively.

The activity can take に. Before a noun use 熱心な, as in 熱心な学生 (ねっしんながくせい), an enthusiastic student. Before a verb use 熱心に.

## How the kanji combine

This card preserves the assigned kana spelling. In the usual spelling 熱心, [熱](../kanji/熱.md) contributes heat or ardor and [心](../kanji/心.md) contributes heart or mind. The on-reading combination ねつ + しん becomes ねっしん, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一生懸命 (いっしょうけんめい) emphasizes trying with all one's effort. 不熱心 (ふねっしん), unenthusiastic, is a direct negative counterpart.

## Distinctive meaning

The word concerns engagement and effort, not proven skill. Someone may study 熱心に while still finding the subject difficult. [上手](上手.md) evaluates ability instead.

The spelling 熱心 in the N3 source is this same ねっしん, not a separate word requiring a new article.
