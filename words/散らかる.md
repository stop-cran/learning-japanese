# 散らかる (ちらかる) - be untidy; lie scattered around

## Meaning

散らかる describes a place being untidy or things lying scattered around.
It is an intransitive godan verb: the untidy place or scattered things take が.

- 机の上に書類が散らかっています。
  (つくえのうえにしょるいがちらかっています。) - Papers are scattered over the desk.
- 子供が遊んだ後、部屋が散らかりました。
  (こどもがあそんだあと、へやがちらかりました。) - The room became untidy after the children played.

散らかっている commonly describes the resulting messy state.
Forms include 散らかります, 散らかった, 散らかって, and 散らからない.
The ending る does not make it an ichidan verb.

## How the kanji combine

[散](../kanji/散.md) contributes scattering, with the native reading ち here.
らかる is the written kana ending.
This is a whole lexical verb, not a passive conjugation of 散らかす.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[散らかす](散らかす.md) (ちらかす) is transitive:
部屋を散らかす means make a mess of the room,
while 部屋が散らかる describes the room becoming messy.
[片付ける](片付ける.md) (かたづける) describes tidying it.
[汚れる](汚れる.md) (よごれる) means get dirty, a different property.

## Distinctive meaning

The intransitive construction does not deny that someone caused the mess;
it leaves that person out of the central grammatical role.
[混乱](混乱.md) (こんらん), [荒れる](荒れる.md) (あれる),
and [混雑](混雑.md) (こんざつ) also have disorder uses,
but the practical focus here is scattered objects or an untidy space.
Sparse raindrops or separate rice grains do not by themselves imply that disorder.
