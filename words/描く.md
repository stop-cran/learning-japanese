# 描く (えがく) - draw; depict

## Meaning

描く is a transitive godan verb with two readings taught here: **えがく** and **かく**. Both can mean drawing, painting, or sketching. Mark the picture or subject with を.

- 子どもが猫の絵を描いています。 (こどもがねこのえをかいています。) - The child is drawing a picture of a cat.
- この小説は家族の生活を描いています。 (このしょうせつはかぞくのせいかつをえがいています。) - This novel depicts a family's life.

The dictionary assigns the broader "depict; describe" sense in the second example to **えがく**, not to かく. For the first example, 絵を描く can be read えをかく or えをえがく.

## How the kanji combine

描 is the single written component meaning "draw; portray." The ending く inflects: えがいて / かいて, えがいた / かいた. There is no multi-kanji compound to separate.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[書く](書く.md) (かく) is the spelling to learn for writing letters or text. [絵](絵.md) (え), "picture," helps make the drawing sense explicit. 描く is not restricted to pencil drawings: painting is also included.

## Distinctive meaning

Reading matters even when the written form stays the same. A novel can 描く a person's life without containing illustrations; read that descriptive use えがく. Do not infer that every meaning under a shared dictionary entry belongs equally to both readings. Conversely, the drawing sense is not reserved exclusively for either pronunciation.
