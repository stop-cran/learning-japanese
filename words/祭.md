# 祭 (まつり) - festival

## Meaning

祭 is a festival or festive celebration. The same word is also written 祭り. It can refer to a local or religious festival; the noun alone does not tell you what ceremonies, performances, or food stalls will be present.

- 夏の祭に友達と行きました。 (なつのまつりにともだちといきました。) - I went to the summer festival with a friend.
- この町の祭は来週です。 (このまちのまつりはらいしゅうです。) - This town's festival is next week.

Use 祭に行く for going to a festival. If describing something done there, 祭で marks the event as the setting.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 祭 writes the native noun まつり. In the alternate spelling 祭り, the final り is written out. Neither spelling turns the noun into the verb 祭る (まつる), which concerns enshrining or worshipping.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お祭り](お祭り.md) (おまつり) adds お and refers to a festival as well. The forms overlap in reference; the お does not establish a different kind of event. There is no ordinary direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "feast" here concerns a celebration, not merely eating a large dinner. This card teaches the festival sense, rather than importing the entry's separate colloquial internet uses into ordinary festival descriptions.
