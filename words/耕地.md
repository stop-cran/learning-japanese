# 耕地 (こうち) - arable land; farmland

## Meaning

耕地 is arable land or farmland: land used or intended for cultivating crops.
It names the land, not the act of tilling it.
耕地の面積 (こうちのめんせき) means "the area of farmland."

- この地域では耕地が減っています。 (このちいきではこうちがへっています。) - Farmland is decreasing in this region.
- 川の近くに耕地が広がっています。 (かわのちかくにこうちがひろがっています。) - Farmland extends along the river.

## How the kanji combine

[耕](../kanji/耕.md) contributes tilling or cultivation, and
[地](../kanji/地.md) contributes land. The action qualifies the land:
land for cultivation. The on readings コウ + チ give こうち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

農地 (のうち) also means agricultural land.
[畑](畑.md) (はたけ) is cultivated field land, often contrasted with
[田](田.md) (た), a rice field. Both kinds can be discussed as 耕地.
[土地](土地.md) (とち) is land more broadly, without the cultivation role.

## Distinctive meaning

耕地 does not necessarily mean that someone is ploughing it at this moment.
Nor does it identify the crop or guarantee a good harvest.
The source-related sense here is farmland; the dictionary's separate
Brazilian estate sense is not needed to interpret these examples.
