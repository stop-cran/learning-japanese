# 写る (うつる) - be photographed; appear in a photograph

## Meaning

写る is an intransitive godan verb: be photographed or appear in a photographic image.
The person or thing appearing takes が, and the photograph can take に.

- 写真の右側に私が写っています。 (しゃしんのみぎがわにわたしがうつっています。) - I appear on the right side of the photograph.
- 暗くて、顔がはっきり写りませんでした。 (くらくて、かおがはっきりうつりませんでした。) - It was dark, and the face did not come out clearly in the photograph.

Forms include 写ります (うつります), 写った (うつった) and 写らない (うつらない).
The dictionary also records being projected; photography is this article's main production pattern.

## How the kanji combine

[写](../kanji/写.md) supplies うつ, with る written in kana.
This is a complete native verb, not the passive conjugation of [写す](写す.md).
The grammatical passive of 写す is 写される (うつされる).

## Synonyms and antonyms

花を写す (はなをうつす), "photograph a flower," presents the photographer's action;
花が写真に写る (はながしゃしんにうつる), "a flower appears in the photograph," presents the subject.
[映る](映る.md), also うつる, overlaps in photographic and projected-image uses,
and is useful for reflections and images appearing on a screen.

## Distinctive meaning

The official spelling guide contrasts being recorded, ビデオに写る (ビデオにうつる),
with the playback/display viewpoint of 映る.
It explicitly allows 映る when referring to the displayed image or simultaneous live display.
Do not turn the practical 写/映 distinction into an absolute photo-versus-video rule.
