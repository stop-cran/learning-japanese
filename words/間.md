# 間 (あいだ) - space between; interval

## Meaning

間 names the space or interval between things. Use AとBの間 for "between A and B."
It also names a period during which something happens.

- 駅と学校の間に公園があります。 (えきとがっこうのあいだにこうえんがあります。) - There is a park between the station and the school.
- 夏休みの間、祖母の家にいました。 (なつやすみのあいだ、そぼのいえにいました。) - I stayed at my grandmother's house during summer vacation.

**Another reading: ま.** This is a separately recorded native noun for a pause,
time, or space, and also a room. It is not a free pronunciation substitute
for あいだ in the examples above.

- 少し間を置いてから、答えました。 (すこしまをおいてから、こたえました。) - I answered after a short pause.
- 椅子と椅子の間を空けてください。 (いすといすのまをあけてください。) - Please leave space between the chairs.

The chair example also allows あいだ. It illustrates a shared spatial use, not a
construction restricted to ま.

間を置く (まをおく) highlights a pause. 茶の間 (ちゃのま), "family sitting room,"
shows the room use in a familiar expression.

## How the kanji combine

[間](../kanji/間.md) writes the whole native noun あいだ. This reading is not the
かん heard in compounds such as 時間 (じかん), "time."

## Synonyms and antonyms

中 (なか), "inside," places something within a container or group; 間 highlights
the intervening space. There is no general antonym covering space and time alike.

## Distinctive meaning

A time noun takes の: 休みの間 (やすみのあいだ), "during the break." A clause can
precede it directly: 寝ている間 (ねているあいだ), "while sleeping."
間に often locates an event within that interval, rather than describing a
state continuing throughout it.

For ま, learn expressions such as 間を置く as whole constructions. The two native
readings overlap in space and time, but that overlap does not license changing
every あいだ to ま.
