# ヨーロッパ (ヨーロッパ) - Europe

## Meaning

ヨーロッパ is the geographical name Europe.
As a noun, it takes ordinary place particles: ヨーロッパへ or ヨーロッパに for a destination,
ヨーロッパで for an activity there, and ヨーロッパの for noun modification.

- 来年、ヨーロッパへ旅行する予定です。 (らいねん、ヨーロッパへりょこうするよていです。) - I plan to travel to Europe next year.
- 大学でヨーロッパの歴史を勉強しています。 (だいがくでヨーロッパのれきしをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying European history at university.

In the second sentence, の connects the region with its history; it does not turn the name into a na-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. Retain both the long-vowel mark ー and the small ッ: ヨー・ロッ・パ.
JMdict records Portuguese *Europa* as the loan source.
It also records the rare ateji 欧羅巴, but those characters are not a literal meaning-by-meaning decomposition of the katakana word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

欧州 (おうしゅう) is another name for Europe.
[アジア](アジア.md) is another geographical region, not an antonym.
[外国](外国.md) (がいこく), "foreign country/countries," depends on the speaker's reference country and is not a synonym for Europe.

## Distinctive meaning

ヨーロッパ is not the name of one country.
It also should not be treated as exactly interchangeable with the European Union, a political organization whose membership is a different concept.
Specify a country or institution when the distinction matters.
