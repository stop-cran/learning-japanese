# 鳴く (なく) — make an animal call

## Meaning

鳴く (なく) describes an animal making its call.
English chooses a more specific verb according to the animal: a bird chirps or sings, a cat meows, and a dog barks.
鳴く itself need not specify which of those sounds is meant.

- 猫が鳴いています。 (ねこがないています。) — A cat is meowing.
- 朝、鳥が鳴きます。 (あさ、とりがなきます。) — Birds sing in the morning.

The animal is the subject, marked by が. The て-form is 鳴いて (ないて), and the past is 鳴いた (ないた).

## How the kanji combine

[鳴](../kanji/鳴.md) supplies the calling or sounding meaning and writes な.
く completes the native verb. Different endings make different verbs:
鳴る (なる) is to sound or ring, and 鳴らす (ならす) is to make something sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

泣く (なく) means to cry or shed tears. It shares the kana reading but not the ordinary animal-call meaning.
鳴る suits a ringing phone or bell; 鳴く suits an animal calling.
鳴らす introduces someone causing the sound, rather than merely reporting that it occurs.

## Distinctive meaning

English "cry" can mean either weeping or an animal's call.
鳴く makes the animal-call sense clear; it does not assert sadness or tears.
For example, 猫が鳴いている says the cat is vocalizing, not that it is weeping.
