# 儲ける (もうける) - earn; gain a profit; have a child

## Meaning

儲ける is a transitive ichidan verb for earning or gaining, especially
making a financial profit. It also has an established "have a child" use.

- 株でお金を儲けました。 (かぶでおかねをもうけました。) - I made money from stocks.
- 二人は子供を二人儲けました。 (ふたりはこどもをふたりもうけました。) - The couple had two children.

The second use reports having children; it does not call the children
financial profit. Forms include 儲けます (もうけます), 儲けて
(もうけて), and 儲けない (もうけない).

## How the kanji combine

儲 writes the native base もう, followed by ける. Keep the complete
word distinct from 儲かる (もうかる); changing the ending changes the
verb's grammatical pattern.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[稼ぐ](稼ぐ.md) (かせぐ) overlaps with earning money, and
[得る](得る.md) (える) is a broader verb for obtaining or gaining.
[儲かる](儲かる.md) describes profitability rather than directly
taking the thing earned as an を-object.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records having a stroke of luck; not every instance
requires doing paid work. 設ける (もうける), "establish; provide," is
a separately written word. The Japanese dictionary relates their origins,
but that is not permission to exchange the modern spellings in every sentence.
