# 付ける (つける) — attach; add

## Meaning

One basic use of 付ける is to attach or add one thing to another.
It is transitive: mark what is attached with を and the receiving surface or object with に.
JMdict marks this sense as usually written in kana, so recognize つける as well as 付ける.

- シャツにボタンを付けました。 (シャツにボタンをつけました。) — I attached a button to the shirt.
- かばんに名札を付けます。 (かばんになふだをつけます。) — I will attach a name tag to the bag.

The polite form is 付けます (つけます); the plain past is 付けた (つけた).

The same ichidan verb also means **turn on**, as in 電気をつける (でんきをつける),
"turn on the light." The light or device takes を; kana つける is a useful ordinary spelling.

- 暗いので、電気をつけてください。 (くらいので、でんきをつけてください。) — It is dark, so please turn on the light.

## How the kanji combine

[付](../kanji/付.md) represents attachment. Its native reading is つ here, followed by the written ending ける.
This is one verb, not a kanji compound. The kana ending helps distinguish it from 付く (つく).

## Synonyms and antonyms

付く is the intransitive partner: something sticks or is attached.
ボタンが付いている (ボタンがついている) means "a button is attached"; it describes the state, not someone's attaching action.
[取る](取る.md) (とる), in its removal sense, can describe the reverse action: ボタンを取る, "remove a button."
For switching on a light, the opposite is 消す (けす), "turn off," not 取る.

## Distinctive meaning

つける has many uses and spellings; attachment and switching on do not establish an unrestricted spelling rule for every occurrence.
Start with the concrete pattern "attach X to Y."
Whether someone actively attaches X or X is already attached decides between 付ける and 付く in this pattern.
