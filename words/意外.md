# 意外 (いがい) - unexpected; surprising

## Meaning

意外 describes something that differs from what someone expected. Use 意外な before a noun, 意外だ or 意外です as a predicate, and 意外に or 意外と for "unexpectedly" or "surprisingly."

- 意外な結果になりました。 (いがいなけっかになりました。) - The result was unexpected.
- この問題は意外と簡単でした。 (このもんだいはいがいとかんたんでした。) - This problem was surprisingly easy.

The second example suggests that the speaker had expected more difficulty. 意外 does not tell us whether the surprise is welcome; both a pleasant result and disappointing news can be unexpected.

## How the kanji combine

[意](../kanji/意.md) contributes thought or expectation, and [外](../kanji/外.md) means outside. Together they suggest something outside one's expectations. The on readings are イ + ガイ; this is a meaning aid, not a claim about historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

予想外 (よそうがい), "outside expectations," is close in meaning. 予想どおり (よそうどおり), "as expected," gives a useful contextual opposite. [普通](普通.md) (ふつう), "ordinary," is not an exact antonym: an ordinary fact can still surprise a particular person.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 意外 with [以外](以外.md), also いがい, meaning "other than" or "except." 意外な答え is an unexpected answer; これ以外の答え means an answer other than this one. Their identical pronunciation does not make their grammar interchangeable.
