# 別れる (わかれる) — part, break up

## Meaning

別れる means to part from someone. In a relationship context, it can mean to break up or separate.

- 駅で友達と別れました。（えきで ともだちと わかれました。）— I parted from my friend at the station.
- 彼と別れました。（かれと わかれました。）— I broke up with him. (In a romantic context.)

The person one parts from commonly takes と. This is an ichidan verb: 別れます わかれます, 別れて わかれて.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single native verb. [別](../kanji/別.md) expresses separation and supplies わか; れる is the written okurigana. There is no two-kanji compound to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 離れる はなれる emphasizes being apart or moving away, including physical distance.
- 分かれる わかれる means to split or branch, for example into groups. Its spelling highlights division rather than a personal farewell.
- [会う](会う.md) あう, "meet," is a useful situational contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

A temporary farewell and the end of a relationship can use the same verb. Context decides: the station example need not imply a quarrel or breakup. Do not translate every 別れる as "divorce."
