# 懇談 (こんだん) — friendly talk; informal meeting

## Meaning

懇談 is talking together in a relaxed, friendly way. Digital Daijisen defines it as 打ち解けて親しく話し合うこと (うちとけてしたしくはなしあうこと), “opening up and talking together on friendly terms,” and gives 先生と父兄が懇談する (せんせいとふけいがこんだんする), “teachers and parents have a talk.” JMdict glosses it “friendly chat; relaxed conversation; informal meeting”; with する, it is a verb.

- 学期末に、担任の先生と保護者の懇談がある。 (がっきまつに、たんにんのせんせいとほごしゃのこんだんがある。) — At the end of the term, there is a talk between the homeroom teacher and parents.
- 社長は若手社員と昼食をとりながら懇談した。 (しゃちょうはわかてしゃいんとちゅうしょくをとりながらこんだんした。) — The company president chatted with young employees over lunch.

## How the kanji combine

[懇](../kanji/懇.md) (こん) means warm sincerity, and [談](../kanji/談.md) (だん) means to talk; [Digital Daijisen, 談](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E8%AB%87) lists 懇談 under that sense, sense 1. と marks the person one talks with: 若手社員と懇談した.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[相談](相談.md) (そうだん) is asking for advice or discussing a problem, and [会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) is a formal meeting. [話し合い](話し合い.md) (はなしあい) is a discussion to settle something. 懇談 stresses that the talk is friendly and relaxed.

## Distinctive meaning

Related words are 懇談会 (こんだんかい), “an informal meeting; a get-together,” and 懇親会 (こんしんかい), “a social gathering; a get-together.”
