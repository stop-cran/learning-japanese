# 改正 (かいせい) - revision; amendment

## Meaning

改正 is revision or amendment, especially of laws, rules, or regulations to address unsuitable or inadequate points. 改正する is transitive: use を for what is revised.

- 学校は校則を改正しました。 (がっこうはこうそくをかいせいしました。) - The school revised its rules.
- 法律の改正について話し合いました。 (ほうりつのかいせいについてはなしあいました。) - We discussed amendment of the law.

The noun pattern Nの改正 means "revision of N." The verb forms include 改正します and 改正した.

## How the kanji combine

[改](../kanji/改.md), "revise," combines with [正](../kanji/正.md), "correct." Together they express revising to put something right. The on readings are カイ + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[修正](修正.md) (しゅうせい), "correction; revision," and [変更](変更.md) (へんこう), "change," overlap. 改定 (かいてい) also concerns revising arrangements or rules; 改訂 (かいてい) is useful for revising written works. These are usage tendencies, not non-overlapping boxes.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 改正 for every change or physical repair. It is particularly useful with rules and legal arrangements. The word presents a revision as putting something right; it does not prove that everyone agrees the change is an improvement. It shares the reading かいせい with [快晴](快晴.md), an unrelated weather word.
