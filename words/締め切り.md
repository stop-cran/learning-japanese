# 締め切り (しめきり) - deadline; closing

## Meaning

締め切り is a deadline or cutoff: the point when submissions, applications, or another period of acceptance close. **締切**, the spelling in the source list, and **締切り** represent the same word and reading.

- 申し込みの締め切りは金曜日です。
  (もうしこみのしめきりはきんようびです。) - The application deadline is Friday.
- 締め切りまでに作文を出してください。
  (しめきりまでにさくぶんをだしてください。) - Please submit your essay by the deadline.

締め切りに間に合う (しめきりにまにあう) means "meet the deadline"; 締め切りを過ぎる (しめきりをすぎる) means "pass the deadline."

## How the kanji combine

This is the noun associated with [締め切る](締め切る.md) (しめきる), "close up; close applications." 締め contributes closing, and [切](../kanji/切.md) contributes completing the action, not cutting a submitted paper.

The separate physical sense is closing a door or window, also written 閉め切り with [閉](../kanji/閉.md). The list's "Closed/No Entrance" glosses belong with a closed opening, not with the deadline meaning. They do not make 締め切り a general expression for every prohibition on entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[期限](期限.md) (きげん) also means a deadline or allowed term. 締め切り foregrounds the cutoff for acceptance; 期限 also covers such things as a loan's repayment term. [終わり](終わり.md) (おわり) is an end more generally, not necessarily an application cutoff.

## Distinctive meaning

The deadline is not the whole period before it, and saying 締め切り does not specify whether late submissions will be accepted. This page teaches the deadline sense and recognizes physical closing; the dictionary's specialized cofferdam sense is outside the practical introduction.
