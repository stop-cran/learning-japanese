# また (また) — again; in addition

## Meaning

また means **again or another time** as an adverb. As a connective, it adds information: **also, and, or in addition**.

- 来年また日本へ行きたいです。 (らいねんまたにほんへいきたいです。) — I want to go to Japan again next year.
- この部屋は広いです。また、窓も大きいです。 (このへやはひろいです。また、まどもおおきいです。) — This room is spacious. In addition, the windows are large.

The second use is common in organized explanations and writing; it does not mean the windows are large “again.”

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. 又 is a recognized spelling, but the kana form serves both repetition and addition. No kanji decomposition is needed.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もう一度 (もういちど), “once more,” focuses clearly on repetition. [それに](それに.md), “besides,” adds another point. まだ, “still/not yet,” is a different word despite the similar sound.

## Distinctive meaning

Context decides whether また repeats an event or adds a statement. また明日 (またあした), “See you tomorrow,” uses the prospect of meeting again. Do not treat また as the normal noun-listing “and” between two objects.
