# もしかしたら - perhaps; maybe

## Meaning

もしかしたら introduces a possibility or tentative guess: perhaps,
maybe, or by some chance.

- もしかしたら、明日は雪が降るかもしれません。 (もしかしたら、あしたはゆきがふるかもしれません。) - Perhaps it will snow tomorrow.
- もしかしたら、鍵は机の上にあるのでは？ (もしかしたら、かぎはつくえのうえにあるのでは？) - Maybe the key is on the desk?

It often accompanies かもしれない, but that ending is not obligatory,
as the second example shows.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual; 若しかしたら is a rare recognized spelling. Do not
turn the character 若 into a separate "young" meaning in the expression.
Learn the full possibility expression, not merely the ending したら.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もしかすると](もしかすると.md) is a close alternative.
[多分](多分.md) (たぶん) and [おそらく](おそらく.md) often suggest
"probably"; [あるいは](あるいは.md) has a possibility use too.
These are not fixed numerical probability bands.

## Distinctive meaning

This can suggest an uncertain or unexpected possibility, rather than
asserting that something happened coincidentally.
[もし](もし.md), "if," introduces a condition and is not automatically
interchangeable with a standalone "maybe." Do not infer certainty or
a requirement for one particular sentence ending.
