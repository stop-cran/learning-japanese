# 結構 (けっこう) — fine; sufficient

## Meaning

結構 can describe something as fine or splendid, or as sufficient and acceptable. As a na-adjective it takes な before a noun: 結構なお品 (けっこうなおしな), "a fine item," is a polite expression of appreciation.

- これで結構です。 (これでけっこうです。) — This will be fine.
- いいえ、もう結構です。 (いいえ、もうけっこうです。) — No, thank you; I've had enough.

As an adverb it means "quite/rather": 結構高い (けっこうたかい), "quite expensive."

## How the kanji combine

結 concerns joining, and [構](../kanji/構.md) concerns construction or arrangement. The on-reading compound is けっこう. Its everyday evaluative meanings must be learned as usages, not derived literally from the characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

十分 (じゅうぶん), "sufficient," overlaps with the enough sense. すばらしい, "wonderful," overlaps with praise. Neither covers all uses.

## Distinctive meaning

結構です can accept an arrangement or decline an offer because nothing more is needed. Do not memorize it as an unconditional "yes." Add これで, もう, or an explanation so your intention is clear.
