# 占める (しめる) - account for; occupy

## Meaning

占める means to account for a proportion or occupy a place or position. It is a transitive ichidan verb: 占めます, 占めた, 占めない. The share or space occupied takes を.

- 女性が参加者の半分を占めています。 (じょせいがさんかしゃのはんぶんをしめています。) - Women make up half of the participants.
- 大きな机が部屋の大部分を占めています。 (おおきなつくえがへやのだいぶぶんをしめています。) - A large desk takes up most of the room.

In the first example, 女性 is the group accounting for the share, and 参加者の半分 is that share. Reversing the roles changes the statement.

## How the kanji combine

占 contributes occupying or holding in this word. The reading is し before める. 占う (うらなう), "tell fortunes," uses the same character but is a different native verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[割合](割合.md) (わりあい) names a proportion; 占める states what accounts for it. [閉める](閉める.md), "close," is another しめる but does not describe percentages or occupied space.

## Distinctive meaning

重要な位置を占める (じゅうようないちをしめる) means "occupy an important position." It may concern a role rather than a literal seat. 占めている frequently describes an existing share or position; it need not mean that an act of taking possession is underway.
