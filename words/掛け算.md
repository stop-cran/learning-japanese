# 掛け算 (かけざん) - multiplication

## Meaning

掛け算 is multiplication, one of the arithmetic operations. It is a noun: 掛け算をする means to do multiplication.

- 掛け算の問題を解きました。 (かけざんのもんだいをときました。) - I solved some multiplication problems.
- 今日は学校で掛け算を習いました。 (きょうはがっこうでかけざんをならいました。) - Today I learned multiplication at school.

## How the kanji combine

[掛](../kanji/掛.md), in the native stem かけ, contributes multiplying in this use; [算](../kanji/算.md) contributes calculation. This is a native-first mixed compound: かけ + サン, with サン voiced to ざん. The card uses the native-first type `wago`, not a claim that both parts are native readings.

掛ける has a multiplication sense, usually written かける. Its existing [word article](掛ける.md) begins with the separate hanging and telephone-call senses; the shared spelling does not make multiplication a kind of hanging.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計算](計算.md) (けいさん) is calculation more generally. 足し算 (たしざん) is addition, 引き算 (ひきざん) subtraction, and 割り算 (わりざん) division. They are different operations, not four interchangeable translations.

## Distinctive meaning

Division can undo multiplication under suitable conditions, but is not a universal linguistic antonym. The dictionary also records 掛算 and かけ算 for かけざん. Learn the voiced ざん rather than reading the compound as かけさん.
