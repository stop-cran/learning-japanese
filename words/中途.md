# 中途 (ちゅうと) - partway; mid-course

## Meaning

中途 is a point partway through a journey, activity, or period.
It is a noun. 中途で locates an event during the course of
something, before its completion.

- 雨が強くなったので、中途で引き返しました。 (あめがつよくなったので、ちゅうとでひきかえしました。) - The rain grew heavier, so we turned back partway.
- その計画は中途で変更されました。 (そのけいかくはちゅうとでへんこうされました。) - That plan was changed partway through.

The word identifies a stage along the way. It does not measure an
exact fifty-percent point.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) contributes being within or in the middle,
and [途](../kanji/途.md) a way or course. Together they place
something within a course before its end. Read チュウ + ト.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[途中](途中.md) (とちゅう) overlaps in journey and in-progress
uses. The two words use the same characters in opposite order,
but this is not a general rule for reversing other compounds.
[最中](最中.md) (さいちゅう) especially highlights being right
in the midst of an activity.

## Distinctive meaning

中途 often occurs in established compounds.
中途採用 (ちゅうとさいよう), "mid-career recruitment,"
contrasts with recruiting new graduates.
中途半端 (ちゅうとはんぱ) means unfinished or half-hearted
in appropriate contexts. Bare 中途 does not automatically accuse
someone of doing a task half-heartedly.
