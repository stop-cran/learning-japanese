# 商業 (しょうぎょう) - commerce; commercial activity

## Meaning

商業 means commerce or commercial activity, especially the economic activity of buying and selling goods.

- この町は商業が盛んです。 (このまちはしょうぎょうがさかんです。) - Commerce thrives in this town.
- 駅の周辺には商業施設が集まっています。 (えきのしゅうへんにはしょうぎょうしせつがあつまっています。) - Commercial facilities are concentrated around the station.

商業施設 (しょうぎょうしせつ) names commercial facilities, such as shopping facilities. The compound does not make 商業 itself a word for a particular building.

## How the kanji combine

[商](../kanji/商.md) concerns trade or selling, and [業](../kanji/業.md) an occupation or line of activity. Together they provide a learning association with commerce.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[商売](商売.md) overlaps with trade or doing business. [営業](営業.md) can concern business operations or sales. [貿易](貿易.md) concerns trade, especially international trade; it is not disjoint from commerce merely because its scale or geographic emphasis differs.

## Distinctive meaning

商業 often names an activity or economic sector. A company participating in it is an organization, not automatically the meaning of the bare noun 商業. The related modifier 商業的 (しょうぎょうてき), "commercial," can describe a commercial orientation, but the base word does not itself mean a television advertisement.
