# 出張 (しゅっちょう) - business trip; official trip

## Meaning

出張 means going away from one's usual workplace for work or official duties. It is a noun and an intransitive する-verb.

- 来週、大阪に出張します。 (らいしゅう、おおさかにしゅっちょうします。) - Next week I am going to Osaka on business.
- 田中さんは今、出張中です。 (たなかさんはいま、しゅっちょうちゅうです。) - Tanaka is away on a business trip now.

The destination takes に in 大阪に出張する. 出張中 means being away on the assignment; the word does not require an overnight stay.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) suggests going out. 張 has extending or stretching associations; use the combination as a reminder of work extending beyond the usual workplace, not as a historical derivation. Keep the small っ and the long ちょう in しゅっちょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[旅行](旅行.md) and [旅](旅.md) are broader travel words. A trip can involve both business and sightseeing, but 出張 identifies the work assignment rather than the leisure purpose. [出勤](出勤.md) means reporting for work, not necessarily going to a different place on assignment.

## Distinctive meaning

出張 is not a synonym for every journey, daily commute, or permanent transfer to a new post. Related expressions such as 出張サービス (しゅっちょうサービス), a service provided at the customer's location, retain the idea of work being performed away from the usual base.
