# シャワー (シャワー) — shower

## Meaning

シャワー is a shower used for washing. The everyday expression シャワーを浴びる（シャワーをあびる） means to take a shower.

朝、シャワーを浴びます。（あさ、シャワーをあびます。） — I take a shower in the morning.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "shower." Small ャ combines with シ to make シャ; the final ー lengthens ワ. There are no kanji parts.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お風呂（おふろ） means a bath or bathing facilities. お風呂に入る（おふろにはいる）, "take a bath," uses a different verb and particle from シャワーを浴びる.

## Distinctive meaning

浴びる treats the spray of water as something received over the body. Do not copy the English verb "take" into Japanese. In a hotel description, シャワー can name the facility itself, not just the act of washing.
