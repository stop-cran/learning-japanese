# 包丁 (ほうちょう) - kitchen knife

## Meaning

包丁 is a kitchen knife used for preparing food. It is also written 庖丁,
with the same reading ほうちょう; learn 包丁 as the ordinary production spelling.

- 包丁で野菜を切りました。 (ほうちょうでやさいをきりました。) - I cut the vegetables with a kitchen knife.
- 使った包丁を洗ってから片付けました。 (つかったほうちょうをあらってからかたづけました。) - I washed the kitchen knife after using it and then put it away.

## How the kanji combine

In 包丁, [包](../kanji/包.md) is read ホウ and 丁 チョウ.
Recognize the complete word rather than inventing a "wrapping + block" definition.
The alternate 庖丁 uses 庖 in place of 包; it is not another pronunciation or
a different sort of knife.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ナイフ](ナイフ.md) is the broader loanword "knife," including knives used at
the table and other kinds. 包丁 specifically points to the kitchen preparation
tool; the words can overlap rather than partitioning all blades absolutely.
[鋏](鋏.md) (はさみ) means scissors, a different tool.

## Distinctive meaning

包丁で切る (ほうちょうできる) uses で for the cutting instrument.
An English "carving knife" can be a kind of kitchen knife, but 包丁 is not limited
to knives for carving a roast. The spellings 包丁 and 庖丁 do not by themselves
distinguish different kinds of knife.
