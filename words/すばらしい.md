# すばらしい (すばらしい) - wonderful; splendid

## Meaning

すばらしい expresses strong positive admiration: wonderful, splendid, or excellent. It can describe a view, performance, idea, achievement, or experience.

- 山からの景色はすばらしかったです。 (やまからのけしきはすばらしかったです。) - The view from the mountain was wonderful.
- それはすばらしい考えですね。 (それはすばらしいかんがえですね。) - That is an excellent idea.

It is an i-adjective: すばらしい景色, without な. The past is すばらしかった; the adverbial form is すばらしく.

## How the kanji combine

素晴らしい is a normal alternative spelling. Its kanji are assigned to an existing native word; do not interpret it as literally meaning clear weather or require sunshine for the adjective to apply.

## Synonyms and antonyms

よい means good more generally. 見事 (みごと), "splendid; impressive," often praises a successful result or skill. 悪い (わるい), "bad," provides a broad evaluative contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

すばらしい is stronger praise than simply saying something is acceptable or convenient. It does not merely state that an event caused surprise: the evaluation is normally admiring. It can praise abstract things such as ideas as readily as visible beauty.
