# ちびちび — little by little; in small sips

## Meaning

Taking, using, or doing something in small amounts. Drinking is a useful starting scene, but not the only use.

- 熱いお茶をちびちび飲んだ。 — I drank the hot tea in small sips.
- 大切なチョコレートを毎日ちびちび食べている。 — I eat a little of my treasured chocolate each day.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[少し](少し.md)ずつ means “a little at a time” more neutrally. [ごくごく](ごくごく.md) and [がぶがぶ](がぶがぶ.md) suggest larger gulps.

## Distinctive meaning

ちびちび飲む（のむ）and ちびちび使う（つかう）focus on small installments, not simply slow bodily movement. The tone can suggest savoring or sparing use; context decides whether it is approving.
