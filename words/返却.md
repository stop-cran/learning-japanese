# 返却 (へんきゃく) — return (of something borrowed)

## Meaning

返却 means returning something that was borrowed or entrusted. JMdict also glosses it as giving back or repayment. It can be used with する.

- 借りた本は来週までに返却してください。 (かりたほんはらいしゅうまでにへんきゃくしてください。) — Please return the books you borrowed by next week.
- レンタカーの返却は午後六時までです。 (レンタカーのへんきゃくはごごろくじまでです。) — The rental car must be returned by 6 p.m.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[返](../kanji/返.md) means giving back, and [却](../kanji/却.md) can mean removing or disposing of something; Digital Daijisen lists 返却 under that sense of 却. Together, they mean handing something back.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[返す](返す.md) (かえす) is the native verb for returning something (JMdict entry 1512130). [貸し出し](貸し出し.md) (かしだし), “lending; loaning” (JMdict entry 1590240), is the step before a 返却.

## Distinctive meaning

返却 is specifically glossed for things borrowed or entrusted, while 返す has many other senses, such as turning over or replying.
