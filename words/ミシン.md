# ミシン (ミシン) - sewing machine

## Meaning

ミシン is a sewing machine.

- ミシンで袋を縫いました。 (ミシンでふくろをぬいました。) - I sewed a bag with a sewing machine.
- このミシンの使い方を教えてください。 (このミシンのつかいかたをおしえてください。) - Please show me how to use this sewing machine.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[機械](機械.md) (きかい), "machine," is the broader category. [裁縫](裁縫.md) (さいほう) is sewing or needlework, and [縫う](縫う.md) (ぬう) is the action of sewing, not the machine.

## Distinctive meaning

Despite its connection with English "sewing machine," ミシン is not the Japanese word for every machine. Keep the katakana spelling and short vowels; do not substitute a long vowel by analogy with another loanword.
