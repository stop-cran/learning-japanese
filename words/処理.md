# 処理 (しょり) — processing; handling

## Meaning

処理 means processing or dealing with something so that the required work is carried out.
It can concern data, documents, materials, or a matter that needs attention.
The verb is transitive: **something を処理する**, with を marking what is handled.

- コンピューターがデータを処理しています。 (コンピューターがデータをしょりしています。) — The computer is processing data.
- この書類は今日中に処理します。 (このしょるいはきょうじゅうにしょりします。) — I will deal with these documents by the end of today.

## How the kanji combine

[処](../kanji/処.md) contributes handling or dealing with, while [理](../kanji/理.md) connects to order and organizing.
Together they express handling something in an organized way; this is not a literal place plus an abstract reason.
The on readings are ショ + リ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

対処 たいしょ is coping or dealing with a situation; its verb uses **situation に対処する**.
処分 しょぶん can mean disposal, such as throwing things away, though it also has other senses.
[解決](解決.md) かいけつ means a solution or resolution and draws attention to a problem being resolved.

## Distinctive meaning

The object determines what processing involves. データを処理する means processing data, not necessarily deleting it.
処理 can include disposal in a suitable context, but "throw away" is not a safe translation for every occurrence.
Likewise, saying that something was handled does not by itself explain what procedure was used.
