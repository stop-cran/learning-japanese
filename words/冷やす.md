# 冷やす (ひやす) - cool; chill something

## Meaning

冷やす means to cool or chill something, including refrigerating it.
It is a transitive godan verb: the thing cooled takes を.

- 冷蔵庫で麦茶を冷やします。 (れいぞうこでむぎちゃをひやします。) - I chill barley tea in the refrigerator.
- 氷で足を冷やしました。 (こおりであしをひやしました。) - I cooled my foot with ice.

The examples demonstrate language, not treatment instructions.
Forms include 冷やします, 冷やさない, 冷やして and 冷やした.
冷す is another recorded spelling of ひやす.

## How the kanji combine

[冷](../kanji/冷.md) contributes cooling and is read ひ here;
やす completes the native verb. Keep the complete reading separate
from さます in [冷ます](冷ます.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

冷ます (さます) also means to cool something, especially letting
something hot lose heat. 冷やす is useful for actively chilling it.
These are tendencies, not a rule assigning every starting temperature
to one verb.
[冷える](冷える.md) (ひえる) and [冷める](冷める.md) (さめる)
are intransitive: 麦茶が冷える, the barley tea becomes chilled.
[涼む](涼む.md) (すずむ) concerns cooling oneself or enjoying cool air.

## Distinctive meaning

頭を冷やす (あたまをひやす) means to cool down or regain composure,
overlapping with [落ち着く](落ち着く.md) (おちつく).
肝を冷やす (きもをひやす) means to have a fright.
Learn these complete expressions: the latter does not make bare 冷やす
an unrestricted substitute for every verb meaning fear or frighten.
Neither idiom requires an actual refrigerator.
