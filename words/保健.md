# 保健 (ほけん) - health preservation; hygiene

## Meaning

保健 concerns preserving or promoting health: health protection, hygiene, or sanitation. In school it can also name health education.

- 保健の授業で睡眠の大切さを学びました。 (ほけんのじゅぎょうですいみんのたいせつさをまなびました。) - We learned the importance of sleep in health class.
- 気分が悪くなったので、保健室へ行きました。 (きぶんがわるくなったので、ほけんしつへいきました。) - I felt ill, so I went to the school infirmary.

保健室 (ほけんしつ) is a school's health room or infirmary; 保健所 (ほけんじょ) is a public health center. Learn the complete compound and its institutional context.

## How the kanji combine

保 contributes preserving or maintaining; 健 contributes health. This is an action-and-object combination: preserving health. The on readings are ホ + ケン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[健康](健康.md) (けんこう) refers to health or being healthy. [清潔](清潔.md) (せいけつ) concerns cleanliness or hygiene, one part of health protection.

[医療](医療.md) (いりょう) is medical care. It is related, but treatment and the wider work of preserving public or school health are not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

保健 often appears in educational, public-health, and institutional expressions. It is not a general replacement for "I am healthy": 健康 describes that state. Do not confuse the sound ほけん with 保険, insurance, a different word and spelling.
