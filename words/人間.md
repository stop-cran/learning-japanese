# 人間 (にんげん) — human being

## Meaning

人間 is a noun for a human, a person as a kind of creature: 人間らしい (humane, human-like), 人間関係 にんげんかんけい (human relations), 人間は考える動物だ (humans are thinking animals).
Read じんかん, it is a separate dictionary word for "the world"; that reading does not replace にんげん in these examples.

- 人間は言葉を使って考えを伝えます。 (にんげんはことばをつかってかんがえをつたえます。) - Humans communicate ideas using language.
- 彼は信頼できる人間です。 (かれはしんらいできるにんげんです。) - He is a person you can trust.

The first example discusses humans generally; the second describes an individual.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) (person) plus [間](../kanji/間.md) (between) offers a useful association with people living among other people, not a literal definition or a historical derivation. The reading にんげん uses the on readings にん (人) and げん (間, the voiced form of ケン; not カン), as opposed to 人 じん in words like 外国人.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: [人](人.md) ひと (person), [人類](人類.md) じんるい (humankind), 人物 じんぶつ (a person, a character).
- Contrast: [動物](動物.md) どうぶつ (animal), [神](神.md) かみ (god).

## Distinctive meaning

人間 stresses "human" as opposed to nonhuman animals, machines or gods; 人 is the plain "person". 人間性 にんげんせい means human nature or character.
The English gloss "man" can mean humankind here, not specifically a male adult.
