# 人間 (にんげん) — human being

## Meaning

人間 is a human, a person as a kind of creature: 人間らしい (humane, human-like), 人間関係 にんげんかんけい (human relations), 人間は考える動物だ (humans are thinking animals).
Read じんかん, it is a literary word for "the human world".

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) (person) plus [間](../kanji/間.md) (between): historically "the space between people", that is, the human world, later the people in it. The reading にんげん uses the on readings にん (人) and げん (間, the voiced form of ケン; not カン), as opposed to 人 じん in words like 外国人.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 人 ひと (person), 人類 じんるい (humankind), 人物 じんぶつ (a person, a character).
- Contrast: 動物 どうぶつ (animal), 神 かみ (god).

## Distinctive meaning

人間 stresses "human" as opposed to animals, machines or gods; 人 is the plain "person". 人間性 にんげんせい means human nature or character.
