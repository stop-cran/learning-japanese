# 私鉄 (してつ) - private railway

## Meaning

私鉄 is a private railway, or a private railway company or its network in context. It is a noun used when distinguishing railway operators.

- この駅には、私鉄の電車が止まります。
  (このえきには、してつのでんしゃがとまります。) - Trains operated by a private railway stop at this station.
- 毎朝、私鉄を利用して通勤しています。
  (まいあさ、してつをりようしてつうきんしています。) - I commute on a private railway every morning.

In ordinary Japanese transport discussion, 私鉄 is often contrasted with **JR**. JR companies succeeded the former national railway; the conventional JR/私鉄 distinction continues even though JR companies are not simply present-day national railways.

## How the kanji combine

[私](../kanji/私.md) contributes private rather than public ownership, and [鉄](../kanji/鉄.md) stands for railway, as in 鉄道. This is a modifier + head compound. The on readings are シ + テツ: してつ, not the independent pronoun reading わたし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鉄道](鉄道.md) (てつどう) is railway or rail transport generally. 私鉄 classifies the operator, whereas [地下鉄](地下鉄.md) (ちかてつ) identifies an underground railway and [電車](電車.md) (でんしゃ) the train. These are different distinctions, not mutually exclusive transport categories.

国鉄 (こくてつ), "national railway," also names the former Japanese National Railways in historical discussion.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 私鉄 as a train privately owned by an individual, or assume it is a luxury service. Nor should the literal components be used to classify every modern privately owned railway company without regard to the conventional JR contrast. The operator and local naming practice matter.
