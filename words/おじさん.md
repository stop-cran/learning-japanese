# おじさん

## Meaning

おじさん means an uncle, and it can also refer familiarly to a middle-aged or older man who is not a relative.

- おじさんの家に行きました。 (おじさんの いえに いきました。) — I went to my uncle's house.
- あのおじさんは近所の人です。 (あの おじさんは きんじょの ひとです。) — That man is a neighbor.

## How the kanji combine

Kana avoids choosing among several spellings. 伯父さん identifies an uncle older than one's parent; 叔父さん identifies a younger one. 小父さん is a spelling for an unrelated man. All are read おじさん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

おばさん means aunt or a middle-aged woman. おじいさん, with a long い sound, means grandfather or an elderly man. Keep that pronunciation difference clear.

## Distinctive meaning

The familiar address is not automatically polite just because it ends in さん. Calling an unfamiliar adult おじさん can sound overly familiar or unwelcome; すみません is often safer for getting a stranger's attention. When formally describing your own uncle to outsiders, おじ is useful.
