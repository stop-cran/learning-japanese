# 冶金 (やきん) — metallurgy

## Meaning

冶金 is metallurgy: the science and technology of extracting metals from raw
materials and working them into useful materials and forms. It includes more
than simply melting something. It is a technical noun.

- 大学で冶金を学んでいます。
  (だいがくでやきんをまなんでいます。) — I am studying metallurgy at university.
- 冶金について研究しています。
  (やきんについてけんきゅうしています。) — I am doing research on metallurgy.

冶金を学ぶ (やきんをまなぶ) means "study metallurgy";
冶金について (やきんについて) means "about metallurgy."

## How the kanji combine

[冶](../kanji/冶.md) (ヤ) contributes smelting or working metal.
[金](../kanji/金.md) (キン) means metal here, not money or gold alone.
The relationship is action plus object: working metal. The whole noun names
the technical field, not just one operation. This is a meaning aid, not an
account of the word's historical formation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

冶金学 (やきんがく) specifically names metallurgy as a science.
[金属](金属.md) (きんぞく) means the material "metal," not the field that studies
and processes it. 製錬 and 精錬 (both せいれん) concern smelting or refining:
related processes rather than the whole discipline.
There is no useful everyday opposite of the field's name.

## Distinctive meaning

冶金 belongs in scientific, educational and industrial contexts. It does not
mean making money, and it is not limited to gold. 錬金術 (れんきんじゅつ),
"alchemy," is a different term, not a synonym for modern metallurgy.

Use [溶かす](溶かす.md) (とかす) when describing an action such as melting
butter or dissolving sugar; 冶金 names the metalworking field and its technology,
not any act of making something liquid.
