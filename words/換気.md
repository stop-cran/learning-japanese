# 換気 (かんき) - ventilation; airing a room

## Meaning

換気 is ventilation: replacing indoor air with outside air.
換気する means to ventilate or air a space.

- 窓を開けて換気しました。
  (まどをあけてかんきしました。) - I opened the window to air the room.
- この部屋は換気が必要です。
  (このへやはかんきがひつようです。) - This room needs ventilation.

部屋の換気 (へやのかんき) means "ventilation of a room."
The first example states the method with 窓を開けて,
"by opening the window."

## How the kanji combine

[換](../kanji/換.md) contributes exchange or replacement, and
[気](../kanji/気.md) contributes air here.
The combination suggests exchanging the air, not changing one's mood.
The on readings are カン + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[空気](空気.md) (くうき) names air itself.
[換える](換える.md) (かえる) is a broader verb for exchanging or replacing.
換気 identifies a particular air-exchange activity rather than any exchange.

## Distinctive meaning

Ventilation can be natural or mechanical; opening a window is one method,
not the definition of the word.
換気 does not by itself mean heating, cooling, or guaranteeing clean air.
