# そんな — that sort of

## Meaning

そんな means "that sort of" or "like that." It often refers to something the listener has said or to an idea already in the conversation. Place it directly before a noun.

そんな話は初めて聞きました。 (そんなはなしははじめてききました。) — This is the first time I've heard a story like that.

## How the kanji combine

そんな is a fixed kana form in the こんな・そんな・あんな・どんな series. It is not an ordinary な-adjective: do not add another な or use そん as its predicate form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

その話 means "that story"; そんな話 means "a story of that kind." [あんな](あんな.md) presents a kind treated as more distant, while こんな means "this kind of."

## Distinctive meaning

For degree, use そんなに: そんなに急がなくてもいいです (そんなにいそがなくてもいいです), "You don't have to hurry so much." Alone, そんな！ can express shocked disbelief, but that reaction should not be mistaken for the word's basic noun-modifying meaning.
