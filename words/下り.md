# 下り (くだり) - descent; downbound train

## Meaning

下り is a noun for a descent or downward-going route. It also names a downbound train or direction.
It can modify a noun with の, as in 下りの坂 (くだりのさか), "a downhill slope."
It also occurs directly in fixed compounds such as 下り坂 (くだりざか), "downhill slope,"
and 下り列車 (くだりれっしゃ), "downbound train."

- 下り坂では、ゆっくり歩きましょう。 (くだりざかでは、ゆっくりあるきましょう。) - Let's walk slowly on the downhill slope.
- 次の下り列車は十時に出ます。 (つぎのくだりれっしゃはじゅうじにでます。) - The next downbound train leaves at ten.

For trains, 下り means toward the designated ending point of the route. On many routes this is away from Tokyo, but "away from Tokyo" is not a universal definition.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) contributes the downward direction and is read くだ before り. 下り is the noun form associated with 下る (くだる), "go down." The り is part of this word, not a particle.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上り (のぼり), "ascent; upbound," is the contrasting direction. [下がる](下がる.md) (さがる) is a verb for going down or decreasing, not a replacement for the train-direction noun.

## Distinctive meaning

A 下り train is not necessarily moving downhill at the moment. The railway label follows the route's designated orientation, whereas 下り坂 in the first example describes a physical slope. Check destinations and route information rather than inferring compass direction or elevation from 下り alone.
