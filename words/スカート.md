# スカート — skirt

## Meaning

スカート is a skirt. To say that someone puts one on or wears one, use はく or its resulting-state form はいている.

- 妹は青いスカートをはいています。 (いもうとはあおいすかーとをはいています。) — My younger sister is wearing a blue skirt.
- このスカートは少し長いです。 (このすかーとはすこしながいです。) — This skirt is a little long.

The garment takes を with はく. The noun does not conjugate and does not change for plural reference.

## How the kanji combine

スカート is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "skirt." The ー lengthens カ; keep that long vowel when reading it aloud.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ズボン means trousers. ワンピース means a one-piece dress in this clothing context, not a skirt alone. These name different garments rather than opposite qualities.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese chooses different wearing verbs for different items. スカートをはく is the useful pattern for this lower-body garment; do not automatically use 着る (きる) for every English sentence containing "wear."
