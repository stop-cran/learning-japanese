# 解剖 (かいぼう) — dissection; autopsy; analysis

## Meaning

解剖 means dissection: cutting open a body to examine its internal structure. Depending on the purpose and context, it can mean an autopsy or postmortem examination. It is not limited to finding a cause of death.

- 医学生は解剖で体の構造を学びます。 (いがくせいはかいぼうでからだのこうぞうをまなびます。) — Medical students learn the body's structure through dissection.

It also means analyzing something in detail to clarify its structure or causal relationships. Digital Daijisen gives the figurative example 事件を解剖する (じけんをかいぼうする), "analyze a case."

解剖 is a noun and a transitive する verb: **Nを解剖する** means to dissect or analyze N. The object and context distinguish physical dissection from figurative analysis.

## How the kanji combine

[解](../kanji/解.md) contributes undoing or separating, and [剖](../kanji/剖.md) contributes splitting open or dissecting. The related actions combine in the idea of opening something up to examine it. This is a meaning aid, not a reconstruction of the word's history. The on readings are カイ + ボウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[分析](分析.md) ぶんせき is analysis and does not imply a physical cut. It overlaps the figurative sense of 解剖. [分解](分解.md) ぶんかい means taking apart or breaking down into components and also has an analysis sense.

[検査](検査.md) けんさ is inspection, examination or testing more generally. A medical test is not automatically 解剖: 解剖 specifically brings in dissection in its physical sense. There is no single everyday opposite covering both the physical and figurative senses.

## Distinctive meaning

In medical or biological contexts, 解剖 identifies dissection or an autopsy, not every examination of a patient. In figurative use, it presents detailed analysis as opening something up to understand how it works.

Distinguish 解剖学 (かいぼうがく), "anatomy," the field of study, from a particular dissection or autopsy.
