# よく (よく) — often; well

## Meaning

よく has two important everyday uses: doing something often, and doing it well, properly, or thoroughly. It modifies the predicate rather than taking な or の.

- 週末はよく映画を見ます。 (しゅうまつはよくえいがをみます。) — I often watch movies at the weekend.
- 説明をよく読んでください。 (せつめいをよくよんでください。) — Please read the explanation carefully.

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is usual. 良く is a recognized spelling associated with the "well" sense; other dictionary spellings also exist. In the related adjective paradigm, よく is the adverbial form of 良い (よい), also commonly pronounced いい in its dictionary form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

しばしば also means often. 上手に (じょうずに) means skillfully, so it overlaps with one use but not with frequency. たまに, "occasionally," contrasts with the frequent-action meaning.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes frequency from quality: よく眠れました (よくねむれました) normally means "I slept well," not "I slept often." With a negative, よく分かりません (よくわかりません) means "I don't really understand / don't understand well." Do not assume the English translation must always be "often."
