# つかつか — walking briskly and directly

## Meaning

つかつか describes walking forward briskly and without hesitation.

- 彼女は受付までつかつか歩いていった。
  — She walked briskly straight to the reception desk.
- 彼は迷わず壇上につかつか進み、話し始めた。
  — He strode straight onto the stage without hesitation and began speaking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

つかつか conveys direct, purposeful steps, unlike a relaxed stroll.

## Distinctive meaning

It often conveys purposeful, direct steps rather than a relaxed stroll.
