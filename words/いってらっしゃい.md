# いってらっしゃい - take care when heading out

## Meaning

いってらっしゃい is said to someone setting out, often in reply to
いってきます. Depending on the situation, English uses "Take care,"
"Have a good day," or "See you when you get back."

- A: いってきます。 - I am off.
- B: いってらっしゃい。気をつけてね。 (いってらっしゃい。きをつけてね。) - Take care. Be careful!

The departing person says いってきます; the person seeing them off says
いってらっしゃい. The reply can also be used without hearing the first
formula explicitly.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. The recognized spelling
[行](../kanji/行.md)ってらっしゃい uses the same character as
[行く](行く.md) (いく), "go." This is a complete greeting to learn as a unit,
not an ordinary instruction to conjugate afresh for each departure.

## Synonyms and antonyms

気をつけて (きをつけて), "be careful; take care," can accompany the greeting,
as in the dialogue. おかえりなさい, "welcome back," belongs to the return
rather than the departure.

## Distinctive meaning

The phrase suits seeing someone off with an expected return, such as someone
leaving home for work or school. It is not a universal translation of "goodbye"
for any farewell, and it is not the departing person's announcement.
The English "have a good day" is a contextual rendering, not a restriction
to departures early in the morning.
