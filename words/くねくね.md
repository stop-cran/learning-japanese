# くねくね — winding; wriggling and twisting

## Meaning

くねくね describes repeated twists or curves, often in something that is moving or bending.

- ミミズが土の上をくねくね進んでいった。
  — An earthworm wriggled along the soil.
- 坂道は山の中をくねくねと続いている。
  — The mountain road winds through the hills.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[うねうね](うねうね.md) also describes a winding shape. くねくね often makes the repeated
twisting movement more vivid, while うねうね can emphasize a winding course or shape.

## Distinctive meaning

The word may describe a path or a moving body; the surrounding subject identifies which. It does
not specify whether the movement is slow or fast.
