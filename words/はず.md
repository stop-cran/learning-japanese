# はず - expected to; should be so

## Meaning

はず expresses an expectation supported by what you know: something should be true or is expected to happen. The usual predicate is はずだ or polite はずです.

田中さんはもう家にいるはずです。 (たなかさんはもういえにいるはずです。) - Tanaka should already be at home.

Attach はず to a plain-form verb or i-adjective; for na-adjectives and nouns, the nonpast affirmative patterns are na-adjective + な and noun + の: 来るはず (くるはず), 高いはず (たかいはず), 静かなはず (しずかなはず), 休みのはず (やすみのはず).

今日は休みのはずです。 (きょうはやすみのはずです。) - Today is supposed to be a day off.

## How the kanji combine

The grammatical use is normally written in kana. 筈 is its kanji spelling, but this construction should be learned as a whole rather than through the character's other meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

たぶん means "probably" without by itself naming the basis of the expectation. べき expresses what someone ought to do; はず is not generally advice or an obligation.

## Distinctive meaning

An expectation can prove wrong. 来ないはずです (こないはずです) means "I expect they won't come," while 来るはずがありません (くるはずがありません) means "There is no way they will come." The placement of the negative changes what is being asserted.
