# もじもじ — fidgeting bashfully or hesitantly

## Meaning

もじもじ describes fidgeting or hesitating because of shyness, uncertainty, or discomfort.

- 彼女は質問したそうに、もじもじしていた。
  — She was fidgeting as if she wanted to ask a question.
- 子どもは知らない人の前で、もじもじと答えた。
  — The child answered hesitantly in front of a stranger.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もじもじ conveys visible hesitation or restlessness, unlike speaking or acting confidently.

## Distinctive meaning

It can describe both fidgeting behavior and hesitant speech.
