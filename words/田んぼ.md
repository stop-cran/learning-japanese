# 田んぼ (たんぼ) - rice paddy

## Meaning

田んぼ is a rice field or rice paddy. It is a noun for the field, not a
general word for every farm or everything grown on one.

- 田んぼに水を入れます。 (たんぼにみずをいれます。) - We put water into the rice paddy.
- 道の両側に田んぼが広がっています。 (みちのりょうがわにたんぼがひろがっています。) - Rice fields stretch out on both sides of the road.

に marks the destination of the water in the first sentence. A rice field
does not stop being a 田んぼ whenever it is temporarily drained.

## How the kanji combine

[田](../kanji/田.md) contributes the rice-field meaning. Learn the whole
native word たんぼ; the written んぼ is not a general suffix to attach to
other crop names. 田圃 is another recorded spelling for たんぼ, marked ateji
in JMdict. It does not establish an independent productive 圃 = ぼ rule.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[田](田.md) (た) also names a rice field. [畑](畑.md) (はたけ) normally
contrasts as a dry field for crops rather than a flooded rice paddy.
[耕地](耕地.md) (こうち) is cultivated land more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

The community source row writes 田ぼ with reading たんぼ. In the pinned
JMdict family, **田ぼ is search-only**, while the reading たんぼ is expressly
restricted to 田んぼ and 田圃. This article normalizes the source to 田んぼ;
it does not present 田ぼ/たんぼ as an unrestricted recommended pair.
The rare spelling 田畝 is tied to older でんぽ/でんぼ readings in that
snapshot, not to たんぼ.
