# 心理 (しんり) - state of mind; psychology

## Meaning

心理 is a person's state of mind or the mental processes behind a reaction or action. It is a noun: Nの心理 means "N's psychology/state of mind." 心理的な (しんりてきな) means "psychological."

- 子どもの心理を理解したいです。 (こどものしんりをりかいしたいです。) - I want to understand children's psychology.
- 試験の前は心理的な負担が大きいです。 (しけんのまえはしんりてきなふたんがおおきいです。) - The psychological burden is considerable before an exam.

The second example concerns mental strain rather than the physical weight of something.

## How the kanji combine

[心](../kanji/心.md) contributes "mind; heart," and [理](../kanji/理.md) contributes principles or workings. The combination points to the workings of the mind. The on readings are シン + リ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[気持ち](気持ち.md) (きもち) directly names feelings. 心理 can draw attention to the mental process behind them. [性格](性格.md) (せいかく) concerns character or personality, rather than just the state someone is in now.

## Distinctive meaning

心理 does not necessarily mean a disorder or an unconscious motive. It can describe ordinary thinking and reactions, including group behavior. 心理学 (しんりがく), with 学 added, specifically names psychology as an academic discipline. A person's 心理 and the study of 心理学 should therefore not be treated as identical grammatical expressions.
