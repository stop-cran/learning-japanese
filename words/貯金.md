# 貯金 (ちょきん) - savings; putting money aside

## Meaning

貯金 is a noun for savings or putting money aside. As a transitive する verb, it means to save money or make a deposit.

- 毎月、一万円を貯金しています。 (まいつき、いちまんえんをちょきんしています。) - I save ten thousand yen every month.
- 旅行のために貯金を使いました。 (りょこうのためにちょきんをつかいました。) - I used my savings for a trip.

The first example names the action; the second names the accumulated money. お金を貯金する marks the money with を. 貯金がある (ちょきんがある) means having savings.

## How the kanji combine

貯 means store or accumulate, and [金](../kanji/金.md) means money here. The compound expresses storing money for later use. The on readings are チョ + キン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[節約](節約.md) (せつやく) means economizing or using fewer resources. You can 節約する without actually putting the difference into savings. [預ける](預ける.md) (あずける) means entrusting or depositing something and is broader than money. [使う](使う.md) (つかう), "spend; use," provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

貯金 is not restricted to a particular kind of bank account. 小銭を貯金箱に入れる (こぜにをちょきんばこにいれる), "put coins in a savings box," shows the same saving idea outside a bank. Conversely, an account balance is not necessarily money intentionally set aside for the future; use context rather than assuming a financial product from the word.
