# 帰宅 (きたく) - returning home

## Meaning

帰宅 is returning or going home. The noun forms the intransitive verb 帰宅する: the person going home is the subject. A time expression can indicate when they arrive.

- 昨日は九時に帰宅しました。 (きのうはくじにきたくしました。) - Yesterday I got home at nine.
- 帰宅したら、電話してください。 (きたくしたら、でんわしてください。) - Please call me when you get home.

The past form inside 帰宅したら refers here to completing a future return home; the request makes the time relationship clear.

## How the kanji combine

[帰](../kanji/帰.md) means "return," and [宅](../kanji/宅.md) supplies "home; residence." This action-and-destination combination already includes the homeward destination. The on readings are キ + タク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[帰る](帰る.md) (かえる) is broader: it can describe returning to one's country or another base. 家に帰る (いえにかえる) is a direct everyday equivalent of 帰宅する. [戻る](戻る.md) (もどる) emphasizes going back, without necessarily going home.

## Distinctive meaning

帰宅 fits schedules, reports, and conversation about arriving home; it is not limited to an official setting. It does not mean bringing another person home, nor returning to any arbitrary location. 帰宅時間 (きたくじかん) means the time of returning home, not the duration of a holiday trip.
