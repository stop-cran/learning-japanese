# 契約 (けいやく) - contract; agreement

## Meaning

契約 is a noun for a contract or agreement and takes する for entering into one. Use 相手と契約する for contracting with another party, or 契約を結ぶ (けいやくをむすぶ), "conclude a contract."

- その会社と一年間の契約を結びました。 (そのかいしゃといちねんかんのけいやくをむすびました。) - I entered into a one-year contract with that company.
- 契約する前に条件を確認してください。 (けいやくするまえにじょうけんをかくにんしてください。) - Please check the terms before signing up.

契約書 (けいやくしょ) is the contract document. 契約の条件 (けいやくのじょうけん) means its terms, and 契約期間 (けいやくきかん) its duration.

## How the kanji combine

契 contributes pledging or agreeing, while [約](../kanji/約.md) contributes a promise or undertaking. The two reinforce the idea of an agreed commitment. This explains the compound's meaning without deriving modern contract rules from the characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[約束](約束.md) (やくそく), "promise; arrangement," also covers personal commitments such as meeting a friend. 契約 points to an agreement with defined terms. [予約](予約.md) (よやく) is a reservation; booking a table is not expressed simply by replacing 予約 with 契約.

## Distinctive meaning

契約 is the agreement, not necessarily the sheet of paper. When the document itself matters, say 契約書. The word alone does not establish the agreement's exact obligations, length, or legal validity; those come from the actual terms and context. A service subscription can also be a 契約.
