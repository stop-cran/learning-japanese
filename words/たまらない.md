# たまらない - unbearable; irresistible

## Meaning

たまらない describes something unbearable or irresistible. It behaves like an い-adjective expression: たまらなかった is past, and たまりません is polite. It can also intensify a feeling after a て-form.

- この暑さはたまらない。 (このあつさはたまらない。) - This heat is unbearable.
- 試験の結果が心配でたまりません。 (しけんのけっかがしんぱいでたまりません。) - I am terribly worried about the exam results.

For an い-adjective, use its くて form: うれしくてたまらない, "so happy I can hardly contain it." For a な-adjective, use で as in 心配でたまらない. A desire can use たい → たくて: 会いたくてたまらない (あいたくてたまらない), "desperately want to see someone."

## How the kanji combine

The expression is usually written in kana; 堪らない is a recorded kanji spelling. Its negative-looking ending contributes the idea that one cannot endure or contain the experience. It does not make every use emotionally negative.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[我慢](我慢.md) (がまん), "endurance; putting up with something," helps explain the unbearable sense. [辛い](辛い.md), read つらい for "painful; hard," can describe hardship without the same emphasis on overwhelming degree.

## Distinctive meaning

Pleasure as well as discomfort can be たまらない: この香りがたまらない (このかおりがたまらない) can mean "this aroma is irresistible." Read the whole situation. The て-form construction intensifies a feeling or urge; it is not a general command meaning "must do."
