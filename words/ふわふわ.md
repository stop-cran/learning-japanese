# ふわふわ — fluffy; floating lightly

## Meaning

Light, airy softness, or something floating and moving without firm support. It can also suggest an unsteady or unfocused state.

- このケーキはふわふわしていて、口の中でほどける。 — This cake is fluffy and falls apart in your mouth.
- 白い羽（はね）がふわふわと舞い落ちた（まいおちた）。 — A white feather floated gently down.
- 熱があって、頭がふわふわする。 — I have a fever, and my head feels light and unsteady.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ふかふか](ふかふか.md) emphasizes deep, yielding softness. [もこもこ](もこもこ.md) suggests bulky, rounded fluff or lumps. [もちもち](もちもち.md) is elastic and chewy, not airy.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ふわふわしたパン, ふわふわの毛, and ふわふわと浮く（うく）. ふわふわする can describe texture or a subjective unsettled sensation; it does not establish a medical diagnosis. Figurative descriptions of someone's attitude can imply lack of seriousness or a firm plan, so not every use is complimentary.
