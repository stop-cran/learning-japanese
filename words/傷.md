# 傷 (きず) - wound; scratch

## Meaning

傷 is a wound or injury on the body, or a scratch, chip, or similar damage on an object. Useful patterns include **Nに傷がある**, "N has a wound or scratch," and 傷が治る (きずがなおる), "a wound heals."

- 指の傷が治りました。 (ゆびのきずがなおりました。) - The wound on my finger has healed.
- 机に小さな傷があります。 (つくえにちいさなきずがあります。) - There is a small scratch on the desk.

The noun itself does not identify what caused the damage. Its location and the surrounding description help distinguish a cut, scrape, or other mark.

## How the kanji combine

傷 is a single kanji with the native reading きず. The word needs no okurigana. Kana キズ is also recorded; other kanji spellings in the dictionary are marked rare.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怪我](怪我.md) (けが) refers to an injury or being injured. 傷 can point to the resulting damaged spot and also applies to objects. [痛み](痛み.md) (いたみ) is pain, which may accompany a wound but is not the wound itself.

## Distinctive meaning

心の傷 (こころのきず), "an emotional wound," extends the damage image to hurt feelings. Do not assume every 傷 is visibly bleeding or that every experience of pain leaves a visible 傷.
