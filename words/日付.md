# 日付 (ひづけ) - calendar date

## Meaning

日付 is a calendar date, including a date written on a document. It is a noun. Use 日付を書く for "write the date," 日付を確認する for "check the date," and 日付が変わる when the calendar moves to a new day.

- ここに今日の日付を書いてください。 (ここにきょうのひづけをかいてください。) - Please write today's date here.
- 帰ったときには、日付が変わっていました。 (かえったときには、ひづけがかわっていました。) - By the time I got home, it was already the next day.

The second example describes crossing midnight; it does not mean that someone edited a document.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) contributes day, and [付](../kanji/付.md) contributes attaching or marking. Together they suggest a day attached to a record. The native reading is ひづけ, with づ, not ひずけ in the kana spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

日時 (にちじ) includes both date and time. [曜日](曜日.md) (ようび) identifies a weekday, such as Monday, rather than the numbered date on the calendar.

## Distinctive meaning

English "date" can also mean a romantic appointment. That meaning is [デート](デート.md), not 日付. When completing a form, the requested date format may include a year as well as month and day; 日付 itself does not impose a particular order or writing system. There is no useful direct antonym.
