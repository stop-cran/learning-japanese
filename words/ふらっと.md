# ふらっと — casually or by chance

## Meaning

ふらっと describes going somewhere casually, without much planning, or by chance.

- 散歩の途中で、ふらっとパン屋に立ち寄った。
  — While out walking, I casually stopped by a bakery.
- 休日は目的を決めず、ふらっと街を歩くのが好きだ。
  — On days off, I like to wander around town without a set destination.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ふらっと suggests an unplanned, casual visit rather than a carefully scheduled trip.

## Distinctive meaning

The word also has an unsteady or dizzy sense in other contexts; these examples focus on casual movement.
