# 理解 (りかい) — understanding

## Meaning

理解 is understanding or comprehension. It combines with する, and what is understood takes を.

- 説明を理解できました。 (せつめいをりかいできました。) — I was able to understand the explanation.
- この言葉の意味を理解しています。 (このことばのいみをりかいしています。) — I understand the meaning of this word.

## How the kanji combine

[理](../kanji/理.md) concerns reason or the way something makes sense;
[解](../kanji/解.md) contributes resolving or understanding. They reinforce the idea of
grasping how something fits together. The on readings are リ + カイ.
This is a meaning connection for the compound, not a literal instruction to "untie a reason."

## Synonyms and antonyms

わかる, also written [分かる](../words/分かる.md) (わかる), overlaps in meaning. Notice the useful pattern change:
意味を理解する (いみをりかいする) and 意味がわかる (いみがわかる) both concern understanding meaning.
[誤解](../words/誤解.md) (ごかい) is misunderstanding: an interpretation has gone wrong.

## Distinctive meaning

Understanding a person's view is not the same as agreeing with it.
[賛成](../words/賛成.md) (さんせい) concerns agreement or support. A learner can understand the content
of an argument without supporting its conclusion, so do not translate 理解 automatically as "agreement."
