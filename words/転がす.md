# 転がす (ころがす) - roll something along; tip over

## Meaning

転がす means making something roll, or tipping or throwing it over. It is a transitive godan verb: 転がします, 転がして, 転がした.

- 子供に向かってボールを転がしました。 (こどもにむかってボールをころがしました。) - I rolled the ball toward the child.
- うっかりコップを転がしてしまいました。 (うっかりコップをころがしてしまいました。) - I accidentally knocked the glass over.

The object takes を. The verb describes the action on it; the action can still be accidental.

## How the kanji combine

[転](../kanji/転.md) contributes rolling or turning. In the native spelling 転がす, 転 writes ころ and がす is okurigana. This is one verb, not a compound of independently pronounced kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[転がる](転がる.md) (ころがる) is intransitive: ボールが転がる, "the ball rolls," versus ボールを転がす, "roll the ball." [倒す](倒す.md) (たおす) overlaps in tipping or knocking something over.

[ころころ](ころころ.md) and [ごろごろ](ごろごろ.md) can describe the manner of rolling. They do not replace the verb's transitive grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

Rolling an object along is not the same operation as winding or rolling up paper with [巻く](巻く.md) (まく). Learn the complete action, not just English "roll."

The dictionary also records casual driving, leaving something lying about, and resale-for-profit uses. This article's examples select physical rolling and tipping rather than claiming that every extended use requires a visibly rotating object.
