# あいさつ (あいさつ) — greeting

## Meaning

あいさつ is a greeting; あいさつする means **to greet someone**. The person greeted can take に. The word covers expressions used on meeting and parting, not only saying hello.

- 先生にあいさつしました。 (せんせいにあいさつしました。) — I greeted the teacher.
- 朝のあいさつは大切です。 (あさのあいさつはたいせつです。) — Morning greetings are important.

する supplies the verb forms: あいさつします and あいさつした. The noun by itself does not conjugate.

## How the kanji combine

The kanji spelling is 挨拶, read あいさつ as a Chinese-derived compound. This page uses the accessible kana spelling; learners can recognize the whole word without decomposing the unfamiliar characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

おはようございます is one particular greeting. 自己紹介 (じこしょうかい) means a self-introduction, which gives information about yourself rather than merely greeting someone. Neither is an opposite of あいさつ.

## Distinctive meaning

あいさつ names a social act or its words. It can also mean a short formal address, such as a greeting speech at an event; an ordinary daily greeting need not be a speech.

For the address sense: 校長が開会のあいさつをしました (こうちょうがかいかいのあいさつをしました),
"The principal gave an opening address." Here あいさつ is not just a brief hello.
