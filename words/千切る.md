# 千切る (ちぎる) - tear off; rip to pieces

## Meaning

千切る, usually written **ちぎる**, means tearing something into pieces
or tearing, picking, or plucking part of it off.
The object takes を.

- 紙を小さくちぎりました。
  (かみをちいさくちぎりました。) - I tore the paper into small pieces.
- パンを手でちぎって食べました。
  (パンをてでちぎってたべました。) - I tore pieces off the bread by hand and ate them.

The verb is godan despite ending in る:
ちぎります, ちぎった, ちぎって, and ちぎらない.
花をちぎる (はなをちぎる) can mean plucking a flower.

## How the kanji combine

[千](../kanji/千.md) and [切](../kanji/切.md) occur in the recorded spelling
千切る. Learn the established whole reading ちぎる.
"Into many little pieces" is a memory aid, not a claim that the action
produces exactly a thousand cuts or that 千 has the productive reading ちぎ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[破る](破る.md) (やぶる) and [裂く](裂く.md) (さく) overlap in tearing.
[切る](切る.md) (きる) is broader cutting; a knife-cutting instruction
such as finely chopping food is not automatically best translated with ちぎる.
ちぎれる, "be torn off; come apart," is the intransitive comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

The usual picture is separation by tearing or pulling off, not necessarily
neatly sliced pieces. Keep this apart from the homophone 契る (ちぎる),
"pledge; vow."
Dictionaries also record a bound intensifier after a verb's ます-stem,
as in ほめちぎる, "praise lavishly." Learn attested combinations;
this is not permission to attach ちぎる to every verb.
