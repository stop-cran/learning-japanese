# 政治家 (せいじか) — politician

## Meaning

政治家 is a politician. It names a political role without itself praising or condemning the person.

- 彼女は政治家になりました。 (かのじょは せいじかに なりました。) — She became a politician.
- その政治家の意見には賛成できません。  
  (その せいじかの いけんには さんせいできません。)  
  — I cannot agree with that politician's opinion.

## How the kanji combine

[政治](政治.md) せいじ is politics: [政](../kanji/政.md) and [治](../kanji/治.md) both contribute
governing or administering. [家](../kanji/家.md) カ adds a person active in that field.
It is field + practitioner, with on readings セイ + ジ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

政治屋 (せいじや) is a disapproving label suggesting politics pursued for personal advantage, not
an equally neutral alternative. 議員 (ぎいん) is a member of an assembly; not every political role
is that specific office. There is no natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

家 here does not mean a house or family. Nor does it certify moral virtue: a speaker can criticize
someone while still using the ordinary word 政治家.

For 政治家 compared with 政治屋, and why 屋 is not always derogatory, see
[occupation and role endings](../articles/occupation-and-role-endings.md).
