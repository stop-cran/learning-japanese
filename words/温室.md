# 温室 (おんしつ) - greenhouse; hothouse

## Meaning

温室 is a greenhouse or hothouse: an enclosed growing space that protects plants and helps maintain suitable conditions. Use 温室で for an activity inside it and 温室の中 (おんしつのなか) for its interior.

- 温室でトマトを育てています。 (おんしつでトマトをそだてています。) - We are growing tomatoes in a greenhouse.
- この温室では冬も花を育てています。 (このおんしつではふゆもはなをそだてています。) - They grow flowers in this greenhouse in winter too.

## How the kanji combine

[温](../kanji/温.md), "warm," modifies [室](../kanji/室.md), "room; chamber." The on readings オン + シツ give おんしつ. The warm enclosure is a useful association for the plant-growing building.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ビニールハウス (びにーるはうす) names a plastic-covered growing structure. It is a related kind of protected growing space, not a guarantee that every 温室 is covered with plastic.

## Distinctive meaning

温室 is not the general word for any warm room. A heated bedroom does not become a 温室 merely because it is comfortable. The word names the growing facility; it does not itself specify a particular heating system or temperature.
