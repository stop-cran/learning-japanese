# 家庭 (かてい) - household; home life

## Meaning

家庭 means the home or household as a setting for family life. It often concerns how people live together, rather than the building alone.

家庭で日本語を話します。 (かていでにほんごをはなします。) - We speak Japanese at home.

家庭の事情で休みました。 (かていのじじょうでやすみました。) - I took time off because of family circumstances.

## How the kanji combine

[家](../kanji/家.md), "house; family," combines with [庭](../kanji/庭.md), "garden; courtyard." Their on readings form かてい. The modern word means the household, not specifically a house's garden.

## Synonyms and antonyms

家族 (かぞく) refers to family members as a group. 家 (いえ) can mean the house or home. 世帯 (せたい) is a household viewed as a unit, for example in administrative counts.

## Distinctive meaning

家庭 is useful in compounds such as 家庭料理 (かていりょうり), "home cooking," and in discussions of domestic life. It is not normally the destination word in an everyday "I'm going home"; 家に帰る (いえにかえる) is the straightforward expression.
