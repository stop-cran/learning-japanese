# リズム - rhythm

## Meaning

リズム is rhythm: a pattern of beats or recurring movement.
It is used in music and also in expressions such as
生活のリズム (せいかつのリズム), "the rhythm of daily life."
It is a noun.

- 音楽のリズムに合わせて踊ります。 (おんがくのリズムにあわせておどります。) - I dance to the rhythm of the music.
- 夜更かしで生活のリズムが崩れました。 (よふかしでせいかつのリズムがくずれました。) - Staying up late disrupted my daily rhythm.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[調子](調子.md) (ちょうし) includes a rhythm or musical-pattern sense,
as well as other senses such as condition or tone.
[テンポ](テンポ.md) concerns tempo, the speed of the music.
拍子 (ひょうし) can name musical time or a beat.

## Distinctive meaning

Changing the speed and changing the pattern of beats are different changes,
so リズム is not simply another name for テンポ.
The daily-life expression concerns a recurring pattern, not necessarily a sound.
