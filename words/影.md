# 影 (かげ) - shadow; silhouette

## Meaning

影 is a shadow, silhouette, or visible figure. It can also mean a reflection or
image. This is a noun: 影が見える (かげがみえる), "a shadow/figure is visible."
Context tells you whether something blocks light or appears as an image.

- 壁に猫の影が映っています。 (かべにねこのかげがうつっています。) - The cat's shadow is showing on the wall.
- 水面に山の影が映っていました。 (すいめんにやまのかげがうつっていました。) - The mountain's reflection appeared on the water.

The first is a cast shadow; the second is a reflected image, not a dark hiding place.

## How the kanji combine

影 is a single kanji writing the native noun かげ. Learn its whole-word meaning
here rather than dividing it into smaller written shapes as if they were words.
Its on reading エイ occurs in compounds, but does not replace かげ in these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[陰](陰.md) (かげ) covers shade, a place behind something, and the background.
Compare 木の影, "a tree's shadow," with 木の陰, "the shade of/behind a tree."
English "shadow" can overlap both spellings; this is not a rule that their meanings
can never touch. [光](光.md) (ひかり), "light," is a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

For hiding behind something, use 陰 as the unmarked spelling: 木の陰に隠れる
(きのかげにかくれる), "hide behind a tree." JMdict marks 影 for this
"behind/background" sense as non-standard. The community list gives both words
a broad shade/shadow gloss; that does not erase the spelling distinction.
