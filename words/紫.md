# 紫 (むらさき) - purple; violet

## Meaning

紫 names the color purple or violet. As a noun it connects to another noun with の.
紫色 (むらさきいろ) makes the color reference explicit.

- 紫の花が咲いています。 (むらさきのはながさいています。) - Purple flowers are blooming.
- この布は紫色です。 (このぬのはむらさきいろです。) - This cloth is purple.

The same dictionary entry also records the plant purple gromwell, usually written
ムラサキ or むらさき, and 紫 as a culinary name for soy sauce.
The plant name does not mean that its flowers must be purple: Daijisen describes white flowers.

## How the kanji combine

紫 is one kanji with the native reading むらさき, not a compound assembled from two words.
The color use is connected with the dye obtained from the gromwell root.
The plant and color are related uses, not a rule for translating every occurrence as a color.

## Synonyms and antonyms

パープル and バイオレット are loanwords for purple and violet.
[色](色.md) (いろ) is the general word "color."
There is no single lexical antonym for 紫.

## Distinctive meaning

For an ordinary color description, learn 紫の + noun or 紫色.
The soy-sauce use is a specialized name, not a claim that all purple liquids are soy sauce.
Context decides whether the word names a color, the plant, or the condiment.
