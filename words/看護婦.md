# 看護婦 (かんごふ) - female nurse, older term

## Meaning

看護婦 is an older, gender-specific term for a female nurse. Learn it for recognizing older speech and writing; the current general professional term is 看護師 (かんごし), regardless of gender.

- 古い小説には「看護婦」という言葉が出てきます。 (ふるいしょうせつには「かんごふ」ということばがでてきます。) - The word "kangofu" appears in old novels.

## How the kanji combine

看護 (かんご) means nursing or care, and [婦](../kanji/婦.md), read ふ, supplies the female designation. 看 and 護 contribute to the established compound 看護 rather than naming two separate jobs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

看護師 is the useful modern replacement. 医者 (いしゃ), "doctor," is a different occupation, not an opposite or a more polite synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The female restriction is part of this word's meaning, not an implication that nursing is only women's work. JMdict marks 看護婦 obsolete and deprecated. A vocabulary list containing it therefore establishes a recognition target, not a recommendation to use it as today's default job title.
