# 競争 (きょうそう)

## Meaning

競争 is competition or rivalry. With する it means to compete, whether in a game, business, or another attempt to do better than others.

- 二つの店が競争しています。 (ふたつの みせが きょうそうしています。) — The two shops are competing.
- 友達と速さを競争しました。 (ともだちと はやさを きょうそうしました。) — I competed with a friend to see who was faster.

と marks a competitor; を can identify what is being compared.

## How the kanji combine

競 concerns competing, and [争](../kanji/争.md) concerns contesting or striving against others. Their related meanings form the on-reading compound きょう + そう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

試合 (しあい) is an organized match or game. 競争 is broader and need not be a scheduled event. 協力 (きょうりょく), "cooperation," is a useful contrast, though people can cooperate within a competitive setting.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 競争 with 競走, also きょうそう, which specifically concerns racing. 競争 can concern prices, performance, customers, or other goals, not only physical speed.
