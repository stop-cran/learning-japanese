# そちら

## Meaning

そちら refers to that direction, place, or side, often near or associated with the listener. It can also politely refer to the listener, their household, or their organization.

- そちらに座ってください。 (そちらに すわってください。) — Please sit over there.
- そちらの天気はどうですか。 (そちらの てんきは どうですか。) — How is the weather where you are?

Use ordinary noun-like particles: そちらに for a destination or location, そちらの for "your side's" or "that place's."

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual; 其方 is a recognized but uncommon spelling. The そ- beginning places it in the same demonstrative family as そこ and それ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そっち is more casual. こちら refers to this side; あちら to a side away from both participants; どちら asks which side or direction.

## Distinctive meaning

そちら is not limited to physical distance. On a telephone call it can mean "where you are," even if the listener is far away. It is more courteous than bluntly replacing every "you" with あなた, but context still determines the intended reference.
