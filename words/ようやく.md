# ようやく - finally; barely; gradually

## Meaning

ようやく is an adverb for something finally coming about after time,
waiting or difficulty. It can also emphasize only just managing to
achieve something.

- 長い話し合いの末、ようやく決まりました。 (ながいはなしあいのすえ、ようやくきまりました。) - After a long discussion, it was finally decided.
- 重い荷物をようやく持ち上げました。 (おもいにもつをようやくもちあげました。) - I only just managed to lift the heavy luggage.

Context determines whether the emphasis is the eventual result or
the effort and narrow margin involved; these are not mutually exclusive.

## How the kanji combine

Use the kana spelling ようやく. The source row's 漸く is a recognized
rare kanji spelling of this same adverb, not a different word or reading.
There is no compound to split into separate on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[やっと](やっと.md) also covers "at last" and "barely."
[とうとう](とうとう.md) and [遂に](遂に.md) (ついに) overlap in
eventual results, but do not supply every narrow-margin use.
[だんだん](だんだん.md) and [徐々に](徐々に.md) (じょじょに)
are useful comparisons for gradual change.

## Distinctive meaning

Dictionaries also record "gradually; little by little," so a passage
may describe a developing state rather than one final achievement.
Do not translate every occurrence mechanically as "finally."
This gradual sense is separate from an additional historical "slowly"
use in the public dictionary; that historical use is not the main
lesson here.

The examples above concern reaching a result. ようやく does not
by itself mean that every stage of the process was slow.
