# ちゃわん — rice bowl

## Meaning

ちゃわん is a bowl used for rice, or a vessel for tea in the appropriate context. At an ordinary meal, it commonly means an individual's rice bowl.

ちゃわんにご飯をよそいます。 (ちゃわんにごはんをよそいます。) — I serve rice into the rice bowl.

## How the kanji combine

The usual mixed and kanji spellings are 茶わん and 茶碗. [茶](../kanji/茶.md) contributes "tea" and 碗 means a bowl. The readings are チャ + ワン; the word is not restricted to holding tea despite its spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お椀 (おわん) often refers to a bowl for soup. どんぶり is a larger, deeper bowl, also used to name rice-bowl dishes. コップ is a drinking glass or cup.

## Distinctive meaning

The intended vessel depends on the meal or tea setting. The kana spelling here is the same word as 茶碗, not another category of bowl. Use context rather than assuming that every English "bowl" must be translated ちゃわん.
