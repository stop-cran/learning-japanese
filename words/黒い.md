# 黒い (くろい) — black

## Meaning

黒い is an い-adjective: 黒い猫 (a black cat), 黒い髪 (black hair), 日に焼けて黒い (tanned dark). Figuratively it can mean "dishonest" (腹黒い はらぐろい, scheming).

## How the kanji combine

[黒](../kanji/黒.md) with okurigana い: くろ + い. The noun is 黒 くろ, and 真っ黒 まっくろ is "pitch black". The on reading コク is in 黒板 こくばん (blackboard).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [白い](白い.md) しろい.
- Related: 黒っぽい くろっぽい (blackish), 暗い くらい (dark).

## Distinctive meaning

黒い is a colour; 暗い is dark in the sense of little light.
