# 集まる (あつまる) — gather together

## Meaning

集まる means **to gather or come together**. The people or things gathering are the subject,
often marked with が.

- 友達が駅の前に集まりました。（ともだちがえきのまえにあつまりました。）— My friends gathered in front of the station.
- 明日、九時にここに集まってください。（あした、くじにここにあつまってください。）— Please gather here at nine tomorrow.

It is a godan verb: 集まります（あつまります）, 集まらない（あつまらない）, 集まって（あつまって）.
Ending in る does not make it an ichidan verb.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native verb. [集](../kanji/集.md) writes あつ, while まる is the
**okurigana**. The dictionary notation あつ.まる shows that boundary; there is no two-kanji compound to analyze.

JMdict also records 集る for あつまる, but labels its okurigana irregular.
Use the clearer spelling 集まる for this reading; the alternate spelling does not identify a different verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 集合する（しゅうごうする）also means "assemble" or "meet up," and is useful for an organized gathering.
- [会う](会う.md)（あう）means "meet" or "see someone"; it need not involve a group assembling in one place.
- 集める（あつめる）means "collect" or "bring together": 人を集める（ひとをあつめる）, "gather people together."
- 散る（ちる）, "scatter" or "disperse," can contrast with gathering, but is not an opposite that fits every context.

## Distinctive meaning

集まる presents the gathering as what the group does, even if someone arranged it.
集める presents someone as bringing the group or objects together.
Compare 人が集まる（ひとがあつまる）, "people gather," with 人を集める, "gather people."
