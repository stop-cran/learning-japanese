# ぞろぞろ — in a crowd, one after another

## Meaning

ぞろぞろ describes many people or things moving together or appearing one after another.

- 開店を待つ人たちが、入口にぞろぞろ集まってきた。
  — People waiting for the store to open gathered at the entrance in a crowd.
- 退場する人たちが、廊下をぞろぞろ歩いていった。
  — The people leaving walked down the corridor one after another.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぞろぞろ emphasizes a numerous group or succession, rather than a few people walking together.

## Distinctive meaning

The subject is usually a group or a succession, not one person's quick movement.
