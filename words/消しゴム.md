# 消しゴム (けしゴム)

## Meaning

消しゴム means an eraser, or "rubber" in British English: the stationery item used to remove pencil marks.

- 消しゴムを貸してください。 (けしゴムを かしてください。) — Please lend me an eraser.
- 消しゴムで字を消しました。 (けしゴムで じを けしました。) — I erased the writing with an eraser.

で marks the tool used for erasing. The noun itself does not conjugate like a verb.

## How the kanji combine

[消](../kanji/消.md)し comes from 消す (けす), "erase." ゴム is a loanword for rubber, from Dutch. This is a native-first mixed compound, not an entirely native word; the metadata groups it under wago.

## Synonyms and antonyms

字消し (じけし) is another word for an eraser. 鉛筆 (えんぴつ), "pencil," is the associated writing tool, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

ゴム alone means rubber or a rubber item in context; 消しゴム specifically names an eraser. The mixed hiragana and katakana reading reflects the two parts and is not a pronunciation change.
