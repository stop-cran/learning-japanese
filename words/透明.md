# 透明 (とうめい) - transparent; see-through

## Meaning

透明 describes something transparent or clear enough to see through.
Use 透明な before a noun and 透明だ or 透明です as a predicate.

- この容器は透明なので、中が見えます。
  (このようきはとうめいなので、なかがみえます。) - This container is transparent, so you can see inside.
- 湖の水は透明でした。
  (みずうみのみずはとうめいでした。) - The lake water was clear.

透明になる (とうめいになる) means to become transparent;
透明にする means to make something transparent.
The word can also describe a clear, unclouded quality, as in 透明な声 (とうめいなこえ).

## How the kanji combine

透 contributes passing through, and [明](../kanji/明.md) brightness or clarity.
Together they express transparency. The on readings are トウ + メイ.
There is no separate 透 card in this collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[透き通る](透き通る.md) (すきとおる) is a verb for being transparent
or clear-sounding. [澄む](澄む.md) (すむ) overlaps for unclouded water or sound.
[清い](清い.md) (きよい) and [きれい](きれい.md) can describe clear or pure water,
but their meanings also extend beyond visible transparency.
不透明 (ふとうめい), opaque or unclear, supplies a contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

透明 does not guarantee [清潔](清潔.md) (せいけつ), hygienic cleanliness,
or safe drinking water. Clear water can still contain contamination.
The raw study-list gloss "cleanness" should not be learned as unrestricted
cleanliness; the central idea is transparency or an unclouded quality.
