# 狂う (くるう) - to go awry; lose one's mind

## Meaning

狂う is an intransitive godan verb. It can mean that a mechanism becomes inaccurate,
a plan goes wrong, or a person loses their mind. The affected thing or person takes が.

- この時計は時間が狂っています。 (このとけいはじかんがくるっています。) - This clock is showing the wrong time.
- 雨で予定が狂いました。 (あめでよていがくるいました。) - The rain threw our plans off.

Useful forms are 狂います, 狂った, 狂って, and 狂わない. The dictionary form ends in う,
not る: くるう has three morae. In the clock example, 狂っている describes the inaccurate state.

## How the kanji combine

狂 writes the stem of the native verb くるう. The character carries an association with
madness or being out of order, but the complete word has several contextual uses.
A clock or schedule can 狂う without a literal reference to mental illness.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[壊れる](壊れる.md) (こわれる), "break; stop working," overlaps with mechanical trouble.
A clock can be 狂っている while still running, so being inaccurate is not necessarily being broken.
[違い](違い.md) (ちがい), "difference," is a noun and does not express this change of state.

## Distinctive meaning

Applied to a person, 狂う can be strongly judgmental or insulting; it is not a neutral clinical
diagnosis. For practical study, keep the clock and plan constructions distinct from that personal use.
The shared idea of something going off course helps memory, but it does not make every English
"wrong" naturally translatable with 狂う.
