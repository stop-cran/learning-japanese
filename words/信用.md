# 信用 (しんよう) - trust; credit

## Meaning

信用 is trust or confidence in someone's reliability, or in the truth of information. **Nを信用する** means to trust N. 信用がある describes having credibility; 信用を失う (しんようをうしなう) means to lose trust or one's good standing.

- 私は彼の言葉を信用しています。 (わたしはかれのことばをしんようしています。) - I trust what he says.
- 約束を破ると、信用を失います。 (やくそくをやぶると、しんようをうしないます。) - If you break promises, you lose people's trust.

In finance, 信用 also means credit: the trust underlying lending or transactions with later payment.

## How the kanji combine

[信](../kanji/信.md) contributes believing or trusting; [用](../kanji/用.md) contributes use or reliance. Together they suggest treating someone or something as reliable. The readings are シン + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[信頼](信頼.md) (しんらい) also means trust or reliance. Their meanings overlap; 信用 is not restricted to money, and 信頼 is not restricted to close friendships. 不信 (ふしん), "distrust," supplies a contrasting attitude.

## Distinctive meaning

The object matters: 信用する can concern a person, an explanation, or information. The noun's financial sense does not mean the verb simply translates "borrow." Nor does ordinary 信用 mean confidence in one's own ability; 自信 (じしん) is the useful word for self-confidence.
