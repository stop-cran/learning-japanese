# 独立 (どくりつ) - independence; self-reliance

## Meaning

独立 is independence or self-reliance. As an intransitive する-verb, it means to become independent. It can concern supporting oneself, establishing one's own business, or a country's political independence.

- 姉は親から独立して、自分で生活しています。 (あねはおやからどくりつして、じぶんでせいかつしています。) - My older sister has become independent of our parents and supports herself.
- その国は五十年前に独立しました。 (そのくにはごじゅうねんまえにどくりつしました。) - That country became independent fifty years ago.

から can mark the person or organization from which someone becomes independent. 独立した + noun describes something independent or separate.

## How the kanji combine

独 contributes being on one's own, and [立](../kanji/立.md) contributes standing. "Standing on one's own" is a useful component image for the compound. Read ドク + リツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

自立 (じりつ) also concerns self-reliance. 依存 (いぞん), "dependence," is a useful contrast in that context. [一人](一人.md) (ひとり), "one person; alone," describes number or company, not necessarily financial or political independence.

## Distinctive meaning

The domain matters: living alone does not by itself establish that someone pays their own expenses, and political independence is not a description of solitude. 独立して働く can mean working independently; the surrounding information explains what ties or support have changed.
