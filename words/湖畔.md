# 湖畔 (こはん) — lakeside; lakefront

## Meaning

湖畔 is the **lakeside or lakefront**, the area beside a lake. It names a location, not the lake water itself.

- 湖畔を散歩しました。（こはんをさんぽしました。）— I took a walk along the lakeside.
- 湖畔のホテルに泊まりました。（こはんのホテルにとまりました。）— I stayed at a lakeside hotel.

を marks the route of the walk. In 湖畔のホテル, の connects the hotel with its lakeside location; the word is not an i-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[湖](../kanji/湖.md) supplies コ, lake, and [畔](../kanji/畔.md) supplies ハン, side or bank. The modifier-plus-head compound specifies the side of a lake.

Do not use the independent reading あぜ for 畔 in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

湖岸 (こがん) also means lakeshore or lakeside. [岸](岸.md) (きし) is a shore or bank more generally; it does not specify a lake.

河畔 (かはん) is riverside. [湖](湖.md) (みずうみ) names the body of water; [陸](陸.md) (りく), land or shore, contrasts land with water more broadly. These are geographical comparisons, not strict antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

湖畔 can locate a hotel, path or other place beside a lake without claiming it is physically in the water. It does not mean every waterfront, since a riverbank or seacoast is not necessarily beside a lake.
