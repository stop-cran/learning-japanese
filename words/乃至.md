# 乃至 (ないし) - from ... to; or

## Meaning

乃至, usually written **ないし**, connects a range or presents alternatives. Between quantities it can mean "from ... to," including intermediate amounts; between categories it can mean "or."

- 作業には二日ないし三日かかります。 (さぎょうにはふつかないしみっかかかります。) - The work takes two to three days.
- 連絡は電話ないしメールでお願いします。 (れんらくはでんわないしメールでおねがいします。) - Please contact us by phone or email.

The first example gives a range. The second offers communication methods, not a numerical interval. Do not assume that every ないし has the same English translation.

## How the kanji combine

[乃](../kanji/乃.md) supplies a connecting sense; 至 has the sense of reaching or extending to. The compound is read ナイ + シ. Learn the established conjunction as a whole, not as a rule for translating either character in every word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

For ranges, AからBまで explicitly gives the two endpoints. [または](または.md) and [あるいは](あるいは.md) overlap with the alternative "or" use. [それとも](それとも.md) often introduces another possibility in a question; it is not a replacement for numerical-range ないし. There is no single useful antonym for both uses.

## Distinctive meaning

ないし suits written instructions and explanations; in conversation, から…まで or a suitable ordinary "or" expression may be clearer. The kanji spelling 乃至 is a recognition target, not a recommendation to avoid kana. Use the units and surrounding context to tell a range from separate alternatives.
