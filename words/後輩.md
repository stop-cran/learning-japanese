# 後輩 (こうはい) - junior colleague or student

## Meaning

後輩 is someone junior in a school, workplace, or other shared activity.
A later entrant or less experienced participant can be someone's 後輩.
The dictionary also recognizes younger people; age is relevant in some
uses, but it is not the only basis for the relationship.

- 会社の後輩に仕事を教えています。 (かいしゃのこうはいにしごとをおしえています。) - I am teaching the work to a junior colleague.
- 彼は大学の後輩です。 (かれはだいがくのこうはいです。) - He is a junior from my university.

Use 後輩です to identify the relationship, not 後輩する.

## How the kanji combine

[後](../kanji/後.md) contributes later or behind, and 輩 contributes
fellows or companions. The modifier-plus-head relationship suggests
someone coming later in a shared setting. The on readings are コウ + ハイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[先輩](先輩.md) (せんぱい) is the corresponding senior colleague or student.
年下 (としした), younger in age, and 部下 (ぶか), a subordinate,
identify different relationships. They may describe the same person,
but neither is a universal replacement for 後輩.

## Distinctive meaning

A younger manager need not be someone's 後輩 in that workplace;
an older newcomer can be junior by entry or experience.
The word itself does not establish a management reporting line or
give someone unlimited authority over another person.
