# 構成 (こうせい) — composition; arrangement of parts

## Meaning

構成 is how parts make up an organized whole, such as a book, presentation, or group.
構成する can mean to put those parts together or constitute the whole.
The passive form 構成される describes something being made up of parts.

- この本は三つの章で構成されています。 (このほんはみっつのしょうでこうせいされています。) — This book is made up of three chapters.
- 発表の構成を考えています。 (はっぴょうのこうせいをかんがえています。) — I am planning the structure of my presentation.

In the first example, で marks the parts from which the book is composed.

## How the kanji combine

[構](../kanji/構.md) contributes putting together or building an arrangement;
[成](../kanji/成.md) contributes forming or bringing something into being.
Together they focus on parts forming a whole. The on readings are コウ + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

構造 (こうぞう), "structure; framework," overlaps with 構成.
For planning a presentation's sections, 構成 directs attention to the selection and arrangement of the parts.
構造 is useful when the framework or internal structure is the point. This is a useful focus difference, not a strict boundary.
There is no need for a single antonym covering books, organizations, and physical objects alike.

## Distinctive meaning

構成 is not limited to physically constructing something.
It can describe an existing composition, as in the three-chapter example, or the work of organizing one.
English "composition" here concerns parts and arrangement; it does not automatically mean a school writing assignment.
