# 異常 (いじょう) - abnormality; abnormal

## Meaning

異常 is a noun and な-adjective for a departure from the normal state. Use 異常がある, "there is an abnormality," 異常な状態 (いじょうなじょうたい), "an abnormal condition," or 異常に + adjective, "unusually..."

- 検査では異常が見つかりませんでした。 (けんさではいじょうがみつかりませんでした。) - The examination found no abnormality.
- 今年の夏は異常に暑いです。 (ことしのなつはいじょうにあついです。) - This summer is unusually hot.

The noun can refer to a problem detected in equipment or the body. It does not by itself identify the cause or severity.

## How the kanji combine

異 suggests difference or deviation; [常](../kanji/常.md) suggests the ordinary or usual state. Together they describe being different from what is normal. The readings are イ + ジョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

正常 (せいじょう), "normal; functioning normally," is the direct contrast. [普通](普通.md) (ふつう) means ordinary or usual more broadly.

[変](変.md) (へん), "strange," can express an everyday impression; 異常 is useful when comparing something with an expected condition.

## Distinctive meaning

異常なし (いじょうなし), "no abnormalities," is a compact report-style expression. Calling a person 異常 can sound strongly judgmental; it is not a neutral translation of every English "unusual."

Do not confuse this word with [以上](以上.md) (いじょう), "at least; above": identical pronunciation does not make them related meanings.
