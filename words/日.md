# 日 (ひ) — day; sun

## Meaning

日, read ひ, means a day, the sun, or its light. The surrounding words indicate which sense is intended.

- 今日は特別な日です。 (きょうはとくべつなひです。) — Today is a special day.
- 日が沈みました。 (ひがしずみました。) — The sun has set.

日が長い (ひがながい) means that the daylight period is long. その日 (そのひ) means "that day."

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) represents the whole native word ひ. Other compounds and date expressions use different readings, such as ニチ or か; the character does not always read ひ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

太陽 (たいよう) specifically names the sun. 昼 (ひる) means daytime or noon, and 夜 (よる) contrasts with the daylight part of a day.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not count ordinary durations by freely attaching ひ to every number. For example, 二日 (ふつか) means two days or the second day of a month. The standalone noun and the calendar/counting system require separate reading patterns.
