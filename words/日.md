# 日 (ひ) — day; sun

## Meaning

日, read ひ, means a day, the sun, or its light. The surrounding words indicate which sense is intended.

- 今日は特別な日です。 (きょうはとくべつなひです。) — Today is a special day.
- 日が沈みました。 (ひがしずみました。) — The sun has set.

日が長い (ひがながい) means that the daylight period is long. その日 (そのひ) means "that day."

The separate reading **にち** is used after numbers for days of the month and for counting days:

- 十五日に出発します。 (じゅうごにちにしゅっぱつします。) — I leave on the fifteenth.
- 十五日間滞在します。 (じゅうごにちかんたいざいします。) — I will stay for fifteen days.

The first gives a calendar date; the second gives a duration. 日 as にち is not the sun or sunshine sense.
The number-plus-day system includes special readings, so do not simply append にち to every number.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) represents the whole native word ひ. Other compounds and date expressions use different readings, such as ニチ or か; the character does not always read ひ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[太陽](太陽.md) (たいよう) specifically names the sun. [昼](昼.md) (ひる) means daytime or noon, and [夜](夜.md) (よる) contrasts with the daylight part of a day.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not count ordinary durations by freely attaching ひ to every number. For example, [二日](二日.md) (ふつか) means two days or the second day of a month. 一日 is ついたち for the first day of a month, but いちにち for one day. The standalone noun and the calendar/counting system require separate reading patterns.
