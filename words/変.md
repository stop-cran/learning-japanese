# 変 (へん) — strange; odd

## Meaning

変 describes something strange, odd, or not as expected. It is a na-adjective: 変な音 (へんなおと), "a strange sound," and 変です, "is strange."

- 隣の部屋から変な音がします。 (となりのへやからへんなおとがします。) — A strange sound is coming from the next room.
- この味は少し変です。 (このあじはすこしへんです。) — This tastes a little odd.

The negative is 変ではない (へんではない), or conversational 変じゃない, not an i-adjective form.

## How the kanji combine

[変](../kanji/変.md) has the on-reading ヘン. Although the character also concerns change, the adjective here evaluates something as unusual rather than simply reporting a change.

## Synonyms and antonyms

おかしい can also mean odd or wrong, and sometimes amusing. 普通 (ふつう), "ordinary/normal," gives a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

変 can suggest that something is suspicious or wrong, not just interestingly different. Calling someone 変な人 (へんなひと) may sound unkind; use the word with care when describing a person.
