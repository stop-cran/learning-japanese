# 火事 (かじ) — destructive fire

## Meaning

火事 is an uncontrolled, destructive fire, such as a building burning. It names the incident, not fire as a substance or an ordinary cooking flame.

- 近所で火事がありました。 (きんじょでかじがありました。) — There was a fire in the neighborhood.
- 火事です！ (かじです！) — Fire!

Use 火事になる (かじになる) for something developing into a fire.

## How the kanji combine

[火](../kanji/火.md), "fire," combines with [事](../kanji/事.md), "event; matter." The on readings カ + ジ form the word for a fire incident.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[火](火.md) (ひ) is fire or flame generally. 火災 (かさい) is a more formal word for a destructive fire, common in official reporting. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

A candle burning normally is not a 火事. 火を消す (ひをけす), "put out the flame," can describe an ordinary household action. 火事を防ぐ (かじをふせぐ), "prevent a fire," concerns preventing a dangerous incident.
