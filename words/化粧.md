# 化粧 (けしょう) - makeup; applying makeup

## Meaning

化粧 means makeup or the act of applying it. Use 化粧する or 化粧をする,
"put on makeup." 化粧を落とす (けしょうをおとす) means to remove makeup.
The noun can therefore refer to both the activity and what is on someone's face.

- 出かける前に化粧をしました。 (でかけるまえにけしょうをしました。) - I put on makeup before going out.
- 寝る前に化粧を落とします。 (ねるまえにけしょうをおとします。) - I remove my makeup before going to bed.

The verb after 化粧 matters: する applies it, while 落とす removes it.

## How the kanji combine

[化](../kanji/化.md) contributes change or transformation, and 粧 contributes
adornment or dressing the appearance. Together they suggest altering or preparing
an outward appearance. The on readings here are け + しょう; learn the
whole reading rather than substituting 化's reading か from another compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

メイク is another word for makeup. 化粧品 (けしょうひん) specifies cosmetic
products, whereas 化粧する names applying makeup.

## Distinctive meaning

化粧 does not specify a style, amount, or the person's gender. 濃い化粧
(こいけしょう), "heavy makeup," adds information that the noun lacks.
The dictionary also records decorative finishing, but an ordinary sentence
about someone preparing to go out naturally supports the cosmetic meaning.
