# 妙 (みょう) - odd; strange

## Meaning

妙 is a na-adjective for something odd, strange, or curious. Use 妙な before a noun, 妙だ or 妙です as a predicate, and 妙に as an adverb.

- 隣の部屋から妙な音が聞こえます。 (となりのへやからみょうなおとがきこえます。) - I can hear a strange sound from the next room.
- 今日は妙に静かですね。 (きょうはみょうにしずかですね。) - It is strangely quiet today, isn't it?

The adverb 妙に presents the quietness as unusual or unexpected, not simply as a neutral measurement of volume.

## How the kanji combine

妙 is a single-kanji word read with the on reading ミョウ. The written parts of the character are not separate words to translate into this adjective's meaning. Learn the whole adjective and its な/に forms.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[変](変.md) (へん) also describes something strange or odd. [不思議](不思議.md) (ふしぎ) can highlight wonder or difficulty explaining something. These overlap, but neither supplies a universal substitute in every expression.

## Distinctive meaning

妙 can suggest that something does not fit one's expectations, without proving that it is dangerous or wrong. A 妙な音 invites attention because it is unusual; its cause may be harmless. Calling a person 妙 can nevertheless sound critical, so avoid treating it as a neutral label for any individual difference.
