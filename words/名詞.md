# 名詞 (めいし) - noun

## Meaning

名詞 is the grammatical term "noun." Names of people, objects, places, and
abstract ideas are familiar examples; this is a practical introduction, not
an exhaustive grammatical definition.

- 「本」は名詞です。 (「ほん」はめいしです。) - "Book" is a noun.
- この文の名詞を探してください。 (このぶんのめいしをさがしてください。) - Please find the nouns in this sentence.

## How the kanji combine

[名](../kanji/名.md) contributes naming, while [詞](../kanji/詞.md) contributes a word
or grammatical word class. The on readings are メイ + シ.
The compound names the word class, not a personal name.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動詞](動詞.md) (どうし) is a verb, and [形容詞](形容詞.md) (けいようし) is an adjective.
[代名詞](代名詞.md) (だいめいし) is a pronoun.
Some Japanese school-grammar descriptions include pronouns within nouns;
others discuss 名詞 and 代名詞 separately. Follow the framework being used.

## Distinctive meaning

A noun's sentence role is not fixed: 本 can be an object in 本を読む
(ほんをよむ), "read a book," as well as part of a subject.
Do not identify nouns solely as "the subject words."
[名刺](名刺.md), "business card," is a homophone, not another spelling of this term.
