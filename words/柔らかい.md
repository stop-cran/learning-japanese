# 柔らかい (やわらかい) - soft; flexible

## Meaning

柔らかい is an i-adjective meaning soft, tender, or flexible. It can describe texture, a flexible body, or a gentle impression.

このパンは柔らかいです。 (このパンはやわらかいです。) - This bread is soft.

柔らかい布で拭いてください。 (やわらかいぬのでふいてください。) - Please wipe it with a soft cloth.

It attaches directly to a noun. Its negative is 柔らかくない (やわらかくない), and its past form is 柔らかかった (やわらかかった).

## How the kanji combine

柔 supplies やわ, and らかい is okurigana. This is one native adjective, not a compound. The adjective's inflectional base includes らか; the written kanji boundary is not the grammatical stem boundary.

## Synonyms and antonyms

軟らかい is another spelling of やわらかい. 硬い (かたい), "hard," is a useful texture contrast. 優しい (やさしい), "gentle; kind," overlaps only in some nonphysical uses.

## Distinctive meaning

柔らかい also describes a gentle voice or flexible thinking. Soft bread is not necessarily weak, and flexible thinking is not a literal material texture. Let the noun determine which aspect of "soft" or "flexible" is intended.
