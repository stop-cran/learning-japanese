# 宇宙 (うちゅう) - universe; outer space

## Meaning

宇宙 is the universe or cosmos, and also outer space. It is a noun. Which English
translation fits depends on whether the sentence concerns the whole universe or
travel and conditions beyond Earth.

- 宇宙にはたくさんの星があります。 (うちゅうにはたくさんのほしがあります。) - There are many stars in the universe.
- いつか宇宙に行ってみたいです。 (いつかうちゅうにいってみたいです。) - I would like to try going into space someday.

宇宙の with a following noun means "of the universe" or "in space," according to
the noun: 宇宙の研究 (うちゅうのけんきゅう), "research into the universe."

## How the kanji combine

In the classical account of this compound, 宇 refers to the extent of space
and 宙 to the extent of time. The combination covers space and time as a whole;
the modern uses taught here are "universe" and "outer space."
The readings are ウ + チュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[空](空.md) (そら) is the sky overhead, not automatically outer space.
空間 (くうかん) means space as an extent or area, including space inside a room.
[世界](世界.md) (せかい), "world," can refer to human society rather than the cosmos.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 宇宙 for every English "space." A free parking space or room on a desk
does not involve the universe. 宇宙旅行 (うちゅうりょこう) specifically means space
travel, not travel through an ordinary open area.
