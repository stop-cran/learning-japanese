# 家賃 (やちん) - rent for housing

## Meaning

家賃 is a noun for the rent paid to use a house, apartment,
or other rented building. Use 家賃を払う, "pay the rent,"
and 家賃が高い, "the rent is high."

- このアパートの家賃は月に六万円です。 (このアパートのやちんはつきにろくまんえんです。) - The rent for this apartment is sixty thousand yen a month.
- 毎月、家賃を銀行から振り込みます。 (まいつき、やちんをぎんこうからふりこみます。) - I pay the rent by bank transfer every month.

月に states the monthly rate in the first example. 家賃 names
the payment, not the act of renting or the rented property itself.

## How the kanji combine

[家](../kanji/家.md) contributes a house, and 賃 contributes
a fee or payment. The house specifies what the fee is for.
The reading mixes native や with on-reading ちん, a kun-plus-on
pattern called yutoyomi. This card groups the native-first formation
under wago rather than describing both readings as native.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[料金](料金.md) (りょうきん) is a broader charge or fee.
[費用](費用.md) (ひよう) means expenses.
[借りる](借りる.md) (かりる) is the verb "borrow; rent,"
not the name of the payment.

## Distinctive meaning

家賃 does not automatically include every housing expense.
Utilities, deposits, and other charges may be separate.
The rental arrangement determines what is included; the noun alone
does not promise an all-inclusive monthly total.
