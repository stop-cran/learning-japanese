# アナウンサー (アナウンサー) — announcer; broadcaster

## Meaning

アナウンサー means an **announcer, presenter, or broadcaster**, particularly someone who presents information on television or radio.

アナウンサーがニュースを読んでいます。 (あなうんさーがにゅーすをよんでいます。) — The announcer is reading the news.

It is a noun naming a person or occupation: アナウンサーです, “is an announcer.” It is not a verb for making an announcement.

## How the kanji combine

This is the English loanword “announcer,” written in katakana. There are no kanji to decompose. The final ー lengthens the vowel: アナウンサー, not a clipped アナウンサ in this article's standard spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

司会者 (しかいしゃ) is a host or master of ceremonies who runs an event or program. 記者 (きしゃ) is a reporter or journalist. A person may perform more than one role, but the titles emphasize different work.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese アナウンサー often describes a broadcasting professional, not simply anyone who announces something once. The related noun アナウンス names an announcement or the act of announcing, not the person.
