# 仕上がる (しあがる) - be finished; be completed

## Meaning

仕上がる describes work or something being made reaching its finished state. It is an intransitive godan verb: the finished thing takes が. Forms include 仕上がります, 仕上がった, and 仕上がらない.

- 注文した服が仕上がりました。 (ちゅうもんしたふくがしあがりました。) - The clothes I ordered are finished.
- この絵は明日までに仕上がる予定です。 (このえはあしたまでにしあがるよていです。) - This picture is expected to be finished by tomorrow.

## How the kanji combine

[仕](../kanji/仕.md) writes し, the stem associated with native する, doing, and [上がる](上がる.md), with [上](../kanji/上.md), contributes reaching completion here. This is a native verbal formation, not two on readings meaning a literal upward movement. 仕上る is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[完成](完成.md) かんせい with する also describes reaching a finished form. [終わる](終わる.md) おわる is broader ending. The transitive partner is 仕上げる (しあげる): 服を仕上げる means to finish making the clothes; 服が仕上がる presents the clothes as finished.

## Distinctive meaning

仕上がる can concern how the result turns out: きれいに仕上がる means to come out nicely. Completion alone does not guarantee excellent quality. Keep the distinction between the thing becoming finished and someone deliberately finishing it; changing が to を does not make 仕上がる transitive.
