# 声 (こえ) — voice

## Meaning

声 is a voice: the sound of someone speaking, calling, or singing.
It is a noun. In this use, it names the voice itself rather than what the person said.

- 先生の声が聞こえます。 (せんせいのこえがきこえます。) — I can hear the teacher's voice.
- 小さな声で話してください。 (ちいさなこえではなしてください。) — Please speak quietly.

In the second example, で shows the manner of speaking: "in a quiet voice."
小さな (ちいさな), literally "small," describes volume here, not the speaker's body.

## How the kanji combine

[声](../kanji/声.md) carries the whole native Japanese reading こえ.
There is no multi-kanji combination in this headword.
In 大声 (おおごえ), "loud voice," the first consonant of こえ becomes voiced: ごえ. Learn that compound's actual reading rather than mechanically joining おお and こえ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[音](音.md) (おと) is sound more generally, including a bell or a machine.
A voice is a kind of sound, so the meanings overlap, but 声 highlights its vocal source.
音声 (おんせい) can mean voice or speech and also audio, for example the sound accompanying a video.
There is no single noun that serves as the opposite of every use of 声.

## Distinctive meaning

The first example says that the teacher's voice is audible; it does not say that the listener understood the words.
For that reason, avoid translating 声 as "speech content" merely because someone is talking.
Likewise, a loud 声 is not necessarily an angry one.
