# 物価 (ぶっか) - prices in general; price level

## Meaning

物価 is a noun for commodity prices or prices in general. It is useful when discussing the overall price level of a place or period, rather than the price tag on one particular item.

- 最近、物価が上がっています。 (さいきん、ぶっかがあがっています。) - Prices have been rising recently.
- この町は物価が高いです。 (このまちはぶっかがたかいです。) - Prices are high in this town.

Use 物価が上がる (ぶっかがあがる), "prices rise," and 物価が下がる (ぶっかがさがる), "prices fall." The high-price statement does not by itself explain why prices are high.

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md) contributes "things; goods," and 価 contributes price or value. Together they name the prices of goods collectively. The established reading is **ぶっか**, with a small っ, not ぶつか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[値段](値段.md) (ねだん) and [価格](価格.md) (かかく) can identify a specific item's price. Compare このパンの値段, "the price of this bread," with この町の物価, "the price level in this town."

## Distinctive meaning

物価 connects naturally with discussion of the cost of living, but it does not directly state an individual's total expenses. Different households buy different things. Similarly, a general rise in 物価 need not mean every single product became more expensive by the same amount.
