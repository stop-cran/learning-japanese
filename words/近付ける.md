# 近付ける (ちかづける) - bring close; let approach

## Meaning

近付ける means to bring something near, or let someone approach.
It is transitive: use **thing を + reference point に + 近付ける**.
近づける is another spelling with the same reading and meanings.

- 顔を鏡に近付けました。
  (かおをかがみにちかづけました。) - I brought my face closer to the mirror.
- 危険な場所に子どもを近付けないでください。
  (きけんなばしょにこどもをちかづけないでください。) - Please do not let children go near the dangerous place.

The word also concerns letting people come close or bringing people together
socially. Context determines physical or personal proximity.
As an ichidan verb it gives 近付けます, 近付けた, and 近付けない.

## How the kanji combine

[近](../kanji/近.md) contributes nearness and
[付](../kanji/付.md) bringing or attaching.
The native elements combine as ちか + づける.
The spelling 近づける leaves the second element in kana; neither spelling
requires physical attachment or touching.

## Synonyms and antonyms

寄せる (よせる) also means bring near.
近付く (ちかづく), also written 近づく, describes the subject approaching;
[近寄る](近寄る.md) (ちかよる) likewise means draw near.
[離す](離す.md) (はなす), "move or keep apart," provides a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 椅子を机に近付ける (いすをつくえにちかづける),
"bring a chair closer to the desk," with 人が机に近付く
(ひとがつくえにちかづく), "a person approaches the desk."
The を/が difference reflects different verbs, not interchangeable particles.
The public dictionary also recognizes bringing a result closer to a target,
as in 目標に近付ける (もくひょうにちかづける).
