# 裏切る (うらぎる) - betray; let down

## Meaning

裏切る is a transitive godan verb: betray someone, go back on loyalty, or disappoint an expectation.
The person, trust, or expectation affected takes **を**. Forms include 裏切った (うらぎった),
裏切らない (うらぎらない), and 裏切ります (うらぎります).

- 友人の信頼を裏切りたくありません。 (ゆうじんのしんらいをうらぎりたくありません。) - I do not want to betray my friend's trust.
- この映画は期待を裏切らなかった。 (このえいがはきたいをうらぎらなかった。) - This film did not disappoint my expectations.

The first example concerns loyalty; the second evaluates whether an experience lived up to what was expected.

## How the kanji combine

裏 (うら) evokes the reverse or hidden side, and [切](../kanji/切.md) appears in 切る (きる), "cut."
The compound reads うらぎる, with き voiced to ぎ. These components can help recall a break in loyalty,
but that association is not a historical derivation of the verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[信頼](信頼.md) (しんらい), "trust," names what may be betrayed.
[失望](失望.md) (しつぼう) describes disappointment, whereas 裏切る identifies what lets someone down.
[守る](守る.md) (まもる), "keep/protect," contrasts in expressions about keeping a promise.

## Distinctive meaning

期待を裏切る need not accuse a person of treachery: a result can simply fail to meet expectations.
Conversely, 良い意味で期待を裏切る (よいいみできたいをうらぎる) means to defy expectations in a good way.
The surrounding evaluation, not the verb alone, establishes whether that surprise is welcome.
