# 舌 (した) - tongue

## Meaning

舌 is the tongue, the body part inside the mouth. It is a noun and can take ordinary case particles.

- 熱いお茶で舌をやけどしました。 (あついおちゃでしたをやけどしました。) - I burned my tongue on hot tea.
- 医者に「舌を出してください」と言われました。 (いしゃに「したをだしてください」といわれました。) - The doctor told me to stick out my tongue.

舌を出す (したをだす) describes putting out the tongue; 舌をかむ (したをかむ) means biting it. The body part is the を-marked object in these expressions.

## How the kanji combine

The character 舌 represents the whole native word した. There is no two-kanji combination. Do not confuse it with 下 (した), "below": the reading is the same, but the spelling and meaning are different.

## Synonyms and antonyms

口 (くち), "mouth," names the larger opening containing the tongue, not the same body part. 味覚 (みかく) means the sense of taste, whereas 舌 names the organ. Neither is an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

English "tongue" can mean a language in phrases such as "mother tongue." Do not automatically substitute 舌 for [言葉](言葉.md) (ことば) in that meaning. In the examples here, the reference is physical, and the particles and verbs show what happens to the tongue.
