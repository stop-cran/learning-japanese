# 忙しい (いそがしい) — busy; occupied

## Meaning

忙しい describes being busy or occupied, or a period with much to do. It is an い-adjective.
Name the source of the busyness with で when useful.

- 今日は仕事で忙しいです。 (きょうはしごとでいそがしいです。) — I am busy with work today.
- 忙しい日が続いています。 (いそがしいひがつづいています。) — The busy days are continuing.

## How the kanji combine

[忙](../kanji/忙.md) carries the busy/occupied meaning and is read いそが in this word;
しい is the written adjective ending. This is a native adjective, not a Sino-Japanese compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

多忙 (たぼう) means being very busy. Its grammar differs: 多忙な人 (たぼうなひと),
"a very busy person," uses な, whereas 忙しい人 (いそがしいひと) does not.
暇 (ひま) can mean free or unoccupied and gives a useful contrast: 今日は暇です
(きょうはひまです), "I am free today."

## Distinctive meaning

忙しい describes how occupied someone or some period is; it does not itself identify a particular
job or task. For the negative, replace final い with くない: 忙しくない (いそがしくない),
"not busy." Do not form the adjective by adding な to 忙しい.
