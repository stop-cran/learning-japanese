# 発表 (はっぴょう) — announcement; presentation

## Meaning

発表 is a noun for making something known, such as announcing a result or presenting work.
Use 発表する as a verb and mark the content with を.

- 授業で調べたことを発表します。 (じゅぎょうでしらべたことをはっぴょうします。) — I will present what I researched in class.
- 結果を発表しました。 (けっかをはっぴょうしました。) — They announced the results.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) suggests sending or bringing something out; [表](../kanji/表.md)
contributes the association with showing it. Together they describe bringing information before
an audience. The compound is read はっぴょう, with a small っ and ぴょ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[報告](../words/報告.md) (ほうこく) is a report. The words overlap, but the focus differs: reporting what
happened is 報告, while presenting or announcing something is 発表. A report can itself be presented,
so these are not mutually exclusive categories.

## Distinctive meaning

発表 does not require a large public audience. A classroom presentation, as in the first example,
also fits. Nor does it necessarily mean publication in print: the important action is making
the content known, not a particular medium.
