# みんな - everyone; all

## Meaning

みんな means everyone or all members of a relevant group. It can also mean everything.

- みんなが教室にいます。 (みんながきょうしつにいます。) - Everyone is in the classroom.
- みんなで写真を撮りましょう。 (みんなでしゃしんをとりましょう。) - Let's all take a photo together.

みんなで means doing something together as a group. The context identifies which people are included.

## How the kanji combine

Kana みんな is usual. The kanji spelling [皆](../kanji/皆.md) also exists, with readings みな and みんな. The dictionary describes みんな as less formal and more emphatic than みな.

## Synonyms and antonyms

全員 (ぜんいん) explicitly refers to all members or participants. 誰も (だれも) with a negative predicate can express "nobody"; it is not negative on its own.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 皆さん (みなさん) as a polite way to address a group. For objects, みんな can mean "all," as in お菓子をみんな食べた, "ate all the sweets"; it is not restricted to people.
