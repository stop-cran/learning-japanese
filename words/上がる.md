# 上がる (あがる) — rise; go up

## Meaning

上がる means to rise or go up. It is an intransitive godan verb: the thing rising takes が. Forms include 上がります, 上がって, and 上がらない.

- 気温が上がりました。 (きおんがあがりました。) — The temperature rose.
- 階段を上がってください。 (かいだんをあがってください。) — Please go up the stairs.

In the second example, を marks the route traversed, not an object being raised.

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md) supplies the idea of upward direction. The kana がる complete the native verb. Use 上がる for あがる; the spelling 上る can instead lead learners to the separate reading のぼる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上げる (あげる) means to raise something: 値段を上げる (ねだんをあげる), "raise the price." 下がる (さがる), "go down," gives the opposite direction.

## Distinctive meaning

上がる describes upward change without specifying who causes it. It also appears in everyday extensions such as 雨が上がる (あめがあがる), "the rain stops." Do not translate every occurrence literally as climbing.
