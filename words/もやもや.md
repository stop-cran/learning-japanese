# もやもや — hazy; vaguely troubled

## Meaning

もやもや describes a hazy or murky appearance, or an unresolved, hard-to-pin-down feeling of dissatisfaction or unease.

- 霧で山がもやもやとかすんで見える。 — The mountain looks hazy through the mist. (霧: きり.)
- 理由を説明してもらえず、まだもやもやしている。 — I still feel unsettled because nobody has explained the reason.
- 友達に話したら、胸のもやもやが少し晴れた。 — Talking to a friend cleared away some of the unease in my chest. (胸: むね.)

もやもやする describes the state. The feeling can also be a noun: もやもやが残る, "an unresolved feeling remains."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぼんやり](ぼんやり.md) overlaps in indistinct appearance, but often describes absentmindedness. [くよくよ](くよくよ.md) emphasizes repeatedly brooding over something. [すっきり](すっきり.md) can contrast with unresolved unease.

## Distinctive meaning

もやもや does not identify a single emotion or its cause. Anger, doubt, and dissatisfaction can all contribute. The mental use is not automatically a clinical description of depression, nor does it always mean that the person cannot concentrate.
