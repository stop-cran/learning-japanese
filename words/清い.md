# 清い (きよい) - clean; pure; untainted

## Meaning

清い is an i-adjective meaning clean or clear, and also pure, honest, or untainted in character. Physical and moral purity are related uses, not a claim that clean water makes a person virtuous.

- 清い水が山から流れてきます。
  (きよいみずがやまからながれてきます。) - Clear, clean water flows down from the mountain.
- 彼女は清い心を持っています。
  (かのじょはきよいこころをもっています。) - She has a pure heart.

The adverbial form is 清く (きよく), and the negative is 清くない (きよくない).

## How the kanji combine

[清](../kanji/清.md) contributes clarity or purity. The kun reading きよ.い includes the adjective ending い; the word is きよい, not the compound reading せい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[きれい](きれい.md) and [清潔](清潔.md) (せいけつ) overlap in cleanliness, but きれい can also mean beautiful and 清潔 hygienic. [正直](正直.md) (しょうじき) overlaps the honesty use. [汚い](汚い.md) (きたない), "dirty," can contrast with physical cleanliness.

## Distinctive meaning

The English "clear" here includes unclouded water, not just an explanation that is easy to understand. A favorable appearance alone does not establish the moral purity suggested by 清い心. Choose the expression for the particular kind of cleanliness or purity intended.
