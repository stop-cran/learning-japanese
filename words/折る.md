# 折る (おる) — fold; break

## Meaning

折る can mean folding something or breaking it by bending. It takes an object with を.
Paper can be folded without being damaged; a branch can snap.

- 紙を半分に折ってください。 (かみをはんぶんにおってください。) — Please fold the paper in half.
- 木の枝を折らないでください。 (きのえだをおらないでください。) — Please do not break the tree's branches.

The past form is 折った (おった), and the negative is 折らない (おらない).
The action need not be deliberate, and the person responsible can be left unstated.

## How the kanji combine

[折](../kanji/折.md) carries both folding and breaking senses. The final る belongs to the native verb.
Which English verb fits depends on the object and situation, not on changing the kanji reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The useful partner is 折れる (おれる), "break; be folded." It describes the affected thing undergoing the change,
rather than someone doing it to an object.
枝が折れました (えだがおれました) means "The branch broke."

## Distinctive meaning

Contrast 枝を折った (えだをおった), "broke a branch," with 枝が折れた (えだがおれた), "a branch broke."
In the first, 枝 is the object of someone's action; the subject (the doer) is left unstated.
In the second, 枝 is the subject: the branch undergoes the change, without saying what caused it.
折れる is a separate verb, not simply the passive ending of 折る.
