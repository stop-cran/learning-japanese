# 俳句 (はいく) - haiku

## Meaning

俳句 is a haiku, a short poetic form. Its traditional Japanese pattern is 5-7-5 sound units, commonly called morae, with a seasonal word, 季語 (きご). Free-form and nonseasonal haiku also exist, so these are not exceptionless conditions for everything called 俳句.

- 春の俳句を作りました。 (はるのはいくをつくりました。) - I composed a spring haiku.
- この俳句には春の季語が入っています。 (このはいくにははるのきごがはいっています。) - This haiku contains a spring seasonal word.

The sound units are Japanese morae, not English syllables. A mora is a timing unit: ん and small っ each count as a unit, for example.

## How the kanji combine

俳 connects this compound with haikai verse, and 句 is a phrase or verse. The on readings combine as はい + く. Learn 俳句 as the name of a form, not as any arbitrary short sentence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[詩](詩.md) (し) is broader poetry or a poem. [句](句.md) (く) can itself refer to a haiku in context, besides its phrase and verse meanings. [詩人](詩人.md) (しじん), "poet," names a person rather than a poem.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not define every haiku as seventeen English syllables or require it to occupy three printed lines. The familiar 5-7-5 description concerns the traditional Japanese sound pattern. Conversely, merely making an English sentence short does not automatically make it a haiku.
