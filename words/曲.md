# 曲 (きょく) — a piece of music

## Meaning

曲 is a musical piece, composition, song or track. It names an individual item of music,
not necessarily something with sung words. A piano piece can be a 曲.

- この曲が好きです。 (このきょくがすきです。) — I like this piece of music.
- ピアノの曲を聞いています。 (ピアノのきょくをきいています。) — I am listening to a piano piece.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-character word using the on reading of [曲](../kanji/曲.md), キョク.
Its classification as kango does not require it to contain two kanji.
The musical sense is learned directly; the character's "bend" sense does not explain how to translate this noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

歌 (うた), "song; singing," highlights singing, whereas 曲 also includes instrumental compositions.
音楽 (おんがく), "music," names the broader art or activity.
For a particular track you enjoyed, この曲, "this piece," is more specific than 音楽 in general.
There is no useful opposite noun for an individual musical piece.

## Distinctive meaning

The on-reading noun 曲 is different from the native verbs 曲がる (まがる), "bend; turn,"
and 曲げる (まげる), "bend something."
In この曲が好きです, 曲が is the noun きょく followed by the particle が;
it is not the beginning of the verb まがる.
