# 透き通る (すきとおる) - be transparent; be clear-sounding

## Meaning

透き通る is an intransitive verb meaning to be transparent or clear enough to see through. It also describes a clear voice or sound.

- 水が透き通っていて、川底まで見えます。 (みずがすきとおっていて、かわぞこまでみえます。) - The water is so clear that you can see right down to the riverbed.
- 彼女は透き通った声で歌いました。 (かのじょはすきとおったこえでうたいました。) - She sang in a clear voice.

It is a godan verb ending in る: the negative is 透き通らない (すきとおらない), and the te-form is 透き通って (すきとおって).

## How the kanji combine

透 contributes passing through or transparency, and [通](../kanji/通.md) contributes passing through. Learn the whole native reading すきとおる, keeping とお rather than と.

## Synonyms and antonyms

透明 (とうめい), transparent, is a noun/na-adjective rather than this verb. 澄む (すむ), become clear or unclouded, overlaps for water and sound without being an unrestricted replacement in every construction. [清い](清い.md) can describe clear or pure water; visible transparency alone does not establish purity or drinkability.

## Distinctive meaning

In 透き通った声, った describes the voice's quality; it need not mean that the voice changed from opaque to transparent. [はっきり](はっきり.md) can describe distinctness or clear delivery, but a clear-sounding voice and an easily understood statement are not automatically the same quality.
