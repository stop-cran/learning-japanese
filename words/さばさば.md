# さばさば — frank; easy-going

## Meaning

さばさば describes a frank, easy-going manner that does not dwell on unpleasant matters.

- 彼女はさばさばした性格で、嫌なことをいつまでも引きずらない。
  — She is easy-going and does not dwell on unpleasant things.
- 別れた後もさばさばした様子で、すぐに自分の生活へ戻った。
  — Even after the breakup, they seemed unburdened and soon returned to their own life.

## Synonyms and antonyms

さばさば describes an open, unburdened attitude. さっぱり has several other senses, including
freshness and lack of progress, so context matters.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records a relieved or refreshed feeling. These examples focus on an
easy-going manner and attitude.
