# 月曜 (げつよう) - Monday

## Meaning

月曜 means Monday. It is the shorter form of [月曜日](月曜日.md) (げつようび),
with the same weekday meaning. It functions as a time noun, not an adjective.

- 次の月曜に会いましょう。 (つぎのげつようにあいましょう。) - Let's meet next Monday.
- この店は月曜が休みです。 (このみせはげつようがやすみです。) - This shop is closed on Mondays.

に marks when the meeting will happen. In the second sentence, が identifies the day
that is the shop's day off. A timetable or notice may simply use 月曜.

## How the kanji combine

[月](../kanji/月.md) supplies the moon-related weekday element, read ゲツ here.
[曜](../kanji/曜.md), read ヨウ, identifies the weekday designation.
The final 日 of 月曜日 has been left off; this does not change Monday into a month.

## Synonyms and antonyms

月曜日 is the direct fuller equivalent. [月](月.md) has other readings and meanings,
including moon and month, so it cannot replace the complete weekday word indiscriminately.
Other weekdays are contrasting calendar items, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Context determines whether 月曜 means a particular Monday or a repeated Monday schedule.
毎週月曜 (まいしゅうげつよう), "every Monday," makes repetition explicit.
Keep the reading げつよう: つきよう is not the weekday name.
