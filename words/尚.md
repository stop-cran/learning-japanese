# 尚 (なお) — still; even more; furthermore

## Meaning

Usually written なお, this word can show that a situation continues (“still”), raise a degree (“even more”), or introduce an additional point (“furthermore,” “please note”).

- 祖父は今もなお働いている。 (そふはいまもなおはたらいている。) — My grandfather is still working.
- 入場は無料です。なお、予約が必要です。 (にゅうじょうはむりょうです。なお、よやくがひつようです。) — Admission is free. Please note that a reservation is required.

## How the kanji combine

[尚](../kanji/尚.md) writes the whole word なお, with no kana ending. The two examples keep the usual kana spelling. Both なお and 尚 can represent the word taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[まだ](まだ.md) overlaps in the continuing-state sense. [更に](更に.md) (さらに) overlaps when adding a point or raising the degree: 人が増えれば、なお難しい (ひとがふえれば、なおむずかしい) means “If there are more people, it is even harder.”

## Distinctive meaning

When なお begins a new sentence in a notice, ask what extra information follows. It need not mean that something is *still* happening.
