# 組み合わせ (くみあわせ) - combination; pairing

## Meaning

組み合わせ means a combination or assortment of things brought together. The source spelling 組合せ has the same reading and meanings; 組合わせ and 組み合せ are also dictionary spellings.

- 色の組み合わせを変えました。 (いろのくみあわせをかえました。) - I changed the color combination.
- この二つの食材はよい組み合わせです。 (このふたつのしょくざいはよいくみあわせです。) - These two ingredients make a good combination.

An additional use is contest pairings: 試合の組み合わせ (しあいのくみあわせ), the matchups for the games.

## How the kanji combine

[組](../kanji/組.md) contributes putting or grouping together; [合](../kanji/合.md) contributes bringing together. The native sequence くみあわせ relates to 組み合わせる (くみあわせる), combine. This article's noun is not itself that transitive verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[合わせる](合わせる.md) (あわせる) is a verb for bringing things together or matching them. [セット](セット.md) can name a set of things; 組み合わせ emphasizes which things are brought together.

[試合](試合.md) (しあい) is a match or contest itself. Its 組み合わせ concerns who is paired with whom, not the entire event.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional mathematical use means a combination: a selection in which order does not distinguish the result. Everyday uses are not limited to this technical definition. Different okurigana spellings do not create different mathematical or contest meanings.
