# 上等 (じょうとう) - superior quality; excellent

## Meaning

上等 describes high quality or an excellent standard. It can modify a noun with な or の: 上等な品 (じょうとうなしな), "a fine-quality item." As a predicate, use 上等だ or 上等です.

- 客に上等なお茶を出しました。 (きゃくにじょうとうなおちゃをだしました。) - I served the guests fine-quality tea.
- 初めて作ったにしては、上等です。 (はじめてつくったにしては、じょうとうです。) - It is excellent for a first attempt.

The second example evaluates the result against the circumstances rather than assigning an official quality grade.

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md) contributes upper or high, and [等](../kanji/等.md) contributes a grade or rank. The first modifies the second: a high grade. The on readings are ジョウ + トウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

高級 (こうきゅう), "high-grade; luxury," overlaps when describing goods. [最高](最高.md) (さいこう), "best; highest," places something at the top rather than simply at a high standard. 下等 (かとう), "inferior-grade," contrasts in suitable classifications.

## Distinctive meaning

上等 can also be an exclamation: 上等だ! may mean "That's just fine!" or, in a confrontation, "Bring it on!" That defiant use is not a polite compliment. Tone and situation matter, so begin with clear descriptions of quality rather than borrowing the confrontational response indiscriminately.
