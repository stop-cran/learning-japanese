# 節約 (せつやく) - economizing; conserving

## Meaning

節約 is a noun and transitive する verb for saving resources by using or spending less. The thing conserved takes を.

- 電気を節約するために、使っていない部屋の明かりを消します。 (でんきをせつやくするために、つかっていないへやのあかりをけします。) - To save electricity, I turn off the lights in unused rooms.
- お弁当を作って、食費を節約しています。 (おべんとうをつくって、しょくひをせつやくしています。) - I make packed lunches to cut food expenses.

時間を節約する (じかんをせつやくする) means saving time. The word is not restricted to money or household bills.

## How the kanji combine

節 contributes restraint or moderation here; [約](../kanji/約.md) contributes reducing or keeping things limited. Together they express using resources economically. The reading is セツ + ヤク, without a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[貯金](貯金.md) (ちょきん) means putting money aside. 節約 can help you do that, but reducing spending and accumulating savings are different actions. [無駄](無駄.md) (むだ), waste or wastefulness, gives a useful contrast. 浪費 (ろうひ), wasteful expenditure, is a more direct opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

English "save" covers several ideas. 節約する suits using less water, time, or money, not rescuing a person or saving a computer file. It also does not require that the conserved amount be placed in a bank: 食費を節約した can be true even if the money is then spent on something else.
