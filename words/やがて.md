# やがて (やがて) - before long; eventually

## Meaning

やがて is an adverb describing something that comes about after time passes: "before long; eventually; in due course." It can be used in a future prediction or a past narrative.

- 雨はやがてやむでしょう。 (あめはやがてやむでしょう。) - The rain will probably stop before long.
- その子はやがて医者になりました。 (そのこはやがていしゃになりました。) - That child eventually became a doctor.

The first example can concern a short wait; the second compresses a much longer development. The word does not set one fixed interval.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is the normal study spelling. The dictionary records rare kanji spellings such as 軈て, but those are not needed to understand or write these examples. Do not invent a character-by-character derivation for the kana word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[そのうち](そのうち.md) also points to an unspecified later time. [何時か](何時か.md) (いつか), usually いつか, can express a less specifically anticipated "someday." やがて suits a narrated progression toward the later event.

## Distinctive meaning

やがて is not limited to an event that is about to happen this instant. It can link stages in a life story or a gradual change. The surrounding verb tense decides whether the speaker predicts what will happen or reports what eventually happened; やがて itself does not conjugate.
