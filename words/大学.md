# 大学 (だいがく) -- university

## Meaning

大学 is a university or college-level institution. Depending on the country and context, English may use "university" or "college."

来年、日本の大学に入ります。 (らいねん、にほんのだいがくにはいります。) -- I will enter a Japanese university next year.

大学で勉強する (だいがくでべんきょうする) means studying at university. Use に with entering an institution and で with an activity carried out there.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md), read だい, suggests a higher or greater level; [学](../kanji/学.md), read がく, concerns learning. The established compound names higher education, not simply a physically large school.

## Synonyms and antonyms

学校 (がっこう), school, is broader. 高校 (こうこう), high school, is a different educational stage. 大学院 (だいがくいん) is graduate school rather than a synonym for all university study.

## Distinctive meaning

大学 can refer to the institution or, in context, the place where it operates. 大学生 (だいがくせい) adds 生 to name a university student; 大学 itself is not the word for the person enrolled.
