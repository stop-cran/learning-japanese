# 雑音 (ざつおん) — noise; interference

## Meaning

雑音 is unwanted or distracting noise. It also means static/interference in a signal, and figuratively gossip or irresponsible criticism treated as an unwanted distraction.

雑音が多くて、相手の声が聞こえない。（ざつおんがおおくて、あいてのこえがきこえない。） — There is so much interference that I cannot hear the other person's voice.

周囲の雑音に惑わされず、仕事を続けた。（しゅういのざつおんにまどわされず、しごとをつづけた。） — I kept working without being distracted by the chatter and criticism around me.

## How the kanji combine

[雑](../kanji/雑.md) ザツ suggests miscellaneous or extraneous things; [音](../kanji/音.md) オン means sound. This modifier-and-head compound names extraneous sound, not every sound produced by several things together.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[音](音.md) おと is the broader sound/noise word. [物音](物音.md) ものおと names a sound or noise from something, while [騒音](騒音.md) そうおん emphasizes disturbing noise or din. In the figurative use, compare [噂](噂.md) うわさ, rumor/gossip, and [批判](批判.md) ひはん, criticism.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling criticism 雑音 presents it dismissively as interference with what matters. It is not a neutral replacement for every opinion or every justified criticism. In a telephone or recording context, the same word can refer to actual signal noise, without any human gossip.
