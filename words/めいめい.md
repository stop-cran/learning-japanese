# めいめい - each; individually

## Meaning

めいめい considers each individual separately.
It can work as a noun, directly qualify an action, or connect to a following noun with の.
The source spelling is 銘々; 銘銘 is another recorded spelling, but kana are usual.

- めいめいが意見を述べました。 (めいめいがいけんをのべました。) - Each person expressed an opinion.
- 進路はめいめい違います。 (しんろはめいめいちがいます。) - Each person has a different path ahead.
- めいめいの荷物を持ってください。 (めいめいのにもつをもってください。) - Please carry your own luggage.

The final example asks each person to carry their own luggage; it does not mean that everyone carries the same bag.

## How the kanji combine

The written forms 銘々 and 銘銘 repeat 銘, read メイ; 々 repeats the preceding kanji.
The deck groups this word as kango. Daijisen traces めいめい to a sound change
from 面面 (めんめん), "each person."
Do not turn 銘's engraving association into an invented "engraving twice" origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[それぞれ](それぞれ.md), [各々](各々.md) (おのおの), and [各自](各自.md) (かくじ)
overlap in distributing attention or action among individuals.
[一人一人](一人一人.md) (ひとりひとり) explicitly counts people one by one.
[お互い](お互い.md) (おたがい) concerns a mutual relationship; めいめい does not inherently mean "each other."

## Distinctive meaning

めいめい focuses on individuals rather than on a collective action.
It need not imply selfishness or disagreement.
In めいめいが意見を述べる, each person speaks; whether they agree is a separate matter.
