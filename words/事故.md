# 事故 (じこ) — accident

## Meaning

事故 is an accident or an unexpected incident causing trouble or harm. 交通事故 (こうつうじこ) is a traffic accident.

駅の近くで事故がありました。 (えきのちかくでじこがありました。) — There was an accident near the station.

事故にあわないように気をつけてください。 (じこにあわないようにきをつけてください。) — Please be careful to avoid getting into an accident.

## How the kanji combine

[事](../kanji/事.md) concerns an event or matter; 故 can concern a reason or mishap. The compound uses on-readings, じ + こ. Its everyday meaning is learned as a whole, not as any event with a cause.

## Synonyms and antonyms

事件 (じけん) is an incident or case, often one attracting attention or investigation. 故障 (こしょう) is a breakdown or malfunction. A malfunction can cause an accident, but the words identify different things.

## Distinctive meaning

事故が起きる (じこがおきる) means "an accident happens"; 事故を起こす (じこをおこす) means "cause an accident." The noun does not itself establish responsibility. There is no single direct antonym; 無事故 (むじこ) describes an accident-free record or state.
