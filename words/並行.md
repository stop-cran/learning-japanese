# 並行 (へいこう) - moving side by side; concurrency

## Meaning

並行 describes moving alongside something or activities proceeding concurrently.
It is a noun and a する verb. A useful pattern is Aと並行してBを進める,
"advance B in parallel with A."

- この道は川と並行しています。 (このみちはかわとへいこうしています。) - This road runs alongside the river.
- 二つの調査を並行して進めています。 (ふたつのちょうさをへいこうしてすすめています。) - We are carrying out two investigations in parallel.

## How the kanji combine

[並](../kanji/並.md) contributes side-by-side arrangement; [行](../kanji/行.md)
contributes going or proceeding. Together they evoke proceeding alongside:
ヘイ + コウ. 併行 is another spelling of this same dictionary family.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[同時](同時.md) (どうじ) emphasizes the same time.
[一緒](一緒.md) (いっしょ) and [共に](共に.md) (ともに) can express doing
things together; [並ぶ](並ぶ.md) (ならぶ) can describe being alongside.
平行 (へいこう) is the usual mathematical parallelism term, but its dictionary
family also recognizes concurrent activity, so the two spellings do not form an
absolute distinction between space and time.

## Distinctive meaning

Parallel tasks need not start and end at exactly the same instant; they proceed
alongside one another during a relevant period. That differs from merely doing
one task after another. The road example is a conventional description of its
course, not a claim that the road itself is physically moving.
