# いちばん — first; most

## Meaning

いちばん means "number one" or "first," and before a description it means "most." The comparison group can be expressed with で.

- この中でいちばん安いです。 (このなかでいちばんやすいです。) — It is the cheapest of these.
- 私はいちばんに着きました。 (わたしはいちばんにつきました。) — I arrived first.

With a noun, いちばんの友達 (いちばんのともだち) can mean "one's best friend." With an adjective, use いちばん directly: いちばん大きい (いちばんおおきい), "biggest."

## How the kanji combine

The usual kanji spelling is 一番: [一](../kanji/一.md), "one," plus [番](../kanji/番.md), "number/order." Hiragana いちばん represents the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[最も](最も.md) (もっとも) likewise means "most" and is useful in more formal description. [最後](最後.md) (さいご), "last," contrasts with the ordering sense, not with every superlative.

## Distinctive meaning

いちばん does not always mean "best": いちばん寒い (いちばんさむい) is "coldest." The following expression determines what ranks highest.
