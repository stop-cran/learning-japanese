# めらめら — flames flaring up

## Meaning

Flames rising and flaring visibly.

- 暖炉（だんろ）の火がめらめら燃（も）えている。 — The fire in the fireplace is blazing.
- たき火の炎（ほのお）がめらめらと上がった。 — The campfire's flames flared upward.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ゆらゆら](ゆらゆら.md) can describe a flame's wavering movement. [ぱちぱち](ぱちぱち.md) describes crackling sound, while [もくもく](もくもく.md) often describes smoke billowing.

## Distinctive meaning

めらめら燃える foregrounds visible fire, not a particular noise. A figurative “flame” of jealousy or determination can use the same image, but the phrase's subject supplies that emotional reading.
