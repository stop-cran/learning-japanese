# 銅 (どう) - copper; bronze medal (abbreviation)

## Meaning

銅 is copper, the metal. In competition results, どう can also abbreviate a bronze medal.

- 銅は電気をよく通します。 (どうはでんきをよくとおします。) - Copper conducts electricity well.
- その選手は銅を獲得しました。 (そのせんしゅはどうをかくとくしました。) - That athlete won bronze.

The second sentence concerns a medal result, not winning a supply of pure copper.
銅メダル (どうメダル) makes the medal meaning explicit.

## How the kanji combine

[銅](../kanji/銅.md) is one kanji read with the on reading ドウ in the primary entry here.
The native reading あかがね also names copper; the dictionary additionally records あか for 銅.
赤金 can be read あかがね. These material readings do not all inherit the medal abbreviation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[金属](金属.md) (きんぞく), "metal," is the broader category.
[鉄](鉄.md) (てつ), iron, and [銀](銀.md) (ぎん), silver, are different metals.
[銅貨](銅貨.md) (どうか) is a copper coin, not an alternative name for every object made of copper.

## Distinctive meaning

The bronze-medal abbreviation is restricted to **どう** in the inspected entry;
do not read the competition example あかがね.
Bronze is an alloy, so the medal use does not make pure copper and bronze the same material.
The unrelated adverb [どう](どう.md), "how," has the same sound but not this meaning.
