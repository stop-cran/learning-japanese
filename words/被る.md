# 被る (かぶる) - wear on the head; take the blame

## Meaning

被る, read かぶる here, is a godan verb. One use means to put on or wear something over the head; another means to shoulder a burden or take blame. Both take an object with を. Kana かぶる is usual in these uses.

- 外では帽子をかぶってください。 (そとではぼうしをかぶってください。) - Please wear a hat outside.
- 彼は友人の罪をかぶりました。 (かれはゆうじんのつみをかぶりました。) - He took the blame for his friend's wrongdoing.

The past form is かぶった; the negative is かぶらない.

## How the kanji combine

被 is the single written kanji; る completes the native verb. Learn the reading with the expression, because 被る also writes the separate verb こうむる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[着る](着る.md) (きる) describes putting on clothing such as a shirt, while [履く](履く.md) (はく) is used for shoes and lower-body clothing. English "wear" does not determine the Japanese verb by itself.

## Distinctive meaning

The burden sense of かぶる is not a general equivalent of "suffer." The separate 被る (こうむる) means to suffer or receive something, as in 損害を被る (そんがいをこうむる), "sustain damage."

The community list's English "to suffer" does not establish which reading its compiler intended. This card retains its supplied かぶる and teaches verified uses; the こうむる contrast does not acquire a separate N3 assignment.
