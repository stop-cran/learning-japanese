# 勝つ (かつ) — win

## Meaning

勝つ means winning a contest or prevailing over an opponent. Use に for the opponent or the contest:
兄に勝つ (あににかつ), "beat my older brother," or 試合に勝つ (しあいにかつ), "win a match."

- 昨日の試合に勝ちました。 (きのうのしあいにかちました。) — We won yesterday's match.

It is a godan verb: its ending changes across forms. Learn 勝ちます (かちます), 勝った (かった),
and 勝って (かって), rather than forming a past tense with つた.

## How the kanji combine

[勝](../kanji/勝.md) carries the winning meaning; つ is the written verb ending.
The native reading かつ is separate from ショウ in 勝利 (しょうり), "victory."

## Synonyms and antonyms

勝利する (しょうりする) also expresses gaining victory, using a noun plus する.
[負ける](負ける.md) (まける), "lose," is the direct contrast in a contest.
Both 勝つ and 負ける can use に for the opponent.

## Distinctive meaning

The English phrase "beat someone" does not make 勝つ transitive with を: 兄に勝った
(あににかった) describes winning against him, not physically hitting him.
The word reports the competitive result, not how much effort it required.
