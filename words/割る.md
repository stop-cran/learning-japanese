# 割る (わる) — split; break

## Meaning

割る means splitting or breaking something, including cracking an egg or breaking a glass.
It is transitive: **object を割る** tells what is broken or split.
It is a godan verb despite ending in る: the polite form is 割ります (わります), the て-form is 割って
(わって), and the past is 割った (わった).

- 卵を割って、ボウルに入れます。 (たまごをわって、ボウルにいれます。) — Crack the egg and put it into a bowl.
- コップを割ってしまいました。 (コップをわってしまいました。) — I broke a glass.
- 十二を三で割ると、四になります。 (じゅうにをさんでわると、よんになります。) — Twelve divided by three is four.

For arithmetic division, XをYで割る means "divide X by Y": を marks the number being divided, and で the divisor.

## How the kanji combine

[割](../kanji/割.md) contributes splitting or dividing, followed by the native verb ending る.
The reading is わる, not the on reading カツ found in compounds such as 分割 ぶんかつ, "division."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[割れる](割れる.md) われる describes something breaking: コップが割れました (コップがわれました), "The glass broke."
It does not name an agent acting on the glass.
[切る](切る.md) きる means cut, as in 紙を切る (かみをきる), "cut paper."
[分ける](分ける.md) わける means divide or share and need not involve physically breaking an object.

## Distinctive meaning

割る does not itself tell you whether the action was intentional. Cracking an egg for cooking and breaking a glass
are both possible uses.
Its division sense also applies to arithmetic, as the third example shows; no physical breaking is involved.
Choose 割る or 割れる according to whether you describe someone acting on the object or the object breaking.
