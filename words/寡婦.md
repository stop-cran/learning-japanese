# 寡婦 (かふ) — widow; woman who has not remarried

## Meaning

寡婦 can mean a woman whose husband has died and who has not remarried. It can also be used of a woman who has not remarried after divorce. JMdict groups both uses with the broader gloss “unmarried woman.” That gloss is not a reason to call a woman 寡婦 just because she has never married. Use 独身 (どくしん) to say someone is single.

- 夫の死後、彼女は寡婦として二人の子を育てた。 (おっとのしご、かのじょはかふとしてふたりのこをそだてた。) — After her husband died, she raised two children as a widow.

## How the kanji combine

[寡](../kanji/寡.md) is か and [婦](../kanji/婦.md) is ふ. The form 寡婦 has another dictionary reading, やもめ. Learn かふ for the example above; やもめ is a whole-word reading, not a reading to divide between 寡 and 婦.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[独身](独身.md) (どくしん) means single, regardless of sex, and need not imply a previous marriage. [妻](妻.md) (つま) identifies a wife, not a widow.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer the reason for someone being unmarried from a label alone. In an administrative document, look for its definition rather than assuming that a dictionary gloss decides eligibility for a benefit.
