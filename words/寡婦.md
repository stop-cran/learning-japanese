# 寡婦 (かふ) — widow; woman who has not remarried

## Meaning

寡婦 can mean a woman whose husband has died and who has not remarried. It can also be used of a woman who has not remarried after divorce. JMdict also gives the broader gloss “unmarried woman.” English “widow” fits the first situation but not the second, and 独身 (どくしん) is the neutral word for being single.

- 夫の死後、彼女は寡婦として二人の子を育てた。 (おっとのしご、かのじょはかふとしてふたりのこをそだてた。) — After her husband died, she raised two children as a widow.
- その記録の「寡婦」には、離婚後に再婚していない女性も含まれる。 (そのきろくの「かふ」には、りこんごにさいこんしていないじょせいもふくまれる。) — In that record, the term also includes women who have not remarried after divorce.

## How the kanji combine

[寡](../kanji/寡.md) is か and [婦](../kanji/婦.md) is ふ. The form 寡婦 has another dictionary reading, やもめ. Learn かふ for these examples; do not split やもめ into those two on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[独身](独身.md) (どくしん) means single, regardless of sex, and need not imply a previous marriage. [妻](妻.md) (つま) identifies a wife, not a widow.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer the reason for someone being unmarried from a label alone. In an administrative document, look for its definition rather than assuming that a dictionary gloss decides eligibility for a benefit.
