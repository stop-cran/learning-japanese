# 中身 (なかみ) - contents; substance

## Meaning

中身 is what is inside something: its contents, interior, or
filling. It can also mean the substance or content of a talk,
plan, or other work. **中味**, also なかみ, is another spelling.

- 箱の中身を確かめました。 (はこのなかみをたしかめました。) - I checked the contents of the box.
- 話は長いですが、中身がありません。 (はなしはながいですが、なかみがありません。) - The talk is long, but it has no substance.

The second example criticizes the content; it does not mean
there is a physically empty container.
In sword terminology, 中身 can also name the blade, as distinct
from its outer fittings or sheath.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) contributes なか, "inside"; 身 contributes
み, associated here with the main substance or part.
The native combination names the substance inside.
In 中味, [味](../kanji/味.md) supplies the alternative written
み; the dictionary labels that spelling ateji. 中味 does not
therefore mean only a food's flavor.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[内容](内容.md) (ないよう) overlaps with contents and substance.
[実](実.md) (み) also has a substance/content use.
[身](身.md) (み) can name a main part, including a sword blade.
[内側](内側.md) (うちがわ) emphasizes the inside or inner side.
Compare the contents of a box with its inner surface rather than
assuming every use of these words is interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

The contents are not necessarily liquid, edible, or valuable.
Middle and inside are related character associations, but 中身
does not simply mean a geometric midpoint.
Keep this なかみ identity separate from unrelated dictionary
entries that happen to share the spelling 中身.
