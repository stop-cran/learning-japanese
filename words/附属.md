# 附属 (ふぞく) - attachment; affiliation; belonging to

## Meaning

附属 means being attached or affiliated to something, or belonging to it as a subordinate part. 付属 is another spelling of the same word and reading.

- この病院は大学に附属しています。 (このびょういんはだいがくにふぞくしています。) - This hospital is affiliated with the university.
- カメラに付属する説明書を読みました。 (カメラにふぞくするせつめいしょをよみました。) - I read the manual that comes with the camera.

## How the kanji combine

附 conveys attachment and 属 belonging: an attached or affiliated relationship. The on readings are フ + ゾク. Neither character has a card yet. In the alternate spelling 付属, [付](../kanji/付.md) carries the attachment association instead.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[属する](属する.md) (ぞくする) overlaps in belonging to a group or category. [付く](付く.md) (つく) covers attachment more broadly. An institutional affiliation is not necessarily a physical object stuck to another one.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 大学に附属する, be affiliated with a university, and 大学附属の学校, a university-affiliated school. The dictionary also records 附属 as short for an affiliated school. That school use is context-dependent, not a new meaning for every attached accessory. The two spellings do not create separate word identities.
