# 親指 (おやゆび) - thumb; big toe

## Meaning

親指 means the thumb or big toe. 手の親指 (てのおやゆび) specifies a thumb; 足の親指 (あしのおやゆび) specifies a big toe.

- 右手の親指をけがしました。 (みぎてのおやゆびをけがしました。) - I injured my right thumb.
- 靴がきつくて、足の親指が痛いです。 (くつがきつくて、あしのおやゆびがいたいです。) - My shoes are tight, and my big toe hurts.

The hand or foot can be left unstated when the context makes it clear.

## How the kanji combine

[親](../kanji/親.md), read おや, modifies [指](../kanji/指.md), read ゆび. "Parent/main digit" is a memory association for the established native compound, not a historical derivation. Do not substitute the on readings シン + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[指](指.md) (ゆび) is the broader word for a finger or toe. 小指 (こゆび) names the little finger or little toe; it is a contrasting digit, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not limit 親指 to the hand because English usually translates it as "thumb." 足の親指 is the big toe, not a thumb attached to a foot. Read the larger phrase before choosing the English body-part term.
