# 小指 (こゆび) - little finger; little toe

## Meaning

小指 is the little finger or little toe. Use 手の小指 (てのこゆび) or 足の小指 (あしのこゆび) when the body part is not already clear.

- 右手の小指をけがしました。 (みぎてのこゆびをけがしました。) - I injured the little finger on my right hand.
- 足の小指を机の脚にぶつけました。 (あしのこゆびをつくえのあしにぶつけました。) - I banged my little toe against a table leg.

The hand or foot can remain unstated when the situation identifies it.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) (こ), small, modifies [指](../kanji/指.md) (ゆび), a digit. Both parts have native readings. The compound names the particular little digit; it is not a general description of any finger that happens to be small.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[指](指.md) (ゆび) is the broader finger/toe word. [親指](親指.md) (おやゆび) names the thumb or big toe. [薬指](薬指.md) (くすりゆび), the ring finger in a hand context, is adjacent to the little finger; these are different digits, not synonyms or antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not limit 小指 to the hand just because the English source list says "little finger." The same noun covers the foot's little toe. Adding 手の or 足の resolves the distinction without changing the reading こゆび.
