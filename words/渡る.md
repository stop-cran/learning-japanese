# 渡る (わたる) — cross over

## Meaning

渡る means to cross or go across, such as from one side of a road or river to the other.
The road, bridge, or stretch crossed can take を.

- 道を渡ります。 (みちをわたります。) — I cross the road.
- あの橋を渡りましょう。 (あのはしをわたりましょう。) — Let's cross that bridge.

The dictionary classifies this use as intransitive: the traveler moves, rather than changing the road.
Here を marks a route or space crossed. It is not evidence that every verb with を is transitive.
渡る is a godan verb, with 渡ります (わたります) and 渡って (わたって).

## How the kanji combine

[渡](../kanji/渡.md) supplies crossing, followed by the native verb ending る.
Its reading is わた- here.
The dictionary also records 渉る for this crossing sense; this article teaches the selected spelling 渡る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

渡す (わたす) is related but gives a different perspective: it can mean carrying something across
or handing it over. 本を渡す (ほんをわたす), "hand over a book," concerns the object transferred,
not the speaker crossing a road.

## Distinctive meaning

渡る focuses on getting across, not merely moving along.
The water radical is a spelling cue, not a requirement that the route contain water:
道を渡る works for crossing a road.
