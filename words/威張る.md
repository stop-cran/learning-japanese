# 威張る (いばる) - swagger; act arrogantly

## Meaning

威張る means to put on airs, swagger, or throw one's weight around.
It describes a display of superiority, not simply quiet satisfaction
with an achievement. It is an intransitive godan verb.

- 彼は部下の前で威張っています。 (かれはぶかのまえでいばっています。) - He throws his weight around in front of his subordinates.
- そんなに威張らないでください。 (そんなにいばらないでください。) - Please do not act so arrogantly.

Forms include 威張ります, 威張って, and 威張らない.
The person displaying the attitude is the subject.

## How the kanji combine

威 has authority or imposing-power associations, and 張 has stretching
or asserting associations. Use them as a meaning aid for the established
verb, not as proof of its historical derivation. The reading いばる
must be learned as a whole; the dictionary also lists えばる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[自慢](自慢.md) (じまん), "boasting; pride," can involve talking about
an achievement or something one owns. 威張る focuses on acting superior,
which need not include a spoken boast. 謙虚な (けんきょな), "modest,"
provides an attitude contrast rather than a mechanical verb replacement.

## Distinctive meaning

The word is generally critical in the behavior illustrated here.
Do not use it as an unrestricted translation of positive English
"be proud." The person may or may not have genuine authority or an
achievement to justify the display.
