# 御覧 (ごらん) - a look; try it

## Meaning

御覧, also written ご覧, belongs to several constructions. Learn the complete expression rather than treating bare ごらん as a verb that conjugates like 見る.

- ご覧のとおり、部屋は空です。 (ごらんのとおり、へやはからです。) - As you can see, the room is empty.
- ほら、ごらん。虹が出ているよ。 (ほら、ごらん。にじがでているよ。) - Look, there's a rainbow.
- このお菓子、おいしいよ。食べてごらん。 (このおかし、おいしいよ。たべてごらん。) - This sweet is good. Try it.

The last example has **verb て-form + ごらん**, an invitation to try the action. Bare ごらん can also direct someone to look.

## How the kanji combine

[御](../kanji/御.md) supplies ご, and 覧 (らん) concerns seeing or looking. The compound's respectful seeing sense appears in expressions such as ご覧のとおり. The short look/try uses are also commonly written ごらん; the cited dictionary marks those senses usually kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ごらんになる](ごらんになる.md) is an honorific expression for someone else's seeing or watching. [見る](見る.md) (みる) is neutral, while [拝見](拝見.md)する (はいけんする) is humble for one's own side's looking. These are not freely interchangeable social directions.

[試す](試す.md) (ためす) means trying or testing something. 食べてごらん invites trying an action; it does not turn every object of 試す into an object of bare ごらん.

## Distinctive meaning

The short invitation ～てごらん often belongs to familiar encouragement, for example to a child. Its connection with respectful ご覧 does **not** make every use appropriate as a formal instruction to a superior. For a polite request to look, recognize ご覧ください (ごらんください), "please look."
