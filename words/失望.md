# 失望 (しつぼう) - disappointment

## Meaning

失望 is disappointment when a hope or expectation fails. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb. Use 人が結果に失望する, "someone is disappointed in/with the result": the experiencer takes が and the cause or target can take に.

- 試験の結果に失望しました。 (しけんのけっかにしつぼうしました。) - I was disappointed with the examination result.
- 彼の無責任な態度に失望した。 (かれのむせきにんなたいどにしつぼうした。) - I was disappointed by his irresponsible attitude.

失望させる (しつぼうさせる) means "disappoint someone," with the affected person marked by を.

## How the kanji combine

[失](../kanji/失.md) contributes losing, and [望](../kanji/望.md) contributes hope or expectation. The combination evokes the loss of what one hoped for. It does not require literally losing a possession.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[がっかり](がっかり.md) also expresses disappointment and suits a direct conversational reaction. [希望](希望.md) (きぼう), "hope," contrasts with the lost expectation, though it is not a grammatical replacement in the same sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

失望 can concern a disappointing outcome or a person who has failed one's expectations. It need not imply permanent hopelessness. When describing your reaction, identify the relevant result or behavior rather than treating 失望 as a general word for every kind of sadness.
