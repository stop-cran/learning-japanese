# 勉強 (べんきょう) — study

## Meaning

勉強 is study; 勉強する means "to study." It can be used for a subject or for studying in general.

- 毎日、日本語を勉強します。（まいにち、にほんごを べんきょうします。）— I study Japanese every day.
- 今日は家で勉強します。（きょうは いえで べんきょうします。）— I will study at home today.

## How the kanji combine

[勉](../kanji/勉.md) ベン conveys effort; [強](../kanji/強.md) キョウ conveys strength. Their senses provide an effort-and-exertion memory aid, but the established word means "study," not "strong homework." Both readings are on readings.

This is a fixed compound, not a rule that adding 強 to any activity means studying it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 学習 がくしゅう also means learning or study and is common when discussing educational activities or learning processes.
- [習う](習う.md) ならう emphasizes receiving instruction or lessons; 勉強する can include studying alone.
- [休む](休む.md) やすむ, "rest," can contrast with studying in a schedule, but is not a direct lexical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

勉強 usually foregrounds the activity or effort of learning, not proof that something has been mastered. 勉強になりました（べんきょうに なりました）means "I learned something useful."

The dictionary also records a price-reduction sense in bargaining. That is separate from the study sense taught here.
