# ポスト (ポスト) - postbox; mailbox

## Meaning

ポスト commonly means a postbox or mailbox. It can name a public collection box for outgoing mail or a box that receives mail at a home.

- 手紙をポストに入れました。 (てがみをポストにいれました。) - I put the letter in the postbox.

を marks the letter, and に marks where it was put. Context identifies whether someone is posting a letter or placing it in a recipient's box.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "post." There are no kanji components or long vowels in ポスト.

## Synonyms and antonyms

郵便局 (ゆうびんきょく) is a post office, not the box. 郵便受け (ゆうびんうけ) specifically emphasizes a receptacle for receiving mail.

## Distinctive meaning

ポスト also has other uses, including a job position and an online post. The letter example selects the physical mailbox sense. Do not translate every English "post" as a postal service or assume every Japanese ポスト is a street collection box.
