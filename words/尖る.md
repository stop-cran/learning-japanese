# 尖る (とがる) - be pointed; be on edge

## Meaning

尖る describes tapering to a point or being pointed. It also describes being
touchy or on edge, a sharp or displeased voice or manner, and a provocative
or rebellious attitude.

- この鉛筆は先が尖っています。
  (このえんぴつはさきがとがっています。) - This pencil has a pointed tip.
- 彼は尖った声で返事をしました。
  (かれはとがったこえでへんじをしました。) - He replied in a sharp voice.

尖っている can describe an existing state, not only the process of becoming sharp.
The verb is godan: 尖ります, 尖って, 尖らない.
とんがる is another recorded reading.

## How the kanji combine

尖 supplies the pointedness idea; the native word is とがる, with る in kana.
There is no separate 尖 card in this collection.
Do not infer the whole range of emotional uses from the character's shape alone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鋭い](鋭い.md) (するどい) overlaps in sharpness but also describes keen perception.
[いらいら](いらいら.md) and [ぴりぴり](ぴりぴり.md) overlap with an irritable or tense state.
[怒る](怒る.md) (おこる) names anger more generally.
丸い (まるい), round, can contrast with a pointed shape in an appropriate comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

A pointed pencil is not necessarily cutting like a blade.
The study-list English "sour" concerns a displeased expression or manner,
not a sour taste: use 酸っぱい (すっぱい) for that taste.
Sharpness of voice does not mean literal pointedness or necessarily loudness.
