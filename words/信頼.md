# 信頼 (しんらい) - trust; confidence; reliance

## Meaning

信頼 is a noun for trust or confidence in someone or something. 信頼する is transitive: the trusted person or thing takes を. With the noun, Nへの信頼 expresses trust directed toward N.

- 私はこの医師を信頼しています。 (わたしはこのいしをしんらいしています。) - I trust this doctor.
- この情報は信頼できます。 (このじょうほうはしんらいできます。) - This information can be trusted.

信頼できる is the potential form, "can trust" or, from the information's perspective, "is trustworthy." 信頼を失う (しんらいをうしなう) means to lose trust.

## How the kanji combine

[信](../kanji/信.md) contributes belief or trust, and [頼](../kanji/頼.md) contributes relying on someone or something. Together they express confidence that one can rely on the target.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[信じる](信じる.md) (しんじる) covers believing and trusting, including accepting a claim as true. [信用](信用.md) (しんよう) also overlaps with trust and confidence. These are not divided by an absolute emotional-versus-financial boundary. [疑う](疑う.md) (うたがう), "doubt," gives a contrasting attitude.

## Distinctive meaning

信頼 can concern a person, information, or a system. It is not restricted to close friendships. The word reports confidence in reliability; it does not prove the target is objectively reliable. For self-confidence, do not assume that bare 信頼 expresses "confidence in myself" without specifying the target.
