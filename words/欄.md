# 欄 (らん) - printed column; form field

## Meaning

欄 names a section or column of printed material, or a space or field in
a form or web page. It is a noun and also follows other nouns:
住所欄 (じゅうしょらん), "address field," and
読者欄 (どくしゃらん), "readers' section."

- 名前の欄に記入してください。 (なまえのらんにきにゅうしてください。) - Please fill in the name field.
- 新聞のスポーツ欄を読みました。 (しんぶんのスポーツらんをよみました。) - I read the newspaper's sports section.

## How the kanji combine

This is the single character 欄 with its on reading ラン.
In these uses it names an allocated section or space, rather than
combining separate word parts.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[項目](項目.md) (こうもく) can identify an item or field in a form;
欄 emphasizes the space provided for it.
[列](列.md) (れつ) can name a row or column in an arrangement.
[段](段.md) (だん) also has a printing-column sense.
These are contextual distinctions, not rules that prevent all overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

A 欄 can be blank: it is the field, not necessarily what someone has written.
The dictionary also records an original handrail/railing meaning for らん;
the practical examples here teach the printed-section and form-field senses.
Do not transfer the separate word 欄 (おばしま) to these examples.
