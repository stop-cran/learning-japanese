# 炊事 (すいじ) - cooking; kitchen work

## Meaning

炊事 means cooking or meal-preparation work.
It is a noun and an intransitive する verb:
炊事する or 炊事をする means to do the cooking.

- キャンプでは交代で炊事をしました。 (キャンプではこうたいですいじをしました。) - At camp, we took turns doing the cooking.
- 炊事の道具を用意しました。 (すいじのどうぐをよういしました。) - I prepared the cooking equipment.

In 炊事をする, を connects the activity noun to する.
Do not treat the food as a direct を-object of 炊事する.

## How the kanji combine

炊 contributes cooking or boiling, and [事](../kanji/事.md) contributes
a task or activity. Cooking qualifies the kind of work.
The on readings are スイ + ジ.
This is not read with 炊く (たく), the separate native verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[料理](料理.md) (りょうり) also names cooking, and can additionally
mean the prepared food or a dish. 調理 (ちょうり) means cooking
or food preparation.
[家事](家事.md) (かじ) is broader housework, including cooking,
cleaning, and laundry.

## Distinctive meaning

炊事 focuses on doing the cooking, not on every household chore
or on culinary arts as an artistic discipline.
Nor is it the meal-eating activity [食事](食事.md) (しょくじ).
For making a particular dish, use a suitable object-taking verb,
such as ご飯を[作る](作る.md) (ごはんをつくる), "prepare a meal."
