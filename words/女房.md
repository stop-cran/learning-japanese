# 女房 (にょうぼう) - wife

## Meaning

女房 means a wife, especially a husband referring to his own wife.
It is a noun, not a title to add automatically to someone's name.

- 女房と二人で旅行に行きました。
  (にょうぼうとふたりでりょこうにいきました。) - I went on a trip with my wife.
- これは女房が選んだお土産です。
  (これはにょうぼうがえらんだおみやげです。) - This is a souvenir my wife chose.

女房と means "with my wife"; 女房が identifies who chose the souvenir.
The speaker's relationship and the context identify whose wife is meant.

## How the kanji combine

[女](../kanji/女.md) concerns a woman, and 房 has a room or chamber association.
The established compound is にょうぼう, not おんな + ふさ.
The component associations alone do not explain the modern wife meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[妻](妻.md) (つま) is the safer starting choice for a neutral reference to one's wife.
[家内](家内.md) (かない) also has this use.
[奥さん](奥さん.md) (おくさん) and [夫人](夫人.md) (ふじん)
are useful comparisons, but their ways of referring to or addressing people differ.
[夫](夫.md) (おっと), "husband," names the other spouse rather than an opposite quality.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 女房 as a complete relationship word. Do not use it as the general noun
for any woman or assume it is the right respectful address for someone else's wife.
In modern wife-use, it can carry a somewhat disparaging tone; its effect depends
on the speaker and context. This is not a claim that every use is insulting.
In historical writing, 女房 can also name a court lady; that is a separate
dictionary sense, not evidence that the wife in an everyday sentence works at a court.
