# 奉仕 (ほうし) — service to others

## Meaning

奉仕 means devoting work to others or to a cause. With する it is a verb; に can mark the person or community served.

- 地域に奉仕したい。 (ちいきにほうししたい。) — I want to serve my community.
- 店の前に「特価でご奉仕」と書いてあった。 (みせのまえに「とっかでごほうし」とかいてあった。) — The sign outside the shop said, “Special prices for our customers.”

## How the kanji combine

[奉](../kanji/奉.md) carries the idea of offering or serving; [仕](../kanji/仕.md) also relates to service. Together they name service, not the occupation of the person providing it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[サービス](サービス.md) can refer to customer service or something offered free. 奉仕 and サービス overlap in sales language, but サービス also names services with no suggestion of personal dedication.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the setting before translating: a community group's 奉仕活動 (ほうしかつどう) is service work; a shop advertising ご奉仕品 (ごほうしひん) is offering specially priced goods, not announcing a volunteer shift.
