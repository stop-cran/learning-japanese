# 奉仕 (ほうし) — service to others

## Meaning

奉仕 means devoting work to others or to a cause. With する it is a verb; に can mark the person or community served. In a shop, 奉仕 can instead mean offering goods at a reduced price or a service free of charge.

- 地域に奉仕したい。 (ちいきにほうししたい。) — I want to serve my community.
- 店で奉仕品を買った。 (みせでほうしひんをかった。) — I bought an item on special offer at the shop.

## How the kanji combine

[奉](../kanji/奉.md) carries the idea of offering or serving; [仕](../kanji/仕.md) also relates to service. Together they name service, not the occupation of the person providing it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[サービス](サービス.md) can refer to customer service or something offered free. 奉仕 and サービス overlap in sales language, but サービス also names services with no suggestion of personal dedication.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the setting before translating: a community group's 奉仕活動 (ほうしかつどう) is service work; a shop advertising 奉仕品 (ほうしひん) is offering specially priced goods, not announcing a volunteer shift.
