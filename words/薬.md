# 薬 (くすり) — medicine

## Meaning

薬 means **medicine or medication**. It includes tablets, liquid medicine, and preparations applied to the body.

- 薬を飲みました。 (くすりをのみました。) — I took the medicine.
- この薬を腕に塗ります。 (このくすりをうでにぬります。) — I apply this medicine to my arm.

Japanese uses 飲む (のむ) for taking oral medicine, including tablets. 塗る (ぬる) means applying or spreading a substance on a surface.

## How the kanji combine

[薬](../kanji/薬.md) is a single character read with the native word くすり. Its on reading ヤク occurs in compounds such as 薬局 (やっきょく), “pharmacy”; it does not replace くすり in this noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

錠剤 (じょうざい) specifically means tablets. 薬 is broader. 毒 (どく), “poison,” offers a meaning contrast, but the distinction depends on the substance and use, not a simple grammatical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

薬 names the medicine, not the medical profession or the act of treatment. Saying 薬を飲む tells us how it is taken, not what dosage or treatment is appropriate.
