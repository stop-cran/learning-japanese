# 詳しい (くわしい) - detailed; knowledgeable

## Meaning

詳しい is an i-adjective. Information can be detailed, while a person can be knowledgeable about a subject. Mark that subject with に: 日本の歴史に詳しい (にほんのれきしにくわしい), "knowledgeable about Japanese history."

- 詳しい説明をお願いします。 (くわしいせつめいをおねがいします。) - Please give me a detailed explanation.
- 姉はこの町の歴史に詳しいです。 (あねはこのまちのれきしにくわしいです。) - My older sister knows a lot about this town's history.

The adverbial form is 詳しく (くわしく): 詳しく説明する, "explain in detail." The negative is 詳しくない (くわしくない).

## How the kanji combine

詳 contributes detail or thorough knowledge and is read くわ here. The kana しい completes the adjective. Learn this native reading with the whole word, rather than treating it as an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[細かい](細かい.md) (こまかい) can describe fine detail, but also small physical size. [正しい](正しい.md) (ただしい) means correct. 疎い (うとい), "unfamiliar; poorly informed," contrasts with being knowledgeable about a field.

## Distinctive meaning

Detail is not a guarantee of accuracy: 詳しい説明 may contain many particulars yet still be mistaken. Likewise, describing a person as 詳しい identifies knowledge of the stated subject, not universal expertise. Preserve the に pattern when saying what someone knows well.
