# 死ぬ (しぬ) — to die

## Meaning

死ぬ means to die. It is an intransitive verb: the subject is the person, animal, or other living thing whose life ends.

- 金魚が死んでしまいました。 (きんぎょが しんで しまいました。) — Sadly, the goldfish died.
- その魚はもう死んでいます。 (その さかなは もう しんで います。) — That fish is already dead.

It is a godan verb ending in ぬ: 死にます (しにます), 死んで (しんで), 死んだ (しんだ).
In the second example, 死んでいる describes the resulting state, "be dead," rather than an ongoing process of dying.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native verb, not a compound. [死](../kanji/死.md) supplies し, and ぬ is the dictionary-form okurigana.
The matching sound of the on reading シ does not make this native verb a kango.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 亡くなる (なくなる), "pass away," is a euphemistic alternative, especially when referring sensitively to a person's death.
- 生きる (いきる), "live," supplies the contrasting idea. 生きている (いきている) means "be alive"; compare [生](../kanji/生.md).

## Distinctive meaning

死ぬ is direct wording. It is normal in factual descriptions and discussions of animals, but may sound blunt when talking about a bereaved person's family.
Adding ます makes the grammar polite; it does not remove that bluntness. Choose 亡くなる when a gentler expression is appropriate.
