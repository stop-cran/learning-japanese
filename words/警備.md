# 警備 (けいび) - guarding; security

## Meaning

警備 is guarding or protective security: watching over a place or event and preparing to prevent trouble.
It can include defense or policing duties, depending on the setting.
警備する is transitive; を marks what is guarded.

- 警備員が入口を警備しています。 (けいびいんがいりぐちをけいびしています。) - A security guard is guarding the entrance.
- 会場の警備を強化しました。 (かいじょうのけいびをきょうかしました。) - We strengthened security at the venue.

警備員 (けいびいん) is a security guard, the person doing the work.
警備 itself can name the work or arrangements.

## How the kanji combine

[警](../kanji/警.md) contributes alertness or warning, and [備](../kanji/備.md) preparation.
These related ideas help recall being prepared and keeping watch.
The on readings combine as ケイ + ビ.
This is a meaning association, not a claim about the word's historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[守る](守る.md) (まもる) means protecting or guarding, among other uses.
[防ぐ](防ぐ.md) (ふせぐ) means preventing or defending against something.
[安全](安全.md) (あんぜん) names safety, the condition sought rather than the guard activity itself.
[警察](警察.md) (けいさつ) means the police; 警備 is not limited to work done by police officers.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 警備 for the guarding function or its organization, not as the ordinary noun for one guard.
An event's 警備 can involve personnel and procedures; the word does not guarantee that no incident will occur.
