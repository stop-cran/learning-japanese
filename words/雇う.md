# 雇う (やとう) - employ; hire

## Meaning

雇う is a transitive godan verb meaning employ or hire someone. The employer is the subject, and the person employed takes を.

- 会社は新しい社員を雇いました。 (かいしゃはあたらしいしゃいんをやといました。) - The company hired a new employee.
- 通訳を一日だけ雇います。 (つうやくをいちにちだけやといます。) - We will hire an interpreter for just one day.

Use 雇います, 雇って, and 雇わない. The second example shows that the work need not be a permanent job.

## How the kanji combine

雇 contributes hiring or employing. The native verb is やとう, with う written as okurigana. This is a single-kanji verb, not a compound with separate on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[働く](働く.md) (はたらく), "work," describes the worker's activity, not the employer's hiring action. 採用する (さいようする) can mean selecting or recruiting someone; 解雇する (かいこする), "dismiss from employment," is a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

雇われる is the passive "be employed/hired": 会社に雇われる (かいしゃにやとわれる), "be employed by a company." Keep the viewpoints separate. Saying 私は社員を雇っています means "I employ staff," not "I am an employee." The relationship may be short-term or continuing; the verb alone does not specify contract length or conditions.
