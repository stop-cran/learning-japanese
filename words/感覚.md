# 感覚 (かんかく) - sense; sensation

## Meaning

感覚 names a bodily sensation or a sense for perceiving and judging something. Useful noun patterns are Nの感覚, "the sensation/sense of N," and 感覚がある, "have sensation/a sense."

- 寒くて指の感覚がなくなりました。 (さむくてゆびのかんかくがなくなりました。) - My fingers lost feeling because of the cold.
- 暗い部屋では時間の感覚が分からなくなります。 (くらいへやではじかんのかんかくがわからなくなります。) - In a dark room, I lose my sense of time.

The first concerns physical feeling; the second concerns one's awareness of time. The word does not by itself mean an emotion.

## How the kanji combine

[感](../kanji/感.md) contributes feeling, and [覚](../kanji/覚.md) contributes perceiving or becoming aware. The related meanings reinforce each other in カン + カク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[気持ち](気持ち.md) (きもち) can describe feelings or how something feels, but 指の感覚 specifically concerns sensation in the fingers. [気分](気分.md) (きぶん) concerns mood or how one feels overall. These words overlap in English translation without being substitutes in every phrase.

## Distinctive meaning

感覚 can be bodily or abstract: 距離の感覚 (きょりのかんかく) means a sense of distance. Read the attached noun to identify what is being perceived. Do not confuse it with 間隔 (かんかく), a different word meaning an interval or gap.
