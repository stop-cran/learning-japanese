# 奨学金 (しょうがくきん) - student financial support

## Meaning

奨学金 is financial support for study. Depending on the scheme, it can be a
scholarship or grant, or a student loan that must be repaid. It is a noun:
奨学金に申し込む (しょうがくきんにもうしこむ) means "apply for student funding."

- 返済の必要がない奨学金を受けています。 (へんさいのひつようがないしょうがくきんをうけています。) - I receive a scholarship that does not have to be repaid.
- 卒業後、奨学金を返しています。 (そつぎょうご、しょうがくきんをかえしています。) - After graduating, I am repaying my student loan.

The first example explicitly excludes repayment; the second makes repayment part
of the situation.

## How the kanji combine

奨 contributes encouraging or supporting, [学](../kanji/学.md) contributes study,
and [金](../kanji/金.md) supplies money. 奨学 describes support for learning;
金 is the financial support itself. The readings are ショウ + ガク + キン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

A grant and a loan are contrasting funding arrangements, not synonyms with
identical conditions. [留学](留学.md) (りゅうがく), "study abroad," is one possible
purpose of an 奨学金, but the word does not require overseas study.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer "free money" from the English gloss "scholarship." Both grant and
student-loan senses belong to the same dictionary entry. Check the particular
program's repayment requirements and eligibility conditions. The noun alone
does not establish whether selection is based on merit, financial need, or
another criterion.
