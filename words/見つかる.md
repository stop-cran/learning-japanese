# 見つかる (みつかる) — be found; turn up

## Meaning

見つかる means to be found or discovered. It is an intransitive godan verb: the thing found takes が. Its forms are 見つかります, 見つかって, and 見つからない.

- なくした鍵が見つかりました。 (なくしたかぎがみつかりました。) — The key I lost has turned up.
- いい仕事が見つかるといいですね。 (いいしごとがみつかるといいですね。) — I hope you find a good job.

English may naturally describe someone "finding" a job, but the Japanese sentence makes the job the subject of 見つかる.

見付かる is a recorded rare spelling of the same word. It does not change the reading みつかる or the intransitive construction.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) supplies the seeing association, followed by つかる in kana. Learn the whole native verb rather than treating its spelling boundary as the conjugation base.

## Synonyms and antonyms

見つける (みつける) is transitive: 鍵を見つける (かぎをみつける), "find the key." 探す (さがす), "search for," describes the search, which may or may not succeed.

## Distinctive meaning

見つかる is not a passive conjugation of 見つける, even though "be found" is a good translation. It reports discovery without requiring an explicit finder.
