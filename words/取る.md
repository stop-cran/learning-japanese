# 取る (とる) — take; pick up

## Meaning

取る describes taking, picking up, or grasping something. This article starts with that physical action.
Mark the object with を.

- 机の上のペンを取ってください。 (つくえのうえのペンをとってください。) — Please take the pen on the desk.

Despite ending in る, it is a godan verb: 取ります (とります), 取った (とった), 取って (とって),
and 取らない (とらない). Do not form its polite version by simply removing る.

## How the kanji combine

[取](../kanji/取.md) carries the taking meaning; る completes the native verb.
This is a single-kanji word with a kana ending, not a compound of separate kanji meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[持つ](持つ.md) (もつ) also describes holding or taking something. In a concrete sequence, 取る can focus
on picking up the pen, while 持っている (もっている) describes holding it afterward.
Their meanings overlap, so this is a contrast between these uses, not an absolute boundary.

[置く](置く.md) (おく), "put; place," provides the reverse action in this scene: putting the pen down.

## Distinctive meaning

Taking an object does not necessarily mean keeping it or becoming its owner.
取る has many further uses in fixed combinations, but a learner should not assume that every English
"take" expression can be translated with this spelling.
