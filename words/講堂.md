# 講堂 (こうどう) — auditorium; assembly hall

## Meaning

講堂 is a large hall for lectures, assemblies, ceremonies, or similar gatherings, often in a school. It names the room or building, not the event taking place there.

卒業式は講堂で行います。 (そつぎょうしきはこうどうでおこないます。) — We will hold the graduation ceremony in the auditorium.

## How the kanji combine

講 contributes the idea of lecturing or explaining, and [堂](../kanji/堂.md) means a hall. The on readings こう + どう form the compound. Both vowels are long.

## Synonyms and antonyms

教室 (きょうしつ) is an ordinary classroom. 体育館 (たいいくかん) is a gymnasium; it may also host assemblies, but its name emphasizes physical education. ホール is a broader loanword for a hall.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 講堂で for an event held there and 講堂に for movement toward it. Do not confuse こうどう with the homophone 行動, "action; behavior": a shared pronunciation does not make them the same word.
