# 議論 (ぎろん) — discussion; argument

## Meaning

議論 is discussion or argument about an issue. Use 議論する for the activity.
A topic can be introduced with について, and a discussion partner with と.

- この問題について議論しました。 (このもんだいについてぎろんしました。) — We discussed this problem.
- 友達と議論しました。 (ともだちとぎろんしました。) — I had a discussion with a friend.

## How the kanji combine

[議](../kanji/議.md) contributes deliberation or discussion, and
[論](../kanji/論.md) contributes argument or discourse. The meanings reinforce each other:
ideas are being discussed, not merely pronounced aloud. The on readings are ギ + ロン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

話し合い (はなしあい) also means a discussion or talk. It overlaps with 議論 rather than
forming a clean opposite.
[相談](../words/相談.md) (そうだん) is useful when the purpose is consultation or asking someone for advice;
that is a different focus from arguing through an issue.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read the English gloss "argument" as a guarantee of anger. 議論 can involve differing
positions, but the word alone does not tell you that the speakers are quarrelling.
Conversely, it names a discussion, not automatically a decision or agreement reached afterward.
