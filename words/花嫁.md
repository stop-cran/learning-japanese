# 花嫁 (はなよめ) - bride

## Meaning

花嫁 is a bride: a woman in the wedding or newly married role.
It is not the ordinary label for every married woman at every later stage of life.

- 花嫁は白いドレスを着ていました。 (はなよめはしろいドレスをきていました。) - The bride was wearing a white dress.
- 花嫁にお祝いの言葉を伝えました。 (はなよめにおいわいのことばをつたえました。) - I offered the bride my congratulations.

## How the kanji combine

[花](../kanji/花.md) is はな, flower, and 嫁 is よめ, bride or daughter-in-law.
Learn the established native compound はなよめ.
The flower association is a useful image for the celebratory role, not a requirement that the bride carry flowers.

## Synonyms and antonyms

新婦 (しんぷ) is another word for a bride, often paired with 新郎 (しんろう), bridegroom.
花婿 (はなむこ) is the corresponding groom.
[嫁](嫁.md) (よめ) overlaps with bride but can also mean daughter-in-law and, in some usage, wife.
[妻](妻.md) (つま) is wife more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

花嫁 foregrounds the wedding or newlywed role; 妻 foregrounds the marital relationship.
The same person may be both, so this is a difference of perspective, not a claim that the categories exclude each other.
