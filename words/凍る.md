# 凍る (こおる) - freeze; become frozen

## Meaning

凍る is an intransitive godan verb meaning to freeze or become frozen over. The water, surface, or other thing that freezes takes が.

- バケツの水が凍りました。 (バケツのみずがこおりました。) - The water in the bucket froze.
- 道が凍っているので、気をつけてください。 (みちがこおっているので、きをつけてください。) - The road is icy, so please be careful.

凍っている (こおっている) describes a frozen state. The past is 凍った (こおった), and the negative is 凍らない (こおらない).

## How the kanji combine

凍 supplies the freezing meaning, with る as the written verb ending. Learn こおる with おお: it is not written こうる. This is one native verb, not a multi-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冷える](冷える.md) (ひえる) means to become cold. Something can cool without freezing.

溶ける (とける), "melt," is a practical reverse process for ice. 凍らせる (こおらせる), "freeze something; cause it to freeze," shifts attention to an agent causing the change.

## Distinctive meaning

English "freeze" can be transitive or intransitive, but this Japanese verb takes the freezing thing as subject. Compare 水が凍る, "water freezes," with 水を凍らせる, "freeze water." A surface described as 凍っている may be covered with ice; the sentence need not mean that every material in the object has frozen solid.
