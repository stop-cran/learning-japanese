# 砂糖 (さとう)

## Meaning

砂糖 means sugar, especially the sweet ingredient added to food and drinks. It names a substance rather than a countable serving.

- コーヒーに砂糖を入れますか。 (コーヒーに さとうを いれますか。) — Do you put sugar in your coffee?

砂糖を入れる marks sugar with を and the drink receiving it with に. For an amount, add an expression such as 少し (すこし), "a little."

## How the kanji combine

砂 means sand, while 糖 concerns sugar. The granular appearance is a useful meaning association, not a claim that sugar contains sand. The on readings are さ + とう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

シュガー is the English-derived alternative. 塩 (しお), "salt," is another seasoning, not an antonym. 甘い (あまい), "sweet," describes a taste rather than naming sugar.

## Distinctive meaning

Food can be sweet without added 砂糖. Conversely, mentioning 砂糖 identifies an ingredient, not how sweet the finished dish tastes. Do not use 砂糖 as the adjective "sweet."
