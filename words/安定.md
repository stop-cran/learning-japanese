# 安定 (あんてい) - stability; steadiness

## Meaning

安定 is stability or steadiness. 安定する is an intransitive verb: something **が安定する**, "becomes stable." 安定している describes an ongoing stable condition, and 安定した can modify a noun.

- 収入が安定しています。 (しゅうにゅうがあんていしています。) - My income is steady.
- 安定した仕事を探しています。 (あんていしたしごとをさがしています。) - I am looking for a stable job.

The word can concern finances, a physical balance, or someone's condition. The context supplies what is no longer fluctuating or insecure.

## How the kanji combine

[安](../kanji/安.md) contributes ease or security, and [定](../kanji/定.md) contributes being settled or fixed. The two reinforce the idea of a settled, steady condition. The readings are アン + テイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[安心](安心.md) concerns feeling reassured; a person may feel reassured because their income is stable, but the words describe different things. [安全](安全.md) means safety, not steadiness. 不安定 (ふあんてい), "unstable," is the direct negative counterpart.

## Distinctive meaning

安定 does not require complete immobility or an unchanging numerical value. An income or condition can be described as stable despite small variations. Learn 安定した生活 (あんていしたせいかつ), "a stable life," rather than assuming every noun use takes な.
