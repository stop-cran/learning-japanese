# 正式 (せいしき) - official; formal

## Meaning

正式 describes something as official, proper, or following the recognized form. Use 正式な before a noun and 正式に before a verb. The dictionary also permits noun-modifying の.

- 正式な招待状が届きました。 (せいしきなしょうたいじょうがとどきました。) - An official invitation arrived.
- 二人は正式に結婚しました。 (ふたりはせいしきにけっこんしました。) - The two officially married.

正式な名前 (せいしきななまえ) is an official or full proper name, in contrast to a nickname or provisional label. 正式 does not itself say which authority recognizes the name or procedure.

## How the kanji combine

[正](../kanji/正.md) contributes correctness or the proper form, and [式](../kanji/式.md) contributes a form, rule, or ceremony. Together they describe conformity to an established form. The reading is セイ + シキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

公式 (こうしき) also means official in contexts such as announcements and records. The words overlap, but 正式 particularly helps contrast a recognized arrangement with a temporary or informal one.

非公式 (ひこうしき), "unofficial," is a contextual opposite. [丁寧](丁寧.md) (ていねい), "polite; careful," concerns manner rather than official standing.

## Distinctive meaning

English "formal" can describe stiff language or dress. 正式 should not automatically replace every use of that English adjective. A 正式な決定 (せいしきなけってい), "official decision," concerns the decision's standing, not whether the person announcing it speaks politely.
