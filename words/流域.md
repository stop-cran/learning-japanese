# 流域 (りゅういき) - river basin; drainage area

## Meaning

流域 is a river's basin or catchment area: the area whose water drains
into that river and its tributaries. It can also describe the area along
a river. It is a noun.

- この川の流域には多くの町があります。 (このかわのりゅういきにはおおくのまちがあります。) - There are many towns in this river's basin.
- 大雨で流域の住民に避難が呼び掛けられました。 (おおあめでりゅういきのじゅうみんにひなんがよびかけられました。) - Heavy rain prompted calls for residents of the basin to evacuate.

## How the kanji combine

[流](../kanji/流.md) contributes flowing water, and
[域](../kanji/域.md) an area or range.
The combination identifies an area by the river that drains it.
The on readings are リュウ + イキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[盆地](盆地.md) (ぼんち) names a basin-shaped landform surrounded by
higher ground. [谷](谷.md) (たに) is a valley.
[地域](地域.md) (ちいき) and [区域](区域.md) (くいき) are broader
area terms. English "basin," "valley" or "area" can overlap in context;
the Japanese words do not all specify the same boundary.

## Distinctive meaning

A drainage basin is not just the visible river channel, nor must the
whole basin be a single hollow enclosed by mountains.
The catchment sense follows where water drains; the along-river use
need not be a precise hydrological boundary.
