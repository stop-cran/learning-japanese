# 蒔く (まく) - sow seeds

## Meaning

蒔く means sow seeds. It is a transitive godan verb: the seed takes を, and the place can take に.

- 畑に種を蒔きました。 (はたけにたねをまきました。) - I sowed seeds in the field.
- 春になったら、花の種をまきます。 (はるになったら、はなのたねをまきます。) - When spring comes, I will sow flower seeds.

The dictionary marks this word as usually written in kana. Learn 種をまく alongside the kanji spelling.

## How the kanji combine

蒔 contributes the sowing meaning; く completes the native verb. Read まく, with forms 蒔きます (まきます), 蒔いて (まいて), and 蒔いた (まいた). There is no multi-kanji compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[植える](植える.md) (うえる), "plant," commonly describes putting a plant or seedling into the ground. 蒔く focuses on sowing seed.

[撒く](撒く.md) (まく) means scatter or sprinkle and can overlap scattering seed. [巻く](巻く.md), also まく, is a separate verb meaning wind or roll up; a shared reading does not make the three words interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

種をまく can be figurative: doing something that produces later consequences, as in sowing the seeds of a problem. Do not assume that every such use concerns farming.

The dictionary also records sprinkling gold or silver powder in lacquer decoration, as in the art of 蒔絵 (まきえ). This specialized recognition sense is not a claim that 蒔く replaces 撒く for every material that can be scattered.
