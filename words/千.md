# 千 (せん) - thousand

## Meaning

千 means one thousand and forms larger numbers and quantities. The basic standalone value is 1,000; a following counter or unit tells you what is being counted.

- この本は千円です。 (このほんはせんえんです。) - This book costs one thousand yen.
- 会場に三千人が集まりました。 (かいじょうにさんぜんにんがあつまりました。) - Three thousand people gathered at the venue.

## How the kanji combine

[千](../kanji/千.md) uses the on reading せん here. Learn sound changes in number combinations: 三千 is さんぜん and 八千 is はっせん, while 二千 is にせん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

百 (ひゃく) is one hundred, and 万 (まん) is ten thousand. These are neighboring units in the number system, not opposites of 千.

## Distinctive meaning

千 is a numeral, not a general object counter. In 千円, 円 supplies the currency unit; in 千人 (せんにん), 人 counts people. Do not add a general counter merely because English uses a plural noun after "thousand."
