# 幕 (まく) - curtain; act in a play

## Meaning

幕 is a noun for a curtain or hanging screen, particularly useful in theater vocabulary. It also names or counts an act of a play.

- 音楽が始まると、幕が上がりました。 (おんがくがはじまると、まくがあがりました。) - When the music began, the curtain rose.
- この劇は三幕に分かれています。 (このげきはさんまくにわかれています。) - This play is divided into three acts.

第一幕 (だいいちまく) means "Act One." An act is a division of the work, not necessarily the entire play.

## How the kanji combine

幕 is one kanji, using the on reading マク. There is no compound relationship to split. Its theatrical curtain sense helps connect the physical object with an act or the conclusion of a performance.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[カーテン](カーテン.md) also means a curtain and is useful for household window curtains. [舞台](舞台.md) (ぶたい) names a stage, not its curtain. [終わり](終わり.md) (おわり) is a broader word for an end.

## Distinctive meaning

幕を開ける (まくをあける), "raise/open the curtain," can figuratively mean begin; 幕を閉じる (まくをとじる) can mean bring something to a close. Thus 長い歴史に幕を閉じた (ながいれきしにまくをとじた) describes closing a long chapter of history, without requiring a real theater curtain.
