# 夕刊 (ゆうかん) - evening newspaper edition

## Meaning

夕刊 is the evening edition of a newspaper.

- 駅で夕刊を買いました。
  (えきでゆうかんをかいました。) - I bought the evening paper at the station.
- 夕刊にそのニュースが載っていました。
  (ゆうかんにそのニュースがのっていました。) - That news was in the evening edition.

夕刊を読む (ゆうかんをよむ) means to read the evening paper. It identifies the edition, not merely the time someone happens to read a paper.

## How the kanji combine

[夕](../kanji/夕.md) supplies evening with the native reading ゆう; [刊](../kanji/刊.md) contributes publication or issue with on カン. This is mixed kun-plus-on reading, not two on readings.

The collection stores this native-first mixed compound as wago, as with [夕飯](夕飯.md) (ゆうはん).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[新聞](新聞.md) (しんぶん), newspaper, is broader. 朝刊 (ちょうかん), morning edition, is the useful contrast.

[夕方](夕方.md) (ゆうがた) names early evening, not a newspaper edition.

## Distinctive meaning

A morning edition read in the evening does not thereby become a 夕刊. Conversely, an evening edition remains a 夕刊 if read later.

The noun does not guarantee that every newspaper issues both a morning and an evening edition.
