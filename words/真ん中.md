# 真ん中 (まんなか) - middle; center

## Meaning

真ん中 is the middle or center of a space, group, or arrangement. It is a noun.

- 部屋の真ん中にテーブルがあります。 (へやのまんなかにテーブルがあります。) - There is a table in the middle of the room.
- 真ん中の席に座りました。 (まんなかのせきにすわりました。) - I sat in the middle seat.

Use の to specify what has a middle or to modify the item located there.

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md), read ま, adds emphasis to [中](../kanji/中.md), なか, "middle; inside." The established spoken form includes ん: まんなか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

中央 (ちゅうおう) also means center and often appears in more formal descriptions. 端 (はし), "edge; end," and 隅 (すみ), "corner," give useful positional contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

中 alone can mean inside, without specifying the center. 真ん中 points to the middle, though ordinary conversation does not always imply a mathematically exact midpoint.
