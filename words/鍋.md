# 鍋 (なべ) - cooking pot; hot pot

## Meaning

鍋 is a noun for a cooking pot or pan, including a saucepan. It can also abbreviate 鍋料理 (なべりょうり), a hot-pot dish.

- 鍋に水を入れてください。 (なべにみずをいれてください。) - Please put water in the pot.
- 今夜は家族で鍋を食べます。 (こんやはかぞくでなべをたべます。) - Tonight, our family is having hot pot.

In the first example, に marks the container. In the second, 鍋 refers to the food, not to eating the cookware. 鍋を洗う (なべをあらう) means washing the pot.

## How the kanji combine

鍋 is a single character read with the native reading なべ. There is no multi-kanji relationship to divide. In 鍋料理, the added 料理 (りょうり) makes the food sense explicit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

フライパン identifies a frying pan; やかん is a kettle. These are related kitchen vessels rather than opposites. [料理](料理.md) (りょうり), if used alone, means cooking or a dish more broadly and does not specifically identify hot pot.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb often clarifies the sense: buy or wash a 鍋, but eat 鍋 as a meal. The noun does not specify one required material, size, or recipe. A pot can be described more precisely with modifiers such as 大きな鍋 (おおきななべ), "a large pot."
