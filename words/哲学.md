# 哲学 (てつがく) - philosophy

## Meaning

哲学 is the noun "philosophy." It can name the academic field or a person's considered approach to life or an activity. With a study verb, use 哲学を学ぶ (てつがくをまなぶ), "study philosophy."

- 大学で哲学を学んでいます。 (だいがくでてつがくをまなんでいます。) - I am studying philosophy at university.
- 彼女には独自の人生哲学があります。 (かのじょにはどくじのじんせいてつがくがあります。) - She has her own philosophy of life.

哲学の本 (てつがくのほん) is a philosophy book. 哲学的な問題 (てつがくてきなもんだい) means a philosophical question; the 的な form makes the adjective-like connection explicit.

## How the kanji combine

哲 contributes wisdom or sagacity, and [学](../kanji/学.md) contributes learning or a field of study. Together they name philosophy. This component explanation is a memory aid, not a full history of the Japanese term.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[考え](考え.md) (かんがえ) is a thought, idea, or view. A single passing thought is not necessarily a 哲学. [科学](科学.md) (かがく), "science," names another field of inquiry, not the opposite of philosophy.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes a university subject from a guiding outlook. 人生哲学 need not mean that its holder studies academic philosophy professionally. Conversely, 哲学を学ぶ does not merely mean thinking hard about a practical problem; it can specifically identify what someone studies.
