# もっと (もっと) — more; further

## Meaning

もっと asks for or describes a greater amount or degree than the present or understood standard. It is an adverb.

- もっとゆっくり話してください。 (もっとゆっくりはなしてください。) — Please speak more slowly.
- 日本語をもっと勉強したいです。 (にほんごをもっとべんきょうしたいです。) — I want to study Japanese more.

## How the kanji combine

もっと is written in hiragana, with a small っ before と. Hold a brief closure before と; do not read the small っ as a separate つ syllable. There are no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

さらに also means "further; still more." 一番 (いちばん) means "most" or "number one" in a comparison, not simply "more." もう少し (もうすこし), "a little more," specifies a smaller additional amount.

## Distinctive meaning

The point of comparison can be implicit. In the first example, the listener's current speaking speed provides it. もっと大きい (もっとおおきい) means "bigger," not "biggest." For "one more apple," りんごをもう一つ (りんごをもうひとつ) specifies the additional count more clearly than もっと alone.
