# 夫 (おっと) — husband

## Meaning

夫 means "husband." It is a neutral relationship noun, especially useful when referring to your own husband in conversation with other people.

夫は今、仕事をしています。 (おっとはいま、しごとをしています。) — My husband is working now.

## How the kanji combine

[夫](../kanji/夫.md) is a single kanji with the native reading おっと. The small っ matters: pause before the final と. The on reading ふう occurs in 夫婦 (ふうふ), "married couple."

## Synonyms and antonyms

妻 (つま) is "wife," the corresponding spouse term rather than a logical opposite. ご主人 (ごしゅじん) is a conventional respectful way to refer to someone else's husband. 配偶者 (はいぐうしゃ) means "spouse" in formal contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

夫 is a reference term, not ordinarily how you call your husband to get his attention. Relationship, context, and personal preference influence alternatives such as 主人 (しゅじん) and 旦那 (だんな); they are not simply interchangeable levels of politeness.
