# おはよう (おはよう) - good morning

## Meaning

おはよう is the familiar morning greeting. おはようございます is the more polite form.

- A: おはよう。よく眠れた？ (おはよう。よくねむれた？) - Morning. Did you sleep well?
- B: おはよう。うん、よく眠れたよ。 (おはよう。うん、よくねむれたよ。) - Morning. Yes, I slept well.
- 先生、おはようございます。 (せんせい、おはようございます。) - Good morning, teacher.

## How the kanji combine

The expression is usually written in kana. お早う is also recorded, with [早](../kanji/早.md), but this card uses おはよう. Preserve the final う in ordinary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

おはようございます has the same greeting meaning with greater politeness. [おやすみ](おやすみ.md), "good night," is a different greeting, not a polite version of おはよう.

## Distinctive meaning

Some industries and workplaces use おはよう on arrival or at the start of work even outside the morning. That is a setting-specific convention, not a rule that the greeting is appropriate at every hour with everyone. Without that convention, learn its ordinary morning use.
