# 辺り (あたり) - vicinity; around

## Meaning

辺り is the area around a place, without specifying an exact boundary. Use この辺り for "around here" and place + の辺り for "the area around a place." It is a noun, so particles such as に and で follow it.

- この辺りに郵便局はありますか。 (このあたりにゆうびんきょくはありますか。) - Is there a post office around here?
- 駅の辺りで会いましょう。 (えきのあたりであいましょう。) - Let's meet near the station.

After a time expression, kana あたり can also mark approximation: 来週あたり (らいしゅうあたり), "sometime around next week."

## How the kanji combine

辺 concerns an area or surroundings. The native word is あたり; り is its written kana ending. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[近く](近く.md) (ちかく) emphasizes nearness, while [周り](周り.md) (まわり) can focus on what surrounds something. Their meanings overlap with 辺り, but the point of reference still matters. [頃](頃.md) (ころ) is useful for approximate times rather than a neighborhood.

## Distinctive meaning

この辺り gives a loose location, not a measured distance. The reference point can be supplied by the situation: a speaker may mean the street, neighborhood, or part of a map being discussed.
