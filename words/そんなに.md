# そんなに (そんなに) — that much

## Meaning

そんなに means "that much; so much; to that extent." It refers to a degree suggested by the conversation or the listener's behavior.

そんなに急がなくてもいいです。（そんなにいそがなくてもいいです。） — You do not need to hurry that much.

このかばんはそんなに高くないです。（このかばんはそんなにたかくないです。） — This bag is not that expensive.

## How the kanji combine

This kana expression combines そんな, "that kind of," with に to modify an action or degree. It is not a kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

こんなに means "this much," emphasizing the speaker's immediate situation. あんなに points to "that much" in a more distant or shared situation.

## Distinctive meaning

With a negative, そんなに often means "not very; not that much." It need not deny the quality completely. Before a noun use そんな, as in そんな話（そんなはなし）, "that kind of story," without に.
