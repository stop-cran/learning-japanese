# ページ - page

## Meaning

ページ means a page. After a number it can count pages or identify a numbered page.

- 十ページ読みました。 (じゅっページよみました。) - I read ten pages.
- 五ページを開いてください。 (ごページをひらいてください。) - Please open to page five.

The first sentence states a quantity; the second gives a location in the book. Both use a number directly before ページ.
For the count of ten pages, じっページ is also accepted alongside じゅっページ.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is katakana, from English "page." The dictionary also records 頁 with the reading ページ, but the loanword does not require that character in everyday writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

枚 (まい) counts flat objects such as sheets of paper. A sheet may have two printed pages, so counting 枚 and counting ページ need not give the same total.

## Distinctive meaning

ページ can refer to a printed page or a digital page, such as a web page. In either medium, learn the surrounding sentence to decide whether a number means "this many pages" or "page number..."
