# てくてく — walking steadily along

## Meaning

Continuing on foot at a steady pace, often for a noticeable distance.

- 駅から家まで、てくてく歩いた。 — I walked steadily all the way home from the station.
- 川に沿（そ）って一時間てくてく歩いた。 — I walked along the river for an hour.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とぼとぼ](とぼとぼ.md) often suggests weary or dejected plodding, while [すたすた](すたすた.md) suggests a brisker walk. These are overlapping impressions, not measured walking speeds.

## Distinctive meaning

てくてく歩く（あるく）does not by itself say that the walker is sad, lost, or reluctant. It foregrounds the continued steps. Unlike [うろうろ](うろうろ.md), it does not inherently suggest aimless wandering.
