# タクシー

## Meaning

タクシー means a taxi or cab hired to take passengers to a destination.

- タクシーで駅に行きました。 (タクシーで えきに いきました。) — I went to the station by taxi.
- ここでタクシーを降ります。 (ここで タクシーを おります。) — I will get out of the taxi here.

で marks the means of transport in the first example. Use タクシーに乗る (タクシーにのる) for getting in or taking a taxi, and を降りる for getting out.

## How the kanji combine

The word is a katakana loan. The final ー lengthens シ, so keep the long vowel in タクシー. Shorter dictionary variants are not the basic spelling taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ハイヤー is a related hired-car service term, but booking and service arrangements may differ. バス and 電車 are other transport choices, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

タクシー names the vehicle or service, not the driver. タクシーの運転手 (タクシーのうんてんしゅ) means taxi driver. Learn the particles with the whole travel expression rather than assigning one particle permanently to the noun.
