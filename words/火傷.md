# 火傷 (やけど) - burn injury; scald

## Meaning

火傷 is a burn or scald injury. It also forms 火傷する (やけどする), to get burned or scalded. The ordinary kana spelling やけど is common.

- 熱いお湯で手に火傷をしました。
  (あついおゆでてにやけどをしました。) - I scalded my hand with hot water.
- 熱い鍋に触って火傷をした。
  (あついなべにさわってやけどをした。) - I touched a hot pot and got burned.

手に marks the injured location in the first example. 火傷をする and 火傷する both occur.

## How the kanji combine

[火](../kanji/火.md) means fire, and 傷 contributes injury. The spelling describes the kind of injury, but やけど is a whole-word special reading, not カ plus an on reading of 傷.

傷 has no kanji card in the current collection; it still belongs in the written word's metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怪我](怪我.md) (けが), injury, and [傷](傷.md) (きず), wound or injury, are broader words. 熱傷 (ねっしょう) is a related medical term for a burn.

Combustion and injury are not the same: [燃える](燃える.md) (もえる) describes burning or being on fire, whereas 火傷 names the injury.

## Distinctive meaning

A scald from hot liquid is a 火傷 even without a flame. Do not read this spelling as かしょう in the ordinary やけど expressions taught here.

The dictionary also records figurative getting one's fingers burned, for example suffering through a risky investment. That use describes an adverse experience, not a literal skin injury.
