# 定規 (じょうぎ) — ruler

## Meaning

定規 is a ruler. It names the physical tool, as in drawing a straight line, rather than a rule
that tells people how to behave.

- 定規でまっすぐな線を引きます。 (じょうぎでまっすぐなせんをひきます。) — I draw a straight line with a ruler.
- 定規を貸してください。 (じょうぎをかしてください。) — Please lend me a ruler.

In the first sentence, で marks the tool used.

## How the kanji combine

[定](../kanji/定.md) contributes the idea of something fixed or established,
and [規](../kanji/規.md) that of a measure or standard. Together they name a tool that
provides a fixed guide. The on-reading compound is じょうぎ: the 規 reading キ is voiced to ギ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

物差し (ものさし) also means a ruler or measuring stick. It overlaps with 定規 as the name
of a tool; its dictionary senses also include a figurative standard or yardstick.
That does not make every use of the two words interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

The character 規 appears in words about rules, but the selected meaning of 定規 is concrete.
In the examples, you can use it or lend it. Keep the complete reading じょうぎ together
rather than guessing a reading from one character at a time.
