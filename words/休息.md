# 休息 (きゅうそく) - rest; respite

## Meaning

休息 is rest, respite, or relaxation. It is a noun and an intransitive する-verb. 休息を取る (きゅうそくをとる) means to take a rest.

- 仕事の後で、十分な休息を取りました。
  (しごとのあとで、じゅうぶんなきゅうそくをとりました。) - I got plenty of rest after work.
- 木陰でしばらく休息しました。
  (こかげでしばらくきゅうそくしました。) - We rested in the shade for a while.

Neither sentence specifies that the person went to sleep.

## How the kanji combine

[休](../kanji/休.md) concerns resting; [息](../kanji/息.md) contributes breath or repose. Their associated meanings suggest taking a breather. The on readings are キュウ + ソク, not the same sounds as 休憩 (きゅうけい).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[休憩](休憩.md) typically frames rest as a break within an activity. [休養](休養.md) (きゅうよう) emphasizes resting to restore one's strength or condition. [休む](休む.md) (やすむ) is a broader everyday verb. These perspectives overlap; they do not impose a fixed number of minutes.

## Distinctive meaning

Rest can occur during work or afterward, and it need not be a holiday. Distinguish the same-reading [急速](急速.md) (きゅうそく), "rapid": identical pronunciation does not make the words related in meaning.
