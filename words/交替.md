# 交替 (こうたい) - replacement; taking turns

## Meaning

交替 means alternation, replacement, a change of shifts, or taking turns.
It is a noun used with する. 交代 is another spelling of the same word,
also read こうたい.

- 二人で交替しながら運転します。 (ふたりでこうたいしながらうんてんします。) - The two of us take turns driving.
- 次の駅で運転手が交替します。 (つぎのえきでうんてんしゅがこうたいします。) - The driver changes at the next station.

Personが交替する describes a change of personnel; Aと交替する can
identify the person one changes places with. 選手を交替させる
(せんしゅをこうたいさせる) means "substitute a player," with を marking
the person whose replacement is brought about.

## How the kanji combine

[交](../kanji/交.md) contributes exchanging or alternation, and
[替](../kanji/替.md) contributes replacing. Together they concern one
person or thing taking another's place. The on readings are コウ + タイ.
In 交代, 代 likewise contributes substitution.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[交換](交換.md) (こうかん) concerns exchange or replacement.
[代わる](代わる.md) (かわる) can mean take someone's place, while
[替える](替える.md) (かえる) actively replaces something.
These overlap, but their particle patterns and customary objects differ.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not impose an absolute rule that 交代 happens only once while 交替
must repeat. Both spellings cover changes of roles and turns.
"Relief" here can mean relieving someone on duty, not automatically rescue
or a feeling of reassurance. "Rotation" can mean rotating duties, not
necessarily physical spinning. Context narrows these broad English glosses.
