# 産地 (さんち) - producing area; place of origin

## Meaning

産地 is the place where something is produced or comes from, such as a growing region for fruit or a manufacturing area. It is a noun: 産地を調べる means to check the place of origin.

- このりんごの産地は青森です。 (このりんごのさんちはあおもりです。) - These apples come from Aomori.
- 野菜を買うときは産地を確認します。 (やさいをかうときはさんちをかくにんします。) - When I buy vegetables, I check where they were grown.

The dictionary also records a plant or animal's locality or habitat, and a colloquial use for a person's birthplace. The product examples are not a rule excluding those uses.

## How the kanji combine

[産](../kanji/産.md) contributes producing or giving birth, and [地](../kanji/地.md) contributes place or land. Production qualifies the place. The on readings サン + チ give さんち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[原産](原産.md) げんさん concerns native or original origin; 原産地 (げんさんち) explicitly names that place. 生産地 (せいさんち) identifies a production area. [出身](出身.md) しゅっしん and [故郷](故郷.md) こきょう are useful for a person's origins or hometown. Their uses overlap in some contexts, not in every product label.

## Distinctive meaning

産地 tells you where something comes from, not automatically where it was sold, packed, or shipped. It does not itself certify quality. A region can be a fruit's 産地 even when the seller is elsewhere.
