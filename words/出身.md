# 出身 (しゅっしん) - place or background of origin

## Meaning

出身 identifies where someone comes from, including a place, school, or social background. It is a noun, not a bare verb meaning "graduate." Put the origin directly before it: 大阪出身 (おおさかしゅっしん), "from Osaka."

- 私は大阪出身です。 (わたしはおおさかしゅっしんです。) - I am from Osaka.
- 彼女はこの大学の出身です。 (かのじょはこのだいがくのしゅっしんです。) - This is the university she comes from.

The school expression identifies an educational background. To state a graduation event explicitly, use [卒業](卒業.md)する, as in この大学を卒業しました, "She graduated from this university."

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) contributes coming out or originating, and 身 contributes the body or person. Together they point to a person's origins. The reading しゅっしん has a small っ: it is not しゅつしん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[生まれ](生まれ.md) (うまれ) can identify birth or birthplace. 出身 is broader: the place someone regards as their home background need not be reduced to the building or town where their birth occurred.

[住所](住所.md) (じゅうしょ) is an address, not an origin.

## Distinctive meaning

出身 does not automatically identify current residence, citizenship, or a date of graduation. 出身はどちらですか (しゅっしんはどちらですか), "Where are you from?", asks about background; context determines whether a place, school, or another origin is relevant. There is no useful general antonym.
