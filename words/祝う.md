# 祝う (いわう) - celebrate; congratulate

## Meaning

祝う is a transitive godan verb for celebrating a happy event or congratulating
someone on it. The event takes を: 誕生日を祝う (たんじょうびをいわう),
"celebrate a birthday." Useful forms are 祝います, 祝って, and 祝った.

- 家族で祖母の誕生日を祝いました。 (かぞくでそぼのたんじょうびをいわいました。) - We celebrated my grandmother's birthday as a family.
- 友達の合格を祝って、みんなで食事をしました。 (ともだちのごうかくをいわって、みんなでしょくじをしました。) - We had a meal together to celebrate our friend's passing the exam.

The object can be an achievement, not just an anniversary or holiday.

## How the kanji combine

祝 writes the native verb stem いわ-, with う as the dictionary-form ending.
The character conveys celebration or congratulation. There is no second written
component to combine, and the reading here is not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お祝い](お祝い.md) (おいわい) is the related noun, "celebration" or a
congratulatory gift. [おめでとう](おめでとう.md) is something you say:
"Congratulations!" Neither replaces the conjugated verb unchanged.
[悲しむ](悲しむ.md) (かなしむ), "grieve," contrasts emotionally rather than
being an exact opposite in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

祝う describes the act of marking a happy occasion. The celebration might involve
a meal, a message, or a gift; the verb does not prescribe a party.
友達の合格を祝う makes clear what is being celebrated, whereas merely saying
"congratulations" in English can leave that event unstated.
