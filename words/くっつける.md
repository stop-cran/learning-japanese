# くっつける - attach; put side by side

## Meaning

くっつける means attach or stick something to something else, including pasting or gluing. It is transitive: the thing moved or attached takes を; its destination can take に.

- 紙をのりでくっつけました。 (かみをのりでくっつけました。) - I glued the paper together.
- 二つの机をくっつけました。 (ふたつのつくえをくっつけました。) - I pushed the two desks together.

The second example is an additional place-together use: the desks need not be glued. The ichidan forms include くっつけます, くっつけて, くっつけない.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is the usual writing. The dictionary also records くっ付ける, containing [付](../kanji/付.md), attach.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くっつく](くっつく.md) is intransitive: 紙がくっつく, the paper sticks together. [付ける](付ける.md) (つける) is a broader attach/add verb, while [貼る](貼る.md) (はる) is useful for sticking a sheet, label or similar item onto a surface.

[並べる](並べる.md) (ならべる) arranges things in a row or alongside each other; it does not by itself require them to touch.

## Distinctive meaning

Physical contact or joining is central to the examples, not necessarily a permanent bond. The dictionary also records a colloquial matchmaking use, getting people married; that is not a neutral substitute for every use of the marriage noun.
