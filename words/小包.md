# 小包 (こづつみ) - parcel; package

## Meaning

小包 is a noun for a parcel or package, especially one sent or received.
Use **小包を送る** (こづつみをおくる), "send a parcel," and **小包が届く**
(こづつみがとどく), "a parcel arrives."

- 友人に小包を送りました。 (ゆうじんにこづつみをおくりました。) - I sent a parcel to a friend.
- 母から小包が届きました。 (ははからこづつみがとどきました。) - A parcel arrived from my mother.

に marks the recipient in the first example; から identifies the sender in the second.
小包を開ける (こづつみをあける) means "open a package."

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) has the native reading こ, "small," and 包 represents 包み (つつみ), "a bundle/wrapped thing."
The initial つ voices to づ in こづつみ. Keep that **づ** in the kana spelling.
The components form a modifier-plus-head combination, but the lexical meaning is a parcel, not a postal size specification.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[荷物](荷物.md) (にもつ) is broader: luggage, baggage, or things being carried or shipped.
[包み](包み.md) (つつみ) focuses on something wrapped.
[手紙](手紙.md) (てがみ), "letter," is another kind of mail, not an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

小包 identifies the packaged item, not its contents or the wrapping process.
For "wrap something," use [包む](包む.md) (つつむ).
A parcel may contain a gift, books, or other objects; the noun itself does not say what has been sent.
