# 一生懸命 (いっしょうけんめい) — with utmost effort

## Meaning

一生懸命 describes doing something as hard as one can. Before a verb, both 一生懸命 and 一生懸命に are used.

- 試験のために一生懸命勉強しました。 (しけんのためにいっしょうけんめいべんきょうしました。) — I studied very hard for the exam.
- 子供は一生懸命に走りました。 (こどもはいっしょうけんめいにはしりました。) — The child ran as hard as they could.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) and [生](../kanji/生.md) suggest a whole lifetime; 懸 evokes staking or committing, and [命](../kanji/命.md) means life. These are memory associations for the established expression, not instructions to translate it literally.

## Synonyms and antonyms

頑張る (がんばる), "try hard; persevere," is a related verb. 一生懸命 describes how an action is carried out; it is not itself a verb.

## Distinctive meaning

The emphasis is sincere, concentrated effort, not guaranteed success. It also behaves as a na-adjective: 一生懸命な姿 (いっしょうけんめいなすがた), "the sight of someone trying hard." Preserve the small っ and long しょう in the reading.
