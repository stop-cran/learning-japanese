# 別荘 (べっそう) - holiday home; villa

## Meaning

別荘 is a holiday or vacation home kept separately from one's usual residence.
It can be translated as "villa," but need not mean a palace or an especially luxurious building.

- 夏は山の別荘で過ごします。 (なつはやまのべっそうですごします。) - We spend the summer at our holiday home in the mountains.
- 湖の近くに別荘を借りました。 (みずうみのちかくにべっそうをかりました。) - We rented a holiday home near the lake.

## How the kanji combine

[別](../kanji/別.md) contributes being separate or another; 荘 has a house or
residence association here. The whole reading is べっそう: ベツ + ソウ becomes
べっそう, with a small っ. 荘 is read ソウ in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[住宅](住宅.md) (じゅうたく) is the broader housing/residence term.
[ホテル](ホテル.md) is a hotel, and [旅館](旅館.md) (りょかん) a Japanese-style inn.
Spending a holiday at one does not make that accommodation a 別荘.

## Distinctive meaning

The separate holiday-home role matters more than size or ownership: a rented
vacation house can also be a 別荘. Colloquially, 別荘 can jokingly mean prison
or jail; that is a separate contextual sense, not a claim that ordinary villas are prisons.
