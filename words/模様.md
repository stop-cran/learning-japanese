# 模様 (もよう) - pattern; appearance

## Meaning

模様 is a pattern or design, such as a design on fabric. It also describes the state or appearance of a situation. With a clause before 模様だ or 模様です, it can report how the situation appears to be.

- この布には花の模様があります。 (このぬのにははなのもようがあります。) - This cloth has a floral pattern.
- 電車は遅れている模様です。 (でんしゃはおくれているもようです。) - It appears that the train is delayed.

For the situation sense, 会場の模様を伝える (かいじょうのもようをつたえる) means "report what is happening at the venue." It concerns the scene, not necessarily a decorative design.

## How the kanji combine

模 contributes a model or pattern, and [様](../kanji/様.md) contributes form, appearance, or manner. Together they suit both a visible design and the appearance of a situation. The reading is モ + ヨウ; 様 is not read さま here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[柄](柄.md) (がら) overlaps for a design on clothing or fabric. [様子](様子.md) (ようす) overlaps for the state of a situation. Neither comparison covers all uses of 模様.

## Distinctive meaning

In the train example, 模様 marks an assessment of the current situation. It does not guarantee that the speaker has personally observed every relevant fact. Conversely, 花の模様 is a concrete design, not a conjecture. The words immediately before 模様 help identify which meaning is intended.
