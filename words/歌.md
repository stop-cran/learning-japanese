# 歌 (うた) - song

## Meaning

歌 means a song or singing. 歌を歌う (うたをうたう), "sing a song," is a normal expression despite repeating the sound うた.

- 子供たちが歌を歌っています。 (こどもたちがうたをうたっています。) - The children are singing a song.

好きな歌 (すきなうた) is a song you like; 歌が上手 (うたがじょうず) describes being good at singing.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [歌](../kanji/歌.md) writes the native noun うた. The related verb is 歌う (うたう), with a kana ending that changes when conjugated.

## Synonyms and antonyms

曲 (きょく) is a piece of music, including instrumental pieces. 音楽 (おんがく) is music more broadly. A piano piece without singing is not normally called a 歌 merely because it is music.

## Distinctive meaning

In literary contexts 歌 can also mean a Japanese poem, especially a tanka. For everyday beginner use, the song and singing senses are the practical starting point.
