# 騒々しい (そうぞうしい) - noisy; boisterous; unsettled

## Meaning

騒々しい describes noisy, clamorous, or boisterous surroundings.
It is an い-adjective: 騒々しい声 (そうぞうしいこえ),
"a loud, clamorous voice"; 騒々しくなる, "become noisy."

- 隣の部屋が騒々しくて、勉強できません。 (となりのへやがそうぞうしくて、べんきょうできません。) - The room next door is so noisy that I cannot study.
- 選挙の前は町が騒々しくなります。 (せんきょのまえはまちがそうぞうしくなります。) - The town becomes noisy and bustling before the election.

It also describes a restless or unsettled situation or atmosphere,
not necessarily just a high volume of sound.
騒々しい世の中 (そうぞうしいよのなか) can describe turbulent times.

## How the kanji combine

騒 contributes noise or commotion. The mark 々 repeats the preceding
kanji; 騒騒しい is also recognized. Keep the full reading そうぞうしい,
including the long vowels. The repeated part is voiced as ぞう.
The native-adjective classification does not make this an ordinary
two-noun compound with a separately translated しい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[騒がしい](騒がしい.md) (さわがしい) overlaps in noise and turbulence.
[うるさい](うるさい.md) often conveys annoyance and also has fussy uses.
[賑やか](賑やか.md) (にぎやか) can present liveliness positively or
neutrally, but the words are not separated by an absolute positive/negative rule.
[静か](静か.md) (しずか), quiet, contrasts with the sound sense.

## Distinctive meaning

Noisy and socially unsettled are related but different readings of a scene.
The adjective does not automatically describe every busy schedule or
every kind of unsettled weather. Forms include 騒々しかった,
"was noisy," and 騒々しくない, "not noisy."
