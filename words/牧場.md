# 牧場 (ぼくじょう) - livestock farm; pasture

## Meaning

牧場 is a livestock farm or ranch, and can also mean pasture or grazing land. The dictionary records both ぼくじょう and まきば for this word.

- 牧場で牛を飼っています。 (ぼくじょうでうしをかっています。) - They keep cattle on the farm.
- 馬が牧場で草を食べています。 (うまがまきばでくさをたべています。) - Horses are eating grass in the pasture.

The second example uses まきば. Recognize both readings rather than deciding the reading from the character 場 alone.

## How the kanji combine

牧 concerns herding or tending livestock; [場](../kanji/場.md) contributes a place. This is an activity-plus-place combination, a place for livestock. ぼくじょう uses ボク + ジョウ; まきば is the native-reading alternative.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[牧畜](牧畜.md) (ぼくちく) is livestock farming as an activity; [家畜](家畜.md) (かちく) are the animals. [農家](農家.md) (のうか) concerns farmers or farming households, and [農業](農業.md) (のうぎょう) is agriculture more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

牧場 is centered on livestock or grazing land, not every cultivated vegetable field. A farm can have buildings and equipment as well as open pasture; the word does not require an entirely open, building-free landscape.
