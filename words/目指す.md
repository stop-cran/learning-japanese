# 目指す (めざす) - aim for; head for

## Meaning

目指す is a transitive godan す verb: aim for an achievement, role, or destination.
The thing aimed at normally takes を. It can describe an ambition or physical movement toward a place.

- 医者を目指しています。 (いしゃをめざしています。) - I am aiming to become a doctor.
- 優勝を目指して練習しています。 (ゆうしょうをめざしてれんしゅうしています。) - I am practicing with the aim of winning the championship.
- 駅を目指して歩きました。 (えきをめざしてあるきました。) - I walked toward the station.

In the first example, 医者 is the role sought, not a doctor being watched.

## How the kanji combine

[目](../kanji/目.md), "eye," and [指](../kanji/指.md), with its pointing association,
help remember directing oneself toward a goal. This is a learning association, not a historical derivation.
The native reading is めざす; forms include 目指します (めざします),
目指して (めざして), and 目指さない (めざさない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[狙う](狙う.md) (ねらう) also means aim at or seek, and has additional targeting uses.
[向かう](向かう.md) (むかう) overlaps in heading toward a destination, often with に or へ.
[目標](目標.md) (もくひょう) is a goal or target; 目指す describes aiming toward it.

## Distinctive meaning

目指している states a goal or direction, not that the person has arrived or succeeded.
医者を目指す means aiming to become a doctor, not "keep an eye on a doctor."
The expression can present a long-term aspiration as well as a destination on today's route.
