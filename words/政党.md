# 政党 (せいとう) - political party

## Meaning

政党 is a political party: an organized group pursuing shared political aims.
It is a noun. 政党を支持する (せいとうをしじする) means support a political party;
政党に入る (せいとうにはいる) means join one.

- 新しい政党ができました。 (あたらしいせいとうができました。) - A new political party has been formed.
- どの政党を支持していますか。 (どのせいとうをしじしていますか。) - Which political party do you support?

Supporting a party does not by itself establish formal membership.

## How the kanji combine

[政](../kanji/政.md) contributes politics or government, and [党](../kanji/党.md)
contributes a party or faction. The first specifies the political kind of group.
Read セイ + トウ, with two long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[党](党.md) (とう) is a close political synonym and occurs in party names.
[政治](政治.md) (せいじ) concerns politics as an activity or field;
[政府](政府.md) (せいふ) is a government. A party is not identical to either.

## Distinctive meaning

A party member is 党員 (とういん), not simply a 政党.
The loanword [パーティー](パーティー.md) commonly names a social gathering,
although its dictionary entry also records a political-party sense.
Neither the English word party nor the source list's parenthetical member wording
justifies confusing the organization with a person belonging to it.
