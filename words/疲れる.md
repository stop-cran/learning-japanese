# 疲れる (つかれる) — get tired

## Meaning

疲れる describes becoming tired or weary. It is intransitive: the tired person is the subject, not an object made tired by this verb.
It is a ru-verb, so the polite form is 疲れます (つかれます) and the past is 疲れた (つかれた).

- 長い時間歩いて、疲れました。 (ながいじかんあるいて、つかれました。) — I walked for a long time and got tired.
- 今日は疲れているので、早く寝ます。 (きょうはつかれているので、はやくねます。) — I am tired today, so I will go to bed early.

疲れている describes the resulting tired state. 疲れた can also report how you now feel after exertion.

## How the kanji combine

[疲](../kanji/疲.md) represents つか-, with れる written in kana.
The ending belongs to the verb itself: 疲れる is not a passive form just because it ends in れる.
The kanji has an on reading ヒ, but that is not the reading of this native verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

疲労 (ひろう) is the noun "fatigue."
眠い (ねむい) means sleepy: wanting to sleep is distinct from being worn out.
[痛い](痛い.md) いたい means painful or sore, not tired, though pain and fatigue can occur together.

## Distinctive meaning

疲れる can describe physical or mental weariness without specifying a medical cause.
It does not by itself diagnose illness or say that the person is already asleep.
For a simple report about yourself, 疲れました is complete; you do not need to force an object before it.
