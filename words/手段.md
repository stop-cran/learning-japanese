# 手段 (しゅだん) - means; method

## Meaning

手段 is a noun for the means or method used to achieve something. The focus is on how a goal can be reached, not the goal itself. Use 手段を選ぶ for choosing a means and 手段がない for lacking one.

- 電話は便利な連絡手段です。 (でんわはべんりなれんらくしゅだんです。) - The telephone is a convenient means of communication.
- 別の手段で駅まで行きましょう。 (べつのしゅだんでえきまでいきましょう。) - Let's get to the station by another means.

で marks the means used in the second example. 交通手段 (こうつうしゅだん) means a mode or means of transportation.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md) can suggest a way of doing something, not just a physical hand. [段](../kanji/段.md) suggests a step or stage. Together they form the established noun for an approach or means. The on readings are シュ + ダン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[方法](方法.md) (ほうほう) broadly names a method or way of doing something and often overlaps. [目的](目的.md) (もくてき) names the aim. Means and ends are complementary concepts, not simple good/bad opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

手段を選ばない (しゅだんをえらばない) means not being particular about the means used, often with a critical implication of doing whatever it takes. 手段 itself is neutral: whether a method is appropriate depends on what it involves.
