# 表情 (ひょうじょう) - facial expression

## Meaning

表情 is a facial expression or look on someone's face. It is a noun: **Nの表情** names someone's expression, and 表情が変わる describes a change in it.

- 彼女はうれしそうな表情をしていました。 (かのじょはうれしそうなひょうじょうをしていました。) - She had a happy-looking expression.
- その話を聞いて、彼の表情が変わりました。 (そのはなしをきいて、かれのひょうじょうがかわりました。) - His expression changed when he heard that story.

**Adjective + 表情をする** describes having or making that expression. It is not the same as simply adding する to 表情.

## How the kanji combine

[表](../kanji/表.md) contributes showing outwardly, and [情](../kanji/情.md) contributes feeling or emotion. Together they connect outward appearance with feeling. The on readings are ヒョウ + ジョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[表現](表現.md) (ひょうげん) means expression in a broader sense, including words and art. 表情 is the useful noun for the face in these examples. 無表情 (むひょうじょう) describes an expressionless appearance.

## Distinctive meaning

An expression provides outward evidence, not guaranteed access to someone's feelings. うれしそう explicitly means "looking happy," while [演技](演技.md) can produce an expression as part of a performance. The word can extend to other expressive appearances, but facial expression is the core use taught here.
