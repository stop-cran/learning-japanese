# 厘 (りん) — one-hundredth; rin

## Meaning

厘 is a unit meaning one-hundredth. JMdict glosses it as one-hundredth, for example 0.1 percent (one-hundredth of a 割), 0.3 mm (one-hundredth of a sun) or 0.0375 grams (one-hundredth of a monme). As a historical term, it is also the rin, a monetary unit of 0.001 yen.

- 打率は三割二分五厘です。 (だりつはさんわりにぶごりんです。) — The batting average is .325.
- 昔は一円の千分の一を一厘と呼びました。 (むかしはいちえんのせんぶんのいちをいちりんとよびました。) — In the past, one-thousandth of a yen was called one rin.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji word. The character [厘](../kanji/厘.md) is read りん here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[分](分.md) (ぶ) is ten times larger: JMdict glosses it as one-tenth, including one percent as one-tenth of a 割 (entry 1502850), so one 分 equals ten 厘. [パーセント](パーセント.md) is a percent; as a fraction of a 割, one 厘 is 0.1 percent.

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen gives the monetary rin as one-thousandth of a yen and one-tenth of a 銭 (sen). The compound 九分九厘 くぶくりん means almost certainly or in all probability (JMdict entry 1243880).
