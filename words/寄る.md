# 寄る (よる) — draw near; stop by

## Meaning

寄る means to draw near or move closer. It also means stopping by a place, for example while on the way somewhere else.
It is **intransitive**: the subject approaches or visits; the destination can be marked with に.
It follows the **godan** conjugation pattern: its ending changes to give 寄ります (よります), 寄って (よって), and 寄らない (よらない).

- もっと近くに寄ってください。 (もっとちかくによってください。) — Please come closer.
- 帰りに店に寄ります。 (かえりにみせによります。) — I will stop by the shop on the way back.

## How the kanji combine

[寄](../kanji/寄.md) carries the approaching or stopping-by meaning, followed by the verb ending る.
This is a native verb, not the on reading キ plus る.
The subject is the one moving closer; the verb does not directly mean "move an object closer."

## Synonyms and antonyms

近づく (ちかづく), "approach," overlaps with the nearness sense.
[訪ねる](訪ねる.md) (たずねる), "visit," can describe visiting someone without the same suggestion of dropping in along the way.
寄せる (よせる), "bring near," is the useful transitive contrast:
椅子を机に寄せる (いすをつくえによせる), "move a chair closer to the desk."

## Distinctive meaning

Moving closer and briefly visiting are related but not identical situations.
The shop example means going into or calling at the shop, not merely reducing the distance to its building.
For moving an object, use a suitable transitive construction such as 寄せる rather than putting that object before 寄る with を.
