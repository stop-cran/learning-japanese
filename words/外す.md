# 外す (はずす) - remove; unfasten

## Meaning

外す means to remove, detach, or unfasten something. It is a transitive godan verb: 外します, 外した, 外さない. Use を for the thing removed.

- 眼鏡を外して、本を置きました。 (めがねをはずして、ほんをおきました。) - I took off my glasses and put down the book.
- シャツのボタンを一つ外しました。 (シャツのボタンをひとつはずしました。) - I undid one button on my shirt.

The button example means unfastening it in this context, not necessarily cutting it off the shirt.

## How the kanji combine

[外](../kanji/外.md) contributes the idea of being out of an attached or expected position. In this verb it is read はず before す. Do not substitute the noun reading そと into the verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

外れる (はずれる) is the intransitive partner: ボタンが外れた, "the button came undone." [脱ぐ](脱ぐ.md) (ぬぐ) is used for taking off clothing, while [取る](取る.md) (とる) has broader taking and removing uses.

## Distinctive meaning

外す also appears in 席を外す (せきをはずす), "step away from one's seat; be temporarily away," and 的を外す (まとをはずす), "miss the target." Neither requires removing a physical component. The object identifies the intended sense, so "remove" is a starting gloss rather than a translation that fits every construction.
