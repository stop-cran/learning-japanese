# 半島 (はんとう) - peninsula

## Meaning

半島 is a peninsula: land projecting into the sea while remaining connected
to a larger landmass. It is a noun.

- 地図で半島の形を調べました。 (ちずではんとうのかたちをしらべました。) - I checked the shape of the peninsula on a map.
- その町は半島の先にあります。 (そのまちははんとうのさきにあります。) - The town is at the tip of the peninsula.

半島の先 identifies the peninsula's end or tip; it does not name a separate island.

## How the kanji combine

[半](../kanji/半.md) contributes half or partial, and
[島](../kanji/島.md) island. Think of land that is island-like but not
completely detached. This is a meaning aid, not a geometrical claim that
exactly half its boundary must be water. The on readings are ハン + トウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[島](島.md) (しま) is an island, surrounded by water.
[大陸](大陸.md) (たいりく) is a continent or mainland.
岬 (みさき) is a cape or headland; the words are related geographical
descriptions, not freely interchangeable labels.

## Distinctive meaning

The connection to other land matters more than an imagined fixed shape.
A 半島 can contain several towns or mountains; it is not a beach or
[海岸](海岸.md) (かいがん), "coast," merely because it borders the sea.
This card teaches the geographical sense, not a context-dependent label
for a particular country or its people.
