# 夫人 (ふじん) - wife; Mrs. (respectful)

## Meaning

夫人 is a respectful noun or title for a wife or married woman. It can follow a person's name as a title, or an office-holder's title to refer to that person's wife. **大統領夫人** (だいとうりょうふじん) means "the president's wife."

- 田中夫人にお会いしました。 (たなかふじんにおあいしました。) - I met Mrs. Tanaka.
- 大統領は夫人と出席しました。 (だいとうりょうはふじんとしゅっせきしました。) - The president attended with his wife.

In the second example, と marks the accompanying person. The respectful register is part of this modern use.

## How the kanji combine

[夫](../kanji/夫.md) connects the spelling with marriage, and [人](../kanji/人.md) denotes a person. フ + ジン gives ふじん. Learn the compound as a whole: adding the standalone glosses "husband" and "person" would not produce its meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[妻](妻.md) (つま) is a neutral word useful for referring to one's own wife. [奥さん](奥さん.md) (おくさん) is another way to refer to someone's wife. [婦人](婦人.md), also ふじん, means a woman/lady and has different kanji.

## Distinctive meaning

夫人 is not the neutral default for introducing one's own wife in ordinary conversation. Nor does it simply mean "woman" in every setting. Distinguish the respectful wife/title use from the homophone 婦人 instead of relying on pronunciation alone.
