# 対 (たい) - versus; to in a ratio

## Meaning

対 (たい) connects opponents, as in "A versus B," or numbers in a score or ratio.

- 今日の試合は日本対韓国です。 (きょうのしあいはにほんたいかんこくです。) - Today's match is Japan versus South Korea.
- 三対一で勝ちました。 (さんたいいちでかちました。) - We won three to one.

For a ratio, 二対一 (にたいいち) means "two to one." The same connector does not itself tell you whether the numbers are a score, a vote, or a mixing ratio.

As a prefix, 対 can mean toward, with, or against a named party: 対米貿易 (たいべいぼうえき) means "trade with the United States," not automatically opposition to it.

## How the kanji combine

[対](../kanji/対.md) is one character with the on reading タイ here. Its facing or opposing association helps connect the versus and comparison uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[対する](対する.md) (たいする) supplies a related verb construction such as Nに対する, "toward/in relation to N." The connector A対B is not freely interchangeable with that construction. There is no single useful antonym for a ratio connector.

## Distinctive meaning

対 (つい), "pair/set," is a separate word: 一対 (いっつい) is one pair. The community source gave a pair/set gloss to the たい row, but that does not license reading the pairing word たい. This card teaches たい and marks つい as a contrast, not an additional sourced level assignment.
