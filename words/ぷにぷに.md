# ぷにぷに — soft and squishy

## Meaning

Soft, yielding, slightly springy flesh or material, especially the feeling when gently pressed.

- 猫の肉球（にくきゅう）はぷにぷにしている。 — The cat's paw pads are soft and squishy.
- このおもちゃは、押すとぷにぷにする。 — This toy feels squishy when you press it.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic repeating ぷに. The repeated pattern depicts texture; it does not require an audible squelch, and there are no kanji to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もちもち](もちもち.md) overlaps in springiness but especially describes elastic food texture. [ふかふか](ふかふか.md) suggests thick cushioning rather than fleshy squishiness. [ごつごつ](ごつごつ.md) gives a contrasting hard, knobbly impression.

## Distinctive meaning

ぷにぷにする and ぷにぷにした感触（かんしょく, feel）are useful patterns. The word often has a playful or affectionate tone, but describing someone's body this way without invitation can be intrusive. Do not assume that soft and squishy means wet, sticky, or suitable to eat.
