# ちゃん (ちゃん) — affectionate name suffix

## Meaning

ちゃん is an affectionate or familiar suffix attached to a name or nickname. It is common for children and can be used for close friends or pets. It is not limited to female people.

ゆきちゃん、一緒に遊ぼう。（ゆきちゃん、いっしょにあそぼう。） — Yuki, let's play together.

## How the kanji combine

This suffix is written in kana and attaches directly: ゆき + ちゃん. There is no の between the name and the suffix.

## Synonyms and antonyms

さん is the safer neutral-polite name suffix. くん is another familiar suffix, often used for boys or younger male colleagues, but its distribution is not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

ちゃん conveys closeness, not simply grammatical politeness. Using it for an unfamiliar adult or a superior can sound patronizing. Follow the relationship and the person's preferred form of address rather than choosing ちゃん solely from their gender.
