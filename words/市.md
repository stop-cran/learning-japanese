# 市 (し) — city

## Meaning

市 means a city as a municipality. It is also a suffix attached directly to a city's name, without の: 大阪市 (おおさかし), "Osaka City".

- 私は横浜市に住んでいます。  
  わたしはよこはましにすんでいます。  
  "I live in Yokohama City."
- 市の図書館を利用します。  
  しのとしょかんをりようします。  
  "I use the city library."

With the separate native reading **いち**, 市 means a market or fair where goods are sold.
It is a noun rather than the city-name suffix.

- 日曜日に市が立ちます。 (にちようびにいちがたちます。) - A market is held on Sundays.
- 朝市で野菜を買いました。 (あさいちでやさいをかいました。) - I bought vegetables at the morning market.

市が立つ (いちがたつ) is the expression for a market being held; it does not mean that a city stands up.

## How the kanji combine

[市](../kanji/市.md) uses the on reading し here. It is one character, not a multi-kanji compound. 市 read いち means a market; that is a different word and is not the city-name suffix.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[都市](都市.md) (とし) means an urban city more generally. [町](町.md) (まち), "town; neighborhood", can describe a lived-in area without identifying its exact administrative status. Neither is a universal replacement for 市 in an official name.

## Distinctive meaning

Adding 市 makes the municipal unit explicit. In an address, keep the place name and its official suffix together; 市 is not a freely interchangeable label for every settlement.
The market reading いち does not replace し in 大阪市. This article retains its city reading
and N4 study label while also teaching the separately sourced N3 market word.
