# じりじり — inching along; scorching; impatient

## Meaning

Slow but steady change, mounting impatience, or the intense impression of the sun scorching down.

- 列（れつ）がじりじり前に進（すす）んだ。 — The line inched forward.
- 日差（ひざ）しがじりじり照（て）りつける。 — The sun beats down fiercely.
- 返事を待ちながら、じりじりしていた。 — I grew impatient while waiting for a reply.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[のろのろ](のろのろ.md) overlaps in slow movement; [いらいら](いらいら.md) overlaps in irritation. [ずんずん](ずんずん.md) usually gives progress a more vigorous impression.

## Distinctive meaning

The word also has sizzling and ringing-sound uses. Do not force a single English gloss onto じりじり進む, じりじりする, and the scorching-sun scene.
