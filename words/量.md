# 量 (りょう) - quantity; amount

## Meaning

量 is a noun for quantity, amount, volume, or a portion. Use **X の量** to identify what is being measured. It also works as a suffix in compounds such as 仕事量 (しごとりょう), "workload."

- 砂糖の量を減らしました。 (さとうのりょうをへらしました。) - I reduced the amount of sugar.
- この店は料理の量が多いです。 (このみせはりょうりのりょうがおおいです。) - This restaurant serves large portions.

**量が多い** and **量が少ない** describe a large or small amount. **量を量る** (りょうをはかる) means "measure the amount"; the noun and verb have different readings despite sharing a character.

## How the kanji combine

量 is the entire word, read with the on reading リョウ. Its meaning concerns how much there is. The long vowel matters: the noun is りょう, not りょ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[数](数.md) (かず) emphasizes a count or number. [質](質.md) (しつ), "quality," provides a useful contrasting dimension, as in 質と量 (しつとりょう), "quality and quantity"; it is not the opposite amount.

## Distinctive meaning

量 does not specify a unit by itself. Grams, liters, servings, or another measure come from context. A large 量 is not necessarily good or bad: reducing sugar and increasing necessary supplies can both be desirable in their respective situations.
