# 端 (はし) - edge; end; tip

## Meaning

端 is the edge, end, or tip of something. Use Nの端 to identify the object whose boundary is meant: 道の端 (みちのはし), "the edge of the road."

- 机の端にコップを置かないでください。 (つくえのはしにコップをおかないでください。) - Please do not put the glass on the edge of the desk.
- 列の端に立っていました。 (れつのはしにたっていました。) - I was standing at the end of the line.

に marks the position in both examples. The shape of the object determines whether English "edge," "end," or "tip" fits best.

## How the kanji combine

端 represents the whole native noun はし here. It is a single-kanji word with no compound to split. The dictionary also records はじ, but this page teaches the assigned reading はし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[真ん中](真ん中.md) (まんなか), "middle," contrasts with a peripheral position. [先](先.md) (さき) can mean a tip or something ahead, but has other time and sequence meanings. It is not interchangeable with 端 throughout its uses.

## Distinctive meaning

端 describes a position or boundary, not automatically the conclusion of an event. Also distinguish the same kana spelling in [橋](橋.md), "bridge," and [箸](箸.md), "chopsticks." Context and kanji identify the word; sharing はし does not make them meanings of this entry.
