# 要領 (ようりょう) - gist; knack

## Meaning

要領 names the important points or gist of something. It also means
the knack or practical way of handling a task.

- 説明を聞いても、要領がつかめません。 (せつめいをきいても、ようりょうがつかめません。) - Even after hearing the explanation, I can't grasp the main point.
- 彼女は要領よく仕事を進めます。 (かのじょはようりょうよくしごとをすすめます。) - She handles the work efficiently.

Learn 要領を得ない (ようりょうをえない), "miss the point; be unclear,"
and 要領がいい / 悪い (ようりょうがいい / わるい), "have / lack
the knack of handling things."

## How the kanji combine

[要](../kanji/要.md) contributes essential or important.
[領](../kanji/領.md) is read リョウ in this established compound.
Learn the whole word for essentials or a grasp of how to proceed;
the territory meaning in 領土 does not give a useful literal
translation here. The on readings are ヨウ + リョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[要点](要点.md) (ようてん) and [要旨](要旨.md) (ようし) overlap
with main points and gist. [方法](方法.md) (ほうほう) is a method
more generally. [能率](能率.md) (のうりつ) concerns efficiency or
work rate, rather than naming the gist of an explanation.

## Distinctive meaning

The first example concerns understanding what matters; the second
concerns effective handling. 要領がいい can be praise for competence,
but context can also suggest someone is clever at arranging things
to their own advantage. It does not automatically mean dishonest.

Do not confuse this word with the homophone 容量 (ようりょう),
"capacity." Shared pronunciation does not give them the same meaning.
