# 針 (はり) - needle; clock hand

## Meaning

針 is a needle or pin. It also names a clock hand or similar pointer.
It is a noun; the object or context tells you which kind is meant.
時計の針 (とけいのはり) means "the hands of a clock."

- 針に糸を通しました。
  (はりにいとをとおしました。) - I threaded the needle.
- 時計の針が止まっています。
  (とけいのはりがとまっています。) - The hands of the clock have stopped.

In the first example, に marks what the thread passes through.
The second uses 針 for the pointers, not for a sewing implement inside the clock.

## How the kanji combine

針 is one kanji representing the native noun はり.
There is no multi-kanji relationship within the word.
The on reading シン appears in compounds such as 秒針 (びょうしん), "second hand."

## Synonyms and antonyms

ピン overlaps with some pin-like objects, but does not replace 針 in every expression.
[時計](時計.md) (とけい) names the whole clock or watch, while 針 identifies a part.
A thread and a needle are cooperating tools, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the accompanying noun: 縫い針 (ぬいばり), "sewing needle," makes the use explicit,
while 時計の針 selects the clock sense.
The ばり in 縫い針 is a voiced compound form of はり; it does not change the standalone reading.
