# はがす - peel off; strip off

## Meaning

はがす means to peel or strip off something attached to a surface.
It is transitive: the attached thing removed takes を, and its original surface can take から.

- 瓶からラベルをはがしました。 (びんからラベルをはがしました。) - I peeled the label off the bottle.
- 壁のポスターをはがしてください。 (かべのポスターをはがしてください。) - Please take the poster off the wall.

This is a godan verb ending in す: はがします, はがして, はがした and はがさない.

## How the kanji combine

JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana. 剥がす is a recognized spelling;
the source list's 剥す is marked irregular okurigana. Both are read はがす here.
The character contributes peeling or stripping, not a second spoken word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[外す](外す.md) (はずす) is broader removal or unfastening.
むく is useful for taking off an outer covering to expose what is inside,
as in みかんの皮をむく (みかんのかわをむく), "peel a mandarin."
The verbs can overlap for removing bark, but a label and a fruit do not establish an unrestricted substitution rule.
[貼る](貼る.md) (はる), "stick on; paste," gives a contextual reversal.

## Distinctive meaning

The focus is separation from an attached surface, not necessarily tearing the material.
Nor does English "rip off" here automatically give はがす the commercial meaning "overcharge."
Learn the Japanese object and action rather than importing every English idiom.
