# わくわく — thrilled with anticipation

## Meaning

わくわく describes excited anticipation: being thrilled or eagerly looking forward to what comes next.

- 明日の旅行を考えると、わくわくする。 — Thinking about tomorrow's trip makes me excited.
- 子供たちはわくわくしながら箱を開けた。 — The children opened the box in excited anticipation.

Common patterns are わくわくする, わくわくしている, and わくわくしながら. A noun can follow the した form: わくわくした気持ち, "a feeling of excitement."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[どきどき](どきどき.md) focuses on the racing-heart experience and can result from fear. [はらはら](はらはら.md) emphasizes worry or suspense about the outcome. わくわく usually frames anticipation more positively.

## Distinctive meaning

Excitement can contain nervous energy, so わくわく does not guarantee perfect calm. Nevertheless, it is not a general equivalent of distressing anxiety. Something can be わくわくする話, "an exciting story"; this construction does not license a universal わくわくな before any noun.
