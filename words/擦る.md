# 擦る (こする) - rub; scrub; scrape

## Meaning

擦る, read こする here, means to rub, scrub, or scrape a surface.
The word is usually written こする.
It is a transitive godan verb: こすります, こすった, こすって,
こすらない. The affected thing takes を.

- 眠くて目をこすりました。
  (ねむくてめをこすりました。) - I rubbed my eyes because I was sleepy.
- 布で汚れた所をこすってください。
  (ぬのでよごれたところをこすってください。) - Please rub the dirty spot with a cloth.

で marks the implement in the second example. Rubbing something
does not guarantee that it becomes clean or shiny.

## How the kanji combine

擦 concerns rubbing. In this native verb it writes こす, followed
by る; this is not a Sino-Japanese compound.
The same character occurs in several separate words with different
readings. Their meanings must not all be attached to こする.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ごしごし](ごしごし.md) describes vigorous rubbing or scrubbing.
[磨く](磨く.md) (みがく) can describe scrubbing or polishing,
often with attention to the finished surface.
[削る](削る.md) (けずる) can describe scraping material away.
These overlap without making every rubbing action a removal or polishing task.

## Distinctive meaning

Another word written 擦る is **する**, a godan verb used for rubbing,
chafing, filing or frosting a surface, and striking a match.
For example, マッチを擦った (マッチをすった) means "struck a match."
That example illustrates する, not the primary reading こする.
Its forms include 擦ります (すります) and 擦らない (すらない).

The source list mixes those する glosses into a こする row.
Learn the readings separately; this is not permission to substitute
every sense of one word into the other. Slang and gaming uses of
こする are outside this article's practical rubbing selection.
