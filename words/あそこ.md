# あそこ (あそこ) — over there

## Meaning

あそこ means "over there": a place away from both the speaker and the listener. It stands for a location, so attach the particle required by the sentence.

- あそこに駅があります。  
  あそこにえきがあります。  
  "There is a station over there."
- あそこで待ちましょう。  
  あそこでまちましょう。  
  "Let's wait over there."

The first example uses に for where something exists; the second uses で for where an action happens.

## How the kanji combine

Use the normal hiragana spelling あそこ. The dictionary also records 彼処 and 彼所, but marks these kanji spellings as rare.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ここ is "here", near the speaker; そこ is "there", near the listener or a place already mentioned. [どこ](どこ.md) asks "where?" These words contrast locations rather than opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

あそこ replaces a place expression. Before a noun, use あの: あの店 (あのみせ), "that shop over there", rather than putting あそこ directly before 店.
