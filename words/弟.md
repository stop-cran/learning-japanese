# 弟 (おとうと) — younger brother

## Meaning

弟 is a younger brother: younger relative to the sibling, not necessarily a young child. When talking to people outside the family, 弟 is the usual plain term for one's own younger brother.

- 弟は学生です。  
  おとうとはがくせいです。  
  "My younger brother is a student."
- 弟さんは何歳ですか。  
  おとうとさんはなんさいですか。  
  "How old is your younger brother?"

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun. The whole character [弟](../kanji/弟.md) is read おとうと, with no okurigana. Do not split that reading between imaginary components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[兄](兄.md) あに is an older brother; [妹](妹.md) いもうと is a younger sister. These are contrasting family relationships, not freely interchangeable synonyms or strict opposites. 兄弟 きょうだい can refer to brothers or to siblings more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

弟さん おとうとさん is a polite way to refer to someone else's younger brother. Within a family, direct address is often by name rather than by the bare word 弟. This article teaches おとうと, not the other readings used in compounds such as 弟子 でし (apprentice).
