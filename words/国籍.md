# 国籍 (こくせき) - nationality; citizenship

## Meaning

国籍 is nationality or citizenship: membership of a state.
It is a noun. 国籍を取得する (こくせきをしゅとくする) means to
acquire nationality; 国籍を持つ (こくせきをもつ) means to hold it.

- 申込書には国籍を書く欄があります。 (もうしこみしょにはこくせきをかくらんがあります。) - The application form has a field for nationality.
- 彼女は日本の国籍を取得しました。 (かのじょはにほんのこくせきをしゅとくしました。) - She acquired Japanese nationality.

An additional use concerns a ship's or aircraft's national registration:
船の国籍 (ふねのこくせき), "a ship's nationality."
This does not describe the citizenship of the people aboard it.

## How the kanji combine

[国](../kanji/国.md) contributes country, and
[籍](../kanji/籍.md) contributes registration or membership.
国 specifies the national affiliation. The on readings are コク + セキ.
This association does not explain every country's nationality law.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[国民](国民.md) (こくみん) names a country's people or an individual
national; 国籍 names the status rather than the person.
[出身](出身.md) (しゅっしん) identifies origin or background, and
[市民](市民.md) (しみん) can concern city residence or civic membership.
These do not by themselves settle nationality.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not equate nationality with ethnicity, birthplace, current residence,
or the language someone speaks. The card's examples illustrate words
and constructions, not eligibility advice or a rule about how many
nationalities someone may hold.
The ship/aircraft sense is additional teaching, not a separate N2 assignment.
