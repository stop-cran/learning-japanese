# 鳩 (はと) — pigeon; dove

## Meaning

鳩 means a **pigeon or dove**. It names birds of the pigeon/dove family, not just one species or colour. The kana spelling ハト is also used. The noun itself does not mark singular or plural.

- 公園で鳩を見ました。 (こうえんではとをみました。) — I saw a pigeon in the park.
- あの鳥は鳩です。 (あのとりははとです。) — That bird is a pigeon.

These sentences identify the kind of bird without specifying its species.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun: [鳩](../kanji/鳩.md) is read **はと** as a whole, with no okurigana. Its other character readings are not substitutes for はと in this noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鳥](鳥.md) (とり), "bird," and [動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," are broader categories. [鶴](鶴.md) (つる), "crane," and [鷹](鷹.md) (たか), "hawk; falcon," name different birds, not synonyms. There is no useful antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English often uses "pigeon" and "dove" for different familiar birds, but both can translate はと. Do not treat colour alone as a rule for choosing a different Japanese word.

In 伝書鳩 (でんしょばと), "carrier pigeon; homing pigeon," the bird name changes from はと to ばと. Learn the compound's complete reading, not でんしょはと.
