# 肉 (にく) - meat; flesh

## Meaning

肉 means meat as food, and more broadly flesh.
In everyday meal choices, it commonly contrasts with fish or vegetables.

- この料理には肉が入っています。 (このりょうりにはにくがはいっています。) - This dish contains meat.
- 肉を小さく切ります。 (にくをちいさくきります。) - I cut the meat into small pieces.

The first example identifies an ingredient with が; the second makes meat
the object of 切る (きる), "cut," with を.

## How the kanji combine

[肉](../kanji/肉.md) is a single-character word using the on-reading ニク.
It also appears as the final part of compounds naming specific meats.
There is no second character to divide off.

## Synonyms and antonyms

牛肉 (ぎゅうにく) is beef, 豚肉 (ぶたにく) pork, and 鶏肉 (とりにく) chicken meat.
魚 (さかな), "fish," is a food-category contrast, not a strict antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

肉 alone does not specify the animal. Ask or use the full compound if that
matters. Its broader "flesh" sense also appears outside food contexts, so
not every occurrence means meat being eaten.
