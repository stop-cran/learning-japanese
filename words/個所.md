# 個所 (かしょ) - place; point; counted location

## Meaning

個所, also written 箇所, names a place, point, part, or passage being identified. It also works as a counter for such locations or portions.

- 間違っている個所に印をつけてください。 (まちがっているかしょにしるしをつけてください。) - Please mark the places that are incorrect.
- この文章には、直す個所が三か所あります。 (このぶんしょうには、なおすかしょがさんかしょあります。) - There are three places to correct in this passage.

After numbers, the counter is also written カ所, ヶ所, か所, ヵ所, or ケ所. 三ヶ所 is さんかしょ, not さんけしょ. The cited dictionary distinguishes these counter spellings from its noun spellings 個所 and 箇所.

## How the kanji combine

[個](../kanji/個.md) singles out an individual unit and uses its on reading カ here; [所](../kanji/所.md), ショ, contributes a place. Learn the whole カ + ショ compound rather than applying the familiar counter reading コ to 個. 箇 is an alternative first character; it has no separate card here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[場所](場所.md) (ばしょ) and [所](所.md) (ところ) overlap in place meanings. [部分](部分.md) (ぶぶん) names a part or section. 個所 is especially useful for picking out or counting particular spots, including spots in a text.

## Distinctive meaning

An English "passage" here can be a portion of text, not necessarily people passing through or a bill being passed. The word is not the generic counter for every object; 三個 (さんこ), "three items," and 三か所 (さんかしょ), "three places," count different things.
