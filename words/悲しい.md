# 悲しい (かなしい) — sad; sorrowful

## Meaning

悲しい describes feeling sad, unhappy, or sorrowful. This article focuses on the person's feeling
specified in the selected dictionary sense.

- 友達が引っ越してしまって、悲しいです。 (ともだちがひっこしてしまって、かなしいです。) — I am sad because my friend has moved away.
- 昨日はとても悲しかったです。 (きのうはとてもかなしかったです。) — I was very sad yesterday.

The first example gives a reason; the second describes the past feeling.

## How the kanji combine

[悲](../kanji/悲.md) carries the sadness meaning and is read かな in this word.
しい completes the native adjective. There is no second kanji whose meaning needs to be added.

## Synonyms and antonyms

寂しい (さびしい) can emphasize loneliness, so it may fit the first example when missing companionship
is the focus. Its meanings overlap with sadness; do not treat the two as sealed-off emotional categories.
嬉しい (うれしい), "glad; happy," offers a useful contrast.
[残念](残念.md) (ざんねん) instead highlights disappointment or a regrettable outcome.

## Distinctive meaning

悲しい is an い-adjective: 悲しくない (かなしくない) is "not sad,"
and 悲しかった (かなしかった) is "was sad." The examples express an emotional state;
they do not diagnose an illness or specify how long the feeling will last.
