# 性別 (せいべつ) - sex; gender classification

## Meaning

性別 concerns classification or distinction by sex or gender.
It is a noun; 性別を問わず (せいべつをとわず) means regardless of sex or gender.

- 性別を問わず応募できます。 (せいべつをとわずおうぼできます。) - You can apply regardless of sex or gender.
- このアンケートでは性別の回答は任意です。 (このアンケートではせいべつのかいとうはにんいです。) - Answering the gender question is optional in this survey.

性別による差 (せいべつによるさ), differences by sex or gender, specifies the basis
of the comparison. The word alone does not tell us what caused such differences.

## How the kanji combine

[性](../kanji/性.md) contributes sex or gender here, and [別](../kanji/別.md)
contributes distinction or classification. The second part focuses the broad 性
on a basis for distinguishing categories. Read セイ + ベツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[性](性.md) (せい) also has sex and gender senses, alongside other meanings.
[男性](男性.md) (だんせい) and [女性](女性.md) (じょせい) name male and female
people or categories; they do not mean the classification itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Translate 性別 in its particular context rather than imposing one universal
biological, social or legal definition. The noun itself does not specify which
response options a form offers, whether answering is required, or how the
organization defines the categories.
