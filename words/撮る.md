# 撮る (とる) - photograph, film

## Meaning

撮る is a transitive godan verb for taking photographs or shooting a film or video. The image or subject being captured takes を.

- 公園で写真を撮りました。 (こうえんでしゃしんをとりました。) - I took a photograph in the park.
- 子どもが踊るところを動画で撮りました。 (こどもがおどるところをどうがでとりました。) - I filmed the child dancing.

Forms include 撮ります, 撮った, and 撮らない (とります、とった、とらない).

## How the kanji combine

撮 writes と, with る as okurigana. It identifies the photographic use of the spoken verb とる; the familiar spelling 取る is not the spelling selected for this sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

撮影する (さつえいする) is another expression for photographing or filming. 録る (とる) is especially associated with recording audio or video.

## Distinctive meaning

For a still photograph, use 写真を撮る. For recording a television program or sound, 録る or 録画する・録音する may be the appropriate wording. A shared pronunciation does not make all spellings of とる equally suitable for every recording task.
