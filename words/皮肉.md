# 皮肉 (ひにく) - irony; sarcasm; unexpected twist

## Meaning

皮肉 can name sarcasm or an ironic remark, often criticizing indirectly.
It can also describe an ironic, unexpected outcome that runs contrary
to someone's intention or expectation.

- 彼の言葉には皮肉がこもっていました。 (かれのことばにはひにくがこもっていました。) - His words were full of sarcasm.
- 節約のために買ったのに、すぐ壊れるとは皮肉なものです。 (せつやくのためにかったのに、すぐこわれるとはひにくなものです。) - It is ironic that something bought to save money broke so soon.

皮肉な modifies a noun; 皮肉を言う means to make a sarcastic remark.
The second example is an ironic outcome, not necessarily anyone speaking sarcastically.

## How the kanji combine

[皮](../kanji/皮.md) contributes skin and
[肉](../kanji/肉.md) flesh.
Those literal components do not by themselves explain modern irony.
Learn the compound's whole meaning rather than inventing a historical
story from the character glosses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[悪口](悪口.md) (わるぐち) is insulting or disparaging talk;
it need not employ irony. [意外](意外.md) (いがい) overlaps in an
unexpected result, but not every surprise is ironic.
率直な言い方 (そっちょくないいかた), a frank way of speaking, can
contrast with an indirectly cutting remark.

## Distinctive meaning

The family also records surface/superficial and archaic body senses.
These are additional recognition senses, not an instruction to replace
ordinary [表面](表面.md) (ひょうめん) with 皮肉 in modern descriptions.
Sarcasm, an unexpected turn, and those older senses are different uses:
do not force one English translation into all of them.
