# 順 (じゅん) - order; turn

## Meaning

順 is a noun or suffix meaning order, sequence, or turn.
順に means "in order" or "one after another."
After a sorting criterion, 順 specifies the order: 名前順 (なまえじゅん), "name order," and 年齢順 (ねんれいじゅん), "age order."

- 名前の順に並んでください。 (なまえのじゅんにならんでください。) - Please line up in name order.
- 順に一人ずつ話しましょう。 (じゅんにひとりずつはなしましょう。) - Let's speak one at a time, in turn.

The second example combines 順に with 一人ずつ, "one person at a time," making the turn-taking explicit.

## How the kanji combine

順 represents the whole order-related noun here.
The on-reading is ジュン, one mora sequence じゅ followed by ん, not じ・ゆ・ん.
There are no separate written components to translate as a compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[順番](順番.md) (じゅんばん) is another useful word for order or someone's turn.
順序 (じゅんじょ) emphasizes sequence.
[逆](逆.md) (ぎゃく), "reverse," gives 逆の順 (ぎゃくのじゅん), "reverse order," rather than an absence of order.

## Distinctive meaning

An ordering instruction may still need a starting direction: age order alone does not explicitly say youngest first or oldest first.
順 tells you that a sequence or criterion matters; additional wording supplies its details.
Do not confuse 順に with doing everything simultaneously.
