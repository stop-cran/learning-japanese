# 当てる (あてる) - hit; put against; guess correctly

## Meaning

当てる is a transitive ichidan verb: 当てます, 当てない, 当てた. It can mean making something hit a target, placing something against a surface, or correctly guessing an answer. With physical contact, AをBに当てる identifies what makes contact and what it touches.

- ボールを的に当てた。 (ボールをまとにあてた。) - I hit the target with the ball.
- 額に冷たいタオルを当てた。 (ひたいにつめたいタオルをあてた。) - I held a cold towel against my forehead.

The guessing use takes the answer or item guessed with を: 年齢を当てる (ねんれいをあてる), "guess someone's age correctly."

## How the kanji combine

[当](../kanji/当.md) supplies the idea of hitting or matching the target. The native verb reading is あてる; てる is okurigana, not another word. This is one written kanji, not a compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[当たる](当たる.md) describes something hitting or being right: ボールが的に当たる, "the ball hits the target." 当てる presents someone bringing about that contact. [貼る](貼る.md) means sticking or pasting something on; 当てる can simply mean holding it against a surface.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the construction, not just English "hit." 当てる does not by itself mean striking violently. In a guessing game, 答えを当てた (こたえをあてた) means "got the answer right," rather than merely offering a guess.
