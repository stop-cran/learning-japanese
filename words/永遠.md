# 永遠 (えいえん) - eternity; forever

## Meaning

永遠 names eternity or permanence without an end. Learn 永遠の + noun for
"eternal..." and 永遠に + predicate for "forever."

- 二人は永遠の愛を誓いました。 (ふたりはえいえんのあいをちかいました。) - The two pledged eternal love.
- この思い出は永遠に忘れません。 (このおもいではえいえんにわすれません。) - I will never forget this memory.

The second sentence combines 永遠に with a negative verb. The meaning is
"not forget, ever," rather than "forget forever."

## How the kanji combine

永 contributes lasting or enduring, and [遠](../kanji/遠.md) contributes far or
distant. Their meanings reinforce an image of time extending without an end.
The on readings are エイ + エン; keep both parts of えいえん audible.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[永久](永久.md) (えいきゅう) also concerns permanence, with substantial overlap.
[一時](一時.md) (いちじ), in its "temporary" use, provides a contrast, but learn
its own constructions rather than replacing 永遠 word for word.

## Distinctive meaning

永遠 can express an ideal, a promise, or deliberate exaggeration, not just a
scientific statement about infinite time. It does not by itself mean that a
living person is immortal. In 永遠の愛, what is presented as lasting forever
is the love, not necessarily the lovers' physical lives.
