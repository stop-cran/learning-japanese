# 不幸 (ふこう) - misfortune; unhappiness

## Meaning

不幸 means misfortune, sorrow, or unhappiness. It is a noun and na-adjective: 不幸な出来事 (ふこうなできごと), "an unfortunate event," and 不幸だ, "be unfortunate; be unhappy." It also has a particular bereavement use.

- 彼女は不幸な出来事を乗り越えました。 (かのじょはふこうなできごとをのりこえました。) - She overcame an unfortunate experience.
- 身内に不幸があったので、欠席します。 (みうちにふこうがあったので、けっせきします。) - I will be absent because of a death in the family.

In the second example, 身内に不幸がある is understood as bereavement, not simply having a relative who feels unhappy.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) negates, and [幸](../kanji/幸.md) contributes happiness or good fortune. The compound names a lack of good fortune or a misfortune. The on readings are フ + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[幸福](幸福.md) (こうふく) and [幸せ](幸せ.md) (しあわせ) provide positive contrasts. [悲しい](悲しい.md) (かなしい) describes feeling sad; 不幸 can describe an adverse circumstance as well as unhappiness.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary's death sense is especially associated with the death of a relative or someone close. Do not use 不幸 as a general substitute for every mention of death. Conversely, 不幸な出来事 need not mean a death: the wording and situation determine whether the broader misfortune sense or the bereavement sense fits.
