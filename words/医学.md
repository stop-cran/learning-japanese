# 医学 (いがく) — medical science

## Meaning

医学 is medicine as an academic or scientific field: knowledge about the human body, disease, and treatment. It is a noun, not the medicine that you swallow.

大学で医学を勉強しています。 (だいがくでいがくをべんきょうしています。) — I am studying medicine at university.

## How the kanji combine

[医](../kanji/医.md), "medicine; doctor," and [学](../kanji/学.md), "study," form an on-reading compound: い + がく. Together they identify a field of study.

## Synonyms and antonyms

医療 (いりょう) is medical care or treatment in practice. 薬 (くすり) is a drug or remedy. 医者 (いしゃ) is a doctor, the person rather than the discipline.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 医学の before a noun, as in 医学の本 (いがくのほん), "a book on medicine." 医学を学ぶ (いがくをまなぶ) means studying medicine; it does not by itself say that the student is already a qualified doctor. There is no ordinary direct antonym.
