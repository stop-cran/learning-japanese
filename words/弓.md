# 弓 (ゆみ) — bow (for arrows or a violin); archery

## Meaning

弓 is a bow for shooting arrows. It can also mean archery as a skill, and the bow used to play a violin or a similar instrument.

- 彼は弓を引いて、矢を的に向けた。 (かれはゆみをひいて、やをまとにむけた。) — He drew the bow and aimed the arrow at the target.
- バイオリンの弓を新しいものに替えた。 (ばいおりんのゆみをあたらしいものにかえた。) — I replaced my violin bow with a new one.

## How the kanji combine

[弓](../kanji/弓.md) writes the whole native word ゆみ. In 弓道 (きゅうどう), Japanese archery, the same character is read きゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

矢 (や) is the arrow that a 弓 shoots; 弓矢 (ゆみや) names bow and arrows together.

## Distinctive meaning

In the second example, 弓 is the bow used to play a violin. For traditional Japanese archery, use 弓道 (きゅうどう). The quiz keeps 弓 apart from お辞儀 and 礼, which can also be glossed “bow” but mean bowing the head.
