# 練習 (れんしゅう) — practice

## Meaning

練習 means practice, training, or an exercise done to develop a skill. Add する to form "practice." The skill or activity can take を.

毎日、ピアノを練習します。 (まいにち、ピアノをれんしゅうします。) — I practice piano every day.

日本語で話す練習をしています。 (にほんごではなすれんしゅうをしています。) — I am practicing speaking Japanese.

## How the kanji combine

練 suggests refining or training, and [習](../kanji/習.md) suggests learning through repeated activity. Their on readings form レン + シュウ. Keep the long う in れんしゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

勉強 (べんきょう) is study or learning more broadly. 復習 (ふくしゅう) is reviewing material already learned. Neither always involves repeatedly performing a skill.

## Distinctive meaning

For practicing an action expressed by a verb, put its plain form before 練習: 話す練習, "speaking practice." Do not attach を directly to an unmodified verb. 練習 can concern music, sport, pronunciation, driving, or other skills, not only classroom exercises.
