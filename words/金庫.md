# 金庫 (きんこ) - safe; vault

## Meaning

金庫 is a safe, strongbox, or vault used to keep money and valuables secure. It is a noun: **金庫に入れる** means to put something in a safe, and 金庫から出す means to take it out.

- 大切な書類を金庫に入れました。 (たいせつなしょるいをきんこにいれました。) - I put the important documents in the safe.
- 金庫の鍵が見つかりません。 (きんこのかぎがみつかりません。) - I cannot find the key to the safe.

The name does not restrict its contents to cash. Documents and other valuables fit the same storage use.

## How the kanji combine

[金](../kanji/金.md) contributes money or valuables, and 庫 means a storehouse or storage place. 金 specifies what the secure store is associated with. The on readings are キン + コ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[財布](財布.md) (さいふ) is a wallet or purse for carrying money. A 金庫 is a secure storage container or room, not something identified by portability. [箱](箱.md) (はこ), "box," does not itself imply the protective function of a safe.

## Distinctive meaning

金庫 can also name a treasury, depository, or funding institution. In 信用金庫 (しんようきんこ), "credit association," it is part of an institution's name, not simply a box. Context distinguishes a physical storage place from a financial organization.
