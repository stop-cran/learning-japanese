# 様々 (さまざま) - various; diverse

## Meaning

様々 describes variety or diversity. It behaves as a na-adjective: use 様々な before a noun, or 様々だ and 様々です as predicates.

- 会議で様々な意見が出ました。 (かいぎでさまざまないけんがでました。) - Various opinions came up at the meeting.
- 参加した理由は人によって様々です。 (さんかしたりゆうはひとによってさまざまです。) - The reasons for participating vary from person to person.

The first example introduces different kinds of opinion; the second says that people's reasons are not all alike.

## How the kanji combine

[様](../kanji/様.md) contributes form, manner, or state. The mark 々 repeats the preceding kanji; it is not another kanji with an independent meaning. In さまざま, the second さま is voiced to ざま. The spelling 様様 also exists.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いろいろ](いろいろ.md) also means "various" and can describe an assortment. [同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ), "the same," offers a contrast when the issue is uniformity. It is not an opposite in every sentence: several varied objects can still share some properties.

## Distinctive meaning

様々 highlights differences, not just a large count. A hundred identical cups are many cups, but their number alone does not make them 様々なカップ. Also keep the grammar distinct from the i-adjective 多い (おおい), "numerous": the direct noun connection here is 様々な, not 様々い.
