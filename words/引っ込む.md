# 引っ込む (ひっこむ) - draw back; sink inward; withdraw

## Meaning

引っ込む means drawing back or inward, sinking in, or becoming recessed.
It also describes withdrawing from public view or retiring indoors.
It is an intransitive godan verb: 引っ込みます, 引っ込んだ, 引っ込んで.

- カメの頭が甲羅の中に引っ込みました。 (カメのあたまがこうらのなかにひっこみました。) - The turtle's head withdrew into its shell.
- その家は道路から少し引っ込んだ所にあります。 (そのいえはどうろからすこしひっこんだところにあります。) - The house stands a little back from the road.
- 父は自分の部屋に引っ込みました。 (ちちはじぶんのへやにひっこみました。) - My father withdrew to his room.

The house example describes its position, not a house physically retreating.

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) contributes pulling back;
[込](../kanji/込.md)む contributes inward direction here.
Learn the native compound ひっこむ, with a small っ.
The independent crowding sense of 込む is not the meaning of this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下がる](下がる.md) (さがる) can mean stepping back.
[沈む](沈む.md) (しずむ) overlaps with sinking, but does not require
the inward/recessed perspective. [引退](引退.md) concerns withdrawing
from an active career; entering a room is not automatically retirement.

## Distinctive meaning

引っ込める (ひっこめる) is the related transitive verb: withdraw or pull
something back. Do not give 引っ込む the same object pattern.
An order such as 引っ込んでいろ, "stay out of it," can be blunt and hostile;
recognizing that use is not a recommendation for a polite request.
