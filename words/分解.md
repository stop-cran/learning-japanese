# 分解 (ぶんかい) - disassembly; decomposition; analysis

## Meaning

分解 means taking something apart into components, or analyzing it
into its elements. It also names chemical decomposition.
With する, distinguish an action on something from something decomposing.

- 修理のために機械を分解しました。 (しゅうりのためにきかいをぶんかいしました。) - I took the machine apart for repair.
- この物質は熱で分解します。 (このぶっしつはねつでぶんかいします。) - This substance decomposes under heat.

In the first sentence, を marks what is taken apart.
In the second, the substance is the subject and で marks the cause.
The dictionary records both transitive and intransitive uses for the
disassembly/analysis and chemical senses.

In mathematics, 分解 also means factorization, as in
因数分解 (いんすうぶんかい). That is not physically dismantling an object.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) contributes dividing, and [解](../kanji/解.md)
undoing or solving. Together they suggest separating a whole into its parts.
The on readings are ブン + カイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[分析](分析.md) (ぶんせき) is analysis and need not involve physical disassembly.
[組み立てる](組み立てる.md) (くみたてる), assemble, contrasts with taking
a machine apart. [腐る](腐る.md) (くさる) describes rotting or spoiling,
not every kind of chemical decomposition.

## Distinctive meaning

Disassembly does not necessarily destroy the parts: a machine may be
reassembled after repair.
Nor should every substance disappearing into water be called chemical
decomposition. [溶ける](溶ける.md) (とける), melt or dissolve,
describes different processes.
The object and field determine what "breaking down" means here.
