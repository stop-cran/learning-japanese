# 円 (えん) - yen; circle

## Meaning

円 (えん) names the Japanese currency unit, the yen, and also a geometric circle. After a number it expresses an amount in yen, not money in any currency.

- この本は千円です。 (このほんはせんえんです。) - This book costs one thousand yen.
- コンパスで円を描きます。 (コンパスでえんをかきます。) - I draw a circle with a compass.

There is also a separate native noun 円 (まる), also written 丸, meaning "circle." For example, 紙にまるを描いてください (かみにまるをかいてください) means "Please draw a circle on the paper." Its dictionary entry additionally records "money" as **slang**. That slang sense is not the currency reading えん and is not a model for reading prices.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [円](../kanji/円.md) supplies the whole word. The primary reading here, エン, is an on reading; まる belongs to the separate native word. The shared character does not make the readings freely interchangeable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お金](お金.md) (おかね) is general money. [丸い](丸い.md) (まるい) is the adjective "round," rather than a currency unit or the noun naming a circle.

## Distinctive meaning

For a price such as 百円, read ひゃくえん, not ひゃくまる. For shapes, both えん and まる occur, with えん useful in geometric descriptions. The source list's two readings are retained here without treating its broad English "money" as a single unrestricted meaning.
