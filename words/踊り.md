# 踊り (おどり) - dance; dancing

## Meaning

踊り names a dance or the activity of dancing. It can describe what someone performs, watches, or learns.

祭りで踊りを見ました。 (まつりでおどりをみました。) - I watched a dance at the festival.

この踊りを習いたいです。 (このおどりをならいたいです。) - I want to learn this dance.

## How the kanji combine

踊り is a noun related to 踊る (おどる), "dance." 踊 is written with the reading おど, and り completes the noun. The verb's ます-stem (the form before ます) is also 踊り, but here the form functions as a noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ダンス is another common word for dance, especially in expressions naming modern or Western dance styles. 舞 (まい) names dance too, often in traditional performance contexts. These are not strict mutually exclusive categories.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 踊る for the action of dancing; 踊り lets you discuss the dance itself. 盆踊り (ぼんおどり) is dancing associated with the Bon festival. There is no general antonym of the noun "dance."
