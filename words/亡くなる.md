# 亡くなる (なくなる) — pass away

## Meaning

亡くなる means to die, expressed less directly as "pass away." It is an intransitive godan verb: 亡くなります, 亡くなって, 亡くなった.

- 祖父は去年亡くなりました。 (そふはきょねんなくなりました。) — My grandfather passed away last year.
- その作家が亡くなったと聞きました。 (そのさっかがなくなったとききました。) — I heard that the writer had passed away.

## How the kanji combine

[亡](../kanji/亡.md) marks the death-related meaning; くなる is written in kana. The ending reflects なる, "become," but learn なくなる as the complete verb. Its stem for ます is なくなり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

死ぬ (しぬ) directly means "die." 亡くなる is a euphemistic alternative, not a different biological event. 生まれる (うまれる), "be born," is a life-event contrast rather than a grammatical reverse of the verb.

## Distinctive meaning

The usual respectful context concerns a person's death. Do not confuse this spelling with 無くなる (なくなる), "disappear; run out; cease to exist." お金がなくなる (おかねがなくなる) means money runs out, not that it passes away. The person who dies takes が, or は when made the topic.
