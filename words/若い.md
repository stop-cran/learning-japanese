# 若い (わかい) — young; youthful

## Meaning

若い describes someone as young or youthful. It is an i-adjective and attaches directly to
a noun: 若い先生 (わかいせんせい), "a young teacher."
It does not mean that the person must be a child.

- 私たちの先生はまだ若いです。
  (わたしたちのせんせいはまだわかいです。)
  — Our teacher is still young.
- 母は若いころ、よく泳ぎました。
  (はははわかいころ、よくおよぎました。)
  — My mother often swam when she was young.

## How the kanji combine

[若](../kanji/若.md) supplies the youth meaning and represents わか; the final い is written
in kana. This is a native adjective. Its past form is 若かった (わかかった), and its
negative is 若くない (わかくない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

幼い (おさない) can mean very young or childish; it is not a neutral replacement for every
description of a young adult. 老いる (おいる), "grow old," offers an aging contrast,
but is a verb rather than another adjective.
若者 (わかもの) is the noun "young person."

## Distinctive meaning

若い can be a relative judgment within a role or age group.
Calling someone a 若い先生 says something about age, not necessarily about when they joined
the school. Keep youth distinct from being new to a particular job.
