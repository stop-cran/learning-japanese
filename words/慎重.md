# 慎重 (しんちょう) - cautious; prudent

## Meaning

慎重 is a na-adjective and noun expressing care, caution, or prudence. Use **慎重な + noun**, **慎重に + verb**, and **慎重だ / 慎重です** as the predicate.

- 慎重に判断してください。 (しんちょうにはんだんしてください。) - Please make your judgment carefully.
- 彼女は仕事選びに慎重です。 (かのじょはしごとえらびにしんちょうです。) - She is cautious about choosing a job.

慎重な判断 (しんちょうなはんだん) is "a careful judgment." In the second example, に identifies the matter toward which the caution is directed.

## How the kanji combine

慎 contributes being careful or circumspect; [重](../kanji/重.md) contributes weight or seriousness. Together they suggest treating a matter seriously and carefully. This is a meaning aid, not a literal claim that a cautious person is physically heavy.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[注意](注意.md) (ちゅうい) is attention or caution; 注意する describes paying attention or taking care. 軽率 (けいそつ), "rash; thoughtless," is the dictionary's contrasting term. A person can act decisively while still being 慎重.

## Distinctive meaning

慎重 emphasizes considering risks or consequences rather than simply moving slowly. 慎重に話す (しんちょうにはなす) can mean choosing one's words carefully, not necessarily speaking at a low speed. It also does not guarantee a correct result: the word describes the approach, while the outcome requires separate information.
