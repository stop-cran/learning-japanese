# ダム - dam

## Meaning

ダム is a dam: a structure that holds back water, commonly across
a river. Dams can serve purposes such as storing water, generating
electricity, or controlling floods.

- このダムは町に水を供給しています。
  (このダムはまちにみずをきょうきゅうしています。) - This dam supplies water to the town.
- 旅行中に大きなダムを見学しました。
  (りょこうちゅうにおおきなダムをけんがくしました。) - I visited a large dam during my trip.

The noun refers to the structure, not every lake or water supply.

## How the kanji combine

ダム is a katakana loanword from English "dam," with no written
kanji components. The final ム is part of the word's pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

堰堤 (えんてい) is a related term for a dam or embankment.
[湖](湖.md) (みずうみ), "lake," names a body of water, not
the structure holding it back. There is no useful general antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not learn ダム as the English adjective "dumb." That gloss
is an error in the pinned community vocabulary list; its supplied
dictionary identity and the inspected Japanese dictionary entry
both identify a dam. Unrelated personal and place names on a
dictionary lookup page do not change this noun's meaning.
