# 杉 (すぎ) - Japanese cedar

## Meaning

杉 is Japanese cedar, *Cryptomeria japonica*. It names a particular kind of tree, not every tree or everything called a cedar in English.

- 山に杉を植えました。 (やまにすぎをうえました。) - We planted Japanese cedars on the mountain.
- 杉の木が高く伸びています。 (すぎのきがたかくのびています。) - The Japanese cedar is growing tall.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single kanji with the native reading すぎ, not a compound to split into separate words. Botanical writing also uses スギ. JMdict marks that katakana form as kana-only; it does not supply a different pronunciation of the written character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[木](木.md) is a tree or wood more generally. [松](松.md), pine, names a different kind of tree, not an opposite of 杉 or another reading of it.

## Distinctive meaning

The scientific name helps identify the intended species when English tree names vary. JMdict also records 椙 as a rare spelling; recognition of that variant is not a reason to replace the ordinary teaching spelling 杉.
