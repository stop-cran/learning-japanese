# げらげら — roaring with laughter

## Meaning

げらげら describes loud, hearty, unrestrained laughter.

- 友達の冗談に、みんなげらげら笑った。 — Everyone roared with laughter at my friend's joke. (冗談: じょうだん.)
- 子供たちは変な声をまねして、げらげらと笑っていた。 — The children were imitating a funny voice and laughing loudly.

The usual construction is げらげら笑う or げらげらと笑う. The laughter verb supplies the action; do not treat every sound word as a standalone する verb.

## How the kanji combine

The repeated kana form evokes the sound and continuing manner of human laughter. It is a voice-imitation expression, not a kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くすくす](くすくす.md) is quiet chuckling or giggling. [にこにこ](にこにこ.md) describes a cheerful smile and does not require laughter aloud. Quiet laughter is a contrast in manner, not a strict logical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

げらげら can sound carefree, but in a solemn setting it may suggest an inappropriate lack of restraint. The expression identifies the laughter's manner, not whether the joke is kind or cruel. It is not a general word for smiling.
