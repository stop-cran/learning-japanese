# こつこつ — steadily; diligently

## Meaning

こつこつ describes continuing with patient, steady effort.

- 彼女は毎朝こつこつ単語を復習している。
  — She diligently reviews vocabulary every morning.
- 新しい自転車を買うため、毎月こつこつ貯金している。
  — I am steadily saving money each month to buy a new bicycle.

## Synonyms and antonyms

こつこつ emphasizes quiet persistence over time. せっせと also describes diligent work, often
with more emphasis on active busyness.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can also imitate a light tapping or knocking sound. These examples use its steady-effort
sense.
