# 五 (ご) — five

## Meaning

五 is the number five, read ご in ordinary number recitation and many number-plus-counter combinations. It can also be written 5.

一から五まで数えます。 (いちからごまでかぞえます。) — I count from one to five.

## How the kanji combine

[五](../kanji/五.md) uses the on reading ゴ here. It combines with other number characters: 十五 (じゅうご), "fifteen," and 五十 (ごじゅう), "fifty." The order changes the number.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[五つ](五つ.md) (いつつ) counts five things in the native counting series. 五人 (ごにん) means five people, and 五冊 (ごさつ) means five books or other bound volumes.

## Distinctive meaning

The numeral does not replace every counter expression. Five days is 五日 (いつか), whereas May is 五月 (ごがつ). Learn these complete forms rather than assigning ご automatically to every occurrence of 五. There is no meaningful lexical antonym of a number.
