# 薄める (うすめる) - dilute; tone down

## Meaning

薄める is a transitive ichidan verb: dilute something, lighten a color or make a flavor less strong.
It also means softening an impression or toning down an effect.
The substance or effect weakened takes を; added material can take で.

- 水でジュースを薄めてください。 (みずでジュースをうすめてください。) - Please dilute the juice with water.
- 白を加えて色を薄めました。 (しろをくわえていろをうすめました。) - I added white to lighten the color.

Forms include 薄めます (うすめます), 薄めた (うすめた) and 薄めない (うすめない).
Abstract uses concern reduced strength or impact, not pouring water over words.

## How the kanji combine

[薄](../kanji/薄.md) supplies うす, associated with thinness or weak concentration;
める is written in kana. This native verb is related to [薄い](薄い.md) (うすい).
The verb describes making something less strong, while the adjective describes its state.

## Synonyms and antonyms

薄くする (うすくする) overlaps in making something less concentrated.
薄まる (うすまる) is intransitive: 味が薄まる (あじがうすまる), "the flavor becomes weaker."
[濃い](濃い.md) (こい) describes a strong or concentrated state; 濃くする (こくする) can contrast with dilution.

## Distinctive meaning

Concentration and physical thickness are different dimensions.
薄める suits weakening a drink or color, not automatically cutting a board thinner.
The abstract sense can concern the force of a statement or an impression; it does not guarantee
that the original content has been denied or erased.
