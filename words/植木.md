# 植木 (うえき) - garden tree; potted plant

## Meaning

植木 is a garden plant or tree, or a potted plant. Context determines
whether it is growing in the garden or in a container.

- 毎朝、植木に水をやります。 (まいあさ、うえきにみずをやります。) - I water the garden plants every morning.
- 玄関に植木の鉢を置きました。 (げんかんにうえきのはちをおきました。) - I put a potted plant by the entrance.

The word does not require every plant to be a large tree.
Nor does it alone specify a species or a pot.

## How the kanji combine

[植](../kanji/植.md) contributes the native stem うえ from
[植える](植える.md), "plant," and [木](../kanji/木.md) is き,
"tree." Both parts use native readings. The established noun includes
the potted-plant use rather than literally naming the act of planting.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[植物](植物.md) (しょくぶつ) is the broader biological word for plants,
including wild vegetation. [木](木.md) (き) can mean a tree or wood.
盆栽 (ぼんさい), "bonsai," is a more specific cultivated presentation,
not a synonym for every potted plant.

## Distinctive meaning

植木 names the plant; 植える is the verb for planting it.
Do not confuse the noun's native reading うえき with the on-reading
ショク found in 植物. The appropriate care depends on the actual
plant, not on this general noun.
