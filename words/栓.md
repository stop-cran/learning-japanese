# 栓 (せん) - stopper; plug; tap valve

## Meaning

栓 is a stopper or plug that closes an opening, such as a bottle or a bath drain.
It also means a tap, stopcock or valve controlling flow in a pipe.

- お風呂の栓を抜いてください。 (おふろのせんをぬいてください。) - Please pull out the bath plug.
- ガスの栓を閉めました。 (ガスのせんをしめました。) - I closed the gas valve.

抜く (ぬく) removes the plug; 閉める (しめる) closes the valve.
The second example does not mean removing a cork from a gas pipe.

## How the kanji combine

栓 is a single-character noun using the on reading セン.
It has no separate written components to translate as a compound.
The character occurs in names of both stoppers and flow-control fittings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[蛇口](蛇口.md) (じゃぐち) is a water faucet.
[コック](コック.md) has a separate valve meaning besides its cook/chef meaning.
コルク names cork; a 栓 need not be made from that material.
There is no useful single opposite noun.

## Distinctive meaning

The object and action determine whether English "stopper," "plug," or "valve" fits.
栓 is not a generic name for every lid, button or electrical switch.
Learn 栓をする (せんをする), "put in a stopper; plug an opening,"
alongside the removal and valve-closing patterns above.
