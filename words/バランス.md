# バランス - balance; equilibrium

## Meaning

バランス is balance, equilibrium, or a suitable proportion among parts.
It can concern the body, weights, a design, or a mix of activities.
Useful patterns include バランスを取る (バランスをとる),
"keep or achieve a balance," and バランスがいい, "be well balanced."

- 片足で立って、バランスを取ります。 (かたあしでたって、バランスをとります。) - I stand on one foot and keep my balance.
- 仕事と休みのバランスが大切です。 (しごととやすみのバランスがたいせつです。) - A balance between work and rest is important.

The dictionary also permits intransitive バランスする, "balance."
That does not make する necessary in every noun expression.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "balance," not a kanji compound.
Do not infer every use of the English word from the loanword alone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[釣り合う](釣り合う.md) (つりあう) is a verb for balancing or
being well matched. [安定](安定.md) (あんてい), "stability,"
can concern a steady condition rather than a proportion among parts.
アンバランス means unbalanced or out of proportion.

## Distinctive meaning

Balance does not require equal numerical amounts: a suitable work/rest
balance need not mean equal hours.
The word can also concern the balance of income and expenditure
or of debits and credits.
[差し引き](差し引き.md) (さしひき) names deduction or the net
balance after subtraction; it is not a substitute for physical equilibrium.
