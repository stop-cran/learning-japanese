# 知り合い (しりあい) - acquaintance

## Meaning

知り合い is an acquaintance: a person one knows. It is a noun;
Nの知り合い means an acquaintance of N.

- 駅で知り合いに会いました。 (えきでしりあいにあいました。) - I met an acquaintance at the station.
- 彼女は母の知り合いです。 (かのじょはははのしりあいです。) - She is an acquaintance of my mother's.

に marks the person met in the first example. The noun itself does not
say how, when, or how closely the people came to know each other.

## How the kanji combine

[知](../kanji/知.md) contributes knowing, and [合](../kanji/合.md)
contributes the mutual relationship in this word. The related verb
知り合う (しりあう) means become acquainted. The noun is しりあい,
with the written kana り and い; it is not simply a noun to which
する is added to mean become acquainted.

## Synonyms and antonyms

知人 (ちじん) also means an acquaintance or someone one knows.
[友人](友人.md) (ゆうじん) and [友達](友達.md) (ともだち) name friends.
Being acquainted does not by itself establish a close friendship.
[仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) identifies a companion or fellow group member.

## Distinctive meaning

The source spelling 知合い represents the same acquaintance identity.
This article uses 知り合い. The dictionary's search-only flag on 知合い
is lookup information, not proof that the spelling is invalid Japanese
or a measurement of how often it occurs.
