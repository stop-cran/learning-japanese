# 急速 (きゅうそく) - rapid

## Meaning

急速 describes rapid progress, change, or development. It is a na-adjective: 急速な変化 (きゅうそくなへんか), "rapid change." Use 急速に before a verb to say that a process happens rapidly.

- この町の人口は急速に増えています。 (このまちのじんこうはきゅうそくにふえています。) - This town's population is increasing rapidly.
- 急速な変化についていけません。 (きゅうそくなへんかについていけません。) - I cannot keep up with the rapid changes.

Neither sentence says the change is beneficial: the word describes its pace.

## How the kanji combine

[急](../kanji/急.md) contributes urgency or haste, while [速](../kanji/速.md) contributes speed. The two related elements reinforce the idea of rapid movement or development. The on readings are キュウ + ソク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[速い](速い.md) (はやい) is a broad i-adjective for speed. [急に](急に.md) (きゅうに), "suddenly," can emphasize an abrupt onset, whereas 急速に emphasizes how fast the process proceeds. A change can be both sudden and rapid.

## Distinctive meaning

急速 is especially useful when comparing a rate of change over time. It does not automatically mean "unexpected," and it is not the general instruction "Hurry up!" A gradual or slow process provides a contextual contrast; the relevant opposite depends on what is changing.
