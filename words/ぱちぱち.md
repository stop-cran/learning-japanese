# ぱちぱち — crackling; clapping; blinking

## Meaning

Repeated little snaps or crackles, hands clapping, or eyes blinking.

- たき火がぱちぱち音を立てている。 — The campfire is crackling.
- 子どもたちはぱちぱち手をたたいた。 — The children clapped their hands.
- まぶしくて、目をぱちぱちさせた。 — The brightness made me blink repeatedly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぱんぱん](ぱんぱん.md) also describes sharp claps. [ばきばき](ばきばき.md) usually suggests stronger cracking; [かちかち](かちかち.md) overlaps in clicking.

## Distinctive meaning

目をぱちぱちさせる is movement, not necessarily an audible sound. A fire crackling, an audience applauding, and someone blinking require separate scenes even though they share one word.
