# 下がる (さがる) — go down; become lower

## Meaning

下がる means to go down or become lower, in physical position, price, temperature, or another level. It is an intransitive godan verb: 下がります, 下がって, 下がらない.

- 野菜の値段が下がりました。 (やさいのねだんがさがりました。) — Vegetable prices have fallen.
- 危ないので、少し下がってください。 (あぶないので、すこしさがってください。) — It is dangerous, so please step back a little.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) carries the downward association and is read さ here; がる is okurigana. This written boundary is not the grammatical stem. Learn the verb as さがる.

The source spelling 下る is recorded for さがる with irregular okurigana. This card uses the clearer spelling 下がる. In ordinary writing, 下る is read くだる, a separate verb meaning "go down; descend", as in 坂を下る (さかをくだる, go down a slope).

## Synonyms and antonyms

上がる (あがる), "go up," is the corresponding upward contrast. 下げる (さげる) is transitive: 値段を下げる (ねだんをさげる), "lower the price." 降りる (おりる) is used for getting off a vehicle or descending from a higher place.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss "get down" needs context: 下がる is not the general expression for getting off a bus. It also means moving back from one's current position, as in the safety request above. Mark what becomes lower with が, not を.
