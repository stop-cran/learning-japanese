# 駐車 (ちゅうしゃ) - parking

## Meaning

駐車 is parking a vehicle. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb in the
dictionary. A place can take に: ここに駐車する, "park here."
駐車している describes being parked.

- この場所には駐車できません。 (このばしょにはちゅうしゃできません。) - You cannot park in this location.
- 駅の近くに駐車しました。 (えきのちかくにちゅうしゃしました。) - I parked near the station.

駐車禁止 (ちゅうしゃきんし) means "no parking"; 駐車場 (ちゅうしゃじょう)
is a parking lot or car park.

## How the kanji combine

駐 contributes remaining stationed or stopped, and [車](../kanji/車.md) contributes
a vehicle. Together they concern a vehicle remaining parked. The readings are
チュウ + シャ; keep the long vowel in ちゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

停車 (ていしゃ) is stopping a vehicle and need not mean leaving it parked.
[渋滞](渋滞.md) (じゅうたい) is traffic congestion: cars stopped in a traffic
queue are not thereby described as parked.
車を止める (くるまをとめる) can mean stopping or parking a car, depending on context.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun names the parking action or state, not the parking space itself.
駐車場 names the facility. Signs and regulations can use technical distinctions
between parking and stopping, so the vocabulary comparison here is not a complete
statement of traffic law; follow the rules applicable to the location.
