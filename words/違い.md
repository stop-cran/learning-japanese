# 違い (ちがい) - difference; discrepancy

## Meaning

違い is a noun for a difference or distinction. AとBの違い means "the difference between A and B."
Use 違いがある for "there is a difference" and 違いが分かる for "understand or notice the difference."

- この二つの言葉の違いが分かりますか。 (このふたつのことばのちがいがわかりますか。) - Do you understand the difference between these two words?
- 値段には大きな違いがあります。 (ねだんにはおおきなちがいがあります。) - There is a large difference in price.

An adjective can modify the noun: 大きな違い is a large difference, and 小さな違い
(ちいさなちがい) is a small one.

## How the kanji combine

[違](../kanji/違.md) supplies the idea of differing or diverging.
違い is related to the verb [違う](違う.md) (ちがう), "differ; be different."
The final い is part of a noun form here, not an i-adjective ending:
use 違いがある rather than conjugating it like 高い.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[共通](共通.md) (きょうつう), "shared; in common," gives the contrasting viewpoint.
[間違い](間違い.md) (まちがい) usually identifies a mistake. A 違い can be neutral:
two colors or prices need not involve anyone making an error.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun identifies a distinction without necessarily judging one side correct or superior.
When comparing language forms, specify which aspect differs: meaning, usage, pronunciation, or politeness.
AとBの違い names the comparison itself; the sentence still needs to explain what the difference is.
