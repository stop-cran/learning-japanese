# 桁 (けた) - beam; numerical digit

## Meaning

桁 has a structural meaning, a horizontal beam or girder, and a numerical meaning:
a digit position or the number of places in a number.

- 橋の桁を点検します。 (はしのけたをてんけんします。) - We will inspect the bridge girders.
- この番号は六桁です。 (このばんごうはろくけたです。) - This identifying number has six digits.
- 百は三桁の数です。 (ひゃくはさんけたのかずです。) - One hundred is a three-digit number.

In comparisons of scale, 桁 can also express an order of magnitude.
一桁 (ひとけた) and 二桁 (ふたけた) are useful complete forms to learn.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single character with the native reading けた.
The 木 element can serve as a visual reminder of a wooden beam; that is a mnemonic, not a derivation of every numerical use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[数字](数字.md) (すうじ) can name a numeral; 桁 instead helps identify its position or count places.
[位](位.md) (くらい) also names numerical places, as in the ones or tens place.
[柱](柱.md) (はしら) is a pillar, not the usual translation of a horizontal 桁.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss "column" needs context: do not use 桁 for any vertical building column or arbitrary spreadsheet column.
In 六桁の番号, 桁 counts digits, not six separate identifying numbers.
The structural and numerical senses share a spelling and reading but are not interchangeable in every sentence.
