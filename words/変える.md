# 変える (かえる) - change something

## Meaning

変える means to change, alter, or revise something. It is transitive: the thing
changed takes を. A resulting state, time, or choice can take に.

- 予定を変えました。 (よていをかえました。) - I changed the plan.
- 会議の時間を三時に変えます。 (かいぎのじかんをさんじにかえます。) - I will change the meeting time to three o'clock.

It is an ichidan verb: 変えます, 変えない, 変えて.

## How the kanji combine

[変](../kanji/変.md) supplies the idea of change; える is okurigana.
The grammatical base is かえ-, obtained by removing final る, not all the kana
written after the character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

変わる (かわる) describes something changing: 予定が変わった (よていがかわった),
"the plan changed." It is the intransitive partner, not an antonym.
直す (なおす), "fix," specifically suggests putting something right.

## Distinctive meaning

変える need not mean improving something. Nor is it automatically the spelling
for every English "change": replacing an item or exchanging money may use
other かえる spellings, such as 替える or 換える.
