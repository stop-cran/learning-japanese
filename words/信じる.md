# 信じる (しんじる) — believe; trust

## Meaning

信じる means to believe something or place trust in someone.
It is transitive: a person or claim can take を.
A complete proposition can instead be followed by と to state what is believed.

- 私はあなたを信じています。 (わたしはあなたをしんじています。) — I trust you.
- 彼は来ると信じています。 (かれはくるとしんじています。) — I believe that he will come.

The polite form is 信じます (しんじます), and the plain past is 信じた (しんじた).

## How the kanji combine

[信](../kanji/信.md) contributes trust or belief through the on reading シン.
It combines with the verb ending じる; there is no second kanji.
This article groups the on-based verb under kango, but the complete word inflects as a Japanese verb.
The kana ending does not turn シン into a kun reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[思う](思う.md) (おもう) can express thinking or holding an opinion.
信じる more directly expresses belief or trust; for example, trusting a person's account is not simply thinking about that person.
[疑う](疑う.md) (うたがう), "doubt; distrust," gives a useful opposing attitude.

## Distinctive meaning

The word covers both accepting something as true and having faith in a person.
It does not by itself prove that the belief is correct.
Compare 信用 (しんよう), the noun "trust; confidence," which can also take する.
Learn the object or quoted proposition along with the verb, rather than translating every English "think" as 信じる.
