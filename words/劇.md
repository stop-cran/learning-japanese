# 劇 (げき) - play; drama

## Meaning

劇 is a noun for a play or dramatic performance. Useful combinations include **劇を見る**
(げきをみる), "watch a play," **劇に出る** (げきにでる), "appear in a play," and
**劇を上演する** (げきをじょうえんする), "stage a play."

- 学校で短い劇を見ました。 (がっこうでみじかいげきをみました。) - I watched a short play at school.
- 妹は劇で王様の役を演じました。 (いもうとはげきでおうさまのやくをえんじました。) - My younger sister played the king in the play.

In the second sentence, で identifies the performance in which she played the role.
劇 names the work or performance, not the building where it happens.

## How the kanji combine

劇 is a single-character kango noun using the on reading ゲキ.
There is no compound to divide. The same reading occurs in [劇場](劇場.md) (げきじょう),
"theater," and [悲劇](悲劇.md) (ひげき), "tragedy."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[芝居](芝居.md) (しばい) overlaps with a play or theatrical performance.
[ドラマ](ドラマ.md) can refer to drama, including television drama.
[映画](映画.md) (えいが) identifies a film; these labels distinguish forms of presentation rather than opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

English "play" also means playing a sport, game, or instrument. Those actions are not expressed by
the noun 劇. 劇をする can describe putting on a play, not simply "playing" in the broad English sense.
For clarity, learn the specific performance verbs in the examples.
