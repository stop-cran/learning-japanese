# 一生 (いっしょう) - a lifetime; all one's life

## Meaning

一生 names an entire lifetime. It can function as a noun or directly modify an action,
meaning "for one's whole life." Before another noun, use の: 一生の思い出 (いっしょうのおもいで),
"a memory to treasure for a lifetime."

- この日のことは一生忘れません。 (このひのことはいっしょうわすれません。) - I will never forget this day as long as I live.
- 祖母はこの町で一生を過ごしました。 (そぼはこのまちでいっしょうをすごしました。) - My grandmother spent her whole life in this town.

The first example combines the lifetime expression with a negative verb. 一生 itself does not mean "never."
In the second, を marks the lifetime as what was spent.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," modifies [生](../kanji/生.md), "life."
The compound presents one complete life as a span. The readings form いっしょう, with a small っ
and a long しょう; do not read it as いちせい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[人生](人生.md) (じんせい) also concerns a human life, including its experiences and course.
一生 foregrounds the whole span. [生活](生活.md) (せいかつ) concerns living and daily life,
not necessarily an entire lifetime.

## Distinctive meaning

Expressions such as 一生に一度 (いっしょうにいちど), "once in a lifetime," define their scale
by a person's life, not by eternity. The speaker's own future lifetime is understood in the first example;
the word does not identify whose life without context.
