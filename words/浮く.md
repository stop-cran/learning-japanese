# 浮く (うく) — float

## Meaning

浮く means to float. In the physical sense taught here, something stays at the surface rather than sinking.
It is intransitive: the floating thing is the subject, not an object being acted on.

- 木は水に浮きます。 (きはみずにうきます。) — Wood floats on water.
- 葉が水に浮いています。 (はがみずにういています。) — A leaf is floating on the water.

浮く is a godan verb. Its forms include 浮きます (うきます) and 浮いている (ういている).

## How the kanji combine

[浮](../kanji/浮.md) carries the floating meaning; く is the verb ending.
The kanji has the native reading う- here. Other verbs written with 浮 have different endings,
so learn 浮く as a whole verb rather than treating く as optional.

## Synonyms and antonyms

浮かぶ (うかぶ) also means to float, but has additional uses such as アイデアが浮かぶ
(アイデアがうかぶ), "an idea comes to mind." That expression is worth learning as its own pattern.
沈む (しずむ), "sink," is the direct contrast for the physical water example.

## Distinctive meaning

Floating is not the same as [泳ぐ](泳ぐ.md) (およぐ), "swim."
The leaf in the example is supported by the water without swimming through it.
Keep this article's physical sense separate from other uses of 浮く rather than assuming
that every floating expression in English takes the same Japanese verb.
