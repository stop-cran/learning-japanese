# 甚だしい (はなはだしい) - extreme; excessive

## Meaning

甚だしい is an i-adjective for an extreme or excessive degree.
It often describes undesirable conditions, but the word is not restricted to a fixed list of bad things.
Its adverbial form is 甚だしく (はなはだしく).

- 両者には甚だしい違いがある。 (りょうしゃにははなはだしいちがいがある。) - There is an enormous difference between the two.
- この地域は被害が甚だしかった。 (このちいきはひがいがはなはだしかった。) - The damage in this area was severe.

## How the kanji combine

甚 writes the native adjective はなはだしい; there is no multi-character reading to assemble.
The related adverb 甚だ (はなはだ), "extremely," is a separate word.
Do not conjugate 甚だしい as a na-adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ひどい](ひどい.md) can mean terrible or severe.
[きつい](きつい.md) and [激しい](激しい.md) (はげしい) overlap in strong or intense conditions.
[深刻](深刻.md) (しんこく) concerns gravity, and [重大](重大.md) (じゅうだい) seriousness or significance.
These distinctions guide a choice in context; they do not create absolute non-overlapping compartments.

## Distinctive meaning

The focus is degree: extreme difference, serious damage, excessive behavior.
It has a more formal feel than many everyday uses of ひどい.
English "terrible" is not a license to use it for every morally bad person or every unpleasant object.
