# 何時 (なんじ) — what time

## Meaning

何時 asks for the hour of the clock: 今何時ですか (What time is it now?), 何時に起きますか (What time do you get up?), 何時から何時まで (from what time to what time).

## How the kanji combine

[何](../kanji/何.md) (what) plus [時](../kanji/時.md) (o'clock); an on + kun mix (なん is the kun reading of 何, ジ the on reading of 時). 何 is read なん, not なに, because it comes before a counter. The answer uses the same counter: 三時 さんじ, **四時 よじ**, **七時 しちじ**, **九時 くじ**.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 何時間 なんじかん (how many hours), 何分 なんぷん (how many minutes), いつ (when).
- 何時 asks for a clock time. いつ asks for a general time (day, month, occasion).

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 何時 なんじ (what time) with 何時間 なんじかん (how many hours); only the second has 間.
