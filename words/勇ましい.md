# 勇ましい (いさましい) - brave; rousing

## Meaning

勇ましい is an い-adjective for someone or something brave, valiant, or
courageous. It also describes something vigorous or rousing, such as a voice.

- 彼女は勇ましく立ち向かいました。 (かのじょはいさましくたちむかいました。) - She faced the challenge bravely.
- 子供たちは勇ましい声で歌いました。 (こどもたちはいさましいこえでうたいました。) - The children sang in rousing voices.

勇ましく modifies an action; 勇ましかった is the past form.
Before a noun, use 勇ましい directly, without な.

## How the kanji combine

[勇](../kanji/勇.md) supplies the courage association. The native adjective is
read いさましい, including its written ending ましい. It is related in form
and meaning to 勇む (いさむ), "be in high spirits; be eager."
Do not substitute the on reading ユウ in this adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

勇敢 (ゆうかん) also means brave, with な before a noun.
[勇気](勇気.md) (ゆうき), courage, is a noun rather than the same adjective.
臆病 (おくびょう), timid or cowardly, gives a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

A rousing song or vigorous declaration need not describe an act of physical
heroism. Conversely, a brave act need not involve a loud voice. Let the noun
and situation determine whether courage or a spirited manner is being emphasized.
The word is not restricted to one gender.
