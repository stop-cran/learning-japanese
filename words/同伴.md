# 同伴 (どうはん) — accompanying; going together

## Meaning

同伴 means going somewhere together with someone, or bringing someone along. JMdict glosses it “accompanying; being accompanied by; going with”, and it can be used as a verb, 同伴する. Digital Daijisen defines it as going along together with someone.

- 子どもは、親の同伴が必要です。
  (こどもは、おやのどうはんがひつようです。)
  "Children must be accompanied by a parent."
- 彼は妻を同伴して、パーティーに来ました。
  (かれはつまをどうはんして、パーティーにきました。)
  "He came to the party with his wife."

These are original usage examples, not quotations from a dictionary or other source.

## How the kanji combine

[同](../kanji/同.md) (ドウ) contributes doing something together; [伴](../kanji/伴.md) (ハン) contributes accompanying someone and taking them along. This is a modifier + head relation: going along together. It explains the compound's meaning, not the historical origin of either character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一緒](一緒.md) (いっしょ), "together," is broader: it can describe doing anything together, not only going somewhere with someone. [連れる](連れる.md) (つれる), "take someone along," is a verb for taking someone with you; 同伴 is a noun, used as 同伴する. [連れ](連れ.md) (つれ), "companion; accompanying person," names the person who comes along; 同伴 names the act of going together.

There is no single fixed antonym. Going alone, 単独 たんどく, “sole; single; solo”, is a contrast, not a fixed opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

同伴 is about going or attending together with someone. Digital Daijisen adds that it is used especially of a man and a woman going together. The person who comes along is a 同伴者 どうはんしゃ, “companion”.
