# あげる (あげる) - to give

## Meaning

あげる describes giving something to someone, viewed from the giver's side. Mark the recipient with に and the gift with を.

- 友達に本をあげました。 (ともだちにほんをあげました。) - I gave a friend a book.

It is an ichidan verb: あげます, あげない, あげて. After a verb's て-form, it presents an action as a favor:

- 妹に本を読んであげました。 (いもうとにほんをよんであげました。) - I read a book to my younger sister.

## How the kanji combine

This giving sense is normally written in kana. 上げる is a related spelling, especially familiar for the separate meaning "raise"; the kana spelling avoids making that the focus here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

くれる describes giving toward the speaker or someone viewed as close to the speaker. もらう describes receiving. These are different viewpoints, not simple opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

あげる is usual for giving to peers or people below one's social position. In respectful situations, さしあげる may be appropriate. Directly offering to do something with てあげる can sound patronizing because it emphasizes the favor; it is not a universally polite offer.
