# 日曜 (にちよう) - Sunday

## Meaning

日曜 is a noun meaning Sunday. It is the shorter form corresponding to 日曜日 (にちようび), with the final 日 omitted. Both identify the same weekday.

- 次の日曜に会いましょう。 (つぎのにちようにあいましょう。) - Let's meet next Sunday.
- この店は日曜が休みです。 (このみせはにちようがやすみです。) - This shop is closed on Sundays.

In the first example, に marks when to meet. The shop sentence gives a recurring schedule; context rather than a plural ending produces the English "Sundays."

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) contributes the sun-associated weekday name, and [曜](../kanji/曜.md) identifies a day of the week. The readings combine as にち + よう. Keep the long よう sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[日曜日](日曜日.md) is the full corresponding word, not a different day. Other weekdays contrast in the calendar, but there is no antonym meaning "the opposite of Sunday."

## Distinctive meaning

日曜 is useful in schedules, notices, and plans, without changing the day referred to by 日曜日. Do not assume that the word itself guarantees a holiday or a day off: the shop example explicitly states the closure. A person's working pattern is separate information. Likewise, "next Sunday" requires context or a modifier such as 次の; 日曜 alone does not specify which week.
