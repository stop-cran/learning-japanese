# 枚 (まい) - counter for thin flat objects

## Meaning

枚 is a counter for thin, flat objects such as sheets of paper, cards,
plates, or coins. Put it after a number: 一枚 (いちまい), one flat object;
二枚 (にまい), two; 三枚 (さんまい), three.

- 紙が二枚あります。 (かみがにまいあります。) - There are two sheets of paper.
- 切手を三枚買いました。 (きってをさんまいかいました。) - I bought three postage stamps.

The noun identifies what is counted. In the second example, 切手を is the
object of 買いました, and 三枚 gives its quantity. Another useful pattern is
二枚の紙 (にまいのかみ), two sheets of paper.

## How the kanji combine

[枚](../kanji/枚.md) is one character using the on reading マイ. It follows
the number without okurigana. The numeral and counter form one count;
there is no の between 二 and 枚 in 二枚.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冊](../kanji/冊.md) (さつ) counts bound volumes, and [匹](../kanji/匹.md)
(ひき) counts many kinds of animals. These are different counters, not
synonyms for 枚 or opposites of it. A book's flat shape does not by itself
make 枚 its ordinary counter.

## Distinctive meaning

枚 is not limited to paper and does not mean that the objects must be
rectangular. Learn the conventional counter with the object. In the counts
above, まい stays まい; the source-listed character reading バイ belongs to
a separate archaic expression, not a change used for counting three items.
