# 歯磨き (はみがき) - toothpaste; toothbrushing

## Meaning

歯磨き can name the substance used to clean teeth: toothpaste, tooth
powder, or dentifrice. It also names the activity of brushing one's teeth.
The community source specifically supplies the substance meaning;
both uses are useful to recognize.

- 歯ブラシに歯磨きをつけました。 (はブラシにはみがきをつけました。) - I put toothpaste on the toothbrush.
- 寝る前に歯磨きをします。 (ねるまえにはみがきをします。) - I brush my teeth before bed.

The activity can take する. 歯磨き粉 (はみがきこ) is another word for
toothpaste or tooth powder; the character 粉 does not require a dry
powder product in every use.

## How the kanji combine

[歯](../kanji/歯.md) は, "tooth," combines with 磨き みがき,
from [磨く](磨く.md) (みがく), "polish; brush teeth."
[磨](../kanji/磨.md) keeps the native みが reading. The noun combines
the thing cleaned with the cleaning action; it is not シマ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

歯を磨く (はをみがく) clearly states the action, with 歯 as the object
of the transitive verb. 歯磨きする packages that activity into a noun
plus する; it does not mean polishing a tube of toothpaste.
歯ブラシ is the toothbrush, not the paste or the activity.

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding verb helps identify the sense: putting 歯磨き on a brush
uses the substance meaning, while 歯磨きをする describes dental care.
Do not translate every occurrence as either "toothpaste" or "brushing"
without checking what the sentence says is being done.
