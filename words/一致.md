# 一致 (いっち) - agreement; correspondence

## Meaning

一致 is agreement or a match between things. 一致する is intransitive:
AとBが一致する means "A and B agree," and AがBと一致する means "A matches B."
It can describe opinions, figures, accounts, or other corresponding information.

- 二人の意見が一致しました。 (ふたりのいけんがいっちしました。) - The two people's opinions agreed.
- この数字は記録と一致していません。 (このすうじはきろくといっちしていません。) - This number does not match the record.

一致している describes an existing match; 一致した can report that agreement
was reached or that a match was found, according to context.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) contributes one or unity, and 致 contributes bringing or
reaching. Together the word presents things as coming into agreement.
The reading is イッチ, with a small っ, not いちち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ), "the same," overlaps in some comparisons but does not
share the する construction. 不一致 (ふいっち), "disagreement; mismatch,"
is a direct contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "coincidence" here can mean correspondence, such as two
measurements matching. It does not make 一致 a substitute for every surprising
chance event. Check what the two matching things are before choosing the
translation; agreement of opinions need not be accidental.
