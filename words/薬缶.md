# 薬缶 (やかん) - kettle

## Meaning

薬缶 is a kettle used for boiling water.
Kana **やかん** is usual; the source's written form is retained here.
薬缶でお湯を沸かす (やかんでおゆをわかす) means to boil water in a kettle.

- 薬缶でお湯を沸かしました。 (やかんでおゆをわかしました。) - I boiled water in a kettle.
- 薬缶に水を入れてください。 (やかんにみずをいれてください。) - Please put water in the kettle.

で identifies the utensil in the first example;
に identifies the container receiving water in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[薬](../kanji/薬.md) contributes medicine and [缶](../kanji/缶.md)
a vessel or container.
Daijisen connects the name with the vessel's former use for decocting medicine.
Today it does not mean that the water must contain medicine.
Learn the established on-based reading **やかん**, not a mechanical やくかん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[釜](釜.md) (かま) includes cooking pots and tea kettles,
so English "kettle" can overlap.
[鍋](鍋.md) (なべ) is a cooking pot or pan, not automatically this water-boiling utensil.
ケトル is another dictionary word for a kettle.

## Distinctive meaning

The name does not require one particular material or the presence of a whistle.
The unrelated time word [夜間](夜間.md) has the same reading やかん.
The dictionary also records a colloquial abbreviation for "bald head";
that does not change the kitchen meaning or make the utensil word insulting.
