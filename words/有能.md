# 有能 (ゆうのう) - capable; competent

## Meaning

有能 is a な-adjective, also usable as a noun, describing someone as capable or competent. It evaluates ability to perform a role or task effectively.

- 彼女は有能な研究者です。 (かのじょはゆうのうなけんきゅうしゃです。) - She is a capable researcher.
- 新しい担当者はとても有能です。 (あたらしいたんとうしゃはとてもゆうのうです。) - The new person in charge is very competent.

Before a noun, use 有能な. At the end of a polite sentence, use 有能です; the negative is 有能ではありません. This is not an い-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[有](../kanji/有.md) contributes having or possessing, and [能](../kanji/能.md) contributes ability. Together they express possessing useful ability. Both vowels are long in ゆうのう: ユウ + ノウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上手](上手.md) (じょうず) means skillful or good at an activity. [優秀](優秀.md) (ゆうしゅう) evaluates excellence and can overlap with 有能. 無能 (むのう), "incompetent," is the dictionary's direct antonym, but is a strong negative judgment when applied to someone.

## Distinctive meaning

有能 does not just mean that someone is able to do one action at this moment. 泳げる (およげる), "can swim," states a particular ability; 有能 evaluates competence more broadly in context. It also does not automatically describe kindness, honesty, or social status. A positive judgment about ability is not a judgment about every aspect of a person.
