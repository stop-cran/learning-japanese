# 直角 (ちょっかく) - right angle; at right angles

## Meaning

直角 is a right angle, exactly ninety degrees.
直角に describes meeting or turning at a right angle.

- この角は直角です。
  (このかくはちょっかくです。) - This angle is a right angle.
- 二本の線が直角に交わっています。
  (にほんのせんがちょっかくにまじわっています。) - The two lines intersect at right angles.

The reading かく in the first example is angle,
not the separate [角](角.md) (かど), corner.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) contributes straight or upright alignment here,
and [角](../kanji/角.md) angle.
The established compound means a right angle, not a straight angle of 180 degrees.
チョク + カク becomes ちょっかく, with small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[垂直](垂直.md) (すいちょく) can describe a perpendicular relationship:
the directions meet at a right angle.
[角度](角度.md) (かくど) is the angle or its measure more generally.
鋭角 (えいかく), acute angle, is less than ninety degrees;
鈍角 (どんかく), obtuse angle, is between ninety and 180 degrees.

## Distinctive meaning

An angle stays right when the diagram is rotated.
It need not have one arm upright on the page.
In a direction or drawing instruction, 直角に concerns the angular relationship,
not whether an object looks straight or is in the correct place.
