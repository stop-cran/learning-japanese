# 真っ青 (まっさお) - deep blue; ghastly pale

## Meaning

真っ青 describes deep or bright blue. It also describes a face becoming
very pale, for example from shock, fear, or illness.

- 空は真っ青でした。 (そらはまっさおでした。) - The sky was a vivid blue.
- その知らせを聞いて、彼は真っ青になりました。 (そのしらせをきいて、かれはまっさおになりました。) - He went deathly pale when he heard the news.

It is a na-adjective: 真っ青な空 (まっさおなそら), a vividly blue sky.

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md) contributes intensity, and [青](../kanji/青.md)
the color association. Learn まっさお as the established whole reading,
including っさ; do not substitute まっあお mechanically.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[青い](青い.md) (あおい) also has a pale-face use.
[青白い](青白い.md) (あおじろい), pale or bluish-white, overlaps in
complexion. [紺](紺.md) (こん) specifically names navy or deep blue;
not every vivid blue is navy.

## Distinctive meaning

The pale-face use does not require a literally saturated blue face.
Translate the whole situation, not just the written character 青.
There is no single opposite covering both the sky's color and a person's
frightened appearance.
