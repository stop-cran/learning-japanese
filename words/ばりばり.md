# ばりばり — ripping or crunching; working vigorously

## Meaning

Forceful ripping or crunching sounds, or energetic, full-on activity.

- 厚（あつ）い紙をばりばり破（やぶ）いた。 — I tore the thick paper with a loud ripping sound.
- 兄（あに）は新しい職場（しょくば）でばりばり働（はたら）いている。 — My older brother is working energetically at his new workplace.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[びりびり](びりびり.md) overlaps in ripping; [ぱりぱり](ぱりぱり.md) in crisp crunching. [もりもり](もりもり.md) can also suggest vigorous activity.

## Distinctive meaning

ばりばり働く does not require noise. The word also describes stiffness or starchiness, and continuous loud sound. Do not turn the common heavier impression relative to ぱりぱり into an exact loudness rule.
