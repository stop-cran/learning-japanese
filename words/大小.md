# 大小 (だいしょう) — large and small, size

## Meaning

大小 joins two opposites, [大](../kanji/大.md) (big) and [小](../kanji/小.md) (small). Together they name the whole dimension: not "big and small" as two things,
but "size" or "how large or small something is".

- 大小さまざまな石 — stones of all sizes
- 大小を問わず — regardless of size
- 大小の差 — the difference in size

## How the kanji combine

This is a very productive kango pattern: two antonymous kanji produce an abstract noun for the scale between them.

| Compound | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 大小 | だいしょう | size |
| [長短](長短.md) | ちょうたん | length; pros and cons |
| 高低 | こうてい | height, pitch |
| 多少 | たしょう | quantity, "somewhat" |
| 遠近 | えんきん | distance |

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close in meaning: 大きさ おおきさ (the plain native noun for "size"), サイズ (loanword, for clothing and shoe sizes).
- Opposite members: [大](../kanji/大.md) ↔ [小](../kanji/小.md). There is no single antonym of the whole word.
- Related: [中小](中小.md) — "medium and small", a different pairing.

## Distinctive meaning

大小 is written and formal. In daily speech 大きさ is the usual way to ask or state size, and shirts come in サイズ.
大小 is common in set phrases such as 大小を問わず, 大小にかかわらず and 大小さまざま.
