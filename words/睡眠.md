# 睡眠 (すいみん) - sleep

## Meaning

睡眠 is a noun for sleep. It is useful when discussing sleep as a bodily activity, its amount, or its quality. A practical construction is 睡眠を取る (すいみんをとる), "get some sleep."

- 昨日は十分な睡眠を取りました。 (きのうはじゅうぶんなすいみんをとりました。) - I got enough sleep yesterday.
- 最近は睡眠時間が短いです。 (さいきんはすいみんじかんがみじかいです。) - Recently, my sleeping hours have been short.

睡眠時間 (すいみんじかん) is the time spent sleeping. 睡眠の質 (すいみんのしつ) means "sleep quality"; duration and quality are different things.

## How the kanji combine

睡 and [眠](../kanji/眠.md) both contribute sleeping. Their similar meanings reinforce each other, with the on readings スイ + ミン. This is not a verb-object compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[眠る](眠る.md) (ねむる) is the verb "sleep." [寝る](寝る.md) (ねる) can mean going to bed or lying down as well as sleeping. [休む](休む.md) (やすむ) means resting or taking time off and need not involve sleep.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the noun in constructions such as 睡眠が必要です (すいみんがひつようです), "Sleep is necessary," rather than assuming that every noun can freely take する. To say "I slept," 眠りました or 寝ました supplies the ordinary verb construction. Being in bed does not by itself establish that one is asleep.
