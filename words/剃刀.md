# 剃刀 (かみそり) - razor

## Meaning

剃刀 is a razor, the tool used for shaving. The dictionary marks the tool sense usually written in kana; recognize かみそり and カミソリ as well as the kanji spelling.

- 新しいかみそりを買いました。 (あたらしいかみそりをかいました。) - I bought a new razor.
- 電気かみそりでひげをそります。 (でんきかみそりでひげをそります。) - I shave my beard with an electric razor.

電気かみそり (でんきかみそり) is an electric shaver.

## How the kanji combine

剃 concerns shaving and 刀 a blade, which helps associate the spelling with a shaving tool. The assigned かみそり is a special whole-word reading: do not split it into invented independent readings for 剃 and 刀.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ナイフ](ナイフ.md) is a knife. Both involve blades, but a knife is not automatically a shaving razor. ひげをそる means "shave one's beard"; 剃刀 names the tool rather than the shaving action.

## Distinctive meaning

This lesson teaches the physical tool sense. The word is not a general translation for every sharp object, and the kana spelling does not change the tool into a different lexical item.
