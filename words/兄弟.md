# 兄弟 (きょうだい) — siblings

## Meaning

兄弟 means siblings: brothers and sisters. Depending on context, it can refer specifically to brothers, but the written characters do not make every use male-only.

- 兄弟はいますか。 (きょうだいはいますか。) — Do you have any siblings?
- 私たちは三人兄弟です。 (わたしたちはさんにんきょうだいです。) — There are three of us siblings.

The second example counts the speaker as one of the three. A question about how many brothers or sisters someone has may instead require an answer excluding the speaker; make the intended count clear.

## How the kanji combine

[兄](../kanji/兄.md), "older brother," and [弟](../kanji/弟.md), "younger brother," combine with the on-readings キョウ + ダイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

姉妹 (しまい) specifically means sisters. 一人っ子 (ひとりっこ), "only child," is a useful contrast to having siblings.

## Distinctive meaning

兄弟 is an ordinary family noun, not inherently a humble form. ご兄弟 (ごきょうだい) adds respectful wording when referring to someone else's siblings.
