# 時刻 (じこく) - time of day

## Meaning

時刻 is a noun for a particular time, such as the clock time when something happens. It is useful for departures, arrivals, appointments, and setting clocks.

- 電車の出発時刻を確認しました。 (でんしゃのしゅっぱつじこくをかくにんしました。) - I checked the train's departure time.
- 約束の時刻に着きました。 (やくそくのじこくにつきました。) - I arrived at the agreed time.

に marks the time at which the arrival occurs. 出発時刻 (しゅっぱつじこく) identifies a departure time, while 到着時刻 (とうちゃくじこく) identifies an arrival time.

## How the kanji combine

[時](../kanji/時.md) means time, and [刻](../kanji/刻.md) contributes a division or point of time here. Their related meanings reinforce each other in ジ + コク. The compound does not mean the action of engraving something, another meaning associated with 刻.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時間](時間.md) (じかん) can describe duration: 二時間かかる (にじかんかかる), "take two hours." 時刻 instead helps specify the clock point, such as three o'clock. 時間 also has broader scheduling uses, so this is a useful focus distinction rather than an absolute division.

## Distinctive meaning

Asking for a train's departure 時刻 calls for when it leaves, not how long the journey lasts. [確認](確認.md) (かくにん), "checking/confirmation," pairs naturally with this noun when verifying a schedule. A stated time is not itself a guarantee that the event will occur punctually.
