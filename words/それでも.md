# それでも (それでも) - nevertheless; even so

## Meaning

それでも is a conjunction meaning "nevertheless; even so; and yet." It accepts the preceding situation, then introduces something that still holds despite it.

- 雨が降っています。それでも出かけます。 (あめがふっています。それでもでかけます。) - It is raining. Even so, I am going out.
- 何度も説明しました。それでも分かってもらえませんでした。 (なんどもせつめいしました。それでもわかってもらえませんでした。) - I explained many times. Nevertheless, I could not get them to understand.

The second statement does not deny that the first happened. It presents an unexpected or persistent outcome in light of it.

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana expression, with no written kanji to divide. As a useful structural guide, それ points back to the preceding situation, and でも adds "even so." The dictionary marks kana spelling as usual.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しかし](しかし.md), "however; but," introduces contrast more generally. それでも specifically highlights what remains true despite the preceding point. A result connector such as だから, "therefore," establishes a different relationship, not a simple antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "still" can describe persistence over time or concession. それでも expresses the concessive "still" in "It is difficult, but I will still try." It is not a general replacement for まだ in "I am still waiting." Check whether the sentence means "despite that" before choosing it.
