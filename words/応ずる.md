# 応ずる (おうずる) - respond; comply; accept

## Meaning

応ずる means responding to a call or request, or accepting or
meeting a demand. The thing responded to takes に.
It is closely related to [応じる](応じる.md) (おうじる).

- 会社は客の要求に応ずる。
  (かいしゃはきゃくのようきゅうにおうずる。) - The company meets its customers' demands.
- 友達の誘いに応じました。
  (ともだちのさそいにおうじました。) - I accepted my friend's invitation.

Useful forms include 応じます, 応じて, 応じた and 応じない.
The second example illustrates the shared 応じ- forms rather than
changing the meaning to an unrelated verb.

## How the kanji combine

応 contributes responding or answering and is read おう here.
The verb ending is written in kana. This is a Sino-Japanese base
with a Japanese verb ending, not a multi-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

応じる has its own dictionary entry. JMdict labels 応ずる a
ずる alternative of a じる verb; Japanese monolingual dictionaries
also explain the relationship through the older サ変 conjugation
and the developed じる form. These are descriptions in different
grammatical frameworks, not a universal rule for every verb ending in る.

[答える](答える.md) (こたえる) and
[返事](返事.md)する (へんじする) overlap in answering.
[従う](従う.md) (したがう) concerns following or complying.

## Distinctive meaning

"Meet" here can concern meeting a demand, not meeting a friend
at the station. Accepting an invitation is likewise not the same as
merely receiving a parcel. Learn the complete に construction and
the actual inflected forms instead of mechanically attaching
ます to 応ずる as 応ずります.
