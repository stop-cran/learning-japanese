# 大工 (だいく) - carpenter

## Meaning

大工 is a carpenter, especially a craftsperson working on wooden buildings
or their wooden parts. It is a noun identifying an occupation.

- 父は大工です。 (ちちはだいくです。) - My father is a carpenter.
- 大工さんに家の修理を頼みました。 (だいくさんにいえのしゅうりをたのみました。) - I asked a carpenter to repair the house.

さん can be added when referring to the worker respectfully. The person
asked takes に in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes greatness or importance, and
[工](../kanji/工.md) contributes craft or construction. These associations
help recognize the established trade name; the modern word does not mean
"large factory" or guarantee the highest professional rank.
Read ダイ + ク: だいく, not だいこう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[職人](職人.md) (しょくにん) is a craftsperson more broadly.
[工員](工員.md) (こういん) is a factory worker; [技師](技師.md)
(ぎし) is an engineer or technical professional. These are not automatic
alternative names for every carpenter.

## Distinctive meaning

大工 does not name every person working at a construction site.
[工事](工事.md) (こうじ) names construction work; the occupation is the
person's role. Nor does the word alone certify that the person designed
the building as an architect. Use the actual trade and task rather than
translating every skilled building worker as 大工.
