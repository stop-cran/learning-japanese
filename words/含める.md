# 含める (ふくめる) - include; make someone understand

## Meaning

含める means including something in a group, total, or scope.
It also means explaining or instructing so that someone understands.
It is a transitive ichidan verb: 含めます, 含めて.

- この料金には朝食を含めています。 (このりょうきんにはちょうしょくをふくめています。) - We include breakfast in this price.
- 子供にかんで含めるように説明しました。 (こどもにかんでふくめるようにせつめいしました。) - I explained it to the child patiently and clearly.

The first uses XにYを含める, include Y in X.
The second uses the established expression かんで含めるように,
as if chewing something for someone, figuratively explaining
carefully so the listener can understand.
It is not an instruction to chew while talking.

## How the kanji combine

[含](../kanji/含.md) contributes containing or including.
The native reading is ふくめる, not the on reading ガン.
Both this verb and [含む](含む.md) (ふくむ) are transitive;
they are not an intransitive/transitive pair.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[含む](含む.md) often describes what something contains:
朝食を含む料金 (ちょうしょくをふくむりょうきん),
a price including breakfast.
[加える](加える.md) (くわえる) can mean adding or including.
[教える](教える.md) (おしえる) is a broader teaching or telling
verb; the instruction sense of 含める focuses on getting something
understood rather than naming every kind of classroom teaching.

## Distinctive meaning

Inclusion can involve a person, a fee, or an item under discussion;
it need not mean physically putting an object in a box.
Additional dictionary senses concern including a nuance, putting
something in someone's mouth, or imparting flavor.
Those need their particular objects and contexts; they do not make
every [入れる](入れる.md) (いれる), put in, interchangeable.
