# ことこと — gently simmering; light clinking

## Meaning

A gentle, repeated clink or rattle; in cooking, a quiet simmer with small, regular bubbling sounds.

- 豆を弱火（よわび）でことこと煮る。 — Simmer the beans gently over low heat.
- 鍋（なべ）のふたがことことと鳴っている。 — The pot lid is making a light rattling sound.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic repeating こと. It is not the noun こと repeated for grammatical emphasis and does not involve a kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぐつぐつ](ぐつぐつ.md) also describes simmering, often with more conspicuous bubbling. [じゅうじゅう](じゅうじゅう.md) is sizzling, not simmering. [からから](からから.md) can describe a drier, hollow rattle.

## Distinctive meaning

ことこと煮る and ことことと音がする are useful patterns. The word can describe a light knock or clink away from the stove, so food is not its only domain. The contrast with ぐつぐつ is a sensory tendency, not a precise thermometer reading or a rule that the lid must move.
