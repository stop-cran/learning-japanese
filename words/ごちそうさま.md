# ごちそうさま - thank you for the meal (after eating)

## Meaning

ごちそうさま expresses thanks for a meal one has received.
ごちそうさまでした is a polite, complete after-meal formula.
Plain ごちそうさま is useful in familiar interaction.

- ごちそうさまでした。とてもおいしかったです。
  (ごちそうさまでした。とてもおいしかったです。) - Thank you for the meal. It was very good.
- 食べ終わった子供が「ごちそうさま」と言いました。
  (たべおわったこどもが「ごちそうさま」といいました。) - The child finished eating and said, "Thank you for the meal."

The expression acknowledges the meal; it does not require a lavish
feast or a restaurant bill paid by someone else.

## How the kanji combine

The base [ごちそう](ごちそう.md), a meal as a treat or treating
someone, takes the respectful ending [様](../kanji/様.md) (さま).
Written forms include ご馳走様, ご馳走さま, and 御馳走様.
The kango category follows the Sino-Japanese base ちそう;
the expression is usually written in kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いただきます](いただきます.md) is the corresponding formula
before beginning a meal, not another way to say that one has finished.
[ありがとう](ありがとう.md) expresses thanks generally.
[感謝](感謝.md) (かんしゃ), [お礼](御礼.md) (おれい), and
[礼](礼.md) (れい) concern gratitude, with different constructions.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss "feast" fits the noun ごちそう, not the function
of ごちそうさま as a greeting. Learn who says the expression and when.
A separate joking use responds to someone's display of romantic
affection or boasting about a partner; that is not the mealtime
sense taught here.
