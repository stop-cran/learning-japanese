# 困る (こまる) — be in difficulty

## Meaning

困る means to be troubled, be at a loss, or have difficulty dealing with a situation.
It is intransitive: the person experiencing the difficulty is the subject.

- 財布をなくして困っています。 (さいふをなくしてこまっています。) — I have lost my wallet and am in a difficult situation.
- 道が分からなくて困りました。 (みちがわからなくてこまりました。) — I did not know the way and was at a loss.

The polite form is 困ります (こまります), and the て-form is 困って (こまって).
In the first example, 財布を belongs to なくす, "lose," not to 困る.

## How the kanji combine

[困](../kanji/困.md) supplies the difficulty meaning and is read こま in 困る; る is written in kana.
This is a native Japanese verb. The on reading コン appears in 困難 (こんなん), "difficulty; hardship."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[難しい](難しい.md) (むずかしい) describes something difficult: a problem, task, or situation.
困る describes someone being affected by difficulty.
困らせる (こまらせる) means to put someone in difficulty or inconvenience them; it reverses the perspective, rather than serving as a synonym.
There is no single opposite for every sort of predicament.

## Distinctive meaning

困る can be useful even when the speaker cannot name a specific emotion.
Being unable to find a route or recover a wallet can leave someone 困っている without necessarily making them angry or ashamed.
Translate the practical situation, not just one English emotion such as "embarrassed."
