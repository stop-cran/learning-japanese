# とろとろ — soft and melted; thick

## Meaning

とろとろ can describe a solid melting until soft, or a liquid becoming somewhat thick and viscous.

- チーズがとろとろに溶けました。
  (ちーずがとろとろにとけました。) — The cheese melted until soft and runny.
- 弱火（よわび）でスープをとろとろと煮込みました（にこみました）。
  — I simmered the soup gently over low heat.

In the first sentence, **とろとろに** describes the resulting texture. In the second,
**とろとろと** describes gentle cooking; it does not by itself say that the soup became thick.
The form without と can also modify the simmering verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[どろどろ](どろどろ.md) can describe a thick, sludgy consistency or mud; the words can overlap
for thick liquids, so context matters. [ねばねば](ねばねば.md) emphasizes sticky viscosity.
[ことこと](ことこと.md) can describe a gentle simmer, but does not itself mean that food melted.

## Distinctive meaning

とろとろ can also describe dozing or moving sluggishly. Compare [うとうと](うとうと.md),
which focuses on a light doze. The context determines which use is intended: a slow-moving
vehicle is not necessarily melted, and dozing does not necessarily involve cooking.
