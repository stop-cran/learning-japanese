# ずけずけ — speaking bluntly without reserve

## Meaning

Speaking frankly and uninhibitedly, often with too little regard for the listener's feelings.

- 彼は人の欠点（けってん）をずけずけ言う。 — He bluntly points out people's faults.
- 初対面（しょたいめん）なのに、ずけずけと私生活（しせいかつ）を聞かれた。 — Although we had just met, I was asked blunt questions about my private life.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[きっぱり](きっぱり.md) can overlap in direct speech but often emphasizes decisiveness rather than tactlessness. [はきはき](はきはき.md) concerns clear, brisk delivery, not necessarily blunt content.

## Distinctive meaning

ずけずけ言う（いう）often sounds critical of the speaker's manner. It is not a reliable compliment meaning merely “honest,” and it says nothing by itself about whether the statement is true.
