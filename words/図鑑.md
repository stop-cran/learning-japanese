# 図鑑 (ずかん) - illustrated reference book

## Meaning

図鑑 is an illustrated reference or identification book. It uses pictures, photographs or diagrams with explanations to help readers recognize and learn about its subjects.

- 図鑑で鳥の名前を調べました。 (ずかんでとりのなまえをしらべました。) - I looked up the bird's name in an illustrated reference book.
- 子供は昆虫の図鑑を読んでいます。 (こどもはこんちゅうのずかんをよんでいます。) - The child is reading an insect reference book.

## How the kanji combine

[図](../kanji/図.md) contributes pictures or diagrams, and 鑑 contributes a reference or model to consult. Read ズ + カン. The whole compound names the reference work, not merely any object with a picture.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本](本.md) is a book more generally. 絵本 (えほん), picture book, often presents an illustrated story; a 図鑑 is organized to provide information or identify subjects. Pictures alone do not establish which word is appropriate.

## Distinctive meaning

An English gloss "picture book" can hide the reference function. A bird guide or illustrated insect encyclopedia is a straightforward 図鑑; not every illustrated children's story is one. A 図鑑 need not be only for children or only about animals.
