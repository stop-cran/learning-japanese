# 川上 (かわかみ) — upstream

## Meaning

川上 is the upper part of a river, toward the source: 川上へ向かう (head upstream), 川上の村 (a village upstream). It is also a surname and, in business
language, the upstream stage of a supply chain (川上から川下まで, "from upstream to downstream").

## How the kanji combine

[川](../kanji/川.md) (river) plus [上](../kanji/上.md) (upper): "the upper part of the river". Here 上 reads かみ, an old noun reading for "upper part". Its opposite 川下 かわしも ("downstream") uses しも for 下.
The かみ／しも pair also appears in 上座 かみざ ("upper seat") and 下座 しもざ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: 川下 かわしも (downstream).
- Close: 上流 じょうりゅう (the upper reaches, more technical), 源流 げんりゅう (the source).
- Contrast: 下流 かりゅう (the lower reaches).

## Distinctive meaning

Be careful: 川の上 かわのうえ is "above the river" (over the surface), e.g. 川の上に橋がある. 川上 is a place along the river toward the source.
