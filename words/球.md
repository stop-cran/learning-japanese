# 球 (きゅう) - sphere; ball

## Meaning

With the reading **きゅう**, 球 is a noun for a sphere or ball, including in mathematics. It also serves as a counter for pitches in baseball. The reading **たま** belongs to another dictionary entry and can mean a ball, including a sports ball or pitch.

- 地球はほぼ球の形をしています。 (ちきゅうはほぼきゅうのかたちをしています。) - The Earth is approximately spherical.
- 速い球を投げました。 (はやいたまをなげました。) - I threw a fast ball.

For the counter, 十球 (じゅっきゅう) means "ten pitches." This uses the counter reading きゅう, rather than the noun reading たま shown above.

## How the kanji combine

[球](../kanji/球.md) names the rounded shape. The on reading is きゅう; the native reading taught alongside it is たま. This single spelling therefore supports more than one lexical reading, not one freely interchangeable pronunciation in every construction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ボール](ボール.md) overlaps with the sports-ball use. [玉](玉.md) (たま) shares a dictionary entry with 球/たま, but the entry records spelling preferences. It does not make every sense equally suitable for every spelling.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 球 for the ball or sphere focus. In the shared たま entry, bullets favor 弾, while gems favor 玉 or 珠. The coin use is a suffix usually pronounced だま, as in 百円玉 (ひゃくえんだま), not a general standalone meaning "coin" for 球/たま. Those contrasts do not change the ball meaning illustrated here.
