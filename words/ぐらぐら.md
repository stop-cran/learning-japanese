# ぐらぐら — wobbling; unstable

## Meaning

Unsteady shaking, especially when something lacks a firm support or attachment.

- この椅子（いす）は脚（あし）がぐらぐらする。 — This chair's leg wobbles.
- 抜（ぬ）けそうな歯（は）がぐらぐらしている。 — The tooth that is about to come out is loose and wobbly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ゆらゆら](ゆらゆら.md) also describes swaying, often without the same unstable impression. [がたがた](がたがた.md) overlaps in rattly or shaky objects; [ふらふら](ふらふら.md) often concerns a person's balance.

## Distinctive meaning

ぐらぐらする can also describe wavering judgment. In a cooking context, 湯（ゆ）がぐらぐら煮立つ（にたつ）means water boiling vigorously, not an unstable cooking pot.
