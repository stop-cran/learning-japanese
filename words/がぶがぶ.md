# がぶがぶ — guzzling a drink

## Meaning

Drinking in large, repeated mouthfuls. It can suggest an excessive amount, but does not inherently say that the drinker is doing something wrong.

- のどが渇（かわ）いて、水をがぶがぶ飲んだ。 — I was thirsty and guzzled water.
- 彼は大きなコップでお茶をがぶがぶ飲んでいる。 — He is gulping down tea from a large glass.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ごくごく](ごくごく.md) and [ぐびぐび](ぐびぐび.md) also describe gulping; the borders are not fixed measurements of mouthful size. [ちびちび](ちびちび.md) describes taking little amounts.

## Distinctive meaning

Use がぶがぶ飲む（のむ）. The word can also describe liquid sloshing in a full stomach, a related sensation rather than another drinking technique. Do not generalize it to every act of eating or swallowing.
