# 少しも (すこしも) - not at all; not a bit

## Meaning

少しも combines with a negative predicate to mean "not at all" or "not even a
little." The negation is part of the construction, not an optional detail.

- この映画は少しも怖くありません。 (このえいがはすこしもこわくありません。) - This film is not frightening at all.
- 何度説明しても、少しも分かってくれません。 (なんどせつめいしても、すこしもわかってくれません。) - No matter how often I explain, they do not understand me at all.

The first sentence negates an i-adjective with 怖くありません. The second
negates a verb phrase with 分かってくれません.

## How the kanji combine

[少](../kanji/少.md) contributes a small amount in 少し (すこし).
Adding も gives the "even a little" emphasis that the negative predicate
denies. This native expression is not a Sino-Japanese compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[少し](少し.md) alone means "a little": 少し怖い means "a little frightening."
[全然](全然.md) (ぜんぜん) with a negative predicate can similarly mean
"not at all." Keep the full construction in view when comparing them.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 少し分かります, "I understand a little," with 少しも分かりません,
"I do not understand at all." Adding も while dropping the negative ending
does not preserve this meaning. 少しも can emphasize zero change as well:
少しも変わっていない means "has not changed a bit."
