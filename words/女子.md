# 女子 (じょし) - girls; women

## Meaning

女子 is a noun referring to girls or women. It is useful in school,
sports, and group labels: 女子学生 (じょしがくせい) means a
female student, and 女子チーム means a girls' or women's team.
The context, not the word alone, establishes age.

- このクラスは女子が多いです。 (このクラスはじょしがおおいです。) - This class has many girls.
- 女子チームが決勝に進みました。 (じょしチームがけっしょうにすすみました。) - The women's team advanced to the final.

女子 can appear on its own or directly before another noun in a
compound. In the sports example, it identifies the team's category.

## How the kanji combine

[女](../kanji/女.md) contributes female or woman, and
[子](../kanji/子.md) contributes child or person in this established
compound. The on readings are じょ + し. The presence of 子
does not restrict the modern word to young children.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[女性](女性.md) (じょせい) is a broader word for a woman or
women. [女の子](女の子.md) (おんなのこ) directly says "girl."
[男子](男子.md) (だんし) is the corresponding male category,
for example in school or sports labels.

## Distinctive meaning

A category label and a way of addressing an individual are different
uses. 女子 is not a general-purpose equivalent of polite English
"ma'am." Choose person references with the actual relationship and
setting in mind rather than translating labels mechanically.
