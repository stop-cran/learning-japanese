# 全体 (ぜんたい) - whole; entirety

## Meaning

全体 is the whole or entirety of something. **N全体** means "the whole of N," 全体の modifies a following noun, and 全体として means "as a whole."

- 建物全体が揺れました。 (たてものぜんたいがゆれました。) - The whole building shook.
- 全体として、計画は順調です。 (ぜんたいとして、けいかくはじゅんちょうです。) - Overall, the plan is progressing well.

The second example evaluates the overall situation; it does not assert that every detail is perfect.

## How the kanji combine

[全](../kanji/全.md) contributes all or complete, and [体](../kanji/体.md) contributes a body or organized whole. Together they express the complete entity. The readings are ゼン + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ) also means all or the whole amount. 全体 is useful when viewing something as an integrated whole. 一部 (いちぶ), "a part," is the contrasting scope. [全然](全然.md) (ぜんぜん) is a different adverb, not another reading of 全体.

## Distinctive meaning

A separate emphatic-question use appears in 一体全体どうしたの？ (いったいぜんたいどうしたの？), "What on earth happened?" Here 全体 helps intensify the question rather than meaning an entire object. Do not translate that use as free-choice English "whatever"; its force comes from a question about what happened.
