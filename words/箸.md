# 箸 (はし) — chopsticks

## Meaning

箸 means chopsticks. お箸 (おはし) is a common form with the polite prefix お. Use で to mark them as the tool for eating.

箸でご飯を食べます。 (はしでごはんをたべます。) — I eat rice with chopsticks.

お箸をください。 (おはしをください。) — Please give me some chopsticks.

## How the kanji combine

箸 alone writes the native word はし. There is no second component or okurigana. The kana spelling はし also occurs, with context distinguishing this noun from other words pronounced the same way.

## Synonyms and antonyms

割り箸 (わりばし) are disposable chopsticks that are split apart before use. スプーン and フォーク are other eating utensils, not antonyms. 橋 (はし), "bridge," is an unrelated word despite sharing the kana reading.

## Distinctive meaning

A pair can be counted with 膳 (ぜん): 一膳 (いちぜん), "one pair of chopsticks." Japanese 箸 does not need a plural ending. When asking for another pair at a table, the polite お箸 is a practical expression to remember.
