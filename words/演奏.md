# 演奏 (えんそう) - musical performance

## Meaning

演奏 names a musical performance, especially playing instruments. With する it becomes a transitive verb: 曲を演奏する (きょくをえんそうする), "perform a piece." The instrument can be introduced with で, while the piece takes を.

- ピアノでこの曲を演奏します。 (ピアノでこのきょくをえんそうします。) - I will perform this piece on the piano.
- 生の演奏を聞きました。 (なまのえんそうをききました。) - I listened to a live performance.

演奏が始まる (えんそうがはじまる), "the performance begins," treats it as a noun rather than as the act of doing something.

## How the kanji combine

[演](../kanji/演.md) contributes performing or presenting, and 奏 contributes playing music. The second character narrows the performance to music; 演奏 is not a general label for every kind of stage performance.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[弾く](弾く.md) (ひく) names playing instruments such as a piano or guitar. 演奏する can describe the musical performance without choosing an instrument-specific verb. [歌う](歌う.md) (うたう) directly names singing. [音楽](音楽.md) is music itself, not necessarily a performance.

## Distinctive meaning

演奏 works for a soloist, an ensemble, a concert, or a recording. It does not by itself guarantee a public event or a professional performer. To ask specifically whether something is live, look for 生の演奏 rather than inferring that from 演奏 alone.
