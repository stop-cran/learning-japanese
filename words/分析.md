# 分析 (ぶんせき) - analysis

## Meaning

分析 is analysis: examining something's elements, structure, or relationships to understand it. **Nを分析する** means to analyze N. 分析の結果 (ぶんせきのけっか) means "the results of the analysis."

- 集めたデータを分析します。 (あつめたデータをぶんせきします。) - We will analyze the collected data.
- 失敗の原因を分析しました。 (しっぱいのげんいんをぶんせきしました。) - We analyzed the causes of the failure.

The noun can occur in 分析が必要です, "analysis is necessary," without identifying an analyst.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) contributes dividing or distinguishing, and 析 contributes splitting or analyzing. Together they suggest distinguishing the parts or features of a whole to understand it. The readings are ブン + セキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[調査](調査.md) (ちょうさ) concerns investigation or gathering information; 分析 concerns examining what that information shows. [研究](研究.md) (けんきゅう) is research more broadly. A research project may include both investigation and analysis.

## Distinctive meaning

分析 is not restricted to chemistry or mathematics: it can concern behavior, language, or a business problem. Nor must the thing analyzed be physically taken apart. Collecting numbers is not by itself analyzing them, and an analysis can still be mistaken; the word identifies an activity, not a guarantee of a correct conclusion.
