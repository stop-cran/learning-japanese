# 滑る (すべる) — to slide; to slip

## Meaning

滑る means sliding or gliding, deliberately or accidentally. It is a godan verb despite ending in える: 滑ります, 滑らない, 滑って.

- 氷の上で滑りました。 (こおりのうえですべりました。) — I slipped on the ice.
- スキーで斜面を滑ります。 (スキーでしゃめんをすべります。) — I ski down the slope.

## How the kanji combine

滑 supplies すべ, and る is okurigana. The native verb is not an ichidan verb: removing る and adding ます would produce the wrong form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

転ぶ (ころぶ) means falling over. You can 滑る without actually falling. 止まる (とまる), "stop," contrasts with continuing to slide, but is not an opposite for every figurative use.

## Distinctive meaning

In the slope example, を marks the route traveled, not an object being made slippery. Context distinguishes enjoying a controlled glide from losing your footing. The warning 滑りやすい (すべりやすい) means "slippery; easy to slip on."
