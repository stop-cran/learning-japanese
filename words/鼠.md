# 鼠 (ねずみ) - mouse; rat

## Meaning

鼠 is a noun covering a mouse or rat. The word alone does not make the same size/species distinction as the two English nouns.
JMdict marks the animal sense as usually written in kana, and both ねずみ and ネズミ are useful spellings to recognize.

- 台所に小さなねずみがいました。 (だいどころにちいさなねずみがいました。) - There was a small mouse in the kitchen.
- ネズミが壁の穴から出てきました。 (ネズミがかべのあなからでてきました。) - A mouse/rat came out of a hole in the wall.

Use が for the animal that appears and から for the place it comes out of.

## How the kanji combine

鼠 represents the whole native noun ねずみ.
There is no compound to split into separate component words.
The assigned kanji spelling and the kana spellings name the same animal category in the examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," is the broader category.
ねずみ色 (ねずみいろ) names a gray color.
The color sense of 鼠 is an abbreviation of that color word, not an instruction to translate every animal mention as "gray."

## Distinctive meaning

In a color list, ねずみ can mean dark or slate gray. For an explicit full expression:
ねずみ色の上着 (ねずみいろのうわぎ), "a gray jacket."
Animal movement, food, or nests point to the animal sense; fabric and color choices can point to the abbreviated color sense.
The surrounding context decides.
