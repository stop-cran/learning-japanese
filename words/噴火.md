# 噴火 (ふんか) - volcanic eruption

## Meaning

噴火 is a volcanic eruption, in which a volcano emits material such
as ash, gas, or lava. 噴火する is intransitive: the volcano takes が.

- 火山が噴火しました。 (かざんがふんかしました。) - The volcano erupted.
- 噴火の後、村に灰が降りました。 (ふんかのあと、むらにはいがふりました。) - Ash fell on the village after the eruption.

The noun works in 噴火が起こる (ふんかがおこる), "an eruption occurs."

## How the kanji combine

噴 contributes spouting or emitting, and [火](../kanji/火.md) fire.
The on readings are フン + カ. Their associations help recognize
the volcanic event, but do not require every eruption to show visible flames.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[火山](火山.md) (かざん) is the volcano, not the eruption.
[爆発](爆発.md) (ばくはつ) is an explosion or outburst more broadly.
An explosive eruption can involve both, but not every explosion is volcanic.
[火事](火事.md) (かじ) names a destructive fire, not a volcanic eruption.

## Distinctive meaning

噴火 identifies the kind of event, not its size, duration, or exact effects.
The spelling's 火 is not a reason to use it for every fire.
Likewise, figurative anger "erupting" is not the basic volcanic use
taught on this page.
