# 花瓶 (かびん) - flower vase

## Meaning

花瓶 is a vase for displaying flowers. It is an ordinary noun for the container, whether it is made of glass, ceramic, or another material.

- 花瓶に花を入れました。 (かびんにはなをいれました。) - I put flowers in a vase.
- この花瓶は母の物です。 (このかびんはははのものです。) - This vase belongs to my mother.

With 入れる, the flowers take を and the vase, as their destination, takes に.

## How the kanji combine

[花](../kanji/花.md) means flower and 瓶 means bottle or a similar container. Both use on readings here: か + びん. Do not substitute the independent word 花's reading はな in the usual reading of this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

瓶 (びん) is a bottle or jar, without specifically identifying its use for flowers. 植木鉢 (うえきばち) is a plant pot, usually containing soil and a growing plant.

## Distinctive meaning

A 花瓶 displays cut flowers; it is not the usual word for the pot in which a living plant grows. The compound names the intended function, not a particular size or shape.
