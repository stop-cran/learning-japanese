# 侵入 (しんにゅう) - intrusion; trespass

## Meaning

侵入 is intrusion, trespass, or invasion into another domain.
It presents the entry as encroaching or unwelcome, not merely moving inside.
The する verb is intransitive: the intruder is the subject and the
place entered can take に.

- 泥棒が家に侵入しました。 (どろぼうがいえにしんにゅうしました。) - A thief broke into the house.
- 建物への侵入を防ぎます。 (たてものへのしんにゅうをふせぎます。) - We prevent intrusion into the building.

In the second sentence, を marks the noun 侵入 as what is prevented;
it does not make the verb 侵入する transitive.

## How the kanji combine

侵 contributes invading or encroaching, and [入](../kanji/入.md)
contributes entering. The first qualifies the manner or character of entry.
The on readings are シン + ニュウ, including the long vowel in にゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入る](入る.md) (はいる) is broader "enter" and does not itself imply
an intrusion. The homophone 進入 (しんにゅう) describes going or moving
into a place; 侵入 adds the encroachment idea.
The shared reading does not make their spellings freely interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

家に侵入する is not simply a more elaborate way to say that a guest
enters a house. Likewise, the source's "invasion" gloss does not limit
the word to warfare: unauthorized entry into a building is a normal
intrusion context. Judge the complete situation, not just 入.
