# とこや (とこや) - barber; barbershop

## Meaning

とこや means a barber or a barbershop. With a movement verb, it commonly names the place.

- 昨日、とこやで髪を切ってもらいました。 (きのう、とこやでかみをきってもらいました。) - I had my hair cut at the barber's yesterday.

で marks where the haircut happened. 切ってもらう (きってもらう) says that someone else did the cutting for the speaker, rather than that the speaker cut their own hair.

## How the kanji combine

The common kanji spelling is 床屋 (とこや). 屋, read や, appears in names of shops and occupations. The whole word must be learned as "barber; barbershop," not translated literally as a floor shop.

## Synonyms and antonyms

理髪店 (りはつてん) is another word for a barbershop. 美容院 (びよういん) is a beauty or hair salon, a related service rather than an exact equivalent.

## Distinctive meaning

Context decides whether とこや refers to the worker or the establishment. とこやに行く (とこやにいく) normally means going to the barber's, not approaching a particular person.
