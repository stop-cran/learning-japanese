# 現す (あらわす) - reveal; show

## Meaning

現す is a transitive godan verb meaning to reveal or show something that becomes
visible or apparent. Mark what is shown with を. Its polite form is 現します
(あらわします), and its て-form is 現して (あらわして).

- 雲の間から月が姿を現しました。 (くものあいだからつきがすがたをあらわしました。) - The moon emerged from between the clouds.
- 彼女は大会で実力を現した。 (かのじょはたいかいでじつりょくをあらわした。) - She showed her ability in the competition.

姿を現す literally presents someone's or something's appearance; naturally
translated, it can mean "appear" or "show up."

## How the kanji combine

[現](../kanji/現.md) contributes becoming apparent or visible. あらわす is the
native reading of the whole verb; す changes when the verb conjugates.
There is no two-character compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[現れる](現れる.md) (あらわれる) is intransitive: 月が現れる, "the moon appears."
[見せる](見せる.md) (みせる) emphasizes showing something to someone.
Neither has exactly the same construction as 姿を現す.

## Distinctive meaning

[表す](表す.md), also あらわす, is associated especially with representing or
expressing: 気持ちを表す (きもちをあらわす), "express one's feelings."

meanings. They are not unrestricted spelling substitutes, nor are those
preferences absolute sense restrictions.
