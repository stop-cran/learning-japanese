# 話しかける (はなしかける) - address someone; begin talking

## Meaning

話しかける means to address someone or initiate talking to them.
It is an ichidan verb: 話しかけた, 話しかけて, 話しかけない.
The person addressed normally takes に.

- 知らない人に話しかけられました。 (しらないひとにはなしかけられました。) - A stranger started talking to me.
- 先生に話しかけてみました。 (せんせいにはなしかけてみました。) - I tried speaking to the teacher.

It can also emphasize beginning to talk. Addressing someone does not guarantee that they answer.
The word is not necessarily an aggressive approach, despite the English dictionary gloss "accost."

## How the kanji combine

[話](../kanji/話.md) appears in 話す (はなす). Its stem 話し combines with かける.
The alternate spelling 話し掛ける uses [掛](../kanji/掛.md), but the whole reading remains はなしかける.
Here かける contributes directing or beginning the speaking action, not literally hanging speech on a hook.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話す](話す.md) (はなす) is broader speaking or talking; it need not single out the act of initiating contact.
[呼ぶ](呼ぶ.md) (よぶ) can mean call, summon, name or invite.
Some calling and addressing situations overlap, but the verbs are not unrestricted substitutes.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 人に話しかける as a complete pattern.
The dictionary's transitive classification does not mean that the addressee must be marked with を.
A continuing mutual discussion is better expressed by [話し合う](話し合う.md) when that is the intended action.
