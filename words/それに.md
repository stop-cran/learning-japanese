# それに (それに) — moreover; besides

## Meaning

それに means **in addition, besides, or moreover**. It adds another relevant point to the preceding statement, often another reason for the same conclusion.

この店は安いです。それに、駅から近いです。 (このみせはやすいです。それに、えきからちかいです。) — This shop is inexpensive. Besides, it is close to the station.

The connective can begin a sentence. It does not need です or another ending of its own; the following clause supplies the statement.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is kana. It contains それ, “that,” and に, giving a useful association with adding something “to that.” Treat the connective as an established expression, not as a literal translation in every context.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そして also connects statements and can express sequence. それに specifically emphasizes an additional point. しかし, “however,” introduces a contrast instead.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use それに as an automatic translation of every English “and.” It does not ordinarily join two nouns into a simple list. Also distinguish the connective from an ordinary pronoun plus particle, as in それに書く (それにかく), “write on that.”
