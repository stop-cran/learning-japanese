# 第一 (だいいち) - first; foremost; above all

## Meaning

第一 identifies the first item, the foremost concern, or the best in a
ranking. It also works adverbially to introduce the main point or an
additional decisive reason.

- 第一の理由は費用です。 (だいいちのりゆうはひようです。) - The first reason is the cost.
- 安全が第一です。 (あんぜんがだいいちです。) - Safety comes first.
- 第一に、名前を書いてください。 (だいいちに、なまえをかいてください。) - First, please write your name.
- 今は出かけたくありません。第一、雨が降っています。 (いまはでかけたくありません。だいいち、あめがふっています。) - I don't want to go out now. Besides, it is raining.

Use の to modify an ordinary following noun, as in 第一の理由. Learn
established compounds separately rather than guessing them from English word order.

## How the kanji combine

第 marks an ordinal position, and [一](../kanji/一.md) contributes one:
"number one." The established readings are ダイ + イチ, giving だいいち,
not だいち. 第１ is also recorded with the same reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[最初](最初.md) (さいしょ) focuses on the beginning or first in sequence.
[いちばん](いちばん.md) can mark the top position or degree.
[まず](まず.md) introduces an initial step.
[重要](重要.md) (じゅうよう), "important," need not mean the single highest
priority; 第一 can rank that priority.

## Distinctive meaning

第一 is not limited to a numbered list: 第一に考える (だいいちにかんがえる)
can mean "consider above all." In the rain example it introduces a reason,
not the literal first raindrop or a contrast meaning "however."
Read the whole construction to distinguish order, priority, and discourse use.
