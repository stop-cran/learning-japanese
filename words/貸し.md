# 貸し (かし) - lending; a favor owed

## Meaning

貸し is a noun for lending or a loan viewed from the lender's side.
It also names a favor for which someone owes you something in return.
The useful pattern 人に貸しがある means having a claim on someone
because of an earlier loan or favor.

- 彼には一万円の貸しがあります。 (かれにはいちまんえんのかしがあります。) - He owes me ten thousand yen.
- これで貸しが一つできたね。 (これでかしがひとつできたね。) - Now you owe me one.

The second sentence can be playful, but it can also sound pointed.
It frames the help as something to be repaid rather than an unconditional gift.

## How the kanji combine

[貸](../kanji/貸.md) expresses lending. 貸し has the same form as the
stem of [貸す](貸す.md) (かす), and functions here as a noun.
The written し belongs to that native form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

借り (かり) names the opposite side: something borrowed or a debt owed.
[借りる](借りる.md) (かりる) is the verb "borrow."
貸し and 借り can describe the same transaction from different people's
perspectives; they are not interchangeable labels.

## Distinctive meaning

Check whose perspective the sentence adopts. 私には彼への貸しがある
means that he owes me, not that I owe him. The social-favor use preserves
this direction even when no money changes hands.
