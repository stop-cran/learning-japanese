# 苗 (なえ) — seedling; young plant

## Meaning

苗 is a **seedling or young plant**, often one being raised for planting out. It can name rice seedlings, vegetable seedlings or other young plants; it is not the seed itself.

- トマトの苗を買いました。（トマトのなえをかいました。）— I bought tomato seedlings.
- 苗を畑に植えました。（なえをはたけにうえました。）— I planted the seedlings in the field.

の identifies the kind in トマトの苗. In the second sentence, を marks what is planted and に the destination.

## How the kanji combine

[苗](../kanji/苗.md) writes the whole native noun なえ. There are no separately pronounced word components to combine.

The same character has ビョウ in [育苗](育苗.md) (いくびょう), raising seedlings, and the bound form なわ- in [苗代](苗代.md) (なわしろ), a rice-seedling bed. Those are not replacements for なえ in the independent noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[芽](芽.md) (め) names a sprout, shoot or bud, including a bud on an established tree. 苗 presents a young plant as a whole; the words can describe related stages without being freely interchangeable.

苗木 (なえぎ) specifies a sapling or young tree. [種](種.md) (たね) is a seed, and [植物](植物.md) (しょくぶつ) is the broader category plant or vegetation. These form useful comparisons, not a set of strict antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

苗 focuses on the plant's young growing stage, especially in cultivation. It does not mean any small leaf or every shoot on a mature plant, and it is not restricted to plants grown from seed: cultivated cuttings can also be called 苗.
