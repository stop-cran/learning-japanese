# 懐かしい (なつかしい) - fondly remembered; nostalgic

## Meaning

懐かしい describes someone or something remembered with affection:
dear, missed or nostalgic. It is an i-adjective. A familiar song, place
or person can bring back the feeling.

- この歌を聞くと、学生時代が懐かしいです。 (このうたをきくと、がくせいじだいがなつかしいです。) - Hearing this song makes me nostalgic for my student days.
- 懐かしい友達から手紙が来ました。 (なつかしいともだちからてがみがきました。) - A letter came from an old friend I remember fondly.

Use 懐かしい directly before a noun, without な.
Forms include 懐かしかった, "felt nostalgic," and 懐かしくなる,
"come to feel nostalgic."

## How the kanji combine

懐 has bosom, heart and feelings associations. In this native adjective,
it writes なつ, with かしい in kana. Those associations help recall the
spelling; they are not a historical derivation of nostalgia from a body part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[恋しい](恋しい.md) (こいしい) also describes someone or something missed,
but can emphasize longing rather than fond recollection.
[思い出](思い出.md) (おもいで) is a memory itself;
[思い出す](思い出す.md) (おもいだす) is recalling something.
Remembering alone does not necessarily produce 懐かしい feelings.

## Distinctive meaning

The feeling can be warm and wistful together; it does not simply mean sad
or lonely. English "dear" here does not mean expensive, and "desired" in
a vocabulary list should not erase the affectionate connection with
what is remembered. Seeing something again can evoke the feeling even
when one was not actively wishing to return.
