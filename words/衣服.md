# 衣服 (いふく) - clothing

## Meaning

衣服 is a noun for clothes or clothing. It can refer collectively to the things
people wear, without specifying one garment or a particular style. Useful
constructions include 衣服を着る (いふくをきる), "put on clothing," and
衣服を脱ぐ (いふくをぬぐ), "take off clothing."

- ぬれた衣服を着替えました。 (ぬれたいふくをきがえました。) - I changed out of my wet clothes.
- 季節に合った衣服を選びましょう。 (きせつにあったいふくをえらびましょう。) - Choose clothing suited to the season.

In the second example, 季節に合った describes the clothes before the noun.

## How the kanji combine

衣 concerns clothing, and [服](../kanji/服.md) also concerns clothes or dress.
The two related meanings reinforce the general category rather than separating
upper and lower garments. The on readings are イ + フク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[服](服.md) (ふく) is also "clothes" and is convenient in everyday questions about
what to wear. [洋服](洋服.md) (ようふく) specifies Western-style clothing.
[着物](着物.md) (きもの), in its kimono sense, names a particular type of clothing.
衣服 is not restricted to either style.

## Distinctive meaning

衣服 works well when discussing clothing as a category, for example in care
instructions or explanations about climate. It does not mean a costume specifically,
and its plural English translation does not require a Japanese plural ending.
The context determines whether one person's clothes or clothing generally is meant.
