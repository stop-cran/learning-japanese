# 悪口 (わるくち・わるぐち) - bad-mouthing; insulting remarks

## Meaning

悪口 is a noun for speaking ill of people, name-calling, or insulting remarks. Both **わるくち** and **わるぐち** are recorded readings with the same meaning. This page's examples demonstrate the assigned reading わるくち.

- 人の悪口を言わないでください。 (ひとのわるくちをいわないでください。) - Please do not speak ill of other people.
- 友達の悪口を聞いて、悲しくなりました。 (ともだちのわるくちをきいて、かなしくなりました。) - I felt sad when I heard insulting remarks about my friend.

人の悪口 identifies the person being spoken about; 悪口を言う is the useful phrase "speak ill of someone."

## How the kanji combine

[悪](../kanji/悪.md) contributes bad through the native element わる, and [口](../kanji/口.md) contributes mouth or speech. Together they describe harmful talk. The くち/ぐち variation does not create separate meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[文句](文句.md) (もんく) can mean a complaint or objection. A complaint about a broken product is not necessarily 悪口 about a person. [評判](評判.md) (ひょうばん) is reputation or circulating opinion, which can be favorable.

## Distinctive meaning

The translation "slander" can be too narrow if understood as a legal claim: 悪口 does not itself establish falsity or a legal offense. It evaluates the speech as insulting or unkind. Likewise, do not reject the reading わるくち just because you have encountered わるぐち elsewhere.
