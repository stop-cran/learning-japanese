# 渇く (かわく) - be thirsty; crave

## Meaning

渇く means to be or feel thirsty. A useful expression is 喉が渇く (のどがかわく), "be thirsty," literally describing the throat.

- 運動の後で喉が渇きました。 (うんどうのあとでのどがかわきました。) - I felt thirsty after exercising.
- 喉が渇いたので、水を飲みました。 (のどがかわいたので、みずをのみました。) - I drank water because I was thirsty.

It also has a figurative "thirst for; crave" use: 愛に渇いた人 (あいにかわいたひと), "a person starved for affection." The desired thing takes に in that construction.

## How the kanji combine

渇 supplies the thirst meaning; く completes the native godan verb. Its forms include 渇きます, 渇いて, and 渇かない. The same pronunciation does not make it identical to the ordinary drying verb [乾く](乾く.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[からから](からから.md) can describe a parched throat. [望む](望む.md) (のぞむ) and [欲しい](欲しい.md) (ほしい) overlap with wanting, but their particle patterns do not transfer unchanged to figurative Nに渇く.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 渇く as the clear teaching spelling for thirst, and 乾く for ordinary drying such as clothes becoming dry. This is not an absolute spelling ban: the dictionary also records 喉が乾く for "be thirsty." The figurative craving use is not a statement that the desired thing is wet or dry.
