# 争う (あらそう) - compete; dispute

## Meaning

争う means competing or contending with someone, or quarreling over something.
It is a godan verb: 争います (あらそいます), 争わない (あらそわない),
and 争って (あらそって).

- 二つのチームが優勝を争っています。 (ふたつのチームがゆうしょうをあらそっています。) - Two teams are competing for the championship.
- 兄弟が財産をめぐって争っています。 (きょうだいがざいさんをめぐってあらそっています。) - The brothers are disputing over property.

相手と争う (あいてとあらそう) identifies an opponent. JMdict labels the verb
intransitive, but を can mark the prize or position being contested, as in 優勝を争う.
Do not turn the label into a rule forbidding を in every construction.

## How the kanji combine

[争](../kanji/争.md) contributes contention or dispute. The native verb is あらそう,
not the on reading ソウ plus う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[競争](競争.md) (きょうそう) names competition; 競争する means to compete.
[戦う](戦う.md) (たたかう) overlaps in fighting or competing.
[喧嘩](喧嘩.md) (けんか) is a quarrel or fight. [協力](協力.md) (きょうりょく),
"cooperation," offers a contextual contrast, not an automatic opposite in every situation.

## Distinctive meaning

A contest described by 争う need not be violent. Conversely, disputing a claim is not
merely participating in a scheduled match. The competing goal, opponent and surrounding
construction establish the intended use.
