# テーブル - table

## Meaning

テーブル is a table, such as one used for meals, conversation, or meetings. It is a noun.

- テーブルの上に花があります。 (テーブルのうえにはながあります。) - There are flowers on the table.
- テーブルをきれいに拭きました。 (テーブルをきれいにふきました。) - I wiped the table clean.

テーブルの上 specifies the top of the table; に marks where something is located.

## How the kanji combine

This English-derived loanword is written in katakana. The ー lengthens テ, and the final sound is ル: テーブル. There are no written kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

机 (つくえ) commonly means a desk used for reading, writing, or work. 食卓 (しょくたく) specifically identifies a dining table.

## Distinctive meaning

The distinction between テーブル and 机 concerns typical use, not a strict rule about height or the presence of drawers. A low table can still be a テーブル. There is no direct antonym.
