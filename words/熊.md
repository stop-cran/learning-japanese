# 熊 (くま) — bear (animal)

## Meaning

熊 means a **bear**, the animal. クマ is another way to write the same noun in kana. The noun itself does not mark singular or plural.

- 山に熊がいます。 (やまにくまがいます。) — There is a bear in the mountains.
- 動物園で熊を見ました。 (どうぶつえんでくまをみました。) — I saw a bear at the zoo.

The first example uses に for where the animal exists; the second uses で for where the act of seeing takes place.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun: [熊](../kanji/熊.md) is read **くま** as a whole, with no okurigana. The character's on-reading ユウ does not replace くま in this noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," is a broader category, not a synonym specific to bears. 小熊 (こぐま), also written 子熊, means a small bear or bear cub. There is no useful antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

This is the English animal noun **bear**, not the verb in "bear a burden" or "bear fruit." A kana spelling such as クマ does not change that meaning or turn the word into an on-reading.

Compounds need their own meanings: 小熊 has ぐま instead of くま, while 熊手 (くまで) means a rake, not a kind of bear. The presence of 熊 alone does not prove that a compound names an animal.
