# トランプ (トランプ) - playing cards

## Meaning

トランプ is a noun for Western-style playing cards.
トランプをする means "play cards," while トランプで遊ぶ means "play with playing cards."
The word does not itself specify a particular game.

- 旅行中、友達とトランプをしました。 (りょこうちゅう、ともだちとトランプをしました。) - I played cards with a friend during the trip.
- トランプをよく切ってから配ってください。 (トランプをよくきってからくばってください。) - Shuffle the cards well before dealing them.

切る (きる) means "shuffle" in this card-playing expression, not physically cut the cards.
配る (くばる) means to distribute or deal them.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword with no kanji to decompose.
JMdict identifies English "trump" as its source, but the Japanese meaning covers playing cards
generally, not just a trump card or trump suit.
Keep the final プ; there is no long-vowel mark.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[カード](カード.md) (カード) is broader and can refer to many kinds of cards.
トランプ identifies the playing-card context more specifically.
Other games are alternatives rather than opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Translate the established Japanese meaning, not the narrow meaning suggested by the English source word.
トランプをする tells you the activity is card play; it does not reveal whether the group is playing
poker, another game, or for money.
Additional context supplies the rules and purpose of the game.
