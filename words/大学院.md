# 大学院 (だいがくいん) - graduate school

## Meaning

大学院 is a graduate school, where advanced study and research include
master's and doctoral programs. It names the educational institution or
organization, not the student.

- 大学を卒業して、大学院に進みました。 (だいがくをそつぎょうして、だいがくいんにすすみました。) - After graduating from university, I went on to graduate school.
- 姉は大学院で研究しています。 (あねはだいがくいんでけんきゅうしています。) - My older sister is doing research at graduate school.

大学院に進む (だいがくいんにすすむ) means to proceed to graduate school;
で marks where the research takes place in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

[大学](大学.md) (だいがく) supplies the university-level study association:
[大](../kanji/大.md), great or advanced, with [学](../kanji/学.md), study.
[院](../kanji/院.md) identifies an institution. The final element is the
head of the compound. Learn ダイ + ガク + イン without dropping the く.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大学](大学.md) is university more broadly; [大学生](大学生.md)
(だいがくせい) names a university student. 大学院生 (だいがくいんせい)
specifically names a graduate student. 修士課程 (しゅうしかてい) and
博士課程 (はくしかてい) identify master's and doctoral programs.

## Distinctive meaning

A 大学院 may be associated with a university or exist as an independent
graduate institution. The word does not require a particular building or
campus arrangement. Compare [博士](博士.md): its primary article reading
はかせ can refer to a learned expert, while はくし is the relevant reading
in the academic-degree and 博士課程 expressions here.
