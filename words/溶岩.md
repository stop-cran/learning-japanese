# 溶岩 (ようがん) - lava

## Meaning

溶岩 is lava: molten rock that has emerged onto the surface,
and also the rock formed when it cools and solidifies.
The word is not limited to a glowing liquid.

- 火口から溶岩が流れ出しました。 (かこうからようがんがながれだしました。) - Lava flowed out of the crater.
- 海岸には固まった溶岩があります。 (かいがんにはかたまったようがんがあります。) - There is solidified lava on the coast.

## How the kanji combine

[溶](../kanji/溶.md) contributes melting, and
[岩](../kanji/岩.md) rock: melted rock.
The on readings are ヨウ + ガン.
熔岩 is a recognized but rare alternative spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

マグマ is magma, the molten material beneath the surface;
the surface distinction explains why it is not always translated
as 溶岩.
[岩](岩.md) (いわ) is rock generally.
[噴火](噴火.md) (ふんか) is an eruption, the event rather than
the material.

## Distinctive meaning

[火山](火山.md) (かざん) names the volcano and
[火口](火口.md) (かこう) its crater.
These related nouns are not synonyms for lava.
Conversely, cold, solidified material may still be called 溶岩;
the spelling's "melting" element is not a requirement that it
remain hot and liquid.
