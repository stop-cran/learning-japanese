# 心身 (しんしん) - mind and body

## Meaning

心身 means mind and body considered together. It is a noun; use の before
a related noun, as in 心身の健康 (しんしんのけんこう), "mental and physical health."

- 心身ともに疲れました。 (しんしんともにつかれました。) - I became tired both mentally and physically.
- 心身の健康は大切です。 (しんしんのけんこうはたいせつです。) - Mental and physical health are important.

ともに makes the "both" explicit in the first example.
心身 is not simply another spelling for the physical body alone.

## How the kanji combine

[心](../kanji/心.md), mind or heart, and 身, body or person, form a
coordinate pair. The on readings シン + シン give しんしん.
Neither character is read with its standalone native reading here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[精神](精神.md) (せいしん) can focus on mental life.
[身体](身体.md) (しんたい) and [体](体.md) (からだ) focus on the body.
They help identify the two aspects combined in 心身, rather than being
unrestricted replacements for the whole pair.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 心身の before a noun, not an invented 心身な construction.
The selected reading is しんしん. The dictionary's older reading しんじん
and rare alternative spellings are lookup information, not forms needed
to produce the ordinary expressions above.
