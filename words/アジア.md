# アジア (アジア) — Asia

## Meaning

アジア is the name Asia. It behaves as a noun: use の before another noun to mean "Asian" or "of Asia".

- アジアの歴史を勉強しています。  
  アジアのれきしをべんきょうしています。  
  "I am studying Asian history."
- アジアを旅行したいです。  
  アジアをりょこうしたいです。  
  "I'd like to travel around Asia."

In the second example, を marks the region traveled through.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is katakana. The dictionary also records 亜細亜 as rare ateji: characters used to represent the foreign name, not a literal description of the region.

## Synonyms and antonyms

東アジア (ひがしアジア), "East Asia", names a subregion, not all of Asia. ヨーロッパ, "Europe", is another geographic region rather than an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

アジアの does not name a single language or nationality. Specify a country, language, or community when that is what you mean; the region contains many of each.
