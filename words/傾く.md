# 傾く (かたむく) - tilt; incline

## Meaning

傾く describes something inclining or slanting. It also covers the sun or moon going down, an enterprise declining, or an opinion tending toward one side.

- 強い風で木が傾いています。 (つよいかぜできがかたむいています。) - The tree is leaning because of the strong wind.
- 日が西に傾いてきました。 (ひがにしにかたむいてきました。) - The sun has begun to sink toward the west.
- 会社の経営が傾いています。 (かいしゃのけいえいがかたむいています。) - The company's business is declining.
- 話を聞いて、賛成の意見に傾きました。 (はなしをきいて、さんせいのいけんにかたむきました。) - After hearing the explanation, I came around toward supporting the proposal.

It is an intransitive godan verb: 傾きます, 傾いて, 傾かない.

## How the kanji combine

[傾](../kanji/傾.md) writes かたむ, followed by く. Leaning is a useful image connecting its physical and figurative uses, not a claim that a company literally tips over. The modern reading taught here is かたむく; the cited entry marks かたぶく as outdated.

## Synonyms and antonyms

傾ける (かたむける) is the transitive partner, "tilt something." [片寄る](片寄る.md) (かたよる) overlaps in leaning or favoring one side. [傾向](傾向.md) (けいこう) is the noun "tendency; trend."

## Distinctive meaning

An opinion can incline toward a position without becoming a final decision. English "decline" in the business example means weakening, not refusing an invitation: that refusal use belongs to [断る](断る.md) (ことわる). Do not assume every use of "down" or "decline" takes 傾く.
