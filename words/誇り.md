# 誇り (ほこり) - pride; source of pride

## Meaning

誇り is a noun for pride or something that brings credit to a person or group. Learn Nに誇りを持つ (Nにほこりをもつ), "take pride in N," and Nを誇りに思う (Nをほこりにおもう), "feel proud of N."

- 自分の仕事に誇りを持っています。 (じぶんのしごとにほこりをもっています。) - I take pride in my work.
- このチームは町の誇りです。 (このチームはまちのほこりです。) - This team is the pride of the town.

The first sentence names a feeling; the second names what gives the community that feeling.

## How the kanji combine

誇 carries the pride or boasting association. 誇り is the noun associated with 誇る (ほこる), "be proud of; boast of." The final り belongs to the noun form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[自慢](自慢.md) (じまん) overlaps in pride and boasting. 誇り can concern an inward sense of dignity or value without describing an act of telling others about it.

自信 (じしん), "confidence," concerns belief in one's ability; it need not concern something already achieved.

## Distinctive meaning

誇り does not automatically mean arrogance. The surrounding sentence determines whether pride is admired or criticized. Do not confuse it with [埃](埃.md) (ほこり), "dust": the words sound the same but have unrelated meanings. Particles help identify the intended pattern, especially 誇りを持つ and 誇りに思う.
