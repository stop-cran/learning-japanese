# 問題 (もんだい) — problem; question

## Meaning

問題 can mean **a question or problem to solve**, **an issue to consider**, or **trouble**.
Context tells you whether something is an exercise or a difficulty.

- この問題は難しいです。（このもんだいはむずかしいです。）— This question is difficult.
- パソコンに問題があります。（パソコンにもんだいがあります。）— There is a problem with the computer.
- 問題はありません。（もんだいはありません。）— There is no problem.

## How the kanji combine

[問](../kanji/問.md) contributes questioning and [題](../kanji/題.md) contributes a topic or task:
a **matter posed for an answer or consideration**.
The reading is on + on: モン + ダイ → もんだい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [質問](質問.md)（しつもん）is a question or inquiry directed to someone. It does not normally mean a malfunction or social problem.
- 宿題（しゅくだい）means "homework"; homework may contain several 問題.
- 話題（わだい）means a topic of conversation, without necessarily implying a problem.
- [答える](答える.md)（こたえる）, "answer," names an action in response to a question, not an antonym of 問題.

## Distinctive meaning

A 問題 in a test is not necessarily a badly written or troublesome question; it can simply be an item to answer.
Outside study, 問題がある often signals that something is wrong or needs attention.
Do not translate every English "question" as 問題: 質問 is often better when someone asks for information.
