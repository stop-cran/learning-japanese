# 消毒 (しょうどく) - disinfection; disinfect

## Meaning

消毒 is disinfection: treating something to make harmful microorganisms such as bacteria or viruses harmless. 消毒する is transitive; the thing treated takes を.

- 看護師が傷口を消毒しました。 (かんごしがきずぐちをしょうどくしました。) - The nurse disinfected the wound.
- 使用する前に器具を消毒します。 (しようするまえにきぐをしょうどくします。) - We disinfect the instruments before use.

消毒液 (しょうどくえき) is a disinfectant solution. These are language examples, not instructions about which treatment or product to use.

## How the kanji combine

[消](../kanji/消.md) contributes eliminating, and [毒](../kanji/毒.md) harm or toxicity. Together they suggest removing harmful effects. Do not reduce the modern word to removing a literal poisonous chemical; the hygiene use is central.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[清潔](清潔.md) (せいけつ) describes cleanliness or hygiene, while 消毒 names a treatment. [きれい](きれい.md), "clean; beautiful," does not by itself establish disinfection. Cleaning visible dirt and disinfecting are not interchangeable descriptions.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list and JMdict also give English "sterilization." Do not read that broad gloss as a guarantee that every organism has been eliminated. Japan's Ministry of Health describes 消毒 as making bacteria and viruses harmless and explicitly warns that disinfectants do not work against every bacterium or virus. This card does not define a complete technical sterilization standard.
