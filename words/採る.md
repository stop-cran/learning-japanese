# 採る (とる) - adopt; pick; collect

## Meaning

採る means to adopt a method or proposal, choose a course, or
pick and gather things such as fruit, flowers, and mushrooms.
It is a transitive godan verb, with the object marked by を.

- その案を採ることにしました。 (そのあんをとることにしました。) - We decided to adopt that proposal.
- 山でキノコを採りました。 (やまでキノコをとりました。) - I gathered mushrooms in the mountains.

Further uses include taking a sample, extracting juice, assuming
an attitude, taking on workers or students, and drawing in water
or light. For example, 血液を採る (けつえきをとる) means
"take a blood sample," and 新人を採る (しんじんをとる) can
mean "hire new staff."

Forms include 採ります (とります), 採って (とって), and
採らない (とらない). Despite ending in る, it is not ichidan.

## How the kanji combine

[採](../kanji/採.md) associates taking with picking, collecting,
or selecting. Its native reading supplies と; る is the ending.
The written character is not read サイ in this standalone verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[取る](取る.md) (とる) is a broader taking verb whose actual uses
also overlap with selection and gathering. [取り入れる](取り入れる.md)
(とりいれる) can mean adopting an idea.
[雇う](雇う.md) (やとう) overlaps with hiring, not with picking fruit.
[捕る](捕る.md) (とる) concerns catching; collecting insects can
overlap with 採る rather than creating a living-versus-nonliving rule.

## Distinctive meaning

The object identifies the use. Adopting a proposal, collecting a
specimen, and employing a person are not one unrestricted action.
The shared reading とる does not allow free replacement among
採る, 取る, 捕る, and [撮る](撮る.md), "photograph; film."
