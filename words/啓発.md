# 啓発 (けいはつ) — enlightenment; raising awareness

## Meaning

啓発 means helping someone recognize or understand something, or the act of raising awareness. It can be used with する.

- 市はごみの分別について住民への啓発を進めています。 (しはごみのぶんべつについてじゅうみんへのけいはつをすすめています。) — The city is raising residents' awareness about sorting waste.
- 学校は交通安全の啓発活動を行っています。 (がっこうはこうつうあんぜんのけいはつかつどうをおこなっています。) — The school is conducting an awareness campaign on traffic safety.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[啓](../kanji/啓.md) is read ケイ and [発](../kanji/発.md) is read ハツ here. Together they form the established word 啓発, read けいはつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

知らせる means to inform someone; 啓発 emphasizes awakening or developing understanding or awareness.

## Distinctive meaning

啓発 concerns awakening or developing understanding; it is not simply the act of announcing information.
