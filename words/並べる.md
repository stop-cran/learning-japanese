# 並べる (ならべる) - arrange in a line

## Meaning

並べる is a transitive ichidan verb: someone lines things up or arranges them side by side. The arranged items take を, and the destination or arrangement location can take に.

- 机を一列に並べました。 (つくえをいちれつにならべました。) - I arranged the desks in one row.
- テーブルにお皿を並べてください。 (テーブルにおさらをならべてください。) - Please set out the plates on the table.

Forms include 並べます, 並べた, and 並べない (ならべます、ならべた、ならべない).

## How the kanji combine

並 writes なら, with べる in kana. For conjugation, remove the final る: the ichidan stem is ならべ, not merely the portion represented by the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

並ぶ (ならぶ) is intransitive: people or things form a line. 置く (おく), "put," does not by itself imply an orderly row or side-by-side arrangement.

## Distinctive meaning

Contrast 椅子を並べる (いすをならべる), "arrange chairs," with 椅子が並ぶ (いすがならぶ), "chairs stand in a row." The difference concerns who acts and what takes を or が, not whether the arrangement is neat.
