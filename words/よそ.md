# よそ - elsewhere; outside one's group; disregard

## Meaning

よそ can name another place or somewhere else. It also concerns
people or places outside one's own family or group.

- この店になければ、よそで探します。 (このみせになければ、よそでさがします。) - If this shop doesn't have it, I'll look elsewhere.
- よその家のことには口を出さないでください。 (よそのいえのことにはくちをださないでください。) - Please don't meddle in another household's affairs.

Use よそで for an action elsewhere, よそに for a destination,
and よその before a noun in the other-place or other-group uses.

There is a separate construction: Nをよそに, "paying no attention
to N; disregarding N."
親の心配をよそに、彼は旅に出た
(おやのしんぱいをよそに、かれはたびにでた) means
"Disregarding his parents' concern, he set off on a journey."

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. The source spelling 余所 uses
[余](../kanji/余.md) and [所](../kanji/所.md), with an association
of another place. Other recognized spellings include 他所 and 外.
Learn the whole word よそ; the spelling 外 does not make it the
same lexical identity as the separate words そと or ほか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[外](外.md) (そと) concerns outside, and [ほか](ほか.md) other things
or places. [他人](他人.md) (たにん) concerns other people.
[内](内.md) (うち) is a useful inside/own-group contrast.
[無視](無視.md) (むし) overlaps with disregarding in ～をよそに,
not with every spatial use of よそ.

## Distinctive meaning

The boundary may be social rather than a building's wall.
よその人 (よそのひと) is someone from outside the relevant group;
it does not automatically mean a foreign national.
Conversely, ～をよそに is not a direction phrase meaning that
someone physically went outside. Keep the whole construction.
