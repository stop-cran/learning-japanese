# 影響 (えいきょう) - influence; effect

## Meaning

影響 is influence, impact, or an effect on something. As a する verb, it takes に
for what is affected: AがBに影響する, "A affects B."
As a noun, it appears in 影響を受ける, "be influenced," and
影響を与える, "have an influence."

- 天気は売り上げに影響します。
  (てんきはうりあげにえいきょうします。) - The weather affects sales.
- 私は祖母の考え方に影響を受けました。
  (わたしはそぼのかんがえかたにえいきょうをうけました。)
  - I was influenced by my grandmother's way of thinking.

良い影響 (よいえいきょう) and 悪い影響 (わるいえいきょう) specify a positive or negative effect.

## How the kanji combine

影 means "shadow; image," and 響 concerns resonance or an echo.
The compound names influence extending from one thing to another; the component images
are a memory aid, not a literal description of every influence. The on readings are エイ + キョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[効果](効果.md) (こうか) concerns an effect or effectiveness, such as a medicine's effect.
[結果](結果.md) (けっか) names an outcome. 影響 instead highlights how one thing affects another.

## Distinctive meaning

The particles change with the construction: Bに影響する, but Bに影響を
[与える](与える.md) (あたえる). Do not carry English transitive "affect" over as
Bを影響する. The word itself does not say whether the influence is beneficial.
