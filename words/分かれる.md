# 分かれる (わかれる) - branch; split; separate

## Meaning

分かれる describes something branching or dividing into parts, or people
separating into groups and dispersing. It is an intransitive ichidan verb:
the thing that divides takes が, and the resulting groups or directions can take に.

- 道がここで二つに分かれます。 (みちがここでふたつにわかれます。) - The road forks into two here.
- 私たちは二手に分かれて探しました。 (わたしたちはふたてにわかれてさがしました。) - We split into two groups and searched.

Useful forms are 分かれます, 分かれて and 分かれない.
意見が分かれる (いけんがわかれる) means that opinions differ.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) contributes division into parts, with the native verb
わかれる. The ending is かれる; learn the complete ichidan stem 分かれ.
The rare spelling 岐れる and search-only 分れる do not replace the ordinary 分かれる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[分ける](分ける.md) (わける) is transitive: 人を二組に分ける
(ひとをふたくみにわける), "divide people into two groups."
分かれる instead describes the people dividing: 人が二組に分かれる.

[別れる](別れる.md), also わかれる, concerns parting from someone or
ending a relationship. A road forking is not a relationship breakup.
[割れる](割れる.md) (われる) can also describe divided opinions, although
its everyday examples often concern something cracking or breaking.

## Distinctive meaning

An intransitive form does not mean that no one chose the division:
the searchers can intentionally split up. Nor is 分かれる a passive
conjugation of 分ける. Context distinguishes branching, splitting into
groups, and dispersing; the verb does not require equal-sized parts.
