# 古い (ふるい) — old

## Meaning

古い is an い-adjective for things, ideas and times that are not new: 古い家 (an old house), 古い友達 (an old friend, a long-standing one), 古い考え (an outdated idea), 古本 ふるほん (second-hand book).

## How the kanji combine

[古](../kanji/古.md) with okurigana (the kana after the kanji, like い in 古い): ふる + い. Related: 古さ ふるさ (age, oldness), 古くなる ふるくなる (get old).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [新しい](新しい.md) あたらしい.
- Close: 昔の むかしの (of the past), 旧 きゅう- (former, as in 旧友).
- For a person's age, 年を取った or 年寄り としより is used rather than 古い.

## Distinctive meaning

古い means "of long standing" or "outdated". For a person, 古い友達 means a friend of long date, not an elderly friend; for an elderly person, use 年寄り としより or 年配の人 ねんぱいのひと (年上 としうえ only means "older than me").
