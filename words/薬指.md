# 薬指 (くすりゆび) - ring finger

## Meaning

薬指 is the ring finger, between the middle finger and little finger.

- 左手の薬指に指輪をしています。 (ひだりてのくすりゆびにゆびわをしています。) - I wear a ring on the ring finger of my left hand.
- 薬指は、中指と小指の間の指です。 (くすりゆびは、なかゆびとこゆびのあいだのゆびです。) - The ring finger is the finger between the middle finger and little finger.

The dictionary additionally records the fourth toe, counting from the big toe. In a hand-related context, the ring-finger meaning is intended.

## How the kanji combine

[薬](../kanji/薬.md), medicine, is read くすり, and [指](../kanji/指.md), finger, is read ゆび. Both readings are native. Learn くすりゆび as the established body-part name; the written elements alone are not a verified account of its historical naming.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[指](指.md) (ゆび) is the broader word for a finger or toe. 中指 (なかゆび), middle finger, and 小指 (こゆび), little finger, are neighboring digits, not synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

English finger numbering varies according to whether the thumb is counted. "Ring finger" or "the finger between the middle and little fingers" avoids the ambiguity of an unexplained "third finger" or "fourth finger." The name does not require a ring to be present or indicate which hand it is on.
