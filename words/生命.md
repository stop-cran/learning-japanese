# 生命 (せいめい) - life; living existence

## Meaning

生命 names life or living existence. It is useful for life as a biological phenomenon and for the life that must be protected or can be lost. It is a noun, not a verb meaning "live."

- 水は生命にとって大切です。 (みずはせいめいにとってたいせつです。) - Water is important for life.
- 人間の生命を守ることが第一です。 (にんげんのせいめいをまもることがだいいちです。) - Protecting human life comes first.

In the first sentence, にとって means "for" from the standpoint of what life needs. In the second, を marks what is protected.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) concerns being alive, and [命](../kanji/命.md) contributes life or lifespan. Their related meanings reinforce one another. The on readings are セイ + メイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[命](命.md) (いのち) overlaps strongly when referring to a living being's life. [人生](人生.md) (じんせい) is a person's life course or experience, while [生活](生活.md) (せいかつ) concerns daily life or livelihood.

## Distinctive meaning

English "life" covers several different ideas. A question about enjoying daily life is not automatically about 生命. Conversely, 生命の起源 (せいめいのきげん), "the origin of life," concerns the existence of living things, not a person's routine.
