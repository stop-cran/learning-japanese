# 奥さん (おくさん) - someone else's wife

## Meaning

奥さん is a respectful everyday term for someone's wife. It can also be used to address a married woman, although a person's name is often preferable when known.

- 田中さんの奥さんは先生です。 (たなかさんのおくさんはせんせいです。) - Mr. Tanaka's wife is a teacher.

It is a noun, so use の to connect it to another noun or a person's name.

## How the kanji combine

奥 is read おく here; さん is the familiar respectful suffix. Learn 奥さん as an established social term, not as a literal description of where a woman belongs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

妻 (つま) is the usual neutral term when referring to one's own wife to others. 奥様 (おくさま) is more formally respectful. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The distinction involves the speaker's relationship to the person, not a different kind of marriage. Avoid mechanically using 奥さん for your own wife in formal introductions.
