# 法廷 (ほうてい) — court of law; courtroom

## Meaning

法廷 is the place where a trial is held. Daijisen notes that it is usually inside a courthouse, 裁判所 (さいばんしょ), so 法廷 is the courtroom rather than the whole building.

- 証人は法廷で事故の様子を説明した。 (しょうにんはほうていでじこのようすをせつめいした。) — The witness described the accident in court.
- 裁判の日、記者たちは朝から法廷の外で待っていた。 (さいばんのひ、きしゃたちはあさからほうていのそとでまっていた。) — On the day of the trial, reporters waited outside the courtroom from the morning.

## How the kanji combine

[法](../kanji/法.md) (ほう) is law and [廷](../kanji/廷.md) (てい) is a court. In 宮廷 (きゅうてい), “imperial or royal court,” the same 廷 refers to a ruler’s court instead.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[裁判](裁判.md) (さいばん) is the trial or judgment itself; 法廷 is where it takes place.

## Distinctive meaning

In the first example 法廷で marks the place where the witness spoke. 法廷 is a court of law, not a court for sports such as tennis; that is コート.
