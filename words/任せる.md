# 任せる (まかせる) - entrust; leave to

## Meaning

任せる is a transitive ichidan verb: leave a matter or decision to someone,
or entrust them with it. The basic pattern is **matter を person に任せる**.
Its polite form is 任せます, its negative 任せない, and its て-form 任せて.

- 旅行の計画は姉に任せました。 (りょこうのけいかくはあねにまかせました。) - I left planning the trip to my older sister.
- 後のことは私に任せてください。 (あとのことはわたしにまかせてください。) - Please leave the rest to me.

は presents the matter as a topic in these sentences; に still identifies
the person taking responsibility.

## How the kanji combine

[任](../kanji/任.md) conveys entrusting or responsibility. In the spelling
任せる, 任 supplies まか and せる is written in kana. Learn 任せる as its
own ichidan verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ) asks someone to do something.
[預ける](預ける.md) (あずける) entrusts something or someone to another's
care. 任せる particularly suits leaving the handling or decision to the
other person. These meanings overlap but do not have identical emphasis.

## Distinctive meaning

The recipient need not be a person: [運](運.md)に任せる (うんにまかせる)
means "leave to chance," using a related sense.
任せて, "Leave it to me," offers to take responsibility; it does not mean
"I have already finished." Depending on context, leaving someone in charge
may allow discretion, but the verb alone does not define every limit of authority.
