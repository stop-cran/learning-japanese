# 東洋 (とうよう) - the Asian East

## Meaning

東洋 names the East as an Asian cultural or geographical region, commonly
in contrast with the Western world. Its scope depends on context; it can include
Japan, China and India rather than only one fixed modern subregion.

- 大学で東洋の歴史を学んでいます。
  (だいがくでとうようのれきしをまなんでいます。) - I study the history of the Asian East at university.
- この本は東洋と西洋の美術を比較しています。
  (このほんはとうようとせいようのびじゅつをひかくしています。) - This book compares Eastern and Western art.

東洋医学 (とうよういがく) is Eastern medicine, an established compound whose
particular traditions must be identified from context.

## How the kanji combine

[東](../kanji/東.md) contributes east, and [洋](../kanji/洋.md) is the ocean
element also seen in 西洋. Learn the established regional name rather than
translating it as any ocean east of the speaker. The on readings are トウ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[アジア](アジア.md), Asia, overlaps geographically but is not an unrestricted
replacement in every historical or cultural label.
[西洋](西洋.md) (せいよう), the Western world, is the conventional counterpart.
[東](東.md) (ひがし) alone normally identifies the direction east.

## Distinctive meaning

The older English gloss "the Orient" points to this regional concept.
It does not make 東洋 the name of one country or fix identical boundaries in
every period. JMdict's separate dated "Japan" sense, used in China, is not a
rule that modern Japanese 東洋 ordinarily means Japan alone.
