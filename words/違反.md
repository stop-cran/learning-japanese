# 違反 (いはん) - violation; breach

## Meaning

違反 is a violation of a rule, law, or agreement. With する, the rule being violated takes に: 規則に違反する (きそくにいはんする), "violate the rules." The Japanese construction does not copy English's direct object after "violate."

- その行為は規則に違反します。 (そのこういはきそくにいはんします。) - That action violates the rules.
- ここに車を止めると、駐車違反になります。 (ここにくるまをとめると、ちゅうしゃいはんになります。) - Parking here will be a parking violation.

違反者 (いはんしゃ) is a person who has committed a violation.

## How the kanji combine

[違](../kanji/違.md) suggests departing from or going against something; [反](../kanji/反.md) contributes opposition. Their meanings reinforce the idea of acting contrary to a standard. The on readings are イ + ハン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[違う](違う.md) (ちがう) can simply mean "differ"; difference alone is not a violation. [間違い](間違い.md) (まちがい) is a mistake and need not break a rule. [守る](守る.md) (まもる), as in 規則を守る, provides the practical contrast "follow the rules."

## Distinctive meaning

違反 presupposes some applicable rule or requirement. It can concern school rules, sports, or contracts as well as criminal law; the word itself does not specify the severity or punishment.
