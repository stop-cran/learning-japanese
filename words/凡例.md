# 凡例 (はんれい) — explanatory notes; legend

## Meaning

凡例 names explanatory conventions or usage notes, often at the beginning of a
dictionary or another reference book. It can explain how to read entries,
abbreviations or symbols. On a map or diagram, it means the **legend** explaining
the symbols, not a legendary story.

- 辞書を使う前に、凡例を読んでください。 (じしょをつかうまえに、はんれいをよんでください。) — Please read the usage notes before using the dictionary.
- 地図の凡例で、記号の意味を確認しました。 (ちずのはんれいで、きごうのいみをかくにんしました。) — I checked the meanings of the symbols in the map's legend.

Both examples use the noun: 凡例を読む (はんれいをよむ), "read the explanatory
notes," and 地図の凡例 (ちずのはんれい), "a map's legend."

## How the kanji combine

[凡](../kanji/凡.md) contributes a general or overall scope here, not a judgment
of mediocrity. [例](../kanji/例.md) contributes conventions or established usage,
not only an individual example. Together, they concern the conventions for
using or interpreting the material. The on-readings are ハン + レイ: **はんれい**.
This association helps remember the word; it is not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[説明](説明.md) (せつめい), "explanation," and [解説](解説.md) (かいせつ),
"explanation; commentary," are broader. [注](注.md) (ちゅう) is an annotation
that may explain a particular passage. A 凡例 explains conventions for the
work or its symbols; it need not be a separate note on each difficult word.
There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 凡例 for every introductory paragraph, example or personal memo.
A book's [はじめに](はじめに.md), "introduction; preface," may discuss its purpose
without giving the usage conventions that a 凡例 provides. Context determines
whether English "usage notes," "explanatory notes," "legend" or "key" fits.

The homophone 判例 (はんれい) means a judicial precedent, not a map legend.
Learn the whole spelling and reading rather than choosing ボン merely because
it occurs in [平凡](平凡.md) (へいぼん).
