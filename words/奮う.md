# 奮う (ふるう) — muster one's courage or energy

## Meaning

Use 奮う when someone musters courage or energy. In 勇気を奮う (ゆうきをふるう), 勇気 is what the person summons, not an object being physically shaken.

- 勇気を奮って初めての舞台に立った。 (ゆうきをふるってはじめてのぶたいにたった。) — I mustered my courage and went onstage for the first time.
- 最後の坂では、気力を奮って歩いた。 (さいごのさかでは、きりょくをふるってあるいた。) — On the last hill, I summoned my energy and kept walking.

## How the kanji combine

[奮](../kanji/奮.md) supplies ふる and う is the ending. In the connecting form 奮って (ふるって), the small っ is part of the change from う to って.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勇気](勇気.md) is courage itself. 奮う describes calling on it. Do not confuse 奮う with [振る](振る.md) (ふる), which can mean to wave or shake something.

## Distinctive meaning

The examples show the transitive use with を. The dictionary also records an intransitive use, for spirits or energy rising; the word does not always take an object.
