# 生長 (せいちょう) - plant growth

## Meaning

生長 means growth, especially the growth of plants in the learning focus here.
生長する is intransitive: the plant that grows takes が.

- 苗が順調に生長しています。 (なえがじゅんちょうにせいちょうしています。) - The seedlings are growing steadily.
- 毎週、植物の生長を記録します。 (まいしゅう、しょくぶつのせいちょうをきろくします。) - We record the plants' growth every week.

The second example uses the noun as the object of 記録する, record.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) contributes life or growing, and [長](../kanji/長.md)
contributes becoming longer or greater. Together the related ideas concern growth.
Read セイ + チョウ, the same pronunciation as [成長](成長.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

成長 (せいちょう) covers growth or development of people, animals and plants,
as well as organizations and economies. [育つ](育つ.md) (そだつ) is a native
verb for growing or being brought up. [茂る](茂る.md) (しげる) emphasizes thick,
leafy growth rather than growth of every kind.

## Distinctive meaning

The plant focus is useful, but do not memorize an absolute rule that 生長 has
never described people or animals, or that 成長 cannot describe plants.
The broader uses are recorded in Japanese dictionaries, including older examples.
For this card, learn the plant-growth usage rather than importing every figurative
use of the same-reading 成長.
