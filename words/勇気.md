# 勇気 (ゆうき) - courage; bravery

## Meaning

勇気 is courage or the nerve to do something difficult or frightening. It is a noun. Use 勇気がある, "have courage," 勇気を出す, "summon up courage," and plain verb + 勇気, "the courage to do."

- 勇気を出して、先生に質問しました。 (ゆうきをだして、せんせいにしつもんしました。) - I worked up the courage to ask the teacher a question.
- 間違いを認める勇気が必要です。 (まちがいをみとめるゆうきがひつようです。) - It takes courage to admit a mistake.

The examples concern everyday interpersonal difficulty, not physical danger alone.

## How the kanji combine

勇 contributes bravery, and [気](../kanji/気.md) contributes spirit or a mental disposition. The first element describes the spirit: a courageous state of mind. The on readings are ユウ + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[覚悟](覚悟.md) (かくご) emphasizes readiness to face consequences; 勇気 emphasizes the courage to act despite difficulty. Fear can provide a contrast, but feeling afraid does not exclude showing courage.

## Distinctive meaning

勇気 does not mean recklessness or confidence that success is certain. A person may have doubts and still act courageously. 勇気をもらう (ゆうきをもらう), "gain courage from someone/something," describes receiving encouragement, not transferring a physical object. Keep the noun's particles rather than treating it as an i-adjective.
