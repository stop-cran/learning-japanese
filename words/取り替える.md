# 取り替える (とりかえる) — replace; swap

## Meaning

取り替える means to replace something or exchange it for something else. It is a transitive ichidan verb: 取り替えます, 取り替えて, and 取り替えない.

- 古い電池を新しい電池に取り替えました。 (ふるいでんちをあたらしいでんちにとりかえました。) — I replaced the old batteries with new ones.
- 友達と席を取り替えました。 (ともだちとせきをとりかえました。) — I swapped seats with a friend.

を marks what is replaced or exchanged. に can mark the replacement, while と can identify the other participant in a swap.

## How the kanji combine

[取](../kanji/取.md) appears in 取り, the connecting form of 取る (とる), "take." 替える (かえる) contributes replacement. The final verb controls the compound's ichidan conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

交換する (こうかんする) also means to exchange or replace. 変える (かえる), "change," is broader: changing a plan need not involve exchanging one physical item for another.

## Distinctive meaning

取り替える can describe either replacing a used item or swapping two items between people. The context and particles clarify whether anything is returned in exchange.
