# 文字 (もじ) - written character; writing

## Meaning

文字 names a written character, such as a kana character, kanji, or letter of an alphabet. It also means writing as a means of recording language.

- 文字を大きく書いてください。 (もじをおおきくかいてください。) - Please write the characters larger.
- 話した内容を文字にしました。 (はなしたないようをもじにしました。) - I put what was said into writing.

**文字にする** means to put something into written form. This is not limited to handwriting; the point is representing the content in writing.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) contributes writing or written language, and [字](../kanji/字.md) contributes a written character. Together they reinforce written representation. Learn the established reading もじ.

The same dictionary entry also records **文字 (もんじ)** for a written character or writing. This is another reading of the same word, not a separate kind of script; the examples above use もじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[字](字.md) (じ) overlaps with the character sense. [単語](単語.md) (たんご) is a word, which may contain several characters; [文章](文章.md) (ぶんしょう) is connected written text. These are different units.

## Distinctive meaning

English "letter" here means a character such as A, not a message sent to someone: [手紙](手紙.md) (てがみ) names that message. Neither もじ nor もんじ inherently limits the word to the Roman alphabet.
