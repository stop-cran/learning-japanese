# かまう - mind; be concerned

## Meaning

かまう means to mind or be concerned about something. It is a godan verb: かまいます, かまって and かまわない. The negative is especially useful for saying something is acceptable.

- ここに座ってもかまいませんか。 (ここにすわってもかまいませんか。) - Would it be all right if I sat here?
- 明日でもかまいません。 (あしたでもかまいません。) - Tomorrow is fine too.

Verb て-form + もかまわない means that doing something is not a problem. A positive かまいます is not the normal answer granting permission.

## How the kanji combine

This page uses kana. The kanji spelling is [構](../kanji/構.md)う, also read かまう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

気にする (きにする) also means to be concerned about something. ～てもいい is another common permission pattern.

## Distinctive meaning

かまわない can sound like "I don't mind" rather than an enthusiastic invitation. A separate use of かまう takes を and means to pay attention to or spend time with someone; do not force the permission interpretation onto every sentence.

子供をかまっていたので、電話に出られませんでした。 (こどもをかまっていたので、でんわにでられませんでした。) - I was occupied with the child, so I could not answer the phone.
This positive use describes giving the child attention, not granting permission.
