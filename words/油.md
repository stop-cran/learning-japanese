# 油 (あぶら) - oil

## Meaning

油 is a noun for oil, including cooking oil and oil used on machinery. The context identifies the substance; the word alone does not specify that it is edible.

- フライパンに油を入れます。 (フライパンにあぶらをいれます。) - I put oil in the frying pan.
- この機械には油が必要です。 (このきかいにはあぶらがひつようです。) - This machine needs oil.

In the first sentence, を marks what is added and に marks the container. Useful descriptions include 油が多い (あぶらがおおい), "there is a lot of oil," and 油のにおい, "the smell of oil."

## How the kanji combine

油 writes the whole native word あぶら. There is no compound to divide. The on reading ユ appears in compounds such as [石油](石油.md) (せきゆ), "petroleum"; that does not change the standalone reading here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

脂 (あぶら) is a separate, same-sounding noun for fat or grease, as in 肉の脂 (にくのあぶら), "fat in meat." The spelling helps distinguish the intended substance. 石油 identifies petroleum rather than any oil. Neither is an opposite of 油.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the noun together with its context: cooking oil, machine oil, and petroleum are not interchangeable products. Likewise, English "oily" normally needs a construction such as 油の多い rather than treating 油 as an adjective.
