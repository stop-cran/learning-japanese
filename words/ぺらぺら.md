# ぺらぺら — fluent; chattering; flimsy

## Meaning

ぺらぺら describes fluent foreign-language speech, incessant or glib talking, rapidly turning pages, or thin, flimsy material.

- 姉はスペイン語がぺらぺらだ。 — My older sister speaks Spanish fluently.
- 彼は秘密までぺらぺらしゃべってしまった。 — He chattered away and even let the secret slip. (秘密: ひみつ.)
- この上着はぺらぺらで、冬には寒い。 — This jacket is flimsy and too cold for winter. (上着: うわぎ.)

For fluency, ～語がぺらぺらだ and ～語をぺらぺら話す are useful patterns. Before a speech verb, ぺらぺら（と）しゃべる emphasizes the flowing manner. For material, ぺらぺらの紙 (かみ) describes thin paper.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ひそひそ](ひそひそ.md) specifies a low voice rather than fluency. [がやがや](がやがや.md) describes a crowd's hubbub rather than one speaker's language ability.

## Distinctive meaning

Fluency can be praise, while spilling information can be criticism. Neither sense says that everything spoken is accurate or sophisticated. Do not apply the adjective grammar of ぺらぺらな英語 mechanically to every sound-imitation expression.
