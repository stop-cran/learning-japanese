# 少女 (しょうじょ) - young girl

## Meaning

少女 is a noun for a girl or young female person, generally evoking childhood or adolescence. It is useful in descriptions, stories, and category names. It does not itself state a family relationship.

- 物語の主人公は十二歳の少女です。 (ものがたりのしゅじんこうはじゅうにさいのしょうじょです。) - The story's main character is a twelve-year-old girl.
- 少女は窓の外を見ていました。 (しょうじょはまどのそとをみていました。) - The girl was looking out of the window.

少女のころ (しょうじょのころ) means "when she/I was a girl," with the person supplied by context.

## How the kanji combine

[少](../kanji/少.md) contributes youth here, not a small numerical quantity. [女](../kanji/女.md) contributes female. The first element qualifies the second: a young female person. Keep the long vowel in しょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[女の子](女の子.md) (おんなのこ) is an everyday comparison for a girl. [少年](少年.md) (しょうねん), "boy/youth," is a related age-category word rather than a strict opposite applicable to every context.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 少女 as "daughter" unless the sentence independently establishes that relationship, or infer sexual status from the word. The source gloss overreaches on both points. The dictionary also records an archaic age category from the ritsuryo period; that historical category does not set a modern legal or conversational age boundary.
