# 辞める (やめる) - resign; quit

## Meaning

辞める means to resign, retire, or leave a job, position, or similar role.
It is a transitive ichidan verb: what the person leaves takes を.
The polite form is 辞めます, the past 辞めた, and the negative 辞めない.

- 来月、今の仕事を辞めます。 (らいげつ、いまのしごとをやめます。) - I will leave my current job next month.
- 彼女は教師を辞めて、店を開きました。 (かのじょはきょうしをやめて、みせをひらきました。) - She stopped working as a teacher and opened a shop.

会社を辞める (かいしゃをやめる) means leaving one's company, not shutting the
company down.

## How the kanji combine

[辞](../kanji/辞.md) contributes resigning or giving up a position. It is read
や in this native verb, with める in kana. The spelling helps identify the
employment or role-related meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[止める](止める.md) (やめる) concerns stopping an action or practice, such as
quitting smoking. 辞める focuses on leaving a position or affiliation.
[就職](就職.md) (しゅうしょく), "finding employment," is a contrasting event,
not a grammatical opposite formed from the same verb.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "retire" does not require old age or permanent withdrawal
from work. Someone can 辞める one job and immediately start another.
Also distinguish the voluntary-looking active sentence from a passive account
of being dismissed; 辞める alone does not specify every circumstance behind
the departure.
