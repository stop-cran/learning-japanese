# 単純 (たんじゅん) - simple; uncomplicated

## Meaning

単純 is a na-adjective meaning simple, uncomplicated, or straightforward.
Use **単純な + noun**, **単純です**, and **単純に + verb**.
When applied to a person or their thinking, it can suggest being naive or simple-minded.

- この機械の仕組みは単純です。 (このきかいのしくみはたんじゅんです。) - This machine has a simple mechanism.
- 問題はそんなに単純ではありません。 (もんだいはそんなにたんじゅんではありません。) - The problem is not that simple.

単純な作業 (たんじゅんなさぎょう) is an uncomplicated task.
The negative 単純ではない denies simplicity, not the existence of the problem.

## How the kanji combine

[単](../kanji/単.md) contributes singleness or simplicity; 純 contributes being unmixed.
The related ideas combine into a lack of complexity.
The on readings タン + ジュン give たんじゅん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[簡単](簡単.md) (かんたん) overlaps with simple and easy.
単純 focuses on complexity; a simple repeated task can still be tiring or difficult to do well.
[複雑](複雑.md) (ふくざつ), "complicated," is the direct opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

単純 is not automatically praise. 単純な人 (たんじゅんなひと) can sound dismissive,
suggesting someone is easily influenced or lacks subtlety.
For mechanisms or explanations, however, a lack of complexity may be welcome.
Choose the evaluation from the sentence rather than assuming English "simple" is uniformly positive or negative.
