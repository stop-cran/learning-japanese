# まじめ (まじめ) - earnest; conscientious

## Meaning

まじめ describes a serious, sincere attitude toward something, or a person who acts conscientiously. It is a na-adjective.

- 田中さんはまじめな学生です。 (たなかさんはまじめながくせいです。) - Tanaka is a conscientious student.
- まじめに答えてください。 (まじめにこたえてください。) - Please answer seriously.

Use な before a noun and に when describing how an action is done. As a predicate, say まじめです.

## How the kanji combine

The word can also be written 真面目. JMdict labels that spelling ateji: the established written form should not be treated as an ordinary on-reading compound. Kana is straightforward and natural.

## Synonyms and antonyms

真剣 (しんけん) emphasizes earnest concentration or taking something seriously. 不まじめ (ふまじめ), "not serious; irresponsible," supplies a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

A まじめな人 can still enjoy jokes. The word often praises reliability, rather than claiming someone is always solemn. It also does not translate every English "serious": a serious injury needs vocabulary about severity, not a conscientious attitude.
