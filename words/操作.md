# 操作 (そうさ) - operation; manipulation

## Meaning

操作 is handling or operating something, especially controls or equipment. It is a noun and transitive する verb: 機械を操作する (きかいをそうさする), "operate a machine." It also describes manipulation for someone's benefit.

- この機械の操作は簡単です。 (このきかいのそうさはかんたんです。) - This machine is easy to operate.
- 数字を操作してはいけません。 (すうじをそうさしてはいけません。) - You must not manipulate the figures.

The second example concerns altering figures to serve a purpose, not merely pressing buttons on a calculator.

## How the kanji combine

操 contributes handling or controlling, and [作](../kanji/作.md) contributes doing or making. Together they point to carrying out control or manipulation. The on readings are ソウ + サ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[運転](運転.md) (うんてん) can refer to driving a vehicle or running machinery. 操作 focuses on handling controls. [管理](管理.md) (かんり) concerns management or oversight, which need not involve personally operating the device.

## Distinctive meaning

操作 is not inherently dishonest: 操作方法 (そうさほうほう) simply means operating instructions or a method of operation. A negative reading arises in contexts such as manipulating information or figures. The English gloss "operation" should not be extended to surgery; the Japanese word here concerns handling or control.
