# 商品 (しょうひん) - merchandise; goods for sale

## Meaning

商品 is merchandise or a product considered as something for sale. It can
refer to one item or goods collectively; the noun does not itself mark plural.

- この商品はよく売れています。 (このしょうひんはよくうれています。) - This product is selling well.
- 店員が商品を棚に並べています。 (てんいんがしょうひんをたなにならべています。) - A shop assistant is arranging merchandise on the shelves.

商品の値段 (しょうひんのねだん) means the price of a product.
新商品 (しんしょうひん) is a new product.

## How the kanji combine

[商](../kanji/商.md) contributes trade or commerce, and [品](../kanji/品.md)
contributes an article or item. The first qualifies the second: an item of
commerce. The on readings are ショウ + ヒン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[製品](製品.md) (せいひん) emphasizes a manufactured product, while 商品
emphasizes its commercial role. [品物](品物.md) (しなもの) is a broader word
for an article or goods, without necessarily focusing on sale.

## Distinctive meaning

The same object can be both 製品 and 商品: one description concerns production,
the other commerce. 商品 is not restricted to a particular size, shop, or
brand. Also distinguish it from 賞品 (しょうひん), a prize: the identical
pronunciation does not make a competition prize the same lexical item as
merchandise.
