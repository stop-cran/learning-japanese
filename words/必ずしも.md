# 必ずしも (かならずしも) - not necessarily; not always

## Meaning

必ずしも is used with a negative statement to reject a generalization: something is not necessarily, not always, or not entirely true. It does not usually say that the opposite is invariably true.

- 高い物が必ずしもいいとは限りません。 (たかいものがかならずしもいいとはかぎりません。) - Expensive things are not necessarily good.
- 彼の説明は必ずしも正しくありません。 (かれのせつめいはかならずしもただしくありません。) - His explanation is not entirely correct.

In the first sentence, clause + とは限らない means "it is not necessarily the case that..." In the second, the adjective supplies the negative ending directly.

## How the kanji combine

[必](../kanji/必.md) relates to certainty or necessity. The rest of 必ずしも is kana. Learn the complete adverb and its negative construction rather than assigning separate kanji meanings to ずしも.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[必ず](必ず.md) (かならず) expresses certainty or an instruction to do something without fail. It is not interchangeable with 必ずしも. 全く正しくない (まったくただしくない), "not correct at all," is stronger than denying complete correctness.

## Distinctive meaning

The scope of the negative matters. 必ずしも成功しない (かならずしもせいこうしない) allows both success and failure; it rejects guaranteed success. 必ず失敗する (かならずしっぱいする) instead predicts certain failure. Do not drop the negative from the first pattern or translate partial negation as "never."
