# 等分 (とうぶん) - division into equal parts

## Meaning

等分 is dividing something into equal parts, or the equal parts themselves.
It is a noun and a transitive する verb: what is divided takes を.

- ケーキを四等分しました。
  (ケーキをよんとうぶんしました。) - I divided the cake into four equal pieces.
- 二人で費用を等分して払いました。
  (ふたりでひようをとうぶんしてはらいました。) - The two of us split the cost equally and paid.

A number before 等分 specifies the number of equal shares:
三等分 (さんとうぶん), division into three equal parts.

## How the kanji combine

[等](../kanji/等.md) contributes equality, and [分](../kanji/分.md)
division or shares. Equality qualifies the division. The on readings are トウ + ブン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[分ける](分ける.md) (わける) and [割る](割る.md) (わる) can describe
division without by themselves guaranteeing equal shares.
[半分](半分.md) (はんぶん) is half; 等分 does not limit the number of parts to two.

## Distinctive meaning

The relevant equality may be size, amount or cost, depending on what is divided.
Equal portions are not automatically [公平](公平.md) (こうへい), fair,
under every rule: equal payment and payment according to use can differ.
等分 describes the division, not a universal judgment about justice.
