# 注す (さす) - pour; add drops of liquid

## Meaning

注す means pouring or adding liquid, including small amounts such as eye drops or oil.
The family also covers serving drinks. Kana さす is usual.
It is a transitive godan verb: 注します, 注して, 注した, 注さない.

- 目が乾いたので、目薬をさしました。 (めがかわいたので、めぐすりをさしました。) - My eyes were dry, so I put in eye drops.
- スープに水を少し注しました。 (スープにみずをすこしさしました。) - I added a little water to the soup.

Use に for the receiving place or mixture and を for the liquid when both are stated.
酒をさす (さけをさす) is serving or offering sake, not growing rice for it.

## How the kanji combine

[注](../kanji/注.md) contributes pouring and has the native reading さ here; す completes the verb.
The spelling does not change the reading to そそぐ, which belongs to 注ぐ.
Recognize kana さす as well as the written source form 注す.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[注ぐ](注ぐ.md) (そそぐ) is a broader pouring verb and also has figurative uses for directing effort.
[酌む](酌む.md) (くむ) overlaps in serving sake.
[足す](足す.md) (たす) and [加える](加える.md) (くわえる) cover addition more generally.
Adding a little liquid does not make all these verbs interchangeable in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

A second family sense is applying color, especially written 点す:
紅をさす (べにをさす), "apply rouge/lipstick."
Here [点](../kanji/点.md) has the reading さ, not テン.
Do not treat that spelling preference as permission to write every lipstick expression with 注す.
[塗る](塗る.md) (ぬる) means applying material over a surface more broadly.
The unrelated [指す](指す.md), "point," has the same pronunciation.
