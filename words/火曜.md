# 火曜 (かよう) - Tuesday

## Meaning

火曜 is Tuesday, a shorter form of [火曜日](火曜日.md) (かようび). It works as a time noun in schedules and ordinary statements about when something happens.

- 次の会議は火曜です。 (つぎのかいぎはかようです。) - The next meeting is on Tuesday.
- 火曜と木曜に日本語を勉強します。 (かようともくようににほんごをべんきょうします。) - I study Japanese on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

に can mark the scheduled day. For a repeated schedule, 毎週火曜 (まいしゅうかよう) explicitly means "every Tuesday." In timetable labels, the still shorter 火 can represent Tuesday when the context makes the weekday clear.

## How the kanji combine

[火](../kanji/火.md), "fire," identifies this member of the weekday series; [曜](../kanji/曜.md) is the shared weekday element. The on readings カ + ヨウ give かよう. This is a calendar name, not a description of fiery weather.

## Synonyms and antonyms

火曜日 refers to the same weekday and retains the final 日. [木曜](木曜.md) (もくよう) is Thursday, a different day rather than an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

火曜 does not specify which week's Tuesday. The date, tense, or surrounding schedule resolves that. It also names a weekday, not a duration of one day.
