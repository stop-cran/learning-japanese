# 連続 (れんぞく) - succession; consecutive occurrence

## Meaning

連続 is a succession or series in which events follow one another. Use
連続する, "occur consecutively," or a number and counter + 連続で for
"...in a row."

- 三日連続で雨が降りました。 (みっかれんぞくであめがふりました。) - It rained three days in a row.
- 同じ番号が二回連続で出ました。 (おなじばんごうがにかいれんぞくででました。) - The same number came up twice consecutively.

連続した出来事 (れんぞくしたできごと) means a succession of events.
The time scale or counted occasions define what counts as consecutive.

## How the kanji combine

[連](../kanji/連.md) contributes linking or following in a series, and
[続](../kanji/続.md) contributes continuing. Their related meanings combine
into linked continuation. The on readings are レン + ゾク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[続く](続く.md) (つづく) describes continuing or following.
[続ける](続ける.md) (つづける) describes continuing something.
連続 emphasizes the sequence or lack of an intervening break in the relevant
series. Interruption is a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

三日連続で雨が降った means rain occurred on three consecutive days; it need
not mean rain fell without pause for seventy-two hours. Similarly, consecutive
wins concern adjacent matches, not constant play. Identify the unit before
translating 連続 as literally unbroken activity.
