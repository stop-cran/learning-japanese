# 正午 (しょうご) - noon

## Meaning

正午 is noon, twelve o'clock in the daytime. It is a time noun. **正午に** identifies when something happens; 正午までに sets a deadline of noon.

- 会議は正午に終わります。 (かいぎはしょうごにおわります。) - The meeting ends at noon.
- 正午までに戻ってください。 (しょうごまでにもどってください。) - Please return by noon.

正午ごろ (しょうごごろ) means "around noon." Adding ごろ deliberately makes the time approximate rather than changing the meaning of 正午 itself.

## How the kanji combine

[正](../kanji/正.md) contributes exactness or being right at a point, while [午](../kanji/午.md) supplies the noon association. Together they identify noon itself. The on readings are ショウ + ゴ; the initial しょう has a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[昼](昼.md) (ひる) can mean daytime or the middle part of the day, so it is broader than the precise time 正午. 昼の十二時 (ひるのじゅうにじ) expresses the same clock time. Midnight is a contrasting time, not another meaning of 正午.

## Distinctive meaning

正午 avoids ambiguity between twelve in the daytime and twelve at night. It is useful in schedules, announcements, and deadlines. It identifies a time, not the meal eaten near it: [昼食](昼食.md) (ちゅうしょく) is lunch and need not begin exactly at noon.
