# 土産 (みやげ) - souvenir gift; gift brought on a visit

## Meaning

土産 is a gift bought while traveling, often a local specialty, or a present brought
when visiting someone. It is a noun. With the prefix お, [お土産](お土産.md)
is read おみやげ.

- 京都で家族に土産を買いました。
  (きょうとでかぞくにみやげをかいました。)
  - I bought souvenir gifts for my family in Kyoto.
- 友人の家を訪ねるとき、土産にお菓子を持っていきました。
  (ゆうじんのいえをたずねるとき、みやげにおかしをもっていきました。)
  - When visiting a friend's home, I brought sweets as a gift.

土産に identifies the role of the sweets: they were brought as a visiting gift.
The second use does not require a long-distance trip.

## How the kanji combine

[土](../kanji/土.md) contributes land, and [産](../kanji/産.md) contributes production.
Those meanings fit an association with local products, but みやげ is a whole-word
special reading. Do not divide it into invented individual readings for 土 and 産.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[贈り物](贈り物.md) (おくりもの) and [プレゼント](プレゼント.md) mean gifts more broadly.
A birthday present is not automatically a 土産; the travel or visit context matters.
There is no useful general antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "souvenir" can mean a keepsake retained by the traveler.
The core use here highlights something brought as a gift for someone, including consumable
local foods. The noun does not require an object intended for permanent display.
