# こんこん — knock-knock; cough-cough

## Meaning

こんこん imitates repeated knocking, coughing, or the cry of a fox. It also occurs in descriptions of steadily falling snow.

- ドアをこんこんとたたいたが、返事はなかった。 — I knocked on the door, but there was no answer.
- 子供がこんこんとせきをしている。 — The child is coughing repeatedly.
- 雪がこんこんと降り続いている。 — Snow keeps falling thickly.

The sound or manner modifies a verb with と: こんこんとたたく, こんこんとせきをする, こんこんと降る. The verb identifies which sense is intended.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とんとん](とんとん.md) overlaps in tapping or knocking. [どんどん](どんどん.md) can suggest louder, heavier pounding. These contrasts are tendencies rather than fixed measurements of volume.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not merge this entry with the separate homophones 懇々 (earnestly), 昏々 (deeply asleep), or 滾々 (flowing copiously). Those words have their own dictionary identities; shared kana do not make their senses interchangeable.
