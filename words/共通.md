# 共通 (きょうつう) - shared; in common

## Meaning

共通 describes something common to, or shared by, multiple people or things.
A useful noun pattern is 共通のN. With する, it means "be shared" or "have something in common";
the things sharing a feature can be marked with に.

- 私たちには共通の趣味があります。 (わたしたちにはきょうつうのしゅみがあります。) - We have a hobby in common.
- この二つの話には共通する点があります。 (このふたつのはなしにはきょうつうするてんがあります。) - These two stories have points in common.

共通点 (きょうつうてん) is a useful compound for a shared feature or point of similarity.
共通 is not an ordinary noun for a community of people.

## How the kanji combine

[共](../kanji/共.md) contributes "together; shared," and [通](../kanji/通.md) contributes "pass through; extend through."
The combination suggests something applying across several members, rather than a feature confined to one.
The on readings are キョウ + ツウ, both with long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ), "the same," overlaps, but two people can share one interest without being
the same in other respects. [違い](違い.md) (ちがい), "difference," contrasts with a shared point.
[普通](普通.md) (ふつう), "ordinary," concerns normality, not shared membership.

## Distinctive meaning

English "common" can mean either "shared" or "frequently encountered." 共通 teaches the shared
relationship here. A 共通の友人 (きょうつうのゆうじん) is a mutual friend, not a friend judged
ordinary or unremarkable. Identify who or what shares the feature before choosing this word.
