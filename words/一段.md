# 一段 (いちだん) - even more; one level

## Meaning

一段 can mean one step or level. In the adverbial expression **一段と**,
it means "even more" or "to a further degree."

- 雨の後、一段と寒くなりました。 (あめのあと、いちだんとさむくなりました。) - After the rain, it became even colder.
- 階段を一段上がりました。 (かいだんをいちだんあがりました。) - I went up one stair.

Before a noun, 一段の進歩 (いちだんのしんぽ) means "further progress."
In this degree sense, the adverb usually has と; 一段 is not limited to
physically climbing stairs.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), one, counts a [段](../kanji/段.md), step or level.
The literal unit helps recall the further-degree use, but 一段と寒い is
not a measurement of exactly one degree Celsius.
The reading is いちだん, without the small っ found in some other 一 compounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一層](一層.md) (いっそう), "even more," overlaps in intensifying a degree.
[もっと](もっと.md), "more," also works in requests.
[更に](更に.md) (さらに) can additionally introduce another item or argument;
that listing function is not automatically the same as 一段と.

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding noun or predicate identifies whether a physical level or
a greater degree is meant. In grammar, 一段 also abbreviates 一段動詞
(いちだんどうし), an ichidan verb. That technical label is a separate use,
not the meaning of 一段と寒くなる.
