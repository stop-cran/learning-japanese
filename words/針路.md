# 針路 (しんろ) - course; heading

## Meaning

針路 is a course or direction of travel, especially for a ship or aircraft.
It is a noun. 針路を変える (しんろをかえる) means to change course;
針路をとる (しんろをとる) means to take or set a course.

- 船は針路を南に変えました。 (ふねはしんろをみなみにかえました。) - The ship changed course to the south.
- 針路を外れないように注意してください。 (しんろをはずれないようにちゅういしてください。) - Please take care not to go off course.

The second sentence uses 針路を外れる (しんろをはずれる), "deviate from course."

## How the kanji combine

[針](../kanji/針.md), needle or pointer, combines with
[路](../kanji/路.md), route or course. Daijisen relates the word to
determining direction with a compass. This explains the compass-pointer
association; it is not an account of the characters' historical origins.
The on readings are シン + ロ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[方向](方向.md) (ほうこう) is a broader direction or course.
[コース](コース.md) also names a course or route.
The homophone 進路 (しんろ) can name a route and someone's future
course, such as what to do after graduation.
The two spellings overlap rather than forming absolute literal-versus-figurative categories.

## Distinctive meaning

Daijisen also records 針路 for an intended direction, including an
organization's course. It is therefore not restricted to an instrument
display or a numerical compass bearing.
For a student's future choices, learn the established expression
進路を決める (しんろをきめる), "decide one's future course."
