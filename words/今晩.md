# 今晩 (こんばん) — tonight

## Meaning

今晩 is this evening or tonight. It can directly introduce a plan, invitation or description of this
part of the day. With の it modifies a noun: 今晩の予定 (こんばんのよてい), "tonight's plans."

- 今晩、一緒にご飯を食べませんか。 (こんばん、いっしょにごはんをたべませんか。) — Would you like to have dinner together tonight?
- 今晩は家にいます。 (こんばんはいえにいます。) — I'll be at home tonight.

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) selects the present occasion, and [晩](../kanji/晩.md) supplies evening or night.
It is a modifier-plus-time-word compound, read コン + バン.
The whole word points to tonight, not evenings in general.

## Synonyms and antonyms

今夜 (こんや) also means "tonight; this evening." Their basic meanings overlap; do not invent a strict
hour dividing them. 毎晩 (まいばん), "every night," changes the time reference from this occasion to a repeated one.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the time expression こんばん from the greeting **こんばんは**, "good evening."
The greeting is normally written in kana, and its final は is pronounced わ.
In 今晩は家にいます, は is instead the topic particle in a complete sentence about tonight;
the shared opening sound does not turn the sentence into a greeting.
