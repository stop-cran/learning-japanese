# 浮かべる (うかべる) - set afloat; bring to mind

## Meaning

浮かべる is a transitive ichidan verb: set something afloat, show an expression on one's face,
or bring an image or memory to mind. The boat, expression or mental image takes を.

- 小さな船を湖に浮かべました。 (ちいさなふねをみずうみにうかべました。) - I set a small boat afloat on the lake.
- 彼女は笑顔を浮かべました。 (かのじょはえがおをうかべました。) - She smiled.
- 子供のころの景色を心に浮かべました。 (こどものころのけしきをこころにうかべました。) - I pictured the scenery of my childhood.

Forms include 浮かべます (うかべます), 浮かべた (うかべた) and 浮かべない (うかべない).
Transitivity describes the sentence pattern; it does not mean every smile is deliberately put on.

## How the kanji combine

[浮](../kanji/浮.md) supplies the native reading う, with かべる written in kana.
The same character appears in [浮かぶ](浮かぶ.md), but the complete verbs have different conjugations.

## Synonyms and antonyms

船が浮かぶ (ふねがうかぶ), "a boat floats," describes the boat; 船を浮かべる describes setting it afloat.
[思い出す](思い出す.md) (おもいだす) overlaps when recalling a memory.
[想像](想像.md) (そうぞう) concerns imagining and need not involve something remembered.

## Distinctive meaning

笑顔を浮かべる describes showing a smile, not making a face float on water.
Likewise, an image brought to mind need not be a literal object or a true memory.
Do not translate every use as "launch": that suits a boat, not the facial-expression example.
