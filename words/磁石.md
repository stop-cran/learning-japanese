# 磁石 (じしゃく) - magnet; magnetic compass

## Meaning

磁石 is a magnet. It can also mean a magnetic compass, the instrument that
uses a magnet to indicate direction.

- 磁石に鉄のクリップがくっつきました。 (じしゃくにてつのクリップがくっつきました。) - The iron paper clip stuck to the magnet.
- 磁石で北の方角を確かめました。 (じしゃくできたのほうがくをたしかめました。) - I checked which direction was north with a compass.

じせき is another dictionary reading of 磁石.
The article's primary reading is じしゃく.

## How the kanji combine

磁 contributes magnetism, and [石](../kanji/石.md) contributes stone.
The on readings ジ + シャク give じしゃく; 石 also has セキ in じせき.
The stone association does not mean that every modern magnet is a natural rock.

## Synonyms and antonyms

マグネット is another magnet word.
方位磁針 (ほういじしん) explicitly names a directional compass.
English "compass" can also name a drawing instrument; that is not this magnetic use.

## Distinctive meaning

The two uses are related but not identical: a magnet on a refrigerator is not
therefore a compass. Nor does the example mean that magnets attract every
kind of metal. The material matters.
