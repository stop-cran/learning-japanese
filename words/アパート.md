# アパート - apartment; apartment building

## Meaning

アパート can mean an apartment building or an individual apartment in it.
The context decides whether the whole building or someone's home is meant.

- 駅の近くのアパートに住んでいます。 (えきのちかくのアパートにすんでいます。) - I live in an apartment near the station.

に marks the place of residence. アパートを借りる (アパートをかりる) means
"rent an apartment," with を marking what is rented.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword shortens the longer apartment/apartment-house expressions.
The long-vowel mark in パート is part of the spelling; there are no kanji
components to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

マンション in Japanese also refers to an apartment or apartment building,
not normally an English mansion. 不動産 (ふどうさん), "real estate," is a
broader category rather than a synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Housing advertisements often use アパート for smaller, lower-rise buildings
and マンション for more substantial blocks, but those labels alone do not
guarantee construction, amenities, or ownership. Check the actual listing
rather than treating the English-sounding name as a technical specification.
