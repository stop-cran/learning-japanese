# 凹凸 (おうとつ) - unevenness; disparity

## Meaning

凹凸 names unevenness: raised and sunken parts of a surface. It also names an
imbalance or disparity. It is a noun, not an ordinary な-adjective.

- この壁には凹凸があります。 (このかべにはおうとつがあります。) - This wall has an uneven surface.
- 表面の凹凸を調べました。 (ひょうめんのおうとつをしらべました。) - We examined the surface's unevenness.

凹凸のある表面 (おうとつのあるひょうめん) means "a surface with bumps and hollows."

## How the kanji combine

[凹](../kanji/凹.md) contributes the sunken part and [凸](../kanji/凸.md) the raised part.
The contrasting parts together name the uneven whole. The on readings are オウ + トツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[でこぼこ](でこぼこ.md), also written 凸凹, overlaps in physical unevenness.
It also has an imbalance or mismatch sense.
Notice the reversed character order and learn each whole word with its reading.
[ごつごつ](ごつごつ.md) emphasizes hard, angular irregularities;
[ざらざら](ざらざら.md) can describe finer-grained roughness.
平ら (たいら), "flat; level," provides a useful contrast for a surface.

## Distinctive meaning

Unevenness need not feel gritty: a smooth, wavy surface can still have raised and sunken parts.
The abstract disparity sense is not literal roughness under the fingers.
Do not turn every でこぼこ expression into 凹凸 merely by reversing the characters.
