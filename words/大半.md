# 大半 (たいはん) - majority; most

## Meaning

大半 names more than half or most of something. As a noun, use **Nの大半**, "most of N," or **大半のN**, "most N." It can also work adverbially as "mostly; largely."

- 参加者の大半は学生でした。 (さんかしゃのたいはんはがくせいでした。) - Most of the participants were students.
- 作業は大半終わりました。 (さぎょうはたいはんおわりました。) - The work is mostly finished.

The first example identifies the majority of a group. The second describes how much of the work has been completed.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes "large," while [半](../kanji/半.md) contributes "half." Together they name the greater share, not exactly one half. Learn the established reading **たいはん**, with たい rather than だい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大部分](大部分.md) (だいぶぶん) closely overlaps in meaning, "the greater part; most." [半分](半分.md) (はんぶん) means half, while [全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ) means all. These useful quantity contrasts should not be collapsed into synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

大半 does not provide an exact percentage, nor does it mean that every member of the group qualifies. It can describe people, time, materials, or work, depending on the noun. If a precise threshold matters, state the number rather than expecting 大半 to supply it.
