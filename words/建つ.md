# 建つ (たつ) - be built; be erected

## Meaning

建つ describes a building or similar structure being built or
coming into existence. It is intransitive: the structure takes が.
The godan forms include 建ちます, 建った, 建って, and 建たない.

- 駅の近くに新しい病院が建ちました。
  (えきのちかくにあたらしいびょういんがたちました。) - A new hospital was built near the station.
- ここには以前、古い家が建っていました。
  (ここにはいぜん、ふるいいえがたっていました。) - An old house used to stand here.

建っている can describe the resulting standing structure.
The location can take に.

## How the kanji combine

[建](../kanji/建.md) supplies construction and the native reading
た, followed by つ. This is one verb, not a passive ending added
to 建てる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[建てる](建てる.md) (たてる) is transitive: 家を建てる,
"build a house," versus 家が建つ, "a house is built."
[立つ](立つ.md), also たつ, is used for a person standing.
Shared pronunciation does not make these spellings interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

English "be erected" describes the structure's emergence, without
requiring the sentence to name a builder. Do not use 家を建つ
for "build a house." The source-list gloss includes "to erect,"
but the applicable dictionary entry is intransitive.
