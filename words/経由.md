# 経由 (けいゆ) - via; going by way of

## Meaning

経由 describes going through an intermediate place or channel.
Use place + 経由で to say "via that place," or place + を経由する to describe the route as a verb.
The destination remains separate from the place passed through.

- 京都経由で大阪へ行きます。 (きょうとけいゆでおおさかへいきます。) - I will go to Osaka via Kyoto.
- このバスは駅を経由します。 (このバスはえきをけいゆします。) - This bus goes via the station.

In the second example, を marks a route point, rather than a thing changed by an action.
On travel information, 東京経由 (とうきょうけいゆ) can stand alone as "via Tokyo."

## How the kanji combine

[経](../kanji/経.md) contributes passing through, and [由](../kanji/由.md) contributes the idea of a way
or route. Their meanings combine around the path taken.
This card teaches ケイ + ユ, けいゆ; the dictionary also records けいゆう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通る](通る.md) (とおる), "pass through," is a useful everyday comparison.
[途中](途中.md) (とちゅう) names being partway through a route or activity, rather than specifying
the route with "via." [直接](直接.md) (ちょくせつ), "directly," can contrast with an intermediate route.

## Distinctive meaning

経由 tells you how the destination is reached, not necessarily that the traveler changes vehicles
at the intermediate point. A bus can travel via a station while passengers remain aboard.
The word also applies to channels through which information or goods pass.
