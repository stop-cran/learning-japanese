# 暇 (ひま) — free time; not busy

## Meaning

暇 is free time or leisure. It also works as a な-adjective meaning free, unoccupied, or not busy: 暇な日 (ひまなひ), "a day when one is free."

- 日曜日は暇です。 (にちようびはひまです。) — I am free on Sunday.
- 本を読む暇がありません。 (ほんをよむひまがありません。) — I do not have time to read a book.

## How the kanji combine

暇 is one kanji with the native reading ひま. Kana ひま is also used. The reading taught here is not the reading of 暇 in every fixed expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

忙しい (いそがしい), "busy," is the practical opposite. 自由時間 (じゆうじかん) means time set aside for free use. 休み (やすみ) is a break or day off, which is not necessarily unoccupied.

## Distinctive meaning

暇 can imply having nothing to do, so asking a professional 暇ですか may sound less tactful than asking whether they have a moment. 暇がある focuses on available time; 暇です describes someone's unoccupied state.
