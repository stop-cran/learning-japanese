# ざわざわ — crowd murmuring; rustling

## Meaning

A spreading murmur or commotion, rustling leaves, or a related unsettled feeling.

- 開演（かいえん）前の会場（かいじょう）はざわざわしていた。 — The venue was buzzing with conversation before the performance.
- 風で木の葉（このは）がざわざわ鳴（な）った。 — The leaves rustled in the wind.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[がやがや](がやがや.md) overlaps in a noisy crowd. [さらさら](さらさら.md) can overlap in lighter rustling. [しーん](しーん.md) offers a hushed-silence contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

ざわざわする need not mean a loud argument: many little voices can make the impression. The dictionary also records a chill-like feeling. Neither a crowd nor leaves are required in every use.
