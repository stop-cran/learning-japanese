# ファスナー - zipper; zip fastener

## Meaning

ファスナー is a zipper or zip fastener, for example on clothing or
a bag. It is a noun, not a verb meaning to fasten anything.

- 上着のファスナーを閉めました。 (うわぎのファスナーをしめました。) - I closed the zipper on my jacket.
- かばんのファスナーが壊れました。 (かばんのファスナーがこわれました。) - The zipper on my bag broke.

Use ファスナーを開ける (ファスナーをあける) for opening a zipper.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ジッパー and チャック are other dictionary-listed names for a
zipper. [ボタン](ボタン.md), a button, can also close clothing,
but is a different fastening device rather than a synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The borrowing comes from English "fastener," but the selected
Japanese meaning is narrower: a zip fastener.
Do not assume that every screw, clip, button, or other device
called a fastener in English is a ファスナー.
