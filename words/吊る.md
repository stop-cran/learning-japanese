# 吊る (つる) - hang; suspend

## Meaning

吊る means to hang or suspend something, for example with a rope or other support from above. In this use it is transitive: the suspended thing takes を.

- クレーンで荷物を吊りました。
  (クレーンでにもつをつりました。) - We suspended the load with a crane.
- けがをした腕を布で吊っています。
  (けがをしたうでをぬのでつっています。) - I am supporting my injured arm in a cloth sling.

Despite ending in る, this is a godan verb: 吊ります (つります), 吊って (つって), and 吊らない (つらない).

## How the kanji combine

吊 supplies the hanging or suspending meaning. The kana る completes this native verb; it is not an on-reading compound. Keep the spelling distinct from [釣る](釣る.md), also つる, which concerns fishing or luring.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[吊るす](吊るす.md) (つるす) also means hanging something up. [掛ける](掛ける.md) (かける) and [下げる](下げる.md) (さげる) overlap in hanging uses but have broader meanings. Do not infer a strict size or weight boundary between 吊る and 吊るす.

## Distinctive meaning

This lesson focuses on suspending an object. The same dictionary family contains other uses, including sumo and kana-written descriptions of something being pulled tight. Those do not make every occurrence transitive or give the fishing spelling the same meaning.
