# ロビー - lobby; lounge

## Meaning

ロビー is a lobby or lounge, such as an entrance-associated
reception or waiting area in a hotel, theater or other building.

- ホテルのロビーで待ち合わせました。 (ホテルのロビーでまちあわせました。) - We arranged to meet in the hotel lobby.
- 開演までロビーで待っていました。 (かいえんまでロビーでまっていました。) - We waited in the lobby until the performance began.

で marks where the meeting or waiting takes place.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[待合室](待合室.md) (まちあいしつ) is a waiting room;
[玄関](玄関.md) (げんかん) is an entrance or entrance hall.
Their spaces can overlap in a particular building.
[廊下](廊下.md) (ろうか) instead names a connecting corridor.
ラウンジ can also mean lounge, but has other uses of its own.

## Distinctive meaning

English hall can name several spaces. ロビー is not the ordinary
name for every corridor or for the physical entrance door.
It can also refer to an anteroom associated with a legislature,
as recorded in the Japanese dictionary; the examples here teach
the practical building-space use, not every English political
verb or noun associated with lobbying.
