# 入れる (いれる) — put in

## Meaning

入れる means to put something in or let someone in. It is a transitive ichidan verb: 入れます, 入れて, 入れない. The thing put in takes を; its destination takes に.

かばんに本を入れます。（かばんにほんをいれます。） — I put a book in my bag.

部屋に猫を入れないでください。（へやにねこをいれないでください。） — Please do not let the cat into the room.

## How the kanji combine

[入](../kanji/入.md) expresses entering. In this verb it is read い, with れる written as okurigana. The conjugation base is 入れ（いれ）, not just the written kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

入る（はいる） describes entering: 猫が入る, "the cat goes in." 出す（だす）, "take out," contrasts with putting something in.

## Distinctive meaning

入れる presents someone as causing the entry. It is not the potential form "can enter" of 入る; that form is 入れる read はいれる.
