# 終える (おえる) - finish; complete something

## Meaning

終える means to finish or bring something to an end. In this use it is a transitive ichidan verb: the task or activity takes を. Its forms include 終えます (おえます), 終えた (おえた), and 終えない (おえない).

- 仕事を終えて、家に帰りました。 (しごとをおえて、いえにかえりました。) - I finished work and went home.
- 昨日、その小説を読み終えました。 (きのう、そのしょうせつをよみおえました。) - Yesterday I finished reading that novel.

The second example uses verb stem + 終える: 読みます gives 読み, then 読み終える. It expresses completing that action, not merely stopping partway through.

## How the kanji combine

[終](../kanji/終.md) supplies the idea of an end. The native reading and ending make おえる; there is no second written component. Keep the え in forms such as 終えた.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[終わる](終わる.md) (おわる) can present the event itself as ending: 仕事が終わる, "work ends." Compare 仕事を終える, "finish work." [始める](始める.md) (はじめる), "begin something," contrasts with completion; [止める](止める.md) (やめる), "quit; stop doing," can describe stopping without completing.

## Distinctive meaning

The useful learner pattern is someone completing a task, even if the subject is unstated. A dictionary also records a rare intransitive use, but that is not a reason to ignore the を/が contrast in these ordinary examples. Choose the construction as well as the English gloss.
