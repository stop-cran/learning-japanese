# 輪 (わ) - ring; loop; circle

## Meaning

輪 is a noun for a ring, loop, circle, or hoop. It can refer to an object's shape or to people arranged in a circle. It also describes a social circle.

- ひもで輪を作りました。 (ひもでわをつくりました。) - I made a loop with the string.
- 子どもたちは輪になって座りました。 (こどもたちはわになってすわりました。) - The children sat in a circle.

輪を作る means to make a ring or circle; 輪になる describes forming one. 友達の輪 (ともだちのわ) means "a circle of friends."

## How the kanji combine

輪 is a single kanji read as the native noun わ. The whole word gives the circular-form meaning. Its on reading リン in compounds is not a replacement for わ in 輪になる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[円](円.md) (えん) names a circle in geometry and also the currency unit yen. 輪 is useful for a loop or ring-shaped arrangement rather than a filled circular area.

[指輪](指輪.md) (ゆびわ) specifically names a finger ring. 輪 alone does not automatically mean jewelry.

## Distinctive meaning

The social sense does not require people to sit in a physical circle: 友達の輪が広がる (ともだちのわがひろがる) means "one's circle of friends expands." Context distinguishes a loop of material, a hoop, an arrangement of people, and the group they form.
