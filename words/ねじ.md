# ねじ - screw; clock spring

## Meaning

ねじ is a screw, a threaded fastener.
ねじを締める (ねじをしめる) means tightening a screw;
ねじを緩める (ねじをゆるめる) means loosening one.

- ドライバーでねじを締めました。
  (ドライバーでねじをしめました。) - I tightened the screw with a screwdriver.
- このねじは長すぎます。
  (このねじはながすぎます。) - This screw is too long.

The tool takes で in the first example. The mark を identifies the screw
being tightened, not the screwdriver.

## How the kanji combine

This article uses the usual kana spelling ねじ; ネジ is also recorded.
螺子, 捻子 and 螺旋 are rare kanji forms in the inspected dictionary family.
Do not assign the readings of those characters separately to the kana word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[釘](釘.md) (くぎ), "nail," is another fastener, but not a synonym:
a screw has a thread and is turned to fasten it.
ドライバー is the screwdriver used to turn the screw,
not another name for the screw itself.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional sense is a winding key or spring in a clock or toy.
時計のねじを巻く (とけいのねじをまく), "wind a clock,"
uses [巻く](巻く.md) (まく). It is not necessarily tightening a mounting screw
on the outside of the clock.
The screw and winding-mechanism senses share the word but name different parts.
