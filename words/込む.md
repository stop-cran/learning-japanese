# 込む (こむ) - become crowded

## Meaning

こむ means to become crowded or congested. The source spelling is 込む; for this independent crowding sense, 混む is usual. It is an intransitive godan verb: こみます, こんで, こんだ and こまない.

- 朝の電車は混んでいます。 (あさのでんしゃはこんでいます。) - The morning train is crowded.
- この道は夕方になると混みます。 (このみちはゆうがたになるとこみます。) - This road gets congested in the evening.

## How the kanji combine

[込](../kanji/込.md) writes こ in 込む; む is okurigana. Keep the assigned spelling recognizable, but learn the crowding examples with ordinary 混む.

## Synonyms and antonyms

空く (すく), "become less crowded," gives the useful contrast: 電車が空いている means the train is not crowded.

## Distinctive meaning

The ending ～込む also attaches to a verb's ます-stem, as in 入り込む (はいりこむ), "go into." That suffix has several meanings and is not simply "be crowded" added to another verb.

For ～込む as a verb extension, see [the article on verb extensions](../articles/verb-extensions-direction-and-aspect.md).
