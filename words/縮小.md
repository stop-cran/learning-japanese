# 縮小 (しゅくしょう) - scaling down; reduction

## Meaning

縮小 is making something smaller or reducing its scale or extent. 縮小する has both transitive and intransitive uses: **Xを縮小する**, "scale down X," and **Xが縮小する**, "X becomes smaller."

- この画像を縮小してください。
  (このがぞうをしゅくしょうしてください。) - Please reduce the size of this image.
- 事業の規模が縮小しています。
  (じぎょうのきぼがしゅくしょうしています。) - The scale of the business is shrinking.

The first example asks someone to change an image; the second describes a reduction without naming who causes it.

## How the kanji combine

縮 contributes shrinking or contracting, and [小](../kanji/小.md) smallness. Their related contributions express reducing size. The readings are シュク + ショウ: しゅく is short, while しょう has a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[減らす](減らす.md) (へらす) and [減る](減る.md) (へる) concern reducing an amount or a quantity decreasing. [圧縮](圧縮.md) (あっしゅく) includes compression and reduction, but shrinking an image's displayed dimensions is not necessarily the same operation as compressing its data.

[拡大](拡大.md) (かくだい), enlargement or expansion, gives the direct size-and-scale contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

縮小 is not limited to reducing a numerical count: it can concern image dimensions, a range, or organizational activities. It does not require reducing something to nothing. JMdict's 縮少 is search-only; retain 縮小 as the teaching spelling.
