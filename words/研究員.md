# 研究員 (けんきゅういん) — research staff member

## Meaning

研究員 is a researcher or research staff member associated with an organization, laboratory, or
institution. The specific duties and employment status depend on the position.

- 彼女は大学の研究員です。 (かのじょは だいがくの けんきゅういんです。) — She is a research staff member at a university.
- 研究員として働いています。 (けんきゅういんとして はたらいています。) — I work as a researcher.

## How the kanji combine

[研究](研究.md) けんきゅう names research: [研](../kanji/研.md) concerns studying or refining,
and [究](../kanji/究.md) investigating thoroughly. [員](../kanji/員.md) イン adds the staff or
organizational-member role. This is activity + member, with on readings ケン + キュウ + イン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[研究者](研究者.md) (けんきゅうしゃ) identifies a person by research activity rather than a particular staff role.
It can describe the same person. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

研究員 does not universally mean a junior researcher, an assistant, or a permanent employee.
Institutions may use it as a precise job title, but their job structures are not encoded by 員 alone.

For 研究員 compared with 研究者, see
[occupation and role endings](../articles/occupation-and-role-endings.md).
