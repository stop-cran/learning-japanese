# 以降 (いこう) - from a point onward; since

## Meaning

以降 marks the period from a given point onward. The reference point may be
past or future; the word is not limited to plans.

- 四月以降、新しい料金になります。 (しがついこう、あたらしいりょうきんになります。) - The new prices apply from April onward.
- それ以降、連絡はありません。 (それいこう、れんらくはありません。) - There has been no contact since then.

Use の before a following noun: 来週以降の予定
(らいしゅういこうのよてい), "plans for next week onward."

## How the kanji combine

[以](../kanji/以.md) supplies the reference point. [降](../kanji/降.md),
also associated with descending, has the established later-in-time role in
this compound. Learn イ + コウ as a temporal expression, not as physical descent.
已降 is a dictionary-listed rare spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[以後](以後.md) (いご) is closely related. Both can mark the period after a stated
point; do not invent a strict past-versus-future split.
[以来](以来.md) (いらい) particularly suits continuing or repeated situations
since an earlier event. [以前](以前.md) (いぜん) points in the opposite direction.

## Distinctive meaning

A stated date is normally included: 四月以降 includes April, not only May and later.
This is the ordinary date-boundary convention, not a promise that every event
description has an identical boundary interpretation. State an exact date when
a deadline or eligibility rule needs greater precision.
