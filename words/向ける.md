# 向ける (むける) - turn toward; direct

## Meaning

向ける is a transitive ichidan verb: turn, point, or direct something toward a target. Use **thing を + target に向ける**.

- カメラを山に向けました。 (カメラをやまにむけました。) - I pointed the camera toward the mountain.
- 彼女は窓の方に顔を向けました。 (かのじょはまどのほうにかおをむけました。) - She turned her face toward the window.

The object is what changes direction, not the destination. Useful forms are 向けます, 向けて, and 向けない.

## How the kanji combine

[向](../kanji/向.md) contributes facing or direction. The character writes む here, with ける completing the native verb. It is related to [向く](向く.md) (むく), "face; turn."

## Synonyms and antonyms

Compare 顔を向ける, "turn one's face," with 顔がこちらを向いている (かおがこちらをむいている), "the face is turned this way." The first explicitly handles the face as an object. そらす, "avert; turn away," contrasts with directing one's gaze toward something.

## Distinctive meaning

The direction can be figurative: 問題に注意を向ける (もんだいにちゅういをむける), "direct attention to a problem." There is no physical object being rotated in that expression. Keep track of the を-marked object and に-marked target instead of translating 向ける as an unspecified "turn."
