# 有難い (ありがたい) - grateful; appreciated

## Meaning

有難い describes something welcome or appreciated and the grateful feeling it
evokes. The dictionary marks it as usually written in kana: ありがたい.
It is an i-adjective: ありがたかった, "was appreciated"; ありがたくない, "not welcome."

- 手伝ってくれて、ありがたいです。 (てつだってくれて、ありがたいです。) - I am grateful for your help.
- 忙しい時に助けてもらえるのは、有難いことです。 (いそがしいときにたすけてもらえるのは、ありがたいことです。) - Being able to get help when I am busy is something I appreciate.

English "grateful" often describes the person feeling thanks; the Japanese examples
evaluate the help as welcome and express the speaker's appreciation.

## How the kanji combine

The spellings 有難い and 有り難い both have the reading ありがたい.
As a learning association, [有](../kanji/有.md), "exist; have," and
[難](../kanji/難.md), "difficult," can suggest something not to take for granted.
Do not translate a modern sentence mechanically as "difficult to exist."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[感謝](感謝.md) (かんしゃ) names gratitude; 感謝する means feeling or expressing it.
[ありがとう](ありがとう.md) directly thanks someone. They overlap in appreciation,
but their grammatical forms are not interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not merely call a favor useful: it can convey the recipient's
thankfulness. Calling assistance ありがたい is different from saying that a task is
[難しい](難しい.md) (むずかしい), "difficult," despite the shared character in the written forms.
