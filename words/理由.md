# 理由 (りゆう) — reason; grounds

## Meaning

理由 is the reason for an action, decision, or situation. It can be a motive, explanatory ground, or stated excuse. A verb clause can modify it directly.

遅れた理由を説明してください。 (おくれたりゆうをせつめいしてください。) — Please explain why you were late.

## How the kanji combine

[理](../kanji/理.md) contributes reason or principle, and [由](../kanji/由.md) contributes a cause or basis. Their on readings form り + ゆう. Write full-size ゆ: りゆう, not りゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

原因 (げんいん) emphasizes the cause producing a result. わけ can also mean reason or circumstances and appears in many grammatical expressions. 言い訳 (いいわけ) more specifically suggests an excuse or justification.

## Distinctive meaning

A 理由 may explain a person's choice without being a physical cause. Use noun + の理由, as in 欠席の理由 (けっせきのりゆう), "the reason for an absence." The noun does not guarantee that the stated explanation is true or convincing.
