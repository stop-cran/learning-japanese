# 唇 (くちびる) - lip; lips

## Meaning

唇 names a lip or the lips around the opening of the mouth. Japanese does not require a plural ending to refer to both lips.

- 冬は唇が乾燥しやすいです。 (ふゆはくちびるがかんそうしやすいです。) - My lips tend to get dry in winter.
- 唇に口紅を塗りました。 (くちびるにくちべにをぬりました。) - I put lipstick on my lips.

上唇 (うわくちびる) is the upper lip; 下唇 (したくちびる) is the lower lip.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 唇 is read くちびる as a whole native word. There is no 唇 kanji card in the current collection. 脣 is a rare kanji spelling recorded in the dictionary.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[口](口.md) (くち) means mouth, not specifically the lips. [口紅](口紅.md) (くちべに) is lipstick, a cosmetic applied to 唇.

Upper and lower lips are contrasting locations, not separate opposites of the word 唇.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 唇 when the lip itself matters: its dryness, movement or color. It is not the general word for an opening, an entrance or a person's way of speaking, even though 口 can have broader uses.
