# 出入り (でいり) - coming and going; incomings and outgoings

## Meaning

出入り is movement out and in, or coming and going. It also concerns regular visits or dealings with a place. 出入りする is useful for that movement or association.

- この建物は人の出入りが多いです。
  (このたてものはひとのでいりがおおいです。) - Many people come and go in this building.
- その業者は以前からこの会社に出入りしています。
  (そのぎょうしゃはいぜんからこのかいしゃにでいりしています。) - That supplier has been making regular visits to this company for some time.

The noun can also mean money coming in and going out, as in お金の出入りを記録する (おかねのでいりをきろくする), "record receipts and payments." In figures, 出入り can concern fluctuation or a difference in amount.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md), で, contributes going out, and [入](../kanji/入.md), いり, coming in. The opposing directions combine as one native word. Learn the complete reading でいり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出入口](出入口.md) (でいりぐち) names an entrance and exit, rather than the movement through it. [収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう) and [支出](支出.md) (ししゅつ) separately identify incoming and outgoing money. [増減](増減.md) (ぞうげん) overlaps in numerical fluctuation.

## Distinctive meaning

出入りしている can describe a regular visitor's connection with an organization, not just their position in a doorway. The pinned entry restricts でいり to the spelling 出入り and also records ではいり. Use 出入り for this card; the entry's spelling list is not a rule prohibiting other spellings in all Japanese sources.
