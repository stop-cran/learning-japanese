# 植える (うえる) — plant

## Meaning

植える means to plant a tree, flower, seedling, or crop. The thing planted takes を; the place where it is planted takes に.

- 庭に木を植えました。 (にわにきをうえました。) — I planted a tree in the garden.
- 春に花を植えます。 (はるにはなをうえます。) — I plant flowers in spring.

It is a transitive ichidan verb: 植えます (うえます), 植えて (うえて), 植えない (うえない).

## How the kanji combine

植 supplies the planting meaning and the reading う. The written ending is える; the conjugating stem is 植え (うえ), not just the portion written in kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

育てる (そだてる), "raise; grow," focuses on nurturing something. 植える focuses on putting it in place to grow. 抜く (ぬく), "pull out," describes a contrasting action.

## Distinctive meaning

English "grow vegetables" can mean cultivating them over time, for which 野菜を育てる (やさいをそだてる) is useful. 植える does not by itself describe all the care after planting.
