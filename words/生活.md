# 生活 (せいかつ) — life and living

## Meaning

生活 concerns life as it is lived: daily routines, circumstances, and the business of living.
It is a noun and also forms the verb 生活する, "live; lead one's life."

- 日本で生活しています。 (にほんでせいかつしています。) — I live in Japan.
- 新しい生活が始まりました。 (あたらしいせいかつがはじまりました。) — A new life has begun.

The first sentence uses で for the place where daily life takes place.
The second can describe a new way of living, not the birth of a new person.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) brings the idea of life, and [活](../kanji/活.md) brings living and activity.
The related meanings reinforce one another, directing attention to living one's life.
The on readings are セイ + カツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

暮らし (くらし) overlaps with 生活 in referring to day-to-day life or living circumstances.
人生 (じんせい), "one's life," provides a different perspective: it can frame a person's life as a whole
rather than focusing on their present routines.
These are useful contrasts in focus, not a rule that the words can never describe the same experience.

## Distinctive meaning

生活 is not simply a biological label for being alive.
日本で生活する tells us about carrying on life in Japan; the emphasis is not just where a person happens to be standing.
The word also does not by itself say whether that life is pleasant, difficult, busy, or quiet.
