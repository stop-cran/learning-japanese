# お辞儀 (おじぎ) - bow; bowing

## Meaning

お辞儀 is a bow or bowing. Use お辞儀をする or お辞儀する for the action. The person bowed to can take に.

- 先生にお辞儀をしました。 (せんせいにおじぎをしました。) - I bowed to the teacher.
- 彼は深くお辞儀をして、部屋を出ました。 (かれはふかくおじぎをして、へやをでました。) - He bowed deeply and left the room.

The expression describes the bodily gesture; its purpose can be a greeting, thanks, respect, or an apology.

## How the kanji combine

The spelling 御辞儀 also represents おじぎ: [御](../kanji/御.md) writes the prefix お. In お辞儀, that prefix is written in kana. The base 辞儀 has the on-readings ジ + ギ; [辞](../kanji/辞.md) has speech-related associations and 儀 concerns formal conduct. Learn the established bowing meaning rather than a literal translation such as "refusal ceremony."

The kango grouping describes the base 辞儀, not the origin of the Japanese prefix.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[礼](礼.md) (れい) also names a bow, as in 礼をする (れいをする). [礼儀](礼儀.md) (れいぎ) concerns manners or etiquette more generally, not one particular bow.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that every お辞儀 expresses gratitude: the gesture's social meaning depends on the situation. Nor does the polite prefix prescribe one correct angle or duration for every occasion. This article teaches the word and its basic construction, not a complete etiquette system.
