# 宗教 (しゅうきょう) - religion

## Meaning

宗教 is religion, a religious faith, or religious affiliation. It is a noun. Use 宗教を信じる (しゅうきょうをしんじる), "believe in a religion," or 宗教について学ぶ (しゅうきょうについてまなぶ), "learn about religion."

- 世界の宗教について勉強しています。 (せかいのしゅうきょうについてべんきょうしています。) - I am studying the religions of the world.
- 特定の宗教は信じていません。 (とくていのしゅうきょうはしんじていません。) - I do not believe in any particular religion.

The negative sentence reports the speaker's affiliation or belief; it is not a judgment about other people's beliefs.

## How the kanji combine

宗 contributes a central doctrine or religious tradition, and [教](../kanji/教.md) contributes teaching. Together they name religion as a system of teachings and belief. The on readings are シュウ + キョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[信仰](信仰.md) (しんこう) emphasizes faith or devotion. 宗教 can name the religious tradition itself, while 信仰 can focus on a person's belief. [信じる](信じる.md) (しんじる) is the broader verb "believe; trust."

## Distinctive meaning

Not every English "belief" is a 宗教: believing tomorrow's weather forecast does not constitute a religion. Likewise, the noun alone does not tell you how strongly a person practices a faith. 宗教の違い (しゅうきょうのちがい), "differences in religion," uses の rather than な to connect these nouns.
