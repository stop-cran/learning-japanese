# 禁煙 (きんえん) - no smoking; quitting smoking

## Meaning

禁煙 has two closely related uses: a person's abstaining from smoking and a rule prohibiting smoking in a place. On a sign, 禁煙 means "No smoking."

- この店は禁煙です。 (このみせはきんえんです。) - This establishment is non-smoking.
- 父は先月から禁煙しています。 (ちちはせんげつからきんえんしています。) - My father has not been smoking since last month.

The first sentence states a rule for the premises. The second describes someone's continuing effort or state of abstinence. 禁煙する does not need an object such as たばこを.

## How the kanji combine

禁 contributes prohibition or restraint; [煙](../kanji/煙.md), "smoke," stands for tobacco smoking in this combination. The relation is restricting smoking, read キン + エン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[煙](煙.md) (けむり) is smoke itself. 喫煙 (きつえん) means smoking; a 喫煙所 (きつえんじょ) is a designated smoking area. These contrast with 禁煙, but 喫煙 by itself does not grant permission in every location.

## Distinctive meaning

Look at the subject and setting before translating. A restaurant's 禁煙 does not mean that the restaurant has overcome a personal habit, and a person's 禁煙 does not automatically impose a rule on everyone nearby.
