# 秒 (びょう) - second of time

## Meaning

秒 is a second as a unit of time: sixty seconds make one minute. It follows a number in expressions such as 一秒 (いちびょう), "one second," and 三十秒 (さんじゅうびょう), "thirty seconds."

- 三十秒待ってください。 (さんじゅうびょうまってください。) - Please wait thirty seconds.
- あと五秒で始まります。 (あとごびょうではじまります。) - It will start in another five seconds.

In the first sentence, the duration can directly accompany 待つ without に. In the second, あと五秒で identifies the remaining time until the start.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 秒 supplies the unit with the on reading ビョウ. There is no compound to divide. Keep びょう together when reading a number plus the unit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

分 (ふん/ぷん) is a minute, a different unit rather than a synonym or opposite. [時間](時間.md) (じかん) can describe time or hours. A second is not "second place": ranking requires a different expression.

## Distinctive meaning

秒 can occur in a duration or within a clock reading. 三十秒待つ measures waiting; 三時二分十秒 (さんじにふんじゅうびょう) specifies a clock time. Do not confuse the unit with an ordinal number just because English uses "second" for both. The dictionary also records an angular second, but that requires a measurement context beyond ordinary timekeeping.
