# 需要 (じゅよう) - demand; need

## Meaning

需要 is a noun for demand or need, including demand for goods or services in economics. Use **X の需要** or **X に対する需要** to identify what is wanted. **需要がある** means "there is demand."

- この地域では保育サービスの需要が高まっています。 (このちいきではほいくサービスのじゅようがたかまっています。) - Demand for childcare services is growing in this area.
- 需要が供給を上回りました。 (じゅようがきょうきゅうをうわまわりました。) - Demand exceeded supply.

Useful combinations include 需要が増える (じゅようがふえる), "demand increases," and 需要が減る (じゅようがへる), "demand decreases."

## How the kanji combine

需 contributes needing or seeking; [要](../kanji/要.md) contributes need or requirement. The components reinforce the idea of something being wanted or required. The readings are ジュ + ヨウ: じゅよう, not じゅうよう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[供給](供給.md) (きょうきゅう), "supply," is the economic counterpart. [必要](必要.md) (ひつよう) describes necessity or need more broadly. [依頼](依頼.md) (いらい) is a request or commission addressed to someone.

## Distinctive meaning

需要 is not the act of saying "please do this." It describes the demand that exists, whether or not a particular person has voiced a request. This distinction prevents confusing market demand with requesting help. The homophone 受容 (じゅよう), "acceptance," has different kanji and meaning.
