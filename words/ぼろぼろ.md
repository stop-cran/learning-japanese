# ぼろぼろ — tattered; crumbling; worn out

## Meaning

Something worn or falling apart, material coming away in pieces, or a person feeling badly worn down.

- この古いかばんは、もうぼろぼろだ。 — This old bag is already falling apart.
- クッキーがぼろぼろ崩（くず）れた。 — The cookie crumbled into pieces.
- 忙しい一週間で、心も体もぼろぼろだ。 — After a busy week, I feel mentally and physically worn out.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぼそぼそ](ぼそぼそ.md) overlaps in dry, unappealing food. [くたくた](くたくた.md) and [へとへと](へとへと.md) overlap in exhaustion, without necessarily describing things falling into pieces.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare ぼろぼろのかばん with ぼろぼろ崩れる: a damaged state versus crumbling movement. 涙（なみだ）がぼろぼろこぼれる describes tears spilling in drops, not “tattered tears.”
