# そのまま (そのまま) - as it is; unchanged

## Meaning

そのまま means "as it is" or "without changing that state." It works adverbially before a verb, and そのままの can modify a noun.

- 机の上の書類は、そのままにしてください。 (つくえのうえのしょるいは、そのままにしてください。) - Please leave the documents on the desk as they are.
- 野菜は切らずに、そのまま焼きます。 (やさいはきらずに、そのままやきます。) - We roast the vegetables whole, without cutting them.

そのままにする means to leave something in the state being referred to. The second example specifies which change is not made.

## How the kanji combine

This kana expression combines [その](その.md), "that," with まま, which presents a continuing state. The dictionary records spellings with 儘, but the usual kana form is sufficient for this card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[変える](変える.md) (かえる), "change something," describes the contrasting action. 変えずに (かえずに), "without changing," can explain the basic idea.

このまま means "as things are now/like this," while そのまま refers back to the state being indicated or discussed.

## Distinctive meaning

そのまま does not inherently mean neglect. Leaving a carefully arranged table unchanged can be intentional. It can also describe proceeding without a pause: そのまま帰った (そのままかえった), "went straight home," when the context establishes where the person was and what they did next.
