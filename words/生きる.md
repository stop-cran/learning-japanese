# 生きる (いきる) - live; be alive

## Meaning

生きる means to live or remain alive. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 生きます, 生きて, 生きた and 生きない.

- この魚はまだ生きています。 (このさかなはまだいきています。) - This fish is still alive.
- 祖母は九十歳まで生きました。 (そぼはきゅうじゅっさいまでいきました。) - My grandmother lived to ninety.

生きている commonly describes the state of being alive, rather than a temporary activity.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) contributes life; きる is okurigana. For conjugation, remove only る to obtain 生き, the ichidan stem.

## Synonyms and antonyms

住む (すむ) means to reside somewhere: 日本に住む is "live in Japan." 死ぬ (しぬ), "die," is the relevant contrast to remaining alive.

## Distinctive meaning

English "live" covers both existence and residence; 生きる does not normally answer where someone lives. The ordinary spelling is 生きる; the dictionary also records the rarer 活きる.
