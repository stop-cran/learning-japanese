# ジェット機 (ジェットき) - jet airplane

## Meaning

ジェット機 is a noun meaning a jet airplane.
It identifies an aircraft by its jet propulsion, not by who owns it or whether it carries passengers.
Useful constructions include **ジェット機に乗る**, "board/ride in a jet,"
and **ジェット機で移動する**, "travel by jet."

- 空港で大きなジェット機を見ました。 (くうこうでおおきなジェットきをみました。) - I saw a large jet airplane at the airport.
- ジェット機が滑走路から飛び立ちました。 (ジェットきがかっそうろからとびたちました。) - A jet airplane took off from the runway.

The subject of the second sentence is the aircraft itself, marked by が.
Retain the small ッ in ジェット.

## How the kanji combine

This is a hybrid: the katakana loan element ジェット combines with [機](../kanji/機.md), read キ,
which identifies a machine or aircraft in such compounds.
The result is written with both scripts, not entirely in katakana.
The loan-based construction is classified here as gairaigo.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飛行機](飛行機.md) (ひこうき) is the broader word "airplane."
プロペラ機 (プロペラき), "propeller aircraft," provides a useful propulsion contrast.
[便](便.md) (びん) can identify a flight or service rather than the aircraft operating it.

## Distinctive meaning

ジェット機 is not limited to a private jet or a particular airline.
Passenger, cargo, or other uses require additional context.
Likewise, English "jet" can describe something other than an aircraft, but this compound's 機 fixes the airplane meaning.
Use 飛行機 when the propulsion distinction is not relevant.
