# 湯飲み (ゆのみ) - teacup

## Meaning

湯飲み is a teacup, especially the Japanese-style cup used for tea.
湯のみ, 湯呑み, 湯呑 and 湯飲 are recognized spellings of the same word.

- 湯飲みにお茶を注ぎました。 (ゆのみにおちゃをそそぎました。) - I poured tea into a teacup.
- お客様のために湯飲みを二つ用意しました。 (おきゃくさまのためにゆのみをふたつよういしました。) - I prepared two teacups for the guests.

## How the kanji combine

[湯](../kanji/湯.md), hot water, combines with
[飲](../kanji/飲.md), drinking. The native elements are ゆ + のみ,
the latter related to 飲む (のむ).
The word is shortened from 湯飲み茶碗 or 湯呑み茶碗
(ゆのみぢゃわん); the longer form has a voiced ぢゃ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[カップ](カップ.md) is a broader cup word.
[コップ](コップ.md) and [グラス](グラス.md) commonly name drinking
glasses, but are not governed by an absolute material-only boundary.
茶碗 (ちゃわん) can name a tea bowl or, in everyday contexts,
a rice bowl; the surrounding expression matters.

## Distinctive meaning

The characters do not mean that this cup holds only plain hot water.
湯飲み is a normal vessel for tea.
An often handleless Japanese-style teacup is a useful image,
not a universal definition that every カップ has a handle or that
every 湯飲み must be made of one material.
