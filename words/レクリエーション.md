# レクリエーション - recreation

## Meaning

レクリエーション is recreation: activities or rest that refresh people
and provide enjoyment outside their work or study.
It can refer to an organized recreational activity.

- 研修の後で、レクリエーションに参加しました。 (けんしゅうのあとで、レクリエーションにさんかしました。) - After the training, I joined the recreational activities.
- 職場のレクリエーションでゲームをしました。 (しょくばのレクリエーションでゲームをしました。) - We played games as a workplace recreational activity.

It is a noun; the examples use に参加する, participate in,
and で to identify the setting.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana borrowing associated with English recreation.
The source spelling レクリェーション belongs to the same pinned
dictionary family. レクレーション and リクリエーション
are also recorded forms. This card uses レクリエーション as its headword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[娯楽](娯楽.md) (ごらく) is entertainment or recreation;
[遊び](遊び.md) (あそび) is play.
[休養](休養.md) (きゅうよう) emphasizes recuperative rest.
[レジャー](レジャー.md) can concern free time and its recreational use.
These perspectives overlap without requiring the same activity in every case.

## Distinctive meaning

Recreation need not be a competitive sport or a children's game.
Do not silently import "creating something again": the pinned dictionary's
separate re-creation sense is restricted to リクリエーション,
not licensed for every spelling above. The source レクリェーション
is mapped here for recreation, not for that restricted sense.
