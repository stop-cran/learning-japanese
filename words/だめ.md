# だめ — no good; not allowed

## Meaning

だめ says that something is no good, will not work, or is not allowed. It behaves as a na-adjective: だめな方法 (だめなほうほう), "an ineffective method," and だめです, "that won't do."

- この方法ではだめです。 (このほうほうではだめです。) — This method won't work.
- ここに車を止めてはだめです。 (ここにくるまをとめてはだめです。) — You must not park here.

Verb て-form + はだめ expresses prohibition. In casual speech, 止めちゃだめ (とめちゃだめ) contracts 止めてはだめ.

## How the kanji combine

The usual kana word can be written 駄目. This is a mixed on-plus-kun reading: 駄 is ダ and [目](../kanji/目.md) is め. The kango category follows the on-first convention; the everyday meaning belongs to the whole expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

いけない overlaps in prohibitions. いい can indicate that something is acceptable, but is not the opposite of every sense of だめ.

## Distinctive meaning

A sharp だめ！ means "No!" or "Stop!" Its force depends on context and tone. Saying that a plan will not work and forbidding an action are related but different uses.
