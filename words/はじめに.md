# はじめに - first of all; introduction

## Meaning

はじめに means "first," "first of all," or "at the beginning."
It can introduce the initial step in a sequence.
As a noun, especially a book heading, it can also mean an introduction or preface.

- 初めに、名前を書いてください。 (はじめに、なまえをかいてください。) - First, please write your name.
- この本の「はじめに」を読みました。 (このほんの「はじめに」をよみました。) - I read this book's introduction.

The first example orders actions; the second names a part of a book.
The same English "introduction" does not make these the act of introducing one person to another.

## How the kanji combine

初めに and 始めに are recognized spellings at はじめに.
[初](../kanji/初.md) and [始](../kanji/始.md) both concern beginning,
but spelling choices depend on use; recognition is not a rule of free replacement in every context.
This kana-primary card covers both source forms without creating duplicate identities.
The first-step example uses 初めに, consistent with the spelling guidance discussed under [始め](始め.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[まず](まず.md) and [最初](最初.md) (さいしょ), in 最初に,
overlap when introducing the first action.
[初めて](初めて.md) (はじめて) instead describes doing something for the first time:
writing one's name first today need not be a first-ever experience.
[紹介](紹介.md) (しょうかい) names introducing or making something known, not every book's preface.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the complete expression rather than treating はじめに as a verb meaning "begin."
In a book title or heading, kana はじめに is a recognized noun use.
The source rows 始めに and 初めに share a dictionary family; the blank gloss
on one row does not establish a separate meaning.
