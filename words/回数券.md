# 回数券 (かいすうけん) - book of tickets; multi-use coupon tickets

## Meaning

回数券 is a set or book of tickets for a fixed number of uses, such as journeys or admissions. Such sets are often discounted, but the terms depend on the service.

- プールの回数券を買いました。 (プールのかいすうけんをかいました。) - I bought a set of admission tickets for the pool.
- この回数券は十回分です。 (このかいすうけんはじゅっかいぶんです。) - This ticket set covers ten uses.

十回分 means an amount for ten occasions. The example describes this set, not a rule that all 回数券 contain ten tickets.

## How the kanji combine

[回](../kanji/回.md) and [数](../kanji/数.md) form [回数](回数.md) (かいすう), "number of times." This modifies [券](../kanji/券.md), ケン, "ticket; coupon." Read the whole word かいすうけん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[券](券.md) (けん), [切符](切符.md) (きっぷ), and チケット are broader ticket words. 定期券 (ていきけん) is a period or commuter pass: a stated period and its conditions, rather than a fixed allotment of uses, define that product.

## Distinctive meaning

回数券 is not a guarantee of unlimited rides or visits. Check the number of uses, validity period, and service conditions. The word also does not establish that a particular transport operator currently sells such tickets.
