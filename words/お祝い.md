# お祝い (おいわい) - celebration; congratulatory gift

## Meaning

お祝い means congratulations or a celebration of a happy event. It can also mean a gift given for that occasion. お祝いをする means "celebrate."

友達の結婚のお祝いをします。 (ともだちのけっこんのおいわいをします。) - We will celebrate our friend's marriage.

これは入学のお祝いです。 (これはにゅうがくのおいわいです。) - This is a gift to celebrate your starting school.

## How the kanji combine

お adds politeness to 祝い (いわい), a noun related to 祝う (いわう), "celebrate." 祝 carries the celebration meaning, and い completes the written noun. The prefix is not part of the kanji's reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

プレゼント is a gift without necessarily naming a congratulatory occasion. おめでとう is what you can say to congratulate someone; お祝い names the congratulation, celebration, or gift.

## Distinctive meaning

Use occasion + の + お祝い to explain what is being celebrated, such as 誕生日のお祝い (たんじょうびのおいわい), "a birthday celebration." The context determines whether a party, gesture, or present is intended. There is no single useful lexical opposite.
