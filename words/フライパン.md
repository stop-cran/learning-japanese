# フライパン - frying pan

## Meaning

フライパン is a frying pan. It names the cookware, not the food or the act of frying.
Use フライパンで for the pan used in cooking.

- フライパンで卵を焼きます。 (フライパンでたまごをやきます。) - I cook an egg in a frying pan.
- 使ったフライパンを洗いました。 (つかったフライパンをあらいました。) - I washed the frying pan I had used.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana and has no kanji to combine.
Learn フライパン as the whole cookware name; the final パン does not mean bread here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鍋](鍋.md) (なべ) is a broader cooking-pot or pan word, also used for a hot-pot dish.
フライパン specifies a frying pan. A やかん is a kettle, another kitchen vessel
but not a synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun alone does not specify the pan's material, coating, size, or heat source.
Nor does it imply deep-frying: the egg example uses 焼く (やく).
Choose the cooking verb for the actual preparation.
