# キロ - kilo; kilogram or kilometer

## Meaning

キロ commonly shortens キログラム, "kilogram," or キロメートル, "kilometer." The situation tells you whether the quantity is weight or distance.

米を二キロ買いました。 (こめをにキロかいました。) - I bought two kilograms of rice.

駅まで三キロあります。 (えきまでさんキロあります。) - It is three kilometers to the station.

キロ follows the number and does not change for plural quantities.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana form represents the borrowed metric prefix kilo-, meaning one thousand. In the full unit names it precedes グラム or メートル. In the examples, it stands as the shortened unit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

キログラム and キロメートル remove the ambiguity. グラム and メートル are the corresponding base unit names in these expressions: one kilogram is 1,000 grams, and one kilometer is 1,000 meters.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that every キロ means kilograms simply because that is one common gloss. A road sign, walking distance, or journey favors kilometers; food weight favors kilograms. Use the full unit when the context does not make the measurement clear.
