# 章 (しょう) - chapter; badge

## Meaning

章 names a chapter or major section of a written work. Use の to connect it to a book, or a number to identify a particular chapter.

- この本は十の章に分かれています。 (このほんはじゅうのしょうにわかれています。) - This book is divided into ten chapters.
- 第三章を読んでください。 (だいさんしょうをよんでください。) - Please read chapter three.

第一章 (だいいっしょう) is "chapter one"; 第二章 (だいにしょう) is "chapter two." Notice the small っ in 第一章.

## How the kanji combine

章 is a single-kanji noun with on reading ショウ. It supplies the whole chapter meaning; there is no internal compound to split. Keep its long vowel distinct from a short しょ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

節 (せつ) can name a section, often a subdivision within a chapter in an organized text. A ページ is a page, a different unit: one chapter can occupy many pages. These are contrasts in organization, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

章 also has a badge, medal, or insignia sense, visible in compounds such as 校章 (こうしょう), "school badge/emblem," and 勲章 (くんしょう), "decoration; medal." That meaning is not a type of book chapter. The surrounding word or situation identifies which sense is intended.
