# ペン — pen

## Meaning

ペン is a pen, broadly an ink-based writing instrument. It does not identify a particular pen mechanism or ink color.

- ペンで名前を書いてください。 (ペンでなまえをかいてください。) — Please write your name in pen.
- ペンを一本貸してください。 (ペンをいっぽんかしてください。) — Please lend me a pen.

Use で for the instrument used in writing. Pens are commonly counted with 本 (ほん), with forms such as 一本 (いっぽん) and 二本 (にほん).

## How the kanji combine

ペン is a katakana borrowing of English "pen," with no kanji. The small circle in ペ distinguishes it from ベ and ヘ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ボールペン specifically means a ballpoint pen. 鉛筆 (えんぴつ), "pencil," is a useful writing-tool contrast, not a strict antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "pen" can also mean an animal enclosure, but this Japanese loanword's everyday sense is the writing tool. If instructions specify ペンで書く (ペンでかく), writing in pencil may not satisfy them.
