# 呼ぶ (よぶ) — call; summon

## Meaning

呼ぶ means to call out, call someone over, or summon them.
The person called can take を; a name can also be the object when you call it aloud.

- 大きな声で名前を呼びました。 (おおきなこえでなまえをよびました。) — I called the name out loudly.
- 先生を呼んでください。 (せんせいをよんでください。) — Please call the teacher over.

The polite form is 呼びます (よびます), and the て-form is 呼んで (よんで).

呼ぶ also means **invite** someone to a place or occasion. Use person + を and the destination or occasion + に.

- 誕生日に友達を家に呼びました。 (たんじょうびにともだちをいえによびました。) — I invited friends to my home for my birthday.

## How the kanji combine

[呼](../kanji/呼.md) supplies calling; ぶ is the ending of this native Japanese verb.
The reading よぶ is not assembled from multiple kanji. In 呼吸 (こきゅう), "breathing," the same character instead uses the on reading コ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[電話](電話.md) (でんわ) refers to a telephone call. 先生に電話する (せんせいにでんわする) is "phone the teacher"; 先生を呼ぶ is "summon the teacher."
The actions can overlap in a situation, but 呼ぶ does not specify using a telephone.
There is no single opposite that covers calling out, summoning, and naming.

## Distinctive meaning

Another use is calling someone or something by a designation:
この犬をポチと呼んでいます。 (このいぬをポチとよんでいます。) — We call this dog Pochi.
Here を marks the dog and と introduces its name. No calling aloud or movement toward the speaker is required in this naming use.
