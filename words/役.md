# 役 (やく) — role; duty

## Meaning

役 is a role, assignment, or responsibility. It identifies the part someone performs within a larger
activity, whether or not that activity is their job. Use Nの役 for "the role of N."

- 私は会議で説明する役です。 (わたしはかいぎでせつめいするやくです。) — My role in the meeting is to explain things.

The phrase 説明する役 (せつめいするやく) puts the verb directly before 役: "the role of explaining."

役 can also name a position of responsibility or a part in a performance:

- 委員会で重要な役を任されました。 (いいんかいでじゅうようなやくをまかされました。) — I was entrusted with an important role on the committee.
- 劇で医者の役を演じました。 (げきでいしゃのやくをえんじました。) — I played a doctor in the play.

In the second sentence, 医者の役 identifies the character played, not the actor's actual occupation.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji noun using the on reading ヤク. [役](../kanji/役.md) itself supplies the meaning
"role; duty"; there is no second character to combine with it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

役目 (やくめ) also means a duty or function. [仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) covers work, tasks, and employment.
They overlap, but 役 highlights someone's assigned part: two people with the same job can have different
roles in a meeting. There is no single opposite needed for this use.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 役 to answer "What part do you play?" rather than assuming it names an occupation.
The example explains a responsibility through a verb phrase; it does not give the speaker a professional
title. The performance sense likewise identifies an assigned part rather than every specialized use of やく.
