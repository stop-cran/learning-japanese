# 粗 (あら) - flaw; fault

## Meaning

粗 is a noun for a flaw or fault, especially in a person or their work. This use is usually written あら. Learn あらを探す (あらをさがす), "look for faults," and あらが見える (あらがみえる), "flaws become apparent."

- 人のあらばかり探さないでください。 (ひとのあらばかりさがさないでください。) - Please do not keep looking for other people's faults.
- よく見ると、作品のあらが見えます。 (よくみると、さくひんのあらがみえます。) - On closer inspection, flaws in the work become apparent.

The same dictionary entry also includes the bony parts left after a fish is filleted. In that food context, アラ is a usual spelling: 魚のアラ (さかなのアラ), "fish trimmings."

## How the kanji combine

粗 carries an association with roughness or coarseness. Here it represents the whole native noun あら, not a two-character compound. Do not confuse this noun with the adjective 粗い (あらい), "coarse; rough."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[欠点](欠点.md) (けってん) is a weakness or shortcoming; [欠陥](欠陥.md) (けっかん) can describe a defect. These overlap, but あらを探す particularly evokes fault-finding. A person's strengths are a contextual contrast, not another meaning of あら.

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding noun and activity settle the interpretation: inspecting someone's work suggests faults, while preparing fish suggests trimmings. The fault sense does not require a visible blemish on a surface.
