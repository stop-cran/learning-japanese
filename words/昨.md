# 昨 (さく) - previous; last

## Meaning

昨 (さく) is a prefix used in established time words to indicate the previous period. Learn it through complete words such as 昨年 (さくねん), "last year," and 昨夜 (さくや), "last night." It is not a standalone conversational answer meaning "yesterday."

- 昨年、日本へ行きました。 (さくねん、にほんへいきました。) - I went to Japan last year.
- 昨夜はよく眠れました。 (さくやはよくねむれました。) - I slept well last night.

These whole expressions can function as time phrases without に. Do not construct arbitrary time words merely by adding 昨 to any noun.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [昨](../kanji/昨.md) contributes "previous" in these compounds and uses the on reading サク. In 昨年, 年 supplies the year; in 昨夜, 夜 supplies the night.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[去年](去年.md) (きょねん) refers to the same year as 昨年. [昨日](昨日.md) is ordinarily read きのう; the reading さくじつ belongs to the same written word in a different style. Do not derive きのう by pronouncing each kanji separately.

## Distinctive meaning

The prefix is useful for recognizing written and spoken time expressions, not as a general replacement for English "last." "The last page," for example, means the final page rather than the previous day or year.
