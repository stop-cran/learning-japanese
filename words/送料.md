# 送料 (そうりょう) - postage; shipping charge

## Meaning

送料 is the charge for sending something: postage, carriage or a shipping fee.
It is a noun. Useful expressions include 送料がかかる, "shipping costs money,"
and 送料無料 (そうりょうむりょう), "free shipping."

- 送料はいくらですか。 (そうりょうはいくらですか。) - How much is shipping?
- この商品の値段には送料が含まれています。 (このしょうひんのねだんにはそうりょうがふくまれています。) - The price of this item includes shipping.

The second sentence explicitly includes the fee; the word 送料 alone does not
tell you whether a quoted product price includes it.

## How the kanji combine

[送](../kanji/送.md) contributes sending, and [料](../kanji/料.md)
contributes a fee or charge. Sending specifies what the charge pays for.
The on readings combine as そう + りょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[料金](料金.md) (りょうきん) is a broader fee or charge.
[費用](費用.md) (ひよう) is expense or cost; [代金](代金.md) (だいきん)
is money paid for goods or services.
[値段](値段.md) (ねだん) is a price, not specifically the fee for sending an item.
There is no ordinary opposite of 送料 as a charge category.

## Distinctive meaning

送料 concerns sending, not the purchase price of the contents.
It need not be a government postal charge: a private delivery service can also charge 送料.
Free shipping does not mean that the goods themselves are free.
The quiz guards against broad cost/price labels are not a claim that these money words
are interchangeable in every bill or sentence.
