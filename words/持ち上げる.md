# 持ち上げる (もちあげる) - lift up; flatter

## Meaning

持ち上げる is a transitive ichidan verb meaning to lift something up. The object takes を. It also has a figurative sense of flattering or praising someone extravagantly.

- 二人で重い箱を持ち上げました。 (ふたりでおもいはこをもちあげました。) - The two of us lifted the heavy box.
- そんなに私を持ち上げないでください。 (そんなにわたしをもちあげないでください。) - Please do not flatter me so much.

Forms include 持ち上げます, 持ち上げた, and 持ち上げない. The potential 持ち上げられる can express being able to lift something.

## How the kanji combine

[持](../kanji/持.md) contributes holding through 持つ (もつ), and [上](../kanji/上.md) contributes upward movement through 上げる (あげる). The connecting form 持ち combines with 上げる to describe holding and raising. The compound conjugates at its final verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上げる](上げる.md) (あげる) is broader, covering raising and other uses. [持つ](持つ.md) (もつ) can mean holding or carrying without upward motion. Neither by itself reproduces every use of 持ち上げる.

## Distinctive meaning

Context separates literal lifting from praise. A box strongly suggests physical movement, while a response to extravagant compliments suggests flattery. A person can also literally be lifted, so the object being human does not establish a rigid rule. Read the situation, not just the noun before を.
