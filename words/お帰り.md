# お帰り (おかえり) - welcome home; respectful return

## Meaning

お帰り has two uses. As a greeting, it means "welcome home; welcome back,"
short for お帰りなさい (おかえりなさい). As a noun, it respectfully refers
to someone's return or journey back.

- A: ただいま。 - I'm home.
- B: お帰り。 (おかえり。) - Welcome home.
- お帰りは何時ですか。 (おかえりはなんじですか。) - What time will you be returning?

The greeting is said to the returning person, not by the returning person.

## How the kanji combine

お adds respect to [帰り](帰り.md), the noun related to 帰る (かえる).
[帰](../kanji/帰.md) carries the returning-home idea.
The noun's respectful お does not make the shortened greeting equally
formal in every relationship.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いらっしゃい](いらっしゃい.md) welcomes an arrival more generally.
[いってらっしゃい](いってらっしゃい.md) belongs to seeing someone off,
not receiving them back.

## Distinctive meaning

Bare お帰り is familiar in the homecoming exchange; お帰りなさい is
the fuller greeting. Neither is a universal translation of every "welcome."
The noun can also concern someone's going home, as context determines
the direction of the return.
