# 愚か (おろか) — foolish; stupid

## Meaning

愚か describes a person who does not think things through, or an act that is foolish. Digital Daijisen gives 戦争など愚かなことだ (せんそうなどおろかなことだ), “War and the like are foolish.”

- 同じ失敗を三度も繰り返すなんて、私は本当に愚かだった。 (おなじしっぱいをさんどもくりかえすなんて、わたしはほんとうにおろかだった。) — I was truly foolish to repeat the same mistake three times.
- 目先の利益のために森を全部切ってしまうのは、愚かなことだ。 (めさきのりえきのためにもりをぜんぶきってしまうのは、おろかなことだ。) — Cutting down the whole forest for short-term profit is a foolish thing to do.

## How the kanji combine

[愚](../kanji/愚.md) alone writes おろ, and か is okurigana. 愚か is a na-adjective: 愚かな人, 愚かだ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[馬鹿](馬鹿.md) (ばか) is the everyday word and can be an insult. [賢い](賢い.md) (かしこい) and [利口](利口.md) (りこう) are opposites. 愚かしい (おろかしい) is an i-adjective meaning “foolish; stupid.”

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict treats …はおろか, “not to mention …,” as a separate entry, usually written in kana; its kanji spelling is 疎か, not 愚か. 愚か者 (おろかもの) is “a fool.”
