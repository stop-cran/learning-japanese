# 相違 (そうい) - difference; discrepancy

## Meaning

相違 is a difference or discrepancy, useful in formal comparisons of
opinions, accounts, or details. AとBの相違 names a difference between
A and B. 相違する is intransitive: it describes things differing.

- 二つの報告には相違があります。 (ふたつのほうこくにはそういがあります。) - There are discrepancies between the two reports.
- 私たちの意見は相違しています。 (わたしたちのいけんはそういしています。) - Our opinions differ.

相違点 (そういてん) is a point of difference.
A difference need not mean that either person is dishonest or mistaken.

## How the kanji combine

[相](../kanji/相.md) contributes a relationship between things, and
[違](../kanji/違.md) differing.
Together they suggest things differing in relation to one another.
The on readings are ソウ + イ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[違い](違い.md) (ちがい) is an everyday noun for a difference.
[差](差.md) (さ) can name a difference or gap;
[異なる](異なる.md) (ことなる) is a verb meaning differ.
一致 (いっち), agreement or matching, contrasts in relevant details.

## Distinctive meaning

相違 is not a transitive verb for deliberately changing something:
相違する describes a difference rather than making an object different.
Its formal tone is useful in reports, but does not require that the
difference be numerically measurable.
