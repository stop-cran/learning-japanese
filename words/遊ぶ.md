# 遊ぶ (あそぶ) — play; enjoy oneself

## Meaning

遊ぶ covers play and spending time for enjoyment. Use と for a companion and で for the place where the activity happens.
Its polite form is 遊びます (あそびます); its て-form is 遊んで (あそんで).

- 子どもたちは公園で遊んでいます。 (こどもたちはこうえんであそんでいます。) — The children are playing in the park.
- 土曜日に友達と遊びます。 (どようびにともだちとあそびます。) — I will hang out with a friend on Saturday.

遊びに行く (あそびにいく) means going somewhere for recreation or a social visit:
友達の家に遊びに行きます。 (ともだちのいえにあそびにいきます。) — I will visit a friend at home.
Here 遊び is the form before ます, followed by に行く; bare 遊ぶ is not a general verb for every kind of visit.

## How the kanji combine

[遊](../kanji/遊.md) carries the play/recreation meaning, and ぶ completes the native verb あそぶ.
The verb can describe adults enjoying time together; it is not restricted to children's games.

## Synonyms and antonyms

楽しむ (たのしむ), "enjoy," focuses on enjoyment and can take what is enjoyed with を.
遊ぶ names recreational activity itself; the meanings overlap, but the sentence patterns are not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

English "play" covers meanings outside 遊ぶ. For performing on a piano, say ピアノを弾く (ピアノをひく),
"play the piano." Do not translate every English occurrence of "play" with the same Japanese verb.
