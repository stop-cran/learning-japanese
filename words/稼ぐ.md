# 稼ぐ (かせぐ) - earn; make money

## Meaning

稼ぐ is a godan verb used for earning income. The money or amount earned takes を; the means of earning can take で. It can also describe working hard for a living without naming an object.

- アルバイトで生活費を稼いでいます。 (アルバイトでせいかつひをかせいでいます。) - I earn my living expenses through a part-time job.
- 家族のために毎日稼いでいます。 (かぞくのためにまいにちかせいでいます。) - I work every day to support my family.

The gain need not be money: 時間を稼ぐ (じかんをかせぐ) means "buy time" or "gain time."

## How the kanji combine

稼 carries the earning or working meaning. The ending ぐ belongs to the native verb かせぐ. Its て-form is 稼いで (かせいで), and its past form is 稼いだ (かせいだ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[働く](働く.md) (はたらく) focuses on working; 稼ぐ can focus on what that work brings in. [収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう) is the noun "income," not a verb describing earning it.

## Distinctive meaning

稼ぐ does not itself say how profitable the work is or whether the earner is wealthy. Likewise, 時間を稼ぐ describes securing extra time, not earning money for each hour.
