# じゃあ

## Meaning

じゃあ means "well then," "in that case," or "so" when moving to a conclusion, decision, or next step in conversation.

- じゃあ、明日会いましょう。 (じゃあ、あした あいましょう。) — Well then, let's meet tomorrow.
- もう遅いですね。じゃあ、帰ります。 (もう おそいですね。じゃあ、かえります。) — It is late already. In that case, I'll go home.

It commonly begins a new turn or sentence and does not take noun-modifying な or の.

## How the kanji combine

This kana form is a conversational contraction of では. The shorter じゃ also occurs. The small ゃ and following あ together give the long vowel in じゃあ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

では is less casual. それでは more explicitly marks the transition. There is no useful direct antonym to this conversational connector.

## Distinctive meaning

じゃあ can respond to the situation, not only introduce a logically necessary consequence. In じゃあね, it also helps close an informal conversation: "See you, then." It is not a general translation of English "then" for every past time reference.
