# なかなか — quite; not readily

## Meaning

With an affirmative evaluation, なかなか means "quite; considerably," often with a sense that the result is appreciable or better than expected. With a negative verb, it means "not readily; not easily."

- この本はなかなか面白いです。 (このほんはなかなかおもしろいです。) — This book is quite interesting.
- バスがなかなか来ません。 (バスがなかなかきません。) — The bus is taking a long time to come.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. 中々 is a recognized spelling, with 々 repeating 中. The repeated native element なか does not mean that every modern use is literally "middle-middle."

## Synonyms and antonyms

かなり also expresses a considerable degree. なかなか〜ない contrasts with easily or promptly achieving something; 簡単に (かんたんに), "easily," can provide the relevant comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the whole predicate. なかなか分かりません (なかなかわかりません) means "I am having trouble understanding," not "I understand considerably." The negative pattern need not mean "never"; it often describes difficulty or delay despite effort or expectation.
