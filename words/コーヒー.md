# コーヒー (コーヒー) — coffee

## Meaning

コーヒー means coffee, especially the drink in ordinary ordering and drinking contexts. It does not by itself specify hot, iced, sweetened, or unsweetened.

- 毎朝、コーヒーを飲みます。  
  まいあさ、コーヒーをのみます。  
  "I drink coffee every morning."
- コーヒーを一杯ください。  
  コーヒーをいっぱいください。  
  "One cup of coffee, please."

## How the kanji combine

Use katakana. Both ー marks lengthen the preceding vowel: コー and ヒー. 珈琲 is another dictionary spelling using ateji; its characters do not combine into a literal definition of coffee. JMdict lists English and Dutch source forms.

## Synonyms and antonyms

紅茶 (こうちゃ), "black tea", and お茶 (おちゃ), "tea", are other drinks rather than opposites. アイスコーヒー specifically means iced coffee.

## Distinctive meaning

杯 is a cupful counter: 一杯 (いっぱい), "one cup". The word コーヒー itself does not change for singular or plural. In a cafe, details such as size, temperature, and milk are supplied separately.
