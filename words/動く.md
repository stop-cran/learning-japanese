# 動く (うごく) — to move; to operate

## Meaning

動く means that something moves or changes position. It also describes a machine working. This is an intransitive godan verb: 動きます, 動かない, 動いて. The moving thing is marked with が.

- カーテンが風で動いています。 (カーテンがかぜでうごいています。) — The curtain is moving in the wind.
- この時計はまだ動きます。 (このとけいはまだうごきます。) — This clock still works.

## How the kanji combine

[動](../kanji/動.md), "move," supplies うご, with く as okurigana. The verb is native Japanese, unlike the on-reading compound 運動 (うんどう).

## Synonyms and antonyms

動かす (うごかす) is transitive: 机を動かす (つくえをうごかす), "move a desk." 止まる (とまる), "stop," is a useful contrast. 移動する (いどうする) emphasizes changing location.

## Distinctive meaning

動く does not identify who causes the movement. A machine that 動かない "does not work" may be switched off or broken; the verb alone does not establish the cause.
