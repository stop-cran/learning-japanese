# トップ (トップ) - top; leading position

## Meaning

トップ is a noun for the top or leading position.
It can also identify an organization's senior leadership.
Useful expressions include **トップに立つ** (トップにたつ), "take the lead,"
and **会社のトップ** (かいしゃのトップ), "the company's top leadership."

- 私たちのチームがトップに立ちました。 (わたしたちのチームがトップにたちました。) - Our team took the lead.
- 会社のトップが新しい方針を説明しました。 (かいしゃのトップがあたらしいほうしんをせつめいしました。) - The head of the company explained the new policy.

In the second sentence, トップ refers to a person at the top rather than a physical position.
ページのトップ is the top of a page.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written entirely in katakana.
The small ッ marks the consonant closure before プ: トップ.
There is no kanji composition to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上](上.md) (うえ) expresses "above/top" in broader spatial uses.
[最初](最初.md) (さいしょ), "the beginning/first," concerns sequence and is not always the same as the highest ranking.
最下位 (さいかい), "last place," contrasts with a top ranking.

## Distinctive meaning

Context decides whether トップ concerns a position, ranking, or leadership.
On a website it can also abbreviate トップページ, the site's home page: トップに戻る means "return to the home page" in that navigation context.
That is not necessarily the same action as scrolling to the top of the current page.
Do not copy every English meaning of "top" into Japanese.
