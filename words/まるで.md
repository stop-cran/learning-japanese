# まるで (まるで) - as if; completely

## Meaning

まるで is an adverb used in vivid comparisons and emphatic descriptions. With ようだ or みたいだ it means "as if/just like." With a negative predicate it can mean "not at all."

- まるで夢のようです。 (まるでゆめのようです。) - It is just like a dream.
- 説明を聞いても、まるで分かりません。 (せつめいをきいても、まるでわかりません。) - Even after hearing the explanation, I don't understand at all.

It also intensifies a difference: 前とはまるで違う (まえとはまるでちがう), "It is completely different from before."

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is hiragana. 丸で is a recorded but rare kanji spelling. Learn まるで as the complete adverb rather than translating the character 丸 as "circle" in these sentences.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本当](本当.md) (ほんとう) concerns truth or reality. A まるで...ようだ comparison can be vivid without asserting literal identity. [全く](全く.md) (まったく), "completely/not at all," overlaps in emphatic uses but does not automatically supply the same comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

For the comparison pattern, nouns take のようだ, plain verbs take ようだ directly, and nouns can attach directly to みたいだ. Thus まるで夢のようだ compares something to a dream; it does not state that the event was actually dreamed. Keep the negative in view when translating まるで分からない.
