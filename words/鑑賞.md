# 鑑賞 (かんしょう) - appreciation of art or music

## Meaning

鑑賞 is appreciating a work of art, music, poetry, or a performance.
鑑賞する is transitive: the work being viewed or listened to takes を.

- 美術館で絵画を鑑賞しました。
  (びじゅつかんでかいがをかんしょうしました。) - I viewed and appreciated paintings at the art museum.
- 休日は家で音楽を鑑賞します。
  (きゅうじつはいえでおんがくをかんしょうします。) - On days off, I enjoy listening to music at home.

音楽鑑賞 (おんがくかんしょう) names music appreciation or listening to music
as an activity. The noun is not limited to things seen with the eyes.

## How the kanji combine

鑑 contributes examining or discerning; [賞](../kanji/賞.md) contributes
appreciating or valuing. Their related meanings suggest engaging with
a work's qualities, rather than receiving a prize.
The on readings are カン + ショウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

観賞 (かんしょう) is another word associated with enjoying something by
looking at it, including scenery or ornamental plants.
Learn its spelling with the object rather than treating the homophones
as different pronunciations of one spelling.
[見る](見る.md) (みる), "see; look at," is broader than art appreciation.

## Distinctive meaning

English "appreciation" also means gratitude, but
[感謝](感謝.md) (かんしゃ) is the relevant word for thanking someone.
鑑賞 does not mean that a viewer must give a work a favorable review,
nor does it identify the act of buying the work.
The object and setting distinguish appreciation of a performance from
expressing thanks to its performers.
