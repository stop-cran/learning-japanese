# 番号 (ばんごう) — identifying number

## Meaning

番号 is a number or series of digits used to identify something, such as a room, telephone connection,
or place in a queue.

- 部屋の番号を教えてください。 (へやのばんごうをおしえてください。) — Please tell me the room number.

Use の to identify what the number belongs to: 部屋の番号, "the room's number."
電話番号 (でんわばんごう) is the compound for a phone number.

## How the kanji combine

[番](../kanji/番.md) contributes numbering or order, and [号](../kanji/号.md) contributes a number or designation.
Together they identify an item by its number. The on readings are バン + ゴウ;
the final お sound is long in ばんごう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[数](数.md) (かず), "number; amount," answers a quantity question.
部屋の数 (へやのかず) asks how many rooms there are, while 部屋の番号 asks which numbered room is meant.
These two English uses of "number" are not interchangeable in Japanese.

## Distinctive meaning

An identifying number can contain several digits without representing a count.
A phone number identifies a connection; it does not count telephones.
Likewise, room number 305 does not by itself tell you that a building contains 305 rooms.
There is no useful single antonym for this labeling function.
