# 保証 (ほしょう) - guarantee; warranty

## Meaning

保証 is a guarantee or assurance. 保証する is a transitive する verb: Nを保証する, "guarantee N." The noun also means a product warranty.

- この製品には一年間の保証が付いています。 (このせいひんにはいちねんかんのほしょうがついています。) - This product comes with a one-year warranty.
- この情報の正確さは保証できません。 (このじょうほうのせいかくさはほしょうできません。) - I cannot guarantee the accuracy of this information.

保証できない denies the ability to give an assurance; it does not by itself declare the information false.

## How the kanji combine

保 contributes keeping or ensuring, and 証 contributes attesting or evidence. Together they suggest standing behind an assurance. The on readings are ホ + ショウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[約束](約束.md) (やくそく) is a promise; it can overlap with an assurance but does not automatically imply a warranty. [責任](責任.md) (せきにん) is responsibility, which may be involved in a guarantee but is a different noun.

## Distinctive meaning

The inspected entry also lists 保障 for the general guarantee/assurance sense, but restricts its warranty sense to the spelling 保証. Do not transfer every use freely between the spellings. A 保証 does not specify its detailed coverage by itself: duration, conditions, and what is promised need to be stated. 保証書 (ほしょうしょ) is a written guarantee or warranty document.
