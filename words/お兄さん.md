# お兄さん (おにいさん) — older brother (polite)

## Meaning

お兄さん is a respectful way to refer to another person's older brother. It can also address one's own older brother within the family.

- お兄さんはどこに住んでいますか。 (おにいさんはどこにすんでいますか。) — Where does your older brother live?
- お兄さん、ちょっと手伝って。 (おにいさん、ちょっとてつだって。) — Big brother, help me for a moment.

Family address habits differ; this is not a requirement to call every older brother by this exact form.

## How the kanji combine

[兄](../kanji/兄.md) carries the older-brother meaning. In this word it is read にい, with お before it and さん after it. Keep the long vowel: にい, not に.

## Synonyms and antonyms

兄 (あに) is the usual reference to one's own older brother when speaking to outsiders. お姉さん (おねえさん) means older sister; 弟 (おとうと) means younger brother.

## Distinctive meaning

お兄さん can also address a young man who is not a relative. That familiar use is context-dependent, not a universal polite substitute for a person's name.
