# どきどき — heart pounding

## Meaning

どきどき evokes a pounding heartbeat, often caused by nervousness, excitement, attraction, or physical exertion.

- 発表の前は、胸がどきどきする。 — My heart pounds before a presentation. (発表: はっぴょう; 胸: むね.)
- 階段を駆け上がって、心臓がどきどきしていた。 — My heart was pounding after I ran up the stairs. (駆け上がる: かけあがる; 心臓: しんぞう.)
- 結果をどきどきしながら待った。 — I waited for the result with my heart racing.

どきどきする describes the bodily state. どきどきと is also possible before a suitable verb; どきどきしながら means "while feeling one's heart race."

## How the kanji combine

The repeated kana expression evokes the sound or felt rhythm of a heartbeat. Its sound-imitation and bodily-state uses overlap: an audible noise is not required.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[わくわく](わくわく.md) emphasizes eager anticipation. [はらはら](はらはら.md) emphasizes anxious suspense about what might go wrong. Neither necessarily describes the heartbeat itself.

## Distinctive meaning

どきどき is not inherently positive or negative. Fear, a pleasant encounter, and running can all make the heart pound. Do not translate every instance as "excited" or infer a medical diagnosis from this everyday description.
