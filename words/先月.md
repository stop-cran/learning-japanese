# 先月 (せんげつ) — last month

## Meaning

先月 is the month before this one: 先月日本へ行きました (I went to Japan last month), 先月の売り上げ (last month's sales).

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md) (previous, earlier) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month): "the earlier month". Both on'yomi: セン + ゲツ. The same 先 appears in 先週 せんしゅう (last week) and 先日 せんじつ (the other day).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Series: 先月, [今月](今月.md) こんげつ (this month), 来月 らいげつ (next month).
- Close: 前月 ぜんげつ (the previous month, used in reports and statistics).

## Distinctive meaning

先月 is the everyday "last month". 前月 means "the preceding month" relative to a given month in documents, not necessarily relative to now.
