# 蛍 (ほたる) - firefly

## Meaning

蛍 means a **firefly**, also called a lightning bug. The Japanese word names insects of the firefly family, not just one particular species. They are known for their light, but some species do not glow conspicuously.

- 川で蛍を見ました。 (かわでほたるをみました。) - I saw fireflies at the river.
- 蛍が光っています。 (ほたるがひかっています。) - The fireflies are glowing.

The second sentence describes the insects being observed; it does not claim that every firefly species glows. The river example likewise does not make a riverside habitat part of the word's definition.

## How the kanji combine

[蛍](../kanji/蛍.md) alone writes the native noun ほたる. There is no compound to divide or kana ending to add.

The noun is often written in kana, **ほたる** or **ホタル**. JMdict marks it usually kana-written; its katakana alternative is kana-only. **螢** is the older character form, not a different pronunciation. Keep that spelling distinction separate from the character's on-reading ケイ in 蛍光 (けいこう), fluorescence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[虫](虫.md) (むし), insect/bug, is a broader category. 昆虫 (こんちゅう) is the biological insect category; neither identifies a firefly specifically. [蚊](蚊.md) (か), mosquito, names a different insect, not an antonym.

There is no direct antonym. [蛍光灯](蛍光灯.md) (けいこうとう), fluorescent lamp, names equipment rather than an insect.

## Distinctive meaning

In ordinary animal descriptions, use ほたる, not ケイ. 蛍狩り (ほたるがり) means firefly watching; dictionaries also record a dated firefly-catching sense. The word does not require interpreting every modern outing as catching the insects.

Seisenban records older uses of 蛍 for a small remaining fire or ember and, in historical Gion usage, a sex worker or associated tea house. It can also be the title of a chapter of *The Tale of Genji*. These context-bound uses do not make 蛍 the neutral modern word for [火](火.md) (ひ), fire, or for a person or establishment. This lesson teaches the insect noun.
