# 作成 (さくせい) - drawing up; document creation

## Meaning

作成 means making or drawing up a report, plan, contract, or similar document.
It also covers creating a digital file, website, or account.
作成する is transitive; what is created takes を.

- 会議の報告書を作成しました。 (かいぎのほうこくしょをさくせいしました。) - I prepared the meeting report.
- 新しいアカウントを作成してください。 (あたらしいアカウントをさくせいしてください。) - Please create a new account.

Forms include 作成します, 作成した, and 作成しない.

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) contributes making, and [成](../kanji/成.md) contributes bringing something into a completed form.
The combination suggests producing the document or record. The on readings are サク + セイ.
These associations are a meaning aid, not a claim that the result must be flawless.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作製](作製.md), also さくせい, commonly concerns producing an object, model, or drawing.
[作る](作る.md) (つくる) is the broad everyday making verb.
[準備](準備.md)する (じゅんびする) means prepare or get ready; preparations can include creating a document without being limited to that task.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not turn the 作成/作製 comparison into "nonphysical versus physical" without exceptions:
documents have physical copies, and 作製 can be used for drawings.
The object and established usage guide the choice.
The source gloss "frame" means formulate or draw up here, not putting a picture in a frame.
