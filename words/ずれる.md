# ずれる - shift out of place; be misaligned

## Meaning

ずれる describes something shifting from its position or being out of
alignment with a reference. The mismatch can involve physical position,
time, or the point of an argument.

- 眼鏡がずれています。 (めがねがずれています。) - My glasses are out of position.
- 二人の休みが一日ずれています。 (ふたりのやすみがいちにちずれています。) - The two people's days off are one day apart.
- その答えは質問からずれています。 (そのこたえはしつもんからずれています。) - That answer is off the point of the question.

It is an intransitive ichidan verb: ずれます, ずれて, ずれない.
ずれている can describe a mismatch already present, not just a visible
sliding movement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずらす](ずらす.md) is the transitive partner: 眼鏡をずらす means to
shift the glasses. [滑る](滑る.md) describes sliding or slipping, whereas
ずれる emphasizes displacement or mismatch.
[遅れる](遅れる.md) specifically concerns lateness or delay; a time
offset need not always be later.

## Distinctive meaning

A difference in alignment is not necessarily a large movement, an injury,
or somebody's deliberate action. State the relevant reference when it is
not obvious, as 質問から does in the third example.
Learn the spelling ずれる; the dictionary's search-only づれる record is
not an equal recommended spelling for this lesson.
