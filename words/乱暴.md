# 乱暴 (らんぼう) - violent; rough; reckless

## Meaning

乱暴 describes violent or rowdy behavior, and rough, careless or reckless
handling and language. As a な-adjective it uses 乱暴な before a noun
and 乱暴に before an action.

- 道具を乱暴に扱わないでください。 (どうぐをらんぼうにあつかわないでください。) - Please do not handle the tools roughly.
- 乱暴な運転は危険です。 (らんぼうなうんてんはきけんです。) - Reckless driving is dangerous.

The noun also names violence or rowdiness; 乱暴する is an intransitive
する verb meaning to act violently.

- 人に乱暴してはいけません。 (ひとにらんぼうしてはいけません。) - Do not behave violently toward others.

With a person as the target, 乱暴する or its passive 乱暴される
can mean assault, including sexual assault. They are not neutral ways
to say that someone was impolite.
The adjective can criticize an unreasonable demand or a wild argument too.

## How the kanji combine

[乱](../kanji/乱.md) contributes disorder, and [暴](../kanji/暴.md)
contributes violence or an outburst. These related associations support
the word's uncontrolled or rough behavior. The on readings are ラン + ボウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暴れる](暴れる.md) (あばれる) means acting violently or struggling.
[荒い](荒い.md) (あらい) also describes roughness, including behavior.
[粗末](粗末.md) (そまつ) can overlap when something is treated carelessly.
[丁寧](丁寧.md) (ていねい) provides a contrast in careful handling or polite language.

## Distinctive meaning

Rough treatment need not involve hitting someone.
Conversely, 乱暴 is not the ordinary description of a merely rough-textured surface.
This card selects the violence/rowdiness and rough/reckless senses;
the dictionary's additional sexual-assault sense is outside these examples.
