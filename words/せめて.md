# せめて - at least; even just (minimum wish)

## Meaning

せめて expresses the minimum someone hopes to obtain or achieve when the fuller
wish cannot be met: "at least" or "even just."
It is an adverb, often with a request, desire or wish.

- せめて名前だけでも教えてください。 (せめてなまえだけでもおしえてください。) - Please at least tell me your name.
- せめて一日休みたいです。 (せめていちにちやすみたいです。) - I would like even just one day off.

だけでも reinforces "even just" in the first example.
The second identifies one day as a minimum wish, not a factual estimate of how long someone rests.

## How the kanji combine

This adverb is written in kana.
It is not 攻め手 (せめて), a separate noun for the attacking side or a method of attack.
Also distinguish 攻めて, the て-form of [攻める](攻める.md) (せめる), "attack."
Matching kana alone does not establish one lexical identity or grammatical role.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[少なくとも](少なくとも.md) (すくなくとも) gives a minimum estimate or the least
that can be said. It can be used in factual assessments without the wish prominent in せめて.
[最低](最低.md) (さいてい), especially 最低でも, can likewise state a minimum.
[精々](精々.md) (せいぜい), "at most; at best," instead has an upper-limit use.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate this adverb as "method of attack."
That raw source gloss belongs to 攻め手, while the supplied dictionary ID identifies
the minimum-wish adverb taught here.
Nor is せめて a neutral replacement for every numerical "at least":
せめて十人来てほしい (せめてじゅうにんきてほしい) expresses hoping that at least ten people come.
