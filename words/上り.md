# 上り (のぼり) - ascent; upbound

## Meaning

上り names an ascent, an uphill section, or movement in an "up" direction. In transport, 上り identifies the direction toward a route's designated starting point. An 上り列車 (のぼりれっしゃ) is an up train.

- この道は上りが多いです。 (このみちはのぼりがおおいです。) - This road has many uphill stretches.
- 上りの電車は三番線から出ます。 (のぼりのでんしゃはさんばんせんからでます。) - The upbound train leaves from platform three.

Transport 上り is a route designation, not a statement that the vehicle is climbing a slope. Tokyo is the up direction on many routes, but not a universal destination for every 上り service.

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md) contributes the upward direction. 上り is a native noun related to [上る](登る.md) (のぼる), "go up." 登り and 昇り are also dictionary spellings for ascent, but the inspected train sense specifically uses 上り.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下り](下り.md) (くだり) gives the opposite direction: descent or downbound travel. [登山](登山.md) (とざん) specifically concerns climbing mountains; 上り can instead be a stretch of road or a transport direction. The spelling 上り also occurs with the meaning "upstream" or "uphill"; compare [川上](川上.md) (かわかみ), "upstream."

## Distinctive meaning

Use **上りの + noun**, as in 上りの電車, or a compound such as 上り列車. The label is used beyond trains, including roads and buses. It does not by itself tell you the compass direction, elevation change, or whether Tokyo is involved; check that route's designation.
