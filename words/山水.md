# 山水 (さんすい) — landscape scenery

## Meaning

山水 is natural scenery, literally "mountains and water". It is a literary and aesthetic word, tied to the East Asian landscape tradition:
山水画 さんすいが (landscape painting), 山紫水明 さんしすいめい (an idiom for scenic beauty, "purple mountains, clear water"), and 枯山水 かれさんすい (a dry landscape garden).

## How the kanji combine

A pair of two nouns forms a whole: [山](../kanji/山.md) (mountain) and [水](../kanji/水.md) (water) stand for the classical elements of a landscape. This is a "noun-and-noun" compound, not an
antonym pair like [大小](大小.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 景色 けしき (the everyday word for scenery), 風景 ふうけい (landscape, view), 自然 しぜん (nature).
- Related pairs: 山川 さんせん／やまかわ (mountains and rivers), 山河 さんが (mountains and rivers, also the land).
- Antonym: none.

## Distinctive meaning

In daily conversation, scenery is 景色, not 山水. 山水 appears in art and garden terms and in classical, poetic or Chinese-influenced contexts.
