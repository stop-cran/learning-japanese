# 広い (ひろい) — wide, spacious

## Meaning

広い describes something wide, broad or spacious.

- この部屋は広いです。 (このへやはひろいです。) — This room is spacious.
- 広い道を歩きました。 (ひろいみちをあるきました。) — I walked along a wide road.

## How the kanji combine

This is one kanji plus okurigana: [広](../kanji/広.md) supplies ひろ, and い is the adjective ending. It is not a compound.

As an い-adjective, it directly modifies a noun: 広い[部屋](部屋.md) (ひろいへや), "a spacious room." Its negative is 広くない (ひろくない), "not spacious."

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 狭い せまい, "narrow, cramped," is the natural opposite.
- 大きい おおきい, associated with [大](../kanji/大.md), describes large size more generally. 広い highlights breadth or space rather than size in every dimension.
- 広がる ひろがる means to spread out; it describes a change or extension, not just the state of being spacious.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose 広い when width or available area matters. A 広い部屋 has plenty of space; the word does not by itself mean that the room has a high ceiling. Do not add な before a noun: 広い部屋, not 広いな部屋.
