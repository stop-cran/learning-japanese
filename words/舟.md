# 舟 (ふね) - boat; watercraft

## Meaning

舟 is a boat or watercraft. It belongs to the same dictionary spelling
family as [船](船.md). JMdict notes that 舟 often refers to a small,
hand-propelled craft; this is a tendency, not a rule that 船 must be large.

- 舟に乗って川を渡りました。 (ふねにのってかわをわたりました。) - We crossed the river in a boat.
- 岸に小舟がありました。 (きしにこぶねがありました。) - There was a small boat by the bank.

に marks the craft being boarded in 舟に乗る. 小舟 (こぶね)
means a small boat.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [舟](../kanji/舟.md) writes the native noun ふね.
The combining form ふな- appears in 舟橋 (ふなばし), "pontoon bridge."
小舟 illustrates the voiced combining form -ぶね. These are forms
inside words, not interchangeable standalone pronunciations.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[船](船.md) is an alternative spelling, not an opposite.
[ボート](ボート.md) can mean a boat or rowboat; [ヨット](ヨット.md)
is a sailing boat or yacht. Those labels can overlap with the broad
watercraft sense, even though the words are not interchangeable for
every vessel.

The dictionary family also records tank, tub, vat and trough senses.
[風呂](風呂.md) and [お風呂](お風呂.md) have bath-related uses;
the exclusions account for tub-related label overlap, not a claim
that bathing is a boat journey.

## Distinctive meaning

The examples here concern watercraft, but that is not an exhaustive
account of the spelling family. Choose the meaning from the setting
and construction. Do not infer a fixed size boundary from 舟 versus
船, or assume that every object called a tub should be written 舟.
