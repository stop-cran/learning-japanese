# およそ - approximately; generally

## Meaning

およそ is an adverb meaning "about," "roughly," or "approximately" before a quantity. It can also mean "generally" or "as a rule" in a broad statement.

- 駅までおよそ十分かかります。 (えきまでおよそじゅっぷんかかります。) - It takes about ten minutes to the station.
- 参加者はおよそ百人でした。 (さんかしゃはおよそひゃくにんでした。) - There were approximately a hundred participants.

The estimate may be above or below the stated number; およそ does not specify the margin.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 凡そ is a recognized kanji spelling, but the practical unit to learn is the whole adverb およそ. It is not a loanword or a phrase requiring an extra particle before the number.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[約](約.md) (やく) also introduces approximate quantities. だいたい overlaps with "roughly" and "generally." [正確](正確.md) (せいかく), "accurate; exact," provides a contrast when precision rather than approximation is at issue.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume every およそ gives an estimate. In およそ考えられない (およそかんがえられない), "quite unthinkable," it strengthens the negative instead. Number estimates, generalizations, and this emphatic use are related dictionary senses but need different English translations. Read the expression following およそ before deciding which meaning fits.
