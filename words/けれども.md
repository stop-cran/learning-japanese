# けれども (けれども) - but; although

## Meaning

けれども connects contrasting ideas: "but" or "although." It can follow a complete clause, or begin a new sentence meaning "however."

- 高いけれども、おいしいです。 (たかいけれども、おいしいです。) - It is expensive, but delicious.
- 今日は休みだけれども、早く起きました。 (きょうはやすみだけれども、はやくおきました。) - Although today is a day off, I got up early.

After a present affirmative noun or na-adjective, keep だ: 学生だけれども (がくせいだけれども), "although a student." Verbs and i-adjectives do not need this だ. Polite forms also work: ありますけれども, 静かですけれども (しずかですけれども).

## How the kanji combine

This grammatical word is normally written in kana. けれど and the shorter けど are related forms; けど is particularly common in casual conversation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

が can also link clauses with "but." しかし is a sentence-level "however"; it does not attach to predicates in the same way.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every けれども introduces a strong contradiction. It can provide background or soften an unfinished request: 質問があるんですけれども (しつもんがあるんですけれども), "I have a question..." The listener may infer the invitation to respond.
