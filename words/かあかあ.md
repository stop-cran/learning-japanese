# かあかあ — cawing

## Meaning

A crow's repeated caws.

- からすが屋根（やね）の上でかあかあ鳴（な）いている。 — A crow is cawing on the roof.
- 公園でかあかあという鳴き声（なきごえ）が聞こえた。 — I heard cawing in the park.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ちゅんちゅん](ちゅんちゅん.md) and [ぴよぴよ](ぴよぴよ.md) evoke smaller bird chirps rather than this crow-like sound. They are useful voice comparisons, not grammatical opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

かあかあ鳴く is the ordinary sound pattern. カーカー is another recorded spelling. A child-directed noun use can also name the crow itself; that is not a reason to replace からす in neutral adult prose.
