# 例えば (たとえば) — for example

## Meaning

例えば means **for example or for instance**. It introduces a specific illustration of a broader point.

日本の料理が好きです。例えば、すしが好きです。 (にほんのりょうりがすきです。たとえば、すしがすきです。) — I like Japanese food. For example, I like sushi.

It can stand at the beginning of a sentence or before the example within a longer sentence. It does not require に.

## How the kanji combine

[例](../kanji/例.md) contributes the idea of an example. The reading たとえば is a native expression, also often written entirely in kana. Learn it as a fixed adverb rather than trying to change the final ば like a verb ending.

## Synonyms and antonyms

例 (れい) is the noun “example.” 例えば introduces an example; 例を挙げる (れいをあげる) means “give an example.” つまり, “in other words,” reformulates or summarizes instead of necessarily providing an instance.

## Distinctive meaning

An example is illustrative, not an exhaustive list. Saying 例えば、すし does not say that sushi is the only relevant Japanese food. The expression is appropriate in ordinary conversation as well as explanations in writing.
