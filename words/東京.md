# 東京 (とうきょう) — Tokyo

## Meaning

東京 is the capital of Japan: 東京に住んでいる (I live in Tokyo), 東京駅 とうきょうえき (Tokyo Station), 東京タワー, 東京都 とうきょうと (the Tokyo Metropolis).
The pronunciation is とうきょう, with two long vowels.

## How the kanji combine

[東](../kanji/東.md) (east) plus [京](../kanji/京.md) (capital): "eastern capital", a modifier-plus-noun compound of on readings (トウ + キョウ). The name dates from 1868, when Edo was renamed and became the imperial capital.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Earlier name: 江戸 えど (Edo).
- Related: 京都 きょうと (Kyoto, the former capital; also uses 京), 関東 かんとう (the Kanto region around Tokyo), 東北 とうほく (the Tohoku region, north-east Honshu).

## Distinctive meaning

東京 is both the city and the metropolis (都). Compare 東京 ("eastern capital") with 京都 きょうと ("capital city"): both names contain 京 but they name different cities.
