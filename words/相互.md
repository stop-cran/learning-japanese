# 相互 (そうご) - mutual; reciprocal

## Meaning

相互 describes a mutual or reciprocal relationship.
Useful patterns are 相互の + noun and 相互に + action:
相互の理解 (そうごのりかい), mutual understanding.
It also occurs directly before another noun in compounds.

- 相互の信頼が大切です。 (そうごのしんらいがたいせつです。) - Mutual trust is important.
- 二つの学校は相互に協力しています。 (ふたつのがっこうはそうごにきょうりょくしています。) - The two schools cooperate with each other.

The second sentence presents cooperation as reciprocal,
not simply help given in one direction.

## How the kanji combine

[相](../kanji/相.md) and [互](../kanji/互.md) both contribute
relationships involving one another. Their related meanings reinforce
mutuality. The on readings are ソウ + ゴ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[互い](互い.md) (たがい) and [お互い](お互い.md) (おたがい)
also express each other or one another.
[共同](共同.md) (きょうどう) concerns acting or holding something jointly;
[一緒](一緒.md) (いっしょ) expresses togetherness.
Those are related ideas, not automatic substitutes for a reciprocal relationship.

## Distinctive meaning

相互理解 (そうごりかい) means mutual understanding.
Learn this compound and the 相互の/相互に patterns rather than freely
inserting な: 相互 is recorded as a noun taking の, not simply as a
regular な-adjective. Mutuality also does not guarantee equal amounts
of effort or an identical benefit for each side.
