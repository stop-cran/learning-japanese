# 予約 (よやく) - reservation; booking

## Meaning

予約 is a reservation, booking, or appointment made in advance. 予約する means to reserve or book; the thing reserved can take を.

ホテルの部屋を予約しました。 (ホテルのへやをよやくしました。) - I booked a hotel room.

明日の七時に二人で予約したいです。 (あしたのしちじにふたりでよやくしたいです。) - I would like to make a reservation for two at seven tomorrow.

In a restaurant context, 二人で specifies the party size.

## How the kanji combine

[予](../kanji/予.md) contributes the idea of doing something beforehand; [約](../kanji/約.md) concerns an agreement or arrangement. Their on readings form よやく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

約束 (やくそく) is a promise or arrangement between people more broadly. 予定 (よてい) is a plan or schedule; having a plan does not mean a service has been booked. キャンセルする means canceling.

## Distinctive meaning

予約 can also cover an advance order for a product. It need not imply that payment has already been made. Distinguish 予約があります, "I have a reservation," from 予約したいです, "I would like to make a reservation."
