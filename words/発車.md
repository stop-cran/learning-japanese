# 発車 (はっしゃ) - vehicle departure

## Meaning

発車 is a noun for the departure or starting of a train, bus, car, or similar vehicle. 発車する is intransitive: the departing vehicle takes が. A departure point can take を or から.

- 電車がまもなく発車します。 (でんしゃがまもなくはっしゃします。) - The train will depart shortly.
- バスは定刻に駅を発車しました。 (バスはていこくにえきをはっしゃしました。) - The bus left the station on time.

In the second example, 駅を marks the place left behind, not a thing being operated on. 発車時刻 (はっしゃじこく) means departure time.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) contributes starting or setting out, and [車](../kanji/車.md) identifies a vehicle. Together they name a vehicle's departure. The compound is はっしゃ, with a small っ before しゃ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出発](出発.md) (しゅっぱつ) is broader: people and journeys can depart or begin. 発車 focuses on the vehicle. 停車 (ていしゃ), "stopping a vehicle," contrasts with starting; [到着](到着.md) (とうちゃく), "arrival," names reaching the destination.

## Distinctive meaning

The 車 component does not restrict 発車 to private cars: train and bus announcements provide natural examples. However, a person leaving home on foot is not described with 発車する. Choose the word according to what is departing, rather than translating every English "leave" the same way.
