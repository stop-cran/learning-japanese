# 鋭い (するどい) - sharp; keen

## Meaning

鋭い is an i-adjective describing a sharp edge or point. It also describes
intense sensations and keen perception. Put it directly before a noun; the
adverbial form is 鋭く (するどく).

- このナイフの刃は鋭いです。 (このナイフのははするどいです。) - This knife's blade is sharp.
- 彼女は鋭い質問をしました。 (かのじょはするどいしつもんをしました。) - She asked a perceptive question.

鋭い痛み (するどいいたみ) is a sharp pain, while 勘が鋭い (かんがするどい)
describes keen intuition. These are different applications of sharpness, not
claims that a thought or pain has a physical blade.

## How the kanji combine

鋭 carries the idea of sharpness. In the native adjective it is read するど, with
い as the written ending. There is no two-character compound to split.

## Synonyms and antonyms

鈍い (にぶい), "dull; blunt; slow to perceive," supplies an opposite in several
contexts, though the exact contrast depends on the noun.
[賢い](賢い.md) (かしこい) means clever or wise; 鋭い emphasizes penetrating
perception rather than every aspect of intelligence.

## Distinctive meaning

A 鋭い質問 can identify an important issue; it need not be rude. With a voice,
gaze, criticism, or [痛み](痛み.md), the word instead emphasizes a piercing or
cutting quality. Do not translate every occurrence with the same physical
"pointed" image.
