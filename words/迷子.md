# 迷子 (まいご) - lost child; lost person

## Meaning

迷子 is a noun for a child or person who is lost or separated from their companions.
迷子になる means "get lost"; 迷子です describes someone as lost.
The word can refer to an adult too, despite the written 子.

- デパートで迷子になりました。 (デパートでまいごになりました。) - I got lost in the department store.
- 迷子の子供を案内所に連れて行きました。 (まいごのこどもをあんないじょにつれていきました。) - I took the lost child to the information desk.

で names where someone got lost. In the second sentence, 迷子の modifies 子供;
案内所に gives the destination.

## How the kanji combine

[迷](../kanji/迷.md) relates to losing one's way, and [子](../kanji/子.md) means child.
The native expression has the established reading まいご; do not assemble it from メイ + シ.
The written components help with recognition but do not impose a child-only restriction on usage.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[迷う](迷う.md) (まよう) is broader: it can mean losing one's way or being undecided.
道に迷う (みちにまよう), "lose one's way," overlaps with 迷子になる.
Being reunited with one's companions is a contrasting outcome, not an antonym noun.

## Distinctive meaning

迷子 focuses on the lost person or their situation.
It does not mean an object has gone missing, and it does not by itself establish danger or abandonment.
In an announcement, 迷子のお知らせ (まいごのおしらせ) introduces information about a lost child or person.
