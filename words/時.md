# 時 (とき) - time; moment; occasion

## Meaning

時 is a noun meaning a time, moment, or occasion. After a description it forms a useful "when" expression. Attach nouns with の, な-adjectives with な, and い-adjectives or plain verbs directly.

- 子供の時、ここに住んでいました。 (こどものとき、ここにすんでいました。) - I lived here when I was a child.
- 暇な時に、この本を読んでください。 (ひまなときに、このほんをよんでください。) - Please read this book when you have time.

Other patterns include 忙しい時 (いそがしいとき), "when busy," and 帰る時 (かえるとき), "when returning." に can mark the occasion on which the main action occurs.

## How the kanji combine

[時](../kanji/時.md) is a single kanji with the native reading とき here. It can also be written とき in these constructions. The clock expression 三時 (さんじ), "three o'clock," uses a different reading; do not read it さんとき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時間](時間.md) (じかん) often concerns time as a duration or available resource. [場合](場合.md) (ばあい) emphasizes a case or circumstance. 時 can instead locate an event relative to another situation.

## Distinctive meaning

Verb tense before 時 can change the timing. 家を出る時に鍵を確認した (いえをでるときにかぎをかくにんした) means checking the key when about to leave; 家を出た時 points to having left. A past main sentence does not require every verb before 時 to be past.
