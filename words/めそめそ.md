# めそめそ — whimpering; tearfully snivelling

## Meaning

めそめそ describes tearful whimpering or weeping, often viewed by the speaker as lingering or overly timid.

- おもちゃが壊れて、子供がめそめそ泣いていた。 — The child was whimpering because the toy had broken. (壊れる: こわれる.)
- 弟はめそめそして、理由を話そうとしなかった。 — My little brother kept snivelling and would not explain why.

Use めそめそ泣く, optionally with と, or めそめそする. めそめそしないで is a request to stop whimpering, not a neutral question about someone's feelings.

## How the kanji combine

This repeated kana mimetic evokes a human crying manner and its sound. It can describe tearfulness without claiming that loud sobs are audible.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しくしく](しくしく.md) depicts quiet, sustained crying without the same built-in tendency toward a critical assessment. [くよくよ](くよくよ.md) concerns brooding and need not involve tears.

## Distinctive meaning

めそめそ frequently carries a belittling or impatient tone. Avoid treating it as the neutral translation of every "cry" or using it casually to describe serious grief. The speaker's stance matters as much as the volume of the crying.
