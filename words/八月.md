# 八月 (はちがつ) — August

## Meaning

八月 is August: 八月は暑い (August is hot), 八月十五日 はちがつじゅうごにち (August 15th, around the Obon festival).

## How the kanji combine

[八](../kanji/八.md) (eight) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month): the eighth month, read はち + がつ without contraction.
"Eight months" is 八か月 はっかげつ, with the contraction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 葉月 はづき (old name of the eighth month).
- Related: [八日](八日.md) ようか (the 8th).

## Distinctive meaning

八月 uses ハチ and not やっつ or よう. The contraction はっ appears before k, s, t, p: 八本 はっぽん.
