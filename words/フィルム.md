# フィルム (フィルム) — photographic film

## Meaning

フィルム means **film**. In photography it is the light-sensitive material used to record images, including a roll of film.

カメラにフィルムを入れました。 (かめらにふぃるむをいれました。) — I loaded film into the camera.

フィルムを一本買う (ふぃるむをいっぽんかう) means “buy one roll of film.” The word フィルム alone does not specify a single roll.

## How the kanji combine

This is the English loanword “film,” written in katakana. Small ィ combines with フ to write フィ. The spelling フイルム, with full-size イ, is also dictionary-listed and occurs in established names.

## Synonyms and antonyms

写真 (しゃしん) is a photograph, not the unexposed film used to make it. 映画 (えいが) is the ordinary word for a movie. A デジタルカメラ records images digitally rather than requiring photographic film.

## Distinctive meaning

The broader word can also concern thin film material, so context matters. This article focuses on the source list's photographic use; it does not imply that every modern camera needs a roll of film.
