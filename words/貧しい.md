# 貧しい (まずしい) — poor; needy

## Meaning

貧しい describes poverty or insufficient resources. In its financial sense it describes a person's circumstances,
not whether an item is inexpensive.

- 子供のころ、私の家は貧しかったです。 (こどものころ、わたしのいえはまずしかったです。) — My family was poor when I was a child.

It is an い-adjective: 貧しい家庭 (まずしいかてい), "a poor household," needs no な.
Its past form is 貧しかった, as in the example.

## How the kanji combine

[貧](../kanji/貧.md) supplies the poverty or lack meaning.
This native adjective has the reading まずしい; the kana しい complete the written word.
There is no second kanji component.

## Synonyms and antonyms

貧乏 (びんぼう) also describes being poor, but it uses な before a noun: 貧乏な家庭 (びんぼうなかてい).
[豊か](豊か.md) (ゆたか) can express being well-off or having ample resources; 豊かな生活 (ゆたかなせいかつ) is "an affluent or abundant life."
Neither comparison changes 貧しい into a な-adjective.

## Distinctive meaning

貧しい can extend beyond money to an inadequate supply or quality of something.
Keep the context visible: the family example concerns financial circumstances, not a judgment about the family's worth.
It is not a general substitute for every English use of "poor," such as "poor weather."
