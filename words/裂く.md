# 裂く (さく) - tear apart; split open

## Meaning

裂く is a transitive godan verb: tear or rip something apart, or cut it open.
The affected thing takes を. Forms include 裂きます, 裂いた, 裂いて, and 裂かない.

- 布を細く裂きました。 (ぬのをほそくさきました。) - I tore the cloth into narrow strips.
- 二人の仲を裂くようなことは言わないでください。 (ふたりのなかをさくようなことはいわないでください。) - Please do not say things that would drive the two of them apart.

The second example uses the established forcible-separation sense, not physical damage to the people.

## How the kanji combine

裂 contributes splitting or tearing and supplies さ; く completes the native verb.
The related spelling [割](../kanji/割.md)く is also read さく, not わく.
For allocating part of one's time, use that spelling:
時間を割いて話を聞きました (じかんをさいてはなしをききました), "I made time to listen."
Do not write this allocation use as 時間を裂く.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[破る](破る.md) (やぶる) overlaps in tearing, while [切る](切る.md) (きる) is the broader cutting verb.
[離す](離す.md) (はなす) means separate or keep apart without necessarily implying this forcible rupture.
裂ける (さける) describes something splitting or tearing, without the same object-taking construction.

## Distinctive meaning

裂く and 割く belong to one dictionary family, but not every sense accepts both spellings.
The tearing and forcible-separation senses are marked 裂く; allocation is marked 割く.
The shared cutting-open sense does not erase these restrictions.
The unrelated homophone 咲く (さく), "bloom," is not this verb.
