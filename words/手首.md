# 手首 (てくび) - wrist

## Meaning

手首 is the wrist, the joint area where the hand meets the forearm.

- テニスで手首を痛めました。 (テニスでてくびをいためました。) - I hurt my wrist playing tennis.
- 手首に時計をつけています。 (てくびにとけいをつけています。) - I am wearing a watch on my wrist.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md) is the hand, and [首](../kanji/首.md) contributes the idea of a neck-like connecting part. The native readings are て + くび. The complete word names a wrist, not the neck of a person.

## Synonyms and antonyms

腕首 (うでくび) is another dictionary term for the wrist. [手](手.md) means the hand, [腕](腕.md) the arm, and [指](指.md) a finger; they are neighboring body parts, not interchangeable names for this joint.

## Distinctive meaning

手首を曲げる (てくびをまげる) means to bend the wrist. Rare 手頸 and the dictionary's search-only 手頚 are spelling-recognition information, not reasons to replace 手首 in the examples. The ordinary reading taught here remains てくび.
