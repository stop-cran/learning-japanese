# 木 (き) - tree; wood

## Meaning

木 means a tree or wood as a material. It is a noun; the surrounding sentence makes the meaning clear.

- 庭に大きな木があります。 (にわにおおきなきがあります。) - There is a large tree in the garden.
- これは木でできた机です。 (これはきでできたつくえです。) - This is a desk made of wood.

In the second example, で marks the material, not the location of an activity.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [木](../kanji/木.md) writes the native noun き. Its compound readings, such as モク in 木曜日, are not replacements for this standalone word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

木材 (もくざい) means timber or lumber. 森 (もり), "forest," names an area with many trees rather than one tree. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

木の椅子 (きのいす) is a wooden chair: の connects the material noun to the object. 木 itself is not an i-adjective meaning "wooden."
