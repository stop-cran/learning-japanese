# 勧める (すすめる) - recommend; encourage; offer

## Meaning

勧める is a transitive ichidan verb. It can recommend an action or item, encourage someone, or offer something for them to accept. The recipient takes に; a recommended or offered item takes を.

- 医者は私に運動するように勧めました。 (いしゃはわたしにうんどうするようにすすめました。) - The doctor advised me to exercise.
- 客にお茶を勧めました。 (きゃくにおちゃをすすめました。) - I offered the guest tea.

For an action, dictionary-form verb + ように勧める is useful. 本を勧める (ほんをすすめる) means "recommend a book."

## How the kanji combine

勧 contributes urging or encouraging and is read すす here; める completes the verb. Forms include 勧めます (すすめます), 勧めない (すすめない), and 勧めて (すすめて).

## Synonyms and antonyms

薦める (すすめる) is another spelling for recommending something or someone, such as a book or a candidate. The dictionary does not extend that spelling to the encouragement and offering senses taught here. [進める](進める.md), also すすめる, is a different verb meaning to advance or move something forward.

## Distinctive meaning

勧める does not establish that the other person accepted. Offering tea, recommending a book, and urging someone to exercise describe the speaker's action; the recipient can still decline. Keep the recommended thing or action separate from the person receiving the recommendation.
