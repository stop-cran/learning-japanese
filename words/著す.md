# 著す (あらわす) - author; publish a work

## Meaning

著す means writing or publishing a work, such as a book. It is a transitive
godan verb ending in す: 著します (あらわします), 著した (あらわした),
and 著して (あらわして).

- 彼女は日本の歴史について本を著しました。 (かのじょはにほんのれきしについてほんをあらわしました。) - She wrote a book about Japanese history.
- その学者は多くの本を著した。 (そのがくしゃはおおくのほんをあらわした。) - That scholar authored many books.

## How the kanji combine

[著](../kanji/著.md) contributes authorship or writing. 著わす is another spelling
of the same verb, with the same reading あらわす.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[書く](書く.md) (かく) is broader: writing a note or a character need not be authoring
a work. [出版](出版.md) (しゅっぱん) concerns publication; 出版する does not by itself
say that the publisher wrote the book. [著者](著者.md) (ちょしゃ) names an author.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not merge this verb with the homophones [表す](表す.md), "express; represent,"
and [現す](現す.md), "reveal; show." Official spelling guidance gives 著す for
writing a work and putting it out into the world. A phrase about showing emotion
is not automatically a use of 著す simply because its reading is あらわす.
