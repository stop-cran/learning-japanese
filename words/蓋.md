# 蓋 (ふた) - lid; cap

## Meaning

蓋 is a lid or cap covering an opening, such as the top of a pot, box or bottle. The kana spellings ふた and フタ are also used.

- 鍋に蓋をしてください。 (なべにふたをしてください。) - Please put a lid on the pot.
- 瓶の蓋が開きません。 (びんのふたがあきません。) - I cannot open the bottle's cap.

## How the kanji combine

蓋 writes the native noun ふた. There is no 蓋 card yet. Do not substitute a character-dictionary on reading for this everyday word's reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[カバー](カバー.md) is a broader cover or covering. [栓](栓.md) (せん) is a stopper or plug closing an opening; the shape and conventional name of the closure matter. [表紙](表紙.md) (ひょうし) is specifically a book's cover, not a generic container lid.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 蓋をする, put a lid on, 蓋を開ける (ふたをあける), open the lid, and 蓋を閉める (ふたをしめる), close it. The noun itself is the covering object, not the action of hiding or covering something.
