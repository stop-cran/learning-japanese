# うろうろ — wandering restlessly; hanging about

## Meaning

うろうろ describes moving about without a clear direction or purpose, often because someone is lost, uncertain, or restless.

- 出口が分からず、駅の中をうろうろした。 — I wandered around inside the station, unable to find the exit. (出口: でぐち.)
- 予約の時間まで、店の前をうろうろ歩いていた。 — I paced around outside the shop until my appointment time. (予約: よやく.)

Common forms are うろうろする, うろうろしている, and うろうろと歩く. The と is optional with a following motion verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぶらぶら](ぶらぶら.md) can describe a relaxed stroll with no particular destination. うろうろ more readily suggests uncertainty or restlessness. [すたすた](すたすた.md) pictures brisk, purposeful walking instead.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling someone うろうろしている can sound disapproving, especially if they are getting in the way, but the word does not establish criminal or suspicious intent. Someone looking for an exit may simply be confused. It describes the person's behavior, not a general adjective meaning "bad."
