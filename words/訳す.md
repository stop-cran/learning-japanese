# 訳す (やくす) - translate

## Meaning

訳す means to translate or interpret something from one language into another. It is a transitive godan verb: を marks the material translated, and に can mark the target language.

- この文を日本語に訳してください。
  (このぶんをにほんごにやくしてください。) - Please translate this sentence into Japanese.
- 彼女は英語の小説を日本語に訳しています。
  (かのじょはえいごのしょうせつをにほんごにやくしています。) - She is translating an English novel into Japanese.

Useful forms are 訳します (やくします), 訳して (やくして), and 訳さない (やくさない).

## How the kanji combine

[訳](../kanji/訳.md) supplies the translation element and the on-derived reading やく; す is the verb ending. This is not a two-kanji compound, and it conjugates as 訳す, not as an ichidan verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[翻訳](翻訳.md) (ほんやく) names translation and forms 翻訳する. [通訳](通訳.md) (つうやく) particularly concerns interpreting spoken communication and can also name the interpreter.

[直す](直す.md) (なおす), usually repair or correct, also occurs in expressions such as 日本語に直す, put into Japanese. That overlap does not make every repair a translation.

## Distinctive meaning

The language-to-language sense is central here. English "interpret" can also mean explain a dream, judge someone's intentions, or understand a rule; do not assume that 訳す covers all those uses.

The target language normally takes に: 英語を日本語に訳す means translate English into Japanese, not the reverse.
