# 略す (りゃくす) - abbreviate; omit

## Meaning

略す means to abbreviate or shorten an expression, and also to omit
or leave something out. It is a transitive godan verb ending in す:
略します, 略した, 略して, 略さない.
Mark the expression or omitted material with を.

- 「国際連合」を「国連」と略します。 (「こくさいれんごう」を「こくれん」とりゃくします。) - We abbreviate "United Nations" to "UN."
- 詳しい説明は略します。 (くわしいせつめいはりゃくします。) - I will omit the detailed explanation.

In XをYと略す, Y is the shortened form.

## How the kanji combine

[略](../kanji/略.md) contributes abbreviation or omission with its
on-derived reading リャク; す completes this modern godan verb.
The article type is kango, but that does not make its conjugation a
する conjugation. 略する (りゃくする) is a separately recorded verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[省略](省略.md) (しょうりゃく) and [省く](省く.md) (はぶく) overlap
in omission. [縮める](縮める.md) (ちぢめる) can shorten something.
[略語](略語.md) (りゃくご) names an abbreviation rather than the act
of abbreviating. [飛ばす](飛ばす.md) (とばす) can mean skipping material.

## Distinctive meaning

Leaving an explanation out and shortening its wording are different
operations; 略す covers both. Not every physical shortening or deletion
is therefore interchangeable with it.
This card teaches the abbreviation and omission senses, not the dictionary's
additional taking/capturing/stealing sense.
