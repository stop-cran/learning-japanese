# ぽかん — staring blankly or open-mouthed

## Meaning

ぽかん describes a blank, vacant look or an open mouth, often in surprise or confusion.

- 知らせを聞いて、彼はぽかんとした。
  — He stared blankly after hearing the news.
- 子どもたちは口をぽかんと開けて空を見た。
  — The children looked at the sky with their mouths hanging open.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぽかん emphasizes a momentary vacant or open-mouthed reaction, not focused attention.

## Distinctive meaning

It can describe the expression itself or the way someone stares.
