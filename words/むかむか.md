# むかむか — nauseated; seething with anger

## Meaning

むかむか describes a queasy, nauseated feeling or rising anger and disgust.

- 脂っこい物を食べすぎて、胃がむかむかする。 — I ate too much greasy food, and my stomach feels queasy. (脂っこい: あぶらっこい; 胃: い.)
- あの失礼な言い方を思い出すと、むかむかする。 — Remembering that rude way of speaking makes my anger rise. (失礼: しつれい.)

Use むかむかする or むかむかしている. と is possible before する: むかむかとする. The subject or surrounding cause helps distinguish nausea from anger.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いらいら](いらいら.md) commonly emphasizes irritation or impatience; むかむか can emphasize stronger anger or disgust, but also has the bodily nausea sense. [すっきり](すっきり.md) can contrast with lingering discomfort in an appropriate context.

## Distinctive meaning

胃がむかむかする does not say that someone has actually vomited. Conversely, anger-related むかむか need not mean literal stomach illness. It is useful in everyday reports of feelings, not a diagnosis or a blanket replacement for every kind of pain.
