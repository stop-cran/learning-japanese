# 伝統 (でんとう) - tradition

## Meaning

伝統 is a tradition: something handed down and maintained within a community, institution, or field. It is a noun, used with verbs such as 守る (まもる), "preserve," and 受け継ぐ (うけつぐ), "inherit; carry on."

- この町では古い伝統を守っています。 (このまちではふるいでんとうをまもっています。) - This town preserves an old tradition.
- 若い職人がその伝統を受け継いでいます。 (わかいしょくにんがそのでんとうをうけついでいます。) - Young craftspeople are carrying on that tradition.

The adjective-like form is 伝統的な (でんとうてきな), "traditional," as in 伝統的な音楽, "traditional music."

## How the kanji combine

[伝](../kanji/伝.md) contributes transmitting or handing down, and 統 contributes continuity or a connected line. Together they suggest continuity maintained through transmission. The on readings are デン + トウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[習慣](習慣.md) (しゅうかん) means a habit or custom and may concern an individual's repeated behavior. [文化](文化.md) (ぶんか), "culture," is broader than a particular tradition. A practice can belong to all these categories in different descriptions.

## Distinctive meaning

伝統 highlights what has been passed on, not simply something done repeatedly this week. It also does not automatically mean that a practice is good, unchanging, or legally required. Preserving a tradition and adapting its details are not necessarily contradictory; the word alone does not settle that debate.
