# 書留 (かきとめ) - registered mail; written record

## Meaning

書留 is registered mail: a postal service that keeps a record of delivery handling. It abbreviates 書留郵便 (かきとめゆうびん).

- この書類を書留で送ってください。 (このしょるいをかきとめでおくってください。) - Please send these documents by registered mail.
- 書留を受け取りました。 (かきとめをうけとりました。) - I received the registered mail.

で marks the sending method. The dictionary also recognizes 書留 as a record or the act of noting something down; the postal meaning is the practical starting point in these examples.

## How the kanji combine

[書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing and [留](../kanji/留.md) keeping something. This native compound is read かき + とめ, not ショ + リュウ. The headword spelling does not show all the kana found in the related verb 書き留める (かきとめる), "write down; make a note of."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[郵便](郵便.md) (ゆうびん) is the broader mail/postal-service word. [記録](記録.md) (きろく) is a record or recording more generally; a record is not necessarily registered mail. [メモ](メモ.md) names a note or memo.

## Distinctive meaning

登録 (とうろく) is useful for registration with an organization or system; do not use 書留 for signing up for a course. The postal service has different types and conditions. The word alone does not promise one universal fee, delivery time or compensation limit.
