# 蘇る (よみがえる) - revive; be restored; memories return

## Meaning

蘇る means to come back to life, revive, or be restored.
It can also describe memories returning vividly.
The revived thing or returning memory takes が.

- 昔の記憶が鮮やかに蘇りました。 (むかしのきおくがあざやかによみがえりました。) - Old memories came back vividly.
- 修理によって、古い時計が蘇りました。 (しゅうりによって、ふるいとけいがよみがえりました。) - The old clock was brought back to life through repair.

This is an **intransitive godan** verb, despite ending in える:
蘇ります (よみがえります), 蘇った (よみがえった), and
蘇らない (よみがえらない). The past is not 蘇えた.

## How the kanji combine

蘇 contributes revival or restoration. Learn the whole native verb
よみがえる, not an on-reading compound.
甦る is another recognized spelling. Dictionaries also record
蘇える and 甦える as irregular okurigana spellings; those spellings
do not turn the verb into an ichidan verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[回復](回復.md) (かいふく) overlaps with recovery or restoration.
[思い出す](思い出す.md) (おもいだす) concerns someone recalling
something: 私が昔を思い出す (わたしがむかしをおもいだす),
"I recall the past." Compare 記憶が蘇る, where the memory returns.
消える (きえる), disappear, can contrast with a memory returning,
but is not an opposite for every revival.

## Distinctive meaning

Revival can be literal or figurative: a tradition, activity or
old object can be described as coming back to life.
The clock example does not claim that a clock is a living organism.
Nor does 記憶が蘇る mean deliberately memorizing new information.

The verb describes revival itself, not a direct action with the
revived thing as an object. Do not change 時計が蘇る into
時計を蘇る to mean that someone repairs the clock.
