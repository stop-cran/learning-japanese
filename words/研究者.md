# 研究者 (けんきゅうしゃ) — researcher

## Meaning

研究者 is a researcher: someone who carries out research. It identifies an activity or field-based
role without specifying one particular employment position.

- 彼女は日本語の研究者です。 (かのじょは にほんごの けんきゅうしゃです。) — She is a researcher of the Japanese language.
- この会議には多くの研究者が参加します。  
  (この かいぎには おおくの けんきゅうしゃが さんかします。)  
  — Many researchers will take part in this conference.

## How the kanji combine

[研究](研究.md) けんきゅう is research: [研](../kanji/研.md) contributes studying or refining,
and [究](../kanji/究.md) investigating thoroughly. [者](../kanji/者.md) シャ identifies a person.
The formation is activity + person, with on readings ケン + キュウ + シャ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[研究員](研究員.md) (けんきゅういん) also refers to a researcher but presents the person through a research staff
role in an organization. One person can be both. There is no natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

研究者 does not automatically mean an amateur, a professor, or an independently employed scholar.
It leaves those distinctions unspecified. Do not infer a rank difference just from 者 versus 員.

For 研究者 compared with 研究員, see
[occupation and role endings](../articles/occupation-and-role-endings.md).
