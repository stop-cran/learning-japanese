# 楽器 (がっき) - musical instrument

## Meaning

楽器 is a musical instrument. It is a noun naming the instrument, not the action of playing it.

- この楽器の名前を教えてください。 (このがっきのなまえをおしえてください。) - Please tell me the name of this instrument.
- 子供のころから楽器を演奏しています。 (こどものころからがっきをえんそうしています。) - I have played musical instruments since I was a child.

楽器を演奏する (がっきをえんそうする) means to play a musical instrument.

## How the kanji combine

[楽](../kanji/楽.md) contributes music here, not the "easy" sense read ラク. 器 contributes an implement or instrument. Music specifies the purpose of the instrument. ガク + キ becomes がっき; learn the small っ with the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ピアノ](ピアノ.md), [バイオリン](バイオリン.md), and [ギター](ギター.md) are particular instruments. [道具](道具.md) (どうぐ), a tool or implement, is broader and does not by itself specify music. Instrument names do not need an ordinary antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Different instruments take different everyday playing verbs: ピアノを[弾く](弾く.md) (ひく), play the piano, but [笛](笛.md)を[吹く](吹く.md) (ふえをふく), play a flute or blow a whistle. Do not assume that one Japanese equivalent of English "play" works with every 楽器.
