# 右 (みぎ) — right-hand side

## Meaning

右 means the right side or direction. It is a noun: use の to describe a noun on the right, and a suitable particle when giving a location or direction.

- 次の角を右に曲がってください。  
  つぎのかどをみぎにまがってください。  
  "Please turn right at the next corner."
- 右のドアを開けてください。  
  みぎのドアをあけてください。  
  "Please open the door on the right."

## How the kanji combine

[右](../kanji/右.md) is a single kanji with the native reading みぎ. Compounds can use other readings, such as ゆう in 左右 (さゆう), "left and right".

## Synonyms and antonyms

左 (ひだり), "left", is the direct directional opposite. 右側 (みぎがわ) makes "the right side" explicit and overlaps with this spatial use.

## Distinctive meaning

右 depends on viewpoint. The right side of someone facing you appears on your left. Also, English "right" meaning "correct" is not 右; use an expression appropriate to correctness instead.
