# クリーム - cream

## Meaning

クリーム is cream, including food cream and cream-like products such as skin cream. It is a noun; the surrounding words identify what kind is meant.

- コーヒーにクリームを入れます。 (コーヒーにクリームをいれます。) - I put cream in my coffee.
- 手にクリームを塗りました。 (てにクリームをぬりました。) - I put cream on my hands.

The first example concerns a food product; the second concerns skin care. に marks where the cream goes, while を marks the cream itself.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "cream." There are no kanji components. The long-vowel mark belongs after リ: クリーム, not クリム.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[バター](バター.md), butter, is a related dairy product but not an interchangeable ingredient. 生クリーム (なまクリーム) identifies fresh cream used in cooking and desserts. These are product distinctions, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume every クリーム is edible: ハンドクリーム is hand cream. Conversely, the word does not by itself specify that a dessert cream is whipped, sweetened, or made from dairy ingredients. JMdict also records クリーム as an abbreviation of アイスクリーム, but the full アイスクリーム is clearer when ice cream is specifically intended without supporting context.
