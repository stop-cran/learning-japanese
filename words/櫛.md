# 櫛 (くし) - comb

## Meaning

櫛 is a comb, a toothed implement used for arranging hair. It is a noun naming the tool, not by itself the verb "comb."

- 櫛で髪をとかしました。 (くしでかみをとかしました。) - I combed my hair with a comb.
- 櫛をかばんに入れておきました。 (くしをかばんにいれておきました。) - I put a comb in my bag for later.

櫛で髪をとかす (くしでかみをとかす) describes using the comb on one's hair.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun, 櫛 read くし, not an on-reading compound. The character has no kanji card in the current collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

A ブラシ is a brush; the tools share uses but do not have the same structure. 串 (くし), skewer, is a different word with the same reading, not another spelling of the hair tool.

## Distinctive meaning

In the examples, で marks the tool and を marks the hair being combed. Do not turn the English noun/verb pair "comb" into an assumed Japanese 櫛する verb.
