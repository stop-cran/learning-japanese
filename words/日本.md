# 日本 (にほん／にっぽん) — Japan

## Meaning

日本 is Japan: 日本に住んでいる (live in Japan), 日本人 にほんじん (a Japanese person), 日本語 にほんご (the Japanese language), 日本料理 にほんりょうり (Japanese food).

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) (sun) + [本](../kanji/本.md) (origin, source): "the origin of the sun", that is, the land where the sun rises, as seen from China. Both are on readings, with two accepted pronunciations: にほん (ニホン) and にっぽん (ニッポン).
The latter doubles the ホ to ッポ. It is the stronger, more official-sounding form, used in sports cheers (ニッポン!), on stamps and banknotes and in formal titles such as 日本銀行 にっぽんぎんこう (Bank of Japan).
にほん is the more common in everyday speech, but both are accepted.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 日本国 にほんこく／にっぽんこく (formal), 我が国 わがくに ("our country"), 日の本 ひのもと (literary).
- Related: 外国 がいこく (foreign country), 東洋 とうよう (the East).

## Distinctive meaning

With 人 and 語, にほん is usual (日本人, 日本語). Both readings are correct; にっぽん sounds more emphatic or formal, so use にほん by default.
