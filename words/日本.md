# 日本 (にほん／にっぽん) — Japan

## Meaning

日本 is Japan: 日本に住んでいる (live in Japan), 日本人 にほんじん (a Japanese person), 日本語 にほんご (the Japanese language), 日本料理 にほんりょうり (Japanese food).

Both にほん and にっぽん are readings of the country name.

- 日本に住んでいます。 (にほんにすんでいます。) — I live in Japan.
- 頑張れ、日本！ (がんばれ、にっぽん！) — Go, Japan!

The second is a cheering example. The two readings do not name different countries.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) (sun) + [本](../kanji/本.md) (origin, source) gives the familiar
component association "the origin of the sun." Both pronunciations use on-reading forms:
にほん (ニホン) and にっぽん (ニッポン). In にっぽん, notice the small っ and the
ぽ sound. This is a whole-word pronunciation to learn, not a general rule for changing ホ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 日本国 にほんこく／にっぽんこく (formal), 我が国 わがくに ("our country"), 日の本 ひのもと (literary).
- Related: [外国](外国.md) がいこく (foreign country), 東洋 とうよう (the East).

## Distinctive meaning

Learn compound readings individually: 日本人 is にほんじん and 日本語 is にほんご
in the examples here. The existence of two readings for the country name does not mean
every compound or proper institutional name freely alternates.
The primary card reading remains にほん; にっぽん is also taught as an accepted country-name reading.
