# 万年筆 (まんねんひつ) -- fountain pen

## Meaning

万年筆 is a fountain pen: a pen with a nib supplied by an internal ink reservoir or cartridge.

祖父にもらった万年筆で手紙を書きました。 (そふにもらったまんねんひつでてがみをかきました。) -- I wrote a letter with the fountain pen my grandfather gave me.

The writing instrument takes で. The letter being written takes を.

## How the kanji combine

[万](../kanji/万.md), read まん, and [年](../kanji/年.md), read ねん, literally suggest ten thousand years, an image of long use. 筆, read ひつ, names a writing implement. The established compound means fountain pen, not a brush with a literal ten-thousand-year lifetime.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ペン is broader. ボールペン is a ballpoint pen, using a small rolling ball rather than a fountain-pen nib. 鉛筆 (えんぴつ) is a pencil.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 万年筆 for every expensive or refillable pen. The word identifies a particular kind of writing instrument. This card teaches the reading まんねんひつ; keep the final part ひつ distinct from ぴつ in 鉛筆.
