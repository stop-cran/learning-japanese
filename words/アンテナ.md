# アンテナ - antenna

## Meaning

アンテナ is an antenna for transmitting or receiving signals. It is a noun, as in
テレビのアンテナ, "a television antenna." A figurative use concerns a means of
gathering information or noticing new developments.

- 屋根にアンテナを取り付けました。 (やねにアンテナをとりつけました。) - We installed an antenna on the roof.
- 新しい情報にアンテナを張っています。 (あたらしいじょうほうにアンテナをはっています。) - I keep my antenna out for new information.

The second example is figurative: the speaker stays alert to information rather
than stretching a physical antenna.

## Synonyms and antonyms

電波 (でんぱ) means radio or electromagnetic waves, not the antenna
that handles them. [情報](情報.md) (じょうほう) is the information being gathered.
There is no single direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the katakana spelling アンテナ as a whole. The figurative use does not mean
that any information itself can be called an アンテナ; it concerns the means of
collecting it or one's alertness to it.
