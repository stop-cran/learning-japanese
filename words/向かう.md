# 向かう (むかう) -- face; head toward

## Meaning

向かう means to face something or to move toward a destination. The surrounding action tells you whether someone stays in place or travels.

- 窓に向かって座りました。 (まどにむかってすわりました。) -- I sat facing the window.
- 今、駅に向かっています。 (いま、えきにむかっています。) -- I am heading toward the station now.

The target or direction commonly takes に or へ. This is an intransitive godan verb: 向かいます (むかいます), 向かわない (むかわない), 向かって (むかって), and 向かった (むかった).

## How the kanji combine

[向](../kanji/向.md) contributes facing or orientation and supplies む. The written かう completes the native verb. Its て-form follows the う-to-って pattern, not the ichidan pattern.

## Synonyms and antonyms

行く (いく), go, is broader and does not itself emphasize orientation toward a target. 向ける (むける) is transitive when someone directs or turns something toward a target.

## Distinctive meaning

Heading toward a place does not establish arrival. 駅に向かっています reports being on the way; 駅に着きました (えきにつきました) reports having reached the station.
