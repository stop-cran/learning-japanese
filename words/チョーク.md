# チョーク - chalk

## Meaning

チョーク commonly means chalk, including the material used to write on a blackboard.
It is a noun.

- 白いチョークで黒板に名前を書きました。
  (しろいチョークでこくばんになまえをかきました。) - I wrote my name on the blackboard in white chalk.

で marks the writing material or instrument. 白墨 (はくぼく) is another
dictionary word for chalk.

## How the kanji combine

This English-derived word is written in katakana.
The long-vowel mark in チョー is part of the spelling; there are no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鉛筆](鉛筆.md) (えんぴつ), pencil, is another writing instrument, not a synonym.
The separate loanword from English chock is also recorded as チョーク
or チョック: a wedge placed under a wheel, or a doorstop.
Those are not kinds of blackboard chalk.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list combines chock and chalk, but the pinned principal entry
1078240 contains only chalk. The chock use belongs to separate entry 2854421.
Another entry, 2854422, records a wrestling chokehold and an engine choke.
The shared Japanese spelling does not merge these English origins or uses.
Context identifies which チョーク is meant; the additional identities do not
each receive an independent N2 classification here.
