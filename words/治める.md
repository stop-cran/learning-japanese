# 治める (おさめる) - govern; settle a dispute

## Meaning

治める is a transitive ichidan verb meaning to govern or administer, and also to subdue or settle a disturbance or dispute. The country, community, or problem being dealt with takes を.

- その王は国を治めました。 (そのおうはくにをおさめました。) - That king governed the country.
- 二人の争いを治めるのは難しかった。 (ふたりのあらそいをおさめるのはむずかしかった。) - It was difficult to settle the dispute between the two.

The polite form is 治めます (おさめます), the past 治めた (おさめた), and the negative 治めない (おさめない).

## How the kanji combine

[治](../kanji/治.md) contributes bringing order or governing in this word. It writes おさ, with める in kana. Its use here is not the same reading as 治す (なおす), "cure; heal."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[支配](支配.md)する (しはいする) overlaps in ruling or controlling and can emphasize domination. [管理](管理.md)する (かんりする) concerns management or oversight; managing a password or timetable is not automatically 治める.

治まる (おさまる) is intransitive: 争いが治まる (あらそいがおさまる), "the dispute dies down," contrasts with someone settling it.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not choose the spelling from sound alone. [納める](納める.md) is used for paying taxes; [収める](収める.md) is used for achieving results. 治める here concerns governance or bringing a disturbance under control, not either of those actions. The English "settle" is also broader: settling into a new home can be [落ち着く](落ち着く.md) (おちつく).
