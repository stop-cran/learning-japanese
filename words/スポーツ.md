# スポーツ -- sport; sports

## Meaning

スポーツ names sport or sports, such as tennis, swimming, or football. It can refer to the broad category rather than one named event.

週末は友達とスポーツをします。 (しゅうまつはともだちとスポーツをします。) -- I play sports with friends on weekends.

Use スポーツをする for participating in sport. スポーツを見る (スポーツをみる) means watching sports, which does not imply playing them.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword comes from English "sports." The ー lengthens ポ. Japanese スポーツ does not change its ending to distinguish one sport from several; context supplies that distinction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

運動 (うんどう) includes exercise and physical activity more broadly. 競技 (きょうぎ) focuses on a competitive event or discipline. Their meanings overlap, but not every kind of 運動 is being presented as a sport.

## Distinctive meaning

There is no single universal opposite of スポーツ. When describing a preference, distinguish liking to watch from liking to participate: スポーツが好きです (スポーツがすきです) alone does not tell the listener which you mean.
