# こらえる - endure; hold back

## Meaning

こらえる means enduring something difficult or holding back a feeling or response. It is a transitive ichidan verb: こらえます, こらえて, こらえない.

- 寒さをこらえて、外で待ちました。 (さむさをこらえて、そとでまちました。) - I endured the cold and waited outside.
- 笑いをこらえるのが大変でした。 (わらいをこらえるのがたいへんでした。) - It was hard to keep from laughing.

What one endures or restrains takes を. The word can also mean forgiving or letting something pass: 今度だけはこらえてください (こんどだけはこらえてください), "Please let it go just this once."

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 堪える is a recognized spelling; 怺える is marked rare. Learn the reading こらえる here. The same character 堪 also occurs with other readings, so its presence alone does not select this verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[我慢](我慢.md) (がまん), with する, overlaps in endurance and self-restraint. 耐える (たえる) overlaps in enduring a difficulty, but the practical patterns differ: 寒さをこらえる versus 寒さに耐える, "endure the cold."

[押さえる](押さえる.md) (おさえる) has suppression uses as well as physical holding; 抑える is another おさえる spelling used for controlling feelings. [許す](許す.md) (ゆるす) overlaps with the forgiveness sense, not with every example of enduring cold.

## Distinctive meaning

Holding back laughter or tears is not the same construction as an object withstanding heat. こらえる especially suits a person's endurance or restraint; do not infer that a heat-resistant material can use every expression with this verb.

こらえる and たえる have separate lexical families despite the shared possible spelling 堪える. Do not change the reading to たえる while keeping this card's conjugation and examples.
