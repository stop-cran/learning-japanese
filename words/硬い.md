# 硬い (かたい) - hard; stiff

## Meaning

硬い describes something hard or resistant to bending or pressure. It is an i-adjective: 硬い石 (かたいいし), "a hard stone," with no な.

- このパンは硬くて、食べにくいです。 (このパンはかたくて、たべにくいです。) - This bread is hard and difficult to eat.

Its negative is 硬くない (かたくない), and its past is 硬かった (かたかった).

## How the kanji combine

硬 represents かた in this native adjective; い completes the spelling. 硬い is a useful spelling for material hardness, especially stone and metal.

## Synonyms and antonyms

柔らかい (やわらかい), "soft," is the relevant physical contrast. 固い and 堅い are other spellings of かたい whose uses overlap, but spelling choices depend on the expression; they are not freely interchangeable everywhere.

## Distinctive meaning

English "hard" also means difficult. A difficult question is 難しい問題 (むずかしいもんだい), not 硬い問題. Keep resistance or stiffness separate from difficulty when choosing the Japanese adjective.
