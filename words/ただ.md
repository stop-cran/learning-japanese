# ただ (ただ) - free of charge; merely

## Meaning

ただ has several related uses. As a noun-like expression, it means free of charge: ただです, "it is free," and ただで, "without paying."

- この地図はただでもらえます。 (このちずはただでもらえます。) - You can get this map for free.

As an adverb, it means just or merely:

- ただ話を聞きたかっただけです。 (ただはなしをききたかっただけです。) - I simply wanted to listen.

Before a noun, ただの can mean ordinary or mere: ただの箱 (ただのはこ), "an ordinary box." As a conjunction, ただ adds a qualification:

- 行きたいです。ただ、時間がありません。 (いきたいです。ただ、じかんがありません。) - I want to go. However, I do not have time.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses kana, the dictionary's usual spelling. The source spellings 只 and 唯 belong to the same entry and are marked rare kanji forms. The dictionary especially associates 只 with the free-of-charge sense when kanji is used; that is not an absolute spelling boundary.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[無料](無料.md) (むりょう) means free of charge, not "merely." [普通](普通.md) (ふつう) overlaps with the ordinary sense, not the conjunction. 有料 (ゆうりょう), "paid/fee-charging," contrasts with the no-charge use.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the construction: ただで concerns price, ただの can downplay something, and sentence-initial ただ can introduce a reservation. A shared dictionary entry does not make these meanings interchangeable in every sentence.
