# コンピュータ -- computer

## Meaning

コンピュータ is a computer. It is a general term rather than the name of one particular size or shape of device.

このコンピュータで日本語の文章を書いています。 (このコンピュータでにほんごのぶんしょうをかいています。) -- I am writing Japanese text on this computer.

Here で marks the tool used for writing. コンピュータを使う (コンピュータをつかう) means to use a computer.

## How the kanji combine

The word is adapted from English "computer" and written in katakana. Small ュ combines with ピ, followed by ー: ピュー.
[コンピューター](コンピューター.md), with a final long-vowel mark, is another standard spelling of the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

パソコン, shortened from パーソナルコンピュータ, refers to a personal computer. It is narrower than the general category コンピュータ.

## Distinctive meaning

The presence or absence of final ー does not distinguish two kinds of machine. Keep the spelling consistent with the context or publication. A コンピュータ is also not merely its screen; モニター names a display rather than the whole computing device.
