# 洋品店 (ようひんてん) - Western-style apparel and accessories shop

## Meaning

洋品店 is a shop handling Western-style clothing and associated
accessories or personal articles. It names the shop, not a garment.

- 駅前の洋品店でシャツを買いました。 (えきまえのようひんてんでしゃつをかいました。) - I bought a shirt at the apparel shop in front of the station.
- 母は洋品店を経営しています。 (はははようひんてんをけいえいしています。) - My mother runs an apparel and accessories shop.

The term does not specify the shop's size or say that every item
is imported from another country.

## How the kanji combine

[洋](../kanji/洋.md) supplies Western-style, [品](../kanji/品.md)
goods, and [店](../kanji/店.md) shop. 洋品 (ようひん) concerns
Western-style articles, especially clothing, accessories and personal
items. The on readings combine as ヨウ + ヒン + テン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[洋服](洋服.md) (ようふく) names Western-style clothing itself.
[店](店.md) (みせ) and [ショップ](ショップ.md) are broader words for
a shop. An [アクセサリー](アクセサリー.md) is an accessory, not
the place selling it. There is no useful single lexical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The 洋 here classifies the goods as Western-style; it does not mean
that the proprietor or the shop must be foreign. Nor is 洋品店 simply
a synonym for every kind of shop that happens to sell imported goods.
The clothing-and-related-articles scope matters.
