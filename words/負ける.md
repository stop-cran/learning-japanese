# 負ける (まける) — lose a contest

## Meaning

負ける means to lose or be defeated in a contest. The loser is the subject, not an object marked with を.
The opponent or contest can be marked with に.

- 試合に負けました。 (しあいにまけました。) — I lost the match.
- 弟に負けました。 (おとうとにまけました。) — I lost to my younger brother.

It is an ichidan verb: replace the final る with ます for 負けます, and with た for 負けた.
"Ichidan" names this conjugation pattern.

## How the kanji combine

There is one written kanji: [負](../kanji/負.md), using its losing sense.
The native reading is まける, and ける is written in kana. This is not a compound of separate kanji meanings.
The same character also appears in 負う (おう), "bear a burden"; that is a different verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勝つ](勝つ.md) (かつ), "win," is the direct contrast: 試合に勝つ (しあいにかつ), "win a match."
Winning and losing both use に in this contest pattern; copying English "win/lose the match" can lead to the wrong particle.

## Distinctive meaning

This use concerns an unfavorable result, not misplacing an object.
JMdict also records a price-reduction sense, but that has different grammar; the contest examples here do not teach it.
For the defeat sense, 敗ける is another spelling recorded by the dictionary; 負ける is the spelling used in this article.
