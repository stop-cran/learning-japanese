# じっと (じっと) - still; intently; patiently

## Meaning

じっと is an adverb for staying still, directing sustained attention, or enduring patiently. じっとする means "stay still"; before another verb, no additional と is needed.

- 写真を撮るので、じっとしていてください。 (しゃしんをとるので、じっとしていてください。) - Please stay still while I take the photo.
- 猫が窓の外をじっと見ています。 (ねこがまどのそとをじっとみています。) - The cat is gazing intently out of the window.

じっと耐える (じっとたえる) means "endure patiently." The following verb identifies whether stillness, concentration, or restraint is the main point.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native mimetic expression, normally written in kana. It does not need a kanji decomposition or a foreign-language origin. The small っ marks a brief closure before と; the emphatic spelling じーっと stretches the first part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[静か](静か.md) (しずか) describes quietness or calm. A person can be quiet while moving, so 静かに and じっと are not identical. [動く](動く.md) (うごく), "move," contrasts with the stillness use.

## Distinctive meaning

じっと見る is sustained, intent looking, not merely seeing something by chance. Likewise, じっと待つ (じっとまつ) suggests waiting without restless action. The word does not guarantee literal silence in every use; it highlights a lack of movement, a fixed focus, or controlled response according to context.
