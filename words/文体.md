# 文体 (ぶんたい) - writing style

## Meaning

文体 is the style or manner of written expression. It can concern an author's
characteristic writing or a form of written language, such as classical versus modern
usage. It is a noun.

- この作家の文体は読みやすいです。 (このさっかのぶんたいはよみやすいです。) - This author's writing style is easy to read.
- 文体を「です・ます」に統一してください。 (ぶんたいを「です・ます」にとういつしてください。) - Please make the writing consistently use the desu/masu style.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) contributes writing or text; [体](../kanji/体.md) contributes
form or style here, rather than a person's body. The compound names the form of
written expression: ブン + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[スタイル](スタイル.md) is a broader style term. [文章](文章.md) (ぶんしょう)
can concern a written text and its style; 文体 specifically foregrounds how it is written.
書体 (しょたい), "typeface or style of lettering," instead concerns the shapes of
written characters, not the author's wording.

## Distinctive meaning

文体 can involve sentence endings, vocabulary, rhythm and patterns of expression.
Changing a font need not change the 文体. Conversely, changing from a formal written
style to a conversational one need not involve changing the typeface.
The language-form sense is not a claim that all writers using modern Japanese have
the same personal style.
