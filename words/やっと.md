# やっと -- at last; barely

## Meaning

やっと describes something finally happening after waiting or effort. It can also mean managing something only just or with difficulty.

- 長く待って、やっとバスが来ました。 (ながくまって、やっとバスがきました。) -- After a long wait, the bus finally came.
- 駅まで走って、やっと電車に間に合いました。 (えきまではしって、やっとでんしゃにまにあいました。) -- I ran to the station and only just caught the train.

It is an adverb, so it goes with the event or achievement rather than conjugating itself.

## How the kanji combine

The ordinary spelling is hiragana. The small っ is part of the pronunciation: やっと, not やと. The rare dictionary spelling 漸と is not needed for everyday use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ようやく also expresses eventual achievement. ついに marks an eventual outcome and can be used for unwelcome developments; やっと more readily presents an outcome as awaited or hard-won.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not reduce やっと to a neutral "later." It adds the speaker's sense of delay, effort, or a narrow margin. The "finally" and "barely" readings can overlap when someone succeeds after struggling.
