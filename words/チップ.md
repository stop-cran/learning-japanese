# チップ (チップ) - tip; chip

## Meaning

チップ includes separate loanwords corresponding to English **tip**
and **chip**. A gratuity for service belongs to the tip entry.
A microchip, casino token, or wood chip belongs to the separate chip entry.

- 荷物を運んでくれた人にチップを渡しました。 (にもつをはこんでくれたひとにチップをわたしました。) - I gave a tip to the person who carried my luggage.
- この機械には小さなチップが入っています。 (このきかいにはちいさなチップがはいっています。) - This machine contains a small chip.
- カジノでチップを使いました。 (カジノでチップをつかいました。) - I used chips at a casino.

The first is a gratuity; the other two illustrate different chip uses.
For wood, 木材のチップ (もくざいのチップ) identifies the material.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana form with a small ッ, not チプ.
Its shared Japanese spelling does not turn the English tip and chip
sources into one dictionary identity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

心付け (こころづけ) also means a gratuity.
[お礼](御礼.md) (おれい) is broader thanks or a gift of thanks;
not every お礼 is money paid for service.
[先端](先端.md) (せんたん), "tip; leading edge," concerns an
end or forefront rather than a gratuity.

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding action and setting select the identity. A service
payment is not a semiconductor just because both are チップ.
Tipping practices vary by setting; knowing the noun does not establish
that a tip is expected or appropriate in a particular transaction.

The tip family also records a baseball foul tip and a ballpoint-pen
tip. Those do not establish that every English use of "tip,"
including a piece of advice, is automatically チップ.
