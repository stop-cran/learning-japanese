# 柱 (はしら) - pillar; mainstay

## Meaning

柱 is a pillar or structural post. It can also be a figurative mainstay:
a person or thing that others depend on.

- この柱が屋根を支えています。 (このはしらがやねをささえています。) - This pillar supports the roof.
- 彼女はチームの柱です。 (かのじょはチームのはしらです。) - She is a mainstay of the team.

The first example names a physical support; the second does not mean that
the person is literally a building component.

## How the kanji combine

[柱](../kanji/柱.md) writes the whole native noun はしら without okurigana.
In 支柱 (しちゅう), prop or support, the same character uses the on reading
チュウ. Do not split はしら into separate readings for the visible shapes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[支える](支える.md) (ささえる) is the verb support or hold up, as in the roof
example. The English support also translates [援助](援助.md) (えんじょ),
aid or assistance, and [賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい), agreement with a proposal.
Those uses are not all names for physical posts.

[棒](棒.md) (ぼう) names a stick, rod, or pole by its shape. A pole may serve
as a structural post, but 棒 alone does not say that it supports anything.

## Distinctive meaning

The English post here means a structural support, not mail or a postbox.
The figurative use emphasizes a main source of strength or stability;
calling someone チームの柱 is not merely saying that the person agrees
with the team's opinions.
