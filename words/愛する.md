# 愛する (あいする) - to love

## Meaning

愛する is a transitive verb meaning "to love." Mark the person, place, or thing loved with を.
It can describe family affection, romantic love, or deep attachment to something.

- 彼は家族を愛しています。 (かれはかぞくをあいしています。) - He loves his family.
- 彼女は音楽を愛する人です。 (かのじょはおんがくをあいするひとです。) - She is someone who loves music.

愛している expresses an ongoing feeling. Useful forms include 愛します, 愛した, and the negative 愛さない.

## How the kanji combine

[愛](../kanji/愛.md) supplies the meaning "love" and the on reading アイ.
Adding する makes the verb. Compare the noun [愛](愛.md) (あい), "love; affection":
家族への愛 (かぞくへのあい) is "love for one's family," whereas 家族を愛する is "love one's family."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[好き](好き.md) (すき) expresses liking or fondness and can also convey romantic affection.
Its grammar differs: 音楽が好きです versus 音楽を愛しています.
[嫌い](嫌い.md) (きらい), "disliked," contrasts in feeling but is not a verb with the same conjugation.

## Distinctive meaning

愛する presents love as a substantial attachment. That can make it a weightier choice than English
casual "I love this snack!" Context and the relationship matter; it is not limited to romantic declarations.
The second example uses a plain-form verb before 人, not an adjective ending in な.
