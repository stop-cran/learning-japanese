# マイク (マイク) - microphone

## Meaning

マイク is a noun for a microphone: equipment that receives sound for amplification, recording, or transmission.

- マイクを使って話してください。 (マイクをつかってはなしてください。) - Please speak using the microphone.
- このマイクは声をよく拾います。 (このマイクはこえをよくひろいます。) - This microphone picks up voices well.

マイクを使う (マイクをつかう) means "use a microphone." In the second example, 声を拾う describes capturing sound, not physically picking up a voice.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword, so there are no written kanji to analyze. The dictionary marks マイク as an abbreviation of マイクロホン, "microphone." The short form has three kana units: マ・イ・ク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

マイクロホン names the same equipment in its longer form. A speaker, スピーカー, outputs sound rather than receiving it; the devices play complementary roles, not the roles of an adjective and its opposite. [声](声.md) (こえ) is the voice the equipment may capture.

## Distinctive meaning

A マイク is not defined by making sound louder: it can also be used for recording or a call. It is the sound-input part of the setup. Although マイク can also look like a person's foreign name in Japanese writing, the equipment meaning here belongs to the microphone entry; context distinguishes a person from the device.
