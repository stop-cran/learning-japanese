# 以前 (いぜん) - before; previously

## Meaning

以前 locates something earlier than a reference point. After a time expression it means "before; prior to"; used independently, it means "previously" or refers to the past. 以前の + noun describes an earlier or former thing.

- この建物は戦争以前に建てられました。 (このたてものはせんそういぜんにたてられました。) - This building was built before the war.
- 以前は大阪に住んでいました。 (いぜんはおおさかにすんでいました。) - I used to live in Osaka.

以前は makes the earlier situation the topic, often inviting comparison with the present. The past tense comes from the predicate, not from 以前 itself.

## How the kanji combine

[以](../kanji/以.md) marks a reference or boundary in expressions like this; [前](../kanji/前.md) supplies "before." Together they locate something on the earlier side of a reference point.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[前](前.md) (まえ) also expresses "before": 食事の前 (しょくじのまえ), "before a meal." [以来](以来.md) (いらい) means "since," looking forward from a starting point. It is not a synonym for 以前.

## Distinctive meaning

以前 does not itself mean "since." Compare 以前の仕事 (いぜんのしごと), "my former job," with a period continuing from a starting date. For a duration measured back from now, 三年前 (さんねんまえ), "three years ago," is a straightforward model; do not translate English "ago" mechanically by adding 以前.
