# スーツケース (スーツケース) — suitcase

## Meaning

スーツケース means a suitcase used to carry clothes and other belongings when traveling. The word does not itself specify size, wheels, or whether it is checked baggage.

- 服をスーツケースに入れました。  
  ふくをスーツケースにいれました。  
  "I put my clothes in the suitcase."
- 空港でスーツケースを預けました。  
  くうこうでスーツケースをあずけました。  
  "I checked my suitcase at the airport."

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to "suitcase". Its visible parts are スーツ and ケース; keep both long-vowel marks. There are no kanji to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

かばん is a broader word for a bag or case. 荷物 (にもつ), "luggage; belongings", names what someone carries collectively and is not limited to suitcases. There is no ordinary direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Despite the スーツ part, a スーツケース is not restricted to carrying a suit. It names the type of travel container, while its contents can be clothing, books, or other belongings.
