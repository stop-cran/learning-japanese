# 温暖 (おんだん) - warm; mild

## Meaning

温暖 describes warmth or mild temperatures, especially in descriptions of a
region's climate. It is a na-adjective and can also function as a noun.
Use 温暖な before a noun and 温暖です as a polite predicate.

- この地方は一年中温暖です。 (このちほうはいちねんじゅうおんだんです。) - This region has a mild climate throughout the year.
- 温暖な気候を利用して、果物を育てています。 (おんだんなきこうをりようして、くだものをそだてています。) - They make use of the mild climate to grow fruit.

温暖な地方 means "a warm region"; it does not say that every day there is hot.

## How the kanji combine

温 and 暖 both contribute warmth. Their related meanings reinforce each other in
a compound read オン + ダン. This is a description of temperature, not a claim
that each character marks a separate season.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暖かい](暖かい.md) (あたたかい) can describe warm weather and many everyday
experiences. 温暖 suits a general climate description; it is not a replacement for
every use of "warm." 寒冷 (かんれい), "cold," gives a useful climatic contrast.
[寒い](寒い.md) (さむい) is an everyday adjective for cold weather or feeling cold.

## Distinctive meaning

地球温暖化 (ちきゅうおんだんか) means global warming: 化 adds a change into a
warmer state. 温暖 alone describes a state, not the process of becoming warmer.
For a welcoming smile or kind personality, choose vocabulary about friendliness
rather than treating this climate adjective as an unrestricted metaphor.
