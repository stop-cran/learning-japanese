# 楽しみ (たのしみ) - enjoyment; something to look forward to

## Meaning

楽しみ means pleasure, enjoyment, or something that gives you pleasure. It also expresses happy anticipation.

読書が毎日の楽しみです。 (どくしょがまいにちのたのしみです。) - Reading is my daily pleasure.

旅行を楽しみにしています。 (りょこうをたのしみにしています。) - I am looking forward to the trip.

Learn the second pattern as thing + を + 楽しみにする, often in the continuing form 楽しみにしています.

## How the kanji combine

[楽](../kanji/楽.md) contributes the enjoyment meaning. 楽しみ is a native noun related to 楽しむ (たのしむ), "enjoy," and 楽しい (たのしい), "enjoyable."

## Synonyms and antonyms

喜び (よろこび) is joy or delight. 楽しい describes something as enjoyable; 楽しみ can name the pleasure itself or anticipation of it. 不安 (ふあん), "anxiety," is a possible emotional contrast, not a universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

明日が楽しみです (あしたがたのしみです) means "I am looking forward to tomorrow," not "Tomorrow is currently fun." In anticipatory noun modification, な is possible: 楽しみな旅行 (たのしみなりょこう), "a trip one is looking forward to."
