# 留まる (とどまる) - remain; stay; be limited to

## Meaning

留まる means to remain or stay in a place or position.
It also means to be limited or confined to a particular scope.
It is an intransitive godan verb, often written とどまる.

- 彼は現地に留まって仕事を続けました。 (かれはげんちにとどまってしごとをつづけました。) - He remained there and continued working.
- 被害は小規模に留まりました。 (ひがいはしょうきぼにとどまりました。) - The damage remained limited in scale.

に can mark the place, position, or limit.
Forms include 留まります, 留まって, and 留まらない,
read とどまります, とどまって, and とどまらない.

## How the kanji combine

[留](../kanji/留.md) contributes remaining or staying.
The written division is とど + まる, but learn the whole verb とどまる.
止まる, with [止](../kanji/止.md), is also a recognized spelling of this とどまる family.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[残る](残る.md) (のこる) overlaps in remaining, including staying behind.
[滞在](滞在.md)する (たいざいする) describes staying in a place for a period.
[限る](限る.md) (かぎる) concerns limiting or restricting;
留まる presents what stays within a limit rather than an agent imposing it.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish this reading from [止まる](止まる.md) (とまる),
which can describe stopping movement or operation.
留まる can also write that とまる in perching or fixed-in-place uses;
see the separate article for examples.
The public dictionary also recognizes stopping for とどまる;
the distinction is not an absolute rule that it can never mean stop.
The examples here teach remaining and limited scope, not overnight lodging specifically.
