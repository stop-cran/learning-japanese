# 感慨 (かんがい) — deep emotion; strong feelings

## Meaning

感慨 is a deep feeling that wells up in the heart. Digital Daijisen defines it as 心に深く感じて、しみじみとした気持ちになること (こころにふかくかんじて、しみじみとしたきもちになること), “feeling something deeply and being filled with a quiet, heartfelt mood,” and gives 感慨にひたる (かんがいにひたる), “be immersed in deep emotion.” JMdict glosses it “deep emotion; strong feelings.”

- 三十年ぶりに母校を訪れ、感慨にひたった。 (さんじゅうねんぶりにぼこうをおとずれ、かんがいにひたった。) — Visiting my old school for the first time in thirty years, I was immersed in deep emotion.
- 完成した橋を見て、工事に関わった人たちは感慨深い表情を浮かべた。 (かんせいしたはしをみて、こうじにかかわったひとたちはかんがいぶかいひょうじょうをうかべた。) — Looking at the finished bridge, the people who had worked on it looked deeply moved.

## How the kanji combine

[感](../kanji/感.md) (かん) is feeling, and [慨](../kanji/慨.md) (がい) means to be filled with stirring thoughts; [Digital Daijisen, 慨](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E6%85%A8) lists 感慨 under 心を揺さぶる思いで一杯になる (こころをゆさぶるおもいでいっぱいになる), “to be filled with heart-stirring feelings.” 感慨深い (かんがいぶかい), in the second example, is an i-adjective; JMdict glosses it “deeply emotive; moving.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

[感動](感動.md) (かんどう) and [感激](感激.md) (かんげき) are being moved or stirred by something. [しみじみ](しみじみ.md) is an adverb for a feeling that sinks in deeply and quietly; Digital Daijisen uses it to define 感慨. JMdict lists 感慨 only as a noun, while 感動 and 感激 can also be する verbs; 感慨 is used in phrases such as 感慨にひたる and 感慨深い.

## Distinctive meaning

感慨無量 (かんがいむりょう) is “being filled with deep emotion.” Do not confuse 感慨 with 灌漑 (かんがい), “irrigation,” which has the same reading.
