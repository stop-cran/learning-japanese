# 過失 (かしつ) - error; negligence; fault

## Meaning

過失 names an error, blunder, or negligent lapse. In accounts of responsibility, it often concerns harm caused through a failure to take due care rather than a deliberate act. The dictionary also records the broader meaning "fault; defect."

- 運転手の過失で事故が起きました。 (うんてんしゅのかしつでじこがおきました。) - An accident occurred because of the driver's negligence.
- 会社は自分たちの過失を認めました。 (かいしゃはじぶんたちのかしつをみとめました。) - The company acknowledged its fault.

This is a noun. 過失がある means that someone bears fault; 過失を認める means acknowledging it.

## How the kanji combine

[過](../kanji/過.md) has associations with error or going beyond a limit; [失](../kanji/失.md) with loss or failure. Their meanings reinforce the idea of an error or lapse. The on readings are カ + シツ; this is not the native verb 過ごす.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[誤り](誤り.md) (あやまり), [間違い](間違い.md) (まちがい), and [ミス](ミス.md) are useful general error words. [事故](事故.md) (じこ) names an accident or incident, not necessarily anyone's negligence. 故意 (こい), deliberate intent, provides an important contrast when discussing responsibility.

## Distinctive meaning

"Accident" alone does not capture the care or responsibility issue in 過失. Conversely, an ordinary error need not be a legal case. In legal contexts, the applicable duty and standard of care need separate explanation; the word alone does not determine liability.
