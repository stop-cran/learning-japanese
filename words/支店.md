# 支店 (してん) - branch office; branch store

## Meaning

支店 is a branch office or branch store of a business. It can be a bank branch or a regional business office, not just a retail shop. Use **company の支店** to identify the business and a place name before 支店 to identify the location.

- 銀行の大阪支店で働いています。 (ぎんこうのおおさかしてんではたらいています。) - I work at the bank's Osaka branch.
- この町にも支店があります。 (このまちにもしてんがあります。) - There is a branch in this town too.

で marks the workplace in the first example; に marks where a branch exists in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[支](../kanji/支.md) contributes a branch or subsidiary division; [店](../kanji/店.md) contributes a business establishment. 支 modifies 店 to distinguish a branch from the central establishment. The on readings are シ + テン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

本店 (ほんてん), "main store; head office," is the corresponding central establishment. [店](店.md) (みせ) is a shop more generally, and [事務所](事務所.md) (じむしょ) is an office without necessarily being a branch.

## Distinctive meaning

支店 describes the location's relationship to the larger business. A small independent shop is not a 支店 just because it is small, and a large branch remains a 支店. The word does not specify the branch's staff size or range of services.
