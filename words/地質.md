# 地質 (ちしつ) - geological makeup

## Meaning

地質 concerns the nature and structure of the rocks and layers that
make up the ground. English translations include geological features,
geological makeup, or geology when discussing an area's underlying materials.

- 工事の前に、この土地の地質を調べます。
  (こうじのまえに、このとちのちしつをしらべます。) - We will investigate this site's geology before construction.
- この地域の地質について勉強しています。
  (このちいきのちしつについてべんきょうしています。) - I am studying the geological makeup of this region.

地質調査 (ちしつちょうさ) is a geological survey.
The noun identifies what is examined, not the act of examining it.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) contributes earth or ground and
[質](../kanji/質.md) quality or substance.
The first qualifies the second: the material character of the ground.
The on readings are チ + シツ, giving **ちしつ**.

## Synonyms and antonyms

地形 (ちけい), terrain or topography, concerns the shape of the land.
[地理](地理.md) (ちり) is geography.
[地盤](地盤.md) (じばん) concerns ground and its supporting condition.
These related terms do not erase the distinction between underlying
materials, surface shape, and a broader study of places.

## Distinctive meaning

The same written 地質 has a separate dictionary entry read **じしつ**,
meaning the quality or texture of fabric. That is not an alternative
reading for the geological meaning taught here.
The source's ちしつ and geological gloss belong together.
