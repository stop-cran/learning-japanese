# そよそよ — a breeze blowing gently

## Meaning

A light, gentle movement of air.

- 窓（まど）から風がそよそよ入ってくる。 — A gentle breeze is coming through the window.
- 春の風がそよそよと吹（ふ）いている。 — A spring breeze is blowing softly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[びゅうびゅう](びゅうびゅう.md) offers a forceful, howling-wind contrast. [ゆらゆら](ゆらゆら.md) can describe the movement of something swaying in a breeze, rather than the breeze itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 風がそよそよ吹く. “Gently” is only an approximate English gloss: do not substitute そよそよ for every gentle action, such as carefully touching an object with [そっと](そっと.md).
