# たちまち - in an instant; suddenly

## Meaning

たちまち is an adverb for something happening in a very short time
or a situation changing suddenly. It directly modifies a predicate.

- うわさはたちまち町中に広がりました。
  (うわさはたちまちまちじゅうにひろがりました。) - The rumor spread throughout the town in no time.
- 空がたちまち暗くなりました。
  (そらがたちまちくらくなりました。) - The sky suddenly grew dark.

たちまちのうちに is another pattern for "in no time."
The word can highlight the rapid result, not just the start of an action.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana; 忽ち is a dictionary spelling
with the same reading. This kana-primary article has no written-kanji
components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すぐに](すぐに.md) also means "right away."
[突然](突然.md) (とつぜん) overlaps with sudden occurrence.
徐々に (じょじょに), "gradually," contrasts with rapid change.
These are overlapping perspectives, not strict divisions between speed
and surprise in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

English "all at once" here can mean suddenly. It does not require
several people acting together, unlike the simultaneity emphasized by
[一斉](一斉.md) (いっせい). Nor does たちまち name a precise
number of seconds; it presents the development as very quick.
