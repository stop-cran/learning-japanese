# 栄養 (えいよう) - nutrition; nourishment

## Meaning

栄養 is nutrition or nourishment. It is a noun, not the name of one specific food. Useful patterns are 栄養がある, "be nutritious," 栄養を取る (えいようをとる), "get nourishment," and 栄養が足りない (えいようがたりない), "not get enough nutrition."

- 栄養のバランスを考えて食事を作ります。 (えいようのバランスをかんがえてしょくじをつくります。) - I prepare meals with nutritional balance in mind.
- この食品は栄養が豊富です。 (このしょくひんはえいようがほうふです。) - This food is rich in nutrients.

豊富 (ほうふ) means abundant; the second sentence describes the food's nutritional content.

## How the kanji combine

栄 suggests flourishing, while 養 concerns nourishing or sustaining. Together they name nourishment that supports living things. The on readings are エイ + ヨウ. This is a meaning aid, not a claim about the word's historical formation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[食品](食品.md) (しょくひん) names food products, and [食事](食事.md) (しょくじ) names a meal or eating. They can supply 栄養 but do not mean nutrition themselves. 栄養不足 (えいようぶそく), "insufficient nutrition," describes a lack rather than a separate opposite substance.

## Distinctive meaning

For an individual nutrient, 栄養素 (えいようそ) is more specific. 栄養 alone does not state a calorie count or establish that a food meets every dietary need; additional information supplies those claims.
