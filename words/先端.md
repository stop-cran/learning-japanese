# 先端 (せんたん) - tip; leading edge

## Meaning

先端 is the tip or far end of something, especially a long or pointed object.
It also means the forefront or leading edge of a development, field or trend.

- 棒の先端に印を付けました。 (ぼうのせんたんにしるしをつけました。) - I put a mark on the tip of the stick.
- この研究所は技術の先端を行っています。 (このけんきゅうじょはぎじゅつのせんたんをいっています。) - This research institute is at the forefront of technology.

Nの先端 identifies what has a tip or forefront.
In the second example, 先端を行く (せんたんをいく) is figurative;
the institute is not walking to the end of a stick.

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md) contributes the foremost point, and 端 an end or extremity.
The related meanings reinforce the tip or leading part.
The on readings are セン + タン, not the standalone native readings さき + はし.
尖端 is a rare dictionary spelling for the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[端](端.md) (はし) more broadly names an edge or end;
[先](先.md) (さき) also has tip/end meanings besides time and sequence uses.
最先端 (さいせんたん) emphasizes the very forefront or cutting edge.
[頂上](頂上.md) (ちょうじょう) names a top or summit, not every projecting tip.

## Distinctive meaning

A 先端 need not be needle-sharp: a stick or rod can have a blunt end.
The figurative use concerns being out in front, not simply the main point of an argument.
Nor does calling technology 先端 establish that it is safe, effective or better
for every purpose; the word identifies a leading position.
