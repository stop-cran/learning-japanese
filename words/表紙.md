# 表紙 (ひょうし) - book cover; binding

## Meaning

表紙 is the cover or binding of a book, magazine, or similar publication.
本の表紙 (ほんのひょうし) means a book's cover.

- この本の表紙は青いです。 (このほんのひょうしはあおいです。) - This book's cover is blue.
- 雑誌の表紙に写真が載っています。 (ざっしのひょうしにしゃしんがのっています。) - There is a photograph on the magazine cover.

表紙に marks the cover as a surface where something appears.
The word is not restricted to the front cover; 裏表紙
(うらびょうし) specifies the back cover.

## How the kanji combine

[表](../kanji/表.md) contributes the outer or visible side, and
[紙](../kanji/紙.md) paper. The first qualifies the second in a
word for a publication's cover. The on readings are ヒョウ + シ.
Actual covers can be made from leather, cloth, or other materials;
the written 紙 does not impose a paper-only definition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[カバー](カバー.md) can name a covering, including a protective
cover or jacket put over a book. It need not be identical to
the book's 表紙 itself. [表面](表面.md) (ひょうめん) is the
broader word for a surface or exterior.

## Distinctive meaning

表紙 does not mean the pages inside or the act of binding a book.
JMdict also records slang 表紙する for appearing on a magazine cover.
That additional recognition is not the ordinary verb for manufacturing
or attaching a cover.
