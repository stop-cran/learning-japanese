# 納得 (なっとく) - acceptance; being convinced

## Meaning

納得 is acceptance or understanding that leaves someone convinced or satisfied. 納得する is a する verb. A useful pattern is **説明や理由に納得する**, "be satisfied with an explanation or reason."

- 詳しい説明を聞いて、納得しました。 (くわしいせつめいをきいて、なっとくしました。) - After hearing the detailed explanation, I was convinced.
- この条件には納得できません。 (このじょうけんにはなっとくできません。) - I cannot accept these conditions.

The first example emphasizes understanding and satisfaction; the second emphasizes acceptance. **納得できる理由** (なっとくできるりゆう) is "a convincing reason," and **納得していない** means "not convinced."

## How the kanji combine

納 contributes taking in or accepting, and [得](../kanji/得.md) contributes obtaining or grasping. They provide a memory aid for taking something in and finding it acceptable. The established reading is なっとく, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[理解](理解.md) (りかい) is understanding; a person can understand a claim yet remain unconvinced. 同意 (どうい) emphasizes agreement. [満足](満足.md) (まんぞく) is satisfaction more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

納得 is more than merely hearing or decoding an explanation. It presents the explanation or situation as making sense to the person or being acceptable to them. Do not assume that a polite acknowledgment proves 納得: the speaker may still disagree or have unanswered questions.
