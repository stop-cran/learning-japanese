# いかがわしい — dubious; disreputable

## Meaning

いかがわしい describes something dubious, unreliable, disreputable, or indecent. It expresses
disapproval; the context decides whether the concern is dishonesty, impropriety, or both.

- いかがわしい商売には関わりたくありません。  
  (いかがわしい しょうばいには かかわりたく ありません。)  
  — I do not want to get involved in a dubious business.
- いかがわしい雑誌 (いかがわしい ざっし) — an indecent magazine

It is an i-adjective: いかがわしくない, "not dubious or indecent."

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is hiragana. JMdict also lists 如何わしい, but learn the whole adjective rather
than deriving its modern meaning by translating the individual characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怪しい](怪しい.md) (あやしい) can express suspicion without specifying its cause. [疑わしい](疑わしい.md) (うたがわしい)
often questions credibility or truth. 信頼できる (しんらいできる), "trustworthy," contrasts with
the unreliable sense, but is not an antonym for every indecent use.

## Distinctive meaning

いかがわしい店 (いかがわしいみせ) can imply a shady or seedy establishment. It need not mean
that the business is sexual or illegal, and it is stronger than merely saying a shop looks unusual.

For いかがわしい compared with 怪しい and 疑わしい, see
[strange, suspicious, and rare](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
