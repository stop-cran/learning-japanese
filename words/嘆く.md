# 嘆く (なげく) — lament; grieve; sigh

## Meaning

嘆く means to lament, grieve, or sigh over something. It can also mean to deplore a situation or social trend.

- 彼女は友人との別れを嘆きました。 (かのじょはゆうじんとのわかれをなげきました。) — She lamented parting from her friend.
- 人々は町の変化を嘆いています。 (ひとびとはまちのへんかをなげいています。) — People lament the changes in the town.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji verb. [嘆](../kanji/嘆.md) is read なげ in 嘆く; く is the okurigana, the kana ending written after the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[悲しむ](悲しむ.md) (かなしむ) means to feel sad; 嘆く often emphasizes expressing grief or regret about a situation. Their meanings overlap when grieving a loss.

## Distinctive meaning

嘆く expresses grief, regret, or disapproval; the “admiration” sense belongs to compounds such as 感嘆, not to this verb.
