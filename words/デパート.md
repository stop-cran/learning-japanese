# デパート (デパート) — department store

## Meaning

デパート is a department store: a large shop organized into sections selling different kinds of goods.

デパートで母の誕生日のプレゼントを買いました。 (デパートでははのたんじょうびのプレゼントをかいました。) — I bought my mother's birthday present at a department store.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana abbreviation of デパートメントストア, "department store." The long vowel mark belongs after パ: デパート. There are no kanji components in this spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

百貨店 (ひゃっかてん) is another word for a department store. スーパー is a supermarket, while コンビニ is a convenience store. All are types of shops, but their names are not interchangeable merely because they sell some of the same products.

## Distinctive meaning

The Japanese abbreviation retains the store meaning; it is not the ordinary word for a department within a company or university. デパートの地下 (デパートのちか) means "the department store's basement," often a useful location when shopping for food. Use で for shopping there and に or へ when describing going there.
