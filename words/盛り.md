# 盛り (さかり) - peak; prime

## Meaning

盛り, read **さかり**, is the height or peak of a season or activity, or the prime of life. Use **noun + の盛り** for the peak of something.

- 今は夏の盛りです。 (いまはなつのさかりです。) - It is now the height of summer.
- 桜は盛りを過ぎました。 (さくらはさかりをすぎました。) - The cherry blossoms are past their peak.

As a suffix, the reading becomes ざかり: 働き盛り (はたらきざかり) describes the prime of one's working life. This is a life-stage use, not a measurement of physical height.

## How the kanji combine

盛 contributes flourishing or fullness; り completes the noun さかり. The single character has different readings in other words, so recognize the whole expression rather than assigning it one fixed pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ピーク also names a peak. 盛り is especially useful for a flourishing period, while 終わり (おわり), "end," contrasts with that stage rather than being an exact antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The same spelling read **もり** belongs to a separate word meaning a serving or helping of food: 大盛り (おおもり), "a large serving." That is not the さかり taught here. The source list's serving gloss belongs to もり; it does not make "summer's helping" the meaning of 夏の盛り.
