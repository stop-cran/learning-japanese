# ばったり (ばったり) - with a thud; unexpectedly

## Meaning

ばったり is a mimetic adverb with several distinct uses. It can describe a sudden fall with a thud or flop, an unexpected encounter, or something abruptly stopping. It can take と.

- 疲れて、ベッドにばったりと倒れました。 (つかれて、ベッドにばったりとたおれました。) - Exhausted, I flopped onto the bed.
- 駅で友達にばったり会いました。 (えきでともだちにばったりあいました。) - I unexpectedly ran into a friend at the station.

連絡がばったり途絶えた (れんらくがばったりとだえた), "contact abruptly stopped," illustrates the third use. It does not describe a collision or a noise.

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana mimetic expression, not a kanji compound or an imported English word. Small っ is part of ばったり; the dictionary also records ばたり and katakana spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[たまたま](たまたま.md) describes chance circumstances broadly. The encounter use of ばったり specifically presents unexpectedly meeting someone. [突然](突然.md) (とつぜん) describes suddenness more generally without the falling or meeting imagery.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose the interpretation from the verb: 倒れる for falling, 会う for meeting, and 途絶える for stopping. The source's English "plump" is an opaque adverbial gloss here, not a description of someone's body shape. "With a thud" or "with a flop" makes the relevant action clearer.
