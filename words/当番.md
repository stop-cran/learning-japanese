# 当番 (とうばん) - turn on duty; person on duty

## Meaning

当番 is a turn at carrying out an assigned duty, or the person whose turn it is.
It commonly concerns work shared in rotation, such as classroom cleaning or reception.

- 今日は私が掃除の当番です。
  (きょうはわたしがそうじのとうばんです。) - It is my turn to do the cleaning today.
- 次の当番は誰ですか。
  (つぎのとうばんはだれですか。) - Who is on duty next?

Nの当番 identifies the task; 当番の人 (とうばんのひと) identifies the person on duty.
Use 当番を決める (とうばんをきめる) for deciding who takes the duty.

## How the kanji combine

[当](../kanji/当.md) contributes being assigned the relevant turn,
and [番](../kanji/番.md) a turn in a series. The on readings are トウ + バン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[係](係.md) (かかり) and [担当](担当.md) (たんとう) can identify someone
responsible for a task, without necessarily implying rotation.
[順番](順番.md) (じゅんばん) is a broader order or turn;
[務め](務め.md) (つとめ) concerns the duty to perform.

## Distinctive meaning

当番 does not mean every job or moral obligation. It identifies whose turn
or scheduled duty it is. Someone can have a permanent occupation and also
take a temporary 当番 within it; the duty need not be paid employment.
