# 起床 (きしょう) - getting out of bed

## Meaning

起床 is getting up from bed. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb: 六時に起床する (ろくじにきしょうする), "get up at six."

- 毎朝六時に起床します。
  (まいあさろくじにきしょうします。) - I get out of bed at six every morning.
- 合宿中の起床時間は六時半です。
  (がっしゅくちゅうのきしょうじかんはろくじはんです。) - The getting-up time during the training camp is six thirty.

## How the kanji combine

[起](../kanji/起.md) concerns rising, and [床](../kanji/床.md) here contributes bed or bedding, not the room's floor. The on readings combine as キ + ショウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[起きる](起きる.md) (おきる) also means get up, and can describe waking or other events in additional uses. [覚める](覚める.md) (さめる) concerns waking or regaining alertness; waking does not itself establish that someone has left bed.

## Distinctive meaning

目は覚めましたが、まだ起床していません (めはさめましたが、まだきしょうしていません), "I am awake, but I haven't got out of bed yet," separates the two events. 起床時間 is a useful timetable expression; 起床 does not mean rising prices or the sunrise.
