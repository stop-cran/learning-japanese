# 用途 (ようと) - use; intended purpose

## Meaning

用途 names the use or purpose to which something is put, especially
an object or money. It is a noun: Xの用途 is "what X is used for."

- この道具の用途を教えてください。 (このどうぐのようとをおしえてください。) - Please tell me what this tool is used for.
- 用途に合わせて材料を選びます。 (ようとにあわせてざいりょうをえらびます。) - We choose materials to suit the intended use.

用途が広い (ようとがひろい) describes having a wide range of uses.
The word identifies a use; it does not by itself say that the thing
is being used at this moment.

## How the kanji combine

[用](../kanji/用.md) contributes use, and [途](../kanji/途.md)
contributes a way or course. Together they suggest the way something
is put to use. The on readings are ヨウ + ト.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[目的](目的.md) (もくてき) is a purpose or objective more broadly.
[使用](使用.md) (しよう) names using something, while 用途 focuses on
what it is used for. [役目](役目.md) (やくめ) concerns a role or function.
These meanings can overlap; they are not rigidly separated categories.

## Distinctive meaning

A tool can have several 用途 even while it sits unused in a drawer.
Conversely, saying that someone 使用した, "used it," does not identify
the purpose. Learn 用途に合わせて, "to suit the use," rather than
treating 用途 as a general verb meaning "use."
