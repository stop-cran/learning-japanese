# 双子 (ふたご) - twins; a twin

## Meaning

双子 means twins or a twin. It is a noun and does not change form between English singular and plural. 双子の兄 (ふたごのあに), "my older twin brother," identifies one member of the pair.

- 私たちは双子です。 (わたしたちはふたごです。) - We are twins.
- 双子の妹は別の町に住んでいます。 (ふたごのいもうとはべつのまちにすんでいます。) - My younger twin sister lives in another town.

The second sentence uses の to connect 双子 to 妹. It does not say that the speaker has two younger sisters.

## How the kanji combine

双 contributes "pair; two," and [子](../kanji/子.md) contributes "child." Learn the native reading ふたご, with こ voiced to ご. The word can continue to describe twins when they are adults.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[兄弟](兄弟.md) (きょうだい) can refer to siblings more broadly; siblings need not be twins. [二人](二人.md) (ふたり), "two people," counts a pair without specifying any family relationship.

## Distinctive meaning

双子 does not by itself establish that the twins are identical, the same sex, or look alike. Those facts require additional description. Likewise, 子 does not impose a present age limit: adult twins remain 双子. Context identifies whether the word refers to the pair or to one of them.
