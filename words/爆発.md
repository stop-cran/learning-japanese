# 爆発 (ばくはつ) - explosion; outburst

## Meaning

爆発 is an explosion or blast. 爆発する is intransitive: the thing that explodes takes が. The noun also describes an emotional outburst, such as pent-up anger or discontent erupting.

- 工場で爆発が起きました。 (こうじょうでばくはつがおきました。) - An explosion occurred at the factory.
- 長年の不満がついに爆発しました。 (ながねんのふまんがついにばくはつしました。) - Years of discontent finally erupted.

In the second sentence, the subject is 不満, "discontent," so the explosion is figurative. No physical blast is asserted.

## How the kanji combine

爆 contributes bursting or exploding; [発](../kanji/発.md) contributes emergence or occurrence. Their combination names a sudden explosive release. The on readings are バク + ハツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[火事](火事.md) (かじ) is a fire. A fire and an explosion may occur in the same incident, but one does not automatically imply the other. 噴火 (ふんか) specifically names a volcanic eruption rather than every kind of 爆発.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare ガスが爆発した (ガスがばくはつした), "gas exploded," with 怒りが爆発した (いかりがばくはつした), "anger erupted." The same intransitive pattern supports physical and emotional uses. The noun by itself does not identify the cause or whether the event was deliberate.
