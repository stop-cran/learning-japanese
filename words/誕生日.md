# 誕生日 (たんじょうび) - birthday

## Meaning

誕生日 is a birthday, the annual date celebrating a person's birth.

- 誕生日はいつですか。 (たんじょうびはいつですか。) - When is your birthday?
- 誕生日に本をもらいました。 (たんじょうびにほんをもらいました。) - I received a book for my birthday.

Use a person's name or relationship + の + 誕生日: 母の誕生日 (ははのたんじょうび), "my mother's birthday."

## How the kanji combine

誕 and [生](../kanji/生.md) form 誕生 (たんじょう), "birth." [日](../kanji/日.md), "day," adds the native reading ひ, voiced to び. This is an on-first mixed reading, not three on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

生年月日 (せいねんがっぴ) is a full date of birth, including the year. 記念日 (きねんび) is an anniversary or commemorative day more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

A birthday is not necessarily a party. 誕生日おめでとう means "Happy birthday"; 誕生日 names the occasion, not the act of congratulating someone.
