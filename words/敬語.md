# 敬語 (けいご) - honorific language; polite expressions

## Meaning

敬語 is language that expresses respect, deference, or politeness with regard to people and their relationship.
It can refer to the system of honorific language or to particular respectful expressions.

- 仕事で使う敬語を勉強しています。 (しごとでつかうけいごをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying honorific language used at work.
- 「食べる」の尊敬語は「召し上がる」です。 (「たべる」のそんけいごは「めしあがる」です。) - The honorific expression for "eat" is meshiagaru.

Respectful language is not just longer vocabulary: choosing it depends on who acts, who is addressed, and the social setting.

## How the kanji combine

[敬](../kanji/敬.md), "respect," qualifies [語](../kanji/語.md), "language; word": respectful language.
The on readings are ケイ + ゴ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

A traditional three-way account distinguishes 尊敬語 (そんけいご), which raises the person referred to;
謙譲語 (けんじょうご), which humbles the speaker's side in relation to another;
and 丁寧語 (ていねいご), polite language toward the listener.
This is an introductory classification, not the only one: the 2007 Japanese guidelines use five categories.
[お](お.md) can serve as an honorific or polite prefix, but adding it mechanically does not make every expression appropriate.

## Distinctive meaning

敬語 is the broader category, not just 尊敬語.
For example, the polite ending です belongs to politeness toward the listener; it does not by itself say that the subject is an honored person.
Learn a particular expression with its speaker, referent, and situation rather than translating 敬語 simply as "formal."
