# たらたら — dripping or trickling steadily

## Meaning

たらたら describes liquid flowing or dripping in a steady stream, often slowly.

- 暑さで額から汗がたらたら流れてきた。
  — Sweat streamed down from his forehead in the heat.
- 壊れた蛇口から水がたらたら漏れている。
  — Water is steadily leaking from the broken faucet.

## Synonyms and antonyms

たらたら suggests a continuous trickle or stream, rather than isolated drops.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can also describe someone talking or complaining incessantly; these examples focus on liquid.
