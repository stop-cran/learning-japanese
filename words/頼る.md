# 頼る (たよる) - rely on

## Meaning

頼る is a godan verb meaning to rely on, depend on, or turn to someone or something for help.
Learn Xに頼る, "depend on X," and Xを頼って, "turning to/relying on X."
Both constructions are recorded; do not treat one particle as the sole possibility.

- 困ったときは、家族に頼ってください。 (こまったときは、かぞくにたよってください。) - When you are in trouble, please turn to your family.
- 地図を頼って駅まで歩きました。 (ちずをたよってえきまであるきました。) - I walked to the station using the map as my guide.

The て-form is 頼って (たよって), and the negative is 頼らない (たよらない).

## How the kanji combine

[頼](../kanji/頼.md) contributes reliance in this native word. The stem is たよ, followed by the godan ending る.
The same character appears in 頼む (たのむ), but the two verbs differ in both reading and construction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頼む](頼む.md) means to ask or request something.
頼る describes dependence or seeking support; a spoken request need not be reported.
[信じる](信じる.md) (しんじる), "believe; trust," overlaps with trust in a helper, but belief alone is not necessarily reliance.

## Distinctive meaning

The support can be practical, emotional, or informational. A person, a map, or experience can all be something one relies on.
頼らずに (たよらずに) means "without relying on."
Whether reliance is sensible or excessive is supplied by context, not fixed by the verb itself.
