# そう (そう) — so; is that so?

## Meaning

そう refers back to a way of doing something or to what someone has said: "like that; so". As a response, it can agree with a statement or question it.

- 「明日は休みですか。」「そうです。」  
  「あしたはやすみですか。」「そうです。」  
  "'Is tomorrow a day off?' 'That's right.'"
- 「この本は難しくないよ。」「そう？」  
  「このほんはむずかしくないよ。」「そう？」  
  "'This book isn't difficult.' 'Really?'"

## How the kanji combine

Use hiragana. The dictionary records 然う but marks that kanji spelling as rare. This word belongs with こう, [ああ](ああ.md), and どう in the manner-demonstrative series.

## Synonyms and antonyms

本当に？ (ほんとうに？), "Really?", overlaps with a questioning そう？. However, そう is not the general degree adverb "really": it cannot simply replace 本当に in "really difficult".

## Distinctive meaning

Intonation matters: questioning そう？ differs from agreeing そう. The polite response そうですか can acknowledge new information as "I see" without expressing disbelief. The attached ending in おいしそう, "looks delicious", is a separate construction, not this conversational response.
