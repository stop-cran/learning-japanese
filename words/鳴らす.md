# 鳴らす (ならす) - ring; make a sound

## Meaning

鳴らす means to make something sound: ring a bell, sound a horn,
or make another noise. What produces the sound takes を.
It is a transitive godan verb.

- 入口のベルを鳴らしてください。 (いりぐちのベルをならしてください。) - Please ring the bell at the entrance.
- 彼は指を鳴らしました。 (かれはゆびをならしました。) - He snapped his fingers.

Forms include 鳴らします, 鳴らさない, 鳴らして and 鳴らした.
The verb covers more than a ringing tone: context can specify a beat,
a snort or cracking joints.

## How the kanji combine

[鳴](../kanji/鳴.md) contributes sound; らす completes the native
reading ならす. Compare [鳴る](鳴る.md) (なる):
ベルが鳴る, "the bell rings," versus ベルを鳴らす, "ring the bell."
Do not confuse ならす with the animal-call reading なく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[叩く](叩く.md) (たたく) and [打つ](打つ.md) (うつ) can describe
striking an instrument. 鳴らす foregrounds the resulting sound, which
need not be made by a blow. [演奏](演奏.md) (えんそう) concerns
musical performance, not every noise or alarm.

## Distinctive meaning

Two further uses deserve recognition. 名投手で鳴らした
(めいとうしゅでならした), "was renowned as a great pitcher,"
uses で for what someone was known for. 不平を鳴らす
(ふへいをならす) means voicing complaints.
These do not literally require a ringing object. The construction and
context determine the sense; the verb is not a general replacement for
every "say" or "be famous." The dictionary's archaic flatulence sense
is outside this article's practical teaching.
