# 止む (やむ) — cease; let up

## Meaning

止む means **to cease or stop**, especially for something continuing over time, such as rain, wind, or a sound. The thing that stops is the subject.

- 雨が止みました。 (あめがやみました。) — The rain stopped.
- 風が止むまで待ちましょう。 (かぜがやむまでまちましょう。) — Let's wait until the wind dies down.

It is a godan verb: 止みます (やみます), 止まない (やまない), 止んだ (やんだ), 止んで (やんで).

## How the kanji combine

[止](../kanji/止.md) writes や in this word, followed by む. The same kanji appears in other stopping verbs, but their readings and endings must be learned separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

止まる (とまる) often describes movement or operation stopping, as with a train. 止める (やめる), “stop doing/quit,” describes someone ending an activity. 続く (つづく), “continue,” provides a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

止む presents cessation without naming someone who causes it. For “stop the car,” use 車を止める (くるまをとめる), not 車を止む. Keep the negatives distinct: 止まない (やまない) is from 止む; 止まらない (とまらない) is from 止まる.
