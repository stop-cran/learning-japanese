# 迷信 (めいしん) - superstition; superstitious belief

## Meaning

迷信 is a superstition or superstitious belief. Calling something 迷信 presents
it as a belief without a rational foundation; it is not a neutral word for every belief.

- それは迷信だと思います。 (それはめいしんだとおもいます。) - I think that is a superstition.
- 祖父はその迷信を信じていました。 (そふはそのめいしんをしんじていました。) - My grandfather believed that superstition.

The second sentence reports what he believed; it does not say that the speaker agrees with it.

## How the kanji combine

[迷](../kanji/迷.md) contributes being misled or going astray, while
[信](../kanji/信.md) contributes belief.
The on readings are メイ + シン. The whole compound conveys a negatively evaluated belief.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[信仰](信仰.md) (しんこう) is faith or religious belief.
[宗教](宗教.md) (しゅうきょう) is religion.
These are related topics, not synonyms that automatically carry 迷信's judgment.
[信じる](信じる.md) (しんじる), "believe," says what someone does without itself labeling the belief superstition.

## Distinctive meaning

迷信 can concern everyday luck or omens as well as matters associated with religion.
Do not use the word merely because a practice is unfamiliar.
Its evaluative force can make it dismissive in conversation.
