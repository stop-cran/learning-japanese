# 振る (ふる) - wave; shake; allocate

## Meaning

振る is a transitive godan verb. Its physical uses include waving, shaking, or swinging something; the moved object takes を. Forms include 振ります, 振った, and 振って.

- 駅で友達に手を振りました。 (えきでともだちにてをふりました。) - I waved to my friend at the station.

It can also mean sprinkling or shaking something onto a surface.

- 野菜に塩を少し振ってください。 (やさいにしおをすこしふってください。) - Please sprinkle a little salt on the vegetables.

Another use is assigning work or roles: recipient に + task or role を + 振る.

- 上司は私に新しい仕事を振りました。 (じょうしはわたしにあたらしいしごとをふりました。) - My supervisor assigned me a new task.

役を振る (やくをふる) similarly concerns assigning a role, including casting an actor.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 振 represents the native verb ふる; る changes with conjugation. There is no compound to split. Physical shaking offers a memory anchor, but cannot predict all the word's established uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[揺れる](揺れる.md) (ゆれる) describes something swaying or shaking intransitively. 振る presents someone moving the object. The same-sounding [降る](降る.md) (ふる), as in rain falling, is a different verb and spelling.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the object with each sense: 手を振る, 塩を振る, and 仕事を振る are different practical constructions. The English word "shake" alone cannot explain the assignment sense, and a dictionary's long sense list does not make every object suitable for every use.
