# 積極的 (せっきょくてき) - proactive; active

## Meaning

積極的 is a な-adjective for taking an active, positive, or assertive approach. It describes willingness to act or engage rather than waiting passively. Use **積極的な + noun** and **積極的に + verb**.

- 授業で積極的に質問しています。 (じゅぎょうでせっきょくてきにしつもんしています。) - I actively ask questions in class.
- 新しい計画に積極的な人が多いです。 (あたらしいけいかくにせっきょくてきなひとがおおいです。) - Many people are keen to engage with the new plan.

**X に積極的だ** identifies what someone takes an active attitude toward. The negative is 積極的ではない, "not proactive; not very keen."

## How the kanji combine

[積](../kanji/積.md) contributes piling up, and 極 contributes an extreme or pole. Learn 積極 (せっきょく) as the lexical unit for positive, active initiative rather than deriving the modern sense from a literal pile. 的, テキ, forms a descriptive adjective. セキ + キョク becomes せっきょく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

消極的 (しょうきょくてき), "passive; unenthusiastic," is the direct contrast. 熱心 (ねっしん), "enthusiastic; devoted," overlaps but emphasizes earnest interest or application.

## Distinctive meaning

"Positive" here concerns attitude and action, not a positive test result or a positive number. 積極的 does not necessarily describe a talkative personality: quietly volunteering for work can also show an active approach.
