# 矢印 (やじるし) - arrow symbol

## Meaning

矢印 is an arrow-shaped symbol, used to show direction, a connection, or where to look.

- 矢印の方向に進んでください。
  (やじるしのほうこうにすすんでください。) - Please go in the direction of the arrow.
- 地図に矢印を書きました。
  (ちずにやじるしをかきました。) - I drew an arrow on the map.

It is a noun; 矢印で示す (やじるしでしめす) means indicate with an arrow.

## How the kanji combine

矢 means an arrow, and [印](../kanji/印.md) a mark: an arrow-shaped mark. The native readings are や + しるし, with voicing to じるし in this compound.

矢 has no kanji card in the current collection. This does not change the word's spelling or its two-character metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[印](印.md) (しるし), mark or sign, and [記号](記号.md) (きごう), symbol, are broader. [目印](目印.md) (めじるし) identifies a distinguishing mark or landmark; it need not be arrow-shaped.

[方向](方向.md) (ほうこう) is the direction itself, not the drawn arrow showing it.

## Distinctive meaning

The ordinary sense is the symbol, not a physical arrow shot from a bow.

The dictionary also records colloquial romantic interest or the direction of someone's attention, using the arrow image. That secondary use does not make 矢印 a general replacement for every use of [関心](関心.md) (かんしん) or [興味](興味.md) (きょうみ), interest.
