# 無限 (むげん) -- infinity; limitless

## Meaning

無限 means infinity or an absence of limits. As a modifier it means
infinite or limitless; both 無限の and 無限な are recorded patterns.
It can concern extent, quantity, possibility or time.

- 可能性は無限です。 (かのうせいはむげんです。) -- The possibilities are limitless.
- 無限の広がりを想像してください。 (むげんのひろがりをそうぞうしてください。) -- Imagine an infinite expanse.

無限に (むげんに) supplies an adverbial "infinitely" or "without limit."
The noun can also mean eternity in a time context.

## How the kanji combine

無, absence, and [限](../kanji/限.md), a limit, make the no-limit
association clear. The on-readings are ム + ゲン. This is not the
same reading as 無数 (むすう).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [無数](無数.md) (むすう) means countless or innumerable. A vast finite number can be called 無数 without a mathematical infinity claim.
- [永久](永久.md) (えいきゅう) and [永遠](永遠.md) (えいえん) overlap in permanence or eternity, rather than every kind of unlimited extent.
- [限界](限界.md) (げんかい) and [限度](限度.md) (げんど) name limits or bounds.

## Distinctive meaning

In mathematics, infinity is not merely a very large finite number.
Ordinary language also uses 無限 expansively or hyperbolically, so
無限の可能性 need not be a measured mathematical assertion. Do not
turn that difference into a claim that 無限 and 無数 can never overlap
in ordinary descriptions.
