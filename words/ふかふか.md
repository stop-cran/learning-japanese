# ふかふか — soft and cushiony

## Meaning

Soft, thick, and yielding when pressed, especially bedding, cushions, or fluffy bread.

- 干した（ほした）布団（ふとん）がふかふかになった。 — The futon became soft and fluffy after being aired.
- ふかふかのパンにバターを塗った（ぬった）。 — I spread butter on the soft, fluffy bread.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ふわふわ](ふわふわ.md) often emphasizes airiness or lightness, including floating movement. [もこもこ](もこもこ.md) foregrounds bulky fluff. [ほくほく](ほくほく.md) overlaps for certain cooked foods but emphasizes a flaky, crumbly interior.

## Distinctive meaning

ふかふかの布団, ふかふかした土（つち, soil）, and ふかふかしている are useful patterns. The impression is often something you can sink into or compress, rather than just a furry appearance. JMdict also records an archaic absent-minded manner sense; the bedding and food senses are the practical focus here, not a claim that the word has only one use.
