# 幾つ (いくつ) — how many; how old

## Meaning

幾つ asks "how many?" and can also ask someone's age, "how old?"
Both senses are marked **usually written in kana** in JMdict, so the examples use いくつ.
The kanji spelling is useful for recognition, not a requirement for ordinary writing.

- りんごはいくつありますか。 (りんごはいくつありますか。) — How many apples are there?
- お子さんはいくつですか。 (おこさんはいくつですか。) — How old is your child?

The second sentence has an age interpretation from context; いくつ does not ask only about quantities of objects.

## How the kanji combine

[幾](../kanji/幾.md) supplies いく, expressing an unspecified number, and the written つ completes the native Japanese question word.
This is not an on-reading compound.
Learn the whole いくつ alongside the counting forms ending in つ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

何歳 (なんさい) asks age explicitly and is useful when that is the intended question.
[いくら](いくら.md) (幾ら), "how much," asks about an amount or price rather than serving as a general substitute for "how many objects?"
いくつか (幾つか) means "some; several": adding か changes the expression from a question word to an indefinite quantity.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose between a number question and an age question from the sentence, not from the spelling.
Writing 幾つ instead of いくつ does not remove that ambiguity.
おいくつですか adds politeness when asking an age; it still needs an appropriate social context.
There is no meaningful antonym of "how many"; answer it with a quantity or, in the age use, an age.
