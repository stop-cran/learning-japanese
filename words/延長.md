# 延長 (えんちょう) - extension; prolongation

## Meaning

延長 is extending or lengthening something, including a period or a
physical distance. Someone extending something marks it with を.
JMdict also records intransitive uses, describing an extension
without naming an agent.

- 会議を三十分延長しました。
  (かいぎをさんじゅっぷんえんちょうしました。) - We extended the meeting by thirty minutes.
- 工事で道路が延長されました。
  (こうじでどうろがえんちょうされました。) - The road was extended through construction work.

The second example uses the passive 延長される, "be extended."

It is also a noun: 道路の延長 (どうろのえんちょう) can mean the
road's total length. A duration such as 三十分 specifies the amount
of extra time in the first example.

## How the kanji combine

[延](../kanji/延.md) contributes extending, and
[長](../kanji/長.md) length. Their related meanings reinforce the
idea of making something longer: エン + チョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[伸ばす](伸ばす.md) (のばす), also written 延ばす in extension
uses, overlaps in making something longer.
[伸びる](伸びる.md) (のびる), including the spelling 延びる,
describes something becoming longer.
[拡大](拡大.md) (かくだい) concerns enlargement or expansion;
[広がる](広がる.md) (ひろがる) can mean spreading or extending.
These overlaps do not make every bodily stretch or increase an 延長.

## Distinctive meaning

[延期](延期.md) (えんき) is postponement. For a meeting, moving it
to a later date is 延期; making it last longer is 延長.
The dictionary also records continuation uses and a sports abbreviation
for 延長戦 (えんちょうせん), extra time or extra innings.
Those do not make every use a sporting event.
