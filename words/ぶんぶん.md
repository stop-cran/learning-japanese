# ぶんぶん — buzzing or swinging vigorously

## Meaning

ぶんぶん can describe a low, repeated buzzing sound, or vigorous repeated movement through
the air.

- 大きな蜂が、花のまわりをぶんぶん飛んでいた。
  — A large bee was buzzing around the flowers.
- 彼は暑さをしのごうと、うちわをぶんぶん振った。
  — He waved the fan vigorously to cool himself.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぶくぶく](ぶくぶく.md) describes bubbling, not buzzing or swinging. [ぱちぱち](ぱちぱち.md)
can describe crisp repeated sounds, while ぶんぶん suggests a sustained buzzing or swishing
movement.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb and subject disambiguate the sound from the action: a bee may buzz, while a person
may swing or wave something vigorously. The examples do not imply that every repeated movement
makes a buzzing sound.
