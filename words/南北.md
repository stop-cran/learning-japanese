# 南北 (なんぼく) - north and south; north-south

## Meaning

南北 names south and north together, or a north-south direction or extent.
It is a noun, with useful expressions such as 南北に, "from north to south,"
and 南北の, "north-south."

- この道は南北に延びています。 (このみちはなんぼくにのびています。) - This road runs north-south.
- 南北の気温を比べました。 (なんぼくのきおんをくらべました。) - We compared temperatures in the south and the north.

In a discussion of the Korean peninsula, 南北 can abbreviate South Korea
and North Korea. That political reference depends on the topic.

## How the kanji combine

[南](../kanji/南.md), "south," and [北](../kanji/北.md), "north," form an
opposite pair. Learn the whole reading なんぼく: 北 appears as ぼく here,
not the unvoiced ほく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[東西](東西.md) (とうざい), "east and west," gives the other directional axis,
not a synonym. 南 (みなみ) and 北 (きた) name the directions separately.

## Distinctive meaning

The character order is south-then-north, although English often says
"north and south." Do not reverse the Japanese reading to match English.
Geographical 南北 is not automatically a statement about Korea or any other
particular pair of states.
