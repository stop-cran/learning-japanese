# くたくた — worn out; limp or softened

## Meaning

くたくた describes an exhausted person, worn-out clothing or material, or food cooked until very soft.

- 山道を歩いて、もうくたくただ。 — I am completely worn out after walking along the mountain path. (山道: やまみち.)
- 何年も着たシャツはくたくたになっている。 — The shirt I have worn for years has become worn and limp.
- キャベツをくたくたになるまで煮た。 — I cooked the cabbage until it was very soft. (煮る: にる.)

Use くたくただ or くたくたになる for the resulting state. くたくたのシャツ describes a worn-out shirt; くたくたに煮る specifies how soft to cook something.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[へとへと](へとへと.md) closely overlaps for an exhausted person but does not ordinarily describe cooked vegetables. [ぐったり](ぐったり.md) highlights limpness and listlessness. [しっかり](しっかり.md) can contrast when discussing something's firmness or substance.

## Distinctive meaning

Soft vegetables are not necessarily a failed dish: the desired texture depends on the recipe. Similarly, くたくたのシャツ need not be torn. Do not translate all material uses as literal "fatigue."
