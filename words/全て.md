# 全て (すべて) - everything; all

## Meaning

全て means everything or all of the relevant set. It works as a noun, before another noun with の, and adverbially with a predicate.

- 全ての窓を閉めてください。 (すべてのまどをしめてください。) - Please close all the windows.
- 仕事は全て終わりました。 (しごとはすべておわりました。) - All the work is finished.

The first sentence uses **全ての + noun**. The second places 全て with the predicate to indicate that no part of the stated work remains unfinished.

## How the kanji combine

[全](../kanji/全.md) contributes wholeness or entirety. It writes the native word すべて with て as okurigana. JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana, so すべて is an important spelling to recognize too.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ) overlaps strongly in the sense "all; everything." [全員](全員.md) (ぜんいん) specifically means every person in a group. 一部 (いちぶ), "a part," contrasts with the whole set.

## Distinctive meaning

The relevant set comes from context: 全ての窓 in a room does not necessarily mean every window in the building. Be careful with negation. 全てが正しいわけではありません (すべてがただしいわけではありません) means "Not everything is correct," not "Everything is incorrect." The scope of the negative changes the conclusion.
