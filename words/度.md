# 度 (たび) - occasion; time; degree

## Meaning

度 has two readings taught here. **たび** names an occasion or time.
Noun + の度に and dictionary-form verb + 度に mean "each time":
旅行の度に, "on every trip," and 会う度に, "whenever we meet."

- この歌を聞く度に、故郷を思い出します。 (このうたをきくたびに、こきょうをおもいだします。) - Every time I hear this song, I remember my hometown.

**ど** is a separate dictionary identity used as a counter for
occurrences and for degrees of temperature, angle, or a scale.

- その映画は二度見ました。 (そのえいがはにどみました。) - I have seen that film twice.
- 気温は二十度です。 (きおんはにじゅうどです。) - The temperature is twenty degrees.

一度 is いちど and 二度 is にど in this occurrence-counting pattern.

## How the kanji combine

[度](../kanji/度.md) supplies the whole written word. The primary
reading たび is native, so this card is labeled wago; ど is its
on reading in the separate counting and measurement entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[回](回.md) (かい) also counts occurrences: 二回, "twice."
[時](時.md) (とき) can name a time or occasion without expressing
the repeated "each time" relationship of 度に.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not substitute たび and ど freely. 聞く度に uses たび,
while 二度 and 二十度 use ど. The two readings share a spelling,
but their useful constructions must be learned separately.
