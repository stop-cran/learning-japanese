# 事情 (じじょう) - circumstances; reasons

## Meaning

事情 names circumstances, conditions, or reasons surrounding a matter.
It can explain why someone acts a certain way. Use 事情を説明する
(じじょうをせつめいする), "explain the circumstances," or 事情がある,
"there are circumstances/reasons."

- 家庭の事情で、仕事を休みました。 (かていのじじょうで、しごとをやすみました。) - I took time off work because of family circumstances.
- まず事情を聞かせてください。 (まずじじょうをきかせてください。) - First, please tell me what happened.

The first example gives a category of reason without revealing the private details.

## How the kanji combine

[事](../kanji/事.md) contributes an affair or matter.
[情](../kanji/情.md) contributes the circumstances or actual state here, rather
than necessarily an emotion. Together they point to the circumstances of a matter.
The on readings are じ + じょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[理由](理由.md) (りゆう) directly names a reason.
[状況](状況.md) (じょうきょう) describes a situation or state of affairs.
事情 often helps explain the background, while 状況 can describe what is
happening; this is a useful focus difference, not a ban on overlap.
[都合](都合.md) (つごう) can concern someone's circumstances or convenience.

## Distinctive meaning

事情 does not itself mean an excuse, a secret, or a bad situation.
事情が分かる (じじょうがわかる), "understand the circumstances," can mean
knowing enough background to interpret an event. A speaker may keep details
unstated with 事情があって, "because of certain circumstances," but the
word also suits a full, explicit explanation.
