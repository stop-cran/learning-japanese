# 秘密 (ひみつ) - secret; secrecy

## Meaning

秘密 is a secret or the state of keeping information private. Useful constructions are **秘密の + noun**, **thing を秘密にする**, and **秘密を守る**.

- この話は秘密にしてください。 (このはなしはひみつにしてください。) - Please keep this matter secret.
- 約束したので、秘密を守ります。 (やくそくしたので、ひみつをまもります。) - I promised, so I will keep the secret.

秘密にする means treating information as secret; 秘密を守る means not disclosing what has been entrusted or kept confidential.

## How the kanji combine

秘 contributes concealing or keeping secret, and 密 contributes secrecy or closeness. Here the two reinforce the idea of information not openly shared. The on readings are ヒ + ミツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

内緒 (ないしょ) overlaps with keeping something to oneself or not telling someone. 公開 (こうかい), "making public," contrasts with secrecy in an information-sharing context. [しゃべる](しゃべる.md) can describe telling something; 秘密をしゃべる means revealing a secret.

## Distinctive meaning

秘密 is not necessarily evidence of wrongdoing: a surprise gift can be 秘密. It can also mean the key behind an outcome, as in 成功の秘密 (せいこうのひみつ), "the secret of success." That use need not involve a promise of confidentiality; it may simply identify what explains the success.
