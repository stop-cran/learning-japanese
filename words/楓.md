# 楓 (かえで) - maple tree

## Meaning

楓 names maple trees, plants of the genus *Acer*. It is a plant name, not a general word for autumn colors.

- 庭に楓を植えました。 (にわにかえでをうえました。) - I planted a maple tree in the garden.
- 楓の葉が赤くなりました。 (かえでのはがあかくなりました。) - The maple leaves have turned red.

楓の葉 (かえでのは) specifies the leaves; 楓の木 (かえでのき) specifies the tree.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [楓](../kanji/楓.md) writes the whole native noun かえで. It can also be written かえで or カエデ. Do not pronounce its visible 木 and 風 shapes as two spoken words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[紅葉](紅葉.md), read もみじ, can also mean a maple tree. That article's primary reading こうよう concerns autumn foliage; do not use こうよう as the reading of the maple-tree example above.

[木](木.md) (き), tree, and [植物](植物.md) (しょくぶつ), plant, are broader nouns. Neither identifies the species. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

楓 read **ふう** is separately indexed as Formosan sweetgum (*Liquidambar formosana*), a different plant. This is not a freely interchangeable pronunciation of かえで.

Compounds need their own meanings: 楓葉 (ふうよう) means maple leaf, whereas the standalone ふう plant name means sweetgum. The character alone does not impose one botanical identity on every compound.
