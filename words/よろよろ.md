# よろよろ — tottering and staggering

## Meaning

よろよろ describes moving unsteadily, as if about to lose balance.

- 強い風に吹かれて、彼はよろよろ歩いた。
  — Blown by the strong wind, he staggered along.
- 疲れた足で、よろよろと階段を上った。
  — With tired legs, she climbed the stairs unsteadily.

## Synonyms and antonyms

よろよろ emphasizes unstable balance; it is not the deliberate, steady pace of すたすた.

## Distinctive meaning

It often describes a person who is close to stumbling but continues moving.
