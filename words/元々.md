# 元々 (もともと) - originally; from the outset

## Meaning

元々 means originally, from the beginning, or by nature.
It describes an original state or something true from the outset.
Kana もともと is usual; this article retains the source spelling.

- この建物は元々学校でした。 (このたてものはもともとがっこうでした。) - This building was originally a school.
- 彼女は元々料理が好きです。 (かのじょはもともとりょうりがすきです。) - She has always liked cooking.

元々の + noun describes the original thing or state:
元々の計画 (もともとのけいかく), "the original plan."

There is also a useful **〜てももともと** construction:
失敗してももともとだ (しっぱいしてももともとだ),
"Even if I fail, I will be no worse off."
It presents the result as leaving one in the same position as before.

## How the kanji combine

[元](../kanji/元.md) contributes origin or an earlier state.
々 repeats the preceding character, so the native reading もと is repeated.
The mark is not a separate kanji with a reading of its own.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本来](本来.md) (ほんらい) overlaps with originally or by nature.
[元](元.md) (もと) also has former/original uses.
[最初](最初.md) (さいしょ) names a beginning or first point;
元々 often contrasts the original situation with what followed.

## Distinctive meaning

"By nature" here concerns an inherent or original characteristic,
not automatically the natural environment.
The no-worse-off construction is not a general substitute for
[同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ), "the same"; learn the complete pattern.
