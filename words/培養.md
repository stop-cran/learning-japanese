# 培養 (ばいよう) — cultivation; biological culture

## Meaning

培養 is the managed growing of microorganisms, cells, tissues, or other biological material.

- 細胞を培養する。 (さいぼうをばいようする。) — to culture cells

## How the kanji combine

[培](../kanji/培.md) is associated with cultivating or fostering, while [養](../kanji/養.md) is associated with nourishing or raising. Together they form the established word 培養, used here for biological culture; its meaning is lexicalized rather than a fixed sum of separate character glosses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[育てる](育てる.md) describes everyday raising or growing, such as bringing up a child or growing vegetables; 培養 in this article is managed biological culture. [教養](教養.md) describes a person's cultivated knowledge or refinement. Its English gloss can overlap with “culture,” but the Japanese words are not interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

This article focuses on the biological sense of 培養; broader dictionary senses are not covered here.
