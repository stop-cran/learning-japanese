# 培養 (ばいよう) — cultivation; biological culture

## Meaning

培養 is growing microorganisms, cells, tissues, or other biological material under managed conditions. It also has broader senses of cultivating or fostering something.

- 細胞を培養する。 (さいぼうをばいようする。) — to culture cells

## How the kanji combine

[培](../kanji/培.md) is associated with cultivating, and [養](../kanji/養.md) with nourishing or raising. The whole word covers biological growth and broader cultivation; its meaning is not restricted to the literal sum of those glosses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

培養 describes managed growth of biological material or, figuratively, fostering a skill or idea. [教養](教養.md), by contrast, describes a person's cultivated knowledge or refinement. Their English glosses can overlap, but the Japanese words are not interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

The biological use commonly refers to controlled growth in a suitable environment. The word also appears figuratively for fostering a skill or an idea, so not every use involves laboratory work.
