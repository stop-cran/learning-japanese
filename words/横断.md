# 横断 (おうだん) - crossing; traversing

## Meaning

横断 is crossing from one side to another. 横断する is the corresponding verb.
In 道路を横断する (どうろをおうだんする), "cross a road," を marks the
space traversed; it does not mean that the road is being altered.

- 信号が青になってから、道路を横断しました。 (しんごうがあおになってから、どうろをおうだんしました。) - I crossed the road after the light turned green.
- 電車でアメリカ大陸を横断しました。 (でんしゃでアメリカたいりくをおうだんしました。) - I traveled across the American continent by train.

The second example extends the same idea to a large geographical area.

## How the kanji combine

[横](../kanji/横.md) contributes "across; sideways," and [断](../kanji/断.md)
contributes cutting or dividing. The relationship is directional: cutting or
passing across. The on readings combine as おう + だん. Movement uses need not
involve literally cutting anything.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[渡る](渡る.md) (わたる) is another useful verb for crossing a road or river.
縦断 (じゅうだん), traveling lengthwise through an area, provides a directional
contrast, not a universal opposite. East-west versus north-south can matter for
continental routes, but crossing a street does not require a compass direction.

## Distinctive meaning

横断 emphasizes passage across an area rather than simply arriving at a destination.
横断歩道 (おうだんほどう) means a pedestrian crossing: the whole compound
names the designated place, while 横断 names the crossing action.
