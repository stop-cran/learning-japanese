# おしゃれ (おしゃれ) - stylish; dressing up

## Meaning

おしゃれ describes being stylish or fashionable. As a na-adjective, use おしゃれな before a noun and おしゃれです as a predicate. As a noun with する, it describes dressing up or making oneself look attractive.

- おしゃれな服ですね。 (おしゃれなふくですね。) - Those are stylish clothes.
- 今日はおしゃれをして出かけます。 (きょうはおしゃれをしてでかけます。) - Today I am dressing up to go out.

It can also name a stylish dresser: 彼女はおしゃれです (かのじょはおしゃれです) can describe her as someone who dresses stylishly.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. お洒落 is another spelling, containing 洒 and [落](../kanji/落.md); the characters are not a literal definition involving spilling or falling. This kana headword has no written kanji in its metadata. Keep the small ゃ in しゃ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[流行](流行.md) (りゅうこう) names a fashion or trend. Being おしゃれ is an assessment of style, not merely a statement that something is currently widespread. 格好いい (かっこいい), "cool; good-looking," overlaps but is not limited to careful dress.

## Distinctive meaning

おしゃれする is not a suru form meaning "to become a trend"; here it means dressing up. It need not imply expensive clothing or a formal occasion. The adjective can also describe a stylish place or object, so it is not restricted to a person's clothing.
