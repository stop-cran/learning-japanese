# 車掌 (しゃしょう) - train conductor

## Meaning

車掌 is a train conductor: a member of the onboard staff whose duties concern passengers and operation of the service, rather than simply driving the train.

- 車掌に乗り換えについて聞きました。
  (しゃしょうにのりかえについてききました。) - I asked the conductor about changing trains.
- 車掌が車内放送で次の駅を案内しました。
  (しゃしょうがしゃないほうそうでつぎのえきをあんないしました。) - The conductor announced the next station over the onboard public-address system.

車掌に聞く marks the person asked with に. It does not mean asking the train itself.

## How the kanji combine

[車](../kanji/車.md) supplies the vehicle setting. 掌 contributes taking charge of duties, not a literal palm of the hand in this compound. Read シャ + ショウ as **しゃしょう**; keep the long しょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[運転手](運転手.md) (うんてんしゅ) is a driver. A 車掌 and a driver are different roles, even though staffing arrangements vary. A passenger is another contrasting role, not a linguistic antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The practical focus here is the train conductor. The public dictionary also recognizes conductors on buses; the word is not logically confined to rail vehicles. It does not imply that every train or bus has a separate conductor, or that every conductor performs exactly the same tasks.

Do not use 車掌 for a musical conductor merely because English uses "conductor" for both occupations.
