# 要求 (ようきゅう) - demand; firm request; requirement

## Meaning

要求 is a demand, firm request, or requirement. **要求する** is transitive: **人に物を要求する** means "demand something from someone." The requested action can be expressed with **verb + ことを要求する**.

- 客は店に返金を要求しました。 (きゃくはみせにへんきんをようきゅうしました。) - The customer demanded a refund from the shop.
- 会社は説明することを要求されました。 (かいしゃはせつめいすることをようきゅうされました。) - The company was required to provide an explanation.

The second example is passive. The company receives the demand; it is not the one making it.

## How the kanji combine

[要](../kanji/要.md) contributes needing or wanting, and [求](../kanji/求.md) contributes seeking or asking for. The related ideas reinforce each other in ヨウ + キュウ. Keep both long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[依頼](依頼.md) (いらい) is a request, often asking someone to undertake a task. [頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ) is the broader verb "ask; request." 要求 highlights a claim or requirement rather than functioning as a polite request formula.

## Distinctive meaning

要求 does not automatically imply anger, but it can sound forceful if used for a small personal favor. A specification can also contain requirements without anyone speaking angrily. **要求に応じる** (ようきゅうにおうじる) means "meet/comply with a demand"; refusing a demand is a response, not the noun's direct opposite.
