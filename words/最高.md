# 最高 (さいこう) - highest; best

## Meaning

最高 describes the highest level or the best quality. Useful patterns are
最高の + noun, 最高です, and compounds such as 最高気温 (さいこうきおん),
"maximum temperature."

- 今日の最高気温は三十度です。 (きょうのさいこうきおんはさんじゅうどです。) - Today's high temperature is thirty degrees.
- 最高の一日でした。 (さいこうのいちにちでした。) - It was a wonderful day.

The first example gives a maximum measurement. The second expresses enthusiastic
appreciation, rather than a formally measured ranking of every day.

## How the kanji combine

[最](../kanji/最.md) contributes "most," and [高](../kanji/高.md) contributes
"high." The modifier makes the degree superlative: highest. The on readings
are サイ + コウ. The evaluative "best" meaning extends beyond physical height.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[最低](最低.md) (さいてい) supplies "lowest" or "worst," according to context.
[最も](最も.md) (もっとも), "most," modifies another adjective or expression;
it is not interchangeable with the complete evaluation 最高です.

## Distinctive meaning

最高 can describe something objectively highest yet unwelcome, such as a
record-high temperature. Conversely, 最高！ can simply mean "Fantastic!"
The noun and situation decide whether the intended meaning is a numerical
maximum or praise. Do not assume "highest" automatically means "best for us."
