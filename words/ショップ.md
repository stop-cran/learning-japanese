# ショップ - shop

## Meaning

ショップ is a shop or retail store. It is a noun borrowed from English
shop, not a Japanese verb meaning to go shopping.

- 駅の近くに新しいショップができました。 (えきのちかくにあたらしいショップができました。) - A new shop opened near the station.
- このショップで帽子を買いました。 (このショップでぼうしをかいました。) - I bought a hat at this shop.

で marks where the purchase takes place in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in katakana, with no kanji components. ショ is a
contracted sound and ッ marks the pause before プ. Keep the small ョ and ッ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[店](店.md) (みせ) and [商店](商店.md) (しょうてん) overlap in the shop
sense. 店 also covers establishments such as restaurants.
[書店](書店.md) (しょてん) specifically identifies a bookstore.

## Distinctive meaning

ショップ is not inherently limited to boutiques or to a particular size
or ownership. オンラインショップ means an online shop; the word is not
restricted to a physical storefront. [買い物](買い物.md) (かいもの) is
shopping, the activity rather than the business.
