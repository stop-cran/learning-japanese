# 税金 (ぜいきん) - tax; taxes

## Meaning

税金 is money paid as tax. It is a noun: 税金を払う (ぜいきんをはらう) means "pay tax," and 税金を納める (ぜいきんをおさめる) is another useful payment expression.

- 毎年、税金を納めています。 (まいとし、ぜいきんをおさめています。) - I pay taxes every year.
- 税金は公共サービスに使われます。 (ぜいきんはこうきょうサービスにつかわれます。) - Tax money is used for public services.

The second example focuses on what the collected money funds. 税金がかかる (ぜいきんがかかる) means that tax applies or is charged.

## How the kanji combine

税 supplies the tax meaning and is read ゼイ. [金](../kanji/金.md), キン here, contributes money. The first character specifies what kind of money is meant: tax money, not gold as a material.

## Synonyms and antonyms

税 (ぜい) appears in tax names such as 所得税 (しょとくぜい), "income tax." [料金](料金.md) (りょうきん) is a charge for a service or use; it is not automatically a tax. [費用](費用.md) (ひよう) is the broader idea of cost.

## Distinctive meaning

English "duty" can mean a tax, but it also means an obligation or task. 税金 covers the monetary tax meaning, not someone's general responsibilities. A price may include or exclude tax; the noun alone does not tell you which pricing arrangement applies.
