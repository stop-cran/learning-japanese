# 予習 (よしゅう) — study ahead for a lesson

## Meaning

予習 is **studying material in advance of a lesson**. Add する to make a verb. The material prepared can take を.

- 明日の授業の予習をします。 (あしたのじゅぎょうのよしゅうをします。) — I will prepare for tomorrow's lesson.
- 次の課を予習しました。 (つぎのかをよしゅうしました。) — I studied the next lesson in advance.

予習します and 予習した are the polite and past forms. Both 予習する and 予習をする are useful.

## How the kanji combine

[予](../kanji/予.md) contributes “beforehand,” and [習](../kanji/習.md) contributes learning or practice. The on readings ヨ + シュウ form よしゅう: learning ahead of the lesson.

## Synonyms and antonyms

復習 (ふくしゅう), “review,” studies material after it has been taught. 準備 (じゅんび), “preparation,” is broader and includes arranging books, equipment, or other practical needs.

## Distinctive meaning

予習 is not merely packing a schoolbag or arriving early. It concerns engaging with the upcoming study material. Whether it counts as 宿題 (しゅくだい), “homework,” depends on whether the teacher assigned it.
