# アルバム (アルバム) - album

## Meaning

アルバム is a noun meaning an album, including a photograph album or a music album.
The material and the surrounding verb usually make the intended kind clear.
**写真のアルバム** (しゃしんのアルバム) explicitly identifies a photo album.

- 旅行の写真をアルバムに入れました。 (りょこうのしゃしんをアルバムにいれました。) - I put the travel photos in an album.
- 好きな歌手の新しいアルバムを聞いています。 (すきなかしゅのあたらしいアルバムをきいています。) - I am listening to my favorite singer's new album.

The first example uses に for the destination of the photos.
The second uses を for the album being listened to.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana and has no kanji combination.
Read the complete form アルバム rather than replacing its vowels with an English pronunciation.
The spelling has no long-vowel mark or small ッ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[写真](写真.md) (しゃしん) is a photograph; an album can organize many photographs.
[歌](歌.md) (うた) is a song; a music album can contain multiple songs or other recorded pieces.
These name possible contents, not synonyms for the collection.

## Distinctive meaning

アルバム identifies an organized collection, not necessarily one particular physical medium.
Context can concern a printed book, recorded music, or a digital collection.
Seeing 新しいアルバム, "a new album," alone does not tell you which kind is intended.
Use the topic, a modifying noun, or verbs such as 入れる and 聞く to resolve it.
