# どしどし — rapidly and one after another

## Meaning

どしどし describes many things arriving or happening in quick succession; it can also invite people to act without hesitation.

- 応募のメールが、朝からどしどし届いている。
  — Applications have been arriving one after another since morning.
- 質問があれば、遠慮せずどしどし聞いてください。
  — If you have questions, please ask freely and without hesitation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どしどし emphasizes abundance and momentum, not just a single fast action.

## Distinctive meaning

Context determines whether it describes rapid succession or an encouraging invitation.
