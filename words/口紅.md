# 口紅 (くちべに) - lipstick

## Meaning

口紅 is lipstick, the cosmetic used to color the lips. It names the cosmetic rather than the whole activity of putting on makeup.

- 赤い口紅を買いました。 (あかいくちべにをかいました。) - I bought red lipstick.
- 唇に口紅を塗ります。 (くちびるにくちべにをぬります。) - I apply lipstick to my lips.

口紅を塗る (くちべにをぬる) and 口紅をつける are ways to say put on lipstick.

## How the kanji combine

[口](../kanji/口.md) (くち), mouth, identifies where it is used; [紅](../kanji/紅.md) (べに) supplies the cosmetic rouge association. This is a native-reading modifier-plus-head compound.

紅 has a rouge/lipstick sense specifically at べに. Do not transfer that sense to every other reading of the character. The name does not require every modern lipstick to be red.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[化粧](化粧.md) (けしょう) is makeup or applying makeup more generally. 口紅 is one particular cosmetic.

[唇](唇.md) (くちびる) means the lips themselves. It is the place of application, not another name for lipstick.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the object from the action: 口紅を買う, buy lipstick; 口紅を塗る, apply lipstick. Do not use 口紅 as a generic term for every lip-care product or every kind of makeup.
