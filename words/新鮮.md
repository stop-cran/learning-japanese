# 新鮮 (しんせん) - fresh

## Meaning

新鮮 is a な-adjective meaning "fresh." It describes food that retains freshness and can also describe an idea or experience that feels refreshingly new.

- この店では新鮮な魚が買えます。 (このみせではしんせんなさかながかえます。) - You can buy fresh fish at this shop.
- その考え方は私には新鮮でした。 (そのかんがえかたはわたしにはしんせんでした。) - That way of thinking felt fresh to me.

Use 新鮮な before a noun and 新鮮です as a predicate. 新鮮さ (しんせんさ) names the quality, "freshness."

## How the kanji combine

[新](../kanji/新.md) means new; 鮮 contributes freshness or vividness. The two reinforce the idea of something fresh and not stale. The on readings are シン + セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[新しい](新しい.md) (あたらしい) means new more generally. A newly purchased object can be 新しい without being something you would naturally describe as 新鮮.

[古い](古い.md) (ふるい), "old," is a contextual contrast. For food, loss of freshness and actually being rotten are different degrees; [腐る](腐る.md) (くさる) names spoiling or rotting.

## Distinctive meaning

With an idea, 新鮮 describes the impression it gives, not a proven date of invention. With food, it concerns freshness, not whether it is raw: do not equate 新鮮 with 生 (なま). Let the noun and context determine which aspect of freshness is intended.
