# 奈良 (なら) — Nara (city or prefecture)

## Meaning

奈良 is Nara. The short name can refer to either the prefecture or the city within it. Add 県 (けん) for the prefecture or 市 (し) for the city when the distinction matters.

- 春休みに奈良へ行った。 (はるやすみにならへいった。) — I went to Nara during spring break.
- 奈良県の地図で奈良市を探した。 (ならけんのちずでならしをさがした。) — I looked for the city of Nara on a map of Nara Prefecture.

## How the kanji combine

[奈](../kanji/奈.md) supplies な at the beginning of the name; [良](../kanji/良.md) is read ら here. Recognize なら as a name: interpreting 良 as “good” will not tell you which place is meant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[県](県.md) identifies an administrative area; [市](市.md) identifies a city. Neither word is another name for Nara.

## Distinctive meaning

奈良県 and 奈良市 share the name but not the same boundary. Someone who lives in 奈良県 need not live in 奈良市.
