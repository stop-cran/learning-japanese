# 評判 (ひょうばん) - reputation; public estimation

## Meaning

評判 is a noun for reputation, public estimation, or what people say about someone or something. Use 評判がいい for a good reputation and 評判が悪い for a bad one.

- この店は料理がおいしいと評判です。 (このみせはりょうりがおいしいとひょうばんです。) - This restaurant has a reputation for good food.
- その医師は患者からの評判がいいです。 (そのいしはかんじゃからのひょうばんがいいです。) - That doctor has a good reputation among patients.

In the first example, と introduces what people say. In the second, から identifies whose evaluation contributes to the reputation.

## How the kanji combine

評 contributes evaluating or commenting, and [判](../kanji/判.md) contributes judging. Their related meanings combine in a word about the judgment circulating about a person or thing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[評価](評価.md) (ひょうか) is an evaluation or assessment; it may be made by one particular evaluator. 評判 emphasizes reputation or received opinion. [噂](噂.md) (うわさ), "rumor," concerns circulating talk and can overlap without necessarily assessing quality.

## Distinctive meaning

評判 is not automatically praise: a reputation can be poor. Nor does a good reputation guarantee that every customer agrees or that a claim has been verified. The expression reports what is said or how something is regarded, rather than establishing an objective score.
