# 差す (さす) — to hold up an umbrella

## Meaning

In 傘を差す (かさをさす), 差す means to put up or hold up an umbrella. It is a godan verb: 差します (さします), 差して (さして), 差した (さした).

雨が降っているので、傘を差しました。 (あめがふっているので、かさをさしました。) — It was raining, so I put up my umbrella.

Another common use is light shining in:

窓から日が差しています。 (まどからひがさしています。) — Sunlight is coming through the window.

## How the kanji combine

[差](../kanji/差.md) writes さ, with す completing the verb. Learn each construction; the character's "difference" meaning does not explain all uses of 差す.

## Synonyms and antonyms

傘を閉じる (かさをとじる), "close an umbrella," reverses the umbrella action. 指す (さす), "point," and 刺す (さす), "pierce," are different words despite the shared pronunciation.

## Distinctive meaning

The umbrella sense takes を, while sunlight is the subject in 日が差す. The dictionary also records extending an arm straight ahead in dance. That narrow sense is not a general instruction to translate every "stretch out one's hand" as 差す; 差し出す (さしだす) is useful for holding something out or offering it.
