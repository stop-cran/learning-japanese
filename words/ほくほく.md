# ほくほく — fluffy and flaky inside

## Meaning

A soft, fluffy, crumbly cooked texture, especially potatoes, sweet potatoes, or squash. It also describes someone quietly delighted with a pleasing result.

- この焼きいも（やきいも）は、中がほくほくしている。 — This baked sweet potato is fluffy inside.
- かぼちゃをほくほくに煮る（にる）。 — Cook the squash until it is tender and fluffy.
- 臨時収入（りんじしゅうにゅう）があって、父はほくほくしている。 — Dad is beaming because he has received some unexpected income.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ほかほか](ほかほか.md) concerns warmth; food can be both ほかほか and ほくほく. [もちもち](もちもち.md) is elastic and chewy instead of crumbly. [ふかふか](ふかふか.md) overlaps in softness but also describes cushioning.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ほくほくしたじゃがいも, ほくほくの焼きいも, or ほくほくする. The texture sense is not simply “hot”: a hot soup is not thereby ほくほく. The pleased-person sense commonly suggests satisfaction with a gain or good fortune, not a general synonym for every kind of happiness.
