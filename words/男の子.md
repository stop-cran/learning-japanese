# 男の子 (おとこのこ) — boy

## Meaning

男の子 means a boy, including a baby boy. Depending on context it can refer to someone's son, but it does not by itself identify whose child he is.

公園で男の子が遊んでいます。 (こうえんでおとこのこがあそんでいます。) — A boy is playing in the park.

## How the kanji combine

[男](../kanji/男.md) is read おとこ, "male/man," and [子](../kanji/子.md) is こ, "child." The linking の is written in kana. Learn the whole expression as the ordinary word for a boy rather than translating it as "a man's child."

## Synonyms and antonyms

女の子 (おんなのこ) means a girl. 子供 (こども) means a child without specifying gender. 少年 (しょうねん) is another word for a boy, with different age and stylistic associations.

## Distinctive meaning

In casual speech 男の子 can extend to a young man, but it is not a neutral label for an adult man in every setting. For "my son," 息子 (むすこ) makes the family relationship explicit. 男の子の名前 (おとこのこのなまえ) means "the boy's name."
