# 年中 (ねんじゅう) - all year round; all the time

## Meaning

年中, read ねんじゅう, means "all year round." It also works adverbially as "all the time"
or "constantly," without necessarily measuring a literal calendar year.
It can directly precede the description or action.

- この町は年中暖かいです。 (このまちはねんじゅうあたたかいです。) - This town is warm all year round.
- 彼は年中仕事の話をしています。 (かれはねんじゅうしごとのはなしをしています。) - He is constantly talking about work.

The first example concerns the seasons across the year. The second characterizes a repeated habit;
it need not mean uninterrupted talking every minute.

## How the kanji combine

[年](../kanji/年.md) contributes "year," and [中](../kanji/中.md), read ジュウ here, contributes
"throughout." Together they describe coverage of the whole year.
Keep ねんじゅう distinct from other readings of 中.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一年中 (いちねんじゅう) also means "throughout the year."
[毎年](毎年.md) (まいとし), "every year," describes recurrence from year to year, not necessarily
continuity through each year. Annual travel can happen 毎年 without happening 年中.

## Distinctive meaning

The habitual sense can carry emphasis or complaint, depending on what follows and the speaker's tone.
It is not a precise numerical frequency.
Reading matters: the dictionary's kindergarten-year sense belongs to ねんちゅう, not the
ねんじゅう taught here. Conversely, the "all the time" sense is specifically compatible with
ねんじゅう; do not exchange readings solely because the kanji match.
