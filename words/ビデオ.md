# ビデオ - video

## Meaning

ビデオ is a noun for video, including moving images and a short video film. It can also abbreviate a videotape or a videocassette recorder. The object and setting clarify whether the speaker means content, a tape, or equipment.

- 旅行のビデオを家族と見ました。 (りょこうのビデオをかぞくとみました。) - I watched the video of our trip with my family.
- 押し入れに古いビデオが何本もありました。 (おしいれにふるいビデオがなんぼんもありました。) - There were many old videotapes in the closet.

The first example concerns recorded content. The storage location, age, and counter 本 in the second support the physical-tape interpretation. **ビデオを撮る** (ビデオをとる) means "shoot a video."

## How the kanji combine

ビデオ is a katakana loanword corresponding to "video." There are no kanji components. The established spelling begins with ビ; learn the Japanese pronunciation rather than trying to preserve the English consonants exactly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

動画 (どうが) also names moving-image content or a video. [映画](映画.md) (えいが) is a film or movie; it is not a synonym for every short recording. [写真](写真.md) (しゃしん) is a still photograph.

## Distinctive meaning

ビデオ does not inherently specify VHS. That interpretation fits the tape sense, but a short digital recording can also be a ビデオ. Conversely, in a conversation about older equipment, the abbreviated recorder sense may be intended rather than a particular recording.
