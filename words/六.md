# 六 (ろく) — six

## Meaning

六 is the number six. ろく is the basic reading used in counting and many number-counter combinations.

- 六時に起きます。 (ろくじにおきます。) — I get up at six o'clock.
- 机が六台あります。 (つくえがろくだいあります。) — There are six desks.

Japanese usually combines a number with the counter appropriate to what is counted. The noun itself does not acquire an English-style plural ending.

## How the kanji combine

[六](../kanji/六.md) is a single numeral, read with the on-reading ロク here. It can also be written with the digit 6.

## Synonyms and antonyms

六つ (むっつ) is the native general counting form. 六日 (むいか) means six days or the sixth day of a month. These are related number expressions, not freely interchangeable readings of every occurrence.

## Distinctive meaning

Some counters change the sound: 六本 (ろっぽん) counts six long objects, and 六百 (ろっぴゃく) means six hundred. Learn such combinations as units instead of always attaching an unchanged ろく.
