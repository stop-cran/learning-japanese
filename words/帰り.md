# 帰り (かえり) — return journey

## Meaning

帰り means **return, coming back, or the journey home/back**. It is a noun and also the ます-stem of 帰る (かえる).

- 帰りはバスに乗ります。 (かえりはばすにのります。) — I will take the bus on the way back.
- 学校の帰りにパンを買いました。 (がっこうのかえりにぱんをかいました。) — I bought bread on the way home from school.

The pattern Xの帰りに identifies something done while returning from X.

## How the kanji combine

[帰](../kanji/帰.md) writes かえ and り completes the noun. Compare the verb 帰る and its polite form 帰ります: the stem is the whole 帰り, not just the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

行き (いき), “the outward journey,” contrasts with 帰り. 戻り (もどり) also means a return, but is useful more broadly for going back to an earlier place or state.

## Distinctive meaning

帰り is not a general translation of every English noun “return.” Returning a borrowed book is 本を返す (ほんをかえす); 帰り normally concerns someone or something coming back, especially home or to a base.
