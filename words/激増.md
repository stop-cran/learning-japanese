# 激増 (げきぞう) - sharp increase; sudden rise

## Meaning

激増 is a sharp or sudden increase in number or amount. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb:
the increasing quantity takes が.

- 利用者の数が激増しました。 (りようしゃのかずがげきぞうしました。) - The number of users increased sharply.
- 観光客の激増で、駅が混んでいます。 (かんこうきゃくのげきぞうで、えきがこんでいます。) - The station is crowded because of the sharp increase in tourists.

## How the kanji combine

激 contributes intensity; [増](../kanji/増.md) contributes increase.
The first character qualifies the second: this is a marked increase, not just any growth.
The on readings combine as ゲキ + ゾウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[増加](増加.md) (ぞうか) and [増える](増える.md) (ふえる) describe increases without themselves requiring sharpness.
[急激](急激.md) (きゅうげき) describes abrupt or drastic change in either direction.
激減 (げきげん), a sharp decrease, supplies the opposite direction.

## Distinctive meaning

激増する reports the increase; 激増させる explicitly expresses causing it.
The word does not specify a numerical threshold or establish that the change is beneficial.
Use the actual figures when the size of the increase matters.
