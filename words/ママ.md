# ママ (ママ) - mom; mama

## Meaning

ママ is a familiar noun for one's mother, like "Mom" or "Mama." It can be used to address her directly or mention her within family conversation.

- ママ、これを見て。 (ママ、これをみて。) - Mom, look at this.
- 子供はママの手を握っていました。 (こどもはママのてをにぎっていました。) - The child was holding Mom's hand.

The first example is an address, so no subject particle is needed after ママ. In the second, の connects the mother to her hand.

## How the kanji combine

ママ uses katakana and has no kanji components to explain. The repeated マ writes the familiar family word; it is not an abbreviation of the spelling お母さん. Learn the whole expression with its social setting.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お母さん](お母さん.md) (おかあさん) is another family address and reference term. 母 (はは) is useful when speaking about one's own mother to someone outside the family. Choice depends on the relationship and situation, not a universal age cutoff.

## Distinctive meaning

ママ also refers to a female proprietor of a bar or similar establishment. In that setting, someone called ママ need not be the speaker's mother. This is a second sense of the same dictionary entry. The family sense is familiar, so do not assume it is the neutral choice for every formal introduction or workplace conversation.
