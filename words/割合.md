# 割合 (わりあい) -- proportion; ratio

## Meaning

割合 describes a proportion, ratio, rate, or percentage: how an amount relates to another amount or to a whole.

- このクラスでは、留学生の割合が高いです。 (このクラスでは、りゅうがくせいのわりあいがたかいです。) -- This class has a high proportion of international students.
- 水とジュースを二対一の割合で混ぜます。 (みずとジュースをにたいいちのわりあいでまぜます。) -- Mix water and juice in a two-to-one ratio.

Xの割合 identifies whose share is discussed. A specified mixing ratio can take で.

## How the kanji combine

[割](../kanji/割.md) contributes division or shares, and [合](../kanji/合.md) contributes fitting or relating together. The native reading is わりあい; the noun is usually written without the kana visible in the fuller spelling 割り合い.

## Synonyms and antonyms

比率 (ひりつ) is a more explicitly numerical ratio. パーセント is a way to express a proportion per hundred, not a replacement for every use of 割合.

## Distinctive meaning

A high proportion does not necessarily mean a large total number. A small class can have a high 割合 of international students. Keep the comparison or whole clear when interpreting the number.
