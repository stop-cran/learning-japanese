# 鉢 (はち) - bowl; plant pot

## Meaning

鉢 is a bowl or basin, and also a pot for growing a plant.
The intended kind of vessel comes from context.

- 大きな鉢にサラダを盛りました。 (おおきなはちにサラダをもりました。) - I served the salad in a large bowl.
- 花を鉢に植えました。 (はなをはちにうえました。) - I planted the flower in a pot.

Another recorded use is the crown or skull of the head, as in 鉢が大きい, "have a large head."
It is not the ordinary general word for the whole head.

## How the kanji combine

This is one character, read はち, not a two-character compound.
The dictionary traces the vessel word to Sanskrit pātra through the phonetic transcription 鉢多羅 はったら.
Its Sino-Japanese reading and the deck's kango grouping do not imply an ultimately Chinese lexical origin,
or that the vessel must be made of metal.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ボール](ボール.md) includes a separate "bowl" identity as well as "ball."
[ちゃわん](ちゃわん.md) is a rice bowl or teacup, and [丼](丼.md) (どんぶり) is a deep bowl or a dish served in one.
These vessel uses overlap without making every bowl interchangeable for every purpose.
[鍋](鍋.md) (なべ) and [釜](釜.md) (かま) name cooking vessels; English "pot" alone does not identify which Japanese noun is appropriate.

## Distinctive meaning

For a flowerpot, 植木鉢 (うえきばち) makes the plant-growing purpose explicit.
The number [八](八.md) also reads はち, but is a different word and character.
Do not import the skull sense into an ordinary instruction about serving food.
