# 存ずる (ぞんずる) - know; think (humble)

## Meaning

存ずる is a humble verb for knowing, being aware, thinking, or believing.
It also covers feeling or considering something in the corresponding
respectful expression. Practical polite forms include 存じます
(ぞんじます) and 存じません (ぞんじません).

- その件は存じません。 (そのけんはぞんじません。) - I do not know about that matter.
- お役に立てれば幸いに存じます。 (おやくにたてればさいわいにぞんじます。) - I would be happy if I could be of help.

In the second sentence, 幸いに存じます expresses how the speaker would feel,
not knowledge of a fact. Use the humble expression for the speaker's side,
not to elevate the listener's knowing.

## How the kanji combine

[存](../kanji/存.md) is read ゾン here. Learn 存ずる together with
[存じる](存じる.md) (ぞんじる) and the shared polite stem 存じ.
The spelling does not make it an ordinary godan verb ending in る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知る](知る.md) (しる), [思う](思う.md) (おもう), and [感じる](感じる.md)
(かんじる) overlap in the appropriate knowing, thinking, and feeling senses.
[ご存じ](ご存じ.md) (ごぞんじ) is honorific rather than humble.

## Distinctive meaning

Grammatical labels differ by framework. JMdict calls this an "Ichidan -zuru"
alternative to the -jiru verb; Japanese dictionaries describe 存ずる as
サ変 and 存じる as its ichidan development. These labels are not evidence
for different meanings or for a godan pattern. Learn attested forms such
as 存ずる and 存じます rather than inventing 存ずります.
