# 気付く (きづく) - notice; become aware

## Meaning

気付く means to notice or realize something. It is an intransitive godan verb. Mark the thing noticed with に; for a fact expressed as a clause, use a clause in noun-modifying form + ことに気付く.
Before こと, plain verbs and i-adjectives attach directly. Affirmative nonpast na-adjectives
use な or formal である; noun predicates can use である, not unchanged だ.
Past and negative predicates keep their endings. See the [こと attachment guide](../articles/koto-wake-and-formal-nouns.md).

- 自分の間違いに気付きました。 (じぶんのまちがいにきづきました。) - I noticed my mistake.
- 財布がないことに気付きました。 (さいふがないことにきづきました。) - I realized that my wallet was missing.

The past is 気付いた (きづいた); the negative is 気付かない (きづかない). 気付かなかった means that someone did not notice, not necessarily that they deliberately ignored something.

## How the kanji combine

[気](../kanji/気.md) contributes attention or awareness, and [付](../kanji/付.md) contributes attaching or coming into contact. The verb combines on-reading キ with native 付く (つく), voiced to づく. This mixed on-plus-kun form is grouped under kango in the card metadata. 気づく is another standard dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知る](知る.md) (しる) can mean learning or coming to know a fact. [分かる](分かる.md) (わかる) can mean understanding it. 気付く emphasizes the point when it enters your awareness; noticing a mistake does not necessarily mean understanding its cause.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling is きづく, with づ. Learn 間違いに気付く as a whole pattern rather than transferring English "notice something" directly into an を-object construction.
