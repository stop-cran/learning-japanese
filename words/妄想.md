# 妄想 (もうそう) — wild fancy; delusion

## Meaning

妄想 is an imagined idea with little basis in reality. It can be a wild fantasy in everyday conversation; translating every occurrence as the clinical-sounding “delusion” can make a casual remark sound too serious.

- 宝くじが当たったら何を買おうかと妄想した。 (たからくじがあたったらなにをかおうかともうそうした。) — I indulged in a fantasy about what I'd buy if I won the lottery.
- 旅行の話はまだ私の妄想にすぎない。 (りょこうのはなしはまだわたしのもうそうにすぎない。) — That trip is still just a fantasy of mine.

## How the kanji combine

[妄](../kanji/妄.md) points to the lack of a sound basis; [想](../kanji/想.md) points to the thought or imagining. The reading in both examples is もうそう. Dictionaries also record ぼうそう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[想像](想像.md) is broader: imagining how a room will look from a measured plan need not be 妄想. [空想](空想.md) is closer to fantasy or daydreaming.

## Distinctive meaning

Take your cue from the speaker. The lottery example is playful speculation, not a diagnosis or a claim that the speaker actually won.
