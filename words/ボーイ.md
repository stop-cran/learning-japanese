# ボーイ (ボーイ) - boy; male attendant

## Meaning

ボーイ is a noun with two dictionary senses: "boy," and a male service worker such as a hotel porter, bellboy, or waiter. The service sense describes a role, not necessarily a child.

- ホテルのボーイが荷物を部屋まで運んでくれました。 (ホテルのボーイがにもつをへやまではこんでくれました。) - The hotel porter carried my luggage to the room.
- ボーイにコーヒーを頼みました。 (ボーイにコーヒーをたのみました。) - I ordered coffee from the waiter.

These examples use the service-worker sense; the hotel or restaurant setting determines the translation.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "boy." The long-vowel mark gives ボー, followed by イ. There are no kanji components to explain, and borrowing the English word does not make every English use transferable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[男の子](男の子.md) (おとこのこ) is a useful ordinary choice for a male child. [少年](少年.md) (しょうねん) also refers to a boy or youth.

[店員](店員.md) (てんいん) identifies a shop employee without specifying a male waiter or porter.

## Distinctive meaning

When describing a child, do not automatically replace 男の子 with ボーイ merely because both can translate "boy." When reading a hotel scene, consider the occupational sense before inferring anyone's age. To attract a staff member's attention, すみません is more useful than treating ボーイ as a general form of address.
