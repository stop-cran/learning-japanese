# 羽 (はね) - feather; wing

## Meaning

羽, read はね, is a noun meaning "feather" or "wing."
The intended object is supplied by context. The spelling 羽根 also represents these meanings.

- 道で鳥の羽を拾いました。 (みちでとりのはねをひろいました。) - I picked up a bird's feather on the road.
- 鳥が羽を広げました。 (とりがはねをひろげました。) - The bird spread its wings.

A loose feather fits the first example; the bird's wings fit the second.
Both are dictionary-supported uses of はね, not different readings assigned by the English translation.

羽根 (はね) also names a shuttlecock in badminton or in the traditional game 羽根つき (はねつき).
This is an additional use, not a claim that every loose feather is a shuttlecock.

- 羽根つきの羽根を買いました。 (はねつきのはねをかいました。) - I bought a shuttlecock for hanetsuki.

## How the kanji combine

[羽](../kanji/羽.md) is a single character writing the native word はね.
There is no compound reading to assemble. The alternative 羽根 adds [根](../kanji/根.md) in the spelling,
but does not make every dictionary sense of 羽根 automatically available to every spelling.
Here learn the whole written form 羽根 as はね, not a literal "feather root."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[翼](翼.md) (つばさ), "wing," overlaps with the second example but does not simply mean a single feather.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the noun and counter separately instead of assigning the counter meaning to はね.
The community source row pairs はね with an incompatible counter gloss.
This card deliberately follows the verified feather/wing identity; the list compiler's original
intention has not been established. The counter comparison is not an additional N3 assignment.
For badminton, the dictionary restricts the shuttlecock sense to はね but not to only one of 羽 and 羽根.
For hanetsuki it specifically lists 羽根. Do not transfer the alternate reading は of 羽
to the complete spelling 羽根.
