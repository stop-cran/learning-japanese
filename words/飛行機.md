# 飛行機 (ひこうき) — airplane

## Meaning

飛行機 means an **airplane**. It is the ordinary word for the aircraft used on many passenger flights.

飛行機で北海道へ行きます。 (ひこうきでほっかいどうへいきます。) — I will go to Hokkaido by plane.

Use で for the means of travel. With 乗る (のる), use に: 飛行機に乗る (ひこうきにのる), “board/take a plane.”

## How the kanji combine

[飛](../kanji/飛.md) means flying, and [行](../kanji/行.md) contributes movement. Together 飛行 (ひこう) means flight. [機](../kanji/機.md) identifies a machine: a machine for flying. The on readings are ヒ + コウ + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

航空機 (こうくうき) is the broader technical term “aircraft.” ヘリコプター specifically names a helicopter. 電車 (でんしゃ), “train,” offers a travel alternative rather than a lexical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

飛行機 names the vehicle, not a particular scheduled flight. A flight number is commonly expressed with 便 (びん). The same plane can operate different flights at different times.
