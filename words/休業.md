# 休業 (きゅうぎょう) - temporary closure; suspension of business

## Meaning

休業 is a suspension of business or work: a shop or other operation is taking a break or temporarily closed. It is a noun and an intransitive する-verb.

- 店は改装のため、一週間休業します。
  (みせはかいそうのため、いっしゅうかんきゅうぎょうします。) - The shop will close for a week for renovations.
- 入口に「本日休業」と書いてありました。
  (いりぐちに「ほんじつきゅうぎょう」とかいてありました。) - A notice at the entrance said, "Closed today."

本日休業 is a useful notice expression. The period and reason for the closure come from the rest of the message, not from 休業 alone.

## How the kanji combine

[休](../kanji/休.md) contributes resting or stopping, and [業](../kanji/業.md) work or business: business is suspended. The readings are キュウ + ギョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[営業](営業.md) (えいぎょう) concerns operating a business. 廃業 (はいぎょう) means giving up or discontinuing a business or practice, rather than merely taking a temporary break. [休暇](休暇.md) (きゅうか) is leave or vacation.

## Distinctive meaning

休業 is not limited to shops: it can concern other work or school operations. Distinguish an operation's suspension from one person's absence and from simply shutting a door. A closed door alone does not establish that the business is 休業中 (きゅうぎょうちゅう).
