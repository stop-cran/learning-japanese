# 暗い (くらい) — dark; dim

## Meaning

暗い describes darkness or poor lighting. A 暗い部屋 (くらいへや) is a dark room:
it may simply have little light, without having black walls.
This is an い-adjective, so it goes directly before the noun.

- この部屋は暗いです。 (このへやはくらいです。) — This room is dark.
- 暗くなったので、電気をつけました。 (くらくなったので、でんきをつけました。) — It got dark, so I turned on the light.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [暗](../kanji/暗.md) supplies the darkness meaning; い is the adjective ending.
The native reading is くらい.
In 暗くなる (くらくなる), "get dark," い changes to く before なる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[明るい](明るい.md) (あかるい), "bright; well-lit," is the direct opposite for lighting.
[黒い](黒い.md) (くろい) names black or dark coloration; that is a different property.
A room can have black furniture and still be 明るい if it has plenty of light.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 暗い for how much light there is, rather than substituting it for every English use of "black."
The forms 暗くない (くらくない), "not dark," and 暗かった (くらかった), "was dark,"
follow the usual い-adjective pattern.
The examples above focus on literal lighting. 暗い can also mean "gloomy" emotionally:
暗い気持ち (くらいきもち), "a gloomy mood." This is an extension beyond light levels,
not a description of black color.
