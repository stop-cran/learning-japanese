# 体制 (たいせい) - system; organizational setup; regime

## Meaning

体制 is the system or setup under which something operates. In politics
it can name a regime or the ruling establishment. It is a noun.

- 新しい体制で仕事を始めます。 (あたらしいたいせいでしごとをはじめます。) - We will start work under a new organizational setup.
- その国の政治体制を研究しています。 (そのくにのせいじたいせいをけんきゅうしています。) - I am studying that country's political system.

体制を整える (たいせいをととのえる) means to put the necessary setup in
order. It can involve staffing, procedures, responsibilities, or other
arrangements; the word does not mean people alone.

## How the kanji combine

[体](../kanji/体.md) contributes a body or organized whole;
[制](../kanji/制.md) contributes a system or regulation.
The related meanings describe an organized operating structure.
Read タイ + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[体系](体系.md) (たいけい) emphasizes related parts forming a coherent
system. [制度](制度.md) (せいど) concerns an institutional system;
[組織](組織.md) (そしき) has organization and structure uses.
Their ranges overlap, so do not treat them as mutually exclusive labels
for ideas, rules, and people.

## Distinctive meaning

The political use can refer to those in power as well as the arrangements
of rule: 反体制 (はんたいせい) means anti-establishment.
That sense does not make every workplace 体制 a national government.
Keep 態勢 (たいせい), a posture or state of readiness, separate even when
a particular situation could be described from either perspective.
