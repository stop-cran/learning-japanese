# 正確 (せいかく) - accurate; precise

## Meaning

正確 describes something accurate, correct, precise, or exact.
It is a na-adjective: **正確な + noun**, **正確です**, and **正確に + verb**.
正確さ (せいかくさ) is the derived noun "accuracy."

- 正確な住所を教えてください。 (せいかくなじゅうしょをおしえてください。) - Please tell me the exact address.
- 名前を正確に書いてください。 (なまえをせいかくにかいてください。) - Please write the name accurately.

The first example describes information; the second describes how an action is performed.
正確ではない means "is not accurate."

## How the kanji combine

[正](../kanji/正.md) contributes correctness, and [確](../kanji/確.md) contributes certainty or firmness.
The related ideas reinforce being reliably correct in the details.
The on readings セイ + カク give せいかく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[正しい](正しい.md) (ただしい) overlaps with correct, but can also concern what is right in a broader sense.
[確実](確実.md) (かくじつ) emphasizes certainty or reliability.
不正確 (ふせいかく), "inaccurate," is the direct negative counterpart.

## Distinctive meaning

正確 concerns correspondence with facts, measurements, or the intended details.
正確な時計 (せいかくなとけい) keeps accurate time; this does not make 正確 a general noun meaning
"punctuality." Nor does it mean that an object is genuine rather than counterfeit.
For this word, keep "accurate/precise" as the core and let context determine the natural English translation.
Do not confuse it with the same-reading [性格](性格.md), "personality."
