# 聞こえる (きこえる) — be audible

## Meaning

聞こえる means **to be audible or reach someone's ears**. The sound takes が. English often translates it with “can hear,” even though it is not the regular potential form of 聞く.

- 外から音楽が聞こえます。 (そとからおんがくがきこえます。) — I can hear music from outside.
- 声が小さくて、よく聞こえません。 (こえがちいさくて、よくきこえません。) — Your voice is quiet, so I can't hear you well.

It is an ichidan verb: 聞こえます, 聞こえない, 聞こえた.

## How the kanji combine

[聞](../kanji/聞.md) writes き, while こえる is okurigana. The conjugation stem is 聞こえ (きこえ), obtained by removing only the final る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

聞く (きく) can mean hearing or deliberately listening. 聞ける (きける), its potential, often concerns ability or an opportunity to listen. 聞こえない means that a sound is not audible.

## Distinctive meaning

聞こえる describes perception rather than someone's deliberate effort. It can also describe an impression: 変に聞こえる (へんにきこえる), “sound strange.” Neither use requires translating it as an English passive.
