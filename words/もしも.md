# もしも (もしも) - if; supposing

## Meaning

もしも emphasizes a hypothetical situation: "if," "in case," or "supposing."
It is an adverb used with a conditional sentence, not a replacement for the
conditional ending itself. The examples use たら and なら.

- もしも雨が降ったら、家で映画を見ましょう。 (もしもあめがふったら、いえでえいがをみましょう。) - If it rains, let's watch a film at home.
- もしも一か月休めるなら、旅行したいです。 (もしもいっかげつやすめるなら、りょこうしたいです。) - If I could take a month off, I would like to travel.

The second example imagines an opportunity, not necessarily a likely event.

## How the kanji combine

This is an emphatic form of [もし](もし.md), written in kana. The dictionary
also records the rare spelling 若しも, but no kanji decomposition is needed
to use the ordinary expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もし introduces a hypothetical condition without the added emphasis of もしも.
The difference is not a strict division between possible and impossible events.
Both rely on the rest of the sentence to show the condition and its consequence.

## Distinctive meaning

もしも often introduces an unwelcome possibility, but it is not restricted to
bad outcomes. Compare the rainy-day backup plan with the imagined holiday.
もしもの時 (もしものとき), "in an emergency; if the unexpected happens," uses
もしもの before a noun. This contingency use should not be confused with a
question meaning "whether."
