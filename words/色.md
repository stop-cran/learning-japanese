# 色 (いろ) — color

## Meaning

色 is the ordinary noun for a color or shade. It can also refer to someone's complexion or expression,
but the basic use is the visible color of something.

- この色が好きです。（このいろがすきです。） — I like this color.
- このシャツは色がきれいです。（このシャツはいろがきれいです。） — This shirt has a beautiful color.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun, not a compound. [色](../kanji/色.md) is read いろ, with no okurigana.
The compound readings ショク and シキ belong to other words; they do not replace いろ in these sentences.

## Synonyms and antonyms

カラー also means "color," especially in product descriptions and expressions contrasting color with black-and-white.
It overlaps with 色 but does not replace every figurative use.
There is no direct antonym of 色: [白](../kanji/白.md), white, and [黒](../kanji/黒.md), black, are colors rather than its opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

色 is a noun, not an adjective meaning "colorful." To connect it to another noun, use の where appropriate:
色の名前（いろのなまえ）, "the name of a color." Learn color descriptions such as 茶色（ちゃいろ）, "brown,"
as words in their own right.
