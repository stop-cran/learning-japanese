# 倹しい (つましい) — frugal; modest in one's living

## Meaning

つましい describes living without extravagance: frugal, simple, or modest.
It is usually written in kana. This article uses the recognized spelling 倹しい
to help you read the kanji, not to recommend replacing the usual kana spelling.

- 二人はつましい暮らしをしています。 (ふたりはつましいくらしをしています。) — The two of them lead a modest life.
- 私たちの生活はつましいですが、満足しています。 (わたしたちのせいかつはつましいですが、まんぞくしています。) — Our life is modest, but we are content.

It is an い-adjective: つましい暮らし, not つましいな暮らし.
The polite predicate is つましいです; the negative is つましくありません.

## How the kanji combine

[倹](../kanji/倹.md) supplies つま, with しい as the written ending:
倹しい is つましい. There is no two-kanji compound to split into on readings.
JMdict also recognizes 約しい (つましい), marking that alternative kanji spelling as rare.
That spelling flag does not make the pronunciation つましい archaic.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[倹約](倹約.md) (けんやく) concerns thrift and avoiding wasteful spending.
Compare 倹約する, "economize," with つましい暮らし, "a frugal or modest life."
[地味](地味.md) (じみ), plain or unshowy, overlaps in descriptions of restrained living;
it can also describe subdued colors without making a claim about spending.
[贅沢](贅沢.md) (ぜいたく), luxury or extravagance, supplies a contrast.

The separate adjective つつましい, often spelled 慎ましい, can describe modest or reserved behavior
and also frugal or simple living. Do not manufacture a strict behavior-versus-lifestyle split:
the meanings overlap, but つましい and つつましい are different pronunciations.

## Distinctive meaning

つましい does not itself say that someone is unhappy, destitute, or stingy.
[けち](けち.md) is generally a criticism of someone's willingness to spend or share.
Nor does つましい mean "easy" merely because English can describe this life as "simple":
the examples concern modest living, not the difficulty of a task.
