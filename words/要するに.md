# 要するに (ようするに) - in short

## Meaning

要するに introduces a summary or the essential point: "in short," "to sum up,"
or "to put it simply." It functions as an adverbial expression before the
concluding statement.

- 値段が高く、使い方も難しいです。要するに、おすすめできません。 (ねだんがたかく、つかいかたもむずかしいです。ようするに、おすすめできません。) - It is expensive and difficult to use. In short, I cannot recommend it.
- 要するに、何をすればいいのですか。 (ようするに、なにをすればいいのですか。) - In short, what should I do?

The first example summarizes an evaluation; the second asks for the practical
point of a longer explanation.

## How the kanji combine

[要](../kanji/要.md) contributes the essential point or gist. It uses the on
reading ヨウ within the established expression 要するに. The kana するに are
part of this expression, not additional kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[つまり](つまり.md) also introduces a conclusion or restatement.
要するに explicitly frames what follows as the main point of the preceding
material. 詳しく言えば (くわしくいえば), "to explain in detail," moves in the
opposite explanatory direction but is not a lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Using 要するに does not guarantee that a summary is fair or complete.
When summarizing someone else's position, check that the conclusion really
follows. In a question, it can sound impatient if it dismisses details the
other person considers important; the phrase itself is not inherently rude.
