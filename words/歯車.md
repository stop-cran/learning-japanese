# 歯車 (はぐるま) - gear; cogwheel

## Meaning

歯車 is a gear or cogwheel: a toothed wheel that meshes with another part
to transmit motion. It is a noun.

- この機械では二つの歯車がかみ合っています。 (このきかいではふたつのはぐるまがかみあっています。) - Two gears mesh in this machine.
- 歯車が壊れて、時計が止まりました。 (はぐるまがこわれて、とけいがとまりました。) - A gear broke, and the clock stopped.

歯車がかみ合う describes gears meshing. Keep the whole wheel distinct from
one of its individual teeth.

## How the kanji combine

[歯](../kanji/歯.md), は, contributes teeth, and
[車](../kanji/車.md), くるま, contributes a wheel.
The wheel is characterized by its teeth. In the established compound,
くるま voices to ぐるま, giving はぐるま, not はくるま.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[車輪](車輪.md) (しゃりん) is a vehicle wheel, not automatically a gear.
[機械](機械.md) (きかい) names a machine; a gear may be one of its parts.
There is no useful general antonym for a cogwheel.

## Distinctive meaning

Figuratively, 組織の歯車 (そしきのはぐるま), "a cog in an organization,"
describes a person as a functioning part of a larger system.
This use does not mean that every occurrence of 歯車 refers to a person.
Context separates the mechanical part from the organizational comparison.
