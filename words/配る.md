# 配る (くばる) - distribute; hand out

## Meaning

配る means distribute, hand out or deliver things to recipients. It is transitive: the things distributed take を; recipients can take に.

- 学生にプリントを配りました。 (がくせいにプリントをくばりました。) - I handed out worksheets to the students.
- 入口で地図を配っています。 (いりぐちでちずをくばっています。) - They are handing out maps at the entrance.

An additional use is allocating or stationing people, such as 警備員を配る (けいびいんをくばる), station security guards.

It is godan: 配ります (くばります), 配って (くばって), 配らない (くばらない).

## How the kanji combine

[配](../kanji/配.md) supplies the native stem くば and the distribution association; る is the verb ending. 賦る is a rare kanji spelling in the dictionary, not a new reading to learn here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[配達](配達.md) (はいたつ) concerns delivery, often to destinations. [届ける](届ける.md) (とどける) focuses on bringing something to its recipient or destination. 配る foregrounds distribution, but there is no exceptionless rule requiring it to name a particular number of recipients.

[渡す](渡す.md) (わたす) means hand over or pass; it need not describe an organized distribution.

## Distinctive meaning

The same distribution image also appears in attention expressions such as 気を配る (きをくばる), pay attention or take care. Learn the whole expression; bare 配る does not mean worry in every context.
