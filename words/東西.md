# 東西 (とうざい) - east and west; East and West

## Meaning

東西 means east and west, or the east-west direction.
It also names the cultural East and West in a contrast.

- この道路は東西に延びています。 (このどうろはとうざいにのびています。) - This road extends east to west.
- 東西の文化を比較しました。 (とうざいのぶんかをひかくしました。) - We compared Eastern and Western cultures.

東西に in the first example marks the orientation, not two separate trips.
The countries or traditions included in the cultural contrast depend on context.

## How the kanji combine

[東](../kanji/東.md) contributes east and [西](../kanji/西.md) west.
This opposite-pair compound is read トウ + ザイ, with サイ voiced.
The dictionary's alternative ひがしにし is obsolete; it is not the reading used in these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The separate nouns [東](東.md) (ひがし) and [西](西.md) (にし) name the individual directions.
南北 (なんぼく), "north and south," contrasts with the east-west axis.
[全国](全国.md) (ぜんこく) means the whole country, not simply the two directions.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not replace 全国 with 東西 wherever English says "the whole country."
Expressions combining directions can evoke a wide area, but context and the whole expression matter.
The dictionary also records an attention-getting call restricted to とうざい;
that separate use does not make an ordinary east-west road sentence a call to an audience.
