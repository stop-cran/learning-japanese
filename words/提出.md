# 提出 (ていしゅつ) - submission; handing in

## Meaning

提出 is a noun for submitting or handing something in. 提出する is transitive: mark the document, assignment, or other submitted item with を and the recipient or office with に.

- 金曜日までにレポートを提出してください。 (きんようびまでにレポートをていしゅつしてください。) - Please submit the report by Friday.
- 申請書を事務所に提出しました。 (しんせいしょをじむしょにていしゅつしました。) - I submitted the application form to the office.

提出期限 (ていしゅつきげん) is the submission deadline. までに marks a deadline, not an action continuing until that time.

## How the kanji combine

提 contributes bringing forward or presenting; [出](../kanji/出.md) contributes putting out. Together they describe presenting material for another party to receive or consider. The readings are テイ + シュツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出す](出す.md) (だす), "put out; hand in," is broader and can cover the same action when the setting is clear.

[受け取る](受け取る.md) (うけとる), "receive," describes the other side of the handover, not a grammatical opposite that can replace 提出.

## Distinctive meaning

English "presentation" here means presenting documents or evidence, not necessarily delivering a speech. 提出 applies to digital submissions as well as paper handed over physically. It does not guarantee approval: a submitted application can still be under review. Keep submitting, receiving, and accepting as separate stages.
