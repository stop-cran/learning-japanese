# とんとん — tap-tap; smoothly; break-even

## Meaning

とんとん imitates repeated taps or knocks. It also describes smooth progress and an even or break-even result.

- 肩をとんとんとたたかれた。 — Someone tapped me on the shoulder. (肩: かた.)
- 話がとんとん進んで、来月引っ越すことになった。 — Things moved along smoothly, and we decided to move next month.
- 売り上げと費用が同じで、収支はとんとんだった。 — Sales equalled costs, so we broke even. (売り上げ: うりあげ; 費用: ひよう; 収支: しゅうし.)

Use とんとん（と）たたく for tapping. The smooth-progress use also appears in とんとん拍子に (とんとんびょうしに), "without a hitch." For an even result, use とんとんだ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こんこん](こんこん.md) also imitates knocking. [どんどん](どんどん.md) can depict louder pounding or rapid progress, but it does not mean breaking even.

## Distinctive meaning

とんとんの収支 is neither a profit nor a loss. Do not interpret this as a tapping sound, or apply the predicate とんとんだ indiscriminately to every adverbial sense.
