# 電池 (でんち) - battery; electric cell

## Meaning

電池 is a battery or electric cell, a source that converts energy into electrical energy.
The noun covers more than disposable batteries.

- リモコンの電池を交換しました。 (リモコンのでんちをこうかんしました。) - I replaced the batteries in the remote control.
- この電池は充電できます。 (このでんちはじゅうでんできます。) - This battery can be recharged.

電池が切れる (でんちがきれる) means that a battery runs out.
Whether a particular 電池 is rechargeable depends on its type, not on this noun alone.

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) contributes electricity and [池](../kanji/池.md) a pool or reservoir association.
Learn the complete technical word rather than imagining an ordinary pond containing electricity.
The on readings are デン + チ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[乾電池](乾電池.md) (かんでんち) is a dry-cell battery, a more specific kind.
充電池 (じゅうでんち) specifies a rechargeable battery.
[電力](電力.md) (でんりょく), electric power, names a physical quantity or supplied power, not the battery itself.

## Distinctive meaning

電池 does not guarantee a particular size, voltage, chemistry, or safe charging method.
The technical family also includes names such as 太陽電池 (たいようでんち), "solar cell";
not every use refers to the small cylindrical batteries used in household equipment.
