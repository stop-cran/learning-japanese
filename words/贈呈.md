# 贈呈 (ぞうてい) — presentation of a gift or prize

## Meaning

贈呈 means presenting a gift, prize or the like. JMdict glosses it as “presentation (of a gift, prize, etc.),” and Digital Daijisen defines it as giving something to someone, or presenting something humbly, with 進呈 as an equivalent. It can be used with する.

- 優勝したチームにトロフィーが贈呈されました。 (ゆうしょうしたチームにトロフィーがぞうていされました。) — A trophy was presented to the winning team.
- 卒業生が学校に記念品を贈呈しました。 (そつぎょうせいががっこうにきねんひんをぞうていしました。) — The graduates presented a commemorative gift to the school.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[贈](../kanji/贈.md) means giving as a gift, and [呈](../kanji/呈.md) means presenting or offering. Together, they mean presenting a gift.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[贈る](贈る.md) (おくる) is the verb for giving a gift, and [差し上げる](差し上げる.md) (さしあげる) is giving humbly. 進呈 しんてい, “presentation (e.g. of a gift)” (JMdict entry 1366150), has a similar meaning. [贈り物](贈り物.md) (おくりもの) is the gift itself.

## Distinctive meaning

The compound 贈呈式 ぞうていしき is a presentation ceremony (JMdict entry 1771940).
