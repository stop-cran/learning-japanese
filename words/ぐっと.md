# ぐっと — firmly; considerably

## Meaning

ぐっと can describe a forceful, concentrated action or a marked change in degree.

- 力を入れて、重い引き出しをぐっと引いた。
  — I pulled the heavy drawer open with a firm effort.
- 気温が一晩でぐっと下がった。
  — The temperature dropped considerably overnight.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぐっと highlights a concentrated effort or a noticeable change. ぎゅっと more specifically
emphasizes squeezing or tightly holding something; ぐいぐい often suggests persistent force or
forward pressure.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the surrounding verb to determine whether ぐっと describes effort, a sudden increase or
decrease, or another marked change.
