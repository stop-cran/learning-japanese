# 足す (たす) — to add

## Meaning

足す means adding numbers or adding more of something. It is a transitive godan verb: 足します, 足さない, 足して. Use AにBを足す for "add B to A."

- 二に三を足すと五になります。 (ににさんをたすとごになります。) — Adding three to two makes five.
- スープに水を少し足します。 (スープにみずをすこしたします。) — I add a little water to the soup.

## How the kanji combine

[足](../kanji/足.md) supplies た, with す as okurigana. The character's sufficiency association helps connect 足す with 足りる, "be enough." This is not the noun 足 (あし), "foot."

## Synonyms and antonyms

加える (くわえる) also means adding and has broader uses. 引く (ひく), "subtract," is the arithmetic opposite. [足りる](足りる.md) describes whether the resulting amount is sufficient.

## Distinctive meaning

For reading an equation aloud, 二足す三は五 (にたすさんはご) means "two plus three is five." Ordinary sentences can instead mark both quantities with に and を, as in the first example.
