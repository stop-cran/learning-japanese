# 快適 (かいてき) - comfortable; pleasant

## Meaning

快適 describes something comfortable or pleasant to experience, such as a room, journey,
or living environment. It behaves as a na-adjective: 快適な部屋, "a comfortable room";
快適です, "is comfortable"; 快適に, "comfortably."

- この部屋は涼しくて快適です。
  (このへやはすずしくてかいてきです。) - This room is cool and comfortable.
- 新しい椅子のおかげで、快適に仕事ができます。
  (あたらしいいすのおかげで、かいてきにしごとができます。)
  - Thanks to the new chair, I can work comfortably.

The negative is 快適ではない or the polite 快適ではありません.
There is no i-adjective form such as かいてきい.

## How the kanji combine

快 contributes pleasantness, and [適](../kanji/適.md) contributes suitability.
Together they suggest conditions that suit someone and feel pleasant.
Both are on readings: カイ + テキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[楽](楽.md) (らく) can mean easy or comfortable, often with attention to effort or burden.
[楽しい](楽しい.md) (たのしい) means enjoyable or fun: comfortable seating does not by itself
make an activity entertaining. 不快 (ふかい), "unpleasant," supplies a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

快適 focuses on the quality of the experience or conditions.
A trip may be 快適 because the seat, temperature, and noise level are agreeable,
even if the purpose of the trip is serious rather than fun.
