# オイル - oil; lubricant

## Meaning

オイル is oil. Depending on the context, it can be cooking oil,
lubricating oil, engine oil or petroleum. It is not restricted to
industrial products.

- フライパンにオリーブオイルを入れます。
  (フライパンにオリーブオイルをいれます。) - I put olive oil in the frying pan.
- 車のオイルを交換しました。
  (くるまのオイルをこうかんしました。) - I changed the car's oil.

オイルを交換する means to change the oil. The vehicle context
identifies engine oil in the second example; the bare noun alone
does not specify the product.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana borrowing from English "oil," with no kanji to
divide. Keep the three kana オ・イ・ル distinct; オ is not a
long-vowel spelling オー.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[油](油.md) (あぶら) also covers cooking and machine oils.
[石油](石油.md) (せきゆ) identifies petroleum and has a colloquial
kerosene sense. [脂](脂.md) (あぶら) is fat or grease.
English "oil/grease" overlaps across these labels, but the Japanese
words are not freely interchangeable in every product name.

## Distinctive meaning

オイル is not a guarantee that something is edible or suitable for
an engine. JMdict also records massage oil or ointment and kerosene
senses. Learn a complete product term such as エンジンオイル,
"engine oil," rather than treating all these substances as one product.
