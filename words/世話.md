# 世話 (せわ) — care; assistance

## Meaning

世話 is care, help, or looking after someone. Use Xの世話をする or Xを世話する for providing that care.

- 留守の間、猫の世話をお願いします。 (るすのあいだ、ねこのせわをおねがいします。) — Please look after the cat while I'm away.
- 毎日、弟の世話をしています。 (まいにち、おとうとのせわをしています。) — I look after my younger brother every day.

## How the kanji combine

[世](../kanji/世.md), "world; society," and [話](../kanji/話.md), "talk," form the established word せわ. Their individual meanings do not directly predict the modern care sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

手伝い (てつだい), "help; assistance," often concerns helping with a task. 世話 can involve continuing responsibility for a person, animal, or their needs.

## Distinctive meaning

お世話になる (おせわになる) looks from the receiving side: being helped or looked after. お世話になりました (おせわになりました) expresses thanks for such help. Adding お does not reverse the relationship; する versus なる is the crucial difference.
