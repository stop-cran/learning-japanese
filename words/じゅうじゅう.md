# じゅうじゅう — sizzling on a hot surface

## Meaning

The sustained sizzle of meat, fish, or other food cooking against hot metal or fat.

- フライパンの上で肉がじゅうじゅう焼けている。 — The meat is sizzling in the frying pan.
- 熱い鉄板（てっぱん）に野菜をのせると、じゅうじゅうと音がした。 — When I put the vegetables on the hot griddle, they sizzled.

## How the kanji combine

A native sound word repeating じゅう, with a long vowel. Katakana ジュージュー is another dictionary spelling; this card uses hiragana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぐつぐつ](ぐつぐつ.md) concerns bubbling liquid, and [ことこと](ことこと.md) a gentle simmer or light clinking. [ぱりぱり](ぱりぱり.md) describes a crisp texture or crackling break, not the same stage of cooking.

## Distinctive meaning

Use じゅうじゅう焼く, じゅうじゅうと音を立てる, or じゅうじゅういっている. It describes the sound, not whether the food is cooked through. The unrelated word 重々（じゅうじゅう）means “fully” or “repeatedly,” as in 重々承知（しょうち）; that is not the sizzling mimetic and is not a kanji spelling for this sense.
