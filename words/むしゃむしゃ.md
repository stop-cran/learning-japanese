# むしゃむしゃ — munching heartily

## Meaning

Eating with repeated, vigorous chewing: munching or gobbling rather than taking delicate little bites.

- 馬が草をむしゃむしゃ食べている。 — The horse is munching grass.
- おなかがすいて、おにぎりをむしゃむしゃ食べた。 — I was hungry and gobbled up the rice balls.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もぐもぐ](もぐもぐ.md) focuses on mouth movement; [もりもり](もりもり.md) often suggests a healthy appetite. [ぱくぱく](ぱくぱく.md) can fit the same hearty eating scene.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn むしゃむしゃ食べる. It does not specify that the food is crunchy: grass, bread, or rice may all be involved. A shaggy/unkempt sense is marked archaic in the inspected dictionary and is not the everyday focus here.
