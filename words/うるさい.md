# うるさい

## Meaning

うるさい means noisy or annoyingly loud. It also describes something bothersome, persistent, or fussy, even when volume is not the issue.

- 外がうるさくて、眠れません。 (そとが うるさくて、ねむれません。) — It is noisy outside, so I cannot sleep.
- 父は食べ物にうるさいです。 (ちちは たべものに うるさいです。) — My father is particular about food.

It is an i-adjective: うるさい音 (うるさいおと), "an annoying noise"; negative うるさくない.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is normal. Dictionary spellings include 煩い and 五月蝿い, but beginners need not construct the meaning from those characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

静か (しずか), "quiet," contrasts with the noise sense. 大きな音 (おおきなおと) describes a loud sound without necessarily expressing annoyance.

## Distinctive meaning

うるさい often conveys the speaker's irritation, not an objective sound measurement. Said directly to someone as うるさい！, it can mean "Shut up!" and sound harsh. The pattern Xにうるさい can instead mean being demanding or knowledgeable about X.
