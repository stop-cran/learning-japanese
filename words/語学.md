# 語学 (ごがく) - foreign-language study

## Meaning

語学 is a noun for studying foreign languages. It can refer to the field of learning languages or, in expressions about ability, someone's command of them.

- 留学する前に、語学の勉強を続けました。 (りゅうがくするまえに、ごがくのべんきょうをつづけました。) - I continued my language studies before studying abroad.
- 彼女は語学が得意です。 (かのじょはごがくがとくいです。) - She is good at languages.

Useful compounds include 語学学校 (ごがくがっこう), "language school," and 語学力 (ごがくりょく), "language proficiency." 語学の勉強 means the study of languages; a named language can make the subject more specific.

## How the kanji combine

[語](../kanji/語.md) means language or words, and [学](../kanji/学.md) contributes study. 語 specifies what is studied. Both use on readings: ゴ + ガク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

外国語 (がいこくご) means a foreign language, not the activity of learning it. [学問](学問.md) (がくもん) is academic learning more broadly. 言語学 (げんごがく) explicitly names linguistics, the study of language as a system.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records a "linguistics" sense for 語学, so the boundary is not absolute. In expressions such as 語学学校 and 語学が得意, however, language learning or proficiency is the useful interpretation. Someone can be skilled at speaking languages without studying linguistic theory. There is no natural single-word antonym for this field of study.
