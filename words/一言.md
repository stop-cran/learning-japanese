# 一言 (ひとこと) - a word; brief comment

## Meaning

一言 is a word, a few words, or a brief comment.
It is a noun used with speaking verbs, as in 一言言う (ひとこという), "say a word,"
and 一言お願いします (ひとことおねがいします), "Please say a few words."

- 最後に一言お願いします。
  (さいごにひとことおねがいします。) - Please say a few words in closing.
- 彼は一言も話しませんでした。
  (かれはひとこともはなしませんでした。) - He did not say a single word.

The first requests a brief contribution, not exactly one dictionary word.
The second uses 一言も with a negative predicate to emphasize complete silence.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," combines with [言](../kanji/言.md), "word; speech."
The native readings are ひと + こと.
The count-like image can refer to one short utterance rather than a strict count of lexical items.
ひと言 is another spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一語 (いちご) explicitly counts one word as a linguistic unit.
A long speech contrasts with a brief comment, but there is no single antonym covering all
uses of 一言. [話す](話す.md) (はなす) supplies the speaking action in the negative example.

## Distinctive meaning

In 一言お願いします, brevity is the practical request.
A response such as 今日はありがとうございました (きょうはありがとうございました),
"Thank you for today," can fulfill it even though it contains several words.
Keep that conversational unit separate from literal vocabulary counting.
