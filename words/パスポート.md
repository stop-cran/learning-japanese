# パスポート (パスポート) - passport

## Meaning

パスポート is a passport. It is a noun used with verbs such as 見せる (みせる), "show," and 忘れる (わすれる), "forget." A border or travel-related request may ask you to present it.

- パスポートを見せてください。 (パスポートをみせてください。) - Please show your passport.
- パスポートを家に忘れてしまいました。 (パスポートをいえにわすれてしまいました。) - I unfortunately left my passport at home.

を marks the document shown or forgotten. 家に identifies where it was left in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

There are no kanji in this katakana word. The long-vowel mark ー extends ポ, giving パスポート. Learn the complete spelling; it is not a kanji compound that can be interpreted character by character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

旅券 (りょけん) is another word for a passport, useful to recognize in administrative contexts. A ビザ is a visa, not a different spelling of passport: the two name different travel-related documents or authorizations.

## Distinctive meaning

パスポート identifies the document, not a ticket or the whole process of obtaining entry permission. パスポートの番号 (パスポートのばんごう) means "passport number." A passport's existence does not itself tell you whether a traveler needs any other document; that depends on the particular journey, not the vocabulary word.
