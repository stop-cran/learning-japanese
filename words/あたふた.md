# あたふた — hurrying about in a panic

## Meaning

あたふた describes rushing around in a flustered or panicked way.

- 出発の時間が近づき、彼はあたふたと荷造りした。
  — As departure time approached, he hurriedly packed in a panic.
- 突然の来客に、母はあたふたしていた。
  — My mother was flustered by the unexpected visitor.

## Synonyms and antonyms

あたふた conveys disorganized urgency, unlike calm, orderly preparation.

## Distinctive meaning

It often describes a person's hurried reaction to an unexpected situation.
