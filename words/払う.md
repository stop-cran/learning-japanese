# 払う (はらう) - pay

## Meaning

払う means to pay money or a bill. It is transitive: the amount, charge, or payment takes を.

- レジでお金を払います。 (レジでおかねをはらいます。) - I pay at the checkout.
- 家賃はもう払いました。 (やちんはもうはらいました。) - I have already paid the rent.

The second example makes the rent the topic with は. This is a godan verb: 払います (はらいます), 払わない (はらわない), 払って (はらって), 払った (はらった).

## How the kanji combine

[払](../kanji/払.md) writes はら, followed by う. For the negative, the final う changes to わ before ない; it does not become あ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

支払う (しはらう) also means pay, especially a charge or amount due. 買う (かう), "buy," describes a purchase; paying rent or a fee is not necessarily buying an object.

## Distinctive meaning

払う has other senses, such as brushing something off. The object distinguishes them: paying お金 is not the same action as brushing away dust. This card's main use is financial payment.
