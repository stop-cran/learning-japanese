# 気に入る (きにいる) - take a liking to

## Meaning

気に入る means to like something, take a liking to it, or find it pleasing.
The verb ending is godan: **気に入った** (きにいった), **気に入らない** (きにいらない).
With the person as the topic, the thing liked can take **が**.

- このかばんが気に入りました。 (このかばんがきにいりました。) - I took a liking to this bag.
- この店を気に入っています。 (このみせをきにいっています。) - I really like this shop.

The second example uses を with 気に入っている, a useful pattern for an established preference.
気に入った店 (きにいったみせ) is "a shop one likes."

## How the kanji combine

[気](../kanji/気.md) refers here to one's feelings or taste. The particle に connects it with
[入](../kanji/入.md) in 入る (いる), "enter." Learn the whole expression きにいる;
the verb is not pronounced はいる in this phrase.
This is a fixed phrase with a native verb, not a two-kanji on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[好き](好き.md) (すき) expresses liking with adjective-like grammar: この店が好きです.
[好み](好み.md) (このみ) is a noun for taste or preference.
気に入らない, "not like/be displeased with," negates this expression directly.

## Distinctive meaning

気に入る suits an approving reaction to something encountered or chosen, and 気に入っている
describes the continuing favorable feeling. It need not describe a new preference every time.
English "suit" here means please someone's taste, not necessarily fit their body or match their clothes.
For looking good on someone, compare [似合う](似合う.md) (にあう).
