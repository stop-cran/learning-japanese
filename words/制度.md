# 制度 (せいど) — institutional system

## Meaning

制度 is an established system or institutional arrangement: rules and procedures through which an activity is organized.
It can concern how a school, company, or society handles a particular matter.
It is a noun; use 新しい制度 (あたらしいせいど), "a new system," or 制度を変える (せいどをかえる), "change the system."

- 新しい制度が来年から始まります。 (あたらしいせいどがらいねんからはじまります。) — The new system will begin next year.
- この制度を利用できますか。 (このせいどをりようできますか。) — Can I make use of this system?

## How the kanji combine

[制](../kanji/制.md) contributes regulation or a system; [度](../kanji/度.md) contributes a standard or norm here.
Together they point to an established institutional arrangement, not a numbered degree or count of occurrences.
The on readings are セイ + ド.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[規則](規則.md) きそく is a rule or regulation: a 制度 may involve multiple 規則.
仕組み しくみ concerns a structure, mechanism, or how something works, so it can describe an arrangement from another perspective.
体制 たいせい refers to a system or organizational structure and can also mean a governing regime.

## Distinctive meaning

English "system" is broader than 制度. An explanation of how a machine works is not automatically about an institution.
Use 制度 when the point is the established arrangement people operate under or make use of.
The word itself does not say whether that arrangement is effective, fair, or legally required.
