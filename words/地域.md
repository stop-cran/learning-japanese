# 地域 (ちいき) - area; region

## Meaning

地域 is an area, region, or locality.
It can identify a broad geographical area or a local community, depending on context.
Use 地域に for something located there, 地域で for an activity, and 地域の for noun modification.

- この地域では冬に雪がたくさん降ります。 (このちいきではふゆにゆきがたくさんふります。) - A lot of snow falls in this region in winter.
- 地域の人たちと公園を掃除しました。 (ちいきのひとたちとこうえんをそうじしました。) - I cleaned the park with people from the local community.

地域によって違う (ちいきによってちがう) means "differ by region."

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) contributes land or place, and 域 a bounded area or range.
Together they suggest a geographical area considered as a unit.
The on-readings チ + イキ give ちいき; retain both adjacent い sounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[地方](地方.md) (ちほう) also means region and can contrast areas outside a central city with the center.
[場所](場所.md) (ばしょ) is a place more generally, including a small specific spot.
[近所](近所.md) (きんじょ) is the nearby neighborhood, usually a narrower contextual comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

地域 does not prescribe a fixed size or administrative boundary.
A school district, climate zone, and surrounding community can each be described as a 地域 when the context defines it.
Translate 地域の as "local" only when that local-community sense fits, not automatically in every geographical description.
