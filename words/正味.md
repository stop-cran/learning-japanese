# 正味 (しょうみ) - net weight; net amount

## Meaning

正味 can mean net weight: the weight of the contents without their packaging. It also identifies the actual or effective amount after excluding something irrelevant, such as breaks from working time.

- 箱を除くと、正味は二キロです。 (はこをのぞくと、しょうみはにキロです。) - Excluding the box, the net weight is two kilograms.
- 休憩を除いて、正味六時間働きました。 (きゅうけいをのぞいて、しょうみろくじかんはたらきました。) - Excluding breaks, I worked six full hours.

[除く](除く.md) (のぞく) means "exclude." The second sentence measures effective working time, not the total time between arrival and departure.

## How the kanji combine

[正](../kanji/正.md) contributes true or proper, and [味](../kanji/味.md) has a content or substance association beyond literal flavor. The established combination concerns the actual content or amount. Read ショウ + ミ, not せいみ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[重量](重量.md) (じゅうりょう) is weight generally. 総重量 (そうじゅうりょう), "gross weight," can include packaging and contrasts with net weight in that setting. The dictionary also records net/cost price, overlapping [価格](価格.md) (かかく) or [値段](値段.md) (ねだん) in a price context, and actual [内容](内容.md) (ないよう), "contents."

## Distinctive meaning

The excluded part depends on what is measured: packaging for weight, or breaks for effective working time. Do not memorize a rule that 正味 always means a deduction of tax or always means the sale price. The source row explicitly anchors net weight; the other recorded uses are additional recognition.
