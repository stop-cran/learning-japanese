# 費用 (ひよう) — cost; expense

## Meaning

費用 is the cost of doing something: the money an activity, service, or project requires.
Use a noun plus の to name the activity, as in 旅行の費用 (りょこうのひよう), "travel expenses."

- 旅行の費用は全部で五万円でした。 (りょこうのひようはぜんぶでごまんえんでした。) — The total cost of the trip was fifty thousand yen.
- 費用は会社が負担します。 (ひようはかいしゃがふたんします。) — The company will cover the cost.

## How the kanji combine

[費](../kanji/費.md) concerns spending, and [用](../kanji/用.md) concerns use.
Together they name the expense involved in carrying something out, rather than an object bought.
The on readings are ヒ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[料金](料金.md) (りょうきん) is a fee, charge, or fare. A service's 料金 can be one part of the total 費用 of an activity.
For a trip, the overall 費用 may include more than one charge.
These are overlapping viewpoints, not interchangeable labels in every phrase.

## Distinctive meaning

費用 can be necessary and worthwhile; the word does not mean "waste."
When asking how much an activity will require, 費用 keeps attention on its expense.
It is a noun, not a verb meaning "to spend."
