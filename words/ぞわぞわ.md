# ぞわぞわ — shivery and skin-prickling

## Meaning

ぞわぞわ describes an uneasy shiver or prickly feeling, often when something chills or
unsettles a person.

- 冷たい風が首筋を通り、背中がぞわぞわした。
  — A cold breeze passed over the back of my neck, and a shiver ran down my back.
- 暗い廊下の物音を聞いて、腕がぞわぞわした。
  — Hearing a sound in the dark hallway made my skin crawl.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぞくぞく](ぞくぞく.md) can also describe shivering, including from cold or excitement. The
words overlap; ぞわぞわ often emphasizes a skin-prickling or unsettling sensation.

## Distinctive meaning

The word describes a physical or uneasy sensation; it does not identify its cause. A cold
breeze, a frightening sound and an emotional reaction are different contexts, not interchangeable
explanations.
