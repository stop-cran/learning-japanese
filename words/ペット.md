# ペット — pet

## Meaning

ペット means a pet: an animal kept as a companion. It names the animal's relationship to people, not a species.

- ペットを飼っていますか。 (ぺっとをかっていますか。) — Do you keep a pet?
- このアパートではペットを飼えません。 (このあぱーとではぺっとをかえません。) — Pets cannot be kept in this apartment building.

飼う (かう) is the verb for keeping an animal. Its potential negative 飼えない (かえない) means "cannot keep."

## How the kanji combine

ペット is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "pet." The small ッ matters: it marks the closure before ト. There is no kanji compound to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

動物 (どうぶつ) means an animal more generally. 犬 (いぬ) and 猫 (ねこ) identify species; either may be a pet, but not every dog or cat belongs to someone.

## Distinctive meaning

English also uses "pet" as a verb meaning to stroke an animal. Japanese ペット is a noun here; use なでる for stroking rather than forming ペットする with that English meaning.
