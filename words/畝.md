# 畝 (うね) — raised planting ridge; rib

## Meaning

畝 is a **raised row of earth for planting crops**. It is the planting strip, not the lower groove or furrow between strips.

- 畑に畝を作りました。（はたけにうねをつくりました。）— I made raised planting rows in the field.
- この生地には畝があります。（このきじにはうねがあります。）— This fabric has ribs.

The second example uses the related shape sense: parallel raised ribs or ridges, as in ribbed fabric. The dictionary also applies the word to ridged forms in terrain or the sea.

## How the kanji combine

[畝](../kanji/畝.md) writes the whole native noun うね. It is not a compound whose visible parts should be pronounced separately.

畦 is another dictionary spelling of うね. That does not make every word written 畦 read うね: the separate あぜ family concerns a paddy boundary.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[畔](畔.md) (あぜ) is the earth boundary between rice fields. 畝 うね identifies the planting row; 畔 あぜ identifies the boundary ridge. The difference is practical function, not simply that one is raised and the other is flat.

畝間 (うねま) is a furrow between planting ridges. [畑](畑.md) (はたけ) is the field containing the rows, not one row itself.

## Distinctive meaning

The same written character also has the separate reading せ for a traditional Japanese land-area unit and ほ for a Chinese land measure. Those are not the reading or meaning of 畝 in the two examples here. This article's primary reading stays うね.

English "furrow" sometimes appears in broad character glosses, but for the planting distinction here keep track of the raised row and the intervening groove.
