# 折れる (おれる) — snap; become folded

## Meaning

折れる describes something breaking by snapping or something becoming folded. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: the affected thing takes が. Forms include 折れます, 折れて, and 折れない.

- 強い風で枝が折れました。 (つよいかぜでえだがおれました。) — A branch snapped in the strong wind.
- 本のページの角が折れています。 (ほんのぺーじのかどがおれています。) — The corner of a page in the book is folded over.

## How the kanji combine

[折](../kanji/折.md) carries the bending or breaking idea; れる completes おれる. Despite the English translation "be broken," this is a dictionary-form intransitive verb, not the passive conjugation of 折る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

折る (おる) is transitive: 紙を折る (かみをおる), "fold paper." 壊れる (こわれる) means to break or stop working more generally, without necessarily snapping.

## Distinctive meaning

The object and context tell you whether there is a fracture or only a fold. The verb also has figurative uses such as yielding in an argument, but physical snapping and folding are the basic contrasts here.
