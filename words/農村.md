# 農村 (のうそん) - farming village; rural community

## Meaning

農村 is a farming village or rural community centered on agriculture.
It is a noun; before another noun it can characterize something as rural,
as in 農村生活 (のうそんせいかつ), "rural life."

- 私は小さな農村で育ちました。
  (わたしはちいさなのうそんでそだちました。) - I grew up in a small farming village.
- 農村の生活について学んでいます。
  (のうそんのせいかつについてまなんでいます。) - I am learning about life in rural communities.

で marks the place of growing up in the first example.
The second uses の to connect the community to its life or way of living.

## How the kanji combine

[農](../kanji/農.md) contributes farming and
[村](../kanji/村.md) a village: farming qualifies the kind of community.
The readings are ノウ + ソン.
村 is not read むら inside this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[村](村.md) (むら) is broader "village."
[田舎](田舎.md) (いなか) can mean the countryside or one's hometown;
it need not identify farming as the main livelihood.
[地方](地方.md) (ちほう) can refer to a region or provincial areas.
[都市](都市.md) (とし), "city," supplies a useful urban/rural contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

農村 is not a claim that every resident is a farmer, that the place lacks
modern services or that it has a particular administrative designation.
Conversely, not every place officially called 村 is necessarily a farming community.
Keep the agricultural focus without turning it into a stereotype about rural life.
