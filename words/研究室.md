# 研究室 (けんきゅうしつ) - research laboratory; academic office

## Meaning

研究室 is a research laboratory, seminar room, or a professor's office, depending on the institution and context.

- 先生は研究室にいます。 (せんせいはけんきゅうしつにいます。) - The professor is in their office.
- 研究室で実験をしています。 (けんきゅうしつでじっけんをしています。) - We are conducting an experiment in the laboratory.

に marks someone's location; で marks the setting of the experiment.

## How the kanji combine

[研](../kanji/研.md) and [究](../kanji/究.md) form 研究 (けんきゅう), "research." [室](../kanji/室.md), "room," adds しつ: a room associated with research.

## Synonyms and antonyms

実験室 (じっけんしつ) specifically highlights experiments. 教室 (きょうしつ) is a classroom. 自習室 (じしゅうしつ) is a room for independent study.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "study room" should be understood in an academic or research setting. Do not assume that every quiet room where a learner does homework is a 研究室, or that every 研究室 contains scientific equipment.
