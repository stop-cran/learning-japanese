# 願い (ねがい) - wish; request

## Meaning

願い is a wish, hope, or request. It is a noun, related to the verb 願う (ねがう), "wish; ask." **願いがかなう** means that a wish comes true; **願いをかなえる** means to fulfill a wish.

- 留学したいという願いがかないました。 (りゅうがくしたいというねがいがかないました。) - My wish to study abroad came true.
- 一つお願いがあります。 (ひとつおねがいがあります。) - I have a request.

The first example states the content of the wish with という. The second uses the polite form お願い, not a command to someone. 願い can also be a prayer; in that context the recipient or setting clarifies its religious character.

## How the kanji combine

[願](../kanji/願.md) contributes wishing or asking. The native stem ねが- combines with い to form the noun. Compare 願う and its stem 願い rather than inventing separate meanings for the final kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[希望](希望.md) (きぼう) overlaps in hope or desire. [依頼](依頼.md) (いらい) emphasizes requesting a task or service. [夢](夢.md) (ゆめ) can be a dream or aspiration.

## Distinctive meaning

A 願い need not have been spoken to another person. Context distinguishes an inward wish from an actual request. In written application names, 願 can also appear as a suffix: 休暇願 (きゅうかねがい), "leave application." A heartfelt wish is not automatically an administrative application.
