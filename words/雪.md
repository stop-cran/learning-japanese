# 雪 (ゆき) — snow; snowfall

## Meaning

雪 (ゆき) is snow: the material falling from the sky or lying on the ground.
It is a noun, not a verb meaning "to snow." For falling snow, use 雪が降る (ゆきがふる).

- 雪が降っています。 (ゆきがふっています。) — It is snowing.
- 道に雪があります。 (みちにゆきがあります。) — There is snow on the road.

The first sentence describes snowfall; the second only says snow is present.
雪の日 (ゆきのひ) means "a snowy day."

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [雪](../kanji/雪.md) writes the native noun ゆき.
There is no compound to split into smaller word meanings.
In the compound 積雪 (せきせつ), "snow cover," the same character instead has the on reading セツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

大雪 (おおゆき) specifies heavy snow or heavy snowfall, not a different substance.
積雪 focuses on fallen snow or snow cover.
[雨](雨.md) (あめ) is rain: a useful weather contrast, but not an antonym of snow.

## Distinctive meaning

雪 alone does not tell you the depth, whether it is currently falling, or whether a road is safe to use.
Choose the rest of the sentence to convey that information.
In 雪が降る, が marks what is falling; in 雪を見る (ゆきをみる), "see snow," を marks what is seen.
