# ナンバー - number; license plate

## Meaning

ナンバー is a noun borrowed from English "number." A useful application is
an identifying number. It can also abbreviate ナンバープレート,
"number plate; license plate."

- 部屋のナンバーを教えてください。 (へやのナンバーをおしえてください。) - Please tell me the room number.
- 車のナンバーを確認しました。 (くるまのナンバーをかくにんしました。) - I checked the car's license plate.

In the second sentence, ナンバー may focus on the registration number visible
on the plate rather than the physical plate itself.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword with no kanji components.
The ー lengthens バ: ナンバー, not ナンバ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[番号](番号.md) (ばんごう) is useful for an identifying number.
[数字](数字.md) (すうじ) concerns numerals or numerical figures, while
[数](数.md) (かず) can name a number or amount.
[番](番.md) (ばん) also appears in numbering and order.
Their ranges overlap, but they are not interchangeable in every compound.

## Distinctive meaning

A car's ナンバー is not the number of cars. Likewise, English "number"
does not authorize substituting ナンバー for every Japanese counting
expression. Learn the object being numbered and the established expression
together.
