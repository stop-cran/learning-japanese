# 俄 (にわか) - sudden; improvised

## Meaning

にわか describes something sudden or abrupt. It also describes something
improvised or put together for the occasion.
It is usually written in kana; 俄 is the dictionary spelling represented here.
Use にわかな before a noun and にわかに before a predicate.

- 空がにわかに暗くなりました。
  (そらがにわかにくらくなりました。) - The sky suddenly grew dark.
- にわかな思いつきで旅行に出かけました。
  (にわかなおもいつきでりょこうにでかけました。) - I went on a trip on a sudden whim.

The first に makes the expression adverbial.
The second example has な joining にわか to the noun 思いつき.
にわか仕込み (にわかじこみ) describes hurried preparation or knowledge
acquired for the immediate occasion.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji spelling of the whole native word にわか.
It is not read with the character's on reading ガ in these uses.
The kana form is not a different word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[突然](突然.md) (とつぜん) and [急に](急に.md) (きゅうに)
overlap in sudden occurrence.
[ふと](ふと.md) often describes an unplanned thought or small action;
not every にわかに event has that incidental quality.
準備した (じゅんびした), "prepared," can contrast with improvised preparation
in a suitable sentence, but is not an antonym of every sudden event.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional slang use is にわかファン, a bandwagon or newly enthusiastic fan.
This labels the kind of involvement being discussed; it does not prove that
every recent fan is insincere.
Do not confuse sudden onset with the short duration [一瞬](一瞬.md) (いっしゅん), "an instant,"
or the grouping [一度に](一度に.md) (いちどに), "all at once."
