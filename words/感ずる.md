# 感ずる (かんずる) - feel; be moved

## Meaning

感ずる means to feel, sense, or experience something, and can also mean
being moved or impressed. It is closely related to
[感じる](感じる.md) (かんじる).

- 庭の花を見て、春の訪れを感ずる。
  (にわのはなをみて、はるのおとずれをかんずる。) - Looking at the flowers in the garden, I sense the arrival of spring.
- この説明には矛盾があると感ずる。
  (このせつめいにはむじゅんがあるとかんずる。) - I feel that there is a contradiction in this explanation.

The first example uses を for what is sensed.
In the second, clause + と gives the judgment or impression.
These are original examples illustrating the dictionary form.

## How the kanji combine

[感](../kanji/感.md) supplies the feeling association and the reading カン.
ずる is the written verb ending, not another kanji.
This is a Sino-Japanese base with a Japanese ending, not an ordinary
godan verb simply because its dictionary form ends in る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

感じる has its own dictionary entry and overlaps in feeling and sensing.
[思う](思う.md) (おもう) can overlap when reporting what one feels or thinks.
[感動](感動.md) (かんどう) highlights being moved rather than every
physical sensation.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict calls 感ずる a ずる alternative of a じる verb.
Japanese monolingual dictionaries classify 感ずる as サ変, an irregular
conjugation, and explain 感じる as its developed ichidan form.
Those frameworks describe the relationship differently; neither licenses
the invented godan form 感ずります.
Use the [感じる article](感じる.md) for the practical 感じ- forms.

The dictionary also records a dated illness-catching sense.
That is not a reason to use 感ずる as an unrestricted modern substitute
for "catch a cold."
