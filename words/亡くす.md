# 亡くす (なくす) - lose someone through death

## Meaning

亡くす means losing someone through death. The person who dies takes を;
the subject is the bereaved person. It is a transitive godan verb.

- 彼女は去年、父を亡くしました。 (かのじょはきょねん、ちちをなくしました。) - She lost her father last year.
- 友人は幼いころに母を亡くしました。 (ゆうじんはおさないころにははをなくしました。) - My friend lost their mother in childhood.

Forms include 亡くします, 亡くさない, 亡くして and 亡くした.
Transitivity does not mean that the bereaved person caused the death.

## How the kanji combine

[亡](../kanji/亡.md) marks the death-related meaning in this spelling;
くす completes the native word. Learn the full reading なくす rather than
substituting an on reading of 亡.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[失う](失う.md) (うしなう) also covers bereavement, but has broader loss uses.
[亡くなる](亡くなる.md) (なくなる), "pass away," presents the deceased
person as the subject: 父が亡くなった (ちちがなくなった).
That is a viewpoint contrast, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

[無くす](無くす.md), also なくす, concerns losing something or eliminating
it. Losing a wallet uses that identity, not 亡くす.
The spelling taught here does not describe an ordinary farewell, a missing
person whose fate is unknown, or the end of a romance. Context can make
別れる (わかれる) a bereavement expression, but separation alone does not
establish death.
