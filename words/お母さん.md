# お母さん (おかあさん) — mother, mom

## Meaning

お母さん is how one refers to or addresses someone's mother, and how children address their own mother: お母さん、ただいま (Mom, I'm home), 友達のお母さん (a friend's mother).

## How the kanji combine

お (honorific prefix) + [母](../kanji/母.md) + さん (respectful suffix). The 母 is read かあ here, which is not a standard on or kun reading (ボ, はは) but part of the word かあさん.
It is a native word with a special reading of the kanji. Short form: 母さん かあさん; more respectful: お母様 おかあさま.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Own mother, to outsiders: 母 はは; casual: ママ; older male-speech: おふくろ.
- Antonym: [お父さん](お父さん.md) おとうさん (father).

## Distinctive meaning

Use 母 はは for your own mother when speaking to people outside the family, and お母さん for someone else's mother or when talking to your mother directly.
