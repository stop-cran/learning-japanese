# シャツ — shirt

## Meaning

シャツ means a shirt. Depending on context it may be an outer shirt, often with a collar and buttons, or an undershirt worn beneath other clothes.

白いシャツを着ています。 (しろいシャツをきています。) — I am wearing a white shirt.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is normally written in katakana. シャ represents the combined sound しゃ, with a small ャ. The final ツ is full-sized; シャツ does not contain a long-vowel mark or a doubled consonant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ワイシャツ specifies a dress shirt. Tシャツ (ティーシャツ) is a T-shirt. 肌着 (はだぎ) is underwear or clothing worn next to the skin and can clarify the undergarment sense.

## Distinctive meaning

シャツ is not a general word for every upper-body garment: a coat or sweater has its own name. Use [着る](着る.md) (きる) for putting on a shirt and 着ている for wearing it. When the distinction matters, specify the kind of shirt rather than relying on シャツ alone.
