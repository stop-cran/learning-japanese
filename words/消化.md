# 消化 (しょうか) - digestion; assimilation; working through

## Meaning

消化 means digestion: breaking down food so the body can use it. It is also used for mentally assimilating information and for working through or consuming an allotted amount, schedule, or workload.

- 食べ物を消化するには時間がかかります。 (たべものをしょうかするにはじかんがかかります。) - It takes time to digest food.
- 説明の内容をまだ十分に消化できていません。 (せつめいのないようをまだじゅうぶんにしょうかできていません。) - I have not yet fully digested the explanation.
- 予定していた試合をすべて消化しました。 (よていしていたしあいをすべてしょうかしました。) - We completed all the scheduled matches.

消化する is transitive in these examples: the thing digested, assimilated, or worked through takes を.

## How the kanji combine

[消](../kanji/消.md) has disappearing or using-up associations, and [化](../kanji/化.md) change or transformation associations. They provide a learning association with processing something, not a scientific explanation of digestion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[吸収](吸収.md) concerns absorption and also has mental-assimilation uses; digestion and bodily absorption are not the same physiological step. [理解](理解.md) overlaps with understanding information, and [消費](消費.md) with consuming or using something up. [処理](処理.md), [済ませる](済ませる.md), and [終える](終える.md) overlap with working through or finishing a task, not with every biological use.

## Distinctive meaning

The food meaning is the source-selected teaching anchor; the information and workload uses are additional. 消化不良 (しょうかふりょう) means indigestion and can also suggest insufficient assimilation of ideas. Do not confuse the word with the homophone 消火 (しょうか), extinguishing a fire. JMdict's separate transformation sense is marked archaic, not presented here as an unrestricted modern substitute for 化学的な変化, "chemical change."
