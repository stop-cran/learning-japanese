# 議会 (ぎかい) - parliament; legislative assembly

## Meaning

議会 is a legislative assembly: a body such as a parliament, congress, or local
council that debates and decides public matters. The appropriate English name
depends on the institution. Use 議会で, "in the assembly," or 議会の議員
(ぎかいのぎいん), "a member of the assembly."

- 議会で新しい法律について話し合いました。 (ぎかいであたらしいほうりつについてはなしあいました。) - They discussed a new law in parliament.
- 市の議会が予算を承認しました。 (しのぎかいがよさんをしょうにんしました。) - The city council approved the budget.

The word can therefore refer to a local body, not just a national legislature.

## How the kanji combine

[議](../kanji/議.md) contributes deliberation or discussion, and
[会](../kanji/会.md) contributes an assembly. The first specifies what kind
of assembly is meant. The on readings combine as ぎ + かい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[国会](国会.md) (こっかい) names a national legislature, including Japan's
National Diet. [会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) is a meeting or conference:
reversing the kanji changes the word. [政府](政府.md) (せいふ), "government,"
is related, but does not simply name the same institution.

## Distinctive meaning

議会 identifies an institutional assembly, not every gathering where people
discuss something. A company can hold a 会議 without holding a parliament.
Conversely, 議会 can refer to the body beyond one particular meeting.
There is no useful general antonym; distinguish institutions by their roles.
