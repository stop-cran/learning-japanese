# 傘 (かさ)

## Meaning

傘 means an umbrella or parasol. It is an ordinary noun for the object, whether open or folded.

- 雨が降っているので、傘を持っていきます。 (あめが ふっているので、かさを もっていきます。) — It is raining, so I will take an umbrella.

傘を差す (かさをさす) means to put up or use an umbrella; 傘を畳む (かさをたたむ) means to fold one.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single kanji read with native かさ, not a compound. Its umbrella-like shape is a useful visual memory aid.

## Synonyms and antonyms

日傘 (ひがさ) specifies a parasol used against the sun. 折り畳み傘 (おりたたみがさ) is a folding umbrella. A raincoat is an alternative form of protection, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

傘 names the protective object, not the rain or the act of opening it. In everyday rainy-weather contexts, English "umbrella" is the natural translation; the wider dictionary meaning also includes sun umbrellas.
