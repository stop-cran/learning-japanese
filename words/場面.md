# 場面 (ばめん) - scene; situation

## Meaning

場面 is a noun for a scene, setting, or situation in which something occurs. It can describe part of a film or story, or a real-life situation. Use 場面で for what happens in that setting.

- 映画の最後の場面で泣きました。 (えいがのさいごのばめんでなきました。) - I cried during the film's final scene.
- こういう場面では、落ち着いて話すことが大切です。 (こういうばめんでは、おちついてはなすことがたいせつです。) - In this kind of situation, it is important to speak calmly.

The first example identifies a part of a film. The second identifies circumstances, not a filming location.

## How the kanji combine

[場](../kanji/場.md) contributes a place or setting, and [面](../kanji/面.md) contributes a face or aspect. Together they name a scene or aspect of unfolding events. The reading mixes native ば with on めん; this native-first mixed word is grouped under the app's `wago` category, as [場所](場所.md) is.

## Synonyms and antonyms

場所 (ばしょ) focuses on a location. [状況](状況.md) (じょうきょう) focuses on circumstances or the state of affairs. 場面 can combine a setting with the action or moment taking place there.

## Distinctive meaning

A single location can host several different 場面, and a conversation may move to a new 場面 without anyone physically changing rooms. The word is therefore not just another way to ask for a street address. In stories, it helps identify a particular episode or scene.
