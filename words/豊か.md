# 豊か (ゆたか) - abundant; prosperous

## Meaning

豊か is a na-adjective for abundance or richness. It can describe plentiful resources, a rich experience, or financial prosperity. Use 豊かな before a noun, 豊かだ or 豊かです as a predicate, and 豊かに as an adverb.

- この地域は自然が豊かです。 (このちいきはしぜんがゆたかです。) - This region is rich in natural surroundings.
- 経済的に豊かな生活を送っています。 (けいざいてきにゆたかなせいかつをおくっています。) - They lead a financially comfortable life.

経験豊かな先生 (けいけんゆたかなせんせい) means "a teacher with a wealth of experience."

## How the kanji combine

豊 contributes abundance or richness and is read ゆた here. The kana か completes the native adjective. It is not a separate suffix to attach freely to other kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[豊富](豊富.md) (ほうふ) also means abundant or plentiful, particularly in describing available quantities. [貧しい](貧しい.md) (まずしい), "poor; meager," contrasts with 豊か in financial or resource descriptions.

## Distinctive meaning

豊か is not restricted to having money. A 豊かな表現 (ゆたかなひょうげん) is rich expression, and 経験豊か concerns experience. Name the dimension of richness when it could be ambiguous. The word also does not guarantee that a materially prosperous person is happy; wealth and personal satisfaction are separate claims.
