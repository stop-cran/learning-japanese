# 繭 (まゆ) — cocoon

## Meaning

繭 is a **cocoon**, a protective covering made by an insect larva. Silkworm cocoons are a familiar example and a source of silk thread; the word is not restricted to silkworms.

- 蚕が繭を作ります。 (かいこがまゆをつくります。) — Silkworms make cocoons.
- 繭から糸を取ります。 (まゆからいとをとります。) — We obtain thread from cocoons.

In the first sentence, を marks the cocoon being made. In the second, から identifies the source of the thread. **蚕** かいこ is the silkworm, not its cocoon.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [繭](../kanji/繭.md) writes the native noun まゆ. There is no compound to split or okurigana to add. Its enclosed thread-and-insect shapes are a useful visual mnemonic, not a historical derivation supplied by this article.

## Synonyms and antonyms

**さなぎ** means pupa: the insect at a developmental stage, not the covering around it. A cocoon and a pupa are not synonyms. [巣](巣.md) す names a nest, hive or web according to the animal; learn that dwelling word separately rather than substituting it for 繭. There is no useful direct antonym to memorize.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the **covering**, **insect** and **material** separate. [糸](糸.md) いと means thread or yarn; [絹](絹.md) きぬ means silk as a material. 繭 まゆ names the cocoon, not silk fabric.

**眉** まゆ, eyebrow, is a homophone, not another meaning of this spelling. Context distinguishes the words when heard; the written characters distinguish them when read.
