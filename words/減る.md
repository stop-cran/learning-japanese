# 減る (へる) - decrease; become fewer

## Meaning

減る means that an amount or number decreases. It is an intransitive godan verb: the thing that becomes less takes が.

- この町の人口が減っています。 (このまちのじんこうがへっています。) - This town's population is decreasing.
- 参加者が二人減りました。 (さんかしゃがふたりへりました。) - The number of participants decreased by two.

The second example gives the amount of decrease. Compare 参加者が二人に減りました (さんかしゃがふたりにへりました), "The number of participants fell to two": に marks the resulting number.

The past is 減った (へった), not へた; the negative is 減らない (へらない).

## How the kanji combine

減 writes the native stem へ, followed by the ending る. It is one verb, not a compound. Despite ending in える sounds, it follows godan patterns such as 減ります (へります) and 減って (へって).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[増える](増える.md) (ふえる), "increase," is the direct opposite. [減らす](減らす.md) (へらす) is transitive: 支出を減らす (ししゅつをへらす), "reduce expenses." 減る instead describes the decrease: 支出が減る.

## Distinctive meaning

減る does not identify who caused the change. The sentence may describe a deliberate reduction or something that happened without planning. Preserve both contrasts when learning it: が versus を for the verb pair, and a bare amount versus amount + に for "by" versus "to."
