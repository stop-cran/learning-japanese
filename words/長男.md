# 長男 (ちょうなん) - eldest son

## Meaning

長男 is the eldest son in a family. He may be the only son.
It identifies birth order among sons, not among all children.

- 長男は東京で働いています。
  (ちょうなんはとうきょうではたらいています。) - My eldest son works in Tokyo.
- 彼は長男ですが、姉がいます。
  (かれはちょうなんですが、あねがいます。) - He is the eldest son, but he has an older sister.

## How the kanji combine

[長](../kanji/長.md) contributes seniority, and
[男](../kanji/男.md) identifies the male child in this family role.
Use the on readings チョウ + ナン. The whole reading is ちょうなん,
not a spelling-based guess using ダン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[息子](息子.md) (むすこ) means son without specifying birth order.
[長女](長女.md) (ちょうじょ) is the eldest daughter.
次男 (じなん) means the second son.
[兄](兄.md) (あに), older brother, describes a different relationship.

## Distinctive meaning

A 長男 can have older sisters and need not have younger siblings.
The word does not itself mean heir or prescribe a particular family duty.
Nor does it indicate that the son is still a child.
