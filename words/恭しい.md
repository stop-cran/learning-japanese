# 恭しい (うやうやしい) — reverent; respectful

## Meaning

恭しい describes conduct that shows deep respect. Digital Daijisen defines it as 相手を敬って、礼儀正しく丁寧である, “respecting the other party and being courteous and polite,” and gives 神前で恭しく頭を下げる (しんぜんでうやうやしくあたまをさげる), “bow reverently before the gods.”

- 受賞者は賞状を恭しく受け取った。 (じゅしょうしゃはしょうじょうをうやうやしくうけとった。) — The prizewinner received the certificate reverently.
- 祖父は先祖の写真の前で、恭しく手を合わせた。 (そふはせんぞのしゃしんのまえで、うやうやしくてをあわせた。) — In front of the photographs of our ancestors, my grandfather reverently put his hands together.

## How the kanji combine

[恭](../kanji/恭.md) alone writes うやうや, and しい is okurigana. 恭しく (うやうやしく) is the adverb form, used in both examples to describe how an action is done.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[丁寧](丁寧.md) (ていねい) can mean polite or careful in general. [敬う](敬う.md) (うやまう) is to respect someone, and [尊敬](尊敬.md) (そんけい) is respect or esteem; 恭しい describes the manner of an action done with that respect.
