# 自然科学 (しぜんかがく) - natural science

## Meaning

自然科学 is natural science: systematic study of the natural world.
It includes fields such as physics, chemistry, biology, earth science,
and astronomy. Observation and experiment are important methods.

- 自然科学の本を読んでいます。 (しぜんかがくのほんをよんでいます。) - I am reading a book about natural science.
- 物理や化学は自然科学の分野です。 (ぶつりやかがくはしぜんかがくのぶんやです。) - Physics and chemistry are fields of natural science.

Use 自然科学の + noun, as in 自然科学の研究 (しぜんかがくのけんきゅう),
"research in the natural sciences."
English can use singular "natural science" or plural "natural sciences."

## How the kanji combine

[自然](自然.md) (しぜん), nature, qualifies [科学](科学.md) (かがく), science.
The written components are [自](../kanji/自.md), oneself;
[然](../kanji/然.md), being so; [科](../kanji/科.md), a branch;
and [学](../kanji/学.md), study.
Learn the compound through its two established words, not as four separate
English commands. The on readings are シ + ゼン + カ + ガク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[物理](物理.md) (ぶつり), physics, and [化学](化学.md) (かがく),
chemistry, are member fields, not synonyms for the whole category.
社会科学 (しゃかいかがく), social science, and 人文科学
(じんぶんかがく), the humanities, are useful neighboring categories.

## Distinctive meaning

自然科学 is not every kind of organized study.
Mathematics supplies important methods, but the word does not automatically
classify mathematics itself as a natural science.
Likewise, enjoying nature is not necessarily doing scientific research.
