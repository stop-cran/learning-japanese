# 無沙汰 (ぶさた) - long silence; neglecting to keep in touch

## Meaning

無沙汰 means failing to write, call, visit or otherwise keep in contact for some time. The prefixed form ご無沙汰 is useful when acknowledging one's own silence.

- 長い間、ご無沙汰していました。 (ながいあいだ、ごぶさたしていました。) - I am sorry I have been out of touch for so long.
- ご無沙汰しておりますが、お元気ですか。 (ごぶさたしておりますが、おげんきですか。) - It has been a while since I contacted you; how are you?

## How the kanji combine

無 conveys absence, and 沙汰 is treated here as the unit for communication or news: an absence of contact. The reading is ブ + サ + タ. None of these written characters has a card yet; this is a modern meaning association, not a historical derivation from the individual shapes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[久しぶり](久しぶり.md) (ひさしぶり) describes something happening after a long interval. 無沙汰 instead focuses on the lack of contact during that interval. [怠る](怠る.md) (おこたる) is a broader verb for neglecting something.

## Distinctive meaning

The standard acknowledgement ご無沙汰しております is not an accusation that the listener failed to write. Nor is 無沙汰 the usual word for neglecting any arbitrary task. The dictionary also records the rare spelling 不沙汰; the source spelling taught here is 無沙汰.
