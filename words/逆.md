# 逆 (ぎゃく) - reverse; opposite

## Meaning

逆 describes a reversed direction, order, or relationship. Useful patterns are 逆だ, "it is the reverse," 逆の + noun, and 逆に, "in reverse; conversely."

- 写真の順番が逆です。 (しゃしんのじゅんばんがぎゃくです。) - The photographs are in reverse order.
- 駅とは逆の方向に歩いていました。 (えきとはぎゃくのほうこうにあるいていました。) - I was walking in the opposite direction from the station.

逆に考える (ぎゃくにかんがえる) means "think about it the other way around." What is reversed must be understood from context.

## How the kanji combine

逆 is a single-kanji word using the on reading ギャク. It conveys going against an expected direction or arrangement. There is no second character to supply another part of the meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[反対](反対.md) (はんたい) overlaps in "opposite direction" but can also mean opposition to a proposal. 逆 particularly highlights reversal. [正しい](正しい.md) (ただしい), "correct," contrasts with 逆 when a reversed arrangement is wrong, but is not its universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

逆 is relational: left and right, first and last, or cause and result may exchange positions. A reversed arrangement is not necessarily a mistake. In discussion, 逆に can introduce the contrary possibility, not literal backward movement. Look for the original direction or assumption before choosing an English translation.
