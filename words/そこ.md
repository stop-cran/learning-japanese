# そこ - there; that place

## Meaning

そこ means "there": a place near the listener or a place already mentioned in conversation. It stands for the location, rather than modifying a following noun.

そこに座ってください。 (そこにすわってください。) - Please sit there.

昨日、新しい店に行きました。そこでパンを買いました。 (きのう、あたらしいみせにいきました。そこでパンをかいました。) - Yesterday I went to a new shop. I bought bread there.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is hiragana. The dictionary labels the source spelling 其処 a rarely used kanji form, not the normal everyday model. そこ belongs to the ここ・そこ・あそこ・どこ location series.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ここ means "here," near the speaker. あそこ points farther from both speaker and listener. そちら can indicate the listener's direction or side and is useful in more polite directions.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose the particle for the action: そこで marks where something is done; そこに can mark a destination or where something exists. For "that shop," use その店 (そのみせ), not そこ店. A previously mentioned place need not be physically near the listener.
