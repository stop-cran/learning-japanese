# ぴよぴよ — little birds cheeping

## Meaning

Small, light bird cheeps, especially associated with chicks.

- ひよこが箱の中でぴよぴよ鳴（な）いている。 — A chick is cheeping in the box.
- 巣（す）からぴよぴよという声が聞こえた。 — I heard little cheeps coming from the nest.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ちゅんちゅん](ちゅんちゅん.md) also represents little bird chirps, often sparrows. The association is not an exclusive species-identification rule.

## Distinctive meaning

ぴよぴよ鳴く evokes the voice. The inspected dictionary also lists ひよひよ, but its separate “moving gently” sense is restricted to that reading; do not transfer it automatically to this card's ぴよぴよ.

See [Japanese onomatopoeia](../articles/japanese-onomatopoeia.md) and [motion and sound](../articles/onomatopoeia-motion-and-sound.md).
