# 複雑 (ふくざつ) — complicated

## Meaning

複雑 describes something complex, complicated, or intricate. It is a な-adjective: 複雑な + noun, 複雑です as a statement, and 複雑に before a verb.

この機械の使い方は複雑です。 (このきかいのつかいかたはふくざつです。) — This machine is complicated to use.

## How the kanji combine

複 suggests multiplicity or overlapping elements, while [雑](../kanji/雑.md) suggests a mixture. Their on readings form フク + ザツ. The combined meaning concerns elements intertwined in a complicated whole.

## Synonyms and antonyms

簡単 (かんたん), "simple" or "easy," is a useful contrast. 難しい (むずかしい) means difficult; something can be difficult without having a complicated structure.

## Distinctive meaning

The English noun gloss "complexity" should not hide the everyday adjective pattern 複雑な問題 (ふくざつなもんだい), "a complicated problem." 複雑な気持ち (ふくざつなきもち) means mixed feelings, not necessarily a feeling that is intellectually hard to understand. 複雑さ expresses "complexity" as a quality.
