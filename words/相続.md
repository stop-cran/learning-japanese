# 相続 (そうぞく) - inheritance; succession

## Meaning

相続 is inheritance or succession. As the transitive verb 相続する, it takes the inherited property with **を** and can identify the previous owner with **から**.

- 祖母から家を相続しました。 (そぼからいえをそうぞくしました。) - I inherited a house from my grandmother.
- 相続の手続きについて相談しました。 (そうぞくのてつづきについてそうだんしました。) - I sought advice about the inheritance procedures.

Inheritance is the practical focus of these examples. In historical family contexts, 家督を相続する (かとくをそうぞくする) means to succeed to the headship of a family. This is not a general expression for receiving an ordinary present.

## How the kanji combine

[相](../kanji/相.md) contributes a relationship between parties, and [続](../kanji/続.md) contributes continuation. Together, the word concerns continuation from one holder to a successor. The on readings are ソウ + ゾク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

引き継ぐ (ひきつぐ), "take over; carry on," is broader and works for duties or ongoing work. [受け取る](受け取る.md) (うけとる), "receive," does not by itself identify inheritance.

## Distinctive meaning

相続 identifies the kind of transfer or succession, not just possession of an item. In legal contexts its consequences depend on the applicable rules; the vocabulary alone does not say what someone is entitled to inherit. There is no single antonym that covers both succession and inheritance.
