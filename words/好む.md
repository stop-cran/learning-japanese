# 好む (このむ) - like; prefer

## Meaning

好む means to like or prefer something. It is a transitive godan verb: 好みます, 好まない, 好んだ. The thing liked takes を. A comparison with より can make the preference explicit.

- 彼は静かな場所を好みます。 (かれはしずかなばしょをこのみます。) - He likes quiet places.
- 私はコーヒーより紅茶を好みます。 (わたしはコーヒーよりこうちゃをこのみます。) - I prefer tea to coffee.

Without an explicit comparison, 好む need not mean that one option has defeated all the others; it can simply describe a liking.

## How the kanji combine

[好](../kanji/好.md) contributes liking. In this native verb it writes この, followed by the inflecting ending む. The related noun [好み](好み.md) (このみ) means taste or preference. 好んで (このんで) is the te-form, used for doing something willingly or by preference.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[好き](好き.md) (すき) expresses liking with different grammar: 紅茶が好きです, "I like tea." Do not copy its が pattern into the basic 紅茶を好む construction.

[嫌う](嫌う.md) (きらう), "dislike; avoid out of dislike," provides an opposite with comparable verb grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

好む works well when describing tastes, habits, or tendencies, including those of another person. It is not a command to enjoy something. In an ordinary personal declaration, 好きです is also a natural option; the difference is not that 好む must describe a stronger feeling.
