# 国 (くに)

## Meaning

国 means a country or nation. Depending on context, it can also refer to someone's home country or to the national government.

- あなたの国では何語を話しますか。 (あなたの くにでは なにごを はなしますか。) — What language do people speak in your country?
- 来月、国に帰ります。 (らいげつ、くにに かえります。) — I will return to my home country next month.

Use 国の before another noun: 国の名前 (くにのなまえ), "the country's name."

## How the kanji combine

[国](../kanji/国.md) stands alone with the native reading くに. In compounds it often has the on reading こく, as in [外国](外国.md) (がいこく), "foreign country."

## Synonyms and antonyms

国家 (こっか) is a more formal word for a state or nation. 故郷 (ふるさと), "hometown," identifies one's place of origin rather than necessarily an entire country.

## Distinctive meaning

国 does not inherently mean Japan or the speaker's birthplace; context supplies which country is intended. The home-country sense is particularly useful with 帰る, "return home."
