# 発つ (たつ) - depart; set off

## Meaning

発つ means to depart or set off on a journey. It is an intransitive
godan verb: 発ちます, 発った, 発って, 発たない.

- 明日の朝、東京を発ちます。
  (あしたのあさ、とうきょうをたちます。) - I leave Tokyo tomorrow morning.
- 彼女は九時の列車で発ちました。
  (かのじょはくじのれっしゃでたちました。) - She departed on the nine o'clock train.

を can mark the place departed from; it is not an object being
acted on. で can identify the means of transport.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) contributes departure and is read た here.
つ is the verb ending. The on reading ハツ appears in compounds
such as 出発 (しゅっぱつ), not in this verb's pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出発](出発.md) with する is another way to express departure.
[出かける](出かける.md) (でかける) overlaps with setting out,
including everyday outings. [到着](到着.md) (とうちゃく),
"arrival," gives the journey's other endpoint.

## Distinctive meaning

立つ (たつ) also has a departure sense; 発つ makes that use
visible in the spelling. It does not mean that someone stands up
on a plane. Distinguish [経つ](経つ.md), also たつ, for time
elapsing, and [建つ](建つ.md) for a structure being built.
