# くらくら — dizzy; giddy

## Meaning

くらくら describes a spinning or reeling feeling, especially when someone is dizzy.

- 暑い日に歩き続けて、目の前がくらくらしてきた。
  — After walking in the heat, everything started to spin before my eyes.
- 回転遊具から降りると、頭がくらくらした。
  — When I got off the spinning ride, I felt dizzy.

## Synonyms and antonyms

くらくら focuses on a dizzy or giddy sensation. ぐらぐら describes wobbling or instability,
while ふらふら often describes unsteady movement.

## Distinctive meaning

The word describes a sensation, not its cause. Heat, motion, and illness can all make someone feel
くらくら.
