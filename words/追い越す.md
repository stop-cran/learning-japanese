# 追い越す (おいこす) - overtake; surpass

## Meaning

追い越す means to pass or overtake someone or something that was
ahead. It can also mean surpassing someone in ability or achievement.
The overtaken or surpassed target takes を.

- バスが前の車を追い越しました。
  (バスがまえのくるまをおいこしました。) - The bus overtook the car in front.
- 弟は数学で兄を追い越しました。
  (おとうとはすうがくであにをおいこしました。) - The younger brother surpassed his older brother in mathematics.

It is a transitive godan verb ending in す:
追い越します, 追い越して, 追い越した, 追い越さない.
で identifies the field of comparison in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

[追](../kanji/追.md) contributes following or pursuing;
[越](../kanji/越.md) going beyond.
The native verb compound connects following with getting past the target:
おい + こす. It is not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

追い抜く (おいぬく) overlaps in overtaking and surpassing.
[越す](越す.md) (こす) has broader crossing or exceeding uses;
it does not always imply passing someone who was ahead.
[追い掛ける](追い掛ける.md) (おいかける) is pursuit without
necessarily reaching or passing the target.

## Distinctive meaning

The second example is about comparative achievement, not physical
movement. Conversely, merely driving behind another vehicle does not
establish that it has been overtaken.
JMdict lists 追越す and 追いこす as search-only forms; the card
uses 追い越す for production.
