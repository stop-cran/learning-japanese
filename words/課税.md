# 課税 (かぜい) - taxation; imposing tax

## Meaning

課税 is taxation: imposing or assessing a tax. 課税する expresses that action; the item taxed can be marked with に.

- 政府は輸入品に課税します。 (せいふはゆにゅうひんにかぜいします。) - The government imposes tax on imported goods.
- この収入は課税の対象です。 (このしゅうにゅうはかぜいのたいしょうです。) - This income is subject to taxation.

課税対象 (かぜいたいしょう) means something subject to tax. Japanese dictionaries also record 課税 as the tax imposed, so it is not a rule that the noun can never refer to the assessed tax itself.

## How the kanji combine

[課](../kanji/課.md) contributes imposing or assigning, as in 課す (かす); [税](../kanji/税.md) supplies tax. This is an action-and-object relationship: imposing tax. Their on readings combine as カ + ゼイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[税](税.md) (ぜい) and [税金](税金.md) (ぜいきん) name tax or tax money. 納税 (のうぜい) is payment of taxes, a different viewpoint from imposing them. 非課税 (ひかぜい), not subject to tax, contrasts with the taxable status.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse being subject to 課税 with having already paid. The useful construction here is Xに課税する, not an invented rule that every noun before する must take を. The tax authority, taxpayer, and taxed item have different roles.
