# 貸す (かす) — lend; rent out

## Meaning

貸す means letting someone else use something temporarily: lending it, or renting it out for payment.
The lender is the subject. Mark the borrower with に and the thing lent with を.

- 友達に本を貸しました。（ともだちにほんをかしました。） — I lent a book to a friend.
- ペンを貸してください。（ペンをかしてください。） — Please lend me a pen.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native verb with one kanji. [貸](../kanji/貸.md) is read か here, and す is okurigana.
It is a godan verb: 貸します（かします）, 貸さない（かさない）, 貸して（かして）.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[借りる](借りる.md) かりる, "borrow; rent," names the other side of the same transaction.
貸し出し（かしだし） is the related noun "lending," useful for a library or lending service.
Giving something away is different because a loan normally involves temporary use, not a permanent gift.

## Distinctive meaning

English "rent" can hide the direction, but Japanese distinguishes 貸す, renting **out**, from 借りる, renting **from** someone.
In ペンを貸してください, the person addressed is asked to lend, even when English would naturally say "May I borrow a pen?"
