# 受け取り (うけとり) - receiving; receipt

## Meaning

受け取り is a noun for receiving or collecting something. It can also name a written receipt
acknowledging that money or goods have been received.
受取り and 受取 are other spellings, also read うけとり.

- 荷物の受け取りは午後五時までです。 (にもつのうけとりはごごごじまでです。) - Parcels can be collected until five p.m.
- 家賃の受取をもらいました。 (やちんのうけとりをもらいました。) - I received a receipt for the rent.

In the first example, 荷物の受け取り names an act; in the second, 受取 names the document.
The paper sense is explicitly illustrated under 受取 in the Japanese dictionary.

## How the kanji combine

This native noun corresponds to the stem of [受け取る](受け取る.md) (うけとる), "receive."
[受](../kanji/受.md) supplies う and [取](../kanji/取.md) と; the kana complete うけとり.
The two receiving/taking associations reinforce accepting something, rather than forming on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

領収書 (りょうしゅうしょ) names a receipt document, especially for payment.
[受け取る](受け取る.md) is the related verb; [もらう](もらう.md) also describes receiving from someone.
Sending is the other side of a delivery, not the opposite of a paper receipt.

## Distinctive meaning

English "receipt" can mean either receiving or its written acknowledgment.
Do not assume every 荷物の受け取り refers to a cash-register slip.
Also distinguish the noun from the verb stem in 受け取ります (うけとります), "receive":
the written sequence 受け取り alone does not determine its grammatical role in a longer sentence.
