# 森林 (しんりん) - forest; woods

## Meaning

森林 means forest or woods: an area occupied by trees growing together,
or the plant community there. It is a noun.

- この地域には広い森林があります。 (このちいきにはひろいしんりんがあります。) - This region has extensive forest.
- 森林を守る活動に参加しました。 (しんりんをまもるかつどうにさんかしました。) - I took part in activities to protect forests.

Use 森林の before a related noun, as in 森林の保護
(しんりんのほご), "forest protection."

## How the kanji combine

[森](../kanji/森.md) and [林](../kanji/林.md) both contribute wooded
areas. Their related meanings reinforce the forest idea.
The on readings are シン + リン, not the standalone native words
もり + はやし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[森](森.md) (もり) and [林](林.md) (はやし) overlap with 森林.
[山林](山林.md) (さんりん) can describe mountain forest or woodland.
These are useful differences in wording or perspective, not an everyday
formula assigning each word an exact tree count or area.

## Distinctive meaning

The compound names woodland, not timber as a material.
The number of 木 shapes does not establish a strict small-versus-large
boundary, and these words alone do not certify whether a wooded area
is naturally grown or planted by people.
Learn the actual landscape or management context.
