# 爪 (つめ) - fingernail; claw

## Meaning

爪 names a fingernail or toenail, or an animal's claw or talon. The dictionary also includes a hoof.
For a person's nails, a modifier can identify the hand or foot.

- 爪を短く切りました。
  (つめをみじかくきりました。) - I cut my nails short.
- 猫の爪は鋭いです。
  (ねこのつめはするどいです。) - The cat's claws are sharp.
- 足の爪が痛いです。
  (あしのつめがいたいです。) - My toenail hurts.

Additional uses name a plectrum for a stringed instrument, or a claw-like hook, catch or clasp on a tool or mechanism.
These are not all parts of an animal's body.

## How the kanji combine

爪 is a single-character native noun, つめ, with no okurigana.
The bound form つま- occurs in some compounds, such as 爪先 (つまさき), "tiptoe; tip of a toe."
It does not replace standalone つめ freely. There is no separate 爪 card in the current collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[釘](釘.md) (くぎ) is a fastening nail driven into material. English "nail" can name either object, but Japanese 爪 and 釘 are different words.
指 (ゆび), "finger; toe," names the digit rather than its nail.
There is no ordinary lexical opposite of a nail or claw.

## Distinctive meaning

爪を切る means trimming biological nails, not cutting construction nails.
Learn the referent from context: a cat's claw, a person's toenail and a mechanical catch share a written word without being the same object.
