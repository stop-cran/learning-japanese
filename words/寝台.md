# 寝台 (しんだい) - bed

## Meaning

寝台 is a bed: a platform or piece of furniture used for sleeping.
The selected reading is しんだい.

- 部屋には二つの寝台がありました。 (へやにはふたつのしんだいがありました。) - There were two beds in the room.
- 寝台車で一晩過ごしました。 (しんだいしゃでひとばんすごしました。) - I spent the night in a sleeping car.

寝台車 (しんだいしゃ) is a sleeping car on a train.
The compound gives a useful setting for recognizing 寝台.

## How the kanji combine

[寝](../kanji/寝.md), sleeping or lying down, qualifies
[台](../kanji/台.md), a platform or stand.
The on readings シン + ダイ form a word for a sleeping platform or bed.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ベッド](ベッド.md) also means a bed.
[布団](布団.md) (ふとん) names bedding, including the mattress or quilt,
rather than necessarily a raised piece of furniture.
寝床 (ねどこ) can identify a bed or place to sleep.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict's English "couch" belongs with the bed/lying-down meaning here;
do not use it as a rule that every living-room [ソファー](ソファー.md)
is ordinarily called 寝台.
The dictionary also records ねだい for the same spelling, but this
article's starting reading remains しんだい.
