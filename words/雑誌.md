# 雑誌 (ざっし) — magazine; periodical

## Meaning

雑誌 is a magazine or other periodical, including journals issued at regular intervals. It can be printed or digital.

毎月、料理の雑誌を買います。 (まいつき、りょうりのざっしをかいます。) — I buy a cooking magazine every month.

## How the kanji combine

[雑](../kanji/雑.md) suggests variety or a mixture, and 誌 concerns written records. The compound is read ざっし: the small っ lengthens the "sh" sound for an extra beat, rather than adding a separate つ. The kanji associations help recall the word but do not mean that every magazine must cover unrelated subjects.

## Synonyms and antonyms

新聞 (しんぶん) is a newspaper. 本 (ほん) is a book, a broader everyday comparison for reading material. A 専門誌 (せんもんし) is a specialist journal or magazine. These categories do not form direct opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 冊 (さつ) to count physical volumes: 雑誌を一冊 (ざっしをいっさつ), "one magazine." 月刊 (げっかん) means monthly publication, while 週刊 (しゅうかん) means weekly. 雑誌 identifies the publication type rather than an individual article inside it.
