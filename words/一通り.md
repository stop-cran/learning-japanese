# 一通り (ひととおり) - once-over; all parts; ordinary

## Meaning

一通り can describe going over the relevant parts in a general pass,
without dwelling on every detail. It can also refer to more or less
everything needed or the whole process.

- 説明書を一通り読みました。 (せつめいしょをひととおりよみました。) - I read through the instructions.
- 料理は一通りそろっています。 (りょうりはひととおりそろっています。) - The dishes are all here.

The first does not promise exhaustive study or complete mastery.
一通りの can also mean ordinary or usual, especially with a negative:
一通りの苦労ではない (ひととおりのくろうではない),
"no ordinary hardship."
The dictionary additionally records "one method."

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) contributes native ひと;
[通](../kanji/通.md)り contributes とおり, a passage or way.
Learn ひととおり, not いちつうり.
The whole expression's general-pass, overall-range and ordinary uses
cannot all be reduced to counting one occurrence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ざっと](ざっと.md) overlaps in a quick, broad treatment.
[一応](一応.md) (いちおう) can qualify an action as basic or provisional.
[普通](普通.md) (ふつう) overlaps in ordinary/usual, but its train sense
does not belong to 一通り. [全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ) more directly says all.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the construction in view: 一通り読む describes the scope of a reading,
whereas 一通りの苦労ではない evaluates something as beyond the ordinary.
Do not replace every habitual "usually" with 一通り or assume it is a
universal synonym of [一度](一度.md) (いちど), once.
The source's several English glosses need these different contexts.
