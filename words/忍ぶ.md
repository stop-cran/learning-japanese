# 忍ぶ (しのぶ) — endure; hide oneself

## Meaning

忍ぶ has two uses. It can mean to bear something painful, such as shame or inconvenience; Digital Daijisen gives 恥を忍んで申し上げます (はじをしのんでもうしあげます), “I say this, swallowing my shame,” and 不便を忍ぶ (ふべんをしのぶ), “put up with inconvenience.” It can also mean to stay out of sight or act without being noticed, as in 人目を忍ぶ (ひとめをしのぶ), which JMdict glosses “to avoid being seen; to be clandestine.”

- 恥を忍んで、先生にもう一度説明をお願いした。 (はじをしのんで、せんせいにもういちどせつめいをおねがいした。) — Swallowing my pride, I asked the teacher to explain it once more.
- 二人は人目を忍んで、夜の公園で会っていた。 (ふたりはひとめをしのんで、よるのこうえんであっていた。) — The two of them met in the park at night, keeping out of sight.

## How the kanji combine

[忍](../kanji/忍.md) alone writes しの, and ぶ is okurigana, the kana ending. 忍んで (しのんで) is the te-form used in both examples. The same character is read にん in 忍耐 (にんたい), “endurance; patience.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

[我慢](我慢.md) (がまん) and [こらえる](こらえる.md) are everyday words for putting up with something. [隠れる](隠れる.md) (かくれる) simply means to hide or be hidden; in its hiding sense 忍ぶ is about acting so that other people do not notice, as when two people meet secretly.

## Distinctive meaning

A different verb, 偲ぶ (しのぶ), means to recall someone or something fondly; JMdict keeps it as a separate entry and does not list 忍ぶ as its spelling. 忍びない (しのびない) means “cannot bear to (do),” as in 見るに忍びない (みるにしのびない), “painful to watch.”
