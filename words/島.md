# 島 (しま) — island

## Meaning

島 means an island: land surrounded by water. It can refer to a small island or a large inhabited one. Use 島に for a destination and 島で for an activity there.

船で小さな島に行きました。 (ふねでちいさなしまにいきました。) — I went to a small island by boat.

## How the kanji combine

島 is a single kanji with the native reading しま. In some names and compounds it becomes じま, as in 小島 (こじま), "small island." The on reading とう appears in 半島 (はんとう), "peninsula."

## Synonyms and antonyms

半島 is connected to a larger landmass, unlike an island. 大陸 (たいりく) is a continent. These are useful geographical contrasts, not exact opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume every place name ending in 島 uses しま; names require their own readings. The basic noun identifies the land itself, not automatically the beach, the surrounding sea, or a tropical holiday destination.
