# 紅茶 (こうちゃ) — black tea

## Meaning

紅茶 is black tea, such as the tea served plain, with milk, or with lemon. It names the type of tea rather than whether anything has been added.

- 朝は紅茶を飲みます。 (あさはこうちゃをのみます。) — I drink black tea in the morning.
- 紅茶にミルクを入れますか。 (こうちゃにミルクをいれますか。) — Would you like milk in your tea?

## How the kanji combine

紅 means "red; crimson," and [茶](../kanji/茶.md) means tea. The on readings combine as コウ + チャ. Japanese names this tea with a red-color association, while English calls it black tea.

## Synonyms and antonyms

緑茶 (りょくちゃ), "green tea," is another tea category. お茶 (おちゃ) is the broader word for tea, often referring to Japanese green tea in everyday contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

紅茶 does not mean "tea without milk." Milk tea made with black tea is still a kind of 紅茶. Likewise, the written 紅 does not make this a general term for every red-colored herbal drink.
