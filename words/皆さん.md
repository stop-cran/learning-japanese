# 皆さん (みなさん) - everyone

## Meaning

皆さん is a courteous way to address or refer to everyone in a group. It is common in greetings, classroom instructions, and announcements.

- 皆さん、おはようございます。 (みなさん、おはようございます。) - Good morning, everyone.
- 皆さんの意見を聞きたいです。 (みなさんのいけんをききたいです。) - I would like to hear everyone's opinion.

It can stand outside the sentence as an address, or take ordinary noun particles: 皆さんに, "to everyone," and 皆さんの, "everyone's."

## How the kanji combine

[皆](../kanji/皆.md) is read みな here and means all or everyone. さん adds respect. Keep the reading みなさん; the casual word みんな does not supply an extra ん in this standard form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

みんな is more casual. 皆様 (みなさま) is more formal and respectful, useful for public or business addresses.

## Distinctive meaning

"Everyone" means the people relevant to the situation, not necessarily every person in the world. さん makes this a people-oriented courteous expression; it is not the way to say that all the books or all the food are present.
