# 色彩 (しきさい) — color; coloring

## Meaning

色彩 means color: the coloring and shades that something has, as in a painting or a landscape.

- この画家の絵は、明るい色彩で知られている。 (このがかのえは、あかるいしきさいでしられている。) — This painter’s pictures are known for their bright colors.
- 秋の山は、赤や黄色の色彩が美しい。 (あきのやまは、あかやきいろのしきさいがうつくしい。) — In autumn the mountains are beautiful with their reds and yellows.

## How the kanji combine

[色](../kanji/色.md) (しき) and [彩](../kanji/彩.md) (さい) both relate to color. In this word 色 is read しき, not いろ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[色](色.md) (いろ) is the everyday word for color. [カラー](カラー.md) (からー) is a loanword, as in カラー写真 (からーしゃしん), a color photograph.

## Distinctive meaning

Daijisen also records a figurative use for a tendency, as in 保守的色彩が強い (ほしゅてきしきさいがつよい), “a strongly conservative character”; JMdict lists only the color sense taught here.
