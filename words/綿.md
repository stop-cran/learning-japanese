# 綿 (めん) - cotton

## Meaning

綿 read **めん** means cotton as a material. It is a noun and can modify another noun with の.

- このシャツは綿でできています。 (このしゃつはめんでできています。) - This shirt is made of cotton.

The same character read **わた** belongs to a separate dictionary entry. It names the cotton plant and also batting, wadding, or padding.

- 庭で綿を育てています。 (にわでわたをそだてています。) - I grow cotton plants in the garden.
- ぬいぐるみに綿を詰めました。 (ぬいぐるみにわたをつめました。) - I stuffed the toy with padding.

The last example teaches わた, not a second meaning to assign automatically to めん. Padding described as わた need not establish the exact fiber composition.

## How the kanji combine

綿 is a single character connected with cotton. めん is its on reading; わた is the native reading used for the distinct lexical meanings above. The plant can also be written わた or ワタ. Learn the reading together with the context rather than swapping readings mechanically.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[絹](絹.md) (きぬ), "silk," and [毛](毛.md) (け), used for hair or wool according to context, name different materials. They are alternatives, not antonyms of cotton.

## Distinctive meaning

A fabric label and a discussion of stuffing can show the same character while calling for different readings. This card keeps めん as its primary reading, but explicitly distinguishes the plant and padding uses of わた.
