# 籠 (かご) - basket; cage

## Meaning

籠 means a basket or cage. Kana かご or カゴ is usual, so the examples use that spelling. It is a noun; use かごに for putting something into it and かごから for taking something out.

- 買い物かごにパンを入れました。 (かいものかごにパンをいれました。) - I put bread in the shopping basket.
- 鳥がかごから出ました。 (とりがかごからでました。) - The bird came out of the cage.

The contents and situation tell you whether a particular かご is a carrying basket or an enclosure.

## How the kanji combine

籠 represents the whole native word かご. Its bamboo element offers a useful visual association with a woven basket; it does not mean that every modern かご must be made of bamboo. 篭 is another recorded kanji form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[箱](箱.md) (はこ) is a box, while [袋](袋.md) (ふくろ) is a bag. All can hold things, but their typical shapes and construction differ. They are related container words, not automatic substitutes or opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

A supermarket's plastic basket is still a かご. In 鳥かご (とりかご), "birdcage," the enclosing function matters instead. Avoid assigning "basket" and "cage" to different pronunciations here: both uses share かご and the same dictionary identity.
