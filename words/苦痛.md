# 苦痛 (くつう) - pain; suffering

## Meaning

苦痛 is physical pain or mental suffering. It is a noun: 苦痛を感じる (くつうをかんじる) means "feel pain/distress," and Nが苦痛だ means "N is painful or distressing."

- 治療中、強い苦痛を感じました。 (ちりょうちゅう、つよいくつうをかんじました。) - I felt severe pain during treatment.
- 私には、長い会議が苦痛です。 (わたしには、ながいかいぎがくつうです。) - Long meetings are an ordeal for me.

The second example describes the speaker's distress, not necessarily a physical injury.

## How the kanji combine

[苦](../kanji/苦.md) contributes suffering or hardship, and [痛](../kanji/痛.md) contributes pain. These related meanings reinforce one another in a noun covering painful experience. The on readings are ク + ツウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[痛み](痛み.md) (いたみ) directly names pain, and [痛い](痛い.md) (いたい) is an adjective for being painful. [苦しい](苦しい.md) (くるしい) describes suffering, difficulty, or distress. Their meanings overlap, but their grammatical forms differ.

## Distinctive meaning

苦痛 can describe an experience that is difficult to endure, including psychological strain. It does not automatically identify a medical cause. Compare 頭が痛い (あたまがいたい), "my head hurts," with 苦痛を感じる: the first locates the pain, while the second foregrounds the experience of suffering.
