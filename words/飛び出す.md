# 飛び出す (とびだす) - rush out; stick out

## Meaning

飛び出す means to jump, rush, or dart out. It also describes something protruding. It is an intransitive godan verb: 飛び出します, 飛び出した, 飛び出さない.

- 猫が庭から飛び出しました。 (ねこがにわからとびだしました。) - A cat darted out of the garden.
- 箱から棒が飛び出しています。 (はこからぼうがとびだしています。) - A stick is sticking out of the box.

The first example describes sudden movement; the second describes a protruding state. The stick need not be moving.

## How the kanji combine

[飛](../kanji/飛.md) belongs to 飛ぶ (とぶ), "fly; jump." Its stem 飛び combines with [出](../kanji/出.md) in 出す (だす), giving the image of outward motion. The complete compound is intransitive despite the transitive verb 出す on its own.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出る](出る.md) (でる), "go out; emerge," is broader and need not suggest a sudden exit. [飛ぶ](飛ぶ.md) does not by itself specify coming out from somewhere.

## Distinctive meaning

With a departure place, を is possible: 家を飛び出す (いえをとびだす) can mean rushing or running away from home. Here を marks the place left, not an object being thrown out. For direction, 道路に飛び出す (どうろにとびだす) means darting into the road. Read the particle to distinguish the starting point from the destination.
