# 中指 (なかゆび) - middle finger; middle toe

## Meaning

中指 is the middle finger, between the index and ring fingers.
On the foot, it can mean the middle or third toe, counting from
the big toe.

- 右手の中指をけがしました。 (みぎてのなかゆびをけがしました。) - I injured the middle finger of my right hand.
- 足の中指が痛いです。 (あしのなかゆびがいたいです。) - My middle toe hurts.

手の中指 and 足の中指 make the intended body part explicit.
The dictionary also records ちゅうし as a reading; the primary
reading taught here is なかゆび.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) contributes なか, "middle";
[指](../kanji/指.md) contributes ゆび, "finger; toe."
The native modifier-plus-head combination identifies the digit
in the middle. It is not read なかし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[指](指.md) (ゆび) is the broader finger/toe word.
[薬指](薬指.md) (くすりゆび) is the ring finger;
[小指](小指.md) (こゆび) is the little finger or little toe.
These identify different digits, not interchangeable names.

## Distinctive meaning

English finger numbering varies with whether the thumb is
counted. Identify the middle finger by its position between the
index and ring fingers instead of relying on a bare number.
For the foot, the third toe is the middle of the five toes.
