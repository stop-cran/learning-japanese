# 菓子 (かし) - confectionery; sweets

## Meaning

菓子 is confectionery or sweets: a category including such things as candy, cakes, and biscuits. It is a noun and does not specifically mean pastry. The familiar form with お is [お菓子](お菓子.md) (おかし).

- お茶と一緒に菓子を出しました。 (おちゃといっしょにかしをだしました。) - I served sweets with the tea.
- この店は和菓子を売っています。 (このみせはわがしをうっています。) - This shop sells Japanese confectionery.

和菓子 (わがし) names Japanese-style confectionery; 洋菓子 (ようがし) names Western-style confectionery.

## How the kanji combine

菓 contributes the confectionery meaning. [子](../kanji/子.md) is the second element of this established food noun, not a literal child being described. The on readings are カ + シ. In 和菓子 and 洋菓子, か becomes voiced が.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ケーキ](ケーキ.md) is a particular kind of sweet, not a replacement for the entire category. おやつ names a snack or the occasion for one; the snack need not be confectionery.

## Distinctive meaning

The source-list gloss "pastry" is too narrow. A cake, candy, and a Japanese sweet need not share pastry dough to fall under 菓子. Conversely, the word does not mean every [食品](食品.md) (しょくひん), "food product." Learn it as a food category rather than a cooking method or a meal.
