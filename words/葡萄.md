# 葡萄 (ぶどう) — grapes

## Meaning

葡萄 means **grapes**, and can also refer to the grapevine. The word itself does not mark singular or plural.

朝ご飯にぶどうを食べました。 (あさごはんにぶどうをたべました。) — I ate grapes with breakfast.

A bunch is ぶどう一房 (ぶどうひとふさ); one individual grape can be counted as 一粒 (ひとつぶ).

## How the kanji combine

葡萄 is a Chinese-derived word read ブ + ドウ. Learn the two characters as the spelling of the whole plant name, rather than inventing a separate meaning for each to explain the fruit.

JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana. **ぶどう** and **ブドウ** are useful everyday spellings; the complex kanji spelling remains worth recognizing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

干しぶどう (ほしぶどう) means raisins, dried grapes. ワイン is wine, a product rather than another name for the fruit. There is no ordinary opposite of grapes.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes the fruit from the vine. The obsolete reading えび also appears in the dictionary, but it is not the beginner reading to substitute for ぶどう when buying or discussing grapes today.
