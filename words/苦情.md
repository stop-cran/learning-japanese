# 苦情 (くじょう) - complaint; grievance; objection

## Meaning

苦情 is a complaint or grievance, especially dissatisfaction about harm, inconvenience or disadvantage and its expression to someone involved. Useful patterns are 苦情を言う (くじょうをいう), make a complaint, and 苦情を受ける (くじょうをうける), receive complaints.

- 店に商品の苦情を伝えました。 (みせにしょうひんのくじょうをつたえました。) - I told the shop my complaint about the product.
- 近所から騒音の苦情が来ました。 (きんじょからそうおんのくじょうがきました。) - A noise complaint came from the neighbors.

Dictionaries also include "troubles" or difficult circumstances. That broader coverage does not make 苦情 the default everyday word for every personal worry or painful experience.

## How the kanji combine

[苦](../kanji/苦.md), suffering or difficulty, qualifies [情](../kanji/情.md), feeling or circumstances: a grievance about a troublesome situation. The on readings are ク + ジョウ. This explains the modern association, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[文句](文句.md) (もんく) and [不平](不平.md) (ふへい) overlap in complaints or grumbling. [不満](不満.md) (ふまん) is dissatisfaction, which need not be voiced. 苦情 often concerns a problem addressed to a responsible person or organization, but is not restricted to a formal legal complaint.

## Distinctive meaning

This is a noun: learn 苦情を言う or 苦情を伝える, not an assumed general 苦情する verb. A complaint may express an objection, but not every logical disagreement is a grievance about mistreatment or inconvenience.
