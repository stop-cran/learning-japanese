# やる - do; carry out casually

## Meaning

やる is a conversational verb for doing an activity or carrying out a task.
The activity commonly takes を. It is godan: やります, やらない, やって, やった.

- 宿題はもうやりました。 (しゅくだいはもうやりました。) - I have already done my homework.
- 一緒にゲームをやろう。 (いっしょにゲームをやろう。) - Let's play a game together.

The second example uses やろう, the form for proposing or intending an action.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is the usual spelling. The source-list form 遣る is a rare kanji spelling
of this word; it should not displace やる in ordinary beginner writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

する is the more neutral general verb for "do." やる often sounds more
casual or direct, even in its polite form やります. やめる, "stop; quit,"
can contrast with doing an activity but is not a universal antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

やる also means "give" in expressions such as 花に水をやる
(はなにみずをやる), "water the flowers." With people, that giving sense can
sound condescending; あげる is usually the safer neutral choice.
The do-sense and give-sense need different context.
