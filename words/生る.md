# 生る (なる) - bear fruit on a plant

## Meaning

生る means to bear fruit. It is an intransitive godan verb, usually
written なる. In a useful Japanese construction, the fruit takes が
and the plant or location takes に.

- この木には毎年りんごがなります。 (このきにはまいとしりんごがなります。) - This tree bears apples every year.
- 庭の木に実がなりました。 (にわのきにみがなりました。) - The tree in the garden bore fruit.

The Japanese subject in these examples is the apples or fruit, even
though English can make the tree the subject of "bear."
Forms include なります, ならない, なって and なった.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) has growth and life associations.
Here it writes な, with る in kana. The dictionary's usual-kana note
explains the examples' spelling; 生る still has the whole reading なる,
not いきる or はえる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

実る (みのる) can describe fruit or crops ripening and also figurative
efforts bearing fruit. [実](実.md) (み) is the fruit itself.
Fruit forming or growing on a plant is not the same action as picking
or harvesting it.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep this identity separate from [なる](なる.md), "become," and
[鳴る](鳴る.md), "ring; sound." Kana なる can represent different words,
so the construction and subject matter determine which word is intended.
This article teaches the botanical sense, not a blanket figurative
translation of every English "bear fruit" or every historical 生る
reading.
