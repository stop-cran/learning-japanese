# がちがち — stiff and rigid; teeth chattering

## Meaning

Strong stiffness or rigidity, including being tense, and the hard repeated sound of chattering teeth.

- 寒くて歯（は）ががちがち鳴（な）った。 — My teeth chattered with the cold.
- 初めての発表（はっぴょう）で、体ががちがちになった。 — My body went rigid during my first presentation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[かちかち](かちかち.md) also describes hardness and nervous stiffness. [ぶるぶる](ぶるぶる.md) focuses on trembling rather than rigidity, though both can occur when someone is cold.

## Distinctive meaning

がちがちになる is a useful state-change pattern. がちがちの規則（きそく）can describe inflexible rules. Voicing can make the impression heavier, but does not create an exact hardness scale between がちがち and かちかち.
