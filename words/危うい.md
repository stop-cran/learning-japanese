# 危うい (あやうい) - precarious; in danger

## Meaning

危うい describes something in danger, precarious or in jeopardy.
It can concern physical safety or an uncertain prospect.
It is an i-adjective: 危うくない (あやうくない), "not precarious,"
and 危うかった (あやうかった), "was in danger."

- 病人の命が危ういです。 (びょうにんのいのちがあやういです。) - The patient's life is in danger.
- このままでは、会社の将来が危ういです。 (このままでは、かいしゃのしょうらいがあやういです。) - At this rate, the company's future is in jeopardy.

The dictionary also notes the expression 危ういところ (あやういところ),
"a dangerous point or situation."

## How the kanji combine

[危](../kanji/危.md) contributes danger and supplies あや.
The ending うい completes the native adjective.
Learn the full reading あやうい; it differs from あぶない in 危ない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[危ない](危ない.md) (あぶない) and [危険](危険.md) (きけん) overlap in danger.
危険 is a な-adjective rather than an i-adjective.
[怪しい](怪しい.md) (あやしい) can concern a doubtful prospect, but also suspicious or eerie things;
it is not simply another spelling of 危うい.

## Distinctive meaning

危うい can focus on something threatened or insecure, not only a thing that causes harm.
For a sudden everyday "Watch out!" warning, learn 危ない！ as its own useful expression.
The distinction is not an absolute physical-versus-abstract rule.
