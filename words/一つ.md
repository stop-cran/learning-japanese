# 一つ (ひとつ) — one item

## Meaning

一つ means one item in the general native counting series. It is useful when counting an object without selecting a more specific counter.

- りんごを一つください。 (りんごをひとつください。) — One apple, please.
- 質問が一つあります。 (しつもんがひとつあります。) — I have one question.

The count can follow the noun and its particle, as in りんごを一つ. Before a noun, use 一つの: 一つの質問 (ひとつのしつもん), "one question."

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) writes the native number element ひと, and つ belongs to the general counting form. Learn ひとつ rather than reading this as いちつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一個 (いっこ) is another way to count one individual object. 二つ (ふたつ), "two items," continues the same native series.

## Distinctive meaning

一つ is not a universal replacement for every "one." A person is 一人 (ひとり), and a book is normally 一冊 (いっさつ). The general form is useful for objects, portions, and abstract items such as questions, but specific counting conventions still matter.
