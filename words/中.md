# 中 (なか) - inside; middle

## Meaning

中 refers to an inside, an interior position, or the middle. It also means "among" members of a group.

- 箱の中に鍵があります。 (はこのなかにかぎがあります。) - There is a key inside the box.
- 三つの箱のうち、中の箱を選びました。 (みっつのはこのうち、なかのはこをえらびました。) - Of the three boxes, I chose the middle one.

Use noun + の中 for "inside/among ..."; the resulting location can take に for existence or で for an action. この中から (このなかから) means "from among these."

The separate reading **ちゅう** is used as a noun for medium size or middle grade, and as a suffix:

- サイズは大・中・小があります。 (サイズはだい・ちゅう・しょうがあります。) - The sizes are large, medium, and small.
- 十人中三人が賛成しました。 (じゅうにんちゅうさんにんがさんせいしました。) - Three out of ten people agreed.
- 会議中は電話に出られません。 (かいぎちゅうはでんわにでられません。) - I cannot answer the phone during a meeting.

In 十人中, the suffix means "within/among"; in 会議中, it means "during." An activity noun + 中 can also
describe an activity underway: 今、会議中です (いま、かいぎちゅうです), "I am in a meeting now."
These suffix uses attach to the preceding expression, not as unrestricted replacements for noun + の中.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) alone writes the native noun なか. The readings ちゅう and じゅう occur in other words or suffix constructions; they are not freely interchangeable with なか in 箱の中.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[外](外.md) (そと), "outside," contrasts with the interior sense. [真ん中](真ん中.md) (まんなか) emphasizes the center or very middle.

## Distinctive meaning

中 does not always mean an exact geometric center. A key can be in a corner and still be 箱の中. Likewise, a person described as being グループの中, "within the group," need not occupy its physical center.
