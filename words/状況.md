# 状況 (じょうきょう) - situation; state of affairs

## Meaning

状況 names a situation, surrounding conditions, or the state of affairs.
Use 状況を説明する (じょうきょうをせつめいする), "explain the situation,"
and 状況によって, "depending on the situation."

- 現在の状況を教えてください。 (げんざいのじょうきょうをおしえてください。) - Please tell me the current situation.
- 状況によって、予定を変えるかもしれません。 (じょうきょうによって、よていをかえるかもしれません。) - We may change the plan depending on the circumstances.

The word does not say whether conditions are good or bad; the surrounding
sentence supplies that judgment.

## How the kanji combine

[状](../kanji/状.md) contributes state or condition, and 況 also concerns
conditions or circumstances. Their related meanings reinforce each other.
The on readings combine as じょう + きょう, with a long vowel in each part.
情況 is another dictionary spelling, not a separate reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[状態](状態.md) (じょうたい) can describe a thing's condition.
[事情](事情.md) (じじょう) overlaps with circumstances, often providing
background or reasons. [様子](様子.md) (ようす) can describe how something
looks or seems. These words have overlapping ranges, not rigid partitions.

## Distinctive meaning

状況 lets a speaker refer to a whole set of relevant conditions without listing
them again. 状況が変わった (じょうきょうがかわった), "the situation changed,"
may concern several factors, not just one object's physical state.
It does not inherently name a crisis. A report of a calm or improving situation
uses the same noun, so there is no useful general-purpose antonym.
