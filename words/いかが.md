# いかが - how, how about

## Meaning

いかが is a polite way to ask "how?" or "how about...?" It is especially useful for asking someone's opinion or making an offer.

- お味はいかがですか。 (おあじはいかがですか。) - How does it taste?
- お茶はいかがですか。 (おちゃはいかがですか。) - Would you like some tea?

The offer is understood from the situation; the expression does not literally contain a verb meaning "want."

## How the kanji combine

This native expression is normally written in kana. The dictionary also records 如何, but the everyday spelling does not require a kanji-by-kanji analysis.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どう is the less formal counterpart in many questions: どうですか, "How is it?" There is no direct antonym. どのように asks "in what way" and is not a general replacement in offers.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn いかがですか as a polite question pattern, not as a word meaning "good." Whether it asks for an assessment or offers something depends on what precedes は and on the situation.
