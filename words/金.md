# 金 (かね) - money; also きん for gold

## Meaning

The same written character represents two dictionary identities:

- **金 (かね)**: money; also metal. For money, [お金](お金.md) (おかね) is a useful polite everyday form. 金を使う (かねをつかう) means "spend money."
- **金 (きん)**: gold, including the metal. In shogi it also abbreviates 金将 (きんしょう), the gold general.

Examples keep the readings separate:

- そんなに金はかかりません。 (そんなにかねはかかりません。) - It does not cost that much money.
- この指輪は金でできています。 (このゆびわはきんでできています。) - This ring is made of gold.
- 金を一つ前に動かしました。 (きんをひとつまえにうごかしました。) - I moved the gold general one square forward.

The last sentence belongs to a shogi game, not a financial transaction.

## How the kanji combine

[金](../kanji/金.md) supplies the whole word. かね is the native reading used for this card's primary identity; きん is a Sino-Japanese reading. The wago metadata describes the primary reading, not every use of the character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[現金](現金.md) (げんきん) specifies cash. [金属](金属.md) (きんぞく) is the broad term for metal. [銀](銀.md) (ぎん), "silver," contrasts with gold but is not its antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read the shogi piece or gold material as かね merely because the spelling matches. Conversely, the presence of 金 does not mean money must be made of gold. Bare かね can sound blunt in polite conversation; お金 avoids that problem in many everyday exchanges.
