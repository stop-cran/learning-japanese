# 忘れる (わすれる) — forget

## Meaning

忘れる means to forget something, including leaving an item behind through forgetfulness.
It is transitive: を can mark what is forgotten.

- 友達の名前を忘れました。 (ともだちのなまえをわすれました。) — I forgot my friend's name.
- 傘を家に忘れました。 (かさをいえにわすれました。) — I left my umbrella at home.

The second example does not mean the umbrella ceased to exist in memory; it was left in the wrong place.

## How the kanji combine

[忘](../kanji/忘.md) supplies the forgetting meaning. The character is read わす, followed by the
written ending れる. This is a native verb, not a compound of two kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[覚える](覚える.md) (おぼえる) can mean committing something to memory.
思い出す (おもいだす) means recalling it. These contrast with different stages of forgetting:
first learn a name, then perhaps forget it, and later remember it again.

## Distinctive meaning

忘れる is an ichidan verb: remove the final る before endings such as ます and ない.
Thus 忘れます (わすれます) and 忘れない (わすれない) keep れ.
For an action, a clause can attach through の: 電話するのを忘れました
(でんわするのをわすれました), "I forgot to call."
