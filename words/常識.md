# 常識 (じょうしき) - common sense; common knowledge

## Meaning

常識 is knowledge, judgment, or conduct treated as ordinary and expected in a
community. Depending on context, it can mean common sense, common knowledge,
or accepted social practice.

- それは常識だと思っていました。 (それはじょうしきだとおもっていました。) - I thought that was common knowledge.
- 常識は時代によって変わります。 (じょうしきはじだいによってかわります。) - What is regarded as common sense changes with the times.

常識がある means to have common sense. 常識的な (じょうしきてきな) is the
related adjective for something sensible or conventional.

## How the kanji combine

[常](../kanji/常.md) contributes ordinary or usual, and [識](../kanji/識.md)
contributes knowledge or discernment. The first qualifies the second:
ordinary, shared understanding. The on readings are ジョウ + シキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知識](知識.md) (ちしき) is knowledge generally, including specialist knowledge.
常識 adds the expectation that people would ordinarily know or understand it.
非常識 (ひじょうしき) describes a lack of common sense or departure from
accepted conduct.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling something 常識 is not proof that everyone knows it or that it is
universally correct. The word can carry a social judgment: 常識がない,
"has no common sense," may sound strongly critical. Be especially careful
when describing unfamiliar cultural practices, where the assumed shared
background may differ.
