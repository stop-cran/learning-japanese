# 街角 (まちかど) - street corner

## Meaning

街角 is a street corner.

- 次の街角を右に曲がってください。 (つぎのまちかどをみぎにまがってください。) - Please turn right at the next street corner.
- 街角で友達に会いました。 (まちかどでともだちにあいました。) - I met a friend on a street corner.

The spelling 町角 is also recorded with the same reading.

## How the kanji combine

街 contributes the town or street setting, and [角](../kanji/角.md)
the corner. The first part qualifies the second: まち + かど.
Keep the native reading かど here, not カク or つの.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[角](角.md) (かど) is broader: it can name a corner or edge outside a
street setting. [交差点](交差点.md) (こうさてん) names an intersection.
A street corner can be at an intersection, but the corner and the entire
junction are not the same description.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read this as がいかく or interpret 角 as an animal's horn.
The spelling identifies a familiar town location, not a mathematical
angle measurement or the inside nook of a room.
