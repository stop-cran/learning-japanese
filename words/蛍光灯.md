# 蛍光灯 (けいこうとう) - fluorescent lamp; slow-on-the-uptake person (dated)

## Meaning

蛍光灯 is a fluorescent lamp or light.

- 部屋の蛍光灯を交換しました。 (へやのけいこうとうをこうかんしました。) - I replaced the room's fluorescent lamp.

There is also a **dated** figurative use for someone slow to react or understand.
For recognition: 彼は蛍光灯だ (かれはけいこうとうだ) can mean "He is slow on the uptake."
Applied to a person this is disparaging, not a neutral modern description or a compliment.

## How the kanji combine

蛍 contributes "firefly" and [光](../kanji/光.md) "light."
Together 蛍光 (けいこう) means fluorescence; [灯](../kanji/灯.md), "lamp," is the head of the compound.
Thus it is a fluorescence lamp, not a lamp containing fireflies.
The readings are ケイ + コウ + トウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[電灯](電灯.md) (でんとう) is the broader electric-light term.
[明かり](明かり.md) (あかり) can mean light or illumination, without specifying the technology.
[鈍い](鈍い.md) (にぶい) can describe slowness or dullness, but has many other uses.

## Distinctive meaning

蛍光灯 does not name every electric lamp: an LED lamp is not fluorescent merely because it lights a room.
Keep the literal lighting use separate from the dated person-label sense.
The latter needs human context; the ordinary replacement sentence above concerns actual equipment.
