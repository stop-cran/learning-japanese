# 会議 (かいぎ) -- meeting; conference

## Meaning

会議 is a meeting held for discussion, planning, or decision-making. It can be a small workplace meeting or a larger conference.

- 会議は三時に始まります。 (かいぎはさんじにはじまります。) -- The meeting starts at three.
- 明日はオンラインで会議をします。 (あしたはオンラインでかいぎをします。) -- We will have an online meeting tomorrow.

Useful patterns include 会議をする, hold a meeting, and 会議に出る (かいぎにでる), attend a meeting.

## How the kanji combine

[会](../kanji/会.md), read かい, contributes gathering; [議](../kanji/議.md), read ぎ, contributes discussion or deliberation. The compound names a gathering for deliberation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

打ち合わせ (うちあわせ) often concerns arranging or coordinating practical details. ミーティング is a loanword that overlaps with 会議; the name alone does not determine how formal a meeting is.

## Distinctive meaning

Meeting someone by chance is not normally a 会議. For the act of encountering a friend, use 会う (あう). 会議 names the discussion event, not every situation that English can call "a meeting."
