# 原料 (げんりょう) - raw materials; ingredients

## Meaning

原料 is raw material or an ingredient used as an input in making a product.
It is a noun. Nを原料にする means "use N as a raw material";
Nを原料として uses として, "as."

- この紙は木を原料にしています。 (このかみはきをげんりょうにしています。) - This paper uses wood as its raw material.
- 小麦はパンの原料の一つです。 (こむぎはパンのげんりょうのひとつです。) - Wheat is one of the ingredients used to make bread.

The input may be a plant, mineral, chemical, or another substance.
"Raw" describes its role as production material, not necessarily an
uncooked or wholly unprocessed state.

## How the kanji combine

[原](../kanji/原.md) contributes original or source, and
[料](../kanji/料.md) contributes material.
Together they identify material at the input side of production.
The on readings are ゲン + リョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[材料](材料.md) (ざいりょう) also means materials or ingredients and
overlaps substantially. It can additionally describe information used
in a judgment. [資料](資料.md) (しりょう) concerns reference information
or documents, not a physical ingredient merely because English says "material."
[製品](製品.md) (せいひん), a manufactured product, is a useful output-side contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The role depends on the production process. A product from one process
may become 原料 in another. A machine used to make paper is equipment,
not automatically a raw material of the paper.
