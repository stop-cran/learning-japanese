# 濁る (にごる) - become cloudy; become impure

## Meaning

濁る is an intransitive godan verb for losing clarity or purity.
Liquid can become muddy or cloudy; sound or color can become dull or indistinct.

- 雨で川の水が濁りました。 (あめでかわのみずがにごりました。) - The river water became muddy because of the rain.
- 声が濁っています。 (こえがにごっています。) - The voice sounds hoarse or unclear.

The thing changing takes が. Useful forms include 濁ります, 濁った and
濁って. The related transitive verb 濁す (にごす) means to make something
cloudy or unclear.

## How the kanji combine

濁 writes the native stem にご-, followed by る. This is a godan verb:
the negative is 濁らない (にごらない), not にごない.
The whole word describes a change or resulting loss of clarity, not just
the presence of the water-related shape in the character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[曇る](曇る.md) (くもる) also involves lost clarity, for example clouding
or fogging. [汚染](汚染.md) (おせん) concerns pollution or contamination;
water can look cloudy without being dangerously polluted, and contamination
can be invisible. [澄む](澄む.md) (すむ), "become clear," gives a contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

濁る can also describe moral corruption or impurity, not just dirty water.
In pronunciation, a consonant can 濁る, "become voiced": for example,
か becoming が. This is a linguistic use, not a claim that が is unclean.
Likewise, a 濁った音 (にごったおと), "dull or unclear sound," must be
interpreted as sound rather than literal muddy liquid.
