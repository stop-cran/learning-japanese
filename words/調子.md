# 調子 (ちょうし) - tone; condition

## Meaning

調子 can mean a musical tone, pitch, or rhythm; a way or manner of speaking; or the condition in which a person or machine is functioning.

- ピアノの調子が狂っています。 (ピアノのちょうしがくるっています。) - The piano is out of tune.
- 今日は体の調子がいいです。 (きょうはからだのちょうしがいいです。) - I am feeling well today.

Nの調子 identifies whose or what condition/tone is in question. 調子がいい/悪い (ちょうしがいい/わるい) describes good or poor form. 穏やかな調子で話す (おだやかなちょうしではなす) means "speak in a calm tone."

## How the kanji combine

[調](../kanji/調.md) contributes tuning, harmony, or condition. [子](../kanji/子.md) is a noun-forming element here, not a literal child. The on readings are チョウ + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[状態](状態.md) (じょうたい) is state or condition more broadly. 車の調子 (くるまのちょうし) can focus on how a car runs; 車の状態 can cover its condition generally. [気分](気分.md) (きぶん) concerns mood or how one feels, rather than a piano's tuning.

## Distinctive meaning

The attached noun is essential. 声の調子 (こえのちょうし) concerns vocal tone; 体の調子 concerns health or form. 調子 alone is not a diagnosis. Likewise, その調子 (そのちょうし), "that's the way/keep it up," responds to how someone is doing something, not necessarily to music.
