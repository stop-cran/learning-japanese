# 整理 (せいり) - sorting; putting in order

## Meaning

整理 is sorting, organizing, or putting things in order. 整理する is transitive:
the things or information organized take を.

- 机の上の書類を整理しました。 (つくえのうえのしょるいをせいりしました。) - I organized the papers on my desk.
- 話す前に、自分の考えを整理しましょう。 (はなすまえに、じぶんのかんがえをせいりしましょう。) - Put your thoughts in order before speaking.

整理している can describe organizing in progress. 整理された資料
(せいりされたしりょう) are materials that have been organized.

## How the kanji combine

整 contributes making orderly, and [理](../kanji/理.md) contributes arranging or
putting into order. Their related meanings reinforce organization rather than
naming two separate tasks. The readings are セイ + リ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[片付ける](片付ける.md) (かたづける) can mean tidying or putting things away.
整理する draws attention to sorting or arranging them, including information
that cannot be physically put in a drawer. 整頓 (せいとん) concerns orderly
arrangement and overlaps with the physical tidying use.

## Distinctive meaning

The thoughts example shows why 整理 is not just cleaning. It can involve grouping,
selecting, or clarifying material so that it is manageable. It does not promise
that anything has been washed, nor does it automatically mean throwing things
away. In business or financial contexts, the word also has specialized senses;
do not translate those mechanically as cleaning a room.
