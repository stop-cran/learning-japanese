# 遅い (おそい) — slow; late

## Meaning

遅い is an i-adjective meaning either "slow" in speed or "late" in time. The surrounding words show which is intended.

- このバスは遅いです。 (このバスはおそいです。) — This bus is slow.
- 今日は帰りが遅くなります。 (きょうはかえりがおそくなります。) — I'll get home late today.

Useful forms are 遅く (おそく), 遅かった (おそかった), and 遅くない (おそくない).

## How the kanji combine

[遅](../kanji/遅.md) supplies おそ, and い is the adjective ending. The ordinary spelling 遅い serves both meanings; different kanji are not needed for "slow" and "late."

## Synonyms and antonyms

速い (はやい), "fast," contrasts with slow speed. 早い (はやい), "early," contrasts with a late time. 遅れる (おくれる) is a verb meaning "be late; fall behind."

## Distinctive meaning

遅い does not always imply missing a deadline. 夜遅く (よるおそく) simply means "late at night." By contrast, もう遅い (もうおそい) can mean "it's too late now."
