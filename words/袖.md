# 袖 (そで) - sleeve

## Meaning

袖 is the sleeve of a garment. It is a noun:
シャツの袖 means "a shirt sleeve," and 袖が長い means "the sleeves are long."
袖を通す (そでをとおす) can describe putting one's arms through a garment's sleeves.

- このシャツは袖が少し長いです。
  (このシャツはそでがすこしながいです。) - This shirt's sleeves are a little long.
- 暑いので、袖をまくりました。
  (あついので、そでをまくりました。) - It was hot, so I rolled up my sleeves.

長袖 (ながそで) means long sleeves or long-sleeved clothing;
半袖 (はんそで) means short sleeves or short-sleeved clothing.
The surrounding garment or situation supplies the English noun.

## How the kanji combine

袖 is one kanji for the whole native noun そで.
There is no pair of word components to translate.
In 長袖 and 半袖, the preceding character describes the sleeve's length.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[腕](腕.md) (うで) is an arm, the body part inside a sleeve.
[衣服](衣服.md) (いふく) means clothing as a broader category.
Long and short sleeves contrast, but an arm is not an antonym of a sleeve.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep body and garment separate: 腕を上げる (うでをあげる) raises an arm,
whereas 袖をまくる rolls up the fabric.
袖 can occur in specialized extended uses, but the examples here teach the clothing meaning.
