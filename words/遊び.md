# 遊び (あそび) — play; recreation

## Meaning

遊び means **play, a game, or recreation**. It is a noun, not the dictionary form of a verb.

- 子どもの遊びを見ていました。 (こどものあそびをみていました。) — I was watching the children's play.
- 日曜日に友達の家へ遊びに行きます。 (にちようびにともだちのいえへあそびにいきます。) — On Sunday I am going to a friend's house to hang out.

In 遊びに行く, the purpose pattern is a verb's ます-stem + に + 行く: “go to play/have fun.”

## How the kanji combine

[遊](../kanji/遊.md) writes あそ; び is written in kana. 遊び (あそび) is both the noun and the complete ます-stem of 遊ぶ (あそぶ), “play.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

ゲーム is a game, often one with rules. 遊び is broader. 仕事 (しごと), “work,” provides a useful contrast when distinguishing leisure from obligations.

## Distinctive meaning

遊び focuses on enjoyment rather than a specific sport or organized activity. 遊びに行く can mean visiting someone socially, not necessarily playing a children's game.
