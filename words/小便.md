# 小便 (しょうべん) - urine; urination

## Meaning

小便 names urine or urination. 小便をする means to urinate. The reading しょんべん is also recorded, but しょうべん is this card's primary reading.

- 小便をしたくなりました。 (しょうべんをしたくなりました。) - I needed to pee.
- この検査では小便を調べます。 (このけんさではしょうべんをしらべます。) - This test examines urine.

The first concerns the action; the second the bodily substance. In a medical explanation, 尿 (にょう), "urine," is a useful alternative.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) contributes small, and [便](../kanji/便.md) the excretion-related ベン sense here. Contrast 大便 (だいべん), "feces; defecation." The existing [便](便.md) article teaches the different reading びん for transport or mail; it does not make 小便 a transport word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

尿 (にょう) names urine; 排尿 (はいにょう) names urination. [トイレ](トイレ.md) names the facilities and can euphemistically refer to using them. トイレに行く (トイレにいく), "go to the restroom," is useful when announcing a break without explicitly naming the bodily substance.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list uses colloquial English "piss," but do not assume that every occurrence of 小便 has the same offensiveness as that English word. It is direct bodily vocabulary, so wording and setting matter. JMdict additionally records a colloquial "breaking a contract" sense; it is separate from the urine examples, not a meaning to infer from every occurrence.
