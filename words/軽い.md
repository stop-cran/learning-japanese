# 軽い (かるい) — light in weight

## Meaning

軽い means **light, not heavy**. It is an い-adjective: 軽いかばん (かるいかばん), “a light bag.”

この箱は軽いです。 (このはこはかるいです。) — This box is light.

The negative is 軽くない (かるくない), and the past is 軽かった (かるかった). Use 軽いです for a polite statement, not 軽いだ.

## How the kanji combine

軽 is read かる in this native adjective, followed by い. There is only one written kanji; the everyday reading here is かるい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

重い (おもい), “heavy,” is the direct weight contrast. 小さい (ちいさい), “small,” concerns size: a small object can still be heavy. 明るい (あかるい), “bright,” translates a different meaning of English “light.”

## Distinctive meaning

軽い also describes something minor or not severe: 軽いけが (かるいけが), “a minor injury.” Context determines whether weight or seriousness is meant. This figurative use does not turn 軽い into a general synonym for “easy” in every sentence.
