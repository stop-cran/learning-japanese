# 元 (もと) - former; origin

## Meaning

元, read もと, represents two dictionary identities taught separately here.

**Former or previous:** use 元の before a noun or 元- as a prefix, as in 元社長 (もとしゃちょう), "former company president." 元 can also refer to a previous state or function adverbially as "formerly."

- 彼は元の同僚です。 (かれはもとのどうりょうです。) - He is a former colleague.
- 使った物は元に戻してください。 (つかったものはもとにもどしてください。) - Please put what you used back where it was.

元は学校だった (もとはがっこうだった) means "it used to be a school."

**Origin or source:** this is a separate noun identity, not another sense number in the former/ex- entry.

- うわさの元を調べています。 (うわさのもとをしらべています。) - I am investigating the source of the rumor.

## How the kanji combine

[元](../kanji/元.md) is a single character with the native reading もと. Its associations with beginnings and earlier states help connect the meanings, but shared spelling does not eliminate their lexical distinction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[以前](以前.md) (いぜん), "previously; before," overlaps with the time-related use. 起源 (きげん), "origin," compares with the source-related noun. [現在](現在.md) (げんざい), "the present," contrasts with former status.

## Distinctive meaning

元の is not always "former": 元の場所 can mean the original or previous place to which something should return. Decide whether the sentence concerns previous status or the source from which something arose. Neither use changes the reading to げん.
