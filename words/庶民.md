# 庶民 (しょみん) — ordinary people; common people

## Meaning

庶民 means ordinary people, considered apart from elites or people of exceptional wealth or status. It does not specify citizenship.

- この博物館では、江戸時代の庶民の暮らしを紹介している。 (このはくぶつかんでは、えどじだいのしょみんのくらしをしょうかいしている。) — This museum shows how ordinary people lived in the Edo period.
- 当時、砂糖は庶民には高すぎた。 (とうじ、さとうはしょみんにはたかすぎた。) — At the time, sugar was too expensive for ordinary people.

## How the kanji combine

[庶](../kanji/庶.md) (しょ) suggests the many or the common people; [民](../kanji/民.md) (みん) means people. Together they identify a social group rather than naming any one occupation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[国民](国民.md) (こくみん) identifies people in relation to a country and [市民](市民.md) (しみん) in relation to a city or civic life. Neither word by itself contrasts people with an elite.

## Distinctive meaning

庶民の暮らし asks about ordinary people’s lives. The price example concerns what was within their reach, not a claim that every 庶民 had the same income.
