# 厚い (あつい) — thick

## Meaning

厚い is an i-adjective describing thickness: a thick book, wall, or layer. Its negative is 厚くない（あつくない）.

この本は厚いです。（このほんはあついです。） — This book is thick.

It can also describe deep kindness or warm treatment, especially in expressions such as 厚いもてなし（あついもてなし）, "warm hospitality."

## How the kanji combine

厚 carries the thickness meaning; い is the adjective ending. The reading is native Japanese. The spelling 篤い occurs in some figurative uses, but 厚い is the spelling to learn for physical thickness.

## Synonyms and antonyms

薄い（うすい）, "thin," contrasts with physical 厚い. 太い（ふとい） describes the thickness of something like a rope or tree trunk.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 厚い with 暑い, "hot weather," or 熱い, "hot to the touch," both also あつい. Nor does "deep" make 厚い interchangeable with 深い（ふかい）, as in a deep lake.
