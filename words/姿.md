# 姿 (すがた) - figure; appearance

## Meaning

姿 is a noun for the visible figure, form, or appearance of someone or something.
It can describe a person as seen from a distance or how someone looks in particular clothing.
Use person + の姿 to identify whose figure is being described.

- 遠くに母の姿が見えました。 (とおくにははのすがたがみえました。) - I could see my mother's figure in the distance.
- 着物姿の写真を撮りました。 (きものすがたのしゃしんをとりました。) - I took a photograph of someone dressed in a kimono.

In 着物姿, 姿 follows the clothing noun and means "dressed in a kimono."
Context supplies the person; the compound does not identify their gender or relationship to the speaker.

## How the kanji combine

姿 is one character writing the native word すがた. Learn the complete reading rather than
trying to turn the written components into a compound.
It refers to appearance, not exclusively to beauty or to one body type.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[形](形.md) (かたち) also means shape or form and can focus on an outline.
[格好](格好.md) (かっこう) overlaps in descriptions of appearance or dress.
姿 in 母の姿 can convey the recognizable person in view, not merely the shape of clothing.

## Distinctive meaning

姿を見せる (すがたをみせる) means "show oneself; appear," while 姿が見えない
(すがたがみえない) means someone or something is not in sight.
The second expression does not necessarily mean the person is absent from the entire area.
Visibility and appearance, rather than exact physical location, are central.
