# 上手 (じょうず) -- skillful; good at

## Meaning

上手 describes doing something skillfully. The activity or skill commonly takes が.

- 姉は料理が上手です。 (あねはりょうりがじょうずです。) -- My older sister is good at cooking.
- 子どもが上手に字を書きました。 (こどもがじょうずにじをかきました。) -- The child wrote the characters skillfully.

It is a na-adjective: 上手な人 (じょうずなひと), a skilled person, and 上手に + verb, doing something well. The negative is 上手ではない (じょうずではない).

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md), read じょう, suggests a high level; [手](../kanji/手.md) suggests skill or technique. 手 has the conventional on-reading form ず in this compound. Learn じょうず as the established pronunciation, not as うえ + て.

## Synonyms and antonyms

下手 (へた), poor at or unskillful, is the opposite. 得意 (とくい) presents something as a person's strong point; its emphasis is not quite the same as evaluating a performance.

## Distinctive meaning

Being 上手 at something does not necessarily mean liking it. The word evaluates ability, not preference. Other readings of 上手, such as かみて, belong to different meanings and are not substitutes here.
