# 決まる (きまる) - be decided; be settled

## Meaning

決まる describes a matter becoming decided or settled. It is intransitive: the matter takes が, rather than being an を-marked object.

- 旅行の日が決まりました。 (りょこうのひがきまりました。) - The date of the trip has been decided.
- まだ場所が決まっていません。 (まだばしょがきまっていません。) - The location has not been decided yet.

It is a godan verb: 決まります (きまります), 決まらない (きまらない), 決まって (きまって).

## How the kanji combine

[決](../kanji/決.md) supplies the settling or deciding meaning. The ordinary spelling is 決まる. The shorter spelling 決る occurs in the source list but omits the usual ま in the okurigana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[決める](決める.md) is the transitive partner: 日を決める (ひをきめる), "decide on a date." 決まる focuses on the settled matter, not the decision-maker.

## Distinctive meaning

The English translation often looks passive, but 決まる is not a passive conjugation of 決める. 決まっている can describe an already fixed arrangement; it need not mean that a decision is happening right now.
