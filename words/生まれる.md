# 生まれる (うまれる) — to be born

## Meaning

生まれる means to be born, and more generally to come into being: 東京で生まれました (I was born in Tokyo), 赤ちゃんが生まれる (a baby is born), 新しい考えが生まれる (a new idea arises).
The usual spelling has 生 with okurigana まれる; 産まれる is a variant spelling that stresses giving birth.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) with the kun reading う.まれる. It is the intransitive partner of 生む うむ (to give birth, to produce): 子供を生む (give birth to a child), 子供が生まれる (a child is born).
The noun 生まれ うまれ (birth, birthplace; 東京生まれ, "born in Tokyo") comes from the same stem. Birthday: 誕生日 たんじょうび.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 誕生する たんじょうする (be born, formal), 生じる しょうじる (arise, occur).
- Antonym: 死ぬ しぬ (die); related: 生きる いきる (live).

## Distinctive meaning

生まれる is normally used in the past tense for a person's own birth: 大阪で生まれました (I was born in Osaka), because the birth is a completed event.
大阪生まれです ("I'm Osaka-born") is another way to state one's birthplace.
