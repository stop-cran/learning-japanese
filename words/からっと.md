# からっと — crisp and dry; bright and clear

## Meaning

からっと describes food fried crisp and dry, laundry that dries thoroughly, or clear, sunny
weather.

- 天ぷらがからっと揚がった。
  — The tempura fried up crisp.
- 雨が上がって、空がからっと晴れた。
  — The rain stopped, and the sky cleared.

## Synonyms and antonyms

For food, からっと emphasizes a crisp, non-greasy finish. For weather, it suggests clear,
pleasant conditions; the context selects the sense.

## Distinctive meaning

からっと can also describe an open, frank manner. These examples focus on food and weather, so
do not infer the conversational sense from them.
