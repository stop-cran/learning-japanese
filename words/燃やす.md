# 燃やす (もやす) - burn something; kindle enthusiasm

## Meaning

燃やす is the transitive verb for burning something:
the thing burned takes を.
It is a godan verb: 燃やします, 燃やして, 燃やした.

- 落ち葉を燃やしています。 (おちばをもやしています。) - They are burning fallen leaves.
- 選手たちは闘志を燃やしています。 (せんしゅたちはとうしをもやしています。) - The athletes are fired up with fighting spirit.

In the second example, 闘志 (とうし), fighting spirit, is what is figuratively
set ablaze. No literal fire is required.

## How the kanji combine

[燃](../kanji/燃.md) contributes burning or blazing.
The native reading is も.やす; やす belongs to the written inflection.
Compare the separate transitive form 燃す (もす).
Its shorter reading does not turn 燃やす into もす.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[燃える](燃える.md) (もえる) is intransitive:
落ち葉が燃える (おちばがもえる), "the fallen leaves burn."
[焼く](焼く.md) (やく) also has burning uses as well as cooking uses.
[焦がす](焦がす.md) (こがす) emphasizes scorching or charring.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare を燃やす, burn something, with が燃える, something burns.
English "burn" alone does not tell you which Japanese verb and particle to choose.
Nor is 燃やす the general verb for cooking a meal simply because heat is involved.
The emotional construction retains を even when English uses "be fired up."
