# 思い出 (おもいで) - memory; recollection

## Meaning

思い出 is a memory of an experience or a collection of such memories. It is a noun: 子供のころの思い出 (こどものころのおもいで) means "childhood memories."

- この写真を見ると、旅行の思い出がよみがえります。 (このしゃしんをみると、りょこうのおもいでがよみがえります。) - Looking at this photograph brings back memories of the trip.
- 友達と過ごした夏は、いい思い出になりました。 (ともだちとすごしたなつは、いいおもいでになりました。) - The summer I spent with my friends became a happy memory.

思い出になる describes an experience becoming something remembered. The noun itself does not specify singular or plural.

## How the kanji combine

[思](../kanji/思.md) contributes thought or remembering, and [出](../kanji/出.md) contributes coming out. Relate the word to [思い出す](思い出す.md) (おもいだす), "recall." The noun's ending is で, not the verb's だす; learn the whole form おもいで.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[記憶](記憶.md) (きおく) can concern memory as a mental function or stored information. 思い出 highlights remembered experiences, often personally meaningful ones. [忘れる](忘れる.md) (わすれる), "forget," is a related contrasting action, not an opposite noun.

## Distinctive meaning

思い出 is not limited to pleasant experiences: 悲しい思い出 (かなしいおもいで) means "a sad memory." It also does not mean a physical souvenir; [お土産](お土産.md) (おみやげ) can be an object that helps bring back a 思い出.
