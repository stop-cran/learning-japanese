# 資本 (しほん) - capital; business funds

## Meaning

資本 is capital: funds or resources used as a basis for a business or productive
activity. It is a noun, not a verb meaning to finance something.

- 会社を始めるには資本が必要です。 (かいしゃをはじめるにはしほんがひつようです。) - Capital is needed to start a company.
- この会社には外国の資本が入っています。 (このかいしゃにはがいこくのしほんがはいっています。) - This company has foreign investment.

資本が入る describes capital coming into a business; the source can be
identified by a preceding の phrase.

## How the kanji combine

[資](../kanji/資.md) contributes resources or funds, and [本](../kanji/本.md)
contributes a base or foundation. Together they identify resources forming
the basis of an enterprise. The on readings are シ + ホン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

資金 (しきん) emphasizes funds available or needed for a purpose.
[財産](財産.md) (ざいさん) is property or wealth someone owns. The ideas
overlap, but ownership alone does not make every possession business capital.

## Distinctive meaning

English "capital" has unrelated meanings that 資本 does not supply: it is
not a capital city or an uppercase letter. Context also matters within
business writing, where capital may be discussed in financial or economic
terms. In ordinary study, start with the concrete question of what resources
support the business, rather than treating 資本 as a synonym for any money.
