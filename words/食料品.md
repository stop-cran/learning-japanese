# 食料品 (しょくりょうひん) - food groceries

## Meaning

食料品 means foodstuffs or groceries viewed as goods to buy, store, or supply. It includes ordinary food products rather than naming a prepared meal.

- スーパーで食料品を買いました。 (スーパーでしょくりょうひんをかいました。) - I bought groceries at the supermarket.
- この店は食料品を売っています。 (このみせはしょくりょうひんをうっています。) - This shop sells food products.

It is a noun; use an appropriate verb such as 買う or 売る for the shopping action.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md), しょく, and [料](../kanji/料.md), りょう, form 食料, "food supplies." [品](../kanji/品.md), ひん, presents them as articles or products.

## Synonyms and antonyms

食品 (しょくひん) broadly overlaps with food products. 食べ物 (たべもの) is the everyday "food, things to eat." 日用品 (にちようひん) are everyday necessities and can include nonfood goods.

## Distinctive meaning

English "groceries" sometimes includes soap and other household purchases. 食料品 specifically concerns food goods, so it should not automatically cover everything bought in the same supermarket trip.
