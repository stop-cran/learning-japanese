# 偉い (えらい) — admirable; important

## Meaning

偉い can praise someone as admirable or commendable. It can also describe someone important or high-ranking.
These are different evaluations: admirable conduct and a position of importance need not go together.
As an い-adjective, it directly modifies a noun: 偉い人 (えらいひと).

- 毎日練習していて、偉いね。 (まいにちれんしゅうしていて、えらいね。) — It's admirable that you practise every day.
- あの人は会社で偉い人です。 (あのひとはかいしゃでえらいひとです。) — That person holds an important position at the company.

The first is praise in a familiar conversation; the second describes standing within an organization.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native adjective, not a compound of two kanji.
[偉](../kanji/偉.md) carries the idea of greatness or distinction, and the written い supplies the adjective ending.
Its adverbial form is 偉く (えらく).

## Synonyms and antonyms

立派 りっぱ can also mean praiseworthy or impressive, but it uses な before a noun:
立派な人 (りっぱなひと).
偉大 いだい means great or outstanding, as in 偉大な人 (いだいなひと).
Neither comparison makes admiration a statement about someone's physical size.

## Distinctive meaning

Read 偉い in context rather than assigning one moral meaning to every occurrence.
偉い人 may mean an important person, not necessarily a kind person.
Other senses of えらい exist, but the dictionary marks several of them as usually kana;
the examples here teach the admiration and status senses of 偉い.
