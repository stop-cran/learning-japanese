# ふわり — softly and gently

## Meaning

ふわり describes a gentle, soft, floating movement or sensation.

- 花びらが一枚、水面にふわりと落ちた。
  — A petal drifted softly down onto the water.
- カーテンが風を受けて、ふわりと揺れた。
  — The curtain swayed gently in the breeze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ふわり emphasizes a soft, graceful movement; it does not suggest a heavy impact.

## Distinctive meaning

It often appears with と to depict one gentle, floating motion.
