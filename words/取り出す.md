# 取り出す (とりだす) - take out; pick out; retrieve

## Meaning

取り出す means to take something out, pick it out from among other
things, or fetch and retrieve it. It is a transitive godan verb.
The object takes を; から can identify its source.

- かばんから財布を取り出しました。 (かばんからさいふをとりだしました。) - I took my wallet out of my bag.
- 必要な資料を取り出してください。 (ひつようなしりょうをとりだしてください。) - Please retrieve the necessary materials.

Forms include 取り出します (とりだします), 取り出して
(とりだして), and 取り出さない (とりださない).
The source may be a container, a collection, or a storage system.

## How the kanji combine

[取](../kanji/取.md) supplies 取り, associated with taking;
[出](../kanji/出.md) supplies 出す (だす), "put out."
The combination presents taking something out, not the
intransitive act of the thing coming out by itself.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出す](出す.md) (だす) is broader taking or putting out.
[引き出す](引き出す.md) (ひきだす) can emphasize drawing out,
including withdrawing money. [取り入れる](取り入れる.md)
(とりいれる) instead concerns taking in or adopting.

## Distinctive meaning

English "produce" can mean bringing something out to show it.
In that use, 取り出す does not say it was manufactured.
The second example could refer to finding and retrieving stored
documents; it does not necessarily describe making new ones.
