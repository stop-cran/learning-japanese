# 円周 (えんしゅう) - circumference

## Meaning

円周 is the circumference of a circle: its boundary, or the length
around that boundary. It is a noun used in geometry and measurement.

- この円の円周は約三十センチです。
  (このえんのえんしゅうはやくさんじゅっセンチです。) - This circle's circumference is about thirty centimetres.
- ひもで円周の長さを測りました。
  (ひもでえんしゅうのながさをはかりました。) - I measured the length of the circumference with string.

の links the circle with its circumference. In the second example,
で identifies the instrument used to measure it.

## How the kanji combine

[円](../kanji/円.md) contributes a circle, and
[周](../kanji/周.md) its extent around the outside.
The circle specifies what kind of perimeter is meant: エン + シュウ.
円 has its circle meaning here, not its currency meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[周囲](周囲.md) (しゅうい) and [周り](周り.md) (まわり)
can also refer to a circumference, as well as surroundings.
円周 is specifically tied to a circle; it is not the general word
for the neighbourhood around a station.

## Distinctive meaning

直径 (ちょっけい), diameter, measures across a circle through its
centre; 円周 concerns going around it. Likewise, a circumference is
not an area. Keep the measured quantity separate from the circle's
surface and from the everyday surroundings sense of 周り.
