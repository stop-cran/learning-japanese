# 連絡 (れんらく) — contact

## Meaning

連絡 means contact or communication. 連絡する means to get in touch or pass on information. The person contacted normally takes に.

明日、先生に連絡します。（あした、せんせいにれんらくします。） — I will contact the teacher tomorrow.

何かあったら連絡してください。（なにかあったられんらくしてください。） — Please contact me if anything happens.

## How the kanji combine

[連](../kanji/連.md) suggests linking or connecting, and 絡 also concerns connection. Their on readings form れん + らく. Learn the whole compound as communication rather than a physical knot.

## Synonyms and antonyms

電話する（でんわする） specifically means to telephone. 連絡する is broader: the communication can be by phone, message, email, or another method.

## Distinctive meaning

連絡がある means that a message or contact comes; 連絡を取る（れんらくをとる） means to get in touch. It does not necessarily describe a long conversation or a close personal relationship.
