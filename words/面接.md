# 面接 (めんせつ) - interview

## Meaning

面接 is an interview, especially one for employment, admission, or
selection. It is a noun and can take する; JMdict marks the verb intransitive.

- 明日は会社の面接を受けます。 (あしたはかいしゃのめんせつをうけます。) - I will have a job interview tomorrow.
- 面接で志望理由を聞かれました。 (めんせつでしぼうりゆうをきかれました。) - I was asked my reason for applying in the interview.

An applicant can say 面接を受ける (めんせつをうける), "undergo an
interview." The person conducting it can be the subject of 面接する:
役員が面接する。 (やくいんがめんせつする。) - The executives conduct
the interview. The verb alone does not invariably identify the applicant.

## How the kanji combine

[面](../kanji/面.md) suggests facing someone, and
[接](../kanji/接.md) contact or meeting. メン + セツ gives めんせつ;
keep せつ here rather than importing the small っ from [接近](接近.md) (せっきん).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[インタビュー](インタビュー.md) commonly concerns an interview for
reporting or a broadcast. 面接 is the useful ordinary choice for a job
interview. [試験](試験.md) (しけん) is an examination or test;
an interview can be part of a selection process without being a written test.

## Distinctive meaning

The job/selection association is a tendency, not a ban on every other
kind of interview. Larger dictionaries also describe directly meeting
someone for questioning or advice. The word itself does not specify
that the applicant succeeded.
