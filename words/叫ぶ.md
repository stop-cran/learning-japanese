# 叫ぶ (さけぶ) - shout; cry out

## Meaning

叫ぶ is a godan verb for shouting, screaming, or crying out. JMdict classifies the vocal-action use as intransitive. Quote the words shouted with と; the person addressed can take に. Its forms include 叫びます, 叫んで, and 叫んだ.

- 「危ない！」と叫びました。 (「あぶない！」とさけびました。) - I shouted, "Watch out!"
- 彼は大声で助けを求めて叫びました。 (かれはおおごえでたすけをもとめてさけびました。) - He cried out loudly for help.

The word focuses on a forceful voice, not merely speaking audibly.

## How the kanji combine

叫 writes the native stem さけ-, and ぶ is the verb ending. There is no multi-kanji compound. Notice the sound change to ん in 叫んで and 叫んだ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[泣く](泣く.md) (なく) means "cry; weep." English "cry" can translate both verbs, but 叫ぶ does not require tears. [話す](話す.md) (はなす) means speaking or talking without specifying a shout.

## Distinctive meaning

叫ぶ also appears in forceful public appeals: 平和を叫ぶ (へいわをさけぶ), "call for peace." In that construction, を marks the cause being advocated, not the person shouted at. Thus the verb can describe vocal urgency or emphatic advocacy, rather than anger alone.
