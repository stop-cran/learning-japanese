# 返る (かえる) - return; turn over

## Meaning

返る is an intransitive godan verb for returning or coming back. The returning thing takes が.

- 貸した本が返ってきました。 (かしたほんがかえってきました。) - The book I lent out came back.
- 我に返りました。 (われにかえりました。) - I came to my senses.

The second describes a return to one's normal awareness, not a trip home. Forms include 返ります (かえります), 返らない (かえらない) and 返って (かえって); do not conjugate it like ichidan [変える](変える.md).

The dictionary also records an intransitive "turn over" sense. For that sense, recognize the spelling 反る (かえる), without assuming every written 反る has this reading.

## How the kanji combine

[返](../kanji/返.md) contributes return; る is the ending of this single-kanji verb. Alternate [反](../kanji/反.md) provides a reversal association, but the complete spelling and reading must be learned together.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[返す](返す.md) (かえす) is transitive: 本を返す (ほんをかえす), "return a book," versus 本が返る, "a book comes back." [帰る](帰る.md) (かえる) is the practical default for going home; [戻る](戻る.md) (もどる) more broadly describes going back or returning to an earlier place or state.

[裏返す](裏返す.md) (うらがえす) means turn something over or inside out. Its object pattern does not automatically apply to intransitive 返る.

## Distinctive meaning

There is a separate verb **反る (そる)**, "warp; curve; arch; bend backward." Do not attach that meaning to the reading かえる merely because the kanji match. Conversely, recognizing 反る at かえる does not make it the everyday spelling for going home.

For the wider spelling and conjugation contrast, see [Kaeru: change, replace, exchange, and return](../articles/kaeru-change-replace-and-return.md).
