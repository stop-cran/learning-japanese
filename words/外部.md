# 外部 (がいぶ) - outside; exterior

## Meaning

外部 is the outside or exterior of something. It can also mean outside an organization, group or system.

- 外部から建物を調べました。 (がいぶからたてものをしらべました。) - We examined the building from the outside.
- 外部の専門家に相談しました。 (がいぶのせんもんかにそうだんしました。) - We consulted an outside specialist.

The specialist need not be outdoors: 外部 here means not belonging to the organization in question.

## How the kanji combine

[外](../kanji/外.md) means outside and [部](../kanji/部.md) a part or section. 外 modifies 部, giving "outside part": ガイ + ブ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[外](外.md) (そと) is the everyday outside noun. [屋外](屋外.md) (おくがい) specifically concerns being outdoors or outside a building. 内部 (ないぶ) is the corresponding inside or internal part.

## Distinctive meaning

Use noun grammar: 外部の人 (がいぶのひと), "someone from outside," not an English-shaped 外部な人. Physical and organizational boundaries are both possible; 外部 does not itself mean foreign or from another country.
