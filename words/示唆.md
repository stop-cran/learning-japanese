# 示唆 (しさ) — suggestion; hint; implication

## Meaning

示唆 means a suggestion or hint that points toward an idea or possibility without stating it directly. It can be used with する.

- 調査結果は、新しい対策が必要なことを示唆しています。 (ちょうさけっかは、あたらしいたいさくがひつようなことをしさしています。) — The survey results suggest that new measures are needed.
- 彼の発言は計画の変更を示唆していました。 (かれのはつげんはけいかくのへんこうをしさしていました。) — His remarks hinted at a change in plans.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[示](../kanji/示.md) means to show or indicate. In 示唆, [唆](../kanji/唆.md) is read サ, giving the compound reading しさ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

提案 (ていあん) is a proposal that puts forward an idea or plan; 示唆 can point indirectly to an idea without proposing a specific action.

## Distinctive meaning

示唆 points indirectly to an idea or possibility; it does not necessarily state a firm conclusion.
