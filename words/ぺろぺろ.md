# ぺろぺろ — licking repeatedly

## Meaning

Repeated tongue movements, especially licking or lapping.

- 猫が前足（まえあし）をぺろぺろなめている。 — The cat is licking its front paw.
- 子どもがアイスをぺろぺろなめた。 — The child licked the ice cream.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぺろり](ぺろり.md) can present a lick or tongue movement as one quick action. It also overlaps with ぺろぺろ in eating something up quickly.

## Distinctive meaning

ぺろぺろなめる makes the repetition clear. It does not mean “speak fluently”: that is a use of the similar-looking [ぺらぺら](ぺらぺら.md). The quick-eating sense is broader than a literal series of licks.
