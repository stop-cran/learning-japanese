# 続々 (ぞくぞく) - in succession; one after another

## Meaning

続々 describes things happening or appearing successively, one after another.
It is an adverb; 続々と is also used.
People arriving, reports appearing and new products coming out are useful settings.

- 会場に人が続々と集まってきました。 (かいじょうにひとがぞくぞくとあつまってきました。) - People kept arriving at the venue one after another.
- 新しい情報が続々と入ってきます。 (あたらしいじょうほうがぞくぞくとはいってきます。) - New information keeps coming in.

The emphasis is on a continuing succession, not a numbered turn-taking procedure.

## How the kanji combine

[続](../kanji/続.md) contributes continuing or following.
々 repeats the preceding character; 続続 is another dictionary spelling,
not a different word. The repeated Chinese-derived element appears as ぞくぞく.
Learn that established whole reading rather than automatically voicing every
compound containing 続.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[次々](次々.md) (つぎつぎ) also means one after another.
[連続](連続.md) (れんぞく) names continuity or a succession.
[順々](順々.md) (じゅんじゅん) emphasizes order or turns, which 続々 does not
by itself specify. The words overlap, but their constructions and emphases differ.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse this word with [ぞくぞく](ぞくぞく.md), the shivering or
thrilled sensation. A shared reading does not give 続々 the meaning "feel a chill."
Likewise, 続々と人が来る says that people keep coming; it does not specify
an exact arrival rate or require that they stand in a line.
