# まごまご - be flustered; be at a loss

## Meaning

まごまご describes being confused, flustered, or unsure what to do. It is a mimetic adverb and is also used with する.

- 初めての駅で、まごまごしてしまった。 (はじめてのえきで、まごまごしてしまった。) - At the unfamiliar station, I got flustered and did not know what to do.
- まごまごしていると、電車に乗り遅れますよ。 (まごまごしていると、でんしゃにのりおくれますよ。) - If you keep dithering, you will miss the train.

Use まごまごする or まごまごしている for the behavior or state. Do not attach it directly before a noun as though it were an ordinary い-adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[うろうろ](うろうろ.md) can describe aimless movement and also bewilderment. There is real overlap: this is not a strict mental-versus-physical division.

[あわてる](あわてる.md) means become flustered or hurry; [困る](困る.md) (こまる) can describe being in difficulty or at a loss. [ぐずぐず](ぐずぐず.md) can emphasize dawdling or hesitation.

## Distinctive meaning

まごまご focuses on confused, ineffective hesitation rather than merely walking around or deliberately taking time to think. It can sound impatient when used to criticize someone else's delay; the situation and tone matter.
