# グランド - grand; large

## Meaning

The selected グランド identity means "grand; large." The dictionary marks it as a modifier used before a noun. Learn it through established compounds rather than assuming it behaves like a freely usable な-adjective.

- この店のグランドオープンは来月です。 (このみせのグランドオープンはらいげつです。) - This shop's grand opening is next month.
- ホールにグランドピアノがあります。 - There is a grand piano in the hall.

グランドオープン is an established Japanese expression for a grand opening. グランドピアノ names a particular piano type; it does not mean any piano that happens to be large.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword has no kanji components. In the selected identity, it corresponds to English "grand." The entire compounds above have established meanings and should not be reconstructed solely from the modifier.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大きい](大きい.md) (おおきい) is an ordinary adjective meaning "big." [立派](立派.md) (りっぱ) can express impressiveness or excellence. Neither is automatically substitutable inside a fixed compound such as グランドピアノ.

## Distinctive meaning

A separate noun, グラウンド or グランド, means a sports ground or playing field.
学校のグラウンド (がっこうのグラウンド) is a school's sports ground.
That is a different word from the "grand" modifier taught above.

The community list also supplies "gland" and "electrical ground," but its intended identities remain uncertain. Those glosses are not asserted as meanings of the "grand" modifier, nor does this card rule out every specialized technical use.
