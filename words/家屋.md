# 家屋 (かおく) - house; building

## Meaning

家屋 means a house or building viewed as a physical structure. It is useful in descriptions of construction, property and building damage.

- 台風で多くの家屋が被害を受けました。 (たいふうでおおくのかおくがひがいをうけました。) - Many houses were damaged by the typhoon.
- この地域には古い木造の家屋が残っています。 (このちいきにはふるいもくぞうのかおくがのこっています。) - Old wooden houses remain in this area.

家屋 does not itself specify one building versus several; the first example obtains the quantity from 多くの.

## How the kanji combine

[家](../kanji/家.md) and [屋](../kanji/屋.md) both contribute house/building meanings. They reinforce one another: カ + オク. This compound does not use the standalone reading いえ or the shop-suffix reading や.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[家](家.md) (いえ) is the everyday house/home word and can also name a household. [建物](建物.md) (たてもの) is a general physical-building noun. [ビル](ビル.md) commonly concerns a multistory building.

## Distinctive meaning

家屋 focuses on the structure, not family members or the emotional meaning of "home." It is more useful in a damage report than as the default way to say "I'm going home." Do not infer that every 家屋 must be detached or that nonresidential buildings are automatically excluded.
