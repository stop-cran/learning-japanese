# 劣る (おとる) - be inferior; fall short

## Meaning

劣る is an intransitive godan verb meaning to be inferior or less good in some respect.
In AはBに劣る, B is the comparison standard. A quality or field can be marked with で:
品質で劣る (ひんしつでおとる), "be inferior in quality."

- この製品は品質で他社の製品に劣ります。 (このせいひんはひんしつでたしゃのせいひんにおとります。) - This product is inferior in quality to other companies' products.
- 安くても、性能は劣りません。 (やすくても、せいのうはおとりません。) - Even though it is inexpensive, its performance is not inferior.

Useful forms are 劣っている (おとっている), "is inferior," and 劣らない (おとらない), "is no worse."

## How the kanji combine

劣 expresses inferiority in this native verb. The dictionary-form ending is る, but its godan negative
is 劣らない, not 劣ない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[優れる](優れる.md) (すぐれる), "excel; be superior," gives the main evaluative contrast.
[負ける](負ける.md) (まける) can mean losing a competition; 劣る compares qualities without requiring a contest.
[遅れる](遅れる.md) (おくれる) can describe lateness, which 劣る does not express by itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Specify the dimension of comparison when possible. Saying that a product is inferior in durability is more precise
than declaring it inferior in every way.
The negative 劣らない can convey favorable comparison: "not inferior" does not necessarily mean the two things are identical.
