# 運転手 (うんてんしゅ) - driver

## Meaning

運転手 is a driver, especially someone whose job is driving a taxi, bus, truck, or similar vehicle.

- 父はバスの運転手です。 (ちちはバスのうんてんしゅです。) - My father is a bus driver.
- 運転手さんに行き先を伝えました。 (うんてんしゅさんにいきさきをつたえました。) - I told the driver my destination.

Use vehicle or service + の + 運転手 to specify the kind of driver. さん can make reference to or address of the person more courteous.

## How the kanji combine

[運](../kanji/運.md) conveys carrying or operating, [転](../kanji/転.md) turning, and [手](../kanji/手.md) a person performing a role. 運転 means driving or operation; 手 here is read しゅ, not て.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ドライバー is another word for a driver. 乗客 (じょうきゃく), "passenger," names the contrasting role rather than an opposite action.

## Distinctive meaning

運転手 names the person, not the activity. Say 運転する (うんてんする) for "drive." In a job description, 運転手です says what someone does for a living; simply being able to drive does not make that one's occupation.
