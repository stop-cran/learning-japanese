# 物差し (ものさし) - ruler; yardstick

## Meaning

物差し is a ruler or measuring stick.
Figuratively, it is a yardstick or criterion used to judge something.
物差しで測る (ものさしではかる) means to measure with a ruler.

- 物差しで線の長さを測りました。 (ものさしでせんのながさをはかりました。) - I measured the length of the line with a ruler.
- お金だけを物差しにして、成功を判断してはいけません。 (おかねだけをものさしにして、せいこうをはんだんしてはいけません。) - You should not judge success using money as the only yardstick.

The first example uses で for the instrument.
In **Nを物差しにする**, N is adopted as a standard of judgment.

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md) contributes things, and [差](../kanji/差.md)
writes the さし element in this measuring-tool word.
The native reading is もの + さし.
The characters offer a recognition aid, not a historical derivation
from the modern English gloss "difference."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[定規](定規.md) (じょうぎ) overlaps for a ruler.
[基準](基準.md) (きじゅん) and [標準](標準.md) (ひょうじゅん)
compare with the figurative standard.
[目安](目安.md) (めやす) can be a practical guide or approximate benchmark.

## Distinctive meaning

A figurative 物差し need not be an exact numerical measurement.
It identifies what a judgment is based on, not merely the size of the result.
English "ruler" here means a measuring tool, not a monarch.
