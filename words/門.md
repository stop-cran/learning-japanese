# 門 (もん) — gate

## Meaning

門 means a gate, especially an entrance through a wall or boundary around a school, temple, house, or other grounds.

学校の門の前で待っています。（がっこうのもんのまえでまっています。） — I am waiting in front of the school gate.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji noun using the on reading もん. There are no compound parts or okurigana. Other readings in names or fixed expressions should be learned separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

入口（いりぐち） is an entrance more generally. ドア is a door, such as the door to a room, and does not automatically mean a gate.

## Distinctive meaning

門 can mean the gate structure as well as the opening used to pass through it. 正門（せいもん）, "main gate," is useful when arranging where to meet. 門 is not the general label for every indoor doorway.
