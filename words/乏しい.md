# 乏しい (とぼしい) — scarce; meagre

## Meaning

乏しい describes an insufficient or meagre supply of something, including
people with needed skills or experience. It also has a financial sense:
being poor or having little to live on.

- 人材が乏しい。 (じんざいがとぼしい。) — There is a shortage of capable people.
- 乏しい経験 (とぼしいけいけん) — limited experience
- 乏しい生活 (とぼしいせいかつ) — an impoverished life

It is an い-adjective: 乏しい goes directly before a noun, without な.
The negative is 乏しくない (とぼしくない), "not scarce," and the past is
乏しかった (とぼしかった), "was scarce." Use 乏しいです for a polite predicate.

## How the kanji combine

[乏](../kanji/乏.md) supplies the insufficiency meaning. This is a native
adjective, とぼしい, with しい written as okurigana; it is not a two-kanji
compound. Dictionaries also recognize the reading ともしい for this spelling.
This article uses とぼしい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不足](不足.md) (ふそく), "shortage; insufficiency," overlaps in meaning but
has different grammar: compare 人材が乏しい with 人材が不足している
(じんざいがふそくしている), "there is a shortage of capable people."
[豊富](豊富.md) (ほうふ), "abundant; plentiful," gives the opposite
quantity contrast: 豊富な経験 (ほうふなけいけん), "extensive experience,"
uses な, unlike 乏しい経験.

[貧しい](貧しい.md) (まずしい) and [貧乏](貧乏.md) (びんぼう) overlap
with its poverty sense. 貧しい can also concern inadequate quantity or
quality; do not treat it as exclusively about money.

## Distinctive meaning

乏しい is about insufficiency, not necessarily complete absence.
[少ない](少ない.md) (すくない) describes a small number or amount;
乏しい emphasizes that the supply is limited or inadequate. Their meanings
can overlap, so this is not a rule forbidding 少ない in shortage contexts.
In the financial use, the word describes circumstances, not a person's worth.
