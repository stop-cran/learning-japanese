# 備える (そなえる) — equip; prepare for

## Meaning

備える means to provide equipment or make provision for something.
Two useful patterns are **item を備える**, "provide the item," and **event に備える**, "prepare for the event."
It is an ichidan verb: remove る for 備えます (そなえます), and use 備えて (そなえて) to connect to another action.

- 家に消火器を備えました。 (いえにしょうかきをそなえました。) — We equipped our home with a fire extinguisher.
- 災害に備えて、水を用意しています。 (さいがいにそなえて、みずをよういしています。) — We are keeping water ready in preparation for a disaster.

In the first example, に marks the place equipped and を marks the equipment.
In the second, に marks the event being prepared for.

## How the kanji combine

[備](../kanji/備.md) supplies preparation or equipment; える is the written verb ending.
The native reading そなえる belongs to the whole verb, rather than an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[準備](準備.md)する (じゅんびする) means to prepare or make arrangements and covers many kinds of getting ready.
備わる (そなわる) describes being equipped or endowed with something.
It is not simply a different conjugation of 備える.

## Distinctive meaning

備える directs attention to provision: having equipment, resources, or preparations in place for a need.
災害に備える does not mean that you cause or arrange a disaster; it means making yourself ready for one.
Do not assign a single English "prepare" pattern to both the equipment and event constructions.
