# 人込み (ひとごみ) - crowd of people

## Meaning

人込み is a crowd of people, with attention to people gathered densely in a place. It is a noun: 人込みの中 (ひとごみのなか) means "in the crowd."

- 駅前の人込みで友達を見失いました。 (えきまえのひとごみでともだちをみうしないました。) - I lost sight of my friend in the crowd outside the station.
- 人込みを避けて、別の道を通りました。 (ひとごみをさけて、べつのみちをとおりました。) - I took another road to avoid the crowd.

で gives the setting in the first sentence; を marks what is avoided in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md), ひと, supplies people. [込](../kanji/込.md) contributes crowding or being packed in, with こみ voiced to ごみ in this compound. The recorded spellings 人ごみ and 人混み represent the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

群衆 (ぐんしゅう) also means a crowd or mass of people. 人込み particularly helps describe the crowded surroundings one is moving through. 人の少ない場所 (ひとのすくないばしょ), "a place with few people," supplies a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The ごみ here is not the noun meaning rubbish. Do not interpret 人込み as an insult about people. It also does not establish why the crowd formed: commuters, shoppers, or festival visitors can all create 人込み. The focus is the dense gathering, not a shared intention.
