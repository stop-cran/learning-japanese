# 覚ます (さます) - awaken; bring out of sleep

## Meaning

覚ます is a transitive godan verb meaning to awaken or bring out of sleep. Learn the expression 目を覚ます (めをさます), "wake up," with its object を.

- 大きな音で目を覚ましました。 (おおきなおとでめをさましました。) - I woke up because of a loud noise.
- 赤ちゃんが昼寝から目を覚ましました。 (あかちゃんがひるねからめをさましました。) - The baby woke up from a nap.

Although English "wake up" is intransitive here, Japanese 目を覚ます contains the object 目. The past is 覚ました (さました), and the negative is 覚まさない (さまさない).

## How the kanji combine

[覚](../kanji/覚.md) carries the awareness or awakening meaning; ます is part of this verb's dictionary form, not the polite ending by itself. The polite form is 覚まします (さまします).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[覚める](覚める.md) (さめる) is intransitive: 目が覚める (めがさめる), "wake up."

[起きる](起きる.md) (おきる) can describe waking or getting up. Someone may wake but remain in bed.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the particle distinction: 目を覚ます versus 目が覚める. Do not assume the transitive label means that 目を覚ます must wake another person. Also distinguish 冷ます (さます), "cool something down," despite its identical pronunciation. This card focuses on awakening, not every figurative use of 覚ます.
