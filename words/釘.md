# 釘 (くぎ) - nail; fastening spike

## Meaning

釘 is a nail or spike used for fastening, usually a pointed metal piece driven into material such as wood. The word also covers small nail-like pins, including pins in a pachinko machine.

- 板を釘で留めました。 (いたをくぎでとめました。) - I fastened the board with nails.
- 壁の釘に絵を掛けました。 (かべのくぎにえをかけました。) - I hung a picture on a nail in the wall.

釘を打つ (くぎをうつ) means drive in a nail. In the first example, で marks the fastening material or means.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun, 釘 read くぎ, not an on-reading compound. The character has no kanji card in the current collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ピン](ピン.md) overlaps as a pin, but covers several objects beyond nails. A screw is ねじ: it is not simply another name for every 釘. The body's fingernail or toenail is 爪 (つめ), a different word despite English "nail."

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the physical fastener first. English "nail" does not establish a body-part meaning for 釘, and the pachinko-pin use does not make every device called a pin a nail.
