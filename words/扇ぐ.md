# 扇ぐ (あおぐ) - to fan

## Meaning

扇ぐ means making a current of air by fanning. It is a transitive godan verb ending in ぐ:
扇ぎます (あおぎます), 扇いで (あおいで), 扇いだ (あおいだ).

- うちわで顔を扇ぎました。 (うちわでかおをあおぎました。) - I fanned my face with a handheld fan.
- 暑かったので、手で扇いでいました。 (あつかったので、てであおいでいました。) - It was hot, so I was fanning myself with my hand.

で marks the means, such as a fan or one's hand; を can mark what is being fanned.

## How the kanji combine

扇 is the fan character, and ぐ completes this native verb. Learn the whole word あおぐ;
the spelling does not call for an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

風を送る (かぜをおくる), "send a current of air," describes the effect.
冷やす (ひやす), "cool something," is broader: fanning is only one possible method.
There is no single useful opposite verb.

## Distinctive meaning

This is not a general verb for anything that flaps. A bird flapping its wings does not by itself
establish the action described by 扇ぐ. The dictionary also records a figurative inciting sense,
but the physical examples here concern moving air.
