# 日課 (にっか) - daily routine; daily work

## Meaning

日課 is a noun for an activity done as part of a daily routine, daily work
or an assigned daily lesson. It can describe a personal practice, not just schoolwork.

- 毎朝の散歩が日課です。 (まいあさのさんぽがにっかです。) - A walk every morning is part of my daily routine.
- 寝る前に本を読むのを日課にしています。 (ねるまえにほんをよむのをにっかにしています。) - I make it a daily practice to read before bed.

Nを日課にする means to make N a daily practice. A verb phrase can be
turned into that N with の, as in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md), "day," modifies [課](../kanji/課.md), an assigned
lesson or task: a daily task or practice.
This on-reading compound has the whole reading にっか, with a small っ,
not にちか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[習慣](習慣.md) (しゅうかん), "habit; custom," overlaps with a repeated
personal practice, but habits need not be daily.
[日常](日常.md) (にちじょう) is broader everyday life or routine.
[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) and [作業](作業.md) (さぎょう) can name work or
tasks without specifying a daily recurrence.

## Distinctive meaning

日課 is the activity or recurring work, not simply the frequency word
[毎日](毎日.md) (まいにち), "every day."
It can be chosen, customary or assigned; the character 課 does not
make every 日課 an official school lesson or an unpleasant obligation.
