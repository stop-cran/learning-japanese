# 幸福 (こうふく) — happiness; well-being

## Meaning

幸福 means happiness or well-being. It can be a noun, as in 幸福を願う (こうふくをねがう), "wish for happiness,"
or modify another noun with な, as in 幸福な生活 (こうふくなせいかつ), "a happy life."

- 家族の幸福を願っています。 (かぞくのこうふくをねがっています。) — I wish for my family's happiness.
- 幸福な生活を送りたいです。 (こうふくなせいかつをおくりたいです。) — I want to lead a happy life.

## How the kanji combine

[幸](../kanji/幸.md) contributes happiness or good fortune, and [福](../kanji/福.md) contributes blessing or good fortune.
Their related meanings reinforce a favorable condition in life.
The on readings combine as コウ + フク; this is not a sequence of two independent adjectives.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[幸せ](幸せ.md) (しあわせ) also means happiness or good fortune and overlaps with 幸福.
[嬉しい](うれしい.md) (うれしい) expresses being glad or pleased; it is an い-adjective, so its grammar differs from 幸福な.
不幸 (ふこう), "unhappiness; misfortune," is a useful contrast to 幸福.

## Distinctive meaning

幸福 can name the happiness or well-being that someone hopes for, rather than just a reaction to one piece of news.
The family example is a wish for that person's life to go well.
Do not infer a fixed rule that 幸福 must last a long time or that 幸せ can only describe a brief feeling; their meanings overlap.
