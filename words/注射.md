# 注射 (ちゅうしゃ) — injection

## Meaning

注射 is an injection or shot. 注射する means to give an injection; 注射を受ける (ちゅうしゃをうける) clearly describes receiving one.

- 病院で注射を受けました。 (びょういんでちゅうしゃをうけました。) — I received an injection at the hospital.
- 看護師が患者に薬を注射しました。 (かんごしがかんじゃにくすりをちゅうしゃしました。) — The nurse injected the medicine into the patient.

In the second example, に marks the recipient and を marks what is injected. 注射をする is another common way to express the action.

## How the kanji combine

[注](../kanji/注.md) contributes pouring or administering, and 射 contributes projecting or sending in. The on-readings are チュウ + シャ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

予防接種 (よぼうせっしゅ) means vaccination, a purpose-specific medical procedure rather than every injection. 薬 (くすり), "medicine," names the substance, not the method of giving it.

## Distinctive meaning

注射 does not by itself mean vaccine: injections can have other purposes. For beginner conversation about being the patient, 注射を受ける avoids ambiguity about who performs the procedure.
