# 放る (ほうる) - throw; neglect; abandon

## Meaning

放る is a transitive godan verb: throw or toss something, or leave something alone, neglected, or unfinished. The object takes を.

- ボールを遠くへ放った。 (ボールをとおくへほうった。) - I threw the ball a long way.
- 仕事を途中で放ってはいけません。 (しごとをとちゅうでほうってはいけません。) - You must not abandon the work halfway through.

With the reading ほうる, the forms are 放ります (ほうります), 放って (ほうって), and 放らない (ほうらない). 放っておく (ほうっておく) means to leave something or someone alone; whether that is welcome or negligent depends on context.

## How the kanji combine

[放](../kanji/放.md) contributes releasing or letting go, and る completes the native verb. Its on reading ホウ is not an explanation of this verb's conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[投げる](投げる.md) (なげる) also means throw and can describe abandoning work. [怠る](怠る.md) (おこたる) highlights neglecting a duty; [捨てる](捨てる.md) (すてる) is throwing away or discarding.

[放す](放す.md) (はなす), "release; let go," is a different verb with a different reading. It is not interchangeable with every throwing or neglecting use of 放る.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the actual throwing and leaving-alone senses rather than treating the source gloss "let go" as an unrestricted translation. JMdict also records ほる for this verb and rare 抛る. Its search-only 投る form is not a spelling to adopt for ordinary production.
