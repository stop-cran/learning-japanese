# 反省 (はんせい) - self-reflection; remorse

## Meaning

反省 is reflection on oneself, especially reviewing one's past conduct
or faults. 反省する is transitive: what is reviewed can take を.
The word can also express regret or remorse.

- 自分の行動を反省しています。 (じぶんのこうどうをはんせいしています。) - I am reflecting on my behavior.
- 同じ失敗を繰り返さないように反省しました。 (おなじしっぱいをくりかえさないようにはんせいしました。) - I reflected on my mistake so as not to repeat it.

反省している describes ongoing reflection or remorse.
反省点 (はんせいてん) identifies a point to reflect on or improve.

## How the kanji combine

[反](../kanji/反.md) contributes turning back, and
[省](../kanji/省.md) contributes examining oneself here.
The compound suggests looking back over oneself or one's conduct.
Read ハン + セイ. The ministry reading ショウ of 省 does not
belong in はんせい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

省みる (かえりみる) also means reflect on oneself or one's conduct.
[悔やむ](悔やむ.md) (くやむ) overlaps in regret; feeling regret does
not necessarily describe a review of what to change.
[考える](考える.md) (かんがえる) is broader "think; consider."
There is no single antonym covering both introspection and remorse.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "meditation" and "contemplation" should be understood in
this self-review setting, not as every quiet mood or meditation exercise.
[謝る](謝る.md) (あやまる) means apologize: someone can reflect
without having said an apology, and an apology does not prove sincere reflection.

For light or a condition being reflected in something, use
[反映](反映.md) (はんえい), not this self-examining sense.
