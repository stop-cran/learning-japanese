# 成長 (せいちょう) - growth; development

## Meaning

成長 is a noun for growth or development. 成長する is intransitive:
the child, person, organization, or economy that grows takes が.
It can describe physical growth, maturation, or developing capability.

- 子供は一年で大きく成長しました。 (こどもはいちねんでおおきくせいちょうしました。) - The child grew a great deal in one year.
- この会社は急速に成長しています。 (このかいしゃはきゅうそくにせいちょうしています。) - This company is growing rapidly.

成長を見守る (せいちょうをみまもる) means watching over someone's
growth. To say that someone causes growth, 成長させる provides
the causative form; ordinary 成長する does not take that same
direct-object pattern.

## How the kanji combine

[成](../kanji/成.md) contributes becoming or developing, and
[長](../kanji/長.md) contributes growing or becoming greater.
The related ideas combine into growth. Their on readings are
せい + ちょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[育つ](育つ.md) (そだつ) describes growing or being brought up.
[育てる](育てる.md) (そだてる) is the transitive counterpart,
"raise; nurture." [増える](増える.md) (ふえる) concerns an increase
in amount or number, not necessarily maturation.

## Distinctive meaning

The subject tells you what sort of growth is intended. A child's
成長 may involve maturity as well as height; a company's 成長
concerns organizational or economic expansion. The word is not restricted
to reaching adulthood, and it does not name a fixed age threshold.
