# 農家 (のうか) - farmer; farming household

## Meaning

農家 is a farmer or farming household. Depending on context, it can identify the
people running a farm, the household as a unit, or a farmhouse.
It is a noun: 農家です means "is a farmer" or "is a farming family."

- この農家は米と野菜を作っています。
  (こののうかはこめとやさいをつくっています。)
  - This farming household grows rice and vegetables.
- 山の近くに古い農家が残っています。
  (やまのちかくにふるいのうかがのこっています。)
  - An old farmhouse remains near the mountain.

The first sentence describes producers; the second, through 古い and the location,
describes a building.

## How the kanji combine

農 contributes farming, and [家](../kanji/家.md) contributes a household or house.
The first character specifies the household's activity.
The on readings are ノウ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[農業](農業.md) (のうぎょう) names agriculture, the activity or industry.
[農民](農民.md) (のうみん) names people engaged in farming.
農家 can instead treat a farming household as one unit, so the words are not interchangeable in every statistic.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 農家 automatically as a physical house merely because it contains 家.
In news about crop production or farm income, it can refer to the people or enterprise.
Conversely, a sentence describing an old 農家's rooms is talking about the building.
