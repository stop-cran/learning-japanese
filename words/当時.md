# 当時 (とうじ) - at that time; in those days

## Meaning

当時 refers to the time or period under discussion, often a past period. It works adverbially and as a noun before の.

- 当時、私はまだ学生でした。 (とうじ、わたしはまだがくせいでした。) - At that time, I was still a student.
- これは当時の写真です。 (これはとうじのしゃしんです。) - This is a photograph from those days.

The reference time must be understood from the conversation or surrounding text. 当時は can contrast that period with a later one.

## How the kanji combine

[当](../kanji/当.md) contributes "the one in question" or "that particular," and [時](../kanji/時.md) contributes time. The compound points to the time being referred to. Its on readings are トウ + ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

その時 (そのとき) also means at that time and can point to a particular moment. 当時 readily frames the circumstances of an era or stage of life. [今](今.md) (いま), "now," provides a contrast when discussing how conditions have changed.

## Distinctive meaning

当時 does not specify a calendar date by itself. A speaker might mean their school years, the time a building opened, or the period of an event already mentioned. The second example concerns when the photograph belongs, not the present moment when someone is looking at it.
