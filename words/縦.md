# 縦 (たて) - vertical; lengthwise dimension

## Meaning

縦 names the vertical direction or dimension. Depending on the object's orientation, it can also mean its lengthwise or front-to-back dimension rather than a height standing above the ground.

- 紙を縦に折ってください。 (かみをたてにおってください。) - Please fold the paper lengthwise.
- 写真の縦は二十センチです。 (しゃしんのたてはにじゅっセンチです。) - The photograph is twenty centimeters high.

縦に describes orientation; 縦の線 (たてのせん) is "a vertical line."

## How the kanji combine

縦 is a single kanji representing the native noun たて. It is not the verb 立つ (たつ), "stand," even though vertical orientation can help you remember it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[横](横.md) (よこ) is the corresponding horizontal or side-to-side direction. 高さ (たかさ), "height," names a measurement but does not cover all the lengthwise uses of 縦.

## Distinctive meaning

Check how the object is oriented before translating 縦 as "height." A sheet lying flat still has a 縦 direction. It is not automatically the physically upright axis in every context.
