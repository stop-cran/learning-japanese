# 情報 (じょうほう) - information; intelligence

## Meaning

情報 is a noun for information, reports, or intelligence. It also refers to information represented in data or signals. Useful patterns include 情報を集める (じょうほうをあつめる), "gather information," and 情報を確認する (じょうほうをかくにんする), "check information."

- 台風についての情報を集めています。 (たいふうについてのじょうほうをあつめています。) - I am gathering information about the typhoon.
- 個人情報を入力しないでください。 (こじんじょうほうをにゅうりょくしないでください。) - Please do not enter personal information.

情報がある means information is available or someone has it; a sentence can specify whose information or which topic with の.

## How the kanji combine

[情](../kanji/情.md) concerns circumstances or the state of affairs here; [報](../kanji/報.md) concerns reporting or conveying. Together they name communicated knowledge about something, not simply "an emotional report."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知識](知識.md) (ちしき) is knowledge someone has acquired. 情報 can be a particular incoming report that has not yet been checked.

[知らせ](知らせ.md) (しらせ) is news or a notification; [連絡](連絡.md) (れんらく) concerns contact or communication.

## Distinctive meaning

情報 does not guarantee truth: 不正確な情報 (ふせいかくなじょうほう) is "inaccurate information." In 軍事情報 (ぐんじじょうほう), "military intelligence," intelligence means collected information, not someone's mental ability. Context determines the domain; the word is not restricted to computers or military matters.
