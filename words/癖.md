# 癖 (くせ) - habit; quirk

## Meaning

癖 is a habit or tendency, often an undesirable one, or a distinctive quirk.
It is a noun. A plain verb before 癖 identifies the repeated behavior:
爪をかむ癖 (つめをかむくせ), "a habit of biting one's nails."

- 考えるとき、髪を触る癖があります。 (かんがえるとき、かみをさわるくせがあります。) - I have a habit of touching my hair when I think.
- この人の字には癖があります。 (このひとのじにはくせがあります。) - This person's handwriting has a distinctive style.

癖がある describes possessing a habit or noticeable characteristic; 癖を直す
(くせをなおす) means correcting a habit.

## How the kanji combine

癖 supplies the whole native noun くせ. The same character also has the on reading
ヘキ in compounds, but that is not how to read the examples here. The kana forms
くせ and クセ are also recognized.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[習慣](習慣.md) (しゅうかん) is a habit or customary practice, including regular
routines. 癖 draws attention to a person's repeated tendency or a characteristic
feature. The meanings overlap; not every 癖 is morally bad.

## Distinctive meaning

The handwriting example concerns individuality, not an action the handwriting
repeatedly performs. The object and context distinguish a behavioral habit from
a characteristic quirk. Do not automatically translate 癖がある as "has a bad
habit" when discussing handwriting, a flavor, or another distinctive quality.
