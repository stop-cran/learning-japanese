# 会員 (かいいん) - member

## Meaning

会員 is a member of an association, club, or membership-based service.
It is a noun: Xの会員 means "a member of X," and 会員になる means "become a member."
Context determines whether an individual or the membership collectively is intended.

- このスポーツクラブの会員になりました。 (このスポーツクラブのかいいんになりました。) - I joined this sports club.
- 会員は無料で利用できます。 (かいいんはむりょうでりようできます。) - Members can use it free of charge.

The second sentence states a particular membership benefit; the word 会員 does not itself guarantee free access.

## How the kanji combine

[会](../kanji/会.md) contributes an association or organized gathering, and [員](../kanji/員.md) a member.
The first character identifies the kind of group whose member is meant.
The on-readings カイ + イン give かいいん; retain both い sounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[メンバー](メンバー.md) is a broader loanword for a member, including someone in a temporary team.
社員 (しゃいん) is a [company](会社.md) employee, not necessarily a member of a club or association.
非会員 (ひかいいん), "nonmember," is the direct membership-status contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

会員 focuses on belonging to a defined membership group, not merely being present at an event.
A customer may be a 会員, but buying something does not by itself establish membership.
Related notices include 会員登録 (かいいんとうろく), "membership registration," and 会員証 (かいいんしょう), "membership card."
