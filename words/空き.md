# 空き (あき) - vacancy; free space or time

## Meaning

空き is a noun for unoccupied space, a vacancy, or available time. 空きがある
means that space or an opening is available; 空きがない means there is none.
The surrounding noun tells you whether this concerns rooms, seats, appointments, or storage.

- この駐車場にはまだ空きがあります。
  (このちゅうしゃじょうにはまだあきがあります。) - This parking lot still has spaces available.
- 明日の午後、空きはありますか。
  (あしたのごご、あきはありますか。) - Do you have any availability tomorrow afternoon?

空き時間 (あきじかん) is free time between commitments. ディスクの空き容量
(ディスクのあきようりょう) means a disk's available storage capacity.

## How the kanji combine

[空](../kanji/空.md) conveys emptiness or unoccupied space. 空き is a noun related
to [空く](空く.md) (あく), "become empty; become available"; き completes its spelling.
There is no multi-kanji compound to split.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暇](暇.md) (ひま) also concerns free time, but does not describe a vacant parking space.
満席 (まんせき), "all seats occupied," is a contextual opposite when discussing seating.

## Distinctive meaning

空き focuses on capacity that is not occupied, rather than the activity of taking a rest.
An available appointment slot does not necessarily mean the person has nothing else to do.
In 空きはありますか, the type of opening can remain unstated when both speakers know it.
