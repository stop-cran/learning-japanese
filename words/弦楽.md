# 弦楽 (げんがく) — music for strings

## Meaning

弦楽 is music played on string instruments such as the violin and cello. It names the kind of music, not a single instrument.

- 休日に弦楽四重奏の演奏会へ行った。 (きゅうじつにげんがくしじゅうそうのえんそうかいへいった。) — On my day off I went to a string-quartet concert.
- この曲は、弦楽のために書かれた。 (このきょくは、げんがくのためにかかれた。) — This piece was written for strings.

## How the kanji combine

[弦](../kanji/弦.md) (げん) is a string, and [楽](../kanji/楽.md) (がく) is music. 弦楽器 (げんがっき), with 器 “instrument,” names the instruments themselves.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[音楽](音楽.md) (おんがく) is music in general. An [オーケストラ](オーケストラ.md) (おーけすとら) is the ensemble itself, and 管弦楽 (かんげんがく) is orchestral music. 弦楽 is music for strings.

## Distinctive meaning

Dictionaries also record the alternative spelling 絃楽 for the same word. In the first example, 弦楽四重奏 (げんがくしじゅうそう) is a string quartet.
