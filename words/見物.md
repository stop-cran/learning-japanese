# 見物 (けんぶつ) — sightseeing, watching

## Meaning

見物 is going to see something worth seeing, as a sight, show or event: 京都を見物する (go sightseeing in Kyoto), 花火見物 はなびけんぶつ (watching fireworks), 見物人 けんぶつにん (spectator).
In 見ものだ it also means "worth seeing": 今日の試合は見ものだ.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) (see) plus 物 (thing): "a thing to see", read with the on readings ケン + ブツ. The same spelling 見物 read みもの is a separate word meaning "something worth seeing".

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 観光 かんこう (tourism, more commercial), 見学 けんがく (observing for study, e.g. a factory).
- Related: 見る ([見る](見る.md)), 観覧 かんらん (viewing, formal).

## Distinctive meaning

見物 is about looking at things without taking part, like crowds at a festival. 観光 is travelling to see sights, and 見学 has a study purpose (工場見学, a factory visit).
