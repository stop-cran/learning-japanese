# 年齢 (ねんれい) - age

## Meaning

年齢 is a noun for age, especially a person's age expressed or considered as a number of years. It works in descriptions, forms, comparisons, and participation requirements.

- 申込書に名前と年齢を書いてください。 (もうしこみしょになまえとねんれいをかいてください。) - Please write your name and age on the application form.
- この講座は年齢に関係なく参加できます。 (このこうざはねんれいにかんけいなくさんかできます。) - You can join this course regardless of age.

年齢を聞く (ねんれいをきく) means "ask someone's age." Whether it is appropriate to ask is a matter of the relationship and purpose, not the noun's grammar.

## How the kanji combine

[年](../kanji/年.md) contributes years, and 齢 contributes age. The related meanings reinforce each other in the compound. Its on readings are ネン + レイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[年](年.md) (とし) can also mean age, but has other year-related uses. [歳](歳.md) (さい) is used with a number to state an age, as in 二十五歳 (にじゅうごさい), "twenty-five years old." 年齢 is the category, not a replacement age counter.

## Distinctive meaning

年齢が高い (ねんれいがたかい) means older or of a higher age; 年齢が低い means younger. 年齢 does not mean a historical era or the length of every object. A form asking 年齢 expects age, not a calendar year or date of birth unless requested separately.
