# 伸びる (のびる) - grow; stretch; improve

## Meaning

伸びる means to become longer, stretch, or grow, and also to develop or improve.
It is an intransitive ichidan verb: what grows takes が.
The polite form is 伸びます, the past 伸びた, and the negative 伸びない.

- 髪がずいぶん伸びました。 (かみがずいぶんのびました。) - My hair has grown quite a lot.
- 毎日練習して、泳ぐ力が伸びました。 (まいにちれんしゅうして、およぐちからがのびました。) - Through daily practice, my swimming ability improved.

身長が伸びる (しんちょうがのびる) means growing taller. The verb describes
the change, not an object that someone directly lengthens.

## How the kanji combine

伸 contributes stretching or growing. It is read の in this native verb, with
びる written in kana. The improvement meaning extends beyond physical length.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[伸ばす](伸ばす.md) (のばす) is the transitive partner: 髪を伸ばす means
letting or making one's hair grow longer, while 髪が伸びる describes its growth.
縮む (ちぢむ), "shrink," gives a physical opposite in suitable contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

The same-reading spelling 延びる shares some extension senses, but not every
sense transfers freely. The inspected dictionary assigns improvement to 伸びる,
and extension of a deadline or postponement to 延びる.
期限が延びた (きげんがのびた) means "the deadline was extended"; it does not
mean that the deadline improved. Compare [延期](延期.md) (えんき), "postponement."
