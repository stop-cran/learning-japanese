# 挙げる (あげる) - raise; cite an example

## Meaning

挙げる is a transitive ichidan verb with several uses: citing or listing examples,
raising a hand, holding a ceremony, and achieving a result.
Forms include 挙げます (あげます), 挙げて (あげて), and 挙げた (あげた).

- 例を二つ挙げてください。 (れいをふたつあげてください。) - Please give two examples.
- 二人は去年、結婚式を挙げました。 (ふたりはきょねん、けっこんしきをあげました。) - The two of them held their wedding ceremony last year.

Also learn 手を挙げる (てをあげる), "raise one's hand," and
成果を挙げる (せいかをあげる), "achieve results." The object helps identify the use.

## How the kanji combine

挙 has a raising association. With the kana ending げる it writes the native verb あげる.
That association helps with raising a hand, but does not by itself explain every established phrase.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上げる](上げる.md) overlaps in raising.
[揚げる](揚げる.md) covers deep-frying and sending up a kite or flag.
For example-giving, 例を[示す](示す.md) (れいをしめす), "show an example," is a useful related expression.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the object and spelling together rather than memorizing "raise" as an unrestricted rule.
For flying a kite, learn 凧を揚げる (たこをあげる); the English word "fly" alone is
not a reason to assign that use to 挙げる.
Other dictionary uses include arresting a culprit and nominating a candidate; the examples
here do not imply that every occurrence involves physically lifting something.
