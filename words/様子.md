# 様子 (ようす) — situation; appearance

## Meaning

様子 can describe how a situation is developing or how a person or thing appears and behaves.
It is useful when checking how someone is doing, rather than naming one isolated physical feature.

- 子供の様子を見に行きました。 (こどものようすをみにいきました。) — I went to see how the child was doing.
- 彼はいつもと様子が違います。 (かれはいつもとようすがちがいます。) — He seems different from usual.

Use noun + の + 様子 to identify whose condition or what situation is being observed.
In the second example, the difference might be in behaviour or manner, not just clothing.

## How the kanji combine

[様](../kanji/様.md) contributes manner, appearance, or situation.
[子](../kanji/子.md) is part of the established noun here; it does not mean that the subject must be a child.
Learn the on-reading compound as ようす, with す at the end rather than こ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[状態](状態.md) (じょうたい) also means a state or condition.
Both can be useful for describing how something is, but 様子 is particularly useful when describing how it looks or behaves to an observer.
Neither word has one general-purpose antonym: a good condition and a bad condition are both conditions.

## Distinctive meaning

様子 does not guarantee knowledge of someone's inner feelings.
An observed manner can suggest how a person is doing without establishing the cause.
The 様 in this word is descriptive; it is not the respectful name suffix 様 (さま).
