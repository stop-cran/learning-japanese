# 平均 (へいきん) - average; mean

## Meaning

平均 is an average or mean. It is a noun and can become the transitive verb 平均する, "average" a set of values. **平均を出す** (へいきんをだす) means "calculate an average."

- テストの平均点は七十点でした。 (テストのへいきんてんはななじゅってんでした。) - The average test score was seventy.
- 三回の記録を平均しました。 (さんかいのきろくをへいきんしました。) - We averaged the results of three attempts.

In a simple arithmetic average, add the values and divide by their number. **一日平均** (いちにちへいきん) means "an average per day," as in 一日平均二時間, "an average of two hours a day."

平均 also has a separate **balance; equilibrium** meaning. **平均を保つ** (へいきんをたもつ) means "keep one's balance," not "maintain a numerical average" in this use. Recognize the same balance meaning in 平均台 (へいきんだい), a gymnastics balance beam. For balance or equilibrium, 平均する is intransitive, unlike the transitive "average the values" use above.

## How the kanji combine

[平](../kanji/平.md) contributes levelness or evenness; 均 contributes uniformity or equality. Together they give a useful image of evening out differing values. The on readings are ヘイ + キン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[普通](普通.md) (ふつう), "ordinary; normal," is not necessarily a calculated average. [合計](合計.md) (ごうけい) is a total; averaging requires an additional step beyond adding the figures.

## Distinctive meaning

An average need not match any individual observation. Scores of sixty and eighty average seventy even if nobody scored seventy. **平均的な** (へいきんてきな), "average; typical," is a related adjectival form, whereas the bare noun is useful in calculations and comparisons such as 平均より高い, "higher than average."
