# ゆっくり - slowly, at leisure

## Meaning

ゆっくり means "slowly," "unhurriedly," or "at leisure." It can concern physical pace or taking enough time to relax.

- もう少しゆっくり話してください。 (もうすこしゆっくりはなしてください。) - Please speak a little more slowly.
- 日曜日は家でゆっくりします。 (にちようびはいえでゆっくりします。) - On Sunday I will relax at home.

It can modify a verb directly or with と: ゆっくりと歩く (ゆっくりとあるく), "walk slowly."

## How the kanji combine

This mimetic expression is written in hiragana. There are no kanji components to unpack. The small っ is part of its normal pronunciation and spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

速く (はやく), "quickly," contrasts with the speed sense. のんびり overlaps with being relaxed or taking things easily. Neither comparison covers every use.

## Distinctive meaning

ゆっくり休んでください (ゆっくりやすんでください) means "Get a good rest" or "Take your time resting," not an instruction to perform rest at a slow speed. Translate the whole action rather than forcing "slowly" into every sentence.
