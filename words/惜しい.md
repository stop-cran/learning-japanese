# 惜しい (おしい) - regrettable; precious; so close

## Meaning

惜しい is an i-adjective expressing regret about losing something valuable or missing a desired result. It can also describe something precious, something too good for a particular use or recipient, or an attempt that was almost successful.

- あと一点で勝てたのに、惜しかった。 (あといってんでかてたのに、おしかった。) - We could have won with one more point; it was so close.
- 捨てるには惜しい品です。 (すてるにはおしいしなです。) - It is too good an item to throw away.
- 時間が惜しいので、タクシーで行きます。 (じかんがおしいので、タクシーでいきます。) - Time is precious, so I will take a taxi.

Use 惜しい directly before a noun. The negative is 惜しくない (おしくない), and the past is 惜しかった (おしかった).

## How the kanji combine

惜 suggests valuing something and regretting its loss. It writes お, followed by しい in this native adjective. There is no second character to combine with it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[残念](残念.md) (ざんねん) also expresses a regrettable or disappointing outcome; 惜しい readily highlights a near miss or something one is reluctant to lose. [貴重](貴重.md) (きちょう), [大切](大切.md) (たいせつ), and [大事](大事.md) (だいじ) overlap in preciousness or value, but take な before a noun.

[近い](近い.md) (ちかい) can mean close or nearly the same. 惜しい as a reaction to an attempt is "almost, but not quite," not a general description of physical nearness.

## Distinctive meaning

惜しい does not automatically mean that something is expensive or that the speaker has made a moral mistake. 惜しい！ after a shot or answer acknowledges that it came close; it does not say the attempt succeeded. The reason for regret or reluctance needs context.
