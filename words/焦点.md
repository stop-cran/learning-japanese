# 焦点 (しょうてん) - focus; focal point

## Meaning

焦点 is a focus or focal point. In optics, it concerns the point where light rays converge. It also names the central issue or point attracting attention in a discussion.

- カメラの焦点を合わせてください。 (カメラのしょうてんをあわせてください。) - Please focus the camera.
- 議論の焦点は費用の問題です。 (ぎろんのしょうてんはひようのもんだいです。) - The focus of the discussion is the question of cost.

焦点を合わせる (しょうてんをあわせる) means to bring into focus; 焦点が合う (しょうてんがあう) means to be in focus. Geometry also has a technical focus sense, such as the foci of an ellipse.

## How the kanji combine

焦 has burning or scorching associations, and [点](../kanji/点.md) means point. A point at which focused sunlight can concentrate heat is a learning association, not a historical derivation. Read ショウ + テン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中心](中心.md) (ちゅうしん) can name a center or central focus more broadly. [要点](要点.md) (ようてん) names the main points or gist; [重点](重点.md) (じゅうてん) emphasizes where importance or effort is placed. [集中](集中.md) (しゅうちゅう) concerns concentration or focusing, rather than necessarily naming an optical point.

## Distinctive meaning

焦点 is not every dot or point. In a discussion, identify the question at issue, not merely a mark on a page. [商店](商店.md), "shop," has the same reading しょうてん but a different spelling and meaning.
