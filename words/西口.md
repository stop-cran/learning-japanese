# 西口 (にしぐち) — west exit

## Meaning

西口 is the west exit of a station or building: 西口で会いましょう (let's meet at the west exit), 池袋駅西口, 西口改札 にしぐちかいさつ.

## How the kanji combine

[西](../kanji/西.md) (west, kun にし) plus [口](../kanji/口.md) (mouth, "opening", kun くち, voiced to ぐち): a modifier-plus-noun compound of kun readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: 東口 ひがしぐち (east exit).
- Related: [南口](南口.md) みなみぐち, 北口 きたぐち, 出口 でぐち, 入口 いりぐち.

## Distinctive meaning

The compass exits are everyday station vocabulary. Do not confuse 西口 (にしぐち) with 西日 にしび (the western sun in the afternoon): the same 西 but a different second part.
