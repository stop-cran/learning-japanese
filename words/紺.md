# 紺 (こん) - navy blue; deep blue

## Meaning

紺 names a deep, dark blue, often translated as navy blue. It is a color noun, not an い-adjective.

- 紺のスーツを買いました。 (こんのスーツをかいました。) - I bought a navy-blue suit.
- この制服は紺です。 (このせいふくはこんです。) - This uniform is navy blue.

Use 紺の + noun, or 紺です to identify the color. 紺色 (こんいろ) is another way to name it.

## How the kanji combine

紺 is the whole single-character noun, using the on reading コン. There is no two-character compound to divide. In 紺色, 色 (いろ) explicitly adds "color."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[青](青.md) (あお) names blue more broadly, while [青い](青い.md) (あおい) is the adjective. 紺 narrows the shade rather than replacing every use of 青. Other colors are contrasts, not a single universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

English "deep blue" and "navy blue" are useful approximations, not an exact color-code specification. The Japanese dictionary describes a dark blue with a purplish cast. Context or a physical sample is more precise when matching a particular fabric or paint.

Do not create an adjective by adding い: 紺のシャツ is the model phrase here.
