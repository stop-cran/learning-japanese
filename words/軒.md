# 軒 (けん) - building counter; eaves

## Meaning

Read **けん**, 軒 is a counter for buildings, especially houses, and for shops as establishments. It follows a number rather than acting as the ordinary noun "building."

- この道にはパン屋が三軒あります。 (このみちにはパンやがさんげんあります。) - There are three bakeries on this street.

Useful forms are 一軒 (いっけん), 二軒 (にけん), 三軒 (さんげん), and 何軒 (なんげん), "how many houses/shops?"

Read **のき**, the same character is a separate noun meaning **eaves**, the edge of a roof that projects beyond the wall.

- 軒の下で雨宿りしました。 (のきのしたであまやどりしました。) - I sheltered from the rain under the eaves.

## How the kanji combine

There is one written character and two lexical uses. The counter uses the on reading けん, with sound changes after some numbers. The architectural noun uses the native reading のき. Do not read 軒の下 as けんのした in the eaves expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

For counting buildings, 軒 contrasts with [人](人.md) (にん), counting people: three houses need not contain three people. [屋根](屋根.md) (やね) is the roof as a whole, while のき names its projecting edge.

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding construction is essential. A number before 軒 points toward a counter; 軒の下 describes a place. "Eaves" is not a meaning of the counter reading けん, even though the written form is identical.
