# 赤道 (せきどう) - equator

## Meaning

赤道 is the equator, the imaginary circle at latitude zero dividing Earth into
the Northern and Southern Hemispheres. It also names the celestial equator
in astronomy, commonly 天の赤道 (てんのせきどう).

- この国は赤道の近くにあります。 (このくにはせきどうのちかくにあります。) - This country is near the equator.
- 船で赤道を越えました。 (ふねでせきどうをこえました。) - We crossed the equator by ship.

の近くに means near; を marks the line crossed with 越える.

## How the kanji combine

[赤](../kanji/赤.md) contributes red, and [道](../kanji/道.md) a way or path.
Recognize their written contributions, but learn 赤道 as the established
geographical and astronomical term, not a compositional name for any red road.
Read セキ + ドウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

緯度 (いど) is latitude, and 北半球 (きたはんきゅう) and 南半球 (みなみはんきゅう)
are the Northern and Southern Hemispheres. 熱帯 (ねったい) is the tropics,
a zone rather than the equatorial line itself.

## Distinctive meaning

An imaginary geographic line is not a road one can walk along.
Do not confuse the reading せきどう with the separate cadastral-road term
赤道 read あかみち or あかどう. The same written characters do not establish
the same word or meaning.
