# 下 (した)

## Meaning

下 means below, underneath, or the lower part. In the basic location pattern, Xの下 means "under X."

- 猫は机の下にいます。 (ねこは つくえの したに います。) — The cat is under the desk.
- 名前を写真の下に書いてください。 (なまえを しゃしんの したに かいてください。) — Please write your name below the photograph.

下 behaves as a noun: の connects it to the reference object, and に can mark the resulting location.

Two additional readings have narrower uses:

- **げ** names a low grade or inferior level, as in 上・中・下 (じょう・ちゅう・げ), "high, middle, low." It also labels the second volume of a two-volume work or the third of three: この小説の下を読みました (このしょうせつのげをよみました), "I read the final volume of this novel." 下巻 (げかん) makes the volume sense explicit.
- **しも** can mean a river's lower reaches: 川の下にある村 (かわのしもにあるむら), "a village in the lower reaches of the river." Here it does not mean a village physically underneath the river.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) is used alone with the native reading した. Other readings, such as か in compounds, do not replace した in 机の下.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上](上.md) (うえ), "above; on top," is the usual positional opposite. 中 (なか), "inside," gives a different spatial relationship. For the river reading しも, the corresponding upstream reading of 上 is かみ.

## Distinctive meaning

Underneath something is not the same as touching its underside. 下 can also describe relative age or rank, as in 年下 (としした), "younger"; the basic idea of being lower extends beyond physical height.

The source's broad "under; below" gloss must not erase the distinctions between した, げ, and しも. Learn each reading with its construction: a lower grade or volume, lower river reaches, and an ordinary position under a desk are not freely interchangeable.
