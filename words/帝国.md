# 帝国 (ていこく) — empire

## Meaning

帝国 names an empire rather than its ruler. In a historical Japanese context it can also abbreviate 大日本帝国 (だいにっぽんていこく), the Empire of Japan.

- 授業でローマ帝国の歴史を学んだ。 (じゅぎょうでろーまていこくのれきしをまなんだ。) — We studied the history of the Roman Empire in class.
- 展示の地図には、その帝国の国境が描かれていた。 (てんじのちずには、そのていこくのこっきょうがえがかれていた。) — The map in the exhibit showed the borders of that empire.

## How the kanji combine

[帝](../kanji/帝.md) (てい) is associated with an emperor; [国](../kanji/国.md) (こく) means a country or state. Both have on readings in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

皇帝 (こうてい) is an emperor, the person; 帝国 is the state. [国](国.md) (くに) can refer to a country without specifying how it is governed.

## Distinctive meaning

In ローマ帝国 the name before 帝国 tells you which empire. Do not read every appearance of 帝国 as a reference to Japan.
