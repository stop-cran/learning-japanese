# 認める (みとめる) — recognize; acknowledge

## Meaning

認める can mean to notice or recognize something. It also has an admission sense:
accepting that something is a fact. Keep these uses separate rather than treating them
as a single kind of approval.

- 小さな変化を認めました。 (ちいさなへんかをみとめました。) — I observed a small change.
- 自分の間違いを認めました。 (じぶんのまちがいをみとめました。) — I admitted my mistake.

The object takes を. This is an ichidan verb: 認めます (みとめます), 認めた (みとめた).

## How the kanji combine

[認](../kanji/認.md) carries the recognition or acknowledgment association.
The kana める completes the native verb みとめる. Learn this entire reading; a kanji's
other recorded readings do not provide interchangeable pronunciations for the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

気づく (きづく) means to notice or become aware of something: 変化に気づく (へんかにきづく),
"notice a change." Its に pattern differs from 変化を認める.
[否定](../words/否定.md) (ひてい), "denial," provides a contrast to admitting a fact, not to every act of noticing.

## Distinctive meaning

認める also has an approval sense, but admitting a mistake does not mean approving of it.
Nor does it automatically mean forgiving: [許す](../words/許す.md) (ゆるす) supplies that meaning in
an appropriate context. Translate the whole sentence, especially what is being 認める.
