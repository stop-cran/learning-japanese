# 九つ (ここのつ) — nine things

## Meaning

九つ means nine in the native counting series. Use it for nine items when the general つ count is appropriate. The complete form already includes the counting ending; do not add another counter to it.

- りんごが九つあります。  
  りんごがここのつあります。  
  "There are nine apples."

It can also state a child's age: 九つです (ここのつです), "I'm nine".

## How the kanji combine

[九](../kanji/九.md) represents nine, with the native element ここの followed by つ. Learn ここのつ as a whole form, not as きゅう plus つ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

九個 (きゅうこ) is another count for nine small items. 九歳 (きゅうさい) explicitly means nine years old. These overlap in particular settings but belong to different counting patterns.

## Distinctive meaning

The general つ series is not a replacement for every counter. For nine people use 九人 (きゅうにん), and for nine o'clock use 九時 (くじ). An appropriate counter determines the form and sometimes the reading.
