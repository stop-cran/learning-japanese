# げほげほ — coughing heavily

## Meaning

Repeated, forceful coughing, often with a rough or wet-sounding quality.

- 隣（となり）の部屋で、誰（だれ）かがげほげほせきをしている。 — Someone in the next room is coughing heavily.
- ほこりでげほげほとせきこんだ。 — The dust made me break into a heavy cough.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こんこん](こんこん.md) can describe coughing too, often with a lighter impression. These are impressions of a sound, not a reliable way to identify a medical condition.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn げほげほせきをする and げほげほせきこむ. This is a human-voice sound word, unlike the knocking sense of こんこん. The context identifies whether a sound is a cough or something tapping.
