# どうぞ - please; go ahead

## Meaning

どうぞ encourages someone to accept an offer or do something, and can add courtesy to a request. It is also used when handing something over.

- どうぞ座ってください。 (どうぞすわってください。) - Please have a seat.
- お茶をどうぞ。 (おちゃをどうぞ。) - Please have some tea.

In the second example, the offered item takes を, and the rest of the invitation is understood.

## How the kanji combine

どうぞ is written in kana. There is no kanji compound to break down; learn its long お sound and final ぞ as one expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ください makes requests in patterns such as 水をください and 座ってください. お願いします is another request expression, but their grammatical patterns differ.

## Distinctive meaning

どうぞ alone can mean "Go ahead" or "Here you are," depending on the situation. It is not a universal replacement for English "please" and does not by itself turn a plain command into the full polite てください construction.
