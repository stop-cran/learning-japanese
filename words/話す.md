# 話す (はなす) — to speak, to talk

## Meaning

話す is to speak, talk or tell: 日本語を話す (speak Japanese), 友達と話す (talk with a friend), 先生に話す (tell the teacher), 話してください (please tell me).

## How the kanji combine

[話](../kanji/話.md) with the kun reading はな plus okurigana す. A godan verb with the す-pattern: 話します, 話さない, 話して, 話した, potential 話せる.
The noun 話 はなし (a talk, story) is the same word with the okurigana dropped: 話が長い (a long story). The on reading ワ is in [会話](会話.md) かいわ and 電話 でんわ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 言う いう (say), しゃべる (chat, casual), 語る かたる (narrate, formal), 伝える つたえる (convey).
- Related: [聞く](聞く.md) きく (listen), 放す はなす／離す はなす (release, separate; homophones with different kanji).

## Distinctive meaning

話す is about conveying speech or conversing, and 言う focuses on the words said (「はい」と言う). 話す takes the listener with と (together) or に (told to), and the language with を.
