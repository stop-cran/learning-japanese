# 庁 (ちょう) - government office; agency

## Meaning

庁 is a government office or agency. It can stand as a noun or serve as an
ending in an administrative name.

- 庁の職員に相談した。 (ちょうのしょくいんにそうだんした。) - I consulted a member of the agency's staff.
- 府庁に書類を提出した。 (ふちょうにしょるいをていしゅつした。) - I submitted the documents to the prefectural office.

府庁 (ふちょう) specifically names the prefectural office of Osaka or Kyoto.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [庁](../kanji/庁.md) supplies the on reading チョウ.
In 府庁, [府](../kanji/府.md) identifies the prefectural administration and
庁 supplies the office association: フ + チョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[局](局.md) (きょく) can also name a bureau or office, but institutional names
determine which ending is used. They are not freely interchangeable ranks.
[事務所](事務所.md) (じむしょ) and [オフィス](オフィス.md) are broader words
for office premises or workspace; they do not by themselves identify a government agency.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 庁 with the relevant administrative body or its full name.
The English gloss "office" does not make 庁 a general substitute for a private
professional's office or every room where clerical work occurs.
Its overlap with other office labels does not establish unrestricted substitution.
