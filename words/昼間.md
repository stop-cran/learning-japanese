# 昼間 (ひるま) - daytime

## Meaning

昼間 means daytime or during the day, contrasted with nighttime. It names a span of the day, not just noon.

- 昼間は家にいません。 (ひるまはいえにいません。) - I am not at home during the day.

昼間の仕事 (ひるまのしごと) means "daytime work." の connects the time noun with another noun; は in the example presents daytime as the topic or contrast.

## How the kanji combine

[昼](../kanji/昼.md), ひる, contributes day, and [間](../kanji/間.md), ま, contributes an interval. Both are native readings. The alternative on reading ちゅうかん occurs, but this card teaches everyday ひるま.

## Synonyms and antonyms

夜 (よる) is the useful contrast. 昼 (ひる) can mean daytime but can also focus on midday or lunchtime. 午後 (ごご) is specifically the afternoon or p.m. period.

## Distinctive meaning

昼間 is not identical to "afternoon." A morning activity can happen 昼間 when the point is that it happens during daylight rather than at night. The word does not impose fixed working hours.
