# 飽きる (あきる) - grow tired of; lose interest

## Meaning

飽きる means becoming bored with something or losing the desire to continue it. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 飽きます, 飽きた, 飽きない. Mark what you have grown tired of with に.

- 同じ曲に飽きました。 (おなじきょくにあきました。) - I got tired of the same song.
- この景色は何度見ても飽きません。 (このけしきはなんどみてもあきません。) - I never tire of this view, however often I see it.

飽きている describes the resulting state of being bored with something; 飽きた presents the loss of interest as having happened.

## How the kanji combine

飽 carries the idea of having had enough. In this native verb it is read あ, followed by the okurigana きる. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[疲れる](疲れる.md) (つかれる) concerns tiredness or exhaustion, not necessarily lost interest. [退屈](退屈.md) (たいくつ) names boredom: you can be bored because there is nothing to do, without having tired of a particular activity.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb can follow another verb's stem: 聞き飽きる (ききあきる), "get tired of hearing," and 食べ飽きる (たべあきる), "get tired of eating." These point to repeated experience. 食べ飽きた can express being fed up with a food; it need not simply mean that your stomach is full.
