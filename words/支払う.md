# 支払う (しはらう) — pay

## Meaning

支払う means to pay. The bill, fee, or amount paid takes を; the means of payment can take で.
It is a verb, not a noun requiring する.

- 料金をカードで支払います。 (りょうきんをカードでしはらいます。) — I will pay the fee by card.
- 家賃はもう支払いました。 (やちんはもうしはらいました。) — I have already paid the rent.

The past form is 支払った (しはらった); the negative is 支払わない (しはらわない).

## How the kanji combine

JMdict explicitly identifies [支](../kanji/支.md) as **ateji** here: a character used for its sound,
not a reliable literal contribution such as "support."
[払](../kanji/払.md) corresponds to はらう, "pay." Do not explain the word as "support + pay."

The spelling mixes し with the native reading はらう. Under this repository's on-first mixed-reading convention
it is filed as `kango`; that is a display classification, not a claim that this is an ordinary two-on-reading compound
or proof of its historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

払う (はらう) also means "pay": 料金を払う (りょうきんをはらう) is another way to say "pay the fee."
But 払う also has nonfinancial uses, such as ほこりを払う, "brush off the dust."
支払う is not a replacement for 払う in that dusting expression.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 支払う as a payment verb with its own spelling. Its extra character does not make it a combination
of providing support and making a payment. もう支払いました (もうしはらいました), "I have already paid,"
reports that the payment was made; it is not a general expression for handing any object to somebody.
