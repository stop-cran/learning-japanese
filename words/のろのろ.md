# のろのろ — slowly and sluggishly

## Meaning

のろのろ describes sluggish movement or progress, often slower than someone wants or expects.

- 渋滞で車がのろのろ進んでいる。 — Cars are crawling along in the traffic jam. (渋滞: じゅうたい.)
- のろのろしていたら、バスに乗り遅れた。 — I dawdled and missed the bus. (乗り遅れる: のりおくれる.)

Use it directly with the verb or with と: のろのろと歩く. のろのろする describes acting slowly or dawdling.

## How the kanji combine

The repeated kana form depicts a sluggish manner. It is a native mimetic, not a kanji compound and not necessarily an imitation of audible movement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ゆっくり](ゆっくり.md) can neutrally mean slowly or approvingly mean taking one's time. のろのろ more readily conveys impatience or unwanted delay. [すたすた](すたすた.md) contrasts in walking pace; [どんどん](どんどん.md) can contrast in momentum.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not tell us why progress is slow: traffic, exhaustion, or reluctance can all explain it. Avoid using it as a neutral instruction to relax; のろのろしないで, "don't dawdle," is a complaint, unlike a reassuring ゆっくりでいい, "take your time."
