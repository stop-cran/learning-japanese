# 努力 (どりょく) — effort

## Meaning

努力 is the effort someone puts toward a goal. 努力する means "make an effort; strive."
It can describe sustained work, not just a single attempt.

- 日本語が上手になるように努力しています。 (にほんごがじょうずになるようにどりょくしています。) — I am working hard to improve my Japanese.

In this example, a plain verb phrase followed by ように states the result the person is aiming for.
The noun also fits 努力が必要です (どりょくがひつようです), "Effort is needed."

## How the kanji combine

[努](../kanji/努.md) means making an effort, and [力](../kanji/力.md) adds the idea of strength or exertion.
Together they name applied effort, not merely physical strength. The on readings are ド + リョク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

努める (つとめる) is a related native verb meaning "endeavor."
苦労 (くろう), "hardship; trouble," highlights difficulty experienced rather than simply the effort someone chooses to make.
A task can involve both 努力 and 苦労, but the words do not describe exactly the same aspect.

## Distinctive meaning

努力 says that someone is trying; it does not guarantee success. It also need not be physical:
practicing a language or changing a habit can involve 努力.
Avoid translating every English "try" with it when only one tentative action is intended.
