# 立ち上がる (たちあがる) - stand up; start up

## Meaning

立ち上がる is an intransitive godan verb for rising to one's feet. It can also describe a computer or application starting up.

- 彼は椅子から立ち上がりました。 (かれはいすからたちあがりました。) - He stood up from his chair.
- パソコンがなかなか立ち上がりません。 (パソコンがなかなかたちあがりません。) - The computer is taking a long time to start up.

The person or system that rises or starts is marked by が or presented with は. The starting position can take から. Forms include 立ち上がります, 立ち上がった, and 立ち上がらない.

## How the kanji combine

[立](../kanji/立.md) contributes standing through 立つ (たつ), and [上](../kanji/上.md) contributes upward movement through 上がる (あがる). 立ち is the connecting stem. The compound emphasizes the transition into standing rather than merely being upright.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[立つ](立つ.md) (たつ) is the broader verb for standing. [座る](座る.md) (すわる), "sit down," contrasts with physically standing up. [起きる](起きる.md) (おきる) can mean waking or getting up, which need not immediately involve standing.

## Distinctive meaning

The computer example describes the system's change of state. The corresponding transitive expression is パソコンを立ち上げる (パソコンをたちあげる), "start the computer." Keep that が/を distinction: 立ち上がる does not take the computer as a direct object. The physical and computing uses share an image of becoming active, but the computer is not literally standing.
