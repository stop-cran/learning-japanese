# バック (バック) - back or rear; reverse movement

## Meaning

バック is an English-derived word with several related uses.
It can name a back or rear, a background, or backing and support.
For moving backwards, バックする is intransitive; a vehicle doing so is the subject.

- 車がゆっくりバックしました。 (くるまがゆっくりバックしました。) - The car reversed slowly.
- 山をバックに写真を撮りました。 (やまをバックにしゃしんをとりました。) - I took a photo with the mountains as the backdrop.
- 彼には有力なバックがいる。 (かれにはゆうりょくなバックがいる。) - He has an influential backer.

バック can also name reverse gear. Other specialized dictionary uses include refunding,
a back player, backstroke and backhand; these are not all one interchangeable grammatical use.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana and has no kanji components.
The primary identity taught here comes from English "back," not "bag."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[後ろ](後ろ.md) (うしろ) overlaps with a back or rear location; [背中](背中.md) (せなか) specifically names the bodily back.
[裏](裏.md) (うら) can name a reverse or hidden side.
[応援](応援.md) (おうえん), [援助](援助.md) (えんじょ), [支える](支える.md) (ささえる)
and [味方](味方.md) (みかた) overlap with support or a supporter, not every physical rear.

## Distinctive meaning

For reversing, the direction is backwards: merely [戻る](戻る.md) (もどる), returning to an earlier place,
does not require facing backwards.
The refund sense permits transitive する; that does not make the reversing sense transitive too.
[バッグ](バッグ.md) is the primary spelling for a bag; its dictionary family also recognizes irregular バック.
Thus the spelling contrast is useful, but "バック can never mean bag" would be too strong.
