# 下宿 (げしゅく) -- lodging; boarding house

## Meaning

下宿 can name lodging in a rented room or boarding house, or the lodgings themselves. 下宿する means to live in such accommodation.

- 大学の近くに下宿しています。 (だいがくのちかくにげしゅくしています。) -- I am lodging near the university.
- 下宿から駅まで歩いて十分です。 (げしゅくからえきまであるいてじゅっぷんです。) -- It is ten minutes on foot from my lodgings to the station.

For the verb, use に for where someone lodges. 下宿しています describes the ongoing living arrangement.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) has the on reading げ; [宿](../kanji/宿.md), read しゅく, concerns lodging. Learn the established compound as a whole rather than interpreting it as a bedroom physically downstairs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

寮 (りょう) is a dormitory, often associated with a school or employer. アパート names an apartment building or apartment, not specifically a boarding arrangement.

## Distinctive meaning

下宿 emphasizes accommodation as a living arrangement rather than a sightseeing hotel's single-night stay. It can suggest room and board, but the word alone is not a guarantee that every meal is included.
