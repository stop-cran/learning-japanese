# 調査 (ちょうさ) - investigation; survey

## Meaning

調査 is an investigation, inquiry, or survey carried out to obtain information.
調査する is transitive: 原因を調査する (げんいんをちょうさする), "investigate the cause."
As a noun, Xについての調査 is "an investigation/survey about X."

- 警察が事故の原因を調査しています。 (けいさつがじこのげんいんをちょうさしています。) - The police are investigating the cause of the accident.
- 学生の生活について調査を行いました。 (がくせいのせいかつについてちょうさをおこないました。) - We conducted a survey of students' lives.

調査の結果 (ちょうさのけっか) means "the results of the investigation/survey."
調査中 (ちょうさちゅう) means "under investigation" or "a survey is in progress."

## How the kanji combine

[調](../kanji/調.md) contributes examining or checking in this word, and 査 inspection.
Their combined meaning centers on finding out facts.
The on-readings are チョウ + サ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[調べる](調べる.md) (しらべる) is a broader everyday verb for looking into or checking something.
[研究](研究.md) (けんきゅう) is research; collecting survey information may be part of a larger research project.
[質問](質問.md) (しつもん) is a question or questioning, one possible method rather than the whole investigation.

## Distinctive meaning

調査 does not specify a questionnaire. Investigators might inspect a site, read records, interview people,
or combine methods.
Nor does 調査中 imply that the answer is already known: it identifies an ongoing inquiry rather than its conclusion.
