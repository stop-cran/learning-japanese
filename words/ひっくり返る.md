# ひっくり返る (ひっくりかえる) - turn over; topple over

## Meaning

ひっくり返る describes something turning over, capsizing or toppling.
It can also describe a situation or decision being reversed, or a person
falling over or lying on their back.
It is an intransitive godan verb: the thing turning over takes が.

- 波でボートがひっくり返りました。 (なみでボートがひっくりかえりました。) - The boat capsized because of a wave.
- コップがひっくり返って、水がこぼれました。 (コップがひっくりかえって、みずがこぼれました。) - The glass tipped over and the water spilled.

Forms include ひっくり返ります, ひっくり返って and ひっくり返らない.
The dictionary also records ひっくりがえる.

## How the kanji combine

[返](../kanji/返.md)る contributes turning or reversal.
The source spelling 引っ繰り返る, with [引](../kanji/引.md) and 繰,
represents the same word. Do not copy the transitive partner's rare-spelling
label onto every spelling in this entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ひっくり返す](ひっくり返す.md) takes the affected object with を:
コップをひっくり返す, "knock the glass over."
[倒れる](倒れる.md) and [転ぶ](転ぶ.md) overlap in falling over;
[転がる](転がる.md) also covers rolling or lying on a surface.

## Distinctive meaning

The ending る does not make this ichidan: the polite form is
ひっくり返ります, not ひっくり返ます.
English may use "be overturned," but this Japanese verb is not simply the
passive conjugation of ひっくり返す.
The cause can be external, as the wave example shows.
