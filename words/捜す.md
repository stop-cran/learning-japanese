# 捜す (さがす) - search for; look for

## Meaning

捜す means to search or look for something. The thing sought takes を.
It is a transitive godan verb: 捜します さがします, 捜した さがした, 捜さない さがさない.

- なくした鍵を捜しています。 (なくしたかぎをさがしています。) - I am looking for the keys I lost.
- 部屋を捜しましたが、鍵は見つかりませんでした。 (へやをさがしましたが、かぎはみつかりませんでした。) - I searched the room, but I did not find the keys.

In the second sentence, the room takes を as the place searched through, rather than as the thing missing.
The context identifies what the search is for.

## How the kanji combine

[捜](../kanji/捜.md) supplies the searching idea and the reading さが; the final す remains kana.
The on reading ソウ occurs in separate words such as 捜査 そうさ, investigation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[探す](探す.md) さがす belongs to the same dictionary entry. JMdict particularly associates 探す with something desired
and 捜す with something lost. This is a spelling tendency, not an absolute rule splitting every search into two categories.
[求める](求める.md) もとめる also has a seeking sense, alongside its requesting uses.

## Distinctive meaning

Searching does not guarantee finding. 捜した reports the attempt; 見つけた みつけた reports finding something.
The second example makes that distinction explicit: a search happened without a successful discovery.
Do not infer from the spelling alone that every use describes a police investigation.
