# 面白い (おもしろい) — interesting; amusing

## Meaning

面白い (おもしろい) describes something that interests or engages you.
Depending on context it can also mean amusing or funny; an interesting subject need not make anyone laugh.
It is an i-adjective: 面白い本 (おもしろいほん), "an interesting book."

- この本は面白いです。 (このほんはおもしろいです。) — This book is interesting.
- 科学の話は面白かったです。 (かがくのはなしはおもしろかったです。) — The talk about science was interesting.

Notice the past form 面白かった, not 面白いでした.

## How the kanji combine

[面](../kanji/面.md) can mean a face or surface, and [白](../kanji/白.md) means white.
The word uses native readings, おも + しろい, but its modern meaning cannot be obtained by translating those two glosses literally.
"White face" is not a definition of 面白い. This article does not claim a historical explanation for the spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[楽しい](楽しい.md) (たのしい) emphasizes enjoyment or fun.
面白い can instead focus on interest: a difficult question may be 面白い because it makes you think.
つまらない, "boring; uninteresting," supplies the useful opposite in that sense; it is usually written in kana.

## Distinctive meaning

面白い is an evaluation, not a claim that something is easy or cheerful.
For a comedy it may suggest humor; for a scientific explanation it may suggest intellectual interest.
Let the subject and context guide the English translation instead of mechanically choosing "funny."
