# 理想 (りそう) - ideal; ideals

## Meaning

理想 is a noun for an ideal: the state, model, or goal someone considers best. Use 理想の before a noun and 理想とする for taking something as one's ideal.

- 静かな町で暮らすのが私の理想です。 (しずかなまちでくらすのがわたしのりそうです。) - My ideal is to live in a quiet town.
- 理想の仕事を探しています。 (りそうのしごとをさがしています。) - I am looking for my ideal job.

The related form 理想的な (りそうてきな) means "ideal," as in 理想的な環境, "an ideal environment." Do not replace the の in 理想の仕事 with な without using the 的 form.

## How the kanji combine

[理](../kanji/理.md) contributes principle or reason, and [想](../kanji/想.md) contributes a conception or thought. Together they name an envisioned ideal. The components do not prove that every ideal is realistic.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[希望](希望.md) (きぼう) names hope or a wish; 理想 supplies a model of what is best. [現実](現実.md) (げんじつ), "reality," gives a useful contrast between what is envisioned and what actually exists.

## Distinctive meaning

An ideal need not be impossible, and reality need not be disappointing. The distinction is between a desired model and actual conditions, not automatically good versus bad. 私の理想 also makes the evaluation personal: different people may have different ideal jobs or ways of living.
