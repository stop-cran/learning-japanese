# 金曜 (きんよう) - Friday

## Meaning

金曜 is a noun meaning Friday, the shortened form of [金曜日](金曜日.md) (きんようび). Both identify the same weekday.

- 次の金曜に会いましょう。 (つぎのきんようにあいましょう。) - Let's meet next Friday.
- この店は金曜だけ休みです。 (このみせはきんようだけやすみです。) - This shop is closed only on Fridays.

金曜に marks when something happens. 金曜の朝 (きんようのあさ) means "Friday morning"; 金曜まで means "until Friday" or "by Friday," according to the rest of the sentence. 毎週金曜 (まいしゅうきんよう) specifies every Friday.

For an action deadline, use 金曜までに (きんようまでに): 金曜までに出してください (きんようまでにだしてください), "Please submit it by Friday." This differs from continuing an activity until Friday.

## How the kanji combine

[金](../kanji/金.md), associated with gold or metal, supplies Friday's identifying element in the weekday series. [曜](../kanji/曜.md) marks a day of the week. 金曜 keeps the キン + ヨウ portion of 金曜日 and leaves off 日.

## Synonyms and antonyms

金曜日 is the full equivalent, not a different day or a different level of politeness. [木曜](木曜.md) (もくよう), Thursday, and [土曜](土曜.md) (どよう), Saturday, are neighboring days, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

A short form need not be slang: 金曜 fits timetables, notices, and ordinary conversation. In a brief schedule, 金曜 may refer to a recurring Friday slot; in an arrangement between friends, context may identify one particular Friday. Add a date or 次の when necessary to avoid scheduling ambiguity.
