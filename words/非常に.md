# 非常に (ひじょうに) — extremely

## Meaning

非常に is an adverb meaning "very," "extremely," or "exceedingly." It intensifies a quality or state and fits careful speech and explanatory writing.

この問題は非常に重要です。 (このもんだいはひじょうにじゅうようです。) — This issue is extremely important.

今日は非常に寒いです。 (きょうはひじょうにさむいです。) — It is extremely cold today.

## How the kanji combine

[非](../kanji/非.md) contributes "not" and [常](../kanji/常.md) "ordinary." 非常 has the reading ヒ + ジョウ; に makes the adverbial expression taught here. "Beyond the ordinary" is a useful meaning guide, not a word-for-word translation of every sentence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

とても is a broadly useful everyday "very." 大変 (たいへん) can also intensify a description. 少し (すこし), "a little," contrasts in degree.

## Distinctive meaning

非常に does not mean an emergency is occurring. 非常 appears with an emergency meaning in other compounds, but 非常に寒い simply describes extreme cold. It can intensify favorable or unfavorable qualities.
