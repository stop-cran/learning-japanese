# 黄色 (きいろ) - yellow

## Meaning

黄色 is the color yellow. As a color noun, it commonly modifies another noun with の: 黄色の花 (きいろのはな), "a yellow flower."

- 黄色のかばんを買いました。 (きいろのかばんをかいました。) - I bought a yellow bag.

It can also be a predicate with です: この花は黄色です (このはなはきいろです), "This flower is yellow."

## How the kanji combine

黄 contributes き, "yellow," and [色](../kanji/色.md) contributes いろ, "color." These are native readings. The reading taught here is きいろ, including both adjacent い sounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

黄色い (きいろい) is the related i-adjective: 黄色い花 also means "a yellow flower." 赤 (あか), "red," and 青 (あお), "blue," are contrasting colors, not logical opposites of yellow.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse the noun 黄色 with the adjective 黄色い. Their meanings overlap, but their grammar differs: 黄色のかばん versus 黄色いかばん. The の pattern is a useful default for this noun.
