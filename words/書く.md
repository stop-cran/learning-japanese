# 書く (かく) — to write

## Meaning

書く is to write: 手紙を書く (write a letter), 名前を書いてください (please write your name), 日記を書く (keep a diary), 漢字を書く (write kanji).
It also means "to compose": 小説を書く (write a novel), メールを書く (write an email).

## How the kanji combine

[書](../kanji/書.md) with the kun reading か plus okurigana く. A godan verb: 書きます, 書かない, 書いて, 書いた, potential 書ける.
Noun form: 書き (書き方 かきかた, "way of writing"; 手書き てがき, "handwriting"; 書き取り かきとり, "dictation"). The on reading ショ is in [読書](読書.md) どくしょ and 辞書 じしょ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [読む](読む.md) よむ (read).
- Homophone: 描く かく／えがく (draw, paint: 絵を描く) and 掻く かく (scratch), 欠く かく (lack). They are different verbs with different kanji.
- Close: 記す しるす (put on record, literary), 綴る つづる (spell, compose).

## Distinctive meaning

書く is for letters, characters and text, while pictures use 描く: 絵を描く (えをかく), "draw a picture." Learn 描く for this distinction rather than treating every かく as 書く.
