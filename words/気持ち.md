# 気持ち (きもち) — feeling; state of mind

## Meaning

気持ち is a feeling, sensation, or state of mind. It can concern an emotion toward someone or how an experience feels physically.

- あなたの気持ちが分かります。 (あなたのきもちがわかります。) — I understand how you feel.
- この風は気持ちがいいです。 (このかぜはきもちがいいです。) — This breeze feels pleasant.

## How the kanji combine

[気](../kanji/気.md), read き, concerns mind or spirit; [持](../kanji/持.md)ち, もち, comes from 持つ, "hold." This is a mixed on-plus-kun formation, not an all-on compound. The metadata uses kango for its on-first pattern.

## Synonyms and antonyms

気分 (きぶん) overlaps in mood and physical feeling. 感情 (かんじょう) more explicitly names emotion. 気持ちがいい, "feel pleasant," contrasts with 気持ちが悪い (きもちがわるい), which can mean feeling sick or finding something unpleasant.

## Distinctive meaning

気持ち is a noun, not an adjective meaning "happy." Use an accompanying description or construction to say what the feeling is. 私の気持ち (わたしのきもち) means "my feelings"; the word itself does not identify the person experiencing them.
