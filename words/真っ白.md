# 真っ白 (まっしろ) - pure white; blank

## Meaning

真っ白 describes pure whiteness. It also describes something blank,
such as unmarked paper or a mind with no accessible thoughts.

- 雪で庭が真っ白になりました。 (ゆきでにわがまっしろになりました。) - The snow turned the garden completely white.
- 緊張して、頭の中が真っ白になりました。 (きんちょうして、あたまのなかがまっしろになりました。) - I was so nervous that my mind went blank.

Use な before a noun: 真っ白な紙 (まっしろなかみ), pure white paper.
Whether paper is unmarked or simply white depends on context.

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md) intensifies [白](../kanji/白.md), white:
まっ + しろ. Keep the small っ and the whole reading まっしろ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[白い](白い.md) (しろい) is the ordinary color adjective.
[空っぽ](空っぽ.md) (からっぽ), empty, overlaps with some blank-mind
descriptions, but an empty box is not thereby white.
[真っ黒](真っ黒.md) (まっくろ) contrasts in color, not in every
figurative use.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary's obsolete reading ましろ is restricted to the spelling
真白. It is not an instruction to pronounce 真っ白 without っ.
Likewise, do not treat the blank-mind expression as a literal claim
about the color of someone's brain.
