# 湯 (ゆ) — hot water

## Meaning

湯 means hot water. In everyday speech, the form お湯 (おゆ) is especially useful. It can refer to water for drinking, cooking, washing, or bathing.

お茶を入れるために、お湯を沸かします。 (おちゃをいれるために、おゆをわかします。) — I boil water to make tea.

## How the kanji combine

湯 is one kanji with the native reading ゆ. The お in お湯 is a conventional polite prefix, not a change in the substance being named.

## Synonyms and antonyms

水 (みず) is water, often specifically unheated or cool water when contrasted with お湯. 熱湯 (ねっとう) is very hot or boiling water; it is stronger than 湯 alone.

## Distinctive meaning

お湯 does not have to be actively boiling. In a bath or hot-spring setting, 湯 can name the hot bath or spring itself. Follow the context: the same word can refer to water in a kettle or water used for bathing.
