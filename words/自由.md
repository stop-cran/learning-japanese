# 自由 (じゆう) — freedom

## Meaning

自由 expresses freedom or liberty. It works as a noun and as a な-adjective, meaning that な connects it to a following noun.
Use に when it describes how an action is done.

- 自由な時間がほしいです。 (じゆうなじかんがほしいです。) — I want time that I can use as I like.
- 好きな色を自由に選んでください。 (すきないろをじゆうにえらんでください。) — Please feel free to choose any color you like.

Thus 自由な時間 describes the time, while 自由に選ぶ describes choosing.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) has the self association; [由](../kanji/由.md) has reason or source associations.
Learn the established compound as freedom to act according to one's own choice.
This is a meaning aid, not a claim that the modern English glosses reconstruct the word's history.
The on readings are ジ + ユウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

無料 (むりょう) means free of charge. It answers a price question, not a question about freedom.
不自由 (ふじゆう) can describe inconvenience or difficulty; its uses are not simply a one-for-one reversal of every English use of "free."

## Distinctive meaning

自由 does not promise that something costs nothing. 自由に選んでください grants choice, even if the selected item must be paid for.
Keep both the meaning and the connectors in view: 自由です at the end of a polite sentence, 自由な before a noun, and 自由に before an action.
