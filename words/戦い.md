# 戦い (たたかい) - battle; struggle; contest

## Meaning

戦い is a noun for a battle, fight, struggle, or contest. It can describe armed conflict, competition, or a figurative struggle against an obstacle. The opponent or challenge can be marked by と: 時間との戦い (じかんとのたたかい), "a race against time."

- 長い戦いが終わりました。 (ながいたたかいがおわりました。) - The long battle came to an end.
- 締め切り前は時間との戦いでした。 (しめきりまえはじかんとのたたかいでした。) - Before the deadline, it was a race against time.

The second example involves pressure and limited time, not physical combat.

## How the kanji combine

[戦](../kanji/戦.md) contributes fighting or competing. 戦い is the noun related to 戦う (たたかう), "fight/compete"; the kana ending is part of the native form. 闘い is another recorded spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[戦争](戦争.md) (せんそう) names war. 戦い is broader and can refer to a particular battle or a nonmilitary struggle. A sports contest can also be described as 戦い when emphasizing competition; that does not turn the event into a war.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the surrounding phrase to identify the kind of struggle. 戦いに勝つ (たたかいにかつ) means "win a battle/contest"; 戦いに負ける means "lose." Winning and losing are opposite outcomes of a 戦い, not opposite meanings of the noun itself.
