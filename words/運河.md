# 運河 (うんが) - canal

## Meaning

運河 is a canal, an artificial waterway. A central use is a navigable
channel made for boats and shipping, although canals can also serve
water-supply or other purposes.

- 船が運河を通って海に出ました。 (ふねがうんがをとおってうみにでました。) - The ship passed through the canal and went out to sea.
- 運河に沿って歩きました。 (うんがにそってあるきました。) - I walked along the canal.

パナマ運河 (パナマうんが) is the Panama Canal.

## How the kanji combine

[運](../kanji/運.md) contributes transport or carrying; [河](../kanji/河.md)
contributes a river or watercourse. The compound associates a watercourse
with transportation. Its on readings combine as ウン + ガ, with 河's カ
voiced in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[川](川.md) or [河](河.md) (かわ) means "river; stream."
運河 identifies a canal rather than simply any river or any area of water.
A canal may connect natural waterways, so the two can appear in the
same route description.

## Distinctive meaning

An English gloss "waterway" is broader than the usual canal meaning:
not every navigable river is an 運河. Conversely, learning the shipping
use does not mean that a canal must have no other function or still
carry commercial traffic today.
