# 灰色 (はいいろ) - gray; grey

## Meaning

灰色 is gray, the color between black and white. Use **灰色の + noun** to describe a gray object, or **灰色だ／です** to state its color.

- 灰色のコートを着ています。 (はいいろのコートをきています。) - I am wearing a gray coat.
- 空が灰色になりました。 (そらがはいいろになりました。) - The sky turned gray.

Gray and grey are English spelling variants, not different Japanese colors.

## How the kanji combine

[灰](../kanji/灰.md) (はい) means ash, and [色](../kanji/色.md) (いろ) means color: a modifier-plus-head combination, "ash color." Both parts use native readings, はい + いろ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

グレー is a borrowed name for gray. [黒い](黒い.md) (くろい), "black," and [白い](白い.md) (しろい), "white," provide useful color contrasts; an ash-colored object need not contain actual ash.

## Distinctive meaning

灰色 is a color noun, not an i-adjective: say 灰色のコート, not 灰色いコート. The reading here is はいいろ. The additional dictionary reading かいしょく is marked outdated; it is not the reading to substitute in these everyday examples.
