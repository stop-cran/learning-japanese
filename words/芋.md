# 芋 (いも) — tuber

## Meaning

芋 is a **broad food word for tubers and similar underground crops**, including potatoes, taro and yams. The whole word or situation identifies the kind; this is not a strict botanical classification.

- 芋をゆでます。（いもをゆでます。）— I will boil the tubers.
- この料理には里芋を使います。（このりょうりにはさといもをつかいます。）— We use taro in this dish.

In the first example, the context must tell you which kind is being cooked. In the second, 里芋 (さといも) specifies taro.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [芋](../kanji/芋.md) writes the native noun いも. It is not a compound of separately pronounced parts.

In じゃが芋 (じゃがいも), potato, and 里芋 (さといも), taro, 芋 supplies いも. 薩摩芋 (さつまいも), sweet potato, is another specific food name. These words are usually written in kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

じゃがいも, さといも and さつまいも identify different foods; do not use them as unrestricted substitutes for one another. 芋 is less specific. It has no direct food antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The food sense is the teaching focus. JMdict also records derogatory **yokel or bumpkin** and dismissive **dud or worthless thing** senses. These do not make いも a neutral label for a person; avoid using the insult as ordinary descriptive vocabulary.

Do not assume the English translation must always be "potato." A recipe naming 里芋 calls for taro, not whichever tuber happens to be available.
