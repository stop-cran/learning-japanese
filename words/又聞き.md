# 又聞き (またぎき) — hearsay; learning by hearsay

## Meaning

又聞き means hearing something indirectly, as it is passed along. JMdict glosses it as “hearsay; learning by hearsay,” and Digital Daijisen defines it as hearing something passed on, or hearing it indirectly. It can be used with する. JMdict also lists the spelling また聞き.

- その話は友達からの又聞きなので、本当かどうかわかりません。 (そのはなしはともだちからのまたぎきなので、ほんとうかどうかわかりません。) — I heard that story secondhand through a friend, so I don't know whether it's true.
- 又聞きした情報をそのまま信じてはいけません。 (またぎきしたじょうほうをそのまましんじてはいけません。) — You shouldn't take information you heard secondhand at face value.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[又](../kanji/又.md), used as a prefix, marks something as indirect, and [聞](../kanji/聞.md) means hearing. Digital Daijisen gives 又聞き as an example of that prefix use. Together, they mean hearing something indirectly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[噂](噂.md) (うわさ) is a rumor or gossip; Digital Daijisen's example for 又聞き is hearing a rumor secondhand. 伝聞 でんぶん also means hearsay or a report (JMdict entry 1442420). [聞く](聞く.md) (きく) is hearing in general, and [間接](間接.md) (かんせつ) is indirectness.

## Distinctive meaning

又聞き describes how information was received, indirectly; it does not say whether the information is true. 又貸し またがし, “sublease; lending (something one has borrowed) to someone else” (JMdict entry 1525120), uses the same prefix.
