# 楽しい (たのしい) — enjoyable, fun

## Meaning

楽しい is an い-adjective meaning enjoyable, fun, or pleasant.

- 友達と話すのは楽しいです。 (ともだちとはなすのはたのしいです。) — Talking with friends is fun.
- 昨日の旅行は楽しかったです。 (きのうのりょこうはたのしかったです。) — Yesterday's trip was enjoyable.

The past form is 楽しかった たのしかった; the adverbial form is 楽しく たのしく.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native adjective, not a compound. [楽](../kanji/楽.md) supplies たの, and しい is the okurigana. The same character also occurs in 楽 らく, "easy; comfortable," but that is a different word with different grammar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 面白い おもしろい can mean interesting, amusing, or enjoyable; it can emphasize interest or humor rather than enjoyment alone.
- 嬉しい うれしい means happy or glad, often about something welcome that has happened.
- つまらない means boring or uninteresting in the relevant contrast; it is normally written in kana.

## Distinctive meaning

楽しい仕事 たのしいしごと is enjoyable work. 楽な仕事 らくなしごと is easy work: 楽 takes な before a noun, but 楽しい does not. A difficult activity can still be 楽しい.
