# お弁当 (おべんとう) - boxed meal; bento

## Meaning

お弁当 is a packed or boxed meal, often eaten for lunch. It may be homemade or bought.

- 明日はお弁当を持っていきます。 (あしたはおべんとうをもっていきます。) - I will take a packed lunch tomorrow.
- 駅でお弁当を買いました。 (えきでおべんとうをかいました。) - I bought a bento at the station.

The food is the object of 作る (つくる), "make," or 食べる (たべる), "eat."

## How the kanji combine

The polite prefix お attaches to the Sino-Japanese word 弁当: 弁 is べん and [当](../kanji/当.md) is とう. Learn this established food word as a unit rather than deriving its meaning from modern individual-character glosses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

弁当 without お names the same thing. 昼ご飯 (ひるごはん), "lunch," specifies the meal's timing, not its packaging.

## Distinctive meaning

お弁当 is not the empty lunchbox, which is 弁当箱 (べんとうばこ). Nor must every bento be eaten at noon.
