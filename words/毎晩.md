# 毎晩 (まいばん) - every night

## Meaning

毎晩 means every night or every evening. It describes an activity or condition recurring on successive evenings, rather than something continuing all night.

- 毎晩、寝る前に本を読みます。 (まいばん、ねるまえにほんをよみます。) - Every night, I read before going to bed.
- 最近は毎晩寒いです。 (さいきんはまいばんさむいです。) - It has been cold every night lately.

It normally modifies the sentence directly without に. Use の before another noun: 毎晩の習慣 (まいばんのしゅうかん), "a nightly habit."

## How the kanji combine

[毎](../kanji/毎.md) means every, and [晩](../kanji/晩.md) means evening or night. Both use on readings: まい + ばん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

毎朝 (まいあさ), "every morning," gives a contrasting part of the day. 今晩 (こんばん) is this evening or tonight, not a recurring schedule.

## Distinctive meaning

毎晩 answers "how often?" It does not answer "for how many hours?" 一晩中 (ひとばんじゅう), "all night," expresses duration within one night. Reading for ten minutes nightly is still 毎晩読む.
