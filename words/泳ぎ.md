# 泳ぎ (およぎ) - swimming; swimming technique

## Meaning

泳ぎ is a noun meaning "swimming." It can draw attention to someone's swimming ability or the way they swim. Use 泳ぎが上手 (およぎがじょうず), "good at swimming," and 泳ぎを練習する (およぎをれんしゅうする), "practice swimming."

- 彼女は泳ぎが上手です。 (かのじょはおよぎがじょうずです。) - She is good at swimming.
- 先生の泳ぎをよく見てください。 (せんせいのおよぎをよくみてください。) - Watch carefully how the teacher swims.

In the second sentence, the focus is the observable swimming, not the teacher's journey to the pool.

## How the kanji combine

[泳](../kanji/泳.md) carries the swimming meaning. 泳ぎ is the noun form associated with [泳ぐ](泳ぐ.md) (およぐ), "swim": the final ぐ becomes ぎ in this stem form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[水泳](水泳.md) (すいえい) names swimming as an activity or sport. It overlaps with 泳ぎ but fits expressions such as 水泳の授業 (すいえいのじゅぎょう), "swimming class."

[上手](上手.md) and [下手](下手.md) evaluate skill; they are not opposites of swimming itself.

## Distinctive meaning

泳ぎ is not a finite verb. Compare 彼女は泳ぎが速い (かのじょはおよぎがはやい), "She is a fast swimmer," with 彼女は速く泳ぐ (かのじょははやくおよぐ), "She swims fast." In the first Japanese sentence, 泳ぎ is a noun marked by が; in the second, 速く modifies the verb 泳ぐ.
