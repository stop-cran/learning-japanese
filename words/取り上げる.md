# 取り上げる (とりあげる) - take up; confiscate; cover a topic

## Meaning

取り上げる is a transitive ichidan verb. It can mean physically picking something up, taking it away, or selecting a topic for discussion or reporting. The item or topic takes を.

- 先生は生徒から携帯電話を取り上げました。 (せんせいはせいとからけいたいでんわをとりあげました。) - The teacher confiscated the student's mobile phone.
- 会議でこの問題を取り上げます。 (かいぎでこのもんだいをとりあげます。) - We will take up this issue at the meeting.

In the first example, から identifies whose possession it was taken from. 新聞が事件を取り上げる (しんぶんがじけんをとりあげる) means "a newspaper covers an incident," not that it confiscates the incident.

## How the kanji combine

[取](../kanji/取.md) contributes taking, and [上](../kanji/上.md) contributes raising. The connecting stem 取り of 取る combines with the verb 上げる. The literal "pick up" sense helps you recognize the spelling, but does not predict every extended use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[取る](取る.md) (とる), "take," is broader. [上げる](上げる.md) (あげる), "raise," does not by itself express confiscation or selecting a news topic. Giving something back is a contextual opposite of confiscation, not of every 取り上げる use.

## Distinctive meaning

The object resolves much of the meaning: an object can be picked up or taken away; an issue can be taken up for consideration. 資格を取り上げる (しかくをとりあげる) means revoking someone's qualification. Do not translate every occurrence literally as lifting upward.
