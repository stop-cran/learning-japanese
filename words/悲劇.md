# 悲劇 (ひげき) - tragedy

## Meaning

悲劇 is a noun with two related uses: a tragic drama or play, and a tragic event or calamity in real life.
**悲劇を見る** can concern a performance; **悲劇が起こる** (ひげきがおこる) means that a tragedy occurs.
The surrounding topic identifies which sense is intended.

- 授業で有名な悲劇を読みました。 (じゅぎょうでゆうめいなひげきをよみました。) - We read a famous tragedy in class.
- 同じ悲劇を繰り返してはいけません。 (おなじひげきをくりかえしてはいけません。) - We must not repeat the same tragedy.

The second sentence can express a serious wish to prevent a past disaster from happening again.
It does not imply that the event was a theatrical performance.

## How the kanji combine

[悲](../kanji/悲.md) contributes sorrow, while 劇 contributes drama.
Together they transparently describe tragic drama.
The word also extends to sorrowful real-world events.
The on readings ヒ + ゲキ give ひげき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[劇](劇.md) (げき) is drama or a play without specifying a tragic outcome.
喜劇 (きげき), "comedy," is the contrasting dramatic category.
災害 (さいがい), "disaster," identifies a kind of damaging event; 悲劇 emphasizes its tragic character.

## Distinctive meaning

悲劇 is stronger than an everyday disappointment.
For real events, it conveys a judgment about suffering or devastating loss rather than giving a technical classification.
For literature, it can identify a genre or particular work.
Do not assume 喜劇 is the natural opposite of every real-life tragedy simply because it contrasts with the theatrical sense.
