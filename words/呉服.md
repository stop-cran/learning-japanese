# 呉服 (ごふく) — kimono fabrics; cloth for Japanese clothes

## Meaning

呉服 is cloth for Japanese clothes: kimono fabrics. JMdict glosses it as cloth for Japanese clothes, kimono fabrics, textile and drapery, and gives a second sense, silk fabrics, cross-referring to 太物 ふともの, which it glosses as drapery such as cotton or hemp, as opposed to silk (entry 2653980). Digital Daijisen gives the same two senses, plus a third: twill and similar fabrics made with a weaving technique transmitted from the ancient Chinese state of Wu.

- 祖母は昔、呉服の店を営んでいました。 (そぼはむかし、ごふくのみせをいとなんでいました。) — My grandmother used to run a kimono-fabric shop.
- デパートの呉服売り場で、帯を選びました。 (デパートのごふくうりばで、おびをえらびました。) — I chose an obi in the department store's kimono section.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[呉](../kanji/呉.md) refers to the ancient Chinese state of Wu, and by extension to China, and [服](../kanji/服.md) means clothes. Digital Daijisen lists 呉服 under the sense of 呉 meaning China.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[着物](着物.md) (きもの) and [和服](和服.md) (わふく) are Japanese clothes; Digital Daijisen defines 呉服 as a general term for the woven fabrics used for Japanese clothes. [布](布.md) (ぬの) and [生地](生地.md) (きじ) are cloth or fabric in general.

## Distinctive meaning

A shop that sells 呉服 is a 呉服屋 ごふくや, “kimono fabrics shop; draper” (JMdict entry 1269200).
