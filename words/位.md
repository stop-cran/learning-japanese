# 位 (くらい) - rank; grade

## Meaning

The noun 位 (くらい) names rank, standing, or a grade or level. It also names a digit's place in a number.

- その人は位が高い。 (そのひとはくらいがたかい。) - That person holds a high rank.
- 百の位の数字は三です。 (ひゃくのくらいのすうじはさんです。) - The digit in the hundreds place is three.

For rank, useful patterns include 位が高い/低い (くらいがたかい/ひくい), "be high/low in rank."

A **separate dictionary identity**, the suffix/particle くらい or ぐらい, expresses approximation after an amount: 十分くらい待ちました (じゅっぷんくらいまちました), "I waited about ten minutes." This is not the noun "rank."

## How the kanji combine

[位](../kanji/位.md) alone writes the native noun くらい. The approximation expression is taught in kana here. Dictionaries also record 位 for it, but shared spelling does not erase the grammatical distinction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[地位](地位.md) (ちい) also names a position or standing. [程度](程度.md) (ていど) means degree or extent, rather than being interchangeable with every rank use. High and low ranks contrast; the noun itself has no single opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read every written 位 as くらい: 一位 (いちい), "first place," uses イ. Likewise, 十分くらい contains an approximating expression, not a social rank. Notice the whole construction before choosing a reading or meaning.
