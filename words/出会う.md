# 出会う (であう) - encounter; come across

## Meaning

出会う means to encounter or come across someone or something, including
meeting by chance. It is an intransitive godan verb. The person encountered
can take に or と; an encountered thing naturally takes に.
The past is 出会った (であった).

- 旅先で昔の友達に出会いました。 (たびさきでむかしのともだちにであいました。) - I ran into an old friend while traveling.
- この図書館で大切な本に出会いました。 (このとしょかんでたいせつなほんにであいました。) - I came across an important book in this library.

The book example extends encountering beyond people.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) contributes coming out, and [会](../kanji/会.md)
contributes meeting. The native elements combine as で + あう.
The final あう conjugates: 出会います, 出会わない, 出会って.
The meaning is not restricted to encounters outdoors.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会う](会う.md) (あう) is the broader verb "meet" and readily describes
a planned meeting. [出会い](出会い.md) (であい) is the related noun,
"encounter." Parting is a contrasting event rather than a replacement in
the same sentence pattern.

## Distinctive meaning

出会う can highlight an initial or significant encounter without requiring
that it be romantic. The dictionary lists several spellings; 出遭う tends
to suggest an unwelcome encounter, while 出合う is especially associated
with things such as rivers meeting. These are not grounds for assigning a
different emotional meaning to every use of the standard spelling 出会う.
