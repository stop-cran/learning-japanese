# 集団 (しゅうだん) - group; collective

## Meaning

集団 is a group or mass considered together. It is a noun, not a verb meaning "gather."
Use Xの集団 for "a group of X," 集団で for acting "as a group," and 集団の for noun modification.

- 生徒たちは集団で移動しました。 (せいとたちはしゅうだんでいどうしました。) - The students moved as a group.
- 駅の前に観光客の集団がいました。 (えきのまえにかんこうきゃくのしゅうだんがいました。) - There was a group of tourists in front of the station.

The first sentence focuses on collective movement. The second identifies the people as a group rather than counting them individually.

## How the kanji combine

[集](../kanji/集.md) contributes gathering, and 団 a group or body.
Together they present gathered individuals as one collective.
The on-readings are シュウ + ダン; the first vowel is long.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[グループ](グループ.md) is a broadly useful loanword for a group.
[団体](団体.md) (だんたい) can emphasize an organized body or association, although uses overlap.
[個人](個人.md) (こじん), "individual," supplies a useful contrast between collective and individual action.

## Distinctive meaning

集団 does not by itself tell you whether the people have formal membership, share an opinion,
or are simply moving together. Supply that information separately.
Expressions such as 集団生活 (しゅうだんせいかつ), "communal living," emphasize the shared setting rather than any particular group size.
