# 夕方 (ゆうがた) — early evening

## Meaning

夕方 is the later part of the afternoon or early evening, around the approach of night. Its boundary is approximate, not a fixed hour.

- 夕方に帰ります。 (ゆうがたにかえります。) — I will return home in the early evening.
- 夕方、友達に会います。 (ゆうがた、ともだちにあいます。) — I will meet a friend in the early evening.

## How the kanji combine

[夕](../kanji/夕.md) ゆう contributes "evening." [方](../kanji/方.md), with the native reading かた voiced to がた here, helps name the part of the day toward evening.

This is a native kun+kun compound. Learn the voiced がた in the complete word ゆうがた.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [夜](夜.md) よる refers to night and can also correspond to English "evening." It generally points later or more toward darkness than 夕方.
- [朝](朝.md) あさ, morning, names the contrasting beginning of the day.
- 夕日 ゆうひ means the setting sun itself, not the whole time period.

## Distinctive meaning

English "evening" covers a broad span. 夕方 is especially useful for the transition out of daytime; 夜 is often better for nighttime activities. Do not assign a universal clock-time cutoff to either word.
