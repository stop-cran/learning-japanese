# 稲 (いね) - rice plant

## Meaning

稲 is the rice plant, including the crop growing in a field. It is a noun, not the ordinary word for cooked rice on a plate.

- 田んぼで稲が育っています。 (たんぼでいねがそだっています。) - Rice plants are growing in the paddy field.
- 秋に稲を刈ります。 (あきにいねをかります。) - We harvest the rice plants in autumn.

The first example has the plant as the が-marked subject; the second makes it the を-marked object of cutting or harvesting. 稲を育てる (いねをそだてる) means "grow rice plants."

## How the kanji combine

稲 represents the whole native noun いね. This is a single-kanji word, not a combination of two independently pronounced parts. The plant name can also be written イネ in katakana; that spelling does not make it a foreign loanword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[米](米.md) (こめ) names rice as grain, while [ご飯](ご飯.md) (ごはん) can mean cooked rice or a meal. These are related stages and uses, not interchangeable names in every sentence. No ordinary antonym is needed for a plant species.

## Distinctive meaning

English "rice" covers the plant, grain, and food. Japanese lets you distinguish them. When describing the growing crop or its harvest, 稲 is the useful choice; when ordering rice with dinner, it is not.
