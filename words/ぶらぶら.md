# ぶらぶら — dangling; strolling aimlessly

## Meaning

ぶらぶら describes something dangling and swinging, leisurely wandering, or spending time without a particular task.

- 子供が椅子に座って足をぶらぶらさせている。 — The child is sitting on a chair, swinging their dangling legs. (椅子: いす.)
- 買い物のあと、商店街をぶらぶら歩いた。 — After shopping, I strolled around the shopping street. (商店街: しょうてんがい.)
- 今日は家でぶらぶらしていた。 — I spent today loafing around at home.

Use ぶらぶら歩く, optionally with と, or ぶらぶらする. ぶらぶらさせる means to make something dangle or swing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[うろうろ](うろうろ.md) often suggests restless or uncertain wandering. [のんびり](のんびり.md) emphasizes being unhurried and relaxed, without necessarily wandering or idling.

## Distinctive meaning

A stroll can be pleasant; 仕事もしないでぶらぶらしている, "loafing around without working," can be critical. Let the context supply the judgment. Although the dictionary permits adjective-like uses for dangling, ぶらぶらした足 is a safer model than extending な indiscriminately across the wandering and idle senses.
