# 使用 (しよう) - use; utilization

## Meaning

使用 names the use of something. As a transitive する verb, it takes the item used with を: 道具を使用する (どうぐをしようする), "use a tool." It works well in instructions, notices, and explanations of equipment.

- この部屋では携帯電話を使用しないでください。 (このへやではけいたいでんわをしようしないでください。) - Please do not use mobile phones in this room.
- 使用後は電源を切ってください。 (しようごはでんげんをきってください。) - Please turn off the power after use.

使用中 (しようちゅう) means "in use"; 使用方法 (しようほうほう) means "instructions/method of use."

## How the kanji combine

[使](../kanji/使.md) contributes using or employing, and [用](../kanji/用.md) contributes use or function. The two related elements reinforce putting something to use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[使う](使う.md) (つかう) is the straightforward everyday verb and overlaps closely with 使用する. [利用](利用.md) (りよう) can emphasize making use of a resource, service, or opportunity. The distinction is not an absolute division into permitted objects.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "employment" means employing something for a purpose here, not necessarily getting a job. Compare [就職](就職.md) (しゅうしょく) for entering employment. Also distinguish 使用 (しよう) from the identical-sounding volitional しよう, "let's do it": the surrounding noun or verb construction resolves the difference.
