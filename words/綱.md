# 綱 (つな) - rope; cord

## Meaning

綱 is a rope, cord or line used for tying, pulling or supporting.
It often brings a substantial rope to mind, but size alone is not a universal dictionary boundary.

- 船を綱で岸につなぎました。
  (ふねをつなできしにつなぎました。) - I tied the boat to the shore with a rope.
- みんなで綱を引きました。
  (みんなでつなをひきました。) - Everyone pulled on the rope.

The word also names the rope belt of a yokozuna, a sumo grand champion.
That specialized use does not make an ordinary rope a sumo belt.

## How the kanji combine

綱 is a single-character native word, つな. There is no compound or okurigana to divide.
The character's thread-related shape can help recall a rope, but that is not a historical derivation supplied here.
The biological classification term 綱, read こう, is a separate word, not another pronunciation to substitute in these sentences.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[縄](縄.md) (なわ) also means rope or cord; the words overlap rather than dividing all ropes into exclusive categories.
[紐](紐.md) (ひも) often suggests a string, cord or strap.
[コード](コード.md) can mean a cord or cable, but also computer code or a musical chord.
ロープ is the loanword for rope.

There is no ordinary lexical opposite of a rope. Cutting or untying it describes an action, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

つな names the physical line or belt, not "connection" in every abstract sense.
Recognize it in 綱引き (つなひき), "tug-of-war," without changing the standalone reading to こう.
