# おかず - side dishes; food served with rice

## Meaning

おかず names dishes eaten with the staple food of a meal, especially rice.
The word can refer to one dish or several; Japanese does not require
an English-style plural ending.

- 今日のおかずは焼き魚です。 (きょうのおかずはやきざかなです。) - Today's dish to go with the rice is grilled fish.
- お弁当におかずを三品入れました。 (おべんとうにおかずをさんぴんいれました。) - I put three side dishes in the boxed lunch.

三品 (さんぴん) counts three dishes or items of food.

## How the kanji combine

JMdict marks the word as usually kana-written and お菜 and お数 as rare
written forms. This card teaches おかず, not a rule for pronouncing
[菜](../kanji/菜.md) or [数](../kanji/数.md) as かず in other words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

惣菜 (そうざい) and 副食 (ふくしょく) overlap with the meal-accompaniment
sense. [ご飯](ご飯.md) (ごはん) can mean the cooked rice, or the whole
meal in another use. 主食 (しゅしょく), "staple food," provides the relevant contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

English "side dish" can misleadingly suggest that おかず must be a small
vegetable portion. Fish or meat served with rice can be おかず too.
The music and slang senses in the dictionary are separate from this
card's meal examples; do not import them into an ordinary lunch order.
