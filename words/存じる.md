# 存じる (ぞんじる) - know; think (humble)

## Meaning

存じる is a humble verb for knowing or being aware, and for thinking,
believing, or feeling. It presents the speaker's side humbly in a respectful
exchange; it is not an honorific way to describe the listener's knowledge.
It is ichidan: 存じます, 存じません, 存じて.

- そのことは存じております。 (そのことはぞんじております。) - I am aware of that.
- これでよいと存じます。 (これでよいとぞんじます。) - I believe this will be all right.

In the opinion example, と marks the content of the thought. The verb does
not guarantee certainty or agreement from the listener.

## How the kanji combine

[存](../kanji/存.md) uses ゾン here and contributes awareness or thought.
じる belongs to the established verb. This is a Sino-Japanese verb pattern,
not an instruction to add じる freely to any noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知る](知る.md) (しる) and [思う](思う.md) (おもう) supply ordinary knowing
and thinking uses. [存ずる](存ずる.md) (ぞんずる) is the related form with
the same core humble uses.
[ご存じ](ご存じ.md) (ごぞんじ) instead honors the knowledgeable person:
ご存じですか means "Do you know?"

## Distinctive meaning

存じ上げる (ぞんじあげる) is another humble expression used for knowing or
thinking of a respected referent, including a person or their name.
Do not replace every occurrence of 知る mechanically: the relationship
between speaker, listener, and referent matters. In casual conversation
with a close friend, ordinary 知る or 思う is generally more appropriate.
