# 繋げる (つなげる) - connect; link together

## Meaning

繋げる means to connect or link things, and can also mean to tie or fasten something.
It is transitive: mark what is connected with を; に or と can identify the other end.
These uses are usually written in kana: つなげる.

- 二本のケーブルをつなげました。
  (にほんのケーブルをつなげました。) - I connected the two cables.
- パソコンをプリンターにつなげます。
  (パソコンをプリンターにつなげます。) - I will connect the computer to the printer.
- 担当者に電話をつなげてください。
  (たんとうしゃにでんわをつなげてください。) - Please put the call through to the person in charge.

## How the kanji combine

繋 contributes linking. The ending げる is okurigana, and the whole native verb is ichidan:
繋げます, 繋げた, 繋げて, 繋げない.
JMdict also records the outdated spelling 繫げる; it is not the primary spelling taught here.
There is no separate 繋 card in this collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[繋ぐ](繋ぐ.md) (つなぐ) overlaps strongly in connecting and tying, including putting a call through.
[結ぶ](結ぶ.md) (むすぶ) can mean tying or connecting; [接続](接続.md) (せつぞく)する is useful for equipment and networks.
Do not invent an absolute rule that only one of these verbs can describe a cable connection.

[繋がる](繋がる.md) (つながる) describes the connection arising or existing.
切り離す (きりはなす), "disconnect; separate," can express the reverse action.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare ケーブルをつなげる, "connect the cable," with ケーブルがつながる, "the cable is connected."
The shared connection meaning does not erase the object-versus-subject distinction.
Connecting a call is an additional practical use, not a literal claim about tying a telephone with rope.
