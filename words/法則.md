# 法則 (ほうそく) - law; rule

## Meaning

法則 is a law or rule. It can name a regular relationship holding under
specified conditions, such as a scientific law, or a rule that should be followed.

- 科学者は自然の法則を調べています。 (かがくしゃはしぜんのほうそくをしらべています。) - Scientists are investigating the laws of nature.
- この並び方には一定の法則があります。 (このならびかたにはいっていのほうそくがあります。) - There is a consistent rule to this arrangement.

## How the kanji combine

[法](../kanji/法.md) contributes law or principle;
[則](../kanji/則.md) rule/standard. ホウ + ソク gives ほうそく.
方則 is another spelling to recognize; 法則 is the learning headword here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[法律](法律.md) (ほうりつ) concerns legal law or legislation, rather than every
scientific relationship. [規則](規則.md) (きそく) and [決まり](決まり.md)
(きまり) often name rules or regulations. [原理](原理.md) (げんり) concerns
an underlying principle; a law and the principle explaining it can be related
without being identical in every technical discussion.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not impose a strict "nature versus humans" boundary: 法則 can include rules
to observe as well as regularities. Conversely, one isolated coincidence is not
automatically a 法則, and a rule may have conditions on when it applies.
