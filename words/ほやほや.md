# ほやほや — fresh and just made

## Meaning

ほやほや describes something freshly made, newly arrived, or just beginning in a role.

- ほやほやの焼きたてパンを買った。
  — I bought freshly baked bread.
- 彼は社会人ほやほやで、仕事を覚えているところだ。
  — He has just started working life and is learning the job.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ほやほや emphasizes newness and freshness, not simply that something is warm.

## Distinctive meaning

It commonly modifies a noun with の and can also describe a newly begun status.
