# する (する) — do; perform

## Meaning

する means to do or perform an action. It is irregular: します, しない, して, and した, not forms made by treating it as a regular る-verb.

- 週末は何をしますか。 (しゅうまつはなにをしますか。) — What will you do this weekend?
- 毎日、日本語を勉強します。 (まいにち、にほんごをべんきょうします。) — I study Japanese every day.

## How the kanji combine

する is normally written in hiragana. The rare dictionary spelling 為る is not needed for ordinary beginner writing. There is no kanji decomposition in this card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

やる overlaps with "do," particularly in casual speech, but cannot replace する in every fixed expression. 行う (おこなう) is a more formal "carry out." There is no single antonym covering all uses.

## Distinctive meaning

Many established action nouns form verbs with する: 勉強する (べんきょうする), "study," and 散歩する (さんぽする), "take a walk." This is not permission to attach it to any noun. The object may take を, as 日本語 does above.

Learn other common patterns separately: コーヒーにします means "I'll have coffee" when choosing; 音がする (おとがする) means "there is a sound." Neither is translated naturally by mechanically inserting "do."
