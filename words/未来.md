# 未来 (みらい) — the future

## Meaning

未来 is the future, especially when considering the time or world that lies ahead.
It is a noun: 未来について (みらいについて) means "about the future,"
and 未来の followed by a noun means "future ...".

- 子供たちの未来について考えています。 (こどもたちのみらいについてかんがえています。) — I am thinking about the children's future.
- 明るい未来を望んでいます。 (あかるいみらいをのぞんでいます。) — I hope for a bright future.

Two field-specific uses explain the source's "life, tense" parenthetical.
In Buddhist terminology, 未来 can mean a future existence or world to come.
In grammar, it can refer to future tense, as in 未来時制 (みらいじせい),
"future tense." Neither meaning is intended by the ordinary hopes in the
examples above.

## How the kanji combine

[未](../kanji/未.md) contributes "not yet" and [来](../kanji/来.md) contributes "come."
The combination offers a useful meaning guide: the time that has not yet come.
The on readings are ミ + ライ; this explanation concerns the compound, not the historical shapes of its kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[将来](将来.md) (しょうらい) also means "future" and includes the idea of future prospects.
It overlaps with 未来, so a person's future is not forbidden with either word.
[過去](過去.md) (かこ), "the past," supplies the contrasting direction in time.

## Distinctive meaning

未来 is useful for looking ahead broadly, including a distant future; it does not set an exact date
or a fixed distance in years.
In 明るい未来, [明るい](明るい.md) describes encouraging prospects, not literal illumination.
Use a specific day or date when the purpose is to schedule an event.
