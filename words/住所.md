# 住所 (じゅうしょ) - address

## Meaning

住所 is the address of a home, business, or other premises. It can also refer to a person's residence or domicile.

- ここに住所を書いてください。 (ここにじゅうしょをかいてください。) - Please write your address here.
- 新しい住所を教えてください。 (あたらしいじゅうしょをおしえてください。) - Please tell me your new address.

It is a noun; を marks the information written or supplied in these examples.

## How the kanji combine

[住](../kanji/住.md), じゅう, concerns residing, and [所](../kanji/所.md), しょ, means a place. Together they name a residence or its identifying address.

## Synonyms and antonyms

場所 (ばしょ) is a place or location more generally. 宛先 (あてさき) is the intended recipient or destination of a letter or message. These can be related to an address but are not the same information in every setting.

## Distinctive meaning

住所 is the ordinary choice for a street or residential address on a form. For an email address, use メールアドレス; the digital address is not normally called a 住所 without a special metaphorical context.
