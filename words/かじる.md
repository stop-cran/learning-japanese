# かじる - gnaw; dabble in

## Meaning

かじる means biting into, nibbling, or gnawing at something. Figuratively, it means dabbling in a subject or having a little knowledge of it.

- りんごを少しかじりました。 (りんごをすこしかじりました。) - I took a small bite of the apple.
- 学生のころ、フランス語を少しかじりました。 (がくせいのころ、フランスごをすこしかじりました。) - I dabbled in French when I was a student.

This is a transitive godan verb: かじります, かじって, かじらない. The final る does not make it an ichidan verb.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 齧る, 囓る, and 噛る are recorded kanji spellings; recognize them without assuming that the lesson requires one as the everyday written form. The kana-only headword therefore has `kanji: []`.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[噛む](噛む.md) (かむ) means biting or chewing more generally. [もぐもぐ](もぐもぐ.md) depicts small mouth movements in chewing or indistinct speech. For learning, [学ぶ](学ぶ.md) (まなぶ) does not itself restrict the depth of study to a little dabbling.

## Distinctive meaning

フランス語をかじる does not describe biting a language: the object selects the figurative use. Having かじった a subject does not establish expertise. Conversely, the physical verb does not always mean eating an entire item.
