# ぶつぶつ - grumbling; muttering; small bumps

## Meaning

ぶつぶつ describes low muttering or grumbling. As a noun it can also refer to small bumps or spots on the skin; this is a different use, not a sound made by a rash.

- 彼はぶつぶつ文句を言っています。 (かれはぶつぶつもんくをいっています。) - He is grumbling complaints.
- 腕にぶつぶつができました。 (うでにぶつぶつができました。) - Small bumps appeared on my arm.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぼそぼそ](ぼそぼそ.md) overlaps in subdued speech, while [ぶうぶう](ぶうぶう.md) also has a complaining use. [文句](文句.md) (もんく) and [不平](不平.md) (ふへい) name complaints rather than necessarily their quiet delivery.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn ぶつぶつ言う (いう), mutter or grumble, and ぶつぶつができる, develop bumps. The dictionary also records simmering and cutting into small pieces: 鍋がぶつぶつ煮えている (なべがぶつぶつにえている), the pot is simmering, and ぶつぶつと切る (きる), cut into pieces. Do not force the complaint translation into those constructions. ブツブツ is another recorded spelling.
