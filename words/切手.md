# 切手 (きって) — postage stamp

## Meaning

切手 usually means a postage stamp, the small item attached to mail to show that postage has been paid.

- 郵便局で切手を買いました。 (ゆうびんきょくできってをかいました。) — I bought stamps at the post office.
- 封筒に切手を貼ってください。 (ふうとうにきってをはってください。) — Please put a stamp on the envelope.

Count individual stamps with 枚 (まい): 切手を二枚 (きってをにまい), "two stamps."

## How the kanji combine

[切](../kanji/切.md) and [手](../kanji/手.md) form a native compound with the established reading きって. The small っ marks a held consonant; do not read it as きて.

## Synonyms and antonyms

郵便切手 (ゆうびんきって) is the fuller term. はがき means a postcard, the item mailed rather than the stamp attached to it.

## Distinctive meaning

English "stamp" also covers rubber stamps and the act of stamping. Those are not generally 切手. This word's beginner-relevant meaning is the postage item, not any printed mark or seal.
