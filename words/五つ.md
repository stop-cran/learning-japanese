# 五つ (いつつ) — five things

## Meaning

五つ counts five things with the native series: 五つ目 いつつめ (the fifth), 卵を五つ使う (use five eggs).

## How the kanji combine

[五](../kanji/五.md) with the kun reading いつ plus okurigana つ: いつ + つ. Here the usual number reading ゴ does not appear.
Related: 五日 いつか (the 5th day), with the same いつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 五個 ごこ (five pieces).
- Related: 五 ご is the on reading used in 五人 ごにん, 五月 ごがつ.

## Distinctive meaning

いつつ is everyday counting; 五人 is read ごにん (not いつにん).
