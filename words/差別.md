# 差別 (さべつ) - discrimination

## Meaning

差別 can mean distinction or differentiation, but an important everyday use is discrimination against people: treating them unfairly because of a group or characteristic. 差別する is transitive. Mark the person with を and the basis of discrimination with で or を理由に.

- 国籍で人を差別してはいけません。 (こくせきでひとをさべつしてはいけません。) - You must not discriminate against people on the basis of nationality.
- 差別をなくすために話し合いました。 (さべつをなくすためにはなしあいました。) - We discussed how to eliminate discrimination.

差別を受ける (さべつをうける) means "experience discrimination."

## How the kanji combine

[差](../kanji/差.md) contributes difference, and [別](../kanji/別.md) contributes separating or distinguishing. Together they concern making distinctions. The whole word's use about people can carry the additional judgment of unfair treatment; that judgment is not obtained by mechanically translating the characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[区別](区別.md) (くべつ) means distinguishing or telling things apart. Distinguishing categories does not in itself assert unfair treatment, so it is useful when that is not intended.

[平等](平等.md) (びょうどう), "equality," and [公平](公平.md) (こうへい), "fairness," supply contextual contrasts to discriminatory treatment.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 差別 as a neutral substitute for every English "difference." [違い](違い.md) (ちがい) can simply name a difference. In social contexts, 差別する is a consequential description of treatment, not merely an observation that people differ.
