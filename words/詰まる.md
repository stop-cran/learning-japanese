# 詰まる (つまる) - be packed; be clogged

## Meaning

詰まる describes a space being packed or a passage being blocked.
It is intransitive: the container, passage or contents can be the subject, depending on the construction.

- 箱に本が詰まっています。
  (はこにほんがつまっています。) - The box is packed with books.
- 排水管が詰まりました。
  (はいすいかんがつまりました。) - The drainpipe became clogged.

Additional uses include becoming shorter or narrower, and getting stuck for an answer.
言葉に詰まる (ことばにつまる) means "be at a loss for words," not that words literally block a pipe.

- 洗ったら、セーターの丈が詰まりました。
  (あらったら、セーターのたけがつまりました。) - The sweater became shorter when I washed it.

## How the kanji combine

[詰](../kanji/詰.md) contributes packing or pressing together. The written まる belongs to the native verb.
This is godan: 詰まります, 詰まった, 詰まって. It is not ichidan despite ending in る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[詰める](詰める.md) (つめる) commonly describes packing something deliberately:
箱に本を詰める, "pack books into a box," versus 箱に本が詰まる, "the box is packed with books."
[縮む](縮む.md) (ちぢむ) overlaps with the shortening or shrinking use.
込む (こむ) can describe crowding; not every crowded place contains a blocked passage.

空く (あく), "become empty; become available," or 通る (とおる), "go through," can contrast in particular contexts.
Neither is an opposite for every sense of 詰まる.

## Distinctive meaning

Packing, clogging and coming to a verbal impasse share a tight-space image, but have different subjects and particles.
Something can be tightly packed without being dirty, and a blocked pipe need not be full of useful contents.
