# とても - very

## Meaning

とても intensifies a quality: "very" or "extremely." It can modify both
i-adjectives and na-adjectives without changing its own form.

- この本はとても面白いです。 (このほんはとてもおもしろいです。) - This book is very interesting.
- この町はとても静かです。 (このまちはとてもしずかです。) - This town is very quiet.

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is the normal spelling. The dictionary spelling 迚も is rare and
not needed for ordinary use. とっても, with a small っ, is a more emphatic
spoken variant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

非常に (ひじょうに), "very; extremely," is a more formal alternative.
少し (すこし), "a little," expresses a lower degree rather than an absolute
opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

With a negative expression of ability, とても can mean "simply cannot":
一人ではとてもできません (ひとりではとてもできません), "I simply cannot do
it alone." Do not translate that as "cannot do it very much."
For ordinary "not very," あまり plus a negative is the safer beginner pattern.
