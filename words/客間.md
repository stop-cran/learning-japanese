# 客間 (きゃくま) - reception room; guest room

## Meaning

客間 is a room used to receive visitors: a reception room, parlor, or guest room. It is a noun.

- お客様を客間に案内しました。
  (おきゃくさまをきゃくまにあんないしました。) - I showed the visitors into the reception room.
- 客間でお茶を飲みながら話しました。
  (きゃくまでおちゃをのみながらはなしました。) - We talked over tea in the guest room.

The destination takes に in the first sentence; で marks the place of the conversation in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[客](../kanji/客.md), guest, specifies the purpose of the [間](../kanji/間.md), room. Learn the complete reading きゃくま: キャク combines with ま, rather than reading both characters with on readings. This on-first mixed reading uses the repository's `kango` category.

## Synonyms and antonyms

応接間 (おうせつま) also means a reception room. [居間](居間.md) (いま) is the household's living room, and [部屋](部屋.md) (へや) is the general room word. A home's arrangements determine whether one room serves several functions.

## Distinctive meaning

English "guest room" can suggest a bedroom for overnight visitors. 客間 foregrounds receiving guests, not necessarily a dedicated bedroom. This does not mean that guests can never sleep in a room called 客間.
