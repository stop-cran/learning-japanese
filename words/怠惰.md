# 怠惰 (たいだ) — lazy; idle; indolent

## Meaning

怠惰 describes being lazy and slack. Digital Daijisen defines it as なまけてだらしないこと, idling and being sloppy, and gives 怠惰な人 (たいだなひと), “a lazy person.”

- 夏休みの間、彼は怠惰な生活を送っていた。 (なつやすみのあいだ、かれはたいだなせいかつをおくっていた。) — During the summer vacation, he led an idle life.
- 怠惰な自分を変えたくて、毎朝走ることにした。 (たいだなじぶんをかえたくて、まいあさはしることにした。) — Wanting to change my lazy self, I decided to run every morning.

## How the kanji combine

[怠](../kanji/怠.md) (たい) and [惰](../kanji/惰.md) (だ) both mean lazy or idle. Before a noun, use な: 怠惰な生活.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怠ける](怠ける.md) (なまける) is the everyday verb “be lazy,” and [怠る](怠る.md) (おこたる) is to neglect something that should be done. [だらしない](だらしない.md) (sloppy; undisciplined) overlaps. JMdict gives 勤勉 (きんべん), “diligent,” as the antonym of 怠惰, and 怠慢 (たいまん) is negligence.

## Distinctive meaning

惰性 (だせい) shares 惰 but means “force of habit,” not laziness itself.
