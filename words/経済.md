# 経済 (けいざい) — economy and economics

## Meaning

経済 can name an economy or the subject of economics.
The surrounding words tell you whether the topic is economic conditions or studying how an economy works.

- 日本の経済について勉強しています。 (にほんのけいざいについてべんきょうしています。) — I am studying Japan's economy.
- 大学で経済を学んでいます。 (だいがくでけいざいをまなんでいます。) — I am studying economics at university.

経済 is a noun; in the first example, 日本の identifies whose economy is under discussion.

経済 can also refer to someone's finances or financial circumstances. A common related form is
経済的な (けいざいてきな), "economic; financial": 経済的な理由で旅行をやめました。
(けいざいてきなりゆうでりょこうをやめました。) — I canceled the trip for financial reasons.

## How the kanji combine

[経](../kanji/経.md) has meanings including passing through, while [済](../kanji/済.md) includes
settling and bringing something to completion. Those broad character meanings do not by themselves
predict the modern word "economy." Treat 経済 as an established compound, not a literal phrase such as "finished passing."
This is a reading aid, not a proposed history of the term.

The on readings are ケイ and サイ, with the second voiced to ザイ: けいざい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

経済学 (けいざいがく), "economics," explicitly names the field of study.
It overlaps with the study sense of 経済 but is not a substitute in 日本の経済 when that phrase means Japan's economy itself.

## Distinctive meaning

経済 does not merely mean the cash someone has on hand.
It allows discussion of economic activity and conditions at a broader level.
Distinguish the economy being studied from the study of it; English often makes that distinction with "economy" versus "economics."
