# ダイヤモンド - diamond; baseball infield

## Meaning

ダイヤモンド names the mineral diamond and a diamond gemstone.
It also names the baseball diamond or infield and the diamonds suit in cards.
ダイアモンド is another spelling in the same dictionary family.

- 指輪に小さなダイヤモンドがついています。
  (ゆびわにちいさなダイヤモンドがついています。) - A small diamond is set in the ring.
- 選手たちがダイヤモンドに集まりました。
  (せんしゅたちがダイヤモンドにあつまりました。) - The players gathered on the baseball diamond.

The sports context selects the second meaning; it is not a claim that
the players gathered on a gemstone.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English diamond.
JMdict recognizes ダイヤモンド and ダイアモンド without a rarity flag;
the spelling list alone does not establish a frequency ranking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ダイヤ](ダイヤ.md) is an abbreviation for diamond, but can separately
mean a transport schedule. [宝石](宝石.md) (ほうせき) is gemstone more
broadly, not a name restricted to diamonds.
[玉](玉.md) (たま) and [石](石.md) (いし) also have jewel or gem senses;
their other ball and ordinary-stone meanings remain distinct.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every gemstone is a diamond, and the baseball meaning does not name
the whole stadium. The playing-card meaning concerns a suit, not a claim
that the card contains the mineral. Let the surrounding nouns and activity
select the use rather than forcing all three into the jewelry sense.
