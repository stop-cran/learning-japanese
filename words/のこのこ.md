# のこのこ — appearing unconcernedly

## Meaning

のこのこ describes going somewhere or showing up without concern, especially when the circumstances make the action seem shameless or ill-advised.

- 遅刻したのに、彼はのこのこ会議に現れた。
  — Even though he was late, he casually showed up at the meeting.
- 閉店時間を過ぎて、客がのこのこ店に入ってきた。
  — A customer strolled into the shop after closing time.

## Synonyms and antonyms

のこのこ often carries a critical or bemused tone, unlike a neutral description of walking.

## Distinctive meaning

It describes the person's unconcerned manner and the situation's mismatch.
