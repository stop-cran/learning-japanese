# 尽くす (つくす) — use up; devote oneself to

## Meaning

尽くす can mean using all of something or devoting one’s efforts to someone or something. Following the stem of another verb, it can show that the action is carried through completely.

- 町のために力を尽くした。 (まちのためにちからをつくした。) — I devoted my efforts to the town.
- 彼女は長年、地域のために尽くしてきた。 (かのじょはながねん、ちいきのためにつくしてきた。) — She has devoted herself to the community for years. Here there is no を object: 地域のために says whom her work benefits.
- 用意した料理をみんなで食べ尽くした。 (よういしたりょうりをみんなでたべつくした。) — Together we ate up all the food we had prepared.

## How the kanji combine

[尽](../kanji/尽.md) supplies つ, followed by the written kana くす. In 食べ尽くす (たべつくす), 食べ is the stem of 食べる and 尽くす adds completion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

尽きる (つきる) means something runs out: 力が尽きた (ちからがつきた), “my strength ran out.” 力を尽くした instead describes the effort someone put forth. [努力](努力.md) (どりょく) is the noun “effort.” Compare [注ぐ](注ぐ.md) (そそぐ) in 力を注ぐ (ちからをそそぐ), “pour one’s energy into something.”

## Distinctive meaning

最善を尽くす (さいぜんをつくす) means “to do one’s best.” The effort can be complete even when the result one hoped for does not follow.
