# 砂 (すな) - sand

## Meaning

砂 means sand or fine grit. It names the material collectively rather than counting individual grains.

- 靴の中に砂が入りました。 (くつのなかにすながはいりました。) - Sand got into my shoes.
- 子どもたちが砂で遊んでいます。 (こどもたちがすなであそんでいます。) - The children are playing with sand.

In the second sentence, で identifies the material being used for play.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 砂 writes the native reading すな. No okurigana or additional kanji is needed. The on reading さ belongs to compounds, not to these standalone examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

土 (つち) is soil or earth; 石 (いし) is a stone. They are related materials, not direct antonyms. 砂浜 (すなはま) is a sandy beach, the place rather than merely its material.

## Distinctive meaning

砂 need not come from a beach: it may be in a sandbox, on a path, or mixed into another material. If individual grains matter, say 砂の粒 (すなのつぶ), "grains of sand."
