# 湖 (みずうみ) - lake

## Meaning

湖 means a lake, a body of water surrounded by land. It is a noun, not a description of the water's quality or depth.

湖の近くでキャンプをしました。 (みずうみのちかくでキャンプをしました。) - We camped near the lake.

この湖は深いです。 (このみずうみはふかいです。) - This lake is deep.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 湖 writes the complete native noun みずうみ. Keep both ず and the following う when spelling the reading. In lake names, the same character can use the on reading コ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

池 (いけ) means a pond. [海](海.md) (うみ) is the sea, while [川](川.md) (かわ) is a river. These are related geographical categories rather than antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

湖 and 池 are not separated by one universal size rule in everyday naming. Learn the established name of a particular body of water. 湖の水 (みずうみのみず) means "lake water"; の connects the location or source with the water being discussed.
