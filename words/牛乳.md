# 牛乳 (ぎゅうにゅう) -- cow's milk

## Meaning

牛乳 is cow's milk. In an ordinary food or shopping context, English often translates it simply as "milk."

朝ごはんのときに牛乳を飲みます。 (あさごはんのときにぎゅうにゅうをのみます。) -- I drink milk with breakfast.

Use 牛乳を飲む for drinking it and 牛乳を買う (ぎゅうにゅうをかう) for buying it. The liquid is the object of these actions.

## How the kanji combine

[牛](../kanji/牛.md), read ぎゅう, identifies the animal; 乳, read にゅう, means milk. This modifier-and-head compound specifies milk from a cow. Keep both long vowels in ぎゅうにゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ミルク overlaps with "milk," but its meaning depends on context and can cover milk products or preparations. 豆乳 (とうにゅう), soy milk, is a different drink and is not 牛乳.

## Distinctive meaning

The Japanese noun already specifies the source animal. It does not name every pale, milk-like drink, and it does not mean a particular quantity. To request one glass, add a measure: 牛乳を一杯 (ぎゅうにゅうをいっぱい).
