# 料理 (りょうり) — cooking; cuisine; dish

## Meaning

料理 can mean cooking as an activity, a cuisine, or a prepared dish. 料理する means "to cook".

- 毎日、家で料理をします。  
  まいにち、いえでりょうりをします。  
  "I cook at home every day."
- この料理はおいしいです。  
  このりょうりはおいしいです。  
  "This dish is delicious."

## How the kanji combine

The on readings are リョウ + リ. [料](../kanji/料.md) evokes materials or ingredients, while [理](../kanji/理.md) evokes arranging or putting in order. A useful meaning-based memory aid is "handling ingredients to prepare food"; this is not a literal translation of two independent words or a claim about the compound's historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

調理 ちょうり focuses on cooking or food preparation. [食事](食事.md) しょくじ is a meal, which can contain several dishes. There is no natural direct antonym; eating and cooking are related activities, not opposite word meanings.

## Distinctive meaning

The context separates the activity from its result: 料理をする りょうりをする is "cook", while 料理を食べる りょうりをたべる is "eat the prepared food". 料理が上手 りょうりがじょうず describes someone as good at cooking, not good at eating.
