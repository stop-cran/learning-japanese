# 役所 (やくしょ) - government office; public office

## Meaning

役所 is a government or public administrative office. Depending on context, it can refer to the organization or the place where its work is done.

- 役所で住所変更の手続きをしました。
  (やくしょでじゅうしょへんこうのてつづきをしました。) - I completed the change-of-address procedure at the government office.
- この役所は午後五時に閉まります。
  (このやくしょはごごごじにしまります。) - This government office closes at five in the afternoon.

市役所 (しやくしょ), a city office or city hall, is a useful related compound.

## How the kanji combine

[役](../kanji/役.md) contributes official duties, and [所](../kanji/所.md) a place: an office concerned with public administration. The on readings are ヤク + ショ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[官庁](官庁.md) (かんちょう) also refers to government offices or authorities. [事務所](事務所.md) (じむしょ) and [オフィス](オフィス.md) are broader office words and need not involve government.

[役人](役人.md) (やくにん) names a government official, not the office itself. There is no simple opposite office-name.

## Distinctive meaning

The public-administration component matters: a private company's office is not automatically a 役所. Conversely, the word need not refer only to the physical building.
