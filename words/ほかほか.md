# ほかほか — warmly steaming

## Meaning

Comfortably warm, often freshly cooked food, bedding, or a body warmed through. With food it can suggest steam and appetizing heat, without requiring visible steam.

- ほかほかのご飯を茶わんによそった。 — I served some steaming warm rice in a bowl.
- 乾燥機（かんそうき）から出したタオルは、まだほかほかしている。 — The towels from the dryer are still lovely and warm.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぽかぽか](ぽかぽか.md) often emphasizes pleasant bodily or sunny warmth. [あつあつ](あつあつ.md) emphasizes intense heat. [ほくほく](ほくほく.md) describes a fluffy, flaky cooked texture rather than temperature alone.

## Distinctive meaning

ほかほかのご飯 and ほかほかしている are common constructions. The term is not restricted to food: freshly warmed towels or bedding also fit. Do not translate every use as “scalding”; it often foregrounds inviting warmth. For a cool touch, compare [ひんやり](ひんやり.md).
