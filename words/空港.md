# 空港 (くうこう) — airport

## Meaning

空港 is an airport, where aircraft arrive and depart and passengers use travel facilities.

空港までバスで行きます。 (くうこうまでバスでいきます。) — I will go to the airport by bus.

空港で友達を待っています。 (くうこうでともだちをまっています。) — I am waiting for a friend at the airport.

## How the kanji combine

[空](../kanji/空.md) means sky or air, and [港](../kanji/港.md) means harbor or port: an "air port." The on-readings are くう + こう. Keep both long vowels when pronouncing くうこう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

飛行場 (ひこうじょう), "airfield," overlaps but can refer to a facility without the passenger role suggested by an airport. 駅 (えき) is a train station; 港 (みなと) is a harbor. They are transport-location comparisons, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

空港 names the airport as a place, not the airplane or the flight. Use に for arrival or destination, as in 空港に着く (くうこうにつく), "arrive at the airport," and で for an action carried out there. 国際空港 (こくさいくうこう) is an international airport.
