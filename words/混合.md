# 混合 (こんごう) - mixing; mixture

## Meaning

混合 names mixing things together or the resulting mixture. It is a noun and a する verb. What someone mixes takes を; an intransitive description can instead present the things combining with が.

- 二種類の粉を混合します。 (にしゅるいのこなをこんごうします。) - Mix two kinds of powder.
- 空気はいくつかの気体の混合物です。 (くうきはいくつかのきたいのこんごうぶつです。) - Air is a mixture of several gases.

混合する describes combining; 混合した can describe something that has been mixed. 混合物 (こんごうぶつ) names a mixed substance. The word 混合 alone does not specify the proportions.

## How the kanji combine

[混](../kanji/混.md) contributes mixing and [合](../kanji/合.md) joining. The related ideas reinforce bringing different things into a mixture. The on readings are コン + ゴウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[合わせる](合わせる.md) (あわせる) has a mixing/combining use, as well as matching and adjusting uses. 混ぜる (まぜる) is another useful mixing verb. [組み合わせ](組み合わせ.md) (くみあわせ) can emphasize which things are paired or combined, without saying that they are blended into a material.

## Distinctive meaning

Mixing is not automatically [混乱](混乱.md) (こんらん), confusion, or [混雑](混雑.md) (こんざつ), crowding. A mixture can be deliberately made and well controlled. Conversely, 混合 does not promise that everything dissolves or becomes uniform.
