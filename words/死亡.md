# 死亡 (しぼう) — death

## Meaning

死亡 names death or dying. As 死亡する (しぼうする), it is an intransitive verb meaning "die":
the person who dies is the subject, not a direct object.
The noun is useful when treating a death as an event being recorded or described.

- その事故で二人が死亡しました。 (そのじこでふたりがしぼうしました。) — Two people died in that accident.
- 死亡した人の数を調べました。 (しぼうしたひとのかずをしらべました。) — We checked the number of people who died.

## How the kanji combine

[死](../kanji/死.md) means death, and [亡](../kanji/亡.md) concerns being deceased or perishing.
These are reinforcing meanings, not a sequence of two separate events.
The on readings combine as シ + ボウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[死ぬ](死ぬ.md) (しぬ) is the native verb "die."
[亡くなる](亡くなる.md) (なくなる), "pass away," is marked as a euphemism in JMdict: a less direct way to refer to death.
That makes it an important alternative when talking about someone's bereavement, rather than simply substituting compounds.

## Distinctive meaning

死亡 readily names the event as a noun; 死ぬ is a verb.
In 死亡した人, the past verb phrase modifies 人, giving "a person who died."
Do not assume that 死亡 is automatically considerate wording just because it is a Sino-Japanese compound;
the purpose and sensitivity of the conversation still matter.
