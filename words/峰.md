# 峰 (みね) — mountain peak; back of a blade

## Meaning

峰 can mean a mountain peak or ridge. On a blade, the same word names the back, opposite the cutting edge.

- 雲の上に白い峰が見えた。 (くものうえにしろいみねがみえた。) — A white peak was visible above the clouds.
- 図では、包丁の峰と刃が別の色で示されている。 (ずでは、ほうちょうのみねとはがべつのいろでしめされている。) — In the diagram the back of the kitchen knife and its cutting edge are shown in different colors.

## How the kanji combine

[峰](../kanji/峰.md) writes the entire noun みね. In 高峰 (こうほう), “lofty peak,” 峰 is read ほう instead.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頂上](頂上.md) (ちょうじょう) is the summit or top; [麓](麓.md) (ふもと) is the mountain’s foot. For a blade, compare [刃](刃.md) (は), the cutting edge, not the back.

## Distinctive meaning

Look at what the 峰 belongs to. A reference to snow and clouds suggests a mountain; 包丁の峰 (ほうちょうのみね) identifies a particular part of a kitchen knife.
