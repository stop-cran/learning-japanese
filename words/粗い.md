# 粗い (あらい) - coarse; rough

## Meaning

粗い describes something that is not fine, closely spaced or carefully finished:
large grains, an open mesh, or rough workmanship. It is an i-adjective;
粗く (あらく) modifies an action.

- この砂は粒が粗いです。 (このすなはつぶがあらいです。) - This sand has coarse grains.
- この網は目が粗い。 (このあみはめがあらい。) - This net has a coarse mesh.
- この仕事は仕上がりが粗い。 (このしごとはしあがりがあらい。) - The finish on this work is rough.

## How the kanji combine

粗 contributes coarseness or lack of fineness. The native reading is あらい;
do not use the on reading ソ to read this adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ざらざら](ざらざら.md) describes a rough, gritty texture felt on a surface.
[粗末](粗末.md) (そまつ) can describe poor quality or careless treatment.
[細かい](細かい.md) (こまかい), "fine; detailed," contrasts with coarse grains or broad detail;
[丁寧](丁寧.md) (ていねい), "careful," contrasts with rough workmanship.

## Distinctive meaning

[荒い](荒い.md), also あらい, concerns rough force or behavior in examples such as
波が荒い (なみがあらい), "the waves are rough." Official spelling guidance instead
uses 粗い for 網の目が粗い and 仕事が粗い. Do not assume that every rough surface,
rough action and rough personality takes the same character.
