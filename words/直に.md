# 直に (じかに) - directly; firsthand

## Meaning

直に (じかに) is an adverb meaning directly, in person, or firsthand. It describes access or contact without the intermediary that would otherwise be expected. The dictionary marks this use as usually written in kana: じかに.

- 作家にじかに話を聞きました。 (さっかにじかにはなしをききました。) - I heard from the author in person.
- 床にじかに座らないでください。 (ゆかにじかにすわらないでください。) - Please do not sit directly on the floor.

The first example concerns personal contact; the second concerns physical contact without something such as a cushion underneath.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) contributes directness. Dictionaries trace the じか base to a
changed form of the Sino-Japanese reading じき. に belongs to the adverbial expression.
That historical connection does not make the modern meanings interchangeable:
直に also represents the distinct word じきに, contrasted below.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[直接](直接.md) (ちょくせつ) overlaps with "directly." 間接的に (かんせつてきに), "indirectly," is a useful contrast when information comes through another person or route.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep じかに separate from じきに, "soon/right away" or "readily," dictionary entry 1430700. Compare じかに聞く, "hear firsthand," with じきに着く (じきにつく), "arrive soon." The community gloss mixes these identities, but this assignment teaches the supplied じかに identity, not interchangeable pronunciations of one meaning.
