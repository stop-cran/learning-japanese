# 繋がり (つながり) - connection; relationship

## Meaning

繋がり is a connection, link or relationship. It can concern people, things or ideas.
JMdict marks it as usually written in kana: つながり is an important ordinary spelling.

- 地域の人たちとのつながりを大切にしています。
  (ちいきのひとたちとのつながりをたいせつにしています。) - I value my connections with people in the community.
- 二つの事件のつながりを調べています。
  (ふたつのじけんのつながりをしらべています。) - We are investigating the connection between the two incidents.

Use AとBのつながり for the connection between A and B, or 人とのつながり for a connection with a person.

## How the kanji combine

繋 supplies the connection idea. 繋がり is the noun corresponding to [繋がる](繋がる.md) (つながる), "be connected."
The written がり belongs to the native word; this is not an on-reading compound.
There is no separate kanji card for 繋 in the current collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[関係](関係.md) (かんけい) is a relationship or connection; [関連](関連.md) (かんれん) emphasizes relation or relevance.
[接続](接続.md) (せつぞく) often names a connection between lines, equipment or services.
[仲](仲.md) (なか) focuses on an interpersonal relationship.

断絶 (だんぜつ), "severance; a break in relations," can contrast with a continuing connection.
It is not a universal opposite for every physical link or association of ideas.

## Distinctive meaning

This is the **connection itself**, not the act of deliberately connecting things.
Compare つながりがある, "there is a connection," with [繋げる](繋げる.md) (つなげる), "connect."
A connection need not be friendly, romantic or causal unless the context says so.
