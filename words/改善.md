# 改善 (かいぜん) - improvement; betterment

## Meaning

改善 means improvement: changing an unsatisfactory condition, method, or system for the better. 改善する can describe making the improvement with を, or a situation improving with が.

- 仕事の進め方を改善しました。 (しごとのすすめかたをかいぜんしました。) - We improved the way we do our work.
- 交通の状況が改善しました。 (こうつうのじょうきょうがかいぜんしました。) - Traffic conditions improved.

The first sentence names what people changed; the second presents a better state without specifying who brought it about. 改善が必要です (かいぜんがひつようです) means "Improvement is necessary."

## How the kanji combine

改 contributes changing or revising, and 善 contributes goodness. The relationship is changing something toward a better state. Both use on readings: カイ + ゼン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

改良 (かいりょう) also means improvement, especially of an existing thing or method. [上達](上達.md) (じょうたつ) focuses on skill becoming better. 悪化 (あっか), "deterioration," is an opposite for conditions.

## Distinctive meaning

改善 highlights addressing a problem or weakness, not simply making any change. A [変更](変更.md) (へんこう), "change," may help or may not.

The business philosophy of continuous improvement is also called カイゼン. That specialized label does not restrict the ordinary word 改善 to factories or business: everyday working conditions and public services can also be improved.
