# 停車 (ていしゃ) - vehicle stopping; halt

## Meaning

停車 is the stopping of a train, bus, car, or similar vehicle. In the following 停車する examples, the vehicle is what stops; に marks the stopping point.

- この電車は次の駅に停車します。
  (このでんしゃはつぎのえきにていしゃします。) - This train stops at the next station.
- バスが停車してから席を立ってください。
  (バスがていしゃしてからせきをたってください。) - Please wait until the bus has stopped before getting up from your seat.

停車中 (ていしゃちゅう) describes a vehicle while it is stopped. A scheduled station stop and a stop for another reason can both be 停車.

## How the kanji combine

[停](../kanji/停.md) contributes stopping, and [車](../kanji/車.md) identifies the vehicle. The second character is not limited to a private car: trains and buses are included. Read テイ + シャ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[停止](停止.md) (ていし) is broader stopping or suspension; a machine can 停止 without being a vehicle. [発車](発車.md) (はっしゃ) is the vehicle's departure or starting. [停留所](停留所.md) (ていりゅうじょ) names a stopping place, not the stopping action.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every English noun "stop" as 停車: a bus stop as a place is different from the bus coming to a stop. The word also does not by itself give the duration or establish that the vehicle has reached its final destination.
