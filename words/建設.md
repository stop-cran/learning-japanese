# 建設 (けんせつ) - construction; establishment

## Meaning

建設 is a noun for construction or establishment. 建設する is a transitive verb: mark what is being built with を. It can describe infrastructure such as roads and bridges as well as buildings.

- 市は新しい橋を建設しています。 (しはあたらしいはしをけんせつしています。) - The city is building a new bridge.
- 駅の建設には三年かかりました。 (えきのけんせつにはさんねんかかりました。) - Construction of the station took three years.

建設中 (けんせつちゅう) means "under construction." This describes work in progress, not a completed facility.

## How the kanji combine

[建](../kanji/建.md) contributes building or establishing, and 設 contributes setting up or providing. Their related meanings combine in a word for creating a substantial structure or undertaking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[建築](建築.md) (けんちく) concerns buildings and architecture. 建設 also fits civil engineering projects such as roads. [建てる](建てる.md) (たてる) is a native verb used for erecting buildings; it does not replace 建設する in every infrastructure expression.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can extend beyond physical structures, as in 新しい社会の建設 (あたらしいしゃかいのけんせつ), "building a new society." That extension concerns establishing something substantial, not literally assembling walls. Context decides whether the construction is physical or figurative; the kanji alone do not restrict it to houses.
