# 血液 (けつえき) - blood

## Meaning

血液 means blood, especially as a substance being examined or discussed in biological and medical contexts.
It is not a different substance from [血](血.md) (ち).

- 病院で血液検査を受けました。 (びょういんでけつえきけんさをうけました。) - I had a blood test at the hospital.
- 血液は体の中を循環しています。 (けつえきはからだのなかをじゅんかんしています。) - Blood circulates through the body.

## How the kanji combine

[血](../kanji/血.md) contributes blood; [液](../kanji/液.md) contributes liquid or fluid.
The second character identifies the material as a fluid.
The on readings combine as ケツ + エキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[血](血.md) (ち) is the ordinary native noun for blood.
[液体](液体.md) (えきたい) is liquid more generally, not specifically blood.
[血圧](血圧.md) (けつあつ) is blood pressure.
There is no useful single opposite of the substance name.

## Distinctive meaning

The difference from 血 is often one of expression and context, not a strict scientific-versus-everyday division.
Learn established combinations such as 血液検査 rather than replacing every ち with けつえき.
Do not infer a blood type, a disease, or a test result from 血液 alone.
