# 家主 (やぬし) - landlord; house owner

## Meaning

家主 can mean a landlord or landlady, a house owner, or the head of a household. In a rental context, the landlord sense is especially relevant.

- 家主に家賃を払いました。
  (やぬしにやちんをはらいました。) - I paid the rent to the landlord.
- 修理について家主に相談しました。
  (しゅうりについてやぬしにそうだんしました。) - I consulted the landlord about the repairs.

The person consulted or paid takes に in these examples.

## How the kanji combine

[家](../kanji/家.md), here や, contributes house, and [主](../kanji/主.md), ぬし, owner or master. These are native readings, not カ + シュ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大家](大家.md) (おおや) is a close landlord/landlady word. Keep that reading separate from たいか, a great expert, despite the identical characters.

[主人](主人.md) (しゅじん) has proprietor and household-head uses as well as husband. [住民](住民.md) (じゅうみん), resident, does not imply ownership.

## Distinctive meaning

The wider owner/head-of-household sense need not involve renting a property to someone. Conversely, merely being a member of a household does not make someone its 家主.

This card teaches やぬし. The dictionary also recognizes いえぬし; do not substitute an on-reading pronunciation for the word taught here.
