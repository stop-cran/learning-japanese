# ドライブ (ドライブ) - drive; trip by car

## Meaning

ドライブ names a drive or trip by car. In ドライブする, it can describe going for a drive. Use ドライブに行く for "go for a drive," with に marking the purpose of going.

- 日曜日に友達とドライブに行きました。 (にちようびにともだちとどらいぶにいきました。) - I went for a drive with a friend on Sunday.
- 海岸沿いをドライブしました。 (かいがんぞいをどらいぶしました。) - We drove along the coast.

In the second sentence, を marks the route traveled, not a vehicle being acted on. A destination can instead take へ: 海へドライブに行く, "go for a drive to the sea."

## How the kanji combine

ドライブ is a katakana word without kanji components. Read the four written units ド・ラ・イ・ブ; the last sound is ブ, not an English final consonant cluster. The spelling identifies the Japanese word and its Japanese constructions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[運転](運転.md) (うんてん) focuses on operating a vehicle. ドライブ can present the outing or journey as a whole. [旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう), "travel; trip," does not specify transport by car.

## Distinctive meaning

Someone saying ドライブに行きました may be describing an outing they joined; the sentence alone does not establish who drove. For an explicit account of operating the car, 車を運転しました makes that action clear. The trip need not be long or involve staying overnight. There is no useful direct antonym.
