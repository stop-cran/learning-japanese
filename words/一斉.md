# 一斉 (いっせい) - all at once; simultaneous

## Meaning

一斉 describes multiple people or things acting or happening together at
the same time. The useful adverbial pattern is **一斉に + verb**.

- 子供たちは一斉に手を挙げました。 (こどもたちはいっせいにてをあげました。) - The children all raised their hands at once.
- 合図で全員が一斉に走り出しました。 (あいずでぜんいんがいっせいにはしりだしました。) - At the signal, everyone started running at the same time.

It also occurs before another noun in compounds such as 一斉清掃
(いっせいせいそう), a coordinated cleanup.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) contributes unity, and 斉 contributes being aligned
or together. The combined idea is acting in unison. Learn いっせい with
the small っ, not いちせい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[同時](同時.md)に (どうじに) emphasizes the same time.
[一度に](一度に.md) (いちどに) can mean "in one go," even when individual
parts are completed successively within that one occasion.
[いちいち](いちいち.md) can instead describe attending to items separately.

## Distinctive meaning

一斉に focuses on a coordinated or simultaneous action by the relevant
participants. It does not mean that they must all be in one place, or that
the action has happened only once. Keep simultaneity separate from a
numerical count of occasions.
