# 夕立 (ゆうだち) - sudden summer shower

## Meaning

夕立 is a sudden, heavy summer rain shower, typically in the afternoon
or evening. Thunder may accompany it, but is not required.

- 帰り道で夕立にあいました。 (かえりみちでゆうだちにあいました。) - I was caught in a sudden summer shower on the way home.
- 夕立がやんだので、外に出ました。 (ゆうだちがやんだので、そとにでました。) - The summer shower stopped, so I went outside.

## How the kanji combine

[夕](../kanji/夕.md) contributes evening, and [立](../kanji/立.md) evokes
something arising. Learn the native compound ゆうだち as a unit:
the second part is voiced, rather than read たち.
The characters are a memory aid, not a rule that the rain must start
after sunset.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[雨](雨.md) (あめ) is rain generally. にわか雨 (にわかあめ) is a sudden
shower without the same summer association.
[ざあざあ](ざあざあ.md) describes heavy rushing rain or water, not the
name of this weather event.

## Distinctive meaning

The useful contrast is a brief, sudden summer downpour versus rain
in general. Do not translate every evening's rain as 夕立, or reject
an afternoon 夕立 because 夕 suggests evening.
English "shower" here is weather, not the bathing
[シャワー](シャワー.md).
