# 病院 (びょういん) — hospital

## Meaning

病院 means **hospital**. In everyday speech, going to 病院 can also mean going to a doctor or clinic
for an outpatient visit; it does not imply staying overnight.

- 熱があるので、病院に行きます。（ねつがあるので、びょういんにいきます。）— I have a fever, so I am going to the hospital.
- 母は病院で働いています。（はははびょういんではたらいています。）— My mother works at a hospital.

Use に for a destination and で for where an activity takes place.

## How the kanji combine

[病](../kanji/病.md) contributes "illness" and [院](../kanji/院.md) contributes "institution":
an **institution concerned with illness and its treatment**, with 病 modifying 院.
The reading is on + on: ビョウ + イン → びょういん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 診療所（しんりょうじょ）means "clinic" or "medical office," a more specific description of a medical facility.
- [病気](病気.md)（びょうき）means "illness"; it describes a condition, not the place where someone receives care.
- 入院（にゅういん）means hospitalization, while 退院（たいいん）means discharge from hospital.

There is no direct antonym of 病院.

## Distinctive meaning

病院に行く, "go to the hospital/doctor," is not the same as 入院する, "be admitted to hospital."
The distinction matters even when both happen in the same building.

Keep the pronunciation separate from 美容院（びよういん）, "beauty salon":
病院 starts with びょう, while 美容院 starts with び・よう.
