# かゆい (かゆい) - itchy

## Meaning

かゆい is an い-adjective meaning itchy. It is also written 痒い; the kana spelling is sufficient for the examples.

- 背中がかゆいです。 (せなかがかゆいです。) - My back is itchy.
- 昨日は腕がかゆかったです。 (きのうはうでがかゆかったです。) - My arm was itchy yesterday.

The past is かゆかった, and the negative is かゆくない, "not itchy."

## How the kanji combine

This card uses the kana form. In the spelling 痒い, 痒 carries the itch meaning and い completes the adjective. Do not add a kanji reading to the kana spelling when speaking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[むずむず](むずむず.md) can describe an itchy or tickly sensation, as in 鼻がむずむずする (はながむずむずする), "one's nose tickles." It also has a figurative restless-urge use. [ぽりぽり](ぽりぽり.md) can describe light scratching: the action and the sensation are related, not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

かゆい describes how something feels, not the cause or a medical diagnosis. It does not itself say that the person is scratching. Unlike English "itching to leave," the figurative urge to act is not the basic use taught by this adjective.
