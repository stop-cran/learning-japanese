# 接続 (せつぞく) - connection; joining

## Meaning

接続 is connecting or joining things, or their connection.
接続する can be transitive or intransitive:
AをBに接続する connects A to B; AがBに接続する says that A connects to B.

- パソコンをインターネットに接続しました。 (パソコンをインターネットにせつぞくしました。) - I connected the computer to the internet.
- この列車は次の駅で急行に接続します。 (このれっしゃはつぎのえきできゅうこうにせつぞくします。) - This train connects with an express at the next station.

The transport use concerns services connecting so that passengers can transfer.
It does not mean that every connection is itself a passenger changing vehicles.
接続が悪い (せつぞくがわるい) can describe a poor connection; context identifies the kind.

## How the kanji combine

[接](../kanji/接.md) contributes contact or joining, and
[続](../kanji/続.md) contributes continuation.
The related ideas support things joining into a connection.
The on readings are セツ + ゾク: せつぞく, without the small っ of
[接近](接近.md) (せっきん).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[繋ぐ](繋ぐ.md) (つなぐ) is a native transitive connecting verb.
[乗り換える](乗り換える.md) (のりかえる) describes changing vehicles;
[連絡](連絡.md) (れんらく) also has a transport-connection sense.
切断 (せつだん), disconnection, provides a contrast for a joined line.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every connection is a physical wire: devices, services and linguistic units can connect.
The pinned dictionary additionally records 接続 as an abbreviation associated with
接続語 (せつぞくご), a grammatical connective.
接続詞 (せつぞくし) is the specific part-of-speech term "conjunction."
Do not interpret the train example as a grammatical conjunction.
