# 週末 (しゅうまつ) — weekend

## Meaning

週末 means the weekend. It can set the time of an activity with は or に,
or modify another noun with の: 週末の予定 (しゅうまつのよてい), "plans for the weekend."

- 週末は家で休みます。 (しゅうまつはいえでやすみます。) — I'll rest at home this weekend.
- 週末に友達に会います。 (しゅうまつにともだちにあいます。) — I'll meet a friend at the weekend.

## How the kanji combine

[週](../kanji/週.md) supplies "week," and [末](../kanji/末.md) supplies its ending part.
It is a period-plus-ending compound: シュウ + マツ.
Compare 月末 (げつまつ), "end of the month," and 年末 (ねんまつ), "end of the year."

## Synonyms and antonyms

平日 (へいじつ), "weekday; ordinary non-holiday day," is a useful contrast.
A day off is not necessarily a 週末: someone's free day might fall elsewhere in the week.
Likewise, talking about the weekend does not imply that everyone is free from work.

## Distinctive meaning

週末 locates an activity in the weekly cycle rather than describing whether it is work or leisure.
In the second example, the first に marks when the meeting happens and the second に marks whom the speaker meets.
If precise arrangements matter, add the actual day and time rather than relying on the word "weekend" alone.
