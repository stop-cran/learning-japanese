# あっさり — light in flavor; readily

## Meaning

A light, uncomplicated quality, or an action done readily without much resistance or fuss.

- 今日はあっさりしたスープが飲みたい。 — Today I want a light soup.
- 頼（たの）んでみたら、あっさり引き受けて（ひきうけて）くれた。 — When I asked, they readily agreed to take it on.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[さっぱり](さっぱり.md) overlaps in light, refreshing food. [こってり](こってり.md) is a useful contrast for rich or heavy flavor, but not an opposite that works in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

あっさりした味（あじ）does not automatically mean tasteless. あっさり断る（ことわる）can mean refusing outright, while あっさり勝つ（かつ）means winning easily. Those action uses are not descriptions of food.
