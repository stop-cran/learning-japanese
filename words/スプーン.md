# スプーン - spoon

## Meaning

スプーン is a spoon used for eating, serving, or stirring. It is an ordinary noun.

- スプーンでスープを飲みます。 (スプーンでスープをのみます。) - I eat soup with a spoon.
- スプーンを二本ください。 (スプーンをにほんください。) - Please give me two spoons.

で marks the utensil used. 本 (ほん) is a useful counter for spoons; 二本 is にほん.

## How the kanji combine

The English loanword "spoon" is written in katakana. The long mark after プ matters: スプーン, not a reading with a short プ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

さじ also means spoon and appears in cooking measures such as 大さじ (おおさじ), "tablespoon." フォーク and 箸 (はし) name other eating utensils rather than opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

An ordinary dining スプーン is not automatically a standardized measuring spoon. A recipe's 大さじ specifies a measurement, whereas a table spoon's actual capacity may vary.
