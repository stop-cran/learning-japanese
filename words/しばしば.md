# しばしば (しばしば) - often; frequently

## Meaning

しばしば is an adverb meaning "often," "frequently," or "repeatedly."
It describes recurring events without specifying an exact count.
Place it before the predicate without adding に or する for this meaning.

- この道はしばしば渋滞します。 (このみちはしばしばじゅうたいします。) - This road often gets congested.
- 彼とは図書館でしばしば会います。 (かれとはとしょかんでしばしばあいます。) - I often see him at the library.

The events recur; the adverb does not say that they continue without interruption.

## How the kanji combine

The assigned spelling is kana-only. JMdict also lists spellings such as 屡々
but marks the word as usually written in kana. The repeated shape is useful
to recognize; it is not a reason to invent a kanji decomposition or an English
loanword origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[たびたび](たびたび.md) also describes repeated occasions.
[よく](よく.md) can mean "often," as well as "well" in other contexts.
[時々](時々.md) (ときどき), "sometimes," is a useful frequency comparison,
not a precise mathematical opposite. Context determines the actual frequency.

## Distinctive meaning

This general frequency adverb is JMdict entry 1005580. A separate entry,
2179090, describes repeated blinking; identical kana do not make that the
identity behind every sentence meaning "often." The community row supplied
the blinking ID, so this article uses the corrected frequency identity while
keeping that provenance distinct. Neither example here describes blinking.
