# 大変 (たいへん) - difficult, serious

## Meaning

大変 describes something difficult, demanding, or serious. As a na-adjective, it takes な before a noun: 大変な仕事 (たいへんなしごと), "a demanding job."

- 引っ越しは大変でした。 (ひっこしはたいへんでした。) - Moving house was hard work.
- それは大変ですね。 (それはたいへんですね。) - That sounds difficult.

The second expression can acknowledge someone's trouble sympathetically.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md), たい, means "great," and [変](../kanji/変.md), へん, concerns change or unusual conditions. The established adjective is not limited to a literal "big change."

## Synonyms and antonyms

難しい (むずかしい) focuses on difficulty, including intellectual difficulty. 楽な (らくな), "easy, undemanding," can contrast with 大変な when discussing work.

## Distinctive meaning

大変 also works directly as an intensifying adverb, "very": 大変おいしいです (たいへんおいしいです), "It is very delicious." No な is used in that pattern. Do not force the negative sense "troublesome" into every sentence containing 大変; the grammatical use matters.
