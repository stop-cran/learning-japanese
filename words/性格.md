# 性格 (せいかく) — personality; character

## Meaning

性格 names a person's character, personality, or disposition. It describes what someone is like,
not just how they feel at one moment.

- 妹は明るい性格です。 (いもうとはあかるいせいかくです。) — My younger sister has a cheerful personality.

In this example 明るい (あかるい) describes a cheerful disposition, not physical brightness.

## How the kanji combine

[性](../kanji/性.md) contributes the idea of nature; [格](../kanji/格.md) contributes the idea of character.
Together they identify someone's characteristic disposition.
The on readings are セイ + カク. This use of 性 concerns nature, not gender.

## Synonyms and antonyms

性質 (せいしつ) overlaps when describing a person's nature, but also names properties of things
or materials. [気分](気分.md) (きぶん), "mood; feeling," is a useful contrast: a passing mood does not by itself
define someone's 性格. Neither is an opposite of personality.

## Distinctive meaning

Use a description plus 性格, as in 明るい性格, or a statement about it:
二人は性格が違います (ふたりはせいかくがちがいます), "The two have different personalities."
The word is a noun, not a judgment that someone's character is good or bad; the surrounding description
supplies that evaluation.

The dictionary also applies 性格 to the character of things or events: この会議は性格が違います
(このかいぎはせいかくがちがいます), "This meeting is different in character."
That extension does not attribute a human personality to the meeting.
