# 売り切れ (うりきれ) - sold out; no stock left

## Meaning

売り切れ is the sold-out state: the available stock or tickets have
all been sold. It is a noun, so 売り切れです means "It is sold out."

- このパンはもう売り切れです。 (このパンはもううりきれです。) - This bread is already sold out.
- チケットは売り切れでした。 (チケットはうりきれでした。) - The tickets were sold out.

売り切れの品 (うりきれのしな) means "an item that is sold out."

## How the kanji combine

[売](../kanji/売.md) contributes selling; [切](../kanji/切.md)
contributes completion or exhaustion in this expression, not cutting a
physical object. The native parts are 売り (うり) + 切れ (きれ),
also found in [売り切れる](売り切れる.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

売り切れる (うりきれる) is the verb "be sold out." Compare
パンが売り切れる with パンは売り切れです. [販売](販売.md)
(はんばい), "sale; selling," does not itself say that stock has run out.

## Distinctive meaning

売り切れ does not mean a shop is closed, a product has a discount, or an
item was never stocked there. It describes selling out. A new delivery or
another performance can change availability, so it does not necessarily
mean the item will never be sold again.
