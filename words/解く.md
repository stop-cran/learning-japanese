# 解く (とく) - untie; solve

## Meaning

解く is a transitive godan verb. It can mean undoing a fastening or working out a problem. The thing untied or solved takes を. Its forms include 解きます, 解いて, and 解いた.

- 靴ひもを解きました。 (くつひもをときました。) - I untied my shoelaces.
- この問題を一緒に解きましょう。 (このもんだいをいっしょにときましょう。) - Let's solve this problem together.

The second example concerns finding an answer, not explaining a finished answer to someone else.

## How the kanji combine

[解](../kanji/解.md) contributes untying, releasing, or resolving. It writes the native stem と-, with く completing the verb. Physical unfastening and working out a problem are established uses, not interchangeable translations in every sentence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結ぶ](結ぶ.md) (むすぶ), "tie," is a useful opposite for the shoelace example, not for every sense of 解く. [分かる](分かる.md) (わかる), "understand," concerns understanding, whereas 解く describes working out or resolving something.

## Distinctive meaning

The same verb can mean clearing up a misunderstanding: 誤解を解く (ごかいをとく). This extends the idea of removing what prevents understanding. Do not use the physical gloss "unfasten" as a universal instruction for opening doors or turning off devices; the object and the established expression matter.
