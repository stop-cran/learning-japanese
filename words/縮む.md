# 縮む (ちぢむ) - shrink; contract

## Meaning

縮む means to shrink or become smaller in size or extent.
It is an intransitive godan verb: the thing shrinking takes が.

- 洗濯したら、セーターが縮みました。 (せんたくしたら、セーターがちぢみました。) - The sweater shrank when I washed it.
- 寒さで体が縮むように感じました。 (さむさでからだがちぢむようにかんじました。) - I felt as though my body were shrinking from the cold.

Forms include 縮みます (ちぢみます), 縮んで (ちぢんで),
縮んだ (ちぢんだ), and 縮まない (ちぢまない).
The past is not ちぢみた.

## How the kanji combine

縮 contributes shrinking or contracting. In this native verb it writes
ちぢ, followed by む. The primary reading here is ちぢむ;
JMdict also records ちじむ. Recognizing that variant does not change
this card's primary spelling or conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[縮める](縮める.md) (ちぢめる) is transitive: 長さを縮める
(ながさをちぢめる), "shorten the length."
[伸びる](伸びる.md) (のびる), "grow longer; stretch," gives
a contrast in physical dimensions.
[縮小](縮小.md) (しゅくしょう) concerns reduction in size or scale.

## Distinctive meaning

縮む describes the change without naming an agent who causes it.
It does not necessarily mean losing individual items, as in
[減る](減る.md) (へる), "decrease; become fewer."
The character also occurs in [縮れる](縮れる.md) (ちぢれる),
used for wavy or curled hair. Learn these as distinct verbs rather
than making every occurrence of 縮 mean only shorter length.
