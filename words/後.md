# 後 (あと) — after; later

## Meaning

後 means "after" or "later." Use noun + の後 or a verb's plain past form + 後. 後で means "later" or "afterward."

- 授業の後で話しましょう。 (じゅぎょうのあとではなしましょう。) — Let's talk after class.
- ご飯を食べた後、散歩しました。 (ごはんをたべたあと、さんぽしました。) — I took a walk after eating.

It can also name what remains: 後五分 (あとごふん), "five more minutes."

The same character has other readings with their own constructions:

- **ご** means "after," often as a suffix: 一時間後に戻ります (いちじかんごにもどります), "I will return in one hour." Compare 食後 (しょくご), "after a meal."
- **のち** means "later; afterwards": 詳細は後ほどお知らせします (しょうさいはのちほどおしらせします), "We will provide the details later." 後の世 (のちのよ), "future generations; a future age," illustrates its future sense.
- **うしろ**, normally written [後ろ](後ろ.md), means "back; behind; rear": 家の後ろに庭があります (いえのうしろににわがあります), "There is a garden behind the house." Writing 後 without ろ for this reading omits the usual okurigana.

## How the kanji combine

This is the single character [後](../kanji/後.md), read あと as a native word. Its other readings belong to other expressions; 五分後 (ごふんご) means "five minutes later."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[前](前.md) (まえ), "before," provides the time contrast. 後ほど (のちほど) is a more formal way to say "later."

## Distinctive meaning

後 is a noun in patterns such as 食事の後 (しょくじのあと), not a particle that can simply follow any dictionary-form verb. Use 食べた後, not 食べる後, for "after eating."

Do not substitute うしろ in that temporal pattern. The community source pairs うしろ with a conflicting time gloss; this card preserves the supported spatial identity separately from あと, ご, and のち. The compiler's intended reading cannot be recovered from that mismatch.
