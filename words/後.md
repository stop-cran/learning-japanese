# 後 (あと) — after; later

## Meaning

後 means "after" or "later." Use noun + の後 or a verb's plain past form + 後. 後で means "later" or "afterward."

- 授業の後で話しましょう。 (じゅぎょうのあとではなしましょう。) — Let's talk after class.
- ご飯を食べた後、散歩しました。 (ごはんをたべたあと、さんぽしました。) — I took a walk after eating.

It can also name what remains: 後五分 (あとごふん), "five more minutes."

## How the kanji combine

This is the single character [後](../kanji/後.md), read あと as a native word. Its other readings belong to other expressions; 五分後 (ごふんご) means "five minutes later."

## Synonyms and antonyms

前 (まえ), "before," provides the time contrast. 後ほど (のちほど) is a more formal way to say "later."

## Distinctive meaning

後 is a noun in patterns such as 食事の後 (しょくじのあと), not a particle that can simply follow any dictionary-form verb. Use 食べた後, not 食べる後, for "after eating."
