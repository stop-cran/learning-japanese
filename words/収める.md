# 収める (おさめる) - put away; achieve

## Meaning

収める is a transitive ichidan verb for putting something into its place or achieving a result. Use **物を場所に収める** for putting an item into a place, and **成果を収める** (せいかをおさめる) for achieving results.

- 本を棚に収めました。 (ほんをたなにおさめました。) - I put the books away on the shelf.
- チームは良い成績を収めました。 (チームはよいせいせきをおさめました。) - The team achieved good results.

It can also mean accepting a gift: どうぞお収めください (どうぞおおさめください), "Please accept this." That polite invitation does not mean "please achieve this."

## How the kanji combine

[収](../kanji/収.md) suggests gathering or taking in. The kana める complete the native verb, whose forms include 収めます and 収めて. The "taking in" association helps connect storage and acquisition without predicting every collocation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入れる](入れる.md) (いれる) broadly means "put in"; 収める can suggest placing something where it belongs. [得る](得る.md) (える) overlaps with the gaining sense, but cannot replace the verb in every storage expression.

## Distinctive meaning

The same reading is also written **納める**. For paying taxes or supplying goods, use that spelling: 税金を納める (ぜいきんをおさめる), "pay taxes." A shared reading does not make every meaning interchangeable between 収 and 納. Keep the achievement phrase 成績を収める separate from payment.
