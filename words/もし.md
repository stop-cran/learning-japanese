# もし -- if; supposing

## Meaning

もし introduces an imagined condition and makes the "if" setting explicit. It is an adverb used with a conditional sentence.

- もし雨が降ったら、家にいます。 (もしあめがふったら、いえにいます。) -- If it rains, I will stay home.
- もし時間があれば、一緒に散歩しませんか。 (もしじかんがあれば、いっしょにさんぽしませんか。) -- If you have time, would you like to take a walk together?

The first example uses たら; the second uses ば. もし introduces the possibility, while the verb ending supplies the conditional grammar.

## How the kanji combine

This word is normally written in hiragana. 若し is a dictionary-listed rare spelling. The kana form is the practical choice here, and there are no written kanji to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

Conditional endings can often express "if" without もし. Adding it foregrounds the hypothetical situation. 必ず (かならず), certainly or without fail, is not an antonym to insert into the same grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

もし alone does not turn any ordinary statement into a complete conditional. Learn it with a suitable ending and a following consequence or response. It is unrelated in function to the calling expression もしもし.
