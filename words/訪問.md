# 訪問 (ほうもん) - visit; call on

## Meaning

訪問 is a visit or call on someone. 訪問する is transitive, with を marking the person, home, organization, or place visited. 訪問の目的 (ほうもんのもくてき) means "the purpose of the visit."

- 昨日、先生のお宅を訪問しました。 (きのう、せんせいのおたくをほうもんしました。) - I visited my teacher's home yesterday.
- 来週、その会社を訪問する予定です。 (らいしゅう、そのかいしゃをほうもんするよていです。) - I plan to visit that company next week.

The second sentence presents a scheduled visit; 訪問 itself does not establish whether an appointment has been made.

## How the kanji combine

[訪](../kanji/訪.md) contributes visiting, and [問](../kanji/問.md) contributes asking or inquiring. The compound names calling on someone; it does not imply that every visit is an interview. The reading is ホウ + モン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[訪ねる](訪ねる.md) (たずねる) is a native verb for visiting and overlaps with 訪問する. [見舞い](見舞い.md) (みまい) concerns a visit or expression of concern connected with illness or misfortune, rather than every kind of visit.

## Distinctive meaning

English "call" can mean telephoning, but a 訪問 is a visit, not simply a telephone call. [電話](電話.md)する is the basic expression for phoning. 訪問 works in descriptions of both personal and organizational visits; it need not imply tourism or a long stay. There is no single general antonym.
