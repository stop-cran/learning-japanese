# どんな (どんな) - what kind of

## Meaning

どんな goes directly before a noun to ask about its kind, qualities, or character. It is a pre-noun expression, not an i-adjective or an ordinary na-adjective.

- どんな音楽が好きですか。 (どんなおんがくがすきですか。) - What kind of music do you like?
- 新しい先生はどんな人ですか。 (あたらしいせんせいはどんなひとですか。) - What is the new teacher like?

With でも, the pattern can mean "any; no matter what": どんな質問でもいいです (どんなしつもんでもいいです), "Any question is fine."

## How the kanji combine

This word is written in kana. It belongs with こんな, "this kind of," そんな, "that kind of," and あんな, "that kind over there." The final な is already part of どんな; do not add another な before the noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[どの](どの.md) asks which member of a set: どの本 (どのほん), "which book?" どんな本 asks what sort of book. [どれ](どれ.md), "which one?", can stand without a following noun, unlike the basic どんな + noun pattern.

## Distinctive meaning

An answer need not be a formal category. どんな人ですか can invite "a kind person" or a description of someone's character. In a question, it seeks information; in どんな...でも, it broadens the range rather than asking the listener to choose one kind.
