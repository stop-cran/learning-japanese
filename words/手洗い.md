# 手洗い (てあらい) - handwashing; restroom

## Meaning

手洗い names washing one's hands or a place or basin for doing so. It can also mean a restroom, especially in お手洗い (おてあらい). A further use is washing clothes by hand rather than by machine.

- 食事の前に手洗いをしましょう。
  (しょくじのまえにてあらいをしましょう。) - Let's wash our hands before eating.
- お手洗いはどこですか。
  (おてあらいはどこですか。) - Where is the restroom?
- このセーターは手洗いしてください。
  (このセーターはてあらいしてください。) - Please wash this sweater by hand.

The first example uses 手洗いをする, and the third uses 手洗いする. Both express washing. For the body-part action, 手を洗う (てをあらう), "wash one's hands," is another clear construction.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md), て, contributes the hand, and [洗](../kanji/洗.md), あら, washing. The hand is what gets washed in one use and the means of washing in another. Learn the distinction from the object and context.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お手洗い](お手洗い.md) is a polite restroom expression; [トイレ](トイレ.md) also names a toilet or restroom. [洗濯](洗濯.md) (せんたく) concerns laundry without itself specifying washing by hand.

## Distinctive meaning

A clothing label marked 手洗い is not instructing you to wash your hands. Conversely, a request for the お手洗い normally asks for the restroom, not a laundry service. The same spelling therefore needs a context-sensitive translation.
