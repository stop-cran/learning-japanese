# 越す (こす) - cross over; pass beyond

## Meaning

越す is a godan verb for crossing something or passing through a period. The crossed boundary or elapsed period can take を. Learn 越します, 越して, and 越した.

- 山を越すと、海が見えます。 (やまをこすと、うみがみえます。) - When you cross the mountain, you can see the sea.
- 今年は実家で年を越します。 (ことしはじっかでとしをこします。) - This year I will see in the New Year at my family home.

It can also mean exceeding a quantity or moving house: 東京に越した (とうきょうにこした), "moved to Tokyo."

## How the kanji combine

[越](../kanji/越.md) supplies the idea of crossing or going beyond, read こ- here; す is okurigana. 超す is another spelling in the dictionary, especially for exceeding a limit or quantity. That preference is not a blanket spelling prohibition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[越える](越える.md) (こえる) overlaps in crossing and exceeding uses, but expressions should be learned as complete units. [引っ越す](引っ越す.md) (ひっこす) explicitly identifies moving house.

## Distinctive meaning

年を越す marks passing into the next year, not crossing a physical object. Conversely, English "the speech went over well" concerns audience reception and is not a meaning of 越す. The source list's audience-related gloss should not guide Japanese usage.
