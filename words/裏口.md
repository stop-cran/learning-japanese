# 裏口 (うらぐち) - rear entrance; back door

## Meaning

裏口 is an entrance or exit at the back of a building. It is a noun:
裏口から means "through/from the back entrance," and 裏口に identifies
that entrance as a location or destination.

- 裏口から店に入りました。 (うらぐちからみせにはいりました。) - I entered the shop through the back entrance.
- 荷物は裏口に置いてください。 (にもつはうらぐちにおいてください。) - Please leave the packages at the back entrance.

## How the kanji combine

[裏](../kanji/裏.md), "back; rear," modifies [口](../kanji/口.md),
an opening or entrance here. The native readings combine as うら + ぐち:
口's くち is voiced in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入口](入口.md) (いりぐち), "entrance," and [出口](出口.md)
(でぐち), "exit," describe function without specifying the back of a building.
[玄関](玄関.md) (げんかん) names the main entrance or entryway in the
ordinary house comparison; it is not the default word for a rear service entrance.

## Distinctive meaning

The physical rear entrance taught here need not be secret or unauthorized.
Staff, deliveries or ordinary visitors may use it legitimately. Likewise,
the word identifies the entrance, not necessarily just the movable door panel.
