# サークル - club; circle

## Meaning

サークル names a group or club whose members share an interest. A university hobby group or a company's sports club can be a サークル; it is not restricted to company sports.

- 大学で写真のサークルに入りました。 (だいがくでしゃしんのサークルにはいりました。) - I joined a photography club at university.
- 週末にサークルの仲間と練習します。 (しゅうまつにサークルのなかまとれんしゅうします。) - I practice with my fellow club members on weekends.

It also means a circular shape: 紙にサークルを描きました (かみにサークルをかきました), "I drew a circle on the paper."

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword comes from English **circle**. There are no kanji components. Keep the long vowel in サー.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[グループ](グループ.md) means a group more generally. [会](会.md) (かい) can mean an association or club, as well as a gathering. [仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) can concern fellow members or the group they form.

For the shape, compare [丸](丸.md) (まる), [輪](輪.md) (わ), and [円](円.md) (えん). Their other meanings, such as a ring-shaped object or Japanese yen, are not automatically meanings of サークル.

## Distinctive meaning

Shared interests, not a fixed number of members, characterize the club use. The word alone does not establish how formal the organization is or whether it competes as a team. Joining a サークル and drawing a サークル are different senses of the same written word.
