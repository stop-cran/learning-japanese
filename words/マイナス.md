# マイナス - minus; negative; disadvantage

## Meaning

マイナス expresses minus or subtraction, a negative number, or a negative effect. It also names a deficit, loss, or disadvantage.

- 五マイナス二は三です。 (ごマイナスにはさんです。) - Five minus two is three.
- 気温はマイナス五度です。 (きおんはマイナスごどです。) - The temperature is minus five degrees.
- その経験は決してマイナスにはなりません。 (そのけいけんはけっしてマイナスにはなりません。) - That experience will certainly not be a disadvantage.

マイナスする can mean subtract or take away; a calculation can also be expressed with [引く](引く.md) (ひく). マイナスの before a noun can mean negative or unfavorable, as in マイナスの影響 (マイナスのえいきょう), "a negative effect."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[引き算](引き算.md) (ひきざん) is subtraction. [不利](不利.md) (ふり) is disadvantage, and [損](損.md) (そん) can mean loss or disadvantage.

プラス, "plus; positive; advantage," is the counterpart in arithmetic and many evaluations. A negative number is not the same concept as a decrease: a quantity can fall while remaining above zero.

## Distinctive meaning

Context selects the domain. マイナス can refer to the minus sign, negative electric charge or a negative terminal, an unfavorable image, or a negative test result. For a test, "negative" does not mean a bad outcome; it means the tested-for result was not detected.

The financial loss sense is not a blanket translation for losing a sports match. Likewise, do not treat every English technical use of "negative" as interchangeable without identifying what is negative.
