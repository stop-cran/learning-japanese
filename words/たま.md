# たま (たま) - occasional; infrequent

## Meaning

たま means "occasional; infrequent." Two useful patterns are **たまの + noun**, "an occasional ...," and **たまに + verb**, "occasionally; once in a while."

- たまに、この店で昼ご飯を食べます。 (たまに、このみせでひるごはんをたべます。) - I eat lunch at this shop once in a while.
- たまの休みは、家でゆっくり過ごします。 (たまのやすみは、いえでゆっくりすごします。) - On an occasional day off, I relax at home.

The expression describes low frequency. It does not specify a fixed interval such as once a month.

## How the kanji combine

This word is usually written in kana. 偶 and 適 are recorded spellings for たま in this meaning, but the page uses the straightforward kana form. There is no compound to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時々](時々.md) (ときどき), "sometimes," also describes events that recur, with overlapping use. [偶然](偶然.md) (ぐうぜん), "by chance," instead concerns whether an event was coincidental. Infrequent does not necessarily mean accidental.

## Distinctive meaning

With this reading, 偶 means "occasional," not "even number," "couple," or "friend." Also distinguish たまに from たまたま, "by chance": an occasional restaurant visit can be planned. Learn frequency and coincidence as separate ideas rather than deriving a meaning from the shared kana.
