# 所 (ところ) — place; spot

## Meaning

所 means a place or spot. It is often written ところ, especially when the meaning is abstract rather than a physical location.

- 静かな所で休みましょう。 (しずかなところでやすみましょう。) — Let's rest somewhere quiet.
- 駅から近い所に住んでいます。 (えきからちかいところにすんでいます。) — I live somewhere near the station.

## How the kanji combine

[所](../kanji/所.md) alone writes the native reading ところ. The on-reading しょ occurs in compounds, but is not the reading of this standalone word. Casual speech may shorten ところ to とこ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

場所 (ばしょ) also means place or location. ここ, "here," points to a particular contextually identified place. There is no direct antonym for the general noun.

## Distinctive meaning

所 can also mean an aspect or part: いいところ, "a good point." In grammatical time-stage patterns, kana ところ is useful: 今から出かけるところです (いまからでかけるところです), "I am just about to go out." That pattern uses a plain nonpast verb before ところ and should not be translated as a literal physical place. Learn its other time-stage forms separately.
