# 女の子 (おんなのこ) — girl

## Meaning

女の子 means a girl, including a baby girl. It can refer to someone's daughter when the family relationship is already clear, but it does not inherently mean "my daughter."

公園で女の子が遊んでいます。 (こうえんでおんなのこがあそんでいます。) — A girl is playing in the park.

## How the kanji combine

[女](../kanji/女.md), おんな, "woman; female," connects through の to [子](../kanji/子.md), こ, "child." Both words have native readings. Learn the whole expression for "girl."

## Synonyms and antonyms

男の子 (おとこのこ), "boy," is the corresponding expression. 娘 (むすめ) more directly identifies a daughter. 女の人 (おんなのひと) means a woman.

## Distinctive meaning

Speakers sometimes use 女の子 for a young adult woman, but the child-associated wording can sound patronizing in professional or formal settings. 女性 (じょせい), "woman; female," is often more suitable there. 女の子 is not limited to a speaker's own children.
