# ぼうっと — in a daze; hazily

## Meaning

A vacant, unfocused state, or something visible dimly and indistinctly.

- 寝起（ねお）きで、まだぼうっとしている。 — I have just woken up and am still in a daze.
- 霧（きり）の中に明かりがぼうっと見えた。 — A light was dimly visible in the fog.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぼんやり](ぼんやり.md) strongly overlaps in absent-mindedness and indistinct appearance. [もやもや](もやもや.md) overlaps in haziness, but also describes unresolved feelings.

## Distinctive meaning

The familiar spelling ぼーっと is another form of the same word, not a separate card. The dictionary also records a roaring or flaring-fire use; that is distinct from a person sitting vacantly.
