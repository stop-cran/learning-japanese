# 願う (ねがう) — wish for; request

## Meaning

願う (ねがう) means to wish or hope for something; it can also mean to request or pray for it.
Use a desired thing with を, or describe a desired outcome with a clause followed by ように.

- みんなの幸せを願っています。 (みんなのしあわせをねがっています。) — I hope everyone will be happy.
- また会えるように願っています。 (またあえるようにねがっています。) — I hope we can meet again.

The first example names the wish's object; the second gives the outcome, "be able to meet again."

For a request addressed to others:

- 皆様のご協力を願います。 (みなさまのごきょうりょくをねがいます。) — We ask for everyone's cooperation.

This is a transitive godan verb. Its negative is 願わない (ねがわない).

## How the kanji combine

[願](../kanji/願.md) supplies the wishing or requesting meaning and writes ねが.
う completes the native verb. Its て-form is 願って (ねがって), and its past is 願った (ねがった).
The related noun 願い (ねがい) names a wish or request.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[望む](望む.md) (のぞむ) overlaps with wishing or hoping for something.
[頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ) highlights a request addressed to someone for an action or favor.
A wish expressed with 願う need not identify a person who could carry it out.
This is a distinction in focus, not a claim that 願う cannot make a request.

## Distinctive meaning

Wanting an outcome does not assert that it will happen.
Recognize the polite request expression お願いします (おねがいします), "please; I ask this favor,"
but do not assume that the dictionary form 願う has the same politeness or can replace the whole expression unchanged.
