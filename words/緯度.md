# 緯度 (いど) - latitude

## Meaning

緯度 is latitude: an angular measure locating a place north or south of
the equator. North latitude is 北緯 (ほくい); south latitude is
南緯 (なんい).

- 地図でこの町の緯度を調べました。 (ちずでこのまちのいどをしらべました。) - I checked this town's latitude on a map.
- 緯度は度で表します。 (いどはどであらわします。) - Latitude is expressed in degrees.

This is an angle, not a distance stated directly in kilometers.

## How the kanji combine

緯 has latitude and horizontal-thread associations;
[度](../kanji/度.md) contributes degree or measure.
The on readings are イ + ド. The character associations help recall
the coordinate term; they are not a historical explanation of its origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

経度 (けいど), "longitude," is the corresponding east-west coordinate.
Latitude and longitude locate a place together; they are not synonyms or
opposites. A high latitude means farther from the equator, not necessarily
a place at a high altitude.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse [井戸](井戸.md) (いど), "water well," with this word.
The equal readings do not make their written forms interchangeable.
An address, an elevation, and a latitude identify different information
about a place.
