# 車庫 (しゃこ) - garage; vehicle depot

## Meaning

車庫 is a garage, carport, or depot used to house vehicles. Depending on context, the vehicles may be private cars, buses, or trains.

- 車を車庫に入れました。
  (くるまをしゃこにいれました。) - I put the car in the garage.
- バスは夜になると車庫に戻ります。
  (バスはよるになるとしゃこにもどります。) - The buses return to the depot at night.

車庫入れ (しゃこいれ) means putting a car into a garage or parking it in that space.

## How the kanji combine

[車](../kanji/車.md) contributes vehicles, and [庫](../kanji/庫.md) a place for storage. The vehicle specifies what is housed. Read シャ + コ as **しゃこ**; this is not くるまくら.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[駐車場](駐車場.md) (ちゅうしゃじょう) is a parking place or parking facility. It can be indoors, including a parking garage, so "車庫 indoors, 駐車場 always outdoors" is not a reliable distinction. 車庫 foregrounds housing or keeping vehicles.

[小屋](小屋.md) (こや) is a hut or shed more generally, not specifically a vehicle facility. There is no general opposite building.

## Distinctive meaning

車庫 does not itself mean a repair business, a railway station, or a place restricted to one private automobile. "Depot" here concerns where vehicles are kept, not every logistics warehouse or passenger terminal.
