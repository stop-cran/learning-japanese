# 迎える (むかえる) — welcome; go to meet

## Meaning

迎える means to receive or welcome someone, including going out to meet them.
The person received takes を.

- 駅で友達を迎えました。 (えきでともだちをむかえました。) — I welcomed my friend at the station.
- 空港で家族を迎えます。 (くうこうでかぞくをむかえます。) — I will meet my family at the airport.

It is an ichidan verb: 迎えます and 迎えた replace the final る.

## How the kanji combine

[迎](../kanji/迎.md) carries the receiving or greeting meaning.
The native reading is むかえる, and える is written in kana.
There is one written kanji, not a compound assembled from two separate words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会う](会う.md) (あう) means to meet: 友達に会う (ともだちにあう).
迎える makes the receiving role explicit and uses 友達を迎える instead.
[送る](送る.md) (おくる), in its escorting or seeing-off sense, gives a useful counterpart:
友達を駅まで送る (ともだちをえきまでおくる), "take a friend to the station."

## Distinctive meaning

会う can describe a chance meeting. 迎える here focuses on receiving someone who arrives.
The verb does not by itself say whether you drove, walked, or waited at the meeting point.
歓迎 (かんげい) also means welcome, with a warm-reception sense.
