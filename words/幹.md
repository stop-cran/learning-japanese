# 幹 (みき) - tree trunk; backbone

## Meaning

幹 is the trunk of a tree, as distinct from its branches. It can also mean a backbone
or foundation figuratively. It is a noun.

- この木は幹が太いです。 (このきはみきがふといです。) - This tree has a thick trunk.
- 木の幹に触れました。 (きのみきにふれました。) - I touched the tree's trunk.

## How the kanji combine

[幹](../kanji/幹.md) is a single character read みき in this native noun.
Its on reading カン occurs in compounds such as [新幹線](新幹線.md) (しんかんせん).
Those compounds do not change the reading of the standalone noun to かん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[枝](枝.md) (えだ) is a branch; [木](木.md) (き) names a tree or wood more generally.
For a figurative foundation, [基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) and [基盤](基盤.md) (きばん)
overlap in meaning, but do not ordinarily name a tree's trunk.

## Distinctive meaning

English "trunk" can also mean luggage or an elephant's trunk. 幹 does not name those things.
The dictionary separately records 幹/柄 read から, "trunk; stem; stalk."
That separate word is not a reason to pronounce every 幹 either way.
