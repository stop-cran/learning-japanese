# 知恵 (ちえ) - wisdom; practical ingenuity

## Meaning

知恵 is wisdom, good sense, or the ingenuity used to find a way forward. It is a noun, used in expressions such as 知恵を出す (ちえをだす), "come up with ideas," and 知恵を借りる (ちえをかりる), "draw on someone's wisdom."

- みんなで知恵を出して、問題を解決しました。 (みんなでちえをだして、もんだいをかいけつしました。) - We pooled our ideas and solved the problem.
- 祖母の知恵を借りたいです。 (そぼのちえをかりたいです。) - I would like to draw on my grandmother's wisdom.

The second example can be a request for advice, but 知恵 is not simply the noun for any message giving instructions.

## How the kanji combine

[知](../kanji/知.md) contributes knowing. 恵 is associated with benefit or favor; in this established word, the whole combination names wisdom. The readings are チ + エ. Treat "knowledge used beneficially" as a memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知識](知識.md) (ちしき) means knowledge. 知恵 emphasizes judgment or resourceful use of what one knows, though the concepts overlap. Knowing many facts and finding a workable solution are not identical abilities.

## Distinctive meaning

知恵を借りる does not mean borrowing an object that must be returned. It asks for someone's insight. The dictionary also records the Buddhist concept of insight leading to enlightenment, especially with the spelling 智慧; that specialized context should not be assumed in an everyday request for help.
