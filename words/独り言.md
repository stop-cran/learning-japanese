# 独り言 (ひとりごと) - talking to oneself; soliloquy

## Meaning

独り言 is talking to oneself or something said to oneself.
It also corresponds to a soliloquy or monologue.
The usual expression for saying something to oneself is 独り言を言う.

- 彼は小さな声で独り言を言っていました。 (かれはちいさなこえでひとりごとをいっていました。) - He was talking to himself in a low voice.
- 「鍵はどこだろう」と独り言を言いました。 (「かぎはどこだろう」とひとりごとをいいました。) - I said to myself, "Where could my keys be?"

The low voice belongs to the first example, not to the definition of every
独り言.

## How the kanji combine

独り contributes being alone or by oneself;
[言](../kanji/言.md) contributes words or speech.
The complete reading is ひとりごと.
ひとり言 and 独りごと are alternative spellings.
JMdict marks 一人言 and 一人ごと as rare spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話す](話す.md) (はなす) and [言う](言う.md) (いう) are broader speech verbs.
独り言を言う narrows the speech to self-directed utterance.
[会話](会話.md) (かいわ), conversation, provides a contrast when people
are exchanging remarks with one another.

## Distinctive meaning

独り言 is not simply whispering: a whispered remark addressed to another
person need not be talking to oneself.
Nor does one person addressing an audience automatically make the speech
an 独り言. Keep the self-directed speech meaning rather than assigning
it to every situation with only one speaker.
