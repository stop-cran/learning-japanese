# 観念 (かんねん) - idea; resignation to a situation

## Meaning

観念 is an idea, notion, or concept. It can also express an awareness
or sense of something, as in 時間の観念 (じかんのかんねん),
"a sense of time."

- 固定観念にとらわれないでください。
  (こていかんねんにとらわれないでください。) - Please do not be bound by fixed ideas.
- あの人は時間の観念がありません。
  (あのひとはじかんのかんねんがありません。) - That person has no sense of time.

観念する has a distinct resignation sense: accepting a situation
as unavoidable or making up one's mind to face it.

- 負けたと観念しました。
  (まけたとかんねんしました。) - I accepted that I had lost.

Here と introduces what the speaker accepts, not an idea being invented.

## How the kanji combine

[観](../kanji/観.md) contributes viewing or conceiving, and
[念](../kanji/念.md) thought or attention.
The related mental associations help recall the abstract noun.
The on readings カン + ネン give かんねん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[考え](考え.md) (かんがえ), [思想](思想.md) (しそう), and
[アイデア](アイデア.md) overlap in thought or ideas, depending on context.
[諦める](諦める.md) (あきらめる) overlaps in giving up, while
[覚悟](覚悟.md) (かくご) can emphasize readiness to face consequences.
Those comparisons concern different senses, not one interchangeable set.

## Distinctive meaning

[納得](納得.md) (なっとく) can express acceptance through being convinced.
観念する can instead describe resignation even when the situation
is unwelcome. It does not mean approval of everything that happened.
Specialist philosophical and Buddhist uses also exist; the examples here
teach everyday abstract awareness and accepting an outcome, not a full
account of those traditions.
