# 気候 (きこう) - climate

## Meaning

気候 is a noun meaning "climate": the characteristic weather conditions of a place or season, rather than just today's weather. Identify the place before の気候, as in 日本の気候 below.

- この地方は気候が温暖です。 (このちほうはきこうがおんだんです。) - This region has a mild climate.
- 日本の気候について調べています。 (にほんのきこうについてしらべています。) - I am researching Japan's climate.

In the first example, この地方は sets the topic, while 気候が identifies what is mild. In the second, について introduces the subject being investigated.

## How the kanji combine

[気](../kanji/気.md) contributes the atmospheric aspect, and [候](../kanji/候.md) is associated with seasons and weather conditions. Together the characters name the pattern of atmospheric conditions. The reading is き + こう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[天気](天気.md) (てんき) is the useful comparison for the weather on a particular occasion: 今日の天気 (きょうのてんき), "today's weather." Both words concern weather, but they direct attention to different timescales. Neither is an antonym of the other.

## Distinctive meaning

気候 is helpful when comparing regions, describing seasonal patterns, or discussing a place's suitability for crops or daily life. One rainy afternoon does not by itself describe a region's climate. Conversely, saying that a region's climate is dry does not mean it can never rain there.
