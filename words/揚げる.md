# 揚げる (あげる) - deep-fry; hoist

## Meaning

揚げる is a transitive ichidan verb. It means deep-frying food, and also launching,
hoisting, or sending something up, as with fireworks, flags, and kites.
Forms include 揚げます (あげます), 揚げて (あげて), and 揚げた (あげた).

- 油で野菜を揚げました。 (あぶらでやさいをあげました。) - I deep-fried vegetables in oil.
- 公園で凧を揚げました。 (こうえんでたこをあげました。) - I flew a kite in the park.

The item fried or sent up takes を. The second example concerns flying a kite,
not an aircraft pilot's general action of flying.

## How the kanji combine

揚 has raising and deep-frying associations. Here あげる is one native verb;
げる is written in kana. There is no two-kanji compound to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上げる](上げる.md) (あげる) covers raising more broadly.
[挙げる](挙げる.md) is used in expressions such as citing examples or holding a ceremony.
[焼く](焼く.md) (やく), "grill; bake," is a different cooking method, not an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling helps identify the use: 野菜を揚げる concerns frying, while
凧を揚げる concerns sending a kite into the air. Neither is the giving sense of
[あげる](あげる.md). Shared pronunciation does not make all these spellings
interchangeable in every expression.
