# いつでも (いつでも) - anytime; at all times

## Meaning

いつでも is an adverb meaning "at any time" or "at all times." It can offer freedom to choose a time, or describe something holding whenever you look.

- わからないことがあれば、いつでも聞いてください。 (わからないことがあれば、いつでもきいてください。) - If there's anything you don't understand, ask anytime.
- この店はいつでも客が多いです。 (このみせはいつでもきゃくがおおいです。) - This shop has lots of customers at any time.

It goes directly with the clause; no に is needed after いつでも in these examples.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. It combines [いつ](いつ.md), "when," with でも to give an unrestricted choice of time. The dictionary also records 何時でも; the card's reading is いつでも.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いつも](いつも.md) describes what habitually happens or is always the case. いつでも is especially useful for "whenever you like," as in an invitation.

[時々](時々.md) (ときどき), "sometimes," contrasts with an all-times statement, but is not the opposite of an invitation to come anytime.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not memorize "never" as a separate meaning of いつでも. The predicate and scope of negation determine the whole sentence. いつでも行けるわけではありません (いつでもいけるわけではありません) means "It isn't the case that I can go anytime," not "I can never go."
