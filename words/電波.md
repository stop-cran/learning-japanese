# 電波 (でんぱ) - radio wave; radio reception; signal

## Meaning

電波 means radio waves and, in everyday communication contexts, a radio signal or reception.
It is a noun.

- ここは携帯電話の電波が弱いです。 (ここはけいたいでんわのでんぱがよわいです。) - The mobile phone signal is weak here.
- この場所では電波が届きません。 (このばしょではでんぱがとどきません。) - The signal does not reach this location.

電波が悪い (でんぱがわるい) describes poor reception.
The examples do not mean that the phone's battery is low.

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) contributes electricity and [波](../kanji/波.md) a wave.
The on readings are デン + ハ, with ハ becoming ぱ in **でんぱ**.
The technical compound is narrower than all electromagnetic radiation:
visible light is not normally called 電波.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[信号](信号.md) (しんごう) is a signal more generally and need not use radio waves.
[波](波.md) (なみ) is the broader wave word.
[電池](電池.md) (でんち) names a battery; reception and remaining battery charge are different things.

## Distinctive meaning

The radio-wave sense belongs to **でんぱ**.
The dictionary also records a slang use for someone saying strange things, including kana forms
デンパ and デムパ. That disparaging use is not a neutral description of reception,
and デムパ is not an alternative reading to use for an ordinary radio signal.
