# 光る (ひかる) - to shine; glitter

## Meaning

光る means to shine, glow, or glitter. It describes something emitting light or appearing bright through reflected light.

- 暗い部屋で時計が光っています。 (くらいへやでとけいがひかっています。) - The clock is glowing in the dark room.
- 指輪が日に当たって光りました。 (ゆびわがひにあたってひかりました。) - The ring glittered in the sunlight.

It is an intransitive godan verb: 光ります, 光らない, 光って. The shining thing takes が; it is not an object being shone by someone.

## How the kanji combine

[光](../kanji/光.md) carries the light meaning and is read ひか here, followed by る. The related noun 光 (ひかり) means light. Keep its noun ending distinct from the dictionary-form verb ひかる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

輝く (かがやく) also means shine or sparkle, often with an impression of brilliance. 照らす (てらす) means illuminate something and takes an object with を.

## Distinctive meaning

A polished object can 光る without producing its own light. The verb describes visible brightness, not a scientific claim about its source. It also differs from 明るい (あかるい), which describes something as bright rather than naming the shining action or state.
