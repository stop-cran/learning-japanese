# 原稿 (げんこう) - manuscript; draft

## Meaning

原稿 is a manuscript, draft, or prepared text for publication, submission, or delivery.
It can also be the notes prepared for a speech.
In publishing, English "copy" can mean this original text, not just a duplicate.

- 雑誌に載せる原稿を書いています。 (ざっしにのせるげんこうをかいています。) - I am writing a manuscript for a magazine.
- 発表の原稿を読み直しました。 (はっぴょうのげんこうをよみなおしました。) - I reread the text prepared for my presentation.

## How the kanji combine

[原](../kanji/原.md) contributes original or source; 稿 contributes a draft or manuscript.
The first character qualifies the written text as its source version.
The on readings combine as ゲン + コウ.
The character association does not require the text to be handwritten or unfinished.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[案](案.md) (あん) can be a plan, proposal, or draft more generally.
[文章](文章.md) (ぶんしょう) names written text without necessarily identifying a manuscript.
[ノート](ノート.md) and [メモ](メモ.md) can concern notes; not all notes are prepared 原稿.
[コピー](コピー.md) can mean a copy or copying, so its English label needs the publishing-versus-duplication distinction.

## Distinctive meaning

A 原稿 can be digital and can already be ready for publication.
The word does not by itself mean "first draft," a notebook, or the act of copying.
English "contribution" here concerns a contributed text, not any donation or helpful act.
Choose the surrounding noun to make the purpose clear: a magazine manuscript and a speech text need not have the same format.
