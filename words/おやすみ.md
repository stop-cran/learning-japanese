# おやすみ (おやすみ) - day off; rest; good night

## Meaning

おやすみ has distinct noun and greeting uses. As お休み, it politely names a day off or absence; it can also respectfully refer to someone's sleep or rest. As a greeting, おやすみ is a shortened "good night."

- 明日はお休みですか。 (あしたはおやすみですか。) - Do you have tomorrow off?
- 先生はもうお休みになりました。 (せんせいはもうおやすみになりました。) - The teacher has already gone to bed. (respectful)
- おやすみ。また明日。 (おやすみ。またあした。) - Good night. See you tomorrow.

お休みになる uses お + verb stem + になる to honor the person resting. It is not the ordinary construction for respectfully describing your own sleep.

## How the kanji combine

お休み uses [休](../kanji/休.md), related to [休む](休む.md) (やすむ), "rest; take time off." The kana headword keeps no kanji in its metadata. The greeting is also written in kana and is short for おやすみなさい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[休み](休み.md) (やすみ) broadly names a rest or day off; [休日](休日.md) (きゅうじつ) specifies a non-working day. [睡眠](睡眠.md) (すいみん) is the noun "sleep," not a greeting. おやすみなさい gives the fuller good-night expression.

## Distinctive meaning

The prefix お does not mean that every use has the same register. The shortened greeting is familiar; the noun and the respectful お休みになる construction work differently. The first example asks about time off, not whether the listener will sleep all day.
