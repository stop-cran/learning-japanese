# 通り掛かる (とおりかかる) - happen to pass by

## Meaning

通り掛かる means to happen to pass by a place or person.
Use を for a place passed; this route marker does not mean that the place is changed.
通りかかる is another established spelling.

- 店の前を通り掛かったとき、友達に会いました。
  (みせのまえをとおりかかったとき、ともだちにあいました。) - I met a friend when I happened to pass the shop.
- 通り掛かった人に道を聞きました。
  (とおりかかったひとにみちをききました。) - I asked a passerby for directions.

It is godan: 通り掛かります, 通り掛かって, 通り掛からない.
Keep とおり, not とうり, in the reading.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) supplies 通り from [通る](通る.md) (とおる), passing.
[掛](../kanji/掛.md) supplies かかる in this established compound.
The whole native verb means happening to pass by, not hanging up a passage.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通り過ぎる](通り過ぎる.md) (とおりすぎる) emphasizes going past a point.
通る is broader passage through or along a route.
[寄る](寄る.md) (よる) can mean stopping by; merely passing by does not say
that someone stopped or visited.

## Distinctive meaning

The chance element concerns being at that place in passing. It does not say
that the person's whole journey was aimless or accidental.
通り掛かった人 is a person who happened to pass, not necessarily someone
who came specifically to help or someone you failed to meet.
