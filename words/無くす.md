# 無くす (なくす) - lose something

## Meaning

無くす means to lose something. It is transitive: the lost item takes を, even when losing it was accidental.

- 駅で財布をなくしました。 (えきでさいふをなくしました。) - I lost my wallet at the station.
- この紙をなくさないでください。 (このかみをなくさないでください。) - Please do not lose this paper.

This is a godan verb: なくします, なくさない, なくして, なくした.

## How the kanji combine

無 expresses absence in this spelling, followed by くす. The word is usually written in kana, as in the examples. 無くす is retained here as a recognizable dictionary spelling; it need not replace everyday なくす.

## Synonyms and antonyms

なくなる describes something becoming missing or no longer being there: 財布がなくなった (さいふがなくなった), "my wallet is missing." 見つける (みつける), "find," is a useful contrasting action.

## Distinctive meaning

なくす also means eliminating something, as in reducing an unwanted problem to nothing. That sense may be deliberate; losing a wallet usually is not. Do not confuse this verb with 亡くす (なくす), used for losing someone through death.
