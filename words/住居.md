# 住居 (じゅうきょ) - dwelling; residence

## Meaning

住居 names a dwelling or residence: the place where someone lives. The noun does not establish ownership.

- 新しい住居を探しています。
  (あたらしいじゅうきょをさがしています。) - I am looking for a new place to live.
- この町に住居を定めました。
  (このまちにじゅうきょをさだめました。) - I established my residence in this town.

JMdict also records an intransitive する-verb for residing. The examples here use the noun; [住む](住む.md) (すむ) supplies the useful verb pattern 場所に住む, "live in a place."

JMdict includes "address" in this family. Retain that as dictionary recognition, not a rule that 住居 freely replaces [住所](住所.md) (じゅうしょ) in address questions or form fields. The second example instead uses the public dictionary's pattern 住居を定める, "establish one's residence."

## How the kanji combine

[住](../kanji/住.md) and [居](../kanji/居.md) both contribute residing or being in a place. Their related meanings reinforce the idea of a dwelling. The readings are ジュウ + キョ; keep the long vowel in じゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[住宅](住宅.md) (じゅうたく) concerns housing or residential buildings. [住所](住所.md) (じゅうしょ) names an address or residence, while [家屋](家屋.md) (かおく) focuses on the physical building. These overlap without requiring every address question to concern a building's structure.

[自宅](自宅.md) (じたく) specifies one's own home, not necessarily an owned property. [お宅](お宅.md) (おたく) can respectfully refer to another person's home; do not mechanically use it to honor your own residence.

## Distinctive meaning

A rented house or boarding accommodation can be someone's 住居. The word is not restricted to a detached house. Distinguish the living place from [家庭](家庭.md) (かてい), the household or family life, and from [宿泊](宿泊.md) (しゅくはく), the action of staying overnight.

[宿](宿.md) (やど) often names travel accommodation, but its dictionary family also records a home or dwelling. Do not assume that every use of 宿 excludes a place where someone lives.
