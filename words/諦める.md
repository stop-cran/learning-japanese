# 諦める (あきらめる) - give up; abandon hope

## Meaning

諦める is a transitive ichidan verb: to give up a hope, goal, or plan, or to accept that something cannot be achieved. Mark what is given up with を. A planned action can take verb + のを諦める.

- まだ夢を諦めていません。 (まだゆめをあきらめていません。) - I haven't given up on my dream yet.
- 雨が強くなったので、登山するのを諦めました。 (あめがつよくなったので、とざんするのをあきらめました。) - The rain got heavier, so I gave up the plan to go mountain climbing.

Useful forms are 諦めない (あきらめない), "not give up," and 諦めた (あきらめた), "gave up."

## How the kanji combine

諦 carries the word's giving-up meaning here; める supplies the written verb ending. Learn 諦める as a whole native verb, not as an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[止める](止める.md) (やめる) can describe stopping an activity without suggesting disappointed hopes. 諦める highlights letting go of a desired outcome.

[続ける](続ける.md) (つづける), "continue," is a practical contrast, but continuing an activity and keeping hope are not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

諦めないで (あきらめないで), "Don't give up," encourages someone to keep trying. Conversely, 諦める can describe accepting a limit rather than merely interrupting an action. A person can stop working for the night without abandoning their [夢](夢.md) (ゆめ), "dream."
