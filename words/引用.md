# 引用 (いんよう) - quotation; citation

## Meaning

引用 is a quotation or citation. 引用する is a transitive verb meaning to quote or cite material. Mark the quoted material with を; から can identify the source.

- レポートで小説の一節を引用しました。 (レポートでしょうせつのいっせつをいんようしました。) - I quoted a passage from a novel in my report.
- この引用はどの本からですか。 (このいんようはどのほんからですか。) - Which book is this quotation from?

引用した文章 (いんようしたぶんしょう) means "the text that was quoted." 引用 can name the act or the quoted material.

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) suggests drawing or bringing in; [用](../kanji/用.md) means use. The compound presents bringing material into use in another text or statement. This is a learning cue for the established meaning, not a reconstruction of its history.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[参考](参考.md) (さんこう) concerns consulting something for guidance. You can consult a book without reproducing its words.

[利用](利用.md) (りよう), "use; utilization," is broader and does not identify an act of quotation.

## Distinctive meaning

引用 is not a general verb for writing similar ideas. Distinguish your own [文章](文章.md) (ぶんしょう), "text," from material you explicitly quote or cite. 出典 (しゅってん), "source," names where that material comes from, not the quotation itself. There is no single everyday antonym needed for this use.
