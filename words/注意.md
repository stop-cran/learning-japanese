# 注意 (ちゅうい) — attention, caution

## Meaning

注意 means attention or caution. With する, it can mean "pay attention" or "be careful." It can also refer to warning or reprimanding someone.

- 車に注意してください。 (くるまに ちゅういして ください。) — Please watch out for cars.
- 先生に注意されました。 (せんせいに ちゅういされました。) — I was told off by the teacher.

In the first example, に marks what to watch out for. In the passive second example, に marks the person who gave the reprimand.
The subject and the situation therefore matter: not every 注意する sentence means simply "be careful."

## How the kanji combine

[注](../kanji/注.md) contributes directing or concentrating, and [意](../kanji/意.md) contributes the mind or intention.
As a meaning aid, think "direct the mind toward something": a verb-and-object relationship.
The reading is on + on, チュウ + イ → ちゅうい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 気を付ける (きをつける), "be careful," overlaps with the caution sense. It does not replace 注意する when the intended meaning is "tell someone off."
- 不注意 (ふちゅうい), "carelessness, inattention," contrasts with paying attention. It is not the opposite of every warning or reprimand.

## Distinctive meaning

注意 on a sign is a request for caution, not an English "notice" meaning any announcement whatsoever.
The same word can describe your own attentiveness or another person's corrective warning. Learn the whole construction and its context.
