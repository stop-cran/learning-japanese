# 子孫 (しそん) - descendants; offspring

## Meaning

子孫 means descendants or offspring: people in a continuing family line,
including children, grandchildren, and later generations.
Nの子孫 identifies whose descendants are meant.

- 彼女はその作家の子孫です。 (かのじょはそのさっかのしそんです。) - She is a descendant of that writer.
- 子孫に美しい自然を残したいです。 (しそんにうつくしいしぜんをのこしたいです。) - I want to leave a beautiful natural environment to future generations.

In the second example, に marks who will receive what is left.
Japanese does not require a plural ending: context determines whether
English uses "a descendant" or "descendants."

## How the kanji combine

[子](../kanji/子.md) contributes child, and [孫](../kanji/孫.md)
contributes grandchild or descendants.
The related generations evoke a continuing line.
The on readings are シ + ソン, not the standalone こ + まご.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[子](子.md) (こ) and [子供](子供.md) (こども) can name one's own children.
[孫](孫.md) (まご) specifies a grandchild.
[祖先](祖先.md) (そせん), ancestors, points in the other generational direction.
Child, grandchild, and descendant are not interchangeable relationship labels.

## Distinctive meaning

子孫 is broader than grandchildren alone.
Being someone's 子孫 also does not identify the exact number of generations
between them; add more information if that relationship matters.
The word can discuss future generations without counting particular children.
