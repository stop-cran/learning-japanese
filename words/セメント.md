# セメント - cement

## Meaning

セメント is cement, a binding material used in construction.
Ordinary construction cement is mixed with water and hardens;
it is an ingredient in concrete rather than a name for every finished concrete mixture.

- 工事で使うセメントを注文しました。 (こうじでつかうセメントをちゅうもんしました。) - I ordered cement for the construction work.
- セメントに水を加えて練ります。 (セメントにみずをくわえてねります。) - Mix water into the cement and work it into a paste.

In the second example, に marks what the water is added to.
セメント is a material noun, not a verb meaning to build.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word borrows English cement.
There are no written kanji to divide.
Keep the Japanese sequence セ・メ・ン・ト.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[コンクリート](コンクリート.md), concrete, combines cement and water with
aggregate such as sand and gravel.
モルタル, mortar, is another cement-based mixture.
These are related materials, not unrestricted synonyms of セメント.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the binder from the mixture or structure made with it.
A concrete wall is not simply a wall of cement alone.
Also avoid explaining hardening as merely "drying out":
the public dictionary distinguishes cement types, including ones that harden in water.
