# サンダル - sandal

## Meaning

サンダル names sandals, typically open footwear held on by straps or bands.
The same form can refer to one sandal or a pair; Japanese does not add an
English plural ending.

- 暑い日はサンダルを履きます。 (あついひはサンダルをはきます。) - I wear sandals on hot days.

Footwear uses 履く (はく), not the upper-body clothing verb 着る (きる).

## How the kanji combine

The English loanword is written in katakana as サンダル.
There are no kanji to divide, and the final ル belongs to the noun,
not a verb ending.

## Synonyms and antonyms

靴 (くつ), "shoes," is the broader everyday footwear term.
スリッパ usually refers to slippers, often worn indoors.
These categories can overlap in shape; intended use also matters.

## Distinctive meaning

To count pairs of footwear, use 足 (そく): サンダル一足 (サンダルいっそく),
"one pair of sandals." A noun modifier takes の, as in 子どものサンダル
(こどものサンダル), "a child's sandals." There is no natural direct antonym.
