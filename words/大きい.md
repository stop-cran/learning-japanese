# 大きい (おおきい) — big

## Meaning

大きい describes something big or large. It is an い-adjective: 大きくない (おおきくない), "not big," and 大きかった (おおきかった), "was big."

このかばんは大きいです。 (このかばんはおおきいです。) — This bag is big.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) supplies the size meaning and the reading おお; きい completes the written adjective. For conjugation, replace the final い, not the whole okurigana きい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

小さい (ちいさい), "small," is the ordinary opposite. 広い (ひろい) means wide or spacious. 大きな is a related form used before nouns, not a replacement for the predicate 大きいです.

## Distinctive meaning

Size is not always physical: 大きい声 (おおきいこえ) is a loud voice, and a consequence can be 大きい when it is significant. Choose the English translation for the thing being described rather than translating every instance literally as "big."
