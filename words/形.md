# 形 (かたち) — shape; form

## Meaning

形 is the shape or physical form of something. It is a noun: 丸い形 (まるいかたち), "a round shape," and 星の形 (ほしのかたち), "the shape of a star."

このパンは面白い形をしています。 (このパンはおもしろいかたちをしています。) — This bread has an interesting shape.

## How the kanji combine

[形](../kanji/形.md) alone writes the native word かたち. This reading is distinct from けい in compounds such as 三角形 (さんかくけい), "triangle." The present word does not require a second kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

姿 (すがた) can describe someone's or something's appearance or figure. 型 (かた) can mean a mold, model, or type. 大きさ (おおきさ), "size," is a different property: objects can have the same shape but different sizes.

## Distinctive meaning

形 can also refer to outward form or the way something is arranged, not just its outline. In the physical-shape pattern 形をしている, する describes the form something has; it does not mean the object is actively "doing a shape."
