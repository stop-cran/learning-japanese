# レポート — report; written assignment

## Meaning

レポート is a report or paper, including a written assignment for a class. It can also form レポートする, "report on."

- 金曜日までにレポートを出します。 (きんようびまでにレポートをだします。) — I'll submit the paper by Friday.
- 記者が現場の様子をレポートしました。 (きしゃがげんばのようすをレポートしました。) — The journalist reported on the situation at the scene.

Useful student expressions are レポートを書く (レポートをかく), "write a report," and レポートを提出する (レポートをていしゅつする), "submit a report."

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "report." The long-vowel mark belongs after ポ. リポート is another recognized form and is also used in reporting contexts.

## Synonyms and antonyms

報告 (ほうこく) is reporting or informing; 報告書 (ほうこくしょ) is a written report. 作文 (さくぶん) is a composition, not necessarily a report on research or observations.

## Distinctive meaning

In a class, レポート often names work submitted for assessment, not a school report card. Its required length, format, and content depend on the assignment; the noun alone does not establish those requirements.
