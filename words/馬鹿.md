# 馬鹿 (ばか) - fool; foolish

## Meaning

馬鹿 can be a noun meaning a fool or a na-adjective meaning foolish, stupid, or absurd. It can be insulting, especially when directed at another person.

- 馬鹿な間違いをしてしまいました。 (ばかなまちがいをしてしまいました。) - I made a foolish mistake.
- あの時の私は馬鹿でした。 (あのときのわたしはばかでした。) - I was a fool at that time.

Before a noun, use 馬鹿な. As a predicate, use 馬鹿だ／です; the negative is 馬鹿ではない, not an i-adjective form.

There is also a "trivial; insignificant" meaning. Learn it in the fixed
expression ばかにならない, "not insignificant; not to be dismissed."
This expression need not be an insult to anyone.

## How the kanji combine

[馬](../kanji/馬.md) means horse and 鹿 means deer, but JMdict marks 馬鹿 as **ateji**, a sound-based spelling. Do not derive "fool" by combining the animals' meanings. The word is also usually written in kana as ばか or バカ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

愚か (おろか) also means foolish, with a different tone and range of uses. 賢い (かしこい), "wise; clever," contrasts with the foolishness meaning; it is not a replacement for every idiom containing 馬鹿.

## Distinctive meaning

The force depends on context, relationship, and delivery. Self-criticism, playful exchanges, and direct insults are not equivalent uses. A friendly interpretation in a drama is not permission to address an unfamiliar person that way. This card teaches recognition and careful use, not the assumption that the word is harmless banter.
