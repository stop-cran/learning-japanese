# カー (カー) - car

## Meaning

カー is the katakana loanword corresponding to English "car."
It is useful to recognize in compound vehicle names and product terms.
レンタカー is a rental car; スポーツカー is a sports car.
Treat the full compound as the noun in a sentence.

- 旅行先でレンタカーを借りました。
  (りょこうさきでレンタカーをかりました。) - I rented a car at my travel destination.
- 兄はスポーツカーに乗っています。
  (あにはスポーツカーにのっています。) - My older brother drives a sports car.

The particle depends on the verb: 借りる takes を for what is rented,
while 乗る takes に for the vehicle.

## How the kanji combine

There are no kanji in カー. The long-vowel mark ー lengthens カ:
カー is not the short syllable カ.
In the compounds above, the preceding element specifies the kind of car.
No kanji decomposition is needed.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[車](車.md) (くるま) is the practical everyday noun for a car.
[自動車](自動車.md) (じどうしゃ) explicitly names an automobile.
They overlap with the vehicle meaning of カー, although the established compound spellings differ.

## Distinctive meaning

Knowing the English source does not make every English phrase containing "car" a
ready-made Japanese expression. Learn useful compounds as units.
For a straightforward sentence such as "I bought a car," 車を買いました
(くるまをかいました) is a useful model rather than forcing カー into it.
