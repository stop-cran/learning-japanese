# 日陰 (ひかげ) - shade; place out of the sun

## Meaning

日陰 is shade: a place where direct sunlight is blocked.
It is a noun. Use 日陰で for an activity there and 日陰に for a
location or destination, according to the verb.

- 暑いので、日陰で休みましょう。 (あついので、ひかげでやすみましょう。) - It is hot, so let's rest in the shade.
- 自転車を日陰に置きました。 (じてんしゃをひかげにおきました。) - I put my bicycle in the shade.

A place can be 日陰 without being completely dark.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) supplies the sun, and 陰 shade or a screened
place: shade from the sun. The native reading is ひ + かげ.
日蔭 is another recorded spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[陰](陰.md) (かげ) is broader and also covers being behind something
or out of sight. [影](影.md), also かげ, can mean a cast shadow or
silhouette. 日向 (ひなた), a sunny place, gives the physical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The pinned family also records a sunlight sense, but restricts it to
日影 and 日かげ. That is not an opposite meaning to assign freely
to the spelling 日陰. Learn this card's shade spelling and examples
together rather than treating all ひかげ spellings as interchangeable.
