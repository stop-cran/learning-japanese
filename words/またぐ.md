# またぐ - step over; span; straddle

## Meaning

またぐ means to step over or across something. It also describes spanning
something or sitting astride it. It is a transitive godan verb ending in ぐ:
またぎます, またいで, またいだ, またがない. The thing crossed takes を.

- 水たまりをまたいで、向こうへ行きました。 (みずたまりをまたいで、むこうへいきました。) - I stepped over the puddle and went to the other side.
- その橋は谷をまたいでいます。 (そのはしはたにをまたいでいます。) - That bridge spans the valley.

The second example illustrates spanning rather than a person's walking
movement. Do not confuse またいで, the て-form here, with the separate
verb またがる, sit astride.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual; 跨ぐ is the dictionary's kanji spelling. Learn the whole
native verb rather than inventing an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[越える](越える.md) (こえる) and [渡る](渡る.md) (わたる)
also concern crossing, but do not necessarily describe stepping over an
obstacle with the legs apart. [乗る](乗る.md) (のる) means board or ride:
boarding a bus is not thereby またぐ.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not restrict the word to a single walking step: the spanning and
astride uses are established too. Conversely, a crossing verb does not
automatically tell you that someone stepped over rather than walked
through, swam across, or used a vehicle.
