# かりかり — hard-crisp; irritable

## Meaning

A firm, dry crispness, or a light scratching sound. With a person's mood, it means touchy, tense, or irritable.

- ベーコンをかりかりに焼いた。 — I fried the bacon until it was crisp.
- そんなにかりかりしないで、少し休もう。 — Don't get so worked up; let's take a short break.
- 鉛筆（えんぴつ）が紙の上でかりかりと音を立てた。 — The pencil made a scratching sound on the paper.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[さくさく](さくさく.md) often suggests a lighter, crumbly crispness. [ぱりぱり](ぱりぱり.md) often involves thin material breaking crisply. [こりこり](こりこり.md) is firmer resistance in the bite.

## Distinctive meaning

かりかりに焼く, かりかりした食感（しょっかん）, and かりかりする are common, but the subject decides whether texture or mood is intended. In pet contexts, カリカリ is also a noun for dry kibble. The crisp texture sense can be desirable; the irritable sense is not a compliment.
