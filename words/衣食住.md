# 衣食住 (いしょくじゅう) - food, clothing and shelter

## Meaning

衣食住 groups clothing, food, and housing: the basic material necessities of life.
The conventional English phrase "food, clothing and shelter" puts the first
two items in the opposite order from the Japanese characters.

- 衣食住に困っている人を支援しています。 (いしょくじゅうにこまっているひとをしえんしています。) - We support people struggling to meet their basic needs for food, clothing, and shelter.
- 衣食住の費用を計算しました。 (いしょくじゅうのひようをけいさんしました。) - I calculated the costs of food, clothing, and housing.

の links the noun to 費用, "costs." 衣食住が足りる
(いしょくじゅうがたりる) means having these basic needs sufficiently met.

## How the kanji combine

[衣](../kanji/衣.md) is clothing, [食](../kanji/食.md) food, and
[住](../kanji/住.md) housing or dwelling. Three parallel necessities are
listed together rather than forming a modifier-and-head pair.
The on readings are イ + ショク + ジュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[生活](生活.md) (せいかつ), life or livelihood, is broader.
[衣服](衣服.md) (いふく) concerns clothing only; it does not include the other
two parts of 衣食住. There is no useful single-word antonym for this grouped set.

## Distinctive meaning

衣食住 concerns material living needs. It is not a claim that these exhaust
everything people need, nor does the word alone specify luxury or a minimum
legal standard. The surrounding sentence supplies the level and circumstances.
