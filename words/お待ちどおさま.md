# お待ちどおさま (おまちどおさま) - sorry to have kept you waiting

## Meaning

お待ちどおさま is said to someone whom one has kept waiting, for example when bringing something or becoming ready. It acknowledges the wait with an apologetic greeting.

- お待ちどおさま。お茶が入りました。 (おまちどおさま。おちゃがはいりました。) - Sorry to keep you waiting. The tea is ready.
- お待ちどおさま。こちらへどうぞ。 (おまちどおさま。こちらへどうぞ。) - Sorry to have kept you waiting. This way, please.

## How the kanji combine

[待](../kanji/待.md) concerns waiting. The dictionary also records お待ち遠様, with [遠](../kanji/遠.md) and [様](../kanji/様.md); learn the established whole reading おまちどおさま rather than assembling on readings. This card keeps the spelling お待ちどおさま.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お待たせしました](お待たせしました.md) has the same wait-acknowledging function and explicitly has a polite past-form ending. [ごめんなさい](ごめんなさい.md) and [すみません](すみません.md) are broader apologies, not specifically statements about waiting.

## Distinctive meaning

Write どお in おまちどおさま. The dictionary flags お待ちどうさま as irregular kana usage; the long sound does not make the two written sequences identical. This expression is not "please wait," and its dictionary recognition alone does not establish a universal register ranking against お待たせしました.
