# 高価 (こうか) - expensive; costly

## Meaning

高価 describes something with a high price or considerable monetary value. It functions as a な-adjective: 高価な品 (こうかな しな), "an expensive item"; 高価です, "is expensive." The negative is 高価ではない.

- これは高価な時計です。 (これはこうかなとけいです。) - This is an expensive watch.
- その道具は高価なので、今回は借ります。 (そのどうぐはこうかなので、こんかいはかります。) - That tool is expensive, so I will borrow it this time.

The second sentence uses 高価なので to explain a decision with "because it is expensive."

## How the kanji combine

[高](../kanji/高.md) contributes high, and 価 contributes price or value. The first component describes the level of the second: "high in price." The reading is こうか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[高い](高い.md) (たかい) also means expensive in a price context, but can additionally mean tall or high. [安い](安い.md) (やすい), "inexpensive," is an everyday contrast. 安価 (あんか) is the corresponding compound for low cost.

## Distinctive meaning

高価 keeps the monetary interpretation clear: 高価な建物 concerns an expensive building, not a tall one. It does not necessarily mean good value for money or superior quality. Something can be 高価 yet unsuitable for your needs. For something emotionally precious but inexpensive, the intended value needs a different expression rather than an automatic translation as 高価.
