# 取れる (とれる) - come off; be removed

## Meaning

取れる is an intransitive ichidan verb for something coming off or being removed. The thing that comes off takes が. It can also describe a stain disappearing or a troublesome condition going away.

- シャツのボタンが取れました。 (シャツのボタンがとれました。) - A button came off my shirt.
- 洗っても、この汚れは取れません。 (あらっても、このよごれはとれません。) - Even after washing, this dirt will not come off.

The first sentence does not specify who, if anyone, caused the button to come off. 取れている can describe the resulting detached state.

## How the kanji combine

[取](../kanji/取.md) carries the taking or removal association; れる completes the native verb. Its forms include 取れます (とれます), 取れない (とれない), and 取れて (とれて).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[取る](取る.md) (とる) names taking or removing something: ボタンを取る, "remove a button." [付く](付く.md) (つく), "be attached," provides a useful contrast for attached objects.

## Distinctive meaning

The same form is also the potential of 取る: 予約が取れました (よやくがとれました), "I was able to get a reservation." There, nothing has fallen off. Decide whether the sentence describes detachment or obtaining something; the spelling alone cannot settle it.
