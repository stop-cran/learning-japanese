# 濃い (こい) - deep-colored; strong; concentrated

## Meaning

濃い is an i-adjective for depth of color, strength of flavor or smell, and density or concentration. Use it directly before a noun: 濃い色 (こいいろ), "a deep color," or 濃いお茶 (こいおちゃ), "strong tea."

- このスープは味が濃いです。 (このスープはあじがこいです。) - This soup has a strong flavor.
- 濃い青のシャツを選びました。 (こいあおのシャツをえらびました。) - I chose a deep-blue shirt.

The adverbial form is 濃く: 味を濃くする (あじをこくする), "make the flavor stronger." The negative is 濃くない.

## How the kanji combine

濃 supplies the idea of concentration or density. The native adjective is こい; its final い changes with inflection. There is no multi-kanji compound to break down.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[薄い](薄い.md) (うすい) contrasts with 濃い for light color or weak flavor. [厚い](厚い.md) (あつい) describes physical thickness, such as a thick book. A concentrated soup and a thick book do not use the same Japanese adjective simply because English can call both "thick."

## Distinctive meaning

濃い describes intensity in the relevant dimension. Strong tea need not be physically viscous, and a deep color need not imply poor lighting. Let the noun identify whether the sentence concerns taste, color, or density rather than assigning one English translation to every use.
