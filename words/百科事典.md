# 百科事典 (ひゃっかじてん) - encyclopedia

## Meaning

百科事典 is an encyclopedia: a reference work explaining subjects
across many fields of knowledge. The same word is also written
百科辞典. The source's kana ひゃっかじてん names this word.

- 百科事典で火山について調べました。 (ひゃっかじてんでかざんについてしらべました。) - I looked up volcanoes in an encyclopedia.
- 図書館で百科事典を借りました。 (としょかんでひゃっかじてんをかりました。) - I borrowed an encyclopedia from the library.

In the first example, で marks the reference used.
The word does not require paper volumes rather than an electronic format.

## How the kanji combine

[百](../kanji/百.md), hundred, combines with [科](../kanji/科.md),
fields or branches, in 百科, many fields of knowledge.
[事](../kanji/事.md) contributes matters or subjects, and 典 a
reference text in 事典, encyclopedia. 百科 modifies 事典.
Learn ひゃっか + じてん, with small っ, rather than saying ひゃくか.
In 百科辞典, [辞](../kanji/辞.md) replaces 事 in the spelling,
without changing the whole word's encyclopedia meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[辞書](辞書.md) (じしょ) and [辞典](辞典.md) (じてん)
usually identify dictionaries explaining words, readings, or meanings.
An encyclopedia instead explains subjects and things.
The standalone homophone 事典 (じてん) is the encyclopedia/reference term.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer from the spelling 百科辞典 that this word merely means
any language dictionary. Both full spellings are recorded for
ひゃっかじてん. Nor does 百 demand exactly one hundred entries,
subjects, or volumes.
