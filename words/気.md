# 気 (き) -- spirit; mood

## Meaning

気 can refer to a person's spirit, mind, or mood. Learn it with its surrounding expression: a single English equivalent will not fit every phrase.

- 気を強く持ってください。 (きをつよくもってください。) -- Keep your spirits up.
- 明日の試験のことを考えると、気が重いです。 (あしたのしけんのことをかんがえると、きがおもいです。) -- I feel weighed down when I think about tomorrow's exam.

In these expressions, 気 is a noun: を marks what is maintained, while が marks what feels heavy. Do not add な as if 気 itself were an adjective.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [気](../kanji/気.md) has the on reading き. Here its contribution is mental or emotional state; there is no second component to combine.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[気分](気分.md) (きぶん) is useful for how someone feels at a particular time. 心 (こころ) means heart or mind more broadly. Neither can replace 気 in every established expression.

## Distinctive meaning

"Spirit" here need not mean a ghost or supernatural being. In the examples it concerns emotional strength or state of mind. Particles and the following verb or adjective are essential parts of the meaning.
