# 応対 (おうたい) - attending to people; handling callers

## Meaning

応対 is attending to people or handling exchanges with customers,
visitors or callers. It also covers dealing with complaints.
応対する is intransitive in JMdict; a useful pattern is
相手に応対する (あいてにおうたいする).

- 店員が客に丁寧に応対しました。
  (てんいんがきゃくにていねいにおうたいしました。) - The shop assistant attended to the customer politely.
- 電話の応対を練習しています。
  (でんわのおうたいをれんしゅうしています。) - I am practising how to handle telephone calls.

In the second example, を marks the telephone-handling activity being practised;
it does not make 応対する a transitive verb in that sentence.

## How the kanji combine

応 contributes responding, and [対](../kanji/対.md) facing
or dealing with another party. オウ + タイ connects responding
with an exchange. Do not treat the character 対 as proof that
the interaction must involve opposition or an argument.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[応接](応接.md) (おうせつ) also concerns receiving visitors;
the words overlap. [受付](受付.md) (うけつけ) can be a reception
desk or the acceptance of a request.
[歓迎](歓迎.md) (かんげい) emphasizes welcoming, while 応対
does not by itself evaluate how warmly someone is received.

## Distinctive meaning

応対 is not restricted to telephone calls.
対応 (たいおう) is broader handling or responding and can concern
a situation as well as an exchange with a person. That is a difference
of focus, not a rule that all customer-related uses must be 応対.
The dictionary flags 応待 as irregular kanji usage; the production
spelling taught here is 応対.
