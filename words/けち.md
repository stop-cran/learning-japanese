# けち (けち) - stingy; miser

## Meaning

けち describes stinginess or a stingy person. It functions as a noun and a な-adjective: けちな人 means "a miserly person," and けちだ means "is stingy." Applied to someone, it generally expresses criticism rather than neutral budgeting.

- 彼はお金にけちです。 (かれはおかねにけちです。) - He is stingy with money.
- けちだと思われたくありません。 (けちだとおもわれたくありません。) - I don't want people to think I am stingy.

The second example quotes a judgment with と. The word can criticize a reluctance to spend or share even when the person has money available.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in kana, also as ケチ. There is no written kanji decomposition here. Dictionary recognition of an occasional kanji spelling does not make that spelling necessary for reading or using the everyday word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[節約](節約.md) (せつやく) means saving or economizing and does not inherently criticize the person. 気前がいい (きまえがいい), "generous," contrasts with stinginess. Being generous is not the same as having a large income.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every sensible effort to save money as けち. Compare 節約している, "is economizing," with けちだ, "is stingy": the latter evaluates the person's attitude. Tone and familiarity matter, and calling someone けち can be insulting even in a short sentence.
