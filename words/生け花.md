# 生け花 (いけばな) - ikebana; Japanese flower arrangement

## Meaning

生け花 is ikebana, the Japanese art of arranging flowers. It can refer to the
practice or an arrangement produced through it.

- 毎週、生け花を習っています。 (まいしゅう、いけばなをならっています。) - I take ikebana lessons every week.
- 玄関に生け花を飾りました。 (げんかんにいけばなをかざりました。) - I displayed an ikebana arrangement in the entrance hall.

生花 is another recognized spelling of いけばな. 活け花 and 活花 are
dictionary-listed rare kanji spellings.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) occurs in 生ける (いける), arranging flowers, and
[花](../kanji/花.md) means flower. This is a native verb-stem-plus-noun compound:
いけ + はな becomes いけばな, with は voiced to ば.
The alternative 活け花 spelling uses [活](../kanji/活.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

華道 (かどう), the way or art of flower arrangement, is a related name for
the discipline. A flower bouquet is not automatically an example of this art;
生け花 is more specific than simply mentioning flowers.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep spelling and reading together. 生花 can also be read せいか, and that reading
has a "fresh/natural flowers" sense. Do not transfer that sense to いけばな or read
生け花 as せいか. This article teaches the arrangement word.
