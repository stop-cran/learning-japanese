# 灰 (はい) - ash; ashes

## Meaning

灰 is a noun for ash, the powdery residue associated with burning. Japanese does not change its form to distinguish English singular "ash" from plural "ashes." The amount is supplied by the sentence.

- 暖炉に灰が残っています。 (だんろにはいがのこっています。) - Ash remains in the fireplace.
- 灰が風で飛んでいきました。 (はいがかぜでとんでいきました。) - The ash blew away in the wind.

灰になる (はいになる) means "turn to ash." It describes the resulting state rather than the process of producing smoke.

## How the kanji combine

灰 is one character with the native reading はい. There is no compound to split. In 灰色 (はいいろ), "gray," the same word combines with 色 (いろ), "color," to name an ash-like color.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[煙](煙.md) (けむり) is smoke, not the residue left behind. Dust and sand can look similar to ash but identify different materials. There is no single useful antonym for 灰.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun sounds the same as the response はい, "yes." A complete construction makes the meaning clear: 灰が残る concerns ash, while はい as a reply acknowledges someone. Also distinguish material from color: a 灰色の服 (はいいろのふく) is gray clothing, not clothing made from ash.
