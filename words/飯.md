# 飯 (めし) - cooked rice; meal

## Meaning

飯 can mean cooked rice or a meal. It is a noun with a casual, sometimes rough tone. JMdict labels these senses as male language; that is a register label, not a rule that women cannot use the word.

- 飯、もう食った？ (めし、もうくった？) - Have you eaten yet?
- この飯はうまい。 (このめしはうまい。) - This rice is good.

The first is a familiar exchange, with the similarly casual 食った. The second can refer to the rice itself when the context makes that clear. Neither is a model for polite service encounters.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [飯](../kanji/飯.md) uses the native reading めし here. The same character has the on reading ハン in [ご飯](ご飯.md) (ごはん). Shared spelling does not make the whole words identical in tone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ご飯 is a broadly useful alternative for rice or a meal. [食事](食事.md) (しょくじ) names a meal or dining without the same rough familiarity. [米](米.md) (こめ) refers to rice as grain, especially before cooking.

## Distinctive meaning

飯を食う (めしをくう) can mean eating a meal; the broader expression これで飯を食っている (これでめしをくっている) means making a living from this. The livelihood use is figurative. Choose the expression for the relationship and setting rather than treating the dictionary's male-language label as an absolute speaker-gender restriction.
