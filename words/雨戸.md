# 雨戸 (あまど) - sliding storm shutter

## Meaning

雨戸 is a sliding storm shutter fitted outside a window or opening.
It protects the opening against wind and rain. It is a noun, not the name of a weather event.

- 台風が来る前に、雨戸を閉めました。 (たいふうがくるまえに、あまどをしめました。) - I closed the storm shutters before the typhoon arrived.
- 朝になったので、雨戸を開けました。 (あさになったので、あまどをあけました。) - It was morning, so I opened the storm shutters.

## How the kanji combine

[雨](../kanji/雨.md) supplies the native combining form あま-, "rain,"
and [戸](../kanji/戸.md), と, concerns a door or panel.
The second element is voiced to ど in あまど. These are native readings, not on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[戸](戸.md) (と) is the broader word for a door.
[窓](窓.md) (まど), "window," names the opening or window, not its storm shutter.
Opening and closing the shutter are opposite actions; there is no useful single antonym for the object.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list's "storm door" should not suggest only a second hinged entrance door.
The dictionary's specific sense is a sliding storm shutter.
Learn the whole word so the 戸 character does not obscure what kind of fitting is meant.
