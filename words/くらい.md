# くらい — approximately; to the extent

## Meaning

くらい, also ぐらい, marks an approximate quantity or a degree illustrated by a phrase.

- 十分くらい待ちました。 (じゅっぷんくらい まちました。) — I waited about ten minutes.
- 歩けないくらい疲れました。 (あるけないくらい つかれました。) — I was so tired I could not walk.

Attach it directly to a counted quantity, without の. In the second example, the plain-form
clause 歩けない supplies the degree: tired to the point of being unable to walk.

## How the kanji combine

This particle is normally written in kana. The spelling 位 also occurs, but the noun 位
(くらい), "rank; standing," is a separate JMdict entry. [暗い](暗い.md) くらい, "dark," is an
unrelated adjective despite the identical kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ほど](ほど.md) overlaps in approximation and degree. [ころ](ころ.md), with suffix ごろ, locates
an approximate time: 三時ごろ, "around three o'clock," rather than a duration like 三時間くらい,
"about three hours." There is no universal antonym for the particle.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not substitute くらい for every occurrence of ほど or かぎり. Whole patterns matter: the
proportional 読めば読むほど, "the more one reads," is not learned by replacing a single gloss.

For くらい／ぐらい compared with ほど, だけ, and ばかり, see
[the particle-neighbor section](../articles/koto-wake-and-formal-nouns.md#particle-neighbors-ほど-くらいぐらい-だけ-and-ばかり).
