# おまわりさん (おまわりさん) - police officer (familiar)

## Meaning

おまわりさん is a friendly, familiar term for a police officer. It is useful when describing an officer in everyday conversation, particularly in language used with children.

- おまわりさんに道を聞きました。 (おまわりさんにみちをききました。) - I asked a police officer for directions.

に identifies the person asked; 道を聞く (みちをきく) is the expression for asking the way.

## How the kanji combine

The usual kana spelling avoids any kanji decomposition. Another spelling is お巡りさん. The expression contains polite お and さん around a form associated with going on rounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

警察官 (けいさつかん) is the neutral occupational term, "police officer." 警察 (けいさつ) usually names the police as an organization, not one individual officer.

## Distinctive meaning

The familiar tone does not mean the officer is literally a friend. Use 警察官 when a neutral description of the occupation is needed, for example in formal writing.
