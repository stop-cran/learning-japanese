# 雰囲気 (ふんいき) - atmosphere; mood; ambience

## Meaning

雰囲気 is the atmosphere, mood, or feel of a place, group, or situation.
It can also describe an air or aura around a person.
It is a noun: 雰囲気がいい means "have a nice atmosphere," and
Nの雰囲気 identifies whose or what atmosphere is meant.

- この喫茶店は落ち着いた雰囲気ですね。
  (このきっさてんはおちついたふんいきですね。)
  - This cafe has a relaxed atmosphere, doesn't it?
- 会議の雰囲気が急に変わりました。
  (かいぎのふんいきがきゅうにかわりました。) - The mood of the meeting suddenly changed.

優しい雰囲気の人 (やさしいふんいきのひと) is someone with a gentle air.

## How the kanji combine

雰 concerns atmospheric vapor or mist, 囲 contributes surrounding, and
[気](../kanji/気.md) contributes air or feeling.
Together they offer an image of a surrounding atmosphere, including a figurative social one.
The on readings are フン + イ + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[気分](気分.md) (きぶん) concerns a person's mood or condition.
[空気](空気.md) (くうき) is air and can also describe a social atmosphere.
雰囲気 need not describe the speaker's own emotional state.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the standard reading and spelling ふんいき, with ん before い.
JMdict also records spoken ふいんき as an irregular form.
The modern examples concern ambience, not the Earth's physical atmosphere;
the dictionary marks that physical-atmosphere sense obsolete.
