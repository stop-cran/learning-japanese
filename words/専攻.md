# 専攻 (せんこう) - academic major; specialization

## Meaning

専攻 is a major subject or field of specialized study. 専攻する is transitive:
**subject を専攻する**, "major in; specialize in a subject."
The noun can name the field itself: 専攻は何ですか (せんこうはなんですか),
"What is your major?"

- 大学で経済学を専攻しています。 (だいがくでけいざいがくをせんこうしています。) - I am majoring in economics at university.
- 私の専攻は日本文学です。 (わたしのせんこうはにほんぶんがくです。) - My major is Japanese literature.

In the first sentence, で gives the educational setting and を marks the
subject studied as a specialization.

## How the kanji combine

専 contributes concentration on a particular thing, and 攻 here suggests
applying oneself to study. Together they name focused academic study.
The on readings combine as せん + こう. Do not translate the compound
literally as attacking a subject.

## Synonyms and antonyms

専門 (せんもん) is a specialty or area of expertise; it need not be a
university major. [勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう), "study," and
[学ぶ](学ぶ.md) (まなぶ), "learn," are broader.
Taking one language class does not by itself establish that language as your 専攻.

## Distinctive meaning

専攻 identifies an academic focus rather than simply something one finds
interesting. Its English translation depends on the educational system:
"major" is convenient, but "specialized field of study" may fit better.
Use the whole construction, including を with 専攻する, rather than copying
the English preposition "in" into the Japanese sentence.
