# 斜 (はす) - slant; diagonal direction

## Meaning

斜, read はす, means a slant or diagonal direction.
It is a noun that can modify another noun with の; はすに describes an oblique direction.

The Japanese dictionary gives this phrase:

- 道をはすに横切る。 (みちをはすによこぎる。) - Cross the road diagonally.

を marks the route crossed. はすに describes the direction,
not the identity of the road or a fixed numerical angle.

## How the kanji combine

斜 writes the whole native word はす here; there is no compound to split.
The same character also has the reading しゃ and appears in [斜め](斜め.md),
ななめ, under a separate dictionary family.
Those facts do not make every reading a free substitute in every construction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

斜め (ななめ) closely overlaps for a slant or diagonal.
[傾く](傾く.md) (かたむく) is a verb for tilting or inclining.
[縦](縦.md) (たて) and [横](横.md) (よこ) identify vertical and horizontal
orientations against which a diagonal can be described.

## Distinctive meaning

The source row has no English gloss, but its reading はす identifies the intended entry.
Do not silently replace that reading with ななめ or しゃ because the written form is shared.
The attested phrase above demonstrates this word's use without inventing additional
rare-form collocations or assigning it all figurative senses of 斜め.
