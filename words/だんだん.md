# だんだん — gradually

## Meaning

だんだん describes change happening by degrees: "gradually," "little by little," or "more and more." It works directly before the predicate; だんだんと is also possible.

- だんだん寒くなってきました。 (だんだんさむくなってきました。) — It has gradually been getting colder.
- 漢字がだんだん読めるようになりました。 (かんじがだんだんよめるようになりました。) — I gradually became able to read kanji.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 段々 is a kanji spelling: [段](../kanji/段.md), read だん, evokes steps or stages, and 々 repeats the character. This repeated on-reading formation explains the kango category despite the kana headword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

少しずつ (すこしずつ) emphasizes small increments. 徐々に (じょじょに) is another word for gradually. 急に (きゅうに), "suddenly," contrasts with gradual change.

## Distinctive meaning

Use だんだん with a developing change rather than merely a fixed low speed. ゆっくり歩く (ゆっくりあるく), "walk slowly," does not by itself mean that walking changes progressively over time.
