# 即座 (そくざ) — immediate; on the spot

## Meaning

即座 means immediate or right there on the spot. JMdict also glosses it as impromptu or instant.

- 質問に即座に答えました。 (しつもんにそくざにこたえました。) — She answered the question immediately.
- 彼は即座に申し出を断りました。 (かれはそくざにもうしでをことわりました。) — He turned down the offer on the spot.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[即](../kanji/即.md) means immediately, and [座](../kanji/座.md) means a seat or to sit. Together, they describe acting immediately, right there on the spot.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すぐに](すぐに.md) and [直ちに](直ちに.md) (ただちに) also mean immediately. 即席 そくせき, “impromptu; improvised; instant” (JMdict entry 1404240), shares 即.

## Distinctive meaning

即座 stresses responding right there on the spot, as its JMdict glosses show.
