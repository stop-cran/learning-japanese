# 船 (ふね) — ship; boat

## Meaning

船 is a boat, ship, or other watercraft. It is a broad noun: the word alone does not tell you
whether the vessel is a large passenger ship or a much smaller craft.
船に乗る (ふねにのる) means "board or ride a boat."

- 船で島に行きました。 (ふねでしまにいきました。) — I went to the island by boat.
- 港で船に乗りました。 (みなとでふねにのりました。) — I boarded a boat at the harbor.

In the first example, で marks the means of travel, not a material the island is made from.
In the second, 港で locates the boarding and 船に identifies the vessel.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [船](../kanji/船.md) writes the native noun ふね.
The related combining form is ふな-, as in 船便 (ふなびん), "sea mail."
セン, heard in 船長 (せんちょう), "ship's captain," is the on reading instead.

## Synonyms and antonyms

舟 is another spelling of ふね; JMdict notes that it often refers to a small, hand-propelled craft.
That is a tendency, not a rule that 船 must always be large.
[ボート](ボート.md) can mean a boat and, on its own, a rowboat.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose English "boat" or "ship" according to the vessel and context, not a fixed size boundary
encoded by 船. In travel expressions, distinguish 船で行く (ふねでいく), "go by boat,"
from 船に乗る, which presents boarding or riding the vessel.
