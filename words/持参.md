# 持参 (じさん) - bring or take along

## Meaning

持参 means bringing or taking something along. 持参する is transitive:
the item takes を, and a destination or occasion can take に.

- 明日はお弁当を持参してください。 (あしたはおべんとうをじさんしてください。) - Please bring a packed lunch tomorrow.
- 会議に資料を持参しました。 (かいぎにしりょうをじさんしました。) - I took the materials along to the meeting.

持参した資料 means "the materials brought along."
The item need not be a document: food, money, and equipment can also be 持参する.

## How the kanji combine

[持](../kanji/持.md) contributes holding or carrying;
[参](../kanji/参.md) has a going or coming association.
Together they help recall taking something with oneself.
The on readings combine as ジ + サン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[持つ](持つ.md) (もつ) is broader: holding or possessing something does not
necessarily involve a journey. [運ぶ](運ぶ.md) (はこぶ) emphasizes carrying
or transporting. 持っていく (もっていく), "take along," and
持ってくる (もってくる), "bring along," make direction explicit.

[連れる](連れる.md) (つれる) concerns taking someone along:
友達を連れてくる (ともだちをつれてくる), "bring a friend along."
持参 here concerns an item.

## Distinctive meaning

持参 does not itself fix the speaker's viewpoint as "toward here" or "away from here."
The destination and context determine whether English uses "bring" or "take."
An instruction to bring an item does not say that it will be supplied at the destination.
