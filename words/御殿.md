# 御殿 (ごてん) - palace

## Meaning

御殿 names a palace or courtly residence.

- 昔の御殿を見学しました。 (むかしのごてんをけんがくしました。) - We toured an old palace.
- この部屋は御殿の一部です。 (このへやはごてんのいちぶです。) - This room is part of the palace.

## How the kanji combine

[御](../kanji/御.md) contributes a respectful prefix, read ご here;
[殿](../kanji/殿.md) contributes a palace or grand residence, read てん.
Learn ごてん as the established whole word, not as a name followed by
the address suffix どの. JMdict also lists the spelling ご殿.

## Synonyms and antonyms

宮殿 (きゅうでん) is another word for a palace. These words can overlap;
the choice for a particular historic building depends on its established
name and context, not a universal rule that each palace belongs to only
one category.

[家](家.md) (いえ) is a broader word for a house or home. A house does
not become a 御殿 merely because its owner is addressed respectfully.

## Distinctive meaning

The English dictionary gloss court concerns the palace or royal-court
sense here, not a sports court or a court of law. The short answer palace
avoids that English ambiguity.

The separate 殿 との entry's archaic building sense does not make
御殿 ごてん archaic. Keep the word's reading and lexical identity separate
from the other readings recorded for the same final character.
