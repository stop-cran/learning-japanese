# あんなに - to that extent; so much

## Meaning

あんなに is an adverb meaning "that much," "to that degree," or "so." It points to a degree understood from the situation or a shared memory. Use it before a verb or description rather than directly as a noun modifier.

- あんなに遠いとは思いませんでした。 (あんなにとおいとはおもいませんでした。) - I did not think it was that far.
- あんなに練習したのに、負けてしまいました。 (あんなにれんしゅうしたのに、まけてしまいました。) - Even though I practiced so much, I lost.

In the second example, あんなに emphasizes the amount of practice; のに supplies the disappointed "even though" connection.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in hiragana. It belongs with [あんな](あんな.md), "that kind of," but the に form expresses degree adverbially. There is no kanji decomposition or loanword source to learn.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こんなに](こんなに.md) points to "this much," and [そんなに](そんなに.md) to "that much," often in response to the listener's information. These are contrasts in reference, not greater and smaller quantities.

## Distinctive meaning

あんなに can refer to something both speakers remember, not just something physically far away. It often conveys surprise at an extent, but it does not itself say whether that extent is good or bad. Compare あんな人 (あんなひと), "that kind of person," with あんなに親切な人 (あんなにしんせつなひと), "someone that kind."
