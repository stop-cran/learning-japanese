# 勧告 (かんこく) - recommendation; advice

## Meaning

勧告 is advice or a recommendation to take an action. It is a noun; 勧告する is transitive. A recommended action expressed as a noun can take を, as in 辞職を勧告する (じしょくをかんこくする), "recommend resignation."

- 委員会は辞職を勧告しました。 (いいんかいはじしょくをかんこくしました。) - The committee recommended resignation.
- 勧告を受けましたが、まだ決めていません。 (かんこくをうけましたが、まだきめていません。) - I received the recommendation, but I have not decided yet.

Receiving a 勧告 does not itself mean agreeing with it or carrying it out.

## How the kanji combine

[勧](../kanji/勧.md) contributes recommending or urging, and [告](../kanji/告.md) contributes telling or informing. Together they convey communicating advice about an action. The on-readings are カン + コク. This is a meaning aid, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [勧める](勧める.md) (すすめる) is a broader native verb for recommending, encouraging or offering something.
- [推薦](推薦.md) (すいせん) emphasizes endorsing a person or thing as suitable; [提案](提案.md) (ていあん) puts forward an idea or proposed action for consideration. Their English translations can overlap with "recommendation."
- [警告](警告.md) (けいこく) draws attention to danger, a problem or consequences. A 勧告 may accompany a warning, but recommending an action does not always warn of danger.
- [命令](命令.md) (めいれい), "order; command," presents an instruction as a command rather than advice. It is a contrast in force, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

勧告 fits an institutional recommendation, such as a committee advising an action. It need not be gentle advice, and the word alone does not establish its legal force or the recipient's compliance. Check who recommends what, and in which setting, rather than automatically translating every 勧告 as either a binding order or a casual suggestion.
