# 頂戴 (ちょうだい) - receive humbly; please give me

## Meaning

頂戴 has two important constructions with different registers.
**物を頂戴する** humbly describes receiving something.
With food or drink, 頂戴する can also mean humbly having or consuming it.
By contrast, the request **ちょうだい** is familiar, not automatically humble.

- お手紙を頂戴しました。 (おてがみをちょうだいしました。) - I received your letter. (Humble.)
- その本をちょうだい。 (そのほんをちょうだい。) - Please give me that book. (Familiar.)
- ちょっと待ってちょうだい。 (ちょっとまってちょうだい。) - Please wait a moment. (Familiar.)

For a thing, use **noun + をちょうだい**. For an action, use **verb's て-form + ちょうだい**,
not its dictionary form. Requests are usually written in kana.

## How the kanji combine

[頂](../kanji/頂.md) and 戴 both contribute receiving respectfully in this compound.
Their meanings reinforce one another in the humble receiving use.
The on readings チョウ + ダイ give ちょうだい.
The characters do not make the standalone request polite in every situation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いただく](いただく.md) overlaps with humble receiving and eating.
[ください](ください.md) supplies a polite request form: 待ってください, "please wait."
[もらう](もらう.md) describes receiving without the same humble marking.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish what the speaker is doing: reporting receipt with 頂戴しました or asking someone to act with ちょうだい.
The familiar request can be unsuitable in a formal encounter.
JMdict associates the request with feminine language, but that is a usage annotation, not a rule excluding speakers of other genders.
Relationship, tone, and construction matter more than a blanket label of "polite."
