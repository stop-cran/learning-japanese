# 日常 (にちじょう) - everyday life; routine

## Meaning

日常 names everyday life or the ordinary routine. It can modify a
noun with の, as in 日常の会話, "everyday conversation," or
join established compounds such as 日常生活, "daily life."
It can also function adverbially for what one ordinarily does.

- 旅行が終わって、日常に戻りました。 (りょこうがおわって、にちじょうにもどりました。) - The trip ended, and I returned to everyday life.
- 日常生活で使う言葉を勉強しています。 (にちじょうせいかつでつかうことばをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying words used in daily life.

日常的な (にちじょうてきな) is a related adjectival form:
日常的な問題, "an everyday problem."

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) contributes "day," and
[常](../kanji/常.md) contributes what is regular or usual.
Together they point to the regular course of day-to-day life.
The readings are にち + じょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[毎日](毎日.md) (まいにち) means "every day" and specifies
frequency. [普通](普通.md) (ふつう) describes what is ordinary
or usual more broadly. 非日常 (ひにちじょう) contrasts an
experience outside the everyday routine.

## Distinctive meaning

Something 日常的 need not happen on every calendar day.
The word concerns what belongs to normal life, not a precise
schedule. Likewise, 日常 does not inherently mean dull or unpleasant;
that judgment must come from the context.
