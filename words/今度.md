# 今度 (こんど) — this time; next time

## Meaning

今度 refers to the occasion in focus: "this time", "next time", or an upcoming occasion. It can also refer to a recent occasion; the surrounding conversation and tense determine the time.

- 今度は私が払います。  
  こんどはわたしがはらいます。  
  "This time, I'll pay."
- 今度の日曜日に会いましょう。  
  こんどのにちようびにあいましょう。  
  "Let's meet this coming Sunday."

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) (now, current) modifies [度](../kanji/度.md) (occasion): "the occasion currently in view". The on readings are コン + ド. The compound does not necessarily mean the exact present moment.

## Synonyms and antonyms

今回 こんかい explicitly selects "this time"; 次回 じかい selects the next occasion in a sequence. 前回 ぜんかい means "last time", a useful contrast rather than an antonym for every use of 今度.

## Distinctive meaning

今度 does not by itself specify a date. 今度また会いましょう こんどまたあいましょう means "Let's meet again sometime"; it need not make a definite appointment. Use a date or a phrase such as 今度の日曜日 when the timing matters.
