# 医療 (いりょう) - medical care

## Meaning

医療 is a noun for medical care or treatment. It can refer to care received by a patient or to the
services and systems that provide it. **医療を受ける** (いりょうをうける) means "receive medical care."

- 誰もが必要な医療を受けられることが大切です。 (だれもがひつようないりょうをうけられることがたいせつです。) - It is important that everyone can receive the medical care they need.
- この地域では医療費の負担が問題です。 (このちいきではいりょうひのふたんがもんだいです。) - The burden of medical expenses is a problem in this area.

医療費 (いりょうひ) means medical expenses. 医療機関 (いりょうきかん) refers to an institution providing medical services.

## How the kanji combine

[医](../kanji/医.md) contributes medicine or medical practice; 療 contributes treating or healing.
The related components reinforce the idea of medical treatment. Their on readings are イ and リョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[医学](医学.md) (いがく) is medicine as a field of knowledge, rather than the care provided.
治療 (ちりょう) focuses on treating an illness or injury.
[診察](診察.md) (しんさつ) is a medical examination; it can be part of receiving care, but does not name the whole system.

## Distinctive meaning

English "medicine" can mean a subject, treatment, or a drug. 医療 covers the care and treatment side;
for a medicine you take, use [薬](薬.md) (くすり).
Learn a construction such as 医療を受ける rather than treating this noun as a direct substitute for every English medical expression.
