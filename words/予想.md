# 予想 (よそう) — prediction; expectation

## Meaning

予想 is an expectation or prediction about what will happen. It is also used with する as a verb.
For a predicted statement, put a plain-form clause before と予想する.

- 明日は雨が降ると予想しています。 (あしたはあめがふるとよそうしています。) — I expect it to rain tomorrow.
- 試合の結果を予想しました。 (しあいのけっかをよそうしました。) — I predicted the result of the match.

The first predicts a statement; the second uses を for the outcome being predicted.

## How the kanji combine

[予](../kanji/予.md) contributes "beforehand"; [想](../kanji/想.md) concerns a thought or conception.
Together they suggest thinking ahead to an outcome. The on readings are ヨ + ソウ.
The combination names a prediction, not a promise that it will come true.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予定](予定.md) (よてい) concerns plans or arrangements, though its dictionary meanings also include
expectation and estimate. A useful contrast is predicting rain versus scheduling a meeting.
想像 (そうぞう), "imagination," need not be about a future event.

## Distinctive meaning

A 予想 can be wrong. It does not establish that the speaker controls the event or has arranged it.
Use the whole construction 雨が降ると予想する (あめがふるとよそうする);
と links the predicted statement to the prediction verb.
