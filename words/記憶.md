# 記憶 (きおく) - memory; recollection

## Meaning

記憶 is memory, including the ability to retain information and what remains
in someone's mind. 記憶する is a transitive verb: information + を + 記憶する.

- 子供のころの記憶が残っています。 (こどものころのきおくがのこっています。) - I still have memories of my childhood.
- その番号は記憶していません。 (そのばんごうはきおくしていません。) - I do not have that number memorized.

記憶がある means to have a recollection; 記憶にない (きおくにない) means that
something is not in one's memory.

## How the kanji combine

[記](../kanji/記.md) contributes recording, and 憶 contributes remembering.
The two related ideas combine into keeping something in memory. The on
readings are キ + オク, with no small っ between them.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[覚える](覚える.md) (おぼえる) is useful for learning or remembering something.
[思い出す](思い出す.md) (おもいだす) focuses on recalling something from memory.
記憶 names the memory itself or, with する, retaining information.

## Distinctive meaning

The word also occurs in computing for memory or storage, as in 記憶装置
(きおくそうち), "storage device." Context distinguishes a person's recollection
from data storage. A negative statement such as 記憶にありません reports lack
of recollection; it does not by itself establish that an event never happened.
