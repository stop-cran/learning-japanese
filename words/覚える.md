# 覚える (おぼえる) — memorize; remember

## Meaning

覚える means to memorize, learn by heart, or remember. The item learned takes を.
It is an ichidan verb: 覚えます (おぼえます), 覚えた (おぼえた).

- 新しい言葉を十個覚えました。 (あたらしいことばをじゅっこおぼえました。) — I learned ten new words.
- その名前を覚えています。 (そのなまえをおぼえています。) — I remember that name.

The second example describes retaining something in memory, not necessarily studying it right now.

## How the kanji combine

[覚](../kanji/覚.md) supplies the memory association in this native verb. The える after it
completes おぼえる and changes with the ending. Do not replace it with the ending of 覚める
(さめる), "wake up": that is a different word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

思い出す (おもいだす) focuses on recalling something from memory.
覚える can instead describe putting it into memory, as in learning the new words above.
[忘れる](../words/忘れる.md) (わすれる), "forget," is a useful contrast to retaining it.

## Distinctive meaning

English "remember" covers both keeping something in mind and bringing it back to mind.
Compare 名前を覚えている (なまえをおぼえている), "remember a name,"
with 名前を思い出す (なまえをおもいだす), "recall a name."
This article focuses on the memory sense, not every use of 覚える.
