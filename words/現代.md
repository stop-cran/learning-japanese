# 現代 (げんだい) - present age; modern times

## Meaning

現代 is a noun for the present age or modern times. It describes an era or contemporary society, not merely this instant. Use 現代の + noun or established compounds such as 現代社会 (げんだいしゃかい), "contemporary society."

- 現代の生活ではインターネットが重要です。 (げんだいのせいかつではインターネットがじゅうようです。) - The internet is important in modern life.
- この授業では現代の日本文学を読みます。 (このじゅぎょうではげんだいのにほんぶんがくをよみます。) - In this class, we read contemporary Japanese literature.

The の in 現代の生活 connects two nouns; do not substitute な.

## How the kanji combine

[現](../kanji/現.md) contributes the present or currently existing; [代](../kanji/代.md) means an age or generation here. The modifier-plus-head relationship is "the present age." The readings are ゲン + ダイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[現在](現在.md) (げんざい) refers to the present time or current state. [今](今.md) (いま) is the everyday "now."

[近代](近代.md) (きんだい) can name a historical modern period. Its boundaries depend on the subject and historical framework; it is not automatically interchangeable with 現代.

## Distinctive meaning

現代 does not mean "today" as a calendar date. [昔](昔.md) (むかし), "long ago," offers an everyday contrast, while a history course may define more precise periods. Do not attach one universal starting year to 現代 across literature, art, and political history.
