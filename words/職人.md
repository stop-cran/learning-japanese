# 職人 (しょくにん) - craftsperson; artisan

## Meaning

職人 is a craftsperson, artisan, or skilled tradesperson. It identifies
someone through a practical trade or skilled workmanship.

- この椅子は職人が手作業で作りました。 (このいすはしょくにんがてさぎょうでつくりました。) - A craftsperson made this chair by hand.
- 彼は革の職人です。 (かれはかわのしょくにんです。) - He is a leather craftsperson.

手作業で means by hand in the first example. It specifies the method used
there, not a rule that a 職人 can never use machinery.

## How the kanji combine

[職](../kanji/職.md) contributes an occupation or trade, and
[人](../kanji/人.md) contributes a person. The first character qualifies
the person. The on readings are ショク + ニン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

大工 (だいく) names a carpenter, one particular trade.
[技師](技師.md) (ぎし) identifies an engineer or technical professional;
職人 can also be used of skilled technical workers or mechanics.
[工員](工員.md) (こういん) identifies a factory worker, without by itself
establishing the same craft expertise.

## Distinctive meaning

職人 is not confined to traditional handicrafts. It can extend to a highly
skilled specialist in another field. That does not make it a neutral label
for every employee. The word alone also does not certify a particular
qualification, employment contract, or gender.
