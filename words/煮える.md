# 煮える (にえる) - be boiled; be cooked through

## Meaning

煮える is an intransitive ichidan verb for being boiled or cooked, especially
food cooking in liquid. The food is marked with が, not as a direct object
with を.

- 芋が煮えました。 (いもがにえました。) - The potatoes are cooked.
- 肉はまだよく煮えていません。 (にくはまだよくにえていません。) - The meat is not thoroughly cooked yet.

Useful forms are 煮えます, 煮えた and 煮えて. The related transitive
verb 煮る (にる) describes cooking or simmering something: 肉を煮る
(にくをにる), "simmer the meat."

## How the kanji combine

煮 writes the native stem に-, followed by える. This is not a two-kanji
compound. Learn the pair 煮る / 煮える rather than treating every verb
ending in える as a passive.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[沸く](沸く.md) (わく) is a straightforward verb for water coming to a boil.
煮える can also describe liquid boiling; its food-cooking use is not a ban on
that meaning. [炊く](炊く.md) (たく) commonly concerns cooking rice or grains,
while [焼ける](焼ける.md) (やける) concerns baking, grilling or burning.
The cooking method matters.

## Distinctive meaning

煮えた can describe the result of cooking, not just bubbles appearing.
If meat is まだ煮えていない, it is not ready through cooking yet;
this is not merely a statement that the stove has not been switched on.
For deliberately heating water, compare the transitive [沸かす](沸かす.md)
(わかす).
