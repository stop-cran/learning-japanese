# 砕く (くだく) - smash; crush into pieces

## Meaning

砕く means break something into small pieces, smash it, or crush it into powder. It is transitive: the material affected takes を.

- 氷を細かく砕きました。 (こおりをこまかくくだきました。) - I crushed the ice into small pieces.
- 岩を砕く機械です。 (いわをくだくきかいです。) - It is a machine for breaking up rock.

Additional uses include crushing someone's hopes and making difficult content understandable. 噛み砕いて説明する (かみくだいてせつめいする) means explain in accessible terms, literally using the image of chewing something into smaller pieces.

The godan forms include 砕きます (くだきます), 砕いて (くだいて), 砕かない (くだかない).

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 砕 supplies the native stem くだ; く is the verb ending. There is no 砕 card in the current collection. The dictionary also records 摧く as a rare kanji spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[砕ける](砕ける.md) (くだける) is the intransitive counterpart: 氷が砕ける, the ice breaks into pieces. [壊す](壊す.md) (こわす), break or damage, need not produce small fragments. [割る](割る.md) (わる) can split something into pieces without emphasizing fine crushing.

[崩す](崩す.md) (くずす) foregrounds breaking down a coherent shape or arrangement; it is not limited to grinding material.

## Distinctive meaning

The physical use highlights fragmentation, not merely a machine ceasing to work. Figurative combinations must be learned separately: simplifying an explanation does not mean damaging its content.
