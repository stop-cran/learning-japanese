# 汁 (しる) - juice; sap; soup; broth

## Meaning

汁 means juice or sap from something, and also soup or broth.
It can stand alone as a noun or appear at the end of a compound.

- レモンの汁を絞りました。 (レモンのしるをしぼりました。) - I squeezed juice from a lemon.
- 汁が熱いので、気をつけてください。 (しるがあついので、きをつけてください。) - The soup is hot, so please be careful.

The food context identifies the soup in the second example; 汁 does
not itself mean hot. Learn compound readings individually, such as
味噌汁 (みそしる), miso soup.

## How the kanji combine

汁 writes the whole native word しる, with no second character or
okurigana to divide. Learn the noun's reading rather than substituting
an on reading from another compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ジュース](ジュース.md) overlaps for juice and has broader beverage uses.
[スープ](スープ.md) overlaps for soup.
[液体](液体.md) (えきたい), liquid, is a broader physical category,
not a name for every particular juice or broth.

## Distinctive meaning

The same dictionary family also records つゆ and the spelling 液.
The reading しる is restricted to 汁 there. Its dipping-sauce sense is
restricted to つゆ, so do not transfer that sense automatically to
しる. This article teaches the juice/sap and soup/broth senses.
