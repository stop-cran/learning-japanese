# 貢献 (こうけん) - contribution

## Meaning

貢献 is a contribution that furthers a goal, organization, or cause.
The intransitive verb 貢献する takes に for what benefits: 社会に貢献する (しゃかいにこうけんする),
"contribute to society." A means can be marked with で.

- この研究は医療の発展に貢献しました。 (このけんきゅうはいりょうのはってんにこうけんしました。) - This research contributed to advances in medical care.
- 自分の経験を生かして、地域に貢献したいです。 (じぶんのけいけんをいかして、ちいきにこうけんしたいです。) - I want to use my experience to contribute to the community.

As a noun, 地域への貢献 (ちいきへのこうけん) means "a contribution to the community."

## How the kanji combine

貢 contributes offering or paying tribute, and 献 contributes presenting or dedicating.
Their combined image helps explain giving something toward a larger purpose.
The on-readings are コウ + ケン. This component explanation is not a claim that present-day contributions must be physical offerings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[協力](協力.md) (きょうりょく) is cooperation or assistance.
貢献 highlights the benefit or advancement produced.
[寄付](寄付.md) (きふ) is a donation; money or goods can contribute to a cause, but 貢献 also includes research, labor, or expertise.

## Distinctive meaning

The word evaluates an action as serving a goal. Simply participating does not by itself establish a contribution.
There is no single everyday antonym: preventing progress and making no contribution are different claims.
Avoid translating "services" here as a shop's customer-service department.
