# 寒い (さむい) — cold weather

## Meaning

寒い describes cold weather, cold surroundings, or feeling cold in those surroundings.
It is an い-adjective: 寒い日 (さむいひ), "a cold day"; 寒くない (さむくない), "not cold."

- 今日は寒いです。 (きょうはさむいです。) — It is cold today.
- この部屋は寒いですね。 (このへやはさむいですね。) — This room is cold, isn't it?

## How the kanji combine

This is a native Japanese word, not a compound of on readings.
[寒](../kanji/寒.md) carries the cold meaning and is read さむ; the written い completes the adjective.
The kana after a kanji that show part of a word's ending are called *okurigana*.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冷たい](冷たい.md) (つめたい) describes cold to the touch, as in 冷たい水 (つめたいみず), "cold water."
寒い instead fits the weather or the surrounding room in the examples above.
暑い (あつい), "hot," is the weather contrast: 暑い日 (あついひ), "a hot day."

## Distinctive meaning

English uses "cold" for both the air around you and the temperature of a drink.
Japanese distinguishes these uses: 寒い fits a chilly day, while 冷たい fits chilled water.
Learn the adjective together with what is cold rather than substituting one translation everywhere.
