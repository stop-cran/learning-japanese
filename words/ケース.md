# ケース (ケース) - case; container; instance

## Meaning

ケース is a noun with both a physical and an abstract use. It can be a case or container, or a particular instance or situation.

- 眼鏡をケースに入れました。 (めがねをケースにいれました。) - I put my glasses in their case.
- このようなケースは珍しいです。 (このようなケースはめずらしいです。) - A case like this is unusual.

The second example does not identify a container. **このケースでは** means "in this case," setting the situation in which the following statement applies.

## How the kanji combine

ケース is written in katakana as a loanword corresponding to English "case." The mark ー lengthens ケ; there are no kanji components to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[箱](箱.md) (はこ) means a box and overlaps with some containers. [場合](場合.md) (ばあい) names a situation or circumstance, and [例](例.md) (れい) an instance or example. The appropriate comparison depends on which ケース sense is intended.

## Distinctive meaning

The two everyday senses belong to the same inspected entry, not separate same-sounding words that require different spellings. A glasses case and a business case are distinguished by context. Do not assume every English use of "case" transfers automatically; for example, grammatical case has its own established Japanese terminology.
