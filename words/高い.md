# 高い (たかい) — high, tall, expensive

## Meaning

高い is an い-adjective with three common senses:

- **High, tall**: 高い山 (a high mountain), 背が高い (tall in height).
- **Expensive**: このカメラは高いです (this camera is expensive).
- **High (in degree)**: 熱が高い, 声が高い (a high voice).

## How the kanji combine

[高](../kanji/高.md) with the okurigana い: たか + い. Related forms: 高さ たかさ (height), 高める たかめる (to raise), 高まる たかまる (to rise). Past: 高かった; negative: 高くない; adverbial: 高く.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonyms: 低い ひくい (low), [安い](安い.md) やすい (cheap).
- Related: 高価 こうか (high-priced, formal).

## Distinctive meaning

For prices 高い is the plain word, and 安い is the opposite. For height, 高い is used for tall things; a tall person is usually 背が高い, and 長い is for length ([長](../kanji/長.md)).
