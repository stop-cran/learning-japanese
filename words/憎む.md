# 憎む (にくむ) - hate; detest

## Meaning

憎む is a transitive godan verb meaning to hate or detest. The person or thing hated takes を.
Learn 憎みます, the polite form, and 憎んで, the て-form.

- 彼女は暴力を憎んでいます。 (かのじょはぼうりょくをにくんでいます。) - She hates violence.
- 私は彼を憎んでいません。 (わたしはかれをにくんでいません。) - I do not hate him.

憎んでいる can describe an ongoing feeling. The negative 憎まない and the passive 憎まれる
have different meanings: "do not hate" and "be hated."

## How the kanji combine

[憎](../kanji/憎.md) writes the native stem にく-, followed by む. There is no compound to divide.
The related noun 憎しみ (にくしみ) names hatred; 憎む describes the feeling directed at its object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[嫌う](嫌う.md) (きらう) can express dislike, hatred or shunning. It overlaps with 憎む;
do not treat the two as having a rigid intensity boundary in every context.
[嫌い](嫌い.md) (きらい) expresses dislike with different grammar: 彼が嫌いです,
compared with 彼を憎んでいます.

[愛する](愛する.md) (あいする), "love," provides a contrasting feeling.

## Distinctive meaning

憎む expresses hatred or detestation, not merely finding a proposed action inconvenient.
The passive 憎まれる describes being the target of that feeling, not feeling hatred toward somebody else.
Conversely, 憎んでいない only denies hatred; it does not assert affection or approval.
