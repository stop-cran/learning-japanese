# どっと - in a burst; in a rush; suddenly

## Meaning

どっと describes a burst of laughter or applause, people or things arriving
in a rush, or a sudden development. It is an adverb.

- 観客がどっと笑いました。 (かんきゃくがどっとわらいました。) - The audience burst out laughing.
- 開店すると、客がどっと入ってきました。 (かいてんすると、きゃくがどっとはいってきました。) - When the shop opened, customers came rushing in.
- 仕事が終わると、疲れがどっと出ました。 (しごとがおわると、つかれがどっとでました。) - Once work was over, the fatigue hit me all at once.

The predicate supplies the action or change; どっと alone does not mean laugh.

## How the kanji combine

This mimetic expression is written in kana.
The final と is already part of どっと; do not routinely append another と.
The small っ distinguishes it from an unbroken どと.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一斉](一斉.md) (いっせい), often 一斉に, emphasizes simultaneous action.
[突然](突然.md) (とつぜん) overlaps in sudden occurrence.
[どんどん](どんどん.md) can describe rapid or continuous progress;
that is not automatically a single collective burst.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every use requires a crowd: the fatigue example concerns one person's sudden state.
Conversely, rushing customers need not make a particular sound.
Do not confuse this expression with ドット, the separate loanword meaning dot.
