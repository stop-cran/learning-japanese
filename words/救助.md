# 救助 (きゅうじょ) - rescue; relief

## Meaning

救助 is a noun for rescue or aid, especially helping people out of danger. 救助する is transitive: the person rescued takes を. The passive 救助される describes being rescued.

- 消防隊が住民を救助しました。 (しょうぼうたいがじゅうみんをきゅうじょしました。) - The firefighters rescued the residents.
- 山で救助を待っていました。 (やまできゅうじょをまっていました。) - We were waiting to be rescued in the mountains.

In the second sentence, 救助 is the object of 待つ, "wait for," rather than a verb. 救助活動 (きゅうじょかつどう) means rescue operations.

## How the kanji combine

救 contributes saving or rescuing, and [助](../kanji/助.md) contributes helping. Their related meanings reinforce the rescue sense. The reading きゅうじょ has a long う after きゅ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[助ける](助ける.md) (たすける) is a broader verb for helping or saving. [援助](援助.md) (えんじょ) names assistance or support, including funding and supplies, without necessarily describing removal from immediate danger.

## Distinctive meaning

A person may receive financial 援助 after being physically 救助された. These are different aspects of helping, not contradictory actions. For an ordinary request to help with homework or carry a box, choose the expression suited to that task rather than treating 救助 as a general substitute for every English "help."
