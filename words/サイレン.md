# サイレン - siren; signaling sound

## Meaning

サイレン names a siren or the sound it produces. It can signal danger, an emergency vehicle, or a designated time.

- 救急車のサイレンが聞こえます。 (きゅうきゅうしゃのサイレンがきこえます。) - I can hear an ambulance siren.
- 正午にサイレンが鳴りました。 (しょうごにサイレンがなりました。) - A siren sounded at noon.

サイレンが鳴る (サイレンがなる) describes it sounding; サイレンを鳴らす (サイレンをならす) describes someone sounding it.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English **siren**, with no kanji components. The final ン is part of the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ベル](ベル.md) is a bell, another kind of sound-producing signal. A bell and a siren are not automatically the same mechanism or sound. [警告](警告.md) (けいこく) is a warning; it can be written or spoken without any siren.

[合図](合図.md) (あいず) is a signal or cue to notice or act, and [信号](信号.md) (しんごう) is a signal carrying information. A siren's sound can serve as either; neither broader word specifies a siren.

## Distinctive meaning

A siren sounding does not by itself identify a fire, police incident, or other particular emergency. The second example is a time signal. Japanese dictionaries also record the mythological Siren under サイレン; the practical examples here concern signaling, not mythology.
