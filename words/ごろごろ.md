# ごろごろ — rumbling; rolling; lazing about

## Meaning

A low rumble, growl, or purr; also rolling heavy objects, lazing around, or things lying about in abundance. It can describe a bothersome foreign-body sensation.

- 遠くで雷（かみなり）がごろごろ鳴っている。 — Thunder is rumbling in the distance.
- 日曜日は家でごろごろしていた。 — I spent Sunday lazing around at home.
- 目に砂が入って、ごろごろする。 — Sand got in my eye, and it feels gritty, as though something is in it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[からから](からから.md) can be a lighter, hollow rattle. [ざらざら](ざらざら.md) describes granular surface roughness rather than specifically something lodged in the eye. [どろどろ](どろどろ.md) has a separate rumbling sense as well as its thick-fluid meanings.

## Distinctive meaning

ごろごろ鳴る, ごろごろ転がる（ころがる）, and ごろごろする have different functions. 猫がのどをごろごろ鳴らす describes purring; 石がごろごろある describes stones lying about in numbers. The eye sensation does not identify its cause, and the lounging sense need not involve literally rolling across the floor. A child may also call thunder ごろごろ as a noun.
