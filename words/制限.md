# 制限 (せいげん) - restriction; limit

## Meaning

制限 is a restriction or limit. As a transitive する-verb, it means to limit something. The thing restricted takes を, and a specified maximum or range can take に.

- 入場者数を百人に制限します。 (にゅうじょうしゃすうをひゃくにんにせいげんします。) - We will limit admission to one hundred people.
- この試験には時間制限があります。 (このしけんにはじかんせいげんがあります。) - This exam has a time limit.

時間制限 (じかんせいげん) and 年齢制限 (ねんれいせいげん), "age restriction," identify what is limited. 制限がある means that a restriction exists.

## How the kanji combine

[制](../kanji/制.md) contributes regulating or controlling; [限](../kanji/限.md) contributes a boundary or limit. Together they concern holding something within set bounds. Read セイ + ゲン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[限る](限る.md) (かぎる) can also mean to limit. [禁止](禁止.md) (きんし) means prohibition: a restriction may allow something within bounds, while a prohibition bars the specified activity. 無制限 (むせいげん), "unlimited," contrasts with a stated limit.

## Distinctive meaning

A limit does not tell you whether it is a maximum, an age range, or another condition until the context specifies it. 制限を設ける (せいげんをもうける) means "set a restriction"; 制限を解除する (せいげんをかいじょする) means "lift a restriction." The noun itself does not express approval or disapproval.
