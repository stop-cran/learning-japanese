# 弾 (たま) - bullet

## Meaning

弾 (たま) is a noun for a bullet. The spelling helps distinguish this meaning from other words pronounced たま. In descriptions, が can mark the bullet and に the place where it is found.

- 博物館には古い弾が展示されています。 (はくぶつかんにはふるいたまがてんじされています。) - Old bullets are on display in the museum.
- 壁に弾が残っていました。 (かべにたまがのこっていました。) - A bullet remained in the wall.

These examples name the object; the noun is not itself a verb meaning "shoot."

## How the kanji combine

The single character 弾 carries the bullet meaning with the native reading たま. The on reading ダン appears in compounds such as 弾丸 (だんがん), "bullet." Do not transfer that reading to this standalone noun automatically.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[玉](玉.md) and [球](球.md) also have the reading たま. 玉 is used for such things as balls or gems, while 球 is useful for balls and spheres. These spellings share a dictionary entry, but that does not make them good substitutes in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict specifically associates the bullet sense with 弾 as a spelling preference. Learn that practical association rather than treating the entire entry's ball, jewel, and other senses as the definition of this card. There is no useful direct antonym.
