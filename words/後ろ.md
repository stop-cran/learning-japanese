# 後ろ (うしろ) — behind, the back

## Meaning

後ろ is the space behind something or the back side: 後ろを見る (look behind), 車の後ろ (the back of the car), 後ろに立つ (stand behind), 後ろ向き うしろむき (facing backward).

## How the kanji combine

[後](../kanji/後.md) with the kun'yomi うし.ろ, written with okurigana ろ. Opposite of 前 まえ ([前](../kanji/前.md)). The word is purely wago.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: 前 まえ (front).
- Related: 後 あと (after), 裏 うら (the reverse side, back of something), 背中 せなか (a person's back).

## Distinctive meaning

後ろ is for physical location (behind, at the back). For "after" in time use 後 あと or 後で, and for "back side" of a surface use 裏.
