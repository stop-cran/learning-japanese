# 奇しい (あやしい) — strange; suspicious (rare spelling)

## Meaning

奇しい is a rare spelling of あやしい, normally written [怪しい](怪しい.md). Learn it for recognition,
not as a recommended way to write ordinary modern sentences. It does not have a separate, fixed
"wonderful rather than suspicious" meaning.

A reading example, rather than an invented rare collocation:

- この表の「奇しい」は「あやしい」と読みます。  
  (この ひょうの「奇しい」は「あやしい」と よみます。)  
  — In this table, 奇しい is read あやしい.

For ordinary production, use 怪しい, as in 彼の話は怪しい (かれの はなしは あやしい),
"His story sounds fishy."

## How the kanji combine

[奇](../kanji/奇.md) represents あや in this spelling; しい is the written kana ending.
The character's unusual or extraordinary associations are not a rule assigning a new lexical meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

Compare the ordinary spelling [怪しい](怪しい.md) and the bewitching or alluring spelling
[妖しい](妖しい.md). There is no useful antonym specific to the rare spelling.

## Distinctive meaning

The あやしい spelling is documented under **あやしい【怪・妖・奇】** in the *Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten*.
It is not a spelling listed in the inspected JMdict entry for 怪しい; that limitation is why the
Japanese dictionary is cited explicitly here.

Keep classical 奇し (くし／くすし), "mysterious," and 奇しくも (くしくも), "by a strange coincidence,"
separate. The character dictionary also records めずらしい for 奇しい; the reading on **this card**
is あやしい, not an assertion that every occurrence of those written characters has that reading.

For the spelling and reading distinctions, see
[strange, suspicious, and rare](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
