# 真っ先 (まっさき) - first of all; very front

## Meaning

真っ先 identifies the foremost position or beginning. The useful
adverbial pattern 真っ先に means first, before the others or before
other things.

- 困ったとき、真っ先に母に相談しました。 (こまったとき、まっさきにははにそうだんしました。) - When I was in trouble, I consulted my mother first.
- 彼は真っ先に手を挙げました。 (かれはまっさきにてをあげました。) - He was the first to raise his hand.

真っ先に思い浮かぶ (まっさきにおもいうかぶ) means come to mind first.

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md) intensifies [先](../kanji/先.md), ahead or first.
Learn まっ + さき as one word. Here 先 is さき, not セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[先頭](先頭.md) (せんとう) identifies the head of a line or leading
position. [最初](最初.md) (さいしょ) concerns the beginning or first item.
[まず](まず.md) introduces what comes first.
These overlap without replacing every construction freely.

## Distinctive meaning

Being first among several responses is different from doing something
for the first time in one's life: [初めて](初めて.md) (はじめて)
expresses that latter idea. Nor does 真っ先 mean every outward-facing
front surface of an object.
