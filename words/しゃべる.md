# しゃべる - talk; chat

## Meaning

しゃべる means to talk, chat, or chatter. It is a godan verb despite ending in べる: しゃべります, しゃべって, しゃべらない.

- 友達と一時間しゃべりました。 (ともだちといちじかんしゃべりました。) - I chatted with a friend for an hour.
- このことは誰にもしゃべらないでください。 (このことはだれにもしゃべらないでください。) - Please don't tell anyone about this.

Use と for a conversation partner, に for someone addressed, and を when explicitly marking content or a language, as in 日本語をしゃべる (にほんごをしゃべる), "speak Japanese." The verb can also stand without a stated object.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses the kana spelling. 喋る is the corresponding kanji form, and JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana. There is no loanword origin or multi-kanji decomposition to supply.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話す](話す.md) (はなす) broadly means speak or talk; しゃべる readily fits conversational activity or chatter. [言う](言う.md) (いう) focuses on saying particular words. 黙る (だまる), "fall silent," gives a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

しゃべる can sound relaxed, but it does not necessarily mean pointless or excessive speech. Context can make it neutral chatting, talkativeness, or revealing information. The second example concerns disclosure, not simply the sound of someone's voice.
