# 霜 (しも) - frost

## Meaning

霜 is a noun meaning "frost": the fine ice crystals that form on a
cold surface. Learn 霜が降りる (しもがおりる), "frost forms,"
and 霜がつく, "become covered with frost."

- 今朝、庭に霜が降りました。 (けさ、にわにしもがおりました。) - Frost formed in the garden this morning.
- 草に白い霜がついています。 (くさにしろいしもがついています。) - The grass is covered with white frost.

に marks the place or surface. In 霜が降りる, 降りる is read
おりる, not ふる. Learn the expression as a unit rather than
translating English "frost falls" into a rain-like verb pattern.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 霜 represents the whole native noun しも.
Its rain element provides a visual weather association. There is no
multi-character compound whose parts separately supply the spoken word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[雪](雪.md) (ゆき) is snow, which falls as precipitation.
[氷](氷.md) (こおり) is ice more generally. Frost is related to
both but is not another name for a layer of fallen snow.
Warm weather is a contextual contrast, not a noun antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb 降りる in the fixed weather expression does not mean
that frost forms in exactly the same way as snow. The word is useful
for describing a cold morning's surfaces, including grass and the ground.
