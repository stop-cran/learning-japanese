# 再び (ふたたび) - again; once more

## Meaning

再び is an adverb meaning again, once more, or a second time. It presents an event or state as occurring after an earlier instance. It modifies the predicate directly, without adding に.

- 十年後、私たちは再び会いました。 (じゅうねんご、わたしたちはふたたびあいました。) - Ten years later, we met again.
- 雨が再び降り始めました。 (あめがふたたびふりはじめました。) - It started raining again.

再び会える日 (ふたたびあえるひ) means "the day when we can meet again." The expression does not specify how long the interval will be.

## How the kanji combine

再 contributes recurrence or repetition. The native reading is ふたたび, with び written as okurigana. Learn the whole adverb rather than trying to pronounce 再 with an on reading here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[また](また.md) overlaps in the sense "again" and has other uses, including adding information. [もう一度](もう一度.md) (もういちど), "one more time," is particularly useful in a request to repeat an action. The expressions can describe the same repeated event.

## Distinctive meaning

再び directs attention to recurrence, not to simply adding another unrelated item. In 再び開く (ふたたびひらく), "open again," something has opened before. It does not count the total number of openings precisely: translating it as "a second time" should not create an unwarranted claim that there were exactly two occasions.
