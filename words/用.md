# 用 (よう) -- use; purpose

## Meaning

用 can mean a use or purpose. As a suffix, it follows the intended user or activity and means "for the use of" or "for."

- この箱は別の用に使います。 (このはこはべつのようにつかいます。) -- I will use this box for another purpose.
- これは子ども用のいすです。 (これはこどもようのいすです。) -- This is a chair for children.

In the suffix pattern, attach 用 directly to the preceding noun, then use の before the item: 子ども用のいす.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [用](../kanji/用.md) is read with the on reading よう. It contributes use or purpose both as a noun and in compounds such as 仕事用 (しごとよう), for work.

## Synonyms and antonyms

用途 (ようと) explicitly names an intended use. 目的 (もくてき) is a goal or purpose. 使う (つかう) is the verb "use," not a grammatical substitute for this noun or suffix.

## Distinctive meaning

用 also means business or an errand: 用があります (ようがあります), "I have something to do." Context separates that engagement from an object's intended use. 子ども用 does not mean "children's errand."
