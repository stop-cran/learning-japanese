# 十 (じゅう) — ten

## Meaning

十 means ten. **じゅう** is the ordinary numerical reading, used in counting and in many number compounds. **とお** is the native form completing the general counting series ひとつ, ふたつ … ここのつ, とお.

- 十円あります。 (じゅうえんあります。) — I have ten yen.
- りんごが十あります。 (りんごがとおあります。) — There are ten apples.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [十](../kanji/十.md) writes both readings. The primary reading じゅう is Sino-Japanese, so the page is classified as kango; とお is native. Write とお, not とう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

九 (きゅう) and 十一 (じゅういち) are neighboring numbers, not synonyms or opposites. The numeral 10 represents the same quantity.

## Distinctive meaning

The general native counting form is とお, without a final つ. Counter combinations must be learned separately: 十人 is じゅうにん, "ten people," while 十日 is とおか, "the tenth day; ten days." Do not substitute とお wherever じゅう appears.
