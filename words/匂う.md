# 匂う (におう) - give off a smell; be fragrant

## Meaning

匂う is an intransitive godan verb: something gives off a smell or fragrance.
The source of the smell can take が.

- 花がよく匂います。 (はながよくにおいます。) - The flowers give off a strong fragrance.
- この魚は少し臭います。 (このさかなはすこしにおいます。) - This fish smells a little bad.

The spelling 匂う is usual for a pleasant smell, and 臭う for an unpleasant
one. These are spelling preferences, not two unrelated pronunciations
or an absolute rule that every scent must be classified the same way.
The て-form is 匂って (におって), and the negative is 匂わない (におわない).

## How the kanji combine

匂 writes the native stem にお-, followed by う. There is no compound to
divide. The alternate spelling 臭う uses 臭 with the same whole-word reading.
The related noun [におい](におい.md) means a smell or scent.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[香り](香り.md) (かおり) names a fragrance. [嗅ぐ](嗅ぐ.md) (かぐ) describes
sniffing or perceiving a smell: 花の匂いを嗅ぐ (はなのにおいをかぐ),
"smell the flowers' scent." That differs from the flowers giving it off.
[臭い](臭い.md) (くさい) is an adjective, "smelly," not this verb's noun form.

## Distinctive meaning

The complete dictionary family also includes glowing or brightness and
figuratively showing hints of something. The brightness use is literary;
it is not the default interpretation of a fish that 臭う.
In an appropriate context, 臭う can suggest that something seems suspicious,
rather than report a literal odor. Do not reduce every such use to the
act of sniffing.
