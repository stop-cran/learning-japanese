# 何分 (なにぶん) - please; after all

## Meaning

なにぶん can earnestly ask for consideration, "please," or introduce
a circumstance behind a request or explanation, "after all; in any case."
何ぶん is another spelling.

- 何分よろしくお願いします。 (なにぶんよろしくおねがいします。) - Please give this your kind consideration.
- 何分初めてのことなので、分からないことが多いです。 (なにぶんはじめてのことなので、わからないことがおおいです。) - As this is my first time, there is a lot I do not understand.

In the second example, the following clause states the circumstance;
なにぶん asks the listener to take it into account.
The dictionary also records a noun/no-adjectival "some; a little; of some
kind" sense. That is separate from its request and explanatory uses.

## How the kanji combine

[何](../kanji/何.md) supplies indefiniteness and
[分](../kanji/分.md) has part or portion associations.
Learn the complete reading なにぶん for this entry.
The same written 何分 read なんぷん asks "how many minutes?"
That is a separate dictionary entry, not the reading taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[なにしろ](なにしろ.md) overlaps in foregrounding a reason.
[どうぞ](どうぞ.md) and [お願いします](お願いします.md)
(おねがいします) also occur in requests, but have their own grammar.
なにぶん is not a replacement for every polite verb or request ending.

## Distinctive meaning

なにぶん does not turn the following explanation into a question about
time. It often suits a considerate request or apology; the surrounding
wording still determines politeness and what is being requested.
The quantity sense, request sense and explanatory sense should not be
forced into a single literal translation such as "what part."
