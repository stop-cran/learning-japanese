# 卒業 (そつぎょう) — graduation

## Meaning

卒業 means graduation or completion of a course of study. It forms the verb 卒業する. The school or institution one graduates from commonly takes を.

- 三月に高校を卒業します。 (さんがつにこうこうをそつぎょうします。) — I'll graduate from high school in March.
- 大学を卒業して、働き始めました。 (だいがくをそつぎょうして、はたらきはじめました。) — I graduated from university and started working.

## How the kanji combine

卒 carries a finishing association, and [業](../kanji/業.md) refers here to study or work. Their on readings combine as ソツ + ギョウ. Keep the long ぎょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

入学 (にゅうがく), "entering a school," marks the beginning rather than the completion of schooling. 中退 (ちゅうたい) means leaving before completing the program, not graduating.

## Distinctive meaning

Although the school takes を, 卒業する describes the student's completion or departure, not an action done to the school. In broader contexts, 卒業 can mean moving on from a hobby, role, or group; the school sense is the starting point.
