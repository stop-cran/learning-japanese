# 知らせ (しらせ) - news; notification

## Meaning

知らせ is a noun for news, word received, or a notification. It can be personal news or an announcement, not just news reporting. Useful patterns include **知らせが届く**, "news arrives," and **知らせを受ける**, "receive notice."

- 友達からうれしい知らせが届きました。 (ともだちからうれしいしらせがとどきました。) - I received happy news from a friend.
- 学校から休校の知らせがありました。 (がっこうからきゅうこうのしらせがありました。) - There was a notice from the school that it would be closed.

から identifies the source, and の in 休校の知らせ identifies the content. The polite form お知らせ appears in notices and announcements.

## How the kanji combine

[知](../kanji/知.md) contributes knowing. 知らせ is related to the stem of [知らせる](知らせる.md) (しらせる), "inform": the noun names what is communicated. Its reading is しらせ, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[連絡](連絡.md) (れんらく) emphasizes contact or communicating information. [便り](便り.md) (たより) can be news or correspondence from someone. [ニュース](ニュース.md) also means news, including reported news.

## Distinctive meaning

知らせる is the verb "inform"; 知らせ is the noun. Do not simply put を after a recipient: 友達に知らせる means "tell a friend," while 友達からの知らせ means "news from a friend." 知らせ can be good or bad; a positive feeling is not built into the noun.
