# やっぱり (やっぱり) - as expected; after all

## Meaning

やっぱり often means "as I thought" or "sure enough": a result matches an earlier expectation. It can also introduce a final choice or a return to an earlier idea.

- やっぱり、この店はおいしいですね。 (やっぱり、このみせはおいしいですね。) - This restaurant's food is good, just as expected.
- お茶にします。いえ、やっぱりコーヒーにします。 (おちゃにします。いえ、やっぱりコーヒーにします。) - I'll have tea. No, I'll have coffee after all.

It is an adverb, so it does not take な or conjugate.

## How the kanji combine

The ordinary spelling is kana, with small っ. やっぱり is the more conversational counterpart of やはり. The rare spelling 矢っ張り is not needed for ordinary use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

やはり has closely related meanings and suits less conversational prose. 予想どおり (よそうどおり), "as predicted," overlaps with the expectation-confirming use, not every changed decision.

## Distinctive meaning

It is not an unrestricted synonym of "absolutely." Its emphasis usually relates to an expectation, reconsideration, or what remains true after considering alternatives. Read that relationship from the conversation rather than translating only the intensity.
