# 価格 (かかく) - price; cost

## Meaning

価格 is a noun for the price or monetary value of something. Useful patterns are **商品の価格** (しょうひんのかかく), "the product's price," and **価格が高い／安い**, "the price is high/low."

- この商品の価格を調べました。 (このしょうひんのかかくをしらべました。) - I checked the price of this product.
- 原料の価格が上がっています。 (げんりょうのかかくがあがっています。) - The price of raw materials is rising.

For a change someone causes, use 価格を上げる (かかくをあげる), "raise the price"; が上がる describes the increase itself.

## How the kanji combine

価 contributes value or price, and [格](../kanji/格.md) contributes standard or rank. The compound names an assessed monetary amount. カ + カク gives かかく; the character association is a learning aid, not a calculation of a product's worth.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[値段](値段.md) (ねだん) also means "price" and works well in everyday shopping. [価値](価値.md) (かち), "value; worth," can concern importance or usefulness beyond money. These are not interchangeable in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

価格 suits product descriptions, price comparisons, and economic discussion without being restricted to specialist language. A high 価格 says what something costs, not necessarily that it is useful or good. In English, "cost" may instead mean an expense incurred; check what amount the Japanese sentence is naming.
