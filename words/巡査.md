# 巡査 (じゅんさ) - police officer; constable

## Meaning

巡査 is a police officer; in the Japanese rank system it names the lowest police rank. The broader use for an officer is also recorded, so not every occurrence is a precise statement of rank.

- 巡査に道を聞きました。 (じゅんさにみちをききました。) - I asked a police officer for directions.
- その巡査は交番に勤務しています。 (そのじゅんさはこうばんにきんむしています。) - That constable works at a neighborhood police box.

The noun names a person or rank, not the action of patrolling.

## How the kanji combine

巡 has going-around or patrolling associations, and [査](../kanji/査.md) examining or inspecting. Together they provide an association with police work. They do not imply that the officer must currently be walking a patrol.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[警官](警官.md) is a general police-officer word. [おまわりさん](おまわりさん.md) is a familiar way of referring to an officer. [警察](警察.md) usually concerns the police organization but can also refer to an officer in context. These overlaps do not make the terms identical in formality or rank.

## Distinctive meaning

Use context to distinguish the rank from the wider person reference. 巡査 does not name a police station or the entire police organization, and it is not a synonym for every specialist role such as a detective.
