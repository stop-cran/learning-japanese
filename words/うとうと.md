# うとうと — dozing; nodding off

## Meaning

うとうと describes slipping into a light doze or nodding off rather than sleeping deeply.

- 電車でうとうとして、降りる駅を過ぎてしまった。 — I dozed off on the train and went past my stop.
- 昼ご飯のあと、ソファでうとうと眠っていた。 — After lunch, I was dozing lightly on the sofa.

Use うとうとする or うとうとしている. It can also modify a sleeping verb, directly or with と. うとうとしていた describes actual dozing, not only feeling sleepy.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぐっすり](ぐっすり.md) emphasizes deep, sound sleep. [すやすや](すやすや.md) describes peaceful sleep. [ぼんやり](ぼんやり.md) can describe inattention while someone is still awake.

## Distinctive meaning

眠い (ねむい), "sleepy," names the desire or tendency to sleep; うとうと suggests already drifting in and out of light sleep. It is not a general term for every nap: a nap may become deep sleep instead.
