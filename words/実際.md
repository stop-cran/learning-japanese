# 実際 (じっさい) — actually; in reality

## Meaning

実際 (じっさい) brings attention to what is really the case, rather than only imagined, reported, or expected.
As an adverb it means "actually," "really," or "indeed." It can also name reality or actual practice.
Two useful patterns are 実際に + verb, "actually do ...," and 実際の + noun, "the actual ...".

- 実際に使ってみました。 (じっさいにつかってみました。) — I actually tried using it.
- 実際、この方法は便利です。 (じっさい、このほうほうはべんりです。) — In fact, this method is useful.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality or substance; [際](../kanji/際.md) has an occasion or circumstance sense here.
Together they point to actual circumstances. This is a learning explanation of the compound, not a historical derivation.
The on readings ジツ + サイ combine as じっさい, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

本当に (ほんとうに), "really; truly," can emphasize truth or degree.
実際 is especially useful when contrasting a claim or expectation with what happens in practice.
実は (じつは, pronounced じつわ), "to tell the truth; actually," can introduce information the listener did not know.
These overlap, but choosing 実は to disclose a fact is not the same conversational move as describing an actual trial with 実際に.

## Distinctive meaning

実際 does not itself say whether the facts are good or bad.
In 実際に使う (じっさいにつかう), "actually use," the point is direct use rather than merely talking about it.
Avoid treating the English filler "actually" as a cue to insert 実際 into every correction or change of topic.
