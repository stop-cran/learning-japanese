# 後者 (こうしゃ) - the latter

## Meaning

後者 means "the latter": the second of two items just mentioned or contrasted.
It can refer to people, objects, plans, or ideas. As a noun, it takes ordinary particles
such as は, が, and を.

- 電車とバスなら、後者のほうが安いです。 (でんしゃとバスなら、こうしゃのほうがやすいです。) - Between the train and the bus, the latter is cheaper.
- 二つの案のうち、後者を選びました。 (ふたつのあんのうち、こうしゃをえらびました。) - Of the two proposals, I chose the latter.

In the first example, 後者 refers to the bus because it was named second. It does not mean
whichever option is newer, better, or physically farther behind.

## How the kanji combine

[後](../kanji/後.md) contributes "after; later," and [者](../kanji/者.md) contributes "one."
The compound identifies the later-mentioned one. Here 者 does not restrict the reference to a person.
The on readings are コウ + シャ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[前者](前者.md) (ぜんしゃ), "the former," is the paired contrast: the first of the two.
二つ目 (ふたつめ), "the second one," can identify position more generally, while 後者
depends on the preceding comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

This word helps avoid repeating a long name in explanations and comparisons.
It is useful only when the reader can recover which item came second.
If several possible pairs have been introduced, repeating the actual name can be clearer than
using 後者 and leaving the reference ambiguous.
