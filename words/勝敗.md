# 勝敗 (しょうはい) - victory or defeat; outcome of a contest

## Meaning

勝敗 is victory or defeat: the outcome of a game, battle, or other contest. It concerns which side wins or loses, not an assertion that one particular side won.

- 最後の一点で勝敗が決まりました。 (さいごのいってんでしょうはいがきまりました。) - The final point decided the outcome.
- 勝敗よりも、全力を出すことが大切です。 (しょうはいよりも、ぜんりょくをだすことがたいせつです。) - Giving your all matters more than winning or losing.

勝敗が決まる (しょうはいがきまる) describes the result being decided. 勝敗を決める (しょうはいをきめる) describes deciding it.

## How the kanji combine

[勝](../kanji/勝.md) contributes victory, and [敗](../kanji/敗.md) defeat. The opposite pair names the two possible sides of a competitive result. Read ショウ + ハイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勝負](勝負.md) (しょうぶ) overlaps in victory-or-defeat uses and can additionally name the contest itself. [結果](結果.md) (けっか), "result; outcome," is broader and can concern an exam or investigation. [勝ち](勝ち.md) (かち) and [負け](負け.md) (まけ) identify a win and a loss separately.

## Distinctive meaning

勝敗 is a noun; do not add する to mean "compete." When saying 私の勝ちです, "I win," the noun is 勝ち, not 勝敗. The result of a contest may be unsettled or a draw, so mentioning its 勝敗 does not prove that a winner has already been decided.
