# 馬 (うま) — horse

## Meaning

馬 (うま) means a horse. The noun does not itself specify whether the animal is young,
used for riding, or pulling something.

- 馬が走っています。 (うまがはしっています。) — A horse is running.
- 馬に乗りました。 (うまにのりました。) — I rode a horse.

In the second sentence, に marks what is ridden.
The same noun can refer to one horse or more than one when the surrounding context makes the number clear.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [馬](../kanji/馬.md) writes the native noun うま.
In 乗馬 (じょうば), "horse riding," and 馬車 (ばしゃ), "horse-drawn carriage,"
it instead has the on reading バ. These compound readings do not replace うま as the reading of the standalone animal name.

## Synonyms and antonyms

子馬 (こうま) can specify a foal; the dictionary also records a small horse or pony sense.
[牛](../kanji/牛.md) (うし) is cattle, a different animal rather than a synonym or opposite of 馬.
乗馬 can name the activity of horse riding or a riding horse, depending on the sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

馬 names the animal, whereas 馬車 names a vehicle drawn by one.
The shared character helps identify the relationship, but a horse and a carriage are not interchangeable objects.
Likewise, the activity 乗馬 needs the whole compound; 馬 alone does not mean "riding."
