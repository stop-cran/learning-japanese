# 笛 (ふえ) - flute; whistle

## Meaning

笛 names a flute or similar wind instrument, and also a whistle used for
signaling. The context tells you whether music or a signal is intended.
The basic action is 笛を吹く (ふえをふく), "play a flute/blow a whistle."

- 子供たちが笛を吹いています。 (こどもたちがふえをふいています。) - The children are playing flutes.
- 審判が笛を吹きました。 (しんぱんがふえをふきました。) - The referee blew the whistle.

笛の音 (ふえのおと) is the sound of a flute or whistle.

## How the kanji combine

笛 writes the whole native noun ふえ. There is no multi-kanji compound to
divide. The character's presence does not specify one exact instrument:
the dictionary covers several flute- or pipe-like instruments as well as
the signaling whistle.

## Synonyms and antonyms

フルート identifies the flute more specifically; ホイッスル names a whistle.
笛 is broader. [吹く](吹く.md) is the verb used for blowing it, whereas a
stringed instrument may require a different playing verb.

## Distinctive meaning

Translate the whole situation. A referee's 笛 is not normally a musical
performance, and an English "pipe" in this sense is an instrument, not
plumbing. The object itself and its use determine the translation. There
is no useful general antonym: silence is an absence of sound, not an
opposite kind of 笛.
