# すれ違う (すれちがう) - pass each other; fail to meet

## Meaning

すれ違う means to pass each other, often moving in opposite directions.
It can also mean to miss meeting each other because the timing or movements
do not coincide, or for views and arguments to fail to connect.

- 廊下で先生とすれ違いました。 (ろうかでせんせいとすれちがいました。) - I passed my teacher in the corridor.
- 今日は時間が合わず、彼とすれ違ってしまいました。 (きょうはじかんがあわず、かれとすれちがってしまいました。) - Our schedules did not match today, and I missed meeting him.
- 二人の話がすれ違っています。 (ふたりのはなしがすれちがっています。) - The two are talking past each other.

The verb is intransitive and godan: すれ違います, すれ違って.
The third use need not mean an angry quarrel.

## How the kanji combine

The spelling uses [違](../kanji/違.md) with the native ending ちがう.
The dictionary also records 擦れ違う, making the initial すれ visible
with 擦. The kana spelling すれちがう represents the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通り過ぎる](通り過ぎる.md) means to go past; it does not require two
parties passing each other. [衝突](衝突.md) can concern a collision or
clash, but people who すれ違う need not collide physically.
[違う](違う.md) is broader difference, not this whole passing/missed-contact
expression.

## Distinctive meaning

The passing and missed-meeting senses must be distinguished in context:
the first example can involve seeing the teacher, while the second concerns
a meeting that did not happen. Likewise, talking past each other is not
automatically deliberate opposition.
