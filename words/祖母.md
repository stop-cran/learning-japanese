# 祖母 (そぼ) - grandmother

## Meaning

祖母 is grandmother, whether on one's mother's or father's side. It is the usual neutral term for referring to one's own grandmother when speaking to others.

- 祖母は京都に住んでいます。 (そぼはきょうとにすんでいます。) - My grandmother lives in Kyoto.
- 母方の祖母に会いました。 (ははかたのそぼにあいました。) - I met my maternal grandmother.

The second example explicitly identifies the side of the family.

## How the kanji combine

[祖](../kanji/祖.md) contributes an ancestral generation and [母](../kanji/母.md) the female parent: そ + ぼ. Learn the compound reading rather than reading 母 as はは here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

おばあさん is the familiar or respectful alternative, used in address or for someone else's grandmother. 祖父 (そふ), "grandfather," is the corresponding family role, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

そぼ is primarily a reference term, not the usual way a child calls to their grandmother. Do not confuse おばあさん with おばさん, "aunt; middle-aged woman"; the long あ matters.
