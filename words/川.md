# 川 (かわ) - river; stream

## Meaning

川 is a river or stream: flowing water in a channel. It is the ordinary word for a river, regardless of whether English chooses "river" or "stream" for a particular size.

この川は海に流れています。 (このかわはうみにながれています。) - This river flows into the sea.

川の近くに住んでいます。 (かわのちかくにすんでいます。) - I live near a river.

## How the kanji combine

[川](../kanji/川.md) writes the whole native noun かわ. 河 is an alternative kanji spelling, but 川 is a useful everyday starting point.

## Synonyms and antonyms

小川 (おがわ) is a small stream. [海](海.md) (うみ) is the sea; [湖](湖.md) (みずうみ) is a lake. These name different bodies of water rather than opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

As part of a river's name, 川 can be read かわ or がわ. That variation belongs to the particular name; it does not make がわ the normal standalone pronunciation of "river." Learn a river name's reading as a whole.
