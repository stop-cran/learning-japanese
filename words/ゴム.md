# ゴム - rubber; gum material

## Meaning

ゴム names rubber, including natural and synthetic rubber. It can also name plant gum: not every material called ゴム has the same elastic properties.

- この手袋はゴムでできています。 (このてぶくろはゴムでできています。) - These gloves are made of rubber.
- ゴムは伸びます。 (ゴムはのびます。) - Rubber stretches.

The second example concerns elastic rubber, not every plant gum. In stationery contexts, ゴム can abbreviate [消しゴム](消しゴム.md) (けしゴム), an eraser. The dictionary also records a colloquial condom sense; context selects the object.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword comes from Dutch **gom**. 護謨 is a rare ateji spelling, using characters to represent the borrowed word; it is not a transparent compound definition. Katakana ゴム is the practical spelling here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ガム](ガム.md) ordinarily names chewing gum in its food use. Its dictionary family also records a material sense overlapping ゴム, but that does not make ゴム the everyday name for the snack. 消しゴム specifically identifies an eraser rather than rubber in general.

## Distinctive meaning

English "gum" is not a blanket translation rule. ゴム does not ordinarily mean the tissue around the teeth, and the elastic-rubber examples are not about chewing gum. Learn the material or object supplied by the surrounding phrase.
