# 典型 (てんけい) - typical example; model

## Meaning

典型 is a noun for a representative or characteristic example: something that clearly exhibits a type or pattern. Use Nの典型 for "a typical example of N."

- これはよくある失敗の典型です。 (これはよくあるしっぱいのてんけいです。) - This is a typical example of a common mistake.
- その家はこの地方の建築の典型です。 (そのいえはこのちほうのけんちくのてんけいです。) - That house is a representative example of this region's architecture.

The related adjective construction is 典型的な (てんけいてきな), "typical," as in 典型的な例 (てんけいてきなれい), "a typical example." 典型的に means "typically."

## How the kanji combine

典 contributes a standard or model, and 型 contributes a form or pattern. Their related meanings combine to identify a model of a type. The on readings are テン + ケイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[例](例.md) (れい) is an example more generally; an example need not be especially representative. [種類](種類.md) (しゅるい) names a kind or category, whereas 典型 can identify something that embodies that category. An exception, 例外 (れいがい), can provide a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

"Model" does not necessarily mean something to imitate. A 失敗の典型 is a characteristic failure, not an excellent performance. Similarly, the adjective needs 的な in the pattern taught here; do not assume the noun 典型 freely works like an English adjective.
