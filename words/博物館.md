# 博物館 (はくぶつかん) - museum

## Meaning

博物館 is a noun meaning museum: an institution that collects, studies, or displays materials for visitors and learning. Its exhibits may concern history, nature, science, or another field.

- 博物館で昔の道具を見ました。 (はくぶつかんでむかしのどうぐをみました。) - I saw tools from the past at the museum.
- この博物館は月曜日が休みです。 (このはくぶつかんはげつようびがやすみです。) - This museum is closed on Mondays.

Use 博物館に行く for going to a museum and 博物館で学ぶ (はくぶつかんでまなぶ) for learning there. The particle follows the role the place plays in the sentence.

## How the kanji combine

博 contributes breadth or extensive knowledge, [物](../kanji/物.md) contributes things, and [館](../kanji/館.md) identifies an institutional building. 博物 points toward a broad range of things studied; 館 is the head naming the institution. Read ハク + ブツ + カン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[美術館](美術館.md) (びじゅつかん) specifically names an art museum. In ordinary naming, 博物館 and 美術館 help distinguish collection types, though "museum" is a broad English category. [図書館](図書館.md) (としょかん) is a library, not another word for a museum.

## Distinctive meaning

The name alone does not establish whether entry is free, which displays are available, or whether photography is permitted. Look for the institution's notices. A small local collection can be a 博物館 just as a large national institution can.
