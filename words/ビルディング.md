# ビルディング - multistory building; tall building

## Meaning

ビルディング is a multistory or tall building, such as an office or
commercial building. [ビル](ビル.md) is its shortened form.

- 駅の前に高いビルディングがあります。 (えきのまえにたかいビルディングがあります。) - There is a tall building in front of the station.
- あのビルディングの三階に事務所があります。 (あのビルディングのさんがいにじむしょがあります。) - There is an office on the third floor of that building.

The second example locates the office within a building; it does not
describe the act of constructing one.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "building,"
with no written kanji. ビルヂング is another dictionary-recorded
spelling. Keep ディ in this article's primary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[建物](建物.md) (たてもの) is the broader physical-building word.
[家屋](家屋.md) (かおく) also concerns a house or building as a
structure. ビルディング is not the default word for every shed,
small detached house, or other built structure.

## Distinctive meaning

The Japanese loanword does not inherit every English use of "building":
it is not the activity of building something.
Its meaning also does not specify who owns it or guarantee a particular
construction material. The full form and abbreviation ビル do not
name different kinds of building merely because their lengths differ.
