# 幼児 (ようじ) - young child; toddler

## Meaning

幼児 is a young child or toddler, especially a child before primary
school age. Ordinary usage does not impose one universal exact
age range in every country and context.

- この絵本は幼児向けです。 (このえほんはようじむけです。) - This picture book is intended for young children.
- 幼児を連れて旅行しました。 (ようじをつれてりょこうしました。) - I traveled with a young child.

In a Japanese legal classification, 幼児 means a child aged one
or older who has not yet begun primary school.
That is a specified legal use, not the only possible everyday scope.

## How the kanji combine

[幼](../kanji/幼.md), very young, qualifies [児](../kanji/児.md),
child. The on readings are ヨウ + ジ.
The same written 幼児 can occur under the separate word おさなご;
this card's sourced reading is ようじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[子供](子供.md) (こども), [子](子.md) (こ), and
[児童](児童.md) (じどう) are broader child terms with their own
ordinary and institutional uses.
[赤ん坊](赤ん坊.md) (あかんぼう) and [あかちゃん](あかちゃん.md)
focus on babies. [大人](大人.md) (おとな), an adult, is a life-stage
contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The legal one-year boundary should not become an unsupported claim
that every English "infant" or "baby" is always excluded in ordinary
translation. Conversely, 幼児 is not every person under eighteen.
The homophone [用事](用事.md) (ようじ), an errand or something to do,
is unrelated in meaning.
