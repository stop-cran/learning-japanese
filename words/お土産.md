# お土産 (おみやげ) — souvenir gift

## Meaning

お土産 is a souvenir or local specialty bought while traveling, especially as a gift for someone else. It can also mean a present brought when visiting someone. Food such as regional sweets can be お土産; it need not be a lasting keepsake.

- 家族にお土産を買いました。  
  かぞくにおみやげをかいました。  
  "I bought souvenir gifts for my family."

## How the kanji combine

[土](../kanji/土.md) suggests a locality and [産](../kanji/産.md) its products. 土産 has the whole-word reading みやげ; do not divide it into regular readings of the two characters. お is a polite prefix, not part of either kanji's reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

プレゼント is a general present. 記念品 (きねんひん), "commemorative item", emphasizes remembering an occasion. Neither automatically carries お土産's travel-or-visit setting, and there is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

お土産です (おみやげです), "This is a little gift from my trip", is a natural way to present it. The word describes the gift's social setting, not a requirement that it be expensive.
