# 通る (とおる) - pass through; go along

## Meaning

通る means to pass through, go along, or go past somewhere. It is a godan verb: 通ります, 通って, 通った and 通らない.

- 毎朝この道を通ります。 (まいあさこのみちをとおります。) - I take this road every morning.
- 電車がトンネルを通りました。 (でんしゃがトンネルをとおりました。) - The train passed through a tunnel.

The place takes を because it is the route traversed. This does not make it an object being acted upon; the dictionary classifies this use as intransitive.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) represents passage and is read とお; る is okurigana. Keep the long vowel in とおる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

通す (とおす) is transitive: to let or put something through. 渡る (わたる) emphasizes crossing from one side to another.

## Distinctive meaning

通る also has non-spatial senses, such as passing a test. Those do not erase the basic route pattern. Distinguish it from 通う (かよう), regular travel to a place.
