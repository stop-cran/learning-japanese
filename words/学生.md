# 学生 (がくせい) — student

## Meaning

学生 is a student, especially at a university or college: 大学生 だいがくせい (university student), 学生証 がくせいしょう (student ID), 学生割引 がくせいわりびき (student discount).
In ordinary use it is a student of higher education; younger pupils are 小学生 しょうがくせい, 中学生 ちゅうがくせい, 高校生 こうこうせい.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) (study) + [生](../kanji/生.md) (セイ, here "person (who does something)", a common suffix as in 先生 and 高校生): "person who studies". Both are on readings: ガク and セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 生徒 せいと (pupil in junior or senior high school), 児童 じどう (primary-school pupil), 学徒 がくと (literary).
- Related: [先生](先生.md) せんせい (teacher).

## Distinctive meaning

In daily conversation 学生 mostly points to a university student: 「私は学生です」 is typical for a college student. For a school pupil one says 生徒 or the school-level word (中学生).
