# ナイロン (ナイロン) - nylon

## Meaning

ナイロン means nylon, a synthetic polyamide material used in
fibers and other products. It is a noun. ナイロン製
(ナイロンせい) means "made of nylon."

- このかばんはナイロン製です。 (このかばんはナイロンせいです。) - This bag is made of nylon.
- 釣りにはナイロンの糸を使っています。 (つりにはナイロンのいとをつかっています。) - I use nylon line for fishing.

The first example describes the material, not the bag's color
or a brand. The second uses 糸 for a line rather than necessarily
sewing thread.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[綿](綿.md) (めん) is cotton; [絹](絹.md) (きぬ) is silk.
ポリエステル is polyester, a different synthetic material.
Sharing a use in textiles does not make these interchangeable
names. [生地](生地.md) (きじ) can mean fabric, the product
rather than one specific fiber material.

## Distinctive meaning

ナイロン is not a general translation for every plastic or
synthetic fiber. Nor is it limited to finished clothing.
Material labels identify a material; the word alone does not
establish whether a particular object is waterproof, heatproof,
or suitable for a particular use.
