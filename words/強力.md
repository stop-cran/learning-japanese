# 強力 (きょうりょく) - powerful; strong

## Meaning

強力 describes substantial power, force, or effectiveness. It is a な-adjective:
強力な + noun, or 強力です at the end of a polite statement. 強力に can modify
an action.

- この機械には強力なモーターが付いています。 (このきかいにはきょうりょくなモーターがついています。) - This machine has a powerful motor.
- 私たちはその計画を強力に支援します。 (わたしたちはそのけいかくをきょうりょくにしえんします。) - We will give that plan strong support.

The support example concerns the strength of an action, not physical muscle.

## How the kanji combine

[強](../kanji/強.md) means strong, and [力](../kanji/力.md) means power.
The first character qualifies the second: strong power or force.
The on readings here are キョウ + リョク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[強い](強い.md) (つよい) is a broad i-adjective for "strong."
[弱い](弱い.md) (よわい), "weak," supplies a contrast, but its grammar differs:
弱い力, not 弱いな力.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep きょうりょく separate from ごうりき. A different JMdict entry lists 強力
(ごうりき) for herculean strength and a mountain carrier-guide. Those are not
the meanings taught for きょうりょく here. The community source mixes that
English gloss with きょうりょく; this card follows the verified Japanese reading
without claiming to recover the compiler's intention or assign ごうりき a level.
