# 協力 (きょうりょく) - cooperation; collaboration

## Meaning

協力 is cooperation, collaboration, or help toward a shared aim. It is a noun and an intransitive する-verb. Use 人と協力する for working together with someone, and 活動に協力する for helping an activity.

- 友達と協力して、会場を片づけました。 (ともだちときょうりょくして、かいじょうをかたづけました。) - I worked with my friends to tidy up the venue.
- アンケートにご協力ください。 (アンケートにごきょうりょくください。) - Please help by taking the survey.

In the request, ご adds politeness to the noun. The survey takes に, not the direct-object pattern アンケートを協力する.

## How the kanji combine

協 contributes joining together, and [力](../kanji/力.md) contributes strength or effort. Together they suggest combining efforts. The on readings are キョウ + リョク; keep the small ょ in both syllable groups.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[手伝う](手伝う.md) (てつだう) means helping someone with a task and takes different constructions, such as 仕事を手伝う. [共同](共同.md) (きょうどう) concerns doing or holding something jointly. Both can overlap with 協力.

## Distinctive meaning

Cooperation need not mean everyone does the same work or has equal responsibility. One person can supply information while another organizes the event. 協力をお願いする (きょうりょくをおねがいする) asks for cooperation: を belongs with お願いする here, not with 協力する.
