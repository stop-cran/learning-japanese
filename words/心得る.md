# 心得る (こころえる) - know; understand; have a command of

## Meaning

心得る means to know or understand something, or to have practical
knowledge of a skill. It is a transitive ichidan verb:
心得ます, 心得た, 心得て, 心得ない.
What is understood or known takes を, or は when made the topic.

- 仕事の手順はよく心得ています。
  (しごとのてじゅんはよくこころえています。) - I know the work procedure well.
- 彼女は応急手当を心得ています。
  (かのじょはおうきゅうてあてをこころえています。) - She knows how to give first aid.

It can also express understanding and accepting a responsibility.
心得ました can acknowledge an instruction with a firm "Understood."
That can sound formal or emphatic rather than like casual daily chat.

## How the kanji combine

[心](../kanji/心.md), read こころ, combines with [得](../kanji/得.md)る,
える, associated with acquiring or grasping.
The native verb suggests taking something in mentally.
It is not an on-reading compound pronounced しんとく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知る](知る.md) (しる) broadly concerns knowing or finding out.
[理解](理解.md)する (りかいする) and [分かる](分かる.md) (わかる)
overlap in understanding. [承知](承知.md)する (しょうちする) can
express awareness or acknowledgment.
[納得](納得.md)する (なっとくする) can add being convinced or satisfied;
knowing a procedure does not necessarily express that judgment.
[詳しい](詳しい.md) (くわしい) describes being knowledgeable about
a subject; [マスター](マスター.md)する emphasizes mastering a skill.
Knowing how to do something need not imply that degree of mastery.

## Distinctive meaning

The skill example concerns knowing how to do something, not merely
having heard its name. Nevertheless, 心得る does not automatically
certify expert mastery: 少し心得ている (すこしこころえている)
can mean having some knowledge.
Do not read 得る as うる inside this word.
