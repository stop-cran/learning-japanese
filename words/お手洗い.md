# お手洗い (おてあらい) -- restroom

## Meaning

お手洗い is a courteous everyday word for a toilet or restroom. It is useful when asking where the facilities are.

すみません、お手洗いはどこですか。 (すみません、おてあらいはどこですか。) -- Excuse me, where is the restroom?

For going there, use お手洗いに行く (おてあらいにいく). The destination takes に.

## How the kanji combine

お is a polite prefix. [手](../kanji/手.md), read て, means hand; [洗](../kanji/洗.md) supplies あら in 洗い, washing. The underlying image is handwashing, but the whole expression has the established meaning "restroom."

## Synonyms and antonyms

トイレ is another ordinary word for the toilet or restroom. 浴室 (よくしつ) is a bathing room; it is not a substitute when asking where the toilet is.

## Distinctive meaning

English "bathroom" can name different rooms in different countries. お手洗い directs a listener toward toilet facilities, not a place to take a bath. Nor does the polite お mean that the facility belongs to someone socially superior.
