# 公演 (こうえん) — public performance

## Meaning

公演 is a public performance, such as a theatrical or musical presentation.
It can name the event, and 公演する describes giving such a performance.

- 公演は七時に始まります。 (こうえんはしちじにはじまります。) — The performance starts at seven.
- 劇団は来月、東京で公演します。 (げきだんはらいげつ、とうきょうでこうえんします。) — The theater company will perform in Tokyo next month.

In the second example, で identifies the place of the performance.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md), "public," qualifies [演](../kanji/演.md), "perform."
The relation is a public presentation of a performance, not a reference to government ownership.
The on readings are コウ + エン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上演 (じょうえん) focuses on staging a work such as a play or opera.
演奏 (えんそう) focuses on musical performance.
A single event may involve these activities and also be a 公演, but the words highlight different aspects;
演奏 is not a general replacement for performing a spoken play.

## Distinctive meaning

公演 directs attention to a performance presented publicly, rather than simply practicing a role or instrument.
"Public" does not tell you whether tickets cost money.
Keep the written characters in mind: hearing こうえん alone is not enough to identify which word is intended.
