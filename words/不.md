# 不 (ふ) - un-; non-

## Meaning

不 is a negative prefix, not a standalone replacement for "no."
The reading **ふ** occurs in [不安](不安.md) (ふあん), "anxiety," and
[不利](不利.md) (ふり), "disadvantageous."
The reading **ぶ** occurs in words such as 不器用 (ぶきよう),
"clumsy; unskillful." Learn the reading with each established word.

- この場所は交通が不便です。 (このばしょはこうつうがふべんです。) - This place is inconvenient for transportation.
- 私は手先が不器用です。 (わたしはてさきがぶきようです。) - I am clumsy with my hands.

The second example shows the ぶ reading in an established word,
not a free choice to pronounce every 不 as ぶ.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) contributes negation to the element that follows it.
In [不便](不便.md), it negates convenience. The dictionary records ふ and
ぶ as separate prefix entries; the ぶ entry also includes the spelling 無.
That does not make 不 and 無 interchangeable in every compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ない expresses grammatical negation in many constructions.
不 instead helps form lexical words: 便利ではない (べんりではない),
"not convenient," and 不便, "inconvenient," use different structures.
Other negative prefixes have their own established combinations.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not add 不 mechanically to any noun or adjective to invent an opposite.
Its contribution ranges from absence or negation to an unfavorable quality.
The complete word determines its reading, grammar, and practical meaning;
recognizing the prefix helps with vocabulary but does not replace learning
those whole words.
