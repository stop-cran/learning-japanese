# 流行 (りゅうこう) - fashion; prevalence

## Meaning

流行 is a fashion, trend, or widespread popularity. It also describes the prevalence or spread of a disease. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb: the thing becoming widespread takes が.

- 若い人の間で、この曲が流行しています。 (わかいひとのあいだで、このきょくがりゅうこうしています。) - This song is popular among young people.
- インフルエンザが流行しています。 - Influenza is spreading.

Before a noun, use 流行の or 流行している: 流行の服 (りゅうこうのふく), "fashionable clothes." Do not infer a な-adjective solely from the English gloss "fashionable."

## How the kanji combine

[流](../kanji/流.md) contributes flowing or spreading, and [行](../kanji/行.md) contributes going or circulating. Together they suggest something circulating among people. This explains the shared idea behind fashion and disease prevalence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

はやる is a close verb for being fashionable or prevalent. [傾向](傾向.md) (けいこう) names a tendency or trend, including patterns that are not matters of popularity. 流行遅れ (りゅうこうおくれ) means "out of fashion."

## Distinctive meaning

流行 is not necessarily praise: a disease's 流行 is not popularity in the sense of people liking it. Even for clothing or music, the word says something is circulating or in vogue; it does not guarantee quality or describe how long the trend will last.
