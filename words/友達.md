# 友達 (ともだち) — friend

## Meaning

友達 is a friend: 友達と遊ぶ ともだちとあそぶ (play with friends), 友達になる (become friends), 親友 しんゆう (close friend).
It can mean one friend or several friends.

## How the kanji combine

[友](../kanji/友.md) (friend, kun とも) plus 達, read だち here: the suffix たち, voiced to だち by rendaku after とも.
達 as たち marks a group of people (私たち, 子供たち), and 友達 originated as "friend + group", but it is now an ordinary word for a single friend too.
It is a regular spelling, not a jukujikun. The on reading of 達 is タツ (発達 はったつ), which is not used in 友達.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 友人 ゆうじん (friend, more formal), 仲間 なかま (companion, group member), 親友 しんゆう (close friend), 友 とも (literary).

## Distinctive meaning

友達 is warm and casual, for people of any age. 友人 sounds written or formal and suits an introduction (友人の田中さん, "my friend Mr Tanaka").
