# 官庁 (かんちょう) - government offices; authorities

## Meaning

官庁 is a government office or agency, or government authorities
considered collectively. It is a noun.

- 関係する官庁に問い合わせました。
  (かんけいするかんちょうにといあわせました。) - I contacted the relevant government office.
- 官庁で働く友人がいます。
  (かんちょうではたらくゆうじんがいます。) - I have a friend who works at a government agency.

官庁に identifies the recipient of the inquiry; 官庁で identifies
where the friend works. The plural English gloss does not require
the Japanese noun to refer to several offices in every sentence.

## How the kanji combine

[官](../kanji/官.md) contributes governmental or official administration,
and [庁](../kanji/庁.md) an administrative office or agency.
The first component identifies the official kind of office.
The on readings are カン + チョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

役所 (やくしょ) also names a government office.
[庁](庁.md) (ちょう) and [局](局.md) (きょく) appear in administrative
names, but are not interchangeable ranks in every organization.
[事務所](事務所.md) (じむしょ) and [オフィス](オフィス.md) are broader
office words and do not themselves specify a government agency.

## Distinctive meaning

[政府](政府.md) (せいふ) refers to the government or administration.
官庁 can identify a particular administrative office or the relevant
authorities, not necessarily the government as a whole.
It does not mean any private company simply because that company
has offices.
