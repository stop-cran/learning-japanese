# 覗く (のぞく) - peek; show partly

## Meaning

覗く is a godan verb for peeking through an opening, looking into something, or taking a brief look. It can also describe something showing partly, without anyone deliberately looking.

- 穴から部屋の中を覗きました。 (あなからへやのなかをのぞきました。) - I peeked into the room through a hole.
- 帰りに本屋を覗きました。 (かえりにほんやをのぞきました。) - I took a quick look in a bookstore on the way home.
- ポケットから切符が覗いています。 (ポケットからきっぷがのぞいています。) - A ticket is sticking out of the pocket.

The looking uses are transitive: を marks what is looked into or at. In the third example the ticket itself is partly visible, so 切符 takes が. Forms include 覗きます, 覗いた, and 覗かない.

## How the kanji combine

覗 represents のぞ in this native verb; く is okurigana. Kana のぞく is also useful to recognize. The spelling alone does not tell you whether someone is peeking or an object is showing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[見る](見る.md) (みる) is broader "see; look." 覗く adds an opening, a look into somewhere, or a brief/partial view. [見える](見える.md) (みえる) overlaps with the visibility use but does not itself require only a small part to show.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not make "secretly" a requirement: looking through a microscope or briefly looking into a shop need not be furtive. Nor is every 覗く transitive. Keep it separate from the homophone [除く](除く.md) (のぞく), "remove; exclude"; のぞいた alone needs context to identify the word.
