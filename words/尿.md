# 尿 (にょう) — urine

## Meaning

尿 is urine, the liquid itself. You may see it in instructions about providing a sample.

- 検査のため、尿を容器に入れた。 (けんさのため、にょうをようきにいれた。) — I put urine in a container for the test.
- 医師は尿の色を確認した。 (いしはにょうのいろをかくにんした。) — The doctor checked the color of the urine.

## How the kanji combine

[尿](../kanji/尿.md) is a one-character noun with the on reading にょう. In 尿検査 (にょうけんさ), 尿 tells you what is being tested.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[小便](小便.md) (しょうべん) can name the liquid or the act of urinating. 排尿 (はいにょう) specifically names passing urine.

## Distinctive meaning

尿の色 (にょうのいろ) is the color of the liquid. The first example likewise uses 尿 as the thing put in the container, not as an action.
