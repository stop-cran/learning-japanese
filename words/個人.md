# 個人 (こじん) - individual; private person

## Meaning

個人 is a noun for an individual person, considered separately from a group or organization. Use 個人の to modify a noun: 個人の意見 (こじんのいけん), "an individual's opinion." 個人的な (こじんてきな) is a related form meaning personal.

- これは会社の意見ではなく、私個人の意見です。 (これはかいしゃのいけんではなく、わたしこじんのいけんです。) - This is my personal opinion, not the company's position.
- この大会には個人でも参加できます。 (このたいかいにはこじんでもさんかできます。) - You can also enter this tournament as an individual.

The first example distinguishes whose position is represented, rather than whether the opinion is secret.

## How the kanji combine

個 contributes an individual unit, and [人](../kanji/人.md) contributes person. Together they identify a person as an individual. The reading is こじん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[人](人.md) (ひと) is the broad word "person." 個人 explicitly highlights the individual rather than a collective. [会社](会社.md) (かいしゃ), "company," is a contextual contrast, not an opposite for every use.

## Distinctive meaning

English "private" can mean confidential, but 個人 does not inherently say that information must be kept secret. 個人情報 (こじんじょうほう) is personal information because it concerns an individual. In legal contexts, 個人 can distinguish a natural person from an incorporated entity; the ordinary examples simply contrast a person with a group.
