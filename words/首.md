# 首 (くび) — neck

## Meaning

首 (くび) is the neck, the body part joining the head to the torso.
It can be the subject of a description or the location where something is worn.

- 首が痛いです。 (くびがいたいです。) — My neck hurts.
- 首にマフラーを巻きました。 (くびにマフラーをまきました。) — I wrapped a scarf around my neck.

The second example uses に for the place around which the scarf is wrapped.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [首](../kanji/首.md) writes the native noun くび.
It is not read シュ in this standalone body-part word.
The on reading シュ appears in 首都 (しゅと), "national capital," a different compound with its own meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頭](頭.md) (あたま) is the head, not the neck.
手首 (てくび) is the wrist, and 足首 (あしくび) is the ankle.
These related compounds name other narrow connecting parts, but neither one can replace 首 when you mean the neck.
There is no useful antonym for the body-part sense.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the plain noun's physical neck sense separate from the meanings of larger words.
The sentence 首が痛い locates the pain at the neck; substituting 頭が痛い (あたまがいたい)
changes it to "my head hurts." That is a change in the body part, not just a different way to phrase the same complaint.
