# 実績 (じっせき) - actual results; track record

## Meaning

実績 means actual results or achievements: what someone or something has
accomplished in practice. It can describe a track record in work, business,
or another activity. Nの実績 identifies whose results or which activity is meant.

- この会社には海外での販売実績があります。 (このかいしゃにはかいがいでのはんばいじっせきがあります。) - This company has a sales track record overseas.
- 今月の実績を前年と比べました。 (こんげつのじっせきをぜんねんとくらべました。) - We compared this month's actual results with the previous year's.

実績を上げる (じっせきをあげる) means to achieve results;
実績を残す (じっせきをのこす) means to leave a record of achievement.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actual or real, and [績](../kanji/績.md)
contributes results or achievements.
The first qualifies the results as actually produced.
ジツ + セキ becomes じっせき, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[成績](成績.md) (せいせき) concerns assessed performance or grades.
[功績](功績.md) (こうせき) highlights meritorious achievement.
[結果](結果.md) (けっか) is an outcome more generally.
実績 focuses on the results an activity or record of work has actually produced.

## Distinctive meaning

実績 can support a favorable evaluation, but it does not inherently promise
uniformly excellent results. A comparison can show that the actual figures
fell short of a target.
Likewise, 実績がない says there is no established record in the relevant
area; it does not prove that future success is impossible.
