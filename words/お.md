# お - honorific or polite prefix

## Meaning

お- is a prefix attached to a word, not a standalone adjective meaning "honorable." It can express respect, make wording polite, or participate in a humble expression. Its role depends on the whole construction.

- お名前を教えてください。 (おなまえをおしえてください。) - Please tell me your name.
- お茶を飲みます。 (おちゃをのみます。) - I will drink tea.
- 荷物をお持ちします。 (にもつをおもちします。) - I will carry your luggage.

The first example respectfully refers to the listener's name. [お茶](お茶.md) is an established polite word, not "honorable tea." In the third, **お + verb's ます-stem + する** humbly presents the speaker's action for someone else; 持ち comes from 持ちます.

## How the kanji combine

This kana prefix can also be written [御](../kanji/御.md). JMdict marks the kana spelling as usual. There is no kanji decomposition for the written word お.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ご- is a related prefix, as in ご家族 (ごかぞく), "your family." お- tends to accompany native readings and ご- Sino-Japanese readings, but established words include exceptions such as お茶.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the complete expression rather than adding お to every noun or verb. お名前 refers respectfully to someone else's name; an established word like お茶 can refer to your own tea. Neither spelling nor prefix alone determines who is being honored.
