# 寸法 (すんぽう) - dimensions; measurements

## Meaning

寸法 is the dimensions, size, or measurements of something, such as its
length, width, and height. 寸法を測る (すんぽうをはかる) means to measure
its dimensions.

- 棚の寸法を測りました。 (たなのすんぽうをはかりました。) - I measured the dimensions of the shelves.
- この箱は寸法が合いません。 (このはこはすんぽうがあいません。) - This box is not the right size.

The word also has an extended plan or arrangement sense:
明日出発する寸法です (あしたしゅっぱつするすんぽうです), "The plan is
to leave tomorrow." This is not a statement about a physical measurement.

## How the kanji combine

寸 contributes a measure or small unit, and [法](../kanji/法.md) a method
or standard. Together they concern measured dimensions; this association
does not by itself explain the historical development of every extended use.
Learn the whole reading すんぽう, with ぽう rather than ほう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大小](大小.md) concerns size or large and small.
[幅](幅.md) identifies width or breadth rather than all dimensions.
[計画](計画.md) and [予定](予定.md) overlap with the separate planning
use, not with the physical size of a box.

## Distinctive meaning

Dimensions need a relevant unit and the appropriate measurement directions.
寸法 alone does not say whether a number is centimeters or inches.
Conversely, the plan/arrangement sense is genuine; do not force a literal
length interpretation onto every expression ending in 寸法だ.
