# 提案 (ていあん) - proposal; suggestion

## Meaning

提案 is a proposal or suggestion presented for consideration. 提案する is transitive: the proposed idea takes を, and its recipient can take に. A plain verb plus ことを提案する expresses a proposed action.

- 会議で新しい方法を提案しました。 (かいぎであたらしいほうほうをていあんしました。) - I proposed a new method at the meeting.
- 先生に、発表を来週にすることを提案しました。 (せんせいに、はっぴょうをらいしゅうにすることをていあんしました。) - I suggested to the teacher that we give the presentation next week.

提案に賛成する (ていあんにさんせいする) means "agree with a proposal." Here に belongs to 賛成する, not to the act of proposing.

## How the kanji combine

提 contributes putting forward or presenting, and 案 contributes a plan or idea. Together they describe putting an idea before others. The reading is テイ + アン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion; it need not recommend an action. [計画](計画.md) (けいかく) is a plan, which may be the content of a proposal.

[命令](命令.md) (めいれい), "order; command," is a useful contrast in force, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

A 提案 remains something to consider. It does not imply acceptance, authorization, or [実行](実行.md) (じっこう), "carrying out." The noun works for a practical everyday suggestion as well as an organizational proposal; its meaning does not require an elaborate written document.
