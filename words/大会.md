# 大会 (たいかい) - tournament; convention

## Meaning

大会 is a noun for an organized gathering, such as a convention or rally, or for a tournament
or competitive meet. A preceding word often identifies the kind of event.
Use 大会に参加する (たいかいにさんかする), "participate in an event," or 大会に出る, "enter a tournament."

- 来月、テニスの大会に出ます。 (らいげつ、テニスのたいかいにでます。) - I will enter a tennis tournament next month.
- 全国から会員が大会に集まりました。 (ぜんこくからかいいんがたいかいにあつまりました。) - Members gathered from across the country for the convention.

The first example is competitive; the second need not involve competing at all.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md), "large," modifies [会](../kanji/会.md), "meeting; gathering."
The compound suggests a substantial organized event. Its reading is タイ + カイ,
not だいかい. The components are a memory aid, not a minimum attendance requirement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[試合](試合.md) (しあい) is a match or game. A 大会 can contain several 試合.
[会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) is a meeting for discussion; 大会 can be a broader convention
with multiple activities. There is no single antonym covering all these event types.

## Distinctive meaning

The event's name and setting determine whether "tournament," "convention," or another event word
fits best in English. Do not infer sports from 大会 alone.
全国大会 (ぜんこくたいかい), "national tournament or convention," likewise needs context
to show whether participants compete, deliberate, or gather for another purpose.
