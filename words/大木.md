# 大木 (たいぼく) — a large tree

## Meaning

大木 is a big, tall or thick-trunked tree: 神社の大木 (the big tree of a shrine), 大木が倒れた (the big tree fell). As a surname, 大木 is usually read おおき.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) (big) modifies [木](../kanji/木.md) (tree), using on readings (ダイ→タイ, ボク): "big tree". The plain native phrase is 大きな木 おおきなき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 巨木 きょぼく (a huge tree, emphasises enormous size), 老木 ろうぼく／おいき (an old tree, emphasises age), 大樹 たいじゅ (a great tree, literary; figurative "great patron" in 寄らば大樹の陰), 古木 こぼく (an old tree).
- Antonym: 若木 わかぎ (young tree), 苗木 なえぎ (sapling).

## Distinctive meaning

大木 is neutral and means a trunk of good size, not necessarily old. 巨木 is more dramatic, 老木 stresses age, and 大樹 is more literary.
