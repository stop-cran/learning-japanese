# 手伝い (てつだい) - help; helper

## Meaning

手伝い is help or assistance, and can also mean a person helping. It is a noun. Use Nの手伝いをする, "help with N," or 手伝いに来る, "come to help."

- 引っ越しの手伝いに来ました。 (ひっこしのてつだいにきました。) - I came to help with the move.
- 祭りの手伝いを二人探しています。 (まつりのてつだいをふたりさがしています。) - We are looking for two helpers for the festival.

The person counter 二人 makes the helper meaning clear in the second sentence.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md) contributes the image of a helping hand, and [伝](../kanji/伝.md) is part of the established spelling of [手伝う](手伝う.md) (てつだう), "help." 手伝い is its noun form. Do not build a literal meaning "transmit a hand" from separate character glosses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

手伝う describes the action directly: 引っ越しを手伝う, "help with a move." [助ける](助ける.md) (たすける) also means help, but can extend to rescuing or saving someone in difficulty.

## Distinctive meaning

手伝い can concern a small contribution to someone else's task, not necessarily a professional assistant's job. お手伝い (おてつだい) adds お and can also refer to helping with chores. Context decides whether the focus is the assistance or the person providing it.
