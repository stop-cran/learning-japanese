# 来週 (らいしゅう) — next week

## Meaning

来週 means **next week**, relative to the week in which someone is speaking.

- 来週、友達に会います。（らいしゅう、ともだちにあいます。）— I will meet a friend next week.
- 来週の月曜日は休みです。（らいしゅうのげつようびはやすみです。）— I have next Monday off.

It can stand directly before a clause as a time expression. You do not normally need に after bare 来週.
Use の to connect it to a noun: 来週の月曜日, "next Monday."

## How the kanji combine

[来](../kanji/来.md) contributes "coming" and [週](../kanji/週.md) means "week":
**coming + week**, with 来 modifying 週.
Both readings are on readings: ライ + シュウ → らいしゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 今週（こんしゅう）means "this week."
- 先週（せんしゅう）means "last week."
- 毎週（まいしゅう）means "every week," expressing repetition rather than the next week alone.

These are useful calendar contrasts, not freely interchangeable synonyms or strict logical antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

来週 names the next calendar week, not a duration of seven days starting now.
一週間（いっしゅうかん）means "one week" as a period of time.
When a precise appointment matters, add the weekday or date rather than relying on 来週 alone.
