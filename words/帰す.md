# 帰す (かえす) - send home; send back

## Meaning

帰す means to send someone home or back. It is a transitive godan verb: the person sent back takes を, and a destination can take に or へ.

- 先生は生徒を早く家に帰しました。 (せんせいはせいとをはやくいえにかえしました。) - The teacher sent the students home early.
- 今日は客を帰す前に、お礼を言いました。 (きょうはきゃくをかえすまえに、おれいをいいました。) - Today I thanked the guests before sending them home.

Learn 帰します (かえします), 帰さない (かえさない) and 帰して (かえして). For this send-home verb, the reading is かえす. The same written form also represents a separate verb, 帰す (きす), meaning "end in" or "attribute to"; it is not a second pronunciation to substitute into these examples.

## How the kanji combine

[帰](../kanji/帰.md) contributes homeward return. It writes かえ here, followed by す; this is one native verb, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[帰る](帰る.md) (かえる) is intransitive: 生徒が帰る (せいとがかえる), "the students go home." Compare 生徒を帰す, "send the students home."

[返す](返す.md), also かえす, is the useful default for returning an object: 本を返す (ほんをかえす), "return a book." 帰らせる (かえらせる), the causative of 帰る, can mean make or let someone go home; the context determines the degree of compulsion or permission.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use 帰す merely because English says "return." It focuses on sending a person back, whereas 帰る describes that person's own return. Sending someone home need not be a punishment. The dictionary also lists 還す at かえす; recognize the spelling without turning it into a new conjugation.
