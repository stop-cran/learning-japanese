# 部分 (ぶぶん) - part; section

## Meaning

部分 is a part, portion, or section of a whole. It can refer to a physical object, a passage of text, or one aspect of a matter. Use whole + の部分, or a describing clause directly before 部分.

- この部分をもう一度読んでください。 (このぶぶんをもういちどよんでください。) - Please read this part once more.
- 壊れた部分を修理しました。 (こわれたぶぶんをしゅうりしました。) - I repaired the broken part.

分からない部分 (わからないぶぶん) means "the part I do not understand." The clause 分からない identifies which portion is meant.

## How the kanji combine

[部](../kanji/部.md) concerns a section or division; [分](../kanji/分.md) contributes a share or divided part. The overlapping meanings reinforce each other. The readings are ブ + ブン, so keep both ぶ sounds in ぶぶん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一部 (いちぶ) can mean some part or a portion. [全体](全体.md) (ぜんたい), "the whole," provides the relevant contrast. [全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ) emphasizes all of something rather than a selected part.

## Distinctive meaning

部分 does not require a detachable component. A confusing paragraph can be a 部分 of an article, even though it is not a spare part. The word also does not specify the size or importance of the portion.
