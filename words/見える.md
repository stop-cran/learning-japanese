# 見える (みえる) - be visible; be in sight

## Meaning

見える means that something is visible or comes into view. The visible thing normally takes が.

- この窓から山が見えます。 (このまどからやまがみえます。) - You can see the mountains from this window.
- 暗くて、字がよく見えません。 (くらくて、じがよくみえません。) - It is dark, so I cannot see the writing clearly.

It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 見えます (みえます), 見えない (みえない), 見えた (みえた).

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) writes み, followed by える. The conjugation stem is 見え, not just the written kanji. Keep え when adding ます or ない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

見る (みる) describes looking at or watching something. 見られる (みられる), the potential of 見る, can concern the ability or opportunity to watch. 見える focuses on visibility from the observer's situation.

## Distinctive meaning

The English "can see" does not mean 見える is simply a conjugated form of 見る. It is its own verb. It also means "look; seem" in patterns such as 若く見える (わかくみえる), "look young."
