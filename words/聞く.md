# 聞く (きく) — to hear, to listen, to ask

## Meaning

聞く has three common senses:

- **Hear or listen**: 音楽を聞く (listen to music), ラジオを聞きます, 話を聞く (listen to a story).
- **Ask**: 先生に聞く (ask the teacher), 道を聞く (ask the way), 聞いてみる (try asking).
- **Obey, heed**: 言うことを聞く (do as told), 聞き分けがいい (obedient).

## How the kanji combine

[聞](../kanji/聞.md) with the kun reading き plus okurigana く. A godan verb with the usual く pattern: 聞きます, 聞かない, 聞いて, 聞いた. 聞こえる きこえる ("can be heard") is a separate intransitive verb: 音が聞こえる.
Spelling variants: 聴く きく (listen attentively, to music or a lecture), 訊く きく (ask, a rare spelling), and 利く／効く きく (be effective), which are only homophones.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 尋ねる たずねる (ask, more formal), 質問する しつもんする (ask a question), 伺う うかがう (humble "ask/hear").
- Related: [話す](話す.md) はなす (talk), [見る](見る.md) みる (see).

## Distinctive meaning

聞く is the all-purpose word, and 聴く stresses active, attentive listening. With 聞こえる, the sound reaches you without effort, so 鳥の声が聞こえる ("I can hear birds") is different from 鳥の声を聞く ("I listen to the birds").
