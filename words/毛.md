# 毛 (け) — hair; fur

## Meaning

毛 means hair, including body hair, and the fur or wool of animals. It can refer to a covering of hair or to individual hairs, depending on context.

- この猫は毛が長いです。  
  このねこはけがながいです。  
  "This cat has long fur."
- コートに犬の毛がついています。  
  コートにいぬのけがついています。  
  "There is dog hair on the coat."

## How the kanji combine

毛 is one kanji, read け as this native noun. Compounds can use the on reading もう, as in 毛布 (もうふ), "blanket". The reading of the standalone word remains け.

## Synonyms and antonyms

髪 (かみ) specifically means hair on the human head. [髭](髭.md) (ひげ) refers to facial hair. 毛 is the broader term, so these are more specific categories rather than opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

For someone's hairstyle or haircut, 髪 is usually the useful choice. 毛 works naturally for animal fur and individual hairs; it does not imply a particular hairstyle.
