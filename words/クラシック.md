# クラシック (クラシック) - classical music; classic

## Meaning

クラシック is a noun for classical music, shortened from
クラシック音楽. It is also a na-adjective meaning "classic"
or "classical," and can name a classic work.

- 母はクラシックをよく聴きます。 (はははクラシックをよくききます。) - My mother often listens to classical music.
- クラシックなデザインの時計を買いました。 (クラシックなデザインのとけいをかいました。) - I bought a watch with a classic design.

The first example uses the noun; the second uses な to connect
the adjective to a noun. A literary discussion can instead use
クラシック for an established classic work, not a piece of music.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "classic"
and "classical." There are no kanji components. Keep the small
ッ before ク: クラシック. The spelling does not require
an extra small ッ after ラ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[音楽](音楽.md) (おんがく) is the broader word "music."
古典 (こてん) refers to classics or classical works in various
fields. Pop music is a genre contrast to classical music, not a
general antonym for every use of クラシック.

## Distinctive meaning

クラシック does not simply mean "old." The design example
describes a style, even if the watch was newly made.
Likewise, the noun in a listening context identifies a kind of music,
not merely any recording released a long time ago.
