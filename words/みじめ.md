# みじめ (みじめ) - miserable; pitiable; wretched

## Meaning

みじめ describes a miserable, wretched or pitiable condition. It can concern circumstances, defeat or how someone feels about their situation.

- 試合に大差で負けて、みじめな気持ちになりました。 (しあいにたいさでまけて、みじめなきもちになりました。) - After losing the match by a wide margin, I felt miserable.
- 彼はみじめな暮らしをしていました。 (かれはみじめなくらしをしていました。) - He was living a wretched life.

It is a na-adjective: みじめな姿 (みじめなすがた), "a pitiable sight," and みじめに負ける (みじめにまける), "lose miserably."

## How the kanji combine

惨め is the recorded kanji spelling, with 惨 supplying the miserable or wretched association. The headword here is written in kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[悲しい](悲しい.md) (かなしい), "sad," overlaps without necessarily suggesting a wretched condition. [かわいそう](かわいそう.md), [哀れ](哀れ.md) (あわれ) and [気の毒](気の毒.md) (きのどく) can express pity or sympathy, but their constructions and tone are not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling someone みじめ can sound harsh or humiliating. Do not treat it as a neutral sympathy formula. It does not mean only financial poverty, and the inspected dictionaries do not restrict it to archaic usage.
