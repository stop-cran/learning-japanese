# 働く (はたらく) — work

## Meaning

働く means to work or labor. The person working is the subject, and the workplace normally takes で.

兄は銀行で働いています。（あにはぎんこうではたらいています。） — My older brother works at a bank.

今日は八時間働きました。（きょうははちじかんはたらきました。） — I worked for eight hours today.

## How the kanji combine

[働](../kanji/働.md) supplies the working meaning. This is a native godan verb: 働きます, 働いて, 働かない. The written く changes with the conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

仕事をする（しごとをする） also means to work or do a job. 休む（やすむ）, "rest; take time off," contrasts with working in a work-schedule context.

## Distinctive meaning

働いています can describe ongoing employment, not only an action happening at this instant. Unlike English "work" as a noun, 働く is a verb; use 仕事 for a job or work as a thing to do.
