# 汚す (よごす) - soil; stain

## Meaning

汚す means to make something dirty or stained. It is a transitive godan
verb: the thing made dirty takes を.

- 子供が服を汚しました。 (こどもがふくをよごしました。) - The child got their clothes dirty.
- 床を汚さないでください。 (ゆかをよごさないでください。) - Please do not dirty the floor.

Forms include 汚します (よごします), 汚して (よごして), and
汚さない (よごさない). The object can be left unstated when it is clear;
that does not change the verb into an intransitive one.

## How the kanji combine

[汚](../kanji/汚.md) supplies よご in this reading, and す completes the
native verb. The masu-stem is 汚し (よごし), as in 汚します (よごします).
The negative instead uses 汚さない (よごさない), not 汚しない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[汚れる](汚れる.md) (よごれる) is intransitive: 服が汚れる
(ふくがよごれる), clothes get dirty. Compare 服を汚す (ふくをよごす),
get clothes dirty. The changed thing is the subject of one verb and the
object of the other.

[汚染](汚染.md) (おせん), pollution or contamination, can describe harmful
contamination rather than just a harmless mark on a sleeve. The ranges
overlap; visible dirt is not required for 汚染.
[洗う](洗う.md) (あらう), wash, is a practical contrast when cleaning an object.

## Distinctive meaning

The same spelling also has the reading けがす, which shares this dictionary
entry. JMdict especially associates the disgrace or defile sense with
けがす; it does not impose an absolute physical-versus-figurative division
on the two readings. 穢す is another listed spelling, but the よごす reading
in this entry is restricted to 汚す.

This article's clothes and floor examples use よごす. English soil here
is a verb meaning make dirty, not the noun [土](土.md) (つち), earth or soil.
