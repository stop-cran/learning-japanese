# 煙 (けむり) — smoke

## Meaning

煙 means smoke or fumes. It is a noun: it names what is seen or smelled, not the act of smoking.

- 窓から煙が出ています。 (まどからけむりがでています。) — Smoke is coming out of the window.
- 遠くに煙が見えます。 (とおくにけむりがみえます。) — I can see smoke in the distance.

In the first example, が marks the smoke, and から identifies where it is coming from.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [煙](../kanji/煙.md) writes the native noun けむり.
The full reading belongs to the character in this word; there is no written kana ending.
The on reading エン instead appears in compounds such as 禁煙 (きんえん).

## Synonyms and antonyms

蒸気 (じょうき) means steam or vapor, not smoke.
Both can be discussed as something rising or coming out, but sharing that description does not make them synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

煙 names smoke itself. 禁煙 (きんえん) concerns abstaining from smoking or prohibiting it,
and on a sign means "No smoking."
Do not treat 煙 as though it already meant that prohibition.
