# 直流 (ちょくりゅう) - direct current; DC

## Meaning

直流 is direct current, abbreviated DC: electrical current flowing
in one direction rather than periodically reversing.
It is a noun.

- この装置は直流で動きます。
  (このそうちはちょくりゅうでうごきます。) - This device runs on direct current.
- この回路には直流が流れています。
  (このかいろにはちょくりゅうがながれています。) - Direct current flows through this circuit.

直流電源 (ちょくりゅうでんげん) means a DC power supply.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) contributes a straight or direct association, and
[流](../kanji/流.md) flow.
The on readings are チョク + リュウ.
The flow image helps recall the electrical term; it does not mean
that current must follow a geometrically straight wire.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[交流](交流.md) (こうりゅう), alternating current or AC, is the electrical contrast.
Its separate social-exchange meaning is unrelated to this contrast.
[電気](電気.md) (でんき), electricity, is a broader concept,
not another name for DC.

## Distinctive meaning

Direction is the important distinction here.
DC need not have a perfectly unchanging magnitude; a varying one-directional
current is not thereby alternating current.
The vocabulary does not identify a safe voltage or authorize connecting a device
to an unspecified power supply. Use the device's actual electrical requirements.
