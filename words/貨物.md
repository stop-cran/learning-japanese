# 貨物 (かもつ) - cargo; freight

## Meaning

貨物 is cargo or freight: goods considered as something transported. Useful compounds include 貨物列車 (かもつれっしゃ), "freight train," and 貨物船 (かもつせん), "cargo ship."

- 港で貨物を積んでいます。 (みなとでかもつをつんでいます。) - They are loading cargo at the port.
- この列車は貨物を運びます。 (このれっしゃはかもつをはこびます。) - This train carries freight.

The noun does not itself say that the goods are being sold or that they belong to a passenger.

## How the kanji combine

[貨](../kanji/貨.md) contributes goods, and [物](../kanji/物.md) contributes things. Together they name goods in a transport setting. The on readings here are カ + モツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[荷物](荷物.md) (にもつ) includes luggage and packages; it is useful for a traveler's bags as well as things being delivered. [商品](商品.md) (しょうひん) emphasizes merchandise for sale. [品物](品物.md) (しなもの) is a broader concrete article or goods.

## Distinctive meaning

貨物列車 contrasts with a train carrying passengers, not with an empty train. A cargo ship can carry many kinds of goods: 貨物 is not limited to one material, container size, or commercial owner. This card keeps the source reading かもつ.
