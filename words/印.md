# 印 (しるし) - mark; sign

## Meaning

印 (しるし) is a mark or sign used to identify or indicate something. It can also be a symbol or token.

- 地図に赤い印を付けました。 (ちずにあかいしるしをつけました。) - I put a red mark on the map.
- これは感謝のしるしです。 (これはかんしゃのしるしです。) - This is a token of my gratitude.

Use 印を付ける (しるしをつける) for making or attaching a mark, and 印がある (しるしがある) for a mark being present. Nのしるし can express what something symbolizes.

## How the kanji combine

印 writes the whole native word しるし. There is no compound to divide. This reading should be learned separately from イン in words relating to seals or printing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

記号 (きごう) is a symbol or notation, such as one used in a system. 証拠 (しょうこ) specifically means evidence. A mark can provide evidence in context, but simply calling it 印 does not establish what it proves.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary groups several しるし spellings while noting preferences: 印 and 標 especially for a mark or badge, 証 and 証し especially for evidence or proof. Those are preferences, not categorical prohibitions. For a token of gratitude, the kana spelling しるし is useful. Do not collapse all these contexts into the single English gloss "evidence."
