# さて (さて) - well then; now

## Meaning

さて is a conjunction or interjection used to move the conversation or activity forward: "well," "now," or "then." It can open a new stage, return attention to a task, or introduce a question for consideration.

- さて、次の問題に進みましょう。 (さて、つぎのもんだいにすすみましょう。) - Now, let's move on to the next question.
- さて、何から始めようか。 (さて、なにからはじめようか。) - Well then, what shall we start with?

It stands at the beginning of the utterance rather than attaching to a noun with a particle. The sentence after it supplies the proposal, question, or new information.

## How the kanji combine

Use hiragana さて. Dictionaries record rare kanji spellings, but there are no written components to explain in this normal spelling. It is not a loanword or a verb form to conjugate.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ところで](ところで.md), "by the way," often introduces another topic. [では](では.md), "well then; in that case," can mark a transition tied to what has just been established. The differences are tendencies of discourse, not fixed rules about topic size.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "now" is about organizing the exchange, not necessarily the present clock time. さて does not replace [今](今.md) (いま) in a statement such as "I am busy now." Its contribution is a conversational signal; the following clause carries the main content.
