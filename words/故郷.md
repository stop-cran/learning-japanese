# 故郷 (こきょう) - hometown; native place

## Meaning

故郷 is a noun for one's hometown, birthplace, native place, or old
home. The reading taught here is こきょう. The important relationship
is between the place and the person who regards it as home.

- 夏休みに故郷へ帰ります。 (なつやすみにこきょうへかえります。) - I will return to my hometown during the summer holidays.
- 故郷の友人から手紙が来ました。 (こきょうのゆうじんからてがみがきました。) - A letter arrived from a friend in my hometown.

Use 人の故郷 to specify whose native place it is. The word does not
require a village: a city can also be someone's 故郷.

## How the kanji combine

故 contributes an earlier or former association, and 郷 contributes
a home district or native place. The first character qualifies the
second. Their on readings combine as こ + きょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ふるさと is a closely related word and another reading encountered
with the spelling 故郷. [田舎](田舎.md) (いなか) can mean the
countryside or one's country home, but does not automatically identify
a birthplace. [出身](出身.md) (しゅっしん) states where someone
comes from and uses different grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

An old or historically important village is not a 故郷 merely because
it is old. Personal origin or attachment is central. A person's present
address and their 故郷 may be different places; the noun does not mean
"current residence."
