# つまらない — boring

## Meaning

つまらない means boring, uninteresting, or dull. It is an い-adjective: つまらなかった means "was boring."

この映画はつまらないです。 (このえいがはつまらないです。) — This movie is boring.

同じことばかりではつまらないです。 (おなじことばかりではつまらないです。) — It is boring if it is always the same thing.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana, though 詰まらない and 詰らない are recorded. In the sense taught here, learn the whole expression as an adjective rather than translating its parts as "not become blocked."

## Synonyms and antonyms

面白い (おもしろい), "interesting" or "entertaining," is a useful opposite. 退屈 (たいくつ) concerns boredom and is used with different grammar, such as 退屈です.

## Distinctive meaning

つまらない can also mean trivial, worthless, or pointless, so it may sound dismissive. The conventional gift phrase つまらないものですが modestly downplays a gift; it is not a literal recommendation that the gift is boring. Avoid casually applying つまらない to another person's valued work.
