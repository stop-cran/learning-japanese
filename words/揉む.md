# 揉む (もむ) - rub; crumple; massage

## Meaning

揉む involves rubbing, pressing, or working something with the hands.
It can mean crumpling something or massaging a body part.
Kana もむ is usual. It is a transitive godan verb:
もみます, **もんで**, もんだ.

- 肩を揉んでもらいました。 (かたをもんでもらいました。) - I had someone massage my shoulders.
- きゅうりを塩で揉みます。 (きゅうりをしおでもみます。) - I rub the cucumbers with salt.

を marks what is worked or massaged.
The cucumber example concerns rubbing or working in salt, not gently stroking the surface.

The passive **もまれる** can describe being jostled in a crowd,
or gaining experience by facing the pressures of the world.

- 彼は実社会で揉まれて成長しました。 (かれはじっしゃかいでもまれてせいちょうしました。) - He matured through the rough-and-tumble of real life.

## How the kanji combine

揉 writes the native verb もむ; む is its inflecting ending.
There is no multi-kanji compound to divide.
The full reading should be learned with its んで-form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[擦る](擦る.md) (こする) overlaps with rubbing.
[撫でる](撫でる.md) (なでる) often describes gentler stroking.
[ごしごし](ごしごし.md) describes vigorous rubbing or scrubbing;
it is a manner expression, not an exact replacement in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb has further uses for heated discussion, training, or jostling and rocking.
Do not transfer a shoulder-massage translation to each one.
In the experiential passive, the person is shaped by difficulties;
the sentence need not describe literal hands or a physical massage.
