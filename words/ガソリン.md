# ガソリン (ガソリン) — gasoline; petrol

## Meaning

ガソリン means gasoline in American English or petrol in British English. It names the liquid fuel, not fuel of every kind.

- ガソリンが少なくなりました。  
  ガソリンがすくなくなりました。  
  "We're getting low on gasoline."
- 車にガソリンを入れます。  
  くるまにガソリンをいれます。  
  "I'll put gasoline in the car."

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana. There is no kanji decomposition. Keep the voiced ガ at the start and the final ン in ガソリン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

燃料 (ねんりょう), "fuel", is the broader category. 軽油 (けいゆ), "diesel fuel", is a different fuel, not another name for ガソリン. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

American English often shortens gasoline to "gas", but Japanese ガス does not normally replace ガソリン for this liquid fuel. The familiar compound ガソリンスタンド names a gas station or petrol station.
