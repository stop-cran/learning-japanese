# 育苗 (いくびょう) — seedling raising

## Meaning

育苗 means **raising seedlings**, caring for young plants before planting them out. It is agricultural and horticultural vocabulary, not a general word for raising children.

- 温室で育苗しています。（おんしつでいくびょうしています。）— We are raising seedlings in a greenhouse.
- 育苗には温度の管理が大切です。（いくびょうにはおんどのかんりがたいせつです。）— Temperature management is important in raising seedlings.

育苗する is a suru verb. The first example describes the activity; the second uses 育苗 as a noun.

## How the kanji combine

[育](../kanji/育.md) supplies イク, raise or nurture, and [苗](../kanji/苗.md) supplies ビョウ, seedling. This is a verb-plus-object relation: raising + seedlings.

Learn いくびょう as the compound, rather than substituting the independent noun's reading なえ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[苗を育てる](育てる.md) (なえをそだてる) expresses the activity in ordinary wording. [育つ](育つ.md) (そだつ) describes the plant growing, whereas 育苗する presents the work of raising it.

栽培 (さいばい), cultivation, is broader and can concern later growing stages too. [苗代](苗代.md) (なわしろ), a rice-seedling bed, names a place rather than the activity.

## Distinctive meaning

育苗 does not by itself mean sowing, transplanting or harvesting. Those can be related stages of cultivation. Nor does the word specify a greenhouse: the greenhouse in the example is the stated setting, not part of the definition.
