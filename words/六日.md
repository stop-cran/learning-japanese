# 六日 (むいか) — the 6th of the month; six days

## Meaning

六日 is a date or a duration: 六月六日 ろくがつむいか (June 6th), 六日間 むいかかん (six days), 六日目 むいかめ (the sixth day).

## How the kanji combine

[六](../kanji/六.md) plus [日](../kanji/日.md): the native day series むいか, from the kun reading むい of 六 and か for days. See [三日](三日.md) for the whole series.
It is not ろくにち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 六つ むっつ (six things), 六 む.
- Contrast: 六月 ろくがつ (June) uses the on reading.

## Distinctive meaning

The date and the duration share one word, so context decides. Do not use the on reading ろく for the date.
