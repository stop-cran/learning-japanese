# 通路 (つうろ) - passageway; aisle

## Meaning

通路 is a passageway or route through which people or vehicles can pass.
It can be an aisle between seats or shelves, not only an enclosed corridor.

- 荷物で通路をふさがないでください。
  (にもつでつうろをふさがないでください。) - Please do not block the passageway with luggage.
- 通路側の席をお願いします。
  (つうろがわのせきをおねがいします。) - I would like an aisle seat, please.

通路を通る (つうろをとおる) means to go through the passageway.
通路側 (つうろがわ) identifies the side next to the aisle.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes passing through, and
[路](../kanji/路.md) contributes a path or route.
The established compound is a route for passage. The on readings are ツウ + ロ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[廊下](廊下.md) (ろうか) is a corridor or hallway.
[道](道.md) (みち) is a road or path more broadly;
[道路](道路.md) (どうろ) focuses on a road used for travel.
These overlap with particular passageways without naming every aisle equally well.
There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The English "passage" here concerns a physical way through, not a paragraph
in a book or the passage of time.
An aisle can be a 通路 even when it has seats or shelves, rather than walls,
along its sides. Its function as a way through is central.
