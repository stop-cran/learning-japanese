# 先々月 (せんせんげつ) - month before last

## Meaning

先々月 is the calendar month before last month.
If the current month is March, 先々月 is January.

- 先々月、日本に引っ越しました。 (せんせんげつ、にほんにひっこしました。) - I moved to Japan the month before last.
- 先々月の電気代を確認しました。 (せんせんげつのでんきだいをかくにんしました。) - I checked the electricity bill for the month before last.

It can stand directly as a time expression, normally without に.
Use の before a following noun, as in 先々月の電気代.

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md) contributes previous or earlier, and
[月](../kanji/月.md) means month.
Repeating 先 takes the reference one month further back than [先月](先月.md).
々 is an iteration mark, not a separate kanji.
The reading is セン + セン + ゲツ; 先先月 is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[先月](先月.md) (せんげつ) is last month.
[来月](来月.md) (らいげつ) is next month, and
[再来月](再来月.md) (さらいげつ) is the month after next.
[先々週](先々週.md) (せんせんしゅう) instead identifies a week.

## Distinctive meaning

先々月 selects a calendar month, not a date exactly sixty days ago.
二か月前 (にかげつまえ), "two months earlier/ago," measures back from a reference point;
it need not identify the whole calendar month meant by 先々月.
Keep both repetitions of せん in the reading.
