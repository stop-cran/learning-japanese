# びしょびしょ — soaking wet

## Meaning

Soaked or drenched, especially clothing, a body, or a surface covered with water.

- 傘を忘れて、服がびしょびしょになった。 — I forgot my umbrella, and my clothes got soaking wet.
- 犬を洗ったら、床（ゆか）までびしょびしょだ。 — After I washed the dog, even the floor was soaking wet.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic repeating びしょ. The しょ sound contains small ょ; it is part of the word's pronunciation. JMdict also records the variant びちょびちょ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぐっしょり](ぐっしょり.md) also means drenched, often saturated fabric or hair. [じとじと](じとじと.md) highlights clamminess. [しっとり](しっとり.md) generally describes a less extreme moist state.

## Distinctive meaning

びしょびしょになる, びしょびしょの服, and びしょびしょにぬれる are practical patterns; do not assume every mimetic requires する. JMdict also records an adverbial rain sense, “incessantly/drizzling.” The primary learner contrast here is a soaked result, not the specific sound or intensity of the rainfall that caused it.
