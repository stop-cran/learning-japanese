# すらすら — smoothly; easily and readily

## Meaning

すらすら describes reading, speaking, or doing something smoothly, without getting stuck.

- 彼女は日本語で質問にすらすら答えた。
  — She answered the questions smoothly in Japanese.
- 練習した漢字をすらすら読めた。
  — I was able to read the practiced kanji without stumbling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すらすら emphasizes a smooth, unimpeded performance. すいすい also suggests ease, often with a
sense of progress or movement.

## Distinctive meaning

In these examples, the action proceeds without hesitation or difficulty; the word does not by
itself say how fast the person is moving.
