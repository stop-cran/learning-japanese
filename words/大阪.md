# 大阪 (おおさか) - Osaka

## Meaning

大阪 names Osaka, the Japanese city or prefecture. Context distinguishes the city from the wider prefecture; 大阪市 (おおさかし) explicitly names the city, and 大阪府 (おおさかふ) the prefecture.

- 来週、大阪に行きます。 (らいしゅう、おおさかにいきます。) - I am going to Osaka next week.
- 友達は大阪に住んでいます。 (ともだちはおおさかにすんでいます。) - My friend lives in Osaka.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md), large, supplies おお, and [阪](../kanji/阪.md), slope, supplies さか. Both are native readings here. The established place name is the point: do not translate it as an ordinary description of any large slope.

## Synonyms and antonyms

大坂 (おおさか) is another dictionary-recorded spelling of the name. This article uses the current spelling 大阪. [阪神](阪神.md) (はんしん) instead refers to Osaka-Kobe or to a Hanshin organization.

## Distinctive meaning

大阪 is a proper name, not a general noun for a hill. Learn 阪 as part of this spelling rather than substituting it for 坂 in every word containing さか.
