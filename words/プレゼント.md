# プレゼント - present; gift

## Meaning

プレゼント is a present or gift. With する, it means to give something as a present.

友達から誕生日のプレゼントをもらいました。 (ともだちからたんじょうびのプレゼントをもらいました。) - I received a birthday present from a friend.

妹に本をプレゼントしました。 (いもうとにほんをプレゼントしました。) - I gave my younger sister a book as a present.

With プレゼントする, mark the recipient with に and the gift with を.

## How the kanji combine

The word is borrowed from English "present" in its gift meaning and written entirely in katakana. There is no kanji decomposition. する supplies the verb grammar; the noun itself does not conjugate.

## Synonyms and antonyms

贈り物 (おくりもの) is a native word for a gift. [お祝い](お祝い.md) can mean a congratulatory gift, specifically connected with a happy occasion. お土産 (おみやげ) is a souvenir or gift associated with a trip or visit.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese プレゼント does not carry the English adjective meaning "present; here" or the time meaning "the present." It focuses on a gift or giving one. A プレゼント need not be expensive or exchanged on a particular holiday.
