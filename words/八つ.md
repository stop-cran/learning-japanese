# 八つ (やっつ) - eight things

## Meaning

八つ counts eight things in the native つ counting series. It is useful for ordinary items when no more specific counter is needed.

- りんごを八つ買いました。 (りんごをやっつかいました。) - I bought eight apples.
- 箱が八つあります。 (はこがやっつあります。) - There are eight boxes.

The count can follow the noun and its particle, as in these examples. 八つの箱 means "eight boxes."

## How the kanji combine

[八](../kanji/八.md) contributes eight; つ belongs to the native counting form. Learn やっつ with a small っ, which marks a pause before つ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

八 (はち) is the number used with many specific counters: 八人 (はちにん), "eight people." 八つ is not the ordinary way to count people.

## Distinctive meaning

八つ can also express eight years of age. For modern clock time, use 八時 (はちじ), "eight o'clock," not 八つ; historical time expressions follow a different system.
