# 喉 (のど) - throat

## Meaning

喉 is the throat. It appears in everyday expressions for thirst, a sore
throat, and the voice.

- 喉が渇きました。 (のどがかわきました。) - I am thirsty.
- 今日は喉が痛いです。 (きょうはのどがいたいです。) - My throat hurts today.

The throat takes が in both expressions. Japanese describes the body part's
condition where English may describe the whole person.

## How the kanji combine

喉 writes the whole native noun のど, without okurigana.
The kana spelling のど is also useful in everyday text; unusual alternative
kanji and older readings are not needed for these patterns.

## Synonyms and antonyms

首 (くび), "neck," names the external neck region rather than the throat
specifically. 声 (こえ), "voice," concerns the sound produced.
These are related body and speech words, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

喉が渇く (のどがかわく) literally describes the throat becoming dry, but its
ordinary meaning is "get thirsty." Learn it as a complete expression.
The noun can also refer to someone's singing voice, but that is not its
meaning in the two examples.
