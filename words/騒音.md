# 騒音 (そうおん) - noise; din

## Meaning

騒音 is a noun for noise or din, especially sound experienced as disturbing. It is useful for traffic, machinery, construction, and neighborhood noise. Use Nの騒音 to identify the source or type.

- 工事の騒音で眠れませんでした。 (こうじのそうおんでねむれませんでした。) - I could not sleep because of the construction noise.
- 窓を閉めると、外の騒音が小さくなりました。 (まどをしめると、そとのそうおんがちいさくなりました。) - When I closed the window, the outside noise became quieter.

The first example uses で to mark a cause. 騒音が小さい describes lower noise volume, not a physically smaller sound.

## How the kanji combine

騒 contributes noisiness or disturbance, and [音](../kanji/音.md) means sound. The first component qualifies the second: sound viewed as noise. Keep the long vowel in そうおん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[音](音.md) (おと) is the broader, more neutral word "sound." [声](声.md) (こえ) identifies a voice rather than evaluating it as a disturbance. [静か](静か.md) (しずか), "quiet," describes a contrasting condition.

## Distinctive meaning

The choice of 騒音 can express a negative assessment: music enjoyed by one person may be disturbing noise to another. It does not by itself establish a legal noise violation or a particular decibel level. For those claims, additional measurements or rules are needed.
