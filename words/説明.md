# 説明 (せつめい) — explanation

## Meaning

説明 is an explanation or description intended to make something understood.
With 説明する, mark the content with を and the person receiving the explanation with に.

- 先生が学生に使い方を説明しました。 (せんせいががくせいにつかいかたをせつめいしました。) — The teacher explained how to use it to the students.
- 説明を聞いても、まだわかりません。 (せつめいをきいても、まだわかりません。) — Even after hearing the explanation, I still do not understand.

## How the kanji combine

[説](../kanji/説.md) supplies the explaining association; [明](../kanji/明.md) supplies
clarity. Together they suggest making something clear through explanation.
The on readings are セツ + メイ. This association helps remember the word, but does not
guarantee that every real 説明 succeeds in making its subject clear.

## Synonyms and antonyms

解説 (かいせつ) also means explanation or commentary. The meanings overlap; "commentary"
is useful when someone explains the subject while commenting on it.
Do not assume that one word is always more detailed or better than the other.

## Distinctive meaning

Explaining and being understood are separate events, as the second example shows.
説明 also does not by itself mean persuading the listener or securing agreement.
When translating English "explain to someone," remember 人に内容を説明する (ひとにないようをせつめいする):
the person takes に, not the content's を.
