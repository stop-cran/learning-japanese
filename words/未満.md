# 未満 (みまん) -- less than (excluding the limit)

## Meaning

After a numerical limit, 未満 means strictly less than that limit.
The boundary itself is **not included**.

- 十八歳未満の人は入れません。 (じゅうはっさいみまんのひとははいれません。) -- People under eighteen cannot enter.
- 千円未満の商品を探しています。 (せんえんみまんのしょうひんをさがしています。) -- I am looking for an item costing less than 1,000 yen.

In the first example an eighteen-year-old is not in the excluded
under-eighteen group. In the second, an item costing exactly 1,000 yen
does not meet the requested price condition.

## How the kanji combine

[未](../kanji/未.md), not yet, and [満](../kanji/満.md), full or
reaching an amount, help recall "not reaching the stated limit."
The on-readings are ミ + マン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [以下](以下.md) (いか) includes the numerical boundary: 十八歳以下 includes eighteen.
- [以上](以上.md) (いじょう) means the boundary or more; 十八歳以上 includes eighteen.
- [不足](不足.md) (ふそく) means a shortage or insufficiency, not simply being below any arbitrary number.
- [下](下.md) (した) has broader below/under uses, without encoding this numerical inclusion rule by itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Being less than a number is not necessarily a deficiency: 千円未満
may be exactly the desirable price range. 未満 is therefore not a
general replacement for 不足. 以下 also has nonnumerical below/inferior
uses; the numerical boundary distinction does not exhaust both words.
