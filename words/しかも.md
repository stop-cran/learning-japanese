# しかも (しかも) - moreover; and yet

## Meaning

しかも is a conjunction adding a noteworthy point: "moreover," "what is more," or "on top of that." Where two qualities contrast, it can also convey "and yet."

- この店は安いです。しかも、おいしいです。 (このみせはやすいです。しかも、おいしいです。) - This place is inexpensive. What's more, the food is delicious.
- 彼は厳しく、しかも優しい先生です。 (かれはきびしく、しかもやさしいせんせいです。) - He is a strict yet kind teacher.

In the second example, both qualities hold together despite their apparent contrast. しかも stands between the points; it is not a particle attached to a noun. The first example uses complete sentences, while the second connects adjective descriptions.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is hiragana. JMdict also records 然も and 而も as rare kanji spellings. Learn しかも as a connector rather than deriving its present use from a character-by-character translation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[それに](それに.md) also adds information. [そして](そして.md) can simply connect events or statements; しかも highlights the added point. [それでも](それでも.md), "even so," makes a contrary outcome explicit and is not freely interchangeable with every additive しかも.

## Distinctive meaning

The extra information can be favorable or unfavorable. しかも does not mean "good news"; it marks an additional point as significant. Its contrastive reading depends on the surrounding claims, so do not use it as a mechanical replacement for [しかし](しかし.md), "however."
