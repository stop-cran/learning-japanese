# エレベーター — elevator; lift

## Meaning

エレベーター is an elevator or lift that carries people or goods between floors of a building.

- エレベーターで五階に上がります。 (エレベーターでごかいにあがります。) — I'll go up to the fifth floor by elevator.
- エレベーターはどこですか。 (エレベーターはどこですか。) — Where is the elevator?

Use エレベーターに乗る (エレベーターにのる) for getting into or riding it, and で when it is the means of reaching another floor.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "elevator," with no written kanji. Keep both long-vowel marks in エレベーター. The variant エレベータ is also recorded.

## Synonyms and antonyms

エスカレーター means an escalator, a moving staircase rather than an enclosed lift. 階段 (かいだん) means stairs.

## Distinctive meaning

English "lift" has many meanings, but this Japanese word names the building equipment in everyday use. Do not confuse it with エスカレーター when asking how to get between floors, especially when stairs are unsuitable.
