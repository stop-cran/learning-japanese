# 近寄る (ちかよる) - approach; draw near

## Meaning

近寄る means to approach or move closer.
It is intransitive: the approaching person or thing takes が,
and the destination or person approached can take に.

- 猫が私に近寄ってきました。
  (ねこがわたしにちかよってきました。) - A cat came up to me.
- 危険なので、その機械には近寄らないでください。
  (きけんなので、そのきかいにはちかよらないでください。) - It is dangerous, so please do not go near that machine.

This is a godan verb: 近寄ります, 近寄って, 近寄らない.
The second example's には combines the destination に with contrastive は.

## How the kanji combine

[近](../kanji/近.md) contributes nearness and
[寄](../kanji/寄.md) drawing near.
The native elements ちか + よる reinforce approaching.
近よる is another recorded spelling with the same reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

近付く (ちかづく), also written 近づく, and
[接近](接近.md) (せっきん) overlap in approaching.
[寄る](寄る.md) (よる) can additionally mean stopping by a place.
[離れる](離れる.md) (はなれる), "move away; separate,"
provides a distance-related contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Approaching does not entail touching or arriving.
In warnings about a person or group, 近寄る can also mean getting involved
or associating with them; it need not describe literal walking.
For bringing an object closer, use a transitive verb such as
[近付ける](近付ける.md), not object を + 近寄る.
