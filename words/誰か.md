# 誰か (だれか) - someone

## Meaning

誰か refers to an unspecified person: "someone" or "somebody." In a question, English often uses "anyone."

- 誰かがドアをノックしています。 (だれかがドアをノックしています。) - Someone is knocking at the door.
- 部屋に誰かいますか。 (へやにだれかいますか。) - Is anyone in the room?

Particles can follow the whole expression: 誰かに聞く (だれかにきく) means "ask someone."

## How the kanji combine

誰 means "who," and the attached か makes the reference indefinite. This か belongs inside the expression; it does not by itself turn the whole sentence into a question.

## Synonyms and antonyms

誰 (だれ) alone asks "who?" 誰も with a negative predicate means "no one": 誰もいません (だれもいません), "Nobody is here."

## Distinctive meaning

誰か does not identify a particular person for the listener. In 誰かいますか, the first か belongs to "someone," while the final か marks the question. Keeping those two jobs separate makes the sentence easier to understand.
