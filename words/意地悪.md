# 意地悪 (いじわる) - unkind; malicious

## Meaning

意地悪 describes unkind, mean, or spiteful behavior or a person acting that way.
It is a noun and な-adjective: 意地悪な人 (いじわるなひと), "an unkind person."
人に意地悪をする means to be mean to someone.

- 弟に意地悪をしないでください。 (おとうとにいじわるをしないでください。) - Please do not be mean to your younger brother.
- わざと教えてくれないなんて、意地悪ですね。 (わざとおしえてくれないなんて、いじわるですね。) - Deliberately refusing to tell me is rather mean.

The kana forms いじわる and イジワル also represent this word.

## How the kanji combine

[意](../kanji/意.md) and [地](../kanji/地.md) form 意地 (いじ), disposition
or willfulness. [悪](../kanji/悪.md) adds the bad or unkind evaluation.
Learn the compound as いじわる: it combines the on-reading base いじ with
native わる, not a three-on-reading sequence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[親切](親切.md) (しんせつ), kind or considerate, supplies a useful contrast.
不親切 (ふしんせつ) can describe being unhelpful; 意地悪 can emphasize
deliberately making things unpleasant for someone.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling an act 意地悪 can be a serious criticism, but it can also occur in
playful complaints. Tone and circumstances matter. The expression does not
mean that every refusal or every strict instruction is malicious; establish
what the person actually did rather than translating any "bad" situation this way.
