# 便箋 (びんせん) - writing paper; notepaper

## Meaning

便箋 is paper intended for writing letters or notes. It is a noun;
便せん is another dictionary spelling with the same reading.

- 便箋に手紙を書きました。 (びんせんにてがみをかきました。) - I wrote a letter on writing paper.
- 新しい便箋を買いました。 (あたらしいびんせんをかいました。) - I bought some new notepaper.

The first sentence uses に for the surface written on and を for the
letter being written.

## How the kanji combine

[便](../kanji/便.md) has a correspondence-related association, and
箋 concerns writing paper or a paper slip. Together they name paper
for correspondence. The on readings here are ビン + セン, not
ベン + セン. This association is not a history of the characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[紙](紙.md) (かみ) is paper generally; 便箋 identifies its intended
use. [封筒](封筒.md) (ふうとう) is an envelope, not the paper put
inside it. 文房具 (ぶんぼうぐ) is the wider category of stationery
or writing supplies.

## Distinctive meaning

English "stationery" can include envelopes and other supplies.
便箋 does not name all of those objects. It need not have a particular
decoration or line pattern, and it is not the written message itself:
that is the [手紙](手紙.md) (てがみ) in the first example.
