# 開通 (かいつう) - opening of a route; service connection

## Meaning

開通 means a road, railway, or similar route opening for use, or a communication connection going into operation. It also covers reopening or resumption after an interruption. 開通する is intransitive: the route or connection is the subject, not a を object.

- 新しい道路が開通しました。 (あたらしいどうろがかいつうしました。) - The new road opened to traffic.
- 電話回線が開通しました。 (でんわかいせんがかいつうしました。) - The telephone line was connected and became operational.
- 通行止めだった道路が再び開通しました。 (つうこうどめだったどうろがふたたびかいつうしました。) - The road that had been closed to traffic reopened.

## How the kanji combine

[開](../kanji/開.md), "open," combines with [通](../kanji/通.md), "pass through." Together they suggest passage or connection becoming available. The on readings are カイ + ツウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[開く](開く.md) (あく; also ひらく in other constructions) is a broader opening verb. 開業 (かいぎょう) concerns starting a business or operation; 再開 (さいかい) means resumption more generally. Neither is a substitute for every route or communication use of 開通.

## Distinctive meaning

開通 reports availability for passage or connection, not merely the completion of construction. It is not the ordinary word for opening a door, a book, or a meeting. Notice the subject pattern 道路が開通する rather than treating English "open" as proof of a transitive 道路を開通する pattern.
