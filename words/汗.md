# 汗 (あせ) - sweat; perspiration

## Meaning

汗 is the noun "sweat" or "perspiration." Learn **汗をかく**, "sweat," as a whole expression: the person sweats, and 汗 takes を. 汗が出る (あせがでる) describes sweat coming out.

- 暑くて、たくさん汗をかきました。 (あつくて、たくさんあせをかきました。) - It was hot, and I sweated a lot.
- タオルで汗を拭きました。 (タオルであせをふきました。) - I wiped away the sweat with a towel.

汗だく means "drenched in sweat"; 汗だくになる describes becoming that way.

## How the kanji combine

汗 is a single character read あせ in this native noun. There is no compound to divide. Its water component can help you recognize the spelling, but the whole character represents sweat here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[涙](涙.md) (なみだ), "tears," is another bodily liquid, not a synonym or opposite. 汗をかく names perspiration rather than crying. There is no ordinary noun that serves as a direct antonym of 汗.

## Distinctive meaning

Sweating can result from exercise, heat, or nervousness; the word itself does not identify the cause. The useful contrast is between producing sweat, 汗をかく, and dealing with it afterward, 汗を拭く. Do not translate English "I sweat" by adding する directly to 汗.
