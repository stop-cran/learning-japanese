# 入る (はいる) - to enter; be inside

## Meaning

入る means to enter, go in, or be included inside something. Mark the destination or container with に.

- 教室に入りました。 (きょうしつにはいりました。) - I entered the classroom.
- かばんに本が入っています。 (かばんにほんがはいっています。) - There is a book in the bag.

The second sentence describes the resulting state. Although English may say "the bag contains a book," Japanese makes 本 the subject with が; 入る is intransitive.

This is a godan verb despite ending in いる: 入ります, 入らない, 入って.

## How the kanji combine

[入](../kanji/入.md) contributes entering. Its reading here is はい, followed by る. The same written form has other readings in other uses; this article teaches はいる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

出る (でる), "go out," contrasts with entering. 入れる (いれる), "put in," is transitive: かばんに本を入れる (かばんにほんをいれる), "put a book in the bag."

## Distinctive meaning

入る can also describe joining a group or fitting inside a container. この箱に入ります (このはこにはいります) can mean "It fits in this box," not necessarily that the object moves by itself.
