# 発想 (はっそう) - idea; way of thinking

## Meaning

発想 can mean an idea or conception, and also a way of thinking or an approach.
The idea-forming use also permits する; the noun uses are very useful on their own.

- それは面白い発想ですね。 (それはおもしろいはっそうですね。) - That is an interesting idea.
- 発想を変えてみましょう。 (はっそうをかえてみましょう。) - Let's try changing our approach.

There is also a specialized musical sense, "expression": how the music's character or feeling is conveyed.
That is not the meaning intended by every everyday reference to a new idea.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) contributes bringing forth; [想](../kanji/想.md) concerns thought or conception.
The idea-forming use is a useful way to remember the compound, not a literal definition of every specialized use.
ハツ + ソウ becomes はっそう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[アイデア](アイデア.md) and [アイディア](アイディア.md) are close in the idea sense.
[考え](考え.md) (かんがえ), [思想](思想.md) (しそう) and [観念](観念.md) (かんねん)
overlap with thoughts or conceptions but do not always emphasize generating an idea or adopting an approach.
[思い付く](思い付く.md) (おもいつく) is the verb "think of; hit upon."
[表現](表現.md) (ひょうげん) is broader expression, including but not limited to music.

## Distinctive meaning

An approach can be conventional or creative; 発想 does not guarantee a brilliant invention.
In 発想を変える, it is the perspective or approach that changes, not necessarily the final opinion.
Use context to distinguish this from the specialized music term.
