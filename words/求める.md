# 求める (もとめる) — request; seek

## Meaning

求める can mean to request something from someone or to seek something one wants to obtain.
It is transitive: the thing requested or sought takes を. The person approached can take に.

- 先生に助けを求めました。 (せんせいにたすけをもとめました。) — I asked the teacher for help.
- 仕事を求めて東京に来ました。 (しごとをもとめてとうきょうにきました。) — I came to Tokyo seeking work.

It can also express wanting or wishing for something: 平和を求める (へいわをもとめる),
"wish for or seek peace." That does not require addressing a request to a particular person.

This is an ichidan verb: remove the final る to form 求めます (もとめます), 求めた (もとめた), or 求めない (もとめない).

## How the kanji combine

[求](../kanji/求.md) carries the requesting or seeking meaning and the reading もと.
The written kana める complete the word; the kanji-card kun reading もと.める marks that spelling boundary.
It does not mark the conjugation boundary: conjugation changes the final る, not the entire める.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ) also means to ask or request, especially useful when asking someone for help or a service.
[探す](探す.md) (さがす) means to search or look for something. 仕事を探す (しごとをさがす), "look for work," overlaps with the seeking sense above.
Seeking something is not the same as already receiving it, so a successful outcome is not built into the verb.

## Distinctive meaning

The object and context decide whether English "request," "demand," or "seek" fits.
求める does not always mean a forceful demand, nor does using the word by itself make a request polite.
The examples here focus on wishes, requests and seeking, rather than every dictionary sense of the verb.
