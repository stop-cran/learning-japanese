# 幽閉 (ゆうへい) — confinement; imprisonment

## Meaning

幽閉する means to confine someone in a place and not let them leave. The place need not be a prison building.

- 物語では、王子は長い間、塔に幽閉されていた。 (ものがたりでは、おうじはながいあいだ、とうにゆうへいされていた。) — In the story, the prince was confined in a tower for a long time.
- 展示の解説には、王が弟を屋敷に幽閉したとある。 (てんじのかいせつには、おうがおとうとをやしきにゆうへいしたとある。) — The exhibit label says that the king confined his younger brother to a residence.

## How the kanji combine

[幽](../kanji/幽.md) (ゆう) suggests seclusion; [閉](../kanji/閉.md) (へい) suggests shutting in. The person confined is marked with を in the active construction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[逮捕](逮捕.md) (たいほ) is an arrest, not the continuing state of being shut away. [解放](解放.md) (かいほう), release, can bring confinement to an end.

## Distinctive meaning

王子は幽閉された presents the prince as the person confined. 王が弟を幽閉した presents the king as the person responsible. The passive ending changes whose situation the sentence describes.
