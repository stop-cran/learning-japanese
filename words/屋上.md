# 屋上 (おくじょう) -- rooftop

## Meaning

屋上 is the rooftop of a building. It is useful when talking about a roof area where people can go, such as a rooftop garden or observation space.

昼休みに屋上でお弁当を食べました。 (ひるやすみにおくじょうでおべんとうをたべました。) -- I ate my packed lunch on the rooftop during the lunch break.

Use 屋上で for an activity there and 屋上に for a destination or location of something.

## How the kanji combine

[屋](../kanji/屋.md), read おく, refers to a building or its roof. [上](../kanji/上.md), read じょう, places something above or on top. Together they name the top surface of a building.

## Synonyms and antonyms

屋根 (やね) is the roof as a covering structure. 最上階 (さいじょうかい) is the highest floor inside the building, not the roof above it.

## Distinctive meaning

屋上 does not guarantee public access. A building can have a rooftop that is closed to visitors. It also does not mean an attic: the word points to the building's exterior top, not a room under the roof.
