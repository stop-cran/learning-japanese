# 力強い (ちからづよい) - powerful; reassuring

## Meaning

力強い describes substantial or strongly expressed power and vigor.
It also means reassuring or encouraging: something gives a sense that
one can rely on support.

- 彼女は力強い声で話しました。
  (かのじょはちからづよいこえではなしました。) - She spoke in a powerful voice.
- 友人の力強い言葉に励まされました。
  (ゆうじんのちからづよいことばにはげまされました。) - I was encouraged by my friend's reassuring words.

It is an i-adjective: 力強い言葉, 力強く話す
(ちからづよくはなす), "speak forcefully," and 力強かった,
"was powerful/reassuring." Do not add な.

## How the kanji combine

[力](../kanji/力.md), power, combines with
[強](../kanji/強.md)い, strong.
The native compound is ちから + づよい, with voicing of つ to づ.
Keep the standard づ in ちからづよい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[強い](強い.md) (つよい) is the broader adjective "strong."
[強力](強力.md) (きょうりょく) is a な-adjective for powerful.
[頼もしい](頼もしい.md) (たのもしい) overlaps in feeling reassured
by someone dependable. 心強い (こころづよい) is another reassuring comparison.
弱々しい (よわよわしい), "feeble; weak-looking," contrasts in force or impression.

## Distinctive meaning

The word is not limited to muscle strength. A voice, gesture, promise,
or source of support can feel 力強い.
The reassuring use reflects an assessment; it does not guarantee that
every promised result will occur. A loud voice alone is not proof of reliability.
