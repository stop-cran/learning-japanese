# 一塁 (いちるい) — first base

## Meaning

In baseball, 一塁 is the first base a runner tries to reach after batting. The spelling names a place on the field, not a score.

- 一塁に走った。 (いちるいにはしった。) — I ran toward first base.
- 走者は一塁にいる。 (そうしゃはいちるいにいる。) — The runner is on first base.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) gives the number one; [塁](../kanji/塁.md) is a base. The number identifies which base.

## Synonyms and antonyms

本塁 (ほんるい) is home plate. It is not another name for first base.

## Distinctive meaning

In the two examples the runner moves to or stands on a base. When the context is a player's position rather than a destination, 一塁 can also abbreviate “first baseman.”
