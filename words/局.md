# 局 (きょく) - bureau; broadcasting station

## Meaning

局 is a noun and noun ending used for a bureau or department, and for offices or
broadcasting stations. The organization is often identified by a longer word:
[郵便局](郵便局.md) (ゆうびんきょく), "post office," or 放送局 (ほうそうきょく),
"broadcasting station."

- この放送局は一日中ニュースを放送しています。
  (このほうそうきょくはいちにちじゅうニュースをほうそうしています。)
  - This station broadcasts news all day.
- 彼はテレビ局で働いています。
  (かれはテレビきょくではたらいています。) - He works at a television station.

When the context is clear, 局 can stand for the relevant office or station.
It is not the general Japanese word for every kind of station.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [局](../kanji/局.md) supplies the word with the on reading キョク.
In 放送局, the preceding 放送, "broadcasting," specifies what kind of 局 it is.

## Synonyms and antonyms

An office or department may be called 部 (ぶ) or 課 (か) in another organizational
structure; those labels are not interchangeable ranks in every institution.
A railway station is 駅 (えき), not a 局 simply because English says "station."

## Distinctive meaning

The reading taught here is きょく, with administrative and broadcasting meanings.
Do not attach the historical English gloss "court lady" to this reading.
Learn the type of organization along with each compound rather than translating 局 identically everywhere.
