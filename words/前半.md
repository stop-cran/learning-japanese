# 前半 (ぜんはん) — first half

## Meaning

前半 is the first half of a period, game or text: 試合の前半 (the first half of the match), 前半は忙しかった (the first half was busy), 今月の前半 (the first half of this month).

## How the kanji combine

[前](../kanji/前.md) (front, before) plus [半](../kanji/半.md) (half): "front half". It forms a pair with [後半](後半.md) こうはん (second half). Both use on'yomi: ゼン + ハン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [後半](後半.md) こうはん (second half).
- Related: 前期 ぜんき (first term), 前編 ぜんぺん (first part of a work).

## Distinctive meaning

前半 and 後半 are used for time spans (a match, a year, a month) and for parts of a work. 半分 ([半分](半分.md)) is simply "half" of a quantity.
