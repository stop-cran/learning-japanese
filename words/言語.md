# 言語 (げんご) - language

## Meaning

言語 is a noun for language: a language system, an individual language, or
language as a subject of study. The reading taught here is げんご.

- この地域では三つの言語が使われています。 (このちいきではみっつのげんごがつかわれています。) - Three languages are used in this region.
- 言語の違いに興味があります。 (げんごのちがいにきょうみがあります。) - I am interested in differences between languages.

Use 言語の + noun to describe something concerning language, such as 言語の研究
(げんごのけんきゅう), "research into language."

## How the kanji combine

[言](../kanji/言.md) contributes saying or words, and [語](../kanji/語.md)
contributes speech or language. The related meanings reinforce the idea of a
language system. The on readings are ゲン + ゴ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[言葉](言葉.md) (ことば) can mean words, wording, or language and overlaps with
言語. 言語 is useful when classifying or discussing languages as systems.
[日本語](日本語.md) (にほんご) names one particular language, Japanese.

## Distinctive meaning

言語 need not refer only to sounds: 手話も言語です (しゅわもげんごです), "Sign
language is also language." Nor does the kanji combination mean that two
separate spoken words are being joined. Learn げんご as a noun for the system,
and use context to identify which language or aspect is meant.
