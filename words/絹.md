# 絹 (きぬ) — silk

## Meaning

絹 means silk, the fiber or fabric made from it. It is a material noun. Use の to connect it to a product: 絹のシャツ (きぬのシャツ), "a silk shirt."

このスカーフは絹でできています。 (このスカーフはきぬでできています。) — This scarf is made of silk.

## How the kanji combine

絹 is a single kanji read きぬ, a native Japanese word. There is no two-kanji combination. The thread component 糸 is a useful visual association with textiles, not a complete historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

シルク is the loanword "silk," frequent in product descriptions. 木綿 (もめん), "cotton," and 毛 (け), "wool; hair," identify different materials; they are alternatives, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

絹 names a material, not every smooth or shiny cloth. A silk-like synthetic fabric is not necessarily 絹. The word can describe both traditional garments and modern clothing, depending on what the fabric is made from.
