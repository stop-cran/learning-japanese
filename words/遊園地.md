# 遊園地 (ゆうえんち) - amusement park

## Meaning

遊園地 is an amusement park: a place set up for recreation with attractions such as rides.

- 日曜日に家族で遊園地に行きました。
  (にちようびにかぞくでゆうえんちにいきました。) - I went to an amusement park with my family on Sunday.
- この遊園地には大きな観覧車があります。
  (このゆうえんちにはおおきなかんらんしゃがあります。) - This amusement park has a large Ferris wheel.

遊園地に行く uses に for the destination; 遊園地で遊ぶ uses で for the place of an activity.

## How the kanji combine

[遊](../kanji/遊.md) contributes recreation, [園](../kanji/園.md) grounds or a park, and [地](../kanji/地.md) a place or area. Together they identify grounds for amusement. The on readings are ユウ + エン + チ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

テーマパーク (theme park) is a useful comparison, but a particular theme is not part of the definition of every 遊園地.

[公園](公園.md) (こうえん), public park, can be a place to relax or play without being an amusement park. [娯楽](娯楽.md) (ごらく) is entertainment or recreation, not the place-name.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not call every park or every place where children play a 遊園地. The recreational attractions distinguish the usual amusement-park use.

The word alone does not state the admission price, the exact rides, or whether a particular attraction is operating.
