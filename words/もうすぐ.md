# もうすぐ — soon; almost

## Meaning

もうすぐ means that an event or point in time is close: "soon," "before long," or "almost." It normally modifies the following predicate without a particle.

- 電車はもうすぐ来ます。 (でんしゃはもうすぐきます。) — The train will arrive soon.
- もうすぐ十二時です。 (もうすぐじゅうにじです。) — It is almost twelve o'clock.

## How the kanji combine

Use kana in ordinary writing. もう combines with すぐ to express nearness to the expected point. The spelling もう直ぐ exists, but is not necessary for this basic expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すぐ can mean immediately, with little or no delay. まもなく is another "soon," often heard in announcements. まだ means "still; not yet" and can contrast with an expected event depending on the sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

もうすぐ does not guarantee a fixed number of seconds or minutes. What counts as soon depends on the event. It also does not mean that the event has already happened: compare もう来ました (もうきました), "has already arrived."
