# 直る (なおる) - be repaired, become correct

## Meaning

直る describes something becoming repaired, corrected, or back to normal. It is an intransitive godan verb: the thing that improves takes が.

- 時計が直りました。 (とけいがなおりました。) - The clock is fixed.
- 作文の間違いが直っています。 (さくぶんのまちがいがなおっています。) - The mistakes in the composition have been corrected.

Forms include 直った and 直らない (なおった、なおらない).

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) writes なお, followed by る. The meaning involves returning to a right or working condition; it is not a two-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[直す](直す.md) is transitive: 時計を直す (とけいをなおす), "repair the clock." 壊れる (こわれる), "break down," is a useful contrast for equipment. 治る (なおる) is the usual spelling for an illness or wound healing.

## Distinctive meaning

An English translation may use a passive, "be repaired," but 直る is not grammatically the passive of 直す. It presents the changed state without naming a repairer, whether or not someone deliberately did the repair.
