# いらっしゃい (いらっしゃい) - welcome; come in

## Meaning

いらっしゃい is a welcome addressed to someone arriving. It can also invite or direct someone to come, as an imperative related to the respectful verb [いらっしゃる](いらっしゃる.md).

- 「いらっしゃい。どうぞ入って。」 (いらっしゃい。どうぞはいって。) - "Welcome. Come on in."
- 明日またいらっしゃい。 (あしたまたいらっしゃい。) - Come again tomorrow.

The first example is a host receiving a visitor. In a shop, the extended greeting いらっしゃいませ is a polite welcome from staff to customers. It is not a request for customers to repeat the same greeting.

## How the kanji combine

This greeting is written in kana. There are no kanji components to explain. Keep the small っ in いらっしゃい; it is part of the spelling and pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ようこそ also welcomes someone. [こんにちは](こんにちは.md) is a daytime greeting and does not specifically express receiving a guest. These expressions can occur in the same encounter, but they perform different conversational jobs.

## Distinctive meaning

Although its source verb is respectful, the bare imperative can sound familiar or directive depending on who speaks to whom. A host's warm いらっしゃい and a command to come over are not identical in tone. Learn the whole exchange rather than treating it as a universal replacement for "hello."
