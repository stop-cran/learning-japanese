# 釣る (つる)

## Meaning

釣る means to fish with a line or catch something by angling. The fish being caught takes を.

- 川で魚を釣りました。 (かわで さかなを つりました。) — I caught fish in the river.
- 大きな魚を釣りたいです。 (おおきな さかなを つりたいです。) — I want to catch a big fish.

It is a godan verb: 釣ります (つります), 釣って (つって), 釣らない (つらない). It is not ichidan despite ending in る.

## How the kanji combine

釣 supplies native つ, followed by る. The written word is a single-kanji verb, not a compound. 釣り (つり) is the associated noun, "fishing."

## Synonyms and antonyms

魚を捕る (さかなをとる) is broader and can include other catching methods. 釣りをする (つりをする) focuses on the fishing activity without claiming a successful catch.

## Distinctive meaning

釣る also means to entice or lure someone, extending the bait-and-catch idea. For a beginner's account of an outing, distinguish trying the activity, 釣りをした, from actually catching fish, 魚を釣った.
