# 依頼 (いらい) - request; commission

## Meaning

依頼 is a request or commission that entrusts a task to someone. As a transitive する verb, use **person に + task を依頼する**: ask that person to undertake the task.

- 先生に翻訳を依頼しました。 (せんせいにほんやくをいらいしました。) - I asked my teacher to do a translation.
- 修理の依頼を受けました。 (しゅうりのいらいをうけました。) - We received a request for repairs.

The noun fits 依頼を受ける, "receive a request," and 依頼を断る (いらいをことわる), "decline a request." The dictionary also records dependence or reliance, but the examples here concern asking someone to handle a matter.

## How the kanji combine

依 conveys depending on someone; [頼](../kanji/頼.md) conveys relying or asking. The two reinforce entrusting something to another person. The on readings are イ + ライ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ) is a useful conversational verb for asking a favor. 依頼する suits descriptions of commissioned work or organized requests. [命令](命令.md) (めいれい), "order," adds authority rather than simply naming a request.

## Distinctive meaning

依頼 does not mean that the recipient has accepted or completed the job. Nor does it necessarily mean payment is involved. Contrast [需要](需要.md) (じゅよう), demand or need in a market: that word does not name the act of asking someone for a service.
