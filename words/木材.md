# 木材 (もくざい) - lumber; timber

## Meaning

木材 is wood used as material: lumber or timber for construction,
manufacturing, or another practical use.

- この机は木材で作られています。 (このつくえはもくざいでつくられています。) - This desk is made of wood.
- 家を建てるために木材を買いました。 (いえをたてるためにもくざいをかいました。) - I bought lumber to build a house.

## How the kanji combine

[木](../kanji/木.md) contributes wood, and [材](../kanji/材.md)
material. The on readings モク + ザイ give もくざい. Reversing the
characters gives the related word 材木 (ざいもく), not the same reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[材木](材木.md) (ざいもく) also means lumber or timber.
[木](木.md) (き) can mean either a tree or wood as a substance.
[材料](材料.md) (ざいりょう) is broader material or ingredients;
not all 材料 is wooden.

## Distinctive meaning

木材 emphasizes useful material, not a forest or a living tree as such.
It need not already be a finished plank: the Japanese definition also
includes wood used as raw material for pulp. This card uses もくざい,
not the separate きざい heading found in the public dictionary wrapper.
