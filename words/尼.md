# 尼 (あま) — nun

## Meaning

尼 can refer to a woman who has entered Buddhist religious life. It can also mean a Catholic nun. Do not assume a particular religion from the character without looking at the setting.

- 物語の主人公は出家して尼になった。 (ものがたりのしゅじんこうはしゅっけしてあまになった。) — The main character in the story left secular life to become a Buddhist nun.
- 尼が寺の歴史を説明してくれた。 (あまがてらのれきしをせつめいしてくれた。) — A nun told us the history of the temple.

## How the kanji combine

[尼](../kanji/尼.md) writes the entire native noun あま. The two syllables do not represent different parts of the character. In 尼僧 (にそう, nun), the same kanji has the on reading に.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[僧侶](僧侶.md) (そうりょ) is a Buddhist cleric; it does not specifically identify a nun. [寺](寺.md) (てら) names a Buddhist temple, the place rather than its residents.

## Distinctive meaning

In the examples, 出家 (しゅっけ, leaving secular life for religious life) and 寺 give the Buddhist setting. Do not use あま as a general way of addressing a woman: dictionaries also record a separate insulting use.
