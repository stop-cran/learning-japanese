# 宜しく (よろしく) - best regards; please treat favorably

## Meaning

宜しく is an expression and adverb with meanings including "well;
suitably" and "please treat favorably." In everyday formulas, its
translation depends on the relationship or task being entrusted
to the listener.

- 初めまして。よろしくお願いします。 (はじめまして。よろしくおねがいします。) - How do you do? I look forward to getting to know you.
- ご家族によろしくお伝えください。 (ごかぞくによろしくおつたえください。) - Please give my regards to your family.
- 後のことはよろしくお願いします。 (あとのことはよろしくおねがいします。) - Please take care of the rest.

In the regards example, に identifies the people who should receive
the greeting. よろしく alone is a shorter, less ceremonious form;
it is not equally suitable for every relationship.

## How the kanji combine

宜 contributes suitability. よろしく is the adverbial form
associated with [よろしい](よろしい.md). JMdict marks
宜しく as usually written in kana, so the examples use よろしく.
There is no multi-kanji compound to split.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[願う](願う.md) (ねがう) concerns wishing or requesting;
お願いします is a conventional request form. "Nice to meet
you" works in an introduction but does not fit every よろしく,
especially the task and regards examples.

## Distinctive meaning

Treat the complete formula as a social action, not a literal
request to remember the speaker's name. The adverbial gloss "well"
does not make よろしく freely interchangeable with よく in
every description of how an action is performed.
