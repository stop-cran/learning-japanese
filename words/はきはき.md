# はきはき — speaking clearly and briskly

## Meaning

Clear, lively, prompt expression or behavior, especially speech that is easy to hear and follow.

- 名前を聞かれて、はきはき答（こた）えた。 — When asked my name, I answered clearly and briskly.
- 彼女は電話ではきはきと話す。 — She speaks clearly and confidently on the phone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[きびきび](きびきび.md) overlaps in brisk, efficient behavior. [ぼそぼそ](ぼそぼそ.md) and [もぐもぐ](もぐもぐ.md) offer indistinct-speech contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

はきはきした話し方（はなしかた）often earns approval, but it does not guarantee truthful or polite content. [ずけずけ](ずけずけ.md) can describe tactless bluntness even when the voice is perfectly clear.
