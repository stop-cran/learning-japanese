# 去る (さる) - leave; go away

## Meaning

去る is a godan verb meaning to leave or go away. In **place を去る**, を marks the place departed from; it is not a thing being physically acted on.

- 彼は何も言わずに部屋を去りました。 (かれはなにもいわずにへやをさりました。) - He left the room without saying anything.
- 嵐は去りました。 (あらしはさりました。) - The storm has passed.

The second example extends departure to something no longer present. The forms are 去ります, 去って, and 去らない, despite the dictionary form ending in る.

## How the kanji combine

[去](../kanji/去.md) contributes leaving or passing away. Here it writes the native verb さる, with る as okurigana. The on reading キョ appears elsewhere, such as 去年 (きょねん), "last year."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[離れる](離れる.md) (はなれる) emphasizes separation or distance. [来る](来る.md) (くる), "come," contrasts with going away in a suitable viewpoint. 去る focuses on departure rather than the destination subsequently reached.

## Distinctive meaning

去る can give a departure a narrative or marked tone; for an ordinary errand, 出る (でる) may be the simpler description of going out. In dated announcements, 去る + date also means "the past/last": 去る四月 (さるしがつ), "this past April." That is a separate prenominal use, not an instruction to leave April.
