# 鈴 (すず) - bell (often globular)

## Meaning

鈴 is a bell, often a rounded bell that jingles when shaken. A small bell
attached to an animal's collar is a familiar example.

- 猫の首に小さな鈴が付いています。 (ねこのくびにちいさなすずがついています。) - A small bell is attached to the cat's neck.
- 鈴を振ると、きれいな音がしました。 (すずをふると、きれいなおとがしました。) - When I shook the bell, it made a pleasant sound.

鈴が鳴る (すずがなる) means the bell rings; 鈴を鳴らす (すずをならす)
means to ring it.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 鈴 writes the native noun すず. There is no compound to divide.
This lesson uses すず, not a different reading substituted into the examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ベル](ベル.md) is useful for signaling bells and devices, such as a bicycle
bell. [鐘](鐘.md) (かね) often names a large hanging bell, such as one at a temple.
Shape and use matter; English "bell" alone does not decide the Japanese word.

## Distinctive meaning

"Often globular" describes a useful prototype, not a universal shape or size
rule. 鈴 also names bells at shrines, including large ones. The noun names the
object even when it is not ringing; it is not the verb "ring."
