# 暖かい (あたたかい) - warm; mild

## Meaning

暖かい describes pleasant warmth, especially the air or weather. It is an
i-adjective: 暖かい日 (あたたかいひ), "a warm day"; 暖かかった, "was warm."

- 今日は暖かいので、窓を開けました。 (きょうはあたたかいので、まどをあけました。) - It is warm today, so I opened the window.

## How the kanji combine

暖 carries the warmth meaning, followed by the okurigana かい. Learn the complete
reading あたたかい; the grammatical adjective base is あたたか-, not just the
part represented by the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

寒い (さむい), "cold," contrasts naturally when talking about the weather.
暑い (あつい), "hot," usually suggests a higher, potentially uncomfortable air
temperature rather than mild warmth.

## Distinctive meaning

温かい has the same reading but is normally chosen for warm food, drinks,
or kindness: 温かいお茶 (あたたかいおちゃ), "warm tea." These spelling tendencies
help distinguish uses; they are not two different pronunciations. The casual
spoken form あったかい is also common.
