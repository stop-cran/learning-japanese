# 岸 (きし) - shore; riverbank

## Meaning

岸 is the land along the edge of a body of water: a shore or bank. It is a noun. The water can be identified with の, as in 川の岸 (かわのきし), "the riverbank."

- 船が岸に近づいています。 (ふねがきしにちかづいています。) - The boat is approaching the shore.
- 向こうの岸に人が見えます。 (むこうのきしにひとがみえます。) - I can see someone on the opposite bank.

に marks the destination approached in the first example and the person's location in the second. 向こうの岸 identifies the far side of the water.

## How the kanji combine

岸 writes the whole native word きし. It is not a compound with separately pronounced parts. In [海岸](海岸.md) (かいがん), "seashore; coast," the character instead has the on reading ガン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

海岸 explicitly concerns the sea. 岸 can concern a [川](川.md) (かわ), river, or other body of water. 沖 (おき), offshore water, offers a spatial contrast in a sea setting, not a general dictionary opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

岸 identifies the boundary on the land side. It does not by itself promise sand, a swimming beach, or a harbor. English "bank" here is geographical, not a financial institution; that is 銀行 (ぎんこう).
