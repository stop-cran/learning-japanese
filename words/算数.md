# 算数 (さんすう) — arithmetic; elementary mathematics

## Meaning

算数 means arithmetic, especially the elementary-school subject, and also calculation. The school subject includes quantities and shapes, not only the four basic arithmetic operations.

弟は算数の宿題をしている。（おとうとはさんすうのしゅくだいをしている。） — My younger brother is doing his math homework.

買い物にも算数が役に立つ。（かいものにもさんすうがやくにたつ。） — Arithmetic is useful for shopping too.

## How the kanji combine

[算](../kanji/算.md) サン concerns calculation and [数](../kanji/数.md) スウ concerns numbers. Together they name calculation with numbers and the elementary subject. Keep the long う in さんすう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計算](計算.md) けいさん is calculation/computation, with overlap in the calculation sense. [数学](数学.md) すうがく is mathematics more broadly and is the school-subject name used beyond the elementary stage. [数える](数える.md) かぞえる is counting individual items, not a name for the whole subject.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not replace every English occurrence of mathematics with 算数: advanced mathematical study normally calls for 数学. Conversely, the school subject 算数 is not restricted to memorizing sums. 算数が得意（さんすうがとくい） means good at arithmetic/the subject.
