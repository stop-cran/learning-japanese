# 試す (ためす) - try out; test

## Meaning

試す is a transitive godan verb meaning to try something out or put it to the test. The object takes を: 方法を試す (ほうほうをためす), "try a method." Its forms include 試します (ためします), 試さない (ためさない), and 試した (ためした).

- 別の方法を試してみましょう。 (べつのほうほうをためしてみましょう。) - Let's try another method.
- この問題で自分の力を試しました。 (このもんだいでじぶんのちからをためしました。) - I tested my ability with this problem.

試してみる combines the verb with てみる to frame an action as trying it and seeing what happens.

## How the kanji combine

[試](../kanji/試.md) contributes trying or testing. The native verb is ためす; す is its inflecting ending. The same character appears with an on reading in 試験 (しけん), "examination."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[調べる](調べる.md) (しらべる) means examining or checking; it need not involve actually trying the thing. Verb + てみる expresses trying an action: 食べてみる, "try eating it." 試す instead supplies its own object.

## Distinctive meaning

English "try" also means attempting to accomplish something. 試す especially suits an experiment or test of a method, object, or ability. Trying something out does not say whether it worked, so add the result rather than assuming success from 試した.
