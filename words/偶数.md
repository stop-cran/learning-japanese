# 偶数 (ぐうすう) - even number

## Meaning

偶数 is an even number: an integer divisible by two without a remainder. Examples include 2, 4 and 6; zero and negative even integers also qualify.

- 二、四、六は偶数です。 (に、よん、ろくはぐうすうです。) - Two, four and six are even numbers.
- 偶数のページを開いてください。 (ぐうすうのページをひらいてください。) - Please open to an even-numbered page.

## How the kanji combine

[偶](../kanji/偶.md), pair or counterpart, modifies [数](../kanji/数.md), number. Pairing things without one left over is a useful way to remember the meaning. The on readings are グウ + スウ; keep both long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

奇数 (きすう), odd number, is the contrasting category. [数](数.md) (かず) is the broader word "number."

## Distinctive meaning

The mathematical meaning is not English "even" meaning flat, equal, or calm. Use の to modify a noun, as in 偶数のページ; 偶数なページ is not the pattern taught here.
