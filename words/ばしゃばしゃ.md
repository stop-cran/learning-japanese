# ばしゃばしゃ — repeated splashing

## Meaning

Repeated splashes, often with water being struck, moved, or scattered.

- 子どもが水面（すいめん）をばしゃばしゃたたいた。 — The child slapped the water's surface, making splashes.
- 犬が浅（あさ）い水の中をばしゃばしゃ歩いた。 — The dog splashed through the shallow water.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[じゃぶじゃぶ](じゃぶじゃぶ.md) and [ざぶざぶ](ざぶざぶ.md) also describe splashing water. Their typical impressions do not provide rigid rules about depth or the kind of container.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ばしゃばしゃ with the action. The inspected entry also contains ぱしゃぱしゃ, but its camera-shutter sense is restricted to that unvoiced reading; do not automatically teach “camera clicking” as a sense of ばしゃばしゃ.

See [Japanese onomatopoeia](../articles/japanese-onomatopoeia.md) and [motion and sound](../articles/onomatopoeia-motion-and-sound.md).
