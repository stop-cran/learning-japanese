# 暮らし (くらし) - daily life; livelihood

## Meaning

暮らし is a noun for daily life, a way of living, or the circumstances in which someone lives.
It can include both daily routines and the practical matter of making ends meet.

- 田舎の暮らしに慣れてきました。 (いなかのくらしになれてきました。) - I am getting used to life in the countryside.
- 物価が上がって、暮らしが苦しくなりました。 (ぶっかがあがって、くらしがくるしくなりました。) - Prices rose, making it harder to make ends meet.

As a suffix it is usually pronounced **ぐらし**: 一人暮らし (ひとりぐらし) means living alone.
This is a pronunciation pattern within the same word, not a different definition.

## How the kanji combine

[暮](../kanji/暮.md) appears in the native verb [暮らす](暮らす.md) (くらす), "live/spend one's days."
暮らし is its related noun. The character also occurs in words about evening,
but 暮らし does not mean "evening life."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[生活](生活.md) (せいかつ) overlaps closely with everyday life and living.
[人生](人生.md) (じんせい) views life as a person's broader course or lifetime.
[生命](生命.md) (せいめい) concerns life as the state of being alive rather than a household's circumstances.

## Distinctive meaning

A phrase such as 豊かな暮らし (ゆたかなくらし), "an abundant/comfortable life,"
can concern material comfort or quality of living, depending on context.
暮らしが苦しい describes difficult living circumstances; it does not have to mean that every moment of life is emotionally painful.
