# スクール (スクール) - school

## Meaning

スクール means a school. It is familiar in names and compounds for places
that teach a particular skill: 英会話スクール (えいかいわスクール),
"English-conversation school," or ダンススクール, "dance school."

- 英会話スクールに通っています。 (えいかいわスクールにかよっています。) - I attend an English-conversation school.
- 姉はダンススクールで働いています。 (あねはダンススクールではたらいています。) - My older sister works at a dance school.

に marks the institution attended; で marks the workplace in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana. The ー lengthens ク: スクール.
It has no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[学校](学校.md) (がっこう) is the general Japanese word for school.
[教室](教室.md) (きょうしつ) can name a classroom, an organized class, or
lessons in a discipline. A school and the room where a lesson happens are not
necessarily the same thing.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the institution's actual name rather than replacing every 学校 with
スクール. The skill-school examples are useful patterns, not a rule that
スクール can only refer to private schools or adult learners. There is no
useful direct antonym.
