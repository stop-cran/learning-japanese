# アイディア - idea

## Meaning

アイディア is a spelling variant of [アイデア](アイデア.md): an idea, suggestion,
or possible solution. The spelling does not establish a separate meaning or register.

- そのアイディアは面白いですね。 (そのアイディアはおもしろいですね。) - That idea is interesting.
- みんなでアイディアを出しましょう。 (みんなでアイディアをだしましょう。) - Let's all contribute ideas.

The small ィ combines with デ in ディ. Preserve that spelling when reading this card;
アイデア is the other dictionary-recorded form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[考え](考え.md) (かんがえ), "thought; idea," overlaps.
[提案](提案.md) (ていあん) concerns a proposal or the act of proposing it.
There is no single useful lexical opposite of an idea.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not invent a distinction such as "creative idea" versus "ordinary idea" between アイディア
and アイデア. Both cards represent the same dictionary entry; they retain the two written
forms supplied by the vocabulary source.
