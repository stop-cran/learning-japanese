# マンション (マンション) - apartment; condominium building

## Meaning

マンション refers to an apartment building or condominium, and can also refer to an apartment in such a building.

- 駅の近くのマンションに住んでいます。 (えきのちかくのマンションにすんでいます。) - I live in an apartment near the station.
- このマンションにはエレベーターがあります。 (このマンションにはエレベーターがあります。) - This apartment building has an elevator.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[アパート](アパート.md) also means an apartment or apartment building. マンション often suggests a more substantial mid- or high-rise building, but the labels do not guarantee a particular construction, height, price or ownership arrangement. [建物](建物.md) (たてもの), "building," is broader.

## Distinctive meaning

Although borrowed from English "mansion," Japanese マンション does not normally mean a grand detached house. A rental apartment can be a マンション; the word alone does not mean that its resident owns it or that it is luxurious.
