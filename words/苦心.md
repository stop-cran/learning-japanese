# 苦心 (くしん) - painstaking effort; taking pains

## Meaning

苦心 is painstaking effort: working or thinking hard to accomplish something difficult. 苦心する is intransitive. A task can take に, or a verb phrase can use のに.

- この説明を分かりやすくするのに苦心しました。 (このせつめいをわかりやすくするのにくしんしました。) - I took great pains to make this explanation easy to understand.
- 苦心して作った模型が完成しました。 (くしんしてつくったもけいがかんせいしました。) - The model I worked so hard on is finished.

## How the kanji combine

[苦](../kanji/苦.md), difficulty or suffering, qualifies [心](../kanji/心.md), mind or heart. The combination suggests the mental trouble taken over a difficult task. The on readings are ク + シン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[苦労](苦労.md) (くろう) can emphasize hardships or struggles more generally. [努力](努力.md) (どりょく), effort, need not foreground difficulty in working out how to accomplish something. [手間](手間.md) (てま) can concern the time and effort a task requires.

## Distinctive meaning

English "pains" here means trouble taken or hard work, not ordinary bodily [痛み](痛み.md) (いたみ). 苦心 does not guarantee success; the completed model is the outcome in that particular example. Do not copy an English direct object into an assumed taskを苦心する pattern.
