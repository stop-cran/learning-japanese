# 蝶 (ちょう) - butterfly

## Meaning

蝶 means a **butterfly**. The word names the animal group, not one particular species.

- 蝶が飛んでいます。 (ちょうがとんでいます。) - A butterfly is flying.
- 庭で蝶を見ました。 (にわでちょうをみました。) - I saw a butterfly in the garden.

Japanese does not mark singular or plural here; context can also make these sentences refer to several butterflies.

## How the kanji combine

[蝶](../kanji/蝶.md) alone writes ちょう. There is no compound to divide or kana ending to add. This standalone noun uses the character's on-reading チョウ, rather than a kun-reading.

The insect noun can also be written **ちょう** or **チョウ**. Kana spelling does not identify a different animal. Historical kana spelling **てふ** is not the modern pronunciation taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

蝶々 (ちょうちょう / ちょうちょ) also means butterfly. 胡蝶 (こちょう) is a poetic word for a butterfly, not the everyday teaching form on this card.

[虫](虫.md) (むし), insect/bug, is a broader category, not a precise synonym. 蛾 (が), moth, [蛍](蛍.md) (ほたる), firefly, and [蚊](蚊.md) (か), mosquito, name other insects. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

This article teaches the animal noun. The character also appears in **蝶ネクタイ** (ちょうネクタイ), bow tie, and **蝶番** (ちょうつがい), hinge. Those words name objects, not butterflies. [ネクタイ](ネクタイ.md) by itself means necktie; it is not another name for the insect.
