# やむをえない - unavoidable; cannot be helped

## Meaning

やむをえない describes a situation or action that cannot be avoided: there is no practical alternative. It can end a sentence or modify a noun directly.

- 台風のため、旅行の中止はやむをえない。
  (たいふうのため、りょこうのちゅうしはやむをえない。) - Because of the typhoon, canceling the trip is unavoidable.
- やむをえない事情で欠席しました。
  (やむをえないじじょうでけっせきしました。) - I was absent due to unavoidable circumstances.

Before a noun, use やむをえない事情, not やむをえないな事情.

## How the kanji combine

やむを得ない and 止むを得ない are recognized spellings of the same expression. The kana-only spelling used as this card's ID still has an empty kanji list.

Learn the complete expression rather than trying to replace its parts freely with any synonyms of 止む or 得る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しょうがない](しょうがない.md) overlaps in "cannot be helped" and is common in conversation. やむをえない is also useful in explanations and notices about unavoidable circumstances.

[必要](必要.md) (ひつよう), necessary, concerns a requirement or need; it is not an automatic replacement in every sentence about an unavoidable event.

## Distinctive meaning

The expression acknowledges a lack of alternatives. It need not mean that the speaker likes or approves of the outcome.

Do not transfer the intensity construction ～てしょうがない, "unbearably" or "so ... that one cannot help it," to やむをえない. The overlap concerns the cannot-be-helped meaning, not every construction of the related expression.
