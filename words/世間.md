# 世間 (せけん) - society; the public

## Meaning

世間 is a noun for the social world, people in general, or the public. It often directs attention to how people outside one's immediate situation see or judge something.

- そのニュースは世間の注目を集めました。 (そのニュースはせけんのちゅうもくをあつめました。) - The news attracted public attention.
- 世間の目を気にしすぎないでください。 (せけんのめをきにしすぎないでください。) - Please don't worry too much about what other people think.

世間の目 literally evokes "the public's eyes," but the phrase concerns social judgment, not physical eyesight.

## How the kanji combine

[世](../kanji/世.md) contributes the world of human life. [間](../kanji/間.md) contributes an interval or shared space. Together they name the sphere in which people live among others. Learn the conventional reading せけん as a whole.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[社会](社会.md) (しゃかい) can describe society and its institutions or structure. 世間 often emphasizes the surrounding public and its attitudes. [世の中](世の中.md) (よのなか) also names the world of human life and overlaps broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

世間 does not mean the planet or all countries geographically. Nor does 世間の意見 (せけんのいけん), "public opinion," establish that every person agrees. It presents a social perspective; the scope of that public is supplied by the conversation.
