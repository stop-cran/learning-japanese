# タイプライター (タイプライター) - typewriter

## Meaning

タイプライター is a noun for a typewriter, a machine used to type characters onto paper. **タイプライターで打つ** (タイプライターでうつ) means "type on a typewriter." で identifies the tool.

- 祖父はタイプライターで手紙を打っていました。 (そふはタイプライターでてがみをうっていました。) - My grandfather used to type letters on a typewriter.
- 博物館で古いタイプライターを見ました。 (はくぶつかんでふるいタイプライターをみました。) - I saw an old typewriter at the museum.

The first example describes the action and its instrument; the second treats the machine as an object on display.

## How the kanji combine

This is the katakana form of English "typewriter." There are no written kanji to decompose. Keep the long vowel marks in ライター; the spelling represents the whole machine's name, not a person called a writer.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ワープロ](ワープロ.md) refers to a word processor, while [パソコン](パソコン.md) is a personal computer. They can be used for writing, but neither is simply another name for a typewriter.

## Distinctive meaning

A keyboard does not by itself make a device a タイプライター. The noun identifies the machine, not the typing skill or a computer application. In historical accounts and collections, the word can name the equipment without implying that the writer is using modern digital text editing.
