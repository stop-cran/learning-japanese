# そうして (そうして) — and then; in that way

## Meaning

そうして connects what follows with what has just been described: **and then, and, or in that way**.

毎日練習しました。そうして、少しずつ上手になりました。 (まいにちれんしゅうしました。そうして、すこしずつじょうずになりました。) — I practiced every day. In that way, I gradually improved.

It can also be understood as そう + して, “doing it that way.” Context determines whether the emphasis is sequence, addition, or the method just described.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is normal. The rare spelling 然うして is recorded, but it is not necessary for everyday writing. そう refers back to that manner; して is the て-form of する.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そして is a closely related connective meaning “and/and then.” それから also introduces a later step. しかし, “however,” instead introduces a contrast; it is not an exact opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss “and” does not mean that そうして links nouns like English “tea and coffee.” For that, Japanese ordinarily uses と or another noun-listing pattern. そうして connects actions, statements, or stages of an account.
