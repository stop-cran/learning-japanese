# 塊 (かたまり) - lump; mass

## Meaning

塊 is a lump, mass, clump, or cluster. It can describe material together as a mass, people grouped together, or someone seen as the embodiment of a quality.

- 大きな氷の塊が浮いています。 (おおきなこおりのかたまりがういています。) - A large lump of ice is floating.
- 入口に人が塊になっていました。 (いりぐちにひとがかたまりになっていました。) - People were bunched together at the entrance.
- 彼は好奇心の塊です。 (かれはこうきしんのかたまりです。) - He is curiosity personified.

固まり is another dictionary spelling of the same かたまり. Learn the word as a whole rather than reading 塊 as カイ here.

## How the kanji combine

塊 writes a single native noun. The alternative spelling 固まり connects visibly with [固まる](固まる.md), "solidify; gather together"; [固](../kanji/固.md) contributes firmness or coming together. This is a useful word-family connection, not a rule that every 塊 must be rigid or hard.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[群れ](群れ.md) (むれ) and [集団](集団.md) (しゅうだん) overlap in group meanings. [束](束.md) (たば) is a bundle or bunch; [包み](包み.md) (つつみ) is a wrapped package. A mass of ice need not be tied or wrapped.

## Distinctive meaning

The people example concerns how people are bunched together, not necessarily a formally organized association. In 好奇心の塊, the quality is figurative: the person is not a physical lump made of curiosity. Context distinguishes a material mass, a group, and an embodiment.
