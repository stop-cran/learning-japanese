# キャプテン (キャプテン) - captain

## Meaning

キャプテン is a noun meaning "captain." A useful setting is a sports team, where it names the player serving as team leader. Use チームのキャプテン, "the team's captain."

- 彼女はバスケットボール部のキャプテンです。 (かのじょはバスケットボールぶのキャプテンです。) - She is the captain of the basketball club.
- 来年のキャプテンをみんなで選びました。 (らいねんのキャプテンをみんなでえらびました。) - We chose next year's captain together.

キャプテンになる means "become captain"; キャプテンを務める (キャプテンをつとめる) means "serve as captain."

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "captain," so there is no kanji decomposition. The small ャ combines with キ to make キャ, not a separate full-sized キヤ syllable sequence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

主将 (しゅしょう) is another word for a team's captain.

A coach directs or trains the team, while a captain has the team's leadership role; the two roles are not interchangeable merely because both involve leadership.

## Distinctive meaning

The English word also names other kinds of captain, but do not assume it is the best translation for every rank or profession. 船長 (せんちょう), for example, specifically identifies a ship's captain. In the team examples, the title says what role someone holds, not that they must be the oldest or most skilled player.
