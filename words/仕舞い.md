# 仕舞い (しまい) - end; finish

## Meaning

仕舞い is an end or finish. It is a noun, used in expressions such as これで仕舞い, "that is the end," and 仕舞いにする, "bring to an end; stop for now." The form おしまい also names the end of something.

- 今日の練習はこれで仕舞いです。 (きょうのれんしゅうはこれでしまいです。) - That is the end of today's practice.
- そろそろ仕事を仕舞いにしましょう。 (そろそろしごとをしまいにしましょう。) - Let's finish work soon.

In the second example, を marks the activity being ended, and にする presents the decision to end it.

## How the kanji combine

[仕](../kanji/仕.md) has senses connected with doing or serving, and [舞](../kanji/舞.md) means dancing. Their separate meanings do not transparently explain "finish." Learn しまい as a native whole word rather than inventing a meaning such as "service dance." 終い is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[終わり](終わり.md) (おわり) also means an end and overlaps with 仕舞い. [始まり](始まり.md) (はじまり), "beginning," supplies a contextual opposite.

[姉妹](姉妹.md) is also read しまい, but means "sisters." It is a different word.

## Distinctive meaning

仕舞い concerns finishing, not kinship. Also distinguish this noun from the auxiliary ～てしまう, which has its own grammatical uses. The dictionary's specialized noh-dance sense is restricted to the spelling 仕舞; it is not a general meaning to attach indiscriminately to 仕舞い.
