# 事務所 (じむしょ) — office

## Meaning

事務所 is an office: a place where administrative or professional work is done. It can be a room, a suite, or an organization's office premises.

書類を事務所に持っていきます。 (しょるいをじむしょにもっていきます。) — I will take the documents to the office.

## How the kanji combine

[事](../kanji/事.md) and [務](../kanji/務.md) form 事務 (じむ), "office work" or "administration." [所](../kanji/所.md) adds "place," read ショ here: ジ + ム + ショ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

オフィス is the related loanword "office." 会社 (かいしゃ) is a company, the organization rather than simply the place where office work occurs. 事務室 (じむしつ) specifically names an office room.

## Distinctive meaning

An individual professional can have a 事務所; the word does not imply a large corporation. Distinguish 事務, the work, from 事務所, its location: 事務所で働く (じむしょではたらく), "work at an office."
