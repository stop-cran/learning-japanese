# 期待 (きたい) - expectation; hopeful anticipation

## Meaning

期待 is a noun for expectation, anticipation, or hope. With する, it means to expect or look forward to a result. An anticipated outcome can take を; a person or thing in which one places hopes can take に.

- 良い結果を期待しています。 (よいけっかをきたいしています。) - I am hoping for a good result.
- 新しい選手に期待しています。 (あたらしいせんしゅにきたいしています。) - I have high hopes for the new player.

期待が大きい (きたいがおおきい) describes high expectations. 期待に応える (きたいにこたえる) means to live up to expectations.

## How the kanji combine

[期](../kanji/期.md) contributes an association with an anticipated time or occasion, while [待](../kanji/待.md) means waiting. Together they suggest looking ahead to something. This is a meaning aid for the compound, not a claim about its historical formation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予想](予想.md) (よそう) focuses on predicting what will happen; that prediction can be unwelcome. 期待 emphasizes what is hoped for or looked forward to. [希望](希望.md) (きぼう) names a hope or wish and need not imply the same expectation of performance.

## Distinctive meaning

期待 is not a neutral translation of every English "expect." Expecting rain from a weather forecast is different from hoping for it. Also recognize 期待の新人 (きたいのしんじん), "a promising newcomer": this の construction describes someone on whom hopes are placed.
