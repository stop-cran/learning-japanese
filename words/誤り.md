# 誤り (あやまり) - error; mistake

## Meaning

誤り is a noun for an error or mistake, including an incorrect statement, calculation, or judgment.
Use 誤りがある for "contain an error" and 誤りを直す (あやまりをなおす) for "correct an error."

- この計算には誤りがあります。 (このけいさんにはあやまりがあります。) - There is an error in this calculation.
- 名前の誤りを直してください。 (なまえのあやまりをなおしてください。) - Please correct the mistake in the name.

The thing containing the error can take に, while の identifies the area in which it occurs.
誤りを認める (あやまりをみとめる) means "acknowledge a mistake."

## How the kanji combine

[誤](../kanji/誤.md) conveys being mistaken. 誤り is the noun related to 誤る (あやまる), "make a mistake";
the written り belongs to the native word. It is not read with the on reading ご found in [誤解](誤解.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[間違い](間違い.md) (まちがい) overlaps broadly with "mistake."
誤解 (ごかい) specifically concerns a mistaken interpretation, whereas a typing or arithmetic error need not involve misunderstanding.
[正しい](正しい.md) (ただしい), "correct," provides a contrast in judgment, not a noun that can replace 誤り grammatically.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling something an 誤り identifies it as incorrect, without saying whether it was deliberate.
Do not confuse あやまり with 謝り, the noun related to [謝る](謝る.md) (あやまる), "apologize":
admitting an error and offering an apology are different actions.
