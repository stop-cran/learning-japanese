# 迎え (むかえ) - meeting an arrival; pickup

## Meaning

迎え is a noun for meeting or welcoming someone arriving. It can also name the person sent to meet a visitor. In everyday travel arrangements, it is useful for a pickup.

- 駅まで迎えに来てくれますか。 (えきまでむかえにきてくれますか。) - Could you come to meet me at the station?
- 迎えの人が入口で待っています。 (むかえのひとがいりぐちでまっています。) - The person sent to meet you is waiting at the entrance.

In the first example, 迎えに gives the purpose of coming. 駅まで identifies how far the person comes; a car is not required by the word itself.

## How the kanji combine

[迎](../kanji/迎.md) contributes meeting or welcoming an arrival. The noun むかえ is related to [迎える](迎える.md) (むかえる), "meet; welcome." The final え is kana, not a second kanji component.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[送る](送る.md) (おくる), "send; accompany someone on their way," offers a directional contrast. Meeting someone arriving and seeing someone off can be complementary parts of a visit, rather than interchangeable actions.

## Distinctive meaning

迎え does not mean any scheduled meeting. A business meeting need not involve greeting an arrival or collecting someone. In 迎えを待つ (むかえをまつ), "wait for one's pickup," it can refer to the person coming to collect you; the context identifies who and how.
