# 亜熱帯 (あねったい) — subtropics

## Meaning

亜熱帯 is the subtropics, one of the climate zones. Digital Daijisen describes it as the zone that comes next after the tropics in warmth, roughly between latitudes 20 and 30 degrees.

- この島は、亜熱帯にあります。
  (このしまは、あねったいにあります。)
  "This island is in the subtropics."
- 植物園で、亜熱帯の植物を見ました。
  (しょくぶつえんで、あねったいのしょくぶつをみました。)
  "I saw subtropical plants at the botanical garden."

These are original usage examples, not quotations from a dictionary or other source.

## How the kanji combine

[亜](../kanji/亜.md) (ア) contributes coming next after something higher; [熱](../kanji/熱.md) (ネツ) contributes heat, a high temperature; [帯](../kanji/帯.md) (タイ) contributes a zone, an area shaped like a belt. In the whole word, ねっ corresponds to ネツ (see [compound sound changes](../articles/compound-sound-changes.md)). This is a modifier + head relation: 亜 + [熱帯](熱帯.md), the zone next to the tropics. It explains the compound's meaning, not the historical origin of any of the three characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[熱帯](熱帯.md) (ねったい), "the tropics," is the warmer zone that 亜熱帯 comes next to. [温帯](温帯.md) (おんたい), "temperate zone," and [寒帯](寒帯.md) (かんたい), "frigid climatic zone," are other climate zones, not synonyms. [地帯](地帯.md) (ちたい), "area; zone; belt," is a general word for any zone or area.

There is no single fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

亜熱帯 is not the tropics themselves: the two words differ only by 亜, which marks the zone that comes next. Digital Daijisen places it next to the tropics in warmth, roughly between latitudes 20 and 30 degrees.
