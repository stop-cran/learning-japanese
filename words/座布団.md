# 座布団 (ざぶとん) — floor cushion

## Meaning

座布団 is a cushion for sitting on, typically placed on the floor rather than used as bedding.

畳の上に座布団を敷いた。（たたみのうえにざぶとんをしいた。） — I laid a floor cushion on the tatami.

どうぞ、この座布団にお座りください。（どうぞ、このざぶとんにおすわりください。） — Please sit on this cushion.

## How the kanji combine

[座](../kanji/座.md) ザ, seating, modifies the whole word [布団](布団.md) ふとん. In the compound, ふ becomes voiced ぶ: ざぶとん, not ざふとん.

The spelling 布団 uses [布](../kanji/布.md) and [団](../kanji/団.md), but JMdict labels that whole spelling ateji, assigned kanji whose ordinary meanings need not explain the word. Do not derive the cushion historically from a literal "cloth group."

## Synonyms and antonyms

布団 ordinarily names Japanese bedding, though its dictionary family also includes a round Zen meditation cushion. Thus bedding versus cushion is a useful everyday distinction, not a claim that 布団 can never mean a cushion. [畳](畳.md) たたみ is the floor mat underneath, not the loose sitting cushion.

## Distinctive meaning

座布団 names the object, not the act of [座る](座る.md) すわる, sitting down. 座ぶとん is another spelling of ざぶとん; the dictionary also records rare 座蒲団 and 坐蒲団.
