# 俊足 (しゅんそく) — fast runner; swift on foot

## Meaning

俊足 means swiftness of foot or a fast runner.

- 俊足の選手が、一塁からホームまで走りました。 (しゅんそくのせんしゅが、いちるいからほーむまではしりました。) — A fast runner ran from first base to home.
- 彼は俊足を生かして、盗塁を決めました。 (かれはしゅんそくをいかして、とうるいをきめました。) — He used his speed to steal a base.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[俊](../kanji/俊.md) conveys talent or excellence, while [足](../kanji/足.md) means foot. Together, they form a word for swiftness on foot and a fast runner; the compound is read しゅんそく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

俊足 describes speed on foot; it is not a general synonym for athletic ability.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also lists a sense restricted to the spelling 俊足: a gifted person. Digital Daijisen gives that sense first; its second 俊足 sense points to 駿足 sense 2, swiftness of foot or a person who is swift, and 駿足 sense 1 is a swift horse. This article focuses on JMdict's first sense, “swiftness of foot; fast runner.”
