# 俊足 (しゅんそく) — fast runner; swift on foot

## Meaning

俊足 means swiftness of foot or a fast runner. JMdict also records a sense meaning a gifted person for this spelling.

- 俊足の選手が、一塁からホームまで走りました。 (しゅんそくのせんしゅが、いちるいからほーむまではしりました。) — A fast runner ran from first base to home.
- 彼は俊足を生かして、盗塁を決めました。 (かれはしゅんそくをいかして、とうるいをきめました。) — He used his speed to steal a base.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[俊](../kanji/俊.md) and [足](../kanji/足.md) form a word whose established common sense is “swift on foot; fast runner.” The complete compound is read しゅんそく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

俊足 describes speed on foot; it is not a general synonym for athletic ability.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can also mean a gifted person in the sense restricted to the spelling 俊足. JMdict lists 駿足 for a separate “swift horse” sense; that sense does not apply to this spelling.
