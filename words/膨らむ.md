# 膨らむ (ふくらむ) - swell; expand

## Meaning

膨らむ means swelling, expanding, bulging, or becoming inflated.
It is an intransitive godan verb with a む ending:
膨らみます, 膨らんで, 膨らんだ.
The thing that expands takes が.

- オーブンの中でパンが膨らみました。 (オーブンのなかでパンがふくらみました。) - The bread rose in the oven.
- 新しい生活への期待が膨らんでいます。 (あたらしいせいかつへのきたいがふくらんでいます。) - My hopes for my new life are growing.

The second is figurative: hopes grow, not a physical balloon.

## How the kanji combine

膨 contributes swelling or expansion. The native word is ふくらむ.
The pinned dictionary also lists 脹らむ, marked a rare kanji
spelling; that is recognition information, not the main spelling
recommended here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[膨らます](膨らます.md) (ふくらます) is transitive:
風船を膨らます (ふうせんをふくらます), inflate a balloon.
膨れる (ふくれる) overlaps in becoming swollen or puffed up,
but also has uses such as sulking; do not transfer every sense
to 膨らむ.
[拡大](拡大.md) (かくだい) is a broader expansion or enlargement
word.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can concern a rounded physical expansion or growing
plans, hopes, and expectations. It need not mean illness or an
abnormal swelling.
Public dictionary usage distinguishes typical subjects of 膨らむ
and 膨れる; these tendencies are not a claim that the verbs
never describe the same swelling object.
