# 適切 (てきせつ) — appropriate

## Meaning

適切 describes something that fits the situation or purpose. As a な-adjective it takes な before a noun and に before a verb:
適切な言葉 (てきせつなことば), "appropriate words"; 適切に使う (てきせつにつかう), "use appropriately."

- 場面に合った適切な言葉を選びましょう。 (ばめんにあったてきせつなことばをえらびましょう。) — Let us choose words appropriate to the situation.
- この説明は適切です。 (このせつめいはてきせつです。) — This explanation is appropriate.

## How the kanji combine

[適](../kanji/適.md) is the main cue for suitability. [切](../kanji/切.md), familiar from "cut," completes this established compound;
the whole word describes an apt fit, not physical cutting. Learn the on readings テキ + セツ together rather than
trying to reconstruct its meaning or history from the two characters' most familiar English glosses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

不適切 (ふてきせつ), "inappropriate," is the direct opposite.
[適当](適当.md) (てきとう) can also mean suitable, but it has a separate careless or haphazard sense.
That ambiguity is a reason not to substitute 適当 automatically.

## Distinctive meaning

適切 evaluates the fit to circumstances, not merely whether something is pleasant.
An appropriate explanation should suit its audience and situation; calling it 適切 does not by itself say it is entertaining.
