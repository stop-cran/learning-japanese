# 健康 (けんこう) - health; healthy

## Meaning

健康 is the noun "health" and a na-adjective meaning "healthy; fit." Use 健康な
before a noun and 健康です as a predicate. In 健康に気をつける, に marks what
one pays attention to.

- 健康のために、毎日歩いています。 (けんこうのために、まいにちあるいています。) - I walk every day for my health.
- 祖父は今も健康です。 (そふはいまもけんこうです。) - My grandfather is still healthy.

健康な体 (けんこうなからだ) is "a healthy body"; 健康を保つ
(けんこうをたもつ) means "maintain one's health."

## How the kanji combine

健 contributes being strong or sound, and 康 contributes being well or at ease.
Their related meanings reinforce the idea of health. The on readings are
ケン + コウ; keep the final long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[元気](元気.md) (げんき) overlaps with being well but can emphasize energy or
spirits. Someone's cheerful appearance is not a medical assessment.
[丈夫](丈夫.md) (じょうぶ) can describe a robust body and also durable objects.
[病気](病気.md) (びょうき), "illness," is a useful contrast, not a judgment of a
person's worth.

## Distinctive meaning

健康 concerns a state of health, not every English use of "sound": a sound
argument needs a different expression. 健康にいい (けんこうにいい), "good for
one's health," describes a claimed benefit; it is not the same grammatical
construction as 健康な人, "a healthy person."
