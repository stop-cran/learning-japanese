# 五日 (いつか) - fifth day; five days

## Meaning

五日 means either the fifth day of a month or a period of five days. Context distinguishes the date from the duration.

- 会議は五月五日です。 (かいぎはごがついつかです。) - The meeting is on May fifth.
- 京都に五日いました。 (きょうとにいつかいました。) - I stayed in Kyoto for five days.

五日間 (いつかかん) explicitly emphasizes a five-day period. For a date used with an action, に is usual: 五日に会います (いつかにあいます), "I'll meet you on the fifth." A duration normally does not take that date-marking に.

## How the kanji combine

[五](../kanji/五.md) is five and [日](../kanji/日.md) is day. Learn the native day-counting reading いつか as a unit rather than constructing ごにち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

五日間 is the duration-focused form. 四日 (よっか) and 六日 (むいか) are the neighboring day counts, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

The sound いつか also occurs in the separate word meaning "someday." 五日 supplies an exact number; kana いつか may need context to distinguish those meanings.
