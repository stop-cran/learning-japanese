# 残り (のこり) - remainder; what is left

## Meaning

残り is a noun for what remains: the rest, a balance, or leftovers. Use **残りの + noun** for remaining things, or **Nの残り** for what is left of N.

- 残りのパンを食べてもいいですか。 (のこりのパンをたべてもいいですか。) - May I eat the remaining bread?
- 試験時間は残り十分です。 (しけんじかんはのこりじゅっぷんです。) - There are ten minutes left in the exam.

In the second example, 残り introduces the amount still available. It does not imply that the ten minutes have already elapsed.

## How the kanji combine

[残](../kanji/残.md) contributes remaining or being left. The native noun is のこり, related to [残る](残る.md) (のこる), "remain." There is no multi-kanji compound to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

残る describes what happens or continues: パンが残る (パンがのこる), "bread remains." 残り names the remaining portion. [全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ), "all," refers to the whole and is a useful quantity contrast rather than a universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

残り does not necessarily mean unwanted scraps. It can refer to valuable time, unused money, or people who have not yet finished a task. The rest of the sentence tells you what process produced the remainder: eating, spending, using time, or dividing a group.
