# 湿る (しめる) - become damp; lose vigor

## Meaning

湿る means to become damp, moist, or wet. It is an **intransitive godan verb**: 湿ります, 湿って, 湿らない. The damp thing takes が; 湿っている describes its resulting state.

- 雨で靴が湿っています。
  (あめでくつがしめっています。) - My shoes are damp from the rain.
- 湿ったタオルで机を拭きました。
  (しめったタオルでつくえをふきました。) - I wiped the desk with a damp towel.

湿った + noun means "damp ..." It often suggests moisture rather than a completely soaked object, but the dictionary does not restrict the verb to one measurable amount of water.

## How the kanji combine

[湿](../kanji/湿.md) supplies the native しめ and the dampness idea; る is okurigana. Do not treat the verb as ichidan: its て-form is **湿って**, not 湿て.

JMdict also records しとる for the physical sense. The additional figurative sense below is restricted to **しめる**.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぬれる](ぬれる.md) means "get wet" and overlaps with 湿る; it is not an absolute contrast between "wet" and "never wet." [湿気](湿気.md) (しっけ) names moisture or humidity, rather than being this change-of-state verb. [乾く](乾く.md) (かわく), "dry," provides a physical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional sense concerns losing vigor, being in a slump, or becoming subdued or dejected. **打線が湿る** (だせんがしめる) describes a batting lineup in a slump; it does not mean that the players are literally wet.

[しょんぼり](しょんぼり.md) emphasizes a downcast manner, and [沈む](沈む.md) (しずむ) can describe a low mood. The figurative 湿る sense is not a diagnosis or a claim that every quiet, reflective occasion is depressing.

The same pronunciation does not merge this verb with [占める](占める.md), "occupy; account for," [締める](締める.md), "fasten," or [閉める](閉める.md), "close."
