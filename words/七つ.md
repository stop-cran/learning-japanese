# 七つ (ななつ)

## Meaning

七つ means seven in the native counting series. It is useful for seven things when a more specific counter is unnecessary or not being used.

- りんごを七つ買いました。 (りんごを ななつ かいました。) — I bought seven apples.

It can also state a child's age: 七つです (ななつです), "I am seven" or "They are seven," according to context.

## How the kanji combine

[七](../kanji/七.md) supplies なな and つ is the native counting ending. Do not read this word しちつ. The entire form belongs to a limited counting series, not a suffix added freely to every number.

## Synonyms and antonyms

七 (なな・しち) is the number seven. 七個 (ななこ) counts individual items, and 七人 (ななにん) counts people. These are alternatives for particular counting contexts, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

七つ is not a universal replacement for every counter. Use 七人 for seven people and 七時 (しちじ) for seven o'clock. The dictionary's old time-system use of 七つ is not the modern clock expression.
