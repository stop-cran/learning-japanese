# 強化 (きょうか) - strengthening; reinforcement

## Meaning

強化 means making something stronger or more effective: strengthening, reinforcement, or intensification. It is a noun and a transitive する-verb; the thing strengthened takes を.

- 会社は情報の管理を強化しました。
  (かいしゃはじょうほうのかんりをきょうかしました。) - The company strengthened its information management.
- チームの守備を強化する必要があります。
  (チームのしゅびをきょうかするひつようがあります。) - We need to strengthen the team's defense.

It can concern systems or capabilities, not only physical muscle or construction materials.

## How the kanji combine

[強](../kanji/強.md) contributes strength, and [化](../kanji/化.md) a change into a state. Together they suggest making stronger. The on readings are キョウ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

強める (つよめる) also means to strengthen, and 補強 (ほきょう) is reinforcement. [拡充](拡充.md) (かくじゅう) often involves expanding and making provision more substantial; [改善](改善.md) (かいぜん) is improvement more generally. The best choice depends on what is being changed.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not turn every English "solidify" into 強化: making a substance physically solid is not automatically the same as strengthening a system. Psychology also uses 強化 for reinforcement; that specialist sense is distinct from merely making something physically tougher.
