# 阪神 (はんしん) - Osaka-Kobe; Hanshin

## Meaning

阪神 refers to Osaka and Kobe together. It also appears in the names of Hanshin organizations, including a railway and a baseball team.

- 阪神地域には多くの人が住んでいます。 (はんしんちいきにはおおくのひとがすんでいます。) - Many people live in the Osaka-Kobe area.
- 父は阪神のファンです。 (ちちははんしんのファンです。) - My father is a Hanshin fan.

In a baseball conversation, the second example refers to the team. Context, not the two kanji alone, identifies the organization.

## How the kanji combine

[阪](../kanji/阪.md) points to [大阪](大阪.md) (おおさか), Osaka, and [神](../kanji/神.md) to 神戸 (こうべ), Kobe, in this regional abbreviation. The on readings ハン + シン give はんしん. This is a conventional name, not a literal phrase meaning "slope god."

## Synonyms and antonyms

大阪 names Osaka rather than Osaka and Kobe together. 関西 (かんさい), Kansai, names a broader region, not an exact synonym for every use of 阪神.

## Distinctive meaning

A reference to 阪神 is not automatically about baseball. Check whether the sentence concerns the region, railway, team or another organization.
