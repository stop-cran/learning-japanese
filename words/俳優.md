# 俳優 (はいゆう) - actor

## Meaning

俳優 is a noun for an actor: someone who performs roles in films, television, theater, or similar productions.
The dictionary includes both "actor" and "actress"; the word is not inherently restricted to men.
Use 俳優になる for "become an actor."

- 彼女は映画で活躍する俳優です。 (かのじょはえいがでかつやくするはいゆうです。) - She is an actor active in films.
- 子どものころから俳優になりたかったです。 (こどものころからはいゆうになりたかったです。) - I have wanted to become an actor since childhood.

映画の俳優 (えいがのはいゆう) means "a film actor"; 好きな俳優 (すきなはいゆう) is "an actor one likes."

## How the kanji combine

俳 contributes an entertainer or performer in this compound.
[優](../kanji/優.md) also has an actor-related use here; do not force its "gentle" or "excellent" meanings into a literal definition.
The on-readings are ハイ + ユウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

役者 (やくしゃ) also means an actor.
[歌手](歌手.md) (かしゅ) is a singer, and [選手](選手.md) (せんしゅ) a sports player or contestant.
Someone can have more than one role, but the occupations are not synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

English "player" in a dictionary gloss refers to a performer in this context, not automatically a football player or music-playback device.
俳優 identifies acting as the relevant activity.
It does not by itself say whether the person is famous, successful, or working professionally full time.
