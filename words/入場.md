# 入場 (にゅうじょう) - entry; admission to a venue

## Meaning

入場 is entering or being admitted to a venue, such as a hall, stadium, or exhibition. 入場する is intransitive; the destination can take に.

- 観客は九時から入場できます。 (かんきゃくはくじからにゅうじょうできます。) - Spectators can enter from nine o'clock.
- チケットを見せて、会場に入場しました。 (チケットをみせて、かいじょうににゅうじょうしました。) - I showed my ticket and entered the venue.

入場料 (にゅうじょうりょう) is an admission fee, and 入場無料 (にゅうじょうむりょう) means "free admission." 入場 itself does not imply that payment is required.

## How the kanji combine

[入](../kanji/入.md) contributes entering, and [場](../kanji/場.md) identifies a place or venue. The relationship is entering the place. The on readings are ニュウ + ジョウ, both containing long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入る](入る.md) (はいる) is the broader verb "enter." [入学](入学.md) (にゅうがく) concerns entering a school as a student, not merely walking into a school hall. 退場 (たいじょう) means leaving or exiting a venue.

## Distinctive meaning

入場 names the act or permission of entry, not the physical doorway. A notice stating 入場できません means admission is unavailable or prohibited; it does not necessarily say that the entrance has disappeared.
