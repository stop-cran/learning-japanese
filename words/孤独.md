# 孤独 (こどく) — loneliness; solitude

## Meaning

孤独 describes being on one's own or feeling cut off from others. Use 孤独を感じる (こどくをかんじる) for feeling lonely, or 孤独な before a noun.

- 人の多い町で、かえって孤独を感じた。 (ひとのおおいまちで、かえってこどくをかんじた。) — In the crowded town I felt all the lonelier.
- 彼は孤独な生活を送っていた。 (かれはこどくなせいかつをおくっていた。) — He was leading a solitary life.

## How the kanji combine

[孤](../kanji/孤.md) (こ) suggests being apart; [独](../kanji/独.md) (どく) also conveys being alone. Together they can refer to an outward situation or to the experience of isolation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一人](一人.md) (ひとり) can simply count one person. [寂しい](寂しい.md) (さびしい) can express feeling lonely; it is an adjective, whereas you can use the noun 孤独 with を.

## Distinctive meaning

A person can feel 孤独 in company. Conversely, 一人で歩いた (ひとりであるいた), “I walked alone,” tells you who was there, not how the walker felt.
