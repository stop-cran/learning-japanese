# そっち — that way; your side

## Meaning

そっち is a casual word for that direction, that place, or that side, often associated with the listener.

- そっちに行ってもいい？ (そっちにいってもいい？) — Can I come over to your side?
- そっちは寒いですか。 (そっちはさむいですか。) — Is it cold where you are?

It can also select one of the things being discussed: そっちがいい, "That one is better." Context supplies what "that" refers to.

## How the kanji combine

そっち is the contracted, conversational form corresponding to そちら. It is normally written in kana; the small っ belongs to the contraction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

こっち refers to this side, while あっち refers to a more distant side. そちら is the less casual alternative. These form a reference system, not ordinary antonym pairs.

## Distinctive meaning

The gloss "over there" is incomplete without context: そっち often points toward the listener or the listener's location. Its casual tone also makes it unsuitable as an automatic replacement for polite そちら when addressing a customer.
