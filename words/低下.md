# 低下 (ていか) - decline; lowering; deterioration

## Meaning

低下 is a decline or lowering in a level, degree, ability, or quality. 低下する is intransitive: the thing that declines takes が.

- 気温が急に低下しました。
  (きおんがきゅうにていかしました。) - The temperature suddenly dropped.
- 睡眠不足で集中力が低下しています。
  (すいみんぶそくでしゅうちゅうりょくがていかしています。) - My concentration is declining because of lack of sleep.

品質の低下 (ひんしつのていか) means deterioration in quality. To explicitly cause a reduction, use a suitable transitive expression, such as 能力を低下させる (のうりょくをていかさせる), "cause a decline in ability."

## How the kanji combine

[低](../kanji/低.md) contributes a low level and [下](../kanji/下.md) downward movement. Their related meanings combine into becoming lower. The on readings are テイ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下がる](下がる.md) (さがる) and [下降](下降.md) (かこう) overlap with going down. [減る](減る.md) (へる) focuses on an amount becoming less. 向上 (こうじょう), "improvement," gives a useful contrast for ability or quality.

## Distinctive meaning

Some decreases are deteriorations, as with concentration; others are simply lower measurements, as with temperature. 低下 does not itself say that the change is morally bad or permanent. [定価](定価.md), "list price," shares the reading but not the meaning.
