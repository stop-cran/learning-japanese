# 金銭 (きんせん) - money; monetary matters

## Meaning

金銭 is money, especially when discussing payment, financial interests, or the handling of money. It is a noun and can modify another noun with の. 金銭的な (きんせんてきな) means "financial; monetary."

- 友人とは金銭の貸し借りをしません。 (ゆうじんとはきんせんのかしかりをしません。) - I do not lend money to or borrow money from friends.
- 金銭的な理由で旅行をやめました。 (きんせんてきなりゆうでりょこうをやめました。) - I gave up the trip for financial reasons.

The second sentence concerns the financial reason, not the physical form of the payment.

## How the kanji combine

[金](../kanji/金.md) contributes money or valuable metal, and 銭 contributes coins or money. This is a reinforcing pair of related meanings, read キン + セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お金](お金.md) (おかね) is a straightforward everyday word for money. [現金](現金.md) (げんきん) specifies cash in contexts such as cash versus card payment; 金銭 does not inherently make that payment-method contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

金銭 is useful in discussions of finances and obligations, but need not imply greed or wrongdoing. 金銭の問題 (きんせんのもんだい) is a money matter. Do not translate every occurrence as "coins": the compound covers money more broadly than the literal contribution of 銭 might suggest.
