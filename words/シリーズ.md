# シリーズ - series

## Meaning

シリーズ is a series: a related set of works, events, or other items.
For example, it can name a planned set of books or a series of sporting
matches. It is a noun.

- このシリーズは全部で三冊です。 (このシリーズはぜんぶでさんさつです。) - This series has three volumes in all.
- 同じシリーズの本をもう一冊買いました。 (おなじシリーズのほんをもういっさつかいました。) - I bought another book in the same series.

冊 counts books here, not a fixed number of items required for every series.

## How the kanji combine

The word comes from English series and is written in katakana.
The ー extends リ; keep that length in シリーズ.
There are no kanji to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一連 (いちれん) can mean a series or sequence.
[連続](連続.md) (れんぞく) emphasizes consecutive occurrence;
[続き](続き.md) (つづき) can name a continuation or next part.
A シリーズ may contain related works without each one being a sequel
to the previous story.

## Distinctive meaning

シリーズ does not mean that activity continues without interruption.
It is not restricted to fiction. In electrical terminology it can also
refer to a series connection; that technical context is different from
the book examples.
