# 花見 (はなみ) - cherry-blossom viewing

## Meaning

花見 is flower viewing, especially enjoying cherry blossoms in spring. It can involve a walk or a gathering; a picnic is not required.

- 日曜日に友達と花見をしました。 (にちようびにともだちとはなみをしました。) - I went cherry-blossom viewing with friends on Sunday.
- 公園へ花見に行きます。 (こうえんへはなみにいきます。) - I am going to the park to see the blossoms.

花見をする names the activity. 花見に行く uses に to mark the purpose of going.

## How the kanji combine

[花](../kanji/花.md), "flower," is はな; [見](../kanji/見.md) comes from 見る, "see." The native compound joins the object and viewing activity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

桜 (さくら) names the cherry tree or its blossoms, not the outing. 散歩 (さんぽ), "walk," may be part of 花見 but does not specify flowers.

## Distinctive meaning

お花見 is a common form with お. Although the literal components are broad, ordinary seasonal use strongly suggests cherry blossoms rather than inspecting any flower at any time.
