# 強い (つよい) — strong; powerful

## Meaning

強い describes strength or intensity. It can describe a person or team, but also wind, light, feelings, or resistance to something.

- 今日は風が強いです。  
  きょうはかぜがつよいです。  
  "The wind is strong today."
- このチームは強いです。  
  このチームはつよいです。  
  "This team is strong."

## How the kanji combine

This is a native adjective, not a multi-kanji compound. [強](../kanji/強.md) supplies つよ, followed by the okurigana い. As an i-adjective it gives 強かった つよかった (was strong) and 強くない つよくない (not strong).

## Synonyms and antonyms

弱い よわい (weak) is the usual opposite. 丈夫 じょうぶ emphasizes health, sturdiness, or durability. 上手 じょうず means skillful: someone who performs a skill well is not necessarily "strong" in the same sense as a powerful team.

## Distinctive meaning

Strong need not mean large or physically muscular. It can describe intensity, as with wind. For competence or resistance, the pattern can be Xに強い: 寒さに強い さむさにつよい means "resistant to cold". The article's reading is つよい; do not substitute another dictionary reading of 強い indiscriminately.
