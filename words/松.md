# 松 (まつ) - pine tree; top grade

## Meaning

松 is a noun for a pine tree. It also names the highest option in the conventional three-tier **松・竹・梅** system: 松 (まつ), 竹 (たけ), and 梅 (うめ).

- 庭に大きな松があります。 (にわにおおきなまつがあります。) - There is a large pine tree in the garden.
- この店では、松のコースが一番高いです。 (このみせでは、まつのコースがいちばんたかいです。) - At this restaurant, the Matsu course is the most expensive.

The first is a tree; the second is a course label, not a statement that the meal contains pine.

## How the kanji combine

松 is a single character read まつ in the native tree name. The visible tree component helps recognize the spelling, but the other component is not a separately spoken word within まつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[梅](梅.md) (うめ) is a plum tree and the lower tier in the stated ranking convention; 竹 means bamboo and the middle tier. As plant names, these are different plants, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

The ranking use depends on a menu, service list, or other context that establishes the options. 松 does not universally mean "most expensive" wherever it appears. Read the actual descriptions and prices: the three plant labels give a conventional ordering, not standardized contents or quality guarantees.
