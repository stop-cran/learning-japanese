# 上達 (じょうたつ) - improvement in skill

## Meaning

上達 is improvement or progress in a skill or ability. 上達する is intransitive in this meaning: the skill improves. Use が for the skill or activity, rather than treating it as something directly altered with を.

- 毎日練習して、日本語が上達しました。 (まいにちれんしゅうして、にほんごがじょうたつしました。) - By practicing every day, my Japanese improved.
- ピアノが上達するには、時間がかかります。 (ぴあのがじょうたつするには、じかんがかかります。) - Getting better at the piano takes time.

The Japanese subject names the language or activity; the English translation may instead make its learner the subject.

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md) contributes upward movement or a higher level, and [達](../kanji/達.md) contributes reaching or attaining. Together they suggest reaching a higher level of ability. The reading is ジョウ + タツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[進歩](進歩.md) (しんぽ) is progress more broadly. [改善](改善.md) (かいぜん) concerns making conditions or methods better, not necessarily a learner becoming more proficient.

[上手](上手.md) (じょうず) describes being skilled; 上達 describes becoming better.

## Distinctive meaning

Someone can 上達する while still being a beginner. The word measures change, not an absolute level of mastery. Conversely, an already skilled person may show little recent improvement. A decline in ability is a contextual contrast, but there is no need to invent a matching two-character opposite for every use of 上達.
