# 残す (のこす) - leave behind; save for later

## Meaning

残す is a transitive godan verb for leaving something behind,
leaving it unfinished, or saving it for later. It can also mean
bequeathing something. What is left takes を; a recipient or
beneficiary can take に.

- 弟のためにケーキを一つ残しました。 (おとうとのためにケーキをひとつのこしました。) - I saved one piece of cake for my younger brother.
- 仕事を残して帰りました。 (しごとをのこしてかえりました。) - I went home leaving some work unfinished.

財産を子供に残す (ざいさんをこどもにのこす) means leaving
property to one's children, for example as an inheritance.
Useful forms are 残します, 残した, and 残さない.

## How the kanji combine

[残](../kanji/残.md) contributes remaining or being left.
The native verb のこす expresses leaving something so that it
remains. The ending す distinguishes this form from 残る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[残る](残る.md) (のこる) is intransitive: ケーキが残る,
"cake remains." 残す describes someone leaving it:
ケーキを残す. [保存](保存.md) (ほぞん), with する,
emphasizes preservation or storage rather than every kind of leaving.

## Distinctive meaning

残す does not automatically mean waste or forgetfulness.
Leaving food can mean failing to finish it or deliberately saving
some for another person. The object's purpose and the surrounding
sentence distinguish an unwanted remainder from a useful reserve.
