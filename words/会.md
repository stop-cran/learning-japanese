# 会 (かい) - gathering; association

## Meaning

会 is a noun for a gathering or meeting, and for an association or club. It also appears as a noun suffix in names of events and groups. Context tells you whether it means one occasion or an organization that continues to exist.

- 来週、友達を集めて会を開きます。 (らいしゅう、ともだちをあつめてかいをひらきます。) - Next week we will gather our friends and hold a get-together.
- 地域の会に入りました。 (ちいきのかいにはいりました。) - I joined a local association.

会を開く means "hold a meeting/gathering"; 会に入る means "join an association."

## How the kanji combine

The single character [会](../kanji/会.md) concerns meeting or coming together and uses the on reading カイ. The verb 会う (あう), "meet," uses a different reading and has different grammar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) points to a meeting for discussion or deliberation. 会 includes social gatherings too. [団体](団体.md) (だんたい) names an organized group; it overlaps with the association meaning, not automatically with a single event.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 読書会 (どくしょかい), "reading group/book discussion meeting," with a sentence about its next gathering. The same name can refer to the group or its event. 会 is not the counter 回 (かい): 三回 (さんかい) means "three times," not three associations.
