# 食べ物 (たべもの) — food

## Meaning

食べ物 means food: things to eat. It is a noun, not a conjugated form of "eat."

- 好きな食べ物は何ですか。 (すきなたべものはなんですか。) — What food do you like?
- 食べ物を冷蔵庫に入れました。 (たべものをれいぞうこにいれました。) — I put the food in the refrigerator.

Japanese does not require a plural ending; the word can refer to food generally or particular things to eat.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) appears in 食べ, the connecting form of 食べる (たべる), "eat." [物](../kanji/物.md) is もの, "thing." Together, 食べ物 is literally an eating-related thing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

飲み物 (のみもの), "drink/beverage," is a parallel formation. 食事 (しょくじ) is a meal or the act of eating; 料理 (りょうり) concerns cooking, cuisine, or prepared dishes.

## Distinctive meaning

食べ物 is broader than a cooked dish: fruit and snacks are also food. When asking about someone's favorite food, this general word avoids restricting the answer to a particular meal or cuisine.
