# うんと (うんと) - a lot; very much

## Meaning

うんと is an adverb emphasizing a large amount or a high degree.
It can also emphasize a large difference in a comparison.

- 今日はうんと勉強しました。 (きょうはうんとべんきょうしました。) - I studied a lot today.
- このかばんはあのかばんよりうんと軽いです。 (このかばんはあのかばんよりうんとかるいです。) - This bag is much lighter than that one.

It modifies the following expression directly, without adding に.
The second example describes the size of the difference, not the amount
of luggage.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[たくさん](たくさん.md) also expresses a large quantity.
[ずっと](ずっと.md) overlaps in the "much more" comparison use, and
[大いに](大いに.md) (おおいに) expresses a high degree.
[少し](少し.md) (すこし), "a little," offers a quantity or degree contrast.
These comparisons do not make every construction interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

うんと does not name a fixed number or percentage, and it need not mean
"too much." The dictionary also records an archaic "with great effort"
sense; the practical focus here is amount and degree, not a general
modern replacement for "try hard."

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 2007420, sense 1; sense 2 is marked archaic. The [Digital Daijisen entry](https://kotobank.jp/word/うんと-442678) distinguishes quantity and degree/comparison uses.
