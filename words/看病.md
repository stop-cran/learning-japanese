# 看病 (かんびょう) - nursing a sick person

## Meaning

看病 is looking after someone who is ill. It is a noun and a transitive する verb: 病人を看病する (びょうにんをかんびょうする), "nurse a sick person." The caregiver need not be a professional nurse.

- 母が熱を出したので、家で看病しました。
  (ははがねつをだしたので、いえでかんびょうしました。) - My mother developed a fever, so I looked after her at home.
- 父の看病のため、仕事を休みました。
  (ちちのかんびょうのため、しごとをやすみました。) - I took time off work to care for my sick father.

## How the kanji combine

看 contributes watching over or caring for someone; [病](../kanji/病.md) concerns illness. Together they identify care directed at an ill person. The on readings are カン + ビョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[世話](世話.md) (せわ) is care or assistance more generally; 看病 specifies illness. [医療](医療.md) (いりょう) concerns medical care, not simply a relative staying with a sick person. 看護 (かんご), nursing or care, is a related word, but 看病 does not itself identify a job or qualification.

## Distinctive meaning

Look for who needs care and why. 子供の世話 (こどものせわ), caring for a child, does not say the child is sick; 子供の看病 does. 看病する is the caregiver's action, not the patient's illness.
