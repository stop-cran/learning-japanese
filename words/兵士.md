# 兵士 (へいし) - soldier

## Meaning

兵士 means a soldier, ordinarily a rank-and-file member of a military force serving under officers.
It names the person, not the whole army, and does not specify an exact rank within that group.

- 兵士たちは橋を守っていた。
  (へいしたちははしをまもっていた。) - The soldiers were guarding the bridge.
- 彼は若い兵士だった。
  (かれはわかいへいしだった。) - He was a young soldier.

## How the kanji combine

[兵](../kanji/兵.md) supplies the soldier or military idea, and
[士](../kanji/士.md) contributes a person in a role.
Together they identify a person in military service.
Use the on readings へい + し, not the separate 兵 reading つわもの.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[武士](武士.md) (ぶし) means a samurai or warrior and is not a general replacement
for a soldier in a modern army. The broad English label "warrior" can nevertheless overlap
with "soldier"; that overlap does not make the Japanese words identical.
The ordinary rank-and-file sense should not be expanded into an unrestricted label for every military position.

## Distinctive meaning

This word focuses on a person's military role. It does not by itself identify the person's
nationality or whether they are fighting at that moment. It is not a general word for anyone who fights.
