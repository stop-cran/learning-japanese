# スープ - soup

## Meaning

スープ is soup, such as vegetable soup or corn soup. It is a noun. Use スープを作る (スープをつくる), "make soup," and スープを飲む (スープをのむ), "have or drink soup."

- 寒い日に温かいスープを飲みました。 (さむいひにあたたかいスープをのみました。) - I had warm soup on a cold day.
- 野菜を入れて、スープを作ります。 (やさいをいれて、スープをつくります。) - I will make soup with vegetables.

Japanese 飲む can describe consuming soup even where English would naturally say "have soup." It does not mean that the soup has no solid ingredients.

## How the kanji combine

スープ is written in katakana and has no kanji components. The long-vowel mark ー extends the ウ sound in ス. Keep that length when reading: スープ, not スプ. コーンスープ names corn soup.

## Synonyms and antonyms

みそ汁 (みそしる) is the established name for miso soup. Learn it as a complete word rather than assuming every soup name must end in スープ. [食事](食事.md) (しょくじ) names the meal, not this particular dish.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary gloss highlights Western-style soup. For study, start with ordinary examples such as vegetable and corn soup rather than using that label as a rule about which cuisines may use the word. スープ itself does not say whether the dish is thick, clear, hot, or cold.
