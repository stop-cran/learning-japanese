# 索引 (さくいん) - index in a book

## Meaning

索引 is an index: an arranged list of terms or subjects with locations where they appear in a book.
Use 本の索引 (ほんのさくいん), "a book's index."

- 本の索引で、その言葉を探しました。 (ほんのさくいんで、そのことばをさがしました。) - I looked for the term in the book's index.
- 索引を見ると、説明のあるページが分かります。 (さくいんをみると、せつめいのあるページがわかります。) - The index tells you which page has the explanation.

## How the kanji combine

索 contributes searching or inquiring, and [引](../kanji/引.md) has a referring or drawing association.
Together they suggest a reference for locating information, not physically pulling a book.
The on readings are サク + イン; keep the くい sequence in さくいん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

目次 (もくじ) is a table of contents, organized around the work's sections or chapters.
索引 instead lets a reader locate a term or subject. [内容](内容.md) (ないよう) means the contents or substance itself, not necessarily a navigational list.

## Distinctive meaning

English "index" also occurs in economics and computing. This introductory sense is specifically the book index;
it does not make 索引 a replacement for every technical English use.
The dictionary's search-only spelling 索隠 is not the spelling to imitate here.
