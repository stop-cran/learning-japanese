# 逸れる (それる) - veer off; digress

## Meaning

逸れる means to turn away from a course, miss a target, or move away from
the subject of a conversation. It is an intransitive ichidan verb:
それます, それない, それて. Kana それる is usual.

- ボールが的からそれました。 (ボールがまとからそれました。) - The ball went wide of the target.
- 話が本題からそれてしまいました。 (はなしがほんだいからそれてしまいました。) - The conversation strayed from the main subject.

The thing changing direction takes が; から can mark the course, target,
or topic left behind. それた道 (それたみち) is a road or path that branches
away, not a command to change someone else's direction.

## How the kanji combine

逸 writes the stem それ; る completes the verb. Learn the whole written-form
and reading pair rather than deriving it from an on reading of 逸.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[迷う](迷う.md) (まよう) can mean getting lost or being undecided. それる
focuses on departure from a direction or subject; it does not necessarily
say that someone no longer knows where they are.
逸らす (そらす), "turn aside; divert," is the transitive counterpart:
話をそらす (はなしをそらす), "divert the conversation."

## Distinctive meaning

The same spelling 逸れる also occurs with はぐれる in a separate dictionary
family, for becoming separated from companions or missing an opportunity.
That does not make はぐれる a freely interchangeable pronunciation of
それる. Check the intended word and construction.
