# 謝る (あやまる) — apologize

## Meaning

謝る means "apologize". Mark the person receiving the apology with に. The mistake or conduct being apologized for can be marked with を.

- 友達に謝りました。  
  ともだちにあやまりました。  
  "I apologized to my friend."
- 遅れたことを先生に謝りました。  
  おくれたことをせんせいにあやまりました。  
  "I apologized to the teacher for being late."

## How the kanji combine

謝 represents apologizing here, with the native reading あやま; る is okurigana. This is a godan verb despite ending in る: 謝ります (あやまります), 謝って (あやまって), 謝らない (あやまらない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

すみません and ごめんなさい are things people say to apologize; 謝る names the action. 許す (ゆるす), "forgive", describes the other person's possible response, not an opposite action.

## Distinctive meaning

謝る reports an apology without specifying its exact words or whether it was accepted. Do not confuse it with the identically pronounced 誤る (あやまる), "make a mistake": making a mistake and apologizing are separate actions.
