# 順番 (じゅんばん) - turn; sequence

## Meaning

順番 is a noun for the order in which things happen or a person's turn in that order.
**順番を待つ** (じゅんばんをまつ) means "wait one's turn"; **順番に** means "in order/one after another."

- 自分の順番を待ってください。 (じぶんのじゅんばんをまってください。) - Please wait your turn.
- 名前を順番に読んでください。 (なまえをじゅんばんによんでください。) - Please read the names in order.

順番が来る (じゅんばんがくる) means "one's turn comes."
私の順番です (わたしのじゅんばんです) announces "It's my turn."

## How the kanji combine

順 contributes order or sequence, while [番](../kanji/番.md) contributes a turn or place in a series.
The two ideas reinforce one another: a turn understood within an order.
The on readings are ジュン + バン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[順](順.md) (じゅん) appears in compact expressions for order, such as 名前の順 (なまえのじゅん), "name order."
[番](番.md) (ばん) alone can also mean a turn.
[番号](番号.md) (ばんごう) is an identifying number; a number may help manage turns without itself meaning "sequence."

## Distinctive meaning

順番 does not specify which ordering rule is in use. People might take turns by arrival, a list,
or a decision made beforehand.
順番を変える (じゅんばんをかえる) means change the order, not change the identity of the people involved.
There is no single useful antonym for both "a turn" and "an order."
