# 多少 (たしょう) — more or less, somewhat, a little

## Meaning

多少 means "more or less" or "to some extent": 多少の違い (some difference), 多少わかります (I understand a little), 多少高くても (even if it's a bit expensive), 金額の多少 (the size of the amount).

As an adverb, it goes before a predicate without に. Before a noun, use 多少の.
As a noun itself, it can refer to the amount or quantity.

- 日本語は多少わかります。 (にほんごはたしょうわかります。) — I understand Japanese to some extent.
- 多少の違いはあります。 (たしょうのちがいはあります。) — There are some differences.

## How the kanji combine

[多](../kanji/多.md) (many) and [少](../kanji/少.md) (few) join two opposites,
placing an amount on a large-to-small scale. This explains the component relationship,
not a rule for deriving every usage. On readings タ + ショウ.
Compare [大小](大小.md) (だいしょう), and pairs like [左右](左右.md) (さゆう).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: [少し](少し.md) (すこし), いくらか (somewhat), 若干 (じゃっかん, a few; some).
- Not antonymous; the kanji are opposites but the word is a unit.

## Distinctive meaning

多少 softens a statement, as in 多少は (to some degree). It can also mean quantity in general (多少にかかわらず, regardless of amount).

For example, 金額の多少にかかわらず (きんがくのたしょうにかかわらず) means
"regardless of how large or small the sum is." Here 多少 names the amount; it does
not simply mean that the sum is small.
