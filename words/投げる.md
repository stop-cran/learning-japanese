# 投げる (なげる) — throw

## Meaning

投げる means to throw, toss, or hurl. Mark the thrown object with を and, when needed, its destination with に.
The verb itself does not specify that the throw is forceful.

- 友達にボールを投げました。 (ともだちにボールをなげました。) — I threw the ball to my friend.
- ここで石を投げないでください。 (ここでいしをなげないでください。) — Please do not throw stones here.

The past form is 投げた (なげた), and the negative is 投げない (なげない).

## How the kanji combine

[投](../kanji/投.md) supplies the throwing idea. The written ending げる completes a single native verb;
it is not a separate word being added to the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

放す (はなす) means letting go or releasing. It overlaps in the object leaving one's control,
but does not by itself describe a throwing motion.
[打つ](打つ.md) (うつ), "hit," is another action involving a ball, not a synonym for throwing.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare ボールを投げる (ボールをなげる), "throw a ball," with ボールを打つ (ボールをうつ), "hit a ball."
The object is unchanged, but the action differs.
This article teaches physical throwing; it does not make "throw" a literal explanation for every extended use of 投げる.
