# 挟む (はさむ) - hold between; pinch

## Meaning

挟む means to hold or put something between two sides, and can also mean catching or pinching something between them.
It is a transitive godan verb ending in む: 挟みます はさみます, 挟んで はさんで, 挟まない はさまない.
The object held or inserted takes を.

- 指で紙を挟みました。 (ゆびでかみをはさみました。) - I held the paper between my fingers.
- 本にしおりを挟みました。 (ほんにしおりをはさみました。) - I put a bookmark between the pages of the book.

The fingers in the first sentence hold from two sides. In the second, に marks where the bookmark is put.

## How the kanji combine

[挟](../kanji/挟.md) contributes holding or putting between and is read はさ before the written ending む.
This is one native verb, not a compound of two spoken words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[握る](握る.md) にぎる emphasizes gripping with the hand; [持つ](持つ.md) もつ is broader holding or possession.
挟む specifically makes the two-sided or intervening position relevant.
挟まる はさまる is intransitive: it describes something getting between or being caught, with that thing marked by が.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb also extends to inserting a comment or interruption, not just a physical object.
An inserted bookmark and a pinched finger illustrate different outcomes of being between two sides; the verb does not always imply injury.
The character dictionary also lists other readings of 挟, but this article teaches the ordinary verb はさむ.
