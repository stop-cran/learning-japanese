# 浮かぶ (うかぶ) - float; come to mind

## Meaning

浮かぶ is an intransitive godan verb: something floats, appears or comes to mind.
The floating thing, expression or idea takes が.

- 木の葉が水に浮かんでいます。 (このはがみずにうかんでいます。) - A leaf is floating on the water.
- 彼の顔に笑みが浮かびました。 (かれのかおにえみがうかびました。) - A smile appeared on his face.
- いい考えが浮かびました。 (いいかんがえがうかびました。) - A good idea came to mind.

Forms include 浮かびます (うかびます), 浮かんだ (うかんだ) and 浮かばない (うかばない).
Also recognize 浮ぶ (うかぶ), indexed as a search-only spelling in the pinned dictionary;
this article uses the headword spelling 浮かぶ.

## How the kanji combine

[浮](../kanji/浮.md) supplies う in this native verb; かぶ is okurigana.
Floating provides a useful association for an image or thought emerging, not a literal account of how ideas move.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[浮く](浮く.md) (うく) also means float; the two verbs overlap in physical uses.
[浮かべる](浮かべる.md) (うかべる) is transitive: 船を浮かべる (ふねをうかべる), "set a boat afloat."
[沈む](沈む.md) (しずむ), "sink," contrasts with floating, not with every idea coming to mind.

## Distinctive meaning

考えが浮かぶ describes an idea occurring, without requiring someone deliberately to construct it.
Compare 景色を心に浮かべる (けしきをこころにうかべる), "picture a scene in one's mind."
The が/を difference matters; 浮かぶ is not the passive conjugation of 浮かべる.
