# 全然 (ぜんぜん) — not at all

## Meaning

With a negative predicate, 全然 means "not at all" or "not in the slightest." It strengthens the negative; it does not mean the weaker "not entirely."

- この問題は全然分かりません。 (このもんだいはぜんぜんわかりません。) — I do not understand this problem at all.
- 今日は全然寒くないです。 (きょうはぜんぜんさむくないです。) — It is not cold at all today.

## How the kanji combine

[全](../kanji/全.md) suggests entirety, and [然](../kanji/然.md) occurs in words describing a state or manner. Read both with on-readings: ぜん + ぜん. Learn the expression with the predicate that follows it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

少しも (すこしも) with a negative also means "not even a little." あまり with a negative usually means "not very/much," so it is weaker: あまり寒くない is "not very cold."

## Distinctive meaning

全然 does not itself conjugate; the verb or adjective supplies the negative form. Affirmative colloquial uses also exist, such as 全然いいよ (ぜんぜんいいよ), "That's totally fine." They are not an error merely because there is no negative ending, but the negative pattern is the most useful starting point for this card.
