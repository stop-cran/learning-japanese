# 背 (せ) - height; stature

## Meaning

背 means a person's height or stature in expressions such as 背が高い
(せがたかい), "tall," and 背が低い (せがひくい), "short."
It is the noun being described, not an adjective by itself.

- 弟は私より背が高いです。 (おとうとはわたしよりせがたかいです。) - My younger brother is taller than I am.

より marks the person used as the comparison.

The height noun is also read **せい**, the reading assigned by the additional
N3 source row. For example, 背の高い人 (せいのたかいひと) means "a tall
person." This reading has its own dictionary entry for height or stature;
it is not a reason to read every occurrence meaning "back" as せい.

## How the kanji combine

[背](../kanji/背.md) writes the native noun せ. Its meanings also include the
back or rear side of something. Read the whole expression to choose the
intended meaning instead of translating the kanji mechanically.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[身長](身長.md) (しんちょう) specifically names bodily height, especially a measurement.
高い and 低い provide the tall/short contrast in this pattern; 背 itself has
no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

[背中](背中.md) (せなか) usually names the back as a body part, whereas 背が高い concerns
stature. For growing taller, use 背が伸びる (せがのびる), literally "height extends."
Do not translate "tall" as 背 alone.

The article keeps its N5 label and primary reading せ; the additional せい
reading does not replace that easier assignment.
