# 豆 (まめ) - bean; pea

## Meaning

豆 is a noun for a bean or pea, or the edible legumes and seeds considered as a group. It does not necessarily identify a particular species. The same noun can refer to one bean or many.

- 豆を水に一晩つけておきます。 (まめをみずにひとばんつけておきます。) - I leave the beans soaking in water overnight.
- このスープには豆がたくさん入っています。 (このスープにはまめがたくさんはいっています。) - This soup contains lots of beans.

For more specific ingredients, learn names such as 大豆 (だいず), "soybeans," and 小豆 (あずき), "adzuki beans," rather than inferring the variety from 豆 alone.

## How the kanji combine

豆 is one kanji with the native reading まめ here. It writes the whole noun without okurigana. Compound names can use different readings, so 大豆 is not read simply by adding まめ to 大.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[野菜](野菜.md) (やさい), "vegetables," is a broader food-category word, not a replacement for the particular bean ingredient in a recipe. Different kinds of beans are related items rather than opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

豆 also appears as a prefix meaning small or miniature, as in 豆電球 (まめでんきゅう), "miniature light bulb." That bulb is not made from a bean. Keep the food noun separate from this size-related compound use, and read the complete word before choosing the English meaning.
