# 回忌 (かいき) — anniversary of a death

## Meaning

回忌 follows a number to name a yearly anniversary of a person’s death, on which a memorial service is held. Daijisen explains it as short for 年回忌 (ねんかいき).

- 来月は祖母の三回忌なので、親戚が集まる。 (らいげつはそぼのさんかいきなので、しんせきがあつまる。) — Next month is the second anniversary of my grandmother’s death, so our relatives will gather.
- 祖父の三回忌の法事は、家族だけで行った。 (そふのさんかいきのほうじは、かぞくだけでおこなった。) — The memorial service for the second anniversary of my grandfather’s death was held with family only.

## How the kanji combine

[回](../kanji/回.md) (かい) counts occurrences, and [忌](../kanji/忌.md) (き) relates to mourning and death anniversaries. In Daijisen’s account, the first anniversary is 一回忌 or 一周忌 (いっしゅうき), and the second is already 三回忌 (さんかいき).

## Synonyms and antonyms

法事 (ほうじ) is the Buddhist memorial service held on such a day. Both 一周忌 and 一回忌 name the first anniversary.

## Distinctive meaning

Translate 三回忌 as “the second anniversary of a death,” as JMdict does, not “the third.” From 三回忌 on, the number before 回忌 is one more than the number of years since the death.
