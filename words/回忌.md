# 回忌 (かいき) — anniversary of a death

## Meaning

回忌 follows a number to name a particular anniversary of a person’s death, such as the second or the sixth. Daijisen explains it as short for 年回忌 (ねんかいき).

- 来月は祖母の三回忌なので、親戚が集まる。 (らいげつはそぼのさんかいきなので、しんせきがあつまる。) — Next month is the second anniversary of my grandmother’s death, so our relatives will gather.
- 父の七回忌には、遠くに住む兄も帰ってきた。 (ちちのしちかいきには、とおくにすむあにもかえってきた。) — For the sixth anniversary of our father’s death, even my older brother, who lives far away, came home.

## How the kanji combine

[回](../kanji/回.md) (かい) counts occurrences, and [忌](../kanji/忌.md) (き) relates to mourning and death anniversaries. The numbered words count the first anniversary as the first occasion and the second anniversary as the third.

## Synonyms and antonyms

法事 (ほうじ) is a Buddhist memorial service. For the first anniversary, 一周忌 (いっしゅうき) is the usual word; JMdict also records 一回忌 (いっかいき) but notes that 一周忌 is usual.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict glosses 三回忌 (さんかいき) as “second anniversary,” so do not translate it as “the third.” From 三回忌 on, the number before 回忌 is one more than the number of years since the death: 七回忌 (しちかいき) is the sixth anniversary, as JMdict glosses it.
