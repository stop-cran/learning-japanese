# 降る (ふる) — fall as rain or snow

## Meaning

降る describes rain, snow, ash, and similar material coming down. It is intransitive:
the falling material is the subject, often marked with が. Its polite form is 降ります (ふります); its て-form is 降って (ふって).

- 雨が降っています。 (あめがふっています。) — It is raining.
- 昨日は雪が降りました。 (きのうはゆきがふりました。) — It snowed yesterday.

## How the kanji combine

[降](../kanji/降.md) contributes coming down, and る completes the native verb ふる.
Do not read this spelling おりる: that verb is written 降りる with an extra り.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[落ちる](落ちる.md) (おちる) covers a broader range of falling or dropping, such as a book falling from a desk.
降りる (おりる) is useful for getting off a vehicle: バスを降りる (バスをおりる), "get off the bus."
These verbs do not become interchangeable just because English uses "down" in their translations.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese describes rainy weather with a rain noun and this verb: 雨が降る (あめがふる).
The ongoing form 降っている (ふっている) describes rain currently falling, rather than a finished rainfall.
