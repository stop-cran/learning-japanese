# まあまあ - so-so; passable; now now

## Meaning

まあまあ describes something as so-so, passable, or reasonably satisfactory, without enthusiastic praise. As an interjection, it can calm someone: "now, now" or "there, there."

- 試験の結果はまあまあでした。 (しけんのけっかはまあまあでした。) - The exam results were not bad.
- まあまあの出来です。 (まあまあのできです。) - It is a passable result.
- まあまあ、落ち着いてください。 (まあまあ、おちついてください。) - Now, now, please calm down.

The dictionary also records a surprised "oh my!" use, labelled feminine speech for that sense. This label does not apply to every use of まあまあ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[まあ](まあ.md) can also soften a reaction or express surprise. The repeated form has its own evaluative "so-so" use.

[良い](良い.md) (よい) and [いい](いい.md), "good," can overlap a restrained "not bad" evaluation. まあまあ may sound lukewarm where strong praise is expected.

## Distinctive meaning

For a noun modifier, まあまあの出来 is a useful pattern; dictionaries also recognize adjectival uses such as まあまあな出来. In conversation, intonation and context distinguish an evaluation from an attempt to soothe or urge someone along.

"Reasonable" here can mean acceptable in quality. It is not by itself the word for a logically justified argument.
