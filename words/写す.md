# 写す (うつす) — copy; photograph

## Meaning

写す means to copy or reproduce something, and also to photograph it.
It is transitive: mark the text or subject with を. For copying text, に can mark the destination.

- 黒板の字をノートに写します。 (こくばんのじをノートにうつします。) — I copy the writing on the blackboard into my notebook.
- カメラで花を写しました。 (カメラではなをうつしました。) — I photographed the flowers with a camera.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native verb. [写](../kanji/写.md) supplies うつ and the copying or representation meaning; す is okurigana.
It is a godan verb: 写します (うつします), 写して (うつして).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[書く](書く.md) (かく) means to write, without necessarily copying an existing text.
写真を撮る (しゃしんをとる) is a common way to say "take a photograph."
写る (うつる) means to be photographed or appear in a photograph; it is the intransitive partner, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

写す emphasizes reproducing something that is already there, whether words, an image, or a photographic subject.
Compare [映](../kanji/映.md)す (うつす), "project; reflect": the reading is the same, but copying words into a notebook uses 写す.
There is no single direct opposite covering both copying and photography.
