# 確か (たしか) — certain; reliable

## Meaning

確か describes something certain or dependable, such as a fact you can stand behind or information you trust.
It is a な-adjective: 確かな情報 (たしかなじょうほう), "reliable information."
At the end of a sentence, use 確かだ or the polite 確かです.

- その情報は確かです。 (そのじょうほうはたしかです。) — That information is reliable.
- 彼が来たことは確かです。 (かれがきたことはたしかです。) — It is certain that he came.

## How the kanji combine

There is one kanji, [確](../kanji/確.md), carrying the idea of certainty or firmness.
The native Japanese word is たしか; the final か is written in hiragana.
This is not a compound made by adding an independent noun か to a kanji read たし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

不確か (ふたしか), "uncertain; unreliable," provides a direct contrast:
不確かな情報 (ふたしかなじょうほう), "unreliable information."
確かめる (たしかめる), "check; make sure," is the related action, not an interchangeable adjective.
You can check information because it is not yet 確か.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the adjective from the separate adverbial use at the start of a recollection:

- 確か、会議は三時からです。 (たしか、かいぎはさんじからです。) — If I remember correctly, the meeting starts at three.

That opening 確か leaves room for a mistaken memory; it does not guarantee the time.
By contrast, 確かです explicitly presents something as certain.
Learn the construction, not just the English gloss "sure."
