# 和服 (わふく) - traditional Japanese clothing

## Meaning

和服 means traditional Japanese-style clothing, especially kimono.
It is a noun: 和服を着る (わふくをきる), "wear Japanese-style clothing."

- 祖母はお正月に和服を着ます。 (そぼはおしょうがつにわふくをきます。) - My grandmother wears traditional Japanese clothing at New Year.
- この店では和服も洋服も売っています。 (このみせではわふくもようふくもうっています。) - This shop sells both Japanese-style and Western-style clothes.

The repeated も in the second sentence includes both categories.

## How the kanji combine

[和](../kanji/和.md) contributes Japanese style and [服](../kanji/服.md)
clothing. The first qualifies the second; the on readings are ワ + フク.
和 does not mean that the garment must promote harmony.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[着物](着物.md) (きもの) commonly means a kimono and overlaps closely.
[浴衣](浴衣.md) (ゆかた) specifies a light informal garment in this tradition.
[衣服](衣服.md) (いふく) and [服](服.md) (ふく) are broader clothing words.
[洋服](洋服.md) (ようふく) is the useful contrasting category, Western-style clothing.

## Distinctive meaning

The contrast concerns style, not manufacturing location or the wearer's nationality.
A Western-style shirt made in Japan is not thereby 和服.
The noun does not by itself specify a formal occasion or one exact garment material.
