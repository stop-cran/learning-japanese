# 戦う (たたかう) - fight; compete

## Meaning

戦う is an intransitive godan verb for fighting, waging battle, or competing. **相手と戦う** (あいてとたたかう) identifies an opponent. The same verb can describe struggling against adversity.

- 決勝で強いチームと戦います。 (けっしょうでつよいチームとたたかいます。) - We will face a strong team in the final.
- 兵士たちは国を守るために戦いました。 (へいしたちはくにをまもるためにたたかいました。) - The soldiers fought to defend their country.

The past is 戦った (たたかった), the negative is 戦わない (たたかわない), and the て-form is 戦って. In the second example, 国を belongs to 守る, not to 戦う.

## How the kanji combine

[戦](../kanji/戦.md) contributes battle or competition with the native reading たたか-. The final う changes with conjugation. The same reading also has the spelling 闘う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

争う (あらそう) overlaps in contending or disputing. [競争](競争.md) (きょうそう) names competition. Cooperation can contrast with fighting in a particular situation, but not every contest implies hostility.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict especially associates 戦う with warfare and competition, and 闘う with resisting adversity, as in 病気と闘う (びょうきとたたかう), "fight an illness." These are spelling preferences, not a hard division into unrelated verbs. In sports, と can identify the opposing team rather than an ally.
