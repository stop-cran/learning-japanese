# 午前 (ごぜん) — a.m., before noon

## Meaning

午前 is the time from midnight to noon: 午前九時 ごぜんくじ (9 a.m.), 午前中 ごぜんちゅう (during the morning), 午前の授業 (morning classes).

## How the kanji combine

[午](../kanji/午.md) (noon) plus [前](../kanji/前.md) (before): "before noon". Both on'yomi: ゴ + ゼン. It is the mirror of [午後](午後.md) (after noon). The a.m./p.m. word goes before the clock time in Japanese.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [午後](午後.md) ごご (p.m.).
- Related: 朝 あさ (morning, as a part of the day), 正午 しょうご (noon). 午前二時 (ごぜんにじ) is 2 a.m.; English can call this "two in the morning," even though it is normally still night rather than 朝.

## Distinctive meaning

午前 is a precise clock term, so 午前三時 is 3 a.m., in the middle of the night. 朝 is the word for the natural morning.
