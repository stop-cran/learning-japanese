# 引き受ける (ひきうける) - undertake; take over

## Meaning

引き受ける means to undertake something, accept responsibility for it,
or take it over from someone else. It is a transitive ichidan verb:
the task or responsibility takes を.

- 会議の準備を引き受けました。 (かいぎのじゅんびをひきうけました。) - I undertook the preparations for the meeting.
- 父に代わって店の経営を引き受けました。 (ちちにかわってみせのけいえいをひきうけました。) - I took over running the shop from my father.

Forms include 引き受けます, 引き受けた, and 引き受けない.
The dictionary also records guaranteeing or vouching for someone,
not just taking on a job.

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) supplies 引き, the combining form of 引く;
[受](../kanji/受.md) supplies [受ける](受ける.md), receive.
Learn the whole compound as taking something on, rather than requiring
literal pulling. The source form 引受る is marked irregular okurigana
in JMdict; 引受ける is also recorded. The article teaches 引き受ける.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[担当](担当.md)する (たんとうする) concerns being in charge of an
assigned task. [承る](承る.md) (うけたまわる) can humbly describe
undertaking a request. [保証](保証.md)する (ほしょうする) overlaps
in guaranteeing or vouching, not every work assignment.
[任せる](任せる.md) (まかせる) presents the other side: entrusting
the matter to someone.

## Distinctive meaning

Accepting responsibility does not mean the work is finished.
Nor is merely receiving a parcel the same as undertaking its delivery.
The pinned dictionary additionally records contracting a disease; that
is a separate recognition use, not the intended reading of 仕事を引き受ける.
The raw source spelling does not establish that it is the preferred form
for a new learner to write.
