# 開会 (かいかい) - opening of a meeting

## Meaning

開会 is the opening or commencement of a meeting or organized gathering. With する, it can describe either the meeting opening or someone opening it.

- 会議は午前十時に開会します。 (かいぎはごぜんじゅうじにかいかいします。) - The meeting opens at ten in the morning.
- これから会議を開会します。 (これからかいぎをかいかいします。) - We will now open the meeting.

In the second pattern, を marks the meeting being opened. 開会の挨拶 (かいかいのあいさつ) means "opening remarks."

## How the kanji combine

[開](../kanji/開.md), "open," acts on [会](../kanji/会.md), "meeting; gathering." Both use カイ, giving かいかい. The repeated sound comes from two different characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[開始](開始.md) (かいし) means "start; commencement" more broadly. 閉会 (へいかい), "closing of a meeting," is the event-stage contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

開会 highlights the start, not the entire time a meeting takes place. [会議](会議.md) (かいぎ) names the meeting or conference itself. Opening a door or a shop is not automatically 開会 merely because English uses "open."
