# 一般 (いっぱん) - general; ordinary

## Meaning

一般 refers to what is general, ordinary, or shared beyond a special group. It is a noun that can modify another noun with の: 一般の人 (いっぱんのひと), "ordinary people; the general public." 一般に means "generally."

- この図書館は一般の人も利用できます。 (このとしょかんはいっぱんのひともりようできます。) - Members of the general public can also use this library.
- 一般に、冬は夏より気温が低いです。 (いっぱんに、ふゆはなつよりきおんがひくいです。) - Generally, air temperatures are lower in winter than in summer.

You will also meet 一般的な (いっぱんてきな), "general; usual," with 的 added.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) means "one," and 般 contributes a kind or category. Together the established compound concerns the general or common category, not a special case. Learn the whole reading いっぱん, including the small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[普通](普通.md) (ふつう) overlaps with "ordinary" or "usual." 一般 can specifically contrast the public with specialists or members of a restricted group. 特殊 (とくしゅ), "special; exceptional," is a useful opposite; [特別](特別.md) (とくべつ) also supplies a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

一般に introduces a generalization, not a claim that every individual case is identical. 一般 does not mean politically "liberal" or "broad-minded": that misleading English gloss is not the meaning taught here. In admissions or notices, check which special group is being contrasted with 一般.
