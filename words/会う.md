# 会う (あう) — to meet

## Meaning

会う is to meet a person: 友達に会う (meet a friend), 駅で会いましょう (let's meet at the station), 久しぶりに会う ひさしぶりにあう (meet after a long time), また会いましょう (see you again).
The person met is marked with に (or と for a mutual meeting): 先生に会う, 彼と会う.

## How the kanji combine

[会](../kanji/会.md) with the kun reading あ + okurigana う. Related: 出会う であう (to come across), 会わせる あわせる (to introduce, let meet).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 会見する かいけんする (hold a press meeting, formal), 面会 めんかい (a visit, interview).
- Antonym: 別れる わかれる (to part).
- Homophone: 合う あう (to match, to fit), 遭う あう (to meet with, as in 事故に遭う).

## Distinctive meaning

会う is meeting a person, usually by plan or encounter. 合う is for things that fit (サイズが合う). 遭う is used for unwanted events: 事故に遭う (have an accident).
