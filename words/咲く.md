# 咲く (さく) — bloom

## Meaning

咲く means **to bloom, blossom, or flower**. The flowers or flowering plant are the subject, commonly marked with が.

- 庭に花が咲きました。 (にわにはながさきました。) — Flowers bloomed in the garden.
- 桜が咲いています。 (さくらがさいています。) — The cherry blossoms are in bloom.

It is a godan verb: 咲きます (さきます), 咲かない (さかない), 咲いた (さいた). Notice the sound change in the past and て-form, 咲いて (さいて).

## How the kanji combine

咲 writes さ and く completes this native verb. The kanji spelling identifies flowering; it is not a general way to write every verb pronounced さく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

花が開く (はながひらく), “flowers open,” can describe a similar event. 散る (ちる) describes petals falling or scattering; 枯れる (かれる) describes withering.

## Distinctive meaning

咲く presents the plant's flowering, not a person's action on it. 咲いている commonly describes the resulting state, “in bloom,” rather than insisting that the petals are visibly opening at that moment.
