# 触れる (ふれる) - touch; touch on

## Meaning

触れる is an ichidan verb: 触れます, 触れて, 触れた. Ordinary touching is **intransitive**, with に marking what is touched.

- 指が冷たい水に触れました。 (ゆびがつめたいみずにふれました。) - My finger touched the cold water.
- 展示品に手を触れないでください。 (てんじひんにてをふれないでください。) - Please do not touch the exhibits.

The second sentence uses a distinct construction: **～に手を触れる**, "touch something with one's hand." The body part takes を; the exhibit still takes に. This does not license freely replacing 水に触れる with 水を触れる.

The word also means coming into contact with an experience, mentioning a topic, or violating a rule:

- 先生はその問題に触れました。 (せんせいはそのもんだいにふれました。) - The teacher touched on that issue.

異文化に触れる (いぶんかにふれる) means "experience another culture"; 法律に触れる (ほうりつにふれる) means "violate the law."

## How the kanji combine

触 contributes contact or touching. In the spelling 触れる, 触 is read ふ and れる is written in kana. It is one written component, not a two-part compound explaining each extended sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[触る](触る.md) (さわる) also means touch and overlaps in physical-contact contexts. [述べる](述べる.md) (のべる), "state," is a comparison for discussing a topic, but mentioning a subject does not necessarily mean explaining it fully.

## Distinctive meaning

The common thread is contact, literal or figurative. Select the translation from the object of に: water, a culture, a topic, and a law require different English wording. The ordinary active 触れる is not itself the passive "be touched."
