# ぎざぎざ — serrated; notched

## Meaning

ぎざぎざ describes a jagged edge or repeated small notches.

- この葉は、ふちがぎざぎざしている。
  — This leaf has a serrated edge.
- ぎざぎざの刃が、厚紙をまっすぐ切った。
  — The serrated blade cut through the thick paper cleanly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぎざぎざ emphasizes a repeated tooth-like edge, not a single crack or a smooth outline.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can describe an edge, indentation, or serrated surface. It does not say whether the
object is sharp enough to cut or safe to touch.
