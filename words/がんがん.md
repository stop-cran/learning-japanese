# がんがん — loud banging; a pounding headache

## Meaning

Loud, forceful repeated sound, or a pounding sensation in the head.

- 隣（となり）の部屋で音楽ががんがん鳴（な）っている。 — Music is blasting in the next room.
- 今日は頭ががんがんする。 — I have a pounding headache today.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[どんどん](どんどん.md) overlaps in repeated banging. [ずきずき](ずきずき.md) describes throbbing pain more generally, while [ばりばり](ばりばり.md) can overlap in noisy or vigorous activity.

## Distinctive meaning

頭ががんがんする does not imply an audible sound inside the head. In casual speech, がんがん働く（はたらく）means working flat out; it need not involve literal banging.
