# 対立 (たいりつ) - opposition; confrontation

## Meaning

対立 is opposition, confrontation or antagonism between people, positions
or interests. With する it is intransitive: the opposing sides take が
and と, rather than a direct object marked を.

- 二人の意見が対立しています。
  (ふたりのいけんがたいりつしています。) - The two people's views are opposed.
- 会社側と対立しました。
  (かいしゃがわとたいりつしました。) - We came into conflict with the company side.

対立する意見 means opposing views. 対立の原因 (たいりつのげんいん)
means the cause of the confrontation.

## How the kanji combine

[対](../kanji/対.md) contributes facing or opposition, and
[立](../kanji/立.md) standing. タイ + リツ suggests sides standing against
one another. This is a meaning association, not proof that physical standing
is required.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[反対](反対.md) (はんたい) can mean opposition more broadly.
[反抗](反抗.md) (はんこう) and [抵抗](抵抗.md) (ていこう) emphasize
defiance or resistance. [衝突](衝突.md) (しょうとつ) has a figurative
clash sense, while [争う](争う.md) (あらそう) can describe disputing or
contending. These are overlapping uses, not unrestricted substitutions.
協調 (きょうちょう), cooperation or coordination, provides a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Opposing positions need not involve a physical fight or a shouted quarrel.
[対照](対照.md) (たいしょう), comparison or contrast, can instead be
an observer's comparison of things that are not fighting or resisting one
another. A difference alone does not establish 対立.
