# 香水 (こうすい) - perfume; scent

## Meaning

香水 is perfume or scent, especially the fragrant liquid used on the body.
香水をつける means "put on perfume"; the perfume takes を.

- 出かける前に香水を少しつけました。 (でかけるまえにこうすいをすこしつけました。) - I put on a little perfume before going out.
- この香水は花の香りがします。 (このこうすいははなのかおりがします。) - This perfume has a floral fragrance.

## How the kanji combine

[香](../kanji/香.md) contributes fragrance, and [水](../kanji/水.md)
contributes water or liquid. The modifier-plus-head combination suggests
a fragrant liquid, not every naturally occurring smell.
The on readings コウ + スイ give こうすい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[香り](香り.md) (かおり) means an aroma or fragrance.
[におい](におい.md) means a smell or scent, pleasant or unpleasant.
Perfume produces a 香り or におい; those words can describe the resulting
smell without naming the bottled product.

## Distinctive meaning

The English word "scent" covers both a fragrant product and a smell.
The second example distinguishes the product from its fragrance rather
than claiming that their meanings never overlap.
This card teaches こうすい. Daijisen separately records Buddhist 香水
read こうずい; that reading and ritual sense are not imported into these examples.
