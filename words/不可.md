# 不可 (ふか) - unacceptable; not permitted

## Meaning

不可 describes something as wrong, improper, or unacceptable. It also means not permitted or not possible, and can name a failing grade. It is a noun or adjectival expression, including a compact label after an activity.

- この商品は返品不可です。 (このしょうひんはへんぴんふかです。) - This item cannot be returned.
- その方法は不可と判断されました。 (そのほうほうはふかとはんだんされました。) - That method was judged unacceptable.

As a grade, 不可 indicates failure: 成績は不可でした (せいせきはふかでした), "The grade was a fail." A grading system's exact numerical thresholds are not supplied by the word.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) contributes negation, and 可 contributes acceptability or possibility. Together they express "not acceptable/possible." The reading is フ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[禁止](禁止.md) (きんし) explicitly names prohibition. 不可能 (ふかのう) emphasizes impossibility. 不可 can concern either permission or feasibility, so context matters.

[可](可.md) (か), "acceptable; permitted," provides the direct contrast in labels or evaluations.

## Distinctive meaning

不可 is useful in notices, conditions, and judgments. It is not an all-purpose conversational replacement for [悪い](悪い.md) (わるい), "bad." 返品不可 states a return restriction; it does not claim that physically moving the item back is impossible. Read the surrounding policy or explanation to understand the kind of restriction.
