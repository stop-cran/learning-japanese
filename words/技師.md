# 技師 (ぎし) - engineer; technical officer

## Meaning

技師 is a noun naming an engineer or technical professional. In a job title, the field and organization help determine whether English "engineer," "technician," or "technical officer" fits best. It is not a label for anyone who happens to be good at a practical task.

- 父は工場で技師として働いています。 (ちちはこうじょうでぎしとしてはたらいています。) - My father works as an engineer at a factory.
- 技師に機械の点検を依頼しました。 (ぎしにきかいのてんけんをいらいしました。) - We asked an engineer to inspect the machine.

として identifies the professional role. In the second example, に marks the person asked and を marks the requested inspection.

## How the kanji combine

技 contributes skill or technique; [師](../kanji/師.md) identifies a skilled practitioner. The first character specifies the technical expertise of the professional. Read the compound with ギ + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[技術](技術.md) (ぎじゅつ) names technology or technical skill, not the person. 技術者 (ぎじゅつしゃ) is another designation for a technical specialist. 職人 (しょくにん) emphasizes a craftsperson's trade and workmanship rather than being an exact synonym for every 技師.

## Distinctive meaning

技師 occurs within professional titles, but the word alone does not establish a universal qualification or job grade. Read the complete title before translating it. The similar-looking [医師](医師.md) (いし) instead identifies a medical doctor.
