# おいでになる - be present respectfully

## Meaning

おいでになる is a respectful expression for being somewhere. It can also mean
coming or going, depending on context. Use it for someone you are honoring,
not as the normal way to describe your own whereabouts.

- 先生は研究室においでになります。 (せんせいはけんきゅうしつにおいでになります。) - The teacher is in the office.
- 明日、こちらにおいでになりますか。 (あした、こちらにおいでになりますか。) - Will you come here tomorrow?

## How the kanji combine

The usual kana expression contains おいで followed by に and なる.
The spelling お出でになる is also recorded, but kana is sufficient.
Inflect the final godan verb: おいでになった, おいでになって.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いらっしゃる](いらっしゃる.md) covers the same respectful being/coming/going range.
いる, 来る (くる), and 行く (いく) are the corresponding ordinary verbs.
No single antonym covers all three meanings.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate になる literally as "become" here: the whole construction
functions as an honorific verb expression. A location with に can indicate
where someone is or their destination; surrounding context resolves that difference.
