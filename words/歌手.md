# 歌手 (かしゅ) - singer

## Meaning

歌手 is a singer: a person identified by singing as a role or occupation. It is a noun, not the verb "sing."

- 姉は歌手になりたいと言っています。 (あねはかしゅになりたいといっています。) - My older sister says she wants to become a singer.
- 好きな歌手のコンサートに行きました。 (すきなかしゅのコンサートにいきました。) - I went to a concert by a singer I like.

Use 歌手になる for "become a singer" and 歌手の before a noun associated with the singer. The word itself does not specify the person's gender or musical genre.

## How the kanji combine

[歌](../kanji/歌.md) contributes singing or song, and [手](../kanji/手.md) identifies a person performing an activity here. The relation is activity plus performer: "singing person." The on readings are カ + シュ; 手 does not refer to the singer's literal hand.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[歌う](歌う.md) (うたう) is the action of singing. Someone can 歌う without being introduced as a 歌手. シンガー is a loanword for singer; neither term supplies a natural antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

歌手 names the performer, while [歌](歌.md) (うた) names the song. Compare 歌手を知っています, "I know the singer," with 歌を知っています, "I know the song." Sharing 歌 does not make these nouns interchangeable.
