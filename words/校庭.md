# 校庭 (こうてい) - schoolyard; school grounds

## Meaning

校庭 is a schoolyard, school playground, or the school's outdoor grounds.
It can be translated as "campus" when that means the outdoor school grounds,
not necessarily the institution's complete collection of buildings.

- 子どもたちが校庭で遊んでいます。 (こどもたちがこうていであそんでいます。) - The children are playing in the schoolyard.
- 校庭に大きな木があります。 (こうていにおおきなきがあります。) - There is a large tree in the school grounds.

Use で for an activity's location and に for the location of something
that exists, as illustrated above.

## How the kanji combine

[校](../kanji/校.md) contributes school, and [庭](../kanji/庭.md)
contributes yard or garden. School qualifies the yard.
The on readings コウ + テイ give こうてい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[キャンパス](キャンパス.md) can name a campus or its premises more broadly.
[庭](庭.md) (にわ) is a yard or garden without the school qualification.
[校舎](校舎.md) (こうしゃ), "school building," is a useful location contrast,
not an interchangeable name for the outdoor yard.

## Distinctive meaning

校庭 need not be a carefully planted garden: it can include a school's
athletic or play area. Do not infer its surface or equipment from the word.
The homophone [肯定](肯定.md) (こうてい), "affirmation," is unrelated;
the spelling and context distinguish them.
