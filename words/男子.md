# 男子 (だんし) - boy; male

## Meaning

男子 can mean a boy, or a male person in a category such as a school group or sporting event. It is a noun. The context determines age: 男子 is not restricted to young men.

- このクラスには男子が十五人います。 (このクラスにはだんしがじゅうごにんいます。) - There are fifteen boys in this class.
- 男子マラソンは明日行われます。 (だんしマラソンはあしたおこなわれます。) - The men's marathon will take place tomorrow.

The sports example shows why "boy" is not the right English translation in every setting.

## How the kanji combine

[男](../kanji/男.md) contributes "male," and [子](../kanji/子.md) contributes "child" or a person-designating element. Here the established compound can include adults. The on readings are ダン + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[女子](女子.md) (じょし) provides the corresponding female category in contexts such as sports. [男](男.md) (おとこ) is a broader standalone word for a man or male; 男の子 (おとこのこ) specifically refers to a boy.

## Distinctive meaning

男子 is useful for group labels and categories, not just an individual person's age description. 子 in a compound does not guarantee that the referent is a child. Similarly, the source gloss "youth; young man" should not be treated as an age restriction applying to every use.
