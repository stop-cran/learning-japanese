# 日中 (にっちゅう) — daytime; Japan–China

## Meaning

日中 has two common meanings with the same reading:

- **Daytime**: 日中は暑い (it's hot during the day), 日中の気温 (the daytime temperature).
- **Japan and China** (日 + 中 as country abbreviations): 日中関係 にっちゅうかんけい (Japan–China relations).

Context decides. There is also the reading ひなか for "daytime, in broad daylight" (日中に出歩く).

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) (day, sun) plus [中](../kanji/中.md) (in, during): "during the day". The ちゅう reading is the one used for "in the midst of", whereas the じゅう
reading means "throughout": 一日中 いちにちじゅう (all day long).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 昼間 ひるま (daytime, everyday), 昼 ひる (noon, daytime), 日中 is more written and formal.
- Antonym: 夜間 やかん (nighttime, formal), 夜 よる, 夜中 よなか (the middle of the night).

## Distinctive meaning

日中 is "during daylight hours" — a period, often in weather reports and notices. 昼間 is a plain word. 昼 can narrow to midday.
