# 日中 (にっちゅう) — daytime; Japan–China

## Meaning

日中 has two meanings with the same reading にっちゅう:

- **Daytime**, as a noun or adverb: 日中は暑いです。 (にっちゅうはあついです。) — It is hot during the day.
  日中の[気温](気温.md) (にっちゅうのきおん) means "the daytime temperature."
- **Japan and China**, a noun using country abbreviations: 日中関係について学んでいます。
  (にっちゅうかんけいについてまなんでいます。) — I am studying Japan–China relations.

Context decides. There is also the separately recorded reading ひなか for "daytime; broad daylight."
Do not transfer the Japan–China meaning to that reading.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) (day, sun) plus [中](../kanji/中.md) (in, during) gives "during the day."
For the country sense, 日 abbreviates 日本 and 中 abbreviates 中国.
Learn the reading contrast in the complete words: 日中 is にっちゅう, while 一日中
(いちにちじゅう) means "all day long." This is not a universal rule for every compound containing 中.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: [昼間](昼間.md) ひるま (daytime), [昼](昼.md) ひる (noon, daytime).
  These overlap with the daytime sense; they do not mean Japan and China.
- Contrast: 夜間 やかん (nighttime), [夜](夜.md) よる (night).
  [夜中](夜中.md) よなか is the middle of the night, a narrower period rather than an exact antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

In its time sense, 日中 is "during daylight hours" — a period useful in weather reports and notices.
昼間 overlaps, while 昼 can narrow to midday. The separate country sense is selected by expressions
such as 日中関係; neither the spelling nor the reading alone decides between the two.
