# インク (インク) - ink

## Meaning

インク is a noun meaning ink, used in writing or printing. It names the material rather than the pen, printer, or cartridge containing it.

- ペンのインクがなくなりました。 (ペンのインクがなくなりました。) - The pen has run out of ink.
- 青いインクで名前を書きました。 (あおいインクでなまえをかきました。) - I wrote my name in blue ink.

In the second example, で marks the material used for writing. インクを補充する (インクをほじゅうする) means to refill the ink, and インクの色 (インクのいろ) means its color.

## How the kanji combine

インク is written in katakana and has no kanji components. Pronounce the three morae イ・ン・ク, including the final ク rather than stopping at the English consonant cluster. The spelling has neither a long-vowel mark nor a small ッ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

墨 (すみ) refers to traditional ink used in contexts such as calligraphy and ink painting; the terms can overlap in translation. 絵の具 (えのぐ) is paint or coloring material for painting, not a replacement for インク in a printer description. [ペン](ペン.md) names an instrument that may contain ink.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not specify whether ink is black, colored, washable, or permanent. Those properties require additional description. Similarly, replacing a cartridge and replenishing ink describe related but different actions. A material has no natural lexical antonym; an empty pen is a state of the instrument, not an opposite substance.
