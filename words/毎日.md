# 毎日 (まいにち) — every day

## Meaning

毎日 is "every day, daily": 毎日日本語を勉強します (I study Japanese every day), 毎日の生活 (daily life), 毎日新聞 まいにちしんぶん (a newspaper's name).

## How the kanji combine

[毎](../kanji/毎.md) (every) plus [日](../kanji/日.md) (day): "each day". Both use on'yomi, マイ + ニチ. The word works as a noun and as an adverb without a particle.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 日々 ひび (day by day, more literary), 連日 れんじつ (day after day, news style).
- Related: [毎年](毎年.md), [毎月](毎月.md), 毎週 まいしゅう, 毎朝 まいあさ, 毎晩 まいばん.

## Distinctive meaning

Neutral and everyday. 日々 is more reflective, for example 日々の努力 (daily efforts).
