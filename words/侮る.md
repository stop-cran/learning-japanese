# 侮る (あなどる) — look down on; underestimate

## Meaning

侮る means to disdain or look down on someone or something, or to make light of it.

- 相手の力を侮ってはいけません。 (あいてのちからをあなどってはいけません。) — You must not underestimate your opponent’s strength.
- 彼は危険を侮って、準備をしませんでした。 (かれはきけんをあなどって、じゅんびをしませんでした。) — He underestimated the danger and did not prepare.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji verb. The character [侮](../kanji/侮.md) uses its kun reading あなど.る; the written ending る follows the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

JMdict glosses include “to look down on,” “to make light of,” and “to despise.”

## Distinctive meaning

The examples use a person's ability and a danger as the targets of 侮る.
