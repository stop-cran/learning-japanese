# 組 (くみ) — group; set

## Meaning

組 is a group of people, and can also be a set of things.
It can count groups: 一組 (ひとくみ) is one group.
The number of groups and the number of people in each group are different.

- 二人一組で練習してください。 (ふたりひとくみでれんしゅうしてください。) — Please practise in pairs.
- 二つの組に分かれてください。 (ふたつのくみにわかれてください。) — Please divide into two groups.

In the first example, 二人 says how many people there are per 一組.
The second example does not specify the size of either group.

組 also names a school class: 私はA組です (わたしはエーぐみです), "I am in Class A."
Here the class-name suffix is read ぐみ; this does not change the standalone reading くみ or the group-counting 一組 (ひとくみ).

## How the kanji combine

The single [組](../kanji/組.md) is read with the native noun reading くみ.
The related verb [組む](組む.md) (くむ) can mean putting together or joining forces.
Do not use 組 by itself as though it were the dictionary form of that verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

班 (はん) also names a group or team.
The overlap does not make every word containing 組 replaceable with 班:
[番組](番組.md) (ばんぐみ), for example, is the established word for a television or other program.
There is no single useful antonym covering all the group and set senses.

## Distinctive meaning

A 組 is treated as a unit, but need not contain exactly two members.
二人一組 explicitly states that each group has two people; 一組 alone does not fix its size.
The voiced reading ぐみ appears in 番組, but 一組 has くみ.
Learn the reading with the expression rather than automatically voicing it after any preceding element.
