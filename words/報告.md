# 報告 (ほうこく) — report

## Meaning

報告 is a report or the act of reporting information.
Use 報告する for "report": the recipient can take に and the content can take を.
A 報告 may be spoken or written; the word alone does not specify a document.

- 先生に結果を報告しました。 (せんせいにけっかをほうこくしました。) — I reported the results to the teacher.
- 明日の会議で報告します。 (あしたのかいぎでほうこくします。) — I will give a report at tomorrow's meeting.

A full reported statement can instead take と:
無事に着いたと報告しました (ぶじについたとほうこくしました), "I reported that I had arrived safely."

## How the kanji combine

[報](../kanji/報.md) concerns reporting or news, and [告](../kanji/告.md) concerns telling or informing.
These related meanings reinforce one another: information is made known to someone.
The on readings are ホウ + コク; preserve the long vowel in ほう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[連絡](連絡.md) (れんらく) is contact or communication: it can be a message arranging a meeting rather than a report of findings.
[説明](説明.md) (せつめい) is explanation, which helps someone understand a matter.
A report can include an explanation, but giving results and explaining how something works are different purposes.
There is no single useful opposite covering every kind of 報告.

## Distinctive meaning

報告 presents information for someone else to receive, often about an event, result, or situation.
In the first example, the teacher is the recipient, not the thing being reported.
The content can be omitted when the conversation already makes it clear, as in the second example.
