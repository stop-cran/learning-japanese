# 問答 (もんどう) - question-and-answer exchange; dialogue

## Meaning

問答 is an exchange of questions and answers, or a dialogue.
It is a noun and a する-verb.
問答を交わす (もんどうをかわす) means to exchange questions and answers;
Nについて問答する means to discuss N through an exchange.

- 先生と生徒の問答が続きました。 (せんせいとせいとのもんどうがつづきました。) - The question-and-answer exchange between the teacher and the student continued.
- 二人は人生について問答しました。 (ふたりはじんせいについてもんどうしました。) - The two had a dialogue about life.

The word can also concern discussion or argument, not only a formal classroom exercise.

## How the kanji combine

[問](../kanji/問.md) contributes asking and [答](../kanji/答.md) answering.
Their complementary actions combine into an exchange.
The established on-based reading is モン + ドウ;
learn the voiced どう rather than reading the second part とう here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[質問](質問.md) (しつもん) identifies an inquiry;
[回答](回答.md) (かいとう) identifies a response.
問答 names their exchange rather than just either individual part.
[会話](会話.md) (かいわ) is conversation more broadly,
while [議論](議論.md) (ぎろん) overlaps in discussion or argument.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every conversation consists of a structured sequence of questions and answers.
Conversely, 問答 is not restricted to an exam or to written questions.
禅問答 (ぜんもんどう) concerns Zen dialogues; that specialized use is not
the only setting in which the general word can occur.
