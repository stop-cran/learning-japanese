# 二人 (ふたり) — two people

## Meaning

二人 counts two people: 二人で食べる (eat together, the two of us), 二人きり ふたりきり (just the two of us), 二人とも ふたりとも (both).

## How the kanji combine

[二](../kanji/二.md) (two) plus [人](../kanji/人.md) (person). The reading ふたり is a whole-word irregular reading: ふた is the native number two, but り is not the usual reading of 人.
Like 一人 ひとり, it is exceptional; 三人 さんにん is regular.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 両人 りょうにん (both persons, formal), 二名 にめい (two people, formal).
- Contrast: [一人](一人.md) ひとり (one person).

## Distinctive meaning

二人 is also "a couple" in context: 二人の関係 (the relationship between the two). Do not read it as にじん. Use ふたり for the ordinary independent count taught here. The dictionary also records ににん; it appears in compounds such as 二人三脚 ににんさんきゃく (three-legged race).
