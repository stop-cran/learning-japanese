# 絶対 (ぜったい) — absolutely; definitely

## Meaning

絶対 emphasizes certainty or an uncompromising intention. This article focuses on the adverbial
use, often 絶対に (ぜったいに), before a statement or action. The following predicate determines
whether the speaker is insisting that something will happen or will not happen.

- 明日は絶対に行きます。 (あしたはぜったいにいきます。) — I will definitely go tomorrow.
- この約束は絶対に忘れません。
  (このやくそくはぜったいにわすれません。)
  — I absolutely will not forget this promise.

Before a noun, **絶対の** means "absolute; unconditional": 絶対の信頼を寄せています
(ぜったいのしんらいをよせています), "I place absolute trust in them."
This の construction differs from adverbial 絶対に; do not assume that な is the connector here.

## How the kanji combine

[絶](../kanji/絶.md) has both cutting-off and beyond-comparison meanings;
[対](../kanji/対.md) concerns facing or opposition. Learn the established compound's meaning
as "absolute; absolutely," rather than translating the characters into a physical action.
ゼツ + タイ becomes ぜったい: hold the small っ before た.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[必ず](必ず.md) (かならず), "without fail; certainly," can also express a firm commitment.
[きっと](きっと.md), "surely; most likely," can express a confident expectation.
These overlap, but do not attach an exact probability to a speaker's choice of adverb.

## Distinctive meaning

絶対 makes the speaker's insistence prominent; it is not evidence that the claim is objectively true.
With a negative predicate, translate the whole combination: 絶対に行かない (ぜったいにいかない)
is "definitely will not go," not "go absolutely."
