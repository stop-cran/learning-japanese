# 光景 (こうけい) - scene; sight

## Meaning

光景 is a noun for a scene, sight, or spectacle as seen by an observer. It can include people and what they are doing, not just the landscape behind them. Use **光景を見る**, "see a scene," or describe what is seen with a clause before 光景.

- 子供たちが遊ぶ光景を眺めていました。 (こどもたちがあそぶこうけいをながめていました。) - I was watching the children at play.
- あの光景は今でも忘れられません。 (あのこうけいはいまでもわすれられません。) - I still cannot forget that scene.

The first sentence presents the children's activity as a scene being observed. In the second, the context supplies whether the remembered sight was pleasant or distressing.

## How the kanji combine

[光](../kanji/光.md) contributes light, and [景](../kanji/景.md) contributes a view or scene. Together they name what appears to the eyes. The on readings are コウ + ケイ; a 光景 need not be brightly lit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[景色](景色.md) (けしき) and [風景](風景.md) (ふうけい) overlap in views and scenery. [場面](場面.md) (ばめん) can identify a scene or stage in an unfolding event, including a story.

## Distinctive meaning

光景 emphasizes the sight as a whole. It can describe a routine scene, an impressive spectacle, or a shocking sight; "beautiful" is not built into the word. There is no single useful opposite for all these uses.
