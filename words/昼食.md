# 昼食 (ちゅうしょく) - lunch; midday meal

## Meaning

昼食 is lunch, the meal associated with the middle of the day. It is a noun: **昼食を食べる** means to eat lunch, and 昼食を取る (ちゅうしょくをとる) means to have lunch.

- 会社の食堂で昼食を取りました。 (かいしゃのしょくどうでちゅうしょくをとりました。) - I had lunch in the company cafeteria.
- 昼食の後で散歩しましょう。 (ちゅうしょくのあとでさんぽしましょう。) - Let's take a walk after lunch.

昼食の時間 (ちゅうしょくのじかん) names lunchtime; it does not specify a particular menu.

## How the kanji combine

[昼](../kanji/昼.md) contributes daytime or midday, while [食](../kanji/食.md) contributes eating or a meal. The first component identifies which meal. The on readings are チュウ + ショク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

昼ご飯 (ひるごはん) and ランチ also mean lunch. 昼食 fits schedules and descriptions of meals without being restricted to those settings. 朝食 (ちょうしょく), "breakfast," and 夕食 (ゆうしょく), "evening meal," are other meals, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

昼食 is the meal, whereas [正午](正午.md) (しょうご) is the clock time noon. Lunch can be eaten before or after twelve. Likewise, [昼](昼.md) by itself can refer to a broader part of the day; the character 食 makes the meal meaning explicit.
