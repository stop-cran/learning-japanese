# あるいは (あるいは) - or; possibly

## Meaning

あるいは connects alternatives as "or." It also works as an adverb meaning
"perhaps" or "possibly." These uses need different English translations:
one presents choices, while the other presents an uncertain possibility.

- 電話、あるいはメールで連絡してください。 (でんわ、あるいはメールでれんらくしてください。) - Please contact us by telephone or email.
- あるいは、彼はもう帰ったのかもしれません。 (あるいは、かれはもうかえったのかもしれません。) - Perhaps he has already gone home.

In the first example, で belongs to the means of contact. In the second,
かもしれません makes the speaker's uncertainty explicit.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses the usual kana spelling. 或いは is a dictionary-listed kanji
spelling, but there is no kanji compound to divide here. The final written は
is pronounced わ: the spoken word is aru-i-wa, despite its spelling あるいは.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[または](または.md) also connects alternatives. [それとも](それとも.md)
is useful when asking which of two possibilities applies. [多分](多分.md)
(たぶん) overlaps with the uncertainty use, not with the conjunction "or."
There is no useful single-word antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Check what follows the word. A list such as 電話あるいはメール presents
alternatives; a whole uncertain statement may instead mean "possibly."
Do not mechanically insert "or" whenever you see あるいは.
