# 冠 (かんむり) - crown; ceremonial cap

## Meaning

冠, read かんむり here, names a crown or diadem, and also traditional ceremonial headgear worn by courtiers or Shinto clergy. It does not mean every everyday hat.

- 王様は金の冠をかぶっていました。
  (おうさまはきんのかんむりをかぶっていました。) - The king was wearing a gold crown.

In kanji terminology, かんむり is a component at the top of a character. For example, 宀 is called うかんむり; this use is not a physical item of clothing.

## How the kanji combine

This is one kanji with the native reading かんむり. Learn the whole pronunciation rather than dividing it into separate sound values. The same written character also has other readings with different uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[帽子](帽子.md) (ぼうし) is the broader everyday hat or cap word. 冠 can also refer to a name or title attached to a program or event, including named sponsorship; [名前](名前.md) (なまえ) is not restricted to that use.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list's English "first; best" belongs to the separate reading **かん**, not かんむり. Recognize 冠たる (かんたる), "foremost; peerless," as in 世界に冠たる技術 (せかいにかんたるぎじゅつ), "world-leading technology." This is supplementary reading-specific teaching, not a new meaning pronounced かんむり.
