# ガソリンスタンド (ガソリンスタンド) — gas station; petrol station

## Meaning

ガソリンスタンド means a **gas station, petrol station, or filling station**, where motorists buy fuel.

近くのガソリンスタンドでガソリンを入れました。 (ちかくのがそりんすたんどでがそりんをいれました。) — I filled up at a nearby gas station.

The location takes で when describing an activity there. Use に with a movement verb when it is the destination.

## How the kanji combine

The katakana compound combines ガソリン, “gasoline,” with スタンド, “stand.” JMdict labels the combination **Japanese-made English**. “Gasoline stand” explains its components but is not the usual English name for the place.

## Synonyms and antonyms

給油所 (きゅうゆじょ) is another term for a filling station, often seen in more formal wording. 駐車場 (ちゅうしゃじょう) is a parking area; it need not sell fuel.

## Distinctive meaning

The word names the business or facility, not one fuel pump. It does not tell you whether service is self-service or attended, which fuel a vehicle needs, or which additional services are offered.
