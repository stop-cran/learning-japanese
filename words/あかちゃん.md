# あかちゃん (あかちゃん) — baby

## Meaning

あかちゃん means a baby or infant. It is an ordinary, affectionate word used by adults as well as children.

あかちゃんが寝ています。（あかちゃんがねています。） — The baby is sleeping.

## How the kanji combine

The usual mixed spelling is 赤ちゃん. 赤 means "red," while ちゃん is an affectionate suffix. Learn the whole word as "baby," rather than translating those parts literally. This page retains the all-kana spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

赤ん坊（あかんぼう） also means "baby." 子供（こども） means "child" more broadly; 大人（おとな）, "adult," is a useful age contrast, not an exact opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

あかちゃん does not specify the baby's sex. It is less clinical than 乳児（にゅうじ）, "infant," as used in medical or administrative contexts.
