# 青い (あおい) — blue; sometimes green

## Meaning

青い is an い-adjective: 青い空 (a blue sky), 青い海 (a blue sea), 顔が青い (a pale face, as from fear or illness). It also covers the green of 青信号 あおしんごう (a green traffic light) and unripe or young things (青い果物 あおいくだもの, unripe fruit).

## How the kanji combine

[青](../kanji/青.md) with okurigana い: あお + い. The noun is 青 あお, 真っ青 まっさお is "deep blue" or "ghostly pale", and the on reading セイ appears in [青年](青年.md) せいねん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 緑 みどり (green, a separate word), ブルー (blue).
- Contrast: [赤い](赤い.md) あかい.

## Distinctive meaning

緑 (みどり) is the general word for green; 青い has conventional green uses such as the traffic-light and unripe-fruit examples above. Its pale-face meaning is illustrated by 顔が青い (かおがあおい, one's face is pale). Separately, the related noun 青二才 (あおにさい) means "greenhorn; inexperienced youngster" and can be disparaging or used modestly about oneself; it does not mean a pale face.
