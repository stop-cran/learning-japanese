# 地下水 (ちかすい) - groundwater

## Meaning

地下水 is groundwater: water present below the ground in spaces or
cracks in soil and rock.
It names the water, not a well or an underground room.

- この井戸は地下水をくみ上げています。
  (このいどはちかすいをくみあげています。) - This well draws up groundwater.
- 地下水の水位が下がりました。
  (ちかすいのすいいがさがりました。) - The groundwater level has fallen.

地下水を調べる (ちかすいをしらべる) means "investigate the groundwater."
の connects it to a property such as 水位, water level.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) and [下](../kanji/下.md) form
[地下](地下.md) (ちか), underground.
That unit qualifies [水](../kanji/水.md), water:
チ + カ + スイ gives ちかすい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[井戸](井戸.md) (いど) is a well used to obtain water.
[泉](泉.md) (いずみ) is a spring, where water emerges.
These are related sources or features, not interchangeable names for groundwater.
Surface water provides a useful contrast in discussing where water is located.

## Distinctive meaning

地下水 is not necessarily an underground river or a large open cavern of water.
It can occupy small spaces in earth materials.
The word alone also gives no guarantee that the water is safe to drink;
location and water quality are different questions.
