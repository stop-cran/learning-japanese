# 熱中 (ねっちゅう) - absorption; enthusiasm

## Meaning

熱中 names being absorbed in, engrossed in, or enthusiastic about something.
The usual verbal construction is **activity + に熱中する**.
熱中している describes an ongoing state of absorption or enthusiasm.

- 子供たちはゲームに熱中しています。 (こどもたちはゲームにねっちゅうしています。) - The children are absorbed in a game.
- 研究に熱中して、時間を忘れていました。 (けんきゅうにねっちゅうして、じかんをわすれていました。) - I became so absorbed in my research that I lost track of time.

熱中する is intransitive: use に, not を, to identify the object of this enthusiasm.
Past 熱中した can also modify a noun, as in 熱中した趣味 (ねっちゅうしたしゅみ), "a hobby I was engrossed in."

## How the kanji combine

[熱](../kanji/熱.md) contributes heat or intensity; [中](../kanji/中.md) contributes being within.
As a learning image, the combination suggests being deeply caught up in an interest.
ネツ + チュウ becomes ねっちゅう, with small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[夢中](夢中.md) (むちゅう) overlaps with being absorbed.
[集中](集中.md) (しゅうちゅう) emphasizes concentrating attention and need not imply enthusiasm.
[興味](興味.md) (きょうみ), "interest," does not by itself imply the same degree of involvement.

## Distinctive meaning

熱中 can describe enjoyable commitment or absorption that makes someone overlook other things.
It is not a standalone Japanese exclamation equivalent to English "Nuts!"
Nor does it diagnose a mental-health condition.
Keep the target of the person's involvement and the に熱中する construction visible when translating.
