# ぼちぼち — gradually or at an unhurried pace

## Meaning

ぼちぼち describes doing something gradually or at a steady, unhurried pace; it can also mean that it is about time to begin.

- 片付けをぼちぼち始めよう。
  — Let's start tidying up little by little.
- 夕方なので、ぼちぼち帰りましょう。
  — It's evening, so let's head home soon.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぼちぼち suggests a relaxed pace or gradual start, not rushing or acting all at once.

## Distinctive meaning

Its sense depends on context: gradual progress and "about time" are related but not identical uses.
