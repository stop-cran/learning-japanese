# うんざり — fed up; weary of something

## Meaning

Feeling that something tedious, repetitive, or unpleasant is more than enough.

- 同じ説明を何度も聞いて、うんざりした。 — I got fed up with hearing the same explanation repeatedly.
- 終わらない雨にうんざりしている。 — I am weary of the endless rain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[退屈](退屈.md)（たいくつ）concerns boredom; [飽きる](飽きる.md)（あきる）means getting tired of something. [がっかり](がっかり.md) instead foregrounds disappointment.

## Distinctive meaning

The cause can take に, as in 雨にうんざりする. This describes mental aversion or weariness, not merely the bodily exhaustion of [へとへと](へとへと.md). It can sound quite negative when said about another person's talk.
