# 電子 (でんし) - electron; electronic

## Meaning

電子 is an electron in physics.
It also works before another word with the meaning "electronic," as in
電子辞書 (でんしじしょ), "electronic dictionary," and 電子書籍 (でんししょせき),
"electronic book."

- 電子は負の電荷を持っています。
  (でんしはふのでんかをもっています。) - An electron has a negative electric charge.
- 分からない言葉を電子辞書で調べました。
  (わからないことばをでんしじしょでしらべました。)
  - I looked up an unfamiliar word in an electronic dictionary.

The first uses 電子 as a noun naming a particle. The second uses it as part of
a compound naming an electronic device.

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) contributes electricity. [子](../kanji/子.md), whose basic meaning
includes "child," contributes the particle idea in this scientific term.
This is not a literal "electric child." The on readings are デン + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[電気](電気.md) (でんき) means electricity and also occurs in names of electrical
devices. It is not a synonym for an individual electron.
紙の本 (かみのほん), "paper book," provides a contextual contrast with an electronic book.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not attach な as though 電子 were a na-adjective.
Learn electronic-device terms as compounds: 電子 + 辞書.
The translation "electronic" in those terms does not mean that the entire device is a single electron.
