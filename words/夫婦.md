# 夫婦 (ふうふ) — married couple

## Meaning

夫婦 means a married couple, traditionally expressed as "husband and wife." It names the pair, not one spouse.
The pattern 夫婦で presents the two people acting together as a couple.

- 私たちは夫婦です。 (わたしたちはふうふです。) — We are a married couple.
- 夫婦で旅行しました。 (ふうふでりょこうしました。) — My spouse and I traveled together.

## How the kanji combine

[夫](../kanji/夫.md) contributes "husband" and [婦](../kanji/婦.md) contributes "wife" here.
This is a paired relationship: the two members together name the couple.
The on readings are フウ + フ, so keep the long first vowel in ふうふ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

夫妻 (ふさい) also means husband and wife, but the cited dictionary describes it as somewhat formal and not used for one's own family.
It is therefore not a drop-in replacement for 夫婦 in the first example.

独身 (どくしん), "unmarried; single," contrasts with being married, but describes a person's status rather than an opposite kind of couple.

## Distinctive meaning

[結婚](結婚.md) (けっこん) names marriage or getting married; 夫婦 names the people in that relationship.
A pair of people being together is not enough by itself to establish that they are 夫婦.
For one member of the pair, use the appropriate individual term, such as 夫 (おっと), "husband," or [妻](妻.md) (つま), "wife."
