# 悪い (わるい) — bad; poor; at fault

## Meaning

悪い describes something bad, poor, or undesirable. It can also assign fault to a person. It does not automatically mean morally evil.

- 今日は天気が悪いです。  
  きょうはてんきがわるいです。  
  "The weather is bad today."
- 私が悪かったです。  
  わたしがわるかったです。  
  "It was my fault."

## How the kanji combine

This native adjective uses [悪](../kanji/悪.md) for わる and the okurigana い. It is not a compound. Its forms include 悪かった わるかった (was bad) and 悪くない わるくない (not bad).

## Synonyms and antonyms

良い よい (good), also commonly いい, is the usual opposite. 下手 へた describes poor skill: 日本語が下手 にほんごがへた means "not skillful at Japanese". It is not interchangeable with 悪い in descriptions of weather or moral behavior.

## Distinctive meaning

悪い can concern quality, condition, conduct, or responsibility; the noun and situation select the sense. The casual apology 悪いね わるいね can mean "Sorry about that", but is not a substitute for a polite apology in every setting. This article uses わるい, not the separate にくい entries also associated with this spelling in dictionaries.
