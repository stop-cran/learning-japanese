# 押し入れ (おしいれ) — built-in closet

## Meaning

押し入れ is a built-in storage closet, typically with sliding doors. It is especially associated with storing bedding in a Japanese-style room.

布団を押し入れにしまいました。 (ふとんをおしいれにしまいました。) — I put the futon away in the closet.

## How the kanji combine

[押](../kanji/押.md) relates to pushing and [入](../kanji/入.md) to putting or going inside. The noun is associated with 押し入れる (おしいれる), "push in." 押入れ is another normal spelling of the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

クローゼット is often a clothes closet, particularly one designed for hanging clothes. 戸棚 (とだな) is a cupboard or cabinet. These overlap in storage function but do not name exactly the same fixture.

## Distinctive meaning

押し入れ names a storage space, not an action of entering or pushing. It need not contain bedding to count as an 押し入れ; the familiar built-in storage arrangement is the important feature.
