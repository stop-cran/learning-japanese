# 魚 (さかな) — fish

## Meaning

魚 is a fish, both the animal and the food: 魚を釣る つる (catch fish), 魚が泳ぐ およぐ (fish swim), 魚を焼く やく (grill fish), 魚屋 さかなや (fishmonger), 焼き魚 やきざかな (grilled fish).

The same noun is also read **うお**. This is another reading for fish, not
a different animal or a restriction to fish that cannot be eaten.
Use the reading of the particular word or expression rather than changing
every さかな mechanically to うお.

- 魚が川を泳いでいます。 (さかながかわをおよいでいます。) - Fish are swimming in the river.
- 魚市場で新鮮な魚を買いました。 (うおいちばでしんせんなさかなをかいました。) - I bought fresh fish at the fish market.

## How the kanji combine

[魚](../kanji/魚.md) alone with the kun reading さかな. The on reading ギョ appears in compounds: [金魚](金魚.md) きんぎょ, 人魚 にんぎょ, 魚類 ぎょるい. The alternative native reading うお appears in 魚市場 うおいちば and 魚座 うおざ.
As the second part of a compound, さかな can become -ざかな, as in 焼き魚 (やきざかな).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 魚類 ぎょるい (fish, the group).
- Related: 肉 にく (meat), 貝 かい (shellfish).

## Distinctive meaning

さかな is the everyday word and covers fish as food. It is commonly traced to 酒菜 (a side dish to go with sake).

Both さかな and うお belong to the inspected fish entry. The article's N5
label and primary reading remain さかな; the additional community N3 row
supplies うお, not a claim that every reading has the same study level.
