# 富 (とみ) — wealth

## Meaning

富 is wealth, riches, or a fortune. It names the wealth itself, not the adjective "rich" or a particular sum of cash.
富を得る (とみをえる) means to acquire wealth.

- 彼は貿易で富を得ました。 (かれはぼうえきでとみをえました。) — He acquired wealth through trade.

Here で identifies the activity through which he obtained the wealth.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [富](../kanji/富.md) writes the native noun とみ.
Compare the related verb 富む (とむ), "be rich; abound": it has a different grammatical role.
The on reading フ appears in compounds such as 豊富 (ほうふ), "abundant."

## Synonyms and antonyms

財産 (ざいさん) means property or assets and can focus on what someone possesses.
[お金](お金.md) (おかね) is money; it is not an exact substitute for wealth in every context.
貧困 (ひんこん), "poverty," supplies the contrasting condition, rather than naming a negative amount of money.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 富 when wealth is the subject, not simply whenever an English sentence contains "rich."
To say something abounds in a quality, the related pattern ～に富む is useful: 変化に富む (へんかにとむ), "be rich in variety."
That is a use of the verb 富む, not permission to treat the noun 富 as an adjective.
