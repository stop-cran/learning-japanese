# 砂丘 (さきゅう) - sand dune

## Meaning

砂丘 is a sand dune: a raised accumulation of sand formed by wind. Dunes occur not only in deserts but also along coasts and in other sandy settings.

- 砂丘を歩きました。 (さきゅうをあるきました。) - We walked across the sand dunes.
- 砂丘の上から海が見えます。 (さきゅうのうえからうみがみえます。) - You can see the sea from the top of the dune.

In the first example, を marks the area traversed. In the second, から gives the viewpoint. Japanese does not require the noun to distinguish one dune from several.

## How the kanji combine

[砂](../kanji/砂.md) contributes sand and [丘](../kanji/丘.md) contributes hill: a modifier-plus-head combination, "a hill of sand." The on readings here are サ + キュウ, giving さきゅう.

JMdict also lists しゃきゅう for this word; this card uses さきゅう. The alternative spelling 沙丘 is marked as irregular kanji usage. These are dictionary alternatives, not a rule for changing サ to シャ in every compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[丘](丘.md) (おか) is a hill without specifying its material. [砂](砂.md) (すな) is the sand itself, not the landform. [砂漠](砂漠.md) (さばく) is a desert region, not an individual dune; a desert need not be covered in dunes. 砂浜 (すなはま) is a sandy beach. These are related geographical words, not exact synonyms or a set of antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 砂丘 for the raised sandy landform, not any patch or grain of sand. A coastal dune can stand near a beach without the two words naming the same feature.
