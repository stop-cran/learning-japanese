# 切れる (きれる) - snap; run out; cut well

## Meaning

切れる is an ichidan verb with several related uses: something snaps or is cut, a supply runs out, or a blade cuts well. In these uses, the thing affected is the subject, typically marked with が.

- ひもが切れました。 (ひもがきれました。) - The cord snapped.
- この包丁はよく切れます。 (このほうちょうはよくきれます。) - This kitchen knife cuts well.
- 電池が切れました。 (でんちがきれました。) - The battery has run out.

Useful forms are 切れて, 切れない, and 切れた. 期限が切れる (きげんがきれる) means a time limit or period of validity expires.

## How the kanji combine

[切](../kanji/切.md) contributes cutting or interruption. れる belongs to this verb's written form. The extension from a broken cord to an ended supply is a useful meaning association, not a claim about historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[切る](切る.md) (きる) describes cutting something: ひもを切る, "cut the cord." 切れる describes the cord's change of state. The knife example instead describes cutting performance, not a knife breaking.

## Distinctive meaning

The form also coincides with the potential of 切る, "can cut." Context matters: ひもが切れた normally reports a break; このはさみなら厚い紙も切れる (このはさみならあついかみもきれる) means "With these scissors, even thick paper can be cut." Do not assume every れる ending is a passive.
