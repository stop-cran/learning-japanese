# 作業 (さぎょう) - work; task; operation

## Meaning

作業 is work viewed as an activity or task to carry out. It can be a noun or an intransitive する verb: **作業する**, "work; perform a task." **作業を始める／終える** means "start/finish the work."

- 二人で作業を進めています。 (ふたりでさぎょうをすすめています。) - The two of us are moving the work forward.
- この部屋で作業してください。 (このへやでさぎょうしてください。) - Please do the work in this room.

作業中 (さぎょうちゅう) means "working; work in progress." On a notice, it identifies an ongoing operation.

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) contributes doing or making, and [業](../kanji/業.md) contributes work or an undertaking. Together, サ + ギョウ emphasizes carrying out work. Keep the long ぎょう in the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) can mean a job, occupation, or work generally. 作業 more directly names the task or activity, which may be part of a job. [働く](働く.md) (はたらく) is the broader verb "work."

## Distinctive meaning

作業 can be physical or involve a computer; it does not inherently mean factory manufacturing. In a work plan, 作業の手順 (さぎょうのてじゅん) is the sequence of steps. Use the word to discuss what must be done, rather than assuming it identifies someone's employment status.
