# 嫌う (きらう) - dislike; shun

## Meaning

嫌う means to dislike, hate, or avoid something out of aversion. It is a transitive godan verb: 嫌います, 嫌った, 嫌わない. Use を for the person or thing disliked.

- 彼は争いを嫌います。 (かれはあらそいをきらいます。) - He dislikes conflict.
- 人混みを嫌って、朝早く出かけました。 (ひとごみをきらって、あさはやくでかけました。) - I went out early to avoid the crowds.

The second example connects aversion to a choice of action. It is not simply failing to encounter a crowd by chance.

## How the kanji combine

嫌 carries dislike or aversion. The native verb uses きら followed by う. Its negative ends in わない because it belongs to the godan う group, not the ichidan group.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[嫌い](嫌い.md) (きらい) expresses dislike with different grammar: 争いが嫌いです. 嫌う is a verb taking を, so the two cannot be swapped without adjusting the sentence. [好き](好き.md) (すき) expresses the contrasting attitude of liking.

## Distinctive meaning

[避ける](避ける.md) (さける), "avoid," describes avoidance without necessarily stating dislike. 嫌って in the crowd example supplies a reason for avoiding something. The passive 嫌われる (きらわれる) means "be disliked"; it does not mean that the subject dislikes somebody else.
