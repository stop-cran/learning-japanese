# 洗う (あらう) — wash

## Meaning

洗う means to wash, cleanse, or rinse. It is transitive: the thing washed takes を.
Hands, dishes, and clothes are straightforward things to use in this pattern.

- 食事の前に手を洗います。 (しょくじのまえにてをあらいます。) — I wash my hands before a meal.
- お皿を洗ってください。 (おさらをあらってください。) — Please wash the dishes.

洗う is a godan verb. Its forms include 洗います (あらいます), 洗って (あらって), and 洗った (あらった).

## How the kanji combine

[洗](../kanji/洗.md) supplies the washing meaning, and う is the verb ending.
This is a native Japanese word, not an on-reading compound.
The kanji is read あら here, rather than using the on reading セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

洗濯 (せんたく), "washing; laundry," is useful when talking about clothes.
掃除 (そうじ), "cleaning," covers actions such as sweeping and dusting as well.
Compare 手を洗う (てをあらう), "wash one's hands," with 部屋を掃除する (へやをそうじする),
"clean a room": cleaning a room does not necessarily involve washing it.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose 洗う for the washing action, not automatically for every English use of "clean."
When you name the thing being washed, mark it with を, as in 手を洗う.
Either the person doing the washing or the thing washed can be omitted when it is clear from context.
Omitting an understood object does not make 洗う an intransitive verb.
