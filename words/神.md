# 神 (かみ) - god; deity

## Meaning

神 is a god, deity, or divine spirit. The intended being depends on the religious or story context; the word can also refer to God in a monotheistic setting. It is a noun.

- 神に祈りました。 (かみにいのりました。) - I prayed to God / a deity.
- この物語には海の神が登場します。 (このものがたりにはうみのかみがとうじょうします。) - A sea deity appears in this story.

に marks the recipient of the prayer. 海の神 means a deity associated with the sea; の links that domain to 神.

## How the kanji combine

The single [神](../kanji/神.md) uses the native reading かみ here. Other words use different readings, as in [神社](神社.md) (じんじゃ), "Shinto shrine." Knowing かみ does not make every occurrence of the character read that way.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仏](仏.md) (ほとけ) belongs to Buddhist vocabulary and is not a blanket synonym for every 神. [紙](紙.md), "paper," and [髪](髪.md), "hair," share the kana reading かみ but are different words.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese nouns do not themselves mark singular versus plural here: context can distinguish a deity from several deities. In slang, 神 can praise something as "god-tier" or amazing. That evaluative use is distinct from identifying an actual object of religious belief.
