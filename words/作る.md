# 作る (つくる) — make; prepare

## Meaning

作る means to make or produce something. It covers preparing food, creating objects, and growing crops.
Mark what is made or grown with を.

- 今日はカレーを作ります。 (きょうはカレーをつくります。) — I will make curry today.
- 庭で野菜を作っています。 (にわでやさいをつくっています。) — I grow vegetables in the garden.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native verb: [作](../kanji/作.md) supplies つく and the making meaning; る is okurigana.
It is a godan verb: 作ります (つくります), 作って (つくって).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[料理](料理.md)する (りょうりする) means to cook; [建てる](建てる.md) (たてる) means to build a building.
They are more specific than 作る.
壊す (こわす, break; destroy) contrasts with making an object, but is not an opposite for every use of 作る.

## Distinctive meaning

作る is the broad everyday spelling. The same reading also has 造る, typically for larger-scale building or manufacturing,
and 創る, emphasizing creation. These are usage tendencies, not a rule that every object forces one spelling.
For an ordinary meal, learn 作る.
