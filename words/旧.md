# 旧 (きゅう) - former; old-

## Meaning

旧 is a prefix meaning "old," "former," or "ex-." It attaches directly
to a noun, often identifying a previous name, building, system, or version.
There is no の between the prefix and that noun.

- これは旧校舎です。 (これはきゅうこうしゃです。) - This is the old school building.
- 名簿には旧姓が載っています。 (めいぼにはきゅうせいがのっています。) - The register lists the person's former surname.

旧校舎 contrasts a former school building with its replacement.
旧姓 (きゅうせい) names a previous surname; the prefix does not
itself explain why the surname changed.

## How the kanji combine

旧 is read with the on reading キュウ and contributes "old" or "former."
In a longer word it modifies what follows, rather than naming an
independent object. This card teaches the productive prefix use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[古い](古い.md) (ふるい) is an adjective meaning "old."
古い校舎 may simply be an aged building; 旧校舎 identifies it as the
old or former one in a relevant sequence. 新 (しん), "new," is the
corresponding prefix contrast, as in 新校舎 (しんこうしゃ).

## Distinctive meaning

"Ex-" is a useful clue, not permission to attach 旧 to every Japanese
word for a former role or relationship. Learn established combinations.
The contrast is often between an earlier and current form, not between
poor and good condition.
