# 無理 (むり) - impossible; unreasonable

## Meaning

無理 describes something as unreasonable or beyond what is possible under the circumstances. It takes な before a noun: 無理な計画 (むりなけいかく), "an unrealistic plan."

一日で全部読むのは無理です。 (いちにちでぜんぶよむのはむりです。) - Reading all of it in one day is impossible.

無理をする means pushing oneself too hard.

無理をしないでください。 (むりをしないでください。) - Please do not overdo it.

## How the kanji combine

無 contributes absence or negation; [理](../kanji/理.md) concerns reason or principle. The on readings form むり. The modern meanings include impracticality and excessive demands, not just a logical contradiction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

不可能 (ふかのう) directly means "impossible." 難しい (むずかしい) means difficult, which need not be impossible. 可能 (かのう), "possible," is a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

無理です can mean "I cannot manage that" rather than declaring it impossible for everyone. A bare 無理 can sound blunt as a refusal. 無理に means forcing or pushing something, not merely doing it with difficulty: do not confuse it with the adjective's noun-linking な.
