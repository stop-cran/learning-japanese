# 評価 (ひょうか) - assessment; appraisal

## Meaning

評価 is an assessment, appraisal, or rating. 評価する is transitive: the work, ability, or object being assessed takes を. The word can also express recognition or appreciation of merit.

- 先生は学生の発表を評価しました。 (せんせいはがくせいのはっぴょうをひょうかしました。) - The teacher evaluated the students' presentations.
- 彼女の努力は高く評価されています。 (かのじょのどりょくはたかくひょうかされています。) - Her efforts are highly regarded.

高く評価する means rating highly; 低く評価する (ひくくひょうかする) means rating poorly or low. An assessment can concern performance or usefulness, not just money.

## How the kanji combine

評 concerns judging or commenting; 価 concerns value. Together they express judging value or merit. The on readings are ヒョウ + カ. This meaning connection does not make every 評価 a financial price calculation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[価値](価値.md) (かち) is value itself. [値段](値段.md) (ねだん) is a price. [批判](批判.md) (ひはん) concerns criticism or critical examination and is not a neutral substitute for every assessment.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that 評価 is automatically praise: an evaluation may be unfavorable. However, in contexts such as 努力を評価する, it can specifically mean recognizing someone's effort. 高く and 低く make the direction of the judgment explicit.
