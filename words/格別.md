# 格別 (かくべつ) - exceptional; especially

## Meaning

格別 describes something special or exceptional, and also works as an adverb meaning particularly or especially. Before nouns, 格別な and 格別の are possible; 格別に can modify a predicate, and the adverb also occurs without に.

- 仕事の後のビールは格別です。 (しごとのあとのビールはかくべつです。) - A beer after work is something special.
- 今年の冬は格別に寒いです。 (ことしのふゆはかくべつにさむいです。) - This winter is exceptionally cold.
- 格別のご配慮に感謝します。 (かくべつのごはいりょにかんしゃします。) - Thank you for your special consideration.

## How the kanji combine

[格](../kanji/格.md) contributes a standard or class, and [別](../kanji/別.md) separation or difference. As a meaning aid, think of something set apart from the usual standard. The on readings are カク + ベツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[特別](特別.md) (とくべつ) overlaps with special or exceptional, and [特に](特に.md) (とくに) with especially. 格別 often highlights a noticeable difference from others or from the ordinary, but is not a fixed stronger degree of 特別. [普通](普通.md) (ふつう), ordinary, provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The word need not praise something: exceptional cold can be unwelcome. In a negative expression such as 格別問題はありません (かくべつもんだいはありません), it means "There is no particular problem," not "There is no exceptionally wonderful problem." The dictionary also records the spelling 各別 at かくべつ.
