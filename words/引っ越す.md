# 引っ越す (ひっこす)

## Meaning

引っ越す means to move house or change one's residence.

- 来月、大阪に引っ越します。 (らいげつ、おおさかに ひっこします。) — I will move to Osaka next month.
- 東京から京都へ引っ越しました。 (とうきょうから きょうとへ ひっこしました。) — I moved from Tokyo to Kyoto.

The mover is the subject. Mark the starting place with から and the destination with に or へ.

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) contributes the form 引っ (ひっ), related to 引く, and [越](../kanji/越.md)す (こす) contributes crossing or moving beyond. Learn the established whole meaning, not a literal instruction to pull something across.

It is a godan verb: 引っ越して (ひっこして), 引っ越さない (ひっこさない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

引っ越し (ひっこし) is the noun "a move; moving house." 移動する (いどうする) describes changing position more broadly. 運ぶ (はこぶ) means to carry or transport something.

## Distinctive meaning

引っ越す concerns a change of residence, not merely shifting a chair or going on a trip. Moving furniture uses a different verb even when it is part of the same house move.
