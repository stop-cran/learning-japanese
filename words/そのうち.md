# そのうち (そのうち) - before long; among those

## Meaning

そのうち has two useful readings in context: an unspecified future "before long; one of these days," and a reference to part of a previously mentioned set, "among them; of those."

- そのうちまた会いましょう。 (そのうちまたあいましょう。) - Let's meet again one of these days.
- 本を五冊買いました。そのうち二冊は小説です。 (ほんをごさつかいました。そのうちにさつはしょうせつです。) - I bought five books. Two of them are novels.

In the second example, そのうち looks back to the five books; it does not predict when anything happens.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The expression combines その, referring back to something, with うち, "inside; within." The spelling その内 is also recorded. These components help connect the time-within-a-period and subset meanings without inventing a separate loanword origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[何時か](何時か.md) (いつか), usually いつか, also leaves a time unspecified. [やがて](やがて.md) (やがて) presents something as coming about in due course. Neither replaces the subset reference in そのうち二冊.

## Distinctive meaning

The future use supplies no firm appointment. Depending on context, it may be a genuine expectation or a vague intention. For arrangements that require a date, add one explicitly. When a number or group has just been mentioned, check whether "of those" is the intended meaning instead.
