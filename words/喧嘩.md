# 喧嘩 (けんか) — quarrel; fight

## Meaning

喧嘩 is a quarrel or fight, ranging from an argument to a physical scuffle. Add する for the verb "quarrel; fight." Mark the other participant with と.

- 弟と喧嘩しました。 (おとうととけんかしました。) — I had a fight with my younger brother.
- 小さなことで喧嘩しないでください。 (ちいさなことでけんかしないでください。) — Please do not quarrel over little things.

## How the kanji combine

喧 and 嘩 both have associations with noisy voices or clamor. Their on readings form けんか. Kana spellings けんか and ケンカ are also useful to recognize.

## Synonyms and antonyms

口論 (こうろん) specifically emphasizes a verbal argument. 仲直りする (なかなおりする) means making up after a quarrel, a useful contrasting action. 話し合う (はなしあう) means discussing something together.

## Distinctive meaning

喧嘩 does not automatically imply that anyone was struck. Context establishes whether it was verbal or physical. It is not the usual neutral word for a sports match or a formal competition.
