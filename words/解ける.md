# 解ける (とける) - come untied; be solved

## Meaning

解ける, read とける, is an intransitive ichidan verb. Something comes loose or untied; a problem or mystery is solved. Mark the thing undergoing the change with **が**.

- 靴のひもが解けました。 (くつのひもがとけました。) - My shoelace came untied.
- 長年の謎が解けました。 (ながねんのなぞがとけました。) - The long-standing mystery was solved.

The past is 解けた (とけた), and the negative is 解けない (とけない). The verb also describes restrictions being lifted or tension easing: 緊張が解ける (きんちょうがとける), "tension eases."

## How the kanji combine

[解](../kanji/解.md) contributes undoing or unraveling. That gives a useful bridge between an undone knot and an unraveled mystery. The reading here is と-, followed by ける; other readings of 解ける require their own context.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[解く](解く.md) (とく) is transitive: someone unties or solves something, marked with を. 溶ける, also とける, describes melting or dissolving and has different kanji.

## Distinctive meaning

解ける also matches the potential form of 解く: この問題が解ける can mean "I can solve this problem." Context distinguishes ability from a problem becoming solved. By contrast, 靴のひもが解けた describes the lace coming undone, not the speaker's ability to untie it. A ban being lifted is another release sense, not literal melting.
