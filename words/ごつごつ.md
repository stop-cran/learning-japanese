# ごつごつ — hard and knobbly

## Meaning

Hard, angular, rugged irregularities, such as protruding rocks, tree roots, or bony joints.

- ごつごつした岩（いわ）の上に腰（こし）を下ろした（おろした）。 — I sat down on a rugged rock.
- 祖父（そふ）の手は、長年の仕事でごつごつしている。 — My grandfather's hands are knobbly from years of work.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ざらざら](ざらざら.md) is finer-grained roughness. [でこぼこ](でこぼこ.md) concerns uneven highs and lows without necessarily being hard or angular. [つるつる](つるつる.md) suggests a smooth surface.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ごつごつした岩, ごつごつの手, or ごつごつしている. A surface can be both ごつごつ in its overall form and ざらざら at a smaller scale. The word can suggest robust, work-worn hands; it is not inherently a diagnosis or an insult. Do not replace every instance of English “rough” with it.
