# 泊まる (とまる) — stay overnight

## Meaning

泊まる means to stay overnight or lodge somewhere. The place where you stay normally takes に.

今夜はホテルに泊まります。（こんやはホテルにとまります。） — I will stay at a hotel tonight.

友達の家に二晩泊まりました。（ともだちのいえにふたばんとまりました。） — I stayed at a friend's house for two nights.

## How the kanji combine

泊 identifies lodging. This native verb is godan: 泊まります, 泊まって, 泊まった. The written kana まる are not an ichidan ending to remove as a unit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

宿泊する（しゅくはくする） is a more formal expression for lodging. 住む（すむ） means to live somewhere as one's residence, not simply spend a night there.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish 泊まる from 止まる, also とまる, meaning "stop." Both are intransitive, but a train stopping and a traveler staying overnight are different actions with different spellings.
