# 下書き (したがき) - rough draft

## Meaning

下書き is a rough draft or preliminary version of writing, made before
the finished version. It also forms 下書きする, "draft."
下書きをする and 下書きを書く are useful noun constructions.

- 手紙の下書きを書きました。 (てがみのしたがきをかきました。) - I wrote a draft of the letter.
- まず下書きをしてから、文章を直します。 (まずしたがきをしてから、ぶんしょうをなおします。) - First I will make a draft, and then revise the text.

Digital text can also be a 下書き; handwriting is not a requirement.
The label marks a preliminary version, not necessarily a badly written one.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md), read した, contributes an underlying or preliminary layer.
[書](../kanji/書.md)き is the noun form associated with writing.
The native elements combine with voicing: した + かき becomes したがき.
Do not read this as the on-reading combination カ + ショ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[原稿](原稿.md) (げんこう) is a manuscript or prepared text and can already
be ready for use. [案](案.md) (あん) can mean a draft or proposal more broadly.
清書 (せいしょ) is making a neat or final copy.
These contrasts concern stage and purpose, not a rule that every 原稿 is unfinished.

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen also records a preliminary drawing or outline, associated
with the spelling 下描き. This is useful additional recognition, not a
separately numbered drawing sense in the current one-sense JMdict entry.
Nor does the English "rough copy" make 下書き a general word for photocopying:
it concerns preliminary work, not merely duplicating an existing sheet.
