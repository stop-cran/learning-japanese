# 緑 (みどり) - green

## Meaning

緑 is the color green. It is a noun and normally takes の before another noun: 緑のシャツ (みどりのシャツ), "a green shirt."

- 私は緑が好きです。 (わたしはみどりがすきです。) - I like green.
- この町は緑が多いです。 (このまちはみどりがおおいです。) - This town has a lot of greenery.

The second example uses the related meaning "green vegetation."

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 緑 represents the whole native reading みどり. There is no okurigana and no i-adjective ending hidden in the pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

グリーン is a loanword for green. 青 (あお) normally names blue, although conventional expressions use it for some things that English calls green. Colors do not have a single ordinary lexical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 緑の, not みどりい, when describing an object as green. The familiar phrase 青信号 (あおしんごう), "green traffic light," is a conventional naming pattern; it does not mean that 緑 and 青 are interchangeable in every color description.
