# 丙午 (ひのえうま) — Fire Horse (calendar term)

## Meaning

丙午 is **Fire Horse**, term 43 in the traditional sixty-term calendar cycle.
Years with this designation include 1906, 1966 and 2026.

- 2026年は丙午です。 (にせんにじゅうろくねんはひのえうまです。) — 2026 is a Fire Horse year.
- 丙午は六十年に一度です。 (ひのえうまはろくじゅうねんにいちどです。) — A Fire Horse year comes once every sixty years.

These examples name years; they are not instructions for converting a
particular historical date between calendars.

## How the kanji combine

[丙](../kanji/丙.md) (ひのえ) is the third of the ten heavenly stems and is
associated with fire. [午](../kanji/午.md) (うま) is the seventh of the twelve
earthly branches, associated with the Horse.
The relationship is **stem + branch**, not an adjective describing an animal.
This primary reading combines the two native calendar names.

Ten stems and twelve branches advance together in sequence; the pairing
repeats after sixty steps. 丙午 is one pairing in that cycle, not the name
of all Horse years.

## Synonyms and antonyms

**へいご** is another documented reading of the same word: ヘイ + ゴ.
It is not a different kind of Fire Horse year.

午年 (うまどし), "year of the Horse," identifies the animal branch without
specifying its stem. It is broader than 丙午. There is no ordinary antonym
for this calendar designation.

## Distinctive meaning

The ordinary animal noun is [馬](馬.md) (うま), not 丙午.
Likewise, "fire" here is a traditional stem association, not a claim that
a horse or a year is literally burning.

干支 (えと) can name the sixty-term cycle, but it can also refer just to
the twelve animal-year signs. Check which sense is intended: the twelve
branches alone do not identify a Fire Horse year.
The calendar label does not predict a person's character or future events.
