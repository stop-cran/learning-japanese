# ゴール - goal; finish line

## Meaning

ゴール is a goal in sports, a race's finish line, or a broader objective. It is a noun; **ゴールする** is also an intransitive する verb for finishing a race.

- 試合の最後にゴールを決めました。 (しあいのさいごにゴールをきめました。) - I scored a goal at the end of the match.
- 彼女は一位でゴールしました。 (かのじょはいちいでゴールしました。) - She finished the race in first place.

**ゴールまであと百メートル** (ゴールまであとひゃくメートル) means "another hundred meters to the finish line." Outside sports, 最終的なゴール (さいしゅうてきなゴール) is an ultimate goal or objective.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword corresponds to "goal." It has no kanji components. The mark ー lengthens the first vowel; ゴール and a hypothetical short ゴル are not the same pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[目標](目標.md) (もくひょう) overlaps in an aim or objective. [目的](目的.md) (もくてき) emphasizes purpose. スタート provides the start-versus-finish contrast in a race.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the sporting context before translating. A football ゴール may be the target or a scored goal, while a runner's ゴール is the finish. Finishing a race does not itself mean winning; 一位で adds first place to the second example.
