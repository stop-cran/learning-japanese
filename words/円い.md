# 円い (まるい) — round; circular

## Meaning

円い means round or circular, especially in outline. It is an i-adjective: 円い + noun, 円く (まるく), and 円くない (まるくない).

- 円いテーブルを買いました。 (まるいテーブルをかいました。) — I bought a round table.
- この皿は円いです。 (このさらはまるいです。) — This plate is round.

## How the kanji combine

[円](../kanji/円.md) supplies the circle association and is read まる here. The adjective ending is い. The character's reading えん in prices does not apply to this adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

丸い, also read まるい, is another spelling of the same adjective and is common more broadly, including for round or ball-shaped things. 四角い (しかくい), "square; rectangular," provides a useful shape contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling 円い often highlights a circular or disc-like shape; 丸い commonly describes a ball-like shape and also overlaps with circular uses. Treat this as a spelling tendency, not a rule that makes 丸いテーブル incorrect. This page retains 円い while teaching the shared reading and meaning.
