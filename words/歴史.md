# 歴史 (れきし) - history

## Meaning

歴史 means history: the course of past events or the subject that studies them. A country, organization, activity, or object can have a history.

学校で日本の歴史を勉強しています。 (がっこうでにほんのれきしをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying Japanese history at school.

この町には長い歴史があります。 (このまちにはながいれきしがあります。) - This town has a long history.

## How the kanji combine

歴 concerns passing through time or a sequence, and 史 concerns history or its record. Their on readings form れきし. The word names history as a whole, not simply one date in the past.

## Synonyms and antonyms

過去 (かこ) means "the past" more broadly. 昔 (むかし) means former times or long ago. 記録 (きろく) is a record that can provide evidence about history without itself covering a historical narrative.

## Distinctive meaning

Use subject + の + 歴史: 会社の歴史 (かいしゃのれきし), "the company's history." A 長い歴史 is a long history, not necessarily a successful or admirable one. Context supplies that evaluation; the noun does not.
