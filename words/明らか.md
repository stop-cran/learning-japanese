# 明らか (あきらか) - clear; obvious

## Meaning

明らか is a na-adjective describing something evident or clearly established. Use 明らかな before a noun, 明らかだ or 明らかです as a predicate, and 明らかに for "clearly."

- これは明らかな間違いです。 (これはあきらかなまちがいです。) - This is an obvious mistake.
- 調査で原因が明らかになりました。 (ちょうさでげんいんがあきらかになりました。) - The investigation made the cause clear.

原因を明らかにする (げんいんをあきらかにする) means "to clarify the cause." Compare を...にする, making something clear, with が...になる, something becoming clear.

## How the kanji combine

[明](../kanji/明.md) connects brightness with clarity. Here its reading is あき, followed by らか. Learn あきらか as a whole adjective rather than treating the kana as another word. This is not a multi-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[はっきり](はっきり.md) also expresses clarity, including clear speech or outlines. 明らか suits an evident fact or conclusion. 不明 (ふめい), "unknown; unclear," provides a useful contrast when information has not been established.

## Distinctive meaning

明らかに間違っている (あきらかにまちがっている) means "clearly wrong": the speaker presents the error as evident. That assertion is stronger than merely saying it seems wrong. Do not automatically use 明らか for every English "clear"; a clear voice, clear weather, and a clear explanation need their own appropriate expressions.
