# 合う (あう) — fit; match

## Meaning

合う describes a fit, match, or agreement. For a physical fit, the thing that fits can take が or be the topic with は;
what it fits takes に.

- この靴は足に合います。 (このくつはあしにあいます。) — These shoes fit my feet.

It can also describe correctness: 答えが合っています (こたえがあっています), "The answer is correct."
The godan forms include 合います (あいます), 合った (あった), and 合って (あって).

## How the kanji combine

[合](../kanji/合.md) supplies the idea of things fitting or coming together.
The native verb is あう; its kana ending う changes in conjugation.
This reading is separate from ゴウ in compounds such as 合計 (ごうけい), "total."

## Synonyms and antonyms

合わせる (あわせる) describes actively matching or combining things, while 合う describes their fitting or matching.
The related meanings do not give the two verbs the same object pattern.

[会う](会う.md) (あう) is a same-sounding verb for meeting someone.
Use the spelling 合う in the shoe-fit example; it is not a social meeting.

## Distinctive meaning

The English "fit" can be transitive, but the matching use of 合う is intransitive: the shoes fit,
rather than a person "doing" the fit to them.
合わない (あわない), "does not fit; does not match," is the useful direct negative.
Other uses of 合う include coming together and a verb ending meaning mutual action; those are separate learning points.
