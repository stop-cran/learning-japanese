# 激しい (はげしい) - intense; violent

## Meaning

激しい describes intense force, activity, emotion, or change. It is an
i-adjective: 激しかった (はげしかった), "was intense"; 激しくない
(はげしくない), "not intense"; 激しく (はげしく), "intensely."

- 昨夜は激しい雨が降りました。 (さくやははげしいあめがふりました。) - There was heavy rain last night.
- この仕事は気温の変化が激しい場所で行います。 (このしごとはきおんのへんかがはげしいばしょでおこないます。) - This work is done in places with extreme temperature changes.

The examples show forceful weather and marked variation, not necessarily
violence by a person.

## How the kanji combine

激 contributes intensity or force. The native adjective is read はげしい;
しい is part of its okurigana and conjugation. It is not read with the on
reading ゲキ in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[強い](強い.md) (つよい), "strong," overlaps with some uses.
[穏やか](穏やか.md) (おだやか), "calm; mild," offers a useful contrast for
weather, movement, or temperament, but not a universal opposite in every phrase.

## Distinctive meaning

English "violent" can be misleading when the subject is a change or an
emotion. 激しい競争 (はげしいきょうそう) is fierce competition; it does not
by itself say that competitors physically attack each other.
Choose a translation that preserves intensity without adding an unsupported
claim about aggression.
