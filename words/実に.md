# 実に (じつに) - truly; indeed

## Meaning

実に is an adverb meaning "truly," "indeed," or "very." It strengthens the speaker's assessment of something. It goes before the expression being emphasized and does not conjugate.

- 実におもしろい話でした。 (じつにおもしろいはなしでした。) - It was a truly interesting story.
- ここからの景色は実に美しいです。 (ここからのけしきはじつにうつくしいです。) - The view from here is truly beautiful.

The emphasis can concern an unfavorable assessment too: 実に残念だ (じつにざんねんだ), "It is truly regrettable."

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes truth or actuality and uses the on reading ジツ. The final に is part of the established adverbial expression. It is not the destination particle attached to a physical object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

本当に (ほんとうに) overlaps with "really; truly." [とても](とても.md) emphasizes degree. [実は](実は.md) (じつは), "actually; the fact is," introduces information or a correction rather than simply intensifying an adjective.

## Distinctive meaning

実に sounds like a considered or emphatic evaluation in these examples. It is not a general replacement for English "surely" when guessing that something will happen. Compare 実に面白い (じつにおもしろい), "truly interesting," with 実は面白い (じつはおもしろい), "actually, it is interesting": changing に to は changes the job of the expression.
