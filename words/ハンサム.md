# ハンサム (ハンサム) - handsome

## Meaning

ハンサム is a na-adjective meaning "handsome." Use **ハンサムな + noun** before the person described, or **ハンサムです** as a polite predicate.

- 兄はハンサムです。 (あにはハンサムです。) - My older brother is handsome.
- あのハンサムな俳優を知っていますか。 (あのハンサムなはいゆうをしっていますか。) - Do you know that handsome actor?

The negative is ハンサムではありません, "is not handsome." Do not add an i-adjective ending and form ハンサムい.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word comes from English "handsome"; it contains no written kanji. Its grammar is Japanese na-adjective grammar, not the grammar of the English source word. Keep the final ム when reading or writing it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[美しい](美しい.md) (うつくしい), "beautiful," overlaps in evaluating appearance but is an i-adjective with different attachment: 美しい人 versus ハンサムな人. The two need not communicate exactly the same impression.

## Distinctive meaning

ハンサム evaluates looks; it does not establish kindness, competence, or another personal quality. The examples describe men without creating an absolute grammatical rule about who may be described. English also uses "handsome" for things such as a large profit, but that figurative English use is not established by this Japanese dictionary entry. Learn the person-description construction first rather than transferring every English sense.
