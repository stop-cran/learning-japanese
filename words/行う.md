# 行う (おこなう) — carry out; conduct

## Meaning

行う means **to carry out, conduct, or perform** an action, often an organized activity. Mark the activity with を.

- 明日、試験を行います。 (あした、しけんをおこないます。) — We will hold an exam tomorrow.
- 会議は予定どおり行いました。 (かいぎはよていどおりおこないました。) — We conducted the meeting as planned.

It is a godan verb: 行います (おこないます), 行わない (おこなわない), 行った (おこなった).

## How the kanji combine

[行](../kanji/行.md) writes おこな, with う as okurigana. 行なう is an alternative spelling of the same word. Do not confuse it with 行く (いく), “go.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

する is the broad everyday verb “do.” 行う is especially useful for carrying out a procedure, event, or planned activity and often sounds more formal. 中止する (ちゅうしする), “cancel/discontinue,” contrasts with proceeding.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss “do” is broad: 行う is not a universal replacement for する. For ordinary eating or shopping, use the appropriate everyday verb. Written 行った is ambiguous without context: おこなった means “carried out,” while いった is the past of “go.”
