# 寝る (ねる) — sleep; go to bed

## Meaning

寝る means to sleep lying down; it also means to go to bed or lie down.
It is an intransitive verb: the person sleeping is the subject, not an object marked with を.
Its forms include 寝ます (ねます), 寝ない (ねない), and 寝て (ねて).

- 昨日は八時間寝ました。 (きのうははちじかんねました。) — I slept for eight hours yesterday.
- もう寝ます。 (もうねます。) — I am going to bed now.

## How the kanji combine

[寝](../kanji/寝.md) supplies ね, with る written in kana.
This is a native Japanese verb, not a Sino-Japanese compound.
It belongs to the group of verbs that drop final る before endings such as ます: 寝る → 寝ます.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[眠る](眠る.md) (ねむる) also means "sleep" and focuses on sleeping itself.
寝る has the additional going-to-bed and lying-down senses, so someone saying もう寝ます need not already be asleep.
[起きる](起きる.md) (おきる), "get up; wake up," gives the useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

寝る does not require a bed as its grammatical object. A place can be added with で:
この部屋で寝ます。 (このへやでねます。) — I sleep in this room.
The important distinction is the activity or posture, not a particular piece of furniture.
