# 騒がしい (さわがしい) — noisy; turbulent

## Meaning

騒がしい means noisy, boisterous or clamorous. It can also describe a troubled or turbulent time or social situation.

隣の部屋が騒がしくて、眠れない。（となりのへやがさわがしくて、ねむれない。） — The room next door is so noisy that I cannot sleep.

騒がしい時代を生きた。（さわがしいじだいをいきた。） — I lived through turbulent times.

## How the kanji combine

騒 contributes noise or commotion in the native adjective さわがしい. Compare [騒ぐ](騒ぐ.md) さわぐ, make noise or become agitated. This is not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[うるさい](うるさい.md) overlaps in noisy or bothersome sound and has further annoying/fussy uses. [ざわざわ](ざわざわ.md) describes crowd murmuring or rustling. [静か](静か.md) しずか contrasts with physical noise, while [穏やか](穏やか.md) おだやか can contrast with turbulence or unrest.

## Distinctive meaning

This is an i-adjective: 騒がしい部屋, a noisy room; 騒がしくなる, become noisy; 騒がしかった, was noisy. The troubled-times use does not mean that every event in that period was literally loud, nor does it make every busy schedule 騒がしい.
