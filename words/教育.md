# 教育 (きょういく) — education

## Meaning

教育 means education, schooling, or training. It is a noun and forms the verb 教育する, "educate" or "train." A person being educated can take を.

子どもの教育について話しました。 (こどものきょういくについてはなしました。) — We talked about the children's education.

新しい社員を教育します。 (あたらしいしゃいんをきょういくします。) — We train new employees.

## How the kanji combine

[教](../kanji/教.md) contributes teaching and [育](../kanji/育.md) contributes bringing up or developing. The on readings are キョウ + イク: note the long きょう but the separate short い in いく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

教える (おしえる) is the everyday verb "teach" or "tell." 勉強 (べんきょう) focuses on the learner's study. 育てる (そだてる) means raising or nurturing.

## Distinctive meaning

教育 often concerns a sustained process of learning and development, not just giving someone one fact. It is not confined to children or school buildings; employee training and education at home can also be 教育.
