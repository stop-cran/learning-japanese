# ありがとう (ありがとう) - thank you; thanks

## Meaning

ありがとう is an interjection expressing thanks. It can stand alone; it does not connect clauses like a conjunction. Plain ありがとう suits familiar interaction. ありがとうございます is a polite form, and ありがとうございました thanks someone for a completed favor or occasion.

- 手伝ってくれてありがとう。 (てつだってくれてありがとう。) - Thanks for helping me.
- 「こちらが資料です。」「ありがとうございます。」 (「こちらがしりょうです。」「ありがとうございます。」) - "Here are the materials." "Thank you."

For an action, verb + てくれてありがとう thanks someone for doing it. The grateful speaker is not the subject of くれて: the other person did the favor.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses the usual kana spelling. 有難う and 有り難う are dictionary-recognized spellings, but there are no written kanji to decompose in ありがとう. Keep the final long vowel: とう is not a short と.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[感謝](感謝.md) (かんしゃ) names gratitude; 感謝しています means "I am grateful." It is not the same grammatical kind of expression.

[すみません](すみません.md) can acknowledge trouble taken on one's behalf, sometimes functioning as thanks as well as an apology.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose the level of politeness for the relationship and situation. Adding どうも can strengthen the expression: どうもありがとうございます, "Thank you very much." There is no useful direct antonym to a thanking formula; an apology serves a different social purpose.
