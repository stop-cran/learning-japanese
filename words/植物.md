# 植物 (しょくぶつ) - plant; vegetation

## Meaning

植物 is a noun meaning "plant" or, collectively, "vegetation."
It covers plants rather than just decorative flowers or things cultivated
for food. Context tells you whether a particular plant or plants in
general are meant.

- この植物には毎日水をあげます。 (このしょくぶつにはまいにちみずをあげます。) - I water this plant every day.
- 山には珍しい植物が生えています。 (やまにはめずらしいしょくぶつがはえています。) - Rare plants grow on the mountain.

植物を育てる (しょくぶつをそだてる) means "grow or raise plants."
植物が育つ describes the plants' growth, with が marking what grows.

## How the kanji combine

植 contributes planting, and [物](../kanji/物.md) contributes "thing"
or "being." The first character identifies the category of being.
The resulting word includes wild plants too; the component 植 does
not mean that a human must have planted each one.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[草](草.md) (くさ), [木](木.md) (き), and [花](花.md) (はな)
identify grass or herbs, trees, and flowers more specifically.
[動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," is a useful biological
contrast, not a simple logical opposite covering everything that is
not a plant.

## Distinctive meaning

English "plant" can also mean an industrial facility. 植物 does not
carry that meaning. In everyday Japanese it identifies a living plant
or plant life; the surrounding noun or verb can specify its kind,
location, or care.
