# こんなに - this much; to this extent

## Meaning

こんなに is an adverb pointing to a degree or amount: "this much," "so," or "to this extent." It can modify an adjective or a verb. The extent is presented as immediate to the speaker, for example something visible or being experienced now.

- 今日はどうしてこんなに寒いんですか。 (きょうはどうしてこんなにさむいんですか。) - Why is it so cold today?
- こんなに食べられません。 (こんなにたべられません。) - I cannot eat this much.

The first sentence points to the current cold; the second may refer to the food placed in front of the speaker.

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana expression, not a kanji compound. Compare noun-modifying こんな with adverbial こんなに: こんな本, "a book like this," versus こんなに高い本, "a book this expensive."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[そんなに](そんなに.md) and [あんなに](あんなに.md) point to degrees associated with other conversational or situational reference points. [とても](とても.md) means "very" without itself pointing to "this" extent.

## Distinctive meaning

こんなに can convey surprise, but surprise is not compulsory. With a negative, it may reject an amount or degree, as in the eating example; it does not become a simple synonym for "not very."
