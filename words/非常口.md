# 非常口 (ひじょうぐち) — emergency exit

## Meaning

非常口 is an emergency exit. Learn it as one complete noun rather than translating each character separately.

- 非常口はどこですか。 (ひじょうぐちはどこですか。) — Where is the emergency exit?
- 非常口の前に荷物を置かないでください。 (ひじょうぐちのまえににもつをおかないでください。) — Please do not leave luggage in front of the emergency exit.

## How the kanji combine

[非](../kanji/非.md) negates [常](../kanji/常.md), "usual; ordinary."
The established word 非常 (ひじょう) means an emergency here.
It modifies [口](../kanji/口.md), an opening or exit in this compound, rather than a person's mouth.

The reading mixes on and kun: ヒ + ジョウ followed by native くち, voiced to ぐち.
This repository groups on-first mixed compounds under `kango`; the label does not mean that all three readings are on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出口](出口.md) (でぐち) is an exit more generally.
非常口 adds the emergency function, so an ordinary 出口 and a marked 非常口 need not describe the same route.
They are related place labels, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

非常 is not being used here as a degree word meaning "very."
非常口 is therefore not a "very big mouth" or an intensified ordinary exit.
Recognize the whole label and follow the building's actual signs and evacuation instructions.
