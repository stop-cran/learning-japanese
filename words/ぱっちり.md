# ぱっちり — wide-open and bright-eyed

## Meaning

ぱっちり describes eyes that are wide open and bright, or the act of opening them fully.

- 子どもはぱっちりした目でこちらを見た。
  — The child looked this way with wide, bright eyes.
- 朝起きると、目がぱっちり開いた。
  — When I woke up, my eyes opened wide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぱっちり focuses on eyes being open and bright, unlike ぱちくり, which describes blinking in surprise.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can modify eyes directly with した or describe them opening fully.
