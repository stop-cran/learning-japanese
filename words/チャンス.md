# チャンス - chance; opportunity

## Meaning

チャンス is a noun for a chance or opportunity to do something. Use a dictionary-form verb before it: 話すチャンス (はなすチャンス), "an opportunity to talk." It can describe a useful opening rather than a guaranteed outcome.

- 日本語を使うチャンスがありました。 (にほんごをつかうチャンスがありました。) - I had a chance to use Japanese.
- このチャンスを逃したくありません。 (このチャンスをのがしたくありません。) - I do not want to miss this opportunity.

チャンスがある says an opportunity exists; チャンスを逃す describes missing it. Learn the noun together with the verb and particle.

## How the kanji combine

チャンス is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "chance." The small ャ combines with チ to form チャ. There is no kanji decomposition to memorize.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[機会](機会.md) (きかい) also names an opportunity or occasion. The words overlap, while チャンス readily suggests an opening to achieve something desirable. [場合](場合.md) (ばあい) names a case or circumstance, not necessarily an opportunity.

## Distinctive meaning

English "chance" can also refer to probability or randomness. The examples here concern an opportunity, not a numerical likelihood. Having a チャンス does not guarantee success: it means the situation gives someone an opening to act. Add the intended action when the opportunity would otherwise be unclear.
