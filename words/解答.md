# 解答 (かいとう) - answer; solution

## Meaning

解答 is an answer or solution to a problem, or the act of working out and giving that answer. It is useful for exercises, puzzles and examination questions.

- 問題に解答してください。 (もんだいにかいとうしてください。) - Please answer the problem.
- 解答を読んで、間違いに気づきました。 (かいとうをよんで、まちがいにきづきました。) - I read the solution and noticed my mistake.

In 問題に解答する, に marks the problem being answered. In 解答を読む, 解答 is a noun and を belongs to 読む.

## How the kanji combine

[解](../kanji/解.md) contributes working something out and [答](../kanji/答.md) an answer. Together they suggest an answer reached by solving: カイ + トウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[回答](回答.md), also かいとう, foregrounds a response to a question, inquiry or request. Compare 問題に解答する, "answer a problem," with アンケートに回答する, "respond to a questionnaire."

[答](答.md) (こたえ), usually 答え in sentences, is a broader answer noun. [答案](答案.md) (とうあん) can name an exam answer or answer script. [解決](解決.md) (かいけつ) concerns resolving a problem or situation, not necessarily writing its answer.

## Distinctive meaning

解答 does not itself certify a correct answer: 間違った解答 (まちがったかいとう) is "an incorrect answer." The contrast with 回答 is a practical spelling/use distinction, not a rule that every English "answer" belongs exclusively to one of them or that one must be written and the other spoken.
