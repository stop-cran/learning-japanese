# 婚姻 (こんいん) — marriage; matrimony

## Meaning

婚姻 names the union of spouses. It is useful when reading about marriage as a legal or institutional relationship; it does not name a wedding ceremony.

- 二人は婚姻届を提出した。 (ふたりはこんいんとどけをていしゅつした。) — The two submitted their marriage registration.
- 婚姻に関する制度を調べた。 (こんいんにかんするせいどをしらべた。) — I looked into the rules governing marriage.

## How the kanji combine

[婚](../kanji/婚.md) is こん and [姻](../kanji/姻.md) is いん. Both contribute to the idea of marriage; 届 (とどけ) in 婚姻届 specifies the notification or form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結婚](結婚.md) can refer to the same marriage and is the natural choice for a personal announcement such as 結婚しました (けっこんしました), “We got married.” [婚約](婚約.md) is an engagement, not the marriage itself.

## Distinctive meaning

A form headed 婚姻届 is about registering a marriage. It does not tell you whether the couple held a wedding.
