# 隼 (はやぶさ) — falcon

## Meaning

隼 means **falcon**, especially the peregrine falcon. Dictionaries also
record a broader falcon use, so not every occurrence is an exact species
identification. The native noun is usually written **はやぶさ** or **ハヤブサ**;
隼 is its kanji spelling.

- 空を隼が飛んでいます。 (そらをはやぶさがとんでいます。) — A falcon is flying through the sky.
- 隼の写真を撮りました。 (はやぶさのしゃしんをとりました。) — I took a photograph of a falcon.

These examples name the bird; they do not teach how to identify a species
from its appearance.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single native noun, not a multi-kanji compound.
The whole character [隼](../kanji/隼.md) is read **はやぶさ** here.
Its on-readings シュン and ジュン are not alternative pronunciations to insert
into these sentences.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [鷹](鷹.md) (たか), "hawk; falcon," is an overlapping bird-of-prey word,
  not a strictly exclusive opposite of はやぶさ.
- [鳥](鳥.md) (とり), "bird," is the broader category.
  [動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," and
  [生き物](生き物.md) (いきもの), "living thing; creature," are broader still.
- 鷲 (わし), "eagle," is a related bird name, not an unrestricted synonym.

There is no useful antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Use はやぶさ for the documented falcon noun, not every bird of prey.
English bird names and Japanese common names do not form a simple
one-to-one species key. Katakana animal spelling does not make this native
word a foreign loanword.

This article teaches the bird, not the separate Hayabusa spacecraft name
or a personal-name reading. The kanji's grade-9 classification does not
erase its non-name lexical use.
