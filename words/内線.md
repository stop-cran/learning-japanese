# 内線 (ないせん) - phone extension; internal line

## Meaning

内線 is an internal telephone line or extension. It can also
refer to indoor wiring or an inner line in the relevant technical
or physical setting.

- 内線番号を教えてください。 (ないせんばんごうをおしえてください。) - Please tell me the extension number.
- 受付から内線で連絡がありました。 (うけつけからないせんでれんらくがありました。) - Reception contacted me on the internal phone line.

内線番号 means "extension number." An office directory might
give 内線123, "extension 123"; the digits can be read
いち・に・さん.
The surrounding context distinguishes a phone extension from
wiring more generally.

## How the kanji combine

[内](../kanji/内.md) contributes "inside; internal," and
[線](../kanji/線.md) contributes "line; wire."
The modifier-plus-head compound uses ナイ + セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[電話](電話.md) (でんわ) is a telephone or phone call more broadly.
外線 (がいせん) can mean an outside telephone line, contrasting
with an organization's internal lines.
[線](線.md) (せん) has broader line and wire uses.

## Distinctive meaning

An extension number is not necessarily a complete public
telephone number: reaching it from outside may require a main
number and an additional step. The word does not prescribe one
universal dialing procedure or extension-number length.
