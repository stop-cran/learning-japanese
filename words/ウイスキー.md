# ウイスキー (ウイスキー) - whisky; whiskey

## Meaning

ウイスキー is a noun naming whisky or whiskey, an alcoholic drink.
It takes ordinary noun particles: ウイスキーを飲む is "drink whisky,"
and ウイスキーの瓶 (ウイスキーのびん) is "a bottle of whisky."

- 棚にウイスキーの瓶があります。 (たなにウイスキーのびんがあります。) - There is a bottle of whisky on the shelf.
- この店ではウイスキーも売っています。 (このみせではウイスキーもうっています。) - This shop also sells whisky.

The first sentence describes the container's location. The second uses も to add whisky
to other things sold at the shop.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana, so there are no kanji to divide.
The assigned spelling uses a full-size イ: ウイスキー.
JMdict also records ウィスキー, with small ィ, as the same drink word.
Keep the final long-vowel mark ー when writing this card's spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[酒](酒.md) (さけ) can name alcoholic drinks broadly, while ウイスキー specifies one kind.
[ビール](ビール.md), "beer," and [ワイン](ワイン.md), "wine," name other drinks;
they are alternatives, not synonyms or antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling variation ウイスキー / ウィスキー does not identify two different dictionary meanings.
Likewise, the English spellings "whisky" and "whiskey" can both translate this word.
A brand, place of production, serving style, or quantity needs additional words;
none is fixed by the noun alone.
