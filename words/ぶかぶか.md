# ぶかぶか / ブカブカ — too big and loose to fit

## Meaning

Clothes, shoes, or something else worn on the body are too large for the wearer, leaving excess room. Think of sleeves hanging beyond the hands or shoes with too much space inside, not merely soft fabric.

**ブカブカ** is the same word written in katakana. This card uses the hiragana form as its stable identity; changing the script does not make it a loanword or a separate review item.

- 兄（あに）のコートを着（き）たら、ぶかぶかで、手（て）が袖（そで）に隠（かく）れた。 — When I put on my older brother's coat, it was so oversized that my hands disappeared inside the sleeves.
- この靴（くつ）は私（わたし）にはぶかぶかで、歩（ある）くと脱（ぬ）げそうだ。 — These shoes are too loose on me and feel as if they might slip off when I walk.
- 子（こ）どもがぶかぶかの帽子（ぼうし）をかぶっている。 — The child is wearing a hat that is much too big.

## Synonyms and antonyms

だぶだぶ also describes baggy clothing, often drawing attention to the excess hanging fabric. 緩い（ゆるい）is the broader word "loose": looseness need not come from choosing an oversized garment.

[ぴったり](ぴったり.md) describes a close or just-right fit; [きつい](きつい.md) can describe clothing that is uncomfortably tight. Neither contrast means that every garment must fit closely.

Do not confuse **ぶかぶか** with [ふかふか](ふかふか.md), "soft and cushiony." A coat can be one, both, or neither: fit and texture are different properties. [ぶくぶく](ぶくぶく.md) has bubbling and swelling uses, including a bulky or baggy appearance, so its meanings partly overlap rather than forming a clean opposing quiz choice.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn **ぶかぶかの服（ふく）**, **ぶかぶかな帽子**, **靴がぶかぶかだ**, and **ぶかぶかしたズボン**. Both の and な occur in noun-modifying patterns; do not turn these examples into a rule for every mimetic word. **私には** identifies the wearer for whom the fit is too loose.

The word describes the fit, not whether the clothes are old, torn, or unfashionable. An intentionally oversized outfit can still be described this way; the word alone does not establish whether the wearer likes it.

Dictionaries also record swollen, yielding material and a loud horn-like sound. Those are separate uses: this card's examples and illustration focus on clothing and footwear, not every dictionary sense.
