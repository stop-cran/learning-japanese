# 休日 (きゅうじつ) — day off, non-working day

## Meaning

休日 is a day when one does not work or attend school: weekends and public holidays.

- 休日は家で過ごす — I spend my days off at home
- 休日出勤 きゅうじつしゅっきん — working on a day off
- 休日ダイヤ — a holiday timetable

## How the kanji combine

[休](../kanji/休.md) (rest) modifies [日](../kanji/日.md) (day): "a rest day". Note 日 reads ジツ here, the same reading as in 平日 へいじつ and 祝日 しゅくじつ,
unlike ニチ in 日曜日.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 休み やすみ (everyday, native; also "break, absence"), お休み, 休暇 きゅうか (leave, a longer or formal absence).
- Related: 祝日 しゅくじつ (a public holiday), 定休日 ていきゅうび (the shop's regular closing day), 休業日 きゅうぎょうび (closure day).
- Antonym: 平日 へいじつ (weekday), 出勤日 しゅっきんび (workday), 営業日 えいぎょうび (business day).

## Distinctive meaning

休日 stresses the day itself; 休み can be a break, absence or holiday; 休暇 is leave (vacation days taken from work).
In schedules 休日 often means 土曜・日曜・祝日 together.
