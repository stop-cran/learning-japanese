# 膝 (ひざ) - knee; lap

## Meaning

膝 is a noun meaning "knee." When someone is sitting, it can also refer to the lap,
the area formed by the knees and thighs.
The action and posture help select the English translation.

- 階段を上ると、膝が痛みます。 (かいだんをのぼると、ひざがいたみます。) - My knee hurts when I climb stairs.
- 猫が私の膝の上で寝ています。 (ねこがわたしのひざのうえでねています。) - A cat is sleeping on my lap.

膝が痛い (ひざがいたい) also means "my knee hurts."
In the second example, 膝の上 supplies the place, and で marks where the cat is sleeping.

## How the kanji combine

膝 writes the native word ひざ as a single character.
Learn the whole reading rather than assigning syllables to its visible components.
The kana form ひざ is also useful when the character is unfamiliar.
No separate kanji compound is needed for the lap meaning here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[足](足.md) (あし) can refer to a foot or leg according to context.
膝 identifies a specific joint or the seated lap, rather than the entire leg.
There is no useful antonym: other body parts are neighboring or contrasting locations, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

The lap sense does not mean that an animal sits directly on the knee joint.
English and Japanese group this seated area differently, so translate the whole situation.
For movement, 膝を曲げる (ひざをまげる), "bend one's knees," clearly selects the joint sense.
