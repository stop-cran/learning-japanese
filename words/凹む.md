# 凹む (へこむ) - become dented; feel down

## Meaning

凹む is an intransitive godan verb: become dented, sink in or develop a depression.
It also means be beaten or give in, feel down in colloquial speech, or suffer a loss.
Use the affected thing or person as the subject, not a direct object being dented.

- 箱の角がへこみました。 (はこのかどがへこみました。) - The corner of the box became dented.
- 試験に落ちて、少しへこんでいます。 (しけんにおちて、すこしへこんでいます。) - I failed the examination and am feeling a little down.

## How the kanji combine

凹 has a concave or sunken-in meaning; the native verb is へこむ, often written
in kana. Associating the visible hollow in 凹 with a dent is a modern-shape
memory aid, not a historical explanation of the word's emotional sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[沈む](沈む.md) (しずむ) overlaps in sinking and low spirits.
[しょんぼり](しょんぼり.md) describes a downcast state;
[がっかり](がっかり.md) emphasizes disappointment.
[負ける](負ける.md) (まける) and [損](損.md) (そん) help with the defeat
and loss senses. Separate 窪む (くぼむ), also written 凹む, concerns a hollow;
do not transfer every emotional へこむ use to that reading.

## Distinctive meaning

Conjugate へこまない, へこみます and へこんだ, not へこみた.
The emotional use can describe a temporary setback, not a medical diagnosis.
The defeat and loss senses depend on context; the word does not require a literal
dent whenever a person feels discouraged or suffers a setback.
