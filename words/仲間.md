# 仲間 (なかま) - companion; fellow member

## Meaning

仲間 is a companion, colleague, or fellow member of a group. It can also refer to the group itself or to things of the same category.

- 仕事の仲間と昼ご飯を食べました。 (しごとのなかまとひるごはんをたべました。) - I ate lunch with my colleagues.
- 私も仲間に入れてください。 (わたしもなかまにいれてください。) - Please let me join the group too.

In the second example, 仲間に入れる means including someone in the group. For classification, トラはネコの仲間です means "Tigers belong to the cat family."

## How the kanji combine

仲 contributes association between people, while [間](../kanji/間.md) contributes an interval or relationship between them. The native readings なか + ま form a word about belonging together. This association is a learning aid, not a literal historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[友達](友達.md) (ともだち) emphasizes friendship. [同僚](同僚.md) (どうりょう) identifies a colleague at work. 仲間 can include either, depending on the shared activity or group.

## Distinctive meaning

仲間 does not guarantee personal intimacy. People can be workmates or members of the same activity without being close friends. Conversely, the word can warmly emphasize solidarity. The source gloss "company" means companions or a group here, not automatically a commercial corporation.
