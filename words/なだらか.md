# なだらか - gently sloping; smooth

## Meaning

なだらか describes a gentle slope or a smooth, gradual course rather
than a sharp rise, drop or interruption. It is a na-adjective:
なだらかな before a noun and なだらかに before an action.

- なだらかな坂をゆっくり上りました。 (なだらかなさかをゆっくりのぼりました。) - I slowly walked up the gentle slope.
- グラフの線はなだらかに上がっています。 (グラフのせんはなだらかにあがっています。) - The line on the graph rises gradually.

The first example describes the incline; ゆっくり separately describes
the walking speed. The second concerns a gradual change, not a landscape.
The word can also describe a smooth surface or process, or a gentle tone
of voice.

## How the kanji combine

This word is written in kana. There is no written compound to decompose.
Learn its adjective pattern rather than treating its final か as a
question particle or adding an i-adjective ending.

## Synonyms and antonyms

緩やか (ゆるやか) overlaps in a gentle slope or gradual development.
[急](急.md) (きゅう) provides a contrast for steepness or abrupt change.
[平ら](平ら.md) (たいら) emphasizes flatness; a なだらかな坂 still slopes.

## Distinctive meaning

Smoothness must be interpreted with the thing described: a gentle incline,
an unbroken course and a mild voice are not one identical physical property.
Do not turn "easy" in a dictionary gloss into a claim that every なだらかな
route is safe or effortless. Nor does a gradual rise guarantee favorable
results. The slope and process uses are the practical scope here.
