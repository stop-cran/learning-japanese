# 黙る (だまる) - be silent; say nothing

## Meaning

黙る is an intransitive godan verb meaning to fall silent or say nothing. Its forms include 黙ります, 黙って, and 黙った. **黙っている** describes remaining silent.

- 彼は質問に答えず、黙っていました。 (かれはしつもんにこたえず、だまっていました。) - He remained silent without answering the question.
- 先生が話し始めると、みんな黙りました。 (せんせいがはなしはじめると、みんなだまりました。) - Everyone fell silent when the teacher began speaking.

The first example presents continued silence; the second presents the change from talking to being silent.

## How the kanji combine

黙 contributes silence, with the native reading だまる and the ending る written in kana. The verb has no second kanji component.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[静か](静か.md) (しずか) describes quietness and can apply to a room or neighborhood. 黙る specifically concerns not speaking; a person can be silent while making other noise. [言う](言う.md) (いう), "say," contrasts with saying nothing in the first example.

## Distinctive meaning

黙れ (だまれ), the imperative, can sound like "shut up." Adding a polite ending elsewhere does not erase the force of an order to stop speaking. 黙って also occurs when an action is done without speaking about it; context determines whether silence implies withholding information or simply listening.
