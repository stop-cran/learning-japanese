# ばらばら — scattered and separated

## Meaning

ばらばら describes things spread apart, separated, or no longer in one organized whole.

- 箱を落として、部品が床にばらばらに散らばった。
  — The box fell, and the parts scattered across the floor.
- メンバーの予定が合わず、集まる時間がばらばらだった。
  — The members' schedules did not line up, so their arrival times were all different.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ばらばら emphasizes separation or lack of coordination, in contrast with things gathered or aligned.

## Distinctive meaning

It applies to physical pieces, timing, opinions, and other things that do not form a unified whole.
