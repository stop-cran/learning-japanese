# しんみり — quietly reflective or sentimental

## Meaning

A quiet, heartfelt, reflective mood, sometimes tinged with sadness.

- 友達と昔の思い出をしんみりと話した。 — I talked quietly and reflectively with a friend about old memories.
- 別れの歌を聞いて、みんなしんみりした。 — The farewell song left everyone in a subdued, sentimental mood.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しょんぼり](しょんぼり.md) overlaps in a subdued state but often foregrounds personal dejection. [静か](静か.md)（しずか）can describe quietness without this emotional depth.

## Distinctive meaning

しんみりと話す and しんみりした雰囲気（ふんいき）can describe a meaningful, intimate moment, not just an unhappy one. It is not simply the absence of sound expressed by [しーん](しーん.md).
