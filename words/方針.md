# 方針 (ほうしん) - policy; course of action

## Meaning

方針 is a policy, guiding principle or general course of action: the direction
one intends to follow when doing something. It can be an organization's policy
or an individual's approach.

- 会社は新しい方針を発表しました。 (かいしゃはあたらしいほうしんをはっぴょうしました。) - The company announced a new policy.
- まず治療の方針を決めましょう。 (まずちりょうのほうしんをきめましょう。) - First, let's decide on the course of treatment.

## How the kanji combine

[方](../kanji/方.md) contributes direction;
[針](../kanji/針.md) a needle/pointer association. ホウ + シン gives ほうしん.
The established policy meaning is not a statement that a plan is a metal needle.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計画](計画.md) (けいかく) is a plan and can specify concrete steps or timing.
方針 often focuses on the guiding direction, but a plan of action and a policy
can overlap. [主義](主義.md) (しゅぎ) is a principle or doctrine;
[方向](方向.md) (ほうこう) also has an abstract direction/course use.
[対策](対策.md) (たいさく) names measures or a strategy directed at a problem
or task. The words can overlap as a course of action; 対策 foregrounds the
issue being dealt with.

## Distinctive meaning

[目標](目標.md) (もくひょう) is a target or goal. Increasing sales by ten
percent is a target; prioritizing long-term customer relationships is a policy
for how to proceed. 方針 by itself does not supply a deadline or numerical result.
方針を決める is to decide a policy; 方針に従う (ほうしんにしたがう) to follow it.
