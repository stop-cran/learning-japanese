# 楠 (くすのき) - camphor tree; camphorwood

## Meaning

楠 means camphor tree, *Cinnamomum camphora*, or its wood. It is an evergreen tree. The same native plant noun also has the reading **くす**.

- 公園に大きな楠があります。 (こうえんにおおきなくすのきがあります。) - There is a large camphor tree in the park.
- 楠の木陰で休みました。 (くすのきのこかげでやすみました。) - I rested in the shade of a camphor tree.

These examples use くすのき. The shorter くす also names the tree; the spellings 楠 and 樟 allow both readings.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [楠](../kanji/楠.md) can write the entire くすのき, not just its first two kana. The のき is part of this dictionary word's reading even though the spelling contains no separate 木.

樟 is another dictionary spelling. Kana forms くすのき, くす, クスノキ and クス also occur; the dictionary marks this plant noun as usually written in kana. That spelling note does not make either native pronunciation a rare reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[木](木.md) (き), tree or wood, and [植物](植物.md) (しょくぶつ), plant, are broader terms. [木材](木材.md) (もくざい) and [材木](材木.md) (ざいもく) mean timber or lumber without identifying camphorwood. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The plant is not identical to the substance 樟脳 (しょうのう), camphor, obtained from it. Calling wood 楠 identifies its kind; it does not mean every piece of timber is camphorwood.

石楠花 (しゃくなげ) names rhododendron, a different plant. Shared spelling material is not evidence that it is a kind of camphor tree or that しゃくなげ is a reading of the single character 楠.
