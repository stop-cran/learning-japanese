# 気体 (きたい) - gas; gaseous substance

## Meaning

気体 is a gas or gaseous substance. It is a noun used when describing matter in its gaseous state; English "vapour" can also be appropriate in context.

- この容器には気体が入っています。
  (このようきにはきたいがはいっています。) - This container holds a gas.
- 気体の性質について学びました。
  (きたいのせいしつについてまなびました。) - We learned about the properties of gases.

## How the kanji combine

[気](../kanji/気.md) contributes air or gas, and [体](../kanji/体.md) a body or substance. Their on readings combine as キ + タイ. Here 体 does not mean a person's body.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ガス](ガス.md) also means gas, including the state-of-matter use; do not insist that it means fuel only. [液体](液体.md) (えきたい), "liquid," and 固体 (こたい), "solid," name contrasting states, not synonyms of 気体.

## Distinctive meaning

気体 identifies a state of matter, not one particular fuel or chemical. [空気](空気.md) (くうき) is air, an example of a gaseous mixture, rather than the word for every gas. 気体 does not mean a social atmosphere or mood.
