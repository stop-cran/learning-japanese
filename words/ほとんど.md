# ほとんど (ほとんど) — almost; mostly

## Meaning

ほとんど means almost, nearly, or mostly. With a negative predicate it means hardly or scarcely. As a noun it can mean the greater part or almost all of something.

- 宿題はほとんど終わりました。 (しゅくだいはほとんどおわりました。) — My homework is almost finished.
- 昨夜はほとんど眠れませんでした。 (さくやはほとんどねむれませんでした。) — I could hardly sleep last night.

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is the usual spelling. 殆ど is a recognized kanji spelling, but the meaning does not require learning that character first. Do not split the kana into smaller words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

だいたい overlaps with "roughly; mostly," but can also mean approximately. 全部 (ぜんぶ), "all," states completeness more directly. 全然 (ぜんぜん) with a negative means "not at all," stronger than the near-zero quantity of ほとんど.

## Distinctive meaning

Before a noun, use の: ほとんどの学生 (ほとんどのがくせい), "most students." 学生のほとんど means "most of the students." Do not omit の and treat ほとんど like an English adjective. In negative sentences, "hardly" need not mean an absolute zero.
