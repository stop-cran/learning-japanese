# 薄い (うすい) - thin; weak in flavor

## Meaning

薄い describes little thickness, a pale color, or a weak flavor or concentration. It is an i-adjective.

- この本は薄いです。 (このほんはうすいです。) - This book is thin.
- このスープは味が薄いです。 (このスープはあじがうすいです。) - This soup has a weak flavor.

Use 薄い紙 (うすいかみ), "thin paper," directly before a noun. 薄くする means to make something thinner or less concentrated.

## How the kanji combine

薄 writes うす, and い completes the adjective. This is a native word, not a compound of separate kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

厚い (あつい) contrasts with physical thinness; 濃い (こい) contrasts with weak flavor or pale color. 細い (ほそい) describes a narrow or slender shape instead.

## Distinctive meaning

The opposite changes with the meaning. A 薄い本 has little thickness, not necessarily little width. 薄いお茶 is weak tea, not tea lacking physical strength.
