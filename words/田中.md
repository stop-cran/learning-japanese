# 田中 (たなか) — Tanaka

## Meaning

田中 is one of the most common Japanese surnames: 田中さん (Mr./Ms. Tanaka), 田中先生. Its literal meaning is "in the rice field".

## How the kanji combine

[田](../kanji/田.md) (rice field, kun た) plus [中](../kanji/中.md) (middle, inside, kun なか): "in the field", a modifier-plus-noun compound, like 山中 やまなか. 中 is read なか, not ちゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Similar names: 山田 やまだ (田 voiced to だ in names), 中村 なかむら, 田村 たむら.
- Related word: 田んぼ たんぼ (a rice paddy).

## Distinctive meaning

Family names are used with さん in everyday contact. Compare 田中 (たなか) with 中田 (なかた), where the order of the same two kanji changes the name.
