# 送り仮名 (おくりがな) - kana endings after kanji

## Meaning

送り仮名 are kana written after a kanji as part of the word's spelling.
They complete its reading and can show inflection, but are not restricted
to letters that change when the word is conjugated.

- 「食べる」の「べる」は送り仮名です。 (「たべる」の「べる」はおくりがなです。) - The べる in 食べる is okurigana.
- 「明るい」の送り仮名は「るい」です。 (「あかるい」のおくりがなは「るい」です。) - The okurigana in 明るい is るい.

In 食べた (たべた), the written ending is べた.
In 休み (やすみ), the noun retains the written み.
These are parts of the word, not separate particles.

## How the kanji combine

送り, related to [送](../kanji/送.md)る (おくる), modifies 仮名 (かな), kana.
か becomes が in this compound: おくり + がな.
[名](../kanji/名.md) is part of 仮名, not an independently added な here.
送りがな is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

振り仮名 (ふりがな) are pronunciation kana printed above or beside kanji;
they are not the same as the inline endings after them.
[読み](読み.md) (よみ) is the reading itself, not this particular spelling device.

## Distinctive meaning

In 本を読む (ほんをよむ), む is okurigana belonging to 読む,
whereas を is a separate particle.
Not every kana character after a kanji is therefore 送り仮名.
Actual spelling conventions also have exceptions and permitted variants;
the definition alone does not determine every word's spelling.
