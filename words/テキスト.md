# テキスト — text; course textbook

## Meaning

テキスト can mean written text or a textbook used for a course. The context determines whether it names the content or the study book.

- このテキストをコピーしてください。 (このテキストをコピーしてください。) — Please copy this text.
- 授業のテキストを忘れました。 (じゅぎょうのテキストをわすれました。) — I forgot the textbook for class.

## How the kanji combine

テキスト is a katakana loanword, not a kanji compound. It has no long-vowel mark. The variant テクスト also exists, especially in discussions of texts as objects of analysis.

## Synonyms and antonyms

教科書 (きょうかしょ) is a textbook, especially a standard school textbook. 文章 (ぶんしょう) means a piece of writing or prose. Neither covers every use of テキスト.

## Distinctive meaning

A language-course instructor may call the course book テキスト even when it is not a school 教科書. In computing, テキスト refers to textual content rather than pictures or other media. Do not automatically interpret it as a phone text message; Japanese often uses メッセージ for that.
