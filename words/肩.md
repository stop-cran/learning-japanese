# 肩 (かた) - shoulder

## Meaning

肩 is the noun "shoulder." Use 肩が for a condition affecting the shoulder and 肩に for something placed or carried on it. Japanese does not require a different plural form when both shoulders are meant.

- 長くパソコンを使うと、肩がこります。 (ながくパソコンをつかうと、かたがこります。) - My shoulders get stiff when I use a computer for a long time.
- 彼はかばんを肩に掛けました。 (かれはかばんをかたにかけました。) - He slung his bag over his shoulder.

肩がこる (かたがこる) is a useful expression for stiff or tense shoulders. It describes the condition, rather than an action you deliberately do to the shoulder.

## How the kanji combine

肩 is a single-character native noun read かた. There are no separate kanji to combine and no inflecting ending. The whole character writes the name of the body part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[首](首.md) (くび), "neck," and [背中](背中.md) (せなか), "back," name neighboring areas, not interchangeable words for the shoulder. Body-part names do not need an invented opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the particle together with the action: 肩に掛ける places something on the shoulder, while 肩をたたく (かたをたたく) means to tap or pat it. The same body-part noun can therefore appear with different particles without changing its lexical meaning.
