# 登山 (とざん) - mountain climbing

## Meaning

登山 is a noun meaning mountain climbing or mountaineering.
With する it becomes an intransitive verb: **登山する**, "climb a mountain."
Useful phrases include 登山に行く (とざんにいく), "go mountain climbing,"
and 登山の準備 (とざんのじゅんび), "preparations for a mountain climb."

- 週末は友人と登山に行きます。 (しゅうまつはゆうじんととざんにいきます。) - I am going mountain climbing with a friend this weekend.
- 初めて登山をするので、必要な道具を確認しました。 (はじめてとざんをするので、ひつようなどうぐをかくにんしました。) - Since this will be my first mountain climb, I checked the necessary equipment.

登山をする is the noun-plus-する construction; を here does not identify a mountain being acted on.

## How the kanji combine

[登](../kanji/登.md) contributes ascending, and [山](../kanji/山.md) supplies the mountain.
The combination directly names ascending a mountain.
The compound is read とざん, with voiced ざん, not とさん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[登る](登る.md) (のぼる) is the broader verb "climb/ascend": 山に登る means "climb a mountain."
ハイキング can describe a walk on trails without the same emphasis on mountain ascent.
下山 (げざん), "descending a mountain," is the directional counterpart.

## Distinctive meaning

登山 names the activity or ascent, not a particular climbing technique.
It does not by itself tell you whether ropes are used or how difficult a route is.
登山靴 (とざんぐつ), "mountain boots," and 登山道 (とざんどう), "mountain trail,"
use 登山 to specify the purpose of the following noun.
