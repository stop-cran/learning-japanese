# 熱帯 (ねったい) - the tropics

## Meaning

熱帯 is a noun for the tropics or tropical zone. Use 熱帯の before a noun to describe something associated with that region.

- この植物は熱帯で育ちます。 (このしょくぶつはねったいでそだちます。) - This plant grows in the tropics.
- 熱帯の気候について調べました。 (ねったいのきこうについてしらべました。) - I researched tropical climates.

で marks where the plant grows. 熱帯に住む (ねったいにすむ) means "live in the tropics," with に marking the place of residence.

## How the kanji combine

[熱](../kanji/熱.md) contributes heat, and 帯 contributes a belt or zone. Heat modifies the zone: a tropical belt. The on-reading combination is ねったい, with a small っ, not ねつたい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

温帯 (おんたい), the temperate zone, and 寒帯 (かんたい), the frigid zone, are contrasting climatic regions. [暑い](暑い.md) (あつい) describes hot weather or how hot a place feels; it does not identify a geographic zone.

## Distinctive meaning

A hot afternoon does not turn a temperate city into 熱帯. The noun classifies a region rather than reporting today's temperature. Likewise, 熱帯の植物 (ねったいのしょくぶつ), "a tropical plant," identifies an association with that climate; it does not by itself explain how to grow the plant or describe every tropical environment as identical.
