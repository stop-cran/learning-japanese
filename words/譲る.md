# 譲る (ゆずる) - hand over; yield

## Meaning

譲る means to hand over or transfer something, give up a place for someone, or yield.
It is a transitive godan verb: AにBを譲る means "give or transfer B to A."
Forms include 譲ります, 譲った, 譲って, and 譲らない.

- 電車でお年寄りに席を譲りました。
  (でんしゃでおとしよりにせきをゆずりました。)
  - I gave up my seat to an elderly person on the train.
- 使わなくなった自転車を友人に譲りました。
  (つかわなくなったじてんしゃをゆうじんにゆずりました。)
  - I passed on a bicycle I no longer used to a friend.

The object may be a possession, a right, a position, or priority rather than something
physically placed in another person's hands.

## How the kanji combine

譲 is the single kanji carrying the yielding or transfer meaning, read ゆず here.
る completes the written verb. Despite the final る, its negative is 譲らない,
not ゆずない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[渡す](渡す.md) (わたす) emphasizes handing something over.
[売る](売る.md) (うる) explicitly means selling.
譲る can also involve payment: 安く譲る (やすくゆずる) means to let someone have something cheaply.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer a free gift from 譲る alone.
It can also describe conceding in a disagreement: 一歩も譲らない
(いっぽもゆずらない), "not give an inch." That use concerns one's position,
not ownership of an object.
