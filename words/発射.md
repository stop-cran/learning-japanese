# 発射 (はっしゃ) - firing or launching a projectile

## Meaning

発射 is firing or launching something such as a rocket, missile or bullet.
With する it is transitive: the thing launched takes を.

- ロケットを発射しました。 (ロケットをはっしゃしました。) - They launched a rocket.
- 発射まであと十秒です。 (はっしゃまであとじゅうびょうです。) - There are ten seconds until launch.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) concerns sending out, and 射 concerns shooting.
The compound names the action of sending a projectile out.
ハツ + シャ becomes はっしゃ, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

撃つ (うつ) means shooting or firing; it is a different dictionary entry from [打つ](打つ.md), "hit; strike."
[投げる](投げる.md) (なげる) is throwing, not automatically firing from a launcher.
[発車](発車.md) has the same reading はっしゃ but means a vehicle's departure.

## Distinctive meaning

In 発射する, the focus is the firing or launch, not a passenger vehicle simply departing.
The source gloss "catapult" refers to launching, not the apparatus as a countable object.
[ロケット](ロケット.md) means a rocket in the example; that article also records the separate locket meaning.
