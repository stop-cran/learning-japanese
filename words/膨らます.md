# 膨らます (ふくらます) - inflate; expand

## Meaning

膨らます means making something swell, expand, bulge, or become
inflated. It is a transitive godan verb with a す ending:
膨らまします, 膨らまして, 膨らました.
The thing made bigger takes を.

- 風船を膨らましました。 (ふうせんをふくらましました。) - I inflated a balloon.
- 子供はほっぺたを膨らましていました。 (こどもはほっぺたをふくらましていました。) - The child was puffing out their cheeks.

The verb describes causing the expansion; the balloon or cheeks
are not the grammatical subjects of these sentences.

## How the kanji combine

膨 contributes swelling or expanding. The native word is ふくらます;
the kana after 膨 belong to the word's spelling and inflection.
This is not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[膨らむ](膨らむ.md) (ふくらむ) is intransitive:
風船が膨らむ, a balloon inflates.
膨らませる (ふくらませる) is another transitive expression
encountered for making something expand.
膨らます itself is an established dictionary headword, not an
incorrect spelling that must be replaced by 膨らませる.

## Distinctive meaning

The object can swell in shape or expand figuratively; inflation
does not necessarily mean pumping in air.
Do not substitute this verb for every increase in an unrelated
measurement. Compare を膨らます, cause something to expand,
with が膨らむ, something expands.
The title does not remove that grammatical distinction.
