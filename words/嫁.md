# 嫁 (よめ) - bride; daughter-in-law

## Meaning

嫁 is a bride or a daughter-in-law. The relationship and surrounding expression
determine which meaning is intended. お嫁さん (およめさん) adds a polite or
familiar framing; it can refer to a bride.

- お嫁さんは白いドレスを着ていました。
  (およめさんはしろいドレスをきていました。)
  - The bride was wearing a white dress.
- 息子の嫁と一緒に料理をしました。
  (むすこのよめといっしょにりょうりをしました。)
  - I cooked together with my son's wife.

In the second example, 息子の嫁 identifies the speaker's daughter-in-law.
The word does not say that the wedding is taking place now.

## How the kanji combine

嫁 is one kanji representing the whole native noun よめ.
There is no multi-kanji compound to divide.
花嫁 (はなよめ), "bride," adds 花 and makes the wedding role explicit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[妻](妻.md) (つま) names a wife from the spouse relationship.
嫁 can instead locate the person in relation to the spouse's family.
Bridegroom and son-in-law are corresponding roles, not antonyms describing opposite qualities.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records a "wife" use with a Kansai dialect label.
Treat that as a usage note, not a rule about who is allowed to say the word.
Because relationship labels can carry different associations, use 妻 for a straightforward
reference to one's wife and 息子の妻 for an explicit "son's wife" when that clarity is useful.
