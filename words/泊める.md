# 泊める (とめる) - lodge someone; put someone up overnight

## Meaning

泊める means to give someone lodging or put them up. It is a transitive
ichidan verb: the person accommodated takes を, while に can mark the
place where they stay.

- 友達を家に泊めました。 (ともだちをいえにとめました。) - I put a friend up at my house.
- 今夜は兄をうちに泊めます。 (こんやはあにをうちにとめます。) - I will put my older brother up at my place tonight.

Forms include 泊めます (とめます), 泊めて (とめて), and
泊めない (とめない).

## How the kanji combine

[泊](../kanji/泊.md) supplies と in this verb, with める written in kana.
The conjugation stem is 泊め (とめ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[泊まる](泊まる.md) (とまる) is intransitive:
友達が家に泊まる (ともだちがいえにとまる), a friend stays at the house.
泊める instead presents someone accommodating that friend.

[宿泊](宿泊.md) (しゅくはく), lodging or an overnight stay, names the
accommodation; 宿泊する describes the guest staying.
[滞在](滞在.md) (たいざい) concerns a period spent in a place rather than
the specific act of giving someone a bed for the night. These are useful
comparisons, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 泊める with the same-sounding 止める (とめる), stop
something. The spelling and the situation distinguish giving someone
lodging from stopping an action.

泊める is not the grammatical causative form of 泊まる. The causative
泊まらせる (とまらせる) is a form of the godan verb; 泊める is its own
ichidan verb with an accommodation meaning.
