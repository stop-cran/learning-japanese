# たった (たった) - only; no more than

## Meaning

たった emphasizes how small an amount or number is: "only," "just," or "no more than." It works adverbially and before quantity expressions, often with だけ or with しか + negative.

- 参加者はたった三人でした。 (さんかしゃはたったさんにんでした。) - There were only three participants.
- 駅までたった五分で着きます。 (えきまでたったごふんでつきます。) - You can reach the station in just five minutes.

The number itself can be objectively large in another context; たった presents it as small relative to an expectation or comparison.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in hiragana. Its small っ is part of the pronunciation: たった, not ただ. There is no kanji compound to analyze, and the final た does not make this expression a past-tense verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ただ](ただ.md) can overlap in the meaning "merely," but it has other uses that たった does not share. [単に](単に.md) (たんに) often limits an explanation or characterization rather than highlighting a small numerical amount.

## Distinctive meaning

たった一人しか来なかった (たったひとりしかこなかった) means "only one person came." The negative is required by しか, not by たった itself: たった一人来た is also possible.

The source's "free of charge; usual; common" glosses do not belong to this entry. たった百円 means "only one hundred yen," not "free."
