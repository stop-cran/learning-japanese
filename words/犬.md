# 犬 (いぬ) — dog

## Meaning

犬 is a dog: 犬を飼う かう (keep a dog), 犬の散歩 さんぽ (walking a dog), 犬が吠える ほえる (a dog barks), 子犬 こいぬ (puppy), 犬小屋 いぬごや (dog house).

## How the kanji combine

[犬](../kanji/犬.md) alone with the kun reading いぬ. The on reading ケン is used in compounds: 愛犬 あいけん (beloved dog), 番犬 ばんけん (watchdog).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 猫 ねこ (cat), ペット (pet), [子犬](子犬.md) こいぬ (puppy).

## Distinctive meaning

犬 is the neutral word. A dog's cry is ワンワン in Japanese, and 犬 can also mean a spy or a lackey in idioms (警察の犬), which is negative.
