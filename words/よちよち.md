# よちよち — toddling with unsteady little steps

## Meaning

よちよち depicts small, uncertain steps, especially those of a child who is learning to walk.

- 赤ちゃんがよちよち歩いている。 — The baby is toddling along.
- 弟はよちよちと母のところへ歩いていった。 — My little brother toddled over to our mother.

The usual model is よちよち歩く, optionally with と. よちよち歩き is the noun "toddling" or "a toddling gait."

## How the kanji combine

This repeated kana mimetic represents visible movement, not a literal sound. There are no kanji components to reconstruct.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ふらふら](ふらふら.md) can describe broader unsteadiness from dizziness or weakness; it does not specifically suggest a toddler's little steps. [すたすた](すたすた.md) describes a brisk, confident-looking gait instead.

## Distinctive meaning

The expression often has an affectionate tone when used of young children or small animals. Applying it to an adult may sound belittling or humorous, so it is not a neutral label for every walking difficulty. Its "unsteady" quality is about the gait, not necessarily the person's emotions.
