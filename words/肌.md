# 肌 (はだ) - skin; surface texture

## Meaning

肌 is a noun for skin, often useful when discussing its feel, appearance, or condition. **肌が乾燥する** (はだがかんそうする) means "the skin becomes dry."

- 冬は肌が乾燥しやすいです。 (ふゆははだがかんそうしやすいです。) - My skin tends to get dry in winter.
- この布は肌に優しいです。 (このぬのははだにやさしいです。) - This fabric is gentle on the skin.

The first example's しやすい indicates a tendency. The second uses 肌に to identify what the fabric is gentle on.

## How the kanji combine

肌 is a single character read はだ in this native noun. Its flesh/body component helps with recognition. There is no two-kanji word to split, and the meaning comes from the whole character here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

皮膚 (ひふ) is skin as a bodily covering, useful in anatomical or medical discussion. [皮](皮.md) (かわ) can mean skin, hide, peel, or an outer covering; it is not a direct replacement in every description of someone's 肌.

## Distinctive meaning

肌 can extend beyond the body to a surface or texture, as in 木の肌 (きのはだ), "the surface/grain of wood." A skincare context makes the body sense clear, while a materials context may not. The word itself does not specify a particular skin color, complexion, or judgment of beauty.
