# 手前 (てまえ) - this side; one's position

## Meaning

手前 can mean the area close in front of you or the nearer side of a reference point. With a landmark it often means before reaching that landmark.

- 手前の席に座ってください。 (てまえのせきにすわってください。) - Please sit in the seat nearer to us.
- 駅の手前で右に曲がってください。 (えきのてまえでみぎにまがってください。) - Please turn right before you reach the station.

It also concerns one's position in other people's eyes. 言い出した手前、断れません (いいだしたてまえ、ことわれません) means "Having proposed it myself, I cannot very well refuse." The earlier action creates a social constraint.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md) suggests one's own sphere and [前](../kanji/前.md) the front. The native readings て + まえ help remember the near-side meaning; they do not turn every social use into a literal body-part description.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[前](前.md) is broader spatially and temporally. [こちら](こちら.md) can mean this side, and [立場](立場.md) is one's position or standpoint. In a spatial contrast, 向こう側 (むこうがわ), "the other side," may oppose 手前; it is not an antonym for every use.

## Distinctive meaning

Dictionaries also record skill, tea-ceremony procedure, humble "I/me," and male-to-inferior "you" uses. These need their own context and register; the pronouns are not neutral substitutes for [私](私.md) or [あなた](あなた.md). お手前 (おてまえ) occurs in skill and tea contexts. Do not learn the source list's "we" as an ordinary plural-pronoun rule. The separate rough pronunciation てめえ is not the てまえ reading practiced here.
