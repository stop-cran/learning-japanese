# 頂点 (ちょうてん) - summit; apex; vertex

## Meaning

頂点 is a summit or highest point. It also names a geometrical vertex
and, figuratively, the top of a field or the peak of a development.

- 山の頂点に立ちました。
  (やまのちょうてんにたちました。) - I stood at the mountain's summit.
- 三角形には三つの頂点があります。
  (さんかくけいにはみっつのちょうてんがあります。) - A triangle has three vertices.
- 彼は人気の頂点にいました。
  (かれはにんきのちょうてんにいました。) - He was at the height of his popularity.

## How the kanji combine

[頂](../kanji/頂.md) contributes summit or top, and
[点](../kanji/点.md) contributes point.
The combination brings the topmost or defining point into focus.
The on readings are チョウ + テン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頂上](頂上.md) (ちょうじょう) overlaps directly for a summit.
[トップ](トップ.md) overlaps in a leading position.
[盛り](盛り.md) (さかり) concerns a peak or flourishing period.
[先端](先端.md) (せんたん) can name a tip or apex,
but not every projecting tip is a mountain summit.

## Distinctive meaning

A vertex need not be at the top of the page: rotating a triangle does not
remove its vertices. Context distinguishes geometry from a peak of popularity.
Broader [上](上.md) (うえ), top/above, and [点](点.md) (てん), point,
do not specify every property of 頂点.
[山](山.md) (やま) also has a climax/peak sense;
[頭](頭.md) (あたま), [先](先.md) (さき),
[端](端.md) (はし), and [末](末.md) (すえ)
have top, tip, or end uses beyond their primary lessons.
These overlaps do not make all their constructions interchangeable.
