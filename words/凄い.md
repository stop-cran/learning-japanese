# 凄い (すごい) — amazing

## Meaning

凄い means amazing, terrific, or extraordinary in degree. It is an い-adjective and is usually written すごい in kana.

この選手はすごいです。 (このせんしゅはすごいです。) — This player is amazing.

昨日はすごい雨でした。 (きのうはすごいあめでした。) — The rain was tremendous yesterday.

## How the kanji combine

凄 writes すご; い completes the adjective. Standard forms include すごかった, "was amazing," and the adverbial すごく, "very."

## Synonyms and antonyms

素晴らしい (すばらしい), "wonderful," is more clearly favorable. ひどい, "terrible," can describe a negative extreme. Neither is a replacement for every use of すごい.

## Distinctive meaning

すごい does not always praise something: it can express an overwhelming or dreadful intensity. In casual speech it also appears directly before another adjective, as in すごい寒い (すごいさむい), "really cold." For standard neutral grammar, use すごく寒い instead. The reaction すごい！ often means "Wow!"
