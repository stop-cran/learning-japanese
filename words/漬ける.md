# 漬ける (つける) — soak; pickle

## Meaning

漬ける means to soak something in liquid, or to pickle or preserve it in salt, vinegar, and similar ingredients. It is transitive: use を for the item and に for what it is placed in.

- 野菜を塩水に漬けます。  
  やさいをしおみずにつけます。  
  "I pickle the vegetables in brine."
- 豆を水に浸けておきます。  
  まめをみずにつけておきます。  
  "I'll leave the beans soaking in water."

浸ける is another spelling for the soaking sense; the pickling sense specifically uses 漬ける.

## How the kanji combine

漬 carries the native reading つ, followed by ける. This is ichidan: 漬けます (つけます), 漬けて (つけて), 漬けない (つけない). The verb stem includes け.

## Synonyms and antonyms

浸す (ひたす), "soak; immerse", overlaps with the liquid-soaking use. 漬かる (つかる) describes something being immersed or pickled, rather than someone placing it there.

## Distinctive meaning

付ける, also つける, is a different verb meaning "attach" among other uses. Learn the food-and-liquid pattern here rather than transferring every meaning of the sound つける to 漬ける.
