# あたりまえ - natural; obvious; ordinary

## Meaning

あたりまえ describes what is natural, reasonable or obvious in the circumstances.
It can also mean ordinary or taken as the normal state of affairs. Useful patterns
include あたりまえだ, "that is only natural," and あたりまえのこと, "an ordinary or obvious thing."

- 約束を守るのはあたりまえです。 (やくそくをまもるのはあたりまえです。) - Keeping your promises is only to be expected.
- 毎日水が使えることを、あたりまえだと思っていました。 (まいにちみずがつかえることを、あたりまえだとおもっていました。) - I had taken being able to use water every day for granted.

The second example concerns something treated as normal, not necessarily something unimportant.

## How the kanji combine

The word is also written 当たり前, with [当](../kanji/当.md) and [前](../kanji/前.md).
Learn the complete expression あたりまえ; translating the characters separately
does not explain its present-day uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[普通](普通.md) (ふつう) overlaps in "ordinary; normal."
[もっとも](もっとも.md) can mean reasonable or justified.
[明らか](明らか.md) (あきらか) concerns something evident or clear.
These overlap, but do not replace one another in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

あたりまえだ can express a speaker's expectation rather than an independently proved fact.
Saying あたりまえでしょう, "of course," to another person can sound dismissive
when they were genuinely asking for help. The tone and context matter.
