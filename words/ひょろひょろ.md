# ひょろひょろ — spindly, frail, or unsteady

## Meaning

ひょろひょろ describes something thin and weak-looking or movement that is unsteady.

- ひょろひょろした苗は、風で大きく揺れていた。
  — The spindly seedling swayed a lot in the wind.
- 彼は疲れて、ひょろひょろした足取りで家に帰った。
  — Tired, he walked home with unsteady steps.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ひょろひょろ suggests thinness or instability, not simply being tall or slender.

## Distinctive meaning

The context distinguishes a spindly shape from unsteady movement.
