# 至急 (しきゅう) - urgent; as soon as possible

## Meaning

至急 conveys urgency: urgently, promptly, immediately, or as soon as possible. It can modify an action directly. Before a noun, learn 至急の, not 至急な.

- 至急、連絡してください。 (しきゅう、れんらくしてください。) - Please contact us as soon as possible.
- 至急の用件で電話しました。 (しきゅうのようけんででんわしました。) - I called about an urgent matter.

The dictionary records both the pressing or immediate quality and the adverbial instruction to act promptly.

## How the kanji combine

至 contributes reaching an extreme or utmost degree, and [急](../kanji/急.md) contributes urgency or haste. The first part intensifies the second. The on readings シ + キュウ give しきゅう; keep the long final vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[急ぐ](急ぐ.md) いそぐ is the verb hurry. [すぐに](すぐに.md) and [直ちに](直ちに.md) ただちに overlap in immediate timing; [早速](早速.md) さっそく often introduces prompt action after an opportunity or trigger. These overlap rather than marking exact numbers of seconds.

## Distinctive meaning

至急 requests prompt attention, not simply high movement speed. It can appear in a notice, message, or spoken request. An urgent administrative matter need not involve physical danger: urgency is not automatically an emergency diagnosis.
