# 心配 (しんぱい) — worry; concern

## Meaning

心配 is worry or concern about someone or something. Two useful patterns are Xが心配だ, "be worried about X,"
and Xを心配する, "worry about X." It also modifies nouns with な.

- 明日の試験が心配です。 (あしたのしけんがしんぱいです。) — I am worried about tomorrow's exam.
- 母は私のことを心配しています。 (はははわたしのことをしんぱいしています。) — My mother is worried about me.

## How the kanji combine

[心](../kanji/心.md) concerns the heart or mind; [配](../kanji/配.md) has distribution or allocation meanings in other words.
For learning, imagine the mind directed toward someone or something, but learn "worry" as the compound's established meaning,
not as a literal translation or claimed etymology. シン + ハイ is realized here as しんぱい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不安](不安.md) (ふあん) overlaps in anxiety and uneasiness.
[安心](安心.md) (あんしん), "peace of mind; relief," gives a useful emotional contrast.
心配する lets you name the object of concern with を; 不安 is not a drop-in replacement in that same verb pattern.

## Distinctive meaning

心配 can express caring concern, not just a judgment that something will fail.
心配しないでください (しんぱいしないでください), "Please do not worry," addresses the listener's concern;
it is reassurance, not proof that there is no risk.
