# 漁業 (ぎょぎょう) - fishing industry; fishery

## Meaning

漁業 names fishing as an industry or occupation, or a fishery. It is a noun,
not the ordinary verb for catching an individual fish.

- この町では漁業が盛んです。 (このまちではぎょぎょうがさかんです。) - The fishing industry is thriving in this town.
- 父は漁業に従事しています。 (ちちはぎょぎょうにじゅうじしています。) - My father works in the fishing industry.

In the second example, に従事する means to work in or engage in a field.

## How the kanji combine

[漁](../kanji/漁.md), fishing, modifies [業](../kanji/業.md), occupation or industry.
The readings are ギョ + ギョウ: ぎょぎょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[釣り](釣り.md) (つり) specifically names angling. It can be recreational or
professional, so "leisure versus work" is not an absolute boundary.
漁 (りょう) is a broader fishing/catch word; [魚](魚.md) (さかな) names the fish,
not the industry.

## Distinctive meaning

漁業 directs attention to an economic activity or field of work. It does not
specify a particular fishing method. Do not infer that every 漁業 activity uses
a fishing rod or that every occurrence names one building or one boat.
