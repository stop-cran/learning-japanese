# 改めて (あらためて) - again; anew; formally

## Meaning

改めて is an adverb for doing something again, afresh or on another occasion.
It can also introduce a deliberate or proper statement, such as a fresh introduction
or an expression of thanks.

- 後日、改めてご連絡します。 (ごじつ、あらためてごれんらくします。) - I will contact you again at a later date.
- 改めて自己紹介をします。 (あらためてじこしょうかいをします。) - Let me introduce myself properly.

The second use need not count an exact second performance. Context can emphasize
taking the occasion to do something deliberately.

## How the kanji combine

[改](../kanji/改.md) concerns change or renewal. The expression is related to
[改める](改める.md) (あらためる), but learn its established adverbial uses;
not every 改めて means "revising something."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[再び](再び.md) (ふたたび), [また](また.md), and [もう一度](もう一度.md)
(もういちど) overlap in repetition. 改めて can put particular focus on a fresh
occasion or renewed attention, rather than simple repetition alone.

## Distinctive meaning

"Formally" here can concern a deliberate, proper introduction or statement.
It does not automatically mean officially authorized:
[正式](正式.md) (せいしき), "official; formal," has its own uses.
An ordinary contact made 改めて need not be a ceremony.
