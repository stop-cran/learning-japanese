# 必要 (ひつよう) — necessary; needed

## Meaning

必要 describes something necessary or needed. Use Nが必要だ to say that N is needed,
and 必要なN before a noun.

- この仕事には車が必要です。 (このしごとにはくるまがひつようです。) — A car is needed for this job.
- 必要な物を持ってきてください。 (ひつようなものをもってきてください。) — Please bring the things you need.

The first example names both the purpose and what it requires.

## How the kanji combine

[必](../kanji/必.md) conveys necessity or something that must be so; [要](../kanji/要.md) contributes
the idea of being needed or essential. The two meanings reinforce each other. The on readings combine
as ヒツ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

大切 (たいせつ), "important; precious," is not identical: a valued possession need not be necessary
for a particular task. 不要 (ふよう), "unnecessary," is a direct contrast when something is not needed.

## Distinctive meaning

必要 states a requirement, not merely a wish. Keep the な attachment in 必要な道具
(ひつようなどうぐ), "necessary tools."
