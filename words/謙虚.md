# 謙虚 (けんきょ) - modest; humble

## Meaning

謙虚 describes a modest or humble attitude: not being boastful or treating oneself as above learning from others.
It is a noun and a na-adjective: 謙虚な before a noun and 謙虚に when modifying an action.

- 彼は成功しても謙虚です。 (かれはせいこうしてもけんきょです。) - He remains humble even after succeeding.
- 先生の助言を謙虚に受け止めます。 (せんせいのじょげんをけんきょにうけとめます。) - I accept the teacher's advice with humility.

## How the kanji combine

謙 contributes humility; 虚 has character meanings including emptiness or openness.
As a mnemonic, imagine making room for another person's ideas instead of being full of one's own importance.
This image is not a historical derivation or a claim that 虚 always has a positive meaning.
The on readings combine as ケン + キョ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

謙遜 (けんそん) can refer to modesty or self-deprecation.
[遠慮](遠慮.md) (えんりょ) often concerns holding back out of consideration;
[恐縮](恐縮.md) (きょうしゅく) concerns feeling obliged or apologetically grateful.
They can occur alongside humility, but do not describe exactly the same attitude in every use.
傲慢 (ごうまん), arrogant, supplies a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Being 謙虚 does not require denying all one's abilities, staying silent, or accepting every claim as true.
It is not the technical name for humble-language verb forms.
A modest dwelling or low-quality object is not automatically 謙虚 merely because English can call it "humble."
