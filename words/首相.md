# 首相 (しゅしょう) - prime minister

## Meaning

首相 is a prime minister or comparable head of government. In reporting on
Germany or Austria, the English equivalent is "chancellor."
It is a noun and can follow a country name with の: 日本の首相
(にほんのしゅしょう), "Japan's prime minister."

- 首相は記者の質問に答えました。 (しゅしょうはきしゃのしつもんにこたえました。) - The prime minister answered the reporter's question.
- 両国の首相が会談しました。 (りょうこくのしゅしょうがかいだんしました。) - The two countries' prime ministers held talks.

The word identifies the office, without giving the officeholder's personal name.

## How the kanji combine

[首](../kanji/首.md) contributes "head; chief," and [相](../kanji/相.md) here
contributes "minister." The relationship is chief plus office: the leading minister.
The on readings combine as しゅ + しょう; 首 is not read くび in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

内閣総理大臣 (ないかくそうりだいじん) is the full title of Japan's prime
minister. [大臣](大臣.md) (だいじん) is a minister more generally.
[大統領](大統領.md) (だいとうりょう), "president," is a different office,
not an antonym or a freely interchangeable translation.

## Distinctive meaning

首相 can refer to leaders outside Japan, so do not build "Japanese" into
the word's definition. Government systems differ, and an English title should
match the country being discussed. Likewise, the [議会](議会.md) (ぎかい)
is an assembly, not another word for the individual heading the government.
