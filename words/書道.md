# 書道 (しょどう) - calligraphy

## Meaning

書道 means calligraphy, especially the East Asian art and discipline of
writing characters beautifully. A typical setting involves writing with
a brush, not merely reading the characters.

- 毎週土曜日に書道を習っています。 (まいしゅうどようびにしょどうをならっています。) - I take calligraphy lessons every Saturday.
- 書道の作品を見に行きました。 (しょどうのさくひんをみにいきました。) - I went to see works of calligraphy.

書道の作品 names a work of calligraphy; 書道 itself names the field.

## How the kanji combine

[書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing, and [道](../kanji/道.md)
contributes a way or discipline here. Together they identify a discipline
of writing, not a physical road. The on readings are ショ + ドウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[習字](習字.md) (しゅうじ), penmanship or calligraphy, emphasizes learning
to write characters well. It overlaps with 書道; do not invent an absolute
division into practice versus art or pen versus brush.

[字](字.md) (じ) can mean handwriting. [筆](筆.md) (ふで) names a brush
and also has extended writing and penmanship senses. Distinguish a tool,
someone's writing, and the artistic discipline.

## Distinctive meaning

A 書道 lesson can include basic practice; the word does not guarantee an
exhibition-quality result. [芸術](芸術.md) (げいじゅつ), the arts, is the
broader field, not a synonym for calligraphy alone.
