# 注ぐ (そそぐ) - pour; devote

## Meaning

注ぐ (そそぐ) means to pour liquid into or onto something. It is a godan verb: 注ぎます, 注いだ, 注いで. In the transitive pattern, liquid takes を and the destination takes に.

- カップにお湯を注いでください。 (カップにおゆをそそいでください。) - Please pour hot water into the cup.

It can also describe pouring water onto plants: 花に水を注ぐ (はなにみずをそそぐ). Figuratively, effort, attention, or affection can be directed toward something.

- 彼女は研究に力を注いでいます。 (かのじょはけんきゅうにちからをそそいでいます。) - She is devoting her energies to research.

注ぐ (つぐ) is a separate, narrower lexical identity for pouring into a vessel or serving food or drink. Kana つぐ is usual. It is also godan: つぎます, ついだ, ついで.

- グラスに水をつぎました。 (グラスにみずをつぎました。) - I poured water into the glass.

## How the kanji combine

[注](../kanji/注.md) represents pouring or directing something toward a target. The single written form does not make the two native verbs interchangeable across all meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入れる](入れる.md) (いれる) broadly means putting something in. [流す](流す.md) (ながす) focuses on making something flow. Pouring into a cup is a shared context, not proof that all their uses match.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not extend つぐ to the watering or figurative-devotion senses taught for そそぐ. The source's bare gloss "pay" is misleading: devotion of attention is not paying money, and the つぐ entry does not support either that figurative meaning or "irrigate."
