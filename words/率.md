# 率 (りつ) - rate; proportion

## Meaning

率 is a rate, ratio, or proportion. It is a noun and an element attached to other nouns: 失業率 (しつぎょうりつ), "unemployment rate," and 合格率 (ごうかくりつ), "pass rate."

- この試験の合格率は六十パーセントです。 (このしけんのごうかくりつはろくじゅうパーセントです。) - The pass rate for this exam is sixty percent.
- 失業率が下がりました。 (しつぎょうりつがさがりました。) - The unemployment rate fell.

The preceding noun tells you what is being measured. 率 by itself does not identify the relevant population or period.

## How the kanji combine

率 is a single-kanji noun using the on reading リツ. In compounds, it supplies the rate or proportion component. There is no second written element in the standalone word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[割合](割合.md) (わりあい) also describes a proportion or ratio. [数](数.md) (かず) identifies a number or count. A count and a rate can change differently because a rate depends on what it is compared with.

## Distinctive meaning

A percentage is one way to express a 率, not a separate meaning imposed by the kanji. A higher 合格率 does not by itself prove that more people passed: the number taking the exam may also have changed. The compound [失業](失業.md) supplies the employment context for 失業率.
