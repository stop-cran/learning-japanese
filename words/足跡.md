# 足跡 (あしあと) - footprints

## Meaning

足跡 means footprints: marks left by feet.
Use 足跡が残る (あしあとがのこる), "footprints remain," or
足跡をたどる (あしあとをたどる), "follow footprints."

- 雪の上に足跡が残っています。 (ゆきのうえにあしあとがのこっています。) - Footprints remain in the snow.
- 犬の足跡を見つけました。 (いぬのあしあとをみつけました。) - I found dog footprints.

The marks can be made by people or animals.

## How the kanji combine

[足](../kanji/足.md), "foot," identifies the source of the
[跡](../kanji/跡.md), "trace." The native readings あし + あと give あしあと.
The dictionary also records そくせき for the spelling 足跡; this card's primary reading
is あしあと, not a mandatory on-reading combination.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[跡](跡.md) (あと) is broader: a trace need not be a footprint.
足音 (あしおと), "footsteps; sound of steps," concerns sound rather than marks.
There is no useful lexical opposite of a footprint.

## Distinctive meaning

The physical marks need not be newly made or still connected with a visible walker.
The dictionary also records a computing use for a record of page visitors, as on a social
networking site. That is a separate application, not proof that every use concerns literal feet.
