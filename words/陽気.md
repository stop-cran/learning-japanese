# 陽気 (ようき) - cheerful; seasonal weather

## Meaning

陽気 has two useful everyday uses. As a na-adjective, it means cheerful, lively, or jovial. As a noun, it can mean the weather or seasonal conditions.

- 彼は陽気な人です。 (かれはようきなひとです。) - He is a cheerful person.
- 今日は春のような陽気です。 (きょうははるのようなようきです。) - The weather is springlike today.

For behavior, 陽気に笑う (ようきにわらう) means "laugh cheerfully." In the weather example, 陽気 is the noun being described, not a personality attributed to the day.

## How the kanji combine

[陽](../kanji/陽.md) contributes sunlight or the bright/yang side, and [気](../kanji/気.md) contributes air, atmosphere, or disposition. These associations help connect the weather and cheerful-disposition uses without making them identical. The on readings are ヨウ + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[明るい](明るい.md) (あかるい) can also describe a cheerful personality. 陰気 (いんき), "gloomy," is a direct contrast in mood or atmosphere. For the weather meaning, compare 天気 (てんき), "weather," rather than a personality adjective.

## Distinctive meaning

陽気 does not identify a calendar season by itself: 春のような陽気 can occur before spring has fully arrived. Likewise, 陽気な人 concerns a person's cheerful manner, not necessarily their reliability, intelligence, or kindness.
