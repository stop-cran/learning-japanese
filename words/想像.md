# 想像 (そうぞう) - imagination; supposition

## Meaning

想像 is imagination or the act of forming an idea about something not directly known. As a transitive する verb, it takes the imagined situation with を: 未来を想像する (みらいをそうぞうする), "imagine the future."

- 十年後の生活を想像してみてください。 (じゅうねんごのせいかつをそうぞうしてみてください。) - Try imagining your life ten years from now.
- 彼の気持ちは想像できません。 (かれのきもちはそうぞうできません。) - I cannot imagine how he feels.

想像では (そうぞうでは) can introduce what one supposes rather than what one has verified.

## How the kanji combine

[想](../kanji/想.md) contributes thought or imagining. 像 contributes an image or representation. Together they suggest forming a mental image; that image can be a possible reality, not just a fantasy.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予想](予想.md) (よそう) concerns predicting or expecting something, especially an outcome. 想像 is not limited to future events. [現実](現実.md) (げんじつ), "reality," contrasts with what is imagined, but an imagined situation can still turn out to be real.

## Distinctive meaning

想像する does not guarantee a correct guess. It can express empathy, speculation, or creative visualization. In 想像以上に難しかった (そうぞういじょうにむずかしかった), "It was harder than I imagined," 想像 provides the prior mental expectation against which the actual experience is compared.
