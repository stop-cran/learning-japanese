# ユーモア (ユーモア) - humor

## Meaning

ユーモア is a noun for humor: the amusing quality of something said, written, or done. Use ユーモアがある, "have humor/be humorous," and ユーモアのある before a noun.

- 彼女はユーモアのある人です。 (かのじょはユーモアのあるひとです。) - She is a person with a sense of humor.
- その話にはユーモアがあって、みんなが笑いました。 (そのはなしにはユーモアがあって、みんながわらいました。) - The story had humor, and everyone laughed.

ユーモアを交える (ユーモアをまじえる) means "include some humor" in a talk or explanation.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword has no kanji components. The first vowel is long: ユー. Learn the whole spelling ユーモア rather than inserting an extra syllable from the pronunciation of an English variety.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冗談](冗談.md) (じょうだん) is a joke or something said in jest. ユーモア can name the quality running through a story rather than one isolated joke. [笑い](笑い.md) (わらい) is laughter or amusement, which may be a response to humor.

## Distinctive meaning

ユーモアがある praises or identifies an amusing quality; it does not guarantee that every listener will find it funny. Likewise, a remark described as humor can still be poorly received. The word concerns humor, not the unrelated English sense of "humor" meaning a person's current mood.
