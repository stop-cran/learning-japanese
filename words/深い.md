# 深い (ふかい) — deep

## Meaning

深い describes depth, as in a deep river, hole, or stretch of snow.
It is an い-adjective: it can directly describe a following noun or finish a sentence.

- この川は深いです。 (このかわはふかいです。) — This river is deep.
- 深い穴があります。 (ふかいあながあります。) — There is a deep hole.

Use 深い川 (ふかいかわ), "a deep river," without な.
The negative form is 深くない (ふかくない), "not deep."

## How the kanji combine

[深](../kanji/深.md) carries the depth meaning, and い is the adjective ending.
The native reading is ふか- in this word.
There is no second kanji whose meaning needs to be combined with it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

浅い (あさい), "shallow," is the useful opposite for a river or hole.
Compare 深い川 (ふかいかわ), "a deep river," with 浅い川 (あさいかわ), "a shallow river."
This contrasts depth, not the river's width or length.

## Distinctive meaning

For the physical sense here, 深い directs attention down from a surface or into an interior.
A river can be narrow and still be deep, so the word is not a general replacement for "large."
