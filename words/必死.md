# 必死 (ひっし) - desperate; frantic

## Meaning

必死 describes desperate, frantic, or all-out effort. It functions adjectivally: 必死な姿 (ひっしなすがた), "a desperate appearance," and 必死の努力 (ひっしのどりょく), "desperate efforts." Use 必死に before an action.

- 電車に間に合うように必死に走りました。 (でんしゃにまにあうようにひっしにはしりました。) - I ran desperately to catch the train.
- 彼女は必死に笑いをこらえました。 (かのじょはひっしにわらいをこらえました。) - She tried desperately to hold back her laughter.

These examples concern intense effort, not literal danger to life. The dictionary also records the noun meaning "inevitable death."

## How the kanji combine

[必](../kanji/必.md) contributes certainty and [死](../kanji/死.md) contributes death, matching that literal noun sense. Modern 必死に frequently describes intense effort without a life-or-death situation. Read ひっし, with small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一生懸命](一生懸命.md) (いっしょうけんめい) emphasizes doing one's best. [真剣](真剣.md) (しんけん) emphasizes seriousness. They overlap, but 必死 can add urgency or desperation.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 必死 with 必至 (ひっし), a separately written word meaning "inevitable; unavoidable." The source's general "inevitable result" gloss belongs to that distinction, not to every use of 必死. An outcome can be inevitable without anyone making a desperate effort. The contrast does not assign 必至 an independent N3 level here.
