# 玄関 (げんかん) — entrance; entryway

## Meaning

玄関 is the entrance or entryway of a building. In a Japanese home, it commonly refers to the area just inside the entrance where shoes are removed before entering the raised indoor floor.

玄関で靴を脱いでください。 (げんかんでくつをぬいでください。) — Please take off your shoes in the entryway.

## How the kanji combine

玄 has associations with depth or mystery; [関](../kanji/関.md) can mean a barrier or gateway. The compound has the on-readings げん + かん. Its present meaning should be learned as a whole, not as a literal description of a dark room.

## Synonyms and antonyms

入口 (いりぐち) is an entrance more generally, including openings into places that are not homes. ドア is the physical door. 出口 (でぐち), "exit," contrasts with entrance function, but is not a name for the opposite kind of domestic room.

## Distinctive meaning

玄関 can include the entrance area, not just the moving door panel. Useful phrases are 玄関の鍵 (げんかんのかぎ), "the front-door key," and 玄関に置く (げんかんにおく), "put it in the entryway."
