# 限界 (げんかい) - limit; boundary

## Meaning

限界 is a limit or boundary beyond which something cannot, or is not expected
to, go. It can concern endurance, ability, knowledge, or capacity.
Use Nには限界がある, "N has limits," and 限界に達する
(げんかいにたっする), "reach the limit."

- 私の体力にも限界があります。 (わたしのたいりょくにもげんかいがあります。) - My physical strength has its limits too.
- もう限界です。少し休ませてください。 (もうげんかいです。すこしやすませてください。) - I am at my limit. Please let me rest a little.

The second example leaves the exact kind of limit to the situation.

## How the kanji combine

[限](../kanji/限.md) contributes a limit, and [界](../kanji/界.md) contributes
a boundary or bounded area. Their related meanings reinforce the idea of an
outer bound. The on readings are げん + かい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[制限](制限.md) (せいげん) is a restriction or limitation, including one imposed
by a rule. 限界 can describe a capacity without anyone setting a rule.
限度 (げんど) also means a limit and overlaps. These words do not impose an
absolute natural-versus-artificial distinction; check what is being limited.

## Distinctive meaning

限界を超える (げんかいをこえる), "go beyond the limit," can describe exceeding
a known or previously assumed boundary. It need not be a logical contradiction:
the "limit" may be a practical estimate or a person's current endurance.
限界 itself is a noun, so "limited" is not expressed by treating it as an
English-style adjective in every sentence.
