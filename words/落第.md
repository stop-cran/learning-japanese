# 落第 (らくだい) - examination failure; not making the grade

## Meaning

落第 means failing an examination or failing to advance to the next school year.
It is a noun and an intransitive する verb: 試験に落第する
(しけんにらくだいする), "fail an examination."
It also extends to falling short of an expected standard.

- 試験に落第して、もう一度勉強し直しました。 (しけんにらくだいして、もういちどべんきょうしなおしました。) - I failed the examination and studied the material again.
- 約束を守れないようでは、リーダーとして落第だ。 (やくそくをまもれないようでは、リーダーとしてらくだいだ。) - If you cannot keep your promises, you do not make the grade as a leader.

## How the kanji combine

[落](../kanji/落.md) contributes falling or failing, and 第 concerns
rank or order. Together the word concerns failing to reach the required
grade or standard. The on readings are ラク + ダイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[落ちる](落ちる.md) (おちる) also means failing an examination.
[失敗](失敗.md) (しっぱい) is failure more generally.
[合格](合格.md) (ごうかく), passing or meeting the standard, provides a contrast.
留年 (りゅうねん) names repeating a school year.

## Distinctive meaning

Not advancing a year and leaving school are different outcomes.
Do not learn the source gloss "dropping out of a class" as a general translation:
退学 (たいがく) and 中退 (ちゅうたい) concern leaving school before completion.
