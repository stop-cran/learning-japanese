# 栗 (くり) - chestnut tree; chestnut

## Meaning

栗 names the chestnut tree and its edible nut. The pinned JMdict entry identifies Japanese chestnut, *Castanea crenata*; Japanese dictionaries also describe other cultivated chestnuts.

- 栗を食べました。 (くりをたべました。) - I ate chestnuts.
- 庭に栗の木があります。 (にわにくりのきがあります。) - There is a chestnut tree in the garden.

栗の木 (くりのき) specifies the tree. 栗の実 (くりのみ) specifies the nut; the noun itself has no separate plural ending.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [栗](../kanji/栗.md) writes the whole native noun くり. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide. The dictionary also marks the word as usually written in kana; くり and クリ are spellings to recognize, not different species established by the script.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[木](木.md) (き), tree or wood, and [植物](植物.md) (しょくぶつ), plant, are broader categories. [実](実.md) (み) and [果実](果実.md) (かじつ) can refer to nuts as well as other fruit. They do not identify the chestnut species. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Context can identify the tree, its nut or its timber. 栗色 (くりいろ) means chestnut brown, a colour like the nut's shell; dictionaries also record 栗 as an abbreviation of this colour name. These senses do not make 栗 a general substitute for every tree, nut, timber or brown object.
