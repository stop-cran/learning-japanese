# 準備 (じゅんび) - preparation; getting ready

## Meaning

準備 is preparation or getting ready. Add する to make the verb "prepare": 準備します, 準備して and 準備した.

- 旅行の準備をしています。 (りょこうのじゅんびをしています。) - I am getting ready for a trip.
- 会議の資料を準備しました。 (かいぎのしりょうをじゅんびしました。) - I prepared the meeting materials.

Noun + の準備 identifies what the preparation is for. With 準備する, を can identify the thing being prepared.

## How the kanji combine

準 is read じゅん; [備](../kanji/備.md), associated with equipping or providing, is び. Learn the compound as preparation, rather than assuming every use involves an official standard.

## Synonyms and antonyms

用意 (ようい) also means preparation, with considerable overlap. 練習 (れんしゅう), "practice," is one possible preparatory activity, not a general synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The bare word 準備 is a noun, even when an English vocabulary list glosses it "to prepare." A sentence needs する or another verb: 準備ができました means "The preparations are complete."
