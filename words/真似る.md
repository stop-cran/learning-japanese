# 真似る (まねる) - imitate; mimic

## Meaning

真似る means to imitate or mimic. It is a transitive ichidan verb:
the person, voice, style, or behavior imitated takes を.

- 子供が先生の話し方を真似ています。 (こどもがせんせいのはなしかたをまねています。) - The child is imitating the teacher's way of speaking.
- 見本を真似て、絵を描きました。 (みほんをまねて、えをかきました。) - I drew a picture by copying the model.

Forms include 真似ます (まねます), 真似た (まねた),
真似て (まねて), and 真似ない (まねない).

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md) associates with what is real, and
[似](../kanji/似.md) with resemblance. The established native word is
まねる; these associations are a spelling aid, not proof of its history
or an instruction to read シン + ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[真似](真似.md) (まね) is the related noun, also used with する.
[倣う](倣う.md) (ならう) concerns following a model and normally marks
that model with に. [似る](似る.md) (にる) means resemble:
彼は父に似ている, he resembles his father, need not involve imitation.

## Distinctive meaning

Copying is not inherently mockery. It can be practice, emulation, or
entertainment. Distinguish reproducing someone's manner from simply
sharing similar features; the resemblance verb has different grammar
and does not itself say that anyone copied anything.
