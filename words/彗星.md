# 彗星 (すいせい) - comet

## Meaning

彗星 is a **comet**.

- 望遠鏡で彗星を見ました。 (ぼうえんきょうですいせいをみました。) - I saw a comet through a telescope.
- この写真には彗星が写っています。 (このしゃしんにはすいせいがうつっています。) - A comet appears in this photograph.

The telescope is the means of observation in the first sentence; the photograph is where the comet's image appears in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[彗](../kanji/彗.md) has broom and comet senses; [星](../kanji/星.md) supplies the celestial-object element. Read the compound **スイ + セイ**. The broom association is a memory aid for a comet's tail, not a claim that a comet is a star in the modern astronomical sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

流星 (りゅうせい), also called 流れ星 (ながれぼし), is a meteor or "shooting star," not a comet. [星](星.md) (ほし) is a broader everyday sky-word, but it does not replace the specific astronomical term 彗星. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not call every brief streak of light a 彗星. This word identifies a comet, not simply anything bright that appears to move across the sky. The reading すいせい also has unrelated homophones; the written form and context identify this word.
