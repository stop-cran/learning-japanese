# 出版 (しゅっぱん) - publication; publishing

## Meaning

出版 is publication, especially bringing a book or similar work out for readers.
It is a noun and a transitive する verb: 本を出版する means "publish a book."
Use 出版される when the work is the subject: "be published."

- 姉は去年、小説を出版しました。
  (あねはきょねん、しょうせつをしゅっぱんしました。) - My older sister published a novel last year.
- この本は来月出版されます。
  (このほんはらいげつしゅっぱんされます。) - This book will be published next month.

出版社 (しゅっぱんしゃ) is a publishing company. 出版 can name the activity
or field without referring to a particular physical copy.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) contributes putting out, and 版 concerns a printing plate or edition.
Together they suggest bringing an edition out.
The combined on reading is しゅっぱん: notice both the small っ and the ぱ sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[印刷](印刷.md) (いんさつ) is printing, the reproduction of text or images.
[発表](発表.md) (はっぴょう) is announcement or presentation.
Printing pages or announcing a plan is not by itself the same event as publishing a book.

## Distinctive meaning

出版 concerns making a work available as a publication, not just writing it.
An author can finish a manuscript before it is published, and a publisher may issue a
work the publisher did not write. Keep authoring, printing, and publication distinct.
