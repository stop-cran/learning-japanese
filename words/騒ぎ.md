# 騒ぎ (さわぎ) - uproar; commotion

## Meaning

騒ぎ is a noun for a commotion, disturbance, or uproar. It can involve noisy activity or a situation that attracts an agitated reaction. Useful patterns include 騒ぎになる (さわぎになる), "turn into an uproar," and 騒ぎを起こす (さわぎをおこす), "cause a disturbance."

- 外で何か騒ぎが起きています。 (そとでなにかさわぎがおきています。) - There is some kind of commotion outside.
- 小さな間違いが大きな騒ぎになりました。 (ちいさなまちがいがおおきなさわぎになりました。) - A small mistake turned into a major uproar.

The noun names the disturbance, not necessarily its cause.

## How the kanji combine

騒 contributes the idea of noisy or agitated activity. 騒ぎ is related to the native verb [騒ぐ](騒ぐ.md) (さわぐ), with ぎ marking the noun form here. There is no on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[事件](事件.md) (じけん) names an incident; an incident can cause a 騒ぎ, but the two words highlight different things. [静か](静か.md) (しずか), "quiet," gives a contextual contrast rather than a noun antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

騒ぎ need not imply violence or criminal behavior. It can describe a fuss over news or a mistake. The context supplies whether the event is serious, amusing, or merely inconvenient.
