# 別に (べつに) - not particularly; separately

## Meaning

別に has two distinct adverbial uses.
With a negative clause it means **not particularly/not especially**.
In an affirmative clause it can mean **separately** or **additionally**.

- 別に怒っていません。 (べつにおこっていません。) - I am not particularly angry.
- 飲み物の代金は別に払います。 (のみもののだいきんはべつにはらいます。) - I will pay for the drinks separately.

In the first example the negative いません completes the relevant pattern.
In the second, 別に describes a separate payment; it does not mean "not particularly paying."

## How the kanji combine

[別](../kanji/別.md) contributes separation or difference, and に makes the established adverbial expression.
The negative construction develops the idea of nothing especially singled out.
Learn the whole reading べつに and identify its use from the clause rather than the character alone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[特に](特に.md) (とくに), "especially," overlaps in negative expressions such as 特に問題はない.
別々 (べつべつ) emphasizes things being separate.
[一緒](一緒.md) (いっしょ), "together," contrasts with the separate-arrangement sense, not with every use.

## Distinctive meaning

A standalone 別に can leave an understood negative unsaid, producing an English response like
"Nothing in particular" or "Not really."
It is not a noun freely meaning "nothing"; the missing clause comes from context.
A short response can sound dismissive depending on tone.
For a clear learner sentence, keep the negative predicate explicit unless the conversational context is supplied.
