# 今月 (こんげつ) — this month

## Meaning

今月 is the current month: 今月は忙しい (I'm busy this month), 今月の予定 (this month's schedule), 今月末 こんげつまつ (the end of this month).

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) (this) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month), both with on'yomi: コン + ゲツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Series: 先月 せんげつ (last month), 今月 こんげつ, 来月 らいげつ (next month).
- Related: [毎月](毎月.md) まいつき (every month).

## Distinctive meaning

The pattern 今 + noun gives "this ..." (今週, 今年, 今朝), but 今年 ことし and 今朝 けさ have special readings, while 今月 is regular.
