# 短期 (たんき) - short term; short period

## Meaning

短期 is a short period or the short term. It is a noun: 短期の + noun,
or a compound such as 短期留学 (たんきりゅうがく), "short-term study abroad."

- 夏休みに短期の講習を受けます。
  (なつやすみにたんきのこうしゅうをうけます。) - I will take a short course during the summer vacation.
- 短期で結果を出すのは難しいです。
  (たんきでけっかをだすのはむずかしいです。) - It is difficult to produce results in a short time.

短期的な計画 (たんきてきなけいかく) is a short-term plan.
Learn 短期の and 短期的な separately rather than adding な directly to 短期.

## How the kanji combine

[短](../kanji/短.md), short, modifies [期](../kanji/期.md), period.
The on readings タン + キ give たんき.
The relevant length is time, not the physical length of an object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[長期](長期.md) (ちょうき), "long term," is the direct counterpart.
[短い](短い.md) (みじかい) is the broader adjective for short length or duration.
[しばらく](しばらく.md), "for a while," can describe a bounded stay,
but it does not always imply a short interval.

## Distinctive meaning

There is no universal number of days that makes something 短期.
A short investment horizon and a short course use different expectations.
短期 describes duration; it does not itself say that an arrangement is
unimportant or that results arrive immediately.
