# 課 (か) - lesson; organizational section

## Meaning

課 is a noun for a lesson or an organizational section. It also follows numbers to count or label textbook lessons. 第三課 (だいさんか) means "Lesson 3"; 課 is not a general counter for books.

- 今日は第三課を勉強します。 (きょうはだいさんかをべんきょうします。) - Today we will study Lesson 3.
- 田中さんは営業課で働いています。 (たなかさんはえいぎょうかではたらいています。) - Tanaka works in the sales section.

In organizational names, the preceding element specifies what the section does.

## How the kanji combine

課 is a single-kanji noun using the on reading カ. Its meanings here concern an assigned unit of study or a unit within an organization. No second component needs to be supplied to make the standalone noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[授業](授業.md) (じゅぎょう) concerns teaching or a class session; 課 can instead be a numbered unit in the textbook used during that session. [課長](課長.md) (かちょう) is a section chief, the person heading a 課.

## Distinctive meaning

Interpret 課 through its context: a textbook's 第三課 is not an office department, and 営業課 is not a lesson about sales. For the counting use, learn 一課 (いっか), with the small っ, alongside 二課 (にか) and 三課 (さんか).
