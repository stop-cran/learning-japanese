# 探る (さぐる) - feel around; investigate

## Meaning

探る means feeling or searching around for something. It also means investigating,
probing into an unknown matter, or sounding out another person's intentions.
It is a transitive godan verb: 探ります, 探った, 探って, 探らない.

- 暗い部屋でスイッチを探りました。 (くらいへやでスイッチをさぐりました。) - I felt around for the switch in the dark room.
- 相手の考えを探るために、質問しました。 (あいてのかんがえをさぐるために、しつもんしました。) - I asked a question to sound out what the other person was thinking.

The thing sought or investigated takes を. The second example is not literally touching someone's thoughts.

## How the kanji combine

[探](../kanji/探.md) contributes searching and has the native reading さぐ here; る completes the verb.
The same character has さが in 探す (さがす). The shared kanji does not make the two endings interchangeable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[探す](探す.md) and [捜す](捜す.md), both さがす, cover looking or searching for something.
[調べる](調べる.md) (しらべる) means investigate, examine, or look up.
探る is particularly useful when proceeding by feeling around or probing what is not yet clear;
this is not a rule that every use must involve secrecy.

## Distinctive meaning

In a further dictionary use, 秘境を探る (ひきょうをさぐる) means explore a little-known region,
including seeking out its natural beauty. This is not a general replacement for every English "enjoy."
The tactile, investigative, and exploratory uses share a verb, but searching does not guarantee success.
