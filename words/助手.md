# 助手 (じょしゅ) - assistant; helper

## Meaning

助手 is a noun for someone who assists another person. It can also refer to an assistant to a professor or a research assistant. Use Nの助手 to identify whom or what the person assists.

- 撮影の助手をしています。 (さつえいのじょしゅをしています。) - I work as a photography assistant.
- 大学で教授の助手として働いています。 (だいがくできょうじゅのじょしゅとしてはたらいています。) - I work at a university as a professor's assistant.

助手として (じょしゅとして) means "in the role of an assistant." It identifies a role, not a specific set of duties shared by every job.

## How the kanji combine

[助](../kanji/助.md) means "help; assist." [手](../kanji/手.md), literally "hand," can identify a person in a role. Together they name a helping person, not just a helping hand as a body part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[助ける](助ける.md) (たすける) is the verb "help; assist." 助手 names the person rather than the action. A supervisor or professor may be the person assisted, but that relationship is not a general antonym pair.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 助手 indiscriminately as "private tutor" or "spouse." Its academic sense concerns an assisting role, not every kind of teaching work. A job description or institutional context is needed to determine exact responsibilities; the word alone does not establish a universal academic rank or qualification.
