# ずるい - sly; unfair

## Meaning

ずるい describes sly, sneaky, or unfair behavior, often when someone
gets an advantage at someone else's expense. It is an i-adjective:
ずるい人 (ずるいひと), a sly person; ずるくない, not unfair.

- 自分だけ楽をするなんて、ずるいです。 (じぶんだけらくをするなんて、ずるいです。) - It is unfair to take it easy while leaving the work to everyone else.
- 彼はずるいやり方で勝ちました。 (かれはずるいやりかたでかちました。) - He won by using an underhanded method.

The complaint in the first example evaluates the advantage, not the
person's intelligence score.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 狡い (ずるい) is a dictionary-listed
kanji spelling marked rare; the kana spelling is the article's primary form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不正](不正.md) concerns wrongdoing or dishonesty.
[こそこそ](こそこそ.md) describes doing something sneakily, but secrecy
alone does not prove unfairness. [利口](利口.md), [賢い](賢い.md), and
[スマート](スマート.md) can concern cleverness or deftness without the
same complaint. [正直](正直.md), honest, is a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every clever action as ずるい. Depending on the context,
the word can also express playful envy or admiration rather than a grave
accusation; the public dictionary notes such noncritical use.
The pinned entry's separate stingy sense is restricted to the reading
こすい, not an unrestricted meaning of ずるい or a reason to replace
[けち](けち.md) in every money-related complaint.
