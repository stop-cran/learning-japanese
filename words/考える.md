# 考える (かんがえる) — think about; consider

## Meaning

考える means thinking about something, considering it, or working out an idea. It can also express a considered judgment or plan.

- この問題を考えてください。 (このもんだいをかんがえてください。) — Please think about this problem.
- 何を食べるか考えています。 (なにをたべるかかんがえています。) — I am thinking about what to eat.

In the second example, 何を食べるか, "what to eat," is the question being considered.

## How the kanji combine

This is one native verb: [考](../kanji/考.md) supplies かんが, and える is okurigana.
It is ichidan: 考えます (かんがえます), 考えて (かんがえて), 考えた (かんがえた), 考えない (かんがえない).
The related noun 考え (かんがえ) can mean a thought, idea, or opinion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [思う](思う.md) (おもう) often expresses an opinion, impression, or feeling. 考える more readily emphasizes thinking something through.
- 悩む (なやむ) means being troubled or worried; thinking about a choice does not necessarily mean worrying about it.
- There is no single general antonym. 考えない (かんがえない) simply means not thinking about or considering something.

## Distinctive meaning

The contrast with 思う is a tendency, not a logical/emotional split. Both can express judgments.
For a problem you are actively working through, 考える is useful; for an immediate "I think..." opinion,
思う is often natural. Choose the whole expression rather than treating English "think" as a one-word translation rule.
