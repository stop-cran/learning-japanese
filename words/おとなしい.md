# おとなしい (おとなしい) - quiet; gentle; well-behaved

## Meaning

おとなしい is an i-adjective for someone or something quiet, gentle, docile, or well-behaved. It can describe a person or a tame animal. It also describes subdued colors or patterns.

- この犬はおとなしいです。 (このいぬはおとなしいです。) - This dog is gentle and well-behaved.
- おとなしい色の服を選びました。 (おとなしいいろのふくをえらびました。) - I chose clothes in a subdued color.

Use おとなしい directly before a noun. The negative is おとなしくない, the past おとなしかった, and the adverbial form おとなしく, as in おとなしく待つ (おとなしくまつ), "wait quietly."

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 大人しい is a recognized spelling, containing [大](../kanji/大.md) and [人](../kanji/人.md), like [大人](大人.md) (おとな), "adult." The whole adjective means quiet or well-behaved, not simply "adult." This spelling association is not a historical derivation; the kana headword retains an empty kanji list.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[静か](静か.md) (しずか) can describe quiet surroundings as well as people. [穏やか](穏やか.md) (おだやか) emphasizes calmness or a mild manner, and [優しい](優しい.md) (やさしい) can emphasize kindness. These overlap without being identical.

[柔らかい](柔らかい.md) (やわらかい), "soft; gentle," overlaps in some impressions, not in every physical-texture use.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer kindness, agreement, or permanent personality merely because someone is quiet at the moment. Likewise, an おとなしい色 is subdued, not obedient. The noun being described and the situation select the intended sense. Unlike 静か and 穏やか, this adjective does not take な before a noun.
