# 開放 (かいほう) - opening; open access

## Meaning

開放 means opening something, leaving it open, or making a place available for people to use. With 開放する, the thing opened takes を.

- 窓を開放して、空気を入れ替えました。 (まどをかいほうして、くうきをいれかえました。) - We opened the windows wide and changed the air.
- 学校は休日に校庭を地域の人に開放しています。 (がっこうはきゅうじつにこうていをちいきのひとにかいほうしています。) - The school makes its grounds available to local people on holidays.

In the second, を marks the grounds and に marks the people given access. 一般開放 (いっぱんかいほう) means "opening to the general public."

## How the kanji combine

[開](../kanji/開.md) contributes opening and [放](../kanji/放.md) leaving something unrestricted. Together they suggest opening something up: カイ + ホウ. Treat this as a meaning aid, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[開ける](開ける.md) (あける) is a useful everyday verb for opening a door or window. [開く](開く.md) (あく) describes something becoming open.

[解放](解放.md), also かいほう, concerns release from confinement or a burden. Making a room open to use and freeing a person from captivity are not the same action just because both are "opening up" in English.

## Distinctive meaning

Opening to the public does not by itself promise free admission or unrestricted access at all times. Likewise, a physically open window need not be available for public use. Context selects the physical-opening or access sense.
