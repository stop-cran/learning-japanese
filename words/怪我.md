# 怪我 (けが) — injury

## Meaning

怪我 means an injury. With する, it means "get injured" or "injure" a part of one's own body: 怪我をする or 怪我する.

転んで怪我をしました。 (ころんでけがをしました。) — I fell and got injured.

サッカーで足を怪我しました。 (サッカーであしをけがしました。) — I injured my leg playing soccer.

## How the kanji combine

怪我 is an ateji spelling of the native word けが: [怪](../kanji/怪.md) and 我 represent the word's sound rather than a compositional meaning such as "strange self." Kana けが and katakana ケガ are also used.

## Synonyms and antonyms

傷 (きず) is a wound, scratch, or damage itself. 病気 (びょうき) means illness, a different kind of health problem.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "to injure" can mislead: 怪我する commonly describes someone suffering an injury. To say you caused another person an injury, a causative such as 怪我をさせる is clearer. The noun 怪我 alone is not an inflected verb.
