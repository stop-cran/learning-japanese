# 倹約 (けんやく) — thrift; frugality

## Meaning

倹約 means avoiding wasteful spending and keeping expenses down.
It is a noun, a transitive する verb, and a な-adjective.

- 小遣いを倹約しています。 (こづかいをけんやくしています。) — I am economizing on my spending money.
- 倹約な生活を送っています。 (けんやくなせいかつをおくっています。) — I lead a frugal life.

With する, the expenditure being reduced takes を. Before a noun, use 倹約な,
as in 倹約な生活, rather than treating 倹約 as an い-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[倹](../kanji/倹.md) contributes thrift; [約](../kanji/約.md) contributes reduction or restraint here.
The related meanings reinforce keeping expenditure limited. This is a meaning aid for this compound,
not a claim that 約 always means "frugal" or that 倹約 means "a promise."
The on readings combine as ケン + ヤク: けんやく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[節約](節約.md) (せつやく), economizing or conserving, overlaps when reducing expenses.
節約 also readily describes saving electricity or time; 倹約 is especially associated with spending and a frugal way of life.
This is a useful tendency, not a rule that every non-money use is impossible.

[貯金](貯金.md) (ちょきん) means putting money aside. 倹約 can make that possible,
but spending less does not guarantee that the difference is saved.
[贅沢](贅沢.md) (ぜいたく), luxury or extravagance, provides a contrast;
it need not be a condemnation of an occasional treat.

## Distinctive meaning

倹約 does not inherently call someone a miser. [けち](けち.md) generally criticizes stinginess:
sensible thrift and unwillingness to spend or share are not automatically the same judgment.
[倹しい](倹しい.md) (つましい), usually written in kana, describes frugal or modest living as an い-adjective.

Here English "economy" means thrift, not the national economy or academic subject foregrounded in
[経済](経済.md) (けいざい). 経済 also has a dictionary sense of being economical;
the words are not opposites, but neither is an unrestricted replacement for the other.
