# 景色 (けしき) — scenery

## Meaning

景色 is the scenery or view: the visible surroundings, such as a landscape or a view from a window.
It is a noun, so 景色がきれいです (けしきがきれいです) means "The scenery is beautiful."

- 窓から海の景色が見えます。 (まどからうみのけしきがみえます。) — There is a view of the sea from the window.
- 山の景色を楽しみました。 (やまのけしきをたのしみました。) — We enjoyed the mountain scenery.

## How the kanji combine

[景](../kanji/景.md) suggests a scene or view, while [色](../kanji/色.md) contributes visible appearance,
not just a particular color. The spelling suits the meaning, but the pronunciation needs a separate explanation.

The Japanese dictionary traces けしき to 気色, using an older set of Sino-Japanese readings called
*Go-on*. The spelling 景色 was applied later. This article therefore uses `kango` for its origin;
it does not teach け as a productive reading of 景. Learn 景色 and けしき together.

## Synonyms and antonyms

風景 (ふうけい) also means "scenery; landscape," and overlaps substantially with 景色.
夜景 (やけい) is specifically a night view or nightscape.
These are useful related words, not opposites, and 景色 has no necessary one-word antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

景色 concerns what is visible in a scene, rather than just an isolated object's color.
In the window example, 海の景色 is the view of the sea, not a name for the sea's blue color.
The scene may be seen directly; 景色 does not require a photograph or painting.
