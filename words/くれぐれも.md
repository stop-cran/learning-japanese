# くれぐれも (くれぐれも) - earnestly; repeatedly

## Meaning

くれぐれも adds heartfelt insistence to a request, warning, or expression of concern:
"please be sure to," "I earnestly ask," or "again and again."
It is an adverb, not a verb meaning "request."

- くれぐれも気をつけてください。 (くれぐれもきをつけてください。) - Please be very careful.
- くれぐれもよろしくお伝えください。 (くれぐれもよろしくおつたえください。) - Please be sure to give them my warm regards.

It can also emphasize repetition: くれぐれも頼む (くれぐれもたのむ), "ask repeatedly and earnestly."
The repetition and sincerity can reinforce each other; the speaker need not literally have asked several times already.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[たびたび](たびたび.md) means repeatedly or often, without necessarily conveying this heartfelt appeal.
[しきりに](しきりに.md) can describe insistent or frequent activity.
[繰り返す](繰り返す.md) (くりかえす) is the verb "repeat."
Their English translations can overlap, but their grammatical roles and contexts differ.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn くれぐれも with the whole request or warning.
It is not a neutral replacement for every "often": an ordinary report that a bus runs frequently does not express the concern shown above.
The word is usually written in kana.
