# プロ (プロ) - professional; pro

## Meaning

プロ is a noun for a professional: someone engaged in an activity as a profession, or someone described as having professional-level expertise. Before another noun, use プロの, as in プロの選手, "professional athlete."

- 彼女はプロの写真家です。 (かのじょはプロのしゃしんかです。) - She is a professional photographer.
- 将来はプロになりたいです。 (しょうらいはプロになりたいです。) - I want to become a professional in the future.

The field is understood from context in the second example. プロになる uses に to mark the resulting status.

## How the kanji combine

In this sense, プロ abbreviates プロフェッショナル. It is written in katakana and has no kanji components. The shorter form is a complete usable noun, not a Japanese prefix that can be attached freely to every word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

アマチュア, "amateur," contrasts in professional status. [上手](上手.md) (じょうず) describes skill, not necessarily paid employment. [職業](職業.md) (しょくぎょう) names an occupation, rather than a person working at professional level.

## Distinctive meaning

Being a プロ and being skillful are related ideas but not identical tests: an amateur may be highly skilled. Praise such as さすがプロですね, "That's a professional for you," comments on expected expertise. Other expansions of the abbreviation exist in specialized contexts; they are not the professional sense selected for this card.
