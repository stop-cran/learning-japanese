# 掘る (ほる) - dig; excavate

## Meaning

掘る means to dig, excavate, or hollow something out, and to dig something up or mine it. It is a transitive godan verb, despite ending in る.

- 庭に穴を掘りました。 (にわにあなをほりました。) - I dug a hole in the garden.
- 畑で芋を掘りました。 (はたけでいもをほりました。) - I dug up potatoes in the field.

The forms are 掘ります (ほります), 掘って (ほって), and 掘らない (ほらない). 穴を掘る names making a hole; 芋を掘る names getting something out of the ground. The dictionary also records figurative delving into a subject.

## How the kanji combine

[掘](../kanji/掘.md) supplies ほ, with る completing the native verb. The hand radical is an association with the action, not a claim that all digging must be done by hand.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[採掘](採掘.md) (さいくつ) is mining or extraction by digging, a noun and する-verb. [彫る](彫る.md) (ほる) shares the pronunciation but concerns carving, engraving, or sculpting.

[穴](穴.md) (あな), "hole," names a possible result or object of digging, not the digging action itself.

## Distinctive meaning

掘る foregrounds digging into material or bringing something out of it; 彫る foregrounds shaping or marking a surface. Learn the object and purpose, not a rule that the same physical tool always requires one spelling.
