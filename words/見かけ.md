# 見かけ (みかけ) - outward appearance

## Meaning

見かけ is outward appearance: how someone or something looks, which may differ from the underlying reality.

- 見かけで人を判断してはいけません。 (みかけでひとをはんだんしてはいけません。) - You should not judge people by their appearance.
- この箱は見かけより重いです。 (このはこはみかけよりおもいです。) - This box is heavier than it looks.

見かけの can also mean "apparent," as in 見かけの大きさ (みかけのおおきさ), "apparent size."

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md), "see," supplies み. 見掛け is another spelling, using [掛](../kanji/掛.md) for かけ. Treat the whole word as appearance, not as a claim that something is literally hanging.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[姿](姿.md) (すがた), [格好](格好.md) (かっこう) and [様子](様子.md) (ようす) overlap in appearance, with other meanings of their own. [表面](表面.md) (ひょうめん) can concern the surface or outward aspect; it is not limited to personal looks.

## Distinctive meaning

見かけ concerns the impression given by what is visible. It is not a guarantee of someone's character or an object's actual qualities. The noun is not the verb 見かける (みかける), "catch sight of."
