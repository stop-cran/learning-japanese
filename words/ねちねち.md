# ねちねち — persistently and insistently

## Meaning

ねちねち describes someone persistently pressing a point or criticizing in an unpleasantly tenacious way.

- 彼は昔の失敗を、いつまでもねちねち責めた。
  — He kept harping on an old mistake.
- 同じ質問をねちねち繰り返されて、少し困った。
  — I was a little troubled by being asked the same question over and over.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ねちねち emphasizes irritating persistence, not simply careful discussion.

## Distinctive meaning

The word also has a sticky or glutinous sense; these examples focus on persistent speech.
