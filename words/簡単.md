# 簡単 (かんたん) - simple; easy

## Meaning

簡単 describes something easy or uncomplicated. As a na-adjective, it takes な before a noun and に when modifying an action.

- これは簡単な問題です。 (これはかんたんなもんだいです。) - This is an easy question.
- 使い方を簡単に説明してください。 (つかいかたをかんたんにせつめいしてください。) - Please explain briefly how to use it.

In the second example, 簡単に asks for an explanation without elaborate detail. The predicate form is 簡単です; its negative is 簡単ではありません.

## How the kanji combine

簡 contributes brevity or simplicity; [単](../kanji/単.md) contributes singleness or simplicity. Together they suggest a lack of complexity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

易しい (やさしい) also means easy. [難しい](難しい.md) (むずかしい) contrasts with ease, while 複雑 (ふくざつ), "complicated," contrasts with simplicity.

## Distinctive meaning

簡単 can describe either low difficulty or a plain, brief form. 簡単な食事 (かんたんなしょくじ) is a simple meal; it need not mean a meal that is easy to eat. Let the following noun determine the intended kind of simplicity.
