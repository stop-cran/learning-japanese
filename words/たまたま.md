# たまたま (たまたま) - by chance; happen to

## Meaning

たまたま is an adverb for something happening by chance or for a circumstance that just happened to be true. It can go before the relevant verb or clause without a particle.

- 駅でたまたま昔の友達に会いました。 (えきでたまたまむかしのともだちにあいました。) - I happened to meet an old friend at the station.
- その日はたまたま休みでした。 (そのひはたまたまやすみでした。) - I happened to have that day off.

The second example describes a coincidental circumstance, not an accidental action.

## How the kanji combine

This word is usually written in kana. The source list uses 偶々, a spelling marked rare in the inspected dictionary. In that spelling, [偶](../kanji/偶.md) is repeated by 々; it is not necessary to write those kanji to use the adverb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[偶然](偶然.md) (ぐうぜん) also expresses coincidence or chance. Deliberately arranging to meet someone provides a contextual contrast, but a chance encounter can still be welcome.

## Distinctive meaning

The word explains the coincidental nature of the situation, not a relaxed or careless manner of doing it. The source's English "casually" is therefore unsafe as a general translation. The dictionary also records an occasional-occurrence sense; the two examples here specifically teach "by chance; happen to," not a regular frequency.
