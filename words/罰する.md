# 罰する (ばっする) - punish; penalize

## Meaning

罰する means to punish or impose a penalty. It is transitive: the person
punished takes を. It is a special する-class verb, with polite 罰します
(ばっします) and past 罰した (ばっした).

- 審判は不正をした選手を罰しました。 (しんぱんはふせいをしたせんしゅをばっしました。) - The referee penalized the player who cheated.
- 規則に違反した者は罰せられます。 (きそくにいはんしたものはばっせられます。) - Those who violate the rules will be punished.

罰せられる is a passive form: the subject receives the punishment rather than
administering it. The sentence does not specify what penalty is imposed.

## How the kanji combine

罰 supplies punishment or penalty. The verb joins its Sino-Japanese element to
する, producing ばっする with a small っ. Compare the noun 罰 (ばつ): do not
simply insert を and assume the verb is pronounced ばつする.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[叱る](叱る.md) (しかる) means scolding. A verbal reprimand and a punishment can
occur together, but imposing a penalty is not the same as speaking angrily.
[許す](許す.md) (ゆるす), "forgive; permit," provides a contrast in some situations.

## Distinctive meaning

罰する does not specify physical punishment and should not be translated as
"hit." It suits contexts involving sanctions, rules, or wrongdoing.
Whether a punishment is justified must come from the situation; the verb
merely describes the act of penalizing someone.
