# 水稲 (すいとう) — wetland rice

## Meaning

水稲 is **wetland rice**, rice grown in flooded paddies. It is agriculture vocabulary rather than a name for cooked rice.

- この地域では水稲を栽培しています。（このちいきではすいとうをさいばいしています。）— Wetland rice is cultivated in this region.
- 水稲の品種を調べています。（すいとうのひんしゅをしらべています。）— I am researching varieties of wetland rice.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md) supplies スイ, water, and [稲](../kanji/稲.md) supplies トウ, rice plant. This is a modifier-plus-head compound: rice characterized by water-based cultivation.

トウ is a compound reading of 稲 here; it does not replace いね in the independent noun [稲](稲.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[稲](稲.md) いね is the broader plant word. 陸稲 (りくとう) means upland rice and provides the cultivation contrast; 水稲 does not mean all rice plants regardless of how they are grown.

[米](米.md) こめ, rice as grain, and [ご飯](ご飯.md) ごはん, cooked rice or a meal, identify different aspects of rice rather than synonyms for this crop term.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 水稲 when the cultivation category matters, for example in agricultural descriptions or variety research. For an ordinary statement that rice plants are growing, 稲 is enough. 水稲 is not a request for rice cooked with extra water.
