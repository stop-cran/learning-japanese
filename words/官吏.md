# 官吏 (かんり) — government official; civil servant

## Meaning

官吏 is a government official or civil servant. JMdict marks it as a dated term. Digital Daijisen defines it as a national public servant (国家公務員), giving 役人 and 官員 as equivalents, and, as a second sense, an official appointed under the Meiji Constitution by the emperor's authority.

- 祖父は戦前、官吏として働いていました。 (そふはせんぜん、かんりとしてはたらいていました。) — Before the war, my grandfather worked as a government official.
- 歴史の本で、汚職をした官吏の話を読みました。 (れきしのほんで、おしょくをしたかんりのはなしをよみました。) — I read about a corrupt government official in a history book.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[官](../kanji/官.md) means a government official, and [吏](../kanji/吏.md) means an official. Together, they mean a government official.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[役人](役人.md) (やくにん), “government official” (JMdict entry 1538050), is one of the equivalents Digital Daijisen gives. [公務員](公務員.md) (こうむいん) means a public employee or civil servant (JMdict entry 1274820).

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict marks 官吏 as dated, and Digital Daijisen's second sense ties it to officials under the Meiji Constitution.
