# パンツ - underpants; trousers

## Meaning

パンツ means underpants. It can also mean trousers or pants worn as
outerwear, and can refer to swimming trunks.
The community source supplies "underpants"; the other uses are additional
recognition, not a reason to erase that everyday meaning.

- 下着売り場でパンツを買いました。 (したぎうりばでパンツをかいました。) - I bought underpants in the underwear department.
- スカートではなく、パンツをはいて行きます。 (スカートではなく、パンツをはいていきます。) - I will go wearing trousers rather than a skirt.

These settings help identify which garment is meant.
The word is a noun, and lower-body garments take はく in these examples.

## How the kanji combine

パンツ is a katakana loanword from English "pants," with no kanji to
divide. Its Japanese uses need checking independently of whether a
particular English variety normally uses "pants" for underwear or trousers.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ズボン](ズボン.md) is a close word for trousers.
[下着](下着.md) (したぎ) is underwear more broadly, not only
underpants. [肌着](肌着.md) (はだぎ) emphasizes clothing worn
next to the skin and includes items such as undershirts.
These category overlaps do not make every item of underwear パンツ.

## Distinctive meaning

Context, the type of garment, and pronunciation can help listeners
identify the intended use. Do not rely on an unqualified universal pitch
rule, or assume that every speaker makes the distinction in the same way.
If the garment matters, describe it or use a more specific clothing word
rather than assuming that a bare パンツ has only one meaning.
