# 手帳 (てちょう) - pocket notebook; diary

## Meaning

手帳 is a small notebook or pocket diary used for notes, appointments, or reminders.

- 手帳に明日の予定を書きました。 (てちょうにあしたのよていをかきました。) - I wrote tomorrow's plans in my pocket diary.
- かばんから手帳を出して、住所を確認しました。 (かばんからてちょうをだして、じゅうしょをかくにんしました。) - I took my notebook out of my bag and checked the address.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md), native て, suggests keeping it at hand; 帳, on チョウ, is a record book. This is mixed native-plus-on reading, categorized here with native-first words, not an all-on compound. 手帖 is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ノート](ノート.md) is a notebook or notes more generally. [日記](日記.md) is a diary or journal, particularly a record of daily experiences. [メモ](メモ.md) is a note or jotting; a 手帳 can contain メモ without being one individual note.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not require an account of every day's events: a schedule or address notebook can be a 手帳. It also occurs in names of official booklets and documents. Dictionaries record certificate/ID uses as abbreviations of particular document names, including 身体障害者手帳 (しんたいしょうがいしゃてちょう) and 警察手帳 (けいさつてちょう); do not translate every ordinary 手帳 as an identity card.
