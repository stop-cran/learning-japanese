# パイプ (パイプ) - pipe; connection

## Meaning

パイプ is a pipe or tube. It can also refer figuratively to a connection,
intermediary, or channel of communication between people or organizations.
It is a noun; surrounding words identify which meaning is intended.

- 水道のパイプから水が漏れています。 (すいどうのパイプからみずがもれています。) - Water is leaking from the water pipe.
- 彼女は地元の企業と強いパイプを持っています。 (かのじょはじもとのきぎょうとつよいパイプをもっています。) - She has strong connections with local companies.

In the second example, と identifies the other side of the connection.
パイプを持つ means having a useful contact or channel, not carrying a physical
tube in this context.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword corresponds to English "pipe." There are no written
kanji components. It has three kana beats, パ・イ・プ, without a long-vowel
mark.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[管](管.md) (くだ) is a tube or pipe and overlaps with the physical sense.
つながり, "connection," can express a broader relationship between people.
[仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) means a companion or fellow member, not necessarily
someone providing a communication channel.

## Distinctive meaning

The connection sense does not imply corruption or secrecy: a useful channel
may be an ordinary professional contact. The noun can also name a tobacco
pipe in an appropriate context. Do not assume that every パイプ transports
water, or that an organizational パイプ is a literal part of a building.
