# あいかわらず - as usual; as before

## Meaning

あいかわらず says that a familiar situation continues as before: "as usual,"
"as ever," or "still." It compares the present situation with an earlier one.

- あいかわらず忙しいです。 (あいかわらずいそがしいです。) - I'm still busy, as usual.
- 彼女はあいかわらず元気ですね。 (かのじょはあいかわらずげんきですね。) - She's as lively as ever, isn't she?

It can also modify a noun with の: あいかわらずの暑さ (あいかわらずのあつさ),
"the same continuing heat."

## How the kanji combine

The kanji spelling is 相変わらず. [変](../kanji/変.md) relates to change;
変わらず (かわらず) is a negative form of 変わる (かわる), "change."
Learn the complete expression rather than reading each character independently.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[まだ](まだ.md) can also mean "still," but need not compare the situation with a familiar pattern.
[そのまま](そのまま.md) emphasizes leaving or continuing a particular state unchanged.
[変わる](変わる.md) (かわる), "change," provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The expression is not automatically criticism. It can describe continuing good health,
continuing difficulties, or an ordinary habit. Context and tone determine whether the speaker
sounds pleased, frustrated, or neutral.
