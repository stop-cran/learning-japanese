# 余る (あまる) — be left over

## Meaning

余る describes an amount left beyond what was used or needed.
It is intransitive: the leftover thing takes が.
The surplus may be food, objects, or time; the verb does not by itself say whether having extra is good or bad.

- 料理がたくさん余りました。 (りょうりがたくさんあまりました。) — A lot of food was left over.
- 切符が二枚余っています。 (きっぷがにまいあまっています。) — There are two tickets left over.

余る is a godan verb: 余ります (あまります), 余った (あまった), and 余って (あまって).

## How the kanji combine

[余](../kanji/余.md) carries the surplus meaning and is read あま; る completes this native verb.
It is not a multi-kanji compound.
The on reading ヨ appears in 余分 (よぶん), "extra; surplus."

## Synonyms and antonyms

残る (のこる), "remain," is broader: it does not by itself say that the remainder exceeds what is needed.
足りる (たりる) means "be enough"; its negative 足りない (たりない), "not be enough," is a useful contrast with having a surplus.
These comparisons concern the available amount, not who owns it.

## Distinctive meaning

If time is left after finishing a task, 時間が余った (じかんがあまった) focuses on the unused time.
Simply saying that time remains need not imply a surplus: there may still be plenty of work to do.
Use 余る when the left-over or excess aspect matters.
