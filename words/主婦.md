# 主婦 (しゅふ) - housewife; female homemaker

## Meaning

主婦 is a noun for a woman in the role of managing a household: a housewife or female homemaker. It describes a domestic role, not a requirement that every woman have that role.

- 母は長年、専業主婦でした。 (はははながねん、せんぎょうしゅふでした。) - My mother was a full-time homemaker for many years.
- この講座には主婦や学生が参加しています。 (このこうざにはしゅふやがくせいがさんかしています。) - Homemakers and students are taking part in this course.

専業主婦 (せんぎょうしゅふ) specifically describes a homemaker who is not also doing paid work. 主婦 alone does not supply that whole qualification.

## How the kanji combine

[主](../kanji/主.md) contributes a main or leading role; [婦](../kanji/婦.md) refers to a woman or wife. Learn the established household-role meaning, rather than treating the characters as a general title for any female leader.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[妻](妻.md) (つま), "wife," identifies a marriage relationship; 主婦 identifies a household role. The words therefore answer different questions.

[家事](家事.md) (かじ), "housework," names the tasks, not the person performing them.

## Distinctive meaning

Some older English glosses say "mistress of the house." The qualification matters: 主婦 does not mean a romantic or sexual "mistress." In contemporary English, "homemaker" or "housewife" avoids that ambiguity. Do not infer someone's employment status or preferences solely from their being married.
