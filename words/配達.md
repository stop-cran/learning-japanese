# 配達 (はいたつ) - delivery

## Meaning

配達 is delivery to a recipient or destination. 配達する is a transitive する verb: **item を destination に配達する**. It fits mail, parcels, newspapers, and prepared food.

- この店は家まで料理を配達してくれます。 (このみせはいえまでりょうりをはいたつしてくれます。) - This shop delivers food to our home.
- 荷物は明日配達される予定です。 (にもつはあしたはいたつされるよていです。) - The parcel is scheduled to be delivered tomorrow.

まで emphasizes the endpoint in the first sentence. 配達される is passive, making the parcel the topic of the second. **配達を頼む** (はいたつをたのむ) means "request delivery."

## How the kanji combine

[配](../kanji/配.md) contributes distributing or allocating, and [達](../kanji/達.md) contributes reaching. Together they suggest bringing something to its destination. The reading is ハイ + タツ: はいたつ, without voicing た.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[届ける](届ける.md) (とどける) broadly means delivering or bringing something to someone. [送る](送る.md) (おくる) means sending, which may describe the sender's action without asserting arrival. [受け取る](受け取る.md) (うけとる), "receive," views the transaction from the recipient's side.

## Distinctive meaning

配達 focuses on the delivery service or action, not every kind of "distribution." Dividing jobs among employees is not parcel delivery. English "delivery" in childbirth or speech presentation also needs different Japanese vocabulary.
