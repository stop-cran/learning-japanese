# 活動 (かつどう) - activity

## Meaning

活動 is a noun and an intransitive する verb for being active or carrying out activities. It can describe people, organizations, animals, and natural phenomena.

- この団体は地域で活動しています。 (このだんたいはちいきでかつどうしています。) - This organization is active in the local community.
- この動物は夜に活動します。 (このどうぶつはよるにかつどうします。) - This animal is active at night.

場所で活動する gives the place of activity. As a noun, 活動 can take を with another verb: 活動を続ける (かつどうをつづける), "continue one's activities." 活動する does not itself take the activity's location as a direct object.

## How the kanji combine

[活](../kanji/活.md) contributes life or active functioning, and [動](../kanji/動.md) contributes movement. Together they express active operation, not merely a single change of position.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[行動](行動.md) (こうどう) focuses on actions or behavior, while 活動 is useful for ongoing or organized activity. [運動](運動.md) (うんどう) can mean exercise or a movement; not every 活動 is physical exercise. [休む](休む.md) (やすむ), "rest," is a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

活動 does not require conscious planning: 火山の活動 (かざんのかつどう) is volcanic activity. Conversely, an organization's 活動 can include discussion and research rather than visible bodily movement. Choose the noun from the kind of activity, rather than translating every English "action" as 活動.
