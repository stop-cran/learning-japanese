# 笑顔 (えがお) - smiling face; smile

## Meaning

笑顔 is a noun for a smiling face or the smile visible on it. Use
笑顔で to describe doing something with a smile, and 笑顔になる for
coming to have a smiling expression.

- 店員が笑顔で迎えてくれました。 (てんいんがえがおでむかえてくれました。) - The shop assistant welcomed me with a smile.
- 写真を見ると、母は笑顔になりました。 (しゃしんをみると、はははえがおになりました。) - When she saw the photograph, my mother smiled.

In the second example, になる describes a change in expression. 笑顔
itself is not a verb meaning "to laugh."

## How the kanji combine

[笑](../kanji/笑.md) contributes smiling or laughter, and
[顔](../kanji/顔.md) means "face." The first element describes the face.
The native reading is えがお: 顔's かお becomes がお in this compound.
Do not read it by combining the on readings of the two characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[笑う](笑う.md) (わらう) is the verb "to laugh; smile."
笑顔 names the expression rather than the action or sound. 泣き顔
(なきがお), a tearful face, is a useful contrasting expression, not
an exhaustive opposite for every situation.

## Distinctive meaning

A smiling face is observable; it does not by itself prove what someone
feels. 笑顔で話す means speaking with a smile, even when a person's
private feelings are more complicated.
