# 養分 (ようぶん) - nutrients; nourishment

## Meaning

養分 is nourishment or the nutrients that support a living thing.
It can concern people, animals or plants, not just one kind of food.

- 植物は根から養分を吸収します。 (しょくぶつはねからようぶんをきゅうしゅうします。) - Plants absorb nutrients through their roots.
- この土には養分が十分にあります。 (このつちにはようぶんがじゅうぶんにあります。) - This soil contains plenty of nutrients.

Useful patterns include 養分を吸収する, "absorb nutrients," and
養分を含む (ようぶんをふくむ), "contain nutrients."

## How the kanji combine

養 contributes nourishing or sustaining, and [分](../kanji/分.md)
a portion or component. Together they suggest nourishing components.
The on readings are ヨウ + ブン; 分 is not read ふん here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[栄養](栄養.md) (えいよう) overlaps with nourishment and nutrition.
栄養素 (えいようそ) names a nutrient or nutritional constituent.
[食物](食物.md) (しょくもつ) names food, rather than necessarily
the nourishing substances it contains.

## Distinctive meaning

養分 is useful when discussing what an organism obtains from its
surroundings. The plant example concerns absorption, not the plant
eating a human-style meal. Do not turn the comparison with 栄養
into an absolute plants-versus-people distinction.
