# 訴える (うったえる) - appeal; complain; sue

## Meaning

訴える is an ichidan verb: 訴えます, 訴えて, 訴えない. Its constructions include bringing a problem to someone's attention, appealing to feelings or reason, and suing someone.

- 患者が医師に胸の痛みを訴えました。 (かんじゃがいしにむねのいたみをうったえました。) - The patient complained to the doctor of chest pain.
- 被害者は会社を訴えました。 (ひがいしゃはかいしゃをうったえました。) - The victim sued the company.

In the first example, **person に + problem を訴える** names the listener and the complaint. In the second, **person or organization を訴える** identifies the party taken to court.

## How the kanji combine

訴 supplies the appeal or complaint meaning; える completes the native verb read うったえる. The small っ is part of the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[言う](言う.md) simply means to say; 訴える presents something as a matter needing attention. [頼む](頼む.md) focuses on asking a favor or making a request, not necessarily a complaint or legal action.

## Distinctive meaning

The particle に also occurs in different patterns: 理性に訴える (りせいにうったえる) is "appeal to reason," and 暴力に訴える (ぼうりょくにうったえる) is "resort to violence." Here に marks what the person appeals or resorts to, not a listener. A complaint about symptoms does not itself imply a lawsuit.
