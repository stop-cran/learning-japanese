# 態度 (たいど) - attitude; manner

## Meaning

態度 is a noun for a person's manner, behavior, or attitude.
It can also mean a position taken toward an issue. Use 人に対する態度
for an attitude toward someone, and 態度を示す for showing a stance.

- 店員の態度はとても丁寧でした。 (てんいんのたいどはとてもていねいでした。) - The shop assistant's manner was very polite.
- 委員会は計画に反対する態度を示しました。 (いいんかいはけいかくにはんたいするたいどをしめしました。) - The committee showed its opposition to the plan.

態度がいい or 態度が悪い evaluates someone's manner.
態度を変える (たいどをかえる) means changing an attitude or stance.

## How the kanji combine

態 contributes a state, appearance, or manner, while
[度](../kanji/度.md) contributes measure or degree. Their established
combination names bearing or attitude. The component glosses are
recognition aids, not a literal measurement formula.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[行動](行動.md) (こうどう) names actions or behavior.
[意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion, which can be expressed
in words without describing the person's manner.
[様子](様子.md) (ようす) refers more broadly to appearance or state.

## Distinctive meaning

態度 can concern how someone treats another person or what position
they adopt on an issue. It does not necessarily mean a bad attitude:
the first example explicitly describes a polite manner. Look for the
modifier and the target of the attitude.
