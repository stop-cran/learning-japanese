# 苗代 (なわしろ) — rice-seedling bed

## Meaning

苗代 is a **bed or nursery for rice seedlings**, where young rice plants are raised before transplanting. This article uses なわしろ; なえしろ is another dictionary reading of the same word.

- 苗代で稲の苗を育てます。（なわしろでいねのなえをそだてます。）— We raise rice seedlings in a seedling bed.
- 田植えの前に、苗代の様子を見に行きました。（たうえのまえに、なわしろのようすをみにいきました。）— Before rice planting, I went to check on the seedling bed.

で marks the place where the seedlings are raised. The nursery and the later planting activity are not the same thing.

## How the kanji combine

[苗](../kanji/苗.md) uses its bound form なわ-, seedling. [代](../kanji/代.md) supplies しろ, here a field or plot. The native compound identifies a plot for raising seedlings, not an era or a replacement charge.

The independent noun [苗](苗.md) is なえ. The combining form なわ- is learned in this complete word; it is not a rule to change every なえ into なわ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

苗床 (なえどこ) is a seedbed or nursery more generally. 苗代 is the rice-growing term taught here.

[田](田.md) (た) and [田んぼ](田んぼ.md) (たんぼ) name rice fields more broadly. [田植え](田植え.md) (たうえ) names planting or transplanting rice seedlings into paddies, not the nursery.

## Distinctive meaning

English "nursery" also names places for children or commercial plant businesses. 苗代 does not automatically carry those meanings. It identifies the growing bed, not a shop selling seedlings or the price of 苗.
