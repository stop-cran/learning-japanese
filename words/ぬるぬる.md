# ぬるぬる — slimy and slippery

## Meaning

A slick coating of slime, oil, or similar material. In informal descriptions of video or animation, it can instead mean exceptionally smooth movement.

- 魚の表面（ひょうめん）はぬるぬるしていて、つかみにくい。 — The fish's surface is slippery, making it hard to hold.
- せっけんが残っていて、床（ゆか）がぬるぬるする。 — Soap is still on the floor, making it slippery.
- このゲームは、画面（がめん）の動きがぬるぬるしている。 — The motion on the screen is very smooth in this game.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[つるつる](つるつる.md) can be smooth and slippery without a coating. [ねばねば](ねばねば.md) emphasizes viscous stickiness; [べたべた](べたべた.md) sticky contact. A surface can be both sticky and slippery, so these are contrasts of focus.

## Distinctive meaning

ぬるぬるする and ぬるぬるした石 are standard state patterns. The screen sense is informal and may be positive, unlike an unwanted slimy floor. The word is unrelated in meaning to ぬるい, “lukewarm”; similar sounds do not make it a temperature term.
