# 解散 (かいさん) - dispersal; disbandment; dissolution

## Meaning

解散 describes a gathering breaking up or people dispersing. It also means disbanding an organization, and the formal dissolution of a legislature. With する, the group can be the subject, or を can mark a group someone disbands.

- 見学の後、駅の前で解散しました。 (けんがくのあと、えきのまえでかいさんしました。) - After the visit, we split up in front of the station.
- バンドは去年解散しました。 (バンドはきょねんかいさんしました。) - The band disbanded last year.
- 衆議院を解散する。 (しゅうぎいんをかいさんする。) - Dissolve the House of Representatives.

The first group finishes an outing; the second ceases operating as a band. The legislature example has a specific institutional meaning.

## How the kanji combine

[解](../kanji/解.md) contributes undoing or releasing; [散](../kanji/散.md) contributes scattering. Together they describe releasing a gathering or organization from its assembled state. The on readings are カイ + サン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[別れる](別れる.md) (わかれる) includes parting and separating, including a couple's breakup. 解散 focuses on an assembled group or organization. [会合](会合.md) (かいごう), "meeting; gathering," names a gathering, not its dispersal.

## Distinctive meaning

解散 does not always mean permanent abolition: people can 解散 after today's outing and meet again tomorrow. Conversely, a band's 解散 can mean the group ends. Do not use it as the default word for ending a romantic relationship merely because English says "break up."
