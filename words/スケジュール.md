# スケジュール (スケジュール) - schedule; program of activities

## Meaning

スケジュール is a schedule or program of planned activities. Useful patterns
include スケジュールを立てる, "make a schedule," and スケジュールを確認する,
"check the schedule."

- 明日のスケジュールを確認しましょう。 (あしたのスケジュールをかくにんしましょう。) - Let's check tomorrow's schedule.
- 来週はスケジュールが詰まっています。 (らいしゅうはスケジュールがつまっています。) - My schedule is packed next week.

スケジュールを変更する (スケジュールをへんこうする) means to change the schedule.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword with no kanji components. Keep the small ュ in
ジュ and the following long-vowel mark: ジュール.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予定](予定.md) (よてい) names plans or an intended arrangement.
[時間割](時間割.md) (じかんわり) often gives the sequence of school lessons.
[ダイヤ](ダイヤ.md) can mean a railway timetable; the train context distinguishes
that word from its diamond homonym.

## Distinctive meaning

A [カレンダー](カレンダー.md) shows dates and can hold a schedule, but a
スケジュール focuses on the planned activities and their arrangement.
"Program" here need not mean a television show or computer software.
It also does not promise that every planned event will actually happen.
