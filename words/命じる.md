# 命じる (めいじる) - order; appoint

## Meaning

命じる is a transitive ichidan verb meaning to order or command.
It can also mean to appoint someone to a role.
For an instruction, use **person + に + action + ように命じる** or identify the assigned task with を.

- 部長は私に報告書を出すように命じました。 (ぶちょうはわたしにほうこくしょをだすようにめいじました。) - The department head ordered me to submit a report.
- 私は新しい支店の責任者を命じられました。 (わたしはあたらしいしてんのせきにんしゃをめいじられました。) - I was appointed to take charge of the new branch.

The second example uses the passive **role + を命じられる**.
Conjugations include 命じます, 命じない, 命じた, and 命じて.

## How the kanji combine

[命](../kanji/命.md) contributes a command in this word, not its other meaning "life."
The on-reading stem メイ combines with the Japanese verbal ending じる.
This makes a conjugating verb rather than a noun used unchanged before する.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[命令](命令.md) (めいれい) names an order and forms 命令する.
[頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ) means to ask or request; it need not assert authority.
任命する (にんめいする) explicitly names appointment to a position.

## Distinctive meaning

命じる presents an instruction as a command, not merely advice or a friendly request.
Authority and the assigned responsibility are central to its use.
Do not translate restaurant "ordering" mechanically with 命じる: ordering food is a different sense of English "order."
In the appointment use, the role identifies what responsibility is being assigned.
