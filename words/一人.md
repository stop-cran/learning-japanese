# 一人 (ひとり) — one person, alone

## Meaning

一人 counts one person and also means "alone": 一人で行く (go alone), 一人暮らし ひとりぐらし (living alone), 一人っ子 ひとりっこ (only child), 学生が一人 (one student).

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) (one) plus [人](../kanji/人.md) (person): "one person". The reading ひとり is a special whole-word reading (jukujikun): a word whose reading can't be built from the kanji's own readings; ひとり can't be built from ひと+り. It is a jukujikun-like irregular word.
It belongs to the same group as 二人 ふたり; from three on the people counter is regular (三人 さんにん).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 単独 たんどく (alone, formal), 独り ひとり (alone, often lonely or literary).
- Antonym: 大勢 おおぜい (many people), 皆 みんな (everyone), [二人](二人.md) ふたり (two people).

## Distinctive meaning

一人 is the neutral word for "one person" and "alone". 独り stresses solitude, and 一人前 いちにんまえ (a full-fledged person, one serving) is read with the on reading にん.
