# 一人 (ひとり) — one person, alone

## Meaning

一人 counts one person and also means "alone": 一人で行く (go alone), 一人暮らし ひとりぐらし (living alone), 一人っ子 ひとりっこ (only child), 学生が一人 (one student).

- 教室に学生が一人います。 (きょうしつにがくせいがひとりいます。) — There is one student in the classroom.
- 今日は一人で帰ります。 (きょうはひとりでかえります。) — I will go home alone today.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) (one) plus [人](../kanji/人.md) (person): "one person".
Learn ひとり as a special people-counting reading, rather than substituting イチ + ニン.
This card groups it with whole-word readings for study; that does not mean the native word has no internal formation.
It belongs with 二人 ふたり. From three onward, the counter normally uses にん
(三人 さんにん), but number readings still need attention: 四人 is よにん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 単独 たんどく (alone, formal), 独り ひとり (alone, often lonely or literary).
- Number/group contrasts, not strict antonyms: 大勢 おおぜい (many people), 皆 みんな (everyone), [二人](二人.md) ふたり (two people).

## Distinctive meaning

一人 is the neutral word for "one person" and "alone". 独り stresses solitude, and 一人前 いちにんまえ (a full-fledged person, one serving) is read with the on reading にん.
