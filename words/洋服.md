# 洋服 (ようふく) — Western-style clothes

## Meaning

洋服 means Western-style clothes, as opposed to traditional Japanese clothing. It includes ordinary modern shirts, trousers, dresses, and similar garments.

- 新しい洋服を買いました。 (あたらしい ようふくを かいました。) — I bought some new clothes.
- 今日は洋服を着ています。 (きょうは ようふくを きて います。) — I am wearing Western-style clothes today.

In an ordinary shopping conversation, the natural English translation may simply be "clothes"; the Japanese word still belongs to the Western-style category.

## How the kanji combine

[洋](../kanji/洋.md), "Western-style," modifies [服](../kanji/服.md), "clothing." This is a modifier-and-head compound.
Both readings are on: ヨウ + フク → ようふく. Here 洋 does not mean that the clothes are related to the ocean.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [服](服.md) (ふく) is a shorter word for clothes, especially ordinary Western-style clothing.
- 和服 (わふく), traditional Japanese clothing, is the contrasting category, not a negative evaluation.
- 着物 (きもの) commonly refers to a kimono; it is not a synonym for 洋服 in that sense.

## Distinctive meaning

洋服 classifies the style of clothing, not the country where it was made. Clothes produced in Japan can be 洋服.
It does not mean only a suit or only formal clothing; use it for the broader clothing category.
