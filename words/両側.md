# 両側 (りょうがわ) - both sides

## Meaning

両側 means both sides, for example the two sides of a road.
It is a noun and can modify a following noun with の.
The reading りょうそく is also recorded for the same word.

- 道の両側に木が並んでいます。 (みちのりょうがわにきがならんでいます。) - Trees line both sides of the road.
- 両側の窓を開けてください。 (りょうがわのまどをあけてください。) - Please open the windows on both sides.

## How the kanji combine

[両](../kanji/両.md) contributes both, and [側](../kanji/側.md) a side.
The main reading combines on リョウ with native がわ: an on-first
mixed reading, called 重箱読み (じゅうばこよみ).
The repository groups this under kango; ガワ is not an on reading.
The alternative りょうそく uses on リョウ + ソク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[両方](両方.md) (りょうほう) means both and can include both sides or
parties. [左右](左右.md) (さゆう) means left and right and can describe
the two sides together. 両側 explicitly supplies the noun "side."

## Distinctive meaning

両側 does not mean any two objects.
It identifies two sides relative to something in the situation.
Also distinguish the separate primary reading そば on the
[側](側.md) word card, which concerns proximity rather than this compound's reading.
