# 混ざる (まざる) - be mixed; mingle; join in

## Meaning

混ざる means be mixed or blended, or mingle and join with other people. It is an intransitive godan verb: what mixes takes が; what it mixes with can take と or に.

- 赤と青の絵の具が混ざった。 (あかとあおのえのぐがまざった。) - The red and blue paints mixed.
- 子供たちに混ざって遊びました。 (こどもたちにまざってあそびました。) - I joined the children and played with them.

交ざる is another dictionary spelling of this same まざる entry. The kana reading is not changed by choosing 交. 雑ざる is marked irregular in the pinned dictionary, not offered here as a default spelling.

## How the kanji combine

[混](../kanji/混.md) contributes mixing; ざる completes the native verb. Forms include 混ざります (まざります), 混ざらない (まざらない), and 混ざった (まざった).

The alternative [交](../kanji/交.md) also has mingling associations. Character associations alone do not establish a universal rule about which materials can mix.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[混じる](混じる.md) (まじる) is a closely overlapping intransitive verb. Do not learn the two as strictly separate mixture types.

[混ぜる](混ぜる.md) (まぜる) is transitive: 絵の具を混ぜる, "mix the paints." Compare 絵の具が混ざる, "the paints mix." [混合](混合.md) (こんごう) names mixing or a mixture.

## Distinctive meaning

In 子供たちに混ざって, someone joins an existing group. This is not necessarily permanent membership or a romantic relationship. Both material mixing and people mingling are part of the entry; neither should be discarded to create a neat spelling rule.
