# 始まり (はじまり) - beginning; start

## Meaning

始まり is a noun for a beginning, start, or origin. Use Nの始まり for "the beginning of N." It can identify the opening stage of an event or what first set a process in motion.

- 春の始まりを感じます。 (はるのはじまりをかんじます。) - I can feel the beginning of spring.
- その出会いが、私たちの友情の始まりでした。 (そのであいが、わたしたちのゆうじょうのはじまりでした。) - That encounter was the beginning of our friendship.

始まりに (はじまりに) can locate something at the beginning; 始まりから (はじまりから) marks the starting point.

## How the kanji combine

[始](../kanji/始.md) supplies the beginning idea. 始まり is a noun associated with 始まる (はじまる), "begin." The final り is part of the noun form; it is not a conjugated predicate by itself.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[開始](開始.md) (かいし), "commencement," can combine with する to describe starting an activity.

[初め](初め.md) (はじめ) overlaps in referring to an initial stage. [終わり](終わり.md) (おわり), "end," is the direct stage contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 始まり with [初めて](初めて.md) (はじめて), "for the first time." One names a beginning; the other describes an experience as a first occurrence. 始まり can be a turning point recognized later, as in the friendship example, not necessarily a formally announced start.
