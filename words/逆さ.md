# 逆さ (さかさ) - upside down; reversed

## Meaning

逆さ describes something inverted, upside down, reversed, or back to front. It is a noun and can modify another noun with な or の.

- 写真が逆さになっています。 (しゃしんがさかさになっています。) - The photograph is upside down.
- 逆さの文字は読みにくいです。 (さかさのもじはよみにくいです。) - Upside-down writing is hard to read.

逆さにする means to turn or put something the wrong way up/round. The context identifies which orientation is reversed.

## How the kanji combine

[逆](../kanji/逆.md) carries the reversal association. The whole native word is さかさ, with the final さ written in kana. 倒さ, using [倒](../kanji/倒.md), is another dictionary spelling with the same reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[逆さま](逆さま.md) (さかさま) has the same basic inversion meanings; JMdict labels 逆さ an abbreviation of it. [逆](逆.md) (ぎゃく) is broader reverse/opposite, including sequence or direction. [裏返す](裏返す.md) (うらがえす) is a verb for turning something over or inside out.

## Distinctive meaning

逆さ can describe the resulting orientation without saying who changed it. It is not a godan verb ending in す. Nor does it mean merely that something is on the [裏](裏.md) (うら), reverse side: identify the actual reversal rather than replacing every use of "back."
