# 代表 (だいひょう) - representative; representation

## Meaning

代表 is a noun for representation or a representative. 代表する is
transitive: the group or category represented takes を. A representative
may act for a group, or something may exemplify a category.

- クラスの代表として会議に出ました。 (クラスのだいひょうとしてかいぎにでました。) - I attended the meeting as the class representative.
- これは日本を代表する料理の一つです。 (これはにほんをだいひょうするりょうりのひとつです。) - This is one of Japan's representative dishes.

として marks the capacity in which someone acts. In the second
example, 代表する describes exemplifying something, not a dish
literally speaking on behalf of a country.

## How the kanji combine

[代](../kanji/代.md) contributes acting in someone's place, and
[表](../kanji/表.md) contributes showing or expressing. Together
they suggest expressing or standing for a larger group.
The readings are だい + ひょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[代理](代理.md) (だいり) emphasizes acting in another's place.
[例](例.md) (れい), "example," overlaps with the illustrative
use, but an example need not be representative of the category as a
whole. Neither comparison supplies a general antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Look for the relationship between the representative and what is
represented. A selected spokesperson and a characteristic work of art
both fit, but in different ways. 代表 does not by itself prove that
every member of a group agrees with the representative.
