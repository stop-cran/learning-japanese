# 田 (た) - rice field

## Meaning

田 is a noun meaning a rice field.
Use 田に for the destination of water or plants, and 田で for an activity carried out in the field.
The word refers to cultivated land, not to rice served as food.

- 春になると、田に水を入れます。 (はるになると、たにみずをいれます。) - When spring comes, we put water into the rice fields.
- 田で米を作っています。 (たでこめをつくっています。) - We grow rice in the fields.

米を作る literally uses "make rice," but here it means cultivating rice, not cooking a meal.
In 田の水 (たのみず), の connects the field with its water.

## How the kanji combine

[田](../kanji/田.md) represents the whole native noun た.
Its divided shape can help a learner picture plots of farmland.
The reading of this independent word should not be mechanically assigned to every surname or compound containing the character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

田んぼ (たんぼ) is another word for a rice field.
[畑](畑.md) (はたけ) refers to a field for crops, contrasting with the rice-field use of 田.
[米](米.md) (こめ) names the rice crop or uncooked grain rather than the land.

## Distinctive meaning

A 田 remains a rice field even when it is not currently flooded or planted.
Seasonal appearance does not change the basic noun.
English "field" is broader: a sports field or a field of research is not automatically a 田.
