# 羊毛 (ようもう) - wool from sheep

## Meaning

羊毛 is wool: the hair or fleece of sheep, including its use as a
textile material. Use 羊毛の before a product or material description.

- このセーターは羊毛でできています。 (このせーたーはようもうでできています。) - This sweater is made of wool.
- 羊毛から糸を作ります。 (ようもうからいとをつくります。) - Yarn is made from wool.

The second example distinguishes the fibre from the yarn made from it;
羊毛 does not necessarily mean that the wool has already been spun.

## How the kanji combine

羊 means sheep, and [毛](../kanji/毛.md) hair, fur or wool.
The combination specifies sheep's wool. Both readings are on readings:
ヨウ + モウ, not the independent native noun 毛 (け).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ウール](ウール.md) is the overlapping loanword for wool.
[毛糸](毛糸.md) (けいと) is knitting wool or woollen yarn.
[毛皮](毛皮.md) (けがわ) is fur-bearing skin or a pelt, not simply
another name for separated wool fibre. [綿](綿.md) (めん), cotton,
is a contrasting material, not a universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The written 羊 identifies the animal. Broader 毛 can concern other
animals or human hair. Conversely, a product described as 羊毛 does
not have to resemble an unprocessed fleece: the word also identifies
the material in a finished garment.
