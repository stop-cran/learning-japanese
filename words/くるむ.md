# くるむ (くるむ) - wrap up; tuck up

## Meaning

くるむ is a transitive verb for wrapping something in a covering, rolling it up in something, or tucking it up.
Use を for what is wrapped and に or で for the covering, as these examples illustrate.

- 赤ちゃんを毛布でくるみました。 (あかちゃんをもうふでくるみました。) - I wrapped the baby in a blanket.
- パンを布にくるんで持っていきます。 (パンをぬのにくるんでもっていきます。) - I will take the bread wrapped in a cloth.

It is godan: くるみます, くるんで, くるまない.
The passive くるまれる can describe being wrapped; the dictionary form くるむ is not itself an intransitive "be engulfed."

## How the kanji combine

The verb is usually written in kana. Its recognized spelling 包む uses [包](../kanji/包.md), "wrap; enclose."
That spelling can also represent the separate verb [包む](包む.md) (つつむ).
This article keeps くるむ as its own reading-specific identity rather than changing the existing つつむ card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

つつむ also means wrapping, and the two verbs overlap in covering something with paper or cloth.
[巻く](巻く.md) (まく) emphasizes winding or rolling; [覆う](覆う.md) (おおう) means covering.
These are useful contrasts, not rules that every object belongs to only one verb.

## Distinctive meaning

くるむ is useful when something is enclosed in a covering, as with a blanket or cloth.
Do not read every occurrence of 包む as くるむ, or transfer all of つつむ's figurative senses to it solely because the spelling can match.
