# 祖先 (そせん) - ancestor; forebears

## Meaning

祖先 means an ancestor or earlier generations of a family line.
It can include the founder of that lineage.
Use **Nの祖先** to identify whose ancestors are meant.

- 私たちの祖先はこの地域に住んでいました。 (わたしたちのそせんはこのちいきにすんでいました。) - Our ancestors lived in this region.
- 祖先から受け継いだ土地を守っています。 (そせんからうけついだとちをまもっています。) - We are looking after land inherited from our ancestors.

Japanese does not require a plural ending; context supplies singular or plural.
The scope can also be broader than one family, as in **人類の祖先**
(じんるいのそせん), "the ancestors of humankind."

## How the kanji combine

[祖](../kanji/祖.md) contributes ancestor or founder, and
[先](../kanji/先.md) contributes preceding or earlier.
Their related meanings locate the ancestral line in earlier generations.
The on readings combine as そ + せん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[先祖](先祖.md) (せんぞ) is a synonym with the characters in the opposite order,
not another spelling read そせん.
[子孫](子孫.md) (しそん), descendants, points in the opposite generational direction.
[祖父](祖父.md) (そふ) and [祖母](祖母.md) (そぼ) specify grandparents,
whereas 祖先 does not fix a particular generation.

## Distinctive meaning

An ancestor is not simply any older person or previous employee.
The family-line relationship matters in these examples.
The word alone does not specify exactly how many generations ago an ancestor lived.
Do not swap the readings of 祖先 and 先祖 just because their meanings overlap.
