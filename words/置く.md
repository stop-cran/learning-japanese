# 置く (おく) — put; place

## Meaning

置く means to put or place something somewhere. The thing placed takes を, and its destination
takes に. It is a transitive godan verb: useful forms are 置きます (おきます),
置いて (おいて), and 置いた (おいた).

- かばんを椅子の上に置いてください。
  (かばんをいすのうえにおいてください。)
  — Please put your bag on the chair.

## How the kanji combine

[置](../kanji/置.md) contributes placement, and the written く completes the verb.
This is the kun reading お.く, not a compound made from on readings.
The on reading チ appears in 位置 (いち), "position; location."

## Synonyms and antonyms

入れる (いれる), "put in," focuses on moving something into a space or container.
Compare 本を机に置く (ほんをつくえにおく), "put a book on the desk," with
本をかばんに入れる (ほんをかばんにいれる), "put a book in a bag."
The English verb "put" covers both, but the Japanese verbs highlight different arrangements.

## Distinctive meaning

置く presents the object as being set at a location. It does not simply mean carrying an object
around, and it need not mean putting it inside something.
In the request above, に marks the destination; it is not the particle で for where an activity
takes place.
