# 工事 (こうじ) - construction work

## Meaning

工事 is construction work, including work on buildings, roads, and other
facilities. It is a noun used with する; 工事をする also means "carry out
construction work." 工事中 (こうじちゅう) means "under construction."

- 橋の工事が始まりました。 (はしのこうじがはじまりました。) - Work on the bridge has begun.
- この道路は工事中です。 (このどうろはこうじちゅうです。) - This road is under construction.

The work can involve building, repairing, or altering a facility; the word
does not by itself say that an entirely new building is being erected.

## How the kanji combine

[工](../kanji/工.md) contributes construction or skilled work, and
[事](../kanji/事.md) contributes an undertaking or matter. The combination
names the construction undertaking. The on readings コウ + ジ give こうじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[建設](建設.md) (けんせつ) overlaps for construction, while
[建築](建築.md) (けんちく) particularly concerns buildings and architecture.
[作業](作業.md) (さぎょう) and [仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) mean work more
broadly. These are overlapping ranges, not mutually exclusive kinds of project.

## Distinctive meaning

工事 directs attention to construction work being carried out.
It does not name every factory activity, and it is not the ordinary word
for any task simply because tools are involved. A 工事中 notice describes
the state of the work, not a guarantee that the whole site is impassable.
