# 理科 (りか) - science; science subjects

## Meaning

理科 is science as a school subject. It also has broader educational uses:
natural sciences and related fields, including mathematics, engineering
and medicine, or the corresponding university faculties.
It is a noun, not a する verb meaning "do science."

- 今日の理科の授業では植物について勉強しました。 (きょうのりかのじゅぎょうではしょくぶつについてべんきょうしました。) - In today's science class, we studied plants.
- 子どものころ、理科が好きでした。 (こどものころ、りかがすきでした。) - I liked science when I was a child.

## How the kanji combine

[理](../kanji/理.md) contributes principle or reason, and
[科](../kanji/科.md) a branch or course of study.
The combination names scientific fields or instruction.
The on readings are リ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[科学](科学.md) (かがく) concerns science as systematic knowledge or inquiry.
[自然科学](自然科学.md) (しぜんかがく) specifically names natural science.
文科 (ぶんか) contrasts the literary/humanities side of education with 理科.

## Distinctive meaning

For a child's school timetable, 理科 is the useful subject name.
This does not make "school science only" a rule:
the broader field and university-faculty senses are also recorded.
Do not turn the practical 理科/科学 distinction into a claim that their meanings never overlap.
