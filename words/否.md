# 否 (いや) - no; rather

## Meaning

The reading here is いや, an interjection saying "no" or rejecting a preceding suggestion. It can also introduce a correction, including a correction to one's own words. It is normally written in kana.

- 「明日は休み？」「いや、仕事だよ。」 (「あしたはやすみ？」「いや、しごとだよ。」) - "Are you off tomorrow?" "No, I'm working."
- 三人、いや、四人来ます。 (さんにん、いや、よにんきます。) - Three people, no, four people are coming.

The second example is a correction, not a statement that the speaker dislikes the visitors.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [否](../kanji/否.md) carries a negative meaning. There is no compound to divide. Recognize the written form 否, but use いや as the reading for this interjection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いいえ](いいえ.md) is a useful polite negative answer; [はい](はい.md) supplies an affirmative contrast. [嫌](嫌.md) (いや), "unpleasant; unwanted," shares the sound but has different grammar and meaning.

## Distinctive meaning

いや can sound blunt when it directly contradicts someone; tone and the following explanation matter. This entry is not a noun meaning "the noes" or opposing votes. Nor should another reading of 否 be substituted for いや simply because both involve negation.
