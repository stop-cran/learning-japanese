# 絶えず (たえず) - constantly; without interruption

## Meaning

絶えず is an adverb meaning constantly, continually or without interruption.
Depending on the event, this can describe continuing activity or recurring
events that keep coming.

- この機械は絶えず動いています。
  (このきかいはたえずうごいています。) - This machine is running constantly.
- 客が絶えず訪れます。
  (きゃくがたえずおとずれます。) - Customers keep coming.

The second example need not mean that the same customer remains present
forever. It presents arrivals as an ongoing stream.

## How the kanji combine

[絶](../kanji/絶.md) appears in 絶える (たえる), cease or
die out. 絶えず contains its stem 絶え plus the negative form ず:
without ceasing. This explains this expression, not every construction
containing ず.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[常に](常に.md) (つねに), [いつも](いつも.md), [始終](始終.md)
(しじゅう), [年中](年中.md) (ねんじゅう) and [ずっと](ずっと.md)
have always or continuing uses. [しきりに](しきりに.md) can describe
incessant or repeated activity.
[いつでも](いつでも.md) has an all-times use as well as anytime;
[どんどん](どんどん.md) has a continuous/successive use beyond its
rapid-progress meaning.

Those overlaps do not make every anytime or rapid-progress use a substitute
for 絶えず. [よく](よく.md) and [しばしば](しばしば.md) can instead
describe frequency without necessarily presenting an uninterrupted stream.

## Distinctive meaning

絶えず does not by itself give a numerical frequency or an eternal duration.
The relevant period comes from context. Avoid replacing it mechanically
with an expression meaning merely several times or whenever convenient.
