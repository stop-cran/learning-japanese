# 垂直 (すいちょく) - vertical; perpendicular

## Meaning

垂直 describes a vertical orientation, or a perpendicular relationship: meeting a reference line or plane at a right angle. Use 垂直な before a noun and 垂直に for an orientation or manner.

- この柱は垂直に立っています。 (このはしらはすいちょくにたっています。) - This pillar stands vertically.
- 二つの線は互いに垂直です。 (ふたつのせんはたがいにすいちょくです。) - The two lines are perpendicular to each other.

A は B に垂直だ means "A is perpendicular to B." The reference matters.

## How the kanji combine

垂 contributes hanging down, and [直](../kanji/直.md) contributes straightness. The readings combine as スイ + チョク. These character associations help recall the word; the perpendicular sense concerns a relationship between directions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[水平](水平.md) is horizontal or level. [縦](縦.md) can describe the vertical or lengthwise direction, but does not by itself specify a right angle between two lines.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every 垂直 as "upright on the page." Two slanted lines can be perpendicular to one another. Conversely, the ordinary vertical use is about orientation relative to the local horizontal, not an arbitrary diagonal chosen as a reference.
