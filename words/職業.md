# 職業 (しょくぎょう) — occupation; profession

## Meaning

職業 names someone's occupation or profession: the kind of work they do.
It is a noun, so a simple identification can use 職業は〜です (しょくぎょうは〜です),
"the occupation is ...".

- 父の職業は医者です。 (ちちのしょくぎょうはいしゃです。)
  — My father's occupation is doctor.

This identifies the work category; it does not describe what task he is doing at this moment.

## How the kanji combine

[職](../kanji/職.md) concerns employment or a post, and [業](../kanji/業.md) concerns work
or an occupation. The two related work meanings reinforce each other.
The on readings are ショク + ギョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) also covers jobs and employment, but can name a task or assignment.
職場 (しょくば) is the workplace, not the occupation.
[就職](就職.md) (しゅうしょく) names finding employment; it describes an event rather than the kind of work.

## Distinctive meaning

If someone asks about 職業, an occupation such as 医者 (いしゃ), "doctor," answers the category.
Naming a specific task to finish today answers a different question.
Do not force a contrast in every sentence: 仕事 and 職業 overlap when the topic really is
someone's occupation.
