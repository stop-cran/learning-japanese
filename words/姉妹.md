# 姉妹 (しまい) - sisters

## Meaning

姉妹 is a noun meaning sisters, considered together through their sibling relationship.
It can describe two or more sisters. 二人姉妹 (ふたりしまい) means "two sisters";
三人姉妹 (さんにんしまい) means "three sisters."

- 私たちは三人姉妹です。
  (わたしたちはさんにんしまいです。) - We are three sisters.
- あの二人は顔が似ていますが、姉妹ではありません。
  (あのふたりはかおがにていますが、しまいではありません。)
  - Those two look alike, but they are not sisters.

The word does not tell you which individual is older. Use 姉 or 妹 when that
relationship to a particular person matters.

## How the kanji combine

[姉](../kanji/姉.md), "older sister," and [妹](../kanji/妹.md), "younger sister,"
form a paired compound covering sisters together.
The on readings シ + マイ differ from the individual native words
[姉](姉.md) (あね) and [妹](妹.md) (いもうと).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[兄弟](兄弟.md) (きょうだい) can mean brothers or siblings.
姉妹 specifically identifies sisters. Brothers are a related family grouping,
not an antonym in the sense of an opposite quality.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read the two characters separately as あねいもうと in this word.
The dictionary also records a special reading きょうだい for 姉妹, but the reading
taught here is しまい. Counting sisters does not require adding a separate plural ending.
