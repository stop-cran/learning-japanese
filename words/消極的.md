# 消極的 (しょうきょくてき) - passive; half-hearted

## Meaning

消極的 describes an attitude that is passive, reluctant to take the initiative, or negative about doing something. It is a na-adjective: 消極的な態度 (しょうきょくてきなたいど), "a passive attitude." Use に to identify what someone is reluctant about.

- 彼は新しい計画に消極的です。 (かれはあたらしいけいかくにしょうきょくてきです。) - He is reluctant about the new plan.
- 会議では消極的な意見が多く出ました。 (かいぎではしょうきょくてきないけんがおおくでました。) - Many unenthusiastic opinions were expressed at the meeting.

消極的に can modify an action. The word describes the stance, not a grammatical passive verb form.

## How the kanji combine

[消](../kanji/消.md) has diminishing or extinguishing associations, and [極](../kanji/極.md) a pole or extreme. 消極 concerns a nonassertive stance, and 的 makes an adjective expressing that character. Learn the established whole word rather than deriving its meaning from "extinguish an extreme."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[積極的](積極的.md) (せっきょくてき), "proactive; enthusiastic," is the direct contrast. [慎重](慎重.md) (しんちょう), "cautious; prudent," can describe careful consideration without a lack of initiative. [遠慮](遠慮.md) (えんりょ) concerns holding back out of consideration.

## Distinctive meaning

Being 消極的 about one proposal does not establish that someone is generally lazy or pessimistic. The reluctance may concern that particular action. Likewise, a negative attitude is not the same thing as every use of English "negative," such as a negative number.
