# どうして — why; for what reason

## Meaning

どうして usually asks why something is so or why someone did something. It can be used with a polite question ending or in casual conversation.

- どうして日本語を勉強しているんですか。 (どうしてにほんごをべんきょうしているんですか。) — Why are you studying Japanese?
- どうして来なかったの。 (どうしてこなかったの。) — Why did you not come?

## How the kanji combine

Ordinary writing uses kana. The rare spelling 如何して is not needed for everyday use. The recognizable formation どう + して also helps explain its dictionary sense "how; by what means."

## Synonyms and antonyms

なぜ is another "why," often suited to explanatory writing. なんで is common in conversation. どうやって more explicitly asks about a method, "how do you do it?"

## Distinctive meaning

Although どうして can ask about means, a listener often understands a request for a reason. Choose どうやって when the procedure is the point. A bare どうして？ can sound curious, surprised, or accusatory depending on context and tone; the word itself does not guarantee politeness.
