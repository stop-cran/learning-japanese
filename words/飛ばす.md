# 飛ばす (とばす) - send flying; skip over

## Meaning

飛ばす is a transitive godan verb. It can mean to make something fly or send it flying, and it also means to skip or leave something out. Mark the affected or omitted thing with を.

- 子どもたちは紙飛行機を飛ばしています。 (こどもたちはかみひこうきをとばしています。) - The children are flying paper airplanes.
- 難しい問題は飛ばして、次に進みました。 (むずかしいもんだいはとばして、つぎにすすみました。) - I skipped the difficult question and went on to the next one.

The past is 飛ばした (とばした); the negative is 飛ばさない (とばさない).

## How the kanji combine

[飛](../kanji/飛.md) supplies the flying or leaping association; ばす completes the native verb. In the omission use, the action passes over something rather than dealing with it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飛ぶ](飛ぶ.md) (とぶ) is intransitive: 紙飛行機が飛ぶ, "the paper airplane flies." 飛ばす presents someone making it fly.

[投げる](投げる.md) (なげる) means to throw. It emphasizes the throwing action, not every way of sending something through the air.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "skip over; omit" is a real sense, not a reading error. In a reading task, 一行飛ばす (いちぎょうとばす) means "skip a line"; nothing literally flies. Context and the object select the meaning, so do not force the physical translation into every sentence.
