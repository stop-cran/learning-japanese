# 村 (むら) - village

## Meaning

村 means a village, either a small settlement or a local administrative unit
called a village. It is a noun for a place or community.

- 祖父は山の近くの村に住んでいます。 (そふはやまのちかくのむらにすんでいます。) - My grandfather lives in a village near the mountains.

に marks where he lives. 村の人たち (むらのひとたち) means "the people of
the village."

## How the kanji combine

村 writes the native noun むら without okurigana. The character also has
the on-reading ソン in compounds. Place names must be learned individually;
do not assume every name ending in 村 has the same pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

町 (まち), "town," and 市 (し), "city," provide useful settlement and
administrative contrasts. 田舎 (いなか), "countryside," describes a broader
setting and need not name one particular village.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling somewhere a 村 does not imply that every resident farms or that
the place lacks modern services. The word identifies the settlement or
municipal category, not a complete description of its way of life.
