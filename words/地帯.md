# 地帯 (ちたい) - area; zone; belt

## Meaning

地帯 is an area or zone characterized by something, such as its
terrain, climate, or industrial activity. It is a noun.

- この地帯は冬に雪が多いです。 (このちたいはふゆにゆきがおおいです。) - This area gets a lot of snow in winter.
- 川沿いに工業地帯が広がっています。 (かわぞいにこうぎょうちたいがひろがっています。) - An industrial zone extends along the river.

工業地帯 (こうぎょうちたい) is an industrial area or belt.
The word identifies an extent of land, not just its boundary line.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) contributes land or place, and
[帯](../kanji/帯.md) contributes a belt or zone. The combination
suggests an area of land considered as a zone. Read チ + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[地域](地域.md) (ちいき), [地区](地区.md) (ちく), and
[区域](区域.md) (くいき) overlap in area meanings.
地帯 is useful when a shared geographical or other characteristic
defines the area. This is an emphasis, not an absolute size ranking.

## Distinctive meaning

English "belt" here is geographical, not a belt worn with trousers.
A 地帯 need not be a narrow strip or an official administrative
division. Its characteristic and extent come from the surrounding
description, not from a fixed shape encoded in the word.
