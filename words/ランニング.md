# ランニング - running; tank top

## Meaning

ランニング names running as an activity. It is a noun; ランニングをする
means to go running or do some running.

- 毎朝、公園でランニングをしています。 (まいあさ、こうえんでランニングをしています。) - I go running in the park every morning.

It also abbreviates ランニングシャツ: a tank top, vest or singlet worn
as an undergarment or sportswear.

- 白いランニングを着ています。 (しろいランニングをきています。) - I am wearing a white singlet.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[走る](走る.md) (はしる) is the general verb "run."
[運動](運動.md) (うんどう) covers exercise more broadly.
In the clothing use, [肌着](肌着.md) (はだぎ) is clothing worn next to
the skin, and [シャツ](シャツ.md) can also include an undershirt.

## Distinctive meaning

The activity and the garment are two established senses, not a claim
that any shirt worn while running is called ランニング.
食後のランニング (しょくごのランニング) concerns running after a meal;
ランニングを着る selects the clothing sense.
