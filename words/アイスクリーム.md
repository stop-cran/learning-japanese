# アイスクリーム - ice cream

## Meaning

アイスクリーム is ice cream. It is a noun, taking ordinary noun particles: **アイスクリームを食べる**, "eat ice cream," and **アイスクリームが好き**, "like ice cream."

- 食後にアイスクリームを食べました。 (しょくごにアイスクリームをたべました。) - I ate ice cream after the meal.
- バニラのアイスクリームを一つください。 (バニラのアイスクリームをひとつください。) - One vanilla ice cream, please.

The second example uses 一つ for one serving in an ordering context. A flavor can precede の, as in チョコレートのアイスクリーム, "chocolate ice cream."

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana and corresponds to "ice cream." There are no kanji components to decompose. Keep the long-vowel mark in クリーム: アイスクリーム, not アイスクリム.

## Synonyms and antonyms

アイス can be a shortened label in a dessert context, though the surrounding words matter. [氷](氷.md) (こおり) means ice, not ice cream. [クリーム](クリーム.md) names cream and is not by itself the frozen dessert.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate the two English components independently when ordering: the established Japanese food name is アイスクリーム. A request for 氷 would normally concern ice, for example in a drink. This card teaches the ordinary food word rather than the detailed regulatory categories used on product labels.
