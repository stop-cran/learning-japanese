# パイロット (パイロット) - pilot

## Meaning

パイロット is a pilot, especially a person who flies an aircraft. It is a noun naming the person: **パイロットになる** means to become a pilot, and パイロットとして働く means to work as one.

- 将来、パイロットになりたいです。 (しょうらい、パイロットになりたいです。) - I want to become a pilot in the future.
- パイロットが出発前に天気を確認しました。 (パイロットがしゅっぱつまえにてんきをかくにんしました。) - The pilot checked the weather before departure.

The first sentence expresses a career aspiration, not the action of flying at that moment.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword corresponds to English "pilot." Small ッ gives a pause before ト; the written form has no long-vowel mark. There are no kanji components to explain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

操縦士 (そうじゅうし) also names a pilot or qualified operator. [運転手](運転手.md) (うんてんしゅ) is a driver, for example of a car or bus. Both involve operating a vehicle, but the occupation terms are not interchangeable everywhere.

## Distinctive meaning

The word identifies an operator, not every person traveling in a [飛行機](飛行機.md) (ひこうき). A passenger is not a パイロット just because they fly as a traveler. Use a separate verb such as 操縦する (そうじゅうする), "pilot; operate," when describing the action.
