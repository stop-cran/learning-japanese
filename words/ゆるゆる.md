# ゆるゆる — loose or leisurely

## Meaning

ゆるゆる describes a loose fit or an unhurried pace.

- ベルトがゆるゆるで、ズボンが落ちそうだ。
  — The belt is so loose that the trousers might fall down.
- 休日はゆるゆる過ごした。
  — I spent the day off at a leisurely pace.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ゆるゆる can describe either a loose physical fit or relaxed activity; the surrounding words identify which.

## Distinctive meaning

It contrasts with tightly fitted or briskly paced activity, and is usually written in kana.
