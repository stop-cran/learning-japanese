# ふわっと — lightly floating or spreading

## Meaning

ふわっと describes something rising, drifting, or spreading lightly and softly.

- 窓から風が入り、羽毛がふわっと舞い上がった。
  — A breeze came through the window, and a feather floated up lightly.
- 焼きたてのパンの香りが、部屋にふわっと広がった。
  — The smell of freshly baked bread gently spread through the room.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ふわっと suggests a light, airy movement or sensation rather than a heavy or abrupt one.

## Distinctive meaning

It can also describe an uncertain or nervous manner in other contexts.
