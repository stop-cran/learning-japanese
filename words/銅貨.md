# 銅貨 (どうか) - copper coin

## Meaning

銅貨 is a noun meaning a copper coin. It identifies a kind of coin by its material, rather than giving a denomination, exchange value, or number of coins.

- 博物館で古い銅貨を見ました。 (はくぶつかんでふるいどうかをみました。) - I saw old copper coins at the museum.
- 箱の中に銅貨が三枚入っていました。 (はこのなかにどうかがさんまいはいっていました。) - There were three copper coins in the box.

Coins can be counted with 枚 (まい). In the second sentence, 三枚 supplies the number; 銅貨 itself does not distinguish singular from plural.

## How the kanji combine

銅 means copper, and 貨 contributes money or currency here. The first component specifies the material of the monetary object. The word is read どうか, with a long first vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[硬貨](硬貨.md) (こうか) is the broader word "coin," contrasting with paper money. 銅貨 specifies a material category within coins. [お金](お金.md) (おかね) is money generally, not specifically a copper coin.

## Distinctive meaning

The sound どうか also occurs in an unrelated expression used in requests. Here the kanji and noun constructions identify the copper-coin word: 銅貨を見た describes seeing coins, not asking a favor. The dictionary identity taught on this page is the coin noun; a shared pronunciation does not join those meanings into one lexical entry.
