# 互い (たがい) — each other

## Meaning

互い is a noun referring to people or sides in relation to one another.
Use 互いに (たがいに) with an action, and 互いの (たがいの) before a noun to mean "each other's."
It is not an い-adjective despite its final kana.

- 二人は互いに質問しました。 (ふたりはたがいにしつもんしました。) — The two asked each other questions.
- 二人は互いの名前を知っています。 (ふたりはたがいのなまえをしっています。) — The two know each other's names.

## How the kanji combine

[互](../kanji/互.md) supplies the idea of a mutual relation. This native word has one kanji,
read たが, followed by い; it is not a two-part Sino-Japanese compound.
The on reading ゴ appears in 相互 (そうご), "mutual; reciprocal."

## Synonyms and antonyms

相互 has a closely related meaning.
[一緒](一緒.md) (いっしょ) instead concerns doing something together in its relevant use.
一緒に帰る (いっしょにかえる), "go home together," describes shared activity;
it does not mean that each person is doing the action to the other.

## Distinctive meaning

Look for the relation between participants, not merely their number.
In 互いの名前, each person knows the other's name.
The word does not by itself imply friendship or agreement: people who disagree can still address one another.
