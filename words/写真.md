# 写真 (しゃしん) — photograph

## Meaning

写真 means a photograph or photography. The useful expression 写真を撮る (しゃしんをとる) means "take a photograph."

- 昨日、写真を撮りました。 (きのう、しゃしんを とりました。) — I took a photograph yesterday.
- この写真は母が撮りました。 (この しゃしんは ははが とりました。) — My mother took this photograph.

Use 撮る for taking the picture; it is different from simply possessing or looking at a photograph.

## How the kanji combine

[写](../kanji/写.md) contributes copying or depicting, and [真](../kanji/真.md) contributes reality or truth.
As a meaning aid, think "depict reality," a verb-and-object relationship, without treating that as proof of an image's accuracy.
Both readings are on: シャ + シン → しゃしん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- フォト is an English-derived word for a photo.
- 画像 (がぞう), "image," is broader: an image on a screen need not be a photograph.
- 絵 (え) can mean a drawing or painting. It contrasts with a photograph in that context, but is not a direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

写真 is the ordinary word for a photographic image, whether digital or printed. The spelling does not imply that it is unedited or reliable evidence.
Although older dictionary senses include moving pictures, the modern everyday word for a movie is [映画](映画.md) (えいが), not 写真.
