# 吠える (ほえる) - bark; howl; roar

## Meaning

吠える is an intransitive ichidan verb for barking, howling, or roaring.
The animal making the sound takes が; the person or thing it barks at can take に.
Forms include 吠えます, 吠えた, 吠えて, and 吠えない.

- 犬が知らない人に吠えています。
  (いぬがしらないひとにほえています。) - The dog is barking at a stranger.
- 遠くで犬が吠える声が聞こえました。
  (とおくでいぬがほえるこえがきこえました。)
  - I heard a dog barking in the distance.

The verb also has figurative uses for a howling wind, a roaring sea, or someone
yelling forcefully. These are vivid descriptions, not neutral substitutes for every kind of sound.

## How the kanji combine

吠 is the single kanji carrying the barking meaning; it is read ほ here.
える completes the written verb. Its ichidan stem is 吠え (ほえ).
No multi-kanji compound is involved.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鳴く](鳴く.md) (なく) covers animal calls more broadly.
[叫ぶ](叫ぶ.md) (さけぶ) means to shout or cry out.
吠える highlights a barking or roaring quality rather than a quiet call.

## Distinctive meaning

English "cry" is ambiguous. 吠える can describe a loud shout, but does not mean
ordinary tearful crying simply because someone is upset.
The choice of animal and context determines whether "bark," "howl," or "roar" is the natural translation.
