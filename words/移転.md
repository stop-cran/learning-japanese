# 移転 (いてん) - relocation; transfer of property

## Meaning

移転 is relocation to another site, especially of premises such as an
office or shop. It is a noun and a する verb. It also names the transfer
of property or rights in legal and administrative contexts.

- 会社は来月、大阪に移転します。 (かいしゃはらいげつ、おおさかにいてんします。) - The company will relocate to Osaka next month.
- 本社を東京に移転しました。 (ほんしゃをとうきょうにいてんしました。) - We relocated the head office to Tokyo.

The first subject relocates; in the second, を marks the premises being
relocated. に gives the destination. 所有権の移転 (しょゆうけんのいてん)
means "transfer of ownership."

## How the kanji combine

[移](../kanji/移.md), shift or transfer, and [転](../kanji/転.md),
turn or change, contribute related change-of-place ideas.
The on readings are イ + テン. The compound does not mean that the
building must physically roll to its new site.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[引っ越す](引っ越す.md) (ひっこす) is the everyday verb for moving home.
[移動](移動.md) (いどう) covers changes of position more broadly;
moving to the next classroom need not be relocating one's premises.
[移す](移す.md) and [移る](移る.md) distinguish transitive and
intransitive transfer constructions.

## Distinctive meaning

An office relocation may involve a new address without transporting the
old building. The legal use likewise concerns a change in ownership or
rights, not necessarily a change in an object's physical location.
English legal "demise" can mean a transfer or conveyance; it does not make
移転 a general Japanese word for death.
