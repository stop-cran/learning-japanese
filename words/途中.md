# 途中 (とちゅう) — on the way; midway

## Meaning

途中 places something along a journey before arrival, or within an activity before completion.
It is a noun that can also work in adverbial expressions about when something happens.

- 学校へ行く途中で、友達に会いました。 (がっこうへいくとちゅうで、ともだちにあいました。) — I met a friend on the way to school.
- まだ仕事の途中です。 (まだしごとのとちゅうです。) — I am still in the middle of my work.

Use a noun plus の, as in 仕事の途中, or a verb phrase directly before 途中, as in 学校へ行く途中.
For an action already in progress, 本を読んでいる途中 (ほんをよんでいるとちゅう) means "in the middle of reading a book."

## How the kanji combine

[途](../kanji/途.md) contributes a way or course, and [中](../kanji/中.md) places you within it.
The on readings are ト + チュウ.
The same combination works with a physical route or the progress of an activity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

道中 (どうちゅう) can mean a journey or "on the way" during one.
That overlaps with the travel use of 途中, but does not make it the right replacement for every unfinished activity.
In 仕事の途中, the focus is work that is still in progress.

## Distinctive meaning

途中で can introduce an event that occurs along the way, as in the meeting example.
The word does not require the exact numerical halfway point.
Translate it as "on the way" or "partway through" when English "halfway" would suggest an unwarranted measurement.
