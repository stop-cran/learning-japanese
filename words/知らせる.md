# 知らせる (しらせる) - to notify; let know

## Meaning

知らせる means to inform someone or let them know something. Mark the recipient with に and the information with を.

- 友達に新しい住所を知らせました。 (ともだちにあたらしいじゅうしょをしらせました。) - I let my friend know my new address.
- 日にちが決まったら、知らせてください。 (ひにちがきまったら、しらせてください。) - Please let me know when the date is decided.

It is a transitive ichidan verb: 知らせます, 知らせない, 知らせて. The person and information can be omitted when already clear.

## How the kanji combine

[知](../kanji/知.md) contributes knowing. らせる completes the written word, but the ichidan stem is しらせ. Compare 知る (しる), "know" or "come to know," with 知らせる, which makes information available to another person.

## Synonyms and antonyms

伝える (つたえる) means convey or pass on. 教える (おしえる) can mean tell as well as teach. 連絡する (れんらくする) emphasizes contacting or communicating.

## Distinctive meaning

知らせる focuses on notification, not necessarily on giving a detailed explanation or lesson. The related noun 知らせ (しらせ) means news or a notice; お知らせ often labels an announcement.
