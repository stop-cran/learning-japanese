# 付近 (ふきん) - vicinity; nearby area

## Meaning

付近 is the vicinity or area near a reference point.
It is a noun and can follow a place name directly:
駅付近 (えきふきん), the area near the station.
Nの付近 is also possible.

- 駅の付近にホテルがあります。 (えきのふきんにホテルがあります。) - There is a hotel near the station.
- この付近では駐車できません。 (このふきんではちゅうしゃできません。) - You cannot park around here.

付近の店 (ふきんのみせ) means shops in the vicinity.

## How the kanji combine

[付](../kanji/付.md) contributes attachment or closeness, and
[近](../kanji/近.md) nearness. Associate the combination with
the area close to a reference point. The on readings are フ + キン.
This memory association does not establish a measured radius.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[近く](近く.md) (ちかく), [近所](近所.md) (きんじょ), and
[周辺](周辺.md) (しゅうへん) overlap in nearby or surrounding
areas. 近所 is useful for a local neighborhood, while 付近
readily takes a particular landmark as its reference.
[側](側.md) (そば) can emphasize being close by or beside
something. None imposes a universal distance threshold.

## Distinctive meaning

附近 is a dictionary-listed alternative spelling, marked rare
in the pinned JMdict. Use 付近 as the main spelling here.
The word identifies a surrounding area, not an exact address,
a particular adjacent building, or a whole municipality by default.
