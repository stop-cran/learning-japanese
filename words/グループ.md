# グループ (グループ) - group

## Meaning

グループ is a group, especially of people. It can be a temporary classroom
group, a circle of people, or an established group. It is a noun:
グループで means "as a group" or "in groups," according to the activity.

- 四人のグループで話し合ってください。 (よにんのグループではなしあってください。) - Please discuss it in groups of four.
- 私たちは三つのグループに分かれました。 (わたしたちはみっつのグループにわかれました。) - We split into three groups.

The first example gives the membership size; the second gives the number of
groups. グループに入る (グループにはいる) means joining a group.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "group." There are no
kanji components. The long-vowel mark in ルー belongs to the spelling and
pronunciation: グループ, not グルプ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[チーム](チーム.md) is a team, typically organized for a shared task or contest.
[団体](団体.md) (だんたい) can refer to an organized body or a group.
[仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) can identify companions or fellow members rather than
the group as a unit.

## Distinctive meaning

A グループ need not have a leader, permanent membership, or a competitive goal.
The classroom example may describe people grouped together just for one
activity. Conversely, the word does not imply that the members are close
friends. Add the purpose or membership when the context does not make it clear.
