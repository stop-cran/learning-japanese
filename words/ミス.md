# ミス (ミス) - mistake; Miss

## Meaning

ミス is a mistake, error, or blunder. It is a noun and can take する:
ミスをする or ミスする means making a mistake. A separate same-spelling word
is the title "Miss" or "Ms.," used as a prefix.

- 計算でミスをしました。 (けいさんでミスをしました。) - I made a mistake in the calculation.
- 同じミスを繰り返さないようにします。 (おなじミスをくりかえさないようにします。) - I will try not to repeat the same mistake.

For the separate title, ミス・スミス means "Miss Smith." This is not a description
of an error made by Smith.

## How the kanji combine

These words are written in katakana. The mistake entry records English "miss"
as its source; do not assume that ミス is simply a Japanese shortening of the
whole English word "mistake." The personal-title use belongs to a separate
dictionary identity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[間違い](間違い.md) (まちがい) also means a mistake.
[失敗](失敗.md) (しっぱい) concerns failure or an unsuccessful attempt. A small
ミス can be corrected without the entire project becoming a failure.

## Distinctive meaning

The error sense does not automatically imply dishonesty or intentional
rule-breaking. Keep it distinct from [不正](不正.md) (ふせい).
The title sense is not a general replacement for さん in everyday Japanese
address; it belongs to contexts using that title. Neither identity means
"miss someone" in the emotional English sense.
