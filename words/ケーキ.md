# ケーキ - cake

## Meaning

ケーキ is cake, whether a whole cake or a portion identified by context. The noun itself does not change for singular and plural.

- 誕生日にケーキを作りました。 (たんじょうびにケーキをつくりました。) - I made a cake for the birthday.
- このケーキは甘すぎません。 (このケーキはあますぎません。) - This cake is not too sweet.

Use an appropriate counter or quantity expression when the number of cakes or slices matters.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "cake." The long-vowel mark ー is part of the spelling: ケーキ, not ケキ. There is no kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お菓子 (おかし) is a broader category of sweets and snacks. パン is bread, not the ordinary synonym for cake. These food categories do not form a natural antonym pair.

## Distinctive meaning

ケーキ does not guarantee a birthday cake, icing, or one particular recipe. More specific names such as チョコレートケーキ identify the variety. English figurative expressions containing "cake" should not be translated by substituting ケーキ word for word.
