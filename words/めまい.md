# めまい - dizziness; vertigo

## Meaning

めまい is a noun for dizziness, giddiness, or vertigo: a sensation of
spinning, swaying, or becoming light-headed.

- 急に立ち上がったら、めまいがしました。 (きゅうにたちあがったら、めまいがしました。) - I felt dizzy when I stood up suddenly.
- めまいがするので、少し座ります。 (めまいがするので、すこしすわります。) - I feel dizzy, so I will sit down for a while.

めまいがする is the useful pattern for experiencing the symptom.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual; 目眩 and 眩暈 are recognized spellings of めまい.
Learn the whole reading rather than inventing a regular on-reading
combination. 眩暈 also appears in a separate medical-reading entry
げんうん; that does not change this card's reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ふらふら](ふらふら.md) can describe dizziness or unsteadiness, but also
wandering or other unstable states. [酔う](酔う.md) (よう), "become
intoxicated; get motion sick," describes related conditions rather than
being an unrestricted synonym for every dizzy sensation.

## Distinctive meaning

めまい names a symptom, not its diagnosis or one guaranteed cause.
It is not an i-adjective despite ending in い: say めまいがする, not
conjugations invented from めまい. The identically written titles of
novels or a film are not additional symptom meanings.
