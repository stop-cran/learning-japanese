# タイヤ - tire; tyre

## Meaning

タイヤ is a tire: the outer covering fitted around a vehicle's wheel.
Both tire and tyre are English spellings of the meaning.

- 自転車のタイヤに空気を入れました。
  (じてんしゃのタイヤにくうきをいれました。) - I put air in the bicycle tire.
- 古いタイヤを交換します。
  (ふるいタイヤをこうかんします。) - I will replace the old tire.

タイヤに marks what receives the air; タイヤを marks the part being replaced.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English tire.
The pinned community-list form is タイア. JMdict recognizes タイア as a rare
kana form in the same family; this article uses タイヤ while explicitly
preserving that source alias. The spelling flag does not mean the source
row names a different vehicle part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[車輪](車輪.md) (しゃりん) is the wheel as a whole, not just its tire.
[輪](輪.md) (わ) is broader, including a ring or wheel.
Replacing a タイヤ does not necessarily mean replacing the entire 車輪.
There is no useful universal antonym for this component.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use タイヤ for every circular vehicle part or for an arbitrary ring.
The wheel and its outer tire are related components, not unrestricted
synonyms. The rare タイア spelling is recognition coverage, not a measured
claim about present-day spelling frequency.
