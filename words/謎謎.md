# 謎謎 (なぞなぞ) - riddle; puzzle

## Meaning

なぞなぞ is a riddle or puzzle, especially a question posed for someone to
work out. It is a noun. なぞなぞを出す means to pose a riddle;
なぞなぞを解く means to solve one.

- 子供たちになぞなぞを出しました。 (こどもたちになぞなぞをだしました。) - I posed a riddle to the children.
- このなぞなぞの答えが分かりますか。 (このなぞなぞのこたえがわかりますか。) - Do you know the answer to this riddle?

に marks the people receiving the riddle in the first sentence.
The answer may depend on wordplay rather than factual knowledge.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The source writes 謎謎; 謎々 is another recorded spelling.
Both repeat なぞ, "riddle; mystery." The mark 々 repeats 謎 and is not
another separately pronounced character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[謎](謎.md) (なぞ) is broader: a mystery can remain unexplained without
anyone having deliberately posed it as a game.
クイズ is a quiz, and パズル a puzzle; their ranges overlap with riddles
but also include other kinds of questions or games.

## Distinctive meaning

English "puzzle" does not make なぞなぞ the name of every jigsaw puzzle
or every difficult practical problem. The question-and-answer game is
the useful starting scene here. Although riddles can be for children,
the noun is not grammatically restricted to a child's question.
Solving one need not involve physically assembling anything.
