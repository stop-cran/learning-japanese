# 奔走 (ほんそう) — working hard to get things done; running about

## Meaning

奔走 can mean rushing around, especially making a sustained effort to get something done. The activity may involve contacting people or arranging things, not literally running from place to place.

- 父は会場探しに奔走した。 (ちちはかいじょうさがしにほんそうした。) — My father worked hard to find a venue.
- 職員は祭りの準備に奔走している。 (しょくいんはまつりのじゅんびにほんそうしている。) — The staff are rushing around getting the festival ready.

## How the kanji combine

[奔](../kanji/奔.md) and [走](../kanji/走.md) both evoke running. The compound puts that activity at the service of a task in these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[走る](走る.md) can describe running a race. [努力](努力.md) means effort more generally; studying quietly every evening takes 努力 without necessarily involving 奔走.

## Distinctive meaning

Look for the goal before に: 会場探しに奔走する says what all that effort is for. It does not say that the venue has already been found.
