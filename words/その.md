# その (その) - that before a noun

## Meaning

その goes directly before a noun: "that ..." It often points to something near the listener, or refers back to something already mentioned.

- その本を見せてください。 (そのほんをみせてください。) - Please show me that book.
- 昨日映画を見ました。その映画はおもしろかったです。 (きのうえいがをみました。そのえいがはおもしろかったです。) - I saw a film yesterday. That film was interesting.

No extra の or な is needed between その and its noun. It does not normally stand alone in place of the noun.

## How the kanji combine

その is normally written in kana. The source-list spelling 其の is rare in modern everyday writing and does not indicate a different meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

この points toward the speaker's side; あの can point away from both speaker and listener. それ stands alone where その requires a following noun: それをください, "Please give me that."

## Distinctive meaning

その is not limited to visible objects. It can retrieve a person, event, or idea from the conversation. In that use, English may prefer "the" rather than "that"; Japanese still makes the connection to the previously introduced topic explicit.
