# 追い掛ける (おいかける) - chase; run after

## Meaning

追い掛ける means to chase, run after or pursue someone or something.
追いかける is another standard spelling in the same dictionary entry.
Both spellings represent the same verb.

- 犬がボールを追い掛けています。
  (いぬがボールをおいかけています。) - The dog is chasing the ball.
- 子供たちは公園で友達を追いかけていました。
  (こどもたちはこうえんでともだちをおいかけていました。) - The children were chasing their friends in the park.

The target takes を. This is a transitive ichidan verb:
追い掛けます, 追い掛けて, 追い掛けた, 追い掛けない.

## How the kanji combine

追い is the continuing form of [追う](追う.md) (おう), "chase,"
written with [追](../kanji/追.md).
The second part is written [掛](../kanji/掛.md)ける here.
Learn the whole native compound; translating 掛 as "hang" does not
by itself explain "chase."

## Synonyms and antonyms

追う overlaps in the pursuit meaning.
[追い越す](追い越す.md) (おいこす) means to overtake or get ahead
of someone, a different result.
[逃げる](逃げる.md) (にげる), "flee," can describe what the pursued
person does, not the same action from an interchangeable viewpoint.

## Distinctive meaning

Chasing does not guarantee catching the target.
JMdict also records a subsequent-event use specifically in
追いかけて or 追いかけるように. That construction note does not
make every standalone 追い掛ける mean "then."
The rare 追い駆ける and search-only 追掛ける spellings are not
production targets here.
