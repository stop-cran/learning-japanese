# 林 (はやし) -- woods; grove

## Meaning

林 is an area where trees grow together: woods, a grove, or a stand of trees.

家の裏に小さな林があります。 (いえのうらにちいさなはやしがあります。) -- There is a small grove behind the house.

林の中 (はやしのなか) means inside the woods. Use 林を歩く (はやしをあるく) for walking through them; を marks the area traversed.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 林 writes the native noun はやし. Its two 木 shapes offer a visual reminder of trees together, but they are not pronounced as two separate words in 林.

## Synonyms and antonyms

森 (もり) often suggests a denser or more extensive wooded area. There is overlap, and the vocabulary alone does not impose a precise size boundary. 木 (き) names a tree rather than a whole wooded area.

## Distinctive meaning

林 names the place or grouping of trees, not a person who works in forestry. "Forester" is therefore not an appropriate translation for this noun. The English "wood" here also means woodland, not a piece of timber.
