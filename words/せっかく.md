# せっかく - with effort; a valuable opportunity

## Meaning

せっかく highlights effort or trouble someone has taken, a welcome opportunity,
or another person's kind offer. Context determines the English translation.
It can be adverbial, or modify a noun with せっかくの.

- せっかく来たのだから、中を見ていきましょう。 (せっかくきたのだから、なかをみていきましょう。) - Since we've come all this way, let's have a look inside.
- せっかく作ったのに、誰も食べませんでした。 (せっかくつくったのに、だれもたべませんでした。) - Although I went to the trouble of making it, no one ate it.
- せっかくのお誘いですが、今日は行けません。 (せっかくのおさそいですが、きょうはいけません。) - Thank you for the kind invitation, but I cannot go today.

In the first example, のだから gives a reason to make use of the occasion.
In the second, のに marks the disappointing contrast; that disappointment is
not an unavoidable meaning of せっかく by itself.

## How the kanji combine

The source spelling is **折角**. Digital Daijisen traces the older noun meaning
taking pains to a Han-shu anecdote about breaking a horn:
[折](../kanji/折.md) contributes breaking and [角](../kanji/角.md) a horn.
That historical account is not a literal folded-corner definition of the modern
expression. Learn せっかく as a whole, with the small っ. The primary spelling
on this card is kana, so its metadata does not include unwritten characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[貴重](貴重.md) (きちょう) means precious or valuable; [手間](手間.md)
(てま) concerns time and trouble; [努力](努力.md) (どりょく) is effort.
They help unpack different contexts, but せっかく does not simply replace each noun.

## Distinctive meaning

Both positive and thwarted uses are possible: taking advantage of an opportunity,
regretting wasted effort, or acknowledging an invitation before declining.
Learn the surrounding construction rather than translating every occurrence as
in vain or with great difficulty. A recorded kanji spelling does not require
writing this expression in kanji.
