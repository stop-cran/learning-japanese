# 目下 (めした) - junior person; social subordinate

## Meaning

目下, read めした, refers to someone junior to oneself in age, rank, or position.
It can also name that relative status. 目下の人 (めしたのひと) is "a person junior to oneself."

- 目下の人にも丁寧に接します。 (めしたのひとにもていねいにせっします。) - I treat people junior to me politely too.
- 彼は私より目下です。 (かれはわたしよりめしたです。) - He is junior to me.

It is a relationship term, not a statement that someone is less valuable as a person.

## How the kanji combine

[目](../kanji/目.md) is read め and [下](../kanji/下.md) is read した.
The native compound presents relative social position as "below."
It is not an instruction to read every occurrence of 目下 this way.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[目上](目上.md) (めうえ) is the contrasting term for someone senior.
[後輩](後輩.md) (こうはい) is someone junior through later entry into a school,
workplace, or activity. 部下 (ぶか) more specifically concerns a workplace subordinate.
The categories can overlap without being identical.

## Distinctive meaning

The same spelling 目下 has a separate reading **もっか**, meaning "at present; now":
目下検討中です (もっかけんとうちゅうです), "It is currently under consideration."
That is a different dictionary entry, not another meaning pronounced めした.
The pinned study-list row combines めした with the English gloss "at present,now";
this card follows its めした reading and entry 1535320 rather than teaching the mismatched gloss.
The hierarchical wording can sound blunt when used directly about someone.
