# 瓶詰 (びんづめ) - bottling; bottled

## Meaning

瓶詰 means bottling, and 瓶詰する means putting something into
bottles or jars. **瓶詰め** is another dictionary spelling.
瓶詰の before a noun means bottled or packed in a jar.

- ジャムを瓶詰めにしました。 (ジャムをびんづめにしました。) - I put the jam into jars.
- 瓶詰のジャムを買いました。 (びんづめのジャムをかいました。) - I bought some jarred jam.

The first uses Xを瓶詰めにする. The transitive する verb also allows
Xを瓶詰めする. Neither form means manufacturing the empty glass
containers.

## How the kanji combine

[瓶](../kanji/瓶.md) names the container, and
[詰](../kanji/詰.md) contributes packing or filling, as in
[詰める](詰める.md) (つめる). The container specifies what the
contents are packed into.
This is a mixed on-plus-kun formation: ビン + づめ, with voicing
of つめ. The app groups on-first mixed compounds under `kango`;
this is not an all-on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[缶詰](缶詰.md) (かんづめ) concerns canned contents or canning,
with a different kind of container. [瓶](瓶.md) (びん) names the
bottle or jar itself. Bottling can be part of
[保存](保存.md) (ほぞん), preservation, but that word does not
specify a container.

## Distinctive meaning

瓶詰 may concern jars as well as narrow-necked bottles.
It does not by itself guarantee sterilization, a vacuum seal, or a
particular shelf life. Learn the voiced ending:
びんづめ, not びんつめ.
