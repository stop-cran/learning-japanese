# 組み立てる (くみたてる) - assemble; put together

## Meaning

組み立てる means assemble parts into a whole, construct something, or put together the structure of a story or similar work. It is transitive: what is assembled takes を.

- 説明書を見ながら棚を組み立てました。 (せつめいしょをみながらたなをくみたてました。) - I assembled the shelving unit while looking at the instructions.
- 話の筋を組み立てます。 (はなしのすじをくみたてます。) - I work out the structure of the story.

The ichidan forms include 組み立てます (くみたてます), 組み立てて (くみたてて), 組み立てない (くみたてない).

## How the kanji combine

[組](../kanji/組.md) contributes assembling or joining; [立](../kanji/立.md) contributes setting up. The native compound combines 組み with 立てる. The result need not be a physically upright object.

組立てる is another dictionary spelling. The full-okurigana headword makes the two verb parts easy to recognize.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[組む](組む.md) (くむ) also means assemble, as well as team up and other uses. [作る](作る.md) (つくる) is the broader make verb.

[建てる](建てる.md) (たてる) is useful for constructing a building. 組み立てる emphasizes how parts form a whole, whether a piece of furniture or an account of events.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional grammar use is 文を組み立てる (ぶんをくみたてる), form a sentence. The common idea is constructing an organized whole, not merely collecting unrelated pieces or making two things touch.
