# 水道 (すいどう) — water supply, tap water

## Meaning

水道 is the water supply system and the tap water that comes from it: 水道水 すいどうすい (tap water), 水道代 すいどうだい (water bill), 水道が止まる (the water supply stops), 水道の水.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md) (water) + [道](../kanji/道.md) (way, road): "a way for water", that is, pipes. Both are on readings (スイ + ドウ); this is the on reading ドウ of 道 as in 柔道.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 蛇口 じゃぐち (tap, faucet), 下水道 げすいどう (sewer), 上水道 じょうすいどう (water supply).
- Compare: 水 みず (water) is the substance; 水道 is the system.

## Distinctive meaning

水道 is the standard word for "tap water" and the plumbing; do not use it for natural water like a river or the sea.
