# いつも — always; usually

## Meaning

いつも describes what happens consistently or habitually: "always," "all the time," or, in everyday routines, "usually." It normally goes directly with the predicate, without に.

- いつも七時に起きます。 (いつもしちじにおきます。) — I usually get up at seven.
- いつもありがとうございます。 (いつもありがとうございます。) — Thank you for everything you do.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is kana. 何時も is a dictionary-listed kanji spelling, but learn いつも as a whole expression; it is not the question 何時 (なんじ), "what time?"

## Synonyms and antonyms

毎日 (まいにち) specifies "every day." 時々 (ときどき) means "sometimes," and たいてい means "usually; in most cases."

## Distinctive meaning

いつもの + noun means "the usual": いつもの席 (いつものせき), "the usual seat." With a negative predicate, いつも describes a recurring negative situation; placement and context matter. For an unambiguous "never," expressions such as 一度も〜ない (いちども〜ない), "not even once," are often clearer.
