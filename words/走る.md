# 走る (はしる) — run

## Meaning

走る means to run. It can describe a person's movement or a vehicle traveling along a road or track.

- 毎朝、公園で走ります。（まいあさ、こうえんではしります。） — I run in the park every morning.
- 電車が走っています。（でんしゃがはしっています。） — A train is moving along.

## How the kanji combine

This is one native verb, not a compound. [走](../kanji/走.md) is read はし, followed by the okurigana る.
Despite the i-sound before る, it is a **godan** verb: 走ります（はしります）, 走らない（はしらない）, 走って（はしって）.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[歩く](歩く.md) あるく means "walk," a different way of moving rather than a strict opposite.
[止まる](止まる.md) とまる, "stop," provides the practical contrast to continued motion.
走行（そうこう） is a more technical noun for a vehicle's running or travel.

## Distinctive meaning

走る does not necessarily mean that someone operates a vehicle: 電車が走る describes the train moving.
For driving or operating a vehicle, compare [運転](運転.md) うんてん.
In 道を走る（みちをはしる）, "run along a road," を marks the route; it does not make 走る a transitive verb.
