# 接近 (せっきん) - approach; drawing close

## Meaning

接近 is approaching or drawing close: physically, in age or level of skill,
or in relations between people. 接近する is intransitive: what approaches
takes が, and the person or thing approached can take に.

- 台風が日本に接近しています。 (たいふうがにほんにせっきんしています。) - A typhoon is approaching Japan.
- 二人の実力は接近しています。 (ふたりのじつりょくはせっきんしています。) - The two people's ability levels are close to each other.

彼に接近する (かれにせっきんする) can mean becoming close or friendly with him;
the surrounding context determines the relationship.

## How the kanji combine

[接](../kanji/接.md) contributes coming close or into contact, and [近](../kanji/近.md)
contributes nearness. Together the related ideas emphasize closing distance.
セツ + キン becomes セッキン: learn the small っ rather than せつきん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

近付く (ちかづく), also written 近づく, is a close native-verb comparison.
[近い](近い.md) (ちかい) describes nearness.
[接する](接する.md) (せっする) can mean touching, bordering on, or dealing with;
it has additional uses that 接近 does not automatically share.

## Distinctive meaning

Approaching does not entail making physical contact. Nearness in ability does
not mean the people are standing together, and becoming friendly does not
necessarily mean a romantic relationship. Learn which kind of distance the
sentence is describing.
