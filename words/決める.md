# 決める (きめる) — decide; settle

## Meaning

決める means to decide, choose, or settle something.
As a transitive verb, it takes the matter being decided with を. An option settled on can take に.

- 旅行の日を決めました。 (りょこうのひをきめました。) — We decided on the day for the trip.
- 昼ご飯はカレーに決めました。 (ひるごはんはカレーにきめました。) — I settled on curry for lunch.

This is an ichidan verb: change the final る to make 決めます (きめます), 決めた (きめた), or 決めない (きめない).

## How the kanji combine

[決](../kanji/決.md) contributes deciding or settling and is read き in this word.
The kana める complete the written verb.
The source spelling boundary き.める is not the conjugation boundary: the stem used with ます is 決め, not 決 alone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

決まる (きまる) is the intransitive counterpart:
旅行の日が決まりました (りょこうのひがきまりました), "The day for the trip has been decided."
That sentence focuses on the settled result rather than who made the decision.
[選ぶ](選ぶ.md) (えらぶ), "choose; select," overlaps when selecting an option, but 決める also covers fixing an arrangement.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 日を決める (ひをきめる), "decide on a day," with 日が決まる (ひがきまる), "the day is decided."
The を/が difference reflects the different verbs, not a freely interchangeable particle choice.
In the lunch example, に identifies the chosen answer: curry.
