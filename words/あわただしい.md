# あわただしい - hectic; hurried

## Meaning

あわただしい describes hectic, rushed activity or a period with little time to
settle down. It is an i-adjective: あわただしかった, "was hectic";
あわただしく modifies an action.

- 出発前は、あわただしい時間を過ごしました。 (しゅっぱつまえは、あわただしいじかんをすごしました。) - I spent a hectic period before departure.
- 朝からあわただしく準備をしています。 (あさからあわただしくじゅんびをしています。) - I have been rushing around getting ready since morning.

It can also describe rapid or sudden developments, as in 情勢があわただしく変わる
(じょうせいがあわただしくかわる), "the situation changes rapidly."

## How the kanji combine

The spellings 慌ただしい and 慌しい both have the reading あわただしい.
遽しい is recorded as rare. Recognizing the spellings is not a reason to
replace the source's kana form or treat it as an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[忙しい](忙しい.md) (いそがしい), "busy," overlaps, but does not necessarily convey
the same rushed atmosphere. [ばたばた](ばたばた.md) can depict bustling activity.
落ち着く (おちつく), "settle down," offers a useful contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

[あわてる](あわてる.md) describes becoming flustered or hurrying. あわただしい
describes a quality of the activity or circumstances. Neither expression means
that someone has failed to understand an explanation merely because the source
gloss contains English "confused."
