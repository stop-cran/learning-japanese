# 特急 (とっきゅう) — limited express

## Meaning

特急 is a **limited-express train or service**, typically making relatively few stops. The exact service class depends on the railway operator.

この特急は次の駅に止まりますか。 (このとっきゅうはつぎのえきにとまりますか。) — Does this limited express stop at the next station?

Some 特急 services require a separate limited-express ticket; other operators use the name for limited-stop services without that requirement. Check the particular service rather than inferring the fare from the name alone.

## How the kanji combine

[特](../kanji/特.md) contributes “special,” and [急](../kanji/急.md) contributes “rapid.” 特急 abbreviates 特別急行 (とくべつきゅうこう). The sound changes from トク + キュウ to とっきゅう, with small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

急行 (きゅうこう) is an express service. 各駅停車 (かくえきていしゃ) stops at every station. 新幹線 (しんかんせん) names a high-speed railway system, not simply any 特急.

## Distinctive meaning

“Faster than an express” is a useful rough description, not an unconditional speed guarantee. Stop patterns, routes, and operators matter. Learn 特急 as a service label and check whether it serves your destination.
