# 直接 (ちょくせつ) - directly; firsthand

## Meaning

直接 means "directly," "in person," or "firsthand," depending on context.
It can modify a verb directly, without に. Before a noun, 直接の is a useful pattern.

- 分からないことは先生に直接聞いてください。 (わからないことはせんせいにちょくせつきいてください。) - Ask the teacher directly about anything you do not understand.
- これは事故の直接の原因ではありません。 (これはじこのちょくせつのげんいんではありません。) - This is not the direct cause of the accident.

In the first sentence, the teacher is the person approached, without another person relaying the question.
In the second, the issue is the relationship between a cause and an event.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) contributes straightness or directness, and 接 contributes touching or connection.
Together they suggest connection without an intervening link.
The on readings are チョク + セツ; preserve the small ょ in ちょく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

間接 (かんせつ), "indirect," is the paired contrast.
[自分](自分.md) (じぶん), "oneself," can occur in 自分で聞く, but doing something oneself
does not express every sense of directness.
[経由](経由.md) (けいゆ), "via," can identify an intermediate route instead.

## Distinctive meaning

English "immediate" can refer to speed or lack of delay, but 直接 need not mean "right now."
You can arrange to ask someone directly tomorrow. Likewise, a direct question may be made by phone;
whether physical presence is intended comes from the situation, not from the word alone.
