# 幼い (おさない) - very young; immature

## Meaning

幼い is an i-adjective describing someone very young, or something immature in a childlike way. Before a noun, attach it directly: 幼い子供 (おさないこども), "a young child."

- 幼いころ、この町に住んでいました。 (おさないころ、このまちにすんでいました。) - I lived in this town when I was little.
- 彼の考え方はまだ幼いです。 (かれのかんがえかたはまだおさないです。) - His way of thinking is still immature.

The first sentence concerns age; the second evaluates maturity, not the person's date of birth. Useful forms include 幼かった, "was very young," and 幼くない, "not immature/very young."

## How the kanji combine

幼 carries the idea of early childhood or immaturity. The character writes おさな here, with final い marking the adjective form. It is a native word, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[若い](若い.md) (わかい) means young more broadly and can describe an adult. [小さい](小さい.md) (ちいさい) concerns small size, though context can also connect it with childhood. Neither automatically gives the immaturity judgment in the second example.

## Distinctive meaning

幼い can be affectionate or descriptive when speaking of childhood. Applied to an adult's thinking or behavior, it can sound critical. The noun it modifies and the situation determine whether the focus is tender age, youthful appearance, or insufficient maturity.
