# 亥 (い) — Boar zodiac sign

## Meaning

亥 is the **Boar sign**, the twelfth and last of the twelve zodiac signs.
English descriptions of the cycle may also call it the Pig.
Here the word identifies a sign, not an animal standing in front of you.

- 私の干支は亥です。 (わたしのえとはいです。) — My zodiac sign is the Boar.
- 十二支の最後は亥です。 (じゅうにしのさいごはいです。) — The last of the twelve zodiac signs is the Boar.

十二支 (じゅうにし) names the twelve-sign set. In the first example,
干支 (えと) has its everyday twelve-sign zodiac sense. 干支 can also mean
the sixty-term calendar cycle that combines stems and branches;
that is not simply another name for a list of twelve signs.

## How the kanji combine

[亥](../kanji/亥.md) writes the whole native sign noun い. There is no
second character to divide off. Its on-reading ガイ belongs to compounds
such as 辛亥 (しんがい), the Metal Boar term of the sixty-term cycle,
not the standalone noun in the examples.

Recognize 亥年 (いどし), **year of the Boar**: the sign modifies
[年](../kanji/年.md), year. Learn the complete reading いどし here;
the separate noun [年](年.md) has the primary reading とし.
Daijisen also records 亥年 for a person born in that year.
The year can also be called いのしし年: zodiac expressions
can name the sign by its associated animal. This does not make い a
general word for a living wild boar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[猪](猪.md) いのしし names a living wild boar; [豚](豚.md) ぶた names
a domestic pig and can also mean pork. These animal nouns are not
unrestricted substitutes for the sign noun 亥. A zodiac sign has no
useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the sign word when identifying a place in the zodiac cycle.
Do not replace いのしし with い when describing a wild animal you saw.
Likewise, a Boar sign does not specify that a person is male:
English *boar* has animal senses that this Japanese noun does not share.

Older texts also use 亥 for an hour around 10 p.m., a direction
approximately north-northwest, or the tenth lunar month. These historical
senses are separate from everyday sign identification. Dictionary hour
conventions include both 9–11 p.m. and 10 p.m.–midnight; there is no
single conversion asserted here. The lunar month is not simply October.
For ordinary clock-time discussion, [時刻](時刻.md) じこく means time of day.
