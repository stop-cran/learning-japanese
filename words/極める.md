# 極める (きわめる) - reach an extreme; take to the limit

## Meaning

極める describes taking something to an extreme or reaching its highest
degree or limit. It is a transitive ichidan verb; a useful pattern is
**noun を極める**.

- その王朝は栄華を極めました。 (そのおうちょうはえいがをきわめました。) - That dynasty reached the height of splendor.
- 会場は混乱を極めていました。 (かいじょうはこんらんをきわめていました。) - The venue was in utter chaos.

The first example uses the dictionary-attested expression 栄華を極める.
栄華 (えいが) means prosperity or splendor; an extreme need not be bad.
The second example presents confusion as being at an extreme degree.

Forms include 極めます (きわめます), 極めて (きわめて), and
極めない (きわめない).

## How the kanji combine

[極](../kanji/極.md) contributes an extreme or limit and supplies きわ;
める is okurigana. The conjugation stem is 極め (きわめ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

極まる (きわまる) is the intransitive counterpart for reaching an extreme:
the situation itself is the subject. It is godan, with 極まって
(きわまって), not 極めて.

[達する](達する.md) (たっする) and [至る](至る.md) (いたる) can
describe reaching a level or stage, marked with に. Compare 限界に達する
(げんかいにたっする), reach the limit, with 栄華を極める, reach the height
of splendor. Shared reaching meanings do not make the particle patterns
interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

This article teaches きわめる in the extreme/peak senses. The identically
written 極める きめる belongs to a different entry, also spelled 決める,
meaning decide or settle. Do not read this article's examples as きめる.

The peak or extreme is important here. Simply reaching an ordinary count or
arriving at a station does not by itself call for 極める. Nor should the
transitive construction be confused with the separate usually-kana adverb
[極](極.md) (ごく), very or extremely.
