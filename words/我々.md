# 我々 (われわれ) - we; our group

## Meaning

我々 is a first-person plural pronoun, "we." Particles identify its role: 我々は for a topic, 我々に for a recipient or affected group, and 我々の for possession or association.

- 我々はこの計画を支持します。 (われわれはこのけいかくをしじします。) - We support this plan.
- これは我々の責任です。 (これはわれわれのせきにんです。) - This is our responsibility.

The group includes the speaker. Whether it also includes the listener depends on whom the speaker is speaking for.

## How the kanji combine

我 carries the native reading われ, referring to the self. 々 repeats the preceding kanji, giving われわれ. It is a repetition mark, not a separate kanji with an independent meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

私たち (わたしたち), formed from [私](私.md) and たち, is another way to say "we." [彼ら](彼ら.md) (かれら), "they," shifts the reference to another group rather than serving as an ordinary antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

我々 can sound collective and assertive, making it useful when presenting a group's position. It is not restricted to a particular speaker gender or to government statements. In an intimate conversation, 私たち may better fit the tone. As with other Japanese pronouns, repeated use is unnecessary when the group is already clear.
