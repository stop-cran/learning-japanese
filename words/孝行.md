# 孝行 (こうこう) - filial piety

## Meaning

孝行 means caring for and showing devotion to one's parents, often translated
"filial piety." It is used as a noun, with する, and as a な-modifier;
filial care is not merely obedience.
親に孝行する (おやにこうこうする) means "be good to one's parents";
に identifies the recipients of the care.

- 両親が元気なうちに孝行したいです。 (りょうしんがげんきなうちにこうこうしたいです。) - I want to be good to my parents while they are still well.
- 週末に実家を訪ねて、親孝行をしました。 (しゅうまつにじっかをたずねて、おやこうこうをしました。) - I visited my family home at the weekend and did something for my parents.

孝行な子 (こうこうなこ), "a child who is good to their parents," illustrates
the な-modifier use.

## How the kanji combine

孝 contributes filial devotion, and [行](../kanji/行.md) contributes conduct
or carrying something out. Together they concern conduct expressing care
for parents. The on readings コウ + コウ give こうこう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

親孝行 (おやこうこう) makes the parent relationship explicit.
親不孝 (おやふこう), "being unfilial," provides a contrast.
[愛情](愛情.md) (あいじょう), "affection," is broader: a feeling alone does
not specify how someone treats their parents.

## Distinctive meaning

孝行 can concern everyday attention and practical help, not only money or
an exceptional sacrifice. The examples illustrate ways to show care;
the word does not prescribe one universally required family arrangement.
This card focuses on the parent-directed sense.
