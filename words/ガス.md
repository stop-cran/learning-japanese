# ガス (ガス) — gas

## Meaning

ガス means gas, including gaseous fuel used for cooking or heating and a substance in the gaseous state. It also has a dictionary-listed gasoline/petrol sense, but ガソリン is the clearer ordinary word when asking about car fuel.

このアパートはガスを使っています。 (このアパートはガスをつかっています。) — This apartment uses gas.

ガス代が高くなりました。 (ガスだいがたかくなりました。) — The gas bill has gone up.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is katakana. JMdict identifies Dutch and English source connections. 瓦斯 is a rare ateji spelling: the characters are not a literal definition of the substance.

## Synonyms and antonyms

気体 (きたい) is the scientific category "gas," contrasted with 液体 (えきたい), "liquid," and 固体 (こたい), "solid." 電気 (でんき), "electricity," is an alternative household energy source, not an antonym of the state of matter.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not transfer every English use of "gas" without context. ガス also appears for dense fog, flatulence, or a gas cooker in particular uses. In ガス代, the reference is the household gas service, not automatically gasoline for a car.
