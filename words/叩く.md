# 叩く (たたく) - hit; tap; clap

## Meaning

叩く is a transitive godan verb covering striking, tapping, knocking, or beating.
The object and manner tell you the intended action: ドアを叩く, "knock on a door";
手を叩く (てをたたく), "clap one's hands"; 太鼓を叩く (たいこをたたく), "play a drum."

- 入る前に、ドアを軽く叩きました。 (はいるまえに、ドアをかるくたたきました。) - I knocked lightly on the door before entering.
- 子どもたちは歌に合わせて手を叩きました。 (こどもたちはうたにあわせててをたたきました。) - The children clapped along with the song.

The て-form is 叩いて (たたいて), the past 叩いた, and the negative 叩かない.

## How the kanji combine

叩 represents the striking action in this native verb.
The kana く is the dictionary-form ending; there is no second kanji contributing a separate word.
Both gentle tapping and stronger blows fall within the basic range.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[打つ](打つ.md) (うつ) overlaps with hitting, but each verb has its own established objects and expressions.
[触る](触る.md) (さわる), "touch," does not necessarily involve a tap or blow.
[批判](批判.md) (ひはん), "criticism," overlaps with figurative 叩く.

## Distinctive meaning

Internet or media contexts can use 叩く for attacking or harshly criticizing someone, without physical contact.
Do not translate every occurrence as violence.
Likewise, "dust" in a broad gloss can concern beating dust out of something, not every method of cleaning a surface.
