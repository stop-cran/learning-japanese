# 二 (に) - two

## Meaning

二 is the number two. The reading に is used in ordinary numerical counting and many number-plus-counter combinations.

- りんごを二個買いました。 (りんごをにこかいました。) - I bought two apples.

Here 個 (こ) is the counter. 二時 (にじ) means "two o'clock," and 二月 (にがつ) means "February."

## How the kanji combine

The single character [二](../kanji/二.md) represents two and uses the on reading ニ here. The digit 2 is another normal way to write the number.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一 (いち) and 三 (さん) are the adjacent numbers. 二つ (ふたつ), "two things," belongs to the native counting series; it is not read につ.

## Distinctive meaning

Knowing the number does not guarantee the pronunciation of every counter phrase. 二人 is ふたり, "two people," and 二日 is ふつか, "the second day of the month; two days." Learn these whole combinations rather than applying に mechanically.
