# 晴れ (はれ) — clear weather

## Meaning

晴れ means clear or fine weather. It is a noun, commonly used to state the weather or label a forecast.

- 今日は晴れです。  
  きょうははれです。  
  "The weather is clear today."
- 明日は晴れの予報です。  
  あしたははれのよほうです。  
  "The forecast for tomorrow is clear weather."

Use の when this noun modifies another noun, as in the second example; do not treat it as an い-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[晴](../kanji/晴.md) relates to clear skies. 晴れ is read はれ and is related to 晴れる (はれる), "clear up". The noun names the weather condition, while the verb can describe a change in the sky.

## Synonyms and antonyms

曇り (くもり), "cloudy weather", and 雨 (あめ), "rain", are useful forecast contrasts. 晴天 (せいてん) is another noun for clear weather, often used in more formal descriptions.

## Distinctive meaning

晴れ does not mean "hot". A cold winter day can have clear weather. Temperature and sky conditions require separate descriptions.
