# じゃ (じゃ) — well then

## Meaning

じゃ is a conversational "then; well then," used to move to a decision, next action, or farewell. The longer form じゃあ has the same basic use.

じゃ、明日会いましょう。（じゃ、あしたあいましょう。） — Well then, let's meet tomorrow.

じゃ、私は帰ります。（じゃ、わたしはかえります。） — In that case, I will go home.

## How the kanji combine

There are no kanji. This use developed as a shortened form of では. The spelling じゃ combines じ with small ゃ; it is not pronounced じや.

## Synonyms and antonyms

では is more suitable in formal speech. それでは also introduces a next step. These are register alternatives rather than opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Sentence-initial じゃ connects with the preceding situation. The same sound also occurs inside じゃない, a conversational form of ではない; that negative construction is not the "well then" use taught here.
