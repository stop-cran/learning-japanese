# 夜 (よる) — night

## Meaning

夜 means night and also covers many situations translated as "evening" in English.

- 夜に本を読みます。 (よるにほんをよみます。) — I read books at night.
- この店は夜も開いています。 (このみせはよるもあいています。) — This shop is open at night too.

**夜 can also be read よ**, with the same night/evening meaning. Learn it in expressions rather
than replacing every よる mechanically:

- 夜が明けました。 (よがあけました。) — Day broke.

Here よ is the noun "night"; the whole expression 夜が明ける describes night giving way to daylight.
The primary reading よる remains the starting point for the examples above.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun. [夜](../kanji/夜.md) represents the whole word よる; it has no okurigana and no compound parts.

Other words use different readings: 今夜 こんや means "tonight," and 夜中 よなか means "the middle of the night." Those readings do not replace よる indiscriminately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [夕方](夕方.md) ゆうがた emphasizes late afternoon or early evening, near the end of daylight.
- 夜間 やかん means nighttime and is common in labels or descriptions of operating hours.
- [昼](昼.md) ひる, in its "daytime" sense, is the natural contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

夜 does not mean only midnight or the time after going to bed. A meal, lesson or shop visit can take place at 夜 while people are still active. 夜中 narrows the reference to the middle of the night.
