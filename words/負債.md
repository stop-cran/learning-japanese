# 負債 (ふさい) — debt; liabilities

## Meaning

負債 is what is owed: a debt or liabilities.

- その会社は多額の負債を抱えています。 (そのかいしゃはたがくのふさいをかかえています。) — That company has a large amount of debt.
- 負債を返すのに十年かかりました。 (ふさいをかえすのにじゅうねんかかりました。) — It took ten years to pay off the debt.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[負](../kanji/負.md) means bearing a burden, and [債](../kanji/債.md) means debt. Together, they describe a debt that one bears.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[借金](借金.md) (しゃっきん) overlaps in meaning; its JMdict glosses also include “loan” and “borrowing money” (entry 1323940). 債務 さいむ, which JMdict glosses as debt, liabilities, or an obligation to a person or party (entry 1292110), is a related term.

## Distinctive meaning

負債 names what is owed, not the act of borrowing; [借りる](借りる.md) (かりる) is the verb for borrowing.
