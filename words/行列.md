# 行列 (ぎょうれつ) - queue; procession; matrix

## Meaning

行列 is a line, queue, or procession of people or things. In mathematics, it also means a matrix: a rectangular arrangement of numbers or symbols.

- 店の前に長い行列ができています。
  (みせのまえにながいぎょうれつができています。) - A long queue has formed in front of the shop.
- これは三行二列の行列です。
  (これはさんぎょうにれつのぎょうれつです。) - This is a matrix with three rows and two columns.

行列 is a noun in both examples. The queuing sense can also take する. In the mathematical expression, 行 counts horizontal rows and 列 vertical columns.

## How the kanji combine

[行](../kanji/行.md) contributes proceeding or a row, and [列](../kanji/列.md) a row or line. The complete reading is ギョウ + レツ. The two uses must be learned from context, not inferred from a single English meaning of either character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[列](列.md) (れつ) also names a row, line, or queue. [並ぶ](並ぶ.md) (ならぶ) describes lining up or standing in a line. パレード names a parade, and マトリックス is another word for a matrix.

## Distinctive meaning

A queue can be waiting in place; a procession can be moving. Neither interpretation turns a mathematical 行列 into a group of people, and the mathematical sense does not mean just one column. Context such as a shop entrance or a calculation establishes which sense is intended.
