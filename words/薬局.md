# 薬局 (やっきょく) — pharmacy

## Meaning

薬局 means a pharmacy or chemist's shop: a place associated with obtaining medicines.
It is a noun, so a destination can be expressed with 薬局に行く (やっきょくにいく), "go to a pharmacy."

- 薬局で薬を買いました。 (やっきょくでくすりをかいました。) — I bought medicine at a pharmacy.
- 駅の近くに薬局があります。 (えきのちかくにやっきょくがあります。) — There is a pharmacy near the station.

## How the kanji combine

[薬](../kanji/薬.md) means medicine, and [局](../kanji/局.md) supplies the office or service-establishment element.
Medicine specifies what kind of establishment is meant.
The on readings ヤク + キョク combine as やっきょく: the first ending changes to the small っ before きょ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[病院](病院.md) (びょういん) is a hospital or medical institution, not another name for a pharmacy.
Both belong to health-care vocabulary, but the pharmacy example concerns obtaining medicine rather than having a medical consultation.
There is no useful opposite place-name for 薬局.

## Distinctive meaning

The word identifies a pharmacy; it does not tell you which medicines are available or whether a particular purchase requires a prescription.
Learn やっきょく as a complete pronunciation, keeping both the small っ and the small ょ.
Do not pronounce it やくきょく.
