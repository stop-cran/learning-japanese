# 下げる (さげる) - lower; hang

## Meaning

下げる means to lower something, hang it down, or move it back.
It is transitive: the object moved takes を.

- テレビの音を下げてください。 (テレビのおとをさげてください。) - Please turn down the television's volume.
- 首からカメラを下げています。 (くびからカメラをさげています。) - I have a camera hanging around my neck.
- 椅子を少し後ろに下げました。 (いすをすこしうしろにさげました。) - I moved the chair back a little.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) contributes downward movement, followed by げる as okurigana.
This is an ichidan verb: 下げます, 下げない, 下げて. Its base is さげ-.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上げる (あげる), "raise," contrasts with lowering. 下がる (さがる) is the
intransitive partner: 値段が下がる (ねだんがさがる), "the price goes down."
Neither comparison replaces the hanging or moving-back senses automatically.

## Distinctive meaning

The object determines the natural English verb. 食器を下げる
(しょっきをさげる) means "clear away the dishes," not necessarily physically
move them to a lower shelf. Learn these object-and-verb combinations together.
