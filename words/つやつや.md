# つやつや — lustrous and glossy

## Meaning

A smooth-looking, lustrous surface, often suggesting healthy hair or skin, or appetizingly glossy food.

- 猫の毛がつやつやしている。 — The cat's fur is glossy.
- 炊きたて（たきたて）のご飯がつやつやと光っている。 — The freshly cooked rice has a glossy sheen.

## How the kanji combine

A native repetition of つや, “luster.” The dictionary also records 艶々 and 艶艶; this card uses kana. Repetition intensifies the descriptive impression, not the number of objects.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[てかてか](てかてか.md) can suggest oiliness or excessive shine. [ぴかぴか](ぴかぴか.md) highlights bright shine, cleanliness, or newness. [ぱさぱさ](ぱさぱさ.md) is a useful contrast for dry-looking hair.

## Distinctive meaning

つやつやした髪（かみ）, つやつやの髪, and つやつやしている are natural patterns. The word foregrounds visible luster, not necessarily slipperiness when touched. Its often favorable tone differs from greasy-looking てかてか, but that difference is contextual rather than an absolute good/bad division.
