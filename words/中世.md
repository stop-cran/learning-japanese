# 中世 (ちゅうせい) - Middle Ages; medieval period

## Meaning

中世 is the Middle Ages or medieval period.
In Japanese history, it especially concerns the Kamakura and
Muromachi periods. It is a noun: 中世の日本
(ちゅうせいのにほん), "medieval Japan."

- 大学で日本の中世史を学んでいます。 (だいがくでにほんのちゅうせいしをまなんでいます。) - I am studying medieval Japanese history at university.
- この本は中世の町の生活を描いています。 (このほんはちゅうせいのまちのせいかつをえがいています。) - This book depicts life in a medieval town.

中世史 (ちゅうせいし) means medieval history.
Use の when connecting the era noun to a following noun in
the ordinary pattern 中世の町.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) contributes middle, and [世](../kanji/世.md)
an age or era. Their modifier-plus-head relationship suggests
a middle age in a historical framework. Read チュウ + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[近代](近代.md) (きんだい) names a modern era, while
[現代](現代.md) (げんだい) concerns the contemporary age.
These are different period labels, not freely interchangeable
ways of saying [時代](時代.md) (じだい), "era."

## Distinctive meaning

The label does not establish one universal starting and ending year
for every country or field. A discussion of medieval Europe uses
a different historical framework from medieval Japan.
Specify the country and subject rather than transferring the dates
of one framework mechanically into another.
