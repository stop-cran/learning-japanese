# 山岳 (さんがく) — mountains; mountain range

## Meaning

山岳 refers to mountains, especially high, rugged mountains considered together. It does not name just the summit of a single peak.

- この地域には険しい山岳が連なっている。 (このちいきにはけわしいさんがくがつらなっている。) — Rugged mountains stretch across this region.
- 山岳地帯では、春でも雪が残る。 (さんがくちたいでは、はるでもゆきがのこる。) — In the mountainous region, snow remains even in spring.

## How the kanji combine

[山](../kanji/山.md) (さん) and [岳](../kanji/岳.md) (がく) both concern mountains. 山岳地帯 (さんがくちたい) names a mountainous area.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[山](山.md) (やま) is the general word for a mountain. [頂上](頂上.md) (ちょうじょう) identifies the summit, while [麓](麓.md) (ふもと) identifies the foot.

## Distinctive meaning

山岳 describes the terrain; [登山](登山.md) (とざん) describes the activity of climbing a mountain. Snow can remain in 山岳地帯 even when no one is climbing.
