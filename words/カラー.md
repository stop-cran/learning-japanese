# カラー (カラー) - color; character; collar

## Meaning

カラー means color. It can also describe the distinctive character or atmosphere of a group, work, or style. A related する use means coloring or dyeing one's hair.

- この写真はカラーです。 (このしゃしんはカラーです。) - This photograph is in color.
- この雑誌には独自のカラーがあります。 (このざっしにはどくじのカラーがあります。) - This magazine has a distinctive character of its own.
- 美容院で髪をカラーしました。 (びよういんでかみをカラーしました。) - I had my hair colored at the salon.

A separate clothing word with the same spelling means **collar**. Context decides: シャツのカラー can refer to a shirt's color or its collar. 襟 (えり) also means "collar."

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana word with no kanji decomposition. English "color" and "collar" converge on the same Japanese form カラー; their different English pronunciations do not supply different Japanese readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[色](色.md) (いろ) is the everyday color noun. [性格](性格.md) (せいかく) and [特徴](特徴.md) (とくちょう) overlap with character or characteristic, while [雰囲気](雰囲気.md) (ふんいき) overlaps with atmosphere. These are not interchangeable in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

モノクロ, "monochrome," contrasts with カラー for a photograph, not with the clothing collar. カラーする in the salon example concerns hair dyeing; it is not a universal way to say "color anything." The source gloss bundles color and collar, but the dictionary indexes them separately.
