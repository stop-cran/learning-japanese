# キロメートル (キロメートル) — kilometer

## Meaning

キロメートル is a kilometer, a unit of distance equal to 1,000 meters. It follows a number directly.

駅まで二キロメートルあります。（えきまでにキロメートルあります。） — It is two kilometers to the station.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana unit name combines キロ, "thousand" in metric units, with メートル, "meter." JMdict records the word as a loan from French *kilomètre*. Rare kanji spellings are not needed in ordinary use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The symbol km represents the same unit. メートル is the smaller base unit; キログラム is a unit of mass, not distance.

## Distinctive meaning

In conversation キロ often shortens キロメートル when the topic is distance. It can instead mean kilograms when discussing weight, so do not interpret キロ without context. The Japanese unit name stays the same after one, two, or more.
