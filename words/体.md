# 体 (からだ) — body

## Meaning

体 means the body. Depending on the sentence, it can emphasize someone's physical build or health rather than the body as an object.

- 運動の後で体を洗います。  
  うんどうのあとでからだをあらいます。  
  "I wash my body after exercising."
- 体に気をつけてください。  
  からだにきをつけてください。  
  "Please take care of your health."

## How the kanji combine

[体](../kanji/体.md) has the native reading からだ as this standalone noun. In compounds it can have a different reading, as in 体育 (たいいく), "physical education". 身体 is another spelling that can be read からだ, but also has the separate reading しんたい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

身体 (しんたい) is a more formal word for the body. 心 (こころ), "mind; heart", is a useful contrast when discussing physical and mental well-being, not a literal antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn expressions as complete units: 体にいい (からだにいい) means "good for your health". Here に marks what benefits; 体 itself has not become an adjective.
