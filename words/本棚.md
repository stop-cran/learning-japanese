# 本棚 (ほんだな) - bookshelf; bookcase

## Meaning

本棚 is a bookshelf or bookcase: shelving intended to hold books.
The word can describe a complete unit, not only one horizontal board.

- 読んだ本を本棚に戻しました。 (よんだほんをほんだなにもどしました。) - I returned the book I had read to the bookshelf.

を marks the book moved; に marks its destination.

## How the kanji combine

[本](../kanji/本.md), "book," combines with 棚 (たな), "shelf."
The second word becomes だな through voicing. This is a mixed on-plus-kun
compound: ホン + たな, not an all-on reading. The repository classifies
on-first mixed words as `kango`.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[棚](棚.md) is the broader word for shelves or racks, regardless of what
they hold. 本箱 (ほんばこ) can also name a bookcase.
A library is a collection or institution, not a synonym for the furnishing.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese does not require a plural ending when several shelves are meant.
Do not read the written parts as ほんたな: the voiced だ is part of the
established pronunciation ほんだな.
