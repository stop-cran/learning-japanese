# 一人一人 (ひとりひとり) - each person; individually

## Meaning

一人一人 means each person individually. It can work as a noun or adverb, emphasizing the separate members of a group. Use 一人一人の before a noun and 一人一人に for "to each person."

- 一人一人の意見を聞きました。 (ひとりひとりのいけんをききました。) - I listened to each person's opinion.
- 先生は学生一人一人に声をかけました。 (せんせいはがくせいひとりひとりにこえをかけました。) - The teacher spoke to each student.

The emphasis is on including the individuals, not simply treating the class as one collective unit.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," and [人](../kanji/人.md), "person," form [一人](一人.md) (ひとり). Repeating it gives the distributive meaning "each person." Following the 一人 card, this is grouped with whole-word readings for study; do not replace ひとり with イチニン here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[皆](皆.md) and [全員](全員.md) (ぜんいん) mean everyone. 一人一人 makes individual attention or responsibility especially explicit. They are overlapping viewpoints, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

"One by one" fits when an action proceeds through people in turn. But 一人一人の意見 does not itself require a particular speaking order: it means each person's opinion. The expression concerns people; it is not the generic counting expression for each item in a pile of objects.
