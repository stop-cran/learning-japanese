# ずうずうしい - impudent; shameless

## Meaning

ずうずうしい describes someone as brazen, shameless or unreasonably forward: acting without the restraint or consideration the speaker expects. It is an i-adjective.

- 他人の家で勝手に冷蔵庫を開けるなんて、ずうずうしい。 (たにんのいえでかってにれいぞうこをあけるなんて、ずうずうしい。) - How brazen to open someone else's refrigerator without asking.
- ずうずうしい態度に腹が立ちました。 (ずうずうしいたいどにはらがたちました。) - The shameless attitude made me angry.

## How the kanji combine

The word can also be written 図々しい or 図図しい. The mark 々 repeats [図](../kanji/図.md). Digital Daijisen identifies this spelling as ateji: do not derive the personality judgment from 図's ordinary diagram meaning. The kana spelling keeps the long う sounds visible.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[厚かましい](厚かましい.md) closely overlaps in brazen or shameless behavior. [遠慮](遠慮.md), restraint or consideration, provides a useful contrast, but the appropriate degree of restraint depends on the situation.

## Distinctive meaning

This is normally criticism, not neutral praise for confidence or courage. Calling someone ずうずうしい can be harsh. In the adverbial form ずうずうしく, the final い changes to く; before a noun, use ずうずうしい directly, without な.
