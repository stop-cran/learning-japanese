# 火山 (かざん) — volcano

## Meaning

火山 is a volcano: 火山が噴火する (the volcano erupts), 活火山 かっかざん (active volcano), 火山灰 かざんばい (volcanic ash).
Japan has many volcanoes; 富士山 ふじさん is the best known.

## How the kanji combine

[火](../kanji/火.md) (fire) modifies [山](../kanji/山.md) (mountain): "a mountain of fire", a modifier-plus-noun compound. It is not a pair of equal parts like 大小 or 男女.
The readings are the on readings カ and ザン (voiced サン).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 噴火 ふんか (an eruption), 溶岩 ようがん (lava), 火口 かこう (crater).
- 休火山 きゅうかざん ("dormant volcano") and 死火山 しかざん ("extinct volcano") are older terms that the Japan Meteorological Agency no longer uses.
  It now classifies volcanoes as 活火山 (those that erupted within roughly the past 10,000 years or show active fumaroles); see the
  [JMA explanation](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/kishou/know/kazan/katsukazan_toha/katsukazan_toha.html).
- Antonym: none.

## Distinctive meaning

火山 is the geologic object. 火事 かじ is a fire (disaster), and 山火事 やまかじ is a wildfire: the same two kanji in the opposite order give
a very different word.
