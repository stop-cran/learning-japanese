# おかげ — help; thanks to

## Meaning

おかげ refers to help or a beneficial influence. おかげで introduces what made a good result possible: "thanks to."

- 先生のおかげで、合格しました。 (せんせいのおかげで、ごうかくしました。) — Thanks to my teacher, I passed.
- 早く出たおかげで、間に合いました。 (はやくでたおかげで、まにあいました。) — Because we left early, we made it in time.

Use noun + のおかげで, a plain verb or i-adjective before おかげで, and な after a na-adjective: 静かなおかげで (しずかなおかげで), "thanks to it being quiet."

## How the kanji combine

Usually written in kana, おかげ also has the spelling お陰. The initial お belongs to this established expression; do not replace it with ご.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ため can state a cause more neutrally. せいで generally assigns blame for an undesirable result.

## Distinctive meaning

おかげ normally presents the cause appreciatively, although irony is possible. おかげさまで is a conventional grateful response, as in おかげさまで元気です (おかげさまでげんきです), "I'm well, thank you."
