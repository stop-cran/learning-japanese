# しあさって - in three days' time

## Meaning

しあさって ordinarily means three days from today: the day after あさって. It can function as a time noun or adverb.

- しあさって、友達が来ます。 (しあさって、ともだちがきます。) - My friend is coming in three days.
- 会議はしあさっての午前中です。 (かいぎはしあさってのごぜんちゅうです。) - The meeting is in the morning three days from today.

In the standard sequence, today is day 0, 明日 (あした) day 1, あさって day 2, and しあさって day 3. Counting today as the first day makes it the fourth calendar day in that inclusive count, not four days from now.

## How the kanji combine

The primary spelling here is kana. 明々後日 and 明明後日 also write the whole expression; do not assign a separate invented reading to each character. JMdict additionally records the rare reading みょうみょうごにち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

三日後 (みっかご) means three days later, with the reference day supplied by context. あさって means the day after tomorrow. やのあさって has regionally variable use and is not a universally reliable replacement.

## Distinctive meaning

Regional usage can place しあさって four days from today instead. When arranging an important date, confirm the calendar date rather than assuming everyone uses the same relative-day sequence. The examples above use the standard day-3 meaning.
