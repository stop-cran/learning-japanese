# ばね (ばね) - mechanical spring; impetus

## Meaning

ばね, also written バネ, is a mechanical spring: a part that deforms and tends to return to its former shape.
The word also describes bounce in someone's legs and, figuratively, an impetus that helps someone move forward.

- このばねは弱くなっています。 (このばねはよわくなっています。) - This spring has become weak.
- 彼は足のばねが強い。 (かれはあしのばねがつよい。) - He has a powerful spring in his legs.
- 失敗をばねにして、もう一度挑戦しました。 (しっぱいをばねにして、もういちどちょうせんしました。) - I used the failure as motivation and tried again.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. Rare spellings include 発条, 弾機 and 撥条;
ばね is a special whole-word reading here, not an ordinary combination of those characters' on-readings.
The device sense also has the reading ぜんまい, but that is not a replacement reading for the two figurative uses taught above.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ねじ](ねじ.md) can mean a screw and also a clock's winding spring, so it is not limited to fasteners.
[きっかけ](きっかけ.md) overlaps with a trigger or impetus; ばね figuratively emphasizes the force used to make progress.
ぜんまい can name a coiled winding spring rather than every possible spring mechanism.

## Distinctive meaning

A mechanical spring need not be a visible spiral coil; a leaf spring also fits.
The English homonyms "spring" as the season [春](春.md) (はる) or a water source [泉](泉.md) (いずみ)
are not the mechanical meaning.
In Xをばねにする, X is turned into a driving force, not literally converted into a metal part.
