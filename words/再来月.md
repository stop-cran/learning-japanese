# 再来月 (さらいげつ) — month after next

## Meaning

再来月 is the calendar month after next. If the current month is March, 再来月 is May, not a particular day exactly two months away.

- 再来月、日本に行きます。 (さらいげつ、にほんにいきます。) — I'm going to Japan the month after next.
- 再来月の予定はまだ決まっていません。 (さらいげつのよていはまだきまっていません。) — My plans for the month after next are not decided yet.

## How the kanji combine

再, "again," extends [来](../kanji/来.md) + [月](../kanji/月.md), "coming month," by one more month. Learn the reading さらいげつ; 再 is さ here, not さい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[来月](来月.md) (らいげつ) is next month. 先月 (せんげつ) is last month, and 先々月 (せんせんげつ) is the month before last.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 再来月 directly as a time expression, normally without に. Add の when it modifies a noun, as in 再来月の予定. For a date two months after another reference point, 二か月後 (にかげつご) is more suitable.
