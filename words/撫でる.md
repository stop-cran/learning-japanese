# 撫でる (なでる) - stroke; caress

## Meaning

撫でる means to stroke, caress, pet or gently rub something, often with
the hand. It is a transitive ichidan verb; what is stroked takes を.

- 猫の背中を優しく撫でました。 (ねこのせなかをやさしくなでました。) - I gently stroked the cat's back.
- 母は子供の頭を撫でました。 (はははこどものあたまをなでました。) - The mother stroked her child's head.

Forms include 撫でます, 撫でない, 撫でて and 撫でた.
で can mark an implement or body part: 手で撫でる (てでなでる),
"stroke with one's hand."

## How the kanji combine

撫 writes な, and でる completes the native verb. There is no second
kanji word to combine with it. Kana なでる is also readable in context;
the written form identifies the stroking action.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[擦る](擦る.md) (こする) covers rubbing, scrubbing and scraping.
撫でる foregrounds a gentler stroking action rather than vigorous
scrubbing. さする, "rub; stroke," also overlaps.
[触る](触る.md) (さわる) is broader touching or handling, without
necessarily moving the hand in a stroke.

## Distinctive meaning

The word also describes smoothing or combing down hair:
髪を撫でる (かみをなでる), "smooth one's hair."
English "brush" does not mean that every use involves a brush or a
cleaning task. The phrase 胸を撫で下ろす (むねをなでおろす),
"feel relieved," uses the distinct compound 撫で下ろす;
do not assign that entire idiom to every standalone 撫でる.
