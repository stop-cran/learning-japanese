# 朝ごはん (あさごはん) -- breakfast

## Meaning

朝ごはん is breakfast, the morning meal. It names the meal, not a particular menu.

朝ごはんにパンと卵を食べました。 (あさごはんにパンとたまごをたべました。) -- I had bread and an egg for breakfast.

Use 朝ごはんを食べる for "eat breakfast." In the example, に marks the meal for which the food is eaten.

## How the kanji combine

[朝](../kanji/朝.md), read あさ, specifies the time of day; ごはん means a meal here. Although ごはん can mean cooked rice, breakfast need not include rice.

朝ご飯 is another ordinary spelling. The formation mixes native あさ with Sino-Japanese ご飯; the app's `wago` category groups this native-first mixed word. 朝御飯 is a dictionary-listed rare spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

朝食 (ちょうしょく) also means breakfast and is useful in notices and hotel information.
[昼御飯](昼御飯.md) (ひるごはん), lunch, is a different meal rather than an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate ごはん mechanically as "rice" in meal names. 朝ごはんを作る (あさごはんをつくる) means making breakfast, whatever food is served.
