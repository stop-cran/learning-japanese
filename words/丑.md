# 丑 (うし) — Ox zodiac sign

## Meaning

丑 is the **Ox sign**, the second of the twelve zodiac signs.
It identifies a place in the cycle, not a living cow or ox.

- 私の干支は丑です。 (わたしのえとはうしです。) — My zodiac sign is the Ox.
- 十二支の二番目は丑です。 (じゅうにしのにばんめはうしです。) — The second of the twelve zodiac signs is the Ox.

十二支 (じゅうにし) names the twelve-sign set. In the first example,
干支 (えと) has its everyday twelve-year zodiac sense. 干支 can also
mean the sixty-term calendar cycle combining stems and branches. The
reading かんし is documented for that sixty-term sense, not as a substitute
for えと's twelve-year zodiac sense.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [丑](../kanji/丑.md) writes the whole native sign noun
うし, with no okurigana or second character to separate.

Recognize 丑年 (うしどし), **year of the Ox**: 丑 identifies the sign
and [年](../kanji/年.md) the year. Learn the whole reading うしどし;
the separate noun [年](年.md) has the primary reading とし.
Daijisen also records 丑年 for a person born in that year.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[牛](牛.md) うし is the ordinary animal noun, also written ウシ.
It names the animal associated with the sign, but is not an unrestricted
substitute for the sign spelling 丑. The sign has no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the spelling tied to the context: 丑 for the sign, 牛 or ウシ for
the animal. Both are read うし. The sign's English name "Ox" does not
make 丑 the ordinary noun for an ox.

Older uses of 丑 include an hour around 2 a.m., a traditional direction
labelled north-northeast, and the twelfth lunar month. Dictionaries give
both 1–3 a.m. and 2–4 a.m. hour mappings, not one universal modern interval.
The direction is distinct from 丑[寅](寅.md) (うしとら), northeast.
These are not ordinary modern time or direction substitutes, and the
lunar month is not Gregorian December.
