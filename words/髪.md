# 髪 (かみ) — hair on the head

## Meaning

髪 (かみ) means the hair on someone's head.
Use it when talking about washing, cutting, length, or the appearance of that hair.

- 髪が長いです。 (かみがながいです。) — My hair is long.
- 髪を洗いました。 (かみをあらいました。) — I washed my hair.

The owner can be supplied by context; 髪 does not itself mean "my hair."

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [髪](../kanji/髪.md) writes the native noun かみ.
散髪 (さんぱつ), "a haircut," uses an altered form of the on reading ハツ.
白髪 (しらが), "white or gray hair," should be learned as a whole-word reading,
not used to invent a general reading が for 髪.

## Synonyms and antonyms

髪の毛 (かみのけ) also means head hair.
毛 (け) is broader: it can refer to hair, fur, or wool, so it does not by itself restrict the meaning to a person's head.
[紙](紙.md) (かみ), "paper," has the same kana spelling as 髪 but is a separate, unrelated word in use.

## Distinctive meaning

The head-hair restriction matters: do not call a cat's fur 髪 merely because English can call it "hair."
Use the broader 毛 where fur is intended.
髪 by itself also says nothing about color; white or gray hair is the more specific 白髪.
