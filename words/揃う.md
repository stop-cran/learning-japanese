# 揃う (そろう) - be complete; be all present; match

## Meaning

揃う describes a full set being present, people gathering, or things being
equal or matching. It is an intransitive godan verb ending in う:
揃います, 揃わない, 揃って (そろって).

- 必要な書類が揃いました。 (ひつようなしょるいがそろいました。) - All the necessary documents are now together.
- 参加者が全員揃いました。 (さんかしゃがぜんいんそろいました。) - All the participants have arrived.
- 箱の大きさが揃っています。 (はこのおおきさがそろっています。) - The boxes are uniform in size.

が marks what is complete or matching. 揃っている can describe the resulting
state, not just the moment when the last item arrives.

## How the kanji combine

揃 writes そろ; う is the dictionary-form ending. The same stem occurs in
the transitive partner [揃える](揃える.md) (そろえる).
書類が揃う describes the documents being complete; 書類を揃える describes
someone assembling the required documents.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[集まる](集まる.md) (あつまる) emphasizes gathering. 揃う can additionally
highlight everyone or everything required being present.
[合う](合う.md) (あう), "fit; match," overlaps with the matching sense.
[足りる](足りる.md) (たりる) asks whether there is enough; a sufficient
quantity need not be a complete set of particular items.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not force the full-set sense into every occurrence: 声が揃う
(こえがそろう) can mean voices being in unison. Conversely, gathering
several items does not automatically establish a complete collection.
Context identifies which completeness, gathering, or uniformity is intended.
