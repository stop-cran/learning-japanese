# べらべら — chattering freely; thin and flimsy

## Meaning

Talking a lot without restraint, sometimes indiscreetly. Another use concerns thin, flimsy material.

- 彼は会ったばかりの人に、家のことまでべらべら話した。 — He freely told someone he had just met even about his family affairs.
- この紙は薄（うす）くてべらべらだ。 — This paper is thin and flimsy.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぺらぺら](ぺらぺら.md) overlaps in chatter and thin material, and also has a familiar language-fluency use. The two are not safe opposites merely because their first consonants differ.

## Distinctive meaning

べらべら話す often criticizes saying too much or revealing things carelessly. It should not be used automatically as praise for someone's foreign-language skill. Context determines whether the speech is objectionable.
