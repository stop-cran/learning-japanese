# 香典 (こうでん) — condolence money; funeral gift

## Meaning

香典 is a condolence gift offered in connection with a funeral, usually
money. It names the offering, not the funeral ceremony or incense itself.
The dictionary definition also allows goods; "money" is a practical
translation, not an absolute restriction.

- 香典を用意しました。 (こうでんをよういしました。) — I prepared condolence money.
- 香典を渡しました。 (こうでんをわたしました。) — I handed over the condolence money.

In both examples, を marks the gift as the object. 用意する (よういする)
means "prepare"; 渡す (わたす) means "hand over."

## How the kanji combine

[香](../kanji/香.md) supplies the incense connection; [典](../kanji/典.md)
also occurs in ceremonial vocabulary. Learn this as an established
funeral-offering compound, not a freely productive formula:
典 alone does not mean "money." The on readings コウ + デン give こうでん.

The same word is also written 香奠 (こうでん). JMdict marks that alternate
spelling as rare; the flag does not make the pronunciation こうでん rare.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[贈り物](贈り物.md) (おくりもの) and [プレゼント](プレゼント.md) mean
a gift more generally, without specifying a funeral.
[寄付](寄付.md) (きふ) is a donation or contribution, not specifically
a funeral condolence gift. [お祝い](お祝い.md) (おいわい), a celebration
or congratulatory gift, concerns a happy occasion instead.
There is no single useful lexical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the whole word for the funeral-related offering. Do not translate it
as an ordinary birthday present, the funeral itself, or a stick of incense.
The word does not tell you an appropriate amount or every community's
customs; this is vocabulary guidance, not an etiquette prescription.
