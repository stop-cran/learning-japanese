# 団地 (だんち) - housing complex; planned estate

## Meaning

団地 commonly names a planned residential development or housing complex.
The focus is the grouped development, not just one room.

- 私は駅の近くの団地に住んでいます。
  (わたしはえきのちかくのだんちにすんでいます。) - I live in a housing complex near the station.
- 団地の中に公園があります。
  (だんちのなかにこうえんがあります。) - There is a park within the housing complex.

に marks where someone lives in the first example.
住宅団地 (じゅうたくだんち) explicitly identifies a residential development.

## How the kanji combine

[団](../kanji/団.md) contributes a group and
[地](../kanji/地.md) land or an area.
Together they suggest an area developed as a group.
The on readings are ダン + チ, not the トン found in 布団.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[アパート](アパート.md) names an apartment or apartment building.
[住宅](住宅.md) (じゅうたく) is housing or a residence more generally.
A 団地 may contain apartment buildings, but the words do not specify
the same unit of housing in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer ownership, a particular rent, or public management from the word alone.
The public dictionary also records grouped nonresidential developments:
工業団地 (こうぎょうだんち) is an industrial estate or park.
That broader use does not turn every isolated factory into a 団地.
