# 意志 (いし) - will; determination

## Meaning

意志 is a noun for the will or determination to do something. A plain nonpast
verb can describe its content: 続ける意志 (つづけるいし), "the will to continue."
意志が強い (いしがつよい) means strong-willed.

- 彼女には最後まで続ける意志があります。 (かのじょにはさいごまでつづけるいしがあります。) - She has the determination to continue to the end.
- 自分の意志で参加しました。 (じぶんのいしでさんかしました。) - I participated of my own free will.

In the second example, で identifies the basis for the choice, not a physical
location. 自分の意志 contrasts with being pressured into doing something.

## How the kanji combine

[意](../kanji/意.md) contributes intention or thought; 志 contributes resolve or
aspiration. The related meanings combine into directed will. The on readings
are イ + シ. This is a meaning-based explanation, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意思](意思.md) is also いし and overlaps in "intention," particularly when
communicating what someone wants. 意志 highlights willing and determination.
[決心](決心.md) (けっしん) names a resolution or decision.

## Distinctive meaning

意志 is not automatically an English legal "will" disposing of property.
It describes volition. 意志が弱い (いしがよわい), "weak-willed," is a useful
contrast to 意志が強い; changing the adjective is clearer than inventing one
opposite noun for every use.
