# ダイヤル - dial; dial a number

## Meaning

ダイヤル is a dial, including a telephone's rotary dial, an instrument's
marked face or scale, or a control used to select a setting.
ダイヤルする means dial a telephone number.
ダイアル is another recognized spelling.

- ラジオのダイヤルを回しました。
  (ラジオのダイヤルをまわしました。) - I turned the radio dial.
- 番号を確認してからダイヤルしてください。
  (ばんごうをかくにんしてからダイヤルしてください。) - Check the number before dialing.

The first sentence describes the physical control. The second describes
the telephone action, not necessarily the movement of an old rotary dial.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword comes from English dial.
The ヤ in ダイヤル is full-sized, not the small ャ of a contracted sound.
The recognized ダイアル spelling belongs to the same entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[回す](回す.md) (まわす) describes turning something and also has a
telephone-dialing sense. It is broader than ダイヤルする.
[電話](電話.md) (でんわ) names a telephone or call; it is not the name
of the dial part in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

A clock face and a telephone dial need not operate in the same way.
Likewise, using ダイヤルする does not guarantee that the speaker physically
rotates a wheel. Keep the noun's control/face meaning separate from the
telephone action rather than interpreting every dial as an entire telephone.
