# 相手 (あいて) - partner; other party; opponent

## Meaning

相手 is a noun for the person on the other side of an interaction: a partner, the person addressed, or an opponent. The relationship, rather than friendship or hostility, determines the meaning.

- 日本語で話す相手がほしいです。 (にほんごではなすあいてがほしいです。) - I want someone to speak Japanese with.
- 試合の相手は兄です。 (しあいのあいてはあにです。) - My opponent in the match is my older brother.

相手の話を聞く (あいてのはなしをきく) means "listen to what the other person says." Here 相手 can be a customer, colleague, or conversation partner.

## How the kanji combine

[相](../kanji/相.md) suggests a mutual relationship, and [手](../kanji/手.md) can represent a person taking part. Read the native compound あい + て, not a sequence of on readings. The characters help recall someone interacting with another person.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[友達](友達.md) (ともだち) identifies a friend; [仲間](仲間.md) (なかま) identifies a companion or fellow group member. Neither requires the two-sided interaction emphasized by 相手. There is no single opposite covering all its uses.

## Distinctive meaning

相手をする means to engage with someone or keep them company. 相手にする means to deal with or pay attention to someone; 相手にしない can mean "ignore." Do not translate 相手 mechanically as either "friend" or "enemy."
