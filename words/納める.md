# 納める (おさめる) - pay taxes; deliver goods

## Meaning

納める means to pay something due, such as taxes or fees, or to deliver goods to their intended recipient. It is a transitive ichidan verb: what is paid or delivered takes を, and the recipient can take に.

- 税金を納めました。 (ぜいきんをおさめました。) - I paid my taxes.
- 注文された品を店に納めます。 (ちゅうもんされたしなをみせにおさめます。) - We will deliver the ordered goods to the shop.

The forms include 納めます (おさめます), 納めて (おさめて), and 納めない (おさめない).

The family also includes putting something away and accepting a gift. 本を箱に納める (ほんをはこにおさめる) means "put the books away in a box"; どうぞお納めください (どうぞおおさめください) invites someone to accept what is offered.

## How the kanji combine

納 carries an association with putting something in its proper place or handing it in. Here it writes おさ, followed by める. This is a native verb, not a compound made from on-readings. The association is a learning aid, not a rule predicting every object or spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[払う](払う.md) (はらう) and [支払う](支払う.md) (しはらう) also express paying. [届ける](届ける.md) (とどける) emphasizes delivery to a destination, and [供給](供給.md)する (きょうきゅうする) emphasizes providing a supply. These overlap without being unrestricted substitutes.

[収める](収める.md), also おさめる, shares storage and acceptance uses with 納める. [受け取る](受け取る.md) (うけとる) focuses on receiving something.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling matters. Use 納める in 税金を納める, but 収める in 成績を収める (せいせきをおさめる), "achieve results." The shared reading and dictionary family do not assign every sense to both spellings. Likewise, paying something due is not the same viewpoint as accepting someone else's gift: the object and situation identify the use.
