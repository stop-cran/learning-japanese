# 合理 (ごうり) - rationality

## Meaning

合理 is the noun rationality: conformity to reason or logical
justification. The related adjective is 合理的 (ごうりてき),
"rational; reasonable; logical."

- 彼は合理を重んじます。 (かれはごうりをおもんじます。) - He values rationality.
- 合理的な方法を選びました。 (ごうりてきなほうほうをえらびました。) - We chose a rational method.

Use 合理的な before a noun and 合理的に before a verb:
合理的に判断する (ごうりてきにはんだんする), "judge rationally."
The English adjective "rational" in the source is accounted for by this
construction, not by treating the noun 合理 as an ordinary な-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[合](../kanji/合.md) contributes fitting or according with, and
[理](../kanji/理.md) contributes reason or logic. Their relationship
suggests according with reason. The on readings are ゴウ + リ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

道理 (どうり) concerns reason or a reasonable principle, and
理性 (りせい) concerns the faculty of reason.
[理由](理由.md) (りゆう) identifies a reason or grounds for something,
rather than automatically naming the quality of being rational.
不合理 (ふごうり), irrationality or unreasonableness, provides a contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

A claim of rationality is not a guarantee of moral goodness, agreement,
or the absence of emotion. Keep the noun and the derived adjective
distinct: this card's additional 合理的 teaching explains a useful
construction, not another independently assigned N2 word.
