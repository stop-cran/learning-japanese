# 親しい (したしい) - close; familiar

## Meaning

親しい describes a close or familiar relationship. It is an i-adjective:
親しい友達 is "a close friend," and 人と親しい means being close to someone.
The past is 親しかった, and the negative is 親しくない.

- 彼女とは学生のころから親しいです。 (かのじょとはがくせいのころからしたしいです。) - I have been close to her since we were students.
- 親しい友達だけを家に招きました。 (したしいともだちだけをいえにまねきました。) - I invited just my close friends to my home.

親しくなる (したしくなる) describes becoming close; と marks the other person
in 誰かと親しくなる.

## How the kanji combine

[親](../kanji/親.md) contributes closeness or familiarity here. It is read した
in the native adjective 親しい; しい supplies the kana ending. Its separate
reading おや, "parent," does not make every 親しい relationship a family relationship.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[親友](親友.md) (しんゆう) is a noun for a close friend, while 親しい describes
the relationship. [近い](近い.md) (ちかい) can mean near in distance; people sitting
near one another need not be 親しい.

## Distinctive meaning

"Intimate" is possible in English, but 親しい does not itself claim a romantic
or sexual relationship. It can describe trusted friends, colleagues, and other
familiar people. Focus on the interpersonal closeness in the examples, rather
than the physical distance between the people.
