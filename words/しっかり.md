# しっかり - firmly, properly

## Meaning

しっかり describes something being done firmly, securely, or properly. Its English translation depends on the action rather than on a single physical property.

- 手すりをしっかり握ってください。 (てすりをしっかりにぎってください。) - Hold the handrail firmly.
- 試験の前にしっかり復習しました。 (しけんのまえにしっかりふくしゅうしました。) - I reviewed thoroughly before the exam.

It can modify a verb directly or appear with と: しっかりと握る.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. Rare dictionary spellings such as 確り are not needed for everyday writing. Preserve both small っ and final り.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ちゃんと overlaps with the "properly" sense in conversation. ゆっくり means "slowly" or "at leisure"; slowness is not the opposite of doing something securely or thoroughly.

## Distinctive meaning

しっかりした人 (しっかりしたひと) is a dependable or levelheaded person. This した form modifies a noun; do not turn the adverb into しっかりな. The core idea is reliable substance or execution, not merely muscular strength.
