# 階段 (かいだん) — stairs

## Meaning

階段 means stairs, a stairway, or a staircase. It names the structure used to move between levels, indoors or outdoors.

- 階段で二階に上がります。  
  かいだんでにかいにあがります。  
  "I'll take the stairs to the second floor."
- 階段を下りてください。  
  かいだんをおりてください。  
  "Please go down the stairs."

In the second example, を marks the route traveled, not an object being acted on.

## How the kanji combine

階 refers to a level or floor; [段](../kanji/段.md) refers to a step or tier. Their on readings combine as かい + だん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

エレベーター, "elevator", and エスカレーター, "escalator", are useful alternatives for moving between floors, not antonyms. 段差 (だんさ) means a difference in level or a raised step, not necessarily a staircase.

## Distinctive meaning

階段 names the stairs as a whole. A floor number such as 二階 (にかい), "second floor", identifies a building level rather than the stairs leading to it.
