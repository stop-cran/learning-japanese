# 基地 (きち) - operating base; military base

## Meaning

基地 is a base from which people operate, such as a military or expedition base. It is a noun and names a place, not just an abstract foundation.

- 探検隊は基地に戻りました。
  (たんけんたいはきちにもどりました。) - The expedition returned to its base.
- 地図で基地の場所を確認しました。
  (ちずできちのばしょをかくにんしました。) - We checked the location of the base on the map.

## How the kanji combine

基 contributes a base or foundation, and [地](../kanji/地.md) a place or land. Their on readings combine as キ + チ. Together they suggest the place that supports an operation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) is a physical or figurative foundation; 基地 is an operational location. ベース also has an operating-base sense and a basis/foundation sense. ベース can also be a separate word meaning musical bass. Do not transfer every meaning of the borrowed word to 基地.

## Distinctive meaning

Military bases are important examples, but the word is not military-only: the expedition sentence uses the same place sense. Conversely, a supporting [台](台.md) (だい), such as a stand under an object, is not automatically an operational 基地 merely because English can call both a "base."
