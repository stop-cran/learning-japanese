# 女優 (じょゆう) - actress; female actor

## Meaning

女優 is a noun for an actress or female actor. It describes a performer in film, television, theater, or similar dramatic work. **女優になる** means "become an actress."

- 彼女は有名な女優です。 (かのじょはゆうめいなじょゆうです。) - She is a well-known actress.
- その女優は映画で医師を演じました。 (そのじょゆうはえいがでいしをえんじました。) - The actress played a doctor in the film.

In the second sentence, 女優 names the performer's occupation; 医師 names the character's occupation.

## How the kanji combine

[女](../kanji/女.md), "woman; female," modifies [優](../kanji/優.md), which contributes "performer" here. ジョ + ユウ gives じょゆう. Do not translate 優 as "gentle" and infer a personality requirement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[俳優](俳優.md) (はいゆう) is the broader occupational term "actor" and can include women. 男優 (だんゆう) specifies a male actor. These are role labels, not judgments of ability or importance.

## Distinctive meaning

The English choice between "actress" and "female actor" depends on the context and editorial preference. In Japanese, 女優 explicitly marks a woman performer, while 俳優 need not specify gender. Being a singer or television personality does not by itself establish that someone is acting; check the activity described.
