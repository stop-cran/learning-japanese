# 陸 (りく) - land; shore

## Meaning

陸 (りく) is land, particularly in contrast to the sea or other water. It is a noun. 陸に上がる (りくにあがる) means "go ashore," while 陸から (りくから) means "from land."

- 船から陸に上がりました。 (ふねからりくにあがりました。) - We went ashore from the ship.
- 長い航海の後、陸が見えました。 (ながいこうかいのあと、りくがみえました。) - After a long voyage, land came into view.

The second example identifies land as opposed to open water; it does not necessarily refer to a beach.

## How the kanji combine

陸 is a single character using the on reading リク here. The whole noun means land or shore. It also supplies the land element in [大陸](大陸.md) (たいりく), "continent."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[海](海.md) (うみ), "sea," gives the relevant contrast. [海岸](海岸.md) (かいがん) names the seacoast specifically, while 陸 can include land farther inland.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list assigns りく but glosses it as the legal-document numeral "six." That gloss does not match the inspected りく dictionary identity. This card deliberately retains the verified land/shore reading and meaning; it does not claim to have recovered the compiler's historical intention or assign a numeral reading to りく.
