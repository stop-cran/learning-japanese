# おっしゃる (おっしゃる) - to say respectfully

## Meaning

おっしゃる is the respectful counterpart of 言う (いう), "say." It raises the person whose speech you describe, such as a teacher or customer. Quote the spoken content with と.

- 先生は明日来るとおっしゃいました。 (せんせいはあしたくるとおっしゃいました。) - The teacher said they would come tomorrow.
- 何とおっしゃいましたか。 (なんとおっしゃいましたか。) - What did you say?

It is a special godan verb: おっしゃいます, not おっしゃります. Other useful forms are おっしゃらない and おっしゃって.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. 仰る is a dictionary-listed kanji spelling, but learners need not replace the normal kana form with it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

言う is neutral. [申す](申す.md) (もうす) is humble or deferential and is used for one's own speech, rather than to elevate another speaker.

## Distinctive meaning

おっしゃる concerns the status of the person speaking within the reported situation. It does not merely make any sentence containing "say" polite: use 言います for neutral polite reporting of your own words, not 私がおっしゃいます. The person's name can also precede とおっしゃる to respectfully state what they are called.
