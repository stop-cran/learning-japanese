# 予定 (よてい) — plans; schedule

## Meaning

予定 refers to plans or an arrangement for something expected to happen.
It works as a noun, as in 明日の予定 (あしたのよてい), "tomorrow's plans."
To say what is planned, use a plain nonpast verb directly before 予定, or a noun followed by の.

- 来週、日本へ行く予定です。 (らいしゅう、にほんへいくよていです。) — I plan to go to Japan next week.
- 出発の予定は月曜日です。 (しゅっぱつのよていはげつようびです。) — The departure is planned for Monday.

The first pattern is 行く予定, not 行くの予定. 予定する also means to plan or schedule something.

## How the kanji combine

[予](../kanji/予.md) contributes "beforehand," and [定](../kanji/定.md) contributes deciding or settling.
Together they give a useful way to remember an arrangement made ahead of time. The on readings are ヨ + テイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

計画 (けいかく) also means a plan and can describe the scheme for carrying something out.
[予想](予想.md) (よそう) is a prediction: forecasting tomorrow's weather does not mean scheduling that weather.
These words can overlap in English translation, so distinguish an arrangement from a forecast.

## Distinctive meaning

予定です presents a planned event, not a guarantee that it will occur.
It can describe an individual's plans or an event arranged by others.
The noun's scheduling use is especially useful when asking what is happening on a particular day.
