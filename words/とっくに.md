# とっくに - long ago; already

## Meaning

とっくに means long ago or already, presenting an event as having happened
well before the reference point. It directly modifies the predicate.

- 宿題はとっくに終わりました。 (しゅくだいはとっくにおわりました。) - I finished my homework long ago.
- 彼はとっくに帰っています。 (かれはとっくにかえっています。) - He has long since gone home.

The second example describes the resulting situation, not a journey still in progress.
The expression does not specify an exact number of minutes or years.

## How the kanji combine

The usual learning form here is kana とっくに.
The dictionary also records 疾っくに with the same reading.
Keep the small っ; it marks the consonant pause before く.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[既に](既に.md) (すでに) and [もう](もう.md) overlap in already.
とっくに emphasizes that the event was completed well before the point under discussion.
まだ, "not yet" with a negative predicate, supplies a useful contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Long before is relative to the situation: the homework could have been finished
earlier today, rather than in the distant historical past.
Do not add another に after とっくに.
The surrounding predicate, rather than the adverb alone, supplies what happened.
