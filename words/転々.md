# 転々 (てんてん) - moving from one to another; rolling

## Meaning

転々 describes moving successively from one place, position, or person to another.
Learn 転々と and the pattern 場所を転々とする (ばしょをてんてんとする),
"move from place to place."

- 彼は各地を転々としました。 (かれはかくちをてんてんとしました。) - He moved from one region to another.
- 職を転々と変えました。 (しょくをてんてんとかえました。) - I moved from job to job.

It also has a physical rolling or tumbling use, as in 石が転々と転がる
(いしがてんてんところがる), "a stone rolls along."
The spelling 転転 is also recorded.

## How the kanji combine

[転](../kanji/転.md) contributes turning or changing; 々 repeats it.
The repeated on reading テン + テン belongs to this complete expression.
The changing-place association is a memory aid, not a claim that each job change involves literal rolling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[移動](移動.md) (いどう) is movement or relocation more broadly.
[転がる](転がる.md) (ころがる) is the intransitive verb "roll."
[点々](点々.md) (てんてん) instead concerns scattered points or appearances.

## Distinctive meaning

Repeated transitions are important in the job and travel examples:
one ordinary move does not by itself establish 転々.
The spelling distinguishes it from 点々 even though both are pronounced てんてん.
A distribution of houses is 点々; a person moving from town to town is 転々.
