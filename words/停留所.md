# 停留所 (ていりゅうじょ) - bus or tram stop

## Meaning

停留所 is a designated stopping place for a bus, tram, or similar service. It is a noun naming the place, not the action of stopping.

- 次の停留所で降ります。 (つぎのていりゅうじょでおります。) - I will get off at the next stop.
- 停留所でバスを待っています。 (ていりゅうじょでバスをまっています。) - I am waiting for the bus at the stop.

で marks where you get off or wait. To give the stop as a destination, use に or まで as appropriate: 停留所まで歩く (ていりゅうじょまであるく), "walk to the stop."

## How the kanji combine

停 contributes stopping, [留](../kanji/留.md) contributes remaining, and [所](../kanji/所.md) contributes a place. 停留 describes stopping or staying, and 所 identifies the place for it. The readings are テイ + リュウ + ジョ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

バス停 (バスてい) explicitly names a bus stop. [駅](駅.md) (えき) means station, particularly in railway travel. A bus stop and a railway station may be near each other without being the same facility.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun does not guarantee a building, shelter, or ticket counter. A marked stopping point can be a 停留所. JMdict also records ていりゅうしょ; this article teaches the assigned reading ていりゅうじょ rather than treating the two as separate kinds of stop.
