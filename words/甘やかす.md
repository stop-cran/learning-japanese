# 甘やかす (あまやかす) - pamper; spoil someone

## Meaning

甘やかす means to pamper or spoil someone by indulging them.
It is a **transitive godan verb** ending in す; the person indulged takes を.
Forms include 甘やかします (あまやかします), 甘やかして (あまやかして),
and 甘やかした (あまやかした).

- 子どもを甘やかしすぎないでください。 (こどもをあまやかしすぎないでください。) - Please do not spoil the child too much.
- 今日は少し自分を甘やかして、好きなお菓子を食べました。 (きょうはすこしじぶんをあまやかして、すきなおかしをたべました。) - Today I indulged myself a little and ate my favorite sweets.

## How the kanji combine

[甘](../kanji/甘.md) connects sweetness with indulgence or leniency.
It supplies あま, with やかす completing the verb.
This is not an instruction to sweeten food.

## Synonyms and antonyms

甘える (あまえる) presents relying on or taking advantage of another person's kindness;
the person relied on often takes に. 甘やかす instead presents the person doing the indulging.
[甘い](甘い.md) (あまい) can describe a sweet taste or leniency, but is an adjective, not this action.
厳しくする (きびしくする), "be strict," supplies a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

甘やかす can criticize excessive indulgence, but 自分を甘やかす can also be a light way
to describe treating oneself. It is not the general verb for helping, caring for, or raising a child.
English "spoil" here concerns indulgence, not food going bad.
