# しとしと — soft steady drizzle

## Meaning

Rain falling quietly and gently, often for a sustained period rather than in a noisy downpour.

- 朝から雨がしとしと降っている。 — A gentle rain has been falling since morning.
- 庭の葉に、春の雨がしとしとと降り注いだ（ふりそそいだ）。 — A soft spring rain fell over the leaves in the garden.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ざあざあ](ざあざあ.md) contrasts with forceful, noisy rain. [ぽつぽつ](ぽつぽつ.md) foregrounds separated drops, and [ぱらぱら](ぱらぱら.md) a scattered light patter. These impressions can overlap during the same shower.

## Distinctive meaning

しとしと降る and しとしとと降り続く（ふりつづく）are useful. JMdict also records a moist/damp adjectival sense, but the rain construction is the practical focus. [しっとり](しっとり.md) often describes a moist result or a calm manner; it is not simply the interchangeable “short form” of しとしと.
