# 睨む (にらむ) - glare at; keep an eye on

## Meaning

睨む means to glare or scowl at someone. It can also mean keeping a watchful
eye on someone, including regarding them with suspicion.
It is a transitive godan verb: にらみます, にらんだ, にらんで, にらまない.
The spelling にらむ is usual; 睨む has the same reading and meaning.

- 彼は私をにらみました。
  (かれはわたしをにらみました。) - He glared at me.
- 彼は警察ににらまれています。
  (かれはけいさつににらまれています。) - The police are keeping a suspicious eye on him.

The first example takes the person looked at with を.
The second is passive: 警察に identifies the police as the watchers.
It does not necessarily describe a continuous angry stare.

## How the kanji combine

This is one native verb, not a multi-kanji compound.
睨 supplies にら and む is okurigana.
Recognizing the kanji does not require replacing the usual kana spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[見る](見る.md) (みる) is broader seeing or looking.
[眺める](眺める.md) (ながめる) can mean gazing or staring without necessarily scowling.
[疑う](疑う.md) (うたがう) overlaps with suspecting, and
[調べる](調べる.md) (しらべる) with examining or investigating.
These are contextual overlaps, not substitutes for every use of にらむ.

## Distinctive meaning

Additional senses include examining carefully, estimating or suspecting,
and taking something into consideration:
犯人だとにらむ (はんにんだとにらむ), "suspect that someone is the culprit,"
and 将来をにらむ (しょうらいをにらむ), "take the future into account."
The latter overlaps with [考える](考える.md) (かんがえる), "consider";
it does not mean glaring angrily at the future.
[考慮](考慮.md)する (こうりょする), "take into account," is another direct comparison.
Read the object and construction before choosing an English translation.
