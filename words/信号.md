# 信号 (しんごう) - signal; traffic light

## Meaning

信号 is a signal carrying information. It also means a traffic light or traffic signal. It is a noun; useful combinations include 信号を送る (しんごうをおくる), "send a signal," and 信号が変わる (しんごうがかわる), "the traffic light changes."

- 赤信号で車が止まりました。 (あかしんごうでくるまがとまりました。) - The car stopped at a red light.
- この装置は光で信号を送ります。 (このそうちはひかりでしんごうをおくります。) - This device sends signals using light.

In the second example, で marks the means of communication. A signal need not be a road sign or a colored lamp.

## How the kanji combine

[信](../kanji/信.md) contributes communication or a message, and [号](../kanji/号.md) contributes a sign or designation. Together they describe a sign that communicates information. The reading is シン + ゴウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[合図](合図.md) (あいず) is a cue or signal, often one used to coordinate an action. It overlaps with 信号, but an electronic signal is naturally described as 信号. 標識 (ひょうしき) is a sign or marker, such as a road sign.

## Distinctive meaning

In street directions, 次の信号 (つぎのしんごう) can identify "the next traffic light." In an electronics explanation, the same noun may identify information transmitted through a wire. Translate by context rather than treating "traffic light" as the word's entire meaning. There is no general antonym.
