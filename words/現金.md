# 現金 (げんきん) - cash; self-interested

## Meaning

As a noun, 現金 means cash or money on hand. 現金で払う means "pay in cash": で marks the payment method.

- この店では現金で払ってください。 (このみせではげんきんではらってください。) - Please pay in cash at this shop.

As a な-adjective, 現金 describes a calculating or self-interested attitude, especially a quick change when a benefit appears.

- お菓子をあげると言ったら急に手伝うなんて、現金だね。 (おかしをあげるといったらきゅうにてつだうなんて、げんきんだね。) - Suddenly offering to help when I promise sweets? How self-interested!

The second use evaluates behavior; it does not mean someone is made of cash or literally receives money.

## How the kanji combine

[現](../kanji/現.md) contributes what is present or actual; [金](../kanji/金.md) contributes money. Together they point to available money. Read ゲン + キン. The attitude sense is a further established use, not a separate spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[お金](お金.md) (おかね) is broader money vocabulary. [カード](カード.md)で払う contrasts with 現金で払う as a payment method. [けち](けち.md) means stingy; being self-interested is not necessarily refusing to spend.

## Distinctive meaning

Grammar helps: 現金を持っている means having cash, while 現金な人 describes a self-serving person. Both senses belong to this dictionary identity. Do not restrict the second sense to professional mercenaries just because an English dictionary gloss uses "mercenary."
