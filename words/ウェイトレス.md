# ウェイトレス - waitress

## Meaning

ウェイトレス is a waitress, a woman serving customers in a restaurant
or similar setting. It names the worker, not the food or service charge.

- ウェイトレスに水をお願いしました。 (ウェイトレスにみずをおねがいしました。) - I asked the waitress for water.
- 彼女はレストランでウェイトレスとして働いています。 (かのじょはレストランでウェイトレスとしてはたらいています。) - She works as a waitress at a restaurant.

として means "as" in the second example, identifying the job.

## How the kanji combine

There are no kanji components. In ウェイトレス, ェ is small.
The dictionary also lists ウエイトレス and indexes ウエートレス
and ウェートレス as search variants. These spellings refer to the
same occupational word, not four different jobs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ウェイター is the corresponding male occupational term.
[店員](店員.md) (てんいん), "shop employee," is broader and does
not itself identify a female restaurant server. ホールスタッフ is
another workplace label that does not mark the worker as female.

## Distinctive meaning

Recognizing a gendered occupational word is different from choosing how
to address someone. This noun is not a required way to call a server
over; a polite すみません can get someone's attention without guessing
their role or gender.
