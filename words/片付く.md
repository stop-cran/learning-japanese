# 片付く (かたづく) - be tidied; be settled

## Meaning

片付く means becoming tidy, being dealt with or settled, or being finished. It is an intransitive verb: the room, problem, or task is what reaches the resulting state.

- 部屋がきれいに片付きました。 (へやがきれいにかたづきました。) - The room is tidy now.
- やっと仕事が片付きました。 (やっとしごとがかたづきました。) - The work is finally finished.
- 話し合いで問題が片付きました。 (はなしあいでもんだいがかたづきました。) - The problem was settled through discussion.

片づく is another spelling. This godan verb has 片付きます, 片付いて, and 片付かない.

## How the kanji combine

This native compound uses [片](../kanji/片.md), かた, with [付](../kanji/付.md), つく voiced to づく in this word. Putting something to one side is a learning association; the established verb also covers a task's resolution or completion. Do not translate each character literally to produce its whole range of meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[片付ける](片付ける.md) is the transitive partner: 部屋を片付ける (へやをかたづける), "tidy the room." [済む](済む.md) (すむ) overlaps in completion, and [解決](解決.md) (かいけつ) in resolving a problem.

## Distinctive meaning

部屋が片付く presents the room's resulting condition; it does not identify who did the tidying. English may use "be put in order," but 片付く is not grammatically a passive form of 片付ける. "Dispose of" in a gloss can mean dealing with a matter, not necessarily throwing it in the rubbish.
