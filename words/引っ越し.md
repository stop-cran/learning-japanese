# 引っ越し (ひっこし) - moving home; relocation

## Meaning

引っ越し names moving one's home or premises, including an office.
It is a noun and can form the intransitive verb 引っ越しする.
引越し, the source spelling, is another dictionary spelling.

- 来月の引っ越しの準備をしています。 (らいげつのひっこしのじゅんびをしています。) - I am preparing for next month's move.
- 引っ越しに二日かかりました。 (ひっこしにふつかかかりました。) - The move took two days.

引っ越し先 (ひっこしさき) is the place one moves to.
The noun names the relocation, not the new home itself.

## How the kanji combine

This is the noun associated with [引っ越す](引っ越す.md) (ひっこす).
[引](../kanji/引.md) contributes the 引っ element and
[越](../kanji/越.md) crossing or moving beyond.
Learn the established whole word, including the small っ, rather than
translating it as an instruction to pull something across.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[移転](移転.md) (いてん) overlaps in relocating premises and is useful
for an office or shop. [移動](移動.md) (いどう) is broader movement:
moving a desk to the window need not be a 引っ越し.
There is no single opposite covering every change of residence.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every English "moving" as 引っ越し.
Moving your hand or carrying a parcel does not by itself mean changing
residence or premises. Nor does a 引っ越し require buying the new property:
moving between rented homes also fits.
