# 僧侶 (そうりょ) - Buddhist priest; monk

## Meaning

僧侶 is a Buddhist priest or monk. It names a clerical role, not every Buddhist
believer. Depending on context, it can refer to one person or to Buddhist
clergy collectively.

- 彼は僧侶です。 (かれはそうりょです。) - He is a Buddhist priest.
- お寺で僧侶の話を聞きました。 (おてらでそうりょのはなしをききました。) - I listened to a Buddhist priest's talk at the temple.

## How the kanji combine

[僧](../kanji/僧.md) ソウ supplies the Buddhist monk or priest meaning;
侶 リョ means a companion or fellow. Together they form an established noun
for Buddhist clergy. The "fellow" contribution does not require a plural
translation: one person can be a 僧侶.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[坊さん](坊さん.md) (ぼうさん), also お坊さん (おぼうさん), is a familiar,
respectful expression for a Buddhist priest or monk. 僧侶 is more formal.
This is a register difference, not a rule that they describe different occupations.

神父 (しんぷ), a Catholic priest, is a different religious role, not an antonym.
English "priest" alone does not make these terms interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 僧侶 for the religious person, and [寺](寺.md) (てら) for a Buddhist temple.
The noun itself does not specify the person's rank or one universal way of life.
Its ordinary Buddhist reference is the learning focus here, not a claim that
the word has never been used more broadly in religious writing.
