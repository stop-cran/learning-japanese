# 引き分け (ひきわけ) - draw; tie game

## Meaning

引き分け is a draw or tie in a contest: the contest ends with neither
side treated as the winner or loser. It is a noun.

- 試合は引き分けに終わりました。 (しあいはひきわけにおわりました。) - The match ended in a draw.
- 昨日の試合は二対二の引き分けでした。 (きのうのしあいはにたいにのひきわけでした。) - Yesterday's match was a two-all draw.

引き分けになる (ひきわけになる) describes the result becoming a draw.
The noun does not itself conjugate like a verb.

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) and [分](../kanji/分.md) form the native
word ひきわけ, related to 引き分ける (ひきわける).
Learn the contest-result meaning as a whole rather than deriving
it by literally pulling objects apart. 引分け is the source spelling;
引分 is also recorded.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勝ち](勝ち.md) (かち) is a win, and [負け](負け.md) (まけ)
a loss. [勝敗](勝敗.md) (しょうはい) concerns victory or defeat
more generally. [分ける](分ける.md) (わける) also has a tie-result
use, alongside the dividing and sharing uses its article starts with.

## Distinctive meaning

An equal score during a game is not yet its final 引き分け.
The rules may require extra play or another way of deciding a winner.
English "tie" can also name a [ネクタイ](ネクタイ.md), a necktie;
that is a different meaning, not another use of this contest noun.
