# 支配 (しはい) - rule; domination; control

## Meaning

支配 is rule, domination, or control. The transitive verb **支配する** takes the person, territory,
or situation controlled with **を**. It can also describe a feeling or influence that dominates someone.

- その王は広い地域を支配していました。 (そのおうはひろいちいきをしはいしていました。) - That king ruled a large region.
- 不安に支配されないように、友人に相談しました。 (ふあんにしはいされないように、ゆうじんにそうだんしました。) - I talked to a friend so that I would not be overwhelmed by anxiety.

In the passive 支配される, に can identify the controlling influence.
支配下 (しはいか) means "under the control" of someone or something.

## How the kanji combine

[支](../kanji/支.md) contributes handling or directing here; [配](../kanji/配.md) contributes arranging or allocating.
Together they form an established word for directing or controlling.
The reading is シ + ハイ; translating the characters separately as "support" and "distribute" would miss the whole-word meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[管理](管理.md) (かんり) emphasizes management or administration.
[影響](影響.md) (えいきょう) means influence; influencing someone need not amount to controlling them.
[独立](独立.md) (どくりつ), "independence," contrasts with being under another's rule.

## Distinctive meaning

支配 can concern institutions, territory, or an overpowering mental state, not just direct orders.
It is a stronger description than simply having some effect on an outcome.
When you mean routine management of equipment or schedules, check whether 管理 expresses the intended relationship more accurately.
