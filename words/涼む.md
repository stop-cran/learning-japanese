# 涼む (すずむ) - cool oneself; enjoy cool air

## Meaning

涼む means to cool oneself off or enjoy cool air, for example by resting in
the shade. It is an intransitive godan verb ending in む. A place for cooling
off can take で.

- 木の下で少し涼みましょう。 (きのしたですこしすずみましょう。) - Let's cool off under the tree for a while.
- 夕方、川のそばで涼みました。 (ゆうがた、かわのそばですずみました。) - In the evening, I enjoyed the cool air by the river.

Useful forms are 涼みます, 涼んで (すずんで), and 涼まない.

## How the kanji combine

[涼](../kanji/涼.md) contributes refreshing coolness and is read すず here.
The ending む identifies this verb; it does not conjugate like the related
adjective 涼しい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[涼しい](涼しい.md) (すずしい) describes pleasantly cool conditions.
[冷える](冷える.md) (ひえる) describes becoming cold or chilled, including
unwanted coldness. 涼む focuses on taking advantage of cool conditions.

## Distinctive meaning

The evening example does not restrict 涼む to evenings. Cooling off under a
tree during the day also fits. For chilling a drink, 冷やす (ひやす) is the
transitive verb; do not make the drink an object of 涼む with を.
