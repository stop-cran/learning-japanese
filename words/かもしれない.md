# かもしれない (かもしれない) - may; might; possibly

## Meaning

かもしれない marks a possibility rather than a firm claim. Attach it to a plain-form verb or i-adjective: 行くかもしれない, "might go"; 高いかもしれない, "might be expensive." After a present affirmative noun or na-adjective, omit だ: **学生かもしれない**, **静かかもしれない**.

- 明日は雨かもしれません。 (あしたはあめかもしれません。) - It might rain tomorrow.
- 彼はもう帰ったかもしれない。 (かれはもうかえったかもしれない。) - He may already have gone home.

かもしれません is the polite ending. Past and negative content remains inside the proposition: 行かなかったかもしれない means "might not have gone."

## How the kanji combine

This expression is usually written in kana; かも知れない is also recorded. Learn the whole pattern rather than translating each particle and 知れない separately. Its negative-looking ending does not make the entire proposition negative.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[きっと](きっと.md) conveys a stronger expectation, rather than the open possibility expressed here. [多分](多分.md) (たぶん), "probably," can accompany a prediction, but is an adverb, not an identical clause ending.

## Distinctive meaning

かもしれない does not assign a numerical probability. It leaves room for alternatives. English "may" can also grant permission, but permission is not this expression's function: 入ってもいい, "you may enter," is different from 入るかもしれない, "someone might enter."
