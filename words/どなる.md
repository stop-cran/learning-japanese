# どなる - shout; yell

## Meaning

どなる means to shout or yell, often angrily or in scolding.
The kanji spelling is 怒鳴る.
It is a godan verb: どなります, どなって, どなった, どならない.

- そんなに大きな声でどならないでください。 (そんなにおおきなこえでどならないでください。) - Please do not shout so loudly.
- 彼は部下をどなりました。 (かれはぶかをどなりました。) - He shouted at his subordinate.

The person being shouted at can take を.
The loud-calling use need not involve anger in every instance.

## How the kanji combine

In 怒鳴る, [怒](../kanji/怒.md) is associated with anger and
[鳴](../kanji/鳴.md) with making a sound.
These associations help recall the written form, but do not prove that every use
requires anger or that the word is assembled from the independent on readings.
This article retains the source list's kana form どなる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[叫ぶ](叫ぶ.md) (さけぶ) also means shout or cry out.
[叱る](叱る.md) (しかる) means scold or reprimand;
scolding need not always be shouted.
[怒る](怒る.md) (おこる) can mean feel angry or scold,
but merely feeling anger does not imply raising one's voice.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary supports both loud calling and loud scolding.
Do not turn the spelling's 怒 into an anger-only restriction.
Conversely, どなる is not a general verb for speaking, making any sound,
or telephoning someone; the vocal manner matters.
