# せっせと — diligently; busily working

## Meaning

Applying oneself steadily and industriously to an activity.

- 母は庭（にわ）の草をせっせと抜（ぬ）いている。 — My mother is diligently pulling weeds in the garden.
- 旅行のために、せっせとお金を貯（た）めた。 — I diligently saved money for the trip.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ばりばり](ばりばり.md) and [もりもり](もりもり.md) can also suggest energetic work. [きびきび](きびきび.md) often focuses more on crisp, efficient movements.

## Distinctive meaning

The final と is already included: せっせと働く（はたらく）, not せっせとと働く. The expression describes effort, not proof that the work succeeds or that the activity is morally good.
