# 冗談 (じょうだん) - joke; jest

## Meaning

冗談 is a joke or remark not meant seriously. It is a noun.
Use 冗談を言う (じょうだんをいう), "tell a joke; joke," rather than adding する
as the default verb. 冗談です means "I'm joking" or "It's a joke."

- 兄はよく冗談を言って、みんなを笑わせます。
  (あにはよくじょうだんをいって、みんなをわらわせます。)
  - My older brother often makes jokes and makes everyone laugh.
- 「本当に引っ越すの？」「ううん、冗談だよ。」
  (「ほんとうにひっこすの？」「ううん、じょうだんだよ。」)
  - "Are you really moving?" "No, I'm joking."

冗談で言った (じょうだんでいった) means "said it as a joke."

## How the kanji combine

冗 contributes something superfluous or not serious here, and [談](../kanji/談.md)
contributes talk. The compound names joking talk, not necessarily a long or wordy speech.
The on readings are ジョウ + ダン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ジョーク overlaps with "joke." [嘘](嘘.md) (うそ) is a lie: a joking statement
may be untrue, but the words describe different aspects of the communication.
[本当](本当.md) (ほんとう), "true; real," contrasts with the pretended move in the exchange.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling a remark 冗談 describes it as a joke, but does not guarantee that listeners
find it funny or harmless. Tone, relationship, and context matter.
冗談ではありません explicitly says the speaker is not joking.
