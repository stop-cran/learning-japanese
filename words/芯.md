# 芯 (しん) - core; wick

## Meaning

芯 is the core or central inner part of something. Depending on the object,
it can name a pencil's lead, a candle's wick, plant pith, or material inserted
inside something to give it shape. It is a noun.

- 鉛筆の芯が折れました。 (えんぴつのしんがおれました。) - The pencil lead broke.
- ろうそくの芯に火をつけました。 (ろうそくのしんにひをつけました。) - I lit the candle's wick.

Use Nの芯 to identify what has the core or inner material.
The English translation follows the object; a wick is not pencil lead.

## How the kanji combine

芯 is a single character read with the on reading シン.
There is no second character or kana ending to divide.
Its central-part meaning helps connect the different physical uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[中心](中心.md) (ちゅうしん) also means center or core and can identify
a main focus. [真ん中](真ん中.md) (まんなか) emphasizes a middle position.
芯 can instead name the actual inner material, not just where the center is.

## Distinctive meaning

鉛筆の芯 means the writing core, not the pencil's geometric midpoint.
Clothing can likewise contain 芯 as an inner stiffening material.
The spelling [心](心.md), read こころ on its own article, concerns mind
or feelings; do not read 芯 as こころ.
The dictionary's separate botanical senses are outside this card's focus.
