# 貸し出し (かしだし) - lending; loan service

## Meaning

貸し出し is lending or loaning something out. It is useful for a library's lending service, equipment loans, and similar arrangements. 貸し出しする also functions as a verb.

- 図書館の本の貸し出しは、一人五冊までです。 (としょかんのほんのかしだしは、ひとりごさつまでです。) - The library lends up to five books per person.
- この店では、自転車の貸し出しをしています。 (このみせでは、じてんしゃのかしだしをしています。) - This shop rents out bicycles.

The noun does not by itself establish whether borrowing is free. 貸出 and 貸出し are dictionary spellings of the same かしだし.

## How the kanji combine

This native compound joins the stems of [貸す](貸す.md), "lend," and 出す (だす), "put out." [貸](../kanji/貸.md) concerns lending; [出](../kanji/出.md) contributes the outward direction. 貸し出す (かしだす) is the related verb, while the final し in 貸し出し forms the noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[貸し](貸し.md) can also name lending, but additionally covers a favor owed. [借りる](借りる.md) (かりる), "borrow; rent," takes the recipient's viewpoint. Returning a borrowed item is a separate action, not another meaning of 貸し出し.

## Distinctive meaning

On a library notice, 貸し出し names the service or transaction, not a book's purchase price. 貸し出し中 (かしだしちゅう) can mean that an item is currently on loan. Keep the direction straight: the library lends; the reader borrows.
