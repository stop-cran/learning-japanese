# 修正 (しゅうせい) - correction; revision

## Meaning

修正 is a correction, revision, or modification that adjusts something already made or decided. 修正する is a transitive する verb: **document or plan を修正する**.

- 報告書の数字を修正しました。 (ほうこくしょのすうじをしゅうせいしました。) - I corrected the figures in the report.
- 計画を少し修正する必要があります。 (けいかくをすこししゅうせいするひつようがあります。) - We need to revise the plan a little.

The first example corrects information; the second adapts a plan. 修正 does not require the original to contain a spelling error. Useful noun constructions include 修正を加える (しゅうせいをくわえる), "make revisions," and 修正後 (しゅうせいご), "after revision."

## How the kanji combine

修 contributes putting in order or improving, and [正](../kanji/正.md) contributes correctness. Together they suggest bringing something into a more suitable or correct state. The on readings are シュウ + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

訂正 (ていせい) emphasizes correcting an error, especially in stated or written information. [変更](変更.md) (へんこう) is a broader change. [修理](修理.md) (しゅうり) normally concerns repairing a damaged or malfunctioning thing.

## Distinctive meaning

修正 focuses on adjusting the existing version rather than necessarily replacing it completely. It works for text, figures, plans, and images. Do not automatically use it for repairing a broken bicycle just because English allows "fix" in both contexts.
