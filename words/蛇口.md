# 蛇口 (じゃぐち) - faucet; tap

## Meaning

蛇口 is a faucet or tap: the fitting used to control water flowing out of a pipe.

- 蛇口をひねって水を出しました。
  (じゃぐちをひねってみずをだしました。) - I turned the tap to run the water.
- 蛇口から水が漏れています。
  (じゃぐちからみずがもれています。) - Water is leaking from the faucet.

蛇口を閉める (じゃぐちをしめる) means "turn off the tap." The tap is the object of 閉める; water is what stops flowing.

## How the kanji combine

蛇 is the snake character, read ジャ here. [口](../kanji/口.md) contributes an opening or outlet, using native くち voiced to **ぐち**. Remember the complete mixed reading **じゃぐち**, not じゃこう.

This on-first mixed compound uses the repository's `kango` category. The character association is a memory aid, not evidence here for a particular historical account of faucet design.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[コック](コック.md) also has a tap or flow-valve meaning, separate from its "cook" meaning. [水道](水道.md) (すいどう) concerns the water supply or tap water; it is not simply the name of this fitting. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

蛇口 names the device, not the water, sink, or pipe as a whole. The turning example suits a twist-operated tap; it is not a claim that every tap has that mechanism. Do not confuse the じゃぐち entry with the separate public-dictionary へびくち heading.
