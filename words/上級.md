# 上級 (じょうきゅう) - advanced level; upper grade; high rank

## Meaning

上級 is an advanced or upper level, grade, or rank. It is a noun and can modify another noun with の.

- 来月から上級のクラスで勉強します。 (らいげつからじょうきゅうのクラスでべんきょうします。) - Starting next month, I will study in the advanced class.
- この教材は上級者向けです。 (このきょうざいはじょうきゅうしゃむけです。) - These teaching materials are intended for advanced learners.

上級者 (じょうきゅうしゃ) is someone at an advanced level. The model pattern here is 上級の + noun, not an automatically inferred 上級な.

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md) supplies upper, and 級 grade or rank. Together they point to a higher position in a graded scale, not necessarily a physically high location. Keep both long vowels in じょうきゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[高度](高度.md), [高等](高等.md), and [高級](高級.md) have overlapping high-level or high-grade uses, although their usual combinations differ. [級](級.md), [段](段.md), [位](位.md), and [クラス](クラス.md) are broader grade, rank, or class words. 初級 (しょきゅう), beginner level, and 中級 (ちゅうきゅう), intermediate level, provide useful contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

上級 identifies a place in a hierarchy or learning scale. It does not automatically mean luxurious, expensive, or the very highest possible rank. Nor does it name a senior person merely because that person joined a school or company earlier; [先輩](先輩.md) (せんぱい) concerns that relationship.
