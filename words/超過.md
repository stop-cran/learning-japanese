# 超過 (ちょうか) - excess; exceeding a limit

## Meaning

超過 is a noun meaning an excess or going over an amount, limit or estimate. With する it describes exceeding that reference amount.

制限時間を超過しました。 (せいげんじかんをちょうかしました。) - I exceeded the time limit.

重量超過で追加料金がかかりました。 (じゅうりょうちょうかでついかりょうきんがかかりました。) - There was an extra charge for excess weight.

In the first example, 制限時間 names the limit that was exceeded. Learn the whole construction rather than assuming that every noun before を is an affected object.

## How the kanji combine

[超](../kanji/超.md) contributes exceeding or going beyond, and [過](../kanji/過.md) contributes passing or excess. The two reinforce the idea of going past a reference amount; this is not a verb-plus-object compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[越える](越える.md) (こえる), also written 超える in the inspected family, can mean to exceed or be more than something. [オーバー](オーバー.md) overlaps for going over a limit but also has unrelated overcoat and exaggeration uses.

[余分](余分.md) (よぶん) describes something extra or surplus. [余裕](余裕.md) (よゆう) can name a surplus or margin available: room within a budget is not the same situation as exceeding the budget.

## Distinctive meaning

超過 makes a reference amount or boundary central. It does not mean excellence merely because someone surpasses others, and it does not require that every excess be undesirable. Establish what amount is being exceeded.

The dictionary classifies the する use as intransitive. This label and the natural 制限時間を超過する construction should not be collapsed into a simplistic rule that を always proves a transitive verb.
