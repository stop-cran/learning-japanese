# バイバイ (バイバイ) - bye-bye; goodbye

## Meaning

バイバイ is a casual goodbye, borrowed from English "bye-bye." It is appropriate in familiar conversation, not a general-purpose formal farewell.

- また明日。バイバイ！ (またあした。バイバイ！) - See you tomorrow. Bye!
- 友達にバイバイと言いました。 (ともだちにバイバイといいました。) - I said goodbye to my friend.

It can also be used as a noun with する: バイバイする means to say goodbye or part. That does not necessarily imply a permanent separation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[さようなら](さようなら.md) is another goodbye, with different situational and stylistic associations. [では](では.md) can introduce a conversational leave-taking; it also has other discourse uses. Neither word is interchangeable with バイバイ in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep バイバイ separate from the homophone [売買](売買.md) (ばいばい), "buying and selling." The borrowed farewell is not the commercial noun. In a formal interaction, choose a farewell appropriate to the relationship rather than assuming this friendly expression is automatically suitable.
