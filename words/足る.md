# 足る (たる) - suffice; be worthy of

## Meaning

足る means to be enough or sufficient for a purpose. It also occurs in
**verb dictionary form + に足る**, "be worth doing; be worthy of."

- 千円で足ります。 (せんえんでたります。) - A thousand yen will suffice.
- これは信頼するに足る情報です。
  (これはしんらいするにたるじょうほうです。) - This is information worthy of trust.

足る is a godan verb: 足ります, 足らない, 足って.
The first example's 足ります can also be a form of the related ichidan
verb [足りる](足りる.md); the negative 足らない makes the 足る pattern clearer.
In everyday quantity questions, 足りる is a useful starting point.

## How the kanji combine

[足](../kanji/足.md) has a sufficiency meaning as well as its foot/leg
meaning. Here it supplies た, with る completing the native verb.
Do not read this verb as あし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[足りる](足りる.md) shares the sufficiency and worthiness meanings.
[十分](十分.md) (じゅうぶん), "sufficient," and
[間に合う](間に合う.md) (まにあう), "serve the purpose; suffice,"
overlap in judging whether a need is met.
[不足](不足.md)する (ふそくする), "be insufficient," supplies a contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

見るに足る (みるにたる) means "worth seeing," not "enough things to see."
The に足る construction often suits evaluative writing.
The amount sufficient for a task need not be large in absolute terms.
足る can also mean serving or answering a purpose; context supplies that purpose.
