# 椀 (わん) - bowl (often wooden); bowlful counter

## Meaning

椀 is a bowl, associated especially with wooden or lacquered soup bowls.
It can also count bowls of food or drink.

- 椀にみそ汁をよそいました。 (わんにみそしるをよそいました。) - I served miso soup into a bowl.
- 汁を一椀いただきました。 (しるをいちわんいただきました。) - I had one bowl of soup.

In the second sentence, 一椀 (いちわん) counts a serving by the bowl;
it does not mean that the speaker ate the container.
お椀 (おわん) is the familiar prefixed form.

## How the kanji combine

椀 is one character with the on reading ワン.
Its 木 component offers a wood association, not a universal rule
that every object called わん must be wooden.
No 椀 kanji card is currently available.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[碗](碗.md), also わん, is associated with ceramic or porcelain bowls.
JMdict distinguishes these material-qualified entries, while Digital Daijisen
groups the spellings under one わん heading. Treat the associations as useful
spelling tendencies, not an absolute ban on overlap.

[ちゃわん](ちゃわん.md) can name a rice bowl or tea vessel;
[丼](丼.md) (どんぶり) is a deeper bowl or a rice-bowl dish.
[皿](皿.md) (さら) is a plate or dish.

## Distinctive meaning

Both 椀 and 碗 have the bowlful-counter use. Their shared reading is not
evidence that they mean [湾](湾.md), "bay."
The public 椀 page also contains obsolete もい and まり headings;
those readings are not the わん word taught here.
