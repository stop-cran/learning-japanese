# がちゃがちゃ — clanking; cluttered; complaining noisily

## Meaning

An irregular clatter, a cluttered impression, or persistent noisy complaining.

- かぎをがちゃがちゃ鳴（な）らしながら探（さが）した。 — I searched through the keys, making them clatter.
- 引き出し（ひきだし）の中が小物（こもの）でがちゃがちゃしている。 — The drawer is cluttered with little things.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[がたがた](がたがた.md) and [かたかた](かたかた.md) overlap in rattling sounds. [ぐしゃぐしゃ](ぐしゃぐしゃ.md) can also describe a mess, but does not necessarily evoke clanking.

## Distinctive meaning

がちゃがちゃ言う（いう）can describe irritating complaints. A separate noun use names capsule-toy machines or their toys; the dictionary also records an insect name. Neither is the general meaning of a drawer making noise.
