# 輸入 (ゆにゅう) - import; importation

## Meaning

輸入 means importation, especially bringing goods into a country from abroad. Add する to make the transitive verb "import."

- この会社は外国から果物を輸入しています。 (このかいしゃはがいこくからくだものをゆにゅうしています。) - This company imports fruit from abroad.
- 輸入したチーズを買いました。 (ゆにゅうしたチーズをかいました。) - I bought imported cheese.

Mark the goods with を and the source with から. Useful forms are 輸入します, 輸入しない, and 輸入して.

## How the kanji combine

輸 contributes transport and [入](../kanji/入.md) entry. Together they suggest bringing things inward. Learn the full reading ゆにゅう, with the long final vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

輸出 (ゆしゅつ), "export," is the direct trade-direction contrast. 購入 (こうにゅう) means purchase; something bought locally need not be imported.

## Distinctive meaning

輸入 concerns the boundary crossed, not simply carrying something into a room. 輸入品 (ゆにゅうひん) means imported goods. The noun alone names the activity or category; a sentence about doing it needs a predicate such as 輸入する.
