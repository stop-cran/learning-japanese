# 読書 (どくしょ) — reading books

## Meaning

読書 is reading books as an activity or hobby. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb:
読書する or 読書をする means "read books." It does not take a particular book as a direct object.

- 趣味は読書です。 (しゅみはどくしょです。) — My hobby is reading.
- 寝る前に二十分ほど読書をします。 (ねるまえににじゅっぷんほどどくしょをします。) — I read for about twenty minutes before bed.

Related expressions include 読書の秋 (どくしょのあき), autumn as the "season for reading,"
and 読書感想文 (どくしょかんそうぶん), a book report expressing one's impressions.

## How the kanji combine

[読](../kanji/読.md) (read) plus [書](../kanji/書.md) (writing, here "books"): "read books", a verb-plus-object compound (the object comes second, as in Chinese word order). Both are on readings (ドク + ショ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本](本.md)を[読む](読む.md) (ほんをよむ), "read a book," is the plain phrase and can identify the particular book being read.
読者 (どくしゃ) is a reader, and 図書館 (としょかん) is a library.
執筆 (しっぴつ), writing a text, is a contrasting activity, not a lexical opposite of reading.

## Distinctive meaning

読書 can name a hobby or a particular period spent reading, as in the bedtime example.
It need not be a lifelong habit. Use 読む for reading a specified text: 手紙を読む (てがみをよむ),
"read a letter," not 手紙を読書する. The distinction is both the book-reading activity and
the verb's construction, not simply habitual versus one-time reading.
