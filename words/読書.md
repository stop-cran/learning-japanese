# 読書 (どくしょ) — reading books

## Meaning

読書 is reading as an activity or hobby: 趣味は読書です (my hobby is reading), 読書をする (read), 読書の秋 どくしょのあき (autumn, the "season for reading"), 読書感想文 どくしょかんそうぶん (book report).

## How the kanji combine

[読](../kanji/読.md) (read) plus [書](../kanji/書.md) (writing, here "books"): "read books", a verb-plus-object compound (the object comes second, as in Chinese word order). Both are on readings (ドク + ショ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 本を読む (read a book, the plain phrase), 読者 どくしゃ (reader), 図書館 としょかん (library).
- Opposite activity: 執筆 しっぴつ (writing a text).

## Distinctive meaning

読書 is the abstract hobby or habit of reading and rarely names a single act like 本を読む. 手紙を読書する is wrong: use 読む for letters, signs and kanji, and keep 読書 for books.
