# 削る (けずる) - shave; pare; cut down

## Meaning

削る means shaving or scraping material away, including sharpening a pencil.
It also means cutting down an amount, such as a budget, expenses, or time.
This is a transitive godan verb: 削ります, 削って, 削らない.

- ナイフで鉛筆を削ります。 (ナイフでえんぴつをけずります。) - I sharpen a pencil with a knife.
- 予算を削りました。 (よさんをけずりました。) - We cut the budget.

A further use is deleting or striking out part of a text:
不要な一文を削りました。 (ふようないちぶんをけずりました。) - I deleted an unnecessary sentence.
This deletion use is additional teaching, not a separate N2 assignment.

## How the kanji combine

削 contributes cutting away or shaving. The native reading is けず.る:
the kanji supplies けず, and る is the written ending.
This is one native verb, not a combination of on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[減らす](減らす.md) (へらす) more generally means reducing an amount.
[省く](省く.md) (はぶく) overlaps in omission or cutting down;
[消す](消す.md) (けす) overlaps in erasing.
[切る](切る.md) (きる) has broader cutting uses, including reductions.

## Distinctive meaning

削る need not mean removing a large piece in one cut; shaving a surface is a central use.
Nor does every use require repeated tiny reductions.
The source list's "take a percentage" belongs to the separate reading はつる in JMdict, not this けずる entry.
Do not learn it here as a general expression for charging commission.
