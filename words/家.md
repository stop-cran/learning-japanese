# 家 (いえ) — house, home

## Meaning

家 means a house or home and can also refer to a household. This article uses the reading いえ.

- 家に帰ります。 (いえにかえります。) — I am going home.
- この家は広いです。 (このいえはひろいです。) — This house is spacious.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun: [家](../kanji/家.md) represents all of いえ. It is not a compound and has no okurigana.

家 can also be written for うち in the sense "one's home or household," often written simply うち. That is another established word-reading pair, not a freely substitutable pronunciation in every expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- うち commonly presents a home or household as one's own. It overlaps with いえ; there is no absolute rule that one means only a building and the other only an emotional home.
- [家族](家族.md) かぞく names the family members, not the dwelling.
- [部屋](部屋.md) へや usually names a room, not the entire house. There is no simple direct antonym of 家.

## Distinctive meaning

Use いえ when talking about a house or home without needing a specialized term. It is not restricted to detached houses in every use: someone can 帰る to their 家 when their home is an apartment.
