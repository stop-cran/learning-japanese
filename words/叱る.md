# 叱る (しかる) - to scold; reprimand

## Meaning

叱る is a transitive godan verb meaning "scold" or "reprimand."
Mark the person reprimanded with を. The passive 叱られる means "be scolded";
the person doing the scolding then takes に.

- 先生は生徒を叱りました。 (せんせいはせいとをしかりました。) - The teacher scolded the student.
- 遅刻して、母に叱られました。 (ちこくして、ははにしかられました。) - I was scolded by my mother for being late.

The reason can be supplied by a preceding clause, as in the second sentence.
Useful forms are 叱って, 叱った, and 叱らない. Despite ending in る, it is not an ichidan verb.

## How the kanji combine

叱 writes the stem of the native word しかる, with る as okurigana.
The dictionary also records 𠮟る, and marks the word as usually written in kana.
Learn しかる as a complete word; the spelling variation does not create a new meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怒る](怒る.md) (おこる) can describe getting angry and can also describe scolding.
叱る specifically presents a reprimand, but this does not prove the speaker is calm or well-intentioned.
[注意](注意.md) (ちゅうい) in 注意する can be a warning or correction without the same force.

## Distinctive meaning

The contrast between scolding someone and being scolded is useful in personal narratives:
生徒を叱る versus 先生に叱られる.
The verb itself does not evaluate whether the reprimand was fair, gentle, or educational.
Those judgments need context, not a rigid distinction from 怒る.
