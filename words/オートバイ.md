# オートバイ - motorcycle

## Meaning

オートバイ means a motorcycle or motorbike. Use に with riding and で when it is the means of transportation.

- 兄はオートバイに乗っています。 (あにはオートバイにのっています。) - My older brother rides a motorcycle.
- オートバイで会社に行きます。 (オートバイでかいしゃにいきます。) - I go to work by motorcycle.

The context decides whether 乗っています describes a current ride or a usual practice.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word has no kanji components. JMdict traces it to the Japanese-made English-based formation auto-bi(cycle). It is an established Japanese word, not ordinary English "auto bike."

## Synonyms and antonyms

バイク commonly means a motorcycle in everyday Japanese. 自転車 (じてんしゃ) means a bicycle; 自動車 (じどうしゃ) means a car or automobile.

## Distinctive meaning

Unlike English "bike," which often means a pedal bicycle, オートバイ clearly names a motorized two-wheeled vehicle in its ordinary use. Do not translate it as "bicycle" just because the source formation contains part of that English word.
