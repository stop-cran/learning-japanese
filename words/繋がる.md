# 繋がる (つながる) - be connected; lead to

## Meaning

繋がる describes being connected, linked or related. It is intransitive:
the connected thing takes が; に or と can identify what it connects to.
JMdict marks these uses as usually written in kana, so recognize つながる.

- この道は駅につながっています。
  (このみちはえきにつながっています。) - This road connects to the station.
- やっと電話がつながりました。
  (やっとでんわがつながりました。) - The phone call finally got through.
- 毎日の練習が上達につながります。
  (まいにちのれんしゅうがじょうたつにつながります。) - Daily practice leads to improvement.

The verb also describes a connection with a matter or a family relationship:
血がつながっている (ちがつながっている) means "be related by blood."
It need not describe a physical knot.

## How the kanji combine

繋 carries the idea of linking; がる is okurigana. This is a native godan verb:
繋がります, 繋がった, 繋がって. There is no separate 繋 card in this collection.
Learn it with the transitive [繋げる](繋げる.md), not by treating every verb ending in る as ichidan.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通じる](通じる.md) (つうじる) overlaps for roads or communication getting through.
[続く](続く.md) (つづく) can describe things continuing in a line.
[繋ぐ](繋ぐ.md) (つなぐ) and [結ぶ](結ぶ.md) (むすぶ) can describe connecting or tying things, with different patterns.

切れる (きれる), "be cut off," can contrast with an established connection:
電話が切れた (でんわがきれた), "the call was disconnected."

## Distinctive meaning

つながる describes the connection or its coming about, not necessarily someone's deliberate connecting action.
The result pattern XがYにつながる can express an outcome, but does not by itself prove that X is the only cause of Y.
