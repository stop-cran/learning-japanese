# さっさと (さっさと) — quickly; without hesitation

## Meaning

さっさと describes doing something promptly or briskly, without lingering or hesitation. The final と belongs to this adverbial form.

さっさと荷物をまとめて出発した。（さっさとにもつをまとめてしゅっぱつした。） — We quickly packed our things and set off.

It can also convey indifference or a cold manner. Context matters: prompt action is not necessarily considerate action.

彼は私を残してさっさと行ってしまった。（かれはわたしをのこしてさっさといってしまった。） — He went straight off, leaving me behind.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[早速](早速.md) さっそく and [すぐに](すぐに.md) emphasize acting without delay. [きびきび](きびきび.md) describes brisk, efficient behavior. [ぐずぐず](ぐずぐず.md), dawdling or hesitating, provides a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

さっさと concerns the manner or promptness of an action, not just the measurable speed of a vehicle. Nor should every occurrence be translated as unfriendly: the second example's departure and abandonment supply context that the first example does not.
