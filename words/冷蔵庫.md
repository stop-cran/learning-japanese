# 冷蔵庫 (れいぞうこ) — refrigerator

## Meaning

冷蔵庫 means **refrigerator or fridge**, an appliance for keeping food and drink chilled.

牛乳を冷蔵庫に入れてください。 (ぎゅうにゅうをれいぞうこにいれてください。) — Please put the milk in the refrigerator.

With 入れる (いれる), the item takes を and the destination takes に. 冷蔵庫の中 (れいぞうこのなか) means “inside the refrigerator.”

## How the kanji combine

[冷](../kanji/冷.md) contributes coldness; 蔵 contributes storage. Together 冷蔵 (れいぞう) is refrigerated storage. 庫 adds a storage place or unit. The on readings combine as レイ + ゾウ + コ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

冷凍庫 (れいとうこ) is a freezer, which keeps things frozen rather than merely chilled. A household refrigerator may include a freezer compartment, but the functions remain distinct.

## Distinctive meaning

冷蔵庫 names an appliance or storage unit, not a generally cold room. The English shortening “fridge” and “refrigerator” both correspond to this same ordinary Japanese noun; 冷蔵庫 is not unusually formal.
