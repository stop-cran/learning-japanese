# ご飯 (ごはん) — cooked rice; meal

## Meaning

ご飯 means **cooked rice** or, more broadly, **a meal**.

- ご飯をもう少しください。（ごはんをもうすこしください。）— A little more rice, please.
- 一緒にご飯を食べませんか。（いっしょにごはんをたべませんか。）— Would you like to have a meal together?
- 今日の朝ご飯はパンです。（きょうのあさごはんはパンです。）— Today's breakfast is bread.

The last example shows that the meal sense does not require rice.

## How the kanji combine

This spelling has only one kanji: [飯](../kanji/飯.md), read ハン, "cooked rice; meal."
It is preceded by the conventional **polite prefix ご**, also represented by 御 in other spellings.
Thus ご + はん gives ごはん; ご is not a reading of 飯 and is not a separate word meaning food.

The article uses `kango` because the base uses the on reading ハン, not the native reading めし.
This is a prefix plus a Sino-Japanese base, not a two-kanji compound or an on+kun reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 米（こめ）means rice grains, usually before cooking; ご飯 is the ordinary choice for cooked rice.
- 飯（めし）can mean the same rice or meal, but sounds more informal and often masculine or rough.
- [食事](食事.md)（しょくじ）means "meal" or "having a meal"; it does not specifically mean cooked rice.

There is no natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

ご飯 is ordinary everyday vocabulary, not a highly formal way of referring only to another person's food.
Here ご is a conventional polite or beautifying addition: it makes the expression polite without assigning
the meal to anyone. You can use ご飯 for your own meal too.
Whether it means rice or a whole meal depends on the situation.
