# 屋根 (やね) - roof

## Meaning

屋根 is a roof, the covering over a building or similar structure. It is a noun. Use building + の屋根 to say whose roof it is, and 屋根の上 (やねのうえ) for "on top of the roof."

- 家の屋根を修理しました。 (いえのやねをしゅうりしました。) - We repaired the roof of the house.
- 屋根に雪が積もっています。 (やねにゆきがつもっています。) - Snow has accumulated on the roof.

In the first example, を marks what was repaired; in the second, に marks the surface where the snow has collected.

## How the kanji combine

[屋](../kanji/屋.md) is associated with a building, and 根 with a root or base. The native readings や + ね form the established word for a roof. The written association is not a literal description of the roof as a root, nor an explanation of the word's historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

天井 (てんじょう) is the ceiling viewed from inside a room. [屋上](屋上.md) (おくじょう) is the rooftop area or top of a building. These terms refer to related locations, not identical structures.

## Distinctive meaning

A sloping tiled roof is a 屋根 even when it is not an accessible rooftop space. Looking up indoors, however, you usually describe the room's 天井 rather than automatically calling the visible surface its 屋根.
