# 値 (ね) - price; cost

## Meaning

値, read ね here, is a price or cost. It is a noun: 値が上がる (ねがあがる), "the price rises," and 値を下げる (ねをさげる), "lower the price."

- 野菜の値が上がりました。 (やさいのねがあがりました。) - Vegetable prices rose.
- 店は商品の値を下げました。 (みせはしょうひんのねをさげました。) - The shop lowered the price of the goods.

The first sentence makes the price the subject with が. The second uses を because someone actively changes it.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [値](../kanji/値.md) writes the whole native noun ね. There is no compound to divide. Reading the character requires attention to the word and context; this article teaches the price reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[値段](値段.md) (ねだん) and [価格](価格.md) (かかく) also name price. They overlap substantially with 値 in money contexts. [価値](価値.md) (かち) concerns value or worth, which may be discussed without specifying a selling price.

## Distinctive meaning

値 in expressions such as 値が高い (ねがたかい), "be expensive," concerns the amount charged. It does not by itself prove that the item is useful, well made, or valuable to a particular person. A price can rise without the item's quality changing.
