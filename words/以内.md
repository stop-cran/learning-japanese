# 以内 (いない) -- within

## Meaning

以内 follows a stated limit: a duration, distance, budget, or other measured amount. It means staying within that limit, including the boundary amount.

- 二日以内に返事をください。 (ふつかいないにへんじをください。) -- Please reply within two days.
- 百字以内で書いてください。 (ひゃくじいないでかいてください。) -- Please write in one hundred characters or fewer.

Keep the unit before 以内: 二日以内, not just 二以内. に commonly marks a time limit; で can mark a limit within which a task is performed.

## How the kanji combine

[以](../kanji/以.md), read い, helps establish a reference boundary. [内](../kanji/内.md), read ない, means inside. Together they place the amount on the inside of the stated limit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

以下 (いか) means "at most" or "no greater than." 未満 (みまん) means "less than" and excludes its boundary. These distinctions matter in numerical instructions.

## Distinctive meaning

The reference point for a time limit comes from context. 三日以内 (みっかいない) is within three days, not automatically "before the third day of the month."
