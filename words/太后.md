# 太后 (たいこう) — empress dowager; queen mother

## Meaning

太后 is an empress dowager or queen mother. JMdict glosses it as “empress dowager; queen mother,” and Digital Daijisen defines it as a title for a grand empress dowager (太皇太后) or an empress dowager (皇太后).

- 太后は若い皇帝を支えました。 (たいこうはわかいこうていをささえました。) — The empress dowager supported the young emperor.
- 歴史ドラマで、太后が大きな力を持つ場面がありました。 (れきしドラマで、たいこうがおおきなちからをもつばめんがありました。) — In a historical drama, there was a scene in which the empress dowager held great power.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[太](../kanji/太.md) can be added to the titles of honored persons, and [后](../kanji/后.md) means empress. Digital Daijisen lists 太后 under that sense of 太. Together, they form a title for an empress dowager.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[女王](女王.md) (じょおう) is a queen or female monarch; JMdict's second sense is a queen consort (entry 1345020). 皇太后 こうたいごう, “Empress Dowager; Queen Mother” (JMdict entry 1280450), has the same meaning as 太后, and 太皇太后 たいこうたいごう is a grand empress dowager (JMdict entry 2177070).

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen defines 太后 through two other titles, 太皇太后 and 皇太后. 皇后 こうごう, which JMdict glosses as “empress (consort)” (entry 1280390), is a different title.
