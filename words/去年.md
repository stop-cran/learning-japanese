# 去年 (きょねん) — last year

## Meaning

去年 means the calendar year before the current one.

- 去年、日本へ行きました。（きょねん、にほんへ いきました。）— I went to Japan last year.
- 去年の冬は寒かったです。（きょねんの ふゆは さむかったです。）— Last winter was cold.

## How the kanji combine

[去](../kanji/去.md) キョ conveys going away or passing; [年](../kanji/年.md) ネン means year. 去 specifies which year: the one that has passed. This is an on-plus-on compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 昨年 さくねん also means "last year" and is a more formal alternative.
- [今年](今年.md) ことし means "this year."
- [来年](来年.md) らいねん, "next year," is the forward-looking contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

去年 does not mean any rolling twelve-month period before today; it identifies the preceding calendar year. It can stand directly as a time expression, as in 去年、日本へ行きました; no に is needed in that example.

The source also records the old reading こぞ. Use きょねん for the everyday word taught here.
