# これから (これから) - from now on; after this

## Meaning

これから marks the period starting now or after the current activity. It can introduce an immediate plan or refer more broadly to the future.

- これから昼ご飯を食べます。 (これからひるごはんをたべます。) - I am going to have lunch now.
- これからも日本語を勉強します。 (これからもにほんごをべんきょうします。) - I will keep studying Japanese from now on, too.

も adds the idea of continuing or including the future period.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is kana. これ, "this," combines with から, "from," to point forward from the present situation. Rare kanji spellings are unnecessary for ordinary use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

今から (いまから), "starting now," often overlaps for immediate actions. それから, "after that," follows a previously mentioned event rather than necessarily starting at the speaker's present moment.

## Distinctive meaning

これから does not always promise an action lasting indefinitely. In the lunch example, it simply places the next activity after the present moment. Use the surrounding sentence to establish the time span.
