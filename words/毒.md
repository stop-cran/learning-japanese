# 毒 (どく) - poison; harmful influence

## Meaning

毒 is a noun meaning poison or venom. It can also express harm or a harmful influence without referring to a literal toxic substance.

- このキノコには毒があります。 (このキノコにはどくがあります。) - This mushroom contains poison.
- 夜更かしは体に毒です。 (よふかしはからだにどくです。) - Staying up late is bad for your health.

毒がある describes having poison or toxicity. In 体に毒, に identifies what is adversely affected. The second example is an ordinary expression about harm, not a claim that staying awake creates a particular chemical poison.

## How the kanji combine

毒 is the entire word, read with the on reading ドク. Learn it as a noun rather than trying to split the single character into an English definition. It also appears in 有毒 (ゆうどく), "poisonous," and 無毒 (むどく), "nonpoisonous."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[害](害.md) (がい), "harm," is broader than a poisonous substance. [薬](薬.md) (くすり), "medicine," can form a contextual contrast, but medicines and poisons are not logically exclusive categories. 無毒 negates toxicity in a description; it does not mean "medicine."

## Distinctive meaning

The English words "poison" and "venom" can both translate literal 毒; the Japanese noun alone does not specify how a substance reaches the body. Figurative expressions also need context. Learn the construction around 毒 instead of treating every occurrence as a physical material.
