# 灯油 (とうゆ) - kerosene; paraffin fuel

## Meaning

灯油 is kerosene, also called paraffin in some varieties of English.
It is a petroleum fuel used in equipment such as kerosene heaters and lamps.

- 冬に備えて灯油を買いました。
  (ふゆにそなえてとうゆをかいました。) - I bought kerosene in preparation for winter.
- このストーブは灯油を使います。
  (このストーブはとうゆをつかいます。) - This heater uses kerosene.

灯油ストーブ (とうゆストーブ) specifies a kerosene heater.
Not every appliance called a ストーブ uses this fuel.

## How the kanji combine

[灯](../kanji/灯.md) contributes a lamp or light, and [油](../kanji/油.md) oil:
oil associated with lighting. The on readings are トウ + ユ.
The name does not restrict its modern uses to lamps.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[石油](石油.md) (せきゆ) broadly names petroleum and also has a colloquial
kerosene use. [オイル](オイル.md) likewise has a recorded kerosene sense,
alongside other oils. [ガソリン](ガソリン.md) is gasoline, a different fuel.

## Distinctive meaning

灯油 is not an interchangeable name for every [油](油.md) (あぶら).
Cooking oil and gasoline are not 灯油. The English "paraffin" in this entry
means the liquid fuel, not a claim that 灯油 is paraffin wax.
Use the product's actual name rather than inferring it from "oil."
