# 乗り物 (のりもの) — vehicle

## Meaning

乗り物 is a vehicle or means of transport that people ride in or on, such as a bus, bicycle, or train.

子どもは乗り物の絵を描いています。 (こどもはのりもののえをかいています。) — The child is drawing pictures of vehicles.

## How the kanji combine

[乗](../kanji/乗.md) comes from 乗る (のる), "ride." Its ます-stem 乗り joins [物](../kanji/物.md) (もの), "thing": something to ride. 乗物 is another spelling of the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

車 (くるま) usually means a car and is narrower. 交通機関 (こうつうきかん) is a more formal expression for means or systems of transport. 徒歩 (とほ), traveling on foot, is a practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

乗り物 is not limited to road vehicles; boats and aircraft also fit. In an amusement-park context it can mean a ride or attraction. The context identifies whether someone is discussing transport or entertainment rather than a strict technical class of machine.
