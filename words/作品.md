# 作品 (さくひん) - creative work

## Meaning

作品 is a created work or piece, such as a painting, novel, film, or musical
composition. It is a noun. 作者の作品 (さくしゃのさくひん) identifies an author's
or artist's work; 作品を作る describes creating a piece.

- 美術館で学生の作品を見ました。 (びじゅつかんでがくせいのさくひんをみました。) - I saw students' work at the art museum.
- これは彼女の最初の作品です。 (これはかのじょのさいしょのさくひんです。) - This is her first work.

The medium can remain unstated when the context supplies it. A 作品 need not
have been made by a famous professional.

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) contributes making or creating, and [品](../kanji/品.md)
contributes an item or product. Together they name a created piece. The on
readings are サク + ヒン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[製品](製品.md) (せいひん) emphasizes a manufactured product.
[商品](商品.md) (しょうひん) presents something as merchandise for sale.
One object can be discussed as a 作品 and as a 商品, but those descriptions
focus on different aspects.

## Distinctive meaning

English "work" also means employment, effort, or a task. Those are not automatic
uses of 作品: "I have work tomorrow" does not refer to a creative piece.
Similarly, a musician's composition and the act of performing it are related but
different. 作品 identifies the work, not every occurrence of a performance.
