# 児童 (じどう) - children; juvenile

## Meaning

児童 means children. In a school context it especially refers to elementary-school children;
it also occurs in expressions about children's books and welfare.
This card focuses on that practical use, not the age definitions of a particular law.

- 児童たちが校庭で遊んでいる。
  (じどうたちがこうていであそんでいる。) - The children are playing in the schoolyard.
- この図書館には児童向けの本が多い。
  (このとしょかんにはじどうむけのほんがおおい。) - This library has many books for children.

## How the kanji combine

[児](../kanji/児.md) and [童](../kanji/童.md) both contribute the idea of a child.
They form a near-synonym compound reinforcing that category, rather than describing
two separate people. The on readings combine as じ + どう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[子供](子供.md) (こども) and [子](子.md) (こ) overlap in meaning,
but can also identify someone's child through a family relationship.
[少年](少年.md) (しょうねん) can overlap in the broader juvenile sense, though its everyday meaning is boy.
[生徒](生徒.md) (せいと) usually identifies junior-high or high-school pupils in school terminology;
its broader sense of a learner receiving instruction can overlap with the elementary-school use of 児童.
[大人](大人.md) (おとな), adult, gives a useful age-category contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

児童 identifies children as a category, especially in the school-related uses above.
Do not infer that it is an honorific way to refer to another person's son or daughter,
or that it always imposes one universal legal age boundary.
The English gloss "juvenile" here describes a young person, not an accusation of misconduct.
