# 見下ろす (みおろす) - look down from above; look down on

## Meaning

見下ろす means to look down at something from a higher position, or to command a view from above.

- 展望台から町を見下ろしました。 (てんぼうだいからまちをみおろしました。) - I looked down over the town from the observation deck.
- この部屋から海が見下ろせます。 (このへやからうみがみおろせます。) - You can look down over the sea from this room.

It also has a contemptuous "look down on; despise" sense: 人を見下ろす (ひとをみおろす) can mean treating someone as inferior.

It is a transitive godan verb: 見下ろします, 見下ろして, 見下ろさない.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) supplies み, "see," and [下](../kanji/下.md) supplies おろす, the downward association. The written division is 見 + 下ろす.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[眺める](眺める.md) (ながめる), "gaze at; look out over," need not involve a downward view. 見下す (みくだす) expresses contempt; that overlap does not mean 見下ろす is restricted to physical height.

## Distinctive meaning

English "overlook" is ambiguous. A room can overlook the sea, but 見下ろす is not the ordinary verb for accidentally overlooking an error. Conversely, do not insist that looking down on a person must describe their physical position: the contempt sense is also recorded.
