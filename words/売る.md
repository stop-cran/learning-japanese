# 売る (うる) — sell

## Meaning

売る means to sell something. The seller is the subject, and the thing sold is marked with を.

- この店は本を売っています。 (このみせはほんをうっています。) — This shop sells books.
- 古い車を売りました。 (ふるいくるまをうりました。) — I sold my old car.

## How the kanji combine

This is one kanji plus okurigana, not a compound: [売](../kanji/売.md) supplies う, and る completes the dictionary form.

売る is a godan verb: 売ります うります, 売らない うらない, 売った うった. The kana ending changes when the verb is conjugated.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 売れる うれる describes goods selling, often selling well. 本が売れる (ほんがうれる) means "books sell"; 本を売る (ほんをうる) means "sell books."
- [買う](買う.md) かう, "buy," describes the other side of the transaction.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 売る for the seller's action, not the buyer's. The ordinary commercial sense does not require a shop: an individual can sell a used car or book. Figurative senses exist, but the examples here concern selling goods.
