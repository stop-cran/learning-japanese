# 道順 (みちじゅん) - route; directions

## Meaning

道順 is the route to follow or directions explaining how to get somewhere.

- 駅までの道順を教えてください。 (えきまでのみちじゅんをおしえてください。) - Please tell me how to get to the station.
- 友達に地図で道順を説明しました。 (ともだちにちずでみちじゅんをせつめいしました。) - I used a map to explain the route to my friend.

## How the kanji combine

[道](../kanji/道.md), "way," supplies the native reading みち, and [順](../kanji/順.md), "order; sequence," supplies on-reading ジュン. This native-first mixed reading is called yutōyomi; it is not an all-on compound ドウジュン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[コース](コース.md) overlaps in "route; course" but has other course-related uses. [方向](方向.md) (ほうこう) can be a direction or course without giving all the steps. [順番](順番.md) (じゅんばん) is an order or turn, not by itself directions to a destination.

## Distinctive meaning

道順 is about the way to proceed, not necessarily a travel schedule with times and dates. Learn 駅までの道順, "the route to the station," rather than treating every English "itinerary" as an exact substitute.
