# 覆う (おおう) - cover; conceal

## Meaning

覆う means to cover something, often spreading across it or hiding it from view. It is a transitive godan verb: 覆います (おおいます), 覆わない (おおわない), 覆った (おおった).

Mark what is covered with を. The covering material or means can take で.

- 布で机を覆いました。 (ぬのでつくえをおおいました。) - I covered the desk with a cloth.
- 山は雪に覆われています。 (やまはゆきにおおわれています。) - The mountain is covered with snow.

The second example uses the passive 覆われる: the mountain receives the covering, and 雪に identifies what covers it.

## How the kanji combine

覆 writes the native stem おお, followed by the inflecting ending う. There is no compound to split. Notice the two お sounds in the reading: おおう, not おう. The negative changes the ending to わない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[包む](包む.md) (つつむ) emphasizes wrapping or enclosing. [隠す](隠す.md) (かくす) emphasizes keeping something from view or knowledge; it need not involve a physical covering. [被る](被る.md) (かぶる) is useful for wearing something over the head.

## Distinctive meaning

Covering need not be intentional: snow or clouds can cover a landscape. 覆う describes the covering relationship, not necessarily an act of deception. Removing a covering reverses the situation, but there is no single opposite verb that fits cloth, snow, clouds, and every figurative use.
