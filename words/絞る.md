# 絞る (しぼる) - wring; squeeze; narrow down

## Meaning

絞る is to wring or squeeze something, for example to remove liquid from a cloth. It is a transitive godan verb: **タオルを絞る**, "wring out a towel."

- ぬれたタオルを絞りました。
  (ぬれたタオルをしぼりました。) - I wrung out the wet towel.
- レモンを搾って、汁を加えます。
  (レモンをしぼって、しるをくわえます。) - Squeeze the lemon and add the juice.

**搾る** has the same reading. JMdict particularly associates 絞る with wringing cloth and 搾る with extracting liquid, including juice or milk. These are spelling preferences, not absolute sense restrictions; 絞る is also used for squeezing a lemon.

A useful extension is narrowing a range or focus:

- 候補を三つに絞りました。
  (こうほをみっつにしぼりました。) - We narrowed the candidates down to three.

Here を marks what is narrowed and に the resulting limit. Forms include 絞ります (しぼります), **絞って** (しぼって), and 絞らない (しぼらない).

## How the kanji combine

絞 is the single character for the native verb, with る as okurigana. Its meaning includes constricting or wringing. 搾 emphasizes squeezing out in this use. Neither spelling is a two-character compound, and neither supplies a new independent pronunciation for the verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[押す](押す.md) (おす) is pushing or pressing more broadly; 絞る often involves wringing, extracting, or concentrating, rather than any pressure at all. [締める](締める.md) (しめる) overlaps with tightening, such as tightening a drawstring.

[減らす](減らす.md) (へらす), "reduce," overlaps with reducing a range or amount. 候補を絞る additionally foregrounds selecting a narrower set.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the object together with these further dictionary-recorded uses:

- 知恵を絞る (ちえをしぼる) - rack one's brains.
- 声を絞る (こえをしぼる) - strain one's voice.
- 音量を絞る (おんりょうをしぼる) - turn down the volume.
- 袋の口を絞る (ふくろのくちをしぼる) - tighten the mouth of a bag.
- レンズを絞る (レンズをしぼる) - stop down a lens.

The word can also mean scold severely or put someone through hard training. 絞られる (しぼられる) can therefore mean "get a severe scolding," not literally be wrung like a towel. These extensions do not make every use of English "press" or "train" interchangeable with 絞る. The dictionary's extortion/exploitation sense, as well as specialized bow, sumo, and go uses, is outside this practical introduction.
