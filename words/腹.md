# 腹 (はら) — belly; abdomen

## Meaning

腹 means the belly or abdomen. English sometimes translates it as "stomach," but that need
not identify the internal digestive organ precisely.
The word names the body region that can ache or feel full.

- 食べすぎて、腹が痛くなりました。
  (たべすぎて、はらがいたくなりました。)
  — I ate too much and got a stomachache.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [腹](../kanji/腹.md) represents the whole native noun はら.
Its on reading フク appears in 空腹 (くうふく), "hunger; an empty stomach."
Do not read the standalone word here as ふく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お腹 (おなか) is a polite expression for the same bodily region.
Learn its complete reading rather than adding お to はら.
胃 (い) names the stomach organ; use that distinction when the anatomy matters.
[背中](背中.md) (せなか), "back," names a different body region, not a grammatical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

腹が痛い (はらがいたい), "my belly hurts," reports abdominal pain without diagnosing which
organ causes it. For a polite everyday expression, お腹が痛い (おなかがいたい) is useful.
The kana change to なか belongs to that expression; it is not a replacement reading to apply
to every compound containing 腹.
