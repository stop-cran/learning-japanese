# おもちゃ - toy

## Meaning

おもちゃ is a toy or plaything. It is a noun; use おもちゃで遊ぶ
(おもちゃであそぶ) for "play with toys," with で marking what is used in play.

- 子どもがおもちゃで遊んでいます。 (こどもがおもちゃであそんでいます。) - A child is playing with a toy.

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is the ordinary spelling. A dictionary may give 玩具 for おもちゃ,
but that whole-word reading is not a combination of regular readings of
the two characters. 玩具 can also be read がんぐ in a more formal vocabulary item.

## Synonyms and antonyms

遊び (あそび) means play or amusement, the activity rather than an object.
人形 (にんぎょう), "doll," names one possible kind of toy. There is no direct
everyday antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

おもちゃの車 (おもちゃのくるま) means "a toy car": の connects the two nouns.
The word can figuratively describe someone or something treated as a plaything,
but its basic learner use is a physical object for play.
