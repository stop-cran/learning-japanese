# 弔問 (ちょうもん) — condolence call

## Meaning

弔問 is a visit to a bereaved family to express condolences after a death. JMdict marks it as a formal or literary word; 弔問する means to make such a visit.

- 祖父が亡くなり、近所の人たちが弔問に訪れた。 (そふがなくなり、きんじょのひとたちがちょうもんにおとずれた。) — When my grandfather died, neighbors came to offer their condolences.
- 社長は、亡くなった取引先の会長の遺族を弔問した。 (しゃちょうは、なくなったとりひきさきのかいちょうのいぞくをちょうもんした。) — The company president paid a condolence call on the family of a business partner’s late chairman.

## How the kanji combine

[弔](../kanji/弔.md) (ちょう) means to mourn, and [問](../kanji/問.md) (もん) here means to visit. 弔問客 (ちょうもんきゃく) are the visitors who come to offer condolences.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[見舞い](見舞い.md) (みまい) is a visit or message to someone who is ill or has suffered a misfortune; 弔問 is specifically after a death. A [葬式](葬式.md) (そうしき) is the funeral ceremony itself, not the visit.

## Distinctive meaning

In the second example, the object marked with を is the bereaved family who receive the visit, not the person who died.
