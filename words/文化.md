# 文化 (ぶんか) - culture

## Meaning

文化 means culture: learned practices, ideas, arts, and ways of living shared
within a society or group. It is a noun, not a verb meaning "to become cultured."

- 日本の文化について勉強しています。 (にほんのぶんかについてべんきょうしています。) - I am studying Japanese culture.

について means "about." The phrase 日本の identifies which culture is under
discussion without claiming that everyone in Japan behaves identically.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) contributes writing or cultivated learning, and
[化](../kanji/化.md) suggests transformation. Learn the established compound
as "culture," not the literal action "change writing." ブン + カ gives ぶんか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

習慣 (しゅうかん), "custom; habit," can name one part of a culture.
文明 (ぶんめい), "civilization," often focuses on developed social and
material systems. These words overlap but have different scope.

## Distinctive meaning

Use の before another noun: 文化の違い (ぶんかのちがい), "cultural differences."
文化 can describe contemporary and local practices, not just traditional
arts. The same spelling also names the historical Bunka era, a separate
proper-name use.
