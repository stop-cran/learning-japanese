# 完了 (かんりょう) - completion

## Meaning

完了 means completion of a task or process. 完了する can describe either a process finishing, with **が**, or someone completing it, with **を**. 完了しました is useful in status messages.

- 登録が完了しました。 (とうろくがかんりょうしました。) - Registration is complete.
- 今日中に作業を完了します。 (きょうじゅうにさぎょうをかんりょうします。) - We will complete the work today.

完了している describes a completed state. A message saying 完了 tells you that the relevant operation has finished, not merely that it has started.

## How the kanji combine

[完](../kanji/完.md) contributes completeness, and 了 contributes finishing. The two reinforce the idea of bringing a process to completion. The on readings are カン + リョウ; りょう contains a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[完成](完成.md) (かんせい) emphasizes a finished product or completed whole, such as a building or painting. [終わる](終わる.md) (おわる) is a broader verb for ending. 未完了 (みかんりょう), "not yet completed," provides the direct status contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

An activity can end because it was interrupted; 完了 instead presents the required process as complete. That does not independently certify the quality of its result. A successful upload may be complete even if the uploaded document still needs revision.
