# 逃がす (にがす) - let go; let get away

## Meaning

逃がす is a transitive godan verb. It can mean deliberately setting something
free, or failing to catch or retain something. The released or missed target
takes を.

- 捕まえた虫を逃がしました。 (つかまえたむしをにがしました。) - I released the insect I had caught.
- 魚を逃がしてしまいました。 (さかなをにがしてしまいました。) - I let the fish get away.

The second sentence need not describe an intentional release.
It is also possible to いい機会を逃がす (いいきかいをにがす),
"miss a good opportunity." Useful forms are 逃がします and 逃がして.

## How the kanji combine

[逃](../kanji/逃.md) contributes escape or getting away; it writes に here,
followed by がす. Learn the complete reading にがす.
逃す (のがす) is a related word with overlapping missed-opportunity uses,
not an instruction to read 逃がす as のがす.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[放す](放す.md) (はなす), "release; let go," and [解放](解放.md)
(かいほう), "release; liberation," overlap with deliberate release.
[逃げる](逃げる.md) (にげる) instead describes the escaping subject:
魚が逃げた (さかながにげた), "the fish escaped."
[失う](失う.md) (うしなう) can describe losing something, including a chance.

## Distinctive meaning

The shared event does not give 逃げる and 逃がす the same subject and object.
Nor does English "let" prove intention: losing one's grip on a fish is different
from deciding to free it, but both can use 逃がす.
Do not extend its missed-chance sense to every misplaced possession.
