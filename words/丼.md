# 丼 (どんぶり) - deep bowl; rice-bowl dish

## Meaning

丼, also written どんぶり, names a deep food bowl, traditionally
a ceramic bowl. It also names a dish of rice with a topping such
as meat, fish, or vegetables.

- 丼にご飯をよそいました。 (どんぶりにごはんをよそいました。) - I served rice into the deep bowl.
- 昼ご飯に天丼を食べました。 (ひるごはんにてんどんをたべました。) - I had a tempura rice bowl for lunch.

The first sentence names the container. The second names the
prepared dish, not an act of eating the container itself.

## How the kanji combine

This is one character representing the whole word どんぶり.
The dictionary also records どん. Learn established dish names
such as 牛丼 (ぎゅうどん), "beef rice bowl," and 天丼
(てんどん), "tempura rice bowl"; do not infer every compound's
reading from a universal shortening rule.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ちゃわん](ちゃわん.md) is a rice bowl or, in a tea context,
a tea vessel. A どんぶり is typically larger and deeper than an
individual rice 茶わん. [ボール](ボール.md), also ボウル,
can mean a bowl such as a mixing bowl; the same spelling ボール
also represents the unrelated ball word.
[皿](皿.md) (さら) is a plate or dish rather than this bowl shape.

## Distinctive meaning

Whether 丼 names the vessel or a rice-bowl meal depends on the
setting. A menu item ending in 丼 identifies a dish; it does not
promise that the customer receives the bowl to take home.
