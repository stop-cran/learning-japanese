# 装置 (そうち) - device; apparatus

## Meaning

装置 is a device, apparatus, or installation set up for a function.
Learn it first as a noun: 測定装置 (そくていそうち), "measuring device," and 安全装置 (あんぜんそうち), "safety device."
Use 装置を使う (そうちをつかう) for using the apparatus.

- この装置で温度を測ります。 (このそうちでおんどをはかります。) - We measure temperature with this device.
- 新しい装置を工場に設置しました。 (あたらしいそうちをこうじょうにせっちしました。) - We installed a new device in the factory.

In the first example, で marks the instrument. In the second, を marks the thing installed and に its location.

## How the kanji combine

装 contributes equipping or fitting out, and [置](../kanji/置.md) placing or setting.
Their combined image is equipment arranged for use.
The on-readings are ソウ + チ; keep the long vowel in そう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[機械](機械.md) (きかい) is a machine.
装置 can describe a functional unit or setup, including something within a larger machine.
[道具](道具.md) (どうぐ) is a tool or implement, a broader everyday comparison.
[設備](設備.md) (せつび) can refer to facilities or equipment collectively.

## Distinctive meaning

装置 names what is installed; 設置 (せっち) names setting something in place.
Although JMdict also records a verbal use of 装置する, the noun-plus-設置する pattern above makes the distinction easy to learn.
The word does not itself specify size or whether electricity is required.
