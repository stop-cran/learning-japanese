# 諸島 (しょとう) - archipelago; group of islands

## Meaning

諸島 names a group of islands, or an archipelago. It refers to the group rather than one individual island.

- この諸島には小さな島がたくさんあります。 (このしょとうにはちいさなしまがたくさんあります。) - This archipelago has many small islands.
- 地図でその諸島を探しました。 (ちずでそのしょとうをさがしました。) - I looked for that group of islands on a map.

## How the kanji combine

[諸](../kanji/諸.md) contributes various or many, and [島](../kanji/島.md) supplies the head noun, islands.
This is a modifier-and-head combination: the islands are considered as a group.
The on readings ショ + トウ give しょとう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[島](島.md) (しま) names an island; 諸島 names a group of them.
These differ in scope, not as opposites. When referring to one island within the group, use 島 or that island's name rather than treating 諸島 as an interchangeable singular noun.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the whole reading しょとう, not しょしま. 島 has both トウ and しま in other words, but this compound selects トウ.
諸島 is a geographic grouping word; it does not by itself specify the size or climate of the islands.
The examples above describe an imagined group, not a claim about a particular named archipelago.
