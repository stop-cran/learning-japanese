# 大統領 (だいとうりょう) - president of a country

## Meaning

大統領 names a country's president. It is a noun and an official title, not the general word for any organization's chief executive.

- 大統領が演説をしました。 (だいとうりょうがえんぜつをしました。) - The president gave a speech.
- 彼女は大統領に選ばれました。 (かのじょはだいとうりょうにえらばれました。) - She was elected president.

The second sentence uses に for the office to which she was elected. 大統領の演説 (だいとうりょうのえんぜつ) is "the president's speech."

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes great or principal. 統 contributes uniting or governing, and 領 contributes leading. 大 qualifies the leadership expressed by 統領. The on readings are ダイ + トウ + リョウ. These component associations help remember the title, not determine a country's constitutional powers.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[首相](首相.md) (しゅしょう) is a prime minister, a different office. [社長](社長.md) (しゃちょう) is a company president. The English word "president" therefore needs context before choosing the Japanese title.

## Distinctive meaning

大統領 does not by itself tell you how the officeholder was selected or how much power the office has. JMdict also records a familiar vocative use resembling "boss!" or "big man!" That separate use is not evidence that 大統領 is an ordinary job title for a corporate CEO.
