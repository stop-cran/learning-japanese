# 作法 (さほう) - manners; etiquette

## Meaning

作法, read さほう, is manners or etiquette: an accepted way of behaving or performing
an action in a particular setting. It is a noun, used in Nの作法, "the etiquette of N,"
and 作法を学ぶ, "learn the proper forms."

- 茶道の作法を学んでいます。
  (さどうのさほうをまなんでいます。) - I am learning the etiquette of the tea ceremony.
- 食事の作法は国によって違います。
  (しょくじのさほうはくにによってちがいます。) - Dining etiquette differs from country to country.

The word can concern ordinary social occasions as well as traditional arts.
It does not itself identify one universal set of rules.

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) contributes doing or making, and [法](../kanji/法.md) contributes
a method or rule. Together they suggest prescribed ways of doing things.
The on readings here are サ + ホウ; keep the short さ in さほう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[礼儀](礼儀.md) (れいぎ) also concerns etiquette and courtesy.
作法 draws attention to particular forms or procedures.
[方法](方法.md) (ほうほう), "method," is broader and need not concern polite behavior.

## Distinctive meaning

Use さほう for the etiquette meaning taught here.
The dictionary also records a "way of making" sense and the reading さくほう,
but its etiquette sense is specifically tied to さほう.
Do not infer that both readings can be substituted in the dining example.
