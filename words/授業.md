# 授業 (じゅぎょう) — class, lesson

## Meaning

授業 means a lesson, class, or the activity of teaching in class. It can refer to the teaching session rather than the group of students.

- 九時に授業が始まります。 (くじにじゅぎょうがはじまります。) — Class starts at nine.
- 今日は日本語の授業があります。 (きょうはにほんごのじゅぎょうがあります。) — There is a Japanese class today.

## How the kanji combine

授 ジュ (give, impart) + [業](../kanji/業.md) ギョウ (work, activity): an on + on compound. Together they name the activity of imparting instruction. This meaning aid does not make 授業 a word for every kind of work.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 講義 こうぎ means a lecture, especially a teacher's structured explanation; not every 授業 is a lecture.
- [勉強](勉強.md) べんきょう means study and can happen alone outside class.
- [教室](教室.md) きょうしつ can mean the classroom, the place rather than the teaching session. There is no natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

授業があります means there is a class scheduled. It does not merely say that a classroom or a group of classmates exists. From the student's perspective, use 授業を受ける じゅぎょうをうける, "to take or attend a class"; 授業をする usually describes conducting the lesson.
