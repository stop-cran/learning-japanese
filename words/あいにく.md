# あいにく (あいにく) - unfortunately; sorry, but

## Meaning

あいにく introduces an inconvenient or disappointing circumstance: "unfortunately"
or "I'm sorry, but..." It works as an adverb, and あいにくの can modify a noun.

- あいにく、田中は外出しております。 (あいにく、たなかはがいしゅつしております。) - I'm sorry, but Tanaka is out.
- 旅行の日は、あいにくの雨でした。 (りょこうのひは、あいにくのあめでした。) - Unfortunately, it rained on the day of our trip.

The first sentence could be said to a caller by someone in Tanaka's office.
あいにく explains why the caller's wish cannot be met; it does not itself mean
"out" or express a refusal.

## How the kanji combine

This native word is usually written in kana. 生憎 is a dictionary-listed kanji
spelling, but the characters are not a transparent compound that explains the
modern meaning. Learn あいにく as a whole expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[残念](残念.md) (ざんねん) describes regret or disappointment. あいにく instead
frames a circumstance as unfortunate for the situation at hand.
[幸い](幸い.md) (さいわい), "fortunately," gives a useful opposite perspective.

## Distinctive meaning

あいにく can soften unwelcome information, but it is not a general replacement
for an apology after causing harm. Its focus is the awkward circumstance,
such as someone's absence or bad weather, rather than accepting responsibility.
