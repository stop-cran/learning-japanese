# 腐る (くさる) - rot; spoil

## Meaning

腐る is an intransitive godan verb for rotting, decaying, or going bad. The thing that deteriorates takes が. 腐っている (くさっている) can describe the resulting spoiled or rotten condition.

- 冷蔵庫に入れなかった肉が腐ってしまいました。 (れいぞうこにいれなかったにくがくさってしまいました。) - The meat I did not put in the refrigerator spoiled.
- この木は根が腐っています。 (このきはねがくさっています。) - This tree's roots are rotten.

The verb describes deterioration, not an instruction to make something rot. For food, it is different from merely saying that the food has cooled down.

## How the kanji combine

腐 carries the decay meaning, and る completes the native verb くさる. Despite ending in る, it follows the godan pattern: 腐らない (くさらない), 腐ります (くさります), 腐った (くさった).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[古い](古い.md) (ふるい) means old; old food or wood is not automatically rotten. [新鮮](新鮮.md) (しんせん), "fresh," supplies a useful contrast when discussing food, though it has different grammar.

## Distinctive meaning

This card teaches physical spoilage and decay. The word has figurative uses too, but a smell, appearance, or age alone is not what the verb grammatically means: 腐っている asserts a deteriorated condition.
