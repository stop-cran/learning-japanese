# バッグ (バッグ) - bag

## Meaning

バッグ is a noun for a bag used to carry belongings. **バッグに入れる** (バッグにいれる) means "put into a bag"; **バッグから出す** (バッグからだす) means "take out of a bag."

- 財布をバッグに入れました。 (さいふをバッグにいれました。) - I put my wallet in my bag.
- 旅行用のバッグを買いました。 (りょこうようのバッグをかいました。) - I bought a bag for traveling.

In the first example, を marks what is put inside and に marks the destination. The bag is not the object being inserted.

## How the kanji combine

Katakana represents the borrowed English word "bag." The small ッ marks a consonant hold before グ: **バッグ**. Keep it when writing and pronouncing this form. There is no kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鞄](鞄.md) (かばん) overlaps with bags used to carry belongings. [袋](袋.md) (ふくろ) is another broad word for a bag or sack, including simple packaging. These categories overlap rather than dividing all objects by one fixed material.

## Distinctive meaning

バッグ does **not** mean a software bug. That separate word is **バグ**, without the small ッ. Compare バッグを買う, "buy a bag," with バグを直す (バグをなおす), "fix a software bug." Similar English source words do not make the Japanese pronunciations or meanings interchangeable.
