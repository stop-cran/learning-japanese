# 割合に (わりあいに) - comparatively

## Meaning

割合に is an adverb meaning comparatively or relatively.
It assesses something against a relevant standard or expectation,
which can remain understood.

- この部屋は割合に広いです。 (このへやはわりあいにひろいです。) - This room is comparatively spacious.
- 今日は割合に涼しいです。 (きょうはわりあいにすずしいです。) - It is relatively cool today.

Place the whole adverb before the description. No second に is added.
The source's kana form わりあいに names this same adverb.

## How the kanji combine

[割](../kanji/割.md) contributes a share or proportion, and
[合](../kanji/合.md) a relation or fitting together.
The native base わりあい is followed by に in this established adverb.
The characters remain part of its written form even though the source row is kana-only.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[比較的](比較的.md) (ひかくてき) and [割と](割と.md) (わりと)
also make relative assessments.
[割合](割合.md) (わりあい) has a proportion/ratio noun use and an
adverbial use; this card specifically teaches the に form.

## Distinctive meaning

割合に does not specify a percentage or always mean "a little."
Something can be comparatively spacious without being spacious by every standard.
Do not transfer the noun's mixing-ratio construction directly into this adverb:
二対一の割合で (にたいいちのわりあいで) means "in a two-to-one ratio,"
not "comparatively two to one."
