# 飛ぶ (とぶ) — fly; soar

## Meaning

飛ぶ (とぶ) means to fly or soar. It describes what the flying thing does,
not an action performed on a direct object. This is called an intransitive use.

- 飛行機が飛んでいます。 (ひこうきがとんでいます。) — An airplane is flying.
- 鳥が空を飛んでいます。 (とりがそらをとんでいます。) — A bird is flying through the sky.

In the second sentence, を marks the space traversed. It does not make 空, "sky," an object that the bird causes to fly.
The て-form is 飛んで (とんで), and the past is 飛んだ (とんだ).
This is a godan verb: the polite form is 飛びます (とびます).

## How the kanji combine

[飛](../kanji/飛.md) writes と, and ぶ completes the native verb.
The same kanji has the on reading ヒ in 飛行機 (ひこうき), "airplane."
Learn とぶ as a whole verb, not as the on reading plus an ending.

## Synonyms and antonyms

飛ばす (とばす), "make or send something flying," is the transitive partner in this use.
It takes the thing being sent flying as its object, unlike the flying subject of 飛ぶ.
For "jump; leap," JMdict especially associates とぶ with the spelling 跳ぶ.
That is a spelling preference, not a claim that the two spellings divide every possible use without overlap.
For the source list's "hop" sense: ウサギがぴょんぴょん跳んでいます。
(ウサギがぴょんぴょんとんでいます。) — A rabbit is hopping along.

## Distinctive meaning

This article focuses on flight and the related jumping or hopping sense; the dictionary also records other senses of とぶ.
Seeing 飛 in a longer expression does not guarantee literal travel through the air:
for example, 飛ばす can also mean skipping or omitting something.
