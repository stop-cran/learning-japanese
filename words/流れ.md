# 流れ (ながれ) - flow; current; course

## Meaning

流れ is a flow or current, such as water moving through a river. It can also name the course of events or the steps in a process. **Nの流れ** identifies what is flowing or progressing.

- この川は流れが速いです。 (このかわはながれがはやいです。) - This river has a fast current.
- 最初に、作業の流れを説明します。 (さいしょに、さぎょうのながれをせつめいします。) - First, I will explain the workflow.

人の流れ (ひとのながれ) is a flow of people, while 時間の流れ (じかんのながれ) is the passage of time.

## How the kanji combine

[流](../kanji/流.md) contributes flowing. 流れ is a noun related to [流れる](流れる.md) (ながれる), "flow," with れ retained in its written form. It is not a two-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[川](川.md) (かわ) names a river; 流れ can name the movement of its water. [順番](順番.md) (じゅんばん) concerns order or turns, whereas 作業の流れ can describe how the steps connect in a process.

## Distinctive meaning

The physical-flow image extends naturally to events and procedures, but 流れ remains a noun. Compare 水が流れる, "water flows," with 水の流れ, "the flow of water." English "current" meaning "present-day" is a different use and is not translated with this noun.
