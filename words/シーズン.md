# シーズン - season

## Meaning

シーズン means a season: a division of the year, a period associated with an activity, a sporting season, or a season of a television program. It is a noun.

- もうすぐスキーのシーズンです。 (もうすぐスキーのシーズンです。) - The ski season is almost here.
- このドラマの第２シーズンを見ています。 (このドラマのだいにシーズンをみています。) - I am watching the second season of this drama.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English season. The long mark in シー represents a long vowel; there are no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[季節](季節.md) きせつ overlaps for seasons of the year. [時期](時期.md) じき is a period or appropriate time more broadly. A television シーズン is not simply a weather season.

## Distinctive meaning

シーズン is not confined to sport, nor does it always mean one of spring, summer, autumn, and winter. The surrounding noun identifies which activity or series supplies the period. シーズン中 (シーズンちゅう) means during the season; シーズン前 (シーズンまえ) means before it.
