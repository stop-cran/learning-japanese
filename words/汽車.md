# 汽車 (きしゃ) - steam train

## Meaning

汽車 means a steam locomotive or steam train. It appears naturally in descriptions
of older railways, stories, and steam-train excursions.

- 子どもたちは汽車の写真を見ています。 (こどもたちはきしゃのしゃしんをみています。) - The children are looking at photographs of a steam train.

## How the kanji combine

汽 contributes steam or vapor, and [車](../kanji/車.md) means vehicle.
The on-readings キ + シャ give きしゃ: a steam-powered railway vehicle.
The whole word does not refer to an ordinary car.

## Synonyms and antonyms

列車 (れっしゃ) is the general word for a train. 電車 (でんしゃ) specifically
names an electric train and is common for everyday urban rail travel.
These are transport distinctions, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Older usage can call a long-distance train 汽車 even without specifying steam.
In some rural contexts the word also refers to non-electric or diesel trains.
Those uses do not make "steam-powered" a reliable technical description of
every vehicle a speaker calls 汽車.
