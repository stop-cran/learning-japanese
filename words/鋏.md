# 鋏 (はさみ) - scissors

## Meaning

鋏 means scissors or shears. It is a noun and is often written in kana as はさみ or ハサミ. When describing cutting, mark the tool with で and the material being cut with を.

- 鋏で紙を切りました。 (はさみでかみをきりました。) - I cut the paper with scissors.
- この鋏はよく切れます。 (このはさみはよくきれます。) - These scissors cut well.

In the first sentence, 切る (きる) is transitive. The second uses 切れる (きれる) to describe the scissors' cutting performance. Japanese does not require the noun to change form to match English "a pair of scissors."

## How the kanji combine

鋏 is a single character writing the native word はさみ. Its metal component suits a cutting tool, but the full reading belongs to the word as a whole. Kana spelling does not make ハサミ an English loanword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ナイフ](ナイフ.md) is a knife, a different cutting tool. 包丁 (ほうちょう) is a kitchen knife. These are practical alternatives for some tasks, not exact synonyms or opposites of scissors.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse はさみ with the action verb 挟む (はさむ), "put between; pinch." The noun names a tool; はさむ alone does not mean "cut with scissors." The basic sense taught here covers scissors and shears, without requiring every dictionary use of the same headword.
