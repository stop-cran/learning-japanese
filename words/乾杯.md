# 乾杯 (かんぱい) - cheers; celebratory toast

## Meaning

乾杯 is a toast, and 乾杯する means to make or join in one.
As an exclamation, 乾杯! means "Cheers!"
Use Nに乾杯 for a toast to someone or something.

- 二人の幸せに乾杯！
  (ふたりのしあわせにかんぱい！) - A toast to the couple's happiness!
- 食事の前に、みんなで乾杯しました。
  (しょくじのまえに、みんなでかんぱいしました。) - We all made a toast before the meal.

The first example names what the toast celebrates with に.
The second describes the shared action with みんなで.

## How the kanji combine

[乾](../kanji/乾.md) contributes dry, and
[杯](../kanji/杯.md) a cup or drinking vessel.
The traditional association is emptying the cup.
The established reading is かんぱい, with ぱい here rather than はい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

乾杯 is the useful Japanese exclamation when raising a drink in a toast.
English "toast" can also mean browned bread or browning food, but those
are different meanings, not definitions of 乾杯.
[飲む](飲む.md) (のむ), "drink," is broader and does not by itself
specify a celebratory toast.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary records drinking a glass dry as well as the celebratory use.
That association is not an instruction to empty a glass at once:
the inspected encyclopedia explicitly says this is not necessary.
The word alone does not tell a guest how much to drink.
乾盃 is a rare alternative spelling; 乾杯 is the spelling taught here.
