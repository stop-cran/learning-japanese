# 生 (せい) - life; also なま for raw

## Meaning

**生 (せい)** is a noun for life or living, considered as a concept.

- 生と死について考えました。 (せいとしについてかんがえました。) - I thought about life and death.

**生 (なま)** belongs to a separate dictionary entry. It means raw or uncooked, and can also describe something unprocessed or unedited. Use 生の before a noun, or 生で for "in a raw state."

- この魚は生では食べません。 (このさかなはなまではたべません。) - I do not eat this fish raw.

In drink orders, なま can abbreviate 生ビール (なまビール), draft/unpasteurized beer:

- 生を一杯お願いします。 (なまをいっぱいおねがいします。) - One draft beer, please.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) is a single character, but these are distinct words and constructions. セイ is an on reading; なま is a native reading. The metadata keeps せい as the assigned primary reading, not as the pronunciation of the food or beer examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[命](命.md) (いのち) also means life and can focus on a living being's life. 死 (し), "death," contrasts with 生 (せい). For raw food, 焼いた (やいた), "cooked/grilled," supplies a contextual contrast; it is unrelated to the life/death opposition.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read a beer order as せい. The community source attached the beer/raw gloss to a せい row as well as a なま row; this article preserves the readings but separates their dictionary-supported meanings. 生のデータ (なまのデータ), "raw data," illustrates the unprocessed sense rather than something alive.
