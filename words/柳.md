# 柳 (やなぎ) - willow tree

## Meaning

柳 is a willow, a tree or shrub of the genus *Salix*. It can also refer specifically to a weeping willow. The broader word does not mean that every willow has long hanging branches.

- 川のそばに柳があります。 (かわのそばにやなぎがあります。) - There is a willow beside the river.
- 庭に柳を植えました。 (にわにやなぎをうえました。) - I planted a willow in the garden.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [柳](../kanji/柳.md) writes the native noun やなぎ. Botanical writing also uses ヤナギ. In the separate poetic term [川柳](川柳.md) せんりゅう, 柳 instead has the on reading リュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[木](木.md) き, tree or wood, is broader. [杉](杉.md) すぎ, Japanese cedar, and [桐](桐.md) きり, paulownia, name other kinds of trees, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also lists 楊 and 楊柳 as spellings of やなぎ. It restricts the alternative reading ようりゅう to 楊柳; that is not another pronunciation of standalone 柳 in this family. Its specific weeping-willow sense is restricted to やなぎ / ヤナギ rather than ようりゅう.

A separate entry 柳 りゅう names a traditional Chinese lunar mansion, a division of the sky. Do not substitute that reading when talking about an ordinary willow.
