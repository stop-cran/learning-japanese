# 解説 (かいせつ) - explanation; commentary

## Meaning

解説 is an explanation or commentary that helps an audience understand a topic, work, or event. 解説する is transitive: the topic being explained takes を.

- 先生が詩の意味を解説しました。 (せんせいがしのいみをかいせつしました。) - The teacher explained the meaning of the poem.
- テレビで試合の解説を聞きました。 (テレビでしあいのかいせつをききました。) - I listened to commentary on the match on television.

解説を読む (かいせつをよむ) means "read the explanation/commentary"; 解説を聞く means "listen to it."

## How the kanji combine

[解](../kanji/解.md) contributes unraveling or making clear; [説](../kanji/説.md) contributes explaining. The near-related elements reinforce explanation. Their on readings are カイ + セツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[説明](説明.md) (せつめい), "explanation," overlaps widely. 解説 is useful for explanatory treatment or commentary on material such as a poem or match. This is not an absolute rule that every 解説 is longer or more expert than every 説明.

## Distinctive meaning

Sports 解説 can explain play or strategy rather than merely announce each action. The word itself does not guarantee that a commentary is correct, exhaustive, or easy to understand. Use the object and situation to choose between English "explanation" and "commentary."
