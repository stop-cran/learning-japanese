# 落ち着く (おちつく) - calm down; settle

## Meaning

落ち着く is an intransitive godan verb meaning to calm down or settle. It can describe regaining composure, a situation becoming stable, or settling into a place.

- 少し休んだら、気持ちが落ち着きました。 (すこしやすんだら、きもちがおちつきました。) - After resting a little, I felt calmer.
- 引っ越しが終わり、やっと新しい家に落ち着きました。 (ひっこしがおわり、やっとあたらしいいえにおちつきました。) - The move is over, and I have finally settled into the new house.

The forms include 落ち着きます (おちつきます), 落ち着いて (おちついて), and 落ち着かない (おちつかない).

It can also mean that an arrangement is settled: 結局、この案に落ち着きました (けっきょく、このあんにおちつきました), "In the end, we settled on this proposal."

## How the kanji combine

[落](../kanji/落.md) writes お in 落ち, and [着](../kanji/着.md) writes つ in 着く. The native combination suggests coming to rest in a settled state, not necessarily physically falling. 落着く and 落ちつく are other spellings of おちつく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あわてる](あわてる.md), "become flustered," is a useful contrast for composure. [安定](安定.md)する (あんていする) overlaps in becoming stable, and [決まる](決まる.md) (きまる) in an arrangement becoming settled.

[静か](静か.md) (しずか) describes quietness; [安心](安心.md) (あんしん) emphasizes relief or freedom from worry. Regaining composure does not always mean becoming silent or resolving every worry.

## Distinctive meaning

落ち着いた before a noun can describe a calm person or subdued colors: 落ち着いた色 (おちついたいろ), "a subdued color." The dictionary also recognizes fitting or harmonizing with something, overlapping with [似合う](似合う.md) (にあう), but this is not the only meaning of the verb.

Do not replace every English "settle" with 落ち着く. Settling a dispute actively can be [治める](治める.md) or [収める](収める.md), both read おさめる; paying an amount due can be [納める](納める.md) (おさめる). The complete construction determines the intended sense.
