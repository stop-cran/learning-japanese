# 奇妙 (きみょう) - strange; peculiar

## Meaning

奇妙 means strange, odd, or peculiar. It is a na-adjective: 奇妙な音 (きみょうなおと), "a strange sound"; 奇妙に聞こえる (きみょうにきこえる), "sound strange"; 奇妙だ, "be strange." Despite its English translations, it does not take an い-adjective ending.

- 隣の部屋から奇妙な音が聞こえました。 (となりのへやからきみょうなおとがきこえました。) - I heard a strange sound from the next room.
- 彼の説明は少し奇妙でした。 (かれのせつめいはすこしきみょうでした。) - His explanation was a little peculiar.

The second sentence evaluates how the explanation seemed; it does not establish that it was false.

## How the kanji combine

奇 contributes unusualness, and 妙 contributes strangeness or an unexpected quality here. Their related meanings reinforce one another. The on readings are キ + ミョウ. 妙 can carry other meanings elsewhere; those do not all belong in this compound's translation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[変](変.md) (へん) overlaps with "odd." [不思議](不思議.md) (ふしぎ) can emphasize mystery or wonder. [珍しい](珍しい.md) (めずらしい) concerns rarity or novelty, which need not feel strange. [普通](普通.md) (ふつう) supplies a contextual contrast of ordinariness.

## Distinctive meaning

奇妙 describes something as outside ordinary expectations. It need not mean dangerous, dishonest, or supernatural. Calling a person's behavior 奇妙 can sound critical, so the word is not a neutral substitute for "unusual" in every introduction.
