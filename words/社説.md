# 社説 (しゃせつ) - editorial; leading article

## Meaning

社説 is an editorial or leading article expressing a newspaper's or magazine's institutional opinion or position. It is not simply the first news story printed on a page.

- 新聞の社説を読みました。
  (しんぶんのしゃせつをよみました。) - I read the newspaper's editorial.
- その社説は教育の問題を取り上げています。
  (そのしゃせつはきょういくのもんだいをとりあげています。) - That editorial addresses problems in education.

社説で主張する (しゃせつでしゅちょうする) means to argue or advocate something in an editorial. 社説 is the text, not the editor who writes it.

## How the kanji combine

[社](../kanji/社.md) supplies the publishing organization, and [説](../kanji/説.md) an argument or stated view. The association is the organization's expressed position. Read シャ + セツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[記事](記事.md) (きじ) is an article or news story more broadly; not every 記事 is a 社説. 論説 (ろんせつ), argumentative commentary, overlaps with editorial writing. [論文](論文.md) (ろんぶん) is a paper or thesis, not automatically a newspaper's institutional editorial.

## Distinctive meaning

A 社説 can discuss facts, but its defining role is presenting a position or judgment. Conversely, an individual person's opinion column is not automatically the publication's 社説. English "leader" in the dictionary means a leading article here, not a person in command.
