# 万 (まん) - ten thousand

## Meaning

万 is the numerical unit 10,000. Japanese groups large numbers around this unit rather than only around thousands.

- この自転車は二万円です。 (このじてんしゃはにまんえんです。) - This bicycle costs twenty thousand yen.
- この町には三万人が住んでいます。 (このまちにはさんまんにんがすんでいます。) - Thirty thousand people live in this town.

For exactly ten thousand, say 一万 (いちまん). 二万 is 20,000; 十万 (じゅうまん) is 100,000; 百万 (ひゃくまん) is one million.

## How the kanji combine

[万](../kanji/万.md) alone uses the on reading まん. Its older form 萬 may still appear in formal monetary writing, but 万 is the ordinary modern form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

千 (せん) is one thousand, while 億 (おく) is one hundred million. These are different numerical units, not synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

万 is not itself the counter for people or money. Add the appropriate unit afterward: 万円 uses 円 for yen, and 万人 uses 人 for people. Keep both the multiplier and the following unit when interpreting an amount.
