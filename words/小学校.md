# 小学校 (しょうがっこう) - elementary school

## Meaning

小学校 means elementary or primary school, the school stage before junior high school. It can name the institution or its building.

妹は小学校に通っています。 (いもうとはしょうがっこうにかよっています。) - My younger sister attends elementary school.

小学校の前で待ちます。 (しょうがっこうのまえでまちます。) - I will wait in front of the elementary school.

In the first example, に marks the school attended.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md), "small," is attached to 学校, "school," formed from [学](../kanji/学.md), "learning," and [校](../kanji/校.md), "school." The reading is しょうがっこう, with both the long しょう and the small っ in がっこう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

学校 (がっこう) is the general word for school. 中学校 (ちゅうがっこう) is junior high school. 小学生 (しょうがくせい) means an elementary-school pupil, not the school.

## Distinctive meaning

小学校 is an established educational category, not any physically small school. A large 小学校 remains an elementary school. The noun takes の when modifying another noun: 小学校の先生 (しょうがっこうのせんせい), "an elementary-school teacher."
