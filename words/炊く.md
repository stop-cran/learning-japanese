# 炊く (たく) - cook rice or grains

## Meaning

炊く is a transitive godan verb whose practical standard use is cooking
rice or other grains. The food takes を.

- 毎朝、ご飯を炊きます。
  (まいあさ、ごはんをたきます。) - I cook rice every morning.
- 米を洗ってから炊いてください。
  (こめをあらってからたいてください。) - Wash the rice before cooking it.

The polite form is 炊きます, the て-form 炊いて, and the past 炊いた.
The く ending changes to いて/いた here; it does not produce 炊くて.

## How the kanji combine

炊 writes the native verb's た, followed by the inflecting く.
That spelling boundary is not the whole conjugation stem: 炊きます
contains the ます-stem たき.
JMdict marks 煮く as search-only, not a preferred spelling to copy.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[料理](料理.md) (りょうり) and [炊事](炊事.md) (すいじ) cover food
preparation or cooking more generally.
[作る](作る.md) (つくる) and [こしらえる](こしらえる.md) also have
food-preparation uses. Not every food made with these words is 炊いた food.
[焚く](焚く.md), also たく, concerns burning fuel or stoking/heating;
the shared pronunciation does not make the spellings interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not generalize the rice use into an unrestricted standard verb for
boiling water or cooking any food. JMdict's broader boil/simmer/stew sense
is explicitly Kansai dialect. Recognize that regional use without removing
its qualification or assuming all speakers use it identically.
