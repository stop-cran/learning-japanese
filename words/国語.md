# 国語 (こくご) - national language; Japanese school subject

## Meaning

国語 is a noun meaning a country's national language. In a Japanese school context, it names Japanese language study as a school subject, including reading and writing.

- 一時間目は国語です。 (いちじかんめはこくごです。) - The first lesson is Japanese.
- 国語の授業で作文を書きました。 (こくごのじゅぎょうでさくぶんをかきました。) - We wrote an essay in Japanese class.

Use 国語の + noun for things associated with the subject: 国語の教科書 (こくごのきょうかしょ), "a Japanese-language textbook."

## How the kanji combine

[国](../kanji/国.md) means "country," and [語](../kanji/語.md) means "language; words." 国 modifies 語: the language of a country. The reading is こく + ご.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[日本語](日本語.md) (にほんご) identifies the Japanese language without requiring the school-subject or national-language viewpoint. 日本語を勉強しています, "I am studying Japanese," is a useful general statement for a language learner. 国語 is not its opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The context of a timetable strongly favors the school-subject interpretation. Do not automatically replace 日本語 with 国語 whenever English says "Japanese." In a broader discussion, the "national language" meaning depends on which country is under discussion; it is not simply a different spelling of the name 日本語. The two examples here deliberately use the Japanese classroom setting to make the reference clear.
