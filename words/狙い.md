# 狙い (ねらい) - aim; target; intention

## Meaning

狙い is a noun for an aim or target. It can concern aiming a weapon
or what someone intends to achieve.
狙いを定める (ねらいをさだめる) means setting or taking aim.

- 的に狙いを定めました。
  (まとにねらいをさだめました。) - I took aim at the target.
- この計画の狙いを説明してください。
  (このけいかくのねらいをせつめいしてください。) - Please explain the aim of this plan.

The first example has に for the target and を for the aim being set.
The second asks about purpose, not where a weapon is pointing.

## How the kanji combine

狙 supplies ねら and い completes the native noun.
It is related to [狙う](狙う.md) (ねらう), "aim at; aim for."
狙い is not a する verb that automatically replaces 狙う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[目的](目的.md) (もくてき), "purpose," and
[目標](目標.md) (もくひょう), "goal; target," overlap with the intended-result use.
[対象](対象.md) (たいしょう) identifies the object or target of an activity.
[つもり](つもり.md) expresses intention in its own constructions.
Their overlapping English labels do not remove differences of grammar or focus.

## Distinctive meaning

狙いが外れた (ねらいがはずれた) can mean that the aim missed
or the intended result did not materialize.
An intention is not a guarantee of success.
For a plan's purpose, 狙い presents what it is directed toward;
it need not imply a secret or malicious motive.
