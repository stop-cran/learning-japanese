# 草 (くさ) — grass; weeds

## Meaning

草 means grass or other small, nonwoody plants. Depending on the setting, English may call the plants "weeds". The word itself does not always say that they are unwanted.

- 庭に草が生えています。  
  にわにくさがはえています。  
  "Grass is growing in the garden."
- 庭の草を抜きました。  
  にわのくさをぬきました。  
  "I pulled the weeds in the garden."

## How the kanji combine

[草](../kanji/草.md) is a single kanji with the native reading くさ. In compounds it can be read そう, as in 雑草 (ざっそう), "weeds".

## Synonyms and antonyms

雑草 explicitly identifies weeds. 芝生 (しばふ) means a lawn or turf, rather than any grasslike plant. 木 (き), "tree", is a useful plant-category contrast, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

草 can refer collectively to vegetation; it does not need an English-style plural ending. Learn it with verbs such as 生える (はえる), "grow", and 抜く (ぬく), "pull out", to make the intended meaning clear.
