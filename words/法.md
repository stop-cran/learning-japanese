# 法 (ほう) - law; method

## Meaning

法 is a noun for law or a principle. As a suffix, ～法 appears in the names of laws or acts.
It also has a method sense, so a word ending in 法 is not necessarily the name of a statute.

- 私たちは法を守らなければなりません。 (わたしたちはほうをまもらなければなりません。) - We must obey the law.
- 自分に合う勉強法を探しています。 (じぶんにあうべんきょうほうをさがしています。) - I am looking for a study method that suits me.

For the legal suffix, 道路交通法 (どうろこうつうほう), "Road Traffic Act," is a concrete example.
In 勉強法, by contrast, 法 means a way of studying.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [法](../kanji/法.md) supplies the complete word, using the on reading ホウ.
Within a compound, preceding material identifies what law or method is meant.
Do not translate each occurrence as "Act" without checking the whole expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[法律](法律.md) (ほうりつ) explicitly names law or laws.
[方法](方法.md) (ほうほう), "method," is a useful comparison for the nonlegal sense.
These are two related areas of meaning, not interchangeable replacements in every compound.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 法 both as a standalone noun and as an element in longer words.
The source-list gloss "Act" points to the legal naming use, which this card teaches explicitly.
The broader word also supports method expressions; those do not describe legally required behavior.
There is no single opposite covering both uses.
