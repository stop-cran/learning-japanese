# 優劣 (ゆうれつ) — superiority or inferiority; relative merits

## Meaning

優劣 is a noun for superiority or inferiority, or relative merits: whether one side is better or worse than another in a relevant respect. It does not mean inferiority alone.

- 二人の能力に優劣はない。 (ふたりののうりょくにゆうれつはない。) — Neither person's ability is superior to the other's.
- どちらの製品もよくできていて、優劣をつけがたいです。 (どちらのせいひんもよくできていて、ゆうれつをつけがたいです。) — Both products are well made, and it is hard to say which is better.

優劣をつける (ゆうれつをつける) means deciding which is better. 優劣をつけがたい says that this is difficult; the second example explicitly describes both products favorably.

## How the kanji combine

[優](../kanji/優.md) contributes superiority here, rather than gentleness; [劣](../kanji/劣.md) contributes inferiority. This opposite pair spans both sides of the evaluation. The on readings are ユウ + レツ: keep the long ゆう in ゆうれつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[比較](比較.md) (ひかく), "comparison," names the act of comparing, which need not rank things as better or worse.

[質](質.md) (しつ), "quality; inherent nature," can neutrally describe what something is like. 優劣 foregrounds relative superiority or inferiority; 質 can also be evaluated as good or bad, so these are not mutually exclusive categories.

## Distinctive meaning

Specify the relevant dimension, such as ability or product performance. 優劣はない says that neither side is superior in the relevant comparison, not that two people or products are identical in every respect. Avoid using the word as an unqualified judgment of a person's worth.
