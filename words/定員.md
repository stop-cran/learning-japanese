# 定員 (ていいん) - capacity; prescribed headcount

## Meaning

定員 is an established number of people: a prescribed complement of staff or students, or the capacity of a vehicle, room, or event.

- このエレベーターの定員は十人です。
  (このエレベーターのていいんはじゅうにんです。) - This elevator has a capacity of ten people.
- 講座は定員に達したため、募集を締め切りました。
  (こうざはていいんにたっしたため、ぼしゅうをしめきりました。) - We closed registration because the course had reached its capacity.

定員に達する (ていいんにたっする) means to reach the prescribed number. 定員を超える (ていいんをこえる) means to exceed it.

## How the kanji combine

[定](../kanji/定.md) contributes setting or fixing, and [員](../kanji/員.md) members or a number of people. Together they identify a set headcount. Read テイ + イン as ていいん, keeping both consecutive い sounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

人数 (にんずう) is the number of people more generally. [数](数.md) (かず) is a broader number or amount. 満員 (まんいん) describes being full of people, not the capacity figure itself.

## Distinctive meaning

A bus with a 定員 of forty need not currently have forty passengers. The word gives the prescribed or permitted number, not necessarily the actual attendance. Likewise, a recruitment quota does not tell you that every place has been filled.
