# 一家 (いっか) - a household; the whole family

## Meaning

一家 is a noun for a family or household considered as one group. 一家で means "as a family" or "with the whole family"; a surname can identify the household, as in 田中さん一家 (たなかさんいっか).

- 一家で北海道に引っ越しました。 (いっかでほっかいどうにひっこしました。) - The whole family moved to Hokkaido.
- 田中さん一家を夕食に招きました。 (たなかさんいっかをゆうしょくにまねきました。) - I invited the Tanaka family to dinner.

Here 一家 names the people, not the building they live in. 一家の生活 (いっかのせいかつ) means "the household's life" or "the family's livelihood."

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," modifies [家](../kanji/家.md), "house/family": one family unit. The on-reading combination has a small っ: いっか, not いちか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[家族](家族.md) (かぞく) also means family and can focus on family members. 一家 highlights the household as a unit, though the meanings overlap. [家](家.md) (いえ) can mean a house or home; use that when the physical building is your subject.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every English "a house" as 一家. English "household" is a better guide for this use. The word also has dictionary senses relating to a school or style and an organized-crime family, but those require their own contexts and are not implied by a family-dinner sentence.
