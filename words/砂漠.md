# 砂漠 (さばく) - desert

## Meaning

砂漠 is a desert, an arid region with very little rainfall. It is a noun:
砂漠の + noun describes something associated with a desert.

- 砂漠では水が貴重です。 (さばくではみずがきちょうです。) - Water is precious in the desert.
- 砂漠を車で横断しました。 (さばくをくるまでおうだんしました。) - We crossed the desert by car.

In the first example, で marks where the situation holds. In the second, を
marks the region crossed, while 車で identifies the means of transport.

## How the kanji combine

砂 contributes sand, and 漠 contributes a vast, barren expanse in this word.
The combination evokes a sandy expanse, but the geographical category is
not limited to landscapes covered in sand. The on readings are サ + バク.
沙漠 is a dictionary-listed rare spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

砂 (すな) is sand, not a desert. 砂浜 (すなはま) is a sandy beach; sharing sand
does not make the two landscapes interchangeable. An oasis contrasts with
the surrounding dry region, but is not a general antonym of 砂漠.

## Distinctive meaning

The word identifies a kind of region, not simply a hot day or an empty place.
Rainfall and aridity are more useful guides than assuming every desert must
be hot. In a broader discussion of [自然](自然.md) (しぜん), "nature," 砂漠
names one environment rather than an absence of nature.
