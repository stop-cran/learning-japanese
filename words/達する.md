# 達する (たっする) - reach; attain

## Meaning

達する means to reach a place, level, amount, or stage. A useful pattern is
**subject が endpoint に達する**. With quantities it often means that a total
has reached a particular figure. Learn the forms 達します (たっします),
達した (たっした), and 達しない (たっしない).

- 気温が三十五度に達しました。 (きおんがさんじゅうごどにたっしました。) - The temperature reached 35 degrees.
- 参加者の数は百人に達しました。 (さんかしゃのかずはひゃくにんにたっしました。) - The number of participants reached one hundred.

に marks the level reached, not the cause of the increase.

## How the kanji combine

[達](../kanji/達.md) contributes reaching or attaining. Its Sino-Japanese
element combines with する in the contracted form たっする.
There is no second kanji in the written word; keep the small っ in both
the dictionary form and 達します.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[到着](到着.md) (とうちゃく) names arrival at a destination.
[届く](届く.md) (とどく) can describe something reaching someone or a place.
達する is particularly useful for stated levels and totals. These overlap
in the broad idea of reaching, but their sentence patterns are not interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

Reaching a threshold does not necessarily mean going beyond it. 百人に達した
says that the count reached one hundred; it does not itself say "more than one
hundred." [限界](限界.md)に達する (げんかいにたっする), "reach the limit,"
similarly focuses on the endpoint. 達しなかった means it was not reached.
