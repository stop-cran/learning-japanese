# 年代 (ねんだい) - era; period of years

## Meaning

年代 is an age, era, or period of years. It can also concern when something
dates from. After a decade number, 年代 gives an English "-s" meaning:
1990年代 (せんきゅうひゃくきゅうじゅうねんだい), "the 1990s."

- この曲は1990年代に流行しました。 (このきょくはせんきゅうひゃくきゅうじゅうねんだいにりゅうこうしました。) - This song was popular in the 1990s.
- この建物が作られた年代を調べています。 (このたてものがつくられたねんだいをしらべています。) - We are researching the period when this building was built.

The second example does not promise that a specific day or month is known.

## How the kanji combine

[年](../kanji/年.md) contributes years, and [代](../kanji/代.md) contributes
an age or period. The combination frames time in terms of a period of years.
The on readings are ねん + だい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時代](時代.md) (じだい) also means an era and can emphasize its historical
character. [年齢](年齢.md) (ねんれい) is a person's age, especially as a
number. 同じ年代の人 (おなじねんだいのひと), "people of a similar age
group," need not have exactly the same age.

## Distinctive meaning

年代 can group years or people more broadly than a single date or birthday.
1990年 names one year; 1990年代 names the decade from 1990 through 1999.
That difference matters when identifying music, buildings, or historical events.
The word itself has no fixed duration without further context.
