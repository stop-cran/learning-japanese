# 難しい (むずかしい) — difficult; hard to do

## Meaning

難しい (むずかしい) describes something difficult to do, solve, or understand.
It is an i-adjective: 難しい問題 (むずかしいもんだい) is "a difficult problem."
The negative is 難しくない (むずかしくない), "not difficult"; the past is 難しかった (むずかしかった).

- この問題は難しいです。 (このもんだいはむずかしいです。) — This problem is difficult.
- 漢字を覚えるのは難しいです。 (かんじをおぼえるのはむずかしいです。) — Learning kanji is difficult.

In the second example, の turns "learn kanji" into the activity being judged difficult.

## How the kanji combine

There is one written kanji: [難](../kanji/難.md), difficulty or trouble.
In this word it writes むずか, and しい completes the native adjective.
The character's on reading ナン belongs to other words, such as 困難 (こんなん), "difficulty."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[易しい](易しい.md) (やさしい), "easy," gives the direct contrast for a task or explanation.
JMdict marks that easy adjective as usually written in kana, やさしい.
困難 overlaps with difficulty, but its grammar differs: 困難な問題 (こんなんなもんだい), not 困難い問題.

## Distinctive meaning

English "hard" can describe either a difficult task or a solid material.
難しい covers the difficulty side; 硬い (かたい) is appropriate for the hardness of stone or metal.
Calling a task 難しい does not by itself declare it impossible.
