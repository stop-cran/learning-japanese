# 務める (つとめる) - serve as; play a role

## Meaning

務める means to fulfill a role or serve in a position. Mark the role with を:
司会を務める (しかいをつとめる) is "act as the host," and 議長を務める (ぎちょうをつとめる) is "serve as chair."
The position need not be one's paid employment.

- 会議では私が司会を務めます。
  (かいぎではわたしがしかいをつとめます。) - I will act as the host at the meeting.
- 彼女は長年、委員長を務めました。
  (かのじょはながねん、いいんちょうをつとめました。) - She served as committee chair for many years.

## How the kanji combine

[務](../kanji/務.md) concerns duties or tasks. Here it writes the native verb つとめる, not an on-reading compound.
It is ichidan: 務めます, 務めた, 務めて, 務めない. The written ending is める.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勤める](勤める.md) (つとめる) usually foregrounds working for an employer:
会社に勤める (かいしゃにつとめる), "work for a company." The dictionaries record some overlap, so this is not an absolute spelling ban.
[努める](努める.md), also つとめる, instead concerns making an effort.
[就任](就任.md) (しゅうにん)する emphasizes taking office, rather than the whole period of serving.

辞める (やめる), "resign; quit," can mark the end of holding a post. There is no single opposite for every role.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn **役を務める** (やくをつとめる), "perform a role," alongside **会社に勤める**, "work for a company."
A person can do both, but the particles and what is being described differ.
Do not interpret 努める, "strive," as another employment spelling.
