# 続ける (つづける) — continue something

## Meaning

続ける means to keep something going or keep up an activity. It is transitive: the activity
being continued can be marked with を. It is an ichidan verb, meaning that forms such as
続けます (つづけます) and 続けた (つづけた) replace the final る.

- 毎日、日本語の勉強を続けています。
  (まいにち、にほんごのべんきょうをつづけています。)
  — I am keeping up my Japanese studies every day.

## How the kanji combine

[続](../kanji/続.md) supplies the continuation meaning; ける is the written kana ending in
続ける. This is a native Japanese verb, not an on-reading compound.
Learn the spelling つづける, with づ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

続く (つづく) is the intransitive partner: something continues.
Compare 雨が続いています (あめがつづいています), "the rain is continuing," with
勉強を続けています (べんきょうをつづけています), "I am continuing my studies."
やめる, "stop an activity," gives a useful contrast when someone decides not to continue.

## Distinctive meaning

The difference from 続く is not simply that 続ける lasts longer. 続ける presents someone as
maintaining the activity, while 続く presents the activity or situation as continuing.
Choose the verb and particle together rather than replacing が with を in an otherwise unchanged sentence.
