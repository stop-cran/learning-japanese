# 捕まる (つかまる) - be caught; be arrested

## Meaning

捕まる means to be caught or arrested. It is an intransitive godan verb, even though its English translation often looks passive: 捕まります, 捕まった, 捕まらない.

- 泥棒が警察に捕まりました。 (どろぼうがけいさつにつかまりました。) - The thief was caught by the police.
- 雨でタクシーがつかまりません。 (あめでタクシーがつかまりません。) - Because of the rain, I cannot get a taxi.

The thief takes が in the first sentence. In the second, the taxi takes が: the expression means that a taxi cannot be found or secured, not that it is being arrested.

## How the kanji combine

[捕](../kanji/捕.md) contributes catching or capturing. The native reading is つか before まる. In the capture sense, JMdict notes 捕まる as the usual spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[捕まえる](捕まえる.md) (つかまえる) is transitive: 泥棒を捕まえる, "catch a thief." [逃げる](逃げる.md) (にげる), "escape; run away," supplies a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The same dictionary entry includes "hold on to," usually written in kana or 掴まる: 手すりにつかまる (てすりにつかまる), "hold on to the handrail." Here に identifies the support held. This is not a passive arrest meaning. Check the noun, particle, and spelling together rather than treating every つかまる as "be caught."
