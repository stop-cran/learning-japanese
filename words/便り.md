# 便り (たより) - news; correspondence

## Meaning

便り is news, tidings, correspondence, or a letter from someone. Useful noun patterns include **person から便りがある**, "hear from someone," and **便りを出す**, "send a letter; write to someone."

- 海外にいる友達から便りが届きました。 (かいがいにいるともだちからたよりがとどきました。) - I received news from a friend living abroad.
- 母に久しぶりに便りを出しました。 (ははにひさしぶりにたよりをだしました。) - I wrote to my mother for the first time in a while.

The polite form お便り (おたより) is useful when referring to correspondence. As a suffix, the reading becomes **だより**, as in 学校だより (がっこうだより), "school newsletter."

## How the kanji combine

[便](../kanji/便.md) contributes communication or a message in this word. The native reading is たよ-, followed by り; do not substitute the on readings seen in words such as 便利.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[手紙](手紙.md) (てがみ) directly names a letter. [知らせ](知らせ.md) (しらせ) is news or a notification and can cover announcements. 頼り, also たより, concerns reliance or someone to depend on, not correspondence.

## Distinctive meaning

便り can emphasize hearing from someone rather than the physical sheet of paper. It does not necessarily mean public news reporting. The voiced suffix だより is a pronunciation change in this use, not a reason to change the standalone reading to だより.
