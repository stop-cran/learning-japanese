# 生存 (せいぞん) - survival; being alive

## Meaning

生存 is being alive or surviving. 生存する is intransitive: the living being
that survives takes が. 生存者 (せいぞんしゃ) means a survivor.

- 事故の後、三人の生存が確認されました。 (じこのあと、さんにんのせいぞんがかくにんされました。) - After the accident, three people were confirmed to be alive.
- この魚は冷たい水の中でも生存できます。 (このさかなはつめたいみずのなかでもせいぞんできます。) - This fish can survive even in cold water.

生存を確認する means confirming that someone is alive; を belongs to 確認する,
not to a transitive use of 生存する.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) contributes life, and [存](../kanji/存.md) contributes
continuing to exist. Together they concern existing alive. The on readings are
セイ + ゾン; learn せいぞん for this card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[生きる](生きる.md) (いきる) is the everyday verb live or be alive.
[存在](存在.md) (そんざい) is broader existence: an object or idea can exist without
being alive. [生命](生命.md) (せいめい) names life or living existence.
[死亡](死亡.md) (しぼう), death, is a useful contrast when reporting whether someone survived.

## Distinctive meaning

生存 focuses on remaining alive, not on someone's daily routine or address.
[生活](生活.md) (せいかつ) concerns living one's daily life. 生存 does not by itself
say that a survivor is healthy or unharmed, nor is it limited to humans.
