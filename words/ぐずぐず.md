# ぐずぐず — dawdling; grumbling; unsettled

## Meaning

Delaying or hesitating, complaining, or a state that does not settle properly.

- 出かける前にぐずぐずして、バスに遅（おく）れた。 — I dawdled before leaving and missed the bus.
- 子どもは片付（かたづ）けたくなくて、ぐずぐず言っていた。 — The child grumbled because they did not want to tidy up.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[だらだら](だらだら.md) overlaps in sluggish or drawn-out activity; [ぶうぶう](ぶうぶう.md) in complaints. [きびきび](きびきび.md) offers a brisk-action contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

ぐずぐずする often carries criticism of delay, not just slow physical speed. The word also describes unsettled weather or looseness. Those uses should not be translated as someone literally “grumbling.”
