# 英語 (えいご) — English language

## Meaning

英語 means the English language, not an English person or the country England.
It includes varieties of English spoken around the world.

- 英語を勉強しています。（えいごをべんきょうしています。） — I am studying English.
- 英語で名前を書いてください。（えいごでなまえをかいてください。） — Please write your name in English.

## How the kanji combine

[英](../kanji/英.md) エイ identifies English here, and [語](../kanji/語.md) ゴ means language.
This is an on+on modifier-and-head compound: "English" specifies which language.
Compare [日本語](日本語.md) にほんご, "Japanese."

## Synonyms and antonyms

英会話（えいかいわ）, "English conversation," is narrower: it emphasizes conversation rather than all uses of English.
外国語（がいこくご） means "foreign language," which is not a synonym specifically for English.
Language names such as 日本語 are alternatives, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 英語を with verbs that take the language as their object, such as studying it.
Use 英語で to mark English as the language used to say or write something.
英語 is not restricted to British English merely because 英 can also refer to Britain.
