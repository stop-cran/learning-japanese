# せかせか — hurriedly; restlessly

## Meaning

せかせか describes moving or acting in a hurried, restless way.

- 朝から予定が詰まり、彼はせかせか歩いていた。
  — With a full schedule from the morning, he was walking about in a hurry.
- 時間を気にして、母はせかせかと家事を済ませた。
  — Watching the time, my mother hurried through the housework.

## Synonyms and antonyms

せかせか emphasizes agitation or restlessness in the hurried movement. せっせと describes
diligent activity and does not necessarily imply impatience.

## Distinctive meaning

The word describes the manner of acting, not simply having many tasks to do.
