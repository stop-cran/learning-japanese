# ひっそり — quiet and secluded

## Meaning

ひっそり describes a place or life that is quiet, still, or removed from attention.

- 森の奥に、小さな小屋がひっそり建っている。
  — A small cottage stands quietly deep in the forest.
- 彼は町の片隅で、ひっそり暮らしている。
  — He lives quietly in a corner of town.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ひっそり suggests quietness or unobtrusive presence, unlike a lively or bustling atmosphere.

## Distinctive meaning

It can describe a place's stillness or a person's modest, inconspicuous way of living.
