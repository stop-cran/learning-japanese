# 活用 (かつよう) - practical use; conjugation

## Meaning

活用 has two useful meanings. In ordinary contexts, it is "putting something to good use":
Nを活用する names the resource used. In grammar, it means conjugation or inflection,
the changes in a word's form.

- 空いている部屋を教室として活用します。 (あいているへやをきょうしつとしてかつようします。) - We will put the empty room to use as a classroom.
- 日本語の動詞は活用します。 (にほんごのどうしはかつようします。) - Japanese verbs conjugate.

The first use is transitive; the room takes を. In the second, the verb is the thing that changes form.
動詞の活用 (どうしのかつよう) is "verb conjugation."

## How the kanji combine

[活](../kanji/活.md) suggests life or activity, and [用](../kanji/用.md) means use.
Together they convey making something actively useful. This is a component-based memory aid,
not a literal definition of grammatical conjugation. The readings are カツ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[使用](使用.md) (しよう) is use of something; [利用](利用.md) (りよう) also means using or taking advantage of it.
活用 emphasizes putting available resources or qualities to effective use.
These comparisons do not replace the technical grammar meaning.

## Distinctive meaning

Study the two constructions separately. アプリを活用する concerns making useful use of an app,
not "conjugating an app." In language study, 活用 can concern adjectives as well as verbs,
so the English classroom term "verb conjugation" is narrower than the full technical use.
