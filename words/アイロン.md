# アイロン (アイロン) - clothes iron

## Meaning

アイロン is a noun for an iron used to press clothes.
The useful action pattern is Xにアイロンをかける, "iron X":
the garment takes に, and アイロン takes を.
The same noun can also refer to a hair iron when the hair-styling context makes that clear.

- シャツにアイロンをかけました。 - I ironed the shirt.
- アイロンを使った後は、電源を切ってください。 (アイロンをつかったあとは、でんげんをきってください。) - Please switch off the iron after using it.

The first example describes the action; アイロンがある simply says that an iron is available.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana, with four morae: ア・イ・ロ・ン.
There is no long-vowel mark or small ッ in the spelling.
No kanji decomposition is needed for the assigned form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鉄](鉄.md) (てつ) is iron as a metal; it is not the name of this household appliance.
[洗濯](洗濯.md) (せんたく) means washing clothes, a related household activity but not ironing.
ヘアアイロン explicitly identifies a hair-styling iron.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not transfer all meanings of the English word "iron" to アイロン.
For clothes, the phrase アイロンをかける is more useful than assuming a bare noun automatically becomes a verb.
The object and context distinguish clothing care from hair styling.
Neither sense means an iron supplement or iron ore.
