# 大使 (たいし) - ambassador

## Meaning

大使 is an ambassador, especially a representative of a country in diplomatic relations. It is a noun. A country plus の can identify the country represented, and 大使になる means "become an ambassador."

- 日本の大使が会議に出席しました。 (にほんのたいしがかいぎにしゅっせきしました。) - Japan's ambassador attended the meeting.
- 彼女は大使に任命されました。 (かのじょはたいしににんめいされました。) - She was appointed ambassador.

In the second example, に identifies the office to which she was appointed. 大使の仕事 (たいしのしごと) means "an ambassador's work."

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes great or high-ranking, and [使](../kanji/使.md) contributes a messenger or envoy. Together they name a senior representative. The reading is タイ + シ; the first character is not read だい in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[代表](代表.md) (だいひょう) is a representative more broadly. Not every representative is an ambassador. 外交官 (がいこうかん) means a diplomat and names a broader professional category.

[大使館](大使館.md) (たいしかん) is an embassy, the institution or premises rather than the ambassador as a person.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the person, office, and workplace separate when translating. 大使に会う (たいしにあう) means "meet the ambassador"; 大使館に行く (たいしかんにいく) means "go to the embassy." The shared first two characters do not make the two nouns interchangeable. There is no useful ordinary antonym for this role.
