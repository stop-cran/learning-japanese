# 臭い (くさい) - smelly; stinking

## Meaning

臭い, read **くさい**, is an i-adjective meaning smelly or stinking. It evaluates a smell negatively rather than simply naming a scent.

- この靴は臭いです。 (このくつはくさいです。) - These shoes smell bad.
- ごみが臭くなりました。 (ごみがくさくなりました。) - The rubbish has become smelly.

Before a noun, use 臭い directly: 臭い靴 (くさいくつ), "smelly shoes." The negative is 臭くない (くさくない), and the past is 臭かった (くさかった). Do not add な.

## How the kanji combine

臭 contributes the idea of an unpleasant smell. In this native adjective, the character is read くさ and the final い is written in kana. There is no compound to split.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[におい](におい.md), "smell," is a noun, not this adjective. The spelling 臭い can also represent that noun when read **におい**, especially for unpleasant smells; it belongs to a different dictionary entry. いいにおいがする, "smell good," is a useful practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Readings and grammar distinguish the two identities: 臭い靴 (くさいくつ), "smelly shoes," but 靴の臭い (くつのにおい), "the smell of the shoes." The source list's noun gloss does not describe くさい. Calling a person 臭い can be hurtful; the examples here describe objects.
