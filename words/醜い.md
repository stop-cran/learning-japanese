# 醜い (みにくい) -- ugly; disgraceful

## Meaning

醜い is an i-adjective for something ugly or unattractive. It also
condemns conduct or a situation as unsightly, unseemly or disgraceful.
It is a harsh evaluation, especially when directed at a person.

- その建物は醜いと思います。 (そのたてものはみにくいとおもいます。) -- I think that building is ugly.
- 醜い争いはやめましょう。 (みにくいあらそいはやめましょう。) -- Let's stop this disgraceful fighting.

Forms include 醜くない (みにくくない), 醜かった (みにくかった)
and 醜さ (みにくさ), ugliness.

## How the kanji combine

醜 writes みにく; い is the adjective ending. There is no multi-kanji
compound here. 見憎い is a recorded rare spelling, not evidence for
reading ordinary 醜い as a sequence of on-readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [みっともない](みっともない.md) overlaps in unsightly or shameful appearance and conduct.
- [下品](下品.md) (げひん) emphasizes vulgarity; [汚い](汚い.md) (きたない) includes dirty or messy appearance and morally dirty behavior.
- [恥ずかしい](恥ずかしい.md) (はずかしい) can describe something embarrassing, but also the person's feeling of embarrassment.
- 美しい (うつくしい), beautiful, contrasts with the appearance sense.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confine 醜い to faces or bodies: 醜い争い evaluates the conduct
of a quarrel. Conversely, an embarrassing moment need not be ugly or
morally disgraceful. These words overlap without being interchangeable
in every sentence.
