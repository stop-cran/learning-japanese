# 候補 (こうほ) - candidate; possible choice

## Meaning

候補 is a candidate or possible choice for a position, award, plan, or other selection. It can also name candidacy or nomination. It is a noun: 候補になる means "become a candidate," and 候補に挙がる (こうほにあがる) means "be mentioned as a candidate."

- 彼女は次の代表の候補です。 (かのじょはつぎのだいひょうのこうほです。) - She is a candidate to be the next representative.
- 旅行先の候補を三つ選びました。 (りょこうさきのこうほをみっつえらびました。) - We chose three possible travel destinations.

候補者 (こうほしゃ) specifically identifies a candidate who is a person.

## How the kanji combine

[候](../kanji/候.md) has a waiting sense, and 補 contributes filling or supplementing. "Waiting to fill a position" is a useful memory aid for a candidate, not a literal description of every modern use or a claim about historical formation. The reading is コウ + ホ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[選択](選択.md) (せんたく) concerns choosing or a choice. 候補 identifies something still under consideration. [推薦](推薦.md) (すいせん) is recommendation; recommending someone can make them a candidate, but the two nouns name different things.

## Distinctive meaning

A 候補 has not necessarily been selected. The word does not imply a political election: travel destinations, names for a project, and locations for an event can all be candidates. There is no general opposite; an unsuccessful candidate remains a candidate in a description of the earlier selection process.
