# じゃんけん (じゃんけん) - rock-paper-scissors

## Meaning

じゃんけん is rock-paper-scissors, the hand game. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb: **じゃんけんをする** or **じゃんけんする**, "play rock-paper-scissors."

- じゃんけんで順番を決めましょう。
  (じゃんけんでじゅんばんをきめましょう。) - Let's decide the order by rock-paper-scissors.
- じゃんけんに負けました。
  (じゃんけんにまけました。) - I lost at rock-paper-scissors.

じゃんけんで marks the method of deciding something. じゃんけんに勝つ (じゃんけんにかつ) means "win at rock-paper-scissors."

## How the kanji combine

Kana is the usual spelling. **ジャンケン** is also recorded, while じゃん拳 is marked as a rare kanji spelling. The kana-primary article therefore has an empty primary kanji array.

The `wago` study category here distinguishes this Japanese game word from an ordinary imported English game name; it is not a claim establishing a particular historical etymology.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ゲーム](ゲーム.md) is a game more broadly, and [遊び](遊び.md) (あそび) is play or recreation. Neither specifically identifies rock-paper-scissors. Winning and losing are contrasting outcomes, not opposites of the game's name.

## Distinctive meaning

The hands are グー, rock; チョキ, scissors; and パー, paper. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, and paper beats rock. Equal hands produce a tie. The game can be used to decide turns or settle a choice; playing it does not necessarily imply a prize or gambling.
