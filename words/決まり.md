# 決まり (きまり) - rule; settled arrangement

## Meaning

決まり is a rule or established arrangement. It can also refer to a matter being settled. As a noun, it combines with verbs such as 守る (まもる), "observe," or predicates such as だ.

- 学校の決まりを守りましょう。 (がっこうのきまりをまもりましょう。) - Let's follow the school rules.
- 集合時間は九時で決まりです。 (しゅうごうじかんはくじできまりです。) - Nine o'clock is settled as the meeting time.

The first example concerns a continuing rule; the second announces a decision for a particular occasion.

## How the kanji combine

[決](../kanji/決.md) contributes deciding or settling. 決まり is related to [決まる](決まる.md) (きまる), "be decided." The noun ends in り; it is not the finite verb meaning "will be decided."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[規則](規則.md) (きそく) also means a rule or regulation. 決まり can describe an everyday arrangement without suggesting a formally written rulebook. [決定](決定.md) (けってい) emphasizes a decision or determination.

## Distinctive meaning

Something can be the 決まり because people have established it, not because it is a law. The related expression お決まりの (おきまりの) can describe something customary or predictable. In これで決まり (これできまり), "that settles it," the context is a conclusion, not a newly announced general rule.
