# 最後 (さいご) — end; final

## Meaning

最後 means the end of something or the final item in a sequence. Use 最後の before a noun, 最後に for "lastly; at the end," and 最後まで for "until the end."

- これが最後の質問です。 (これがさいごのしつもんです。) — This is the last question.
- 映画を最後まで見ました。 (えいがをさいごまでみました。) — I watched the film all the way to the end.

## How the kanji combine

[最](../kanji/最.md), "most," and [後](../kanji/後.md), "after; behind," suggest the furthest point in a sequence. The on readings are さい + ご.

## Synonyms and antonyms

最初 (さいしょ), "beginning; first," is the direct contrast. 終わり (おわり) also means an end. 最終 (さいしゅう) occurs in compounds such as 最終電車, "the last train."

## Distinctive meaning

English "last week" is 先週 (せんしゅう), not 最後の週 unless you mean the final week of a period. 最後 identifies an endpoint within a relevant sequence, rather than automatically meaning "previous."
