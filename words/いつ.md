# いつ - when

## Meaning

いつ asks when something happens. The answer can be a date, a day, a season, or another time expression; it need not be a clock time.

- 誕生日はいつですか。 (たんじょうびはいつですか。) - When is your birthday?
- いつ日本に来ましたか。 (いつにほんにきましたか。) - When did you come to Japan?

In a basic question such as いつ来ますか, いつ normally stands without に.

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is the normal spelling. The dictionary spelling 何時 is rare for いつ and can also suggest the different word なんじ, "what time?"

## Synonyms and antonyms

何時 (なんじ) specifically asks for a clock time. 何日 (なんにち) asks for a day of the month or a number of days, depending on context. Neither replaces every use of いつ.

## Distinctive meaning

Particles build useful expressions: いつから means "since when" or "from when," and いつまで means "until when." Learn these as time questions, not as statements about an unspecified future date.
