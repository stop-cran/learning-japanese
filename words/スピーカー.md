# スピーカー (スピーカー) - audio speaker; loudspeaker

## Meaning

スピーカー names an audio speaker or loudspeaker, a device that produces
sound from an audio signal. It is a noun.

- パソコンにスピーカーをつなぎました。 (パソコンにスピーカーをつなぎました。) - I connected speakers to the computer.
- スピーカーから大きな音が聞こえます。 (スピーカーからおおきなおとがきこえます。) - I can hear a loud sound from the speaker.

に marks what the speakers are connected to; から marks where the sound
comes from. The noun itself need not specify one device or several.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword has no kanji components. Keep both long vowels:
スピーカー. スピーカ is another dictionary-recorded spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ラウドスピーカー is a fuller loanword for a loudspeaker.
拡声器 (かくせいき), "megaphone; loudhailer," concerns amplifying a voice.
It is a related device, not a replacement for every computer speaker.

## Distinctive meaning

スピーカー can also name a person speaking or giving a talk; the public
dictionary records that use too. Thus the equipment examples are not an
equipment-only rule. An assembly's presiding "speaker" is [議長](議長.md)
(ぎちょう), a specific role rather than simply anyone addressing an audience.
