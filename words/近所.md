# 近所 (きんじょ) — neighborhood; vicinity

## Meaning

近所 means the nearby area, especially around a home or another established location. It is a noun: 近所に for existence or destination, 近所で for an activity.

近所に小さい公園があります。 (きんじょにちいさいこうえんがあります。) — There is a small park in the neighborhood.

## How the kanji combine

[近](../kanji/近.md), "near," combines with [所](../kanji/所.md), "place." The on-reading compound is きんじょ; 所 is voiced to じょ in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

近く (ちかく) more generally means "nearby" and can take an explicit reference point: 駅の近く (えきのちかく), "near the station." 隣 (となり) is immediately next door or adjacent, a narrower relationship.

## Distinctive meaning

Use の before a noun: 近所の店 (きんじょのみせ), "a neighborhood shop." 近所の人 (きんじょのひと) means someone from the neighborhood, not necessarily the person living in the very next house. The area has no fixed distance or administrative boundary.
