# 論ずる (ろんずる) - discuss; argue; take into consideration

## Meaning

論ずる means discussing or treating a topic, or arguing a point.
It is transitive: the topic can take を, or be introduced
with について, about.

- 環境問題を論ずる。 (かんきょうもんだいをろんずる。) - Discuss environmental problems.
- 現代文学について論ずる。 (げんだいぶんがくについてろんずる。) - Discuss modern literature.

These are dictionary-attested constructions.
An additional sense is taking something into consideration
or making an issue of it, often in negative expressions.
論ずるに足りない (ろんずるにたりない) means "not worth discussing."

## How the kanji combine

[論](../kanji/論.md) contributes discussion or argument,
with on reading ロン and the verbal ending ずる.
The related [論じる](論じる.md) (ろんじる) has the same
three core senses in the pinned dictionary.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[議論](議論.md)する (ぎろんする) overlaps in discussion or debate.
[話し合う](話し合う.md) (はなしあう) emphasizes talking together;
論ずる can also describe one author's treatment of a topic.
[考える](考える.md) (かんがえる) more broadly means thinking
about or considering something.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not conjugate this as an ordinary godan verb ending in る.
JMdict labels it a ずる alternative of a じる verb;
traditional Japanese dictionaries describe it under サ変.
These are different grammatical frameworks.
For practical modern conjugation, the related 論じる gives
論じます, 論じて and 論じた. Recognize 論ずる as well,
rather than inventing a past form such as 論ずった.

The English argue does not require an angry quarrel.
The word concerns treating an issue or presenting an argument.
