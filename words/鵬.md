# 鵬 (ほう) — peng; giant mythical bird

## Meaning

鵬 names the **peng**, an enormous bird in Chinese mythology. In the story
associated with *Zhuangzi*, it transforms from a fish. Learn the word for
mythological and literary recognition, not as an ordinary animal name.

- 鵬は想像上の鳥です。 (ほうはそうぞうじょうのとりです。) — The peng is an imaginary bird.

This is a description of a mythical creature, not a claimed sighting.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-character Sino-Japanese noun: [鵬](../kanji/鵬.md) is
**ホウ** here and writes the entire bird name. There are no compound parts
to combine. The long vowel matters: ほう, not ほ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 鵬 (おおとり) is another documented reading for this mythical bird.
  The wider おおとり dictionary family includes large birds and different
  imaginary birds, with spelling tendencies tied to the sense. This article
  teaches 鵬 as the peng; shared pronunciation does not make every bird
  spelling freely interchangeable.
- [鳳凰](鳳凰.md) (ほうおう), "Chinese phoenix; fenghuang," names a different
  mythical bird. It is a useful comparison, not an exact synonym for the peng.
- [鳥](鳥.md) (とり), "bird," is the broader category.
  [鴻鵠](鴻鵠.md) (こうこく), "large birds; great person," is a literary
  large-bird expression with a separate figurative use, not this bird's name.

There is no useful antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

**ほう** is this article's primary reading. JMdict also records **ぼう** for
the standalone word; **おおとり** is independently documented as a lexical
alternate. Neither alternate is a reason to replace every ホウ in compounds.

The bird word おおとり is separate from 大トリ (おおとり), the last or key
performer. 鵬 is not a spelling of that performer word.

"Peng" keeps this bird distinct from the fenghuang and from the phoenix
associated with rebirth from ashes. Do not infer a rebirth story from an
English dictionary's broad "phoenix" gloss.
