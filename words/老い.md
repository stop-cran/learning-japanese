# 老い (おい) - old age; aging

## Meaning

老い is a noun naming old age or the experience of aging. Use 老いを感じる (おいをかんじる),
"feel one's age," or 老いについて考える (おいについてかんがえる), "think about aging."
It can modify another noun with の.

- 最近、老いを感じます。 (さいきん、おいをかんじます。) - Lately I feel my age.
- この本は老いについて書かれています。 (このほんはおいについてかかれています。) - This book is about aging.

The word can also refer collectively to old people, as in 老いも若きも (おいもわかきも), "old and young alike."

## How the kanji combine

[老](../kanji/老.md) carries the aging meaning. The noun おい is related to 老いる (おいる), "grow old."
The final い does not make 老い an i-adjective; 老いを感じる treats it as a noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[老人](老人.md) (ろうじん) refers to an elderly person rather than the process of aging.
[年齢](年齢.md) (ねんれい) is age as a number or life stage.
若さ (わかさ), "youthfulness," contrasts with old age, but being young is not the opposite of having an age.

## Distinctive meaning

老い lets the speaker discuss aging as an experience or theme. It does not specify an exact age threshold,
and it is not a neutral label to attach indiscriminately to an older person in conversation.
The collective expression above should be learned as an expression, not as a model for addressing someone as 老い.
