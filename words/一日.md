# 一日 (いちにち・ついたち)

## Meaning

一日 has two essential beginner readings. **いちにち** means one day or the whole day; **ついたち** is the normal reading for the first day of a month.

- 一日かかります。 (いちにち かかります。) — It takes one day.
- 一日、家にいました。 (いちにち、いえに いました。) — I stayed home all day.
- 来月の一日に会いましょう。 (らいげつの ついたちに あいましょう。) — Let's meet on the first of next month.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," and [日](../kanji/日.md), "day," give the on-reading compound いちにち. The calendar reading ついたち belongs to the whole written word; do not divide it into ordinary readings of the two characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一日中 (いちにちじゅう) makes "all day" explicit. 二日 (ふつか) means two days or the second of the month, unlike the usual duration/date reading split here.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records "first day of the month" under いちにち. That dictionary sense does not make it the normal beginner calendar reading: learn ついたち when naming the date, and いちにち for duration.
