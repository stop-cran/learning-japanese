# 撮影 (さつえい) — photography; filming

## Meaning

撮影 is taking photographs or filming/recording moving images. With する it is transitive: something を撮影する, photograph or film something.

この美術館では撮影が禁止されている。（このびじゅつかんではさつえいがきんしされている。） — Photography is prohibited in this art museum.

この映画は京都で撮影された。（このえいがはきょうとでさつえいされた。） — This film was shot in Kyoto.

## How the kanji combine

撮 contributes taking pictures and 影 contributes the image or shadow. The action-and-object combination concerns capturing images; さつえい uses on-readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[撮る](撮る.md) とる is the everyday verb for taking a photograph or recording a scene. [写す](写す.md) うつす also includes photographing, not just copying. [写真](写真.md) しゃしん can name both a photograph and photography; 撮影 emphasizes the image-taking activity.

## Distinctive meaning

撮影 can cover still photographs and video. It does not by itself mean a finished [映画](映画.md) えいが, movie, or the physical [フィルム](フィルム.md), photographic film. [写る](写る.md) うつる describes appearing in a photograph, rather than the photographer's transitive action.
