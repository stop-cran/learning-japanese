# 再三 (さいさん) - again and again; repeatedly

## Meaning

再三 means again and again or repeatedly. It works as an adverb, and 再三の modifies a following noun.

- 再三連絡しましたが、返事がありません。 (さいさんれんらくしましたが、へんじがありません。) - I contacted them repeatedly, but there has been no reply.
- 再三のお願いで、ようやく承諾を得ました。 (さいさんのおねがいで、ようやくしょうだくをえました。) - After repeated requests, I finally obtained their consent.

The repeated actions may be requests, warnings, attempts, or other occurrences. The word alone does not specify a rate such as three times per day.

## How the kanji combine

[再](../kanji/再.md) contributes again and [三](../kanji/三.md) three. The established combination expresses recurrence; do not turn the characters into an exact requirement of three occurrences. The on readings are サイ + サン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[たびたび](たびたび.md) and [しばしば](しばしば.md) overlap in repeated or frequent occurrence. [しきりに](しきりに.md) can add an insistent or persistent quality. [再び](再び.md) (ふたたび), "again; once more," can refer to a single recurrence, whereas 再三 presents repetition.

## Distinctive meaning

再三の連絡 (さいさんのれんらく) means repeated contact, not a third contact specifically. Use 再三連絡する or 再三の連絡 rather than treating 再三 itself as a する verb. Frustration can arise from context, but the word does not by itself prove anger or rudeness.
