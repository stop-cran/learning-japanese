# 刻む (きざむ) - mince; engrave; mark a rhythm

## Meaning

刻む is a transitive godan verb: cut something finely, or carve marks into a surface. The object takes を. Its て-form is 刻んで (きざんで), and the past is 刻んだ (きざんだ).

- ねぎを細かく刻んでください。
  (ねぎをこまかくきざんでください。) - Please chop the green onion finely.
- 石に名前を刻みました。
  (いしになまえをきざみました。) - I engraved a name on the stone.

## How the kanji combine

[刻](../kanji/刻.md) concerns carving and also measured time. The native verb 刻む brings these branches together through cutting marks or marking successive moments; this is a learning association, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[切る](切る.md) (きる) is the broader "cut" verb. 刻む can specify small cuts rather than one division. 心に刻む (こころにきざむ) means to etch something into one's mind, overlapping [覚える](覚える.md) (おぼえる), "remember," without literally carving a person.

## Distinctive meaning

リズムを刻む means "beat out a rhythm"; 時計が時を刻む (とけいがときをきざむ) describes a clock marking the passing moments. For example, 小さな時計が静かに時を刻んでいました (ちいさなとけいがしずかにときをきざんでいました), "A small clock was quietly ticking away." The object and context select the sense, not a rule that 刻む always involves a knife.
