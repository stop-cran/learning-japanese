# 夢中 (むちゅう) - absorbed; engrossed

## Meaning

夢中 describes being absorbed in something, sometimes to the point of forgetting oneself or one's surroundings. It works as a noun or na-adjective. Use Nに夢中だ, "be engrossed in N," and 夢中で + verb, "do something with complete absorption."

- 子どもはゲームに夢中です。 (こどもはゲームにむちゅうです。) - The child is engrossed in a game.
- 夢中で本を読んでいて、時間を忘れました。 (むちゅうでほんをよんでいて、じかんをわすれました。) - I was so absorbed in reading that I lost track of time.

に identifies the absorbing object; で describes how the action is carried out.

## How the kanji combine

[夢](../kanji/夢.md) contributes "dream," and [中](../kanji/中.md) contributes "inside; midst." "Inside a dream" is a helpful image for losing awareness of one's surroundings. The on readings are ム + チュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[好き](好き.md) (すき) expresses liking. 夢中 can go further by describing how fully something occupies someone's attention. You can like a book without becoming so absorbed that you forget the time.

## Distinctive meaning

夢中 need not describe a literal dream, trance, or medical condition. In ordinary hobby and reading contexts, "engrossed" is more useful than the dictionary gloss "delirium." The word can convey enthusiasm, but it can also imply that someone is no longer attending to other things.
