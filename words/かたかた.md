# かたかた — light rattling or clattering

## Meaning

Small, repeated hard sounds, often from something light tapping or rattling.

- 風で雨戸（あまど）がかたかた鳴（な）った。 — The storm shutter rattled in the wind.
- キーボードをかたかた打（う）つ音が聞こえる。 — I can hear the light clatter of typing on a keyboard.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[がたがた](がたがた.md) often feels heavier or more unstable. [かちかち](かちかち.md) can suggest more distinct clicks. These impressions overlap, particularly without sound or sentence context.

## Distinctive meaning

Use かたかた鳴る for a rattling object. Unlike がたがた, this card does not teach an automatic “grumbling” meaning. Changing an unvoiced consonant to a voiced one is not a universal rule for generating every secondary sense.
