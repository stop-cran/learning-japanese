# 動詞 (どうし) - verb

## Meaning

動詞 is the grammatical term "verb." It is itself a noun: 動詞を使う (どうしをつかう) means "use a verb," and 動詞の形 (どうしのかたち) means "a verb form."

- 「食べる」は動詞です。 (「たべる」はどうしです。) - "Eat" is a verb.
- この動詞を過去形にしてください。 (このどうしをかこけいにしてください。) - Please put this verb into the past tense.

The second request concerns changing the form of the verb being studied, not changing the noun 動詞 itself.

## How the kanji combine

[動](../kanji/動.md) contributes movement or action, and 詞 contributes word or expression. This gives a useful reminder of the category, but not a complete grammatical definition. The on readings are ドウ + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

名詞 (めいし), "noun," and 形容詞 (けいようし), "adjective," are contrasting grammatical categories, not antonyms in the ordinary meaning sense. 動詞 names the category, while a word such as [使う](使う.md) is a member of it.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every verb describes visible movement. ある and いる express existence, yet are also verbs. Conversely, a word referring to an action may be a noun. Use the word's grammatical behavior and forms rather than the literal "movement word" mnemonic to decide whether it is a 動詞.
