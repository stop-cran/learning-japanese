# 鞄 (かばん) - bag, satchel

## Meaning

かばん is a bag for carrying belongings, such as a school bag, satchel, or briefcase. It does not specify one particular material or shape.

- 鞄に本を入れました。 (かばんにほんをいれました。) - I put a book in my bag.
- その黒い鞄は私のです。 (そのくろいかばんはわたしのです。) - That black bag is mine.

The container takes に in the first sentence; the item being put inside takes を.

## How the kanji combine

鞄 represents the whole reading かばん. The dictionary marks the word as usually written in kana, and both かばん and カバン are useful spellings to recognize.
Borrowing explanations have been proposed from Chinese and Dutch; the exact origin remains uncertain. The kanji spelling is not evidence of an inherited native word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

バッグ broadly overlaps with かばん. 袋 (ふくろ) is a sack or pouch; かご is a basket. Those words are often more precise for a shopping sack or woven basket.

## Distinctive meaning

Although dictionaries can include "basket" among translations, do not treat かばん as the default word for every container. The practical core is a portable bag for one's things, with its exact English name supplied by context.
