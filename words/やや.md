# やや (やや) - somewhat; slightly

## Meaning

やや expresses a limited degree: "somewhat," "slightly," or "on the ... side." Place it before the quality or condition being qualified, without adding に.

- この靴はやや大きいです。 (このくつはややおおきいです。) - These shoes are slightly large.
- 電車はやや遅れて到着しました。 (でんしゃはややおくれてとうちゃくしました。) - The train arrived a little late.

In the first example, the shoes are somewhat too large for the relevant fit; the statement does not quantify their size in centimeters.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. JMdict records 稍 and 漸 as rare kanji spellings, but there is no need to substitute them for やや. Treat it as a complete adverb rather than trying to assign separate meanings to the repeated kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[少し](少し.md) (すこし) and [ちょっと](ちょっと.md) also express a small amount or degree. やや is useful in measured descriptions and assessments. A larger degree, such as "very," supplies a contextual contrast rather than a single fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

やや qualifies a particular feature; it does not generally mean an incomplete physical part of an object. It can also indicate a short interval, as in ややあって, "after a short while." This is the degree/time adverb, not the separate interjection identity mistakenly cited in the community source.
