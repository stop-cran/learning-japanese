# 素 (もと) - base; ingredient mix

## Meaning

素 (もと) is a noun for a base, raw material, or ingredient used to make something. It is especially useful for prepared mixes and soup bases.

- スープの素を買いました。 (スープのもとをかいました。) - I bought soup base.
- この粉はケーキの素です。 (このこなはケーキのもとです。) - This powder is a cake mix.

Nの素 identifies what the base is used to make. The noun does not imply that the ingredient is unprocessed: a prepared mix can also be 素.

## How the kanji combine

素 is one spelling of the native noun もと. The inspected entry also includes [元](元.md), 本, and 基, with different spelling preferences across its meanings. It especially associates 素 with material, ingredient, or mix. Shared reading and entry membership do not make every spelling equally suitable in every phrase.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[材料](材料.md) (ざいりょう) means materials or ingredients more broadly. スープの材料 can include all the ingredients; スープの素 identifies a base used to make the soup. A finished product contrasts with its ingredients but is not a universal antonym of もと.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not read this culinary word そ. Mathematical "prime" belongs to the separate 素 (そ) entry. The community source's lone English gloss "prime" conflicts with its supplied もと identity. This card intentionally retains the dictionary-supported Japanese identity and teaches its ingredient sense; the historical compiler's intended meaning remains uncertain, rather than having been recovered.
