# 調整 (ちょうせい) - adjustment; coordination; tuning

## Meaning

調整 is adjustment, regulation, tuning, or coordination to bring something
into a suitable state. 調整する is transitive: what is adjusted takes を.

- 会議の日程を調整しています。
  (かいぎのにっていをちょうせいしています。) - We are coordinating the meeting schedule.
- 画面の明るさを調整しました。
  (がめんのあかるさをちょうせいしました。) - I adjusted the screen brightness.

The first example reconciles arrangements; the second changes a setting.
調整中 (ちょうせいちゅう) means adjustment or coordination is in progress.

## How the kanji combine

[調](../kanji/調.md) contributes tuning or harmonizing, and
整 contributes arranging or making orderly.
Their related meanings reinforce bringing things into a suitable balance.
The readings are チョウ + セイ; the character associations are not an etymology.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[調節](調節.md) (ちょうせつ) often foregrounds regulating a level or amount.
調整 also readily describes coordinating schedules or interests.
This is a tendency, not a rule separating people from machines.
[加減](加減.md) (かげん) overlaps when moderating a quantity.
[合わせる](合わせる.md) (あわせる) is useful for making something match a reference.

## Distinctive meaning

Adjustment need not mean repairing a broken object.
[修正](修正.md) (しゅうせい), revision or correction, and
[変更](変更.md) (へんこう), change, overlap when modifying an existing plan.
[整理](整理.md) (せいり), primarily sorting or putting in order,
also has adjustment and regulation in its dictionary scope.
The words are not freely interchangeable in every construction.
