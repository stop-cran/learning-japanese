# 恵まれる (めぐまれる) - be blessed with; abound in

## Meaning

恵まれる means to be blessed with something favorable, or to be rich in it.
It is an intransitive ichidan verb. In Nに恵まれる, に marks the favorable
resource, ability, opportunity, or circumstance.

- 旅行中は天候に恵まれました。 (りょこうちゅうはてんこうにめぐまれました。) - We were blessed with good weather during the trip.
- 彼女は音楽の才能に恵まれています。 (かのじょはおんがくのさいのうにめぐまれています。) - She is blessed with musical talent.

恵まれた環境 (めぐまれたかんきょう) is a favorable or privileged environment.
The た form can describe an existing fortunate condition, not just a completed event.

## How the kanji combine

恵 contributes favor or blessing, and めぐまれる is the native reading.
Daijisen traces this verb to a passive form of 恵む (めぐむ), "bestow a favor."
For present-day use, learn the whole verb and Nに pattern.
Forms include 恵まれます (めぐまれます) and 恵まれない (めぐまれない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[豊か](豊か.md) (ゆたか) and [豊富](豊富.md) (ほうふ) overlap in abundance.
[幸運](幸運.md) (こううん) concerns good fortune.
恵まれる often combines favorable circumstances with the idea of having something available.
恵まれない describes lacking such advantages; it does not necessarily mean possessing nothing.

## Distinctive meaning

The favorable interpretation matters: 天候に恵まれた normally means the weather
suited the purpose, not merely that weather existed.
Being 恵まれている does not guarantee happiness or deny a person's effort.
It identifies an advantage, which may be material or nonmaterial.
