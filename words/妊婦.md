# 妊婦 (にんぷ) — pregnant woman

## Meaning

妊婦 refers to a woman who is pregnant. It names a person, not pregnancy as a condition and not someone simply because she is a parent.

- この席は妊婦も利用できます。 (このせきはにんぷもりようできます。) — Pregnant women can use this seat too.
- 受付で妊婦向けの冊子をもらった。 (うけつけでにんぷむけのさっしをもらった。) — I received a booklet for pregnant women at reception.

## How the kanji combine

[妊](../kanji/妊.md) identifies pregnancy. [婦](../kanji/婦.md) identifies the woman in this compound; it does not say whether she is married. Notice ぷ in にんぷ rather than an unchanged ふ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[女性](女性.md) simply means a woman. [母親](母親.md) means a mother, and does not say that she is pregnant now.

## Distinctive meaning

The ending 向け (むけ) in the second example tells you who the booklet is intended for. A 妊婦向けの冊子 is a booklet for pregnant women, not a booklet describing one particular person.
