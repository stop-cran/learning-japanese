# まだ - still; not yet

## Meaning

まだ presents a situation as continuing. With an affirmative predicate it often means "still"; with a negative predicate it often means "not yet."

まだ雨が降っています。 (まだあめがふっています。) - It is still raining.

宿題はまだ終わっていません。 (しゅくだいはまだおわっていません。) - I have not finished my homework yet.

The negative in the second sentence belongs to the verb; まだ itself does not mean "not."

## How the kanji combine

Usually written in hiragana, まだ can also be written 未だ, using [未](../kanji/未.md), "not yet." The written form also has the reading いまだ in other contexts; this page teaches まだ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もう with an affirmative predicate often means "already." Compare もう終わりました (もうおわりました), "I have already finished," with まだ終わっていません. もう + negative commonly means "no longer."

## Distinctive meaning

まだ can also mean "only" when a stage is earlier than expected: まだ三時です (まださんじです), "It is only three." Read it with the whole predicate and context, rather than assigning one English translation to every occurrence.
