# 本 (ほん) — book

## Meaning

本 is a book: 本を読む (read a book), 本屋 ほんや (bookshop), 絵本 えほん (picture book), 本棚 ほんだな (bookcase), 一冊の本 (a book).
It is also a counter for long, thin things (see below).

## How the kanji combine

A single kanji, [本](../kanji/本.md), read with its on reading ホン. The kanji pictures a tree with its root marked, the base of something; "book" is a later sense.
The same character gives 日本 にほん and 本当 ほんとう (truth), where it means "origin, true".
Counter: 一本 いっぽん, 二本 にほん, 三本 さんぼん; books themselves are counted with 冊 さつ, not 本.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 書物 しょもつ (book, formal), 図書 としょ (books, as in a library).
- Related: 雑誌 ざっし (magazine), 辞書 じしょ (dictionary).

## Distinctive meaning

本 is the ordinary word for a book. Count books with 冊 (一冊, 二冊), and count long thin things such as pencils and bottles with 本; mixing them up is a common learner mistake.
