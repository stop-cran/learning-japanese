# まず (まず) — first of all

## Meaning

まず means first of all or to begin with. It introduces the first action, point, or priority in a sequence.

- まず、手を洗ってください。 (まず、てをあらってください。) — First, please wash your hands.
- まず名前を書いて、それから質問に答えてください。 (まずなまえをかいて、それからしつもんにこたえてください。) — First write your name, and then answer the questions.

## How the kanji combine

The ordinary spelling is hiragana. An alternative is [先](../kanji/先.md)ず, using the character associated with what comes before. The kana-only form is enough for everyday instructions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

最初に (さいしょに) means "at the beginning; first." それから means "after that," and 次に (つぎに) means "next." 最後に (さいごに), "lastly," is a useful sequence contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

まず organizes actions or reasoning; it is not the adjective "first" in every English phrase. For "the first day," say 最初の日 (さいしょのひ), not まず日. Other contextual meanings exist, such as "probably" or "for now," but beginner instructions usually use the sequence meaning illustrated here.
