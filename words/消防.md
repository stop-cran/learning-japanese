# 消防 (しょうぼう) - firefighting; fire service

## Meaning

消防 means firefighting. It can also abbreviate a reference to the fire service, brigade, or its personnel when context supplies the institution or people. Learn it primarily as a noun and as the first part of compounds.

- 学校で消防訓練を行いました。 (がっこうでしょうぼうくんれんをおこないました。) - We held a fire drill at school.
- 消防が現場に到着しました。 (しょうぼうがげんばにとうちゃくしました。) - The fire service arrived at the scene.

The first sentence concerns an activity; the second uses the institutional abbreviation. A particular building is a 消防署 (しょうぼうしょ), "fire station."

## How the kanji combine

[消](../kanji/消.md) contributes extinguishing or eliminating, and 防 contributes preventing or defending against. Together they express fighting and guarding against fire. The reading is ショウ + ボウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

消火 (しょうか) specifically names extinguishing a fire. 消防 has a broader service or activity perspective. [警察](警察.md) (けいさつ), "police," names a different public service, not a synonym or opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Compounds make the referent precise: 消防車 (しょうぼうしゃ) is a fire engine, and 消防士 (しょうぼうし) is a firefighter. Do not translate 消防 as a fire-station building in every sentence. The bare noun may stand for the responding service, while the longer word tells you whether the speaker means a person, vehicle, or station.
