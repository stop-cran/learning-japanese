# ぺちゃんこ — squashed flat

## Meaning

Flattened or crushed down so that something loses its thickness or volume.

- 箱の下でパンがぺちゃんこになった。 — The bread was squashed flat under the box.
- 空き缶（あきかん）をぺちゃんこにつぶした。 — I crushed the empty can flat.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぐしゃぐしゃ](ぐしゃぐしゃ.md) can describe crumpling or a distorted mess, without requiring flatness. [ふかふか](ふかふか.md) suggests soft, well-padded volume rather than a flattened result.

## Distinctive meaning

ぺちゃんこになる presents the result; ぺちゃんこにする presents causing it. Something can be flat but intact: the word does not necessarily mean torn, broken into pieces, or wet.
