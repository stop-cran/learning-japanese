# 大家 (おおや) - landlord; landlady

## Meaning

大家, read おおや, is a landlord or landlady: the person who rents a property to a tenant.
大家さん is a useful way to refer to that person politely.
This is a noun, not a verb meaning "rent."

- 大家さんに家賃を払いました。
  (おおやさんにやちんをはらいました。) - I paid the rent to the landlord.
- 水が漏れていたので、大家さんに連絡しました。
  (みずがもれていたので、おおやさんにれんらくしました。)
  - Water was leaking, so I contacted the landlady.

English chooses "landlord" or "landlady" from context; the Japanese noun does not encode that distinction.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes "big," and [家](../kanji/家.md) contributes "house."
The native readings combine as おお + や. Learn the established whole-word meaning
rather than translating the characters as "a big house." 大屋 is also listed as a spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

家主 (やぬし) overlaps in the property-owner or landlord role.
A tenant is on the other side of the rental relationship, not an opposite personality type.
[借りる](借りる.md) (かりる) describes renting or borrowing; [家賃](家賃.md) (やちん) is the rent.

## Distinctive meaning

For the reading おおや, learn the rental-property meaning, not "rich family."
Ownership, rent collection, and day-to-day property management need not be handled by
the same person in every rental arrangement.
