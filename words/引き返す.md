# 引き返す (ひきかえす) - turn back; retrace one's steps

## Meaning

引き返す means to turn back along the way one has come.
In this everyday movement use it is intransitive: the traveler is the subject,
and a return destination can take に or へ.

- 道を間違えたので、引き返しました。 (みちをまちがえたので、ひきかえしました。) - I went the wrong way, so I turned back.
- 忘れ物に気づいて、家まで引き返しました。 (わすれものにきづいて、いえまでひきかえしました。) - I realized I had forgotten something and went back as far as home.

This is a godan verb ending in す: 引き返します, 引き返して,
and 引き返さない.

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) supplies the 引き element;
[返](../kanji/返.md) contributes returning or reversing.
Learn the established compound ひきかえす. Its final す does not
make the modern travel use transitive. 引返す is a recorded spelling
and the form in the pinned source row.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[戻る](戻る.md) (もどる) more broadly means going back or returning
to an earlier place or state. [帰る](帰る.md) (かえる) focuses on
returning home or to a base. [バック](バック.md)する can mean
moving backwards; turning around and walking forwards along the return
route is still 引き返す.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "repeat; send back; bring back" gloss is not a safe guide
to this modern movement construction. Digital Daijisen also records
older literary repetition, reversal, and returning uses, so those
meanings are not simply nonexistent. Their presence does not make
引き返す the everyday transitive verb for sending someone home or
returning a book. Compare [帰す](帰す.md) (かえす),
[返す](返す.md) (かえす), and [繰り返す](繰り返す.md)
(くりかえす), "repeat."
