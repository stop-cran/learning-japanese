# 水平 (すいへい) - horizontal; level

## Meaning

水平 describes being horizontal or level, like the surface of still water. Use 水平な before a noun and 水平に for an orientation or manner.

- 棚板が水平かどうか確かめました。 (たないたがすいへいかどうかたしかめました。) - I checked whether the shelf board was level.
- 箱を水平に持ってください。 (はこをすいへいにもってください。) - Please hold the box level.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md), water, and [平](../kanji/平.md), flat or level, evoke the level surface of still water. Read スイ + ヘイ. This explains the physical association, not an instruction to translate 水平 as the amount or height of water.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[垂直](垂直.md) is vertical or perpendicular. [平ら](平ら.md) describes flatness: a flat board can still be tilted. [横](横.md) can describe a horizontal, sideways or widthwise direction more generally. [水準](水準.md) can mean a level or standard, including abstract achievement; those English "level" uses do not all mean horizontal.

## Distinctive meaning

水平 itself teaches horizontal orientation, not the visible horizon. [水平線](水平線.md) is the water horizon or a horizontal line. 水位 (すいい) is water level in the sense of its height. Digital Daijisen also records 水平 for a leveling instrument; this is a separate noun use, not a reason to call every measuring instrument 水平.
