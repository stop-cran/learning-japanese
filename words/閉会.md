# 閉会 (へいかい) - closing a meeting or event

## Meaning

閉会 is the closing or conclusion of a meeting, assembly or organized event.
It is a noun and a する verb with both transitive and intransitive patterns:
organizers close a meeting, or the meeting comes to a close.

- 議長は会議の閉会を宣言しました。 (ぎちょうはかいぎのへいかいをせんげんしました。) - The chair declared the meeting closed.
- 大会は午後五時に閉会しました。 (たいかいはごごごじにへいかいしました。) - The convention closed at five in the afternoon.

## How the kanji combine

[閉](../kanji/閉.md) contributes closing; [会](../kanji/会.md) contributes
meeting or gathering. This verb-object association names closing the gathering:
ヘイ + カイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[開会](開会.md) (かいかい), opening an event, is the useful opposite.
[終了](終了.md) (しゅうりょう) and [終わる](終わる.md) (おわる) express
ending more broadly. [閉じる](閉じる.md) (とじる) can also close a meeting.
[中止](中止.md) (ちゅうし) is stopping or cancellation, not necessarily a
planned closing after the proceedings.

## Distinctive meaning

閉会式 (へいかいしき) is a closing ceremony; the ceremony and the whole event
need not occupy the same time span. The word also occurs with legislative sessions.
Do not use it as the general expression for shutting a physical door or for every
shop's business closing time.
