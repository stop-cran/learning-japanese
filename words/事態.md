# 事態 (じたい) - situation; state of affairs

## Meaning

事態 is a noun for a situation or state of affairs, especially one that has developed and may require a response. It often has a negative connotation, but the word itself does not mean "disaster."

- 事態は思ったより深刻です。 (じたいはおもったよりしんこくです。) - The situation is more serious than we thought.
- 話し合いの後、事態は少し改善しました。 (はなしあいのあと、じたいはすこしかいぜんしました。) - After the discussion, the situation improved a little.

事態を理解する (じたいをりかいする) means "understand the situation." The noun can be the subject of worsening or improvement, or the object of an attempt to understand it.

## How the kanji combine

[事](../kanji/事.md) contributes an event or matter, and 態 contributes state or condition. Together they concern the state of a matter. The on readings are ジ + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[状態](状態.md) (じょうたい) can describe the condition of a machine, body, or other thing. [状況](状況.md) (じょうきょう) describes a situation or surrounding circumstances. These overlap with 事態, but a developing issue is a useful focus for this word.

## Distinctive meaning

最悪の事態 (さいあくのじたい) means "the worst-case situation"; 予想外の事態 (よそうがいのじたい) means "an unexpected turn of events." Those modifiers add information. Do not build their meanings into every unmodified occurrence of 事態 or treat it as an obligatory emergency.
