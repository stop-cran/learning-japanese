# びっしり — closely and densely packed

## Meaning

びっしり describes things packed closely together with little or no empty space.

- 本棚には専門書がびっしり並んでいる。
  — The bookshelf is packed tightly with reference books.
- ノートには細かい文字がびっしり書かれていた。
  — The notebook was densely filled with small writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

びっしり emphasizes dense coverage, unlike a sparse or widely spaced arrangement.

## Distinctive meaning

It can also describe a schedule or workload being completely full.
