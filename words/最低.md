# 最低 (さいてい) - lowest; minimum; worst

## Meaning

最低 describes the lowest value or level, and can also mean the worst or simply awful. Before an amount, 最低 or 最低でも means "at least."

- 今日の最低気温は五度でした。 (きょうのさいていきおんはごどでした。) - Today's lowest temperature was five degrees.
- この仕事には最低でも二時間かかります。 (このしごとにはさいていでもにじかんかかります。) - This job will take at least two hours.

最低の成績 (さいていのせいせき) means "the worst results." Said about behavior, それは最低だ can mean "That's awful."

## How the kanji combine

[最](../kanji/最.md) contributes the extreme end of a scale, and 低 contributes lowness. Together they mean "lowest." The on readings are サイ + テイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[最高](最高.md) (さいこう), "highest; best," is the contrasting extreme. 少なくとも (すくなくとも) overlaps with "at least" when giving a lower bound, but does not replace 最低 in an emotional judgment like 最低だ.

## Distinctive meaning

A low measurement is not necessarily bad: 最低気温 reports a value, not a criticism of the weather. Conversely, an irritated 最低だ need not be a literal ranking against every alternative. Before a noun, useful patterns include 最低の条件 (さいていのじょうけん), "minimum conditions," and 最低な行為 (さいていなこうい), "appalling behavior"; the evaluative context matters.
