# それなのに - and yet; despite that

## Meaning

それなのに introduces something contrary to what the preceding situation
would lead one to expect: and yet, despite that, or nevertheless.
Use it at a connection between statements: **situation。それなのに、unexpected fact**.

- 何度も説明しました。それなのに、まだ分かってもらえません。 (なんどもせつめいしました。それなのに、まだわかってもらえません。) - I explained it many times. And yet, they still do not understand.
- 十分寝ました。それなのに、まだ眠いです。 (じゅうぶんねました。それなのに、まだねむいです。) - I slept enough. Despite that, I am still sleepy.

The contrast is between an expectation and the actual situation, not merely
between two unrelated facts.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[それでも](それでも.md) (nevertheless/even so) and
[しかし](しかし.md) (however/but) overlap in introducing a contrast.
[けれど](けれど.md) and [けれども](けれども.md) also express contrast,
but have attachment patterns beyond this sentence-linking expression.
There is no single antonym that fits every contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

それなのに can convey surprise, disappointment or criticism, but those feelings
are not mandatory parts of every translation.
It is not automatically "therefore": the unexpected outcome is not being
presented as the natural result of the first statement.
For attaching のに directly to a preceding predicate, use that construction's
own attachment rules rather than treating それなのに as a verb ending.
