# スライド - slide; shifting

## Meaning

スライド can name a projected slide or transparency. Modern presentation
software also uses it for an individual slide, as recorded in the public
dictionary. In a laboratory, it can abbreviate a microscope slide,
スライドガラス or スライドグラス.

- 次のスライドを見てください。 (つぎのスライドをみてください。) - Please look at the next slide.
- 日程を一週間スライドさせました。 (にっていをいっしゅうかんスライドさせました。) - We shifted the schedule by one week.

スライドする also means to slide or shift position. The second example
uses スライドさせる to cause a schedule shift, not to project an image.
The dictionary further records indexing or pegging a value to a changing
scale, such as adjusting payments with prices.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずらす](ずらす.md) shifts something's position or timing.
[滑る](滑る.md) describes sliding or slipping. Their movement meanings
overlap with スライド, but neither is the noun for a presentation image
or a microscope slide.

## Distinctive meaning

The context determines whether the word names an image, a piece of glass,
or movement. Do not import every English use of "slide," such as the name
of a playground apparatus. A shifted schedule is not necessarily delayed:
specify the direction or new date when it matters.
