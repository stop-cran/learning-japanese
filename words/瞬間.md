# 瞬間 (しゅんかん) - instant; moment

## Meaning

瞬間 is a noun for a very brief moment or instant. その瞬間 means
"at that moment." A plain verb can describe the moment:
ドアを開けた瞬間, "the instant I opened the door."

- ドアを開けた瞬間、猫が外へ出ました。 (ドアをあけたしゅんかん、ねこがそとへでました。) - The instant I opened the door, the cat went outside.
- その瞬間を写真に撮りました。 (そのしゅんかんをしゃしんにとりました。) - I captured that moment in a photograph.

The first example connects an event with a sharply identified moment;
the second treats the moment as the object recorded in a photograph.

## How the kanji combine

瞬 contributes blinking or something momentary, and
[間](../kanji/間.md) contributes an interval. The first character
specifies how brief the interval is. Their on readings combine as
しゅん + かん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時](時.md) (とき) is broader "time; occasion."
[秒](秒.md) (びょう) is the measured unit "second."
瞬間 may be translated "a second" in casual English, but it does
not specify a duration of exactly one second.

## Distinctive meaning

瞬間 can identify a point in time rather than a period with a measurable
length. In the door example, the main point is immediate timing, not
how long opening the door took. Use a measured duration when an exact
number of seconds matters.
