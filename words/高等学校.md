# 高等学校 (こうとうがっこう) - senior high school

## Meaning

高等学校 is a senior high school: the school stage after junior high school in Japan. 高校 (こうこう) is the usual shorter form in everyday conversation.

- 姉は高等学校の先生です。 (あねはこうとうがっこうのせんせいです。) - My older sister is a high-school teacher.
- この高等学校には図書館があります。 (このこうとうがっこうにはとしょかんがあります。) - This high school has a library.

The full form often appears in official school names, documents, and formal descriptions.

## How the kanji combine

[高](../kanji/高.md) means high and [等](../kanji/等.md) grade or rank. [学](../kanji/学.md) and [校](../kanji/校.md) form 学校, "school." Learn the sound sequence こうとう + がっこう, including the doubled consonant in 学校.

## Synonyms and antonyms

高校 is an abbreviation of the same word, not a different school level. 中学校 (ちゅうがっこう) is junior high school, and 大学 (だいがく) is university.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 高等学校 as "university" simply because 高等 suggests higher-level education. As an institution name, the complete compound specifically identifies senior high school.
