# 大通り (おおどおり) - main street; boulevard

## Meaning

大通り is a main street or major thoroughfare, rather than a small side street.
It is a noun; の connects it to another noun.

- 大通りを渡って、右に曲がってください。 (おおどおりをわたって、みぎにまがってください。) - Cross the main street and turn right.
- その店は大通りに面しています。 (そのみせはおおどおりにめんしています。) - The shop faces the main street.

大通りの向こう (おおどおりのむこう) means "the other side of the main street."

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md), "large," modifies [通り](通り.md), "street,"
written with [通](../kanji/通.md). The native elements combine as
おお + どおり: とおり becomes どおり here.
The reading is おおどおり, not だいつうり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[道](道.md) (みち) and [道路](道路.md) (どうろ) are broader road words.
裏通り (うらどおり), "back street," is a useful contrast, not a guarantee
that every city has exactly one main street.

## Distinctive meaning

English "boulevard" is a possible translation, not a requirement for trees,
a particular number of lanes, or a legally defined road category.
The word identifies a prominent street in the relevant area.
