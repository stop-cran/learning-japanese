# 食品 (しょくひん) - food products; foodstuffs

## Meaning

食品 means food or food products viewed as a category of goods. It is a noun, used in contexts such as production, shops, packaging, and food safety. It does not mean merchandise of any kind.

- この店では食品を売っています。 (このみせではしょくひんをうっています。) - This shop sells food products.
- 食品は涼しい場所に置いてください。 (しょくひんはすずしいばしょにおいてください。) - Please keep the food in a cool place.

食品を marks the goods sold; 食品は introduces the food as the topic of the storage instruction.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) contributes food or eating, and [品](../kanji/品.md) contributes an article or product. Food specifies the kind of product. The on readings are ショク + ヒン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[食べ物](食べ物.md) (たべもの) means things to eat. [食事](食事.md) (しょくじ) concerns a meal or eating it. [商品](商品.md) (しょうひん) means merchandise more broadly and can include items that are not food.

## Distinctive meaning

The community source's "commodity" gloss needs the qualification "food." Clothes and books may be 商品 but are not 食品. The word also does not promise that an item is processed or packaged: those properties require more detail. 冷凍食品 (れいとうしょくひん), for example, specifically means frozen food.
