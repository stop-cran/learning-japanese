# 発達 (はったつ) - development; growth

## Meaning

発達 is development or growth in abilities, bodily functions, or systems. It is a noun and intransitive する verb: Nが発達する, "N develops."

- 子どもの言葉が発達してきました。 (こどものことばがはったつしてきました。) - The child's language ability has been developing.
- この地域では交通機関が発達しています。 (このちいきではこうつうきかんがはったつしています。) - This region has a well-developed transportation system.

The first example describes development over time; the second describes a developed state.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) contributes emerging or developing, and [達](../kanji/達.md) contributes reaching or attaining. Together they suggest growth toward fuller development. The compound is read はったつ, with a small っ, rather than はつたつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[成長](成長.md) (せいちょう) can focus on growing or maturing. [発展](発展.md) (はってん) can focus on expansion or progress in an activity or society. Their uses overlap, so learn the nouns they accompany rather than assigning rigid categories.

## Distinctive meaning

発達 is not simply any increase in size or quantity. It can describe a system becoming more developed or capabilities becoming fuller. Conversely, the noun is not inherently a medical label. In ordinary sentences such as 交通機関が発達している, it refers to the development of infrastructure, not a person's diagnosis.
