# 駐車場 (ちゅうしゃじょう) — parking lot

## Meaning

駐車場 is a place for parking cars: a car park, parking lot, or parking garage. The word does not require an outdoor lot.

- 駅の近くに駐車場があります。 (えきのちかくにちゅうしゃじょうがあります。) — There is a car park near the station.
- 駐車場に車を止めました。 (ちゅうしゃじょうにくるまをとめました。) — I parked the car in the parking lot.

## How the kanji combine

駐 suggests stopping or stationing, [車](../kanji/車.md) means a vehicle, and [場](../kanji/場.md) means a place. 駐車 (ちゅうしゃ), "parking," plus 場 gives "parking place." Preserve both long vowels in ちゅうしゃじょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

駐輪場 (ちゅうりんじょう) is a designated parking area for bicycles and similar two-wheeled vehicles. A 車庫 (しゃこ) is a garage or vehicle storage building.

## Distinctive meaning

駐車場 names the facility, not the act of parking; that is 駐車する (ちゅうしゃする). Its name alone does not say whether parking is free, paid, public, or restricted to customers.
