# 逆らう (さからう) - go against; defy

## Meaning

逆らう is a godan verb meaning "go against; oppose; disobey." The person, instruction, or force resisted takes **に**, even when English uses a direct object.

- 親の言うことに逆らった。 (おやのいうことにさからった。) - I went against what my parents said.
- 風に逆らって歩きました。 (かぜにさからってあるきました。) - I walked against the wind.

The dictionary classifies it as intransitive. Useful forms are 逆らいます, 逆らって, and 逆らわない. In the second example, 逆らって describes how the walking proceeds.

## How the kanji combine

逆 contributes reversal or opposition. The complete native verb is さからう, with らう written in kana. It is not formed by adding する to the kanji's on reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[従う](従う.md) (したがう), "follow; obey," contrasts directly when the issue is obeying someone's direction. [親](親.md) (おや), "parent," is the source of the instruction in the first example, not a special grammatical requirement.

## Distinctive meaning

逆らう can describe resistance to a natural force as well as defiance of a person. The verb alone does not judge whether resistance is justified. Keep the に pattern: English "defy an order" corresponds to 命令に逆らう (めいれいにさからう), not a mechanical を translation of the English object.
