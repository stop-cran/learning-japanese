# 案内 (あんない) — guidance

## Meaning

案内 is guidance or information. With する, it means to guide someone or show them around. In a person-and-destination pattern, を marks the person guided and に or へ marks the destination. When showing someone around an area, を can instead mark that area; the person being guided may be left understood.

お客さんを部屋に案内します。（おきゃくさんをへやにあんないします。） — I will show the guest to the room.

学校の中を案内してください。（がっこうのなかをあんないしてください。） — Please show me around the school.

## How the kanji combine

案 has meanings such as a plan or proposal; [内](../kanji/内.md) means inside. Both use on readings. Treat "guidance" as the established compound meaning, not a literal instruction to go inside.

## Synonyms and antonyms

説明（せつめい） is an explanation. 案内 often concerns finding one's way or using a facility; an explanation can concern any subject.

## Distinctive meaning

案内 can also be written information, as in 利用案内（りようあんない）, "instructions for use." A person need not physically lead you anywhere.
