# 修繕 (しゅうぜん) - repair; mending

## Meaning

修繕 is repairing or mending something damaged. 修繕する is transitive: mark the item repaired with を. Repairs can concern clothing, buildings, or other property.

- 古い屋根を修繕しました。
  (ふるいやねをしゅうぜんしました。) - We repaired the old roof.
- この建物は修繕が必要です。
  (このたてものはしゅうぜんがひつようです。) - This building needs repairs.

The first example names what someone repairs. The second uses the noun with が必要, "is necessary."

## How the kanji combine

修 contributes putting something right here, as in 修理, and 繕 contributes mending or repair. The related meanings reinforce repairing damage. The on readings are シュウ + ゼン, with ぜ rather than せ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[修理](修理.md) (しゅうり) and [直す](直す.md) (なおす) overlap with repairing something. Do not impose an absolute rule that 修理 is only for machines and 修繕 only for clothes: the roof example is also a repair.

[修正](修正.md) (しゅうせい) concerns correction or revision, such as changing a text or plan. A correction is not automatically physical mending.

## Distinctive meaning

修繕 presents an item as being put back into usable condition; it does not by itself say that the item has become completely new or that every defect has been eliminated. Repair and replacement are different ways to deal with damage.
