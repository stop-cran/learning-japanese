# 検査 (けんさ) - inspection; examination

## Meaning

検査 is an inspection, examination, or test that checks a condition or detects a problem. **Nを検査する** means to inspect or test N. **検査を受ける** (けんさをうける) means to undergo a test or examination.

- 工場で製品を検査します。 (こうじょうでせいひんをけんさします。) - We inspect the products at the factory.
- 明日、病院で検査を受けます。 (あした、びょういんでけんさをうけます。) - I am having a medical test at the hospital tomorrow.

The first subject performs the inspection; the second person receives it. 検査の結果 (けんさのけっか) is "the test results."

## How the kanji combine

検 contributes checking or examining, and 査 contributes investigating or examining. The near-synonymous components reinforce careful checking. Their on readings are ケン + サ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[試験](試験.md) (しけん) includes examinations of knowledge and tests of performance. [調査](調査.md) (ちょうさ) concerns investigation or gathering information. [検討](検討.md) (けんとう) is considering or examining a proposal, rather than automatically testing a physical item.

## Distinctive meaning

検査 can apply to medical samples, factory products, and baggage. It does not by itself mean that a problem was found or that treatment was given. Use the context to decide between English "inspection," "test," "scan," and "examination."
