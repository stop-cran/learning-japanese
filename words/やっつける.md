# やっつける - beat; finish off; get done

## Meaning

やっつける is a colloquial transitive ichidan verb: beat an opponent, attack or finish someone off, or get a task out of the way.

- ゲームで強い敵をやっつけた。
  (ゲームでつよいてきをやっつけた。) - I beat a strong enemy in the game.
- 残りの仕事をやっつけてから帰ろう。
  (のこりのしごとをやっつけてからかえろう。) - Let's get the remaining work done before going home.

The object takes を. Useful forms are やっつけます, やっつけた, and やっつけない.

## How the kanji combine

This word is normally written in kana. The dictionary also recognizes spellings such as 遣っ付ける, but the kana spelling does not require kanji metadata or a literal character-by-character derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[倒す](倒す.md) (たおす) and [破る](破る.md) (やぶる) overlap in defeating an opponent; 破る also means tear or break a rule. [攻撃](攻撃.md) (こうげき) concerns an attack, not necessarily a successful defeat.

[片付ける](片付ける.md) (かたづける) and [済ませる](済ませる.md) (すませる) overlap in getting work done. The task sense need not involve an enemy.

## Distinctive meaning

The wording can sound blunt or casual. In work contexts it can suggest dispatching the task quickly or carelessly, though carelessness is not mandatory in every use.

"Finish off" may imply killing in a suitable context, but beating an opponent in a game does not. Do not impose one outcome on every example.
