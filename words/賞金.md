# 賞金 (しょうきん) - prize money; monetary award

## Meaning

賞金 is money offered or given as a prize or reward. Use 賞金をもらう (しょうきんをもらう), "receive prize money," or 賞金が出る (しょうきんがでる), "prize money is offered."

- 大会で優勝して賞金をもらいました。 (たいかいでゆうしょうしてしょうきんをもらいました。) - I won the tournament and received prize money.
- このコンクールには賞金が出ます。 (このコンクールにはしょうきんがでます。) - This competition offers prize money.

The word identifies the monetary award, not the act of winning or the winner.

## How the kanji combine

[賞](../kanji/賞.md) contributes a prize or reward, and [金](../kanji/金.md) money. 金 specifies the form of the award: money. Read ショウ + キン, with a long しょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[賞品](賞品.md) (しょうひん) is an item awarded as a prize, such as a trophy or other goods. [賞](賞.md) (しょう) is broader and need not identify either money or a physical item. [優勝](優勝.md) (ゆうしょう) names championship victory, not its monetary reward.

## Distinctive meaning

A contest can award 賞金, 賞品, both, or neither. Do not translate 賞金 as the winner's salary: its award character matters. A reward need not be for a sporting victory, so the competition examples are not an exclusive definition.
