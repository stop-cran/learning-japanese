# 揃える (そろえる) - assemble a full set; arrange uniformly

## Meaning

揃える means to collect the needed items, arrange or prepare them, or make
things match. It is a transitive ichidan verb: 揃えます, 揃えない, 揃えて.
The things assembled or adjusted take を.

- 必要な書類を揃えました。 (ひつようなしょるいをそろえました。) - I assembled all the necessary documents.
- 箱の大きさを揃えました。 (はこのおおきさをそろえました。) - I made the boxes uniform in size.
- 玄関で靴を揃えてください。 (げんかんでくつをそろえてください。) - Please arrange the shoes neatly at the entrance.

Getting a full set and arranging existing things are related uses, not a
requirement that every example involve buying missing items.

## How the kanji combine

揃 writes そろ, followed by える. Compare [揃う](揃う.md) (そろう):
書類が揃った means "the documents are all together"; 書類を揃えた means
"someone assembled the documents." The が/を contrast distinguishes a
state or event from an action on the items.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[集める](集める.md) (あつめる) means collect or gather without always implying
an intended full set. [準備](準備.md) (じゅんび) is preparation more broadly.
[並べる](並べる.md) (ならべる) arranges things in a line or order; 揃える
also covers matching their size or obtaining all the required pieces.

## Distinctive meaning

揃える does not mean making things identical in every respect. 色を揃える
(いろをそろえる), "match the colors," specifies one relevant feature.
Learn what is being made complete, orderly, or uniform in each construction.
