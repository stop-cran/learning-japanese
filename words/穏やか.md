# 穏やか (おだやか) - calm; gentle; peaceful

## Meaning

穏やか is a な-adjective for calm conditions or a gentle, unagitated manner. Use 穏やかな before a noun, 穏やかに before a verb, and 穏やかです as a predicate. It can describe the sea, weather, a person's character, or how someone speaks.

- 今日は海が穏やかです。 (きょうはうみがおだやかです。) - The sea is calm today.
- 彼女は穏やかに話した。 (かのじょはおだやかにはなした。) - She spoke calmly and gently.

穏やかな話し合い (おだやかなはなしあい), "an amicable discussion," highlights the absence of an aggressive confrontation.

## How the kanji combine

穏 carries the idea of calmness or peacefulness. The whole native adjective is read おだやか; やか is part of its written ending. There is no second kanji to combine with it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[静か](静か.md) (しずか) often concerns quietness or little activity. 穏やか can instead focus on gentleness even when someone is speaking. [優しい](優しい.md) (やさしい) overlaps in descriptions of people but can emphasize kindness. [激しい](激しい.md) (はげしい), "intense/violent," provides a useful contrast for waves or behavior.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every "quiet person" as 穏やかな人. The phrase describes temperament, not simply how many words someone says. Likewise, 穏やかな天気 suggests mild, settled conditions rather than an absence of sound.
