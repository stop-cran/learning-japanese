# 自習 (じしゅう) - self-study

## Meaning

自習 is studying or practicing on one's own. 自習する describes doing
this learning without receiving instruction from a teacher at that time.
It can also name a school study period when the teacher is absent.

- 放課後、図書館で自習します。 (ほうかご、としょかんでじしゅうします。) - I will study on my own in the library after school.
- 先生がお休みなので、今日は自習です。 (せんせいがおやすみなので、きょうはじしゅうです。) - The teacher is away, so today we have a self-study period.

自習室 (じしゅうしつ) is a room for self-study.
Working independently does not require being the only person in the room.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) contributes oneself, and [習](../kanji/習.md)
contributes learning or practice: learning by oneself.
The on readings combine as ジ + シュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) and [学習](学習.md) (がくしゅう)
are broader study words. [練習](練習.md) (れんしゅう) emphasizes practice.
[習う](習う.md) (ならう) foregrounds receiving instruction.
Those perspectives help explain 自習 without making all learning activities
mutually exclusive.

## Distinctive meaning

自修 is another dictionary spelling for general self-study.
JMdict restricts its teacher-absent classroom sense to **自習**;
do not automatically apply that particular sense to 自修.
Independent study can involve assigned work, so 自習 does not itself
mean that nobody set the task.
