# 電力 (でんりょく) - electric power

## Meaning

電力 is electric power: the rate at which electrical energy is transferred or used.
It is measured in watts, W. The noun also appears when discussing the supply or consumption of power.

- この装置の消費電力は百ワットです。 (このそうちのしょうひでんりょくはひゃくワットです。) - This device's power consumption is 100 watts.
- 電力の供給が止まりました。 (でんりょくのきょうきゅうがとまりました。) - The power supply stopped.

消費電力 (しょうひでんりょく) identifies power consumption, not the total energy used over an unspecified period.

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) contributes electricity, modifying [力](../kanji/力.md), power.
The on readings are デン + リョク.
This is the technical electrical word, not a description of someone's muscular strength.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[電気](電気.md) (でんき), "electricity," can overlap in everyday supply discussions.
[電流](電流.md) (でんりゅう) is current, measured in amperes.
[エネルギー](エネルギー.md) is energy; electrical energy accumulated over time
is not the same quantity as power.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the units and quantities separate: watts measure power,
whereas watt-hours measure energy.
A device's power rating alone does not say how much energy it uses without a duration.
The vocabulary does not imply that greater current and greater power are interchangeable descriptions in every circuit.
