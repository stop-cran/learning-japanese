# ピンク - pink

## Meaning

ピンク names the color pink. Use ピンクの before a noun and
ピンクです to identify a color; do not add the i-adjective ending い.

- ピンクのシャツを買いました。 (ピンクのシャツをかいました。) - I bought a pink shirt.
- この花はピンクです。 (このはなはピンクです。) - This flower is pink.

## Synonyms and antonyms

桃色 (ももいろ), literally associated with peach blossoms, is another
word for pink. [赤](赤.md) (あか), red, is a related color rather than
an interchangeable name for every pink object. Colors do not have a
single lexical antonym here.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun can also modify a noun in an erotic or pornographic sense,
as in ピンク映画 (ピンクえいが), an erotic film. That additional
dictionary sense does not make a pink shirt or flower sexual.
The color sense is the selected N2 mapping on this card.
