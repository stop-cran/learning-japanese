# 不吉 (ふきつ) — ominous; unlucky

## Meaning

不吉 means ominous or unlucky. JMdict glosses it as “ominous; sinister; unlucky; inauspicious,” and Digital Daijisen defines it as being of ill omen, or showing signs of misfortune. It is used as a noun and as a na-adjective.

- 黒い猫は不吉だと言う人もいます。 (くろいねこはふきつだというひともいます。) — Some people say black cats are unlucky.
- 試合の前に靴ひもが切れて、不吉な気がしました。 (しあいのまえにくつひもがきれて、ふきつなきがしました。) — My shoelace broke before the match, and it felt like a bad omen.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) means not, and [吉](../kanji/吉.md) means good luck. Together, they mean not auspicious.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不運](不運.md) (ふうん) is misfortune or bad luck (JMdict entry 1491290), and [不幸](不幸.md) (ふこう) is unhappiness or misfortune (JMdict entry 1492350). 吉 きち is good fortune (JMdict entry 2230960), the word that 不 negates in 不吉.

## Distinctive meaning

不吉 is about omens: Digital Daijisen defines it as showing signs of misfortune, not as misfortune that has already happened.
