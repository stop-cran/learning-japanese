# うきうき — buoyant and cheerful

## Meaning

A light, happy, high-spirited feeling, often connected with something pleasant to come.

- 明日から旅行なので、うきうきしている。 — I feel cheerful and excited because my trip starts tomorrow.
- 友達からの手紙を読んで、うきうきした。 — Reading my friend's letter put me in high spirits.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[わくわく](わくわく.md) overlaps strongly in excited anticipation. [しょんぼり](しょんぼり.md) offers a downcast contrast; not every neutral mood needs either word.

## Distinctive meaning

Use うきうきする or うきうきした気分（きぶん）. The recorded spelling 浮き浮き is optional; this card keeps the usual kana form. The floating image is a learning association, not a claim about historical word formation.
