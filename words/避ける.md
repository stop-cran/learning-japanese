# 避ける (さける) - avoid a situation; evade

## Meaning

避ける (さける) means avoiding a situation or evading a subject, question, or responsibility. In these uses it is a transitive ichidan verb: the thing avoided takes を. Its forms include 避けます (さけます), 避けない (さけない), and 避けた (さけた).

- 混雑を避けて、早めに出発しました。 (こんざつをさけて、はやめにしゅっぱつしました。) - We left early to avoid the crowds.
- 彼はその質問に答えるのを避けた。 (かれはそのしつもんにこたえるのをさけた。) - He avoided answering that question.

The second example uses clause + のを to turn "answering that question" into the object.

## How the kanji combine

避 contributes avoidance. The native reading here is さける, with ける as okurigana. The same spelling also represents よける; the readings are not freely interchangeable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[逃げる](逃げる.md) (にげる) means escaping or running away. 避ける can describe planning not to encounter a problem in the first place. "Face the problem" is a contextual contrast, not a conjugation or fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

For physically dodging something, the dictionary distinguishes 避ける (よける): ボールをよけた, "dodged the ball." Its avoidance-of-contact sense is restricted to よける, whereas the situation/question sense is restricted to さける. The source gloss mixed these; retain さける for this card's main teaching.
