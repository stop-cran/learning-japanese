# 鏡 (かがみ) — mirror

## Meaning

鏡 is a mirror: a surface used to see a reflected image, especially your own face or appearance. It is an ordinary noun.

出かける前に鏡を見ます。 (でかけるまえにかがみをみます。) — I look in the mirror before going out.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 鏡 is read かがみ. This native reading differs from キョウ in compounds such as 望遠鏡 (ぼうえんきょう), "telescope." There is no smaller word combination to parse here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ミラー is a loanword also meaning "mirror," used especially in some product and equipment names. ガラス means glass, the material; ordinary glass is not necessarily a mirror.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese says 鏡を見る, literally "look at the mirror," where English often says "look in the mirror." 鏡に映る (かがみにうつる) means "be reflected in the mirror." The particle に marks the surface where the image appears, not the person doing the looking.
