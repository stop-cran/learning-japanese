# 内容 (ないよう) — contents; substance

## Meaning

内容 is what something contains: its content, substance, or details.
It is useful for what is said in a meeting, written in a message, or included in an explanation.
Use X の内容 for "the content of X."

- 会議の内容を説明してください。 (かいぎのないようをせつめいしてください。) — Please explain what was discussed at the meeting.
- この本の内容は難しいです。 (このほんのないようはむずかしいです。) — The content of this book is difficult.

## How the kanji combine

[内](../kanji/内.md) means "inside" and [容](../kanji/容.md) supplies a containing sense.
Together they direct attention to what is contained, not the container's outer appearance.
The on readings are ナイ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

中身 (なかみ) also means contents and can refer to what is physically inside something as well as its substance.
It overlaps with 内容, so "physical versus abstract" is not an absolute boundary.
[形式](形式.md) (けいしき), "form; format," offers a useful contrast: the way information is arranged versus what it says.

## Distinctive meaning

内容 is not limited to written text. A spoken explanation or meeting can have 内容.
Nor does naming the 内容 tell you whether it is good, detailed, or correct; those judgments need additional words.
In the book example, the difficulty is in what the book says, not necessarily in its physical design.
