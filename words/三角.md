# 三角 (さんかく) — triangle; triangular shape

## Meaning

三角 means a triangle or triangular shape. Before a noun it commonly takes の; 三角形（さんかくけい） is another word for a triangle.

ノートに三角を描いた。（ノートにさんかくをかいた。） — I drew a triangle in my notebook.

紙を三角に折ってください。（かみをさんかくにおってください。） — Please fold the paper into a triangular shape.

## How the kanji combine

[三](../kanji/三.md) サン, three, modifies [角](../kanji/角.md) カク, angle/corner: a three-cornered shape. Use the compound's on-reading さんかく, not さんかど.

## Synonyms and antonyms

四角（しかく） means a square or quadrilateral shape, while [角](角.md) かど names a corner. One corner is not itself a triangle. [数学](数学.md) すうがく is mathematics, the wider subject rather than this particular shape.

## Distinctive meaning

三角 names the whole shape, not an individual angle or the sum of its angle measurements. The に in 三角に折る marks the resulting shape. It does not turn the noun into a verb meaning fold.
