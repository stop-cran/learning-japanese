# いらっしゃる - be; come; go respectfully

## Meaning

いらっしゃる is the respectful counterpart of いる, 来る (くる), and 行く (いく).
The subject is someone being honored, not normally the speaker.
Context tells whether the person is present, coming, or going.

- 先生は教室にいらっしゃいます。 (せんせいはきょうしつにいらっしゃいます。) - The teacher is in the classroom.
- 明日、何時にいらっしゃいますか。 (あした、なんじにいらっしゃいますか。) - What time will you come tomorrow?

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is the ordinary spelling. Learn the word as a unit, not as an
invented combination of kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[おいでになる](おいでになる.md) also respectfully expresses being, coming, or going.
いる, 来る, and 行く are the corresponding ordinary verbs, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

This is a special godan verb: the polite form is いらっしゃいます, not
いらっしゃります. Its te-form is いらっしゃって.
After another verb's te-form it respectfully describes an ongoing action:
読んでいらっしゃる (よんでいらっしゃる), "is reading." Politeness to the listener
and respect toward the subject are related but distinct.
