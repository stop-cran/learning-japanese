# もたもた — moving slowly and inefficiently

## Meaning

もたもた describes dawdling or handling something inefficiently, often when quicker action is expected.

- もたもたしていると、電車に乗り遅れるよ。
  — If you dawdle, you'll miss the train.
- 荷物の準備にもたもたしてしまった。
  — I ended up taking too long to prepare my luggage.

## Synonyms and antonyms

もたもた suggests inefficient slowness; it can sound critical, unlike a neutral description of a relaxed pace.

## Distinctive meaning

It often appears with する and commonly implies that the delay is inconvenient.
