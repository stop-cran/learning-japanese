# ブレーキ (ブレーキ) - brake; restraint

## Meaning

ブレーキ is a noun meaning a brake. Figuratively, it can mean a check or restraint on a development or behavior.

- 赤信号を見て、ブレーキを踏みました。 (あかしんごうをみて、ブレーキをふみました。) - I saw the red light and pressed the brake.
- 費用の増加にブレーキをかける必要があります。 (ひようのぞうかにブレーキをかけるひつようがあります。) - We need to put the brakes on rising costs.

ブレーキを踏む describes pressing the brake pedal. ブレーキをかける means applying a brake or restraint; に can mark what is being restrained in the figurative construction.

## How the kanji combine

The katakana spelling has no kanji components. Keep the long vowel in レー: ブ・レー・キ. The word corresponds to English "brake," not "break" meaning a rest or damage.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[止める](止める.md) (とめる), "stop something," describes an action rather than the braking device. アクセル, "accelerator," names a contrasting vehicle control, not an antonym applicable to every figurative use. 停止 (ていし) names stopping or suspension.

## Distinctive meaning

The accompanying verb matters: ブレーキが利く (ブレーキがきく) means the brakes work effectively, while ブレーキをかける names applying them. Figurative ブレーキ does not require that the activity cease completely; it may describe checking or slowing its growth. The cost example is therefore about restraint, not a physical vehicle.
