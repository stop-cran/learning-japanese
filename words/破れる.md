# 破れる (やぶれる) - get torn; wear out; fall through

## Meaning

破れる is an intransitive ichidan verb meaning get torn, rip, or wear out. It can also describe negotiations or a similar arrangement breaking down.

- 袋が破れて、中の物が落ちました。
  (ふくろがやぶれて、なかのものがおちました。) - The bag tore, and the things inside fell out.
- 交渉が破れました。
  (こうしょうがやぶれました。) - The negotiations broke down.

The affected thing takes が. Useful forms are 破れます (やぶれます), 破れた (やぶれた), and 破れない (やぶれない).

## How the kanji combine

[破](../kanji/破.md) supplies the native stem やぶ, followed by れる. In the lexical verb taught here, the whole form is an intransitive ichidan verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[破く](破く.md) (やぶく) and [破る](破る.md) (やぶる) can describe someone tearing something: 紙を破く. Contrast 紙が破れる, the paper tears.

[壊れる](壊れる.md) (こわれる) has broader breaking or loss-of-function uses and also overlaps in negotiations falling through. Not every broken machine or shattered object is naturally described with 破れる.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep this spelling separate from 敗れる (やぶれる), lose or be defeated. Sharing a pronunciation does not merge their dictionary identities.

The potential form of the godan verb 破る is also written 破れる. Context and construction distinguish "can tear" from the independent intransitive verb here; the written ending alone is not enough.

Do not assume that every figurative use of transitive 破る has a matching intransitive use of 破れる.
