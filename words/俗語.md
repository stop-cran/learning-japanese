# 俗語 (ぞくご) — colloquial word; slang

## Meaning

俗語 means colloquial language or slang: informal words or expressions used in ordinary conversation.

- 「マジ」は「本当に」という意味で使われる俗語です。 (まじはほんとうにといういみでつかわれるぞくごです。) — “Maji” is a slang word used to mean “really.”
- この小説には、当時の俗語がいくつも出てきます。 (このしょうせつには、とうじのぞくごがいくつもでてきます。) — Several slang terms from that time appear in this novel.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[俗](../kanji/俗.md) relates to ordinary or colloquial things, and [語](../kanji/語.md) means word or language. Together they form a word for colloquial language or slang.

## Synonyms and antonyms

俗語 is a category label for colloquial language; it does not automatically say whether a particular expression is offensive.

## Distinctive meaning

Check the context before using an informal expression in formal writing.
