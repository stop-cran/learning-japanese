# すてき - lovely; wonderful

## Meaning

すてき expresses an appreciative judgment: lovely, wonderful, charming, or great. It is a na-adjective. Use すてきな before a noun and すてきです as a polite predicate.

- すてきな写真ですね。 (すてきなしゃしんですね。) - What a lovely photo.
- 昨日はすてきな時間を過ごしました。 (きのうはすてきなじかんをすごしました。) - I had a wonderful time yesterday.

The second example shows that the praise is not limited to visual beauty. An experience can be すてき too.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses kana. The recognized spelling 素敵 represents the same word, but JMdict marks it as ateji. Do not derive the meaning by combining literal glosses of 素 and 敵, such as an "enemy"; those would give a misleading explanation of the compliment.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[美しい](美しい.md) (うつくしい) means beautiful and has い-adjective grammar. [きれい](きれい.md) can mean beautiful or clean. すてき praises appeal or quality but does not specifically assert cleanliness.

## Distinctive meaning

すてき is an expression of the speaker's positive response, not an objective measurement of quality. It can describe people, clothes, places, ideas, and experiences when they seem appealing. In a compliment, すてきですね gives an appreciative reaction; it does not imply romantic interest by itself or restrict who may use the word.
