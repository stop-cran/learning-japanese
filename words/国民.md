# 国民 (こくみん) - people of a nation; citizens

## Meaning

国民 is a noun for the people of a country, considered as members of that nation. It can be collective, "the people," or refer to an individual citizen or national. Country + の国民 identifies which country's people are meant.

- 国民の意見を聞くことが大切です。 (こくみんのいけんをきくことがたいせつです。) - It is important to listen to the people's views.
- 彼女は日本国民です。 (かのじょはにほんこくみんです。) - She is a Japanese national.

The second sentence concerns national membership, not simply the place where she currently lives.

## How the kanji combine

[国](../kanji/国.md) means country or nation, and [民](../kanji/民.md) means people. 国 modifies 民: the people of a nation. The on readings are コク + ミン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[住民](住民.md) (じゅうみん) identifies residents of an area and does not by itself establish nationality. [市民](市民.md) (しみん) can mean city residents or citizens in a civic sense. Context determines how these groups overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that everyone physically in a country is included in 国民, particularly in legal or political statements. Conversely, a country's nationals may live abroad. The word itself does not explain a country's nationality laws or individual rights.
