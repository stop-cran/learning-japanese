# 体積 (たいせき) - volume (three-dimensional)

## Meaning

体積 is the volume occupied by a three-dimensional object or substance.
It is a noun, commonly measured in cubic units.

- この立方体の体積を求めてください。 (このりっぽうたいのたいせきをもとめてください。) - Please calculate the volume of this cube.
- 水は凍ると体積が増えます。 (みずはこおるとたいせきがふえます。) - Water increases in volume when it freezes.

立方センチメートル (りっぽうセンチメートル), "cubic centimeters," measures
volume; square centimeters instead measure area.

## How the kanji combine

[体](../kanji/体.md) contributes a solid body or object;
[積](../kanji/積.md) contributes the mathematical product association.
The compound concerns a body's volume. This is a learning association,
not a claim that every shape's volume is found by multiplying three
visible edge lengths. The on readings are タイ + セキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

面積 (めんせき) is area, a two-dimensional measure.
容積 (ようせき) concerns volume or a container's capacity; 容量
(ようりょう) has capacity uses more broadly. Their practical ranges
overlap, but the object and kind of capacity must be identified.

## Distinctive meaning

English "volume" can also mean loudness, but 音量 (おんりょう), not 体積,
is the ordinary word for that meaning. Likewise, a person's ability or
a computer's storage capacity is not automatically 体積 just because
English permits "capacity." A change in shape need not change volume.
