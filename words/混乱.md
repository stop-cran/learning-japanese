# 混乱 (こんらん) - confusion; disorder

## Meaning

混乱 is confusion, disorder, or chaos. It can describe a person's thinking or a disorganized situation. The intransitive verb 混乱する means "become confused; fall into disorder."

- 説明が違うので、頭が混乱しています。 (せつめいがちがうので、あたまがこんらんしています。) - The explanations differ, so I am confused.
- 大雪で交通が混乱しました。 (おおゆきでこうつうがこんらんしました。) - Heavy snow disrupted transportation.

The subject takes が. To cause confusion, use 混乱させる or 混乱を招く (こんらんをまねく).

## How the kanji combine

混 contributes mixing, and 乱 contributes disorder. The compound joins these related ideas into a loss of clear order. The on readings are コン + ラン; this is a useful meaning relationship, not a history of the characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[混雑](混雑.md) (こんざつ) concerns crowding or congestion. A crowded station may lead to 混乱, but crowding alone does not necessarily mean that people or procedures are confused.

## Distinctive meaning

混乱 can describe a temporary state caused by conflicting information; it need not imply a lack of intelligence. It also need not involve physical violence, despite the English gloss "chaos." 頭が混乱する locates the confusion in someone's thinking, while 交通が混乱する describes a system whose orderly functioning has been disrupted.
