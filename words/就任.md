# 就任 (しゅうにん) - taking office; assuming a post

## Meaning

就任 is taking up an office or post. 就任する is intransitive: the person takes the position, with the position marked by **に**.

- 彼女は四月に社長に就任しました。
  (かのじょはしがつにしゃちょうにしゅうにんしました。) - She took office as company president in April.
- 新しい市長が就任しました。
  (あたらしいしちょうがしゅうにんしました。) - The new mayor has taken office.

In the first example, 四月に gives the time, while 社長に identifies the office.

## How the kanji combine

就 contributes taking a position, and [任](../kanji/任.md) a duty or appointment. Together they express entering the responsibility. The on readings are シュウ + ニン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[就く](就く.md) (つく) also occurs in a position-taking pattern, such as 役職に就く (やくしょくにつく), "take a post." [就職](就職.md) (しゅうしょく) is obtaining employment, not necessarily assuming a named office.

退任 (たいにん), leaving an office, is the direct position-taking contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

English "inauguration" may suggest a ceremony, but 就任 does not require a public celebration. Nor does "installation" here mean installing a computer program. 就任式 (しゅうにんしき) explicitly names a ceremony marking the assumption of office.
