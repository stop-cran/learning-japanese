# 沈む (しずむ) - sink; become downcast

## Meaning

沈む means to sink or go below a surface. It also describes the sun setting and a person's mood becoming low. It is an intransitive godan verb: 沈みます, 沈んだ, 沈まない.

- 石が水の底に沈みました。 (いしがみずのそこにしずみました。) - The stone sank to the bottom of the water.
- 太陽が海に沈んでいきます。 (たいようがうみにしずんでいきます。) - The sun is sinking below the sea's horizon.

The sinking thing takes が. に can mark the place it sinks to; in the sunset example the description is visual, not a claim that the sun enters the water.

## How the kanji combine

沈 supplies the sinking idea and is read しず before む. This is one native verb. Its て-form is 沈んで, not 沈みて.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[浮く](浮く.md) (うく), "float; rise to the surface," provides a physical contrast. 沈める (しずめる) is the transitive partner, "make something sink," not the opposite action.

## Distinctive meaning

気分が沈む (きぶんがしずむ) means "feel down." This figurative use describes a low mood without by itself naming a medical condition. 沈んでいる can describe a submerged object's resulting state or someone's subdued mood; the subject tells you which meaning fits.
