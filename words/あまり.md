# あまり (あまり) - not very; not much

## Meaning

あまり with a negative predicate means "not very" or "not much." The negative ending is essential to this beginner pattern; あまり alone does not supply "not."

- この料理はあまり辛くないです。 (このりょうりはあまりからくないです。) - This dish is not very spicy.
- テレビはあまり見ません。 (テレビはあまりみません。) - I do not watch much television.

With a noun or na-adjective predicate, the same pattern works: あまり静かではありません (あまりしずかではありません), "it is not very quiet."

## How the kanji combine

This adverb is usually written in kana. 余り is another spelling; the related noun can mean a remainder or surplus. Do not substitute that noun meaning into every sentence.
The remainder noun is read あまり; that sense is not assigned to the conversational adverb あんまり in the inspected dictionary entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あんまり](あんまり.md) is a conversational variant of the adverb. [全然](全然.md) (ぜんぜん) with a negative can mean "not at all," a stronger denial than "not very."

## Distinctive meaning

Affirmative expressions such as あまりにも can mean "excessively." Always inspect the whole construction: the negative pattern taught here limits degree or frequency rather than asserting excess.
