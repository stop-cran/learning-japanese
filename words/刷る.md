# 刷る (する) - print

## Meaning

刷る means to print, for example sheets, books, or leaflets. It is a
transitive verb: the material being printed takes を.

- チラシを百枚刷りました。 (チラシをひゃくまいすりました。) - We printed one hundred leaflets.
- この本は何部刷りますか。 (このほんはなんぶすりますか。) - How many copies of this book will you print?

The dictionary also records applying color or a pattern to cloth using
a wooden block. The spelling 摺る is another recorded form.

## How the kanji combine

[刷](../kanji/刷.md) contributes printing. The native reading is す,
followed by る; learn the whole dictionary form する.
This is not the on reading サツ used in [印刷](印刷.md), printing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

印刷する also means to print. [コピー](コピー.md) and [写す](写す.md)
concern copying more broadly; making a copy is not always printing.
[出版](出版.md) concerns publication, which is not identical to the
physical printing operation.

## Distinctive meaning

Despite the identical pronunciation, 刷る is a regular godan verb, not
general-purpose irregular [する](する.md), do. Compare:

- 刷ります / 刷って / 刷らない (すります / すって / すらない) - print / print and... / do not print
- します / して / しない - do / do and... / do not do

Do not form the printing verb's polite form as 刷します.
