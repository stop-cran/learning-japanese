# 親 (おや) — parent; parents

## Meaning

親 means a parent or parents. The word itself does not specify mother versus father,
and context determines whether one parent or more than one is meant.
As a noun, it connects to another noun with の: 親の仕事（おやのしごと）, "a parent's job."

- 親に電話しました。（おやにでんわしました。） — I called my parents.
- 親と一緒に住んでいます。（おやといっしょにすんでいます。） — I live with my parents.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun: [親](../kanji/親.md) is read おや, with no okurigana.
It is not the シン reading used in compounds such as 両親（りょうしん）, "both parents."

## Synonyms and antonyms

両親 explicitly means both parents. [父](../kanji/父.md) ちち and [母](../kanji/母.md) はは specify father and mother,
and are the usual non-honorific choices when speaking about your own parents to others.
The counterpart in the family relationship is [子](../kanji/子.md), "child," rather than an opposite kind of parent.

## Distinctive meaning

親 is an ordinary relationship noun, not an honorific way of addressing someone's mother or father.
For familiar direct address, compare [お母さん](お母さん.md) おかあさん and [お父さん](お父さん.md) おとうさん.
Do not assume that 親 always means both parents; use 両親 when that distinction matters.
