# 飲む (のむ) — to drink

## Meaning

飲む is to drink: 水を飲む (drink water), お茶を飲みませんか (would you like some tea?), 薬を飲む (take medicine), 飲み会 のみかい (drinking party).
It also means "to swallow": 飲み込む のみこむ, and 要求を飲む (accept a demand).

## How the kanji combine

[飲](../kanji/飲.md) with the kun reading の plus okurigana む. A godan (う-verb): polite 飲みます, negative 飲まない, て-form 飲んで, past 飲んだ.
The noun form 飲み appears in [飲み物](飲み物.md) のみもの (drink) and 飲み会 のみかい.
The て／た forms use the ん sound change typical of む-verbs (読む→読んで).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: [食べる](食べる.md) (eat), 吸う すう (inhale, smoke), 召し上がる (respectful "eat or drink").
- 飲酒 いんしゅ is "drinking alcohol" in writing; お酒を飲む is the everyday phrase.

## Distinctive meaning

Medicine is 飲む in Japanese (薬を飲む), as the pill is swallowed, whereas English "take". Soup is 飲む or 食べる depending on how it is eaten.
