# 桑 (くわ) — mulberry tree

## Meaning

桑 is the **mulberry tree**. It names a kind of tree, not trees in general.

- 庭に桑の木があります。（にわにくわのきがあります。）— There is a mulberry tree in the garden.
- 桑の葉を集めました。（くわのはをあつめました。）— I gathered mulberry leaves.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [桑](../kanji/桑.md) writes the native plant name くわ. There are no separately pronounced components within this word. クワ is also used as a katakana plant-name spelling; it is not evidence that the word is a foreign loanword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[木](木.md) き is the broader word for a tree. 桑 specifies mulberry. 桑の木 (くわのき), mulberry tree, and 桑の葉 (くわのは), mulberry leaves, identify the tree and its leaves respectively.

There is no useful direct antonym for a tree name.

## Distinctive meaning

くわ in this article is a plant name. A shared pronunciation with another word does not make the meanings interchangeable. When describing the fruit, 桑の実 (くわのみ) makes that part of the tree explicit.
