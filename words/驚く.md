# 驚く (おどろく) — be surprised

## Meaning

驚く means to be surprised, amazed, or startled. It is an intransitive godan verb: 驚きます, 驚いて, 驚いた. The person experiencing surprise is the subject; its cause often takes に.

その話に驚きました。（そのはなしにおどろきました。） — I was surprised by that story.

大きな音に驚きました。（おおきなおとにおどろきました。） — I was startled by a loud noise.

## How the kanji combine

驚 expresses surprise. Here its native reading is おどろ, with く written as the verb ending. Learn the normal spelling 驚く rather than its rare dictionary alternatives.

## Synonyms and antonyms

びっくりする is a conversational alternative. 驚かす（おどろかす） means to surprise someone: it describes causing the reaction.

## Distinctive meaning

The surprise may be pleasant, unpleasant, or simply unexpected. 驚く by itself does not mean that the person is delighted or frightened; the situation determines that nuance.
