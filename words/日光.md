# 日光 (にっこう) - sunlight

## Meaning

日光 is sunlight or sunshine: light coming from the sun. It is a noun.

- この植物には日光が必要です。 (このしょくぶつにはにっこうがひつようです。) - This plant needs sunlight.
- 窓から日光が入ってきました。 (まどからにっこうがはいってきました。) - Sunlight came in through the window.

日光を浴びる (にっこうをあびる) means "be exposed to sunlight" or "bask in the sun." Here を marks the light received, not a physical object being held.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) contributes sun, and [光](../kanji/光.md) contributes light. The relation is source plus light: "sun's light." The on-reading combination has a small っ, giving にっこう rather than にちこう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[太陽](太陽.md) (たいよう) names the sun itself, not its light. 日差し (ひざし) also concerns sunlight, especially its shining or felt strength. 日陰 (ひかげ), "shade," is a useful environmental contrast, not a substance opposite to sunlight.

## Distinctive meaning

日光 is not any bright light: an electric lamp produces light without producing 日光. The same spelling and reading also name Nikkō, the city in Tochigi Prefecture. A sentence about visiting 日光 may concern the place; a plant needing 日光 concerns sunlight.
