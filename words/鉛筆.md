# 鉛筆 (えんぴつ) — pencil

## Meaning

鉛筆 is a pencil, especially the ordinary wooden kind. Use で to mark it as a writing tool, and 本 (ほん) to count pencils.

鉛筆で名前を書いてください。 (えんぴつでなまえをかいてください。) — Please write your name in pencil.

## How the kanji combine

鉛 means "lead" and 筆 means "writing brush" or a writing implement. The compound is read えんぴつ; 筆 has the form ぴつ here. The spelling is not a statement that a modern pencil contains metallic lead: its writing core is graphite-based.

## Synonyms and antonyms

シャープペンシル, often shortened to シャーペン, is a mechanical pencil. ボールペン is a ballpoint pen. These are alternative writing instruments, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

鉛筆 does not normally mean any pen-shaped object. Useful associated phrases include 鉛筆を削る (えんぴつをけずる), "sharpen a pencil," and 色鉛筆 (いろえんぴつ), "colored pencil." 一本 (いっぽん), 二本 (にほん), and 三本 (さんぼん) show the pronunciation changes in its usual counter.
