# 被害 (ひがい) - damage; harm suffered

## Meaning

被害 is damage, injury, or harm viewed from the side that suffers it.
It is a noun: 被害を受ける (ひがいをうける), "suffer damage," and 被害が出る (ひがいがでる), "damage occurs/is reported."
Do not turn it into a general verb by simply adding する.

- 台風で多くの家が被害を受けました。 (たいふうでおおくのいえがひがいをうけました。) - Many houses were damaged by the typhoon.
- この地域では大きな被害はありませんでした。 (このちいきではおおきなひがいはありませんでした。) - There was no major damage in this area.

The second sentence rules out major damage, not necessarily every minor effect.

## How the kanji combine

被 contributes suffering or being subjected to something, and [害](../kanji/害.md) harm.
Together they highlight harm received.
The on-readings are ヒ + ガイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[損害](損害.md) (そんがい) overlaps with damage or loss, especially when assessing a loss.
[怪我](怪我.md) (けが) is a physical injury; 被害 also covers property and other harms.
加害 (かがい), "inflicting harm," describes the other side of the relationship, not the absence of damage.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun appears with disasters, accidents, and crimes, so it does not by itself establish deliberate wrongdoing.
被害者 (ひがいしゃ) is a victim or affected person, while 被害額 (ひがいがく) is the monetary amount of damage.
Neither expression implies that every kind of harm can be fully measured in money.
