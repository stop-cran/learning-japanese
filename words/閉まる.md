# 閉まる (しまる) - close; be shut

## Meaning

閉まる describes something closing, rather than someone deliberately closing it. It is an intransitive godan verb: 閉まります, 閉まって, 閉まった and 閉まらない.

- ドアが閉まりました。 (ドアがしまりました。) - The door closed.
- この店は六時に閉まります。 (このみせはろくじにしまります。) - This shop closes at six.

閉まっている describes the resulting closed state. The thing that closes is marked with が or presented as the topic with は.

## How the kanji combine

[閉](../kanji/閉.md) contributes closing, followed by the okurigana まる. Learn the complete verb しまる alongside its forms.

## Synonyms and antonyms

閉める (しめる) is transitive: ドアを閉める means someone closes the door. 開く (あく), "open," is the intransitive opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse 閉まる with 締まる, the usual spelling for becoming tight. Although dictionary entries group overlapping senses, the ordinary door-closing spelling is 閉まる.
