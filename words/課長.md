# 課長 (かちょう) — section manager

## Meaning

課長 is the **manager or chief of a section** in a company or other organization. It is a job title, not a word for any experienced employee.

課長に相談しました。 (かちょうにそうだんしました。) — I consulted the section manager.

The title can follow a surname: 田中課長 (たなかかちょう), “Section Manager Tanaka.” It can also address the person directly in a workplace.

## How the kanji combine

課 means a section or organizational unit here; [長](../kanji/長.md) means its head. The on readings カ + チョウ combine to form かちょう. 課 can mean a lesson elsewhere, but “lesson chief” is not the intended analysis.

## Synonyms and antonyms

部長 (ぶちょう) heads a department or division, often above a 課長. 社長 (しゃちょう) is a company president. Exact hierarchy and English job-title translations depend on the organization.

## Distinctive meaning

課長 describes responsibility for a named organizational section. It is more specific than 上司 (じょうし), “one's superior/boss,” which describes a relationship rather than a particular rank.
