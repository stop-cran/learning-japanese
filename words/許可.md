# 許可 (きょか) - permission; authorization

## Meaning

許可 is permission or authorization for an action. 許可する is transitive: the person granting permission allows an action marked with を. The recipient can take に.

- 先生が私たちに教室の使用を許可しました。 (せんせいがわたしたちにきょうしつのしようをきょかしました。) - The teacher allowed us to use the classroom.
- 写真を撮るには許可が必要です。 (しゃしんをとるにはきょかがひつようです。) - Permission is required to take photographs.

許可を得る (きょかをえる) means "obtain permission." 許可なく (きょかなく) means "without permission."

## How the kanji combine

[許](../kanji/許.md) contributes allowing, and 可 contributes approval or acceptability. The two related meanings combine around authorizing an action. The on readings are キョ + カ; the first part is きょ, not きょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[許す](許す.md) (ゆるす) can mean allow, but also forgive. 許可 is about authorization, not forgiveness for a past wrong. [承認](承認.md) (しょうにん) is approval or recognition and can overlap in administrative settings.

[禁止](禁止.md) (きんし), "prohibition," contrasts with permission.

## Distinctive meaning

Permission concerns whether an action is allowed, not whether it is physically possible. It can come from a teacher, owner, organization, or public authority; it need not be a government license. In conversation, asking 写真を撮ってもいいですか, "May I take a photograph?", can request 許可 without using the noun itself.
