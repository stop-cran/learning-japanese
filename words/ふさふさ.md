# ふさふさ — thick and luxuriant

## Meaning

ふさふさ describes hair, fur, or foliage growing thickly in soft, abundant tufts.

- その犬はふさふさしたしっぽを振っている。
  — The dog is wagging its bushy tail.
- ふさふさの髪が風になびいた。
  — Her thick, luxuriant hair moved in the breeze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ふさふさ suggests abundant, full growth, unlike sparse or thin hair.

## Distinctive meaning

It commonly modifies hair, fur, or other things that grow in tufts.
