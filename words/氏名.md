# 氏名 (しめい) - full name

## Meaning

氏名 means a person's full name. It is useful on forms and in requests for identifying information: **氏名を記入する** (しめいをきにゅうする), "enter one's full name."

- ここに氏名を書いてください。
  (ここにしめいをかいてください。) - Please write your full name here.
- 申込書には氏名と住所が必要です。
  (もうしこみしょにはしめいとじゅうしょがひつようです。) - The application form requires your full name and address.

The dictionary gloss "identity" concerns identifying a person by name. 氏名 does not mean an identity card, personality, or every aspect of someone's identity.

## How the kanji combine

氏 contributes the family or clan name, and [名](../kanji/名.md) the name. The compound brings together the family and personal name: シ + メイ becomes しめい.

JMdict also records うじな. Learn **しめい** for the full-name use here; the separate public-dictionary うじな heading concerns a surname, so its explanation must not be pooled with this one.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[名前](名前.md) (なまえ) is broader and less administrative: it can name a person, animal, or thing, and context can make it a full name. [名](名.md) (な) also overlaps in "name." 姓 (せい), "surname," identifies only the family-name part, not the entire 氏名.

## Distinctive meaning

氏名 is a noun, not a verb meaning "introduce yourself." A request for 氏名 does not by itself specify a particular writing system or name order. Follow the form's separate instructions, including any フリガナ field for the pronunciation.
