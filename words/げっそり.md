# げっそり — disheartened; dejected

## Meaning

げっそり describes looking or feeling deeply discouraged or deflated.

- 大会が中止だと聞き、選手たちはげっそりした。
  — The athletes were disheartened when they heard the tournament was canceled.
- 休みの前日に追加の仕事を頼まれて、彼はげっそりした。
  — He was deflated when he was given extra work on the day before his time off.

## Synonyms and antonyms

げっそり can describe a strongly deflated reaction. がっかり focuses on disappointment, while
しょんぼり describes looking downcast or subdued.

## Distinctive meaning

This card focuses on the discouraged sense. The dictionary also records uses for becoming very
thin; context determines which meaning is intended.
