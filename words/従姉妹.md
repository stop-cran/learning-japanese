# 従姉妹 (いとこ) - female cousin

## Meaning

従姉妹 is a female cousin: a daughter of one's aunt or uncle.
The reading taught here is いとこ. The dictionary marks the word as
usually written in kana.

- 従姉妹と映画を見ました。 (いとことえいがをみました。) - I watched a film with my female cousin.
- 彼女は母の妹の娘で、私の従姉妹です。 (かのじょはははのいもうとのむすめで、わたしのいとこです。) - She is my mother's younger sister's daughter, so she is my cousin.

The spelling indicates a female cousin, but does not by itself establish
whether she is older or younger than the person whose cousin she is.

## How the kanji combine

従 has a following or secondary-relationship association;
[姉](../kanji/姉.md) and [妹](../kanji/妹.md) mean older and younger
sister. The established spelling names a cousin relationship, not one's
own sisters. Read いとこ as a whole-word reading, not one syllable per
character. The dictionary also lists じゅうしまい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[従兄弟](従兄弟.md) (いとこ) specifies a male cousin.
[親戚](親戚.md) (しんせき) means relatives more broadly.
Kana いとこ is useful when the written male/female distinction is not
needed. A cousin is not a sibling merely because the people are close.

## Distinctive meaning

The same spoken いとこ can correspond to different cousin spellings.
Do not change the reading to あね or いもうと just because 姉 or 妹
appears. Those are readings of the separate sister words, not this
whole-word cousin reading.
