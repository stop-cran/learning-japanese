# もぐもぐ — chewing; mumbling

## Meaning

Small repeated mouth movements while chewing, or speech that stays indistinct in the mouth.

- 子どもがパンをもぐもぐ食べている。 — The child is chewing some bread.
- 口の中でもぐもぐ言わずに、もう一度話してください。 — Please say it again without mumbling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[むしゃむしゃ](むしゃむしゃ.md) emphasizes hearty eating, while [ぱくぱく](ぱくぱく.md) can highlight repeated opening of the mouth. [ぼそぼそ](ぼそぼそ.md) overlaps in subdued, indistinct speech.

## Distinctive meaning

もぐもぐ食べる and もぐもぐ言う（いう）need different translations. Chewing need not be noisy or greedy. The dictionary also records wriggling movement, but that is not the main learning scene here.
