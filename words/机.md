# 机 (つくえ) — desk

## Meaning

机 is a desk: a piece of furniture used for writing, studying, or similar work. It is a noun and does not itself specify a school or office.

机の上に本があります。 (つくえのうえにほんがあります。) — There is a book on the desk.

この机で毎日勉強します。 (このつくえでまいにちべんきょうします。) — I study at this desk every day.

## How the kanji combine

机 alone writes the word つくえ. There is no compound to divide. Learn all three kana in the reading; the character is not read with an on-reading in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

デスク also means desk. テーブル is generally a table, including a dining table; the words overlap for some furniture but are not identical in every setting. 椅子 (いす), "chair," is an associated object rather than an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

机の上 (つくえのうえ) identifies the space on top of the desk. In the study example, で marks where the action takes place. A desk is not the same as a workplace or office: Japanese uses other nouns for those locations.
