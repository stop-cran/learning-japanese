# 自然 (しぜん) - nature; natural

## Meaning

自然 is a noun meaning "nature" and a na-adjective meaning "natural"
or "unforced." As an adverb, 自然に or 自然と can mean
"naturally" or "spontaneously."

- この島には豊かな自然があります。 (このしまにはゆたかなしぜんがあります。) - This island has a rich natural environment.
- 写真では自然な笑顔を見せてください。 (しゃしんではしぜんなえがおをみせてください。) - Please give a natural smile for the photograph.
- その話を聞いて、自然と涙が出ました。 (そのはなしをきいて、しぜんとなみだがでました。) - Hearing that story, tears came to my eyes spontaneously.

Use な before a noun when describing its natural quality; compare
自然の美しさ, "the beauty of nature," where の connects two nouns.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) contributes "self," and [然](../kanji/然.md)
contributes "so" or "being that way." Together they suggest being so
of itself. This component association helps connect nature with things
happening without deliberate intervention.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[天然](天然.md) (てんねん) also concerns natural origin.
[普通](普通.md) (ふつう) means ordinary or usual, which is not
necessarily the same as unforced. 不自然 (ふしぜん), "unnatural;
awkward," is the direct negative counterpart in many descriptive uses.

## Distinctive meaning

自然 does not automatically mean environmentally beneficial or morally
good. 自然な日本語 means natural-sounding Japanese, not language
about forests. Decide whether the sentence names the natural world,
describes a quality, or explains how something happens.
