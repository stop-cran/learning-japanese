# 手続き (てつづき) - procedure; formalities

## Meaning

手続き means the required steps or formal procedure for getting something done, especially an application, registration, or legal/administrative process. 手続 is another spelling.

- 入学の手続きを済ませました。 (にゅうがくのてつづきをすませました。) - I completed the enrollment formalities.
- この窓口で住所変更の手続きができます。 (このまどぐちでじゅうしょへんこうのてつづきができます。) - You can complete the change-of-address procedure at this counter.

## How the kanji combine

[手](../kanji/手.md) contributes a way of doing something, and [続](../kanji/続.md) connects with continuing or following through. The native compound is て + つづき, not an all-on reading. The written づ in てつづき belongs to the 続く (つづく) family.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[順序](順序.md) can refer to an order or procedure; [方法](方法.md) is a method more generally. [処理](処理.md) describes processing or handling something. 手続き focuses on the prescribed steps or formalities, not every practical operation or change during a process.

## Distinctive meaning

手続きをする is a useful noun construction, as in 申請の手続きをする (しんせいのてつづきをする), "carry out the application procedure." This pattern does not establish a universal ban on every other verbal construction. The word can describe procedures beyond government paperwork, but it does not simply mean any event occurring in sequence.
