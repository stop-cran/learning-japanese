# 墨 (すみ) - sumi ink; inkstick

## Meaning

墨 names traditional ink used for calligraphy or ink painting, including
an inkstick and the ink prepared from it. Liquid calligraphy ink is also
within this material meaning.

- 墨で名前を書きました。 (すみでなまえをかきました。) - I wrote my name in sumi ink.
- イカの墨を使った料理です。 (イカのすみをつかったりょうりです。) - This is a dish made with squid ink.

The second illustrates another ordinary sense: squid or octopus ink.
The dictionary also records a carpenter's inking string; the word does
not always identify a bottle of writing liquid.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji noun with the whole reading すみ, not a compound
whose parts receive separate readings. The character is 墨, not the
homophone [隅](隅.md), corner.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[インク](インク.md) and [インキ](インキ.md) are broader ink names used for
writing and printing. Their English translations overlap with 墨, but
that does not make every printer ink a traditional calligraphy material.
There is no useful lexical opposite meaning an opposite kind of ink.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the context to distinguish an inkstick, prepared ink, animal ink, or
the carpenter's tool. The すみ reading is the one taught here; other uses
of the written character do not change this word to ぼく.
