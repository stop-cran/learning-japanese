# 在学 (ざいがく) - being enrolled in school

## Meaning

在学 is attending an educational institution as a student or being enrolled there. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb. 学校に在学している describes being a student at that school.

- 姉は大学に在学しています。 (あねはだいがくにざいがくしています。) - My older sister is enrolled at university.
- 在学中に留学しました。 (ざいがくちゅうにりゅうがくしました。) - I studied abroad while I was a student.

在学中 (ざいがくちゅう) means during one's period of enrollment. 在学している describes the continuing state, not just entering the building.

## How the kanji combine

[在](../kanji/在.md) contributes being or existing in a place, and [学](../kanji/学.md) contributes study or schooling. Together they concern being at an institution as a student. The on readings are ザイ + ガク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入学](入学.md) (にゅうがく) concerns entering or enrolling at a school; 在学 concerns the period of being its student. [通学](通学.md) (つうがく) focuses on commuting to school, while [出席](出席.md) (しゅっせき) concerns attendance at a class, meeting, or similar occasion.

## Distinctive meaning

Someone can be enrolled without being physically on campus at the moment. A visitor standing inside a school is not thereby 在学している. [卒業](卒業.md) (そつぎょう), graduation, marks a different stage; 在学 alone does not say whether or when a person will graduate.
