# 光 (ひかり) — light

## Meaning

光 names light itself: what shines from a source, passes through a window, or reaches your eyes.
It is a noun, not the verb "shine."
Use a source followed by の, as in 太陽の光 (たいようのひかり), "sunlight."

- 窓から光が入ってきます。 (まどからひかりがはいってきます。) — Light comes in through the window.
- 太陽の光がまぶしいです。 (たいようのひかりがまぶしいです。) — The sunlight is dazzling.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [光](../kanji/光.md) writes the whole native noun ひかり.
There is no extra り after it in this spelling.
By contrast, the related verb 光る (ひかる), "shine," writes る after the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

明かり あかり also means light or illumination and can name a lamp or other light.
日光 にっこう specifically means sunlight.
[暗い](暗い.md) くらい, "dark," describes a contrasting condition; it is an adjective, not a noun that replaces 光.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the light from the object producing it.
明かりをつけてください (あかりをつけてください), "Please turn on the light," names a controllable light.
In 窓から光が入る (まどからひかりがはいる), "light enters through the window," 光 is the light entering,
not a lamp moving into the room.
