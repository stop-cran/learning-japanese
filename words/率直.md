# 率直 (そっちょく) - frank; candid

## Meaning

率直 means frank or candid: expressing a view openly rather than hiding
what one really thinks. Use **率直な + noun** or **率直に + verb**.

- 率直な意見を聞かせてください。 (そっちょくないけんをきかせてください。) - Please give me your candid opinion.
- 率直に言って、その案には賛成できません。 (そっちょくにいって、そのあんにはさんせいできません。) - Frankly, I cannot agree with that proposal.

The second example introduces an openly stated assessment; it does not
turn the assessment into an objective fact.

## How the kanji combine

率 contributes a straightforward or unadorned quality here, and
[直](../kanji/直.md) contributes straightness or directness.
Their related meanings reinforce candid expression.
The on readings ソツ + チョク appear as そっちょく, including the small っ.
率 has other readings in words about rates; those do not replace ソツ here.

**卒直 (そっちょく)** is a rare dictionary spelling of this same word.
Recognize 卒直 when encountered; the examples use the usual 率直.
[卒](../kanji/卒.md) in that alias does not make frankness a graduation meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[正直](正直.md) (しょうじき) overlaps in honesty, and
[素直](素直.md) (すなお) can describe an open, straightforward attitude.
[まっすぐ](まっすぐ.md) also has a figurative straightforward/honest use,
besides its directional meaning.
These overlaps do not make every honest person outspoken in every situation.

## Distinctive meaning

率直 is not an automatic synonym for rude or tactless.
A request for a candid opinion does not require insulting language.
Nor is it a word for a geometrically straight line: the straightness here
concerns expression or attitude.
