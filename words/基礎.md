# 基礎 (きそ) - foundation; basis

## Meaning

基礎 is a foundation or basis, either physical or figurative. It is a noun and can modify another noun with の.

- 建物の基礎を調べました。
  (たてもののきそをしらべました。) - We examined the building's foundations.
- 日本語の基礎をしっかり学びたいです。
  (にほんごのきそをしっかりまなびたいです。) - I want to learn the basics of Japanese thoroughly.

## How the kanji combine

基 contributes a base, and 礎 a foundation or foundation stone. The related meanings reinforce one another. The on readings are キ + ソ. This association helps with meaning; it is not a claim about the historical origin of either character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[基本](基本.md) (きほん) also concerns basics or a foundation; [基](基.md) (もと) is another basis or foundation word. [基準](基準.md) (きじゅん) is a standard or criterion used for judgment. A foundation supports something; a criterion provides a reference for assessing it.

## Distinctive meaning

In learning, 基礎 points to knowledge or skills that support later work, not necessarily knowledge that is easy for everyone. 基礎を固める (きそをかためる) means "strengthen the foundations"; context determines whether the foundations are concrete or figurative.
