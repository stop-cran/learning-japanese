# 炎 (ほのお) - flame; blaze

## Meaning

炎 is a flame or blaze: the visible burning part of a fire. It is a noun. Useful combinations include 炎が上がる (ほのおがあがる), "flames rise," and 炎が消える (ほのおがきえる), "a flame goes out."

- ろうそくの炎が揺れています。 (ろうそくのほのおがゆれています。) - The candle flame is flickering.
- 暖炉の中で炎が上がっています。 (だんろのなかでほのおがあがっています。) - Flames are rising in the fireplace.

The word can also appear figuratively for intense emotion, as in 情熱の炎 (じょうねつのほのお), "the flame of passion."

## How the kanji combine

炎 is one written character, carrying the native reading ほのお. Its visible shape contains two 火 forms, which can serve as a memory aid for flames; this is not a historical explanation. Spell the reading with おお, not おう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[火](火.md) (ひ) is fire more broadly. [煙](煙.md) (けむり) is smoke, not flame. [火事](火事.md) (かじ) is a fire as an incident, such as a building fire.

## Distinctive meaning

A small candle has an 炎 without there being a 火事. Conversely, describing smoke does not necessarily describe a visible flame. Choose the noun according to the part of the scene you want to identify.
