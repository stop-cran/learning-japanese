# 初めて (はじめて) — for the first time

## Meaning

初めて marks a first experience or first occurrence.
As an adverb it modifies the action directly; use 初めての before a noun for "one's first ..."

- 昨日、初めて日本料理を作りました。 (きのう、はじめてにほんりょうりをつくりました。) — Yesterday I cooked Japanese food for the first time.
- これは初めての海外旅行です。 (これははじめてのかいがいりょこうです。) — This is my first trip abroad.

## How the kanji combine

[初](../kanji/初.md) contributes a first occurrence, followed by the kana めて.
The whole expression has the native reading はじめて.
For your own examples, use 初めて for this adverb.
JMdict also records 始めて as a spelling of it, but 始めて can instead be the て-form of [始める](始める.md) はじめる, "begin."
Context distinguishes the first-time adverb from the verb form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

最初 さいしょ identifies a beginning or the first item in an order.
まず, "first of all," introduces the first step of a sequence and is usually written in kana.
Neither automatically says that the speaker has never done the activity before.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 初めて勉強しました (はじめてべんきょうしました), "I studied it for the first time,"
with 勉強を始めてください (べんきょうをはじめてください), "Please start studying."
The sound はじめて is shared, but first experience and starting an action are different.

After another verb's て-form, **verb-て + 初めて** can also mean "only after doing ..."
自分でやって初めて分かりました (じぶんでやってはじめてわかりました) means
"I understood only after trying it myself."
