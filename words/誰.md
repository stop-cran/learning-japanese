# 誰 (だれ) — who

## Meaning

誰 asks which person is meant. Particles show the person's role in the sentence.

- 誰がこの料理を作りましたか。 (だれがこのりょうりをつくりましたか。) — Who made this dish?
- これは誰の傘ですか。 (これはだれのかさですか。) — Whose umbrella is this?

誰に (だれに) asks "to whom," and 誰と (だれと) asks "with whom." When asking who the subject is, 誰が is the usual starting pattern, not 誰は.

## How the kanji combine

誰 is a single-character spelling of the native word だれ. The hiragana spelling だれ is also ordinary; there is no compound to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どなた is a more polite alternative. 何 (なに) asks about a thing or content instead of a person's identity.

## Distinctive meaning

Small additions change the question word's use: 誰か (だれか) means "someone," and 誰もいません (だれもいません) means "there is nobody." The negative predicate matters; 誰も is not independently a negative word in every construction.
