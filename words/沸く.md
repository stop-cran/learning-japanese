# 沸く (わく) - boil, grow hot

## Meaning

沸く is an intransitive godan verb: water boils or becomes hot enough for the intended use. The liquid takes が.

- お湯が沸きました。 (おゆがわきました。) - The water has boiled.

It also describes excitement spreading through a crowd or place.

- 会場が歓声に沸きました。 (かいじょうがかんせいにわきました。) - The venue erupted in cheers.

Forms include 沸きます, 沸いた, and 沸かない (わきます、わいた、わかない).

## How the kanji combine

沸 writes わ, and く completes the native verb. Its heat association helps connect the literal and excited-crowd senses, but does not turn excitement into a physical temperature measurement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

沸かす (わかす) is transitive: お湯を沸かす, "boil water." 冷える (ひえる), "cool down," contrasts with becoming hot. 湧く (わく), "well up," is another verb and spelling.

## Distinctive meaning

お風呂が沸く (おふろがわく) means the bath water is heated and ready, not that it must reach boiling point. Translate 沸く according to the object and use, rather than treating every occurrence as a claim about boiling temperature.
