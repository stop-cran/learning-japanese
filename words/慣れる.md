# 慣れる (なれる) — get used to

## Meaning

慣れる means to get used to something or become familiar with it. Use **person が + thing に + 慣れる**:
に marks the situation, activity, or thing that becomes familiar. 慣れている describes being accustomed to it.

- 新しい仕事に慣れました。 (あたらしいしごとになれました。) — I have got used to my new job.
- 朝早く起きることに慣れています。 (あさはやくおきることになれています。) — I am used to getting up early.

In the second example, こと makes "getting up early" into the activity that に can mark.

## How the kanji combine

[慣](../kanji/慣.md) supplies the idea of familiarity through experience. This is one native verb, not a compound:
the ending れる is part of its dictionary form. Here it does not make the verb passive.
Its negative is 慣れない (なれない), and its past is 慣れた (なれた).

## Synonyms and antonyms

慣らす (ならす) means to accustom someone or something; it presents the action of producing familiarity.
[習う](習う.md) (ならう) means learning from a teacher or taking lessons.
You can take lessons without yet feeling accustomed to the activity, so 習う and 慣れる are not substitutes.

## Distinctive meaning

慣れる concerns familiarity, not necessarily liking something or becoming highly skilled.
まだ慣れていません (まだなれていません), "I am not used to it yet," is therefore different from saying that you cannot do it.
For this becoming-accustomed sense, JMdict specifically favors the spelling 慣れる.
