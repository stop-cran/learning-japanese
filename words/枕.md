# 枕 (まくら) - pillow; introductory lead-in

## Meaning

枕 is a pillow or bolster used to support the head. It also means an introductory lead-in to a speech or story, particularly a rakugo performance.

- 枕の高さが合いません。 (まくらのたかさがあいません。) - The pillow is not the right height for me.
- 落語家は枕で客を笑わせた。 (らくごかはまくらできゃくをわらわせた。) - The rakugo performer made the audience laugh during the opening lead-in.

膝枕 (ひざまくら) is resting one's head on someone's lap. Here 枕 appears after another word and still contributes support for the head.

## How the kanji combine

枕 writes the native word まくら as a whole. Do not split the reading into an invented multi-kanji compound or infer it from a separate character reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[はじめに](はじめに.md), "introduction; to begin with," can label opening material.

[紹介](紹介.md) (しょうかい) often introduces a person or presents something to others. 枕 in storytelling means the opening lead-in, not an introduction of two people to one another.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes the bed item from the rhetorical use. A story's 枕 comes before the main story; the same word is not a general replacement for every preface, explanation, or recommendation.
