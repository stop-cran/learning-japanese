# 蚊 (か) - mosquito

## Meaning

蚊 means a mosquito. It is a noun, pronounced with a short か. 蚊に刺される (かにさされる) means "be bitten by a mosquito."

- 蚊に刺されて、腕がかゆいです。 (かにさされて、うでがかゆいです。) - My arm is itchy from a mosquito bite.
- 窓から蚊が一匹入ってきました。 (まどからかがいっぴきはいってきました。) - A mosquito came in through the window.

The counter for this small creature is 匹: 一匹 (いっぴき), "one."

## How the kanji combine

The single character 蚊 writes the native noun か. There is no compound or okurigana to separate. The kana spelling カ also names the same insect.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[虫](虫.md) (むし), "insect; bug," is the broader everyday category, not a precise synonym. 蚊帳 (かや) is a mosquito net; 蚊取り線香 (かとりせんこう) is a mosquito coil.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun itself names the animal, not its bite or an illness. In 蚊に刺された (かにさされた), "I was bitten by a mosquito," に identifies the source of the bite in the passive construction.
