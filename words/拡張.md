# 拡張 (かくちょう) - expansion; extension

## Meaning

拡張 is extending or enlarging a range, scale, or capacity. 拡張する is transitive: the thing extended takes を. It can concern physical space or the functions available in a system.

- 市は道路を拡張しました。 (しはどうろをかくちょうしました。) - The city widened the road.
- このソフトの機能を拡張できます。 (このソフトのきのうをかくちょうできます。) - You can extend this software's functionality.

## How the kanji combine

拡 contributes widening, and 張 stretching or extending. Their related meanings reinforce expansion. The on readings are カク + チョウ, with a long final vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[拡大](拡大.md) (かくだい) covers enlargement, including magnifying an image. [拡充](拡充.md) (かくじゅう) often emphasizes fuller provision or stronger facilities and services. [延長](延長.md) (えんちょう) includes making a period or distance longer. The words overlap, but their examples do not establish unrestricted substitution.

## Distinctive meaning

Extending software functions is not necessarily making its picture larger on the screen. Likewise, 道路を拡張する can concern a wider road, whereas 道路を延長する concerns extending its length. The object and context identify the kind of expansion.
