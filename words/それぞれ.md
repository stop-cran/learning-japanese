# それぞれ (それぞれ) - each; respectively

## Meaning

それぞれ means "each" or "respectively," considering members
individually within a group. It works as a noun or adverb.
Before another noun, use それぞれの.

- 学生はそれぞれの意見を発表しました。 (がくせいはそれぞれのいけんをはっぴょうしました。) - The students each presented their own opinions.
- 兄と妹はそれぞれ東京と大阪に住んでいます。 (あにといもうとはそれぞれとうきょうとおおさかにすんでいます。) - My older brother and younger sister live in Tokyo and Osaka respectively.

In the second example, the order establishes the pairing: brother
with Tokyo, sister with Osaka. It does not say that both people live
in both cities.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. The form repeats それ with voicing in
the second element, giving それぞれ. The dictionary lists rare
kanji spellings, but no kanji decomposition is needed for this card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一人一人](一人一人.md) (ひとりひとり) emphasizes each individual
person; それぞれ can also refer to things or groups.
[みんな](みんな.md) concerns everyone or everything collectively,
without necessarily emphasizing the separate members.

## Distinctive meaning

それぞれ does not mean that every member must be different.
The members may each make the same choice. Its contribution is the
individual distribution of a property or action, or the ordered pairing
of corresponding items.
