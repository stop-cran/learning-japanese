# 急激 (きゅうげき) - sudden; sharp; drastic

## Meaning

急激 describes an abrupt, rapid, or drastic change. It is a na-adjective:
急激な before a noun and 急激に before a verb.

- 気温が急激に下がりました。 (きおんがきゅうげきにさがりました。) - The temperature dropped sharply.
- 急激な変化についていけません。 (きゅうげきなへんかについていけません。) - I cannot keep up with the rapid changes.

The word can concern increases or decreases; it does not specify a direction.
急激な増加 (きゅうげきなぞうか) is a sharp increase.

## How the kanji combine

[急](../kanji/急.md) contributes speed or abruptness; 激 contributes intensity.
Together they suggest change that is rapid or marked. The on readings are
キュウ + ゲキ. The second character is not read げつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[突然](突然.md) (とつぜん) emphasizes something happening suddenly or unexpectedly.
急激 focuses on the abrupt or sharp character of a development.
[急](急.md) (きゅう) also overlaps, but has additional meanings, including urgency.
緩やかな (ゆるやかな), "gradual; gentle," is a useful contrast for a change.

## Distinctive meaning

In the temperature example, the important point is a sharp drop, not simply that
the speaker was surprised. For an unexpected visitor, 突然来た describes the
arrival more directly than treating the person as an 急激な visitor.
English "radical" here concerns the scale of change, not automatically a political
identity.
