# 物凄い (ものすごい) - tremendous; frightful

## Meaning

物凄い describes an extraordinary degree: tremendous, incredible, or extreme.
It can also describe something frightful, eerie, or awful.
Kana ものすごい is usual; the written form here preserves the source spelling.

- ものすごい音がしました。 (ものすごいおとがしました。) - There was a tremendous noise.
- このケーキはものすごくおいしいです。 (このけーきはものすごくおいしいです。) - This cake is incredibly delicious.

It is an i-adjective. Use ものすごい before a noun and
**ものすごく** before another adjective or a verb.
The cake example shows that the intensifier need not express fear or criticism.

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md) contributes the もの element, which intensifies
the native adjective 凄い (すごい) here.
Do not translate the compound mechanically as "a frightening object."
Both written characters remain part of the word even though 凄 has no linked card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[凄い](凄い.md) (すごい) overlaps strongly.
[恐ろしい](恐ろしい.md) (おそろしい) can express fear
and also an extraordinary degree.
[とても](とても.md) and [非常に](非常に.md) (ひじょうに)
are useful comparisons for the intensifying use.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "earth-shattering" is an intensity gloss, not a claim
that an earthquake must occur. The word can be emphatic in conversation;
it does not supply a numerical threshold.
Choose between an admiring, alarming, or simply extreme interpretation from context.
