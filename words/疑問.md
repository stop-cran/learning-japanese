# 疑問 (ぎもん) - doubt; question

## Meaning

疑問 is a doubt or question about something not understood or not accepted as certain.
Useful noun constructions are 疑問がある, "have a question or doubt," and
Nに疑問を持つ, "have doubts about N."
A clause ending in か can identify what is in question.

- この説明には疑問があります。
  (このせつめいにはぎもんがあります。) - I have doubts about this explanation.
- その方法が安全かどうかは疑問です。
  (そのほうほうがあんぜんかどうかはぎもんです。)
  - Whether that method is safe is questionable.

疑問を解く (ぎもんをとく) means to resolve a doubt or answer a question.
The word concerns uncertainty, not an attempted answer.

## How the kanji combine

[疑](../kanji/疑.md) contributes doubt, and [問](../kanji/問.md) contributes questioning.
Together they identify a matter that calls for clarification. The on readings are ギ + モン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[質問](質問.md) (しつもん) is a question asked to obtain information.
A 疑問 can remain in your mind without being spoken.
[問題](問題.md) (もんだい) can be a problem, issue, or exercise, not necessarily a personal doubt.

## Distinctive meaning

疑問です can signal skepticism rather than a neutral request for information.
It does not mean "a guess": a guess proposes an answer, whereas 疑問 marks something
still in question. Context determines how strongly the speaker doubts the claim.
