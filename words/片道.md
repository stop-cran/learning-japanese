# 片道 (かたみち) - one-way trip; one-way ticket

## Meaning

片道 means one way of a journey, as opposed to going and returning. It can also abbreviate "one-way ticket" in a ticket-buying context.

- 駅まで片道三十分かかります。 (えきまでかたみちさんじゅっぷんかかります。) - It takes thirty minutes each way to the station.
- 東京まで片道でお願いします。 (とうきょうまでかたみちでおねがいします。) - A one-way ticket to Tokyo, please.

片道の料金 (かたみちのりょうきん) is the one-way fare; 片道切符 (かたみちきっぷ) explicitly names a one-way ticket.

## How the kanji combine

[片](../kanji/片.md), かた, supplies one side or one of a pair; [道](../kanji/道.md), みち, supplies the way or journey. The first modifies the second: the journey in one direction. These are native readings, not ヘン + ドウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[往復](往復.md) (おうふく) means a round trip or return ticket. 一方通行 (いっぽうつうこう) names one-way traffic, a different issue from whether a traveller buys a return ticket.

## Distinctive meaning

片道三十分 gives the time for one leg, not the combined outward-and-return time. A 片道 ticket does not mean the railway itself permits travel in only one direction.
