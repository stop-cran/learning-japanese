# お金持ち (おかねもち) - wealthy person

## Meaning

お金持ち means a rich or wealthy person. It applies to any gender, not just a "rich man."

- あの人はお金持ちです。 (あのひとはおかねもちです。) - That person is wealthy.
- お金持ちになりたいです。 (おかねもちになりたいです。) - I want to become rich.

It is a noun: お金持ちの友達 (おかねもちのともだち) means "a wealthy friend." Do not add an i-adjective ending.

## How the kanji combine

お is a courteous prefix. [金](../kanji/金.md), read かね, means money, and [持](../kanji/持.md) occurs in 持つ, "have" or "hold." 持ち forms the idea of someone possessing something.

## Synonyms and antonyms

金持ち (かねもち) omits the prefix. 裕福 (ゆうふく), "well-off," is a more formal na-adjective. 貧しい (まずしい), "poor," expresses the contrasting condition.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare [お金](お金.md), the money itself, with お金持ち, the person who has substantial wealth. Someone merely carrying money is not necessarily an お金持ち: the word describes financial circumstances, not what is currently in their wallet.
