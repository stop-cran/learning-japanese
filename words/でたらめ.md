# でたらめ - nonsense; haphazard

## Meaning

でたらめ describes statements without a sound basis or actions done arbitrarily, without a coherent plan. It can be a noun or take な before a noun and に before a verb.

- その話はでたらめです。 (そのはなしはでたらめです。) - That story is nonsense.
- 数字をでたらめに並べないでください。 (すうじをでたらめにならべないでください。) - Please do not arrange the numbers haphazardly.

## How the kanji combine

出鱈目 is an ateji spelling: characters used to write the word, not a literal definition assembled from their meanings. [出](../kanji/出.md) and [目](../kanji/目.md) are therefore spelling cues here, not independently translated parts of the meaning. The primary kana spelling does not imply that the word is a foreign loan.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[嘘](嘘.md) is a lie or falsehood; でたらめ can describe baseless nonsense without identifying a deliberate attempt to deceive. [適当](適当.md) has a careless or slapdash use that overlaps, but its suitable/appropriate meaning does not mean でたらめ.

## Distinctive meaning

でたらめな答え is an arbitrary or nonsensical answer. This is generally a criticism of reliability or method, not a neutral technical term for statistically random sampling. English "rubbish" in this sense does not mean literal household garbage.
