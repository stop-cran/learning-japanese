# 借金 (しゃっきん) - debt; borrowing money

## Meaning

借金 is a noun for debt or borrowed money. 借金する means "borrow money; get into debt." The focus is on the borrower and the obligation to repay, not on lending money to someone else.

- 車を買うために借金しました。 (くるまをかうためにしゃっきんしました。) - I borrowed money to buy a car.
- 借金を少しずつ返しています。 (しゃっきんをすこしずつかえしています。) - I am paying back my debt little by little.

借金がある (しゃっきんがある) means "have debts." In the second example, を marks what is repaid.

## How the kanji combine

[借](../kanji/借.md) means "borrow," and [金](../kanji/金.md) means "money": a verb-object relationship, borrowing money. The reading is しゃっきん, with a small っ before きん; do not pronounce a full く there.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お金を借りる (おかねをかりる) describes borrowing money directly. [貸す](貸す.md) (かす), "lend," describes the other side of the transaction. [返す](返す.md) (かえす), "return; repay," is the action that reduces or settles the debt.

## Distinctive meaning

借金 can name both the act of borrowing and the resulting money owed. It does not itself tell you the interest rate, lender, or reason for borrowing. English "loan" can describe several perspectives, so use the Japanese particles and predicate to establish who owes what.
