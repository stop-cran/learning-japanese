# 暮れ (くれ) - dusk; year-end

## Meaning

暮れ names the close of a day or period. 日の暮れ (ひのくれ) means nightfall;
年の暮れ (としのくれ) means the end of the year. Without an explicit period,
暮れ can refer to year-end when the context makes that clear.

- 日の暮れまでに家に帰りました。 (ひのくれまでにいえにかえりました。) - I got home by nightfall.
- 年の暮れは店が忙しくなります。 (としのくれはみせがいそがしくなります。) - The shop gets busy at the end of the year.

暮れ is a noun. The first example uses までに to set a deadline, not to say
that the journey continued throughout the evening.

## How the kanji combine

[暮](../kanji/暮.md) has the association of a day or period drawing to a close.
暮れ is related to the native verb 暮れる (くれる), "grow dark; draw to a close."
The final れ belongs to the written word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[夕方](夕方.md) (ゆうがた) names the late-afternoon/evening part of the day.
暮れ emphasizes its close. 年末 (ねんまつ) overlaps with the year-end meaning.
夜明け (よあけ), "dawn," contrasts with nightfall, not with every sense of 暮れ.

## Distinctive meaning

An English "end" is broader than 暮れ. Use the surrounding time expression
to identify what is ending rather than substituting 暮れ for the end of any
object or activity. 年の暮れ and 日の暮れ make the intended time scale explicit.
