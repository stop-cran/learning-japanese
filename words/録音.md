# 録音 (ろくおん) - audio recording

## Meaning

録音 is recording sound, or the sound recording produced.
録音する is transitive: 音を録音する (おとをろくおんする)
means to record sound.

- 会議の内容を録音しました。 (かいぎのないようをろくおんしました。) - I made an audio recording of the meeting.
- 昨日の録音を聞き直しました。 (きのうのろくおんをききなおしました。) - I listened to yesterday's recording again.

The first example concerns making the recording; the second
concerns the result.

## How the kanji combine

[録](../kanji/録.md), record, takes [音](../kanji/音.md),
sound, as its object: recording sound.
The on readings are ロク + オン.
Keep all four morae, ろ・く・お・ん; do not omit く.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[記録](記録.md) (きろく) is recording or a record more generally.
録画 (ろくが) concerns video recording;
収録 (しゅうろく) can include recording audio or video.
[テープレコーダー](テープレコーダー.md) names one recording
device, not the action.

## Distinctive meaning

録音 is not restricted to magnetic tape. A digital sound file can
also be a 録音. Conversely, writing minutes of a meeting records
information but is not by itself recording the sound.
録る (とる) can mean recording audio or video; that spelling
does not carry every photographic sense of 撮る (とる).
