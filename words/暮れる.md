# 暮れる (くれる) - grow dark; draw to a close

## Meaning

暮れる means to grow dark as evening arrives, or for a day or year to draw to a close. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: 暮れます, 暮れて, 暮れた and 暮れない.

- 日が暮れる前に帰りましょう。 (ひがくれるまえにかえりましょう。) - Let's go home before it gets dark.
- もうすぐ今年も暮れます。 (もうすぐことしもくれます。) - This year will soon draw to a close too.

The day or period is the subject, not an object marked with を.

## How the kanji combine

[暮](../kanji/暮.md) contributes evening or an ending; れる is okurigana. The conjugational stem is 暮れ, not just the written kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

明ける (あける) contrasts with nightfall in 夜が明ける, "day breaks." 終わる (おわる) is the broader verb "finish; end."

## Distinctive meaning

暮れる is not a general replacement for "finish" with any activity. Also distinguish it from くれる, "give to me/us": identical sound does not mean identical vocabulary.
