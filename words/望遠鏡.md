# 望遠鏡 (ぼうえんきょう) - telescope

## Meaning

望遠鏡 is a telescope, an optical instrument for viewing distant objects.
It is not limited to observing stars.

- 望遠鏡で月を見ました。 (ぼうえんきょうでつきをみました。) - I looked at the moon through a telescope.
- この望遠鏡なら遠くの船も見えます。 (このぼうえんきょうならとおくのふねもみえます。) - With this telescope, even distant ships are visible.

## How the kanji combine

[望](../kanji/望.md) has a looking-into-the-distance association here;
[遠](../kanji/遠.md) contributes far, and 鏡 an optical/mirror association.
ボウ + エン + キョウ gives ぼうえんきょう. This does not mean that a telescope
is literally a "hope mirror."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[顕微鏡](顕微鏡.md) (けんびきょう) is a microscope, for viewing tiny details.
[眼鏡](眼鏡.md) (めがね) are eyeglasses, while [鏡](鏡.md) (かがみ) is a mirror.
The shared character does not make these instruments interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

望遠鏡で見る uses で for the instrument. Both lenses and mirrors can be used
in telescope designs; learning the word does not require treating every telescope
as an ordinary mirror.
