# 絵画 (かいが) - painting; pictorial art

## Meaning

絵画 is a noun for a painting or picture, or painting as an artistic field. It fits discussion of artworks, exhibitions, and artistic study.

- 美術館で日本の絵画を見ました。 (びじゅつかんでにほんのかいがをみました。) - I saw Japanese paintings at the art museum.
- 姉は大学で絵画を学んでいます。 (あねはだいがくでかいがをまなんでいます。) - My older sister is studying painting at university.

The first example refers to works; the second refers to the field. Japanese does not require a plural ending for several paintings.

## How the kanji combine

[絵](../kanji/絵.md) and [画](../kanji/画.md) both contribute the idea of a picture or depiction. Their similar meanings reinforce each other. In this compound 絵 is read カイ, not the standalone え; 画 is ガ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[絵](絵.md) (え) is a broad everyday word for a picture or drawing. [写真](写真.md) (しゃしん) identifies a photograph. [芸術](芸術.md) (げいじゅつ) covers the arts more broadly, including art forms beyond pictures. These are distinctions of medium or scope, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "picture" can be too broad: an English picture may be a photograph, while 絵画 points to pictorial artwork. For an ordinary request to draw a picture, 絵を描く (えをかく) is a useful expression rather than treating 絵画 as a verb.
