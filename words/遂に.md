# 遂に (ついに) - finally; in the end

## Meaning

遂に is an adverb for an eventual outcome: "finally," "at last," or "in the end." It looks back over the process, wait, or expectation leading to that result. Kana ついに is usual.

- 三年かかって、遂に本が完成しました。 (さんねんかかって、ついにほんがかんせいしました。) - After three years, the book was finally completed.
- 最後まで待ちましたが、彼は遂に来ませんでした。 (さいごまでまちましたが、かれはついにきませんでした。) - We waited until the end, but he never came.

With a negative verb, it can say that an expected event did not happen even by the end. The negative belongs to the verb, not to 遂に alone.

## How the kanji combine

遂 suggests bringing something through to an outcome. The written form represents ついに; the kana に completes the adverb. There is no multi-kanji compound to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とうとう](とうとう.md) also marks an eventual result. [やっと](やっと.md) often highlights difficulty, waiting, or relief. [最後](最後.md) (さいご) names the last part or end; it is not itself the same adverb.

## Distinctive meaning

遂に does not guarantee a happy ending: something long feared can finally happen too. Nor is it simply a list marker for the last instruction. In a sequence such as "first mix, then bake, finally serve," a phrase such as 最後に can identify the last step without implying a long-awaited outcome.
