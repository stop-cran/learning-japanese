# おいしい (おいしい) — delicious

## Meaning

おいしい means **delicious or good-tasting**. It is an い-adjective and can describe food or drink directly.

- このスープはおいしいです。 (このすーぷはおいしいです。) — This soup is delicious.
- おいしいパンを買いました。 (おいしいぱんをかいました。) — I bought some tasty bread.

The past is おいしかった, and the negative is おいしくない. Do not add な before a noun or だ after the plain present form.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The spelling [美](../kanji/美.md)[味](../kanji/味.md)しい associates “beauty/goodness” and “taste” with the word. Read 美味しい as a whole; assigning an ordinary separate お reading to 美 and い reading to 味 would be misleading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

うまい also means tasty and can sound more casual or blunt. まずい means bad-tasting. おいしくない is the direct grammatical negative and may sound less harsh than まずい.

## Distinctive meaning

おいしい evaluates taste, not food safety, price, or nutritional value. The apparent お is part of the modern word: learners should not remove it to form a supposedly plain adjective.
