# 発電 (はつでん) - electricity generation

## Meaning

発電 is generating electricity. 発電する describes the generating process;
発電所 (はつでんしょ) is a power station.

- 風の力で発電します。 (かぜのちからではつでんします。) - We generate electricity using wind power.
- 太陽光発電が増えています。 (たいようこうはつでんがふえています。) - Solar power generation is increasing.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) concerns producing or sending forth, and [電](../kanji/電.md) concerns electricity.
In the selected sense, the compound names producing electricity.
The reading is はつでん, without changing つ to a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[電気](電気.md) (でんき) is electricity, or an electric light in the relevant context, not specifically its generation.
[電力](電力.md) (でんりょく) is electric power; it is not the generating action itself.
[生産](生産.md) (せいさん) is production, particularly of agricultural or manufactured output.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer from "generation" that 発電 means producing any kind of object.
The dictionary records both transitive and intransitive する, so the word is not limited to one transitivity label.
A separate recorded sense is sending a telegram; the examples here select electricity generation,
not [電報](電報.md) (でんぽう) or ordinary email.
