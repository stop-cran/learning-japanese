# 実例 (じつれい) - actual example; real case

## Meaning

実例 is an actual example, instance, or real case used to illustrate something. It is a noun. **実例を挙げる** (じつれいをあげる) means "give an actual example."

- 実例を挙げて説明してください。
  (じつれいをあげてせつめいしてください。) - Please explain by giving an actual example.
- この本には、成功と失敗の実例が載っています。
  (このほんには、せいこうとしっぱいのじつれいがのっています。) - This book contains real cases of success and failure.

An 実例 may be good or bad. A real failure can illustrate a problem just as a success illustrates a useful approach.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality and [例](../kanji/例.md) an example or instance. The first qualifies the second: an example that actually occurs. The on readings are ジツ + レイ, じつれい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[例](例.md) (れい) is the broader word for an example, case, or precedent. An invented example can still be a 例; 実例 emphasizes an actual instance. [例え](例え.md) (たとえ) can be an example or an analogy, which need not report a real event.

[サンプル](サンプル.md), "sample; specimen," can overlap when a real sample illustrates a point, but it does not make every collected specimen a historical case.

## Distinctive meaning

The English "illustration" here is an illustrative instance, not automatically a drawing. Likewise, a real precedent can be an 実例, but the word alone does not say that the case is legally binding or should be copied. Keep the actuality of the example separate from its quality, authority, and relevance.
