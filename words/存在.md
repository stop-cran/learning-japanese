# 存在 (そんざい) — existence; presence

## Meaning

存在 names existence or presence. 存在する means "exist"; the thing that exists can be marked with が.
It is not a verb for making something exist.

- 同じ名前の町が二つ存在します。 (おなじなまえのまちがふたつそんざいします。) — Two towns with the same name exist.
- その存在を知りませんでした。 (そのそんざいをしりませんでした。) — I did not know of its existence.

The second sentence uses 存在 as a noun: it is the object of "know," not a shortened form of a transitive 存在する.

## How the kanji combine

[存](../kanji/存.md) contributes existing, and [在](../kanji/在.md) contributes being present.
Their related meanings reinforce existence or presence. The on readings are ソン + ザイ.
Neither component makes the compound mean possession in the sense of owning an object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ある](ある.md) and [いる](いる.md) also express being or existing; they are normally written in kana.
For locating an ordinary object, 机の上に本があります (つくえのうえにほんがあります), "There is a book on the desk," is a direct everyday pattern.
いる serves the corresponding role for people and other animate beings.
不在 (ふざい), "absence," is not a general opposite of existence: an absent person still exists.

## Distinctive meaning

存在する makes existence or presence the point of the statement.
It can apply to a person, object, or abstract matter; do not impose the ordinary ある/いる distinction on it.
For simple locations, learn the ordinary ある and いる patterns rather than replacing them automatically with 存在する.
