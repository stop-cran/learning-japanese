# 生じる (しょうじる) - arise; produce

## Meaning

生じる is an ichidan verb used both intransitively, "arise; result," and transitively, "produce; cause."
Compare 問題が生じる (もんだいがしょうじる), "a problem arises," with 誤解を生じる (ごかいをしょうじる),
"cause a misunderstanding."

- 計画に問題が生じました。 (けいかくにもんだいがしょうじました。) - A problem arose with the plan.
- この表現は誤解を生じるおそれがあります。 (このひょうげんはごかいをしょうじるおそれがあります。) - This expression risks causing misunderstanding.

In the first, the problem is the subject. In the second, the expression causes the misunderstanding.
Useful forms are 生じて (しょうじて), 生じた (しょうじた), and 生じない (しょうじない).

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) contributes coming into existence or producing.
The word uses the on-reading ショウ with the verbal ending じる.
Its る drops in 生じない; do not conjugate it as a godan verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[起こる](起こる.md) (おこる), "happen; occur," overlaps with the intransitive sense.
[生まれる](生まれる.md) (うまれる), "be born; come into being," offers another existence-related comparison.
[消える](消える.md) (きえる), "disappear," is a contextual reversal, not an exact opposite for every use.

## Distinctive meaning

The same spelling can describe either the emergence of a result or its production by a cause.
Read が and を carefully rather than memorizing a single transitivity label.
This wording suits explanatory descriptions; it does not require that the result be deliberate or desirable.
