# 恐れる (おそれる) — fear; be afraid of

## Meaning

恐れる means to fear or be afraid of something. It is transitive: use を for the feared thing or outcome.

- 私は失敗を恐れています。 (わたしはしっぱいをおそれています。) — I am afraid of failure.
- 失敗を恐れないでください。 (しっぱいをおそれないでください。) — Please do not fear failure.

An outcome can be feared even when it has not happened.

## How the kanji combine

[恐](../kanji/恐.md) supplies the fear meaning and is read おそ here; れる is the written ending.
This is a native verb, not a compound, and the ending is not a separate word meaning "can."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[怖い](怖い.md) (こわい) can express a similar feeling through adjective grammar:
失敗が怖い (しっぱいがこわい), "Failure frightens me."
[心配](心配.md) (しんぱい) names worry or concern. The meanings overlap, but being concerned about
someone's welfare need not present that person as an object of fear.

## Distinctive meaning

恐れる is an ichidan verb: 恐れます (おそれます) and 恐れない (おそれない) are formed
by replacing final る. Keep its object pattern distinct from 怖い:
失敗を恐れる versus 失敗が怖い. The difference here is the construction, not a claim that one
expression is automatically stronger than the other.
