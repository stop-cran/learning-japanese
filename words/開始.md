# 開始 (かいし) - start; commencement

## Meaning

開始 is the start or beginning of an activity. It is a noun and a する-verb, usable both for starting something and for something starting. Learn the particles in the whole sentence.

- 午前九時に受付を開始します。 (ごぜんくじにうけつけをかいしします。) - We will start registration at nine in the morning.
- 試合が開始しました。 (しあいがかいししました。) - The match started.

The first sentence presents what the organizers start, with を. The second presents the event as subject, with が. As a noun, 開始時間 (かいしじかん) means "starting time."

## How the kanji combine

[開](../kanji/開.md) contributes opening, while [始](../kanji/始.md) contributes beginning. The two related ideas combine as the commencement of an activity. Their on readings are カイ + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[始める](始める.md) (はじめる) and [始まる](始まる.md) (はじまる) provide a useful everyday transitive/intransitive pair. 開始 is useful in announcements, schedules, and descriptions of organized activity. 終了 (しゅうりょう), "ending; completion," is the corresponding opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

開始 names an event's beginning, not every sense of English "start." For setting off on a journey, [出発](出発.md) (しゅっぱつ) focuses on departure. A noun phrase such as 開始前 (かいしまえ), "before the start," contains no claim that someone has already begun.
