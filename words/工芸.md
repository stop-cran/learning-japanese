# 工芸 (こうげい) - craft; industrial arts

## Meaning

工芸 concerns craft or industrial arts, especially making useful objects
with artistic value, and the resulting works. Pottery, lacquerware, and
textiles are useful examples. It is a noun.

- この町は伝統的な工芸で知られています。 (このまちはでんとうてきなこうげいでしられています。) - This town is known for its traditional crafts.
- 博物館で日本の工芸を学びました。 (はくぶつかんでにほんのこうげいをまなびました。) - I learned about Japanese crafts at the museum.

The dictionary range also includes techniques of making things.
Do not treat artistic usefulness as a claim that every use of 工芸
names one particular handmade object.

## How the kanji combine

[工](../kanji/工.md) contributes making or craft, and
[芸](../kanji/芸.md) contributes art, craft, or skill.
The related associations point to skilled making.
The on readings are コウ + ゲイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[芸術](芸術.md) (げいじゅつ) is the broader arts word.
[技術](技術.md) (ぎじゅつ) concerns technique or technology.
[工業](工業.md) (こうぎょう) concerns manufacturing industry;
[製造](製造.md) (せいぞう) concerns manufacturing.
Sharing the activity of making does not make all four unrestricted substitutes.

## Distinctive meaning

English "industrial arts" here is not simply every industrial activity.
工芸 can join practical use and aesthetic value, so it is not restricted
to purely decorative fine art. Conversely, the noun alone does not
guarantee that an item was made entirely by hand without machinery.
