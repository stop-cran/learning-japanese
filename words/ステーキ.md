# ステーキ (ステーキ) - steak

## Meaning

ステーキ is steak, typically a substantial piece of meat cooked as an individual portion. On a menu, the unqualified word often suggests beef.

- レストランでステーキを注文しました。 (レストランでステーキをちゅうもんしました。) - I ordered steak at the restaurant.
- このステーキは柔らかいです。 (このステーキはやわらかいです。) - This steak is tender.

It is a noun; use ステーキを焼く (ステーキをやく) for cooking or grilling a steak.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword comes from English "steak" and is written in katakana. The long-vowel mark belongs after テ: ステーキ. There is no kanji decomposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

牛肉 (ぎゅうにく) is beef as meat, not a particular dish. ハンバーグ is a cooked ground-meat patty dish, different from an ordinary cut steak.

## Distinctive meaning

The name may include another ingredient, such as ポークステーキ for pork steak. It is therefore useful to check the full menu name rather than treating every occurrence as an absolute guarantee of beef.
