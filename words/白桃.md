# 白桃 (はくとう) - white peach

## Meaning

白桃 means white peach. It can also name the particular peach cultivar Hakutō, described in Japanese dictionaries as having white flesh. A specific cultivar name and a broader food label need not identify exactly the same range of products.

- 白桃を二つ買いました。 (はくとうをふたつかいました。) - I bought two white peaches.
- これは白桃の缶詰です。 (これははくとうのかんづめです。) - These are canned white peaches.

The fruit's skin need not be uniformly white: the cultivar description also notes reddish colouring.

## How the kanji combine

[白](../kanji/白.md) contributes white and [桃](../kanji/桃.md) peach. The first modifies the second: a white peach. The on readings combine as ハク + トウ, giving はくとう rather than the standalone native reading もも.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[桃](桃.md) (もも) names peach generally. [果物](果物.md) (くだもの) is the broader category "fruit," not another name for this particular kind. A narrower peach name is not an interchangeable synonym for every 桃.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the whole reading はくとう for the fruit word here. Dictionaries also record 白桃 read しろもも for an ornamental white-flowered peach, distinguished from the fruit cultivar, and for a white-fruited yamamomo cultivar. Those separate uses do not invalidate はくとう or make しろもも the reading to substitute into the food examples.
