# 思想 (しそう) - thought; ideology

## Meaning

思想 is a noun for thought, ideas, or ideology. It is useful when discussing a person's or group's ideas as a body of thought, rather than merely reporting a passing thought.

- 大学で近代の思想を学んでいます。 (だいがくできんだいのしそうをまなんでいます。) - I am studying modern thought at university.
- 二人は政治思想が違います。 (ふたりはせいじしそうがちがいます。) - The two have different political ideologies.

Use 思想を学ぶ (しそうをまなぶ), "study a body of thought," and 思想の違い (しそうのちがい), "differences in thought or ideology."

## How the kanji combine

[思](../kanji/思.md) contributes thinking, and [想](../kanji/想.md) contributes thought or conception. The related meanings reinforce one another in a noun for ideas or a system of thought. The readings are し + そう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[考え](考え.md) (かんがえ) can refer to a thought, view, or plan in a particular situation. [意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion one may express about an issue. These overlap with 思想, but do not necessarily imply an underlying outlook or ideology.

## Distinctive meaning

思想 does not itself mean that the ideas are correct, extreme, or political. The surrounding topic supplies those details. It suits discussion of philosophy or social ideas, but for "What do you think we should eat?" a phrase built with 考える is a more useful starting model than treating 思想 as every English "thought."
