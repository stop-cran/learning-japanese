# 感じる (かんじる) - feel; sense

## Meaning

感じる is an ichidan verb for feeling, sensing, or experiencing something.
It can take an object with を, or a perceived judgment with と.
The experience may be a physical sensation or a personal impression.

- 寒さを感じます。 (さむさをかんじます。) - I feel the cold.
- この仕事は難しいと感じました。 (このしごとはむずかしいとかんじました。) - I felt that this work was difficult.

寒さ is the noun "coldness," so it takes を. 難しい is an adjective
inside a quoted judgment, followed by と. Forms include 感じます,
感じた, and 感じない.

## How the kanji combine

[感](../kanji/感.md) contributes feeling or perception through its on
reading カン. The verb ending じる supplies Japanese inflection.
This card groups the on-based formation under kango; じる is not a
second kanji or a kun reading of 感.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[思う](思う.md) (おもう) expresses thinking or an opinion.
感じる emphasizes an experienced impression. [感動](感動.md)
(かんどう) names being emotionally moved and can take する.
These overlap in some contexts rather than dividing thought and emotion
into fixed compartments.

## Distinctive meaning

The construction tells you what is being felt: a noun with を or a
judgment with と. A feeling or impression
is the experiencer's response, not proof that the judgment is objectively true.
