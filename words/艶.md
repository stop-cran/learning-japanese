# 艶 (つや) - gloss; luster

## Meaning

艶 is the gloss, sheen or luster of a surface, such as polished wood, hair or skin.
It names a quality, not necessarily a coating applied to produce that quality.

- この布には美しい艶があります。
  (このぬのにはうつくしいつやがあります。) - This cloth has a beautiful sheen.
- 靴を磨いて艶を出しました。
  (くつをみがいてつやをだしました。) - I polished my shoes to bring out their shine.

The word also extends to a rich or mellow quality in a voice, youthful freshness in skin, or personal appeal.
声に艶がある (こえにつやがある) describes an attractive richness of voice, not a visible coating on it.

## How the kanji combine

艶 is a single-character native noun, つや, with no okurigana.
This reading belongs to the whole word; it is not the character's on reading エン.
There is no separate 艶 card in the current collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[つやつや](つやつや.md) vividly describes a glossy, lustrous appearance.
[てかてか](てかてか.md) can emphasize conspicuous or oily shine; [ぴかぴか](ぴかぴか.md) can suggest sparkling cleanliness or newness.
[魅力](魅力.md) (みりょく), "charm; appeal," overlaps with an extended sense but is not limited to a surface sheen.

艶消し (つやけし), "matte finish; removal of gloss," contrasts with a glossy finish.
It is not a universal opposite for every attractive voice or personality.

## Distinctive meaning

艶がある means "have a sheen" in the surface sense; 艶を出す means "bring out a shine."
The source gloss includes English "glaze," but this does not establish that 艶 names the glazing material or the act of glazing pottery.
Distinguish an object's visual quality from a substance applied to it.
