# 教授 (きょうじゅ) - professor; instruction

## Meaning

教授 is a noun meaning professor. It also names teaching or instruction and, in that sense,
forms the transitive verb **教授する**. Use **personに + subject matterを + 教授する**
for teaching someone a subject.

- 田中教授の講義を受けています。 (たなかきょうじゅのこうぎをうけています。) - I am taking Professor Tanaka's lectures.
- 彼は大学で音楽理論を教授しています。 (かれはだいがくでおんがくりろんをきょうじゅしています。) - He teaches music theory at a university.

The first occurrence is a title attached after a name. The second is the instruction sense,
not "work as a professor" simply formed by adding する to the job title.

## How the kanji combine

[教](../kanji/教.md) contributes teaching, and 授 contributes giving or imparting.
Together they express imparting knowledge; the person-title meaning is learned as another established use.
The on readings are キョウ + ジュ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[先生](先生.md) (せんせい) is a broader designation and form of address for teachers and various professionals.
[教師](教師.md) (きょうし) names a teacher.
[教える](教える.md) (おしえる) is a flexible native verb for teaching or telling; 教授する suits descriptions of instruction.

## Distinctive meaning

A professor is not just anyone addressed as 先生. Conversely, someone who 教授する teaches,
but the verb alone does not establish their academic rank.
For direct address, 田中先生 can be appropriate even when the person is introduced in writing as 田中教授.
Keep the role noun and the action meaning separate.
