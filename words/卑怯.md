# 卑怯 (ひきょう) - cowardly; unfair

## Meaning

卑怯 describes cowardly conduct or an unfair, mean, underhanded way
of behaving. It is a noun and な-adjective: 卑怯なやり方
(ひきょうなやりかた), "an underhanded method."

- 相手をだますのは卑怯です。 (あいてをだますのはひきょうです。) - Deceiving the other person is unfair.
- 責任を他人に押しつけるのは卑怯な態度です。 (せきにんをたにんにおしつけるのはひきょうなたいどです。) - Pushing responsibility onto others is cowardly behavior.

卑怯に describes the manner of an action; 卑怯ではない is the negative.

## How the kanji combine

卑 has a low or base association, and 怯 a fear or timidity association.
Those can help recall the unfavorable evaluation, but are not a verified
history of the word. Read the whole compound ヒ + キョウ, ひきょう.
The public dictionary qualifies its possible older spelling history;
do not present a literal component story as established etymology.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずるい](ずるい.md) overlaps with slyness or unfairness.
[汚い](汚い.md) (きたない) can describe dirty tactics as well as dirt.
[勇気](勇気.md) (ゆうき), courage, supplies a contrast to cowardice;
[公平](公平.md) (こうへい), fairness, contrasts with unfair treatment.
Neither is an antonym covering every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

卑怯 is a criticism of conduct or character, not a neutral diagnosis
that someone feels afraid. Being frightened does not by itself
justify calling someone 卑怯. The unfairness sense also means that
the word is not limited to running away from physical danger.
