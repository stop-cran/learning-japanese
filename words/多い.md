# 多い (おおい) — many, much, numerous

## Meaning

多い is an い-adjective: 人が多い (there are many people), 雨が多い (it rains a lot), 多くの人 おおくのひと (many people), 多く おおく (many, much, a large number).
It usually works as a predicate: 日本は地震が多い (Japan has many earthquakes). Before a noun it is usually replaced by 多くの人 or たくさんの人 rather than 多い人.

## How the kanji combine

[多](../kanji/多.md) with okurigana い: おお + い. Note the same sound as 大きい おおきい ([大](../kanji/大.md)), a different word: 大きい is "big", 多い is "many".

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [少ない](少ない.md) すくない.
- Close: たくさん (a lot, adverb), 数多く かずおおく (in great numbers).

## Distinctive meaning

多い describes the number or amount of things being large, in the "X is plentiful" pattern (AはBが多い). たくさん is the usual adverb for "a lot".
