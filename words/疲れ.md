# 疲れ (つかれ) - tiredness; fatigue

## Meaning

疲れ is a noun meaning tiredness or fatigue. It names the condition itself, so it can be something one feels, that remains, or that goes away.

- 仕事の疲れがまだ残っています。 (しごとのつかれがまだのこっています。) - I am still tired from work.
- よく寝たら、疲れが取れました。 (よくねたら、つかれがとれました。) - After a good sleep, my tiredness went away.

疲れを感じる (つかれをかんじる) means "feel tiredness." In 疲れが取れる, fatigue is the subject of "go away," not a person actively taking an object.

## How the kanji combine

[疲](../kanji/疲.md) contributes fatigue. The noun is read つかれ, with れ in kana. Compare the related verb [疲れる](疲れる.md) (つかれる), "get tired"; the noun is not a separate on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

疲れる describes becoming tired, while 疲れ names the tiredness. Compare 疲れました (つかれました), "I got tired," with 疲れがあります (つかれがあります), "there is fatigue." 元気 (げんき), "well; energetic," offers a contextual contrast, not a literal opposite noun in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

English often translates a Japanese noun construction with an adjective: 疲れが残っている becomes "still tired." That does not make 疲れ an adjective. The noun is useful when specifying the source or persistence of tiredness, as in 旅の疲れ (たびのつかれ), "fatigue from the journey."
