# 豊富 (ほうふ) - abundant; plentiful

## Meaning

豊富 describes something available in abundance, whether material resources,
choices, knowledge, or experience. It is a な-adjective:
豊富な + noun, or noun + が + 豊富です.

- この店は商品の種類が豊富です。 (このみせはしょうひんのしゅるいがほうふです。) - This shop has a wide variety of products.
- 彼女は豊富な経験を持っています。 (かのじょはほうふなけいけんをもっています。) - She has extensive experience.

豊富に can modify a verb: 栄養を豊富に含む (えいようをほうふにふくむ),
"contain abundant nutrients."

## How the kanji combine

豊 contributes abundance, and [富](../kanji/富.md) contributes wealth or
plentiful resources. Their related meanings reinforce each other.
The on readings are ホウ + フ, with a long first vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[豊か](豊か.md) (ゆたか), "rich; abundant," overlaps but also describes broad
qualities of life or expression. [不足](不足.md) (ふそく), "shortage,"
contrasts with having an abundant supply.
[たっぷり](たっぷり.md) emphasizes a generous amount in a different construction.

## Distinctive meaning

豊富 is not a general translation for every English use of "rich."
It highlights quantity or variety, as in abundant experience or many available
choices. The examples do not say the shop owner or experienced person is
financially wealthy. Identify what is abundant rather than transferring the
description indiscriminately to its owner.
