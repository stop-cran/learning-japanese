# 詫びる (わびる) - apologize; make an apology

## Meaning

詫びる means to apologize, acknowledging one's fault and asking forgiveness.
It is a transitive ichidan verb. The conduct apologized for can take を,
and the person receiving the apology takes に.

- 遅刻したことを先生に詫びました。 (ちこくしたことをせんせいにわびました。) - I apologized to the teacher for being late.
- 彼女に手紙で詫びました。 (かのじょにてがみでわびました。) - I apologized to her in a letter.

The second sentence leaves the reason understood; で identifies the means.
Forms include 詫びます, 詫びて and 詫びない.

## How the kanji combine

詫 represents the apology association in this native verb; びる is written
in kana. No 詫 kanji card is currently available.
Learn わびる as a word rather than predicting it from an on reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[謝る](謝る.md) (あやまる) also names apologizing and is the everyday
conversational choice. Compared with it, 詫びる is more formal and is
also used in writing or for serious faults. This is a register tendency,
not a rule that the verbs can never describe the same apology.
[ごめんなさい](ごめんなさい.md) and [すみません](すみません.md)
can be the words of an apology, whereas 詫びる describes performing that act.
[悔やむ](悔やむ.md) (くやむ) can describe regret without an apology to anyone.

## Distinctive meaning

An apology does not guarantee that the other person forgives the speaker.
The verb does not specify the exact words used. A formal apology formula is
お詫び申し上げます (おわびもうしあげます), "I offer my apologies";
do not replace お詫び with お謝り in that expression.
Digital Daijisen connects its historical origin with 侘びる, but that is
not permission to substitute the latter spelling freely in modern apology sentences.
