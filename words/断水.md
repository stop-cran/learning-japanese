# 断水 (だんすい) - water supply outage

## Meaning

断水 is an interruption of the water supply.
断水する can describe the supply stopping or someone shutting it off.

- 工事のため、明日の午後は断水します。
  (こうじのため、あしたのごごはだんすいします。) - Because of construction work, the water supply will be off tomorrow afternoon.
- 断水に備えて、水を用意しました。
  (だんすいにそなえて、みずをよういしました。) - I prepared water in case of a water outage.

断水中 (だんすいちゅう) means that the outage is in progress.
The noun does not by itself identify who caused it.

## How the kanji combine

[断](../kanji/断.md) contributes cutting off or interruption and
[水](../kanji/水.md) water.
The compound concerns cutting off the water supply, not dividing water
into pieces. The on readings are ダン + スイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

停電 (ていでん) is a power outage, not a water outage.
給水 (きゅうすい) means supplying water;
給水が再開する (きゅうすいがさいかいする), "the water supply resumes,"
describes recovery from an interruption.

## Distinctive meaning

A planned shutoff for repairs can be 断水 as well as an unexpected outage.
The word does not mean that a river has dried up or that the water is salty.
[淡水](淡水.md) (たんすい) means fresh water; keep だん and たん distinct.
