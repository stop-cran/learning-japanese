# 測定 (そくてい) - measurement; measuring

## Meaning

測定 is measurement: determining a quantity such as length, temperature
or weight, often with an instrument.
It is a noun and a transitive する verb: **quantity を測定する**.
測定値 (そくていち) is a measured value.

- 装置で水の温度を測定します。 (そうちでみずのおんどをそくていします。) - We measure the water temperature with an instrument.
- 測定の結果を記録しました。 (そくていのけっかをきろくしました。) - I recorded the measurement results.

で in the first sentence marks the means of measuring, while を marks
what is measured.

## How the kanji combine

[測](../kanji/測.md) contributes measuring or gauging, and
[定](../kanji/定.md) contributes determining or establishing.
Together they suggest determining a value by measurement.
The on readings combine as そく + てい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計る](計る.md) (はかる) belongs to a broader measuring family that also
recognizes 測る. [測量](測量.md) (そくりょう) overlaps in measurement
but is especially useful for land surveying.
[観測](観測.md) (かんそく) is observation, including collecting measurements;
not every act of observation is the same as measuring a quantity.

## Distinctive meaning

測定 describes the measuring operation, not a guarantee that the result is exact.
An instrument or method can have error.
The word is not a general replacement for finding someone's opinion or checking
whether an event occurred; say what quantity or property is being measured.
