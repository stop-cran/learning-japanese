# 温度 (おんど) - temperature

## Meaning

温度 is the temperature of something. Use **Nの温度** to specify what is being measured, 温度を測る (おんどをはかる) for measuring it, and 温度が高い・低い (たかい・ひくい) for a high or low temperature.

- お湯の温度を測りました。 (おゆのおんどをはかりました。) - I measured the temperature of the hot water.
- 冷蔵庫の温度を下げてください。 (れいぞうこのおんどをさげてください。) - Please lower the refrigerator's temperature.

温度を下げる describes causing the temperature to fall; 温度が下がる describes the temperature falling.

## How the kanji combine

温 contributes warmth or temperature, and [度](../kanji/度.md) contributes degree or measure. Together they name the measured degree of hotness or coldness. The readings are オン + ド.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[気温](気温.md) (きおん) specifically concerns air temperature; 体温 (たいおん) is body temperature. 温度 is the broader noun. [熱い](熱い.md) (あつい) describes something as hot, rather than naming its temperature.

## Distinctive meaning

Temperature and the sensation of heat are related but grammatically different. Say 温度が高い for "the temperature is high"; a person feeling cold may say [寒い](寒い.md). 温度 itself has no opposite: high and low are contrasting values of the same quantity.
