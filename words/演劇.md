# 演劇 (えんげき) - theatre; drama

## Meaning

演劇 means theatre or drama as a performed art, or a dramatic play.
It is a noun, not the name of the theatre building.

- 大学で演劇を学んでいます。
  (だいがくでえんげきをまなんでいます。) - I am studying theatre at university.
- 週末に友達と演劇を見に行きました。
  (しゅうまつにともだちとえんげきをみにいきました。) - I went to see a play with a friend at the weekend.

を marks what is studied or watched. In 演劇を見に行く,
見に states the purpose of going: to see a play.

## How the kanji combine

[演](../kanji/演.md) contributes performing, and
[劇](../kanji/劇.md) drama or a play. Together, エン + ゲキ
connect performing with dramatic art. Learn the compound rather than
assuming that every performance, such as a musical recital, is 演劇.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[劇](劇.md) (げき) and [芝居](芝居.md) (しばい) overlap in the
play or drama meaning. [ドラマ](ドラマ.md) can also mean drama,
including television drama. [舞台](舞台.md) (ぶたい) can name a
stage production as well as the stage itself.

## Distinctive meaning

English "theatre" can name an art or a building.
[劇場](劇場.md) (げきじょう) names a theatre venue;
[映画館](映画館.md) (えいがかん) is a cinema or movie theatre.
Neither building meaning makes 演劇 a destination noun for the building.
Use the complete expression: 演劇を見る, "watch a play," but
劇場に行く, "go to a theatre."
