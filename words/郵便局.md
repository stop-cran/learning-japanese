# 郵便局 (ゆうびんきょく)

## Meaning

郵便局 means a post office, where people use postal services such as mailing letters and parcels or buying stamps.

- 郵便局で切手を買いました。 (ゆうびんきょくで きってを かいました。) — I bought stamps at the post office.
- 郵便局は駅の近くにあります。 (ゆうびんきょくは えきの ちかくに あります。) — The post office is near the station.

## How the kanji combine

郵 concerns the post, and [便](../kanji/便.md) contributes delivery or communication in 郵便 (ゆうびん), "mail." [局](../kanji/局.md) adds an office or bureau. The reading is ゆうびん + きょく, with a long ゆう and small ょ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ポスト is a mailbox or posting box, not the post office itself. 郵便 identifies the mail or postal service; adding 局 names the office.

## Distinctive meaning

郵便局 names an institution and location, not the person delivering letters. Some post offices offer additional services, but those should not be assumed from the word alone. There is no natural direct antonym.
