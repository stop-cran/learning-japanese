# 単語 (たんご) — word; vocabulary item

## Meaning

単語 means a word considered as an individual vocabulary item. It is useful when discussing what a learner
knows, looks up, or memorizes.

- この単語の意味を調べました。 (このたんごのいみをしらべました。) — I looked up the meaning of this word.
- 毎日、新しい単語を五つ覚えます。 (まいにち、あたらしいたんごをいつつおぼえます。) — I learn five new vocabulary words every day.

単語を覚える (たんごをおぼえる) means "learn vocabulary words." The noun by itself does not specify
whether one word or several are intended; context supplies that information.

## How the kanji combine

[単](../kanji/単.md) contributes "single," qualifying [語](../kanji/語.md), "word; language."
Together they point to a word as one unit. The readings are タン + ゴ.
"Single" here does not mean that the word contains only one written character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[言葉](言葉.md) (ことば) is broader: it can refer to a word, phrase, expression, speech, or language.
[文](文.md) (ぶん), "sentence," and [文字](文字.md) (もじ), "written character," are contrasting units, not antonyms.

For example, [日本語](日本語.md) (にほんご), "Japanese language," can be learned as one 単語 although its spelling
contains three kanji.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling's length and the word's difficulty are separate from its status as a vocabulary item.
Do not read 単語 as "simple language": a specialized or difficult word is still a 単語.
Here the compound selects 単's single-unit sense, not its uncomplicated sense.
