# 海水浴 (かいすいよく) - sea bathing; swimming in the sea

## Meaning

海水浴 is sea bathing or going for a swim or dip in the ocean. 海水浴に行く (かいすいよくにいく) means to go sea bathing; 海水浴を楽しむ (かいすいよくをたのしむ) means to enjoy it.

- 夏休みに家族で海水浴に行きました。 (なつやすみにかぞくでかいすいよくにいきました。) - My family and I went sea bathing during the summer holiday.
- 波が穏やかな日に海水浴を楽しみました。 (なみがおだやかなひにかいすいよくをたのしみました。) - We enjoyed swimming in the sea on a day when the waves were calm.

## How the kanji combine

[海](../kanji/海.md), "sea," and [水](../kanji/水.md), "water," form 海水 (かいすい), "seawater." That modifies [浴](../kanji/浴.md), "bathing." The on readings are カイ + スイ + ヨク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[水泳](水泳.md) (すいえい), "swimming," is broader and includes pool swimming. [泳ぐ](泳ぐ.md) (およぐ), "swim," describes swimming movement; 海水浴 names the seaside bathing activity, which can include just going for a dip.

## Distinctive meaning

A trip to the beach is not automatically 海水浴 if nobody bathes or swims. Nor is this the ordinary label for taking a bath at home. The sea setting is part of the word, not an optional detail supplied only by the examples.
