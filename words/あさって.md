# あさって (あさって) - day after tomorrow

## Meaning

あさって means the day after tomorrow, two calendar days after today. It can locate an event in time without に.

- あさって、友達に会います。 (あさって、ともだちにあいます。) - I will meet a friend the day after tomorrow.

Before another noun, use の: あさっての予定 (あさってのよてい), "the plans for the day after tomorrow."

## How the kanji combine

The kana spelling is useful in everyday writing. The kanji spelling is 明後日, read as a whole word あさって rather than by assigning one syllable to each character. The same spelling also has the more formal reading みょうごにち.

## Synonyms and antonyms

あした is tomorrow; きょう is today; きのう is yesterday. These name different days, not interchangeable versions of あさって.

## Distinctive meaning

Its reference moves with the day of speaking. For travel arrangements or messages read later, an actual date can be clearer. "Two days later" in a story need not mean あさって.
