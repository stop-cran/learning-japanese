# 亀 (かめ) — turtle; tortoise

## Meaning

亀 means a **turtle or tortoise**. It is a broad animal name, not a label only for land-dwelling tortoises. The kana spelling カメ is also used. The noun itself does not mark singular or plural.

- 池に亀がいます。 (いけにかめがいます。) — There is a turtle in the pond.
- 亀が泳いでいます。 (かめがおよいでいます。) — A turtle is swimming.

These examples concern an aquatic turtle; they do not mean that every kind of 亀 lives in a pond.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun: [亀](../kanji/亀.md) is read **かめ** as a whole, with no okurigana. Its on-readings are not substitutes for かめ in this animal noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," is a broader category, not a synonym specific to turtles. ウミガメ (うみがめ), also written 海亀, means "sea turtle"; リクガメ (りくがめ), also written 陸亀, means "tortoise." There is no useful antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English may distinguish a land-dwelling "tortoise" from other "turtles," but the broad Japanese word かめ covers both. The more specific words above identify a narrower kind of animal; they are not alternate readings of the single character.

海亀 combines [海](../kanji/海.md), "sea," with 亀; かめ becomes がめ in うみがめ. The complete spelling 陸亀 is read りくがめ, not りくかめ.
