# 会場 (かいじょう) — event venue

## Meaning

会場 is the place where a gathering or event takes place: a meeting venue, hall, site, or grounds. It need not be a building.

コンサートの会場は駅の近くです。 (コンサートのかいじょうはえきのちかくです。) — The concert venue is near the station.

## How the kanji combine

[会](../kanji/会.md) suggests a meeting or gathering, and [場](../kanji/場.md) a place. Both take on-readings, かい + じょう. The result identifies a place by the event being held there.

## Synonyms and antonyms

場所 (ばしょ) is a place in general. 会議室 (かいぎしつ) is a meeting room; it can serve as a 会場, but the two words do not have the same scope. 会館 (かいかん) names a hall or institutional building.

## Distinctive meaning

会場に着く (かいじょうにつく) means "arrive at the venue"; 会場で待つ (かいじょうでまつ) means "wait at the venue." に marks the destination and で the place of the action. There is no direct opposite: the word describes an event-related role, not a fixed kind of architecture.
