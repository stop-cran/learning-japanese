# 合計 (ごうけい) - total; sum

## Meaning

合計 names a total obtained by adding amounts. 合計する means to add up or total something, with the amounts marked by を.

- 料金は合計で三千円です。 (りょうきんはごうけいでさんぜんえんです。) - The charges come to 3,000 yen in total.
- 一週間の支出を合計しました。 (いっしゅうかんのししゅつをごうけいしました。) - I added up a week's expenses.

合計で introduces a combined amount. 合計はいくらですか (ごうけいはいくらですか), "What is the total?", asks for the sum rather than the price of one item.

## How the kanji combine

[合](../kanji/合.md) contributes combining or bringing together, and [計](../kanji/計.md) contributes counting or calculating. The compound describes calculating quantities together. The on readings are ゴウ + ケイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[足す](足す.md) (たす) means to add; 合計する emphasizes obtaining the combined total. [全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ), "all/everything," does not itself imply arithmetic. A subtotal, 小計 (しょうけい), can contrast with a final total on a bill.

## Distinctive meaning

Check which amounts are included. 合計 tells you that figures are combined, but does not automatically mean tax, shipping, or every other charge has been included. A noun phrase such as 三人分の合計 (さんにんぶんのごうけい), "the total for three people," specifies the scope.
