# 式 (しき) - ceremony; formula

## Meaning

式 is a noun with several practical uses. It can be a ceremony, including a
wedding ceremony when that is understood, or a mathematical formula or expression.
After another element, it can mean a style or system, as in 日本式
(にほんしき), "Japanese style."

- 式は十時に始まります。 (しきはじゅうじにはじまります。) - The ceremony begins at ten.
- この式を使って、面積を求めます。 (このしきをつかって、めんせきをもとめます。) - We use this formula to calculate the area.

In the first example, the participants know which ceremony is meant.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [式](../kanji/式.md) uses its on reading シキ.
Ideas of a prescribed form or arrangement help connect its uses, but that is
a meaning guide, not a claim about the character's historical origin.
There is no second kanji within the word itself.

## Synonyms and antonyms

儀式 (ぎしき) more explicitly names a ceremony or ritual.
[方法](方法.md) (ほうほう) is a method; it overlaps with the system or manner
use, not with every occurrence of 式. An equation is one kind of mathematical
expression, so not every 式 needs an equals sign.

## Distinctive meaning

Let the surrounding words select the sense. 式に出る (しきにでる), "attend
the ceremony," does not mean attending a formula. Conversely, 式を書く
(しきをかく) in a mathematics lesson means writing an expression.
Avoid forcing one English equivalent across ceremonies, mathematics, and style
compounds. There is no shared antonym for all three.
