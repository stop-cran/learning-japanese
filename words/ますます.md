# ますます - increasingly; more and more

## Meaning

ますます is an adverb describing an existing tendency becoming stronger: "increasingly," "more and more," or "even more." What increases is the degree of the change or quality, not necessarily a numerical total.

- 日本語の勉強がますます楽しくなりました。 (にほんごのべんきょうがますますたのしくなりました。) - Studying Japanese has become more and more enjoyable.
- 村の人口はますます減っています。 (むらのじんこうはますますへっています。) - The village's population is dwindling further and further.

The second example concerns a decline. It does not say the population is growing just because ますます appears.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana; 益々 is another recognized spelling. Learn the repeated-sound adverb as one word. It is not two copies of the polite verb ending ます and does not make a sentence polite on its own.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[だんだん](だんだん.md) emphasizes gradual change. ますます emphasizes further development or intensification, without itself specifying that each step is gradual. [もっと](もっと.md) means "more" and also works in direct requests.

## Distinctive meaning

The direction comes from the predicate: ますます増える means "increase more and more," while ますます減る means "decrease further." It can describe improving, worsening, or emotionally neutral trends. Avoid treating it as an inherently positive prediction or as an instruction simply to add more of something.
