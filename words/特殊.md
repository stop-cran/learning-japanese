# 特殊 (とくしゅ) - special; particular in kind

## Meaning

特殊 describes something of a special, particular or unusual kind rather than
the general case. Use 特殊な before a noun and 特殊だ or 特殊です as a predicate.

- この作業には特殊な道具が必要です。
  (このさぎょうにはとくしゅなどうぐがひつようです。) - This task requires specialized tools.
- それは特殊な例で、一般には当てはまりません。
  (それはとくしゅなれいで、いっぱんにはあてはまりません。) - That is a special case and does not apply generally.

特殊な事情 (とくしゅなじじょう) are particular or unusual circumstances.
The word need not praise their quality.

## How the kanji combine

[特](../kanji/特.md) contributes specialness, and 殊 an exceptional or distinct
quality. Their related ideas reinforce the departure from the general case.
The on readings are トク + シュ. There is no separate 殊 card in this collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[特別](特別.md) (とくべつ) also means special and can concern special treatment.
[独特](独特.md) (どくとく) emphasizes a distinctive characteristic.
[一般](一般.md) (いっぱん), general, and [普通](普通.md) (ふつう), ordinary,
provide contextual contrasts.

## Distinctive meaning

特殊 is not necessarily superior, bizarre or one of a kind numerically.
The English "unique" here must not be turned into an absolute claim that
only one example exists, as with [唯一](唯一.md) (ゆいいつ), sole or only.
A specialized tool may be produced in large numbers.
