# チーズ (チーズ) - cheese

## Meaning

チーズ is the food cheese. It is a noun: チーズを食べる, "eat cheese," and チーズを入れる, "add cheese." The same written word also occurs as an interjection used when taking photographs.

- パンにチーズをのせました。 (パンにチーズをのせました。) - I put cheese on the bread.
- 写真を撮ります。はい、チーズ！ (しゃしんをとります。はい、チーズ！) - I'm taking the photo. Say cheese!

In the second example, nobody is requesting food. The phrase prompts the people being photographed to pose with a smile.

## How the kanji combine

This word is written in katakana, with no kanji to divide. The long-vowel mark ー extends チ, and ズ is voiced. Preserve the long vowel when reading or spelling チーズ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[バター](バター.md) is butter, a different food rather than a synonym. [牛乳](牛乳.md) (ぎゅうにゅう), "milk," is related as a dairy product, but the names are not interchangeable ingredients.

## Distinctive meaning

チーズ is normally a mass noun in food contexts: the word itself does not tell you whether someone means a slice, a piece, or a whole cheese. Add a quantity expression when needed. The photographic はい、チーズ is an established expression, not an example of treating every food noun as an exclamation.
