# におい (におい) — smell; scent

## Meaning

におい is a smell or scent. It can be pleasant or unpleasant; the noun alone does not always tell you which.

- いいにおいがします。 (いいにおいがします。) — It smells good.
- 台所からカレーのにおいがします。 (だいどころからカレーのにおいがします。) — There is a smell of curry coming from the kitchen.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses hiragana. The usual kanji spellings include 匂い and 臭い; 臭い is used for unpleasant smells. Kana におい avoids choosing between those spellings. 臭い can also be read くさい as an adjective, so context matters.
The source spelling 匂い (におい) belongs to the same noun entry.
The dictionary's unpleasant-smell note for 臭い is a spelling preference,
not an absolute ban on 匂い appearing in a negative context.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[香り](香り.md) (かおり) often suggests a fragrance or pleasant aroma. 悪臭 (あくしゅう) explicitly means a bad smell. These are useful distinctions, not a claim that every におい is unpleasant.

## Distinctive meaning

においがする describes perceiving a smell: が marks the smell, and する is not translated as "do." においをかぐ means to smell or sniff something deliberately. A person noticing a smell need not be actively sniffing, so learn these two patterns separately.
