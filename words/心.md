# 心 (こころ) — heart; mind

## Meaning

心 means **heart, mind, or spirit**: the inner life of feelings, thoughts, and intentions. The best English translation depends on the context.

友達の言葉で心が温かくなりました。 (ともだちのことばでこころがあたたかくなりました。) — My friend's words warmed my heart.

心の中 (こころのなか), “inside one's heart/mind,” uses の to connect the nouns.

## How the kanji combine

[心](../kanji/心.md) is a single character with the native reading こころ. Its on reading シン appears in compounds; the different readings are not interchangeable within this standalone word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

気持ち (きもち) often names a feeling or attitude. 心 is broader. 心臓 (しんぞう) means the physical heart as an organ, whereas 心 here concerns inner experience. 体 (からだ), “body,” gives a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

English divides “mind” and “heart” more sharply than many uses of 心 do. Do not restrict it to either reasoning or emotion. Likewise, a gloss such as “core” should not be used to infer that こころ names any object's physical center.
