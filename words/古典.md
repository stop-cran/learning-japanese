# 古典 (こてん) - classic work; the classics

## Meaning

古典 refers to classics or classic works, especially books.
It can concern works from an older period or works recognized as
lasting reference points in a field.

- 授業で日本の古典を読んでいます。
  (じゅぎょうでにほんのこてんをよんでいます。) - We are reading Japanese classics in class.
- この本は経済学の古典として知られています。
  (このほんはけいざいがくのこてんとしてしられています。) - This book is known as a classic of economics.

The second example uses として, "as," to identify the work's standing.
古典の授業 (こてんのじゅぎょう) is a class on the classics.

## How the kanji combine

[古](../kanji/古.md) contributes old or ancient.
典 contributes a book, canon, or established standard.
Their modifier-and-head relationship helps recall an old or
established classic work. The on readings are コ + テン.
This does not mean that any worn-out book is a 古典.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[クラシック](クラシック.md) overlaps with classic or classical
works, and also commonly names classical music.
現代文学 (げんだいぶんがく), contemporary literature, is a useful
period contrast when 古典 concerns older literature; not every
field divides its works into those exact two categories.

## Distinctive meaning

The source gloss "old book" is broader than the practical classic-work
selection here. A recently printed copy can contain a 古典;
the age or condition of that physical copy is not the deciding factor.
Conversely, an old publication need not be a recognized masterpiece.
The period and the field determine what counts as a classic.
