# 人通り (ひとどおり) - pedestrian traffic

## Meaning

人通り is the passage or flow of people along a street or through a place:
pedestrian traffic. 人通りが多い means that many people pass through;
人通りが少ない means that few do.

- この通りは人通りが多いです。 (このとおりはひとどおりがおおいです。) - This street has a lot of pedestrian traffic.
- 夜は人通りが少なくなります。 (よるはひとどおりがすくなくなります。) - Fewer people pass through at night.

The noun itself does not say that the place is crowded.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) contributes ひと, people;
[通](../kanji/通.md)り contributes passing through.
とおり becomes どおり in this native compound.
Distinguish ひとどおり from [一通り](一通り.md), ひととおり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通行](通行.md) (つうこう) concerns passage by people or vehicles.
[交通](交通.md) (こうつう) is broader traffic or transportation.
[通り](通り.md) (とおり) can name the street and also has a traffic sense.
These overlap without making a street identical to the people using it.

## Distinctive meaning

人通り describes people passing, not simply a group standing together.
It does not mean a traffic jam, a work commute, or a count of residents.
In 人通りの多い道 (ひとどおりのおおいみち), "a street with many passersby,"
the fuller modifier supplies how busy the pedestrian flow is.
