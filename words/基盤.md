# 基盤 (きばん) - foundation; underlying infrastructure

## Meaning

基盤 is an underlying base or foundation that supports an activity, organization, or system. Depending on context, "infrastructure" is a useful translation. It is a noun.

- 地域の産業を支える基盤を整えます。
  (ちいきのさんぎょうをささえるきばんをととのえます。) - We will develop the foundation supporting the region's industries.
- 新しいサービスは、共通のソフトウェア基盤を使っています。
  (あたらしいサービスは、きょうつうのソフトウェアきばんをつかっています。) - The new services use a shared software platform.

The second sentence illustrates the computing use: a platform on which other work or services operate.

## How the kanji combine

基 contributes a base, and 盤 a board or supporting surface. Their related contributions suggest underlying support. The on readings are キ + バン. This is a meaning aid, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) and [基本](基本.md) (きほん) also concern foundations or basics. 基盤 is especially useful for the supporting framework or conditions on which something operates; this is an emphasis, not an absolute prohibition on overlapping uses.

## Distinctive meaning

基盤 need not be a concrete slab. Financial resources, institutions, or technical systems can provide a foundation. An electronics circuit board or substrate is ordinarily written 基板 (きばん), a separate same-reading word; do not infer a circuit board from every occurrence of 基盤.
