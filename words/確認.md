# 確認 (かくにん) - confirmation; verification

## Meaning

確認 is a noun for checking or confirmation. 確認する is transitive: use を for the information or condition being checked. A question clause can specify what you need to establish.

- 予約の時間を確認します。 (よやくのじかんをかくにんします。) - I'll check the reservation time.
- ドアが閉まっているか確認してください。 (ドアがしまっているかかくにんしてください。) - Please check whether the door is closed.

In the second sentence, か marks the embedded question "whether it is closed." 確認しました (かくにんしました) means "I checked/confirmed it"; the context supplies what was established.

## How the kanji combine

[確](../kanji/確.md) means certain or firm, and [認](../kanji/認.md) means recognize or acknowledge. Together they express establishing something with certainty. The on readings are カク + ニン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[確かめる](確かめる.md) (たしかめる) is close to "make sure/check." [調べる](調べる.md) (しらべる) can describe broader investigation or looking something up; 確認する focuses on checking the fact or status in question. The activities can overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

確認 is not automatically approval or agreement. A person may check a proposal's details without accepting the proposal. Similarly, asking 確認してもいいですか (かくにんしてもいいですか), "May I check?", requests an opportunity to verify information, not permission to change it.
