# 三千 (さんぜん) — 3,000

## Meaning

三千 is 3,000: 三千円 さんぜんえん (3,000 yen), 三千人 さんぜんにん (3,000 people).

## How the kanji combine

[三](../kanji/三.md) (three) multiplies [千](../kanji/千.md) (thousand). The initial セン is voiced to ゼン after ん. Similarly 八千 はっせん (8,000), with the contraction of 八 to はっ.
Compare [三百](三百.md) さんびゃく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 二千 にせん, 四千 よんせん, 五千 ごせん, 八千 はっせん.
- Related: [千円](千円.md) せんえん.

## Distinctive meaning

Say さんぜん, not さんせん. Other thousands, such as 二千 にせん, take no voicing.
