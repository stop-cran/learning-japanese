# 励ます (はげます) — encourage; cheer someone on

## Meaning

励ます means encouraging or cheering someone on or up. In the ordinary person-encouragement use, the person takes を.

- 友達を励ましました。 (ともだちをはげましました。) — I encouraged my friend.
- 友人の言葉に励まされました。 (ゆうじんのことばにはげまされました。) — I was encouraged by my friend's words.

The second sentence is passive: 励まされる (はげまされる) means "be encouraged." The speaker receives encouragement rather than encouraging someone else.

## How the kanji combine

[励](../kanji/励.md) writes はげ, with ます in kana. Here ます is part of the dictionary-form spelling, **not a polite ending**. This is one native godan verb ending in す, not a compound of on readings.

Useful forms are 励まします (はげまします), 励まして (はげまして), 励ました (はげました) and 励まさない (はげまさない). Compare the separate verb [励む](励む.md) (はげむ), "work hard," whose polite form is 励みます (はげみます).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[激励](激励.md) (げきれい) is a noun for encouragement; 激励する is a related する verb. [応援](応援.md) (おうえん) includes support and cheering, but can also mean practical backup.

[慰める](慰める.md) (なぐさめる), "comfort; console," emphasizes easing distress. 励ます can urge someone onward. Encouragement and consolation may occur together; this is a difference of emphasis, not mutually exclusive compartments.

## Distinctive meaning

Encouragement does not guarantee success or that the recipient immediately feels better. The verb is not restricted to encouraging other people: one can encourage oneself too. This article focuses on encouragement, not the separate voice-raising sense recorded in dictionaries.
