# 殺す (ころす) — kill

## Meaning

殺す means to cause a living being to die. It is transitive: the being affected is an object marked with を.
It is a す-ending godan verb, a verb whose ending changes across forms: 殺します (ころします), 殺した (ころした), and 殺さない (ころさない).

- 猫がネズミを殺しました。 (ねこがネズミをころしました。) — The cat killed a mouse.

The sentence names both the cause and the animal that died. It does not give the mouse the subject role.

## How the kanji combine

[殺](../kanji/殺.md) supplies the killing meaning and the reading ころ; the kana す complete the verb.
The source form ころ.す marks the boundary before that written ending.
There is no multi-kanji compound to interpret.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[死ぬ](死ぬ.md) (しぬ), "die," is intransitive: ネズミが死にました (ネズミがしにました), "The mouse died."
It describes the death without identifying someone or something that caused it.
[助ける](助ける.md) (たすける) can mean to save or rescue, a useful contrast in a life-saving context, but it also has the broader meaning "help."

## Distinctive meaning

"Murder" is not the right English translation in every context: 殺す also applies to animals and other living organisms.
The word describes causing death; the subject and circumstances determine the appropriate translation.
Do not confuse its transitive grammar with the subject pattern used by 死ぬ.
