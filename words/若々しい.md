# 若々しい (わかわかしい) - youthful; young-looking

## Meaning

若々しい is an i-adjective describing a youthful appearance, voice or manner.
Someone can be 若々しい without being young in years.

- あの人はいつも若々しいです。 (あのひとはいつもわかわかしいです。) - That person always looks youthful.
- 母は若々しい声で話します。 (はははわかわかしいこえではなします。) - My mother speaks in a youthful voice.

Before a noun, use 若々しい directly. The adverbial form is 若々しく:
若々しく見える (わかわかしくみえる), "look youthful."
The negative is 若々しくない.

## How the kanji combine

[若](../kanji/若.md) supplies the native young/youth association.
々 repeats the preceding character; 若若しい is another recognized spelling.
Learn the whole reading わかわかしい, not the on reading ジャク repeated twice.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[若い](若い.md) (わかい) can directly concern age and also overlaps in
youthful qualities. 若々しい emphasizes the impression of youth.
[幼い](幼い.md) (おさない) can concern being very young or immature;
it is not a free replacement for a compliment about a youthful voice.

## Distinctive meaning

The ordinary modern examples here describe youthfulness, not a medical judgment
or an exact age. Digital Daijisen additionally records an immature/childlike
use with literary examples, so "always a compliment" would be too strong.
Do not assume that repeating 若 produces a simple numerical meaning "very young."
