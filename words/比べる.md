# 比べる (くらべる) - to compare

## Meaning

比べる means to compare two or more things. A useful pattern is AとBを比べる, "compare A and B." AをBと比べる presents B as the comparison partner.

- 二つの店の値段を比べました。 (ふたつのみせのねだんをくらべました。) - I compared the prices at the two shops.
- 去年と比べて、今年は暖かいです。 (きょねんとくらべて、ことしはあたたかいです。) - Compared with last year, this year is warm.

It is a transitive ichidan verb: 比べます, 比べない, 比べて. Both と比べて and に比べて can introduce a standard of comparison.

## How the kanji combine

比 expresses comparison; べる is the written okurigana. The grammatical stem used before ます is くらべ, not just the portion represented by the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

比較する (ひかくする) is a more formal equivalent. 選ぶ (えらぶ), "choose," describes a possible next step after comparing, not the comparison itself.

## Distinctive meaning

比べる does not imply deciding which item is better. You can compare sizes, prices, or similarities without selecting a winner. The comparison must be supplied by the context or named explicitly.
