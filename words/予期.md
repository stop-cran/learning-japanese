# 予期 (よき) - expectation; anticipation

## Meaning

予期 is anticipating that something will happen. It is a noun and a transitive する verb: 結果を予期する (けっかをよきする), "anticipate a result." It does not say whether that result is wanted.

- その結果は予期していました。 (そのけっかはよきしていました。) - I had expected that result.
- 予期しない問題が起こりました。 (よきしないもんだいがおこりました。) - An unexpected problem occurred.

予期していなかった means "had not expected." 予期せぬ (よきせぬ), found before nouns such as 出来事 (できごと), is another expression meaning "unexpected."

## How the kanji combine

[予](../kanji/予.md) contributes beforehand or in advance. [期](../kanji/期.md) contributes expecting or looking toward an appointed time. Together they present an expectation formed before the event.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[期待](期待.md) (きたい) can include hopeful anticipation. 予期 can concern an unwelcome result just as readily as a welcome one. [予想](予想.md) (よそう) overlaps in predicting an outcome; it can emphasize what one thinks the outcome will be.

## Distinctive meaning

予期 is not a guarantee or evidence that the event must occur. Its negative is particularly useful when something happens outside the expected course. Distinguish "I did not expect it" from "I did not want it": 予期していなかった expresses the former, without deciding the latter.
