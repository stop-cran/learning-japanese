# スター (スター) - star; celebrity

## Meaning

スター is a star performer or celebrity, such as a prominent actor or athlete. It can also mean a star-shaped symbol and, in suitable contexts, a celestial star.

- 彼女は映画のスターになりました。 (かのじょはえいがのスターになりました。) - She became a movie star.
- その選手はチームのスターです。 (そのせんしゅはチームのスターです。) - That player is the team's star.

It is a noun: use スターになる, "become a star," rather than treating it as an adjective ending in い.

## How the kanji combine

スター is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "star." The long vowel mark ー lengthens タ. Japanese supplies a vowel after the initial consonant, giving ス・ター; there are no kanji to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[星](星.md) (ほし) is the basic word for a star in the sky. [有名](有名.md) (ゆうめい), "famous," describes being widely known and has different grammar: 有名な人 (ゆうめいなひと), "a famous person."

## Distinctive meaning

スター often foregrounds someone as a conspicuous performer or attraction, not just a person whose name is known. The dictionary also records symbol and celestial uses, so "celebrity" is not an exclusive meaning. Context decides whether a speaker is discussing a person, a design, or an astronomical object; ordinary sky descriptions can use 星.
