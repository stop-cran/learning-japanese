# 調節 (ちょうせつ) - regulation; adjustment; control

## Meaning

調節 is regulation or adjustment to a suitable level.
調節する is transitive: the temperature, volume, or other thing regulated takes を.

- 音量を調節してください。
  (おんりょうをちょうせつしてください。) - Please adjust the volume.
- エアコンで室温を調節しています。
  (エアコンでしつおんをちょうせつしています。) - We regulate the room temperature with the air conditioner.

調節できる means can be adjusted.

## How the kanji combine

[調](../kanji/調.md) contributes tuning or bringing into balance.
節 contributes the idea of moderating or keeping within bounds in this compound.
Learn the established whole word with on readings チョウ + セツ.
The separate [節](節.md) (ふし), joint or tune, is not its pronunciation here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[調整](調整.md) (ちょうせい) overlaps broadly in adjustment.
It is also useful for coordination among schedules or interests;
調節 commonly concerns regulating an amount or level.
[加減](加減.md) (かげん) overlaps when adjusting heat or quantity.
[合わせる](合わせる.md) (あわせる) can describe matching a setting to a reference.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not turn the comparison with 調整 into an absolute mechanical-versus-human rule.
[管理](管理.md) (かんり) can concern controlling or maintaining a condition
through ongoing oversight, rather than just changing its setting.
[整理](整理.md) (せいり) also includes adjustment/regulation in its dictionary range.
[支配](支配.md) (しはい), rule or domination, is not an automatic substitute
for adjusting the volume merely because English uses control for both.
