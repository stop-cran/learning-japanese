# 停電 (ていでん) - power outage; blackout

## Meaning

停電 is an interruption of the electricity supply: a power outage or power cut. 停電する describes the power going off.

- 台風で停電しました。
  (たいふうでていでんしました。) - The typhoon caused a power outage.
- 停電中はエレベーターが使えません。
  (ていでんちゅうはエレベーターがつかえません。) - The elevator cannot be used during the power outage.

停電に備える (ていでんにそなえる) means "prepare for a power outage." 復旧する (ふっきゅうする), "be restored," is useful when discussing the return of the supply.

## How the kanji combine

[停](../kanji/停.md) contributes stopping, and [電](../kanji/電.md) electricity. Together they concern the interruption of electric power. The readings are テイ + デン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[断水](断水.md) (だんすい) is an interruption of the water supply, not a power outage. [停止](停止.md) (ていし) is a broader stoppage or suspension. 電気がつく (でんきがつく), "the light comes on," can describe a visible sign of restoration.

## Distinctive meaning

A planned power cut can be 停電 as well as an unexpected failure. The word alone does not identify the cause, geographical extent, or duration. An individual appliance failing while electricity remains available is not necessarily a 停電.
