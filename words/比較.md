# 比較 (ひかく) - comparison

## Meaning

比較 is comparison. 比較する is a transitive する verb: **A と B を比較する**, "compare A and B," or **A を B と比較する**, "compare A with B."

- 二つの製品の値段を比較しました。 (ふたつのせいひんのねだんをひかくしました。) - I compared the prices of the two products.
- 去年と比較すると、今年は雨が少ないです。 (きょねんとひかくすると、ことしはあめがすくないです。) - Compared with last year, there is less rain this year.

The second example uses と比較すると to establish the reference for a judgment. A noun phrase such as 二つの案の比較 (ふたつのあんのひかく) means "a comparison of two proposals."

## How the kanji combine

比 contributes comparing or setting things side by side; 較 also contributes comparison. The two reinforce the same central action. The on readings are ヒ + カク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[比べる](比べる.md) (くらべる) is the corresponding everyday verb "compare." [判断](判断.md) (はんだん) is judgment or assessment, which may follow a comparison but is not the comparison itself.

## Distinctive meaning

比較 does not automatically mean deciding a winner. A comparison can describe similarities, differences, prices, or changes over time. State the dimension when useful, as with 値段を比較する. Also distinguish 比較 from 比較的 (ひかくてき), "relatively," which is a related adverbial expression rather than the noun alone.
