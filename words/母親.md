# 母親 (ははおや) - mother

## Meaning

母親 is a mother, viewed as a parent or in relation to a child.
It is a noun: 子供の母親 (こどものははおや), "the child's mother."
It can describe one's own mother or someone else's; the word itself does
not identify whose mother is meant.

- その子は母親と一緒に来ました。 (そのこはははおやといっしょにきました。) - The child came with their mother.
- 私の母親は図書館で働いています。 (わたしのははおやはとしょかんではたらいています。) - My mother works at a library.

と identifies the accompanying person in the first sentence.

## How the kanji combine

[母](../kanji/母.md) means mother, and [親](../kanji/親.md) means parent.
The native readings はは + おや identify the maternal parent.
This is not a compound read with the on readings ボ and シン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

母 (はは) is a usual way to refer to one's own mother when speaking to
others. [お母さん](お母さん.md) (おかあさん) is used in address and in
references shaped by politeness and family perspective.
[父親](父親.md) (ちちおや), "father," is a parallel family role, not an
antonym in the sense of an opposing action.

## Distinctive meaning

母親 is useful when identifying or discussing the parent-child relationship.
It is not the ordinary way to call out "Mom!" directly to someone.
Choose a family address term for that purpose rather than assuming every
English "mother" translates identically. The noun also does not specify the
mother's occupation, age, or personality.
