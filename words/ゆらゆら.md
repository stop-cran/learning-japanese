# ゆらゆら — swaying back and forth

## Meaning

Repeated swaying, rocking, or wavering movement.

- 風でカーテンがゆらゆら揺（ゆ）れている。 — The curtain is swaying in the breeze.
- 小さな船（ふね）が波（なみ）でゆらゆら揺れた。 — The little boat rocked on the waves.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぐらぐら](ぐらぐら.md) often adds a sense of unstable support. [ぶらぶら](ぶらぶら.md) can describe loose dangling movement. These tendencies do not make every moving object fit only one word.

## Distinctive meaning

ゆらゆら揺れる is a useful phrase. A flame may also waver ゆらゆら: the word describes its movement, not a sound. A swaying boat is not necessarily damaged or about to fall over.
