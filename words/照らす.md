# 照らす (てらす) - illuminate; shine on

## Meaning

照らす is a transitive godan verb: light shines on or illuminates a target,
which takes を. A light source can be the subject; a person can also direct
light using a lamp.

- 月が海を照らしています。 (つきがうみをてらしています。) - The moon is illuminating the sea.
- 懐中電灯で足元を照らしました。 (かいちゅうでんとうであしもとをてらしました。) - I shone a flashlight at the ground by my feet.

The second example uses で for the instrument. Forms include 照らします,
照らして, and 照らさない.

## How the kanji combine

[照](../kanji/照.md) contributes illumination here. The written division is
て + らす; the godan verb changes to てらし before ます.

## Synonyms and antonyms

照る (てる), [光る](光る.md) (ひかる), and [輝く](輝く.md) (かがやく) are
intransitive shining verbs. Compare 月が照る, "the moon shines," with
月が海を照らす, "the moon illuminates the sea." [光](光.md),
[明かり](明かり.md), and [灯](灯.md) are light nouns, not the same grammatical
construction.

## Distinctive meaning

照らす also means to compare with or refer to something. 規則に照らして判断する
(きそくにてらしてはんだんする) means "judge in light of the rules."
Here に introduces the standard, not a lamp or an illuminated object.
[比べる](比べる.md) and [比較](比較.md) describe comparison more generally;
[参考](参考.md) is a reference/consultation noun. These overlaps do not make
照らす a substitute for every comparison or every use of reference material.
Also keep 照れる (てれる), "feel shy," separate from this verb.
