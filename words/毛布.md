# 毛布 (もうふ) - blanket

## Meaning

毛布 is a noun meaning "blanket." It names a covering used for
warmth, for example on a bed or over one's knees. Learn 毛布をかける,
"put a blanket over someone," and 毛布をかぶる, "cover oneself
with a blanket."

- 寒いので、毛布をもう一枚ください。 (さむいので、もうふをもういちまいください。) - It is cold, so please give me another blanket.
- 子供に毛布をかけました。 (こどもにもうふをかけました。) - I put a blanket over the child.

枚 (まい) counts blankets as flat items. In the second example,
に marks the person being covered and を marks the covering.

## How the kanji combine

毛 contributes hair or wool, and 布 contributes cloth. The first
character describes the material associated with the cloth. The
on readings combine as もう + ふ. This component explanation
does not make wool a requirement for every modern 毛布; synthetic
blankets can also be called 毛布.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[布団](布団.md) (ふとん) names Japanese bedding, including a
quilt or mattress depending on the compound and context.
A 毛布 is a blanket, not the whole [ベッド](ベッド.md)
or bedding arrangement. A sheet is another bedding item rather
than an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

毛布 identifies the object, not how it must be used. It can
provide warmth while sleeping, sitting, or traveling. Learn the
covering verb with its particles instead of translating every
English "put on" with a clothing verb.
