# 人気 (にんき) - popularity

## Meaning

人気 (にんき) is popularity or public favor. It is a noun: 人気がある means
"be popular," 人気がない means "not be popular," and 人気のある modifies a noun.
に can identify the group among whom someone or something is popular.

- この歌手は若者に人気があります。 (このかしゅはわかものににんきがあります。) - This singer is popular with young people.
- 週末は人気のある店に行列ができます。 (しゅうまつはにんきのあるみせにぎょうれつができます。) - On weekends, queues form at popular shops.

人気を集める (にんきをあつめる) means attracting popularity.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) contributes people, and [気](../kanji/気.md) contributes
feeling or disposition. Together, in にんき, they concern people's favor or
interest. Read it ニン + キ, not ひとけ in the examples above.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[評判](評判.md) (ひょうばん) is reputation or what people say about something;
it may be good or bad. 人気 emphasizes being liked or attracting interest.
Unpopularity is expressed directly with 人気がない.

## Distinctive meaning

The same spelling has a separate lexical identity, 人気 (ひとけ), meaning human
presence: 人気のない道 (ひとけのないみち), "a deserted road."
Compare 人気のない歌手 (にんきのないかしゅ), "an unpopular singer."
The road lacks people; the singer lacks popularity. The kanji alone do not
settle the reading, and "sign of life" is not the meaning of にんき.
