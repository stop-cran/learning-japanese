# 番組 (ばんぐみ) — broadcast program

## Meaning

番組 means a program or show, especially on television or radio. It names the content, not the television set or radio device.

毎朝、ラジオの番組を聞きます。（まいあさ、ラジオのばんぐみをききます。） — I listen to a radio program every morning.

## How the kanji combine

[番](../kanji/番.md) contributes the idea of order or a turn, while [組](../kanji/組.md) relates to grouping or arrangement. This is mixed on-plus-native reading: ばん + くみ becomes ばんぐみ. It is grouped under kango here because the first part is on-read.

## Synonyms and antonyms

放送（ほうそう） means broadcasting or a broadcast. ニュース番組（ニュースばんぐみ） is a news program, one kind of 番組.

## Distinctive meaning

For a television program use 番組を見る; for radio use 番組を聞く. English "program" also means software or an event schedule, so 番組 is not a universal replacement for every use of that English word.
