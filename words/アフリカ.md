# アフリカ - Africa

## Meaning

アフリカ is Africa, the continent. It is a place name, not the name of a single country or language.

いつかアフリカに行きたいです。 (いつかアフリカにいきたいです。) - I would like to go to Africa someday.

アフリカの地図を見ています。 (アフリカのちずをみています。) - I am looking at a map of Africa.

Use ordinary noun particles: に for a destination and の to connect the name with another noun.

## How the kanji combine

The usual modern spelling uses katakana, as many foreign place names do. Older kanji spellings are dictionary alternatives, not needed for everyday writing. There are no written kanji components in アフリカ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

アフリカ大陸 (アフリカたいりく) explicitly means "the African continent." Other continent names, such as アジア, are geographical comparisons, not synonyms or opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

アフリカの文化 can mean "African culture," but Africa contains many countries, communities, and languages. Name the country or community when that precision matters.
