# あっち — over there

## Meaning

あっち casually points to a place or direction away from both speaker and listener. Particles show its role: あっちに, "over there," or あっちへ, "that way."

駅はあっちです。 (えきはあっちです。) — The station is over there.

あっちへ行こう。 (あっちへいこう。) — Let's go that way.

## How the kanji combine

This is the conversational form of あちら and is normally written in kana. The dictionary spelling 彼方 is rare for this reading; it is not needed for everyday writing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

こっち points toward the speaker; そっち toward the listener; どっち asks "which way?" あちら is the less casual counterpart.

## Distinctive meaning

Depending on context, あっち can also indicate "that one" or the other party. It is not a respectful way to identify a person. When giving directions to a customer or introducing someone politely, あちら is generally more suitable than あっち.
