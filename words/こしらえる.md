# こしらえる - make; prepare; fabricate

## Meaning

こしらえる means to make or prepare something, including an object
or food. It can also mean to build something.
It is a transitive ichidan verb: こしらえます, こしらえた,
こしらえて, こしらえない. What is made takes を.

- 祖父は木で小さな箱をこしらえました。
  (そふはきでちいさなはこをこしらえました。) - My grandfather made a small box out of wood.
- 母がお弁当をこしらえてくれました。
  (ははがおべんとうをこしらえてくれました。) - My mother made a packed lunch for me.

An additional use is inventing an excuse or story:
言い訳をこしらえた (いいわけをこしらえた), "made up an excuse."
The object tells you whether making is practical or figurative.

## How the kanji combine

The dictionary spelling is 拵える, but the word is usually written
in kana. This card uses the complete native reading こしらえる,
without assigning separate meanings to each kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作る](作る.md) (つくる) is a broad everyday making verb.
[用意](用意.md)する (よういする), [準備](準備.md)する (じゅんびする),
and [支度](支度.md)する (したくする) overlap in preparing.
[製造](製造.md)する (せいぞうする) and [生産](生産.md)する (せいさんする)
frame making as manufacture or production.
[建てる](建てる.md) (たてる) specifically concerns constructing buildings.

## Distinctive meaning

A source gloss "manufacture" does not limit こしらえる to a factory.
Conversely, preparing a lunch and fabricating an excuse are not
interchangeable activities simply because this verb can describe both.
The examples teach making, building, preparing, and making up;
other dictionary uses, such as money and relationships, are outside
this practical selection.
