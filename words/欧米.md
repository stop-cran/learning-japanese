# 欧米 (おうべい) - Europe and America; the West

## Meaning

欧米 refers collectively to Europe and America, often especially
Europe and the United States. It can also be translated "the West."
It is a noun, not the name of a single country.

- この製品は欧米で売られています。
  (このせいひんはおうべいでうられています。) - This product is sold in Europe and America.
- 欧米の歴史を勉強しています。
  (おうべいのれきしをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying the history of Europe and America.

で identifies where the product is sold, and の connects the region
with the history studied.

## How the kanji combine

[欧](../kanji/欧.md) abbreviates Europe, and
[米](../kanji/米.md) abbreviates America, particularly the United
States in terms such as 米国 (べいこく).
The two regions form a paired compound: オウ + ベイ.
米 does not mean rice in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[西洋](西洋.md) (せいよう) also names the Western world;
its cultural and historical uses overlap with 欧米.
[欧州](欧州.md) (おうしゅう) and
[ヨーロッパ](ヨーロッパ.md) name Europe, not both halves of 欧米.

## Distinctive meaning

The category's boundaries depend on the context. Do not infer a fixed
country list or assume that every society grouped under 欧米 has
the same culture. Conversely, this is a conventional geographical
expression, not a statement about a direction from the speaker.
