# 期間 (きかん) — period; duration

## Meaning

期間 is a period or interval of time. It treats time as a stretch, for example the period covered
by a trip or a course. A useful pattern is **noun + の期間**, "the period of ...".

- 旅行の期間は一週間です。 (りょこうのきかんはいっしゅうかんです。) — The trip lasts one week.
- その期間は日本にいました。 (そのきかんはにほんにいました。) — I was in Japan during that period.

## How the kanji combine

[期](../kanji/期.md) supplies a period or term; [間](../kanji/間.md) supplies the interval.
Together the characters focus on the span of a period rather than a single instant.
Their on readings are キ + カン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時間](時間.md) (じかん) is a broader time word and can also express duration.
時刻 (じこく) points to a time such as 午後三時 (ごごさんじ), "3 p.m."
For the trip example, 一週間 is a duration; 三時 would instead identify a clock time.

## Distinctive meaning

期間 can name the interval itself even when its length is already known from context: その期間,
"that period." The word is not a unit such as an hour or a week, and it does not by itself
specify how long the interval lasts.
There is no need for a forced antonym; a point in time and a period are different ways of locating events.
