# または - or; either alternative

## Meaning

または connects alternatives: "or" or "either ... or ..."
It is especially useful in notices, instructions, and clearly stated choices.

- 名前はペンまたは鉛筆で書いてください。 (なまえはペンまたはえんぴつでかいてください。) - Please write your name with a pen or pencil.

The two nouns form one alternative phrase; で then marks the writing tool.

## How the kanji combine

Hiragana is usual; 又は is another spelling. The final は is pronounced わ,
as in the particle は. The written kana reading remains または, not またわ.
Do not interpret it as merely adding "also" to an unrelated sentence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

か can also join alternatives, as in ペンか鉛筆 (ペンかえんぴつ).
それとも often introduces another possibility in a question.
そして means "and then; and," connecting rather than selecting alternatives.

## Distinctive meaning

または does not automatically mean "otherwise, if not" between any two
sentences. That conditional use may need そうでなければ.
Whether one or several choices are allowed depends on the surrounding
instructions; the word alone is not a complete selection rule.
