# 害 (がい) — harm

## Meaning

害 means harm, damage, or a harmful influence. It names the harmful effect rather than a particular wound.
Useful patterns include 害がある, "be harmful," and 害を与える (がいをあたえる), "cause harm."

- この虫は人に害を与えません。 (このむしはひとにがいをあたえません。) — This insect does not harm people.

In this sentence, を marks the harm being caused and に marks who would be affected.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [害](../kanji/害.md) writes this Sino-Japanese noun with the on reading ガイ.
No second component narrows it to a particular type of damage.
Other words add that perspective, as in 被害 (ひがい), damage suffered.

## Synonyms and antonyms

被害 focuses on harm or damage on the receiving side.
損害 (そんがい) also means damage or loss and can be useful when discussing what has been lost or damaged.
無害 (むがい), "harmless," contrasts with 有害 (ゆうがい), "harmful"; these describe whether something causes harm rather than naming a beneficial effect.

## Distinctive meaning

害 is a noun, not an adjective to attach directly before another noun.
For "a harmful thing," use 有害な物 (ゆうがいなもの), or a phrase such as 害のある物 (がいのあるもの).
Absence of harm is not the same claim as usefulness or benefit.
