# 一昨年 (おととし) - year before last

## Meaning

一昨年 is the calendar year before last year: two calendar years before the
current one. It can stand directly as a time expression without に.

- 一昨年、日本に来ました。 (おととし、にほんにきました。) - I came to Japan the year before last.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), [昨](../kanji/昨.md), and [年](../kanji/年.md) identify one year
further back than last year. The everyday reading おととし belongs to the
whole written word, not to three separately pronounced characters.
The same spelling also has the on-reading いっさくねん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[去年](去年.md) (きょねん) is "last year." 再来年 (さらいねん), "the year after
next," is the corresponding forward-looking expression, not a synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

一昨年 does not mean a rolling period exactly twenty-four months before today.
For a following noun, use の: 一昨年の夏 (おととしのなつ), "the summer before
last." Keep the pronunciation おととし distinct from 一昨日 (おととい), "the
day before yesterday."
