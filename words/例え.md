# 例え (たとえ) - example; analogy; metaphor

## Meaning

例え is a noun for an illustrative example or a figurative comparison, such as a
simile or metaphor. Use 例えを使う, "use an example or analogy," and
Nという例え, "the comparison or expression N."

- 先生は身近な例えを使って説明しました。
  (せんせいはみぢかなたとえをつかってせつめいしました。)
  - The teacher explained it using a familiar example.
- 「人生は旅だ」という例えがあります。
  (「じんせいはたびだ」というたとえがあります。)
  - There is a metaphor that life is a journey.

The first makes an explanation accessible; the second describes one thing in terms of another.
In context, English "analogy" may fit better than "example."

## How the kanji combine

[例](../kanji/例.md) contributes the idea of an example, with the reading たと here;
え completes the noun's spelling.
JMdict associates the example sense especially with 例え and the figurative sense
especially with 喩え or 譬え. These are spelling preferences, not absolute sense restrictions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[例えば](例えば.md) (たとえば) introduces an example: "for example."
It is not the same noun construction as 例えを使う.
比喩 (ひゆ) is a term for figurative language.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse this noun with the same-sounding たとえ in たとえ雨でも,
"even if it rains." That expression does not name an analogy.
The particles in 例えを使う and 例えがある make the noun role clear.
