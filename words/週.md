# 週 (しゅう) - week

## Meaning

週 is a week. It functions as a noun and follows numbers in expressions that count weeks. It also appears in frequency phrases such as 週に二回 (しゅうににかい), "twice a week."

- 週に三回、泳いでいます。 (しゅうにさんかい、およいでいます。) - I swim three times a week.
- その次の週は忙しいです。 (そのつぎのしゅうはいそがしいです。) - I am busy the following week.

For a duration, 一週間 (いっしゅうかん) means "one week" and 二週間 (にしゅうかん) means "two weeks." Notice the small っ in いっしゅう.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [週](../kanji/週.md) supplies the unit, using the on reading シュウ. In compounds, other elements locate or count that unit: [今週](今週.md) (こんしゅう) is "this week."

## Synonyms and antonyms

週間 (しゅうかん) appears in duration expressions and named week-long periods. 曜日 (ようび) identifies a day of the week rather than a seven-day period. These are related calendar terms, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

週に三回 states a rate, not three consecutive weeks. Likewise, 次の週 locates a week in relation to another event or period. A calendar's choice of the first weekday must come from context, not from 週 alone.
