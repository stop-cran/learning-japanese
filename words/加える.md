# 加える (くわえる) - add; include

## Meaning

加える means to add something, append it, or include it in a group.
It is a transitive ichidan verb: AにBを加える means "add B to A."
The thing added takes を; the receiving mixture, total, or group takes に.

- スープに塩を少し加えてください。
  (スープにしおをすこしくわえてください。) - Please add a little salt to the soup.
- 私も仲間に加えてください。
  (わたしもなかまにくわえてください。) - Please let me join the group too.

In arithmetic, 三に二を加える (さんににをくわえる) means "add two to three."
Forms include 加えます, 加えた, and 加えない.

## How the kanji combine

[加](../kanji/加.md) carries the addition meaning and is read くわ here.
える completes the written verb. The conjugation changes the final る:
the stem is 加え (くわえ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[足す](足す.md) (たす) overlaps in adding quantities.
[加わる](加わる.md) (くわわる) is intransitive: 仲間に加わる means to join the group,
whereas someone 加える another person.
[引く](引く.md) (ひく), "subtract," is an opposite in arithmetic.

## Distinctive meaning

The object need not be physical: 説明を加える (せつめいをくわえる) means to add an explanation.
English "sum up" should not lead you to use 加える for every summary of a speech;
the central idea here is addition or inclusion.
