# 昨夜 (ゆうべ) — last night

## Meaning

昨夜, read ゆうべ, means last night or yesterday evening.

- 昨夜はよく眠れました。 (ゆうべはよくねむれました。) — I slept well last night.
- 昨夜、友達から電話がありました。 (ゆうべ、ともだちからでんわがありました。) — A friend called me last night.

As a relative time expression, ゆうべ can modify an event directly without に. Use の to modify a noun: 昨夜の雨 (ゆうべのあめ), "last night's rain."

## How the kanji combine

[昨](../kanji/昨.md) contributes "previous," and [夜](../kanji/夜.md) means "night." The reading ゆうべ belongs to the whole spelling rather than being assembled from individual on-readings, so this card uses jukujikun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

昨日の夜 (きのうのよる) makes the same time reference explicitly. 今夜 (こんや) means tonight. 昨夜 also has the separate on-reading さくや.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling alone does not tell a reader whether ゆうべ or さくや is intended. Hiragana ゆうべ makes the pronunciation explicit without changing the last-night meaning. さくや is the less colloquial alternative.
