# 泣く (なく) -- cry; weep

## Meaning

泣く means to cry or shed tears. In this use it is intransitive: the person crying takes が.

- 赤ちゃんが泣いています。 (あかちゃんがないています。) -- The baby is crying.
- うれしくて泣きました。 (うれしくてなきました。) -- I cried with happiness.

This is a godan verb: 泣きます (なきます), 泣かない (なかない), 泣いて (ないて), and 泣いた (ないた). Notice なく becomes ないて in the て-form.

## How the kanji combine

泣 supplies な and carries the crying meaning. The written く completes the native dictionary form. There is no multi-kanji compound to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

涙を流す (なみだをながす), "shed tears," describes the visible action more explicitly. 笑う (わらう), "laugh" or "smile," is a useful emotional contrast, though laughter and tears can occur together.

## Distinctive meaning

泣く does not necessarily imply sadness: relief and happiness can also cause tears. Keep it distinct from 鳴く (なく), used for animal calls such as a bird chirping. The shared pronunciation does not make the spellings interchangeable in ordinary use.
