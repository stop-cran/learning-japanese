# 着せる (きせる) - dress someone; put clothes on

## Meaning

着せる is a transitive ichidan verb: put clothing on someone. In 人に服を着せる (ひとにふくをきせる), に marks the person receiving the clothing, and を marks the clothing. The past is 着せた (きせた); the negative is 着せない (きせない).

- 寒かったので、子供にコートを着せました。
  (さむかったので、こどもにコートをきせました。) - It was cold, so I put a coat on the child.
- 人形に新しい服を着せました。
  (にんぎょうにあたらしいふくをきせました。) - I dressed the doll in new clothes.

## How the kanji combine

[着](../kanji/着.md) contributes wearing clothes, with せる completing the native verb. Learn 着せる as a lexical verb; it is not the modern causative form of 着る, which is 着させる (きさせる).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[着る](着る.md) (きる) describes putting on or wearing clothes oneself. [被せる](被せる.md), read かぶせる in the linked card, describes placing something over something else or putting headwear on someone.

The spelling 被せる is also recognized for **きせる** in this entry. A shared written form does not merge those two verb identities or change either card's primary reading.

## Distinctive meaning

Beyond clothes, 着せる can mean coat or plate a surface, or pin blame on someone. 罪を着せる (つみをきせる) means "pin a crime on someone"; 恩に着せる (おんにきせる) means to make someone feel indebted for a favor. These are not examples of dressing someone in clothes.
