# 子犬 (こいぬ) — puppy

## Meaning

子犬 is a puppy: 子犬が生まれる うまれる (puppies are born), 子犬を飼う かう (keep a puppy), かわいい子犬. It is also written 仔犬.

## How the kanji combine

[子](../kanji/子.md) (child) modifies [犬](../kanji/犬.md) (dog): "child dog". Both use kun readings: こ + いぬ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: 成犬 せいけん (adult dog).
- Related: 子猫 こねこ (kitten), 子供 こども (child).

## Distinctive meaning

子犬 is for a young dog; 子 is a prefix for the young of animals: 子猫, 子牛 こうし, 子羊 こひつじ.
