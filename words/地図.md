# 地図 (ちず) — map

## Meaning

地図 is a map showing places and their spatial relationships.

- 地図を見てください。（ちずを みてください。）— Please look at the map.
- スマートフォンで地図を見ます。（スマートフォンで ちずを みます。）— I look at a map on my smartphone.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) チ means land or ground; [図](../kanji/図.md) ズ means a diagram or drawing. 地 specifies the subject of the 図: a representation of land and places.

Both are on readings. The word is ちず, not じず; 地 has different readings in different compounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 図 ず is broader: it may be a diagram or figure that is not a map.
- [土地](土地.md) とち means land or a plot of land, the place itself rather than its representation.
- There is no natural direct antonym of "map."

## Distinctive meaning

地図 is not limited to paper: a digital map is also a 地図. Conversely, not every drawing is a 地図; a drawing of an object without geographic information is better described more generally as a 図 or picture.
