# 概論 (がいろん) - general outline; introduction

## Meaning

概論 is a general outline or introductory account of a subject. It presents the broad picture rather than treating every detail.

- 日本文学の概論を読みました。 (にほんぶんがくのがいろんをよみました。) - I read a general introduction to Japanese literature.
- この授業では言語学の概論を学びます。 (このじゅぎょうではげんごがくのがいろんをまなびます。) - In this class, we study an introduction to linguistics.

It can occur in book and course titles, such as 経済学概論 (けいざいがくがいろん), "Introduction to Economics."

## How the kanji combine

概 contributes a broad outline and [論](../kanji/論.md) a discussion or account. 概 modifies 論: a discussion in broad outline. The reading is ガイ + ロン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

概要 (がいよう) names an outline or overview; 要約 (ようやく) is a summary. [あらすじ](あらすじ.md) often gives the outline of a story. These meanings overlap, but a course surveying a field is not necessarily a shortened version of one particular text.

[紹介](紹介.md) (しょうかい) also translates as "introduction," but can introduce a person or present a product. 概論 does not mean introducing two people to one another.

## Distinctive meaning

"Introductory" describes the scope of the account, not a guarantee that every sentence is easy for a beginner. 概論 is not simply a synonym for the first paragraph of any text. The dictionary also recognizes 概論する, "discuss in outline"; the examples here use the noun.
