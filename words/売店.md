# 売店 (ばいてん) - kiosk; facility shop

## Meaning

売店 is a shop, kiosk, or sales stand, especially a small shop within a station, hospital, school, or other facility. It can sell snacks, newspapers, souvenirs, or other goods.

- 駅の売店で水を買いました。 (えきのばいてんでみずをかいました。) - I bought water at a station kiosk.
- 病院の売店は一階にあります。 (びょういんのばいてんはいっかいにあります。) - The hospital shop is on the first floor.

Use **売店で + buying action** for shopping there; 売店にあります can locate something in the shop.

## How the kanji combine

[売](../kanji/売.md) contributes selling, and [店](../kanji/店.md) contributes a shop: a place where goods are sold. Both use on readings, ばい + てん; 売 is not read う in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[店](店.md) (みせ), [商店](商店.md) (しょうてん), and [ショップ](ショップ.md) are broader shop words. 売店 particularly suits a sales outlet associated with a facility or venue, rather than naming every kind of independent shop or restaurant.

## Distinctive meaning

A 売店 need not be outdoors or a temporary stall. The word also does not specify that it sells only tickets or only souvenirs. The station or hospital context identifies the outlet; look at the actual shop to know its stock.
