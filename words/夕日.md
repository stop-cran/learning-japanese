# 夕日 (ゆうひ) - evening sun; evening sunlight

## Meaning

夕日 is the evening or setting sun. It can also refer to its light.
夕陽 is another spelling of ゆうひ.

- 海に沈む夕日を見ました。 (うみにしずむゆうひをみました。) - I watched the evening sun sink into the sea.
- 夕日が窓から差し込んでいます。 (ゆうひがまどからさしこんでいます。) - Evening sunlight is streaming through the window.

## How the kanji combine

[夕](../kanji/夕.md), evening, qualifies [日](../kanji/日.md), sun.
The native readings ゆう + ひ give ゆうひ.
The spelling 夕陽 uses 陽 for the sun or sunlight without changing
this whole word's reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[太陽](太陽.md) (たいよう) is the sun without an evening restriction.
[日光](日光.md) (にっこう) and [日差し](日差し.md) (ひざし)
refer to sunlight.
[日の入り](日の入り.md) (ひのいり) names sunset as an event or its time.
朝日 (あさひ), the morning sun, is a useful time-of-day contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

夕日 names the sun or its light, not just the time
[夕方](夕方.md) (ゆうがた).
日の入り and 夕日 overlap in English descriptions of sunset, but
日の入りの時刻 (ひのいりのじこく) asks for the time of sunset.
The dictionary also records 夕日 as せきじつ; do not apply that
reading automatically to the alternative spelling 夕陽.
