# 議員 (ぎいん) - member of an assembly

## Meaning

議員 is a noun for a member of a legislative or deliberative assembly.
It can refer to a national legislator or a member of a local council; context identifies the body.
議員になる means "become an assembly member."

- 彼女は市の議員になりました。 (かのじょはしのぎいんになりました。) - She became a city council member.
- その議員は議会で質問しました。 (そのぎいんはぎかいでしつもんしました。) - That assembly member asked a question in the assembly.

Useful compounds include 国会議員 (こっかいぎいん), "member of the National Diet,"
and 市議会議員 (しぎかいぎいん), "city council member."

## How the kanji combine

[議](../kanji/議.md) contributes deliberation or discussion, while [員](../kanji/員.md) denotes a member.
The first character specifies the kind of membership. The readings are ギ + イン:
retain both vowel beats in ぎいん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[議会](議会.md) (ぎかい) is the assembly itself, not an individual member.
[国会](国会.md) (こっかい) names Japan's national legislature.
An employee of an assembly's office is not necessarily a 議員; workplace and membership are different relationships.
There is no useful lexical opposite to memorize.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not restrict the word to a single country's parliament just because an English gloss says
"member of the Diet." Nor does it mean everyone who works in politics.
The assembly or council relationship is central. A person's party, views, and responsibilities
require further information; none is encoded in 議員 alone.
