# 偶然 (ぐうぜん) — by chance

## Meaning

偶然 describes something happening by chance rather than through an arrangement.
As an adverb it can sit directly before a verb; no に is needed in 偶然会う (ぐうぜんあう), "meet by chance."
It is also a noun meaning a coincidence.

- 駅で友達に偶然会いました。 (えきでともだちにぐうぜんあいました。) — I happened to meet a friend at the station.
- 同じ日に生まれたのは偶然です。 (おなじひにうまれたのはぐうぜんです。) — It is a coincidence that we were born on the same day.

## How the kanji combine

[偶](../kanji/偶.md) contributes chance; [然](../kanji/然.md) contributes the idea of being so or in that state.
Think of the combination as "happening that way by chance," not as the even-number sense of 偶.
The on readings are グウ + ゼン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

たまたま also expresses happening to do something or being somewhere by chance; this sense is usually written in kana.
[突然](突然.md) とつぜん means sudden or abrupt. A chance meeting can also be sudden, but the two words
describe different aspects: lack of arrangement versus abruptness.

## Distinctive meaning

"Accidentally" here need not imply damage or a mistake. Finding a friend by chance may be welcome.
In the station example, 偶然 tells us that the meeting was not arranged; it does not merely say that the friend appeared quickly.
