# 友人 (ゆうじん) - friend

## Meaning

友人 is a noun meaning a friend.
It is more formal than 友達 (ともだち), but can refer to an equally close relationship.
Useful phrases include **学生時代の友人** (がくせいじだいのゆうじん), "a friend from my student days,"
and **友人の紹介** (ゆうじんのしょうかい), "an introduction by a friend."

- 学生時代の友人に会いました。 (がくせいじだいのゆうじんにあいました。) - I met a friend from my student days.
- 友人が引っ越しを手伝ってくれました。 (ゆうじんがひっこしをてつだってくれました。) - A friend helped me move.

Use に for the person met in 友人に会う, and と for shared activity in 友人と旅行する.
The noun alone does not specify the friend's gender.

## How the kanji combine

[友](../kanji/友.md) contributes friendship; [人](../kanji/人.md) identifies a person.
Together they name a person with whom one has a friendship.
The on readings ユウ + ジン give ゆうじん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[友達](友達.md) is the close everyday counterpart.
[親友](親友.md) (しんゆう) explicitly identifies a close friend.
[同僚](同僚.md) (どうりょう) identifies someone through work, whether or not friendship is also involved.

## Distinctive meaning

The difference from 友達 is primarily register, not a compulsory difference in affection.
友人 fits a formal introduction or written account, but is not restricted to writing.
Do not translate it as merely an acquaintance to manufacture a sharp distinction.
Similarly, it does not imply that every friend described this way is one's closest friend.
