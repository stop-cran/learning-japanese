# 袋 (ふくろ) - bag; sack; pouch

## Meaning

袋 is a noun for a bag, sack, or pouch used to contain things. It can be made of paper, cloth, plastic, or another material; the word alone does not specify which.

- この袋にりんごを入れてください。 (このふくろにりんごをいれてください。) - Please put the apples in this bag.
- 買い物の袋を忘れました。 (かいもののふくろをわすれました。) - I forgot my shopping bag.

袋に入れる marks the destination with に; 袋から出す (ふくろからだす), "take out of the bag," uses から for the source.

## How the kanji combine

袋 is one kanji read as the native noun ふくろ. In compounds, its initial sound can change: 紙袋 (かみぶくろ) means "paper bag." Learn each compound's reading rather than assuming the change in every possible combination.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鞄](鞄.md) (かばん) often names a carrying bag such as a handbag, satchel, or briefcase. 袋 emphasizes a containing bag or pouch; the categories can overlap.

[箱](箱.md) (はこ), "box," names a differently shaped container, not an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

At a shop, 袋はいりますか (ふくろはいりますか) asks "Do you need a bag?" English "bag" covers several Japanese nouns, so consider the object rather than memorizing a one-to-one match. This card focuses on containers, not specialized or figurative dictionary senses of 袋.
