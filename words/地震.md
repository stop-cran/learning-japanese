# 地震 (じしん) — earthquake

## Meaning

地震 means an earthquake. It is a noun: 地震がある reports that an earthquake occurs, and 地震が起きる (じしんがおきる) says that one happens.

- 昨日の夜、地震がありました。  
  きのうのよる、じしんがありました。  
  "There was an earthquake last night."
- 地震で窓が揺れました。  
  じしんでまどがゆれました。  
  "The windows shook because of the earthquake."

In the second sentence, で marks the cause.

## How the kanji combine

[地](../kanji/地.md) means ground or earth; 震 relates to shaking. Their on readings combine as じ + しん, giving the meaning association "ground shaking".

## Synonyms and antonyms

揺れ (ゆれ), "shaking", describes motion and need not be caused by an earthquake. 津波 (つなみ), "tsunami", names a different phenomenon. Neither is an exact synonym, and there is no ordinary direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

自信 (じしん), "self-confidence", sounds the same but is a different word. In writing, 地 identifies the earth-related meaning; in conversation, the sentence provides the distinction.
