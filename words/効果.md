# 効果 (こうか) - effect; effectiveness

## Meaning

効果 names an effect or the effectiveness of something. It is a noun: 効果がある
means "have an effect; be effective," and 効果がない means "have no effect."
The thing producing the effect can precede の.

- この薬は痛みに効果があります。 (このくすりはいたみにこうかがあります。) - This medicine is effective against pain.
- 広告の効果を調べています。 (こうこくのこうかをしらべています。) - We are investigating the advertisement's effectiveness.

In the medicine example, に marks the condition against which the medicine works.
効果的な (こうかてきな) is the related adjective "effective."

## How the kanji combine

効 concerns working or having efficacy; [果](../kanji/果.md) contributes a result.
Together they name the result produced by something's action. The readings are
コウ + カ, with a long first vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結果](結果.md) (けっか) is a result or outcome more generally. 効果 emphasizes
what an action, treatment, or other cause brings about, often in relation to an
intended purpose. An outcome can exist even when an attempted treatment had no
効果.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun does not establish how large or beneficial an effect is. Add a description
or context rather than assuming 効果 means complete success. It also appears in
production terms such as 音響効果 (おんきょうこうか), "sound effects": these are
created effects for a scene, not a statement that the sound is medically effective.
