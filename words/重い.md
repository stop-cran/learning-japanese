# 重い (おもい) — heavy

## Meaning

重い means **heavy** in weight. It can also describe something **serious or severe**, such as an illness.

- このかばんは重いです。（このかばんはおもいです。）— This bag is heavy.
- 重い病気で入院しました。（おもいびょうきでにゅういんしました。）— I was hospitalized with a serious illness.

It is an i-adjective: 重いかばん means "a heavy bag," and 重くない（おもくない）means "not heavy."
Do not add な before a noun.

## How the kanji combine

This is not a compound of two kanji. [重](../kanji/重.md) supplies the native stem おも,
and the written い is the adjective ending, or **okurigana**.
The dictionary notation おも.い marks where that kana ending begins.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 軽い（かるい）is the ordinary opposite for weight: "light." It also contrasts with 重い for the severity of an illness.
- 重要な（じゅうような）means "important." Unlike 重い, it does not by itself suggest physical weight or a burden.
- 貴重な（きちょうな）means "precious" or "valuable," not "heavy."

## Distinctive meaning

重い can suggest weight, seriousness, or difficulty to bear, but it is not a general translation of every
English "important." A valued possession need not be 重い.
For a literal weight comparison, 重い／軽い is the useful pair.
