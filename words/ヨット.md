# ヨット - sailboat; yacht

## Meaning

ヨット is a yacht or sailboat, especially a sailing boat with one mast in the inspected dictionary description. It is a noun. **ヨットに乗る** means "ride in; sail on a yacht," and **ヨットで** can identify the means of travel.

- 夏休みにヨットに乗りました。 (なつやすみにヨットにのりました。) - I went sailing during the summer holiday.
- 沖に白いヨットが見えます。 (おきにしろいヨットがみえます。) - I can see a white sailboat offshore.

The first sentence does not say whether the speaker operated the boat or was a passenger. **ヨットを操縦する** (ヨットをそうじゅうする) explicitly describes controlling or handling it.

## How the kanji combine

This boat name is written in katakana, with no kanji decomposition. The small ッ marks a consonant pause before ト: ヨット. It is different from the long-vowel mark used in ゴール.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[船](船.md) (ふね) is the broader word "boat; ship." [ボート](ボート.md) can refer to a boat without specifying sails. These words overlap in reference but do not guarantee the same kind of vessel.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume the English image of an expensive luxury motor yacht. A modest sailing boat can be a ヨット. The dictionary's "especially" note is a usage preference, not an absolute rule that a vessel with more than one mast cannot receive the name.
