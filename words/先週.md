# 先週 (せんしゅう) — last week

## Meaning

先週 is the week before the current one. It can function directly as a time expression, normally without に.

先週、友達に会いました。 (せんしゅう、ともだちにあいました。) — I met a friend last week.

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md) contributes "previous" here, and [週](../kanji/週.md) means "week." The on readings are せん + しゅう. Keep the long う: せんしゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

今週 (こんしゅう) is this week, 来週 (らいしゅう) next week, and 先々週 (せんせんしゅう) the week before last. 一週間前 (いっしゅうかんまえ) means one week ago, measured back from a reference time.

## Distinctive meaning

先週 identifies a calendar period, not a duration of seven days. Use 一週間 for "for one week." Before a noun, connect with の: 先週の授業 (せんしゅうのじゅぎょう), "last week's class." English "last" meaning final instead calls for 最後, not 先週.
