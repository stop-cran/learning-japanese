# こんな - this kind of

## Meaning

こんな means "this kind of," "such," or "like this." It refers to a type,
quality, or situation the speaker is presenting. It normally comes directly
before a noun, without another な or の.

- こんな本を探していました。 (こんなほんをさがしていました。) - I was looking for a book like this.
- こんなに寒いとは思いませんでした。 (こんなにさむいとはおもいませんでした。) - I did not think it would be this cold.

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana demonstrative. Its final な is part of the word, not an invitation
to add another adjective connector.

## Synonyms and antonyms

この identifies a particular noun: この本 (このほん), "this book."
こんな本 means a book of this kind. そんな refers to that kind, あんな to
a more distant kind, and どんな asks "what kind?"

## Distinctive meaning

The related pattern こんなに modifies degree before an adjective or verb.
Do not use こんな alone as the ordinary pronoun "this"; use これ when pointing
to a thing without a following noun.
