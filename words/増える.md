# 増える (ふえる) — increase in amount

## Meaning

増える means to increase in number or amount.
It is intransitive: the increasing thing is the subject, not an object someone adds to.

- この町では人口が増えています。 (このまちではじんこうがふえています。) — The population of this town is increasing.
- 仕事が増えました。 (しごとがふえました。) — The amount of work has increased.

This is an ichidan verb: drop the final る to make 増えます (ふえます) or 増えて (ふえて).
"Ichidan" here names that conjugation pattern, not a separate meaning.

## How the kanji combine

[増](../kanji/増.md) expresses increase. The following える belongs to the native Japanese verb's spelling and inflection.
The metadata reading ふ.える places the dot before those kana.
The on reading ゾウ instead appears in 増加 (ぞうか), "increase."

## Synonyms and antonyms

増やす (ふやす) means to increase something: 仕事を増やす (しごとをふやす), "increase the amount of work."
Compare that with 仕事が増える, "the amount of work increases."
減る (へる), "decrease," is the useful opposite for the direction of change.

## Distinctive meaning

増える reports a larger quantity without specifying who caused it.
It does not mean that an individual object becomes thicker or taller.
JMdict also records 殖える (ふえる), especially for population growth or capital appreciation; this article uses 増える for the general increase meaning.
