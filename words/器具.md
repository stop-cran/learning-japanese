# 器具 (きぐ) - utensil; tool; apparatus

## Meaning

器具 is a utensil, implement, instrument, or apparatus used for a task. It is a noun; the surrounding words tell you whether the setting is cooking, medicine, an experiment, or another activity.

- 調理器具を使った後で洗いました。
  (ちょうりきぐをつかったあとであらいました。) - I washed the cooking utensils after using them.
- 実験に必要な器具をそろえました。
  (じっけんにひつようなきぐをそろえました。) - We assembled the apparatus needed for the experiment.

## How the kanji combine

器 contributes an instrument or utensil, and [具](../kanji/具.md) an implement or equipment. Their related meanings reinforce one another. The on readings are キ + グ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[道具](道具.md) (どうぐ) also means a tool or implement. [装置](装置.md) (そうち) is a device or apparatus. [機械／器械](機械.md) (きかい) overlaps in the apparatus sense; 器械 is particularly associated with instruments. These are useful tendencies, not watertight compartments.

## Distinctive meaning

器具 does not itself mean an electrically powered machine, and it is not restricted to kitchen utensils. Compare 調理器具, "cooking utensils," with 実験器具 (じっけんきぐ), "experimental apparatus." Learn the purpose from the complete expression.
