# グラス (グラス) - drinking glass

## Meaning

グラス is a noun for a drinking glass. **グラスに注ぐ** (グラスにそそぐ) means "pour into a glass"; **グラスで飲む** (グラスでのむ) identifies the vessel used.

- グラスに水を注ぎました。 (グラスにみずをそそぎました。) - I poured water into a glass.
- このグラスでジュースを飲んでください。 (このグラスでジュースをのんでください。) - Please drink the juice from this glass.

The glass-related dictionary entry also includes the material "glass," but the examples above name a container.

## How the kanji combine

Katakana represents the borrowed word, with no kanji decomposition. Two different English words, **glass** and **grass**, have the same Japanese form グラス; the spelling alone does not identify which word is intended.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[コップ](コップ.md) overlaps with the drinking-vessel meaning. [ガラス](ガラス.md) names the glass material and helps distinguish what a container is made from from what it is called.

## Distinctive meaning

There is a **separate lexical identity** グラス meaning **grass**, the plant. In vegetation or plant descriptions, that word belongs with [草](草.md) (くさ), not with drinking vessels. It is not a second sense of the supplied glass entry. This distinction establishes the two meanings, not their relative frequency or separate examination levels. The two drinking examples deliberately use the vessel identity.
