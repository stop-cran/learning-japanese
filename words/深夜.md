# 深夜 (しんや) - late at night

## Meaning

深夜 is late at night or the depths of the night.
It is a time noun, also used adverbially.
深夜に places an event late at night; 深夜まで gives an activity's time limit.

- 深夜まで仕事をしていました。 (しんやまでしごとをしていました。) - I was working until late at night.
- 深夜の電話で目が覚めました。 (しんやのでんわでめがさめました。) - A late-night phone call woke me up.

深夜の + noun gives "late-night," as in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

[深](../kanji/深.md), deep, qualifies [夜](../kanji/夜.md), night.
The on readings シン + ヤ give しんや.
The image is a time deep into the night, not a measurement of physical depth.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[夜中](夜中.md) (よなか) and 真夜中 (まよなか) substantially overlap
in the middle or dead of night.
[夜](夜.md) (よる) covers night more broadly.
The words do not divide all nighttime hours into fixed, nonoverlapping intervals.

## Distinctive meaning

Ordinary 深夜 does not guarantee precisely 00:00.
If a timetable, rule, or other specific context defines a range,
that local definition matters; it is not a universal dictionary boundary.
State the clock time when precision is required.
