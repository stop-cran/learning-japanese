# 隻 (せき) - counter for ships

## Meaning

隻 is a counter used with a numeral to count ships or boats. It is not the ordinary noun meaning a ship.
一隻 (いっせき) counts one ship; 二隻 (にせき) counts two.

- 港に船が一隻あります。 (みなとにふねがいっせきあります。) - There is one ship in the harbour.
- 二隻の船が見えます。 (にせきのふねがみえます。) - I can see two ships.

The first sentence places the numeral-counter expression after 船が. The second uses 二隻の before 船.
In 一隻, learn the complete reading いっせき rather than adding いち and せき without the sound change.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [隻](../kanji/隻.md) writes the counter, using the on reading セキ.
In 一隻, [一](../kanji/一.md) supplies the number one and 隻 supplies the ship counter.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[船](船.md) and [舟](舟.md), both read ふね, are nouns for boats or ships; they name the object rather than count it.
[枚](枚.md) (まい) counts thin, flat objects and is not a replacement ship counter.
Other numbers change the quantity, not the counter's lexical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The source also records counting one half of a pair, such as half of a folding screen.
Its counter for bundle items such as fish, birds or arrows is marked archaic; the ship-counter sense is not.
Do not transfer that archaic flag to every 隻 expression or treat the character as a counter for every object.
