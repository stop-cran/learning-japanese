# 専制 (せんせい) - autocracy; despotism

## Meaning

専制 is autocratic or despotic control: those in power decide matters
arbitrarily according to their own will.
It is a noun and can take する; political compounds include
専制政治 (せんせいせいじ), autocratic government.

- この本は専制政治の歴史を説明しています。 (このほんはせんせいせいじのれきしをせつめいしています。) - This book explains the history of autocratic government.
- 人々は政府の専制に反対しました。 (ひとびとはせいふのせんせいにはんたいしました。) - The people opposed the government's autocratic rule.

Nの専制 identifies whose arbitrary control is meant.
The public dictionary also describes a superior handling matters in this manner,
not exclusively one named form of national government.

## How the kanji combine

[専](../kanji/専.md) contributes exclusive control or acting on one's own,
and [制](../kanji/制.md) contributes control or rule.
The combination concerns control concentrated in the ruler's will.
The on readings are セン + セイ.
擅制 is a rare dictionary spelling, not the ordinary spelling recommended here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

独裁 (どくさい) also concerns dictatorship or autocratic control.
民主主義 (みんしゅしゅぎ), democracy, is a useful political comparison;
the contrast does not establish the exact institutions of any particular country.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse せんせい with [先生](先生.md), "teacher."
Nor does 専 make this a term for an academic specialization:
[専攻](専攻.md) (せんこう) serves that purpose.
専制 characterizes arbitrary rule, not every decision made by someone who holds authority.
