# コーチ (コーチ) - coach; coaching

## Meaning

コーチ is a coach or the activity of coaching. It is a noun and can form the transitive verb コーチする. A person receiving instruction can take に, while the skill taught takes を.

- コーチに泳ぎ方を教わりました。 (コーチにおよぎかたをおそわりました。) - I learned how to swim from the coach.
- 子どもたちにテニスをコーチしています。 (こどもたちにテニスをコーチしています。) - I coach the children in tennis.

In the first example, コーチ identifies the instructor. In the second, コーチしています describes the teaching activity.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana word with no written kanji. The long-vowel mark ー lengthens the vowel after コ. Learn コーチ as a whole rather than treating コ and チ as meaningful components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[先生](先生.md) (せんせい) can refer to a teacher and is also a form of address. [監督](監督.md) (かんとく) can identify a manager or head coach in sport. Actual divisions of responsibility depend on the team.

## Distinctive meaning

The coach may be a person providing targeted instruction rather than the person responsible for the entire organization. English "coach" can also mean a bus, but the sports-instruction use taught here does not supply that meaning. Use the surrounding activity to distinguish the person from the act of coaching.
