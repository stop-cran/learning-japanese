# しぼむ (しぼむ) - wilt; shrivel; deflate

## Meaning

しぼむ is to wilt, shrivel, sag, or deflate: something loses its freshness, fullness, or inflated shape. It is an intransitive godan verb ending in む.

- 花がしぼんでしまいました。
  (はながしぼんでしまいました。) - The flowers have wilted.
- 風船が少ししぼみました。
  (ふうせんがすこししぼみました。) - The balloon deflated a little.

The thing changing takes が. The polite form is しぼみます; the て-form is **しぼんで**, and the past is **しぼんだ**. Do not treat the ending む as an ichidan ending.

Expectations or hopes can also figuratively lose their fullness or momentum: 夢がしぼむ (ゆめがしぼむ), "one's dream withers/fades." The dream does not literally contain air.

## How the kanji combine

This article uses kana, the usual spelling in the dictionary entry. 萎む is also recorded; 凋む is marked a rarely used kanji form. Both spell the same native verb, not separate compounds to translate character by character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[枯れる](枯れる.md) (かれる) concerns a plant withering or dying. The meanings overlap for flowers, but a balloon that loses air is not ordinarily described as 枯れる.

しおれる also means wilt or droop and has an emotional dejection use. 膨らむ (ふくらむ), "swell; become inflated," provides a useful contrast with the loss-of-fullness sense.

## Distinctive meaning

しぼむ describes the change in the flower, balloon, or expectation, not someone directly squeezing an object. [絞る](絞る.md) (しぼる), "wring; squeeze," is a different verb. Their similar sounds do not make them an unrestricted intransitive/transitive pair.

The source's "fade away" is not permission to replace every [消える](消える.md) (きえる), "disappear; go out," with しぼむ. Ask what is losing its fullness or vigor in the particular context.
