# 代わり (かわり) — substitute; replacement

## Meaning

代わり is a replacement or substitute. Noun + の代わりに means "instead of" that thing or "in place of" that person.

- 牛乳の代わりに豆乳を使います。 (ぎゅうにゅうのかわりにとうにゅうをつかいます。) — I use soy milk instead of milk.
- 私の代わりに会議に出てください。 (わたしのかわりにかいぎにでてください。) — Please attend the meeting in my place.

For a replacement item, use 代わりの + noun: 代わりの鍵 (かわりのかぎ), "a replacement key."

## How the kanji combine

[代](../kanji/代.md) contributes substitution. わり completes the native noun かわり. 替わり is another dictionary spelling, but 代わり is useful for the person-or-thing substitution taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

代理 (だいり) refers to acting as a representative or proxy. 交換 (こうかん) describes an exchange. Neither replaces 代わり in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

代わりに can also introduce a return or trade-off: helping someone in return for their help, for example. The relationship is substitution or compensation, not simply chronological succession.
