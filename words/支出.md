# 支出 (ししゅつ) - expenditure; expenses

## Meaning

支出 is money spent or paid out: expenditure or expenses. It can name an individual outlay or a total for a period. 支出する means "expend; disburse," with the money or funds taking を and the purpose often taking に.

- 今月は支出が増えました。 (こんげつはししゅつがふえました。) - My expenses increased this month.
- 会社は研修に百万円を支出しました。 (かいしゃはけんしゅうにひゃくまんえんをししゅつしました。) - The company spent one million yen on training.

支出を減らす (ししゅつをへらす) means "reduce expenditure."

## How the kanji combine

[支](../kanji/支.md) contributes disbursing or paying, as in 支払う. [出](../kanji/出.md) contributes going out. Together they identify money paid out. The on readings are シ + シュツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう), "income," is the incoming side of a budget, contrasted with outgoing 支出. [費用](費用.md) (ひよう) names costs or expenses associated with something. [支払う](支払う.md) (しはらう) is the verb for paying.

## Distinctive meaning

支出 suits discussions of household budgets, company accounts, or public spending. It does not by itself say that an expense was wasteful. Whether a payment is necessary or excessive is a separate judgment; 支出 simply places it on the money-going-out side.
