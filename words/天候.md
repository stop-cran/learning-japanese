# 天候 (てんこう) — weather conditions

## Meaning

天候 is the weather, viewed as the conditions under which something happens.
It is useful when weather affects a journey, an outdoor activity, or a plan.
It is a noun: 天候が悪い means "the weather is bad," and 天候によって means "depending on the weather."

- 天候が悪いので、今日は出かけません。 (てんこうがわるいので、きょうはでかけません。) — The weather is bad, so I will not go out today.
- 天候によって予定を変えます。 (てんこうによってよていをかえます。) — We will change the plan depending on the weather.

## How the kanji combine

[天](../kanji/天.md) contributes "sky," and [候](../kanji/候.md) contributes weather or seasonal conditions.
Together they name atmospheric conditions, not two separate things.
Both readings are on readings: テン + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[天気](天気.md) てんき also means weather and works in questions such as 天気はどうですか
(てんきはどうですか), "How is the weather?"
気候 きこう means climate: a broader pattern, rather than the conditions for today's outing.
Good and bad weather are contrasting kinds of weather, not antonyms of the noun itself.

## Distinctive meaning

天候 by itself does not say sunny, rainy, good, or bad.
Add the condition you mean. In a sentence about changing plans, the word directs attention to the weather
as a circumstance to take into account; it does not turn a short-term forecast into a statement about climate.
