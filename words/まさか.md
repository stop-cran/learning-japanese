# まさか (まさか) - no way; surely not

## Meaning

まさか can stand alone as an exclamation of disbelief: "No way!" or "You don't say!"
As an adverb it expresses that something is very unlikely or contrary to expectation,
often with a negative predicate.

- 「田中さんが優勝したよ。」「まさか！」
  (「たなかさんがゆうしょうしたよ。」「まさか！」)
  - "Tanaka won the championship." "No way!"
- まさかこんな所で会うとは思いませんでした。
  (まさかこんなところであうとはおもいませんでした。)
  - I never expected to meet you in a place like this.

In the second example, 思いませんでした is negative: the speaker had not expected
the encounter, even though it actually happened.

## How the kanji combine

まさか is normally written in kana. There is no kanji decomposition for this spelling.
JMdict also lists the rare ateji 真逆; that listing is not a reason to replace
the practical kana form or assign meanings by translating its characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本当](本当.md) (ほんとう) in 本当ですか, "Really?", can ask for confirmation.
まさか adds stronger disbelief or unexpectedness.
[多分](多分.md) (たぶん), "probably," instead expresses an estimate of likelihood.

## Distinctive meaning

まさか is not a literal guarantee that something cannot happen.
The speaker may be reacting to an event already known to be true.
With の, まさかの時 (まさかのとき) means an unexpected emergency or unforeseen situation,
as in preparing for such an eventuality.
