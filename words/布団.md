# 布団 (ふとん) - futon; Japanese bedding

## Meaning

布団 refers to Japanese bedding, including the mattress laid underneath the sleeper and the quilt or duvet used above. Context may mean the bedding as a whole or a particular component.

- 夜、畳の上に布団を敷きます。 (よる、たたみのうえにふとんをしきます。) - At night, I lay out a futon on the tatami.
- 朝、布団をたたみました。 (あさ、ふとんをたたみました。) - I folded up the bedding in the morning.

Use 敷く (しく) for laying out the mattress and たたむ for folding it.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is 布団. JMdict marks it as ateji; 蒲団 is an older, less usual spelling. Do not treat the modern characters, "cloth" and "group/round mass," as a complete historical explanation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

敷き布団 (しきぶとん) specifies the mattress underneath; 掛け布団 (かけぶとん) specifies the covering above. ベッド is a bed, and 寝具 (しんぐ) is bedding more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

English "futon" sometimes means a convertible sofa. Japanese 布団 does not automatically imply that furniture. The useful core is the bedding; notice the change from ふとん to ぶとん in the component names.
