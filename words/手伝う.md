# 手伝う (てつだう) — to help with a task

## Meaning

手伝う means helping someone with work or a task. It is a transitive godan verb: 手伝います, 手伝わない, 手伝って. The task or person helped can take を.

- 料理を手伝います。 (りょうりをてつだいます。) — I will help with the cooking.
- 忙しいので、私を手伝ってください。 (いそがしいので、わたしをてつだってください。) — I am busy, so please help me.

## How the kanji combine

Learn [手](../kanji/手.md)[伝](../kanji/伝.md)う as a whole word read てつだう, rather than treating つだ as a general reading of 伝. The ending う conjugates. 手 means "hand"; "lend a hand" is a useful English memory aid, not a literal character-by-character derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

助ける (たすける) is broader help or rescue, including saving someone from danger. 手伝う emphasizes participating in what someone is doing. 邪魔する (じゃまする), "get in the way," is a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

手伝いましょうか means "Shall I help?" Helping with a task does not necessarily mean taking it over completely. Use the actual task when useful: 引っ越しを手伝う (ひっこしをてつだう), "help with a move."
