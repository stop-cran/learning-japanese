# 良い (よい) — good

## Meaning

良い describes something as good, nice, or favorable. This article uses the reading よい;
learn いい alongside it. Both can give a positive evaluation, but their inflected forms do
not follow two separate regular patterns.

- これは良い方法です。 (これはよいほうほうです。) — This is a good method.
- 昨日は天気がよかったです。
  (きのうはてんきがよかったです。)
  — The weather was good yesterday.

## How the kanji combine

[良](../kanji/良.md) represents the positive-quality meaning; い is the adjective ending.
This is a native i-adjective. It attaches directly to a noun, as in 良い方法, without な.
For the basic negative and past, use よくない and よかった, including when your starting
form is いい. Do not make these forms as いくない or いかった.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[悪い](悪い.md) (わるい), "bad," is the useful opposite for this general evaluation.
[正しい](正しい.md) (ただしい), "correct; right," overlaps only in some contexts:
a good idea and a correct answer are not the same kind of judgment.

## Distinctive meaning

良い does not itself tell you *why* something is good. Context may supply quality, suitability,
or pleasantness. The central learner trap is grammatical: remembering いい must not make
you forget the よ- forms used for the past, negative, and adverbial よく.
