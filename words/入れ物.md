# 入れ物 (いれもの) - container; receptacle

## Meaning

入れ物 is a container or receptacle: something used to put other things
in. It does not by itself specify a material, shape, or lid.

- この入れ物に砂糖を入れます。 (このいれものにさとうをいれます。) - I put sugar in this container.
- ふたのある入れ物を探しています。 (ふたのあるいれものをさがしています。) - I am looking for a container with a lid.

In the first example, に marks the container and を marks its contents.

## How the kanji combine

The noun combines the stem of [入れる](入れる.md) (いれる), "put in,"
with [物](../kanji/物.md) (もの), "thing."
[入](../kanji/入.md) uses its native reading. Together the parts suggest
a thing for putting something in. 容れ物 is another dictionary spelling,
using [容](../kanji/容.md); 入物 and 容物 have irregular okurigana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[箱](箱.md) (はこ), "box," and [ケース](ケース.md), "case," can be
particular kinds of container. [袋](袋.md) (ふくろ), "bag," is another
form. Their shapes differ; 入れ物 is the broader functional description.

## Distinctive meaning

An empty container remains an 入れ物: it need not currently hold
anything. The dictionary separately records a euphemistic coffin use;
ordinary kitchen examples do not imply that sense.
Do not transfer every abstract English meaning of "case" to 入れ物.
