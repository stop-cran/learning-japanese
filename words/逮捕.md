# 逮捕 (たいほ) - arrest

## Meaning

逮捕 is an arrest or apprehension. 逮捕する is a transitive suru verb: **person を逮捕する**, "arrest someone."

- 警察は容疑者を逮捕しました。 (けいさつはようぎしゃをたいほしました。) - The police arrested the suspect.
- 容疑者が逮捕されたとニュースで聞きました。 (ようぎしゃがたいほされたとニュースでききました。) - I heard on the news that the suspect had been arrested.

The second example uses 逮捕される, the passive "be arrested." If the arresting party is named in a passive sentence, it can take に: 警察に逮捕される.

## How the kanji combine

逮 contributes apprehending or catching up with someone, and [捕](../kanji/捕.md) contributes catching or seizing. The two elements reinforce the idea of apprehension. The on readings are タイ + ホ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[捕まえる](捕まえる.md) (つかまえる) is broader "catch; capture" and can apply outside police work. 釈放 (しゃくほう), "release from custody," describes movement in the opposite direction within a legal process.

## Distinctive meaning

逮捕 names the act of taking someone into custody. It does not by itself establish guilt or mean that a court has convicted the person. In ordinary news reading, keep "suspect," "arrest," and "conviction" conceptually separate rather than treating them as equivalent stages.
