# 夜間 (やかん) - nighttime; at night

## Meaning

夜間 identifies the nighttime period and can also be used adverbially.
It is useful in descriptions of operating hours, work, travel, or services:
夜間の診療 (やかんのしんりょう), "nighttime medical care."

- この病院は夜間も診療しています。 (このびょういんはやかんもしんりょうしています。) - This hospital also provides medical care at night.
- 夜間は入口を閉めます。 (やかんはいりぐちをしめます。) - We close the entrance at night.

夜間の + noun gives "nighttime" or "night" as a modifier.
は can mark nighttime as the period being discussed.

## How the kanji combine

[夜](../kanji/夜.md), night, modifies [間](../kanji/間.md),
a span or interval: the span of the night.
The on readings are ヤ + カン, giving やかん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[夜](夜.md) (よる) is the broad everyday night word.
[深夜](深夜.md) (しんや) and [夜中](夜中.md) (よなか)
emphasize the late or middle part of the night.
昼間 (ひるま), daytime, provides the contrasting period.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary describes the period from sunset to the following sunrise,
not a universally fixed set of clock hours.
A particular service or regulation may define its own nighttime hours.
The unrelated homophone [薬缶](薬缶.md) (やかん) means a kettle;
the shared sound does not establish a shared meaning.
