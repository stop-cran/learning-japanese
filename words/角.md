# 角 (かど) — corner

## Meaning

角 (かど) is a corner or edge, including a street corner where you turn.

次の角を右に曲がってください。 (つぎのかどをみぎにまがってください。) — Please turn right at the next corner.

机の角にひざをぶつけました。 (つくえのかどにひざをぶつけました。) — I bumped my knee against the corner of the desk.

There is also a distinct word すみ, "corner; nook; recess," which can be written
角 as well as [隅](隅.md). For this meaning, 隅 makes the intended reading clear:
部屋の隅にいすがあります。 (へやのすみにいすがあります。) — There is a chair
in the corner of the room. This is not a reason to read the street-corner examples
above as すみ.

## How the kanji combine

角 is a single kanji. This card teaches the native word かど. Other readings of the same character belong to other uses: つの is a horn, and カク appears in words about angles.

## Synonyms and antonyms

隅 (すみ), also writable as 角, is a corner or recess within an area, such as the corner of a room. 端 (はし) is an edge or end. These overlap in English but are not interchangeable everywhere.

## Distinctive meaning

Think of かど as an edge where surfaces or routes meet, often a projecting corner. In directions, を in 角を曲がる marks the point passed or turned at, not an object physically bent.

Source: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html), entry 1206110, senses 1 and 2.
The separate すみ identity is entry 1246440, sense 1. Its corner meaning is not
"horn," which belongs to the different reading つの.
