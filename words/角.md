# 角 (かど) — corner

## Meaning

角 (かど) is a corner or edge, including a street corner where you turn.

次の角を右に曲がってください。 (つぎのかどをみぎにまがってください。) — Please turn right at the next corner.

机の角にひざをぶつけました。 (つくえのかどにひざをぶつけました。) — I bumped my knee against the corner of the desk.

## How the kanji combine

角 is a single kanji. This card teaches the native word かど. Other readings of the same character belong to other uses: つの is a horn, and カク appears in words about angles.

## Synonyms and antonyms

隅 (すみ) is a corner or recess within an area, such as the corner of a room. 端 (はし) is an edge or end. These overlap in English but are not interchangeable everywhere.

## Distinctive meaning

Think of かど as an edge where surfaces or routes meet, often a projecting corner. In directions, を in 角を曲がる marks the point passed or turned at, not an object physically bent.
