# 芝生 (しばふ) - lawn; turf

## Meaning

芝生 is a noun for a lawn or turf: grass growing as a covering over an area. It can name the grass surface on which people sit or walk, rather than a single plant.

- 芝生の上に座って、お弁当を食べました。 (しばふのうえにすわって、おべんとうをたべました。) - We sat on the lawn and ate our lunches.
- 芝生に入らないでください。 (しばふにはいらないでください。) - Please keep off the grass.

上に specifies the surface in the first example. In the second, に marks the area one must not enter. Seeing a lawn does not itself imply permission to walk on it.

## How the kanji combine

芝 refers to turf grass; [生](../kanji/生.md) connects with growth. The established native word is しばふ, with 生 read ふ here. Learn that reading with the whole word rather than substituting a familiar セイ or なま reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[草](草.md) (くさ) is broader grass or herbaceous vegetation, including growth that is not a lawn. [庭](庭.md) (にわ) is a garden or yard; a garden may contain 芝生, paving, trees, or other surfaces.

## Distinctive meaning

English "grass" can refer to individual plants, vegetation in general, or a lawn. 芝生 narrows the focus to turf or a grass-covered area. Useful combinations include 芝生の手入れ (しばふのていれ), "lawn maintenance," rather than treating 芝生 as a count of separate blades.
