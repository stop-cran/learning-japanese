# 姉 (あね) — older sister

## Meaning

姉 is an older sister. It describes her relationship to a younger sibling, not a particular age group.

- 姉は東京に住んでいます。 (あねはとうきょうにすんでいます。) — My older sister lives in Tokyo.
- お姉さんはお元気ですか。 (おねえさんはおげんきですか。) — Is your older sister well?

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun. [姉](../kanji/姉.md) represents the whole word あね, without okurigana.

The polite expression お姉さん is read おねえさん, not おあねさん. Learn it as a complete family expression. 姉妹 しまい, "sisters," uses the on-reading シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- お姉さん おねえさん can refer politely to someone else's older sister or address an older sister within a family.
- [妹](妹.md) いもうと means younger sister.
- [兄](兄.md) あに means older brother. These family relationships contrast with 姉, but there is no general antonym of "sister."

## Distinctive meaning

When speaking to someone outside your family, あね is the ordinary reference to your own older sister. It is not normally the address form you would use to call her. お姉さん also has some non-family address uses, so context still matters.
