# 物質 (ぶっしつ) - substance; matter

## Meaning

物質 is a material substance, or matter in a scientific discussion. It is a noun and can refer to a particular substance or the physical material making things up.

- この物質は水に溶けます。 (このぶっしつはみずにとけます。) - This substance dissolves in water.
- 研究者は物質の性質を調べています。 (けんきゅうしゃはぶっしつのせいしつをしらべています。) - The researchers are studying the properties of matter.

Use **物質の + noun** to discuss its properties, amount, or behavior. A substance need not be a solid object that can be picked up.

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md) contributes thing or material, and [質](../kanji/質.md) contributes substance or nature. Together they concern physical substance. The on-reading combination has a small っ: ブツ + シツ becomes ぶっしつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[材料](材料.md) (ざいりょう) means material or ingredients used to make something. 物質 identifies the substance itself, without requiring a manufacturing purpose. 精神 (せいしん), "mind; spirit," can contrast with matter in philosophical contexts, not in every scientific sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

English "matter" also means a topic or problem; that is not automatically 物質. Likewise, a document called "reference material" is not being classified as a chemical substance. Use the physical-substance meaning as the anchor when deciding whether this word fits.
