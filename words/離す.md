# 離す (はなす) - separate; keep apart

## Meaning

離す is a transitive godan verb meaning to separate things, move something away, or keep things apart.
Use **thing + を離す** for what is moved, and **reference point + から** for what it is moved away from.

- 机を壁から少し離してください。 (つくえをかべからすこしはなしてください。) - Please move the desk a little away from the wall.
- 二つの椅子を離して置きました。 (ふたつのいすをはなしておきました。) - I placed the two chairs apart.

Conjugations include 離します, 離さない, 離した, and 離して.
In 離して置く, the separation describes the arrangement, not necessarily a large distance.

## How the kanji combine

離 contributes separation or distance, and す completes the native verb.
The character connects it visibly with 離れる, but the different ending changes the construction.
Learn はなす as a whole, including its godan さない and して forms.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[離れる](離れる.md) (はなれる) is intransitive: something becomes or is distant.
[放す](放す.md), also はなす, emphasizes letting go or releasing.
近づける (ちかづける), "bring closer," provides a spatial opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

離す focuses on separation: moving a desk away from a wall does not mean releasing it into freedom.
Some contexts allow overlap with 放す, but the spellings highlight different meanings and are not unrestricted substitutes.
Also distinguish [話す](話す.md), "speak," which has the same reading.
Use the written form, particles, and the object together to identify the intended verb.
