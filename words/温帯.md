# 温帯 (おんたい) - temperate zone

## Meaning

温帯 is the temperate zone, a broad climatic region contrasted with tropical and cold regions. It is a noun: 温帯の気候 (おんたいのきこう) means "a temperate-zone climate."

- 温帯の気候について勉強しています。 (おんたいのきこうについてべんきょうしています。) - I am studying temperate-zone climates.
- この植物は温帯に広く分布しています。 (このしょくぶつはおんたいにひろくぶんぷしています。) - This plant is widely distributed in the temperate zone.

## How the kanji combine

[温](../kanji/温.md) contributes mildness or warmth; [帯](../kanji/帯.md) means a belt or zone here, not a clothing sash. The modifier and head use the on readings オン + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[熱帯](熱帯.md) (ねったい), "tropical zone," and 寒帯 (かんたい), "cold zone," provide the geographical contrasts. They are climatic categories, not descriptions of today's weather.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer that a 温帯 location is warm every day or that every place in it has the same seasons. The word describes a broad climate zone; altitude, location, and season still matter.
