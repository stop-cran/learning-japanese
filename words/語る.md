# 語る (かたる) - narrate; speak of

## Meaning

語る is a transitive godan verb for telling or speaking about something, particularly an experience, story, or thought. Use **topic を語る** or **topic について語る**; に can identify the listener.

- 祖父は子供たちに昔の話を語りました。 (そふはこどもたちにむかしのはなしをかたりました。) - My grandfather told the children stories about the past.
- この写真は当時の生活を語っています。 (このしゃしんはとうじのせいかつをかたっています。) - This photograph tells us about life at that time.

The second example is figurative: evidence can "tell" or reveal something without speaking. The past is 語った (かたった), and the negative is 語らない (かたらない).

## How the kanji combine

[語](../kanji/語.md) supplies the idea of telling and the native stem かた-. Its on reading ゴ appears instead in words such as [日本語](日本語.md) (にほんご).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話す](話す.md) (はなす) broadly means speaking or talking. 語る draws attention to the content being recounted. [言う](言う.md) (いう) can introduce a brief utterance without suggesting a narrative.

## Distinctive meaning

語る also has a recitation or chanting sense in narrative performance, such as 平家物語を語る (へいけものがたりをかたる), "recite the Tale of the Heike." That does not make it a general replacement for [読む](読む.md), "read." Choose it for recounting, expressive telling, or what evidence reveals.
