# すなわち (すなわち) - namely; that is

## Meaning

すなわち is a conjunction introducing a restatement or identification: "namely; that is to say; i.e." The following words explain more precisely what the preceding expression means.

- 期限は一週間、すなわち七日間です。 (きげんはいっしゅうかん、すなわちなのかかんです。) - The deadline is one week, that is, seven days.
- 参加は任意です。すなわち、参加してもしなくてもかまいません。 (さんかはにんいです。すなわち、さんかしてもしなくてもかまいません。) - Participation is optional. That is, you may choose whether to participate.

No particle is needed simply to connect すなわち with the restatement. Each clause still follows its own ordinary grammar.

## How the kanji combine

The assigned form is kana-only, so there are no written kanji to decompose. The dictionary also records 即ち and marks kana spelling as usual. Learn すなわち as a complete connector, not as a borrowed English abbreviation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[つまり](つまり.md) can also introduce a restatement or conclusion. [例えば](例えば.md) (たとえば), "for example," has a different job: an example need not exhaust or define the preceding category. There is no useful simple antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the restatement sense when the second part actually identifies or explains the first. "Fruit, for example apples" does not mean "fruit, namely apples" in every context. すなわち makes a tighter explanatory connection than merely adding a new item to a list.
