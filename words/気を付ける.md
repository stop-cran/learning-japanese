# 気を付ける (きをつける) - be careful; pay attention; take care

## Meaning

気を付ける means be careful or pay attention. The expression includes を: the thing to watch out for is marked with に, as in 車に気を付ける (くるまにきをつける), "watch out for cars."

- 道を渡るときは、車に気を付けてください。 (みちをわたるときは、くるまにきをつけてください。) - Please watch out for cars when crossing the road.
- 気を付けて帰ってね。 (きをつけてかえってね。) - Take care on your way home.

付ける conjugates as ichidan: 気を付けます (きをつけます), 気を付けて (きをつけて), 気を付けない (きをつけない). 気をつける is another spelling of the same expression.

## How the kanji combine

[気](../kanji/気.md), attention or mind here, is connected by を to [付](../kanji/付.md) in 付ける, "attach; direct toward." This suggests directing one's attention, not a literal object being glued on.

キ is an on reading, while つける is native. The app groups this on-first mixed expression under kango, as it does [気付く](気付く.md); it is not an all-on two-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[注意](注意.md) (ちゅうい) and [用心](用心.md) (ようじん) overlap in paying attention or taking care against trouble. 気付く (きづく), "notice; realize," instead concerns becoming aware of something.

## Distinctive meaning

気を付けて is also a parting "Take care." It does not by itself mean provide childcare or look after another person's needs; compare [世話](世話.md) (せわ). Keep the internal を even when another noun takes に: 車を気に付ける is not the expression taught here.
