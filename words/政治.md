# 政治 (せいじ) — politics

## Meaning

政治 is politics or the activity of governing: how public decisions are made and how a society is governed.
It is a noun. 政治について means "about politics," while 政治に関心がある
(せいじにかんしんがある) means "have an interest in politics."

- 政治に興味があります。 (せいじにきょうみがあります。) — I am interested in politics.
- 私たちは政治について話しました。 (わたしたちはせいじについてはなしました。) — We talked about politics.

## How the kanji combine

[政](../kanji/政.md) contributes public affairs or government, and [治](../kanji/治.md)
contributes governing or administering. The two reinforce the idea of governance.
This compound uses the on readings セイ + ジ; 治 does not mean medical treatment here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

政府 (せいふ), "government; administration," is related but names the governing body rather than
the whole field of politics. A discussion of 政治 can include parties, elections and public decisions,
not just the current 政府. There is no useful single opposite to "politics."

## Distinctive meaning

Use 政治 when the subject is political affairs, not merely any act of managing something.
The noun also works with の: 政治の話 (せいじのはなし), "a conversation about politics."
Neither the word nor its kanji specify approval or disapproval of those politics.
