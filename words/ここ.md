# ここ (ここ) — here; this place

## Meaning

ここ refers to a place near the speaker, or the current place under discussion. It behaves like a place noun and takes particles.

- ここに座ってください。 (ここにすわってください。) — Please sit here.
- ここで写真を撮ってもいいですか。 (ここでしゃしんをとってもいいですか。) — May I take photos here?

## How the kanji combine

ここ is normally written in hiragana. Dictionary spellings such as 此処 are rare and are not necessary for ordinary beginner writing. There are no kanji components in this card's spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そこ is "there" near the listener or at a place already mentioned; あそこ is "over there," away from both speaker and listener. どこ asks "where?" These belong to the same location-word series.

## Distinctive meaning

The particle depends on the sentence: ここにいます means "I am here," while ここで食べます (ここでたべます) means "I eat here." ここ is not この: この must precede a noun, as in この場所 (このばしょ), "this place." The word can also indicate a point in a text or discussion.
