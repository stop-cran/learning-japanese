# ねばねば — viscous and stringy

## Meaning

Sticky, gluey viscosity, often capable of forming threads, as with natto or cut okra. It can also be a noun for the sticky substance or quality.

- 納豆（なっとう）を混ぜる（まぜる）と、ねばねばしてくる。 — Natto becomes sticky and stringy when you stir it.
- オクラのねばねばが好きだ。 — I like the sticky texture of okra.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[べたべた](べたべた.md) emphasizes a sticky surface or coating. [ぬるぬる](ぬるぬる.md) foregrounds slippery slime, and [どろどろ](どろどろ.md) a thick, fluid consistency. These properties can overlap in one food.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ねばねばする and ねばねばした食べ物. As a noun, ねばねばを洗い流す means to wash away the sticky material. The word is not necessarily negative: valued food textures can be ねばねば. Stringiness is a useful prototype, not a requirement that every sticky substance visibly stretch into threads.
