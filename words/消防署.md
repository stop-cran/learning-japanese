# 消防署 (しょうぼうしょ) - fire station

## Meaning

消防署 is a fire station: the local public-service institution or station concerned with firefighting and related emergency work. It can identify the organization as well as its building.

- 消防署の前に消防車が止まっています。 (しょうぼうしょのまえにしょうぼうしゃがとまっています。) - A fire engine is parked in front of the fire station.
- 学校の授業で、近くの消防署を見学しました。 (がっこうのじゅぎょうで、ちかくのしょうぼうしょをけんがくしました。) - We visited a nearby fire station as part of a school lesson.

消防車 (しょうぼうしゃ) is the vehicle in the first sentence, not another reading of 消防署.

## How the kanji combine

[消](../kanji/消.md) contributes extinguishing, [防](../kanji/防.md) preventing or defending, and [署](../kanji/署.md) a public office or station. 消防 specifies the service and 署 the institution. Read ショウ + ボウ + ショ; the final しょ is short.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[消防](消防.md) (しょうぼう) names firefighting and can abbreviate the fire service or its personnel. 消防署 is more specific about the institution or station. 消防士 (しょうぼうし) is a firefighter. [交番](交番.md) (こうばん) is a neighborhood police box, a different facility.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the endings separate: 署 (しょ) for the station, 車 (しゃ) for the fire engine, and 士 (し) for a firefighter. This is not a reason to lengthen the last syllable of しょうぼうしょ or to use the station noun for a vehicle.
