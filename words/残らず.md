# 残らず (のこらず) - all; without exception

## Meaning

残らず is an adverb meaning "all," "completely," or "without leaving any out." It emphasizes that none of the relevant things or people is left over or omitted.

- お皿の料理を残らず食べました。 (おさらのりょうりをのこらずたべました。) - I ate all the food on the plate, leaving none.
- 参加者は残らず会場に入りました。 (さんかしゃはのこらずかいじょうにはいりました。) - Every participant entered the venue, without exception.

The context supplies the set: the food on that plate or the participants in that event, not everything in existence.

## How the kanji combine

[残](../kanji/残.md) contributes remaining or being left over. The expression is built from [残る](残る.md) (のこる), "remain," with the negative form ず: literally, leaving none remaining. Learn 残らず as a fixed adverb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ) and [全て](全て.md) (すべて) also express "all." 残らず makes the absence of an exception or remainder particularly explicit. [残す](残す.md) (のこす), "leave behind," provides a useful contrast: 料理を残す means leaving some food uneaten.

## Distinctive meaning

残らず modifies the action or situation; it is not a na-adjective. Compare 料理を残らず食べる, "eat all the food," with 料理が残る, "some food remains." The negative-looking ず does not make the whole sentence negative: the first example positively says that everything was eaten.
