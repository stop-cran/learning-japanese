# 弱い (よわい)

## Meaning

弱い means weak: lacking strength, intensity, resistance, or ability in some respect.

- 今日は風が弱いです。 (きょうは かぜが よわいです。) — The wind is light today.
- 私は暑さに弱いです。 (わたしは あつさに よわいです。) — I do not cope well with heat.

It is an i-adjective: 弱い光 (よわいひかり), "a weak light"; negative 弱くない (よわくない). に can identify something a person has little resistance to.

## How the kanji combine

弱 supplies よわ and the weakness meaning; い completes the adjective. Its on reading じゃく appears in compounds, not in this word's ordinary pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

強い (つよい), "strong," is the usual contrast. [下手](下手.md), "unskillful," specifically concerns ability at an activity, rather than physical force or tolerance.

## Distinctive meaning

弱い is broader than physical frailty. 風が弱い describes low intensity, while 暑さに弱い describes sensitivity. The complement and situation determine the dimension of weakness; the adjective alone is not a medical diagnosis.
