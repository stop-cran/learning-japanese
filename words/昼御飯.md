# 昼御飯 (ひるごはん) -- lunch

## Meaning

昼御飯 means lunch, the midday meal. The same word is also written 昼ご飯 or 昼ごはん; those spellings are useful for everyday writing.

今日は友達と昼ごはんを食べます。 (きょうはともだちとひるごはんをたべます。) -- Today I will have lunch with a friend.

Use 昼ごはんを食べる for eating lunch. 昼ごはんに何を食べますか (ひるごはんになにをたべますか) asks what someone will eat for lunch.

## How the kanji combine

[昼](../kanji/昼.md), read ひる, sets the meal in the daytime. [御](../kanji/御.md), read ご, and [飯](../kanji/飯.md), read はん, form ご飯, a meal or cooked rice.
The formation is mixed native-first ひる + Sino-Japanese ご飯, grouped as `wago` here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

昼食 (ちゅうしょく) and ランチ also name lunch. [朝ごはん](朝ごはん.md) is breakfast, a different meal rather than an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The meal need not include rice. A sandwich can be someone's 昼ごはん. The 御 spelling does not make this a different meal from 昼ごはん or require a ceremonially formal setting.
