# なる (なる) - to become

## Meaning

なる describes becoming something or entering a new state. Use noun + になる, na-adjective + になる, or an i-adjective's く-form + なる.

- 姉は先生になりました。 (あねはせんせいになりました。) - My older sister became a teacher.
- 部屋が静かになりました。 (へやがしずかになりました。) - The room became quiet.
- 暖かくなりましたね。 (あたたかくなりましたね。) - It has gotten warmer, hasn't it?

It is an intransitive godan verb: なります, ならない, なって. The thing changing is the subject; do not mark it as an object with を.

## How the kanji combine

成る is a possible spelling, but kana is usual for the everyday becoming sense. The word is not the same verb as 鳴る (なる), "sound; ring."

## Synonyms and antonyms

変わる (かわる) means change without necessarily naming the resulting state. する can describe deliberately making something a certain way: 部屋を静かにする (へやをしずかにする), "make the room quiet."

## Distinctive meaning

なる presents the change, not who caused it. Whether the change was planned is a matter of context. Its に marks a resulting role or state, rather than necessarily indicating movement to a physical destination.
