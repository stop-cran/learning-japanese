# なる (なる) - to become

## Meaning

なる describes becoming something or entering a new state. Use noun + になる, na-adjective + になる, or an i-adjective's く-form + なる.

- 姉は先生になりました。 (あねはせんせいになりました。) - My older sister became a teacher.
- 部屋が静かになりました。 (へやがしずかになりました。) - The room became quiet.
- 暖かくなりましたね。 (あたたかくなりましたね。) - It has gotten warmer, hasn't it?

It is an intransitive godan verb: なります, ならない, なって. The thing changing is the subject; do not mark it as an object with を.

Reaching a point and serving a useful role also fit this verb:

- 来月、十歳になります。 (らいげつ、じゅっさいになります。) - I will turn ten next month.
- この資料は参考になります。 (このしりょうはさんこうになります。) - This material serves as a useful reference.

The second example uses 参考になる, not a general replacement of "be useful" by bare なる.

## How the kanji combine

成る is a possible spelling, but kana is usual for the everyday becoming sense. The word is not the same verb as [鳴る](鳴る.md) (なる), "sound; ring."

The source also writes 為る (なる), which JMdict marks as a rare kanji spelling of this same
identity. 成る and 為る do not create two different verbs here; both source rows are taught
through kana なる. 為る can also spell the separate verb する, "do"; that reading
does not belong to the becoming expressions taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[変わる](変わる.md) (かわる) means change without necessarily naming the resulting state. [する](する.md) can describe deliberately making something a certain way: 部屋を静かにする (へやをしずかにする), "make the room quiet."

## Distinctive meaning

なる presents the change, not who caused it. Whether the change was planned is a matter of context. Its に marks a resulting role or state, rather than necessarily indicating movement to a physical destination.
