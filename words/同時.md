# 同時 (どうじ) - same time; simultaneous

## Meaning

同時 means the same time. The noun becomes the adverbial expression **同時に**, "at the same time; simultaneously." **X と同時に** means "at the same time as X."

- 二人は同時に話し始めました。 (ふたりはどうじにはなしはじめました。) - The two people began speaking at the same time.
- ベルが鳴ると同時に、子供たちが走り出しました。 (ベルがなるとどうじに、こどもたちがはしりだしました。) - Just as the bell rang, the children started running.

The second sentence attaches a dictionary-form clause to と同時に. Before another noun, 同時 also forms compounds such as 同時通訳 (どうじつうやく), "simultaneous interpreting."

## How the kanji combine

[同](../kanji/同.md), "same," modifies [時](../kanji/時.md), "time." The on readings are ドウ + ジ. The relationship between the components directly supports the meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一度に](一度に.md) (いちどに), "all at once," emphasizes grouping things into one occasion. [一緒](一緒.md) (いっしょ) can mean together, including participation or company, without specifying identical timing.

## Distinctive meaning

Two people may start at the same time while being in different places. 同時 therefore does not imply physical togetherness. Conversely, people working together might take turns rather than act simultaneously. For the adverbial meaning, remember に instead of assuming the bare noun functions like English "simultaneously."
