# 弊害 (へいがい) — harmful effect; adverse effect

## Meaning

弊害 is a bad effect or harmful influence that a practice, system or habit has on other things.

- スマートフォンの使いすぎには、睡眠不足などの弊害がある。 (すまーとふぉんのつかいすぎには、すいみんぶそくなどのへいがいがある。) — Overusing smartphones has harmful effects such as lack of sleep.
- 古い規則が、若い社員の働き方に弊害を及ぼしている。 (ふるいきそくが、わかいしゃいんのはたらきかたにへいがいをおよぼしている。) — The old rules are having a harmful effect on how young employees work.

## How the kanji combine

[弊](../kanji/弊.md) (へい) here expresses something harmful, and [害](../kanji/害.md) (がい) means harm. Together they name the damage that a practice or condition causes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[被害](被害.md) (ひがい) is damage that someone suffers, as from a storm or a crime. [損害](損害.md) (そんがい) is the damage or loss caused. 弊害 names the bad effect of a practice, not the victim’s loss.

## Distinctive meaning

The same 弊 appears in 弊社 (へいしゃ), a humble business word for “our company.” There 弊 lowers the speaker’s own side; it does not say that the company is harmful.
