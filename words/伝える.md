# 伝える (つたえる) — convey; tell

## Meaning

伝える means to convey or pass something on, such as information or a message.
It is transitive: the content takes を, and the recipient can take に.
With quoted words, the quotation takes と instead.

- 予定を母に伝えました。 (よていをははにつたえました。) — I told my mother the plans.
- 先生に「少し遅れます」と伝えてください。 (せんせいに「すこしおくれます」とつたえてください。) — Please tell the teacher, "I will be a little late."

The polite form is 伝えます (つたえます), and the て-form is 伝えて (つたえて).

## How the kanji combine

[伝](../kanji/伝.md) carries the passing-on meaning, with the native reading つた and the ending える.
This is one verb rather than a compound assembled from separate kanji meanings.
Compare the on reading デン in 伝言 (でんごん), "a verbal message."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[言う](言う.md) (いう) focuses on saying or uttering something.
伝える brings the communication to someone into view; the teacher example specifically asks for a message to be passed on.
伝わる (つたわる) is the intransitive counterpart: information spreads or a feeling comes across.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 気持ちを伝える (きもちをつたえる), "convey one's feelings," with 気持ちが伝わる
(きもちがつたわる), "one's feelings come across."
The を/が difference tracks whether the sentence describes someone conveying the message or the message reaching the other side.
