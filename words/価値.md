# 価値 (かち) - value; worth

## Meaning

価値 is a noun meaning value, worth, or merit. It can concern money, usefulness, cultural importance,
or personal significance. Use 価値がある, "have value," or a plain dictionary-form verb + 価値がある, "be worth doing."

- この資料には歴史的な価値があります。 (このしりょうにはれきしてきなかちがあります。) - These documents have historical value.
- この映画は見る価値があります。 (このえいがはみるかちがあります。) - This film is worth seeing.

In 見る価値, the verb directly modifies the noun: no の is inserted between 見る and 価値.

## How the kanji combine

価 contributes price or worth, and [値](../kanji/値.md) contributes value.
The related meanings reinforce one another in this on-reading compound: カ + チ.
The whole noun is broader than a price printed on a label.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[価格](価格.md) (かかく) and [値段](値段.md) (ねだん) concern price.
An inexpensive object can have great personal 価値.
[意味](意味.md) (いみ), "meaning; significance," overlaps in some evaluations but is not a substitute in every monetary context.
価値がない means "have no value."

## Distinctive meaning

When using 価値が高い (かちがたかい), "have high value," make clear what kind of value you mean.
高い does not force a monetary interpretation here.
Similarly, the negative 見る価値がない judges something not worth watching; it is stronger than saying that you personally have not watched it.
