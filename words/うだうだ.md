# うだうだ — idling; going on at length

## Meaning

うだうだ can describe idling without making progress or talking on and on about something.

- 休日は家でうだうだ過ごしてしまった。
  — I ended up idling at home on my day off.
- 同じ不満をうだうだ言わずに、解決策を考えよう。
  — Instead of going on about the same complaint, let's think of a solution.

## Synonyms and antonyms

うだうだ often carries impatience or disapproval. It is not a neutral description of resting or
having a careful, extended discussion.

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding verb clarifies whether the scene is about wasting time or talking at length.
Neither use means that a person is simply physically slow.
