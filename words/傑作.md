# 傑作 (けっさく) - masterpiece; hilarious absurdity

## Meaning

傑作 is an outstanding work, a masterpiece.
It is also used humorously for something absurdly funny, such as an unintentionally hilarious remark or blunder.
JMdict marks this second use jocular and dated; do not treat the two evaluations as the same kind of praise.

- この小説は彼の傑作です。 (このしょうせつはかれのけっさくです。) - This novel is one of his masterpieces.
- それは傑作な話だ。 (それはけっさくなはなしだ。) - That is a hilariously absurd story.

The second is an example recorded by Daijisen for the humorous use.
There 傑作 can take な before a noun; the outstanding-work sense is a noun.

## How the kanji combine

傑 contributes excellence; [作](../kanji/作.md) contributes a work or creation.
Their combination directly helps recall an exceptional work.
ケツ + サク gives けっさく, with small っ.
The humorous evaluation is an additional lexical use, not an instruction to call every mistake excellent.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作品](作品.md) (さくひん) names a work without necessarily judging it outstanding.
[面白い](面白い.md) (おもしろい) and [おかしい](おかしい.md) overlap with the amusing or absurd evaluation in suitable contexts.
No single opposite covers both meanings.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list's "boner" and "blunder" need the humorous absurdity context.
傑作 is not a general synonym for any error, failure, or accident.
Likewise, a work need not be an author's single best work to be called a 傑作.
