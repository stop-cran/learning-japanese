# 機会 (きかい) — opportunity

## Meaning

機会 is an opportunity or occasion to do something.
Use a dictionary-form verb before it to say what the opportunity is for:
話す機会 (はなすきかい), "an opportunity to speak."

- 日本語を話す機会が増えました。 (にほんごをはなすきかいがふえました。) — I now have more opportunities to speak Japanese.
- 機会があれば、京都に行きたいです。 (きかいがあれば、きょうとにいきたいです。) — If I get the chance, I would like to go to Kyoto.

機会がある (きかいがある) means an opportunity exists; it does not say that someone has already taken it.

## How the kanji combine

[機](../kanji/機.md) contributes the opportunity sense here, not the machine sense.
[会](../kanji/会.md) contributes meeting or coming together.
As a meaning aid, think of circumstances coming together to make an occasion possible.
The on readings combine as キ + カイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

チャンス is another word for an opportunity.
可能性 (かのうせい) concerns possibility, likelihood, or potential; it is useful for the probability meaning of English "chance."
An opportunity to act and the likelihood of an outcome are different questions, so 機会 is not a universal translation of "chance."

## Distinctive meaning

機会 and 機械 (きかい), "machine," sound the same but have different second kanji and meanings.
Context and spelling distinguish them. In 日本語を話す機会, the phrase is about an occasion for speaking, not a speaking machine.
