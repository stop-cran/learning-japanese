# 真剣 (しんけん) - serious; earnest

## Meaning

真剣 describes being serious or earnest about what one is doing. It is a
な-adjective: 真剣な顔 (しんけんなかお), "a serious expression."
Use 真剣に before a verb to describe acting with concentration or commitment.

- 将来について真剣に考えています。 (しょうらいについてしんけんにかんがえています。) - I am thinking seriously about my future.
- 子供たちは真剣な顔で話を聞いていました。 (こどもたちはしんけんなかおではなしをきいていました。) - The children listened with serious expressions.

The adverbial に modifies the thinking; な connects the description to a noun.

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md) contributes "true; real," and 剣 means "sword."
The compound also has the literal noun sense "a real sword," as opposed to
a wooden practice weapon. That literal contrast is a useful association,
not a reason to translate a serious discussion as a weapon.

## Synonyms and antonyms

真面目 (まじめ), "serious; diligent," can overlap, including a person's
conscientious attitude. 本気 (ほんき), "serious intent," emphasizes genuinely
meaning something. Joking or treating a matter lightly provides a contextual
contrast, not a single antonym for every use.

## Distinctive meaning

真剣 concerns earnest engagement, not necessarily a person's permanent character.
Someone who usually jokes can listen 真剣に to important news.
English "serious" can also mean medically severe or dangerous; do not automatically
use 真剣 for a serious injury. Identify whether you mean an attitude or the
severity of a situation.
