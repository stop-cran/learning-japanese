# 壁 (かべ) — wall

## Meaning

壁 is a wall or partition, especially the vertical surface enclosing or dividing a room. It is an ordinary noun: 壁の色 (かべのいろ), "the color of the wall."

壁に写真をかけました。 (かべにしゃしんをかけました。) — I hung a photograph on the wall.

## How the kanji combine

壁 is one kanji carrying the native reading かべ. There is no compound to split. The on reading へき appears in words such as 壁画 (へきが), "wall painting; mural."

## Synonyms and antonyms

塀 (へい) commonly means a freestanding boundary wall or fence. 床 (ゆか), "floor," and 天井 (てんじょう), "ceiling," name other room surfaces, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

壁 also describes a figurative barrier: 言葉の壁 (ことばのかべ), "the language barrier." Context distinguishes a physical wall from a difficulty that prevents progress. A literal 壁 may be indoors or outdoors; it is not limited to a room's interior surface.
