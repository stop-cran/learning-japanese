# 畳 (たたみ) -- tatami mat

## Meaning

畳 is a tatami mat, a firm floor covering used in Japanese-style rooms. Traditional mats have a woven rush surface and a straw core; modern materials can differ.

畳の部屋でお茶を飲みました。 (たたみのへやでおちゃをのみました。) -- I drank tea in a room with tatami flooring.

畳の部屋 uses の to connect the flooring to the room. 畳の上に座る (たたみのうえにすわる) means sitting on the tatami.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 畳 writes the native noun たたみ. This card teaches the name of the mat; there is no two-character compound to split into separate readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

床 (ゆか) is the floor more generally. カーペット is carpet, another kind of floor covering, not another name for a tatami mat.

## Distinctive meaning

畳 is not just a loose cushion or any woven mat. It names a specific kind of flooring. The word alone does not establish an exact size: mat dimensions vary, so use actual measurements when precision matters.
