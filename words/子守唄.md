# 子守唄 (こもりうた) — lullaby

## Meaning

子守唄 is a song sung to help a child fall asleep. The pinned JMdict entry also lists 子守歌; that spelling is ranked more frequent in the snapshot.

- 母は赤ちゃんに子守唄を歌いました。 (はははあかちゃんにこもりうたをうたいました。) — The mother sang a lullaby to the baby.
- 祖母が歌ってくれた子守唄を覚えています。 (そぼがうたってくれたこもりうたをおぼえています。) — I remember the lullaby my grandmother sang for me.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[子](../kanji/子.md) means child, [守](../kanji/守.md) means to protect or look after, and [唄](../kanji/唄.md) contributes うた, “song.” The compound is read こもりうた.

## Synonyms and antonyms

歌 (うた) is a general word for a song; 子守唄 specifically names a song sung to soothe a child.

## Distinctive meaning

子守唄 is specifically a song for soothing a child; it is not simply any song.
