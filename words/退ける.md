# 退ける (どける) - move aside; put out of the way

## Meaning

退ける means to move someone or something aside or out of the way.
It is a transitive ichidan verb; its kana spelling is どける.
Mark what is moved with を; a destination can take に.

- 通路の荷物をどけてください。 (つうろのにもつをどけてください。) - Please move the luggage out of the passage.
- 椅子を少し横にどけました。 (いすをすこしよこにどけました。) - I moved the chair a little to the side.

Forms include どけます, どけて, どけた, and どけない.
The request asks someone to move the luggage, not simply to step aside themselves.

## How the kanji combine

[退](../kanji/退.md) supplies the moving-aside association in the spelling 退ける.
Learn the complete word どける; this reading is not derived from the on reading タイ.
The kana stem before ます is どけ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[退く](退く.md) (どく) is intransitive: 人がどく, someone moves aside,
contrasts with 人をどける, moving someone aside.
[動かす](動かす.md) (うごかす) is a broader verb for moving something.
[避ける](避ける.md) has a physical よける use that can overlap;
its primary さける avoidance use is a separate learning point.

## Distinctive meaning

This card teaches どける, not every verb written 退ける.
Other readings include しりぞける and のける, with their own uses.
Do not import all their dismissing, rejecting, or auxiliary senses into どける.
With people, a bare command can sound forceful; the examples use a request.
