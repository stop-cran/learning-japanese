# 今朝 (けさ) — this morning

## Meaning

今朝 means this morning: the morning of the day being discussed as "today." It can directly modify a verb without に.

今朝、六時に起きました。（けさ、ろくじにおきました。） — I got up at six this morning.

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md), "now; current," combines with [朝](../kanji/朝.md), "morning." The everyday reading けさ belongs to the whole compound. Do not try to assign け and さ to the two kanji as their normal separate readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

朝（あさ） means morning generally. 毎朝（まいあさ） is "every morning"; 明日の朝（あしたのあさ） is "tomorrow morning."

## Distinctive meaning

今朝 remains "this morning" when you speak later in the day, so it commonly occurs with past-tense verbs. It does not mean "right now in the morning"; the event's exact time comes from context or an added time expression.
