# 自衛 (じえい) - self-defense

## Meaning

自衛 means self-defense or protecting oneself. It is a noun and takes する as a verb. 自衛のために means for self-defense or self-protection.

- 自衛のために、夜道では周囲に気をつけています。 (じえいのために、よみちではしゅういにきをつけています。) - For my own protection, I pay attention to my surroundings when walking at night.
- 自衛の方法について話し合いました。 (じえいのほうほうについてはなしあいました。) - We discussed ways to protect ourselves.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) contributes oneself, and 衛 contributes guarding or defense. Oneself specifies who is protected. The on readings are ジ + エイ; the second part is えい, not a separate word meaning good.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[守る](守る.md) まもる means to protect or defend more broadly. [防ぐ](防ぐ.md) ふせぐ marks the threat being prevented or warded off. 自衛 brings the self-protection purpose into focus; it need not name a physical fight.

## Distinctive meaning

自衛 occurs in military vocabulary, including 自衛隊 (じえいたい), the Self-Defense Forces, but is not confined to that institution. Calling an action 自衛 does not by itself establish that the action is legally justified or effective.
