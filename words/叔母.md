# 叔母 (しゅくぼ) — aunt (a parent's younger sister)

## Meaning

叔母 read しゅくぼ is an aunt who is a younger sister of one's father or mother. Digital Daijisen defines しゅくぼ as 父母の妹, a younger sister of one's father or mother, and adds おば as an equivalent. JMdict glosses the entry “aunt” and notes that 叔母 can mean an aunt younger than one's parent.

- 叔母は、父や母の妹にあたります。 (しゅくぼは、ちちやははのいもうとにあたります。) — An aunt (しゅくぼ) is a younger sister of one's father or mother.
- 父の姉は伯母、妹は叔母と書き分けます。 (ちちのあねははくぼ、いもうとはしゅくぼとかきわけます。) — A father's older sister is written 伯母, and his younger sister 叔母.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[叔](../kanji/叔.md) refers to a younger sibling of one's father or mother, and [母](../kanji/母.md) means mother. Together they name a parent's younger sister. Both parts use on readings: しゅく from シュク and ぼ from ボ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[伯母さん](伯母さん.md) (おばさん) is a familiar, courteous word for an aunt; its spelling uses 伯 for an aunt older than the relevant parent. 叔父 (しゅくふ, also read おじ) is the male counterpart, an uncle younger than one's parent (JMdict entry 1607070). [親戚](親戚.md) (しんせき) covers relatives in general.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling 叔母 is also read おば. The Joyo table lists 叔母 with the special reading おば. In JMdict, the reading おば carries priority tags, while しゅくぼ carries none and is restricted to the spelling 叔母. Do not assume that every printed 叔母 is pronounced しゅくぼ. 伯母 and 叔母 are both read おば; JMdict notes that 伯母 can mean an aunt older than one's parent and 叔母 one younger, so the kanji show what おば alone leaves open.
