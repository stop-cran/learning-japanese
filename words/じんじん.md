# じんじん — tingling; throbbing numbness

## Meaning

A lingering tingling or throbbing sensation, including pain or numbness.

- 冷たい手を温（あたた）めると、指（ゆび）がじんじんした。 — My fingers tingled as I warmed my cold hands.
- 長く座っていて、足がじんじんしびれた。 — After sitting for a long time, my legs felt numb and tingly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずきずき](ずきずき.md) emphasizes throbbing pain; [びりびり](びりびり.md) can suggest an electric-shock-like tingle. [ぴりぴり](ぴりぴり.md) also overlaps in tingling.

## Distinctive meaning

じんじんする describes a felt quality, not a diagnosis. The word also has ringing/buzzing uses, including insects. Same-reading kanji words about virtue or depth are separate dictionary identities, not hidden meanings of this card.
