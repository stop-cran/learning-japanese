# 世界 (せかい) — world

## Meaning

世界 means the world, and it can also mean a particular sphere of life, such as the world of music.

- 世界にはいろいろな国があります。 (せかいにはいろいろなくにがあります。) — There are many different countries in the world.
- 音楽の世界に興味があります。 (おんがくのせかいにきょうみがあります。) — I am interested in the world of music.

## How the kanji combine

[世](../kanji/世.md) contributes the idea of the world or society; [界](../kanji/界.md) contributes a realm or domain.
The two ideas combine into "world" or a particular world of activity. The on readings are セ + カイ, not セイ + カイ.
This is a meaning-based reading aid, not an account of the word's historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[社会](社会.md) (しゃかい, society) focuses on people and their social life.
地球 (ちきゅう, Earth) names the planet, so it cannot replace 世界 in "the world of music."
There is no useful single antonym of 世界.

## Distinctive meaning

世界 can cover countries and peoples together or a field of experience. Context decides its scale.
Use a noun + の + 世界 for an ordinary phrase such as 音楽の世界 (おんがくのせかい), "the world of music."
