# 暖房 (だんぼう) - indoor heating

## Meaning

暖房 is indoor heating or a heating system. 暖房する means to heat a room or building.

- 寒いので暖房をつけました。 (さむいのでだんぼうをつけました。) - I turned on the heating because it was cold.
- 寝る前に暖房を消します。 (ねるまえにだんぼうをけします。) - I turn off the heating before going to bed.

In these everyday expressions, 暖房 is a noun and を marks the system being switched on or off.

## How the kanji combine

暖 contributes warmth and 房 a room: だん + ぼう. The compound concerns making an indoor space warm.

## Synonyms and antonyms

冷房 (れいぼう), "air cooling," is the direct practical contrast. ヒーター names a heater, while 暖房 can refer to the heating function or system.

## Distinctive meaning

An エアコン may provide both 暖房 and 冷房. 暖房 is not the general word for heating food or water; its ordinary scope is indoor temperature control.
