# 主人 (しゅじん) — proprietor; one's husband

## Meaning

主人 can mean the proprietor or person in charge of a household or establishment. It is also a conventional way to refer to one's own husband.
Context matters: the owner of a shop and someone's spouse are different uses.

- 店の主人に聞きました。 (みせのしゅじんにききました。) — I asked the shop's proprietor.
- 主人は今、家にいません。 (しゅじんはいま、いえにいません。) — My husband is not at home now.

## How the kanji combine

[主](../kanji/主.md) (master; person in charge) modifies [人](../kanji/人.md) (person).
The on readings シュ + ジン give しゅじん. The husband sense is a conventional use of the whole word, not the sum "main person" in every context.

## Synonyms and antonyms

夫 (おっと) means "husband" without 主人's master or proprietor sense.
店長 (てんちょう) is a shop manager, who need not own the shop.
持ち主 (もちぬし) means an owner or possessor. There is no single antonym covering all uses of 主人.

## Distinctive meaning

For a straightforward, neutral reference to one's own husband, 夫 is a useful choice.
A common polite way to refer to someone else's husband is ご主人 (ごしゅじん).
主人 should not be used as a general word for any man or any employee.
Its "master" sense also occurs in relationships of service; that does not make it a universal synonym for "boss."
