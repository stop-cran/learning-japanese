# 近い (ちかい) — near; close

## Meaning

近い describes nearness in space or time. It can also mean close in relationship or similar in character.

- 駅はここから近いです。（えきはここからちかいです。） — The station is near here.
- 試験の日が近いです。（しけんのひがちかいです。） — The day of the exam is close.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native i-adjective, not a compound. [近](../kanji/近.md) is read ちか, followed by the okurigana い.
Forms include 近くない（ちかくない）, "not near," and 近かった（ちかかった）, "was near."

## Synonyms and antonyms

遠い（とおい）, "far; distant," is the direct opposite for distance.
近く（ちかく） can function as a noun meaning the vicinity: 駅の近く（えきのちかく）, "near the station."
It is related to 近い but does not fit the same grammatical position in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

For "a nearby shop," use 近くの店（ちかくのみせ）. Direct modification with 近い is natural when a reference point
or comparison is supplied: 駅に近い店（えきにちかいみせ）, "a shop near the station," or
一番近い店（いちばんちかいみせ）, "the nearest shop." Do not add な.
When locating something, 駅の近くにあります（えきのちかくにあります）, "It is near the station,"
uses the noun 近く plus に. Learn both patterns rather than substituting 近い for English "near" everywhere.
