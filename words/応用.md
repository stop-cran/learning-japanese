# 応用 (おうよう) - practical application

## Meaning

応用 is putting knowledge, a principle or a technique to practical
use. 応用する is transitive: what is applied takes を, and
the field or situation it is applied to can take に.

- 学んだ知識を仕事に応用します。
  (まなんだちしきをしごとにおうようします。) - I apply what I have learned to my work.
- これは数学の応用問題です。
  (これはすうがくのおうようもんだいです。) - This is a mathematics problem that requires applying what you have learned.

Before a field name, 応用 can mean "applied":
応用物理学 (おうようぶつりがく), applied physics.
This use does not require inserting な.

## How the kanji combine

応 contributes responding or adapting, and
[用](../kanji/用.md) use. オウ + ヨウ connects use with
meeting an actual situation. This is a meaning aid, not proof that
every use of an object is automatically 応用.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[活用](活用.md) (かつよう) overlaps in putting something to
effective use. [適用](適用.md) (てきよう) and
[あてはめる](あてはめる.md) concern applying a rule or method
to a case. [使用](使用.md) (しよう) and
[利用](利用.md) (りよう) are broader use words.
The focus here is bringing something learned or developed to practical use.

## Distinctive meaning

English "apply/application" has other meanings:
[塗る](塗る.md) (ぬる) applies a substance to a surface, and
[申し込む](申し込む.md) (もうしこむ) is applying for something.
Those meanings do not automatically transfer to 応用.
Likewise, applied physics is a field name, not a job application.
