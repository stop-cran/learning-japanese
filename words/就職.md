# 就職 (しゅうしょく) - finding employment

## Meaning

就職 means finding employment or getting a job. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb. The person becomes employed; an employer or workplace can take に: 会社に就職する (かいしゃにしゅうしょくする), "get a job at a company."

- 大学を卒業して、銀行に就職しました。 (だいがくをそつぎょうして、ぎんこうにしゅうしょくしました。) - After graduating from university, I got a job at a bank.
- 就職が決まって、安心しました。 (しゅうしょくがきまって、あんしんしました。) - I felt relieved once my employment was arranged.

就職活動 (しゅうしょくかつどう) means job-hunting activities; it names the search, not a guaranteed result.

## How the kanji combine

就 contributes taking up or entering, and [職](../kanji/職.md) contributes employment or an occupation. Together they describe entering employment rather than performing each day's work.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[働く](働く.md) (はたらく) means working. [仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) names work or a job. Someone can discuss 仕事 before or after the event of 就職. 退職 (たいしょく), leaving employment, is a useful opposing transition.

## Distinctive meaning

就職 is not a general word for an inauguration, opening ceremony, or appointment celebration. That broad English gloss in the community source is not supported by the selected entry. The dictionary-backed teaching here is the change into employment, without claiming to recover the compiler's intended extra meaning.
