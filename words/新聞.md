# 新聞 (しんぶん) — newspaper

## Meaning

新聞 is a newspaper: 新聞を読む (read the newspaper), 新聞を取る (subscribe to a newspaper), 新聞記事 しんぶんきじ (newspaper article), 朝刊 ちょうかん (morning edition), 新聞社 しんぶんしゃ (newspaper company).

## How the kanji combine

[新](../kanji/新.md) (new) plus [聞](../kanji/聞.md) (hear, news): "new things heard", that is, news. On readings シン + ブン; 聞 has the readings ブン and モン, and ブン is the one used here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: ニュース (news, gairaigo), 雑誌 ざっし (magazine), 新聞紙 しんぶんし (newsprint, the paper itself).

## Distinctive meaning

新聞 means the newspaper medium. "News" as content is ニュース or 報道 ほうどう; 新聞 is also the "paper" itself, as in 新聞で包む (wrap in newspaper).
