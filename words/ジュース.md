# ジュース (ジュース) - juice; soft drink; sports deuce

## Meaning

ジュース is a noun for juice. In broader beverage usage, it can also mean a sweetened or soft drink, often fruit-based.

- 朝、オレンジジュースを飲みました。 (あさ、オレンジジュースをのみました。) - I drank orange juice in the morning.
- ジュースを一本ください。 (ジュースをいっぽんください。) - One bottle of juice or soft drink, please.

The menu or product determines exactly which drink is meant in the second example. The name alone does not guarantee pure fruit juice.

A separate sports word, also written ジュース, means "deuce"; デュース is another recognized spelling of that sports identity.

- テニスの試合でジュースになりました。 (テニスのしあいでジュースになりました。) - The tennis game reached deuce.

## How the kanji combine

Both identities use katakana, with ジュ as a contracted sound and ー marking a long vowel. There are no kanji to decompose. Identical Japanese spelling does not make English "juice" and "deuce" one dictionary identity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飲み物](飲み物.md) (のみもの) is the broader category "drink." [水](水.md) (みず) is another beverage, not an antonym. In sports, デュース names the same scoring term; it is not another drink.

## Distinctive meaning

The source combines beverage and sports meanings under one supplied ID. JMdict separates them: beverage entry 1065950, senses 1-2; sports entry 2843595, sense 1. Both are taught here with their contexts, without treating the sports use as a beverage sense or assigning separate JLPT levels to the senses.
