# 作物 (さくもつ) - crops; agricultural produce

## Meaning

作物 names crops or agricultural produce. It is a noun: **作物を育てる** (さくもつをそだてる) means to grow crops, and 作物を収穫する means to harvest them.

- この土地では、いろいろな作物を育てています。 (このとちでは、いろいろなさくもつをそだてています。) - We grow various crops on this land.
- 雨が少なく、作物が育ちません。 (あめがすくなく、さくもつがそだちません。) - There is little rain, and the crops are not growing.

作物 can refer to plants still growing, not just produce already collected or displayed for sale.

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) contributes making or cultivating; [物](../kanji/物.md) means a thing. Together they identify what is produced through cultivation. The readings are サク + モツ, not the もの reading of standalone 物.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[野菜](野菜.md) (やさい), "vegetables," names one group of crops, not the whole category. [農業](農業.md) (のうぎょう) is agriculture, the activity or industry. [収穫](収穫.md) (しゅうかく) focuses on gathering a crop or the resulting yield.

## Distinctive meaning

The farming context is essential for 作物 (さくもつ). Do not translate it mechanically as any "made thing," such as a manufactured tool or a painting. Nor does 作物 by itself tell you whether the harvest was abundant or successful.
