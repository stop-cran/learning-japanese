# すると (すると) — then; thereupon

## Meaning

すると connects a preceding situation with what happens next: "then", "thereupon", or "and as a result". It often introduces a discovered or observed development in a story.

- ドアを開けました。すると、猫が入ってきました。  
  ドアをあけました。すると、ねこがはいってきました。  
  "I opened the door. Then a cat came in."

It can also introduce an inference:

- 今日は休みですか。すると、店は閉まっていますね。  
  きょうはやすみですか。すると、みせはしまっていますね。  
  "Is today a day off? Then the shop is closed, isn't it?"

## How the kanji combine

The conjunction is written in kana. Its form matches する plus conditional と, but here it stands at the start of a new statement referring back to the previous one.

## Synonyms and antonyms

それから commonly advances a sequence: "after that". [それでは](それでは.md), "well then; in that case", often introduces a decision or next action based on the situation. These are not unrestricted substitutes.

## Distinctive meaning

すると highlights how the next development follows from what preceded it. It is not an all-purpose translation of English "then", especially when "then" merely names a particular past time.
