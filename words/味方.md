# 味方 (みかた) - ally; supporter

## Meaning

味方 is a noun for someone on your side: an ally or supporter.
Person + の味方 identifies whose side someone is on.
味方する is an intransitive verb meaning "side with" or "support"; the supported person takes に.

- 私はあなたの味方です。 (わたしはあなたのみかたです。) - I am on your side.
- 彼は困っている友人に味方しました。 (かれはこまっているゆうじんにみかたしました。) - He stood by his friend who was in difficulty.

味方になる (みかたになる) means "become an ally; take someone's side."
The relationship can arise in an argument, a competition, or another situation requiring support.

## How the kanji combine

[味](../kanji/味.md) ordinarily has taste-related meanings, while [方](../kanji/方.md) can indicate a side.
JMdict marks 味方 as ateji, a spelling using characters for their sounds.
Do not invent "a taste direction" as the word's meaning or treat it as a transparent kango compound.
Learn the native word みかた together with this established spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[敵](敵.md) (てき), "enemy; opponent," is the direct contrast in an opposing-sides context.
[仲間](仲間.md) (なかま), "companion; fellow member," emphasizes belonging together.
A companion may be an ally, but membership and active support are not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "friend" is useful when it means someone supporting your side.
It does not make 味方 a general replacement for a personal friend in every social context.
Someone can agree with you in a dispute without being a close friend.
