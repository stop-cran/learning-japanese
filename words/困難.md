# 困難 (こんなん) - difficulty; difficult

## Meaning

困難 is a noun for difficulty or hardship and a na-adjective meaning difficult.
Use 困難な before a noun, or describe an action with verb + ことは困難です.

- 一人でこの問題を解決することは困難です。 (ひとりでこのもんだいをかいけつすることはこんなんです。) - It is difficult to solve this problem alone.
- 多くの困難を乗り越えました。 (おおくのこんなんをのりこえました。) - We overcame many difficulties.

The first example evaluates an action. The second treats difficulties as obstacles,
using 困難を乗り越える.

## How the kanji combine

[困](../kanji/困.md) concerns being troubled or in difficulty; [難](../kanji/難.md)
contributes difficulty or hardship. Their related meanings reinforce the compound.
The readings are コン + ナン; pronounce both nasal beats in こんなん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[難しい](難しい.md) (むずかしい) is an i-adjective with overlapping uses.
[困る](困る.md) (こまる) describes someone being troubled, rather than directly
classifying a task as difficult. [簡単](簡単.md) (かんたん), "easy; simple," is
a useful contrast for tasks.

## Distinctive meaning

困難 does not automatically mean impossible. However, it can also be used as a
softened way of saying that something cannot feasibly be done. A response such as
対応は困難です (たいおうはこんなんです), "It would be difficult to accommodate
that," may function as a refusal. Judge that implication from the situation, not
from the adjective alone.
