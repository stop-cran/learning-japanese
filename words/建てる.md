# 建てる (たてる) — build; construct

## Meaning

建てる means to build or put up a building. It is transitive: the thing being built takes を. The person described can arrange for the construction, not necessarily do all the work personally.

- ここに家を建てます。  
  ここにいえをたてます。  
  "I'll build a house here."
- 父は去年、家を建てました。  
  ちちはきょねん、いえをたてました。  
  "My father built a house last year."

## How the kanji combine

This is one native Japanese verb, not a compound of two kanji. [建](../kanji/建.md) supplies た, and てる is okurigana. It is an ichidan verb: 建てます たてます, 建てた たてた, 建てない たてない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作る](作る.md) つくる means "make" more broadly. 立てる たてる, written with [立](../kanji/立.md), can mean setting something upright; it is not the default spelling for constructing a house. 壊す こわす (break; demolish) contrasts with 建てる when discussing a building.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 家を建てる いえをたてる (build a house) with 家が建つ いえがたつ (a house is built). 建つ たつ is the intransitive partner: it describes the building coming into existence rather than naming an object someone builds.
