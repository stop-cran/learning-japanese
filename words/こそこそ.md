# こそこそ — sneakily; on the sly

## Meaning

こそこそ describes acting secretly or stealthily to avoid being noticed.

- 二人は廊下でこそこそ話していた。 — The two were talking on the sly in the hallway. (廊下: ろうか.)
- 彼は誰にも見られないように、こそこそと裏口から出た。 — He slipped out the back entrance so nobody would see him. (裏口: うらぐち.)

Use こそこそ（と）話す or another suitable action verb. こそこそする describes behaving furtively more generally; こそこそした態度 (たいど) is "a furtive manner."

## How the kanji combine

This repeated kana mimetic depicts a secretive manner. It need not imitate a particular audible rustle or whisper.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ひそひそ](ひそひそ.md) focuses on a low voice, though it too can suggest secrecy. [そっと](そっと.md) may mean discreetly or quietly but can instead emphasize gentle handling with no concealment.

## Distinctive meaning

こそこそ often conveys the observer's suspicion or disapproval. It does not prove that the action is wrong: someone preparing a surprise may act secretly for a kind reason. Conversely, quiet speech alone need not be sneaky.
