# このごろ (このごろ) - these days; lately

## Meaning

このごろ refers to the period around now, especially a current tendency, repeated experience, or noticeable change. It commonly translates as "these days" or "lately."

- このごろ、よく雨が降ります。 (このごろ、よくあめがふります。) - It rains a lot these days.
- このごろ忙しくて、本を読んでいません。 (このごろいそがしくて、ほんをよんでいません。) - I have been busy lately and have not been reading.

It can introduce a time setting without に. このごろの生活 (このごろのせいかつ) means "life these days."

## How the kanji combine

The word can also be written この頃. この means "this," and ごろ refers to a time or period. The kana spelling is natural and does not hide a separate kanji-based meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[最近](最近.md) (さいきん) also means "recently" or "these days." It readily introduces a single recent event as well as a current pattern. 昔 (むかし), "the old days," provides a broader time contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

このごろ does not identify a fixed number of days or mean only "today." It looks across a recent stretch of time. 今 (いま), "now," is more suitable when the point is this exact moment.
