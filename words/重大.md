# 重大 (じゅうだい) - serious; grave; significant

## Meaning

重大 is a な-adjective for something serious, weighty, or highly significant. Use 重大な before a noun and 重大だ or 重大です as a predicate. It often highlights consequences that deserve careful attention.

- 重大な問題が見つかりました。 (じゅうだいなもんだいがみつかりました。) - A serious problem was discovered.
- これは将来に関わる重大な決定です。 (これはしょうらいにかかわるじゅうだいなけっていです。) - This is a major decision affecting the future.

重大さ (じゅうだいさ) is the noun "seriousness" or "significance," as in 問題の重大さ, "the seriousness of the problem."

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) contributes weight or importance, and [大](../kanji/大.md) contributes greatness or large scale. The reinforcing combination describes substantial significance, not necessarily physical weight or size.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[重要](重要.md) (じゅうよう) also means important. 重大 is useful when emphasizing the gravity or consequences of a matter. [大切](大切.md) (たいせつ) can describe something personally precious or deserving care; it need not suggest a grave situation.

## Distinctive meaning

重大な事故 (じゅうだいなじこ), "a serious accident," concerns seriousness, not merely a large-looking accident. Yet 重大 is not inherently negative: an important decision or discovery can also have weighty significance. Read the noun and context rather than equating the adjective with "bad."
