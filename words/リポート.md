# リポート — report

## Meaning

リポート means a report, paper, or an account of events. It is a noun and forms リポートする, "report." [レポート](レポート.md) is another spelling and pronunciation of the same dictionary word.

調査の結果をリポートにまとめました。 (ちょうさのけっかをリポートにまとめました。) — I put the survey results together in a report.

記者が現地からリポートしています。 (きしゃがげんちからリポートしています。) — A journalist is reporting from the scene.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana borrowing related to English "report." Keep the long vowel in ポー. リポート and レポート differ in their first syllable, not in a kanji reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

報告 (ほうこく) means reporting or a report. 報告書 (ほうこくしょ) explicitly identifies a written report document. These overlap with the borrowed word.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not invent a strict division in which リポート is always spoken and レポート always written. Context and conventional collocations matter; the dictionary records both forms for reports and reportage. A classroom paper and a journalist's report involve different tasks even when the vocabulary overlaps.
