# 請う (こう) - beg; request; invite

## Meaning

請う is a transitive verb meaning to ask for, beg for, request, or invite.
In the request construction used below, what is requested takes を;
the person asked can take に.

- 先生に教えを請いました。 (せんせいにおしえをこいました。) - I asked the teacher for instruction.
- 心から許しを請います。 (こころからゆるしをこいます。) - I sincerely ask for forgiveness.

This is a **special-class godan verb ending in う**, not an ichidan verb.
Learn 請います (こいます), 請わない (こわない),
請うて (こうて), and 請うた (こうた).
Do not form its て or past form as こって / こった.

## How the kanji combine

請 contributes asking or inviting; う is the written ending.
The whole native verb is read こう, not an on reading of 請.
乞う is another spelling in the same entry, also read こう.
The written character associations do not establish an absolute
request-versus-begging division between the spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ) is a broader everyday request word;
[求める](求める.md) (もとめる) can mean request or seek.
[招く](招く.md) (まねく) overlaps in inviting.
[願う](願う.md) (ねがう) and [祈る](祈る.md) (いのる) are useful
comparisons for wishing or praying.

## Distinctive meaning

The selected N2 mapping concerns asking, requesting, and inviting.
JMdict additionally records praying or wishing, as in asking for divine
protection; that added sense is not separately assigned an N2 level here.
The unrelated [こう](こう.md), "like this," has the same sound but is not this verb.
The instruction-request construction is more formal than a casual
お願い; dictionary recognition is not evidence that every invented
請う collocation will sound natural.
