# 図形 (ずけい) - geometric figure; shape

## Meaning

図形 is a figure or shape, especially a represented or geometric shape.
Circles and triangles are familiar examples; a figure can also be more complex
or three-dimensional.

- 紙に丸い図形を描きました。 (かみにまるいずけいをかきました。) - I drew a round shape on the paper.
- この二つの図形は大きさが違います。 (このふたつのずけいはおおきさがちがいます。) - These two figures differ in size.

図形の面積 (ずけいのめんせき) means "the area of a figure." The mathematical
context tells you what kind of shape is being discussed.

## How the kanji combine

[図](../kanji/図.md) contributes a drawing or diagram, and [形](../kanji/形.md)
shape or form. Together they identify a shape considered as a figure.
Both use on readings: ズ + ケイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[形](形.md) (かたち) is broader and can describe an object's physical shape.
[図](図.md) (ず) is a diagram or visual representation.
[図表](図表.md) (ずひょう) concerns charts, diagrams, and graphical or tabular
presentation; it is not restricted to geometric shapes.

## Distinctive meaning

English "figure" can also mean a number or someone's bodily appearance.
Those meanings do not automatically become 図形. Here the focus is a shape
or its representation, not a numerical value simply because it is printed.
There is no general antonym of a figure.
