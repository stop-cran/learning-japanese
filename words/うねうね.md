# うねうね — winding; curving repeatedly

## Meaning

うねうね describes a line or shape that bends and curves repeatedly.

- 山の斜面に細い道がうねうねと続いている。
  — A narrow road winds along the mountainside.
- 川が草原の中をうねうね流れていく。
  — The river winds through the meadow.

## Synonyms and antonyms

うねうね emphasizes a repeated, flowing curve. It is not the word for a straight route or a
single sharp turn.

## Distinctive meaning

The word describes the shape or path itself; it does not say whether the route is difficult,
long, or slow to travel.
