# 泥 (どろ) - mud

## Meaning

泥 is mud or wet dirt. It is a noun, used in combinations such as
泥がつく, "get mud on something," 泥を落とす (どろをおとす), "remove mud,"
and 泥だらけ, "covered in mud."

- 雨の後、靴に泥がつきました。
  (あめのあと、くつにどろがつきました。) - After the rain, mud got on my shoes.
- 玄関に入る前に、靴の泥を落としてください。
  (げんかんにはいるまえに、くつのどろをおとしてください。)
  - Please remove the mud from your shoes before entering the entrance hall.

泥水 (どろみず) is muddy water. It names water mixed with mud rather than every
kind of dirty or cloudy liquid.

## How the kanji combine

泥 is a single kanji for the whole native word どろ.
There are no independent word components to translate.
In compounds such as 泥水, 泥 specifies what is mixed with the water.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[土](土.md) (つち) is earth or soil and need not be wet.
汚れ (よごれ) means dirt or a stain more broadly: a grease stain is not necessarily 泥.
Dryness is a contrast in condition, not a separate antonym for this substance.

## Distinctive meaning

The English word "dirt" is broader than 泥.
Use 泥 when the muddy material itself matters; use a broader description for dirt whose
substance is unspecified. These examples teach the ordinary substance meaning, not specialized abbreviations.
