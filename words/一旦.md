# 一旦 (いったん) - once; temporarily

## Meaning

一旦, usually written いったん, can identify a temporary step or interruption
before a later development. It can also mean "once" in a condition: once
something happens, a particular consequence follows.

- いったん家に帰ってから、また出かけます。 (いったんいえにかえってから、またでかけます。) - I will go home for the time being and then go out again.
- いったん約束したら、守ってください。 (いったんやくそくしたら、まもってください。) - Once you have made a promise, please keep it.

The first describes an intermediate step. The second does not count the
total number of promises; it concerns what follows making one.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) means one, and 旦 is associated with dawn or morning.
The dictionary also records an archaic "one morning" sense, but that is
not a literal translation rule for the modern adverb. Learn いったん,
including the small っ, as a whole expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一時](一時.md) (いちじ) can describe a temporary situation.
[一応](一応.md) (いちおう) can leave an action provisional or indicate
a precaution. [一度](一度.md) (いちど) simply counts one occurrence in
examples such as 一度行った, "went once."

## Distinctive meaning

Temporary does not specify a fixed number of minutes. The later step can
be stated explicitly, as with また出かける, or understood from context.
Do not substitute いったん for every English "once": the conditional
example means "once you have promised," not "on one occasion in the past."
