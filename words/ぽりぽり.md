# ぽりぽり — light crunching; scratching an itch

## Meaning

Repeated crunching bites, or small scratching movements.

- きゅうりをぽりぽり食べた。 — I ate the cucumber with light crunches.
- 彼は頭をぽりぽりかいた。 — He scratched his head.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[かりかり](かりかり.md) and [ぱりぱり](ぱりぱり.md) can describe overlapping crisp-food sounds. [ごしごし](ごしごし.md) suggests vigorous rubbing or scrubbing rather than little scratches.

## Distinctive meaning

ぽりぽり食べる and 頭をぽりぽりかく depict different actions. Scratching one's head may accompany embarrassment, but the word itself does not establish that emotion or a medical cause.
