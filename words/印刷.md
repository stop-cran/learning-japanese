# 印刷 (いんさつ) - printing

## Meaning

印刷 is printing: producing text or images on paper or another surface. Add する for the transitive verb "print." The document or image takes を; a printer or method can take で.

- この資料を三部印刷してください。 (このしりょうをさんぶいんさつしてください。) - Please print three copies of this document.
- 写真をカラーで印刷しました。 (しゃしんをカラーでいんさつしました。) - I printed the photograph in color.

三部 counts copies of the document, which may contain several sheets. 印刷中 (いんさつちゅう) means "printing in progress."

## How the kanji combine

印 contributes a mark or impression, and 刷 contributes printing. Together they name producing marked impressions of text or images. The on readings are イン + サツ. This explains the components' meanings without specifying one historical printing technique.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[コピー](コピー.md) is copying or a copy; copying an existing paper document and printing a digital file are related but different operations. [出版](出版.md) (しゅっぱん) means publication, which involves making a work available, not merely printing it.

There is no general opposite of printing; deleting a file does not undo a printed page.

## Distinctive meaning

印刷 names the production step, not the writing of the content or the ownership of a printer. In a computer menu, 印刷 is the "Print" command. For saying that you wrote something by hand, use [書く](書く.md) (かく), not 印刷する.
