# 男女 (だんじょ) — men and women

## Meaning

男女 pairs [男](../kanji/男.md) (man) and [女](../kanji/女.md) (woman). It means "the sexes", "men and women", usually as a category:
男女平等 (equality of the sexes), 男女共学 (co-education), 男女別 (separated by sex).

## How the kanji combine

A paired-opposites compound, like [大小](大小.md) and [長短](長短.md), but here the pair names two groups rather than a scale.
The fixed order is 男女; 女男 isn't used.
The four-character idiom 老若男女 ろうにゃくなんにょ ("old, young, men and women") means "people of all ages and sexes". It shows
the on readings ナン and ニョ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 男と女 おとこと おんな (native, informal), 男性と女性 (formal, individual people), 両性 りょうせい (both sexes).
- Contrast: 男女 is a pair, so the whole word has no antonym; compare 男 ↔ 女.

## Distinctive meaning

男女 refers to the categories: 男女の差, 男女の違い. For individuals, use 男性 だんせい and 女性 じょせい (polite) or 男の人 and 女の人 (everyday).
