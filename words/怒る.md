# 怒る (おこる) — get angry

## Meaning

怒る means to get angry. In this sense it is intransitive: the person becoming angry is the subject.
怒っている (おこっている) describes being angry.

- 父は私に怒っています。 (ちちはわたしにおこっています。) — My father is angry with me.

Here に marks the person at whom the anger is directed; it is not an を-marked object.

## How the kanji combine

[怒](../kanji/怒.md) writes おこ, and る is the ending. This is a native verb using a kun reading.
The same written verb 怒る can also be read いかる, which JMdict marks as formal or literary.
This article uses おこる. The noun 怒り (いかり) means "anger."

## Synonyms and antonyms

しかる, also written 叱る (しかる), means to scold. JMdict marks that word as usually written in kana.
Scolding is an action directed at someone; anger is the feeling.
The distinction is not absolute vocabulary separation: 怒る also has a transitive "scold" sense,
which JMdict notes is usually used in the passive.

## Distinctive meaning

This is a godan verb even though it ends in る. Its forms include 怒ります (おこります),
怒らない (おこらない), and 怒って (おこって).
Compare the state in the example with 怒りました (おこりました), "got angry," which reports
the change or event.
