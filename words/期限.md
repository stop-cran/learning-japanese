# 期限 (きげん) - deadline; term; time limit

## Meaning

期限 is a set term or time frame, or the deadline at which an allowed time ends. It is a noun. 提出期限 (ていしゅつきげん) is a submission deadline; 期限を延ばす (きげんをのばす) extends a deadline or term.

- 申し込みの期限は金曜日です。
  (もうしこみのきげんはきんようびです。) - The application deadline is Friday.
- 返却の期限を一週間延ばしてもらいました。
  (へんきゃくのきげんをいっしゅうかんのばしてもらいました。) - I had the return deadline extended by a week.

## How the kanji combine

[期](../kanji/期.md) concerns a period or appointed time; [限](../kanji/限.md) a limit. Together they highlight a time subject to a limit. The on readings are キ + ゲン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[期間](期間.md) (きかん) is a period or duration, while 期限 highlights a set term or its limit. [時期](時期.md) (じき) concerns a time or season for something. These overlap in broad English "period" labels, but they are not freely interchangeable in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

期限が切れる (きげんがきれる) means that the allowed time expires. It does not mean that a physical object is cut. A question about 期限 often asks "by when?"; do not reduce all uses to a single calendar date, because the word can also name the allowed term.
