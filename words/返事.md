# 返事 (へんじ) - reply, response

## Meaning

返事 is a reply or response, spoken or written. Add する for "reply." The person or message being answered commonly takes に.

- 先生のメールに返事をしました。 (せんせいのメールにへんじをしました。) - I replied to my teacher's email.
- 名前を呼ばれたら、返事をしてください。 (なまえをよばれたら、へんじをしてください。) - Please respond when your name is called.

Both 返事する and 返事をする are possible.

## How the kanji combine

[返](../kanji/返.md), へん, contributes returning, and [事](../kanji/事.md), じ, contributes a matter or act. Think of a response going back to the person who spoke or wrote.

## Synonyms and antonyms

答え (こたえ) is an answer, including a solution to a problem. 返信 (へんしん) specifically concerns replying to correspondence such as an email. 返事 is not restricted to written messages.

## Distinctive meaning

A brief はい when called can be a 返事 even if it supplies no factual solution. This distinguishes acknowledging or responding to someone from finding the correct answer to a test question.
