# 充てる (あてる) - allocate; earmark

## Meaning

充てる means allocating or devoting a resource to a particular purpose.
Use **resource を + purpose に + 充てる**: the resource can be time or money.
It is a transitive ichidan verb: 充てます (あてます), 充てた (あてた),
充てない (あてない).

- 余暇を読書に充てています。 (よかをどくしょにあてています。) - I devote my spare time to reading.
- ボーナスの一部を旅費に充てました。 (ボーナスのいちぶをりょひにあてました。) - I allocated part of my bonus to travel expenses.

The point is what the resource is used or earmarked **for**, not simply that
it is kept unused in reserve.

## How the kanji combine

[充](../kanji/充.md) represents the native stem あ in this allocation verb;
てる is okurigana and changes with conjugation. This is one written kanji,
not a compound. Its filling/allotting association is a memory aid, not a
derivation of the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

割り当てる (わりあてる), "assign; allot," overlaps in allocating work or
resources. [配る](配る.md) (くばる) foregrounds distributing things or
placing personnel; [振る](振る.md) (ふる) can mean assigning work to someone.
These are related uses, not freely interchangeable constructions.

[当てる](当てる.md), also あてる, is the spelling to learn for hitting a target,
holding something against a surface, or guessing correctly. Dictionaries
also record allotting uses in the broader あてる family, so do not treat
these spellings as having completely separate meanings in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

In 余暇を読書に充てる (よかをどくしょにあてる), the time is devoted to reading.
English "set aside" can instead suggest saving something untouched:
[蓄える](蓄える.md) (たくわえる) concerns storing or accumulating, not this
verb's resource-for-purpose relation. There is no single opposite that
reverses all uses of 充てる.
