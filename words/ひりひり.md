# ひりひり — stinging or smarting pain

## Meaning

A raw, smarting, often burning kind of sting.

- 日焼（ひや）けした肩（かた）がひりひりする。 — My sunburned shoulders sting.
- 辛（から）いスープを飲んで、舌（した）がひりひりした。 — The spicy soup left my tongue burning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ちくちく](ちくちく.md) often feels more like little pricks. [ぴりぴり](ぴりぴり.md) overlaps in sharp tingling or burning, especially with spicy food.

## Distinctive meaning

ひりひりする reports a sensation; it does not identify an injury or tell how serious a symptom is. The association with a burning feeling does not mean that physical heat must still be present.
