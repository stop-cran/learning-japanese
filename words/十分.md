# 十分 (じゅうぶん) — enough; sufficient

## Meaning

十分 means enough for a purpose. Use 十分な before a noun. Before a verb, both plain 十分 and 十分に are possible, meaning "enough," "sufficiently," or "thoroughly" as context requires.

- 時間は十分あります。 (じかんはじゅうぶんあります。) — There is plenty of time.
- 十分に休んでください。 (じゅうぶんにやすんでください。) — Please get enough rest.

## How the kanji combine

[十](../kanji/十.md), "ten," and [分](../kanji/分.md), "part," give the on-reading spelling じゅうぶん. The alternative 充分 is also used for this meaning. Treat "full measure" as a memory aid, not a rule for translating each character.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[足りる](足りる.md) is the verb "be enough." 不十分 (ふじゅうぶん) means insufficient. たくさん means a large quantity, which need not be sufficient for the task.

## Distinctive meaning

十分 can also be the written number-and-counter expression "ten minutes," read じゅっぷん or じっぷん, not じゅうぶん. Compare 十分な時間 (じゅうぶんなじかん), "enough time," with 十分待つ (じゅっぷんまつ), "wait ten minutes."
