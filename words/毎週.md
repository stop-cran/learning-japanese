# 毎週 (まいしゅう) - every week

## Meaning

毎週 means "every week" or "weekly." It describes a recurring schedule and can modify a clause without a following particle.

- 毎週、図書館に行きます。 (まいしゅう、としょかんにいきます。) - I go to the library every week.
- 毎週金曜日に日本語を勉強します。 (まいしゅうきんようびににほんごをべんきょうします。) - I study Japanese every Friday.

The second example identifies which weekday the weekly activity happens on.

## How the kanji combine

[毎](../kanji/毎.md), まい, means "every," and [週](../kanji/週.md), しゅう, means "week." Both are on readings in this transparent time expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

毎日 (まいにち) means "every day," and 毎月 (まいつき) means "every month." 来週 (らいしゅう), "next week," names one relative week rather than a repeating frequency.

## Distinctive meaning

毎週 does not itself say "exactly once per week." For that, use 毎週一回 (まいしゅういっかい) or 週に一回 (しゅうにいっかい). Before a noun, の is useful: 毎週の予定 (まいしゅうのよてい), "the weekly schedule."
