# 季節 (きせつ) - season; time of year

## Meaning

季節 means a season or a particular time of year. It covers the yearly cycle of spring, summer, autumn, and winter, and periods associated with characteristic weather or activities.

どの季節が好きですか。 (どのきせつがすきですか。) - Which season do you like?

桜の季節になりました。 (さくらのきせつになりました。) - Cherry-blossom season has arrived.

## How the kanji combine

季 concerns a season, while 節 can indicate a division or turning point. The on-reading compound きせつ names a division of the year. Both characters belong in the written word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

シーズン also means "season," particularly for sports, tourism, and commercial activities. 時期 (じき) means a period or suitable time more broadly, without necessarily referring to an annual season.

## Distinctive meaning

季節 is a noun, so use の to connect it to another noun: 季節の野菜 (きせつのやさい), "seasonal vegetables." The four seasons are contrasting parts of a cycle, not antonyms of 季節 itself.
