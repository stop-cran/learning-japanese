# 会話 (かいわ) — conversation

## Meaning

会話 is conversation, talk between people: 日本語で会話する (converse in Japanese), 英会話 えいかいわ (English conversation), 会話の練習 (conversation practice), 会話が弾む はずむ (the conversation flows).

## How the kanji combine

As a memory aid, associate [会](../kanji/会.md) (meet) and [話](../kanji/話.md) (talk) with people talking together. Both use on readings (カイ + ワ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: おしゃべり (chat, casual), 対話 たいわ (dialogue between parties), 談話 だんわ (talk, statement).
- Verb: [話す](話す.md) はなす (speak). 会話 is a noun that takes する.

## Distinctive meaning

会話 is neutral for spoken exchange and is the word used for language classes (英会話教室). 対話 suggests a deliberate dialogue, for example between countries or between a teacher and a pupil.
