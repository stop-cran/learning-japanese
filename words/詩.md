# 詩 (し) - poem; poetry

## Meaning

詩 is a noun meaning a poem or poetry. The same Japanese noun can refer to an individual work or poetry as a form of writing.

- 日本語で短い詩を書きました。 (にほんごでみじかいしをかきました。) - I wrote a short poem in Japanese.
- 授業でその詩を声に出して読みました。 (じゅぎょうでそのしをこえにだしてよみました。) - We read that poem aloud in class.

を marks the work being written or read. 詩を書く (しをかく) means to write poetry; 詩を読む (しをよむ) means to read it. A poet is 詩人 (しじん).

## How the kanji combine

The single character 詩 supplies the entire noun with the on reading シ. There is no multi-kanji relationship to explain. In 詩人, the added 人 identifies the person who writes poetry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[歌](歌.md) (うた) can refer to a song and also occurs in poetry-related vocabulary, but singing is not required for something to be 詩. [小説](小説.md) (しょうせつ) is a novel or fictional narrative, another literary form rather than an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "verse" need not mean just one line here: 詩 can name a whole poem. Do not infer a fixed syllable count or rhyme scheme from the noun alone. Specific forms such as 俳句 (はいく) have their own names and conventions; the broad word 詩 does not supply all those rules.
