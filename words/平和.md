# 平和 (へいわ) — peace

## Meaning

平和 means peace or harmony. It can name peace between groups or describe a life without conflict and disruption.
As a noun it can stand alone; when describing another noun, use 平和な.

- 世界の平和を願っています。 (せかいのへいわをねがっています。) — I hope for world peace.
- 平和な毎日を過ごしています。 (へいわなまいにちをすごしています。) — I am spending my days peacefully.

Use 平和です (へいわです), not 平和なです, at the end of an ordinary polite statement.

## How the kanji combine

[平](../kanji/平.md) brings the idea of calm or evenness, while [和](../kanji/和.md) brings harmony.
The related ideas combine in the established word for peace. The on readings are ヘイ + ワ.
The "Japanese style" meaning of 和, seen in 和食 (わしょく), is not the meaning used here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[戦争](戦争.md) (せんそう), "war," is the direct contrast when discussing peace between countries.
[静か](静か.md) (しずか) can mean quiet or calm. A quiet room and a peaceful society are not the same claim, though the words overlap in some descriptions of calm surroundings.

## Distinctive meaning

平和 need not describe complete silence. People can talk and celebrate in a peaceful town.
Conversely, merely saying a place is quiet does not establish that it is free from conflict.
Choose the word for the kind of calm you mean, rather than translating every English "peaceful" identically.
