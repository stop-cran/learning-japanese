# 生意気 (なまいき) - cheeky; impertinent

## Meaning

生意気 describes a person, remark or manner judged cheeky, impertinent,
cocky or presumptuous. The speaker sees the behavior as getting above
what the person's position or experience warrants.

- 生意気なことを言って、すみません。 (なまいきなことをいって、すみません。) - I am sorry for speaking presumptuously.
- その生意気な態度に腹が立ちました。 (そのなまいきなたいどにはらがたちました。) - That cheeky attitude made me angry.

It is a na-adjective: 生意気な態度, "an impertinent attitude";
生意気に話す (なまいきにはなす), "speak cockily."
The judgment belongs to the speaker, not to an objective age rule.

## How the kanji combine

The mixed reading combines native [生](../kanji/生.md) なま with
the on readings [意](../kanji/意.md) イ and [気](../kanji/気.md) キ.
Although 意 and 気 have intention and spirit associations, learn the
whole conventional word. "Raw intention spirit" is not a reliable
definition or a demonstrated historical explanation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずうずうしい](ずうずうしい.md) and
[厚かましい](厚かましい.md) (あつかましい) overlap in shameless,
brazen behavior. 生意気 can focus instead on cockiness or talking as
though one knows better. Modest behavior can provide a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Children can be called 生意気, but the word is not limited to children.
It can criticize an adult's manner as well. Do not translate it as
unqualified praise for courage or confidence. Even if a speaker uses it
teasingly, calling someone 生意気 can offend; tone and relationship
matter.
