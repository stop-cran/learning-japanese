# なるほど -- I see; that makes sense

## Meaning

なるほど acknowledges that an explanation makes sense or that something is now understood. It can stand as a response or introduce the speaker's reaction.

- このボタンを押すと、ドアが開きます。なるほど。 (このボタンをおすと、ドアがあきます。なるほど。) -- Pressing this button opens the door. I see.
- なるほど、それで遅れたんですね。 (なるほど、それでおくれたんですね。) -- I see; that's why you were late.

It does not conjugate like a verb meaning "understand."

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in hiragana. The dictionary also lists 成る程, but there is no need to replace the ordinary kana spelling in everyday writing. Learn the whole expression rather than translating its parts literally.

## Synonyms and antonyms

そうですか acknowledges new information. 分かりました (わかりました) directly states that the speaker has understood and can also acknowledge an instruction. Their conversational functions overlap without being identical.

## Distinctive meaning

なるほど often suggests that the speaker sees the logic behind something. It is not automatically a promise to act, an apology, or wholehearted agreement with a proposal. Choose the response that conveys the intended commitment.
