# 輸送 (ゆそう) - transport; transportation

## Meaning

輸送 is transporting people or goods, especially by vehicles,
ships or aircraft. As a transitive する verb, it takes the transported
people or goods with を.

- トラックで食料を輸送しました。 (トラックでしょくりょうをゆそうしました。) - We transported food by truck.
- この船は島まで乗客を輸送します。 (このふねはしままでじょうきゃくをゆそうします。) - This ship transports passengers to the island.

## How the kanji combine

[輸](../kanji/輸.md), transport or transfer, and
[送](../kanji/送.md), send, reinforce the movement meaning.
The on readings are ユ + ソウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[運ぶ](運ぶ.md) (はこぶ) is the broad everyday carry/transport verb.
[交通](交通.md) (こうつう) covers traffic and transportation.
[郵送](郵送.md) (ゆうそう) specifies sending by post;
[配達](配達.md) (はいたつ) emphasizes delivery to a destination.
[届ける](届ける.md) (とどける) overlaps in delivering.

## Distinctive meaning

輸送 foregrounds the transport operation, not only the final arrival.
It is not limited to international trade or mail.
[交通機関](交通機関.md) (こうつうきかん) names a means or system of
transport, while 輸送 focuses on moving people or goods.
English "transport" or "transportation" can describe either, so the
English gloss alone does not establish their different Japanese uses.
There is no single direct antonym for every transport operation.
