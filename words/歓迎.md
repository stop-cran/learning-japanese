# 歓迎 (かんげい) - welcome; warm reception

## Meaning

歓迎 is a welcome or favorable reception. 歓迎する is transitive:
**person or thing を歓迎する**, "welcome someone or something."
It can describe welcoming visitors or receiving a suggestion positively.

- 新しい先生をみんなで歓迎しました。 (あたらしいせんせいをみんなでかんげいしました。) - We all welcomed the new teacher.
- ご意見を歓迎します。 (ごいけんをかんげいします。) - We welcome your opinions.

The second example expresses willingness to receive input; it is not about
greeting an arriving person.

## How the kanji combine

歓 contributes joy or delight, and [迎](../kanji/迎.md) contributes receiving
or meeting someone who comes. Together they suggest receiving gladly.
The on readings combine as かん + げい. This meaning guide does not explain
the historical shapes of the characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[招待](招待.md) (しょうたい) means inviting someone. You may invite guests
and then welcome them, but those are different actions. [喜ぶ](喜ぶ.md)
(よろこぶ), "be pleased," describes a feeling rather than necessarily receiving
someone. Refusing someone entry would contrast with a welcome, but is not
implied by the absence of a celebration.

## Distinctive meaning

歓迎会 (かんげいかい) is a welcome party or gathering. 歓迎 itself does not
require a party: a friendly reception or an expressed willingness to accept
something can count. Do not turn the English "reception" into a blanket meaning
for a front desk; this word concerns how someone or something is received.
