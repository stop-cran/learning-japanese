# 改造 (かいぞう) - remodeling; modification; reorganization

## Meaning

改造 is remodeling, modifying, or reorganizing something. It can concern a physical object's construction or functions, or a larger organization. 改造する is transitive: the thing changed takes を.

- 古い車を改造しました。 (ふるいくるまをかいぞうしました。) - I modified an old car.
- 内閣の改造が発表されました。 (ないかくのかいぞうがはっぴょうされました。) - A cabinet reshuffle was announced.

In computing contexts, 改造 also covers modding, such as altering a game's behavior. This is a context-specific use of the same modification idea.

## How the kanji combine

[改](../kanji/改.md), "revise; change," combines with [造](../kanji/造.md), "make; construct." The association is changing how something is made or put together, rather than merely producing it unchanged. The on readings are カイ + ゾウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[変更](変更.md) (へんこう), "change," is broader. 改築 (かいちく) concerns rebuilding or structural alteration of a building; 改装 (かいそう) concerns remodeling or refitting. Their scope overlaps with 改造 rather than establishing an absolute partition.

[修理](修理.md) (しゅうり), "repair," focuses on fixing a fault or damage. 改造 does not require the original object to have been broken.

## Distinctive meaning

The object determines the scale: a car modification and a cabinet reshuffle are not the same practical action. Do not translate every 改造 as a repair or assume it necessarily makes the result better. Nor is it a general word for constructing something new from nothing.
