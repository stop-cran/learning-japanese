# 供給 (きょうきゅう) - supply; provision

## Meaning

供給 names the provision of something needed, such as water, electricity, or goods.
供給する is transitive: XにYを供給する means "supply Y to X."
As an economics noun, 供給 is "supply" in the supply-and-demand relationship.

- この施設は町に水を供給しています。 (このしせつはまちにみずをきょうきゅうしています。) - This facility supplies water to the town.
- 需要が増えて、供給が追いつきません。 (じゅようがふえて、きょうきゅうがおいつきません。) - Demand has increased, and supply cannot keep up.

水の供給 (みずのきょうきゅう) means "the water supply" or "the supply of water."
The passive 供給される means "be supplied."

## How the kanji combine

[供](../kanji/供.md) contributes providing or offering, and [給](../kanji/給.md) contributes supplying.
The two characters reinforce the provision meaning. Their on-readings are キョウ + キュウ;
both syllables have long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[与える](与える.md) (あたえる), "give," is broader: a single gift does not necessarily constitute a supply.
[需要](需要.md) (じゅよう), "demand," is the paired economic contrast, not a verb meaning to take supplies away.
[不足](不足.md) (ふそく) describes shortage rather than the act of supplying.

## Distinctive meaning

供給 emphasizes making a resource available to users or a destination.
It does not mean "service" in every English sense: helping a customer politely is not automatically 供給.
The resource and recipient clarify the meaning much better than the isolated English gloss.
