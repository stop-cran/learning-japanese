# カバー (カバー) - cover; covering; make up for

## Meaning

カバー is a cover or covering. With する, it also describes covering a range, performing a cover version of a song, or compensating for a shortfall or another person's absence.

- 本にカバーを掛けました。 (ほんにカバーをかけました。) - I put a cover on the book.
- このバンドは昔の歌をカバーしています。 (このバンドはむかしのうたをカバーしています。) - This band performs covers of old songs.
- この地図は市内全域をカバーしています。 (このちずはしないぜんいきをカバーしています。) - This map covers the whole city.
- 欠席した同僚の仕事をカバーしました。 (けっせきしたどうりょうのしごとをカバーしました。) - I covered the work of an absent colleague.

The object determines whether the covering is physical or figurative.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "cover," with no written kanji. Keep the long vowel in バー. カヴァー is another dictionary spelling, not a separate kind of cover.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[覆う](覆う.md) (おおう) overlaps in physical covering. [補う](補う.md) (おぎなう) overlaps in making up for a lack. [被せる](被せる.md) (かぶせる) can describe putting something over something else; its constructions differ from simply adding する to カバー.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer every English use of "cover" from this loanword. A protective covering, a song performance, an area included, and support for someone else are distinct established uses. A book's カバー is not necessarily identical to its 表紙 (ひょうし), the book cover itself.
