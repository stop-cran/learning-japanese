# 何 (なに・なん) — what

## Meaning

何 asks "what." Both なに and なん are essential readings; the surrounding expression determines which to use.

- 何を食べますか。 (なにをたべますか。) — What will you eat?
- これは何ですか。 (これはなんですか。) — What is this?

なに is usual before を and が in basic questions: 何がほしいですか (なにがほしいですか), "What do you want?" Use なん before です in 何ですか, and in many counter expressions: 何人 (なんにん), "how many people," and 何時 (なんじ), "what time."

## How the kanji combine

[何](../kanji/何.md) represents the question word. This single-character spelling serves both readings; they are not freely interchangeable pronunciations in every phrase.

## Synonyms and antonyms

誰 (だれ) asks "who," どこ asks "where," and どれ asks "which one." These request different kinds of information rather than opposing 何.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not apply one reading to every compound. For instance, 何曜日 (なんようび) asks which day of the week, while 何色 (なにいろ) asks what color. Learn frequent expressions with their readings.
