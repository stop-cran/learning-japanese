# コンクリート - concrete (building material)

## Meaning

コンクリート is the building material concrete. It is made by mixing cement, water, sand, and aggregate such as gravel, then allowing the mixture to harden.

- この壁はコンクリートでできています。 (このかべはコンクリートでできています。) - This wall is made of concrete.
- コンクリートの床は冷たいです。 (コンクリートのゆかはつめたいです。) - The concrete floor is cold.

Use コンクリートの + noun to describe a concrete object or surface.

## How the kanji combine

This is a borrowing from English **concrete**. The rare spelling 混凝土 is also recorded, but it is not the practical default for writing the word; the primary katakana spelling has no written kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

セメント (cement) is a binder used in concrete, not a name for every finished concrete mixture. 鉄筋コンクリート (てっきんコンクリート) means reinforced concrete, with steel reinforcement.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not import English concrete's adjective meaning "specific" into this noun. For that meaning, recognize [具体](具体.md) (ぐたい) in 具体的な説明 (ぐたいてきなせつめい), "a specific/concrete explanation." コンクリート names the construction material in the examples above.
