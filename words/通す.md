# 通す (とおす) - let through; carry through

## Meaning

通す is a transitive godan verb for letting or putting something through. Mark what passes with を; a destination or opening can take に.

- 車を通すために、道をあけました。 (くるまをとおすために、みちをあけました。) - I cleared the way to let a car through.
- 針に糸を通します。 (はりにいとをとおします。) - I thread a needle.

人を部屋に通す (ひとをへやにとおす) means showing someone into a room. Here the person is the object, and the room is the destination.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) concerns passage or connection; す completes とおす. The reading has two お sounds, not とうす. Forms include 通します (とおします) and 通した (とおした).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[通る](通る.md) (とおる) describes passing: 車が通る, "a car passes." 通す describes letting it pass: 車を通す. [続ける](続ける.md) (つづける) overlaps with continuation, but does not necessarily imply reaching the end.

## Distinctive meaning

After a verb's ます-stem, ～通す means doing it through to the end: 最後まで読み通しました (さいごまでよみとおしました), "I read it through to the end." Separately, 通して練習する (とおしてれんしゅうする) means practicing through from beginning to end without a break.

Do not learn the source's bare "overlook" as "fail to notice." A contextual idea of letting something pass is different from not seeing a fault at all.
