# 企業 (きぎょう) - business enterprise; company

## Meaning

企業 is a noun for a business enterprise or company. It is useful when discussing businesses as economic organizations, their activities, or their sizes.

- この企業は医療機器を作っています。 (このきぎょうはいりょうききをつくっています。) - This company makes medical equipment.
- 地元の企業で働きたいです。 (じもとのきぎょうではたらきたいです。) - I want to work for a local business.

企業で働く uses で for the organization where someone works. 企業の活動 (きぎょうのかつどう) means "a company's activities."

## How the kanji combine

企 carries a planning or undertaking association; [業](../kanji/業.md) concerns work or business. Together they name an organized business undertaking. The readings are キ + ギョウ; keep the long vowel in ぎょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会社](会社.md) (かいしゃ), "company," overlaps strongly. 企業 is useful for discussing the business entity or sector without focusing on a particular workplace.

[工場](工場.md) (こうじょう), "factory," names a production facility. A business can operate several factories.

## Distinctive meaning

大企業 (だいきぎょう) means a large enterprise; 中小企業 (ちゅうしょうきぎょう) refers to small and medium-sized enterprises. 企業 does not inherently mean a giant corporation.

Do not confuse it with 起業 (きぎょう), "starting a business." That same-sounding word names the startup act; 企業 in this card names the enterprise itself, not any undertaking such as a household chore.
