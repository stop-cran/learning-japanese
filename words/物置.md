# 物置 (ものおき) - storeroom; storage shed

## Meaning

物置 is a place for keeping things not currently in use:
a storeroom or storage shed. It does not specify that the building is large.
物置にしまう (ものおきにしまう) means to put something away in storage.

- 自転車を物置に入れました。 (じてんしゃをものおきにいれました。) - I put the bicycle in the storage shed.
- 庭の物置に道具があります。 (にわのものおきにどうぐがあります。) - The tools are in the garden shed.

に identifies the storage location or destination.
物置の中 (ものおきのなか) means inside the storeroom or shed.

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md), things, combines with [置](../kanji/置.md),
associated with putting or placing. As a meaning aid, it is a place to put things.
The native readings are もの + おき, related to 置く (おく).
The noun is written 物置 here, without the verb ending く.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[倉庫](倉庫.md) (そうこ) is a storehouse or warehouse and overlaps in storage.
[小屋](小屋.md) (こや) names a hut or shed more broadly.
[押し入れ](押し入れ.md) (おしいれ) is a built-in closet, not necessarily a separate storage room.

## Distinctive meaning

The focus is the place used to store things, not the abstract activity of storage.
[貯蔵](貯蔵.md) (ちょぞう) concerns storing supplies or goods.
A garden building is not automatically a 物置 if it serves a different purpose.
