# じたばた — flailing; struggling in a panic

## Meaning

じたばた describes flailing or making a confused, ineffective effort to get out of a difficult
situation.

- 締め切りの直前にじたばたしても、仕上がりはよくならない。
  — Flailing around just before the deadline will not improve the result.
- 道に迷ってじたばたするより、地図を確認しよう。
  — Rather than panic when lost, let's check the map.

## Synonyms and antonyms

じたばた emphasizes restless, unproductive struggling. ばたばた has broader uses for busy,
noisy, or repeated movement.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can describe literal flailing or a panicky response. These examples focus on struggling
without making useful progress.
