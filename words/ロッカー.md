# ロッカー - locker

## Meaning

ロッカー is a locker: a storage cabinet or compartment,
often one assigned to an individual in a school, workplace or gym.
コインロッカー is a coin-operated locker.

- 荷物をロッカーに入れました。 (にもつをロッカーにいれました。) - I put my belongings in a locker.
- ロッカーの鍵を返してください。 (ロッカーのかぎをかえしてください。) - Please return the locker key.

に marks the destination in the first example; の connects
the locker to its key in the second.

## How the kanji combine

The storage word comes from English locker and is written
in katakana. The small ッ and the long カー are both part
of the word: ロッカー.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[戸棚](戸棚.md) (とだな) is a cupboard or cabinet and can
also translate as locker. [棚](棚.md) (たな) is shelving
more generally, including open shelves.
Not every storage space is a ロッカー.

## Distinctive meaning

A separate dictionary identity, also written ロッカー,
means a rocker or rock-and-roll musician.
That English source is rocker, not locker; shared Japanese
spelling does not turn a storage cabinet into a musician.
This card's N2 mapping is the locker identity.
