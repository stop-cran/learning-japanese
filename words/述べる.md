# 述べる (のべる) - state; express in words

## Meaning

述べる means to state or express something in words. It is a transitive ichidan verb: **内容を述べる**, with を marking the content expressed. Forms include 述べます, 述べて, and 述べた.

- 会議で自分の意見を述べました。 (かいぎでじぶんのいけんをのべました。) - I stated my opinion at the meeting.
- その理由を簡単に述べてください。 (そのりゆうをかんたんにのべてください。) - Please briefly state the reason.

It also reports a statement with **clause + と述べる**. The content can be spoken or written; the verb does not by itself select one medium.

## How the kanji combine

述 supplies the stating or expressing meaning, with the native reading のべる. The kana べる complete the written verb. Other kanji spellings are recorded as rare; 述べる is the spelling taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[言う](言う.md) (いう) is the broad verb "say." 述べる draws attention to articulating content, such as an opinion or explanation. [黙る](黙る.md) (だまる), "say nothing," can contrast with stating one's view, but is not a grammatical opposite in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

述べる fits reports, presentations, and requests to set out a point. It does not guarantee that the statement is long, true, or persuasive: 簡単に述べる explicitly asks for brevity. Do not use it as a general verb for expressing emotion without words.
