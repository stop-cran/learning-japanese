# 数学 (すうがく) - mathematics

## Meaning

数学 is mathematics, either the field of study or a school subject.
It includes more than arithmetic: numbers, shapes, equations, and their relationships.

- 今日は数学の試験があります。 (きょうはすうがくのしけんがあります。) - There is a mathematics exam today.

For studying it, say 数学を勉強する (すうがくをべんきょうする), with を marking
the subject studied. 数学 alone is not a する verb.

## How the kanji combine

[数](../kanji/数.md), "number," combines with [学](../kanji/学.md), "study;
learning." The on-readings スウ + ガク produce すうがく. Keep the long う
sound in the first part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

算数 (さんすう) names elementary-school arithmetic/mathematics in Japan.
計算 (けいさん) is calculation, an activity within mathematics rather than
the whole subject. Neither is an opposite of 数学.

## Distinctive meaning

Use の when attaching the subject to another noun: 数学の先生
(すうがくのせんせい), "mathematics teacher." A source gloss "arithmetic" gives
only part of the range; translating 数学 simply as mathematics is safer.
