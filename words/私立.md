# 私立 (しりつ) - privately established institution

## Meaning

私立 describes private establishment and operation of an institution,
especially a school. It is a noun and の-modifier. 私立大学
(しりつだいがく) means private university.

- 妹は私立の学校に通っています。 (いもうとはしりつのがっこうにかよっています。) - My younger sister attends a private school.
- この大学は私立です。 (このだいがくはしりつです。) - This university is privately established.

私立 can also stand for a private school when that context is understood.

## How the kanji combine

[私](../kanji/私.md) contributes private, and [立](../kanji/立.md)
contributes establishment here. The first character specifies the kind
of establishment; it does not describe physically standing alone.
The on readings are シ + リツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[公立](公立.md) (こうりつ) normally concerns a local public body's
institution; [国立](国立.md) (こくりつ) concerns national establishment.
These contrast with 私立 in the institutional classification.
市立 (しりつ) means municipally established, not privately established.

## Distinctive meaning

To distinguish 私立 from same-sounding 市立 in speech, 私立 can be read
わたくしりつ; 市立 can be read いちりつ. These disambiguating readings
do not make ordinary しりつ incorrect.
Private establishment does not imply secrecy, restricted admission to
the premises, or a particular fee in every case.
