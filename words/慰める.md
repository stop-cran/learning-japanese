# 慰める (なぐさめる) - comfort; console

## Meaning

慰める means comforting or consoling someone who is sad, disappointed or
distressed. It is a transitive ichidan verb; the person comforted takes を.

- 泣いている友達を慰めました。 (ないているともだちをなぐさめました。) - I comforted my crying friend.
- 母の言葉に慰められました。 (ははのことばになぐさめられました。) - I was comforted by my mother's words.

The second sentence is passive, 慰められる: the speaker receives comfort.
Forms of the active verb include 慰めます, 慰めない and 慰めた.

## How the kanji combine

慰 writes なぐさ, with める in kana. This is one native verb, not a
compound of on readings. The character's consoling association helps
recall the whole word; it does not specify what method of comfort is used.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[助ける](助ける.md) (たすける) is broader help or rescue.
励ます (はげます), "encourage," can urge someone onward;
慰める emphasizes easing their distress. Both actions can occur together,
but encouragement and consolation are not interchangeable in every situation.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb can also concern soothing one's own feelings or providing diversion,
as in 心を慰める (こころをなぐさめる), "soothe one's heart."
The dictionary gloss "amuse" does not make this the ordinary verb for every
fun activity. Comforting someone does not guarantee that their problem is
solved or that they immediately stop feeling sad.
