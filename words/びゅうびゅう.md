# びゅうびゅう — wind howling or whistling

## Meaning

A sustained, repeated whistling or howling sound, especially from strong wind.

- 外では風がびゅうびゅう吹（ふ）いている。 — The wind is howling outside.
- すき間（ま）からびゅうびゅうと風の音が聞こえた。 — I heard wind whistling through the gap.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[そよそよ](そよそよ.md) describes a gentle breeze. The inspected dictionary also groups ひゅうひゅう and ビュービュー with びゅうびゅう; this collection does not create duplicate cards for those recorded variants.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn びゅうびゅうと吹く with its wind scene. The dictionary also records wheezing-like sounds, so “wind” is the primary lesson, not the only possible source of the sound.
