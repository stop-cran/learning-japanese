# 水素 (すいそ) - hydrogen

## Meaning

水素 is hydrogen, the chemical element with the symbol H. In ordinary discussions of gases, it can refer to hydrogen gas.

- 水素の元素記号は H です。 (すいそのげんそきごうは H です。) - The chemical symbol for hydrogen is H.
- 水は水素と酸素からできています。 (みずはすいそとさんそからできています。) - Water is made up of hydrogen and oxygen.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md) contributes water, and 素 contributes a basic constituent or element. The readings are スイ + ソ. The name associates the element with water; it does not mean that hydrogen itself is water.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[酸素](酸素.md), oxygen, is another element, not an opposite of hydrogen. [水](水.md), water, is a compound containing both elements, not another name for either one.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the element and the substance containing it separate. The 水 in 水素 is not read みず, and 水素 is not a general term for moisture, a water droplet, or every gas.
