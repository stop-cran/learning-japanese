# 展開 (てんかい) - unfolding; development; expansion

## Meaning

展開 describes something unfolding, developing, or spreading out. It can concern a story's progression, an argument, an activity, or deployment over an area. It is a noun and a する verb.

- 物語は意外な展開を見せました。 (ものがたりはいがいなてんかいをみせました。) - The story took an unexpected turn.
- 会社は海外で事業を展開しています。 (かいしゃはかいがいでじぎょうをてんかいしています。) - The company is developing its business overseas.

Both perspectives occur: 事件が展開する (じけんがてんかいする), "events unfold," and 議論を展開する (ぎろんをてんかいする), "develop an argument."

## How the kanji combine

展 contributes spreading or unfolding, and [開](../kanji/開.md) opening. These character meanings support the idea of opening out; the on readings are テン + カイ. The word need not describe a physical object being opened.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[発展](発展.md) and [発達](発達.md) overlap in development; [拡大](拡大.md) and [拡張](拡張.md) concern expansion. 展開 does not necessarily imply improvement, so it is not interchangeable with [進歩](進歩.md) in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

Technical uses include expanding an algebraic expression, such as `a(b + c) = ab + ac`, and unfolding a solid into a flat net, as in 展開図 (てんかいず). In computing it can mean decompressing a file: ファイルを展開する. In that specific context 圧縮 (あっしゅく), "compression," supplies a contrast, not a universal antonym for every 展開. A further business sense concerns sharing information; context determines the intended specialized use.
