# おかけください - please have a seat

## Meaning

おかけください is a polite invitation to sit down.
The seat or place can be marked with に.

- どうぞ、こちらにおかけください。 - Please have a seat here.
- 椅子におかけください。 (いすにおかけください。) - Please sit on the chair.

It addresses the person being invited to sit; it does not announce
that the speaker is sitting down.

## How the kanji combine

The dictionary also records お掛け下さい and おかけ下さい, but marks
the expression as usually kana-written.
[掛](../kanji/掛.md) belongs to かける; [下](../kanji/下.md) occurs in 下さい.
Here お + the stem かけ + ください forms a respectful request.
The sitting sense is related to 腰を掛ける (こしをかける), "sit down,"
not the hanging sense introduced in [掛ける](掛ける.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[座る](座る.md) (すわる) is the ordinary sitting verb.
座ってください (すわってください) also requests that someone sit.
[どうぞ](どうぞ.md), "please; go ahead," can accompany the invitation.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn this as a complete seating invitation. Do not interpret it as
"please hang something" merely because かける has that meaning elsewhere,
or substitute すわり into this expression without considering the construction.
