# 裸 (はだか) - naked; bare

## Meaning

裸 is a noun for nakedness or a bare, uncovered state. Use 裸です as a predicate, 裸の before a noun, and 裸で to describe doing something while unclothed. It is not an い-adjective.

- 温泉に入る前に、裸になります。 (おんせんにはいるまえに、はだかになります。) - I undress before entering the hot spring bath.
- 裸で外に出ないでください。 (はだかでそとにでないでください。) - Please do not go outside naked.

上半身が裸 (じょうはんしんがはだか) means "bare-chested" or "with the upper body unclothed." Specifying the body part limits the description.

## How the kanji combine

裸 is one character carrying the whole native reading はだか. There is no written kana ending and no multi-character compound. Its meaning concerns lacking clothing or covering.

## Synonyms and antonyms

服を着ている (ふくをきている), "wearing [clothes](服.md)," supplies the practical contrast. 裸足 (はだし), "barefoot," specifically concerns feet; it does not mean that the whole person is naked.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can also describe an object without its covering or wrapping, so context matters. Do not automatically translate all uses as total human nudity. The grammar remains useful: 裸の, not 裸な, is the basic noun-modifying pattern taught here.
