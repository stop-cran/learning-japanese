# 現象 (げんしょう) - phenomenon

## Meaning

現象 is a phenomenon: something that occurs and can be observed or described.
It can concern nature, society, or another observable process.
Useful noun combinations include 現象が起こる (げんしょうがおこる), "a phenomenon occurs,"
and 現象を調べる (げんしょうをしらべる), "investigate a phenomenon."

- 虹は自然の現象です。
  (にじはしぜんのげんしょうです。) - A rainbow is a natural phenomenon.
- 同じ現象が別の場所でも起こりました。
  (おなじげんしょうがべつのばしょでもおこりました。)
  - The same phenomenon occurred in another place too.

不思議な現象 (ふしぎなげんしょう) is "a puzzling phenomenon," but the noun
alone does not say that an occurrence is mysterious.

## How the kanji combine

[現](../kanji/現.md) contributes appearing or becoming apparent, and 象 contributes a
form or appearance. Together they name an observable occurrence.
The on readings are ゲン + ショウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出来事](出来事.md) (できごと) is an event or happening.
現象 can frame what happens as an observable process or pattern.
[原因](原因.md) (げんいん), "cause," is a related concept, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Naming a 現象 is not the same as explaining its cause.
The word works for an ordinary rainbow as well as something initially unexplained.
It also need not refer to a one-time event: the second example concerns the same
kind of occurrence in two places.
