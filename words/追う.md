# 追う (おう) — chase; pursue

## Meaning

追う means to chase or follow after someone or something. The pursued target is marked with を.

- 犬がボールを追っています。 (いぬがボールをおっています。) — The dog is chasing the ball.
- 警官が犯人を追いました。 (けいかんがはんにんをおいました。) — The police officer pursued the offender.

The polite form is 追います and the て-form is 追って.
Although the dictionary form ends in う, the negative is 追わない (おわない), not おあない.

## How the kanji combine

[追](../kanji/追.md) supplies the chasing or following meaning.
This is a native verb with the reading おう; the final う is written in kana.
Do not confuse it with the different verb 負う (おう), "bear; shoulder."

## Synonyms and antonyms

追いかける (おいかける) also means to chase or run after something and overlaps with the pursuit use here.
[逃げる](逃げる.md) (にげる), "flee," can describe the other participant's action:
the pursuer chases, while the person pursued may flee.
These are different viewpoints, not verbs that can replace one another.

## Distinctive meaning

追う tells you about pursuit, not its successful completion.
A sentence saying that someone chased another person does not establish that they caught that person.
Keep the target marked with を even when English uses "after."
