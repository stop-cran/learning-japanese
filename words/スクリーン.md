# スクリーン — screen; projection surface

## Meaning

スクリーン means a screen, especially the surface on which a film, slide, or other image is projected.

- 写真をスクリーンに映しました。 (しゃしんをスクリーンにうつしました。) — I projected the photograph onto the screen.
- 大きなスクリーンで映画を見ました。 (おおきなスクリーンでえいがをみました。) — I watched a film on a large screen.

Use に for the surface an image is projected onto.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "screen." There is no kanji decomposition. The long-vowel mark gives リー in スクリーン; it is part of the word's spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

画面 (がめん) is the screen or displayed image of a television, computer, or phone. モニター often names a display device. These overlap in some contexts but emphasize different things.

## Distinctive meaning

スクリーン is particularly useful for cinema and projection. It also has other dictionary uses, including partitions, but it is not a universal translation for every English "screen" or the verb "screen." For an ordinary phone display, 画面 is a useful default.
