# 煙い (けむい) - smoky

## Meaning

煙い describes a smoky condition, often when smoke makes a place uncomfortable.
It is an i-adjective: 煙くない, not smoky; 煙かった, was smoky.

- 煙くて、目を開けていられません。 (けむくて、めをあけていられません。) - It is so smoky that I cannot keep my eyes open.
- 部屋が煙いので、窓を開けました。 (へやがけむいので、まどをあけました。) - I opened the window because the room was smoky.

## How the kanji combine

[煙](../kanji/煙.md) contributes smoke; the native reading boundary is けむ.い.
The final い is the adjective's written ending.
There is only one kanji here, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[煙](煙.md) (けむり) is the noun "smoke."
[もくもく](もくもく.md) describes smoke or clouds billowing; it does not itself mean that the room feels smoky.
煙くない is the straightforward negative of the adjective.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse the noun けむり with the adjective けむい.
A visible white cloud is not automatically 煙い: the adjective concerns a smoky condition, not just a shape.
JMdict also records the spelling 烟い and the reading けぶい; the primary form here is 煙い けむい.
These alternatives are not evidence that every combination has the same frequency.
