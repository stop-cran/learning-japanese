# 応じる (おうじる) - to respond; comply

## Meaning

応じる is an intransitive ichidan verb meaning "respond to," "accept," or "comply with."
The request, invitation, or situation responded to takes に. Useful forms are 応じます,
応じて, and 応じない.

- 会社は客の要求に応じました。 (かいしゃはきゃくのようきゅうにおうじました。) - The company complied with the customer's request.
- 経験に応じて仕事を任せます。 (けいけんにおうじてしごとをまかせます。) - We assign work according to experience.

In the first example, 応じる goes beyond merely hearing the request.
In the second, Nに応じて means "according to N" or "in a way suited to N."
必要に応じて (ひつようにおうじて) is "as needed."

## How the kanji combine

応 supplies the idea of responding or answering and the on reading オウ.
The ending じる makes a verb; this is not the native reading of a multi-kanji compound.
Keep the long おう in おうじる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[返事](返事.md) (へんじ) concerns a reply. A person can reply to a request while declining it,
so 返事をする and 要求に応じる do not necessarily report the same outcome.
[従う](従う.md) (したがう), "follow; obey," overlaps when complying with instructions.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun before に helps select the English translation. An invitation can be accepted,
a demand met, and work adjusted to someone's experience. Do not reduce every example to
"answer a question," or transfer the English verb's object pattern to Japanese as 要求を応じる.
