# 虎 (とら) - tiger

## Meaning

虎 is a tiger. It is a noun naming the animal, without specifying its sex or whether it is wild or captive.

- 動物園で虎を見ました。 (どうぶつえんでとらをみました。) - I saw a tiger at the zoo.
- 虎が木の下で休んでいます。 (とらがきのしたでやすんでいます。) - A tiger is resting under a tree.

The first sentence makes the tiger the を-marked object of seeing; the second makes it the が-marked subject. For the presence of a living tiger, use いる.

## How the kanji combine

虎 represents the whole native noun とら. There is no multi-kanji compound to split. The katakana spelling トラ is also dictionary-supported; katakana here does not mean that the animal name must be an English loanword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," names the broader category. [猫](猫.md) (ねこ), "cat," is a useful familiar comparison but is not a substitute for the species name in the zoo example. There is no natural general antonym for tiger.

## Distinctive meaning

The kanji and kana spellings can identify the same animal. In a simple sentence, the surrounding particles and predicate are more important for interpretation than the choice between 虎 and トラ. This card focuses on the animal meaning, not every figurative or colloquial use of the word.
