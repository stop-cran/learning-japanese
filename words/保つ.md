# 保つ (たもつ) - maintain; preserve

## Meaning

保つ means to maintain, preserve or retain something. This article focuses on the transitive たもつ: what is kept in a state takes を.

- 健康を保つために毎日歩いています。 (けんこうをたもつためにまいにちあるいています。) - I walk every day to maintain my health.
- 部屋の温度を一定に保ってください。 (へやのおんどをいっていにたもってください。) - Please keep the room temperature constant.

In the second example, 一定に (いっていに) specifies the state maintained.

## How the kanji combine

[保](../kanji/保.md) supplies たも, and つ is the written ending. The godan forms are 保ちます (たもちます), 保って (たもって) and 保たない (たもたない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[維持](維持.md) (いじ), with する, also concerns maintaining a condition. [保存](保存.md) (ほぞん), with する, covers preservation or storage. [持つ](持つ.md) (もつ) includes holding and possessing; it does not mean that every ordinary "have" sentence should use 保つ.

## Distinctive meaning

Keeping a state may require continuing effort. 保つ does not by itself mean repairing something, improving it, or owning it. The object and any に-phrase tell you what is being maintained and in what state.
