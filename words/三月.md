# 三月 (さんがつ) — March

## Meaning

三月 is March: 三月三日 さんがつみっか (March 3rd), 三月に卒業する (graduate in March).

## How the kanji combine

[三](../kanji/三.md) (three) plus [月](../kanji/月.md) (month): the third month. Months are the on-reading number plus がつ. 三 is サン as usual; there is no sound change before がつ.
The duration "three months" is 三か月 さんかげつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 弥生 やよい (old name of the third month).
- Neighbours: 二月 にがつ (February) and 四月 しがつ (April).

## Distinctive meaning

三月 is the calendar month; 三か月 is a span of time. Compare 三日 みっか (date) with 三月 さんがつ.
