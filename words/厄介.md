# 厄介 (やっかい) - troublesome; burden; care

## Meaning

厄介 is a na-adjective and noun for something troublesome, burdensome, or bothersome. A separate use concerns receiving someone's care or support, especially in **Nの厄介になる**.

- 厄介な問題が起こりました。 (やっかいなもんだいがおこりました。) - A troublesome problem arose.
- しばらく叔父の厄介になりました。 (しばらくおじのやっかいになりました。) - I stayed with my uncle and depended on his support for a while.

Use 厄介な + noun or 厄介だ / 厄介です. The second example does not call the uncle a nuisance.

## How the kanji combine

厄 suggests misfortune or trouble; 介 is associated with mediation or involvement. Learn the established combination as a unit for trouble and dependence on care, rather than as a literal equation of two English glosses. The reading やっかい includes a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[面倒](面倒.md) (めんどう) overlaps in both trouble and looking after someone. [難しい](難しい.md) (むずかしい), "difficult," can describe a problem's difficulty without necessarily emphasizing the burden of dealing with it.

## Distinctive meaning

The construction determines the perspective. 厄介な人 (やっかいなひと) describes a troublesome person, whereas 人の厄介になる describes receiving that person's care or support. Do not translate every use as "nuisance" and reverse who is helping whom. The latter expression acknowledges dependence rather than serving as a neutral statement of location alone.
