# ため息 (ためいき) - sigh

## Meaning

ため息 is a sigh, a noticeably deep outward breath associated
with feelings such as disappointment, worry, admiration, or relief.
Learn **ため息をつく**, "let out a sigh."

- 結果を見て、ため息をつきました。
  (けっかをみて、ためいきをつきました。) - I sighed when I saw the result.
- その美しい景色に、思わずため息が出ました。
  (そのうつくしいけしきに、おもわずためいきがでました。) - The beautiful scenery made me sigh involuntarily.

ため息が出る presents the sigh as coming out. The surrounding
sentence identifies the feeling; a sigh is not always disappointment.

## How the kanji combine

[息](../kanji/息.md) contributes breath and is read いき.
The beginning is written ため here. **溜息** and **溜め息**,
both ためいき, are recognized spellings of the same word.
The primary spelling ため息 keeps only 息 as a written component.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[息](息.md) (いき) is breath more generally, and
[呼吸](呼吸.md) (こきゅう) is breathing.
[はあ](はあ.md) can represent the sound of a sigh;
[ほっと](ほっと.md) can describe breathing out in relief.
They are not all interchangeable noun constructions.

## Distinctive meaning

ため息をつく need not produce a written sound such as はあ.
It describes the breath itself and its emotional context.
Do not assume that someone sighing is necessarily annoyed with
the listener, or that every ordinary exhalation is a ため息.
