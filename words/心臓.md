# 心臓 (しんぞう) - heart

## Meaning

心臓 is the heart as a bodily organ. It is a noun. 心臓が動く (しんぞうがうごく) describes the heart functioning or beating, and 心臓の病気 (しんぞうのびょうき) means heart disease.

- 心臓は血液を全身に送ります。 (しんぞうはけつえきをぜんしんにおくります。) - The heart sends blood throughout the body.
- 走った後、心臓がどきどきしました。 (はしったあと、しんぞうがどきどきしました。) - My heart pounded after I ran.

The second sentence describes the physical sensation of a heartbeat, not simply an abstract emotion.

## How the kanji combine

[心](../kanji/心.md) contributes heart, and 臓 contributes an internal organ. Together they identify the bodily heart. The on readings are シン + ゾウ. This compound selects an anatomical meaning from the broader associations of 心.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[心](心.md) (こころ) often concerns the mind, feelings, or emotional heart. [胸](胸.md) (むね) is the chest, the body region containing the heart. These English-related meanings do not make the Japanese nouns interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

心臓 can also be figurative: a machine's central working part may be called its "heart." That does not mean every English expression containing "heart" takes 心臓. For feelings "deep in one's heart," 心の奥 (こころのおく) is a different construction. Start with the organ meaning and learn figurative phrases in context.
