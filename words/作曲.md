# 作曲 (さっきょく) - musical composition

## Meaning

作曲 is a noun for composing music and takes する for "compose." It can stand without an object, or the piece being composed can take を.

- 休みの日に作曲しています。 (やすみのひにさっきょくしています。) - I compose music on my days off.
- 友達がこの曲を作曲しました。 (ともだちがこのきょくをさっきょくしました。) - My friend composed this piece.

作曲家 (さっきょくか) is a composer. 作曲した人 (さっきょくしたひと) identifies the person who composed something without requiring a professional title.

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) means make or create, and [曲](../kanji/曲.md) means a tune or musical piece here. Their relationship is verb plus object: create a tune. In さっきょく, 作's サク becomes サッ before キョク; hold the consonant briefly at the small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

曲を作る (きょくをつくる) is a plain-language equivalent. [演奏](演奏.md) (えんそう) means performing music, which is different from composing it. [作文](作文.md) (さくぶん) concerns writing a composition in words. The shared English word "composition" does not make 作曲 and 作文 interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

作曲 concerns creating the music, not necessarily writing the lyrics or performing the finished song. 作詞 (さくし) means writing lyrics. One person may do all these things, but a credit saying 作曲 identifies the musical-composition role. There is no useful general antonym for composing.
