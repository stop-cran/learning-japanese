# 教会 (きょうかい) — church

## Meaning

教会 means a church, either the building or the religious congregation. In ordinary contexts it refers to a Christian church.

日曜日に教会へ行きます。（にちようびにきょうかいへいきます。） — I go to church on Sundays.

## How the kanji combine

[教](../kanji/教.md) contributes "teaching; religion," and [会](../kanji/会.md) means a meeting or association. Both use on readings: きょう + かい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

寺（てら） is a Buddhist temple, and 神社（じんじゃ） is a Shinto shrine. These name different religious institutions, not synonyms for 教会.

## Distinctive meaning

The same word can refer to people organized as a church as well as the place where they gather. Do not confuse it with 協会（きょうかい）, "association": the pronunciation matches, but the spelling and meaning differ.
