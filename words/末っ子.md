# 末っ子 (すえっこ) - youngest child

## Meaning

末っ子 is the youngest child in a family: the last-born among siblings. It describes birth order, not the person's present age.

- 私は三人きょうだいの末っ子です。 (わたしはさんにんきょうだいのすえっこです。) - I am the youngest of three siblings.
- 末っ子も今年で三十歳になります。 (すえっこもことしでさんじゅっさいになります。) - Even our youngest child turns thirty this year.

## How the kanji combine

[末](../kanji/末.md), end or last, is すえ here, and [子](../kanji/子.md) contributes child. Learn the whole pronunciation すえっこ, including the small っ before こ, rather than the two on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[子供](子供.md) can mean a child or someone's offspring without specifying birth order. 長男 (ちょうなん), eldest son, and 長女 (ちょうじょ), eldest daughter, specify both an order and a gender; 末っ子 does not specify gender.

## Distinctive meaning

An adult can still be the 末っ子. Do not substitute [幼い](幼い.md), young or immature, just because English uses "child" in the translation. Being the youngest also says nothing by itself about personality.
