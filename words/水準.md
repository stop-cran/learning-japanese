# 水準 (すいじゅん) - level; standard

## Meaning

水準 is a noun for a level or standard, such as a standard of living, an educational level, or a level of performance. Use **X の水準**, or combine it in expressions such as 生活水準 (せいかつすいじゅん), "standard of living."

- この国の生活水準は高いです。 (このくにのせいかつすいじゅんはたかいです。) - This country has a high standard of living.
- 技術の水準を上げる必要があります。 (ぎじゅつのすいじゅんをあげるひつようがあります。) - We need to raise the level of technical skill.

高い and 低い describe a high or low level. 水準に達する (すいじゅんにたっする) means "reach a standard."

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md) contributes water and 準 a standard or level. A level water surface is a useful image for a reference level. JMdict also records the literal meaning "water level"; it is distinct from the abstract applications above.

## Synonyms and antonyms

レベル overlaps with "level." 基準 (きじゅん) emphasizes a criterion or reference used for judging something, whereas 水準 can describe the level actually achieved.

## Distinctive meaning

水準 does not inherently mean "high quality." A 水準 can be high, low, or unchanged. Specify the comparison or field when needed: educational attainment and living standards are different dimensions, even when both are described with 水準.
