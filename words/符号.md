# 符号 (ふごう) - symbol; sign; code

## Meaning

符号 is a sign or symbol used to represent something, including a code. In mathematics it can mean a number's positive or negative sign.

- モールス符号を勉強しています。 (モールスふごうをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying Morse code.
- 答えの符号が逆です。 (こたえのふごうがぎゃくです。) - The sign of the answer is reversed.

## How the kanji combine

[符](../kanji/符.md), a token or sign, and [号](../kanji/号.md), a sign or designation, reinforce the idea of a representational mark. The on readings are フ + ゴウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[記号](記号.md) (きごう) overlaps broadly in symbols. [印](印.md) (しるし) is a mark or indication, and [コード](コード.md) can be a code. Learn the intended system rather than assuming every English use of "sign" calls for 符号.

## Distinctive meaning

A mathematical 符号 tells you positive or negative, not the number's size. A roadside sign, a signal to a person and a natural indication need their own context; 符号 is not an unrestricted replacement for all of them.
