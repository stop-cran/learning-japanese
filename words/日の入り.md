# 日の入り (ひのいり) - sunset; time of sunset

## Meaning

日の入り is sunset: the sun going below the horizon, or the time when
that happens.

- 今日の日の入りは午後六時ごろです。 (きょうのひのいりはごごろくじごろです。) - Sunset today is around six in the evening.
- 日の入りまでに山を下りましょう。 (ひのいりまでにやまをおりましょう。) - Let's come down the mountain before sunset.

The noun names the event or time, not the sun as an object.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md), ひ, supplies the sun;
の connects it to [入](../kanji/入.md)り, from 入る, to enter.
The whole expression concerns the sun setting.
日入 and 日の入 are additional dictionary spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暮れ](暮れ.md) (くれ) overlaps in sunset or sundown but has other uses,
including year-end. [沈む](沈む.md) (しずむ) can describe the sun setting:
日が沈む, "the sun sets."
日の出 (ひので), sunrise, is the contrasting event.

## Distinctive meaning

[夕方](夕方.md) (ゆうがた) names the broader late-day period,
not the precise setting event or its time.
日の入りが早い means that sunset is early; it does not necessarily
mean that the sun is moving faster.
No exact clock time is contained in the word itself.
