# 落とし物 (おとしもの) - lost property; something dropped

## Meaning

落とし物 is lost property: something dropped or lost without realizing it. It can also occur in 落とし物をする, describing losing an item.

- 道で落とし物を拾いました。 (みちでおとしものをひろいました。) - I picked up a lost item in the street.
- 落とし物をしたので、交番で相談しました。 (おとしものをしたので、こうばんでそうだんしました。) - I had lost something, so I asked for help at the police box.

落とし物はありませんか (おとしものはありませんか) can ask about lost property. The surrounding situation identifies whether someone is looking for their own item or handling found property.

## How the kanji combine

[落](../kanji/落.md) appears in 落とす (おとす), "drop; lose." Its noun-forming stem 落とし combines with [物](../kanji/物.md) (もの), "thing." 落し物 is another spelling of おとしもの, omitting the written と; it does not change the pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[忘れ物](忘れ物.md) (わすれもの) emphasizes leaving something behind rather than taking it along. The categories overlap in lost-property situations; the distinction is not a rule that every item belongs to exactly one category.

[無くす](無くす.md) (なくす) describes losing something as a verb, while [拾う](拾う.md) (ひろう) describes finding and picking an item up.

## Distinctive meaning

An object need not remain permanently missing to be called a 落とし物: a finder can be holding it. Nor is every deliberately dropped object lost property. The established expression concerns an unintended loss, not simply an object moving downward.
