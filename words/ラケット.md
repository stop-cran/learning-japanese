# ラケット (ラケット) - racket

## Meaning

ラケット is a noun for a racket used in sports such as tennis or badminton.
It also names the table-tennis implement called a "paddle" or "bat" in some varieties of English.
Specify the sport with **sport + のラケット** when necessary.

- 新しいテニスのラケットを買いました。 (あたらしいテニスのラケットをかいました。) - I bought a new tennis racket.
- 卓球のラケットを貸してください。 (たっきゅうのラケットをかしてください。) - Please lend me a table-tennis paddle.

Both examples mark the equipment with を.
To identify what is used to hit a ball, use で: ラケットでボールを打つ,
"hit a ball with a racket."

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana, with a small ッ before ト.
Read ラケット as the complete Japanese word.
There is no kanji combination or implied native-character spelling to learn.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[テニス](テニス.md) is a sport that uses rackets, not a synonym for the equipment.
[道具](道具.md) (どうぐ) is the broader category of tools or implements.
バット describes a bat in other sports, but English naming differences do not change the Japanese term for a table-tennis racket.

## Distinctive meaning

ラケット includes both strung rackets and the solid table-tennis implement.
The sport determines the relevant design.
The translation "paddle" is restricted to that sporting context: this is not the ordinary word for a paddle used to move a boat.
Likewise, English "racket" meaning loud noise is not the meaning taught by this entry.
