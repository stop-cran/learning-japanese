# がっしり — solidly built; sturdy

## Meaning

がっしり describes a strong, substantial build or something made firmly and sturdily.

- がっしりした体格の選手が、チームに加わった。
  — A solidly built athlete joined the team.
- この棚は太い木材でがっしり作られている。
  — This shelf is sturdily made from thick lumber.

## Synonyms and antonyms

がっしり describes strength or solid construction. Unlike [ずっしり](ずっしり.md), it does
not mean that something feels heavy when lifted.

## Distinctive meaning

It commonly modifies a person's build or describes sturdy construction. It does not itself
describe a tight fastening or a precise fit.
