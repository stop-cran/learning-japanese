# 左手 (ひだりて) — left hand

## Meaning

左手 is the left hand: 左手でボールを投げる (throw the ball with the left hand). It also means "on the left side": 左手に銀行があります (there is a bank on the left).

## How the kanji combine

[左](../kanji/左.md) (left) plus [手](../kanji/手.md) (hand): ひだり + て, both kun readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [右手](右手.md) みぎて.
- Related: 左利き ひだりきき (left-handed), 左側 ひだりがわ (the left side).

## Distinctive meaning

Same pattern as 右手: either the hand or a direction. 左利き is the word for being left-handed.
