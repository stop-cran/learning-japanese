# 釣り合う (つりあう) - balance; go well together

## Meaning

釣り合う means to balance or be in equilibrium. It also describes things that suit one another or make a good match. It is an intransitive godan verb.

- 左右の重さが釣り合っています。
  (さゆうのおもさがつりあっています。) - The weights on the left and right balance.
- この机に釣り合う椅子を探しています。
  (このつくえにつりあういすをさがしています。) - I am looking for a chair that goes well with this desk.

XとYが釣り合う presents two things as balanced or well matched. Xに釣り合うY identifies Y as a suitable match for X. Forms include 釣り合います, 釣り合って, and 釣り合わない.

## How the kanji combine

釣 writes つり, also seen in the fishing word [釣る](釣る.md). [合](../kanji/合.md), read あ, contributes fitting or coming together. Learn this compound as balancing or matching; its meaning is not "fish together." The dictionary also records つり合う and 釣合う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[合う](合う.md) (あう) is a broader fit or match. [似合う](似合う.md) (にあう) is useful when something suits someone's appearance. [マッチ](マッチ.md) with する also expresses going well together.

## Distinctive meaning

Balance need not mean numerical identity: a price and the quality offered can be 釣り合う when they are proportionate. Likewise, a matching desk and chair need not look identical. 釣り合っている describes their balanced or compatible state, not someone actively adjusting them.
