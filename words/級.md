# 級 (きゅう) - class; rank; level

## Meaning

級 names a class, grade, or rank. It also attaches to numbers to identify levels in tests and ranking systems. The value of a particular number depends on the system.

- 次は二級の試験を受けます。 (つぎはにきゅうのしけんをうけます。) - Next I will take the level-two exam.
- 彼は柔道で三級です。 (かれはじゅうどうでさんきゅうです。) - He holds third kyu in judo.

Learn number combinations as spoken units: 一級 is いっきゅう, 二級 is にきゅう, and 三級 is さんきゅう.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 級 supplies the noun and suffix, with on reading キュウ. There is no multi-kanji relationship to divide. Keep the long vowel: きゅう, not きゅ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

学校の学年 (がっこうのがくねん) identifies a school year, while [クラス](クラス.md) can identify a class group. 級 also has a school-class sense, but these words cannot replace every ranked suffix. 段 (だん) identifies another set of ranks in activities such as martial arts.

## Distinctive meaning

A 級 label needs a context: an examination, a sport, or a classification scheme. It does not name one universal standard. Modern JLPT labels are N1-N5; do not mechanically replace the N with 級 when naming those levels.
