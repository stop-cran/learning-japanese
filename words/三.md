# 三 (さん) -- three

## Meaning

三 is the number three. さん is used when reciting numbers and in many combinations with counters.

教室に学生が三人います。 (きょうしつにがくせいがさんにんいます。) -- There are three students in the classroom.

In 三人 (さんにん), 人 is the counter for people. For ordinary quantities, choose the appropriate counter rather than placing さん directly before any object noun.

## How the kanji combine

The single numeral [三](../kanji/三.md) has the on reading さん. Its three horizontal strokes represent the number. The Arabic numeral 3 can write the same quantity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

三つ (みっつ) means three things in the native general counting series. It is related vocabulary, not the reading さん with an extra suffix pronounced つ.

## Distinctive meaning

Counter combinations need their own pronunciation: 三本 (さんぼん), three long cylindrical things, changes the counter's initial sound. Date words can use another pattern altogether, as in 三日 (みっか), the third day or three days. Learning さん does not predict every word containing 三.
