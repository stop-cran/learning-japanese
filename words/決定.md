# 決定 (けってい) - decision; determination

## Meaning

決定 is a noun for a decision. 決定する can describe deciding something or something being settled. Distinguish the object pattern Nを決定する from the result pattern Nが決定する.

- 会議で開催日を決定しました。 (かいぎでかいさいびをけっていしました。) - We decided the event date at the meeting.
- 開催日が決定しました。 (かいさいびがけっていしました。) - The event date has been decided.

As a noun, 決定を待つ (けっていをまつ) means "wait for a decision." The word does not itself tell you who made it.

## How the kanji combine

[決](../kanji/決.md) contributes deciding; [定](../kanji/定.md) contributes fixing or settling. The two reinforce reaching a settled choice. ケツ + テイ becomes けってい, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[決める](決める.md) (きめる) and [決まる](決まる.md) (きまる) make the transitive/intransitive distinction with separate native verbs.

[決心](決心.md) (けっしん) focuses on making up one's mind or resolving to act. 決定 can concern an agreed schedule or policy without emphasizing personal resolve.

## Distinctive meaning

決定 fits an announced or recorded decision, but does not guarantee that it can never be changed. 未決定 (みけってい), "undecided," contrasts with a settled result. Read the particles to see whether the sentence highlights the decider's action or the resulting arrangement.
