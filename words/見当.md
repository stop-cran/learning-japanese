# 見当 (けんとう) - estimate; rough idea

## Meaning

見当 is a noun for an estimate, guess, or rough idea. Two useful constructions are **見当がつく**, "have an idea," and **見当をつける**, "form an estimate." The negative 見当がつかない means "have no idea."

- いくらかかるか見当がつきません。 (いくらかかるかけんとうがつきません。) - I have no idea how much it will cost.
- 食事代は一人三千円見当です。 (しょくじだいはひとりさんぜんえんけんとうです。) - The meal will cost about three thousand yen per person.

In the second example, 見当 is a suffix after an amount, meaning "approximately." It does not promise an exact price.

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) contributes seeing or assessing; [当](../kanji/当.md) contributes hitting or matching a target. As a memory aid, think of assessing where the target lies. The compound uses ケン + トウ; this mnemonic is not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[予想](予想.md) (よそう) is a prediction or expectation. [およそ](およそ.md) can express an approximate amount. [検討](検討.md), also けんとう, means examination or consideration and is a different word.

## Distinctive meaning

見当 does not itself mean "be found." [見つかる](見つかる.md) is a verb for that. Learn 見当 with its construction: the missing idea in 見当がつかない may concern a place, a cost, or an explanation, not a missing physical object.
