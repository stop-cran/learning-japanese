# 列 (れつ) — row; line; queue

## Meaning

列 is a line or row formed by people or things.
It can describe a waiting queue, a row of seats, or another ordered arrangement.
In 列に並ぶ (れつにならぶ), "line up in a queue," に marks the arrangement you join.

- 列に並んでください。 (れつにならんでください。) — Please line up.
- この列の最後はどこですか。 (このれつのさいごはどこですか。) — Where is the end of this queue?

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-character word: [列](../kanji/列.md) supplies the entire meaning and its on reading レツ.
There is no second component to combine and no okurigana after it.
It belongs with compounds such as 行列 ぎょうれつ, "queue; procession," and 列車 れっしゃ, "train."

## Synonyms and antonyms

行列 ぎょうれつ also names a queue or procession and is useful when talking about people lined up.
[線](線.md) せん is a line in the sense of a drawn stroke or a line on a surface, not the word for a queue of customers.
There is no simple opposite noun that applies to every kind of 列.

## Distinctive meaning

The English translation "line" can hide the arrangement sense.
For a waiting line, think of the people standing in sequence, not a mark drawn on the ground.
The English dictionary gloss "file" likewise means people or things in a line here, not a saved computer file.
