# 夕べ (ゆうべ) - evening; last night

## Meaning

夕べ has three related uses that should not be collapsed into one translation.
The plain noun "evening" is marked **poetic** in JMdict. An event title can
use **noun + の夕べ**, "an evening of ...". In another use, ゆうべ means
"last night" or "yesterday evening."

- 今週末、「音楽の夕べ」が開かれます。 (こんしゅうまつ、「おんがくのゆうべ」がひらかれます。) - An "Evening of Music" will be held this weekend.
- ゆうべは早く寝ました。 (ゆうべははやくねました。) - I went to bed early last night.

The future event in the first example shows why の夕べ cannot automatically
mean yesterday evening.

## How the kanji combine

[夕](../kanji/夕.md) contributes evening. The native word ゆうべ is written
with べ as okurigana here; 夕 alone is another recorded spelling.
This is not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[夕方](夕方.md) (ゆうがた) is useful for ordinary references to the evening
part of a day. [昨夜](昨夜.md) can also be read ゆうべ for "last night";
JMdict says that sense is usually written 昨夜.
"Morning" contrasts with poetic evening, not with all the word's uses at once.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list's bare gloss "evening" is supported, but it omits the poetic
qualification. Do not replace it with a claim that 夕べ must mean "last night,"
or teach it as an unrestricted scheduling word. Context and the の construction
distinguish the poetic, event-title, and previous-night uses within the same entry.
