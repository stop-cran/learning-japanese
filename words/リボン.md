# リボン - ribbon

## Meaning

リボン is a ribbon: a narrow strip used for decoration, tying or similar
purposes. It is a noun. The word also occurs in gymnastics and in
インクリボン, an ink ribbon used in a printing mechanism.

- プレゼントに赤いリボンを結びました。 (プレゼントにあかいリボンをむすびました。) - I tied a red ribbon around the present.
- 髪にリボンを付けています。 (かみにリボンをつけています。) - She is wearing a ribbon in her hair.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[テープ](テープ.md) includes tape-like strips and can overlap with ribbon
in a printing mechanism.
[帯](帯.md) (おび) can also name a band or strip.
[紐](紐.md) (ひも) is a cord or string; not every ribbon is a cord,
and not every cord is a ribbon.

## Distinctive meaning

A decorative ribbon can be tied into a bow, but リボン is not restricted
to the finished knot. The printing use also shows why the word does not
always mean an ornament or hair accessory.
It is not a verb meaning "tie."
