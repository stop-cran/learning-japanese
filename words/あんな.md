# あんな — that kind of

## Meaning

あんな means "that kind of" or "like that," referring to a kind or situation treated as distant from the immediate conversation. It goes directly before a noun, without another な or の.

あんな家に住みたいです。 (あんないえにすみたいです。) — I want to live in a house like that.

## How the kanji combine

あんな is a kana word in the こんな・そんな・あんな・どんな series. It is a fixed noun-modifying form, not an ordinary な-adjective whose final な can simply be removed.

## Synonyms and antonyms

こんな means "this kind of"; そんな often takes up something the listener has just mentioned. どんな asks "what kind of?"

## Distinctive meaning

あの家 identifies "that house"; あんな家 describes its kind or character. あんな can convey admiration, surprise, or disapproval, but is not inherently negative. For degree before a verb or adjective, use あんなに: あんなに大きい (あんなにおおきい), "that big."
