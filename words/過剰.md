# 過剰 (かじょう) - excess; too much

## Meaning

過剰 means excess: more than is needed, appropriate, or allowed by a relevant standard. It is a noun and na-adjective: 過剰な + noun, or 過剰に + a verb or adjective.

- 過剰な心配は、体によくありません。 (かじょうなしんぱいは、からだによくありません。) - Excessive worry is not good for your health.
- 商品を過剰に生産してしまいました。 (しょうひんをかじょうにせいさんしてしまいました。) - We ended up producing too many goods.

供給過剰 (きょうきゅうかじょう) means oversupply. The relevant standard may concern need rather than a legally fixed numerical limit.

## How the kanji combine

[過](../kanji/過.md) contributes going beyond; 剰 contributes a remainder or surplus. The two ideas reinforce being beyond what is required. Their on readings are カ + ジョウ. 剰 remains part of the spelling even though it has no separate card here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[余分](余分.md) (よぶん) can mean extra or surplus, including a useful spare. [超過](超過.md) (ちょうか) emphasizes exceeding a limit or estimate. 不足 (ふそく), shortage, provides a useful contrast with excess.

## Distinctive meaning

過剰 is not simply "a large amount": the amount is excessive relative to something. 過剰な反応 (かじょうなはんのう), an excessive reaction, is possible without a countable stock of objects. Do not attach it like an i-adjective.
