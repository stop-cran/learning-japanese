# 後半 (こうはん) — second half

## Meaning

後半 is the latter half of a period, game or text: 試合の後半 (the second half of the match), 後半戦 こうはんせん (the second half of a season), 今年の後半 (the second half of this year).

## How the kanji combine

[後](../kanji/後.md) (after, behind) plus [半](../kanji/半.md) (half): "rear half". Both on'yomi, with 後 read コウ, not ゴ: コウ + ハン. This is the pair of [前半](前半.md) ぜんはん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [前半](前半.md) ぜんはん (first half).
- Related: 後期 こうき (second term), 後編 こうへん (latter part of a work).

## Distinctive meaning

Remember 後 reads コウ here but ゴ in [午後](午後.md). Another sense: 二十代後半 にじゅうだいこうはん is "the late twenties".
