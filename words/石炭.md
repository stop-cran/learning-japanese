# 石炭 (せきたん) - coal

## Meaning

石炭 is the noun "coal," the solid fuel extracted from the ground. It can be the object of verbs such as 掘る (ほる), "dig," or 燃やす (もやす), "burn." It does not itself specify a quantity.

- 昔、この町では石炭を掘っていました。 (むかし、このまちではせきたんをほっていました。) - Coal used to be mined in this town.
- この発電所では石炭を燃やしています。 (このはつでんしょではせきたんをもやしています。) - Coal is burned at this power station.

The first sentence places the mining in the past; 石炭 itself is not a word restricted to historical contexts.

## How the kanji combine

[石](../kanji/石.md) contributes stone, and 炭 contributes coal or charcoal. Their combination names coal as a stone-like fuel. This is a component-based memory aid, not a scientific explanation of how coal forms.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[石油](石油.md) (せきゆ), "petroleum," is another fuel resource, but a different substance. 木炭 (もくたん), "charcoal," is produced from wood; it should not be confused with mined 石炭.

## Distinctive meaning

Coal is a material, not the general Japanese word for energy, electricity, or fuel. Burning 石炭 may be part of electricity generation, but the noun still names what is burned. Learn the modern reading せきたん; the dictionary's older reading is not needed for these examples.
