# こけこっこー — a rooster crowing

## Meaning

A rooster's “cock-a-doodle-doo,” represented in Japanese sounds.

- 朝、にわとりがこけこっこーと鳴（な）いた。 — A rooster crowed in the morning.
- 遠くから、こけこっこーという声が聞こえた。 — I heard a rooster crowing in the distance.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぴよぴよ](ぴよぴよ.md) often represents chicks peeping, not an adult rooster's crow. [かあかあ](かあかあ.md) is a crow's caw: English uses “crow” for both a bird and a different bird's call.

## Distinctive meaning

This is a conventional sound expression, not the general noun for every chicken. The dictionary also records a child-directed noun use for a rooster. Keep the small っ and the final long vowel in this card's spelling.
