# がさがさ — rustling; dry and rough

## Meaning

がさがさ can describe a coarse rustling sound or a dry, rough texture.

- 枯れ葉を踏むと、がさがさと音がした。
  — The dry leaves rustled when I stepped on them.
- 冬になると、手の肌ががさがさする。
  — In winter, the skin on my hands gets rough and dry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

The sound sense is coarse and distinct, as when dry leaves or paper move. The texture sense
describes roughness, not a smooth or moisturized surface.

## Distinctive meaning

Context separates the audible rustle from the feeling of dry skin. JMdict also records a
colloquial description of a person's manner and a fishing-related noun; neither is taught here.
