# 硬貨 (こうか) - coin

## Meaning

硬貨 is a coin used as money. It is a noun: 硬貨を入れる means to insert a coin, and 硬貨で払う (こうかではらう) means to pay with coins. Coins can be counted with 枚 (まい).

- 百円硬貨を一枚入れてください。 (ひゃくえんこうかをいちまいいれてください。) - Please insert one hundred-yen coin.
- 財布に硬貨が三枚あります。 (さいふにこうかがさんまいあります。) - There are three coins in my wallet.

A denomination before 硬貨 identifies the coin's value, not the number of coins.

## How the kanji combine

硬 means hard or solid, and 貨 contributes currency or money. The first component qualifies the second, fitting metal coinage. The on readings are コウ + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

紙幣 (しへい), "banknote," is a contrasting form of money, not a logical opposite. [お金](お金.md) (おかね) is broader and can include coins, notes, and money considered as funds. コイン also means coin.

## Distinctive meaning

硬貨 identifies the object as currency; its hardness alone does not make any metal disk a 硬貨. 小銭 (こぜに), "small change," overlaps with coins but emphasizes small amounts or denominations. A large-denomination coin is still a 硬貨.
