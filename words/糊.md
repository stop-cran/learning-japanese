# 糊 (のり) - adhesive paste; clothing starch

## Meaning

糊 is paste or glue used for sticking things together. It also names clothing starch, used to give fabric body or stiffness. Kana のり is useful to recognize.

- 封筒に糊をつけました。 (ふうとうにのりをつけました。) - I put glue on the envelope.
- シャツに糊をつけます。 (シャツにのりをつけます。) - I starch the shirt.

The object tells you whether the intended use is adhesion or clothing care; the word does not mean only school craft glue.

## How the kanji combine

糊 represents the whole native noun のり. It is not the same word as 海苔 (のり), edible seaweed, despite the shared pronunciation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

接着剤 (せっちゃくざい) is an adhesive or bonding agent, a broader useful comparison. [貼る](貼る.md) (はる) names an action, "stick; paste": 糊 is the material, while 糊で紙を貼る (のりでかみをはる) means sticking paper with paste.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the material from the verb in English: 糊をつける is "apply paste," and シャツに糊をつける is naturally "starch a shirt." The clothing use is not a general name for every kind of dietary starch. This card teaches のり, not other readings or words found under the same character on a dictionary page.
