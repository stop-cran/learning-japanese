# 吐き気 (はきけ) - nausea; urge to vomit

## Meaning

吐き気 is nausea: the feeling that one may vomit.
It is a noun. 吐き気がする means "feel nauseous," and
吐き気がある means "have nausea."

- バスに乗っているうちに、吐き気がしてきました。 (バスにのっているうちに、はきけがしてきました。) - I began to feel nauseous while riding the bus.
- 吐き気がひどいので、少し休みます。 (はきけがひどいので、すこしやすみます。) - The nausea is bad, so I will rest for a while.

する in 吐き気がする describes experiencing the sensation.
Do not turn this into an ordinary transitive verb 吐き気をする.

## How the kanji combine

吐き comes from [吐く](吐く.md) (はく), "vomit."
[気](../kanji/気.md), read け here, contributes the feeling or indication.
け is an on-reading, not a native kun reading.
The complete word is はきけ, not はきき; JMdict marks alternate はきげ as irregular.
The formation mixes native 吐き with on-reading け, rather than using two native readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

吐く describes actually vomiting; 吐き気 does not say that vomiting has occurred.
[酔う](酔う.md) (よう) can describe motion sickness, one possible cause in the bus example.
[気分](気分.md) (きぶん) is broader: 気分が悪い can report nausea or another kind of feeling unwell.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun names a symptom, not its medical cause.
It need not imply alcohol, a vehicle, or any particular diagnosis.
The contrast with 吐く lets you say whether someone feels sick or has actually vomited.
