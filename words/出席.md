# 出席 (しゅっせき) — attendance

## Meaning

出席 means attendance or being present at a class, meeting, or other gathering. Add する for "attend". Mark the event attended with に.

- 明日の会議に出席します。  
  あしたのかいぎにしゅっせきします。  
  "I'll attend tomorrow's meeting."
- 授業に出席しましたか。  
  じゅぎょうにしゅっせきしましたか。  
  "Did you attend class?"

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) contributes coming out or appearing, and [席](../kanji/席.md) means a seat or place at a gathering. The on readings しゅつ and せき combine as しゅっせき, with small っ before せ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

欠席 (けっせき), "absence; nonattendance", is the direct contrast. 参加 (さんか), "participation", emphasizes joining an activity; 出席 emphasizes being present at an occasion.

## Distinctive meaning

出席する does not by itself mean commuting regularly. [通う](通う.md) (かよう) describes repeated attendance or trips to a place, while 出席する can describe attendance at one particular meeting.
