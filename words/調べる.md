# 調べる (しらべる) — investigate; look up

## Meaning

調べる means to investigate, examine, check, or look something up.
The question, information, or object being examined can take を.

- 辞書で言葉の意味を調べました。 (じしょでことばのいみをしらべました。) — I looked up the meaning of a word in a dictionary.
- 出発の時間を調べます。 (しゅっぱつのじかんをしらべます。) — I will check the departure time.

It is an ichidan verb: 調べます (しらべます), 調べた (しらべた).

## How the kanji combine

[調](../kanji/調.md) has the investigation association here. The kana べる completes
the native verb しらべる. Do not substitute the on reading チョウ when reading this
kanji-plus-kana spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[探す](../words/探す.md) (さがす) means to look for something. Compare 辞書を探す (じしょをさがす),
"look for a dictionary," with 辞書で調べる (じしょでしらべる), "look something up in a dictionary."
One treats the dictionary as what you seek; the other uses it to find information.

## Distinctive meaning

English "look up" is only one use of 調べる. The verb can also concern examining an object
or investigating a question; it does not require a dictionary or a website.
The sentence's object and the means marked with で make the intended activity clearer.
