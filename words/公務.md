# 公務 (こうむ) - official public duties

## Meaning

公務 is official or public business: duties carried out for the state
or a public body, or in a public-service role. It is a noun.
公務で (こうむで) can explain that someone is acting on official business.

- 市長は公務で出張しています。 (しちょうはこうむでしゅっちょうしています。) - The mayor is away on official business.
- 公務と私生活を分けて考えます。 (こうむとしせいかつをわけてかんがえます。) - I consider official duties separately from private life.

To describe performing the duties, use a noun construction such as
公務を行う (こうむをおこなう), not an assumed 公務する conjugation.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md) contributes public or governmental, and
[務](../kanji/務.md) contributes duties or tasks. 公 qualifies 務.
The on readings are コウ + ム.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと), work or job, is broader.
[事務](事務.md) (じむ) concerns administrative work and can be part of
public duties without covering every 公務.
[公務員](公務員.md) (こうむいん) names a public employee, not the duties.
Private affairs provide a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Public business here does not mean every business transaction visible
to the public, or every errand a public employee performs privately.
The word does not tell you the person's exact occupation or whether
the task takes place inside an office.
