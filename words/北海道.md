# 北海道 (ほっかいどう) — Hokkaido

## Meaning

北海道 is the northernmost of Japan's four main islands and a prefecture: 北海道に行く (go to Hokkaido), 北海道旅行 ほっかいどうりょこう (a Hokkaido trip), 札幌 さっぽろ (its capital, Sapporo).

## How the kanji combine

[北](../kanji/北.md) (north) plus 海 (sea) plus 道 (circuit, an old regional division): the name given in 1869, with 道 as in the old regional circuits 東海道 とうかいどう and 南海道. 北 + 海 + 道 gives ほっ (ホク → ほっ before a k-sound) + かい + どう.
So it is "the northern sea circuit". It is an on-reading compound, and 北 is the same character as in きた (kun).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 東北 とうほく (north-east Honshu), 北国 きたぐに (northern country), 北方 ほっぽう／ほくほう.
- Old name: 蝦夷 えぞ (Ezo).

## Distinctive meaning

北海道 is the only one of the 47 prefectures whose name ends in 道 (do); the others end in 県, 府 or 都. The ホク sound changes to ほっ.
