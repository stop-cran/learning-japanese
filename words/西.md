# 西 (にし) - west

## Meaning

西 is west, one of the four main compass directions. It is a noun, so particles indicate direction or location.

駅から西へ歩いてください。 (えきからにしへあるいてください。) - Please walk west from the station.

公園は学校の西にあります。 (こうえんはがっこうのにしにあります。) - The park is west of the school.

The second example uses place + の + 西 to locate something relative to a reference point.

## How the kanji combine

[西](../kanji/西.md) writes the whole native noun にし. In compounds it can have on readings, as in 西洋 (せいよう), "the Western world."

## Synonyms and antonyms

東 (ひがし), "east," is the opposite direction. 北 (きた) and 南 (みなみ) mean north and south. 西側 (にしがわ) more explicitly means "the west side."

## Distinctive meaning

西 is a geographical direction, not automatically the cultural label "the West." For that broader cultural meaning, [西洋](西洋.md) may be appropriate. In a compound or place name, learn the complete reading rather than assuming every 西 is pronounced にし.
