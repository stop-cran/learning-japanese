# 田舎 (いなか) - countryside

## Meaning

田舎 means the countryside or a rural area, in contrast to a city. Use の before another noun: 田舎の生活 (いなかのせいかつ), "life in the countryside."

- 祖母は田舎に住んでいます。 (そぼはいなかにすんでいます。) - My grandmother lives in the countryside.

田舎 can also refer to someone's hometown or family home region: 田舎に帰る (いなかにかえる), "go back home."

## How the kanji combine

[田](../kanji/田.md) evokes fields and 舎 a dwelling. These associations fit rural settlements, but いなか is a whole-word reading, not the normal readings of the characters joined together.

## Synonyms and antonyms

都会 (とかい), "city; urban area," supplies the useful geographical contrast. 故郷 (ふるさと) emphasizes one's hometown or place of origin, which need not be rural.

## Distinctive meaning

田舎 may be affectionate when describing home, but dismissive when used to suggest a place is backward. Context and tone matter; it is not automatically either praise or an insult.
