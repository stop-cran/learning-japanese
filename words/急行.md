# 急行 (きゅうこう) - express train; rushing

## Meaning

急行 commonly names an express train, a service that skips some stations. As 急行する, it also means "hurry" or "rush to somewhere."

急行に乗ります。 (きゅうこうにのります。) - I will take the express train.

警察が現場に急行しました。 (けいさつがげんばにきゅうこうしました。) - The police rushed to the scene.

The destination of 急行する takes に or へ.

## How the kanji combine

[急](../kanji/急.md), "urgent; hurry," combines with [行](../kanji/行.md), "go." The on readings give きゅうこう. The train sense is short for 急行列車 (きゅうこうれっしゃ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

普通 (ふつう) or 各駅停車 (かくえきていしゃ) identifies a local stopping service in railway contexts. 特急 (とっきゅう) means limited express. Service names and stopping patterns depend on the railway.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not treat 急行 as an all-purpose adjective meaning "speedy." It names a train category or, with する, rapid travel toward a destination. Check the actual stops and fare rules rather than assuming every 急行 works identically.
