# 働き (はたらき) - work; functioning

## Meaning

働き is a noun for work, someone's performance or contribution, or the functioning of something. Use **noun の働き** to identify whose work or what function is being discussed.

- 彼の働きに感謝しています。 (かれのはたらきにかんしゃしています。) - I am grateful for his work.
- 心臓の働きについて勉強しました。 (しんぞうのはたらきについてべんきょうしました。) - I studied how the heart functions.

The first example concerns a person's contribution; the second concerns an organ's activity, not employment.

## How the kanji combine

[働](../kanji/働.md) contributes working or acting. 働き is the noun associated with [働く](働く.md) (はたらく), using the stem はたらき. The final き belongs to this noun form; it is not a separate kanji reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) can name a job or task. 働き can focus on what someone contributes or how well they perform. [機能](機能.md) (きのう) overlaps with the function sense, especially when describing a body part, device, or system.

## Distinctive meaning

働き is not a universal substitute for English "work." To state your occupation, 仕事 is usually the useful starting word; to explain what an organ does, its 働き is appropriate. よい働きをする (よいはたらきをする) evaluates good performance or an effective contribution rather than naming a profession.
