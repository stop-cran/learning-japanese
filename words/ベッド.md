# ベッド (ベッド) — bed

## Meaning

ベッド means a bed, usually the raised piece of furniture used for sleeping.

この部屋にはベッドが二つあります。（このへやにはベッドがふたつあります。） — There are two beds in this room.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "bed." The small ッ marks a consonant closure before ド. Keep the final ド: ベット is not the standard spelling of this furniture word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

布団（ふとん） is bedding, often a futon laid out for sleeping rather than a raised bed frame. 寝室（しんしつ） is a bedroom, the room rather than the bed itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ベッドで寝る（ベッドでねる） for "sleep in a bed," with で marking where sleeping happens. ベッドに入る（ベッドにはいる） means "get into bed," with に marking the destination.
