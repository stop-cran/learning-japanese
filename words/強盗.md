# 強盗 (ごうとう) - robber; robbery

## Meaning

強盗 is a noun that can name either a robber or the act of robbery.
Robbery involves taking property through force or threats; the word is not a general label for every kind of theft.
The surrounding sentence distinguishes the person from the crime.

- 警察が強盗を捕まえました。 (けいさつがごうとうをつかまえました。) - The police caught the robber.
- この店で強盗事件がありました。 (このみせでごうとうじけんがありました。) - There was a robbery at this shop.

強盗事件 (ごうとうじけん) explicitly identifies a robbery incident.
強盗に遭う (ごうとうにあう) means to suffer a robbery or be robbed.

## How the kanji combine

[強](../kanji/強.md) contributes force or strength, and [盗](../kanji/盗.md) contributes stealing.
The combination associates theft with force. Its on readings are ゴウ + トウ; preserve both long vowels in ごうとう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[泥棒](泥棒.md) (どろぼう) broadly names a thief or theft.
[盗む](盗む.md) (ぬすむ) is the verb "steal."
[犯人](犯人.md) (はんにん) means the perpetrator of a crime, not specifically a robber.
The police are participants on the other side of an incident, not a lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "burglary" focuses on unlawful entry and is not an unrestricted substitute for 強盗.
When reading news, identify whether 強盗 refers to a person, an offense, or modifies another noun.
The difference between 強盗を捕まえる and 強盗事件を調べる is catching the offender versus investigating the incident.
