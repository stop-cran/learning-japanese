# はあはあ — panting and gasping

## Meaning

Audible, repeated heavy breathing.

- 階段（かいだん）を走って上（のぼ）り、はあはあと息（いき）をした。 — I ran up the stairs and panted.
- 散歩（さんぽ）のあと、犬がはあはあしている。 — The dog is panting after the walk.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ほっと](ほっと.md) can describe a deep sigh or relief rather than repeated panting. [げほげほ](げほげほ.md) represents coughing, a different action.

## Distinctive meaning

はあはあする and はあはあと息をする concern breathing. They do not alone establish why someone is out of breath. The spelling ハアハア is the same expression, not a separate English-derived word.
