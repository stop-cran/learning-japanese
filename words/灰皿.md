# 灰皿 (はいざら) — ashtray

## Meaning

灰皿 is an ashtray: a container for ash and cigarette ends. The word names the object, not a smoking area.

- 灰皿を洗いました。 (はいざらをあらいました。) — I washed the ashtray.
- この部屋には灰皿がありません。 (このへやにははいざらがありません。) — There is no ashtray in this room.

Use 灰皿の + noun when modifying another noun, as in 灰皿の中 (はいざらのなか), "inside the ashtray."

## How the kanji combine

灰 (はい), "ash," combines with 皿 (さら), "dish." The second element voices to ざら in the compound, giving はいざら rather than はいさら.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ごみ箱 (ごみばこ) is a rubbish bin, not a synonym for an ashtray. 皿 alone is a dish or plate and does not specify this purpose.

## Distinctive meaning

The transparent "ash + dish" construction helps identify the object even if its shape is not a flat plate. Whether smoking is allowed depends on the location's rules, not merely on knowing the word 灰皿.
