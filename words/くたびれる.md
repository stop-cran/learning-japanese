# くたびれる - get tired; become worn out

## Meaning

くたびれる means become tired or exhausted. It also describes something becoming worn or battered through use.

- 一日中歩いて、くたびれました。 (いちにちじゅうあるいて、くたびれました。) - I walked all day and got tired.
- この靴はずいぶんくたびれています。 (このくつはずいぶんくたびれています。) - These shoes are quite worn out.

It is an ichidan verb: くたびれます, くたびれて, くたびれない.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. The dictionary spelling 草臥れる is ateji: do not derive the pronunciation by reading each character in its ordinary way.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[疲れる](疲れる.md) (つかれる) also means get tired and can describe wear in objects. Do not treat people versus objects as an absolute boundary between these words.

[くたくた](くたくた.md), worn out, and [へとへと](へとへと.md), completely exhausted, vividly describe a state. くたびれる is a verb describing becoming tired; くたびれている describes the resulting state.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can describe bodily fatigue or wear from long use; it does not automatically mean boredom. In an additional construction, a verb's masu stem can precede くたびれる: 待ちくたびれる (まちくたびれる), get tired of waiting.
