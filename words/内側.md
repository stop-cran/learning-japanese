# 内側 (うちがわ) — inner side, inside

## Meaning

内側 is the inner side of something: ドアの内側 (the inside of the door), 線の内側に立つ (stand inside the line), 内側から鍵をかける (lock from the inside).

## How the kanji combine

[内](../kanji/内.md) (うち, inside) plus 側 (がわ, side, with voicing from かわ). Both kun'yomi. It is the opposite of 外側 そとがわ (outer side).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: 外側 そとがわ (outside, outer side).
- Close: 内部 ないぶ (the interior, formal), 中 なか (inside).

## Distinctive meaning

内側 is a side or surface relative to a boundary (door, line, circle). 中 is the space inside a container. 内部 is more technical.
