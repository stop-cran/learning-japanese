# お参り (おまいり) - shrine or grave visit; worship

## Meaning

お参り is a visit to a shrine, temple, grave, or similar place to pay respects or worship. お参りする is an intransitive する expression; the place visited can take に.

- 正月に神社へお参りに行きました。 (しょうがつにじんじゃへおまいりにいきました。) - I went to a shrine to pay my respects at New Year.
- 家族で祖父のお墓にお参りしました。 (かぞくでそふのおはかにおまいりしました。) - Our family visited my grandfather's grave to pay our respects.

お参りに行く means "go to pay a visit/worship": お参り names the purpose of 行く (いく), "go."

## How the kanji combine

[参](../kanji/参.md) appears in [参る](参る.md) (まいる). Its stem 参り (まいり), with お, forms this native noun. 参る itself has other uses, including humble going and coming; not all of them mean worship.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[拝む](拝む.md) (おがむ) focuses on reverence or a praying posture; [祈る](祈る.md) (いのる) focuses on praying or wishing. [訪問](訪問.md) (ほうもん) and [訪ねる](訪ねる.md) (たずねる) are broader visiting words.

## Distinctive meaning

お参り is not a term for every social visit. A visit to a friend's house is not automatically お参り, and praying need not involve going anywhere. The word also does not prescribe one gesture for every religious setting.
