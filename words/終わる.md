# 終わる (おわる) — end; finish

## Meaning

終わる means ending or finishing. In a common intransitive pattern, the event or activity is what ends.
It also follows another verb's ます stem to mean "finish doing."

- 授業は三時に終わります。 (じゅぎょうはさんじにおわります。) — Class ends at three.
- 本を読み終わりました。 (ほんをよみおわりました。) — I finished reading the book.

## How the kanji combine

The base word is a single native verb, with [終](../kanji/終.md) read お and わる as okurigana.
終る is another dictionary spelling of the same おわる; use the fuller 終わる spelling in ordinary writing.
It is godan: 終わります (おわります), 終わって (おわって), 終わらない (おわらない).
In 読み終わる (よみおわる), the stem 読み from [読む](読む.md) joins 終わる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 終える (おえる) commonly emphasizes finishing something: 仕事を終える (しごとをおえる), "finish work."
- 終了する (しゅうりょうする) is another way to express ending or completion, often used in announcements and formal descriptions.
- 始まる (はじまる), "begin," contrasts with an event ending. [始める](始める.md) (はじめる) describes someone starting something.

## Distinctive meaning

A useful starting contrast is 授業が終わる (じゅぎょうがおわる, class ends) versus
仕事を終える (しごとをおえる, finish work). It is not an absolute rule that 終わる can never take を:
JMdict also records a transitive "finish something" sense.

The ending suffix joins the ます stem, not the dictionary form: 読み + 終わる, not 読む + 終わる.
