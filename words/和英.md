# 和英 (わえい) - Japanese-English; Japanese-English dictionary

## Meaning

和英 means Japanese-English, especially in dictionary labels:
Japanese headwords are explained or translated in English.
和英辞典 (わえいじてん) is a Japanese-English dictionary.
和英 can also abbreviate the dictionary itself.

- 和英辞典でこの言葉を調べました。 (わえいじてんでこのことばをしらべました。) - I looked up this word in a Japanese-English dictionary.
- 新しい和英を一冊買いました。 (あたらしいわえいをいっさつかいました。) - I bought a new Japanese-English dictionary.

The second example uses the abbreviated noun, counted with 冊
(さつ), as for a book.

## How the kanji combine

[和](../kanji/和.md) identifies Japanese here and
[英](../kanji/英.md) English. The order gives the lookup direction.
Read ワ + エイ, not the English word way.
The harmony and hero senses of the individual characters do not
explain this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[辞書](辞書.md) (じしょ), [辞典](辞典.md) (じてん) and
[字引](字引.md) (じびき) are broader dictionary words.
[英和](英和.md) (えいわ) reverses the lookup direction:
English headwords, Japanese explanations.

## Distinctive meaning

The language pair is ordered, not merely a statement that both
languages appear somewhere in the publication.
A combined dictionary can contain both 和英 and 英和 sections,
but the two labels still identify different lookup directions.
Neither means a dictionary of Roman letters as such.
