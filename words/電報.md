# 電報 (でんぽう) — telegram

## Meaning

電報 means a telegram: a message sent through a telegram service. It is not the ordinary word for an email or mobile text message.

- 祖父から電報が届きました。 (そふからでんぽうがとどきました。) — A telegram arrived from my grandfather.
- 結婚のお祝いに電報を送りました。 (けっこんのおいわいにでんぽうをおくりました。) — I sent a congratulatory telegram for the wedding.

Use 電報を送る (でんぽうをおくる) for sending one and 電報が届く (でんぽうがとどく) for its arrival.

## How the kanji combine

[電](../kanji/電.md) concerns electricity, and [報](../kanji/報.md) concerns reporting or informing. The compound's reading is デン + ポウ; 報's ホウ becomes ポウ in this established combination.

## Synonyms and antonyms

手紙 (てがみ) is a letter. メール is commonly email, and 電話 (でんわ) is a telephone call. These are different communication methods, not exact synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

電報 may occur in stories about earlier communication or in ceremonial contexts such as congratulations. Do not substitute it for every short electronic message just because the characters suggest electrical information.
