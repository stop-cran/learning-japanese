# ともかく (ともかく) - anyway; setting aside

## Meaning

ともかく is an adverb meaning "anyway," "at any rate," or
"in any case." It can move attention to what matters now.
In **Nはともかく**, it means "setting N aside" or "apart from N."

- ともかく、まず座って話しましょう。 (ともかく、まずすわってはなしましょう。) - Anyway, let's sit down and talk first.
- 値段はともかく、品質はいいです。 (ねだんはともかく、ひんしつはいいです。) - Leaving the price aside, the quality is good.

The second sentence does not say whether the price is good or bad.
It temporarily removes price from the judgment being made.

## How the kanji combine

This word is usually written in kana. The rare spelling 兎も角 is
ateji: do not read the meaning as a literal combination of a rabbit
and a [角](../kanji/角.md), "corner; horn."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とにかく](とにかく.md) also means "anyway; in any case."
[どうせ](どうせ.md) can present an outcome as inevitable, sometimes
with resignation. ともかく does not itself require that attitude.
[何とか](何とか.md) can describe managing something somehow; that
is not the function of はともかく in the price example.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the whole construction はともかく, not just a single English
adverb. It can set aside a question without denying it or resolving it.
The surrounding sentence determines whether ともかく introduces
practical action, a conclusion, or a change of focus.
