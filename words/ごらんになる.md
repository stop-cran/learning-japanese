# ごらんになる - see or watch, honorific

## Meaning

ごらんになる is an honorific equivalent of 見る (みる), "see, look at, watch." Use it for the action of someone you are treating respectfully, not for your own action.

- 先生はこの映画をごらんになりましたか。 (せんせいはこのえいがをごらんになりましたか。) - Have you seen this film, Professor?
- こちらをごらんになってください。 (こちらをごらんになってください。) - Please look this way.

The thing seen takes を. The final なる conjugates as a godan verb: になります, になった, にならない.

Also recognize the conventional request ご覧ください (ごらんください), "please look."
For example, こちらをご覧ください (こちらをごらんください) means "Please look this way."

## How the kanji combine

The common kanji spelling is ご覧になる. ご precedes the Sino-Japanese element 覧 (らん), followed by the native expression になる. This mixed expression is not formed by substituting a kanji reading into 見る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

見る is neutral. 拝見する (はいけんする) is humble and describes the speaker's side's act of looking; it does not honor the person doing the looking.

## Distinctive meaning

The honorific changes the social presentation of the action, not what the eyes do. In the dictionary form ごらんになる, keep the particle に; do not replace this form with ごらんする.
