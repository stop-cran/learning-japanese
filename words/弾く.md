# 弾く (ひく) - play a string or keyboard instrument

## Meaning

弾く means playing a stringed or keyboard instrument. The instrument takes を.

- 毎晩、ピアノを弾きます。 (まいばん、ピアノをひきます。) - I play the piano every evening.
- 兄はギターを弾いています。 (あにはギターをひいています。) - My older brother is playing the guitar.

It is a godan verb: 弾きます (ひきます), 弾かない (ひかない), 弾いて (ひいて), 弾いた (ひいた). Notice the いて form, not きて.

## How the kanji combine

弾 writes ひ in this verb, followed by く. The same spelling also occurs with the different reading はじく, concerning plucking or flicking; that is not the reading taught by the music examples here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

演奏する (えんそうする) is a broader word for performing music. 吹く (ふく) is used for playing a wind instrument; たたく is useful for striking a drum.

## Distinctive meaning

English uses "play" for many instruments and games. Japanese chooses the verb according to the activity. 弾く is suitable for piano and guitar, not a general translation for playing football or playing with friends.
