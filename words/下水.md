# 下水 (げすい) - sewage; drains

## Meaning

下水 can name wastewater or sewage, and also a drain, gutter, or sewer carrying such water.
Keep the water and the drainage infrastructure distinct even though the same noun covers both.

- 下水を処理してから川に流します。 (げすいをしょりしてからかわにながします。) - We treat the wastewater before releasing it into the river.
- 下水が詰まっています。 (げすいがつまっています。) - The drain is blocked.

The first sentence concerns the water; the second concerns its drainage passage.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) contributes below or downward; [水](../kanji/水.md) contributes water.
These associations help recall drainage, but they do not make 下水 a name for every kind of underground water.
The on readings combine as ゲ + スイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

下水道 (げすいどう) identifies a sewer or sewerage system.
上水 (じょうすい), supplied water suitable for use, provides the water-supply contrast.
[水](水.md) (みず) alone does not specify sewage.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate the infrastructure sense as a drinking-water supply.
The source list's "ditch" and "gutter" refer to drainage passages in this entry, not every possible use of those English words.
The separate reading したみず has its own dictionary entry; its meanings are not additional meanings of げすい.
