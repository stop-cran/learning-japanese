# 起きる (おきる) — get up; wake up

## Meaning

起きる means getting up or waking up. It can also describe an incident happening, especially something unwelcome.
The person waking or the incident occurring is the subject.

- 毎朝、六時に起きます。（まいあさ、ろくじにおきます。） — I get up at six every morning.
- 事故が起きました。（じこがおきました。） — An accident happened.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native verb, not a compound. [起](../kanji/起.md) supplies お, and きる is okurigana.
It is an ichidan verb: 起きます（おきます）, 起きない（おきない）, 起きて（おきて）.

## Synonyms and antonyms

目が覚める（めがさめる） specifically describes waking from sleep; it need not mean getting out of bed.
寝る（ねる）, "sleep; go to bed," is a useful opposite in a daily routine.
起こす（おこす） means waking **someone else**, so it is not interchangeable with 起きる.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 子供が起きた（こどもがおきた）, "The child woke up," with 子供を起こした（こどもをおこした）,
"I woke the child up." The particles reflect who undergoes the change and who causes it.
起きています（おきています） can mean "is awake," not only "is getting up right now."
