# 白い (しろい) — white

## Meaning

白い is an い-adjective: 白い雪 (white snow), 白いシャツ (a white shirt), 顔が白い (a pale face). The noun is 白 しろ, and 真っ白 まっしろ is "pure white".

## How the kanji combine

[白](../kanji/白.md) with okurigana い: しろ + い. 白 appears as しら in 白髪 しらが (white hair) and as ハク in 白紙 はくし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: [黒い](黒い.md) くろい.
- Related: 白っぽい しろっぽい (whitish), 面白い おもしろい (interesting; it contains 白 but is a separate word).

## Distinctive meaning

白い is the plain colour word. 面白い is a separate adjective, "interesting, funny"; learn it as its own word.
