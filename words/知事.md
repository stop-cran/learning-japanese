# 知事 (ちじ) - prefectural governor

## Meaning

知事 is a prefectural governor, the head of a prefectural administration in
Japan. It is a noun and can follow a name as a title.

- 知事が新しい計画を発表しました。 (ちじがあたらしいけいかくをはっぴょうしました。) - The governor announced a new plan.
- 来月、知事選挙があります。 (らいげつ、ちじせんきょがあります。) - There will be a gubernatorial election next month.

知事になる means to become governor; 知事を務める (ちじをつとめる) means to
serve as governor.

## How the kanji combine

[知](../kanji/知.md) usually suggests knowing, and [事](../kanji/事.md) means
affairs or matters. For this established title, "knowing affairs" is at most
a memory aid, not its literal modern job description. Learn the whole
administrative title with the on readings チ + ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

市長 (しちょう) is a city mayor, a different level of local government.
[議長](議長.md) (ぎちょう) is a chairperson or assembly speaker, not a governor.
These are different offices rather than antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

知事 is not limited to a jurisdiction written with [県](県.md) (けん).
東京都知事 (とうきょうとちじ) is the governor of Tokyo. The word names the
office, without specifying the holder's gender or political party.
When reading news, keep the personal name, jurisdiction, and title separate.
