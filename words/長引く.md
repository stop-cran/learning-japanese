# 長引く (ながびく) - drag on; be prolonged

## Meaning

長引く means to go on for a long time, be prolonged, or linger.
It often presents something as taking longer or ending later
than expected. It is an intransitive godan verb; the event or
condition takes が.

- 会議が長引いて、帰りが遅くなりました。 (かいぎがながびいて、かえりがおそくなりました。) - The meeting dragged on, so I got home late.
- 風邪が長引いています。 (かぜがながびいています。) - My cold is lingering.

Forms include 長引きます (ながびきます), 長引いて
(ながびいて), and 長引かない (ながびかない).
The written form 長びく has the same reading.

## How the kanji combine

[長](../kanji/長.md) contributes なが, associated with being long;
[引](../kanji/引.md) contributes 引く, with ひ voiced to び
in this word. The combination suggests duration being drawn out.
Learn the established reading ながびく, not ながひく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[続く](続く.md) (つづく) says something continues.
長引く highlights its extended duration or delayed ending.
[掛かる](掛かる.md) (かかる) can mean taking time without
necessarily suggesting a delay. [延長](延長.md) (えんちょう)
names extension or prolongation; 延長する can describe extending
something deliberately.

## Distinctive meaning

長引く does not identify a person deliberately causing the delay.
In 会議が長引いた, the meeting is the subject.
The word readily describes inconvenient duration, but that is
not a rule that every use must express a negative judgment.
It concerns duration rather than a physically long object.
