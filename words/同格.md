# 同格 (どうかく) - same rank; apposition

## Meaning

同格 means being of the same rank or status.
Use AとBは同格だ or AはBと同格だ to compare them.
同格の + noun describes something of equal standing.

- この二つの賞は同格です。 (このふたつのしょうはどうかくです。) - These two awards are of equal standing.
- 彼を部長と同格に扱いました。 (かれをぶちょうとどうかくにあつかいました。) - We treated him as having the same standing as the department head.

In grammar, 同格 also means **apposition**: expressions placed together to identify the same referent.
In 友人の田中さん (ゆうじんのたなかさん), "my friend Tanaka,"
友人 and 田中さん identify the same person.

## How the kanji combine

[同](../kanji/同.md) means same and [格](../kanji/格.md) supplies rank or status.
The modifier-plus-head combination is read ドウ + カク.
The grammatical sense is a technical extension, not a claim that every noun next to another noun is in apposition.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ) means "same" more broadly.
[平等](平等.md) (びょうどう) concerns equality, including treatment or rights;
equal treatment does not necessarily imply identical organizational ranks.
[地位](地位.md) (ちい) names a position or status without itself saying it is equal to another.

## Distinctive meaning

Specify the respect in which things are 同格.
Equal standing does not make two awards identical objects or two people the same person.
Conversely, the apposition sense concerns how expressions refer, not a workplace promotion.
The dictionary also permits adjective-like use; the の pattern above is a useful starting form.
