# 丸 (まる) - circle; whole

## Meaning

丸 is a circle, and it can also be a prefix meaning "whole; full." The construction determines which meaning applies.

- 紙に大きな丸を描きました。 (かみにおおきなまるをかきました。) - I drew a large circle on the paper.
- ここに来てから丸一年になります。 (ここにきてからまるいちねんになります。) - It has been a full year since I came here.

丸一か月 (まるいっかげつ) similarly means "a full month." It does not mean a full moon. 丸 also occurs as a name suffix, notably in ship names.

## How the kanji combine

丸 is a single-kanji native word, read まる. There is no compound to split. Its circle sense and whole-amount use are established meanings; do not invent a general meaning "moral purity" from the idea of completeness.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[丸い](丸い.md) (まるい) is the i-adjective "round," while 丸 is the noun "circle." [円](円.md) has circle-related uses too, but its reading and other meanings must be learned in their own contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

Position matters: 丸を描く names a shape, 丸一年 places 丸 before a time amount, and ～丸 at the end of a ship's name is a suffix. The suffix does not itself mean that the vessel is circular. These uses should not be merged into one literal English translation.
