# 気分 (きぶん) -- mood; how one feels

## Meaning

気分 names someone's mood or felt condition. It can concern emotional well-being or feeling physically well or unwell.

- 散歩をしたら、気分がよくなりました。 (さんぽをしたら、きぶんがよくなりました。) -- I felt better after taking a walk.
- バスの中で気分が悪くなりました。 (バスのなかできぶんがわるくなりました。) -- I began to feel sick on the bus.

Useful patterns include 気分がいい and 気分が悪い. 気分 is the noun being described; が connects it to the adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[気](../kanji/気.md), read き, concerns feeling or spirit. [分](../kanji/分.md), read ぶん here, contributes to this established compound for one's condition or mood. Do not interpret the word as a literal fraction of air.

## Synonyms and antonyms

気持ち (きもち) overlaps with feeling and sensation. 機嫌 (きげん) concerns a person's mood or temper, especially as reflected in their behavior.

## Distinctive meaning

気分が悪い can report nausea or general illness; it does not automatically mean being angry. The bus example gives a physical context. Conversely, 気分 need not imply a medical problem: a pleasant walk may improve someone's mood.
