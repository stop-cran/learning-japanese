# 中年 (ちゅうねん) - middle age; middle-aged

## Meaning

中年 is middle age, the period of adulthood between youth and
old age. As a noun it modifies another noun with の:
中年の人 (ちゅうねんのひと), "a middle-aged person."

- 中年になってから、日本語を学び始めました。 (ちゅうねんになってから、にほんごをまなびはじめました。) - I began learning Japanese after reaching middle age.
- 写真には中年の夫婦が写っています。 (しゃしんにはちゅうねんのふうふがうつっています。) - A middle-aged couple appears in the photograph.

中年になる describes reaching that life stage. The word itself
does not mean a particular person's numerical age.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) contributes middle, and [年](../kanji/年.md)
years or age. The compound names a middle period of adult life.
The on readings are チュウ + ネン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[青年](青年.md) (せいねん) concerns youth or a young person.
[年寄り](年寄り.md) (としより) refers to an elderly person.
[年齢](年齢.md) (ねんれい) names age more generally.
These expressions classify or describe age differently; they do not
evaluate a person's ability or worth.

## Distinctive meaning

Descriptions often associate 中年 with the forties and fifties,
but the word does not establish a universal legal age bracket.
It is not restricted to men, and an age description is not normally
a suitable way to call out to an individual. Use a person's name
or an appropriate form of address instead.
