# 引退 (いんたい) - retirement from active work

## Meaning

引退 is retirement or withdrawal from an active career or role. 引退する is intransitive: the person retiring is the subject. A field or active role can be marked with から.

- その選手は去年引退しました。 (そのせんしゅはきょねんいんたいしました。) - That athlete retired last year.
- 彼女は政界から引退することを決めました。 (かのじょはせいかいからいんたいすることをきめました。) - She decided to retire from politics.

引退後 (いんたいご) means "after retirement," and 引退を発表する (いんたいをはっぴょうする) means "announce one's retirement."

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) contributes pulling back, while [退](../kanji/退.md) contributes retreating or withdrawing. These related ideas combine as withdrawal from active participation. The on readings are イン + タイ, not the native verb readings ひく and しりぞく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

退職 (たいしょく) concerns leaving an employment position. It can overlap with retirement, but a change of employer is not automatically 引退. [辞める](辞める.md) (やめる) is a broader verb for quitting a job or role.

## Distinctive meaning

引退 focuses on ending an active career or stage of participation, not on reaching a fixed age. An athlete may retire young. The word also does not determine every later activity: a retired player can still coach, commentate, or otherwise remain involved in the sport.
