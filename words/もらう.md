# もらう - receive

## Meaning

もらう means to receive or accept something. The recipient is the subject; the giver takes に or から, and the thing received takes を.

友達にプレゼントをもらいました。 (ともだちにプレゼントをもらいました。) - I received a present from a friend.

With a verb's て-form, it describes receiving the benefit of someone's action.

友達に写真を撮ってもらいました。 (ともだちにしゃしんをとってもらいました。) - I had a friend take my photo.

In the second pattern, に identifies who performs the helpful action.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. 貰う is another spelling. This is a godan verb: もらいます, もらって, もらわない. The negative is not もらあない.

## Synonyms and antonyms

あげる means giving from the giver's perspective. くれる describes giving toward the speaker's side. [いただく](いただく.md) is the humble counterpart of もらう.

## Distinctive meaning

友達にもらった and 友達がくれた can describe the same gift from different grammatical viewpoints. In the てもらう pattern, receiving a favor does not by itself prove that a request was explicitly made; context determines how the help was arranged.
