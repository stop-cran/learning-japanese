# 横 (よこ) — horizontal direction; side

## Meaning

横 can name the horizontal direction or the side of an object.
In spatial descriptions, noun + の横 means "the side of" or "beside" that thing.
横に can describe a position at the side or a horizontal direction.

- 机の横にかばんがあります。 (つくえのよこにかばんがあります。) — There is a bag beside the desk.
- 紙に線を横に引きました。 (かみにせんをよこにひきました。) — I drew a horizontal line on the paper.

The first example locates something beside an object; the second describes the line's orientation.

## How the kanji combine

This is one kanji, [横](../kanji/横.md), used with its kun reading よこ.
There is no multi-kanji composition to unpack.
Learn the noun's spatial patterns rather than adding an adjective ending to it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

縦 (たて), "vertical direction," contrasts with the horizontal sense.
隣 (となり) means the adjoining or neighbouring place: 隣の席 (となりのせき), "the neighbouring seat."
横 emphasizes the side, while 隣 emphasizes adjacency. Their meanings can overlap, but they are not identical in every description.

## Distinctive meaning

横 does not specify "left" or "right." A bag at either side of a desk can be at its 横.
Also, "beside" does not mean that the object itself is lying horizontally: the position and orientation uses must be read in context.
