# 能力 (のうりょく) - ability; capability

## Meaning

能力 means ability or capability: what a person, group, or system can do. It is a noun. Use 能力がある, "have the ability," 能力を伸ばす (のうりょくをのばす), "develop ability," or verb + 能力 to specify the task.

- 彼女には問題を解決する能力があります。 (かのじょにはもんだいをかいけつするのうりょくがあります。) - She has the ability to solve problems.
- 練習を重ねて、自分の能力を伸ばしたいです。 (れんしゅうをかさねて、じぶんののうりょくをのばしたいです。) - I want to develop my abilities through repeated practice.

解決する directly modifies 能力; no の is inserted between that verb and the noun.

## How the kanji combine

[能](../kanji/能.md) contributes ability, and [力](../kanji/力.md) contributes power or capacity. Their related meanings reinforce capability. The on readings are ノウ + リョク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[才能](才能.md) (さいのう) emphasizes talent or aptitude. [技術](技術.md) (ぎじゅつ) concerns skill or technique, and [知識](知識.md) (ちしき) concerns knowledge. Each can contribute to 能力 without being identical to it.

## Distinctive meaning

能力 is not limited to physical strength and does not by itself say whether an ability is innate or acquired. For one immediate action, a form such as 読める (よめる), "can read," may express ability directly without the abstract noun. 能力 lets you discuss that capacity as something evaluated or developed.
