# 破く (やぶく) - tear; rip

## Meaning

破く means to tear or rip something such as paper or fabric. It is a transitive godan verb; を marks what is torn.

- 古い手紙を破きました。
  (ふるいてがみをやぶきました。) - I tore up an old letter.
- 紙を二つに破いた。
  (かみをふたつにやぶいた。) - I tore the paper in two.

Useful forms are 破きます (やぶきます), 破いて (やぶいて), and 破かない (やぶかない). The て-form is not やぶくて.

## How the kanji combine

[破](../kanji/破.md) contributes tearing or breaking and supplies the native stem やぶ; く is the verb ending. Learn the complete verb やぶく even though the kanji card also lists related やぶる and やぶれる readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[破る](破る.md) (やぶる) overlaps directly in tearing. [裂く](裂く.md) (さく) can emphasize splitting or tearing apart, and [千切る](千切る.md) (ちぎる) tearing off or into pieces.

[破れる](破れる.md) (やぶれる) is intransitive in its tearing sense: 紙が破れた, the paper tore. Compare 紙を破いた, someone tore the paper.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not automatically transfer all of 破る's other meanings to 破く. Defeating an opponent or breaking a promise is not the ordinary paper-and-fabric use taught here.
