# にやにや — grinning; smirking

## Meaning

にやにや depicts a grin or smirk, often suggesting private amusement, satisfaction, or something the person is not saying.

- 弟はスマホを見ながらにやにやしていた。 — My younger brother was grinning to himself while looking at his phone.
- 彼は答えを知っているらしく、にやにやと笑った。 — Apparently knowing the answer, he gave a knowing grin.

Common forms are にやにやする, にやにや笑う, and にやにやと笑う. にやにやした顔 describes a grinning or smirking face.

## How the kanji combine

This repeated kana mimetic represents a visible expression, not necessarily any audible sound. It is not a kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[にこにこ](にこにこ.md) gives a friendlier, more openly cheerful impression. [にっこり](にっこり.md) often depicts a warm or broad smile as a distinct moment. [くすくす](くすくす.md) instead describes quiet audible laughter.

## Distinctive meaning

にやにや often carries a less favorable observer's judgment than にこにこ. Still, it need not prove malice, mockery, or sexual intent: remembering a private joke can produce the same expression. Translate according to context rather than always choosing the strongest English "sneering."
