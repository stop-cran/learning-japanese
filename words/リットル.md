# リットル - litre; liter

## Meaning

リットル is the litre, a unit of volume. One litre is one cubic decimetre:
1 L = 1000 mL. "Liter" is the American English spelling of the same unit.

- 水を一リットル飲みました。 (みずをいちリットルのみました。) - I drank one litre of water.
- この容器には二リットル入ります。 (このようきにはにリットルはいります。) - This container holds two litres.

## How the kanji combine

The primary spelling is katakana and contains no kanji.
The word is borrowed from French litre.
立 is also recorded as a written representation of the unit;
that does not make it the primary spelling or a component of リットル.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ミリリットル means millilitre, one thousandth of a litre.
[量](量.md) (りょう) is quantity or amount; 容量
(ようりょう) is capacity. Neither is the name of this particular unit.

## Distinctive meaning

リットル measures volume, not mass.
Do not assume that one litre of every substance weighs one kilogram.
Use the unit after a number, as in 二リットル, and recognize L as its symbol.
