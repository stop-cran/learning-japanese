# 始める (はじめる) — start something

## Meaning

始める means to start or begin something. In this use it is transitive: the activity or thing started is marked with を.

- 日本語の勉強を始めました。 (にほんごのべんきょうをはじめました。) — I started studying Japanese.
- 先生が授業を始めます。 (せんせいがじゅぎょうをはじめます。) — The teacher starts the lesson.

## How the kanji combine

This is one kanji plus okurigana: [始](../kanji/始.md) supplies はじ, followed by める. It is an ichidan verb: 始めます はじめます, 始めた はじめた.

As a suffix, -始める attaches to a verb's ます-stem: 読む → 読みます → 読み始める (よみはじめる), "begin to read." This is a productive grammatical combination, not an on+on compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 始まる はじまる describes something beginning: 授業が始まります (じゅぎょうがはじまります), "the lesson starts." The lesson is the subject, not an object.
- 開始する かいしする also means to start and is common in more formal event or activity descriptions.
- [終わる](終わる.md) おわる, "end, finish," marks the other end of an activity.

## Distinctive meaning

The difference between 始める and 始まる is grammatical, not simply "deliberate" versus "accidental." A scheduled lesson can 始まる, while its teacher 始める the lesson. Select the verb according to whether you describe starting something or something starting.
