# 婚約 (こんやく) - engagement to marry

## Meaning

婚約 is the noun "engagement" in the sense of an agreement to marry. 婚約する means to become engaged; the partner is marked with と. 婚約している describes the resulting engaged state.

- 姉は先月、婚約しました。 (あねはせんげつ、こんやくしました。) - My older sister got engaged last month.
- 彼は大学時代の友人と婚約しています。 (かれはだいがくじだいのゆうじんとこんやくしています。) - He is engaged to a friend from his university days.

婚約者 (こんやくしゃ) means a fiance or fiancee. The Japanese noun does not itself specify the person's gender.

## How the kanji combine

[婚](../kanji/婚.md) contributes marriage, and [約](../kanji/約.md) contributes an agreement or promise. Marriage specifies the subject of the agreement: a promise to marry. The reading is こんやく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結婚](結婚.md) (けっこん) is marriage, not engagement. [約束](約束.md) (やくそく) is a broader promise or arrangement and can concern many things besides marriage.

## Distinctive meaning

English "engagement" also covers appointments, employment, and involvement. 婚約 does not express those meanings. 婚約した and 結婚した therefore describe different events. The word alone does not tell you the date of the wedding or what ceremony took place; it identifies the commitment to marry.
