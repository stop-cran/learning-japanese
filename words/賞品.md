# 賞品 (しょうひん) - prize item; trophy

## Meaning

賞品 is an item given as a prize, such as a trophy or other goods. Use 賞品をもらう (しょうひんをもらう), "receive a prize," or 賞品を贈る (しょうひんをおくる), "present a prize."

- コンクールで賞品をもらいました。 (コンクールでしょうひんをもらいました。) - I received a prize in the competition.
- 優勝者には賞品として自転車が贈られます。 (ゆうしょうしゃにはしょうひんとしてじてんしゃがおくられます。) - The winner will be presented with a bicycle as a prize.

として means "as" in the second sentence. The prize need not be a trophy.

## How the kanji combine

[賞](../kanji/賞.md) contributes an award, and [品](../kanji/品.md) an item or article. 賞 specifies why the item is given. Read ショウ + ヒン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[賞金](賞金.md) (しょうきん) specifically names prize money. [賞](賞.md) (しょう) is a broader prize or award word. [商品](商品.md) is also read しょうひん but means merchandise or goods for sale, not an award item.

## Distinctive meaning

A bicycle offered to a winner is a 賞品 because it is awarded as a prize. The same bicycle can be a 商品 while offered for sale. The difference is its role in the situation, not what material it is made from.
