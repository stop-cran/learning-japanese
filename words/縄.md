# 縄 (なわ) - rope; cord

## Meaning

縄 is a noun for rope or cord. It names the finished object used for tying, fastening, or similar purposes, rather than the plant or raw fiber from which it may be made.

- 縄で箱をしっかり縛りました。 (なわではこをしっかりしばりました。) - I tied the box securely with rope.
- 子どもたちは長い縄で遊んでいます。 (こどもたちはながいなわであそんでいます。) - The children are playing with a long rope.

で marks the material or tool used in these examples. 縄を結ぶ (なわをむすぶ) means "tie a rope" or "tie a knot in a rope," according to context.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 縄 uses the native reading なわ. There is no multi-kanji relationship. Recognize the same word in 縄跳び (なわとび), "jump rope; skipping."

## Synonyms and antonyms

ロープ is a loanword for rope and overlaps with 縄. ひも means string or cord and often suggests a thinner object, but there is no universal thickness boundary separating the words. A specific product's name and use help determine the natural choice.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's gloss "hemp" is too broad as a definition: hemp can be one rope material, but 縄 is not the plant or fiber itself. Nor does 縄 alone guarantee that a rope is made of a particular natural material. Read a material modifier if that distinction matters.
