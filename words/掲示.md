# 掲示 (けいじ) - posting a notice; notice

## Meaning

掲示 is a notice or the posting/displaying of information for people to read.
The transitive verb **掲示する** takes the information with **を** and the posting location with **に**.

- 試験の日程を掲示板に掲示しました。 (しけんのにっていをけいじばんにけいじしました。) - We posted the exam schedule on the noticeboard.
- 入口の掲示を読んでください。 (いりぐちのけいじをよんでください。) - Please read the notice at the entrance.

掲示板 (けいじばん) means a noticeboard or bulletin board. A sentence with 掲示されている
(けいじされている) describes information that has been posted.

## How the kanji combine

掲 contributes putting up or holding forth; [示](../kanji/示.md) contributes showing.
Together they express putting information on display. The on readings ケイ + ジ give けいじ.
The components describe related actions, not a type of paper.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知らせ](知らせ.md) (しらせ), "notice/news," identifies information communicated, without specifying posting.
[広告](広告.md) (こうこく) can advertise something; 掲示 need not be promotional.
[貼る](貼る.md) (はる) describes sticking something onto a surface, which may be the physical way a notice is posted.

## Distinctive meaning

掲示 highlights making information visible at a place where readers can consult it.
It is not a general replacement for speaking an announcement.
In 掲示を読む, the noun refers to the displayed notice; in 掲示する, it names the act of putting information up.
