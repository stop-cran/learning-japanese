# 鯨 (くじら) — whale

## Meaning

鯨 means **whale**. A whale is a mammal, despite the fish-shaped left part of the kanji.

- 海で鯨を見ました。 (うみでくじらをみました。) — I saw a whale at sea.
- 大きな鯨がいます。 (おおきなくじらがいます。) — There is a large whale.

In the first example, で marks where the sighting happens and を marks what is seen; [見る](見る.md) みる appears as 見ました. In the second, が identifies the living animal, which takes います.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [鯨](../kanji/鯨.md) writes the whole native noun くじら, with no compound to split or kana ending to add. **くじら** and **クジラ** are also spellings; JMdict labels this animal name usually written in kana.

The same character uses ゲイ in [捕鯨](捕鯨.md) ほげい, whaling. That does not change the primary reading of the standalone noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[動物](動物.md) どうぶつ, animal, is a broader category, not a precise synonym. [魚](魚.md) さかな, fish, names a different biological category; whales are not fish.

[海](海.md) うみ, sea, names a habitat rather than the animal. There is no useful direct antonym for whale.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn each complete compound's reading. **鯨肉** can be げいにく or くじらにく, both meaning whale meat. Neither makes ゲイ the ordinary pronunciation to substitute for standalone くじら.

The dictionary separately records archaic **いさな** and **いさ**, both spelled 鯨 among their recorded forms. This article practises the present-day animal noun くじら, not those historical readings.
