# 日の出 (ひので) - sunrise; time of sunrise

## Meaning

日の出 is sunrise: the sun appearing above the horizon, or the time
when that happens. It is a noun.

- 日の出前に家を出ました。 (ひのでまえにいえをでました。) - I left home before sunrise.
- 山の上で日の出を見ました。 (やまのうえでひのでをみました。) - I watched the sunrise from the mountaintop.

日の出の時刻 (ひのでのじこく) means the time of sunrise.
The word does not contain a fixed clock time.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md), ひ, supplies the sun; の connects it to
[出](../kanji/出.md), で, related to [出る](出る.md) (でる), come out.
The native combination concerns the sun coming into view.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[日の入り](日の入り.md) (ひのいり), sunset, is the opposite daily event.
[夜明け](夜明け.md) (よあけ) and [明け方](明け方.md) (あけがた)
concern daybreak, the broader transition from night to daylight.
They need not identify the instant when the sun itself appears.

## Distinctive meaning

日の出 names an event or its time, not the sun as an object.
今日の日の出は早い (きょうのひのでははやい), "Sunrise is early today,"
concerns timing rather than the speed of the sun's movement.
Actual sunrise times vary with location and season.
