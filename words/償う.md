# 償う (つぐなう) - make amends; compensate for loss

## Meaning

償う is a transitive godan verb: make up for a loss or debt, or make amends
for wrongdoing. The loss, debt or wrongdoing being made up for takes を.
Compensation can involve money or goods; making amends can also involve actions.

- 彼は損害を償いました。 (かれはそんがいをつぐないました。) - He compensated for the damage.
- 彼は自分の過ちを償おうとしています。 (かれはじぶんのあやまちをつぐなおうとしています。) - He is trying to make amends for his mistake.

Forms include 償います (つぐないます), 償わない (つぐなわない),
償った (つぐなった), and 償って (つぐなって).

## How the kanji combine

[償](../kanji/償.md) contributes making good a loss or making amends.
つぐな is represented by the kanji; う is okurigana.
The compound reading ショウ does not replace つぐな in this verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[補償](補償.md) (ほしょう), used with する, focuses on compensation for loss.
[補う](補う.md) (おぎなう) is broader supplementation or making up for a shortage;
the words can overlap in loss contexts.
Apologizing is not necessarily the same as making amends, and there is no
single opposite that reverses both the compensation and atonement senses.

## Distinctive meaning

償う does not always imply moral guilt: [損害](損害.md) (そんがい), damage or loss,
can be its object. Conversely, making amends for a mistake need not mean
simply paying money. Context determines what would count as making good the harm.

The dictionary also records 償う as つぐのう. Nikkoku identifies つぐなう as
the form much used today; this article teaches つぐなう rather than treating
every recorded pronunciation as the modern default.
