# 降り出す (ふりだす) -- start raining or snowing

## Meaning

降り出す describes rain or snow beginning to fall. It is intransitive: the precipitation takes が.

- 家を出たら、雨が降り出しました。 (いえをでたら、あめがふりだしました。) -- When I left home, it started raining.
- 夜になって、雪が降り出しました。 (よるになって、ゆきがふりだしました。) -- It began to snow after night fell.

The compound is a godan verb ending in す: 降り出します (ふりだします), 降り出して (ふりだして), and 降り出した (ふりだした).

## How the kanji combine

[降](../kanji/降.md) appears in 降り, the combining form of 降る (ふる), fall as rain or snow. [出](../kanji/出.md) appears in 出す, which adds the onset of the action here, not literal outward movement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

降り始める (ふりはじめる) also means to begin falling. 雨がやむ (あめがやむ), "the rain stops," is the useful opposite event.

## Distinctive meaning

降り出す focuses on the beginning, often experienced as a new or sudden development. 降っている (ふっている) reports ongoing rain or snow without saying when it started. The reading is ふりだす, not a form of 降りる (おりる), get off or descend.
