# すべすべ — silky smooth to the touch

## Meaning

Smooth, sleek, and free of noticeable roughness under the hand, often skin, stone, or a finished surface.

- このせっけんを使うと、肌（はだ）がすべすべになる。 — This soap leaves my skin feeling smooth.
- 川で拾った石は、すべすべしていた。 — The stone I picked up in the river felt smooth.

## How the kanji combine

A native mimetic with a repeated すべ pattern. Kana is the practical spelling here; JMdict records rare kanji spellings 滑々 and 滑滑. They are not required for writing the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[つるつる](つるつる.md) overlaps but can foreground shine or slipperiness. [さらさら](さらさら.md) often suggests a dry, light, free-flowing smoothness. [ざらざら](ざらざら.md) is the tactile contrast of a gritty or rough surface.

## Distinctive meaning

Use すべすべする, すべすべした肌, or すべすべの肌. Unlike a warning that a floor is つるつる滑る, this word commonly invites attention to the smooth feel itself. “Silky” is an English comparison, not a claim that the material contains silk or that it must look shiny.
