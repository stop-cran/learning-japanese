# カップ (カップ) - cup

## Meaning

カップ is a cup, especially a vessel such as a coffee cup. The word also appears in measurement, food-container names, and names of sporting trophies or competitions.

- カップにコーヒーを入れました。 (カップにコーヒーをいれました。) - I poured coffee into the cup.
- このカップには取っ手があります。 (このカップにはとってがあります。) - This cup has a handle.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "cup." The small ッ marks a held consonant before プ: カップ, not カプ. It has no written kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[コップ](コップ.md) often suggests a drinking glass or tumbler. マグカップ is a mug. These terms overlap; a handle is a useful common association, not an absolute rule separating every カップ from every コップ.

## Distinctive meaning

カップ can extend beyond an ordinary drinking vessel. For recipe measurements, follow the recipe's specified standard rather than treating an arbitrary coffee cup as a precise unit. In daily use, compounds such as コーヒーカップ make the intended kind clear.
