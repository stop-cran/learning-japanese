# 寂しい (さびしい) — lonely; lonesome

## Meaning

寂しい describes feeling lonely or sensing the absence of companionship or liveliness. It is an i-adjective: 寂しかった means "was lonely," and 寂しくない means "not lonely."

- 友達が帰って、寂しくなりました。 (ともだちがかえって、さびしくなりました。) — After my friends went home, I began to feel lonely.
- 夜の駅は寂しかったです。 (よるのえきはさびしかったです。) — The station felt deserted at night.

The word can describe both a person's feeling and a lonely, desolate place.

## How the kanji combine

寂 carries an association with quietness or loneliness; しい completes the adjective. さみしい is another established reading, while this card uses さびしい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

悲しい (かなしい) means sad more broadly. にぎやか, "lively/bustling," contrasts with the atmosphere of a deserted place, but is not an exact opposite of every personal feeling.

## Distinctive meaning

Being physically alone and feeling lonely are different. 一人 (ひとり) states that someone is alone; 寂しい describes an emotional or atmospheric lack of company or warmth.
