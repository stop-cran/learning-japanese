# できれば (できれば) - if possible

## Meaning

できれば introduces a wish or request as conditional on what is possible.
It means "if possible," not "certainly" or "if I want to."

- できれば、明日までに返事をください。 (できれば、あしたまでにへんじをください。) - If possible, please reply by tomorrow.
- できれば窓側の席に座りたいです。 (できればまどがわのせきにすわりたいです。) - I would like to sit by the window if possible.

In the first example, the request remains clear, but the speaker acknowledges
that meeting the deadline may depend on circumstances.

## How the kanji combine

This is the ば-conditional form of [できる](できる.md), "be possible; be able."
For that ichidan verb, replace the final る with れば: できる → できれば.
出来れば is also a dictionary-listed spelling, but kana is usual.

## Synonyms and antonyms

可能なら (かのうなら), "if possible," gives a comparable condition.
必ず (かならず), "without fail," can contrast in the firmness of a request,
but it is not a grammatical opposite to できれば.

## Distinctive meaning

できれば can soften a request without making the rest of the sentence polite
by itself. Compare できればお願いします (できればおねがいします), "Please, if
possible," with a blunt command after できれば. The predicate and relationship
between speakers still determine the overall tone. This expression's meaning
is dictionary-supported even though the community source supplies no gloss.
