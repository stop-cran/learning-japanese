# タオル - towel

## Meaning

タオル is a towel, used for drying or wiping. It is a noun. In タオルで拭く (タオルでふく), "wipe with a towel," で marks the tool and を marks what is wiped.

- タオルで手を拭きました。 (タオルでてをふきました。) - I dried my hands with a towel.
- 新しいタオルを一枚ください。 (あたらしいタオルをいちまいください。) - Please give me one fresh towel.

枚 (まい) counts towels as flat items: 一枚 (いちまい), 二枚 (にまい). The word does not by itself specify the towel's size.

## How the kanji combine

タオル is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "towel." There are no kanji components. Pronounce the three morae タ・オ・ル; do not insert a long-vowel mark into the spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ハンカチ](ハンカチ.md) is a handkerchief. Its function can overlap with a small towel, but it is not the general name for a bath towel. バスタオル explicitly identifies a bath towel.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's parenthetical "(hand)" is not a restriction to hand towels. Context or a compound identifies the kind. When borrowing one, a phrase such as タオルを貸してください (タオルをかしてください), "Please lend me a towel," requests the object rather than the action of drying.
