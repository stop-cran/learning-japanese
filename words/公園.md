# 公園 (こうえん) — public park

## Meaning

公園 is a public park: a place people visit for outdoor recreation, a walk, or a rest.
The word does not specify its size or what facilities it contains.

- 日曜日に公園で友達と遊びました。 (にちようびにこうえんでともだちとあそびました。) — I spent time playing with friends in the park on Sunday.
- 公園に行きましょう。 (こうえんにいきましょう。) — Let's go to the park.

Use で for an activity taking place in the park and に for the destination of 行く.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md), "public," qualifies [園](../kanji/園.md), "park; garden."
The result is a public park, not just any private garden.
Both readings are on readings: コウ + エン. Keep the long こう distinct from a short こ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[庭](庭.md) (にわ) means a garden or yard, for example one belonging to a house.
広場 (ひろば) is a square, plaza, or open space; a park may contain one without the two words being equivalent.
庭園 (ていえん) also means garden, but does not by itself specify public access.
There is no general antonym of 公園.

## Distinctive meaning

[公演](公演.md) is also pronounced こうえん but means a public performance.
The second kanji separates the place from the event.
JMdict notes 公苑 (こうえん) as an occasional spelling in park names; preserve a particular park's written name rather than changing it to match this headword.
