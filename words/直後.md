# 直後 (ちょくご) - immediately after

## Meaning

直後 means immediately after an event.
Use **noun + の直後** or **plain past verb + 直後**.
に can mark when the next event occurs.

- 会議の直後に電話がありました。
  (かいぎのちょくごにでんわがありました。) - There was a phone call immediately after the meeting.
- 家を出た直後に雨が降り始めました。
  (いえをでたちょくごにあめがふりはじめました。) - It started raining just after I left home.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) contributes immediacy, and
[後](../kanji/後.md) the later side of time.
Read チョク + ゴ. The word locates something directly after a reference event.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[後](後.md) (あと), after, does not itself require a short interval.
[以後](以後.md) (いご) can cover the period from a point onward.
[すぐに](すぐに.md), right away, and [直ちに](直ちに.md) (ただちに),
immediately, emphasize prompt timing without themselves naming the preceding event.
[直前](直前.md) (ちょくぜん), just before, gives the opposite temporal relation.

## Distinctive meaning

直後 does not specify an exact number of seconds.
The interval is understood relative to the event and situation.
[早速](早速.md) (さっそく) and [たちまち](たちまち.md)
also overlap with prompt or rapid developments, but foreground different aspects.
Daijisen additionally records spatial right behind, overlapping
[後ろ](後ろ.md) (うしろ). That is separately sourced recognition:
the pinned principal entry has only the immediately-following sense.
