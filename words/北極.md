# 北極 (ほっきょく) - North Pole; Arctic

## Meaning

北極 is the North Pole. It can also refer to the Arctic region, as a shortened form associated with 北極圏 (ほっきょくけん), the Arctic Circle or Arctic region.

- 北極は北緯九十度にあります。 (ほっきょくはほくいきゅうじゅうどにあります。) - The North Pole is at ninety degrees north latitude.
- 研究者たちは北極の氷を調べています。 (けんきゅうしゃたちはほっきょくのこおりをしらべています。) - The researchers are studying Arctic ice.

The first sentence identifies the geographic point; the second can concern the broader region.

## How the kanji combine

[北](../kanji/北.md) contributes north; [極](../kanji/極.md) contributes a pole or extreme point. This is modifier plus head: the northern pole. ホク + キョク becomes **ほっきょく**, with small っ, not ほくきょく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[南極](南極.md) (なんきょく) is the South Pole or Antarctic. [北](北.md) (きた) is north as a direction, not by itself the North Pole.

## Distinctive meaning

Context determines whether 北極 means the exact pole or the Arctic more broadly. The Arctic and Antarctic are not interchangeable places; the shared 極 does not erase the north/south distinction.
