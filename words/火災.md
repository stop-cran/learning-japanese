# 火災 (かさい) - destructive fire

## Meaning

火災 is a noun for a fire as a damaging event or disaster, rather than a flame being used normally. Useful patterns include 火災が起きる (かさいがおきる), "a fire breaks out," and 火災を防ぐ (かさいをふせぐ), "prevent fires."

- 昨夜、工場で火災が起きました。 (さくや、こうじょうでかさいがおきました。) - A fire broke out at a factory last night.
- 火災の原因を調べています。 (かさいのげんいんをしらべています。) - They are investigating the cause of the fire.

The location of the incident takes で in the first example.

## How the kanji combine

[火](../kanji/火.md) means fire; 災 means disaster or calamity. 火 identifies the kind of disaster: a fire disaster. The readings are カ + サイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[火事](火事.md) (かじ) overlaps closely and is useful in everyday talk about a fire. 火災 fits reports, safety notices, and compounds such as 火災保険 (かさいほけん), "fire insurance."

[火](火.md) (ひ) names fire itself and can refer to a controlled cooking flame.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "conflagration" should not make you assume that 火災 requires an enormous blaze. The important distinction is an unwanted fire incident, not a fixed minimum size. [事故](事故.md) (じこ), "accident," is a broader category, not an antonym; a fire's cause may be accidental or deliberate.
