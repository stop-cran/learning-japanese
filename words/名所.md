# 名所 (めいしょ) - famous place; sights

## Meaning

名所 is a noted place, especially one known for scenery, history, or a local attraction.
In travel contexts its English translation can be "the sights."

- 京都の名所を訪ねました。 (きょうとのめいしょをたずねました。) - I visited famous places in Kyoto.
- ここは桜の名所です。 (ここはさくらのめいしょです。) - This is a famous spot for cherry blossoms.

名所旧跡 (めいしょきゅうせき) is an expression for famous places and historic sites.

## How the kanji combine

[名](../kanji/名.md) contributes fame or renown, and [所](../kanji/所.md) contributes a place.
This modifier-plus-head compound uses メイ + ショ.
Learn the word's reading めいしょ rather than reading 所 as ところ here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[場所](場所.md) (ばしょ) is a place in general.
観光地 (かんこうち), "tourist destination," overlaps but emphasizes tourism.
A [目印](目印.md) (めじるし), "landmark; identifying mark," may be a famous site,
but an ordinary mailbox can also be a 目印 without becoming a 名所.

## Distinctive meaning

名所 names the place or attraction, not just the scenery seen from it.
桜の名所 says what makes the place notable; it does not mean that 桜 is part of its proper name.
The card teaches the modern reading めいしょ, not the separate old uses of などころ.
