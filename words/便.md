# 便 (びん) - transport service; mail

## Meaning

便, read **びん**, can mean a scheduled transport service, flight, trip, or sailing.
It also refers to mail or postal delivery, including its use as a suffix in compounds.
The vehicle, destination, or delivery method supplies the context.

- 東京行きの次の便は何時ですか。 (とうきょうゆきのつぎのびんはなんじですか。) - What time is the next service to Tokyo?
- この荷物は航空便で送ります。 (このにもつはこうくうびんでおくります。) - I will send this parcel by airmail.

The first question could concern a flight or another service already being discussed.
The second uses **delivery method + で**.

## How the kanji combine

[便](../kanji/便.md) has several readings and related meanings, but this card teaches びん.
It stands alone or follows another element, as in 航空便 (こうくうびん).
Do not assume that every meaning attached to the character belongs to this reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飛行機](飛行機.md) (ひこうき) is an airplane, whereas a 便 can identify the service it operates.
[郵便](郵便.md) (ゆうびん) names mail or the postal service.
There is no useful general antonym covering both transport and mail.

## Distinctive meaning

便 distinguishes a particular service or delivery arrangement rather than simply naming travel itself.
This card deliberately retains the source list's びん identity while teaching dictionary-supported meanings.
The list's English gloss "way, means" conflicts with that entry; the original compiler's intended sense remains uncertain.
The supported Japanese meanings are not uncertain, and this editorial choice does not recover the compiler's intent.
