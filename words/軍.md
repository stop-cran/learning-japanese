# 軍 (ぐん) - army; armed forces

## Meaning

軍 is a noun for an army, troops, or armed forces. It can also refer to the military authorities as an institution. **軍の + noun** means "military ..." or "the army's ..."

- 軍が被災地に食料を運びました。 (ぐんがひさいちにしょくりょうをはこびました。) - The armed forces transported food to the disaster area.
- 軍は声明を発表しました。 (ぐんはせいめいをはっぴょうしました。) - The military issued a statement.

The first sentence emphasizes personnel acting; the second can present the institution as the source of an announcement.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 軍 supplies the whole on-reading noun ぐん. It also appears within longer military terms. A one-character kango word still has a complete meaning; it need not be expanded into two characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[軍隊](軍隊.md) (ぐんたい) also means armed forces or troops and overlaps with 軍. 兵士 (へいし) names an individual soldier, whereas 軍 can name the organized force as a whole. They are different levels of description, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

The English "army" may specifically mean land forces, but 軍 can cover the armed forces more broadly. Read compounds and national context before choosing a branch-specific translation. 軍 is also used for groups or teams in some expressions; that does not make every such group literally military.
