# 祭る (まつる) - enshrine; worship

## Meaning

祭る means to enshrine or deify, and to pray to or worship.
It is a transitive godan verb: the deity or spirit honored takes を.
Forms include 祭ります, 祭った, 祭って, and 祭らない.

- この神社は海の神を祭っています。 (このじんじゃはうみのかみをまつっています。) - This shrine enshrines a sea deity.
- 家の神棚に神様を祭っています。 (いえのかみだなにかみさまをまつっています。) - We enshrine a deity at the household shrine.

祀る is another dictionary spelling of the same verb.

## How the kanji combine

[祭](../kanji/祭.md) supplies the native stem まつ, with る completing
the verb. The character's associations with ritual and worship support
the meaning; they are not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[祈る](祈る.md) (いのる) means pray or wish for, and
[拝む](拝む.md) (おがむ) includes worship or prayer.
They overlap with worship without making every wish an act of enshrining.
[お参り](お参り.md) (おまいり) concerns a religious visit or worship.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun [祭](祭.md) (まつり), also written 祭り, names a festival.
Do not infer that 祭る means holding any festival or throwing a party.
The alternate spelling 祀る belongs to the verb, not a free substitute
for the noun 祭り.
