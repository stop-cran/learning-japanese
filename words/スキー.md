# スキー (スキー) - skiing; skis

## Meaning

スキー is a noun for skiing and also for the skis used in the sport. The accompanying verb and situation show whether it names the activity or equipment.

- 冬休みに友達とスキーに行きます。 (ふゆやすみにともだちとスキーにいきます。) - I will go skiing with friends during the winter holiday.
- 店でスキーを借りました。 (みせでスキーをかりました。) - I rented skis at the shop.

スキーをする means to ski or go skiing. スキーに行く uses に for the purpose of going; in スキーを借りる, を marks the equipment being rented.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword without kanji components. The long-vowel mark after キ is part of the spelling and pronunciation: スキー. スキー板 (スキーいた) explicitly names the skis as equipment.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[スケート](スケート.md) is skating, a different activity. [滑る](滑る.md) (すべる), "slide; glide; slip," can describe movement on skis but does not itself name the sport or its equipment. There is no useful single antonym for skiing.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese does not require a plural ending on スキー when it means skis. The number and whether one is discussing equipment come from context. Conversely, a sentence about enjoying スキー can refer to the activity rather than admiring a particular pair of skis.
