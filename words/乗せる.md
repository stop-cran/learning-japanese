# 乗せる (のせる) - give a ride; put on

## Meaning

乗せる is a transitive ichidan verb: put something on something, help someone aboard, or give someone a ride. Use **person or thing を + supporting place or vehicle に乗せる**.

- 友達を車に乗せました。 (ともだちをくるまにのせました。) - I gave my friend a ride in the car.
- 子供を膝に乗せました。 (こどもをひざにのせました。) - I put the child on my lap.

The forms include 乗せます, 乗せて, and 乗せない. The を-marked person is placed or carried; the に-marked noun identifies where.

## How the kanji combine

[乗](../kanji/乗.md) contributes getting on or riding. The native verb のせる is written with せる as okurigana. It is related to [乗る](乗る.md) (のる), "get on; ride."

## Synonyms and antonyms

Compare 友達が車に乗る, "My friend gets in the car," with 友達を車に乗せる, "I let my friend into the car/give them a ride." [降ろす](降ろす.md) (おろす), "let off; unload," gives the opposite transport direction.

## Distinctive meaning

載せる shares this reading and some placement/loading uses; JMdict especially associates it with loading luggage. Its publishing sense, 新聞に記事を載せる (しんぶんにきじをのせる), "publish an article in a newspaper," is restricted to 載せる. Do not transfer that sense to the spelling 乗せる merely because the entry is shared.
