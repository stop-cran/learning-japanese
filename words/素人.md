# 素人 (しろうと) - amateur; layperson; nonexpert

## Meaning

素人 is an amateur, layperson, or nonexpert in a field. It can describe
someone without specialized knowledge or someone who does not pursue
the activity professionally.

- 私は法律については素人です。 (わたしはほうりつについてはしろうとです。) - I am a layperson when it comes to law.
- 素人なので、専門家に相談しました。 (しろうとなので、せんもんかにそうだんしました。) - Since I am not an expert, I consulted a specialist.

The field can be supplied by context. 素人だから does not mean that
the person is incapable of learning or is unintelligent.

## How the kanji combine

The spelling contains 素 and [人](../kanji/人.md), a person. The dictionary
marks しろうと as a special whole-word reading: learn 素人 together rather
than assigning 素 a general reading しろ or 人 a reading うと.

## Synonyms and antonyms

初心者 (しょしんしゃ) is a beginner, someone just starting.
素人 need not be new to a long-term hobby.
玄人 (くろうと) and [プロ](プロ.md), professional, contrast with the
amateur or nonexpert sense.

## Distinctive meaning

An amateur can be skilled; lack of professional status and lack of
experience are not identical. Calling someone else a 素人 can dismiss
their expertise, so the self-descriptive examples above are useful
starting points. This article selects the ordinary amateur/nonexpert
sense, not every specialized or historical dictionary use.
