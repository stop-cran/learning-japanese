# 物音 (ものおと) - noise; sound

## Meaning

物音 is a sound or noise from something happening, such as movement, a knock,
or an object falling. It can be used when the exact source is unknown.
Use 物音がする, "there is a sound," or 物音を立てる
(ものおとをたてる), "make a noise."

- 隣の部屋で物音がしました。 (となりのへやでものおとがしました。) - There was a noise in the next room.
- 赤ちゃんが寝ているので、物音を立てないでください。 (あかちゃんがねているので、ものおとをたてないでください。) - The baby is asleep, so please don't make noise.

The first sentence reports a sound without identifying what caused it.

## How the kanji combine

[物](../kanji/物.md) means a thing, and [音](../kanji/音.md) means sound.
The relationship is source plus sound: a sound made by something.
The native readings combine as もの + おと; keep both vowels at the join.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[音](音.md) (おと) is the broader sound word.
[声](声.md) (こえ) names a voice, so it identifies a source more specifically.
A quiet interval contrasts with hearing 物音, but a noise need not be loud
or disturbing.

## Distinctive meaning

物音 often lets a speaker report that something was heard before explaining
its cause. It does not inherently mean an intruder, a frightening event,
or a very loud crash. In 物音一つしない (ものおとひとつしない),
"not a sound," the negative construction emphasizes silence rather than
describing a particular kind of object.
