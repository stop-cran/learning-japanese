# 主張 (しゅちょう) - assertion; claim

## Meaning

主張 is a claim, assertion, or position someone maintains. 主張する means to
assert or advocate it. Use content + を + 主張する, or a plain clause + と +
主張する.

- 彼は自分の権利を主張しました。 (かれはじぶんのけんりをしゅちょうしました。) - He asserted his rights.
- 彼女はその方法が安全だと主張しています。 (かのじょはそのほうほうがあんぜんだとしゅちょうしています。) - She maintains that the method is safe.

Keep だ before と after a な-adjective or noun predicate: 安全だと.
The sentence reports her position without endorsing it as fact.

## How the kanji combine

[主](../kanji/主.md) contributes a central or governing position; 張 contributes
stretching or putting forth. Together the word concerns putting forward and
maintaining a position. The on readings are シュ + チョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[意見](意見.md) (いけん) is an opinion; 主張 emphasizes asserting or maintaining
one. [賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい) means agreement or support, which may be a
response to someone's claim rather than an opposite of claiming.

## Distinctive meaning

主張 is not a general word for any request. Asking someone to pass the salt
does not normally constitute 主張. The word highlights a position, right, or
view being put forward, whether quietly reasoned or strongly insisted upon;
it does not itself prove that the speaker is shouting.
