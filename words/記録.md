# 記録 (きろく) - record; log

## Meaning

記録 is a record or log, and **記録する** means "record; put on record." The information recorded takes を. It can also name a performance record, as in sport.

- 毎日の体温を記録しています。 (まいにちのたいおんをきろくしています。) - I record my temperature every day.
- 彼女は大会で新しい記録を出しました。 (かのじょはたいかいであたらしいきろくをだしました。) - She set a new record at the competition.

**記録を残す** (きろくをのこす), "leave/keep a record," emphasizes making information available afterward.

## How the kanji combine

[記](../kanji/記.md) means recording or writing down, and 録 also contributes recording or registering information. The related meanings reinforce each other in キ + ロク. The word is not limited to handwriting; a record can be electronic.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[記憶](記憶.md) (きおく) is memory or remembering, which need not produce an external record. [記入](記入.md) (きにゅう) is entering information, such as filling in a form; it focuses on that writing action.

## Distinctive meaning

The performance sense is not an unrelated instruction to write something down. A runner's 記録 may be a measured time, and 記録を更新する (きろくをこうしんする) can mean beating a previous record. In a database context the same phrase can mean updating stored information. The object and setting resolve the difference.
