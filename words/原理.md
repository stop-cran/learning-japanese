# 原理 (げんり) - underlying principle

## Meaning

原理 is a fundamental principle, truth, or law underlying how something
works or how an explanation is built. It is a noun.

- この機械が動く原理を説明してください。 (このきかいがうごくげんりをせつめいしてください。) - Please explain the principle by which this machine works.
- 原理は分かりましたが、使い方はまだ分かりません。 (げんりはわかりましたが、つかいかたはまだわかりません。) - I understand the underlying principle, but I still do not know how to use it.

The word can be used in science and in more abstract reasoning.
The dictionary gloss "theory" concerns an explanatory foundation here,
not every tentative guess.

## How the kanji combine

[原](../kanji/原.md) contributes original or fundamental, and
[理](../kanji/理.md) contributes reason, logic, or truth.
The first character qualifies the second: a foundational principle.
The on readings combine as ゲン + リ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[主義](主義.md) (しゅぎ) can be a principle or doctrine, including one
a person endorses. [説](説.md) (せつ) can be a theory or view.
[基本](基本.md) (きほん) and [基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) concern basics or
foundations; neither must explain the mechanism behind something.

## Distinctive meaning

Understanding a 原理 does not automatically mean knowing all practical
steps. Conversely, being able to follow instructions does not establish
that one understands the underlying principle.
Do not turn the English contrast between "principle" and "theory" into
a claim that 原理 can never be translated as "theory."
