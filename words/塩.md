# 塩 (しお) - salt

## Meaning

塩 is ordinary salt, especially the ingredient used in cooking.
It is a noun; を marks it as the thing added or sprinkled.

- スープに塩を少し入れます。 (スープにしおをすこしいれます。) - I add a little salt to the soup.

に marks what receives the salt. The quantity 少し (すこし), "a little,"
can follow the object phrase without changing the noun.

## How the kanji combine

塩 writes the whole native word しお, with no okurigana.
There is no compound to divide. The on-reading エン appears in words such as
食塩 (しょくえん), "table salt," rather than replacing しお in ordinary speech.

## Synonyms and antonyms

砂糖 (さとう), "sugar," is a contrasting seasoning, not an antonym.
しょっぱい is an adjective meaning "salty"; it describes a taste rather
than naming the ingredient.

## Distinctive meaning

塩 does not itself mean "salty" before any noun. Learn compounds and phrases
individually, such as 塩味 (しおあじ), "salty flavor; salt seasoning."
The everyday reading here is distinct from specialized chemical terminology.
