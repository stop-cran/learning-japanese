# 宅 (たく) - home; residence

## Meaning

宅 is a noun or suffix for a home or residence. After a person's name it identifies that person's home: 山田さん宅 (やまださんたく), "Mr. or Ms. Yamada's home." The standalone noun can also refer to one's own home.

- 昨日、田中さん宅を訪問しました。 (きのう、たなかさんたくをほうもんしました。) - Yesterday I visited Tanaka's home.
- 荷物は山田さん宅に届きました。 (にもつはやまださんたくにとどきました。) - The package arrived at Yamada's home.

The first sentence marks the visited residence with を; the second marks the destination with に.

## How the kanji combine

[宅](../kanji/宅.md) is one kanji read with the on reading たく. It supplies the residence meaning in [自宅](自宅.md) (じたく), "one's own home." There is no second component in the standalone word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[家](家.md) (いえ・うち) also means house or home. A phrase such as 田中さんの家 expresses the same location as 田中さん宅 with a different construction. 自宅 explicitly identifies one's own residence.

## Distinctive meaning

宅 after a name identifies a residence, not every place where that person happens to be.

A wife speaking to someone else can also use 宅 for her own husband:
宅がそのように申しました (たくがそのようにもうしました),
"My husband said so." This is a particular spouse-reference use, not a general
replacement for "husband." It does not turn the named-home examples above into references to a spouse.
