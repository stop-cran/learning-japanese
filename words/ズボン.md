# ズボン (ズボン) — trousers; pants

## Meaning

ズボン means **trousers**, or “pants” in American English. The Japanese noun is not grammatically plural.

黒いズボンをはいています。 (くろいずぼんをはいています。) — I am wearing black trousers.

Use はく for putting on lower-body garments. はいている describes wearing them. When counting trousers, 一本 (いっぽん) can mean one pair.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is katakana. JMdict associates the word with French *jupon*. The modern Japanese meaning must be learned independently of the present-day French garment name; do not derive its meaning merely from resemblance to a foreign word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

パンツ can mean trousers in fashion contexts, but also underpants depending on context and pronunciation. ジーンズ specifically means jeans. スカート is another kind of lower-body garment, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

ズボン is a useful unambiguous choice when English “pants” might be misunderstood. It does not itself specify denim, formal clothing, the wearer's gender, or a particular cut.
