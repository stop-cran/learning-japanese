# 靴 (くつ) — shoes; footwear

## Meaning

靴 (くつ) means shoes or boots. The noun itself does not change form for one shoe versus several;
context supplies the number.
Learn it with はく, "put on; wear" for this item, and 脱ぐ (ぬぐ), "take off."

- 新しい靴を買いました。 (あたらしいくつをかいました。) — I bought new shoes.
- ここで靴を脱いでください。 (ここでくつをぬいでください。) — Please take off your shoes here.

The second sentence is a request about that location, not a rule about every place you enter.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji [靴](../kanji/靴.md) writes the native word くつ.
In 運動靴 (うんどうぐつ), "sports shoes," the initial sound becomes voiced: くつ becomes ぐつ.
The word 靴 by itself is still くつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

運動靴 narrows the kind of footwear to sports shoes or sneakers.
靴下 (くつした) means socks or stockings. Despite sharing 靴 in the spelling,
it names a different garment rather than a smaller kind of shoe.
There is no useful ordinary antonym to memorize for this object noun.

## Distinctive meaning

靴 names the footwear, not a person's feet.
Do not infer a material from the character's 革 component: the word applies to shoes without specifying what they are made of.
Use a modifier when the shoe's purpose or material matters.
