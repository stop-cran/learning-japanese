# 撒く (まく) - scatter; sprinkle; give someone the slip

## Meaning

撒く means scatter, sprinkle, or strew something over an area. It also means distribute things such as flyers, or shake off a pursuer or unwanted companion.

- 庭に水をまきました。 (にわにみずをまきました。) - I sprinkled water in the garden.
- 駅前でチラシをまいています。 (えきまえでチラシをまいています。) - They are handing out flyers in front of the station.
- 追っ手をまいた。 (おってをまいた。) - I gave my pursuers the slip.

It is transitive: what is scattered, distributed, or shaken off takes を. The dictionary marks these senses as usually written in kana.

## How the kanji combine

撒 supplies ま in this native verb; く completes the dictionary form. It is godan: 撒きます (まきます), 撒いて (まいて), 撒いた (まいた).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[散らす](散らす.md) (ちらす), "scatter; disperse," and [配る](配る.md) (くばる), "distribute; hand out," overlap parts of the meaning.

[蒔く](蒔く.md) (まく) is the sowing-seed spelling. 種をまく (たねをまく), "sow seeds," overlaps scattering seed. [巻く](巻く.md), "wind; coil; roll up," is another まく, not the verb for sprinkling water.

## Distinctive meaning

The object determines the reading of the situation: 水をまく distributes water, チラシをまく distributes flyers, and 追っ手をまく loses someone who is following. The last is not a literal scattering of a person into pieces.

Scattering is not necessarily untidy or accidental. Do not reduce the verb to making a mess, or assume that every delivery to a named recipient is described by 撒く.
