# そのほか - the rest; besides that; etc.

## Meaning

そのほか means the rest or the others, something additional, or "etc."
when extending a list. Useful patterns are **そのほかの + noun** for other
items and **そのほかに** for an addition.

- 三人は残ります。そのほかの人は帰ってもいいです。 (さんにんはのこります。そのほかのひとはかえってもいいです。) - Three people will stay. The others may go home.
- 本を二冊買いました。そのほかに、ノートも買いました。 (ほんをにさつかいました。そのほかに、ノートもかいました。) - I bought two books. In addition, I bought a notebook.
- 机の上には、本、ノート、そのほかいろいろな物があります。 (つくえのうえには、ほん、ノート、そのほかいろいろなものがあります。) - On the desk are books, notebooks and various other things.

The last example extends a list; it does not specify every remaining item.

## How the kanji combine

**その他 (そのほか)** and **その外 (そのほか)** are dictionary spellings.
その identifies what has already been mentioned; [他](../kanji/他.md)
or [外](../kanji/外.md) contributes the other or remaining items.
The dictionary additionally indexes 其の他, 其の外 and 其他 as search-only
spellings; they are lookup forms, not recommended everyday spellings.
Written その他 is also read **そのた**, a reading the dictionary marks
as more common when the expression is written in kanji.
That is not a rule that その他 can never be read そのほか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ほか](ほか.md) and [他](他.md) overlap in other/else.
[以外](以外.md) (いがい) identifies what is outside an indicated group.
[など](など.md) is useful for "and so on" after examples.
For additive linking, compare [そのうえ](そのうえ.md) and [それに](それに.md).
These different constructions are not freely interchangeable in every position.

## Distinctive meaning

English "otherwise" can mean "in other respects" or "if not; or else."
The latter conditional warning is not the meaning taught by そのほか.
Use context to identify what is being set aside and what counts as the rest;
the expression does not by itself name a fixed group of people or objects.
