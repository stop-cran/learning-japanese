# 利口 (りこう) - clever; sensible

## Meaning

利口 means clever, intelligent, or sensible. It is a na-adjective:
利口な before a noun and 利口です as a predicate. With お, it also describes
being well-behaved, especially for children or pets.

- 彼女は利口なので、すぐにやり方を覚えました。 (かのじょはりこうなので、すぐにやりかたをおぼえました。) - She is clever, so she quickly learned how to do it.
- 犬がお利口に待っています。 (いぬがおりこうにまっています。) - The dog is waiting patiently and behaving well.

In お利口に待つ, に describes the manner of waiting. The praise concerns
behavior in that situation, not a test of intelligence.

## How the kanji combine

[利](../kanji/利.md) has associations with advantage or effective ability;
[口](../kanji/口.md) means mouth. The established compound means clever or
sensible, not literally an advantageous mouth. These character associations
do not by themselves establish its historical development.
The readings are リ + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[賢い](賢い.md) (かしこい) also means clever or wise.
[鋭い](鋭い.md) (するどい) can emphasize keen perception.
Neither requires the particular child-or-pet praise conveyed by お利口 in the
second example.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling an adult お利口 can sound patronizing in some relationships, so do not
treat it as a universally respectful compliment. 利口 can also praise sensible
judgment rather than school performance. The context determines whether the
focus is intelligence, shrewdness, or good behavior.
