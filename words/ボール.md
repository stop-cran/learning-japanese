# ボール (ボール) - ball; bowl

## Meaning

ボール names two distinct loanwords, not two senses of one
dictionary entry. **Ball** is the round object used in games.
**Bowl** is a container, such as a mixing bowl; that word
also has the spelling ボウル.

- 子供がボールを投げました。 (こどもがボールをなげました。) - The child threw the ball.
- ボールに卵を入れて、よく混ぜてください。 (ボールにたまごをいれて、よくまぜてください。) - Put the eggs in a bowl and mix well.

The first example belongs to the ball entry. The second uses
the separately listed bowl word with the recognized reading ボール.
In baseball, ボール can also name a pitch called a ball,
contrasting with a strike.

## How the kanji combine

Both words are written in katakana and correspond to English
"ball" and "bowl." The long-vowel mark in ボール belongs
to both Japanese forms. ボウル helps distinguish the container
in writing, but ボール for a bowl is not an erroneous reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[球](球.md) (たま) overlaps with the round-object meaning.
[皿](皿.md) (さら) names a plate or dish, not the same
container shape as a mixing bowl. These comparisons belong to
different identities and are not interchangeable synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the action and setting to disambiguate: throwing suggests a
ball, while mixing ingredients suggests a bowl. A bare English
gloss cannot distinguish the two. Their shared spelling does not
merge their dictionary provenance.
