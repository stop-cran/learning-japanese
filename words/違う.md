# 違う (ちがう) — be different

## Meaning

違う means being different. Use AはBと違う to compare A with B; と marks the comparison point.
Although English uses the adjective "different," Japanese 違う is a verb.

- 私の答えはあなたの答えと違います。 (わたしのこたえはあなたのこたえとちがいます。) — My answer is different from yours.
- この二つは色が違います。 (このふたつはいろがちがいます。) — These two are different colors.

## How the kanji combine

[違](../kanji/違.md) carries the difference meaning, and う completes the native verb ちがう.
The polite form is 違います (ちがいます), not 違いです. Before a noun, 違う色 (ちがういろ) means "a different color."

## Synonyms and antonyms

間違う (まちがう) concerns being mistaken or making a mistake.
違う can also mean "be incorrect," but a neutral comparison of two colors need not identify an error.
The noun 違い (ちがい) means "difference."

## Distinctive meaning

A reply such as 違います (ちがいます) may correct a statement: "That is not right."
This corrective use and the neutral "differ" use belong to the same verb; the surrounding conversation supplies the intended judgment.
