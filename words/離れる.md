# 離れる (はなれる) - be apart; move away

## Meaning

離れる is an intransitive ichidan verb for being separated or moving away. Use から to identify what someone or something is distant from.

- 家は駅から少し離れています。 (いえはえきからすこしはなれています。) - The house is a little way from the station.
- 危ないので、窓から離れてください。 (あぶないので、まどからはなれてください。) - It is dangerous, so please move away from the window.

離れている can describe a resulting or continuing state of separation. Forms include 離れます, 離れた, and 離れない.

## How the kanji combine

離 carries separation or distance. In this native word it is read はな, with れる as the verb ending. Learn the whole ichidan verb rather than assuming that every word ending in る follows the same conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[遠い](遠い.md) (とおい) describes being far; 離れる can describe the movement that creates distance. 近づく (ちかづく), "approach," contrasts with moving away. [離す](離す.md) (はなす) is transitive: it means separating or moving something away.

## Distinctive meaning

離れる can also use を to mark the place left: 席を離れる (せきをはなれる), "leave one's seat." This departure を does not make the verb transitive; the seat is not an object being moved. Nor does 離れる inherently mean a long distance: the amount can be small, large, or unstated.
