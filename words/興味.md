# 興味 (きょうみ) — interest; curiosity

## Meaning

興味 is interest or curiosity about something. The topic of interest takes に in the pattern Xに興味がある.

- 日本の歴史に興味があります。 (にほんのれきしにきょうみがあります。) — I'm interested in Japanese history.
- その話にはあまり興味がありません。 (そのはなしにはあまりきょうみがありません。) — I'm not very interested in that story.

興味を持つ (きょうみをもつ) means "take an interest."

## How the kanji combine

興 suggests arousing interest, while [味](../kanji/味.md), "taste," supplies a figurative association with what attracts or engages someone. The on readings are キョウ + ミ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

関心 (かんしん) overlaps in meaning with interest or concern. 趣味 (しゅみ), "hobby," names an activity or taste rather than simply curiosity about a subject.

## Distinctive meaning

興味 is a noun, not the adjective "interesting." Use 興味がある for a person's interest, and 面白い (おもしろい) or 興味深い (きょうみぶかい) to describe something as interesting. Do not form an ordinary verb by adding する to 興味.
