# 床 (ゆか) - floor surface

## Meaning

床, read ゆか, is the floor surface of a room or building. Use 床に for where
something is placed and 床を as the object of an action such as cleaning.

- かばんを床に置きました。 (かばんをゆかにおきました。) - I put my bag on the floor.
- 床をきれいに拭いてください。 (ゆかをきれいにふいてください。) - Please wipe the floor clean.

床がぬれている (ゆかがぬれている) means "the floor is wet."
The noun itself does not specify whether the surface is wood, tile, or
another material.

## How the kanji combine

床 writes the whole native noun ゆか. There is no multi-kanji compound to
divide. The same character has other readings in other words; the reading
here should be learned with the floor meaning rather than guessed from
its written shape.

## Synonyms and antonyms

地面 (じめん) is the ground, typically discussed outdoors.
[畳](畳.md) (たたみ) are tatami mats, which can cover a room's floor.
天井 (てんじょう), "ceiling," is the upper surface of a room, a spatial
contrast rather than a different kind of floor.

## Distinctive meaning

English "floor" also means a level of a building, but that is not this
use of ゆか. 二階 (にかい) is the second floor or storey; 二階の床
(にかいのゆか) is the floor surface on that level. Keeping the distinction
prevents confusing a room's surface with its location in a building.
