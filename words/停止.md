# 停止 (ていし) - stoppage; suspension

## Meaning

停止 is a stoppage, halt, or suspension of movement or activity. 停止する has both intransitive and transitive uses: Xが停止する means X stops; Xを停止する means to stop or suspend X.

- 機械が突然停止しました。
  (きかいがとつぜんていししました。) - The machine suddenly stopped.
- 安全のため、機械の運転を停止しました。
  (あんぜんのため、きかいのうんてんをていししました。) - We stopped operating the machine for safety.

It also applies to a suspension or temporary prohibition: 免許の停止 (めんきょのていし), "suspension of a license," or 支払いの停止 (しはらいのていし), "suspension of payments."

## How the kanji combine

[停](../kanji/停.md) and [止](../kanji/止.md) both contribute stopping. Their related meanings reinforce a halt or cessation. The on readings are テイ + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[止まる](止まる.md) (とまる) and [止める](止める.md) (とめる) are everyday stopping verbs. [中止](中止.md) (ちゅうし) overlaps in discontinuing an activity and also covers calling off a planned event. 再開 (さいかい), "resumption," contrasts with a suspended operation.

## Distinctive meaning

停止 does not guarantee either permanent termination or a particular restart time. The object and notice must supply those details. This article teaches ていし; the dictionary's archaic mourning-related use is restricted to the different reading ちょうじ.
