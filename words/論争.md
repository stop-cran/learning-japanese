# 論争 (ろんそう) - controversy; dispute

## Meaning

論争 is a dispute, controversy, or debate between opposing views.
It is a noun and an intransitive する verb.
Use Nについて論争する or Nをめぐって論争する for the disputed topic;
the other participant can take と.

- 新しい制度をめぐって論争が起きています。
  (あたらしいせいどをめぐってろんそうがおきています。)
  - A controversy has arisen over the new system.
- 二人の研究者がその理論について論争しました。
  (ふたりのけんきゅうしゃがそのりろんについてろんそうしました。)
  - Two researchers debated that theory.

論争が続く (ろんそうがつづく) means that a dispute continues.
The noun does not by itself tell you which side is correct.

## How the kanji combine

[論](../kanji/論.md) contributes argument or reasoned discussion, and
[争](../kanji/争.md) contributes contention.
The combination presents disagreement expressed through arguments.
The on readings are ロン + ソウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[議論](議論.md) (ぎろん) is discussion or argument and need not foreground conflict
as strongly. 口論 (こうろん) is a verbal quarrel.
Agreement contrasts with the participants' disagreement, but does not name the same kind of event.

## Distinctive meaning

論争 can extend through published statements over time; the participants need not
be shouting at each other in one room.
It is therefore useful for a public or scholarly controversy as well as a direct debate.
An exchange of information without opposing positions is not necessarily a 論争.
