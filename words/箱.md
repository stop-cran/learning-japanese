# 箱 (はこ) — box; case

## Meaning

箱 names a box, case, chest, or similar container.
It does not specify the contents or material: 木の箱 (きのはこ) is a wooden box,
while 空の箱 (からのはこ) is an empty box.

- 本を箱に入れました。 (ほんをはこにいれました。) — I put the books into a box.
- この箱は空です。 (このはこはからです。) — This box is empty.

In the first example, を marks what is put away and に marks the container it goes into.

## How the kanji combine

[箱](../kanji/箱.md) writes the single native noun はこ.
There are no separate kanji words to combine inside it.
The bamboo-shaped top is part of the character, not a claim that every 箱 must be made of bamboo.

## Synonyms and antonyms

袋 (ふくろ), "bag; sack; pouch," is another container word.
A paper bag and a paper box differ in form even though both can hold the same purchase.
The material alone does not decide whether to call something 箱 or 袋.
There is no necessary antonym for "box"; empty and full instead describe its state.

## Distinctive meaning

Compounds may have a specific established meaning. 本箱 (ほんばこ) means "bookcase";
it is not simply the name for any box you happen to put a book in.
Notice the voiced ばこ in that word while keeping はこ for the standalone noun.
