# わがまま (わがまま) - selfish; selfishness

## Meaning

わがまま is a na-adjective and noun for selfishness: insisting
on one's own wishes without enough consideration for others.
Use わがままな before a noun and わがままです as a predicate.

- 自分の都合だけで予定を変えるのは、わがままです。 (じぶんのつごうだけでよていをかえるのは、わがままです。) - Changing the plan just for your own convenience is selfish.
- わがままを言って、すみません。 (わがままをいって、すみません。) - I am sorry for making a selfish request.

わがままを言う frames a demand or request as selfish. The
second sentence may soften a request by acknowledging that it
inconveniences the listener.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana, as on this card. Kanji spellings
include 我が儘 and 我儘. The form connects one's own self,
わが, with まま, the way something is or the way one wishes.
This is a useful formation explanation, not a loanword origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

自分勝手 (じぶんかって) also describes self-centered behavior.
[自由](自由.md) (じゆう), "freedom," does not by itself
criticize a person as selfish. [親切](親切.md) (しんせつ),
"kind," provides a possible behavioral contrast, not an exact antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The modern core is selfishness, not every act of disobedience
or every passing whim. Someone can disagree with an instruction
for a considerate reason. Conversely, a polite-sounding request can
still be わがまま if it disregards other people's needs.
