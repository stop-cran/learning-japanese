# 賞 (しょう) - prize; award

## Meaning

賞 is a noun for a prize or award. It can stand alone or follow an award's name, as in ノーベル賞 (ノーベルしょう), "Nobel Prize." **賞をもらう／受ける** means "receive an award."

- 彼女の作品が賞を取りました。 (かのじょのさくひんがしょうをとりました。) - Her work won a prize.
- 受賞者に賞が贈られました。 (じゅしょうしゃにしょうがおくられました。) - The award was presented to the recipient.

The thing recognized can be a work, performance, or achievement. The award need not consist of money.

## How the kanji combine

賞 is one character read しょう, an on reading. It supplies the whole noun, and also the final element in many award names. Learn the long しょう rather than shortening it to しょ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

賞金 (しょうきん) is prize money, while 賞品 (しょうひん) is a prize item. Both are more specific than 賞. [ほめる](ほめる.md), "praise," describes positive evaluation, which need not involve formally giving an award.

## Distinctive meaning

Winning a 賞 and receiving a gift are not the same event: the award recognizes something. A certificate, trophy, money, or public distinction may accompany it. There is no single useful opposite covering every prize; not winning does not automatically mean being punished.
