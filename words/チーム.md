# チーム (チーム) - team

## Meaning

チーム is a noun for a team: people working or competing together as a unit. It can refer to sports, a project, or another coordinated activity. Use チームに入る, "join a team," and チームを作る, "form a team."

- 私は学校のサッカーチームに入っています。 (わたしはがっこうのサッカーチームにはいっています。) - I belong to the school's soccer team.
- この仕事はチームで進めます。 (このしごとはチームですすめます。) - We will work on this task as a team.

The particle で in the second example describes doing the work collectively.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword with no kanji components. Keep the long vowel marked by ー: チーム. The final ム is part of the Japanese word; do not drop it when speaking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[団体](団体.md) (だんたい) is broader "group/organization." [組](組.md) (くみ) can name a group or class, depending on context. チーム particularly suits a group cooperating or competing toward a shared result.

## Distinctive meaning

A チーム need not be a sports team or an entire company. A company can have several project teams, and teammates can have different roles. 相手のチーム (あいてのチーム) is the opposing team in a contest; it remains another チーム rather than being an antonym of the noun.
