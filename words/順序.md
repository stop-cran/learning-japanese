# 順序 (じゅんじょ) - order; sequence; procedure

## Meaning

順序 is the order in which things are arranged or happen. It can also mean a procedure: the steps followed to do something.

- 名前を五十音順に並べるときは、順序を間違えないでください。 (なまえをごじゅうおんじゅんにならべるときは、じゅんじょをまちがえないでください。) - When arranging the names in Japanese syllabary order, please do not get the order wrong.
- 作業の順序を説明します。 (さぎょうのじゅんじょをせつめいします。) - I will explain the procedure for the task.

順序よく (じゅんじょよく) means in an orderly way. The noun does not take する to mean "put in order"; use a full expression such as 順序を決める (じゅんじょをきめる), "decide the order."

## How the kanji combine

[順](../kanji/順.md) and 序 both have order or sequence associations. Together they supply a useful reminder of an arrangement or progression through steps.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[順](順.md) and [順番](順番.md) overlap in order or turn. [次第](次第.md) also has an order or program sense, besides its other uses. [方法](方法.md), "method," overlaps with the procedure sense but need not emphasize the sequence of steps. [順々](順々.md) describes proceeding in sequence.

## Distinctive meaning

Here English "order" means sequence, not a command or a purchase. 順序 often concerns how several things or steps fit together; 順番 can foreground whose turn is next. This is a useful contrast, not a rule that their sequence meanings never overlap.
