# 囚人 (しゅうじん) — prisoner; captive

## Meaning

囚人 is a person held in prison or captivity.

- 囚人が逃げた。 (しゅうじんがにげた。) — The prisoner escaped.

## How the kanji combine

[囚](../kanji/囚.md) and [人](../kanji/人.md) form the whole word for a person held captive. The word does not specify whether the person is convicted, awaiting trial, or held in another way.

## Synonyms and antonyms

囚人 can overlap with labels for people held in a criminal facility, but it is not a precise current legal-status label. A free person is a contextual contrast, not a fixed lexical antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The reading taught here is しゅうじん. JMdict also lists the historical readings めしゅうど and めしうど. Digital Daijisen traces めしゅうど to めしびと and notes めしゅうと as an earlier form; these forms document reading history, not current readings to learn.

Digital Daijisen describes the legal use of 囚人 as an old term and notes that it is no longer used in laws following the 1995 Penal Code revision. Use it as a general or historical word, not as the current statutory name for a particular status.
