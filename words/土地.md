# 土地 (とち) — land, plot, locality

## Meaning

土地 is land as property or terrain: 土地を買う (buy land), 土地の人 (a local person), 土地柄 とちがら (the character of a place), 土地勘 とちかん (familiarity with an area), 広い土地 (a large plot).

## How the kanji combine

[土](../kanji/土.md) (earth) plus 地 (ground): both words mean the ground, so it is a pair of synonyms. On readings ト + チ (土 is ト here, not ド). This is the ト reading of 土, found in few words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 地面 じめん (the ground surface), 敷地 しきち (site), 地所 じしょ (a plot, old).
- Related: 土 つち (soil, as material).

## Distinctive meaning

土地 is land as a place or property; 土 つち is the material (soil), and 地面 is the surface you stand on.
