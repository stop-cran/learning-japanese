# 土地 (とち) — land, plot, locality

## Meaning

土地 is land as property or terrain, or the locality in which people live.

- 土地を買う (とちをかう) — buy land
- 土地の人 (とちのひと) — a local person
- [広い](広い.md)土地 (ひろいとち) — a large plot of land

Additional compounds include 土地柄 (とちがら, the character of a place) and 土地勘 (とちかん, familiarity with an area).

## How the kanji combine

[土](../kanji/土.md) (earth) plus [地](../kanji/地.md) (ground): both kanji relate to the ground. The on readings are ト + チ (土 is ト here, not ド).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 地面 じめん (the ground surface), 敷地 しきち (site), 地所 じしょ (a plot of land).
- Related: 土 つち (soil, as material).

## Distinctive meaning

土地 is land as a place or property; 土 つち is the material (soil), and 地面 is the surface you stand on.
