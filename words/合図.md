# 合図 (あいず) - signal; cue

## Meaning

合図 is a signal that tells someone what to do or notice. It can be a gesture,
sound, or other recognizable cue. Use 合図をする or 合図する, "give a signal,"
and 人に合図する, "signal to someone."

- 準備ができたら、手を上げて合図してください。 (じゅんびができたら、てをあげてあいずしてください。) - When you are ready, raise your hand to signal.
- 先生の合図で、みんな走り始めました。 (せんせいのあいずで、みんなはしりはじめました。) - Everyone started running at the teacher's signal.

The で in 合図で identifies the cue that triggers the action.

## How the kanji combine

[合](../kanji/合.md) suggests matching or bringing together, and [図](../kanji/図.md)
can suggest a sign or plan. Think of a sign that coordinates people, not a
literal "matching diagram." The reading combines native あい with on-reading
ず; it is a mixed-reading word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[信号](信号.md) (しんごう) also means a signal, including traffic lights and
transmitted signals. 合図 is useful when someone recognizes a cue to respond.
The two overlap; a light can serve as a 合図. There is no general-purpose opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

A meaningful cue need not use words: a nod can be a 合図. Conversely, an accidental
sound is not necessarily a signal. In 合図を待つ (あいずをまつ), "wait for the
signal," the listener expects a recognizable indication before acting.
