# どの — which

## Meaning

どの asks "which" before a noun, choosing from a set understood in context. It must be followed by that noun.

どの電車に乗りますか。 (どのでんしゃにのりますか。) — Which train will you take?

どのかばんがあなたのですか。 (どのかばんがあなたのですか。) — Which bag is yours?

## How the kanji combine

どの is normally written in kana. The rare dictionary spelling 何の is not the ordinary way to write it and can suggest other readings in normal text.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どれ asks "which one" without a following noun. どんな asks "what kind of," seeking a description rather than simply selecting an item. この・その・あの are the corresponding non-question noun modifiers.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not insert の between どの and its noun: どの本 (どのほん), "which book." When the questioned phrase is the subject, が is the normal choice, as in the bag example. どの is not the name suffix 殿 (どの).
