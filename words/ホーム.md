# ホーム (ホーム) - railway platform; home

## Meaning

At a railway station, ホーム means a platform: the place beside the track where passengers board or leave trains.

- 電車は三番ホームから出ます。 (でんしゃはさんばんホームからでます。) - The train leaves from platform three.
- ホームで友達を待っています。 (ホームでともだちをまっています。) - I am waiting for a friend on the platform.

**A separate home identity** occurs in home or residential contexts, including 老人ホーム (ろうじんホーム), "a residential home for older people." 祖母は老人ホームで暮らしています (そぼはろうじんホームでくらしています) means "My grandmother lives in a residential home for older people."

## How the kanji combine

Both identities are written ホーム, with a long ホー. The railway word is a shortening of プラットホーム, "platform." The other word corresponds to English "home." Identical katakana does not make the railway sense a development of the home entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[駅](駅.md) (えき) is the station as a whole; a station can have multiple platforms. For one's own home, [家](家.md) (いえ) is useful, while [家庭](家庭.md) (かてい) concerns a household or family life.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate 帰宅する, "go home," by mechanically substituting ホーム into every home expression. Conversely, a station announcement about ホーム is not discussing someone's residence. The location and surrounding compound identify the relevant lexical identity.
