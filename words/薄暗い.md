# 薄暗い (うすぐらい) - dim; poorly lit

## Meaning

薄暗い describes dim or gloomy lighting. It is an i-adjective:
薄暗い部屋 (うすぐらいへや), "a dim room."

- 廊下は薄暗かったです。 (ろうかはうすぐらかったです。) - The corridor was dim.
- 薄暗い部屋で本を読んでいました。 (うすぐらいへやでほんをよんでいました。) - I was reading in a poorly lit room.

The negative is 薄暗くない (うすぐらくない); 薄暗くなる (うすぐらくなる) means "get dim."
Keep the voiced ぐ in うすぐらい.

## How the kanji combine

[薄](../kanji/薄.md), read うす, adds a slight or faint degree to
[暗](../kanji/暗.md)い, "dark." Here くらい becomes ぐらい inside the native compound.
This reading change is a fact about this word, not permission to voice every compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暗い](暗い.md) (くらい) overlaps directly in describing poor lighting.
[明るい](明るい.md) (あかるい), "bright; well-lit," provides the practical contrast.
[黒い](黒い.md) (くろい) names black coloration, not insufficient light.

## Distinctive meaning

The initial 薄 does not describe thin walls or watered-down light.
薄暗い suggests limited light rather than a measurement or a fixed cutoff for darkness.
English "gloomy" here can describe the place's lighting; do not extend the word automatically
to every unhappy person or gloomy prediction.
