# 通訳 (つうやく) - oral translation; interpreter

## Meaning

通訳 is interpreting spoken communication between people using different languages.
It also names the interpreter, the person doing that work.
通訳する is transitive: the speech or language interpreted can take を.

- 会議で英語を日本語に通訳しました。
  (かいぎでえいごをにほんごにつうやくしました。) - I interpreted from English into Japanese at the meeting.
- 通訳が話の内容を伝えました。
  (つうやくがはなしのないようをつたえました。) - The interpreter conveyed what was said.

In the first example, に identifies the language interpreted into.
同時通訳 (どうじつうやく) means simultaneous interpreting.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes conveying or connecting, and
[訳](../kanji/訳.md) contributes translation.
Together they suggest conveying meaning across a language barrier.
The readings are ツウ + ヤク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[翻訳](翻訳.md) (ほんやく) is translation, ordinarily of written material.
[訳](訳.md), read やく in its translation identity, can name a translated version;
that is distinct from its primary わけ, "reason."
The written-versus-spoken contrast is useful without making English "translation"
an impossible description of 通訳.

## Distinctive meaning

English "interpretation" can also mean someone's understanding of a statement:
[解釈](解釈.md) (かいしゃく) and [読み](読み.md) (よみ) cover that kind of sense.
通訳 instead foregrounds communication between languages.
In 通訳を頼む (つうやくをたのむ), context can identify asking for interpreting
help or for an interpreter; the noun is not restricted to a job title.
