# 線路 (せんろ) - railway track; railway line

## Meaning

線路 commonly names a railway track or line: the physical route along
which trains run. It is a noun, not the train vehicle itself.

- 窓から線路が見えます。 (まどからせんろがみえます。) - You can see the railway tracks from the window.
- 線路の近くに住んでいます。 (せんろのちかくにすんでいます。) - I live near the railway line.

線路沿い (せんろぞい) means along the railway line.
The examples concern where the tracks are, not a train's departure time.

## How the kanji combine

[線](../kanji/線.md) contributes a line or track, and
[路](../kanji/路.md) a route or path.
Their related ideas combine into a tracked route.
The on readings are セン + ロ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鉄道](鉄道.md) (てつどう) can describe a railway or the railway
transport system more broadly.
[線](線.md) (せん) is a more general line, including drawn lines and wires.
[道路](道路.md) (どうろ) names a road; the English word "line" does not
make every road or queue a 線路.

## Distinctive meaning

The railway meaning is the practical focus here.
Japanese dictionaries also record electrical or communications line
infrastructure, so "never anything except railway tracks" would be too strong.
