# 商店 (しょうてん) — shop; store

## Meaning

商店 is a shop or store: a business where goods are sold.
It is a noun naming the business, not the act of buying something.

- 駅の前に小さな商店があります。 (えきのまえにちいさなしょうてんがあります。) — There is a small shop in front of the station.
- この商店でパンを買いました。 (このしょうてんでパンをかいました。) — I bought bread at this shop.

In the first example, に marks where the shop is; in the second, で marks where the purchase took place.

## How the kanji combine

[商](../kanji/商.md) concerns commerce and selling; [店](../kanji/店.md) is a shop.
The first character specifies the commercial activity of the second: a shop conducting trade.
The on readings are ショウ + テン, with a long vowel in しょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[店](店.md) (みせ) is a broader word for a shop or establishment and can also refer to a restaurant.
[会社](会社.md) (かいしゃ) means a company: it identifies an organization, not necessarily a storefront.
商品 (しょうひん) is merchandise, the goods a shop sells.
There is no useful direct antonym of 商店.

## Distinctive meaning

商店 does not, by itself, say how large the shop is, who owns it, or what it sells.
The first example adds 小さな (ちいさな) to specify a small shop; that size is not built into the noun.
Do not confuse the place, 商店, with its stock, 商品.
