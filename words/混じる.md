# 混じる (まじる) - be blended; be mixed in; mingle

## Meaning

混じる means be mixed, blended, or combined with something. It also means mingle with or join people. It is intransitive: what is mixed takes が, and the surrounding material or group can take に.

- 砂に小石が混じっていました。 (すなにこいしがまじっていました。) - There were small stones mixed into the sand.
- 大人に交じって子供も参加した。 (おとなにまじってこどももさんかした。) - Children also took part alongside the adults.

交じる is another spelling of this same まじる entry, especially recognized for mingling or association. The pinned dictionary marks 雑じる rare; the shortened spellings 交る and 混る are search-only records, not spellings to imitate here.

## How the kanji combine

[混](../kanji/混.md) contributes mixing; じる completes the native verb. Despite ending in じる, まじる is godan: 混じります (まじります), 混じらない (まじらない), 混じった (まじった).

The alternative [交](../kanji/交.md) also contributes mingling. Dictionaries describe a tendency to use 交 when constituents remain distinguishable and 混 for blending, but this is not an absolute solids-versus-liquids rule.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[混ざる](混ざる.md) (まざる) closely overlaps both mixing and mingling. [混ぜる](混ぜる.md) (まぜる) is transitive and ichidan: someone mixes things, rather than the things being mixed.

[付き合う](付き合う.md) (つきあう) includes associating with people, but the mixed-in-group use here does not necessarily mean dating.

## Distinctive meaning

Read this article as まじる. A separate dictionary headword 混じる (こんじる) is not a reason to conjugate まじる as an ichidan verb.

Noise mixed into a sound, stones among sand, and people among other people are different contexts. Do not turn a useful spelling tendency into a universal ban on one kanji for a whole material category.
