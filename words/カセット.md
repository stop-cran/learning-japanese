# カセット - cassette; game cartridge

## Meaning

カセット can mean a cassette tape. It also has a game-cartridge meaning, so a game context need not involve recorded sound.

- 昔の歌が入ったカセットを見つけました。 (むかしのうたがはいったカセットをみつけました。) - I found a cassette containing old songs.
- ゲーム機にカセットを差し込みました。 (ゲームきにカセットをさしこみました。) - I inserted the game cartridge into the console.

The device and its context identify which physical medium is meant.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana and has no kanji components. Keep the small ッ in カセット: it marks the consonant timing, not an extra full-sized syllable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

カセットテープ explicitly names a cassette tape; [テープ](テープ.md) can also refer to adhesive tape and other strips. カートリッジ is cartridge more generally, and ロムカセット explicitly names a game cartridge.

## Distinctive meaning

A カセット is not automatically the player or recorder. [テープレコーダー](テープレコーダー.md) names a tape recorder. Do not transfer every English use of "cartridge" to カセット merely because the game-medium meanings overlap.
