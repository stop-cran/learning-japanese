# 明るい (あかるい) — bright, cheerful

## Meaning

明るい is an い-adjective for something bright or well-lit. It also describes a cheerful personality or an encouraging outlook.

- この部屋は明るいです。 (このへやはあかるいです。) — This room is bright.
- 妹は明るい人です。 (いもうとはあかるいひとです。) — My younger sister is a cheerful person.

## How the kanji combine

This is a native adjective with one kanji. [明](../kanji/明.md) is read あか here, followed by the okurigana るい. It is not a compound assembled from on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 暗い くらい is the direct contrast for a dark room and can also describe a gloomy mood or outlook.
- [楽しい](楽しい.md) たのしい, "enjoyable," describes enjoyment rather than the amount of light or necessarily someone's general disposition.

## Distinctive meaning

明るい人 is a cheerful person, not simply an intelligent person: English "bright" cannot be substituted mechanically. Likewise, 明るい部屋 describes lighting, not how enjoyable it is to spend time there.
