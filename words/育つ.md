# 育つ (そだつ) — grow up

## Meaning

育つ describes growing or being brought up. The growing person or plant is the subject:
子供が育つ (こどもがそだつ), "a child grows up."
It is an intransitive godan verb, with 育ちます (そだちます), 育って (そだって),
and 育った (そだった) among its forms.

- 私は小さな町で育ちました。
  (わたしはちいさなまちでそだちました。)
  — I grew up in a small town.
- 畑で野菜がよく育っています。
  (はたけでやさいがよくそだっています。)
  — The vegetables are growing well in the field.

## How the kanji combine

[育](../kanji/育.md) supplies the growing-up meaning; the kana つ completes the verb.
This is the native reading そだ.つ. It is not read with イク, the on reading found in
教育 (きょういく), "education."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[育てる](育てる.md) (そだてる) is the transitive partner: someone raises or nurtures someone or something.
Compare 子供が育つ, "a child grows up," with 子供を育てる (こどもをそだてる),
"raise a child."
成長する (せいちょうする), "grow; develop," overlaps, but can emphasize growth and development
rather than where someone was brought up.

## Distinctive meaning

Although English may translate 育つ as "be raised," it is not a Japanese passive conjugation
of 育てる. It is a separate intransitive verb.
The example places the speaker's upbringing in a town; で marks that setting, not a person
doing the raising.
