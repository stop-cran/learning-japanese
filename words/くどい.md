# くどい - long-winded; insistent; overly rich

## Meaning

くどい describes something excessive and tiresome: an explanation that repeats itself, an insistently repeated request, or a taste that is too strong or rich. It is an i-adjective, usually written in kana.

- 同じ説明を何度もするので、くどいと感じました。 (おなじせつめいをなんどもするので、くどいとかんじました。) - Because the same explanation was repeated many times, I found it long-winded.
- このソースは味がくどいです。 (このソースはあじがくどいです。) - This sauce has an overly heavy flavor.

Additional use: くどい配色 (くどいはいしょく), an overdone or gaudy color scheme. The adverbial form is くどく; the past form is くどかった.

## How the kanji combine

諄い is a rare kanji spelling. The kana headword does not need a character-by-character explanation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

しつこい also overlaps in persistence and heavy/rich taste; it is not confined to a person's behavior. [こってり](こってり.md) can describe rich or heavy food without necessarily expressing the same criticism.

[濃い](濃い.md) (こい) can mean strong or concentrated. くどい more directly evaluates the strength or richness as excessive.

## Distinctive meaning

A long explanation is not automatically くどい: the negative judgment concerns tiresome repetition or excess. Likewise, a strong flavor can be welcome. Use くどい when that quality feels overdone, not as a neutral synonym for tasty.
