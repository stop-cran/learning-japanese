# 輸出 (ゆしゅつ) — export

## Meaning

輸出 is exporting goods to another country. It is a noun and forms the transitive verb 輸出する. The goods take を, and the destination can take に or へ.

- この会社は車を海外に輸出しています。 (このかいしゃはくるまをかいがいにゆしゅつしています。) — This company exports cars overseas.
- 今年は食品の輸出が増えました。 (ことしはしょくひんのゆしゅつがふえました。) — Food exports increased this year.

## How the kanji combine

輸 suggests transport, and [出](../kanji/出.md) means going out. The established reading is ゆしゅつ: goods are sent out across a national border.

## Synonyms and antonyms

輸入 (ゆにゅう), "import," is the corresponding movement into a country. 貿易 (ぼうえき), "trade," covers international commercial exchange more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

Export versus import depends on the country whose perspective is being described. One shipment can be an export from one country and an import into another. Ordinary delivery within a country is not 輸出 just because a package leaves a building.
