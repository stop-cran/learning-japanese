# 留学 (りゅうがく) — study abroad

## Meaning

留学 means studying abroad. Use 留学する for the action and に to mark the destination country or institution.
It is an intransitive verb: the person goes abroad to study, rather than "studying an object" with 留学する.

- 来年、日本に留学します。 (らいねん、にほんにりゅうがくします。) — I will study in Japan next year.
- 姉は一年間、フランスに留学していました。 (あねはいちねんかん、フランスにりゅうがくしていました。) — My older sister spent a year studying in France.

The word itself does not specify a one-year stay; the second example adds that information.

## How the kanji combine

[留](../kanji/留.md) contributes staying somewhere, and [学](../kanji/学.md) contributes study.
The compound names a stay for study abroad. Its international sense belongs to the complete word;
neither kanji by itself simply means "foreign country."
The readings are リュウ + ガク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[旅行](旅行.md) りょこう means travel or a trip. A 留学 stay involves travel, but 旅行 alone does not say that study is its purpose.
Conversely, taking a journey does not make it 留学 merely because you learn something along the way.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 留学 to identify an overseas study experience, not just the act of attending any school.
留学する does not state whether someone receives a degree, studies a language, or joins an exchange program;
add those details when they matter.
The useful contrast is purpose and location, not a fixed minimum duration.
