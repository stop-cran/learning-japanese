# さわやか (さわやか) — refreshing; clear and fluent

## Meaning

さわやか means refreshing, bracing or pleasant. It can describe a breeze, a feeling or the pleasant impression someone gives. Its voice/speech use includes a clear voice or fluent, eloquent speech.

窓を開けると、さわやかな風が入ってきた。（まどをあけると、さわやかなかぜがはいってきた。） — When I opened the window, a refreshing breeze came in.

さわやかな声で挨拶をした。（さわやかなこえであいさつをした。） — He greeted us in a clear, pleasant voice.

## How the kanji combine

爽やか is the kanji spelling of the same word, さわやか. The article uses a kana-primary spelling, so its metadata has no primary kanji. Learn the native word as a whole, not the on-reading ソウ plus an independently meaningful やか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[さっぱり](さっぱり.md) and [すっきり](すっきり.md) overlap in feeling refreshed or clear. [涼しい](涼しい.md) すずしい emphasizes pleasant coolness; さわやか is not limited to temperature. [快適](快適.md) かいてき concerns comfort/pleasantness. In speech, compare [はきはき](はきはき.md), clear and brisk, and [ぺらぺら](ぺらぺら.md), fluent speech.

## Distinctive meaning

Use な before a noun and に adverbially: さわやかな朝（さわやかなあさ）, a refreshing morning; さわやかに話す（さわやかにはなす）, speak clearly and pleasantly. Refreshing does not simply mean newly made or physically cold, and clear, pleasant speech is not necessarily rapid chatter.
