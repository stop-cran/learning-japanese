# 給料 (きゅうりょう) — salary; wages

## Meaning

給料 is the money someone receives for their work: salary, wages, or pay.
It names the payment, not the job itself. Useful combinations include 給料をもらう
(きゅうりょうをもらう), "receive one's pay," and 給料が上がる (きゅうりょうがあがる),
"one's pay goes up."

- 今月の給料で新しい自転車を買いました。
  (こんげつのきゅうりょうであたらしいじてんしゃをかいました。)
  — I bought a new bicycle with this month's pay.

## How the kanji combine

[給](../kanji/給.md) contributes giving or paying, while [料](../kanji/料.md) contributes
an amount paid. Together they identify remuneration for work, not "materials being given."
The on readings are キュウ + リョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

月給 (げっきゅう), "monthly salary," specifies a monthly basis.
[収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう), "income," is broader: money from sources other than employment
can also be income. 給料 is therefore not a substitute for every use of 収入.

## Distinctive meaning

The important link is between work and the money paid for it. 給料 alone does not specify a
particular payment interval, and it is not a label for the worker's occupation.
Compare [職業](職業.md) (しょくぎょう), which names the occupation rather than its pay.
