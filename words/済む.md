# 済む (すむ) -- be finished; be settled

## Meaning

済む describes a task, matter, or arrangement reaching completion or being settled. It is intransitive: the thing that is finished takes が.

- 宿題はもう済みました。 (しゅくだいはもうすみました。) -- My homework is already done.
- 準備が済んだら、出発しましょう。 (じゅんびがすんだら、しゅっぱつしましょう。) -- Let's leave when the preparations are finished.

It is a godan verb: 済みます (すみます), 済まない (すまない), 済んで (すんで), and 済んだ (すんだ).

## How the kanji combine

[済](../kanji/済.md) contributes completion or settlement and supplies す. The written む completes the native verb. Its て-form follows the む-to-んで pattern.

## Synonyms and antonyms

終わる (おわる) broadly describes coming to an end. 済ませる (すませる) describes someone getting something done: 宿題を済ませる, finish one's homework.

## Distinctive meaning

済む often presents a matter as dealt with, not merely stopped halfway through. Keep it distinct from 住む (すむ), to live somewhere. They share a pronunciation, but 準備が済む concerns completing preparations, not residing in them.
