# ロケット (ロケット) - rocket; locket

## Meaning

ロケット names two distinct nouns: a rocket and a locket, the small piece of jewelry that can hold a picture or keepsake.

- ロケットが宇宙へ飛んでいきました。 (ロケットがうちゅうへとんでいきました。) - The rocket flew into space.
- 彼女はロケットに家族の写真を入れています。 (かのじょはロケットにかぞくのしゃしんをいれています。) - She keeps a family photograph in her locket.

The first example concerns a space vehicle. In the second, に marks the small container in which a photograph is kept. The surrounding action and objects make the identity clear.

## How the kanji combine

Both words use the same katakana spelling. English "rocket" and "locket" converge as ロケット, including the small ッ before ト. There are no kanji components from which to recover the distinction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飛行機](飛行機.md) (ひこうき), "airplane," is another kind of flying vehicle, not a synonym for rocket. ネックレス, "necklace," is related to the jewelry context: a locket can be worn on one, but not every necklace contains a locket.

## Distinctive meaning

The source combines "locket" and "rocket" under a supplied rocket ID. The inspected dictionary distinguishes rocket entry 1147220 from locket entry 2862534, sense 1 of each. They are not two senses of entry 1147220. Both source identities are taught here; this does not establish independent JLPT assignments for either sense. Neither object has a useful direct antonym.
