# 思いっきり (おもいっきり) - to one's heart's content; with all one's strength

## Meaning

思いっきり is an adverb for doing something as much as one wants or with all one's strength. The action determines whether enjoyment, freedom from restraint, or force is prominent.

- 休日は海で思いっきり遊びました。 (きゅうじつはうみでおもいっきりあそびました。) - On my day off, I had fun at the beach to my heart's content.
- ボールを思いっきり蹴りました。 (ぼーるをおもいっきりけりました。) - I kicked the ball as hard as I could.

It modifies the action directly; no に is required in these examples.

## How the kanji combine

[思](../kanji/思.md) is the written component of this headword. Also recognize 思いっ切り (おもいっきり), a same-entry spelling using [切](../kanji/切.md) that JMdict marks as rare. The related form 思い切り is read おもいきり, without the small っ; do not silently remove it from おもいっきり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一生懸命](一生懸命.md) (いっしょうけんめい) emphasizes utmost effort. [たっぷり](たっぷり.md) concerns an ample amount, and [十分](十分.md) (じゅうぶん) concerns sufficiency. Playing 思いっきり can involve a lot of play, but the phrase highlights doing it fully or without restraint.

## Distinctive meaning

The beach example is not about kicking or physical strength; the kick example is not about satisfaction with a quantity. Learn the action with the adverb rather than translating every instance as "very." The dictionary also records a noun meaning resolution or decisiveness; the practical focus here is the adverb.
