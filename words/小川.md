# 小川 (おがわ) — brook, small stream

## Meaning

小川 is a narrow, shallow stream: 小川のせせらぎ (the murmur of a brook), 小川で遊ぶ (play in a stream), 小川が流れる (a brook flows).
It is also a very common surname.

## How the kanji combine

[小](../kanji/小.md) (small; お here, a prefix form) plus [川](../kanji/川.md) (river; かわ, voiced to がわ): "a small river". The readings are all native (kun), which makes it a
wago word, unlike on-reading compounds such as 河川 かせん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: せせらぎ (a poetic word for the babbling of a brook), 渓流 けいりゅう (a mountain stream), 流れ ながれ (a stream, flow).
- Contrast: 大河 たいが (a large river), 用水路 ようすいろ (an irrigation channel, artificial).
- Antonym: 大川 おおかわ ("big river", used for large rivers and place names).

## Distinctive meaning

川 is the ordinary word for river. 小川 specifically stresses small, calm and picturesque; it is the typical stream of nursery songs
(「春の小川」, "the spring brook").
