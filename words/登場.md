# 登場 (とうじょう) - appearance on the scene; entrance on stage

## Meaning

登場 is an entrance or appearance: an actor comes on stage, a character appears
in a story, or a person or product enters the scene. Use 登場する; に can identify
the work or setting in which someone appears.

- この小説には三人の子どもが登場します。
  (このしょうせつにはさんにんのこどもがとうじょうします。) - Three children appear in this novel.
- 新しい機種が市場に登場しました。
  (あたらしいきしゅがしじょうにとうじょうしました。) - A new model appeared on the market.

登場人物 (とうじょうじんぶつ) are the characters in a story or drama.

## How the kanji combine

[登](../kanji/登.md) contributes coming up or entering, and
[場](../kanji/場.md) the scene or stage. The on readings are トウ + ジョウ.
The established meaning extends beyond physically climbing onto a stage.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[現れる](現れる.md) (あらわれる) is a broader verb for appearing.
[出る](出る.md) (でる) also has appearance and participation uses.
[紹介](紹介.md) (しょうかい) concerns introducing someone or something;
登場 can describe the resulting arrival without naming who introduced it.
退場 (たいじょう), leaving the stage or venue, contrasts with the stage entrance.

## Distinctive meaning

登場 is not limited to a first appearance, and a fictional character need not
be physically present in a real place. Do not confuse entering the scene with
[出席](出席.md) (しゅっせき), attendance at a meeting or class.
