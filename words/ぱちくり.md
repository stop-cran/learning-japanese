# ぱちくり — blinking in surprise

## Meaning

ぱちくり describes opening and blinking one's eyes, often in surprise or puzzlement.

- 知らせを聞いて、彼は目をぱちくりさせた。
  — He blinked in surprise when he heard the news.
- 突然明かりがついて、子どもたちは目をぱちくりした。
  — The children blinked when the lights suddenly came on.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぱちくり emphasizes repeated eye movement in response to surprise, not simply having large eyes.

## Distinctive meaning

It commonly appears with 目 and する or させる.
