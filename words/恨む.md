# 恨む (うらむ) - resent; bear a grudge against

## Meaning

恨む is a transitive godan verb: resent someone or something, feel bitter
toward them, or bear a grudge. The target takes を. Useful forms are
恨みます, 恨んだ and 恨まない.

- 約束を破った友人を恨んでいました。 (やくそくをやぶったゆうじんをうらんでいました。) - I resented the friend who broke their promise.
- 私を恨まないでください。 (わたしをうらまないでください。) - Please do not hold it against me.

## How the kanji combine

恨 writes うら in the native verb 恨む; む is its dictionary-form ending.
[恨み](恨み.md) (うらみ) names the resentment or grudge.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[責める](責める.md) (せめる), "blame; reproach," can involve criticizing
someone, whereas 恨む focuses on bitter feeling toward a target.
[憎む](憎む.md) (にくむ), "hate," overlaps in hostility but does not
itself specify a grudge over a perceived wrong.
[許す](許す.md) (ゆるす), "forgive," provides a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

An English translation such as "curse" need not mean casting a magical
spell: 恨む can simply express bitterness or blame. A person can 恨む
someone without telling them or taking revenge. 怨む is another
dictionary-recognized spelling of this verb.
