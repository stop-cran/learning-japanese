# ランチ - lunch; launch boat

## Meaning

The meal word ランチ means lunch. It also names a Western-style
single-plate lunch set. Both uses are nouns.

- 友達とランチを食べました。 (ともだちとランチをたべました。) - I had lunch with a friend.
- この店のランチは千円です。 (このみせのランチはせんえんです。) - The lunch set at this restaurant costs one thousand yen.

**Separate word for recognition:** ランチ also represents English
"launch" in the sense of a small boat. This is a different dictionary
entry, not a kind of meal. The source list combines "launch, lunch";
that mixed gloss does not establish a separate N2 assignment for each identity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[昼食](昼食.md) (ちゅうしょく), [昼御飯](昼御飯.md) (ひるごはん)
and [お昼](お昼.md) (おひる) can all mean lunch.
[食事](食事.md) (しょくじ) and [ご飯](ご飯.md) (ごはん) cover meals more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the surrounding context to identify the word.
Do not infer a product-launch sense from the boat entry alone.
Digital Daijisen additionally records ランチ【launch】 with a cross-reference
to ローンチ for the business use. This is separate recognition evidence,
not a claim about its frequency or an additional N2 assignment.
