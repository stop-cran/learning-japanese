# 四角 (しかく) - quadrilateral; square shape

## Meaning

四角 is a four-cornered shape, including a square or rectangle. It is a noun and can modify with の or な. 四角に describes a resulting four-cornered shape.

- 紙に四角を描いてください。 (かみにしかくをかいてください。) - Please draw a quadrilateral on the paper.
- このパンを四角に切りました。 (このパンをしかくにきりました。) - I cut this bread into a rectangular shape.

## How the kanji combine

[四](../kanji/四.md) contributes four, and [角](../kanji/角.md) contributes corners or angles. The four corners characterize the whole shape. Use the on readings シ + カク, not よんかど.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[四角い](四角い.md) しかくい is the i-adjective square or rectangular. 正方形 (せいほうけい) specifies a mathematical square, while 長方形 (ちょうほうけい) specifies a rectangle. [三角](三角.md) さんかく names a three-cornered shape, not an antonym in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer four equal sides from 四角 alone. Everyday square-shaped language can include rectangles. [角](角.md) かど names an individual corner; 四角 names the whole four-cornered shape. The broader dictionary also records angularity and figurative stiff or conventional behavior; those extensions do not impose exact side lengths on the shape.
