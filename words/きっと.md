# きっと - surely, almost certainly

## Meaning

きっと presents a prediction or belief with strong confidence: "surely" or "almost certainly." It is an adverb and does not conjugate.

- 明日はきっと晴れます。 (あしたはきっとはれます。) - It will surely be sunny tomorrow.
- きっと大丈夫ですよ。 (きっとだいじょうぶですよ。) - I'm sure it will be all right.

These express the speaker's confidence, not proof that the outcome is guaranteed.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is the normal spelling. The dictionary records rare phonetic spellings such as 屹度, but they are not needed for ordinary writing. Keep the small っ, which marks the held consonant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

たぶん means "probably" and usually conveys less confidence. 必ず (かならず) often makes a stronger claim of certainty or no exceptions. Their use depends on the sentence, so they are not simply interchangeable percentages.

## Distinctive meaning

きっと is especially useful for expectations and reassurance. It reports the speaker's stance toward the proposition; it is not a noun meaning "certainty" and does not turn an uncertain situation into an established fact.
