# ぐんぐん — rapidly; by leaps and bounds

## Meaning

ぐんぐん describes strong, steady progress or growth.

- 春の雨を受けて、苗がぐんぐん伸びた。
  — With the spring rain, the seedlings grew rapidly.
- 新しい練習法を始めてから、記録がぐんぐん伸びている。
  — Since starting a new training method, the results have been improving by leaps and bounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ぐんぐん emphasizes vigorous progress or growth. どんどん can describe a broader range of
successive actions and changes.

## Distinctive meaning

The word suggests momentum in a positive direction in these examples; it does not by itself
identify what caused the progress.
