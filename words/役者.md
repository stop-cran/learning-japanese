# 役者 (やくしゃ) - actor; actress

## Meaning

役者 is a performer who plays a role: an actor or actress.
The noun does not itself restrict the performer to one gender.
役者になる (やくしゃになる) means to become an actor.

- 彼女は舞台で活躍する役者です。 (かのじょはぶたいでかつやくするやくしゃです。) - She is an actor active on the stage.
- 子供のころから役者になりたかったです。 (こどものころからやくしゃになりたかったです。) - I have wanted to become an actor since childhood.

Figuratively, 役者 can also identify a shrewd or cunning person
who handles situations or appearances skillfully.
That interpretation depends on context, not simply on the person's occupation.

## How the kanji combine

[役](../kanji/役.md) contributes a role or part, and
[者](../kanji/者.md) a person: someone who plays a role.
The on readings are ヤク + シャ, giving やくしゃ.
This meaning aid does not make every person with an assigned duty an actor.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[俳優](俳優.md) (はいゆう) is a close occupational synonym.
[女優](女優.md) (じょゆう) explicitly identifies a female actor.
[主役](主役.md) (しゅやく) can mean the leading role or its performer;
役者 alone does not require a leading part.

## Distinctive meaning

[役](役.md) names a role, while [演技](演技.md) (えんぎ) names acting or a performance.
役者 names the person, not those things.
The figurative shrewd-person use can suggest skillful maneuvering;
it is not an automatic accusation that every actor is dishonest.
