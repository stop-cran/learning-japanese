# 点々 (てんてん) - scattered here and there; dots

## Meaning

点々 describes things scattered here and there or appearing at intervals.
Use 点々と before a verb. As a noun it can also mean dots or spots.

- 山の斜面に家が点々と見えます。 (やまのしゃめんにいえがてんてんとみえます。) - Houses can be seen here and there on the mountainside.
- 紙に赤い点々があります。 (かみにあかいてんてんがあります。) - There are red dots on the paper.

The first sentence presents a scattered distribution, not one continuous row of houses.
The spelling 点点 is also recorded.

## How the kanji combine

[点](../kanji/点.md) supplies a point or spot, and 々 repeats the character.
The repeated on reading テン + テン accompanies the idea of separate points.
々 is an iteration mark, not a second kanji with an independent reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あちこち](あちこち.md) and [方々](方々.md), read ほうぼう for places,
can mean "here and there." 点々 emphasizes the separated distribution.
[ぽつぽつ](ぽつぽつ.md) can also describe scattered appearances or drops, with its own uses.
[転々](転々.md) has the same reading but concerns successive moves or rolling.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not turn an English gloss "little by little" into unrestricted gradual improvement.
For gradual progress, [徐々に](徐々に.md) (じょじょに) is a different expression.
点々 focuses on separate points, drops, or intermittent appearances;
転々と職を変える instead describes moving from job to job.
