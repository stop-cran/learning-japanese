# がくがく — trembling; shaking unsteadily

## Meaning

がくがく describes pronounced shaking, such as knees trembling or teeth chattering from cold
or fear.

- 寒さで歯ががくがく鳴った。
  — My teeth chattered from the cold.
- 長い階段を下りた後、膝ががくがくした。
  — After walking down the long stairs, my knees were trembling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

がくがく often suggests an unsteady, repeated shake. The cause may be cold, fear, or strain;
the word alone does not identify which one.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records an outspoken or candid sense. The examples here teach the trembling sense,
not that separate use.
