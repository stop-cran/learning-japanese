# 倫理 (りんり) — ethics; morals

## Meaning

倫理 means ethics or morals: principles about right and wrong conduct.

- 研究を始める前に、倫理について学びます。 (けんきゅうをはじめるまえに、りんりについてまなびます。) — We study ethics before beginning the research.
- この研究には、倫理の審査が必要です。 (このけんきゅうには、りんりのしんさがひつようです。) — This research requires an ethics review.

These are original usage examples, not dictionary quotations.

## How the kanji combine

[倫](../kanji/倫.md) and [理](../kanji/理.md) form the established compound 倫理, read りんり, for ethics or morals.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[道徳](道徳.md) also concerns moral principles and conduct. 倫理 appears in phrases such as 倫理の審査 (ethics review).

## Distinctive meaning

The second example uses 倫理 in the phrase 倫理の審査, an ethics review for a research study.
