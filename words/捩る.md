# 捩る (ねじる) - twist

## Meaning

ねじる means twisting something, such as a cord or a piece of cloth.
It is a transitive godan verb:
ねじります, ねじって, ねじった, ねじらない.
The dictionary marks the kana spelling as usual; 捩る represents this same verb.

- 紙を細くねじりました。
  (かみをほそくねじりました。) - I twisted the paper into a thin shape.
- 針金をねじって固定しました。
  (はりがねをねじってこていしました。) - I secured it by twisting the wire.

The material being twisted takes を.
In the second example, ねじって gives the means of securing something.

## How the kanji combine

捩 supplies ねじ and る is okurigana.
捩る also occurs with other dictionary readings, but this card teaches
the verified reading ねじる. It is not an ichidan verb merely because
its ending contains じる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[回す](回す.md) (まわす) broadly means turning or rotating and includes twisting.
[絞る](絞る.md) (しぼる) includes wringing:
twisting a wet cloth can also be a way to wring it out.
[巻く](巻く.md) (まく), "wind; coil," need not twist the material itself.
Do not turn these differences into an absolute partition of every hand movement.

## Distinctive meaning

The family also records spraining or twisting a body part.
That recognition sense does not make every invented injury expression natural;
this article's examples stay with physical twisting.
The related [ねじ](ねじ.md) is a screw or winding mechanism,
whereas ねじる names an action.
