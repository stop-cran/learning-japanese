# 既に (すでに) - already

## Meaning

既に is an adverb meaning "already." It places an event or state
before the relevant reference point, which can be now or a time in
the past. It does not inflect or need a particle before the predicate.

- 申し込みは既に終わっています。 (もうしこみはすでにおわっています。) - Registration has already closed.
- 駅に着いた時、電車は既に出ていました。 (えきについたとき、でんしゃはすでにでていました。) - When I reached the station, the train had already left.

終わっている and 出ていた describe the result that already held
at the relevant time. A past reference point does not change the
adverb itself.

## How the kanji combine

既 contributes prior completion or "already." The native adverb is
すでに, with に written in kana. JMdict marks the word as usually
written in kana, so すでに and 既に are spellings of the same word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もう](もう.md) also means "already," among other uses.
[まだ](まだ.md) with a negative gives the useful contrast "not yet":
まだ終わっていません, "it has not finished yet."
The words' other meanings prevent treating them as interchangeable
in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

既に does not independently mean "too late." The train example may
imply that the speaker missed the train, but that follows from the
situation. Something can already be completed without anyone being
late or disappointed.
