# 土曜 (どよう) - Saturday

## Meaning

土曜 means Saturday, the same weekday as [土曜日](土曜日.md) (どようび). It is a noun. Use 土曜に for "on Saturday," or attach の before another noun, as in 土曜の朝 (どようのあさ), "Saturday morning."

- 土曜に友達と映画を見ます。 (どようにともだちとえいがをみます。) - I will see a movie with a friend on Saturday.
- この店は毎週土曜が休みです。 (このみせはまいしゅうどようがやすみです。) - This shop is closed every Saturday.

The tense and surrounding expressions determine which Saturday is meant. 土曜 alone does not specify last Saturday or next Saturday.

## How the kanji combine

[土](../kanji/土.md) means earth and supplies the conventional element for this weekday name. [曜](../kanji/曜.md) is the weekday element. The reading is ド + ヨウ. 土曜 omits the final 日 of 土曜日 without changing the weekday.

## Synonyms and antonyms

土曜日 is the full synonymous form, not a different day or a different kind of Saturday. [週末](週末.md) (しゅうまつ), "weekend," names a broader period rather than this one weekday.

## Distinctive meaning

The compact form is useful in schedules and expressions such as 土曜の午後 (どようのごご), "Saturday afternoon." Do not infer that Saturday is necessarily a day off: that depends on the person's school or work schedule. Other weekdays are alternatives in a calendar, not antonyms of 土曜.
