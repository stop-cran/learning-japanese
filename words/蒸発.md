# 蒸発 (じょうはつ) - evaporation; unexplained disappearance

## Meaning

蒸発 is evaporation: a liquid becoming vapor. 蒸発する is intransitive; the substance takes が. The word also has a colloquial use for a person's unexplained disappearance.

- 皿に残った水が蒸発しました。 (さらにのこったみずがじょうはつしました。) - The water left on the plate evaporated.
- 彼は家族を残して蒸発してしまいました。 (かれはかぞくをのこしてじょうはつしてしまいました。) - He disappeared without a trace, leaving his family behind.

The second sentence is about a person leaving and becoming untraceable, not a claim that a human body turned into gas.

## How the kanji combine

[蒸](../kanji/蒸.md) concerns steam, and [発](../kanji/発.md) emission or coming forth. Together they express vapor being given off. Read ジョウ + ハツ; the colloquial disappearance use extends the whole word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[蒸気](蒸気.md) (じょうき) names vapor, not the process of evaporation. 沸騰 (ふっとう) is boiling; evaporation need not wait for a liquid to boil. [消える](消える.md) (きえる), "disappear," is broader and need not concern either vapor or a missing person.

## Distinctive meaning

Water can 蒸発する from a plate without visible boiling. Conversely, not every disappearance is described as 蒸発: the colloquial person-use suggests vanishing without a trace. Use the physical or human context to identify which meaning is intended.
