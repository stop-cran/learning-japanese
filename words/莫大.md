# 莫大 (ばくだい) - enormous; immense

## Meaning

莫大 is a na-adjective meaning "enormous; immense." It is useful for very large amounts, especially money, costs, profits, or losses. Use **莫大な + noun**.

- この工事には莫大な費用がかかります。 (このこうじにはばくだいなひようがかかります。) - This construction work will cost an enormous amount.
- 会社は莫大な利益を得ました。 (かいしゃはばくだいなりえきをえました。) - The company made an immense profit.

Both examples concern amounts. 莫大な費用 can be substantial even if the object being built is not physically gigantic.

## How the kanji combine

莫 has a negating sense in kanji vocabulary, while [大](../kanji/大.md) contributes largeness. Learn バク + ダイ as the established compound "immense." The characters can suggest size beyond ordinary bounds as a mnemonic, not a literal formula or a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大きい](大きい.md) (おおきい) is a broader everyday adjective for "big." [巨大](巨大.md) (きょだい) also means "huge," particularly useful for great size. For an amount, 少額 (しょうがく), "a small sum," can supply a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

莫大 conveys the speaker's assessment of scale, not a numerical threshold. A beginner should learn collocations such as 莫大な費用 and 莫大な利益 rather than treating it as a replacement for every 大きい. Remember the な connector: 莫大な額 (ばくだいながく), "an enormous sum."
