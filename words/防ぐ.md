# 防ぐ (ふせぐ) - prevent; protect against

## Meaning

防ぐ is a transitive godan verb for preventing an unwanted event or protecting against something. **Nを防ぐ** marks what is kept out, warded off, or prevented.

- この壁は風を防ぎます。 (このかべはかぜをふせぎます。) - This wall protects against the wind.
- 事故を防ぐために、点検します。 (じこをふせぐために、てんけんします。) - We inspect it to prevent accidents.

The object is the threat or unwanted event, not normally the person being protected. Forms include 防ぎます, 防いで, and 防がない.

## How the kanji combine

防 supplies the preventing or defending meaning; ぐ completes the native verb read ふせぐ. In the て-form, ぐ changes to いで: 防いで, not 防って.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[避ける](避ける.md) (さける) can mean avoiding a danger by keeping away from it. 防ぐ emphasizes blocking it or preventing its occurrence. [守る](守る.md) (まもる) can directly name the protected person or thing: 子供を守る, "protect children."

## Distinctive meaning

Translate the entire construction. 風を防ぐ does not mean protecting the wind; it means protecting against it. Likewise, 事故を防ぐ means preventing an accident. A statement of purpose with ために says what a measure aims to do, not that it guarantees complete protection.
