# 弾劾 (だんがい) — impeachment; denunciation

## Meaning

弾劾 is a noun with two related uses: **impeachment** in a legal setting, and **denunciation** that exposes wrongdoing and demands accountability. 弾劾する is transitive: the person or conduct denounced takes を.

- 政府の失政を弾劾する。 (せいふのしっせいをだんがいする。) — Denounce the government's misrule.
- 裁判官の弾劾について調べました。 (さいばんかんのだんがいについてしらべました。) — I looked into the impeachment of judges.

The first example illustrates broader denunciation, not necessarily a legal impeachment. In the second, 裁判官 means "judge"; Nの弾劾 is a noun phrase, and について marks the topic being investigated. Useful verb forms are 弾劾します (だんがいします), 弾劾した (だんがいした), and 弾劾しない (だんがいしない).

## How the kanji combine

弾 here contributes exposing wrongdoing or censuring, not its bullet or instrument-playing meaning. [劾](../kanji/劾.md) contributes investigating or pursuing wrongdoing and censure. The related accusatory meanings reinforce each other; this is a contextual meaning explanation, not a historical derivation. Their on-readings are ダン + ガイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[批判](批判.md) (ひはん), "criticism; critical judgment," is broader: a critical assessment need not accuse someone of wrongdoing or begin an impeachment. [責める](責める.md) (せめる), "blame; reproach," also overlaps with the censure sense.

[不正](不正.md) (ふせい) means wrongdoing or dishonesty: it can be what is denounced, not the denunciation itself. Praise, acquittal and removal from office are not one interchangeable antonym of 弾劾.

## Distinctive meaning

弾劾 suits legal or news contexts and strong denunciation. It is not a neutral substitute for ordinary feedback on a film or someone's work. The legal procedure depends on the system concerned; do not assume one country's impeachment rules apply everywhere.

Reporting 弾劾 does not itself establish guilt, the fairness of the accusation, or completed removal from office.
