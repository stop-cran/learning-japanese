# 飾る (かざる) — decorate; display

## Meaning

飾る means to decorate a place or put something on display. It is a transitive godan verb, with forms 飾ります, 飾って, and 飾らない.

- 花で部屋を飾りました。 (はなでへやをかざりました。) — I decorated the room with flowers.
- 壁に写真を飾っています。 (かべにしゃしんをかざっています。) — I have photographs displayed on the wall.

In the first pattern, を marks the place decorated and で marks the decorations used. In the second, を marks the displayed item and に marks its location.

## How the kanji combine

飾 expresses adornment. The final る completes the native verb かざる; there are no separately read compound characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

置く (おく) means to put something somewhere, without necessarily displaying it attractively. 片付ける (かたづける), "put away," can describe removing the displayed items, but is not a universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

飾る is not limited to adding ornaments to an object. It also covers presenting flowers, photographs, or other objects for people to see. Learn both object patterns rather than translating only "decorate."
