# 方面 (ほうめん) - direction; area; field

## Meaning

方面 is a direction or the area in that direction. It also names a field of activity, sphere, or aspect. It is a noun and can follow a place name.

- この電車は新宿方面へ行きます。 (このでんしゃはしんじゅくほうめんへいきます。) - This train goes toward the Shinjuku area.
- 彼は音楽方面で活躍しています。 (かれはおんがくほうめんでかつやくしています。) - He is active in the field of music.

The first sentence gives a direction, not a promise that 新宿 is the train's final stop. 各方面 (かくほうめん) means various quarters, fields, or relevant groups, depending on context.

## How the kanji combine

[方](../kanji/方.md) contributes direction; [面](../kanji/面.md) contributes a side or aspect. The related ideas point to a particular side, direction, or sphere. The on readings are ホウ + メン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[方向](方向.md) (ほうこう) and [方角](方角.md) (ほうがく) concern direction. [地域](地域.md) (ちいき) is an area or region; [分野](分野.md) (ぶんや) a field of activity.

[面](面.md) (めん), "aspect," overlaps the figurative side of 方面. There is no single opposite: the contrasting direction or field depends on the situation.

## Distinctive meaning

方面 often presents an area or field as the side toward which something is directed. A station announcement's 東京方面 (とうきょうほうめん) is "toward Tokyo," not necessarily the administrative district of Tokyo. Its abstract use is broader than compass bearings alone.
