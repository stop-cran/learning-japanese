# 悩む (なやむ) - be troubled; worry

## Meaning

悩む is an intransitive godan verb meaning to be worried or troubled.
It can describe struggling with a problem or being unable to settle a choice.
Use Nに悩む or Nで悩む for the source of difficulty, and a question clause
with か for a decision.

- 進路について悩んでいます。 (しんろについてなやんでいます。) - I am worried about which path to take in my education or career.
- どちらを買うか悩みました。 (どちらをかうかなやみました。) - I struggled to decide which one to buy.

The て-form is 悩んで and the past is 悩んだ. The first sentence describes
an ongoing concern.

## How the kanji combine

悩 writes the native stem なや-, followed by む.
There is no compound to divide. The related noun 悩み (なやみ) names a
worry or trouble, while 悩む describes experiencing it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[心配](心配.md) (しんぱい) also concerns worry, such as concern for someone's
safety. [困る](困る.md) (こまる) describes being in difficulty.
[考える](考える.md) (かんがえる), "think," does not itself imply the distress
or difficulty in reaching a decision that 悩む can convey.

## Distinctive meaning

悩む need not describe a grave crisis: choosing between purchases can cause
indecision. Nor does it guarantee that a decision will follow.
Keep the experiencer as the subject. To say that something troubles another
person, a different construction is needed; simply putting that person before
を悩む does not express "worry that person."
