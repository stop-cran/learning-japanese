# 平日 (へいじつ) - weekday; non-holiday day

## Meaning

平日 names ordinary days rather than holidays: weekdays or non-holiday days in the
relevant calendar or schedule. It is a noun. In many modern work and service schedules
it means Monday through Friday excluding public holidays, but Saturday's treatment
depends on the context.

- この店は平日の午前中がすいています。 (このみせはへいじつのごぜんちゅうがすいています。) - This shop is less crowded on weekday mornings.
- 平日と休日では料金が違います。 (へいじつときゅうじつではりょうきんがちがいます。) - The rates differ between weekdays and holidays.

## How the kanji combine

[平](../kanji/平.md) contributes ordinary or usual here; [日](../kanji/日.md)
contributes day. The compound concerns ordinary days, not a literally flat sun:
ヘイ + ジツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[休日](休日.md) (きゅうじつ) is a holiday or day off; [祝日](祝日.md)
(しゅくじつ) is a national/public holiday. [普段](普段.md) (ふだん) and
[日常](日常.md) (にちじょう) concern ordinary or everyday life more generally,
not necessarily this calendar category.

## Distinctive meaning

Check a timetable or business's stated conditions rather than assuming a universal
Monday-Friday definition. A public holiday on Monday can be excluded from 平日料金
(へいじつりょうきん), "weekday rates." A person's day off on Tuesday does not
automatically make Tuesday a public holiday.
The separate dictionary radical-name sense is restricted to ひらび, not the
へいじつ reading taught here.
