# 審判 (しんぱん) - judgment; referee; umpire

## Meaning

審判 can name a judgment or decision, the activity of refereeing, or the referee/umpire. The action can take する; the official is a noun.

- 明日の試合では、私が審判をします。 (あしたのしあいでは、わたしがしんぱんをします。) - I will referee tomorrow's match.
- 審判が笛を吹きました。 (しんぱんがふえをふきました。) - The referee blew the whistle.

Outside sport, 審判を下す (しんぱんをくだす), "deliver a judgment," concerns a decision, not appointing a referee.

## How the kanji combine

審 contributes examining carefully, and [判](../kanji/判.md) contributes judging or deciding. The linked actions give シン + パン: examine and judge. The compound connects assessment with a decision, explaining both the activity and the official's role.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[判断](判断.md) (はんだん) broadly names judgment or a decision, including everyday decisions. [裁判](裁判.md) (さいばん) names a trial or judicial proceeding. These overlap with the judging domain but are not names for a sports official.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the predicate and context to identify the sense. In the examples, 審判をする describes refereeing, while 笛を吹く (ふえをふく), "blow a whistle," identifies 審判 as the official. The particle が alone does not establish that the noun names a person. The appropriate English role also depends on the sport: "referee," "umpire," and "judge" are not interchangeable labels in every competition.
