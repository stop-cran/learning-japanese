# 航空 (こうくう) - aviation; air transport

## Meaning

航空 is a noun for aviation or travel through the air by aircraft. It appears in expressions about the field, industry, and means of transport, including 航空会社 (こうくうがいしゃ), "airline," and 航空便 (こうくうびん), "airmail/air freight."

- 将来、航空会社で働きたいです。 (しょうらい、こうくうがいしゃではたらきたいです。) - I want to work for an airline in the future.
- この荷物は航空便で送りました。 (このにもつはこうくうびんでおくりました。) - I sent this parcel by air.

航空の歴史 (こうくうのれきし) means "the history of aviation."

## How the kanji combine

航 concerns navigation or travel by a vessel, and [空](../kanji/空.md) supplies the air or sky. Together they specify navigation through the air. Read both long vowels clearly: こうくう, not こくう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飛行機](飛行機.md) (ひこうき) is an airplane, the vehicle itself. [空港](空港.md) (くうこう) is an airport. [鉄道](鉄道.md) (てつどう), rail transport, is another transport category, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

航空 names a field or mode of travel rather than being a general substitute for the verb "fly." For "I went by plane," 飛行機で行きました is a straightforward description. The noun 航空 becomes especially useful inside compounds about services, companies, and aviation-related topics.
