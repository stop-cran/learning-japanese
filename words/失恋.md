# 失恋 (しつれん) - romantic heartbreak; disappointed love

## Meaning

失恋 is disappointment or heartbreak in romantic love, including rejection or love that is not returned. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb: **失恋する**, "be disappointed in love; suffer romantic heartbreak."

- 好きな人に断られて、失恋しました。
  (すきなひとにことわられて、しつれんしました。) - The person I liked turned me down, and I was heartbroken.
- 彼女は失恋の経験について話しました。
  (かのじょはしつれんのけいけんについてはなしました。) - She spoke about her experience of romantic heartbreak.

好きな人に断られる identifies being turned down by the person one likes. The person experiencing the failed love is the subject of 失恋する; it is not a transitive verb meaning "break someone else's heart."

## How the kanji combine

[失](../kanji/失.md) contributes loss or failure, and [恋](../kanji/恋.md) romantic love. Together they identify frustrated or unsuccessful love, not loss of every kind of affection. The on readings are シツ + レン: しつれん, without a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[失望](失望.md) (しつぼう) is disappointment more generally. [別れる](別れる.md) (わかれる), "part; break up," describes separation. A breakup may lead to 失恋, but a person can experience 失恋 after rejection without ever having been in a relationship.

片思い (かたおもい), "one-sided love," concerns feelings that are not reciprocated; the disappointed or failed-love perspective is what 失恋 foregrounds.

## Distinctive meaning

失恋 is not the ordinary word for a physical heart condition or sadness over an examination result. Nor does it require that all affection disappear immediately. "Broken heart" is an English emotional metaphor here; it does not identify an injured body organ.
