# 麓 (ふもと) - foot of a mountain

## Meaning

麓 is the foot or base of a mountain or hill.
Use 山の麓 (やまのふもと) for the foot of a mountain, and に for a location there.

- 山の麓に小さな村があります。 (やまのふもとにちいさなむらがあります。) - There is a small village at the foot of the mountain.
- 麓から頂上まで歩きました。 (ふもとからちょうじょうまであるきました。) - We walked from the foot of the mountain to the summit.

ふもと is the same noun written in kana.

## How the kanji combine

麓 is a single character writing the native word ふもと.
There is no multi-kanji compound to divide. Learn the whole reading;
the on reading ロク is not the reading used in these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頂上](頂上.md) (ちょうじょう), the summit, supplies a useful mountain-location contrast.
[底](底.md) (そこ) means a bottom or lowest surface more broadly.
麓 identifies a mountain's lower area, not the bottom of every container or object.

## Distinctive meaning

Here English "foot" describes a landform's base, not a body part.
The noun does not specify an exact elevation or a fixed distance from the summit.
The spelling 梺 appears as search-only data in the pinned dictionary;
this article uses 麓 rather than presenting it as an equally recommended alternative.
