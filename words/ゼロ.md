# ゼロ - zero

## Meaning

ゼロ means zero, the number 0. It also describes a quantity as absent or nonexistent.

間違いはゼロでした。 (まちがいはゼロでした。) - There were zero mistakes.

今日はゼロ度です。 (きょうはゼロどです。) - It is zero degrees today.

The second example places ゼロ before the unit 度 (ど), "degrees."

## How the kanji combine

ゼロ is a borrowed number word written in katakana. The number may instead be written 0, 〇, or 零 in appropriate notation. The written page title contains no kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

零 (れい) is another word for zero. まる can read the circular symbol in identifiers or other established conventions; it is not the general arithmetic word for zero.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose number readings according to the whole expression. ゼロ and れい overlap, but fixed terms and reading conventions may favor one. ゼロから始める (ゼロからはじめる), "start from zero," can mean starting from scratch rather than referring to a literal measured quantity.
