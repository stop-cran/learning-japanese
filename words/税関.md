# 税関 (ぜいかん) - customs office; customs authority

## Meaning

税関 is customs: the office or authority that handles goods entering or
leaving a country, including inspections and customs duties.

- 空港の税関で荷物を調べられました。 (くうこうのぜいかんでにもつをしらべられました。) - My luggage was inspected at airport customs.
- 税関の職員に質問しました。 (ぜいかんのしょくいんにしつもんしました。) - I asked a customs officer a question.

The noun can name the organization or its office. The first example uses
で for where the inspection took place.

## How the kanji combine

[税](../kanji/税.md) contributes tax or duty, and [関](../kanji/関.md)
a barrier or checkpoint association: a checkpoint concerned with goods
and duties. The on readings are ゼイ + カン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[税](税.md) names tax itself, not the office.
入国審査 (にゅうこくしんさ) is immigration inspection, concerned with
people's entry; it is not simply another name for the goods inspection.
English "customs" here does not mean [習慣](習慣.md), habits or customs.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer that every traveler must pay a tax simply because they pass
through 税関. The word identifies the authority or office, not a claim
about an individual's duty amount. Keep the initial voiced ぜい.
