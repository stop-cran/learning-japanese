# 工員 (こういん) - factory worker

## Meaning

工員 is a factory worker. It is a noun identifying a worker's role,
particularly in factory production.

- 父は工場で工員として働いています。 (ちちはこうじょうでこういんとしてはたらいています。) - My father works as a factory worker.
- この工場には百人の工員がいます。 (このこうじょうにはひゃくにんのこういんがいます。) - There are a hundred factory workers at this factory.

として means "as" in the first example. The second uses 人 (にん)
to count people; 員 is part of the occupational word, not a replacement
for that counter.

## How the kanji combine

[工](../kanji/工.md) contributes making or industrial work, and
[員](../kanji/員.md) contributes a worker or member.
The first character specifies the kind of worker.
The on readings are コウ + イン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[工場](工場.md) (こうじょう) is a factory, the workplace rather than
the person. [同僚](同僚.md) (どうりょう) is a coworker or colleague;
two 工員 may be 同僚, but that relationship is not an occupation.
[技師](技師.md) (ぎし) is an engineer or technical specialist.

## Distinctive meaning

工員 does not mean every employee or every person present in a factory.
It does not by itself specify gender, seniority, employment contract,
or technical qualifications. Do not confuse the person with the building
simply because both 工員 and 工場 begin with 工.
