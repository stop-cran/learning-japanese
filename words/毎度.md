# 毎度 (まいど) - each time; thanks for your custom

## Meaning

毎度 means each time or every time; depending on context, it can also be translated "always" or "often." In customer-facing speech it appears in thanks for repeated patronage.

- 毎度、同じ話を聞きます。 (まいど、おなじはなしをききます。) - I hear the same story every time.
- 毎度ありがとうございます。 (まいどありがとうございます。) - Thank you for your continued custom.

The abbreviated greeting 毎度! can mean "thanks," "welcome," or "hello," especially to regular customers. The dictionary labels this abbreviated use familiar; it is not a universally formal business greeting.

## How the kanji combine

[毎](../kanji/毎.md) contributes every; [度](../kanji/度.md) contributes an occasion or time. Read まい + ど, "on every occasion."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いつも](いつも.md), "always; usually," and [たびたび](たびたび.md), "repeatedly; often," overlap frequency uses.

[ありがとう](ありがとう.md) and [どうも](どうも.md) can express thanks more generally. 毎度ありがとうございます explicitly invokes repeated dealings or patronage.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the customer formula as a whole rather than interpreting 毎度 as a noun meaning gratitude in every sentence. 毎度のこと (まいどのこと) means something that happens each time.

A separate dictionary sense, "hello; I'm coming in" when visiting a home, is identified as Hokuriku dialect. It should not be taught as a nationwide replacement for every visiting greeting.
