# ため (ため) - purpose; for the sake of

## Meaning

ため names a purpose or benefit. In the pattern dictionary-form verb + ために, it means "in order to." Noun + のために means "for" or "for the sake of" that person or goal.

- 日本で働くために、日本語を勉強しています。 (にほんではたらくために、にほんごをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying Japanese in order to work in Japan.
- 家族のために料理を作りました。 (かぞくのためにりょうりをつくりました。) - I cooked for my family.

For this purpose use, the first clause normally names an intended goal. Do not attach ため directly to a noun: 家族のため, not 家族ため.

## How the kanji combine

ため is normally written in kana. 為 is the source-list kanji spelling, but kana is the practical default in these constructions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

目的 (もくてき) is the ordinary noun "purpose" or "objective." ように also occurs in purpose expressions, especially desired abilities or states, but is not freely interchangeable with ために.

## Distinctive meaning

ため can also introduce a cause rather than a goal: 雨のため、試合は中止です (あめのため、しあいはちゅうしです), "The match is canceled because of rain." Nothing intends the rain; context distinguishes cause from purpose.
