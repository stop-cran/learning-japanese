# 点 (てん) — dot or point

## Meaning

点 can be a small dot, spot, or mark. It also has established uses for score points
and for a particular aspect under discussion.

- ここに点を打ってください。 (ここにてんをうってください。) — Please put a dot here.
- この点が大切です。 (このてんがたいせつです。) — This point is important.

The first sentence concerns a visible mark; the second can concern a detail in an explanation.
For a score, 百点 (ひゃくてん) means "one hundred points."

## How the kanji combine

There is one written character, [点](../kanji/点.md).
This word uses its on reading テン, so it is an on-based noun even though it is not a two-kanji compound.
Do not select one of the character's kun readings for the noun taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

For the drawing sense, 線 (せん), "line," makes a useful contrast: a dot marks a small spot,
while a line extends along a path. The two are not interchangeable drawing instructions.

## Distinctive meaning

An English "point" can mean several things, and 点 covers several rather than just one.
Look at the surrounding construction: 点を打つ (てんをうつ) places a dot, a number plus 点 can give a score,
and この点 can pick out an aspect. The small written mark is a starting image, not the limit of the word.
