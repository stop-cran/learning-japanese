# 実る (みのる) -- bear fruit; yield results

## Meaning

実る means bearing fruit or ripening. Figuratively, efforts or plans
実る when they bear fruit: they produce good results.

- 秋になると、稲が実ります。 (あきになると、いねがみのります。) -- In autumn, the rice ripens.
- 長年の努力が実りました。 (ながねんのどりょくがみのりました。) -- Years of effort have borne fruit.

This is an **intransitive godan verb**, despite ending in る:
実ります (みのります), 実らない (みのらない), 実って (みのって)
and 実った (みのった). The thing ripening or effort succeeding takes が.

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) writes みの, connecting fruit with realized
results; る completes the native verb. 稔る is a recorded rare spelling.
The character association is not a reason to conjugate it like 見る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [生る](生る.md) (なる) also describes a plant bearing fruit. 実る additionally makes ripening and successful fruition useful teaching points.
- [収穫](収穫.md) (しゅうかく) is a harvest or fruitful result, not the same grammatical construction as fruit ripening.
- [成功](成功.md) (せいこう) concerns success; [結果](結果.md) (けっか) and [実績](実績.md) (じっせき) concern results or achievements.

## Distinctive meaning

実る is not merely growing larger: a plant can grow before it bears
fruit. The figurative use suggests successful fruition, not just any
outcome, so an unsuccessful result is not automatically 努力が実った.
