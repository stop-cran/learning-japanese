# 女の人 (おんなのひと) - woman

## Meaning

女の人 is an everyday expression for a woman. The whole expression functions as a noun: 女の人が marks a woman as the subject, and 女の人に can identify the person you speak to or ask.

- あの女の人は私の先生です。 (あのおんなのひとはわたしのせんせいです。) - That woman is my teacher.
- 駅で女の人に道を聞きました。 (えきでおんなのひとにみちをききました。) - I asked a woman for directions at the station.

## How the kanji combine

[女](../kanji/女.md), おんな, "woman; female," connects through の to [人](../kanji/人.md), ひと, "person." This is native noun + の + noun, not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[女性](女性.md) (じょせい) also means "woman; female" and is useful in formal or general descriptions. Bare [女](女.md) (おんな) can sound blunt when identifying someone. [男の人](男の人.md) (おとこのひと), "man," is the corresponding expression.

[女の子](女の子.md) (おんなのこ) means "girl," though it can also refer to a young adult woman. That overlap does not make the two expressions equally appropriate in every setting.

## Distinctive meaning

女の人 refers to someone; it is not a respectful form of direct address. Do not call a stranger 女の人 to get her attention. Nor does it inherently mean a wife or girlfriend: those relationships require context or a more specific word.
