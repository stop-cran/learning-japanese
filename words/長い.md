# 長い (ながい) - long

## Meaning

長い is an i-adjective for length in space or duration in time.

- この橋は長いです。 (このはしはながいです。) - This bridge is long.
- 待ち時間が長かったです。 (まちじかんがながかったです。) - The waiting time was long.

Use 長い directly before a noun: 長い髪 (ながいかみ), "long hair." The negative is 長くない; 長く is the adverbial form.

## How the kanji combine

[長](../kanji/長.md) expresses length and is read なが here, followed by い. This native adjective does not use the on reading チョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

短い (みじかい), "short," contrasts with both length and duration. 高い (たかい), "high; tall," describes height rather than length along an object.

## Distinctive meaning

長い時間 means a long time, but 長い人 does not mean a tall person. The usual spelling 長い covers both spatial and temporal length; 永い is another spelling associated with time, not physical distance.
