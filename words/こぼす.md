# こぼす - spill; grumble

## Meaning

こぼす is a transitive godan verb: spill or drop something, including liquid, grains, or tears. It also means voicing complaints or letting a feeling show.

- テーブルに水をこぼしてしまいました。 (テーブルにみずをこぼしてしまいました。) - I accidentally spilled water on the table.
- 彼女は涙をこぼしました。 (かのじょはなみだをこぼしました。) - She shed tears.
- 友達に仕事の愚痴をこぼしました。 (ともだちにしごとのぐちをこぼしました。) - I grumbled to a friend about work.

The spilled thing or expressed complaint takes を. The polite form is こぼします; the て-form is こぼして.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 零す is a recognized spelling, with [零](../kanji/零.md) carrying the spilling association rather than the numerical meaning zero. 溢す is also recorded. Neither spelling requires the everyday kana headword to acquire a written-kanji array.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[零れる](零れる.md) (こぼれる) is the intransitive partner: 水がこぼれた, "water spilled," versus 水をこぼした, "someone spilled water." [落す](落す.md) (おとす; usually 落とす) more broadly means dropping or letting something fall.

For complaints, [文句](文句.md) (もんく) and [不平](不平.md) (ふへい) are nouns; 文句を言う and 不平を言う supply the action. こぼす itself is the verb.

## Distinctive meaning

Being transitive does not make the spill intentional. The same verb also describes a feeling escaping into expression: 笑みをこぼす (えみをこぼす), "let a smile show." The object distinguishes a smile, a complaint, and literal water; not every use is a mishap.
