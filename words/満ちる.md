# 満ちる (みちる) - become full; brim with

## Meaning

満ちる is an intransitive ichidan verb: something becomes full or is filled with something. The place or person can be the subject, with に identifying what fills it. Forms include 満ちます, 満ちた, and 満ちている.

- 子どもたちの顔は希望に満ちていました。 (こどもたちのかおはきぼうにみちていました。) - The children's faces were full of hope.
- 潮が満ちてきました。 (しおがみちてきました。) - The tide has been coming in.

Besides physical or figurative fullness, the word describes the tide rising and the moon waxing: 月が満ちる (つきがみちる). A time-related dictionary sense concerns a term reaching its end or maturity.

## How the kanji combine

[満](../kanji/満.md) contributes fullness. The native verb is みちる, with kana marking the ending. There is no second kanji component. Keep it distinct from the transitive 満たす (みたす), "fill; satisfy."

## Synonyms and antonyms

いっぱいになる also expresses becoming full. 満たす identifies an action that fills something, whereas 満ちる presents the filling or full state itself. For the tide, [引く](引く.md) (ひく), "go out; ebb," supplies the practical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

満ちた can describe a resulting quality before a noun: 自信に満ちた声 (じしんにみちたこえ), "a voice full of confidence." The source gloss "mature" concerns time reaching completion here; it is not a general verb for a child growing up or fruit ripening.
