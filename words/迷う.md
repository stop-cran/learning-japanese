# 迷う (まよう) — get lost; be undecided

## Meaning

迷う means to lose one's way. 道に迷う (みちにまよう) is the phrase to learn for getting lost.
The same verb can describe being unable to decide between choices.

- 駅へ行く途中で道に迷いました。 (えきへいくとちゅうでみちにまよいました。) — I got lost on the way to the station.
- どちらを買うか迷っています。 (どちらをかうかまよっています。) — I cannot decide which one to buy.

In the second example, か closes the embedded question "which one to buy"; the whole question is what the person is undecided about.

## How the kanji combine

[迷](../kanji/迷.md) connects losing the way with uncertainty.
Its native verb is まよう; う is written in kana. The polite form is 迷います, and the て-form is 迷って.

## Synonyms and antonyms

迷子 (まいご) is a lost child or person; 道に迷う describes what happens, while 迷子 names the person in that situation.
For the decision sense, [決める](決める.md) (きめる), "decide," marks moving from uncertainty to a choice.
It is not an opposite for every use of 迷う.

## Distinctive meaning

The sentence about buying something is indecision, not physical disorientation.
Conversely, 道に迷う does not say that the person is hesitating about whether to travel.
Let the construction distinguish these two senses instead of translating both as "lost."
