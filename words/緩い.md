# 緩い (ゆるい) - loose; lax; gentle

## Meaning

緩い describes something not tight or strict, or a slope or curve
that is gentle rather than sharp. It is an i-adjective:
緩くない (ゆるくない), 緩かった (ゆるかった), 緩く (ゆるく).

- 靴のひもが緩いので、結び直しました。 (くつのひもがゆるいので、むすびなおしました。) - My shoelaces were loose, so I tied them again.
- この坂は緩いので、歩きやすいです。 (このさかはゆるいので、あるきやすいです。) - This slope is gentle, so it is easy to walk up.
- 以前より規則が緩くなりました。 (いぜんよりきそくがゆるくなりました。) - The rules have become less strict than before.

緩い also describes slow or weak movement or intensity:

- 川の流れは緩いです。 (かわのながれはゆるいです。) - The river's current is slow.

It can describe a soft or not-firm consistency, too.
Choose the English sense from what is described, rather than
translating every 緩い as loose-fitting.

## How the kanji combine

緩 contributes looseness or relaxation and is read
ゆる here; い completes the native adjective.
This is not the separate adjective 緩やか (ゆるやか).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぶかぶか](ぶかぶか.md) stresses a fit that is too big or loose.
[なだらか](なだらか.md) overlaps in gentle slopes.
[柔らかい](柔らかい.md) (やわらかい) overlaps in softness, not every
loose-fitting or lax use.
[きつい](きつい.md) contrasts in tightness;
[厳しい](厳しい.md) (きびしい) in strictness.

## Distinctive meaning

In a phrase such as 緩い雰囲気 (ゆるいふんいき), it can describe
a relaxed, laid-back atmosphere. It can also mean rough or sloppy
when describing a way of doing things. Context determines whether
the looseness is welcome or a criticism.

Do not add "lukewarm" to ゆるい just because the separate word
ぬるい can also be spelled 緩い.
The Hokkaido/Tohoku dictionary use ゆるくない means "difficult" or
"hard"; it is a regional negative expression, not a general rule
that standard 緩い means easy and every 緩くない means difficult.
