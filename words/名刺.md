# 名刺 (めいし) - business card

## Meaning

名刺 is a business card: a small card bearing a person's name and professional
or contact information. 名刺を交換する (めいしをこうかんする) means "exchange business cards."

- 受付で名刺を渡しました。 (うけつけでめいしをわたしました。) - I handed over my business card at reception.
- 初めに名刺を交換しました。 (はじめにめいしをこうかんしました。) - We exchanged business cards at the beginning.

## How the kanji combine

[名](../kanji/名.md) contributes a person's name.
[刺](../kanji/刺.md) has the on reading シ here.
Daijisen explains this 刺 by the ancient Chinese practice of writing one's name
on a prepared bamboo or wooden slip; the word does not mean "stab a name."
The readings are メイ + シ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[カード](カード.md) is a broader word for a card and includes many things that are not 名刺.
[名詞](名詞.md), "noun," has the same reading めいし but a different spelling and meaning.
There is no useful general antonym for a business card.

## Distinctive meaning

名刺 identifies the kind of card, not a compulsory set of fields or a universal etiquette rule.
Do not use it for every card that happens to display a person's name.
The し is 刺, not 詞 as in the grammar term.
