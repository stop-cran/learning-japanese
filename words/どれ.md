# どれ (どれ) — which one

## Meaning

どれ asks "which one?" from a set of choices, typically three or more. It stands in place of a noun.

あなたのかばんはどれですか。（あなたのかばんはどれですか。） — Which one is your bag?

どれが好きですか。（どれがすきですか。） — Which one do you like?

## How the kanji combine

This word is normally written in kana. It is the question member of the これ・それ・あれ・どれ series. The dictionary spelling 何れ is not needed for ordinary beginner use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どっち or どちら commonly asks which of two. これ means "this one," and それ means "that one."

## Distinctive meaning

Before a noun, use どの: どのかばん, "which bag," not どれかばん. When the unknown choice is the subject, use が rather than making どれ a topic with は.
