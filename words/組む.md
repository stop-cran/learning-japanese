# 組む (くむ) - assemble; team up

## Meaning

組む is a godan verb about putting elements together or joining with someone.
Use Xを組む for assembling something, and 人と組む (ひととくむ) for teaming up with a person.
It also describes crossing legs or folding/linking arms.

- 二人で机の枠を組みました。 (ふたりでつくえのわくをくみました。) - The two of us assembled the desk frame.
- 友達と組んで発表します。 (ともだちとくんではっぴょうします。) - I'll team up with a friend to give the presentation.
- 彼は腕を組んで考えていました。 (かれはうでをくんでかんがえていました。) - He was thinking with his arms folded.

The て-form is 組んで (くんで), and the past is 組んだ (くんだ).

## How the kanji combine

[組](../kanji/組.md) expresses grouping or assembling. The native verb has the stem く and ending む.
The related noun 組 (くみ) can name a group; the verb focuses on bringing parts or participants into a joined arrangement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作る](作る.md) (つくる), "make," covers more kinds of creation.
[協力](協力.md) (きょうりょく), "cooperation," overlaps with teaming up, but does not specify the same physical joining uses.
Taking an assembly apart is a contextual reversal, not one universal opposite for all senses.

## Distinctive meaning

Particles matter: 友達と組む identifies a partner, while 腕を組む identifies what is folded.
Do not translate every occurrence as "assemble" or assume that all uses require a direct object.
