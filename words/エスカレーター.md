# エスカレーター — escalator

## Meaning

エスカレーター is an escalator: a moving staircase between levels of a building.

- エスカレーターで二階に上がりました。 (えすかれーたーでにかいにあがりました。) — I went up to the second floor on the escalator.
- エスカレーターはどこですか。 (えすかれーたーはどこですか。) — Where is the escalator?

Use で when presenting it as the means of going between floors. エスカレーターに乗る (えすかれーたーにのる) means to get on or ride an escalator.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana, with long vowels marked by ー. エスカレータ is a recognized spelling variant; this card uses エスカレーター.

## Synonyms and antonyms

エレベーター means an elevator or lift, not an escalator. 階段 (かいだん) means stairs without implying moving steps. These are alternative facilities, not lexical opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

English-speaking learners can confuse the two long loanwords. An エスカレーター has moving steps; an エレベーター carries passengers in a car. The distinction matters when asking for a route between floors.
