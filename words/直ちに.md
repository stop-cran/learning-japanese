# 直ちに (ただちに) - immediately; without delay

## Meaning

直ちに is an adverb meaning immediately or without delay.
It modifies the following action without another particle.
It can also mean that something directly or automatically leads to a conclusion or result.

- 問題が見つかったら、直ちに連絡してください。 (もんだいがみつかったら、ただちにれんらくしてください。) - If a problem is found, contact us immediately.
- 一度の失敗が直ちに終わりを意味するわけではありません。 (いちどのしっぱいがただちにおわりをいみするわけではありません。) - One failure does not automatically mean it is over.

The first example demands prompt action. The second rejects an automatic inference; it is not simply
a prediction about how many minutes a result will take.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) associates the word with directness, while ちに completes the established adverb.
Learn the whole reading ただちに. It is not the same reading as [直に](直に.md).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すぐに](すぐに.md) overlaps with "right away" in everyday speech.
直ちに can give an instruction an urgent, official tone, but is not confined to written notices.
後で (あとで), "later," is the relevant timing contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

In logical explanations, 直ちに can concern the directness of a connection rather than elapsed time.
Read the whole predicate, particularly a negative such as 意味するわけではない, "does not mean."
Do not interpret English "directly" here as "in person": 直ちに連絡する calls for immediate contact,
not necessarily a face-to-face visit.
