# 申請 (しんせい) - application; formal request

## Meaning

申請 is an application, request, or petition, especially a formal request
for an authority to grant permission, approval, or another action.
It is a noun and a transitive する verb.

- ビザを申請しました。 (ビザをしんせいしました。) - I applied for a visa.
- 市に許可を申請しました。 (しにきょかをしんせいしました。) - I applied to the city for permission.

The thing requested takes を; the authority approached can take に.
申請が認められる (しんせいがみとめられる) means an application is approved.

## How the kanji combine

[申](../kanji/申.md) contributes stating or putting forward, and
請 contributes asking or requesting. The related actions combine in a
formal application. The on readings are シン + セイ.
The spelling does not require every application to be spoken aloud.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[申し込む](申し込む.md) (もうしこむ) also means to apply or put in a request,
including signing up for an activity.
[依頼](依頼.md) (いらい) often concerns asking someone to undertake a task.
These uses overlap; learn the expression used for the particular procedure
rather than treating every application as freely interchangeable wording.

## Distinctive meaning

申請 names making the request, not automatically obtaining approval.
It is not the "application" of a rule or method, as in [適用](適用.md)
(てきよう). An application can also be made to an organization other than
a national government; the authority and procedure come from the context.
