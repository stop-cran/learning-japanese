# 長方形 (ちょうほうけい) - rectangle; oblong

## Meaning

長方形 is a rectangle: a quadrilateral with four right angles.
It is a noun; **長方形の + noun** describes something rectangular.

- 紙に長方形を描いてください。
  (かみにちょうほうけいをかいてください。) - Please draw a rectangle on the paper.
- この机は長方形です。
  (このつくえはちょうほうけいです。) - This desk is rectangular.

## How the kanji combine

[長](../kanji/長.md) contributes length,
[方](../kanji/方.md) a four-sided or square form here, and
[形](../kanji/形.md) shape.
The established compound names the rectangle.
Read チョウ + ホウ + ケイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[四角](四角.md) (しかく) is a broader four-cornered shape;
[四角い](四角い.md) (しかくい) is the adjective square or rectangular.
[正方形](正方形.md) (せいほうけい) additionally requires four equal sides.

## Distinctive meaning

In everyday contrasts, 長方形 often suggests an elongated rectangle
rather than a square. Under the inclusive mathematical definition,
a square is a special rectangle; unequal adjacent sides are not an
unconditional requirement.
A slanting four-sided figure without right angles is not thereby a 長方形.
