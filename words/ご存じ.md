# ご存じ (ごぞんじ)

## Meaning

ご存じ respectfully describes someone knowing something or being acquainted with someone. It is the honorific counterpart of 知っている (しっている), not an ordinary suru-verb.

- この店をご存じですか。 (このみせを ごぞんじですか。) — Do you know this shop?
- あの方は先生のご存じの方です。 (あのかたは せんせいの ごぞんじの かたです。) — That person is someone the teacher knows.

The second example illustrates acquaintance: ご存じの方 means a person known to the person being respected.

## How the kanji combine

ご is an honorific prefix. [存](../kanji/存.md)じ is connected with 存じる (ぞんじる), "know." The established expression ご存じ is honorific, despite 存じる also having humble uses. ご存知 is another recognized spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

知っている is neutral. For your own knowledge, 知っています is normally appropriate; do not use ご存じです to elevate yourself.

## Distinctive meaning

Say ご存じです, not ご存じしています. Before a person noun, use の: ご存じの方. Knowledge of a person does not necessarily imply a close friendship, and "acquaintance" should not be read as intimacy.
