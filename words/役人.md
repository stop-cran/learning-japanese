# 役人 (やくにん) - government official

## Meaning

役人 is a government official: someone working in public administration. It is a noun, not a general title for anyone with an important job.

- 役人が新しい制度について説明しました。
  (やくにんがあたらしいせいどについてせつめいしました。) - A government official explained the new system.
- 彼は地方の役人として働いています。
  (かれはちほうのやくにんとしてはたらいています。) - He works as a local government official.

として marks the capacity in which someone works: here, as an official.

## How the kanji combine

[役](../kanji/役.md) contributes an official role or duty, and [人](../kanji/人.md) the person holding it. The on readings are ヤク + ニン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[公務員](公務員.md) (こうむいん), public employee, overlaps, though the two labels need not select exactly the same people in every context. [役所](役所.md) (やくしょ) is a government office; it is a place or institution, not the official.

[重役](重役.md) (じゅうやく) can name a company executive. Seniority alone does not make such a person a 役人.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the government connection. A person in charge of a private event or a manager in a private company is not automatically a 役人.

The word occurs in both neutral descriptions and criticism of officials. Do not assume that it is always an insult or, conversely, a respectful form of address.
