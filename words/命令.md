# 命令 (めいれい) - order; command

## Meaning

命令 is a noun for an order or command. 命令する means "order someone to do something."
A useful pattern is person + に + plain nonpast verb + ように命令する.

- 上司は部下に待つように命令しました。 (じょうしはぶかにまつようにめいれいしました。) - The supervisor ordered the subordinate to wait.
- コンピューターは命令を実行します。 (コンピューターはめいれいをじっこうします。) - The computer executes instructions.

The second example uses the computing sense: an instruction supplied to a computer.
命令に従う (めいれいにしたがう) means "obey an order," with に marking what is followed.

## How the kanji combine

[命](../kanji/命.md) has a command or mandate meaning as well as its familiar life meaning.
令 also contributes an order or directive. Their related meanings reinforce the command sense.
The readings are メイ + レイ; this word does not mean "an order about life" in every use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ), "ask; request," need not assert the authority associated with an order.
[注文](注文.md) (ちゅうもん) is an order for goods or services, a different meaning of English "order."
[従う](従う.md) (したがう) describes complying, not issuing the command.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling an instruction 命令 presents it as a command, not simply helpful advice.
This can matter in describing workplace or personal interactions.
In computing, however, the term is technical rather than a judgment about interpersonal tone.
The recipient and setting distinguish these uses.
