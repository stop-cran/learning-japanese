# 製品 (せいひん) - manufactured product

## Meaning

製品 is a noun for a manufactured product or finished goods. It names the result of making something, whether one item or a range of products.

- この工場では電気製品を作っています。 (このこうじょうではでんきせいひんをつくっています。) - This factory makes electrical products.
- 製品の説明書を読んでください。 (せいひんのせつめいしょをよんでください。) - Please read the product's instruction manual.

Use 製品の + noun for something associated with a product: 製品の品質 (せいひんのひんしつ), "product quality." 新製品 (しんせいひん) means "a new product."

## How the kanji combine

製 contributes manufacture or making, and [品](../kanji/品.md) means an article or item. The first character describes how the second comes into being: an item made or manufactured.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[商品](商品.md) (しょうひん) presents an item as merchandise for sale. A manufactured item sold in a shop can be both a 製品 and a 商品. These are overlapping viewpoints, not two mutually exclusive physical categories.

## Distinctive meaning

製品 is useful in contexts involving makers, factories, specifications, or quality. It does not by itself say that the item has already been sold. Likewise, the word does not specify whether production involved a huge factory or a smaller operation; the central idea is the resulting made product.
