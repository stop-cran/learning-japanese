# ネックレス - necklace

## Meaning

ネックレス is a necklace, an ornament worn around the neck.
It is a noun. ネックレスをつける means putting on or wearing a necklace;
ネックレスを外す (ネックレスをはずす) means taking one off.

- 母にネックレスを贈りました。
  (ははにネックレスをおくりました。) - I gave my mother a necklace.
- このネックレスは軽いです。
  (このネックレスはかるいです。) - This necklace is light.

に marks the recipient in the first example and を marks the gift.
The word does not require a particular material or a pendant.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "necklace," with no kanji components.
The small ッ marks the consonant hold in ネックレス; it is not an extra vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

首飾り (くびかざり) is another word for a necklace or neck ornament.
[指輪](指輪.md) (ゆびわ), "ring," and イヤリング, "earrings,"
are other kinds of jewelry, not synonyms or antonyms of ネックレス.

## Distinctive meaning

A chain can form part of a necklace, but the word names the neck ornament
as a whole. It does not mean a necktie or every object placed around a neck.
Learn the object together with つける and 外す rather than translating
English "wear" with one verb for every kind of clothing or accessory.
