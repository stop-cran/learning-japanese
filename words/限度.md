# 限度 (げんど) - limit; bounds

## Meaning

限度 is a limit or boundary on an amount, degree, or acceptable extent.
It is a noun. 限度を超える (げんどをこえる) means "exceed the limit";
限度がある means "there is a limit."

- 一日に使える金額には限度があります。 (いちにちにつかえるきんがくにはげんどがあります。) - There is a limit to the amount of money you can use in one day.
- 冗談にも限度があります。 (じょうだんにもげんどがあります。) - Even jokes have their limits.

The second example concerns what is acceptable, not a precisely measured
physical boundary. A numerical 限度 can be specified, but the word does
not supply a number by itself.

## How the kanji combine

[限](../kanji/限.md) contributes a limit or restriction, and
[度](../kanji/度.md) contributes degree or measure.
Think of the degree up to which something is possible or allowed.
The on readings are ゲン + ド.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[限界](限界.md) (げんかい) also concerns a limit or boundary.
[制限](制限.md) (せいげん) concerns restriction;
[範囲](範囲.md) (はんい) concerns a scope or range.
[程度](程度.md) (ていど) is degree or extent, which need not be a maximum.

## Distinctive meaning

限度 can be a practical maximum or a socially acceptable bound.
It is not necessarily a legal restriction, and an unstated limit need
not have a sharp numerical cutoff. A restriction can establish a 限度,
but imposing the restriction and naming its bound are different perspectives.
