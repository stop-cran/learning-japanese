# 学 (がく) - learning; scholarship

## Meaning

学 is a noun for learning, education, or scholarly knowledge. In **学がある** (がくがある), it describes someone as learned or well educated. As a suffix, **～学** names a field of study, corresponding to endings such as "-ology."

- 祖父は学のある人でした。 (そふはがくのあるひとでした。) - My grandfather was a learned person.
- 大学で心理学を学んでいます。 (だいがくでしんりがくをまなんでいます。) - I am studying psychology at university.

The second example illustrates the suffix in 心理学 (しんりがく), not a verb formed by adding する to standalone 学.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [学](../kanji/学.md) uses the on reading ガク. It supplies the learning idea in a noun or names a discipline after another element. The native verb [学ぶ](学ぶ.md) (まなぶ) uses a different reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[学問](学問.md) (がくもん) names scholarly study or learning. [知識](知識.md) (ちしき) focuses on knowledge possessed, while [勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) can describe the activity of studying.

## Distinctive meaning

学がある evaluates a person's learning, not whether they are studying at this moment. For "I study every day," use 勉強する or 学ぶ with suitable grammar. Learn discipline names as complete words: the suffix provides a useful pattern, not permission to attach 学 to any topic indiscriminately.
