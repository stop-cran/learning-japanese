# 夏休み (なつやすみ) - summer vacation

## Meaning

夏休み is a summer vacation or summer holiday, especially the school break. It can also refer to time off work taken in summer.

夏休みに祖母の家へ行きます。 (なつやすみにそぼのいえへいきます。) - I will go to my grandmother's house during summer vacation.

夏休みはいつからですか。 (なつやすみはいつからですか。) - When does summer vacation start?

## How the kanji combine

[夏](../kanji/夏.md), "summer," combines with 休み (やすみ), "rest; holiday," written with [休](../kanji/休.md). Both parts use native readings: なつ + やすみ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

休み is any break, day off, or holiday, depending on context. 冬休み (ふゆやすみ) and 春休み (はるやすみ) are the winter and spring breaks. They name different seasonal holidays rather than strict opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

夏休み names the period away from normal school or work, not necessarily a trip. You can spend it at home. Use の to modify another noun: 夏休みの宿題 (なつやすみのしゅくだい), "summer-vacation homework." Dates and length vary by institution and region.
