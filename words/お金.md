# お金 (おかね) — money

## Meaning

お金 is the everyday word for money: お金がない (I have no money), お金を払う おかねをはらう (pay), お金持ち おかねもち (a rich person), お金を貯める おかねをためる (save money).

## How the kanji combine

お (honorific prefix) plus [金](../kanji/金.md) read かね: this is the kun reading, not きん. The prefix お is usual with かね; plain 金 かね is rougher or used in compounds (金持ち かねもち, 金貸し かねかし). The on reading キン is for "gold" and compounds (現金 げんきん, 料金 りょうきん), and 金 is also read きん in 金曜日 ([金曜日](金曜日.md)).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 現金 げんきん (cash), 資金 しきん (funds, formal), 円 えん (yen, unit).
- Related: お札 おさつ (banknote), 小銭 こぜに (small change).

## Distinctive meaning

お金 is the plain, polite way to say money in daily life. 金 on its own (かね) can sound blunt; in compounds, the reading changes: 貯金 ちょきん, 金額 きんがく.
