# 土曜日 (どようび) — Saturday

## Meaning

土曜日 is Saturday: 土曜日は休みです (Saturday is a day off), 毎週土曜日 (every Saturday). Short forms: 土曜 どよう, and 土 in calendars.

## How the kanji combine

[土](../kanji/土.md) (earth, the element, Saturn 土星 どせい) plus [曜](../kanji/曜.md) (day of the week) plus [日](../kanji/日.md) (day): "Saturn-day".
The reading is ど + よう + び: 土 and 曜 use on readings, while 日 is native ひ voiced to び.
This on-first mixed compound is filed under kango here; the label does not make every component an on reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Neighbours: [金曜日](金曜日.md) きんようび (Friday) and 日曜日 にちようび (Sunday).
- Related: 週末 しゅうまつ (weekend).

## Distinctive meaning

土曜日 and 日曜日 form the weekend (土日 どにち). The weekday names refer to the sun, moon and five planets; the five planets use element names. 土 is the earth element associated with Saturn.
