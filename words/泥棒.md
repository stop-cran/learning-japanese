# 泥棒 (どろぼう)

## Meaning

泥棒 means a thief or burglar. It can also name theft itself, especially with する.

- 泥棒が財布を盗みました。 (どろぼうが さいふを ぬすみました。) — A thief stole a wallet.
- 家に泥棒が入りました。 (いえに どろぼうが はいりました。) — A burglar broke into the house.

In these examples 泥棒 names the person. 泥棒をする instead means to commit theft.

## How the kanji combine

泥 means mud and 棒 means a stick, but those literal meanings do not explain the modern word "thief." Learn どろぼう and its conventional spelling together; this is not a productive pattern for naming occupations.

## Synonyms and antonyms

盗人 (ぬすびと) also means thief. [盗む](盗む.md) is the verb "steal." 警察官 (けいさつかん), "police officer," is a related role, not a linguistic antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

泥棒 does not by itself specify a legally precise offense or prove the use of violence. Translate it according to the scene: "burglar" suits entering a house to steal, while "thief" is broader.
