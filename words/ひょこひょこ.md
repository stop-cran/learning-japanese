# ひょこひょこ — taking small, bobbing steps

## Meaning

ひょこひょこ describes small, repeated, slightly unsteady steps with an up-and-down motion.

- 子どもは石の道を、ひょこひょこ歩いていった。
  — The child walked along the stone path with small, bobbing steps.
- 小さな鳥が、庭をひょこひょこ歩き回っている。
  — A little bird is walking around the garden in short, bobbing steps.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ひょこひょこ emphasizes a repeated bobbing gait rather than a smooth, steady stride.

## Distinctive meaning

It often describes the movement of a small person or animal.
