# 豚肉 (ぶたにく) — pork

## Meaning

豚肉 is pork: meat from a pig. It is a food noun, not the name of the living animal.

豚肉と野菜を炒めます。 (ぶたにくとやさいをいためます。) — I stir-fry pork and vegetables.

この料理には豚肉が入っていますか。 (このりょうりにはぶたにくがはいっていますか。) — Does this dish contain pork?

## How the kanji combine

豚 is ぶた, "pig," and [肉](../kanji/肉.md) is にく, "meat." This is a mixed native-plus-on reading, categorized here with native-first words, not an all-on compound. Learn the ordinary reading ぶたにく as a unit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

牛肉 (ぎゅうにく) is beef, and 鶏肉 (とりにく) is chicken meat. 肉 (にく) is the broader meat category. These are different ingredients, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese can name the animal and add 肉 to specify its meat, whereas English has the separate word "pork." 豚肉 does not identify the cut, cooking method, or seasoning. The question in the second example is useful for checking ingredients rather than assuming the dish's name reveals everything in it.
