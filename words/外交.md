# 外交 (がいこう) - diplomacy; foreign relations

## Meaning

外交 is diplomacy: conducting relations with other countries. It is a noun. Use 外交のN for "N concerning diplomacy," or compounds such as 外交政策 (がいこうせいさく), "foreign policy."

- 大学で外交の歴史を勉強しています。 (だいがくでがいこうのれきしをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying the history of diplomacy at university.
- 外交を通じて問題を解決したいです。 (がいこうをつうじてもんだいをかいけつしたいです。) - We want to resolve the problem through diplomacy.

In the second sentence, を通じて means "through; by means of."

## How the kanji combine

[外](../kanji/外.md) contributes "outside," and [交](../kanji/交.md) contributes "exchange; association." Together they point to relations directed outside one's country. The on readings are ガイ + コウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[政治](政治.md) (せいじ), "politics; government," is broader. 外交 concerns external relations, whereas 内政 (ないせい), "domestic administration," is a useful contextual contrast. Diplomacy and domestic politics still influence one another.

## Distinctive meaning

外交 does not mean simply going abroad or being sociable. In this use, countries and their relations are central. 外交官 (がいこうかん) is a diplomat: 官 adds the person holding an official role. Do not interpret that compound as merely a person who enjoys talking with foreigners.
