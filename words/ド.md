# ド (ド) - extreme; very; emphatic prefix

## Meaning

ド, also written ど, is an intensifying prefix in expressions such as
ド真ん中 (どまんなか), "the very center," and ド素人 (どしろうと),
"a complete amateur." Attach it directly to the following word.

- ボールが的のド真ん中に当たりました。 (ボールがまとのどまんなかにあたりました。) - The ball hit the very center of the target.
- 私は料理のド素人です。 (わたしはりょうりのどしろうとです。) - I am a complete beginner at cooking.

The second example is informal and self-deprecating. Applied to another
person, ド素人 can sound dismissive. Some ド- expressions intensify an insult
rather than merely indicate a high degree.

## How the kanji combine

The prefix is written in kana here. Katakana does not make it a loanword.
Learn established expressions as units; it is not a freely substitutable
adverb to put before any Japanese word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[とても](とても.md), "very," is an independent adverb with different grammar.
It does not reproduce the attached prefix in ド真ん中.
There is no single opposite prefix covering both exactness and insulting emphasis.

## Distinctive meaning

This is the intensifier entry, not the separate musical noun ド, "do" in
solfège. The community list's conflicting English gloss does not establish
its compiler's intention. This card deliberately retains the supplied
prefix identity; the musical homograph is a contrast, not an additional
N3 assignment.
