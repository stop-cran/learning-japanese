# 途端 (とたん) - the moment; just as

## Meaning

途端 identifies the very moment something happens. A useful pattern is **verb in the た-form + 途端に**, "the moment that; just as; as soon as." It links closely timed events, often highlighting a sudden development.

- ドアを開けた途端に、電話が鳴りました。 (どあをあけたとたんに、でんわがなりました。) - The moment I opened the door, the phone rang.
- 立ち上がった途端、めまいがしました。 (たちあがったとたん、めまいがしました。) - Just as I stood up, I felt dizzy.

に can be omitted, as in the second example. The た-form locates the first event at its completion or transition point; the second event follows immediately.

The second event is normally presented as a sudden, unexpected development,
not simply the speaker's planned next step. For an ordinary request, use a
different pattern: 着いたら、すぐに電話してください
(ついたら、すぐにでんわしてください), "Please call as soon as you arrive."
The たら pattern here sets the condition for the request rather than reporting
an unexpected event.

## How the kanji combine

[途](../kanji/途.md) contributes a way or course, and 端 contributes an edge or endpoint. They offer a memory image of a sharply defined point along a course. Learn the established temporal meaning rather than translating the characters literally. The reading is ト + タン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[瞬間](瞬間.md) (しゅんかん) names a moment or instant more generally. ～とすぐに also expresses an immediate sequence, while 途端 draws attention to the abrupt timing.

## Distinctive meaning

途端 is not a general replacement for English "just." It does not mean "merely" or independently express "a little while ago." In その途端 (そのとたん), "at that very moment," その points back to the event whose timing matters. There is no useful direct antonym.
