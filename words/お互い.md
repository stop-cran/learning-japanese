# お互い (おたがい) - each other; one another

## Meaning

お互い refers to the parties on both sides of a relationship: "each other" or "one another." It functions as a noun and in adverbial expressions. Useful patterns are お互いに, "mutually," and お互いのN, "each other's N."

- お互いに助け合いましょう。 (おたがいにたすけあいましょう。) - Let's help one another.
- お互いの名前を知っています。 (おたがいのなまえをしっています。) - We know each other's names.

In the second example, の connects お互い to the possessed noun 名前.

## How the kanji combine

[互](../kanji/互.md) carries the reciprocal idea. お adds politeness to [互い](互い.md) (たがい); い is part of the written native word. This is not an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

互い expresses the same reciprocal relationship without お. [皆](皆.md) (みんな), "everyone," identifies all members of a group but does not by itself say that an action goes both ways.

## Distinctive meaning

お互い need not imply that two people act on each other. お互い忙しいですね (おたがいいそがしいですね), "We're both busy, aren't we?", identifies a shared situation. お互い様 (おたがいさま) similarly acknowledges that a benefit, difficulty, or obligation goes both ways; its tone depends on context.
