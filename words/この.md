# この — this; before a noun

## Meaning

この means "this" directly before a noun. It typically identifies something near the speaker or something the speaker is currently presenting.

- この本を借りてもいいですか。 (このほんをかりてもいいですか。) — May I borrow this book?
- この人は私の先生です。 (このひとはわたしのせんせいです。) — This person is my teacher.

No extra の or な goes between この and the noun.

## How the kanji combine

This word is normally written in hiragana. The dictionary records 此の and 斯の as rare spellings, but they are not needed for ordinary writing. この is one complete prenominal expression.

## Synonyms and antonyms

その means "that" near the listener or already mentioned; あの often points to something farther from both speakers. どの asks "which" before a noun.

## Distinctive meaning

この cannot stand alone as the subject meaning "this thing." Use これ for that: これは私の本です (これはわたしのほんです), "This is my book." Compare この本, "this book," with これ, "this."
