# 陰 (かげ) - shade; concealed side

## Meaning

陰 is a noun for shade or a place screened from light or view. It can also mean the hidden side of something, or activity carried on out of sight.

- 木の陰で休みましょう。 (きのかげでやすみましょう。) - Let's rest in the shade of the tree.
- 子どもがドアの陰に隠れています。 (こどもがドアのかげにかくれています。) - A child is hiding behind the door.

Nの陰 identifies what provides the cover. Use で for an activity performed there and に for a location or destination as required by the verb.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 陰 carries the native reading かげ here. There is no compound to divide. The word links physical shade with being hidden from direct view; that connection is a meaning aid, not a claim about the character's historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[影](影.md), also かげ, can name a cast shadow or silhouette. Compare sitting in 木の陰 with seeing a person's 影 on the ground. 日なた (ひなた), "a sunny spot," contrasts with shade, but not with every figurative use.

## Distinctive meaning

陰で支える (かげでささえる) means "support behind the scenes." 陰で悪口を言う (かげでわるくちをいう) means "speak ill of someone behind their back." Being out of sight can therefore be helpful or unkind; the noun does not decide the evaluation by itself.
