# 学年 (がくねん) - school year; grade

## Meaning

学年 can name an academic year as a period, or a student's year or grade within a school. 同じ学年 (おなじがくねん) means the same school year or grade.

- 学年の終わりに試験があります。 (がくねんのおわりにしけんがあります。) - There is an examination at the end of the school year.
- 彼女は私と同じ学年です。 (かのじょはわたしとおなじがくねんです。) - She is in the same school year as me.

For a numbered student year, forms such as 三年生 (さんねんせい), a third-year student, identify the learner's year. The school context determines what that year corresponds to.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) specifies schooling or study, and [年](../kanji/年.md) a year. This modifier-plus-head compound narrows the year to an academic setting. The on readings are ガク + ネン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[学期](学期.md) (がっき) is a term or semester, not a whole academic year. [クラス](クラス.md) can identify a class group within a year; students in one 学年 need not all share one classroom. 年度 (ねんど) can also name an academic year, and has fiscal-year uses outside schooling.

## Distinctive meaning

English "grade" can mean an assessed mark, but 学年 does not mean a test score. [成績](成績.md) (せいせき) concerns assessed performance. Nor does 学年 itself establish a universal starting month or an identical grade system in every country.
