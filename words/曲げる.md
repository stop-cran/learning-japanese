# 曲げる (まげる) - bend; tilt; distort

## Meaning

曲げる means bend or curve something, or incline part of the body or another object. It is a transitive ichidan verb: the thing bent takes を.

- 針金を曲げました。 (はりがねをまげました。) - I bent the wire.
- 首を少し曲げてください。 (くびをすこしまげてください。) - Please tilt your head a little.
- 事実を曲げてはいけません。 (じじつをまげてはいけません。) - You must not distort the facts.

The figurative uses include distorting the truth and yielding one's principles: 信念を曲げない (しんねんをまげない), "not compromise one's convictions."

## How the kanji combine

[曲](../kanji/曲.md) contributes bending or curvature; げる completes the native verb. Its forms include 曲げます (まげます), 曲げて (まげて), and 曲げた (まげた).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[曲がる](曲る.md) (まがる) is the intransitive counterpart: 針金が曲がった, "the wire bent," versus 針金を曲げた, "I bent the wire." The linked article retains its stable written ID 曲る.

[折る](折る.md) (おる) means fold or break, while [傾く](傾く.md) (かたむく) describes tilting or inclining. These are useful overlapping comparisons, not a rule that every bend makes a crease or break.

## Distinctive meaning

枉げる is a rare spelling associated with figurative distortion or forcing a change of principle. The pinned dictionary restricts the physical bend/tilt senses to 曲げる; do not offer 枉げる as a free everyday spelling for bending wire.

The dictionary also records a pawn sense. It is additional recognition, not the meaning of the wire and head examples above.
