# 中学校 (ちゅうがっこう)

## Meaning

中学校 means junior high school or lower secondary school. In Japan it is the three-year school stage after elementary school.

- 弟は来年、中学校に入ります。 (おとうとは らいねん、ちゅうがっこうに はいります。) — My younger brother will enter junior high school next year.

中学校に通う (ちゅうがっこうにかよう) means to attend junior high school. に marks the school attended.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) means middle. [学](../kanji/学.md) and [校](../kanji/校.md) form 学校 (がっこう), "school." Learn ちゅう + がっこう, with the small っ in the second part.

## Synonyms and antonyms

中学 (ちゅうがく) is a common shortened form. 小学校 (しょうがっこう) precedes this stage, and 高校 (こうこう) follows it. 中学生 (ちゅうがくせい) is the student, not the institution.

## Distinctive meaning

"Middle school" is a useful translation, but school stages vary across countries. The Japanese word names a particular educational level; it does not simply mean a school located between two other schools.
