# 一 (いち) — one

## Meaning

一 is the number one, read いち when reciting numbers. In ordinary writing the Arabic numeral 1 is also common.

一、二、三と数えます。 (いち、に、さんとかぞえます。) — I count one, two, three.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) uses its on reading イチ here. This is the numeral itself, not a guarantee that 一 is pronounced いち in every compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一つ (ひとつ) counts one thing in the native general counting series. 一人 (ひとり) counts one person. 二 (に), "two," is the next number, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese usually needs a counter when specifying a quantity of objects. Some combinations change the pronunciation: 一個 (いっこ), "one piece," and 一分 (いっぷん), "one minute." Learn these as complete number-plus-counter forms rather than attaching an unchanged いち everywhere.
