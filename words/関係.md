# 関係 (かんけい) — relationship; connection

## Meaning

関係 refers to a relationship or connection between people, things, or facts.
AとBの関係 means "the relationship between A and B."
With する, it can mean to be related to something: use 〜に関係する.

- 睡眠と健康の関係を調べています。 (すいみんとけんこうのかんけいをしらべています。) — I am studying the relationship between sleep and health.
- その問題は私の仕事に関係しています。 (そのもんだいはわたしのしごとにかんけいしています。) — That issue is related to my work.

The noun also appears in 関係がある (かんけいがある), "have a connection," and 関係がない (かんけいがない), "have no connection."

## How the kanji combine

[関](../kanji/関.md) contributes involvement or relation, while [係](../kanji/係.md) contributes connection.
The related meanings reinforce one another in the compound.
The on readings are カン + ケイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

関連 (かんれん) also expresses a connection or relevance, especially useful when considering related topics or facts.
It can overlap with 関係; the dictionary gloss alone does not make every phrase interchangeable.
無関係 (むかんけい), "unrelated," provides a contrast: 無関係な話 (むかんけいなはなし), "an unrelated topic."

## Distinctive meaning

関係 is not restricted to personal relationships.
The sleep example asks how two factors are connected; it does not describe friendship.
When translating "relationship," first identify the two sides being related rather than assuming the word always concerns people.
