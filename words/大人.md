# 大人 (おとな) — adult

## Meaning

大人 is a grown-up: 大人になる (become an adult), 大人料金 おとなりょうきん (adult fare), 大人っぽい (mature-looking), 大人げない おとなげない (childish, "not adult-like").

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) (big) plus [人](../kanji/人.md) (person): "big person". The reading おとな is not the sum of おお + ひと: it is a **jukujikun**, a whole-word reading assigned
to the kanji pair. (There is also a different reading たいじん, a person of great stature or a respectful "gentleman", which is rare today.)

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 成人 せいじん (a legal adult; in Japan the age of majority has been 18 since 2022), 大人 is general usage.
- Antonym: 子供 こども／子ども (child), 未成年 みせいねん (minor, legal).
- Related spelling: 大人しい おとなしい is a different adjective, "quiet, docile". It is written with the same kanji but isn't a form of 大人.

## Distinctive meaning

大人 is a social and psychological notion ("mature") as well as an age. 成人 is a legal term, used in 成人式 せいじんしき (coming-of-age ceremony). In ticket prices, 大人 contrasts
with 子供 and 小人 しょうにん (child fare category).
