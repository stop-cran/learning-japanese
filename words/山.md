# 山 (やま) — mountain

## Meaning

山 means a mountain or hill. It can name an individual landform or refer more generally to mountainous country.

- 遠くに山が見えます。  
  とおくにやまがみえます。  
  "I can see a mountain in the distance."
- 日曜日に山に登りました。  
  にちようびにやまにのぼりました。  
  "I climbed a mountain on Sunday."

## How the kanji combine

[山](../kanji/山.md) is one kanji with the native reading やま. Mountain names do not always use this reading: 富士山 is ふじさん, using the on reading さん. Learn a mountain name's full reading separately rather than guessing from the standalone noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

丘 (おか), "hill", often suggests a smaller rise. 谷 (たに), "valley", is a useful landform contrast, not a universal antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

山 also appears figuratively for a heap: 本の山 (ほんのやま), "a pile of books". The basic mountain image helps with this use, but does not mean every occurrence names an actual mountain.
