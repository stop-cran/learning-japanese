# お見舞い (おみまい) — get-well visit; sympathy

## Meaning

お見舞い is a visit or expression of concern for someone affected by illness or misfortune. It can also mean a get-well message or a gift expressing that concern.

入院している友達のお見舞いに行きます。 (にゅういんしているともだちのおみまいにいきます。) — I am going to visit a friend who is in hospital.

## How the kanji combine

お is a polite prefix. [見](../kanji/見.md) and [舞](../kanji/舞.md) occur in 見舞う (みまう), "visit someone in distress"; 見舞い is its noun form. Do not interpret the compound as a literal act of "seeing a dance."

## Synonyms and antonyms

訪問 (ほうもん) is a visit in general, without the get-well or sympathy meaning. お祝い (おいわい) concerns celebration and congratulations instead.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn お見舞いに行く, "go on a get-well visit": に marks the purpose. お見舞いの手紙 (おみまいのてがみ) is a letter of sympathy or concern. The English gloss "inquiry" here means asking after someone's welfare, not an investigation or any general question.
