# 心当たり (こころあたり) - idea; inkling; possible lead

## Meaning

心当たり is having something or someone in mind that may answer
a question: an idea, inkling, or possible lead based on what one knows.
Use 心当たりがある, "have an idea/lead," and 心当たりがない,
"have no idea."

- 鍵をなくした場所に心当たりはありますか。
  (かぎをなくしたばしょにこころあたりはありますか。) - Do you have any idea where you lost your keys?
- その仕事ができる人に心当たりがあります。
  (そのしごとができるひとにこころあたりがあります。) - I know of someone who may be able to do that job.

に marks the matter or person about which one has a possible lead.
The second example does not claim that the person has already
agreed to do the work.

## How the kanji combine

[心](../kanji/心.md) contributes mind, and [当](../kanji/当.md)たり
connects with hitting on something. As a memory aid, picture a
possible answer coming to mind. This is an association, not a
historical derivation. The readings are native: こころ + あたり.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[見当](見当.md) (けんとう) overlaps in having an idea, including
an estimate. [知る](知る.md) (しる) is the broader verb "know";
[知識](知識.md) (ちしき) names knowledge.
[考え](考え.md) (かんがえ) and [アイデア](アイデア.md) cover
ideas more generally, including plans and inventive suggestions.

## Distinctive meaning

心当たり commonly points toward a possible person, place, cause,
or explanation, rather than a newly invented design.
It can be tentative: a lead is not proof.
Learn the noun with がある; do not make 心当たりする the ordinary
verb for knowing or remembering something.
