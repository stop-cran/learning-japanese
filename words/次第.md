# 次第 (しだい) - depending on; as soon as; circumstances

## Meaning

次第 has several constructions. **N次第だ** means "depends on N." **Verb's ます-stem + 次第** means "as soon as that happens"; remove ます from the polite form.

- 行くかどうかは天気次第です。 (いくかどうかはてんきしだいです。) - Whether we go depends on the weather.
- 分かり次第、連絡します。 (わかりしだい、れんらくします。) - I will contact you as soon as I find out.

The immediate-sequence use can also follow an event noun: 到着次第 (とうちゃくしだい), "upon arrival." It is not a free adverb meaning "immediately" that can be placed anywhere.

As a noun, 次第 can mean an order or program, as in 式次第 (しきしだい), "order of a ceremony," or the circumstances: 事の次第を説明する (ことのしだいをせつめいする), "explain how things happened."

## How the kanji combine

[次](../kanji/次.md) contributes succession or what comes next; 第 contributes order or rank. These associations help with the sequence senses, but each grammatical construction needs learning separately. The readings are シ + ダイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[順番](順番.md) (じゅんばん) overlaps with order, and [事情](事情.md) (じじょう) with circumstances. Neither replaces the suffix in 分かり次第.

## Distinctive meaning

天気次第 expresses a condition; 到着次第 expresses the trigger for the next action. Distinguish them through meaning and attachment, rather than assuming every noun + 次第 means "depending on." 次第に (しだいに), "gradually," is another expression, not the immediate-sequence construction.
