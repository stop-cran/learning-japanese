# びくびく — fearful; trembling nervously

## Meaning

びくびく describes being fearful or timid, often with a sense of shrinking back or trembling in expectation of something frightening.

- 大きな犬が近づくと、子猫はびくびくしていた。 — The kitten was trembling fearfully as a large dog approached. (子猫: こねこ.)
- 叱られるのではないかと、びくびくしながら先生を待った。 — I waited nervously for the teacher, afraid I might be scolded. (叱る: しかる.)

Use びくびくする or びくびくしている. Before another verb, びくびくと or びくびくしながら can describe the fearful manner.

## How the kanji combine

The repeated kana form depicts fear or trembling, not necessarily any noise. It is a native mimetic, not a kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[おどおど](おどおど.md) particularly describes visibly timid, hesitant behavior. [はらはら](はらはら.md) can describe an observer's suspense, without the same cowering quality. [ぴんぴん](ぴんぴん.md) concerns vitality, not courage, so it is not a direct opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

びくびく suggests apprehension that persists or repeatedly returns; it is not simply a single startled jump. It can sound critical when applied to another person's timidity. Use it carefully rather than as a neutral diagnosis of anxiety.
