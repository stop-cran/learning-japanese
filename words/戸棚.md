# 戸棚 (とだな) - cupboard; cabinet

## Meaning

戸棚 is an enclosed storage unit with shelves and doors: a cupboard or cabinet.
It can hold dishes, food, clothing, or other objects; the word does not restrict the contents.

- 食器を戸棚にしまいました。 (しょっきをとだなにしまいました。) - I put the dishes away in the cupboard.
- 戸棚の中に何が入っていますか。 (とだなのなかになにがはいっていますか。) - What is inside the cabinet?

に marks the storage destination or location.
戸棚の中 (とだなのなか) is the cabinet's interior.

## How the kanji combine

[戸](../kanji/戸.md), door, qualifies 棚 (たな), shelving:
shelves enclosed behind doors.
The second element is voiced to だな, giving と + だな.
This is a native compound, not the on-reading sequence コ + ホウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[棚](棚.md) (たな) is shelving more generally, including open shelves.
[押し入れ](押し入れ.md) (おしいれ) is a built-in closet,
especially familiar for storing bedding.
These storage meanings overlap without naming exactly the same fixture in every room.

## Distinctive meaning

A dictionary may translate 戸棚 as cupboard, cabinet, locker, closet, or wardrobe.
Choose the English term from the actual furniture and its use;
those glosses do not mean that every shelf or every storage room is a 戸棚.
Doors and enclosed shelving are the useful identifying arrangement.
