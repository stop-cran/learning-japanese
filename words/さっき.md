# さっき (さっき) — a moment ago

## Meaning

さっき means a short while ago or just now. It points to the recent past rather than an exact number of minutes.

さっき電話しました。（さっきでんわしました。） — I called a little while ago.

## How the kanji combine

This word is normally written in kana; 先 is a rare spelling for this reading. The small っ is part of its pronunciation; do not replace さっき with さき, which is a different form with wider meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

先ほど（さきほど） is a more polite alternative. 今（いま） means "now"; 後で（あとで） means "later," providing a useful time contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Use さっきの before a noun: さっきの話（さっきのはなし）, "the conversation from a little while ago." Unlike English "some time ago," さっき normally stays within a recently remembered situation, not a distant past event.
