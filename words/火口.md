# 火口 (かこう) - crater; boiler burner

## Meaning

火口, read かこう here, is a volcanic crater: an opening from which volcanic material or gas can emerge. JMdict also records a boiler-burner sense at this reading; that is a separate application, not a claim that a crater is a manufactured burner.

- 火口から白い煙が出ています。 (かこうからしろいけむりがでています。) - White smoke is coming from the crater.
- 火口には近づかないでください。 (かこうにはちかづかないでください。) - Please do not approach the crater.

## How the kanji combine

[火](../kanji/火.md) contributes fire, and [口](../kanji/口.md) an opening or mouth. The combination suggests an opening associated with fire. The on readings are カ + コウ, giving かこう. This meaning aid does not identify the exact gas or material in any particular eruption.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[火山](火山.md) (かざん) is the volcano, not just its crater. 噴火 (ふんか) is an eruption, an event rather than the opening. [穴](穴.md) (あな), hole or opening, is broader.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not transfer every meaning of the spelling 火口 to かこう. The separate reading ひぐち has burner/nozzle and origin-of-a-fire meanings; ほくち means tinder. 火元 (ひもと) is another word for a fire's origin. These distinctions do not change this card's primary reading.
