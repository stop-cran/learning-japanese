# 敬意 (けいい) - respect; regard

## Meaning

敬意 is a feeling or expression of respect. It is a noun, used with verbs such as **敬意を払う** (けいいをはらう), "pay respect," and 敬意を示す (けいいをしめす), "show respect." The recipient is marked with に.

- 先生に敬意を払っています。 (せんせいにけいいをはらっています。) - I treat my teacher with respect.
- 彼女の努力に敬意を示しました。 (かのじょのどりょくにけいいをしめしました。) - We showed respect for her efforts.

敬意を込めて (けいいをこめて) means "with respect," emphasizing the feeling put into an action or expression.

## How the kanji combine

敬 contributes respecting or honoring, while [意](../kanji/意.md) contributes feeling or intention. 敬 specifies the kind of feeling: respectful regard. The readings are ケイ + イ; retain the final い in けいい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[尊敬](尊敬.md) (そんけい) also means respect or admiration and forms the verb 尊敬する. 敬意 is useful when naming the respect someone shows or feels. Contempt is a contextual opposite, but disagreement is not: people can disagree while showing respect.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 敬意 together with its supporting verb rather than automatically adding する. 敬意を払う is not a monetary payment: 払う here describes giving the appropriate attention or respect. The target can be a person, their efforts, or an achievement.
