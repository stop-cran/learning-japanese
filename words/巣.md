# 巣 (す) - nest; hive; web

## Meaning

巣 is a noun for an animal's nest or dwelling. The English translation depends on the animal: 鳥の巣 (とりのす) is a bird's nest, 蜂の巣 (はちのす) a bee/wasp nest or hive, and 蜘蛛の巣 (くものす) a spider's web.

- 鳥が木の上に巣を作りました。 (とりがきのうえにすをつくりました。) - A bird built a nest in the tree.
- 窓の隅に蜘蛛の巣があります。 (まどのすみにくものすがあります。) - There is a spider's web in the corner of the window.

巣を作る means "build a nest"; 巣に戻る (すにもどる) means "return to the nest."

## How the kanji combine

巣 is one character read with the native reading す. There is no compound to divide. In 鳥の巣, の identifies the creature associated with the dwelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[家](家.md) (いえ) is a house or home and overlaps only as a broad comparison for a dwelling. It does not replace 巣 in the ordinary expression 蜘蛛の巣. A nest has no standard opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not reduce 巣 to "bird's nest." It covers different animal structures and can also mean a den or haunt. Conversely, the word does not identify whether a nest is occupied: that requires context. The animal modifier is particularly helpful when English demands a specific word such as "hive" or "web."
