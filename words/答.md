# 答 (こたえ) - answer; response

## Meaning

答 means an answer or response to a question. It also means the solution to a problem. In ordinary sentences, the spelling 答え is a useful default.

質問の答えが分かりません。 (しつもんのこたえがわかりません。) - I do not know the answer to the question.

答えをここに書いてください。 (こたえをここにかいてください。) - Please write the answer here.

## How the kanji combine

[答](../kanji/答.md) writes the native word こたえ. The headword 答 omits the okurigana found in 答え; both represent the same noun. It is related to 答える (こたえる), "answer."

## Synonyms and antonyms

返事 (へんじ) is a reply, often to a call, message, or request. 解答 (かいとう) is an answer or solution, especially to a test question. 質問 (しつもん), "question," is the corresponding other side of an exchange.

## Distinctive meaning

答え can be correct or incorrect; the noun itself does not guarantee correctness. 正しい答え (ただしいこたえ) means "the correct answer." Use noun grammar for 答え and verb grammar for 答える.
