# なぐる - hit; punch

## Meaning

なぐる means to hit, punch or beat, typically with a fist or an implement.
It describes forceful striking, not every light tap or accidental contact.
The person or thing struck takes を.

- 彼は壁をこぶしで殴りました。 (かれはかべをこぶしでなぐりました。) - He punched the wall with his fist.
- 人を殴ってはいけません。 (ひとをなぐってはいけません。) - You must not hit people.

It is a transitive godan verb despite ending in る:
なぐります, なぐらない, なぐって and なぐった.
で can mark what delivers the blow.

## How the kanji combine

殴る is the principal dictionary spelling. The source row supplies kana,
retained as this article's identity; examples also teach the written form.
擲る and 撲る are marked rare spellings of this word. They do not introduce
different conjugations or justify transferring every reading of those
characters to なぐる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[打つ](打つ.md) (うつ, also ぶつ in its separately taught hitting use)
and [叩く](叩く.md) (たたく) overlap in physical striking.
叩く also covers gentle tapping and clapping, so it is not restricted to
the rough blows suggested by なぐる.

## Distinctive meaning

A single punch can be なぐる; repeated blows are not required.
Conversely, English "hit" can describe a ball reaching a target without
anyone punching it. [当たる](当たる.md) (あたる) and
[当てる](当てる.md) (あてる) describe contact from different viewpoints.
Choose the action and particles, not just the English word "hit."
