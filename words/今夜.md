# 今夜 (こんや) - tonight

## Meaning

今夜 means tonight or this evening. It identifies the night associated with the current day and can function directly as a time expression.

今夜は家で映画を見ます。 (こんやはいえでえいがをみます。) - I will watch a film at home tonight.

今夜、電話してもいいですか。 (こんや、でんわしてもいいですか。) - May I call you tonight?

Unlike an exact clock time, 今夜 normally needs no に to say when an action happens.

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md), "now; this," combines with [夜](../kanji/夜.md), "night." Their on readings コン and ヤ form こんや.

## Synonyms and antonyms

今晩 (こんばん) also means this evening or tonight. 今日 (きょう), "today," covers the day more broadly. 昨夜 (さくや), "last night," refers to the previous night.

## Distinctive meaning

今夜 and 今晩 overlap considerably; they do not mark a universal fixed boundary on the clock. Before another noun, use の: 今夜の予定 (こんやのよてい), "tonight's plans." For the general noun "night," use 夜 (よる), not 今夜.
