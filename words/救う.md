# 救う (すくう) - rescue; save

## Meaning

救う is a transitive godan verb meaning to rescue someone, save something,
or help someone out of difficulty. Use **person を danger/difficulty から救う**.
Its て-form is 救って and its past is 救った.

- 消防士が火事から子供を救いました。 (しょうぼうしがかじからこどもをすくいました。) - A firefighter rescued a child from the fire.
- 友達の言葉に救われました。 (ともだちのことばにすくわれました。) - My friend's words gave me much-needed relief.

The second example is passive, 救われる: the speaker receives help or relief.
It need not describe a physical rescue.

## How the kanji combine

救 writes the native stem すく-, followed by the ending う.
The whole verb means saving or rescuing. There is no multi-kanji relationship
to split, and the identical kana すくう in other words does not establish
the same meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[助ける](助ける.md) (たすける) overlaps with rescuing and also covers
helping with a task. 救う highlights release from danger or hardship.
This is a practical tendency, not a claim that emotional help must use one
word and physical help the other. Causing harm is a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

English "save" covers unrelated actions such as saving a file or saving money.
Do not transfer all of them to 救う. Its core here is rescue or relief.
命を救う (いのちをすくう), "save a life," makes that core explicit, while
the friend's words example shows a natural figurative extension.
