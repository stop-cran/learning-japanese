# 不潔 (ふけつ) - unclean; unsanitary

## Meaning

不潔 describes being dirty, unclean, or unsanitary.
It is a noun and na-adjective: 不潔な服 (ふけつなふく),
unclean clothes.
It also has an indecent or morally dirty sense.

- 不潔な手で食べ物に触らないでください。 (ふけつなてでたべものにさわらないでください。) - Please do not touch food with unclean hands.
- 彼の不潔な話を聞きたくありません。 (かれのふけつなはなしをききたくありません。) - I do not want to listen to his indecent stories.

The second judges the content, not whether the sound or paper
has literal dirt on it.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) negates 潔, associated with cleanliness
or purity. The compound concerns a lack of cleanliness or purity.
The on readings here are フ + ケツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[汚い](汚い.md) (きたない), dirty or messy, overlaps.
[下品](下品.md) (げひん), vulgar or indecent, overlaps with
the evaluative sense, not every hygiene description.
[清潔](清潔.md) (せいけつ), clean or hygienic, is the useful
opposite in cleanliness contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer an exact medical risk from the word alone.
It can criticize hygiene, appearance, or moral/sexual content,
depending on the object. Calling a person 不潔 can be a strong
negative judgment; it is not a neutral description of any erotic
subject or a diagnosis of illness.
