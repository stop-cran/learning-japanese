# 算盤 (そろばん) - abacus

## Meaning

算盤 is an abacus, a calculating tool with movable beads. Kana そろばん is
usual. It is a noun: そろばんを使う (そろばんをつかう), "use an abacus."

- 子供のころ、そろばんを習いました。 (こどものころ、そろばんをならいました。) - I learned to use an abacus as a child.
- この値段ではそろばんが合いません。 (このねだんではそろばんがあいません。) - At this price, it does not pay.

The second example uses the figurative profit-and-loss reckoning sense.
そろばんが合う concerns whether the figures work out profitably; it does
not mean that two physical abacuses match.

## How the kanji combine

[算](../kanji/算.md) supplies the calculation association, and 盤 suggests
a board or tray. Learn 算盤/そろばん as an established whole-word pair,
not as a general rule that 算 can be read そろ in unfamiliar compounds.

The collection's `jukujikun` study label flags that whole-word learning
strategy; it is not a claim about the word's historical origin.
[Kanjipedia](https://www.kanjipedia.jp/kotoba/0002667000) places 算盤 in
angle brackets. Its [notation guide](https://www.kanjipedia.jp/include/popup_kotoba.html)
groups 熟字訓 and 当て字 under that marker, so the brackets alone do not
decide between those historical analyses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計算](計算.md) (けいさん) names calculation rather than this particular
tool. 電卓 (でんたく) is an electronic calculator.
[勘定](勘定.md) (かんじょう) overlaps with reckoning and accounts.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records rare 十露盤 and 珠盤; 十露盤 is marked ateji.
These are recognition spellings, not reasons to replace ordinary そろばん.
The katakana form ソロバン is separately indexed as kana-only; it does not
assign a new character-by-character reading to the kanji spellings.
