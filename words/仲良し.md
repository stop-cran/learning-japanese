# 仲良し (なかよし) - close friendship; close friend

## Meaning

仲良し names a close, friendly relationship or a good friend. It is a noun,
not an i-adjective: 二人は仲良しです means the two get along well.

- 私たちは子供のころから仲良しです。 (わたしたちはこどものころからなかよしです。) - We have been good friends since childhood.
- 彼女は私の仲良しです。 (かのじょはわたしのなかよしです。) - She is a close friend of mine.

人と仲良し means being on friendly terms with someone. Before another noun,
仲良しの is useful: 仲良しの友達 (なかよしのともだち), "a close friend."

## How the kanji combine

[仲](../kanji/仲.md) contributes relations between people, and
[良](../kanji/良.md) contributes goodness. The native elements なか + よし
suggest being on good terms. 仲よし is another spelling; 仲好し is marked
rare in the dictionary. Do not make an adjective 仲良しい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[親友](親友.md) (しんゆう) also names a close friend.
[親しい](親しい.md) (したしい) describes closeness with different grammar.
[仲](仲.md) can describe good or bad relations; 仲良し already supplies
the positive relationship.

## Distinctive meaning

仲良し does not require romance, exclusivity or a preceding quarrel.
[仲直り](仲直り.md) (なかなおり) specifically concerns making up after
a falling-out. This article teaches friendship, not the dictionary's
separate euphemistic intimate-relations sense.
