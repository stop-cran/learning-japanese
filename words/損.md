# 損 (そん) - loss; disadvantage

## Meaning

損 is a loss or disadvantage. 損をする and 損する mean to lose out or suffer a loss. Before a noun, 損な can describe something disadvantageous or not worthwhile.

- この値段で売ると損をします。 (このねだんでうるとそんをします。) - We will make a loss if we sell at this price.
- 安いと思って買いましたが、すぐ壊れて損をしました。 (やすいとおもってかいましたが、すぐこわれてそんをしました。) - I bought it thinking it was cheap, but it broke immediately and I lost out.

損な役回り (そんなやくまわり) is an unrewarding or disadvantageous role. The cost need not be a precise sum of money.

## How the kanji combine

損 is a single character read with the on reading ソン. It supplies the whole loss or disadvantage meaning; there is no multi-kanji relationship to explain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

損失 (そんしつ) also names a loss. [利益](利益.md) (りえき) is profit or benefit. 得 (とく), "gain; advantage," contrasts directly in 損か得か (そんかとくか), "whether it is a loss or a gain."

## Distinctive meaning

損 describes an unfavorable outcome or position. It does not automatically mean that an object has disappeared or that a competition was lost. Ask what benefit, money, or opportunity the person loses out on.
