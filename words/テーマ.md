# テーマ - theme; topic; subject

## Meaning

テーマ is a theme, topic, or subject: what a discussion, piece of writing, work of art, or investigation concerns. Use Nをテーマにする for making N the subject.

- 今日の授業のテーマは環境問題です。
  (きょうのじゅぎょうのテーマはかんきょうもんだいです。) - Today's lesson is about environmental issues.
- 家族をテーマにした映画を見ました。
  (かぞくをテーマにしたえいがをみました。) - I watched a film about family.

研究のテーマを決める (けんきゅうのテーマをきめる) means "choose a research topic." In music, テーマ can identify a theme or recurring central musical idea.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word comes from German Thema. It has no written kanji components. Keep the long vowel in テー; the study reading is テーマ, not テマ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[話題](話題.md) (わだい) overlaps with a topic of discussion. [題](題.md) (だい) can name a title or assigned topic. 主題 (しゅだい) is a theme or main subject.

## Distinctive meaning

A テーマ is not necessarily the work's title. Two films can have different titles but share the theme of family. English "subject" also covers school subjects and grammatical subjects; do not extend the loanword automatically to every English use.
