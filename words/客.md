# 客 (きゃく) — guest; customer

## Meaning

客 names a guest or visitor. It also covers customers and people using a service, depending on the setting.
A guest at home and a customer at a shop can therefore both be 客.

- 今日は客が来ます。 (きょうはきゃくがきます。) — Guests are coming today.
- この店は客が多いです。 (このみせはきゃくがおおいです。) — This shop has many customers.

The noun does not require an English-style plural ending; context and expressions such as 多い supply the number.

## How the kanji combine

There is one kanji, [客](../kanji/客.md), pronounced with its on reading キャク.
This single-character Sino-Japanese noun names a visitor or customer role.
The character also has the reading カク in other words; that does not change this word's reading きゃく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お客さん (おきゃくさん) is an honorific form for a guest or customer: it adds respectful treatment rather than a new type of visitor.
In a shop, [店員](店員.md) (てんいん), "shop employee," is a useful contrasting role.
The employee and customer labels describe what people are doing in that interaction, not opposite kinds of people.

## Distinctive meaning

Translate 客 according to the setting. "Customer" suits the shop example; "guest" suits someone being received at home.
The word alone does not tell you whether money changes hands.
It can also occur in words for audiences and passengers, so do not restrict the character to shopping.
