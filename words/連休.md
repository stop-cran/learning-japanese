# 連休 (れんきゅう) — consecutive days off

## Meaning

連休 is a run of consecutive days off. The important feature is that the days form one unbroken stretch,
not that each day is a national holiday. 三連休 (さんれんきゅう) is three days off in a row.

- 来週は三連休です。 (らいしゅうはさんれんきゅうです。) — Next week I have three consecutive days off.
- 連休に旅行に行きます。 (れんきゅうにりょこうにいきます。) — I am going on a trip during the consecutive days off.

## How the kanji combine

[連](../kanji/連.md), "connected; in succession," qualifies [休](../kanji/休.md), "rest; time off."
The on readings レン + キュウ describe days of rest linked together.

## Synonyms and antonyms

休み (やすみ) can mean rest, a break, or time off without specifying consecutive days.
[休日](休日.md) (きゅうじつ) is a holiday or day off; that word alone does not say there are several in succession.

## Distinctive meaning

Two days off separated by a workday do not form one 連休.
When arranging plans, 連休 tells the listener about the continuous block of free days, not what you will do with it.
