# 怪しい (あやしい) — suspicious; doubtful

## Meaning

怪しい describes something suspicious, doubtful, unreliable, or strangely unexplained. What is
questionable depends on context; the word does not prove that someone has done anything wrong.

- あの話は少し怪しいです。 (あの はなしは すこし あやしいです。) — That story sounds a little fishy.
- 彼が来るかどうか怪しいです。 (かれが くるかどうか あやしいです。) — It is doubtful whether he will come.

It is an i-adjective: 怪しくない (あやしくない), "not suspicious," and 怪しかった
(あやしかった), "was suspicious."

## How the kanji combine

[怪](../kanji/怪.md) supplies あや; しい completes the written adjective. The kanji carries an
association with suspicion and uncanny things, but not every use is supernatural.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[疑わしい](疑わしい.md) focuses on doubts about truth or reliability. [いかがわしい](いかがわしい.md)
can suggest disreputability or indecency. [変](変.md) describes something odd without necessarily
implying suspicion. These meanings overlap rather than forming exclusive categories.

There is no single antonym for all uses: reliable information, a certain outcome, and an ordinary
sound each provide a different contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

怪しい is the ordinary spelling of あやしい. [妖しい](妖しい.md) foregrounds mysterious allure;
[奇しい](奇しい.md) is a rare spelling to recognize, not a default substitute.
Neither the spelling nor the English gloss determines the meaning independently of context.

For the distinctions among these spellings, 疑わしい, いかがわしい, and 珍しい, see
[strange, suspicious, and rare](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
