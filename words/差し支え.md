# 差し支え (さしつかえ) - hindrance; impediment

## Meaning

差し支え is a hindrance or impediment: something that makes a proposed action inconvenient or problematic.
It is a noun. 差し支えがある means there is a problem or impediment;
差し支えがない means there is none.

- 明日の仕事に差し支えはありません。 (あしたのしごとにさしつかえはありません。) - It will not interfere with tomorrow's work.
- 差し支えがなければ、お名前を教えてください。 (さしつかえがなければ、おなまえをおしえてください。) - If you do not mind, please tell me your name.

In the request, the condition leaves room for the person to decline.

## How the kanji combine

[差](../kanji/差.md) and [支](../kanji/支.md) occur in the native verb 差し支える (さしつかえる), "hinder; interfere."
差し支え is its noun form, read さし + つかえ rather than サ + シ.
Do not substitute the different 支える reading ささえる into this word.
差支え is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[じゃま](じゃま.md) means hindrance or interference, and [障害](障害.md) (しょうがい) includes an obstacle.
[問題](問題.md) (もんだい) is broader: a problem or question.
差し支えがない is a useful negative expression, not a new noun meaning permission.

## Distinctive meaning

差し支えなければ is a shorter equivalent of the conditional in the example.
It softens a request; it does not guarantee that answering will have no consequences.
Likewise, 差し支えありません says there is no objection or practical problem in context, not that every possible action is authorized.
