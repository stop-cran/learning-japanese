# 来 (らい) - coming; since; for a period

## Meaning

来, read **らい**, is taught here as an affix, not a freely standing word for every English "next." As a prefix, it means "next; coming" in time expressions. As a suffix, it can mean "since" or "for a period extending to the reference time."

- 来年、日本へ行きます。 (らいねん、にほんへいきます。) - I will go to Japan next year.
- 彼とは十年来の友人です。 (かれとはじゅうねんらいのゆうじんです。) - He and I have been friends for ten years.

The first 来 precedes the time unit. In 十年来, it follows a duration; の connects that expression to 友人.

## How the kanji combine

[来](../kanji/来.md) contributes coming. Its position in the larger word matters: 来年 looks toward the coming year, while 十年来 looks back over a continuing span. Both use らい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[来年](来年.md), [来月](来月.md) (らいげつ), and [来週](来週.md) (らいしゅう) provide useful prefix patterns. [以来](以来.md) (いらい), "since," is a related complete expression; it is not identical to a freely attachable English preposition.

## Distinctive meaning

The suffix use does not imply every situation involving "for ten years." 十年来の友人 connects the friendship to a span continuing to the relevant time. A trip planned to last ten years needs its own duration construction; attaching 来 mechanically would change the viewpoint.
