# ライター (ライター) - lighter; writer

## Meaning

ライター represents two separate words with the same Japanese spelling and pronunciation.

As **lighter**, it names a device for producing a flame:

- 引き出しにライターがありました。 (ひきだしにライターがありました。) - There was a lighter in the drawer.

As **writer**, it names a person who writes:

- 姉は雑誌のライターです。 (あねはざっしのライターです。) - My older sister is a magazine writer.

The first sentence makes ライター a physical object; the second identifies an occupation. Neither meaning should be listed as a sense of the other's dictionary ID.

## How the kanji combine

Both words use katakana and end in the long sound ター. There is no kanji decomposition. The shared spelling reflects how the words are represented in Japanese; it does not make the device and occupation one lexical identity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[作家](作家.md) (さっか) can identify an author or creative writer. [記者](記者.md) (きしゃ) identifies a reporter or journalist. Their work can overlap with that of a ライター, but the occupational labels do not all have the same scope.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse ライター with **ライダー**, a rider or biker. ダ has voicing marks; タ does not. The community source mixed "lighter, rider, writer," but the rider word has a different pronunciation. Here the two ライター identities are taught separately, and ライダー is a spelling contrast rather than an added N3 assignment.
