# 十日 (とおか) — the 10th of the month; ten days

## Meaning

十日 is a date or a duration: 十月十日 じゅうがつとおか (October 10th), 十日間 とおかかん (ten days), 十日目 とおかめ (the tenth day).

## How the kanji combine

[十](../kanji/十.md) read とお plus the day counter か. とお is the kun reading of 十 and the same word as in 十 とお (ten things). See [三日](三日.md) for the series.
It is not じゅうにち. The 20th, 二十日 はつか, is also special.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: 十 とお (ten things, no つ).
- Contrast: [十月](十月.md) じゅうがつ (October).

## Distinctive meaning

The 14th and 24th are じゅうよっか and にじゅうよっか, and the 20th is はつか.
