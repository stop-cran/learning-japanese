# 煎る (いる) - roast; parch; toast

## Meaning

煎る means to roast, parch, or toast ingredients, as when heating beans
or sesame seeds in a pan. The dictionary also includes boiling down to
remove moisture. It is a transitive godan verb.

- フライパンでごまを煎りました。 (フライパンでごまをいりました。) - I toasted sesame seeds in a frying pan.
- 豆を煎ってから冷まします。 (まめをいってからさまします。) - I let the beans cool after roasting them.

The ingredient takes を. Learn 煎ります (いります), 煎って (いって),
and 煎らない (いらない), not an ichidan-style いない.

## How the kanji combine

煎 has cooking/heating associations and is read い in this verb;
る is the written ending. 炒る (いる) is another dictionary spelling of
the same word. 熬る is marked rare. These are not different readings
or separate verbs merely because the characters differ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[あぶる](あぶる.md) can mean toast or expose something to heat;
[焼く](焼く.md) (やく) covers baking, grilling, roasting, and more.
炒める (いためる) typically describes cooking with a little oil.
Do not infer its reading or conjugation from 炒る (いる).

## Distinctive meaning

Roasting seeds is a useful starting example, not a rule that every
dictionary use must be completely dry. Nor does English "fry" make this
a general term for deep-frying in oil.
The unrelated [いる](いる.md), "exist" for living beings, has a different
meaning and conjugation despite the same dictionary-form pronunciation.
