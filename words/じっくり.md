# じっくり — carefully; thoroughly and without haste

## Meaning

じっくり describes taking enough time to consider, examine, or do something carefully.

- 答えを急がず、資料をじっくり読んだ。
  — Without rushing to an answer, I read the materials carefully.
- 旅行の計画を立てる前に、予算をじっくり検討した。
  — Before making travel plans, I considered the budget thoroughly.

## Synonyms and antonyms

じっくり emphasizes careful attention and depth. ゆっくり more directly emphasizes slow pace or
taking one's time.

## Distinctive meaning

Slow pace is not the whole meaning: じっくり suggests sustained attention to the task or subject.
