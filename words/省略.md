# 省略 (しょうりゃく) - omission; abbreviation

## Meaning

省略 means omission or leaving something out. It also means abbreviation or
abridgment: making an expression or account shorter. 省略する is transitive;
what is omitted or shortened takes を.

- ここでは詳しい説明を省略します。 (ここではくわしいせつめいをしょうりゃくします。) - I will omit the detailed explanation here.
- 長い名前を省略して書きました。 (ながいなまえをしょうりゃくしてかきました。) - I wrote the long name in abbreviated form.

省略した説明 (しょうりゃくしたせつめい) is an explanation that someone
omitted or shortened; the context identifies which.

## How the kanji combine

[省](../kanji/省.md) contributes omitting, and [略](../kanji/略.md)
contributes shortening or omission. The related meanings reinforce leaving
parts out. The on readings are ショウ + リャク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[省く](省く.md) (はぶく) overlaps in omission.
[飛ばす](飛ばす.md) (とばす) can mean skipping a line or question.
[削除](削除.md) (さくじょ) concerns deletion; shortening an account need
not mean deleting a stored file. [略語](略語.md) (りゃくご) names a shortened
word or expression rather than the act of abbreviating it.

## Distinctive meaning

English abbreviation can name the process or its result, so it does not create
a rigid translation boundary between 省略 and 略語. Likewise, 省略 need not
be an accidental oversight or imply that the omitted information was worthless.
In a particular context, an omission may be deliberate and useful.
