# 用事 (ようじ) — errand, something to do

## Meaning

用事 is an errand, engagement, or matter that needs your attention. The common pattern 用事がある means "have something to do."

- 今日は用事があります。 (きょうは ようじが あります。) — I have something to do today.
- 銀行に用事があります。 (ぎんこうに ようじが あります。) — I have some business to attend to at the bank.

"Business" here need not mean business activity or employment; a personal errand is enough.

## How the kanji combine

[用](../kanji/用.md) contributes business or a practical need, and [事](../kanji/事.md) contributes a matter or affair.
Think of 事 as the matter being specified: something requiring attention.
Both readings are on: ヨウ + ジ → ようじ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [仕事](仕事.md) (しごと) means work, a job, or a task. 用事 can instead be a personal errand or commitment without being work.
- 暇 (ひま) means free time, or "not busy" in 暇です (ひまです). It contrasts with having commitments, but is not a direct antonym of the noun 用事.

## Distinctive meaning

用事 is useful when the details are not important or you do not want to specify them.
用事がある can explain why someone is unavailable without saying what the commitment is. It does not itself say that the matter is urgent or that the person has a paid job.
