# うっかり — inadvertently; through inattention

## Meaning

Doing or failing to do something because attention slipped, rather than deliberately.

- うっかり財布（さいふ）を家に忘れた。 — I absent-mindedly left my wallet at home.
- うっかりして、降（お）りる駅を通（とお）り過（す）ぎた。 — I wasn't paying attention and went past my station.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぼんやり](ぼんやり.md) and [ぼうっと](ぼうっと.md) can describe an absent-minded state. うっかり more directly frames the resulting act or oversight as inadvertent.

## Distinctive meaning

うっかり忘れる（わすれる）explains a lapse, but does not prove that an excuse is justified. うっかりする means being careless or inattentive; it is not a general expression for every accident beyond someone's control.
