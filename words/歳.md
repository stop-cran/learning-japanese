# 歳 (さい) — years old

## Meaning

歳 is an age counter: it follows a number to say how many years old someone is.
The basic sentence pattern is person + は + number + 歳です.
Treat さい as part of that expression, not as a standalone noun meaning "an age."

- 娘は五歳です。 (むすめはごさいです。) — My daughter is five years old.
- 弟さんは何歳ですか。 (おとうとさんはなんさいですか。) — How old is your younger brother?

何歳 (なんさい) asks "how many years old?"

## How the kanji combine

The single character [歳](../kanji/歳.md) uses the on reading サイ for this counter.
It is therefore classified here with on-reading vocabulary, although the headword contains only one kanji.
The number before it supplies the age; 歳 supplies the unit "years old."

## Synonyms and antonyms

年齢 (ねんれい) is the noun "age." It can name the information being asked for, whereas 歳 is attached to the numeric answer.
年 (とし) can mean either a year or someone's age, depending on context.
Neither is a reason to replace the counter in 五歳 (ごさい).

## Distinctive meaning

Age expressions include special readings. 二十歳 is はたち, "twenty years old"; learn this as a complete expression.
It does not mean that 歳 by itself can be read はたち.
Also distinguish years of age from years on a calendar: the present entry teaches the age-counter use.
