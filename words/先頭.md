# 先頭 (せんとう) - head of a line; front; lead

## Meaning

先頭 is the front or leading position of a line, group, or procession.
It can identify the person or thing at that position.
Use 列の先頭 (れつのせんとう) for the head of a queue.

- 列の先頭に先生がいます。 (れつのせんとうにせんせいがいます。) - The teacher is at the front of the line.
- 彼女が先頭に立って歩きました。 (かのじょがせんとうにたってあるきました。) - She walked at the head of the group.

先頭に立つ can also mean taking the lead in an undertaking.
It need not describe literally standing up.

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md) contributes being ahead, and
[頭](../kanji/頭.md) a head or leading part.
Together they name the head of an ordered group.
The on readings are セン + トウ; keep the long final vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[前](前.md) (まえ) is broader: a position in front or a time before.
[トップ](トップ.md) can mean a top or leading position.
[先端](先端.md) (せんたん) can name a physical tip or a forefront;
it does not automatically identify the first person in a queue.
最後尾 (さいこうび), the very rear, contrasts in an ordered line.

## Distinctive meaning

Being at the head of a queue does not make someone the leader of an
organization or the winner of a competition.
Likewise, 先頭 is not the ordinary name for the physical head on a body.
Its place or leadership sense comes from the surrounding group or activity.
