# 作文 (さくぶん) — composition; essay writing

## Meaning

作文 refers to composing a piece of writing, and to the resulting composition. It is especially useful for a school or language-learning writing task.

- 日本語で作文を書きました。  
  にほんごでさくぶんをかきました。  
  "I wrote a composition in Japanese."
- この作文を読んでください。  
  このさくぶんをよんでください。  
  "Please read this composition."

## How the kanji combine

[作](../kanji/作.md) (make) acts on [文](../kanji/文.md) (writing): "make a written composition", a verb-object relationship. Both readings are on readings, サク + ブン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

文章 ぶんしょう is a broader word for writing or a piece of prose. 作文 often focuses on composing that writing or on a composition as an assignment. There is no natural direct antonym; reading is another activity, not the opposite meaning of 作文.

## Distinctive meaning

作文 is not restricted to a formal argumentative essay. 作文を書いた さくぶんをかいた means "wrote a composition"; 文を書いた ぶんをかいた can mean "wrote a sentence". The reading taught here is さくぶん, not the separate archaic poetry-related さくもん entry.
