# 年間 (ねんかん) - a year; annual period

## Meaning

年間 expresses a period of a year and appears in expressions about annual amounts. With a number, it describes a duration in years.

- この施設には年間十万人が訪れます。 (このしせつにはねんかんじゅうまんにんがおとずれます。) - One hundred thousand people visit this facility each year.
- 日本に三年間住んでいました。 (にほんにさんねんかんすんでいました。) - I lived in Japan for three years.

一年間 (いちねんかん) means "for one year." A duration can stand without に, as 三年間 does above. 年間の費用 (ねんかんのひよう) means the costs for a year.

## How the kanji combine

[年](../kanji/年.md) means year, and [間](../kanji/間.md) contributes an interval or span. Together they emphasize the time covered rather than a date label. The reading is ネン + カン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[年](年.md) (とし) can name a year; 年間 highlights duration or a yearly span. [毎年](毎年.md) (まいとし) means every year and emphasizes recurrence. 一年中 (いちねんじゅう) means throughout the year, a stronger continuity idea than an annual total.

## Distinctive meaning

年間十万人 gives a total over a year; it does not say visitors arrive continuously or in equal monthly numbers. Likewise, 三年間 is a length of time, not "the year three." The exact calendar or accounting period must come from context when it matters.
