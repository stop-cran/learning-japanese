# 放れる (はなれる) - get free; be released

## Meaning

放れる is an intransitive ichidan verb for becoming free of a restraint or being released from what holds something.
The thing released is the subject; から can mark what it becomes free of.

- 犬が鎖から放れた。 (いぬがくさりからはなれた。) - The dog got free of its chain.
- 矢が弦を放れる。 (やがつるをはなれる。) - The arrow leaves the bowstring.

In the second example, を marks what the arrow leaves, not an object acted on.
This departure を does not make the verb transitive.

These attested constructions illustrate release, not a general instruction to spell every departure with 放.
The て-form is 放れて (はなれて).

## How the kanji combine

[放](../kanji/放.md) writes the native verb はなれる.
Here the character matches getting loose or being released.
There is no compound of on-readings to assemble.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[放す](放す.md) (はなす) is transitive: 犬を放す, "release the dog."
[離れる](離れる.md) has the same reading はなれる but more broadly teaches being apart or moving away.
[外れる](外れる.md) (はずれる), [取れる](取れる.md) (とれる) and [抜ける](抜ける.md) (ぬける)
can overlap in detachment, depending on what was holding the object.
[逃げる](逃げる.md) (にげる) focuses on escape; [解放](解放.md) (かいほう) concerns release or liberation.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary keeps this release-focused identity separate from 離れる.
Shared pronunciation does not make either spelling mandatory for every use of the other.
An intransitive dictionary gloss "be released" does not make 放れる a passive conjugation of 放す.
