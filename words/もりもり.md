# もりもり — heartily; full of vigor

## Meaning

Energetic appetite or activity, and strength or enthusiasm welling up.

- 子どもたちは朝ご飯をもりもり食べた。 — The children ate breakfast heartily.
- 応援（おうえん）されて、やる気がもりもり湧（わ）いてきた。 — The encouragement filled me with motivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[むしゃむしゃ](むしゃむしゃ.md) and [ぱくぱく](ぱくぱく.md) overlap in hearty eating. [ばりばり](ばりばり.md) can similarly describe vigorous work, but has additional sharp-sound uses.

## Distinctive meaning

もりもり食べる often gives a positive impression of appetite; it does not mean the food is crunchy. もりもり働く（はたらく）describes energetic work. The surrounding verb supplies the activity.
