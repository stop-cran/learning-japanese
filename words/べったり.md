# べったり — sticking closely or all over

## Meaning

べったり describes something sticking or clinging firmly, or being spread thickly over a surface.

- 子どもは母親にべったりくっついていた。
  — The child was clinging closely to their mother.
- 床に泥がべったりついている。
  — Mud is stuck all over the floor.

## Synonyms and antonyms

べったり emphasizes close contact or broad coverage; it is stronger than a light touch.

## Distinctive meaning

The object and context distinguish affectionate clinging from an unwanted sticky coating.
