# 礼儀 (れいぎ) - manners; etiquette

## Meaning

礼儀 is a noun for manners, courtesy, or etiquette: conduct that shows appropriate consideration or respect in a social setting.

- 子どもに食事の礼儀を教えます。 (こどもにしょくじのれいぎをおしえます。) - I teach children table manners.
- 人の家では礼儀を守りましょう。 (ひとのいえではれいぎをまもりましょう。) - Let's observe good manners in other people's homes.

礼儀を守る means observing etiquette. 礼儀正しい (れいぎただしい) means well-mannered or courteous; use the full expression rather than assuming the noun alone is an い-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[礼](../kanji/礼.md) contributes courtesy or respectful conduct, and 儀 contributes ceremony or form. Together they express the forms of considerate, socially appropriate behavior. The on readings are レイ + ギ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

マナー overlaps with manners or etiquette. [丁寧](丁寧.md) (ていねい) describes politeness or care and can also apply to how work is done. 失礼 (しつれい), discourtesy or rudeness, provides a useful contrast in social conduct.

## Distinctive meaning

礼儀 is not merely grammar or a collection of polite verb endings. It can concern greetings, eating, listening, or conduct as a guest. What counts as appropriate depends on the people and situation; the word itself does not establish one universal rule. In a sentence about 礼儀, identify the specific behavior rather than assuming an unexplained cultural requirement.
