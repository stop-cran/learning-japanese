# 遠足 (えんそく) - school outing; field trip

## Meaning

遠足 is an outing or excursion, especially a school field trip.
A useful pattern is 遠足に行く (えんそくにいく), "go on a school
outing." School 遠足 are typically day trips, but the word does not
require every part of the journey to be on foot.

- 明日は学校の遠足です。
  (あしたはがっこうのえんそくです。) - Tomorrow is our school outing.
- 遠足で動物園に行きました。
  (えんそくでどうぶつえんにいきました。) - We went to the zoo on a field trip.

で presents the outing as the occasion for the visit; に identifies
the destination. The noun can also be used with する.

## How the kanji combine

[遠](../kanji/遠.md) contributes distance, and
[足](../kanji/足.md) the foot or walking. エン + ソク offers a
memory aid, not a rule excluding a bus or train from a modern school trip.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) and [旅](旅.md) (たび) name trips more
broadly. [ハイキング](ハイキング.md) names hiking, while
[ピクニック](ピクニック.md) focuses on a picnic.
An outing may include these activities, but not every hike or picnic
is a school 遠足.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not reduce 遠足 to hiking or picnicking; learn the practical
school-outing meaning. JMdict separately marks the long-walk or on-foot
excursion sense as dated. [散歩](散歩.md) (さんぽ) is an ordinary
walk or stroll; do not call every walk 遠足 because the spelling has 足.
