# 張り切る (はりきる) - be full of energy; be eager

## Meaning

張り切る describes being full of energy, enthusiasm, or eagerness.
A person can 張り切って do something: the て-form connects that
energetic attitude with the action.

- 明日の試合に向けて、選手たちは張り切っています。 (あしたのしあいにむけて、せんしゅたちははりきっています。) - The players are full of enthusiasm for tomorrow's match.
- 初めての仕事なので、張り切って準備しました。 (はじめてのしごとなので、はりきってじゅんびしました。) - Because it was my first job, I prepared with enthusiasm.

It is godan: 張り切ります, 張り切って, and 張り切らない.
The ending is not conjugated like an ichidan ～きる verb.

## How the kanji combine

張り comes from 張る (はる), associated with stretching or tension,
and [切](../kanji/切.md) supplies き in 切る.
Learn the established native compound はりきる rather than deriving
enthusiasm from a literal act of cutting something.
Digital Daijisen's heading 張（り）切る also recognizes 張切る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[元気](元気.md) (げんき) can describe energy or good health.
[熱中](熱中.md) (ねっちゅう) concerns being absorbed or enthusiastic
about something. [積極的](積極的.md) (せっきょくてき) describes an
active, willing approach. 張り切る presents someone as energized for
what they are doing, without by itself claiming physical health.

## Distinctive meaning

Digital Daijisen also records the physical sense "be stretched taut,"
as in 張り切った糸 (はりきったいと), "taut thread."
Do not replace that meaning with human enthusiasm.

In the examples, the people are the energetic participants and the
following verb names their action. This does not establish a universal
object pattern for every physical and figurative use, or make 張り切る
interchangeable with transitive 張る.
