# 今週 (こんしゅう) — this week

## Meaning

今週 means **this week**, the week containing today. It can set the time of a sentence without に.

今週は毎日忙しいです。 (こんしゅうはまいにちいそがしいです。) — I am busy every day this week.

To specify a day or event within it, use の: 今週の金曜日 (こんしゅうのきんようび), “this Friday,” or 今週の予定 (こんしゅうのよてい), “this week's schedule.”

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) contributes “current/this,” and [週](../kanji/週.md) means “week.” The on readings コン + シュウ give こんしゅう. Do not read 今 as the standalone word いま inside this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

先週 (せんしゅう) is last week; 来週 (らいしゅう) is next week. 毎週 (まいしゅう) means every week and expresses repetition rather than one current period.

## Distinctive meaning

今週 names a calendar period, not a duration of seven days starting now. For a duration, use 一週間 (いっしゅうかん), “one week.” Whether a particular calendar starts on Sunday or Monday is not encoded in the word itself.
