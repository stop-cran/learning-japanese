# 獣医 (じゅうい) — veterinarian

## Meaning

獣医 is a **veterinarian**, a medical practitioner who treats animals.
It names a person or profession, not a veterinary clinic or the field
of study itself.

- 私は獣医です。 (わたしはじゅういです。) — I am a veterinarian.
- 犬の病気について獣医に相談しました。 (いぬのびょうきについてじゅういにそうだんしました。) — I consulted a veterinarian about my dog's illness.

In the second sentence, について marks the topic and に marks the person
consulted. [相談](相談.md) そうだんする means to consult or ask for advice.

## How the kanji combine

[獣](../kanji/獣.md) contributes animal, and [医](../kanji/医.md) contributes
medical practitioner. The first component specifies whom the practitioner
treats: an animal doctor. Both use on readings, ジュウ + イ.
Keep the final い: the complete word is じゅうい, not じゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

獣医師 (じゅういし) is another name for the same profession.
[医者](医者.md) いしゃ and [医師](医師.md) いし ordinarily identify a
medical doctor treating people; the 獣 component makes the animal field
explicit. These are related professions, not antonyms.

獣医学 (じゅういがく) means veterinary medicine as a field of study.
It does not name the veterinarian.

## Distinctive meaning

The animal component does not restrict the word to treating wild beasts:
the dog example concerns a familiar domestic animal. Learn the complete
professional term rather than translating each character as a separate noun.
English **vet** here is short for veterinarian, not veteran.
