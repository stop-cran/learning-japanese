# 北 (きた) — north

## Meaning

北 is north, one of the four main compass directions. It is a noun, not an i-adjective.

駅の北に学校があります。（えきのきたにがっこうがあります。） — There is a school north of the station.

## How the kanji combine

[北](../kanji/北.md) alone is read with the native reading きた. The on reading ホク appears in compounds; it is not the reading of this standalone word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

南（みなみ）, "south," is the opposite direction. 東（ひがし）, "east," and 西（にし）, "west," complete the basic set.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 北の before a noun, as in 北の町（きたのまち）, "a northern town." Use 北へ for movement north and 北に for a location to the north. The reference point, such as 駅 in the example, determines what counts as north of something.
