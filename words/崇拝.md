# 崇拝 (すうはい) — worship; deep adoration

## Meaning

崇拝する means to worship or to hold someone in deep, almost worshipful admiration. を marks the object of that devotion.

- 村では昔から山の神を崇拝してきた。 (むらではむかしからやまのかみをすうはいしてきた。) — The villagers have long worshipped the mountain deity.
- 彼は若いころ、その作曲家を崇拝していた。 (かれはわかいころ、そのさっきょくかをすうはいしていた。) — When he was young, he idolized that composer.

## How the kanji combine

[崇](../kanji/崇.md) is すう, reverence; [拝](../kanji/拝.md) is はい, worship or a respectful bow. The word can describe an enduring attitude, not just one gesture.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[尊敬](尊敬.md) (そんけい) is respect or esteem and need not imply adoration. [拝む](拝む.md) (おがむ) can describe praying or making a gesture of worship. [憧れる](憧れる.md) (あこがれる) can express admiration or a longing to be like someone, without implying worship.

## Distinctive meaning

The composer example need not imply religious belief. It conveys much more than simply liking a piece of music.
