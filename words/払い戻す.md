# 払い戻す (はらいもどす) - refund; reimburse

## Meaning

払い戻す means to refund or pay money back, including returning an
amount previously paid or deposited. It is a transitive godan verb:
the money or charge takes を; the recipient can take に.

- 店は客に代金を払い戻しました。 (みせはきゃくにだいきんをはらいもどしました。) - The shop refunded the purchase price to the customer.
- 中止になった公演のチケット代を払い戻します。 (ちゅうしになったこうえんのチケットだいをはらいもどします。) - We will refund the ticket price for the canceled performance.

Forms include 払い戻します, 払い戻して, and 払い戻さない.
払い戻し (はらいもどし) is the corresponding noun, "refund."

## How the kanji combine

払い comes from [払う](払う.md) (はらう), "pay."
[払](../kanji/払.md) contributes payment, and
[戻](../kanji/戻.md), in [戻す](戻す.md) (もどす), contributes
returning something. The native compound describes paying money back.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[返す](返す.md) (かえす) means return something more broadly;
お金を返す (おかねをかえす) can describe giving money back.
返金する (へんきんする) also means refund.
[払い込む](払い込む.md) (はらいこむ), "pay in," gives a useful
transaction contrast, not a universal antonym for every reimbursement.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's English "repay" is too broad as an unrestricted rule.
Learn the refund or return-of-funds setting rather than using 払い戻す
for every repayment of a loan. The word also appears when a depositor's
funds are returned or betting winnings are paid out; it is not limited
to a shop canceling a sale.
