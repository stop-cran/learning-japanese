# 冷凍 (れいとう) - freezing; frozen storage

## Meaning

冷凍 is freezing or keeping something frozen, especially for preservation.
冷凍する is transitive: 食品を冷凍する (しょくひんをれいとうする)
means to freeze food.

- 残ったご飯を冷凍しました。 (のこったごはんをれいとうしました。) - I froze the leftover rice.
- この食品は冷凍で保存してください。 (このしょくひんはれいとうでほぞんしてください。) - Please store this food frozen.

冷凍食品 (れいとうしょくひん) means frozen food;
冷凍庫 (れいとうこ) is a freezer.

## How the kanji combine

[冷](../kanji/冷.md) contributes cold or cooling, and
[凍](../kanji/凍.md) freezing. Their related meanings combine
in a process or state of frozen preservation. Read レイ + トウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[凍る](凍る.md) (こおる) is intransitive: 水が凍る,
"water freezes." 冷凍する instead presents freezing something.
冷蔵 (れいぞう) is refrigerated storage; compare
[冷蔵庫](冷蔵庫.md), a refrigerator.
[冷やす](冷やす.md) (ひやす) more broadly means cooling or chilling.

## Distinctive meaning

Cooling need not reach freezing. 冷凍 and 冷蔵 identify different
storage conditions, even when one appliance has both compartments.
解凍 (かいとう), thawing or defrosting, is a practical reverse process.
These vocabulary examples do not specify safe storage temperatures or times.
