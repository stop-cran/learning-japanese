# しゃがむ (しゃがむ) - squat; crouch

## Meaning

しゃがむ means to squat or crouch by bending the knees and lowering the body. It is an **intransitive godan verb ending in む**: しゃがみます, しゃがんで, しゃがまない.

- しゃがんで靴のひもを結びました。
  (しゃがんでくつのひもをむすびました。) - I crouched down and tied my shoelaces.
- 子どもの隣にしゃがんで話しました。
  (こどものとなりにしゃがんではなしました。) - I crouched beside the child and spoke.

しゃがんでいる describes being in the crouched position. に can mark where someone crouches; the person is the subject, not an object marked with を.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[座る](座る.md) (すわる) means "sit down." Both lower the body, but crouching is not the same posture as sitting on a chair. [立つ](立つ.md) (たつ), "stand," gives a useful contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

しゃがむ names the posture or movement, not an emotion or intention. Someone might crouch to work near the ground, speak at a child's height, or stay out of sight; hiding is not built into every use. It does not mean the athletic action "jump."
