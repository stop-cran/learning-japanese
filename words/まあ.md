# まあ (まあ) - well; come now; oh my

## Meaning

まあ can introduce a hesitant or qualified opinion, like "well..." or "I suppose." It also appears when urging or calming someone, and as an exclamation of surprise.

- 「この案はどうですか。」「まあ、悪くないと思います。」 (「このあんはどうですか。」「まあ、わるくないとおもいます。」) - "What do you think of this proposal?" "Well, I think it's not bad."
- まあ、落ち着いてください。 (まあ、おちついてください。) - Now, please calm down.
- まあ、きれいな花！ (まあ、きれいなはな！) - Oh my, what lovely flowers!

The hesitant and calming uses are adverbial; the last is an interjection. Intonation and context matter, so one English replacement will not fit all three.

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana word with no kanji to decompose. The dictionary also records variants such as まー and まぁ. This card uses まあ, with a sustained vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ええと also signals hesitation, but often while searching for words or information. [ああ](ああ.md) can express recognition or emotion. These are contextual alternatives, not exact synonyms or opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

The source misleadingly attaches "(female)" to "you might say." In the inspected JMdict record, the female-language label belongs specifically to the exclamatory "oh/my/goodness" sense, not the hesitant-opinion sense. It is a sense-specific style label, not a rule forbidding other speakers from using まあ. The first exchange therefore carries no women-only restriction.
