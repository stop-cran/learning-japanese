# 履く (はく) - put on footwear or lower-body clothes

## Meaning

はく means to put on shoes, socks, trousers, a skirt, or similar lower-body clothing. It is a transitive godan verb: 履きます, 履いて, 履いた and 履かない.

- 靴を履いてください。 (くつをはいてください。) - Please put on your shoes.
- 今日は黒いズボンをはいています。 (きょうはくろいズボンをはいています。) - I am wearing black trousers today.

を marks the clothing. ～ている describes the resulting state of wearing it.

## How the kanji combine

履 is read は, followed by く. 履く is especially useful for footwear; 穿く is another spelling encountered for trousers and skirts. Kana はく avoids making a spelling choice.

## Synonyms and antonyms

着る (きる) is used for upper-body garments and many whole-body outfits. 脱ぐ (ぬぐ), "take off," provides the reverse action.

## Distinctive meaning

Japanese divides clothing actions by item. Do not use 履く as a universal translation of "wear": a hat normally takes かぶる, not はく.
