# 向く (むく) — turn toward; face

## Meaning

向く describes turning or facing in a direction. A direction can take を:
右を向く (みぎをむく), "turn to the right."

- 少し右を向いてください。 (すこしみぎをむいてください。) — Please turn a little to the right.

It also describes a continuing orientation:

- この窓は南を向いています。 (このまどはみなみをむいています。) — This window faces south.

Learn the forms 向きます (むきます), 向いた (むいた), and 向いて (むいて).

## How the kanji combine

[向](../kanji/向.md) supplies the orientation meaning; く completes the native verb.
The kanji also appears in 向ける (むける), but that is a related verb, not a conjugated form of 向く.

## Synonyms and antonyms

向ける explicitly describes directing something: 顔を右に向ける (かおをみぎにむける),
"turn one's face to the right." Compare the face as object and right as direction with 右を向く.

向かう (むかう) can mean heading toward a destination. Merely turning to face somewhere does not establish that one travels there.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary allows both transitive and intransitive uses of 向く; learn its actual patterns rather than forcing
every use into a single English object structure.
Another sense is being suited to something. This article concentrates on physical direction, not that suitability sense.
