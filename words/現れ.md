# 現れ (あらわれ) - manifestation; indication

## Meaning

現れ is a noun for a visible manifestation, expression, or indication of something.
Use Nの現れ for something that reveals an underlying feeling, condition, or tendency.
The thing you observe is presented as a sign of what lies behind it.

- その笑顔は喜びの現れです。
  (そのえがおはよろこびのあらわれです。) - That smile is an expression of joy.
- 売り上げの増加は人気の現れだ。
  (うりあげのぞうかはにんきのあらわれだ。) - The increase in sales is a sign of popularity.

These sentences identify a relationship between an observable result and its interpretation.
They are not simply saying that someone appeared in a place.

## How the kanji combine

[現](../kanji/現.md) carries the idea of becoming visible or apparent.
現れ is the noun corresponding to [現れる](現れる.md) (あらわれる), "appear; become evident."
The kana れ belongs to the written noun. 表れ is another spelling listed for the same entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[表現](表現.md) (ひょうげん) can mean expression or representation, including deliberate artistic
expression. 現れ can describe a sign that was not deliberately produced.
There is no single useful opposite covering all these contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

In Nの現れ, N names what is revealed, not the person viewing it.
Calling something a 現れ is an interpretation; the word alone does not prove that the
proposed explanation is correct. It is useful when connecting outward behavior with an inner state.
