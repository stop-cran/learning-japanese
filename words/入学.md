# 入学 (にゅうがく) — school enrollment

## Meaning

入学 means admission or enrollment at a school or university. 入学する means to enter that institution as a student, not merely walk into its building.

- 四月に大学に入学しました。 (しがつにだいがくににゅうがくしました。) — I enrolled at university in April.
- 娘は来年、小学校に入学します。 (むすめはらいねん、しょうがっこうににゅうがくします。) — My daughter will start elementary school next year.

The student is the subject; the institution takes に. The suru forms include 入学します and 入学した.

## How the kanji combine

[入](../kanji/入.md) contributes entering, and [学](../kanji/学.md) contributes study or school. The on-readings ニュウ + ガク form a word for beginning membership as a student.

## Synonyms and antonyms

卒業 (そつぎょう) means graduation, the completion of a course of study. 入る (はいる) is the broader verb "enter," which can also describe entering a room.

## Distinctive meaning

入学 names an educational milestone. 入学式 (にゅうがくしき) is the entrance ceremony; 入学試験 (にゅうがくしけん) is an entrance examination. Taking the examination is not itself enrollment.
