# 四つ角 (よつかど) - crossroads; four corners

## Meaning

四つ角 names a crossroads or intersecting streets, and can also
mean the four corners of something. It is a noun.

- 次の四つ角を右に曲がってください。 (つぎのよつかどをみぎにまがってください。) - Please turn right at the next crossroads.
- 紙の四つ角を押さえてください。 (かみのよつかどをおさえてください。) - Please hold down the four corners of the paper.

The first example identifies a place on a route. The second counts
corners belonging to an object; it is not about a road.

## How the kanji combine

[四](../kanji/四.md)つ supplies the native four element, and
[角](../kanji/角.md) is かど, corner.
Learn **よつかど**, without a small っ. The independent number
四つ is よっつ, but do not insert that full pronunciation into
this established compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[交差点](交差点.md) (こうさてん) is an intersection.
[街角](街角.md) (まちかど) is a street corner, and
[角](角.md) (かど) a corner more generally.
[四角](四角.md) (しかく) concerns a quadrilateral or square shape;
it is not the ordinary word for the crossroads in the first example.

## Distinctive meaning

The written number does not make every occurrence a description
of a square. Four corners of a sheet, an intersection and a whole
four-sided shape are different things to identify.
Use the context to distinguish the two senses of 四つ角.
