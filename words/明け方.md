# 明け方 (あけがた) - dawn; daybreak

## Meaning

明け方 is the time around dawn, when night is giving way to daylight.
It is a noun and can also be used adverbially.

- 明け方に雨がやみました。 (あけがたにあめがやみました。) - The rain stopped around dawn.
- 明け方まで起きていました。 (あけがたまでおきていました。) - I stayed awake until daybreak.

明け方の空 (あけがたのそら) means "the sky at dawn." The word does not prescribe
one fixed clock time for every season and location.

## How the kanji combine

[明](../kanji/明.md) connects with 明ける (あける), "dawn; grow light."
[方](../kanji/方.md) is がた here, marking an approximate time of day,
as in [夕方](夕方.md) (ゆうがた), "early evening." It does not mean a method in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[夜明け](夜明け.md) (よあけ) also means dawn or daybreak.
[朝](朝.md) (あさ), "morning," covers a broader period.
夕方 provides a time-of-day contrast, not a universal antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

明け方 concerns the transition out of night, not any early appointment.
The がた reading belongs to this complete word; do not read it あけほう
by selecting 方's on reading.
