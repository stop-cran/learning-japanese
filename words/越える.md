# 越える (こえる) — cross over; go beyond

## Meaning

越える describes passing over or beyond something, such as a mountain or boundary.
The thing crossed is marked with を.

- 山を越えると、海が見えます。 (やまをこえると、うみがみえます。) — Once you cross the mountain, you can see the sea.
- 国境を越えました。 (こっきょうをこえました。) — We crossed the border.

This is an ichidan verb: 越えます and 越えた are formed by replacing the final る.

## How the kanji combine

[越](../kanji/越.md) provides the crossing or going-beyond idea.
The native reading is こえる, with える written in kana; there is only one kanji in the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

越す (こす) overlaps in the crossing sense, but also has uses such as moving house.
[渡る](渡る.md) (わたる) is another crossing verb: 橋を渡る (はしをわたる), "cross a bridge," is a useful phrase.
Learn the crossing verb with its object rather than substituting one English "cross" everywhere.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict particularly associates 越える with crossing and 超える (こえる) with exceeding a quantity or surpassing something.
These are spelling preferences within overlapping senses, not a claim that the two spellings never overlap.
The mountain and border examples here illustrate the crossing focus.
