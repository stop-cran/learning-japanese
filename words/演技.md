# 演技 (えんぎ) - acting; performance

## Meaning

演技 is acting or a performance, such as an actor's portrayal or a gymnast's routine. 演技する means to act or perform. Useful noun patterns include 演技が上手 (えんぎがじょうず), "good at acting," and 演技を見る, "watch a performance."

- 彼女の演技は自然でした。 (かのじょのえんぎはしぜんでした。) - Her acting was natural.
- 選手たちは音楽に合わせて演技しました。 (せんしゅたちはおんがくにあわせてえんぎしました。) - The athletes performed in time with the music.

The second example is a presented routine, not a claim about the athletes' general competitive results.

## How the kanji combine

[演](../kanji/演.md) contributes performing or presenting; 技 means skill or technique. Together they name skill expressed in a performance. The on readings are エン + ギ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[演奏](演奏.md) (えんそう) concerns musical performance, such as playing an instrument. [芝居](芝居.md) (しばい) can refer to a play or acting. [俳優](俳優.md) (はいゆう) names an actor, not the acting itself.

## Distinctive meaning

演技 can also suggest that someone's displayed reaction is an act rather than sincere behavior: 演技だった, "it was an act." That implication comes from context; describing an actor's 演技 is not an accusation of dishonesty.
