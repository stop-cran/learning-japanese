# 崩す (くずす) - break down; demolish; change a banknote

## Meaning

崩す means break down or destroy a coherent shape, arrangement or stable state. It is transitive: the thing affected takes を. It also means break a banknote into smaller denominations of the same total value.

- 積み木の塔を崩しました。 (つみきのとうをくずしました。) - I knocked down the tower of building blocks.
- 一万円札を崩してもらえますか。 (いちまんえんさつをくずしてもらえますか。) - Could you change a ten-thousand-yen note for me?

Another useful expression is 体調を崩す (たいちょうをくずす), become unwell: one's stable physical condition is disrupted. This is not necessarily something done intentionally.

The godan forms include 崩します (くずします), 崩して (くずして), 崩さない (くずさない).

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 崩 supplies the native stem くず; す is the verb ending. There is no 崩 kanji card in the current collection.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[崩れる](崩れる.md) (くずれる) is intransitive: 塔が崩れる (とうがくずれる), the tower collapses. [壊す](壊す.md) (こわす), break or damage, is broader and can concern a machine ceasing to function without crumbling.

[両替](両替.md) (りょうがえ) covers exchanging currencies or denominations. 札を崩す (さつをくずす) particularly concerns changing a larger note into smaller money, not every foreign-currency exchange.

## Distinctive meaning

Breaking a note does not destroy its monetary value or mean tearing the paper. Context distinguishes physical demolition, a disrupted condition and making smaller change. Likewise, the intransitive counterpart does not automatically license every same-object use of 崩す.
