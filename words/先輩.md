# 先輩 (せんぱい) -- senior colleague or student

## Meaning

先輩 is someone with an earlier start or more experience in the same school, workplace, club, or field. It describes a relationship, not simply a person's age.

- 部活の先輩に相談しました。 (ぶかつのせんぱいにそうだんしました。) -- I consulted a senior member of my club.
- 田中先輩、少し教えてください。 (たなかせんぱい、すこしおしえてください。) -- Tanaka, could you show me how?

The second example uses 先輩 after a name as a form of address. In that context it acknowledges Tanaka as a senior member.

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md), read せん, means earlier or ahead. 輩 refers to fellows or people of a group; its はい reading becomes ぱい in this compound. The whole word identifies someone ahead in the shared setting.

## Synonyms and antonyms

後輩 (こうはい) is a junior member; 同僚 (どうりょう) is a colleague without specifying seniority. 上司 (じょうし) is a workplace superior in the reporting hierarchy.

## Distinctive meaning

A 先輩 need not be your manager, and someone older is not automatically your 先輩. Identify the shared activity or organization that makes the senior relationship relevant.
