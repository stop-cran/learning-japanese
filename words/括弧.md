# 括弧 (かっこ) - brackets; parentheses

## Meaning

括弧 names brackets or parentheses used to enclose writing. It is a noun and can also take する. The word is usually written かっこ.

- 名前を括弧で囲んでください。 (なまえをかっこでかこんでください。) - Please put parentheses around the name.
- 括弧の中に答えを書きます。 (かっこのなかにこたえをかきます。) - We write the answer inside the brackets.

括弧で囲む (かっこでかこむ), enclose in brackets, and 括弧に入れる (かっこにいれる), put in brackets, are useful patterns.

## How the kanji combine

括 contributes enclosing or gathering together, and 弧 an arc or curve. Together they suggest enclosing marks. カツ + コ becomes かっこ; do not pronounce the written compound かつこ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

丸括弧 (まるかっこ) are round parentheses, such as ( ). 鉤括弧 (かぎかっこ) are corner brackets, such as 「 」. These are kinds of 括弧, not interchangeable shapes in every writing convention.

## Distinctive meaning

Context determines which enclosing marks are intended. The English translation "parentheses" is useful but does not mean that every 括弧 must have a round shape. かっこ here is not 格好 (かっこう), appearance or form.
