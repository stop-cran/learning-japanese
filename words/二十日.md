# 二十日 (はつか) - twentieth day, twenty days

## Meaning

二十日 means either the twentieth day of a month or a duration of twenty days. The sentence tells you which.

- 誕生日は五月二十日です。 (たんじょうびはごがつはつかです。) - My birthday is May twentieth.
- 日本に二十日間いました。 (にほんにはつかかんいました。) - I was in Japan for twenty days.

Adding 間 (かん) makes the duration explicit, though 二十日 can itself express a duration.

## How the kanji combine

[二](../kanji/二.md) and [十](../kanji/十.md) write twenty; [日](../kanji/日.md) supplies the day meaning. Learn はつか as the established whole-word reading, not にじゅうにち obtained by mechanically combining number readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

二十歳 (はたち), "twenty years old," is another special twenty-related reading, but names age rather than days. 二日 (ふつか) means the second day or two days.

## Distinctive meaning

二十日に会います (はつかにあいます) means "I'll meet you on the twentieth" in an ordinary scheduling context. A duration behaves differently: 二十日待ちました (はつかまちました), "I waited twenty days."
