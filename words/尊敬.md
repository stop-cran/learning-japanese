# 尊敬 (そんけい) - respect; esteem

## Meaning

尊敬 is respect or esteem for someone. With する it forms a transitive verb: **person を尊敬する**, "respect or look up to someone." 尊敬している describes an ongoing attitude of respect.

- 私は祖母を尊敬しています。 (わたしはそぼをそんけいしています。) - I respect my grandmother.
- 彼は多くの人に尊敬されています。 (かれはおおくのひとにそんけいされています。) - He is respected by many people.

In the passive second example, に marks the people who respect him. 尊敬する人 (そんけいするひと) can mean "a person I respect," with the subject understood from context.

## How the kanji combine

尊 contributes esteeming or regarding someone highly, and 敬 contributes respect or honoring. The near-synonymous components reinforce the attitude of esteem. The on readings are ソン + ケイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[敬意](敬意.md) (けいい) names respect as a feeling or something shown: 敬意を払う, "pay respect." 尊敬 directly forms 尊敬する. Contempt contrasts with respect; ordinary disagreement does not necessarily do so.

## Distinctive meaning

尊敬 can concern someone's character, actions, or achievements. It is not simply obedience to a higher position. 尊敬語 (そんけいご), "honorific language," is a related grammatical term, but using an honorific form does not itself prove personal admiration.
