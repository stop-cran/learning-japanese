# 焼く (やく) — bake; grill

## Meaning

焼く means to cook by heat in ways English distinguishes as baking, grilling, roasting, or toasting. It is a transitive godan verb; the food takes を.

- オーブンでパンを焼きます。 (おーぶんでぱんをやきます。) — I bake bread in the oven.
- フライパンで魚を焼きました。 (ふらいぱんでさかなをやきました。) — I cooked the fish in a frying pan.

Forms include 焼きます, 焼いて, and 焼かない. The equipment and food determine the most natural English cooking verb.

## How the kanji combine

焼 carries a burning or heating association. く completes the native verb やく; it changes to いて in the て-form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

焼ける (やける) is intransitive: パンが焼けました (ぱんがやけました), "The bread has baked/is ready." 煮る (にる), "simmer/boil," describes cooking in liquid.

## Distinctive meaning

焼く is not limited to food: it can also mean burning something or firing pottery. For cooking, do not assume it always specifies an oven; a grill or frying pan can also be involved.
