# ぺろり — polishing food off; a quick lick

## Meaning

Finishing food with apparent ease, or a quick lick or movement of the tongue.

- 大きなケーキをぺろりと食べた。 — I polished off the large piece of cake.
- 犬が私の手をぺろりとなめた。 — The dog gave my hand a quick lick.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぺろぺろ](ぺろぺろ.md) often brings repeated licking into view. [むしゃむしゃ](むしゃむしゃ.md) describes hearty chewing rather than foregrounding easy completion.

## Distinctive meaning

ぺろりと食べる need not involve licking the food. 舌（した）をぺろりと出す means briefly sticking out one's tongue. The word also has a peeling-off use; no single English movement word covers all these scenes.
