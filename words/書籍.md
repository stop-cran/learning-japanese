# 書籍 (しょせき) - book; publication

## Meaning

書籍 is a noun for a book or publication. It names something to read, buy or
keep, not the act of publishing it. Japanese nouns do not require a separate
plural ending.

- 日本の歴史に関する書籍を読みました。
  (にほんのれきしにかんするしょせきをよみました。) - I read a book about Japanese history.
- この店では古い書籍も売っています。
  (このみせではふるいしょせきもうっています。) - This shop also sells old books.

In 書籍を読む, を marks the reading material. 古い supplies old in the second
example; 書籍 itself does not mean an old or rare book.

## How the kanji combine

[書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing or written works, and
[籍](../kanji/籍.md) has book and register associations. Together they name
books or publications, not every kind of registration. The on readings
ショ + セキ give しょせき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本](本.md) (ほん), [書物](書物.md) (しょもつ) and [図書](図書.md) (としょ)
overlap with the book meaning. [冊子](冊子.md) (さっし) can name a booklet
or book; the labels do not define mutually exclusive size categories.

[出版](出版.md) (しゅっぱん), [刊行](刊行.md) (かんこう) and
[発行](発行.md) (はっこう) concern publishing or issuing. English publication
can name an object or an activity, but that ambiguity does not make the
Japanese words freely interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

書籍 is useful when discussing books as reading materials or items offered
for sale. It does not establish a fixed binding, subject, age or physical
size. Use a context such as 一冊の書籍 (いっさつのしょせき), one book,
when the number matters.

The dictionary also records しょじゃく as an outdated reading. This article
teaches しょせき; the alternative is recognition information, not a second
reading to substitute into every example.
