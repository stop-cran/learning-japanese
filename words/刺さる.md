# 刺さる (ささる) - stick into; pierce and lodge

## Meaning

刺さる means that something with a sharp point sticks into or becomes lodged in something.
It is intransitive: the sharp thing takes が, and what it enters takes に.

- 指にとげが刺さりました。 (ゆびにとげがささりました。) - A splinter stuck into my finger.
- この板には釘が刺さっています。 (このいたにはくぎがささっています。) - A nail is stuck in this board.

This is godan despite ending in る: 刺さります, 刺さった, 刺さって, 刺さらない.
刺さっている often describes the resulting lodged state.

## How the kanji combine

[刺](../kanji/刺.md) contributes piercing and supplies さ in 刺さる; the written さる completes the verb.
Compare the separate form 刺す (さす), where the changing ending is す.
Do not form the polite version as 刺さます.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[刺す](刺す.md) is transitive: 板に釘を刺す (いたにくぎをさす), "stick a nail into a board."
[付く](付く.md) (つく) and [くっつく](くっつく.md) are broader attachment words;
gum sticking to a shoe need not pierce it.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional figurative use concerns emotional impact:
先生の一言が胸に刺さりました (せんせいのひとことがむねにささりました),
"The teacher's words hit me deeply."
It can be a moving insight or a painful remark, not necessarily praise.
[感動](感動.md) (かんどう) emphasizes being emotionally moved;
[感心](感心.md) (かんしん) often involves admiration.
