# 一層 (いっそう) - even more

## Meaning

一層 is an adverb meaning "even more" or "all the more." It increases a degree
relative to an earlier situation or an understood comparison. Place it before
the quality or action being intensified; no particle is needed.

- 雨が降って、一層寒くなりました。 (あめがふって、いっそうさむくなりました。) - It rained, and it became even colder.
- 話を聞いて、彼女を一層尊敬するようになりました。 (はなしをきいて、かのじょをいっそうそんけいするようになりました。) - After hearing the story, I came to respect her even more.

Both sentences imply a degree that was already present before the change.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) means "one," and 層 means "layer." The noun 一層 can
literally mean one layer; the adverb presents an increase as a further level.
Learn the contracted on-reading combination いっそう, including the small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もっと](もっと.md) also means "more" and can be used in direct requests.
[更に](更に.md) (さらに) can mean "further" or introduce something additional.
These overlap with 一層, but merely listing another item is not the same as
increasing a degree.

## Distinctive meaning

一層 strengthens both desirable and undesirable developments. It does not mean
"better" by itself: 一層寒い is colder, not more pleasant. より一層
(よりいっそう), "even more," reinforces the comparison without naming an exact amount.
