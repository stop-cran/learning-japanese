# 付き合う (つきあう) - associate with; accompany; date

## Meaning

付き合う means to associate or keep company with someone, including dating.
It can also mean going along with someone or joining in what they are doing.
It is an intransitive godan verb.

- 近所の人たちと長く付き合っています。
  (きんじょのひとたちとながくつきあっています。) - I have maintained a relationship with my neighbors for a long time.
- 買い物に付き合ってくれませんか。
  (かいものにつきあってくれませんか。) - Would you come shopping with me?
- 二人は去年から付き合っています。
  (ふたりはきょねんからつきあっています。) - The two have been dating since last year.

Use person + と for the other person, or activity + に for what one joins in.
Forms include 付き合います, 付き合った, 付き合って, and 付き合わない.

## How the kanji combine

[付](../kanji/付.md) contributes attaching or accompanying, and
[合](../kanji/合.md) contributes joining or mutual involvement.
The native elements つき + あう form the established verb.
The source spelling 付合う has the same reading つきあう;
JMdict marks that spelling search-only, so this page teaches 付き合う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[付き合い](付き合い.md) (つきあい) is the related noun for association
or social relations. [交際](交際.md) (こうさい) covers association and dating;
[交流](交流.md) (こうりゅう) concerns interaction or exchange.
[デート](デート.md) names a date or social outing.

## Distinctive meaning

Context decides whether 付き合う is romantic; it is not restricted to couples.
Going along with someone can mean cooperating with their wishes:
compare [従う](従う.md) (したがう), "follow; obey."
The public dictionary also extends the word to dealing with something,
such as an ongoing problem. That does not turn every use into romance
or make "obey" a replacement for the social-relationship sense.
