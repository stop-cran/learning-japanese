# そのころ - around that time; in those days

## Meaning

そのころ means around that time, at that time or in those days.
その points back to a time supplied by the surrounding context.
The expression can be used as a time phrase without に.

- 十年前、そのころ私は大阪に住んでいました。 (じゅうねんまえ、そのころわたしはおおさかにすんでいました。) - Ten years ago, around that time, I was living in Osaka.
- 会議は三時に終わります。そのころに電話してください。 (かいぎはさんじにおわります。そのころにでんわしてください。) - The meeting ends at three. Please call around then.

The reference can be a past period or another contextually identified time,
including a future one as in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

その頃 (そのころ) is a dictionary spelling of the same expression.
その identifies the relevant time, and 頃 (ころ) concerns a time or period.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[当時](当時.md) (とうじ) also refers to the time in question.
[ころ](ころ.md) and [頃](頃.md) explain the underlying time expression.
[時](時.md) (とき) is a broader word for time or occasion.
There is no fixed short-period versus long-period boundary between
そのころ and 当時.

## Distinctive meaning

そのころ does not by itself identify a clock time or duration.
It can cover a moment or a broader period according to context.
Do not translate every English "then" this way: narrative "and then" and
conditional "in that case" have other constructions.
