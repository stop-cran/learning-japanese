# 飢饉 (ききん) - famine; chronic shortage

## Meaning

飢饉 names a famine or crop failure. It concerns a serious lack of food, not simply feeling hungry before a meal. The word can also describe a chronic shortage of another resource.

- その地域では、干ばつの後に飢饉が起きました。
  (そのちいきでは、かんばつのあとにききんがおきました。) - A famine occurred in that region after the drought.
- 雨が少なく、水飢饉が続いています。
  (あめがすくなく、みずききんがつづいています。) - There has been little rain, and the water shortage continues.

## How the kanji combine

飢 concerns hunger or starvation, while 饉 concerns famine and crop failure. The related meanings reinforce each other. The on readings are キ + キン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不足](不足.md) (ふそく) is shortage or insufficiency more generally. [飢える](飢える.md) (うえる) is the verb "starve; hunger for"; 飢饉 names the famine or shortage rather than an individual's action or state.

## Distinctive meaning

水飢饉 (みずききん), "water shortage; drought," illustrates the extended resource-shortage use. Do not infer that every use concerns food alone. The dictionary also recognizes 飢きん and marks 饑饉 as a rare kanji spelling; this card uses 飢饉.
