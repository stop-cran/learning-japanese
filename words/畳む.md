# 畳む (たたむ) - fold; close a business

## Meaning

畳む means to fold something into a smaller, compact form, such as
clothes, bedding, or an umbrella. It is transitive: the thing folded
takes を. It is often written たたむ.

- 洗濯物を畳んで、棚にしまいました。
  (せんたくものをたたんで、たなにしまいました。) - I folded the laundry and put it away on the shelf.
- 両親は去年、店を畳みました。
  (りょうしんはきょねん、みせをたたみました。) - My parents closed their business last year.

Closing a business is an extended use, not folding its building.
It can also describe winding up a household and vacating the place.
Godan forms include 畳みます, 畳んだ, 畳んで, and 畳まない.

## How the kanji combine

[畳](../kanji/畳.md) writes たた in this native verb, with む as
the ending. The same character writes the mat noun 畳 (たたみ);
do not read the verb as たたみむ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[折る](折る.md) (おる) overlaps with folding, and also means
breaking something by bending it. [しまう](しまう.md) can mean
putting things away; folding and storing are separate actions in
the first example. 広げる (ひろげる), "spread out," contrasts with
folding something compact.

## Distinctive meaning

店を畳む normally concerns ending the business, not merely shutting
its door at closing time. For a door or window, choose a suitable
closing verb such as [閉める](閉める.md) (しめる), not 畳む
unless the object itself is being folded.
