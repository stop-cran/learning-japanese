# まぶた - eyelid

## Meaning

まぶた is an eyelid. Context supplies singular or plural; the word does
not inherently mean two eyelids.

- まぶたが重くなってきました。 (まぶたがおもくなってきました。) - My eyelids are starting to feel heavy.
- 虫に刺されて、右のまぶたが腫れました。 (むしにさされて、みぎのまぶたがはれました。) - An insect bite made my right eyelid swell.

まぶたを閉じる (まぶたをとじる) means close one's eyelids.

## How the kanji combine

The dictionary marks the word as usually written in kana.
瞼 is a recorded spelling; 目蓋 is marked rare, and 眼蓋 is a search-only
form rather than a recommended everyday spelling.
These alternatives do not add written kanji to the primary kana form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[目](目.md) (め) is the eye, not specifically its lid.
上まぶた (うわまぶた) and 下まぶた (したまぶた) identify the upper
and lower eyelid. They are parts, not antonyms of まぶた.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse the eyelid with eyelashes or the pupil.
The first example describes growing sleepiness through heavy eyelids;
the second locates swelling. The same body-part noun fits both without
requiring a plural ending.
