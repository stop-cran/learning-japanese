# 栞 (しおり) - bookmark; guidebook

## Meaning

栞, usually written **しおり**, can be a **bookmark** placed between pages or a **guidebook** giving information or instructions.

- 本にしおりを挟みました。 (ほんにしおりをはさみました。) - I put a bookmark in the book.
- 旅行のしおりを読みました。 (りょこうのしおりをよみました。) - I read the trip guide.

In the first example, the object marks a place in a book. In the second, the しおり itself contains information to read. A bookmark need not be a booklet, and a trip guide need not mark a page.

## How the kanji combine

The one character [栞](../kanji/栞.md) represents the native word **しおり**. 枝折り and 枝折 are additional dictionary spellings of the same word; they do not make the kana pronunciation an on-reading compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[本](本.md) (ほん) is a book more generally. [案内](案内.md) (あんない) concerns guidance or information; a しおり in the guidebook sense can provide that information. Neither word by itself means the page-marking object. There is no useful direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes the two uses: placing something between pages points to a bookmark, while reading a trip or activity's しおり points to its guide. Do not translate every occurrence as "bookmark" merely because that is the first sense learned.
