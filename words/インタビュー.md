# インタビュー - media interview

## Meaning

インタビュー is an interview, especially one gathering someone's answers
for a newspaper, broadcast, or similar coverage. It is a noun and a
する verb.

- 記者が選手にインタビューしました。 (きしゃがせんしゅにインタビューしました。) - A reporter interviewed the athlete.
- 試合の後、インタビューを受けました。 (しあいのあと、インタビューをうけました。) - I was interviewed after the match.

人にインタビューする identifies the person being interviewed.
インタビューを受ける (うける) means to be interviewed, not to ask
the questions oneself.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word has no kanji components. ュ is small in ビュー,
and the final ー lengthens the vowel. Learn the complete spelling
インタビュー rather than adding an extra long-vowel mark after タ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

取材 (しゅざい) is gathering material for reporting more broadly.
面接 (めんせつ) is the usual term for a selection interview, such
as a job interview. Asking [質問](質問.md) (しつもん), "questions,"
can be part of an interview without itself naming the entire event.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every English "interview" is automatically インタビュー in the
same setting. Distinguish the reporter's questioning from the interviewee's
受ける construction. The word itself does not guarantee that the
answers will be broadcast live or printed verbatim.
