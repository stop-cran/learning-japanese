# 百円 (ひゃくえん) — 100 yen

## Meaning

百円 is 100 yen, a very common price: 百円ショップ (100-yen store), 百円玉 ひゃくえんだま (100-yen coin), このペンは百円です (this pen is 100 yen).

## How the kanji combine

[百](../kanji/百.md) (hundred) plus [円](../kanji/円.md) (yen): number plus the currency counter, both with on readings. No 一 is added: 百円, not 一百円.
Other hundreds: 二百円 にひゃくえん, 三百円 さんびゃくえん, 六百円 ろっぴゃくえん, 八百円 はっぴゃくえん (see [三百](三百.md)).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: [千円](千円.md) せんえん (1,000 yen), 十円 じゅうえん (10 yen), 一万円 いちまんえん.

## Distinctive meaning

百円 is a pure amount; with ショップ it names a store type where most items cost 100 yen (plus tax).
