# 者 (もの) - person; one who

## Meaning

者, read もの, means "person," particularly a person identified by a preceding qualifier.
JMdict notes that it is rarely used without one. Learn complete expressions rather than using
bare 者 as an unrestricted replacement for "person."

- 担当の者が後で電話します。 (たんとうのものがあとででんわします。) - The person responsible will call later.
- 私は先ほど電話した者です。 (わたしはさきほどでんわしたものです。) - I am the person who called earlier.

The first example uses a noun plus の; the second uses a plain-form clause.
担当の者 can refer to a member of one's own organization in a business exchange.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [者](../kanji/者.md) writes the native word もの.
It also has the on reading シャ in compounds, but that is not the reading of the standalone
qualified noun in these examples.
The written form distinguishes a person from 物, also read もの.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[人](人.md) (ひと) is a broadly useful word for a person.
[物](物.md) (もの) refers to a thing, not the person meant here.
[担当](担当.md) (たんとう) identifies responsibility; adding の者 identifies the person with that responsibility.

## Distinctive meaning

者 can sound formal or distancing and is useful in self-identification or references to one's own side.
It is not an honorific for a customer merely because it occurs in business language.
The qualifier constraint is a strong usage tendency, not a claim that an unmodified occurrence is impossible.
