# 長女 (ちょうじょ) - eldest daughter

## Meaning

長女 is the eldest daughter in a family. She may be the only daughter.
Use **Nの長女** to identify the family or parent.

- 長女は来年、大学を卒業します。
  (ちょうじょはらいねん、だいがくをそつぎょうします。) - My eldest daughter will graduate from university next year.
- 彼女は長女ですが、兄が二人います。
  (かのじょはちょうじょですが、あにがふたりいます。) - She is the eldest daughter, but she has two older brothers.

## How the kanji combine

[長](../kanji/長.md) contributes seniority, and
[女](../kanji/女.md) identifies the female child in this family role.
The on readings are チョウ + ジョ, not ながおんな.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[娘](娘.md) (むすめ) means daughter without specifying birth order.
[長男](長男.md) (ちょうなん) is the eldest son.
次女 (じじょ) means the second daughter, not necessarily the second child overall.

## Distinctive meaning

長女 is not necessarily the first child: older brothers are possible.
It is also not a synonym for [姉](姉.md) (あね), older sister,
which describes a relationship to a younger sibling.
The family label does not itself specify an age, marital status, or social duty.
