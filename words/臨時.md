# 臨時 (りんじ) - temporary; special; extra

## Meaning

臨時 describes something temporary, provisional, special or extra:
an arrangement made for the occasion rather than the usual schedule.
It is a noun that modifies another noun with の, or forms compounds:
臨時の仕事 (りんじのしごと), "temporary work," and
臨時列車 (りんじれっしゃ), "an extra train."

- 臨時の職員を募集しています。 (りんじのしょくいんをぼしゅうしています。) - We are recruiting temporary staff.
- 今日は臨時休業です。 (きょうはりんじきゅうぎょうです。) - We are closed today as a special closure.
- 祭りの日には臨時列車が出ます。 (まつりのひにはりんじれっしゃがでます。) - Extra trains run on the day of the festival.

## How the kanji combine

臨 contributes facing or responding to an occasion, and
[時](../kanji/時.md) time. Together the word concerns an arrangement
for the circumstances at hand. The on readings are リン + ジ.
This is a character-based association, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一時](一時.md) (いちじ) can mean temporary or for a time.
[特別](特別.md) (とくべつ) overlaps in special arrangements.
[余分](余分.md) (よぶん) concerns an extra amount; 臨時 more directly
identifies an arrangement outside the ordinary schedule.
[定期](定期.md) (ていき), regular or periodic, offers a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Temporary duration and a special nonregular occasion are related but
different uses. An extra train need not imply an emergency or an
unnecessary surplus. Use 臨時の when adding の to a following noun,
not an invented な-adjective pattern.
