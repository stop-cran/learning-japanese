# 矛 (ほこ) - spear; polearm

## Meaning

矛 names a spear or polearm: a weapon with a long shaft.
The dictionary also records broader "weapons; arms" and a context-specific
abbreviation for a parade float decorated with spears.

- 博物館で矛を見ました。 (はくぶつかんでほこをみました。) - I saw a spear at the museum.
- この矛は長いです。 (このほこはながいです。) - This spear is long.

## How the kanji combine

[矛](../kanji/矛.md) is a single character read ほこ in this native noun.
In [矛盾](矛盾.md) (むじゅん), "contradiction," the same character instead has the on reading ム.
Do not replace ほこ with む when reading the standalone noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[武器](武器.md) (ぶき) is a weapon more generally.
盾 (たて), "shield," provides a contrast in the conventional spear-and-shield story
explained in [矛盾](矛盾.md); it is not the word for the spear.

## Distinctive meaning

The full dictionary family includes 鉾, 戈 and other spellings; sharing a family
does not make their historical shapes or every contextual use identical.
Its alternative とかり is restricted to 鋒, not 矛.
In a festival context, the abbreviated float sense does not mean every float is a spear.
The noun is not an instruction for using a weapon.
