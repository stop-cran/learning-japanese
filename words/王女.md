# 王女 (おうじょ) - princess

## Meaning

王女 is a princess, specifically a king's daughter in the practical
meaning taught here. The noun does not by itself say that she rules
the country.

- 王女が式に出席しました。
  (おうじょがしきにしゅっせきしました。) - The princess attended the ceremony.
- この物語では、王女が国を救います。
  (このものがたりでは、おうじょがくにをすくいます。) - In this story, the princess saves the country.

王女の名前 (おうじょのなまえ) is "the princess's name."
The reading here is おうじょ.

## How the kanji combine

[王](../kanji/王.md) contributes king, and
[女](../kanji/女.md) a female person.
The established compound identifies a king's daughter:
オウ + ジョ. Do not infer every royal title from a literal
combination of the character meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[女王](女王.md) (じょおう) commonly means queen; it is not simply
another spelling of 王女. The order and pronunciation differ.
皇女 (こうじょ) identifies an emperor's daughter, while
姫 (ひめ) is another princess or noble-young-lady word with a
broader range of uses.

## Distinctive meaning

English "princess" can cover more titles and uses than a king's
daughter. Choose the appropriate title for the person and context;
do not automatically apply 王女 to every imperial daughter or
every English nickname "princess."
JMdict marks おうにょ as obsolete; it is not an alternative
pronunciation recommended for production here.
