# バンド - musical band; strap

## Meaning

バンド can mean a group of musicians, such as a rock band.
It also names a band of material, a strap, or a watch bracelet.
These are distinct uses of the same English-derived loanword.

- 友達とバンドを組みました。 (ともだちとバンドをくみました。) - I formed a band with friends.
- 腕時計のバンドを交換しました。 (うでどけいのバンドをこうかんしました。) - I replaced the watch strap.

バンドを組む (バンドをくむ) is the musical-group expression.
腕時計のバンド identifies a part of a watch, not a performing group.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "band," not a kanji compound.
The material-band and musical-group uses must be learned as lexical
meanings, not derived from Japanese character shapes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[グループ](グループ.md) is a group more generally; it need not be
musical. [ベルト](ベルト.md) overlaps with a clothing belt or strap.
[帯](帯.md) (おび) names a sash or band, including a paper band;
it is not a substitute for バンド in the musical example.
There is no single opposite covering a group and a strap.

## Distinctive meaning

Other recorded uses include a belt worn with trousers and a frequency
band in communications. The intended sense depends on what is being
described, not just the katakana spelling.

The source's bare English "band" does not establish which use its compiler
intended. Musicians are the practical teaching anchor here; additional
recognition does not independently assign every sense an N2 level.
Unrelated dictionary headings for a waterfront "bund" or alumina are
not extra meanings imported into this entry.
