# 汲む (くむ) - draw or scoop water; consider feelings

## Meaning

汲む means draw, scoop, ladle or pump up liquid, especially water. It is transitive: the liquid takes を.

- 井戸から水を汲みました。 (いどからみずをくみました。) - I drew water from the well.
- バケツで川の水を汲みました。 (バケツでかわのみずをくみました。) - I scooped water from the river with a bucket.

An additional figurative use is considering or understanding someone's feelings or circumstances: 相手の気持ちを汲む (あいてのきもちをくむ), take the other person's feelings into account.

The godan forms include 汲みます (くみます), 汲んで (くんで), 汲まない (くまない).

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 汲 supplies the native stem く; む is the verb ending. There is no 汲 card in the current collection. Drawing something out is a useful association for remembering the word, not proof of a historical metaphor.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[酌む](酌む.md) (くむ) is especially useful for serving or sharing sake. The dictionaries also recognize pouring/drinking uses for 汲む and considering-feelings uses for 酌む; the spellings are not separated by an absolute liquid-versus-feelings rule.

[組む](組む.md) (くむ), assemble or team up, is a different word. [注ぐ](注ぐ.md) (そそぐ) is a broader pouring verb, not a synonym for every water-drawing action.

## Distinctive meaning

Drawing water from its source need not mean pouring it into someone's cup. Learn the relevant action and spelling together while allowing the documented overlap with 酌む. Considering feelings means understanding and allowing for them, not necessarily agreeing with every request.
