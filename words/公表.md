# 公表 (こうひょう) - official public announcement

## Meaning

公表 is a formal public announcement or release of information.
It is a noun and transitive する-verb: 情報を公表する
(じょうほうをこうひょうする), "make information public."
The announcing party can be a public body or a company.

- 会社は調査結果を公表しました。 (かいしゃはちょうさけっかをこうひょうしました。) - The company publicly announced the investigation results.
- 市は来年度の予算案を公表しました。 (しはらいねんどのよさんあんをこうひょうしました。) - The city made next fiscal year's budget proposal public.

The information takes を. The word concerns making it known publicly,
not simply telling one friend something.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md) contributes public, while [表](../kanji/表.md)
has the association of showing or expressing. Together they suggest
bringing information before the public. The on readings are コウ + ヒョウ.
This is a meaning aid, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[発表](発表.md) (はっぴょう) covers announcement and presentation more
broadly, including a classroom presentation.
[報告](報告.md) (ほうこく) is a report, which need not be public.
[知らせる](知らせる.md) (しらせる) is the broader verb notify or let know.
Keeping information confidential provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

"Official" does not limit 公表 to a government announcement.
It also does not guarantee that the information is true, approved by
everyone, or legally required to be disclosed.
Publication of findings can be 公表 without being the production and
sale of a book described by [出版](出版.md) (しゅっぱん).
