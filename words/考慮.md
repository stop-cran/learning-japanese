# 考慮 (こうりょ) - consideration; taking into account

## Meaning

考慮 is consideration of a factor when making a judgment or decision.
As a transitive する verb, it takes を: Nを考慮する means "take N into account."
Another useful construction is Nを考慮に入れる, "include N in one's considerations."

- 費用を考慮して、旅行の計画を変えました。
  (ひようをこうりょして、りょこうのけいかくをかえました。)
  - Taking the cost into account, we changed our travel plans.
- 天気も考慮に入れる必要があります。
  (てんきもこうりょにいれるひつようがあります。) - We need to take the weather into account too.

The factor matters to the decision, but the word does not specify how much weight it receives.

## How the kanji combine

[考](../kanji/考.md) and 慮 both contribute thinking or deliberation.
Their related meanings reinforce the idea of considering something.
The on readings are コウ + リョ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[考える](考える.md) (かんがえる) is the broader verb "think; consider."
[検討](検討.md) (けんとう) focuses on examining or reviewing an option or issue.
考慮 highlights a factor being taken into account within that process.

## Distinctive meaning

This word suits explanations of decisions and planning; it is not a replacement for every
use of "think." 費用を考慮する says cost enters the decision, not that cost is the
sole concern. Considering a request also does not promise that it will be accepted.
