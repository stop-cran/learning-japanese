# 押す (おす) — push

## Meaning

押す means to push in its basic physical sense. The thing being pushed takes を.
It describes the action, not a guarantee that the object moves.

- このドアは押してください。 (このドアはおしてください。) — Please push this door.
- 二人で車を押しました。 (ふたりでくるまをおしました。) — The two of us pushed the car.

In the first sentence, は makes the door the topic instead of marking it with を.
The past is 押した (おした), and the negative is 押さない (おさない).

It also means to stamp or affix a seal: ここにはんこを押してください。
(ここにはんこをおしてください。) — Please put your seal here.
In this pattern, はんこを押す names applying the seal, not pushing it across a surface.

## How the kanji combine

[押](../kanji/押.md) contributes pushing or pressure. The final す completes a native verb.
Do not read the dictionary form as おし; that is the stem seen in 押します (おします).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[引く](引く.md) (ひく), "pull," provides the practical contrast on a door: push versus pull.
押さえる (おさえる), "hold down; hold in place," also involves pressure, but focuses on keeping something steady
rather than pushing it along.

## Distinctive meaning

押す is not simply another word for [打つ](打つ.md) (うつ), "hit."
Pushing a door applies pressure; striking it delivers a blow.
The push/pull contrast is useful for physical movement, but should not be extended into a rule about every expression using either verb.
