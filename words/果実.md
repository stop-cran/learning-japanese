# 果実 (かじつ) - fruit

## Meaning

果実 names a plant's fruit, including berries and nuts. It is useful when discussing plants, fruit production, or fruit used as an ingredient. It does not mean that every fruit is edible.

- この木は秋に赤い果実をつけます。 (このきはあきにあかいかじつをつけます。) - This tree bears red fruit in autumn.
- 果実からジュースを作ります。 (かじつからジュースをつくります。) - We make juice from the fruit.

The noun does not require a separate plural form. The plant and surrounding context identify what kind of fruit is meant.

## How the kanji combine

[果](../kanji/果.md) and [実](../kanji/実.md) both contribute fruit or the product of growth. Their meanings reinforce one another. The on readings combine as カ + ジツ, rather than using the standalone reading み of 実.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[果物](果物.md) (くだもの) is the familiar food-category word for fruit. [実](実.md) (み) can mean fruit, nuts, or seeds, as well as substance in another use. These words overlap, but a botanical fruit need not be something served as 果物.

## Distinctive meaning

Use context rather than treating 果実 and 果物 as interchangeable in every botanical or food expression. JMdict also records legal "fruits; profit" in a separate sense; that specialist use is outside this plant-fruit lesson.
