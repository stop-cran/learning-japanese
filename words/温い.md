# 温い (ぬるい) — lukewarm

## Meaning

ぬるい means lukewarm or tepid. It can describe a hot drink that has cooled too much or a cold drink that is not chilled enough. It is an い-adjective: ぬるくない, "not lukewarm," and ぬるかった, "was lukewarm."

- このお茶はぬるいです。 (このおちゃはぬるいです。) — This tea is lukewarm.
- このビールはぬるいです。 (このビールはぬるいです。) — This beer is lukewarm, not cold enough.

## How the kanji combine

温い is a dictionary spelling, but ぬるい is usually written in kana. 温 has a warmth association. The reading taught here is ぬるい, not あたたかい, which is written 温かい with different okurigana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

温かい (あたたかい) means warm and often suggests comfortable warmth. 熱い (あつい) means hot, and 冷たい (つめたい) cold to the touch. These locate different points on a temperature scale.

## Distinctive meaning

For drinks and bathwater, the comparison is with the intended serving or bathing temperature, not a fixed number of degrees. ぬるい often expresses disappointment that something is not hot enough or not cold enough. Context determines whether mild warmth is desirable.
