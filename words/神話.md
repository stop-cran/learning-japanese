# 神話 (しんわ) - myth; mythology

## Meaning

神話 is a myth or mythology: traditional narratives connecting origins,
natural phenomena, or social life with gods, supernatural beings, or heroes.
The noun can identify a particular myth or a body of mythology.

- ギリシャ神話を読んでいます。 (ギリシャしんわをよんでいます。) - I am reading Greek mythology.
- チームの不敗神話が崩れました。 (チームのふはいしんわがくずれました。) - The team's myth of invincibility was shattered.

The second example is figurative. 不敗 (ふはい) means remaining undefeated;
the phrase presents invincibility as an idealized belief or reputation.

## How the kanji combine

[神](../kanji/神.md), a deity or supernatural presence, qualifies
[話](../kanji/話.md), a tale or account.
The on readings are シン + ワ.
The component image suits traditional mythology without limiting every
extended use to an actual story about a god.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[物語](物語.md) (ものがたり) and [話](話.md) (はなし) are broader
narrative words. 伝説 (でんせつ), legend, overlaps in traditional storytelling
but is not an unrestricted replacement for every myth or mythology.
嘘 (うそ), a lie, is not a neutral synonym for all traditional 神話.

## Distinctive meaning

Daijisen also records something long accepted or admired as absolute
despite an unclear basis. This supports figurative expressions such as
不敗神話, not a rule that every occurrence means deliberate deception.
The word's use does not by itself settle the truth of a religious tradition.
