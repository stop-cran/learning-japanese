# 灯 (ひ) - light; lamplight

## Meaning

灯, read ひ here, is a noun for a light or lamp. It is useful in descriptions of lights seen in a town or through a window. の can identify where the light belongs, as in 町の灯 (まちのひ), "the town's lights."

- 遠くに町の灯が見えます。 (とおくにまちのひがみえます。) - I can see the town's lights in the distance.
- 窓の灯が消えました。 (まどのひがきえました。) - The light in the window went out.

The English translation can be singular or plural; the Japanese noun itself does not require that distinction.

## How the kanji combine

灯 is one character carrying the whole reading ひ in this entry. Do not infer a compound from its visible parts, or replace the assigned reading with another reading simply because it is familiar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[明かり](明かり.md) (あかり) also names light or illumination. [電気](電気.md) (でんき) can refer to an electric light in everyday speech. [火](火.md) (ひ), "fire," is a different word with the same pronunciation.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the spelling and context together: a town's 灯 describes its lights, not a claim that the town is burning. The dictionary-backed reading in these examples is ひ. This card does not assign that reading to every compound containing 灯 or to every occurrence of the character in isolation.
