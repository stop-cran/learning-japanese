# 兄 (あに) — older brother

## Meaning

兄 is an older brother. Use あに especially when referring to your own older brother to someone outside the family.

- 兄は大学生です。 (あにはだいがくせいです。) — My older brother is a university student.
- 兄と映画を見ました。 (あにとえいがをみました。) — I watched a film with my older brother.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun, not a compound. [兄](../kanji/兄.md) represents the whole word あに, with no okurigana.
The polite family word お兄さん (おにいさん) has a different reading; it is not formed by simply putting お and さん around あに.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お兄さん (おにいさん) is useful for someone else's older brother or when addressing an older brother in the family.
[弟](弟.md) (おとうと) is a younger brother, and [姉](姉.md) (あね) is an older sister.
These are family-role contrasts, not interchangeable synonyms or strict logical opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

English "brother" does not specify age, but 兄 does: the brother is older relative to the person being discussed.
Family address varies; あに is primarily a reference word, not the usual way to call out to your brother.
