# 探偵 (たんてい) - detective; secret investigation

## Meaning

探偵 names a detective or investigator, often a private investigator.
私立探偵 (しりつたんてい) explicitly means "private detective."

- 探偵に調査を頼みました。 (たんていにちょうさをたのみました。) - I asked a detective to carry out an investigation.
- 探偵小説を読むのが好きです。 (たんていしょうせつをよむのがすきです。) - I like reading detective stories.

In the first example, に marks the person asked, and を marks the work requested.
The dictionary also records 探偵 as detective work or secret investigation,
including the transitive する form 探偵する. Thus it is not exclusively a word
for a person; context distinguishes the investigator from the activity.

## How the kanji combine

[探](../kanji/探.md) contributes searching or probing, and
[偵](../kanji/偵.md) investigating hidden information.
The two related ideas reinforce investigation; the whole compound can name
the work or the person doing it. The on readings are タン + テイ: たんてい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[刑事](刑事.md) (けいじ) names a police detective in its person sense,
whereas 探偵 is useful for a private investigator. This is a practical
distinction, not a rule that every occurrence of 探偵 excludes police.
[調査](調査.md) (ちょうさ) is a broader investigation, inquiry or survey;
it need not be secret. [調べる](調べる.md) (しらべる) means to examine
or investigate, and [探る](探る.md) (さぐる) includes probing into
something unclear. None of these three by itself names the detective.
There is no single useful antonym for this occupation or activity.

## Distinctive meaning

English "detective" can describe a police officer or a private investigator.
Choose the Japanese word for the intended role rather than assuming
探偵 and 刑事 are interchangeable.
Likewise, 偵's "spy" association does not make every 探偵 an enemy agent.
The compound alone does not say that an investigation succeeds.
