# 憧れる (あこがれる) - yearn for; admire

## Meaning

憧れる expresses longing, aspiration, or admiration toward a person, way of life,
or something one finds attractive. It is an intransitive ichidan verb;
mark the target with **に**.

- 子供のころから、海外での生活に憧れています。 (こどものころから、かいがいでのせいかつにあこがれています。) - Since childhood, I have dreamed of living abroad.
- 私はあの俳優に憧れています。 (わたしはあのはいゆうにあこがれています。) - I admire that actor.

Forms include 憧れます (あこがれます), 憧れて (あこがれて), and 憧れた (あこがれた).
憧れている presents an ongoing feeling, not necessarily a plan already being carried out.

## How the kanji combine

憧 carries longing or admiration. Its native verb reading here is あこが;
れる completes the spelling. Learn the whole reading あこがれる rather than
guessing from the character's on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[望む](望む.md) (のぞむ) overlaps in desiring something, but uses a different object pattern.
[尊敬](尊敬.md) (そんけい)する emphasizes respect or esteem.
Admiration and longing can overlap; these are not rigidly separate emotional categories.

## Distinctive meaning

憧れる does not by itself mean romantic love, nor does admiring an actor necessarily mean
planning to become one. The target and context determine the aspiration.
Do not transfer 望む's を construction mechanically: this card's basic pattern is Nに憧れる.
