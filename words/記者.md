# 記者 (きしゃ) - reporter; journalist

## Meaning

記者 is a noun for a reporter or journalist. It names a person who gathers and reports news, not the resulting article. **新聞記者** (しんぶんきしゃ) is a newspaper reporter; **記者になる** means "become a journalist."

- 記者が市長に質問しました。 (きしゃがしちょうにしつもんしました。) - A reporter asked the mayor a question.
- 姉は新聞社の記者です。 (あねはしんぶんしゃのきしゃです。) - My older sister is a reporter at a newspaper company.

In the first example, が identifies the reporter as the questioner and に the person questioned.

## How the kanji combine

[記](../kanji/記.md), "record; write down," modifies [者](../kanji/者.md), "person." キ + シャ gives きしゃ, a person engaged in reporting. This component relationship helps explain the occupational noun without implying that anyone who writes notes is a journalist.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[記事](記事.md) (きじ) is an article or report, while 記者 is its reporter. [新聞](新聞.md) (しんぶん) names a newspaper, not an employee. A journalist may work in media other than newspapers.

## Distinctive meaning

記者会見 (きしゃかいけん) is a press conference: journalists attend to ask questions and report the answers. The word 記者 does not itself mean a television presenter, novelist, or someone keeping a private diary. Keep the person, the medium, and the written report separate.
