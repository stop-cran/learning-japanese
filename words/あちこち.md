# あちこち (あちこち) - here and there

## Meaning

あちこち refers to various places rather than one particular location. It can act
as a place expression or an adverb. Choose the particle according to what happens:
あちこちに for things located in different places, あちこちで for activities there,
and あちこちを for an area searched or traveled through.

- 部屋のあちこちに本があります。 (へやのあちこちにほんがあります。) - There are books in various parts of the room.
- 鍵を探して、あちこちを見ました。 (かぎをさがして、あちこちをみました。) - I looked here and there for my key.

## How the kanji combine

This word is normally written in kana. The dictionary also records 彼方此方 as a
rare kanji spelling. The forms あちらこちら and あっちこっち belong to the same
entry; learning the kana expression is enough to use the examples here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ここ](ここ.md), "here," and [そこ](そこ.md), "there," identify particular locations.
あちこち distributes attention across several places. 一か所 (いっかしょ), "one
place," is a useful contextual contrast, not a universal antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

"Everywhere" can be a natural English translation, but あちこち does not require a
literal claim about every possible place. In the room example, it conveys that
books are scattered about, not that every inch is covered.
