# 倣う (ならう) - imitate; follow an example

## Meaning

倣う means to imitate, emulate or follow a model or precedent.
Use に for the model: 前例に倣う (ぜんれいにならう),
"follow the precedent."

- 先輩のやり方に倣いました。 (せんぱいのやりかたにならいました。) - I followed my senior colleague's way of doing it.
- 前例に倣って、この方法を使います。 (ぜんれいにならって、このほうほうをつかいます。) - Following the precedent, we will use this method.

The dictionary classifies it as an intransitive godan verb.
Its forms include 倣います, 倣わない, 倣って and 倣った.
English "imitate something" does not justify mechanically using を.

## How the kanji combine

倣 writes なら, followed by う. The dictionary marks this word as
usually written in kana; the source spelling 倣う distinguishes its
meaning. 傚う and 慣らう are marked rare spellings, not recommended
substitutes for every word pronounced ならう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[真似](真似.md) (まね) is imitation and can take する.
[写す](写す.md) (うつす) includes copying but also transferring or
photographing in its own uses.
[従う](従う.md) (したがう) includes following or complying;
倣う specifically brings a model or example into view.

## Distinctive meaning

[習う](習う.md), also ならう, means learning under instruction.
Here the focus is following an existing pattern, not necessarily taking
lessons or receiving instruction. A person can learn a skill and then
choose not to follow an earlier precedent. Shared pronunciation does
not make the two written identities interchangeable.
