# 先々週 (せんせんしゅう) - week before last

## Meaning

先々週 is the calendar week before last week.
It identifies a week rather than a duration of fourteen days.

- 先々週、友達に会いました。 (せんせんしゅう、ともだちにあいました。) - I met a friend the week before last.
- その話は先々週の授業で聞きました。 (そのはなしはせんせんしゅうのじゅぎょうでききました。) - I heard that in class the week before last.

Use it directly as a time expression, normally without に.
Before a following noun, connect with の: 先々週の授業.

## How the kanji combine

[先](../kanji/先.md), previous or earlier, repeats before
[週](../kanji/週.md), week, taking the reference one week further back
than [先週](先週.md).
々 repeats 先; it does not need a separate character card.
The reading is セン + セン + シュウ, with the long う retained.
先先週 is another dictionary spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[先週](先週.md) (せんしゅう) is last week.
[来週](来週.md) (らいしゅう) is next week, and
[再来週](再来週.md) (さらいしゅう) is the week after next.
[先々月](先々月.md) (せんせんげつ) uses months instead.

## Distinctive meaning

this week-before-last expression.
It does not mean any rolling period exactly fourteen days before now:
二週間前 (にしゅうかんまえ), "two weeks earlier/ago," is an interval expression.
