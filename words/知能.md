# 知能 (ちのう) - intelligence; intellect

## Meaning

知能 is intelligence or intellectual ability: the capacity involved in understanding,
learning, and reasoning. It is a noun.
知能が高い (ちのうがたかい) means "have high intelligence," while
知能を調べる means to investigate intelligence.

- 研究者は動物の知能を調べています。
  (けんきゅうしゃはどうぶつのちのうをしらべています。)
  - Researchers are studying animal intelligence.
- この授業では人工知能について学びます。
  (このじゅぎょうではじんこうちのうについてまなびます。)
  - In this class, we study artificial intelligence.

人工知能 (じんこうちのう) is "artificial intelligence"; 人工 supplies the
"artificial" part. The complete compound identifies the field.

## How the kanji combine

[知](../kanji/知.md) contributes knowing or understanding, and [能](../kanji/能.md)
contributes ability. Together they name intellectual ability.
The on readings are チ + ノウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[知識](知識.md) (ちしき) is knowledge: things someone knows.
[能力](能力.md) (のうりょく) is ability more broadly, including abilities outside intellectual tasks.
Knowing many facts and being able to reason are related but not identical descriptions.

## Distinctive meaning

English sometimes uses "brains" for intelligence, but 知能 does not name the physical
organ. Nor does it by itself specify a test score, a particular theory of intelligence,
or a person's worth. The context tells you what aspect of intellectual ability is being discussed.
