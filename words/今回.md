# 今回 (こんかい) - this time; this occasion

## Meaning

今回 is a noun or adverbial expression referring to this particular occasion. It can describe an event about to happen, taking place now, or just completed, depending on the predicate and context.

- 今回は電車で行きます。 (こんかいはでんしゃでいきます。) - This time, I will go by train.
- 今回の試験は難しかったです。 (こんかいのしけんはむずかしかったです。) - The exam this time was difficult.

Use 今回は to make the occasion the topic and 今回の + noun to identify the event or item belonging to it.

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) contributes "this; present," and [回](../kanji/回.md) counts occurrences or rounds. Together they pick out the current occurrence, not a loosely defined stretch of recent time.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[今度](今度.md) (こんど) overlaps with "this time" and can also refer to a next occasion. [今](今.md) (いま) means "now" more directly. 前回 (ぜんかい), "last time," and 次回 (じかい), "next time," are useful neighboring reference points.

## Distinctive meaning

今回 does not mean "lately" as an unspecified recent period. 今回は参加しません (こんかいはさんかしません), "I will not participate this time," concerns a particular event, not a general recent habit. The contrast with other occasions may be understood without naming them explicitly.
