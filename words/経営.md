# 経営 (けいえい) - business management; running an enterprise

## Meaning

経営 is a noun for managing or running a business or organization. 経営する is transitive: the enterprise being run takes を. 経営者 (けいえいしゃ) names someone responsible for running it.

- 叔母は小さな店を経営しています。 (おばはちいさなみせをけいえいしています。) - My aunt runs a small shop.
- この会社の経営は順調です。 (このかいしゃのけいえいはじゅんちょうです。) - This company's business is running smoothly.

The continuing form 経営している describes an ongoing responsibility, not merely being present at the workplace.

## How the kanji combine

[経](../kanji/経.md) contributes managing or directing in this compound, and 営 contributes operating or carrying on an undertaking. Together they describe running an enterprise. The reading けいえい contains two consecutive vowel sequences; keep the full reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[管理](管理.md) (かんり) concerns management or control and can apply to equipment, schedules, and resources. 経営 focuses on running the enterprise. [営業](営業.md) (えいぎょう) can concern sales or business operations; a shop being open for business does not identify who runs it.

## Distinctive meaning

経営する does not simply mean "work at." Someone may work in a restaurant without managing its overall business. It also does not by itself specify legal ownership: the sentence identifies the running of the enterprise, while ownership and job title may need separate explanation.
