# 普及 (ふきゅう) - spread; popularization

## Meaning

普及 is the spread or popularization of something so that it
becomes widely known, used, or available. 普及する has both
intransitive and transitive uses.

- この技術は多くの国に普及しています。 (このぎじゅつはおおくのくににふきゅうしています。) - This technology is in widespread use in many countries.
- その団体は地域にスポーツを普及しています。 (そのだんたいはちいきにスポーツをふきゅうしています。) - The organization is promoting the spread of sports in the community.

In the first, 技術 is what has spread. In the second, the
organization spreads sports, marked by を. 普及させる is also
available when explicitly expressing causing something to spread;
the causative is not the only transitive construction.

## How the kanji combine

[普](../kanji/普.md) contributes general or widespread reach,
and 及 reaching or extending to something. Together they suggest
reaching widely. The on readings are フ + キュウ; keep the
long vowel in きゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[広める](広める.md) (ひろめる) means disseminating or broadening
something. [広がる](広がる.md) (ひろがる) can describe something
spreading. [流行](流行.md) (りゅうこう) concerns fashion or
prevalence; wide adoption does not necessarily make something a
temporary fashion.

## Distinctive meaning

普及 often concerns knowledge, activities, products, or technologies
reaching more people. It is not a general replacement for every
physical spreading action, such as opening out a cloth.
Conversely, something need not be new or fashionable to 普及する.
The word alone does not establish a precise adoption rate.
