# 答案 (とうあん) - exam answers; answer script

## Meaning

答案 is a noun for an examination answer script or answer sheet, and for an answer to an exam question. It focuses on the student's response, not merely the questions printed on the test.

- 試験が終わったら、答案を出してください。 (しけんがおわったら、とうあんをだしてください。) - When the exam is over, please hand in your answer script.
- 先生が答案を採点しています。 (せんせいがとうあんをさいてんしています。) - The teacher is marking the exam answers.

答案を書く (とうあんをかく) means "write an exam answer." Depending on context, the noun can emphasize what was written or the submitted paper containing it.

## How the kanji combine

[答](../kanji/答.md) means "answer," and 案 contributes a plan, draft, or written proposal. Together they identify a formulated answer. The component association does not mean that the submitted work must still be an unfinished draft.

## Synonyms and antonyms

答え (こたえ) is an answer more generally, including outside examinations. [書く](書く.md) (かく), "write," is an action performed when preparing an answer. 問題 (もんだい), "question; problem," is what is answered, not a synonym for the answer script.

## Distinctive meaning

English "exam paper" can refer either to a question paper or to completed student work. The second interpretation is the useful guide for 答案. Keep that distinction when a teacher asks students to submit or reviews and grades their work.
