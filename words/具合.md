# 具合 (ぐあい) — condition; state of health

## Meaning

具合 is the condition of a person or thing, including someone's health or how well a machine works. It is a noun, commonly followed by がいい or が悪い.

- 今日は具合が悪いです。 (きょうはぐあいがわるいです。) — I feel unwell today.
- エアコンの具合はどうですか。 (エアコンのぐあいはどうですか。) — How is the air conditioner working?

## How the kanji combine

[具](../kanji/具.md) is read ぐ, and [合](../kanji/合.md) is あい. This is an on-plus-kun combination, categorized here as kango because the on-reading comes first. Learn the established whole-word meaning rather than deriving "health" from either kanji alone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

調子 (ちょうし) also describes condition or how something is functioning. 病気 (びょうき), "illness," is more specific: saying 具合が悪い does not name or diagnose a disease.

## Distinctive meaning

The condition need not be medical. An object, process, or arrangement can also have a 具合. こんな具合に means "in this way," illustrating its related manner sense. Context and the accompanying adjective determine whether the condition is good or bad.
