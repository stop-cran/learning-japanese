# 見舞う (みまう) -- visit in sympathy; strike

## Meaning

見舞う means visiting or asking after someone out of concern, for
example a person in hospital or affected by a disaster. The concern can
also be conveyed by an inquiry or message, not necessarily a visit in
person.

- 入院中の友人を見舞いました。 (にゅういんちゅうのゆうじんをみまいました。) -- I visited a friend who was in hospital.

A further use means attacking, hitting or dealing a blow. Disasters are
also described with the passive 見舞われる:

- その町は大雨に見舞われました。 (そのまちはおおあめにみまわれました。) -- The town was hit by heavy rain.

It is a transitive godan verb: 見舞います, 見舞わない, 見舞って
and 見舞った (みまいます・みまわない・みまって・みまった).

## How the kanji combine

[見](../kanji/見.md) writes み and [舞](../kanji/舞.md) writes ま;
う completes the native verb. Do not translate the whole word as
"watch a dance" from separate character glosses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [見舞い](見舞い.md) and [お見舞い](お見舞い.md) (みまい・おみまい) name a get-well visit or expression of sympathy.
- [訪ねる](訪ねる.md) (たずねる) and [訪問](訪問.md) (ほうもん) are broader visiting words.
- [慰める](慰める.md) (なぐさめる) emphasizes comforting or consoling.
- [打つ](打つ.md) (うつ) and [攻撃](攻撃.md) (こうげき) overlap with the striking or attack use, not the sympathetic visit.

## Distinctive meaning

The comforting and harmful uses must be read in their constructions.
友人を見舞う normally concerns the friend's welfare; 大雨に見舞われる
does not mean the rain paid a sympathetic visit. Nor should 見舞う
replace every ordinary social visit merely because an older dictionary
quotation uses a broader visiting sense.
