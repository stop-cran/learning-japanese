# 直径 (ちょっけい) - diameter

## Meaning

直径 is a diameter. For a circle, it is a line segment through the center
joining two points on the circle, or the length of that segment.

- この円の直径は十センチです。
  (このえんのちょっけいはじゅっセンチです。) - This circle has a diameter of ten centimeters.
- 直径二十センチの皿を使います。
  (ちょっけいにじゅっセンチのさらをつかいます。) - We use a plate twenty centimeters in diameter.

直径 + measurement + の + noun is a useful size description.

## How the kanji combine

[直](../kanji/直.md) contributes straightness,
and 径 a path or distance across.
The established compound names the diameter.
チョク + ケイ becomes ちょっけい, with small っ;
do not pronounce the whole word ちょくけい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

半径 (はんけい), radius, is the distance from the center to the circle.
A circle's diameter is twice its radius.
[円周](円周.md) (えんしゅう) is the circumference, not the distance across.

## Distinctive meaning

The center matters: a line segment joining two circle points without passing
through the center is not necessarily a diameter.
The term also applies to spheres, but should not be reduced to an
unqualified rule meaning the widest distance across every possible shape.
Check which object or geometrical definition the measurement concerns.
