# どうしても (どうしても) - no matter what

## Meaning

どうしても is an adverbial expression whose force depends on the predicate. With a wish or determination, it means "no matter what" or "really must." With a negative, it can mean that something simply will not happen despite effort.

- どうしてもこの本を読みたいです。 (どうしてもこのほんをよみたいです。) - I really want to read this book, no matter what.
- 名前がどうしても思い出せません。 (なまえがどうしてもおもいだせません。) - I just cannot remember the name, however hard I try.

It can also present an unavoidable circumstance: どうしても行かなければならない, "I have to go, whether I want to or not."

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is hiragana. Think of the expression as considering whatever way one tries, but learn its whole-sentence effect. It is not the question どうして, "why," with an extra question meaning supplied by も.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぜひ](ぜひ.md) expresses a strong wish or invitation. [必ず](必ず.md) (かならず) stresses certainty or an obligation being fulfilled. Neither automatically reproduces どうしても with a negative ability form.

## Distinctive meaning

Polarity matters: どうしてもできない means "cannot do it despite everything," not "can do it by all means." A negative volitional context can instead express refusal. Check whether the sentence describes inability, necessity, or the speaker's determination before choosing the English translation.
