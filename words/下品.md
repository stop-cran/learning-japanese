# 下品 (げひん) - vulgar; indecent

## Meaning

下品 describes something vulgar, indecent, coarse, or lacking refinement.
It is a noun and a na-adjective: 下品な before a noun, 下品に when modifying behavior.

- 下品な冗談はやめてください。 (げひんなじょうだんはやめてください。) - Please stop making vulgar jokes.
- その話し方は下品に聞こえます。 (そのはなしかたはげひんにきこえます。) - That way of speaking sounds crude.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md) supplies a low evaluation; [品](../kanji/品.md) contributes quality or refinement.
The combination concerns a lack of refinement here, not simply objects located below other objects.
The on readings combine as ゲ + ヒン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[汚い](汚い.md) (きたない) can also mean vulgar or indecent in a suitable context, not only physically dirty.
[粗い](粗い.md) (あらい) and [荒い](荒い.md) (あらい) have broader roughness uses.
上品 (じょうひん), refined or elegant, supplies the useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

This is an evaluative, potentially insulting description, not a neutral category of people.
Physical coarseness, low price, unkindness, and vulgar behavior are not automatically the same thing.
The source list's "meanness" should not be learned here as a general word for stinginess.
