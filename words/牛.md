# 牛 (うし) - cattle; cow

## Meaning

牛 is a noun for cattle or an individual cow, bull, or ox. The everyday animal name is **うし**. Japanese nouns do not require a plural ending, so context tells you whether one animal or several are meant.

- 牧場で牛を見ました。 (ぼくじょうでうしをみました。) - I saw cattle at a farm.
- 牛が草を食べています。 (うしがくさをたべています。) - A cow is eating grass.

Large animals such as cattle can be counted with 頭 (とう): 牛が二頭 (うしがにとう), "two cattle."

## How the kanji combine

The single [牛](../kanji/牛.md) character writes the native noun うし. Its on reading ギュウ appears in compounds such as [牛肉](牛肉.md) (ぎゅうにく), "beef." Recognizing that reading does not change the standalone animal name in these examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

牛肉 names the meat, while 牛 in the examples names the animal. 雌牛 (めうし) specifies a female cow and 雄牛 (おうし) a bull. The broad word 牛 does not itself make that distinction.

## Distinctive meaning

English "cow" may specifically mean a female, but 牛 can refer to cattle without specifying sex. In food contexts 牛 can also stand for beef, particularly with the reading ぎゅう. Use context and the actual reading rather than assuming that every occurrence describes a live animal.
