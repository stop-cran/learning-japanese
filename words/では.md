# では - well then

## Meaning

As a conversational connector, では means "well then" or "in that case." It moves from the current situation to a decision, next action, or farewell.

では、始めましょう。 (では、はじめましょう。) - Well then, let's begin.

では、また明日。 (では、またあした。) - Well then, see you tomorrow.

## How the kanji combine

では is written in kana and pronounced **でわ**: the は is pronounced like the topic particle. Its form combines で and は. The frontmatter retains the standard written form では.

## Synonyms and antonyms

それでは is a fuller connector with much the same function. じゃ or じゃあ is a casual contracted counterpart. There is no direct antonym; other connectors express different relationships between statements.

## Distinctive meaning

The same written sequence also occurs as particles after a noun. 日本では (にほんでは), "in Japan," combines location で with topic or contrast は; it does not mean "Japan, well then." Learn the sentence-level connector separately from these attached uses and from ではない, "is not."
