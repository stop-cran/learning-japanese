# 公害 (こうがい) - pollution; environmental nuisance

## Meaning

公害 is harmful pollution or a public environmental nuisance arising
from human activities, including business activities.
It is a noun. The harm concerns people's living environment, health,
or property, rather than every private annoyance.

- 工場の排水が公害の原因になりました。 (こうじょうのはいすいがこうがいのげんいんになりました。) - Wastewater from the factory became a cause of pollution.
- 住民は公害を減らす対策を求めています。 (じゅうみんはこうがいをへらすたいさくをもとめています。) - Residents are calling for measures to reduce environmental pollution.

公害 includes more than chemical contamination: noise, vibration,
offensive odors, and ground subsidence can also be relevant.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md) contributes public, and [害](../kanji/害.md)
contributes harm. The combination concerns harm to the public or its
environment. The on readings are コウ + ガイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[汚染](汚染.md) (おせん) is pollution or contamination.
[騒音](騒音.md) (そうおん) is noise and can be a form of 公害.
[迷惑](迷惑.md) (めいわく) is broader nuisance or inconvenience;
not every 迷惑 is an environmental problem.

## Distinctive meaning

Public harm does not mean that the polluter must be publicly owned.
Do not reduce 公害 to litter alone or to every disagreeable experience.
[郊外](郊外.md) (こうがい), suburbs or outskirts, is an unrelated homophone.
