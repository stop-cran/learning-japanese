# 状態 (じょうたい) — state or condition

## Meaning

状態 describes the state or condition something is in.
Use noun + の状態 to identify the thing whose condition matters.

- 車の状態を調べます。 (くるまのじょうたいをしらべます。) — I will check the car's condition.
- この本はよい状態です。 (このほんはよいじょうたいです。) — This book is in good condition.

状態 does not itself mean good or bad. A description such as よい supplies that evaluation.

## How the kanji combine

[状](../kanji/状.md) contributes condition or form, and 態 contributes condition or appearance.
The similar meanings reinforce one another rather than forming an action-and-object phrase.
Their on readings are ジョウ + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

状況 (じょうきょう) concerns a situation or state of affairs.
It can overlap with 状態, but provides a useful way to focus on surrounding circumstances rather than just one object's condition.
調子 (ちょうし) also has a condition sense: 車の調子 (くるまのちょうし) can focus on how the car is running.
These are differences of focus, not absolute boundaries forbidding overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

状態 lets you describe how something is without claiming why it became that way.
Checking a car's 状態 may lead to an explanation, but the word itself names the condition,
not its cause. It can apply to things and people; it does not specifically label an illness or defect.
