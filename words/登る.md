# 登る (のぼる) — climb; go up

## Meaning

登る means to climb or ascend. Mountains and upward routes provide clear examples.
It is a godan verb: despite ending in る, its polite form is 登ります (のぼります), not 登ます.
The past is 登った (のぼった).

- 友達と山に登りました。 (ともだちとやまにのぼりました。) — I climbed a mountain with a friend.
- 急な坂を登りました。 (きゅうなさかをのぼりました。) — I climbed a steep slope.

The dictionary classifies 登る as intransitive. The を in the second example marks the route traversed,
not an object that the climber changes.

## How the kanji combine

[登](../kanji/登.md) is read のぼ in 登る; る is written in kana.
It is not read トウ in this verb.
登山 (とざん), "mountain climbing," is a separate compound with on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上る (のぼる) shares the upward-movement sense, but it has additional uses that do not transfer freely to 登る.
Compare 日が昇る (ひがのぼる), "the sun rises"; the dictionary also allows 上る for this natural-rising sense, but not 登る.
下りる (おりる), "descend," supplies a useful contrast when coming down a mountain.

## Distinctive meaning

The 登 spelling is a useful anchor for a physical climb.
Do not choose it merely because an English sentence contains "rise" or "go up."
Keep both spelling and construction with the example: 山に登る (やまにのぼる) and 坂を登る (さかをのぼる).
