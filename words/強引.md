# 強引 (ごういん) - pushy; coercive

## Meaning

強引 describes pushing something through forcefully, high-handedly, or
without due regard for resistance and circumstances.
It is a noun and a na-adjective: 強引な before a noun, 強引に
before an action.

- 彼は反対を無視して、計画を強引に進めました。 (かれははんたいをむしして、けいかくをごういんにすすめました。) - He ignored the opposition and pushed the plan through forcefully.
- 強引な勧誘は断りました。 (ごういんなかんゆうはことわりました。) - I turned down the pushy sales approach.

Forcefulness can be social or procedural; physical force is not required.
The word usually conveys criticism rather than simply praising determination.

## How the kanji combine

[強](../kanji/強.md) contributes force or strength, and
[引](../kanji/引.md) contributes pulling.
As a mnemonic, imagine pulling something forward despite resistance.
This is a learning image, not a verified historical derivation.
The on readings are ゴウ + イン, not きょういん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[無理](無理.md) (むり) includes unreasonable or forcible action as
well as impossibility. [ぐいぐい](ぐいぐい.md) describes a forceful manner.
[厚かましい](厚かましい.md) (あつかましい) concerns shamelessness or
brazen behavior; it can accompany pushiness without being identical in every use.

## Distinctive meaning

Being [強い](強い.md) (つよい), strong, is not automatically 強引.
A strong argument can respect the other person's judgment.
強引 instead draws attention to how a person or action overrides resistance
or reasonable considerations.
