# 寝間着 (ねまき) - nightclothes; sleepwear

## Meaning

寝間着 means nightclothes or sleepwear: clothing worn for sleeping.
寝間着, 寝巻 and 寝巻き are spellings of this same word,
all read **ねまき** and all referring to nightclothes.
They do not name separate garment categories.

- 寝間着に着替えました。
  (ねまきにきがえました。) - I changed into my nightclothes.
- 旅行に寝巻きを持っていきます。
  (りょこうにねまきをもっていきます。) - I will take my nightclothes on the trip.

に marks the clothing changed into; を marks what is taken.
The different written forms in the examples do not change the word's reading.

## How the kanji combine

[寝](../kanji/寝.md) has the sleeping association,
[間](../kanji/間.md) has a room or space association, and
[着](../kanji/着.md) contributes clothing.
Learn ねまき as the whole native word rather than reading the three characters
independently from their on readings.
The alternate spellings 寝巻 and 寝巻き use [巻](../kanji/巻.md),
which has a winding or wrapping association; this spelling association
does not by itself establish the word's historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

パジャマ names pajamas and overlaps with sleepwear.
寝間着 is not limited by the word itself to a two-piece pajama set.
[服](服.md) (ふく) is broader clothing; not every item of clothing is nightclothes.
There is no single antonym covering every kind of daytime clothing.

## Distinctive meaning

The category concerns what the clothing is for, not its fabric or price.
A dictionary gloss such as "nightgown" supplies one possible English equivalent,
not a rule that 寝間着 must be a gown.
Treat 寝巻 (ねまき) as a spelling alternative, not as the separate verb
[巻く](巻く.md) (まく), "wind; wrap."
