# 一方 (いっぽう) - one side; on the other hand

## Meaning

一方 can name one side or one of two things: 一方の手 (いっぽうのて), "one hand."
As a connector, it introduces another side of a comparison, translated "on the other hand,"
"whereas," or "meanwhile."

- 姉は東京で働いている。一方、妹は大阪で勉強している。
  (あねはとうきょうではたらいている。いっぽう、いもうとはおおさかでべんきょうしている。)
  - My older sister works in Tokyo. Meanwhile, my younger sister studies in Osaka.
- この町は便利な一方、家賃が高い。
  (このまちはべんりないっぽう、やちんがたかい。) - This town is convenient, but rents are high.

Inside a sentence, attach this contrasting 一方 to a plain-form verb or i-adjective;
use な or である after a na-adjective, and である after a noun.

A different pattern, change verb in dictionary form + 一方だ, describes a continuing
trend: 物価は上がる一方だ (ぶっかはあがるいっぽうだ), "Prices just keep rising."

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," modifies [方](../kanji/方.md), "side; direction."
The combined on reading is いっぽう, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しかし](しかし.md), "however," can introduce an objection or contrast. 一方 is also useful for
placing two parallel situations side by side, without making one refute the other.
[両方](両方.md) (りょうほう), "both sides," contrasts with the noun's "one side."

## Distinctive meaning

Separate comparison from continuing change. 高い一方で contrasts another feature with
being expensive; 高くなる一方だ says the price keeps increasing. 一方 is not a general
replacement for English "only" in unrelated constructions.
