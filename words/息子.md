# 息子 (むすこ) — son

## Meaning

息子 means a son. It identifies a family relationship, so the son need not be a young child. It is a standard nonhonorific word used when speaking about one's own son.

- 息子は大学で勉強しています。  
  むすこはだいがくでべんきょうしています。  
  "My son is studying at university."
- 息子と一緒に料理をしました。  
  むすこといっしょにりょうりをしました。  
  "I cooked together with my son."

## How the kanji combine

[息](../kanji/息.md) and [子](../kanji/子.md) write the native word むすこ. 子 contributes こ, "child"; learn むす as part of this word rather than reading 息 with its standalone reading いき, "breath".

## Synonyms and antonyms

息子さん (むすこさん) is the usual respectful form when referring to someone else's son. 娘 (むすめ), "daughter", is the paired family term. [子供](子供.md) (こども), "child", does not specify son or daughter.

## Distinctive meaning

In family-vocabulary lists, "humble" often signals the form used for one's own family. 息子 is also available for neutral third-person reference; 息子さん adds respect when referring to someone else's son.
