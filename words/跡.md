# 跡 (あと) - trace; remains

## Meaning

跡 is a noun for a trace, track, or mark left behind. It also names the site or remains of something that used to be there. Use **Nの跡** for what left the trace, and **跡が残る** (あとがのこる) for a trace remaining.

- 雪に足の跡が残っています。 (ゆきにあしのあとがのこっています。) - Footprints remain in the snow.
- ここは学校の跡です。 (ここはがっこうのあとです。) - This is the site of a former school.

A mark left by an injury can be a scar: 傷の跡 (きずのあと). The spelling 痕 is particularly associated with scars, but this is a spelling preference, not a restriction excluding 跡.

## How the kanji combine

跡 is one kanji supplying the native noun あと. There is no compound to divide. Think of evidence left after something has passed or disappeared.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[印](印.md) (しるし), "mark; sign," can be deliberately placed; 跡 highlights what remains. [傷](傷.md) (きず) is the injury or damage itself, rather than necessarily its later scar.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not confuse this noun with [後](後.md) (あと), "after; later." 食事の後 (しょくじのあと) means "after the meal"; 食事の跡 means traces left by the meal. The same pronunciation does not make their spellings interchangeable.
