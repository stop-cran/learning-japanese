# 空気 (くうき) - air; atmosphere

## Meaning

空気 means air, including the air in a room or outdoors. It can also describe the social atmosphere or mood of a situation.

- 窓を開けて、空気を入れ替えましょう。 (まどをあけて、くうきをいれかえましょう。) - Let's open the window and let in fresh air.
- 部屋の空気が変わりました。 (へやのくうきがかわりました。) - The atmosphere in the room changed.

The second sentence may concern mood rather than the physical air; context decides.

## How the kanji combine

[空](../kanji/空.md), space or sky, combines with [気](../kanji/気.md), associated with air and states of feeling. The on readings are クウ + キ; keep the long う in くう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

風 (かぜ) is wind, moving air. 雰囲気 (ふんいき) overlaps with the mood or atmosphere sense, not with the substance people breathe.

## Distinctive meaning

空気を読む (くうきをよむ), literally "read the atmosphere," means picking up on the social situation. It does not mean reading weather information or measuring air quality.
