# なんとなく - for some reason; without a clear purpose

## Meaning

なんとなく is an adverb for a feeling or action whose reason is not clear:
"for some reason," "without knowing why," or "without any particular purpose."
It modifies the following expression directly, without an extra に.

- 今日はなんとなく元気が出ません。 (きょうはなんとなくげんきがでません。) - For some reason, I do not feel energetic today.
- なんとなくこの店に入りました。 (なんとなくこのみせにはいりました。) - I went into this shop without any particular reason.

The speaker need not be claiming that there is no cause at all; they may simply
be unable to identify or explain it.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The spelling 何となく uses [何](../kanji/何.md), here read なん.
Learn the established adverb as a whole, not as an instruction to add なく to
an arbitrary question word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[どうも](どうも.md) can also introduce an unexplained impression.
[ぼんやり](ぼんやり.md) emphasizes vagueness or absentmindedness rather than
necessarily an unexplained reason.

## Distinctive meaning

English "somehow" has more than one use. [何とか](何とか.md) (なんとか) can
describe managing something one way or another despite difficulty.
なんとなく does not by itself say that an obstacle was overcome.
The shop sentence describes a loosely motivated choice, not a successful
effort to get through its door.
