# 加熱 (かねつ) - heating; applying heat

## Meaning

加熱 is heating or the application of heat. It is a noun and takes する; Xを加熱する means to heat X.

- 牛乳を鍋に入れて、弱火で加熱します。 (ぎゅうにゅうをなべにいれて、よわびでかねつします。) - Put the milk in a pan and heat it over low heat.
- 実験で金属を加熱しました。 (じっけんできんぞくをかねつしました。) - We heated a metal in the experiment.

The word identifies applying heat, not a particular appliance or a fixed target temperature.

## How the kanji combine

[加](../kanji/加.md) contributes adding, and [熱](../kanji/熱.md) heat. This verb-object combination presents adding or applying heat. The on readings are カ + ネツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暖める](暖める.md) (あたためる), also written 温める, overlaps in heating or warming. [沸かす](沸かす.md) (わかす) is used for heating or boiling water and similar liquids. 冷却 (れいきゃく), cooling, is a useful opposite process.

## Distinctive meaning

加熱 does not by itself say "bring to the boil" or "make comfortably warm." It concerns supplying heat; the material and purpose determine the result. 過熱 (かねつ), overheating, is a different homophone and adds the idea of excessive heat.
