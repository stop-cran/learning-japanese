# 味噌 (みそ) — miso paste

## Meaning

味噌 is miso, a fermented seasoning paste usually made with soybeans. It is used in soups, sauces, and other dishes; it is not the name for every kind of bean paste.

この料理に味噌を入れます。（このりょうりにみそをいれます。） — I add miso to this dish.

## How the kanji combine

[味](../kanji/味.md) means taste or flavor. 噌 is especially familiar in this word. The conventional spelling uses the on readings ミ and ソ, but is a phonetic spelling, not a literal recipe or proof of the word's historical origin. Learn the food name as a whole.

## Synonyms and antonyms

醤油（しょうゆ）, soy sauce, is another fermented seasoning, but its flavor and liquid form differ. あん is the bean paste used in many sweets, not a synonym for 味噌.

## Distinctive meaning

味噌汁（みそしる） is miso soup. Different types of miso vary in color, sweetness, and saltiness, so 味噌 does not describe one fixed color or recipe.
