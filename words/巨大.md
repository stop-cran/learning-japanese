# 巨大 (きょだい) - enormous; gigantic

## Meaning

巨大 is a na-adjective for something enormous in size or scale. Use 巨大な before a noun and 巨大だ or 巨大です as a predicate.

- 港に巨大な船が入ってきました。 (みなとにきょだいなふねがはいってきました。) - An enormous ship came into the harbor.
- その会社は巨大な市場を持っています。 (そのかいしゃはきょだいなしじょうをもっています。) - That company has an enormous market.

The first example describes physical size; the second describes commercial scale. 巨大化する (きょだいかする) means "become huge; grow to an enormous scale."

## How the kanji combine

巨 contributes gigantic size, and [大](../kanji/大.md) contributes bigness. The similar meanings reinforce each other rather than naming two separate dimensions. The on readings are キョ + ダイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大きい](大きい.md) (おおきい) is the broader word for "big." 巨大 makes a stronger size claim. [小さい](小さい.md) (ちいさい), "small," supplies a contextual contrast; 微小 (びしょう), "minute," stresses the other extreme of scale.

## Distinctive meaning

巨大 describes magnitude, not automatically importance, quality, or success. A 巨大な建物 (きょだいなたてもの) is a huge building, but the adjective says nothing about how well it was designed. Unlike the i-adjective 大きい, 巨大 requires な directly before an ordinary following noun: 巨大な建物.
