# 目安 (めやす) - rough standard; guideline; estimate

## Meaning

目安 is a practical reference point, standard, or target used as a guide.
It can also be a rough estimate rather than an exact promised figure.

- 一日一万歩を目安に歩いています。 (いちにちいちまんぽをめやすにあるいています。) - I walk with a target of about ten thousand steps a day.
- この金額は費用の目安です。 (このきんがくはひようのめやすです。) - This amount is a rough guide to the cost.

〜を目安に means "using ... as a guide."

## How the kanji combine

[目](../kanji/目.md) supplies め and [安](../kanji/安.md) supplies やす.
Learn the established word as a reference point; it does not mean
"cheap eyes," and 安 does not make the estimate a guarantee of low cost.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[基準](基準.md) (きじゅん) is a standard or criterion, while
[目標](目標.md) (もくひょう) emphasizes a goal or target.
[見当](見当.md) (けんとう) overlaps with an estimate or rough idea.
These distinctions are tendencies: a goal can itself serve as a 目安.

## Distinctive meaning

A 目安 guides a decision; it need not be a binding requirement, exact
calculation, or maximum limit. The standard/aim and rough-estimate senses
are the focus here, not the separate historical document or abacus uses
recorded in larger Japanese dictionaries.
