# 香り (かおり) - aroma; fragrance

## Meaning

香り is a noun for an aroma, fragrance, or scent. It is useful when describing the character of a smell, especially something appreciated in food, flowers, or perfume. Nの香り identifies its source.

- コーヒーの香りがします。 (コーヒーのかおりがします。) - I can smell the aroma of coffee.
- この花はいい香りがします。 (このはなはいいかおりがします。) - This flower has a lovely fragrance.

香りがする means that a fragrance is perceptible. The person noticing it need not be the grammatical subject. 香りを楽しむ (かおりをたのしむ) means "enjoy the aroma."

## How the kanji combine

香 carries the idea of fragrance. The native noun is read かおり, with り written as okurigana. There is no pair of kanji to combine. The dictionary also records 薫り and 馨り as rare written forms; they are not required replacements.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[におい](におい.md) is a broader useful comparison for "smell." いいにおい can describe a pleasant smell too, so the words do overlap. 香り does not need a negative counterpart to explain its meaning: the absence of a scent is a different idea.

## Distinctive meaning

香り invites attention to the scent itself rather than simply identifying the object producing it. お茶の香り (おちゃのかおり), "the aroma of tea," is therefore a natural description even when no flowers or perfume are involved. Add an adjective when the particular quality matters.
