# 管理 (かんり) - management; control

## Meaning

管理 is management or control of something that needs oversight. 管理する is
transitive: mark the managed resource, facility, or information with を.

- この建物は市が管理しています。 (このたてものはしがかんりしています。) - The city manages this building.
- パスワードを安全に管理してください。 (パスワードをあんぜんにかんりしてください。) - Please manage your passwords securely.

管理している can describe ongoing responsibility, not just one completed task.
管理者 (かんりしゃ) is a manager or administrator; the exact role depends on what
is being managed.

## How the kanji combine

管 contributes overseeing or being in charge here, rather than its concrete
meaning "tube." [理](../kanji/理.md) contributes ordering or putting things into
proper arrangement. Together the compound names management. The readings are
カン + リ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[経営](経営.md) (けいえい) focuses on running a business or organization.
管理 can concern one aspect of that work, such as stock or schedules, and also
applies outside businesses. [支配](支配.md) (しはい) emphasizes rule or domination,
so it is not a neutral substitute in a password instruction.

## Distinctive meaning

管理 does not imply ownership: an organization can manage someone else's property.
It also does not mean "control" in every English expression. Managing health,
money, or data involves maintaining or supervising a state; simply pressing a
button to operate a machine is a different kind of action.
