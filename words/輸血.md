# 輸血 (ゆけつ) - blood transfusion

## Meaning

輸血 is a blood transfusion: introducing blood or blood components
into a person's bloodstream. It is also a する verb.

- 手術中に輸血が必要になりました。 (しゅじゅつちゅうにゆけつがひつようになりました。) - A blood transfusion became necessary during the operation.
- 医師は患者に輸血をしました。 (いしはかんじゃにゆけつをしました。) - The doctor gave the patient a blood transfusion.

In the second example, 患者に identifies the recipient.
輸血を受ける (ゆけつをうける) makes receiving the transfusion explicit.

## How the kanji combine

[輸](../kanji/輸.md) contributes transfer or transport, and
[血](../kanji/血.md) blood: transferring blood.
The on readings are ユ + ケツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

献血 (けんけつ) is donating blood, while 採血 (さいけつ) is taking
a blood sample or drawing blood. Neither is another name for
the recipient's transfusion.
[注射](注射.md) (ちゅうしゃ), an injection, is a broader procedural
comparison, not an interchangeable name for every 輸血.

## Distinctive meaning

輸血 is not limited to whole blood: blood components are included.
The dictionary records both transitive and intransitive する usage;
the surrounding participants tell you who performs or receives
the procedure. This is a vocabulary explanation, not medical advice.
