# 常に (つねに) - always; constantly

## Meaning

常に is an adverb meaning "always," "constantly," or "at all times."
It modifies a predicate and does not conjugate: 常に考える (つねにかんがえる), "keep thinking/always think."
It can describe habitual attention as well as a continuing condition.

- 運転中は常に周りに注意してください。 (うんてんちゅうはつねにまわりにちゅういしてください。) - Always pay attention to your surroundings while driving.
- 彼女は常に新しい方法を探しています。 (かのじょはつねにあたらしいほうほうをさがしています。) - She is constantly looking for new methods.

The first sentence is an instruction; the second describes a continuing habit.

## How the kanji combine

[常](../kanji/常.md) contributes regularity or constancy, with the native reading つね.
The attached に produces the adverbial expression taught here.
Do not add another に after the complete word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[いつも](いつも.md) also means "always" and works in many everyday habitual statements.
[時々](時々.md) (ときどき), "sometimes," contrasts in frequency.
[絶対](絶対.md) (ぜったい), "absolutely," concerns certainty or firmness and is not a general synonym for frequent occurrence.

## Distinctive meaning

Watch the scope of a negative sentence.
常に正しいとは限らない (つねにただしいとはかぎらない) means "not necessarily always correct,"
not "always incorrect."
The adverb itself does not decide whether the claim is literal uninterrupted continuity or a general habit;
the activity and context provide that interpretation.
