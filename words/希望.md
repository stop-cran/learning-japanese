# 希望 (きぼう) - hope; wish

## Meaning

希望 is hope, a wish, or an aspiration. As a noun, it appears in 希望を持つ
(きぼうをもつ), "have hope," and 希望がある, "there is hope."
As a transitive する verb, it takes を for what is desired.

- 将来に希望を持っています。
  (しょうらいにきぼうをもっています。) - I have hope for the future.
- 禁煙の部屋を希望します。
  (きんえんのへやをきぼうします。) - I would like a non-smoking room.

The second example states a preference or request, rather than predicting what will happen.
Forms and applications may ask for 希望, such as a desired date or work location.

## How the kanji combine

希 carries a wishing or hoping association here, and [望](../kanji/望.md) contributes
hope or desire. Their related meanings reinforce one another.
The on readings are キ + ボウ. Do not reduce the whole word to the other sense of 希, "rare."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[望む](望む.md) (のぞむ) is a verb meaning to hope for or desire something.
[期待](期待.md) (きたい) concerns expectation or looking forward to something.
絶望 (ぜつぼう), "despair," contrasts with hope.

## Distinctive meaning

希望 ranges from emotional hope to a practical preference on a form.
Saying 部屋を希望します makes a desired arrangement explicit, but does not establish
that a room has been reserved or that the request can be met.
