# 校舎 (こうしゃ) - school building

## Meaning

校舎 is a school building or schoolhouse. It names a physical building,
rather than the school only as an institution. Nの校舎 identifies whose
school building it is.

- 古い校舎を修理しています。 (ふるいこうしゃをしゅうりしています。) - They are repairing the old school building.
- この学校には校舎が二つあります。 (このがっこうにはこうしゃがふたつあります。) - This school has two school buildings.

## How the kanji combine

[校](../kanji/校.md) contributes school, and 舎 contributes a building
or quarters. This is school plus building: the school building.
The on readings コウ + シャ give こうしゃ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[学校](学校.md) (がっこう) can mean a school as an institution or its
physical premises, so it overlaps without always specifying a building.
[建物](建物.md) (たてもの) is the broader word for a building.
[校庭](校庭.md) (こうてい) is the outdoor schoolyard or grounds.

## Distinctive meaning

A school can have several 校舎, and its grounds can extend beyond them.
Use the surrounding location words to distinguish entering a building from
being somewhere on the school premises. The homophone [後者](後者.md)
(こうしゃ), "the latter," is a different word.
