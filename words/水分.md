# 水分 (すいぶん) - moisture; water content

## Meaning

水分 is water or moisture contained in something. In hydration contexts, it also refers to the fluids a person takes in or loses.

- この果物は水分が多いです。 (このくだものはすいぶんがおおいです。) - This fruit has a high water content.
- 運動の後は、水分を十分に取ってください。 (うんどうのあとは、すいぶんをじゅうぶんにとってください。) - Please drink enough fluids after exercise.

水分を取る (すいぶんをとる) is to take in fluids; it does not require drinking plain water only.

## How the kanji combine

[水](../kanji/水.md) contributes water, and [分](../kanji/分.md) contributes a portion or component. Read スイ + ブン, not the minute-counting reading フン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[水](水.md) names water itself. [湿気](湿気.md) concerns moisture or dampness, often in the air or surroundings; [湿度](湿度.md) is humidity as a degree or measure. These can concern the same water without being universal replacements for the water content of fruit or a person's fluid intake.

## Distinctive meaning

Context explains broad dictionary translations such as "water," "fluid," "humidity" or "juice." 水分 does not indiscriminately name every [液体](液体.md), liquid, or every [飲み物](飲み物.md), beverage. In 水分が多い, the point is the contained moisture, not the number of drinks.
