# 竹 (たけ) - bamboo

## Meaning

竹 is a noun for bamboo, both the plant and its material. It also names the
middle option in the conventional three-tier 松・竹・梅 labels:
松 (まつ), 竹 (たけ), and 梅 (うめ).

- 庭に竹が生えています。 (にわにたけがはえています。) - Bamboo is growing in the garden.
- この竹でかごを作りました。 (このたけでかごをつくりました。) - I made a basket with this bamboo.

に marks where the plant grows; で marks the material in the second example.
A menu's 竹のコース (たけのコース) can instead identify the middle course,
not a meal made from bamboo.

## How the kanji combine

[竹](../kanji/竹.md) alone writes the native noun たけ; there is no compound
to divide. The on reading チク appears in 竹林 (ちくりん), bamboo grove.
The same compound also has the dictionary reading たけばやし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[木](木.md) (き) means tree or wood; it is not another name for the particular
plant bamboo. [松](松.md) (まつ), pine, and [梅](梅.md) (うめ), ume, are different
plants. In the stated ranking convention they instead name the highest and
lowest options, with 竹 between them.

The rank sense is not a general replacement for location words:
[中](中.md) (なか), [中央](中央.md) (ちゅうおう), and
[真ん中](真ん中.md) (まんなか) can describe an inside or central position;
[半ば](半ば.md) (なかば) can identify a halfway point. Their English middle
labels are guarded against the secondary rank sense in this card's quiz
exclusions, not declared freely interchangeable Japanese expressions.

## Distinctive meaning

The plant, material and ranking uses need different contexts. On a menu or
service list, read the stated contents and prices: the conventional ordering
does not guarantee standardized quality or specify what the course contains.
Do not use 竹 as the ordinary word for the middle of a room or a period of time.
