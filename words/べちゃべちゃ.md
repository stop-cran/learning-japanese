# べちゃべちゃ — wet and mushy

## Meaning

Unpleasantly wet, soggy, or messy, especially something that has lost its intended firmness or separateness. It can also describe incessant chatter.

- 水を入れすぎて、ご飯がべちゃべちゃになった。 — I added too much water, and the rice became soggy.
- 雪がとけて、道がべちゃべちゃしている。 — The snow has melted, and the road is slushy.
- 後ろの席で、二人がべちゃべちゃしゃべっている。 — Two people in the seats behind me are chattering away.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[どろどろ](どろどろ.md) emphasizes thick fluid or mud; [べたべた](べたべた.md) sticky contact. [ぱらぱら](ぱらぱら.md) can contrast with soggy rice when describing loose, separate grains.

## Distinctive meaning

べちゃべちゃになる, べちゃべちゃのご飯, and べちゃべちゃした雪 describe a wet state. べちゃべちゃしゃべる is a separate, usually disparaging sound/manner use. The word is not a neutral synonym for “moist”: an attractively moist cake is more likely [しっとり](しっとり.md).
