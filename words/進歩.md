# 進歩 (しんぽ) - progress; improvement

## Meaning

進歩 is progress or advancement toward a better or more developed state. With する, the thing that advances takes が: 技術が進歩する (ぎじゅつがしんぽする), "technology advances."

- 医学は大きく進歩しました。 (いがくはおおきくしんぽしました。) - Medicine has made great advances.
- 毎日練習して、少しずつ進歩しています。 (まいにちれんしゅうして、すこしずつしんぽしています。) - Through daily practice, I am improving little by little.

The noun also follows の in phrases such as 科学の進歩 (かがくのしんぽ), "scientific progress."

## How the kanji combine

[進](../kanji/進.md) means advance; [歩](../kanji/歩.md) concerns steps or walking. Together they suggest moving forward step by step. The readings シン + ポ form an on-reading compound; the meaning here need not involve physical walking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上達](上達.md) (じょうたつ) is especially useful for improvement in a skill. [発展](発展.md) (はってん) can emphasize development or expansion. 退歩 (たいほ) is regression, a direct contrast to advancement.

## Distinctive meaning

[進む](進む.md) (すすむ) can describe a task moving forward without claiming better quality or ability. 進歩 specifically presents change as advancement. Finishing another page of a document and improving one's writing are not the same kind of progress.
