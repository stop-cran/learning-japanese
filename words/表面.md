# 表面 (ひょうめん) - surface; outward appearance

## Meaning

表面 is a surface or exterior. It can also mean what appears outwardly, as opposed to what lies beneath. It is a noun: Nの表面, "the surface of N," and 表面に, "on the surface."

- 机の表面に傷があります。 (つくえのひょうめんにきずがあります。) - There is a scratch on the surface of the desk.
- 表面だけを見て判断しないでください。 (ひょうめんだけをみてはんだんしないでください。) - Please do not judge by appearances alone.

The second example is figurative: it concerns readily visible aspects, not necessarily a material's outer layer.

## How the kanji combine

[表](../kanji/表.md) contributes the outside or visible side, and [面](../kanji/面.md) contributes a face or surface. The first element identifies which aspect or face is meant. The on readings are ヒョウ + メン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[外](外.md) (そと), "outside," can name a location beyond an object or enclosure. 表面 names the object's surface. 内部 (ないぶ), "interior," provides a physical contrast, but it is not interchangeable with every figurative opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

表面 does not necessarily mean the front: a desk's top, a ball's outside, and a material's exposed face can all be surfaces. 表面的な (ひょうめんてきな), "superficial," extends the noun into an adjective describing something that does not go beneath appearances.
