# 苦手 (にがて) - not good at; uncomfortable with

## Meaning

苦手 is a noun and な-adjective meaning not good at something or uncomfortable with it. Mark the activity or thing with が; a verb phrase can be nominalized with の.

- 私は人前で話すのが苦手です。 (わたしはひとまえではなすのがにがてです。) - I'm not good at speaking in front of people.
- 辛い料理は少し苦手です。 (からいりょうりはすこしにがてです。) - I'm not very comfortable with spicy food.

Before a noun, use 苦手な科目 (にがてなかもく), "a subject I struggle with." The second example concerns preference or tolerance, not cooking skill.

## How the kanji combine

[苦](../kanji/苦.md) contributes difficulty or discomfort; [手](../kanji/手.md) has skill-related associations here. Learn the native compound にがて as a whole, not the literal phrase "bitter hand."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[下手](下手.md) (へた) directly evaluates skill. 苦手 can instead express personal unease or dislike.

[得意](得意.md) (とくい) provides the skill/confidence contrast. [嫌い](嫌い.md) (きらい) expresses dislike more directly, but 苦手 is not merely a polite substitute in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

苦手 does not mean total inability. Someone may perform a task while finding it difficult or stressful. When used of a person, it can mean finding that person hard to deal with; it does not describe the person's objective competence. Let the topic establish whether ability, comfort, or preference is involved.
