# 風船 (ふうせん) - balloon

## Meaning

風船 is a balloon used as a toy or decoration.
The reading has a long う: ふうせん.

- 子供が赤い風船を持っています。 (こどもがあかいふうせんをもっています。) - A child is holding a red balloon.
- 部屋を風船で飾りました。 (へやをふうせんでかざりました。) - We decorated the room with balloons.

Useful expressions include 風船を[膨らます](膨らます.md) (ふうせんをふくらます),
inflate a balloon, and 風船が[膨らむ](膨らむ.md) (ふうせんがふくらむ),
a balloon inflates.

## How the kanji combine

[風](../kanji/風.md) contributes wind or air, and
[船](../kanji/船.md) a boat or vessel. Associate the combination
with an air-filled floating object rather than deriving every
modern sense from the characters. The on readings are フウ + セン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

バルーン is another dictionary-listed balloon word.
気球 (ききゅう) names a hot-air or gas balloon, such as a craft
carrying people. [空気](空気.md) (くうき) is air, not the
inflatable object containing it.

## Distinctive meaning

The selected sense concerns a toy or decoration; it does not say
whether the balloon contains air or helium.
JMdict also records an original hot-air/gas-balloon sense, explicitly
marked **dated**. Do not use that historical recognition note to
erase the distinction from modern 気球.
