# 解決 (かいけつ) - solution; resolution

## Meaning

解決 is a noun for the resolution or settlement of a problem. 解決する can be transitive,
"solve something," or intransitive, "be resolved." The particles show which construction is being used.

- みんなで問題を解決しました。 (みんなでもんだいをかいけつしました。) - We solved the problem together.
- 話し合いで問題が解決しました。 (はなしあいでもんだいがかいけつしました。) - The problem was resolved through discussion.

In 問題を解決する, someone acts on the problem. In 問題が解決する, the problem is the subject
of the resolution. 解決していない means that it has not been resolved.

## How the kanji combine

[解](../kanji/解.md) contributes "untie; unravel," while [決](../kanji/決.md) contributes "settle; decide."
The two actions reinforce the idea of working out and settling a difficulty.
Their on readings combine as カイ + ケツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[決定](決定.md) (けってい) is a decision or determination. Choosing a plan does not necessarily
resolve the underlying [問題](問題.md) (もんだい), "problem."
未解決 (みかいけつ), "unresolved," describes the contrasting state; it does not mean that resolution is impossible.

## Distinctive meaning

解決 points to getting a difficulty settled, not just learning information about it.
A correct answer to a vocabulary question is not automatically something to call 解決.
Useful noun combinations include 問題の解決 (もんだいのかいけつ), "resolution of a problem,"
and 解決方法 (かいけつほうほう), "a way to solve it."
