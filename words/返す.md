# 返す (かえす) — return something

## Meaning

返す means to give something back or put it back. It is transitive: the thing returned is marked with を.
The recipient or return destination can take に.

- 友達に本を返しました。 (ともだちにほんをかえしました。) — I returned the book to my friend.
- 借りた傘を返してください。 (かりたかさをかえしてください。) — Please return the umbrella you borrowed.

Its polite form is 返します; its て-form is 返して. The final す changes as part of the godan conjugation pattern.

## How the kanji combine

[返](../kanji/返.md) supplies the returning meaning.
The native reading is かえす, with す written in kana. There is only one kanji in the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

返る (かえる) describes something coming back, rather than someone returning an object.
[帰る](帰る.md) (かえる) is another word, used for going home or returning to one's base.
Compare 本を返す (ほんをかえす), "return a book," with 家に帰る (いえにかえる), "go home."

## Distinctive meaning

English "return" can describe either the giver's action or the returning subject's movement.
返す teaches the first pattern: someone gives or puts something back.
Do not use it as a direct substitute for "I returned home."
