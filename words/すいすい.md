# すいすい — moving or progressing smoothly

## Meaning

Light, unobstructed movement or easy progress.

- 魚が水の中をすいすい泳（およ）いでいる。 — The fish are swimming smoothly through the water.
- 今日は宿題（しゅくだい）がすいすい進（すす）む。 — My homework is going smoothly today.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[するする](するする.md) overlaps in smooth movement. [さくさく](さくさく.md) and [とんとん](とんとん.md) can also describe smooth progress, although their other senses differ.

## Distinctive meaning

すいすい泳ぐ highlights ease, not only speed. In problem-solving or work, the same smoothness is figurative. Do not infer that the task was objectively easy for everyone just because one person progressed すいすい.
