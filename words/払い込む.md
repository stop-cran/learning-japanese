# 払い込む (はらいこむ) - pay in; deposit money

## Meaning

払い込む means to pay money in or make a deposit/payment.
It is a transitive godan verb. The sum or fee takes を, and the
account or destination can take に.

- 会費を払い込みました。 (かいひをはらいこみました。) - I paid in the membership fee.
- 指定の口座にお金を払い込んでください。 (していのこうざにおかねをはらいこんでください。) - Please pay the money into the designated account.

Forms include 払い込みます, 払い込んで, and 払い込まない.
This is not limited to depositing one's personal savings.

## How the kanji combine

払い is the stem of [払う](払う.md) (はらう), "pay."
[払](../kanji/払.md) supplies that payment association, and
[込](../kanji/込.md), in 込む こむ, adds an inward direction.
The native compound names paying in; do not turn every use into
a literal physical movement through an entrance.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[支払う](支払う.md) (しはらう) broadly means pay.
[納める](納める.md) (おさめる) also describes paying amounts due.
[預ける](預ける.md) (あずける) covers entrusting things or
depositing money. 振り込む (ふりこむ) specifically describes a
bank transfer; not every 払い込む use requires that exact method.

## Distinctive meaning

The destination and what is due help identify the transaction.
An instruction to pay a fee in is not necessarily an instruction to
put cash into one's own savings account.
[払い戻す](払い戻す.md) (はらいもどす), "refund," describes
money being returned instead; it is not another spelling of paying in.
