# モダン - modern; stylish

## Meaning

モダン describes something modern or stylish in a contemporary way.
It is a na-adjective: モダンな建物 (もだんなたてもの), "a modern building";
モダンなデザイン, "a modern design."

- このホテルはモダンなデザインです。 (このほてるはもだんなでざいんです。) - This hotel has a modern design.
- 古い家をモダンに改装しました。 (ふるいいえをもだんにかいそうしました。) - We renovated the old house in a modern style.

In the second sentence, に describes the style of the renovation.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana borrowing associated with English "modern."
It has no kanji components; the adjective uses な, not an added い.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[現代](現代.md) (げんだい) names the present or contemporary age;
[近代](近代.md) (きんだい) can name the modern historical period.
[新しい](新しい.md) (あたらしい) means new and can overlap with modern.
These are useful comparisons, not rigidly separated English translations.

## Distinctive meaning

A recently made object is not necessarily モダン in style.
Conversely, an older building may still look モダン.
The word does not by itself mean fashionable with everyone or technologically advanced.
