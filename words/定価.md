# 定価 (ていか) - list price; regular price

## Meaning

定価 is an established selling price: the list or regular price of an item. It need not be the amount paid after a discount.

- この本の定価は千五百円です。
  (このほんのていかはせんごひゃくえんです。) - This book's list price is 1,500 yen.
- 定価より二割安く買いました。
  (ていかよりにわりやすくかいました。) - I bought it for twenty percent less than the regular price.

定価で売る (ていかでうる) means "sell at the regular price." 定価の二割引き (ていかのにわりびき) means twenty percent off that price.

## How the kanji combine

[定](../kanji/定.md) contributes fixing or establishing, and 価 contributes price or value. Their on readings are テイ + カ. The compound identifies a set price, not a rule that the price can never change.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[値段](値段.md) (ねだん) and [価格](価格.md) (かかく) are broader price words. [代金](代金.md) (だいきん) concerns the money paid or payable for something. A discounted purchase can therefore have a 定価 different from its actual 代金.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not automatically read a sale-price label as 定価. Look for what the figure represents: a regular price, a reduction, or the final payment. The homophone [低下](低下.md) (ていか) means decline or lowering and uses different kanji.
