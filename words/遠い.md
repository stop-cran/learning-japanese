# 遠い (とおい) — far away

## Meaning

遠い describes a long distance. Use から to identify the point something is far from.
It is an い-adjective: 遠い町 (とおいまち), "a distant town."

- 駅はここから遠いです。 (えきはここからとおいです。) — The station is far from here.
- 学校は家から遠くありません。 (がっこうはいえからとおくありません。) — The school is not far from home.

## How the kanji combine

[遠](../kanji/遠.md) supplies distance, and い completes the native adjective とおい.
The long vowel is written とお, not とう. The negative begins 遠くない (とおくない), "not far."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[近い](近い.md) (ちかい), "near," is the direct contrast for distance.
遠方 (えんぽう) is a noun for a distant place; it is not an adjective that can simply replace 遠い before any noun.

## Distinctive meaning

The distance is relative to a reference point or practical situation, not a fixed number of kilometers.
Stating ここから, "from here," makes that reference clear and avoids leaving the listener to guess what is far from what.
