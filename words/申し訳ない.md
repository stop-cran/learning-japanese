# 申し訳ない (もうしわけない) - I'm sorry; inexcusable

## Meaning

申し訳ない expresses an apology or the feeling that something is
inexcusable. It can also express apologetic gratitude for a favor.

- お待たせして申し訳ありません。 (おまたせしてもうしわけありません。) - I am sorry to have kept you waiting.
- こんなに手伝っていただいて、申し訳ないです。 (こんなにてつだっていただいて、もうしわけないです。) - Thank you for helping me so much; I feel bad about imposing on you.

The second example conveys gratitude together with concern about the
trouble caused, not an accusation that helping was wrong.

## How the kanji combine

[申](../kanji/申.md) writes もう in 申し (もうし), and
[訳](../kanji/訳.md) writes わけ, an explanation or excuse here.
The expression contains [申し訳](申し訳.md) (もうしわけ), "apology;
excuse," plus ない. 申し訳無い and 申しわけない are recognized spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すまない](すまない.md) and [すみません](すみません.md) share apology
and gratitude uses. [ごめんなさい](ごめんなさい.md) is another apology;
申し訳ありません is useful in a more formal apology.
[ありがとう](ありがとう.md) thanks someone without itself expressing an apology.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every use merely as the emotion "sad" or as sympathy
for someone else's misfortune. This is an apology or an apologetic
response to an imposition. Ordinary polite forms include 申し訳ありません
and 申し訳ございません; they are not categorically ungrammatical
just because the full expression is also treated as an i-adjective.
