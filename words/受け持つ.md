# 受け持つ (うけもつ) - take charge of; be responsible for

## Meaning

受け持つ means taking responsibility for an assigned task, class or area of work.
It is a transitive godan verb ending in つ: the task or group handled takes を.

- 私は午後の受付を受け持ちます。 (わたしはごごのうけつけをうけもちます。) - I will handle reception in the afternoon.
- 田中先生は一年生を受け持っています。 (たなかせんせいはいちねんせいをうけもっています。) - Mr. Tanaka is responsible for the first-year students.

Learn 受け持ちます (うけもちます), 受け持った (うけもった) and 受け持たない (うけもたない).
受け持っている can describe an ongoing assignment.

## How the kanji combine

[受](../kanji/受.md)け contributes receiving or accepting, and [持](../kanji/持.md)つ contributes holding.
The native parts うけ + もつ suggest taking a responsibility into one's hands.
This is a meaning association, not a claim that the job is physically held.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[担当](担当.md) (たんとう) also concerns being in charge of assigned work.
[係](係.md) (かかり) names an assigned duty or the person doing it.
[預かる](預かる.md) (あずかる) overlaps when someone is entrusted with running something,
but also covers keeping belongings or looking after a person.

## Distinctive meaning

受け持つ identifies what someone handles, not necessarily managerial authority.
[責任](責任.md) (せきにん), "responsibility," also concerns accountability, not just the assigned work area.
[任せる](任せる.md) (まかせる), "entrust; leave to," presents assigning the matter to someone;
受け持つ presents the person handling it. They are not a transitive/intransitive pair.
