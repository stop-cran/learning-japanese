# 屑 (くず) - waste material; scrap; trash

## Meaning

屑 is waste material, scraps or dregs. It is usually written in kana as くず or クズ. It can name small pieces left after something is cut or processed.

- 床に木のくずが落ちています。 (ゆかにきのくずがおちています。) - There are wood scraps on the floor.
- 金属のくずを集めて、再利用します。 (きんぞくのくずをあつめて、さいりようします。) - We collect the metal scrap and reuse it.

## How the kanji combine

This is one kanji with the native reading くず, not a compound to split into on readings. There is no 屑 kanji card in the current collection; the source spelling remains in the article and metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[塵](塵.md), here ごみ, overlaps as garbage or refuse. [紙屑](紙屑.md) (かみくず) specifically names waste paper. [残り](残り.md) (のこり) is a broader remainder: something left over is not necessarily waste.

## Distinctive meaning

Scrap can still have value for recycling; 屑 does not require total worthlessness. Applied to a person, くず or クズ is an insulting "scum" or "piece of trash," not a neutral description. This person-targeted sense is additional to the material-scrap meaning.
