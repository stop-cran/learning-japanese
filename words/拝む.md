# 拝む (おがむ) - worship; beg

## Meaning

拝む is a transitive godan verb for paying reverence or assuming a praying
posture, including bringing the palms together. It also means to beg
someone earnestly.

- 仏像を拝みました。 (ぶつぞうをおがみました。) - I paid reverence to the Buddha statue.
- 彼は助けてほしいと両手を合わせて拝みました。 (かれはたすけてほしいとりょうてをあわせておがみました。) - He put his hands together and begged for help.

Forms include 拝みます (おがみます), 拝んだ (おがんだ),
拝んで (おがんで), and 拝まない (おがまない).

## How the kanji combine

[拝](../kanji/拝.md) contributes reverence. The native reading is
おがむ; む is written after the kanji. The compound reading ハイ,
as in [拝見](拝見.md) (はいけん), does not read this verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[祈る](祈る.md) (いのる) focuses on praying or wishing for an outcome.
[頼む](頼む.md) (たのむ) is broader asking or requesting.
Not every request involves the posture or earnestness associated with 拝む.

## Distinctive meaning

There is also a humble "see" use, for something or someone treated as
elevated. JMdict notes that this can be sarcastic in modern Japanese.
It is not a universally safe replacement for ordinary [見る](見る.md)
or polite 拝見する. Decide from the whole expression and tone whether
reverence, begging, or seeing is intended.
