# 面積 (めんせき) - measured area; floor space

## Meaning

面積 is the measured area of a surface or place, including the size
of land and floor space. Typical units are square meters.

- この部屋の面積は二十平方メートルです。 (このへやのめんせきはにじゅうへいほうメートルです。) - This room has an area of twenty square meters.
- 公園の面積を調べました。 (こうえんのめんせきをしらべました。) - I checked the area of the park.

## How the kanji combine

[面](../kanji/面.md) contributes a surface, and [積](../kanji/積.md)
relates to accumulation or a product in measurement. The on readings
メン + セキ form めんせき. This is a word for area, not an instruction
to translate the characters separately.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[広さ](広さ.md) (ひろさ) also describes spatial extent and can refer
to measured area. [体積](体積.md) (たいせき) is three-dimensional
volume. [地域](地域.md) (ちいき) names a region, not its numerical area.

## Distinctive meaning

English "area" can name either a locality or a measurement. 面積 is the
measurement use: a region has a 面積. Do not use cubic meters for area,
or treat it as the ordinary word for an abstract field of activity.
