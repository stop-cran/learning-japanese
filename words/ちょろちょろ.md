# ちょろちょろ — trickling or darting about

## Meaning

ちょろちょろ describes a small, thin flow of liquid or quick, restless movement.

- 壊れた蛇口から水がちょろちょろ流れている。
  — Water is trickling from the broken faucet.
- 子どもが人の間をちょろちょろ走り回っている。
  — A child is darting around among the people.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ちょろちょろ can describe either a small stream or quick, restless movement, depending on the subject.

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding subject distinguishes the small flow of water from the quick movements of a person or animal.
