# 庭 (にわ) — garden; yard

## Meaning

庭 is a garden, yard, or courtyard, such as the outdoor space beside a house.
It need not be translated as a garden full of flowers; "yard" may fit the situation better.

- 子供たちが庭で遊んでいます。 (こどもたちがにわであそんでいます。) — The children are playing in the yard.
- 庭に花を植えました。 (にわにはなをうえました。) — I planted flowers in the garden.

In the first example で marks where playing happens; in the second に marks where the flowers are planted.

## How the kanji combine

This is the native Japanese noun にわ written with one kanji, [庭](../kanji/庭.md).
There are no separate characters to translate and no on-reading combination.
The same character has the on reading テイ in other words, but that does not change this word's reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

庭園 (ていえん) also means a garden or park.
[公園](公園.md) (こうえん) specifically names a public park, so it is not a general replacement for a house's 庭.
These words distinguish kinds of outdoor spaces rather than forming a pair of opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

The English choice between "garden," "yard," and "courtyard" depends on what the place is like.
Do not add flowers or a particular landscaping style just because the Japanese word is 庭.
Also keep 家庭 (かてい), "home; household," separate: it is a whole compound, not another name for the yard.
