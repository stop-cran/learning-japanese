# 河 (かわ) — river

## Meaning

河 is a spelling of かわ, "river" or "stream." For ordinary beginner writing, [川](川.md) is the usual spelling of the same word; 河 is not a different kind of watercourse that requires a separate English translation.

川のそばを歩きました。 (かわのそばをあるきました。) — I walked beside the river.

## How the kanji combine

河 alone carries the native reading かわ here. The character also has the on reading カ in compounds such as 河川 (かせん), "rivers."

## Synonyms and antonyms

川 and 河 share the dictionary entry for かわ. 海 (うみ), "sea," and 湖 (みずうみ), "lake," name other bodies of water rather than opposites of a river.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling 河 is retained here to recognize it when reading, not to replace everyday 川. River names can end in かわ or がわ; learn each name's actual pronunciation rather than treating がわ as the normal standalone reading.
