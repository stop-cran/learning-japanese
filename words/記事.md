# 記事 (きじ) - article; news story

## Meaning

記事 is a written article, news story, report, or account. It is a noun, not the
action "write." Use 記事を読む (きじをよむ), "read an article," or 記事を書く
(きじをかく), "write an article."

- 新聞で新しい図書館の記事を読みました。 (しんぶんであたらしいとしょかんのきじをよみました。) - I read a newspaper article about the new library.
- 旅行についての記事を書いています。 (りょこうについてのきじをかいています。) - I am writing an article about travel.

Nについての記事 makes the topic explicit: "an article about N."

## How the kanji combine

[記](../kanji/記.md) concerns writing or recording; [事](../kanji/事.md) concerns
events or matters. The compound presents a written account of matters or events,
read キ + ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[新聞](新聞.md) (しんぶん) is a newspaper: one newspaper contains multiple 記事.
[記録](記録.md) (きろく) is a record, which need not be an article intended for
readers. [文章](文章.md) (ぶんしょう) describes writing or a passage more generally.
There is no useful general antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

記事 can appear online as well as in print. It does not by itself establish that
the account is accurate or that it reports breaking news. Nor does it translate
the grammatical English term "article" in "a definite article": that is 冠詞
(かんし), a different word.
