# 深まる (ふかまる) - deepen; intensify

## Meaning

深まる means to become deeper, more intense, or more developed.
It is an intransitive godan verb: what deepens takes が.
Forms include 深まります and 深まって.

- 話し合いで、お互いの理解が深まりました。 (はなしあいで、おたがいのりかいがふかまりました。) - Through discussion, our understanding of each other deepened.
- 秋が深まってきました。 (あきがふかまってきました。) - Autumn has been deepening.

The second describes the season advancing, not a physical hole
becoming deeper.

## How the kanji combine

[深](../kanji/深.md) contributes depth.
The native verb is ふかまる; do not insert the on reading シン.
[深い](深い.md) (ふかい) describes being deep, while 深まる
describes a change toward greater depth or intensity.

## Synonyms and antonyms

深める (ふかめる) is transitive: 理解を深める
(りかいをふかめる), deepen one's understanding.
[増す](増す.md) (ます) concerns increasing more generally.
高まる (たかまる) can concern rising levels or heightened
feelings; the words' possible objects and subjects are not identical.

## Distinctive meaning

The increasing depth can concern understanding, relationships,
concern, or a season. It is not necessarily a numerical increase.
Compare 理解が深まる, understanding deepens, with
理解を深める, someone deepens understanding.
The ending る does not make 深まる an ichidan verb.
