# 昔 (むかし) — the old days

## Meaning

昔 means the old days or a long time ago. It can introduce when something happened, or take の before
a noun: 昔の写真 (むかしのしゃしん), "photos from the old days."
In this "old days" use, it does not specify a fixed number of years.

- 昔、ここに学校がありました。 (むかし、ここにがっこうがありました。) — Long ago, there was a school here.
- 祖父は昔の話をしてくれました。 (そふはむかしのはなしをしてくれました。) — My grandfather told me about the old days.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [昔](../kanji/昔.md) writes the native word むかし.
There is no compound structure to decode. In 昔話 (むかしばなし), "folk tale; reminiscence,"
昔 qualifies the kind of story or talk.

## Synonyms and antonyms

過去 (かこ) names the past more generally; 以前 (いぜん) can mean "previously" or "before" a stated point.
昔 frames the time as the old days rather than merely any earlier moment.
[最近](最近.md) (さいきん), "recently; these days," provides a useful temporal contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

昔 can refer to the speaker's own earlier life as well as a distant historical period.
It need not introduce a fairy tale: the school and grandfather examples can be ordinary recollections.
For the exact day before today, use [昨日](昨日.md) (きのう), not the broader 昔.
