# 箒 (ほうき) - broom

## Meaning

箒 is a broom, used for sweeping. The word is usually written in kana as ほうき;
帚 is another kanji spelling to recognize.

- ほうきで庭を掃きました。 (ほうきでにわをはきました。) - I swept the garden with a broom.
- 掃除の後、ほうきを片付けました。 (そうじのあと、ほうきをかたづけました。) - After cleaning, I put the broom away.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single native word, ほうき, not a multi-kanji compound.
The kanji spelling is 箒. Its written shape is not a recipe for how every modern
broom is made.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[掃く](掃く.md) (はく) is the sweeping action; 箒 names the tool.
[ブラシ](ブラシ.md) is a brush, and [掃除](掃除.md) (そうじ) cleaning more generally.
Not every cleaning tool is a broom.

## Distinctive meaning

In ほうきで掃く, で marks the instrument. Do not replace it with を merely
because a broom is involved: 床をほうきで掃く (ゆかをほうきではく) means
"sweep the floor with a broom," with を marking the floor being swept.
