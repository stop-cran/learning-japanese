# 分量 (ぶんりょう) - amount; quantity

## Meaning

分量 is an amount or quantity, for example of an ingredient, writing or work.
Its practical everyday use is a noun. 材料の分量 (ざいりょうのぶんりょう) means
the quantities of the ingredients.

- 砂糖の分量を少し減らしました。 (さとうのぶんりょうをすこしへらしました。) - I reduced the amount of sugar a little.
- この文章は分量が多いです。 (このぶんしょうはぶんりょうがおおいです。) - This text is long in terms of its amount of content.

## How the kanji combine

[分](../kanji/分.md) contributes a part or portion; [量](../kanji/量.md)
contributes quantity or measure. Their combined word concerns how much there is:
ブン + リョウ. It does not necessarily mean one person's allocated share.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[量](量.md) (りょう) is a broad quantity term.
[数](数.md) (かず) emphasizes number; [重量](重量.md) (じゅうりょう) and
[体積](体積.md) (たいせき) specify weight and volume.
A 分量 may be expressed using those measurements, but does not itself fix the unit.
[計る](計る.md) (はかる), including the spelling 量る, is the ordinary measuring verb.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records a transitive 分量する sense, "measure or weigh."
Recognize that additional sense; it is not the default pattern to imitate for
everyday ingredient weighing. 分量を量る (ぶんりょうをはかる), "measure the
quantity," keeps the everyday noun and verb roles clear.
分量が多い concerns how much; whether that amount is excessive depends on the context.
