# 腰 (こし) - lower back; waist; hips

## Meaning

腰 names the lower-back, waist, or hip region of the body. Its English translation depends on the location and activity. **腰が痛い** (こしがいたい) describes pain in the lower back; 腰に手を当てる means to put a hand on one's waist or hip.

- 重い箱を持って、腰を痛めました。 (おもいはこをもって、こしをいためました。) - I hurt my lower back lifting a heavy box.
- 彼女は腰に手を当てました。 (かのじょはこしにてをあてました。) - She put her hand on her hip.

The first example should not automatically be translated as an injury to the hip joint.

## How the kanji combine

腰 is a single kanji using the native reading こし. Learn the whole noun rather than dividing it into separate pronounced components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[背中](背中.md) (せなか) refers to the back more generally; 腰 identifies its lower region in pain descriptions. [腹](腹.md) (はら) names the abdomen, not the same location. Body parts do not require direct antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

A separate use describes resilience or body, especially written コシ: コシのあるうどん (コシのあるうどん), "firm, springy udon." Here the word concerns texture, not noodles having a waist. Do not carry the anatomical translation into that construction.
