# 溜める (ためる) - accumulate; store; leave unpaid

## Meaning

溜める means to collect and keep something so that it builds up,
or to let an obligation remain unsettled. It is a transitive
ichidan verb: what is accumulated or left unpaid takes を.

- 雨水をタンクにためています。
  (あまみずをタンクにためています。) - We collect rainwater in a tank.
- 家賃をためないようにしています。
  (やちんをためないようにしています。) - I make sure not to fall behind on the rent.

Forms include ためます, ためた, ためて, and ためない.
仕事をためる (しごとをためる) means letting work pile up;
deliberately making a useful reserve is not required.

## How the kanji combine

溜 contributes collection or accumulation. It writes た here,
followed by める; the conjugation stem is ため.
Learn the kana spelling ためる alongside 溜める.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[溜まる](溜まる.md) (たまる) is intransitive: 水がたまる
describes water collecting, while 水をためる describes storing it.
[蓄える](蓄える.md) (たくわえる) overlaps with storing up
reserves. 貯める (ためる) is separately recorded for saving money.

## Distinctive meaning

The object distinguishes storing water, building up work, and
leaving rent unpaid. 家賃をためる does not mean putting aside
money to pay the rent. The separate 貯める entry is useful for
financial saving, but dictionary spellings do not create an
absolute prohibition on 溜める in all financial contexts.
