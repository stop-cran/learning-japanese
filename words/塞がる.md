# 塞がる (ふさがる) - be blocked; be closed; be occupied

## Meaning

塞がる describes an opening or passage being blocked or closed, a wound closing, or space or someone's availability being taken up. It is also usually written ふさがる.

- 荷物で通路がふさがっています。 (にもつでつうろがふさがっています。) - The passage is blocked by luggage.
- 両手がふさがっているので、ドアを開けてください。 (りょうてがふさがっているので、ドアをあけてください。) - My hands are full, so please open the door.

## How the kanji combine

塞 supplies the idea of blocking or closing, and がる completes the native verb. No 塞 card is available yet. Learn ふさがる as a whole verb rather than deriving it from another character reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[詰まる](詰まる.md) (つまる) overlaps in being clogged. [閉まる](閉まる.md) (しまる) describes something such as a door closing. The transitive partner [塞ぐ](塞ぐ.md) (ふさぐ) describes blocking something.

## Distinctive meaning

This is an intransitive godan verb despite ending in る: ふさがらない, ふさがります, ふさがって. Compare 通路がふさがる, the passage becomes blocked, with 荷物が通路をふさぐ, luggage blocks the passage. Hands being ふさがっている means they are occupied, not necessarily physically obstructed or injured.
