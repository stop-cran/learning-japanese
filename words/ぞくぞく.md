# ぞくぞく — shivering; thrilled

## Meaning

A shivery feeling from cold or fear, or a thrilling excitement.

- 冷たい風が入ってきて、ぞくぞくした。 — A cold wind came in and made me shiver.
- 大好きな歌手（かしゅ）の声を生（なま）で聞いて、ぞくぞくした。 — Hearing my favorite singer live gave me a thrill.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぶるぶる](ぶるぶる.md) emphasizes visible trembling. [わくわく](わくわく.md) and [うきうき](うきうき.md) overlap in positive excitement, without requiring the shivery impression.

## Distinctive meaning

ぞくぞくする can be pleasant or unpleasant. It is a different lexical use from 続々（ぞくぞく）, “one after another,” as in visitors arriving in succession; identical readings do not make those meanings interchangeable.
