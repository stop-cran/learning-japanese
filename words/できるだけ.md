# できるだけ - as much as possible

## Meaning

できるだけ asks for or describes the greatest feasible extent: "as much as
possible" or "as far as one can." It commonly modifies a following action
or an adverb such as 早く (はやく), "early."

- できるだけ早く来てください。 (できるだけはやくきてください。) - Please come as early as possible.
- できるだけ毎日、日本語を読みます。 (できるだけまいにち、にほんごをよみます。) - I try to read Japanese every day as far as possible.

## How the kanji combine

The kana expression combines できる, "can; be possible," and だけ, "extent;
amount." 出来るだけ is also recorded, but kana is usual.

## Synonyms and antonyms

なるべく similarly means "as far as possible," often presenting a practical
preference or effort. 必ず (かならず), "without fail," is stronger: it does
not leave the same explicit allowance for what is feasible.

## Distinctive meaning

The expression is not restricted to quantity. できるだけ静かに
(できるだけしずかに) means "as quietly as possible."
It sets an achievable limit, so a request using it need not demand an
impossible or absolute maximum.
