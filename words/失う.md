# 失う (うしなう) - lose; be deprived of

## Meaning

失う is a transitive godan verb meaning "lose." Mark what is lost with を. It covers possessions and such things as confidence, opportunities, or someone important to you, rather than just misplacing an object.

- 彼は仕事を失いました。 (かれはしごとをうしないました。) - He lost his job.
- 一度失った信用を取り戻すのは難しいです。 (いちどうしなったしんようをとりもどすのはむずかしいです。) - It is difficult to regain trust once it has been lost.

機会を失う (きかいをうしなう) means to lose an opportunity. 家族を失う (かぞくをうしなう) can describe bereavement.

## How the kanji combine

[失](../kanji/失.md) carries the loss meaning, and う completes the native verb うしなう. Its forms include 失わない (うしなわない), 失います (うしないます), and 失った (うしなった).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[無くす](無くす.md) (なくす) overlaps, especially when something goes missing. [得る](得る.md) (える), "gain; obtain," offers a contrast for things such as opportunities or trust, not a universal opposite in every sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

失う focuses on no longer having something. The grammar does not by itself blame the person who loses it: someone may lose a job or a loved one through events beyond their control.
