# だけど - but; however

## Meaning

だけど introduces a contrast or qualification: "but" or "however." As a sentence-opening conjunction, it responds to the preceding statement. It fits conversational style.

- 行きたいです。だけど、今日は時間がありません。 (いきたいです。だけど、きょうはじかんがありません。) - I want to go. But I do not have time today.
- この店は小さい。だけど、品物は多い。 (このみせはちいさい。だけど、しなものはおおい。) - This shop is small. But it has a lot of goods.

The second statement need not contradict the first; it can simply go against an expectation raised by it.

## How the kanji combine

This kana expression combines the copular form だ with けど. There is no kanji decomposition or loanword origin to supply.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[でも](でも.md) also introduces conversational contrasts. [しかし](しかし.md) is useful in more formal argument or writing. [だから](だから.md), "therefore; so," instead presents a consequence, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish standalone だけど from attaching けど inside a sentence. After a noun or な-adjective predicate, use 学生だけど or 静かだけど. After a verb or い-adjective, use 行くけど or 高いけど, not 行くだけど or 高いだけど. The independent conjunction does not license adding だ to every predicate.
