# 静か (しずか) — quiet; silent

## Meaning

静か (しずか) describes quietness. It is a na-adjective:
use 静かな before a noun, 静かです as a polite statement, and 静かに to describe how an action is done.

- この部屋は静かです。 (このへやはしずかです。) — This room is quiet.
- 静かな場所で勉強します。 (しずかなばしょでべんきょうします。) — I study in a quiet place.
- 静かに話してください。 (しずかにはなしてください。) — Please speak quietly.

The last example asks for quiet speech, not necessarily complete silence.

## How the kanji combine

[静](../kanji/静.md) supplies the quietness meaning and writes しず in this word.
The written か belongs to the word 静か; the な in 静かな部屋 is the connector before the noun.
Do not create an i-adjective form such as 静かい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

うるさい means "noisy; loud" in the relevant contrasting sense; the dictionary marks it as usually written in kana.
無言 (むごん) means not speaking. Someone can be 無言 in a noisy place, so it does not establish that the surroundings are 静か.

## Distinctive meaning

静か describes a state. 静まる (しずまる) means to become quiet,
while 静める (しずめる) means to make someone or something quiet.
These related forms are useful, but replacing 静かです with a verb changes whether you describe a state, a change, or causing a change.
