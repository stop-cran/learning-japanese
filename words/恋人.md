# 恋人 (こいびと) - romantic partner

## Meaning

恋人 is a romantic partner: a boyfriend, girlfriend, lover, or sweetheart. It is a noun and does not specify the partner's gender.

- 週末は恋人と映画を見ます。 (しゅうまつはこいびととえいがをみます。) - I will watch a film with my partner this weekend.
- 恋人から手紙が届きました。 (こいびとからてがみがとどきました。) - A letter arrived from my partner.

と marks the companion in the first example; から marks the sender in the second. 恋人がいる means "have a romantic partner," using the existence verb for a person.

## How the kanji combine

恋 contributes romantic love, and [人](../kanji/人.md) contributes person. This native compound combines こい with ひと, voiced to びと. The whole word identifies a person in a romantic relationship.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[彼女](彼女.md) (かのじょ) can mean girlfriend in the appropriate context, besides its pronoun use. 彼氏 (かれし) means boyfriend. [友達](友達.md) (ともだち) names a friend without itself stating a romantic relationship; it is a contrast, not an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

English "lover" can suggest a specifically sexual or extramarital relationship, but 恋人 need not carry that implication. "Romantic partner" is often a clearer starting gloss. It also does not mean any person one loves: family affection alone does not make someone a 恋人.
