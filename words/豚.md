# 豚 (ぶた) — pig

## Meaning

豚 names a **pig**. ブタ is another way to write the animal name.
The noun itself does not distinguish singular from plural.

- 農場で豚を見ました。 (のうじょうでぶたをみました。) — I saw a pig at the farm.
- この農場では豚を飼っています。 (こののうじょうではぶたをかっています。) — Pigs are kept on this farm.

With [飼う](飼う.md) かう, keep or raise an animal, the animal takes を.
In a food context, 豚 can also mean pork; [豚肉](豚肉.md) ぶたにく
makes the meat meaning explicit.

## How the kanji combine

[豚](../kanji/豚.md) writes the whole native noun ぶた. There is no second
character to divide off. In 豚肉, the animal name modifies
[肉](../kanji/肉.md), meat: native ぶた + on にく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[家畜](家畜.md) かちく, livestock or domestic animals, is a broader category,
not another name for pigs alone. [豚肉](豚肉.md) names pork rather than a living pig.
[牛肉](牛肉.md) ぎゅうにく, beef, and [鶏肉](鶏肉.md) とりにく, chicken meat,
are different ingredients, not antonyms.

[ぶうぶう](ぶうぶう.md) can describe oinking, and also has a child-directed
noun use for a pig. That use is not the same as its honking or grumbling senses.

## Distinctive meaning

Context separates the animal from its meat. 豚を飼う means keeping pigs;
a food label containing 豚 can identify pork. Neither the animal noun nor
the meat noun specifies a cooking method.

Applied to a person, 豚 can be an insult. Avoid using it to describe someone's
appearance; this derogatory use is separate from the neutral animal and food senses.
