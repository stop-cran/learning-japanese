# 儀式 (ぎしき) - ceremony; rite; ritual

## Meaning

儀式 is a ceremony or ritual carried out according to an established form. It is a noun and can describe religious observances or other ceremonial acts.

- 神社で伝統的な儀式を見ました。
  (じんじゃででんとうてきなぎしきをみました。) - I watched a traditional ceremony at a Shinto shrine.
- 儀式は静かに始まりました。
  (ぎしきはしずかにはじまりました。) - The ceremony began quietly.

## How the kanji combine

儀 concerns ceremony or formal observance; [式](../kanji/式.md) concerns a form, method, or ceremony. Their on readings combine as ギ + シキ. The two characters reinforce the idea of a prescribed ceremonial form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[式](式.md) (しき) also names a ceremony, as well as a formula or style in other contexts. [勤め](勤め.md) (つとめ) has a religious-service sense in addition to work or duty. Its ordinary employment meaning is not a synonym of 儀式.

## Distinctive meaning

English "service" can describe a religious ceremony, but it can also mean customer service or help. 儀式 covers the ceremonial use, not every [サービス](サービス.md). Context, rather than the English spelling alone, establishes which sense is intended.
