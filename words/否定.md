# 否定 (ひてい) — denial; negation

## Meaning

否定 is denial or negation. With する, it means to deny or reject something; the content being denied takes を.
It can concern the truth of a statement, not just whether the speaker likes it.

- 彼はその話を否定しました。 (かれはそのはなしをひていしました。) — He denied that account.
- その可能性は否定できません。 (そのかのうせいはひていできません。) — We cannot rule out that possibility.

## How the kanji combine

[否](../kanji/否.md) supplies the negative meaning; [定](../kanji/定.md) contributes determining or deciding.
The combination is a negative judgment: denying, rather than affirming, something. The on readings are ヒ + テイ.
Learn the whole word instead of interpreting it as an instruction to "fix a no."

## Synonyms and antonyms

肯定 (こうてい), "affirmation," is the direct contrast: 肯定する affirms what 否定する denies.
[反対](反対.md) (はんたい), "opposition," concerns being against something.
Opposing a proposal and denying that a reported event happened are different acts, even when both express disagreement.

## Distinctive meaning

否定 does not by itself tell you how forceful or polite the denial is.
In the second example, the negative form 否定できません means "cannot deny," so the possibility is left open.
Do not mistake the negative word inside the sentence for a denial of the possibility itself.
