# 売り切れる (うりきれる) - be sold out

## Meaning

売り切れる is an intransitive ichidan verb: all the available items
are sold, leaving none. The goods or tickets take が.
Useful forms are 売り切れます, 売り切れた and 売り切れない.

- 人気のパンは朝のうちに売り切れました。 (にんきのパンはあさのうちにうりきれました。) - The popular bread sold out during the morning.
- チケットが売り切れる前に買いましょう。 (チケットがうりきれるまえにかいましょう。) - Let's buy tickets before they sell out.

売り切れている describes the resulting sold-out state.

## How the kanji combine

[売](../kanji/売.md) contributes selling. [切](../kanji/切.md)
contributes reaching the end or exhausting the available supply here.
売り (うり) + 切れる (きれる) forms this native compound verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[売り切れ](売り切れ.md) (うりきれ) is the related noun.
[売れる](売れる.md) (うれる), "sell," does not necessarily mean that
every available unit has gone. The transitive counterpart 売り切る
(うりきる) means "sell all of": a seller can 商品を売り切る
(しょうひんをうりきる), while 商品が売り切れる.

## Distinctive meaning

Although English may say "the shop sold out of bread," the subject of
売り切れる is normally the bread or other stock, not the seller.
It is not a passive conjugation of 売る; it is a separate intransitive verb.
