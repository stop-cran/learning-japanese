# 利益 (りえき) - profit; benefit; interests

## Meaning

利益 (りえき) is a noun for profit or gains, and more broadly benefit, advantage, or someone's interests. **利益を得る** (りえきをえる) means "gain a profit/benefit"; **利益が出る** means "make a profit."

- この事業で利益が出ました。 (このじぎょうでりえきがでました。) - This business venture made a profit.
- それは社会全体の利益になります。 (それはしゃかいぜんたいのりえきになります。) - That will benefit society as a whole.

The second example need not describe money. **人の利益を守る** means "protect someone's interests."

## How the kanji combine

[利](../kanji/利.md) contributes advantage or gain, and 益 contributes benefit or increase. The related meanings reinforce each other in リ + エキ. The whole word can apply to business results or a broader favorable outcome.

## Synonyms and antonyms

損失 (そんしつ), "loss," contrasts with financial profit. [損害](損害.md) (そんがい), "damage; loss," can contrast with a benefit, depending on the situation. [得る](得る.md) names the act of gaining rather than the gain itself.

## Distinctive meaning

Profit is not the same as total sales: a business can sell a great deal while having little profit after costs. Likewise, a statement about someone's 利益 need not mean personal selfishness; society or a group can benefit. Let the possessor and context determine whose advantage is being discussed.
