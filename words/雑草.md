# 雑草 (ざっそう) — weeds

## Meaning

雑草 means weeds: plants treated as unwanted growth in a place such as a garden or field.
It is not a name for one particular plant species.
A useful gardening phrase is 雑草を抜く (ざっそうをぬく), "pull out weeds."

- 庭の雑草を抜きました。 (にわのざっそうをぬきました。)
  — I pulled the weeds out of the garden.

## How the kanji combine

[雑](../kanji/雑.md) contributes a miscellaneous or mixed quality, modifying
[草](../kanji/草.md), "grass; herbaceous plants."
Learn the resulting plant word as "weeds," not as a claim that a particular weed must be mixed
with another species. ザツ + ソウ becomes ざっそう, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

草 (くさ) is broader: it can mean grass, herbs, or weeds without specifying their unwanted
presence. 芝生 (しばふ) means a lawn or turf.
The lawn itself and weeds growing in it are therefore different things, even though both
involve low-growing plants.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling plants 雑草 classifies them as weeds in the situation being discussed.
It does not mean every grassy area should be described that way.
For example, 草原 (そうげん), "grassland," names a grassy landscape rather than
automatically judging its plants as unwanted.
