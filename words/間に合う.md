# 間に合う (まにあう)

## Meaning

間に合う means to be in time for something. It can also mean to be enough or serve the purpose.

- 急げば、電車に間に合います。 (いそげば、でんしゃに まにあいます。) — If we hurry, we will make the train.
- 今ある材料で間に合います。 (いまある ざいりょうで まにあいます。) — The ingredients we have now will be enough.

For timing, に marks the event or departure to be reached. For sufficiency, で can mark what will do.

## How the kanji combine

[間](../kanji/間.md) is read ま, followed by the particle に and [合](../kanji/合.md)う (あう), "fit; match." Treat the complete expression as an established verb phrase.

It conjugates as a godan verb: 間に合って (まにあって), 間に合わない (まにあわない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

遅れる (おくれる), "be late," contrasts with the timing sense. 足りる (たりる), "be sufficient," overlaps with the second sense.

## Distinctive meaning

間に合う is not only "arrive early." It means meeting the relevant time limit, even just barely. Something can also 間に合う without moving anywhere when it adequately meets a need.
