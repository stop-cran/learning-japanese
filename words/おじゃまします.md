# おじゃまします (おじゃまします) - excuse the intrusion; I'm coming in

## Meaning

おじゃまします is a polite greeting used when entering someone's home: "Please excuse the intrusion" or "I'm coming in."

- A: どうぞ入ってください。 (どうぞはいってください。) - Please come in.
- B: おじゃまします。 - Thank you; excuse the intrusion.

The guest says it as an acknowledgment of entering the host's space. It is not a claim that the host is genuinely annoyed.

## How the kanji combine

The written form is お邪魔します (おじゃまします). 邪魔 (じゃま) names interference or a nuisance; with お and する it also supplies a visiting expression. The base uses on-readings ジャ + マ, while お and します are Japanese parts of the formula. The kango grouping refers to that base; the kana headword has `kanji: []`.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すみません](すみません.md) can mean "excuse me," but has broader apology and attention-getting uses. [ごめんなさい](ごめんなさい.md) apologizes; it is not itself the standard visiting formula taught here.

[いらっしゃい](いらっしゃい.md), "welcome; come in," is said by the host. It is the other side of the exchange, not a synonym for the guest's greeting.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not use this phrase as a general translation of "excuse me" when asking a stranger for directions. Its visiting use is established as a whole expression. The polite ending ます does not make every literal use of 邪魔 a courteous way to describe another person.
