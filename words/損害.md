# 損害 (そんがい) - damage; loss

## Meaning

損害 names damage or loss, including harm to property or financial interests.
Useful noun constructions are 損害を受ける (そんがいをうける), "suffer damage,"
and 損害を与える (そんがいをあたえる), "cause damage."

- 台風で、店は大きな損害を受けました。 (たいふうで、みせはおおきなそんがいをうけました。) - The shop suffered major damage in the typhoon.
- 事故による損害は百万円でした。 (じこによるそんがいはひゃくまんえんでした。) - The loss caused by the accident amounted to one million yen.

The second example gives a monetary estimate of the loss; 損害 itself need not
include a stated sum.

## How the kanji combine

損 contributes loss or damage, and [害](../kanji/害.md) contributes harm.
Their related meanings reinforce an adverse impact. The on readings are
ソン + ガイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[被害](被害.md) (ひがい) also means damage or harm suffered, often presenting
the affected side of an incident. 損失 (そんしつ) emphasizes loss.
The meanings overlap: an accident can cause both 被害 and 損害, so these are
not mutually exclusive categories.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not translate every English "injury" with 損害. A scraped knee can be described
as a wound or injury, while 損害 directs attention to harm or loss arising from
an event. 損害賠償 (そんがいばいしょう) means compensation for damage; the word
alone does not settle who is legally liable.
