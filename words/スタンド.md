# スタンド (スタンド) - stand; spectator stands; lamp

## Meaning

スタンド is a noun with several uses. It can name spectator stands, a sales stand
or counter, or a support that holds something up.
It can also mean a desk or floor lamp, short for 電気スタンド (でんきスタンド).

- 観客がスタンドから選手を応援しています。
  (かんきゃくがスタンドからせんしゅをおうえんしています。)
  - Spectators are cheering for the players from the stands.
- 暗いので、机のスタンドをつけました。
  (くらいので、つくえのスタンドをつけました。)
  - It was dark, so I switched on the desk lamp.

マイクスタンド supports a microphone. 新聞スタンド (しんぶんスタンド) is a newspaper stand.
In a driving context, スタンド can refer to a ガソリンスタンド, "gas station."
These are distinct uses of the same word, not interchangeable objects.

## How the kanji combine

スタンド is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "stand."
There are no kanji to divide. Some Japanese meanings are understood through longer
expressions, especially the lamp and gas-station uses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

A support may be called a 台 (だい), depending on the object.
[舞台](舞台.md) (ぶたい) is a performance stage: the spectators' スタンド is not
the stage where performers appear.
[ガソリン](ガソリン.md) identifies the fuel in ガソリンスタンド.

## Distinctive meaning

This noun does not replace the verb "stand up," which is 立つ (たつ).
Context selects the object: a light can be switched on, spectators sit in stands,
and a driver stops at a filling station. The English source alone is not enough to choose the meaning.
