# 整数 (せいすう) - integer

## Meaning

整数 is an integer: zero, a positive whole number, or a negative whole number.
For example, -2, -1, 0, 1 and 2 are all 整数; 0.5 is not.

- 零も整数です。 (れいもせいすうです。) - Zero is also an integer.
- 答えを整数で書いてください。 (こたえをせいすうでかいてください。) - Please write the answer as an integer.

で in the second example marks the requested form of the answer.

## How the kanji combine

整 contributes order or completeness, and [数](../kanji/数.md) contributes number.
The modifier-plus-head association helps recall a whole-number value; it does not
restrict the value to a positive number. Read セイ + スウ, with two long vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[小数](小数.md) (しょうすう) concerns decimals; 分数 (ぶんすう) is a fraction.
[数字](数字.md) (すうじ) concerns numerals or numerical figures rather than this
particular class of mathematical values.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep a number's value separate from its written form. The decimal 2.0 still has an
integer value, while 0.5 does not. 整数 does not mean a number that must be greater
than zero, and a minus sign does not prevent a value from being an integer.
