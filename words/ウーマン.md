# ウーマン - woman

## Meaning

ウーマン means "woman." The dictionary notes that it is usually used
in compounds; recognizing it does not mean choosing it as the ordinary
standalone word for every woman.

- 「ウーマン」は「女性」という意味です。 (「ウーマン」は「じょせい」といういみです。) - "Uman" means "woman."
- キャリアウーマンという言葉を聞いたことがあります。 (キャリアウーマンということばをきいたことがあります。) - I have heard the expression "career woman."

The second example illustrates a compound, not a rule that all working
women must be described with that label.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword with no kanji components.
The first ー lengthens ウ: ウー・マ・ン. Learn the Japanese form;
it is not pronounced by reproducing every detail of English "woman."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[女性](女性.md) (じょせい) is a useful general term.
[女](女.md) (おんな) also means woman or female, with its own
register considerations. [男性](男性.md) (だんせい) is the
corresponding male category, not a compound-building rule for ウーマン.

## Distinctive meaning

Knowing the meaning of a loanword does not make it the neutral way to
refer to an unfamiliar person or address someone.
Choose person references for the actual setting rather than replacing
every 女性 with ウーマン.
