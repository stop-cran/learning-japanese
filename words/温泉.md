# 温泉 (おんせん) - hot spring; hot-spring spa

## Meaning

温泉 means a hot spring and also a spa or resort using hot-spring water. 温泉に入る (おんせんにはいる) means to bathe in a hot spring; 温泉へ行く (おんせんへいく) can mean to visit an onsen or hot-spring resort.

- 温泉に入って、疲れが取れました。 (おんせんにはいって、つかれがとれました。) - I had a hot-spring bath, and my tiredness went away.
- 今度の休みに温泉へ行きたいです。 (こんどのやすみにおんせんへいきたいです。) - I would like to visit a hot-spring resort on my next break.

## How the kanji combine

[温](../kanji/温.md), "warm," modifies [泉](../kanji/泉.md), "spring; water source." Their on readings are オン + セン. Read the compound おんせん, not おんいずみ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[泉](泉.md) (いずみ) is a spring or source of water without itself specifying a hot spring. [お風呂](お風呂.md) (おふろ), "bath," is broader in everyday bathing contexts and does not promise hot-spring water.

## Distinctive meaning

The resort sense explains why 温泉へ行く need not describe walking directly to a spring's natural outlet. English "spa" is useful here only with the hot-spring context: 温泉 is not the name for every beauty spa or massage business.
