# きっかけ - trigger; opening; opportunity

## Meaning

きっかけ is something that prompts a start or provides an opening: a trigger, occasion, opportunity, or reason for acting. It is a noun, usually written in kana.

- 旅行をきっかけに、日本語の勉強を始めました。
  (りょこうをきっかけに、にほんごのべんきょうをはじめました。) - A trip prompted me to start studying Japanese.
- 彼は話し掛けるきっかけを探していました。
  (かれははなしかけるきっかけをさがしていました。) - He was looking for an opening to start a conversation.

Noun + をきっかけに presents an occasion that leads to a new action or development. きっかけになる means to become that trigger or opening.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[機会](機会.md) (きかい) and [チャンス](チャンス.md) also name opportunities. きっかけ particularly invites attention to what gets something started. [原因](原因.md) (げんいん) concerns a cause, while [理由](理由.md) (りゆう) concerns a reason; these overlap without making every underlying explanation a starting occasion.

## Distinctive meaning

A small remark can be the きっかけ for an argument without being its whole underlying cause. Conversely, in a conversation, the word can mean a convenient opening or excuse to speak, not an accidental disaster. 切っ掛け is a recorded but rare kanji spelling.

As a cue to begin, きっかけ can overlap [合図](合図.md) (あいず), "signal; cue." A きっかけ need not, however, be a deliberately given signal.
