# だぶだぶ — loose and baggy

## Meaning

だぶだぶ describes clothing that hangs loosely because it is much too large.

- 大きめの服を買ったら、袖がだぶだぶだった。
  — I bought a larger size, and the sleeves were baggy.
- 彼はだぶだぶのズボンをベルトで留めた。
  — He held up his loose trousers with a belt.

## Synonyms and antonyms

だぶだぶ emphasizes excess room and fabric, more than a merely relaxed fit.

## Distinctive meaning

With clothing, だぶだぶ emphasizes excess room and loose fabric rather than a relaxed but well-fitting cut.
