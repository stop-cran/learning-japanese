# 相当 (そうとう) - equivalent; considerable

## Meaning

相当 has several constructions. **Nに相当する** means to correspond to or be equivalent to N.

- この商品券は五千円に相当します。 (このしょうひんけんはごせんえんにそうとうします。) - This gift certificate is equivalent to 5,000 yen.

It can describe something appropriate or proportionate: 年齢相当の知識 (ねんれいそうとうのちしき) means "knowledge appropriate to one's age."

**相当な/相当の + noun** can instead mean "considerable," while the adverb 相当 means "considerably/quite."

- 修理には相当な費用がかかります。 (しゅうりにはそうとうなひようがかかります。) - The repairs will cost a considerable amount.
- 今日は相当寒いです。 (きょうはそうとうさむいです。) - It is quite cold today.

## How the kanji combine

[相](../kanji/相.md) contributes a relation between things, and [当](../kanji/当.md) contributes matching or corresponding. That relationship helps with the equivalence sense. The on readings are ソウ + トウ; the degree meaning should be learned as a whole-word use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[かなり](かなり.md) is close to the adverbial "considerably/quite" sense, but does not replace 相当する in the gift-certificate sentence. [適当](適当.md) (てきとう) can mean appropriate, but has its own range of meanings and does not automatically express equivalence.

## Distinctive meaning

Particles and endings matter. Five thousand yen **に相当する** gives an equivalent value; **相当な** expense describes size without naming an equivalent. Do not reduce every use to "suitable," or treat the degree adverb as if it were a conjugated verb.
