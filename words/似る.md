# 似る (にる) — resemble

## Meaning

似る means to resemble or be similar to someone or something.
For an existing resemblance, use 似ている (にている), or politely 似ています (にています).
The comparison target takes に, not を.

- 妹は母に似ています。 (いもうとはははににています。) — My younger sister resembles my mother.
- この二つの言葉は意味が似ています。 (このふたつのことばはいみがにています。) — These two words have similar meanings.

In 似た名前 (にたなまえ), "similar names," 似た modifies the following noun.

## How the kanji combine

[似](../kanji/似.md) expresses resemblance and is read に in this native verb; る is the written ending.
There is no second kanji to interpret.
The on reading ジ appears in 類似 (るいじ), "similarity."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ) means the same, while [違う](違う.md) (ちがう) can mean differ.
同じ名前 (おなじなまえ), "the same name," is a stronger claim than 似た名前, "similar names."
Something can resemble another thing in one respect and differ in another.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not interpret 似ている as necessarily an action happening at this moment.
Here the ている form describes a state of resemblance.
The verb covers more than visual appearance: the second example compares meanings, not how the written words look.
