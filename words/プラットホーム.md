# プラットホーム - platform

## Meaning

プラットホーム means a platform. A useful everyday scene is the platform
where passengers board a train. **プラットフォーム** is another spelling.

- 電車を降りて、プラットホームで友達を待ちました。 (でんしゃをおりて、プラットホームでともだちをまちました。) - I got off the train and waited for my friend on the platform.
- 電車が来るまで、プラットホームの端から離れていてください。 (でんしゃがくるまで、プラットホームのはしからはなれていてください。) - Please stay away from the edge of the platform until the train arrives.

The word also names a stage or supporting platform.
In computing and business contexts it can mean the environment or
infrastructure on which other work operates:
共通のプラットフォーム (きょうつうのプラットフォーム), "a shared platform."
These extended uses are explained in the public Japanese dictionary.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword. There is no kanji compound.
Recognize both ホーム and フォーム in the full spellings;
their appearance does not give the word the meaning "house."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ホーム](ホーム.md) abbreviates the railway-platform word,
but also represents a separate home-related loanword.
[舞台](舞台.md) (ぶたい) is a performance stage.
[基盤](基盤.md) (きばん) overlaps in the computing-platform or
supporting-infrastructure use, and [台](台.md) (だい) is a supporting stand or base.

## Distinctive meaning

The topic determines the platform: a railway platform is a place,
while a software platform need not be a raised physical surface.
Do not mechanically use the railway sense for every computing occurrence,
or infer that every supporting object is a railway facility.
This page gives practical uses, not all specialized platform terms.
