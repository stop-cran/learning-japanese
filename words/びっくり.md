# びっくり - be surprised; startled

## Meaning

びっくり expresses surprise or a startle reaction. With する, it becomes an
intransitive verb: the person surprised is the subject, and に can mark the cause.

- 大きな音にびっくりしました。 (おおきなおとにびっくりしました。) - I was startled by a loud noise.
- 値段を見て、びっくりしました。 (ねだんをみて、びっくりしました。) - I was surprised when I saw the price.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[驚く](驚く.md) (おどろく) is the corresponding ordinary verb "be surprised."
[ぎょっと](ぎょっと.md) overlaps in being startled, often with a more alarmed impression.
安心する (あんしんする), "feel relieved," names a different reaction, not
the automatic opposite of every surprise.

## Distinctive meaning

The surprise can be pleasant, unpleasant, or simply unexpected.
For "surprise someone," use びっくりさせる: する alone does not make another
person the object. A surprised exclamation such as びっくりした！ means
"That startled me!" without needing an explicit 私.
This native expression is normally written びっくり or ビックリ; preserve the small っ.
The rare kanji spellings 吃驚 and 喫驚 are unnecessary for everyday writing.
