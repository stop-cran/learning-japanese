# 凜 (りん) — dignified; bracing cold

## Meaning

凜 describes a dignified, firmly composed bearing, or cold that feels sharp
and bracing. It can also describe a clear, ringing voice or sound.
Learn the constructions **凜と** and **凜とした + noun**, rather than adding
な as if this were an ordinary な-adjective.

- 凜とした声がひびく。
  (りんとしたこえがひびく。) — A clear, firm voice rings out.
- 凜とした朝の寒気
  (りんとしたあさのかんき) — The bracing chill of the morning.

These short examples come from Daijisen. The second is a noun phrase,
not a complete sentence. In both, とした describes the following noun;
it does not necessarily report an earlier change into that state.
凜と can modify an action. JMdict labels the word a *taru adjective*
and an adverb taking と; that classification does not make 凜 an い-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-character Sino-Japanese word, not a compound.
[凜](../kanji/凜.md) supplies the whole on reading リン and the dignity/coldness
meaning. JMdict also recognizes 凛 (りん) in the same lexical family.
The variant spelling does not introduce another pronunciation or word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[寒い](寒い.md) (さむい) is the broader everyday adjective for cold weather
or surroundings. 凜とした adds the sharp, bracing quality in the morning example;
it is not a neutral substitute for every occurrence of 寒い.
[厳しい](厳しい.md) (きびしい) can describe severe cold, but also strict
standards and harsh circumstances; do not transfer all those uses to 凜.

[澄む](澄む.md) (すむ) overlaps for a clear, resonant voice.
[冷静](冷静.md) (れいせい) emphasizes mental composure and clear-headedness,
not necessarily the dignified outward bearing conveyed by 凜とした.
Warmth contrasts with the cold sense, but is not an opposite of dignity.

## Distinctive meaning

A 凜とした声 can sound clear and firm without being loud, angry or cruel.
Likewise, a dignified manner does not establish someone's social rank or moral
superiority. Select the translation from what is being described.

The full dictionary family also records a tense atmosphere sense.
This article focuses on bearing, sound and bracing cold; it is not an
exhaustive account of every literary or historical construction.
Neither dictionary inclusion nor the variant spelling establishes a frequency
ranking. Personal-name usage is outside this article's scope.
