# 厚かましい (あつかましい) - brazen; shameless

## Meaning

厚かましい is an i-adjective meaning brazen, shameless or presumptuous.
It criticizes someone for asking or acting without appropriate restraint.

- またお金を借りに来るなんて、厚かましいです。 (またおかねをかりにくるなんて、あつかましいです。) - It is shameless to come asking to borrow money again.
- 厚かましいお願いですが、駅まで送っていただけませんか。 (あつかましいおねがいですが、えきまでおくっていただけませんか。) - It is a lot to ask, but could you take me to the station?

Use 厚かましい人 (あつかましいひと), "a brazen person," without な.
The negative is 厚かましくない (あつかましくない).

## How the kanji combine

[厚](../kanji/厚.md) is associated with thickness and supplies あつ here;
かましい completes the adjective. Remember the whole word rather than
reading it as a literal description of a person's body.

## Synonyms and antonyms

図々しい (ずうずうしい) is another word for shameless or brazen behavior.
[遠慮](遠慮.md)する (えんりょする), "hold back out of consideration," supplies a useful
contextual contrast. [厚い](厚い.md) (あつい) by itself means thick, not brazen.

## Distinctive meaning

This is not an ordinary compliment for confidence. In 厚かましいお願いですが,
the speaker acknowledges that their own request may be imposing; that does not
make calling another person 厚かましい polite.
