# 微妙 (びみょう) - subtle; delicate; iffy

## Meaning

微妙 is a na-adjective for something subtle, fine, or delicate.
Use 微妙な before a noun and 微妙に adverbially.
It can also describe a tricky situation, a doubtful prospect, or a colloquial lukewarm assessment.

- 二つの色には微妙な違いがあります。 (ふたつのいろにはびみょうなちがいがあります。) - There is a subtle difference between the two colors.
- 時間までに終わるかは微妙です。 (じかんまでにおわるかはびみょうです。) - It is doubtful whether we will finish on time.
- 「この映画、どうだった？」「うーん、微妙。」 (このえいが、どうだった？うーん、びみょう。) - "How was this film?" "Hmm, not great."

The final exchange is colloquial; the dictionary also records the spelling ビミョー for that use.

## How the kanji combine

微 contributes minuteness or fineness, and 妙 something subtle or hard to capture simply.
Together they support the fine-distinction meaning.
The on-readings are ビ + ミョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[細かい](細かい.md) (こまかい) overlaps with fine detail.
[難しい](難しい.md) (むずかしい) overlaps with a tricky situation but lacks the same range of subtle or lukewarm evaluation.
[明確](明確.md) (めいかく), "clear; definite," is a contextual contrast, not an opposite for every sense.

## Distinctive meaning

A subtle difference is not necessarily bad. A standalone 微妙 as a casual evaluation, however, may signal reservations.
微妙に違う (びみょうにちがう), "differ slightly," illustrates the adverbial "slightly/somewhat" use.
Choose the interpretation from the construction and context rather than assuming praise or criticism from the word alone.
