# 帯 (おび) - sash; band

## Meaning

帯 is a noun for an obi, the sash worn with a kimono. It also names the paper band around a book or CD and, more generally, a band or strip.

- 着物の帯を結びました。 (きもののおびをむすびました。) - I tied the kimono sash.
- この本の帯には著者の写真があります。 (このほんのおびにはちょしゃのしゃしんがあります。) - The paper band on this book has a photograph of the author.

Useful combinations include 帯を締める (おびをしめる), "fasten a sash," and 紙の帯 (かみのおび), "a strip of paper."

## How the kanji combine

The single character 帯 represents the whole native noun おび. Its band or belt association connects the clothing and paper-strip meanings. There is no multi-kanji compound to divide here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ベルト](ベルト.md) is a belt, including a belt worn with trousers. It overlaps in function but does not make a kimono's 帯 the same item. [紐](紐.md) (ひも) is a cord or string rather than a broad sash. These objects have no useful single-word antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the reading おび separate from the suffix 帯 read たい in technical compounds. The dictionary's conduction-band and valence-band gloss belongs to that separate suffix entry, not to おび.
