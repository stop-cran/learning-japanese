# 例える (たとえる) - liken; compare figuratively

## Meaning

例える means to liken something to something else, using a simile,
metaphor, or illustrative comparison. Use **AをBに例える**:
A is what is described, and B is the image used to describe it.

- 人生を旅に例える人もいます。
  (じんせいをたびにたとえるひともいます。) - Some people liken life to a journey.
- 先生は心の働きをコンピューターに例えて説明しました。
  (せんせいはこころのはたらきをコンピューターにたとえてせつめいしました。) - The teacher explained how the mind works by comparing it to a computer.

It is an ichidan verb: 例えます, 例えた, 例えて, 例えない.

## How the kanji combine

[例](../kanji/例.md) contributes an example or illustration.
Its native element here is たと; える completes the written
verb, whose conjugation stem is たとえ. 喩える and 譬える
are also dictionary spellings of the same verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[比べる](比べる.md) (くらべる) is broader comparison, such as
comparing prices. [例え](例え.md) (たとえ) is the related
noun "example; analogy; metaphor," while
[例えば](例えば.md) (たとえば) introduces "for example."

## Distinctive meaning

Likeness does not claim literal identity: life is not physically a
journey in the first example. Comparing two prices to find the
cheaper one does not by itself call for 例える. Keep the を/に
roles clear rather than assuming that any two compared things
have interchangeable roles.
