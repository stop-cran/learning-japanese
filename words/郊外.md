# 郊外 (こうがい) - suburbs; outskirts

## Meaning

郊外 refers to the area outside a city's central built-up district, often
residential suburbs or the commuter belt. It is a location noun.

- 家族は東京の郊外に住んでいます。 (かぞくはとうきょうのこうがいにすんでいます。) - My family lives in the suburbs of Tokyo.

The city takes の, and に marks where the family lives.

## How the kanji combine

郊 concerns the area around a city, while [外](../kanji/外.md) means outside.
Their on-readings コウ + ガイ form こうがい. The reference point is the city,
not the outside of any arbitrary object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

中心部 (ちゅうしんぶ), "central district," is a useful geographical contrast.
田舎 (いなか), "countryside; one's home region," is not interchangeable:
suburbs can be densely populated and closely connected to city life.

## Distinctive meaning

郊外 need not lie outside the city's administrative boundary.
It describes the relationship to the urban center, not a legal border.
For a following noun, use の: 郊外の店 (こうがいのみせ), "a suburban shop."
