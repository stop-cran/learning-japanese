# しかし - however; but

## Meaning

しかし introduces a contrast with what was just said: "however" or "but." It commonly starts a new sentence and does not change form.

この部屋は広いです。しかし、少し暗いです。 (このへやはひろいです。しかし、すこしくらいです。) - This room is spacious. However, it is a little dark.

The second statement qualifies the first; it does not deny that the room is spacious.

## How the kanji combine

Modern writing normally uses hiragana. The dictionary labels 然し and 併し rarely used kanji spellings, not equally usual alternatives to copy. There is no written kanji compound to analyze in しかし.

## Synonyms and antonyms

でも is a more conversational way to start a contrasting statement. けれども can also express contrast and can connect clauses. だから, "so; therefore," introduces a consequence instead of a contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

しかし works well in explanations, presentations, and writing, but may sound more formal than でも in casual conversation. Do not place it directly after a noun as if it were a case particle; use it to connect statements.
