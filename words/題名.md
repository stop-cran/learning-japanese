# 題名 (だいめい) - title of a work; heading

## Meaning

題名 is a title, caption or heading, especially the name given to a work
such as a book, composition or film. 題名をつける means give it a title.

- この本の題名を教えてください。
  (このほんのだいめいをおしえてください。) - Please tell me this book's title.
- 作文に題名をつけました。
  (さくぶんにだいめいをつけました。) - I gave my composition a title.

The work takes の in 本の題名; に can mark what receives the title in
作文に題名をつける.

## How the kanji combine

[題](../kanji/題.md) contributes title or subject, and
[名](../kanji/名.md) contributes name: the name serving as a title.
Both use on readings, ダイ + メイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[題](題.md) (だい) also means title, but can instead mean a topic or test
question. タイトル is the loanword comparison.
[名前](名前.md) (なまえ) is name more broadly; 題名 does not normally name
a person. [項目](項目.md) (こうもく) can name an item or heading within a
list rather than the title of the entire work.

## Distinctive meaning

A work's title is not necessarily its subject or a summary of its contents.
題名を忘れた means forgetting the title, not forgetting everything about
the work. Keep the naming function separate from the broader topic and
question uses of 題.
