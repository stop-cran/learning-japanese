# 話題 (わだい) - topic

## Meaning

話題 is a topic or subject of discussion. As a noun, it occurs in 話題を変える,
"change the topic," and 話題にする, "bring something up."
話題の before a noun can mean "much talked about."

- その話題は後で話しましょう。 (そのわだいはあとではなしましょう。) - Let's discuss that topic later.
- 最近話題の映画を見ました。 (さいきんわだいのえいがをみました。) - I watched a film that has been getting a lot of attention recently.

話題になる (わだいになる) means becoming a topic of conversation or attracting
public discussion; it does not specify whether that attention is favorable.

## How the kanji combine

[話](../kanji/話.md) contributes talk or conversation, and [題](../kanji/題.md)
contributes a subject or topic. The first character identifies the kind of
subject: something talked about. The on readings are ワ + ダイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

テーマ is a theme or subject and can overlap with 話題.
[記事](記事.md) (きじ) is an article, not its topic.
[人気](人気.md) (にんき) concerns popularity; a film can be 話題 because of
controversy rather than because people like it.

## Distinctive meaning

English "subject" also names a school subject or a grammatical subject.
話題 does not automatically cover those meanings. It directs attention to
what people discuss. In 話題の映画, the point is that the film is being talked
about, not that it contains an unusually clear grammatical topic.
