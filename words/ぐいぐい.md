# ぐいぐい — forcefully; drinking heavily

## Meaning

Vigorous, unhesitating force or momentum, including strong pulling and copious drinking.

- 犬がリードをぐいぐい引（ひ）っ張（ぱ）った。 — The dog pulled hard on the leash.
- 彼は大きなコップの水をぐいぐい飲んだ。 — He drank the large glass of water in big gulps.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずんずん](ずんずん.md) overlaps in vigorous progress. [ごくごく](ごくごく.md) and [がぶがぶ](がぶがぶ.md) overlap in the drinking use.

## Distinctive meaning

ぐいぐい引っ張る is physical force; ぐいぐい話を進（すす）める can suggest a forceful conversational manner. Confidence can be welcome or pushy depending on context: the word is not automatically praise.
