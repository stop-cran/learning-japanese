# 中旬 (ちゅうじゅん) - middle ten days of a month

## Meaning

中旬 is the 11th through the 20th of a month, inclusive.
Put a month directly before it, as in 四月中旬 (しがつちゅうじゅん),
"mid-April."

- 四月中旬に引っ越す予定です。 (しがつちゅうじゅんにひっこすよていです。) - I plan to move in mid-April.
- 二月中旬は十一日から二十日までです。 (にがつちゅうじゅんはじゅういちにちからはつかまでです。) - Mid-February runs from the 11th through the 20th.

に can mark the time of an event. 中旬 supplies a period, not a
single exact appointment date.

## How the kanji combine

[中](../kanji/中.md) identifies the middle, and 旬 a ten-day
calendar period. Read チュウ + ジュン.
The whole compound names the month's second ten-day division.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上旬](上旬.md) (じょうじゅん) is the 1st through the 10th.
[下旬](下旬.md) (げじゅん) is the 21st through the end of the month.
[半ば](半ば.md) (なかば), as in 月の半ば (つきのなかば),
is a less exact middle-of-the-month expression.

## Distinctive meaning

The source's "second third of a month" is a conventional calendar
description, not an instruction to divide each month's day count by
three. February's 中旬 still runs from the 11th to the 20th.
The word is not a general label for the middle ten days of any event.
