# 仮定 (かてい) - assumption; supposition

## Meaning

仮定 is an assumption or supposition: something provisionally taken as true for an argument, calculation, or imagined situation. It is a noun and takes する.

- 人口が毎年一パーセント増えると仮定して、計算します。 (じんこうがまいとしいちパーセントふえるとかていして、けいさんします。) - I will calculate on the assumption that the population increases by one percent each year.
- 費用が一万円だと仮定しましょう。 (ひようがいちまんえんだとかていしましょう。) - Let us assume that the cost is ten thousand yen.

One useful way to state a whole premise is plain clause + と仮定する. The noun-predicate example above uses 一万円だと仮定する: "assume that it is ten thousand yen."

## How the kanji combine

仮 contributes provisional or temporary status, and [定](../kanji/定.md) setting or determining. Together they suggest setting something provisionally as a premise. The on readings are カ + テイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

仮説 (かせつ) is a hypothesis or tentative theory and overlaps with 仮定, but can focus on an explanation to investigate. [想像](想像.md) (そうぞう) is imagination or supposition more broadly. [予想](予想.md) (よそう) is a prediction or expectation, not automatically a premise adopted for reasoning.

## Distinctive meaning

仮定する does not assert that the premise has been proved true. The noun also has a grammatical supposition use; the lesson here focuses on ordinary assumptions. The homophones [過程](過程.md), process, [課程](課程.md), curriculum, and [家庭](家庭.md), household, are different words.
