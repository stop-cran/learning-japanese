# 下手 (へた)

## Meaning

下手 means unskillful or poor at doing something. The skill or activity commonly takes が.

- 私は料理が下手です。 (わたしは りょうりが へたです。) — I am bad at cooking.
- 歌うのが下手です。 (うたうのが へたです。) — I am bad at singing.

It is a na-adjective: 下手な歌 (へたなうた), "poor singing." For an activity, plain verb + のが下手 is a useful pattern.

## How the kanji combine

[下](../kanji/下.md), "lower," and [手](../kanji/手.md), "hand," associate with a lower level of skill. The reading へた belongs to the whole word, not the ordinary separate readings した + て.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上手 (じょうず), "skillful," is the direct ability contrast. 苦手 (にがて) can mean being weak at something or feeling uncomfortable with it; it is not limited to measurable skill.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling someone else 下手 can sound blunt. It describes skill, not whether they like the activity. The same spelling has other readings, such as したて in particular expressions; those are not substitutes for へた here.
