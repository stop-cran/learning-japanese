# 御主人 (ごしゅじん) — your husband

## Meaning

御主人 respectfully refers to someone else's husband: "your husband" or "her husband." The common spelling is ご主人, with the prefix in kana.

ご主人もいらっしゃいますか。（ごしゅじんもいらっしゃいますか。） — Will your husband be coming too?

## How the kanji combine

[御](../kanji/御.md), read ご, is an honorific prefix. [主](../kanji/主.md), "master," and [人](../kanji/人.md), "person," form 主人（しゅじん）. Here the established family meaning is "husband."

## Synonyms and antonyms

夫（おっと） is a neutral word for one's own husband. ご主人 and 奥さん（おくさん）, "your wife," are corresponding traditional spouse terms, not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not add ご when referring modestly to your own husband. The traditional wording of 主人 can suggest household hierarchy; using the person's name plus さん avoids that wording and assumptions about their family role.
