# テニス (テニス) - tennis

## Meaning

テニス names the sport tennis. The ordinary expression for playing it is テニスをする.

- 土曜日に友達とテニスをします。 (どようびにともだちとテニスをします。) - I play tennis with a friend on Saturdays.
- テニスの試合を見に行きました。 (テニスのしあいをみにいきました。) - I went to watch a tennis match.

と marks the person playing with you. A place can take で: 公園でテニスをする (こうえんでテニスをする), "play tennis in the park."

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana borrowing from English "tennis." The spelling is テニス, with no long-vowel mark or small ッ. It has no kanji components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

卓球 (たっきゅう), "table tennis," is a different sport. テニスコート names the court, and テニスラケット a tennis racket.

## Distinctive meaning

テニス is the noun naming the sport, not by itself the verb "play." Use をする, を習う (をならう, "learn"), or another suitable verb to describe the activity. Simply knowing the English verb phrase does not determine the Japanese particle.
