# 偉大 (いだい) - great; outstanding

## Meaning

偉大 is a な-adjective describing exceptional greatness, achievement, or significance. Use 偉大な before a noun and 偉大だ or 偉大です as a predicate. The English noun "greatness" can be expressed as 偉大さ (いだいさ).

- 彼は偉大な作家でした。 (かれはいだいなさっかでした。) - He was a great writer.
- この発見の偉大さが、後になって分かりました。 (このはっけんのいだいさが、あとになってわかりました。) - The greatness of this discovery became clear later.

This is substantial praise, rather than a casual way to call an everyday object nice.

## How the kanji combine

[偉](../kanji/偉.md) contributes excellence or greatness, and [大](../kanji/大.md) contributes greatness or large scale. The reinforcing combination describes outstanding stature in a figurative sense. Its reading is いだい, not a combination of the native readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大きい](大きい.md) (おおきい) describes size and can extend to importance; it does not automatically praise achievement. [偉い](偉い.md) (えらい) can praise someone or describe high standing, but has a different range of everyday uses.

## Distinctive meaning

A 偉大な人物 (いだいなじんぶつ) is a person regarded as exceptionally significant. The word is not a measurement of height, wealth, or job rank. Whether someone deserves this description is an evaluation by the speaker; the adjective itself does not establish universal agreement.
