# 震える (ふるえる) - tremble; shiver

## Meaning

震える is an intransitive ichidan verb meaning to tremble, shake, or shiver.
The body, voice, or thing that trembles takes が. A cause can be expressed
with で.

- 寒さで手が震えています。 (さむさでてがふるえています。) - My hands are shaking from the cold.
- 緊張して、声が震えました。 (きんちょうして、こえがふるえました。) - I was nervous, and my voice trembled.

震えます (ふるえます) is polite nonpast, and 震えない (ふるえない) is the
plain negative. 震えている describes trembling in progress.

## How the kanji combine

震 contributes shaking or trembling. The native verb is read ふるえる;
the written える is part of the word and its conjugation. It is not read
with the on reading シン here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[揺れる](揺れる.md) (ゆれる) describes swaying or shaking and can overlap
for moving objects. [振る](振る.md) (ふる) is transitive, as in shaking or
waving something deliberately. It is not a shorter conjugation of 震える.

## Distinctive meaning

震える does not require fear: cold, nervousness, strong emotion, or physical
vibration can provide the context. Equally, it does not mean deliberately
shaking another object. 手が震える says the hand trembles; a sentence about
someone shaking a bottle needs a transitive verb and a different construction.
