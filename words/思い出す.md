# 思い出す (おもいだす) — recall

## Meaning

思い出す means **to recall or bring something back to mind**. The remembered person, event, or information takes を.

- 昔の友達を思い出しました。 (むかしのともだちをおもいだしました。) — I remembered an old friend.
- 名前を思い出せません。 (なまえをおもいだせません。) — I can't recall the name.

This is a godan verb ending in す: 思い出します, 思い出した, 思い出さない. The potential is 思い出せる (おもいだせる), “be able to recall.”

## How the kanji combine

[思](../kanji/思.md)い is the ます-stem of 思う (おもう), “think,” and [出](../kanji/出.md)す means “take out.” Together they suggest bringing something out of memory. This is a native compound verb, not two on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

覚えている (おぼえている) describes retaining something in memory: “remember/know.” 思い出す focuses on recalling it. 忘れる (わすれる), “forget,” provides the practical contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

English “remember” covers both keeping a fact in mind and retrieving it. 思い出す highlights retrieval; it does not by itself mean deliberately learning a new word or memorizing a list.
