# 有料 (ゆうりょう) - fee-charging; paid

## Meaning

有料 means that a fee is charged: paid rather than free of charge.
Use 有料だ or 有料です as a predicate and 有料の before a noun.

- この駐車場は有料です。 (このちゅうしゃじょうはゆうりょうです。) - This car park charges a fee.
- 有料のサービスを利用しました。 (ゆうりょうのサービスをりようしました。) - I used a paid service.

有料で提供する (ゆうりょうでていきょうする) means "provide for a fee."
Do not form an ordinary な-modifier such as 有料なサービス.

## How the kanji combine

[有](../kanji/有.md), having or existing, combines with
[料](../kanji/料.md), a fee or charge: a fee is involved.
The on readings are ユウ + リョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[無料](無料.md) (むりょう) and [ただ](ただ.md) provide the direct
"free of charge" contrast.
[料金](料金.md) (りょうきん) names the charge itself, while
[費用](費用.md) (ひよう) is an expense or cost.

## Distinctive meaning

有料 describes a charging arrangement, not the amount charged or
proof that a particular person has already paid.
An 有料 service may be cheap or expensive.
The dictionary also records an abbreviation for 有料道路
(ゆうりょうどうろ), a toll road; the road meaning needs its context.
