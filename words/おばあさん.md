# おばあさん - grandmother, elderly woman

## Meaning

おばあさん can mean someone's grandmother or an elderly woman who is not related to the speaker. Context separates the two.

- 友達のおばあさんは京都に住んでいます。 (ともだちのおばあさんはきょうとにすんでいます。) - My friend's grandmother lives in Kyoto.
- おばあさんがバスを待っています。 (おばあさんがバスをまっています。) - An elderly woman is waiting for the bus.

## How the kanji combine

The usual kana spelling combines お, ばあ, and さん. When kanji are used, お祖母さん normally identifies a grandmother, whereas お婆さん identifies an elderly woman.

## Synonyms and antonyms

祖母 (そぼ) is the standard way to refer to your own grandmother when speaking to outsiders. おばあちゃん is affectionate. おじいさん means "grandfather" or "elderly man," a related contrast rather than an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the long あ: おばさん, with a short vowel, means "aunt" or a middle-aged woman. Also avoid assuming that an unfamiliar older woman welcomes おばあさん as direct address; age labels can sound presumptuous.
