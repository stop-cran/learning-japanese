# 岩 (いわ) - rock; boulder

## Meaning

岩 is a noun for a rock, boulder, or crag. It is useful for describing a substantial mass of stone in a landscape, such as rocks along a coast or beside a mountain path.

- 川のそばの大きな岩に座りました。 (かわのそばのおおきないわにすわりました。) - I sat on a large rock beside the river.
- 海岸には岩がたくさんあります。 (かいがんにはいわがたくさんあります。) - There are many rocks along the coast.

岩に座る marks the surface with に. 岩の上 (いわのうえ), "on top of the rock," makes that position more explicit. The noun itself does not mark singular or plural.

## How the kanji combine

岩 is one kanji representing the whole native word いわ. Its visible parts are [山](../kanji/山.md), "mountain," and [石](../kanji/石.md), "stone." A mountain-sized stone is a useful modern-shape mnemonic, not a claim that every 岩 belongs to a mountain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[石](石.md) (いし) also means stone and is useful for a smaller individual stone. This is a practical size distinction, not a precise measurement rule. [山](山.md) (やま), "mountain," names a landform rather than a piece or mass of rock.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 岩 for the rocky object or formation, not automatically for every English use of "rock." Music called "rock" is ロック. There is no natural single-word antonym for a boulder; choose a contrast relevant to the landscape instead.
