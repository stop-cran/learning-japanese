# 匁 — monme (traditional mass unit; 3.75 g)

## Meaning

匁 (もんめ) is the **monme**, a traditional Japanese unit of mass equal to **3.75 grams**. You can still encounter it in pearl trading. This article teaches that mass sense, not a modern currency.

- 1匁は3.75グラムです。 (いちもんめはさんてんななごグラムです。) — One monme is 3.75 grams.

This is a unit-conversion example: put the number directly before 匁.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-character word: [匁](../kanji/匁.md) writes the unit name もんめ. There is no compound to split into separate readings or meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[グラム](グラム.md), "gram," is another mass unit, not an equal-sized substitute: 1匁 is **3.75** grams, not one gram. These units have no antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The traditional mass unit is useful for recognizing specialist descriptions such as pearl weights. Its continued use there does not make it the default unit for an ordinary modern measurement.

Historical texts also use もんめ for silver money. That use needs its historical context; do not convert it into modern money using the mass definition. The reading め is a documented shortening in unit expressions, but this article's primary reading and practice form are **もんめ**.
