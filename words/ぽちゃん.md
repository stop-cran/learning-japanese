# ぽちゃん — a plop into water

## Meaning

A splash or plop as something drops into water.

- 小石（こいし）が池（いけ）にぽちゃんと落ちた。 — A little stone dropped into the pond with a plop.
- 水にぽちゃんとしずくが落ちる音を聞いた。 — I heard a drop plop into the water.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ばしゃばしゃ](ばしゃばしゃ.md) and [じゃぶじゃぶ](じゃぶじゃぶ.md) often present repeated splashing. A single plop and an extended splash scene can overlap in English description.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn ぽちゃんと落ちる（おちる）. The inspected entry also records ぼちゃん; this batch keeps one card for that dictionary identity rather than presenting the two spellings as guaranteed separate water-volume categories.
