# 伺う (うかがう) - visit or ask humbly

## Meaning

伺う is a humble godan verb used for one's own side's visit, question, or act of listening. The respectful relationship matters, not simply whether the sentence ends in ます.

- 明日、先生のお宅に伺います。 (あした、せんせいのおたくにうかがいます。) - I will visit my teacher's home tomorrow.
- お名前を伺ってもいいですか。 (おなまえをうかがってもいいですか。) - May I ask your name?

The destination takes に; information being requested takes を. Forms include 伺った (うかがった) and 伺わない (うかがわない).

## How the kanji combine

伺 represents うかが in this native verb; う is okurigana. The spelling is not a compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

訪ねる (たずねる) means "visit," while 聞く (きく) means "ask" or "hear" without this humble marking.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 伺う to present your action deferentially toward the person concerned. Do not describe an honored visitor's action as 伺う merely to sound polite: humble language lowers the actor's side rather than honoring that actor.
