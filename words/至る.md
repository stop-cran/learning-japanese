# 至る (いたる) - reach; arrive at a stage

## Meaning

至る is an intransitive godan verb for reaching a stage, conclusion, or destination. Mark the endpoint with **に**. Its forms include 至ります, 至って, and 至った.

- 話し合いの末、合意に至りました。 (はなしあいのすえ、ごういにいたりました。) - After discussion, we reached an agreement.
- この道は山頂に至る。 (このみちはさんちょうにいたる。) - This path leads to the summit.

The first example presents an outcome reached through a process. The second describes where a route leads, not necessarily a person's arrival at this moment.

## How the kanji combine

至 carries the idea of reaching an endpoint. The native reading is いたる, with る as the written ending. There is no second kanji to combine with it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[着く](着く.md) (つく) is useful for arriving at a station or other destination. [来る](来る.md) (くる) describes coming; it is not interchangeable with 至る in ordinary invitations such as "come here." An endpoint can be contrasted with the start of a process, but that does not give every use one fixed antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

至る suits accounts of developments, conclusions, and routes. The source list's broad "come; arrive" gloss should not obscure these constructions. JMdict separately labels its unrestricted "come; arrive; result in" sense archaic; that is not the everyday usage taught here.
