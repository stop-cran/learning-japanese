# 集中 (しゅうちゅう) - concentration; focusing

## Meaning

集中 is concentration or focusing. With する it can describe focusing on a task, or people and things gathering in one place. Use に for the focus or destination.

- 静かな場所で勉強に集中します。 (しずかなばしょでべんきょうにしゅうちゅうします。) - I concentrate on studying in a quiet place.
- この時間は駅に人が集中します。 (このじかんはえきにひとがしゅうちゅうします。) - At this time, people converge on the station.

There is also a transitive pattern: 注意を一点に集中する (ちゅういをいってんにしゅうちゅうする), "focus one's attention on one point." を identifies what is focused; に identifies where.

## How the kanji combine

[集](../kanji/集.md) contributes gathering, and [中](../kanji/中.md) contributes the center. Together they suggest gathering at a focus. The on readings are シュウ + チュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

注意 (ちゅうい) can mean attention or caution; 集中 emphasizes directing attention toward a focus. 分散 (ぶんさん), "dispersion," contrasts with people or resources being concentrated in one place. Resting is not automatically the opposite of concentration.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume every 集中 concerns mental effort. 人が集中する describes a distribution of people, while 勉強に集中する describes attention. 集中できない (しゅうちゅうできない) means "cannot concentrate"; it does not necessarily mean that the task is intellectually impossible.
