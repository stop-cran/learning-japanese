# 柄 (え) - handle; pattern

## Meaning

柄 (え) is a noun for a handle or grip, such as the shaft or handle of an umbrella or tool.

- 傘の柄が折れました。 (かさのえがおれました。) - The umbrella's handle broke.

柄 (がら) is a separate lexical identity meaning a pattern or design. It can follow another noun, as in 花柄 (はながら), "floral pattern."

- このシャツの柄が好きです。 (このシャツのがらがすきです。) - I like the pattern on this shirt.

The がら identity also concerns character or nature: 柄が悪い (がらがわるい) describes rough or disreputable manners, not an unattractive fabric pattern.

## How the kanji combine

This is one character, not a compound to divide. The written form 柄 represents different words. Choose between the readings here by meaning: え for a handle, がら for a pattern or character. Even 傘の柄 can mean an umbrella's handle (かさのえ) or its pattern (かさのがら); the surrounding context decides.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[模様](模様.md) (もよう) overlaps with the visual-pattern sense. [性格](性格.md) (せいかく) concerns personality rather than a handle or design. Neither supplies a general replacement across the readings.

## Distinctive meaning

柄 (つか), "sword hilt; dagger haft," is another dictionary identity, not a pronunciation variant to substitute freely for がら.

The community source gives がら a conflicting hilt/haft gloss. This article deliberately teaches its dictionary-supported pattern and character meanings; the compiler's historical intention remains uncertain. The contrastive つか reading is not assigned a separate N3 level here.
