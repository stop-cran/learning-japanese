# 戸 (と) — door; especially a traditional door

## Meaning

戸 means a door, especially one in a traditional Japanese setting. It is useful for sliding doors, but the word is not restricted to one door mechanism.

- 戸を開けてください。 (とをあけてください。) — Please open the door.
- 風で戸が閉まりました。 (かぜでとがしまりました。) — The door closed in the wind.

Use 戸を開ける for opening it yourself and 戸が開く (とがあく) for the door opening.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 戸 writes the native noun と. This is not the on reading コ found in other compounds or counting expressions.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ドア is another everyday door word, especially familiar for Western-style doors. 障子 (しょうじ) is a specific kind of paper-covered sliding panel; it is not a name for every 戸.

## Distinctive meaning

戸 names the movable door, not the whole entrance area. 入口 (いりぐち) names where one enters. A building can have an 入口 with several doors, so the two words are related but not interchangeable.
