# 年月 (としつき) - months and years

## Meaning

年月 has two readings taught here: **としつき** and **ねんげつ**.
Both are nouns meaning "months and years," especially a span of time passing.
The dictionary entry does not divide them into separate meanings.

- 長い年月が過ぎました。 (ながいとしつきがすぎました。) - Many months and years passed.
- この研究には十年の年月がかかりました。 (このけんきゅうにはじゅうねんのねんげつがかかりました。) - This research took a period of ten years.

The kana shows the intended reading in each example; neither spelling alone forces one reading.
年月がかかる concerns time required, while 年月が過ぎる concerns time passing.

## How the kanji combine

[年](../kanji/年.md), "year," and [月](../kanji/月.md), "month," form a paired time expression.
The primary reading combines native とし + つき, so this card is labeled wago.
The alternative ねんげつ combines on readings ネン + ゲツ.
These are two readings of the same written word, not two different spellings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時間](時間.md) (じかん), "time," is broader and can concern short periods.
[一生](一生.md) (いっしょう), "a lifetime," measures a span against a life.
年月 names months and years without necessarily covering someone's entire life.

## Distinctive meaning

This word lets a speaker treat a long span as a whole rather than list individual calendar dates.
Do not invent a strict rule that one reading is emotional and the other numerical:
the inspected entry supports the shared meaning for both, and context supplies the emphasis.
