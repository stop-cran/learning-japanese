# 注 (ちゅう) - annotation; explanatory note

## Meaning

注 is a noun for an annotation or explanatory note attached to a text. It may explain a difficult expression, supply a qualification, or direct the reader to additional information.

- 分からない言葉には注を付けました。 (わからないことばにはちゅうをつけました。) - I added notes to the words that were hard to understand.
- ページの下の注を読んでください。 (ページのしたのちゅうをよんでください。) - Please read the note at the bottom of the page.

注を付ける (ちゅうをつける) means "add a note." A label such as 注1 (ちゅういち) identifies a numbered note.

## How the kanji combine

[注](../kanji/注.md) is the complete word here, read with the on reading ちゅう. The same character has other uses in longer words, but this noun specifically names explanatory annotation. The dictionary also records the spelling 註.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[説明](説明.md) (せつめい), "explanation," is broader: it need not be a note attached to a document. [注意](注意.md) (ちゅうい) concerns attention or caution. A 注 can contain a warning, but that is not what every note does.

## Distinctive meaning

English "note" may mean a personal memo, a musical note, or an annotation. 注 fits the explanatory annotation sense, not all three. It is useful for navigating textbooks and reference material: the note supplements the main text rather than necessarily forming the main message itself.
