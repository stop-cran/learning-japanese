# 上げる (あげる) — to raise; to give

## Meaning

上げる is a transitive ichidan verb: 上げます, 上げない, 上げて. It means raising something, but the beginner vocabulary sense "give" is usually written あげる. Mark the gift with を and the recipient with に.

- 友達に本をあげました。 (ともだちにほんをあげました。) — I gave a friend a book.
- 手を上げてください。 (てをあげてください。) — Please raise your hand.

## How the kanji combine

[上](../kanji/上.md), "up," supplies あ; げる is okurigana. This association explains "raise," but does not make every use a literal upward movement.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くれる](くれる.md) presents giving toward the speaker or someone viewed as on the speaker's side. もらう means receiving. 下げる (さげる), "lower," contrasts with the raising sense.

## Distinctive meaning

あげる normally presents giving outward, not someone giving to you. Verb て-form + あげる means doing something for someone: 手伝ってあげる (てつだってあげる), "help someone out." Directly announcing a favor this way can sound patronizing; it is not a universal polite replacement for the plain verb.
