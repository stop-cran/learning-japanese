# 板 (いた) - board; plank

## Meaning

板 is a noun for a board or plank. It can also describe a flat sheet or plate of material, not just wood. A material can precede it with の: 木の板 (きのいた), "a wooden board."

- この板を半分に切ってください。 (このいたをはんぶんにきってください。) - Please cut this board in half.
- 窓にガラスの板をはめました。 (まどにガラスのいたをはめました。) - I fitted a pane of glass into the window.

Flat boards can be counted with 枚 (まい): 板を二枚 (いたをにまい), "two boards."

## How the kanji combine

板 is one character representing the whole native noun いた. It does not require separate readings for smaller written components. In [黒板](黒板.md) (こくばん), "blackboard," the same character uses the on reading バン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[木](木.md) (き) can name wood as a material; 板 names a shaped piece. A piece of wood need not be a board. There is no useful general antonym: a pole, block, and sheet are different shapes rather than opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

板 foregrounds a relatively broad, flat form. The material needs context: 木の板 and ガラスの板 specify different substances. It does not automatically mean a writing board; use the particular object's name, such as 黒板, when that distinction matters.
