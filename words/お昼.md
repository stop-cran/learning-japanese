# お昼 (おひる) - lunch; midday

## Meaning

お昼 is a polite noun used for lunch or midday; it can also refer to daytime.
The surrounding verb usually makes the intended sense clear: **お昼を食べる** means "eat lunch,"
while **お昼ごろ** means "around noon."

- 一緒にお昼を食べませんか。 (いっしょにおひるをたべませんか。) - Would you like to have lunch together?
- お昼ごろに電話します。 (おひるごろにでんわします。) - I will call around noon.

For the meal, を marks what is eaten. For an approximate time, ごろ follows お昼;
this does not promise a call at precisely twelve.

## How the kanji combine

The prefix お adds a polite tone to [昼](../kanji/昼.md) (ひる), "noon/daytime."
The meal meaning is part of the established word, not a second kanji hidden in the spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[昼食](昼食.md) (ちゅうしょく) explicitly names the meal.
[正午](正午.md) (しょうご) specifies noon as a clock time.
[昼間](昼間.md) (ひるま) emphasizes the daytime period, contrasting with night rather than breakfast or dinner.
These comparisons separate the senses rather than supply one opposite for all of them.

## Distinctive meaning

The polite prefix does not make お昼 unsuitable for relaxed conversation.
お昼はまだです (おひるはまだです) can mean "I haven't had lunch yet" when the conversation is about eating.
Do not translate every occurrence as a time of day merely because 昼 itself has that meaning.
