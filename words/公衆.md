# 公衆 (こうしゅう) - the general public

## Meaning

公衆 means the public or people in society generally, rather than one
particular individual. It is a noun that can modify another noun with の
or appear in compounds.

- 公衆の面前で大声を出さないでください。 (こうしゅうのめんぜんでおおごえをださないでください。) - Please do not shout in front of everyone.
- 公衆衛生について勉強しています。 (こうしゅうえいせいについてべんきょうしています。) - I am studying public health.

公衆の面前 means "in public; before other people."
公衆衛生 (こうしゅうえいせい) concerns the public's health.

## How the kanji combine

[公](../kanji/公.md) contributes public, and 衆 contributes a multitude
or people collectively. Together they name the public as people.
The on readings are コウ + シュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[公共](公共.md) (こうきょう) concerns the public or society-wide/shared
affairs and overlaps in public-related expressions.
[世間](世間.md) (せけん) and [社会](社会.md) (しゃかい) can also refer
to society or the public, with different contextual emphases.
These are not fixed, nonoverlapping populations.

## Distinctive meaning

公衆 is not simply the government, and a public-use facility need not be
government-owned or free. Nor does the word necessarily mean an audience
assembled for one performance. Establish whether the context concerns the
general public or a more specific group.
