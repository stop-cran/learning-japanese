# 映す (うつす) - project; reflect

## Meaning

映す is a transitive godan verb for projecting or reflecting an image, or casting a shadow.
The image or reflected thing takes を; the screen or reflecting surface can take に.

- スクリーンに映画を映します。 (スクリーンにえいがをうつします。) - We will project a film onto the screen.
- 鏡に自分の顔を映しました。 (かがみにじぶんのかおをうつしました。) - I reflected my face in the mirror.

Learn 映します (うつします), 映した (うつした) and 映さない (うつさない).
壁に影を映す (かべにかげをうつす) means "cast a shadow on the wall."

## How the kanji combine

[映](../kanji/映.md) supplies the native stem うつ; す is okurigana.
The character connects the image with its projection or reflection, not the transport of a physical object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[映る](映る.md) (うつる) is intransitive: 顔が鏡に映る (かおがかがみにうつる),
"a face is reflected in the mirror."
[写す](写す.md), also うつす, teaches copying and photographing.
[移す](移す.md) has the same reading but concerns moving or transferring.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare recording an image with displaying it: 写真を写す (しゃしんをうつす), "take a photograph,"
but ビデオを映す (ビデオをうつす), "play/show a video."
These are useful spelling distinctions, not a rule that every photographed or displayed image
has only one possible spelling in every context.
