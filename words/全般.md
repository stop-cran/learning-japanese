# 全般 (ぜんぱん) - the whole; overall scope

## Meaning

全般 concerns the whole scope of a subject or activity.
It can follow a noun to mean that field as a whole:
仕事全般 (しごとぜんぱん), work in general.
全般に and 全般的に (ぜんぱんてきに) mean generally or overall.

- 彼は日本の歴史全般に詳しいです。 (かれはにほんのれきしぜんぱんにくわしいです。) - He knows a lot about Japanese history as a whole.
- 今年は全般的に暖かいです。 (ことしはぜんぱんてきにあたたかいです。) - This year is generally warm.

The second sentence is an overall assessment, not a claim that every
single day has been warm.

## How the kanji combine

[全](../kanji/全.md) contributes entirety, and
[般](../kanji/般.md) scope or generality.
Learn the whole reading ぜんぱん: 般's ハン appears as パン here.
The word is not pronounced ぜんはん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[全体](全体.md) (ぜんたい) also names a whole.
[全部](全部.md) (ぜんぶ) often concerns all of a relevant set or amount.
[一般](一般.md) (いっぱん) has general or ordinary uses;
全般 focuses on broad coverage rather than merely being ordinary.
一部 (いちぶ), a part, contrasts with the whole scope.

## Distinctive meaning

全般 does not freely replace an English adjective before any noun.
Learn noun + 全般 and 全般的な + noun:
全般的な傾向 (ぜんぱんてきなけいこう), "an overall tendency."
Looking at the whole does not necessarily mean listing every item separately.
