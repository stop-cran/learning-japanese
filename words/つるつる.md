# つるつる — smooth and slippery

## Meaning

A smooth, slick, sometimes shiny surface. It can emphasize slipperiness; with noodles, it can describe slurping them down.

- 凍った（こおった）道はつるつる滑る。 — The frozen road is very slippery.
- 小石を洗うと、表面（ひょうめん）がつるつるしていた。 — After I washed the pebble, its surface felt smooth.
- 子どもがうどんをつるつると食べている。 — The child is slurping down udon noodles.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic repeating つる. It describes both tactile states and, in the noodle use, a manner associated with sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[すべすべ](すべすべ.md) emphasizes pleasant smoothness to the touch. [ぬるぬる](ぬるぬる.md) suggests a slimy or oily coating. [ざらざら](ざらざら.md) is rough or gritty.

## Distinctive meaning

つるつるした石, つるつるの床, and つるつる滑る（すべる）are useful. Smoothness need not be pleasant: it may make a surface unsafe. The noodle construction uses the eating verb and context; it is not an invitation to substitute つるつる for any expression meaning “delicious.”
