# 森 (もり) — forest

## Meaning

森 is a forest or a wooded area with many trees. It is an ordinary noun; 森の + noun links something to the forest.

朝、森の中を歩きました。 (あさ、もりのなかをあるきました。) — In the morning, I walked through the forest.

## How the kanji combine

森 is a single character with the native reading もり. Its repeated 木 shapes provide a useful visual association with many trees, but the word's reading is not built by repeating き.

## Synonyms and antonyms

林 (はやし) means woods or a grove and overlaps with 森. 森 often suggests a denser or more extensive wooded area, but there is no universal everyday size threshold separating the two.

## Distinctive meaning

森林 (しんりん) is a broader compound used for forests and woodland, often in environmental or administrative discussion. 森 itself is a straightforward landscape word. A forest can contain paths, buildings, or managed areas; the word alone does not certify untouched wilderness.
