# 筆記 (ひっき) - writing down; taking notes

## Meaning

筆記 is writing something down, taking notes, or copying written material.
筆記する is transitive: the information recorded takes を.
The noun can also refer to the resulting notes or writing.

- 説明の要点を筆記しました。 (せつめいのようてんをひっきしました。) - I wrote down the main points of the explanation.
- 講義を聞きながら筆記しました。 (こうぎをききながらひっきしました。) - I took notes while listening to the lecture.

筆記用具 (ひっきようぐ) means writing implements.
筆記試験 (ひっきしけん) is a written examination; the dictionary also records
筆記 as an abbreviation of that expression.

## How the kanji combine

[筆](../kanji/筆.md) contributes a writing implement or writing;
[記](../kanji/記.md) contributes recording. Together they concern recording
in writing. ヒツ + キ becomes ひっき, with a small っ.
The brush association does not require using a traditional brush.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[メモ](メモ.md) can name a brief note or the action of noting something down.
[書き取り](書き取り.md) includes dictation and written copying.
[記録](記録.md) is broader recording, not necessarily in written form.
口述 (こうじゅつ), speaking or dictating, contrasts with writing in suitable contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

筆記 is an external writing activity, not simply keeping information in memory.
It need not reproduce every word verbatim: taking down the main points also fits.
Conversely, 筆記試験 concerns the examination format, not an instruction merely
to take notes on the examiner's speech.
