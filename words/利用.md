# 利用 (りよう) - use of a service or resource

## Meaning

利用 means use or utilization. 利用する means to use a service, facility, opportunity, or resource, taking that thing with を.

- 通勤にはバスを利用しています。 (つうきんにはバスをりようしています。) - I use the bus to commute.
- この図書館は誰でも利用できます。 (このとしょかんはだれでもりようできます。) - Anyone can use this library.

利用できます is the potential form: use is possible or permitted in the situation.

## How the kanji combine

[利](../kanji/利.md) contributes benefit or advantage, and [用](../kanji/用.md) contributes use. The on readings are リ + ヨウ. Do not confuse りよう with りょう; the よ is full-sized.

## Synonyms and antonyms

使う (つかう), "use," is broader and often more conversational. 使用する (しようする) also means use, especially in instructions about equipment or materials.

## Distinctive meaning

利用 is not inherently negative: using a library is ordinary 利用. With a person as the object, however, 人を利用する (ひとをりようする) can suggest taking advantage of them. The object and context matter.
