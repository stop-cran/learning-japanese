# 束 (たば) - bundle; bunch

## Meaning

束 is a bundle, bunch, or sheaf: several things gathered and treated as one unit. Use Nの束 for "a bundle of N." It also counts bundles, as in 一束 (ひとたば).

- 古い手紙の束を見つけました。 (ふるいてがみのたばをみつけました。) - I found a bundle of old letters.
- 花を一束買いました。 (はなをひとたばかいました。) - I bought one bunch of flowers.

The second example counts one bundle, not one individual flower. The number of items inside the bundle is unspecified.

## How the kanji combine

The single [束](../kanji/束.md) represents the whole native noun たば. In 一束, 一 supplies the number and 束 identifies the bundled unit. The same reading appears in 花束 (はなたば), "bouquet."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[組](組.md) (くみ) is a set or group treated as a unit, but its members need not be physically gathered into a bundle. ばらばら means separated or scattered and provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 一束 as ひとたば in these examples rather than reading each character mechanically. The source-list gloss "handbreadth" does not describe the modern たば taught here; it belongs to a different, archaic reading. A 束 tells you how things are grouped, not a fixed length, weight, or number of contents.
