# 的 (まと) - target; focus

## Meaning

的 is a target or mark to aim at. It can also be the object or focus of attention, not just a physical target.

- 矢が的に当たりました。 (やがまとにあたりました。) - The arrow hit the target.
- 彼女は注目の的です。 (かのじょはちゅうもくのまとです。) - She is the center of attention.

In the first example, に marks what the arrow hits. 注目の的 (ちゅうもくのまと) identifies what attracts attention.

## How the kanji combine

[的](../kanji/的.md) supplies the whole native word まと. There is no compound to split. This is different from the on-reading テキ in [目的](目的.md) (もくてき), purpose, or an adjective-making suffix.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[目標](目標.md) (もくひょう), "goal; target," and [狙い](狙い.md) (ねらい), "aim; target," overlap in aiming contexts. [対象](対象.md) (たいしょう) identifies the object of an activity; [焦点](焦点.md) (しょうてん) can name its focus. They are not freely interchangeable in every phrase.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the whole expression: being 注目の的 does not mean someone is being physically shot at. Conversely, まと is not the reading of the suffix in 科学的 (かがくてき), "scientific."
