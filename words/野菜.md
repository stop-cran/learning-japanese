# 野菜 (やさい) — vegetables

## Meaning

野菜 means **vegetables**, such as carrots, cabbage, or onions. It can refer to the category or to
vegetables in a particular dish.

- 毎日、野菜を食べます。（まいにち、やさいをたべます。）— I eat vegetables every day.
- このスープには野菜がたくさん入っています。（このスープにはやさいがたくさんはいっています。）— This soup contains lots of vegetables.

The word itself does not change form for singular versus plural.

## How the kanji combine

[野](../kanji/野.md) contributes "field" and 菜 contributes "vegetables" or "greens."
The field association helps identify the kind of food: **field + edible plants**.
The reading is on + on: ヤ + サイ → やさい.

This is a meaning aid, not a restriction to wild plants. Ordinary cultivated vegetables are 野菜 too.
菜 is part of the spelling even though it does not yet have a card in this deck.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 食べ物（たべもの）, "food," is a broader category that includes vegetables.
- 果物（くだもの）, "fruit," is a useful contrasting food category, not an antonym.
- [肉](../kanji/肉.md)（にく）, "meat," is another food category rather than a word with the opposite meaning.

There is no natural direct antonym of 野菜.

## Distinctive meaning

野菜 is the ordinary word used in shopping, cooking, and everyday meals.
It names a food category, not a claim that something is raw, green, or gathered from the wild.
Those properties require additional words.
