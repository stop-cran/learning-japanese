# 材料 (ざいりょう) - materials; ingredients

## Meaning

材料 is material used to make something, including cooking ingredients. Nの材料 means "materials for N"; 材料にする means "use as material." The word can also refer to information used as a basis for a judgment.

- カレーの材料を買ってきました。 (カレーのざいりょうをかってきました。) - I bought the ingredients for curry.
- この資料を判断の材料にします。 (このしりょうをはんだんのざいりょうにします。) - I will use this document as a basis for making a judgment.

The second example uses 材料 figuratively: no physical object is being manufactured.

## How the kanji combine

材 contributes material or usable timber, and [料](../kanji/料.md) contributes material or supplies. The related meanings reinforce the idea of inputs used to produce something. The on readings are ザイ + リョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[資料](資料.md) (しりょう) concerns reference information or documents. A 資料 can become 材料 for an argument, but cooking ingredients are not normally called 資料.

## Distinctive meaning

Ask what is being made or decided. For cooking, 材料 identifies ingredients; for building, it identifies construction materials; for a decision, it identifies relevant evidence. It does not mean every possession or every piece of equipment. A saucepan is a tool used to cook, not an ingredient in the curry.
