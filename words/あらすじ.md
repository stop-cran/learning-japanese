# あらすじ - outline; plot summary

## Meaning

あらすじ is an outline or summary, especially the main course of a story.
It is a noun: 映画のあらすじ (えいがのあらすじ), "the plot summary of a film."
It gives the broad line rather than every detail.

- この小説のあらすじを教えてください。 (このしょうせつのあらすじをおしえてください。) - Please tell me the outline of this novel.
- 映画を見る前に、あらすじを読みました。 (えいがをみるまえに、あらすじをよみました。) - I read the plot summary before watching the film.

## How the kanji combine

The dictionary marks this word as usually written in kana. Its spelling 粗筋
(あらすじ) offers a learning association: 粗 suggests broad rather than fine detail,
and 筋 can concern a thread or storyline. This is a meaning aid, not a historical derivation.
荒筋 is recorded as a rare spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

要約 (ようやく) is a summary or the act of summarizing. [内容](内容.md) (ないよう)
is the content or substance itself; an あらすじ presents only its main line.
There is no single necessary antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

An あらすじ can reveal important plot developments; the word itself does not promise
a spoiler-free description. The dictionary's English "argument" here can mean a
summary of a work, not a quarrel between people.
