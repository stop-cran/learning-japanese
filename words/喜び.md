# 喜び (よろこび) - joy; delight

## Meaning

喜び is joy or delight, treated as a noun. Use 喜びを感じる (よろこびをかんじる), "feel joy," and Nの喜び for joy associated with an experience or achievement.

- 合格の喜びを家族と分かち合いました。 (ごうかくのよろこびをかぞくとわかちあいました。) - I shared the joy of passing with my family.
- 子供の成長を見ることは大きな喜びです。 (こどものせいちょうをみることはおおきなよろこびです。) - Watching my child grow is a great joy.

The first example names a feeling; the second can describe the experience as a source of joy.

## How the kanji combine

[喜](../kanji/喜.md) carries rejoicing. 喜び is related to the native verb [喜ぶ](喜ぶ.md) (よろこぶ), "be pleased; rejoice." The final び belongs to the noun form; it is not a second kanji meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[うれしい](うれしい.md) means happy or glad but is an い-adjective. [楽しみ](楽しみ.md) (たのしみ) can name enjoyment or something looked forward to. 悲しみ (かなしみ), "sorrow," offers an emotional contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also includes "congratulations" or "felicitations." In a formal expression such as お喜び申し上げます (およろこびもうしあげます), the noun helps convey congratulations. The bare word 喜び is not the everyday exclamation "Congratulations!" Its main practical use is naming joy so that you can describe, share, or express it.
