# 吹く (ふく) — blow

## Meaning

吹く describes blowing wind. In this use, the wind is the subject marked by が, and there is no object being acted on.

- 今日は強い風が吹いています。 (きょうはつよいかぜがふいています。) — A strong wind is blowing today.

The same verb also describes blowing with one's breath or playing a wind instrument:

- 兄はフルートを吹いています。 (あにはフルートをふいています。) — My older brother is playing the flute.

In the second use, the instrument takes を. The て-form is 吹いて (ふいて), not ふって.

## How the kanji combine

[吹](../kanji/吹.md) expresses blowing; く is the changing ending of this native Japanese verb.
There is no second kanji to combine with it. The on reading スイ belongs to compounds such as 吹奏楽 (すいそうがく), "wind-instrument music."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[吸う](吸う.md) (すう) means to inhale or suck inward. It contrasts with blowing air out, but is not the opposite of every use of 吹く: wind does not normally alternate between these two verbs as a person breathes.
拭く (ふく), "wipe," is a different verb with the same pronunciation.

## Distinctive meaning

The particles matter more than one English gloss: 風が吹く describes wind moving, while フルートを吹く describes a person playing an instrument.
Do not infer from the flute example that 吹く is a general verb for playing any musical instrument.
