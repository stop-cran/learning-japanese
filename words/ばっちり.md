# ばっちり — perfectly and just right

## Meaning

ばっちり describes something being perfectly prepared, accurate, or fully accomplished.

- 発表の準備はばっちりできている。
  — The presentation is perfectly prepared.
- 地図を見たので、目的地の場所はばっちり分かった。
  — After looking at the map, I know exactly where the destination is.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ばっちり is conversational and emphatic; it conveys confidence that something is in good order.

## Distinctive meaning

Depending on the sentence, it can mean exactly right, completely done, or thoroughly.
