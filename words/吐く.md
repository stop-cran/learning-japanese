# 吐く (はく) - to exhale; vomit; utter

## Meaning

吐く, read はく, is a transitive godan verb. It can mean "breathe out," "emit," or "vomit."
The thing expelled takes を, though an object can be omitted when context makes it clear.

- ゆっくり息を吐いてください。 (ゆっくりいきをはいてください。) - Please breathe out slowly.
- 気分が悪くなって、吐いてしまいました。 (きぶんがわるくなって、はいてしまいました。) - I felt sick and vomited.

It also describes uttering words, as in 暴言を吐く (ぼうげんをはく), "make abusive remarks."
This does not make it a neutral replacement for every use of "say."
Conjugations include 吐きます, 吐いた, 吐いて, and 吐かない.

## How the kanji combine

吐 writes the stem of the native verb はく, with く as okurigana.
Learn the spelling together with its object: 息を吐く concerns breath, while the context
can identify vomiting without an explicit object.
The verb is not read by combining the character's visible components.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[吸う](吸う.md) (すう), "breathe in; inhale," is the useful directional opposite in breathing:
息を吸う versus 息を吐く.
General breathing includes both directions, so "breathe" alone is an incomplete gloss here.

## Distinctive meaning

The outward direction links several uses, but the object still matters.
吐く does not inherently mean vomiting every time it appears.
Other はく verbs concern wearing clothes or sweeping; those meanings require their own spellings
and contexts rather than being added to this entry.
