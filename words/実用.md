# 実用 (じつよう) - practical use; utility

## Meaning

実用 is practical use or usefulness in actual use, rather than something being only theoretical, ornamental, or experimental. It is a noun.

- この道具は実用に向いています。
  (このどうぐはじつようにむいています。) - This tool is suited to practical use.
- この辞書は実用的です。
  (このじしょはじつようてきです。) - This dictionary is practical.

The second example uses the related な-adjective **実用的** (じつようてき), "practical; useful." Before a noun, use 実用的な, as in 実用的な道具, "a practical tool."

## How the kanji combine

[実](../kanji/実.md) contributes actuality and [用](../kanji/用.md) use: use in actual practice. The on readings combine without a small っ: ジツ + ヨウ, じつよう. This meaning aid does not make every occurrence of 実 a statement about fruit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[使用](使用.md) (しよう) and [利用](利用.md) (りよう) concern use more generally. [活用](活用.md) (かつよう) emphasizes making effective use of something. 実用 foregrounds practical application or utility rather than simply the fact that someone used an object.

[実際](実際.md) (じっさい) concerns actuality or actual practice. 実際に使う means "actually use"; it does not automatically say that the thing is useful or well suited to a task.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep 実用, the noun, separate from 実用的, the adjective. The principal dictionary entry does not present 実用 as a general-purpose する verb equivalent to [使う](使う.md). A beautiful display object can lack practical utility, while an unattractive tool can be highly practical; appearance and usefulness are different judgments.
