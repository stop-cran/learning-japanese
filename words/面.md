# 面 (めん) - surface; aspect; mask

## Meaning

面, read めん, is a noun for a surface or face, a mask or face guard, or an aspect
of something. It can also name a newspaper page.
As an ending, it identifies a particular aspect: 経済面 (けいざいめん), "the economic aspect."

- 立方体には六つの面があります。
  (りっぽうたいにはむっつのめんがあります。) - A cube has six faces.
- この計画には良い面もあります。
  (このけいかくにはよいめんもあります。) - This plan has good aspects too.

能の面 (のうのめん) is a Noh mask; 剣道の面 (けんどうのめん) is kendo head protection.
新聞の一面 (しんぶんのいちめん) refers to the newspaper's front page.
The face sense appears in 面と向かって話す (めんとむかってはなす),
"speak face-to-face."

## How the kanji combine

The single character [面](../kanji/面.md) contributes a face, surface, or aspect.
メン is its on reading. The physical and abstract uses share a useful image of one
side presented for viewing, but are established senses rather than a rule for inventing meanings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

顔 (かお) is the practical general noun for a person's face.
側 (がわ), "side," can identify a side or position. 面 instead suits a surface or an
aspect considered in an evaluation.

## Distinctive meaning

The cube and plan examples show why "face" and "aspect" need different English translations.
面 is not a general word for a corner: geometric corners and surfaces are different.
Choose the meaning from the compound or surrounding context rather than replacing every English "side" with 面.
