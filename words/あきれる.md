# あきれる - be appalled; be astounded

## Meaning

あきれる describes being amazed, shocked, exasperated, or fed up.
It often conveys disbelief or disapproval rather than a simple startle reaction.
It is an intransitive ichidan verb: あきれます, あきれて, あきれた.

- 彼の無責任な発言にあきれました。 (かれのむせきにんなはつげんにあきれました。) - I was appalled by his irresponsible remark.
- 同じ言い訳ばかりで、もうあきれています。 (おなじいいわけばかりで、もうあきれています。) - I'm fed up with hearing the same excuses.

に can mark what causes the reaction. The kanji spelling 呆れる is also recorded;
this card retains the source's kana spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[驚く](驚く.md) (おどろく) and [びっくり](びっくり.md)する describe surprise more generally.
[感心](感心.md) (かんしん)する expresses being impressed or admiring something,
rather than the disapproval illustrated above.

## Distinctive meaning

The negative reaction is a strong tendency in these examples, not a rule that the word can
never express sheer amazement. あきれた can also evaluate something as outrageous;
it is not an automatic compliment equivalent to every English "amazing."
