# 父親 (ちちおや) - father

## Meaning

父親 means a father, presenting the person in the parental role. It is a noun and can refer to one's own father, someone else's father, or fathers generally. Identify whose father with の.

- 彼女の父親は医者です。 (かのじょのちちおやはいしゃです。) - Her father is a doctor.
- 彼は去年、父親になりました。 (かれはきょねん、ちちおやになりました。) - He became a father last year.

父親として (ちちおやとして) means "as a father," useful when discussing that role or its responsibilities. The word itself does not express a particular age or personality.

## How the kanji combine

[父](../kanji/父.md) means father and is read ちち here. [親](../kanji/親.md) means parent and is read おや. The native compound joins "father" and "parent," making the family role explicit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

父 (ちち) is a compact way to refer to one's own father, particularly when speaking outside the family. [お父さん](お父さん.md) (おとうさん) can address a father and can respectfully refer to someone else's father.

[母親](母親.md) (ははおや), "mother," is a corresponding family role, not an opposite in the evaluative sense.

## Distinctive meaning

父親 is useful in descriptions of family relationships and parenthood. It is not the default affectionate call "Dad!" to one's own father. Nor does choosing 父親 automatically signal dislike or emotional distance: the sentence may simply focus on the role rather than the speaker's way of addressing the person.
