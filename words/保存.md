# 保存 (ほぞん) - preservation; saving data

## Meaning

保存 is a noun for preservation or storage. 保存する is
transitive: what is preserved or saved takes を. It applies to
physical things such as food and documents, and to saving computer
data to storage.

- この食品は冷蔵庫で保存してください。 (このしょくひんはれいぞうこでほぞんしてください。) - Please keep this food refrigerated.
- 作業が終わったら、ファイルを保存してください。 (さぎょうがおわったら、ファイルをほぞんしてください。) - Save the file when you finish working.

で can identify the storage environment, while に can identify
the destination: パソコンに保存する, "save on the computer."
保存した is the past form, and 保存しない is the negative.

## How the kanji combine

保 contributes protecting or keeping, and [存](../kanji/存.md)
contributes existing or remaining. The compound combines these
related ideas into keeping something in existence or usable condition.
The readings are ほ + ぞん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[残す](残す.md) (のこす) is broader "leave behind; keep."
保管 (ほかん) emphasizes keeping an item in custody or storage.
Deleting a file is a contextual contrast to saving it, not a
general antonym for every preservation use.

## Distinctive meaning

保存 does not guarantee permanent survival or perfect condition.
The sentence may specify an appropriate temperature, location, or
file destination. In a computer interface, 保存 refers to writing
data to storage, not merely leaving the document open on screen.
