# 零れる (こぼれる) - spill out; overflow

## Meaning

零れる means that liquid, grains, or similar contents spill or fall out. It is usually written **こぼれる**. It is an intransitive ichidan verb: こぼれます, こぼれて, こぼれた.

- コップが倒れて、水がこぼれました。 (コップがたおれて、みずがこぼれました。) - The glass tipped over, and water spilled out.
- 袋から米がこぼれています。 (ふくろからこめがこぼれています。) - Rice is spilling from the bag.
- 彼女の顔に笑みがこぼれました。 (かのじょのかおにえみがこぼれました。) - A smile appeared on her face.

What spills takes が; から can identify the container or opening. Light can also come through a gap: 葉の間から光がこぼれる (はのあいだからひかりがこぼれる), "light filters through the leaves."

## How the kanji combine

[零](../kanji/零.md) has a spilling use in addition to its numerical use. In 零れる it writes こぼ, followed by れる; it is not read れいれる. 溢れる is another recognized spelling for こぼれる, but the same written form can also represent the separate reading あふれる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[こぼす](こぼす.md) is transitive: 水をこぼす, "spill water." 零れる centers on the contents spilling, without taking them as a direct object.

[あふれる](あふれる.md) emphasizes exceeding a container's capacity or a boundary. A glass tipping over can make water こぼれる even when it was not full; that scene does not require あふれる. The meanings overlap, but the verbs are not interchangeable everywhere.

## Distinctive meaning

The ending れる does not make this a passive form of こぼす. It is a separate intransitive verb. Tears, smiles, and light can "come out" figuratively or through an opening without a vessel overflowing.
