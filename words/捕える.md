# 捕える (とらえる) - catch; grasp; captivate

## Meaning

捕える is a recorded spelling of とらえる, also written
**捕らえる** and **捉える**. This transitive ichidan verb can mean
catch or seize, grasp a meaning or feature, and captivate someone's
heart. The object takes を.

- 警察は逃げた男を捕らえました。 (けいさつはにげたおとこをとらえました。) - The police caught the man who had fled.
- 話の要点を捉えてください。 (はなしのようてんをとらえてください。) - Please grasp the main points of the discussion.
- その歌は多くの人の心を捉えました。 (そのうたはおおくのひとのこころをとらえました。) - That song captured many people's hearts.

With the spelling 捕らえる, forms include 捕らえます
(とらえます), 捕らえて (とらえて), and 捕らえない
(とらえない). The spelling 捕える has forms such as 捕えた,
also read とらえた; do not pronounce it とえた.

## How the kanji combine

[捕](../kanji/捕.md) contributes catching or capturing. This is a
native verb, not an on-reading compound. 捉える uses 捉 with the
same whole reading とらえる. The dictionary groups the spellings;
that does not establish an absolute physical-versus-mental boundary
between separate verbs.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[捕まえる](捕まえる.md) (つかまえる) overlaps with catching.
[掴む](掴む.md) (つかむ) can describe both a physical grasp and
grasping an idea. [理解](理解.md) (りかい)する concerns understanding.
[逮捕](逮捕.md) (たいほ) specifically concerns arrest, not every
kind of catching or perception.

## Distinctive meaning

The object is crucial: catching a person, grasping the point, and
capturing someone's interest are different uses. 心を捉える
describes the effect on someone's heart or attention; it does not
mean physically taking hold of that organ.
