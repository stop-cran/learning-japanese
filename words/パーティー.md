# パーティー - party; social gathering

## Meaning

パーティー commonly means a social party or celebration. It is a noun.

- 土曜日にパーティーを開きます。 (どようびにパーティーをひらきます。) - We are holding a party on Saturday.
- 友達の誕生日パーティーに行きました。 (ともだちのたんじょうびパーティーにいきました。) - I went to a friend's birthday party.

パーティーを開く means to hold a party; に marks the event attended with 行く.

## How the kanji combine

This English loanword uses katakana. ティ combines テ with small ィ for the "ti" sound, and both long marks belong to the spelling パーティー. パーティ is another recognized form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

集まり (あつまり), "gathering," is broader and need not be celebratory. 宴会 (えんかい) often suggests a banquet or organized meal-and-drinks gathering.

## Distinctive meaning

The word can also name a group participating in an activity, such as climbing. This article's examples teach the social-event sense; English "party" does not imply the same interpretation in every context.
