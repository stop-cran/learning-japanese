# 一種 (いっしゅ) - a kind; a sort

## Meaning

一種 is a noun meaning one kind, variety, or species. The pattern **Xの一種** classifies something
as a kind of X. **一種のX** can also mean "a sort of X," making an approximate or figurative characterization.

- イルカは哺乳類の一種です。 (イルカはほにゅうるいのいっしゅです。) - Dolphins are a kind of mammal.
- これは一種のゲームです。 (これはいっしゅのゲームです。) - This is a sort of game.

The second sentence can describe an activity as game-like rather than identify a formal category.
Context decides whether 一種の is literal classification or a looser comparison.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), "one," specifies [種](../kanji/種.md), "kind."
The on-reading combination contracts to いっしゅ, with a small っ before しゅ.
Here 種 means a category, not a seed being counted.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[種類](種類.md) (しゅるい) names kinds or categories in general.
一種類 (いっしゅるい) explicitly counts one kind, while 一種の can soften a description.
[同じ](同じ.md) (おなじ), "the same," does not express the same classification relationship.
There is no useful single opposite covering both uses of 一種.

## Distinctive meaning

一種 does not say that two things are identical: calling an activity 一種のゲーム leaves room for differences
from an ordinary game. Keep the position of の in view. Xの一種 places something inside category X;
一種のX can present X as an explanatory label.
