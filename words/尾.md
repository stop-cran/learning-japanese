# 尾 (お) — tail; trailing end

## Meaning

尾 (お) is an animal’s tail. It can also name a trailing part, such as the tail of a kite or comet.

- 犬が尾を振っている。 (いぬがおをふっている。) — The dog is wagging its tail.
- 夜空に長い尾を引く彗星が見えた。 (よぞらにながいおをひくすいせいがみえた。) — A comet with a long trailing tail was visible in the night sky.

## How the kanji combine

[尾](../kanji/尾.md) writes the whole noun お. In 語尾 (ごび), “word ending,” the same character is read び, not お.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[しっぽ](しっぽ.md) also names an animal’s tail. [端](端.md) (はし) can mean an edge or end; the edge of a table is not an animal-like tail.

## Distinctive meaning

A comet’s 尾 extends behind its visible head. The comparison is about the trailing shape, not an animal body part.
