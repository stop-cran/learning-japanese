# 左右 (さゆう) — left and right

## Meaning

左右 means "left and right": 左右を見る (look left and right), 左右対称 さゆうたいしょう (symmetrical). As a verb, 左右する (to affect, decide) as in 運命を左右する うんめいをさゆうする.

The direction word is a noun. The influence use is a transitive する verb:
mark what is affected with を.

- 道を渡る前に左右を見てください。 (みちをわたるまえにさゆうをみてください。) - Look left and right before crossing the road.
- 天気が試合の結果を左右します。 (てんきがしあいのけっかをさゆうします。) - The weather affects the result of the game.

## How the kanji combine

[左](../kanji/左.md) and [右](../kanji/右.md), two opposites joined: サ + ユウ (the ユウ reading of 右). 右 is read ウ in 右折 うせつ, but in this word it is ユウ.
Left comes first in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 両側 りょうがわ (both sides).
- Related: [右手](右手.md), [左手](左手.md).

## Distinctive meaning

The pair is a unit; 右左 みぎひだり is also used for "right-left" (as in 右左を間違える), with the kun readings.
In the weather example, 左右する means influence rather than physically moving
the result left or right. It does not require deliberate control by a person.
