# 競技 (きょうぎ) - sporting event; competition

## Meaning

競技 names a contest, sporting event, or competitive sport. It can identify either the activity being contested or an event in a program. The related 競技する means to compete.

- この競技には十人が参加します。 (このきょうぎにはじゅうにんがさんかします。) - Ten people will take part in this event.
- 水泳は好きな競技の一つです。 (すいえいはすきなきょうぎのひとつです。) - Swimming is one of my favorite sports.

競技に参加する (きょうぎにさんかする) marks the event with に. 競技が始まる (きょうぎがはじまる) means "the event starts."

## How the kanji combine

競 contributes competing, and 技 means skill or technique. Together they suggest a contest of skills. The compound uses the on readings キョウ + ギ; there is no need to read either character with a standalone native reading here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[試合](試合.md) (しあい) often identifies a particular match or game. 競技 can name the sport itself, as the swimming example shows. [競争](競争.md) (きょうそう) is competition more broadly and can concern business or other non-sporting rivalry.

## Distinctive meaning

English "game" is not sufficient in every context. A sporting event may be 競技, but a casual board game or a video game is not automatically described with this word. Read the surrounding event or sport name rather than assuming a one-to-one translation.
