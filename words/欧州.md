# 欧州 (おうしゅう) - Europe

## Meaning

欧州 is a Sino-Japanese name for Europe.

- 来月、欧州へ出張します。 (らいげつ、おうしゅうへしゅっちょうします。) - Next month I will travel to Europe on business.
- 欧州の歴史を勉強しています。 (おうしゅうのれきしをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying European history.

## How the kanji combine

[欧](../kanji/欧.md) represents Europe; [州](../kanji/州.md) contributes
a geographic region or division here. The compound names Europe; 州 does
not mean a US state in every word containing it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ヨーロッパ](ヨーロッパ.md) is the loanword for Europe and overlaps in its
basic reference. 欧州 is a compact compound useful in geographic writing;
that does not make it a different continent.

北欧 (ほくおう) refers to northern Europe or the Nordic region, rather
than Europe as a whole. Its exact geographic scope depends on context.

## Distinctive meaning

Europe and the European Union are not identical categories. 欧州 alone
does not assert that a country is an EU member. Likewise, the word does not
specify that every statement about Europe applies uniformly to every country.

Keep the long おう in おうしゅう; the reading is not おしゅう.
