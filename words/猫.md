# 猫 (ねこ) — cat

## Meaning

猫 is a cat, especially a domestic cat. The noun itself does not specify male or female, young or adult,
or whether the cat belongs to someone.

- 猫が窓のそばにいます。 (ねこがまどのそばにいます。) — There is a cat by the window.
- この猫は私の猫です。 (このねこはわたしのねこです。) — This cat is mine.

In the first example, います presents the cat as a living creature; the sentence is not describing an object with あります.

## How the kanji combine

There is one written kanji: [猫](../kanji/猫.md) carries the native word ねこ.
This is a kun reading, not the on reading ビョウ.
Although the character's left radical is the 犭 form of 犬, the complete character means cat, not a kind of dog.

## Synonyms and antonyms

子猫 (こねこ) narrows the meaning to a kitten or small cat.
愛猫 (あいびょう) can refer to one's beloved pet cat; it adds the owner's affectionate perspective rather than naming a breed.
[犬](犬.md) いぬ is a useful contrasting animal, not an antonym of 猫.

## Distinctive meaning

猫 works as an ordinary animal noun. Use surrounding words to add information: 小さな猫 (ちいさなねこ) is a small cat,
and 私の猫 (わたしのねこ) is my cat.
The same spelling can occur in specialized expressions, but the animal meaning is the focus here.
