# 増減 (ぞうげん) - increase and decrease; fluctuation

## Meaning

増減 concerns increases and decreases in an amount or number.
It can describe fluctuation or adjustment upward or downward.
増減する has both intransitive and transitive uses.

- 季節によって利用者の数が増減します。 (きせつによってりようしゃのかずがぞうげんします。) - The number of users rises and falls with the seasons.
- 必要に応じて人数を増減します。 (ひつようにおうじてにんずうをぞうげんします。) - We adjust the number of people upward or downward as needed.

In the first sentence, the number takes が and changes.
In the second, を marks the number being adjusted.
Neither pattern requires that every single adjustment include both directions.

## How the kanji combine

[増](../kanji/増.md) contributes increasing, and
[減](../kanji/減.md) decreasing.
The opposing operations combine as ゾウ + ゲン.
They concern changes in quantity, not automatically success and failure.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[増加](増加.md) (ぞうか) focuses on an increase;
[減る](減る.md) (へる) describes decreasing.
[変化](変化.md) (へんか) is broader change, not necessarily a
numerical rise or fall. [加減](加減.md) (かげん) overlaps when
adjusting an amount, but also has condition and degree uses.

## Distinctive meaning

増減を調べる (ぞうげんをしらべる), "examine changes in quantity,"
can compare amounts across periods without assuming a repeated cycle.
The compound does not itself give the size, speed, or cause of the changes.
For increase alone, [増大](増大.md) (ぞうだい) also concerns becoming greater.
