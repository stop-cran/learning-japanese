# 発見 (はっけん) - discovery; detection

## Meaning

発見 is discovery, detection, or finding something. 発見する is transitive:
mark what is found with を. The discovery can concern something previously
unknown to everyone, or something simply unnoticed in the situation.

- 研究者が新しい星を発見しました。 (けんきゅうしゃがあたらしいほしをはっけんしました。) - Researchers discovered a new star.
- 書類に小さな間違いを発見しました。 (しょるいにちいさなまちがいをはっけんしました。) - I found a small error in the document.

に marks where the error was found in the second example.
発見された means "was discovered" and lets the sentence focus on the finding
rather than the discoverer.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) contributes bringing forth or bringing to light here;
[見](../kanji/見.md) contributes seeing or finding.
ハツ + ケン becomes ハッケン, with the small っ marking the doubled consonant.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[見つける](見つける.md) (みつける) also means finding and works in many everyday
situations. [発明](発明.md) (はつめい), "invention," concerns devising something;
discovering a star does not mean creating it.

## Distinctive meaning

発見 does not require a scientific breakthrough: noticing an error is also a
finding. It also does not mean merely looking for something. A search may end
without a discovery, while a discovery can happen by accident. Keep the result
of finding distinct from the effort spent searching.
