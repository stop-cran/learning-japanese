# 広場 (ひろば) - plaza; open square

## Meaning

広場 is a plaza or public square, and also an open space or clearing.
It can be a place where people gather, without naming a particular shape.

- 駅前の広場で友達と会いました。 (えきまえのひろばでともだちとあいました。) - I met a friend in the plaza in front of the station.
- 森の中に小さな広場がありました。 (もりのなかにちいさなひろばがありました。) - There was a small clearing in the forest.

The first example uses で for the meeting location.
The second illustrates open space rather than a paved city plaza.

## How the kanji combine

[広](../kanji/広.md) contributes breadth or openness, and
[場](../kanji/場.md) a place. The first qualifies the second:
an open place. The native readings combine as ひろ + ば.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[公園](公園.md) (こうえん) is a public park.
A park can contain a 広場, but a plaza is not necessarily a park.
[場所](場所.md) (ばしょ) is a place or location more generally.
There is no single direct opposite covering every kind of open space.

## Distinctive meaning

English "square" here names a gathering space, not a requirement
for four equal sides. A 広場 need not be large, as 小さな広場 shows.
The word can also be used figuratively for a forum for exchange or
discussion; that use does not require an actual outdoor meeting.
