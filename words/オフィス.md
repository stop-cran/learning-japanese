# オフィス (オフィス) - office; business workspace

## Meaning

オフィス is an office: a place used for business or administrative work.
It is a noun, taking に for a destination and で for where work is done.

- 明日はオフィスで働きます。 (あしたはオフィスではたらきます。) - I will work at the office tomorrow.
- 新しいオフィスは駅の近くにあります。 (あたらしいオフィスはえきのちかくにあります。) - The new office is near the station.

オフィスに戻る (オフィスにもどる) means "return to the office."
The noun does not itself say whether someone owns, rents, or shares the space.

## How the kanji combine

This is the katakana loanword corresponding to English "office."
フィ uses a small ィ to represent the sound fi; オフイス with a full-sized
イ is not the spelling taught here. There are no written kanji to decompose.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[事務所](事務所.md) (じむしょ) also means an office and substantially overlaps.
[会社](会社.md) (かいしゃ) is a company; its office is one place where its
work is done, not necessarily the entire organization.

## Distinctive meaning

オフィス identifies the workspace rather than a public position held by an
official. English "take office" therefore cannot be translated by simply
using オフィス. Nor does the loanword by itself guarantee a fashionable,
large, or open-plan room: those are additional descriptions of an office,
not part of the noun's basic meaning.
