# ひとまず - for now; for the time being

## Meaning

ひとまず marks an action or conclusion as sufficient for the present,
leaving later steps or reconsideration open.

- ひとまず休みましょう。 (ひとまずやすみましょう。) - Let's take a break for now.
- ひとまずこれで大丈夫です。 (ひとまずこれでだいじょうぶです。) - This will do for the time being.

The dictionary also records an "after a fashion" use: something is done,
though not entirely satisfactorily. That use does not say that the result
is excellent or complete.

## How the kanji combine

The word is usually written in kana. 一先ず is a recognized spelling:
一 contributes ひと and 先ず corresponds to まず.
Learn the adverb as a whole, rather than treating it as a number followed
by an independent command to go first.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[一応](一応.md) (いちおう) overlaps in provisional sufficiency.
[一旦](一旦.md) (いったん) can mark a temporary stopping point, while
[しばらく](しばらく.md) concerns a while and can also mean for the present.
[まず](まず.md) can introduce the first step and also has a for-now use.
These overlaps do not make every use interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

ひとまず does not set an exact duration or promise what will happen next.
It presents the current action or judgment as an interim response.
Compare doing something for now with listing the first item in a fixed
sequence: English "first" alone does not explain the provisional nuance.
