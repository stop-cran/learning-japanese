# 勤める (つとめる) — work for; be employed

## Meaning

勤める describes working for an employer or at an institution. For this use, mark the employer with に.
The ongoing form 勤めている (つとめている) describes being employed there.

- 姉は銀行に勤めています。 (あねはぎんこうにつとめています。) — My older sister works for a bank.

It is an ichidan verb: remove the final る to form 勤めます (つとめます), 勤めた (つとめた),
or 勤めて (つとめて).

## How the kanji combine

[勤](../kanji/勤.md) associates the native verb with employment or service.
The kana める completes the word; the reading is not built from the on reading キン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

働く (はたらく), "work," can focus on the activity: 銀行で働く (ぎんこうではたらく),
"work at a bank." Compare its location marker で with 銀行に勤める.
The two descriptions can refer to the same person's employment.

務める (つとめる) means fulfilling a role, while 努める (つとめる) means making an effort.
The shared pronunciation does not make these spellings freely interchangeable.

## Distinctive meaning

勤める is useful when identifying where someone is employed, rather than what they are doing at this moment.
The bank example does not imply that the sister is currently at her desk.
Other dictionary senses exist, but this article focuses on employment.
