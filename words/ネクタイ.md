# ネクタイ — necktie

## Meaning

ネクタイ is a necktie, usually worn with a collared shirt. It does not mean every kind of knot or tie in English.

今日は青いネクタイをしています。 (きょうはあおいネクタイをしています。) — I am wearing a blue tie today.

ネクタイを締めてから出かけます。 (ネクタイをしめてからでかけます。) — I will put on my tie before going out.

## How the kanji combine

ネクタイ is a katakana loanword from "necktie." There is no kanji decomposition. The final タイ represents the "tie" part of the borrowing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

蝶ネクタイ (ちょうネクタイ) is a bow tie. マフラー is a scarf, another neck accessory with a different shape and use. Neither is an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Use ネクタイをする or ネクタイを締める for wearing or putting on a tie, and ネクタイを外す (ネクタイをはずす) for taking it off. Do not extend 着る (きる), the verb for upper-body clothing, to this accessory.
