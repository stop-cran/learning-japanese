# 月 (つき) - moon

## Meaning

月, read つき, commonly means the moon.

- 今夜は月がきれいです。 (こんやはつきがきれいです。) - The moon is beautiful tonight.
- 雲の間から月が見えます。 (くものあいだからつきがみえます。) - The moon is visible between the clouds.

The same reading can also refer to a month in expressions such as 次の月 (つぎのつき), "the following month."

## How the kanji combine

The single character [月](../kanji/月.md) writes the native word つき. Compound readings include ゲツ and ガツ, but these are not interchangeable pronunciations in every word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

太陽 (たいよう), "sun," is a related celestial object, not a strict antonym. 月光 (げっこう) specifically means moonlight.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the vocabulary item separate from the character's calendar uses: 一月 (いちがつ) is January, not "one moon." Learn each compound's reading and meaning rather than replacing つき mechanically.
