# 感激 (かんげき) - being profoundly moved; deep emotion

## Meaning

感激 is a deep emotional response: being strongly moved or impressed.
感激する describes experiencing that response.
The thing that moves someone can take に.

- 友達の温かい言葉に感激しました。
  (ともだちのあたたかいことばにかんげきしました。) - I was deeply moved by my friend's kind words.
- 憧れの歌手に会えて、感激しました。
  (あこがれのかしゅにあえて、かんげきしました。) - I was thrilled to meet the singer I admire.

The second sentence gives the occasion with 会えて, "being able to meet."
感激 describes the person's response, not a property that every listener
must find equally moving.

## How the kanji combine

[感](../kanji/感.md) contributes feeling, while 激 contributes intensity.
The second character strengthens the emotional association of the first.
The on readings カン + ゲキ give かんげき.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[感動](感動.md) (かんどう) overlaps strongly in being moved.
[感心](感心.md) (かんしん) often evaluates something with admiration.
Do not turn these tendencies into a rule that every moving event admits
only one of the words.

## Distinctive meaning

感激 can express a powerful response to kindness, an encounter, or a performance.
It is not a neutral noun for every [感情](感情.md) (かんじょう), "emotion."
[感謝](感謝.md) (かんしゃ) explicitly concerns gratitude:
being moved by a kind act and thanking someone for it are related but
not identical descriptions.
