# くるり — a quick turn around

## Meaning

A turn presented as one quick, compact movement.

- 猫がくるりと振（ふ）り返った。 — The cat quickly turned to look back.
- ダンサーがその場でくるりと回（まわ）った。 — The dancer made a quick turn on the spot.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[くるくる](くるくる.md) often presents repeated or continuing turns. The forms can describe related movements; a single picture may not establish which description fits.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn くるりと回る and くるりと振り返る. A quick reversal of attitude can also be described with くるり. The turning use does not specify a precise angle or insist on a full 360-degree revolution.
