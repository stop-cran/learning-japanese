# 近代 (きんだい) - modern era

## Meaning

近代 is a noun for modern times or the modern era. It can refer broadly to a relatively recent age, or to a defined historical period. **近代の + noun** describes something belonging to that era.

- 大学で日本の近代史を勉強しています。 (だいがくでにほんのきんだいしをべんきょうしています。) - I am studying modern Japanese history at university.
- この本は近代の社会を描いています。 (このほんはきんだいのしゃかいをえがいています。) - This book depicts society in the modern era.

近代史 (きんだいし) is "modern history." In Japanese historical periodization, 近代 can cover the period from the Meiji Restoration to the end of World War II. Boundaries depend on the historical framework and country.

## How the kanji combine

[近](../kanji/近.md), "near," modifies [代](../kanji/代.md), "age; era." The compound suggests an age near the present in historical perspective. Its on readings are キン + ダイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[現代](現代.md) (げんだい) emphasizes the contemporary era. [現在](現在.md) (げんざい) is "the present; now," and [最近](最近.md) (さいきん) means "recently." These are not automatic replacements for a historical period label.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not treat 近代 as simply "today." A work described as 近代文学 (きんだいぶんがく), "modern literature," may be much older than today's publications. The English label "modern" needs the same attention to context.
