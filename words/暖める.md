# 暖める (あたためる) - warm; heat up

## Meaning

暖める means to warm or heat something. It is a **transitive ichidan verb**:
what is warmed takes を. Forms include 暖めます (あたためます),
暖めて (あたためて), and 暖めた (あたためた).

- ストーブで部屋を暖めます。 (ストーブでへやをあたためます。) - I warm the room with a heater.
- 牛乳を温めてから飲みます。 (ぎゅうにゅうをあたためてからのみます。) - I warm the milk before drinking it.

The same reading is also written 温める: スープを温める (スープをあたためる),
"warm up soup." Warming does not necessarily mean bringing something to the boil.

## How the kanji combine

暖 expresses warmth; める completes this native verb. The alternative 温める uses
[温](../kanji/温.md). This article teaches the temperature use shared by the two spellings,
not every figurative use recorded under 温める.
For physical warming, 暖 tends to be used for rooms or air, while 温 is usual
for food or drink. These are spelling tendencies rather than an absolute ban on overlap;
compare the adjective [暖かい](暖かい.md) (あたたかい).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[暖まる](暖まる.md) (あたたまる) describes becoming warm:
部屋を暖める, "warm the room," versus 部屋が暖まる, "the room warms up."
[沸かす](沸かす.md) (わかす) concerns heating a liquid, including boiling water.
冷ます (さます), "let something cool," can supply the reverse action.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the verb classes separate: 暖める is ichidan, but 暖まる is godan.
The difference is not politeness or the amount of heat; it is whether the sentence
presents an action on something or that thing's change of state.
