# 愛情 (あいじょう) - love; affection

## Meaning

愛情 is a noun for affectionate love or care, including feelings toward family, a partner, or an animal. Useful patterns are 人への愛情, "affection for someone," and 愛情を注ぐ (あいじょうをそそぐ), "lavish affection on."

- 子供に愛情を注いで育てました。 (こどもにあいじょうをそそいでそだてました。) - I raised my child with loving care.
- この手紙から母の愛情を感じます。 (このてがみからははのあいじょうをかんじます。) - I feel my mother's affection in this letter.

In the first sentence, に marks the recipient of the affection; を marks 愛情 as the object of 注ぐ. 愛情の深い人 (あいじょうのふかいひと) is "a deeply affectionate person."

## How the kanji combine

[愛](../kanji/愛.md) means love, and [情](../kanji/情.md) contributes feeling or emotion. Together they specify a loving feeling, rather than emotion in general. The readings are アイ + ジョウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[愛](愛.md) overlaps closely. 愛情 makes the affectionate feeling explicit. [恋](恋.md) (こい) concerns romantic love; 愛情 need not be romantic. [感情](感情.md) (かんじょう) is emotion more broadly and can include anger or sadness.

## Distinctive meaning

愛情 describes the feeling, not a particular relationship status. Someone can show it through everyday care without making a declaration of love. Learn 愛情を示す (あいじょうをしめす), "show affection," rather than treating 愛情 as a verb by itself.
