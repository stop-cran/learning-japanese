# 過半数 (かはんすう) - majority; more than half

## Meaning

過半数 is a majority strictly exceeding half of a specified total. It is a noun.

- 出席者の過半数がその案に賛成しました。 (しゅっせきしゃのかはんすうがそのあんにさんせいしました。) - More than half of those present supported the proposal.
- 十人のうち六人なら過半数ですが、五人では過半数になりません。 (じゅうにんのうちろくにんならかはんすうですが、ごにんではかはんすうになりません。) - Six out of ten is a majority, but five is not.

Useful patterns are 過半数を占める (かはんすうをしめる), account for more than half, and 過半数に達する (かはんすうにたっする), reach a majority.

## How the kanji combine

[過](../kanji/過.md) contributes exceeding, [半](../kanji/半.md) half, and [数](../kanji/数.md) a number. Together they identify a number beyond half the total. The on readings are カ + ハン + スウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[大半](大半.md) (たいはん) and [大部分](大部分.md) (だいぶぶん) overlap in the greater part or most of something. 半数 (はんすう) is half the number; 過半数 must exceed that threshold. A minority is a contextual contrast, not necessarily a fixed number of people.

## Distinctive meaning

With a total of 100, 50 does not qualify and 51 does. Merely being the largest group is not enough if it does not exceed half. The relevant total still needs context: the word alone does not decide whether a voting rule counts all members, those present, or votes cast.
