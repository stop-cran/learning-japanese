# 探す (さがす) — look for

## Meaning

探す means looking for or searching for something. The thing sought takes を.
In 仕事を探す (しごとをさがす), work is what you want to find, not a place you are inspecting.

- 新しい仕事を探しています。 (あたらしいしごとをさがしています。) — I am looking for a new job.
- 駅の近くで部屋を探しています。 (えきのちかくでへやをさがしています。) — I am looking for a room near the station.

The past is 探した (さがした), and the negative is 探さない (さがさない).

## How the kanji combine

[探](../kanji/探.md) supplies the searching idea, and す completes the native verb.
The same kanji also appears in 探る (さぐる), "feel around; investigate," but that is a different verb.

## Synonyms and antonyms

見つける (みつける), "find," names a successful discovery rather than the search.
[調べる](調べる.md) (しらべる), "check; investigate," is useful for examining information or details.
Looking for a room with 探す and checking its details with 調べる describe different tasks, though the verbs can overlap in broader search contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

探した says someone searched; it does not by itself report that they found what they wanted.
JMdict particularly associates 探す with something desired and 捜す (さがす) with something lost.
Treat that as a helpful spelling tendency, not an absolute rule that every search must fall into exactly one category.
