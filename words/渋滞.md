# 渋滞 (じゅうたい) - traffic congestion

## Meaning

渋滞 is congestion, especially traffic moving slowly or being backed up.
渋滞する is intransitive: a road or its traffic can be marked with が.
渋滞している describes the ongoing congested state.

- 高速道路が渋滞しています。 (こうそくどうろがじゅうたいしています。) - Traffic is backed up on the expressway.
- 渋滞のため、到着が三十分遅れました。 (じゅうたいのため、とうちゃくがさんじゅっぷんおくれました。) - Because of a traffic jam, we arrived thirty minutes late.

渋滞に巻き込まれる (じゅうたいにまきこまれる) means getting caught in a traffic
jam.

## How the kanji combine

渋 contributes a sense of being obstructed or not proceeding smoothly here;
滞 concerns lingering or stagnating. Their related meanings combine into stalled
or impeded flow. The readings are ジュウ + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[混雑](混雑.md) (こんざつ) is crowding or congestion and can describe a busy shop
or station. 渋滞 particularly suits traffic whose movement is obstructed.
[遅れる](遅れる.md) (おくれる), "be late," can name a consequence, but many delays
have no traffic jam as their cause.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary also records delay or stagnation more broadly, so 渋滞 is not
confined to cars in every use. Nevertheless, a traffic scene is a useful anchor.
It does not state that every vehicle is completely stationary; slow, backed-up
movement can also be described as 渋滞.
