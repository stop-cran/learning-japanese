# 給与 (きゅうよ) - pay; provision of money or goods

## Meaning

給与 commonly names pay received for work. It can include salary and allowances. It also means providing money or goods; in that use, 給与する is a transitive verb.

- 毎月の給与は銀行口座に振り込まれます。
  (まいつきのきゅうよはぎんこうこうざにふりこまれます。) - The monthly pay is deposited into a bank account.
- 会社は従業員に制服を給与しています。
  (かいしゃはじゅうぎょういんにせいふくをきゅうよしています。) - The company provides uniforms to its employees.

The second sentence illustrates the provision sense, not wages made out of uniforms. A recipient can take に and the money or goods を.

## How the kanji combine

[給](../kanji/給.md) and [与](../kanji/与.md) both contribute giving or providing. Their related meanings reinforce each other. The on readings are キュウ + ヨ; keep the long vowel in きゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[給料](給料.md) (きゅうりょう) closely overlaps the employment-pay use. [支給](支給.md) (しきゅう) focuses on supplying or paying something to recipients; [供給](供給.md) (きょうきゅう) is another supply/provision word.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not enforce an exceptionless rule that 給料 always excludes allowances while 給与 always includes every possible benefit. Ordinary meanings overlap, and a document's payroll definitions require its own context. The provision sense is useful in formal descriptions of what an organization furnishes, not only cash payments.
