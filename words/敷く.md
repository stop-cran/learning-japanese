# 敷く (しく) - lay out; spread underneath

## Meaning

敷く usually describes laying something out or putting it underneath, such as a futon, carpet, or mat. In this core use it is a transitive godan verb: the laid-out item takes を and its location takes に.

- 畳の上に布団を敷きました。 (たたみのうえにふとんをしきました。) - I laid out a futon on the tatami.
- 床にじゅうたんを敷きます。 (ゆかにじゅうたんをしきます。) - We lay a carpet on the floor.

Forms include 敷きます, 敷いた, 敷いて, and 敷かない. Laying tracks is also 敷く: 線路を敷く (せんろをしく).

## How the kanji combine

敷 writes し, with く completing this native verb. Its stem 敷き (しき) appears in [敷地](敷地.md) しきち, site, and 敷き布団 (しきぶとん), the futon mattress laid underneath a sleeper.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[置く](置く.md) おく is broader putting or placing. 敷く brings laying out a layer or placing something underneath into focus. [被せる](被せる.md) かぶせる instead puts something over another object; not every covering takes the same verb.

## Distinctive meaning

The verb also extends to instituting or imposing an arrangement and deploying a network, as in 警戒網を敷く (けいかいもうをしく), establish a security net. The dictionary additionally records holding someone down or under control, including 尻に敷く (しりにしく), keep someone under one's thumb. These are contextual extensions, not instructions to put every abstract object on the floor.

A formal or literary intransitive sense means to spread or become widespread. It does not erase the ordinary transitive pattern taught above. 布く and 藉く are search-only spellings in the pinned dictionary, not recommended replacements in the futon example.
