# 整備 (せいび) - maintenance; putting in order

## Meaning

整備 is maintenance or servicing, and also putting things in proper order or
developing and providing what is needed. 整備する is transitive: what is
maintained or put in place takes を.

- 出発前に車を整備しました。 (しゅっぱつまえにくるまをせいびしました。) - We serviced the car before departure.
- 市は公園の設備を整備しています。 (しはこうえんのせつびをせいびしています。) - The city is putting the park's facilities in order.

道路を整備する (どうろをせいびする) can concern developing or improving roads,
not just repairing a single broken object.

## How the kanji combine

整 contributes making orderly, and [備](../kanji/備.md) contributes equipping
or preparing. Together they suggest putting something into a suitable, usable state.
Read セイ + ビ; 整 has no card here, but remains part of the spelling and metadata.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[修理](修理.md) (しゅうり) focuses on repairing; [維持](維持.md) (いじ)
on maintaining a state. [準備](準備.md) (じゅんび) and [用意](用意.md)
(ようい) concern preparation. [整理](整理.md) (せいり) overlaps when sorting
or organizing things, while [設備](設備.md) (せつび) commonly names the equipment or facilities.

## Distinctive meaning

An object need not already be broken to require 整備. The object tells you whether
the task is vehicle servicing, arranging documents, or developing infrastructure.
Do not reduce every use to repair, or assume that the noun guarantees the work is complete.
