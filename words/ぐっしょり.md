# ぐっしょり — drenched through

## Meaning

Thoroughly soaked, often clothing, bedding, or hair saturated with sweat, rain, or another liquid.

- 運動した後、シャツが汗（あせ）でぐっしょりぬれていた。 — After exercising, my shirt was drenched with sweat.
- 雨に打たれて、髪（かみ）がぐっしょりとぬれた。 — My hair became completely soaked in the rain.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic with small っ and a り ending, not an exact repeated form. The voiced initial sound is a descriptive feature, not a kanji-derived meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[びしょびしょ](びしょびしょ.md) overlaps strongly in “soaking wet.” [じとじと](じとじと.md) foregrounds clammy wetness; [しっとり](しっとり.md) usually describes a milder moist state.

## Distinctive meaning

ぐっしょりぬれる, ぐっしょりと汗をかく, and 汗でぐっしょりだ are useful. It is primarily adverbial; ぐっしょりしたシャツ is possible as a state description, but do not treat ぐっしょりな as the default attachment. The word does not identify the liquid or require that water be visibly dripping.
