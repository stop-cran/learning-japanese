# 御礼 (おれい) — thanks; gratitude

## Meaning

御礼 means thanks or gratitude in the sense selected here. JMdict marks this use as polite.
The same word is also written お礼; the kanji-bearing spelling here makes the prefix visible.

- 先生に御礼を言いました。 (せんせいにおれいをいいました。) — I thanked the teacher.

In Nに御礼を言う (Nにおれいをいう), に marks the person thanked and を marks the thanks expressed.

## How the kanji combine

[御](../kanji/御.md) writes the polite prefix お; [礼](../kanji/礼.md), read レイ, supplies "thanks."
This is a native prefix attached to a Sino-Japanese noun, not an all-on-reading two-character compound.
The article uses the kango category for the Sino-Japanese base 礼 while keeping that mixed construction
explicit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

感謝 (かんしゃ) also means gratitude. Compare 感謝しています (かんしゃしています), "I am grateful,"
with 御礼を言う (おれいをいう), "express thanks." The meanings overlap, but the sentence constructions
are not interchangeable word for word. Gratitude has no useful single opposite for this lesson.

## Distinctive meaning

御礼 is a noun; it is not simply a replacement for the spoken expression ありがとう, "thank you."
Learn a phrase such as 御礼を言う to use it in a sentence. This article teaches the reading おれい
and the gratitude sense, not every reading or meaning associated with the written characters.
