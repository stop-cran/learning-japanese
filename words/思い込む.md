# 思い込む (おもいこむ) - be convinced; assume

## Meaning

思い込む means to feel sure of something or be under the impression that it is true. A plain-form proposition followed by と states the belief.

- 会議は明日だと思い込んでいました。 (かいぎはあしただとおもいこんでいました。) - I was convinced that the meeting was tomorrow.
- 彼は自分が正しいと思い込んでいます。 (かれはじぶんがただしいとおもいこんでいます。) - He is convinced that he is right.

It is an intransitive godan verb: 思い込みます (おもいこみます), 思い込んだ (おもいこんだ), 思い込まない (おもいこまない). The quoted clause before と is the content of the conviction, not a person being convinced with を.

## How the kanji combine

[思](../kanji/思.md)い comes from [思う](思う.md), "think." [込](../kanji/込.md)む contributes an inward or intensive association in the compound. Think of an idea becoming firmly fixed; this is a memory aid, not a literal account of thinking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[信じる](信じる.md) (しんじる) broadly expresses belief or trust. [納得](納得.md) (なっとく) can describe being convinced after something makes sense or is accepted. 思い込む highlights the person's settled conviction rather than proving that the reasons are sound.

## Distinctive meaning

The conviction may turn out to be mistaken, as when a meeting date is misremembered. That possibility is not a rule that every 思い込む belief must be false. This lesson focuses on conviction and assumption, not a general translation of every English "think."
