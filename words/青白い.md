# 青白い (あおじろい) - pale; bluish-white

## Meaning

青白い is an い-adjective for a pale or pallid appearance, especially a complexion.
It can also describe a bluish-white color or light.

- 彼は青白い顔をしていました。 (かれはあおじろいかおをしていました。) - He looked pale.
- 画面から青白い光が出ています。 (がめんからあおじろいひかりがでています。) - Bluish-white light is coming from the screen.

Its negative is 青白くない (あおじろくない); its past form is 青白かった (あおじろかった).

## How the kanji combine

[青](../kanji/青.md), "blue," modifies [白](../kanji/白.md), "white."
The native compound is あおじろい, with じろ rather than しろ in this word.
Learn that reading with the complete adjective.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[青い](青い.md) (あおい) can also describe a pale face, though its other color uses are broader.
[白い](白い.md) (しろい) is the plain white adjective.
血色がいい (けっしょくがいい), "have a healthy complexion," provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

A blue-white light is not ill, and a pale face does not by itself establish a medical diagnosis.
Read the noun being described. This word does not simply replace 青い in its conventional
green uses, such as a green traffic light.
