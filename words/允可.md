# 允可 (いんか) - permission; assent

## Meaning

允可 means permission or assent: accepting a request or allowing something.
It is a noun and can form a transitive verb with する. Learn it primarily
for recognizing formal or older writing, rather than as a default conversational request.

- 允可を得る (いんかをえる) - obtain permission.

This pattern is attested in an 1868 governmental text cited by Nikkoku;
it is shown here in dictionary form, not as a quotation of the whole historical sentence.
允可 is the permission obtained; を marks it as the object of 得る (える), "obtain."
The example does not mean "give permission."

JMdict also glosses the word as "compliance." Read that with the surrounding
request or permission context, not as a universal substitute for every kind of obedience.

## How the kanji combine

[允](../kanji/允.md) contributes permitting or assenting, and 可 contributes
approval or acceptability. The near-synonymous parts reinforce the permission idea.
The on readings are イン + カ, giving いんか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[許可](許可.md) (きょか), "permission; authorization," is a more practical
general-purpose word for permission. [許す](許す.md) (ゆるす) can mean
"allow" but also "forgive"; 允可 does not automatically cover that entire range.
[承認](承認.md) (しょうにん), "approval; recognition," can overlap in an approval context.
There is no single everyday antonym that fits every permission or assent situation.

## Distinctive meaning

Recognizing this word does not mean you need to use it in ordinary conversation.
For example, 写真を撮ってもいいですか (しゃしんをとってもいいですか),
"May I take a photograph?", asks for permission without using 允可.
Nor is 允可 restricted here to one legal procedure or permission from an emperor:
the dictionary definition is broader than any one historical example.
