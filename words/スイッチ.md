# スイッチ (スイッチ) - switch

## Meaning

スイッチ is a switch, such as a control for turning a device on or off.
Useful patterns are スイッチを入れる (スイッチをいれる), "switch on,"
and スイッチを切る (スイッチをきる), "switch off."
The entry also includes switching from one thing to another as スイッチする.

- 暗くなったので、電灯のスイッチを入れました。 (くらくなったので、でんとうのスイッチをいれました。) - It got dark, so I switched on the light.
- 使い終わったら、スイッチを切ってください。 (つかいおわったら、スイッチをきってください。) - Please switch it off when you have finished using it.

In these examples the switch is the object of 入れる or 切る.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana word corresponds to English "switch"; there are no kanji to
divide. The small ッ marks the held consonant before チ.
Keep the Japanese form スイッチ rather than adding English consonant clusters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ボタン](ボタン.md), "button," describes a control's physical form; a button can operate
a switch, but switches need not all be buttons.
オン and オフ contrast operating states. They are opposites in that setting,
not antonyms of the noun スイッチ itself.

## Distinctive meaning

入れる here means activating the switch, not necessarily inserting a loose
object. Likewise, 切る means turning it off, not physically cutting it.
The dictionary's changeover use of スイッチする is broader than these
two power-control expressions, so translate the whole action rather than
assuming every "switch" sentence means the same thing.
