# 世紀 (せいき) - century; era

## Meaning

世紀 is a noun for a century, a period of one hundred years. With a number, it can identify a numbered historical century.

- この建物は十九世紀に建てられました。 (このたてものはじゅうきゅうせいきにたてられました。) - This building was built in the nineteenth century.
- 半世紀が過ぎました。 (はんせいきがすぎました。) - Half a century has passed.

一世紀 (いっせいき) means one century; note the small っ. 世紀 can also refer more broadly to an era, as in 科学の世紀 (かがくのせいき), "an age of science."

## How the kanji combine

[世](../kanji/世.md) contributes an age or generation. 紀 is associated with recording or ordering time. Together they form the established time-period word, read セイ + キ. The hundred-year length belongs to the compound's conventional meaning, not to either character alone.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[時代](時代.md) (じだい), "era; period," does not specify a hundred years. [年代](年代.md) (ねんだい) can identify a range or period of years. A century is a unit or historical division, so it has no useful lexical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

世紀の発見 (せいきのはっけん), "the discovery of the century," uses 世紀の to stress exceptional importance, not to give the discovery's duration. A numbered century and a hundred-year span are also different: the twenty-first century is 2001-2100, whereas an arbitrary hundred-year span need not share those boundaries.
