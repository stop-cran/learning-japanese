# ゲーム - game

## Meaning

ゲーム is the noun "game," including video games, board games, and games in sporting contexts. Use ゲームをする for playing a game. The specific activity or title tells the listener what kind of game is meant.

- 友達とボードゲームをしました。 (ともだちとボードゲームをしました。) - I played a board game with a friend.
- このゲームのルールを教えてください。 (このゲームのルールをおしえてください。) - Please explain the rules of this game.

ゲームに勝つ (ゲームにかつ) means "win a game." The game takes に with 勝つ, rather than the を used in ゲームをする.

## How the kanji combine

ゲーム is a katakana loanword corresponding to English "game." There are no kanji to combine. The long-vowel mark ー lengthens ゲ, so keep that vowel length when speaking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[試合](試合.md) (しあい) emphasizes a match or contest, particularly in sport. [遊び](遊び.md) (あそび) covers play or recreation more broadly; not every playful activity is a game with rules.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not assume that an unqualified ゲーム must mean a video game in every conversation. Board-game and sports settings can supply other interpretations. Likewise, English meanings such as hunted animals or the adjective "willing" are not established by this loanword's ordinary game sense. Learn the Japanese word's constructions rather than importing the whole English entry.
