# 喜ぶ (よろこぶ) — be delighted

## Meaning

喜ぶ means to be glad, pleased, or delighted.
It can describe the reaction itself, or take を with the event someone is glad about.

- 子供たちはプレゼントをもらって喜んでいます。 (こどもたちはプレゼントをもらってよろこんでいます。) — The children are delighted to have received presents.
- 友達の成功を喜びました。 (ともだちのせいこうをよろこびました。) — I was glad about my friend's success.

The polite form is 喜びます (よろこびます); the て-form is 喜んで (よろこんで).

## How the kanji combine

[喜](../kanji/喜.md) carries the meaning of joy and is read よろこ in 喜ぶ; ぶ is written in kana.
This is a native Japanese verb. 喜び (よろこび) is also a noun meaning "joy."
Do not read the verb with the on reading キ used in compounds such as 歓喜 (かんき), "great joy."

## Synonyms and antonyms

嬉しい (うれしい) is an adjective meaning happy or glad; 喜ぶ is a verb.
嬉しいです (うれしいです), "I'm glad," and 喜んでいます (よろこんでいます), "is delighted," use different sentence patterns.
[笑う](笑う.md) (わらう) means laugh or smile. A person can be delighted without visibly laughing, and laughter need not express delight.

## Distinctive meaning

喜ぶ focuses on a positive reaction, not a general statement that something is enjoyable.
In 友達の成功を喜ぶ, the success is what prompts the happiness; を does not mean that the speaker causes the friend's success.
The verb allows both this object pattern and a sentence simply describing someone rejoicing.
