# 環境 (かんきょう) - environment; surroundings

## Meaning

環境 is a noun for the surroundings or conditions in which someone lives or something operates.
It can refer to the natural environment, but also to a home, school, workplace, or technical setting.
Use Nの環境 or an established compound to say which environment you mean.

- 静かな環境で勉強したいです。 (しずかなかんきょうでべんきょうしたいです。) - I want to study in a quiet environment.
- 地球の環境を守ることが大切です。 (ちきゅうのかんきょうをまもることがたいせつです。) - It is important to protect Earth's environment.

で marks the setting of an activity in the first sentence. を marks the environment being protected
in the second.

## How the kanji combine

環 contributes the idea of encircling or surrounding, and 境 concerns a boundary, area, or condition.
The compound names surrounding conditions. Its on readings are カン + キョウ,
not the native word さかい used for 境 in other contexts.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[周り](周り.md) (まわり), "surroundings; around," can focus on what is physically nearby.
[自然](自然.md) (しぜん), "nature," overlaps in environmental topics, but a workplace environment
need not concern nature. There is no general antonym that covers every kind of 環境.

## Distinctive meaning

An environment includes conditions that affect people or activities, not simply a location's name.
職場の環境 (しょくばのかんきょう), "workplace environment," can include social conditions.
[地球](地球.md) (ちきゅう), "Earth," supplies the scale in the environmental-protection example,
not a separate meaning of "planet" inside 環境 itself.
