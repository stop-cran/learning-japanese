# 鈍い (にぶい) - dull; blunt

## Meaning

鈍い describes a blunt edge, a dull sound or color, dim light, or slow or insensitive responses. It is an i-adjective.

- この刃は鈍いです。 (このははにぶいです。) - This blade is blunt.
- 反応が鈍いです。 (はんのうがにぶいです。) - The response is sluggish.
- 鈍い音がしました。 (にぶいおとがしました。) - There was a dull sound.

Use it directly before a noun, as in 鈍い音. The negative is 鈍くない (にぶくない), not 鈍いではない.

## How the kanji combine

[鈍](../kanji/鈍.md) supplies にぶ in this native adjective, with い as the written ending. There is no two-kanji compound to split.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[鋭い](鋭い.md) (するどい), sharp or keen, contrasts with bluntness or slow perception in suitable contexts. [遅い](遅い.md) (おそい) overlaps in slowness, but also means late; 鈍い does not automatically mean missing a deadline.

[のろのろ](のろのろ.md) emphasizes sluggish progress. [ゆっくり](ゆっくり.md) can be neutral or approving about taking time. Neither has the i-adjective grammar of 鈍い, and neither means a blunt cutting edge.

## Distinctive meaning

The noun matters: dull sound, dim light, slow reactions and a blunt edge are different applications, not interchangeable physical properties. Describing someone's perception as 鈍い can sound critical; it is not simply a neutral statement about every kind of intelligence.

English dull can also mean boring, as in [つまらない](つまらない.md) or [退屈](退屈.md). That English overlap does not make 鈍い a general substitute for an uninteresting film. Similarly, dull pain and [しくしく](しくしく.md)'s sustained aching are a contextual comparison, not the same thing as its sobbing sense.

The dictionary separately records 鈍い のろい, usually written in kana, with slowness and other senses. This article teaches にぶい; do not merge all senses merely because the written form can coincide.
