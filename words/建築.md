# 建築 (けんちく) - architecture; building construction

## Meaning

建築 names architecture or the construction of buildings. It is a noun and a transitive する verb: 建物を建築する (たてものをけんちくする), "construct a building."

- 大学で建築を学んでいます。 (だいがくでけんちくをまなんでいます。) - I am studying architecture at university.
- ここに新しい図書館を建築しています。 (ここにあたらしいとしょかんをけんちくしています。) - They are constructing a new library here.

The first sentence concerns a field of study; the second concerns the activity of putting up a building. In the second, に identifies the construction site.

## How the kanji combine

[建](../kanji/建.md) and 築 both contribute building or constructing. Their related meanings reinforce one another rather than forming an object-and-action pair. The on readings are ケン + チク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[建てる](建てる.md) (たてる) is a direct verb for putting up a building. [建設](建設.md) (けんせつ) covers construction more broadly, including infrastructure. [建物](建物.md) (たてもの) names the building itself.

## Distinctive meaning

建築 can focus on design and architectural work as well as physical construction. It does not mean every English "construction," such as constructing a sentence. To name a profession, 建築家 (けんちくか) means "architect"; the added 家 identifies a specialist rather than a house in that compound.
