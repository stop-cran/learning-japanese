# お酒 (おさけ) - alcoholic drink

## Meaning

お酒 refers to alcoholic drinks generally and, in a suitable context, to
Japanese sake. It does not always mean rice wine specifically.

- お酒は飲みません。 (おさけはのみません。) - I do not drink alcohol.
- この料理にはお酒を少し入れます。 (このりょうりにはおさけをすこしいれます。) - I put a little sake in this dish.

## How the kanji combine

お is a polite prefix attached to [酒](../kanji/酒.md), read さけ.
It makes this ordinary everyday expression more courteous; it does not change
the drink's ingredients or create a separate category of alcohol.

## Synonyms and antonyms

日本酒 (にほんしゅ) explicitly names Japanese sake. ビール names beer, which is
also a kind of お酒. アルコール can mean alcohol as a substance as well as drinking.
Water or nonalcoholic drinks are practical alternatives, not lexical antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

An invitation involving お酒 may include beer or wine, not just sake.
Use context or the drink's specific name when the distinction matters.
As an object of 飲む (のむ), "drink," it takes を; contrastive は can replace it.
