# 展覧会 (てんらんかい) — exhibition

## Meaning

展覧会 is an exhibition: an organized event where works or objects are displayed for people to view. Art, photographs, and crafts are typical subjects.

日曜日に写真の展覧会へ行きます。 (にちようびにしゃしんのてんらんかいへいきます。) — I am going to a photography exhibition on Sunday.

## How the kanji combine

展 concerns displaying or unfolding, 覧 concerns viewing, and [会](../kanji/会.md) marks a gathering or event. Their on-readings are てん + らん + かい. The final 会 identifies an exhibition event, not the individual object on display.

## Synonyms and antonyms

展示会 (てんじかい) overlaps with exhibition and is also used for product displays and trade shows. 展示 (てんじ) is display or exhibiting. 博物館 (はくぶつかん), "museum," names a place that may host an exhibition.

## Distinctive meaning

展覧会を開く (てんらんかいをひらく) means "hold an exhibition"; 展覧会を見る (てんらんかいをみる) means "view an exhibition." Its 会場 (かいじょう) is the venue. An exhibition can move between venues, so the event and the building should not be treated as the same thing.
