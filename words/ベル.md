# ベル

## Meaning

ベル means a bell, often a small mechanical or electric device used to signal or attract attention.

- 入口のベルを押してください。 (いりぐちの ベルを おしてください。) — Please press the bell at the entrance.
- 自転車のベルが鳴りました。 (じてんしゃの ベルが なりました。) — The bicycle bell rang.

鳴る (なる) describes the bell ringing; 鳴らす (ならす) means to make it ring.

## How the kanji combine

This loanword is written in katakana. ベ has a voiced consonant and the final ル adapts the borrowed word to Japanese pronunciation. No kanji combination is involved.

## Synonyms and antonyms

鐘 (かね) often refers to a larger bell, such as one at a temple. 鈴 (すず) commonly names a small jingling bell. チャイム is a chime or chime-like signal.

## Distinctive meaning

The best Japanese word depends on the bell's shape and use; ベル is not the only translation of English "bell." It can name the signaling device without specifying whether the resulting sound comes from a metal bell or an electronic mechanism.
