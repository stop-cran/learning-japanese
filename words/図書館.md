# 図書館 (としょかん) — library

## Meaning

図書館 means **library**, a place that provides books and other materials for reading or use.

- 図書館で本を読みます。（としょかんでほんをよみます。）— I read books at the library.
- 図書館で本を借りました。（としょかんでほんをかりました。）— I borrowed a book from the library.

で marks the place where the reading or borrowing takes place.

## How the kanji combine

[図](../kanji/図.md) contributes drawings or diagrams, and [書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing or books.
Together 図書（としょ）is a word for **books**.
Adding [館](../kanji/館.md), "hall; building," gives **books + building**, with 図書 modifying 館.

The standard reading is on + on + on: ト + ショ + カン → としょかん.
図 uses ト here, not the ズ familiar from some other words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [書店](書店.md)（しょてん）means "bookstore": a place selling books rather than a library.
- 図書（としょ）means "books," not the institution itself.
- [読書](読書.md)（どくしょ）means "reading books," an activity that can take place in a library or elsewhere.

There is no natural direct antonym of 図書館.

## Distinctive meaning

A 図書館 is not simply any room containing books, nor is it a shop.
Borrowing is a familiar library activity, but the word does not promise that every item may be taken home.
Learn the full reading としょかん as a unit rather than replacing 図 with ズ.
