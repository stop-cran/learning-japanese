# 語 (ご) - word; language

## Meaning

語 is a noun for a word or term, a suffix in language names, and a counter for words. These uses share the reading ご but require different constructions.

- この語の意味を調べました。 (このごのいみをしらべました。) - I looked up the meaning of this word.
- 日本語で百語ぐらいの文章を書きました。 (にほんごでひゃくごぐらいのぶんしょうをかきました。) - I wrote a passage of about one hundred words in Japanese.

In [日本語](日本語.md) (にほんご), 語 identifies the language. In 百語 (ひゃくご), it counts words.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [語](../kanji/語.md) carries meanings connected with words, language, and speech. This card teaches its on reading ゴ. In a language name, the preceding element identifies which language.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[言葉](言葉.md) (ことば) is a broader everyday word for words, speech, or language. 語 suits talking about a word as a linguistic unit. A character is a different counting unit: 百字 (ひゃくじ), "one hundred characters," is not the same as 百語.

## Distinctive meaning

Recognize the job 語 is doing rather than assigning it one English translation everywhere. It does not independently tell you how word boundaries are counted in a particular assignment. If a Japanese writing task specifies 字, follow its character count instead of silently converting it to a word count.
