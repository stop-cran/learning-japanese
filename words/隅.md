# 隅 (すみ) - corner; nook

## Meaning

隅 is a corner, nook, or out-of-the-way part of a space. It is especially useful for an inside corner of a room.

- 部屋の隅に箱があります。 (へやのすみにはこがあります。) - There is a box in the corner of the room.
- 隅まで掃除してください。 (すみまでそうじしてください。) - Please clean right into the corners.

Use の to specify the space: 部屋の隅. に marks an object's location there.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 隅 writes the native noun すみ. There is no compound or okurigana to divide.

## Synonyms and antonyms

角 (かど) commonly names an outside corner or a street corner. 真ん中 (まんなか), "middle," is a useful positional contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

English "corner" covers both 隅 and 角, but a room's tucked-away corner is not the same spatial idea as a corner you turn around in the street. Learn the noun with its setting.
