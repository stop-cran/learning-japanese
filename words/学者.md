# 学者 (がくしゃ) - scholar; academic

## Meaning

学者 is a noun for a scholar or academic: someone engaged in a field of learning or research. Depending on the field, English "scientist" may fit. It can also describe a learned person.

- その学者は日本の歴史を研究しています。 (そのがくしゃはにほんのれきしをけんきゅうしています。) - That scholar researches Japanese history.
- 姉は言語学者になりたいそうです。 (あねはげんごがくしゃになりたいそうです。) - My older sister says she wants to become a linguist.

Fields can combine with 学者 in established names such as 言語学者 (げんごがくしゃ). 学者になる means "become a scholar"; the noun itself is not a verb.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) means study or learning, and [者](../kanji/者.md) identifies a person. This is a field-plus-person relationship: a person devoted to learning. Both readings are on readings, ガク + シャ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

研究者 (けんきゅうしゃ), "researcher," overlaps and emphasizes conducting research. [学生](学生.md) (がくせい) is a student, not a synonym for a professional scholar. A scholar can teach, but 学者 does not itself specify the job title "teacher."

## Distinctive meaning

Choose an English equivalent that suits the discipline rather than translating every 学者 as "scientist." A historian and a physicist can both be 学者. The word identifies scholarly activity or learning; it does not by itself guarantee that a person's claim is correct.
