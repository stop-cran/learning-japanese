# 発行 (はっこう) - publication; issuance

## Meaning

発行 is publishing or issuing something. **Nを発行する** is transitive: an organization issues a publication, certificate, ticket, or similar item. **Nが発行される** is the passive, "N is issued."

- この雑誌は毎月発行されます。 (このざっしはまいつきはっこうされます。) - This magazine is published every month.
- 領収書を発行してください。 (りょうしゅうしょをはっこうしてください。) - Please issue a receipt.

発行日 (はっこうび) is the publication or issue date. The object determines whether English "publish" or "issue" is appropriate.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) contributes sending out or originating, and [行](../kanji/行.md) contributes going or circulating. Together they describe putting something into circulation. The established reading is はっこう, with small っ before こう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出版](出版.md) (しゅっぱん) concerns publishing, especially books and similar works. 発売 (はつばい) means releasing something for sale; a publication can be issued without being sold. Neither is a replacement for every document-issuing use of 発行.

## Distinctive meaning

発行 concerns the act of issuing, not necessarily writing the text or printing a physical copy. The receipt example illustrates that the word extends beyond books and magazines. It is also used for official items such as passports and banknotes, with the issuer understood from context.
