# 今年 (ことし) — this year

## Meaning

今年 is the current calendar year: 今年は暑いですね (It is hot this year, isn't it?), 今年の夏 (this summer), 今年こそ (this is the year!).

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) (now, this) plus [年](../kanji/年.md) (year): "this year". The reading ことし is a **jukujikun**, a whole-word reading, not the sum of いま and とし or こん and ねん. The alternative on-reading こんねん also appears in 今年度 (こんねんど), "this fiscal year".

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Series: 去年 きょねん (last year), 今年 ことし, [来年](来年.md) らいねん (next year).
- 本年 ほんねん is a formal or written equivalent. A New Year card (年賀状, ねんがじょう) might say 本年もよろしく (ほんねんもよろしく), a seasonal greeting such as "I look forward to another good year with you."

## Distinctive meaning

ことし is the everyday choice; こんねん is a formal alternative and can also stand alone.
