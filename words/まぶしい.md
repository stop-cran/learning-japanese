# まぶしい - dazzling; radiant

## Meaning

まぶしい is an i-adjective for dazzling or radiant brightness.
Strong light can be difficult to look at. The word can also describe
someone whose beauty or impressive presence seems dazzling.

- 朝の光がまぶしくて、目を細めました。 (あさのひかりがまぶしくて、めをほそめました。) - The morning light was dazzling, and I narrowed my eyes.
- 彼女の笑顔がまぶしく見えました。 (かのじょのえがおがまぶしくみえました。) - Her smile looked radiant.

Forms include まぶしくない, not dazzling, and まぶしかった, was dazzling.

## How the kanji combine

眩しい is the dictionary spelling. The entry also records まぼしい as
another reading. Recognition of those forms does not require replacing
the primary kana form まぶしい in ordinary practice.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[明るい](明るい.md) (あかるい) can describe brightness without
necessarily implying glare. [ぎらぎら](ぎらぎら.md) emphasizes glaring
or glittering. [輝く](輝く.md) (かがやく) means shine or sparkle and
also has figurative uses.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every well-lit room is uncomfortably dazzling, and not every
figurative use reports eye discomfort. In the smile example, translate
the admiring radiance rather than diagnosing a problem with the light.
The adjective attaches directly to a noun without な.
