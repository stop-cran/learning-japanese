# うがい - gargling; rinsing the mouth

## Meaning

うがい is gargling or rinsing one's mouth. It is a noun used with する: うがいする or うがいをする. The liquid used can take で.

- 水でうがいをしました。 (みずでうがいをしました。) - I gargled with water.
- うがいをしてから、コップを洗いました。 (うがいをしてから、コップをあらいました。) - After rinsing my mouth, I washed the cup.

The ordinary verb forms come from する: うがいします, うがいした, うがいしない. The action need not be described with a separate object naming the throat or mouth.

## How the kanji combine

The teaching spelling is うがい. The community vocabulary source uses 嗽, but JMdict marks that kanji spelling as rare and the word as usually written in kana. Recognizing 嗽 is useful when reading; there is no need to invent a kanji-by-kanji explanation for this kana headword.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[洗う](洗う.md) (あらう) means "wash" more generally and takes the thing washed with を. 口をすすぐ (くちをすすぐ), "rinse the mouth," makes the mouth-rinsing action explicit.

## Distinctive meaning

English "gargle" can name either an action or a preparation used for it. うがい here names the action, not a particular medicine. These examples teach the language pattern and do not imply that gargling prevents or treats any illness.
