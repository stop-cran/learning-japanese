# 発明 (はつめい) - invention

## Meaning

発明 is invention: creating a new device, method, or other useful idea. It can name the act or its result. 発明する is a transitive する verb, with the invented thing marked by **を**.

- 彼女は新しい道具を発明しました。 (かのじょはあたらしいどうぐをはつめいしました。) - She invented a new tool.
- この発明は多くの人の生活を変えました。 (このはつめいはおおくのひとのせいかつをかえました。) - This invention changed many people's lives.

**発明家** (はつめいか) is an inventor. The passive 発明された means "was invented," useful when the invention matters more than naming its inventor.

## How the kanji combine

[発](../kanji/発.md) contributes bringing forth or originating, and [明](../kanji/明.md) contributes clarity or bringing to light. These offer a mnemonic for making a new idea concrete, not a literal historical derivation. The on readings are ハツ + メイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[発見](発見.md) (はっけん), "discovery," concerns finding something; 発明 emphasizes devising it. 開発 (かいはつ), "development," may include improving or producing technology without claiming a distinct new invention.

## Distinctive meaning

Not every creation is naturally described as a 発明. Writing a poem is creation, but "inventing a tool" is the clearer model for this word. The noun alone also makes no legal claim about patentability or ownership.
