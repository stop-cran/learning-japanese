# 動物 (どうぶつ) -- animal

## Meaning

動物 means an animal or animals as a category. The noun itself does not mark singular or plural.

この島には珍しい動物がいます。 (このしまにはめずらしいどうぶつがいます。) -- There are unusual animals on this island.

In ordinary existence sentences, living animals take いる: 動物がいる, "there is an animal" or "there are animals." The context supplies the number.

## How the kanji combine

[動](../kanji/動.md), read どう, concerns movement; [物](../kanji/物.md), read ぶつ, means a thing or entity. This is an established category word, not a rule that anything moving must be an animal.

## Synonyms and antonyms

生き物 (いきもの) is a living thing, a broader expression. 植物 (しょくぶつ), plants, is a useful biological category contrast rather than a strict everyday "opposite."

## Distinctive meaning

動物 is not limited to pets. Dogs, wild mammals, birds, and many other creatures belong to the broader category. ペット specifically concerns an animal kept as a companion; it does not replace 動物 in every setting.
