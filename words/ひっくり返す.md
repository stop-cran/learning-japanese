# ひっくり返す (ひっくりかえす) - turn upside down; overturn

## Meaning

ひっくり返す means turning something over, upside down, or inside out.
It also means knocking something over or overturning a decision.
It is a transitive godan verb: the thing affected takes を.

- フライパンの卵をひっくり返しました。 (フライパンのたまごをひっくりかえしました。) - I flipped the egg in the frying pan.
- うっかりコップをひっくり返しました。 (うっかりコップをひっくりかえしました。) - I accidentally knocked the glass over.
- 裁判所が判決をひっくり返しました。 (さいばんしょがはんけつをひっくりかえしました。) - The court overturned the judgment.

Forms include ひっくり返します, ひっくり返した and ひっくり返さない.
The dictionary also records the reading ひっくりがえす.

## How the kanji combine

[返](../kanji/返.md)す contributes turning or reversal.
Learn the native compound as a whole, not as separate on readings.
The source writes 引っ繰り返す, using [引](../kanji/引.md) and 繰 as well;
JMdict marks that fuller spelling as rare. It is the same word, not a
separate verb meaning to repeat.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[裏返す](裏返す.md) (うらがえす) overlaps in turning over or inside out.
[倒す](倒す.md) (たおす) overlaps in knocking something over.
[ひっくり返る](ひっくり返る.md) is the intransitive partner:
コップがひっくり返る describes the glass toppling, rather than making it an object.

## Distinctive meaning

English "upset" is not a universal translation rule.
ひっくり返す can overturn an arrangement or decision, but merely making someone
feel sad is not the physical or reversal use taught here.
An action can be accidental; transitive does not mean intentional.
