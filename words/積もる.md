# 積もる (つもる) - to pile up; accumulate

## Meaning

積もる is an intransitive godan verb for something piling up or accumulating.
The accumulating material takes が, and the surface or place can take に.
Snow and dust provide useful everyday examples.

- 道に雪が積もっています。 (みちにゆきがつもっています。) - Snow has accumulated on the road.
- 机の上にほこりが積もっていました。 (つくえのうえにほこりがつもっていました。) - Dust had accumulated on the desk.

積もっている can describe the accumulated layer that remains.
Useful forms are 積もります, 積もった, 積もって, and 積もらない.
The る ending does not make this an ichidan verb.

## How the kanji combine

[積](../kanji/積.md) supplies the piling-up or accumulation meaning.
The native reading is つもる, with もる as okurigana.
The written character also appears in words about stacking, but the examples here do not
name a person deliberately stacking an object.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[集まる](集まる.md) (あつまる), "gather," concerns things or people coming together.
積もる emphasizes a buildup, often in layers. [雪](雪.md) (ゆき), "snow," can fall without
remaining on the ground, so falling and accumulating are different events.

## Distinctive meaning

The subject is what builds up, not the person observing it.
Contrast 雪が降る (ゆきがふる), "snow falls," with 雪が積もる, "snow accumulates."
Weather can satisfy the first description without satisfying the second if the snow melts quickly.
This distinction makes 積もる useful in travel and weather reports.
