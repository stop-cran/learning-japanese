# 不正 (ふせい) - wrongdoing; dishonesty

## Meaning

不正 names wrongdoing, dishonesty, unfairness, or an improper irregularity.
It also modifies nouns with な or in established compounds:
不正な行為 (ふせいなこうい), "improper conduct."

- 試験で不正をしてはいけません。 (しけんでふせいをしてはいけません。) - You must not cheat on the examination.
- 会社は不正な取引を調査しています。 (かいしゃはふせいなとりひきをちょうさしています。) - The company is investigating improper transactions.

不正をする means engaging in wrongdoing or cheating. 不正に can describe an
action performed improperly or fraudulently.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) negates what follows, and [正](../kanji/正.md) contributes
rightness or correctness. Together they describe what is not proper or right.
The readings are フ + セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

違法 (いほう) explicitly concerns being against the law.
不正 may concern rules or integrity without itself establishing a legal offense.
[正しい](正しい.md) (ただしい), "correct; right," provides a contextual contrast.
[間違い](間違い.md) (まちがい), "mistake," does not by itself accuse someone of
dishonesty.

## Distinctive meaning

In the examination example, 不正 suggests cheating, not merely giving a wrong
answer. In technical contexts it can describe invalid or improper data without
implying malicious intent. The object and setting determine whether the issue
is fraud, fairness, rule-breaking, or a technical irregularity; do not infer a
person's intention from the word alone.
