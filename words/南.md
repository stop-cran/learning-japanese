# 南 (みなみ) — south

## Meaning

南 names the compass direction south. It is a noun: 南の before another noun, 南に for location, and 南へ for movement.

駅の南に公園があります。 (えきのみなみにこうえんがあります。) — There is a park south of the station.

## How the kanji combine

[南](../kanji/南.md) carries the native reading みなみ. The on reading なん appears in compounds such as 南北 (なんぼく), "south and north." A compound or place name may have a different reading from the standalone direction.

## Synonyms and antonyms

北 (きた), "north," is the opposite. [東](東.md) (ひがし), "east," and 西 (にし), "west," are the other main compass points. 南側 (みなみがわ) means the south side.

## Distinctive meaning

南 is a geographical direction, not automatically the bottom of a page. Maps often place north at the top, but their orientation can differ. State the reference point with の when describing something south of a landmark.
