# クーラー (クーラー) - air conditioner; cooler

## Meaning

クーラー commonly names an air conditioner used to cool a room. It can also name a cooler, such as a portable insulated box for keeping drinks cold.

- 暑いので、クーラーをつけました。 (あついので、クーラーをつけました。) - It was hot, so I turned on the air conditioner.
- キャンプに持っていく飲み物をクーラーに入れました。 (キャンプにもっていくのみものをクーラーにいれました。) - I put the drinks for our camping trip in the cooler.

クーラーボックス makes the portable-box meaning explicit.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "cooler," not a kanji compound. Keep both long vowels, クー and ラー.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[冷房](冷房.md) (れいぼう) is indoor cooling or air conditioning, including references to its operation. エアコン (air conditioner) can describe a system that heats as well as cools. クーラー emphasizes cooling; check the actual equipment rather than inferring all its functions from the label.

## Distinctive meaning

A [冷蔵庫](冷蔵庫.md) (れいぞうこ), refrigerator, is not automatically called a クーラー in ordinary Japanese. The portable cooler meaning does not transfer every English sense of "cooler" to this loanword. The room-cooling and portable-box examples concern different equipment.
