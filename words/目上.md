# 目上 (めうえ) - senior person; social superior

## Meaning

目上 refers to someone senior to oneself in age, rank, or social position.
It can also name that relative status. 目上の人 (めうえのひと) means "a person senior to oneself."

- 目上の人には丁寧に話します。 (めうえのひとにはていねいにはなします。) - I speak politely to people senior to me.
- 彼は私より目上です。 (かれはわたしよりめうえです。) - He is senior to me.

The comparison is relational: a person may be 目上 to one person and not to another.

## How the kanji combine

[目](../kanji/目.md) is read め, and [上](../kanji/上.md) is read うえ.
The established native compound presents relative social position as "above."
This is not a literal location above someone's eyes.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[先輩](先輩.md) (せんぱい) is someone senior through earlier entry into a school,
workplace, or activity; the categories overlap without being identical.
[目下](目下.md) (めした) is the contrasting term for someone junior in age or position.
上司 (じょうし) specifically refers to a workplace superior.

## Distinctive meaning

Being 目上 does not assert that someone is more talented, morally better, or correct.
Nor does age alone explain every situation: the relevant hierarchy depends on context.
Use the term to discuss the relationship rather than as a universal rule for addressing someone directly.
