# 買い物 (かいもの) — shopping, a purchase

## Meaning

買い物 is shopping or something bought: 買い物に行く (go shopping), 買い物をする (do the shopping), 買い物袋 かいものぶくろ (shopping bag), いい買い物をした (it was a good buy).

## How the kanji combine

[買](../kanji/買.md) in its noun form 買い (from 買う) plus 物 (もの, thing): "thing bought". The verb stem plus noun pattern matches [飲み物](飲み物.md) のみもの. 物 is もの here (kun), in contrast to the on reading ブツ in 見物 けんぶつ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related verb: [買う](買う.md) かう (buy).
- Close: ショッピング (shopping, from English, also for leisure browsing), 購入 こうにゅう (purchase, formal).

## Distinctive meaning

買い物 is both the activity and the goods, and works with する (買い物する). ショッピング suggests browsing, while 買い物 is practical shopping such as groceries.
