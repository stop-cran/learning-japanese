# ちゃんと (ちゃんと) - properly; reliably

## Meaning

ちゃんと is an adverb describing doing something properly, reliably,
or with appropriate care. It can emphasize diligent effort or the
fact that something has been done as expected.

- 人の話をちゃんと聞いてください。 (ひとのはなしをちゃんときいてください。) - Please listen properly to what people are saying.
- 鍵はちゃんと閉めました。 (かぎはちゃんとしめました。) - I made sure to lock up properly.

The first example asks for attentive behavior; the second reassures
the listener that the expected action was carried out. ちゃんと
does not change the verb's conjugation.

## How the kanji combine

This is a kana word, with no kanji components or loanword source
to invent. JMdict classifies it as mimetic: it expresses a manner
or condition rather than necessarily imitating an audible sound.
Learn the whole form ちゃんと.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[きちんと](きちんと.md) overlaps in doing something properly or
orderly. [しっかり](しっかり.md) can emphasize firmness, care,
or reliability. These are contextual alternatives, not identical
adverbs in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

The expected standard comes from context. ちゃんと聞く concerns
attention, while ちゃんと閉める concerns completing the action
properly. English "perfectly" can therefore overstate the claim:
the speaker need not mean flawless performance by every possible
measure. In a command, ちゃんと may also imply dissatisfaction
with the listener's earlier behavior.
