# 免税 (めんぜい) - tax exemption; duty exemption

## Meaning

免税 is exemption from a tax or fiscal duty. It is a noun and a suru
expression; the dictionary records both transitive and intransitive uses.

- この商品は免税です。 (このしょうひんはめんぜいです。) - This product is tax-exempt.
- 免税店で買い物をしました。 (めんぜいてんでかいものをしました。) - I shopped at a duty-free store.

免税される (めんぜいされる), "be exempted from tax," is a useful passive
pattern. 免税の品 (めんぜいのしな) means "tax-exempt goods."

## How the kanji combine

免 contributes exemption or release; [税](../kanji/税.md) names tax.
The on readings メン + ゼイ form めんぜい. Here English "duty" means
a tax, not a person's responsibility or assigned task.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[課税](課税.md) (かぜい) is imposing tax, a useful contrast to exemption.
[税金](税金.md) (ぜいきん) names tax money, not the exemption itself.
Reducing a tax and being exempt from it are different arrangements.

## Distinctive meaning

免税 does not itself promise that every purchase or every purchaser is
eligible. The word describes tax treatment; the applicable rules determine
which tax is waived and under what conditions.
