# 酢 (す) - vinegar

## Meaning

酢 is vinegar, the sour seasoning containing acetic acid.
It is a noun naming the ingredient, not an adjective meaning sour.

- 酢を少し入れます。 (すをすこしいれます。) - I add a little vinegar.
- このたれには酢を使っています。 (このたれにはすをつかっています。) - This sauce uses vinegar.

In the first example, を marks the ingredient added.
酢の酸味 (すのさんみ) means "the sourness of vinegar."

## How the kanji combine

酢 is a single character writing the native noun す.
There is no compound reading or kana ending to divide.
This standalone word uses the kun reading, not an on reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

酸っぱい (すっぱい), "sour," describes a taste, not the seasoning itself.
[しょうゆ](しょうゆ.md), soy sauce, and [塩](塩.md) (しお), salt,
are other seasonings, not synonyms or antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Vinegar is not limited to one grain or fruit source: rice vinegar and
fruit vinegars are examples. The ordinary teaching spelling here is 酢.
The dictionary's rare 醋 and 酸 spellings are not needed to produce the
examples above. In particular, do not infer that ordinary 酸 read さん,
"acid," is simply the same word or reading as 酢 read す.
