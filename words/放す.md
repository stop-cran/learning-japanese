# 放す (はなす) - release; let go

## Meaning

放す is a transitive godan verb meaning to release, let go of, or set free. The thing released takes を; a destination can take に or へ.

- 捕まえた魚を川に放しました。 (つかまえたさかなをかわにはなしました。) - I released the fish I had caught into the river.
- 子供は風船から手を放しました。 (こどもはふうせんからてをはなしました。) - The child let go of the balloon.

The second example literally describes releasing one's hand from the balloon. The past is 放した (はなした); the negative is 放さない (はなさない).

## How the kanji combine

[放](../kanji/放.md) contributes letting go or releasing, with the native stem はな and the ending す. There is no multi-kanji compound. The ending changes in forms such as 放して (はなして).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[離す](離す.md) (はなす) also sounds the same and emphasizes separating things or creating distance. The meanings can overlap, especially with hands, but 放す usefully focuses on release from restraint. [話す](話す.md), also はなす, means speaking and is a different word entirely.

## Distinctive meaning

The fish example is about setting something free, not simply moving two objects farther apart. The verb does not by itself say whether the release was accidental or deliberate. Use the kanji and the surrounding object to distinguish "release," "separate," and "speak" when reading.
