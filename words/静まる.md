# 静まる (しずまる) - quiet down; subside

## Meaning

静まる is an intransitive godan verb: something becomes quiet or still,
or a disturbance or strong feeling subsides. The thing becoming quiet
or settling down takes が.

- 音楽が終わると、会場が静まりました。 (おんがくがおわると、かいじょうがしずまりました。) - When the music ended, the venue became quiet.
- しばらくすると、怒りが静まりました。 (しばらくすると、いかりがしずまりました。) - After a while, my anger subsided.

Forms include 静まります (しずまります), 静まって (しずまって),
静まった (しずまった), and 静まらない (しずまらない).

## How the kanji combine

[静](../kanji/静.md) supplies the quietness association and writes しず;
まる completes the native verb. 鎮まる is another spelling, also read しずまる.
JMdict particularly associates 静まる with becoming quiet and 鎮まる
with calming or subsiding. These are preferences, not strict spelling
restrictions that divide every possible sentence.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[静か](静か.md) (しずか) describes a quiet state; 静まる describes a change.
[落ち着く](落ち着く.md) (おちつく) overlaps in calming down.
[黙る](黙る.md) (だまる) specifically concerns not speaking, which is
narrower than a whole place becoming quiet.

## Distinctive meaning

This is not the transitive 静める (しずめる), "make quiet; calm":
compare 会場が静まる, "the venue becomes quiet," with
会場を静める, "quiet the venue."
静まっている can describe the resulting quiet state.
Neither spelling by itself says who caused that state.
