# 段 (だん) - step; tier; rank

## Meaning

段 is a noun and counter for steps or tiers. It also names a level or rank, including a dan rank in martial arts and games such as shogi. The setting determines what is being counted.

- 階段を一段ずつ上ってください。 (かいだんをいちだんずつのぼってください。) - Please go up the stairs one step at a time.
- 兄は柔道二段です。 (あにはじゅうどうにだんです。) - My older brother holds a second-dan rank in judo.

For shelves, 上の段 (うえのだん) means "the upper shelf/tier." These are distinct uses of the same noun, not separate words for each kind of level.

## How the kanji combine

[段](../kanji/段.md) is a single kanji read だん. It contributes a step, tier, or graded level. In numbered expressions such as 一段 and 二段, keep the counter attached to the number.

## Synonyms and antonyms

階 (かい) counts building floors, while 段 can count individual stair steps. [列](列.md) (れつ) describes a row or line rather than a tier's vertical level.

## Distinctive meaning

The English "level" is broad; 段 is not interchangeable with every expression for skill, height, or school grade. A dan rank belongs to the relevant ranking system. It does not give a universal comparison across different martial arts or organizations. Likewise, a staircase's number of 段 is not its number of building floors.
