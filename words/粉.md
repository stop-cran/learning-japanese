# 粉 (こな) - powder; flour

## Meaning

粉 is a noun for powder or finely divided material. In a cooking context it can mean flour, but the word by itself does not specify wheat or even something edible.

- ボウルに粉を入れてください。 (ボウルにこなをいれてください。) - Please put the flour into the bowl.
- 乾いた葉を粉にしました。 (かわいたはをこなにしました。) - I ground the dried leaves into powder.

In the first sentence, a recipe or visible ingredient identifies the flour. In the second, 粉にする describes making something into powder: に marks the resulting form.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 粉 is read こな as this standalone native noun. There is no compound relationship to unpack. Related compounds can use different readings, as in 小麦粉 (こむぎこ), "wheat flour"; do not automatically read every written 粉 as こな.

## Synonyms and antonyms

小麦粉 identifies a particular ingredient, while 粉 identifies a physical form or the contextually understood powder. ほこり means dust, especially the material that collects on surfaces. The meanings can overlap, but kitchen flour is not thereby called household dust.

## Distinctive meaning

English "meal" in a dictionary gloss can mean ground grain, not a prepared breakfast or dinner. Likewise, "powder" is not limited to cosmetics or medicine. Identify the material from the surrounding noun phrase and situation before choosing a narrower English translation.
