# 溶け込む (とけこむ) - dissolve into; blend in

## Meaning

溶け込む means to dissolve or merge into something, or figuratively to blend in
with surroundings or fit into a group. It is an intransitive godan verb.
The thing or person blending in takes が; what it blends into can take に.

- 砂糖が水に溶け込みました。 (さとうがみずにとけこみました。) - The sugar dissolved into the water.
- 彼女は新しいクラスにすぐ溶け込みました。 (かのじょはあたらしいクラスにすぐとけこみました。) - She quickly fitted into her new class.

Forms include 溶け込みます, 溶け込んで, and 溶け込まない.
For surroundings, 風景に溶け込む (ふうけいにとけこむ) means blend into the scenery.

## How the kanji combine

The stem of [溶ける](溶ける.md), melt or dissolve, combines with 込む (こむ),
which contributes an inward or thorough involvement here.
[溶](../kanji/溶.md) concerns dissolving; [込](../kanji/込.md) contributes going into.
The established compound also has social and visual uses, not only a physical one.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[溶ける](溶ける.md) can describe melting or dissolving without specifying merging
into a setting. [混合](混合.md) (こんごう) concerns mixing or a mixture:
not every mixture involves dissolution.
[慣れる](慣れる.md) (なれる) emphasizes becoming familiar with or accustomed to something;
that is a useful comparison, not a guarantee of social integration.

## Distinctive meaning

No literal melting happens when someone fits into a class.
Likewise, blending into scenery can concern how something looks, not joining a community.
Use the surrounding nouns to interpret the physical, social, or visual relationship.
