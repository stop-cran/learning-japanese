# 最も (もっとも) — most; to the greatest degree

## Meaning

最も means "most" or "to the greatest degree". It places something at the top of a comparison, whose group may be stated or understood. Dictionary glosses also include "extremely", but the superlative use is the practical starting point.

- この中で、これが最も重要です。  
  このなかで、これがもっともじゅうようです。  
  "Of these, this is the most important."
- 一年で最も寒い月は何月ですか。  
  いちねんでもっともさむいつきはなんがつですか。  
  "Which is the coldest month of the year?"

## How the kanji combine

[最](../kanji/最.md) contributes the highest degree, with the native reading もっと in this word; も completes もっとも. Keep the small っ in the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一番 (いちばん), "most; number one", is a common conversational alternative. とても means "very" without necessarily ranking something above all alternatives.

## Distinctive meaning

最も often suits formal explanation and comparison. Do not freely substitute it for every "extremely" or "very". Also distinguish the separate word もっとも meaning "reasonable" or "though", often written 尤も; those meanings are not the superlative adverb.
