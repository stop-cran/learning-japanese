# 鳥 (とり) — bird

## Meaning

鳥 means **bird**. It can refer to one bird or to birds generally; the noun does not change form for plural.

- 鳥が木の上にいます。（とりがきのうえにいます。）— There is a bird in the tree.
- あの白い鳥を見てください。（あのしろいとりをみてください。）— Please look at that white bird.

The first example uses います because it describes the presence of a living animal.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun: [鳥](../kanji/鳥.md) is read とり as a whole.
It has no okurigana and no compound parts.
The on reading チョウ appears in other words, such as 野鳥（やちょう）, "wild bird."

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 小鳥（ことり）means "small bird," a narrower description rather than an exact synonym.
- 鶏（にわとり）means "chicken," a particular kind of bird.
- 野鳥（やちょう）means "wild bird."
- [魚](魚.md)（さかな）, "fish," names another animal category, not an antonym.

There is no natural opposite of "bird."

## Distinctive meaning

Outside a food context, 鳥 normally refers to the animal category.
In food contexts, とり can mean chicken meat; 鶏肉（とりにく）makes that meaning explicit,
and 鶏 is the usual kanji for chicken specifically.
Do not assume that every use of 鳥 means chicken or that every bird mentioned is food.
