# 天然 (てんねん) - natural origin; nature

## Meaning

天然 is a noun associated with nature and things occurring naturally rather than being artificially produced.
Use **天然の + noun**, as in 天然の素材 (てんねんのそざい), "natural materials."
It also forms compounds such as 天然ガス (てんねんガス), "natural gas."

- この指輪には天然の石が使われています。 (このゆびわにはてんねんのいしがつかわれています。) - A natural stone is used in this ring.
- この地域では天然ガスが採れます。 (このちいきではてんねんガスがとれます。) - Natural gas is extracted in this region.

The の construction describes origin or character; do not simply substitute 天然な for it.

## How the kanji combine

[天](../kanji/天.md) contributes heaven or nature, and [然](../kanji/然.md) contributes being so or in a particular state.
Together they express a state arising from nature. The on readings テン + ネン give てんねん.
This is a meaning aid, not a claim about the compound's historical formation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[自然](自然.md) (しぜん) also concerns nature and naturalness, including behavior that feels unforced.
[人工](人工.md) (じんこう), "artificial/human-made," is the direct contrast for origin.
The two "natural" words overlap but do not replace one another in every expression.

## Distinctive meaning

In colloquial descriptions of people, 天然 can abbreviate 天然ボケ and suggest being unintentionally scatterbrained.
That use can be teasing or unwelcome; it is not simply praise for being natural.
For materials, 天然 identifies natural origin, not a guarantee of safety, quality, or environmental benefit.
