# 買う (かう) — to buy

## Meaning

買う is to buy: パンを買う (buy bread), 本を買いました, どこで買いましたか (where did you buy it?), 買いたい (I want to buy).
Figurative uses: 才能を買う (rate someone's talent highly) and 喧嘩を買う (take up a fight).

## How the kanji combine

[買](../kanji/買.md) with the kun reading か plus okurigana う. A godan verb with the う-pattern: 買います, 買わない (not 買あない), 買って, 買った, potential 買える.
Noun form: 買い (買い物 かいもの, 買い手 かいて). The on reading バイ is in 売買 ばいばい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Antonym: 売る うる (sell).
- Homophone: 飼う かう (keep an animal): 犬を飼う.
- Related: [買い物](買い物.md) かいもの (shopping), 購入する こうにゅうする (purchase, formal).

## Distinctive meaning

買う is the everyday word. 購入 is formal and used in business or on forms. 買う and 飼う sound alike, so context and kanji show which is meant.
