# 襖 (ふすま) - opaque Japanese sliding panel

## Meaning

襖 is a traditional opaque sliding panel, with paper or cloth on a wooden framework. It can divide rooms or close a storage space.

- 押し入れの襖を開けました。 (おしいれのふすまをあけました。) - I opened the closet's sliding panel.
- 襖を閉めて、部屋を二つに分けました。 (ふすまをしめて、へやをふたつにわけました。) - We closed the sliding panels and divided the room in two.

## How the kanji combine

襖 writes the native noun ふすま as a whole. There is no 襖 card yet. Other dictionary readings of this character can name historical garments; they are not alternative pronunciations to use for the room divider.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[障子](障子.md) (しょうじ) is the related light-transmitting paper screen. [戸](戸.md) (と) and [ドア](ドア.md) are broader door terms. The broad English word "screen" does not establish that 襖 and 障子 have the same construction.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the opacity contrast: a typical 襖 blocks the view and light, whereas a typical 障子 lets diffused light through. This is a panel in Japanese architecture, not the default word for every sliding glass door.
