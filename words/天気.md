# 天気 (てんき) — weather

## Meaning

天気 is the weather of the day: いい天気ですね (nice weather, isn't it), 天気が悪い わるい (the weather is bad), 天気予報 てんきよほう (weather forecast), 天気がいい日 (a day of fine weather).

## How the kanji combine

[天](../kanji/天.md) (sky) + [気](../kanji/気.md) (air, atmosphere): "the air of the sky". Both are on readings (テン + キ).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 気候 きこう (climate, long-term), 天候 てんこう (weather, more formal and written).
- Related: 晴れ はれ (sunny), 曇り くもり (cloudy), 雨 あめ (rain).
- Not to be confused with: 元気 げんき (healthy) and 天気 てんき (weather), a similar pair of sounds that differ in the first kanji.

## Distinctive meaning

天気 is the everyday word and the standard small-talk topic; 天候 is used in news and formal reports (天候不良 てんこうふりょう, poor weather). 気候 is the climate over a season, not today's weather.
