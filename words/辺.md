# 辺 (へん) — area; vicinity

## Meaning

辺 means an area or vicinity without drawing an exact boundary. It often follows この, その, あの, or どの.

- この辺に銀行はありますか。 (このへんにぎんこうはありますか。) — Is there a bank around here?
- 駅はどの辺ですか。 (えきはどのへんですか。) — About where is the station?

この辺の店 (このへんのみせ) means "shops in this area"; use の before the following noun.

## How the kanji combine

辺 is a single-character word with the on reading ヘン. Other readings occur in other words, such as 辺り (あたり), "vicinity," but this card teaches へん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

近く (ちかく), "nearby," stresses nearness. 周辺 (しゅうへん) means the surrounding area. None requires a precise measured distance.

## Distinctive meaning

ここ points more directly to "here," while この辺 gives the broader sense "around here." In mathematics, 辺 can also mean a side of a figure; that separate use should not be confused with the neighborhood sense in この辺.
