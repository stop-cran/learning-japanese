# 成績 (せいせき) - grades; performance record

## Meaning

成績 is an assessed result or performance record, including school grades and results in work or sport. Use 成績がいい or 成績が悪い to describe good or poor performance.

- 今学期は数学の成績が上がりました。 (こんがっきはすうがくのせいせきがあがりました。) - My mathematics grade improved this term.
- そのチームは大会でよい成績を残しました。 (そのチームはたいかいでよいせいせきをのこしました。) - That team achieved good results in the tournament.

成績を上げる (せいせきをあげる) means "improve one's results." Compare the transitive 上げる with 成績が上がる, "results improve."

## How the kanji combine

[成](../kanji/成.md) contributes achieving or accomplishing, and 績 contributes achievements or a record of work. Together they concern the results of an activity. The on readings are セイ + セキ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[結果](結果.md) (けっか) is a broader word for an outcome. 成績 highlights performance that can be assessed. 点数 (てんすう) is a numerical score; a 成績 may involve several scores or a broader record.

## Distinctive meaning

成績 is not restricted to a student's report card, but neither is it every kind of result. An experiment's physical outcome is not automatically someone's 成績. Look for an activity whose success or standard is being evaluated. The word itself does not say whether that evaluation is favorable.
