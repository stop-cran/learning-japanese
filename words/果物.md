# 果物 (くだもの) — fruit

## Meaning

果物 names fruit as food: apples, oranges, bananas, and similar things.
The noun can refer to fruit in general or fruit in a particular meal; it does not require an English-style plural ending.

- 朝ご飯に果物を食べます。 (あさごはんにくだものをたべます。) — I eat fruit for breakfast.
- この果物は甘いです。 (このくだものはあまいです。) — This fruit is sweet.

Use 果物を食べる (くだものをたべる), "eat fruit," or 果物の before a noun when describing something associated with fruit.

## How the kanji combine

[果](../kanji/果.md) supplies the fruit idea, while [物](../kanji/物.md) means a thing.
The spelling points to the category of edible things, but the reading is learned as a whole: くだもの.
This is a jukujikun, a special reading attached to the written word. Do not extract a freely reusable reading くだ for 果.

## Synonyms and antonyms

フルーツ is a loanword also meaning fruit.
[野菜](野菜.md) (やさい), "vegetables," names a contrasting food category, not the logical opposite of fruit.
The distinction here is ordinary food vocabulary, not a complete botanical classification.

## Distinctive meaning

Use 果物 for edible fruit, not for the abstract "fruits" of effort.
結果 (けっか), "result; outcome," also contains 果, but it is a different word with a different reading.
Shared kanji meaning is a learning connection, not permission to substitute one word for the other.
