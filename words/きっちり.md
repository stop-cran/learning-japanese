# きっちり — precisely; neatly and securely

## Meaning

きっちり describes doing something precisely, properly, or with a close fit.

- 予約した時刻に、彼女はきっちり到着した。
  — She arrived exactly at the time she had reserved.
- 道具を箱の中にきっちり収めた。
  — I fitted the tools neatly into the box.

## Synonyms and antonyms

きっちり can describe precision, reliable completion, or a close fit. It overlaps with
[きっかり](きっかり.md) when the focus is an exact time or amount, but it can also emphasize
doing something properly.

## Distinctive meaning

The sentence context determines whether the emphasis is precision, reliable completion, or fit.
It does not itself mean that a place is overcrowded.
