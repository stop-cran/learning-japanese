# 短所 (たんしょ) - shortcoming; disadvantage

## Meaning

短所 is a shortcoming, weak point, or disadvantage. It can concern
a person's character or abilities, and also the drawbacks of a thing or plan.
Use **Nの短所** for "N's shortcomings."

- 私の短所は、すぐに慌てることです。
  (わたしのたんしょは、すぐにあわてることです。) - My weakness is that I get flustered easily.
- この方法には長所も短所もあります。
  (このほうほうにはちょうしょもたんしょもあります。) - This method has both advantages and disadvantages.

The first sentence uses こと to turn the behavior into the thing being identified.
短所がある means "have a shortcoming"; 短所 is a noun, not an i-adjective.

## How the kanji combine

[短](../kanji/短.md) contributes shortness or deficiency and
[所](../kanji/所.md) a place or aspect.
Think of an aspect in which something falls short, not a physically short location.
The on readings are タン + ショ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[欠点](欠点.md) (けってん), flaw or weak point, and
[弱点](弱点.md) (じゃくてん), weakness, overlap.
[欠陥](欠陥.md) (けっかん) often highlights a defect in how something is made or works.
長所 (ちょうしょ), "strong point; advantage," is the direct contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

A shortcoming need not mean total inability or a recent mistake.
An otherwise useful method can have a 短所 such as high cost.
Name the relevant weakness instead of treating the word as a general
judgment that someone is a bad person.
