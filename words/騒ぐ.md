# 騒ぐ (さわぐ) — to make noise; to get agitated

## Meaning

騒ぐ means making a noise, raising a fuss, or becoming excited or agitated. It is an intransitive godan verb: 騒ぎます, 騒がない, 騒いで. The people making the commotion take が.

- 廊下で子供たちが騒いでいます。 (ろうかでこどもたちがさわいでいます。) — The children are making noise in the corridor.
- 胸が騒いで、眠れません。 (むねがさわいで、ねむれません。) — I feel unsettled and cannot sleep.

## How the kanji combine

騒 supplies さわ; ぐ is okurigana. In the て-form, ぐ changes to いで: 騒いで, not 騒って.

## Synonyms and antonyms

静かにする (しずかにする), "be quiet," contrasts with noisy behavior. 興奮する (こうふんする) more directly names excitement. 騒ぐ can describe the commotion that excitement produces.

## Distinctive meaning

This is not a general translation of every happy "I am excited." It can suggest disruptive noise, fuss, panic, or inner agitation. 胸が騒ぐ describes an unsettled feeling without requiring an audible sound.
