# 腕 (うで) — arm

## Meaning

腕 means **arm**, the body part between the shoulder and hand. It is a noun, so a possessor connects with の: 私の腕 (わたしのうで), “my arm.”

右の腕が痛いです。 (みぎのうでがいたいです。) — My right arm hurts.

腕を上げてください。 (うでをあげてください。) — Please raise your arm.

In the second sentence, を marks the body part being moved.

## How the kanji combine

腕 is a single character with the native reading うで; there is no compound to split. The on reading ワン occurs in other vocabulary, not as an interchangeable reading of this everyday noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

手 (て) is the hand, though some expressions use it more broadly. 肩 (かた) is the shoulder. These are neighboring body parts, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

腕 also means skill or ability: 料理の腕 (りょうりのうで), “cooking skill.” Accordingly, 腕を上げる can mean “improve one's skills” as well as literally raising an arm. The surrounding activity determines the interpretation.
