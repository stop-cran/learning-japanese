# 不安 (ふあん) — anxiety; uneasiness

## Meaning

不安 is anxiety or a feeling of insecurity about what may happen. It is a noun and a な-adjective: 不安です means "I am uneasy,"
and 不安な気持ち (ふあんなきもち) means "an uneasy feeling."

- 試験の前は不安です。 (しけんのまえはふあんです。) — I feel anxious before an exam.
- 一人で行くのは少し不安です。 (ひとりでいくのはすこしふあんです。) — I am a little uneasy about going alone.

## How the kanji combine

[不](../kanji/不.md) (not; lack of) negates [安](../kanji/安.md) (ease; peace): "not at ease."
Both use on readings, フ + アン. Read ふあん without adding a consonant between the vowels.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[心配](心配.md) (しんぱい, worry) overlaps, but also works with する: 心配する means "to worry."
[安心](安心.md) (あんしん, peace of mind; relief) is a useful contrast: anxiety versus feeling reassured.

## Distinctive meaning

不安 emphasizes feeling unsure or insecure; it need not mean immediate fear of a visible danger.
Use 不安になる (ふあんになる), "become anxious," rather than treating it like the する-verb 心配する.

The noun also works with 感じる: 将来に不安を感じます (しょうらいにふあんをかんじます),
"I feel anxious about the future." を marks the feeling experienced; に identifies its focus.
