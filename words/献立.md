# 献立 (こんだて) - menu; planned dishes

## Meaning

献立 is a menu or the planned selection of dishes for a meal. 献立を考える means to plan what to serve; 一週間の献立 (いっしゅうかんのこんだて) is a week's meal plan.

- 今夜の献立を考えています。 (こんやのこんだてをかんがえています。) - I am planning tonight's menu.
- 今日の献立は魚料理とサラダです。 (きょうのこんだてはさかなりょうりとサラダです。) - Today's menu is a fish dish and salad.

The dictionary also records a broader program or schedule sense, beyond meals. That does not make it the default translation for every English "program."

## How the kanji combine

献 contributes presenting or offering, while [立](../kanji/立.md) contributes setting up. These associations help remember an arranged offering of dishes, not a proven account of the word's historical formation.

The reading is mixed: on-reading コン followed by the native-derived だて, associated with 立て. It is an on-first mixed reading, not ケンリツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計画](計画.md) (けいかく) is a plan more broadly, and [予定](予定.md) (よてい) concerns plans or a schedule. メニュー can also mean menu. 献立 is especially useful when discussing the dishes chosen, rather than merely the time at which a meal occurs.

## Distinctive meaning

献立 can describe a home meal plan; it need not be a printed restaurant menu. Nor is it the word for a television broadcast program: [番組](番組.md) (ばんぐみ) identifies that kind of program. Let the object being planned determine the translation.
