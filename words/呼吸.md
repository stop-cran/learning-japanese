# 呼吸 (こきゅう) - breathing; respiration

## Meaning

呼吸 names breathing or respiration. 呼吸する means "breathe"; 深く呼吸する (ふかくこきゅうする) means "breathe deeply." As a noun, it appears in expressions such as 呼吸が速い (こきゅうがはやい), "one's breathing is fast."

- ゆっくり呼吸してください。 (ゆっくりこきゅうしてください。) - Please breathe slowly.
- 走った後、呼吸が速くなりました。 (はしったあと、こきゅうがはやくなりました。) - My breathing became faster after running.

The second example describes a change in breathing, not an action performed on another person.

## How the kanji combine

[呼](../kanji/呼.md) contributes breathing out in this compound, and [吸](../kanji/吸.md) contributes drawing in. The two complementary actions together name respiration. The on readings are コ + キュウ; 呼's meaning here is not simply "call."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[息](息.md) (いき) is breath; 息をする is another way to say "breathe." [吸う](吸う.md) (すう) and [吐く](吐く.md) (はく) distinguish inhaling and exhaling rather than naming the whole cycle.

## Distinctive meaning

呼吸 also describes coordination: 二人の呼吸が合う (ふたりのこきゅうがあう), "the two work in sync." This need not mean their lungs move together. 一呼吸置く (ひとこきゅうおく), "pause for a moment," uses a breath as a short interval rather than a precise unit of time.
