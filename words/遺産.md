# 遺産 (いさん) — inheritance; legacy; heritage

## Meaning

遺産 is what someone leaves behind after death, and also what earlier generations have handed down. Digital Daijisen gives two senses: 死後に残した財産 (しごにのこしたざいさん), “property left after death,” and 前代の人が残した業績 (ぜんだいのひとがのこしたぎょうせき), “achievements left by people of an earlier age,” as in 文化遺産 (ぶんかいさん), “cultural heritage.” JMdict glosses it “inheritance; bequest; legacy; heritage.”

- 祖父の遺産は、三人の子どもで分けることになった。 (そふのいさんは、さんにんのこどもでわけることになった。) — It was decided that Grandfather’s estate would be divided among his three children.
- この古い町並みは、地域の大切な遺産として守られている。 (このふるいまちなみは、ちいきのたいせつないさんとしてまもられている。) — These old streets are protected as an important heritage of the region.

## How the kanji combine

[遺](../kanji/遺.md) (い) here means to leave behind after death, sense 2 of イ in [Digital Daijisen, 遺](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E9%81%BA), and [産](../kanji/産.md) (さん) means assets; [Digital Daijisen, 産](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E7%94%A3) lists 遺産 under sense 3, 生活に必要なもとで (せいかつにひつようなもとで), “the means needed for living.”

## Synonyms and antonyms

[財産](財産.md) (ざいさん) is property or wealth in general, including what a living person owns. [相続](相続.md) (そうぞく) is the act of inheriting. [伝統](伝統.md) (でんとう) is a tradition handed down; 遺産 in its second sense is a legacy of achievements left by earlier generations.

## Distinctive meaning

遺言 (ゆいごん), “a will; last words,” uses the other on reading of 遺; JMdict notes that it is read いごん in legal contexts. Related words are 遺族 (いぞく), “bereaved family,” and 世界遺産 (せかいいさん), “World Heritage (site).”
