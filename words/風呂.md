# 風呂 (ふろ) - bath; bathtub

## Meaning

風呂 means a bath, bathtub, or bathing facility. お風呂 (おふろ) is a common everyday form.

- 寝る前にお風呂に入ります。 (ねるまえにおふろにはいります。) - I take a bath before going to bed.
- お風呂のお湯が熱いです。 (おふろのおゆがあついです。) - The bathwater is hot.

Use に入る (にはいる), "enter," for taking a bath. The object is not marked with を in this expression.

## How the kanji combine

[風](../kanji/風.md) and 呂 write the whole word ふろ; do not derive "bath" by adding their separate meanings. A commonly proposed origin connects it with native 室 (むろ), an enclosed chamber, but the historical derivation is disputed. The wago label follows that account, not a claim that the origin is settled.

## Synonyms and antonyms

浴室 (よくしつ) specifically names the bathroom as a room. シャワー is a shower; 銭湯 (せんとう) is a public bathhouse.

## Distinctive meaning

English "bathroom" can politely mean a toilet, but 風呂 concerns bathing. Ask for トイレ when you need the toilet. Context determines whether 風呂 refers to the tub, bathing, or the facility.
