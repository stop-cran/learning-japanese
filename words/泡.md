# 泡 (あわ) - bubbles; foam

## Meaning

泡 is a noun covering individual bubbles and collections of bubbles: foam, froth, soap lather, or the head on a drink. Japanese does not require a different plural form for several bubbles.

- 石けんをよく泡立ててください。 (せっけんをよくあわだててください。) - Please work the soap into a good lather.
- ビールの泡が消えました。 (ビールのあわがきえました。) - The foam on the beer disappeared.

In the first example, 泡立てる (あわだてる) is the related verb "to make foam; to lather." 泡が出る (あわがでる) means that bubbles or foam appear.

## How the kanji combine

This is a single-kanji native noun, read あわ. There is no compound to divide into separate word meanings. The dictionary also records the reading あぶく for 泡; the reading taught here is あわ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ビール](ビール.md) supplies a familiar context, but 泡 is not limited to drinks. 水滴 (すいてき), "a drop of water," is a useful contrast: a drop is liquid, while a bubble encloses gas. Neither is the opposite of the other.

## Distinctive meaning

Choose the English translation from the situation. 石けんの泡 is soap lather, while 水面の泡 (すいめんのあわ) may be bubbles on the water's surface. Translating every occurrence as one countable "bubble" can lose the collective meaning.
