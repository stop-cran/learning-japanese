# 運ぶ (はこぶ) -- carry; transport

## Meaning

運ぶ means moving something from one place to another by carrying or transporting it. The item takes を; the destination commonly takes に or まで.

- この箱を二階に運んでください。 (このはこをにかいにはこんでください。) -- Please carry this box upstairs.
- トラックで野菜を市場まで運びます。 (トラックでやさいをいちばまではこびます。) -- We transport vegetables to the market by truck.

The transport sense is transitive. This is a godan verb: 運びます (はこびます), 運ばない (はこばない), 運んで (はこんで), and 運んだ (はこんだ).

## How the kanji combine

[運](../kanji/運.md) contributes movement or conveyance and supplies はこ. The written ぶ completes the native verb. The reading is not the on reading うん heard in compounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

持つ (もつ) concerns holding or having something and need not imply moving it anywhere. [送る](送る.md) (おくる) concerns sending; the sender need not personally carry the item.

## Distinctive meaning

運ぶ is not limited to carrying things in one's hands. Vehicles, machinery, or several people can perform the transport. The truck example marks the means with で, separate from the object and destination.
