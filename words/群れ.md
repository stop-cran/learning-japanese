# 群れ (むれ) - group; flock; crowd

## Meaning

群れ is a noun for a group, flock, herd, crowd or cluster. It can name
animals, people or things together, not only one animal species.
English chooses a collective noun according to what is grouped.

- 鳥の群れが空を飛んでいます。
  (とりのむれがそらをとんでいます。) - A flock of birds is flying in the sky.
- 駅の前に人の群れができていました。
  (えきのまえにひとのむれができていました。) - A crowd of people had formed in front of the station.

鳥の群れ uses の to identify what makes up the flock. 群れ itself does
not specify a fixed number of members.

## How the kanji combine

[群](../kanji/群.md) writes む, followed by the written ending れ in this
native noun. The related verb 群れる (むれる) means to gather or flock
together. The noun is not that verb's dictionary form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[集団](集団.md) (しゅうだん) is a group or collective.
[団体](団体.md) (だんたい) can name a group or organization; a 群れ
does not by itself say that its members form an organization.
[人込み](人込み.md) (ひとごみ) concerns a crowd of people, while 群れ
can concern other kinds of members.

[集まり](集まり.md) (あつまり) can mean a gathering or collection.
The overlapping English group and gathering labels do not make a flock,
a class, an association and a social event unrestricted Japanese synonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not automatically translate every 群れ as herd or every English group
as 群れ. Identify the members and the situation. For people, the word
views them collectively; it does not establish that they share a formal
membership, purpose or institution.
