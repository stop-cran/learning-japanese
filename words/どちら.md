# どちら (どちら) — which one; which direction

## Meaning

どちら asks **which one**, especially of two alternatives. It also asks which direction or, politely, where.

- お茶とコーヒーと、どちらがいいですか。 (おちゃとこーひーと、どちらがいいですか。) — Which would you like, tea or coffee?
- 駅はどちらですか。 (えきはどちらですか。) — Which way is the station?

It is a pronoun: it can take particles such as が or へ. It does not directly modify a noun like どの.

## How the kanji combine

Kana is usual. The rare kanji spelling [何](../kanji/何.md)[方](../kanji/方.md) is not needed for ordinary use. Learn どちら alongside こちら, そちら, and あちら.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どっち is the more casual form. どれ asks “which one” from a set, commonly three or more. どこ asks “where” without the same polite directional wording.

## Distinctive meaning

“Of two” is a useful choice pattern, not the word's only function. In a location question, どちら need not imply two possible directions. どちら様 (どちらさま) politely asks someone's identity; it is a separate useful expression.
