# スマート (スマート) - stylish; slender; deft

## Meaning

スマート describes stylish dress, a slender build, or skillful, polished
handling of something. It is a na-adjective: スマートな before a noun,
スマートに before an action.

- 彼はスマートなスーツを着ています。 (かれはスマートなスーツをきています。) - He is wearing a stylish suit.
- 問題をスマートに解決しました。 (もんだいをスマートにかいけつしました。) - I resolved the problem deftly.

スマートな体形 (スマートなたいけい) describes a slender figure. A comment
on someone's body is not automatically welcome or an appropriate compliment.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword, not a kanji compound. The ー lengthens マ:
スマート. Its Japanese meanings must be learned rather than inferred from
every use of English "smart."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[おしゃれ](おしゃれ.md) overlaps in stylish appearance; [細い](細い.md)
(ほそい) can describe a slender shape. [器用](器用.md) (きよう) and
[上手](上手.md) (じょうず) overlap in skillful handling, with different
emphases and grammatical contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not automatically translate English "smart" meaning high intelligence as
スマート; [賢い](賢い.md) (かしこい) or [利口](利口.md) (りこう) may fit.
But "never clever" would also be wrong: the dictionary explicitly records
clever or adroit handling. The second example praises a smooth way of
resolving a problem, not necessarily a measured intellectual ability.
