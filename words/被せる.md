# 被せる (かぶせる) - cover; put on someone's head

## Meaning

被せる, read かぶせる here, is a transitive ichidan verb, usually written in kana. It describes putting something over something else, including putting headwear on another person.

- 子供に帽子をかぶせました。 (こどもにぼうしをかぶせました。) - I put a hat on the child.
- 料理に蓋をかぶせておきました。 (りょうりにふたをかぶせておきました。) - I left a lid over the food.
- 失敗の責任を部下にかぶせてはいけません。 (しっぱいのせきにんをぶかにかぶせてはいけません。) - You must not shift the blame for a failure onto a subordinate.

XにYをかぶせる identifies what receives the covering or burden with に and what is put over or imposed with を. The negative is かぶせない; the past is かぶせた.

## How the kanji combine

[被](../kanji/被.md) concerns covering; せる completes this native verb. Learn かぶせる as a verb in its own right, not as a general rule for forming a causative from any verb ending in る.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[被る](被る.md) (かぶる) describes putting something on one's own head or taking blame. 被せる changes the perspective to putting it on someone else or imposing the blame. [覆う](覆う.md) (おおう) overlaps in covering more generally; [カバー](カバー.md) also has physical and figurative covering uses.

## Distinctive meaning

The overlying idea extends to pouring something over an object, covering a tooth with a crown, or adding music over an image. 相手の話にかぶせて話す (あいてのはなしにかぶせてはなす) means to speak over the other person. These uses need context; they are not all headwear.

The separate verb 着せる (きせる), put clothing on someone, also has 被せる among its dictionary spellings. That does not make きせる the primary reading of the かぶせる verb taught here.
