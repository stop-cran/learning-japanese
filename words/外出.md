# 外出 (がいしゅつ) — going out

## Meaning

外出 is going out of one's home or workplace. It is a noun and forms the intransitive verb
**外出する**, "go out."
Useful expressions include 外出禁止 (がいしゅつきんし), "a ban on going out,"
and 外出中 (がいしゅつちゅう), "out at the moment."

- 母は外出しています。 (はははがいしゅつしています。) - My mother is out.
- 午後は用事で外出します。 (ごごはようじでがいしゅつします。) - I am going out on an errand this afternoon.

外出している can describe the state of being away after leaving, not just the movement of exiting.
In the second example, で identifies the reason for going out.

## How the kanji combine

[外](../kanji/外.md) (outside) plus [出](../kanji/出.md) (go out): "go out to the outside". It is a modifier-plus-verb compound: 外 "outside" qualifies 出 "go out". Both on'yomi: ガイ + シュツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[出かける](出かける.md) (でかける) is an everyday overlap for setting out or going out.
外へ出る (そとへでる) literally means to go outside.
在宅 (ざいたく), "being at home," contrasts with the state of being out;
[帰宅](帰宅.md) (きたく), "returning home," instead names a subsequent movement.

## Distinctive meaning

外出 fits notices, news, and messages about someone's availability, and can sound more formal than 出かける.
It is not restricted to writing or excluded from conversation.
Going out does not require remaining outdoors: visiting another building can still be 外出.
The focus is absence from one's usual home or workplace.
