# 外出 (がいしゅつ) — going out

## Meaning

外出 is going out of one's home or workplace: 外出する (go out), 外出禁止 がいしゅつきんし (a ban on going out), 外出中 がいしゅつちゅう (out at the moment), 母は外出しています (my mother is out).

## How the kanji combine

[外](../kanji/外.md) (outside) plus [出](../kanji/出.md) (go out): "go out to the outside". It is a modifier-plus-verb compound: 外 "outside" qualifies 出 "go out". Both on'yomi: ガイ + シュツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 出かける でかける (go out, everyday), 外へ出る そとへでる (go outside).
- Antonym: 在宅 ざいたく (being at home), 帰宅 きたく (going home).

## Distinctive meaning

外出 is formal or written (notices, news, a phone message). In casual speech use 出かける.
