# 国際 (こくさい) - international

## Meaning

国際 expresses a connection between countries. Learners often meet it at the beginning of compounds: 国際会議 (こくさいかいぎ), "international conference," or 国際空港 (こくさいくうこう), "international airport."

- 来月、国際会議に参加します。 (らいげつ、こくさいかいぎにさんかします。) - I will attend an international conference next month.

For an ordinary descriptive adjective, 国際的な (こくさいてきな), "international," is useful. Do not treat bare 国際 as an i-adjective or automatically add な to it.

## How the kanji combine

[国](../kanji/国.md) means country and [際](../kanji/際.md) suggests an interface or boundary. The compound uses on readings コク + サイ and concerns relations across countries.

## Synonyms and antonyms

国内 (こくない), "domestic; within the country," supplies a useful contrast. 外国 (がいこく), "foreign country," does not by itself mean international cooperation or exchange.

## Distinctive meaning

"International" is not the same as "foreign." An international event may take place in your own country and include local participants alongside visitors.
