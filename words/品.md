# 品 (しな) - goods; an item

## Meaning

Read **しな**, 品 is a noun for an item, goods, or stock.

- この店には珍しい品があります。 (このみせにはめずらしいしながあります。) - This shop has unusual items.

Read **ひん**, a separate dictionary entry has the noun meaning refinement, elegance, or dignity:

- 彼女の話し方には品があります。 (かのじょのはなしかたにはひんがあります。) - There is refinement in the way she speaks.

The ひん entry also supplies an item suffix, as in 手作り品 (てづくりひん), "a handmade item," and an item or dish counter:

- 料理をもう一品注文しました。 (りょうりをもういっぴんちゅうもんしました。) - I ordered one more dish.

一品 is いっぴん here; 三品 (さんぴん) means three dishes/items. The ぴん sound occurs in such combinations, not as a separate standalone headword reading.

## How the kanji combine

[品](../kanji/品.md) alone writes the native goods noun しな. The same character also has on-reading ひん uses. The metadata keeps しな as the primary reading, not as a rule for every appearance of 品.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[物](物.md) (もの) is a broad word for a thing. [商品](商品.md) (しょうひん) specifies goods offered for sale. Neither replaces 品 (ひん) in the refinement example.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the construction, not just the character. A shop's 珍しい品 concerns items; 品がある can evaluate elegance. Do not freely pronounce the standalone goods noun ひん merely because compounds and counters use that reading family.
