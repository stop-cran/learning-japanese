# 試し (ためし) - trial; try

## Meaning

試し is a noun for a trial, test, or try. The useful expression 試しに means "as a trial; to try it out." It introduces an action done to see what the result is.

- 試しにこのアプリを使ってみました。 (ためしにこのアプリをつかってみました。) - I tried using this app to see what it was like.
- まずは試しに一つ作りましょう。 (まずはためしにひとつつくりましょう。) - Let's make one as a trial first.

に turns the noun into an expression of how or why the action is undertaken. The first example combines it naturally with ～てみる, "try doing."

## How the kanji combine

[試](../kanji/試.md) contributes testing or trying. 試し is the noun form related to [試す](試す.md) (ためす), "try; test"; し is okurigana. Learn the native reading ためし as a whole.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[試験](試験.md) (しけん) can name an examination or test with a more defined procedure. 試しに can introduce an informal experiment, such as making one sample before deciding to make more.

## Distinctive meaning

試し does not imply success. It labels the attempt as exploratory rather than final. A trial batch may be useful even when it reveals a problem. For the action itself, use 試す or a suitable verb after 試しに rather than assuming every noun becomes a する verb.
