# 泳ぎ方 (およぎかた) — way of swimming

## Meaning

泳ぎ方 means a way or technique of swimming, or "how to swim." It is a noun, not a verb conjugation.

先生に泳ぎ方を教わりました。 (せんせいにおよぎかたをおそわりました。) — I learned how to swim from my teacher.

この泳ぎ方は楽です。 (このおよぎかたはらくです。) — This way of swimming is easy.

## How the kanji combine

[泳](../kanji/泳.md) comes from 泳ぐ (およぐ), "swim." Replace the final ぐ with ぎ to obtain 泳ぎ, the form before ます, and add [方](../kanji/方.md) (かた), "way." The written kanji boundary alone does not identify that stem.

## Synonyms and antonyms

泳ぐ names the action. 水泳 (すいえい) names swimming as an activity or sport. 方法 (ほうほう) is the more general noun "method."

## Distinctive meaning

The pattern extends to other verbs: 読み方 (よみかた), "way of reading," and 食べ方 (たべかた), "way of eating." Here 方 is read かた, not ほう, and attaches to the verb's ます-stem.
