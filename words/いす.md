# いす (いす) — chair

## Meaning

いす means **chair**: a piece of furniture for sitting on. It can also cover seats such as stools, depending on the object.

いすに座ってください。 (いすにすわってください。) — Please sit on the chair.

The place you sit takes に with 座る (すわる). To describe a chair, put the modifier before the noun: 小さいいす (ちいさいいす), “a small chair.”

## How the kanji combine

The ordinary kanji spelling is 椅[子](../kanji/子.md), 椅子 (いす). This is a Chinese-derived word, not a modern European loanword. It is also written イス; katakana alone does not prove English origin. Here the whole word is written in hiragana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[席](席.md) (せき) is a seat or place assigned to someone, for example on a train. It need not mean a movable chair. [ベンチ](ベンチ.md) is a bench; [ソファー](ソファー.md) is a sofa.

## Distinctive meaning

いす normally identifies the physical furniture. A request for an available seat or reservation may instead use 席, even when the seat happens to be a chair.
