# ぼそぼそ — mumbling; dry and crumbly

## Meaning

Subdued, indistinct speech, or food with an unpleasantly dry, unappealing texture.

- 彼は下を向いて、ぼそぼそ話した。 — He looked down and spoke in a mumble.
- このパンはぼそぼそしていて、食べにくい。 — This bread is dry and crumbly and difficult to eat.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もぐもぐ](もぐもぐ.md) also describes mumbling. [ひそひそ](ひそひそ.md) foregrounds whispering; [ぱさぱさ](ぱさぱさ.md) overlaps in dry food, without the speech sense.

## Distinctive meaning

ぼそぼそ話す does not necessarily mean keeping a secret. In ぼそぼそしたパン, “mumbling bread” would be a misleading translation: the word describes texture. The two uses should be learned with their subjects and verbs.
