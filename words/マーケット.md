# マーケット - market

## Meaning

マーケット is a market. It can name a place or event where goods are sold, or the commercial market for a product. It is a noun; a buying location can take で.

- 週末のマーケットで野菜を買いました。 (しゅうまつのマーケットでやさいをかいました。) - I bought vegetables at the weekend market.
- この商品には海外のマーケットがあります。 (このしょうひんにはかいがいのマーケットがあります。) - There is an overseas market for this product.

The first sentence concerns a place to shop. The second concerns buyers and commercial demand, not necessarily a particular building.

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword corresponds to English "market." It has no kanji components. Keep the long vowel in マー and the small ッ in ケット: マーケット, not マケット.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[市場](市場.md) has the reading いちば for a marketplace; しじょう is useful for economic markets. スーパーマーケット is specifically a supermarket, so the longer word does not identify every possible マーケット.

## Distinctive meaning

Decide whether the sentence concerns a physical selling place or an economic market. A market can grow without a building being enlarged. Conversely, going to a local マーケット does not necessarily mean participating in a financial exchange or buying shares.
