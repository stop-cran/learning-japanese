# 日差し (ひざし) - sunlight; sun's rays

## Meaning

日差し is sunlight or the sun's rays shining on a place.
It is a noun, useful when describing the light's strength or how it feels.

- 今日は日差しが強いです。 (きょうはひざしがつよいです。) - The sunlight is strong today.
- 窓から暖かい日差しが入ってきました。 (まどからあたたかいひざしがはいってきました。) - Warm sunlight came in through the window.

日差しを浴びる (ひざしをあびる) means to be exposed to or bask
in sunlight.

## How the kanji combine

[日](../kanji/日.md) contributes the sun, and
[差](../kanji/差.md), through 差す (さす), its light shining in.
The native elements are ひ + さし, voiced to ひざし.
差 here does not mean a numerical difference between days.

JMdict also records 陽射し and 日射し. The source's 陽射 is
marked search-only; this article uses 日差し rather than promoting
that shortened search form as a preferred learner spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[日光](日光.md) (にっこう) also means sunlight.
[光](光.md) (ひかり) and [光線](光線.md) (こうせん) are broader
light terms and do not require the sun.
[日当たり](日当たり.md) (ひあたり) concerns a place's sun exposure;
[日陰](日陰.md) (ひかげ), shade, provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

日差し names the light, not the sun as a celestial object.
強い日差し describes strong sunlight; it does not by itself
give a temperature, ultraviolet measurement, or medical recommendation.
Alternative spellings do not add their characters to this article's
primary 日差し metadata.
