# 得意 (とくい) — one's strong point

## Meaning

得意 describes a strong point or something a person is good at. The useful pattern is
Nが得意だ, "be good at N." Before a noun, use な.

- 私は料理が得意です。 (わたしはりょうりがとくいです。) — I am good at cooking.
- 得意な科目は数学です。 (とくいなかもくはすうがくです。) — My strong subject is mathematics.

## How the kanji combine

[得](../kanji/得.md) contributes a gain or advantage association; [意](../kanji/意.md) concerns the
mind or intention. The established combination names a person's forte in the sense taught here.
These associations help recognition, but "gain an intention" is not a literal definition. Both readings,
トク + イ, are on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

上手 (じょうず) describes skill or proficiency. 得意 frames the activity as someone's strong area;
上手 focuses on being skillful at it. 苦手 (にがて), "not good at," gives a useful contrast:
料理が苦手です (りょうりがにがてです), "I am not good at cooking."

## Distinctive meaning

Being 得意 at something is not the same as liking it. You can describe ability with 得意 without making
a claim about enjoyment. This article selects the forte/ability sense, rather than assuming every use
of the noun 得意 has that meaning.
