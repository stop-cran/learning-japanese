# 慕う (したう) — long for; adore; look up to

## Meaning

慕う is to feel warm attachment to someone or something. Digital Daijisen gives three senses: to miss or long for, as in 遠い祖国を慕う (とおいそこくをしたう), “long for one’s distant homeland”; to follow someone one cannot bear to leave, as in 赤ん坊が母を慕って泣く (あかんぼうがははをしたってなく), “the baby cries after its mother”; and to be drawn to a superior’s character or learning, as in その徳を慕って人が集まる (そのとくをしたってひとがあつまる), “people gather, drawn by that person’s virtue.” JMdict lists the same three: “to yearn for; to adore,” “to follow (someone)” and “to idolize (for virtue, learning, status, etc.).”

- 生徒たちはその先生を姉のように慕っていた。 (せいとたちはそのせんせいをあねのようにしたっていた。) — The students adored that teacher like an older sister.
- 多くの若手が、その監督の人柄を慕って集まった。 (おおくのわかてが、そのかんとくのひとがらをしたってあつまった。) — Many younger people gathered around the director, drawn by the director’s character.

## How the kanji combine

[慕](../kanji/慕.md) alone writes した, and う is okurigana, the kana ending. 慕って (したって), in both examples, is the te-form. を marks the person or thing one is drawn to: 先生を慕う, 人柄を慕う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[憧れる](憧れる.md) (あこがれる) is to admire or yearn for something, and [尊敬](尊敬.md) (そんけい) is respect or esteem. [恋しい](恋しい.md) (こいしい) is an adjective for missing someone or something. 慕う is a verb of warm attachment to a person or place, and also of following someone one admires.

## Distinctive meaning

Related words are 慕わしい (したわしい), “dear; beloved,” 思慕 (しぼ), “yearning; deep affection,” and 慕情 (ぼじょう), “longing; yearning.”
