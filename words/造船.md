# 造船 (ぞうせん) - shipbuilding

## Meaning

造船 is building ships, as an activity or industry.
It is a noun and also forms 造船する.
造船業 (ぞうせんぎょう) is the shipbuilding industry.

- 父は造船の仕事をしています。 (ちちはぞうせんのしごとをしています。) - My father works in shipbuilding.
- この町では造船が盛んです。 (このまちではぞうせんがさかんです。) - Shipbuilding is thriving in this town.

造船所 (ぞうせんじょ) is a shipyard, the place where ships are built
or worked on. The extra 所 identifies a place, not a separate type of ship.

## How the kanji combine

[造](../kanji/造.md) contributes making or constructing, and
[船](../kanji/船.md) a ship.
The compound names constructing ships. The on readings are ゾウ + セン.
船 uses セン here, not its standalone native reading ふね.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[製造](製造.md) (せいぞう) is manufacture more broadly.
[工業](工業.md) (こうぎょう) concerns manufacturing industry.
[船](船.md) (ふね) names the vessel, not its construction.
Shipbuilding and traveling by ship are different activities.

## Distinctive meaning

造船 identifies a field of making things; it does not itself say who
designed, owns, or operates a particular vessel.
Learn the noun patterns above without inferring a blanket ban on direct
objects: Japanese dictionaries also record a ship type followed by を造船する.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
entry 1403720, sense 1, labeled intransitive する.
Additional construction: [Digital Daijisen, 造船](https://www.weblio.jp/content/造船),
which explicitly illustrates a tanker as the object of 造船する.
