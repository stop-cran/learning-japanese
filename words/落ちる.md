# 落ちる (おちる) — fall down

## Meaning

落ちる describes something falling or dropping. It is intransitive: the thing that falls
is the subject, normally marked with が. The person who caused the fall need not be mentioned.

- コップが机から落ちました。 (コップがつくえからおちました。) — The glass fell from the desk.
- 葉が木から落ちた。 (はがきからおちた。) — A leaf fell from the tree.

This is an ichidan verb, which drops る before endings such as ます: 落ちます (おちます).

## How the kanji combine

There is one written kanji: [落](../kanji/落.md), "fall; drop." The following ちる is
okurigana, kana written after a kanji to complete the word and show its inflection.
This is a native Japanese verb, not a compound built from on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

落とす (おとす), "drop something," is the useful partner, not an interchangeable synonym.
Compare 私がコップを落とした (わたしがコップをおとした), "I dropped the glass."
Now the glass takes を, and the person doing the dropping is explicit.

## Distinctive meaning

落ちる does not by itself say whether the fall was accidental or deliberately caused.
Choose between 落ちる and 落とす according to how the sentence presents the event:
something falls, or someone drops something. This article focuses on physical falling,
not every extended sense of the verb.
