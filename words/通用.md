# 通用 (つうよう) - be accepted; be valid

## Meaning

通用 concerns being in accepted or current use, as with a language or currency,
or being valid, as with a ticket. 通用する is intransitive:
the thing accepted or valid takes が, and で can identify the relevant setting.

- この切符は市内のバスで通用します。
  (このきっぷはしないのバスでつうようします。) - This ticket is valid on buses within the city.
- その言い訳はここでは通用しません。
  (そのいいわけはここではつうようしません。) - That excuse will not work here.

The second example extends to an excuse, idea, or method being accepted or working.
通用しない does not necessarily mean physically broken.

に can mark the range in which something is accepted:
世界に通用する新技術 (せかいにつうようするしんぎじゅつ),
"new technology accepted internationally."
This is another construction, not a rule to replace every で with に.

## How the kanji combine

[通](../kanji/通.md) contributes passing or circulating, and
[用](../kanji/用.md) contributes use.
Together they point to something being usable or accepted in a setting.
The readings are ツウ + ヨウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[有効](有効.md) (ゆうこう) also means valid or effective;
[効力](効力.md) (こうりょく) names effect or validity.
[成立](成立.md) (せいりつ) includes holding true, and
[妥当](妥当.md) (だとう) concerns validity or appropriateness.
[働く](働く.md) (はたらく) also has a functioning sense behind English "work";
it is not limited to employment.
That use concerns something operating or having an effect;
the excuse example above instead asks whether the excuse will be accepted.
[効く](効く.md) (きく) concerns having an effect or working effectively,
rather than every kind of accepted use.
These are useful overlaps, not automatic substitutions in every construction.

## Distinctive meaning

[通る](通る.md) (とおる) can mean being accepted, and
[通じる](通じる.md) (つうじる) also has a working or effective sense.
[適用](適用.md) (てきよう) concerns applying a rule or method to a case.
[循環](循環.md) (じゅんかん) is circulation in a broader cyclical sense;
通用 is not the word for blood circulating around the body.
[通貨](通貨.md) (つうか) names currency as money.
通用 concerns accepted use: English "currency" can also name that state,
but 通用 is not itself a word for money.
The additional "going in and out" sense appears in 通用口 (つうようぐち),
an entrance used for regular access, such as by staff.
It is separate from judging whether a ticket or excuse is valid.
