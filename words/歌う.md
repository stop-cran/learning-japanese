# 歌う (うたう) — to sing

## Meaning

歌う means to sing. It can stand without an object, or take the song being sung with を.

- みんなで歌いましょう。 (みんなでうたいましょう。) — Let's sing together.
- 日本語の歌を歌いました。 (にほんごのうたをうたいました。) — I sang a song in Japanese.

## How the kanji combine

[歌](../kanji/歌.md) represents うた, "song," and the okurigana う completes the native verb うたう. This is not a compound. It is a godan verb: 歌います うたいます is polite, and 歌って うたって is its て-form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- 歌唱 かしょう means singing and is more formal than ordinary 歌う.
- [話す](話す.md) はなす means to speak or talk, not to sing. It is a related vocal activity, not an interchangeable synonym.
- There is no natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

歌 is the song or singing as a noun; 歌う is the action. 歌を歌う うたをうたう, literally "sing a song," is normal Japanese, not a repetition to avoid. The word can also concern expressing something in poetry, but ordinary beginner use is singing.
