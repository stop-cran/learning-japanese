# 四日 (よっか) - fourth day; four days

## Meaning

四日 means either the fourth day of a month or a duration of four days.
Its special day-counting reading is よっか in both uses.

- 会議は六月四日です。 (かいぎはろくがつよっかです。) - The meeting is on June fourth.
- 京都に四日間いました。 (きょうとによっかかんいました。) - I stayed in Kyoto for four days.

## How the kanji combine

[四](../kanji/四.md), "four," combines with [日](../kanji/日.md), "day."
This belongs to the native day-counting series, not the regular on-reading
combination シ + ニチ. Include the small っ in よっか.

## Synonyms and antonyms

四日間 (よっかかん) explicitly indicates duration. 四日目 (よっかめ) means
"the fourth day" within a sequence, which need not be the fourth of a month.
These related forms are not interchangeable in every date expression.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes date from duration. A month name before 四日 makes
the calendar date clear; 間 after it makes a period clear.
Do not confuse よっか with 八日 (ようか), "the eighth; eight days."
