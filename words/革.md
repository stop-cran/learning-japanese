# 革 (かわ) - leather

## Meaning

革 is the noun "leather," the material used for items such as shoes, bags, and belts. Use 革の before the name of an item, or 革で to identify the material it is made from.

- 革のかばんを買いました。 (かわのかばんをかいました。) - I bought a leather bag.
- この靴は革でできています。 (このくつはかわでできています。) - These shoes are made of leather.

The first sentence uses の to modify かばん. In the second, で marks the material, not the location where the shoes were manufactured.

## How the kanji combine

革 is one kanji with the native reading かわ in this word. It writes the whole noun; there is no okurigana or compound relationship. Learn the spelling along with the material sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[皮](皮.md), also read かわ, refers to skin, hide, or an outer covering. 革 focuses on leather as a processed material. The identical sound does not make the two written nouns interchangeable in every context.

## Distinctive meaning

The spelling helps distinguish a leather product from an animal's skin or a fruit's peel. 革の靴 describes the shoe material, not the outside surface of a shoe generally. For material labels, read the complete expression: 合成皮革 (ごうせいひかく), "synthetic leather," adds information that the plain word 革 does not convey by itself.
