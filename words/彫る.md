# 彫る (ほる) - carve; engrave; sculpt

## Meaning

彫る means to carve, engrave, sculpt, or chisel. It is a transitive godan verb: the object being made or marked takes を, and に can mark the material or surface.

- 木で人形を彫りました。 (きでにんぎょうをほりました。) - I carved a doll from wood.
- 石に名前を彫りました。 (いしになまえをほりました。) - I engraved a name in the stone.

The forms are 彫ります (ほります), 彫って (ほって), and 彫らない (ほらない). The dictionary also records tattooing under the reading ほる.

## How the kanji combine

彫 supplies ほ, and る completes the native verb. It is a single-kanji verb, not a compound whose component shapes establish a separate rule for each material.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[彫刻](彫刻.md) (ちょうこく) is carving, engraving, or sculpture and can be used with する. [刻む](刻む.md) (きざむ) includes engraving or incising, as well as other uses such as chopping finely.

[掘る](掘る.md) (ほる) concerns digging or excavating. The shared pronunciation does not make a carved name and a dug hole the same operation.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 彫る as ほる for ordinary recognition and production. JMdict also records obsolete える and rare spellings 雕る and 鐫る, but explicitly restricts the reading ほる to 彫る. Those rare forms should not automatically be taught as ほる alternatives.
