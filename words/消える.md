# 消える (きえる) — disappear; go out

## Meaning

消える means to disappear, vanish, or go out. It is intransitive: the thing disappearing takes が. With a light or flame, it means that the light or flame is no longer on.

- 雲が消えました。  
  くもがきえました。  
  "The clouds disappeared."
- 部屋の電気が消えました。  
  へやのでんきがきえました。  
  "The light in the room went out."

## How the kanji combine

[消](../kanji/消.md) relates to disappearing or extinguishing. The written reading boundary is き + える, but the ichidan verb stem is きえ: 消えます (きえます), 消えて (きえて), 消えない (きえない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

消す (けす) is the transitive partner: 電気を消す (でんきをけす), "turn off the light". 現れる (あらわれる), "appear", contrasts with disappearing from view; it is not an opposite for every use of 消える.

## Distinctive meaning

消える describes the change, not who caused it. 電気が消えた can describe a power failure or a light someone switched off; the verb alone does not identify the cause.
