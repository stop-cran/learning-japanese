# 食堂 (しょくどう) — dining hall, cafeteria

## Meaning

食堂 is a dining room, dining hall, cafeteria, or canteen. It can also name an eatery serving ordinary meals.

- 学校の食堂で昼ご飯を食べます。（がっこうの しょくどうで ひるごはんを たべます。）— I eat lunch in the school cafeteria.
- 駅の近くに小さな食堂があります。（えきの ちかくに ちいさな しょくどうが あります。）— There is a small eatery near the station.

## How the kanji combine

[食](../kanji/食.md) ショク supplies the activity, eating; [堂](../kanji/堂.md) ドウ names a hall. It is an activity-plus-place compound: a hall or place for eating. Both are on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- レストラン means restaurant and overlaps with 食堂 when it names a public eating establishment.
- 台所 だいどころ means kitchen, the place for preparing food rather than the dining area.
- There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

Context matters: a school's 食堂 is usually a cafeteria, while a small independent 食堂 may be an everyday diner. Do not assume that all restaurants or all kitchens are 食堂.

The dictionary separately lists 食堂 じきどう for a dining hall at a Buddhist temple. That specialized word is not the everyday しょくどう taught here.
