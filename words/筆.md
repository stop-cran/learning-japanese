# 筆 (ふで) - writing brush

## Meaning

筆 is a noun for a writing or painting brush. It is useful in calligraphy and painting contexts. The word also has extended uses relating to writing and composition.

- 筆で自分の名前を書きました。 (ふででじぶんのなまえをかきました。) - I wrote my name with a brush.
- 使った筆を水で洗いました。 (つかったふでをみずであらいました。) - I washed the brush I had used with water.

で marks the tool in 筆で書く, while を marks the object in 筆を洗う. A description such as 太い筆 (ふといふで) means "a thick brush."

## How the kanji combine

筆 alone writes the native word ふで. There is no compound to split. The character is read ヒツ in [鉛筆](鉛筆.md) (えんぴつ), "pencil," where the sound changes to ぴつ within the compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ペン names a pen and 鉛筆 a pencil; in a supplies list, neither automatically replaces a brush. [絵画](絵画.md) (かいが) names paintings or painting, the work or field rather than the tool.

## Distinctive meaning

Although the dictionary gives a broader range including "pen" and the act of writing, a concrete calligraphy lesson is a useful place to learn the brush sense. In figurative expressions, 筆 can refer to composing text rather than the physical implement. Do not assume every sentence containing it reports someone holding a traditional brush.
