# 寿命 (じゅみょう) - lifespan; service life

## Meaning

寿命 is the length of a living thing's life. It can also mean the usable life of an object or device.

- この動物の寿命は約二十年です。 (このどうぶつのじゅみょうはやくにじゅうねんです。) - This animal's lifespan is about twenty years.
- 電池の寿命が短くなりました。 (でんちのじゅみょうがみじかくなりました。) - The battery's service life has become shorter.

寿命が長い / 短い (じゅみょうがながい / みじかい) means having a long / short lifespan or service life. The first example is illustrative, not a factual claim about an identified species.

## How the kanji combine

寿 has longevity associations, and [命](../kanji/命.md) concerns life. Together they provide a useful association with the duration of life. Read the whole word じゅみょう, with a long みょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[命](命.md) and [生命](生命.md) concern life and have relevant lifetime or working-life extensions. [一生](一生.md) emphasizes a whole lifetime; [人生](人生.md) is a person's life or course of life. [生](生.md) is a broader life word, not a substitute for 寿命 in every duration expression.

## Distinctive meaning

寿命 foregrounds how long life or usefulness lasts. It does not mean the way someone lives day to day, and it does not by itself name a fatal illness. 寿命が来る (じゅみょうがくる) can describe reaching the end of a natural or usable life; an object's failure is not necessarily repairable merely because this expression is used.
