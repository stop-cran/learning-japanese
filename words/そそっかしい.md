# そそっかしい - careless; hasty and prone to mistakes

## Meaning

そそっかしい describes hasty, inattentive behavior that easily leads to mistakes.
It can characterize a person or a particular careless act.
It is an i-adjective: そそっかしい人, そそっかしくない and
そそっかしかった follow the usual pattern.

- 私はそそっかしくて、よく忘れ物をします。 (わたしはそそっかしくて、よくわすれものをします。) - I am rather scatterbrained and often leave things behind.
- 左右の違う靴を履くなんて、そそっかしいですね。 (さゆうのちがうくつをはくなんて、そそっかしいですね。) - Wearing mismatched shoes is rather careless, isn't it?

The behavior is careless or rash, not necessarily deliberately irresponsible.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[うっかり](うっかり.md) commonly presents an inadvertent lapse.
そそっかしい can describe a tendency to make such mistakes.
[おおざっぱ](おおざっぱ.md) concerns roughness or lack of detail, which
can overlap with carelessness without always being a mistake.
[慎重](慎重.md) (しんちょう), careful/cautious, is a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not reduce そそっかしい to moving physically fast.
A quick, accurate worker is not careless merely for being quick.
The characterization can sound critical, so context and tone matter when
applying it to another person.
