# きりきり — sharp gripping pain; grinding

## Meaning

A sharp, gripping pain, especially in descriptions of the stomach. Other uses evoke grinding, tightening, or rapid rotation.

- 胃（い）がきりきり痛む。 — I have a sharp, gripping pain in my stomach.
- 発表（はっぴょう）の前に、おなかがきりきり痛くなった。 — Before the presentation, my stomach began to hurt sharply.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずきずき](ずきずき.md) brings repeated throbs into focus; [ひりひり](ひりひり.md) often describes a smarting sting. These are descriptions of experience, not mutually exclusive illnesses.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 胃がきりきり痛む（いたむ）as a whole phrase. The dictionary also records a brisk “get on with it” manner. Do not translate every instance as stomach pain without checking the action.
