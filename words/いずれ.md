# いずれ (いずれ) - which; eventually

## Meaning

いずれ can refer to "which" member of a set. In いずれか it means "one of
them"; in いずれも it covers all the relevant choices, or both if there are
two. As a time adverb, it means "eventually" or "in due course."

- 二つの方法のいずれかを選んでください。 (ふたつのほうほうのいずれかをえらんでください。) - Please choose one of the two methods.
- いずれも便利です。 (いずれもべんりです。) - Both are convenient.
- いずれ分かります。 (いずれわかります。) - You will understand eventually.

いずれの + noun is another way to express "which." The phrase
いずれにしても means "in any case," setting aside which alternative is true.

## How the kanji combine

The usual spelling is kana. The dictionary also lists 何れ, but the kana
word needs no invented character-by-character explanation. Keep the standard
spelling いずれ rather than treating いづれ as a separate meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[どれ](どれ.md) asks "which one?" and [どちら](どちら.md) can ask "which?"
between alternatives. [皆](皆.md) (みな) overlaps with the "all" meaning of
いずれも, not with its future-time use.

## Distinctive meaning

The small following word matters: か selects an unspecified member, while
も extends the statement across the set. The eventual-time use does not
give a definite date or promise that something will happen immediately.
