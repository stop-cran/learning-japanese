# 誘う (さそう) - invite; entice

## Meaning

誘う is a transitive godan verb meaning to invite someone to do something, or to entice or draw them in. Use person + を and activity/occasion + に.

- 友達を映画に誘いました。 (ともだちをえいがにさそいました。) - I invited my friend to a movie.
- 猫が魚のにおいに誘われて来ました。 (ねこがさかなのにおいにさそわれてきました。) - The cat came, drawn by the smell of fish.

The passive 誘われる (さそわれる) means "be invited/enticed." The past is 誘った (さそった), and the negative is 誘わない (さそわない).

The verb can also mean to evoke a response: 涙を誘う話 (なみだをさそうはなし), "a story that brings tears," names an emotional effect rather than an invitation.

## How the kanji combine

誘 writes the native verb stem さそ, followed by う. There is no multi-kanji compound. The spelling also supports another dictionary reading, いざなう; this card teaches さそう and its forms.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[招く](招く.md) (まねく) can mean inviting or welcoming someone. [招待](招待.md) (しょうたい) is an invitation or the act of inviting as a guest. 誘う readily describes asking a friend to join an activity, without specifying a hosted event.

## Distinctive meaning

An invitation does not establish acceptance: 誘ったけれど断られた (さそったけれどことわられた) means "I invited them, but they declined." The object also distinguishes meanings: invite a person, entice an animal, or evoke tears. Do not read the English "ask" as asking any kind of question.
