# 宣伝 (せんでん) - publicity; advertising; propaganda

## Meaning

宣伝 is promotion or publicity: making a product, event, idea, or cause
known to people. It is a noun and a transitive する verb.
Use thing を宣伝する for publicizing something.

- 店の人が新しい商品を宣伝しています。 (みせのひとがあたらしいしょうひんをせんでんしています。) - The shop staff are promoting a new product.
- 友達に文化祭の宣伝を頼みました。 (ともだちにぶんかさいのせんでんをたのみました。) - I asked a friend to publicize the school festival.

Political propaganda is also within the word's range.
The word alone does not prove that the message is false.

## How the kanji combine

宣 contributes making something publicly known, and
[伝](../kanji/伝.md) transmitting or conveying.
Together they suggest spreading a message. The on readings are セン + デン.
This is a meaning aid, not a historical account of the characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[広告](広告.md) (こうこく) names advertising or an advertisement.
宣伝 can describe promotion more broadly, including people spreading
information by word of mouth. This is not an absolute rule that one
requires payment and the other never does.

## Distinctive meaning

宣伝 concerns getting a message out, not automatically persuading every
recipient or making a sale. 商品の宣伝 (しょうひんのせんでん)
is promotion of a product; the product itself is not the 宣伝.
