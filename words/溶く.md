# 溶く (とく) - mix into liquid; beat eggs

## Meaning

溶く is a transitive verb for mixing something with a liquid or dissolving it.
It also means beating or stirring eggs. The ingredient takes を.

- 小麦粉を水で溶きます。
  (こむぎこをみずでときます。) - Mix the flour with water.
- ボウルで卵を溶いてください。
  (ボウルでたまごをといてください。) - Please beat the eggs in a bowl.

水で identifies the liquid used in the first example. Mixing flour into water
does not imply that it forms a true chemical solution.
It is godan: 溶きます, 溶いた, 溶いて, 溶かない.

## How the kanji combine

[溶](../kanji/溶.md) contributes dissolving or mixing with liquid.
The native reading is とく, with く as okurigana.
There is no second kanji to combine with it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[溶かす](溶かす.md) (とかす) overlaps for dissolving something, but it also
straightforwardly describes melting butter by heat.
[混合](混合.md) (こんごう) is broader mixing; not every mixture involves a liquid.
[解く](解く.md) is the separate same-reading verb for untying or solving.

## Distinctive meaning

卵を溶く means beating eggs, not necessarily cooking scrambled eggs.
JMdict also records a rare melting or smelting sense; it is not the default
model for these kitchen patterns. Do not use 溶く for solving a problem
merely because 解く has the same reading.
