# 頼もしい (たのもしい) - dependable; reassuring; promising

## Meaning

頼もしい describes someone or something one can rely on and feel
reassured by. It can also mean promising, giving reason to expect
something good.

- 彼女は困ったときに助けてくれる頼もしい友人です。
  (かのじょはこまったときにたすけてくれるたのもしいゆうじんです。) - She is a dependable friend who helps me when I am in trouble.
- 若い選手の成長が頼もしいです。
  (わかいせんしゅのせいちょうがたのもしいです。) - The young athlete's progress is promising.

It is an i-adjective: 頼もしい人, 頼もしく感じる
(たのもしくかんじる), "find reassuring," and 頼もしくない,
"not reassuring/dependable."

## How the kanji combine

[頼](../kanji/頼.md) connects the word with reliance or trust.
It is read たの in 頼もしい; もしい completes the adjective.
Do not substitute the related verb 頼る (たよる) as its reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[確か](確か.md) (たしか) and [確実](確実.md) (かくじつ)
overlap with reliability or certainty in some contexts.
頼りない (たよりない), "unreliable; not reassuring," provides
a useful contrast. [信頼](信頼.md) (しんらい) names trust
rather than being the same adjective.

## Distinctive meaning

The reassuring assessment matters: physical strength alone does not
make someone 頼もしい. The promising use expresses expectation,
not a guarantee of future success. Use 頼もしい as the spelling
here; the dictionary's search-only 頼母しい is not a parallel
recommended spelling to practice.
