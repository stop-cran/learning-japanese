# 辞典 (じてん) — dictionary; lexicon

## Meaning

辞典 is a **dictionary**, a reference work explaining words, readings, meanings, or translations. It is often part of a dictionary's name.

辞典で言葉の意味を調べます。 (じてんでことばのいみをしらべます。) — I look up word meanings in a dictionary.

Here で marks the resource used. 国語辞典 (こくごじてん) is a Japanese-language dictionary; 英和辞典 (えいわじてん) is an English–Japanese dictionary.

## How the kanji combine

[辞](../kanji/辞.md) contributes words or language, and 典 contributes a reference work or authoritative text. The on readings ジ + テン give じてん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

辞書 (じしょ) is another ordinary word for dictionary and overlaps strongly with 辞典. The homophone 事典 (じてん) is an encyclopedia or reference work organized around subjects rather than chiefly word meanings.

## Distinctive meaning

辞典 does not specify a printed format: a dictionary can be electronic. In speech, 辞典 and 事典 sound the same, so the title or context identifies which kind of reference is intended. They are not different readings of the same spelling.
