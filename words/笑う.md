# 笑う (わらう) — laugh; smile

## Meaning

笑う describes laughing or smiling. These basic senses are intransitive: the person doing it is the subject,
not an object marked with を.
Context tells you whether there is audible laughter or a smile.

- みんなが笑いました。 (みんながわらいました。) — Everyone laughed.
- 彼女はにっこり笑いました。 (かのじょはにっこりわらいました。) — She smiled.

This is a godan verb: 笑います (わらいます), 笑った (わらった), 笑って (わらって).
The negative is 笑わない (わらわない), with わ before ない.

## How the kanji combine

[笑](../kanji/笑.md) carries the laugh or smile meaning.
う is the native verb ending; the whole word is わらう.
It is not made from an on-reading word ショウ plus a separate う.

## Synonyms and antonyms

微笑む (ほほえむ), "smile," specifies the smiling sense more directly.
笑顔 (えがお), "smiling face; smile," is a noun, not a conjugated form of 笑う.
泣く (なく), "cry; weep," is a useful contrasting action, though people can laugh and cry together.

## Distinctive meaning

笑う also has a transitive sense of ridiculing or laughing at someone or something.
An object with を can therefore change the interpretation:

- 人の失敗を笑わないでください。 (ひとのしっぱいをわらわないでください。) — Please do not laugh at other people's mistakes.

Do not assume every occurrence describes friendly amusement. Conversely, the basic "laugh" and "smile" senses do not require a target of ridicule.
