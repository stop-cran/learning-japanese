# レジ

## Meaning

レジ means a cash register, and also the checkout counter or cashier's position where payment is handled.

- レジでお金を払いました。 (レジで おかねを はらいました。) — I paid at the checkout.
- あちらのレジに並んでください。 (あちらの レジに ならんでください。) — Please line up at that checkout.

The first example uses で for the place of payment; the second uses に for the queue's destination.

## How the kanji combine

レジ is a shortened katakana form of レジスター, from "register." The shortened form is the ordinary useful shopping word, not a grammatical abbreviation that can be expanded with a Japanese suffix.

## Synonyms and antonyms

会計 (かいけい) can refer to settling the bill or payment. セルフレジ is a self-checkout. 店員 (てんいん) is a shop employee, not necessarily the cashier.

## Distinctive meaning

レジ does not mean registration in general. レジの人 means the person at the checkout, while レジが壊れた (レジがこわれた) usually refers to a broken register or checkout machine. Context selects the equipment, place, or staff role.
