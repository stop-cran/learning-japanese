# 女王 (じょおう) - queen

## Meaning

女王 means queen, particularly a female monarch. It is a noun and can also describe a leading woman or female champion in a field.

- 女王が式に出席しました。 (じょおうがしきにしゅっせきしました。) - The queen attended the ceremony.
- 彼女はテニス界の女王と呼ばれています。 (かのじょはテニスかいのじょおうとよばれています。) - She is called the queen of the tennis world.

The second example is an evaluative title, not a statement that she holds royal office.

## How the kanji combine

[女](../kanji/女.md) contributes female, and [王](../kanji/王.md) contributes monarch or ruler. The on readings are ジョ + オウ: write the teaching reading じょおう, not じょうおう. JMdict records the latter as an irregular kana form; that does not change this card's primary reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[王](王.md) (おう) and [王様](王様.md) (おうさま) concern a king or ruler. 王女 (おうじょ), with the characters reversed, means princess and is not another spelling of 女王.

## Distinctive meaning

English "queen" can mean a reigning monarch or a king's consort. JMdict recognizes both for 女王; 王妃 (おうひ) makes the consort role explicit. Avoid assuming every 女王 rules independently, while keeping the distinction from 王女 and from the metaphorical sporting title clear.
