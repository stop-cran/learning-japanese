# 質 (しつ) - quality; inherent nature

## Meaning

質 is a noun for quality or the inherent nature of something. Use Nの質 to identify what is being evaluated. 質が良い (しつがよい) means "be of good quality"; 質を高める (しつをたかめる) means "improve the quality."

- この店は商品の質が良いです。 (このみせはしょうひんのしつがよいです。) - The goods at this shop are of good quality.
- この二つの問題は質が違います。 (このふたつのもんだいはしつがちがいます。) - These two problems differ in nature.

The second example distinguishes what kind of problems they are, rather than judging which problem is better.

## How the kanji combine

[質](../kanji/質.md) is a single kanji read しつ here. It supplies the whole noun's quality or nature meaning. There is no second written component to combine.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[量](量.md) (りょう), "quantity," is a useful contrast: 質 asks what something is like, while 量 asks how much there is. They are different dimensions, not positive and negative evaluations of the same dimension.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not treat every occurrence as "high quality." 質 itself can be good, bad, high, or low, and it can neutrally identify a thing's character. 商品の質が悪い still uses 質 naturally; the negative evaluation comes from 悪い, not from a different noun.
