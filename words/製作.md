# 製作 (せいさく) - manufacture; production

## Meaning

製作 is making or producing something, including manufactured items.
It is a noun and forms the transitive verb 製作する.
The dictionary also explicitly records production of films, plays,
and television programs.

- 工房で家具を製作しています。 (こうぼうでかぐをせいさくしています。) - We make furniture in the workshop.
- この映画は日本で製作されました。 (このえいがはにほんでせいさくされました。) - This film was produced in Japan.

The second example is passive: 製作された identifies something that
was produced. It is not evidence that the word is limited to factory goods.

## How the kanji combine

製 contributes manufacture or making, and [作](../kanji/作.md) making
or producing. Their related meanings reinforce the production idea.
The on readings are セイ + サク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[制作](制作.md), also せいさく, often emphasizes creating artistic
works. The two overlap, particularly in film and media.
[製造](製造.md) and [生産](生産.md) concern manufacturing and production;
[作製](作製.md) also means manufacture or production.
[作る](作る.md) is a broader verb for making.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not memorize an absolute "製作 = industry, 制作 = art" partition:
映画製作 is an ordinary recorded use. A particular organization may
distinguish production roles in its credits, but these spellings alone
do not impose one universal funding-versus-creative-work rule.
製作 names making or production, not automatically the resulting
[製品](製品.md), manufactured product.
