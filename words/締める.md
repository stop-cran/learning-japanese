# 締める (しめる) -- fasten; tighten

## Meaning

締める means to fasten, tie, or tighten something. It is also used for putting on a belt or necktie.

- シートベルトを締めてください。 (シートベルトをしめてください。) -- Please fasten your seat belt.
- 今日はネクタイを締めています。 (きょうはネクタイをしめています。) -- I am wearing a tie today.

The item takes を. This is an ichidan verb: 締めます (しめます), 締めない (しめない), and 締めて (しめて). Remove final る to form the stem 締め-.

## How the kanji combine

締 supplies し in the written word, and める is okurigana. The kanji suggests fastening or drawing tight. Notice that the grammatical stem includes め even though that sound is written in kana.

## Synonyms and antonyms

結ぶ (むすぶ) emphasizes tying or making a knot. 緩める (ゆるめる), "loosen," is a useful opposite for tightening.

## Distinctive meaning

[閉める](閉める.md) has the same reading but normally concerns closing a door, window, or opening. Use 締める for fastening a belt; do not choose the spelling merely from the sound しめる. Tightening and closing can overlap physically without making these spellings universally interchangeable.
