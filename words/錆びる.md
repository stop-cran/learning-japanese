# 錆びる (さびる) — rust; become rusty

## Meaning

錆びる means to rust or become rusty. It is intransitive: the metal object is marked with が, rather than being something を that 錆びる acts on.

雨で自転車のチェーンが錆びた。（あめでじてんしゃのチェーンがさびた。） — The bicycle chain rusted in the rain.

このはさみは錆びている。（このはさみはさびている。） — These scissors are rusty.

## How the kanji combine

錆 carries the rust meaning in the native verb さびる. Learn the whole written form 錆びる, and compare the noun [錆](錆.md) さび. The written ending is びる; do not add that whole ending to the noun pronunciation and produce さびびる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[腐る](腐る.md) くさる includes rot/spoil and broader corrosion or deterioration uses. 錆びる specifically names rusting in the physical examples here. [古い](古い.md) ふるい merely says old; an old object need not be rusty.

## Distinctive meaning

This is an ichidan verb: 錆びます, 錆びない, 錆びた. 錆びている commonly describes the resulting rusty condition, not necessarily an observable change happening at that instant. To remove rust, use 錆を落とす（さびをおとす）, rather than turning 錆びる into a transitive verb.
