# 英文 (えいぶん) - English sentence; English text

## Meaning

英文 is writing in English: an English sentence, passage or text.
The word does not itself specify that there is exactly one sentence.

- 次の英文を日本語に訳してください。 (つぎのえいぶんをにほんごにやくしてください。) - Please translate the following English text into Japanese.
- この英文の意味が分かりません。 (このえいぶんのいみがわかりません。) - I do not understand the meaning of this English sentence.

英文で means "in written English," as in 英文で説明する
(えいぶんでせつめいする), "explain in English in writing."

## How the kanji combine

[英](../kanji/英.md) specifies English, modifying [文](../kanji/文.md),
writing or a sentence. This is modifier + head, with the on readings
エイ + ブン. 英 does not mean "excellent" in this compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[文](文.md) (ぶん) is a sentence or text without specifying its language.
[文章](文章.md) (ぶんしょう) and [テキスト](テキスト.md) can likewise
refer to written material. [英語](英語.md) (えいご) names the English
language, including spoken English, rather than specifically a written text.

## Distinctive meaning

The subject matter need not be England or an English-speaking country.
A paragraph about Japan can be 英文 if it is written in English.
Distinguish the language of the writing from the topic being discussed.
