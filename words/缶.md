# 缶 (かん) - can; tin

## Meaning

缶 is a noun for a can or tin: a metal container for drinks, food, or other goods.
It can also count cans. 一缶 (いっかん) means "one can," while 空き缶 (あきかん) is an empty can.

- 空き缶をここに捨てないでください。 (あきかんをここにすてないでください。) - Please do not discard empty cans here.
- 缶のふたを開けました。 (かんのふたをあけました。) - I opened the lid of the can.

缶のふた uses の to connect the container with its lid. Whether the contents are food or drink comes from context.

## How the kanji combine

This single-character spelling represents かん. JMdict marks the written form as ateji and records Dutch
*kan* and English *can* as loan sources. A kanji spelling therefore does not make this particular container word
a native Japanese compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[瓶](瓶.md) (びん) is a bottle or jar, often glass; [箱](箱.md) (はこ) is a box.
These are alternative containers rather than antonyms.
缶詰 (かんづめ) refers to canned food, not simply any empty metal container.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish the container from its contents: an empty can is still a 缶.
The dictionary also records 缶 as an abbreviation for canned food, so expressions about buying or eating
something may use the container word to identify the packaged product. Do not extend that abbreviated food sense
to every occurrence.
