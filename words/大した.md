# 大した (たいした) - remarkable; considerable

## Meaning

大した goes directly before a noun and describes something remarkable, considerable,
or significant. Do not insert な. It is useful both for praise and in negative
expressions that downplay importance or seriousness.

- 一人で全部作ったんですか。大したものですね。
  (ひとりでぜんぶつくったんですか。たいしたものですね。)
  - You made all of it yourself? That is impressive.
- 大したけがではありません。
  (たいしたけがではありません。) - It is not a serious injury.

大したことはない (たいしたことはない) means "it is no big deal" or "it is nothing
remarkable." The negative belongs to the whole statement; 大した itself has not been conjugated.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md) contributes greatness or magnitude and uses the on reading タイ.
The kana した complete this established expression. This is not a two-kanji compound;
the on-first classification does not mean every part is an on reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

すごい can also express admiration. [大変](大変.md) (たいへん) has different grammar
and can describe difficulty or a serious situation.
The positive 大したものだ contrasts with 大したことはない in evaluation.

## Distinctive meaning

The final た does not make 大した a past-tense description of the following noun.
It can describe a present achievement or an injury now being assessed.
Praise can become ironic in a sarcastic context, so tone matters as well as the phrase itself.
