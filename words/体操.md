# 体操 (たいそう) - gymnastics; physical exercises

## Meaning

体操 means physical exercises or gymnastics. It can describe a routine
for moving and stretching the body, not only competitive gymnastics.
体操をする and 体操する both describe doing the exercises.

- 毎朝、ラジオ体操をします。
  (まいあさ、ラジオたいそうをします。) - I do radio exercises every morning.
- 体操の前に体を温めましょう。
  (たいそうのまえにからだをあたためましょう。) - Let's warm up before the exercises.

In sports contexts, 体操 can also abbreviate 体操競技 (たいそうきょうぎ),
artistic gymnastics. The surrounding context distinguishes that discipline
from a general exercise routine.

## How the kanji combine

[体](../kanji/体.md) contributes body; 操 concerns handling or operating.
The on readings タイ + ソウ form the established exercise word.
This association helps remember the meaning, but is not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[運動](運動.md) (うんどう) covers physical exercise or activity more broadly.
[体育](体育.md) (たいいく) names physical education.
[スポーツ](スポーツ.md) and [競技](競技.md) (きょうぎ) identify sports and
competitive events or disciplines. Their broad English labels can overlap
with the exercise or gymnastics uses, without making every PE lesson a
体操 routine.

## Distinctive meaning

ラジオ体操 is a set of exercises, not necessarily a contest.
Conversely, a reference to Olympic 体操 concerns the sporting discipline.
Do not translate every exercise as gymnastics or assume every 体操 involves
apparatus. JMdict's old school-subject sense is obsolete; it is not a reason
to replace modern 体育 with 体操 in every school context.
