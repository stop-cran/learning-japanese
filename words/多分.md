# 多分 (たぶん)

## Meaning

多分 means probably, likely, or perhaps. It presents an expectation rather than a guaranteed fact.

- 明日は多分雨でしょう。 (あしたは たぶん あめでしょう。) — It will probably rain tomorrow.
- 田中さんは多分来ません。 (たなかさんは たぶん きません。) — Mr. Tanaka probably will not come.

It is an adverb in this use. It can modify affirmative or negative predictions; the negative example predicts nonattendance, not certainty about attendance.

## How the kanji combine

[多](../kanji/多.md) means much or many, and [分](../kanji/分.md) concerns a part or portion. The on readings are た + ぶん. Learn "probably" as the established whole-word meaning, not as a literal mathematical proportion.

## Synonyms and antonyms

きっと usually conveys stronger confidence. [必ず](必ず.md) emphasizes certainty or something happening without fail. These expressions do not represent fixed numerical probabilities.

## Distinctive meaning

Kana たぶん is also common. 多分 does not mean "maybe yes, maybe no" with precisely equal odds, nor does it promise the prediction will come true. A following でしょう or と思います can make the conjectural framing explicit.
