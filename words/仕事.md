# 仕事 (しごと) — work; job

## Meaning

仕事 is work, a job, or a task. It is a noun; 仕事をする (しごとをする) and 仕事する (しごとする) mean "to work."

- 毎日、九時から仕事をします。 (まいにち、くじからしごとをします。) — I work from nine every day.
- 今日は仕事がありません。 (きょうはしごとがありません。) — I have no work today.

## How the kanji combine

The spelling connects [仕](../kanji/仕.md) (serving; doing) with [事](../kanji/事.md) (a matter or task).
Do not analyze しごと as two on readings: ごと is the voiced form of the native reading こと.
For the app's available categories, this is stored as `kango`, the on-first mixed-reading category:
シ + こと becomes しごと. That is a guide to the written reading, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

作業 (さぎょう) focuses on an operation or task being carried out; it does not always mean a person's occupation.
[用事](用事.md) (ようじ) is something to do, an errand, or an engagement, not necessarily paid work.
A [休日](休日.md) (きゅうじつ) is a day off, a situational contrast rather than a direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

仕事 is the broad everyday choice for work and employment, but it also covers individual tasks.
仕事がある can mean having work to do; it need not be a statement about having a permanent job.
