# 骨 (ほね) - bone; supporting frame

## Meaning

骨 is a noun for a bone. It can also describe the supporting frame of an object. **骨が折れる** (ほねがおれる) describes a bone breaking; **骨を折る** (ほねをおる) can describe someone breaking a bone.

- 魚の骨に気をつけてください。 (さかなのほねにきをつけてください。) - Please watch out for fish bones.
- 強い風で傘の骨が折れました。 (つよいかぜでかさのほねがおれました。) - A rib of the umbrella broke in the strong wind.

The second example uses the frame sense: the umbrella has structural ribs, not animal bones.

## How the kanji combine

骨 is one kanji carrying the native reading ほね. The complete character names the supporting body structure; no multi-kanji relationship needs to be explained. The same reading remains in 傘の骨.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[肉](肉.md) (にく) is flesh or meat, a different bodily material rather than an antonym of bone. 骨格 (こっかく) names a skeleton or framework, treating the supporting structure as a whole.

## Distinctive meaning

Context matters in expressions with 折る and 折れる. 骨が折れる can also be an idiom meaning "be laborious; take a lot of effort," without an injury. A statement about an umbrella's ribs, however, can be entirely literal. Do not assume every occurrence of 骨 describes a medical problem.
