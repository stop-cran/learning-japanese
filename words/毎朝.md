# 毎朝 (まいあさ) — every morning

## Meaning

毎朝 means every morning. It describes a repeated routine and normally appears directly before the rest of the sentence without に.

- 毎朝、六時に起きます。 (まいあさ、ろくじにおきます。) — I get up at six every morning.
- 毎朝の散歩を楽しみにしています。 (まいあさのさんぽをたのしみにしています。) — I look forward to my daily morning walk.

Use 毎朝の + noun when describing something associated with each morning.

## How the kanji combine

[毎](../kanji/毎.md), "every," uses the on reading マイ; [朝](../kanji/朝.md), "morning," uses native あさ. This is an on-first mixed compound, classified as kango here rather than implying that both readings are on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

毎晩 (まいばん) means "every evening; every night." 今朝 (けさ) means "this morning," not a repeated morning routine.

## Distinctive meaning

The long-term habit comes from 毎, not from the verb tense alone. 朝、走ります (あさ、はしります) says "I run in the morning"; 毎朝走ります explicitly says it happens every morning. This card uses the everyday reading まいあさ.
