# 踏切 (ふみきり) - railway crossing

## Meaning

踏切 is a railway crossing: a place where a road crosses railway tracks at the same level.
It is a noun. 踏切を渡る (ふみきりをわたる) means to cross the tracks at that crossing.

- 踏切の前で車を止めました。 (ふみきりのまえでくるまをとめました。) - I stopped the car before the railway crossing.
- 電車が通るので、踏切で待っています。 (でんしゃがとおるので、ふみきりでまっています。) - We are waiting at the crossing because a train is passing.

The pinned dictionary also records a starting line or scratch in athletics,
determination, and stepping outside the ring in sumo. The public Japanese
dictionary additionally explains the athletic takeoff action or place.
These are context-dependent uses, not different names for a road junction.

## How the kanji combine

踏 contributes stepping, and [切](../kanji/切.md) cutting or completing.
The native forms ふみ and きり combine as ふみきり.
Their associations help recognize the word, but do not by themselves derive every sense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[交差点](交差点.md) (こうさてん) is an intersection, especially where roads meet.
[横断](横断.md) (おうだん) concerns crossing from one side to another;
横断歩道 (おうだんほどう) is a pedestrian crossing.
Neither expression specifically identifies a railway crossing.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not infer that every 踏切 has a particular barrier or signal arrangement.
The railway-road crossing is the central scene here.
The determination use overlaps with [決心](決心.md) (けっしん);
it does not make every decision a railway expression.
