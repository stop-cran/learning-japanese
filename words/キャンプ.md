# キャンプ - camping; camp

## Meaning

キャンプ is a camp or camping. It is a noun and forms an intransitive する verb: キャンプする, "camp." Use a place with で to say where you camp, and キャンプに行く to say "go camping."

- 週末に湖の近くでキャンプをしました。 (しゅうまつにみずうみのちかくでキャンプをしました。) - We camped near the lake over the weekend.
- 友達とキャンプに行く予定です。 (ともだちとキャンプにいくよていです。) - I plan to go camping with friends.

キャンプをする and キャンプする both express the activity. The を in the first form connects the activity noun to する.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword with no written kanji to analyze. The small ャ makes キャ one combined sound; do not read it as separate キ and ヤ. キャンプ場 (キャンプじょう) is a campsite.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) is travel or a trip, which need not involve camping. [泊まる](泊まる.md) (とまる) means staying overnight and can apply to a hotel or a friend's house as well.

## Distinctive meaning

[テント](テント.md) names a tent, whereas キャンプ names the activity or camp. A sentence about going camping does not identify all the equipment, facilities, or length of stay. Add those details rather than treating the noun as a complete description of the trip.
