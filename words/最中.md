# 最中 (さいちゅう) - in the middle of an activity

## Meaning

最中 is a noun meaning the midst or height of something in progress.
Use activity noun + の最中 or verb in the ている form + 最中.
最中に locates another event during that activity; 最中だ states that it is underway.

- 会議の最中に電話が鳴りました。
  (かいぎのさいちゅうにでんわがなりました。) - The phone rang in the middle of the meeting.
- 今、夕食を作っている最中です。
  (いま、ゆうしょくをつくっているさいちゅうです。) - I am right in the middle of making dinner.

The past 作っている最中でした describes being in the middle of cooking at a past time.
The ongoing activity remains in the ている form before 最中.

## How the kanji combine

[最](../kanji/最.md) contributes "utmost," and [中](../kanji/中.md) contributes "middle."
Together they emphasize being right in the midst of something.
The on readings are サイ + チュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[途中](途中.md) (とちゅう) can locate a point partway through an activity or journey.
[間](間.md) (あいだ) describes an interval.
最中 especially foregrounds an activity as currently underway, without measuring its exact halfway point.

## Distinctive meaning

An interruption is a useful context for 最中に, but is not required, as the cooking
example shows. The dictionary also lists さなか for this meaning; this card teaches
さいちゅう. The spelling さ中 belongs to さなか, not to さいちゅう.
