# 定期 (ていき) - fixed term; regular schedule; commuter pass

## Meaning

定期 concerns a fixed period or term, or something occurring on a regular schedule.
定期の modifies a noun; 定期的に (ていきてきに) means "regularly; periodically."
It also occurs directly in compounds, such as 定期検査 (ていきけんさ), "periodic inspection."

- この機械は定期の検査が必要です。
  (このきかいはていきのけんさがひつようです。)
  - This machine requires periodic inspections.
- 電車の定期を更新しました。
  (でんしゃのていきをこうしんしました。) - I renewed my train commuter pass.

In transport, 定期 abbreviates a fixed-term commuter pass, also called 定期券 (ていきけん).
In banking, 定期預金 (ていきよきん) is a fixed-term deposit and may be shortened to 定期.

## How the kanji combine

[定](../kanji/定.md) contributes fixing or setting, and [期](../kanji/期.md) contributes
a period or time. Together they concern timing that is set.
The on readings are テイ + キ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[普通](普通.md) (ふつう), "ordinary," does not mean "regularly scheduled" in every
context where English says "regular." 不定期 (ふていき) means irregular or without a fixed schedule.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish a fixed duration from repeated timing. A deposit has a specified term;
inspections recur at scheduled intervals. The commuter-pass meaning is an abbreviation,
not a rule that any object used regularly can be called a 定期.
Context resolves the shortened noun.
