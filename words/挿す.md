# 挿す (さす) - insert; plant a cutting

## Meaning

挿す means to insert something, especially a slender object into another object.
It is a transitive godan verb: the object takes を, and the destination takes に.

- 花瓶に花を挿しました。 (かびんにはなをさしました。) - I put flowers into the vase.
- 土に菊の枝を挿しました。 (つちにきくのえだをさしました。) - I planted a chrysanthemum cutting in the soil.

The second use is planting a cutting so that it can root.
The source's English "graft" should not obscure this: it does not necessarily mean joining a branch onto another plant.
Forms include 挿します, 挿して, 挿した, and 挿さない.

## How the kanji combine

挿 contributes insertion and supplies the native さ; す is the verb ending.
Learn the spelling with the construction. The character's on reading ソウ belongs to other words.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[入れる](入れる.md) (いれる) is the broader "put in."
[植える](植える.md) (うえる) means plant, without specifying propagation from a cutting.
The first example also illustrates the flower-arranging use of 挿す;
[生け花](生け花.md) (いけばな) names Japanese flower arrangement as an art or an arrangement.

## Distinctive meaning

刀を腰に挿す (かたなをこしにさす) means "wear a sword in one's belt."
It is not a verb for wearing any clothing: compare [着る](着る.md) (きる).
The dictionary also records shutting, locking, or fastening.
For an ordinary door-closing request, learn [閉める](閉める.md) (しめる) rather than generalizing every insertion example to closing.

[差す](差す.md), also さす, has dictionary-recorded insertion and sword-wearing overlaps,
but its umbrella use does not make 傘を挿す the construction to learn.
[刺す](刺す.md) foregrounds piercing; [指す](指す.md) means point or indicate.
