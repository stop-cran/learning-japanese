# 段階 (だんかい) - stage; graded level

## Meaning

段階 is a stage or phase in a process, or a level distinguished on a scale.
Use **Nの段階**, "the stage of N," and 段階に進む (だんかいにすすむ),
"move to a stage."

- 計画はまだ準備の段階です。
  (けいかくはまだじゅんびのだんかいです。) - The plan is still at the preparation stage.
- 難しさを三つの段階に分けました。
  (むずかしさをみっつのだんかいにわけました。) - We divided the difficulty into three levels.

段階を追って説明する (だんかいをおってせつめいする) means
"explain step by step." The noun can concern an ordered progression,
not only a label for the final result.

## How the kanji combine

[段](../kanji/段.md) contributes a step or grade and
[階](../kanji/階.md) a stair or level. Their related meanings reinforce
successive or graded stages. The on readings are ダン + カイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ステージ](ステージ.md) also has a phase meaning.
[水準](水準.md) (すいじゅん) concerns a level or standard;
段階 can instead distinguish successive phases.
[過程](過程.md) (かてい) is the course of development, within which
several 段階 may be distinguished. These are useful perspectives, not
absolute divisions between unrelated meanings.

## Distinctive meaning

段階 need not be a numbered rank. 段階的に (だんかいてきに) means
"in stages; step by step."
Dictionaries also record a stairs sense for だんかい; for the everyday
staircase, learn [階段](階段.md) (かいだん).
Keep the reversed character order and different readings distinct.
