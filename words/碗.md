# 碗 (わん) - bowl (often ceramic); bowlful counter

## Meaning

碗 is a bowl, associated especially with ceramic or porcelain vessels.
It also counts bowls of food or drink.

- 茶碗にご飯をよそいました。 (ちゃわんにごはんをよそいました。) - I served rice into a bowl.
- ご飯を三杯食べました。 (ごはんをさんばいたべました。) - I ate three bowls of rice.

The compound 茶碗 (ちゃわん), represented by the existing
[ちゃわん](ちゃわん.md) article, is useful for a rice bowl or tea vessel.
For servings, 杯 (はい) counts cupfuls or bowlfuls; 三杯 is さんばい.
The わん bowlful counter is also worth recognizing. Under its combined
椀/碗 heading, Daijisen illustrates it with 一椀の吸い物, "one bowlful of
clear soup." 吸い物 (すいもの) is clear soup. Neither counter means
that someone eats an empty container.

## How the kanji combine

碗 is one character with the on reading ワン.
Its 石 component helps associate this spelling with ceramic vessels;
that association is not a complete etymology or a compulsory material test.
The rare 盌 is another dictionary spelling; 埦 is search-only.
There is currently no 碗 kanji card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[椀](椀.md), also わん, is especially associated with wooden or lacquered bowls.
JMdict separates the material-qualified entries, but Digital Daijisen groups
椀 and 碗 together. The spellings and their bowl-counter uses overlap.

[丼](丼.md) (どんぶり) is a deep bowl or a rice-bowl dish;
[鉢](鉢.md) (はち) includes bowls and plant pots.
[ボール](ボール.md) has a separate bowl identity as well as the unrelated ball word.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the setting and established vessel name, not material alone, when choosing
between bowl words. A 碗 is not every cooking pot or flat plate.
The geographic homophone [湾](湾.md) (わん) means a bay, not tableware.
The same pronunciation does not make it an alternate bowl spelling.
