# 生物 (せいぶつ) - living thing; organism; biology

## Meaning

生物 (せいぶつ) is a noun for a living thing or organism. It is also an abbreviation of 生物学 (せいぶつがく), "biology," particularly when naming the subject studied.

- この池には多くの生物がいます。 (このいけにはおおくのせいぶつがいます。) - Many living things inhabit this pond.
- 高校で生物を勉強しました。 (こうこうでせいぶつをべんきょうしました。) - I studied biology in high school.

The second sentence names a school subject, not a particular creature being studied at that moment.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md), "life; living," qualifies [物](../kanji/物.md), "thing." セイ + ブツ gives せいぶつ. The whole compound names living things; it is not restricted to animals.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[生き物](生き物.md) (いきもの) also means a living thing and is useful in everyday description. 生物 fits scientific discussion as well. [物理](物理.md) (ぶつり), "physics," is another science subject, not an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

The same spelling has a separate reading and identity: **生物 (なまもの)**, also written 生もの, means raw food or perishables. It is not the food meaning of せいぶつ. Compare 生物の授業 (せいぶつのじゅぎょう), "biology class," with 生ものを冷蔵庫に入れる (なまものをれいぞうこにいれる), "put perishables in the refrigerator." Reading and context distinguish the words.
