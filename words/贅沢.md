# 贅沢 (ぜいたく) - luxury; extravagance

## Meaning

贅沢 is a noun and な-adjective for luxury or extravagance. 贅沢する means to indulge oneself; 贅沢な + noun describes something luxurious or beyond basic needs.

- 今日は少し贅沢して、高いレストランで食べました。 (きょうはすこしぜいたくして、たかいレストランでたべました。) - Today I treated myself a little and ate at an expensive restaurant.
- 一人で静かに過ごす時間は、私には贅沢です。 (ひとりでしずかにすごすじかんは、わたしにはぜいたくです。) - Time spent quietly alone is a luxury for me.

The second example shows that the luxury need not be a purchased object.

## How the kanji combine

贅 contributes excess or extravagance; 沢 has abundance or richness associations. Together they reinforce the idea of lavishness. Learn the compound's readings ゼイ + タク rather than using the native reading さわ of 沢.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[豪華](豪華.md) (ごうか), "luxurious; splendid," emphasizes impressive richness or appearance. 贅沢 can focus on the indulgence relative to someone's ordinary needs.

[節約](節約.md) (せつやく), "economizing," offers a practical contrast. [無駄](無駄.md) (むだ), "waste," is a negative judgment, not an automatic synonym.

## Distinctive meaning

贅沢 can express enjoyable indulgence or criticism, depending on the speaker. 贅沢を言う (ぜいたくをいう), "make excessive demands; be too choosy," concerns expectations rather than owning expensive things. Do not assume that every use praises wealth or condemns spending.
