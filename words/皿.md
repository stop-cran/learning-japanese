# 皿 (さら) - plate; dish

## Meaning

皿 is a noun for a plate or dish. With a number it can also count
platefuls or servings of food: 一皿 (ひとさら), "one plateful."
The polite everyday form お皿 (おさら) refers to the same object.

- 使った皿を洗ってください。 (つかったさらをあらってください。) - Please wash the plates you used.
- サラダを一皿注文しました。 (サラダをひとさらちゅうもんしました。) - I ordered one plate of salad.

In the second example, 一皿 counts a serving, not a purchase of an
empty piece of tableware. Physical plates can be counted with 枚
(まい), the counter for flat objects.

## How the kanji combine

皿 is a single character read with the native reading さら.
It supplies the whole noun, with no multi-kanji relationship to divide.
The prefix お in [お皿](お皿.md) adds politeness, not another kind
of dish.

## Synonyms and antonyms

茶碗 (ちゃわん) is a bowl, such as a rice bowl or tea bowl.
[料理](料理.md) (りょうり) can mean a prepared dish of food,
whereas 皿 can name the container. English "dish" therefore needs
different Japanese words in different contexts.

## Distinctive meaning

Read the surrounding action: washing a 皿 concerns tableware, while
ordering 一皿 concerns a serving. The serving use extends from the
container and does not turn 皿 into a general word for cooking.
