# 毎年 (まいとし／まいねん) — every year

## Meaning

毎年 means "every year, annually": 毎年京都へ行きます (I go to Kyoto every year), 毎年夏に (every summer), 毎年恒例 まいとしこうれい (an annual tradition).

## How the kanji combine

[毎](../kanji/毎.md) (every) plus [年](../kanji/年.md) (year). Both readings are used: まいとし (an on + kun mix, with the kun reading とし) is the usual everyday one, and まいねん (with the on reading ネン) is also correct and common. Both are listed in dictionaries.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 毎年 vs 年々 としどし／ねんねん (year by year, with a sense of change over time) and 例年 れいねん (a typical year, as in usual).
- Same pattern: [毎日](毎日.md) まいにち, [毎月](毎月.md) まいつき, 毎週 まいしゅう.

## Distinctive meaning

毎年 states regular repetition. 年々 stresses gradual change (年々暑くなる, "it gets hotter year after year").
