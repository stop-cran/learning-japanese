# 宵 (よい) — evening just after nightfall

## Meaning

宵 is the early part of the night, after the day has grown dark. 宵の口 (よいのくち) makes the idea of the evening still being young especially clear.

- 夏の宵、庭で風の音を聞いた。 (なつのよい、にわでかぜのおとをきいた。) — Early one summer evening I listened to the wind in the garden.
- もう帰るの。まだ宵の口だよ。 (もうかえるの。まだよいのくちだよ。) — Going home already? The evening is still young.

## How the kanji combine

[宵](../kanji/宵.md) writes the whole native noun よい. The の in 夏の宵 links the season to the time of day; it is not part of the reading of 宵.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[夕方](夕方.md) (ゆうがた) points to the latter part of the day around sunset. 宵 looks at the start of night. [夜中](夜中.md) (よなか) is the middle of the night, not another name for 宵.

## Distinctive meaning

宵 does not by itself tell you *which* night is meant. 今宵 (こよい) means “this evening” or “tonight”; it is a poetic way of referring to it.
