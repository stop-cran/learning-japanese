# お宅 (おたく) - your home, respectfully

## Meaning

お宅 respectfully refers to another person's home or household. It is useful when discussing a visit.

- 明日、お宅に伺ってもいいですか。 (あした、おたくにうかがってもいいですか。) - May I visit your home tomorrow?

The destination takes に. For your own home, use 家 (いえ), うち, or 自宅 (じたく), rather than adding honor to yourself with this use of お宅.

## How the kanji combine

The polite prefix お precedes [宅](../kanji/宅.md), read たく and associated with a dwelling. The core is Sino-Japanese, with the Japanese prefix attached.

## Synonyms and antonyms

家 is the ordinary word for a house or home. ご自宅 (ごじたく) is another respectful expression for someone's home. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

お宅 can also refer to the listener or the listener's organization. That pronoun-like use is not a universally polite way to address anyone. The familiar hobby-fan label オタク is a different lexical use; do not apply it when translating this home expression.
