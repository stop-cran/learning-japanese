# 潜る (もぐる) - dive; get underneath; hide

## Meaning

潜る, read もぐる here, is an intransitive godan verb for diving
into or under water. It also means getting into or underneath something,
and hiding oneself or going underground.

- 海に潜って魚を見ました。 (うみにもぐってさかなをみました。) - I dived into the sea and saw fish.
- 猫が布団に潜っています。 (ねこがふとんにもぐっています。) - The cat is tucked under the bedding.
- 追われて地下に潜った。 (おわれてちかにもぐった。) - Being pursued, the person went underground.

Forms include 潜ります (もぐります), 潜って (もぐって), and
潜らない (もぐらない).

## How the kanji combine

潜 writes the native verb base, followed by る. The written form also
has a separate くぐる entry for passing under or through, diving,
evading, or getting through danger. Do not merge its reading and full
sense list into the selected もぐる identity. むぐる is an obsolete
reading recorded in the もぐる family.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[飛び込む](飛び込む.md) (とびこむ) can mean diving or jumping in,
emphasizing entry; 潜る can concern being underwater.
[隠れる](隠れる.md) (かくれる) overlaps with hiding.
[通る](通る.md) (とおる), "pass through," helps distinguish the
other reading くぐる rather than defining every use of もぐる.

## Distinctive meaning

Getting under the bedding need not involve water. Likewise,
[地下](地下.md)に潜る (ちかにもぐる) can mean hiding from the authorities,
not moving into a literal basement.
