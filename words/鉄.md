# 鉄 (てつ) - iron

## Meaning

鉄 is iron, the metal and chemical element Fe. It is a noun. **鉄のN** can describe an iron object, while 鉄でできている means to be made of iron.

- この門は鉄でできています。 (このもんはてつでできています。) - This gate is made of iron.
- 鉄はさびることがあります。 (てつはさびることがあります。) - Iron can rust.

In ordinary descriptions, 鉄 can also refer to steel or iron-based material without a precise specification of composition. A technical context may require a more exact material name.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 鉄 uses the on reading テツ. There is no compound to split. The older written form 鐵 is recorded, but this card uses 鉄.

## Synonyms and antonyms

鋼 (はがね), "steel," is important when distinguishing steel from iron technically. [金](金.md) (きん), "gold," and [銀](銀.md) (ぎん), "silver," name other metals; they are not opposites of iron.

## Distinctive meaning

鉄 can also suggest firmness or strength: 鉄の意志 (てつのいし), "an iron will." This is figurative, not a claim about what a person's will is made from. Learn the material sense first, then let the following noun show when the expression is metaphorical.
