# 笹 (ささ) - bamboo grass

## Meaning

笹 names **bamboo grass**: generally smaller kinds of bamboo, including plants of the genus *Sasa*. It is not a name for every kind of grass.

- 庭に笹が生えています。 (にわにささがはえています。) - Bamboo grass is growing in the garden.
- 笹の葉で舟を作りました。 (ささのはでふねをつくりました。) - I made a boat from a bamboo leaf.

In the second example, で marks the material. Such a folded leaf boat is called 笹舟 (ささぶね).

## How the kanji combine

[笹](../kanji/笹.md) alone writes the native plant noun ささ; there is no compound to divide. Dictionaries also list the spellings 篠 and 小竹 for ささ, marked rare in the frozen JMdict family. Do not read those spellings by assigning a new independent reading to each component.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[竹](竹.md) (たけ), bamboo, overlaps with 笹 as a broader plant category. A useful botanical distinction concerns the sheaths around the stems: sasa generally retains them as the stems mature, unlike take. Size alone is not a universal identification rule.

[草](草.md) (くさ), grass or weeds, and [植物](植物.md) (しょくぶつ), plant or vegetation, are broader descriptions, not names of this particular bamboo group. There is no direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The name helps in descriptions of plants, leaves and leaf-wrapped foods. It does not make every word containing 笹 a plant noun: 笹身 (ささみ) means chicken tenderloin.

Older dictionaries also record ささ as a court-language word for [酒](酒.md) (さけ), alcoholic drink. Keep that historical sense separate from the plant noun; ordinary modern drink vocabulary is 酒 or [お酒](お酒.md) (おさけ). The quiz guards protect that documented secondary sense and broader plant labels, not unrestricted synonymy.
