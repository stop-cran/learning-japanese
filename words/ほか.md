# ほか - other; the rest

## Meaning

ほか means another person or thing, something else, or the rest. Use ほかの before a noun and ほかに for "else" in many questions.

- ほかの店に行きましょう。 (ほかのみせにいきましょう。) - Let's go to another shop.
- ほかに質問はありますか。 (ほかにしつもんはありますか。) - Are there any other questions?

The noun being contrasted may be understood from context.

## How the kanji combine

This page uses kana. [他](../kanji/他.md) is a common kanji spelling for the "other" sense; [外](../kanji/外.md) also occurs in the dictionary entry. These spellings do not mean every occurrence has the same nuance.

## Synonyms and antonyms

別の (べつの) also means "another; different" before a noun. 同じ (おなじ), "same," supplies the contrasting idea.

## Distinctive meaning

ほか is not an i-adjective: say ほかの人, not ほかい人. Noun + のほかに can mean "in addition to"; the full construction and sentence polarity determine whether an English "besides" or "except" interpretation is appropriate.

- 日本語のほかに、英語も勉強しています。 (にほんごのほかに、えいごもべんきょうしています。) - In addition to Japanese, I am studying English.
- ここには私のほかに誰もいません。 (ここにはわたしのほかにだれもいません。) - There is nobody here except me.

The first sentence adds an item; the negative 誰もいません in the second excludes everyone else.
The source spelling 他 read ほか refers to this same noun.
