# 国王 (こくおう) - king; national sovereign

## Meaning

国王 is a country's king or sovereign. It is a noun.
The pinned dictionary also includes queen in this monarch sense;
do not turn the English gloss "king" into a universal male-only rule.

- 国王が式に出席しました。 (こくおうがしきにしゅっせきしました。) - The king attended the ceremony.
- 国王は代表団と会いました。 (こくおうはだいひょうだんとあいました。) - The sovereign met the delegation.

These examples concern a country's monarch, not an informal champion
called the "king" of a sport.

## How the kanji combine

[国](../kanji/国.md) contributes country, and [王](../kanji/王.md)
contributes king or ruler. 国 qualifies 王: the country's monarch.
The on readings are コク + オウ; こくおう is not こっこう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[王](王.md) (おう) and [王様](王様.md) (おうさま) overlap in king
or ruler. [女王](女王.md) (じょおう) specifically identifies a queen.
君主 (くんしゅ) is monarch more broadly; official titles and their
translations depend on the country and context.

## Distinctive meaning

The title alone does not say whether the monarch governs directly
or has a constitutional or ceremonial role.
The dictionary separately records the legal Crown or throne sense,
but this card selects the personal-monarch sense.
Do not freely replace every country's established ruler title with 国王.
