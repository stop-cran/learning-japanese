# ふらふら — unsteady; wandering without thinking

## Meaning

ふらふら describes physical unsteadiness, wavering intentions, or aimless movement without much conscious direction.

- 熱があって、立つとふらふらする。 — I have a fever and feel unsteady when I stand up. (熱: ねつ.)
- 彼はまだ進路を決められず、気持ちがふらふらしている。 — He still cannot decide on his future path, and his resolve keeps wavering. (進路: しんろ.)
- 散歩中、ふらふらと本屋に入った。 — While out walking, I drifted into a bookshop without really thinking.

Common patterns include ふらふらする, ふらふらと歩く, and ふらふらの状態 (じょうたい), "an unsteady state."

## How the kanji combine

This repeated native kana mimetic evokes instability or drifting, rather than requiring an actual noise. It is not a kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぶらぶら](ぶらぶら.md) suggests dangling or leisurely idling; [うろうろ](うろうろ.md) often suggests restless wandering. [しっかり](しっかり.md) can contrast with physical or mental instability, depending on the phrase.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not diagnose a cause from ふらふら alone: illness, fatigue, or other conditions may produce the gait. Its figurative wavering and aimless-motion senses do not necessarily imply dizziness. Use the surrounding subject and verb to identify the intended meaning.
