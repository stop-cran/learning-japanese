# お出かけ (おでかけ) - outing; about to go out

## Meaning

お出かけ can name an outing or trip. It also occurs in the polite greeting お出かけですか, said when someone is about to go out.

- 週末のお出かけが楽しみです。 (しゅうまつのおでかけがたのしみです。) - I am looking forward to our weekend outing.
- A: お出かけですか。 (おでかけですか。) - Are you going out?
- B: はい、ちょっと買い物に。 (はい、ちょっとかいものに。) - Yes, just to do a little shopping.

The question can be a social greeting, not necessarily a request for a detailed itinerary.

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) concerns going out. お出掛け is another spelling of おでかけ, with [掛](../kanji/掛.md) as part of 出掛け. The related native verb is [出かける](出かける.md) (でかける); お adds a polite or familiar presentation of the outing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[外出](外出.md) (がいしゅつ) also names going out. [出発](出発.md) (しゅっぱつ) emphasizes departure, while [旅](旅.md) (たび) and [旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) can describe a trip or travel. The meanings overlap, but お出かけ need not involve a long journey.

## Distinctive meaning

Treat お出かけ as a noun or the noun-like part of the greeting, not as an ordinary finite form of 出かける. お出かけですか does not mean "Are you outside already?" in every situation; the dictionary explicitly recognizes its use to someone about to leave.
