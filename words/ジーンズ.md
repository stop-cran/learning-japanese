# ジーンズ (ジーンズ) - jeans

## Meaning

ジーンズ is a noun for jeans. Use ジーンズをはく for putting them on or wearing them,
and ジーンズをはいている for the state of having them on.
It can refer to one pair or more than one, depending on context.

- 今日はジーンズをはいています。 (きょうはジーンズをはいています。) - I am wearing jeans today.
- 新しいジーンズを一本買いました。 (あたらしいジーンズをいっぽんかいました。) - I bought a new pair of jeans.

一本 (いっぽん) counts one pair in this clothing context.
The dictionary also records a denim-fabric sense, but these examples concern the garment.

## How the kanji combine

The word is written in katakana as ジー + ン + ズ, with a long vowel after ジ.
There are no kanji to explain. Treat ジーンズ as a complete Japanese noun:
do not remove ズ to form a supposed Japanese singular.
Its grammatical behavior is not the same as an English plural noun.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ズボン](ズボン.md) is the broader category of trousers or pants; jeans are one kind.
Denim names the fabric, which can also be used for other garments.
Other clothing choices are contrasts, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

The object determines the appropriate wearing verb: はく suits jeans and other lower-body clothing.
The long-vowel mark is part of the spelling and pronunciation, not decoration.
When the number matters, add a counter expression rather than changing the noun itself,
as 一本 does in the second example.
