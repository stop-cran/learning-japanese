# 文献 (ぶんけん) - reference literature; written sources

## Meaning

文献 is written material consulted as a source: research literature, reference books,
papers or historical documents. It is a noun. The focus is its role as a source, not
whether it is an entertaining literary work.

- 論文を書く前に、関連する文献を調べました。 (ろんぶんをかくまえに、かんれんするぶんけんをしらべました。) - Before writing the paper, I examined the relevant literature.
- この文献には当時の生活が記録されています。 (このぶんけんにはとうじのせいかつがきろくされています。) - This written source records life at that time.

## How the kanji combine

[文](../kanji/文.md) supplies the writing association. 献 (ケン) has meanings such
as offering or presenting; it is not simply a standalone modern word for "document."
Learn 文献 as the established compound ブン + ケン. Associating it with written
material presented as evidence is a memory aid, not an etymological derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[資料](資料.md) (しりょう) is broader source material and can include non-written
material. [書籍](書籍.md) (しょせき), [書物](書物.md) (しょもつ) and [本](本.md)
(ほん) identify books; a book can serve as 文献 without all books being research sources.
[文学](文学.md) (ぶんがく) and [文芸](文芸.md) (ぶんげい) emphasize literature
as a field or art rather than the source function.

## Distinctive meaning

参考文献 (さんこうぶんけん) means references or the bibliography of consulted works.
文献を引用する (ぶんけんをいんようする) means cite a source.
The English "literature" here can mean published work on a research subject; it does not
require fiction, poetry or an artistic judgment. A document's source value depends on context.
