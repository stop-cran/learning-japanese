# 髪の毛 (かみのけ) - hair on the head

## Meaning

髪の毛 is a noun for hair on the head. Depending on context, it can mean someone's hair collectively or an individual strand. Use を for hair being washed or cut and が when describing its condition.

- 髪の毛がまだぬれています。 (かみのけがまだぬれています。) - My hair is still wet.
- 服に髪の毛が一本ついています。 (ふくにかみのけがいっぽんついています。) - There is a hair on your clothes.

一本 (いっぽん) counts one strand in the second example. Japanese need not add a plural ending when referring to all the hair on someone's head.

## How the kanji combine

[髪](../kanji/髪.md) refers to head hair, の links the nouns, and 毛 means hair or an individual hair. The native readings form かみ + の + け. The phrase makes the kind of hair explicit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[髪](髪.md) (かみ) also means head hair and overlaps closely: 髪を切る (かみをきる), "cut one's hair." [毛](毛.md) (け) is broader and can describe body hair or animal fur. Neither word is an antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

髪の毛 does not mean any hair anywhere on the body merely because the English translation is "hair." Its first component narrows the reference to the head. In descriptions of a single strand, surrounding words such as 一本 make the individual-hair interpretation clear.
