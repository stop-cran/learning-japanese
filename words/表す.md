# 表す (あらわす) - express; represent

## Meaning

表す means to express a feeling or idea, or to represent something through a sign, image, or other form. It is a transitive godan verb: 表します, 表さない, 表した. Mark the content with を and a means of expression with で.

- 感謝の気持ちを言葉で表しました。 (かんしゃのきもちをことばであらわしました。) - I expressed my gratitude in words.
- この記号は出口を表しています。 (このきごうはでぐちをあらわしています。) - This symbol represents an exit.

表している can describe what a symbol stands for, not just an action happening at this instant.

## How the kanji combine

[表](../kanji/表.md) contributes outward expression or representation. The kanji writes あらわ, and す is the inflecting ending. 表わす is another recorded spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[表現](表現.md) (ひょうげん) names expression; 表現する overlaps with expressing ideas or feelings. [示す](示す.md) (しめす) means to show or indicate and can overlap in descriptions of signs.

[隠す](隠す.md) (かくす), "conceal," is a contextual contrast, not an opposite for every use of representation.

## Distinctive meaning

[現す](現す.md), also あらわす, is favored for revealing or bringing something into view: 姿を現す (すがたをあらわす), "show oneself." JMdict groups these spellings in one entry but notes different preferences: 表す for expressing/representing, 現す for revealing/showing. These are spelling tendencies, not permission to substitute either spelling indiscriminately or absolute restrictions without exceptions.
