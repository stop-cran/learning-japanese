# 橋 (はし) - bridge

## Meaning

橋 is a bridge, a structure that lets people or vehicles cross a river, road, or other gap.

- この橋を渡ると、駅があります。 (このはしをわたると、えきがあります。) - Cross this bridge and you will find the station.

With 渡る (わたる), "cross," を marks the route crossed. 橋の上 (はしのうえ) means "on the bridge."

## How the kanji combine

橋 is a single character writing the native noun はし. In some compound names it becomes ばし; do not apply that voicing to the standalone word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

歩道橋 (ほどうきょう) is a pedestrian bridge or footbridge. 道 (みち), "road; path," names a route more generally. There is no direct antonym needed for this object.

## Distinctive meaning

箸, "chopsticks," and 端, "edge," can also be written はし in kana. They are different words. Kanji and context help distinguish them; identical kana spelling does not mean identical pitch accent.
