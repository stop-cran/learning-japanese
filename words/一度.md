# 一度 (いちど) — once

## Meaning

一度 means once or one time. It can directly modify a verb without に.

京都に一度行きました。（きょうとにいちどいきました。） — I went to Kyoto once.

もう一度言ってください。（もういちどいってください。） — Please say it one more time.

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) means one; [度](../kanji/度.md) counts occurrences here. Both use on readings, producing いちど.

## Synonyms and antonyms

一回（いっかい） also counts one occurrence. 二度（にど） means twice. 一度も followed by a negative means "not even once": 一度もない, "never; not once."

## Distinctive meaning

The surrounding words matter: もう一度 is "once again," while 一度だけ is "only once." 一度 can also mean one degree when measuring temperature or angles; that is a separate use of 度, not a frequency.
