# かなり (かなり) - considerably; quite

## Meaning

かなり expresses a substantial degree or amount: "considerably," "quite," or
"rather." As an adverb, it goes directly before a predicate. Before a noun,
かなりの is a useful pattern.

- この問題はかなり難しいです。 (このもんだいはかなりむずかしいです。) - This problem is quite difficult.
- 修理にはかなりの時間がかかります。 (しゅうりにはかなりのじかんがかかります。) - The repair will take a considerable amount of time.

It does not specify an exact measurement. What counts as かなり depends on the
speaker's expectations and the thing being assessed.

## How the kanji combine

This word is usually written in kana. JMdict also records rare kanji spellings
such as 可成り; they are not needed to understand the ordinary kana form.
There is no written kanji compound to divide on this card.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[相当](相当.md) (そうとう) can also indicate a considerable degree.
[ずいぶん](ずいぶん.md) can overlap while highlighting the speaker's reaction.
These comparisons are tendencies, not a scale assigning each word a fixed
percentage.

## Distinctive meaning

かなり is stronger than a mere small amount, but it need not mean "extremely."
English "fairly" may sound too weak in some contexts. It also works with
improvement: かなりよくなった means "got considerably better." The following
predicate, not かなり itself, determines whether the assessment is welcome.
