# 寒帯 (かんたい) - frigid climatic zone

## Meaning

寒帯 is a frigid climatic zone: a region classified by a very cold climate,
particularly toward the poles. It is a noun, not a description of
one unusually cold day.

- 授業で寒帯の気候を学びました。
  (じゅぎょうでかんたいのきこうをまなびました。) - We learned about the climate of the frigid zone in class.
- 寒帯に暮らす動物について調べています。
  (かんたいにくらすどうぶつについてしらべています。) - I am researching animals that live in the frigid zone.

寒帯の modifies a following noun; 寒帯に locates something in that zone.

## How the kanji combine

[寒](../kanji/寒.md) contributes cold, and
[帯](../kanji/帯.md) a belt or zone.
The first component describes the kind of zone, not a belt that someone wears.
The on readings カン + タイ give かんたい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[熱帯](熱帯.md) (ねったい), "tropical zone," and [温帯](温帯.md) (おんたい),
"temperate zone," provide useful climatic contrasts.
[寒い](寒い.md) (さむい), "cold," can describe today's weather
without classifying the region as 寒帯.

## Distinctive meaning

寒帯 is a climate-region label, not a synonym for winter or snow.
The word itself does not supply a particular classification system's
temperature thresholds or guarantee identical conditions everywhere
within the zone.
