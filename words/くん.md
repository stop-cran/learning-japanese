# くん (くん) - familiar name suffix

## Meaning

くん is a suffix attached directly to a person's name, especially for a boy or a male peer or junior, including adults. It is not a standalone noun meaning "boy."

- 太郎くんはもう帰りました。 (たろうくんはもうかえりました。) - Taro has already gone home.

It also follows family names: 山田くん (やまだくん). Do not insert の between the name and くん.

## How the kanji combine

This card uses kana. The kanji spelling is 君, with the on reading くん. The same character read きみ belongs to a separate word meaning "you"; that pronoun does not explain the name-suffix grammar.

## Synonyms and antonyms

さん is a more broadly usable polite name suffix. ちゃん is more affectionate or familiar. They express different relationships, not a ladder to use automatically according to age.

## Distinctive meaning

JMdict also records male speakers using くん for female juniors in formal settings, so the suffix is not exclusively male. Such usage depends on local convention. When unsure of the relationship, さん is generally safer; do not assume every younger person should be addressed with くん.
