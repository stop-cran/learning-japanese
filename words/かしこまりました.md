# かしこまりました - certainly; understood

## Meaning

かしこまりました is a respectful, polite response accepting a request or instructions. It is useful in service or formal exchanges with a customer, superior, or someone whose request the speaker is respectfully undertaking.

- 「窓側の席をお願いします。」「かしこまりました。」
  (「まどがわのせきをおねがいします。」「かしこまりました。」) - "A window seat, please." "Certainly."
- 「資料を明日までに送ってください。」「かしこまりました。」
  (「しりょうをあしたまでにおくってください。」「かしこまりました。」) - "Please send the materials by tomorrow." "Understood."

The response acknowledges what the speaker is to do; it does not say the task has already been completed.

## How the kanji combine

This expression is usually written in kana. The dictionary also records 畏まりました. It is the polite past form of かしこまる, used here as an established acknowledgment, not an ordinary report about a past event.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[承知](承知.md) in 承知しました (しょうちしました) also acknowledges understanding or acceptance. [はい](はい.md) can acknowledge instructions, but has broader yes/listening uses. [うん](うん.md) is informal and does not provide the same respectful tone. These are different ways of responding, not a fixed politeness ladder for every situation.

## Distinctive meaning

English "certainly" can assert that a claim is true; かしこまりました is not a universal replacement for that use. Nor is it an apology or the usual casual answer among friends. [もちろん](もちろん.md), "of course," expresses an attitude of readiness or obviousness rather than this particular respectful acceptance formula.
