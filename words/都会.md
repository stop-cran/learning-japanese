# 都会 (とかい) — city; urban setting

## Meaning

都会 means a city and is useful when talking about an urban setting or urban life.
Use の to connect it to a noun: 都会の生活 (とかいのせいかつ), "city life."

- 都会には大きな店がたくさんあります。 (とかいにはおおきなみせがたくさんあります。) — There are many large shops in the city.
- 都会の生活に慣れました。 (とかいのせいかつになれました。) — I have become used to city life.

## How the kanji combine

[都](../kanji/都.md) contributes the city idea; [会](../kanji/会.md) contributes meeting or gathering as a learning association.
Together, ト + カイ names an urban place, not a meeting held at city hall.
The gathering image helps with the spelling but is not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[都市](都市.md) (とし) also means a city or urban area; the terms overlap.
[田舎](田舎.md) (いなか), in its countryside sense, supplies a useful city/country contrast.
田舎 can also mean one's hometown, so context matters when using it as the opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

都会 is useful for describing city surroundings and ways of living. It is not the name of an official municipal status,
and it does not require the place to be a national capital. Do not infer that meaning from 都 alone.
