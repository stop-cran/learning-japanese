# びりびり — ripping; rattling; electric tingling

## Meaning

Ripping something apart, a rattling vibration, or an electric-shock-like sensation.

- 古い封筒（ふうとう）をびりびり破（やぶ）いた。 — I ripped the old envelope apart.
- 大きな音で窓（まど）がびりびり震（ふる）えた。 — The loud sound made the window rattle.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ばりばり](ばりばり.md) overlaps in forceful ripping. [ぴりぴり](ぴりぴり.md) can also describe a sharp tingle or tearing sound; voicing does not impose an exact intensity boundary.

## Distinctive meaning

びりびり破く（やぶく）is not a bodily sensation, whereas 手がびりびりする can describe an electric tingle. Learn the verb and affected thing together rather than treating “electric” as the meaning in every scene.
