# 破る (やぶる) — tear; rip

## Meaning

破る describes tearing or ripping something, with paper providing a clear starting example.
It is transitive: を marks what is torn.
The polite form is 破ります (やぶります), and the past is 破った (やぶった).

- 古い手紙を破りました。 (ふるいてがみをやぶりました。) — I tore up an old letter.
- この紙を破らないでください。 (このかみをやぶらないでください。) — Please do not tear this paper.

The negative request uses 破らない, not 破ない: this is a godan verb.

## How the kanji combine

[破](../kanji/破.md) is read やぶ in 破る; る is written in kana.
The on reading ハ belongs to compounds such as 破壊 (はかい), "destruction."
The single character's broad breaking sense does not determine which object fits every verb containing it.

## Synonyms and antonyms

破れる (やぶれる) is the intransitive partner: 紙が破れました (かみがやぶれました), "The paper tore."
It describes the change without naming an actor.
[切る](切る.md) きる means cut; はさみで紙を切る (はさみでかみをきる) is "cut paper with scissors."
The contrast is the action involved, not simply whether the paper ends up in pieces.

## Distinctive meaning

The English gloss "break" is broader than this introductory paper-tearing use.
壊す (こわす) is a useful verb for breaking a machine; do not automatically substitute 破る for it.
Similarly, 破れる is not the opposite of 破る: the pair distinguishes something getting torn from someone tearing it.
