# トレーニング (トレーニング) - training; workout

## Meaning

トレーニング means training or practice for a skill, job, or
sport. It also means working out, including strength or weight
training. Both トレーニングする and トレーニングをする
describe doing the training.

- 毎朝、ジムでトレーニングしています。 (まいあさ、ジムでトレーニングしています。) - I work out at the gym every morning.
- 発音のトレーニングを続けています。 (はつおんのトレーニングをつづけています。) - I am continuing my pronunciation training.

The first example is physical exercise. The second develops a
language skill rather than physical strength. トレーニングを受ける
(トレーニングをうける) means "receive training."

## How the kanji combine

This katakana loanword comes from English "training."
The long-vowel mark ー belongs to レー; the ending ニング is
part of the borrowed word, not a Japanese verb ending.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[練習](練習.md) (れんしゅう) is practice;
[訓練](訓練.md) (くんれん) is training or drill.
[研修](研修.md) (けんしゅう) is useful for professional training.
[運動](運動.md) (うんどう) covers physical exercise more broadly.
These overlap; トレーニング is not confined to sport.

## Distinctive meaning

A training program, an individual exercise session, and the
ongoing process can all be intended in context. Calling an
activity トレーニング does not establish that it is supervised,
competitive, or part of a formal qualification.
