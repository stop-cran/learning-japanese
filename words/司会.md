# 司会 (しかい) - presiding; host or moderator

## Meaning

司会 is leading or presiding over proceedings, such as a meeting, ceremony, or program. It can also name the person acting as host, presenter, or moderator.

- 明日の会議では私が司会をします。 (あしたのかいぎではわたしがしかいをします。) - I will moderate tomorrow's meeting.
- 司会は次の出演者を紹介しました。 (しかいはつぎのしゅつえんしゃをしょうかいしました。) - The host introduced the next performer.

司会をする and 司会する describe the activity; 司会者 (しかいしゃ) explicitly identifies the person. The noun 司会 itself can already name that role-holder, as in the second example.

## How the kanji combine

司 contributes taking charge, and [会](../kanji/会.md) contributes a gathering or meeting. Taking charge of the proceedings gives a useful component explanation. The on readings シ + カイ give しかい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[議長](議長.md) ぎちょう is a meeting or assembly's chairperson. 司会 also works for presenting an event or program; it does not automatically mean the head of the organization. [会議](会議.md) かいぎ is the meeting, not its moderator.

## Distinctive meaning

Context distinguishes the activity from the person. The word does not mean every entertainer, organizer, or participant merely because they attend the event. Its contribution is guiding the proceedings.
