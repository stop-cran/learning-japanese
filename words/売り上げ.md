# 売り上げ (うりあげ) - sales revenue; takings

## Meaning

売り上げ is the amount brought in by sales: sales revenue, takings or
turnover. It is also written 売上 (うりあげ), without the intervening kana.
Both spellings identify the same noun here.

- 今日の売り上げは五万円でした。 (きょうのうりあげはごまんえんでした。) - Today's takings were fifty thousand yen.
- 新しい店の売り上げが伸びています。 (あたらしいみせのうりあげがのびています。) - The new shop's sales revenue is growing.

## How the kanji combine

[売](../kanji/売.md) contributes selling, and [上](../kanji/上.md)
appears in the native form 上げ (あげ). The written parts are
売り (うり) + 上げ (あげ). Learn the established sales-result noun,
not a rule that anything written with 上 must mean financial profit.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[売れ行き](売れ行き.md) (うれゆき) concerns how well goods are selling.
[販売](販売.md) (はんばい) names the selling activity, and
[収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう) is broader income or receipts, not
necessarily from selling goods or services.
[利益](利益.md) (りえき), "profit," is not the total sales amount.

## Distinctive meaning

A business can have large 売り上げ but little 利益 after costs.
Sales figures can also be discussed alongside quantities sold; do not
assume every English "sales" figure measures the same thing. The yen amount
in the first example makes the monetary reading explicit.
