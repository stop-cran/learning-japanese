# 引っ掛かる (ひっかかる) - get caught; fall for a trick

## Meaning

引っ掛かる means to get caught or snagged on something.
It also means falling for a trick. It is an intransitive godan verb:
the thing caught takes が, and an obstruction or trap can take に.

- セーターがくぎに引っ掛かりました。 (セーターがくぎにひっかかりました。) - My sweater got snagged on a nail.
- うその広告に引っ掛からないでください。 (うそのこうこくにひっかからないでください。) - Please do not fall for deceptive advertising.

Forms include 引っ掛かります, 引っ掛かった, and 引っ掛からない.
The dictionary also records something lingering in one's mind:
彼の言い方が少し引っ掛かります (かれのいいかたがすこしひっかかります),
"Something about his way of putting it bothers me."

## How the kanji combine

[引](../kanji/引.md) supplies the 引っ element, related to 引き;
[掛](../kanji/掛.md) supplies 掛かる (かかる), catching or hanging
in the relevant use. Learn the complete ひっかかる, with small っ,
not ひきかかる. 引っかかる is another recorded spelling.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[捕まる](捕まる.md) (つかまる) overlaps with being caught;
[挟まる](挟まる.md) (はさまる) specifically concerns getting between
two sides. [寄る](寄る.md) (よる) overlaps with the additional
stop-by use. [心配](心配.md) (しんぱい) and [悩む](悩む.md)
(なやむ) provide comparisons for the lingering-concern use.

## Distinctive meaning

Other recorded uses include getting delayed or stopping by, becoming
involved in trouble, and being held up by checks or regulations.
The subject's being caught is different from actively
[だます](だます.md), "deceive," or [はめる](はめる.md), "entrap."
引っ掛かる is not a passive conjugation of either verb.

The separate splash use concerns liquid landing on something:
Digital Daijisen's 車のしぶきが引っ掛かる has the spray as its
grammatical subject. It is not a transitive instruction to splash
someone. These additional uses should be selected by their construction,
not treated as one unrestricted translation of "catch."
