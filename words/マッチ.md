# マッチ (マッチ) — match; matching

## Meaning

マッチ can mean a sporting match or matching well with something. A separate loanword with the same spelling means a small matchstick used to light a fire.

- 箱の中にマッチがあります。 (はこのなかにマッチがあります。) — There are matches in the box.
- この色は部屋によくマッチしています。 (このいろはへやによくマッチしています。) — This color matches the room well.

## How the kanji combine

Katakana is normal. The small ッ marks a brief closure before チ. 燐寸 is a rare ateji spelling for the fire-lighting object, not a spelling to transfer to the sporting or compatibility senses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ライター is a lighter, another object used to produce a flame. 試合 (しあい) is a sports contest. 合う (あう) can express fitting or going well together. These comparisons correspond to different senses, not one interchangeable synonym set.

## Distinctive meaning

マッチする uses irregular する conjugation and commonly takes に for what something matches. A sporting use such as テストマッチ, "test match," is unrelated to a box of matches. JMdict separates the fire-lighting noun from the contest/compatibility entry, so identify the sense from context rather than from the katakana alone.
