# 人 (ひと) — person; people

## Meaning

人, read ひと, means a person or people. The noun does not change form for plural reference.

- あの人は先生です。 (あのひとはせんせいです。) — That person is a teacher.
- 駅には人がたくさんいます。 (えきにはひとがたくさんいます。) — There are many people at the station.

Use いる for a person's presence. Before another noun, 人の can mean "a person's" or "other people's," depending on context.

## How the kanji combine

[人](../kanji/人.md) represents the complete native noun ひと. In compounds it can instead have on-readings: ジン in 日本人 (にほんじん), "Japanese person," and ニン in 三人 (さんにん), "three people."

## Synonyms and antonyms

方 (かた) can respectfully refer to a person. 人間 (にんげん) means a human being or humanity. These words differ in scope and register; they are not opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn the special counting forms 一人 (ひとり) and 二人 (ふたり), rather than treating standalone ひと as a regular counter after any number. あの人 can refer to someone of any gender.
