# ほっと — feeling relief; a deep sigh

## Meaning

Relief as tension eases, or a deep exhalation or sigh.

- 無事（ぶじ）に着いたと聞いて、ほっとした。 — I felt relieved to hear that they had arrived safely.
- 試験（しけん）が終わり、ほっと息（いき）をついた。 — With the exam over, I let out a relieved sigh.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[安心](安心.md)（あんしん）overlaps in relief or peace of mind. [さっぱり](さっぱり.md) and [すっきり](すっきり.md) can also describe a relieved feeling, with other refreshing or clearing uses.

## Distinctive meaning

ほっとする often presents tension releasing in a particular moment. In ほっとため息（いき）をつく, the word can describe the deep sigh itself; context decides its emotion. This is not the English loanword ホット, “hot.”
