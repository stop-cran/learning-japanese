# 今後 (こんご) - from now on; hereafter

## Meaning

今後 refers to the period from now onward. It works adverbially or as a noun: 今後の予定 (こんごのよてい), "plans from here on."

- 今後はもっと気をつけます。 (こんごはもっときをつけます。) - I will be more careful from now on.
- 今後の予定を話し合いましょう。 (こんごのよていをはなしあいましょう。) - Let's discuss our plans going forward.

は can contrast future behavior with what happened before. の connects 今後 to a following noun. The word itself does not specify how long the future period lasts.

## How the kanji combine

[今](../kanji/今.md) supplies now, and [後](../kanji/後.md) supplies after. Their relation is temporal: "after now." The on readings are コン + ゴ, rather than the separate native readings いま and あと.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[これから](これから.md) also means from now on and can introduce an action about to begin. [将来](将来.md) (しょうらい) names the future, often when discussing later prospects. 今後 keeps the present as the starting point.

## Distinctive meaning

今後 suits plans, continuing policies, and promises about future conduct, but is not confined to official documents. 今後もよろしくお願いします (こんごもよろしくおねがいします) asks for continued goodwill or cooperation. The も means "also/continuing," linking the future relationship to the existing one.
