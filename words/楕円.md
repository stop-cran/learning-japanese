# 楕円 (だえん) - ellipse; oval

## Meaning

楕円 names an ellipse or an oval shape.
楕円形 (だえんけい) means an elliptical or oval shape.

- 紙に楕円を描きました。
  (かみにだえんをかきました。) - I drew an ellipse on the paper.
- この鏡は楕円形です。
  (このかがみはだえんけいです。) - This mirror is oval.

In mathematics, an ellipse is defined by a constant sum of distances to
two fixed points, called foci. An everyday description of something as
oval need not guarantee that exact mathematical property.

## How the kanji combine

楕 contributes ellipse, and [円](../kanji/円.md) circle or roundness.
The on readings ダ + エン give だえん.
This is a shape association, not a claim that every rounded object is an ellipse.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[円](円.md) (えん) names a circle.
[丸い](丸い.md) (まるい) and [円い](円い.md) (まるい) describe roundness;
they are not precise replacements for a named ellipse in every context.
There is no single universal antonym covering all other shapes.

## Distinctive meaning

The distinction between an exact mathematical curve and an approximate
everyday shape matters. JMdict recognizes 橢円 as a rare kanji form.
Its だ円 spelling is marked search-only in the pinned snapshot, so this
article does not promote that lookup form to the main spelling.
