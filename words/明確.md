# 明確 (めいかく) - clear; precise

## Meaning

明確 is a na-adjective meaning clear, definite, or precise. Use 明確な before a noun, 明確です as a predicate, and 明確に to describe how something is expressed.

- 明確な答えが必要です。 (めいかくなこたえがひつようです。) - We need a clear answer.
- 自分の意見を明確に伝えました。 (じぶんのいけんをめいかくにつたえました。) - I conveyed my opinion clearly.

To say "clarify" or "make clear," use **Nを明確にする**: 目標を明確にする (もくひょうをめいかくにする), "clarify the goal." The にする construction supplies the change; 明確 by itself is not a verb.

## How the kanji combine

[明](../kanji/明.md) contributes clarity or brightness, and [確](../kanji/確.md) contributes certainty or firmness. The related meanings reinforce the idea of something clearly defined. Their on readings are メイ + カク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[はっきり](はっきり.md) also describes clarity, but has different attachment patterns. [明らか](明らか.md) (あきらか) can mean evident or obvious. 曖昧 (あいまい), "ambiguous/vague," gives a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

An answer can be 明確 without being correct: clarity concerns how definite its meaning is, not whether its claim is true. Conversely, 明確ではない (めいかくではない) says something is not clear or definite, not necessarily false. Keep the adjective grammar even when English translates a whole construction with a verb such as "clarify."
