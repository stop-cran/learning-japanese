# 重量 (じゅうりょう) - weight

## Meaning

重量 is weight. It is a noun, useful when measuring luggage, cargo, or another object. The word itself supplies neither the number nor the unit.

- この荷物の重量は十キロです。
  (このにもつのじゅうりょうはじゅっキロです。) - This piece of luggage weighs ten kilograms.
- 荷物の重量を量りました。
  (にもつのじゅうりょうをはかりました。) - I weighed the luggage.

The second example uses the noun 重量 with the verb 量る (はかる), "measure; weigh." The two occurrences of 量 have different readings.

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) contributes heaviness or weight, and [量](../kanji/量.md) a measured quantity. Together they identify the quantity being measured as weight. Read ジュウ + リョウ; compare [終了](終了.md) (しゅうりょう), which has a different first sound and meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

重さ (おもさ) also names weight or heaviness. [量](量.md) (りょう) is amount or quantity more generally; it can concern volume or portions rather than weight.

## Distinctive meaning

The community source also glosses this row as "heavyweight boxer," but the pinned 重量 family has only the weight sense. Do not memorize the bare noun as a name for a boxer. 重量級 (じゅうりょうきゅう) and ヘビー級 (ヘビーきゅう) name a heavyweight class; they are separate dictionary entries.

The kilogram example uses ordinary luggage-weighing language, not a definition equating every physical measurement of mass and force.
