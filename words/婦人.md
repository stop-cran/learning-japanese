# 婦人 (ふじん) - woman; lady

## Meaning

婦人 is a noun meaning a woman or lady, particularly an adult woman. The inspected dictionary labels it **dated** and **sensitive**. Learn to recognize it in established expressions and older-style writing, rather than treating it as an unrestricted neutral equivalent of "female."

- 婦人服売り場は二階です。 (ふじんふくうりばはにかいです。) - The women's clothing section is on the second floor.
- この小説には上品な婦人が登場します。 (このしょうせつにはじょうひんなふじんがとうじょうします。) - An elegant lady appears in this novel.

The clothing example illustrates an established compound; the dictionary's labels do not mean every occurrence of 婦人服 is obsolete or offensive.

## How the kanji combine

[婦](../kanji/婦.md) has woman or wife associations; [人](../kanji/人.md) means person. Together they form the established term 婦人, read フ + ジン. Component meanings do not prove that the woman must be married.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[女性](女性.md) (じょせい) is a useful general term for women. [夫人](夫人.md) has the same reading ふじん but refers to a wife or a title such as "Mrs." Do not confuse the spellings.

## Distinctive meaning

Register belongs to the whole expression and situation. Recognizing a department-store label is different from choosing how to address or describe a particular person. Do not infer a universal politeness ranking from the English gloss "lady."
