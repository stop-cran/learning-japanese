# 目次 (もくじ) - table of contents

## Meaning

目次 is a table of contents: an ordered list of a book's sections,
chapters, or other headings.

- 目次で読みたい章を探しました。 (もくじでよみたいしょうをさがしました。) - I looked for the chapter I wanted to read in the table of contents.
- この本の目次は最初のページにあります。 (このほんのもくじはさいしょのページにあります。) - This book's table of contents is on the first page.

## How the kanji combine

[目](../kanji/目.md) can suggest listed items and [次](../kanji/次.md)
order or sequence. モク + ジ forms もくじ. Learn this established
compound rather than reading it as the native words め and つぎ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[索引](索引.md) (さくいん) is an index for looking up terms or names,
often with page references. It is not the usual chapter-by-chapter 目次.
[内容](内容.md) (ないよう) and [中身](中身.md) (なかみ) concern
contents or substance, not specifically the table listing the chapters.

## Distinctive meaning

English "contents" can mean either the contents themselves or the
book's contents page. 目次 names the organized listing. The public
dictionary also records the order of items or titles; it does not
make every individual [項目](項目.md) (こうもく), "listed item," a whole 目次.
