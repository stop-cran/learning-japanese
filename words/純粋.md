# 純粋 (じゅんすい) - pure; genuine; unmixed

## Meaning

純粋 describes something pure, genuine, or unmixed. It can concern a material's composition or a person's feelings and motives.

- これは純粋な水です。 (これはじゅんすいなみずです。) - This is pure water.
- 純粋な好奇心から質問しました。 (じゅんすいなこうきしんからしつもんしました。) - I asked out of genuine curiosity.

Use 純粋な + noun or 純粋だ / 純粋です. 純粋に can mean purely or genuinely, as in 純粋に楽しむ (じゅんすいにたのしむ), "enjoy something purely for its own sake."

## How the kanji combine

[純](../kanji/純.md) has purity or genuineness associations, and 粋 an essence or refined quality association. The combination is a reminder of purity, not proof that every use concerns a laboratory substance. Keep すい separate from the reading of the independent word 粋 (いき).

## Synonyms and antonyms

[純情](純情.md) overlaps when discussing an innocent or pure-hearted person. [清い](清い.md), [清潔](清潔.md), and [きれい](きれい.md) have moral-purity uses; [本当](本当.md) and [本物](本物.md) overlap in genuineness. 不純 (ふじゅん), "impure," contrasts with 純粋.

## Distinctive meaning

純粋 does not simply mean washed or hygienic: something can be clean but mixed with another substance. Conversely, using 純粋 about motives does not describe chemical composition. The material and mental uses belong to the same word, so a strict "materials only" shortcut would be misleading.
