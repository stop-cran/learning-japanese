# べとべと — sticky and tacky

## Meaning

べとべと describes an unpleasantly sticky or tacky surface.

- はちみつが手について、べとべとする。
  — Honey gets on my hands and makes them sticky.
- 汗でシャツがべとべとになった。
  — Sweat made the shirt sticky.

## Synonyms and antonyms

べとべと suggests a clinging, often unpleasant stickiness; it is not the pleasant elasticity of もちもち.

## Distinctive meaning

It describes a sticky condition and can pair with する or mark a resulting state with になる.
