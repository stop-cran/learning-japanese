# 卵 (たまご) — egg

## Meaning

卵 means an egg. It can refer to an animal's egg generally; in cooking and shopping, it commonly means a hen's egg.

- 卵を二個買いました。 (たまごをにこかいました。) — I bought two eggs.
- この鳥は卵を産みます。 (このとりはたまごをうみます。) — This bird lays eggs.

個 (こ) is a useful counter for individual eggs.

## How the kanji combine

The single character 卵 writes the native word たまご. There are no component readings to combine. 玉子, with the same reading, is another spelling often used for eggs in cooking.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ゆで卵 (ゆでたまご) is a boiled egg. 鶏 (にわとり), "chicken," names the bird, not the egg or its contents. There is no direct opposite of 卵.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not treat 卵 and 玉子 as a rigid raw-versus-cooked division. 卵 is also used for food; 玉子 is especially associated with hen's eggs and cooking. For biological eggs generally, 卵 is the appropriate broad spelling.
