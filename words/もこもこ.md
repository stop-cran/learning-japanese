# もこもこ — fluffy and bulky

## Meaning

Rounded, bulky fluff or bulges, such as thick fleece, wool, or layered clothes. Depending on the object, it can describe lumpiness rather than softness.

- この上着（うわぎ）はもこもこしていて、とても暖かい。 — This jacket is thick and fluffy, and very warm.
- セーターを二枚着たら、腕（うで）がもこもこして動かしにくい。 — With two sweaters on, my arms feel bulky and are hard to move.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ふわふわ](ふわふわ.md) emphasizes airy lightness. [ふかふか](ふかふか.md) emphasizes yielding cushioning. [でこぼこ](でこぼこ.md) describes an uneven surface without implying warm fluff.

## Distinctive meaning

Use もこもこの服, もこもこした毛, and もこもこする. Clothing may be pleasantly plush or inconveniently bulky; the context decides. The bulging sense can also apply to visible muscles or uneven shapes, so “fluffy” is not a complete translation in every sentence. It is not a word for an actual rustling sound.
