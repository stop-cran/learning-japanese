# 資源 (しげん) - resources

## Meaning

資源 is a noun for resources available for use, especially materials or supplies
supporting life and production. Natural resources include water, minerals, and energy sources.
Use 資源を利用する (しげんをりようする), "use resources," and
資源を守る (しげんをまもる), "protect resources."

- 水は大切な資源です。
  (みずはたいせつなしげんです。) - Water is an important resource.
- この国には石油などの天然資源があります。
  (このくににはせきゆなどのてんねんしげんがあります。)
  - This country has natural resources such as oil.

天然資源 (てんねんしげん) explicitly means "natural resources."
The word 資源 itself does not have separate singular and plural forms.

## How the kanji combine

[資](../kanji/資.md) contributes assets, supplies, or means, and 源 means a source.
The combination suggests sources of useful supplies or means.
The on readings are シ + ゲン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[材料](材料.md) (ざいりょう) is material or ingredients for making something.
資源 views supplies as available resources rather than necessarily ingredients already selected
for a specific product. [財産](財産.md) (ざいさん) concerns property or assets.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling something a 資源 emphasizes its potential usefulness and availability.
It does not say that the supply is unlimited or that using it has no environmental cost.
Consequently, 資源を大切にする (しげんをたいせつにする) means to value resources
and use them carefully.
