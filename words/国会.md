# 国会 (こっかい) - National Diet; parliament

## Meaning

国会 is a national legislative assembly. In a Japanese context it names the National Diet, Japan's parliament. It is a noun; **国会で** can mean "in parliament," and **国会に提出する** means to submit something to parliament.

- 国会で新しい法律について話し合っています。 (こっかいであたらしいほうりつについてはなしあっています。) - A new law is being discussed in parliament.
- 国会議員に手紙を書きました。 (こっかいぎいんにてがみをかきました。) - I wrote a letter to a member of the National Diet.

国会議員 (こっかいぎいん) identifies a legislator, not the assembly as a whole.

## How the kanji combine

[国](../kanji/国.md), "country," modifies [会](../kanji/会.md), "assembly; meeting." The on readings コク + カイ combine with a sound change to こっかい, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[議会](議会.md) (ぎかい) is a broader word for a legislative assembly, including local assemblies. [政府](政府.md) (せいふ), "government," does not identify the same institution as parliament.

## Distinctive meaning

国会 can also refer to a foreign national legislature; the country and context determine the appropriate English name. It is not a general word for any nationwide meeting. Historical texts may use it for the earlier Imperial Diet, whereas the modern Japanese institutional sense refers to the National Diet established in 1947.
