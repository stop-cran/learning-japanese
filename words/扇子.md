# 扇子 (せんす) - folding fan

## Meaning

扇子 is a folding handheld fan.
It opens for use and folds together for carrying.

- 暑いので、扇子で顔を扇ぎました。 (あついので、せんすでかおをあおぎました。) - It was hot, so I fanned my face with a folding fan.
- 扇子を閉じて、かばんに入れました。 (せんすをとじて、かばんにいれました。) - I folded the fan closed and put it in my bag.

で marks the fan used as the means in the first example.
The fan itself is the object of 閉じる (とじる), close, in the second.

## How the kanji combine

扇 contributes fan, and [子](../kanji/子.md) is a noun-forming element here,
not a reference to a child.
The readings are セン + ス: せんす.
Do not substitute the standalone こ reading of 子.

## Synonyms and antonyms

扇 (おうぎ) also names a folding fan.
うちわ is the nonfolding handheld-fan comparison, while
[扇風機](扇風機.md) (せんぷうき) is an electric fan.
[扇ぐ](扇ぐ.md) (あおぐ) names the action of fanning.

## Distinctive meaning

扇子 names the object, not a general verb for cooling something.
Also distinguish センス, the same-sounding loanword for taste, flair or sense.
In センスがいい, "have good taste," the speaker is not necessarily talking about a folding fan.
