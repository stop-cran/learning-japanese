# 生地 (きじ) - fabric; dough

## Meaning

生地 (きじ) is a noun for material such as fabric, or for dough and batter in cooking. The surrounding activity tells you which meaning is intended.

- この生地でスカートを作ります。 (このきじでスカートをつくります。) - I will make a skirt from this fabric.
- パンの生地をこねます。 (パンのきじをこねます。) - I knead the bread dough.

服の生地 (ふくのきじ) is fabric for clothing; ケーキの生地 is cake batter. The same noun can cover different consistencies in cooking, so "dough" is not the right English translation for every recipe.

## How the kanji combine

[生](../kanji/生.md) contributes a raw or unprocessed association, while [地](../kanji/地.md) suggests a base or material. The reading is mixed: native き followed by on-reading ジ, a pattern called *yutoyomi*. It is not the regular on-on reading of these characters.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[布](布.md) (ぬの) overlaps with the fabric sense but does not mean bread dough. [材料](材料.md) (ざいりょう) means materials or ingredients more broadly, including separate ingredients before they become dough.

## Distinctive meaning

The source list pairs きじ with the conflicting English gloss "birthplace." This card retains the verified きじ identity and teaches fabric and dough. That editorial choice does not recover what the original compiler intended; do not learn "birthplace" as the meaning of きじ.
