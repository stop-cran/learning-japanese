# 絵の具 (えのぐ) - paint; coloring materials

## Meaning

絵の具 names paints or coloring materials used for making pictures.
It is a noun and can refer to the material generally or to particular
colors. 絵具 is another spelling, also read えのぐ.

- 赤い絵の具を少し使いました。 (あかいえのぐをすこしつかいました。) - I used a little red paint.
- 絵の具で空を青く塗りました。 (えのぐでそらをあおくぬりました。) - I painted the sky blue with paints.

## How the kanji combine

[絵](../kanji/絵.md), "picture," modifies [具](../kanji/具.md),
materials or implements, through the connecting particle の.
絵 contributes its on reading エ and 具 its on reading グ, with
the native linking の between them. This is not a claim that のぐ
is one reading of 具.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ペンキ](ペンキ.md) is paint for coating surfaces such as walls and
fences; 絵の具 is the useful picture-making comparison.
[色](色.md) (いろ) is color itself, not necessarily the substance used
to produce it. [絵](絵.md) (え) is the picture, not its coloring materials.

## Distinctive meaning

English "paint" covers several contexts, so distinguish making a picture
from coating a building without turning the distinction into an absolute
rule about chemical composition. 絵の具 does not by itself specify
whether the product is watercolor, oil paint or another medium.
