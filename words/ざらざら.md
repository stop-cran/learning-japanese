# ざらざら — rough and gritty

## Meaning

Fine roughness or a sandy, granular feel; also a rattling sound made by many small hard objects.

- この紙は、触るとざらざらしている。 — This paper feels rough to the touch.
- 砂が入って、口の中がざらざらする。 — I got sand in my mouth, and it feels gritty.
- 袋（ふくろ）から豆（まめ）をざらざらと出した。 — I poured the beans out of the bag with a rattling sound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ごつごつ](ごつごつ.md) suggests larger, harder protrusions. [でこぼこ](でこぼこ.md) describes raised and lowered parts of a surface. [すべすべ](すべすべ.md) is smooth under the fingers.

## Distinctive meaning

ざらざらした紙, ざらざらの表面（ひょうめん）, and ざらざらする describe texture. With 出す or 落ちる, ざらざらと can foreground the sound of grains or beans moving. A rough voice can also be called ざらざらした声; this is a sensory extension, not literal sand in the speaker's throat.
