# 鐘 (かね) - bell; chime

## Meaning

鐘 is a bell or chime, often a large hanging bell such as one at a temple. It is a noun.

- 寺の鐘が夕方に鳴ります。 (てらのかねがゆうがたになります。) - The temple bell rings in the evening.
- 鐘の音が遠くまで聞こえました。 (かねのおとがとおくまできこえました。) - The sound of the bell could be heard far away.

鐘が鳴る (かねがなる) describes the bell ringing. 鐘を鳴らす (かねをならす) means to ring it.

## How the kanji combine

鐘 is the single written kanji and represents the noun かね. It is not [金](../kanji/金.md), which also has a reading かね but can mean money or metal. Shared sound does not establish shared meaning.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ベル](ベル.md) often names a signaling bell or device, including a bicycle or door bell. 鈴 (すず) commonly names a small jingling bell, and チャイム a chime or chime-like signal. Shape and use influence the choice; size alone is not an exceptionless naming rule.

## Distinctive meaning

鐘 can name the object even when it is silent. [鳴る](鳴る.md) (なる) is the verb for sounding or ringing. The temple-bell example is a useful prototype, not a claim that every 鐘 belongs to a temple.
