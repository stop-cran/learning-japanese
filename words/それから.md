# それから (それから) - after that; and then

## Meaning

それから connects an event with what happens after it. The first event gives the reference point.

- 宿題をしました。それから、テレビを見ました。 (しゅくだいをしました。それから、テレビをみました。) - I did my homework. After that, I watched television.
- 先にご飯を食べましょう。それから出かけましょう。 (さきにごはんをたべましょう。それからでかけましょう。) - Let's eat first. Then let's go out.

It can organize a past account or a future plan; it does not impose a tense on the following verb.

## How the kanji combine

The normal spelling is kana. それ, "that," and から, "from; after," together point to the next stage after something already mentioned.

## Synonyms and antonyms

その後 (そのあと), "after that," often overlaps. これから starts from now or the current situation, while それから refers back to the event just established.

## Distinctive meaning

Sequence is not necessarily cause. Saying that you watched television それから tells the order, not that doing homework caused you to watch television. For a reason-and-result connection, choose a construction that actually expresses that relation.
