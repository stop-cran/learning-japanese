# 形容動詞 (けいようどうし) - adjectival noun; Japanese na-adjective

## Meaning

形容動詞 names a grammatical class, often called adjectival nouns or na-adjectives in learner descriptions.
In modern Japanese school grammar, its citation form includes だ, as in 静かだ (しずかだ), "is quiet."
Before a noun, that pattern uses な: 静かな部屋 (しずかなへや), "a quiet room."

- 「静かだ」は形容動詞です。 (「しずかだ」はけいようどうしです。) - Shizuka da is classified as a keiyoudoushi.
- この部屋は静かです。 (このへやはしずかです。) - This room is quiet.
- 静かな部屋を探しています。 (しずかなへやをさがしています。) - I am looking for a quiet room.

Learner dictionaries may instead list the stem 静か and explain だ separately.
That difference is one of grammatical analysis, not a different pronunciation of 静か.

## How the kanji combine

[形](../kanji/形.md) and [容](../kanji/容.md) form 形容 (けいよう), describing.
[動](../kanji/動.md) and [詞](../kanji/詞.md) form 動詞 (どうし), "verb."
The whole established term is ケイ + ヨウ + ドウ + シ.
Its inclusion of 動詞 does not mean that 静か describes an action like "run."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[形容詞](形容詞.md) (けいようし) names i-adjectives in the same school-grammar framework.
English "adjective" may include both categories; "adjectival noun" and "quasi-adjective" are alternative descriptions, not antonyms.
Do not infer that the word is an ordinary noun in every construction simply from the English label "adjectival noun."

## Distinctive meaning

The modern な pattern above is an introduction, not an exhaustive historical definition.
Dictionary uses of 形容動詞 also cover classical なり/たり and related たる forms.
Those do not all follow the modern 静かな pattern; keep the grammatical period and framework explicit.
