# 重なる (かさなる) - be piled up; overlap

## Meaning

重なる describes things lying on top of one another, accumulating, or overlapping in time. It is an intransitive godan verb: what overlaps or piles up takes が. An overlapping counterpart can take と.

- 机の上に本が重なっています。 (つくえのうえにほんがかさなっています。) - Books are piled on top of one another on the desk.
- 会議が試験と重なりました。 (かいぎがしけんとかさなりました。) - The meeting coincided with the examination.

Forms include 重なります, 重なった, and 重ならない. Repeated problems can also accumulate: 悪いことが重なる (わるいことがかさなる), bad things happen one after another.

## How the kanji combine

[重](../kanji/重.md) contributes layering or piling up here, not the adjective reading おもい, heavy. なる is the kana ending of the native verb かさなる.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[重ねる](重ねる.md) (かさねる) is the transitive partner: 本を重ねる, stack books, versus 本が重なる, books lie on top of one another. [積もる](積もる.md) (つもる) also describes accumulation, as with snow. [繰り返す](繰り返す.md) (くりかえす) concerns repetition rather than necessarily simultaneous events.

## Distinctive meaning

Coinciding events need not physically touch. Conversely, repeated troubles can 重なる without occurring at exactly the same instant. Identify whether the context concerns physical layers, accumulating events, or simultaneous schedules rather than forcing one English translation into every sentence.
