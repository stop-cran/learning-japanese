# 港 (みなと) — harbor or port

## Meaning

港 is a harbor or port: a place where vessels can arrive, depart, or stay.
It is a noun, not the word for the ship itself.

- 船が港に着きました。 (ふねがみなとにつきました。) — The ship arrived at the port.
- 港で友達を待っています。 (みなとでともだちをまっています。) — I am waiting for a friend at the port.

The arrival example uses に for the destination. The waiting example uses で for the place where the action occurs.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [港](../kanji/港.md) writes the native noun みなと.
There is no separate written kana ending.
Its on reading コウ is used in compounds, but the standalone word taught here is not read こう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

English "harbor" and "port" both translate this sense; Japanese does not need a different spelling
just because English chooses one of those words.
空港 (くうこう), "airport," is a useful related compound rather than a synonym for the standalone maritime noun.

## Distinctive meaning

港 identifies a particular kind of place, not any stretch of sea or shore.
It can refer to a destination in travel without specifying a particular dock or ship.
The first example says where the ship arrived, not where it was built or who owns it.
