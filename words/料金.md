# 料金 (りょうきん) - fee; charge; fare

## Meaning

料金 is a noun for the charge for a service, facility, or use of something. Depending on the setting, English "fee," "charge," or "fare" fits.

- 駐車場の料金を払いました。 (ちゅうしゃじょうのりょうきんをはらいました。) - I paid the parking charge.
- 子供の料金はいくらですか。 (こどものりょうきんはいくらですか。) - How much is the charge for children?

Nの料金 identifies the service or category being charged for. 料金がかかる (りょうきんがかかる) means "a fee is charged/it costs money," and 料金に含まれる (りょうきんにふくまれる) means "be included in the charge."

## How the kanji combine

[料](../kanji/料.md) contributes a fee or charge, and [金](../kanji/金.md) contributes money. Together they specify money charged for something. Their on readings are リョウ + キン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[値段](値段.md) (ねだん) and [価格](価格.md) (かかく) concern price more broadly. 運賃 (うんちん) specifically names a transportation fare. [無料](無料.md) (むりょう), "free of charge," contrasts with having to pay, rather than being an opposite kind of fee.

## Distinctive meaning

The word does not state what a charge includes. Ask about additional charges or included services rather than assuming a quoted 料金 is the entire final cost. This is especially useful when reading a hotel's, parking facility's, or rental service's price information.
