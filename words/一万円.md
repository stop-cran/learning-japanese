# 一万円 (いちまんえん) — 10,000 yen

## Meaning

一万円 is 10,000 yen, the largest banknote in everyday use: 一万円札 いちまんえんさつ, 一万円で買える (can be bought for 10,000 yen), 二万円 にまんえん (20,000 yen).

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md) (one) multiplies [万](../kanji/万.md) (ten thousand), followed by [円](../kanji/円.md) (yen). Unlike 千円 or 百円, the number one is normally spoken and written for 万: 一万円, not 万円 alone.
Japanese counting steps by 万 (10,000): 十万 じゅうまん (100,000), 百万 ひゃくまん (1,000,000).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Related: [千円](千円.md) せんえん (1,000 yen), [百円](百円.md) ひゃくえん.

## Distinctive meaning

Because English groups by thousands but Japanese by ten-thousands, 一万円 is "ten thousand yen", and 十万円 is "a hundred thousand yen". Check this when converting.
