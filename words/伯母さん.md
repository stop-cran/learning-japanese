# 伯母さん (おばさん) - aunt

## Meaning

伯母さん is a familiar, courteous word for an aunt. This spelling specifies an aunt who is older than the relevant parent; 叔母さん uses the same reading for an aunt younger than that parent.

- 伯母さんは母の姉です。 (おばさんはははのあねです。) - My aunt is my mother's older sister.
- 伯母さん、ありがとう。 (おばさん、ありがとう。) - Thank you, Auntie.

In ordinary kana writing, おばさん leaves the older/younger distinction unspecified. When speaking formally about your own aunt to an outsider, 伯母 (おば), without さん, is appropriate.

## How the kanji combine

伯 indicates the elder relation and [母](../kanji/母.md) identifies the female family relationship. Learn 伯母 as the whole-word reading おば. さん adds familiar respect; it is not part of the kinship root.

## Synonyms and antonyms

叔母さん is the younger-aunt spelling. おじさん means "uncle." Neither is a grammatical opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

Keep the vowel short: おばさん is an aunt, whereas おばあさん has a long あ and means "grandmother" or "elderly woman." The distinction between 伯母 and 叔母 is visible in kanji, not audible in おばさん.
