# 解放 (かいほう) - release; liberation

## Meaning

解放 means release or liberation from confinement, control, duties or another burden. 解放する is transitive: the person or thing released takes を.

- 鳥をかごから解放しました。 (とりをかごからかいほうしました。) - I released the bird from its cage.
- 仕事から解放されて、ほっとしました。 (しごとからかいほうされて、ほっとしました。) - I felt relieved to be free of my work.

解放される is passive: the subject is released. から marks what someone is freed from. In computing, メモリを解放する means "free/deallocate memory," not erase every stored file.

## How the kanji combine

[解](../kanji/解.md) contributes undoing a restraint and [放](../kanji/放.md) letting go. The related release ideas reinforce one another: カイ + ホウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[放す](放す.md) (はなす) also means release or let go. [自由](自由.md) (じゆう) names freedom or a free condition; 解放 highlights release into it.

[開放](開放.md), also かいほう, concerns opening something or making access available. 窓を開放する (まどをかいほうする), "leave/open the windows wide," does not mean liberating them from captivity.

## Distinctive meaning

Match the spelling to what changes. Releasing a captive or freeing oneself from a burden uses 解放; opening a facility to visitors uses 開放. This is not a rule that 解放 must be political or that it can only release living things.
