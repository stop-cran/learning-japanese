# 冷える (ひえる) - grow cold, cool down

## Meaning

冷える is an intransitive ichidan verb describing something becoming cold or chilled. It can apply to drinks, body parts, or the surrounding conditions.

- 夜になると、足が冷えます。 (よるになると、あしがひえます。) - My feet get cold at night.
- ジュースがよく冷えています。 (ジュースがよくひえています。) - The juice is nicely chilled.

冷えた (ひえた) is the past; 冷えない (ひえない) is the negative. 冷えている can describe the resulting cold state.

## How the kanji combine

[冷](../kanji/冷.md) writes ひ, and える completes the word. The ichidan conjugation stem is ひえ, including the first kana after the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

冷やす (ひやす) is transitive: ジュースを冷やす, "chill the juice." 温まる (あたたまる), "get warm," contrasts with 冷える.

## Distinctive meaning

冷たい (つめたい) describes something as cold, while 冷える describes cooling or chilling. Compare 水が冷たい (みずがつめたい), "the water is cold," with 水が冷えた (みずがひえた), "the water has cooled."
