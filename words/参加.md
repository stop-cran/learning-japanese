# 参加 (さんか) — participation

## Meaning

参加 means participation in an activity, event, or group undertaking. Add する for "participate."
The activity takes に: 会議に参加する (かいぎにさんかする), "participate in a meeting."

- 明日の会議に参加します。 (あしたのかいぎにさんかします。) — I will take part in tomorrow's meeting.

As a noun, it can name participation itself: 参加は自由です (さんかはじゆうです), "Participation is optional."

## How the kanji combine

[参](../kanji/参.md) contributes taking part, and [加](../kanji/加.md) contributes joining or adding oneself.
The two ideas reinforce involvement in a shared activity. The on readings are サン + カ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

出席 (しゅっせき) concerns attendance or presence. At a meeting, both 出席する and 参加する may fit:
the former highlights being present, the latter taking part. This is a difference of focus, not a rule
that attendance must be passive.

加わる (くわわる), "join," is a related verb. Its form cannot replace the noun 参加 without changing the sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

参加する names participation, not its quality. It does not guarantee that someone spoke often or made a large contribution.
Learn に with the activity rather than translating the English object pattern in "join a meeting" as を.
