# 一昨日 (おととい) — day before yesterday

## Meaning

一昨日 means **the day before yesterday**, two calendar days before today. It can locate an event in time without a following に.

一昨日、友達に会いました。 (おととい、ともだちにあいました。) — I met a friend the day before yesterday.

Before a noun, use の: 一昨日の新聞 (おとといのしんぶん), “the newspaper from the day before yesterday.”

## How the kanji combine

[一](../kanji/一.md), [昨](../kanji/昨.md), and [日](../kanji/日.md) form a conventional spelling for the whole native word おととい. Do not divide this reading into three regular kanji readings.

The same spelling is also read いっさくじつ, a more formal alternative. おとつい is another dictionary-listed native reading; this page uses おととい.

## Synonyms and antonyms

昨日 (きのう) is yesterday; 明後日 (あさって) is the day after tomorrow. 二日前 (ふつかまえ), “two days earlier/ago,” can use a reference point supplied by context.

## Distinctive meaning

一昨日 is anchored to the speaker's “today.” In a narrative about a past date, “two days before that” is not automatically 一昨日: make the reference date clear.
