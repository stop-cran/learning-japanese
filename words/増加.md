# 増加 (ぞうか) - increase; rise

## Meaning

増加 means an increase in number, amount, or extent. It is a noun and a する verb. A central pattern is 数が増加する (かずがぞうかする), "the number increases." To make something increase, 増加させる makes the causative relationship explicit.

- この町の人口は増加しています。 (このまちのじんこうはぞうかしています。) - This town's population is increasing.
- 去年より利用者が十パーセント増加しました。 (きょねんよりりようしゃがじゅっパーセントぞうかしました。) - The number of users increased by ten percent compared with last year.

人口の増加 (じんこうのぞうか) is the noun phrase "population increase."

## How the kanji combine

[増](../kanji/増.md) contributes increasing, and [加](../kanji/加.md) contributes adding. The related elements reinforce becoming greater in quantity. Neither component alone tells you whether the increase is beneficial.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[増える](増える.md) (ふえる) is a close everyday verb for becoming more numerous or greater in amount. 減少 (げんしょう), "decrease," is the dictionary's direct opposite. [成長](成長.md) (せいちょう) can describe growth or maturation rather than a simple numerical increase.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish an increase **by** an amount from reaching a final amount: 十人増加した means "increased by ten people," not "became ten people." Use the comparison point and final total explicitly when reporting figures. 増加 itself does not supply either number.
