# 贈り物 (おくりもの) - gift; present

## Meaning

贈り物 is something given as a gift. It is a noun, not the act of giving itself.

- 母への贈り物を選びました。 (ははへのおくりものをえらびました。) - I chose a gift for my mother.

へ marks the intended recipient and の lets that phrase modify 贈り物. 友達からの贈り物 (ともだちからのおくりもの) is "a gift from a friend," identifying the giver instead.

## How the kanji combine

贈り is the connecting form of 贈る (おくる), "give as a gift," and [物](../kanji/物.md) is もの, "thing." Both parts use native readings. The usual spelling retains り between the kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

プレゼント is an everyday borrowed word with much the same gift meaning. お土産 (おみやげ) is a souvenir or a gift associated with a trip or visit.

## Distinctive meaning

A 贈り物 is defined by being given, not by its price or packaging. It can be handmade. Do not confuse 贈る, giving a gift, with 送る (おくる), sending something.
