# 行儀 (ぎょうぎ) - manners; behavior

## Meaning

行儀 is a noun for manners or behavior, especially how someone conducts themselves around others. Learn it in 行儀がいい, "well-mannered," and 行儀が悪い (ぎょうぎがわるい), "badly behaved; ill-mannered."

- 子どもたちは行儀よく座っていました。 (こどもたちはぎょうぎよくすわっていました。) - The children were sitting nicely.
- 食事中に大声で騒ぐのは行儀が悪いです。 (しょくじちゅうにおおごえでさわぐのはぎょうぎがわるいです。) - Making a loud commotion during a meal is bad manners.

行儀よく modifies an action. Before a noun, 行儀のいい子 (ぎょうぎのいいこ) means "a well-behaved child."

## How the kanji combine

[行](../kanji/行.md) concerns conduct or actions here, rather than going to a destination. 儀 contributes forms or standards of behavior. Together they name the manner in which someone behaves. The readings are ギョウ + ギ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[礼儀](礼儀.md) (れいぎ) also concerns manners, with attention to courtesy and social etiquette. The words overlap, but 行儀 is useful for concrete conduct such as sitting or eating.

[態度](態度.md) (たいど) describes attitude or manner of dealing with someone more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

行儀 itself does not mean "good manners": the evaluation comes from いい or 悪い. Likewise, お行儀 (おぎょうぎ) adds the prefix お without changing the basic noun into an adjective. The contrasting evaluations, not two separate nouns, express good versus bad behavior.
