# 貧乏 (びんぼう) — poverty; being poor

## Meaning

貧乏 describes poverty: having too little income or property to live
comfortably. It is a noun and a な-adjective.

- 学生のころは貧乏でした。 (がくせいのころはびんぼうでした。) — I was poor when I was a student.
- 貧乏な家庭で育ちました。 (びんぼうなかていでそだちました。) — I grew up in a poor household.

Use 貧乏な + noun, but 貧乏です as a polite predicate; do not add な
before です. Dictionaries also recognize intransitive 貧乏する
(びんぼうする), "live in poverty."

## How the kanji combine

[貧](../kanji/貧.md) contributes poverty, and [乏](../kanji/乏.md)
contributes lack or insufficiency. Their related meanings reinforce each
other. The on readings here are ビン + ボウ, giving びんぼう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[貧しい](貧しい.md) (まずしい), "poor; needy," is an い-adjective:
貧しい家庭 (まずしいかてい) needs no な, whereas 貧乏な家庭 does.
[乏しい](乏しい.md) (とぼしい) can also describe an impoverished life,
as well as insufficient resources more generally.

[金持ち](金持ち.md) (かねもち), "wealthy person," gives a financial
contrast, but it is a noun with different attachment: 金持ちの家庭
(かねもちのかてい), "a wealthy household."

## Distinctive meaning

The focus is financial circumstances, not low prices, poor skill, or bad
weather. Poverty is not a judgment about someone's character or generosity;
take care when using a direct label about another person.
The long final vowel matters: びんぼう, not びんぼ.
