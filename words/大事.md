# 大事 (だいじ) — important; precious

## Meaning

大事 is a な-adjective meaning important or precious: 大事な話 (だいじなはなし), "an important discussion," and 大事な人 (だいじなひと), "someone precious to me." As a noun it can mean a serious matter or major incident.

- これは大事な書類です。 (これはだいじなしょるいです。) — This is an important document.
- 事故は大事には至りませんでした。 (じこはだいじにはいたりませんでした。) — The accident did not develop into anything serious.

## How the kanji combine

[大](../kanji/大.md), "big," and [事](../kanji/事.md), "matter," use on-readings: だい + じ. The "major matter" association helps connect the noun and adjective senses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

大切 (たいせつ) overlaps strongly with important or precious. 重要 (じゅうよう) often emphasizes significance. These are comparisons, not guaranteed distinct quiz answers.

## Distinctive meaning

大事にする (だいじにする) means to treasure or take good care of something: 家族を大事にする (かぞくをだいじにする), "cherish one's family." お大事に (おだいじに) is said to someone who is unwell, roughly "Take care / Get well soon." The adjective takes な before nouns and can end a polite sentence with です.
