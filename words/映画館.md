# 映画館 (えいがかん) - cinema; movie theater

## Meaning

映画館 is a cinema or movie theater: the place where people watch films.

- 映画館で映画を見ました。 (えいがかんでえいがをみました。) - I watched a movie at a cinema.
- 駅の近くに映画館があります。 (えきのちかくにえいがかんがあります。) - There is a cinema near the station.

で marks the location of watching; に marks where the cinema exists.

## How the kanji combine

[映](../kanji/映.md), associated with projection, and [画](../kanji/画.md), "picture," form 映画, "film." [館](../kanji/館.md), "building; hall," adds the venue: えい + が + かん.

## Synonyms and antonyms

映画 names the film rather than the building. 劇場 (げきじょう), "theater," is a related venue word, particularly for stage performances. Neither is an opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

English "cinema" can mean filmmaking as an art. 映画館 specifically names a venue, not the art form or the movie industry.
