# 学術 (がくじゅつ) - scholarship; academic pursuits

## Meaning

学術 names scholarly learning and academic pursuits, including specialized research. It is a noun and appears before another noun in compounds such as 学術雑誌 (がくじゅつざっし), academic journal, and 学術研究 (がくじゅつけんきゅう), academic research.

- この図書館には学術雑誌がたくさんあります。 (このとしょかんにはがくじゅつざっしがたくさんあります。) - This library has many academic journals.
- 大学は学術研究を支援しています。 (だいがくはがくじゅつけんきゅうをしえんしています。) - The university supports academic research.

## How the kanji combine

[学](../kanji/学.md) contributes learning, and [術](../kanji/術.md) technique or skill. Together they concern learned and systematic pursuits, not merely a school exercise. The on readings are ガク + ジュツ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[学問](学問.md) (がくもん) overlaps in scholarly study or a discipline. [研究](研究.md) (けんきゅう) emphasizes investigation; [勉強](勉強.md) (べんきょう) is useful for everyday studying. These are overlapping perspectives, not a rule that homework and research can never belong to the same activity.

## Distinctive meaning

The dictionary's English "science" does not restrict 学術 to the natural sciences: scholarly work in the humanities also belongs here. [科学](科学.md) (かがく) is the separate science word. "Scholarship" here concerns learning, not the financial support named by [奨学金](奨学金.md) (しょうがくきん).
