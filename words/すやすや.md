# すやすや — sleeping peacefully

## Meaning

すやすや describes quiet, peaceful, apparently comfortable sleep.

- 赤ちゃんが母の腕の中ですやすや眠っている。 — The baby is sleeping peacefully in its mother's arms. (腕: うで.)
- 猫は日なたですやすやと寝ていた。 — The cat was sleeping peacefully in a sunny spot. (日なた: ひなた.)

Use it with a sleep verb: すやすや眠る, すやすやと眠る, or すやすや寝る. It is not an ordinary な-adjective or a general standalone verb for relaxing.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ぐっすり](ぐっすり.md) emphasizes sound or deep sleep. [うとうと](うとうと.md) describes light dozing or nodding off. The first two overlap, but their descriptive focus differs.

## Distinctive meaning

すやすや often occurs when someone observes a baby, child, or animal sleeping contentedly, but it is not grammatically restricted to those subjects. It does not say how quickly the sleeper fell asleep or specify how many hours they slept.
