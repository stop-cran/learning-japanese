# ぴりぴり — stinging; feeling on edge

## Meaning

A sharp sting or tingle, or a tense, on-edge mood.

- 辛（から）い料理で舌（した）がぴりぴりする。 — The spicy food makes my tongue tingle.
- 締め切り（しめきり）が近く、職場（しょくば）の空気がぴりぴりしている。 — With the deadline near, the atmosphere at work is tense.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ひりひり](ひりひり.md) and [ちくちく](ちくちく.md) overlap in physical stinging. [いらいら](いらいら.md) often emphasizes irritation; someone who is tense may also be irritated.

## Distinctive meaning

ぴりぴりする can describe a person or atmosphere without any physical sting. Other uses include tearing and shrill sounds. These are different scenes, not evidence that an anxious person must be making noise.
