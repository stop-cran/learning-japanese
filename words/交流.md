# 交流 (こうりゅう) - exchange; interaction; AC

## Meaning

交流 is exchange or interaction between people, groups, or cultures.
In this sense it is a noun and intransitive する-verb.
人と交流する describes interacting with someone.

- 留学生と交流しました。 (りゅうがくせいとこうりゅうしました。) - I interacted with the international students.
- 二つの町の文化交流が続いています。 (ふたつのまちのぶんかこうりゅうがつづいています。) - Cultural exchange between the two towns is continuing.

The same word is also the noun for alternating current, AC:

- この機械は交流で動きます。 (このきかいはこうりゅうでうごきます。) - This machine runs on alternating current.

The electrical sense does not describe the machine socializing.

## How the kanji combine

[交](../kanji/交.md) contributes mingling or going back and forth,
and [流](../kanji/流.md) contributes flow. Their combination supports
reciprocal contact or alternating flow. The on readings are コウ + リュウ.
This is a meaning association, not a claim about historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[交換](交換.md) (こうかん) can describe exchanging items.
[交際](交際.md) (こうさい) and [付き合い](付き合い.md) (つきあい)
overlap in social dealings; 交流 need not imply dating.
直流 (ちょくりゅう), direct current, contrasts with the electrical sense,
not with cultural interaction.

## Distinctive meaning

Social exchange need not consist of trading one object for another.
The source gloss "intercourse" is understood here as communication or
social dealings, not a teaching of sexual intercourse.
Context separates the social する use from the electrical noun;
do not automatically attach the same grammar to both.
