# 関わる (かかわる) - be involved; concern

## Meaning

関わる means to be involved in something, have to do with it, or have a serious bearing on it. **係わる** is another dictionary spelling with the same reading かかわる.

- 新しい計画に関わっています。 (あたらしいけいかくにかかわっています。) - I am involved in the new project.
- これは命に関わる問題です。 (これはいのちにかかわるもんだいです。) - This is a life-or-death matter.

It is an intransitive godan verb: the matter takes に. Learn 関わります (かかわります), 関わらない (かかわらない) and 関わって (かかわって).

## How the kanji combine

[関](../kanji/関.md) contributes connection or involvement; わる is written in kana. The alternate spelling uses [係](../kanji/係.md), also associated with connection. These are spellings of the complete native verb, not on-reading compounds.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[関する](関する.md) (かんする) means concern or relate to, as in a document concerning a subject. [関係](関係.md) (かんけい) names a relationship and also forms 関係する, "be related." [参加](参加.md)する (さんかする), "participate," focuses on taking part; involvement need not be voluntary participation.

## Distinctive meaning

In 命に関わる, someone's survival is at stake, not merely a topic being mentioned. English "concern" does not automatically mean feeling worried here.

Do not extend every dictionary sense to every spelling. A "stick to; be particular about" sense of かかわる is restricted to 拘る in the checked entry, not 関わる or 係わる. The familiar separate reading こだわる also has "be particular about" meanings; it is not a replacement pronunciation for this card.
