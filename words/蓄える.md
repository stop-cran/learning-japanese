# 蓄える (たくわえる) - store up; accumulate reserves

## Meaning

蓄える is a transitive ichidan verb for storing or saving something up,
including food, money or energy. It also describes building up knowledge,
experience or skills, and growing or wearing a beard.
貯える is another spelling recognized in the same family.

- 冬に備えて食料を蓄えます。
  (ふゆにそなえてしょくりょうをたくわえます。) - I store up food for winter.
- 彼は豊かな知識を蓄えています。
  (かれはゆたかなちしきをたくわえています。) - He has built up a wealth of knowledge.

The thing accumulated takes を. The polite form is 蓄えます,
the て-form 蓄えて, and the past 蓄えた.
ひげを蓄える means grow or wear a beard, not put a detached beard in storage.

## How the kanji combine

蓄 writes the native element たくわ, with the ending える.
The ichidan stem before ます is たくわえ. The two dictionary spellings
are recognized alternatives; that alone does not establish an absolute
rule reserving one for money and the other for every nonfinancial use.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[貯金](貯金.md) (ちょきん) specifically concerns saving money.
[保存](保存.md) (ほぞん), [しまう](しまう.md), [残す](残す.md)
(のこす), [積む](積む.md) (つむ), [積もる](積もる.md) (つもる) and
[集める](集める.md) (あつめる) have storage, setting-aside or accumulation
uses, with different constructions and emphases.
[こしらえる](こしらえる.md) also has a saving/raising-money sense.

[伸ばす](伸ばす.md) (のばす) overlaps growing a beard and developing
abilities. [伸びる](伸びる.md) (のびる), [高める](高める.md)
(たかめる) and [育てる](育てる.md) (そだてる) provide related
growth/development comparisons, not a rule of free substitution.

## Distinctive meaning

蓄える often presents something as a stock or accumulated resource.
Not every act of gathering people or preserving a document is therefore
best described with it. Use the object to identify material storage,
development of resources or abilities, or the separate beard use.
There is no single opposite that works for all three.
