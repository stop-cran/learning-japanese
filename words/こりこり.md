# こりこり — firm and crunchy; muscle stiffness

## Meaning

Firm resistance under the teeth, especially cartilage, pickles, or certain mushrooms. It can also describe firm or stiff muscles.

- このきくらげは、こりこりした歯ごたえがある。 — This wood-ear mushroom has a firm, crunchy bite.
- 肩（かた）を触る（さわる）と、筋肉（きんにく）がこりこりしていた。 — When I touched my shoulder, the muscles felt tight and hard.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[かりかり](かりかり.md) is often dry, hard crispness; [しゃきしゃき](しゃきしゃき.md) often a fresh vegetable crunch. [もちもち](もちもち.md) is elastic chewiness instead of a firm crunch.

## Distinctive meaning

こりこりした食感（しょっかん）, こりこりとした歯ごたえ（はごたえ）, and 肩がこりこりする are useful. Firmness in food can be enjoyable, whereas a stiff shoulder may be uncomfortable. A sensory description of tight muscles is not a medical diagnosis. The food prototypes differ from かりかり, but some English “crunchy” translations overlap.
