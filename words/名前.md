# 名前 (なまえ) — name

## Meaning

名前 is a person's or thing's name: お名前は何ですか (What is your name?), 名前を書く (write one's name), 犬の名前 (the dog's name).

## How the kanji combine

[名](../kanji/名.md) (name) plus [前](../kanji/前.md) (front, before). Both are read with kun'yomi: な + まえ. Learn the combination as the word "name", rather than trying to translate the two characters separately. The word is a native Japanese word (wago).

## Synonyms and antonyms

- Close: 氏名 しめい (full name on forms), 名 な／めい (name, in compounds), 呼び名 よびな (what one is called).
- Related: 名字 みょうじ／苗字 (family name), 姓 せい (surname), 下の名前 したのなまえ (given name).

## Distinctive meaning

名前 is the everyday word. On forms 氏名 is used. Politely: お名前.
