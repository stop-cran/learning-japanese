# 復習 (ふくしゅう) — study review

## Meaning

復習 is reviewing material already learned. 復習する means to review or revise in the study sense. Mark the material reviewed with を.

昨日の漢字を復習します。（きのうのかんじをふくしゅうします。） — I will review yesterday's kanji.

授業の後で復習しました。（じゅぎょうのあとでふくしゅうしました。） — I reviewed the material after class.

## How the kanji combine

復 contributes returning or repeating, and [習](../kanji/習.md) means learning or practice. The on readings are ふく + しゅう: returning to what you have learned.

## Synonyms and antonyms

予習（よしゅう） is preparation before a lesson, the useful contrast to review afterward. 練習（れんしゅう） is practice and need not revisit a previous lesson.

## Distinctive meaning

English "revision" here means studying again, not editing a draft. Also distinguish 復讐, "revenge," which has the same reading ふくしゅう but a different second kanji and meaning.
