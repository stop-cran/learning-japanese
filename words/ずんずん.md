# ずんずん — advancing steadily; booming

## Meaning

Steady, vigorous forward progress, or a low repeated booming sound.

- 彼は坂道（さかみち）をずんずん登（のぼ）っていった。 — He strode steadily up the slope.
- 太鼓（たいこ）の音がずんずん響（ひび）いてきた。 — The drumbeats came booming toward us.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[どんどん](どんどん.md) overlaps in progress and repeated sound. [ぐいぐい](ぐいぐい.md) can foreground forceful pushing or pulling; [じりじり](じりじり.md) often makes gradual progress feel slower.

## Distinctive meaning

ずんずん進（すす）む can describe more than walking. The impression of steady momentum does not tell whether the person knows the right route or whether the progress is desirable.
