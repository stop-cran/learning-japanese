# 経度 (けいど) - longitude

## Meaning

経度 is longitude: a location's angular position east or west of the reference meridian.
It is expressed in degrees, not in kilometers.

- 地図で経度を確認します。 (ちずでけいどをかくにんします。) - I will check the longitude on the map.
- この地点の経度は東経百四十度です。 (このちてんのけいどはとうけいひゃくよんじゅうどです。) - This point's longitude is 140 degrees east.

東経 (とうけい) means east longitude; 西経 (せいけい) means west longitude.

## How the kanji combine

[経](../kanji/経.md) has a longitudinal/warp-thread association;
[度](../kanji/度.md) contributes degree or measure.
As a mnemonic, connect the lengthwise meridians on a globe with their degree measurement.
This is a learning association, not a historical derivation.
The on readings are ケイ + ド.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[緯度](緯度.md) (いど), latitude, gives the north-south coordinate.
Latitude and longitude are complementary coordinates, not interchangeable names for the same measurement.
位置 (いち), "position," is the broader idea.

## Distinctive meaning

Meridians run toward the poles, but longitude measures how far **east or west** a location is.
Do not confuse the direction a line runs on a map with the direction measured by the coordinate.
経度 alone does not state a distance traveled.
