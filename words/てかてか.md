# てかてか — conspicuously shiny or oily

## Meaning

A noticeable surface shine, often from oil, grease, polishing, or wear. The word frequently implies that the sheen is excessive.

- 暑くて、額（ひたい）がてかてかしてきた。 — It was hot, and my forehead started looking shiny.
- 長年（ながねん）使った机の角（かど）が、てかてかと光っている。 — The corners of the well-used desk have a shiny sheen.

## How the kanji combine

A native kana mimetic with the repeated てか pattern. It describes appearance rather than an audible event; no kanji decomposition is needed.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[つやつや](つやつや.md) often presents luster favorably. [ぴかぴか](ぴかぴか.md) can imply clean or newly polished shine. [さらさら](さらさら.md), for hair or skin, can contrast with an oily feel, though it describes texture more than reflected light.

## Distinctive meaning

Use てかてかする, てかてかした顔, or てかてかと光る. The shine need not literally be wet or greasy: polished and worn surfaces can also qualify. It is therefore not a direct synonym for “dirty,” and its frequent negative coloring should not be promoted into an exceptionless rule.
