# 血 (ち) -- blood

## Meaning

血 is blood, the fluid circulating in the body.

指から血が出ています。 (ゆびからちがでています。) -- My finger is bleeding.

The useful pattern is body part から + 血が出る (ちがでる): blood comes out from that place. が marks the blood, and から marks its source. Blood stopping is 血が止まる (ちがとまる).

## How the kanji combine

The single character 血 writes the native noun ち. In compounds it often has the on reading けつ, as in 血液 (けつえき), blood. Do not substitute that compound reading when saying the standalone word taught here.

## Synonyms and antonyms

血液 is a more technical or medical term for the same substance. 涙 (なみだ), tears, and 汗 (あせ), sweat, are other bodily fluids, not synonyms or opposites.

## Distinctive meaning

English "bleed" is often expressed through a phrase such as 血が出る rather than by treating 血 as a verb. 血です means "It is blood"; it does not mean "It is bleeding."
