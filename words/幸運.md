# 幸運 (こううん) - good luck; good fortune

## Meaning

幸運 is a noun for good luck and a na-adjective meaning "fortunate; lucky." Use **幸運な + noun**, or **幸運にも** (こううんにも), "fortunately."

- 幸運にも、最後の切符が買えました。 (こううんにも、さいごのきっぷがかえました。) - Fortunately, I was able to buy the last ticket.
- こんな機会に恵まれて、幸運です。 (こんなきかいにめぐまれて、こううんです。) - I am fortunate to have this opportunity.

**幸運を祈ります** (こううんをいのります) means "I wish you good luck," literally expressing a wish for someone's good fortune.

## How the kanji combine

[幸](../kanji/幸.md), "fortunate; happy," qualifies [運](../kanji/運.md), "luck; fortune." コウ + ウン gives こううん. Keep both the long こう and the following う in the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[運](運.md) (うん) is luck more generally; 運がいい means "be lucky." [幸福](幸福.md) (こうふく) concerns happiness or well-being rather than just a fortunate turn of events. 不運 (ふうん), "bad luck; misfortune," is a direct contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

幸運 presents a result or circumstance as favorable, often beyond what the person could fully control. It does not deny effort: someone can work hard and still describe a timely opportunity as 幸運. Do not confuse the adjective pattern 幸運な with an i-adjective ending.
