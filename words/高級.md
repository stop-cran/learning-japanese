# 高級 (こうきゅう) - high-grade; luxury

## Meaning

高級 describes a high grade, superior quality, or luxury class.
In this use it is a noun and can modify with な or の; it also occurs
directly before another noun in compounds such as 高級車 (こうきゅうしゃ),
"luxury car."

- 高級な食材を使った料理です。 (こうきゅうなしょくざいをつかったりょうりです。) - It is a dish made with high-quality ingredients.
- あの店は高級品を扱っています。 (あのみせはこうきゅうひんをあつかっています。) - That shop handles high-grade goods.

An additional dictionary sense concerns high rank or level, as in
高級官僚 (こうきゅうかんりょう), "a senior bureaucrat."
JMdict marks that rank sense as a noun / の-modifier, rather than
automatically giving it the quality sense's na-adjective classification.

## How the kanji combine

[高](../kanji/高.md) contributes high, and 級 contributes class, grade,
or rank. Together they mean a high position on a scale of quality or rank.
The on readings are コウ + キュウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上等](上等.md) (じょうとう) overlaps in superior quality.
[豪華](豪華.md) (ごうか) emphasizes lavishness;
[贅沢](贅沢.md) (ぜいたく) concerns luxury or extravagance.
[高価](高価.md) (こうか) specifically concerns a high price.

## Distinctive meaning

Expensive, lavish, and high-quality often go together but are not
identical judgments. 高級 does not supply a price or guarantee that
an item suits someone's needs. A senior official is not a luxury object.
The N2 mapping here selects the quality sense; the rank sense is
additional teaching, not a separately established N2 assignment.
