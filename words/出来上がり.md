# 出来上がり (できあがり) - completion; finished result

## Meaning

出来上がり is a noun for completion or a finished state. It also names how something has turned out: its finish, workmanship, or resulting quality.

- 写真の出来上がりは明日です。
  (しゃしんのできあがりはあしたです。) - The photos will be ready tomorrow.
- この服は出来上がりがきれいです。
  (このふくはできあがりがきれいです。) - This garment is beautifully finished.

The first example concerns when something will be finished; the second evaluates the finished result. 出来上がりを確認する (できあがりをかくにんする) means "check the finished result."

## How the kanji combine

[出](../kanji/出.md) and [来](../kanji/来.md) form 出来 (でき), related to [できる](できる.md). [上](../kanji/上.md), in 上がり (あがり), contributes reaching a finished state here rather than literal upward movement. The related verb is 出来上がる (できあがる), "be finished; be ready."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[完成](完成.md) (かんせい) emphasizes completion of a work or whole. [仕上がる](仕上がる.md) (しあがる) describes something becoming finished. [結果](結果.md) (けっか) is a broader result or outcome, not necessarily a manufactured or prepared finish.

## Distinctive meaning

出来上がりです can announce "It is ready," but 出来上がり is still a noun followed by です. Do not treat it as a general verb meaning "be finished," or translate it as "be suited for" simply because a source list has a loose English gloss. A finished result can be evaluated favorably or unfavorably; completion alone is not a guarantee of quality.
