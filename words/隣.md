# 隣 (となり) — next door; next to

## Meaning

隣 names an adjacent place or the person or house next door. Use Aの隣 for "next to A" and 隣の + noun for "the neighboring ...".

銀行の隣に郵便局があります。 (ぎんこうのとなりにゆうびんきょくがあります。) — There is a post office next to the bank.

隣の人にあいさつしました。 (となりのひとにあいさつしました。) — I greeted the person next to me.

## How the kanji combine

隣 is a single kanji read となり. The kana reading is learned as a whole; no second written component supplies the final り.

## Synonyms and antonyms

近く (ちかく) means nearby, without necessarily being directly adjacent. 横 (よこ) means the side or a position beside something. These can overlap with 隣 but emphasize different spatial relations.

## Distinctive meaning

隣 often locates the next member of a row or arrangement, not merely anything close by. Japanese uses this positional noun with particles, not an English-style preposition: 私の隣に座ってください (わたしのとなりにすわってください), "Please sit next to me."
