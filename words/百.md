# 百 (ひゃく) - hundred

## Meaning

百 is one hundred. It combines with counters or units rather than acting as the ordinary noun for a particular group of objects.

- このペンは百円です。 (このペンはひゃくえんです。) - This pen costs one hundred yen.
- 会場に百人います。 (かいじょうにひゃくにんいます。) - There are one hundred people at the venue.

Ordinary counting uses 百, not a required 一百 for one hundred.

## How the kanji combine

The single character [百](../kanji/百.md) is read ひゃく. The small ゃ forms the combined sound ひゃ, not separate ひ and や syllables.

## Synonyms and antonyms

１００ is the Arabic-numeral representation of the same number. 十 (じゅう), "ten," and 千 (せん), "thousand," are neighboring decimal units, not antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

Multiples change pronunciation in several familiar cases: 三百 is さんびゃく, 六百 is ろっぴゃく, and 八百 is はっぴゃく. Learn these whole forms instead of attaching ひゃく unchanged to every number.
