# すっきり — refreshed; clear and uncluttered

## Meaning

すっきり describes feeling refreshed or relieved, a clear or uncluttered appearance, or something becoming satisfactorily resolved.

- シャワーを浴びたら、すっきりした。 — I felt refreshed after taking a shower. (浴びる: あびる.)
- 机の上を片づけて、部屋がすっきりした。 — Tidying the desktop made the room look uncluttered. (片づける: かたづける.)
- 説明を聞いても、疑問がすっきり解けない。 — Even after hearing the explanation, my questions are not fully resolved. (疑問: ぎもん; 解ける: とける.)

Use すっきりする, すっきりした部屋, or すっきり（と） before a suitable verb. すっきりした is useful before nouns; do not substitute な automatically.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[もやもや](もやもや.md) can contrast with unresolved unease. [ぼんやり](ぼんやり.md) contrasts with clarity in some contexts. [しっかり](しっかり.md) emphasizes firmness or reliable execution, not necessarily a refreshed feeling.

## Distinctive meaning

すっきりしない can mean that something still feels unresolved or unsatisfactory. It need not refer to physical dirt or illness. The dictionary also records thoroughness and negative-polarity uses; do not force the translation "refreshed" into every construction.
