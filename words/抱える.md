# 抱える (かかえる) - hold in one's arms; be burdened with

## Meaning

抱える is a transitive ichidan verb. It describes carrying or holding something in one's arms or under an arm, and also having a burden such as a problem or debt.

- 彼女は大きな箱を抱えています。 (かのじょはおおきなはこをかかえています。) - She is holding a large box in her arms.
- 一人で問題を抱えないでください。 (ひとりでもんだいをかかえないでください。) - Please don't shoulder the problem alone.

Both physical and figurative objects take を. 抱えている can describe the continuing state of holding something or living with a difficulty.

## How the kanji combine

[抱](../kanji/抱.md) contributes holding or embracing. Here the native verb is かかえる, with える written in kana. The character also appears in other verbs, so its presence alone does not determine the reading.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[持つ](持つ.md) (もつ) is broader: hold, carry, or possess. 抱える gives a more specific image of holding with the arms, or bearing something as a responsibility. 放す (はなす), "let go," contrasts with physical holding but is not a universal opposite for every figurative use.

## Distinctive meaning

抱える does not simply mean "hug affectionately." The object can be a bulky box, work to manage, or a problem causing concern. In the second example, there is no physical holding; the armful image helps explain the sense of carrying a burden.
