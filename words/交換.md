# 交換 (こうかん) - exchange; replacement

## Meaning

交換 is exchanging things or replacing one thing with another. It is a noun and transitive する verb. Use AをBと交換する, "exchange A for B," or simply Nを交換する when the replacement is understood.

- 友達と電話番号を交換しました。 (ともだちとでんわばんごうをこうかんしました。) - I exchanged phone numbers with a friend.
- 時計の電池を交換してください。 (とけいのでんちをこうかんしてください。) - Please replace the watch battery.

In the first example, と marks the exchange partner; the numbers take を. In the second, the old battery is replaced, not traded back and forth.

## How the kanji combine

[交](../kanji/交.md) contributes exchange or interaction, and 換 contributes changing or substituting. Together they describe an exchange or replacement. The on readings are コウ + カン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[取り替える](取り替える.md) (とりかえる) overlaps with replacing an item. [変える](変える.md) (かえる), "change," is broader: changing a plan need not involve exchanging two things.

## Distinctive meaning

交換 works with intangible information as well as objects: 意見を交換する (いけんをこうかんする) means "exchange opinions." It does not imply that the participants agree afterward. The shared idea is something being exchanged or substituted, not any change whatever.
