# だらだら — trickling; dragging on; idling

## Meaning

Liquid flowing in a continuing trickle, or activity continuing slackly or at unnecessary length.

- 暑くて、汗（あせ）がだらだら流（なが）れた。 — It was hot and sweat streamed down me.
- 会議（かいぎ）がだらだら続（つづ）いた。 — The meeting dragged on.
- 休日は家でだらだら過（す）ごした。 — I spent the day off idling at home.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ずるずる](ずるずる.md) and [ぐずぐず](ぐずぐず.md) overlap in delay or drawn-out activity. [ぽたぽた](ぽたぽた.md) often presents more distinct drops.

## Distinctive meaning

だらだら話す can sound critical of long, unfocused talk, while a person may casually describe their own lazy day this way. The dictionary also records a gently sloping shape; not every use criticizes behavior.
