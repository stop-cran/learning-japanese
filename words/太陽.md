# 太陽 (たいよう) — the sun

## Meaning

太陽 names the sun.
It is useful when the sun itself is the subject, rather than the date, the daytime, or sunlight falling on something.

- 太陽が雲の間から見えました。 (たいようがくものあいだからみえました。) — The sun appeared through a gap in the clouds.
- 太陽は東から昇ります。 (たいようはひがしからのぼります。) — The sun rises in the east.

## How the kanji combine

[太](../kanji/太.md) has a "great; large" sense in this compound, rather than the thickness meaning of 太い (ふとい).
[陽](../kanji/陽.md) supplies the association with the sun and its light.
Treat the first character as qualifying the second, while learning the whole word as the established name for the sun.
The on readings are タイ + ヨウ; the final よう has a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[日](../kanji/日.md) (ひ) can also mean the sun, but additionally means a day or daytime, depending on context.
[日光](日光.md) (にっこう) is sunlight: the light from the sun rather than the sun as an object.
There is no direct antonym needed for this noun.

## Distinctive meaning

太陽 avoids the day-versus-sun ambiguity of 日.
The two nouns can refer to the same sun, but that does not make them interchangeable in every fixed expression.
For describing light entering a room, 日光 may be the more precise noun; for the object emerging from behind clouds, 太陽 is clear.
