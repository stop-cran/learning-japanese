# 塗る (ぬる) - to apply; paint a surface

## Meaning

塗る means to spread or apply a material over a surface: paint, cream, plaster, and similar coatings. One useful pattern is surface + に + material + を塗る.

- 手にクリームを塗りました。 (てにクリームをぬりました。) - I applied cream to my hands.
- 壁を白く塗りました。 (かべをしろくぬりました。) - I painted the wall white.

The second pattern makes the surface the object with を; 白く describes the resulting color. 壁にしっくいを塗る (かべにしっくいをぬる) means "plaster a wall."

It is a transitive godan verb: 塗ります, 塗らない, 塗って.

## How the kanji combine

塗 contributes covering a surface. The kana る completes the native verb. The verb's ます-stem is ぬり, even though only ぬ is represented by the kanji in the dictionary form.

## Synonyms and antonyms

描く (かく) means draw or paint a picture. 塗る instead focuses on applying material or color. はがす, "peel off," may describe removing a coating, but is not a universal opposite.

## Distinctive meaning

English "paint" can mean creating artwork or coating a wall. 塗る suits the coating action; painting a picture is ordinarily 絵を描く (えをかく).
