# レストラン — restaurant

## Meaning

レストラン means a restaurant, especially a Western-style dining establishment, although actual cuisine and naming vary.

- 駅の前のレストランで昼ご飯を食べました。 (えきのまえのれすとらんでひるごはんをたべました。) — I had lunch at the restaurant in front of the station.
- このレストランは何時に開きますか。 (このれすとらんはなんじにあきますか。) — What time does this restaurant open?

Use で for eating there and に for going there: レストランに行く (れすとらんにいく).

## How the kanji combine

レストラン is written in katakana. JMdict identifies its source language as French. There are no written kanji parts and no long-vowel mark in this word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

食堂 (しょくどう) can mean a dining hall, cafeteria, or eating place. 喫茶店 (きっさてん) emphasizes a coffee shop or tearoom. 飲食店 (いんしょくてん) is a broader term for establishments serving food and drink.

## Distinctive meaning

レストラン is useful for dining out, but it is not the only Japanese name for a place that serves meals. A school cafeteria is usually better identified as a 食堂 than automatically called a レストラン.
