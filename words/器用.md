# 器用 (きよう) - dexterous; skillful

## Meaning

器用 is a な-adjective describing skillful, deft handling of tasks, especially work with the hands. Use 器用な人 (きようなひと), "a dexterous person," or 器用に + verb, "deftly..."

- 姉は手先が器用です。 (あねはてさきがきようです。) - My older sister is good with her hands.
- 彼は紙を器用に折って、鳥を作りました。 (かれはかみをきようにおって、とりをつくりました。) - He deftly folded the paper into a bird.

手先が器用 highlights fine manual skill. 器用 can also describe being adept at managing different practical tasks; it is not limited to a particular craft.

## How the kanji combine

器 has associations with implements and capacity; [用](../kanji/用.md) concerns use. Together they form the established word for practical adeptness. Treat that relationship as a memory aid, not a literal definition of someone using a container.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[上手](上手.md) (じょうず) broadly evaluates doing something well. 器用 draws attention to deftness or versatile handling rather than simply a good result.

不器用 (ぶきよう), "clumsy; awkward," is the direct contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

[得意](得意.md) (とくい) identifies a strong area or something someone feels confident doing. 器用 is not a substitute for every "good at": for an academic strength, use a construction such as 数学が得意です (すうがくがとくいです), "I'm good at mathematics," rather than assuming manual dexterity.
