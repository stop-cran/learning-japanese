# ハイキング (ハイキング) - hiking

## Meaning

ハイキング is a noun for hiking. Use ハイキングに行く
for going on a hike, or ハイキングをする for doing the
activity. A route can take を when it is the path traversed.

- 日曜日に友達とハイキングに行きます。 (にちようびにともだちとハイキングにいきます。) - I am going hiking with friends on Sunday.
- 湖の周りをハイキングしました。 (みずうみのまわりをハイキングしました。) - We hiked around the lake.

と marks companions in the first example. The second を
marks the route around the lake, not an object changed by the action.
The past form with する is ハイキングした.

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana loanword from English "hiking." There are
no kanji to combine. The ending グ belongs to the borrowed
word; Japanese tense and politeness come from する, します,
and related forms.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[散歩](散歩.md) (さんぽ) is a walk or stroll.
[登山](登山.md) (とざん) focuses on ascending a mountain.
The activities can overlap: ハイキング need not mean reaching
a summit, and a hike can include mountain paths.

## Distinctive meaning

ハイキング names the outdoor recreational activity rather than
every instance of [歩く](歩く.md) (あるく), "walk."
The word alone does not establish distance, difficulty, equipment,
or safety. Those details come from the actual route and conditions,
not from a rigid boundary between English activity labels.
