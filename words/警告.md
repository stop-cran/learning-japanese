# 警告 (けいこく) - warning; caution

## Meaning

警告 is a warning or caution about danger, a problem, or consequences. 警告する is a transitive する verb. Use **person に警告する** for the recipient, and **clause と警告する** to state the warning's content.

- 警察は住民に危険を警告しました。 (けいさつはじゅうみんにきけんをけいこくしました。) - The police warned residents of the danger.
- 画面に警告が出ています。 (がめんにけいこくがでています。) - A warning is showing on the screen.

The noun also fits 警告を受ける (けいこくをうける), "receive a warning." A warning may concern misconduct, but the word does not itself mean that a penalty has already been imposed.

## How the kanji combine

[警](../kanji/警.md) contributes alertness or warning; [告](../kanji/告.md) contributes telling or informing. Together they express informing someone so that they take warning. The on readings are ケイ + コク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[注意](注意.md) (ちゅうい) includes attention and caution; 注意する can overlap with warning someone. 忠告 (ちゅうこく), "advice; admonition," can also overlap, but ordinary helpful advice is not automatically a 警告.

## Distinctive meaning

Translate 警告 as "warning," not as a general-purpose "advice." Advice about choosing a pleasant restaurant need not warn of anything. 警告 draws attention to something the recipient should avoid or take seriously, whether spoken by a person or displayed by a device.
