# 区分 (くぶん) - division into sections; classification

## Meaning

区分 means dividing something into sections, the resulting section, or classifying things into categories. It is a noun and can take する.

- ごみを種類ごとに区分します。 (ごみをしゅるいごとにくぶんします。) - We sort the waste by type.
- この表は年齢による区分を示しています。 (このひょうはねんれいによるくぶんをしめしています。) - This table shows the divisions by age.

With する, the thing divided takes を; に can introduce the resulting categories: 三つに区分する (みっつにくぶんする), divide into three sections.

## How the kanji combine

[区](../kanji/区.md) (ク) concerns a delimited division; [分](../kanji/分.md) (ブン) contributes dividing or parts. The characters reinforce a division/classification idea, rather than naming an undivided whole.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[区切る](区切る.md) (くぎる) marks off sections or boundaries. 区分 can describe classification into categories as well as division into sections.

[区別](区別.md) (くべつ) is distinguishing things or recognizing their difference. 区分 focuses on the divisions or grouping, although the uses can overlap. [分ける](分ける.md) (わける) is a broader native verb for dividing or sharing.

## Distinctive meaning

A 区分 need not be a geographic district: age groups, types of waste and divisions of a document are possible. Do not infer a fixed physical size or an official administrative status from 区 alone.
