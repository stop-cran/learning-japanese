# 恨み (うらみ) - resentment; grudge

## Meaning

恨み is resentment, bitterness or a grudge. It is a noun, often involving
someone believed to have caused an injury or wrong.
人に恨みを持つ means "bear a grudge against someone."

- 私は彼に恨みを持っていません。 (わたしはかれにうらみをもっていません。) - I do not bear a grudge against him.
- 昔の恨みを忘れるのは難しい。 (むかしのうらみをわすれるのはむずかしい。) - It is difficult to forget an old grudge.

## How the kanji combine

恨 writes the native element うら, followed by み in this noun.
The related verb is [恨む](恨む.md) (うらむ). There is no two-character
compound to explain.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不満](不満.md) (ふまん) is dissatisfaction or discontent; it need not
involve a lasting personal grudge. [許す](許す.md) (ゆるす) can mean
"forgive," a contrasting response rather than a noun meaning the opposite
of every 恨み.

## Distinctive meaning

恨み is not simply being momentarily annoyed. It draws attention to
resentment being held, without by itself saying that the resentment is
justified or that revenge will follow. 怨み is another dictionary-recognized
spelling for this resentment use.
