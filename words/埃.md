# 埃 (ほこり) - dust

## Meaning

埃 is dust, including fine particles that settle on surfaces or float in the air. It is usually written ほこり or ホコリ. Useful phrases include ほこりがたまる, "dust accumulates," and ほこりを払う (ほこりをはらう), "dust off."

- 棚の上にほこりがたまっています。 (たなのうえにほこりがたまっています。) - Dust has accumulated on the shelf.
- 本のほこりを払いました。 (ほんのほこりをはらいました。) - I dusted off the book.

ほこりっぽい means "dusty," describing a place or atmosphere with noticeable dust.

## How the kanji combine

The single kanji 埃 represents the whole native noun ほこり. There is no multi-kanji compound to analyze. Kana remains useful because the dictionary marks this word as usually written without kanji.

## Synonyms and antonyms

ちり also means dust or fine particles. ごみ is broader rubbish or waste and can include much larger objects. [泥](泥.md) (どろ), "mud," is a different material, not simply another spelling of dust.

## Distinctive meaning

Distinguish 埃 from [誇り](誇り.md) (ほこり), "pride." They share a pronunciation but have unrelated uses. 埃を払う removes dust; 誇りを持つ (ほこりをもつ) means having pride. Context is especially important when both are written in kana. Dust is not necessarily dirt in large visible pieces: a fine layer on an unused shelf also counts.
