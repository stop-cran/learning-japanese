# 預かる (あずかる) - look after; keep in safekeeping

## Meaning

預かる means taking care of someone or holding something entrusted to you.
It is a **transitive godan verb**, despite ending in る:
預かります (あずかります), 預かって (あずかって), 預かった (あずかった).
The person or thing looked after takes を.

- 受付で荷物を預かります。 (うけつけでにもつをあずかります。) - We will hold your luggage at reception.
- 週末は友達の猫を預かりました。 (しゅうまつはともだちのねこをあずかりました。) - I looked after a friend's cat over the weekend.

店を預かる (みせをあずかる) can mean being entrusted with running a shop.

## How the kanji combine

[預](../kanji/預.md) concerns entrusting or custody. Here it is read あず,
with かる completing the native verb. Learn its conjugation alongside the whole word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[預ける](預ける.md) (あずける) presents the other side: leaving something in another's care.
Compare 友達に猫を預ける (ともだちにねこをあずける), "leave a cat with a friend,"
with 友達の猫を預かる (ともだちのねこをあずかる), "look after a friend's cat."
[世話](世話.md) (せわ) concerns care or assistance more broadly.

## Distinctive meaning

預ける and 預かる are both transitive, not a transitive/intransitive pair.
The difference is who entrusts and who accepts responsibility.
Holding someone's belongings in safekeeping is not a gift of ownership to the keeper.
