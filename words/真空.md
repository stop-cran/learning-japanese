# 真空 (しんくう) - vacuum

## Meaning

真空 is a vacuum. In technical use, this can mean a state with pressure
sufficiently low compared with its surroundings; it need not be a perfect
absence of all matter. It is a noun.

- 容器の中を真空にしました。 (ようきのなかをしんくうにしました。) - We created a vacuum inside the container.
- 真空の状態を保ちます。 (しんくうのじょうたいをたもちます。) - We maintain the vacuum.

Nを真空にする means to put N into a vacuum state.
真空の状態 is "a state of vacuum."

## How the kanji combine

[真](../kanji/真.md) contributes true or complete, and [空](../kanji/空.md)
contributes emptiness. The modifier-and-head image is useful, but it does
not make every engineering vacuum a completely matter-free space.
The on readings are シン + クウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[空っぽ](空っぽ.md) (からっぽ) means empty or hollow.
An ordinary empty bottle can still contain air: being 空っぽ is not by
itself evidence that the bottle contains a physical vacuum.
空間 (くうかん) means space, which need not be a vacuum either.

## Distinctive meaning

The starting point here is the physical vacuum sense.
Daijisen also records figurative emptiness or inactivity; those extensions
do not turn every use of English "empty" into 真空.
JMdict's separate Buddhist absolute-void sense is not the technical meaning
taught above. Check the context rather than translating the characters alone.
