# ボーナス - bonus

## Meaning

ボーナス is a bonus: an additional payment or other extra benefit. At work it often refers to a bonus paid separately from ordinary wages, but the word is not limited to employment.

- 夏のボーナスでパソコンを買いました。 (なつのボーナスでパソコンをかいました。) - I bought a computer with my summer bonus.
- このゲームにはボーナスポイントがあります。 - This game has bonus points.

It is a noun: ボーナスが出る (ボーナスがでる), "a bonus is paid," and ボーナスをもらう, "receive a bonus." ボーナストラック is a bonus track on a recording. JMdict also records ボナス.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[給料](給料.md) (きゅうりょう) means salary or wages; [給与](給与.md) (きゅうよ) is broader pay or provision of money or goods. [賞金](賞金.md) (しょうきん) is prize money, such as money won in a competition.

## Distinctive meaning

ボーナス foregrounds something extra. It does not guarantee a particular amount, schedule, or entitlement, and bonus points are not wages. Do not turn the noun into a general する-verb merely because the English word can be used in business contexts.
