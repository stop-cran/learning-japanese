# 用心 (ようじん) - caution; precaution

## Meaning

用心 is caution or taking precautions against a possible problem. It combines with する. A hazard can take に, and 用心して describes acting cautiously.

- 夜道では車に用心してください。 (よみちではくるまにようじんしてください。) - Please watch out for cars on the road at night.
- 念のために用心して、ドアの鍵を確認しました。 (ねんのためにようじんして、ドアのかぎをかくにんしました。) - As a precaution, I checked the door lock.

The second sentence describes guarding against a possibility, not a claim that an accident or intrusion has happened.

## How the kanji combine

[用](../kanji/用.md) concerns using or applying; [心](../kanji/心.md) concerns the mind or attention. The combination suggests applying one's attention carefully. The readings are ヨウ + ジン, with the voiced じ sound in the second element.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[注意](注意.md) (ちゅうい) broadly covers attention and caution. 用心 highlights being on guard against something undesirable. 油断 (ゆだん), letting one's guard down, provides a useful contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

English "care" can mean affection, medical treatment, or maintenance, but 用心 concerns precaution. 用心のために (ようじんのために), "as a precaution," is therefore different from saying that you do something because you care affectionately about a person.
