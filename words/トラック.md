# トラック (トラック) - truck; running track

## Meaning

トラック names two distinct things covered here: a truck or lorry, and a running track. Both are nouns with the same Japanese spelling and reading.

- トラックが荷物を運んでいます。 (トラックがにもつをはこんでいます。) - A truck is carrying goods.
- 選手たちはトラックを走っています。 (せんしゅたちはトラックをはしっています。) - The athletes are running on the track.

In the first example, the vehicle is the subject. In the second, を marks the route or area traversed, not a vehicle being operated. A truck can take the vehicle particle に in トラックに乗る (トラックにのる), "ride in a truck."

## How the kanji combine

This is a katakana spelling with no kanji components. English "truck" and "track" correspond to the same Japanese トラック. The small ッ marks a brief consonant hold before ク; it is not an extra full ツ syllable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[車](車.md) (くるま) is a broader vehicle term in the truck context. [道路](道路.md) (どうろ) is a road, not specifically an athletics track. These comparisons belong to different identities, and neither identity has a natural direct antonym.

## Distinctive meaning

The source lists truck and running track together, but the supplied vehicle entry does not contain the track sense. The truck is JMdict entry 1085760, sense 1; the running track is entry 2850185, sense 1. Context resolves the homograph. Their inclusion on one article is not evidence of a single dictionary entry or separate sense-level JLPT assignments.
