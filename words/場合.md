# 場合 (ばあい) - case; situation

## Meaning

場合 names a case, situation, or set of circumstances. The pattern 場合は
often states what applies "in the case that..." or "if..."

- 雨の場合は、家で練習します。 (あめのばあいは、いえでれんしゅうします。) - If it rains, we will practice at home.
- 遅れる場合は、電話してください。 (おくれるばあいは、でんわしてください。) - If you are going to be late, please call.

## How the kanji combine

[場](../kanji/場.md), "place; situation," joins 合い, related to
[合](../kanji/合.md), "fit; come together." Learn the native reading ばあい,
with two successive あ sounds, rather than guessing on-readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

時 (とき), "time; when," can describe when something happens. 場合 frames it
as a particular case, often for instructions or decisions. 状況 (じょうきょう)
names circumstances but does not replace every conditional pattern.

## Distinctive meaning

Attachment follows noun-modification patterns: nouns take の, na-adjectives
take な, and i-adjectives and plain-form verbs attach directly.
Examples include 必要な場合 (ひつようなばあい), "if necessary," and
安い場合 (やすいばあい), "if it is cheap." Do not omit these connectors.
