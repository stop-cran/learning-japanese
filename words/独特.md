# 独特 (どくとく) - distinctive; characteristic

## Meaning

独特 describes a distinctive or characteristic quality. Both 独特な and
独特の can modify a noun. **N独特の** identifies something characteristic
of N, such as その地方独特の料理 (そのちほうどくとくのりょうり),
"cuisine characteristic of that region."

- このお茶には独特の香りがあります。 (このおちゃにはどくとくのかおりがあります。) - This tea has a distinctive aroma.
- 彼女は独特な話し方をします。 (かのじょはどくとくなはなしかたをします。) - She has a distinctive way of speaking.

Neither sentence by itself says whether the speaker likes the quality.

## How the kanji combine

独 contributes being individual or separate, and [特](../kanji/特.md)
contributes being special or distinctive. The meanings reinforce an individual
characteristic. The on readings are どく + とく.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[特別](特別.md) (とくべつ) means special or exceptional and overlaps in
some descriptions. [特徴](特徴.md) (とくちょう) is a characteristic or feature,
so it names what is distinctive rather than replacing the modifier unchanged.
[普通](普通.md) (ふつう), "ordinary," can contrast in a particular comparison.

## Distinctive meaning

English "unique" can imply that exactly one instance exists. 独特 often focuses
instead on a recognizable character, including a quality shared by a region
or group. Do not force a numerical uniqueness claim into every example.
The dictionary's separate "understood only by oneself" sense is restricted to
the spelling 独得; it is not a second meaning to assign freely to 独特.
