# 点数 (てんすう) - score; number of items

## Meaning

点数 is a numerical score or number of points, for example in a test or game.
It can also mean the number of items or articles.

- テストの点数が上がりました。 (テストのてんすうがあがりました。) - My test score went up.
- 出品する作品の点数を確認しました。 (しゅっぴんするさくひんのてんすうをかくにんしました。) - I checked the number of works to be exhibited.

点数が高い／低い (てんすうがたかい／ひくい) describes a high or low score.
In the second example, counting works does not evaluate their quality.

## How the kanji combine

[点](../kanji/点.md) supplies points or counted items, and [数](../kanji/数.md) their number.
The on readings are テン + スウ.
The two uses depend on what kind of 点 is being counted.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[点](点.md) (てん) itself can mean a point or score.
[成績](成績.md) (せいせき) is a performance record or results, not necessarily one numerical mark.
[数](数.md) (かず) is the broader word for number or quantity.

## Distinctive meaning

Context separates evaluation from counting objects.
商品の点数 (しょうひんのてんすう) can concern how many products there are,
not their review ratings. Do not assume that every use is a school grade.
There is no general antonym covering both meanings.
