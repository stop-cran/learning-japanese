# 忠実 (ちゅうじつ) — faithful; loyal

## Meaning

忠実 describes doing one’s duty with sincere devotion, and also reproducing something as it is, without changing or leaving anything out. Digital Daijisen gives 職務に忠実な人 (しょくむにちゅうじつなひと), “a person faithful to their duties,” and 原文に忠実な翻訳 (げんぶんにちゅうじつなほんやく), “a translation faithful to the original text.”

- 彼女は仕事に忠実で、決まりを一度も破ったことがない。 (かのじょはしごとにちゅうじつで、きまりをいちどもやぶったことがない。) — She is faithful to her work and has never once broken the rules.
- この映画は、原作の小説に忠実に作られている。 (このえいがは、げんさくのしょうせつにちゅうじつにつくられている。) — This film is made faithfully to the original novel.

## How the kanji combine

[忠](../kanji/忠.md) (ちゅう) is loyalty or devotion, and [実](../kanji/実.md) (じつ) here is sincerity; Digital Daijisen lists 忠実 under that sense of 実. Use に to mark what someone or something is faithful to: 仕事に忠実, 原作に忠実.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[正直](正直.md) (しょうじき) is honesty, and [まじめ](まじめ.md) is earnestness. [正確](正確.md) (せいかく) is accuracy; 忠実 adds the idea of staying true to a duty or to an original.

## Distinctive meaning

The same spelling 忠実 is also used for まめ, a separate word meaning diligent or hardworking, which JMdict marks as usually written in kana. This article is always read ちゅうじつ. A related word is 忠誠 (ちゅうせい), “loyalty; allegiance.”
