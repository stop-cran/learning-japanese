# 勢い (いきおい) - force; momentum

## Meaning

勢い is a noun for force, vigor, or momentum. It can describe moving water, an energetic person, or the progress of a team or movement. Use 勢いがある, "have momentum," and 勢いよく, "forcefully; vigorously."

- 水が勢いよく流れています。 (みずがいきおいよくながれています。) - The water is flowing forcefully.
- 二連勝して、チームに勢いがつきました。 (にれんしょうして、チームにいきおいがつきました。) - After two consecutive wins, the team gained momentum.

勢いがつく means to gain momentum; 勢いを失う (いきおいをうしなう) means to lose it. These patterns describe a change in how strongly something is moving or progressing.

## How the kanji combine

勢 represents force or vigor, with い completing the spelling of the native noun いきおい. Despite ending in い, 勢い is not an i-adjective: say 勢いがある, not a made-up adjective conjugation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[力](力.md) (ちから) is broader power or strength. [元気](元気.md) (げんき) describes liveliness or well-being. 勢い instead highlights the force or momentum of activity. 勢いが弱まる (いきおいがよわまる), "the momentum weakens," expresses the relevant contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

勢いで can mean "carried along by the moment" or "on impulse." For example, 勢いで買ってしまった (いきおいでかってしまった) means "I bought it on impulse." That is not the same as carefully deciding that the purchase was necessary.
