# 掛かる (かかる) - take time; cost money

## Meaning

In this use, 掛かる means that something requires time, money, or another resource. The resource takes が, not を. A measured amount can appear directly before the verb.

駅まで歩いて十分かかります。 (えきまであるいてじゅっぷんかかります。) - It takes ten minutes to walk to the station.

修理に五千円かかりました。 (しゅうりにごせんえんかかりました。) - The repair cost five thousand yen.

It is an intransitive godan verb: かかります, かかって, かからない.

## How the kanji combine

[掛](../kanji/掛.md) is the written component; かる is okurigana. The spelling かかる in kana is usual for the resource sense and is used in the examples.

## Synonyms and antonyms

必要 (ひつよう), "necessary," expresses a requirement more generally. お金を使う (おかねをつかう), "spend money," describes someone's action instead of a cost arising.

## Distinctive meaning

Compare 時間がかかる (じかんがかかる), "take time," with 時間をかける (じかんをかける), "spend time on something." 掛かる has other uses, including "hang," but the surrounding nouns determine the meaning; it is not a generic translation of English "take."
