# 身分 (みぶん) -- social status; identity; circumstances

## Meaning

身分 can mean social standing, status, position or rank. It also covers
identity or origins, and one's circumstances or means. Context decides
whether the issue is who someone is, their standing or how they live.

- 社会的な身分で人を判断してはいけません。 (しゃかいてきなみぶんでひとをはんだんしてはいけません。) -- You must not judge people by their social status.
- 身分を証明する書類が必要です。 (みぶんをしょうめいするしょるいがひつようです。) -- A document proving your identity is required.

身分に合った暮らし (みぶんにあったくらし) describes a way of
living suited to one's circumstances or means.

## How the kanji combine

身 contributes the person or oneself; [分](../kanji/分.md) contributes
a part or allotted position. Learn the compound reading みぶん; it is
not the time-unit reading ふん. These associations help recall the word,
not reconstruct its history.

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [地位](地位.md) (ちい) and [立場](立場.md) (たちば) overlap in position or standing, with 立場 also meaning a standpoint.
- [生まれ](生まれ.md) (うまれ) and [出身](出身.md) (しゅっしん) concern birth or origin, not every present social status.
- [事情](事情.md) (じじょう) and [都合](都合.md) (つごう) overlap in circumstances.
- [氏名](氏名.md) (しめい) means a full name. It identifies someone by name, rather than covering every meaning of 身分.

## Distinctive meaning

身分証明書 (みぶんしょうめいしょ) is an identification document:
the 身分 here is not simply an assertion of high social rank. Do not
translate every occurrence as class status, or every identity question
as a request for someone's financial means.
