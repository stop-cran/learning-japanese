# 表 (おもて) - front; outward-facing side

## Meaning

表 is the front, face, or outward-facing surface of something. With a building, it can refer to the front or the outside.

紙の表に名前を書いてください。 (かみのおもてになまえをかいてください。) - Please write your name on the front of the paper.

店の表で待っています。 (みせのおもてでまっています。) - I am waiting out in front of the shop.

**Another reading: ひょう.** As a separate noun, 表 (ひょう) means a table, chart, or organized list of information. It is not the front surface meaning pronounced differently.

- 結果を表にまとめました。 (けっかをひょうにまとめました。) - I organized the results into a table.
- 次の表を見てください。 (つぎのひょうをみてください。) - Please look at the following chart.

Here 表に marks the format into which the results are organized, and 表を marks the chart being viewed.

## How the kanji combine

[表](../kanji/表.md) writes the native noun おもて as a single unit. The same character has another word reading, ひょう, meaning a table or chart.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[裏](裏.md) (うら), "back; reverse side," is the usual contrast for おもて. [前](前.md) (まえ) indicates a position in front or something earlier; 表 more specifically identifies an object's front-facing side or a building's outward side. A ひょう instead organizes information; it is not a furniture table.

## Distinctive meaning

The correct English translation depends on the object, not on a fixed rule that 表 always means "surface." 表 and 裏 can contrast visible and hidden aspects too. For a paper's front, keep the reading おもて rather than the chart word ひょう.
