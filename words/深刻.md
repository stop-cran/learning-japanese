# 深刻 (しんこく) - serious; grave

## Meaning

深刻 describes a serious or grave problem, condition, or concern. It is a
な-adjective: 深刻な問題 (しんこくなもんだい), "a serious problem."
Use 深刻に before an action such as considering or taking something.

- 水不足は深刻な問題です。 (みずぶそくはしんこくなもんだいです。) - The water shortage is a serious problem.
- 彼女はその知らせを深刻に受け止めました。 (かのじょはそのしらせをしんこくにうけとめました。) - She took the news very seriously.

深刻になっている means "is becoming serious" or "has become serious," depending
on the wider context.

## How the kanji combine

[深](../kanji/深.md) means deep, and [刻](../kanji/刻.md) contributes cutting
or engraving. "Deeply cut" is a useful image for something with serious impact,
not a requirement to translate the word literally. The on readings are シン + コク.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[重大](重大.md) (じゅうだい) emphasizes importance or gravity and can overlap.
[大変](大変.md) (たいへん) has broader uses for difficulty or a great degree.
軽い (かるい), "slight," can contrast with a serious problem in context.

## Distinctive meaning

English "serious" can also mean diligent or not joking. 深刻 is not the default
description for a hardworking student; it foregrounds gravity, concern, or
severity. 深刻な顔 (しんこくなかお) suggests a grave expression, not simply a
person who is concentrating on a task.
