# 准教授 (じゅんきょうじゅ) — associate professor

## Meaning

准教授 names an **associate professor**. In the Japanese university system,
this is an established academic rank below [教授](教授.md) きょうじゅ
(professor), with teaching and research responsibilities. It does not mean
a professor's personal helper.

- 彼女は大学の准教授です。 (かのじょはだいがくのじゅんきょうじゅです。) — She is an associate professor at a university.
- 田中准教授の講義を聞きました。 (たなかじゅんきょうじゅのこうぎをききました。) — I listened to Associate Professor Tanaka's lecture.

The second example places the rank after a name. For respectful direct
address, 田中先生 (たなかせんせい) can be appropriate; [先生](先生.md) is not
itself a statement of this particular academic rank.

## How the kanji combine

[准](../kanji/准.md) modifies the rank head [教授](教授.md), marking the
associate rank. Within 教授, [教](../kanji/教.md) contributes teaching and
授 contributes imparting knowledge. The on-readings are ジュン + キョウ + ジュ.
Learn the whole institutional title rather than translating it as
"half a professor."

## Synonyms and antonyms

- [助教授](助教授.md) じょきょうじゅ is the former Japanese title replaced
  by 准教授 in April 2007. It remains relevant when reading older documents.
- 助教 じょきょう, often translated "assistant professor," is a separate rank,
  not an abbreviation of 准教授.
- [助手](助手.md) じょしゅ means assistant and has its own academic uses.
  It does not by itself identify someone as a 准教授.
- [教授](教授.md) きょうじゅ names the professor rank; [先生](先生.md)
  せんせい is a broader designation or respectful form of address.

These are role and title comparisons, not strict antonyms.

## Distinctive meaning

准教授 is a rank noun, not a verb meaning "work as an associate professor."
Use 准教授です (じゅんきょうじゅです) to state the role. The separate
teaching/instruction sense of 教授する does not license 准教授する for having
this job.

The English label "associate professor" is useful, but rank systems and
employment conditions differ between countries and institutions. Do not
assume an exact international equivalence or an automatic future promotion.
JMdict also lists the spelling 準教授 with the same reading; 准教授 is the
current Japanese university title used here.
