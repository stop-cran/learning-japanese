# Onomatopoeia patterns: repetition, endings, and voicing

Start with the [overview and word index](japanese-onomatopoeia.md).
Recognizing a pattern helps you remember a word, but it does not let you manufacture a
dictionary meaning by changing one kana.

## Repetition is common, not compulsory

In **reduplication**, material is repeated: ころ + ころ gives the visible pattern
[ころころ](../words/ころころ.md). In Japanese, repetition is called 畳語（じょうご）.
In these words, repetition often evokes a continuing state or repeated action:

- ビー玉がころころ転がった。 (ビーだまがころころころがった。) — A marble rolled along.
- 子どもが[にこにこ](../words/にこにこ.md)している。 (こどもがにこにこしている。) —
  The child is smiling happily.

The second sentence describes a smiling state, not necessarily many separate smiles.
Do not translate every repeated word with English "again and again."

Other repeated expressions, such as 時々（ときどき）, "sometimes," are not automatically
onomatopoeia. Repeated shape and membership of this vocabulary family are different questions.

## Endings and other form patterns

| Visible form | Example | Learning caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small っ before と | [じっと](../words/じっと.md)待つ（まつ） — wait still; [そっと](../words/そっと.md)置く（おく） — put down gently | The と is already in these dictionary forms. Do not add a second と. |
| Ending in り | [にっこり](../words/にっこり.md)笑う（わらう） — smile; [ぐったり](../words/ぐったり.md)する — be exhausted or limp | り alone is not a reliable meaning or grammar rule. |
| Nasal rhythm | [とんとん](../words/とんとん.md), [こんこん](../words/こんこん.md) | Here ん is part of each repeated unit, not a detachable suffix. |

Small っ can make a form feel checked or abrupt in some sound effects, but じっと can describe
staying still **for a long time**. Likewise, a り ending does not mean "slow": にっこり can
describe a brief smile. Learn whole words and constructions rather than treating these endings
as conjugations with fixed meanings.

[しっかり](../words/しっかり.md) is another useful non-repeated form. It can mean firmly,
securely, or properly depending on its partner: しっかり持つ（もつ）, "hold firmly," and
しっかり勉強する（べんきょうする）, "study properly/hard," are different applications.

## Voicing: a useful comparison, not a decoder

**Voicing** means using vocal-fold vibration for a consonant: /k/ versus /g/ or /t/ versus /d/.
Japanese writes many such pairs with dakuten, the two small marks: こ → ご, と → ど.
In some mimetic pairs the voiced form suggests more weight, force, or intensity.

| Pair | Useful contrast | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ころころ](../words/ころころ.md) / [ごろごろ](../words/ごろごろ.md) | A small object rolling versus a heavier object rumbling or rolling | ごろごろ also describes lazing around; this is not just "large ころころ." |
| [くるくる](../words/くるくる.md) / [ぐるぐる](../words/ぐるぐる.md) | Light, quick spinning versus going round and round, often more insistently | Their uses overlap; object size alone does not decide every sentence. |
| [とんとん](../words/とんとん.md) / [どんどん](../words/どんどん.md) | Light taps versus stronger repeated beats | どんどん also means rapidly or steadily, as in progress. |
| [ぺたぺた](../words/ぺたぺた.md) / [べたべた](../words/べたべた.md) | Repeated sticking or light contact versus stickiness/clinginess in common uses | Both have additional uses; this is not a universal strength scale. |

Handakuten marks /p/ in ぱ, ぴ, ぷ, ぺ, ぽ. Despite its traditional name 半濁点,
/p/ is **not a "half-voiced" consonant**. Nor does it mean a sensation must be halfway
between the corresponding h- and b-words.

Vowels, long sounds, and consonants can contribute to an impression, but avoid rules such as
"い always means small" or "voiced always means loud." The lexical meaning and actual sentence
remain the evidence.

## Keep spelling separate from a new word

[ざあざあ](../words/ざあざあ.md) can also be written ザーザー. Kana script and long-vowel
notation vary in expressive writing. Those changes are not by themselves a reason to memorize
two unrelated meanings or create two independent word cards.

Compare actual situations in [rain and movement](onomatopoeia-sound-and-movement.md) and
[warmth and texture](onomatopoeia-texture-and-warmth.md).
