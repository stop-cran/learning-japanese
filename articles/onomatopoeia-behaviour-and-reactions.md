# Behaviour, reactions and relationships

Words for how people act can carry the speaker's judgment. The sentence and relationship
matter: do not treat a brief English label as an objective personality diagnosis. See also
[body and texture](onomatopoeia-body-and-texture.md) and
[movement and progress](onomatopoeia-light-movement-and-progress.md).

## Hesitation, haste and pressure

質問したいのに、彼はもじもじしていた。  
*He wanted to ask a question, but kept shifting nervously.*

[もじもじ](../words/もじもじ.md) describes bashful or hesitant fidgeting.
[うじうじ](../words/うじうじ.md) can describe indecisive worrying;
[もたもた](../words/もたもた.md) can describe slow or inefficient action.
[うだうだ](../words/うだうだ.md) can describe idling or going on about something.
Compare [くよくよ](../words/くよくよ.md), worrying over things, and
[ぐずぐず](../words/ぐずぐず.md), which also has dawdling uses. These can overlap in a
scene without attributing the same motive.

[せかせか](../words/せかせか.md) describes restless hurry.
[あたふた](../words/あたふた.md) can describe flustered haste;
[じたばた](../words/じたばた.md) can describe struggling, flailing or panicking.
[たじたじ](../words/たじたじ.md) describes faltering under pressure.
The drills use もじもじする, じたばたする and たじたじする, but use
せかせか as a manner of walking or working.

## What the observer conveys

彼はちゃっかり窓際の席を取った。  
*He shrewdly secured a window seat.*

[ちゃっかり](../words/ちゃっかり.md) can imply shrewdly securing an advantage; the
speaker's attitude depends on context. [のこのこ](../words/のこのこ.md) can portray
someone as appearing unconcernedly or brazenly, often with a critical viewpoint.
[へらへら](../words/へらへら.md) can depict a foolish or frivolous smile; it is not
simply a neutral synonym for [にやにや](../words/にやにや.md), smiling to oneself.

[さばさば](../words/さばさば.md) can describe a frank, easy-going manner. The
さばさばした性格 construction describes a characteristic; した here does not insist
that a past state has ended. [すっぱり](../words/すっぱり.md) can describe a decisive
break or refusal, rather than a person's entire character.

## Relations and reactions

誤解のあと、二人の関係がぎくしゃくした。  
*After the misunderstanding, their relationship became strained.*

[ぎくしゃく](../words/ぎくしゃく.md) can describe awkward movement or strained
relations. [ぎすぎす](../words/ぎすぎす.md) can describe a tense, unfriendly atmosphere.
Neither tells us exactly who caused the tension. [ねちねち](../words/ねちねち.md)
can portray criticism or questioning as persistently unpleasant.
[ちやほや](../words/ちやほや.md)する means to pamper or flatter with attention;
ちやほやされる presents the recipient, rather than the person giving the attention.
[つんつん](../words/つんつん.md) can describe repeatedly poking something; the
exercises select that physical action, not an attitude toward another person.

[ぽかん](../words/ぽかん.md)とする can depict a blank or open-mouthed reaction.
It does not specify the reason. [げっそり](../words/げっそり.md)する can express
being disheartened; its card also discusses a gaunt appearance.
