# 樹 — tree; establish

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

樹 concerns **trees**, as in 樹木 じゅもく, trees and shrubs. It also appears in 樹立 じゅりつ, establishment or setting a record. Learn the whole compound: 樹立 is not a kind of tree.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [木](木.md) on the left, a compact middle block with a [口](口.md)-shaped enclosure, and 寸 on the far right. Imagine a tree beside a carefully arranged garden feature. This is a visual memory aid, not a verified historical or phonetic explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ジュ | on | [樹脂](../words/樹脂.md) じゅし - resin; 樹木 じゅもく - trees and shrubs |
| き | kun | 樹 き - tree or shrub; an alternative spelling of the native noun [木](../words/木.md) き |

The き spelling is dictionary-recognized. In that JMdict family, the wood/timber material sense is restricted to **木**, not 樹. This is a restriction on that lexical spelling and sense, not a claim that 樹 can never have a wood-related character gloss.

## Notes

- This card asks **ジュ** through 樹脂. The native き spelling remains recognition information, not a separate question or a new word article duplicating 木.
- 樹脂 does not always mean material taken from a tree: 合成樹脂 ごうせいじゅし means synthetic resin. Do not turn the character association into a rule about the material's actual origin.
- [木](木.md) has a broader tree/wood label and [立](立.md) also concerns setting something up. They are useful comparisons, not unambiguous wrong answers here.
- [村](村.md), village, has just 寸 beside 木. 樹 has an additional dense block between them. [時](時.md), time/hour, has 日 on the left; [持](持.md), hold/have, has a hand-shaped left side. Neither shares 樹's tree or establishment meaning merely because its right side looks similar.
