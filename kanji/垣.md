# 垣 — fence; hedge

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

垣 is a fence or hedge that marks off a house or garden, and by extension anything that separates people. [垣根](../words/垣根.md) かきね is a hedge or fence, and figuratively a barrier between groups. JMdict glosses 垣 かき "fence; hedge; railing" and, in a second sense, "barrier; wall."

The Daijisen character entry marks エン as a reading outside the official table. For エン it gives the senses かき and かきね, with two literary compounds that JMdict does not list. For かき, read がき after another element, it gives 垣根, 石垣 いしがき, "stone wall," 歌垣 うたがき, a "gathering of men and women who sang courtship songs to each other and danced," which JMdict marks archaic, 柴垣 しばがき, "brushwood fence," 竹垣 たけがき, "bamboo fence (hedge)," 玉垣 たまがき, "fence around a shrine," and 人垣 ひとがき, "wall of people; crowd of people lined up (e.g. spectators)."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [土](土.md), "earth, soil," at the left, and at the right [日](日.md), "sun, day," between two horizontal strokes. Imagine an earthen fence at the left of a yard, with the sun shining between a top rail and a bottom rail. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 土 or 日 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| エン | on | 垣牆 えんしょう - hedge; fence; JMdict marks it archaic; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| かき | kun | [垣根](../words/垣根.md) かきね — hedge; fence; barrier |

In 垣根, 垣 supplies かき and 根 supplies ね, giving the native word かきね; neither kanji takes okurigana, the kana ending that follows a kanji in many words. In 石垣 and 人垣, かき becomes がき, a sound change called rendaku, the voicing of the first sound of a following element. In 垣牆, 垣 supplies エン and 牆 supplies ショウ. JMdict's only other example of エン is the astronomy term 紫微垣 しびえん, "Purple Forbidden Enclosure (group of constellations in the northern sky associated with the emperor)."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only かき, with 垣 and 垣根 as examples. エン is not in the official table. Practice selects かき, the reading with a linked word article; エン is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [根](根.md), "root; foundation," in 垣根.
- [坂](坂.md), "slope; incline," [城](城.md), "castle," and [埋](埋.md), "bury; embed," have the same 土 at the left, beside a different right part. All are different kanji.
- [塀](塀.md), "boundary wall; fence," [囲](囲.md), "surround; enclose," [境](境.md), "boundary; border," [界](界.md), "world, boundary," [関](関.md), "involve; barrier," [限](限.md), "limit," and [壁](壁.md), "wall; barrier," overlap the senses "fence," "barrier," "wall," "border" and "limit" that 垣 and 垣根 carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "fence," "wall" or "boundary" as exclusive to any one character.
- JMdict also has 垣間見る かいまみる, "to take a peep at; to catch a glimpse of," written with 垣; its かい matches neither listed reading, so learn that reading with the whole word.
