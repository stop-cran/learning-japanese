# 朗 — cheerful; clear; resonant

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 月 (no. 74), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

朗 can describe something bright and clear, a cheerful mood, or a voice that carries clearly. [朗らか](../words/朗らか.md) ほがらか is cheerful or bright and clear; JMdict glosses it “cheerful; merry; sunny; melodious” and “bright (sky, day, etc.); fine; clear.”

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ロウ. One example of each:

- clear and bright: 晴朗 せいろう, “clear; fair; fine; serene”;
- cheerful, without gloom: 明朗 めいろう, “cheerful; bright”;
- a voice that is clear and carries: 朗々 ろうろう, “clear; sonorous; full-sounding; resonant; bright.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 良 at the left and 月, “moon, month,” at the right. Imagine a clear, resonant voice carrying into the night. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| あき.らか | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists あき.らか; no ordinary JMdict word was found that writes 朗 with this reading |

In 朗らか, 朗 writes ほが and らか is okurigana. KANJIDIC2 also lists あき.らか, but JMdict provides no ordinary word example for that reading. The ロウ examples include 朗読 ろうどく, “reading aloud; recitation,” and 明朗; 朗々 describes a clear, resonant sound.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ロウ with 朗読, 朗々と and 明朗, and ほがらか with 朗らかだ and 朗らかさ. JMdict glosses 朗読 “reading aloud; recitation,” 朗々 “clear; sonorous; full-sounding; resonant; bright,” and 明朗 “cheerful; bright.” The examples 朗らかだ and 朗らかさ use the linked adjective, which JMdict glosses “cheerful; merry; sunny” and “bright (sky, day, etc.); fine; clear.” Practice selects ほが.らか, the reading with a linked word article. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid. The Joyo table prints 朗 in parentheses; it is a compatibility ideograph, not an older form.

## Notes

- [良](良.md) is the left shape and [月](月.md) the right shape of 朗. [有](有.md), “have, exist,” and [朝](朝.md), “morning,” also contain 月; their meanings differ. These three cards supply the distractors, and none shares an attested reading with 朗.
- [明](明.md), “bright, clear,” [晴](晴.md), “clear skies,” [冴](冴.md), “clear; sharp-minded,” [愉](愉.md), “cheerful; joyful; delight,” [快](快.md), “pleasant; agreeable,” [楽](楽.md), “music, enjoyment, ease,” [喜](喜.md), “rejoice,” [響](響.md), “resound; echo,” [声](声.md), “voice,” [音](音.md), “sound,” [陽](陽.md), “sunshine,” [光](光.md), “light; radiance,” and [照](照.md), “illuminate; shine,” overlap the clear, bright, cheerful or resonant-voice senses of 朗 and are excluded from its quiz options.
