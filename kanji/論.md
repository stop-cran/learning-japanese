# 論 — argument; discourse

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

論 concerns arguments and developed discussion. [議論](../words/議論.md) ぎろん is discussion or argument;
論文 ろんぶん is an essay or paper. Here English "argument" can mean reasoning about an issue,
not merely an angry exchange.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 言 on the left supplying words to the ordered-looking
strokes of 侖 on the right. This is only a visual aid; it does not establish a historical
component meaning or pronunciation.

## Notes

問題について議論する (もんだいについてぎろんする) means "discuss a problem."
The issue is introduced with について in this pattern.
[語](語.md), [許](許.md), and [訪](訪.md) share 言, but have different right-hand shapes.
Check the roof-like top and divided lower part on the right of 論.
