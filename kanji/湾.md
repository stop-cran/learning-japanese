# 湾 — bay, gulf

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

湾 means a bay or gulf, an area of water extending into the land.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture water at the left beside the bent, bow-like lower shape on the right: the coast curves around a bay.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.
