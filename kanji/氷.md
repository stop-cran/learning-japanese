# 氷 — ice; freeze

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

氷 concerns ice or freezing. The ordinary noun 氷 is こおり. 氷河 ひょうが means a glacier, while 氷る こおる is a dictionary-listed rare spelling of the verb also written 凍る.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice a 水-like shape with an extra short stroke and imagine water turning to ice. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not an account of the character's historical origin.

The canonical indexing radical is 水, no. 85. Here it is not the three-dot left-hand form 氵 seen in many other water-radical characters.

## Notes

- The ひ row uses the separately indexed archaic ice sense. It does not replace こおり in the linked everyday noun article.
- JMdict marks the spelling 氷る rare, not the pronunciation こおる. The existing [凍る](../words/凍る.md) article teaches that verb under its other listed spelling.
- Keep the long vowel in こおり: write おお, not おう. Distinguish [永](永.md), enduring duration; its central and side strokes differ.
