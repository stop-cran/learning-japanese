# 隣 — next door; adjoining

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

隣 is being right beside something: the next place, the house next door, or the people living there. KANJIDIC2 lists “neighboring”, and Kanjipedia and Digital Daijisen both give となり。となりあう。 (next door; be side by side). On its own, [隣](../words/隣.md) となり is glossed in JMdict “next (to); adjoining; adjacent” and “house next door; neighbouring house; next-door neighbour; next-door neighbor” (entry 1555830).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the ⻖ shape stands on the left. On the right, [米](米.md), "rice grain," sits over 舛, a shape made of [夕](夕.md), "evening," beside 㐄. Picture two households on either side of a wall, passing rice to each other every evening: good neighbours next door. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that ⻖, 米 or 舛 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists リン, with 隣接 and 近隣 among its examples; となる, with 隣り合う となりあう (“to adjoin each other; to sit side by side”, JMdict 1598830); and となり, with 隣. Kanjipedia lists no readings outside the table.
- KANJIDIC2 records ちか as a name reading; Digital Daijisen gives さと, ただ, ちか, ちかし and なが. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings. Digital Daijisen notes 鄰 as a variant form, which JMdict lists as a search-only form of 隣.

These five cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [次](次.md) (“next”) and [翌](翌.md) (“next; subsequent”): the first sense of 隣 となり is “next (to); adjoining; adjacent”, JMdict's first sense of 次 つぎ is “next; following; subsequent” (1316380), and it glosses 翌 よく “the following; next” (2064170).
- [接](接.md) (“touch; join”): the first sense of 接する せっする is “to touch; to come in contact with; to border on; to adjoin; to be adjacent; to be close” (JMdict 1385350), and JMdict glosses 隣接 りんせつ “adjacency; contiguity; being adjoined” (1555870).
- [近](近.md) (“near”): Kanjipedia lists 近隣 and 比隣 among its compounds with 隣, and Digital Daijisen also gives 近隣 as an example; JMdict glosses 近隣 きんりん as “neighbourhood; neighborhood; vicinity” (1242570) and 比隣 ひりん as “nearby; vicinity” (1731780), and the first sense of 近い ちかい is “near; close; short (distance)” (1242130).
- [町](町.md) (“town, neighborhood”): KANJIDIC2 lists “neighboring” for 隣, JMdict glosses 隣人 りんじん “neighbour; neighbor; (people in the) neighbourhood; neighborhood” (1555850), and the first sense of 町 まち is “town; block; neighbourhood; neighborhood” (1603990).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 遠 (“far”), 奥 (“interior; depths”), and 裏 (“reverse side; lining”); none means next to or adjoining.
