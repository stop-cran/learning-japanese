# 完 — complete

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

完 points to completeness or reaching a finished state. [完成](../words/完成.md) (かんせい) is completion, while 完全 (かんぜん) describes something complete or perfect.
A finished product and a flawless product are not necessarily the same thing; the whole word matters.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** put the 宀 roof onto the visible 元 as the final piece of a model, making it complete.
This is a modern construction game, not an etymology.

## Notes

[元](元.md) is the lower visual component; [院](院.md) contains the whole 完 shape on its right.
Do not choose [終](終.md), "end; finish," as a wrong meaning for 完: the meanings overlap even though the shapes differ.
