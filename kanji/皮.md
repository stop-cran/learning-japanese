# 皮 — skin; hide

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 皮 (no. 107) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

皮 concerns skin, hide and outer coverings, including a fruit's peel or a tree's bark. The object being covered determines the appropriate English word; it is not limited to human skin.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the bent outer stroke and the branching lower strokes as a covering being peeled back. This is only a memory image of the present shape.
