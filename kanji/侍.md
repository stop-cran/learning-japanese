# 侍 — samurai; retainer

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

侍 can refer to a samurai, especially a historical retainer. Its kun reading appears in [侍](../words/侍.md) さむらい. KANJIDIC2 also records meanings related to serving or waiting upon someone.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジ | on | 侍従 じじゅう — chamberlain (JMdict entry 1314890). |
| シ | on | 内侍 ないし — maid of honor; lady-in-waiting (JMdict entry 1582520); a historical court title. |
| さむらい | kun | [侍](../words/侍.md) さむらい — samurai (JMdict entry 1314780) |
| はべ.る | kun | 侍る はべる — wait upon; serve (usually written in kana; JMdict entry 1314800). |

## Common words

- [侍](../words/侍.md) さむらい — samurai
- 侍従 じじゅう — chamberlain

## Notes

侍る is usually written in kana, and 内侍 is a historical court title. 士 and 武 also have warrior senses, and JMdict glosses 兵 (“soldier; troops”) read つわもの as “soldier; warrior” (entry 2822790). 臣 means retainer or subject, 仕 means serve or attend, and 僕 can mean servant. Digital Daijisen explains the ジ sense as serving beside a person of high rank.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 父 (“father”), 男 (“man, male”), and 魚 (“fish”); none means a samurai or a retainer.
