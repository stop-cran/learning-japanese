# 侍 — samurai; retainer

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

侍 can refer to a samurai, especially a historical retainer. Its kun reading appears in [侍](../words/侍.md) さむらい. KANJIDIC2 also records meanings related to serving or waiting upon someone.

## Readings

## Notes

The quiz uses さむらい from the selected word article. JMdict marks 侍る as usually written in kana, and 内侍 is a historical title rather than a common beginner word. 士 can also be read as a word for a warrior, so it is excluded from this card’s meaning choices.
