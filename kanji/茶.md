# 茶 — tea

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (as 艹, no. 140) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

茶 means tea, and also appears in the color word 茶色 ちゃいろ, "brown."
The everyday drink is often called [お茶](../words/お茶.md) おちゃ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture tea leaves at the top, a person-shaped roof in the middle, and a small
tree-like base below. Imagine someone tending a tea plant. This is a mnemonic, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

お in お茶 is a polite or beautifying prefix, not part of the reading of 茶.
茶色 combines チャ with [色](色.md) いろ: this is a mixed on+kun word.
Compare the lower shapes of 茶, [英](英.md), and [花](花.md), which share a similar top.
