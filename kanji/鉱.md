# 鉱 — ore; mineral

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鉱 concerns mineral material and ore. 鉱石 names ore or a mineral, while 鉱業 is the mining industry.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 金 beside 広 and imagine a broad deposit of mineral material. This is a visual association, not etymology or a phonetic claim.

The canonical radical 金 appears as the narrow left-hand form.
