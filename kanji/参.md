# 参 — participate

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 厶 (no. 28) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

参 appears in [参加](../words/参加.md) さんか, "participation," and in words concerning visits or consultation.
Do not try to translate every 参 word as "participate": 参考 さんこう means reference or consultation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the three lower slanting strokes joining the shape above.
This suggests participation as a memory aid, not an origin story.

## Common words

- [参加](../words/参加.md) さんか — participation
- 参考 さんこう — reference; consultation
- 参る まいる — humble go/come; also visit a shrine or grave

## Notes

The humble go/come use is usually written in kana, often まいります.
"Humble" describes how the speaker presents the action in polite interaction; it is not an ordinary replacement for every "go."
The shrine-visiting sense is separate. [公](公.md) and [台](台.md) share a 厶 shape but not 参's lower strokes.
