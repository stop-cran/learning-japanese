# 辰 — Dragon zodiac sign

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 辰 (no. 161) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

辰 names the **fifth of the twelve zodiac signs**, the Dragon. The native
sign noun is [辰](../words/辰.md) たつ. The associated mythical creature
is normally written 竜 or 龍, rather than using 辰 as an ordinary creature noun.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine a 厂-like roof and
left edge sheltering two horizontal bars and the lower turning and
sweeping strokes. The indexing radical is the whole 辰, not the 厂-like
outline. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a dragon-picture
origin or a historical component analysis.

Only たつ is selected for practice. シン is verified calendar recognition;
learn 戊辰's complete reading ぼしん rather than freely exchanging
character readings. 戊辰 is also read つちのえたつ. The sixty-term cycle
combines stems and branches; its fifth combination is not the same
category as the fifth branch in the twelve-sign set.

ジン is preserved from the character source, not labelled invalid,
obsolete or name-only. Its lexical allocation remains unverified in
this lesson; do not infer a 辰 reading from an arbitrary part of a
compound's pronunciation.

## Notes

- [農](農.md), agriculture, has a 辰-shaped lower part under a
  曲-shaped top. [震](震.md), quake or tremble, puts an 雨-shaped
  top above a 辰-shaped part. Their complete meanings are different;
  these comparisons do not establish the characters' origins.
- [長](長.md), long or chief, has a similar lower sweep but a
  different upper shape, eight strokes and its own radical.
- Older uses include an hour around 8 a.m., a traditional direction
  labelled east-southeast, and the third lunar month. Dictionary hour
  mappings include both 7–9 a.m. and 8–10 a.m., not one universal
  modern interval. The direction is not an exact modern sixteen-point
  compass bearing, and the lunar month is not Gregorian March.
- 辰巳 (たつみ), southeast, names a different traditional direction
  from standalone 辰; [巳](巳.md) is the next zodiac branch.
- The grade-9 tag classifies name-use kanji; it does not make the sign
  noun or every reading a personal name.
