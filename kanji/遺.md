# 遺 — leave behind; bequeath

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

遺 means to leave something behind, especially after death, and also to be left behind. It appears in 遺産, in 遺憾, “regrettable,” where it means to remain behind, and in 遺言 (ゆいごん), “a will; last words.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 辶, the “movement” part that wraps the lower left, runs under [貴](貴.md), “precious.” [遣](遣.md), “dispatch,” looks very similar, but a different part replaces 貝 at the bottom of 貴.

## Notes

[遣](遣.md) and [貴](貴.md) look similar, but mean dispatch and precious and are read ケン and キ; [遅](遅.md) also has 辶 and means late. [残](残.md), [留](留.md), [去](去.md) and [後](後.md) relate to remaining, staying, leaving or what comes after; [譲](譲.md), [贈](贈.md), [伝](伝.md), [紹](紹.md) and [継](継.md) to handing over, giving, passing on, introducing or succeeding; [失](失.md) and [忘](忘.md) to losing and forgetting. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 13](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=13) lists イ, with 遺棄 (いき), “abandonment,” 遺産 and 遺失 (いしつ), “loss,” and ユイ, with 遺言; it notes that 遺言 is also read イゴン.
