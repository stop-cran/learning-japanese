# 恒 — constant; lasting

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

恒 means constant or lasting: something that stays the same over time. It appears in 恒例, 恒常 (こうじょう), “constancy,” and 恒久 (こうきゅう), “permanence.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 忄, the narrow form of 心 “heart,” is on the left. The right part 亘 is also in 垣, and in 宣 under its roof.

## Notes

[垣](垣.md) has 土 on the left and means fence; [宣](宣.md) puts the same right part under a roof and means proclaim; [恨](恨.md) also starts with 忄 and means resent. [常](常.md) also means usual or constant; JMdict lists 常 first for つね, and its entry for 常に (つねに), “always,” has no other spelling. [永](永.md) and [久](久.md) relate to long duration. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 54](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=54) gives 恒 only コウ, so つね and つねに are outside standard use and are not offered in practice; in ordinary writing, “always” is 常に.
