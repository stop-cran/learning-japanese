# 幣 — currency; Shinto offering

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

幣 is cloth and other things offered to the gods, a gift, and, in most modern words, money and currency. [紙幣](../words/紙幣.md) しへい is paper money; JMdict glosses it "paper money; note; bill."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ヘイ. One example of each:

- cloth offered before the gods: 幣帛 へいはく, "offering of cloth (rope, paper, etc.)," a Shinto term;
- a gift or tribute: 幣物 へいもつ, "Shinto offerings; present to a guest";
- currency or money: 貨幣 かへい, "money; currency; coin."

Its kun readings are ぬさ and みてぐら; KANJIDIC2 does not list みてぐら, which Daijisen's own headword describes as a general name for things offered to the gods. A separate Daijisen headword reads 幣 as まい, which JMdict glosses "reward; present; gift; offering to the gods" and marks as archaic. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find an upper part with 攵 at its right, and 巾, the key, a shape like a hanging cloth, at the bottom. Imagine a hand cutting a cloth into strips: paper streamers for a shrine staff, or slips that pass as money. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 攵 or 巾 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヘイ | on | [紙幣](../words/紙幣.md) しへい — paper money; banknotes |
| ぬさ | kun | 幣 ぬさ - staff with plaited paper streamers; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 紙幣, 紙 supplies シ and 幣 supplies ヘイ, with no sound change. As ぬさ, 幣 is written alone with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict reads this Shinto word へい or ぬさ. In 御幣担ぎ ごへいかつぎ, "superstition; superstitious person," 御 supplies ゴ, 幣 supplies ヘイ, and 担 with the okurigana ぎ is read かつぎ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ヘイ, with 貨幣, 紙幣 and 御幣担ぎ as examples. ぬさ is not in the table. Practice selects ヘイ, the reading with a linked word article. ぬさ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [紙](紙.md), "paper," in 紙幣.
- [中](中.md), "middle, inside," has a vertical stroke running through a box, much as 巾 at the bottom of 幣 has a vertical stroke running through the open frame 冂. [内](内.md), "within, inner," and [南](南.md), "south," are also built on 冂. [常](常.md), "usual; constant," [帯](帯.md), "sash; zone," and [希](希.md), "hope; rare," have the same 巾 at the bottom with a different upper part. All are different kanji.
- [貨](貨.md), "goods; freight; currency," [金](金.md), "gold, money," [札](札.md), "tag; banknote," [供](供.md), "accompany, offer," and [贈](贈.md), "give as a gift; confer," overlap the senses "currency," "money," "banknote," "offering" and "gift" that 幣 and its word carry; 貨 appears with 幣 in 貨幣. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "money" or "offering" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ヘイ senses: 御幣 ごへい, "staff with plaited paper streamers," a Shinto term marked as honorific language, and 奉幣 ほうへい, "offering a wand with hemp and paper streamers to a Shinto god"; and 幣制 へいせい, "monetary system; currency," and 造幣 ぞうへい, "coinage; mintage."
