# 鼓 — drum; rouse

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 鼓 (no. 207) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鼓 is a drum, such as the hourglass-shaped tsuzumi, and it also means beating or striking, and rousing or encouraging. KANJIDIC2 lists “drum”, “beat”, “rouse” and “muster”. [太鼓](../words/太鼓.md) たいこ is “drum”, and 鼓舞 こぶ is “encouragement; inspiration; rousing; stirring up; raising (e.g. morale)”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the shape 壴, which contains [士](士.md), "warrior; professional," and [豆](豆.md), "bean; pea," stands on the left, and [支](支.md), "support," made of [十](十.md), "ten," and the shape 又, stands on the right. Picture a warrior beating a big drum set on its support so hard that dried beans jump on its skin, rousing the whole camp. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 壴 or 支 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists コ, with 鼓動, 鼓舞 and 太鼓, and つづみ, with 鼓 and 小鼓 こつづみ, “small hand drum”. Kanjipedia and Digital Daijisen give the same two readings.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 鼓, and Digital Daijisen gives none. Digital Daijisen notes that 皷 is a variant form (「皷」は異体字).

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [打](打.md) (“strike”), [撃](撃.md) (“shoot; strike”) and [拍](拍.md) (“clap; musical beat”): the beating and striking sense; KANJIDIC2 lists “beat” for 鼓.
- [脈](脈.md) (“pulse; vein”): 鼓動 こどう is “beat; palpitation; pulsation; throbbing”.
- [励](励.md) (“encourage; be diligent”), [勧](勧.md) (“recommend; encourage”) and [奨](奨.md) (“encourage; exhort; promote”): the encouraging sense, as in 鼓舞.
- [激](激.md) (“intense; excite”) and [扇](扇.md) (“fan; incite”): the rousing sense; 鼓舞 can also mean “stirring up”, which is close to exciting or inciting.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 笛 (“flute; whistle”), 琴 (“koto; zither”), and 筆 (“brush; writing”); the flute and koto are other instruments, not drums, and a brush is not an instrument; none means drum or rouse.
