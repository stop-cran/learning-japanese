# 鼓 — drum; rouse

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 鼓 (no. 207) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鼓 is a drum, and it also covers beating and rousing. KANJIDIC2 lists “drum”, “beat”, “rouse” and “muster”. Kanjipedia's three senses begin つづみ。打楽器の一種。 (a tsuzumi, a kind of percussion instrument), うつ。たたく。 (to beat; to strike) and ふるいたたせる。はげます。 (to rouse; to encourage). Digital Daijisen's senses are 打楽器の一。つづみ。たいこ。 (a percussion instrument; a drum), つづみを打つ。たたく。 (to beat a drum; to strike) and 奮い立たせる。励ます。 (to rouse; to encourage), and 太鼓 is among the examples of its first sense. [太鼓](../words/太鼓.md) たいこ is “drum” in JMdict (entry 1408280).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the shape 壴, which contains [士](士.md), "warrior; professional," and [豆](豆.md), "bean; pea," stands on the left, and [支](支.md), "support," made of [十](十.md), "ten," and the shape 又, stands on the right. Picture a warrior beating a big drum set on its support so hard that dried beans jump on its skin, rousing the whole camp. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 壴 or 支 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists コ, with 鼓動, 鼓舞 and 太鼓, and つづみ, with 鼓 and 小鼓 こつづみ, which JMdict glosses as “small hand drum” and also reads しょうこ (entry 2257170). Kanjipedia and Digital Daijisen give the same two readings.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 鼓, and Digital Daijisen gives none. Digital Daijisen notes that 皷 is a variant form (「皷」は異体字).

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [打](打.md) (“strike”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 鼓 includes うつ。たたく。 (to beat; to strike), Digital Daijisen's second includes たたく, and the 打 card lists う.つ.
- [撃](撃.md) (“shoot; strike”) and [拍](拍.md) (“clap; musical beat”): KANJIDIC2 lists “beat” for 鼓, “beat” with the kun reading う.つ for 撃, and “beat (music)” for 拍.
- [脈](脈.md) (“pulse; vein”): JMdict glosses 鼓動 こどう (1268000) as “beat; palpitation; pulsation; throbbing”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “pulse” for 脈.
- [励](励.md) (“encourage; be diligent”), [勧](勧.md) (“recommend; encourage”) and [奨](奨.md) (“encourage; exhort; promote”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 鼓 is ふるいたたせる。はげます。, Digital Daijisen's third is 奮い立たせる。励ます。 (to rouse; to encourage), JMdict glosses 鼓舞 こぶ (1268020) as “encouragement; inspiration; rousing; stirring up; raising (e.g. morale)”, and KANJIDIC2 gives “encourage” for 励, 勧 and 奨.
- [激](激.md) (“intense; excite”): KANJIDIC2 lists “rouse” for 鼓 and “get excited” and “incite” for 激, and JMdict glosses 激励 げきれい (1253760) as “encouragement; spurring (on); cheering (on)”, beside the “encouragement” of 鼓舞.
- [扇](扇.md) (“fan; incite”): the title's “incite” sits beside the “stirring up” of 鼓舞, and JMdict glosses the second sense of 扇ぐ あおぐ (1609000) as “to incite; to instigate” and 扇動 せんどう (1390750) as “incitement; sedition; agitation; abetting.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 笛 (“flute; whistle”), 琴 (“koto; zither”), and 筆 (“brush; writing”); the flute and koto are other instruments, not drums, and a brush is not an instrument; none means drum or rouse.
