# 叔 — uncle or aunt (younger than a parent)

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 又 (no. 29) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

叔 refers to a younger brother or sister of one's father or mother. Digital Daijisen gives two senses: a parent's younger sibling, as in 叔父 and 叔母, and the third in the traditional order of brothers, 伯仲叔季. In the notes column, the Joyo table gives 叔父 the special reading おじ and 叔母 the special reading おば.

## Notes

JMdict notes that 伯父 and 伯母 can mean an uncle or aunt older than one's parent, and 叔父 and 叔母 one younger than one's parent (entries 1607070 and 1607100). The Joyo table gives 伯叔 as its only example word for シュク; its readings おじ for 叔父 and おば for 叔母 are whole-word special readings, not readings of 叔 alone. Kanjipedia also gives the kun reading わか.い, marked as outside the Joyo table (外); it is not in this card's metadata and is not quizzed.

伯 (“uncle or aunt; eldest; count (noble)”) is the older-sibling counterpart and the other kanji in 伯叔, 伯父 and 伯母. 弟 (“younger brother”) overlaps with the younger-sibling sense; Digital Daijisen defines 叔父 しゅくふ as a younger brother of one's father or mother.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 東 (“east”), 校 (“school”), and 物 (“thing, object”); none means an uncle or aunt.
