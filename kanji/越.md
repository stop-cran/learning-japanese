# 越 — cross over; exceed

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 走 (no. 156) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

越 suggests passing over or beyond something. In [越える](../words/越える.md) こえる,
the thing crossed can be a mountain or a boundary. 越す こす also has crossing and moving-house senses;
the whole word and context determine the action.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** follow the long lower sweep of 走 past the right-hand shape as though crossing an obstacle.
This is a memory aid for the modern outline, not a historical analysis.

## Notes

For exceeding a quantity, the spelling 超える (こえる) is especially associated with that sense.
This is a spelling tendency, not a claim that all uses fall into disjoint boxes.
越 and [起](起.md) share 走; distinguish their right sides.
[走](走.md) alone lacks that added part. [迷](迷.md) has a different sweeping frame, 辶.
