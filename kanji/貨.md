# 貨 — goods; freight; currency

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

貨 concerns goods, freight, property or currency. In 硬貨 it contributes to the word for coins; 貨物 concerns cargo or freight.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the upper 化 transforming the [貝](貝.md) below into goods or money. This is a modern-shape story, not a claim that the character was historically built as a picture of transformation.

## Notes

- JMdict lists 貨 たから in the same treasure family as [宝](宝.md) and [財](財.md), without a rarity flag on 貨. This establishes recognition, not equal frequency or a recommendation to replace 宝 in everyday writing.
- Do not mix up 貨 with [貸](貸.md), lend. 貨 has 化 above 貝, whereas 貸 has 代 above it.
