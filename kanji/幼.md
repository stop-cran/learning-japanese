# 幼 — early childhood; immature

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 幺 (no. 52) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

幼 concerns early childhood or immaturity. 幼い おさない can describe someone very young and can also describe childish or immature behavior. 幼児 ようじ means a young child or toddler.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 幺 beside 力 and imagine a small child still developing strength. This is a shape-based mnemonic, not a historical analysis.

## Notes

- 幼い is an い-adjective: 幼 writes おさな and the final い remains kana.
- Immaturity is a contextual extension, not an insult automatically present whenever the word describes a child's age.
