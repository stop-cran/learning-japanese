# 罪 — crime; guilt

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 网 (as 罒, no. 122) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

罪 concerns wrongdoing and the guilt attached to it. The word [罪](../words/罪.md) つみ
can refer to a crime or to a sin; the surrounding discussion determines whether the judgment
is legal, moral, or religious.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the top 罒 as a net hanging over the 非 shape while
wrongdoing is examined. This is a memory scene, not a historical account.

## Notes

The indexing radical is 网, visibly written 罒 here; it is not the eye radical 目.
[置](置.md) and [買](買.md) share that top. [四](四.md) is a simpler outline comparison.
Not every 間違い (まちがい), "mistake," amounts to a 罪.
