# 稿 — manuscript; draft

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

稿 is a piece of writing in draft form: a manuscript. [原稿](../words/原稿.md) げんこう is a manuscript prepared for publication or delivery; JMdict glosses it "manuscript; copy; draft; notes; contribution." The word article teaches "manuscript; draft."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for コウ, a draft of a poem or other writing, as in 稿料 こうりょう, 遺稿 いこう, 草稿 そうこう, 投稿 とうこう and 原稿. It lists the kun reading わら. A note says 稾 is a variant form. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 禾, the grain-plant key, at the left and [高](高.md), "high, expensive," at the right. Imagine a tall stack of manuscript pages. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 禾 or 高 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| コウ | on | [原稿](../words/原稿.md) げんこう — manuscript; draft |
| わら | kun, source-only here | Daijisen and KANJIDIC2 list わら, but JMdict writes 藁 わら, "word usually written using kana alone," "straw," with 藁, not 稿, so no ordinary word is checked here |
| したがき | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists したがき, but JMdict writes 下書き したがき, "draft; rough copy," with 下書き, not 稿, so no ordinary word is checked here |

In 原稿, 原 supplies ゲン and 稿 supplies コウ. In 草稿, 草 supplies ソウ and 稿 supplies コウ; JMdict glosses 草稿 "notes; draft; manuscript." In 投稿, 投 supplies トウ and 稿 supplies コウ; JMdict glosses 投稿 "contribution (to a newspaper, magazine, etc.); submission; post (on a blog, social media, etc.)." The word 稿 こう alone is also in JMdict: "manuscript; version; draft." A title, 青砥稿花紅彩画, is a whole word and is not split into readings here.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows コウ, with 草稿, 原稿 and 投稿, and no kun reading. Practice selects コウ, the reading of the linked word article. わら and したがき are retained from KANJIDIC2 (Daijisen also gives わら) and explained as above. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [原](原.md), "original; plain," in 原稿, supplying ゲン. In the other words named above, [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," supplies ソウ in 草稿 and [投](投.md), "throw," supplies トウ in 投稿.
- [案](案.md), "proposal; draft plan," [写](写.md), "copy, photograph," [文](文.md), "sentence, writing," [書](書.md), "write," [筆](筆.md), "brush; writing," [著](著.md), "write; remarkable," [記](記.md), "record in writing," [誌](誌.md), "written record; journal," [献](献.md), "offer; contribute; dedicate," and [版](版.md), "printing plate; edition," overlap the senses "draft," "copy," "writing," "contribution" and "version" that 稿 and 原稿 carry. All are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [木](木.md), "tree, wood," [私](私.md), "I; private," and [税](税.md), "tax; fiscal duty," look plausible because 木 resembles the 禾 shape, and 私 and 税 are written with 禾 at the left. They are read ボク, モク and き; シ and わたくし; and ゼイ, and mean tree, I and tax. None shares a reading or a meaning with 稿.
- Many other kanji read コウ, such as [高](高.md), [校](校.md), "school," and [考](考.md), "think over," are never offered as wrong answers.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the コウ sense: 稿料 こうりょう, "manuscript fee; payment for a piece of writing"; 遺稿 いこう, "posthumous manuscripts"; 起稿 きこう, "beginning writing (a draft); drafting"; 寄稿 きこう, "contribution (e.g. to newspaper)"; 玉稿 ぎょっこう, an honorific term for "your manuscript"; 拙稿 せっこう, a humble term for "my manuscript"; and 脱稿 だっこう, "finishing writing; completion of a manuscript."
