# 部 — section; department

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 邑 (as right-hand 阝, no. 163) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

部 divides a larger whole into parts or organizational sections. 部分 ぶぶん means a part;
[全部](../words/全部.md) ぜんぶ collects all the parts into "the whole."
The useful word [部屋](../words/部屋.md) へや means a room, but its reading should be learned as a whole word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 立-over-口 cluster as a department sign beside the right-hand 阝.
This is a modern memory aid, not etymology.

## Notes

The right-hand 阝 is the positional form of 邑; the left-hand 阝 in [院](院.md) is indexed as 阜.
