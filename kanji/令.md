# 令 — command; decree

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

令 concerns an order, command, or decree. 命令 めいれい is a command. Other compounds use a respectful or favorable sense, as in 令嬢 れいじょう, someone's daughter in respectful language; they should not be translated mechanically as commands.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine an instruction being issued from under the roof-like top and passed down through the lower strokes.

The indexing radical 人 is the two-stroke roof-like shape at the top, rather than its left-hand variant 亻.

## Notes

- Compare 令 and [今](今.md) いま, now: pay attention to the extra strokes in 令's lower part rather than treating the roof-like tops as enough to identify them.
- The respectful use in 令嬢 does not make every 令 compound polite language.
