# 致 — do humbly; bring about; send

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 至 (no. 133), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

致 is bringing something all the way to a point: sending or drawing it to a destination, or causing a result. As the verb 致す いたす it is the humble word for "do." [一致](../words/一致.md) いっち is agreement or correspondence, and [致す](../words/致す.md) いたす is to do, said humbly. JMdict glosses 致す "to do" and marks it as humble language usually written in kana.

The Daijisen character entry gives five senses for チ. One example of each:

- bringing something to its destination: 誘致 ゆうち, "attraction; lure; invitation";
- going all the way to the end: 致命傷 ちめいしょう, "fatal wound";
- the point where things end up: 合致 がっち, "agreement; concurrence; conformance," besides 一致;
- where feeling leads: 風致 ふうち, "taste; elegance; scenic beauty";
- handing back one's office: 致仕 ちし, "resignation."

More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [至](至.md), "reach; attain," at the left, and 攵, a shape like a hand holding a stick, at the right. Imagine a hand pushing something along until it reaches its destination. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 至 or 攵 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チ | on | [一致](../words/一致.md) いっち — agreement; correspondence |
| いた.す | kun | [致す](../words/致す.md) いたす — do humbly |

In 一致, 一 supplies イチ, which shortens to いっ before チ, and 致 supplies チ. In 致す, 致 writes いた and す is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In polite set phrases the verb is usually written in kana, as in お願いいたします.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows チ, with 誘致, 合致 and 風致 as examples, and いたす, with 致す. Practice selects チ and いた.す, the two readings, both of which have linked word articles.

Two further readings are outside both KANJIDIC2 and the official table. Daijisen has the headword おこす, an old verb written ▽遣す or ▽致す whose first sense is よこす, which JMdict glosses "to send; to forward" and "to hand over (e.g. money)," and the headword おくす, a sound change of おこす with the same meaning. Neither reading is asked here, and none of the three cards chosen to supply this card's wrong answers, 話, 買 and 学, is read おこす or おくす.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [一](一.md), "one," in 一致. 致す is 致 with okurigana only.
- [室](室.md), "room, chamber," and [倒](倒.md), "topple; collapse," also contain 至. [知](知.md), "know," is also read チ. [話](話.md), "talk, speak," [買](買.md), "buy," and [学](学.md), "study," are not similar in shape; they write the verbs 話す はなす, 買う かう and 学ぶ まなぶ, which, like 致す, take one kana of okurigana. These three cards supply this card's wrong answers. All are different kanji.
- [送](送.md), "send; escort," [届](届.md), "reach; deliver; notify," [至](至.md), "reach; attain," [到](到.md), "arrive; attain," [及](及.md), "reach; extend to; and," [達](達.md), "reach, plural suffix," [招](招.md), "invite," and [因](因.md), "cause; factor," overlap the senses "send," "bring to," "reach," "invite" and "cause" that 致 and its words carry; 致 is the key 至 itself with 攵 added, and 招 and 送 appear with 致 in 招致 and 送致, listed with the other Daijisen examples below. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "send" or "reach" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the チ senses: 引致 いんち, "arrest; custody," 招致 しょうち, "invitation; summons; bidding (e.g. to host the Olympics)," 送致 そうち, "sending; forwarding," and 拉致 らち, "taking captive; carrying away; kidnapping"; 致死 ちし, "lethal; fatal"; 極致 きょくち, "culmination; acme; height; peak"; and 雅致 がち, "artistry; good taste; elegance," and 筆致 ひっち, "stroke of the brush; touch."
