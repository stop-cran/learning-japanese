# 限 — limit

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (as left-hand 阝, no. 170) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

限 sets a boundary on amount, time, or eligibility. [限る](../words/限る.md) かぎる means limiting something;
限界 げんかい is a limit, while 期限 きげん can be a deadline.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine left-hand 阝 as a boundary and the bent lower stroke of 艮 as a stop at that boundary.
This is a modern memory aid, not historical derivation.

## Notes

In 会員に限る (かいいんにかぎる), "restricted to members," に marks the permitted group.
For the right-hand shape, notice the stacked horizontal strokes and the long lower turn of 艮.
[降](降.md) has a different right-hand group; [都](都.md) puts 阝 on the other side.
