# 忌 — mourning; abhor; death anniversary

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

忌 relates to avoiding something as taboo, to mourning, and to death anniversaries. 忌避 (きひ) is avoiding or evading, 忌中 (きちゅう) a period of mourning, and 回忌 an anniversary of a death.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 己, “self,” above 心, “heart.” 記 has the same 己 on the right and is also read キ.

## Notes

[忘](忘.md), [志](志.md) and [念](念.md) also have 心 at the bottom, and [己](己.md) alone means self. [嫌](嫌.md) means dislike, [避](避.md) avoid and [禁](禁.md) prohibit; [喪](喪.md) relates to mourning or loss. [憎](憎.md) means hate, close to “abhor,” and [悔](悔.md) includes mourning among its meanings.
