# 汗 — sweat; perspire

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

汗 concerns sweat or perspiration. 汗 あせ is the native noun, and 発汗 はっかん is a noun and suru expression for sweating.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside 干 and imagine drops of sweat on a hot day. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical or phonetic derivation.

The canonical radical 水, no. 85, appears on the left as 氵, the three-dot water form.

## Notes

- Learn 汗をかく あせをかく, sweat, as the whole expression. The linked 汗 article explains this pattern; do not simply attach する to the noun 汗.
- 発汗 demonstrates カン in a compound. Its はっ is the preceding 発 component, not an additional reading of 汗.
- Distinguish [干](干.md), dry or ebb, without the water dots, and [計](計.md), measure or plan, with 言 on the left.
