# 神 — deity

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 示 (as 礻, no. 113) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

神 can refer to a god or deity, but it also participates in words about the mind or spirit.
Compare [神社](../words/神社.md) じんじゃ, "Shinto shrine," with [精神](../words/精神.md) せいしん, "mind; spirit."
The second word is not automatically religious.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** look for the vertical stroke passing through the box-like 申 on the right.
Use it to distinguish 神 from neighboring 礻 characters; no historical derivation is proposed here.

## Notes

[社](社.md) can refer to a shrine, whereas 神 names the deity, not the building.
[祖](祖.md) concerns ancestors and [福](福.md) good fortune; sharing 礻 does not erase those distinctions.

神 has no grammatical marking for singular versus plural by itself. Translate "a god," "the deity," or "gods" as context requires.
