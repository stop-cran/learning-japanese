# 競 — compete; bid

**Strokes:** 20 · **Key (radical):** 立 (no. 117) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

競 concerns competing, contests and bidding. The on readings depend on the whole word: 競争 uses キョウ, while 競馬 uses ケイ. Native 競う and 競る are different verbs, not alternate pronunciations to substitute in every sentence.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See two similar 立-and-兄-like stacks side by side and imagine two contestants at the starting line. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a reconstruction of the character's history.

The canonical key is 立, no. 117; the repeated lower shapes do not change that indexing choice.

## Notes

- 競う is godan: 競います きそいます and 競って きそって. It concerns vying or competing; the particular ability or goal comes from the construction.
- 競る is also godan despite ending in せる: 競ります せります and 競って せって. Its dictionary senses include competing, bidding and selling at auction; do not treat it as a causative form of 競う.
- The linked [競技](../words/競技.md) article focuses on sporting events; [競争](../words/競争.md) is broader and can concern business rivalry. The two compounds overlap rather than forming disjoint categories.
