# 髪 — hair of the head

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 髟 (no. 190) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

髪 specifically concerns the hair on someone's head. [髪](../words/髪.md) かみ is not a general label
for fur or every kind of body hair.
髪を切りました。 (かみをきりました。) — I had my hair cut.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the upper 髟, with its three slanting strokes, as a flowing-hair cue,
then remember the [友](友.md)-shaped lower block. This is a modern shape aid, not historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| かみ | kun | [髪](../words/髪.md) かみ — hair on the head; 髪の毛 かみのけ — head hair |
| ハツ | on | 散髪 さんぱつ — a haircut |

ハツ changes to ぱつ in 散髪. Learn 白髪 (しらが), "white or gray hair," as a whole-word reading;
do not infer that が is the ordinary kun reading of 髪.

## Common words

- 散髪 さんぱつ — a haircut
