# 新 — new

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 斤 (axe, no. 69) · **Phonetic:** the left part (亲, probably indicating the sound シン) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

新 has 斤 (axe) on the right and, on the left, a part that is related to 辛 and 木 (tree). The standard explanation is freshly cut wood, hence "new, fresh". The left part is also thought to give the sound シン;
the exact history is debated, so treat the "cut a tree with an axe" picture as a helpful mnemonic.

## Notes

Its opposite is [古](古.md). Okurigana (the kana after the kanji, like い in 新しい): 新しい (あたらしい, with しい); the common misreading is あらたしい, which is wrong: the adjective is あたらしい, while the related word 新た is あらた.
Look-alike: 親 (parent, おや), which has 見 (see) in place of 斤. The adverb is 新たに あらたに.
