# 継 — succeed to; continue; patch

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

継 is taking up what came before and carrying it on: succeeding to a post, inheriting, keeping something going, even joining a torn piece back on. [継続](../words/継続.md) けいぞく is continuation; JMdict glosses it "continuation; continuance; maintenance; persistence; going on." The word article teaches "continuation; persistence."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ケイ. Sense 1 is to take over what follows, as in 継承, 継続, 継投, 後継 and 中継. Sense 2 is a parent-child relation without blood ties, as in 継子, 継父 and 継母. It lists the kun readings つぐ and まま; a separate headword まま【継】 is placed before a kinship noun to mark a parent-child relation without blood ties or a sibling relation through only one parent, as in 継母. The same character writes the verb 継ぐ つぐ, whose second sense is "to patch (clothes); to mend; to repair."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left. Imagine a thread being tied on to the end of another thread so that it can keep going. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 contributes meaning or that the right part contributes sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケイ | on | [継続](../words/継続.md) けいぞく — continuation; persistence |
| つ.ぐ | kun | 継ぐ つぐ — "to succeed (a person, to a position, etc.); to inherit; to take over; to follow"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| まま- | kun | 継母 ままはは — "stepmother"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 継続, 継 supplies ケイ and [続](続.md), "continue," supplies ゾク. In 継承 けいしょう, "inheritance; succession; accession," 継 supplies ケイ and [承](承.md), "accept; undertake," supplies ショウ; in 中継 ちゅうけい, "relay; hook-up," [中](中.md), "middle, inside," supplies チュウ and 継 supplies ケイ; in 継投 けいとう, "relieving the (starting) pitcher," [投](投.md), "throw," supplies トウ. In 継母 and 継子 ままこ, "stepchild," 継 is read まま and 母 and 子 are read はは and こ; 継父 is "stepfather," read けいふ or ままちち; 継母 is also read けいぼ. The noun 継ぎ つぎ has the glosses "patch; patching," "join; joining" and "successor; heir."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケイ, with 継続, 継承 and 中継, and つぐ, with 継ぐ and 継ぎ, marked ⇔ 接ぐ and 次ぐ. JMdict glosses 接ぐ つぐ "to join; to piece together; to set (bones)" and "to graft (onto a tree)," and 次ぐ つぐ "to follow; to come after; to come next (to); to rank next (to); to rank second (to)." JMdict lists 嗣ぐ, 続ぐ and 襲ぐ as rarely used kanji forms of 継ぐ. Practice selects ケイ, the reading of the linked word article 継続. つ.ぐ and まま- are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; the table does not list まま. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [続](続.md), "continue," supplying ゾク in 継続. In the other words named above, 承, 中, 投, [母](母.md), "mother," and [子](子.md), "child," are the other kanji.
- [続](続.md), "continue," [紹](紹.md), "introduce; inherit," [承](承.md), "accept; undertake," [接](接.md), "touch; join," [次](次.md), "next," [代](代.md), "replace, era," [修](修.md), "repair; amend; cultivate," [直](直.md), "direct; repair," [補](補.md), "supplement; make good," [縫](縫.md), "sew; stitch," and [繕](繕.md), "mend; darn; repair," overlap the senses of continuing, succeeding, inheriting, joining and mending that 継 and its words carry. 継 and 繕 both cover mending: 継ぐ is to patch clothes with a piece, while 繕う つくろう, "to mend; to patch up; to repair; to fix; to darn," is the general word for mending. 接 and 次 also share the reading つ.ぐ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [紙](紙.md), "paper," [雨](雨.md), "rain," and [魚](魚.md), "fish," look plausible because 紙 has the same thread key 糸 at the left, and 雨 and 魚 are common starter cards. They are read シ and かみ; ウ and あめ; and ギョ, うお and さかな. None shares a reading or a meaning with 継.
- Many other kanji read ケイ, such as [係](係.md), "connection; person in charge," [計](計.md), "measure; plan," and [経](経.md), so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
