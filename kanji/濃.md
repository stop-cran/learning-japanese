# 濃 — concentrated; dark

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

濃 expresses concentration, density, or intensity: a strong taste, dark color, or dense fog can be 濃い. The appropriate English translation depends on what is described.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a little water at the left of the crowded right-hand strokes: there is much material and little water, so the mixture is concentrated.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- 濃い is an i-adjective: 濃く こく and 濃かった こかった.
- [薄い](../words/薄い.md) can contrast with weak concentration or pale color. For a book's physical thickness, compare [厚い](../words/厚い.md); do not translate every English use of 'thick' as 濃い.
