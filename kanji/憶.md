# 憶 — remember; recall; guess

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

憶 is keeping something in mind and calling it back, and also forming a guess about what one cannot know for certain. [記憶](../words/記憶.md) きおく is memory or recollection; JMdict glosses it "memory; recollection; remembrance" and, in a second sense used in computing, "memory; storage."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for オク, with おもう as the kun reading: thinking over many things, as in 憶念 おくねん, "(a) thought one will never forget; something one will always remember," and 追憶 ついおく, "recollection; reminiscence"; keeping something in mind without forgetting it, as in 記憶; and guessing, as in 憶説 おくせつ, "hypothesis; speculation; surmise; conjecture," and 憶測 おくそく, "guess; speculation; supposition." JMdict also lists 臆説 and 臆念 as spellings of 憶説 and 憶念, and 臆測 as a rarely used spelling of 憶測; 臆 does not have a kanji card here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 忄, the form of [心](心.md), "heart, mind," used at the left, and [意](意.md), "meaning, intention," at the right; 意 is itself [音](音.md), "sound," above 心. Imagine a heart beside a heart that still hears a sound from long ago: a memory that keeps playing in the mind. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 忄 or 意 contributes meaning or sound, although [億](億.md), "hundred million," has the same 意 at the right and is also read オク. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| オク | on | [記憶](../words/記憶.md) きおく — memory; recollection |

In 記憶, 記 supplies キ and 憶 supplies オク. KANJIDIC2 lists no kun reading for 憶, so this card has no kun row or kun question. JMdict records 憶う as a rarely used spelling of 思う おもう, "to think; to consider; to believe; to reckon," and 憶える as a rarely used spelling of 覚える おぼえる, "to memorize; to memorise; to commit to memory; to learn by heart"; in these spellings 憶 stands for a native verb that is normally written with another kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows オク, with 記憶 and 追憶 as examples, and no kun reading; the Daijisen kun reading おもう is not in the table. Practice selects オク, the reading with a linked word article.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [記](記.md), "record in writing," in 記憶.
- [億](億.md), "hundred million," has the same 意 at the right, with 亻 at the left instead of 忄, and is also read オク. [意](意.md), "meaning, intention," is the whole right-hand part of 憶. [屋](屋.md), "roof, shop," is also read オク. All are different kanji.
- [覚](覚.md), "memorize; awaken," [想](想.md), "thought; conception," [念](念.md), "thought; attention," [思](思.md), "think, feel," [考](考.md), "think over," [察](察.md), "observe; infer," and [記](記.md), "record in writing," overlap the senses "remember," "memory," "think" and "guess" that 憶 and its words carry; JMdict lists 憶える as a rarely used spelling of 覚える and 憶う beside 思う, 想う and 念う as spellings of おもう, 念 appears in 憶念 and 記 in 記憶. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "remember" or "memory" as exclusive to any one character.
