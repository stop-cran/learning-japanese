# 且 — moreover; also

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

Learn 且 through [且つ](../words/且つ.md) かつ, "and; moreover": it adds another point or presents two descriptions together.
The word is usually written **かつ**; the kanji spelling does not change its pronunciation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a narrow shelf with two inner bars, then add a broad bottom bar for "and one more thing."
This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation. The bottom horizontal stroke is the indexing radical 一.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words or lookup qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ショ | on | 苟且（こうしょ）— archaic "makeshift; temporary." Recognition only, not a practice reading. |
| ソ | on | Retained source-only dictionary metadata; no allocated word example is established in the sources inspected for this card. |
| ショウ | on | Retained source-only dictionary metadata; no allocated word example is established in the sources inspected for this card. |
| か.つ | kun | [且つ](../words/且つ.md) かつ — and; moreover, usually written かつ. The dot marks the boundary before written つ. |

Practice selects only **か.つ**. The other readings remain available for lookup; this is not a claim that they never occur.
The separate whole-word reading 苟且（かりそめ）, "temporary; trifling; perfunctory," does not establish an individual ソ reading of 且.

## Common words

- [且つ](../words/且つ.md) かつ — and; moreover (usually kana).

## Notes

Compare [目](目.md), "eye": 且's bottom bar extends beyond its upright sides.
[日](日.md), "sun; day," has only one inner horizontal stroke.
[助](助.md), "help; rescue," adds 力 on the right; that character's reading is not a reading of 且.

かつ is not a universal replacement for English "and." Its linked article shows additive descriptions, not an unrestricted noun-listing rule.
