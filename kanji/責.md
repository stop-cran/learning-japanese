# 責 — blame; hold responsible

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

責 connects blame with being answerable for something. 責める せめる is to blame or reproach,
whereas [責任](../words/責任.md) せきにん is responsibility or duty.
Having responsibility does not itself mean that someone has done something wrong.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine an account that must be answered for, with ruled bars over the 貝-shaped ledger.
This is a modern memory picture, not an etymology.

## Notes

In 人を責める (ひとをせめる), "blame someone," を marks the person blamed.
The top resembles [青](青.md), but the bottom is 貝, not 青's lower part.
[買](買.md) and [賛](賛.md) also have 貝 underneath; neither means assigning blame.
