# 桜 — cherry tree; blossom

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

桜 names a cherry tree and its blossoms. The linked [桜](../words/桜.md) article uses the reading さくら; JMdict glosses it “cherry tree; cherry blossom; sakura.” The same entry also records other whole-word senses, including light pink and a fake buyer or paid audience member, usually written in kana; those are not meanings of the tree itself.

Digital Daijisen gives two senses for オウ. One names the cherry tree, as in 桜花 おうか, cherry blossom; the other names the fruit-cherry tree (ミザクラ, mizakura), as in 桜桃 おうとう, cherry tree (especially sweet cherry or Nanking cherry).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md) at the left and [女](女.md) at the right. Imagine a tree covered in blossoms. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 木 or 女 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| オウ | on | 桜花 おうか — cherry blossom; 観桜 かんおう — cherry blossom viewing; 桜桃 おうとう — cherry (tree; esp. sweet cherry or Nanking cherry); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ヨウ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; no ordinary JMdict entry verifies this as a reading of 桜; not selected for questions here |
| さくら | kun | [桜](../words/桜.md) さくら — cherry tree; cherry blossom; sakura |

In 桜花, 観桜 and 桜桃, the Daijisen character entry places 桜 under オウ. The fruit word 桜桃 is also read さくらんぼ, as is 桜ん坊 さくらんぼ; JMdict marks this word usually written using kana alone. Both spellings refer to cherry fruit. In 桜桃 read さくらんぼ the reading belongs to the whole word, so do not split it among the characters. In 桜ん坊, 桜 is plainly さくら, followed by ん and 坊 read ぼ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows オウ with 桜花 and 観桜, and さくら with 桜, 桜色 and 葉桜. 桜色 さくらいろ means light pink; pale pink; cherry-blossom pink. 葉桜 はざくら means a cherry tree in leaf, or one whose blossoms have fallen and revealed young leaves. Practice selects さくら, the reading with a linked word article. オウ and ヨウ are retained from KANJIDIC2; being outside the practice set does not make them invalid. The table gives 櫻 as the older form.

## Notes

- The common-word article is for the single character 桜, so it has no other kanji.
- [女](女.md) is the shape at the right of 桜; [机](机.md) shares 木 at the left; [杏](杏.md) is another tree name with a different meaning. All are different kanji.
- [花](花.md) overlaps the blossom sense and [咲](咲.md) means “blossom; bloom”; [木](木.md) and [樹](樹.md) overlap the broader tree sense. [株](株.md)’s rooted-plant sense overlaps this cherry-tree title, and the 株 card declares the exclusion for this pair. The other listed cards are kept out of this card’s quiz options. Do not treat “cherry tree” or “blossom” as exclusive to any one character.
