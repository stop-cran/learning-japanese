# 虹 — rainbow

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 虫 (no. 142) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[虹](../words/虹.md) にじ is a rainbow. The visible colours arise from
light interacting with droplets; the character is not a general word for
an insect despite its indexing radical.
In the compound 虹彩 こうさい, the complete word means the iris of the eye.
That anatomical meaning is not a separate standalone sense of 虹.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a tiny 虫-shaped observer
next to an 工-shaped support, watching a rainbow.
This invented scene recalls the present shape; it does not explain the
historical reason for the insect-shaped component.

## Readings

| Reading | Kind | Example or disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| コウ | on | 虹彩 こうさい, iris of the eye. The dictionary word illustrates コウ but has no linked support article here, so this reading is not practised. |
| にじ | kun | [虹](../words/虹.md) にじ, rainbow; directly supported by the existing native-noun article. |

The full コウ/にじ metadata is unchanged. 日本国語大辞典 records
のじ as an ancient eastern dialect form and ぬじ as another attested
form. 字通's old-dictionary quotation also has ヲニジ.
These are qualified lookup records, not corrections of modern にじ.

## Common words

- [虹](../words/虹.md) にじ - rainbow.
- 虹色 にじいろ - rainbow-coloured.
- 虹彩 こうさい - iris of the eye, not a second pronunciation of the noun にじ.

## Notes

- JMdict includes 霓 as a rare spelling of にじ. The complete family is retained; this is not an instruction to replace the normal spelling.
- JMdict's separate 二次 entry retains 虹 as a search-only spelling and notes an anime/manga-related two-dimensional sense. That entry is not the ordinary rainbow noun and does not change this card's meaning.
- 字通 records historical uses for a rainbow-coloured flag, bridge, oil dish, and uses in place of 訌 with disorder, dispute or せめる glosses. It also describes an old dragon-like conception of the rainbow. These source-specific uses are not asserted as freely interchangeable modern words.
- [弧](弧.md), arc, is a broader shape label. [橋](橋.md), bridge, [皿](皿.md), dish, and [灯](灯.md), lamp, are guarded conservatively around the historical object glosses. An oil dish is not oil itself.
- [乱](乱.md), disorder, [争](争.md), dispute, [攻](攻.md), attack, [討](討.md), attack/discuss, [責](責.md), blame, [詰](詰.md), press, and [論](論.md), argument, guard the unresolved range of those historical glosses. [蛇](蛇.md), serpent, guards the broad old creature label. The direct pairs do not assert universal Japanese synonymy or extend other cards' exclusions transitively.
- [紅](紅.md), crimson/deep red, [功](功.md), merit/achievement, and [石](石.md), stone, are distinct meanings. 工 at the right of 虹 also occurs in 紅 and 功; 石 is a simpler box-and-crossing-strokes comparison.
