# 程 — extent

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

程 concerns how far something goes: its degree, amount, or span.
[程度](../words/程度.md) ていど refers to a level or gives an approximate amount.
日程 にってい is a schedule or agenda.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** treat the stacked horizontal lines below the right-hand 口 as levels on a scale.
This is a visual reminder of extent, not a historical analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

## Common words

- [程度](../words/程度.md) ていど — degree; approximately
- 日程 にってい — schedule
- ほど — extent; about, as in 一時間ほど (いちじかんほど), "about an hour"

## Notes

JMdict marks the word 程 ほど as usually written in kana. Recognizing the kanji does not require using it in every ほど phrase.
[秋](秋.md), [種](種.md), and [私](私.md) share the left-hand 禾; compare their right halves with 程's 口 over 王-like bars.
