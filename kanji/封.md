# 封 — seal; close

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

封 concerns a seal or closing something. 封 ふう is a seal, and 封筒 ふうとう is an envelope. The different on reading ホウ occurs in 封建 ほうけん, feudalism; the complete word selects its meaning and reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the two 土-like shapes beside 寸 as a parcel being pressed closed. This is an invented modern-shape scene, not the historical origin of sealing or feudal institutions.

## Notes

- Learn ふうとう with its long う. The ホウ of 封建 is not a direction to pronounce envelope ほうとう.
- Feudalism is the sense of the complete compound 封建, not a universal translation of every 封.
- The canonical radical is 寸 at the right. Similar visible pieces in [寺](寺.md) do not imply the same meaning.
