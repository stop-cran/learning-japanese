# 栄 — flourish; glory

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

栄 concerns flourishing, prosperity, or glory. 栄える さかえる means to prosper or flourish; 栄え はえ can name glory or splendor. 栄養 えいよう is the established word for nutrition, not a word to translate by mechanically joining two English character glosses.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the small upper strokes over 冖 and 木, and imagine a tree flourishing under a bright canopy. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical derivation.

This does not mean that the spelling 見栄 is unsuitable for everyday use.

## Notes

- 栄養 えいよう illustrates エイ for 栄. Its final よう belongs to 養; that sound sequence is not evidence for 栄's separate ヨウ reading.
- The linked 栄える article teaches さかえる, prosper. The different はえる entry means shine or look attractive and also lists 映える. The two readings are not freely interchangeable.
- 出来栄え できばえ demonstrates the bound -ばえ form in a word about the result or workmanship. JMdict also lists 出来映え; the spelling list does not rank their frequency.
- 栄え はえ also has 映え as a listed spelling. Keep this noun and its reading separate from the verb 栄える さかえる.
