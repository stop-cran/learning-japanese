# 姿 — figure; appearance; posture

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

姿 is the visible form of a person or thing: a figure, an appearance, or the way someone holds themselves. [姿](../words/姿.md) すがた is a figure or appearance, and [姿勢](../words/姿勢.md) しせい is posture or attitude.

The Daijisen character entry gives シ the sense of the form and look of the body, as in 姿勢, 姿態 したい, "figure; form; style," 容姿 ようし, "(physical) appearance (of a person); one's face and figure," and 雄姿 ゆうし, "majestic figure; imposing figure; impressive appearance." For すがた it lists 姿見 すがたみ, "full-length mirror," 姿絵 すがたえ, "portrait," 旅姿 たびすがた, "traditional Japanese travel outfit; travel kit," and 寝姿 ねすがた, "one's figure during sleep." JMdict glosses the word 姿 as "figure; form; shape," "appearance; dress; guise," and "state; condition; picture; image," among other senses.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [次](次.md), "next," at the top and [女](女.md), "woman, female," at the bottom. Imagine a woman stepping up next in line and striking a pose so that her whole figure can be seen. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 次 or 女 contributes meaning or sound, although 姿 and 次 are both read シ. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | [姿勢](../words/姿勢.md) しせい — posture; attitude |
| すがた | kun | [姿](../words/姿.md) すがた — figure; appearance |

In 姿勢, 姿 supplies シ and 勢 supplies セイ. Written alone, 姿 is read すがた with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 後ろ姿 うしろすがた, "(a person's) appearance from behind; person as seen from behind; back view," the ろ is okurigana belonging to 後, and 姿 is still すがた.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シ, with 姿勢, 容姿 and 雄姿 as examples, and すがた, with 姿. Practice selects both readings, each of which has a linked word article.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [勢](勢.md), "force; momentum," in 姿勢. The word 姿 is this kanji alone.
- [次](次.md), "next," is the top part of 姿 and is also read シ. [資](資.md), "resources; capital," has the same 次 on top and is also read シ, and [委](委.md), "entrust; committee," has the same 女 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [形](形.md), "shape; form," [体](体.md), "body, form," [状](状.md), "condition; form," and [象](象.md), "elephant; image; shape," overlap the word 姿, "figure; form; shape" and "state; condition; picture; image." [容](容.md), "contain; appearance," overlaps the appearance in 容姿, and [影](影.md), "shadow; silhouette," overlaps a figure seen only in outline. [態](態.md), "attitude; condition," and [構](構.md), "build; stance," overlap 姿勢, "attitude; approach; stance," and [像](像.md), "image; statue," overlaps "picture; image." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "figure" or "appearance" as exclusive to any one character.
