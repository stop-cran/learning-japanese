# 代 — replace, era

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

代 can mean taking another's place, a generation or era, and a charge or fee.
These are uses to learn in words: [時代](../words/時代.md) is an era, while 代わる is to take someone's place.

The modern shape has 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), beside 弋.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** one person hands a hook-shaped marker to the next person taking their place.
This is a memory aid, not a historical claim.
