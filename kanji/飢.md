# 飢 — starve; be hungry

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 食 (as 飠, no. 184), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

飢 concerns hunger from a lack of food, ranging from being hungry to
starving; its words usually describe serious deprivation, and for everyday
hunger お腹が空く おなかがすく is the usual expression.
[飢える](../words/飢える.md) うえる means to starve or be famished, and it
also describes being starved of something, such as affection, or hungry for
knowledge. [飢饉](../words/飢饉.md) ききん is a famine or crop failure and
also a chronic shortage, as in 水飢饉, a water shortage. 飢餓 きが is
starvation, and 飢え うえ is hunger.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: being hungry for lack
of food, as in 飢餓, and a shortage of food after a poor harvest, as in
飢饉. It adds that 饑 can be used interchangeably with 飢.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 飠, the left-hand form of
[食](食.md), beside 几. Imagine food set out beside a small table-like 几
with nothing left on it. This is a memory picture for the modern shape, not
a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キ | on | [飢饉](../words/飢饉.md) ききん - famine; chronic shortage |
| う.える | kun | [飢える](../words/飢える.md) うえる - starve; hunger for |

Practice selects キ and う.える, each with a linked example. 飢える writes
う with 飢 and える in kana; it is an intransitive ichidan verb, so the
final る changes while the stem うえ- stays: うえる becomes うえて, 飢えて, and
うえない, 飢えない. JMdict also lists かつえる,
restricted to the spellings 飢える and 餓える; it is not a KANJIDIC2 or
Joyo reading, so practice does not select it. The official Joyo table lists
キ, with 飢餓, and うえる, with 飢える and 飢え.

## Notes

- [凡](凡.md), "commonplace; mediocre," and [処](処.md), "deal with;
  dispose," share 几 with 飢 but differ in meaning. [館](館.md), "hall;
  large building," shares the 食 component, written 飠 at the left, but
  names a building.
- [食](食.md), "eat, food," supplies the 飠 form and the hunger
  association, but eating and food are not meanings of 飢.
- A chronic shortage, as in 飢饉, overlaps [乏](乏.md), "scarce; lacking,"
  [欠](欠.md), "lack; gap," and [貧](貧.md), "poor; impoverished." The
  crop-failure sense shares the word "failure" with [敗](敗.md), "failure;
  defeat."
- Being starved of or hungry for something, as with 飢える, overlaps
  [欲](欲.md), "desire," and [恋](恋.md), "romantic love; longing." These
  direct meaning comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as
  identical in every compound.
