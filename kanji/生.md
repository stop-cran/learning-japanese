# 生 — life, birth

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 生 (no. 100) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

生 is commonly explained as a pictograph of a plant sprouting from the ground: a shoot with a base line below. From "sprouting" come
"grow", "be born", "live", "raw, fresh" and "unprocessed". Few kanji have so many readings, because the idea
was used for many native Japanese words (生きる, 生まれる, 生える, 生).

- [先生](../words/先生.md) せんせい — teacher
- [学生](../words/学生.md) がくせい — student

## Notes

When a kanji has this many readings, learn by word rather than by rule: セイ is the typical reading in 学生 and 先生, ショウ in older compounds
(一生, 誕生), and the kun readings by the verb (生きる, 生まれる, 生える).
Special readings include 芝生 しばふ, 弥生 やよい and 生憎 あいにく.

Look-alikes: 牛 (cow) has a vertical stroke crossing its horizontals and no long base line; 生 has the long horizontal at the bottom. [土](土.md) (earth) and [年](年.md) (year) share some strokes with 生 but differ in meaning.
