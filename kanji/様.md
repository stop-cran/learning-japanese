# 様 — manner; honorific suffix

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

様 has two especially useful roles: describing the way something appears or behaves, and adding respect after a name.
[様子](../words/様子.md) (ようす) means a situation, appearance, or manner.
The suffix 様 (さま) is an honorific: an ending that shows respect toward the person named.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine 木 as a nameplate post and the marks on the right as a respectful title written on the plate.
This invented scene is not the character's history.

## Notes

様子 is ようす, not ようこ.
The upper right resembles 羊, also visible in [洋](洋.md); 様 has 木 on the left and further strokes below the right-hand upper part.
Read the whole word before deciding whether 様 is descriptive or honorific.
