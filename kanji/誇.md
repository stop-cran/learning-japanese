# 誇 — pride; boast

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

誇 is about pride and boasting. KANJIDIC2 lists “boast,” “be proud,” “pride,” and “triumphantly,” and Kanjipedia gives ほこる, じまんする (to brag) and 大げさに言う (to exaggerate). The noun [誇り](../words/誇り.md) ほこり is glossed in JMdict as “pride; boast; credit (to).” The verb 誇る ほこる (JMdict 1267760) means “to boast of; to be proud of; to take pride in.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [言](言.md), "say; words," with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it, stands on the left. On the right is 夸: [大](大.md), "big, large," on top of [二](二.md), "two," with one more hooked stroke at the bottom. Imagine someone using big words, twice over, to talk up what they have done: a boast. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 夸 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists コ and ほこる, the same readings as KANJIDIC2. KANJIDIC2 records no name readings, and Digital Daijisen's character entry gives none.

These three cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [慢](慢.md) (“arrogance; slackness”): Kanjipedia gives じまんする for 誇, and JMdict glosses 自慢 じまん, a word written with 慢, as “pride (in one's achievements, possessions, etc.); self-praise; boast” (entry 1318680); JMdict also glosses 誇らしい with “proud; haughty; arrogant” (entry 1267730).
- [功](功.md) (“merit; achievement”): JMdict glosses 誇り with “credit (to),” and KANJIDIC2 lists “credit” for 功.
- [凱](凱.md) (“victory song; triumph”): KANJIDIC2 lists “triumphantly” for 誇, and JMdict glosses 勝ち誇る かちほこる as “to triumph; to be elated with success” (entry 1346100).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 恥 (“shame”), 怒 (“anger”), and 疑 (“doubt; suspect”); none means pride or boasting.
