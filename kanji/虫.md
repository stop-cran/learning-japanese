# 虫 — insect; bug

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 虫 (no. 142) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

虫 names insects and bugs, with a broader everyday range that can include worms and other small creatures. Figurative uses concern temperament or a person absorbed in one activity; do not insist on a literal insect in every compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See a small creature with a 中-like body and a curled lower stroke beside a dot. This imagined bug is a memory aid, not an account of the original drawing.

## Notes

- 虫 is itself the indexing radical, no. 142. The キ example 虫部 is a dictionary term for that radical; it does not make キ the ordinary standalone reading of the animal word.
- 虫歯 むしば names tooth decay or a decayed tooth. Its spelling is not a modern medical claim that literal worms cause cavities.
