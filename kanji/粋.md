# 粋 — stylish; essence; pure

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 米 (no. 119), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

粋 is the unmixed best of something: pure, or the essence. It also names a quality of style. [純粋](../words/純粋.md) じゅんすい is "pure; true; genuine; unmixed." Read いき, 粋 is JMdict "chic; smart; stylish; tasteful; refined; sophisticated."

The Daijisen character entry gives 3 senses for スイ. One example of each:

- 1, unmixed, pure: 純粋.
- 2, of fine quality, the cream of something: 抜粋 ばっすい, JMdict "extract; excerpt; selection."
- 3, いき, stylish: 粋人 すいじん, JMdict "person of refined tastes; sophisticated person."

Its kun reading is いき. 〔粹〕 is given as the old form.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [米](米.md), "rice grain," at the left (the radical) and, at the right, a part that looks like 九 above 十 (a simplified form of [卒](卒.md), "graduate; finish"; 卒 itself is not written here). Imagine grain polished until only the pure best is left. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 米 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| スイ | on | [純粋](../words/純粋.md) じゅんすい — pure; genuine; unmixed |
| いき | kun | 粋 いき — "chic; smart; stylish; tasteful; refined; sophisticated"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 純粋, [純](純.md) supplies ジュン and 粋 supplies スイ. In 抜粋, [抜](抜.md), "extract," supplies バツ, written ばっ before す, and 粋 supplies スイ. In 粋人, [人](人.md), "person," supplies ジン and 粋 supplies スイ. 粋 alone is read いき as a whole word. JMdict also lists 粋 read すい as a separate entry (2848938), "essence; the best; cream" and "chic; smart; stylish; tasteful; refined; sophisticated."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows スイ, with 粋人, 純粋 and 精粋, and いき, with 粋. JMdict glosses 精粋 せいすい as "purity; unselfishness." Practice selects スイ, the reading of the linked word article. いき is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- In 純粋, [純](純.md), "pure; genuine," supplies ジュン. Its title overlaps this card's "pure," so it is in this card's quiz exclusions.
- Other Daijisen examples: 生粋 きっすい, JMdict "pure; genuine; trueborn; natural-born," is a whole word and is not split; 国粋 こくすい is "national characteristics"; 無粋 ぶすい is "boorish; inelegant; unpolished; unromantic; tactless."
- [米](米.md), "rice grain," [粉](粉.md), "flour; powder," and [糖](糖.md), "sugar," share the 米 shape at the left with 粋, so they look plausible. They are read ベイ, マイ and こめ; フン, こ and こな; and トウ, and mean rice, flour and sugar. None shares a reading or a meaning with 粋.
- [潔](潔.md), "clean; pure; upright," [清](清.md), "pure, clean," [精](精.md), "refined; vitality," [素](素.md), "unadorned; element," [真](真.md), "true, genuine," [英](英.md), "English; outstanding," [傑](傑.md), "outstanding; excellence; hero," [優](優.md), "gentle; superior," [品](品.md), "goods, refinement," [美](美.md), "beauty," and the B147 card [秀](秀.md), "excel; outstanding," overlap the senses "pure," "genuine," "essence," "excellent," "refined" and "stylish" that 粋 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [出](出.md), [剤](剤.md), [吹](吹.md), [垂](垂.md), [推](推.md), [水](水.md), [炊](炊.md), [睡](睡.md), [穂](穂.md) and [誰](誰.md) share the reading スイ with 粋, and [息](息.md) and [気](気.md) share いき, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
