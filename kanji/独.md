# 独 — alone; sole; Germany (abbreviation)

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (as 犭, no. 94), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

独 is being single or alone, and in some words it stands for Germany. [独り言](../words/独り言.md) ひとりごと is talking to oneself, [独特](../words/独特.md) どくとく is distinctive, [独立](../words/独立.md) どくりつ is independence and [独身](../words/独身.md) どくしん is being unmarried.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses: being without a partner, as in 独身 and 孤独 こどく, "solitude; loneliness; isolation"; being the only one, as in 独立, 独特, 独学 どくがく, "self-education; self-instruction; self-study," and 単独 たんどく, "sole; single; solo"; acting by oneself without regard for others, as in 独裁 どくさい, "dictatorship; despotism; autocracy," 独占 どくせん, "monopoly; monopolization; exclusivity," and 独断 どくだん, "one's own judgement (judgment); decision made without consulting others; arbitrary decision"; and Germany, as in 日独 にちどく, "Japan and Germany; Japanese-German," and 独語 どくご, "German (language)," a word JMdict also glosses "monologue; soliloquy."

JMdict also lists 独 on its own, read どく, as an abbreviation glossed "Germany."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [犬](犬.md), "dog," at the left, in its reduced form 犭, and [虫](虫.md), "insect; bug," at the right. Imagine a dog and a bug, each off on its own. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 犬 or 虫 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ドク | on | [独立](../words/独立.md) どくりつ — independence; self-reliance; [独特](../words/独特.md) どくとく — distinctive; characteristic; [独身](../words/独身.md) どくしん — unmarried; single |
| トク | on | 独鈷 とっこ - single-pronged vajra, a pointed metal implement used in esoteric Buddhist rituals; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ひと.り | kun | [独り言](../words/独り言.md) ひとりごと — talking to oneself; soliloquy |

In 独立, 独 supplies ドク and 立 supplies リツ. In 独特, 特 supplies トク, so that トク does not belong to 独. In 独身, 身 supplies シン. In 独り言, 独 writes ひと and り is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 言 supplies ごと, the voiced form of こと. JMdict also lists 独り ひとり, with the sense "being alone; being by oneself."

The トク reading is retained from KANJIDIC2. In 独鈷 とっこ, 独 supplies トク, which shortens to とっ before コ, the reading of 鈷; JMdict also lists どっこ. The Daijisen character entry also lists 独活 うど, "udo (plant related to ginseng, used in medicine and cooking) (Aralia cordata)," and 独楽 こま, "spinning top," as difficult readings, and JMdict gives 独逸 and 独乙 as ateji, kanji chosen for sound, for ドイツ, "Germany." JMdict marks all three words as usually written in kana alone, so each is learned as a whole word, not split across its kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ドク, with 独立, 独断 and 単独 as examples, and ひとり, with 独り and 独り者. トク is not in the official table. Practice selects ドク and ひと.り, the readings with linked word articles; トク is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid. The Daijisen heading and the official Joyo table also show the older form 獨, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [言](言.md), "say; words," in 独り言, [特](特.md), "special," in 独特, [立](立.md), "stand; set up," in 独立 and [身](身.md), "one's body; oneself," in 独身.
- [犬](犬.md), "dog," is the key at the left, written 犭, and [虫](虫.md), "insect; bug," is the shape at the right. [猫](猫.md), "cat," has the same 犭 at the left with a different shape beside it. [毒](毒.md), "poison; venom," is also read ドク, and [読](読.md), "read," is also read ドク and トク. All are different kanji.
- [特](特.md), "special," [単](単.md), "single; simple," [個](個.md), "individual; item counter," [自](自.md), "self, oneself," [一](一.md), "one," [身](身.md), "one's body; oneself," and [片](片.md), "one-sided; sheet," overlap the senses "single," "alone," "uniqueness" and "oneself" that 独 and its words carry; 特 also supplies the トク of 独特. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "alone" or "single" as exclusive to any one character.
