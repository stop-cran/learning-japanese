# 宮 — palace; imperial shrine

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宮 can be a palace or a Shinto shrine. In 神宮 (じんぐう), think of the shrine sense; in 宮殿 (きゅうでん), think of a palace. The character does not make every shrine a palace.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find the roof and the two box-like forms underneath. [官](官.md) has a roof too, but its lower outline is joined differently.

## Notes

[富](富.md) ends with 田, and [宙](宙.md) has 由 under its roof. Compare [官](官.md) for the shape, [社](社.md) for the shrine sense, and [殿](殿.md) for the palace sense. 神宮 and 神社 (じんじゃ) are not interchangeable titles.
