# 穴 — hole; pit; cavity

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 穴 (no. 116) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

穴 is a hole. [穴](../words/穴.md) あな is the everyday word, and JMdict gives it these senses: "hole; opening; orifice; perforation," "pit; hollow; cavity; hole (e.g. in the ground)," and "burrow; den; lair; holt; hole." It is also used figuratively: "deficit; shortage; (financial) hole; loss," "flaw; fault; defect; weak point; hole (in a theory, plan, etc.)," and "good place not many people know about; well-kept secret."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ケツ: 1, a hole, as in 穴居 and 墓穴; 2, an acupuncture point in Chinese medicine. Its kun reading is あな.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a cover-like top, 宀, over [八](八.md), "eight," spread below it. Imagine the mouth of a tunnel under a lid of earth, with two sides sloping down. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケツ | on | 穴居 けっきょ — "cave dwelling; troglodytism"; 墓穴 ぼけつ — "grave (pit)"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| あな | kun | [穴](../words/穴.md) あな — hole; opening |

In 穴居, 穴 supplies ケツ, written けっ before き, and [居](居.md) supplies キョ. In 墓穴, [墓](墓.md) supplies ボ and 穴 supplies ケツ; JMdict also gives the reading はかあな for 墓穴. 穴 alone is read あな as a whole native word.

JMdict has a separate entry for 穴 read けつ (1254490): it is tagged "word usually written using kana alone" and colloquial, and its senses are colloquial ("ass; arse; buttocks," "rear; end") plus "acupuncture point." It is a different word from 穴 あな and is not taught here.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケツ, with 穴居 and 墓穴, and あな, with 穴. Practice selects あな, the reading of the linked word article. ケツ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 穴 is a single-kanji word, so the linked word has no other kanji. In the examples above, [居](居.md), "be present; dwell," supplies キョ in 穴居 and [墓](墓.md), "grave; tomb," supplies ボ in 墓穴.
- [究](究.md), "investigate thoroughly," and [突](突.md), "thrust; sudden," carry 穴 as their radical, so they look plausible. [定](定.md), "determine; settle," only has a similar cover-like top. They are read キュウ, ク and きわ.める; トツ, カ and つ.く; and テイ, ジョウ and さだ.める. None shares a reading or a meaning with 穴.
- [凹](凹.md), "concave; sunken," [巣](巣.md), "nest; hive; den," [欠](欠.md), "lack; gap," [差](差.md), "difference; gap," and [窓](窓.md), "window," overlap the senses "hollow," "burrow; den," "deficit," "gap" and "opening" that 穴 and its word carry. 欠 also shares the reading ケツ. The B147 card [秘](秘.md), "secret; conceal," overlaps the figurative sense "well-kept secret" of the word 穴, and is excluded here. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [傑](傑.md), [欠](欠.md), [決](決.md), [潔](潔.md), [結](結.md) and [血](血.md) share the reading ケツ with 穴, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
