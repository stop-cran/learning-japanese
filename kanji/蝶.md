# 蝶 — butterfly

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 虫 (no. 142) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

蝶 means **butterfly**. It names a kind of insect, not just one species. It also appears in words for objects, such as a bow tie.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [虫](虫.md) on the left. On the right, a [世](世.md)-like shape sits above [木](木.md). Imagine a butterfly near a tree. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation of the character.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チョウ | on | [蝶](../words/蝶.md) ちょう - butterfly; 蝶ネクタイ ちょうネクタイ - bow tie |

**チョウ** is an on-reading even in the standalone noun 蝶 (ちょう). The full source metadata has no kun-reading entries. 蝶々 (ちょうちょう / ちょうちょ) also means butterfly; learn those complete word forms rather than treating ちょうちょ as the reading of one 蝶.

## Notes

- The insect noun can also be written ちょう or チョウ. Historical kana spelling **てふ**, shown in dictionaries, is not an additional modern reading prompt.
- The source's grade-9/name-use classification does not mean 蝶 is used only in personal names. This lesson teaches the animal noun and selected non-name words, not name readings.
- [葉](葉.md), leaf, has a plant top rather than 虫 on the left. [世](世.md), world/generation, is only the smaller shape recalled in the mnemonic. [採](採.md), pick/collect/adopt, has a hand-shaped left side and a different upper-right shape.
- [虫](虫.md), insect/bug, is a broader category, so it is not an unambiguous wrong answer for a butterfly. 蛾 (が), moth, is a different animal name, not an antonym; do not infer a strict daytime-versus-nighttime rule from the two words.
- A word containing 蝶 need not name an insect: 蝶ネクタイ and 蝶番 name objects. Learn the whole word rather than translating every occurrence literally.
