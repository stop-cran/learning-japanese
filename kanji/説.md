# 説 — explanation; theory

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

説 can concern an explanation, theory, or opinion. [説明](../words/説明.md) せつめい is an explanation;
小説 しょうせつ is fiction. A character's basic associations help organize vocabulary,
but "small explanation" is not a translation of 小説.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine words from 言 being explained by a figure whose mouth
and two lower strokes stand on the right. This is a modern visual story, not an origin claim.

## Notes

An explanation is not necessarily proof that a claim is true.
The speech component is shared with [読](読.md), [記](記.md), and [調](調.md);
inspect the open lower strokes on the right of 説 rather than guessing from 言 alone.
