# 蒸 — steam; sultry heat

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

蒸 concerns steam, heating with steam, and oppressive humid heat. The food-cooking verb 蒸す むす and the weather use of the same verb are related but have different grammatical roles.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture steam rising through a basket beneath the upper 艹, with the four bottom dots 灬 supplying heat. The middle is 丞-shaped; this picture is a memory aid, not a reconstruction of the character's history.

The indexing radical 艸 appears at the top as 艹. The visible heat-like dots are not the indexing radical.

## Notes

- The セイ example 蒸篭 is usually written in kana as せいろ; 蒸籠 is another spelling in the same dictionary family. This is not a reason to read 蒸気 as せいき.
- 蒸す is transitive for steaming something, but intransitive when the weather is hot and humid. 蒸れる is an ichidan verb; 蒸らす is a transitive godan verb. Learn the whole verb rather than swapping the kana endings.
