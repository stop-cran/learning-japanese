# 海 — sea, ocean

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **Phonetic:** 毎 · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

海 is 氵 (water) plus 毎 (older form 每), which is generally taken as the sound component; the sound link is hard to see today (カイ versus マイ).
A story that links 毎 with a mother ("the sea as mother of all things") is popular but should be treated as a mnemonic only, not as the etymology.
It means "sea, ocean", and is also used in the sense of a wide expanse: 雲海 うんかい (sea of clouds).

## Common words

- 海 うみ — sea
- [北海道](../words/北海道.md) ほっかいどう — Hokkaido
- 海外 かいがい — overseas (as in 海外旅行 かいがいりょこう, see [旅行](../words/旅行.md))

## Notes

- Compare the other water kanji [川](川.md) (river) and [水](水.md) (water).
- 海外 かいがい, "outside the sea", means abroad, overseas.
- Look-alikes: [毎](毎.md) (every), the right part of 海 without 氵; and 梅 (plum) and 悔 (regret) share the same 毎 part.
