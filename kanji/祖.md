# 祖 — ancestor

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 示 (as 礻, no. 113) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

祖 looks back along a family line or toward a founder.
In [祖父](../words/祖父.md) そふ, "grandfather," and 祖母 そぼ, "grandmother," it places the relative a generation before one's parents.
先祖 せんぞ reaches further back to ancestors.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the stacked bars of 且 on the right as a reminder of generations.
This is not a claim that the bars historically represented a family tree.

## Notes

Compare [組](組.md): both have 且 on the right, but 祖 has 礻 and 組 has 糸.
[神](神.md) has 申 on the right; [福](福.md) has a different, wider stack of shapes.

おじいさん, "grandfather," has the kanji spelling お祖父さん, but JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana.
Do not read that whole expression mechanically as おそふさん.
