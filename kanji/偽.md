# 偽 — fake; false

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

偽 means false or fake. Digital Daijisen describes it as making something look genuine in order to deceive. A common example is [偽造](../words/偽造.md) ぎぞう, forgery or counterfeiting.

## Notes

Here “source-only” means that a source lists the reading but no checked word example uses it; it does not mean カ is invalid for 偽 in every dictionary or context.

虚 (“empty; void; false”) appears with 偽 in 虚偽, and 装 (“attire; outfit; pretend”) appears in 偽装. 模 (“imitate; model; pattern”) and 倣 (“imitate; emulate”) overlap with copying, and 仮 (“temporary; assumed”) can describe something assumed rather than real. These characters overlap with parts of 偽's meaning range without being interchangeable.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 耳 (“ear”), 山 (“mountain”), and 白 (“white”); none means fake or false.
