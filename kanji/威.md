# 威 — intimidate; authority; dignity

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38), enclosed in the lower middle · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

威 is power that makes others afraid or makes them bow: intimidation, authority, and the dignity of someone who commands respect. [威張る](../words/威張る.md) いばる is to swagger or act arrogantly; JMdict glosses it "to put on airs; to throw one's weight about; to swagger; to act arrogantly."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for イ. One example of each:

- holding people down by force and making them afraid: 威圧 いあつ, "coercion; overpowering; overawing; daunting";
- a strong force that makes people fear and obey: 権威 けんい, "authority; power; influence; prestige";
- a solemn power that must not be challenged: 威厳 いげん, "dignity; majesty; solemnity; gravity."

For the kun reading it gives おどす. It also names 御稜威 みいつ, "power (of the Emperor); Emperor's might," among words with a hard-to-guess reading; JMdict marks みいつ as a special reading of the whole word and the word as honorific and poetic. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a frame made of a long horizontal stroke on top, a long sweeping stroke at the left and, at the right, a long hooked stroke with a slash and a dot; inside it sit a short horizontal stroke and [女](女.md), "woman, female." Imagine a woman guarding a gate with a halberd, so imposing that nobody dares to come near. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 女 or the frame contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| イ | on | [威張る](../words/威張る.md) いばる — swagger; act arrogantly |
| おど.す | kun | 威す おどす - to threaten; to menace; to frighten (into doing); JMdict marks the spelling 威す as rarely used, beside the main spelling 脅す; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| おど.し | kun | 威し おどし - threat; JMdict lists 脅し first and 威し as another spelling; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| おど.かす | kun | 威かす おどかす - to threaten; to menace; to intimidate; JMdict marks the spelling 威かす as rarely used, beside the main spelling 脅かす; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

威張る mixes an on reading and a kun reading: 威 supplies い, its KANJIDIC2 on reading イ, and 張 supplies ば, the voiced form of the は of 張る はる, "to stretch; to spread; to strain; to tighten," followed by る, the okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. This voicing at the start of a second element is called rendaku. JMdict also lists the variant reading えばる for 威張る; え is not a KANJIDIC2 reading of 威, so it has no row here. In 威す, 威し and 威かす, 威 writes おど, followed by す, し or かす.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only イ, with 威力 いりょく, "power; might; authority; influence," 威圧 and 示威 じい, "demonstration; show of force," as examples. おど.す, おど.し and おど.かす are not in the table. Practice selects イ, the reading 威 supplies in the linked word article 威張る; the kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [張](張.md), "stretch tight; assert," in 威張る.
- [成](成.md), "become," [歳](歳.md), "years of age," and [減](減.md), "decrease, reduce," each have the same long hooked stroke with a slash and a dot at the right that 威 has. All are different kanji.
- [権](権.md), "authority; rights," [力](力.md), "power," [勢](勢.md), "force; momentum," [厳](厳.md), "strict; severe; solemn," [圧](圧.md), "pressure; oppress," [恐](恐.md), "fear; awe," [怖](怖.md), "frightening," and [慢](慢.md), "arrogance; slackness," overlap the senses "authority," "power," "dignity," "intimidate" and "act arrogantly" that 威 and its words carry; 権, 力, 勢, 厳 and 圧 appear with 威 in 権威, 威力, 威勢 いせい, "power; might; authority," 威厳 and 威圧. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "authority" or "power" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the イ senses: 威嚇 いかく, "threat; intimidation; menace," and 脅威 きょうい, "threat; menace"; 球威 きゅうい, "(pitcher's) stuff," a baseball term, 国威 こくい, "national prestige," 示威 and 猛威 もうい, "fury; power; menace"; and 威光 いこう, "power; authority; influence," and 威風 いふう, "majesty; dignity."
