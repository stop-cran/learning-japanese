# 規 — standard; rule

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 見 (no. 147) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

規 concerns a standard or measure. [定規](../words/定規.md) じょうぎ is a physical ruler,
while 規則 きそく names a rule or regulation. The shared idea is a standard to work with;
the compound determines whether it is a tool or a rule.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 夫 on the left and 見 on the right. Imagine someone
looking carefully to check a standard. This scene is invented for memory, not offered as etymology.

## Notes

In 定規, キ is voiced to ギ; learn じょうぎ as a whole word.
Compare [親](親.md) and [観](観.md), which also have 見 on the right.
[覚](覚.md) places 見 below its upper parts instead. The position and neighboring strokes
are more useful than the shared component alone.
