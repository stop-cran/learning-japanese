# 腕 — arm; skill

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

腕 names the arm and extends to a person's skill or ability. [腕](../words/腕.md) うで can therefore be literal or figurative; 手腕 しゅわん concerns capability rather than simply listing a hand and an arm.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture an arm bending around the rounded right-hand 宛 shape, beside the flesh sign.

The canonical 肉 key, no. 130, appears as 月 on the left.

## Notes

- 腕を上げる うでをあげる can describe raising an arm or improving one's skill. The surrounding activity determines which.
- [肩](肩.md) is the shoulder and [手](手.md) is the hand. They are neighboring parts, not interchangeable names for the whole arm.
- 腕力 also has a force/violence sense; the strength gloss is not a promise that every occurrence describes peaceful exercise.
