# 情 — emotion; circumstances

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

情 appears in words for feelings and in words for a situation's circumstances.
[感情](../words/感情.md) かんじょう is emotion; 事情 じじょう is the circumstances or reasons behind
a situation. Neither meaning should be forced onto every word containing 情.

The indexing radical 心 appears as 忄 on the left.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** put the [青](青.md) shape beside that heart form and imagine a feeling
having a color. This is only a modern memory aid, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

## Notes

[青](青.md) lacks the heart radical. [性](性.md) and [忙](忙.md) have the same 忄
but different right sides. [感](感.md) and 情 both concern feelings, as in 感情 (かんじょう), "emotion; feelings."
