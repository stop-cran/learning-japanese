# 栗 — chestnut

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

栗 writes **chestnut**, [栗](../words/栗.md) くり: the tree or its edible nut. Its name-use classification in the source does not make this ordinary noun a personal name.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the top block above [木](木.md). Imagine a chestnut resting above its tree. Compare the upper shape with [西](西.md), but do not read 栗 as a compound of the independent words "west" and "tree."

## Notes

- 栗の木 (くりのき) specifies the tree; 栗の実 (くりのみ) specifies the nut.
- The source lists all four readings above, but this card asks only くり. The other rows preserve dictionary information without inventing modern examples.
- 栗色 names a brown like the chestnut's shell. Dictionaries also record 栗 as an abbreviation of that colour name; context distinguishes it from the food or tree.
- Compare [要](要.md), necessity or essentials: its bottom is 女, not 木. [相](相.md) places 木 beside 目 rather than below an upper block.
