# 桐 — paulownia

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

桐 writes the tree name **paulownia**, [桐](../words/桐.md) きり. The wood is used for such things as chests and musical instruments; 桐材 きりざい means paulownia wood.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside [同](同.md). Imagine recognizing the same distinctive tree again. This is an invented visual association, not the historical origin of the character.

## Notes

- The questions select きり, not the two retained on readings. Recognition of a specialist or classical compound does not make it a necessary prompt.
- 桐油 とうゆ comes from trees called アブラギリ and related names, not from キリ. Likewise 梧桐 ごとう / あおぎり names the Chinese parasol-tree, *Firmiana simplex*. Do not infer the botanical species of every compound from standalone 桐.
- [同](同.md), same or equal, lacks 木. [相](相.md), mutual or aspect, has 目 on the right; [村](村.md), village, has 寸 there.
- The source's grade-9 name-use classification does not mean this character occurs only in personal names. The linked article teaches the ordinary plant noun; personal-name readings are not covered here.
