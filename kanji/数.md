# 数 — number

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (as 攵, no. 66) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

数 concerns how many things there are and the act of counting them.
The noun [数](../words/数.md) かず names the number or amount; 数える かぞえる is the action "count."
This distinction is useful before learning compounds about mathematics.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the 米 above 女 on the left as a pile to count, with 攵 as a final checking gesture.
This is not a historical explanation. The canonical radical 攴 takes the right-side form 攵 here.

## Notes

In 人の数 (ひとのかず), "the number of people," 数 is not the written digit itself.
数字 can name such a digit. For shape, [料](料.md) shares 米, while [教](教.md) and [政](政.md) share 攵.
Use these shared parts to recognize the shapes while learning each character's meaning separately.
