# 先 — previous; ahead (in time)

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (legs, no. 10) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

先 is commonly explained as a foot (the top part, related to 止 "stop/foot") above a person (儿): a person whose foot steps forward, so "ahead, first, earlier".
Because the one who goes first is also the one who came earlier, it covers "tip, front", "previous" and "future (what lies ahead)". Details of the ancient form are debated.

## Notes

- **先 vs 前:** [先](先.md) stresses "ahead of / earlier than something in a sequence" (先月 last month, 先生 one who came first); [前](前.md) means "front" and "before" in place or time (午前, 名前, 前に). 先 can point to the future (この先 from here on, the road ahead) as well as the past.
- Look-alike: 失 (lose) has a similar top but the horizontal and slanting strokes cross differently and has no 儿 base; 洗 (wash) contains 先 as its right-hand part.
