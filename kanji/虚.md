# 虚 — empty; void; false

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 虍 (no. 141) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

虚 is about emptiness and about things with nothing real inside. KANJIDIC2 gives “void,” “emptiness,” and “untruth,” and Digital Daijisen's senses run from being empty with nothing in it, through show without substance, to lies (うそ; いつわり). [謙虚](../words/謙虚.md) けんきょ is glossed in JMdict as “modest; humble”; 虚 supplies キョ in the whole word. Kanjipedia and Digital Daijisen both list 謙虚 under a sense of 虚 meaning having no ill intent (邪心をもたない), and Kanjipedia adds すなお (open; straightforward).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the shape 虍 hangs over the top and left like a roof, and the shape 业 sits beneath it. Picture a big frame with only a few upright strokes inside: plenty of room, nothing in it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 虍 or 业 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists only the on readings キョ and コ, with 虚空 and 虚無僧 こむそう as examples for コ; it gives no kun reading. Kanjipedia marks むなしい, うつろ, うろ, and うつける as outside Joyo.
- Digital Daijisen gives further kun readings beyond KANJIDIC2's: うそ, そら, から, うつけ, and うろ. KANJIDIC2 lists no name readings for 虚.

These three cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [空](空.md) (“sky; empty”): it overlaps the title's “empty,” and JMdict lists 空しい as another spelling of 虚しい むなしい.
- [殻](殻.md) (“shell; husk; empty outer covering”): Kanjipedia's first meaning of 虚 includes 中身がない (having no contents), which overlaps an “empty outer covering.”
- [穴](穴.md) (“hole; pit; cavity”): JMdict glosses 虚ろ うつろ as “cavity; hollow; void,” which overlaps “cavity.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 満 (“full; satisfy”), 真 (“true, genuine”), and 重 (“heavy; piled up”); none means empty, void, or false.
