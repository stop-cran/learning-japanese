# 俗 — customs; mundane

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

俗 can refer to customs, ordinary or worldly things, colloquial use, and what is considered lowbrow or vulgar. In [俗語](../words/俗語.md) ぞくご it names colloquial language or slang. 風俗 (ふうぞく) can mean manners, customs or public morals; in another sense it refers to the sex-service industry.

- 風俗 ふうぞく — manners; customs; public morals; also the sex-service industry in a separate sense (JMdict entry 1499960)

## Notes

俗語 does not necessarily mean an offensive or obscene expression. 俗’s “mundane” sense overlaps with 凡 (“commonplace; mediocre”), and its lowbrow sense overlaps with 卑 (“lowly; despicable”); the characters also have distinct senses.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 天 (“heaven, sky”), 新 (“new”), and 名 (“name”); none means customs or the mundane.
