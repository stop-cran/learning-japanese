# 俗 — customs; mundane

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

俗 can refer to customs, ordinary or worldly things, and what is colloquial. In [俗語](../words/俗語.md) ぞくご it names colloquial language or slang.

## Notes

俗語 does not necessarily mean an offensive or obscene expression. Whether a particular term is rude, playful or neutral depends on that term and its context.
