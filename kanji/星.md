# 星 — star; celestial point

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

星 names a star or star-like celestial point in everyday language. The word 星 ほし can include a visible planet rather than imposing a technical astronomical classification. In other words the character can refer to a spot or mark, including a mark for a victory.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 日 above 生 and imagine a small point of light appearing in the sky. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified historical or phonetic explanation.

## Notes

- The suffix-like -ぼし row shows ほし becoming ぼし in 白星 しろぼし. Do not change the standalone word 星 ほし to ぼし.
- 客星 きゃくしょう is astronomy vocabulary for a briefly visible celestial body; this example does not make ショウ the reading of everyday 星.
- 衛星 identifies a satellite, whether natural or artificial in the relevant context; not every 星 is a satellite.
