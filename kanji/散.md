# 散 — scatter

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (as 攵, no. 66) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

散 suggests things spreading apart rather than remaining together. Petals can 散る ちる, "fall";
someone can 散らす ちらす, "scatter" something.
In [散歩](../words/散歩.md) さんぽ, learn the settled meaning "a walk; a stroll," not a literal instruction to scatter.

For modern shape recognition, find the 月-like enclosure low on the left and 攵 on the right.
That 攵 is the positional form of radical 攴. These observations do not establish the character's history.

## Notes

Keep the verb pair distinct: 花が散る (はながちる), "flowers fall," versus 花を散らす
(はなをちらす), "scatter flowers." が marks what falls; を marks what someone scatters.
[政](政.md) and [教](教.md) share 攵; [期](期.md) has a different arrangement of the left-hand bars and 月.
