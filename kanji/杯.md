# 杯 — cupful counter

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

杯 is useful when counting servings in cups, glasses, or bowls. [一杯](../words/一杯.md) (いっぱい) can mean one cupful.
As the noun さかずき, 杯 is a cup for sake or another alcoholic drink.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** set an imaginary drinking cup on the horizontal stroke of the right-hand 不, beside 木.
This deliberately invented scene is not an account of the character's origin.

- 乾杯 かんぱい — a toast; "Cheers!"
- 杯 さかずき — sake cup

## Notes

Learn いっぱい as a whole counter expression: ハイ appears as ぱい here.
The 木 is on the left; [休](休.md) instead has a person-shaped left side and 木 on the right.
The counter counts servings, not a fixed number of millilitres.
