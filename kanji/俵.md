# 俵 — straw bag; bale

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

俵 means a straw bag, sack or bale; it can also count sacks. The independent word [俵](../words/俵.md) たわら uses its kun reading.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |

| たわら | kun | [俵](../words/俵.md) たわら — straw bag; sack (JMdict entry 1583390) |

## Common words

- 土俵 どひょう — (wrestling) ring
- 一俵 いっぴょう — one sackful (JMdict entry 1166100)

## Notes

JMdict lists ヒョウ in 土俵. For the standalone word [俵](../words/俵.md), JMdict tags たわら as common and also lists ひょう, the only reading for the counter sense.

JMdict glosses 俵 as “straw bag; sack; bale,” which overlaps with the card title of 袋 (“bag; sack; pouch”).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 田 (“rice field”), 車 (“car, vehicle”), and 本 (“book, origin”); none means a straw bag or a bale.
