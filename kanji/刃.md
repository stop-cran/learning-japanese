# 刃 — blade; cutting edge

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

刃 concerns a blade or cutting edge. The standalone word [刃](../words/刃.md) は names the cutting part of a knife or sword, not automatically the whole tool. The character also appears in words concerning swords.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare [刀](刀.md), "sword; blade," and notice the extra short mark. Some fonts show it as a separate stroke; the stroke-order diagram here shows it crossing the longer sloping stroke. Let that mark remind you of the cutting edge. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| やいば | kun | 刃 やいば — blade; sword; a separate documented word reading |
| き.る | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this reading; no independently verified word example is established here |

This small lesson practices **は**. ジン, ニン and やいば remain recognition material here; leaving them out of practice does not make them invalid or uniformly rare. The source-only き.る is not a recommendation to replace the spelling of [切る](../words/切る.md) きる, "cut," with an invented spelling.

## Common words

- [刃](../words/刃.md) は — blade; cutting edge
- 刃物 はもの — edged tool; cutting instrument

## Notes

- 刀 かたな names a sword; 刃 は can name its cutting part. Learn the whole word and context rather than treating the two characters as freely interchangeable.
- [乃](乃.md), with connecting or particle-related uses, [凹](凹.md), "concave; sunken," and [凸](凸.md), "convex; protruding," do not share the blade/cutting-edge meaning.
- The same spelling 刃 also has the reading やいば. Its dictionary senses are not all automatically senses of the noun は.
