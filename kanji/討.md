# 討 — attack; discuss

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

討 concerns going after something, either an enemy or a problem.
[討つ](../words/討つ.md) うつ is the native verb for attacking, defeating,
conquering or avenging, and [検討](../words/検討.md) けんとう is
consideration, examination or review. 討伐 とうばつ is subjugation by
military force, 討議 とうぎ is a debate or discussion, and 討論 とうろん is
also a debate or discussion.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: attacking and
subduing an enemy, as in 討伐, and probing a problem thoroughly, as in 討議
and 検討. It lists うつ and たずねる as kun readings. たずねる is not in
KANJIDIC2 or the official Joyo table, so practice does not select it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [言](言.md) on the left and 寸
on the right. Imagine words delivered in exact small measures against an
enemy or a problem until it is dealt with. This is a memory picture for the
modern shape, not a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| トウ | on | [検討](../words/検討.md) けんとう - consideration; examination |
| う.つ | kun | [討つ](../words/討つ.md) うつ - attack; defeat; avenge |

Practice selects トウ and う.つ, each with a linked example. 討つ writes う
with 討 and つ in kana; it is a transitive godan verb. The official Joyo
table lists トウ, with 討伐, 討論 and 検討 as examples, and うつ, with 討つ
and 敵討ち, where it contrasts 討つ with 打つ and 撃つ.

## Notes

- [計](計.md), "measure; plan," shares 言 at the left, and [村](村.md),
  "village," and [寺](寺.md), "Buddhist temple," share 寸 at the right.
  Their meanings differ from 討's.
- Attacking and striking overlaps [攻](攻.md), "attack; go on the
  offensive," [撃](撃.md), "shoot; strike," [打](打.md), "strike," and
  [戦](戦.md), "battle." JMdict lists 撃つ among the written forms of the
  same entry as 討つ, and the Joyo table sets 討つ against both 打つ and
  撃つ.
- Defeating and overcoming overlaps [敗](敗.md), "failure; defeat,"
  [克](克.md), "overcome," [勝](勝.md), "victory," and [殺](殺.md), "kill,"
  because 討つ is glossed "defeat," "conquer" and "destroy."
- Punishing overlaps [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible," and
  [刑](刑.md), "criminal punishment," because KANJIDIC2 glosses 討
  "chastise."
- Discussion and consideration, as in 討議, 討論 and 検討, overlap
  [批](批.md), "criticize; critique," [議](議.md), "deliberate; debate,"
  [論](論.md), "argument; discourse," [談](談.md), "converse; consult,"
  [考](考.md), "think over," [学](学.md), "study," and [研](研.md), "polish;
  study closely."
- Investigation overlaps [査](査.md), "investigate; inspect," [調](調.md),
  "investigate; tune," [究](究.md), "investigate thoroughly," [診](診.md),
  "diagnose; examine medically," and [偵](偵.md), "spy; covert
  investigation," because JMdict glosses 検討 "examination; investigation;
  study." These direct meaning comparisons are excluded from choices, not
  treated as identical in every compound.
- [徹](徹.md), "penetrate; be thorough," declares the pair 徹–討, so this
  card does not repeat it.
- In 検討, 検 supplies ケン. It has no kanji card here yet; this does not
  change the complete word's reading.
