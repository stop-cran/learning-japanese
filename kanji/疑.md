# 疑 — doubt; suspect

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 疋 (no. 103) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

疑 is uncertainty that questions a claim or suspects something is wrong.
[疑う](../words/疑う.md) うたがう can take a person or a statement as the object of doubt.
疑問 ぎもん is a doubt or question, not necessarily a question already spoken aloud.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** pause over the busy left and right halves as if you are unsure which way to go.
Look for the 矢-shaped lower-left part and the 疋-shaped lower-right part. This is a modern visual aid, not etymology.

## Notes

彼の説明を疑っています。 (かれのせつめいをうたがっています。) — I doubt his explanation.
Compare [信じる](../words/信じる.md) しんじる, "believe; trust": the two express contrasting attitudes to a claim.

[知](知.md) and [短](短.md) also contain 矢, but not 疑's upper-left addition.
[起](起.md) is only a looser outline comparison; its long lower sweep belongs to a different structure.

For 疑 compared with 怪, 妖 and 奇, and 疑わしい compared with 怪しい, see [the article on 怪しい and related words](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
