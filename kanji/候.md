# 候 — weather conditions

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

Learn 候 through [天候](../words/天候.md) てんこう, "weather," and 気候 きこう, "climate."
The weather meaning does not explain every compound: 候補 こうほ means a candidate or prospect.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the person-shaped 亻 standing beside the extra vertical stroke,
watching the weather. This is a way to remember the present shape, not its historical origin.

## Common words

- [天候](../words/天候.md) てんこう — weather
- 気候 きこう — climate
- 候補 こうほ — candidate; prospect

## Notes

天候 concerns weather conditions; 気候 concerns climate, the broader pattern of weather.
Do not read 候補 as a weather term merely because it shares 候.
[値](値.md), [借](借.md), and [使](使.md) also begin with 亻, but their right-hand shapes and meanings differ.
