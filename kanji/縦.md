# 縦 — vertical; lengthwise; at will

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

縦 is the direction that runs up-and-down or front-to-back, the opposite of [横](横.md), "horizontal": the vertical, the lengthwise direction. [縦](../words/縦.md) たて is the word. JMdict glosses it "the vertical; height," "front-to-back; length" and "north-to-south." The word article teaches "vertical; lengthwise dimension." In compounds read ジュウ, 縦 can also mean "as one pleases," a sense that the title calls "at will."

The Daijisen character entry gives 2 senses for ジュウ. One example of each:

- the vertical direction, as in 縦断 じゅうだん, "running through (north-south); cutting across; travelling across";
- doing as one pleases, as in 縦覧 じゅうらん, "inspection; unrestricted viewing; reading."

Daijisen's sense 1 also lists 縦横 じゅうおう and 縦走 じゅうそう, "stretching out (in a longitudinal direction); running; traversing"; its sense 2 also lists 操縦 そうじゅう and 放縦 ほうじゅう. Daijisen marks ジュウ as a customary reading (慣) and also gives ショウ (漢) for 放縦, and lists the kun readings たて, ほしいまま, たとい and よしや. KANJIDIC2 does not list ショウ, ほしいまま, たとい or よしや. Daijisen's headword たて lists six senses: up-down, front-back, the longest direction, north-south, a social up-down relationship, and the warp thread of cloth.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left. Imagine threads hanging straight down, strands running top to bottom. The busy right half is not analyzed here. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that any part of 縦 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジュウ | on | 縦断 じゅうだん - running through (north-south); cutting across; travelling across; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| たて | kun | [縦](../words/縦.md) たて — vertical; lengthwise dimension |

In 縦 たて, 縦 stands alone and writes たて. In 縦断, 縦 supplies ジュウ and 断 supplies ダン; in 操縦, 操 supplies ソウ and 縦 supplies ジュウ, and JMdict glosses 操縦 "steering; piloting; flying; control; operation; handling." JMdict glosses 縦横 じゅうおう "length and width; length and breadth; lengthwise and crosswise; vertical and horizontal; the four cardinal points" for its first sense and "as one wishes; as one pleases; at will" for its third. The same spelling is also たてよこ, "length and breadth; length and width." In 縦覧, 覧 supplies ラン. In 放縦 JMdict gives ほうじゅう and ほうしょう for "self-indulgence; looseness; dissolution; licence; license." In たて compounds, 縦軸 たてじく is "vertical axis; vertical line," 縦笛 たてぶえ is "end-blown flute (esp. the recorder)" and 縦糸 たていと is "(weaving) warp"; they keep たて.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジュウ, with 縦横, 縦断 and 操縦, and たて, with 縦. Practice selects たて, the reading of the linked word article. ジュウ is the reading of most 縦 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; it is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 縦 is a single kanji in the linked word, so there is no other kanji to explain.
- JMdict also has 縦 ほしいまま, "selfish; self-indulgent; arbitrary" (tagged "word usually written using kana alone") and 縦 よし, "even if" (tagged "word usually written using kana alone"). They are separate words, not the core of this card.
- [垂](垂.md), "hang down; droop; dangle," [長](長.md), "long, chief," and [高](高.md), "high, expensive," overlap "the vertical; height" and "length." [任](任.md), "responsibility; entrust," overlaps "at will" (leaving things to one's own choice). [従](従.md), "follow; obey; subordinate," is also read ジュウ and overlaps JMdict's "vertical (relationship); hierarchy." [経](経.md), "pass through; sutra," is the warp (lengthwise) thread and in KANJIDIC2 has たていと, "warp thread," so it overlaps the vertical sense. [北](北.md), "north," and [南](南.md), "south," appear in 縦 only through the north-to-south direction, so they are not excluded. The others are kept out of this card's quiz options. [横](横.md), "horizontal," is the opposite of 縦.
- [紙](紙.md), "paper," and [終](終.md), "end; finish," have the same 糸 at the left, and [犬](犬.md), "dog," is a simple shape that looks unlike 縦. They are read シ, かみ; シュウ, お.わる; and ケン, いぬ. None shares a reading or a meaning with 縦.
- Many other kanji read ジュウ, such as [中](中.md), "middle, inside," [住](住.md), "reside, dwell," and [重](重.md), "heavy; piled up," and the card [盾](盾.md), "shield; buckler," shares たて; they are never offered as wrong answers.
- For reference, JMdict's 縦 たて also has "vertical (relationship); hierarchy" and "(weaving) warp." JMdict also lists the spelling 経 for this word, tagged "rarely used kanji form."
