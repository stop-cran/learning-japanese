# 保 — protect; preserve

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9), appearing as 亻 · **JLPT:** N2 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

保 conveys **protecting, preserving or keeping something in a state**. [保つ](../words/保つ.md) たもつ means maintain or preserve; [保存](../words/保存.md) ほぞん covers preservation and saving data.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 亻 beside the box-and-tree shape 呆. Imagine a person keeping something safe. This is a memory aid, not an explanation of the ancient components.

## Notes

- In 保つ, たも is supplied by 保 and つ is the written ending. Learn 保ちます (たもちます), 保って (たもって) and 保たない (たもたない).
- Practice selects ホ and たも.つ; the historical ホウ example is retained without turning it into an alternative pronunciation of everyday 保存.
- [守](守.md), guard or obey, overlaps in protection. It is not a suitable wrong meaning for 保 simply because its English title differs.
