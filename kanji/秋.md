# 秋 — autumn

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

秋 means autumn, or fall. It is both the everyday word [秋](../words/秋.md) (あき) and a part of seasonal compounds.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** the grain-shaped 禾 beside [fire](火.md) suggests a harvest
scene in warm autumn colours. This visual story does not establish how the character originated.

- 春秋 しゅんじゅう — spring and autumn; also years passing

## Notes

The seasonal sequence is [春](春.md) (はる), [夏](夏.md) (なつ), 秋 (あき), [冬](冬.md) (ふゆ).
Do not use the source reading とき as a substitute for あき when naming the season.

[私](私.md) has the same left side but ム instead of 火. [料](料.md) has 米 and 斗, not 禾 and 火.
