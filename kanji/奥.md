# 奥 — interior; depths

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

奥 concerns an inner or farther part, or depths. 奥 おく can be the back of a shop or the depths of a forest; 奥義 おうぎ names the secrets or innermost principles of an art.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the covered upper shape hiding something deep inside, above the large lower strokes.

The indexing radical is 大 (no. 37), associated with the lower strokes. The 米-like shape in the upper interior is not the canonical radical.

## Notes

- 奥 emphasizes going farther inside from an opening or viewpoint, not simply the space outside and behind something.
- 奥まる is an intransitive godan verb. Its pronunciation おくまる does not itself demonstrate the separate source reading くま.
- 奥さん is an established respectful social term, not a literal statement about where a woman belongs. The linked article explains when to use it.
