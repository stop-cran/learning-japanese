# 慕 — yearn for; adore

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慕 means to long for, or be drawn with affection to, someone or something. It appears in 慕う, in 思慕 (しぼ), “yearning; deep affection,” and in 慕情 (ぼじょう), “longing.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The top part, 莫, is also the top of [募](募.md), “recruit,” [墓](墓.md), “grave,” and [暮](暮.md), “dusk,” which are all read ボ, like 慕. Below it is a four-stroke form of 心, “heart,” as at the bottom of [恭](恭.md).

## Notes

[募](募.md), [墓](墓.md) and [暮](暮.md) share the top part and the reading ボ, but mean recruit, grave and dusk. [恋](恋.md), [憧](憧.md) and [懐](懐.md) express longing, yearning or fond attachment; [愛](愛.md) is love; [敬](敬.md), [尊](尊.md), [崇](崇.md) and [仰](仰.md) express respect or reverence. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 140](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=140) lists ボ, with 慕情, 敬慕 (けいぼ), “love and respect,” and 思慕, and したう, with 慕う and 慕わしい (したわしい), “dear; beloved.”
