# 創 — originate; create

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

創 concerns originating something or bringing something new into being. [創作](../words/創作.md) そうさく means creative production or an original work; it can also mean a fabricated story.

創 also has a wound or injury meaning. Do not assume that every word containing it concerns creativity. As a standalone word for a wound, 創 きず is a rare spelling; the ordinary spelling taught here is [傷](../words/傷.md) きず.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 倉-shaped left block beside right-hand 刂 as a blade cutting a new opening. This invented scene is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

Reading questions select only ソウ and つく.る. The other entries remain reference information, not alternative readings of 創作. The two source-only rows do not establish a modern word, meaning or usage; their presence in the character dictionary is not a claim that they are invalid or obsolete.

The dot in つく.る marks the written boundary in 創る, not a different conjugation from 作る. The linked article is headed 作る つくる and explains 創る as a spelling of that same verb.

For general "begin" and "begin to do," learn [始める](../words/始める.md) はじめる. The rare spelling 創める above is limited here to establishing or starting up something, not offered as a general replacement for 始める or its auxiliary use.

## Notes

- [作](作.md)る, [造](造.md)る and 創る share the reading つくる. 創る emphasizes creation; these spelling tendencies do not divide every meaning into rigid compartments. The meal and vegetable examples in the linked 作る article teach 作る, not substitutions with 創る.
- The indexing radical is [刀](刀.md), visibly right-hand 刂 here. [剣](剣.md), "sword; double-edged blade," has a similar right-hand blade but a different left block. A wound is not the sword that causes it.
- Compare [倹](倹.md), "frugal; thrifty," and [験](験.md), "test; verify": their left sides and meanings differ from 創.
