# 用 — use, purpose

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 用 (no. 101) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

用 concerns using something, its purpose, or business that needs attention. [用事](../words/用事.md) means an errand or something to do, not necessarily paid work.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the divided outline as a rack with separate spaces for useful tools. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a claim about the object the character originally represented.

## Notes

Compare [月](月.md): 用 has an extra internal vertical stroke.
[田](田.md) has a closed bottom edge; 用 does not.
The verb is もちいる, with both ち and い: the kanji carries もち and the okurigana is いる.
