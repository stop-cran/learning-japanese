# 恭 — respectful; reverent

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

恭 expresses respect and courtesy toward someone. It appears in 恭しい, in 恭賀 (きょうが), “respectful congratulations,” and in 恭順 (きょうじゅん), “allegiance; submission.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See [共](共.md), “together,” on top; 共 is also read キョウ. Below it is a four-stroke form of 心, “heart.” Picture hearts joined together in respect.

## Notes

[異](異.md) and [選](選.md) contain 共, under 田 or at the lower right, and [暴](暴.md) has a similar shape under 日; they mean different, choose and violence. [敬](敬.md), [尊](尊.md), [崇](崇.md) and [仰](仰.md) express respect or reverence; [寧](寧.md) and [礼](礼.md) politeness or courtesy; [謙](謙.md) modesty; [奉](奉.md), [拝](拝.md) and [召](召.md) respectful serving, worship or respectful action. 恭賀新年 (きょうがしんねん) is a New Year greeting, “Happy New Year.” The [standard-use table, PDF p. 38](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=38) lists キョウ, with 恭賀 and 恭順, and うやうやしい, with 恭しい.
