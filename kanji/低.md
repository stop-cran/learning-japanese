# 低 — low; lower in height

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

低 concerns a low height, position, level, or pitch. 低い ひくい can describe a low voice or low temperature as well as physical height. 'Short' fits a person's height in some English translations, but 低い is not the normal adjective for a short piece of string.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the person-shaped 亻 lowering their head toward the bottom stroke of 氐 on the right.

The canonical radical 人 takes the left-hand form 亻.

## Notes

- 背が低い せがひくい means someone is short in height. Use [短い](../words/短い.md) みじかい for short length or duration, rather than replacing it everywhere with 低い.
- 低める ひくめる means lower something; 低まる ひくまる describes a level becoming lower.
