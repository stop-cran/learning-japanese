# 点 — point; dot

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 火 (as 灬, no. 86) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

点 can be a dot or mark, a score point, or a particular point under discussion.
The noun [点](../words/点.md) てん is a useful starting place: the context decides whether
you are looking at a mark on paper, a result, or an aspect of something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the four bottom dots marking points underneath the
sign-like 占 shape. The dots are the visible form of the fire radical; the imagined sign is not etymology.
