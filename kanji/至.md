# 至 — reach; attain

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 至 (no. 133), the whole character · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

至 concerns reaching an endpoint or a degree. [至る](../words/至る.md)
いたる can describe reaching a place, a stage or a decision.
The character also expresses an utmost degree, as in
[至急](../words/至急.md) しきゅう, urgent or urgently.
Reaching a destination and needing urgent action are not interchangeable
meanings of the complete words.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice the top horizontal line,
the angular 厶-shaped middle, and [土](土.md) at the base.
Imagine a point arriving at the ground. This is a visual memory scene,
not a verified historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | [至急](../words/至急.md) しきゅう - urgent; urgently; [乃至](../words/乃至.md) ないし - from ... to ...; or |
| いた.る | kun | [至る](../words/至る.md) いたる - reach; arrive at a stage or result |

Both readings are selected with linked examples. In 至る, 至 supplies いた
and る is okurigana, the kana ending following the character.
乃至 is usually written ないし; learn the whole connector rather than
treating 至 alone as a free replacement for English "or."

## Common words

- [至る](../words/至る.md) いたる - reach; arrive at a stage
- [至急](../words/至急.md) しきゅう - urgent; urgently
- [乃至](../words/乃至.md) ないし - from ... to ...; or; usually written in kana

## Notes

- [室](室.md), room, includes 至 under a roof-shaped 宀.
  [台](台.md), stand/platform, and [去](去.md), leave, share only
  portions of the shape. None has the meaning reach merely because it looks related.
- Reaching overlaps [到](到.md), [達](達.md), [届](届.md) and
  [着](着.md); utmost degree overlaps [極](極.md).
  They are useful comparisons, not unambiguously wrong meaning answers.
- 至る is an intransitive godan verb. The endpoint may be abstract;
  its use is not restricted to physical travel. The linked article separates
  destination, stage and result contexts rather than giving one English
  preposition as a universal rule.
