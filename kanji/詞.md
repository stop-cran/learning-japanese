# 詞 — words; lyrics; part of speech

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

詞 concerns words and verbal expression. It appears in grammatical category names such as 動詞, verb, and in 歌詞, song lyrics.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 言 on the left supplying words to the 司-shaped block on the right, as if that block were a little lyrics stand. This is not a claim about the right side's historical meaning or sound.

## Notes

- JMdict marks 詞 as a rare kanji spelling in the ことば family headed by 言葉. The flag belongs to this spelling, not to the ordinary pronunciation ことば or to every compound containing 詞.
- 動詞 is itself a noun naming a grammatical category. Its literal association with movement and words is not a complete test for whether another word is a verb.
