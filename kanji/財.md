# 財 — wealth; property

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

財 refers to wealth, property, or assets. [財布](../words/財布.md) さいふ brings that idea into an everyday
object: a wallet. 財産 ざいさん concerns the property or assets themselves, not their container.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** put valuables in the left 貝-shaped compartment, with 才 beside it as its label.
This is a way to recognize the present-day shape, not a claim about its historical construction.

## Notes

Learn サイ in 財布 and ザイ in 財産 with the whole words; the sound is not freely interchangeable.
In 財, 貝 sits on the left. In [貸](貸.md), [責](責.md), and [貧](貧.md), it sits underneath.
Do not confuse owning wealth with lending it or lacking it.
