# 噴 — erupt; spout; gush

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

噴 is something forced out from inside: water, gas, smoke or lava spouting or bursting out. [噴水](../words/噴水.md) ふんすい is a fountain or water jet, and [噴火](../words/噴火.md) ふんか is a volcanic eruption. JMdict glosses the verb 噴く ふく "to emit; to spout; to spurt; to boil over."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for フン, which it marks as a customary reading: blowing something out from inside. Besides 噴火 and 噴水, its examples include 噴煙 ふんえん, "(eruption of) smoke," 噴射 ふんしゃ, "jet; spray; emission; spouting," 噴出 ふんしゅつ, "spewing; gushing; spouting; eruption; effusion," 噴霧器 ふんむき, "sprayer; vaporizer; vaporiser; atomizer," and 自噴 じふん, "gushing forth (e.g. of oil); spouting; natural emergence (of a hot spring)." It lists the kun reading ふく, the same as KANJIDIC2.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [口](口.md), "mouth," at the left, and at the right crossed strokes above [貝](貝.md), "shellfish; seashell." Imagine a clam beside an open mouth, spouting a jet of water like a tiny fountain. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 口 or 貝 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| フン | on | [噴水](../words/噴水.md) ふんすい — fountain; water jet; [噴火](../words/噴火.md) ふんか — volcanic eruption |
| ふ.く | kun | 噴く ふく - to emit; to spout; to spurt; to boil over; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 噴水, 噴 supplies フン and 水 supplies スイ; in 噴火, 噴 supplies フン and 火 supplies カ, with no sound change in either word. In 噴く, 噴 writes ふ and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 噴き出す ふきだす, "to spout out; to spurt out; to gush out; to jet out," uses the same ふ with き, and then 出 supplies だ, followed by す.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows フン, with 噴火, 噴出 and 噴水 as examples; and ふく, with 噴く and 噴き出す, marked as a pair to compare with 吹く ふく, "to blow (of the wind)," which JMdict also glosses, in a later sense, "to emit (smoke, fire, etc.); to spout; to spew." JMdict lists 噴き出す in the same entry as the spelling 吹き出す. Practice selects フン, the reading with linked word articles; ふ.く is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [水](水.md), "water," in 噴水 and [火](火.md), "fire," in 噴火.
- [口](口.md), "mouth," is the left part of 噴, and [古](古.md), "old," has 口 at the bottom, under crossed strokes. [買](買.md), "buy," has the same 貝 at the bottom as the right part of 噴. All are different kanji.
- [吹](吹.md), "blow," [吐](吐.md), "spit out; vomit; breathe out," [爆](爆.md), "burst open; explode," and [発](発.md), "depart, emit," overlap the senses "erupt," "spout" and "gush" that 噴 and its words carry; 吹 is also read ふ.く, and the Joyo table pairs 噴く with 吹く. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "spout" or "erupt" as exclusive to any one character.
