# 給 — pay; bestow

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

給 concerns giving or supplying something, including money paid for work. In [給料](../words/給料.md)
きゅうりょう, it points to pay; 給食 きゅうしょく concerns providing a meal instead.
Do not assume that every word containing 給 refers to wages.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the narrow 糸 on the left tying together a package to hand over;
the right side has the shape 合. This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

- [給料](../words/給料.md) きゅうりょう — salary; wages
- 月給 げっきゅう — monthly salary
- 給食 きゅうしょく — meal service, such as a school lunch service

## Notes

Distinguish [絵](絵.md), [紙](紙.md), and [終](終.md) by the part to the right of 糸.
給料 is money received for work, not the general word for everything one earns or receives.
