# 今 — now

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 𠆢, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** none established · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

今 means "now", "the present" and "this (current)" as in "this year". Its origin is debated: one common view sees a roof-like top over a small mark
(a lid covering something, with the sound being borrowed for "now"), others see it as a pictograph of a different object. Do not rely on any single story;
as a mnemonic only, "a person under a roof, here and now".

## Notes

- Look-alikes: [分](分.md) (minute, part) has the same top two strokes but 刀 underneath; 令 (order) has a different lower part.
- 今 vs [何](何.md): 何 has 亻 on the left and 可 on the right.
- Pattern: 今 + time noun is "this ...": 今日, 今年, 今月, 今週, 今朝. The "last ..." counterpart uses [先](先.md): 先月, 先週.
