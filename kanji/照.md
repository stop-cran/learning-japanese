# 照 — illuminate; shine

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

照 concerns illuminating or shining. Its word family also includes comparison and embarrassment; 照れる is not simply another verb meaning to shine.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture [日](日.md), the sun, beside [召](召.md) above four fire-like dots: a row of lamps illuminates a stage.

The indexing radical 火 appears as the four bottom dots 灬.

## Notes

- 照る is intransitive godan; 照らす is transitive godan and can take the illuminated object with を. [光る](../words/光る.md) describes something shining rather than illuminating an object.
- 照れる is an intransitive ichidan verb: 照れて てれて. Learn its embarrassment sense separately from the light verbs.
- 照らす also has a comparison/reference sense, including judging something in light of a standard. The supporting article explains this without claiming it replaces [比べる](../words/比べる.md) in every comparison.
