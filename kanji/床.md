# 床 — floor; bed

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

床 can concern a floor or a bed. The native word 床 ゆか means a floor, while 床 とこ can mean bed or bedding. [起床](../words/起床.md) きしょう means getting out of bed; the same character is not pronounced identically in every context.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 广 over 木 and imagine a wooden floor beneath a roof. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a verified origin.
