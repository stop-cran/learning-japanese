# 羊 — sheep

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 羊 (no. 123) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

羊 names a sheep. [羊毛](../words/羊毛.md) ようもう is sheep's wool;
the animal and its wool are not the same noun.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the two short upper
strokes as horns above three horizontal bars and a central upright.
The whole character is its own indexing radical. This is a memory aid
for the modern shape, not a verified historical explanation.

やぎ or しだ readings for 羊 alone.

## Notes

- [古](古.md), old, [右](右.md), right, and [川](川.md), river, name
  neither the sheep nor its qualified paper, hair or auspicious uses.
- [未](未.md) normally displays "not yet," but separately names the
  Sheep zodiac branch, also ひつじ. 羊 is the ordinary animal spelling;
  the calendar sign is not an unrestricted substitute.
- 日本国語大辞典 records historical slang uses of ひつじ for paper,
  paper products and banknotes, and for a hairdresser or hairdressing
  shop through wordplay on かみ. Weblio's slang dictionary also
  explicitly lists head hair. These are positive secondary senses,
  not recommendations to replace ordinary [紙](紙.md) or [髪](髪.md).
  Direct precautions also cover [札](札.md), [金](金.md),
  [貨](貨.md), [財](財.md), [毛](毛.md) and [房](房.md);
  not every financial asset or tuft is a sheep.
- 字通 records 羊 standing for 祥 in good, auspicious and auspicious-sign
  uses. Direct broad-label precautions cover [良](良.md), [好](好.md),
  [令](令.md), [幸](幸.md), [福](福.md), [兆](兆.md),
  [運](運.md), [徳](徳.md) and [恩](恩.md). This qualified use
  does not make the animal noun a general modern word for virtue,
  kindness or luck.
- 相羊 has a wandering use in 字通, and 羊 is also the title of an
  obsolete Noh play in 日本国語大辞典. A whole expression or named
  work does not make 羊 alone mean every walk, leisure activity or play.
