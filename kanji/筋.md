# 筋 — muscle; sinew; line; plot

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (as the bamboo top ⺮, no. 118), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

筋 is a muscle or sinew, and from there anything long and thin that runs in a line: a streak, a fiber, the thread of a story or the logic of an argument. [筋](../words/筋.md) すじ is a sinew, a line or the thread of a story, and [筋肉](../words/筋肉.md) きんにく is muscle. JMdict glosses the noun 筋 すじ "muscle; tendon; sinew" in its first sense, "line; stripe; streak" in its fourth, "reason; logic" in its fifth and "plot; storyline" in its sixth.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for キン: flesh made of bundles of long, thin muscle fibers, as in 筋骨 きんこつ, "muscles and bones; body build; physique," 筋肉, 筋力 きんりょく, "physical strength; muscle strength," 心筋 しんきん, "cardiac muscle; heart muscle; myocardium," and 腹筋 ふっきん, "abdominal muscles; abs"; and a long, thin line-like thing, as in 鉄筋 てっきん, "rebar; (iron) reinforcing bar; reinforcing steel."

For すじ it lists 筋道 すじみち, "reason; logic," 粗筋 あらすじ, "outline; summary; argument," 大筋 おおすじ, "outline; summary; gist; basic points," 背筋 せすじ, "spine; line of the backbone," and 血筋 ちすじ, "lineage; stock; descent; strain; blood relationship." JMdict also has the noun 筋 きん, "muscle," which has no word article here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [竹](竹.md), "bamboo," at the top, in its top form ⺮, and below it a shape like [月](月.md), "moon, month," beside [力](力.md), "power." Imagine long bamboo fibers running through a strong arm, the strings of muscle that give it power. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 竹, 月 or 力 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キン | on | [筋肉](../words/筋肉.md) きんにく — muscle |
| すじ | kun | [筋](../words/筋.md) すじ — sinew; line; thread of a story |

In 筋肉, 筋 supplies キン and 肉 supplies ニク. 筋 read すじ stands alone as a word, with no okurigana, and is also the すじ of 筋道, 大筋 and 血筋. In 腹筋 ふっきん, キン follows 腹 フク, which shortens to ふっ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キン, with 筋肉, 筋骨 and 鉄筋 as examples, and すじ, with 筋, 筋書 and 大筋; JMdict's main spelling of 筋書 is 筋書き すじがき, "synopsis; plot; outline; summary." Practice selects both readings, each of which has a linked word article.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [肉](肉.md), "meat; flesh," in 筋肉. The word 筋 contains only this kanji.
- [竹](竹.md), "bamboo," is the key at the top, and [筒](筒.md), "cylinder; tube," and [箱](箱.md), "box," have the same ⺮ at the top above a different shape. [勤](勤.md), "employment; diligence," also ends in 力 and is also read キン. All are different kanji.
- [肉](肉.md), "meat; flesh," [線](線.md), "line; track," [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," [維](維.md), "fiber," [系](系.md), "lineage; system," [統](統.md), "unify; lineage," and [理](理.md), "reason, logic," overlap the senses "muscle," "line," "thread," "fiber," "lineage" and "logic" that 筋 and its words carry; 線 also has the kun reading すじ, and 肉 appears in 筋肉. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "line" or "muscle" as exclusive to any one character.
