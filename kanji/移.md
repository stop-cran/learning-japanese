# 移 — shift; transfer

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

移 concerns moving or passing from one place or state to another. Learn the native pair 移る, something moves or is transferred, and 移す, someone moves or transfers something, together with their particles.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 禾 beside 多 and imagine shifting many stalks to a different place. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical derivation or a claim about the right-hand part's sound.

The indexing key 禾, no. 115, is on the left.

## Notes

- For the basic intransitive pattern, the mover takes が and the destination に: 人が別の場所に移る ひとがべつのばしょにうつる, a person moves to another place. In the transitive pattern, AをBに移す means move A to B.
- Both native verbs are godan: 移ります うつります / 移って うつって, and 移します うつします / 移して うつして.
- The disease-transmission uses are usually written うつる and うつす in kana. [写す](../words/写す.md), also うつす, instead concerns copying or photographing; a shared sound does not make every spelling suitable for moving a desk.
