# 涙 — tears

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

涙 means tears, the fluid from the eyes, and appears in words for crying or being moved to tears.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The water strokes stand beside a 戻-like shape. Imagine tears on the face of someone returning home.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- Learn standalone 涙 as なみだ and the compound reading ルイ separately.
- Tears may come from pain, emotion, or another cause; the character itself does not require sadness.
