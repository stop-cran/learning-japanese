# 譲 — hand over; yield

**Strokes:** 20 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

譲 is about letting something pass to another person: handing it over, or giving way. KANJIDIC2 lists “defer”, “turnover”, “transfer” and “convey”, and Kanjipedia's first sense is ゆずる。ゆずりあたえる (to hand over and give). In [譲る](../words/譲る.md) ゆずる, JMdict gives five senses: “to hand over; to transfer; to turn over; to assign; to convey; to bequeath”, “to give up (e.g. one's seat); to give way”, “to yield; to concede; to give ground; to surrender”, “to sell”, and “to postpone; to put off; to defer”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [言](言.md), "say; words," with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it, stands on the left. The right side, 㐮 (KanjiVG marks it as a variant of the shape 襄), has [六](六.md), "six," at the top, strokes that KanjiVG groups as [三](三.md), "three," in the middle, and the shape 𧘇 at the bottom. Picture saying "after you" and handing over six gifts, then three more. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 㐮 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ジョウ (譲渡 じょうと, JMdict entry 1357040; 譲歩; 謙譲 けんじょう, JMdict entry 1260210) and ゆずる (譲る; 親譲り おやゆずり, JMdict entry 1365210), and gives the old form 讓.
- Kanjipedia also lists せめる outside the Joyo table, matching its third sense, せ（責）める; no checked JMdict word supports it, so it has no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading ゆずり. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings うや, せむ, のり, まさ, and よし. These are name use, not quiz readings.

These sixteen cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [屈](屈.md) (“bend; yield; submit”): JMdict 譲る sense 3 is “to yield; to concede; to give ground; to surrender”, and KANJIDIC2 gives 屈 “yield” and “submit”.
- [与](与.md) (“give; grant”), [授](授.md) (“grant; impart; teach”), [施](施.md) (“carry out; give; alms”) and [贈](贈.md) (“give as a gift; confer”): JMdict 譲る begins “to hand over”, and Kanjipedia gives ゆずりあたえる (to hand over and give).
- [移](移.md) (“shift; transfer”), [伝](伝.md) (“transmit; convey”) and [輸](輸.md) (“transport; convey”): KANJIDIC2 gives 譲 “transfer” and “convey”, and JMdict 譲る sense 1 includes “to transfer” and “to convey”.
- [返](返.md) (“give back; repay”): giving something back is a way of handing it over (JMdict 譲る “to hand over”), and its title shares “give” with “to give up (e.g. one's seat)”.
- [売](売.md) (“sell”) and [販](販.md) (“sell; market”): JMdict 譲る sense 4 is “to sell”.
- [延](延.md) (“prolong; postpone”): JMdict 譲る sense 5 is “to postpone; to put off; to defer”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “defer” for 譲.
- [謙](謙.md) (“humble; modest”): Kanjipedia gives 譲 ② へりくだる「謙譲」 and gives へりくだる for 謙.
- [委](委.md) (“entrust; committee”): JMdict 譲る sense 1 includes “to assign”.
- [退](退.md) (“withdraw; retreat”): JMdict 譲る includes “to give way” and “to give ground”.
- [責](責.md) (“blame; hold responsible”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 譲 is せ（責）める。なじる (to blame; to reproach).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 買 (“buy”), 話 (“talk, speak”), and 読 (“read”). Buying is the other side of selling (譲る can mean “to sell”), and 話 and 読 share the radical 言 with 譲, but none of them means handing over or yielding.
