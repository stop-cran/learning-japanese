# 達 — reach, plural suffix

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 辶 (しんにょう, no. 162) · **Phonetic:** uncertain (the right-hand part) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

達 has 辶 (movement along a road) on the left and below. The 辶 fits the core meaning "get through to, reach": reaching a place, a goal or a level.
The right-hand part is generally taken as a sound component, but its exact identity and role are debated, so no firm etymology is given here.
Extended meanings are "attain skill" (上達) and "develop, grow" (発達) and "deliver" (配達). In Japanese 達 also serves as the plural suffix たち for people (私達, 友達).

## Common words

- [友達](../words/友達.md) ともだち — friend
- 私達 わたしたち — we, us (also written 私たち)
- 上達 じょうたつ — improvement of skill
- 配達 はいたつ — delivery

## Notes

- **たち as a plural:** たち attaches to people and some animals (私達, 子供達, 先生達), not to things. Kana たち is just as common in running text. 友達 is a single friend by now, so 友達たち is not needed; say 友達 for both unless you must stress the plural.
- **Look-alikes:** 達 vs [道](道.md) (road, also with 辶) and 運 (carry, luck, also with 辶): check the part beside 辶.
