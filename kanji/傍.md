# 傍 — beside; nearby

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

傍 means beside or near something. A common example is [傍観](../words/傍観.md) ぼうかん, standing by and watching. Digital Daijisen also lists the sense of branching off from the main line, as in 傍系 and 傍流.

## Common words

- [傍観](../words/傍観.md) ぼうかん — looking on; standing by and watching
- 傍聴 ぼうちょう — attending a hearing or session as an observer
- 傍ら かたわら — side; beside; while (doing)

## Notes

傍目 has two readings with separate JMdict entries: おかめ and はため. Both describe how something looks to an outsider.

傍 overlaps with 側 (“side”), which JMdict lists as the main spelling of そば, and with 辺 (“vicinity; edge”), 近 (“near”) and 隣 (“next door; adjoining”) in the idea of nearness. 隣 refers specifically to what is next door or adjoining.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 赤 (“red”), 午 (“noon”), and 金 (“gold, money”); none means beside or nearby.
