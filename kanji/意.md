# 意 — meaning, intention

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 心 (heart, no. 61) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

意 concerns what is in the mind: a thought, intention, or meaning. Its compounds include meaning, opinions, and attention.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** read the visible [音](音.md) over [心](心.md) as "a sound kept in the mind has meaning". This is a mnemonic only; 音 is not being identified here as a historical phonetic component.

## Notes

[思](思.md) also concerns thought, but its everyday verb is 思う おもう; 意 is useful chiefly through compounds at this level. Do not substitute one kanji for the other.

Compare 意 with [音](音.md): the extra 心 at the bottom changes the character. Meaning and intention are related ideas, but 意味 is not a general replacement for 意図 いと (intention).
