# 東 — east

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The popular explanation is the sun ([日](日.md)) rising behind a tree ([木](木.md)), which makes a good mnemonic for "east". Early forms are
sometimes read differently, as a bag tied at both ends and borrowed by sound for "east", so the sun-in-the-tree account is
a mnemonic and not a settled origin. In the modern shape, 日 sits inside 木 as a crossbar.

## Notes

- In the set of four directions: 東 east, [西](西.md) west, [南](南.md) south, [北](北.md) north. In Japanese the usual order is 東西南北.
- 東京 literally means "eastern capital".
- Look-alikes: 車 (car, [車](車.md)), which has 日 inside a box with horizontal lines, and 来 (come). 東 has a 日 in the middle of 木.
- 東京 is とうきょう, with two long vowels (トウ + キョウ); 東 alone for the direction is ひがし.
