# 角 — angle; corner; horn

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 角 (no. 148) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

角 covers angles and corners as well as horns and antlers. Different words written with this one character have different readings: かど for a corner and つの for a horn are not free variants.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture an angular horn above the boxed lower strokes. This visual association helps recall the modern outline without claiming its historical development.

## Notes

- 角 is itself the indexing radical, no. 148. The linked word article teaches かど and contrasts the other uses; its primary reading should not be applied to every dictionary entry with the same spelling.
- 三角 さんかく illustrates カク in a compound. For a street corner, learn the native かど example rather than substituting the angle reading automatically.
