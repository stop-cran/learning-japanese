# 劾 — censure; impeach

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

劾 concerns investigating or pursuing wrongdoing and censuring it. Learn it through [弾劾](../words/弾劾.md) だんがい, which can mean legal impeachment or a broader denunciation of wrongdoing. Not every criticism is an impeachment, and an accusation alone is not proof of guilt.

The left side resembles [亥](亥.md); [力](力.md), "strength," is the right-hand radical.
**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a forceful challenge beside a claim. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical derivation or an explanation of the reading.

## Notes

- [刻](刻.md), "carve; time," shares the 亥-shaped left side but has 刂 on the right, whereas 劾 has 力. The shared shape does not establish a shared meaning or reading.
- [効](効.md), "efficacy; effectiveness," also has 力 at the right but a different left side. [努](努.md), "make an effort," has 力 below. Neither means censure.
- [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible," overlaps with the censure side of 劾; it is a useful meaning comparison, not a clearly wrong alternative.
- The investigation sense concerns pursuing wrongdoing. Do not treat 劾 as a standalone verb for every survey, lookup or inquiry.
