# 減 — decrease, reduce

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

減 covers a reduction in amount or number: something decreases, or someone reduces it. It contrasts with [増](増.md), increase.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture some of the water draining away past the tightly enclosed-looking right side: the amount is reduced.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- 減る is intransitive and 減らす is transitive. Both are godan verbs: 減って へって and 減らして へらして.
- 加減 combines adding and reducing into adjustment; its uses are broader than numerical subtraction alone.
