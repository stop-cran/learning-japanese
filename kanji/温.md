# 温 — warm, mild

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

温 expresses warmth or mildness, including temperature and a kind manner. Its native verb pair distinguishes becoming warm from warming something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a sun-like box above a dish-like base beside water: warm water is sitting in the dish.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- 温か takes な before a noun; 温かい is an i-adjective. Do not confuse the two endings.
- 温まる is intransitive godan and 温める is transitive ichidan. The temperature sense also occurs with 暖; 暖かい often concerns air temperature. This is a usage tendency, not an absolute partition of every context.
- The bare reading ぬく has a verified lexical example above, but that example is archaic and derogatory, not an ordinary standalone noun for warmth or a recommended way to address someone.
- The existing [温い](../words/温い.md) article teaches ぬるい, lukewarm, usually written in kana. That is a different word and does not establish the exact reading ぬく.
