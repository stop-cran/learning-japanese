# 化 — transform

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 匕 (no. 21) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

化 concerns a change of form or state. [変化](../words/変化.md) へんか means change;
化ける ばける can describe taking another form, as in a story about a transforming animal.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the person-shaped 亻 turning into the different shape on the right.
This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation. The indexing radical is 匕 on the right, not 亻.
