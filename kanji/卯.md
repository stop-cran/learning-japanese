# 卯 — Rabbit zodiac sign

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 卩 (no. 26) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

卯 names the **fourth of the twelve zodiac signs**, the Rabbit or Hare.
The native sign noun is [卯](../words/卯.md) う, not the ordinary living-animal
noun [兎](../words/兎.md) うさぎ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the two separated sides as
a pair of rabbit ears on a calendar label. The left side takes three strokes;
the right side is the indexing radical 卩, called **ふしづくり**, in strokes
four and five. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not an account of
how the character originated.

## Notes

- Compare [卵](卵.md), egg, which has interior dots; [柳](柳.md), willow,
  which has 木 to the left of the 卯 shape; and [印](印.md), stamp or mark,
  which shares the right-side 卩 but has a different left side. These are
  modern-shape comparisons, not etymological explanations.
- A generic mark or sign is not the specific Rabbit branch. The three
  comparison characters do not name this sign. [東](東.md), east, would
  not be a safe wrong meaning answer: 卯 has an old east sense too.
- Historical uses include an hour around 6 a.m., east, and the second lunar
  month. Dictionary hour mappings include both 5–7 a.m. and 6–8 a.m.;
  no single conversion is asserted here. These are not ordinary modern
  navigation or scheduling substitutes.
- The whole word 卯月 (うづき) instead names the **fourth lunar month**.
  Do not derive its month number from standalone 卯's second-month sense
  or equate either lunar month directly with a Gregorian month.
- The grade-9 tag classifies name-use kanji; it does not make the sign noun
  or every reading a personal name. The radical's own readings セツ and
  わりふ are not readings of 卯.
