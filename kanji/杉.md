# 杉 — Japanese cedar

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

杉 names **Japanese cedar**, *Cryptomeria japonica*, also called cryptomeria. The linked [杉](../words/杉.md) すぎ article identifies the tree; English "cedar" alone can refer to other trees too.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md), tree, beside three slanting strokes 彡. Imagine a tree with three narrow sprays of foliage. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation.
