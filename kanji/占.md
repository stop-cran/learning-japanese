# 占 — divine fortunes; occupy

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 卜 (no. 25) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

占 has two important word families: [占う](../words/占う.md) うらなう, tell fortunes or predict, and 占める しめる, occupy a place or account for a share. The same character does not make these verbs interchangeable.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the upright 卜 shape above 口, as though someone is announcing a prediction.

The canonical radical 卜 is the upper component.

## Notes

- 占める しめる can describe a proportion: 半分を占める はんぶんをしめる, account for half. It does not mean close a door; that homophone is [閉める](../words/閉める.md).
- 占う うらなう concerns fortune-telling or prediction. Recognizing the word describes what someone is doing, not proof that the prediction is accurate.
