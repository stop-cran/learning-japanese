# 鮮 — fresh; vivid

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 魚 (no. 195) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鮮 is freshness and vividness: food that is newly caught and lively, and colours or shapes that stand out clearly. KANJIDIC2 lists “fresh”, “vivid”, “clear” and “brilliant”. Kanjipedia's first sense is あざやか。はっきりしている。 (vivid; distinct), and its second is あたらしい。生きがいい。 (new; fresh and lively), with 新鮮 among its examples. Digital Daijisen's first sense is 取りたてで生きがよい。生生しい。 (freshly caught and lively; fresh), also with 新鮮 among its examples, and its second is 形・色がくっきりしている。あざやか。 (clear in shape or colour; vivid). The word [新鮮](../words/新鮮.md) しんせん is glossed in JMdict as “fresh; new” (entry 1362100), and 鮮やか あざやか has the first sense “vivid; bright; brilliant; clear; fresh; vibrant” (entry 1392500).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is [魚](魚.md), "fish," and on the right is [羊](羊.md), "sheep." Imagine a market stall with fish and lamb laid side by side, so fresh that their colours look vivid. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 魚 or 羊 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists セン and あざやか. Kanjipedia also lists あたらしい and すくない outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen gives すくない as a kun reading; these are not KANJIDIC2 readings and have no table rows here.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 鮮; Digital Daijisen gives the name readings あきら, き, まれ, and よし.

These ten cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [新](新.md) (“new”): JMdict glosses 新鮮 as “fresh; new”, and Kanjipedia's second sense of 鮮 begins あたらしい (new), a reading it lists outside the table; the 新 card lists あたら.しい.
- [生](生.md) (“life, birth”): the first sense of 生 なま (JMdict 1378450) is “raw; uncooked; fresh”, Kanjipedia's second sense of 鮮 includes 生きがいい (fresh and lively), and Digital Daijisen's first sense is 取りたてで生きがよい。; the 生 card lists なま.
- [明](明.md) (“bright, clear”): KANJIDIC2 lists “clear” and “brilliant” for 鮮, the first sense of 鮮やか is “vivid; bright; brilliant; clear; fresh; vibrant”, and Kanjipedia's first sense of 鮮 includes はっきりしている (distinct).
- [冴](冴.md) (“clear; sharp-minded”): the first sense of 鮮やか is “vivid; bright; brilliant; clear; fresh; vibrant”, the first sense of 冴える さえる (JMdict 1297050) is “to be clear (of a sight, sound, colour, etc.); to be bright; to be vivid; to be crisp”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “be clear” for 冴.
- [澄](澄.md) (“clear; become transparent; serene”) and [朗](朗.md) (“cheerful; clear; resonant”): KANJIDIC2 lists “clear” for 鮮, 澄 and 朗.
- [少](少.md) (“few, a little”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 鮮 is すくない (few), a reading it lists outside the table, Digital Daijisen gives すくない as a kun reading and as its third sense, and JMdict glosses 鮮少 せんしょう (1616130), a formal or literary term, as “very few; very little”; the 少 card lists すく.ない.
- [乏](乏.md) (“scarce; lacking”): the third sense すくない and 鮮少 “very few; very little” overlap KANJIDIC2's “scarce” for 乏 and JMdict 乏しい とぼしい (1584130), “meagre; meager; scarce; limited; destitute; hard up; lacking; scanty; poor”.
- [希](希.md) (“hope; rare”): 鮮少 is “very few; very little”, and JMdict まれ (1604280), usually written in kana and also spelled 希, is “rare; seldom”; the 希 card lists まれ.
- [珍](珍.md) (“rare; unusual”): 鮮少 is “very few; very little”, and the senses of 珍しい めずらしい (JMdict 1431850) include “rare; uncommon; unusual; curious” and “new; novel”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 腐 (“rot; decay; spoil”), 淡 (“pale; faint; plain; unsalted”), and 濁 (“muddy; cloudy; impure; voiced”); none means fresh or vivid.
