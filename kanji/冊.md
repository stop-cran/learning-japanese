# 冊 — volume; book counter

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 冂 (no. 13) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

冊 is used to count books and bound volumes. 冊子 さっし means a book, booklet, or pamphlet. The counter and the lexical word have related meanings, but their pronunciations must be learned with their whole forms.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the upright strokes as several pages joined together by the long horizontal stroke.

The classical indexing radical is 冂, the outer enclosure-like shape; it is not a separate word within 冊.

## Notes

- 冊子 is さっし, with a small っ. That pronunciation illustrates the サツ series in a compound; it is not evidence for reading this word さくし.
- The サク example 冊封 is a historical term, not a general everyday word for a booklet. The source-only ふみ reading is not a recommendation to rewrite [文](文.md) or [本](../words/本.md) indiscriminately.
