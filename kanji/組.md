# 組 — group; assemble

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

組 concerns putting things or people together.
[組](../words/組.md) くみ names a group or set, while 組む くむ can mean assembling something or joining forces.
番組 ばんぐみ is a television or other program, a whole word to learn rather than translate as "number group."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** let 糸 suggest threads being joined beside the layered 且 shape.
This is a memory association, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

二人一組 (ふたりひとくみ) means "two people to a group," or a pair.
Learn the reading with the expression: the voiced suffix in 番組 does not mean that every occurrence of 組 is ぐみ.

[祖](祖.md) has the same right-hand 且 but a different left side.
[約](約.md) and [紙](紙.md) share 糸, not the right-hand shape.
