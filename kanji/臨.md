# 臨 — face (a situation); attend

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 臣 (no. 131) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

臨 can mean looking down from a height, as in 君臨 (くんりん), reigning over or dominating others. More often in compounds, it describes facing a situation, being present at an event, or something situated by a place. 臨時 (りんじ) is temporary or arranged for a special occasion; 臨海 (りんかい) means coastal. It can also describe copying with an original at hand, as in 臨書 (りんしょ).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [臣](臣.md), "retainer; subject," stands at the left. At the right, a short two-stroke shape sits above three 口 boxes, arranged as in [品](品.md), "goods, refinement." Imagine a retainer attending an inspection, standing face to face with a table of goods. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 臣 or 品 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- The Jōyō table and Digital Daijisen list リン and のぞむ. KANJIDIC2 records み as a nanori, and Digital Daijisen gives み as a name reading; it is a name-only form, not a quiz reading.
- JMdict glosses 臨む as “to look out on; to overlook; to front onto,” “to face (a situation, crisis, etc.); to meet (e.g. death),” “to deal with (an issue),” and “to attend (e.g. a function); to appear (e.g. in court); to be present at; to go to (e.g. a place); to take part in; to participate; to join.”
- Kanjipedia describes 臨 as looking down from above, a person of high rank going to a place (来臨), and being present on the scene. Its second sense is copying a model placed beside one, as in 臨書, which JMdict glosses as “writing (from a copy).”

These thirteen cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- 向 (“face toward”), 対 (“oppose; face”), 顔 (“face; expression”) and 眺 (“gaze; view; look out over”): the facing and looking-out senses.
- 会 (“meet”), 処 (“deal with; dispose”) and 扱 (“handle; deal with”): “to meet (e.g. death)” and “to deal with (an issue).”
- 仕 (“serve, attend”), 居 (“be present; dwell”), 参 (“participate”) and 訪 (“visit; call on”): the attending, being-present, taking-part and going senses.
- 写 (“copy, photograph”): the copying sense, as in 臨書.
- 冒 (“brave danger; venture”): JMdict glosses 冒す as “to brave; to risk; to face; to venture,” and KANJIDIC2 lists “face” for 冒.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 住 (“reside, dwell”), 知 (“know”), and 使 (“use, employ”); none means facing or attending an occasion.
