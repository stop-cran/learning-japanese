# 臨 — face; attend; oversee

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 臣 (no. 131) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

臨 can mean looking down from a high place or overseeing. More often in compounds, it describes facing a situation, being present at an event, or something situated by a place. 臨時 (りんじ) is temporary or arranged for a special occasion; 臨海 (りんかい) means coastal. It can also describe copying with an original at hand, as in 臨書 (りんしょ).

## Notes

The Jōyō table and Digital Daijisen list リン and のぞむ. KANJIDIC2 also records み as a nanori, and Digital Daijisen gives み as a name reading; it is a name-only form, not a reading selected for this card's questions. The title's senses overlap with the displayed labels for 向 (“face toward”), 対 and 顔 (“face”), 監 (“oversee”), and 仕 (“attend”), so those cards are excluded from this meaning quiz.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 住 (“reside; dwell”), 知 (“know”), and 使 (“use; employ”); none means facing or attending an occasion.
