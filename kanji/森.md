# 森 — forest; woods

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

森 concerns woods or a forest. The native noun 森 is read もり, while 森林 しんりん is a compound for forest or woodland.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice three 木 shapes grouped together and imagine many trees in a forest. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a sourced account of the character's historical construction.

## Notes

- [林](林.md) はやし and 森 もり overlap in naming wooded areas. The number of 木 shapes is not a rule fixing the size, density, or exact tree count of a wooded place.
- The シン row is illustrated by 森林. It does not tell you to read the independent native noun 森 as しん.
- Distinguish [休](休.md), rest: its left side is 亻, not another 木.
