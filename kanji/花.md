# 花 — flower

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (as 艹, no. 140) · **Phonetic:** 化 (カ) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

花 is a phonetic compound: 艹 (plants) provides the meaning and 化 provides the sound カ. 化 also means "change", and some dictionaries suggest a connection: a plant that changes into a bloom. That is a possible reading of the sound component, not a settled etymology.
It means "flower", and also "blossom" and, in compounds, things that look like flowers, such as 花火.

- 花見 はなみ — cherry-blossom viewing

## Notes

Do not mix up 花 (flower) with 化 (ばける, change) or 北 (north): 花 has the 艹 on top.
はな also means "nose" (鼻) and "edge, tip" (端), and these are different kanji.
