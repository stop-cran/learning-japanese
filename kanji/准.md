# 准 — quasi-; associate rank

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 冫 (no. 15) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

准 can mark a status corresponding to, but not fully the same as, a named rank.
In [准教授](../words/准教授.md) じゅんきょうじゅ, it helps name the established
rank **associate professor**. Do not interpret it as a literal half-professor
or a trainee awaiting automatic promotion.

Another branch concerns approval: 批准 ひじゅん means ratification.
Not every compound containing 准 is an associate rank.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture the bird-shaped 隹 beside
the two marks of 冫 as a bird standing beside a rank sign. This is an invented
memory scene, not an explanation of the character's historical formation.

## Notes

- [準](準.md) shares ジュン and has related meanings, but its shape has 氵 at
  the upper left and 十 below. 准 has two left-hand 冫 marks and no lower 十.
- The spellings are not freely interchangeable in modern words: learn
  [準備](../words/準備.md) じゅんび (preparation), [基準](../words/基準.md)
  きじゅん (standard), and 批准 ひじゅん (ratification) as written.
  JMdict also recognizes 準教授 じゅんきょうじゅ; the current Japanese university
  title taught here is 准教授.
- For look-alikes, [集](集.md) has 木 below 隹, [進](進.md) has 辶, and
  [難](難.md) has a different, dense left-hand block. Their meanings are not
  associate rank or approval.
