# 粗 — coarse; rough; crude

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 米 (no. 119), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

粗 is being coarse rather than fine: large grains, an open mesh, rough work or something poorly made. [粗い](../words/粗い.md) あらい is coarse or rough, [粗末](../words/粗末.md) そまつ is poor-quality or careless and [粗](../words/粗.md) あら is a flaw or fault. JMdict also files 粗 read そ as a word of its own, "coarse; rough; crude; raw; unrefined."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ソ. One example of each:

- rough and not done with care: 粗野 そや, "rustic; rude; vulgar; rough," besides 粗末;
- a modest word used when offering something: 粗品 そしな, "small present; trifling gift."

For あら it gives 粗筋 あらすじ, "outline; summary; argument." It also lists the kun reading ほぼ; JMdict gives 粗 only as a rarely used spelling of ほぼ, "almost; nearly; for the most part," which is usually written in kana, and KANJIDIC2 does not list ほぼ. More Daijisen examples of the ソ senses are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [米](米.md), "rice grain," at the left, and [且](且.md), "moreover; also," at the right, a shape like a rough stack of layers. Imagine rice grains heaped up in a rough, uneven stack instead of being milled fine. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 米 or 且 contributes meaning or sound, although 且 also has the reading ソ. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソ | on | [粗末](../words/粗末.md) そまつ — poor-quality; careless |
| あら.い | kun | [粗い](../words/粗い.md) あらい — coarse; rough; [粗](../words/粗.md) あら — flaw; fault |
| あら- | kun prefix | 粗削り あらけずり - rough-hewn; incomplete; unrefined; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 粗末, 粗 supplies ソ and 末 supplies マツ. In 粗い, 粗 writes あら and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The noun 粗 あら is the kanji alone with no okurigana; its あら is the same stem as in 粗い, and JMdict files the noun in one entry with the prefix use, "rough" and "crude; raw; natural; wild." The trailing dash in あら- marks a prefix use, one that comes before another element; it is not pronounced. 粗削り and 粗筋 begin with this prefix, and JMdict lists 荒削り as a spelling of 粗削り as well.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ソ, with 粗密 そみつ, "coarseness and fineness; sparse or dense (growth)," 粗野 and 精粗 せいそ, "fineness or coarseness; minuteness or roughness," as examples, and あらい, with 粗い, marked as a pair to compare with 荒い あらい, "rough; wild; violent; rude; coarse; harsh," which has the same reading but a different kanji. It has no separate row for the prefix あら. Practice selects ソ and あら.い, the readings with linked word articles; あら- is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [末](末.md), "end; tip," in 粗末. 粗い is 粗 with okurigana only, and the word 粗 contains only this kanji.
- [祖](祖.md), "ancestor," and [組](組.md), "group; assemble," have the same 且 at the right and are also read ソ. [粉](粉.md), "flour; powder," has the same 米 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [荒](荒.md), "rough; wild; laid waste," and [貧](貧.md), "poor; impoverished," overlap the senses "rough" and "poor-quality" that 粗 and its words carry; 荒 is also read あら.い, and JMdict lists 荒 as a rarely used spelling of the noun あら. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "rough" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ソ senses: 粗雑 そざつ, "coarse; rough; crude," 粗製 そせい, "crude construction; crude manufacture," and 粗略 そりゃく, "coarse; crude; rough"; and 粗酒 そしゅ, "sake (implied to be low-grade)," and 粗茶 そちゃ, "coarse, low-grade tea."
