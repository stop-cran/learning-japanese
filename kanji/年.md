# 年 — year

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 干 (no. 51) · **Phonetic:** none claimed · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

年 means "year" and "age". The oldest forms are commonly explained as grain (禾) together with a person (人) carrying it: a harvest,
and since grain ripens once a year, the harvest cycle came to mean a year. The modern shape has changed so much that the parts are
hard to see, and the dictionary key 干 is a classification convenience rather than a meaning clue. Treat the harvest story as the standard explanation, not a certainty.

## Notes

- As a counter, 年 counts years: 一年 いちねん, 二年 にねん, 三年 さんねん, **四年 よねん** (not よんねん), 七年 しちねん／ななねん, 九年 くねん, 何年 なんねん. It also gives school years (一年生 いちねんせい).
- Do not confuse 年 with [午](午.md) (noon): both end in a long vertical stroke through horizontal bars, but 年 has an extra top stroke and a longer, more complex upper part (6 strokes vs 4).
- Compare [半](半.md) (half), which also ends in a vertical through bars but has two dots on top.
