# 悔 — regret; repent; mourn; frustration

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

悔 is the painful feeling that something has gone wrong: regretting or repenting a mistake, mourning a loss, and the bitter frustration of a defeat. [悔やむ](../words/悔やむ.md) くやむ is to mourn a death or to regret what happened, and [悔しい](../words/悔しい.md) くやしい is feeling bitterly frustrated or disappointed. JMdict glosses 悔やむ "to mourn; to lament" and, in a second sense, "to be sorry; to regret; to repent." It glosses 悔しい "frustrated (over a failure, humiliation or injustice); annoyed; chagrined; (bitterly) disappointed; bitter; vexed."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for カイ: thinking with regret about a failure or a mistake, くいる. Its examples include 悔悟 かいご, "remorse; repentance"; 悔恨 かいこん, "regret; remorse; repentance; contrition"; 悔悛 かいしゅん, "repentance; contrition," which JMdict lists after the spelling 改悛; and 後悔 こうかい, "regret; repentance; remorse." It also lists one compound that JMdict does not have. Its kun readings are くいる, くやむ and くやしい, the same as KANJIDIC2.

Daijisen also gives the reading ケ with the same meaning, in 懺悔, read ざんげ or さんげ, "repentance; confession; penitence." JMdict tags 懺悔 with the fields Christianity and Buddhism and notes that さんげ is usually used in Buddhism and ざんげ elsewhere. ケ is not a KANJIDIC2 reading; in both readings of 懺悔 it is voiced to げ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 忄, the narrow form of [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the left, and [毎](毎.md), "every, each," at the right. Imagine a heart that aches every time it remembers the same mistake. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 忄 or 毎 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| くや.しい | kun | [悔しい](../words/悔しい.md) くやしい — bitterly frustrated; disappointed |

In 悔やむ, 悔 writes く and やむ is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict also lists the spelling 悔む. 悔いる uses the same く with いる, and its noun 悔い くい, "regret; repentance," keeps く with い. In 悔しい, 悔 writes くや and しい is okurigana. So 悔 supplies く in 悔やむ but くや in 悔しい, two separate KANJIDIC2 readings. In 後悔, 後 supplies コウ and 悔 supplies カイ, with no sound change.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カイ, with 悔恨 and 後悔 as examples; くいる, with 悔いる and 悔い; くやむ, with 悔やむ and お悔やみ おくやみ, "condolences," which JMdict marks as polite language; and くやしい, with 悔しい and 悔しがる くやしがる, "to seem bitter (over); to appear vexed; to be resentful; to appear to regret." The official Joyo table also prints a variant form of 悔 in round brackets, which this card does not teach. Practice selects く.やむ and くや.しい, the readings with linked word articles. カイ is the reading of most 悔 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; く.いる is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 悔やむ and 悔しい are 悔 with okurigana only. JMdict lists 口惜しい as a rarely used kanji form of 悔しい; a separate entry, 口惜しい くちおしい, which JMdict marks as a dated term, has the same glosses. 懺, the first kanji of 懺悔, has no card yet.
- [梅](梅.md), "ume; Japanese apricot," has the same 毎 at the right, with 木 at the left. [毎](毎.md), "every, each," is the whole right part of 悔. [慣](慣.md), "accustomed," has the same 忄 at the left. All are different kanji. [海](海.md), "sea, ocean," also has 毎 at the right and is read カイ, like 悔.
- [恨](恨.md), "resent; grudge," [哀](哀.md), "sorrow; pity; pathos," [悲](悲.md), "sadness," and [苦](苦.md), "bitter; suffering," overlap the senses "mourn," "lament," "bitter" and "resentful" that 悔 and its words carry: JMdict glosses 悔やむ "to mourn; to lament," 悔しい "bitter; vexed" and 悔しがる "to be resentful"; 恨 appears with 悔 in 悔恨. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "regret" or "bitter" as exclusive to any one character.
- [惜](惜.md), "pity; precious; begrudge," also carries regret: JMdict glosses 惜しむ "to regret; to lament" and lists 口惜しい as a rarely used kanji form of 悔しい. The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
