# 余 — surplus

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

余 concerns what is left beyond what is used or needed.
[余る](../words/余る.md) あまる describes something being left over; 余分 よぶん describes an extra or surplus amount.
The surplus can be helpful, such as spare time, rather than necessarily a problem.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture extra strokes gathered under the 人-shaped cover after the main work is finished.
This uses the modern outline only; it is not a historical explanation.

## Notes

Unlike many neighboring cards indexed under 人, 余 has its person-shaped component at the top, not a left-hand 亻.
[会](会.md) and [金](金.md) share the roof-like outline; [茶](茶.md) adds a plant-shaped top above a similar middle.
Look at the lower strokes to tell them apart.
時間が余る (じかんがあまる) means "have time left over"; the time, not the person, is the grammatical subject.
