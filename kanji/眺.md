# 眺 — gaze; view; look out over

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

眺 is looking out and taking in a wide view. [眺める](../words/眺める.md) ながめる has three JMdict senses: "to look at; to gaze at; to watch; to stare at," "to look out over; to get a view of; to admire (e.g. the scenery)," and "to look on (from the sidelines); to stand by and watch; to observe." [眺め](../words/眺め.md) ながめ is the noun: "scene; view; prospect; outlook."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for チョウ: to look far into the distance, to gaze. Its kun reading is ながめる.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [目](目.md), "eye," at the left and [兆](兆.md), "trillion; omen," at the right. Imagine an eye gazing across a wide landscape. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 目 or 兆 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チョウ | on | 眺望 ちょうぼう — view; vista; panorama; outlook; prospect; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| なが.める | kun | [眺める](../words/眺める.md) ながめる — look at; look out over; [眺め](../words/眺め.md) ながめ — view |

In 眺める, 眺 writes なが and める is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 眺め, 眺 writes なが and め is the kana ending of the noun. In 眺望, 眺 supplies チョウ and 望 supplies ボウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows チョウ, with 眺望, and ながめる, with 眺める and 眺め. Practice selects なが.める, the reading of the linked word articles. チョウ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The words 眺める and 眺め are 眺 with okurigana only, so they have no other kanji. In 眺望, [望](望.md), "hope," supplies ボウ.
- [相](相.md), "mutual; aspect," [県](県.md), "prefecture," and [省](省.md), "ministry; omit," each contain a 目 shape, as 眺 does, so they look plausible. They are read ソウ, ショウ and あい; ケン and か.ける; and セイ, ショウ and かえり.みる, and mean mutual, prefecture and ministry. None shares a reading or a meaning with 眺.
- [見](見.md), "see, look," [観](観.md), "view; observe," [看](看.md), "watch over; see," [監](監.md), "oversee; watch over," [望](望.md), and [景](景.md), "scenery," overlap the senses "look," "view" and "scenery" that 眺 and its words carry; 見 and 観 are also read み.る. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [兆](兆.md) and many other kanji share the reading チョウ with 眺, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
