# 景 — scenery

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

景 contributes the idea of a scene or view. [景色](../words/景色.md) けしき is the scenery
before you; 夜景 やけい is a view at night, such as a city's lights.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine 日 lighting up the 京-shaped scene below it.
This is a way to remember the modern arrangement, not a historical account.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケイ | on | 風景 ふうけい — scenery; 夜景 やけい — night view |

Learn 景色 けしき as a word-level reading. Do not infer a general reading け for 景 from it;
the word article explains the spelling's relationship to 気色.

## Common words

- [景色](../words/景色.md) けしき — scenery; landscape
- 風景 ふうけい — scenery; view
- 夜景 やけい — night view

## Notes

[京](京.md) is the lower part without the top 日. [高](高.md) has a related tall, stacked
outline but a different lower enclosure. [暗](暗.md) arranges its boxes side by side.
景色 can describe what is visible; it need not mean a painting of that view.
