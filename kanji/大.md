# 大 — big, large

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

大 is a pictograph of a person standing frontally with arms and legs spread, that is, a grown-up, a large figure.
Compare [人](人.md), the same person seen from the side. The idea of an adult spreads to "big" and "great".

As a component it provides the figure in other kanji: 天 (sky, a line above the figure's head), 夫 (a line through
the arms, an adult man) and 太 (extra thick, with a dot). Avoid mixing up 大 with 犬 (dog, with a dot at the upper right) and 太.

Its opposite is [小](小.md). Big/small pairs recur in kango: [大小](../words/大小.md), 大中小 だいちゅうしょう, [中小](../words/中小.md).
