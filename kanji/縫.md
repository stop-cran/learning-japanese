# 縫 — sew; stitch

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

縫 concerns sewing: passing thread through cloth to join or mend it.
[縫う](../words/縫う.md) ぬう is the native verb for sewing or stitching,
and [裁縫](../words/裁縫.md) さいほう is sewing or needlework. 縫合
ほうごう is sewing together, as when a wound is stitched up, and 縫い目
ぬいめ is a seam or stitch.

縫う has a second, figurative sense: to weave one's way through something,
such as a crowd. That sense moves between obstacles; it does not mean
sewing anything.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md) on the left
and 逢 on the right. Inside 逢, 夂 and 丰 are carried along by the 辶-shaped
sweep underneath. Imagine a thread running along a winding path. This is a
memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ホウ | on | [裁縫](../words/裁縫.md) さいほう - sewing; needlework |
| ぬ.う | kun | [縫う](../words/縫う.md) ぬう - sew; stitch; weave one's way through |

Practice selects ホウ and ぬ.う, each with a linked example. 縫う writes ぬ
with 縫 and う in kana; it is a transitive godan verb. The official Joyo
table lists ホウ, with 縫合, 縫製 and 裁縫 as examples, and ぬう, with 縫う
and 縫い目.

## Notes

- [縮](縮.md), "shrink; contract," [練](練.md), "practice; refine," and
  [緒](緒.md), "cord; beginning," share the left-hand 糸 but have different
  right-hand parts and different meanings.
- A needle is the sewing tool, not the act: [針](針.md), "needle; pin,"
  overlaps the sewing words 縫う and 裁縫 and is excluded from meaning
  choices for that reason.
- The weaving-through sense of 縫う overlaps [通](通.md), "pass through;
  commute," and [経](経.md), "pass through; sutra." These direct
  comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as identical in every
  compound.
- In 裁縫, 裁 supplies サイ. It has no kanji card here yet; this does not
  change the complete word's reading.
