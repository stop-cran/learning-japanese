# 綿 — cotton

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

綿 concerns cotton and, with the native reading わた, the cotton plant or padding. Padding named わた does not by itself guarantee that the filling is made of cotton fiber.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 糸 beside a 白-over-巾 shape and imagine white fiber made into cloth. This is an invented modern-shape scene, not a verified account of how the character formed.

The canonical key 糸, no. 120, appears on the left as ⺯.

## Notes

- The linked 綿 article uses めん as its primary reading and explicitly distinguishes the separate わた entry. JMdict marks the plant sense usually written in kana; that flag is not attached to every padding use.
- Learn [木綿](../words/木綿.md) もめん as a complete reading, not きめん. Its dictionary family restricts もめん to 木綿 and the cotton-material sense; the separate きわた tree-name sense must not be transferred to it.
- Compare [絹](../words/絹.md) きぬ, silk, and [毛](../words/毛.md) け, hair or wool according to context. These are contrasting materials, not direct antonyms of cotton.
