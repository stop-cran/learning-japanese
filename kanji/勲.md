# 勲 — meritorious service; order of merit

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

勲 means distinguished service, especially to the state, and the honors that recognize it. A common example is [勲章](../words/勲章.md) くんしょう, a decoration or medal. Digital Daijisen also lists a person of great merit, as in 元勲 げんくん, “elder statesman,” and a rank or order of merit, as in 勲一等 くんいっとう, “order of the 1st class.”

## Notes

JMdict lists 功 (“merit; achievement”) as another spelling of いさお in the same entry. 績 (“achievements; results”) overlaps with accomplishment, 賞 (“prize; reward”) with recognition, and 栄 (“flourish; glory”) with honor.

Digital Daijisen also gives the kun reading いさおし, which Kanjipedia, like いさお, marks as outside the Joyo table (外); いさおし is not in this card's metadata and is not quizzed.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 東 (“east”), 口 (“mouth”), and 黒 (“black”); none means meritorious service or an order of merit.
