# 任 — responsibility; entrust

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

任 connects a duty with the person entrusted to carry it out.
[責任](../words/責任.md) せきにん is responsibility; 任せる まかせる means leaving a matter to someone.
The latter can concern an ordinary decision as well as a substantial task.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the person on the left supporting a set of crossbars on the right, carrying an assigned load.
This is a modern memory aid, not evidence for the character's origin.

## Notes

任せる uses the pattern matter を + person に: 仕事を任せる (しごとをまかせる) means "entrust a task."
The right side differs from [住](住.md), [体](体.md), and [件](件.md); pay particular attention to its slanting top.
Its left-side 亻 is the positional form of 人, not a separate radical number.
