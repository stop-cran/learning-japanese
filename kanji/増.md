# 増 — increase

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

増 is an increase in number or amount. [増える](../words/増える.md) ふえる describes the increase happening; 増やす ふやす describes making the amount larger.
人数が増える (にんずうがふえる) is "the number of people increases."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine another layer being added to the stacked right-hand 曽 beside 土. This is an image for remembering increase, not an etymology.

## Notes

[場](場.md) and [地](地.md) share the left-hand 土. [暗](暗.md) has a different left side but also a tall, layered right-hand structure.
For the opposite direction, use 減る へる, "decrease."
The additional source readings ま.す and ま.し remain in the metadata; they are not substitutions for ふえる in the same written word.
