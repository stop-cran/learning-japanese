# 繭 — cocoon

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), inside the lower enclosure · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

繭 means **cocoon**: a protective covering made by an insect larva, especially a silkworm. The covering and the developing insect inside it are different things.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Under the grass-shaped top, find [糸](糸.md), thread, and [虫](虫.md), insect, inside a divided enclosure. Imagine an insect protected by its thread covering. This is a visual memory aid, not a verified history of the character. The indexing radical is the enclosed 糸, not the grass-shaped top.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| まゆ | kun | [繭](../words/繭.md) まゆ - cocoon |
| きぬ | kun | source-only character reading retained from KANJIDIC2; not selected for questions; see the qualification below |

Use **まゆ** for the ordinary standalone cocoon noun. **ケン** occurs in compounds: Digital Daijisen explicitly gives 繭糸 as an example. This card practices the standalone noun rather than requiring a separate specialist vocabulary article.

KANJIDIC2 also records **きぬ**. JMdict includes 繭糸 with the reading きぬいと in the silk-thread family, alongside 絹糸. That whole-word record does not establish 繭 alone as the ordinary spelling of the material noun きぬ. Keep the character reading as lookup information; use [絹](../words/絹.md) きぬ for the silk material taught here.

## Notes

- A cocoon is the **covering**; さなぎ, pupa, is the insect at a developmental stage. Do not treat the two as interchangeable names.
- **眉** まゆ means eyebrow. It is a different word and spelling, despite the same pronunciation.
- [糸](../words/糸.md) いと names thread; [絹](../words/絹.md) きぬ names silk. Neither is the ordinary name of the whole cocoon.
- Compare [練](練.md), practice/refine, [線](線.md), line/track, and [綿](綿.md), cotton. Their thread component is at the left; 繭 has thread and an insect inside its enclosure.
