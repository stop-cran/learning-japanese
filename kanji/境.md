# 境 — boundary; border

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

境 concerns a boundary, border or region. 境 さかい names a boundary, 国境 こっきょう is a national border, and 環境 かんきょう names an environment or surroundings.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 土 marking a boundary beside the upright, 日-like box and spreading lower strokes on the right.

## Notes

- Learn 境内 as the complete word けいだい, not きょうない. The ケイ example does not make every 境 compound use that reading.
- In 国境 こっきょう, the small っ belongs to the complete word. It is not an additional source reading of 境.
- The environment meaning in 環境 is not a claim that the word only describes a country's border.
