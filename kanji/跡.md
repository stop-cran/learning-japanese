# 跡 — trace; footprint

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 足 (no. 157) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

跡 concerns a trace, track or mark left behind. 足跡 names footprints, while 史跡 names a historic site or remains.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the foot-shaped left side beside 赤 and imagine a red mark left by a foot. This is a mnemonic using modern components, not the character's historical origin.

The canonical radical 足 appears as the narrow left-hand form ⻊.

## Notes

- 足跡 here is あしあと: 足 supplies あし and 跡 supplies あと. The same spelling also has the dictionary reading そくせき, using 足 ソク and 跡 セキ; the displayed native-reading example does not rule out that separate reading.
- The right side resembles [赤](赤.md), red, but the complete character 跡 is not a color word. The 足 radical is also not the whole character.
