# 包 — wrap; enclose

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 勹 (no. 20) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

包 concerns wrapping or enclosing. 包む つつむ means to wrap something; 小包 こづつみ is a parcel. The same written verb can also represent くるむ, a separate dictionary entry usually written in kana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the outer 勹 shape curling around the 巳-like inner block, like wrapping around a small bundle.

The canonical radical 勹 is the outer wrapping-shaped component.

## Notes

- The linked 包む article teaches つつむ. The くるむ row identifies a different native verb with the same possible kanji spelling; it is usually written くるむ, not an instruction to read every 包む occurrence that way.
- 小包 is こづつみ, with づ. Learn the whole parcel word rather than mechanically joining the dictionary forms こ and つつみ.
