# 笑 — laugh

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (as the bamboo top ⺮, no. 118) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

笑 concerns laughter and smiling. [笑う](../words/笑う.md) わらう can describe either,
so a translation need not imply an audible laugh.
笑顔 えがお names a smile or smiling face rather than the action of laughing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the paired bamboo marks at the top bouncing with laughter above the spreading lower strokes.
This is a visual joke for memory, not the character's historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| わら.う | kun | [笑う](../words/笑う.md) わらう — laugh; smile |
| ショウ | on | 爆笑 ばくしょう — a burst of laughter |

Learn 笑顔 as the whole word えがお; do not read it as わらがお or put that whole-word pronunciation into an on-reading row.

## Common words

- [笑う](../words/笑う.md) わらう — laugh; smile
- 笑顔 えがお — smiling face; smile
- 爆笑 ばくしょう — uproarious laughter

## Notes

The indexing radical 竹 is compressed into the bamboo top ⺮.
[等](等.md), [箱](箱.md), and [答](答.md) share that top but have different lower parts.

笑う can also mean ridiculing someone; the surrounding sentence tells you whether the laughter is friendly.
