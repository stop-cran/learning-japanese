# 鯉 — carp

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 魚 (no. 195), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鯉 means **carp**, the freshwater fish. The word is not limited to the colorful ornamental fish called 錦鯉 (にしきごい).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [魚](魚.md), fish, on the left and 里, village, on the right. Imagine a carp in a village pond. This is a memory aid for the visible parts, not a claim that the character literally means "village fish."

The source's **grade-9** classification concerns name-use kanji, not a ninth school grade. This card teaches the ordinary animal noun, not personal-name readings.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| リ | on | 養鯉 ようり - carp breeding; a rare term in JMdict, retained for recognition rather than questions |
| こい | kun | [鯉](../words/鯉.md) こい - carp |

Practice **こい** for the standalone fish name. **リ** is a real compound reading, but the rare breeding term is not needed for this animal-noun practice. Both sourced readings remain in the metadata.

In 錦鯉 (にしきごい), the second part is pronounced **ごい**, a voiced form of こい. Learn the whole compound rather than replacing the source reading with ごい or assuming every compound changes in the same way.

## Common words

- [鯉](../words/鯉.md) こい - carp
- 錦鯉 にしきごい - colored ornamental carp
- 鯉のぼり こいのぼり - carp-shaped streamers flown for Children's Day in May

## Notes

- [魚](魚.md), fish, is the broader category, not an unambiguous wrong meaning choice for 鯉.
- Compare [理](理.md), reason/logic, with a 王-shaped left side instead of 魚; [量](量.md), quantity/amount, has 里 below its upper strokes rather than a fish component beside it.
- [恋](恋.md) also has the reading こい, but means romantic love or longing, not carp. The spelling and context distinguish the words.
- **こい** and **コイ** can also write the animal name. Katakana spelling does not make it a foreign loanword. In longer fish names, the complete word must be checked; containing 鯉 does not by itself guarantee that the animal is a carp.
