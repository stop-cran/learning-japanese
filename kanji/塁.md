# 塁 — base in baseball; fortification

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

On a baseball field, 塁 identifies a base the runner touches. In another sense it refers to a fortification.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 田 at the top and 土 at the bottom; imagine a runner touching a base on the ground.

## Common words

- [一塁](../words/一塁.md) いちるい — first base

## Notes

A runner heading for 一塁 is going to first base, not to a military base.

Follow the 田 shape: in [雷](雷.md) (thunder) it is below rain, in [苗](苗.md) (seedling) it is below grass, and in [町](町.md) (town) it stands beside 丁. In 塁 the earth is at the bottom. A [城](城.md) is a castle. In 塁を守る (るいをまもる), the setting tells you whether someone is defending a fort or guarding a baseball base.
