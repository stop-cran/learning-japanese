# 揮 — wield; command; display

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

揮 is swinging something held in the hand, such as a brush or a baton, and, by extension, directing others and bringing one's power out into the open. [発揮](../words/発揮.md) はっき is the exercise or display of an ability; JMdict glosses it "show (of power, ability, etc.); exhibition; demonstration; display; manifestation."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for キ. The first is swinging the hand around, or swinging something held in the hand, as in 揮毫 きごう, "writing (esp. commissioned calligraphy); drawing; painting," and 指揮 しき, "command; direction; supervision," also "conducting (an orchestra, choir, etc.)." The second is scattering, or bringing something out, as in 揮発油 きはつゆ, "gasoline; petrol; petroleum; benzine; volatile oil," and 発揮. It gives the kun reading ふるう, the same as KANJIDIC2.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the left, written 扌, and [軍](軍.md), "army; troops," at the right. Imagine a general's hand swinging a baton to command a whole army. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 扌 or 軍 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キ | on | [発揮](../words/発揮.md) はっき — exercise or display an ability |
| ふる.う | kun | 揮う ふるう - to swing; to wield (physically); to exert; JMdict heads the entry 振るう; no word article uses this spelling yet, not selected for questions here |

In 発揮, 発 ハツ becomes はっ before き (gemination) and 揮 supplies キ. In 揮う, 揮 writes ふる and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The JMdict entry headed 振るう lists 揮う with no tag and 振う, marked as irregular okurigana usage; its second sense, "to exercise (e.g. power, ability); to exhibit; to display; to wield (metaphorically)," is close to 発揮. JMdict also has the phrase 筆を揮う ふでをふるう, "to drive a quill (pen); to wield the writing brush." In 指揮, 指 supplies シ and 揮 supplies キ; JMdict lists 指麾 as a rarely used kanji form. JMdict lists 揮ごう as a search-only kanji form of 揮毫.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キ, with 揮発油, 指揮 and 発揮 as examples. ふる.う is not in the table. Practice selects キ, the reading of the linked word article. No word article uses ふる.う yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [発](発.md), "depart, emit," in 発揮.
- [運](運.md), "carry; luck," also contains 軍. [担](担.md), "shoulder; take on," and [拾](拾.md), "pick up; gather," also have 扌 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [令](令.md), "command; decree," [命](命.md), "life; command," [勅](勅.md), "imperial order," [将](将.md), "commander; impending," [導](導.md), "guide; conduct," [率](率.md), "rate; lead; frank," [掲](掲.md), "put up; display; publish," and [示](示.md), "indicate," overlap the senses of 揮 and its words: commanding and directing in 指揮, conducting an orchestra, and displaying or showing an ability in 発揮. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "command" or "display" as exclusive to any one character.
- [振](振.md), "wave; swing; allocate," writes 振るう, which JMdict also spells 揮う, "to swing; to wield (physically); to exert." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- [操](操.md), "operate; manipulate; fidelity," and [掌](掌.md), "palm (of the hand); administer," overlap command and control: JMdict glosses 指揮 "command; direction; supervision", 操る "to operate (e.g. a machine); to handle; to manage; to control", and 掌る, a rarely used kanji form of 司る, "to be in charge of; to manage; to direct; to administer". The three are kept out of one another's quiz options.
