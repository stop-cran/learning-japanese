# 寮 — dormitory; quarters

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[寮](../words/寮.md) りょう is a dormitory or shared residential
accommodation, such as a student or employee dormitory. It does not name
every hotel, apartment or house.

The character also has historical official/bureau associations and
tea-room or villa uses. Daijisen's noun entry additionally describes
temple accommodation for monks. Those senses explain older institutional
names; they do not make a modern student dormitory a government office.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the roof 宀 above the
crowded 尞-shaped lower part. Imagine many residents gathered under one
roof. This is a visual memory scene, not a verified origin or phonetic
analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| リョウ | on | [寮](../words/寮.md) りょう - dormitory; 学生寮 がくせいりょう - student dormitory |

Practice selects リョウ, also the Joyo reading. The long vowel belongs in
りょう. Daijisen and a separate JMdict family also recognize historical
つかさ, outside the frozen character-reading list used here. That family
groups several spellings: its religious-officiant sense is restricted
to 司, so it must not be assigned to 寮.

## Common words

- [寮](../words/寮.md) りょう - dormitory; hostel
- 学生寮 がくせいりょう - student dormitory
- 社員寮 しゃいんりょう - employee dormitory
- 寮生 りょうせい - dormitory resident

## Notes

- [察](察.md), observe/infer, and [寧](寧.md), polite/peaceful,
  also have 宀. [案](案.md), proposal/draft plan, has 安 above 木.
  These are shape comparisons, not accommodation terms.
- The lodging or residence senses overlap [宿](宿.md), [宅](宅.md),
  [住](住.md) and [家](家.md). Room and accommodation uses connect with
  [室](室.md) and [館](館.md); the tea-pavilion sense connects with
  [亭](亭.md). The buildings and their uses still differ.
- Historical official or bureau senses overlap [官](官.md), [局](局.md),
  [署](署.md) and [府](府.md). These direct comparisons are excluded
  from choices; the exclusion does not equate every modern institution
  or authorize swapping its name.
