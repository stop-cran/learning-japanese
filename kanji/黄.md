# 黄 — yellow

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 黃 (no. 201) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

黄 concerns yellow. 黄色 is the color noun, and 黄色い is the corresponding i-adjective. The character also appears in established words whose complete readings must be learned.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a yellow sign with a barred top, a boxed center and two feet. This is a modern-shape association, not an account of the original character.

The canonical indexing radical is 黃, no. 201; the visible modern form is 黄. The indexing glyph and the written card form need not be identical.

## Notes

- The source's こ- is a bound reading illustrated by 黄金こがね, not an instruction to shorten き in every compound. The same spelling 黄金 also has a separate おうごん entry; learn each whole word.
- 黄色きいろ contains both adjacent い sounds. The linked noun article teaches きいろ; do not replace it with another source reading without checking the expression.
- The native color noun can modify with の, while 黄色い is an i-adjective. 黄色の花 (きいろのはな) and 黄色い花 (きいろいはな) both mean yellow flowers, using different grammatical constructions.
- Compare [横](横.md), horizontal, which has 木 on the left. Its similar right-hand shape does not make the whole character mean yellow.
