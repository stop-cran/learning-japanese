# 博 — broad knowledge; doctor; expo

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 十 (no. 24), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

博 is breadth: knowing or reaching widely. In short forms it also stands for a doctor's degree or a large exposition. [博物館](../words/博物館.md) はくぶつかん is a museum, and [博士](../words/博士.md) はかせ is an expert or a doctor.

The Daijisen character entry gives ハク three senses. The first is reaching widely, or being versed in many matters, as in 博愛 はくあい, "charity; benevolence; philanthropy," 博士 はくし, "doctor; PhD; Dr.," 博識 はくしき, "extensive knowledge; erudition," and 広博 こうはく, "vast knowledge; extensive knowledge." The second is a short form of 博士, as in 医博 いはく, "doctor of medicine; MD," and 文博 ぶんはく, "doctor of literature." The third is a short form of 博覧会 はくらんかい, "exhibition; exposition; expo; fair," as in 万博 ばんぱく, "world fair; international exposition." For バク it gives gambling, as in 博奕 ばくえき, "gambling," 博徒 ばくと, "gambler," and 賭博 とばく, "gambling." Among hard-to-read words it lists 博士 はかせ and 博打 ばくち, "gambling; gaming."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [十](十.md), "ten," at the left. At the right is a shape like 甫 with an extra dot at its top right (甫 has no card here), standing over [寸](寸.md), "sun (length unit); small." Imagine ten fields of study laid out at the left, and a scholar at the right measuring each one with a small ruler: someone who knows a broad range of things. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that any part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ハク | on | [博物館](../words/博物館.md) はくぶつかん — museum |
| バク | on | 博徒 ばくと - gambler; 賭博 とばく - gambling; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 博物館, 博 supplies ハク, 物 supplies ブツ and 館 supplies カン. 博士 read はかせ is a special reading of the whole word: JMdict tags はかせ as a special reading of the spelling, and it is not split between 博 and 士, so it does not support any single reading of 博. The same spelling read はくし divides as ハク + シ. After ん, ハク becomes ぱく in 万博 ばんぱく. In 賭博, 賭 supplies ト and 博 supplies バク.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ハク, with 博識, 博覧 はくらん, "extensive reading; wide knowledge," and 博士号 はくしごう, "doctor's degree; doctorate; PhD," as examples, and prints 博士 with はかせ in brackets as a special reading; and バク, with 博労 ばくろう, "cattle or horse trader; good judge of horses or cattle," and 博徒. Neither the table nor KANJIDIC2 lists a kun reading; the Daijisen gives ひろい as one, which this card does not use. Practice selects ハク, the reading with a linked word article in which 博 supplies it; バク is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [士](士.md), "warrior; professional," in 博士, and [物](物.md), "thing, object," and [館](館.md), "hall; large building," in 博物館.
- [薄](薄.md), "thin; dilute," contains the same right-hand part as 博, under 艹, and is also read ハク. [協](協.md), "cooperate; join forces," has the same 十 key at the left, and [爆](爆.md), "burst open; explode," is read バク. All are different kanji.
- [広](広.md), "wide, spacious," and [普](普.md), "universal; widespread," overlap the sense of reaching widely in 博 and in words such as 広博 and 博愛; the Daijisen's kun ひろい for 博 is also the reading of 広い. [識](識.md), "knowledge; awareness," overlaps the knowledge in 博識, and [医](医.md), "medicine, doctor," overlaps "doctor," as in 医博. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "broad" or "doctor" as exclusive to any one character.
