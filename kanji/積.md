# 積 — pile up

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

積 covers accumulation and measured quantities.
[積む](../words/積む.md) つむ can mean stacking objects, loading them, or accumulating experience.
In 面積 めんせき, "area," the character belongs to the vocabulary of measurement instead.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the many horizontal strokes in the right-hand 責 shape as a stack beside 禾.
This is a way to remember the modern outline, not an etymology.

## Notes

In the accumulation sense, contrast 箱を積む (はこをつむ), "stack boxes,"
with 雪が積もる (ゆきがつもる), "snow piles up." The first names an action on an object; the second describes accumulation happening.

[種](種.md) and [秋](秋.md) share 禾. [精](精.md) is a useful denser look-alike, but has 米 on the left and 青 on the right.
