# 溶 — melt; dissolve

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

溶 concerns melting, thawing, or dissolving. The native verbs distinguish something melting from someone melting or dissolving it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture water beside [容](容.md), contain: a substance dissolves in the water inside a container.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- 溶ける is intransitive ichidan; 溶かす is transitive godan: 氷が溶ける こおりがとける, ice melts, versus 氷を溶かす こおりをとかす, melt ice.
- 溶く is a separate transitive godan verb: 溶いて といて. Its uses include dissolving or mixing ingredients and beating eggs; its melting sense is marked rare in the inspected dictionary.
- The existing [解ける](../words/解ける.md) article teaches coming untied or being solved. JMdict also lists 解ける in the melting/dissolving spelling family, so this useful teaching contrast is not an absolute spelling ban.
