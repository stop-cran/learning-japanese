# 将 — commander; impending

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

将 has a commander or leader association and also an impending or future association. 将来 しょうらい means future or prospects, while 将棋 しょうぎ names shogi, Japanese chess. Neither complete word should be translated by substituting commander for its first character.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the left-side strokes, the small marks at the upper right and 寸 below; imagine a commander arranging the next move. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a verified origin or an explanation of shogi's history.

## Notes

- JMdict marks 将に まさに as usually written in kana. This entry supplies the about-to sense, not every meaning of the kana sequence まさに.
- 将 はた is an adverb, while 将 しょう is the military noun commander or general. Do not exchange these lexical items. The extended reading examples are lookup material, not recommendations to replace 将来 or the connective または (or) in ordinary prose.
