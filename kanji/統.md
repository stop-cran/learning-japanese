# 統 — unify; lineage

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

統 is bringing things into one line or one whole. [統一](../words/統一.md) とういつ is unification, [統計](../words/統計.md) とうけい is statistics, [伝統](../words/伝統.md) でんとう is tradition, [系統](../words/系統.md) けいとう is a system or lineage and [大統領](../words/大統領.md) だいとうりょう is the president of a country. The Daijisen character entry gives two senses: a single continuing line or connection, as in 系統, 伝統 and 血統 けっとう, "lineage; pedigree; family line; birth"; and bringing together into one, as in 統一, 統計, 統括 とうかつ, "unification; bringing together; generalization," 統制 とうせい, "regulation; control," 統率 とうそつ, "command; lead; generalship; leadership," and 統治 とうち, "rule; reign; government; governing." The Daijisen also has a geology entry: 統 names the layers of rock that correspond to one geological epoch.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left and [充](充.md), "allot; fill," at the right. Imagine filling a spool with thread until every strand forms one continuous line. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 or 充 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 伝統, 伝 supplies デン. In 大統領, 大 supplies ダイ and 領 supplies リョウ. In 系統, 系 supplies ケイ. In 統一, 一 supplies イツ. In 統計, 計 supplies ケイ. In 統べる, 統 writes す and べる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict lists 総べる as a rarely used spelling of the same verb. The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows トウ, with 統一, 統計 and 伝統 as examples, and すべる, with 統べる. Practice selects トウ, the only reading with linked word articles; すべる is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [伝](伝.md), "transmit; convey," in 伝統, [大](大.md), "big, large," and [領](領.md), "territory; jurisdiction," in 大統領, [一](一.md), "one," in 統一 and [計](計.md), "measure; plan," in 統計. 系 in 系統 does not have a kanji card here.
- [充](充.md), "allot; fill," is the shape at the right and is read ジュウ. [練](練.md), "practice; refine," and [紙](紙.md), "paper," share the thread shape at the left. All are different kanji.
- [総](総.md), "whole; overall; total," [治](治.md), "govern; heal," [御](御.md), "honorific; govern," [制](制.md), "system; regulate," [括](括.md), "tie together; enclose," and [結](結.md), "tie; join; conclude," overlap the senses "overall," "ruling" and "governing" that KANJIDIC2 gives for 統, and the idea of gathering into one. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "unify" or "govern" as exclusive to any one character.
