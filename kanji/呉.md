# 呉 — Wu (ancient Chinese state); China

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

呉 is the name of Wu, an ancient Chinese state. Digital Daijisen gives two senses: the ancient state, in the region of present-day Jiangsu and Zhejiang, as in 呉越 and 呉音; and China in general, as in [呉服](../words/呉服.md).

## Notes

The Joyo table gives 呉 only the reading ゴ, with the examples 呉服 and 呉越同舟. Kanjipedia marks the kun readings くれ and く.れる as outside the Joyo table (外); both are in this card's metadata but are not quizzed.

漢 (“Chinese, Sino-”) overlaps with the sense of China, and 与 (“give; grant”) overlaps with the “give” meaning of くれる.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 気 (“spirit, air”), 円 (“yen, circle”), and 足 (“foot, leg”); none means Wu or China.
