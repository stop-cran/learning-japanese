# 万 — ten thousand

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

万 is the simplified (shinjitai) form of 萬, which is explained as a pictograph of a scorpion, borrowed for "ten thousand" by sound. 万 is also recorded as an old variant form of 萬.
As a number it is the big unit of Japanese counting: Japanese groups numbers by 万 (10,000), 億 (100,000,000) and so on, not by thousands.
It also means "all, many" (万能 ばんのう, all-purpose).

## Notes

万 is "ten thousand", so 二万 にまん is 20,000 and 十万 じゅうまん is 100,000. Unlike 千, 万 normally keeps 一 in prices: 一万円 いちまんえん.
Look-alikes: [九](九.md) (nine) and 方 (direction).
