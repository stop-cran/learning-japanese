# 義 — righteousness; duty; meaning

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 羊 (no. 123), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

義 is the right way for a person to act, and it appears in words for duty, principle and meaning. [主義](../words/主義.md) しゅぎ is a principle or doctrine, [意義](../words/意義.md) いぎ is meaning or significance, [義務](../words/義務.md) ぎむ is a duty or obligation and [講義](../words/講義.md) こうぎ is a lecture.

The Daijisen character entry gives five senses. The first three are abstract: the proper way a person should act, as in 義理 ぎり, "duty; sense of duty; honor; honour; decency; courtesy," and 正義 せいぎ, "justice; right; righteousness"; setting private gain aside for the public good, as in 義援金 ぎえんきん, "donation money (esp. disaster relief or charity); contribution," which Daijisen prints as 義捐金, another writing that JMdict lists; and meaning, as in 定義 ていぎ, "definition," and 語義 ごぎ, "meaning of a word."

The last two senses are concrete: a relationship made by agreement and not by blood, as in 義父 ぎふ, "father-in-law," and 義兄弟 ぎきょうだい, "brother-in-law; stepbrother; sworn brother"; and a stand-in for the real thing, as in 義眼 ぎがん, "artificial eye," 義歯 ぎし, "artificial tooth," and 義足 ぎそく, "artificial leg."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 羊 at the top and 我 below it. Imagine a flag flying above a person who stands firm on what is right. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 羊 or 我 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ギ | on | [主義](../words/主義.md) しゅぎ — principle; doctrine; [意義](../words/意義.md) いぎ — meaning; significance; [義務](../words/義務.md) ぎむ — duty; obligation; [講義](../words/講義.md) こうぎ — lecture |

In 主義, 主 supplies シュ; in 意義, 意 supplies イ; in 義務, 務 supplies ム; and in 講義, 講 supplies コウ. 義 reads ギ in all four words. KANJIDIC2 lists no kun reading for 義, and the Daijisen entry gives ギ under both 呉音, the older Chinese-derived (Go-on) layer, and 漢音, a later Chinese-derived layer, so the two layers agree.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ギ, with 義理, 意義 and 正義 as examples. Practice selects ギ, the only reading.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [主](主.md), "master, principal," in 主義, [意](意.md), "meaning, intention," in 意義, [務](務.md), "duties," in 義務 and [講](講.md), "lecture; association," in 講義.
- The key 羊 at the top does not have a kanji card here, and neither does the 我 below it. [議](議.md), "deliberate; debate," and [儀](儀.md), "ceremony; formal conduct," both contain 義 at the right and are also read ギ, as are [疑](疑.md), "doubt; suspect," and [技](技.md), "skill; technique." [養](養.md), "nourish; raise; adopt," has the same 羊 at the top. All are different kanji.
- [役](役.md), "role; duty," [務](務.md), "duties," and [税](税.md), "tax; fiscal duty," overlap the sense "duty" that 義 and 義務 carry, and [意](意.md), "meaning, intention," overlaps "meaning." [正](正.md), "correct, proper," and [敬](敬.md), "respect; honor," overlap "right" and "honor," glosses of 正義 and 義理, and [理](理.md), "reason, logic," overlaps 道理 どうり, "reason; logic; sense; truth; right," and 条理 じょうり, "reason," which the Daijisen noun entry for ぎ lists as one sense of 義. [替](替.md), "replace; substitute," overlaps the stand-in sense of 義眼, 義歯 and 義足. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "duty," "meaning" or "right" as exclusive to any one character.
