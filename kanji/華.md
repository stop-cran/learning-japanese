# 華 — flower; splendor; showiness

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

華 can mean a flower, and it can describe brilliance, splendor, or showy beauty. In [豪華](../words/豪華.md) ごうか, it contributes カ to the whole reading and the sense of lavish splendor; it does not supply はな there. Digital Daijisen also records specialized senses such as a white powder, a self-name for China, and a character-based term for sixty-one years of age.

## Notes

- Joyo lists カ, ケ, and はな. Digital Daijisen gives カ (historical クヮ), ケ (customary), and ゲ (Go-on) for compound uses. The historical クヮ is a pronunciation form of カ, not an additional modern quiz reading. The ゲ form is supported in compounds such as 蓮華 and 散華; it is not the quiz selection.
- KANJIDIC2 records わ as a name reading. Digital Daijisen additionally lists は and はる as name readings. Kanjipedia lists しろい outside Joyo; no ordinary JMdict example was found for it. None of these name/source-only forms is selected.
- Digital Daijisen also prints whole-word or special spelling readings, including 華盛頓 ワシントン. Such forms do not establish another standalone reading for 華.
- “Flower” overlaps [花](花.md), “flower,” and [咲](咲.md), “blossom; bloom”; “splendor” overlaps [栄](栄.md), “flourish; glory.” Digital Daijisen also gives 華 the senses radiance, light or glory (輝き・光・栄え) and something excellent and beautiful, Kanjipedia gives はなやか・美しい, and JMdict glosses 華 (か) as “flashiness; showiness; brilliance; splendor.” These overlap [光](光.md), “light; radiance,” and [美](美.md), “beauty.” JMdict glosses 花/華 (はな) as “flower; blossom; bloom; petal” and “cherry blossom,” overlapping [桜](桜.md), “cherry tree; blossom”; it glosses 華やか as “bright and beautiful; gorgeous; showy; brilliant; splendid” and “prosperous; flourishing; thriving,” overlapping [明](明.md), “bright, clear,” and [盛](盛.md), “thrive; heap up; serve food.” KANJIDIC2 lists “shine” for 華, overlapping [照](照.md), “illuminate; shine,” and JMdict glosses 華がない as “unappealing; uninteresting; lacking charm,” overlapping [魅](魅.md), “charm; bewitch.” Those ten cards are excluded from meaning choices. The [艶](艶.md) card (“gloss; luster; allure”) declares the 艶/華 pair, because KANJIDIC2 lists “luster” for both. The fixed quiz selection is カ from the whole word 豪華.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 犬 (“dog”), 月 (“moon, month”), and 水 (“water”); none means flower or splendor.
