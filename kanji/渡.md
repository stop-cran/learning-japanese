# 渡 — cross over

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

渡 concerns crossing from one side to another or passing something across.
[渡る](../words/渡る.md) わたる describes crossing; 渡す (わたす) can mean handing something over.
The crossing need not be through water: a road can also be crossed.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine water beside the [度](度.md) shape as a stretch you must
cross to reach the far side. This is a visual reminder, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

道を渡ります。 (みちをわたります。) — I cross the road.
Here を marks the route crossed, not a thing being changed.
In 本を渡します (ほんをわたします), "I hand over a book," を instead marks the object handed over.
Check for 氵 when distinguishing 渡 from 度.
