# 岩 — rock; boulder

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

岩 いわ is a rock or boulder. 岩塩 がんえん is rock salt or halite, using the on reading ガン. The complete compound names a mineral substance, not an instruction to pronounce standalone 岩 がん.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See [山](山.md), mountain, above [石](石.md), stone, and imagine a large exposed rock. This is a modern-shape mnemonic rather than a claim that every rock belongs to a mountain.

## Notes

- Compare [石](石.md), stone. English rock and stone glosses overlap, so do not infer a strict size boundary from the mnemonic.
- 岩塩 uses ガン + エン. The えん belongs to [塩](塩.md) here, not to 岩.
- Keep the upper 山 when distinguishing the full character from 石 alone.
