# 郷 — hometown; village

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 邑 (no. 163) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

郷 is about a home place and the countryside: one's hometown, a village or a rural district. KANJIDIC2 gives “home town”, “village”, “native place” and “district”. Kanjipedia's senses are ①さと。むらざと。いなか。 (village; countryside), ②ふるさと。生まれたところ。 (hometown; birthplace), ③ところ。場所。 (place) and ④昔の行政区画の一つ。 (a former administrative division). JMdict glosses [故郷](../words/故郷.md) こきょう “hometown; birthplace; native place; one's old home”, and gives 郷 きょう on its own the first sense “hometown” (entry 2145770).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** KanjiVG splits 郷 into the shape 乡 (strokes 1-3), the shape 艮 (strokes 4-8) and the ⻏ shape on the right. Picture a winding path at the left and a sturdy block in the middle leading to the village on the right: your home village. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 乡, 艮 or ⻏ contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists キョウ (郷里 きょうり, JMdict entry 1238540; 郷土 きょうど, JMdict entry 1238510) and ゴウ (with 近郷 among its examples), and gives the old form 鄕. Kanjipedia lists さと outside the Joyo table.
- JMdict also lists 故郷 as a spelling of ふるさと (entry 1603050, usually written in kana) and as a search-only spelling of 国 くに (entry 1592250). The linked article is 故郷 こきょう.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading くに. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings あき, あきら, and のり. These are name use, not quiz readings.

These four cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [村](村.md) (“village”): KANJIDIC2 lists “village” for 郷, and JMdict さと, written 里 or 郷, has the first sense “village; hamlet” (entry 1550760).
- [国](国.md) (“country”): JMdict 国 くに sense 4 is “home (i.e. hometown, home country)”, with the note “occ. written 故郷 or 郷里” (entry 1592250); KANJIDIC2 records くに as a name reading of 郷, and the 国 card lists くに.
- [区](区.md) (“ward; district”): KANJIDIC2 lists “district” for 郷.
- [郡](郡.md) (“county; district”): KANJIDIC2 lists “district” for both 郡 and 郷, and Kanjipedia describes 郡 as 行政区画の一つ and gives 郷 the fourth sense 昔の行政区画の一つ.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 城 (“castle”), 塔 (“tower; pagoda”), and 殿 (“palace; lord”); none means a hometown or a village.
