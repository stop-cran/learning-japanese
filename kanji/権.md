# 権 — authority; rights

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

権 concerns authority and rights, not simply physical strength.
[権利](../words/権利.md) (けんり) is a right or entitlement; 権力 (けんりょく) is power or authority.
Keeping those two words separate helps: having a right is not the same thing as having power over others.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the dense right-hand shape as an authority badge displayed beside a 木-shaped post.
This is a memory aid, not a derivation.

## Notes

The right-hand lower part resembles the many horizontal strokes of 隹, seen above 木 in [集](集.md).
In 権 the 木 stays at the left.
Do not use "physical strength" as the default translation of 権 just because English can call both kinds of ability "power."
