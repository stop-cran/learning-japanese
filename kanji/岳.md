# 岳 — lofty mountain; peak

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

岳 evokes a high mountain or its peak. 山岳 (さんがく) can refer to mountainous terrain; it need not identify one summit.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 丘 sits over 山. Picture a hill above another mountain and use the bottom 山 to distinguish the shape.

## Notes

[兵](兵.md) has spreading strokes beneath its upper part; [岸](岸.md) and [岩](岩.md) have 山 above the rest. Compare [山](山.md) for mountain, [峰](峰.md) and [頂](頂.md) for peak or summit, and [峠](峠.md) for a mountain pass or figurative peak.
