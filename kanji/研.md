# 研 — polish; study closely

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

研 occurs in sharpening and polishing, and in study or training. Two useful anchors are 研ぐ (とぐ, sharpen)
and [研究](../words/研究.md) (けんきゅう, research).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** imagine the stone-shaped 石 on the left as a sharpening stone,
with the upright strokes on the right marking the tools you will polish. Extend the image to polishing your knowledge.

## Notes

[究](究.md) is the second character of 研究, not another reading or spelling of 研.
Research is a more focused investigation than ordinary [勉強](../words/勉強.md) (べんきょう, study).

The right side resembles the inner part of [開](開.md); 研 instead has 石 on the left, not a surrounding gate.
