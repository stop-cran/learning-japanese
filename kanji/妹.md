# 妹 — younger sister

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

妹 means a younger sister. It identifies a family relationship relative to another sibling, not simply a girl who is young.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine [女](女.md) beside the small tree-like shape 未: a younger sister standing by a sapling. This is a modern visual association, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

Compare [姉](姉.md), older sister, and [弟](弟.md), younger brother. 妹 and 姉 share 女 on the left; inspect the right side before choosing.

When speaking to someone outside the family, 妹 is the neutral way to refer to your own younger sister. 妹さん is a polite way to refer to the listener's. These are usage patterns, not rules for every family conversation.
