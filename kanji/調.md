# 調 — investigate; tune

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

調 has an investigation side in [調べる](../words/調べる.md) しらべる and a tone or condition side
in 調子 ちょうし. Learn the reading with the word: looking up a fact is not the same action
as tuning an instrument, even though the character connects this vocabulary.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** 言 beside the enclosing 周 shape can suggest asking carefully
about what is inside an area. This is an invented memory scene, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

調子がいい (ちょうしがいい) can mean "in good condition"; the subject determines
whether you are discussing a person or something else.
[週](週.md) also contains 周 but has the movement-shaped outer part.
[語](語.md) and [訪](訪.md) instead share 言.
