# 正 — correct, proper

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 止 (no. 77) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

正 expresses correctness, proper form, or what is right. [正しい](../words/正しい.md) can judge either an answer's accuracy or conduct against a standard.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine [止](止.md) stopping at the top horizontal line, exactly the correct point. This is a memory aid, not a claim about historical composition.

## Notes

The English word "right" is ambiguous: 正 concerns correctness, while [右](右.md) is the direction opposite left.
For shape, 正 is [止](止.md) with a top horizontal stroke. Compare [五](五.md), whose middle strokes form a different shape.
