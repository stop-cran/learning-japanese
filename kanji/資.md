# 資 — resources; capital

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

資 points to resources that support an activity. In 資金 しきん those resources are funds;
in [資料](../words/資料.md) しりょう they are materials or information to consult.
Learning both words prevents a money-only interpretation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the upper 次 as the next task and the lower 貝 as its supply of resources.
This is an association for the modern written form, not an origin story.

## Notes

資料 can be useful information rather than a physical raw material.
資格 concerns qualifications or capabilities, not the amount of money a person owns.
[貧](貧.md), [負](負.md), and [責](責.md) share the lower 貝; identify 次 above it in 資.
