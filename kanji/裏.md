# 裏 — reverse side; lining

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

裏 concerns the back, reverse or inside of something, including a lining. It extends to what is hidden or behind the scenes rather than always naming a physical surface.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 衣-like outer strokes enclosing a 里-shaped interior: turn a garment inside out to see its lining. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a derivation.

The indexing radical is 衣; its strokes are arranged around the inner section, rather than standing as a separate left-hand 衤.

## Notes

- [表](表.md) おもて names the front or outward-facing side in this comparison. The appropriate translation of 裏 depends on what has that reverse or hidden side.
- The spelling 裏面 also writes a separate うらめん word, back side or reverse. The りめん example illustrates リ without claiming that every occurrence of 裏面 has that reading.
- 裏切る うらぎる is a complete verb meaning betray or let down, not an instruction to translate 裏 literally as secret in every expression.
