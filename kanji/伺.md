# 伺 — ask humbly; visit humbly

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

伺 appears especially in 伺う うかがう, a humble verb for asking, visiting, or hearing from someone. It lowers the speaker's own side relative to the person being shown respect; it is not simply a more formal spelling of every verb meaning 'ask'.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the person-shaped 亻 waiting quietly beside the small opening in 司 before asking permission to enter.

The canonical radical 人 appears as 亻 on the left.

## Notes

- 明日伺います あしたうかがいます can mean 'I will visit tomorrow.' In お名前を伺います おなまえをうかがいます, the verb instead concerns asking someone's name.
- Do not use 伺う as an honorific description of the respected person's own action. Its core register is humble language.
