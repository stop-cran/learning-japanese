# 風 — wind; style

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 風 (no. 182) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

風 means **wind** and also **style or manner** in compounds. The whole word tells you whether
it concerns weather, an appearance, or a way of doing something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a gust curling around the outer frame and stirring the inner 虫 shape.
This is only a memory aid; it is not a claim that the kanji historically represents an insect in the wind.

## Notes

風（かぜ）, "wind," and 風邪（かぜ）, "a cold," have the same pronunciation.
Use the usual two-character spelling 風邪 for the illness rather than treating bare 風 as its normal spelling.

[同](同.md) has a different enclosed shape; [雨](雨.md) concerns rain rather than moving air.
Long フウ in 台風 is not the same as a short フ.
