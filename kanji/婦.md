# 婦 — woman; wife

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

婦 carries woman or wife meanings in compounds. In [夫婦](../words/夫婦.md) (ふうふ), it is the wife member of a married pair.
主婦 (しゅふ) instead names a housewife or homemaker; the whole word supplies that role.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use [帰](帰.md) as a visual frame, then deliberately give 婦 the full 女 on the left.
This shape comparison is a memory aid, not an origin story.

## Common words

- [夫婦](../words/夫婦.md) (ふうふ) — married couple
- 主婦 (しゅふ) — housewife; homemaker
- 婦人 (ふじん) — woman; lady; the cited dictionary marks this term as dated and potentially sensitive

## Notes

Do not treat every word containing 婦 as a claim that the woman is married. 婦人 has an adult-woman sense.
For [帰](帰.md), inspect the left side; for [始](始.md) and [姉](姉.md), inspect the right side.
[女](女.md) and [妻](妻.md) share the woman and wife meanings found in 婦; learn which sense a whole word uses.
