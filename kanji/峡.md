# 峡 — gorge; strait

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

峡 is a narrow space with higher ground or land on either side. A 峡谷 (きょうこく) is a gorge; in 海峡 (かいきょう) it is water that passes between land masses.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Spot 山 on the left. Imagine standing on one steep wall of the narrow gap and seeing the other side.
