# 伐 — fell trees; attack

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

伐 means cutting down trees, attacking or killing enemies, and boasting. KANJIDIC2 lists “fell”, “strike”, “attack” and “punish”. The word [伐採](../words/伐採.md) ばっさい is “felling timber; cutting down trees; logging; lumbering”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 亻, a variant of [人](人.md), "person," stands on the left, and the shape 戈 stands on the right. Picture 戈 as a long-handled axe held by a woodcutter standing next to a tall tree. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 亻 or 戈 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists バツ only, with 伐採, 征伐 and 殺伐 さつばつ (“brutal (atmosphere, society, etc.); savage; fierce”). Kanjipedia lists ハツ and the kun きる, うつ and ほこる outside the table (外); Digital Daijisen gives バツ and the kun きる and うつ.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 伐; Digital Daijisen gives the name reading のり, a name use, not a quiz reading.

These ten cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [攻](攻.md) (“attack; go on the offensive”), [討](討.md) (“attack; discuss”), [撃](撃.md) (“shoot; strike”) and [打](打.md) (“strike”): 伐 can mean attacking or striking an enemy.
- [切](切.md) (“cut”): cutting down trees, as in 伐る きる, “to cut down (e.g. trees)”.
- [殺](殺.md) (“kill”) and [戦](戦.md) (“battle”): 伐 can mean killing enemies and fighting them.
- [罰](罰.md) (“punishment; penalty; divine retribution”) and [刑](刑.md) (“criminal punishment”): 伐 can mean to punish.
- [誇](誇.md) (“pride; boast”): 伐 can also mean boasting.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 絹 (“silk”), 鶏 (“chicken”), and 缶 (“tin can; container”); silk, chicken and a tin can are a fabric, a bird and a container; none means felling trees or attacking.
