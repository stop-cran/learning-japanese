# 郊 — outskirts; suburbs

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 邑 (no. 163) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

郊 concerns the outskirts or suburban area of a town. 郊外 names suburbs, and 近郊 the surrounding area close to a city.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the 交-shaped part as roads meeting beside a town marked by the right-hand 阝. This is a visual memory story, not etymology.

The right-hand 阝 represents the canonical radical 邑, no. 163. It is not the left-hand 阜 radical used in 防.
