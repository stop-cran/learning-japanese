# 現 — actual; present

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 玉 (as 王 on the left, no. 96) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

現 connects actuality with being present or becoming apparent. [現実](../words/現実.md) げんじつ is reality;
現れる あらわれる describes something appearing or becoming visible.
The indexing radical 玉 takes the left-side shape 王 here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine something real coming into view beside the 王-shaped part.
The visible 見 on the right makes this useful for recall, but it is not a claim about historical formation.

## Notes

雲の間から月が現れた。 (くものあいだからつきがあらわれた。) — The moon appeared between the clouds.
現れる describes what appears; it does not mean that someone looks at it.

[見](見.md) lacks the left-hand component, while [親](親.md) has different material to the left of 見.
[球](球.md) shares the 王-shaped side but has 求 on the right.
