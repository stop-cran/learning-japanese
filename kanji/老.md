# 老 — grow old

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 老 (no. 125) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

老 concerns aging and old age, especially in people. [老人](../words/老人.md) ろうじん
is an older person, while 老いる おいる is the verb "grow old."
This is not the character to choose automatically for an old book or an old building.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** compare the shared upper strokes of 老 and 考, then use 老's
compact curved lower part as the identifying detail. This is shape recognition, not etymology.
