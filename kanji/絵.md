# 絵 — drawing; painting

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

絵 represents a drawn or painted picture. The single-character word [絵](../words/絵.md) え
can name a child's sketch as well as a painting; it does not itself specify a technique.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a picture stitched with the left-hand 糸 beside the 会 shape.
This uses the modern written form, not an account of how the character originated.

## Readings

Although え is a word on its own, KANJIDIC2 classifies エ as an on reading.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| エ | on | [絵](../words/絵.md) え — picture; 絵本 えほん — picture book |
| カイ | on | 絵画 かいが — painting |

## Common words

## Notes

[給](給.md) has 合 rather than 会 on the right. [紙](紙.md) and [終](終.md) also share 糸.
For a photograph, learn [写真](../words/写真.md) しゃしん rather than translating every English
use of "picture" as 絵.
