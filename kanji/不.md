# 不 — not, non-

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

不 often negates the idea that follows it: [不安](../words/不安.md) is a lack of ease, and 不足 is a lack of what is needed.
It belongs to established words; it is not a prefix to add freely to any Japanese word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the top bar blocking the branching strokes below: "not allowed through."
This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Common words

- [不安](../words/不安.md) ふあん — anxiety; uneasy
- 不便 ふべん — inconvenient; inconvenience
- 不足 ふそく — shortage; insufficiency
- 不器用 ぶきよう — clumsy; unskillful
