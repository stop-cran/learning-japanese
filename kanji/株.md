# 株 — share; stump; rooted plant

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

株 can be a company share, a stump left after a tree is cut, or a plant with its roots or stem attached. JMdict gives the exact glosses “stock; share,” “stump,” and “(clump of) roots; plant with attached roots or stem.” The linked [株](../words/株.md) article contrasts the financial sense with the plant sense and notes that one 株 can count a share or a plant.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for シュ: a tree stump, as in 株 しゅ, “stump”; and a rooted plant, as in 雌雄異株 しゆういしゅ, “dioecy; dioecism.” 守株 しゅしゅ is a word-level reading meaning “lack of innovation; stupidity”; do not split that whole-word reading among the characters.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md) at the left and the shape 朱 at the right. Imagine a tree left rooted in the ground, or a share of its value on paper. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 木 or 朱 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In the linked word 株, the whole character is read かぶ. In the compound 雌雄異株, the Daijisen entry places 株 under シュ. The standalone シュ word and 守株 are separate lexical identities.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows かぶ with 株 and 株式 かぶしき, share in a company or stock. Practice selects かぶ, the reading with a linked word article. シュ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; being outside the practice set does not make it invalid.

## Notes

- The common-word article is for the single character 株, so it has no other kanji.
- [未](未.md) and [本](本.md) resemble the tree-like strokes in 株, while [休](休.md) has 木 at the right. All are different kanji.
- [資](資.md) overlaps share capital; [根](根.md), [幹](幹.md) and [柱](柱.md) overlap roots, trunks and posts; [木](木.md) and [樹](樹.md) overlap trees and rooted plants. The broader rooted-plant sense overlaps [桜](桜.md)’s cherry-tree title, so the pair is mutually excluded. The other listed cards are kept out of this card’s quiz options. Do not treat “share,” “stump” or “rooted plant” as exclusive to any one character.
- Digital Daijisen also records くい and くいぜ for 株 as outside-table spellings meaning “stump.” JMdict lists 株 in the くい entry (1279450) with an abbreviation/archaic note, and supports 株 くいぜ in 2645440; its alternate くいせ is marked “out-dated or obsolete kana usage.” The frozen KANJIDIC2 XML and the supplied metadata skeleton list only かぶ, so these spellings do not add metadata readings or quiz selections.
