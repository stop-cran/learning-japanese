# 言 — say; words

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

言 concerns saying something and the words that are said. The everyday verb is [言う](../words/言う.md) いう.
The same shape is visible on the left of [話](話.md), [語](語.md), and [計](計.md).

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the lines above [口](口.md) as words coming out of a mouth.
This explains how to remember the modern shape, not its verified historical formation.

## Notes

Compare [話す](../words/話す.md) はなす, "talk; speak": 言う often singles out what someone says,
while 話す can describe a conversation or speaking a language.
Keep the dictionary spelling いう even though the plain form is often pronounced ゆう.
