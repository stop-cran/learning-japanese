# 堂 — hall

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

堂 refers to a hall or a building used for a particular activity. 食堂 is a place for eating; 本堂 is a temple's main hall.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the roof-like top sheltering a hall, with [口](口.md) as an entrance and [土](土.md) as the ground beneath it. This is a memory aid, not a historical derivation.

- 講堂 こうどう — auditorium; lecture hall
- 本堂 ほんどう — main hall of a Buddhist temple

## Notes

堂 does not mean every kind of room. [室](室.md) is useful for rooms; 堂 is common in names of halls and certain buildings.

Compare the stacked shapes of [京](京.md) and [高](高.md). Check the bottom: 堂 ends in 土.
