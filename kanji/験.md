# 験 — test; verify

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 馬 (no. 187) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

験 concerns **testing, verification, or an effect demonstrated in practice**. It is useful chiefly
through compounds: an examination, an experiment, and experience are different ideas sharing this character.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the horse-shaped 馬 on the left entering a test station under
the roof-like top of the right side. This is a memory story, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

実験 is じっけん, with a small っ, not じつけん.
[試](試.md) and 験 both contribute to 試験, but they are not interchangeable spellings.

[駅](駅.md) shares the left-hand 馬; its right side is much simpler.
The lower marks of [鳥](鳥.md) may look familiar, but 鳥 is a separate character, not the left side of 験.
