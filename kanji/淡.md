# 淡 — pale; faint; plain; unsalted

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

淡 is thin, light or low in strength: a pale colour, a faint taste, a plain manner, water without salt. The linked [淡水](../words/淡水.md) たんすい is JMdict's "fresh water (i.e. not salt water)," and its word article explains that "fresh" here concerns salinity.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for タン. One example of each:

- a colour that is not deep, あわい: 淡彩 たんさい, "light colouring," and 濃淡 のうたん, "light and shade; shade (of colour, color)";
- no salt: 淡水;
- a feeling that is plain, not warm: 淡白 たんぱく, "light (flavor, color, etc.); simple; plain," and its second sense, "frank; candid; easygoing; unselfish; indifferent (to); not particular (about)"; also 冷淡 れいたん, "cool; indifferent; apathetic; half-hearted";
- the old province Awaji: 淡路 あわじ, a place name in JMdict, "Awaji (island)," not a reading to practise.

Daijisen also lists the name-only readings あわ, あわし and あわじ; they are not asked here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right side stacks one [火](火.md), "fire," above another. Imagine water thinned out until its colour is faint, with a small flame flickering over it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or 火 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| タン | on | [淡水](../words/淡水.md) たんすい — fresh water |
| あわ.い | kun | 淡い あわい — light (color, taste, etc.); faint (e.g. smell); pale; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 淡水, 淡 supplies タン and [水](水.md) supplies スイ. 淡水魚 たんすいぎょ, "freshwater fish," adds 魚 ギョ. In 淡い, 淡 writes あわ and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 淡雪 あわゆき, "light snow; light snowfall; thin layer of snow," uses the same あわ; JMdict also lists 泡雪 as another spelling of that word, so it is shared with the [泡](泡.md) card. JMdict lists 淡白 first, then 淡泊 (the form tagged as common in news), and marks 澹泊 and 淡薄 as "rarely used kanji form."

Kanjipedia lists うす.い for 淡, but KANJIDIC2 does not, so it is not part of this card's metadata or questions.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows タン with 淡水, 濃淡 and 冷淡, and あわい with 淡い and 淡雪. Practice selects タン, the reading of the linked word article. あわ.い is shown with a dictionary example and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- [水](水.md), "water," supplies スイ in 淡水. In the other words above, [魚](魚.md), "fish," supplies ギョ in 淡水魚, [濃](濃.md), "concentrated; dark," supplies ノウ in 濃淡 and [冷](冷.md), "cold; chill," supplies レイ in 冷淡.
- [談](談.md), "converse; consult," has the same stacked-火 shape at the right with 言 at the left. [泊](泊.md), "stay overnight; anchor," and [治](治.md), "govern; heal," have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [薄](薄.md), "thin; dilute," [微](微.md), "minute; faint," [涼](涼.md), "cool, refreshing," [軽](軽.md), "lightweight; slight," [弱](弱.md), "weak; frail," [素](素.md), "unadorned; element," [清](清.md), "pure, clean," [白](白.md), "white," and [温](温.md), "warm, mild," overlap the senses of paleness, thinness, plainness, mildness or freshness. 薄 and 涼 also carry うす.い. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "pale," "faint" or "plain" as exclusive to any one character.
