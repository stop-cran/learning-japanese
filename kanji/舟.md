# 舟 — boat; watercraft

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 舟 (no. 137) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

舟 concerns boats and watercraft. [舟](../words/舟.md) ふね is an alternative spelling in the same dictionary family as 船, with a tendency toward small, hand-propelled craft rather than an absolute size rule.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a narrow hull seen from above, with short marks inside it for people or cargo.
