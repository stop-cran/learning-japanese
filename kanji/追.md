# 追 — chase; pursue

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

追 is following after something, often in pursuit.
[追う](../words/追う.md) おう describes chasing or following; 追加 ついか concerns adding something afterward.
Pursuit does not itself tell you whether the pursuer catches up.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine following the stacked inner shape along the lower sweep, trying to catch it.
This is an invented memory picture, not an etymology.

## Notes

追いかける gives another way to express chasing, but 追加 is a separate word meaning an addition.
The canonical radical 辵 is the visible 辶.
With [近](近.md), [週](週.md), and [返](返.md), the outer sweep is shared; the inner stacked shape identifies 追.
