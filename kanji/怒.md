# 怒 — anger

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

怒 names anger and becoming angry. The verb [怒る](../words/怒る.md) can be read おこる or いかる;
JMdict marks いかる as formal or literary. These are readings of the same written verb.
怒り いかり is the noun "anger."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 女 and 又 shapes above [心](心.md), then imagine anger building
under that crowded top. This is a visual memory aid, not an explanation of the character's origin.

## Notes

[始](始.md) and [安](安.md) also contain a 女 shape, but place it differently.

Learn 怒っている (おこっている), "is angry," as well as the dictionary form.
