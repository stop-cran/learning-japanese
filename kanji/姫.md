# 姫 — princess; daughter of noble birth

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

姫 is a princess or a young woman of noble birth. The storybook 姫 and a 王妃 (おうひ, queen consort) need not hold the same position: one title can come from birth, the other from marriage.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Spot 女 at the left and the boxed, angular shape at the right. [臣](臣.md) has a similar angular outline but stands on its own.

## Notes

Compare [妨](妨.md) and [姉](姉.md): both start with 女 but have different right halves. In 姫 read ひめ, both syllables belong to the character. [媛](媛.md) can also be read ひめ in the place name 愛媛 (えひめ); do not replace 姫 with 媛 in ordinary writing. [臣](臣.md) (retainer) is not a title for a daughter. [娘](娘.md) says “daughter” without the rank; [妃](妃.md) in 王妃 identifies a king's wife.
