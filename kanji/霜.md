# 霜 — frost

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 雨 (no. 173) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[霜](../words/霜.md) しも is frost, including frost on cold surfaces
and inside a refrigerator. The same noun can poetically mean white or
grey hair. Character-dictionary branches also include years passing and
comparisons with white, cold, sharp or severe things.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the 雨-shaped top over
[相](相.md), whose present outline has [木](木.md) beside [目](目.md).
Imagine frost above a tree you can see. This is an invented scene for the
modern shape, not a historical or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Kind | Example or disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソウ | on | 霜害 そうがい, frost damage; 星霜 せいそう, passing years. Recognition examples, not linked practice supports. |
| しも | kun | [霜](../words/霜.md) しも, frost; the existing article supports practice. |

Both source readings remain. The じも in compounds such as 初霜
はつじも is a voiced form, not a replacement for the source しも.
Weblio's Wiktionary section additionally lists ショウ. 字通's
ほろぶ and うしなう are positive supplemental glosses, kept qualified
rather than offered as ordinary pronunciations of the weather noun.

## Common words

- [霜](../words/霜.md) しも - frost.
- 霜害 そうがい - frost damage.
- 星霜 せいそう - passing years.

## Notes

- [古](古.md), old, describes old things or times rather than frost;
  passing years does not make 霜 that adjective. [右](右.md), right,
  is a direction and [川](川.md), river, a watercourse, not frost.
- The word article teaches 霜が降りる しもがおりる.
  日本国語大辞典 also records historical or poetic 霜降る and 霜置く;
  they are not universally invalid expressions. Frost does not fall
  through the air as [雪](雪.md), snow, does.
- White-hair and white/cold comparisons receive direct guards with
  [白](白.md), [毛](毛.md), [髪](髪.md), [冷](冷.md),
  [寒](寒.md) and [凜](凜.md). Sharp, intense and severe branches
  also receive precautions with [鋭](鋭.md), [激](激.md),
  [切](切.md), [刻](刻.md) and [険](険.md).
  These are not claims that every cold object or sharp edge is frost.
- Passing years connect conservatively with [年](年.md), [時](時.md),
  [代](代.md), [期](期.md), [歳](歳.md), [齢](齢.md),
  [歴](歴.md) and the qualified time branch of [宙](宙.md).
  A clock time, an age and elapsed years are not identical.
- 日本国語大辞典 separately records medicinal material charred and
  powdered. [粉](粉.md), [末](末.md), [薬](薬.md) and [剤](剤.md)
  receive direct broad-label precautions; the obsolete preparation sense
  does not make ordinary frost a drug or imply that anything is safe to ingest.
- 字通 retains perishing and loss. Direct precautions cover
  [失](失.md), [損](損.md), [負](負.md), [亡](亡.md), [死](死.md),
  [破](破.md), [討](討.md), [耗](耗.md) and [荒](荒.md),
  not a claim that every damaging or attacking action is a frost sense.
  The black color of a charred preparation is not a general "black"
  meaning of 霜.
- Broader senses of 霜降り, such as marbled meat or a mottled pattern,
  belong to that complete word. A sword metaphor in 秋霜 or 霜剣
  does not automatically make bare 霜 an ordinary word for any sword.
