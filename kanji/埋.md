# 埋 — bury; embed

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

埋 connects burying or covering something with filling a space. 埋める うめる can mean burying something or filling a gap; 埋葬 まいそう names burial.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 土 heaped around the dense 里-shaped part until it is buried.

## Notes

- 埋める has separate dictionary entries for うめる and うずめる. The linked word article teaches うめる; the unlinked うずめる row is not permission to change that article's reading.
- 埋まる うまる is intransitive: something is or becomes buried or filled. 埋もれる うもれる is also intransitive. The two 埋める entries are transitive: someone buries or covers something.
- JMdict restricts the vacancy-filling and debt-settling senses of 埋まる to うまる. The うずまる example above uses its unrestricted buried or covered senses, not those restricted senses.
- In the 埋ける example, bank a fire means cover it with ashes to preserve the embers, not put money in a bank.
