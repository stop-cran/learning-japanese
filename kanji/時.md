# 時 — time, hour

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **Phonetic:** 寺 (ジ) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

時 is a phonetic compound: [日](日.md) (sun) gives the meaning, since the sun's course was how time was told, and 寺 (the temple
character, also read ジ) supplies the sound ジ. It means "time", "hour" and "the occasion when".
Older forms may have had a different right-hand component; the sun-plus-sound analysis is the standard one in modern references.

## Notes

- 時 counts o'clock: 一時 いちじ, **四時 よじ**, **七時 しちじ**, **九時 くじ**, with 半 for half past: 三時半 さんじはん.
- 時 (time) is not the same as [間](間.md) (interval): 時間 combines both and means "time" as an amount.
- Look-alike: 持 (hold) shares 寺 but has 扌 on the left. The 日 on the left of 時 is a sun, narrower than [月](月.md) (moon).
