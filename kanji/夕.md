# 夕 — evening

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 夕 (no. 36) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

夕 concerns evening, especially the time as daylight fades. Learn it first in [夕方](../words/夕方.md), rather than assuming that the bare character is the usual conversational word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the curved outline holding a final small stroke of evening light. This is a mnemonic for the present shape, not a verified historical explanation.

- [夕方](../words/夕方.md) ゆうがた — late afternoon, early evening
- 夕日 ゆうひ — evening sun, setting sun
- 夕食 ゆうしょく — evening meal, dinner

## Notes

[外](外.md) adds strokes to the right of a 夕-shaped part; [多](多.md) stacks two such shapes. Keep the whole characters distinct.

Compare 夕方 with [夜](夜.md) よる, the night or darker part of the evening. Their everyday boundaries depend on context, not a fixed clock time. 夕食 mixes kun ゆう with on ショク from [食](食.md).
