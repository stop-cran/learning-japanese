# 切 — cut

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

切 centers on cutting, severing, or bringing something to an end. Not every compound is literal: 大切 means "important" or "precious."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a blade, 刀, cutting beside the [七](七.md)-shaped left part. This is a visual memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

切る is a godan verb: 切ります きります, 切って きって. Do not confuse it with [着る](../words/着る.md) きる, "put on clothing," whose polite form is 着ます きます.

Compare [分](分.md), which emphasizes dividing, and [別](別.md), which emphasizes separation or difference.
