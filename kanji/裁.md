# 裁 — judge; cut out cloth

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

裁 has two meaning groups. Kanjipedia's first sense is たつ: cutting cloth to make clothes, and its second is さばく: judging right and wrong. KANJIDIC2 gives “tailor,” “judge,” “decision,” and “cut out (pattern).” [裁判](../words/裁判.md) さいばん belongs to the judging group; JMdict glosses it as “trial; judgement; judgment.” [裁縫](../words/裁縫.md) さいほう belongs to the cloth group, and Digital Daijisen lists it under its sense of cutting cloth; JMdict glosses it as “sewing; needlework.” In both whole words, 裁 is read サイ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the hooked frame 𢦏 wraps around [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment": the first three strokes of 𢦏 sit across the top, and the rest of its 戈 shape sweeps down the right side. Imagine a sharp hooked edge cutting all the way around a garment, the way a tailor cuts out cloth. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 𢦏 or 衣 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists サイ, たつ, and さばく, and cross-references 断つ and 絶つ for たつ. KANJIDIC2 records no name readings, and Digital Daijisen's character entry gives none.
- Kanjipedia's third sense, ようす (appearance), appears in 体裁 ていさい, “(outward) appearance” (JMdict 1409480).

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [判](判.md) (“judge; seal”) and [審](審.md) (“examine; judge; trial”): they share the title's “judge,” and JMdict glosses 裁判 as “trial; judgement; judgment.”
- [決](決.md) (“decide”) and [定](定.md) (“determine; settle”): KANJIDIC2 gives 裁 “decision,” and JMdict glosses 裁く with “to decide (on).”
- [切](切.md) (“cut”), [断](断.md) (“sever; refuse”), and [絶](絶.md) (“sever; cease”): KANJIDIC2 gives 裁 “cut out (pattern),” JMdict glosses 裁つ as “to cut (cloth),” and the Joyo table cross-references 裁つ with 断つ and 絶つ.
- [縫](縫.md) (“sew; stitch”): JMdict glosses 裁縫 as “sewing; needlework.”
- [布](布.md) (“cloth; distribute”): it shares “cloth” with the title, and JMdict glosses 裁つ as “to cut (cloth).”

The [裂](裂.md) card (“tear apart; split”) declares the 裁/裂 pair, because JMdict glosses 裂く as “to cut up; to cleave; to cut open”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 糸 (“thread; yarn”), 針 (“needle; pin”), and 法 (“law; method”); none means to judge or to cut out cloth.
