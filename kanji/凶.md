# 凶 — ill fortune; evil

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 凵 (no. 17) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凶 concerns ill fortune, an unfavorable outcome, or evil. In
[凶作](../words/凶作.md) きょうさく, it describes a poor harvest; in
凶悪 (きょうあく), it concerns heinousness rather than agricultural failure.
Learn the whole compound rather than translating every 凶 as "bad luck."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the crossing strokes 乂
inside the open-topped frame 凵 as a warning mark for an unfavorable outcome.
This is a memory aid for the current shape, not a historical derivation or
a claim about a phonetic component.

## Notes

- 凶 (きょう) can itself mean bad luck. In fortune-telling, 大凶
  (だいきょう) means very bad luck; 凶兆 (きょうちょう) is an ill omen.
- [悪](悪.md) also concerns badness or evil, but 凶 is not a replacement
  for the ordinary adjective 悪い (わるい).
- [凡](凡.md) concerns being ordinary; [凸](凸.md) and [凹](凹.md)
  concern raised and sunken shapes. Similar outlines do not make their
  meanings interchangeable with 凶.
