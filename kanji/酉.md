# 酉 — Rooster zodiac sign

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 酉 (no. 164) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

酉 names the **tenth of the twelve zodiac signs**, the Rooster.
The native sign noun is [酉](../words/酉.md) とり. It is not the ordinary
spelling for a living [鳥](鳥.md), bird, or [鶏](鶏.md), domestic chicken.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine a framed calendar label
for the Rooster. Compare [西](西.md), west: 酉 has a separate interior
horizontal line as stroke six, before the bottom line at stroke seven.
This is a way to remember the modern shapes, not a claim that 酉 developed from 西.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ユウ | on | 辛酉 しんゆう — Metal Rooster, the 58th term of the sixty-term calendar cycle; also read かのととり; recognition only |
| とり | kun | [酉](../words/酉.md) とり — Rooster zodiac sign; 酉年 とりどし — year of the Rooster |

Only とり is selected for practice. ユウ is a verified compound reading,
not an obsolete or name-only reading, but this lesson focuses on the native
sign noun. Learn 辛酉's two readings as whole expressions; they are not
instructions to exchange individual character readings freely.

## Notes

- 酉 is also the indexing radical in [酒](酒.md), alcohol/sake, and
  [配](配.md), distribute. The verified radical name **ひよみのとり**
  distinguishes this calendar character from 鳥. **とりへん** names the
  left-side component, as in 配; it does not describe standalone 酉 as a compound.
  A shared radical does not make the sign noun an ordinary word for alcohol.
- [曲](曲.md), bend/musical piece, and [画](画.md), picture/brushstroke,
  also have divided enclosures. They and 配 are shape comparisons, not names
  for the Rooster sign. 西 is not a safe wrong meaning answer: 酉 has an
  old west sense too.
- Historical uses include an hour around 6 p.m., west, and the eighth lunar
  month. Dictionary hour mappings include both 5–7 p.m. and 6–8 p.m.;
  no single conversion is asserted here. These are not ordinary modern
  navigation or scheduling substitutes, and lunar month eight is not simply August.
- The twelve signs and the sixty combinations of calendar stems and branches
  are related but different sets. The grade-9 tag classifies name-use kanji;
  it does not make this sign noun a personal name.
