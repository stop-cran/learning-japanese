# 丸 — round; circle

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 丶 (no. 3) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

丸 concerns a round shape or a whole, intact unit. 丸い まるい describes something round; 丸 まる can name a circle or a circle-shaped mark. The same character occurs in names of ships and in words for pills.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the long curving stroke wrapping around a small dot, as if enclosing a round object.

## Notes

- 丸い is an い-adjective: 丸くする まるくする means make something round. 丸める まるめる can mean roll something up or make it round.
- [円](円.md) えん and 丸 まる overlap in the idea of a circle, but 円 also names the yen and is not a universal substitute for 丸.
