# 力 — power

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

力 is commonly explained as a pictograph of a bent arm showing muscle, which gives "strength"; another explanation sees an old plough-like digging tool.
The origin is debated. It means "power, strength, effort, force".
As a key it appears in characters about effort and work: 助 (help), 動 (move), 働 (work). 男 ([男](男.md), man, 田 field + 力) contains 力 as a component, but its key is 田.

## Notes

リョク is the main on reading in compounds; リキ appears in a few older ones. The kun reading ちから can voice inside compounds: 力強い ちからづよい, 底力 そこぢから.

Look-alike: 刀 (sword) has the same two strokes in the same order; in 力 the left-falling stroke crosses the horizontal one, while in 刀 it starts on it.
