# 冠 — crown; peerless

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 冖 (the cover-like upper part, no. 14) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

冠 means a crown or ceremonial head covering, and can also express being foremost or peerless.
[冠](../words/冠.md) (かんむり) names the headgear; [王冠](../words/王冠.md) (おうかん) is a royal crown and also a crown-type bottle cap.
Learn each complete word rather than translating every 冠 compound as a physical crown.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the cover-like 冖 as a crown over [元](元.md), with 寸 at the lower right to hold it in place.
This is an invented memory aid, not a historical explanation or a verified phonetic analysis.

## Notes

The "peerless" meaning belongs to the reading かん, not the native noun かんむり.
Recognize the formal expression 世界に冠たる (せかいにかんたる), "foremost in the world," used before a noun.

In kanji terminology, かんむり names a top component: 宀 is called うかんむり.
冠 itself has 冖, without the upper dot of 宀 in [完](完.md) and [守](守.md).
[冗](冗.md) shares the cover-like top but has a much simpler lower part; 完 contains 元 below its roof, while 守 contains 寸.
These are shape comparisons, not claims of a shared origin.
