# 末 — end; tip

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

末 names an end or extremity. In time words, it selects the closing part of a period:
[週末](../words/週末.md) しゅうまつ is the weekend, and 月末 げつまつ is the end of a month.
The character can also refer to a physical tip.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the long top horizontal spreads out at the upper tip of the 木-like shape.
This is a memory aid based on the modern form, not a claim about its historical origin.

## Notes

In 末 the upper horizontal is longer; in [未](未.md) the lower one is longer.
[本](本.md) has its extra line lower down, while [木](木.md) has only one horizontal.
末 can name the ending portion of a period; it need not mean only its final instant.
