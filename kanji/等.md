# 等 — equal; and so on

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (as the bamboo top ⺮, no. 118) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

等 can describe equality or a class within a ranking.
[平等](../words/平等.md) びょうどう concerns equality, while 一等 いっとう is first class, first place, or first prize.
The whole word tells you whether things are equal or being ranked.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** compare the paired marks of the bamboo top as two equal groups.
This is a memory aid for one meaning, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ひと.しい | kun | 等しい ひとしい — equal |
| トウ | on | 一等 いっとう — first class; first prize |

平等 is pronounced びょうどう: learn the voiced どう in this compound.
Read as など, 等 means "and so on; and the like" after examples; this is usually written in kana as など.
The source also records the grouping suffix -ら, whose kana spelling appears in 彼ら (かれら), "they."

## Common words

- [平等](../words/平等.md) びょうどう — equality
- 等しい ひとしい — equal
- 一等 いっとう — first class; first prize

## Notes

The bamboo radical 竹 appears as ⺮ above 寺.
[待](待.md) also contains 寺, but has a left-hand component instead of a bamboo top.
[笑](笑.md) and [箱](箱.md) share the top but not the lower part.

[同](同.md), "same; equal," overlaps with the equality sense of 等.
