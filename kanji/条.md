# 条 — clause; condition; item; line

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

条 can mark an item or clause in a document, a condition or term, and a line or streak. [条件](../words/条件.md) じょうけん means “condition; term; requirement; qualification; prerequisite.”

The Daijisen character entry gives seven senses for ジョウ. One example of each:

- a thin tree branch: 枝条 しじょう, “branch (of a tree)”;
- a line or streak: 条痕 じょうこん, “linear mark; abrasion; scratch; streak”;
- a route in an ancient land grid: 条里制 じょうりせい, “jōri system of land subdivision in ancient Japan”;
- an orderly line of reasoning: 条理 じょうり, “reason”;
- a clause or item written out: 条項 じょうこう, “clause; article; stipulations”;
- a matter or affair: 別条 べつじょう, “something unusual; something wrong; mishap; accident; serious condition; different situation,” usually in the negative;
- a substitute character for 絛 in 条虫 じょうちゅう, “tapeworm; cestode”; JMdict lists 絛虫 as a rarely used kanji form.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 木, “tree, wood,” at the bottom and the crossed strokes above it. Picture one clause as a line leading down to a point. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either visible part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| すじ | kun | 条 すじ — muscle; tendon; sinew; also line; stripe; no word article yet, not selected for questions here; JMdict heads the entry 筋 and also lists 条 as a spelling |

In 条件, 条 supplies ジョウ and 件 supplies ケン. The reading すじ is also used for 条 in some spellings; the JMdict entry headed 筋 lists 条 as another spelling and includes “line; stripe; streak.” The KANJIDIC2 readings チョウ, デキ and えだ are documented above but are not the standard readings presented by Daijisen or Joyo.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジョウ with 条理, 条約 and 箇条. JMdict glosses 条約 “treaty; pact; convention; agreement” and 箇条 “item; article; clause; section; point.” Practice selects ジョウ, the reading with a linked word article. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid. The table gives 條 in parentheses as the older form.

## Notes

- The other kanji in 条件 is [件](件.md), “case; item.”
- [未](未.md), “not yet,” and [末](末.md), “end; tip,” resemble the 木 base with a changed upper stroke; [束](束.md), “bundle,” also contains a compact 木 shape. These are the distractors, and none shares an attested reading with 条.
- [件](件.md), “case; item,” [個](個.md), “individual; item counter,” [筋](筋.md), “muscle; sinew; line; plot,” [線](線.md), “line; track,” [状](状.md), “condition; form,” [態](態.md), “attitude; condition,” [候](候.md), “weather conditions,” [部](部.md), “section; department,” [章](章.md), “chapter; badge,” [列](列.md), “row; queue,” and [並](並.md), “line up; parallel,” overlap the item, condition, clause/section or line senses in this card’s title and examples, so they are excluded from its quiz options. 枝 (“branch; twig”) is a separate Daijisen sense of 条, but it does not match this card’s quiz title.
- Further Daijisen examples include 柳条 りゅうじょう, “willow twig”; 線条 せんじょう, “line; a streak”; and 条文 じょうぶん, “text (of a law, treaty, contract, etc.); provisions.”
- Daijisen also lists the whole-word spelling 条彫り すじぼり as a hard-to-read form; the extract gives no gloss and no JMdict entry was found. 条播き すじまき means “row seeding; drill seeding; drilling (seeds),” and 発条 ばね means “spring (device).” JMdict marks 発条 as a rarely used kanji form and labels ばね “gikun (meaning as reading) or jukujikun (special kanji reading)” for the whole word; these spellings do not establish readings to split among the kanji. They are whole-word readings, not alternate character readings for the quiz.
