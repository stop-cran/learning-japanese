# 衝 — collide; thrust

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 行 (no. 144) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

衝 is about thrusting at something or running into it. KANJIDIC2 gives “collide,” “thrust,” “pierce,” “stab,” and “prick,” and Kanjipedia's first sense is つく。ぶつかる。つきあたる (thrust; bump; run into). In [衝突](../words/衝突.md) しょうとつ, JMdict gives “collision; crash; impact; running into” and “clash; skirmish; conflict; discord; quarrel; brush.” The whole word reads しょうとつ, with ショウ for 衝.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [行](行.md), "go," is split into a left half and a right half, and [重](重.md), "heavy; piled up," stands between them. Imagine a heavily loaded cart going down the road, thrusting forward until it collides with something. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 行 or 重 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ショウ only. Kanjipedia marks つく outside the Joyo table; Digital Daijisen also gives つく.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings つぎ, みち, もり, and ゆく; these are name use, not quiz readings.
- Kanjipedia's third sense, かなめ (key point), appears in 要衝 ようしょう, “important point; strategic position; key point” (JMdict 1662320).

These ten cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [突](突.md) (“thrust; sudden”): it shares the title's “thrust,” and JMdict lists 衝く as a spelling of 突く.
- [抵](抵.md) (“resist; collide”): it shares the title's “collide.”
- [撃](撃.md) (“shoot; strike”), [打](打.md) (“strike”), and [当](当.md) (“hit; be on target”): JMdict's glosses of 衝く include “to hit” and “to attack,” JMdict glosses 衝撃 しょうげき as “impact; shock; impulse,” and Kanjipedia's first sense of 衝 includes つきあたる (run into).
- [刺](刺.md) (“pierce; prick”): KANJIDIC2 gives 衝 “pierce,” “stab,” and “prick.”
- [押](押.md) (“push”) and [推](推.md) (“infer; recommend; push forward”): JMdict's glosses of 衝く include “to push; to thrust.”
- [争](争.md) (“contend; dispute”) and [戦](戦.md) (“battle”): JMdict's second sense of 衝突 is “clash; skirmish; conflict; discord; quarrel; brush.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 走 (“run”), 止 (“stop, halt”), and 回 (“revolve; times”); none means to collide or to thrust.
