# 昼 — daytime, midday

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

昼 covers daytime and, in a narrower context, the middle of the day. [昼](../words/昼.md) can also mean lunch in conversation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the [日](日.md) near the bottom as daylight held between the 尺-shaped top and the bottom line. This is a modern visual aid, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チュウ | on | 白昼 はくちゅう (daytime; broad daylight) |
| ひる | kun | [昼](../words/昼.md) ひる (daytime; midday), 昼休み ひるやすみ (lunch break) |

## Common words

- [昼](../words/昼.md) ひる — daytime, midday; lunch
- 昼ご飯 ひるごはん — lunch
- 昼休み ひるやすみ — lunch break
- 昼間 ひるま — daytime

## Notes

昼 need not mean exactly 12:00. 正午 しょうご, with [午](午.md), is the precise word for noon.
For shape, distinguish the 尺-shaped top from [春](春.md) and the stacked horizontal strokes of [書](書.md). The daytime contrast is [夜](夜.md), "night."
