# 偉 — admirable; great

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

偉 expresses greatness or something deserving admiration. [偉い](../words/偉い.md) えらい can praise
a person's conduct; it can also describe someone important or high-ranking.
偉大 いだい gives another way to describe greatness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the person-shaped 亻 beside a tall award built from the stacked
lines and central box of 韋. The award is an invented memory picture, not an etymology.

## Common words

- [偉い](../words/偉い.md) えらい — admirable; important
- 偉大 いだい — great; outstanding, as in 偉大な人 いだいなひと, "a great person"

## Notes

Admiration and rank are related uses, not identical judgments: a high-ranking person is not necessarily kind.
[優](優.md) overlaps in excellence but also supplies the "gentle" meaning of 優しい やさしい.
For visual practice, compare the right sides of [使](使.md), [借](借.md), and [体](体.md); 亻 alone does not identify 偉.
