# 唄 — song; ballad

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

唄 appears in names for some traditional Japanese songs, including shamisen-accompanied forms such as 小唄 and 長唄. 歌 is the general word for a song.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| うた | kun | [子守唄](../words/子守唄.md) こもりうた — lullaby |
| バイ | on | 梵唄 ぼんばい — specialized Buddhist chanting |
| うた.う | kun | 唄う うたう — a listed spelling of “sing”; the usual spelling is 歌う |

## Common words

- 小唄 こうた, 長唄 ながうた — traditional song forms
