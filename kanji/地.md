# 地 — ground, land

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

地 concerns ground, land, or a place. It appears both in words for physical ground and in words for geographic locations.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** connect the [土](土.md), "earth," on the left with a patch of ground, and use the spreading right-hand 也 as its outline. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical analysis.

## Common words

- [土地](../words/土地.md) とち — land; a plot of land
- [地図](../words/地図.md) ちず — map
- 地下 ちか — underground
- 地面 じめん — ground surface
- 地震 じしん — earthquake

## Notes

Do not choose チ or ジ from meaning alone: learn each whole word's reading.

土 can refer to soil itself; 地 often concerns the ground or a geographic place. In [社](社.md), 土 is on the right; in 地, it is on the left.
