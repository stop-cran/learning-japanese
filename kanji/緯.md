# 緯 — latitude; weft; woof

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

緯 is the crosswise direction: the threads that run across a loom, and, on a map, the east-west lines that give latitude. [緯度](../words/緯度.md) いど is latitude; JMdict glosses it "latitude" and refers to 経度, the corresponding east-west coordinate in the other direction. The word article teaches "latitude."

The Daijisen character entry gives 3 senses for イ. One example of each:

- the crosswise thread of a woven fabric: 経緯 けいい, JMdict "details; particulars; sequence of events; circumstances; situation; how things got this way," "longitude and latitude" and "warp and weft; warp and woof";
- the left-right or east-west direction: 緯度 いど, "latitude";
- a book of omens and prophecy set against the Confucian classics: 緯書 いしょ, "book of omens appended to Confucian Chinese classics."

Daijisen lists the kun reading よこいと and gives 経緯 as a hard-to-read word read いきさつ. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 糸, "thread," at the left and the shape 韋 at the right, which also appears in [偉](偉.md), "admirable; great," [違](違.md), "differ," and [衛](衛.md), "guard; defense." Imagine one thread running crosswise through the fabric. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 or 韋 contributes meaning or sound, although 偉 and 違 are also read イ. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| イ | on | [緯度](../words/緯度.md) いど — latitude |
| よこいと | kun | 緯 よこいと - "weft; woof (crosswise threads on a loom)"; JMdict heads this entry 横糸 and lists 緯糸, 緯 and よこ糸 as other spellings; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ぬき | kun | 緯 ぬき - the same JMdict entry reads ぬき only with the spelling 緯 (and ぬきいと only with 緯糸); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 緯度, 緯 supplies イ and 度 supplies ド. In 北緯 ほくい, "north latitude," 北 supplies ホク and 緯 supplies イ; 緯線 いせん is "parallel; latitude line." In 経緯 けいい, 経 supplies ケイ and 緯 supplies イ. JMdict also has 経緯 as たてぬき, "warp and weft; warp and woof," and as いきさつ, "circumstances; sequence of events; how things got this way; details; particulars," marked "word usually written using kana alone" and as a gikun (meaning as reading) or jukujikun (special kanji reading); these are whole-word readings and are not split among the two kanji. The weft entry 緯 よこいと also lists い as a reading restricted to the spelling 緯.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows イ, with 緯度, 北緯 and 経緯, and no kun reading. Practice selects イ, the reading of the linked word article 緯度. よこいと and ぬき are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with the weft entry; being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [度](度.md), "degree, number of times," in 緯度, supplying ド. In the other words named above, [北](北.md), "north," supplies ホク in 北緯 and [経](経.md), "pass through; sutra," supplies ケイ in 経緯; KANJIDIC2 also lists たていと, "warp thread," among the kun readings of 経.
- [横](横.md), "horizontal," overlaps the crosswise direction of 緯. [経](経.md), "pass through; sutra," is the warp partner of 緯 in 経緯 and in KANJIDIC2 reads たていと. [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," and [織](織.md), "weave; fabric; organization," overlap the weft thread on a loom. [線](線.md), "line; track," overlaps 緯線 "parallel; latitude line." [度](度.md), "degree, number of times," is the other kanji of 緯度 and [東](東.md), [西](西.md), [南](南.md), [北](北.md) and [方](方.md) are compass words, but none of them means latitude, so they are not excluded. All the others are kept out of this card's quiz options. [縦](縦.md), "vertical; lengthwise; at will," is the opposite direction and a separate card.
- [衛](衛.md), "guard; defense," looks plausible because it has the same 韋 shape inside it, and is read エイ and エ. [緑](緑.md), "green," looks plausible because it also has 糸 at the left, and is read リョク, ロク and みどり. [山](山.md), "mountain," is a common beginner kanji read サン, セン and やま. None shares a reading or a meaning with 緯. Many other kanji read イ, such as [以](以.md), [意](意.md) and [医](医.md), and are never offered as wrong answers.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the イ senses: 黄緯 こうい, "celestial latitude; ecliptic latitude." Daijisen also lists 讖緯 しんい as a word for books of prophecy; JMdict has no entry for it. The Daijisen headword ぬき gives the weft thread and anything running crosswise.
