# 寄 — draw near; stop by

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寄 connects approaching, bringing things near, and stopping in on a visit.
[寄る](../words/寄る.md) (よる) describes the subject moving closer or dropping by; 寄せる (よせる) can describe bringing something closer.
寄付 (きふ), "donation," illustrates a contribution use rather than physical nearness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the large shape under the roof leaning toward the small 口 below to ask about stopping by.
This is a visual story, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

The modern outline has 宀 over 奇, including a lower 可 shape also visible in [何](何.md).
Sharing that shape does not make 何, "what," a meaning of 寄.
Contrast [字](字.md) and [安](安.md) by what is below the roof.
寄る and 寄せる differ in grammar: moving closer yourself is not the same as moving an object closer.
