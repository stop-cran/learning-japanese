# 鋭 — sharp; keen

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鋭 concerns sharpness, a pointed or piercing quality and keen perception. 鋭い is an i-adjective; 鋭利 is a different noun/na-adjective for sharpness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a sharply pointed metal object beside the right-hand 兌-like shape. This is a mnemonic using modern shapes, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical radical 金 appears in its narrow left-hand form.

## Notes

- 鋭い attaches directly before a noun; 鋭利 takes な in attributive adjectival use. Do not transfer one complete word's inflection to the other.
- [鈍](鈍.md), dull or blunt, supplies a useful contrast in edge or perception contexts. It is not an interchangeable answer meaning.
- 鋭意 is an established adverb meaning eagerly or earnestly. It is not a claim that the adverb describes a physical cutting edge.
