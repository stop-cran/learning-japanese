# 消 — extinguish; erase

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

消 links erasing, extinguishing, and disappearing. [消す](../words/消す.md) けす describes
making something disappear or stop, such as erasing writing or turning off a light.
消える (きえる) describes the disappearance or light going out.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine water erasing the three small marks above the 月-shaped
block on the right. This is a visual story, not the character's derivation.

## Notes

電気を消しました。 (でんきをけしました。) — I turned off the light.
Compare 電気が消えました (でんきがきえました), "The light went out."
The upper right has small separated strokes, unlike the more continuous upper part of [青](青.md).
