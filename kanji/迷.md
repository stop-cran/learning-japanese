# 迷 — get lost; be unsure

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

迷 is losing one's way or not knowing which way to choose.
[迷う](../words/迷う.md) まよう can describe getting lost on a road or hesitating over a decision.
The shared idea helps memory, but these are distinct uses of the verb.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** treat the spreading strokes of 米 as several possible turns beside a path.
This is a visual association with the modern character, not its historical origin.

## Notes

Learn 迷子 as the whole word まいご; do not turn that spelling into a general まい reading rule for 迷.
道に迷う (みちにまよう) means "lose one's way."
The radical 辵 appears as 辶. [米](米.md) alone has no enclosing sweep;
[近](近.md) and [送](送.md) have different inner parts.
