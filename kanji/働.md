# 働 — work; function

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

働 expresses working, including doing a job and functioning effectively.
[労働](../words/労働.md) ろうどう names labor; 働く はたらく is the verb "to work."
働き はたらき can describe work or the function something performs.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** put a person-shaped 亻 beside [動](動.md), "move," and imagine a worker
in action. This helps distinguish the written forms; it is not a historical derivation.

## Notes

The extra 亻 distinguishes 働 from [動](動.md): working is not simply any physical movement.
[重](重.md) supplies much of the shared outline; [側](側.md) has a person radical but a different right half.
For a sentence about a job, 店で働く (みせではたらく) means "work at a shop."
