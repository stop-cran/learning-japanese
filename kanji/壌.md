# 壌 — soil; earth for growing plants

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

壌 denotes earth or soil. In 土壌 (どじょう) it can name what a farmer finds beneath the crops.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Distinguish the 土 on the left from the 女 in [嬢](嬢.md).

## Notes

Compare [土](土.md): it can also mean soil, but 土 by itself has the everyday reading つち.

[嬢](嬢.md) starts with 女 and [譲](譲.md) with 言; 壌 starts with 土. [壊](壊.md) also has 土 on the left, but means to break or destroy. Compare [地](地.md) (ground or land): 土壌 points specifically to soil in the farming example.
