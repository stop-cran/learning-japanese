# 議 — deliberate; debate

**Strokes:** 20 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

議 is about discussing a matter and considering positions on it.
[議論](../words/議論.md) ぎろん names discussion or argument, while 会議 かいぎ is a meeting.
The character need not imply an angry quarrel.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** let the left 言 suggest people stating their views beside the elaborate 義 shape.
Use this only to remember the modern form, not as a historical explanation.

## Notes

会議 names a meeting, whereas 議会 names a legislative body; reversing the characters changes the word.
Compare the right side with [語](語.md) and [試](試.md), which share the left 言.
[警](警.md) puts 言 underneath rather than beside its other parts.
