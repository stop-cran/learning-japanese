# 死 — death

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 歹 (no. 78) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

死 means death or dying. It names the end of life, not simply illness; compare [病](病.md).

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** spot 歹 on the left and the curved 匕-shaped part on the right. Imagine two figures becoming still under the top line. This is a modern memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

[生](生.md) supplies the contrasting idea of life. [切](切.md) has a similar small bent shape, but its right-hand part is 刀, not the curved part of 死.
死ぬ is direct wording. When speaking sensitively about a person's death, 亡くなる (なくなる, "pass away") is often more appropriate.
