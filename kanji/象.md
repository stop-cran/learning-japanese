# 象 — elephant; image; shape

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 豕 (no. 152) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

象 names an elephant but also concerns an image, form or appearance. The ショウ compounds below are not about literal elephants.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the long sweeping lower strokes as an elephant's trunk and legs below its compact upper body. This modern-shape picture is not a historical derivation.

KANJIDIC2 indexes 象 under 豕, no. 152. The complete character looks different from that standalone radical; do not replace the canonical key with the animal word 象 itself.

## Notes

- 象 ぞう, elephant, and 象 しょう, form or appearance, have separate dictionary entries. The linked word article 象 teaches the animal reading.
- 象る is a godan verb with transitive and intransitive uses; JMdict marks it usually written in kana as かたどる. The same family also lists 模る and 形取る, but that does not make every word containing those kanji interchangeable.
