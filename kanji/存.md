# 存 — exist; be aware

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 子 (no. 39) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

存 is useful for talking about existence and keeping something in existence. [存在](../words/存在.md) (そんざい) means "existence; presence"; 保存 (ほぞん) means "preservation" or saving data.
The source also records awareness and belief senses, but the words here concentrate on existence and preservation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the small 子 shape still present beneath the upper strokes after everything around it has changed.
This is a modern memory aid, not a derivation.

## Notes

[字](字.md) has a clear 宀 roof over 子; 存 does not. [右](右.md) and [左](左.md) share part of the upper outline but contain different lower shapes.
In a computer context, 保存する (ほぞんする) means to save data, not to rescue a person.
