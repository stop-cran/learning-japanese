# 比 — compare; ratio

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 比 (no. 81) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

比 concerns comparison or a ratio. 比べる くらべる means compare, 比較 ひかく is comparison, and the noun 比 ひ is a ratio or proportion.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the two side-by-side forms and imagine placing two things next to one another to compare them. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified historical account.

## Notes

- 比べる is ichidan: 比べて くらべて, 比べない くらべない. The linked article gives useful comparison patterns rather than treating comparison as an automatic statement of equality.
- Keep the independent ratio noun 比 ひ separate from the complete native verb 比べる くらべる.
- Compare [北](北.md), north. The orientation of the side strokes distinguishes the written forms; the visual similarity does not establish a shared meaning.
