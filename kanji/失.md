# 失 — lose; loss

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

失 connects losing something with things going wrong. 失う (うしなう) means "lose," while [失敗](../words/失敗.md) (しっぱい) describes a failure or mistake.
The object of 失う can be something less tangible than a misplaced possession, such as an opportunity.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the short upper slant above the spreading lower strokes; imagine a small item slipping away from the figure.
This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シツ | on | [失敗](../words/失敗.md) (しっぱい) — failure; 失礼 (しつれい) — discourtesy |
| うしな.う | kun | 失う (うしなう) — lose |

The シツ + ハイ combination in 失敗 becomes しっぱい; do not pronounce it しつはい.

## Common words

- [失敗](../words/失敗.md) (しっぱい) — failure; mistake
- 失う (うしなう) — lose
- 失礼 (しつれい) — discourtesy; also "excuse me" in expressions of apology or departure

## Notes

[夫](夫.md) lacks the short slant at the top. [牛](牛.md) has a related upper outline but a straight central vertical below, rather than 失's broad lower sweeps.
The kana after 失 in 失う belong to the verb: its polite form is 失います (うしないます).
