# 段 — step; level

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 殳 (no. 79) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

段 can mark a step or level, including stages in a process.
階段 (かいだん) is a staircase; 段階 (だんかい) is a stage or level.
[値段](../words/値段.md) (ねだん), "price," is another useful word, whose meaning should be learned as a unit.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the short horizontal tiers on the left as steps beside a support on the right.
This is a present-day shape cue, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

Switching the order of the same two characters changes 階段 into 段階: physical stairs versus a stage or level.
The indexing radical 殳 is on the right, also seen in [殺](殺.md).
[服](服.md) has bent strokes too, but its left side is the distinct 月 shape.
