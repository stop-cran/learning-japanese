# 件 — case; item

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

件 treats a matter as an identifiable case or item.
[事件](../words/事件.md) じけん can refer to an incident or case, while 一件 いっけん means one matter or case.
This is not a general counter for every physical object.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the left-hand person checking one item beside the [牛](牛.md)-shaped right side.
The story links a visible shape to the meaning; it is not historical analysis.

## Notes

Remember the doubled consonant in 一件: いっけん, not いちけん.
[牛](牛.md) alone has no left-side person; [体](体.md) and [住](住.md) have different right-hand shapes.
The indexing radical 人 appears as 亻.
The kun reading くだん is retained in metadata, but the words selected here use ケン.
