# 沙 — sand; sift

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

沙 means sand. It can also mean sandy ground at the water's edge, and sifting things by washing them in water, as in 沙汰 さた. KANJIDIC2 lists “sand”. [無沙汰](../words/無沙汰.md) ぶさた and [御無沙汰](../words/御無沙汰.md) ごぶさた mean a long silence: not writing or contacting someone for a while.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 氵, a variant of [水](水.md), "water," stands on the left. On the right is [少](少.md), "few, a little," made of [小](小.md), "small, little," and a final 丿 stroke. Picture water washing over the ground until only a few small grains are left: sand. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or 少 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists サ only, with 沙汰. Kanjipedia lists シャ, すな, みぎわ and よなげる outside the table (外); みぎわ (the water's edge) is neither a KANJIDIC2 nor a Digital Daijisen reading, so it has no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 writes this reading よなげる, without a dot, but writes よな.げる for 汰; each card copies its own form.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 沙. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings いさ and す; these are name uses, not quiz readings.
- JMdict lists 沙漠 as a rarely used spelling of 砂漠 さばく, “desert”, and Digital Daijisen gives 沙漠 as an example of 沙 meaning sand. Sand is a material, while a desert is a region; compare the [漠](漠.md) card (“desert; vague”).

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [砂](砂.md) (“sand”): JMdict lists 沙 as a spelling of 砂 すな, “sand; grit; fine gravel”.
- [岸](岸.md) (“shore; riverbank”): the sense of sandy ground at the water's edge.
- [粒](粒.md) (“grain; small particle”) and [石](石.md) (“stone”): Digital Daijisen defines sand as very fine grains of stone.
- [汰](汰.md) (“sift; weed out”): both characters can mean sifting by washing in water (よなげる), and both titles contain “sift”.
- [択](択.md) (“select; opt for”) and [選](選.md) (“choose”): sifting sorts the suitable from the unsuitable.
- [洗](洗.md) (“wash”) and [濯](濯.md) (“wash; rinse”): the sifting is done by washing in water.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 鉄 (“iron”), 銅 (“copper”), and 塩 (“salt”); iron, copper and salt are other materials, and none means sand or sifting.
