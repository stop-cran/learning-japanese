# 沙 — sand; sift

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

沙 means sand, and also sifting things by washing them in water. KANJIDIC2 lists “sand”. Kanjipedia's first sense is すな。いさご。 (sand), its second is みぎわ。水辺の砂地。 (the water's edge; sandy ground by the water), and its third is よなげる。水中で洗ってよりわける。 (to wash in water and sort out), with 沙汰 as its example. Under サ, Digital Daijisen likewise gives すな and 水で洗って適否をより分ける (washing in water to sort the suitable from the unsuitable). [無沙汰](../words/無沙汰.md) ぶさた is glossed in JMdict as “not writing or contacting for a while; neglecting to write (call, visit, etc.); failing to write (call, visit, etc.); long silence” (entry 1672130), and [御無沙汰](../words/御無沙汰.md) ごぶさた as “not writing or contacting for a while; neglecting (failing) to write (call, visit, etc.); long silence” (entry 1270650).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 氵, a variant of [水](水.md), "water," stands on the left. On the right is [少](少.md), "few, a little," made of [小](小.md), "small, little," and a final 丿 stroke. Picture water washing over the ground until only a few small grains are left: sand. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or 少 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists サ only, with 沙汰 さた (JMdict entry 1291480). Kanjipedia lists シャ, すな, みぎわ and よなげる outside the table (外); みぎわ is neither a KANJIDIC2 nor a Digital Daijisen reading, so it has no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 writes this reading よなげる, without a dot, but writes よな.げる for 汰; each card copies its own form.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 沙. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings いさ and す; these are name uses, not quiz readings.

These ten cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [砂](砂.md) (“sand”): KANJIDIC2 lists “sand” for 沙, Digital Daijisen's first sense under サ is すな, JMdict entry 1291500 lists 沙 as a kanji form of すな, “sand; grit; fine gravel”, and the 砂 card lists すな.
- [漠](漠.md) (“desert; vague”): JMdict entry 1593800, 砂漠 さばく “desert”, lists 沙漠 as a rarely used kanji form, Digital Daijisen gives 沙漠 as its example of 沙 meaning sand, and KANJIDIC2 gives 漠 “desert”.
- [岸](岸.md) (“shore; riverbank”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 沙 is みぎわ。水辺の砂地。 (the water's edge; sandy ground by the water).
- [粒](粒.md) (“grain; small particle”) and [石](石.md) (“stone”): Digital Daijisen defines すな【砂／▽沙】 (▽ marks a reading outside the official table) as 非常に細かい石の粒 (very fine grains of stone) and いさご as 石のごく細かいもの (very fine pieces of stone).
- [汰](汰.md) (“sift; weed out”): Kanjipedia gives 汰 ① よなげる。よりわける。 and 沙 ③ よなげる。水中で洗ってよりわける。「沙汰」, so both characters are glossed よなげる, and both titles contain “sift”.
- [択](択.md) (“select; opt for”) and [選](選.md) (“choose”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 沙 is よなげる。水中で洗ってよりわける。 (washing in water and sorting out), and Digital Daijisen's second sense under サ is 水で洗って適否をより分ける (sorting the suitable from the unsuitable).
- [洗](洗.md) (“wash”) and [濯](濯.md) (“wash; rinse”): the same Kanjipedia and Digital Daijisen senses begin with washing, 水中で洗って and 水で洗って, and the 洗 card lists あら.う.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 鉄 (“iron”), 銅 (“copper”), and 塩 (“salt”); iron, copper and salt are other materials, and none means sand or sifting.
