# 提 — submit; propose; carry in hand

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

提 is holding something up in the hand and, by extension, holding it out to others: handing in a document or putting forward an idea. [提出](../words/提出.md) ていしゅつ is submission or handing in; JMdict glosses it "presentation (of documents); submission (of an application, report, etc.); production (e.g. of evidence)." [提案](../words/提案.md) ていあん is a proposal or suggestion; JMdict glosses it "proposal; proposition; suggestion."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for テイ. One example of each:

- carrying something hanging from the hand: 提携 ていけい, "cooperation; tie-up; joint business; partnership; alliance";
- holding out or bringing forward: 提供 ていきょう, "offer; tender; providing; supplying; making available";
- going in front and leading others: 提督 ていとく, "admiral; commodore."

For ダイ, Daijisen says the character is used to write Sanskrit sounds, as in 菩提 ぼだい, "bodhi; enlightenment," which JMdict marks as a Buddhist term. It gives the kun reading さげる and names 提灯 (ちょうちん) and 提子 (ひさげ) among words with a hard-to-guess reading; JMdict glosses ひさげ "ceremonial sake decanter with a spout and semicircular handle," usually written in kana. Daijisen's separate entry ひさげ describes the same small pot with a handle and spout; ひさげ is not a KANJIDIC2 reading. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the left, written 扌, and at the right a shape with [日](日.md), "sun, day," at the top over a lower part. Imagine a hand holding a finished report up into the daylight to hand it in. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 扌 or 日 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| テイ | on | [提出](../words/提出.md) ていしゅつ — submission; handing in; [提案](../words/提案.md) ていあん — proposal; suggestion |
| チョウ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2 and, as outside the official table, by Kanjipedia; it is the reading of 提 in 提灯 ちょうちん, "paper lantern; Chinese lantern; Japanese lantern," which Daijisen writes ▽提▽灯 (▽ marks a reading outside the official table) and labels 唐音 とうおん, JMdict "tō-on; Tang reading; on reading of a kanji based on Song dynasty and later Chinese"; JMdict also has compounds such as 提灯行列 and 提灯持ち; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ダイ | on | 菩提 ぼだい - bodhi; enlightenment, a Buddhist term; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| さ.げる | kun | 提げる さげる - to take along; to hold in the hand; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 提出, 提 supplies テイ and 出 supplies シュツ; in 提案, 提 supplies テイ and 案 supplies アン, with no sound change in either word. JMdict also lists 堤出 as a search-only kanji form of 提出. In 提げる, 提 writes さ and げる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict's second sense is "to hang (e.g. from the shoulder or waist)." In 手提げ てさげ, "handbag; purse," 手 supplies て and 提 writes さ with げ after it; JMdict also lists 手さげ, 手提 and 手堤, a search-only kanji form. In 菩提, 菩 supplies ボ and 提 supplies ダイ; 菩 has no card yet. JMdict marks the spelling 提燈 of 提灯 as a word containing out-dated kanji and 挑灯 as a rarely used kanji form; a separate JMdict entry 提灯 ていとう, marked as a rare term, has the same lantern gloss, and there 提 supplies テイ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows テイ, with 提供, 提案 and 前提 ぜんてい, "condition; assumption; prerequisite; hypothesis," as examples; and さげる, with 提げる and 手提げ and the spelling contrast ⇔ 下げる さげる, "to hang; to suspend; to wear (e.g. decoration)," also "to lower; to reduce; to bring down." チョウ and ダイ are not in the table. Practice selects テイ, the reading of the linked word articles. No word article uses ダイ or さ.げる yet, and no word article has 提灯 yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [出](出.md), "go out, exit," in 提出 and [案](案.md), "proposal; draft plan," in 提案.
- [揚](揚.md), "hoist; lift; deep-fry," also has 扌 at the left and 日 at the top of its right part. [探](探.md), "search," and [招](招.md), "invite," also have 扌 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [案](案.md), "proposal; draft plan," [供](供.md), "accompany, offer," [示](示.md), "indicate," [持](持.md), "hold, have," and [運](運.md), "carry; luck," overlap the senses of 提 and its words: proposing in 提案 and 提言, offering in 提供, showing in 提示, and holding or carrying in the hand in 提げる. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "propose" or "carry" as exclusive to any one character.
- Submitting a document, as in 提出, is 提. Submitting to someone, giving in, is a different idea, expressed by 屈する くっする, "to yield; to give in." English "submit" covers both ideas, so [屈](屈.md), "bend; yield; submit," remains an ordinary wrong option for 提.
- [挙](挙.md), "raise; cite; hold (an event)," and [推](推.md), "infer; recommend; push forward," share proposing and recommending: JMdict glosses one sense of 挙げる "to nominate (e.g. for a position); to recommend; to propose (something)", and 推す "to recommend; to endorse (e.g. a candidate); to nominate." The three are kept out of one another's quiz options.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the テイ senses: 提琴 ていきん, "tiqin (2 or 4-stringed Chinese instrument played with a bow)," also "violin," for carrying in the hand; and 提起 ていき, "raising (a question); posing (a problem); bringing up (an issue); presenting," 提言 ていげん, "proposal; motion; recommendation," 提示 ていじ, "presentation (of a passport, ID, etc.); showing; production (e.g. of documents)," 提唱 ていしょう, "advocacy; proposal," and 前提, for holding out or bringing forward.
