# 驚 — be surprised; astonish

**Strokes:** 22 · **Key (radical):** 馬 (no. 187) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

驚 is surprise: being astonished or startled, and surprising someone else. KANJIDIC2 lists “wonder”, “be surprised”, “frightened” and “amazed”. Kanjipedia's first sense is おどろく。おどろかす。 (be surprised; surprise), and Digital Daijisen gives おどろく。 and おどろかす。 as its two senses. The verb [驚く](../words/驚く.md) おどろく is glossed in JMdict as “to be surprised; to be taken aback; to be amazed; to be astonished; to be shocked” and “to be frightened; to be startled; to be alarmed” (entry 1238680). The transitive verb [驚かす](../words/驚かす.md) おどろかす is “to surprise; to frighten; to create a stir” (entry 1238650).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [敬](敬.md), "respect; honor," sits on top of [馬](馬.md), "horse," and the four dots at the bottom of [馬](馬.md) are 灬, marked as a variant of [火](火.md), "fire." Imagine bowing respectfully to a horse, and the horse jumping back in astonishment, all four feet in the air. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 敬 or 馬 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- In 驚く, 驚 writes おどろ and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 驚かす ends in かす instead. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so おどろ.く supplies the one question for that stem, shown as おどろく; おどろ.かす is retained from KANJIDIC2.
- Joyo lists キョウ, おどろく and おどろかす. Kanjipedia lists no readings outside the table.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 驚; Digital Daijisen gives the name reading とし.

These two cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [怖](怖.md) (“frightening”): KANJIDIC2 lists “frightened” for 驚, the second sense of 驚く is “to be frightened; to be startled; to be alarmed”, and 驚かす includes “to frighten”; JMdict glosses 怖い こわい (1593480) as “scary; frightening; eerie; dreadful”.
- [恐](恐.md) (“fear; awe”): the second sense of 驚く is “to be frightened; to be startled; to be alarmed”, and JMdict glosses 恐れる おそれる (1589200), which it also spells 怖れる, as “to fear; to be afraid of”; the 恐 card lists おそ.れる.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 悲 (“sadness”), 怒 (“anger”), and 喜 (“rejoice”); they name other emotions, and none means surprise.
