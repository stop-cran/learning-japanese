# 恨 — resent; grudge

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

恨 is bitterness that stays: resenting someone for a wrong, or regretting what cannot be undone. [恨む](../words/恨む.md) うらむ is to resent; JMdict glosses it "to bear a grudge against; to resent; to blame; to curse; to feel bitter towards." [恨み](../words/恨み.md) うらみ is a grudge; JMdict glosses it "resentment; grudge; malice; bitterness" and, in a second sense noted as especially written 憾み, "matter for regret; regret."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense, resenting or resentment, with examples such as 遺恨 いこん, "grudge; ill will; enmity," 悔恨 かいこん, "regret; remorse; repentance; contrition," and 痛恨 つうこん, "deep regret; great sorrow; bitter grief." It gives the on reading コン and the kun readings うらむ and うらめしい, the same as KANJIDIC2. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 忄, the narrow form of 心, "heart, mind," at the left, and at the right the same shape as at the right of [限](限.md), "limit," and [根](根.md), "root; foundation." Imagine a heart that has reached its limit and grown a deep root of resentment. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 忄 or the right side contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 恨む, 恨 writes うら and む is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 恨み is the noun form of 恨む: 恨 still writes うら, followed by み. In 恨めしい, 恨 writes うら and めしい is okurigana. In 痛恨, 痛 supplies ツウ and 恨 supplies コン. JMdict also has a separate entry うらむ, spelled 憾む or 恨む, "to regret," which is usually written in kana.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows コン, with 遺恨, 痛恨 and 悔恨 as examples; うらむ, with 恨む and 恨み; and うらめしい, with 恨めしい. Practice selects うら.む, the reading with linked word articles. コン and うら.めしい are illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 恨む is 恨 with okurigana only, and 恨み is 恨 followed by kana only. JMdict also accepts 怨む and 怨み as spellings of the same words.
- [限](限.md), "limit," [銀](銀.md), "silver," and [根](根.md), "root; foundation," have the same right side as 恨. [快](快.md), "pleasant; agreeable," also has 忄 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [憎](憎.md), "hate; detest," [苦](苦.md), "bitter; suffering," [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible," and [怒](怒.md), "anger," overlap the senses "hateful," "bitter," "blame" and "resentment" that 恨 and its words carry: 恨む includes "to blame" and "to feel bitter towards," and 恨めしい includes "bitter" and "hateful." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "resent" or "grudge" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples: 恨事 こんじ, "regrettable matter," and 怨恨 えんこん, "enmity; grudge."
