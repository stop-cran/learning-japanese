# 直 — direct; repair

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

直 links directness with putting things right. [直す](../words/直す.md) なおす means to repair or correct;
直接 ちょくせつ means direct, and 正直 しょうじき describes honesty.
These related ideas do not give every word the same translation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** check that the inner 目-shaped section sits above the long bottom stroke,
as though you are putting a crooked arrangement right. This is a visual reminder, not an origin claim.

## Notes

時計を直しました。 (とけいをなおしました。) — I repaired the clock.
時計が直りました。 (とけいがなおりました。) — The clock is fixed.
The first sentence names an action on the clock; the second reports its change.

[目](目.md), [百](百.md), and [耳](耳.md) provide rectangular line-pattern contrasts.
Look at the top crossing stroke and the bottom edge instead of treating all the enclosed horizontal lines alike.
