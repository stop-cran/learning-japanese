# 確 — certain

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

確 connects firmness with certainty: something can be dependable, or a fact can be established.
[確か](../words/確か.md) たしか describes what is certain or reliable; 確かめる たしかめる is the action of making sure.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** let the stone-shaped 石 on the left anchor a fact firmly in place.
This is a memory aid for the present shape, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

確かめる takes the thing checked with を: 時間を確かめる (じかんをたしかめる), "check the time."
Do not infer certainty from every use of 確か: sentence-initial 確か can mean "if I remember correctly."

[研](研.md) shares 石; [集](集.md) has a related-looking cluster of short horizontal strokes;
[短](短.md) is only a looser box-and-crossbar comparison. None means "certain."
