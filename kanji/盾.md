# 盾 — shield; buckler

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

盾 is a shield, the board-like weapon held up to block attacks. JMdict gives 盾 たて as "shield; buckler; escutcheon" and also "pretext; excuse; grounds (for); justification," a shield used figuratively. It is familiar in [矛盾](../words/矛盾.md) むじゅん, "contradiction; inconsistency," where 盾 is paired with 矛, the spear.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ジュン: a weapon that hides the body from an enemy's attack, the shield, as in 矛盾. Its kun reading is たて. The Daijisen word entry たて gives three senses: a board-like weapon against arrows, stones and swords; a means of protecting oneself or one's position; and a commemorative award plaque.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [目](目.md), "eye," as the radical part and a [十](十.md), "ten," cross above it. Imagine holding a shield up in front of your eyes. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 目 or 十 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 矛盾, 矛 supplies ム and 盾 supplies ジュン, giving むじゅん. 盾 alone is read たて. In 後ろ盾 うしろだて, "backing; support; backer; supporter; patron; sponsor" and "shield that protects one's back," 後ろ is read うしろ and 盾 is read だて, its たて with a voiced sound. JMdict lists 楯 as another written form of 盾, and 矛盾 has a "rarely used kanji form" 矛楯. 盾突く たてつく, "to defy; to disobey; to rebel against; to oppose; to resist," also begins with たて. These have no word articles yet.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジュン, with 矛盾, and たて, with 盾 and 後ろ盾. Practice selects ジュン, the reading of the linked word article. たて is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [矛](矛.md), "spear; polearm," in 矛盾. 矛 supplies ム; 盾 supplies ジュン. The 矛盾 article presents the spear-and-shield explanation of the word as an attributed story, not a report of an actual test; this card claims nothing more about its origin.
- [目](目.md), "eye," is the radical of 盾, and [直](直.md), "direct; repair," and [真](真.md), "true, genuine," also contain the 目 shape, so they look plausible. They are read モク, ボク and め; チョク, ジキ and ただ.ちに; and シン and ま, and mean eye, direct and true. None shares a reading or a meaning with 盾.
- [防](防.md), "defend; ward off," [守](守.md), "guard; obey," and [衛](衛.md), "guard; defense," overlap the protective sense of a shield and the Daijisen sense "means of protection." They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- Other kanji read ジュン, such as [順](順.md), "order; sequence," [準](準.md), "standard, conform," and [純](純.md), "pure; genuine," are never offered as wrong answers.
