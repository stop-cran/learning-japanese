# 促 — urge; prompt

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

促 concerns urging, prompting or hastening something. [催促](../words/催促.md) さいそく is pressing someone to act. Related dictionary words include 促進 そくしん, "promotion; acceleration; encouragement; facilitation; spurring on," 督促 とくそく, "urge; demand; importunity," and the verb 促す うながす, "to urge; to encourage; to press; to prompt." The Daijisen character entry also gives a second sense of being cramped or shortened, seen in 促音 そくおん, the doubled consonant sound written with the small っ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), "person," at the left and [足](足.md), "foot, leg," at the right. Imagine someone at your heels urging you to move. 足 also reads ソク, which makes a handy sound cue, but this lesson makes no claim about the historical formation of 促.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソク | on | [催促](../words/催促.md) さいそく — pressing for action; urging |
| うなが.す | kun | 促す うながす - to urge; to encourage; to press; to prompt; not selected for questions here |

In 催促, 促 supplies ソク and 催 supplies サイ. In 促す, 促 writes うなが and す is okurigana. Practice selects ソク. うなが.す is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with 促す, but that word has no linked article yet, so the reading is not selected for questions. This is a teaching selection, not a claim that うなが.す is invalid, obsolete or rare.

## Notes

- [催](催.md), "host an event; sponsor," is the other kanji in 催促. Both characters carry a prompting sense there, but 催 mostly concerns events in other words.
- [足](足.md), "foot, leg," is the right-hand part and also reads ソク. [速](速.md), "fast," and [側](側.md), "side," also read ソク. Only 促 means to urge; 速 concerns speed, which relates to hurrying but is not the same as urging someone.
- [勧](勧.md), "recommend; encourage," and [励](励.md), "encourage; be diligent," also have encouraging meanings. 促す presses or prompts someone to act, 勧める recommends or offers, and 励ます cheers someone on. These uses can overlap, so do not treat "encourage" as exclusive to any one character.
- [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible," is kept out of this card's quiz options because the verb 責める can also mean "to urge; to press; to pester." The usual meaning of [責める](../words/責める.md) is to blame or reproach someone.
