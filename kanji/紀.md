# 紀 — chronicle

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

紀 is associated with a chronicle or account. Digital Daijisen also gives a standalone rules/code-of-conduct sense, ordered recording, recorded history, years/ages, a twelve-year period, and a geological-period use. The rules sense appears in words such as 綱紀 and 風紀; this is broader than JMdict's rare spelling 紀律 for 規律. In [世紀](../words/世紀.md) (せいき), the whole compound means a century or, more broadly, an era. The hundred-year unit belongs to 世紀, not to 紀 by itself.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice 糸 at the left and 己 at the right; picture a thread marking entries in a written account. This is a memory aid, not a claim about historical formation.

## Notes

- [史](史.md), history/chronicle; [記](記.md), written record; [誌](誌.md), written record/journal; and [録](録.md), record/register, have English labels that can overlap with "chronicle." They are excluded from meaning choices for that direct label overlap, not treated as interchangeable in every use.
- [歴](歴.md) overlaps with the history sense; [書](書.md) and [述](述.md) with written accounts; [籍](籍.md) with registers; [規](規.md), [律](律.md), [則](則.md), [典](典.md), [法](法.md), [制](制.md), and [憲](憲.md) with the rules/discipline sense; [整](整.md) with ordered recording; and [順](順.md) with order or sequence. [年](年.md), [期](期.md), [代](代.md), [世](世.md), and [齢](齢.md) overlap with the chronological or age senses. These targeted guards prevent plausible meaning-choice overlaps; they do not claim that the characters or their compounds are interchangeable.
- [著](著.md), write/remarkable, is also guarded for its writing sense, not its remarkable-quality sense. [歳](歳.md), years of age, is guarded conservatively against 紀's sourced years/age sense; this does not make 紀 an age counter or an alternative spelling for 歳.
- [筆](筆.md) and [文](文.md) overlap with writing or written composition. [字](字.md) is guarded for its broader dictionary sense "written text," not because a single written symbol is a chronicle. [準](準.md) is guarded at the standard/rule boundary. [時](時.md) and [刻](刻.md) have broad "time" labels, guarded conservatively against 紀's chronological senses; this does not make 紀 a word for an hour or a clock time.
- [改](改.md), revise/reform, and [巻](巻.md), roll/book volume, share a 己-like shape with 紀; [紙](紙.md), paper, shares the 糸 radical. Their meanings remain distinct.
- JMdict marks 紀律 as a rarely used kanji form of 規律. Learn 規律 as the ordinary spelling rather than inferring that the forms are equally common.
- KANJIDIC2's geological-period gloss and JMdict's suffix entry are specialized; they do not make 紀 a general counter for a century.
