# 楽 — music, enjoyment, ease

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

楽 connects vocabulary about music, enjoyment, and ease. The reading helps distinguish 音楽 おんがく, "music," 楽 らく, "easy; comfortable," and [楽しい](../words/楽しい.md) たのしい, "enjoyable."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the [白](白.md)-shaped center and surrounding short strokes as a lively musical instrument above a [木](木.md) stand. This is a modern memory aid, not a verified origin story.

## Notes

楽な仕事 らくなしごと is an easy job; 楽しい仕事 たのしいしごと is an enjoyable job. Ease and enjoyment need not coincide.
[学](学.md) also has the reading ガク, but has 子 below, not 木. [業](業.md) has no 白-shaped box. In 音楽, [音](音.md) contributes "sound."
