# 卸 — wholesale; grate

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 卩 (no. 26), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

卸 is selling goods in bulk to shops, which is wholesale; the verb written with it also covers grating food and cutting up fish. [卸す](../words/卸す.md) おろす means to sell wholesale, to grate, or to cut up a fish. JMdict glosses 卸す "to sell wholesale," "to grate (e.g. vegetables)" and "to cut up fish," and the noun 卸 おろし "wholesale."

The Digital Daijisen on Weblio has no character entry for 卸. The only Daijisen entry on that page is the word おろし, written 卸 or 卸し, which it describes as sharing its origin with 下ろし and defines as the act of a wholesaler selling goods to retail shops. JMdict lists 卸し among the spellings of 下ろし おろし, "dropping; unloading; removing," a word that also covers grated vegetables and a grater. The kun readings above therefore come from KANJIDIC2, JMdict and the official table.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the left part looks like [午](午.md), "noon," stacked on [止](止.md), "stop, halt," and the key 卩 stands at the right. Imagine a delivery truck that stops at noon in front of a shop, where the wholesale goods are handed over. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 午, 止 or 卩 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 卸す, 卸 writes おろ and す is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The noun 卸 is the kanji alone for the whole of おろし; the same noun can also be spelled 卸し, with し as okurigana, which is the form listed as おろ.し. In 棚卸し, 棚 supplies たな and 卸し keeps おろし. In 卸下, 卸 supplies シャ and 下 supplies カ, voiced to が.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows おろす, with 卸す as the example, marked as a pair to compare with 下ろす おろす and 降ろす おろす, which JMdict glosses "to take down; to bring down; to lower" and "to unload (goods, a truck, etc.)"; and おろし, with 卸 and 卸商 おろししょう, "wholesaler; wholesale dealer." シャ is not in the official table, and おろ.し has no separate row. Practice selects おろ.す, the reading of the linked word article 卸す; シャ, おろし and おろ.し are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 卸す is 卸 with okurigana only.
- [御](御.md), "honorific; govern," contains the whole of 卸 at the right, after 彳. [印](印.md), "stamp; mark," has the same 卩 at the right, and [午](午.md), "noon," looks like the top of 卸's left part. All are different kanji.
- [販](販.md), "sell; market," [売](売.md), "sell," and [商](商.md), "commerce," overlap the sense "wholesale," and [擦](擦.md), "scrape; scrub," and [削](削.md), "shave; sharpen; pare," come close to "grate," senses that 卸 and its word carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "wholesale" or "grate" as exclusive to any one character.
