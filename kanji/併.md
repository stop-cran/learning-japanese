# 併 — combine; join together

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

併 means to join or bring things together. In [併用](../words/併用.md), two or more things are used together. Digital Daijisen also records a second sense, used interchangeably with 並, of standing side by side, as in 併記 へいき “writing side by side” and 併存 へいぞん “coexistence”; JMdict also lists the spellings 並記 and 並存.

## Notes

JMdict marks 併せる as a rarely used kanji form; 合わせる is the common spelling of that verb. 合 also means to fit, 兼 can describe combining roles, and 結 has senses of tying and joining. 並 shares the side-by-side sense, and 共, 協, 接, 括 and 統 have related senses of togetherness, joining or unifying.
