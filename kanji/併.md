# 併 — combine; join together

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

併 means to join or bring things together. In [併用](../words/併用.md), two or more things are used together.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** use the person-shaped 亻 and the right-hand form as a visual cue for bringing things together. This does not claim a historical explanation for the parts.

## Notes

JMdict marks 併せる as a rarely used kanji form; the common spelling of that verb is 合わせる. The quiz uses ヘイ from the more useful support word 併用.

合 (“fit together”) is excluded from meaning choices because its displayed meaning overlaps with “combine; join together.”
