# 玄 — mysterious; profound; dark

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 玄 (no. 95) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

玄 is what is dark and deep, so deep that it is hard to see into: a dark brownish black, a profound principle, a far distance. The linked [玄関](../words/玄関.md) げんかん is JMdict's "entrance; front door; entryway; entranceway; entry hall; vestibule; porch; foyer; mud room." The word is learned as a whole; it is not a description of a dark room.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ゲン. One example of each:

- dark brownish black: 玄米 げんまい, "unpolished rice; unmilled rice; brown rice";
- deep and dark: 玄関, and also 玄妙 げんみょう, "abstruse; occult; mysterious," and 幽玄 ゆうげん, "mysterious profundity; quiet beauty; the subtle and profound; yūgen";
- a deep principle: 玄学, which JMdict has no entry for;
- far away: 玄孫 げんそん, "great-great-grandchild; fourth-generation descendant."

More examples are in the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** this is a small character with a cap stroke on top of a twisted loop below it. Imagine a dark hall with a lid on top and only a thin loop of light showing. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that any part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ゲン | on | [玄関](../words/玄関.md) げんかん — entrance; entryway |
| くろ | kun | 玄米 くろごめ — "unpolished rice; unmilled rice; brown rice"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| くろ.い | kun, source-only here | JMdict has no entry for 玄い; Daijisen and Kanjipedia list くろい as a reading of the character |

In 玄関, [関](関.md) supplies カン and 玄 supplies ゲン: げん + かん. The same ゲン is in 玄米 げんまい, 玄妙, 幽玄 and 玄孫 げんそん above.

JMdict has 玄米 1288150 with the spellings 黒米 and 玄米 and the readings くろごめ, くろまい and こくまい; くろまい and こくまい are restricted to 黒米, so くろごめ is the reading that goes with 玄米. In it 玄 writes くろ and [米](米.md) writes ごめ, the voiced form of こめ. 玄人 is read くろうと or くろと and is "expert; professional; master; connoisseur" in JMdict. Read the word as a whole; くろうと is not split among the two kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ゲン with 玄米, 玄関 and 幽玄, and the special reading 玄人 くろうと. It lists no kun reading. Practice selects ゲン, the reading of the linked word article. The kun readings are shown with a dictionary example or flagged as source-only; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- In 玄関, [関](関.md), "involve; barrier," supplies カン. Other words above use [米](米.md), "rice grain," in 玄米, and 妙, 幽 and 孫 in 玄妙, 幽玄 and 玄孫; of these only [妙](妙.md), "strange; exquisite," and [孫](孫.md), "grandchild; descendants," have cards.
- [率](率.md), "rate; lead; frank," also has 玄 as its key; [畜](畜.md), "livestock; domestic animals," has a similar-looking top; [玉](玉.md), "jewel; ball," is a similar small shape. They look alike at a glance but have different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [黒](黒.md), "black," overlaps the dark black sense and also reads くろ and くろ.い. [暗](暗.md), "dark," [濃](濃.md), "concentrated; dark," and [深](深.md), "deep," overlap the dark and deep senses. [奥](奥.md), "interior; depths," [妙](妙.md), [奇](奇.md), "strange; extraordinary," and [異](異.md), "different; unusual; strange," overlap the mysterious sense, and [遠](遠.md), "far," and [悠](悠.md), "leisurely; distant," overlap the far sense. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Many other kanji also read ゲン, such as 元, 原, 現, 言 and 限, and are never offered as wrong answers.
