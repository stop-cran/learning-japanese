# 呈 — present; offer; reveal

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

呈 means presenting or offering something, and showing something openly. Digital Daijisen gives two senses: offering something to someone, as in 呈上, [贈呈](../words/贈呈.md) and 進呈; and showing something without hiding it, or appearing, as in 呈示 and 露呈.

## Notes

The verb 呈する ていする covers both senses: JMdict glosses it as “to present; to offer,” “to show; to display; to exhibit” and “to assume (e.g. a shape)” (entry 2012430). Kanjipedia also gives the kun reading しめ.す, marked as outside the Joyo table (外); it is not in this card's metadata and is not quizzed.

献 (“offer; contribute; dedicate”) appears with 呈 in 献呈, and 贈 (“give as a gift; confer”) appears in 贈呈. 供 (“accompany, offer”), 与 (“give; grant”) and 提 (“submit; propose; carry in hand”) overlap with offering or submitting. 顕 (“evident; reveal”), 示 (“indicate”), 掲 (“put up; display; publish”) and 現 (“actual; present”) overlap with showing or appearing; 示 also appears in 呈示.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 短 (“short”), 行 (“go”), and 七 (“seven”); none means presenting or revealing.
