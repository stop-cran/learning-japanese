# 瞳 — pupil of the eye; eyes

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

瞳 is the pupil, the dark centre of the eye, and by extension the eyes themselves. [瞳](../words/瞳.md) ひとみ has two JMdict senses: "pupil (of the eye)" and "one's eyes." JMdict also lists 眸 as a "rarely used kanji form" and 睛 as a "search-only kanji form" of the same word.

The Daijisen ひとみ entry gives three senses: the iris, or iris and pupil together, the black of the eye; the pupil, 瞳孔; and, figuratively, the eye or its gaze. The Daijisen character entry gives ドウ, marked as a habitual reading (慣), for ひとみ and gives 瞳孔 and 瞳子 as examples. 瞳孔 どうこう is "pupil (of the eye)," marked [field: anatomy], and 瞳子 どうし is marked "dated term" for "pupil (of the eye)."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [目](目.md), "eye," at the left and [童](童.md), "child; juvenile," at the right. Imagine a tiny figure mirrored in the dark centre of an eye. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 目 or 童 contributes meaning or sound, although 童 is read ドウ, a reading 瞳 also has. Historical formation is not established here.

| トウ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2 and by Kanjipedia, but not in the Daijisen character entry; none of the JMdict words written with 瞳 that were checked reads it とう, so there is no ordinary example |
| ひとみ | kun | [瞳](../words/瞳.md) ひとみ — pupil of the eye; eyes |

瞳 read ひとみ stands alone as a whole word, with no okurigana. In 瞳孔, 瞳 supplies ドウ and [孔](孔.md) supplies コウ. In 瞳子, 瞳 supplies ドウ and 子 supplies シ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ドウ, with 瞳孔, and ひとみ, with 瞳. Practice selects ひとみ, the reading of the linked word article. ドウ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, and トウ is retained as a source-only reading. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The word 瞳 is this kanji alone, so it has no other kanji.
- [目](目.md), "eye," is the radical of 瞳 and the broader everyday eye word. [眺](眺.md), "gaze; view; look out over," overlaps the Daijisen sense "gaze." They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [真](真.md), "true, genuine," [着](着.md), "wear; arrive," and [相](相.md), "mutual; aspect," each contain a 目 shape, as 瞳 does, so they look plausible. They are read シン, ま and まこと; チャク, ジャク and き.る; and ソウ, ショウ and あい, and mean true, wear and mutual. None shares a reading or a meaning with 瞳.
- [童](童.md) and many other kanji read ドウ or トウ, like 瞳, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
- 瞳 and the B147 card 瞬 both involve the eye: 瞳 is the pupil and 瞬 is the blink. The pair is excluded on the 瞬 card, because 瞬 also means "to blink (one's eyes)" and 瞳 is titled "pupil of the eye; eyes."
