# 遅 — late; slow

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

遅 covers both lateness and slowness. [遅刻](../words/遅刻.md) ちこく is arriving late for an expected time;
遅い おそい can instead describe low speed.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 羊-like strokes sheltered under 尸 taking their time above 辶.
This is a modern visual reminder, not an account of the character's development.

## Notes

The opposite depends on the sense: [早い](../words/早い.md) はやい is early, while [速い](../words/速い.md) はやい is fast.
電車が遅れています (でんしゃがおくれています) means "the train is delayed."
[遊](遊.md) has 子 in its right-hand cluster, unlike 遅.
