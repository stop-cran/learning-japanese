# 飯 — cooked rice; meal

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 食 (no. 184) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

飯 means **cooked rice** and, more broadly, a **meal**. Those senses are both useful in everyday ご飯.
Rice as uncooked grains is usually called 米（こめ）.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the [食](食.md)-shaped left side as a food cue and imagine the right-hand
反 as a hand turning a rice bowl toward you. This is a mnemonic, not verified historical analysis.

## Common words

- [ご飯](../words/ご飯.md) ごはん — cooked rice; meal
- 朝ご飯 あさごはん — breakfast
- 晩ご飯 ばんごはん — dinner; evening meal
- 飯 めし — rice; meal, in a more informal and often masculine-sounding register

## Notes

The ご in ご飯 is a conventional polite prefix, not part of the reading of 飯.
People use ご飯 for their own food too; it does not mean "someone else's honored rice."

[館](館.md) and [飲](飲.md) share the food-shaped left component but have different right sides.
飯 refers to rice or a meal, while 飲 appears in words about drinking.
