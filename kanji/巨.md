# 巨 — huge; gigantic

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 工 (no. 48) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

巨 conveys very great size or scale. 巨大 きょだい means enormous or gigantic, while 巨人 きょじん can mean a giant or a great person. The association can extend beyond someone's physical height.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the broad angular outline and the inner horizontal strokes; imagine a very large sign filling its frame. This visual story is not etymology.

KANJIDIC2 indexes 巨 under 工. Keep that canonical key even though the complete shape is not the isolated character 工.
