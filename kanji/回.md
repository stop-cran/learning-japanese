# 回 — revolve; times

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

回 connects turning around with repeated occurrences. [回る](../words/回る.md) まわる describes something turning; 回す まわす describes turning something.
As a counter, 回 counts occurrences: 一回 いっかい is "once" or "one time."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** trace an imaginary circuit around the inner box. This uses the modern appearance, not a claim about the character's origin.
