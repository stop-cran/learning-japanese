# 忠 — loyalty; faithfulness

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

忠 is loyalty and sincere devotion, whether to a lord, a duty or the truth. It appears in 忠実 and in 忠誠 (ちゅうせい), “loyalty; allegiance.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See [中](中.md), “middle,” above [心](心.md), “heart”; 中 is also read チュウ. Picture a heart held steady in the middle.

## Notes

[患](患.md) has 串 above 心, [思](思.md) has 田 and [志](志.md) has 士; they mean be ill, think and will. [義](義.md) (duty), [丹](丹.md) (sincerity), [信](信.md) (trust) and [真](真.md) (true) overlap with loyalty or sincerity. 忠告 (ちゅうこく) is advice or a warning. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 108](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=108) gives 忠 only チュウ.
