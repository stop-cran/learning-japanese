# 蒔 — sow seeds

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

蒔 means sowing seeds; KANJIDIC2 gives “sow (seeds).” [蒔く](../words/蒔く.md) まく is glossed in JMdict as “to sow; to plant; to seed,” and also figuratively as “to sow (the seeds of; e.g. conflict).” JMdict marks the verb as usually written in kana alone, so it often appears as まく. A third JMdict sense, “to sprinkle (gold or silver powder on lacquerware),” points to 蒔絵 まきえ, “gold or silver lacquer; lacquer decoration sprinkled with metal powder.” Kanjipedia lists the same two senses of sowing and of scattering metal powder on lacquer pictures, and a second meaning, うえる: moving plants and planting them again (植物を移しうえる).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the grass crown 艹, also at the top of [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," sits above [時](時.md), "time, hour." Imagine the moment in spring when it is time to put seeds in the ground so the green shoots come up. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 艹 or 時 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- 蒔 is not in the 2010 Joyo table, and the Digital Daijisen page checked for 蒔 has no character entry for it.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings まい, まか, and まき; they are name uses, not quiz readings.
- JMdict also lists 播く as a spelling of 蒔く.

These four cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [植](植.md) (“plant; put in the ground”) and [挿](挿.md) (“insert; plant a cutting”): JMdict glosses 蒔く as “to sow; to plant; to seed,” and Kanjipedia's second meaning of 蒔 is うえる (replanting plants); both overlap “plant.”
- [種](種.md) (“seed; kind”): JMdict's “to seed” for 蒔く and the title's “seeds” overlap “seed.”
- [散](散.md) (“scatter”): JMdict glosses 蒔く as “to sprinkle (gold or silver powder on lacquerware),” and Kanjipedia describes that sense as scattering (散らし落とす) gold and silver powder.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 咲 (“blossom; bloom”), 枯 (“wither; dry up”), and 掘 (“dig; excavate”); none means to sow seeds.
