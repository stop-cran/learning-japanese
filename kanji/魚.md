# 魚 — fish

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 魚 (no. 195) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

魚 is a pictograph of a fish: the head at the top, the body (田-like) in the middle and the tail at the bottom. In the old shapes the tail was drawn as a fan, and in modern print it became four dots (灬).
The 田 in the middle is a convenient mnemonic for a scaly body, not the kanji 田 (field) in meaning.
As a radical it shows up in many fish names: 鯉, 鮪, 鯛 and so on.

## Notes

さかな originally meant the food that goes with sake (酒 + 菜); the word later turned into "fish". This is the widely cited explanation.
Do not confuse 魚 with 鳥 (とり, bird), which has a similar frame.
