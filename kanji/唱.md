# 唱 — chant; recite

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

唱 means to chant or recite aloud. In [合唱](../words/合唱.md), 唱 supplies the singing or chanting sense, while 合 adds “together.” The kun reading となえる appears in 唱える, “to chant; recite; advocate.”
