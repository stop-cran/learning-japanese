# 啓 — teach; enlighten

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

In [啓発](../words/啓発.md), 啓 has the sense of teaching or guiding. Other compounds show specialized uses: 啓蟄 (けいちつ) is a traditional seasonal term, and 拝啓 (はいけい) is a formal letter opening that uses 啓 in the sense “to say respectfully.”
