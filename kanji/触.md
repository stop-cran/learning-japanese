# 触 — touch; contact

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 角 (no. 148) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

触 concerns contact and touching, literal or figurative. The verbs 触る さわる and 触れる ふれる overlap in physical contact but differ in conjugation and in their wider uses.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine an insect 虫 reaching out to touch a horn 角. These are visible modern components used for recall, not an etymological explanation.

The indexing radical is the left-hand 角, no. 148, not the visible 虫 on the right.

## Notes

- The undotted source reading さわ is illustrated inside 触り さわり: 触 supplies さわ and り is written in kana. It is not a claim that bare 触 is an independent noun pronounced さわ.
- 触る is a godan verb and 触れる an ichidan verb. In ordinary ふれる contact uses, に marks what is touched; the linked article separately explains constructions such as 手を触れる てをふれる.
