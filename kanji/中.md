# 中 — middle, inside

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 丨 (no. 2) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

中 shows a vertical line through the centre of a box. Explanations of the underlying picture differ: a line piercing the middle of
a target or enclosure, or a flagpole through a flag. All converge on "centre, inside, in the midst of".
The sense of "hit the centre" survives in 的中 てきちゅう (a direct hit).

## Notes

中 as a suffix means "in the midst of / during" (ちゅう: 勉強中 べんきょうちゅう, 会議中 かいぎちゅう) or "throughout" (じゅう: 一日中).
The two readings are not interchangeable; learn each word with its reading.
