# 嘱 — entrust; request

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

嘱 means to entrust or request. In [委嘱](../words/委嘱.md), it is read ショク in a formal word for commissioning or entrusting someone with a task.
