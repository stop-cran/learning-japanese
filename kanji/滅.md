# 滅 — perish; destroy; die out

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

滅 is something coming to an end and no longer being there: a kind of animal dying out, a country falling, a light going off. The linked [絶滅](../words/絶滅.md) ぜつめつ is JMdict's "extinction; extermination," and its second sense is "eradication; stamping out; wiping out." The word article is about a kind of living thing dying out.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for メツ. One example of each:

- to perish or be destroyed: 絶滅;
- a fire or light going out: 点滅 てんめつ, "switching on and off (of a light); flashing; blinking; flickering";
- the death of a Buddha or a high priest: 入滅 にゅうめつ, "entering Nirvana; death (of Buddha, high priest, saint, etc.)," which JMdict marks [Buddhism].

More examples are in the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part has the shape of [火](火.md), "fire," in its lower part. Imagine a flame going out in water. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| ほろ.ぼす | kun | 滅ぼす ほろぼす — to destroy; to overthrow; to wreck; to ruin; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 絶滅, 絶 supplies ゼツ and 滅 supplies メツ. The same メツ is in 滅亡 めつぼう, 消滅 しょうめつ and 全滅 ぜんめつ below.

In 滅びる, 滅ぶ and 滅ぼす, 滅 writes ほろ and the rest is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 滅びる is intransitive and 滅ぼす is transitive: JMdict marks 滅ぼす with the spellings 亡ぼす and, as "search-only kanji form," 滅す and 亡す. JMdict's 滅びる also lists the spelling 亡びる, and its 滅ぶ lists 亡ぶ. The card [亡](亡.md) shares these kun readings.

滅多に めったに, "rarely; seldom," is a whole-word reading, not a use of メツ: JMdict tags its 滅多に spelling "ateji (phonetic) reading," and it has the same tag on 滅多 めった, so the っ and た of めったに are not given to 滅 or 多. 滅茶 めちゃ, 滅入る めいる, "to feel depressed," and 滅金 めっき, "plating; coating; gilding," are also whole words in Daijisen's list of hard-to-read compounds; JMdict tags 滅茶 as ateji and 滅金 as a "rarely used kanji form" of めっき.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows メツ with 滅亡, 消滅 and 絶滅, ほろびる with 滅びる, and ほろぼす with 滅ぼす. Practice selects メツ, the reading of the linked word article. The kun readings are shown with dictionary examples and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. Kanjipedia also lists ベツ, but KANJIDIC2 does not, and no ordinary word is verified for it here, so it is not in this card's metadata.

## Notes

- In 絶滅, [絶](絶.md), "sever; cease," supplies ゼツ. 滅多に is a whole-word reading in which [多](多.md), "many, much," does not contribute its own reading. Other examples above: 滅亡 is "downfall; ruin; collapse; destruction" with [亡](亡.md), "deceased," 消滅 is "extinction; extinguishment; disappearance" with [消](消.md), "extinguish; erase," 全滅 is "total destruction; complete destruction; annihilation; being wiped out," and 滅却 めっきゃく is "extinguishment; destruction; effacement." 仏滅 ぶつめつ is "Buddha's death" and a "very unlucky day (in the traditional calendar)."
- [海](海.md), "sea, ocean," has 氵 at the left, and [水](水.md), "water," and [火](火.md), "fire," are the two parts of the mnemonic above. They are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [亡](亡.md), [絶](絶.md), [消](消.md), [破](破.md), "tear; break," [壊](壊.md), "break; destroy; demolish," [死](死.md), "death," [殺](殺.md), "kill," [崩](崩.md), "crumble; collapse; demolish," [枯](枯.md), "wither; dry up," and [荒](荒.md), "rough; wild; laid waste," overlap perishing, destroying, dying out and a light going out. [別](別.md), "separate, another," is not a sense match but reads ベツ, the Kanjipedia-only reading above, so it is kept out as well. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "perish," "destroy" or "die out" as exclusive to any one character.
