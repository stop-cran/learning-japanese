# 昇 — rise; ascend

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

昇 concerns rising or moving upward, including upward rank. 昇る のぼる is used for natural rising, such as the sun, and promotion. 昇格 しょうかく means promotion or a rise in status.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 日 above 升 and imagine the sun rising over a platform. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology.

## Notes

- JMdict restricts the physical-climbing sense of のぼる to 上る and 登る, the natural-rising sense to 上る and 昇る, and its promotion sense to 昇る. Do not treat all three spellings as unrestricted substitutes.
- For a concrete contrast, 山に登る やまにのぼる means climb a mountain, while 日が昇る ひがのぼる means the sun rises.
- 昇る is an intransitive godan verb: 昇ります のぼります and 昇った のぼった. Its spelling is not read しょうる.
