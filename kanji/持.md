# 持 — hold, have

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 手 (hand; left-side shape 扌, no. 64) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

持 means holding, carrying, or having something. It can concern an object in the hand or something one possesses.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 扌 hand holding the 寺-shaped part beside it. The hand is your cue for "hold"; this is a modern memory aid, not a phonetic or historical analysis.

## Notes

持 has 扌, the left-side form associated with [手](手.md). [待](待.md) has 彳, while [時](時.md) has [日](日.md). Their shared right-hand shape does not make their readings interchangeable.

持っている もっている can mean either "be holding" or "have/own"; context tells you which. A person need not be physically holding something they own.
