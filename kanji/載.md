# 載 — load; publish

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

載 is about putting something on a carrier and about putting something into print. KANJIDIC2 lists “ride”, “board”, “get on”, “place”, “spread”, “record” and “publish”. In [載る](../words/載る.md) のる, JMdict's first sense is “to be placed on; to be set on; to be piled on; to be loaded on” and its second is “to appear (in print); to be mentioned; to be recorded; to be reported; to be given”. Kanjipedia's first sense, ①のせる。, covers both 物をのせる。車や船に積む。 and 印刷物にのせる。しるす。書きしるす。

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the frame 𢦏 opens with [土](土.md), "earth, soil," at the upper left and closes with the strokes of 戈 down the right side; [車](車.md), "car, vehicle," sits inside it at the lower left. Picture a cart loaded high inside a frame, ready to carry its load. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 𢦏 or 車 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists サイ, with 積載, 掲載 and 記載, and のせる and のる; it pairs 載せる with 乗せる and 載る with 乗る.
- Kanjipedia lists しるす and とし outside the Joyo table. Its second sense is ②とし。一年。, with the example 「千載一遇」; in 千載一遇 せんざいいちぐう (JMdict entry 1388870), ざい corresponds to サイ (see [compound sound changes](../articles/compound-sound-changes.md)).
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings とし and のり; the Daijisen character entry gives the names こと, とし and のり.

These twelve cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [乗](乗.md) (“ride; board”): KANJIDIC2 lists “ride” and “board” for 載; JMdict entry 1600270 spells のせる as both 乗せる and 載せる, and the Joyo table pairs 載せる with 乗せる and 載る with 乗る.
- [刊](刊.md) (“publish; issue”) and [掲](掲.md) (“put up; display; publish”): KANJIDIC2 lists “publish” for 載 and for 掲, and the 刊 card's title shares it; JMdict glosses 掲載 けいさい “publication (e.g. of an article in a newspaper); carrying (e.g. a story); running (e.g. a serial); insertion (e.g. of an advertisement); printing; posting (e.g. on the web)” (entry 1250610).
- [記](記.md) (“record in writing”), [録](録.md) (“record; register”) and [書](書.md) (“write”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 載 includes しるす。書きしるす。, KANJIDIC2 lists “record” for 載, and JMdict glosses 記載 きさい “mention (in a document); record; entry; statement; listing” (entry 1223230).
- [積](積.md) (“pile up”), [荷](荷.md) (“load; baggage; burden”) and [運](運.md) (“carry; luck”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 載 includes 車や船に積む。, JMdict glosses 積載 せきさい “loading; carrying” (entry 1383040), and JMdict entry 1600270 sense 3 is “to load (luggage); to carry; to take on board”.
- [置](置.md) (“put; place”): KANJIDIC2 lists “place” for 載, and JMdict entry 1600270 sense 1 is “to place on (something)”.
- [年](年.md) (“year”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 載 is ②とし。一年。
- [駄](駄.md) (“packhorse; horse load; worthless”): KANJIDIC2 gives 駄 “pack horse” and “horse load”, which overlap this card's “load”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 降 (“descend”), 落 (“fall down”), and 浮 (“float”); none means to load or to publish.
