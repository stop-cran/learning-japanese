# 尾 — tail; trailing end

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

尾 can be an animal’s tail or a trailing end. Compare 語尾 (ごび), the end of a word, with 犬の尾 (いぬのお), a dog’s tail.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Under the cover, look for 毛, “hair.” Imagine a furry tail sticking out below it.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ビ | on | 語尾 (ごび), a word ending. ビ also serves as a counter for fish and shrimp. |
| お | kun | [尾](../words/尾.md) お — tail; trailing end |

## Notes

[毛](毛.md) is the part inside the cover; [居](居.md) has 古 below and [尼](尼.md) has a different hooked lower shape. [末](末.md), [端](端.md) and [終](終.md) can all point to an end. The word [しっぽ](../words/しっぽ.md) can be written 尻尾, but do not take ぽ as an independent reading of 尾.
