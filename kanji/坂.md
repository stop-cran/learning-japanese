# 坂 — slope; incline

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

坂 names a slope or sloping road. The standalone word is 坂 さか; 坂路 はんろ also names a slope or ramp and provides a compound example of ハン.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 土 as the ground rising into a slope beside the angled strokes of 反.

## Notes

- A 坂 can be a sloping street, not only a separate hill. Going up or down is supplied by the surrounding expression.
- Distinguish 坂 from [反](反.md), oppose or reverse, [返](返.md), give back, and [飯](飯.md), cooked rice or a meal. The added left-hand shape matters.
