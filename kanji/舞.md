# 舞 — dance

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 舛 (no. 136) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

舞 concerns dancing and also swirling or fluttering movement.
In [舞台](../words/舞台.md) ぶたい, it helps name a performance stage; in 舞う まう,
it can describe dancing or something turning through the air.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a dancer above the two spreading lower shapes of 舛,
with the closely spaced upper strokes suggesting movement. This is not a historical reconstruction.

## Notes

雪が舞う (ゆきがまう) means "snow swirls in the air"; the subject need not be a dancer.
踊る (おどる) also means "dance," but do not substitute it mechanically for this snow expression.
[年](年.md) and [毎](毎.md) share only some upper-stroke features; [足](足.md) offers a
loose comparison with the lower spread. They are partial visual cues, not identical components.
