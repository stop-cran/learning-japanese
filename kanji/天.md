# 天 — heaven, sky

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

天 is commonly explained as a person ([大](大.md), a figure with arms spread) with a line or large head drawn above: what is above a person is the sky.
In the oldest forms the head was drawn big, and the top line later replaced it. The exact development is debated, so treat the "line above a person" image as a convenient explanation.
It means "heaven", "sky" and by extension "weather" (天気) and "nature, providence" (天の神).

## Notes

Look-alikes: [大](大.md) (3 strokes), 犬 (dog, with a dot at the upper right) and 夫 (husband; its top line crosses the arms). 天 has two horizontal strokes, the top one shorter than the second.
Do not confuse あめ (天, heaven) with あめ ([雨](雨.md), rain), or 飴 (candy).
