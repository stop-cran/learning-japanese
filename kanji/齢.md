# 齢 — age; years lived

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 齒 (no. 211) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

齢 concerns age: the years someone or something has lived. 年齢 is the linked age noun. Do not treat every age expression as a general calendar-year expression.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the visible 歯-shaped left side beside the 令-shaped right side as a tally of years. This is a modern-shape story, not an etymological or phonetic claim.

The canonical indexing radical is 齒, no. 211; its visible left-hand form here is 歯. The source index is not itself an explanation of the character's history.

## Notes

- Daijisen attests 齢を重ねる (よわいをかさねる), grow older. This supplies a concrete context for the age noun without treating it as interchangeable with every use of とし.
- The linked [年](../words/年.md) article teaches とし with year and age senses. The illustrated 年齢ねんれい article is an age noun, not the numeral-plus-さい age-counter construction.
- JMdict also recognizes [歯](歯.md) as a spelling of the age noun よわい. Keep that lexical use separate from 歯's ordinary tooth meaning; the shared spelling is not a license to read all tooth words as よわい. [冷](冷.md) instead means cold/chill.
