# 矢 — arrow; wedge

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 矢 (no. 111) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

矢 is an arrow. [矢印](../words/矢印.md) やじるし is "arrow (symbol)," the arrow-shaped mark that shows a direction. JMdict also gives it a colloquial second sense: "(romantic) interest (for a certain person); direction (of one's attention, etc.)." The word 矢 や, JMdict entry 1537760, means "arrow" and also "wedge; chock"; it is the word itself and not one of the linked word articles.

The Daijisen や entry gives three senses: the weapon and hunting tool that is fitted to a bowstring and shot at a distant target; a wedge for splitting hard things such as wood or stone; and a family-crest design built from arrow shapes. Its character entry lists シ, as in 一矢 いっし and 嚆矢 こうし, and や, as in 矢面, 矢印 and 弓矢.

矢 is its own radical, so it has no separate part to name. **Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the vertical stroke as the arrow's shaft and the spreading strokes at the bottom as its tail. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim about how 矢 was formed. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | 一矢 いっし — "one arrow" and "retort"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| や | kun | [矢印](../words/矢印.md) やじるし — arrow symbol |

In 矢印, 矢 writes や and 印 writes しるし, voiced to じるし in the compound. In 一矢, 一 supplies イチ, spoken いっ, and 矢 supplies シ. 一矢 is also the start of the idiom 一矢を報いる いっしをむくいる, "to retaliate; to return a blow; to strike back (at); to retort," marked as an idiomatic expression. In 矢面 やおもて, "firing line; position where one is subject to questioning, criticism, etc.," 矢 writes や and 面 writes おもて. 嚆矢 こうし is "whistling arrow used to signal the start of battle" and "start (e.g. of a movement); beginning; dawn."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シ, with the idiom 一矢を報いる, and や, with 矢, 矢印 and 矢面. Practice selects や, the reading of the linked word article. シ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [印](印.md), "stamp; mark," in 矢印. It supplies しるし, voiced to じるし. Its title overlaps the symbol sense of the word, so 印 is in the quiz exclusions. [示](示.md), "indicate," [標](標.md), "mark; signpost; goal," and [符](符.md), "token; sign; charm," overlap the sense of a mark that points the way, and [方](方.md), "direction, method," [恋](恋.md), "romantic love; longing," and [念](念.md), "thought; attention," overlap the secondary senses "direction" and "(romantic) interest." They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [午](午.md), "noon," [牛](牛.md), "cattle, cow," and [天](天.md), "heaven, sky," share a long vertical stroke and a crossing bar with 矢, so they look plausible. They are read ゴ and うま; ギュウ and うし; and テン, あま and あめ, and mean noon, cow and sky. None shares a reading or a meaning with 矢.
- Other kanji read シ or や, such as [四](四.md), "four," and [家](家.md), "house, home," are never offered as wrong answers.
