# 族 — tribe, family group

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 方 (no. 70) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

族 refers to a family, kin group, or people sharing an identity. In [家族](../words/家族.md), it concerns the family members, not the building they live in.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the [方](方.md)-shaped left side as a flag and the arrow-shaped 矢 at lower right as a family's shared emblem. This is a memory aid, not a historical account.

## Common words

- [家族](../words/家族.md) かぞく — family
- 民族 みんぞく — people or ethnic group
- 親族 しんぞく — relatives, kin

## Notes

[旅](旅.md) shares much of the left and upper shape; look for 矢 at the lower right of 族. [方](方.md) alone is much simpler.
The family meaning of [家](家.md) overlaps with 族, but 家 also means a house or home; 族 points toward a group of people.
