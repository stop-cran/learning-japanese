# 衣 — clothing; garment

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

衣 concerns clothes and garments. The native noun ころも also has a coating sense, such as batter or icing, while compounds often use イ or エ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a garment hanging below the top strokes, its lower strokes spreading into sleeves and hems. This describes the modern outline for recall, not its verified origin.

## Notes

- 衣 is the indexing radical in its full form here. In characters such as [被](被.md) and [補](補.md), it has the left-hand form 衤; that is not the similar-looking 礻 form of 示.
- 上衣 うわぎ illustrates final -ぎ. The same うわぎ word can be written 上着; the record's other reading じょうい is restricted to 上衣. Do not substitute イ for ぎ in this native word.
- 衣 きぬ is a dictionary-attested clothing word, not a claim that this spelling means silk. Learn it separately from the ころも word and from the clothing compounds.
