# 止 — stop, halt

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 止 (no. 77) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

止 marks stopping, halting, or ceasing. Whether something stops or someone stops it depends on the whole verb, not just this kanji.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the upright strokes as a barrier fixed to the bottom horizontal line: stop here. This is a modern visual aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

車が止まる くるまがとまる means "the car stops"; 車を止める くるまをとめる means "someone stops the car."
The spelling 止める also permits やめる, "to quit or stop an activity," often written in kana; learn the reading in context.
[正](正.md) adds a top horizontal stroke to 止. [上](上.md) has only three strokes.
