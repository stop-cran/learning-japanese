# 悟 — realize; enlightenment; perceive

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

悟 is the moment of understanding: suddenly realizing something, perceiving what is true, and, in Buddhism, attaining enlightenment. [覚悟](../words/覚悟.md) かくご is being mentally prepared for what may come; JMdict glosses it "readiness; (mental) preparedness," "resolution; determination" and "resignation (to one's fate)."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ゴ. The first is suddenly seeing the point and awakening to the truth, さとる or さとり, with examples such as 悟性 ごせい, "wisdom; understanding"; 悟道 ごどう, "(the path of spiritual) enlightenment; attainment of supreme wisdom," a Buddhist term in JMdict; 改悟 かいご, "reform; reformation"; 悔悟 かいご, "remorse; repentance"; 開悟 かいご, "wisdom; enlightenment"; 覚悟; 大悟 たいご, "enlightenment; great wisdom," which JMdict marks as Buddhist and also reads だいご; and 頓悟 とんご, "sudden enlightenment," also Buddhist. The second is being quick to understand, さとい, as in 穎悟 えいご, "intelligent; shrewd." Its kun reading is さとる, the same as KANJIDIC2.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 忄, the narrow form of [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the left, and at the right [五](五.md), "five," over [口](口.md), "mouth." Imagine five mouths explaining the same thing until the heart finally sees it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 忄, 五 or 口 contributes meaning or sound, although 五 is read ゴ, a reading 悟 also has. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ゴ | on | [覚悟](../words/覚悟.md) かくご — mental preparedness; resolve |
| さと.る | kun | 悟る さとる - to sense; to perceive; to notice; to understand; to realize; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 覚悟, 覚 supplies カク and 悟 supplies ゴ, with no sound change. In 悟る, 悟 writes さと and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict glosses 悟る "to sense; to perceive; to notice; to discern; to become aware of," "to understand; to comprehend; to realize" and, as a Buddhist term, "to attain enlightenment." Its noun 悟り さとり keeps さと with り: "comprehension; understanding" and, in Buddhism, "enlightenment; spiritual awakening; satori." JMdict lists 覚る and 覚り as rarely used kanji forms of these words.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ゴ, with 悟性, 覚悟 and 悔悟 as examples, and さとる, with 悟る and 悟り. Practice selects ゴ, the reading with a linked word article. No word article uses さと.る yet; it is illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [覚](覚.md), "memorize; awaken," in 覚悟. The right part of 悟, 五 over 口, has no card of its own yet.
- [口](口.md), "mouth," is the bottom of the right part of 悟. [忙](忙.md), "busy," and [慢](慢.md), "arrogance; slackness," have the same 忄 at the left. All are different kanji. [語](語.md), "language, word," has the same 五 over 口 at the right and, like [五](五.md), "five," is read ゴ.
- [覚](覚.md), "memorize; awaken," [存](存.md), "exist; be aware," [識](識.md), "knowledge; awareness," [知](知.md), "know," [認](認.md), "recognize; acknowledge," [察](察.md), "observe; infer," [弁](弁.md), "speech; discern; valve; bento," [哲](哲.md), "philosophy; wise; sage," and [賢](賢.md), "wise; intelligent," overlap the senses "awaken," "become aware of," "understand," "discern," "realize" and "wisdom" that 悟 and its words carry; 覚 appears with 悟 in 覚悟 and is also read さと.る, as in 覚る. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "realize" or "enlightenment" as exclusive to any one character.
