# 廷 — court (of law or of a ruler)

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 廴 (no. 54) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

廷 refers to a court: a court of law in 法廷, or a ruler’s court in 宮廷 (きゅうてい). The standard-use table gives it only the reading テイ.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The long sweeping 廴 runs under the right-hand part, as in 延 and 建. Picture the floor of a hall where a court sits.

## Notes

[延](延.md) and [建](建.md) share 廴 but hold different parts above it, and [任](任.md) has a similar right-hand part beside 亻. [庭](庭.md) (garden) is also read テイ but sits under 广. [宮](宮.md) and [殿](殿.md) can name a palace, and [裁](裁.md) and [判](判.md) relate to judging.
