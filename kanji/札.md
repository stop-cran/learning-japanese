# 札 — tag; banknote

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

札 can name a tag, label, card, or banknote. The reading depends on the word: 札 さつ is a banknote, while 札 ふだ has uses such as a ticket, tag, or charm.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside the short right-hand hook and imagine a small tag hanging from a wooden object. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not etymology.

## Notes

- The linked 札 article teaches さつ, banknote. The separate ふだ entry is retained here without linking it as if it were that article's primary reading.
- Do not infer the pronunciation of an unfamiliar word merely from the physical object looking like a card. Learn the complete word and its reading.
