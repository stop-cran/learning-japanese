# 肩 — shoulder

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

肩 names the shoulder. The native noun 肩 かた can describe a body location or a condition such as stiff shoulders; the on reading appears in compounds such as 強肩.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See a 戸-shaped top above the flesh-shaped lower part and picture a bag resting on a shoulder. This modern-shape scene is not a verified account of the character's origin.

The canonical key 肉, no. 130, appears below the top part in the compact 月-shaped form. It is not the moon key in this metadata.

## Notes

- 肩がこる かたがこる describes stiff or tense shoulders. 肩に掛ける かたにかける places something on the shoulder; the particle follows the construction.
- 強肩 きょうけん concerns a strong throwing arm. Do not infer that it merely describes large shoulders or that ケン replaces かた in every ordinary body-part expression.
- Compare [戸](戸.md), door/household counter, and the roofed shapes of [局](局.md), bureau/office, and [居](居.md), be present/dwell. They do not name neighboring body parts.
