# 翻 — turn over; reverse

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 羽 (no. 124) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

翻 can describe turning something over, so its other side is exposed, or changing something suddenly. It also appears in words for adapting or transferring material from one form to another, such as 翻案 (ほんあん), an adaptation, and 翻訳 (ほんやく), translation. In 翻訳, the whole compound is read ほんやく; 翻 does not mean “translate” in every word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [番](番.md), "turn in a series," at the left and [羽](羽.md), "feather; bird counter," at the right. Imagine birds in a row, each taking its turn to flip over in the air with a flash of feathers. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 番 or 羽 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

The Jōyō table lists ホン, ひるがえる, and ひるがえす. KANJIDIC2 and Kanjipedia also record ハン outside the Jōyō readings. JMdict's checked ハン example is a specialized mahjong counter, so it is not selected for questions. The title's reverse/turn-over sense can also fit 反, 逆, and 転; those cards are excluded from this card's meaning quiz. JMdict also glosses 翻る as “to flutter (in the wind); to wave; to flap; to fly” and 翻す as “to change (one's mind); to reverse (one's decision)” and “to fly (a flag),” so the displayed titles of 振 (“wave; swing; allocate”), 飛 (“fly”), and 変 (“change; strange”) could be defended as answers. KANJIDIC2 also lists “wave” for 翻, the first word of 波 (“wave; billow”). The title's “reverse” can also be read as a noun for the back of something, which is the displayed meaning of 裏 (“reverse side; lining”). Those five cards are excluded as well.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 開 (“open; unfold”), 話 (“talk, speak”), and 立 (“stand; set up”); none describes turning over or reversing.
