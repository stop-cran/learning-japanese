# 翻 — turn over; reverse

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 羽 (no. 124) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

翻 can describe turning something over, so its other side is exposed, or changing something suddenly. It also appears in words for adapting or transferring material from one form to another, such as 翻案 (ほんあん), an adaptation, and 翻訳 (ほんやく), translation. In 翻訳, the whole compound is read ほんやく; 翻 does not mean “translate” in every word.
