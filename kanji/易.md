# 易 — easy

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The most useful starting point is ease: [易しい](../words/易しい.md) やさしい describes
something that is not difficult. JMdict marks this adjective as usually written in kana, やさしい.
Not every compound has that meaning: 貿易 ぼうえき means international trade.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine 日 resting easily above the slanting strokes of 勿.
This describes the modern outline, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

[優しい](../words/優しい.md) is also やさしい, but means "kind; gentle," not "easy."
[昔](昔.md) puts 日 at the bottom. [場](場.md) has a similar lower sweep but an extra horizontal
under its upper box; [昨](昨.md) puts 日 on the left.
