# 焼 — bake; burn

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

焼 covers baking, grilling, and burning. Its verbs can describe cooking, firing pottery, or damage by heat, so it is not limited to either food or destructive fire.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the [火](火.md) at the left heating a rack suggested by the stacked right-hand strokes: food bakes above the fire.

## Notes

- 焼く is transitive godan: 焼いて やいて. 焼ける is intransitive ichidan: 焼けて やけて. In cooking, 魚を焼く さかなをやく means grill fish; 魚が焼ける さかながやける describes the fish cooking.
- 焼き is also used before or after other elements in food names. The hyphens mark those bound positions, not extra sounds.
- 焼ける can also be the potential form of 焼く. Context distinguishes 'be baked' from 'can bake'.
- Compare [燃える](../words/燃える.md), burn or be on fire. Ordinary 焼く and 焼ける also include burning, so do not treat this as a strict cooking-versus-fire division.
