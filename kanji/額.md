# 額 — forehead; frame; sum

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

額 writes the native forehead word ひたい and the on-reading noun がく for a frame or amount. 金額 is an amount of money and 総額 a total sum.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the 客-shaped block as a framed object beside the head-related 頁 shape. This is a modern-shape association, not a historical derivation.

The canonical radical is 頁, no. 181, on the right.
