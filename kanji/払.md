# 払 — pay

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

払 appears in paying and clearing something away. [支払う](../words/支払う.md) しはらう focuses on payment,
while 払う はらう can mean either paying or brushing something off.
The money meaning does not explain every use of the character.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the hand-side 扌 sweeping past the small ム shape.
Use that picture for "clear away," without treating it as historical etymology.

## Notes

料金を払う (りょうきんをはらう) means "pay the fee."
ほこりを払う means "brush off the dust," not "pay for the dust."

[私](私.md) shares ム but has 禾 instead of 扌.
[打](打.md) and [抱](抱.md) share the hand-side form but have different right sides.
The first character of 支払う is ateji: 支 supplies the sound し here rather than its usual meaning.
See the word article rather than guessing a literal compound meaning.
