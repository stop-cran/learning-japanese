# 県 — prefecture

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

県 names a prefecture and appears after many Japanese prefecture names. It does not label every prefectural-level division: Tokyo uses 都, while Osaka and Kyoto use 府. The administrative meaning can differ when translating divisions in other countries.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Treat the upper eye-like enclosure and the spreading lower strokes as a marked region with routes leading out. This is a map-shaped memory image, not the character's history.

## Notes

- 長野県 ながのけん is Nagano Prefecture. Attach 県 directly to the name; the linked [県](../words/県.md) article demonstrates the construction.
- The collective expression 都道府県 とどうふけん covers Japan's prefectural-level categories. [都](../words/都.md) と appears in 東京都 とうきょうと; [府](府.md) フ appears in 大阪府 おおさかふ.
- Compare [真](真.md), true, and [具](具.md), tools or ingredients. Their similar enclosures do not give them an administrative meaning.
