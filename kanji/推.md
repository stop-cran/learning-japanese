# 推 — infer; recommend; push forward

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

推 is pushing something forward, whether a person toward a post or a line of thought toward a conclusion. [推定](../words/推定.md) すいてい is estimation or inference, and [推薦](../words/推薦.md) すいせん is a recommendation or endorsement. JMdict glosses 推定 "presumption; assumption" and "estimation," and 推薦 "recommendation; referral; endorsement."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for スイ. One example of each:

- pushing or moving forward: 推進 すいしん, "propulsion; drive," also "promotion (of a policy, project, movement, etc.); furtherance; advancement; pushing forward";
- respectfully raising a person up: 推戴 すいたい, "(being) presided over by";
- backing a person to be chosen or used: 推奨 すいしょう, "recommendation; endorsement";
- pushing one's thinking forward, inferring: 推測 すいそく, "guess; conjecture."

It also has a separate noun entry すい【推】, inferring, or 推察 すいさつ, "guess; conjecture; surmise." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the left, written 扌, and at the right 隹, a part with no card of its own that also appears in [唯](唯.md), "only; solely; merely," and [集](集.md), "gather." Imagine a hand gently pushing a small bird forward off a branch so that it flies. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that these parts contribute meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| スイ | on | [推定](../words/推定.md) すいてい — estimation; inference; [推薦](../words/推薦.md) すいせん — recommendation; endorsement |
| お.す | kun | 推す おす - to recommend; to endorse (e.g. a candidate); to nominate; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 推定, 推 supplies スイ and 定 supplies テイ; in 推薦, 推 supplies スイ and 薦 supplies セン, with no sound change in either. In 推す, 推 writes お and す is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict's 推す also means "to infer (from); to deduce; to gather; to conjecture," and marks its fourth sense, "to be a fan of (a singer, character in media, etc.); to stan," as slang. 推し量る おしはかる, "to guess; to conjecture; to surmise," keeps おし, and JMdict lists 推し進める as a second spelling of 押し進める おしすすめる, "to press forward; to forge ahead; to push on."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows スイ, with 推進 and 推薦 as examples, and おす, with 推す, compared (⇔) with 押す おす, "to push; to thrust; to shove." Practice selects スイ, the reading with linked word articles; お.す is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [定](定.md), "determine; settle," in 推定, and [薦](薦.md), "recommend; mat," in 推薦.
- [唯](唯.md), "only; solely; merely," [維](維.md), "fiber," and [雑](雑.md), "miscellaneous," contain the same 隹 as 推. All are different kanji.
- [押](押.md), "push," writes 押す and 押し進める; [進](進.md), "advance," overlaps pushing forward, as in 推進; [察](察.md), "observe; infer," [憶](憶.md), "remember; recall; guess," and [測](測.md), "measure, gauge," overlap inferring, guessing and estimating, as in 推察, 推測 and 推定; and [勧](勧.md), "recommend; encourage," and [奨](奨.md), "encourage; exhort; promote," share "recommend" and "promote," as in 推奨. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "infer" or "recommend" as exclusive to any one character.
- [挙](挙.md), "raise; cite; hold (an event)," and [提](提.md), "submit; propose; carry in hand," share recommending and proposing: JMdict glosses one sense of 挙げる "to nominate (e.g. for a position); to recommend; to propose (something)", and 推 appears with 挙 in 推挙. The three are kept out of one another's quiz options.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first three スイ senses: 推移 すいい, "transition; change; progress; development; shift," and 推力 すいりょく, "thrust; driving force; propulsion," for pushing forward; 推服 すいふく, "admiration," for raising a person up; and 推挙 すいきょ, "recommendation (of a person for a position); nomination," 推薦 and 推挽 すいばん, which JMdict heads 推輓 and marks "formal or literary term," "recommendation (of someone for a position); nomination," for backing a person.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the fourth スイ sense, inferring: 推定, 推理 すいり, "reasoning; inference; deduction," 推量 すいりょう, "guess; conjecture; supposition; inference," 推論 すいろん, "inference; deduction; induction; reasoning," 邪推 じゃすい, "distrust; unjust suspicion," and 類推 るいすい, "analogy."
