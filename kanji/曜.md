# 曜 — day of the week

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

曜 identifies days in the weekly cycle. Its everyday use is in 曜日 ようび, "day of the week," and names such as [土曜日](../words/土曜日.md).

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** let the left-hand [日](日.md) stand for a day and imagine the densely packed right side as a busy weekly calendar. This is a memory aid, not an origin claim.

| ヨウ | on | 曜日 ようび (day of the week), [土曜日](../words/土曜日.md) どようび (Saturday) |

## Common words

- 曜日 ようび — day of the week
- [土曜日](../words/土曜日.md) どようび — Saturday
- [金曜日](../words/金曜日.md) きんようび — Friday
- 月曜日 げつようび — Monday

## Notes

曜日 includes Saturday and Sunday; do not interpret the dictionary gloss "weekday" as only Monday through Friday.
In ようび, 曜 supplies よう and 日 supplies び, a voiced form of the native reading ひ. [時](時.md) shares the left-hand 日; [習](習.md) has a similar wing-shaped top but no 日 at the left.
