# 季 — seasons

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 子 (no. 39) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

季 concerns seasons or a time of year. 季節 きせつ names a season, while 四季 しき names the four seasons together. The character's association does not turn either complete word into a literal English sum of its parts.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 禾 above 子 and imagine a child watching a plant change through the seasons. This is a modern-shape story, not a reconstruction of the character's origin.
