# 物 — thing, object

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 牛 (no. 93) · **Phonetic:** 勿 · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

物 combines 牛 (cow, ox) and 勿. The usual dictionary analysis takes 勿 as a sound component (ブツ) and 牛 as the meaning part: the early sense is usually explained as livestock or animals of various kinds, traditionally said to have widened to "things in general".
Treat this as the standard explanation, not a certainty; the exact origin of 勿 itself is debated.
In Japanese it means a concrete "thing, object", and in compounds also "goods", "a person of a kind" (人物) or "a creature" (動物).

## Notes

- 物 (もの) is a concrete thing; abstract "matters" are 事 (こと): see [事](事.md).
- Verb stem + 物 makes "a thing to do X": 買い物, 飲み物, 食べ物, 着物 きもの (clothes).
- 物 is ブツ in most kango, but モツ in a few such as 荷物, so learn those words as units.
- Look-alikes: on the left, 牛 appears as the squeezed form 牜; on the right, 勿 is a different shape from [力](力.md).
