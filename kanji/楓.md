# 楓 — maple

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

楓 writes **maple**, [楓](../words/楓.md) かえで, the tree. The same character can name a different tree when read ふう; do not assume that every botanical compound containing 楓 denotes a maple.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside [風](風.md), wind. Imagine a maple's leaves moving in the wind. This is a memory aid based on the visible shapes, not a historical explanation of the character.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| フウ | on | 楓葉 ふうよう - maple leaf; separately, 楓 ふう names Formosan sweetgum (*Liquidambar formosana*), not the maple called かえで |
| かえで | kun | [楓](../words/楓.md) かえで - maple tree |

## Common words

- [楓](../words/楓.md) かえで - maple tree
- 楓の葉 (かえでのは) - maple leaves

## Notes

- This card asks かえで. フウ remains available for recognizing the separate plant name and compounds; it is not another freely interchangeable pronunciation of the native maple noun.
- [紅葉](../words/紅葉.md) has the reading もみじ for maple, as well as こうよう for autumn foliage. Not all autumn-colored leaves are maple leaves.
- Compare [風](風.md), wind or style: 楓 adds 木 on the left. [村](村.md), village, has 寸 on the right; [相](相.md), mutual or aspect, has 目.
