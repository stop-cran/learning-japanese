# 婆 — elderly woman

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

婆 refers to an elderly woman. In お婆さん the character supplies ばあ; the お before it and さん after it are separate. The word is also written entirely in kana.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Unlike 姫, 婆 puts 女 underneath. Compare [姿](姿.md), another character with 女 at the bottom; 婆 has [波](波.md), wave, above it.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| バ | on | 老婆 (ろうば), an elderly woman. |
| ばば | kun | 婆 (ばば), an old woman; often written in kana. This is not the long vowel of おばあさん. |
| ばあ | kun | [お婆さん](../words/おばあさん.md) おばあさん — elderly woman |

## Common words

- [お婆さん](../words/おばあさん.md) おばあさん — elderly woman

## Notes

In the physical shape, look for the water strokes of 波 rather than the cover in [疲](疲.md). For family relationships, do not assume every おばあさん is somebody's grandmother: お祖母さん is the usual kanji spelling when that relationship is meant. In [姿](姿.md), the top is 次 rather than [波](波.md). [老](老.md) concerns old age generally; neither an elderly woman nor a [母](母.md) (mother) is necessarily the speaker's grandmother.
