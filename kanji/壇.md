# 壇 — raised platform; rostrum

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

壇 is a raised platform, such as one a speaker stands on. In some compounds it instead points to a professional or artistic circle.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The character has 土 at the left; imagine looking up at a speaker on a raised platform.

## Notes

The ダン of 壇 is different from the タン of 土壇場 (どたんば), an expression for the last possible moment.

[暗](暗.md) starts with 日 rather than 土. Compare [境](境.md) (boundary) and [塔](塔.md) (tower), which share the earth radical but not the right side. [台](台.md) can be a stand for something, whereas the speaker in this example stands on a 壇.
