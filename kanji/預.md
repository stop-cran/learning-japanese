# 預 — deposit; entrust

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

預 concerns entrusting something to another person's care or holding it in custody. 預金 is a bank deposit, 預ける is leaving something in someone's keeping, and 預かる is taking care of it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the 予-shaped left part as an item entrusted to the person represented by 頁. This is a modern-shape association, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 頁, no. 181, on the right.

## Notes

- 預ける is a transitive ichidan verb and 預かる is a transitive godan verb. They differ in viewpoint, not simply as a transitive/intransitive pair.
- 係員に荷物を預ける (かかりいんににもつをあずける) means leave luggage with an attendant; 係員が荷物を預かる (かかりいんがにもつをあずかる) means the attendant takes care of the luggage. Both constructions use 荷物を.
- Compare [予](予.md), beforehand. Its left-side shape occurs here, but 預ける is a complete verb rather than a mechanical combination of two English glosses.
