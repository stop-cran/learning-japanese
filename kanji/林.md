# 林 — grove; woodland

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

林 concerns trees growing together as woods or a grove. 林 はやし is the native noun; 森林 しんりん names forest or woodland.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice two 木 shapes side by side and imagine trees growing together. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a sourced historical classification.

## Notes

- [木](木.md) can name one tree or wood as material. 林 here names a wooded place, not a piece of timber.
- 林 はやし and [森](../words/森.md) もり overlap in naming wooded areas. The number of 木 shapes is a memory aid, not a rule fixing a wood's exact size or tree count.
- Do not confuse the two 木 shapes with [休](休.md), rest, whose left side is the person form 亻.
