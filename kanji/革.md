# 革 — leather; reform

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 革 (no. 177) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

革 concerns leather and occurs in words for reform or reorganization. 革 is the native material noun かわ, while 改革 names reform.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the central crossed shape and surrounding strokes as a leather piece stretched out for work. This is a modern-shape story, not a historical reconstruction.

The complete character is the canonical radical 革, no. 177.

## Notes

- The linked 革 article teaches the material noun かわ. The reform meaning belongs to established compounds such as 改革, not to every isolated かわ noun.
- Compare [皮](皮.md), which has skin, hide and outer-covering uses. The processed-leather distinction is useful, but the shared leather/hide area should not be presented as a complete semantic separation.
- Do not confuse the whole shape with [章](章.md), chapter/badge, or [車](車.md), car/vehicle.
