# 留 — retain; stay

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

留 has the idea of keeping something in place or remaining somewhere.
In [留学](../words/留学.md) りゅうがく, the stay is for study abroad.
留める とめる can describe fastening something so that it stays in place.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** think of the two separated upper pieces as being held over the lower 田.
This is a memory aid only; the field-shaped component does not make 留 a word for farmland.

## Notes

来年、日本に留学します。 (らいねん、にほんにりゅうがくします。) — I will study in Japan next year.
Learn 留守 as a whole word: its meaning "being away" is not a direct translation of the character gloss "stay."

[男](男.md) has 田 above 力; [番](番.md) has a different top above 田.
[魚](魚.md) ends in four dots rather than 留's bottom edge.
