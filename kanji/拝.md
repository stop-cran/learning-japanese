# 拝 — worship; bow in respect

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

拝 concerns worship, reverence or a humble attitude in certain expressions. 拝む おがむ can mean to pay respects or assume a praying posture. 拝見 はいけん is humble seeing, not a claim that looking at a document is an act of religious worship.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside several horizontal strokes crossed by an upright stroke and imagine hands brought together respectfully. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 手; the left-hand shape is 扌.

## Notes

- The inspected JMdict entry for 拝む gives おがむ. It is not independent evidence that the same example is pronounced おろがむ; that source reading remains qualified lookup information.
- 拝見する describes the speaker's own side's seeing humbly, not an honorific description of the listener's action.
- 拝む also has a begging sense and a humble seeing sense; JMdict notes that the latter is sometimes sarcastic in modern Japanese. Do not assume that every occurrence means religious worship.
