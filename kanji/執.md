# 執 — take hold; carry out; cling

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32), at the top left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

執 is taking something firmly in hand: holding a tool to do a task, carrying out duties, and clinging to an idea or a feeling. [執筆](../words/執筆.md) しっぴつ is writing or authoring, taking up the brush to produce a text. JMdict glosses the verb 執る とる "to take (trouble); to attend (to business); to command (army)."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for シツ, which it marks as a customary reading. One example of each:

- taking in hand: 執刀 しっとう, "performing a surgical operation";
- carrying out: 執行 しっこう, "execution; carrying out; performance; enforcement";
- clinging and not letting go: 執拗 しつよう, "persistent; obstinate; tenacious; relentless."

For シュウ it gives one sense, clinging and not letting go, as in 執着 しゅうちゃく, "attachment; adhesion; insistence; tenacity; fixation; obsession," 執念 しゅうねん, "tenacity; persistence; obsession," and 妄執 もうしゅう, "deep-rooted delusion; firm conviction (based on incorrect beliefs)." Its kun reading is とる. A separate Daijisen headword しゅう treats 執 as a noun for a deep attachment; JMdict lists it as 執 しゅう, "attachment; obsession; persistence," marked archaic. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [幸](幸.md), "happiness; good fortune," at the left and [丸](丸.md), "round; circle," at the right. Imagine someone gripping good fortune in one hand and a round ball in the other, refusing to let go of either. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 幸 or 丸 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シツ | on | [執筆](../words/執筆.md) しっぴつ — writing; authoring |
| シュウ | on | 執着 しゅうちゃく - attachment; adhesion; insistence; tenacity; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| と.る | kun | 執る とる - to take (trouble); to attend (to business); to command (army); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 執筆, 執 supplies シツ and 筆 supplies ヒツ. シツ shortens to しっ, with a small っ that doubles the next sound (gemination), and ヒツ becomes ぴつ after it, giving しっぴつ; the same しっ appears in 執行 and 執刀. In 執着, 執 supplies シュウ and 着 supplies チャク. In 執る, 執 writes と and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict reads 確執 かくしつ or かくしゅう, so both on readings occur in that word.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シツ, with 執務 しつむ, "performance of one's official duties," 執筆 and 確執, "discord; antagonism," as examples; シュウ, with 執念, 執心 しゅうしん, "devotion; attachment; obsession," and 我執 がしゅう, "egotism; obstinacy"; and とる, with 執る, marked as a pair to compare with 取る とる, "to take; to pick up; to grab; to catch; to hold," and 採る とる, "to adopt (a method, proposal, etc.); to take (a measure, course of action, etc.)." Practice selects シツ, the reading with a linked word article; シュウ and と.る are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [筆](筆.md), "brush; writing," in 執筆.
- [幸](幸.md), "happiness; good fortune," is the whole left part of 執, and [報](報.md), "report; news," has the same 幸 at the left. [熱](熱.md), "heat; fever," has 丸 at the upper right, as 執 has 丸 at the right. All are different kanji.
- [取](取.md), "take," [握](握.md), "grip; grasp," [持](持.md), "hold, have," [捕](捕.md), "capture," [履](履.md), "footwear; put on shoes; carry out," and [司](司.md), "take charge; official; preside," overlap the senses "take hold" and "carry out" that 執 and its words carry; 取 and 捕 are also read と.る, and the Joyo table pairs 執る with 取る. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "take" or "carry out" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the シツ senses: 執筆 for taking in hand; 執政 しっせい, "administration; government; administrator; chief executive," and 執務 for carrying out; and 確執 and 固執 こしつ, "sticking to (an opinion, theory, belief, etc.); clinging to; adherence," for clinging. For シュウ it also gives 執心, 我執, 固執, which JMdict also reads こしゅう, and 偏執 へんしゅう, "bias; eccentricity; obstinacy," also read へんしつ.
