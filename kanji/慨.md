# 慨 — deep emotion; lament

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慨 is a strong feeling that stirs the heart, whether deep emotion or indignant sorrow. It appears in 感慨, in 憤慨, “indignation,” and in 慨嘆 (がいたん), “lamentation; regret.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 忄, the narrow form of 心 “heart,” is on the left, and [既](既.md), “already,” is on the right.

## Notes

[概](概.md) has 木 in place of 忄 and is also read ガイ, but means outline or roughly; [既](既.md), the right-hand part on its own, is read キ and means already; [慣](慣.md) also starts with 忄 and means accustomed. [嘆](嘆.md), [悲](悲.md) and [哀](哀.md) relate to lament or sorrow; [憤](憤.md), [怒](怒.md) and [憾](憾.md) to indignation, anger or regret; [感](感.md), [激](激.md), [情](情.md) and [胸](胸.md) to feeling, intensity, emotion or the heart. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 25](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=25) gives 慨 only ガイ, with 慨嘆, 憤慨 and 感慨.
