# 献 — offer; contribute; dedicate

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (no. 94), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

献 is offering something up to a higher person or to a god, and by extension giving effort or oneself to a cause. The linked [貢献](../words/貢献.md) こうけん is JMdict's "contribution (furthering a goal or cause)" and "services (to a cause)." The linked [文献](../words/文献.md) ぶんけん is "literature; books (reference); document." The linked [献立](../words/献立.md) こんだて is "menu; bill of fare" and "program; programme; schedule."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ケン. One example of each:

- offering to a superior or a god: 献金 けんきん, "donation; contribution; offering";
- urging a drink on a guest: 献杯 けんぱい, "toast (to someone; esp. the deceased at a post-ceremony meal); drinking to someone's health (or memory)";
- a learned person: Daijisen's example is 文献, which JMdict glosses today as "literature; books (reference); document."

For コン Daijisen gives two senses: the number of rounds of cups exchanged, as in 九献 くこん, "three-times-three exchange of nuptial cups," and the arrangement of dishes, as in 献立. More examples are in the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [犬](犬.md), "dog," at the right. Imagine a dog carrying an offering to its master. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 犬 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケン | on | [文献](../words/文献.md) ぶんけん — reference literature; [貢献](../words/貢献.md) こうけん — contribution |
| コン | on | [献立](../words/献立.md) こんだて — menu; planned dishes |
| たてまつ.る | kun, source-only here | No JMdict word checked writes this reading with 献; see below |

In 文献, [文](文.md) supplies ブン and 献 supplies ケン: ぶん + けん. In 貢献, 貢 supplies コウ and 献 supplies ケン: こう + けん. The same ケン is in 献金, 献杯 and 献身 けんしん, "devotion; dedication; self-sacrifice."

In 献立, 献 supplies コン, a second on reading, and [立](立.md) supplies だて, the voiced form of たて: こん + だて. This is the コン that Daijisen marks as the Go-on reading (呉); it also appears in 九献 and 三献 さんこん, "three-times-three exchange of nuptial cups."

KANJIDIC2 and Daijisen give たてまつる, and Daijisen also gives ささげる. JMdict has 献る 1584070 with the spellings 奉る and 献る: たてまつる is restricted to 奉る and まつる is the reading for both; its first sense is tagged "humble (kenjougo) language" and reads "to offer; to present." JMdict lists 献げる only as a "rarely used kanji form" of ささげる, whose usual spelling is 捧げる. So there is no ordinary word here where 献 writes たてまつ.る; the reading is source-only and not selected for questions.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケン with 献上 けんじょう, "presenting (to)," 献身的 けんしんてき, "devoted; self-sacrificing," and 文献; and コン with 献立 and 一献 いっこん, "one cup (of sake)." Practice selects ケン and コン, the readings of the linked word articles. The Joyo table lists no kun reading for 献; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. The table shows the older form 獻 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- In the linked words, [文](文.md), "sentence, writing," supplies ブン in 文献 and [立](立.md), "stand; set up," supplies だて in 献立. [貢](貢.md), "tribute; contribute," supplies コウ in 貢献.
- [猫](猫.md), "cat," [独](独.md), "alone; sole; Germany (abbreviation)," and [状](状.md), "condition; form," also contain a dog shape (犬, or its left-hand form 犭). They look alike at a glance but have different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [供](供.md), "accompany, offer," [贈](贈.md), "give as a gift; confer," [与](与.md), "give; grant," [授](授.md), "grant; impart; teach," [施](施.md), "carry out; give; alms," [納](納.md), "pay; deliver; put away; accept," [給](給.md), "pay; bestow," and [幣](幣.md), "currency; Shinto offering," overlap the offering and giving senses. [仕](仕.md), "serve, attend," overlaps the service in 貢献 and 献身, and [杯](杯.md), "cupful counter," overlaps the cups of 献杯 and 九献. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Many other kanji also read ケン or コン, such as 建, 検, 今, 金 and 近, and are never offered as wrong answers.
