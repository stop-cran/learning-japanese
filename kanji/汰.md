# 汰 — sift; weed out

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

汰 is about sifting: washing things in water to sort the good from the bad, and washing away what is not needed, as in 淘汰 とうた (weeding out; culling). Kanjipedia also gives the senses of becoming muddy, of conduct that is not clean, and of living in luxury or boasting; KANJIDIC2 also lists “luxury”. [無沙汰](../words/無沙汰.md) ぶさた and [御無沙汰](../words/御無沙汰.md) ごぶさた mean a long silence: not writing or contacting someone for a while.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 氵, a variant of [水](水.md), "water," stands on the left. On the right is [太](太.md), "thick; plump," with [大](大.md), "big, large," at its top and the dot shape 丶 at its bottom. Picture a thick sieve shaken in water until only the big pieces are left and the rest is washed away. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or 太 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists タ only, with 沙汰 さた, first glossed “affair; state; incident; issue; matter”. Digital Daijisen gives only the on reading タ.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 汰, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These eleven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [択](択.md) (“select; opt for”) and [選](選.md) (“choose”): sorting the good from the bad; 淘汰 can also mean “selection”.
- [洗](洗.md) (“wash”) and [濯](濯.md) (“wash; rinse”): the sifting is done by washing in water.
- [捨](捨.md) (“discard; abandon”) and [除](除.md) (“remove; exclude”): washing away what is not needed, as in 淘汰, which can mean “weeding out” or “culling”.
- [抜](抜.md) (“extract”) and [抽](抽.md) (“pull out; extract; abstract”): picking out; 抜く ぬく can mean “to weed” or “to pick out; to choose; to select; to extract”, and 抽出 ちゅうしゅつ can mean “selection (from a group); sampling”.
- [濁](濁.md) (“muddy; cloudy; impure; voiced”) and [汚](汚.md) (“dirty; defile”): the senses of becoming muddy and of conduct that is not clean.
- [誇](誇.md) (“pride; boast”): the sense of boasting.

The [沙](沙.md) card (“sand; sift”) declares the 汰/沙 pair, because Kanjipedia gives both characters the sense よなげる, sifting by washing in water.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 縫 (“sew; stitch”), 磨 (“polish; grind”), and 塗 (“paint; coat”); sewing, polishing and painting are crafts or surface work, and none means sifting or weeding out.
