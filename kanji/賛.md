# 賛 — approve; praise

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

賛 belongs to positive responses: agreeing with a proposal or praising something.
[賛成](../words/賛成.md) さんせい expresses agreement, while 称賛 しょうさん is praise or admiration.
Approval of a plan and praise for an achievement are related, but not identical, acts.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the two 夫 shapes above 貝 as two people giving their approval.
This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a statement about historical character formation.

## Notes

[反対](../words/反対.md) はんたい is opposition, the useful contrast to 賛成.
Compare the doubled upper shapes with [費](費.md), [資](資.md), and [責](責.md).
They all end in 貝, but that shared part does not make their meanings interchangeable.
