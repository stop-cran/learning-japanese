# 凪 — calm seas; lull

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 几 (no. 16) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凪 concerns a lull in the wind and calm conditions at sea. The noun
[凪](../words/凪.md) なぎ names the calm; the verb
[凪ぐ](../words/凪ぐ.md) なぐ describes the wind or sea becoming calm.
This is not a general translation of every English use of "calm."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture the outer frame of
[風](風.md), "wind," with [止](止.md), "stop," inside: the wind has stopped.
The indexing radical is 几, the frame, not the whole character 風.

[Digital Daijisen](https://kotobank.jp/word/凪) identifies 凪 as a
**kokuji**, a character created in Japan. The mnemonic above is a memory
aid for its modern shape, not a detailed historical derivation.

## Common words

- [凪](../words/凪.md) なぎ — calm at sea; lull
- [凪ぐ](../words/凪ぐ.md) なぐ — become calm (wind/sea)

For recognition, 朝凪 (あさなぎ) is the morning calm over the sea, and
夕凪 (ゆうなぎ) is the evening calm. These are whole-word readings.

## Notes

- Distinguish the noun なぎ from the dictionary-form verb なぐ:
  凪です (なぎです), "it is calm at sea," versus 風が凪ぐ
  (かぜがなぐ), "the wind dies down."
- [風](風.md) concerns wind or style, not the lull itself.
  [凡](凡.md) has a dot inside its frame and concerns the ordinary or
  general; [凹](凹.md) describes a concave or sunken shape.
- Grade 9 is the source's name-use classification, not a claim that 凪
  occurs only in names. The weather noun and verb above are not names.
