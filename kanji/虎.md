# 虎 — tiger

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 虍 (no. 141; the upper and left enclosing shape) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

虎 means **tiger**. The native animal word is [虎](../words/虎.md) とら.
In colloquial usage, とら can also name a drunk person; context separates that sense from the animal.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the enclosing 虍 shape as a tiger's head and body,
with the two spreading lower strokes as its legs. This is a memory aid, not a verified reconstruction of the character's origin.

## Notes

- Compare the 虍-shaped upper part of [劇](劇.md) with 虎. 劇 has a different lower block and a blade-shaped right side; its meaning is drama or a play, not tiger.
- 虎穴 is often translated figuratively as "a lion's den" in English. That idiomatic translation does not make 虎 the Japanese word for lion.
- The existing animal-word article retains its separately sourced N3 vocabulary label. A kanji's N1 label does not assign every word containing it to N1.
- Both dictionary readings are preserved. This card's reading questions select only とら with its linked animal example; コ remains available for compound recognition.
