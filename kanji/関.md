# 関 — involve; barrier

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 門 (no. 169) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

関 appears both in connections and in barriers. [関係](../words/関係.md) かんけい means a relationship or connection,
and 関わる かかわる means being involved in something. 関所 せきしょ names a checkpoint.
Learn these word meanings without assuming a checkpoint is a literal part of every relationship.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the crossed strokes inside 門 to picture a checkpoint gate.
This is only a visual learning aid.
