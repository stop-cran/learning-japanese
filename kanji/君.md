# 君 — you; -kun

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

君 appears in personal address and in words for rulers.
[君](../words/君.md) きみ is a familiar "you," not a neutral replacement for every English "you."
After a name, 君 can instead be the suffix くん.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper 尹-shaped strokes over a 口 calling to someone.
This is a modern memory cue, not a historical analysis.

## Common words

- [君](../words/君.md) きみ — you, with interpersonal restrictions
- 君 くん — suffix after a name
- 君主 くんしゅ — sovereign; monarch

## Notes

JMdict labels きみ familiar and describes reference to someone of equal or lower status.
The suffix くん has its own conventions: the dictionary describes use with a male of equal or lower status,
and also use by men addressing women of lower status in formal settings.
Neither reading guarantees politeness. [右](右.md) has a much simpler top above 口.
