# 飲 — drink

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 食 (as 飠, no. 184) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound; some analyses give 欠 a sound role, which is debated) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

飲 is made of 飠 (the left form of [食](食.md), food) and 欠 (a person with the mouth wide open, as in 歌 and 吹).
Together: taking food or liquid into an open mouth, that is, drinking.
The character means "drink", and by extension "swallow" and "take (medicine)": 薬を飲む.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| イン | on | 飲食 いんしょく, 飲料水 いんりょうすい, 飲酒 いんしゅ |
| オン | on | rare, in a few older words |
| の.む | kun | [飲む](../words/飲む.md) のむ, [飲み物](../words/飲み物.md) のみもの, 飲み会 のみかい |

## Common words

- [飲む](../words/飲む.md) のむ — to drink
- [飲み物](../words/飲み物.md) のみもの — a drink, beverage
- 飲料水 いんりょうすい — drinking water

## Notes

- The kun reading の.む is the only one an N5 learner needs. For the okurigana, 飲む takes む and the noun form is 飲み (as in 飲み物 and 飲み会).
- 飲む is used for liquids but also for medicine (薬を飲む) and for smoking in older speech (煙草を飲む), although 吸う すう is normal today.
- Look-alikes: [食](食.md) (eat), with the same 飠 on the left, and 次 (next), whose right side is also 欠 but whose left side is 冫.
