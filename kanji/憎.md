# 憎 — hate; detest

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

憎 concerns hatred or detestation. 憎む にくむ means to hate, and 憎しみ にくしみ is the noun hatred. 憎悪 ぞうお names hatred or abhorrence.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 忄 beside 曽 and imagine a repeated grievance beside the heart. This is a modern-shape memory story, not a verified historical or phonetic account.

The canonical radical is 心; the left-hand shape in 憎 is 忄.
