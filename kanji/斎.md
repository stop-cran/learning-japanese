# 斎 — purification; abstinence; study room

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 文 (no. 67), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

斎 is keeping body and mind pure before a religious rite, by abstaining from certain food and acts, and by extension a quiet room set apart for that or for reading: a study. [書斎](../words/書斎.md) しょさい is a study; JMdict glosses it "study; library; den; home office; reading room."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for サイ. One example of each:

- purifying body and mind before worshipping gods or buddhas, abstinence: 斎戒 さいかい, "purification";
- carrying out a religious rite: 斎主 さいしゅ, "master of religious ceremonies";
- a room for abstinence or for reading: 書斎;
- vegetarian temple food, a monk's meal: 斎食 さいじき, "morning meal (for priests, monks, etc.)," a Buddhist term, with a second sense "food offering at a Buddhist ceremony."

Daijisen gives the kun readings いみ, いもい, いつき, いわい and とき; いもい is not a KANJIDIC2 reading. It names 斎王 and 斎宮 among words with a hard-to-guess reading, read いつきのみこ and いつきのみや; in 斎宮, 斎 is read いつき, the の is not written and 宮 is みや. JMdict has 斎王 only as さいおう, a historical term, "unmarried imperial princess serving at the Ise Grand Shrine or the Kamo Shrine in place of the Emperor," and 斎宮 as さいぐう, also a historical term.

Separate Daijisen entries cover さい【斎】, a Buddhist noun that also follows the names of rooms and the pen names of writers and artists, and とき.

Two more Daijisen headwords, い and ゆ, are written ▽斎, meaning that the official table does not list the reading for 斎, and neither is a KANJIDIC2 reading: い is a prefix meaning pure, sacred or purified, and ゆ is purification or sacredness, placed before nouns. Neither reading is asked here, and none of the three cards chosen to supply this card's wrong answers, 海, 毎 and 買, is read い or ゆ. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

斎 and [斉](斉.md) look alike and are sometimes mixed up: JMdict lists 書斉 as a search-only kanji form of 書斎, and gives 斎行 and 斉行 as spellings of the same word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [文](文.md), "sentence, writing," at the top and [示](示.md), "indicate," at the bottom, between a short stroke at each side. Imagine a page of writing laid on an altar: a quiet room where someone studies and keeps pure. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 文 or 示 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| いつ.く | kun | 斎く いつく - to worship; to enshrine; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 書斎, 書 supplies ショ and 斎 supplies サイ, with no sound change. 斎 read とき stands alone with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 斎む, 斎う and 斎く, 斎 writes い, いわ and いつ, and the final kana is okurigana. JMdict's 忌む entry has a second sense, "to detest," restricted to the spellings 忌む and 諱む, so it does not apply to 斎む. The related noun いみ, spelled 忌み, 斎み, 忌, marked irregular okurigana usage, and 斎, covers "mourning; abstinence," "taboo" and "religious purification," and as a prefix "pure; holy." JMdict also lists 斎 as a spelling of 祝い いわい. ものいみ is written 物忌み in JMdict, not with 斎.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows サイ, with 斎場, 潔斎 and 書斎 as examples, and gives the older form 齋 in parentheses. 斎場 さいじょう is "funeral hall" and "ceremony site," and 潔斎 けっさい is "religious abstinence; purification." The table lists no kun reading. Practice selects サイ, the reading with a linked word article. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [書](書.md), "write," in 書斎.
- [対](対.md), "oppose; face," has 文 at the left, [示](示.md), "indicate," sits at the bottom of 斎, and [票](票.md), "ballot; voting slip," has 示 at the bottom. [海](海.md), "sea, ocean," [毎](毎.md), "every, each," and [買](買.md), "buy," are not similar in shape; they are read カイ, マイ and バイ, on readings of two kana ending in イ like サイ. These three cards supply this card's wrong answers. All are different kanji.
- [清](清.md), "pure, clean," and [純](純.md), "pure; genuine," overlap "purification"; [室](室.md), "room, chamber," and [房](房.md), "room; tassel; bunch," overlap "study room"; and [祭](祭.md), "festival; ritual; worship," which also has 示 at the bottom and is read サイ, overlaps the religious rites of 斎 and 斎場. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "pure" or "room" as exclusive to any one character.
- The study of 書斎 is a room for reading and writing. Studying as learning is a different idea, expressed by 学ぶ まなぶ, "to learn; to study," and 研究 けんきゅう, "research; study; investigation." English "study" covers both ideas, so [学](学.md), "study," and [研](研.md), "polish; study closely," remain ordinary wrong options for 斎.
- For reference, the Daijisen examples not given above: 潔斎 also illustrates the first サイ sense, 斎場 the second, and 山斎 the third; JMdict has no entry for 山斎.
