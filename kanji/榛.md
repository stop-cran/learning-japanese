# 榛 — hazel shrub

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[榛](../words/榛.md) はしばみ names **Asian hazel**, a deciduous shrub that bears edible nuts. The same character also occurs in alder names: 榛の木 (はんのき) is Japanese alder, and 榛 (はり) is an old name for that plant. Hazel and alder are not the same species.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) on the left and 秦 (しん, the Qin dynasty name) on the right, with a 禾-shaped lower part. Imagine a woody shrub beside a grain-like shape. This is a visual memory aid, not a claim about the character's historical formation.

## Common words

- [榛](../words/榛.md) はしばみ - Asian hazel; the dictionary marks this plant name as usually written in kana
- 榛の木 はんのき - Japanese alder, a different plant
- 西洋榛 せいようはしばみ - Eurasian hazel, the species associated with cultivated hazelnuts

## Notes

- This card asks only はしばみ. Both full on and kun lists remain in their original source order; the archaic はり is available for lookup, not questions.
- The source classifies ハン as on. Learn はんのき as an established whole word; its kana alone does not establish the historical on/kun classification.
- Grade 9 is the source's name-use classification, not a claim that 榛 is usable only in personal names. The plant nouns above are non-name uses; a personal-name reading course is outside this card.
- Compare [棒](棒.md), rod or stick, whose lower-right strokes differ, and [春](春.md), the spring season, which has 日 below rather than the full right-hand shape of 榛. [稲](稲.md) names the rice plant and has 禾 on the left, not 木.
- [木](木.md) is a broader tree/wood character; it is not an unambiguous wrong meaning for a character naming a woody plant. Hazel nuts and the hazel plant should also be distinguished in context.
