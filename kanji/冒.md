# 冒 — brave danger; venture

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 冂 (the outer outline of the upper part, no. 13) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

冒 expresses braving danger or venturing despite difficulty in [冒す](../words/冒す.md) (おかす) and [冒険](../words/冒険.md) (ぼうけん), "adventure; risky venture."
It is not a general label for anything dangerous: [危険](../words/危険.md) (きけん) names danger, while 危険を冒す (きけんをおかす) means taking a risk.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture an eye, [目](目.md), beneath a boxlike upper outline, looking ahead despite danger.
This is a memory aid, not a historical sun-plus-eye explanation. The classical indexing radical is 冂, not [日](日.md), even though the upper shape can resemble 日.

- 冒頭 (ぼうとう) — beginning; opening

## Notes

Do not force the risk-taking gloss into every compound: 冒頭 names an opening, not an adventure.
冒す also has a harm/affliction sense: 病に冒される (やまいにおかされる), "be afflicted by a disease," is passive, not someone choosing to take a risk.

[犯](犯.md) also has the reading おか.す, but 犯す is used for committing an offense, as in 罪を犯す (つみをおかす), "commit a crime."
侵す (おかす) concerns invading or infringing and can overlap with 冒す in harm/affliction uses. These are useful spelling distinctions, not perfectly separate meaning compartments.
For shape comparison, [官](官.md) has a roof and a different connected lower outline; neither 官 nor 目 means braving danger.
