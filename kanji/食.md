# 食 — eat, food

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 食 (no. 184) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

食 is commonly explained as a lid or cover (亼) over a vessel of grain or cooked food, a bowl of rice with a lid. Scholars
differ about the exact lower component, so treat the picture as a plausible account rather than settled. It means "eat" and "food".
As a component on the left of other kanji it shrinks to 飠 (as in [飲](飲.md), 飯 めし, 館 かん), and signals food or eating.

## Common words

- [食べる](../words/食べる.md) たべる — to eat
- [食事](../words/食事.md) しょくじ — a meal
- 食堂 しょくどう — dining hall, canteen

## Notes

- 食べる is the polite, neutral, everyday verb; 食う is blunt and 召し上がる めしあがる is respectful.
- Look-alikes: [飲](飲.md) (drink, with 飠 on the left) and 良 (good), which looks like the lower part of 食. 飲み食い のみくい means "eating and drinking".
- When 食 is on the left side of a kanji it is written 飠, with three strokes at the top-left.
