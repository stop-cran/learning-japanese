# 漠 — desert; vague

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as left-hand 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

漠 concerns a desert or vast expanse and something indistinct.
[砂漠](../words/砂漠.md) さばく names a desert;
漠然 ばくぜん means vague or indistinct. Learn the whole words rather
than assuming a vague idea literally describes a landscape.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 氵 beside 莫, whose stacked
shape has 艹, 日 and 大. Imagine a vast scene whose outlines are difficult
to make out. This is a memory aid, not proof of either a historical
water-related origin or a phonetic component.

## Common words

- [砂漠](../words/砂漠.md) さばく - desert
- 漠然 ばくぜん - vague; indistinct
- 広漠 こうばく - vast; boundless

## Notes

- 漠然 uses forms such as 漠然と and 漠然たる; do not automatically
  make it an ordinary な-adjective. The dictionary also records 漠 with
  similar と/たる usage, rather than as the everyday noun for a desert.
- [広](広.md), wide/spacious, overlaps vastness. [荒](荒.md) offers a
  wilderness or waste-land comparison, but a desert need not be a place
  deliberately laid waste.
- [砂](砂.md) names sand, a material. A desert is a region, and not every
  desert is hot or covered with sand. The 水 radical is not a claim that
  a desert contains abundant water.
- [漢](漢.md) also has 氵 and a dense right side; [暮](暮.md) has
  莫 above another 日; [湖](湖.md) has 胡 on the right. Their Chinese,
  dusk/livelihood and lake meanings differ from 漠.
