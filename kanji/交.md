# 交 — intersect; mingle

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 亠 (no. 8) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

交 connects crossing paths with interaction. 交わる まじわる can describe lines intersecting or people associating;
[交通](../words/交通.md) こうつう concerns traffic and transportation.
The whole word determines whether the interaction is physical or social.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** follow the crossed lower strokes as two routes meeting below the cap.
This uses the visible shape for memory; it does not establish its historical origin.

## Notes

[父](父.md) lacks 交's cap, [文](文.md) has a simpler middle, and [六](六.md) lacks its crossed lower strokes.
The many kun readings in metadata belong to different words and inflections.
Do not infer that every verb pronounced まじる or まぜる must be written with 交; spelling depends on the intended word.
