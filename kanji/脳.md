# 脳 — brain; mind

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

脳 concerns the brain and mental activity. [脳](../words/脳.md) のう names the organ and can also refer to brains or the mind; 大脳 だいのう is specifically the cerebrum, not simply a large version of any brain.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the left flesh sign beside a compact control panel: the short upper marks and crossed enclosure on the right can remind you of a head's complicated contents.

The canonical key is 肉, no. 130, written in the month-like 月 form on the left. This is a flesh component, not a claim that the brain is connected with the moon.

## Notes

- Distinguish [頭](../words/頭.md) あたま, the head, from the brain inside it. Both words also have mental extensions, so this is not an absolute physical-versus-figurative division.
- [知能](../words/知能.md) ちのう concerns intellectual ability, not the anatomical organ. English brains can nevertheless overlap with that sense.
- [悩](悩.md) has a heart-related left side and concerns worry. Its similar right side does not make it another spelling of the organ.
