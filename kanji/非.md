# 非 — non-; wrong

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 非 (no. 175) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

非 can negate a category, like English "non-," or express wrongness.
In [非常口](../words/非常口.md) ひじょうぐち, 非常 means an emergency: an occasion outside the ordinary.
The whole word means "emergency exit," not "wrong entrance."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the two upright sides facing away from one another, refusing to agree.
This is an invented visual cue, not a historical account.

## Notes

非常に is an established intensifying expression; do not translate each character mechanically.
[北](北.md) has a different pair of lower strokes, while [耳](耳.md) has connecting horizontal lines.
[年](年.md) is a looser comparison of upright and crosswise strokes.
[不](不.md) also expresses "not" or "non-" in compounds; compare the whole words to see how each negative element is used.
