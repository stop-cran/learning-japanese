# 踊 — dance; hop

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 足 (no. 157) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

踊 concerns dancing and hopping movement. 踊る is the verb to dance, 踊り the dance or dancing, and 舞踊 another noun for dance.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the foot-shaped left side dancing beside the upright 甬-shaped part on the right. This is a visual association, not a historical analysis.

The indexing radical is 足, shown on the left in its narrow form ⻊.
