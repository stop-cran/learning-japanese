# 卓 — table; eminent

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 十 (no. 24), in the lower part · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

卓 connects two useful families: a table or raised stand, and standing out in
excellence. [食卓](../words/食卓.md) しょくたく is a dining table;
卓越 たくえつ means excellence or preeminence. The second is not about furniture.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a 卜-shaped top above
[早](早.md), with [十](十.md) at the bottom. Imagine an unusually tall
table standing out from the others. This is a memory picture for the current
shape, not a verified account of its origin or sound components.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| タク | on | [食卓](../words/食卓.md) しょくたく - dining table |

Practice selects タク, the sole reading in the frozen KANJIDIC2 record.
食 supplies ショク in 食卓. In 卓球 たっきゅう, table tennis,
the compound has a small っ; do not pronounce it mechanically as たくきゅう.

Daijisen and JMdict also record 卓 しょく, including a Buddhist offering
table and a tabletop incense-burner abbreviation. ショク is outside this
card's frozen metadata; it is not a replacement for タク in 食卓.

## Notes

- [机](机.md), desk/writing table, and [台](台.md), stand, overlap the
  furniture sense. [高](高.md), high, [優](優.md), superior, and
  [超](超.md), surpass, overlap height or excellence, not every use of 卓.
- The eminence branch also overlaps [偉](偉.md), great, [英](英.md),
  outstanding, [著](著.md), remarkable, and [冠](冠.md), peerless.
  [凌](凌.md) and [越](越.md) share surpassing or exceeding with 超.
  These direct pairs are excluded from meaning choices; their Japanese
  words are not interchangeable with 卓越 or 卓抜.
- English *table* can also mean a chart, as with [表](表.md). That English
  ambiguity does not make a dining table and a data table the same Japanese word.
- [日](日.md), sun/day, resembles the central box but lacks the top
  and bottom additions. [車](車.md), car/vehicle, has a vertical stroke
  through its central box; [十](十.md), ten, is only the lower cross.
  These are shape comparisons, not meanings of 卓.
