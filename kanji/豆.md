# 豆 — bean; pea

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 豆 (no. 151) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

豆 is a bean or pea. KANJIDIC2 gives “beans,” “pea” and “midget,” and Kanjipedia's first sense calls it the general name for plants of the legume family and one of the five grains (マメ科の植物の総称。五穀の一つ。). The declaring word [豆](../words/豆.md) まめ is a noun that JMdict glosses “legume (esp. edible legumes or their seeds, e.g. beans, peas, pulses); bean; pea,” with a second sense “soya bean (Glycine max); soybean; soy.” The same JMdict entry (1450030) also lists まめ as a prefix meaning “small; miniature; baby; midget; small-scale” or “child,” as in 豆電球 まめでんきゅう, “miniature light bulb; midget lamp.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 豆 contains [口](口.md), "mouth." Picture an open mouth waiting for a spoonful of beans. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 口 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists トウ (豆腐, 納豆 なっとう), ズ (大豆) and まめ. Its トウ line also gives 小豆 (あずき), “adzuki bean (Vigna angularis)”; JMdict 1348560 marks the reading あずき as gikun or jukujikun, a special reading of the whole word, so it is not a reading of 豆 alone.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings ど and ま. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.
- Kanjipedia lists たかつき outside the table (外), for its second sense, a footed vessel for serving food (食物を盛る脚のついた器); no checked JMdict word uses たかつき for 豆. Its third sense abbreviates the old province 伊豆 いず (JMdict 2845049).

These eleven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [穀](穀.md) (“grain; cereals”) and [粒](粒.md) (“grain; small particle”): Kanjipedia calls 豆 one of the five grains (五穀の一つ), and JMdict 五穀 (1268260) is “the five grains (wheat, rice, beans, millet (awa and kibi)).”
- [種](種.md) (“seed; kind”): JMdict's first sense of 豆 is “legume (esp. edible legumes or their seeds, e.g. beans, peas, pulses); bean; pea,” which includes the seeds.
- [小](小.md) (“small, little”), [寸](寸.md) (“sun (length unit); small”) and [微](微.md) (“minute; faint”): KANJIDIC2 gives 豆 “midget,” and the JMdict prefix sense is “small; miniature; baby; midget; small-scale.”
- [子](子.md) (“child”), [児](児.md) (“child; youngster”), [童](童.md) (“child; juvenile”), [幼](幼.md) (“early childhood; immature”) and [稚](稚.md) (“immature; childish; young”): JMdict's prefix senses of 豆 include “baby” and “child.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 芋 (“tuber; yam”), 卵 (“egg; ovum”), and 塩 (“salt”); none means a bean or pea, or something small or childlike.
