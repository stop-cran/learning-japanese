# 三 — three

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

三 is an indicative character: three horizontal lines for the number three, continuing [一](一.md) and [二](二.md).
Write the lines with the middle one shortest and the bottom one longest. Its key is 一, not 二.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| サン | on | [三月](../words/三月.md) さんがつ, 三回 さんかい, 三階 さんがい, 三十 さんじゅう |
| ゾウ | on | rare; not needed at N5 (三和土 たたき is a special whole-word reading) |
| み, み.つ | kun | 三日月 みかづき (crescent moon) |
| みっ.つ | kun | 三つ みっつ (three things) |

Special readings: [三日](../words/三日.md) みっか (3rd day / three days).

## Common words

- [三日](../words/三日.md) みっか — the 3rd; three days
- [三月](../words/三月.md) さんがつ — March
- [三百](../words/三百.md) さんびゃく — 300
- [三千](../words/三千.md) さんぜん — 3,000
- 三つ みっつ — three (things)

## Notes

Sound changes after 三 in counters: 三本 さんぼん, 三杯 さんばい, 三百 さんびゃく (ヒャク → ビャク), 三千 さんぜん (セン → ゼン), 三階 さんがい. People: 一人 ひとり and 二人 ふたり are special, but from three on it is regular: 三人 さんにん.
The look-alike 王 (king) has a vertical stroke through three lines.
