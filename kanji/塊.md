# 塊 — lump; clod; mass

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

塊 is a lump: material stuck together in one mass, such as earth, ice or metal, and by extension a bunch of people or a person who embodies a quality. [塊](../words/塊.md) かたまり is a lump or mass. JMdict glosses 塊 かたまり "lump; mass; bundle; clump; clod; cluster," then "group; crowd" and "embodiment (of an idea, quality, feeling etc.); personification."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for カイ, a lump, with examples such as 塊状 かいじょう, "massive; bulk; block-like; aggregated; agglomerated," 金塊 きんかい, "gold nugget; gold bullion; gold bar; gold ingot," 血塊 けっかい, "blood clot," 山塊 さんかい, "mountain mass; massif," 団塊 だんかい, "mass; lump; clod; clump," and 氷塊 ひょうかい, "lump of ice; block of ice; ice floe." It lists the kun readings かたまり and つちくれ and does not list ケ. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

The entry also names 石塊 among words with a hard-to-guess reading: いしくれ, "(small) stone; rock; piece of stone," which JMdict marks as a dated term, and いしころ, "(small) stone; rock; pebble," for which JMdict marks 石塊 as a rarely used spelling beside 石ころ. The Daijisen has a separate headword くれ, a lump, used mostly after another noun, as in 土くれ; KANJIDIC2 does not list くれ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [土](土.md), "earth, soil," at the left, and at the right 鬼, a shape that is also the kanji for a demon or ogre, which has no card yet. Imagine an ogre squeezing a handful of earth into one hard lump. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 土 or 鬼 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カイ | on | 金塊 きんかい - gold nugget; gold bullion; gold bar; gold ingot; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ケ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; the Daijisen character entry and the official table do not give it, and JMdict has no entry in which 塊 is read け |
| かたまり | kun | [塊](../words/塊.md) かたまり — lump; mass |
| つちくれ | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2 and in the Daijisen character entry; JMdict spells つちくれ 土塊 or 土くれ, "lump of earth; clod," and has no entry writing it with 塊 alone |

The word 塊 is the kanji alone, read かたまり, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict also lists the spelling 固まり for the same word. In 金塊, 金 supplies キン and 塊 supplies カイ. In 土塊 read つちくれ, 土 supplies つち and 塊 supplies くれ, so that word does not show 塊 alone read つちくれ; JMdict also reads 土塊 どかい, with ド and カイ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カイ, with 塊状 and 山塊 as examples, and かたまり, with 塊. ケ and つちくれ are not in the table. Practice selects かたまり, the reading with a linked word article. カイ is the reading of most 塊 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; ケ and つちくれ have no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The common word 塊 contains only this kanji.
- [魅](魅.md), "charm; bewitch," contains the same 鬼 shape, with [未](未.md), "not yet," at the right. [城](城.md), "castle," and [場](場.md), "place, site," have the same 土 at the left with a different right side. All are different kanji.
- [団](団.md), "group; association," [固](固.md), "firm; harden," [群](群.md), "flock; cluster; crowd," [束](束.md), "bundle," [凝](凝.md), "congeal; become absorbed," and [組](組.md), "group; assemble," overlap the senses "lump," "mass," "cluster," "bundle," "group" and "clot" that 塊 and its words carry; 団 also has the kun reading かたまり and appears with 塊 in 団塊, 固 writes the other spelling 固まり, and 凝 appears in 凝塊 ぎょうかい, "clot." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "lump" or "mass" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the カイ sense: 塊根 かいこん, "tuberous root"; 土塊; and 肉塊 にくかい, "lump of meat; piece of flesh."
