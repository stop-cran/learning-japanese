# 治 — govern; heal

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

治 brings together two useful areas: governing or settling affairs, and curing an illness.
[政治](../words/政治.md) せいじ means politics; 治る (なおる) means to recover or heal.
Do not choose the English meaning from the character alone: the whole word decides.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 台-shaped platform beside the water strokes as a place
from which someone restores order. This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

- 治める おさめる — govern; settle
- 治る なおる — recover from illness
- 治す なおす — cure; heal something

## Notes

Compare 病気が治る (びょうきがなおる), "an illness gets better," with 病気を治す
(びょうきをなおす), "cure an illness." The first describes recovery; the second takes the illness as an object.
[始](始.md) has the same 台-shaped right side, but its left side is 女, not 氵.
