# 右 — right

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **Phonetic:** none given · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

右 means "right" (the side, direction or hand) and, by extension, "the above-mentioned" in vertical writing, where 右 refers to what was written to the right.
It is usually analysed as a hand (the top part, a form of 又) over 口. The early forms are a hand, and the 口 was probably added
later; what it originally stood for (the mouth, a container) is debated. As a mnemonic: the right hand brings food to the mouth.

## Notes

Its opposite is [左](左.md). The pair is easy to mix up: 右 is written with the diagonal stroke (ノ) first and then the horizontal, and has 口 at the bottom;
in 左 the horizontal comes first and 工 is at the bottom.
Do not confuse 右 with 石 (stone, いし), where the diagonal starts at the left end of the top horizontal (厂 over 口) and the stroke order starts with the horizontal,
nor with [古](古.md) (old), which has 十 over 口.
