# 妖 — bewitching; supernatural

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list; Kanshudo)

## Meaning and origin

妖 appears in words for supernatural beings and mysterious allure. [妖しい](../words/妖しい.md)
あやしい can suggest bewitching beauty; 妖怪 ようかい names supernatural beings, not simply beautiful people.
The character also has a calamity sense in character dictionaries.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** remember 女 beside 夭 as a figure with an otherworldly outline.
The top stroke of 夭 slopes; it is not the horizontal top of [天](天.md).

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヨウ | on | 妖怪 ようかい — supernatural being; 妖精 ようせい — fairy |
| あや.しい | kun | [妖しい](../words/妖しい.md) あやしい — mysterious; bewitching |
| なま.めく | kun | Dictionary reading なまめく — look alluring; the verified ordinary word spelling is 艶めく なまめく |
| わざわ.い | kun | Dictionary reading わざわい — calamity; compare the ordinary spelling 災い わざわい — misfortune |

KANJIDIC2 retains the last two readings. The inspected JMdict entries for なまめく and わざわい do not
list 妖 as a spelling, so learn these as character-dictionary readings, not recommendations to write
everyday words with 妖. Kanjipedia also describes the alluring and calamity meanings, but lists
なまめかしい rather than KANJIDIC2's なまめく.

## Common words

- [妖しい](../words/妖しい.md) あやしい — bewitching; mysterious
- 妖怪 ようかい — supernatural being; yokai
- 妖精 ようせい — fairy

## Notes

[妹](妹.md) has 未 instead of 夭. [笑](笑.md) has a similar lower 夭 shape but a bamboo top.
Do not change the sloping top stroke into 天.

For 妖しい compared with [怪しい](../words/怪しい.md), and the limits of spelling-based distinctions,
see [strange, suspicious, and rare](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
