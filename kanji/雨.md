# 雨 — rain

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 雨 (no. 173) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

雨 is a pictograph: the top line is the sky, the frame 冂 is a cloud, and the four dots are drops falling from it. The form was squared off over time.
As a radical it forms weather words: [電](電.md) (lightning, electricity), 雪 (snow), 雲 (cloud) and 雷 (thunder).

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ウ | on | 雨天 うてん, 豪雨 ごうう, 梅雨 ばいう／つゆ (special) |
| あめ | kun | [雨](../words/雨.md) あめ, 雨の日 あめのひ, 大雨 おおあめ |
| あま- | kun | 雨傘 あまがさ, 雨戸 あまど, 雨水 あまみず |
| -さめ | kun | 小雨 こさめ, 春雨 はるさめ (voiced from あめ) |

Special reading: 梅雨 is つゆ (rainy season), and also ばいう in the on reading.

## Common words

- [雨](../words/雨.md) あめ — rain
- 大雨 おおあめ — heavy rain
- 雨具 あまぐ — rain gear

## Notes

Compare 両 (りょう, both): 雨 has a roof line with four dots and a hook; 両 has no dots.
雨 is the top of 電 (lightning, electricity), 雪, 雲, 雷 — useful for spotting weather vocabulary.
