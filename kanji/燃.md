# 燃 — burn; blaze

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

燃 concerns burning, blazing, or being fired up with emotion. Its native verbs distinguish something burning from someone burning it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture [火](火.md) at the left beside [然](然.md), whose lower dots also look like fire: imagine a blaze spreading across the shape.

## Notes

- 燃える is intransitive ichidan; 燃やす and 燃す are transitive godan: 燃えて もえて, 燃やして もやして, 燃して もして.
- The exact もす reading belongs to 燃す, not 燃やす. Do not replace the written ending while keeping the same pronunciation.
- The fire image also applies to enthusiasm and other strong feelings. Compare [焼く](../words/焼く.md) and [焼ける](../words/焼ける.md), which can describe burning as well as cooking.
