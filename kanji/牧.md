# 牧 — pasture; raise livestock; pastor

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 牛 (as 牜, no. 93), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

牧 is grazing and keeping livestock, and by extension a place for it. The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ボク. One example of each:

- Graze livestock: [牧場](../words/牧場.md) ぼくじょう, JMdict's "farm (livestock); stock farm; ranch; station" and "pasture; meadow; grazing land."
- Govern: 牧師 ぼくし, "pastor; minister; clergyman; reverend."
- A governing official: 州牧 しゅうぼく, a historical term for a governor.

Other words for the first sense are in Notes. [牧畜](../words/牧畜.md) ぼくちく is "stock-farming; livestock farming; cattle breeding."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [牛](牛.md), "cattle, cow," in its left-hand form 牜, beside 攵 at the right, which has no card here. Imagine a cow with a herder beside it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| まき | kun | [牧場](../words/牧場.md) まきば — pasture; meadow; grazing land; 牧 まき — JMdict's "pasture; grazing land"; the まき reading is not selected as a separate reading |

In 牧場, [場](場.md), "place, site," supplies ジョウ, and the same entry is also read まきば. In 牧畜, [畜](畜.md), "livestock; domestic animals," supplies チク. In 牧師, [師](師.md), "teacher; specialist," supplies シ. In 放牧 ほうぼく, "pasturage; grazing," 放 supplies ホウ and 牧 is the second element. 牧歌 ぼっか, "pastoral song," is a whole word in which ボク sounds ぼっ; it is 牧歌的 ぼっかてき in its adjective form. The reading is sound-changed, not a separate reading of 牧.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ボク with 牧場, 牧師 and 遊牧, and まき with 牧場. Practice selects ボク, the reading of the linked word articles. まき is shown with a dictionary example and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the linked words are 場 (ジョウ) in 牧場 and 畜 (チク) in 牧畜.
- [歌](歌.md), "song, sing," appears in 牧歌, which makes it a plausible wrong answer. [山](山.md), "mountain," and [雨](雨.md), "rain," suit a scene of pasture land and supply this card's other wrong answers. They are different kanji with unrelated readings and meanings.
- Livestock, farm and animal kanji overlap with the first sense: [畜](畜.md), "livestock; domestic animals," [養](養.md), "nourish; raise; adopt," [育](育.md), "grow up; raise," [農](農.md), "agriculture; farming," [牛](牛.md), "cattle, cow," [馬](馬.md), "horse," [羊](羊.md), "sheep," [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," and [野](野.md), "open field." The Daijisen examples of that sense also include 牧草 ぼくそう, "pasture; grass," 牧童 ぼくどう and 牧羊 ぼくよう, 遊牧 and 放牧.
- The title's "pastor" overlaps with [僧](僧.md), "Buddhist monk; priest," [坊](坊.md), "Buddhist priest; boy," and [師](師.md), "teacher; specialist." All twelve kanji named in the last two bullets are kept out of this card's quiz options. The Daijisen "govern" and official senses are shared with [治](治.md), "govern; heal," [御](御.md), "honorific; govern," [官](官.md), "government official," [司](司.md), "take charge; official; preside," and [導](導.md), "guide; conduct," but those senses are not in this card's title, so these five are not excluded.
