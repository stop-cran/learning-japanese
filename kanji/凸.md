# 凸 — convex; protruding

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 凵 (no. 17) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凸 describes a surface projecting outward: **convex**. Its native reading でこ also occurs for a forehead or bump. Contrast [凹](凹.md), which points inward or downward.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The raised middle of 凸 looks like a bump rising above the surrounding surface.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| トツ | on | 凸レンズ とつレンズ - convex lens; 凹凸 おうとつ - unevenness |
| でこ | kun | [凸凹](../words/でこぼこ.md) でこぼこ - unevenness; 凸 でこ - forehead; bump (usually written in kana) |

## Common words

- [凸凹](../words/でこぼこ.md) でこぼこ - uneven and bumpy
- 凸レンズ とつレンズ - convex lens
- 凹凸 おうとつ - unevenness; disparity

## Notes

- 凸凹 でこぼこ combines raised and sunken parts. Its dictionary family also records とつおう for physical unevenness, but restricts the imbalance/difference sense to **でこぼこ**. The linked kana-headed article retains its primary でこぼこ identity.
- 凹凸 おうとつ has the reverse written order. Learn each whole spelling with its reading rather than reversing sounds by analogy.
- [回](回.md), revolve or times, and [同](同.md), same, are visual comparisons, not words for a projecting surface.
