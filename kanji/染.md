# 染 — dye; stain; infect; permeate

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

染 is coloring something by soaking it, and can describe color or another influence soaking in. [伝染](../words/伝染.md) でんせん is “contagion; infection”; [汚染](../words/汚染.md) おせん is “pollution; contamination.” These compounds apply the idea beyond literal dyeing: something can spread or affect something without being colored.

Digital Daijisen gives two senses for セン: infusing color, as in 染色 せんしょく, “dyeing”; and color attaching or soaking in, including receiving an influence, as in 伝染 and 汚染.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the water form 氵 at the upper left, [九](九.md) at the upper right and [木](木.md) along the bottom. Imagine color carried in water soaking into wood. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵, 九 or 木 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 伝染, 伝 supplies デン and 染 supplies セン; in 汚染, 汚 supplies オ and 染 supplies セン. In 染める, 染 writes そ and める is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the same pattern gives 染まる. In 愛染 あいぜん, 染 is read ゼン, an on reading outside the Joyo table.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セン with 染色, 染料 and 汚染; そめる with 染める and 染め物; そまる with 染まる; しみる with 染みる and 油染みる; and しみ with 染み and 染み抜き. 染料 せんりょう means dye, dyestuff or coloring agent; 染め物 そめもの means dyeing or dyed goods; 油染みる あぶらじみる means to become greasy or oil-stained; and 染み抜き しみぬき means stain removal. Practice selects セン, the reading used by both linked articles. The other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; being outside the practice set does not make them invalid. ゼン is recorded by Digital Daijisen and Kanjipedia but is not in the KANJIDIC2 metadata list.

## Notes

- The other kanji in 伝染 is [伝](伝.md), “transmit; convey”; the other kanji in 汚染 is [汚](汚.md), “dirty; defile.”
- [木](木.md) is at the bottom, 水 appears in its three-stroke form 氵 at the upper left, and 九 is at the upper right. All are different kanji from 染.
- JMdict also has the suffix entry じみる, with 染みる marked as a rarely used kanji form and the word usually written using kana alone. This is not a KANJIDIC2 reading of standalone 染.
- [色](色.md) overlaps dye color; [透](透.md) overlaps permeation; [汚](汚.md) overlaps staining or contamination; [伝](伝.md) and [病](病.md) overlap contagion or infection; [塗](塗.md) overlaps painting or coating; and [吸](吸.md) overlaps soaking or absorption. They are kept out of this card’s quiz options. Do not treat “dye,” “stain” or “permeate” as exclusive to any one character.
