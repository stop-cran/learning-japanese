# 辛 — spicy; painful

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 辛 (no. 160) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

辛 concerns a spicy taste, pain or hardship. It also names an eighth calendar sign; the calendar use is a separate identity, not a kind of flavor.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 立-like top above a crossed vertical as a warning sign for a painfully hot taste. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not an account of the character's original object.

辛 is itself the indexing radical, no. 160.

## Notes

- 辛い からい and 辛い つらい have separate entries. The latter is marked usually written in kana, while the linked article primarily teaches the spicy からい reading. Context determines the identity.
- The suffix -づらい attaches to a verb's -ます stem: 見る gives 見 + づらい, 見辛い みづらい, hard to see. It covers physical or emotional difficulty and is usually written in kana; づらい is not a free-standing adjective to substitute for つらい.
- かのと belongs to the separate calendar entry. Do not use that reading for the spicy or painful adjective.
- Compare [幸](幸.md), happiness or good fortune. Similar crossing strokes do not make it another spelling of 辛.
