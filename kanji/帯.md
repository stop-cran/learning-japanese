# 帯 — sash; zone

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

帯 connects a sash or band with an extended belt or zone. 帯 おび is an obi or sash, while 熱帯 ねったい names the tropics. 帯びる おびる can mean to carry or bear something, including having a quality.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the broad upper section over 巾 and imagine a band holding hanging fabric together. This is a modern-shape story, not a verified derivation.

## Notes

- The noun おび and the verb おびる are different words. The verb's written ending is びる; 帯 supplies お in that spelling.
- 熱帯 ねったい has a small っ. Learn the complete reading rather than pronouncing each source reading separately.
