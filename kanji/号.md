# 号 — number; call

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

号 can mark a number, name, or designation; it also has a calling sense.
[番号](../words/番号.md) ばんごう is a useful starting word: a number used to identify something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 口 box calling out an identifying number above the bent lower stroke.
This is a visual story for memory, not etymology.

## Notes

A number in 番号 labels something; it need not count a quantity.

**Compared with [番](番.md) and ordinal [目](目.md):** 号 is used in conventional numbered designations such as 三号室 (さんごうしつ), "room number three."
That need not be 三番目の部屋 (さんばんめのへや), "the third room" in the order you encounter them.
番 can mark either identification or order: 一番 (いちばん) can mean "number one" or "first," depending on context.
Adding 目 makes the sequence position explicit: 一番目 (いちばんめ), "first."
Ordinal 目 also follows other counting expressions, as in 三人目 (さんにんめ), "third person."
号 can express order too, so learn the established expression rather than treating 番, 号, and 番目 as interchangeable.

In [台](台.md) the 口 is at the bottom, while in 号 it is at the top.
[古](古.md) likewise has its box below another element.
The kun readings remain in metadata; this introductory table concentrates on ゴウ compounds.
