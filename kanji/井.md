# 井 — well; well frame

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 二 (no. 7), as the two horizontal strokes · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

井 is a well, a hole dug to draw water, and by extension the square frame around its mouth and a place where people live together. [井戸](../words/井戸.md) いど is a water well. JMdict glosses the noun 井 い simply "well."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for セイ. One example of each:

- a well: 油井 ゆせい, "oil well";
- a place where houses gather: 市井 しせい, "the street; the town";
- being neatly divided: 井然 せいぜん, "orderly; regular; systematic; well-organized," a spelling JMdict lists after 整然;
- the shape of 井: 井目 せいもく, "the nine principal points (on a go board); star points," a spelling JMdict lists after 星目.

For ショウ it gives only the shape of 井, as in 天井 てんじょう, "ceiling." Its kun reading is い, as in KANJIDIC2. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [二](二.md), "two," as the two horizontal strokes, crossed by two vertical strokes. Imagine looking down into a well from above, its mouth boxed in by four crossed wooden beams. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 二 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 井戸, 井 supplies い and 戸 supplies と, voiced to ど; this voicing of the first sound of a later element is called rendaku. In 天井, 天 supplies テン and 井 supplies ショウ, voiced to じょう. In 油井, 油 supplies ユ and 井 supplies セイ. None of these words has okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セイ, with 油井 and 市井 as examples; ショウ, with 天井; and い, with 井戸. Practice selects い, the reading with a linked word article; セイ and ショウ are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. 天井 is a common word, but no word article uses it yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [戸](戸.md), "door; counter for households," in 井戸.
- [耕](耕.md), "till; plough," has 井 at the right. [互](互.md), "mutual," is filed under the same radical 二, and [二](二.md), "two," is the pair of horizontal strokes in 井. All are different kanji.
- [泉](泉.md), "spring; water source," and [額](額.md), "forehead; frame; sum," overlap the senses "well," a water source, and "frame" that 井 carries. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "well" or "frame" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first セイ sense, a well: 井蛙 せいあ, "frog in a well; someone with a narrow perspective," and 鑿井 さくせい, "well drilling."
