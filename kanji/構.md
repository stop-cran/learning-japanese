# 構 — build; stance

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

構 appears in words about building an arrangement and taking a position.
[構成](../words/構成.md) (こうせい) is the composition or arrangement of parts.
構える (かまえる) can mean to set something up or hold something ready.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** treat the many horizontal bars to the right of 木 as a framework being assembled.
This is a modern visual association, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

カメラを構える (カメラをかまえる) means "hold a camera ready."
This is preparation or positioning, not necessarily construction of a physical building.
Compare [横](横.md): both are dense 木-left characters, but 横 has the 黄 shape on the right.
