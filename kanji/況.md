# 況 — situation; circumstances

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

況 concerns a situation or circumstances. 状況 じょうきょう names a state of affairs, while 不況 ふきょう concerns an economic depression or recession. The separate expression 況んや いわんや introduces an emphatic comparison such as much more or let alone, depending on context.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside 兄 and imagine checking the conditions of water beside a person. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not verified etymology.

The canonical radical 水, no. 85, appears as left-side 氵.

KANJIDIC2 also records `まし.て` and `おもむき`. JMdict attests 況して まして, still more or still less according to the construction, marking that kanji spelling rare and the expression usually written in kana. Its written して does not establish the exact まし.て boundary, which would put only て after the character. No suitable word example for that exact boundary or for おもむき was established in the inspected snapshot. These remain qualified character-dictionary lookup readings, not a claim that the attested expression まして is absent.

## Notes

- 況んや is marked usually written in kana in JMdict. Recognize the dictionary spelling without treating it as the default way to write an everyday standalone situation noun.
- The linked 状況 article explains circumstances without saying they must be bad. 不況 is the separate economic word; its negative meaning is not inherent in every use of 況.
- キョウ belongs inside words such as 状況. The native lookup reading おもむき is not a replacement reading for that compound.
- Compare [兄](兄.md), older brother, without the water dots, and [咲](咲.md), bloom, with 口 on the left and a different right-hand shape.
