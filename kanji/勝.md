# 勝 — victory

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

勝 expresses winning or prevailing. [勝つ](../words/勝つ.md) かつ is the verb "win";
勝利 しょうり names a victory.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** spot 力 at the lower right as the strength for a win.
The left side looks like 月, but this memory cue makes no claim about historical components.

## Notes

試合に勝つ (しあいにかつ) means "win a match." 勝つ changes to 勝った かった in the past.
The opposing result is [負ける](../words/負ける.md) まける, "lose."
Distinguish the busy right side from [服](服.md); the similar left outline does not imply similar meanings.
