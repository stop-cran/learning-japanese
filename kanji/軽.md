# 軽 — lightweight; slight

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

軽 concerns lightness in weight, a slight degree or treating something lightly. 軽い describes lightness; 軽減 names a reduction, and 軽んじる means to make light of something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 車 on the left beside 又 over 土, and imagine a vehicle so light it can be lifted with one hand. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology.

## Notes

- 軽忽 きょうこつ supplies キョウ in a complete dictionary word. 剽軽 ひょうきん supplies キン and is marked usually written in kana. Neither example is a rule for changing every ケイ compound to those readings.
- 軽やか has both かろやか and かるやか in its entry. The displayed かろやか supports KANJIDIC2's exact かろ.やか row; it is not inferred from 軽い.
- 軽んじる is an ichidan verb, distinct from the i-adjective 軽い and the na-adjective 軽やか. A figurative act of belittling is not literal low weight.
