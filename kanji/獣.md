# 獣 — beast; animal

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (no. 94), on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

獣 concerns **beasts or animals**. The native noun often brings mammals to
mind, but compounds need their own definitions: [獣医](../words/獣医.md)
じゅうい is a veterinarian, not a doctor restricted to wild beasts.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a dog-shaped 犬 beside a
stack with three small upper strokes, a 田-shaped box, a horizontal line and
a lower 口-shaped box. The dog helps you remember the animal meaning.
This is a cue for the present shape, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

## Readings

All three source readings remain in metadata. Questions select ジュウ and
けもの; けだもの is taught as an alternate reading with a usage warning,
not as a missing, obsolete or exclusively insulting word.

## Notes

[単](単.md) and [戦](戦.md) share a small-stroke upper area and dense box-like
parts, but 獣 has a lower 口-shaped box and 犬 on the right.
[営](営.md) has stacked boxes without that right-hand 犬.
Their single/simple, battle and business meanings are not animal meanings.

For the broad neutral category "animal," use [動物](../words/動物.md) どうぶつ.
けもの and けだもの overlap in their animal sense; do not invent a strict
animal-versus-person division between the pronunciations. In particular,
けだもの applied to a person can be strongly insulting.
