# 期 — period; term

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 月 (no. 74) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

期 helps divide time into periods or terms.
[期間](../words/期間.md) きかん refers to a span of time, and 学期 がっき is a school term.
Think of a stretch with boundaries rather than a single clock reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 其 beside 月 and imagine marking a stretch of dates on a calendar.
This is only a memory aid; it does not establish why the character was historically formed this way.

## Notes

In がっき, the small っ marks a held consonant before き; do not pronounce it がくき.
[朝](朝.md), [服](服.md) and [明](明.md) also contain 月.
Compare the morning sense of 朝, the clothing sense of 服, and the brightness sense of 明 with the period sense of 期.
The 月 radical does not mean that every period expressed with 期 lasts a month.
