# 凹 — concave; sunken

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 凵 (no. 17) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凹 describes a hollow or a surface that sinks inward: **concave**. Contrast [凸](凸.md), convex or projecting outward.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The dip in the top of 凹 looks like a dent. Use that visible hollow to remember the inward direction.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| オウ | on | 凹レンズ おうレンズ - concave lens; 凹凸 おうとつ - unevenness; disparity |
| くぼ.む | kun | 凹む くぼむ, also written 窪む - cave in; become hollow or sunken |
| へこ.む | kun | [凹む](../words/凹む.md) へこむ - become dented; colloquially feel down |
| ぼこ | kun, bound use | [凸凹](../words/でこぼこ.md) でこぼこ - unevenness; raised and sunken parts together |

The linked 凹む article teaches **へこむ** as its primary reading. The same written form has a separate **くぼむ** dictionary family; do not transfer every emotional or defeat sense of へこむ to くぼむ.

## Common words

## Notes

- 凸凹 でこぼこ and 凹凸 おうとつ reverse the character order. They are related words, not freely interchangeable readings of one spelling.
- In 凸凹 でこぼこ, 凹 supplies the sunken-part **ぼこ**. The separately indexed 穴ぼこ あなぼこ, "hole; pothole," has search-only spelling 穴凹 in JMdict; that index spelling is not recommended here as an ordinary written example.
- Contrast [回](回.md), revolve or times, and [同](同.md), same. Their enclosures lack 凹's open-topped dip.
