# 彫 — carve; engrave; sculpt

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 彡 (no. 59), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

彫 is cutting a design, letters or a shape into a material: carving, engraving and sculpting. [彫る](../words/彫る.md) ほる is to carve, engrave or sculpt, and [彫刻](../words/彫刻.md) ちょうこく is carving, engraving or sculpture. JMdict gives 彫る "to carve; to engrave; to sculpt; to chisel" as its first sense and "to tattoo" as its second.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for チョウ: carving patterns or letters into something, as in 彫刻, 彫塑 ちょうそ, "carving; engraving," 彫像 ちょうぞう, "(carved) statue; sculpture," 彫琢 ちょうたく, "carving and polishing," and 木彫 もくちょう, "wood carving; wooden sculpture; woodcraft." It gives ほる as the kun reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [周](周.md), "circumference; circuit," at the left, and 彡, three slanting strokes, at the right. Imagine three chisel cuts made all the way around a block as it is carved into shape. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 周 or 彡 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| チョウ | on | [彫刻](../words/彫刻.md) ちょうこく — carving; engraving; sculpture |

In 彫刻, 彫 supplies チョウ and 刻 supplies コク. In 彫る, 彫 writes ほ and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. After another element, the noun form ほり voices to ぼり, a sound change called rendaku: in 浮き彫り (うきぼり), the part before ぼり is read うき; in 木彫り (きぼり), 木 supplies き. The leading dash in -ぼ.り marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced. JMdict also accepts the spelling 木彫 for きぼり, and the same spelling is read もくちょう, with 木 supplying モク and 彫 supplying チョウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows チョウ, with 彫刻, 彫塑 and 木彫 as examples, and ほる, with 彫る and 木彫り. It has no separate row for ぼり; it lists 木彫り, where 彫 is read ぼり after 木, under ほる. Practice selects チョウ and ほ.る, the readings with linked word articles; -ぼ.り is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

Two further readings are outside both KANJIDIC2 and the official table. Daijisen has a separate headword える, ゑる in historical kana, for a verb written ▽彫る whose first sense is "to carve; to engrave"; the ▽ mark means that the official table does not list this reading of 彫. JMdict's entry for the noun 彫り, also written 彫, "carving; engraving," gives ほり and, marked as an out-dated kana usage, えり. Neither reading is asked here, and none of the three cards chosen to supply this card's wrong answers, 円, 高 and 見, is read える or えり.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [刻](刻.md), "carve; time," in 彫刻. 彫る is 彫 with okurigana only.
- [周](周.md), "circumference; circuit," is the shape at the left of 彫. 彡, the key at the right, does not have a kanji card here; [影](影.md), "shadow; silhouette," has the same 彡 at the right. [掘](掘.md), "dig; excavate," is also read ほ.る, but 掘る ほる, "to dig; to excavate; to hollow," is a different verb from 彫る. All are different kanji.
- [円](円.md), "yen, circle," has the 冂 frame that holds the inside of 周, and [高](高.md), "high, expensive," has 冂 with 口 inside it at the bottom, much as 周 has 士 above 口 inside its frame. [見](見.md), "see, look," is not similar in shape; its kun readings みる, みえる and みせる end in る, as ほる does. These three cards supply this card's wrong answers. All are different kanji.
- [刻](刻.md), "carve; time," [削](削.md), "shave; sharpen; pare," and [像](像.md), "image; statue," overlap the senses "carve" and "statue" that 彫 and its words carry. 刻 writes 刻む きざむ, which JMdict glosses "to carve; to engrave; to chisel; to notch" in its second sense, and appears in 彫刻; 削 writes 削る けずる, "to shave (wood, leather, etc.); to sharpen (e.g. pencil); to plane; to whittle," which is close to carving wood; and 像 appears in 彫像. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "carve" as exclusive to any one character.
