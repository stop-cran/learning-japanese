# 整 — put in order; adjust

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (as 攵, no. 66), at the upper right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

整 is making something orderly: putting things in order, adjusting them until they fit, or being in good order. [整理](../words/整理.md) せいり is sorting or putting in order, [整備](../words/整備.md) せいび is maintenance or putting in place, [調整](../words/調整.md) ちょうせい is adjustment or coordination, [整数](../words/整数.md) せいすう is an integer and [整う](../words/整う.md) ととのう is to be ready, in order or settled.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense: to make disordered things correct and even, to put in order, and to be in good order, as in 整合 せいごう, "adjustment; coordination; integration; conformity," 整然 せいぜん, "orderly; regular; systematic; well-organized," 整列 せいれつ, "standing in a row; forming a line; alignment," and 均整 きんせい, "symmetry; balance; proportion; uniformity." KANJIDIC2 lists the meanings "organize," "arranging," "tune," "tone," "meter" and "key (music)"; the musical ones are not practised here beyond 調整, which JMdict glosses "adjustment; regulation; coordination; reconciliation; tuning; fixing."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [束](束.md), "bundle," at the upper left, 攵 at the upper right and [正](正.md), "correct, proper," at the bottom. Imagine tapping a loose bundle with a stick until it sits straight, then setting it down in exactly the right spot. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 束, 攵 or 正 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 整理, 理 supplies リ. In 整備, 備 supplies ビ. In 調整, 調 supplies チョウ. In 整数, 数 supplies スウ. In 整う and 整える, 整 writes ととの and う or える is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セイ, with 整理, 整列 and 調整 as examples; ととのえる, with 整える; and ととのう, with 整う. It sets 調える and 調う beside the last two as contrasting writings with 調, so check which kanji a sense needs.

Practice selects セイ and ととの.う, the readings with linked word articles; ととの.える is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [理](理.md), "reason, logic," in 整理, [備](備.md), "prepare; equip," in 整備, [調](調.md), "investigate; tune," in 調整 and [数](数.md), "number," in 整数.
- [政](政.md), "politics," shares both the 正 shape and the 攵 shape and is also read セイ. [敬](敬.md), "respect; honor," and [敗](敗.md), "failure; defeat," share the 攵 shape. All are different kanji.
- [順](順.md), "order; sequence," [調](調.md), "investigate; tune," [並](並.md), "line up; parallel," [備](備.md), "prepare; equip," [正](正.md), "correct, proper," [直](直.md), "direct; repair," and [制](制.md), "system; regulate," overlap the senses "order," "tune," "arrange," "prepare," "correct," "fix" and "regulate" that 整 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "put in order" or "adjust" as exclusive to any one character.
