# 脱 — undress; remove; escape

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (as 月, no. 130), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

脱 is taking off what covers something, and from there getting out of a place or group, or something dropping away. [脱ぐ](../words/脱ぐ.md) ぬぐ is to take off clothing, and [脱線](../words/脱線.md) だっせん is a derailment or a digression. JMdict glosses 脱 だつ, used as a prefix, "de- (indicating reversal, removal, etc.); post-."

The Daijisen character entry gives five senses for ダツ. One example of each:

- taking away what covers the outside: 脱衣 だつい, "undressing; taking off one's clothes; stripping";
- getting out of a frame or an organization: 脱出 だっしゅつ, "escape; getting away (from); getting out (of)," besides 脱線;
- taking part of something out, or a part coming away: 脱毛 だつもう, "hair loss";
- something that should be there having dropped out: 脱字 だつじ, "omitted character; omitted letter";
- being free of worldliness: 解脱 げだつ, "liberation from earthly desires and the woes of man; deliverance of one's soul."

It also lists ぬく and ぬける among the kun readings, which KANJIDIC2 does not give for 脱. JMdict accepts 脱ける as a spelling of 抜ける ぬける, "to come out; to fall out," and has a rare verb 脱る とる, "to remove; to get rid of; to take off." [Kanjipedia's character entry](https://www.kanjipedia.jp/kanji/0004564000) marks the on reading タツ as outside the official table. None of these readings is asked here, and none of the three cards chosen to supply this card's wrong answers, 飲, 読 and 聞, is read タツ, ぬく, ぬける or とる. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [肉](肉.md), "meat; flesh," at the left, written 月, and [兄](兄.md), "older brother," at the right with two short strokes above it. Imagine a body beside an older brother pulling his shirt off over his head, the two strokes flying up as it comes off. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 肉 or 兄 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ダツ | on | [脱線](../words/脱線.md) だっせん — derailment; digression |
| ぬ.ぐ | kun | [脱ぐ](../words/脱ぐ.md) ぬぐ — take off clothing |
| ぬ.げる | kun | 脱げる ぬげる - to come off; to slip down; to slip off; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 脱線, 脱 supplies ダツ, which shortens to だっ before セン, the reading of 線. In 脱ぐ, 脱 writes ぬ and ぐ is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same pattern gives 脱げる with げる, the intransitive partner: with 脱げる the clothing or shoe comes off by itself, while with 脱ぐ a person takes it off.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ダツ, with 脱衣, 脱出 and 虚脱 きょだつ, "lethargy; despondency; mental numbness," as examples; ぬぐ, with 脱ぐ; and ぬげる, with 脱げる. Practice selects ダツ and ぬ.ぐ, the readings with linked word articles; ぬ.げる is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [線](線.md), "line; track," in 脱線. The word 脱ぐ contains only this kanji.
- [肉](肉.md), "meat; flesh," is the key at the left, written 月. [説](説.md), "explanation; theory," [税](税.md), "tax; fiscal duty," and [鋭](鋭.md), "sharp; keen," have the same right-hand shape as 脱, beside a different left-hand part. [飲](飲.md), "drink," [読](読.md), "read," and [聞](聞.md), "hear, ask," are not similar in shape; they write the verbs 飲む のむ, 読む よむ and 聞く きく, two-kana verbs like ぬぐ. These three cards supply this card's wrong answers. All are different kanji.
- [逃](逃.md), "escape; flee," [除](除.md), "remove; exclude," [抜](抜.md), "extract," [離](離.md), "separate; detach," [退](退.md), "withdraw; retreat," [去](去.md), "leave, elapse," and [出](出.md), "go out, exit," overlap the senses "escape," "remove," "withdraw" and "get out" that 脱 and its words carry; 離 and 退 appear in 離脱 and 脱退, listed with the other Daijisen examples below, and 出 in 脱出, and Daijisen gives 脱 the kun readings ぬく and ぬける that 抜 also has. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "remove" or "escape" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ダツ senses: 脱皮 だっぴ, "ecdysis; shedding (of skin); sloughing; molting," 脱帽 だつぼう, "removing one's hat," and 着脱 ちゃくだつ, "attaching and detaching; putting on and removing"; 脱退 だったい, "withdrawal (e.g. from an organization); secession; leaving," 脱却 だっきゃく, "ridding oneself; freeing oneself of; growing out of," 逸脱 いつだつ, "deviation; departure," and 離脱 りだつ, "withdrawal; secession; separation; breakaway"; 脱色 だっしょく, "decolourization; decolorization; decolourisation; decolorisation; bleaching"; and 洒脱 しゃだつ, "sophisticated; refined; witty; urbane."
