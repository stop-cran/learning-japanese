# 報 — report; news

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

報 appears in words about conveying information: [報告](../words/報告.md) ほうこく is a report, 情報 じょうほう is information, and 予報 よほう is a forecast.
KANJIDIC2 also records reward and retribution meanings; the words on this card focus on information.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the tall 幸-shaped block on the left as a noticeboard and the right-hand strokes as someone bringing a report. This is a memory scene, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

The indexing radical is 土 at the top of the left-hand structure, not a separate 土 standing beside the rest.
[服](服.md) offers a useful comparison for the bent strokes on the right, though the left halves are quite different.
Both 報 and [告](告.md) relate to telling or informing, as in 報告 (ほうこく), "report."
