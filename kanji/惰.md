# 惰 — lazy; idle

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

惰 means lazy or idle. It appears in 怠惰 and in 惰性 (だせい), “force of habit.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 忄, the narrow form of 心 “heart,” is on the left; on the right, [左](左.md), “left,” sits above [月](月.md).

## Notes

[随](随.md) has a similar ナ-over-月 shape inside 辶 and means follow; [慣](慣.md) and [怪](怪.md) also start with 忄 and mean accustomed and suspicious. [怠](怠.md) also means lazy and combines with 惰 in 怠惰; [慢](慢.md) can mean slackness, as in 怠慢 (たいまん), “negligence”; [緩](緩.md) means loose or slack. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 102](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=102) gives 惰 only ダ, with 惰眠, 惰気 and 怠惰.
