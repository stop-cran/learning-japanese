# 寡 — few; widowed

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寡 has two useful associations: few in number and being without a spouse. 寡黙 (かもく) describes someone of few words; 寡婦 (かふ) is a family-status term.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look beneath the roof: a long middle section gives way to strokes spreading at the foot. Use the outline, not a story about why someone is widowed.

## Notes

[富](富.md) ends in 田; [客](客.md) has 各 under the roof; [察](察.md) has 示 below. [少](少.md) also means “few,” but 少ない (すくない) is the ordinary written form of the adjective. Kanjipedia also lists やもめ outside the standard-use table. The word 寡婦 can be read やもめ as a whole or かふ as a compound. Calling someone 寡黙 says nothing about their marital status.
