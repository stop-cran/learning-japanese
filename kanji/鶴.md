# 鶴 — crane (bird)

**Strokes:** 21 · **Key (radical):** 鳥 (no. 196) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鶴 is the **crane** character: the bird, not a lifting machine. The native noun [鶴](../words/鶴.md) (つる) names a crane; ツル is also used as a kana spelling.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [鳥](鳥.md) on the right and picture a crane standing beside the dense strokes on the left. This is a memory aid for the written shape, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カク | on | 鶴翼 (かくよく) — crane wings; also a wing-shaped battle formation. Recognition example, not a reading question on this card. |
| つる | kun | [鶴](../words/鶴.md) (つる) — crane (bird) |

The on-reading example is a specific compound, not the everyday name of the bird. This card selects つる for questions while retaining カク in the full source metadata.

## Common words

- [鶴](../words/鶴.md) (つる) — crane (bird)
- 折り鶴 (おりづる) — origami crane; also written 折鶴. Here つる becomes づる in the compound.

## Notes

- [確](確.md), "certain," has 石 on the left; [観](観.md), "view; observe," ends in 見; [権](権.md), "authority; rights," has 木 on the left. Their dense strokes can look similar, but none means a crane.
- KANJIDIC2 also includes the English gloss "stork." For the ordinary word つる, learn **crane**: modern dictionaries distinguish it from コウノトリ (こうのとり), "stork." The source gloss is not a rule that the two bird names are interchangeable.
- The poetic bird name たず (historically たづ) is separately documented; it is not the reading selected for this card.
