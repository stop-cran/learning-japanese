# 喚 — call; summon; yell

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

喚 means to call, summon, yell, or cry out. In [喚起](../words/喚起.md), it is part of a word meaning to arouse or awaken attention or awareness.
