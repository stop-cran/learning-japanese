# 凝 — congeal; become absorbed

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 冫 (the two-stroke ice radical at left, no. 15) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凝 connects congealing or stiffness with concentrated attention.
[凝固](../words/凝固.md) ぎょうこ names coagulation or solidification;
[凝る](../words/凝る.md) こる can describe stiff muscles or becoming absorbed
in a hobby. The word and construction determine which meaning applies.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the two marks of 冫 beside
[疑](疑.md) as someone pausing so intently that they seem frozen in place.
This is an invented memory scene, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

The three こご readings are dictionary-verified recognition material, not
additional readings of every example above. In particular, shoulder stiffness
and enthusiasm in the linked 凝る article use **こる**, not こごる.
The written ending of 凝らす is らす in both こらす and こごらす.

## Notes

- Contrast **activity + に凝る** with **工夫 + を凝らす**. The first describes
  becoming absorbed; the second describes applying ingenuity. They are not
  interchangeable verb endings.
- 凝った（こった）, as in 凝った衣装（こったいしょう）, "an elaborate outfit,"
  is an inflected form of こる, not a new character reading.
- [凍](凍.md) concerns freezing, [固](固.md) firmness or hardening, and
  [硬](硬.md) hardness or stiffness. These meanings overlap with parts of
  凝's range; do not treat them as unrelated simply because the shapes differ.
- For shape comparison, [准](准.md) has 隹 beside 冫; 凝 has the more complex
  疑-shaped right side. [冗](冗.md) has a cover-like top, and [凹](凹.md)
  has an inward dip. None means congealing or absorption.
