# 得 — gain; acquire

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

得 centers on obtaining something or gaining an advantage. 得る える is "to obtain."
[得意](../words/得意.md) とくい describes a strong point: something a person is good at.
Do not translate that established word simply as "obtain a meaning."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** store your gain in the compact right-hand stack, with a 日 shape above
the lower strokes. This labels a modern visual association, not an origin or phonetic analysis.

## Notes

[待](待.md) shares 彳; [時](時.md) and [特](特.md) offer similar small lower-right strokes.
得 has a 日-shaped upper-right part, whereas those three have 寺 on the right.
Similarity of shape is not evidence that their readings or meanings match.
