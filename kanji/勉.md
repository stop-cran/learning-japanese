# 勉 — strive, exert effort

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

勉 conveys exertion or diligent effort. Its most useful beginner word is 勉強, "study."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** spot [力](力.md), "power," at the lower right and imagine putting effort into a difficult task. This is a modern memory aid; it does not identify a historical phonetic component.

## Readings

## Common words

- [勉強](../words/勉強.md) べんきょう — study; 勉強する — to study
- 勉強会 べんきょうかい — study group or study meeting
- 勤勉 きんべん — diligent; hard-working

## Notes

勉 is normally encountered in compounds, not as an independent everyday word for studying. [学](学.md) also concerns learning, while 勉 highlights effort.

Do not confuse 勉 with [動](動.md), "move": both include 力, but their other parts differ.
