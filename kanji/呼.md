# 呼 — call out

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

呼 carries the idea of calling: calling to someone, summoning them, or calling them by a name.
In [呼ぶ](../words/呼ぶ.md) よぶ, the person summoned can be marked with を: 先生を呼ぶ (せんせいをよぶ), "call the teacher over."
呼吸 こきゅう is a separate word to learn as a unit: "breathing."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a call leaving 口 and reaching the tall hooked stroke on the right. This is a present-shape aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

Do not translate every English "call" with 呼ぶ. [電話](../words/電話.md) でんわ names a telephone call.
[吸](吸.md) and [吹](吹.md) share the left-hand 口, but their right-hand parts and meanings differ.
