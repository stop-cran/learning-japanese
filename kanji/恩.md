# 恩 — favor; debt of gratitude

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

恩 concerns a kindness received and the gratitude or obligation that goes
with it. [恩](../words/恩.md) おん is a favor or a debt of gratitude, and
[恩恵](../words/恩恵.md) おんけい is the benefit or blessing that comes from
something. 恩人 おんじん is a benefactor, 恩返し おんがえし is repaying a
favor, and 恩師 おんし is a teacher to whom one owes a debt of gratitude.

The Digital Daijisen character entry glosses 恩 as めぐみ (grace;
blessing), いつくしみ (affection; benevolence) and 情け (compassion).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [因](因.md) above
[心](心.md). Imagine a reason or cause resting on the heart: a kindness you
keep in mind until you repay it. This is a memory picture for the modern
shape, not a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Notes

- [因](因.md), "cause; factor," is the upper component of 恩, so the two
  can look alike at a glance. [思](思.md), "think, feel," and
  [患](患.md), "be ill; suffer," share 心 at the bottom but differ in
  meaning.
- A benefit or blessing, as in 恩恵, overlaps [利](利.md), "benefit;
  advantage," [享](享.md), "enjoy; receive benefits," [福](福.md), "good
  fortune," and [幸](幸.md), "happiness; good fortune."
- The English stem "favor" is shared with [好](好.md), "liking;
  favorable," and [良](良.md), "good; favorable," although those kanji
  describe preference or quality, not a kindness received.
- The affection and benevolence in いつくしみ overlap [愛](愛.md), "love,"
  whose card covers affection and care, not only romance. 恩 is still a
  kindness received, not love in general.
- Thanks and gratitude overlap [礼](礼.md), "bow; thanks," and
  [謝](謝.md), "apologize; thank." This card declares the pair 恩–謝.
- An obligation or debt of gratitude overlaps [義](義.md), "righteousness;
  duty; meaning," and [役](役.md), "role; duty." These direct meaning
  comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as identical in every
  compound.
