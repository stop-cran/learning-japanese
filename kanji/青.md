# 青 — blue, green

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 青 (no. 174) · **Phonetic:** 生 ([生](生.md), セイ) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

青 is traditionally analysed as 生 (growing plant, sound セイ) over 丹 (a red mineral pigment, simplified to a shape resembling 月), the colour of fresh plants. The upper part is a phonetic as well as a hint of "fresh".
青 covers both blue and green in traditional Japanese: 青信号 あおしんごう (a green traffic light), 青りんご あおりんご (green apple), 青菜 あおな (greens), and also "young, unripe" (青二才 あおにさい, a greenhorn).

## Notes

Colour siblings: [赤](赤.md), [白](白.md), [黒](黒.md). Compare 緑 みどり, the standard word for green; 青 is still used for traffic lights and vegetables.
Okurigana (the kana after the kanji, like い in 青い): 青い (adjective) versus 青 (noun). Also 青ざめる あおざめる (turn pale).
