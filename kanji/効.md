# 効 — efficacy; effectiveness

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

効 concerns effectiveness or an effect. 効く きく means to work effectively or take effect, while 効果 こうか names an effect. 有効 ゆうこう can mean effective or valid, depending on the thing being described.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the crossing 交 shape beside 力, strength, as though effort is producing an effective result.

The canonical radical 力 is the right-hand component.

## Notes

- 効く is intransitive: 薬が効く くすりがきく means a medicine works. Its shared dictionary entry also includes 利く in expressions about faculties or senses; spelling choices belong to whole expressions.
- The ordinary word 効き目 ききめ means effectiveness. Its written き and separate 目 do not establish that 効 alone normally spells the whole reading ききめ. The source-only ききめ and ならう rows are retained for character-dictionary lookup.
