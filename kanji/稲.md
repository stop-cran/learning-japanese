# 稲 — rice plant

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

稲 names the **rice plant**, not a bowl of cooked rice. Compare [稲](../words/稲.md) いね, the plant, with [米](../words/米.md) こめ, rice as grain, and [ご飯](../words/ご飯.md) ごはん, cooked rice or a meal.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 禾 on the left and the claw-like strokes above the 日 shape on the right. Imagine a hand inspecting rice plants. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- Learn いね as the independent noun and いな- in whole words such as いなほ. The dash marks a bound form; it is not pronounced.
- [穂](穂.md) ほ names a grain-bearing head, a part of the plant rather than the whole plant.
- [移](移.md), shift or transfer, [秒](秒.md), a second of time, and [秋](秋.md), autumn, share 禾 but have different right-hand shapes and meanings.
- The source-only テ remains visible for lookup but is not selected for reading questions. Full metadata is preserved.
