# 顕 — evident; reveal

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

顕 is being clear and conspicuous, and bringing what was hidden into the open. KANJIDIC2 lists “appear” and “existing”. Kanjipedia's first sense is あきらか。はっきりしている。 (clear; distinct) and its second is あらわれる。あきらかにする。あきらかになる。 (appear; make clear; become clear). Digital Daijisen's second sense is 隠れたものを明らかにする。あらわす。 (bring hidden things to light; reveal), and it lists [顕微鏡](../words/顕微鏡.md) けんびきょう among the examples of that sense; JMdict glosses 顕微鏡 “microscope” (entry 1260660). JMdict glosses 顕著 けんちょ “remarkable; striking; obvious” (entry 1260640).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is 显, a shape with [日](日.md), "sun, day," over 业; on the right is 頁, a shape that contains [貝](貝.md), "shellfish; seashell," made of [目](目.md), "eye," over [八](八.md), "eight." Picture the sun coming out and shining on everything, so that all of it becomes evident to the eye. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 显 or 頁 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ケン only, with 顕著, 顕彰 けんしょう (“honouring (publicly); honoring; making someone's good deeds or achievements well-known”, JMdict 1260630) and 顕微鏡, and gives the old form 顯. Kanjipedia lists あきらか and あらわれる outside the table (外).
- Digital Daijisen also gives the kun readings あらわ and あらわす. JMdict lists 顕 and 顕わ among the spellings of あらわ (1560080) and 顕す among those of 表す あらわす (1263490). These are not KANJIDIC2 readings and have no table rows.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings あき and あきら; Digital Daijisen gives あき, あきら, たか and てる. These are name use, not quiz readings.

These seven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [現](現.md) (“actual; present”): KANJIDIC2 lists “appear” for 顕, JMdict lists 顕れる as a rarely used kanji form of 現れる あらわれる, “to appear; to come in sight; to become visible; to come out; to embody; to materialize; to materialise” (1263510), and the 現 card lists あらわ.れる, the same kun reading as 顕's あらわ.れる.
- [著](著.md) (“write; remarkable”): JMdict glosses 顕著 “remarkable; striking; obvious” (1260640) and 著しい いちじるしい “striking; remarkable; considerable” (1427070); the 著 card lists あらわ.す, and Digital Daijisen gives あらわす as a kun reading of 顕.
- [明](明.md) (“bright, clear”): KANJIDIC2 gives 顕 the kun reading あきらか and the 明 card lists あき.らか; Kanjipedia's first sense of 顕 is あきらか, and JMdict glosses 明らか “clear; obvious; evident; plain; definite” (1532310).
- [朗](朗.md) (“cheerful; clear; resonant”): KANJIDIC2 gives 朗 the kun reading あき.らか and the meaning “clear”, and gives 顕 the kun reading あきらか; Kanjipedia's first sense of 顕 is あきらか。はっきりしている。
- [卓](卓.md) (“table; eminent”): KANJIDIC2 lists “eminent” and “high” for 卓, while Kanjipedia's third sense of 顕 is 名高い。地位が高い。 (famous; high in rank) and Digital Daijisen's is 地位・身分が高い。
- [表](表.md) (“surface; chart”): the 表 card lists あらわ.す, and JMdict lists 顕す among the spellings of 表す あらわす, whose fourth sense, “to make widely known”, carries the note “esp. 顕す” (1263490).
- [暴](暴.md) (“violence; outburst”): the 暴 card lists あば.く, and JMdict's first sense of 暴く あばく, “to disclose; to divulge; to expose” (1519330), overlaps Digital Daijisen's second sense of 顕, 隠れたものを明らかにする, and this card's “reveal.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 淡 (“pale; faint; plain; unsalted”), 退 (“withdraw; retreat”), and 滅 (“perish; destroy; die out”); none means evident or reveal.
