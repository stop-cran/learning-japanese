# 実 — reality; fruit

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

実 has two useful learning anchors: what is real or true, and the fruit or seed a plant produces.
[現実](../words/現実.md) (げんじつ) means reality; 実 (み) can mean a fruit, nut, or seed.
These are established word meanings, not a rule that every occurrence must have both at once.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture fruit packed beneath a roof, using the stacked middle strokes and spreading lower strokes as the visual outline of the pile.
This image is not a historical analysis.

- 実 (み) — fruit; nut; seed
- 実る (みのる) — bear fruit; ripen; yield results

## Notes

実る can describe successful results as well as plants, much like English "bear fruit."
[完](完.md) shares the roof but has 元 below; [春](春.md) and [失](失.md) provide comparisons for the crowded horizontal strokes and lower sweeps.
Do not substitute the kun reading み inside げんじつ.
