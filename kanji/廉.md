# 廉 — inexpensive; upright

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

廉 can concern either price or integrity. 廉価 describes a low price; 清廉 (せいれん) describes honesty or uprightness.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 兼 under 广. The character for “dislike” uses the same right-hand shape beside 女 instead.

## Notes

[康](康.md) shares the roof but not the 兼 below it. [嫌](嫌.md) puts 女 beside 兼; [謙](謙.md) starts with speech; [度](度.md) has a different shape beneath 广. [安](安.md) can also mean inexpensive, and [潔](潔.md) can suggest uprightness. かど, usually in kana but also written 廉, can mean grounds, a charge, or suspicion. The word 嫌疑 (けんぎ), “suspicion,” uses 嫌, not 廉. 廉い is a recorded spelling for the price sense of [安い](../words/安い.md) (やすい); the calm sense in the same family is restricted to 安い.
