# 桃 — peach

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

桃 writes **peach**, [桃](../words/桃.md) もも, naming the tree or its fruit. It is not another spelling of [梅](梅.md), ume.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside [兆](兆.md). Imagine the spreading right-hand strokes as branches laden with peaches. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

- 白桃 combines 白's ハク with 桃's トウ. Do not insert the standalone もも into this reading.
- 桃色 ももいろ means pink; 桃 can also abbreviate that colour name in context. A peach-related spelling does not mean that every peach fruit has pink flesh.
- Compare [兆](兆.md), trillion or omen, without 木. [村](村.md) and [相](相.md) share the left-hand tree component but have different right sides and meanings.
