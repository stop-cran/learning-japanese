# 復 — restore; repeat

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

復 concerns returning to an earlier condition or doing something again. 回復 かいふく means recovery or restoration, while 復習 ふくしゅう means reviewing previously learned material. These are whole-word uses, not a rule to translate every 復 as the same English verb.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 彳 beside the stacked right-hand shape ending in 夂 and imagine retracing a route. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not etymology.

## Notes

- The spelling 復 for また is flagged as rare in JMdict; the word is usually written in kana. Retain it for recognition, not as a recommendation to replace ordinary また with this kanji.
- 復習 ふくしゅう is review, not the homophone 復讐 ふくしゅう, revenge.
- Compare [得](得.md), gain or acquire: a similar left side does not supply the same meaning.
