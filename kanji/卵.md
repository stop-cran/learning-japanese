# 卵 — egg; ovum

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 卩 (no. 26) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

卵 writes たまご, an egg, including biological eggs and eggs used as food. The on-reading ラン appears in biological vocabulary such as 卵子 らんし, an egg cell.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the dots inside the two sides as the contents of eggs. Keep all the small strokes when writing the character.

KANJIDIC2 indexes 卵 under 卩 (no. 26). The two-sided whole character is not simply the standalone radical doubled without changes.

## Notes

- The native reading たまご belongs to the whole character; do not try to divide it between its two sides.
- 玉子 たまご is another spelling associated especially with hen's eggs and cooking, but 卵 is also used for food. There is no rigid raw-versus-cooked spelling rule.
- For a counted egg, the linked article gives 二個 にこ, two individual eggs.
