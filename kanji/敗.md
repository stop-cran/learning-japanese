# 敗 — failure; defeat

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (as 攵, no. 66) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

敗 marks an unsuccessful result: a defeat in a contest or a failure in an attempt.
[失敗](../words/失敗.md) しっぱい can be a mistake without any opponent; 敗れる やぶれる
describes being defeated.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 貝-shaped left side being knocked off balance by the
right-hand 攵. This is a visual aid for defeat, not an etymology. 攵 is the visible form of indexing radical 攴.

## Common words

- [失敗](../words/失敗.md) しっぱい — failure; mistake
- 敗れる やぶれる — lose; be defeated
- 勝敗 しょうはい — victory or defeat; the outcome of a contest

## Notes

The doubled consonant in しっぱい belongs to the whole compound; do not read it as しつはい.
[負ける](../words/負ける.md) まける also means "lose"; compare it with 敗れる (やぶれる), "be defeated."
For shape, compare the 貝 portions of [買](買.md) and [員](員.md), and the 攵 in [数](数.md).
