# 倣 — imitate; emulate

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

倣 concerns imitating or following an existing model.
[倣う](../words/倣う.md) ならう means to follow an example or precedent;
[模倣](../words/模倣.md) もほう is imitation or copying.
The model need not be a teacher giving lessons.

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and [放](放.md).
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a person on the left copying the shape
on the right. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation
or a claim that 放's "release" meaning explains imitation.

In 倣う, 倣 writes なら and う is okurigana. JMdict marks the word
usually written in kana; the kanji spelling makes this meaning explicit.

## Notes

- 倣う marks the model with に: 前例に倣う, "follow a precedent."
  [習う](../words/習う.md), also ならう, concerns learning under instruction:
  先生にピアノを習う (せんせいにピアノをならう), "take piano lessons
  from a teacher." The skill takes を; the shared sound does not make
  these words interchangeable.
- [放](放.md), "release," lacks the left-hand 亻. [方](方.md),
  "direction, method," is only part of 放, not the whole right side of 倣.
- [付](付.md), "attach," shares 亻 but has a different right side.
  Keep the entire shape in view rather than identifying a kanji by its radical alone.
