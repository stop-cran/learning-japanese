# 第 — ordinal-number prefix

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118), appearing as ⺮ · **JLPT:** N2 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

第 forms **ordinal numbers**: [第一](../words/第一.md) だいいち is first or foremost. It precedes the number, as in 第三 (だいさん), third. [次第](../words/次第.md) しだい also contains it, but its order, circumstance and grammatical uses need learning as a whole.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the bamboo top and the curved lower strokes. Imagine marking numbered stages in order. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical derivation.

- [次第](../words/次第.md) しだい - depending on; as soon as; circumstances

## Notes

- Compare 第三回 (だいさんかい), "the third occurrence," with 三回目 (さんかいめ), "the third time." 第 goes before the number; ordinal [目](目.md) follows the counting expression.
- [番](番.md) can concern order or an identifying number. 第 is specifically the ordinal prefix in 第三, not a general replacement for 番 or [号](号.md) in every label.
- [弟](弟.md), younger brother, has a different top. Do not omit the six-stroke bamboo top when writing 第.
- Practice selects ダイ; the rare 第宅 spelling does not justify reading an ordinary ordinal as テイ.
