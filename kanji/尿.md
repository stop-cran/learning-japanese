# 尿 — urine

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

尿 refers to urine. In 検尿 (けんにょう), it is what is examined. In 排尿 (はいにょう), it is what the body passes.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 水 below the cover. Imagine a liquid sample; the shape cue is not the history of the word.
