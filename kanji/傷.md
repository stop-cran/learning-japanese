# 傷 — wound; injury; damage

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

傷 is damage done to a body or a thing: a wound, an injury, a scratch, and by extension hurt feelings. [傷](../words/傷.md) きず is a wound or a scratch, and [火傷](../words/火傷.md) やけど is a burn injury or scald. JMdict glosses 傷 きず "wound; injury; cut; gash; bruise; scratch" and, in a later sense, "(emotional) hurt; hurt feelings."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ショウ. One example of each:

- a wound or injury: 外傷 がいしょう, "external injury; external wound; (physical) trauma";
- injuring or damaging: 損傷 そんしょう, "damage; injury";
- grieving or feeling hurt: 感傷 かんしょう, "sentiment; sentimentality."

For きず it gives 傷口 きずぐち, "(opening of a) wound; cut," and 古傷 ふるきず, "old wound; scar." It lists the kun readings きず, いたむ and いためる, the same as KANJIDIC2, and names 火傷 (やけど) among words with a hard-to-guess reading. More Daijisen examples of the ショウ senses are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [人](人.md), "person," as 亻 at the left, and at the right a short slanting stroke and a horizontal one above [日](日.md), "sun, day," which stands over another horizontal and sweeping strokes. Imagine a person who stayed under the sun too long and came home with burned, wounded skin. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 亻 or 日 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ショウ | on | 負傷 ふしょう - injury; wound; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| きず | kun | [傷](../words/傷.md) きず — wound; scratch |
| いた.む | kun | 傷む いたむ - to be injured; to be spoiled (e.g. food); to be damaged; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| いた.める | kun | 傷める いためる - to harm; to damage; to spoil; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

The word 傷 is the kanji alone, read きず, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 負傷, 負 supplies フ and 傷 supplies ショウ. In 傷む, 傷 writes いた and む is okurigana; 傷める uses the same いた with める. 火傷 is different: JMdict marks やけど as a gikun or jukujikun reading, a special reading that belongs to the whole word, so 火傷 is read やけど as a unit and supports neither きず nor ショウ. JMdict also has a separate, rarer entry 火傷 かしょう, "burn; scald," in which 火 supplies カ and 傷 supplies ショウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ショウ, with 傷害 しょうがい, "injury; inflicting bodily injury," 負傷 and 感傷 as examples; きず, with 傷, 古傷 and 傷つく きずつく, "to be wounded; to get injured"; いたむ, with 傷む, marked as a pair to compare with 痛む いたむ, "to hurt; to ache; to feel a pain," and 悼む いたむ, "to grieve over; to mourn; to lament"; and いためる, with 傷める, marked as a pair to compare with 痛める いためる, "to hurt; to injure; to cause pain." Practice selects きず, the reading with a linked word article whose reading 傷 really supplies. ショウ is the reading of most 傷 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; いた.む and いた.める are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [火](火.md), "fire," in 火傷. The word 傷 contains only this kanji.
- [湯](湯.md), "hot water," [陽](陽.md), "sunshine," and [揚](揚.md), "hoist; lift; deep-fry," share the lower right part of 傷, 日 above a horizontal and the same sweeping strokes, with a different shape at the left. All are different kanji.
- [害](害.md), "harm," [損](損.md), "loss; damage," and [痛](痛.md), "pain; hurt," overlap the senses "injury," "damage" and "hurt" that 傷 and its words carry; 損 is also read そこ.なう, "to harm," and 痛 shares the readings いた.む and いた.める. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "injury" or "damage" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ショウ senses: 軽傷 けいしょう, "minor injury," and 重傷 じゅうしょう, "serious wound; serious injury"; 傷害 and 中傷 ちゅうしょう, "slander; libel; defamation; calumny; smear"; and 傷心 しょうしん, "heartbreak; grief; sorrow."
