# 週 — week

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

週 means a **week**, the seven-day unit used for schedules and recurring activities.
It is not the character for an individual day of the week.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the road-shaped 辶 wrapping around 周 as a route that brings you
back to the same place after seven days. This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Common words

- [来週](../words/来週.md) らいしゅう — next week
- 今週 こんしゅう — this week
- 先週 せんしゅう — last week
- 週末 しゅうまつ — weekend
- 一週間 いっしゅうかん — one week

## Notes

[曜](曜.md) appears in weekday names; 週 names the whole week. 一週間 has a small っ:
いっしゅうかん, not いちしゅうかん.

[運](運.md) and [通](通.md) share the road-shaped component. Recognize the enclosed 周 to distinguish 週.
The classical radical is recorded as 辵 even though its form here is 辶.
