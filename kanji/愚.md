# 愚 — foolish; stupid

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

愚 means foolish. It appears in 愚か, in 愚問 (ぐもん), “a foolish question,” and in 愚痴 (ぐち), “an idle complaint.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 禺 sits above [心](心.md), “heart”; the same 禺 is on the right of [偶](偶.md) (グウ), “chance.”

## Notes

[思](思.md), [恩](恩.md) and [患](患.md) also have 心 at the bottom, but mean think, favor and be ill. [鈍](鈍.md) means dull or slow, and [賢](賢.md), “wise,” is the opposite of 愚. JMdict also gives 愚問 a humble sense: a modest way to refer to one’s own question. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 42](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=42) lists グ, with 愚問, 愚鈍 (ぐどん), “stupid; dull,” and 暗愚 (あんぐ), “foolish,” and おろか, with 愚かだ and 愚かしい.
