# 戦 — battle

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 戈 (no. 62) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

戦 covers battle and competition. [戦争](../words/戦争.md) せんそう means war,
but 戦う たたかう can also describe competing against an opponent rather than fighting a war.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the dense, dot-topped left side facing the long diagonal on the right.
Think of two sides in a contest; this is a modern visual aid, not historical evidence.

- [戦争](../words/戦争.md) せんそう — war
- 戦う たたかう — fight; compete against

## Notes

相手と戦う (あいてとたたかう) means "fight or compete against an opponent."
The context decides whether this is armed conflict or a contest. A sports match itself can be called
試合 (しあい); that does not make every use of 戦 a war.

[成](成.md) also has a prominent slant and hook. [試](試.md) and [代](代.md) offer looser
comparisons for the diagonal strokes; check the entire left side before identifying 戦.
