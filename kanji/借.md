# 借 — borrow, rent

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

借 describes receiving something for temporary use: borrowing a book, renting a room, or borrowing money.
[借りる](../words/借りる.md) takes the borrower's perspective, unlike [貸](貸.md), which concerns lending.

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and 昔.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** a person checks out a book from the shelf-like right-hand shape.
This is a memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

In 借金, シャク changes to しゃっ before きん; learn the whole pronunciation しゃっきん.
Compare [貸す](../words/貸す.md) かす, "lend": the same loan can be described from opposite sides.
[作](作.md) shares the left-hand 亻 but has a much simpler right side.
