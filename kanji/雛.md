# 雛 — chick; hina doll

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172), on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

雛 writes **a young bird or chick** and, with the reading ひな, a traditional
**hina doll** displayed for the Dolls' Festival. The [word article](../words/雛.md)
distinguishes the bird and doll senses of ひな from the bird and novice senses
of ひよこ. The shared spelling does not make all those meanings interchangeable.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the bird-shaped 隹 on the right.
Imagine the two repeated hook-like shapes on the left as a little bird's
nest beside it. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not an account
of its historical composition or sound.

## Notes

- [小鳥](../words/小鳥.md) ことり describes a small bird, not necessarily a young
  one. [鶏](../words/鶏.md) にわとり names the domestic chicken without specifying age.
- In 育雛, the chicks have already hatched. Do not confuse raising them with
  incubating eggs; see [Daijisen's 育雛 entry](https://www.weblio.jp/content/育雛).
- [準](準.md), standard/conform, [集](集.md), gather, and [進](進.md), advance,
  also contain a 隹-shaped part but have different surroundings and meanings.
  In 雛, keep the repeated left-hand shapes and the full right-hand 隹.
- The grade-9 tag is character-list metadata, not a claim that the ordinary
  native words are names only. Bird uses are often written in kana; the word
  article qualifies that spelling guidance by reading and sense.
