# 湧 — well up; surge; arise

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

湧 is something coming up from below: water welling out of the ground, and then sweat, tears, feelings or ideas appearing. The linked [湧く](../words/湧く.md) わく has four JMdict senses. The first is "to well (up); to gush out (of water); to spring out; to surge." The second is "to appear (esp. suddenly) (sweat, tears, etc.); to sweat; to cry," the third is "to feel emotions (e.g. joy, bravery, etc.); to spring up (e.g. an idea, emotions, clouds); to well up; to arise," and the fourth is "to hatch; to breed; to propagate; to be infested (esp. of parasitic insects, etc.)." The word article keeps it apart from 沸く わく, "to grow hot (e.g. water); to boil."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ユウ and ヨウ: water wells up. Its examples are 湧出 ゆうしゅつ, "gushing out; welling up; springing up," and 湧泉 ゆうせん, "bubbling spring; gushing spring; fountain." Daijisen's note says 涌 is a variant form of 湧; JMdict marks 涌く as a "rarely used kanji form" of 湧く.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part is the same shape as the kanji [勇](勇.md), "courage; bravery." Imagine courage welling up like spring water. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| わ.く | kun | [湧く](../words/湧く.md) わく — well up; gush out; arise |

In 湧く, 湧 writes わ and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict lists 湧き わき, "welling (up); gushing forth (of water); springing out," as the noun form, and 湧き水 わきみず is "spring water." In 湧水, 湧出 and 湧泉, 湧 supplies ユウ; 湧出 can also be read ようしゅつ, which is where ヨウ is shown.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ユウ with 湧水 and 湧出, and わく with 湧く followed by ⇔ 沸く, which marks the contrast with the other わく. Practice selects わ.く, the reading of the linked word article. ユウ and ヨウ are shown with dictionary examples and ユ is source-only; none of them is asked here, and being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. The names わき and わく that KANJIDIC2 lists for 湧 are not readings taught here.

## Notes

- 湧く is 湧 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji.
- [沸](沸.md), "boil; seethe," also reads わ.く, but 沸く is a different word: 水が湧く is water emerging, 水が沸く is water boiling. Keep them apart.
- [港](港.md), "harbor," [濃](濃.md), "concentrated; dark," and [漁](漁.md), "fishing; fishery," also have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [噴](噴.md), "erupt; spout; gush," [泉](泉.md), "spring; water source," [起](起.md), "wake; rise," [汗](汗.md), "sweat; perspire," [生](生.md), "life, birth," [発](発.md), "depart, emit," [出](出.md), "go out, exit," [泡](泡.md), "bubble; foam; froth," and [源](源.md), "source; headwaters; origin," overlap gushing, springs, sweat, arising and emerging. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "well up," "surge" or "arise" as exclusive to any one character.
