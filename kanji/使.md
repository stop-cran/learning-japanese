# 使 — use, employ

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

使 means using or employing something. It also appears in words for someone sent on a mission, such as 大使, "ambassador."
[使う](../words/使う.md) covers using a tool or language and spending time or money.

The modern shape has 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), beside 吏.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the person working the right-hand strokes like a lever: putting a tool to use.
This is a visual association, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

Contrast [作](作.md), "make," with 使, "use": making a tool and using it are different actions.
[事](事.md) has a similar dense central shape but no left-hand 亻 and a different lower stroke.
Learn 使う as つかう, not the reading of [仕](仕.md)える (つかえる, "serve").
