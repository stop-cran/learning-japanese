# 尽 — exhaust; do to the full

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

尽 concerns using something up, something coming to an end, or giving all one’s effort. 力を尽くす (ちからをつくす) focuses on making the effort; 力が尽きる (ちからがつきる) on strength running out.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look above the two short lower strokes for a shape like 尺. Imagine the last two drops falling after the supply is exhausted.

## Notes

[昼](昼.md) has a 日-like lower part, [沢](沢.md) adds water to the left of 尺, and [屋](屋.md) has a different base under its cover. [奉](奉.md) and [仕](仕.md) overlap with the sense of serving, but using up food in 食べ尽くす (たべつくす) is not an act of service by definition.
