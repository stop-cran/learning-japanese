# 頭 — head

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

頭 names the head in [頭](../words/頭.md) あたま. A related position sense appears in 先頭 (せんとう),
the front of a line or group.
頭が痛いです。 (あたまがいたいです。) — My head hurts.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** check for the 豆 shape on the left and 頁 on the right.
Imagine the left block as a head on a small stand. This is only a shape mnemonic, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

[首](首.md) くび is the neck; [顔](顔.md) かお is the face. These are useful body-part contrasts,
not interchangeable names for the whole head.
[題](題.md), [頼](頼.md), and [類](類.md) also end in 頁; identify the left block before reading.
