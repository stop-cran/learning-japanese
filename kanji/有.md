# 有 — have, exist

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 月 (no. 74) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

有 expresses having something, its presence, or its existence. [有名](../words/有名.md), "famous," can be remembered as having a name that people know.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the strokes above [月](月.md) as an arm holding something it possesses. This is not a claim about the original drawing.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ユウ | on | [有名](../words/有名.md) ゆうめい (famous), 有料 ゆうりょう (fee-charging) |
| ウ | on | 有無 うむ (presence or absence) |
| あ.る | kun | 有る ある (to have; exist), usually written ある |

## Common words

- [有名](../words/有名.md) ゆうめい — famous, well-known
- 有料 ゆうりょう — requiring payment, not free
- 有無 うむ — presence or absence

## Notes

The everyday verb ある is normally written in kana; recognizing 有る does not mean it is the preferred spelling in every sentence.
[右](右.md) has 口 below its upper strokes, whereas 有 has 月. The classical radical here is 月; that classification alone does not establish the component's historical meaning.
