# 専 — specialize; exclusive

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

専 concerns concentrating on a particular thing or doing it exclusively. 専攻 せんこう names an academic major or special study. The native adverb 専ら もっぱら can mean exclusively or mainly; the complete use determines how strong that focus is.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the upper field-like form and the small 寸 beneath it; imagine putting all your attention into one marked area. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not historical or phonetic analysis.

## Notes

- JMdict marks もっぱら as usually written in kana. The kanji spelling documents the native reading; it is not a requirement to write 専ら in ordinary prose.
- Do not force an absolute only meaning onto every use: the dictionary also gives mainly or chiefly for もっぱら.
- 専制 concerns arbitrary or autocratic control, not an academic major. The shared セン reading does not make 専制 and 専攻 interchangeable.
- Compare [申](申.md), humbly say, and [東](東.md), east. Look at the lower strokes rather than recognizing only the central rectangular shape.
