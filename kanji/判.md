# 判 — judge; seal

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (as 刂, no. 18) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

判 appears in words about judging or evaluating: [判断](../words/判断.md) はんだん is a judgment or decision,
and 評判 ひょうばん is reputation or public estimation.
A separate vocabulary connection is a seal used in place of a signature.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the [半](半.md)-shaped left side as two alternatives and the upright
刂 as a divider for judging between them. This is an invented memory scene, not a historical analysis.

## Notes

Learn はんこ as a whole word: JMdict labels 判子 a sound-based spelling and notes the usual kana writing.
The seal meaning belongs to the whole word, not a literal combination of "judgment" and "child."
[半](半.md) lacks the right-hand 刂; [列](列.md) and [利](利.md) have different left sides.
