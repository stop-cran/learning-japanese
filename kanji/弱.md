# 弱 — weak; frail

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (no. 57) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弱 concerns weakness or reduced strength. 弱い よわい is the adjective weak, and 弱視 じゃくし means weak or impaired eyesight. The related verbs distinguish becoming weaker from making something weaker.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the two bow-like portions with short paired marks below them and imagine two bows lacking strength. This is a visual mnemonic, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

- 弱る and 弱まる are intransitive. 弱める is transitive: it concerns causing a reduction, not simply being weak.
- 弱る also has a troubled or at-a-loss sense; not every occurrence describes physical strength.
- [強](強.md), strong, gives a useful meaning contrast rather than a synonym.
