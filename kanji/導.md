# 導 — guide; conduct

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

導 concerns guiding or leading. 指導 しどう is guidance, instruction or coaching. 導く みちびく can mean to guide someone or show the way, and also to derive or deduce a result.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See [道](道.md) above 寸 and imagine a hand showing a route. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a verified historical derivation or a claim that every use involves a literal road.
