# 口 — mouth

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

口 is a pictograph of an open mouth. It extends naturally to openings: an entrance or exit is a "mouth", and the
word for "mouth" counts persons ("mouths to feed" is the usual explanation for the 人口 sense).
As a key it appears in kanji about speaking, eating, and sound: 味, 吸, 叫, 吐, 呼, 咲.

The outer box in 国 and 回 is the enclosure 囗 (くにがまえ), a different key, not a mouth.
