# 又 — again; also; or

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 又 (no. 29) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

又 means “again,” “also” or “or.” As a word, また means again, also, on the other hand, and, or or (JMdict entry 1524930), and 又は または means “or; either … or” (JMdict entry 1524990); JMdict notes that both are usually written in kana. JMdict entry 1524930 also has a prefix sense, “indirect,” and Digital Daijisen likewise says that また- attached to a noun marks something as indirect, as in [又聞き](../words/又聞き.md) and 又貸し.

## Notes

The Joyo table gives 又 only the kun reading また, with the examples 又 and 又は. Here “source-only” means that a source lists the reading but no checked word example uses it; it does not mean ユウ is invalid for 又 in every dictionary or context. Kanjipedia also gives the kun reading ふたた.び; it marks both ユウ and ふたた.び as outside the Joyo table (外). ふたた.び is not in this card's metadata, and neither reading is quizzed.

再 (“again; repeat”) and 復 (“restore; repeat”) overlap with “again”; JMdict lists 復 as a rarely used spelling of また. 且 (“moreover; also”) and 及 (“reach; extend to; and”) overlap with “also” and “and,” and 繰 (“reel; turn pages; repeat”) overlaps with repetition.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 北 (“north”), 国 (“country”), and 男 (“man, male”); none means again, also or or.
