# 策 — strategy; policy

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118), appearing as ⺮ · **JLPT:** N2 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

策 concerns a **plan, policy, measure or strategy**. [対策](../words/対策.md) たいさく names measures or preparations directed at a problem or task. The single-character noun 策 (さく) can also mean a plan or means.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the bamboo top above the central box-and-branch shape. Imagine arranging several measures under one strategy. This is a memory aid, not an explanation of the ancient character.

## Notes

- [案](案.md), proposal or draft plan, and [計](計.md), plan or measure, overlap in planning meanings. Do not turn a useful distinction into a claim that only 策 can describe a strategy.
- 対策 may be preventive or taken after trouble starts. The character alone does not establish that a plan is defensive, effective or already implemented.
- [第](第.md), an ordinal prefix, [箱](箱.md), box, and [筆](筆.md), brush or writing, have different lower components despite their shared bamboo top.
