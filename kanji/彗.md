# 彗 — broom; comet

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 彐 (no. 58) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

彗 has **broom** and **comet** senses. For useful modern vocabulary, start with [彗星](../words/彗星.md) すいせい, "comet." The character's broom reading is recognition material, not a reason to replace ordinary ほうき with 彗 in all writing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the paired upper shapes as broom bristles and the lower part as the grip. A comet's tail can remind you of those bristles. This memory aid is not an etymological claim.

## Notes

- Keep 彗星 separate from a meteor or "shooting star": they name different astronomical phenomena.
- [雪](雪.md), snow, has 雨 across the top. [帰](帰.md), return, has additional left-hand strokes; [習](習.md), learn or practice, ends in 白.
- The indexing radical is 彐, no. 58; locate it at the bottom of the character rather than mistaking the paired upper shapes for two separate characters.
- A font may draw the small lower strokes differently. Use the generated stroke guide for practice; typeface differences alone do not establish different characters.
