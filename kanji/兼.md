# 兼 — combine; concurrent roles

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 八 (a variant in the two top strokes, no. 12) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

兼 brings two or more functions or posts together. [兼ねる](../words/兼ねる.md)
かねる can describe a room serving two purposes or a person holding two posts.
[兼任](../words/兼任.md) けんにん specifically concerns holding concurrent
posts. Concurrent roles need not involve performing two actions at exactly
the same moment.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture two upright posts joined
by several crossbars, sharing one frame. This recalls the modern shape,
not the character's historical origin or a verified phonetic component.
The indexing 八 appears as the paired strokes at the top, rather than as
the spreading bottom strokes seen in [典](典.md).

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケン | on | [兼任](../words/兼任.md) けんにん — holding concurrent posts |
| か.ねる | kun | [兼ねる](../words/兼ねる.md) かねる — combine functions; hold two or more posts |
| -か.ねる | kun | [～かねる](../words/兼ねる.md), usually written in kana after a verb's masu stem: 同意しかねる (どういしかねる) — cannot agree; a difficulty/inability construction, not literal combining |

The two kun entries share the pronunciation かねる but describe different
constructions. Practise one reading key, not two independent readings.
For the suffix, 同意する becomes 同意し + かねる; do not attach it to the
dictionary form or the て-form. It is negative in meaning without ない.
The separate ～かねない pattern means "might," not simply "can."
The linked article explains both uses and their examples.

## Common words

- [兼ねる](../words/兼ねる.md) かねる — combine functions; serve in more than one capacity
- [兼任](../words/兼任.md) けんにん — holding concurrent posts

## Notes

- Between role or function names, 兼 (けん) can mean "also serving as":
  首相兼外相 (しゅしょうけんがいしょう) is a prime minister also serving
  as foreign minister. It is not a general replacement for と or English "and."
- Dictionaries also record a separate "beforehand" branch, as in
  兼題 (けんだい), a poetry topic announced before the meeting.
  Do not force the concurrent-role translation onto every compound.
- Compare [典](典.md) and [具](具.md), which have spreading feet below
  their upper boxes. [曲](曲.md) has a divided enclosure, without 兼's
  long central vertical strokes and spreading lower strokes.
