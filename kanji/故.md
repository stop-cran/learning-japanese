# 故 — reason; former; the late; mishap

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (as 攵, no. 66), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

故 is something from before: a former or old thing, a person who has died, and the reason or circumstance behind an event, including an unexpected mishap. [事故](../words/事故.md) じこ is an accident, [故郷](../words/故郷.md) こきょう is one's hometown or native place, and [故障](../words/故障.md) こしょう is a breakdown or malfunction. JMdict glosses 事故 "accident; incident; trouble" and 故障 "fault; trouble; breakdown; failure; being out of order."

The Daijisen character entry gives six senses for コ. One example of each:

- something from the past: 故事 こじ, "historical event; ancient event";
- long familiar, or former: 故国 ここく, "one's native land";
- old and worn out, used like 古: 反故 ほご, "wastepaper; scrap paper";
- dying: 故人 こじん, "the deceased; the departed";
- an event that causes trouble: 事故;
- deliberately: 故意 こい, "intent; intention; bad faith."

JMdict marks a second sense of 故人, "old friend; longtime acquaintance," as dated. Daijisen also gives the kun reading ことさら, which KANJIDIC2 does not list; JMdict lists 故 as an out-dated kanji usage spelling of 殊更 ことさら, "intentionally; deliberately; designedly; on purpose," usually written in kana. A separate Daijisen entry treats こ【故】 as a prefix before a name for someone who has died; JMdict glosses this 故 こ "the late; the deceased." Another entry, ゆえ【故】, covers a reason or cause, a respectable lineage and, after a noun or verb, "because of." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

Daijisen names 何故 なぜ among words with a hard-to-guess reading. JMdict marks なぜ, "why; how; for what reason," as a gikun or jukujikun reading, a special reading that belongs to the whole word, usually written in kana, so 何故 read なぜ is not split between its kanji. A separate formal or literary entry, 何故 なにゆえ, "why," uses 何 なに and 故 ゆえ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [古](古.md), "old," at the left and 攵 at the right, the same right part as in [攻](攻.md), "attack; go on the offensive," and [教](教.md), "teach, instruct." Imagine an old pot struck with a stick until it breaks: a small mishap that makes it a thing of the past. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 古 or 攵 contributes meaning or sound, although 古 is read コ and ふる.い, readings KANJIDIC2 also lists for 故. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| コ | on | [事故](../words/事故.md) じこ — accident; [故郷](../words/故郷.md) こきょう — hometown; native place; [故障](../words/故障.md) こしょう — breakdown; malfunction |
| ゆえ | kun | 故 ゆえ - reason; circumstances; grounds; cause; usually written in kana; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ふる.い | kun | 故い ふるい - old; aged; ancient; JMdict heads the entry 古い and marks 故い a rarely used kanji form; no word article uses this spelling yet, not selected for questions here |
| もと | kun | 故 もと - former; ex-; past; one-time; JMdict lists 元 and 旧 first; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 事故, 事 supplies ジ and 故 supplies コ. In 故郷, 故 supplies コ and 郷 supplies キョウ; in 故障, 故 supplies コ and 障 supplies ショウ. None of the three has a sound change. 故郷 is also read ふるさと, "hometown; birthplace; native place; one's old home," a separate JMdict entry usually written in kana that lists 古里 as well; ふるさと is a reading of the whole word, not a reading of 故 plus one of 郷. 故 read ゆえ stands alone with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; in 故に ゆえに, "therefore; consequently," に follows it. In 故い, 故 writes ふる and い is okurigana.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows コ, with 故郷, 故意 and 事故 as examples, and ゆえ, with 故 and 故に. ふる.い and もと are not in the table. Practice selects コ, the reading with linked word articles. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [事](事.md), "matter, affair," in 事故, 郷 in 故郷, which has no card yet, and [障](障.md), "obstruct; hinder," in 故障.
- [攻](攻.md), "attack; go on the offensive," [救](救.md), "rescue; save; relief," and [教](教.md), "teach, instruct," also have 攵 at the right. All are different kanji.
- [枯](枯.md), "wither; dry up," [固](固.md), "firm; harden," [個](個.md), "individual; item counter," and [湖](湖.md), "lake," also contain 古 and are read コ.
- [由](由.md), "reason; grounds," [理](理.md), "reason, logic," [因](因.md), "cause; factor," and [訳](訳.md), "translate; explanation," overlap the reason and cause of ゆえ; [亡](亡.md), "deceased," and [死](死.md), "death," overlap "the late" and the dying of 故人 and 物故; [古](古.md), "old," [旧](旧.md), "former; old," [元](元.md), "origin, former," [先](先.md), "previous; ahead (in time)," [昨](昨.md), "yesterday; previous," [昔](昔.md), "old times," and [往](往.md), "going; past," overlap "former" and the old, past things of the first three コ senses, and 古, 旧 and 元 share the readings ふる.い or もと; [厄](厄.md), "misfortune; calamity," overlaps "mishap"; and [障](障.md), "obstruct; hinder," overlaps the "problem; hitch; obstacle; hindrance" sense of 故障. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "reason," "former" or "the late" as exclusive to any one character.
- "The late" means someone who has died, as in 故人, "the deceased; the departed." Being late in time is a different idea, expressed by 遅れる おくれる, "to be late; to be delayed," and 更ける ふける, "to get late." English "late" covers both ideas, so [遅](遅.md), "late; slow," and [更](更.md), "renew; grow late," remain ordinary wrong options for 故.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first コ sense, something from the past: 故実 こじつ, "ancient practices; old customs"; 温故 おんこ, "studying the past; researching history," which JMdict marks as archaic; 世故 せいこ, also read せこ, "worldly affairs"; and 典故 てんこ, "authentic precedent."
- More examples of the second and third コ senses: 故旧 こきゅう, "old acquaintance," 故郷 and 故主 こしゅ, "one's former master"; and 故紙 こし, "used paper; paper for recycling," which JMdict spells 古紙 first.
- More examples of the fourth to sixth コ senses: 物故 ぶっこ, "death (of a person)"; 故障; and 故殺 こさつ, "(voluntary) manslaughter," a law term, and 故買 こばい, "buying stolen goods; receiving," a formal or literary term.
