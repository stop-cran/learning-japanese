# 谷 — valley; ravine

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 谷 (no. 150) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

谷 names a valley, ravine or gorge. The native noun たに and compounds with コク provide useful ways to recognize it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the spreading upper strokes as the sides of a valley leading to the 口-shaped opening below. This is a modern visual association, not a reconstruction of the original character.

| きわ.まる | kun | Source-only reading. JMdict indexes 谷まる きわまる - be stuck; be in a dilemma (search-only kanji form); this index form is not a recommended word example. |

KANJIDIC2 records きわ.まる, and JMdict indexes 谷まる in the family of 極まる and 窮まる. 谷まる is explicitly a search-only kanji form, not a recommended productive spelling. The family's dilemma/stuck sense specifically mentions 窮まる and occasionally 谷まる; this index evidence does not make the ordinary valley noun たに mean a dilemma.
