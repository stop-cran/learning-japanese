# 申 — humbly say

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

申 appears in 申す もうす, a humble way to say something or give one's name.
It also occurs in application words such as [申し込む](../words/申し込む.md) もうしこむ.
The character additionally represents the monkey sign of the traditional zodiac; this is a separate use.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a statement passing straight through a box, entering above and leaving below.
This recalls the shape without claiming a historical derivation.

## Notes

田中と申します。 (たなかともうします。) — My name is Tanaka.
The humble label belongs to this use of 申す; it is not a rule that every compound containing 申 is humble speech.

[田](田.md) keeps its center stroke inside; [由](由.md) extends it only above.
[中](中.md) also has a through-stroke, but no extra horizontal stroke inside the box.
