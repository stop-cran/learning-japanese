# 壁 — wall; barrier

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

壁 is a wall: an upright surface that encloses or divides a space, and by extension a barrier that blocks progress. [壁](../words/壁.md) かべ is a wall. JMdict glosses 壁 かべ "wall; partition" and, in a later sense, "barrier; obstacle; hindrance; difficulty."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ヘキ. One example of each:

- a wall: 壁画 へきが, "fresco; mural; wall painting";
- an earthen wall or fence, a structure built as a partition: 隔壁 かくへき, "barrier wall; bulkhead; partition; septum; diaphragm";
- a flat surface like a wall: 胃壁 いへき, "stomach lining; gastric wall";
- a steep place like a wall, a cliff: 絶壁 ぜっぺき, "precipice; cliff."

For かべ it gives 壁紙 かべがみ, "wallpaper," 壁土 かべつち, "plaster; wall clay; wall mud," and 白壁 しらかべ, "white plaster wall." It names 壁蝨 だに, "tick (Acari spp.); mite," among words with a hard-to-guess reading; JMdict marks that spelling as rarely used and notes that the word is usually written in kana alone. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a 尸 shape with [口](口.md), "mouth," below it at the upper left, [辛](辛.md), "spicy; painful," at the upper right, and [土](土.md), "earth, soil," at the bottom. Imagine a worker grumbling through a painful job, packing earth into a wall. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 口, 辛 or 土 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ヘキ | on | 壁画 へきが - fresco; mural; wall painting; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| かべ | kun | [壁](../words/壁.md) かべ — wall |

The word 壁 is the kanji alone, read かべ, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 壁画, 壁 supplies ヘキ and 画 supplies ガ. After ン or a small っ, ヘキ often becomes ぺき: in 岸壁 がんぺき, "quay; wharf; jetty," 岸 supplies ガン, and in 絶壁, 絶 ゼツ shortens to ぜっ, the small っ doubling the next sound.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ヘキ, with 壁面 へきめん, "surface of a wall," 壁画 and 岸壁 as examples, and かべ, with 壁, 壁土 and 白壁. Practice selects かべ, the reading with a linked word article. ヘキ is the reading of most 壁 compounds, but no word article uses it yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The common word 壁 contains only this kanji.
- [辛](辛.md), "spicy; painful," is the upper right part of 壁. [基](基.md), "foundation; basis," has the same 土 at the bottom, and [坂](坂.md), "slope; incline," has 土 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [塀](塀.md), "boundary wall; fence," [隔](隔.md), "separate; partition; every other," [関](関.md), "involve; barrier," and [障](障.md), "obstruct; hinder," overlap the senses "wall," "partition," "barrier" and "obstacle" that 壁 and its words carry; 隔 and 障 appear with 壁 in 隔壁 and 障壁 しょうへき, "enclosing wall; boundary fence," and also "obstacle; barrier." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "wall" or "barrier" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ヘキ senses: 壁面 and 外壁 がいへき, "outer wall," for a wall; 障壁, 城壁 じょうへき, "castle wall; town walls; defensive wall; rampart," and 鉄壁 てっぺき, "iron wall; impregnable fortress," for a partition; 腹壁 ふくへき, "abdominal wall," for a flat surface; and 岸壁, 岩壁 がんぺき, "wall of rock; rock cliff; rock face," and 氷壁 ひょうへき, "ice wall; ice ridge," for a cliff. For かべ it also gives 壁新聞 かべしんぶん, "wall newspaper; wall poster."
