# 維 — fiber

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

KANJIDIC2 lists "fiber," "tie" and "rope" for 維. The meaning of a compound must still be checked as a whole: [維持](../words/維持.md) (いじ) means maintenance or preservation, but that does not establish "maintenance" as a general standalone meaning of 維. 繊維 (せんい) means fiber.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice 糸 on the left beside 隹 on the right; picture a strand running alongside the bird-shaped form. This describes the modern shape only, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

Practice selects イ, also listed in the Joyo table. KANJIDIC2 gives no kun reading and separately lists the name readings これ, たもつ, つぐ, ゆい and ゆき. JMdict includes 維 as a rarely used kanji form of これ in a pronoun family marked usually written in kana. Keep that qualified spelling and the nanori distinct from the ordinary on/kun metadata and selected practice.

## Common words

- [維持](../words/維持.md) いじ - maintenance; preservation
- 維新 いしん - reformation; renewal
- 繊維 せんい - fiber
- 線維 せんい - a spelling JMdict restricts to medical contexts

## Notes

- The character-level senses "tie" and "rope" do not make every compound with 維 a physical cord. In particular, 維持's maintenance sense belongs to the complete word unless separately established for the character.
- JMdict marks 線維 as used only in medical contexts; the common spelling in the fiber sense is 繊維.
- The broad N1/All reading inventory includes [由](由.md) with ユイ, matching 維's nanori ゆい. The reading-pool audit found this is a latent collision, not a drawable option in the shipped pools. It does not make ゆい an ordinary practice reading of 維.
- [誰](誰.md), who; [催](催.md), host/sponsor an event; and [集](集.md), gather, all include 隹 in different arrangements. Their meanings are not implied by that shared shape.
