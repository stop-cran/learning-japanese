# 維 — fiber

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

KANJIDIC2 lists "fiber," "tie" and "rope" for 維. Digital Daijisen separately gives "main principle," "rope/thread," and a つなぎとめる ("tie or hold fast") sense, with [維持](../words/維持.md) (いじ) as an example. The whole compound means maintenance or preservation; the example supports a hold-fast sense of 維, not "maintenance" as a general standalone gloss. 繊維 (せんい) means fiber.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice 糸 on the left beside 隹 on the right; picture a strand running alongside the bird-shaped form. This describes the modern shape only, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

Practice selects イ, also listed in the Joyo table. KANJIDIC2 gives no kun reading and separately lists the name readings これ, たもつ, つぐ, ゆい and ゆき. Digital Daijisen classifies ユイ as a Go-on on-reading, while KANJIDIC2 classifies ゆい as nanori; these source classifications are preserved without changing the on/kun metadata or selected practice. JMdict includes 維 as a rarely used kanji form of これ in a pronoun family marked usually written in kana.

## Notes

- The character-level senses "tie" and "rope" do not make every compound with 維 a physical cord. In particular, 維持's maintenance sense belongs to the complete word unless separately established for the character.
- JMdict marks 線維 as used only in medical contexts; the common spelling in the fiber sense is 繊維.
- In the revised N1/All reading pools, the on-capable choices [録](録.md), [催](催.md), and [集](集.md) fill all three wrong-option slots, so [由](由.md)'s on-reading ユイ is not drawn as a fallback there. The bounded single-borrow Starter test can still draw it if 由 is borrowed alongside 維. This is static analysis, not Kotlin/native/device verification or assurance for arbitrary pools or multiple borrowed cards.
- [索](索.md) and [緒](緒.md) overlap with 維's rope/tie senses; [糸](糸.md) with its fiber/thread sense; [結](結.md) and [括](括.md) with tying/binding; and [保](保.md), [持](持.md), [支](支.md), and [留](留.md) with holding, supporting, or preserving. These are targeted meaning-choice guards, not unrestricted synonym claims. 維持's broader maintenance meaning remains a whole-word sense.
- [絡](絡.md), entwine/get entangled; [接](接.md), touch/join; and [挟](挟.md), pinch/hold between, are guarded conservatively around binding, joining, or holding. [線](線.md) includes wire as well as a drawn line or route, near the thread/strand sense. These guards do not make tangling, touching, pinching, or a railway line general meanings of 維.
- [録](録.md), record/register; [催](催.md), host/sponsor an event; and [集](集.md), gather, have distinct meanings. Their useful on readings fill the curated reading-choice slots; shared shape alone does not determine meaning.
