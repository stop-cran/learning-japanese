# 貝 — shellfish; seashell

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

貝 names shellfish and their shells. The ordinary native noun かい must be distinguished from the specific snail name used to illustrate バイ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the 目-shaped upper part as a ridged shell, with its opening suggested by the two lower strokes. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical claim.

## Notes

- The バイ row uses the specific sea-snail sense, which JMdict marks usually written in kana; バイ is not a general replacement for かい. A separate abbreviated sense in the same entry names a spinning top traditionally made from that snail's shell.
- 貝 itself is radical no. 154. You can recognize it in [貨](貨.md), [販](販.md), [貯](貯.md) and [貿](貿.md); those examples concern goods, selling, savings and trade, not literal shellfish.
- Compare [見](見.md), see, with its different lower shape, and [目](目.md), eye, without the two lower strokes.
