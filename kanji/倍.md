# 倍 — multiple; double

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

倍 expresses a multiple of an amount. 倍 ばい by itself can mean double or twice as much; after a number, it expresses that many times the reference amount. 倍増 ばいぞう means doubling.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the person-shaped 亻 announcing a doubled amount beside the stacked 立-like top and 口-like bottom.

The canonical radical 人 has the left-hand form 亻.

## Notes

- 三倍 さんばい is three times as much, not an instruction to add three more copies to the original amount.
- Distinguish [位](位.md), rank or position: 倍 has the additional 口-shaped lower part on the right.
