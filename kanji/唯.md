# 唯 — only; solely; merely

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

唯 means only: just this one, and nothing else. [唯一](../words/唯一.md) ゆいいつ means only, sole or unique, as in 唯一の方法 ゆいいつのほうほう, "the only method."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for each on reading. For ユイ it gives "only ... and nothing more," as in 唯一, 唯物論 ゆいぶつろん, "materialism," 唯心論 ゆいしんろん, "spiritualism; idealism; mentalism," and 唯我独尊 ゆいがどくそん, "throughout heaven and earth, I alone am the honored one," which JMdict also glosses "self-conceit; self-centeredness; vainglory."

For イ it gives "yes" as a reply of consent, as in 唯々諾々 いいだくだく, "obedient; submissive; tame; slavish." Its kun reading is ただ. A separate Daijisen headword たんだ, written ▽唯, gives a reading KANJIDIC2 does not list; JMdict has no たんだ entry written with 唯.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [口](口.md), "mouth," at the left and 隹 at the right, the shape also found in [集](集.md) and [進](進.md). Imagine one lone bird with one mouth: it is the only one singing. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 口 or 隹 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ユイ | on | [唯一](../words/唯一.md) ゆいいつ — only; sole; unique |
| イ | on | 唯々諾々 いいだくだく - obedient; submissive; tame; slavish; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ただ | kun | 唯でさえ ただでさえ - even at the best of times; already; (even) under normal circumstances (usually written in kana); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 唯一, 唯 supplies ユイ and 一 supplies イツ, giving ゆいいつ; JMdict also records ゆいつ. In 唯々諾々, the mark 々 repeats the kanji before it: 唯々 is いい and 諾々 is だくだく. In 唯でさえ, 唯 writes ただ and でさえ follows in kana. JMdict also lists 唯 as a rarely used kanji form of the word ただ, "only; merely"; the word article ただ here is written in kana and does not declare 唯.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ユイ, with 唯一, 唯物論 and 唯美主義 ゆいびしゅぎ, "aestheticism; estheticism," as examples; and イ, with 唯々諾々. ただ is not in the official table. Practice selects ユイ, the reading with a linked word article; イ and ただ are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [一](一.md), "one," in 唯一.
- [集](集.md), "gather," has 隹 at the top, and [進](進.md), "advance," has 隹 inside the movement shape 辶. [雑](雑.md), "miscellaneous," has 隹 at the right, as 唯 does. All are different kanji.
- [独](独.md), "alone; sole; Germany (abbreviation)," [単](単.md), "single; simple," and [専](専.md), "specialize; exclusive," overlap the senses "only," "sole" and "solely" that 唯 and its word carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "only" or "sole" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, another Daijisen example of the ユイ sense: 唯識 ゆいしき, "vijnapti-matrata (theory that all existence is subjective and nothing exists outside of the mind)."
