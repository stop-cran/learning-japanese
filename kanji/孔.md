# 孔 — hole; opening

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 子 (no. 39) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

孔 can be a hole or physical opening. The opening in the eye, 瞳孔 (どうこう, pupil), illustrates the compound reading; 穴 is the spelling to write for the ordinary noun あな. Recognize 孔 when reading about a physical opening.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 子 on the left and the long turning hook at the right. [乳](乳.md) has more strokes above its left portion; [札](札.md) puts 木 on the left.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| コウ | on | 瞳孔 (どうこう), the pupil of the eye. |
| ク | on | 孔子 (くじ), an alternate reading of the name Confucius, also read こうし; ク is outside the standard-use table. |
| あな | kun | [孔](../words/穴.md) あな — physical hole or opening. This reading of 孔 is outside the standard-use table. |

## Common words

## Notes

[乱](乱.md) has 舌 rather than 子 on the left. [穴](穴.md) overlaps in meaning and reading: recognize 孔 for a physical opening, but keep 穴 in expressions such as 計画に穴がある (けいかくにあながある), “there is a flaw in the plan.” The hook on the right is also a useful landmark in [乳](乳.md) and [札](札.md), but neither has just 子 on its left.
