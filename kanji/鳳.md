# 鳳 — male Chinese phoenix

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 鳥 (no. 196) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鳳 is the **Chinese phoenix** character. Learn it in [鳳凰](../words/鳳凰.md)
(ほうおう), the mythical bird called the *fenghuang*. In the traditional explanation,
鳳 is male and 凰 is female. That distinction explains the paired characters; it is
not a rule that every modern depiction must show two birds or specify their sex.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [鳥](鳥.md) inside the wide outer frame,
below a short horizontal stroke. Picture a magnificent bird sheltered within the frame.
This is a memory aid for the current shape, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

Source-only does not mean フウ is invalid or absent from every dictionary or context.

**Another documented lexical reading:** 鳳 can also be read おおとり for the mythical bird.
The frozen KANJIDIC2 source puts おおとり in its name-reading section, while other
character dictionaries classify it as kun. Its lexical use is independently documented:
it is not merely a personal name. This card preserves the source's empty kun list rather
than reclassifying metadata. Do not confuse this bird spelling with 大トリ (おおとり),
the last or key performer.

## Common words

- [鳳凰](../words/鳳凰.md) (ほうおう) — Chinese phoenix; fenghuang, also used for its depicted motif

This is useful recognition vocabulary for mythology and decorative designs, not the
ordinary name of a living bird species. Standalone 鳳 (ほう) is marked rare in JMdict.

## Notes

- [風](風.md), "wind; style," has a similar frame but a different interior.
  [鶴](鶴.md), "crane (bird)," and [鷹](鷹.md), "hawk; falcon," also contain 鳥,
  but name real birds rather than the Chinese phoenix.
- The English translation "phoenix" does not by itself imply rebirth from ashes.
  Learn the Chinese bird tradition separately from the phoenix called フェニックス.
- The source's grade-9/name-use classification does not make every occurrence a name.
  鳳凰 is a documented noun; personal-name readings are not a course on this card.
