# 衛 — guard; defense

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 行 (no. 144) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

衛 concerns guarding or defending, and can name the person guarding.
[自衛](../words/自衛.md) じえい is self-defense. The character also has
a surrounding or circling branch, illustrated by
[衛星](../words/衛星.md) えいせい, satellite. Learn that astronomical
word as a whole; a satellite need not literally protect its planet.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the left 彳 and right 亍
around the central 韋-shaped block. Imagine a patrol circling that block.
The two outer sides together are the indexing radical 行, not two
independently pronounced words. This is a memory scene for the present
outline, not a reconstruction of its history or sound.

## Notes

- [古](古.md) is old, [右](右.md) a direction and [川](川.md) a river.
  An old patrol, a right turn or a guarded riverbank is an association,
  not a meaning of 衛.
- [守](守.md), [保](保.md) and [防](防.md) overlap protection.
  [看](看.md), [監](監.md) and [警](警.md) include watching over or
  guarding; observation and defense are not identical activities.
  [兵](兵.md) and [卒](卒.md) retain soldier senses, compared
  conservatively with the guard-person branch.
- [囲](囲.md), [包](包.md) and [括](括.md) include enclosing;
  [周](周.md), [回](回.md), [転](転.md), [輪](輪.md) and
  [舞](舞.md) retain circuit, revolving or circling ranges.
  Direct quiz precautions protect broad labels without claiming that
  every bundle, wheel or dance is a guard or a satellite.
- 衞 is an older form in the sources, not this card's writing target.
  衛 also names a particular ancient Chinese state, read えい.
  That proper name is not a generic word for any country or province.
  字通 additionally records 衛服, a historical territorial category;
  do not apply it to modern administrative divisions.
- Spellings and whole-word readings such as 衛士 えじ and
  兵衛-name endings are not rules for assigning every sound in the
  word to 衛 alone. Likewise, the hygiene sense of 衛生 えいせい
  does not make 衛 alone a general word for health.
