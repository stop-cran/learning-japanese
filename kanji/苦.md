# 苦 — bitter; suffering

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (as 艹, no. 140) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

苦 connects bitterness with hardship and distress, but the words are not interchangeable.
[苦い](../words/苦い.md) にがい describes a bitter taste; 苦しい くるしい describes
something painful or difficult. The reading is part of the meaning you must learn.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine bitter herbs at the top 艹, with 古 below as the identifying
shape. This is a modern memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

The radical 艸 takes the visible form 艹.
Compare the lower 古 with the different shapes in [若](若.md) and [草](草.md).
[古](古.md) alone means "old," not "bitter"; adding a component does not preserve a character's
standalone meaning automatically.
