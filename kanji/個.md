# 個 — individual; item counter

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

個 concerns an individual unit. 個人 こじん means an individual or private person. As a counter, 個 counts items, as in 一個 いっこ, one item; it does not replace every more specific Japanese counter.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a person-shaped 亻 beside the box-like 固, keeping one individual object inside a separate box.

The canonical radical 人 appears as the left-hand form 亻.

## Notes

- 一個 is いっこ, with a small っ, not いちこ. Learn counter pronunciations with the number rather than simply joining dictionary readings.
- 個人 is a person considered individually. Its use of 個 does not mean that 個 is the normal counter for people; people are counted with 人.
