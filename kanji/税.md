# 税 — tax; fiscal duty

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

税 concerns tax, whether as a noun or in the name of a particular tax. English duty here means a monetary tax, not a person's general obligation or a task at work.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look at 禾 on the left and the 兑-shaped right side; imagine setting aside part of a harvest as tax. This is an invented modern-shape scene, not a claim about how taxation created the character.

The canonical key 禾, no. 115, appears on the left.

## Notes

- [税](../words/税.md) and [税金](../words/税金.md) overlap in the tax meaning; they are not automatically two different kinds of payment.
- A fee or charge is not necessarily a tax. See [料金](../words/料金.md) りょうきん for service charges; the reason for the payment matters.
- The left side distinguishes 税 from [祝](祝.md). Their right-hand shapes are also different; similar outlines do not make tax and congratulations synonyms.
