# 味 — taste, flavor

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

味 means taste or flavor and extends to a thing's appeal or character. In compounds such as 意味, the meaning is abstract rather than a food's taste.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** connect the [口](口.md), "mouth," on the left with tasting food. Notice 未 on the right, but do not assume a historical role for it from this mnemonic.

## Notes

[妹](妹.md), "younger sister," has the same right-hand shape but [女](女.md) on the left.
[話](話.md), "speak," differs on both sides: 言 on the left and 舌 (not 未) on the right.

Learn compounds as whole words: 意味 does not mean an edible flavor.
