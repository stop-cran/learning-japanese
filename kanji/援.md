# 援 — aid; assistance

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

援 concerns reaching out to help someone. [援助](../words/援助.md) えんじょ
is aid or assistance, and [応援](../words/応援.md) おうえん is support,
including cheering for someone. 声援 せいえん is cheering or shouted
encouragement, 支援 しえん is support or backing, 救援 きゅうえん is relief
or rescue, and 援軍 えんぐん is reinforcement.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: reaching out a hand
to help, as in 援護, 援助 and 応援, and drawing in an example from
elsewhere, as in 援引 and 援用. It lists たすける and ひく as kun readings.
Those readings are not in KANJIDIC2 or the official Joyo table, so practice
does not select them. Its supplementary note says the 援 in 義援金 and
援護射撃 stands in for 捐 and 掩, respectively.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the left-hand form of
[手](手.md), beside 爰. Inside 爰, ⺤ sits above a part that contains
[友](友.md). Imagine a hand reaching out to a friend to lend aid. This is a
memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| エン | on | [援助](../words/援助.md) えんじょ - aid; assistance; [応援](../words/応援.md) おうえん - support; cheering |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects エン. In 援助, 援 is
えん and 助 is じょ; in 応援, [応](応.md) is おう and 援 is えん. The official
Joyo table lists エン only, with 援助, 応援 and 声援 as examples.

## Notes

- [暖](暖.md), "warm; warm up," shares the right-hand 爰 with 援, and
  [授](授.md), "grant; impart; teach," and [担](担.md), "shoulder; take on,"
  share 扌 at the left. Their meanings differ from 援's.
- Helping and rescuing overlap [助](助.md), "help; rescue," [丞](丞.md),
  "assist; aid," and [支](支.md), "support," because KANJIDIC2 glosses 援
  "help" and "save," JMdict glosses 援助 "assistance; aid; support," and the
  応援, 声援 and 支援 entries also include "support." [保](保.md),
  "protect; preserve," overlaps the "save" sense, as do [守](守.md),
  "guard; obey," and [防](防.md), "defend; ward off," through protection
  and rescue; not every act of aid is defense. [貯](貯.md), "save up;
  store," shares its English word although it concerns putting money or
  goods aside.
- Encouraging and cheering, as in 声援 and the second sense of 応援, overlap
  [励](励.md), "encourage; be diligent," and [勧](勧.md), "recommend;
  encourage." [根](根.md), "root; foundation," shares the stem "root" with
  the "rooting (for)" glosses of 応援 and 声援, although it never concerns
  cheering.
- Reinforcement, as in 援軍 and JMdict's first sense of 応援, overlaps
  [補](補.md), "supplement; make good," and [協](協.md), "cooperate; join
  forces."
- Drawing in an example from elsewhere, the second Daijisen sense with 援引
  and 援用, overlaps [例](例.md), "example; precedent," [典](典.md),
  "reference texts; rules; ceremony," and [引](引.md), "pull; draw." These
  direct meaning comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as
  identical in every compound.
