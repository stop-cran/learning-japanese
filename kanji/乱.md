# 乱 — disorder; disturbance

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 乙 (no. 5) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

乱 concerns a state of disorder or an action that disrupts order. 混乱 こんらん is confusion or disorder. The verb pair 乱れる みだれる and 乱す みだす distinguishes becoming disordered from disturbing something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the straight-looking left cluster being knocked out of order by the hooked stroke on the right.

The indexing radical 乙 appears here as the hooked element on the right; it is not a separate word in this character.
