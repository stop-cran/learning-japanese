# 船 — ship; boat

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 舟 (no. 137) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

船 names watercraft, from boats to ships. The word [船](../words/船.md) ふね does not
by itself tell you the vessel's size or means of propulsion.
In compounds, the reading may be セン or the combining form ふな-.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the narrow 舟 on the left as the vessel and imagine the
口-shaped lower-right part as a cabin window. This is a memory picture, not an origin claim.

## Notes
