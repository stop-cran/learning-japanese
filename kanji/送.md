# 送 — send; escort

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

送 concerns sending something or seeing someone on their way. [送る](../words/送る.md) おくる can mean sending a message,
mailing an item, or taking someone home.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the two upper marks as a parcel being placed above the broad inner shape,
then sent along the sweeping outer path. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical account.

## Notes

Compare [近](近.md) and [通](通.md), which share the sweeping outer shape but have different inner parts.
贈る is also おくる, but means giving a gift or award. Sending an item and giving it as a present are different ideas.
