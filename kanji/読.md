# 読 — read

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 言 (as 訁, no. 149) · **Phonetic:** 売 (from older 賣) · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

読 is the modern form of 讀: 言 (words, speech) plus 賣 as the sound component, which post-war reform simplified to 売. The meaning
side, 言, shows that the character is about words and speech; reading in the old sense was reading aloud. It means "read".
The sound element is only a rough guide today: 売 is read バイ or う.る, not ドク.

## Notes

- The kun reading is よ.む; 読み is the noun form (読み方, 読み物). The ordinary on reading is ドク; トク and トウ are in a few set words.
- Look-alikes with 売 on the right: 続 (continue, 糸 on the left). Do not confuse 売 (sell) with [買](買.md) (buy), which has 罒 over 貝; the two are opposites (売買 ばいばい).
- Do not confuse 読 (言 on the left) with 話 and [書](書.md), which also have a speech or writing side.
- 読む also appears in 空気を読む (read the room) and 数を読む (count).
