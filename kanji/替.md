# 替 — replace; substitute

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 曰 (no. 73) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

替 concerns replacement, substitution, or a change from one thing to another. 替える かえる is transitive, while 替わる かわる describes a replacement or change occurring. 交替 こうたい can mean replacement or taking turns.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the two 夫-like shapes above the lower enclosure and imagine two people taking turns. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical explanation.

The canonical indexing radical is 曰, no. 73, not 日, no. 72. The lower enclosure resembles 日 in print; keep the source's indexing distinction.

## Notes

- The bound かえ- form appears in 替え歌 かえうた: the lyrics are changed while the melody is retained. It is not a separate pronunciation of the whole word.
- 替える is ichidan, with 替えて かえて; 替わる is godan, with 替わって かわって. The same English gloss replace does not make their particle patterns identical.
- JMdict groups 替える, [換える](../words/換える.md) and 代える together, with spelling tendencies rather than exclusive meanings. It also lists 交代 and 交替 together for こうたい.
- 為替 かわせ uses a special whole-word reading; do not assign わせ as an independent reading of 替.
