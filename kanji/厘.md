# 厘 — one-hundredth; rin (1/1000 yen)

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 厂 (no. 27) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

厘 is a unit for very small quantities. For the word [厘](../words/厘.md) りん, Digital Daijisen gives three senses: one-hundredth of one, or of a 割 (wari); in the traditional system of measures, one-hundredth of a 寸 (sun) in length or of a 匁 (monme) in weight; and a monetary unit equal to one-thousandth of a yen, or one-tenth of a 銭 (sen).

## Notes

Digital Daijisen notes that 厘 was originally an abbreviated form of 釐, and JMdict entry 1555420 lists 釐 as an outdated kanji spelling of the same word.

銭 (“coin; sen (1/100 yen)”) is the next larger monetary unit; Digital Daijisen gives one rin as one-tenth of a sen. 分 (“minute, part”), read ぶ, is the fractional unit ten times larger; JMdict glosses 分 ぶ as one-tenth, including 3 mm as one-tenth of a sun (entry 1502850), while 厘 is one-hundredth, including 0.3 mm as one-hundredth of a sun, and 分 and 厘 appear together in 一分一厘 and 九分九厘. 寸 (“sun (length unit); small”) is the length unit that a rin divides: both JMdict and Digital Daijisen give one rin of length as one-hundredth of a sun.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 左 (“left”), 青 (“blue, green”), and 電 (“electricity”); none means a small fractional unit.
