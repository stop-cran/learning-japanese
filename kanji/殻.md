# 殻 — shell; husk; empty outer covering

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 殳 (no. 79) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

殻 is a hard outer covering: the shell of an egg or nut, a husk or a hull. The linked word [殻](../words/殻.md) から is JMdict's "shell; husk; hull; pod; chaff." The same word carries the idea of what is left once the contents are gone. Digital Daijisen's から entry lists, among its senses, the hard covering of an animal's body or of a plant's fruit or seed, such as an eggshell; the skin shed by an insect or other animal; something used up and left empty, such as a lunchbox; and an outer wall one withdraws into, as in 殻に閉じこもる からにとじこもる, "to withdraw into oneself; to shut oneself away; to seclude oneself." Its character entry for カク defines the character as a hard outer skin covering the surface of a thing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 殳 at the right and a 士 shape near the top left. Imagine a hard shell resting on a stick. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 殳 or 士 contributes meaning or sound; [士](士.md), "warrior; professional," is a separate kanji. Historical formation is not established here.

殻 から is the single character writing the whole native noun; JMdict lists 骸 as a "rarely used kanji form," and Daijisen writes 骸 for the sense "a body the soul has left." JMdict has a separate entry 殻 がら, marked "word usually written using kana alone," with senses including "chicken bones (e.g. for soup); chicken carcass" and the suffix "left-overs; remnants"; がら is the voiced form that から takes inside compounds such as 貝殻 and 吸い殻. 殻 かく, "shell (e.g. electron shell)," is another JMdict entry. In 地殻 and 甲殻, [地](地.md) supplies チ and 甲 supplies コウ while 殻 supplies カク; in 貝殻, [貝](貝.md) supplies かい. Whole words are not split: 抜け殻 ぬけがら, "cast-off skin (snake, insect, etc.); husk; empty shell; exuvia; exuviae," and もぬけの殻 もぬけのから, "completely empty (of a residence, etc.); vacant; deserted," with another sense "shed skin (of a snake, insect, etc.)" noted as the original meaning; 籾殻 もみがら, "rice husks; chaff"; and おから, which JMdict writes 御殻 among other spellings and marks "word usually written using kana alone," "okara; soy pulp; tofu dregs."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カク with 甲殻 and 地殻, and から with 殻 and 貝殻, and gives 殼 as the older form of 殻. Practice selects から, the reading with a linked word article. The other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words where possible; being outside the practice set does not make them invalid.

## Notes

- The common word is the single character 殻, so it has no other kanji. [空](空.md), "sky; empty," is read から as a different word, so it is never an option here.
- 殳 is the shape at the right of 殻. [殺](殺.md), "kill," [投](投.md), "throw," and [設](設.md), "establish; provide," also have 殳 at the right. All are different kanji.
- [木](木.md), "tree, wood," [本](本.md), "book, origin," and [休](休.md), "rest," are different kanji with no reading or sense in common with 殻.
- [貝](貝.md), "shellfish; seashell," [皮](皮.md), "skin; hide," [残](残.md), "remain; leftover," [被](被.md), "cover; incur," [外](外.md), "outside," and [袋](袋.md), "bag; sack; pouch," overlap the covering or leftover senses of 殻 and its words. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. [柄](柄.md) shares がら with 殻. Do not treat "shell," "husk" or "leftover" as exclusive to any one character.
