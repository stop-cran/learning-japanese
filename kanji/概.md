# 概 — outline; roughly; bearing

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

概 looks at the whole and ignores the details: an outline, a rough overall view, and a person's visible bearing. The linked [概論](../words/概論.md) がいろん is a general outline or introduction to a subject, a discussion in broad outline.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ガイ: (1) levelling out, treating as a whole, the rough outline, as in 概要 がいよう, "outline; summary; overview; synopsis; abstract; abridgment; abridgement," and 大概 たいがい, "generally; mainly; usually; normally; mostly; for the most part"; and (2) the dignity or spirit that shows in a person, as in 気概 きがい, "strong spirit; mettle; backbone; guts; fighting spirit." Daijisen's note says the original meaning of 概 is a rod for levelling the top of a measure; this card makes no etymology claim from it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md) at the left and [既](既.md) at the right. Imagine a wooden rod that smooths a pile flat. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 木 or 既 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ガイ | on | [概論](../words/概論.md) がいろん — introduction; outline; general remarks |
| おおむ.ね | kun | 概ね おおむね — in general; generally; mostly; roughly; largely; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 概論, 概 supplies ガイ and [論](論.md) supplies ロン. 概ね おおむね is one whole word: JMdict marks sense 1 "word usually written using kana alone" and lists 大旨 as a "rarely used kanji form"; sense 2 is "gist; point; main idea." Its reading おおむね is not split between kanji. Kanjipedia also lists the on reading カイ, a historical reading that this card does not add.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ガイ only, with 概念, 大概 and 概して. 概念 がいねん means "general idea; concept; notion," and 概して がいして means "generally; as a rule." It prints 槪 in round brackets after 概, an older form that this card does not teach. Practice selects ガイ, the reading with a linked word article; the kun reading おおむ.ね comes from KANJIDIC2, and being outside the practice set does not make it invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [論](論.md), "argument; discourse," in 概論.
- [旨](旨.md), "purport; gist; delicious," [約](約.md), "promise; approximately," [般](般.md), "general; kind; category," [粗](粗.md), "coarse; rough; crude," [荒](荒.md), "rough; wild; laid waste," [気](気.md), "spirit, air," [要](要.md), "necessity; essentials," [略](略.md), "abbreviate; omit," [総](総.md), "whole; overall; total," [全](全.md), "whole; complete," [抽](抽.md), "pull out; extract; abstract," and [威](威.md), "intimidate; authority; dignity," overlap the senses of outline, general view, roughly and bearing. 要 appears in 概要 and 気 in 気概. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "outline," "general" or "spirit" as exclusive to any one character.
- [既](既.md), "already; previously," is the shape at the right of 概 and is read キ. [模](模.md), "imitate; model; pattern," and [梅](梅.md), "ume; Japanese apricot," have 木 at the left, like 概. All are different kanji.
