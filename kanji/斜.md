# 斜 — diagonal; slanting; oblique

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 斗 (no. 68), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

斜 is a line or surface that runs at a slant instead of straight across or straight up: diagonal, slanting, oblique. [斜め](../words/斜め.md) ななめ is slanting or diagonal; JMdict glosses it "slanting; tilted; sloping; diagonal; oblique" and, in a second sense, "distorted (feeling); slanted (e.g. view of the world); bad (mood); amiss; awry." [斜](../words/斜.md) はす is a diagonal; JMdict glosses it "diagonal."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for シャ: tilted, at a slant. Its examples are 斜影 しゃえい, "slanted or oblique shadow"; 斜光 しゃこう, "slanting rays of light"; 斜線 しゃせん, "oblique line; forward slash"; 斜面 しゃめん, "slope; slanting surface; bevel"; 斜陽 しゃよう, "setting sun," with a second sense "decline (e.g. of industry)"; and 傾斜 けいしゃ, "inclination; slant; slope; bevel; list; dip." It gives the kun readings ななめ and はす, the same as KANJIDIC2, and has separate entries for しゃ【斜】 and はす【斜】.

Daijisen names 斜交 among words with a hard-to-guess reading, read はすかい. That reading belongs to the whole word and is not split here. JMdict reads the spelling 斜交 as しゃこう, "oblique," and has a separate entry 斜交い はすかい, also spelled はす交い, "aslant; oblique; diagonal; askew; cater-cornered," usually written in kana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [余](余.md), "surplus," at the left and 斗, which has no card here, at the right, the same right part as in [料](料.md), "materials, fee." Imagine pouring the surplus out of a ladle by tipping it at a slant. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 余 or 斗 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シャ | on | 斜面 しゃめん - slope; slanting surface; bevel; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| なな.め | kun | [斜め](../words/斜め.md) ななめ — slanting; diagonal |
| はす | kun | [斜](../words/斜.md) はす — diagonal |

In 斜め, 斜 writes なな and め is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict also lists the spelling 斜 for ななめ, marked irregular okurigana usage, and 傾 for ななめ, marked word containing out-dated kanji or kanji usage; the reading なのめ is marked out-dated or obsolete kana usage. In 斜 read はす, the kanji alone writes はす with no okurigana; JMdict gives the same entry the reading しゃ as well. In 斜面, 斜線 and 傾斜, 斜 supplies シャ; in 傾斜, 傾 supplies ケイ. JMdict also has 斜向かい, spelled はす向かい first, "catercorner; diagonally opposite."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シャ, with 斜面, 斜線 and 傾斜 as examples, and ななめ, with 斜め. はす is not in the table. Practice selects なな.め and はす, the readings with linked word articles. シャ is the reading of most 斜 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; it is illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 斜め is 斜 with okurigana only, and the word 斜 is the kanji alone.
- [余](余.md), "surplus," sits at the left of 斜, and [除](除.md), "remove; exclude," and [途](途.md), "route; course," contain 余. All are different kanji.
- [料](料.md), "materials, fee," also has 斗 at the right, and [塗](塗.md), "paint; coat," and [徐](徐.md), "slowly; gradually," also contain 余.
- [傾](傾.md), "tilt; incline," overlaps "slanting" and appears with 斜 in 傾斜, "inclination; slant"; [坂](坂.md), "slope; incline," and [阪](阪.md), "slope; Osaka in compounds," overlap the slope of 斜面 and the sloping sense of 斜め; and [悪](悪.md), "bad, evil," overlaps the "bad (mood); amiss; awry" sense of 斜め. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "slanting," "tilt," "slope" or "bad" as exclusive to any one character.
