# 両 — both

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

両 brings two sides or members of a pair into view together. In [両方](../words/両方.md) りょうほう, neither side is excluded;
両手 りょうて means both hands.
Do not use "both" as a general replacement for "all" when more than two independent items are meant.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see two inner uprights sheltered by one broad top line: both belong under the same cover.
This describes the modern shape, not its origin.

## Notes

The boxed silhouettes of [雨](雨.md), [内](内.md), and [西](西.md) can distract the eye.
Check the interior rather than treating every broad frame as the same character.
両 also occurs in historical currency and vehicle-counting uses; the pair meaning is the focus here.
The source's kun readings are preserved in metadata, not recommended as substitutes for リョウ in these words.
