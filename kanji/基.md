# 基 — foundation; basis

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

基 is a foundation: first the base a building stands on, then the base of anything else. [基礎](../words/基礎.md) きそ is a foundation or basis, and [基本](../words/基本.md) きほん is the basics. The other common words are listed below.

For キ the Daijisen character entry lists the foundation of a building, as in 基礎; the foundation or ground of a thing, as in 基準, 基地, 基盤 and 基本; being based on something, as in 基因 きいん, a rarely used spelling of 起因, "to be caused by" or "cause"; and, in chemistry, a group of atoms, as in 塩基 えんき, "base." It also lists a counter for installed objects such as machines, stone lanterns and gravestones. KANJIDIC2 gives the meanings "fundamentals," "radical (chem)," "counter for machines" and "foundation."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [土](土.md), "earth, soil," at the bottom, with the boxy shape 其 resting on top of it, the way a building rests on the ground. Remember 基 as the foundation under something. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 土 or 其 contributes meaning or sound, and 土 is simply the key shape at the bottom. Historical formation is not established here.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |

In 基礎, 礎 supplies ソ. In 基本, 本 supplies ホン. In 基準, 準 supplies ジュン. In 基地, 地 supplies チ. In 基盤, 盤 supplies バン. In 基づく, 基 is read もと and づく is the verb ending.

Standing alone, 基 is also read き: JMdict lists it as a counter for installed or mounted objects, such as stone lanterns, gravestones and satellites, and as a chemical term for a group of atoms, as in 塩基.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キ with 基礎, 基準 and 基地 as examples, もと with 基 and 基づく, and もとい with 基. For もと it adds the spelling contrast ⇔ 下, 元, 本: the same reading is written with those other kanji for other senses.

JMdict lists もと as one native noun with the spellings 元, 本, 素 and 基; the senses "(one's) side" and "capital; principal; cost price" are restricted to 元, and the counter senses are restricted to 本, so the article above teaches only the "basis; foundation" sense for 基.

Practice selects キ and もと. The もとい reading is retained from KANJIDIC2, the Joyo table and the Daijisen character entry and is illustrated with an unlinked dictionary word.

The Daijisen character entry also lists 基督 キリスト as a hard-to-read spelling; JMdict marks it as an ateji, a spelling chosen for its sounds rather than its meaning, for "Christ," usually written in kana alone. KANJIDIC2 does not list this reading, so it is not part of the metadata here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [地](地.md), "ground, land," in 基地, [本](本.md), "book, origin," in 基本, [準](準.md), "standard, conform," in 基準 and [盤](盤.md), "board; disc," in 基盤. 礎 in 基礎 does not have a kanji card yet.
- [期](期.md), "period; term," shares the top part 其 and the on reading キ with 基 but is a different kanji. [型](型.md), "mold; type," also has 土 at the bottom, and [勘](勘.md), "intuition; hunch," has a similar top.
- [根](根.md), "root; foundation," [底](底.md), "bottom; base," and [台](台.md), "stand, machine counter," overlap the "foundation" and "counter" senses of 基. [本](本.md), "book, origin," and [元](元.md), "origin, former," overlap the もと sense and are the kanji the Joyo table contrasts with 基, and [素](素.md), "unadorned; element," is another spelling of もと. All of them are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "foundation" or "origin" as exclusive to any one character.
