# 満 — full; satisfy

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

満 covers fullness and having enough. 満員 (まんいん) concerns a place being full of people;
[満足](../words/満足.md) まんぞく concerns satisfaction.
These are related ideas, but a full train is not necessarily a satisfying ride.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the enclosed lower-right space packed full, beside the
water strokes. This visual memory aid is not an account of the character's history.

## Notes

バスは満員です。 (バスはまんいんです。) — The bus is full.
This describes occupancy, not the passengers' feelings.
The water radical also appears in [海](海.md), [港](港.md), and [深](深.md);
their right-hand structures, and their meanings, differ.
