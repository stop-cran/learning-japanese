# 伝 — transmit; convey

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

伝 concerns something being passed on: a message, information, or a feeling.
[伝える](../words/伝える.md) つたえる puts the person conveying it in view;
伝わる つたわる describes it spreading or coming across.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the two short bars on the right as lines of a message beside its messenger.
This is an invented visual reminder, not an account of how the character developed.

## Notes

The left-side 亻 is the visible form of 人.
[会](会.md) places a similar lower shape under a roof-like top rather than beside 亻.
[休](休.md) and [位](位.md) share only the left component.
Do not confuse who acts: 人が伝える (ひとがつたえる) means a person conveys it;
情報が伝わる (じょうほうがつたわる) means information spreads.
