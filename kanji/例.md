# 例 — example; precedent

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

例 brings a particular instance into view so that a larger point can be understood.
The word [例](../words/例.md) れい means an example; 例文 れいぶん is an example sentence.
The character can also concern precedent or established usage, rather than a newly invented illustration.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the person at the left pointing to one item in the row suggested by [列](列.md) on the right.
This is an explicitly modern learning image, not a proposed derivation.

Radical 人 is written as 亻 here.
