# 潜 — dive; submerge; lurk

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

潜 is going under the surface and staying out of sight: first under water, then out of view in general. The linked [潜る](../words/潜る.md) もぐる, read もぐる here, has three JMdict senses: "to dive (into or under water)," "to get under; to get into; to get in; to creep into; to crawl under; to bury oneself; to burrow into; to dig oneself into; to snuggle under," and "to hide oneself (esp. from the government); to conceal oneself; to go underground."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for セン. One example of each:

- diving: 潜水 せんすい, "diving; submerging; going underwater";
- lying hidden, not showing: 潜在 せんざい, "potentiality; dormancy; latency";
- sinking one's mind into something: 沈潜 ちんせん, "sinking into deep thought; sinking to the depths; contemplation";
- short for 潜水艦 せんすいかん, "submarine," as in 原潜 げんせん, JMdict "nuclear submarine," tagged "abbreviation."

More examples are in the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part is a large, busy shape beside it. Imagine a diver under the water of 氵, with the sun far above. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 潜る, 潜 writes もぐ and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same kanji also writes a separate entry くぐる, which JMdict tags "word usually written using kana alone" in all its senses: "to go under; to pass under; to go through; to pass through," "to dive (into or under the water)," "to evade; to get around; to slip past," and "to survive; to surmount." Its second sense cross-refers to もぐる. The もぐる entry also records むぐる as "out-dated or obsolete kana usage." The word article reads 潜る as もぐる, so only もぐ.る is selected. 地下に潜る ちかにもぐる is JMdict "to go underground; to go into hiding," and 潜り込む もぐりこむ is "to slip into; to crawl into (under); to conceal oneself (under)," with もぐり込む and 潜りこむ as "search-only kanji form." In 潜む and 潜める, 潜 writes ひそ with む or める as okurigana; 潜める also means "to lower volume (of a sound or one's voice) so as not to be heard." In 潜水, 潜在 and 潜伏 せんぷく, "concealment; hiding; ambush," 潜 supplies セン. 潜在的 せんざいてき is "latent; potential."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セン with 潜水, 潜在的 and 沈潜, ひそむ with 潜む, and もぐる with 潜る and 潜り込む. It shows the older form 潛 in round brackets, which this card does not teach. Practice selects もぐ.る, the reading of the linked word article. The other readings are shown with dictionary examples and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. Kanjipedia also lists くぐ.る, which KANJIDIC2 has too.

## Notes

- 潜る is 潜 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji. In the examples above, [水](水.md), "water," supplies スイ in 潜水, [在](在.md), "exist; be located," supplies ザイ in 潜在, 伏 in 潜伏 has no card linked here, [沈](沈.md), "sink; subside," supplies チン in 沈潜, [航](航.md), "navigate; sail; fly," supplies コウ in 潜航 せんこう, "submarine voyage; underwater navigation," and [込](込.md), "crowded; included," supplies こ in 潜り込む.
- [漁](漁.md), "fishing; fishery," [溶](溶.md), "melt; dissolve," and [渡](渡.md), "cross over," also have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but have different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [沈](沈.md) overlaps going under the water, [埋](埋.md), "bury; embed," overlaps getting under something, and [密](密.md), "secret; dense; close," overlaps hiding out of sight. [蔵](蔵.md), "storehouse; keep in storage," also reads かく.れる, so it is never offered as a wrong answer. 先, 千, 戦, 川 and 山 also read セン and are never offered either. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
