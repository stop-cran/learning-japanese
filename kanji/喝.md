# 喝 — shout; rebuke

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

喝 appears in loud-voice compounds such as [一喝](../words/一喝.md), a forceful rebuke, and 喝采, cheering. 恐喝 is a separate word meaning blackmail or extortion, not a kind of shout. KANJIDIC2 also lists “hoarse” as a source gloss; that is not the sense taught by these examples.
