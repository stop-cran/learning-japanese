# 外 — outside

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 夕 (evening, no. 36) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

外 combines 夕 (evening) and 卜 (divination, a crack in a bone or shell). A traditional explanation is that divination was normally done in the
morning, so one done in the evening was something "outside" the norm. This explanation is debated, so treat it as a mnemonic. The meanings are "outside", "other" and "foreign".
It is the opposite of [内](内.md).

## Notes

- **外 vs 内:** 外 (そと, outside) and [内](内.md) (うち, inside) are opposites; compare also 中 (inside, middle).
- Look-alikes: 外 has 夕 on the left and 卜 on the right; do not confuse 夕 with 多 (many) or 名 (name), which both have 夕.
- 外 and 内 can be paired in compounds: 内外 ないがい (inside and outside, about).
