# 町 — town, neighborhood

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

町 can identify a town, neighborhood, or town block. The everyday word [町](../words/町.md) (まち) can describe the place where people live without specifying its legal status.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** put [田](田.md), a field, beside the 丁-shaped part. Imagine a T-shaped street running beside fields at the edge of a town. This is a memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チョウ | on | 町長 ちょうちょう — town mayor; 町内 ちょうない — neighborhood |
| まち | kun | [町](../words/町.md) まち — town |

## Common words

- [町](../words/町.md) まち — town, neighborhood
- 町長 ちょうちょう — town mayor
- 町内 ちょうない — neighborhood, area within a town

## Notes

Both まち and ちょう occur in place names; check the particular name rather than applying one reading everywhere.
Compare [男](男.md): its 田 sits above 力, whereas 町 places 田 beside 丁.
Do not automatically substitute 街 (also まち); that spelling particularly suits urban streets or downtown contexts.
