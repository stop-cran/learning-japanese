# 査 — investigate; inspect

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

査 concerns examining or investigating something. 検査 けんさ can be an inspection or test, while 調査 ちょうさ is an investigation or survey. Learn the complete words rather than using サ as an independent verb.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 above the 且-like lower form and imagine examining a wooden object resting on a stand. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

- The 検査 article explains testing or checking against a standard; 調査 emphasizes gathering information. Their uses can overlap, so this is not an exclusive division of every investigation.
- Distinguish the lower 且-like shape from 日 in [昔](昔.md) or [昼](昼.md). This is a written-form comparison, not evidence of a shared pronunciation.
