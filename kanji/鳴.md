# 鳴 — animal call; ring

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 鳥 (no. 196) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鳴 covers animal calls and other sounding or ringing.
[鳴く](../words/鳴く.md) なく describes an animal making its call;
鳴る (なる) describes something such as a bell sounding.
The verb ending determines which construction you have.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** [口](口.md) beside [鳥](鳥.md) suggests a bird making a call.
This is an explicitly modern memory aid, not a verified explanation of the character's historical formation.

- 鳴る なる — sound; ring
- 鳴らす ならす — make something ring

## Notes

泣く (なく) means to cry or shed tears; 鳴く does not assert that an animal is weeping.
鳥 by itself names the animal and lacks the left 口.
[問](問.md) encloses a 口 instead; [駅](駅.md) has 馬 rather than 鳥 as its large component.
