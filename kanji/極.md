# 極 — extreme; pole

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

極 concerns an extreme, limit, or pole. 極限 きょくげん is a limit or extreme limit. The adverb 極 ごく means very or extremely and is usually written in kana. The きわめる family concerns reaching an extreme; the separate きめる family concerns deciding or settling.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside the tightly enclosed right-hand strokes and imagine a marker at the extreme end of a wooden measuring rod. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- The linked 極 article teaches the adverb ごく, usually written ごく. Do not follow that link as though it taught a standalone noun きょく.
- 極める きわめる is transitive and ichidan; 極まる きわまる is intransitive and godan. 極まり きわまり and 極み きわみ are separately listed nouns, not alternative conjugations to substitute freely.
- The same written 極める can also be きめる, decide or settle; that JMdict entry also lists 決める. Its reading row is deliberately not linked to the article teaching きわめる.
- Kanjipedia explicitly lists 極まる きまる with an outside-Joyo reading marker. JMdict's inspected deciding/settling family instead lists 決まる, 決る and 極る. The external entry establishes the written ま boundary; it is recognition evidence, not a recommendation to prefer 極まる for everyday きまる.
- 月極め つきぎめ supplies a real bound -ぎめ example, a monthly contract. JMdict also lists 月極 with つきぎめ; its げっきょく reading is flagged irregular and restricted to 月極, not offered as an equal unqualified alternative.
