# 煮 — boil; simmer; stew

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 火 (as 灬, no. 86), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

煮 is cooking food in a liquid: boiling it, simmering it or stewing it. [煮る](../words/煮る.md) にる is the verb for a cook doing this, and [煮える](../words/煮える.md) にえる is the intransitive partner for food that is being boiled or cooked through. JMdict glosses 煮る "to boil; to simmer; to stew; to seethe" and 煮える "to be boiled; to be cooked."

The Daijisen character entry reads 煮 as シャ, which it marks as a customary reading, with 煮沸 しゃふつ, JMdict's "boiling up." It also gives the kun reading に, as in 煮物 にもの, "nimono; food cooked by boiling or stewing," 雑煮 ぞうに, "soup containing rice cakes and vegetables (New Year's dish)," and 佃煮 つくだに, "preserved food boiled in sweetened soy sauce."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [者](者.md), "person in a role," above 灬, the four dots at the bottom that are the fire shape of [火](火.md), "fire." Imagine a person watching a pot over a fire until the food is soft. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 者 or 灬 contributes meaning or sound, and 者 sharing シャ with this card is not offered as an explanation. Historical formation is not established here.

In 煮る, 煮 writes に and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 煮える, 煮 writes the same に and える is okurigana. In 煮物, 煮 writes に and [物](物.md) writes もの. In 雑煮, 雑 writes ぞう and 煮 writes に; in 佃煮, 佃 writes つくだ and 煮 writes に, so 煮 is the last element of both. The leading dash in -に marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced. In 煮沸, 煮 supplies シャ and [沸](沸.md) supplies フツ. In the expression 業を煮やす ごうをにやす, JMdict's "to lose one's temper; to lose patience; to have enough; to be exasperated; to become irritated; to get fed up," 煮やす is the にやす verb of the に.やす row. 煮凝り にこごり, "jellied fish broth; jellied meat broth," and 煮麺 にゅうめん, "soup of sōmen noodles and various ingredients in a soy sauce broth," are whole-word readings that Daijisen lists among hard-to-read compounds; they are not split among their kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シャ with 煮沸, にる with 煮る and 雑煮, にえる with 煮える and 生煮え なまにえ, "half-cooked," and にやす with 業を煮やす. It has no row for -に. Practice selects に.る, the reading of the linked word articles. The other readings are shown with dictionary examples and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- 煮る and 煮える are 煮 with okurigana only, so neither has another kanji.
- [火](火.md), "fire," [水](水.md), "water," and [肉](肉.md), "meat; flesh," suit a cooking scene, which makes them look plausible; the fire dots (灬) are even part of this character. They are different kanji with unrelated readings and meanings and supply this card's wrong answers.
- [沸](沸.md), "boil; seethe," and [炊](炊.md), "cook rice; boil," share the sense "boil" and are kept out of this card's quiz options. [熟](熟.md), "ripen; mature; skilled," has a Daijisen sense "cook well," as in 半熟 はんじゅく, "half-cooked; half-done; soft-boiled," so it is excluded too; this pair is a shared-sense pair between two cards of the same batch. Do not treat "boil" or "cook" as exclusive to any one character.
- [似](似.md), "resemble," is also read に.る, as in 似る, a different verb that is not 煮る, and [荷](荷.md), "load; baggage; burden," is read に. [者](者.md) shares シャ. None of them is used as a wrong answer.
