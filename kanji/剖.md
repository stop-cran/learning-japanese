# 剖 — dissect; split open

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

剖 concerns cutting something open or separating it to examine it. In [解剖](../words/解剖.md) かいぼう, it contributes the idea of dissecting; the whole word also has a figurative "analysis" sense. Do not use 剖 as a standalone replacement for the general verb 分ける わける, "divide; separate."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the stacked 立-over-口 shape on the left as something opened by the blade-shaped 刂 on the right. This invented scene helps distinguish the modern shape; it is not a historical or phonetic explanation.

## Notes

- The indexing radical is [刀](刀.md); its right-hand form here is 刂.
- [部](部.md), "section; department," has the same stacked left shape but right-hand 阝 instead of 刂. [倍](倍.md), "multiple; double," has left-hand 亻 beside the stacked shape. [副](副.md), "vice-; secondary," shares the right-hand 刂 but has a different left block.
- [分](分.md) and [解](解.md) also have separating or dividing associations. Those related meanings do not make all their words interchangeable.
- 解剖 is not limited to investigating a cause of death: it can concern learning a body's structure, and it can figuratively analyze a case.
