# 綱 — thick rope; hawser; main outline

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

綱 is a heavy line: a thick rope for pulling, tying or holding something up. [綱](../words/綱.md) つな is the word taught here. JMdict glosses it "rope; cord; line" and "grand champion's braided belt," a sumo sense. A hawser is a thick rope for mooring or towing a ship. The word article teaches "rope; cord."

The Daijisen character entry gives 3 senses for コウ. One example of each:

- Sense 1, "thick rope": 綱維. JMdict has no entry for it.
- Sense 2, the main outline of a matter: 綱領 こうりょう, "general plan; main points; summary; platform (e.g. for a campaign); mission statement."
- Sense 3, a large division in biology: 哺乳綱 ほにゅうこう, "Mammalia."

Daijisen also lists the kun reading つな, with the voiced form づな in compounds. Its headword つな【綱】 gives 1, a long cord made by twisting plant fibre or wire; 2, something to cling to for help, as in 命の綱; and 3, in sumo, a yokozuna. A usage note says that by thickness 綱, 縄 and 紐 usually go from thickest to thinnest; that 綱 is twisted fibre or wire, mainly used to support or pull something; and that 縄 is mainly twisted rice straw, mainly used for tying.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left. Imagine many threads twisted together into a rope thick enough to moor a ship. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 contributes meaning or that the right part contributes sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| コウ | on | 綱領 こうりょう — "general plan; main points; summary; platform (e.g. for a campaign); mission statement"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| つな | kun | [綱](../words/綱.md) つな — rope; cord |

綱 つな is one word with no okurigana. JMdict has a separate entry for the same spelling read こう, 綱 こう, "class [biology]," so the reading こう is not a substitute in the sentences of the word article. 綱引き つなひき, "tug of war," keeps つな. In 横綱 よこづな, "yokozuna (highest rank in sumo); grand champion," [横](横.md), "horizontal," supplies よこ and 綱 is read づな, the voiced form of つな that appears after another element; in 手綱 たづな, "reins; bridle," [手](手.md), "hand," supplies た and 綱 is read づな; and in 命綱 いのちづな, "lifeline; safety rope; safety tether," [命](命.md), "life; command," supplies いのち. Other コウ words: 大綱 たいこう, "fundamental principles; main lines" and "outline; summary; general features"; 要綱 ようこう, "main principle; gist; general plan; guidelines; outline"; 綱紀 こうき, "law and order; (official) discipline"; and 政綱 せいこう, "political principle, policy or platform." JMdict also has 大綱 おおづな, "thick rope" and "fundamental principles; outline; general features."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows コウ, with 綱紀, 綱領 and 大綱, and つな, with 綱 and 横綱. Practice selects つな, the reading of the linked word article. コウ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word; being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 綱 is a one-character word, so it has no other kanji. In the other words named above, 横, 手 and 命 are the other kanji.
- [縄](縄.md), "rope; cord," [索](索.md), "rope," and [緒](緒.md), "cord; beginning," are direct overlaps with "thick rope." 綱 and 縄 both name rope: by the Daijisen usage note 綱 is the thicker line of twisted fibre or wire, mainly for supporting or pulling, and 縄 is the ordinary rope, mainly of twisted straw, mainly for tying. [概](概.md), "outline; roughly; bearing," [旨](旨.md), "purport; gist; delicious," and [要](要.md), "necessity; essentials," overlap "main outline"; [線](線.md), "line; track," overlaps JMdict's "line"; and [級](級.md), "grade; school class," [類](類.md), "kind; category," [科](科.md), "department; course," and [般](般.md), "general; kind; category," overlap the biological "class" sense. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [紙](紙.md), "paper," [海](海.md), "sea, ocean," and [夏](夏.md), "summer," look plausible because 紙 has the same thread key 糸 at the left, and 海 and 夏 are common starter cards with kun readings like つな. They are read シ and かみ; カイ and うみ; and カ, ガ, ゲ and なつ. None shares a reading or a meaning with 綱.
- Many other kanji read コウ, such as [稿](稿.md), "manuscript; draft," and [校](校.md), "school," so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
