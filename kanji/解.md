# 解 — unravel; solve

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 角 (no. 148) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

解 spans undoing a tangle and working something out. [理解](../words/理解.md) りかい concerns comprehension;
解く とく can mean untying something or solving a problem. This gives a useful learning connection
between releasing a knot and resolving a difficulty, without making a historical claim.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** locate 角 on the left, with 刀 above 牛 on the right.
Imagine taking a complicated arrangement apart. This is a modern memory scene, not verified etymology.

## Notes

問題を解く (もんだいをとく) means "solve a problem."
Understanding a problem and solving it are related but not identical tasks.
[物](物.md) and [特](特.md) put their cattle component on the left, unlike the lower-right 牛 here.
[料](料.md) is only a looser dense-stroke contrast; it has neither the same left part nor the same layout.
