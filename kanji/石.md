# 石 — stone

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[石](../words/石.md) いし names a stone or rock.
The character remains useful in compounds for precious stones and petroleum, though those words need their own translations.
Here 石 is both the complete character and its indexing radical.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the lower box as a stone under a sloping ledge.
That image uses the present shape; it is not offered as the character's original drawing.

## Notes

道に小さな石があります。 (みちにちいさないしがあります。) — There is a small stone on the road.

[右](右.md) is especially close in shape: watch the upper strokes and their crossing.
[口](口.md) lacks the top entirely; [古](古.md) has 十 above the box rather than 石's slanting stroke.
