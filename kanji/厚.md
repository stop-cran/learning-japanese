# 厚 — thick; kind

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 厂 (no. 27) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

厚 expresses physical thickness in 厚い あつい. It also occurs in kindness or cordial treatment, as in 厚意 こうい, kindness shown by someone else. A thick book and a kind favor are different uses, not a literal claim that kindness has a measurable thickness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a thick stack beneath the 厂 overhang, with a 日-like box above 子. This is a way to remember the present outline.

## Notes

- Distinguish 厚い あつい, thick, from 暑い あつい, hot weather, and 熱い あつい, hot to the touch or intensely felt. The sound alone does not choose the spelling.
- 厚意 is not a claim about the physical thickness of a person or object.
- The additional source reading あか is retained for lookup, not taught here as an ordinary way to say thick or red.
