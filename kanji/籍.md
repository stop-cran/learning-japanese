# 籍 — register; membership

**Strokes:** 20 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

籍 concerns registration or membership and also occurs in words for books. Compare 戸籍, a family register, with 書籍, a book or publication: the complete compound determines the use.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the bamboo-shaped top above a 耒-like left part and 昔 on the right. Imagine keeping an organized register below a shelf of records. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not verified historical analysis of those components.

The canonical key 竹, no. 118, is the compressed ⺮ at the top.

## Notes

- 書籍 is a noun naming a book or publication, not a verb meaning register. Its dictionary family also records the outdated reading しょじゃく; that does not add ジャク to this card's pinned character readings.
- In 戸籍, [戸](戸.md) contributes the household connection. 国籍 uses [国](国.md) in the nationality/citizenship sense; do not equate a family register with citizenship.
- The shared bamboo-shaped top links 籍 visually with [筆](筆.md) and [筒](筒.md), not semantically with every bamboo object.
