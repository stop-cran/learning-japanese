# 循 — follow; cycle

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

循 is associated with following a course or sequence. In [循環](../words/循環.md) じゅんかん, the whole word means circulation or a repeating cycle.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice 彳 on the left and the layered strokes on the right; imagine following the layers around a circuit. This is a memory aid, not a claim about historical formation.

## Notes

- Separate character sources record シュン as a non-Joyo on-reading and したがう as a kun reading. They also list name readings independently of KANJIDIC2, which records no nanori here. These source-specific classifications do not change the pinned metadata or the selected practice reading.
- [順](順.md) includes sequence and obedience; [追](追.md) includes following, and [守](守.md) includes following a rule. [後](後.md) can express a position behind or a time after. These overlap particular following or sequential associations without making the words interchangeable.
- [回](回.md) includes revolving and [周](周.md) includes a circuit; these overlap the going-around sense of 循 without implying that every movement forms a cycle.
- [休](休.md) and [人](人.md) have a person-shaped left side; [本](本.md) is a looser contrast. They provide distinct on-reading choices in the starter pool rather than the closest available shapes.
