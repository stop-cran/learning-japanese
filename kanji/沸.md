# 沸 — boil; seethe

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

沸 concerns boiling or becoming hot. 沸く わく is intransitive and 沸かす わかす is transitive. The words can also concern excitement, so not every use is a statement about a liquid's boiling point.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside the 弗-shaped right side and imagine bubbles rising between the upright strokes. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not historical etymology.

The canonical radical 水, no. 85, has the left-side form 氵.

## Notes

- Compare お湯が沸く おゆがわく, water boils, with お湯を沸かす おゆをわかす, boil water. Both verbs are godan: 沸いて わいて and 沸かして わかして.
- 沸騰 has the complete reading ふっとう, with small っ; フツ is the character reading, not a direction to say ふつとう.
- お風呂が沸く おふろがわく describes bathwater being heated and ready. It need not mean a bath has reached boiling temperature.
- The linked articles also distinguish 沸く from 湧く わく, well up. Identical kana does not make the spellings interchangeable in every sense.
