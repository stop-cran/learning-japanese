# 改 — revise; reform

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (no. 66) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

改 concerns changing, revising or reforming something. 改善 かいぜん names improvement. [改める](../words/改める.md) あらためる is transitive, while 改まる あらたまる can describe something being renewed or changed and also has a formal or ceremonious use.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 己 beside the right-hand 攵 form and imagine checking and revising one's own work. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 攴; its visible right-hand form here is 攵.

## Notes

- 改める is ichidan: 改めます あらためます. 改まる is godan: 改まります あらたまります. Their final kana do not conjugate in the same way.
- 改める also has a check or inspect sense. Context decides whether changing something, inspecting it, or acting formally is intended.
- 改善 explicitly concerns improvement; the broader idea of change does not guarantee a better outcome in every word containing 改.
