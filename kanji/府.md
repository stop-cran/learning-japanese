# 府 — urban prefecture; administration

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

府 concerns an administrative authority or an urban prefecture. 政府 せいふ means government or administration; 府庁 ふちょう is the prefectural office of Osaka or Kyoto. The character's associations do not make every 府 a national government.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 广 surrounding 付 and imagine administrative work taking place under a roof. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical or phonetic claim.
