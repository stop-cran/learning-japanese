# 範 — model; standard

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (in its top form, no. 118) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

範 can mean a model or standard. In [範囲](../words/範囲.md) はんい it occurs in a word for a range or scope; that word's meaning is not simply "model."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a bamboo-shaped top over a 車-shaped section and the remaining strokes, like a model set under a cover. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not an explanation of its origin.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ハン | on | [範囲](../words/範囲.md) はんい - range; scope |

KANJIDIC2 records no kun reading. It lists のり as a name reading; another dictionary also lists のり as a kun reading and recognizes 範 のり among spellings of a word for a rule or model. This dictionary-only spelling is not an alternative reading of 範囲.

## Notes

- [型](型.md) includes mold, pattern and model; [例](例.md) means example. [規](規.md), [則](則.md), [法](法.md), and [典](典.md) include standards or rules, and [式](式.md) includes an established form. These can overlap aspects of 範, without being interchangeable in every compound. [幅](幅.md) includes breadth or range, while 範囲 names an extent or scope.
- [準](準.md) includes a standard or conforming to a model; [律](律.md), [憲](憲.md), [制](制.md), and [令](令.md) include law, rules or regulation. A model, a law, a constitution and a command are not the same kind of thing, even when their senses overlap.
- [域](域.md) concerns a region or range, [程](程.md) a degree or extent, and [般](般.md) includes scope or category. [類](類.md) groups things by kind; 範疇 はんちゅう also names a category. Each comparison is with a bounded sense, not a claim that the words are freely interchangeable.
- [師](師.md) includes a model or exemplar in the character dictionary; [象](象.md) can mean modeling on something, and [儀](儀.md) appears in 地球儀, a globe or model of Earth. A teacher, an act of representation, and a globe are not automatically a standard to follow.
- [車](車.md) names a vehicle; its shape appears within 範. [書](書.md) concerns writing or documents; [達](達.md) includes reaching or a plural suffix. Their shapes and meanings differ from 範.
- Daijisen also lists すすむ as a name reading. Neither that nor the dictionary-only のり is added to the KANJIDIC2 practice metadata.
