# 範 — model; standard

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (in its top form, no. 118) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

範 can mean a model or standard. Digital Daijisen also describes a delimited frame, illustrated by 範囲 and 範疇. In [範囲](../words/範囲.md) はんい it occurs in a word for a range or scope; that word's meaning is not simply "model."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a bamboo-shaped top over a 車-shaped section and the remaining strokes, like a model set under a cover. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not an explanation of its origin.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ハン | on | [範囲](../words/範囲.md) はんい - range; scope |

KANJIDIC2 records no kun reading. It lists のり as a name reading; another dictionary also lists のり as a kun reading and recognizes 範 のり among spellings of a word for a rule or model. This dictionary-only spelling is not an alternative reading of 範囲.

- 規範 きはん - standard; norm

## Notes

- [型](型.md) includes mold, pattern and model; [例](例.md) means example. [規](規.md), [則](則.md), [法](法.md), and [典](典.md) include standards or rules, and [式](式.md) includes an established form. These can overlap aspects of 範, without being interchangeable in every compound. [幅](幅.md) includes breadth or range, while 範囲 names an extent or scope.
- [準](準.md) includes a standard or conforming to a model; [律](律.md), [憲](憲.md), [制](制.md), and [令](令.md) include law, rules or regulation. A model, a law, a constitution and a command are not the same kind of thing, even when their senses overlap.
- [域](域.md) concerns a region or range, [程](程.md) a degree or extent, and [般](般.md) includes scope or category. [類](類.md) groups things by kind; 範疇 はんちゅう also names a category. Each comparison is with a bounded sense, not a claim that the words are freely interchangeable.
- KANJIDIC2 lists model and exemplar for [師](師.md); [象](象.md) can mean modeling on something. [儀](儀.md) is conservatively guarded: 地球儀 is a globe or model of Earth, but the whole word alone does not establish that 儀 itself means model. A teacher, an act of representation, and a globe are not automatically a standard to follow.
- [標](標.md) appears in 標準, a standard; [紀](紀.md) also has a rules or discipline sense. [格](格.md) かく can mean rules or regulations. These are bounded standard/rule comparisons, not claims that a mark, a chronicle or a rank is itself always a standard.
- [鏡](鏡.md) has a model-to-learn-from sense, not a general "standard" meaning for every mirror. JMdict also lists [様](様.md) as a spelling of ためし, example or precedent; this does not establish its frequency or change its descriptive and honorific uses.
- [限](限.md) is a limit, [境](境.md) a boundary or region, and [界](界.md) a boundary or sphere; each is conservatively guarded against 範's delimited-frame sense. A boundary and the area it bounds are not automatically interchangeable.
- [形](形.md) can mean form or style, while 範 can describe a standard pattern. [倣](倣.md) means to imitate or follow an example; a model and following it are distinct, but the English word "model" can also describe an action.
- [本](本.md) also has a model-to-follow sense, and [度](度.md) has a standard or exemplar sense, separate from their common book and degree senses. [像](像.md) is an image or representation; English "model" can also name a representation, although a representation is not necessarily a model to follow.
- [計](計.md) and [測](測.md) concern measuring, and [量](量.md) includes measurable quantity. The dictionary-only のり includes a measurement across or standard length; English "measure" can name a result as well as an action. This comparison does not make 範 an ordinary measuring verb or a measuring device.
- Digital Daijisen also describes a widespread sense of 範; [広](広.md) concerns width or spreading, and [普](普.md) includes widespread applicability. This comparison does not make 範 a general word for universal or a synonym for every kind of standard. [道](道.md) includes a way or moral teaching, compared conservatively with the normative のり sense. JMdict includes an engineering slope sense for のり alongside the spelling 範; [坂](坂.md) is a slope, but this does not establish that 範 is a natural everyday spelling for every slope.
- [車](車.md) names a vehicle; its shape appears within 範. [書](書.md) concerns writing or documents; [達](達.md) includes reaching or a plural suffix. Their shapes and meanings differ from 範.
- Daijisen also lists すすむ as a name reading. Neither that nor the dictionary-only のり is added to the KANJIDIC2 practice metadata.
