# 墜 — plummet; crash

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

墜 conveys a fall from a height or a crash. 墜落 (ついらく), for example, can describe an aircraft going down.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The base is 土. Picture the ground below something that is falling.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ツイ | on | 墜落 ついらく — fall from a height; aircraft crash. |
| お.ちる | kun | [墜ちる](../words/落ちる.md) おちる — fall down. This kun-reading is outside the common-use table; 落ちる is the ordinary spelling. |
| お.つ | kun | Daijisen gives おつ as the classical form corresponding to modern おちる. Recognize it here rather than using it as the modern dictionary form. |

## Common words

- 墜落 ついらく — a fall from a height; an aircraft crash
- [墜ちる](../words/落ちる.md) おちる — fall down

## Notes

Compare [落](落.md), the broader character for falling. 墜ちる is another spelling of the same verb: 墜 writes お and the kana write ちる. Use 落ちる for everyday writing.

Above the 土 you can see [隊](隊.md) (squad); [随](随.md) (follow) also starts with the left-hand 阝 shape. [壁](壁.md) (wall) has 土 below a different top. For meanings, compare [堕](堕.md) (lapse or degenerate) and [陥](陥.md) (fall into): these describe different kinds of falling. An aircraft is one example of something that can fall from a height, not a limit on the use of 墜.
