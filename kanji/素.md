# 素 — unadorned; element

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

素 is the plain, unprocessed state of something, and by extension its basic element. [素質](../words/素質.md) そしつ is aptitude or innate qualities, and [要素](../words/要素.md) ようそ is an element or factor. The other common words are listed below.

For ソ the Daijisen character entry lists senses such as left in its natural state with nothing added, as in 素材 そざい, "ingredient; (raw) material; resource"; the basic thing from which something is formed, as in 要素 and 元素 げんそ, "element; chemical element"; and use in the names of chemical elements, as in 酸素 and 水素. For ス it lists left bare with nothing added, as in 素顔 すがお, "face with no make-up; unpainted face," and 素手 すで, "bare hand; empty hand."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a short stack of horizontal strokes crossed by a vertical stroke at the top, above [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the bottom. Imagine bare, undyed thread with no pattern added, and remember 素 as plain or basic. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that the top shape contributes meaning or sound, and 糸 is simply the key shape at the bottom. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソ | on | [要素](../words/要素.md) ようそ — element; component; factor; [酸素](../words/酸素.md) さんそ — oxygen; [水素](../words/水素.md) すいそ — hydrogen; [素質](../words/素質.md) そしつ — aptitude; innate qualities |
| ス | on | [素直](../words/素直.md) すなお — obedient; frank; 素顔 すがお - face with no make-up; 素手 すで - bare hand |
| もと | kun | [素](../words/素.md) もと — base; ingredient mix |

In 要素, 要 supplies ヨウ. In 酸素, 酸 supplies サン. In 水素, 水 supplies スイ. In 素質, 質 supplies シツ and 素 comes first. In 素直, 素 is read す and 直 is read なお, a mixed reading that combines an on reading with a native one.

Two everyday words also begin with 素 read す: 素敵 すてき, "lovely; wonderful; charming," and 素晴らしい すばらしい, "wonderful; splendid; magnificent." JMdict marks both spellings as ateji, kanji chosen for their sounds rather than their meanings, so they help you recognise 素 but do not show its meaning.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ソ and ス for 素 and has no kun reading for it; its remarks column lists 素人 しろうと as a special reading. 素人 is a whole-word reading, which JMdict marks as gikun or jukujikun, a reading assigned to the word as a unit, so しろうと is learned as one word and is not split into 素 and 人.

Practice selects ソ, ス and もと. The もと reading is retained from KANJIDIC2 and the Daijisen character entry: JMdict lists 素 as one spelling of the native noun もと, which is more often written 元 or 本, and the article above teaches its "base; ingredient mix" sense, as in スープの素. Being outside the table is not proof that a reading is invalid.

The Daijisen character entry also lists もとより; KANJIDIC2 does not, so it is not part of the metadata here, and JMdict gives 素より only as a rarely used kanji form of 元より もとより, "from the beginning" and "of course," which is usually written in kana. Do not read this kanji as そ in 素 もと: the separate JMdict noun 素 そ means "white silk," "prime" or "principle."

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [要](要.md), "necessity; essentials," in 要素, [水](水.md), "water," in 水素, [質](質.md), "quality; substance," in 素質, [直](直.md), "direct; repair," in 素直, and [人](人.md), "person," in 素人. 酸 in 酸素 does not have a kanji card yet.
- [組](組.md), "group; assemble," also reads ソ and has 糸 at the left, but it is a different kanji. [毒](毒.md), "poison; venom," and [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible," have a similar stack of strokes at the top but different bottoms.
- [原](原.md), "original; plain," [単](単.md), "single; simple," and [純](純.md), "pure; genuine," each overlap the "unadorned" sense of 素. [具](具.md), "tools; ingredients," [材](材.md), "timber; material," and [料](料.md), "materials, fee," overlap the "ingredient; material" sense seen in 素 もと and 素材. [元](元.md), "origin, former," and [本](本.md), "book, origin," overlap the もと sense, and [要](要.md) overlaps "element." All of them are kept out of this card's quiz options. [基](基.md), "foundation; basis," also shares the もと sense; that pair is excluded on the 基 card's side. Do not treat "plain" or "origin" as exclusive to any one character.
