# 備 — prepare; equip

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

備 concerns having what is needed, or getting ready before it is needed.
[備える](../words/備える.md) そなえる can mean equipping something or preparing for an event.
準備 じゅんび names preparation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the person-shaped 亻 checking supplies in the divided box at the
lower right. The "supplies" are a memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

備える and 備わる frame the situation differently: equipping something versus something being equipped.
Do not substitute one solely because both begin そな.
[借](借.md), [側](側.md), and [働](働.md) have the same left radical but different arrangements on the right.
