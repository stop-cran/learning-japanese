# 校 — school

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** 交 (コウ) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

校 is a phonetic compound: [木](木.md) (tree, wood) gives the category and 交 (cross, mix) gives the sound コウ. The oldest meaning is usually given
as something wooden, such as a wooden frame or fence; the senses "to compare and check" (校正 こうせい, proofreading) and later "school" developed from it, and the details are debated.
In modern Japanese its main meaning is "school", mostly in compounds.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| コウ | on | [学校](../words/学校.md) がっこう, 高校 こうこう, 校長 こうちょう, 校庭 こうてい, 母校 ぼこう |
| キョウ | on | rare: 校合 きょうごう (collating texts) |

There is no kun reading.

## Common words

- [学校](../words/学校.md) がっこう — school
- 高校 こうこう — senior high school
- 校長 こうちょう — principal
- 校門 こうもん — school gate

## Notes

校 is mostly a suffix or part of a compound: 小学校 しょうがっこう, 中学校 ちゅうがっこう, 高校. It is seldom a word by itself.
Do not confuse it with [学](学.md), "study", which is the first part of 学校.

Look-alikes: 交 (mix, cross) is the right-hand phonetic part and is not a school word by itself. Other characters with the same phonetic 交 include 効 (effect), 郊 (suburb) and 較 (compare).
