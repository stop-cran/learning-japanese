# 更 — renew; grow late

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 曰 (no. 73) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

更 connects several words worth learning separately: [変更](../words/変更.md) へんこう is
an alteration, 更に さらに means "further; even more," and 夜更かし よふかし is staying up late.
A single English gloss will not translate all three.

For shape recognition, notice the central box and the long crossing strokes below it.
The canonical indexing radical is 曰, even though the box can resemble 日 in print.
No historical derivation is asserted here.

- 更に さらに — further; usually written さらに
- 夜更かし よふかし — staying up late

## Notes

In 夜更かし (よふかし), 更 is read ふ, not the on reading コウ.
[使](使.md) has a similar crossing-stroke shape on its right but includes a person on the left.
[東](東.md) and [車](車.md) also arrange a box around a central stroke; their lower strokes differ.
