# 幹 — tree trunk; main part

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 干 (no. 51) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

幹 means a tree's trunk and, by extension, a main or central part. In 幹線 かんせん, "main line; trunk line," it identifies a principal route rather than a branch.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Use the tall left-hand stack and the right-hand [干](干.md) shape as a reminder of an upright trunk supporting the main structure. This is a visual memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

- [木](木.md) names a tree or wood more generally; 幹 singles out its trunk. 幹部 refers to an organization's leadership, not its wooden parts.
- Compare [朝](朝.md), morning, and [乾](乾.md), dry. Their similar left-hand shapes do not give them 幹's trunk meaning.
- The dictionary also records 幹 から, "trunk; stem; stalk," in a separate word family. That word does not change this card's preserved KANJIDIC2 reading list or make every written 幹 interchangeable between readings.
