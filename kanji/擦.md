# 擦 — scrape; scrub

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

擦 concerns rubbing, scraping or scrubbing a surface. [摩擦](../words/摩擦.md) まさつ is friction or rubbing, and [擦る](../words/擦る.md) こする is the everyday verb "rub; scrub; scrape." The same character writes 擦り傷 すりきず, "scratch; graze; scrape; abrasion," and 擦過傷 さっかしょう, "abrasion; scratch; graze," where the scraping leaves a mark on the skin. The word かする, "to graze," can also be written 擦る, but かす.る is not a KANJIDIC2 reading and is not part of the metadata here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the side form of [手](手.md), "hand," at the left and [察](察.md), "observe; infer," at the right. Imagine a hand rubbing a surface; the right-hand part 察 also reads サツ, which makes a handy sound cue. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim about the historical formation of 擦.

In 摩擦, 擦 supplies サツ and 摩 supplies マ. In 擦る, 擦 writes こす and る is okurigana. Practice selects サツ and こす.る; the other kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with unlinked dictionary words. Several different words are spelled with 擦 (する, こする, かする), so match the reading to the word rather than to the character alone.

## Notes

- [摩](摩.md), "rub; chafe," is the other kanji in 摩擦. Both characters concern rubbing, so their meanings overlap.
- [察](察.md), "observe; infer," is the right-hand part and also reads サツ, but 察 alone has nothing to do with rubbing. [祭](祭.md), "festival; ritual; worship," and [際](際.md), "edge; occasion," share the 祭 shape that sits inside 察 and therefore inside 擦.
- [磨](磨.md), "polish; grind," and [掃](掃.md), "sweep; brush," also describe repeated contact with a surface. They are different actions, and these overlaps are why they are kept out of this card's quiz options.
