# 宇 — eaves; space

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

KANJIDIC2 gives 宇 meanings including eaves, roof, house and heaven. In 宇宙 うちゅう, the complete word means universe or outer space. Learn this compound without treating 宇 alone as the ordinary standalone word for every kind of space.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 宀 roof stretching broadly above the lower 于-shaped strokes, then use that broad canopy to recall spatial extent. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not an account of the historical development of the space meaning.
