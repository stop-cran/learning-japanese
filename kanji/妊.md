# 妊 — pregnancy

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

妊 indicates pregnancy. In 妊婦 it identifies the woman's condition; it is not a general character for any parent or child.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look at the left side: 女 in 妊, the person element in [任](任.md). Both have 壬 on the right.

## Notes

Compare [婦](婦.md) (woman; wife). In 妊婦 it is 妊, not 婦, that tells us the woman is pregnant.

[任](任.md) begins with the person element rather than 女. [妨](妨.md) and [姉](姉.md) both begin with 女, but neither has the 壬 on the right of 妊.
