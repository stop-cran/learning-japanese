# 男 — man, male

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

男 combines 田 (rice field) and 力 (strength, often explained as a plough or an arm). The traditional reading is "someone who
works the field with strength", reflecting a historical picture of male labour.
It is a compound ideogram: both parts contribute meaning, neither contributes sound.
