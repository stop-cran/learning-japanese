# 価 — worth; price

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

価 is what something is worth: the price attached to it, or its value and merit. [価値](../words/価値.md) かち is value or worth, [価格](../words/価格.md) かかく is a price or cost, [定価](../words/定価.md) ていか is a list price, [物価](../words/物価.md) ぶっか is prices in general, [評価](../words/評価.md) ひょうか is an assessment or appraisal, and [高価](../words/高価.md) こうか is expensive or costly. For カ the Daijisen character entry lists three senses: the price of a thing, as in 価格, 高価, 定価, 物価 and 安価 あんか, "low-priced; cheap; inexpensive"; worth, as in 価値, 評価 and 真価 しんか, "true value; real worth"; and a numeric value, as in 原子価 げんしか, "valence; valency." A second Daijisen entry for か uses 価 in chemistry for valence and in mathematics for the number of values a function takes. The Daijisen heading also shows the older form 價, which this card does not teach.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the person shape 亻 at the left beside a lid-like shape 覀 at the right, the same shape seen at the top of [要](要.md), "necessity; essentials," and [西](西.md), "west." Imagine a person lifting the lid to read the price tag underneath: what is it worth? This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 覀 contributes meaning or sound, and 亻 is simply the key shape at the left. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| カ | on | [価値](../words/価値.md) かち — value; worth; [価格](../words/価格.md) かかく — price; cost; [定価](../words/定価.md) ていか — list price; regular price; [物価](../words/物価.md) ぶっか — prices in general; price level; [評価](../words/評価.md) ひょうか — assessment; appraisal; [高価](../words/高価.md) こうか — expensive; costly |
| ケ | on | source-only reading from KANJIDIC2, no example word; not selected for questions here |
| あたい | kun | 価 あたい - price; cost; value; worth, a formal or literary noun also written 値; not selected for questions here |

In 価値, 値 supplies チ. In 価格, 格 supplies カク, so 価 is か in both syllables of かかく. In 定価, 定 supplies テイ. In 物価, 物 supplies ブツ, which becomes ぶっ before か. In 評価, 評 supplies ヒョウ. In 高価, 高 supplies コウ. The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カ for 価, with 価値, 価格 and 評価 as examples, and あたい with the spelling contrast 価 ⇔ 値: the same reading is written with 値 in other words. JMdict lists the noun あたい with the spellings 値 and 価. Standing alone as a suffix, 価 is also read か and means "valence; valency." The ケ reading is listed by KANJIDIC2 only: the Joyo table and the Daijisen character entry do not list it, and a search of JMdict found no word that reads 価 as ケ, so it has no example here. Practice selects カ, the only reading with linked word articles. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [値](値.md), "value; price," in 価値, [格](格.md), "rank; character," in 価格, [定](定.md), "determine; settle," in 定価, [物](物.md), "thing, object," in 物価 and [高](高.md), "high, expensive," in 高価. 評 in 評価 does not have a kanji card yet.
- [仮](仮.md), "temporary; assumed," and [化](化.md), "transform," share the person shape at the left and the on reading カ with 価 but are different kanji. [西](西.md) shares the lid-like shape at the right.
- [値](値.md), "value; price," shares both glosses and the あたい reading with 価, and the Joyo table contrasts the two. [費](費.md), "expense; spend," overlaps the "cost" side of 価格. Both are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "value" or "price" as exclusive to any one character.
