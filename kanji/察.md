# 察 — observe; infer

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

察 concerns finding things out: looking closely in [観察](../words/観察.md) かんさつ, or judging from clues in
察する さっする. Observation and inference are connected, but observing something is not the same as guessing what it means.
Learn the complete word to know which activity is intended.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** look beneath 宀 for the 祭 shape, with 示 at the bottom.
Imagine inspecting all the details under a roof. This is a modern visual association, not etymology.

## Common words

- [観察](../words/観察.md) かんさつ — observation; 観察する means to observe
- 警察 けいさつ — police
- 察する さっする — infer; sense; judge

## Notes

In 察する, サツ combines with する as さっする, with a small っ.
警察 names the police; do not try to translate every compound as the separate verb "guess."
Unlike [寒](寒.md), 察 ends in a 示-shaped part. [寝](寝.md) and [家](家.md) have different forms beneath the same roof.
