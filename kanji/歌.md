# 歌 — song, sing

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 欠 (no. 76) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

歌 represents songs and singing. It can also refer to poetry, especially traditional Japanese verse; the everyday starting point is [歌う](../words/歌う.md), "to sing."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the two [口](口.md)-shaped boxes on the left as mouths singing together beside the 欠-shaped right side. This is a memory aid rather than an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

歌 is the noun うた; adding the okurigana う makes the verb 歌う うたう. The verb's polite form is 歌います うたいます.
Compare [何](何.md), with one 可-shaped part, and [飲](飲.md), which shares the right-hand 欠 shape. Neither means "sing."
