# 双 — pair; two together

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 又 (no. 29) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

双 connects with a pair or two things taken together. 双子 ふたご names twins or a twin, and 双方 そうほう refers to both sides or parties.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See two 又-shaped forms side by side: a visible pair. This picture describes the modern form, not its historical derivation.

## Notes

- The ふた row gives a bound stem in 双子, not a claim that 双 is a normal standalone noun pronounced ふた.
- JMdict marks 双ぶ as a rare kanji spelling, not ならぶ as a rare pronunciation. The written ぶ belongs to the word even though KANJIDIC2 lists ならぶ without an okurigana dot.
- 双つ is a search-only dictionary index form, not a recommended spelling for counting two things. The source-only readings are not an invitation to invent matching words.
- 双子 does not by itself say that twins are identical, the same sex, or children at the time of speaking.
