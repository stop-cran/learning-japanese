# 終 — end; finish

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

終 marks an ending or completion. [終わる](../words/終わる.md) (おわる) is a basic way to say that something
ends; 終える (おえる) commonly means bringing an activity to an end.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** imagine tying off the last thread, 糸, as
[winter](冬.md) arrives at the end of the year. The visible 冬 is used only as a memory cue here.

## Notes

授業が終わる (じゅぎょうがおわる) means "class ends." 終わる also has transitive uses and appears after a verb stem,
as in 読み終わる (よみおわる, finish reading); it is not restricted to the pattern "something が ends."
Compare [始](始.md), used in words for beginning.

[紙](紙.md) has the same left side, but a different right side. 冬 alone means winter, not "finish."
