# 酸 — acid; sour

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 酉 (no. 164), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

酸 concerns sourness and acids. [酸性](../words/酸性.md) さんせい is
acidity or an acidic property; [酸っぱい](../words/酸っぱい.md) すっぱい
describes a sour taste. The taste adjective is not a precise chemical test.

[酸素](../words/酸素.md) さんそ means oxygen. This established chemical
name does not mean that oxygen itself is an acid. The character dictionary
also records a painful-hardship sense, beyond the taste and chemical uses
emphasized in the title.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [酉](酉.md) on the left
and 夋 on the right. The latter has a 厶-and-儿 group above 夂.
Imagine the 酉-shaped block as a bottle with a sour-taste label beside it.
This is a memory aid, not a claim about historical derivation or sound.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| サン | on | [酸性](../words/酸性.md) さんせい - acidity; acidic; [酸素](../words/酸素.md) さんそ - oxygen |
| す.い | kun | 酸い すい - sour; a separate adjective from 酸っぱい; no word article yet |

Practice selects サン. 酸い writes す with 酸 and い in kana.
The linked adjective 酸っぱい is すっぱい, with a small っ, and belongs
to a separate dictionary entry. Its shared initial す does not by itself
provide an own-word example for selecting the raw す.い reading.

The official Joyo table places both 酸い and 酸っぱい under すい.
That standard-reading grouping does not erase the difference between the
two complete adjectives or license reading 酸っぱい as すい.

## Notes

- [酒](酒.md), alcohol/sake, has 酉 on the right; [配](配.md),
  distribute, has it on the left beside 己. Standalone [酉](酉.md)
  names the Rooster zodiac sign. Sharing this radical does not make
  those characters mean acid or sour.
- Sour, bitter and spicy tastes are distinct. However, the broader
  painful-hardship senses overlap [苦](苦.md) and [辛](辛.md), so those
  characters are excluded from meaning choices rather than treated as
  unambiguously wrong on taste labels alone.
- JMdict separately records 酸 as a rare spelling in the 酢 す,
  vinegar, family. That spelling does not make every standalone 酸 mean
  vinegar or replace サン in 酸性.
