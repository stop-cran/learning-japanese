# 家 — house, home

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

家 concerns a house, home or household. It can also refer to a specialist as the suffix カ; that use does not mean the person's house.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 宀 roof sheltering the many strokes below like people and belongings inside a home. This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

いえ and うち overlap, but うち commonly presents a home or household as one's own. Do not assume every written 家 can be read either way.

[室](室.md) is a room; [屋](屋.md) occurs in words for roofs, buildings and shops. [安](安.md) shares the roof shape but means something different. 家賃 mixes native や with on チン.

For the endings 者, 家, 師, 士, 手, 員 and 屋 compared, see [the article on occupation and role endings](../articles/occupation-and-role-endings.md).
