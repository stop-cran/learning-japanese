# 嚇 — threaten; intimidate

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

嚇 can mean to threaten or frighten. KANJIDIC2 also records dignity and majesty; this card focuses on the threatening use shown by [威嚇](../words/威嚇.md) (いかく), not every sense of every compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine a warning called out from the 口 beside the dense right-hand shape. This is a memory aid only; it makes no claim about historical formation or sound.

In 威嚇, 威 supplies イ and 嚇 supplies カク. JMdict also lists 嚇かす (おどかす; entry 1578070); that full word has a different okurigana pattern from the listed reading おど.す.

## Notes

- 嚇, 威, 恐 and 怖 can all appear in threat- or fear-related meanings; the words and their roles are not interchangeable. The overlap is excluded from meaning choices.
- Kanjipedia also lists いかる and the full-word reading おどかす (嚇かす). JMdict entry 1578070 verifies this rarely used spelling; neither reading is added to the KANJIDIC2 metadata or the selected quiz list.
