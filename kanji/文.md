# 文 — sentence, writing

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 文 (no. 67) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

文 concerns sentences and written composition. It also appears in words about culture and the literary arts.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the crossing lower strokes as a pen moving across a page beneath the top line. This remembers the modern outline; it is not a reconstruction of an ancient picture.

## Notes

Learn 文字 as もじ; it is not read ぶんじ. The readings in complete words can differ from the most familiar reading of each character.

[字](字.md) often concerns an individual written character, while 文 often concerns a sentence or writing. [父](父.md) has two separate strokes above the crossing strokes; [六](六.md) has a similar top to 文 but does not cross its two lower strokes.
