# 深 — deep

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

深 expresses depth. [深い](../words/深い.md) ふかい can describe a deep river or hole.
The related verbs 深まる (ふかまる) and 深める (ふかめる) extend this to something
becoming deeper or being deepened.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the 木-shaped lower right sits beneath a covered upper space;
imagine looking down through it into deep water. This is not a historical analysis.

## Notes

この川は深いです。 (このかわはふかいです。) — This river is deep.
浅い (あさい), "shallow," is the useful opposite.
Compare [浮](浮.md): its lower right is 子-shaped, while 深 ends in a 木-shaped part.
