# 健 — healthy; robust

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

健 concerns health and vigor. [健康](../words/健康.md) けんこう is health or a
healthy condition; [保健](../words/保健.md) ほけん concerns maintaining health.
[剛健](../words/剛健.md) ごうけん emphasizes strength and robustness, including
strength of character, rather than a medical finding that someone has no illness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [人](人.md) in its narrow 亻
form beside [建](建.md). Imagine a person building up their strength.
The right-hand 建 contains a 聿-shaped upright group and the sweeping 廴.
The story is a memory aid, not a verified account of meaning or sound components.

## Notes

- [建](建.md), build, is the whole right-hand component, but is not another
  spelling of healthy. [件](件.md), matter/case, and [倹](倹.md), frugal,
  share the narrow person component while having different right sides.
- Robustness overlaps the strength expressed by [強](強.md), [剛](剛.md),
  [勁](勁.md) and [力](力.md). These are useful comparisons, not clearly
  wrong meaning answers for this card.
- Health preservation in 保健 and health itself in 健康 are related but
  different ideas; learn each complete word rather than replacing one with the other.
