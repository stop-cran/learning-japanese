# 怠 — neglect; be lazy

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

怠 is slackness: not doing what should be done, or not wanting to work at all. [怠る](../words/怠る.md) おこたる is to neglect or fail to do; JMdict glosses it "to neglect; to be negligent in; to be neglectful of; to fail to do; to leave undone," and gives a second, dated sense, "to improve (of an illness); to get better." [怠ける](../words/怠ける.md) なまける is to be lazy; JMdict glosses it "to be lazy; to be idle; to slack" and, in a second sense, "to neglect (e.g. one's work); to be neglectful of; to skip (e.g. school)."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense, a slackened mind and laziness, with examples such as 怠惰 たいだ, "lazy; idle; indolent; slothful," 怠慢 たいまん, "negligence; neglect; carelessness; procrastination," and 勤怠 きんたい, "diligence and indolence; attendance and absence (at work)." It gives the on readings タイ and ダイ and the kun readings おこたる, なまける and だるい. ダイ and だるい are not KANJIDIC2 readings; JMdict has no word reading 怠 as ダイ, and it spells だるい 怠い, "sluggish; languid; listless," marked as usually written in kana. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [台](台.md), "stand, machine counter," on top and [心](心.md), "heart, mind," below. Imagine a heart lying idle on a stand instead of getting to work. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 台 or 心 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| なま.ける | kun | [怠ける](../words/怠ける.md) なまける — be lazy; neglect |

In 怠る, 怠 writes おこた and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 怠ける, 怠 writes なま and ける is okurigana. In 怠惰, 怠 supplies タイ and [惰](惰.md) supplies ダ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows タイ, with 怠惰 and 怠慢 as examples; おこたる, with 怠る; and なまける, with 怠ける and 怠け者 なまけもの, "lazy person; lazy fellow; slothful person." Practice selects おこた.る and なま.ける. タイ is shown with the linked word 怠惰, but it is not selected for questions on this card. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 怠る and 怠ける write 怠 with okurigana only; [怠惰](../words/怠惰.md) is a compound with [惰](惰.md).
- [始](始.md), "begin, start," and [治](治.md), "govern; heal," have 台 at the right, the same shape as the top of 怠. [念](念.md), "thought; attention," has 心 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [慢](慢.md), "arrogance; slackness," overlaps the sense "neglect" that 怠 carries: 怠慢 is "negligence; neglect; carelessness; procrastination." It is kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "neglect" or "lazy" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples: 過怠 かたい, "negligence; carelessness; mistake"; 緩怠 かんたい, "laxity; negligence"; 懈怠 けたい, "laziness; indolence; negligence (of duties)," which JMdict also reads かいたい, けだい and げたい; and 倦怠 けんたい, "weariness; fatigue; languor; boredom; tedium; ennui."
