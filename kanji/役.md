# 役 — role; duty

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

役 concerns the part someone has to play or the duty they carry out. The noun [役](../words/役.md) やく
can name a responsibility within a group; it does not have to mean paid employment.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** give the narrow 彳 on the left a particular job beside the right-hand 殳 shape.
This is a memory aid for the modern outline, not an account of its origin.

## Notes

[彼](彼.md), [待](待.md), and [後](後.md) share the 彳 left side. Their right sides identify different
characters: sharing this component does not make their meanings interchangeable.
For 役, remember what the person is responsible for, rather than simply the person themselves.
