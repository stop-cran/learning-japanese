# 贈 — give as a gift; confer

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

贈 concerns giving something as a gift or formally presenting it. The complete words distinguish a gift, the act of giving it and a presentation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 貝 on the left holding a valuable gift beside the 曽-shaped block on the right, like a decorated presentation box. This is not a historical or phonetic explanation.
