# 狭 — narrow; cramped; confined

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (as 犭, no. 94), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

狭 is narrow in width or space, and by extension narrow in outlook. [狭い](../words/狭い.md) せまい has two JMdict senses: "narrow; confined; small; cramped" and "limited; narrow-minded; confining."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for キョウ, plus a prefix. One example of each:

- Narrow: 狭義 きょうぎ, JMdict's "narrow sense (e.g. of a word)."
- A mind that is not spacious: 狭量 きょうりょう, "narrow-minded; intolerant; petty."
- Prefix さ: 狭霧 さぎり, "mist; fog." This is a hard-to-read whole word and is not split among its kanji.

Other examples for the first two senses are in Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [犬](犬.md), "dog," in its left-hand form 犭, beside the same right-hand part as in [挟](挟.md), "pinch; hold between." Imagine a dog squeezed into a tight space. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 狭い, 狭 writes せま and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 狭める, 狭 writes せば and める is okurigana; in 狭まる it writes せば and まる is okurigana. In 手狭 てぜま, "narrow; small; cramped," 手 writes て and 狭 writes ぜま. In 広狭 こうきょう, [広](広.md), "wide, spacious," supplies こう and 狭 supplies きょう. 偏狭 uses 偏, which has no card here.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キョウ with 狭量, 広狭 and 偏狭, せまい with 狭い and 狭苦しい, せばめる with 狭める, and せばまる with 狭まる. It prints 狭 with the older form 狹 in parentheses. Practice selects せま.い, the reading of the linked word article. The other readings are shown with dictionary examples and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- 狭い has only okurigana, so it has no other kanji.
- [道](道.md), "road, way," [室](室.md), "room, chamber," and [店](店.md), "shop," suit narrow roads, rooms and shops, which makes them plausible wrong answers. They are different kanji with unrelated readings and supply this card's wrong answers. [挟](挟.md) shares キョウ and the right-hand shape but is never used as a wrong answer, and no other キョウ or コウ card is used either.
- The first sense also covers 狭隘 きょうあい, 狭窄, 狭小 and 狭軌; 狭心症 is a medical term. Other kanji for thin, short or tight overlap with it and are kept out of this card's quiz options: [細](細.md), "slender; fine; detailed," [縮](縮.md), "shrink; contract," [小](小.md), "small, little," [寸](寸.md), "sun (length unit); small," [密](密.md), "secret; dense; close," [詰](詰.md), "pack tightly; press," and [限](限.md), "limit."
- 狭間 はざま is a whole word and is not split among its kanji.
