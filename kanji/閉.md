# 閉 — shut

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 門 (no. 169) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

閉 concerns closing or being closed. [閉める](../words/閉める.md) しめる describes someone shutting a door or window;
閉まる しまる describes the door or window closing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the small 才 shape inside 門 as a brace keeping the gate shut.
This is a memory aid, not a historical analysis.

## Notes

Compare ドアを閉める (ドアをしめる), "close the door," with ドアが閉まる (ドアがしまる), "the door closes."
Inside the shared 門 outline, [間](間.md) has 日 and [問](問.md) has 口; 閉 has neither.
[開](開.md), "open," provides the opposite action.
