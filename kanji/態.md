# 態 — attitude; condition

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

態 concerns manner, appearance or state. [態度](../words/態度.md) たいど
is a person's attitude or manner; [状態](../words/状態.md) じょうたい is
a state or condition more generally. [事態](../words/事態.md) じたい is
a situation or state of affairs, often with attention to events developing.
These are related nouns, not three interchangeable words for attitude.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** see [能](能.md) above
[心](心.md). Imagine someone's heart or attitude beneath what they are
able to do. The association is an invented memory aid, not a verified
historical account of the two components.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| タイ | on | [態度](../words/態度.md) たいど - attitude; manner; [状態](../words/状態.md) じょうたい - state; condition; [事態](../words/事態.md) じたい - situation; state of affairs |
| わざ.と | kun | 態と わざと - deliberately; on purpose; a rare kanji spelling, usually わざと |

Practice selects タイ. The spelling 態と is dictionary-attested, but its
rare-kanji and usually-kana qualifications matter: do not make it the
ordinary writing model for "on purpose." In that spelling, 態 supplies わざ
and と remains in kana. The full reading is retained without selecting it
for questions here.

## Notes

- [熊](熊.md), bear, also has 能 at the top, but ends in 灬 rather than
  心. [能](能.md), ability, alone has neither bottom group.
  [急](急.md), urgent/sudden, and [息](息.md), breath, share 心
  but have different tops and meanings.
- State, appearance and manner overlap [状](状.md), [様](様.md),
  [形](形.md), [然](然.md), [体](体.md) and [容](容.md) in their
  applicable senses. Those comparisons are not unrestricted word substitutions.
- JMdict also lists 態 as an additional spelling in the entries headed by
  体 (てい), 形 (なり) and 様 (ざま).
  They are not alternate pronunciations to insert into 態度 or 状態;
  this card preserves the frozen character-reading inventory rather than
  expanding it from every same-spelling word.
