# 履 — footwear; put on shoes; carry out

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44), across the top and down the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

履 has two sides: footwear and putting it on, and stepping steadily through something, so carrying it out. [履く](../words/履く.md) はく is to put on footwear or lower-body clothes, and [草履](../words/草履.md) ぞうり is a zori, a traditional Japanese sandal.

The Daijisen character entry gives リ two senses. The first is treading step by step, or carrying something out steadily, as in 履行 りこう, "performance (of a duty); fulfillment (of a promise); fulfilment; execution (of a contract)," 履修 りしゅう, "taking (a class); studying; completion (of a course)," 履践 りせん, "implementation; carrying out; practice," and 履歴 りれき, "personal history; background; career," which also begins 履歴書 りれきしょ, "personal history document; curriculum vitae; resume." The second is footwear, as in 草履, 弊履 へいり, "worn-out shoes," and 木履 ぽくり or ぽっくり, "girl's lacquered wooden geta." Its kun list adds ふむ and くつ, which are not KANJIDIC2 or Joyo readings of 履: JMdict gives 履む as another spelling of 踏む ふむ, "to step on; to tread on; to trample on," and 履 as a rarely used spelling of 靴 くつ, "shoe; shoes; boots; footwear; footgear."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the key 尸 across the top and down the left, and under it the whole of [復](復.md), "restore; repeat"; its 3 strokes and 復's 12 make 15. Imagine stepping into your shoes again and again under a low ledge before you set out. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 尸 or 復 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| リ | on | [草履](../words/草履.md) ぞうり — zori; traditional Japanese sandals |
| は.く | kun | [履く](../words/履く.md) はく — put on footwear or lower-body clothes |

In 草履, 履 supplies り, as the Daijisen headword ぞう‐り shows. The ぞう belongs to 草: Daijisen spells it ザウ in historical kana, the voiced form of サウ, its historical spelling of 草's ソウ. ぞう itself is not among 草's KANJIDIC2 readings, ソウ and くさ, and the Joyo table accordingly puts 草履 ぞうり in its appendix of words that are hard to show character by character and notes it in brackets under 履. The り part is still リ, a listed reading of 履. In 履く, 履 writes は and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The noun 履物 はきもの, "footwear; footgear; shoes," keeps the same は, and in 下履き したばき, "outdoor shoes," it becomes ば after した.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows リ, with 履歴, 履行 and 弊履 as examples, and notes 草履 with ぞうり in brackets; and はく, with 履く and 履物. Practice selects both readings, each of which has a linked word article.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," in 草履. 履く is 履 with okurigana only.
- [復](復.md), "restore; repeat," is the whole shape under the 尸 of 履. [屋](屋.md), "roof, shop," has the same 尸 key, and [理](理.md), "reason, logic," is also read リ. All are different kanji.
- [靴](靴.md), "shoes," overlaps the footwear sense, and JMdict lists 履 as a rarely used spelling of 靴 くつ. [着](着.md), "wear; arrive," overlaps 履く, "to put on (lower-body clothing, e.g. pants, skirt, footwear); to wear." [演](演.md), "perform; stage," overlaps 履行, "performance (of a duty)." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "wear" or "carry out" as exclusive to any one character.
