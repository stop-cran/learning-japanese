# 区 — ward; district

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 匸 (no. 23) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

区 concerns a division of an area, such as a ward or district. 地区 ちく is a district or designated area. In 区別 くべつ, the division is conceptual: a distinction between things.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the right-open outer enclosure around crossing strokes, like a marked-off area on a map.

The canonical indexing radical is 匸, the enclosure open on the right.

## Notes

- A municipal 区 く and a 地区 ちく are not necessarily the same administrative unit. 地区 can designate an area for an activity without making it an official ward.
