# 析 — analyze

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

析 is associated with separating things into parts and analyzing them. In [分析](../words/分析.md) ぶんせき, the whole word means analysis: examining elements or relationships to understand something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice 木 at the left and 斤 at the right; imagine separating a complicated idea into smaller pieces. This is a memory aid, not a claim about historical formation.

## Notes

- Other sources record シャク as a non-Joyo on-reading and さく as a kun reading. A separate historical terminology entry reads 析 as へぎ for a thin split board. These are source-specific information, not additions to the KANJIDIC2 on/kun lists or selected practice readings.
- [分](分.md) and [割](割.md) have dividing senses; [剖](剖.md) also has a division or dissection sense; [解](解.md) includes unravelling. [切](切.md) overlaps with cutting and [破](破.md) with tearing. These are guarded for direct meaning overlaps, not because their words are interchangeable.
- [板](板.md), board or plank, overlaps the separate へぎ board sense; it is guarded without making 析 a general replacement for 板.
- [新](新.md) shares the right-hand 斤 shape; [本](本.md) and [休](休.md) contain 木. They are looser visual contrasts chosen to supply distinct reading choices in the starter pool.
