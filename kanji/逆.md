# 逆 — reverse; opposite

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

逆 concerns reversal, an opposite direction or going against something. 逆 can name the opposite, 逆さ describes inversion, and 逆らう means to oppose or disobey.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the upright inner strokes turning back against the direction of the 辶-shaped path. This is a modern-shape association, not a historical derivation.

The indexing radical is 辵, visibly shortened to 辶.

## Notes

- The linked 逆 article teaches ぎゃく. The separate さか entry also records a noun/prefix identity; another word reading does not invalidate ぎゃく.
- 莫逆 ばくげき supplies the ゲキ reading in a complete word meaning cordial relations. Do not translate it by mechanically combining an isolated opposite gloss with the other character.
- 逆らう is a godan verb. Its intransitive dictionary classification is compatible with a に-marked person or thing opposed, as in 命令に逆らう (めいれいにさからう), disobey an order.
