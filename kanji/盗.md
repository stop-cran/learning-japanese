# 盗 — steal

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 皿 (no. 108) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

盗 identifies stealing rather than simply picking something up.
[盗む](../words/盗む.md) ぬすむ is the verb; 盗難 とうなん names theft.
The meaning concerns taking something that is not rightfully yours.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture someone reaching toward the dish-shaped 皿 at the bottom without permission.
This is an invented scene for remembering the modern shape, not its historical origin.

## Notes

自転車を盗まれました。 (じてんしゃをぬすまれました。) — My bicycle was stolen.
盗まれる is the passive form: the sentence presents the theft from the affected person's side.

The bottom is 皿, not the 田 in [留](留.md) or [番](番.md).
[買](買.md) has another stacked, box-like outline, but its lower part is 貝. Buying and stealing are different actions.
