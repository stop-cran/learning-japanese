# 翌 — next; subsequent

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 羽 (no. 124) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

翌 concerns the following time period relative to a reference point, as in 翌日, the following day, and 翌年, the following year. That reference point need not be today.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 羽 above 立 and imagine standing up again on the following day. This is an invented modern-shape memory aid, not a derivation of the meaning or pronunciation.

The canonical key 羽, no. 124, is at the top.

## Notes

- 翌年 has the dictionary readings よくねん and よくとし. The second combines 翌's on reading with 年's native reading; do not infer that とし is another reading of 翌.
- [明日](../words/明日.md) あした is tomorrow, usually relative to the speaking day. 翌日 is the day after the stated reference day; in a story about last year it need not mean tomorrow.
- [次](次.md) can concern the next item or step more broadly. Compare the shapes of 翌, [羽](羽.md), [立](立.md) and [習](習.md), without treating them all as time words.
