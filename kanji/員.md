# 員 — member, employee

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

員 identifies a member of a group or a worker in an organization. The group or workplace usually comes first, as in 店員, "shop employee."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the top [口](口.md) calling a member's name and the lower 貝-shaped part as an identification badge. This is a memory aid, not etymology.

## Common words

- [店員](../words/店員.md) てんいん — shop employee
- 会員 かいいん — member of a club or association
- 会社員 かいしゃいん — company employee
- 全員 ぜんいん — all members; everyone in a group

## Notes

Keep the い after ん in 店員 てんいん and 全員 ぜんいん. The standard reading taught here is てんいん, not ていいん.

[買](買.md) has a different top component. [品](品.md) consists of three boxes; 員 does not.
