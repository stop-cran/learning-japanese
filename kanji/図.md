# 図 — diagram, plan

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

図 can mean a diagram, drawing, or map. The verb 図る extends to planning or trying to achieve something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a framed sheet with marks sketched inside it, like a simple diagram. This is an association with the modern shape, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

The radical is the surrounding enclosure 囗, number 31, not the mouth radical 口, number 30.

Compare [国](国.md), with 玉 inside, and [田](田.md), with a simple cross dividing the enclosure. Learn 図書館 as としょかん rather than substituting ズ.
