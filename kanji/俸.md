# 俸 — salary; stipend

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

俸 concerns salary or a stipend, especially pay associated with official duties.
[俸給](../words/俸給.md) ほうきゅう means salary or pay, particularly for public
employees, but is not restricted to government employment.
年俸 ねんぽう means annual salary.

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and 奉.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a person standing beside a stack of lines
representing a salary record. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical
explanation of the character or a claim about 奉's original meaning.

## Notes

- [棒](棒.md), "rod; stick," has 木 on the left instead of 亻. Write 年俸 for
  annual salary; JMdict marks 年棒 as irregular kanji usage.
- [借](借.md), "borrow; rent," and [付](付.md), "attach," share 亻 but have
  different right sides. Neither names a salary.
- 年俸 specifies an annual basis or a year's salary, not necessarily a single
  yearly payment. The word alone does not establish a payment schedule.
- Historical 俸禄 ほうろく means a retainer's stipend. That historical use does
  not make modern 俸給 a payment in rice.
