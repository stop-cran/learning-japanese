# 庁 — government agency

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

庁 concerns a government office or agency. It is read チョウ in administrative names and in 府庁 ふちょう, the prefectural office of Osaka or Kyoto. It is not a general label for any private business workspace.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 广 over 丁 and imagine the roof of an administrative office with a sign beneath it. This modern-shape story is not etymology.
