# 后 — empress; queen (consort)

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

后 means an empress or queen consort, the wife of a monarch, as in 皇后 and 皇太后. For the reading コウ, Digital Daijisen gives the senses of a monarch and of an empress or queen consort; for ゴ, it gives “behind; after,” as in 午后, and notes that 后 is used interchangeably with 後.

## Notes

The Joyo table gives 后 only the reading コウ, with the examples 皇后 and 皇太后. Kanjipedia marks the readings ゴ, のち, きさき and きみ as outside the Joyo table (外); ゴ and きさき are in this card's metadata but are not quizzed, and のち and きみ are not in this card's metadata.

妻 (“wife”) and 婦 (“woman; wife”) overlap with the sense of a monarch's wife. 後 (“behind, after”) is the usual spelling for the ゴ sense; Digital Daijisen notes that 后 is used interchangeably with 後.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 一 (“one”), 来 (“come”), and 百 (“hundred”); none means an empress or queen.
