# 方 — direction, method

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 方 (no. 70) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

方 can concern a direction, side, or way of doing something. Read かた, it can also be a respectful word for a person. Its meaning depends on the word or construction.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the long lower strokes as routes branching below a signpost. They suggest a direction or a way to go; this is not an origin claim.

- [夕方](../words/夕方.md) ゆうがた — early evening
- 方法 ほうほう — method, way
- 方向 ほうこう — direction
- 読み方 よみかた — pronunciation, way of reading

## Notes

For "how to do", attach 方 to the verb's ます-stem: 読みます よみます becomes 読み方 よみかた. Read this suffix かた, not ほう. In 夕方, がた marks an approximate time of day; it does not mean "method".

In comparisons, standalone 方 is read ほう: こちらの方が安い（こちらのほうがやすい）, "This one is cheaper."
The advice pattern ～た方がいい also uses ほう: 休んだ方がいい（やすんだほうがいい）, "It would be better to rest."

Compare [万](万.md), which lacks 方's top dot, and [力](力.md), which has only two strokes. In あの方 あのかた (that person; respectful), 方 is not a direction.
