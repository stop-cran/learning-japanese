# 跳 — hop; spring up

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 足 (no. 157) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

跳 is springing up off the ground: hopping, leaping or bounding, and also splashing up. KANJIDIC2 lists “hop”, “leap up”, “spring”, “jerk”, “prance”, “buck”, “splash”, “sputter” and “snap”, and Digital Daijisen describes it as はね上がって地面から離れる (springing up and leaving the ground). In [跳ねる](../words/跳ねる.md) はねる, JMdict's first sense is “to jump; to leap; to prance; to spring up; to bound; to hop”, and its second is “to splash (of oil, mud, etc.); to spatter; to splash up”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [足](足.md), "foot, leg," with [口](口.md), "mouth," at its top, stands on the left. On the right is [兆](兆.md), "trillion; omen." Picture a foot that springs up and hops the moment it sees a good omen. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 足 or 兆 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists チョウ, with 跳躍, はねる, with 跳ねる, and とぶ, with 跳ぶ and 縄跳び, and pairs 跳ぶ with 飛ぶ (⇔). The leading dash in -と.び marks a use after another element, as in 縄跳び; it is not pronounced.
- Kanjipedia lists おどる outside the table (外), for its second sense おどる。おどりあがる。. It is not a KANJIDIC2 reading, so it has no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 跳, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These three cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [飛](飛.md) (“fly”): JMdict entry 1429700 writes とぶ as 飛ぶ or 跳ぶ, and its second sense, “to jump; to leap; to spring; to bound; to hop”, is marked esp. 跳ぶ; the Joyo table pairs 跳ぶ with 飛ぶ, and the 飛 card lists と.ぶ.
- [踊](踊.md) (“dance; hop”): its title's “hop” matches KANJIDIC2's “hop” for 跳, and Kanjipedia gives 跳 the reading おどる outside the Joyo table, while the 踊 card lists おど.る.
- [弾](弾.md) (“bullet; play strings; bounce”): JMdict's first sense of 弾む はずむ is “to spring; to bound; to bounce” (entry 1419400), close to 跳ねる's “to spring up; to bound; to hop”.

The [躍](躍.md) card (“leap; jump up”) declares the 跳/躍 pair, because Kanjipedia gives おどる for both characters.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 滑 (“slide; slippery; smooth”), 沈 (“sink; subside”), and 揺 (“shake; sway; waver”); none means hopping or springing up.
