# 次 — next

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 欠 (no. 76) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

次 locates something next in a sequence. The noun [次](../words/次.md) (つぎ) takes の before a following noun:
次の駅 (つぎのえき), "the next station."
The order can concern time, stops, items, or turns rather than physical nearness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** count the two small left-hand marks before moving on to the larger 欠 on the right.
Think "next in order"; this is not an etymology.

- 目次 もくじ — table of contents

## Notes

次の (つぎの) modifies a noun; 次に (つぎに) can introduce what happens next.
The left side has two marks, not the three of 氵.
[飲](飲.md) and [歌](歌.md) also contain 欠, but their other components are different.
