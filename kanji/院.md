# 院 — institution

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

院 occurs in names for **institutions**, including hospitals, some educational establishments, and temples.
It does not mean "hospital" in every compound; learn the whole institution name.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the left-hand 阝 as a boundary beside a roofed building.
The right-hand 完 has a roof above [元](元.md). This is a modern-shape aid, not an origin claim.

## Common words

- [病院](../words/病院.md) びょういん — hospital
- 入院 にゅういん — hospitalization; entering hospital as a patient
- 退院 たいいん — discharge from hospital
- 大学院 だいがくいん — graduate school
- 美容院 びよういん — beauty salon; hair salon

## Notes

病院に行く（びょういんにいく）, "go to the hospital," does not necessarily mean 入院する
（にゅういんする）, "be admitted to hospital."

The left-hand 阝 is the component form of the classical radical 阜.
[館](館.md) also appears in building names, but has a food-shaped left component instead.
院 has the roofed 完 on the right, not bare [元](元.md).
