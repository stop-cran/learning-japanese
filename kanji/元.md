# 元 — origin, former

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (no. 10) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

元 points to a beginning, source, or earlier state. As もと it can also mean "former."
[元気](../words/元気.md), "well; energetic," is a word to learn as a whole rather than translate as "former air."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** [二](二.md)-like lines above two leg-like strokes mark a starting line to return to.
This is a mnemonic, not a reconstruction of the character's history.
