# 艶 — gloss; luster; allure

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 色 (no. 139) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

艶 names a smooth surface’s gloss or luster, as well as an appealing richness in a voice or an alluring, graceful quality in a person or performance. The character can also refer to romance. These figurative senses are related to charm, but they do not mean that 艶 is the material used to glaze an object.

## Notes

The selected quiz reading is **つや**: the existing single-character word 艶 (つや) means “gloss; luster.” エン is a separate on-reading used in compounds such as 妖艶; it is not the reading selected for this question.

KANJIDIC2 also lists つや.めく and なま.めく; JMdict confirms both readings of 艶めく, with different senses. Kanjipedia additionally lists うらやむ as an outside-Jōyō reading, but the reviewed JMdict examples do not align to that form, so it is not a quiz option. Digital Daijisen’s name readings おお・もろ・よし and KANJIDIC2 nanori もろ・よし are name forms, not ordinary readings. The title’s “allure” overlaps with 魅 (“charm; bewitch”), so 魅 is excluded from meaning choices.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 氷 (“ice; freeze”), 星 (“star; celestial point”), and 魚 (“fish”); none means gloss, luster, or allure.
