# 艶 — gloss; luster; allure

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 色 (no. 139) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

艶 names a smooth surface’s gloss or luster. Figuratively, it can describe a smooth, rich voice or youthful skin, the appeal or charm that a performance gains, and an alluring, captivating quality in a person. The character can also refer to romance, as in matters between lovers.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [豊](豊.md), "bountiful; abundant," stands at the left and [色](色.md), "color," at the right. Imagine abundant color shining from a polished surface: gloss with allure. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 豊 or 色 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

The selected quiz reading is **つや**: the existing single-character word 艶 (つや) means “gloss; luster.” エン is a separate on-reading used in compounds such as 妖艶; it is not the reading selected for this question.

KANJIDIC2 also lists つや.めく and なま.めく; JMdict confirms both readings of 艶めく, with different senses. Kanjipedia additionally lists うらやむ as an outside-Jōyō reading, but the reviewed JMdict examples do not align to that form, so it is not a quiz option. Digital Daijisen’s name readings おお・もろ・よし and KANJIDIC2 nanori もろ・よし are name forms, not ordinary readings. The title’s “allure” overlaps with 魅 (“charm; bewitch”). KANJIDIC2 lists “luster” for both 艶 and 華 (“flower; splendor; showiness”), and Kanjipedia describes 艶 as あでやかで美しい (glamorously beautiful), overlapping 美 (“beauty”). JMdict glosses 艶 (つや) as “gloss; luster; lustre; shine; sheen; polish” and, in figurative senses, “charm; color” and “romance; love; sexiness”; it glosses 艶かしい (なまめかしい) as “charming; captivating; bewitching; seductive.” These overlap with 光 (“light; radiance”), 照 (“illuminate; shine”), 磨 (“polish; grind”), 研 (“polish; study closely”), 色 (“color”), 恋 (“romantic love; longing”), 愛 (“love”), and 妖 (“bewitching; supernatural”). 符 (“token; sign; charm”) uses “charm” in its talisman sense, but because a learner could read the shared word as attractiveness, 符 is excluded here as it is on 魅. These twelve kanji are excluded from meaning choices.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 氷 (“ice; freeze”), 星 (“star; celestial point”), and 魚 (“fish”); none means gloss, luster, or allure.
