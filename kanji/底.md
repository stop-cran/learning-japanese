# 底 — bottom; base

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

底 concerns a bottom or base, including the sole of something or the depths beneath a surface. 海底 かいてい is the seabed; 徹底 てってい means thoroughness or thorough enforcement rather than a physical bottom.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 广 over 氐 and imagine looking down to the very bottom of a covered space. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical derivation.
