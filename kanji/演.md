# 演 — perform; stage

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

演 is useful for performing a role, staging a work, or giving a performance.
[公演](../words/公演.md) こうえん is a public performance; 演奏 (えんそう) is specifically
a musical performance. The character does not by itself specify which art is involved.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** take the roofed right-hand 寅 shape and its spreading lower strokes
as a covered stage. The scene is a memory aid, not an explanation of the character's origin.

- [公演](../words/公演.md) こうえん — public performance
- 演じる えんじる — act a part; play a role
- 演奏 えんそう — musical performance
- 上演 じょうえん — staging a play or other work

## Notes

公演は七時に始まります。 (こうえんはしちじにはじまります。) — The performance starts at seven.
演 has a roof at the top right; [漢](漢.md) has a different upper-right structure.
The shared 氵 does not mean that these performance words have a water-related sense.
