# 感 — feeling; sensation

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

感 concerns feeling, sensing, or responding to an experience. [感情](../words/感情.md) かんじょう
names emotion; 感じる かんじる is the verb "to feel." 感想 かんそう names impressions or reactions.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** locate [心](心.md) below the broad 咸-shaped upper group and imagine
an experience reaching the heart. This is a visual aid only; no phonetic or historical claim is made.
