# 縁 — edge; connection; fate

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

縁 is the border of a thing and, by extension, the link between people or events. [縁](../words/縁.md) ふち is the rim or edge; JMdict glosses ふち "rim; brim; edge; brink." The word article teaches "rim; brim; edge."

The Daijisen character entry gives 4 senses for エン. One example of each:

- the rim or edge: 縁辺 えんぺん, "edge; relative; relations; border";
- a connection: 絶縁 ぜつえん, "breaking off relations (with); severing one's connections (with); breaking with (e.g. one's past)" and "insulation (electrical or thermal); isolation";
- a marriage or kin relation: 血縁 けつえん, "blood relationship; consanguinity" and "blood relative";
- the Buddhist indirect cause: 因縁 いんねん, "fate; destiny," "connection; tie; bond; origin," "pretext; justification" and Buddhist "hetu and prataya."

Daijisen also lists ふち (ぶち) with 額縁 and 黒縁, and the kun readings へり, よる, ゆかり, えにし and よすが. Its usage note says ふち and へり are interchangeable for the rim of a desk, a teacup or a cliff, but ふち also applies to frames (眼鏡のふち) where へり is not used, while へり often refers to the periphery of flat things such as 川べり.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 糸, "thread," at the left; the right-hand part resembles the one in [緑](緑.md), "green," but they are different kanji. Imagine a thread running around the border of a cloth. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 縁 ふち the whole word is written with one kanji. In 縁取る ふちどる, 縁 supplies ふち and どる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 縁取り ふちどり is "hemming; bordering." In 縁側 えんがわ, "engawa; external corridor on the outer side of traditional Japanese houses," and 縁起 えんぎ, "omen; sign of luck," these are whole-word readings; 縁日 えんにち is "temple festival; fair; fete day." In 血縁 けつえん, 血 supplies ケツ and 縁 supplies エン. In 因縁 いんねん, 因 supplies イン and 縁 supplies ネン. In 額縁 がくぶち, "(picture) frame," 額 supplies ガク and 縁 supplies ぶち, the voiced form of ふち; 金縁 きんぶち, "gilded edges or rims," and 黒縁 くろぶち, "black rim; black edge," follow the same pattern. 周縁 しゅうえん is "fringe; rim; periphery; borders; outskirts" and 外縁 がいえん is "brink; outer edge."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows エン, with 縁故, 縁日 and 血縁 (and a note that 因縁 is read インネン); and ふち, with 縁, 縁取り and 額縁. It prints the variant 緣 in round brackets. Practice selects ふち, the reading of the linked word article 縁. The other readings are illustrated with dictionary words; being outside the practice set does not make them invalid. Kanjipedia also lists よ.る as an outside-the-standard-table reading, which is not a KANJIDIC2 row and is not taught here.

## Notes

- 縁 contains no other kanji. Among the compounds named above, [血](血.md), "blood," supplies ケツ in 血縁, [因](因.md), "cause; factor," supplies イン in 因縁, [額](額.md), "forehead; frame; sum," supplies ガク in 額縁, [金](金.md), "gold, money," supplies キン in 金縁, and [取](取.md), "take," supplies と in 縁取る.
- [辺](辺.md), [端](端.md), [際](際.md), [境](境.md), [岸](岸.md), [周](周.md) and [畔](畔.md) overlap the edge or rim sense; [刃](刃.md) is the edge of a blade. [係](係.md), [連](連.md), [関](関.md), [絡](絡.md), [結](結.md), [契](契.md) and [仲](仲.md) overlap the connection sense; [運](運.md) and [偶](偶.md) overlap fate and chance. All are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [緑](緑.md), "green," looks plausible because of the similar right-hand shape and the 糸 at the left, and is read リョク, ロク and みどり. [縮](縮.md), "shrink; contract," also has 糸 at the left and is read シュク and ちぢ.む. [魚](魚.md), "fish," is a common beginner kanji read ギョ and さかな. None shares a reading or a meaning with 縁.
- Cards [依](依.md), [因](因.md), [寄](寄.md) and [由](由.md) share the outside-the-table reading よ.る with 縁 and are never offered as wrong answers.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples: 縁海, 外縁, 縁故, 良縁 and 離縁. Daijisen reads 薄縁 as うすべり and 所縁 as ゆかり.
