# 補 — supplement; make good

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

補 concerns supplying what is missing, supplementing or making good a shortage. 候補 こうほ names a candidate, while 補う おぎなう directly expresses supplementing or compensating.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a new patch, the right-hand 甫-shaped block, supplying a missing part of a garment 衤. This is a mnemonic rather than a claim about the component's historical role.

The indexing radical 衣 appears as left-hand 衤, not the similar-looking 礻.
