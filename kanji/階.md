# 階 — storey; stair

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

階 concerns a building level, stairs or a stage. 階段 names stairs, while 二階 identifies a building's second floor.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 皆-shaped right side as people using a staircase beside the left 阝. This is a modern-shape story, not an etymological or phonetic claim.

The canonical indexing radical is 阜, no. 170, in its left-hand form 阝.

## Notes

- The inspected かい entry includes a floor noun and a counter for building storeys. 二階 にかい is a floor designation; it does not name the staircase leading there.
- The separate きざはし entry means stairs and also records stairs at the front of a noh stage. It has no rarity flag in the pinned entry. This does not establish its frequency or make it a replacement for the カイ floor counter.
- The right side resembles [皆](皆.md), all/everyone. That visual comparison does not turn the whole character 階 into the word for everyone.
