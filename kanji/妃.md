# 妃 — royal consort

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

妃 points to a consort or princess. In 王妃 (おうひ), the title identifies a royal spouse; it does not say she inherited the throne herself.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 女 on the left and 己 on the right. [紀](紀.md) and [記](記.md) end in a similar shape but have thread and speech elements on the left.

## Notes

Compare [后](后.md), which can also name a queen or empress consort. Do not identify every 王女 (おうじょ, king's daughter) as a 王妃.

[紀](紀.md) puts thread to the left of 己; [改](改.md) puts a similar shape to the left of its striking element. [姉](姉.md) begins with 女 like 妃 but ends in 市. [妻](妻.md) (wife) and [婦](婦.md) (woman; wife) do not by themselves indicate royalty.
