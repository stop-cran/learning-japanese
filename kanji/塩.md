# 塩 — salt

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

塩 writes the everyday salt word しお. The on-reading エン appears in 食塩 しょくえん, table salt; chemical terminology can also use 塩 えん for a salt.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 土 on the left beside a small box over a shallow 皿-like dish holding salt.

## Notes

- The linked 塩 word article teaches しお. The chemical noun 塩 えん is a separately documented reading, not a reason to change that article's primary reading.
- The chemical category of salts is broader than edible table salt. Do not assume that every substance called a 塩 えん is a food seasoning.
- The canonical radical is 土 on the left, not the dish-like lower right.
