# 油 — oil; grease

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

油 concerns oil or fat. 油 あぶら is the native noun, and 石油 せきゆ names petroleum. The ユウ reading occurs in 油然 ゆうぜん, an expression about something welling up rather than a kind of cooking oil.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside 由 and imagine oil held in a container. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical or phonetic derivation.

## Notes

- Oil may be used in cooking or machinery; the noun alone does not establish that a substance is edible. 石油 is not a substitute for cooking oil in a recipe.
- JMdict labels 油然 as an adverb used with と and a taru adjective, a literary adjective class. Recognize 油然と ゆうぜんと in that construction, not as a second pronunciation of independent 油 あぶら.
- The separate same-sounding noun 脂 あぶら concerns fat or grease. The English ranges overlap; do not invent a universal liquid-versus-solid boundary from the spellings alone.
- Distinguish [由](由.md), reason or grounds, without 氵, and [届](届.md), reach or deliver, with 尸 above the inner form.
