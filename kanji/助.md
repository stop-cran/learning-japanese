# 助 — help; rescue

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

助 covers help, assistance, and rescue. [助ける](../words/助ける.md) たすける describes helping someone;
助かる たすかる describes the helped person's survival or relief.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine 力 supporting the shelf-like 且 on its left. This pictures assistance, not historical derivation.

## Notes

助けて！ (たすけて！) means "Help!" The て-form here requests assistance.
Do not confuse the left-hand 且 with the 田 above 力 in [男](男.md).
