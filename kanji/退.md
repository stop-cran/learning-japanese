# 退 — withdraw; retreat

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

退 expresses moving back or withdrawing from a place or role.
In [退院](../words/退院.md) たいいん, someone leaves hospital care; in 退職 たいしょく,
someone leaves employment. Neither word requires a physical backward step.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 艮-shaped inner figure stepping back along the sweeping path.
This is a modern memory aid, not an account of the original character.

- 退職 たいしょく — retirement; resignation
- 退く しりぞく — step back; withdraw

## Notes

退職 does not specify retirement because of age: resignation is also within its meaning.
The radical 辵 appears as 辶, as it does in [近](近.md), [道](道.md), and [週](週.md).
For 退, look for the upper box and lower angled strokes of 艮 inside that frame.
