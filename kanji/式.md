# 式 — ceremony; formula

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 弋 (no. 56) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

式 can name a ceremony, a formula, or an established form or method.
An 入学式 にゅうがくしき is a school entrance ceremony; a 数式 すうしき is a mathematical expression.
In [形式](../words/形式.md) けいしき, the focus is form or format.
These are word-specific meanings, not one English translation that fits everywhere.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture 工 fitted inside the long slanting, hooked 弋 shape, like a small pattern held in place.
This is a way to remember the modern layout, not a verified account of its origin.

## Notes

[試](試.md) contains the entire 式 shape beside 言, but means testing or trying.
[代](代.md) contains 弋 beside a person component, without 工.

These comparisons help recognition; they do not establish phonetic relationships.
