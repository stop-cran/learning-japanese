# 甚 — extreme; excessive; very

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 甘 (no. 99), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

甚 is a degree that goes past the ordinary: extreme, excessive, very great. The linked [甚だしい](../words/甚だしい.md) はなはだしい is JMdict's "extreme; excessive; terrible; intense; severe; serious; tremendous; heavy (damage)." JMdict marks 甚しい as a "search-only kanji form." The separate adverb 甚だ はなはだ, "very; greatly; extremely; exceedingly; highly; quite," has no word article yet.

The Daijisen character entry gives はなはだ and はなはだしい as the kun readings and, for ジン, lists the words 甚雨, 甚暑, 甚大, 激甚, 幸甚 and 深甚.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [甘](甘.md), "sweet; pamper," at the top and [匹](匹.md), "small-animal counter; equal," below it. Imagine too much of something sweet piled on top, more than anyone needs. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 甘 or 匹 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジン | on, source-only here | 甚大 じんだい — "very great; enormous; serious"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| はなは.だ | kun | [甚だしい](../words/甚だしい.md) はなはだしい — extreme; excessive |
| はなは.だしい | kun | The longer KANJIDIC2 form, the same word 甚だしい はなはだしい with its okurigana だしい; not selected separately |

In 甚だしい, 甚 writes はなはだ and しい is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The repo's alignment labels this はなはだ as はなは.だ, while KANJIDIC2 also lists the longer form はなは.だしい. The adverb 甚だ is a separate word with the same はなはだ.

In 甚大, [大](大.md) supplies ダイ and 甚 supplies ジン: じん + だい. 激甚 げきじん, "intense; violent; severe; vehement," tagged "formal or literary term," and 幸甚 こうじん, "being extremely grateful; being much obliged; being very appreciative; being very happy," tagged "formal or literary term," also use ジン; [激](激.md) supplies ゲキ and [幸](幸.md) supplies コウ. Kanjipedia also lists いた.く, which KANJIDIC2 does not; JMdict has 甚く いたく, "extremely; exceedingly; deeply," tagged "word usually written using kana alone," but it is not in this card's metadata and has no word article.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジン with 甚大, 激甚 and 幸甚; はなはだ with 甚だ; and はなはだしい with 甚だしい. Practice selects はなは.だ, the reading in the linked word article 甚だしい, which the repo's aligner labels by its stem. The other readings are shown with dictionary examples; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- 甚だしい has only this kanji. In the other words above, [大](大.md), "big, large," supplies ダイ in 甚大, [激](激.md), "intense; excite," supplies ゲキ in 激甚 and [幸](幸.md), "happiness; good fortune," supplies コウ in 幸甚.
- [甘](甘.md), [匹](匹.md) and [基](基.md), "foundation; basis," have a similar stack of strokes. They look alike at a glance but have different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [極](極.md), "extreme; pole," [激](激.md), "intense; excite," [過](過.md), "exceed; go beyond," [超](超.md), "exceed; surpass," [最](最.md), "most," [越](越.md), "cross over; exceed," [厳](厳.md), "strict; severe; solemn," [偉](偉.md), "admirable; great," [巨](巨.md), "huge; gigantic," and [重](重.md), "heavy; piled up," overlap the extreme, excessive, very great, severe and heavy senses. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "extreme" or "very" as exclusive to any one character. 人, 刃, 尋, 沈 and 神 also read ジン and are never offered as wrong answers.
