# 作 — make, produce

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

作 concerns making, producing, or preparing something. [作る](../words/作る.md) applies to meals, objects, and many other results;
[作文](../words/作文.md) concerns composing a piece of writing.

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and 乍.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** a person works beside the rack-like strokes on the right, making something.
This is a mnemonic rather than verified etymology.
