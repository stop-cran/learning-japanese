# 楠 — camphor tree

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

楠 writes **camphor tree**, [楠](../words/楠.md) くすのき or くす, *Cinnamomum camphora*. The native noun can also refer to its wood.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside [南](南.md), south. Imagine a camphor tree south of your home. This is a memory aid, not evidence that 楠 was historically formed to mean "southern tree."

## Notes

- This card asks the two verified native readings. The four on readings are preserved in their source order but are not quiz questions; they are not alternative everyday pronunciations of this plant noun.
- 樟 is another dictionary spelling of くすのき or くす. The choice of character does not create two different species.
- 石楠花 (しゃくなげ) means rhododendron, not camphor tree. Learn that word's spelling and pronunciation together; its whole-word reading does not establish any of the four on readings above.
- Compare [南](南.md), south: 楠 adds 木 on the left. [村](村.md), village, and [相](相.md), mutual or aspect, replace the right side with 寸 and 目.
