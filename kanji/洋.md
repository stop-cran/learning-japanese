# 洋 — ocean; Western style

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

洋 has an ocean sense and a Western-style sense. For clothing and food, it usually points to the latter: [洋服](../words/洋服.md) means Western-style clothes, not clothes worn at sea.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the water side 氵 ([水](水.md)) stands next to 羊, "sheep." Imagine a sheep sailing across an ocean. This is only a modern-shape memory aid.

- 西洋 せいよう — the West, Western countries

## Notes

Compare [海](海.md), "sea, ocean": the meanings overlap in the water-related sense, but 洋 in 洋服 marks a cultural style.
[注](注.md) also starts with 氵; its right-hand 主 shape is different from 羊.
