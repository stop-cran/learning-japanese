# 績 — achievements; results

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

績 concerns achievements or a record of results. 成績 can describe school grades or performance in another activity; the character does not say whether the results are good or bad.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 糸 beside 責 and imagine a thread recording the work someone has accomplished. This is an invented modern-shape memory aid, not a verified historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical key 糸, no. 120, appears on the left as ⺯.
