# 介 — mediate; interpose

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

介 often concerns coming between people or providing an intermediary function. 紹介 しょうかい is an introduction; 仲介 ちゅうかい is mediation or brokerage. 介護 かいご instead concerns providing care, so 'mediate' is a learning anchor rather than a universal word translation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the two lower strokes standing between two sides beneath the person-shaped top, acting as a go-between.

## Notes

- 紹介する しょうかいする can introduce a person or present information about something. It is not limited to mediating a dispute.
- 介 has two separate lower strokes. Compare [今](今.md) いま, now, whose lower shape is different.
