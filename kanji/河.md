# 河 — river; watercourse

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

河 concerns a river. 河 かわ and 川 かわ are spellings of the same native river word. 河川 かせん supplies the on reading カ; it means rivers or river systems.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside 可 and imagine a river passing a small opening. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a claim about the character's historical construction or phonetic role.

The canonical radical 水, no. 85, appears as the three left-hand dots 氵.

## Notes

- [川](川.md) and 河 overlap in river meaning. The linked word article recommends 川 for ordinary beginner writing; large river equals 河 is not a universal spelling rule.
- 氷河 ひょうが demonstrates a voiced ガ inside that compound. Learn its complete reading; this does not replace the independent native reading かわ.
- Distinguish [何](何.md), what, with 亻 on the left. Similar-looking components do not make 何 another river character.
