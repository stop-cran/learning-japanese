# 透 — transparent; see through; permeate

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162), wrapping the left and bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

透 is light or a gaze passing straight through something, so that you can see through it, and a liquid or idea soaking all the way through. [透明](../words/透明.md) とうめい is transparent or see-through, and [透き通る](../words/透き通る.md) すきとおる is to be transparent, or clear-sounding.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for トウ: passing through a gap, being see-through, as in 透視 とうし, "seeing through; fluoroscopy; clairvoyance," 透写 とうしゃ, "tracing," 透徹 とうてつ, "transparency; clarity; coherence," 透明 and 浸透 しんとう, "permeation (of a liquid, etc.); soaking; percolation." It gives すく, すかす, すける and とおる as the native readings. JMdict glosses the verb 透く すく "to be transparent; to leave a gap."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 辶, the written form of the key 辵, wrapping under the left and bottom, and [秀](秀.md) above it: 秀 is a grain-plant shape 禾 over [乃](乃.md). Imagine sunlight moving straight through a field of tall grain stalks and coming out the other side. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 辶, 禾 or 乃 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 透明, 透 supplies トウ and 明 supplies メイ. In 透き通る, 透 writes す and き is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji: すき is the stem of 透く. Then 通 writes とお and る is okurigana again, giving すきとおる. 透かす gives す with かす, and 透ける gives す with ける. The とお of 透き通る belongs to 通, not to 透.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows トウ, with 透写, 透明 and 浸透 as examples; すく, with 透く; すかす, with 透かす and 透かし すかし, "watermark (in a stamp, banknote, etc.)"; and すける, with 透ける. とうる and とうす are not in the official table. Practice selects トウ and す.く, the readings with linked word articles; す.かす and す.ける are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words, and とう.る and とう.す have no ordinary example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [明](明.md), "bright, clear," in 透明 and [通](通.md), "pass through; commute," in 透き通る.
- [逃](逃.md), "escape; flee," has the same 辶 and is also read トウ. [迷](迷.md), "get lost; be unsure," has the same 辶 wrapped around 米, a shape close to the 禾 in 透. [塔](塔.md), "tower; pagoda," is also read トウ. All are different kanji.
- [通](通.md), "pass through; commute," [経](経.md), "pass through; sutra," [徹](徹.md), "penetrate; be thorough," and [明](明.md), "bright, clear," overlap the senses "pass through," "penetrate" and "clear" that 透 and its words carry; 明 appears in 透明, 通 in 透き通る, and JMdict lists 透る and 透す as rare spellings of 通る and 通す. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "see through" or "penetrate" as exclusive to any one character.
