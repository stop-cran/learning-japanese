# 侯 — marquis; feudal lord

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

侯 is a historical title for a marquis or feudal lord. The compound [諸侯](../words/諸侯.md) しょこう refers to feudal lords as a group.

## Common words

- [諸侯](../words/諸侯.md) しょこう — feudal lords; barons; princes
- 王侯 おうこう — kings and feudal lords (JMdict entry 1588830)

## Notes

侯 refers to a historical title or rank; it is not the character in 候補 こうほ (“candidate”). JMdict lists 侯補 only as a search-only form for that word.
