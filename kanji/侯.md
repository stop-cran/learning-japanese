# 侯 — marquis; feudal lord

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

侯 is a historical title for a marquis or feudal lord. The historical compound [諸侯](../words/諸侯.md) しょこう refers to feudal lords as a group.

## Common words

- [諸侯](../words/諸侯.md) しょこう — feudal lords; historical term, not an everyday word

## Notes

侯 refers to a historical rank or title; it is not the character in 候補 こうほ (“candidate”). JMdict lists 侯補 only as a search-only form for 候補, which uses 候. JMdict marks 諸侯 as historical and gives it no frequency tag, so this card includes it as a clearly labeled historical example, not as everyday vocabulary. 殿 can mean “lord” as an honorific as well as a palace; 侯 refers specifically to a rank.
