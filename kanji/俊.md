# 俊 — talented; outstanding

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

俊 carries ideas such as talent, excellence and genius. Its reading appears in [俊足](../words/俊足.md), a word whose first JMdict sense is “swiftness of foot; fast runner.”

## Notes

俊足 is a whole-word example, not a claim that 俊 alone means “fast.” JMdict also records a “gifted person” sense for the spelling 俊足. 俊’s talent and excellence meanings overlap with senses of 秀, 英, 佳, 傑, 才, 優, 卓 and 豪, and with 偉 (“admirable; great”): JMdict glosses 偉い えらい as “great; excellent; admirable” (entry 1155780). Digital Daijisen explains 俊 as outstanding talent and intellect, which also overlaps with 賢 (“wise; intelligent”) and 哲 (“philosophy; wise; sage”). Each character has other uses.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 安 (“cheap, peaceful”), 少 (“few, a little”), and 多 (“many, much”); none means talented or outstanding.
