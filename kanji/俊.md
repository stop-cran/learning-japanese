# 俊 — talented; outstanding

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

俊 carries ideas such as talent, excellence and genius. Its reading appears in [俊足](../words/俊足.md), a word whose common sense is “fast runner.”

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シュン | on | [俊足](../words/俊足.md) しゅんそく — fast runner; swift on foot (JMdict entry 1340940) |

## Common words

- [俊足](../words/俊足.md) しゅんそく — fast runner; swift on foot
- 俊英 しゅんえい — excellence; genius

## Notes

俊足 is a whole-word example, not a claim that 俊 alone means “fast.” JMdict also records a “gifted person” sense for the spelling 俊足. 秀 (“excel; outstanding”) and 英 (“English; outstanding”) are excluded from meaning choices because their displayed meanings overlap with this card’s idea of excellence.
