# 百 — hundred

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 白 (no. 106) · **Phonetic:** 白 (commonly explained) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

百 is commonly explained as [一](一.md) over 白 (white), where 白 gives the sound (Old Chinese pronunciations of the two are similar; modern Japanese on readings ハク and ヒャク still resemble each other) and 一 marks the number. Some sources analyse the shape differently, so the story of "one white = hundred" is only a mnemonic.
It means "100" and, figuratively, "many" (百科事典 ひゃっかじてん, encyclopedia; 百貨店 ひゃっかてん, department store).

## Notes

The sound of ヒャク changes after some numbers: 三百 さんびゃく (ビャク), 六百 ろっぴゃく and 八百 はっぴゃく (ピャク), 百 stays ひゃく after 二, 四, 五, 七, 九: 二百 にひゃく, 四百 よんひゃく, 五百 ごひゃく, 七百 ななひゃく, 九百 きゅうひゃく.
Look-alikes: [白](白.md) (white, without the top line) and [日](日.md) (sun).
