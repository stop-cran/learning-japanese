# 魅 — charm; bewitch

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 鬼 (no. 194), wrapping the left and lower side · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

魅 concerns captivating or bewitching attraction.
[魅力](../words/魅力.md) みりょく is charm or appeal: a person's
character, a place or a job can have it. It is not restricted to romance,
physical beauty or physical strength.

Daijisen also gives a supernatural spirit or monster sense. The modern
appeal vocabulary is therefore a useful focus, not the character's
only dictionary meaning.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the large 鬼 shape
beside [未](未.md). Imagine a mysterious figure whose attraction
you do not yet understand. This is an invented visual association,
not a verified historical explanation of either component.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ミ | on | [魅力](../words/魅力.md) みりょく - charm; appeal |

Practice selects ミ, also the Joyo reading. In 魅力, 力 supplies リョク.
The frozen KANJIDIC2 record supplies no kun reading. 魅する みする
is an attested special-class する verb, not a new native reading.

## Common words

- [魅力](../words/魅力.md) みりょく - charm; appeal
- 魅了 みりょう - captivation; 魅了する means to captivate
- 魅惑 みわく - fascination; enchantment
- 魅する みする - charm; fascinate

## Notes

- [未](未.md), not yet, is the right-hand shape.
  [妹](妹.md), younger sister, puts 女 beside 未.
  [末](末.md), end/tip, has the longer horizontal at the top,
  unlike 未. These are shape comparisons, not charm synonyms.
- [妖](妖.md) overlaps bewitching attraction and supernatural beings;
  [怪](怪.md), suspicious/uncanny, overlaps the supernatural sense.
  [符](符.md) also displays charm, but its talisman sense differs
  from personal appeal. These comparisons are excluded to avoid
  ambiguous English choices, not treated as unrestricted substitutions.
- In the linked word article, 魅力がある means to have appeal;
  魅力的な is an adjective expression. The character's association
  with attraction does not by itself determine a whole word's grammar.
