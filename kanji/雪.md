# 雪 — snow

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 雨 (no. 173) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

雪 is snow, both falling snow and the snow lying on the ground.
[雪](../words/雪.md) ゆき is the standalone noun.
雪が降っています。 (ゆきがふっています。) — It is snowing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture snow settling in the short horizontal layers beneath the 雨-shaped top.
This is a present-day visual memory aid, not an explanation of the lower component's historical meaning.

## Notes

[電](電.md) shares the 雨-shaped top but continues much farther downward.
[帰](帰.md) and [書](書.md) provide looser comparisons for the stacked horizontal strokes.
[雨](雨.md) あめ names rain, not snow; the shared radical does not make the two weather words interchangeable.
