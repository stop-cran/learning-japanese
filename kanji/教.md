# 教 — teach, instruct

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (right-side shape 攵, no. 66) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

教 concerns teaching and instruction; in some compounds it also concerns a doctrine or faith. The everyday verb 教える includes telling someone information, not just teaching a lesson.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice [子](子.md) within the left side and picture a child following a teacher's pointing gesture on the right. This is an invented memory scene, not a claim about the origin of 攵.

## Notes

教える views the exchange from the instructor's side; 教わる views it from the learner's side. The verb is not limited to school: a person can 教える a name or an address.

[考](考.md) has a similar upper-left outline, but lacks 子 and 攵. [校](校.md) is another school-related character with a different left-hand component, [木](木.md).
