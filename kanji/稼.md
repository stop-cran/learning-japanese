# 稼 — earn; work hard

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

稼 is working to earn a living. [稼ぐ](../words/稼ぐ.md) かせぐ has three JMdict senses: "to earn (income); to make (money)," "to score (points, victory); to gain (time); to play (for time)," and "to work hard (at one's job); to labor; to labour; to toil." The word article teaches "earn; make money" and also covers 時間を稼ぐ, "gaining time."

The Daijisen character entry gives 3 senses for カ. One example of each:

- planting grain: 稼穡 かしょく, JMdict "planting and harvesting; farming";
- harvested grain: 禾稼 かか;
- working and earning: 稼働 かどう, JMdict "working (and earning money); workload; activity," also "operation (of a machine); running."

Its kun reading is かせぐ. Daijisen also lists 稼業 and 稼動 under the third sense; 稼業 is glossed in the Readings section below.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 禾, the grain-plant key, at the left and [家](家.md), "house, home," at the right; 家 is only the modern visible part, and the card claims nothing about the older form. Imagine carrying the harvest home to earn a living. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 禾 or 家 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カ | on | 稼働 かどう — "working (and earning money); workload; activity"; JMdict lists 稼動 as another spelling; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| かせ.ぐ | kun | [稼ぐ](../words/稼ぐ.md) かせぐ — earn; make money |

In 稼ぐ, 稼 writes かせ and ぐ is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The noun forms 稼ぎ かせぎ, "earnings," and 出稼ぎ でかせぎ, "working away from home (esp. abroad)," use the same stem and have no word article here. In 稼働, 稼 supplies カ and 働 supplies ドウ. In 稼業 かぎょう, "trade; business; occupation; profession; job," 稼 supplies カ and 業 supplies ギョウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カ, with 稼業 and 稼働, and かせぐ, with 稼ぐ and 稼ぎ. Practice selects かせ.ぐ, the reading of the linked word article. カ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 稼ぐ is 稼 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji. In the other words named above, [働](働.md), "work; function," supplies ドウ in 稼働, [業](業.md), "work, occupation," supplies ギョウ in 稼業 and [出](出.md), "go out, exit," supplies で in 出稼ぎ.
- [働](働.md), [労](労.md), "labor; trouble," [業](業.md), [勤](勤.md), "employment; diligence," [努](努.md), "make an effort," [勉](勉.md), "strive, exert effort," and [励](励.md), "encourage; be diligent," overlap "work" and "work hard." [益](益.md), "benefit; profit; gain," [得](得.md), "gain; acquire," and [金](金.md), "gold, money," overlap "earn," "make (money)" and "gain." [勝](勝.md), "victory," overlaps "score (points, victory)." All are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [木](木.md), "tree, wood," [私](私.md), "I; private," and [秒](秒.md), "second as a time unit," look plausible because 木 resembles the 禾 shape, and 私 and 秒 are written with 禾 at the left. They are read ボク, モク and き; シ and わたくし; and ビョウ, and mean tree, I and second. None shares a reading or a meaning with 稼.
- Other kanji read カ, such as [家](家.md), [花](花.md), "flower," and [歌](歌.md), "song, sing," are never offered as wrong answers.
