# 負 — lose; bear a burden

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

負 has two useful starting points: losing a contest and carrying a burden.
[負ける](../words/負ける.md) まける is "to lose," but 負う おう means to bear or shoulder something,
including responsibility. The second meaning is not a kind of defeat.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the small angled cap carrying the large 貝 below it like a load.
This is a modern visual association, not an etymology.

## Notes

責任を負う (せきにんをおう) means "take responsibility"; see [責任](../words/責任.md).
For the contest meaning, [勝つ](../words/勝つ.md) かつ, "win," is the useful opposite.
[買](買.md), [貧](貧.md), and [賛](賛.md) share the lower 貝 but have different tops and meanings.
