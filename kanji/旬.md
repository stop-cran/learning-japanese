# 旬 — ten days; peak season

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72), inside 勹 · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

旬 counts days in tens. [上旬](../words/上旬.md) じょうじゅん is the first ten days of a month, [中旬](../words/中旬.md) ちゅうじゅん the middle ten days, [下旬](../words/下旬.md) げじゅん the last third and [初旬](../words/初旬.md) しょじゅん also the first ten days. The same character also writes the word 旬 しゅん, a peak season.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses: ten days, as in 旬刊 じゅんかん, "published every ten days," and in the words for the parts of a month; and ten years or ten months, as in 旬月 じゅんげつ, "month and ten days; ten months; short time." JMdict lists two separate words written 旬: じゅん, "ten-day period (in a month)," with a second sense, "ten-year period (in one's age); decade"; and しゅん, "season (for fruit, seafood, etc.); best time of the year (for)," with a second sense, "in vogue; popular; hot; fresh."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 勹, a shape that wraps around, with [日](日.md), "sun, day," inside it. Imagine a ten-day stretch wrapped up as one bundle of days. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 勹 or 日 contributes meaning or sound, although 日 does mean "day." Historical formation is not established here.

In 上旬, 上 supplies ジョウ; in 中旬, 中 supplies チュウ; in 下旬, 下 supplies ゲ; and in 初旬, 初 supplies ショ. 旬 stays ジュン in all four. The Daijisen character entry labels ジュン as 呉音, the older Chinese-derived (Go-on) reading, and シュン as 漢音, a later Chinese-derived reading.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジュン, with 旬刊 and 上旬 as examples, and シュン, with 旬 and 旬の野菜. Practice selects ジュン, the only reading with linked word articles; シュン is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [上](上.md), "up, above," in 上旬, [下](下.md), "down, below," in 下旬, [中](中.md), "middle, inside," in 中旬 and [初](初.md), "first time; initial," in 初旬.
- [日](日.md), "sun, day," is the key inside 勹. [句](句.md), "phrase; verse," has 口 inside the same 勹, and [旧](旧.md), "former; old," also has 日, beside a vertical stroke. [純](純.md), "pure; genuine," and [順](順.md), "order; sequence," are also read ジュン. All are different kanji.
- [季](季.md), "seasons," [期](期.md), "period; term," [十](十.md), "ten," [春](春.md), "spring season," [月](月.md), "moon, month," and [日](日.md), "sun, day," overlap the senses "season," "period," "ten," "month" and "day" that 旬 and its words carry; 春 is also read シュン. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "season" or "ten days" as exclusive to any one character.
