# 芋 — tuber; yam

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (as 艹, no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

芋 is useful for **いも**, a broad food word covering potatoes, taro, yams and similar underground crops. The exact kind depends on the whole word and context; 芋 does not identify one species or a strict botanical category in every use. See [芋](../words/芋.md).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 艹 over 于 and imagine leaves above a buried tuber. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical derivation or a claim that 于 contributes a verified sound here.

The canonical indexing radical is 艸; its visible top form in 芋 is 艹.

## Notes

- Do not translate every 芋 as "potato": the compounds above name different foods.
- [宇](宇.md), eaves or space, has 宀 instead of 艹 above 于. [干](干.md), dry or ebb, has neither the plant top nor the lower hook of 芋. [字](字.md), written character, has 宀 above 子.
- The ウ example is a specific plant name, not a pronunciation to substitute into ordinary いも food words. The reading questions select only いも.
- The word いも has derogatory and dismissive secondary senses; the linked article labels them rather than presenting them as neutral descriptions of people.
