# 形 — shape; form

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 彡 (no. 59) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

形 concerns what shape something has or what form it takes.
The noun 形 かたち can name a physical outline; [形式](../words/形式.md) けいしき concerns form or format,
including how information is arranged rather than what it says.
人形 にんぎょう is a doll or puppet, not simply any human body.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the three slanting strokes 彡 beside the squared-off left shape.
Imagine tracing an object's outline three times. This is an invented visual aid, not etymology.

## Notes

人形 uses ギョウ, not ケイ.
[開](開.md) and [研](研.md) contain a similar 開-without-the-gate shape, 开, but neither has 彡 on the right.
[新](新.md) is a looser comparison: its right-hand part is 斤, not three separate slanting strokes.
The radical 彡 is a reliable visual anchor for recognizing 形.
