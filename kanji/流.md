# 流 — flow

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

流 expresses flowing or a current. [流れる](../words/流れる.md) ながれる describes the flow itself;
流す (ながす) can mean letting something flow.
交流 (こうりゅう), exchange or interaction, gives a useful compound beyond literal water.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** follow the three descending strokes at the lower right like
separate streams. The water form 氵 on the left reinforces this visual reminder, not a historical claim.

## Notes

水が流れる (みずがながれる) means "water flows"; 水を流す (みずをながす) means
"let the water run." The が/を contrast helps keep the two verbs apart.
Do not confuse the three lower-right strokes with the two spreading legs of [洗](洗.md).
