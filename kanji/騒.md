# 騒 — make noise; uproar

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 馬 (no. 187), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

騒 is noise and commotion: making a racket, being in an uproar, or being restless and unsettled. [騒ぐ](../words/騒ぐ.md) さわぐ is to make noise or get agitated, [騒ぎ](../words/騒ぎ.md) さわぎ is an uproar or commotion, [騒がしい](../words/騒がしい.md) さわがしい is noisy or turbulent, [騒々しい](../words/騒々しい.md) そうぞうしい is noisy, boisterous or unsettled, [騒音](../words/騒音.md) そうおん is noise or din and [物騒](../words/物騒.md) ぶっそう is dangerous, unsafe or unsettled.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses: making noise or being noisy, as in 騒動 そうどう, "riot; rebellion; turmoil; strife," 騒然 そうぜん, "noisy; confused; uproarious," 狂騒 きょうそう, "mania; wild excitement," and 喧騒 けんそう, "tumult; great noise; clatter; hustle and bustle"; a form of Chinese verse, 騒体; and literature or refined taste, as in 騒人 そうじん, "poet." Its separate entry for 騒 as a literary term says that the verse form is named after 離騒, a poem by Qu Yuan that voices sorrow and indignation about society and politics.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [馬](馬.md), "horse," at the left and 又 above [虫](虫.md), "insect; bug," at the right. Imagine a horse pestered by an insect, stamping and kicking up a racket. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 馬 or 虫 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 騒音, 音 supplies オン. In 物騒, 物 supplies ブツ, which becomes ぶっ before ソウ. In 騒々しい, the repetition mark 々 repeats 騒, the second ソウ is voiced to ぞう, and しい is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 騒ぐ, 騒 writes さわ and ぐ is okurigana; 騒ぎ is the noun form with the okurigana ぎ. In 騒がしい the okurigana is がしい.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ソウ, with 騒動, 騒音 and 物騒 as examples, and さわぐ, with 騒ぐ, 騒ぎ and 騒がしい. The うれい reading is listed by KANJIDIC2 only: the Joyo table and the Daijisen character entry do not list it, and a search of JMdict found no word that reads 騒 as うれい, so it has no example here.

Practice selects ソウ, さわ.ぐ and さわ.がしい, the readings with linked word articles. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [音](音.md), "sound," in 騒音 and [物](物.md), "thing, object," in 物騒.
- 潮騒 しおさい, "roar of the sea; sound of waves," is listed by the Daijisen among its hard-to-read words. In this word 騒 is read さい, which is not one of the readings above. 潮 does not have a kanji card here.
- 馬 is the shape at the left. [験](験.md), "test; verify," and [駐](駐.md), "station; remain," share it, and [掃](掃.md), "sweep; brush," is also read ソウ. All are different kanji.
- [乱](乱.md), "disorder; disturbance," [叫](叫.md), "shout; exclaim," [音](音.md), "sound," [鳴](鳴.md), "animal call; ring," and [激](激.md), "intense; excite," overlap the senses "disturb," "clamor," "make noise" and "excite" that KANJIDIC2 gives for 騒. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "noise" or "disturbance" as exclusive to any one character.
