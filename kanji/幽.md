# 幽 — secluded; faint; confine

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 幺 (no. 52) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

幽 can suggest seclusion, darkness, or something faint. 幽閉 describes a person kept shut away; the usually kana-written かすか can describe a sound barely heard.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for two small thread-like shapes on either side of the central upright, inside the outline at the bottom.

## Notes

Compare the lower outline with [凶](凶.md), [出](出.md) and [山](山.md). [隠](隠.md) suggests hiding; [暗](暗.md), [深](深.md) and [微](微.md) overlap in senses of dark, deep and faint. [淡](淡.md) can also mean faint, as in a pale color or a vague outline. [閉](閉.md) means shut, and [囚](囚.md) also involves confinement.
