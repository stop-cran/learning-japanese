# 利 — benefit; advantage

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (as 刂, no. 18) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

利 points to benefit, advantage, or usefulness. In [便利](../words/便利.md) べんり, it helps express convenience;
利益 りえき can mean profit or benefit.
The adjective pair 有利 ゆうり and 不利 ふり contrasts an advantageous and a disadvantageous position.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the grain-shaped 禾 beside a harvesting blade 刂, yielding a benefit.
This is a modern memory image; no historical derivation or phonetic component is established here.

## Notes

便利な道具 (べんりなどうぐ) means "a handy tool"; convenience does not necessarily mean financial profit.
For きく, JMdict distinguishes the usual 利く spelling for bodily senses and functions from 効く for taking effect.
[私](私.md) and [秋](秋.md) share 禾; [列](列.md) instead shares the narrow blade on the right.
