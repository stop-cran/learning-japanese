# 崎 — cape; promontory

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

崎 can denote a cape, land projecting into water. In 長崎 (ながさき) it appears in a place name; knowing the character does not tell you where a particular cape is.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 山 stands to the left of 奇. Imagine the projecting piece of land at the edge of the mountain.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キ | on | 崎嶇 (きく), steep or precipitous; the word is rare, and キ is outside the standard-use table. |
| さき | kun | 崎 (さき), a cape or headland. It is also the final part of 長崎 (ながさき), Nagasaki. |
| さい | kun | source-only here: KANJIDIC2 lists さい. It is not the ending of 長崎, and this card does not offer it for practice. |
| みさき | kun | [岬](../words/岬.md) (みさき), cape or headland, can also be written 崎. This reading is outside the standard-use table for 崎. |

## Common words

- 崎 (さき) — cape; headland. The same reading ends 長崎 (ながさき), Nagasaki.
- [岬](../words/岬.md) (みさき) — cape; headland. Recognize 崎 as an alternate spelling of this word.

## Notes

[奇](奇.md) has no 山 on its left, [寄](寄.md) has a roof above 奇, and [岩](岩.md) puts 山 above 石, not next to 奇. [岬](岬.md) is the spelling on the linked word card. Both 岬 and 崎 can write みさき; the quiz uses that word, not a guessed pronunciation of a place name.
