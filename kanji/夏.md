# 夏 — summer

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 夊 (no. 35) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

夏 means summer. The ordinary independent word is [夏](../words/夏.md) なつ; compounds such as 初夏 name a part of the season.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the central [目](目.md)-like shape as an eye shaded by the top bar, with the lower strokes stepping outdoors into summer. This is a visual memory aid, not an origin claim.

## Readings

The useful readings below are selective; metadata also retains ガ and ゲ.

## Common words

- [夏](../words/夏.md) なつ — summer
- 夏休み なつやすみ — summer vacation
- 初夏 しょか — early summer

## Notes

The seasonal sequence is [春](春.md) はる, 夏 なつ, [秋](秋.md) あき, [冬](冬.md) ふゆ. 夏 names a season, not the adjective "hot."

Learn なつ for the ordinary word rather than choosing an on-reading from the metadata. The classical radical is the lower 夊, not the central eye-like shape.
