# 値 — value; price

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

値 concerns what something costs or what value it has. [値段](../words/値段.md) ねだん is a price,
whereas 価値 かち can be worth or merit without a price tag.
The noun 値 あたい also names a value in mathematics or computing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** a person-shaped 亻 stands beside [直](直.md), checking a value carefully.
This association uses the visible modern parts; it is not an account of how the character developed.

## Notes

Keep the readings attached to their words: the 値 in 値段 is ね, not チ.
The lower outline of 直 helps distinguish 値 from the boxed shape in [真](真.md).
[住](住.md) and [体](体.md) share the person radical but not the right-hand component.
