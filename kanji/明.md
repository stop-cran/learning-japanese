# 明 — bright, clear

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

明 expresses brightness and, by extension, clarity. [明るい](../words/明るい.md) can describe a well-lit room or a cheerful person.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** combine the light of [日](日.md), the sun, and [月](月.md), the moon, to remember "bright." This explains how to remember the modern shape, not its verified historical formation.

## Common words

- [明るい](../words/明るい.md) あかるい — bright, cheerful
- 説明 せつめい — explanation
- 夜明け よあけ — dawn, daybreak
- 明日 あした — tomorrow; a whole-word reading to learn separately

## Notes

Do not divide あした into ordinary readings of 明 and 日. It is not a model for reading other compounds.
[映](映.md) also has 日 on the left, but its right side is 央, not 月. [朝](朝.md) has 月 on the right but a more complex left side.
