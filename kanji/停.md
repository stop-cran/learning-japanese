# 停 — halt; stopping

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

停 concerns stopping or a halt. [停止](../words/停止.md) ていし is a stoppage, [停電](../words/停電.md) ていでん is a power outage, and [停留所](../words/停留所.md) ていりゅうじょ is a stopping place such as a bus stop.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the person-shaped 亻 stopping beside the tall 亭 block, with its roof-like top and long lower hook.

The canonical radical 人 appears as 亻 on the left.

## Notes

- 停める とめる is transitive; 停まる とまる is intransitive. Compare 車を停める くるまをとめる, stop a car, with 車が停まる くるまがとまる, a car comes to a stop.
- The broader stopping verbs also use [止める](../words/止める.md) and [止まる](../words/止まる.md). The recognized 停 spellings do not license replacing 止 in every expression meaning stop.
