# 結 — tie; join; conclude

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), compressed on the left · **JLPT:** N2 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

結 concerns **tying, joining or bringing something to a conclusion**. [結ぶ](../words/結ぶ.md) むすぶ covers physical tying and wider connections; [結婚](../words/結婚.md) けっこん is marriage, and [結果](../words/結果.md) けっか is a result.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the thread radical beside 吉. Imagine threads tied into a firm connection. This is a modern memory aid, not a historical account.

## Notes

- Learn けっこん and けっか with the small っ. They illustrate the source on-reading ケツ in compounds, not a new independent reading ケ.
- The native verbs have different endings: 結ぶ is a godan verb, 結う is a godan verb ending in う, and 結わえる is an ichidan verb. Their tying meanings overlap, but not every extended use of 結ぶ transfers to the others.
- A 結果 can be good or bad; the kanji does not mean success.
- [絵](絵.md), drawing, [緑](緑.md), green, and [線](線.md), line, share the thread radical but have different right-hand shapes.
