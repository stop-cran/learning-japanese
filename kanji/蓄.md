# 蓄 — store up; amass

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

蓄 means putting things aside and building up a store. KANJIDIC2 gives “amass,” “hoard,” and “store,” and Digital Daijisen explains it as gathering many things in one place and keeping them there (多くのものを一所に集めておく). [蓄える](../words/蓄える.md) たくわえる is glossed in JMdict as “to store; to save up; to stock up on,” and also as “to accumulate (e.g. knowledge); to build up (e.g. experience).”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the grass crown 艹, also at the top of [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," sits above [畜](畜.md), "livestock; domestic animals." Imagine piling up hay before winter so the livestock have a store of grass to eat. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 艹 or 畜 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists チク and たくわえる, including the noun 蓄え. KANJIDIC2 gives the same readings and no name readings.
- JMdict lists 貯える as another spelling of 蓄える.

These five cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [貯](貯.md) (“save up; store”): JMdict glosses 蓄える as “to store; to save up” and lists 貯える as another spelling of the same verb.
- [収](収.md) (“collect; store”) and [蔵](蔵.md) (“storehouse; keep in storage”): KANJIDIC2 lists “store” for 蓄, which overlaps “store” and “storage.”
- [積](積.md) (“pile up”): JMdict glosses 蓄える as “to accumulate (e.g. knowledge); to build up (e.g. experience),” which overlaps piling up.
- [集](集.md) (“gather”): Digital Daijisen explains 蓄 as gathering many things in one place (一所に集めて) and keeping them, and KANJIDIC2 lists “amass.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 使 (“use, employ”), 売 (“sell”), and 借 (“borrow, rent”); none means storing up or amassing.
