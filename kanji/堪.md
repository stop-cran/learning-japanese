# 堪 — endure; withstand

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

堪 can express bearing pressure or difficulty, withstanding a condition, or being worthy of something. In the formal pattern 鑑賞に堪える it means “be worthy of appreciation.” The selected examples distinguish [たえる](../words/たえる.md), which can describe an external condition, from a person's restraint with [こらえる](../words/こらえる.md).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine soil holding firm under weight. This is a memory aid only; it makes no claim about the character's historical formation or sound.

## Notes

- Kanjipedia marks タン, こらえる, こたえる and たまる outside the official reading table; カン is marked for high-school study. They are preserved in the reading inventory but not all selected for questions.
- Digital Daijisen distinguishes useful patterns: たえる centers on resisting external pressure and can describe objects; こらえる often centers on a person's endurance or suppression of a feeling. The distinction is contextual, not a rule that the verbs are interchangeable in every phrase.
- The card selects the two directly supported kun readings with linked examples. Other KANJIDIC2 readings remain recorded but are not selected.
- 堪 and [凌](凌.md), “endure; surpass,” and [抵](抵.md), “resist; collide,” overlap in resistance or endurance meanings; those direct ambiguities are excluded from meaning choices.
