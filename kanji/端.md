# 端 — edge; end; tip

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 立 (no. 117), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

端 is the outer limit of something: its edge, its end or its tip, and by extension the point where something starts. [端](../words/端.md) はし is an edge, end or tip, [先端](../words/先端.md) せんたん is a tip or leading edge and [途端](../words/途端.md) とたん is the very moment something happens, "just as." JMdict glosses the noun 端 はし "end (e.g. of street); tip; point; edge; margin" and, in a second sense, "beginning; start; first."

The Daijisen character entry gives five senses for タン. One example of each:

- being neat and properly set: 端正 たんせい, "decent; proper; correct; upright";
- an edge or end: 末端 まったん, "end; tip; extremities," besides 先端;
- a beginning or clue: 発端 ほったん, "origin; genesis; opening; start," besides 途端;
- matters or affairs: 万端 ばんたん, "all; everything";
- plainly, as it is: 端的 たんてき, "frank; direct; plain; straightforward."

The entry then groups words by kun reading: はし (also ばし or ぱし), as in 片端 かたはし, "one edge; one end; one side"; は (also ば or ぱ), as in 端数 はすう, "fraction; odd amount; leftover amount," 軒端 のきば, "eaves; edge of the eaves," and 半端 はんぱ, "remnant; fragment; incomplete set"; and はた (also ばた), as in 道端 みちばた, "roadside; wayside." The kun list also includes はした, which has no word group of its own; KANJIDIC2 does not list はした, and JMdict has the noun 端 はした, "fraction; odd sum." More Daijisen examples of the タン senses are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [立](立.md), "stand; set up," at the left, and at the right a shape with [山](山.md), "mountain," on top. Imagine standing at the very edge of a mountain top, at the end of the path. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 立 or 山 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| タン | on | [先端](../words/先端.md) せんたん — tip; leading edge; [途端](../words/途端.md) とたん — the moment; just as |
| はし | kun | [端](../words/端.md) はし — edge; end; tip |
| は | kun | 端数 はすう - fraction; odd amount; leftover amount; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| はた | kun | 端から はたから - from outside; from the side (usually written in kana); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| -ばた | kun suffix | 道端 みちばた - roadside; wayside; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| はな | kun | 端から はなから - from the start; from the beginning; from the outset (usually written in kana); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 先端, 先 supplies セン and 端 supplies タン. In 途端, 途 supplies ト and 端 again supplies タン. The word 端 はし is the kanji alone, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict's entry for it also records はじ and はな, but the word article teaches はし. The leading dash in -ばた marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced. There はた is voiced to ばた (rendaku), as in 道端 and 川端 かわばた, "riverside; riverbank." The は reading changes to ぱ after ん in 半端 はんぱ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows タン, with 端正, 末端 and 極端 きょくたん, "extreme; extremity," as examples; はし, with 端 and 片端; は, with 端数, 半端 and 軒端; and はた, with 端, 川端 and 道端. はな is not in the official table, and -ばた has no separate row. Practice selects タン and はし, the readings with linked word articles; は, はた, -ばた and はな are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [先](先.md), "previous; ahead (in time)," in 先端 and [途](途.md), "route; course," in 途端. The word 端 contains only this kanji.
- [新](新.md), "new," also has 立 at the upper left, above 木. [山](山.md), "mountain," is the top of this card's right-hand part, and [短](短.md), "short," is also read タン. All are different kanji.
- [末](末.md), "end; tip," [終](終.md), "end; finish," [際](際.md), "edge; occasion," [辺](辺.md), "vicinity; edge," [境](境.md), "boundary; border," [刃](刃.md), "blade; cutting edge," [隅](隅.md), "corner; nook," [梢](梢.md), "treetop; branch tip," and [極](極.md), "extreme; pole," overlap the senses "edge," "end," "tip," "border" and "extreme" that 端 and its words carry; 末 and 極 also appear with 端 in 末端 and 極端. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "edge," "end" or "tip" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the タン senses: 異端 いたん, "heresy; nonconformism"; 両端 りょうたん, "both ends; either end; both edges"; 端緒 たんしょ, "start; beginning; first step; clue"; and 多端 たたん, "many items; pressure of business."
