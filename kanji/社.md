# 社 — company, shrine

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 示 (as 礻, no. 113) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

社 combines 礻 (altar, spirits and ritual) with 土 (earth, [土](土.md)): the original idea is an altar for the god of the land, hence a shrine and, later, a gathering of people around it.
This extended into "association" and "company", which is why 社 is used in 会社, 社会 and 神社.
The step from "shrine" to "company" is the usual explanation; the details of the earliest usage are not important for learners.

## Notes

Compare 礼 (れい, bow), which has the same left part 礻 but 乚 on the right: 社 has 土. Also do not confuse 社 with 神 (かみ, god), which has 申 on the right and shares the key.
The kun reading やしろ is rare in everyday life: a shrine is normally 神社 or お宮 おみや.
