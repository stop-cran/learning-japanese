# 架 — rack; bridge; overhead; imaginary

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

架 can be a rack or shelf, and it can mean spanning or building something across a gap. The linked word [架空](../words/架空.md) かくう has two distinct senses: JMdict glosses the first “fictitious; imaginary” and the second “aerial; overhead.” 架橋 かきょう names bridge-building and can also refer to the bridge. JMdict lists the standalone noun 架 か as a rack, mount or stand.

Digital Daijisen gives two senses for カ: a supported platform or shelf, as in 架 か; and spanning something overhead, as in 架橋 かきょう.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [加](加.md) above [木](木.md). Imagine a wooden rack carrying a load across a gap. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 加 or 木 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カ | on | [架空](../words/架空.md) かくう — fictitious; imaginary; fanciful; fabricated; aerial; overhead |
| か.ける | kun | 架ける かける — to suspend between two points; to build a bridge; to put up on something; JMdict says this spelling is usually written using kana alone and sometimes as 掛ける; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| か.かる | kun | 架かる かかる — to span; to bridge; to cross; to straddle; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 架空, 架 supplies カ and 空 supplies クウ. In 架ける, 架 writes か and ける is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 架かる similarly uses か followed by かる. JMdict also lists がくう for the overhead sense of 架空; its fictitious sense is restricted to かくう.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カ with 架橋, 架空 and 書架; かける with 架ける ⇔ 掛ける, 懸ける, 賭ける; and かかる with 架かる ⇔ 掛かる, 懸かる. Here 架橋 かきょう means bridge-building or a bridge; 架空 かくう means fictitious or overhead; and 書架 しょか means bookshelf or bookcase. 架ける かける can suspend something between two points or build a bridge; 掛ける and 懸ける かける can mean to hang or suspend; 賭ける かける means to wager, bet, risk or stake. 架かる かかる means to span or bridge; 掛かる and 懸かる かかる also have a “to hang” sense. JMdict marks 掛ける/懸ける and 掛かる/懸かる as words usually written using kana alone, and labels 懸かる as a rarely used kanji form. Practice selects カ, the reading used by the linked word article. The kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; being outside the practice set does not make them invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in 架空 is [空](空.md), “sky; empty.”
- [木](木.md) is the bottom of 架. [末](末.md) and [未](未.md) have similar tree-like strokes but differ in the placement and length of their horizontal strokes. All are different kanji.
- [橋](橋.md), “bridge,” directly overlaps 架’s bridge sense; [掛](掛.md), [懸](懸.md) and [垂](垂.md) overlap hanging or suspension; [渡](渡.md) and [越](越.md) overlap crossing; [建](建.md), [築](築.md) and [構](構.md) overlap construction; [空](空.md) overlaps the imaginary and overhead senses in 架空; and [額](額.md), [台](台.md) and [支](支.md) overlap frame, stand and support. They are kept out of this card’s quiz options. Do not treat “bridge,” “span” or “support” as exclusive to any one character.
