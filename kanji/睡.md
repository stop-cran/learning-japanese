# 睡 — sleep; doze

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

睡 is sleep, including a light sleep or a doze. [睡眠](../words/睡眠.md) すいみん is "sleep," the noun used for the amount or quality of sleep. Other words show the range: 熟睡 じゅくすい is "deep sleep; sound sleep; profound sleep," 午睡 ごすい is "nap; siesta," and 仮睡 かすい is "nap; doze."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for スイ, sleep, as in 睡魔 すいま, JMdict "sleepiness; drowsiness" and "the sandman; Morpheus," 睡眠 and 熟睡. It lists the kun readings ねむる and ねむり, glossed as 眠る (to sleep) and 眠り (sleep). Its other examples include 昏睡 こんすい, 半睡 はんすい and 微睡; 微睡む まどろむ, "to doze (off)," is a Daijisen hard-reading whole word and is not split into per-kanji readings.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [目](目.md), "eye," at the left and [垂](垂.md), "hang down; droop; dangle," at the right. Imagine an eye drooping closed as someone dozes off. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 目 or 垂 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| スイ | on | [睡眠](../words/睡眠.md) すいみん — sleep |
| ねむ.る | kun | 睡る ねむる — "to sleep"; a written form of 眠る, no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ねむ.い | kun | 睡い ねむい — "sleepy; drowsy; somnolent"; a written form of 眠い, no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 睡眠, 睡 supplies スイ and 眠 supplies ミン. In 熟睡, 熟 supplies ジュク and 睡 supplies スイ; in 午睡, 午 supplies ゴ and 睡 supplies スイ. 昏睡, "coma" and "deep sleep; falling fast asleep," has no word article here. For the kun readings, JMdict lists 眠る ねむる as the main spelling, with 睡る as another written form of it. JMdict also lists 眠い ねむい with 睡い as another written form, and 眠り ねむり, "sleep; sleeping," with 睡り as a "rarely used kanji form." The ordinary writing is with 眠, so these 睡 spellings are not the forms to learn first.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows スイ, with 睡眠, 熟睡 and 午睡, and no kun readings. Practice selects スイ, the reading of the linked word article. ねむ.る and ねむ.い are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [眠](眠.md), "sleep; sleepy," in 睡眠. 眠 supplies ミン and is also read ねむ.る and ねむ.い, like 睡. Its title overlaps this card's "sleep," so it is in this card's quiz exclusions. [寝](寝.md), "lie down; sleep," also overlaps and is excluded. Neither is offered as a wrong answer.
- In the other words named above, [熟](熟.md) supplies ジュク in 熟睡 and [午](午.md), "noon," supplies ゴ in 午睡. 熟 is a member of the same batch as 睡.
- [真](真.md), "true, genuine," [着](着.md), "wear; arrive," and [直](直.md), "direct; repair," each contain a 目 shape, as 睡 does, so they look plausible. They are read シン and ま; チャク, ジャク and き.る; and チョク, ジキ and ただ.ちに, and mean true, wear and direct. None shares a reading or a meaning with 睡.
- Other kanji read スイ, such as [水](水.md), "water," and [垂](垂.md), are never offered as wrong answers.
