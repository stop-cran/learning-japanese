# 憤 — indignation; anger

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

憤 is anger or indignation. It appears in 憤慨, in 憤り (いきどおり), “resentment; indignation,” and in 義憤 (ぎふん), “righteous indignation.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 忄, the narrow form of 心 “heart,” is on the left; the right part is the same as in [噴](噴.md), “erupt,” and [墳](墳.md), “burial mound,” which are also read フン.

## Notes

[噴](噴.md) and [墳](墳.md) share the right part and the reading フン, but mean erupt and burial mound; [慣](慣.md) also starts with 忄 and means accustomed. [怒](怒.md) is anger, [恨](恨.md) resentment and [憎](憎.md) hatred; [激](激.md) is intensity, as in 憤激 (ふんげき), “fury; outrage.” 発憤 (はっぷん), “being spurred on,” uses 憤 in the sense “rouse oneself”; JMdict lists it as a second spelling of 発奮. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 136](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=136) lists フン, with 憤慨, 義憤 and 発憤, and いきどおる, with 憤る and 憤り.
