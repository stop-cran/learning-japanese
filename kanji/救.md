# 救 — rescue; save; relief

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (as 攵, no. 66), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

救 is getting someone out of danger or trouble: rescuing, saving and bringing relief. [救う](../words/救う.md) すくう means to rescue or save, and [救助](../words/救助.md) きゅうじょ is rescue or relief. JMdict's entry for 救う lists "to rescue (someone); to save; to help (out of a difficulty); to relieve (from)" and, as a second sense, saving someone spiritually: "to save (spiritually); to redeem (from sin)."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense, rescuing someone from danger or disaster, with キュウ words such as 救援 きゅうえん, "relief; rescue," 救急 きゅうきゅう, "first-aid; emergency (aid)," 救護 きゅうご, "relief; aid," 救済 きゅうさい, "relief; aid; help; rescue," 救出 きゅうしゅつ, "rescue; extricate; reclaim; deliverance," 救難 きゅうなん, "rescue; salvage," 救命 きゅうめい, "lifesaving," and 救助. It also lists ク, which it marks as the older Chinese-derived (Go-on) reading; JMdict has a separate entry for 救済 read くさい or ぐさい, "salvation (from suffering based on Buddha's teachings)." KANJIDIC2 does not list ク, so it is not part of the metadata here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [求](求.md), "seek; request," at the left, and 攵, the side form of the key 攴, at the right; 攴 has no card yet. Imagine someone at the left calling out for help and a rescuer at the right striding in to answer. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 求 or 攵 contributes meaning or sound, although 救 and 求 are both read キュウ. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キュウ | on | [救助](../words/救助.md) きゅうじょ — rescue; relief |
| すく.う | kun | [救う](../words/救う.md) すくう — rescue; save |

In 救助, 救 supplies キュウ and 助 supplies ジョ. In 救う, 救 writes すく and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the noun 救い すくい, "help; rescue; aid; relief," has い instead.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キュウ, with 救助, 救援 and 救急 as examples, and すくう, with 救う and 救い. ク, from the Daijisen, is not in the official table. Practice selects both metadata readings, キュウ and すく.う, each of which has a linked word article.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [助](助.md), "help; rescue," in 救助. 救う is 救 with okurigana only.
- [求](求.md), "seek; request," is the shape at the left of 救 and is also read キュウ; [球](球.md), "ball; sphere," has the same 求 at the right and is also read キュウ; and [教](教.md), "teach, instruct," has the same 攵 at the right and the same 11 strokes. All are different kanji.
- [助](助.md), "help; rescue," [丞](丞.md), "assist; aid," [援](援.md), "aid; assistance," [貯](貯.md), "save up; store," and [保](保.md), "protect; preserve," overlap the senses "rescue," "aid," "save" and "protect" that 救 and its words carry; 助 appears in 救助, 丞 also has the kun reading すく.う, 援 appears in 救援, and English "save" can also mean to store up or to preserve. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "rescue" or "save" as exclusive to any one character.
