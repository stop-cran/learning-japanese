# 裕 — abundant; rich; fertile

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145), appearing as left-hand 衤 · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

裕 describes plenty or roominess, including material prosperity. 裕福 (ゆうふく) means well-off, 富裕 (ふゆう) means wealthy, and 余裕 (よゆう) means spare capacity or leeway.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine 谷 to the right of 衤 as an open space with room to spare. This memory aid follows the present left-right outline, not the character's historical formation.

## Notes

- The indexing radical is 衣, shown at the left as 衤, not 礻.
- [厚](厚.md) has the ordinary spelling 厚い あつい with a dictionary sense "abundant." Although its established card title is "thick; cordial," this ordinary secondary sense makes 厚 a plausible abundance answer for 裕, so the pair is excluded.
- [富](富.md) and [豊](豊.md) also express wealth or abundance; [財](財.md) refers to wealth, property, or assets. Their English labels can overlap with 裕, so these direct pairs are excluded from meaning-quiz choices.
- [多](多.md) can answer the broad quantity sense of "abundant," [楽](楽.md) has an economically comfortable sense, and [広](広.md) matches the roominess/spaciousness sense taught here. These direct pairs are excluded from meaning-quiz choices; none of the guards changes reading metadata.
- Sources record wealth glosses for [福](福.md)/[宝](宝.md), prosperity for [栄](栄.md), and assets/resources for [資](資.md); selected meanings of [余](余.md)/[剰](剰.md) concern surplus, [満](満.md) fullness, and [悠](悠.md) ease/leisure, while JMdict's rich flavor/color applies to whole-word 濃厚 ([濃](濃.md)). These direct quiz exclusions conservatively address broad English-choice ambiguity; they do not assert synonymy or transfer compound senses to characters. In particular, 裕 is not taught as rich-flavored.
- [容](容.md), contain or appearance, concerns what holds or looks a certain way; [補](補.md), supplement or make good, supplies what is missing; [複](複.md), multiple or compound, concerns multiplicity or structure. None means abundant, rich, or fertile.
- Do not treat 裕に as an ordinary spelling of 優に (ゆうに): JMdict marks it as irregular kanji usage. 裕度 is a specialized electrical term, not a general reading example.
