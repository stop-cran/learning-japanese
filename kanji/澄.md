# 澄 — clear; become transparent; serene

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

澄 is clearness: water or air with nothing murky in it, a voice that rings clearly, a mind that is calm. The linked [澄む](../words/澄む.md) すむ has four JMdict senses: "to become clear (water, air, etc.); to become transparent," "to resonate clearly (e.g. voice)," "to become serene; to become tranquil; to be free of worries," and "to pronounce as an unvoiced sound." Its spellings are 澄む and 清む.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for チョウ: clear and serene, as in 澄明 ちょうめい, JMdict "clear and bright," and 清澄 せいちょう, JMdict "clear; serene." For the kun side it lists すむ and すます.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part is a large shape beside it. Imagine water in a glass settling until you can see to the bottom. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チョウ | on | 清澄 せいちょう — clear; serene; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| す.む | kun | [澄む](../words/澄む.md) すむ — become clear; resonate clearly |
| す.ます | kun | 澄ます すます — "to clear; to make clear"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| -す.ます | kun | 聞き澄ます ききすます — "to listen attentively"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 澄む, 澄 writes す and む is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the verb is a godan verb, so 澄みます, 澄んで. 澄ます is transitive. Its senses are "to clear; to make clear," "to be unruffled; to look unconcerned; to feign indifference," "to look demure; to look prim; to put on airs," and "to strain (one's ears); to listen carefully," and JMdict lists 清ます as another spelling. In the suffix use, 研ぎ澄ます とぎすます, "to sharpen; to grind; to whet; to hone; to make keen," and 聞き澄ます attach it to another verb stem. In 清澄, 澄明 and 明澄 めいちょう, "lucid; clear," 澄 supplies チョウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows チョウ with 清澄, すむ with 澄む and 上澄み, and すます with 澄ます and 澄まし顔. 上澄み うわずみ is JMdict "clear layer of fluid (at the top of a mixture); supernatant liquid; supernate," with 上澄 as "irregular okurigana usage," and 澄まし顔 すましがお is "straight face; composed expression; nonchalant look." Practice selects す.む, the reading of the linked word article. The other readings are shown with dictionary examples and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. Name readings such as きよ and すみ are not taught here.

## Notes

- 澄む is 澄 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji. In the examples above, [清](清.md), "pure, clean," supplies セイ in 清澄, [明](明.md), "bright, clear," supplies メイ in 明澄, [聞](聞.md), "hear, ask," supplies き in 聞き澄ます, [研](研.md), "polish; study closely," supplies と in 研ぎ澄ます, [上](上.md), "up, above," supplies うわ in 上澄み, and [顔](顔.md), "face; expression," supplies がお in 澄まし顔, its かお with a voiced sound.
- [海](海.md), "sea, ocean," has 氵 at the left, and [水](水.md), "water," and [川](川.md), "river," are also water kanji. They are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [清](清.md), [透](透.md), "transparent; see through; permeate," [明](明.md), [晴](晴.md), "clear skies," [朗](朗.md), "cheerful; clear; resonant," and [冴](冴.md), "clear; sharp-minded," overlap clarity. The 澄む word article also notes that 清む is a spelling of すむ. [静](静.md), "quiet," overlaps the serene sense, [響](響.md), "resound; echo," overlaps a clear-ringing sound. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. [住](住.md) and [済](済.md) also read す.む, and 済 reads す.ます too, so they are never offered as wrong answers.
