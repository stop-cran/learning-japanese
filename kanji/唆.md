# 唆 — instigate; entice

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

唆 means to instigate or entice. The whole compound [示唆](../words/示唆.md) means “hint; implication”; that meaning is not a literal sum of “instigate.”

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| サ | on | [示唆](../words/示唆.md) しさ — suggestion; hint |
| そそ.る | kun | 唆る そそる is a rarely used spelling; the word is usually written in kana |
| そそのか.す | kun | 唆す そそのかす — instigate; tempt; usually written in kana |
