# 隅 — corner; nook

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

隅 concerns a corner, nook or recess. 隅 names a corner, while 一隅 can mean a corner or nook rather than a complete room.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the right-hand 禺-shaped block tucked into a corner beside the left 阝. This is a modern-shape association, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 阜, no. 170, shown as left-hand 阝.
