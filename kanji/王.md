# 王 — king

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 玉 (王 form, no. 96) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[王](../words/王.md) おう means a king or sovereign. The same character helps name both the ruler and a kingdom, but the complete words are different.
The dictionary indexes 王 under 玉, the jade radical. The visible character here is 王, without 玉's dot.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine three horizontal levels joined by one royal staff.
This is a way to remember four strokes, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

王は城に住んでいます。 (おうはしろにすんでいます。) — The king lives in a castle.

Count three horizontal strokes: [土](土.md) has two, and [工](工.md) has a different central arrangement.
[生](生.md) adds a slanting stroke at the upper left. These small differences change the character.
