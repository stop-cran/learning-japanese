# 酢 — vinegar

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 酉 (no. 164) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

酢 is vinegar. KANJIDIC2 lists “vinegar”, “sour”, “acid” and “tart”. Kanjipedia's first sense is す, a sour liquid seasoning; its second is すい。すっぱい。 (sour), and its third is about repaying, where a guest returns the cup to the host. Digital Daijisen gives a sour liquid, す, and a guest returning the cup. On its own, [酢](../words/酢.md) す is JMdict “vinegar”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [酉](酉.md), "Rooster zodiac sign," stands on the left; picture it as a squat jar. On the right is the shape 乍, all sharp corners. Picture one sip from the jar so sour that your face goes as jagged as the shape beside it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 酉 or 乍 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists サク, with 酢酸, and す, with 酢 and 酢の物 すのもの (“vinegared dish; pickled dish”, JMdict entry 1370280). Digital Daijisen gives す（ず） with examples such as 甘酢 あまず (“sweet vinegar”, JMdict entry 1213540), where the whole word's ず corresponds to す ([compound sound changes](../articles/compound-sound-changes.md)).
- Kanjipedia also lists ソ and すい outside the table (外); すい goes with its second sense. They are not KANJIDIC2 readings and have no table rows here.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 酢, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These four cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [酸](酸.md) (“acid; sour”): KANJIDIC2 lists “acid”, “sour” and “tart” for both 酢 and 酸; JMdict entry 1370270 lists 酸 as a rarely used kanji form of す (vinegar), and Digital Daijisen's headword is す【酢／×醋／▽酸】.
- [返](返.md) (“give back; repay”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 酢 describes a guest returning the cup to the host, Digital Daijisen gives 客が返杯する (a guest returns the cup), and JMdict glosses 返杯 へんぱい “offering a cup (of sake) in return” (entry 1680480).
- [賞](賞.md) (“prize; reward”) and [償](償.md) (“recompense; make amends”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 酢 begins むくいる (repay), and JMdict 報いる むくいる sense 1 is “to reward; to recompense; to repay” (entry 1515630); KANJIDIC2 gives 償 “recompense”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 塩 (“salt”), 麦 (“wheat; barley”), and 油 (“oil; grease”); none means vinegar.
