# 柄 — handle; pattern; nature

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

柄 names a handle or grip and, in a separate native-reading identity, a pattern or design. The linked [柄](../words/柄.md) article distinguishes え, “handle; grip,” from がら, “pattern; design.” The がら entry also covers body build, figure, physique and essential qualities or nature. The readings are not interchangeable: え is the handle, while がら is the pattern or character.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ヘイ. One example of each:

- the part of an object held by hand: 葉柄 ようへい, leaf stalk or petiole;
- power held in someone's hand: 権柄 けんぺい, power or authority;
- something taken as a topic: 話柄 わへい, topic or subject.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md) at the left and [丙](丙.md) at the right. Imagine a wooden handle attached to another part. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 木 or 丙 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| つか | kun | 柄 つか — hilt (of a sword); haft (of a dagger); handle; handgrip; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

The linked article explicitly treats 柄 え and 柄 がら as separate lexical identities, not interchangeable readings of one meaning. 柄 つか is another identity for a hilt or haft. 柄杓 ひしゃく is a whole-word reading; its pronunciation is not split between its kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ヘイ with 横柄 and 権柄ずく; がら with 柄, 家柄 and 身柄; and え with 柄. 横柄 おうへい means arrogant, haughty or insolent; 権柄ずく けんぺいずく means overbearing, imperious, domineering or peremptory; 家柄 いえがら means a family's social standing or lineage; and 身柄 みがら means one's person. Practice selects がら and え, the two readings explained by the linked article. ヘイ and つか are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; being outside the practice set does not make them invalid.

## Notes

- The common-word article is for the single character 柄, so it has no other kanji.
- [木](木.md) is the left component of 柄. [本](本.md) contains 木 with an added stroke and [休](休.md) has 木 at the right. All are different kanji.
- [模](模.md), [図](図.md) and [絵](絵.md) overlap the pattern/design sense; [扱](扱.md), [善](善.md), [握](握.md) and [操](操.md) overlap handle, handling or gripping; [性](性.md) and [格](格.md) overlap nature and character; [背](背.md), [姿](姿.md), [体](体.md) and [身](身.md) overlap body build or figure. They are kept out of this card’s quiz options. Do not treat “handle,” “pattern” or “nature” as exclusive to any one character.
