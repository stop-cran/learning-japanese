# 左 — left

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 工 (no. 48) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

左 means "left" (the side, direction or hand). It is usually analysed as a hand (the top part, a form of ナ/𠂇) over 工; 工 is often explained as a tool or as work done with the left hand
as a helper to the right, though the exact origin is debated. A mnemonic: the left hand holds the tool (工) while the right does the main work.

## Notes

Its opposite is [右](右.md). Stroke order differs: 左 starts with the horizontal, 右 with the diagonal. Mnemonic: 左 has 工 at the bottom, 右 has 口.
Look-alike: 在 (to exist) has 土 at the bottom where 左 has 工.
