# 律 — law; rhythm

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

律 concerns rules or regulation and also occurs in rhythm or articulation vocabulary. 法律 ほうりつ means law. 律儀 りちぎ describes being conscientious or upright, not a separate kind of law.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 彳 beside the brush-like 聿 shape and imagine steps following a written rule or regular beat. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a verified historical or phonetic analysis.
