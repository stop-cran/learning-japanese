# 熟 — ripen; mature; skilled

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 火 (as 灬, no. 86), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

熟 is becoming fully ripe or fully developed, and from there being practised until skilled. The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ジュク. One example of each:

- Cook well: 半熟 はんじゅく, JMdict's "half-cooked; half-done; soft-boiled."
- Ripen: 熟柿 じゅくし, "ripe persimmon."
- Become skilled: 熟練 じゅくれん, "skill; expertise; experience; proficiency; mastery; dexterity."
- Do thoroughly: 熟慮 じゅくりょ, "deliberation; (thoughtful) consideration."

[熟語](../words/熟語.md) じゅくご is a compound word, "kanji compound" in JMdict, with a second sense "idiom; idiomatic phrase." 成熟 せいじゅく, "maturity; ripeness," is a common ripening word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [享](享.md), "enjoy; receive benefits," and [丸](丸.md), "round; circle," together above 灬, the four dots that are the fire shape of [火](火.md), "fire." Imagine a round fruit enjoying slow warmth until it is ripe. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that any part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 熟語, [語](語.md), "language; word," supplies ゴ. In 熟練, [練](練.md) supplies レン. In 熟慮, 慮 supplies リョ. In 半熟 はんじゅく, [半](半.md), "half," supplies はん; in 成熟 せいじゅく, [成](成.md) supplies セイ, and 熟 is the second element in both. 熟す じゅくす, JMdict's "to ripen; to mature," is a verb that uses the same ジュク. 熟れる うれる is JMdict's "to ripen (fruit, grain, etc.); to become ripe." The same spelling 熟す is also used for こなす, which JMdict marks as "word usually written using kana alone," and 熟 can write つくづく, where JMdict marks 熟 as a "rarely used kanji form"; these are not taught here.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジュク with 熟練, 熟慮 and 成熟, and うれる with 熟れる. Practice selects ジュク, the reading of the linked word articles. う.れる is shown with a dictionary example and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the linked word 熟語 is 語 (ゴ). In the examples without word articles, 練 (レン) is in 熟練, and [慮](慮.md), "consider; concern; forethought," supplies リョ in 熟慮.
- [語](語.md), "language; word," appears in 熟語, which makes it a plausible wrong answer. [木](木.md), "tree, wood," and [田](田.md), "rice field," suit ripening crops and supply this card's other wrong answers. They are different kanji with unrelated readings.
- [煮](煮.md), "boil; simmer; stew," and 熟 share the Daijisen sense "cook well." The exclusion of 熟 is listed on the 煮 card, so it is not repeated here. This is a shared-sense pair in the same batch. 熟睡 じゅくすい also uses 熟 as "thoroughly," but that word has no article here.
- [成](成.md), "become," [育](育.md), "grow up; raise," and [実](実.md), "reality; fruit," overlap with ripening and growth. [老](老.md), "grow old," overlaps with maturity. [練](練.md), "practice; refine," [習](習.md), "learn; practise," [慣](慣.md), "accustomed," [技](技.md), "skill; technique," and [腕](腕.md), "arm; skill," overlap with skilled. All nine are kept out of this card's quiz options.
