# 壮 — robust; grand in scale

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 士 (no. 33) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

壮 can express vigor or grandeur. In 壮大 it is the impressive scale of something, not physical strength, that matters.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look at 士 on the right: the upper horizontal is longer. Do not replace it with 土.

## Notes

The adjective 盛ん normally writes flourishing activity with 盛. Recognizing 壮ん does not make it the model spelling.

[状](状.md) ends in 犬, whereas 壮 ends in 士. Find the shape of 壮 above the clothing element in [装](装.md) and below the grass top in [荘](荘.md). [健](健.md) means healthy or robust. [強](強.md), [剛](剛.md) and [豪](豪.md) can also suggest strength or power; in 壮大 the impression is grandeur, not a measurement of force.
