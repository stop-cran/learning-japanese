# 芸 — art; performance; craft

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

芸 concerns artistic skill, accomplishment or performance in words such as [芸術](../words/芸術.md) げいじゅつ, the arts. The separate botanical example 芸香 うんこう illustrates ウン, not an alternative reading of 芸術.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine someone performing below a low stage canopy, using the stacked lower strokes as a cue for a practiced routine.

The canonical key is 艸, no. 140, appearing as 艹 across the top. Keep the source key rather than replacing it with 云.

- JMdict marks 藝術 as an outdated-kanji spelling of 芸術. Learn the present article's spelling and pronunciation together.
