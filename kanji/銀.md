# 銀 — silver

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 金 (as 釒, no. 167) · **Phonetic:** 艮 · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

銀 is a phono-semantic compound: [金](金.md) (metal) gives the sense and 艮 supplies the sound (ギン, related to コン/ゴン in other characters with 艮 such as 根 and 恨). It means "silver", the white metal, and from it "silver-coloured" and, in some words, money (銀行 is a bank, though the "silver" link there is only historical and hedged).
Seeing 艮 as "stay" or a picture in itself is not needed: for learning, treat it as a sound element.

## Common words

- [銀行](../words/銀行.md) ぎんこう — bank
- 銀色 ぎんいろ — silver (colour)
- 銀 ぎん — silver; a silver medal (銀メダル)

## Notes

- Silver and gold: 金銀 きんぎん (gold and silver); in sports, 金・銀・銅 (gold, silver, bronze).
- 銀行 is "bank" even though it has "silver": the word is explained in the [銀行](../words/銀行.md) article.
- Look-alikes: [金](金.md) (gold, money) without the right-hand part, and 根 (root) and 限 (limit), which share the 艮 component.
