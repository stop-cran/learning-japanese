# 痛 — pain; hurt

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 疒 (no. 104) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

痛 signals pain or hurting. The adjective [痛い](../words/痛い.md) いたい lets you say that a body part hurts,
while 痛む いたむ is a verb meaning to ache.
Headaches appear in the compound 頭痛 ずつう.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the inner 甬 shape as a sore body under the 疒 enclosure.
This is an invented memory image, not a claim about the original components' roles.

## Notes

歯が痛いです。 (はがいたいです。) — My tooth hurts.
English uses a verb here, but Japanese 痛い is an adjective; it does not take だ after it.

[通](通.md) contains the same inner 甬 with a movement radical instead.
[用](用.md) resembles only its lower portion; [同](同.md) is a looser box-shaped contrast without 痛's enclosing 疒.
