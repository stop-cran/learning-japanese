# 市 — city; market

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

市 connects two useful areas of vocabulary: cities and markets.
[市民](../words/市民.md) しみん names citizens or city residents, and 市役所 しやくしょ is a city hall.
The kun reading appears in 市場 いちば, a marketplace.
Do not assume that every word containing 市 means a market.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a small canopy above the hanging 巾 shape, like a stall you pass in a city.
This is a memory image based on the written shape, not a historical claim.

## Notes

市 is indexed under 巾, visible in its lower part.
[席](席.md), [常](常.md), and [師](師.md) also contain 巾, but have more material above or beside it.
The dictionary has more than one sense of 市民; its civic meaning is not limited to a literal market community.
