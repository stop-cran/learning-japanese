# 磁 — magnetism; porcelain

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

磁 has two established learning anchors: magnetism and porcelain.
[磁石](../words/磁石.md) じしゃく is a magnet or magnetic compass;
磁器 じき is porcelain. Porcelain is not magnetic simply because the
two words share this character.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [石](石.md) beside the
兹-shaped cluster with two small 幺-like lower forms. Imagine two little
objects drawn toward a magnet beside a stone. This is a visual memory
scene, not a history of magnetism or porcelain.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジ | on | [磁石](../words/磁石.md) じしゃく - magnet; magnetic compass |

Practice selects ジ, also the Joyo reading. 磁石 additionally has the
dictionary reading じせき; 磁 supplies ジ in both, while 石 changes
from シャク to セキ. The frozen source record supplies no kun reading.

## Common words

- [磁石](../words/磁石.md) じしゃく - magnet; magnetic compass
- 磁気 じき - magnetism
- 磁力 じりょく - magnetic force
- 磁器 じき - porcelain

## Notes

- 磁気 and 磁器 are homophones, not interchangeable spellings or senses.
  Learn which complete word the context requires.
- [硬](硬.md), hard/stiff, [研](研.md), polish/study closely, and
  [砂](砂.md), sand, share 石 but have different right sides.
- [引](引.md) concerns pulling and [吸](吸.md) inhaling or drawing in;
  neither is a general substitute for magnetism. Likewise, [器](器.md)
  names a vessel or instrument, not specifically its porcelain material.
- A modern magnet need not be a natural stone, and magnetism does not
  mean attracting every metal. The character is a vocabulary cue,
  not a complete explanation of the physical effect.
