# 務 — duties

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

務 concerns a task, duty, or role to be carried out. In [事務](../words/事務.md) じむ,
those tasks are administrative or clerical work.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** locate 力 at the lower right and imagine it doing the work beneath
the crossing upper strokes. This is a shape-based memory aid, not an etymology.

## Common words

- [事務](../words/事務.md) じむ — clerical work; administration
- 義務 ぎむ — duty; obligation
- 務める つとめる — carry out a role

## Notes

務める focuses on a role someone performs. [勤める](../words/勤める.md) つとめる instead highlights employment;
努める つとめる means to make an effort.
In [野](野.md), the hook-bearing part is on the right; do not mistake that outline for 務.
