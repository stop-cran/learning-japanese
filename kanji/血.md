# 血 — blood

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 血 (no. 143) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

血 means blood and can extend to ancestry or passionate feelings. Learn the standalone noun ち alongside compounds using ケツ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a drop of blood above a dish. The visible extra top stroke helps distinguish 血 from [皿](皿.md); the picture is not a historical claim.

## Notes

- The indexing radical is 血 itself, not 皿, despite the visual similarity.
- 血が出る ちがでる expresses bleeding: blood comes out. The noun 血 is not itself an English-style verb meaning bleed.
