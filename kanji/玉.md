# 玉 — jewel; ball

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 玉 (no. 96) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

玉 concerns jewels and rounded objects. The native word たま can name a ball, bead or jewel; in coin names the final element is often だま. Learn the object and the whole expression rather than translating every 玉 as a precious stone.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare the 王-like framework with its extra dot: imagine a small jewel fastened to a stand. This is a visual memory aid, not a derivation from the character for king.

## Notes

- The hyphens in たま- and -だま mark bound uses; they are not written in 玉砂利 or 百円玉. Digital Daijisen describes 玉砂利 as relatively large, rounded gravel.
- The linked [玉](../words/玉.md) article teaches たま. Its separate ギョク row is a different documented reading, not a replacement for たま in an ordinary ball expression.
- Compare [王](王.md), king, and [主](主.md), master or principal. The position and presence of the dot matter; neither is another spelling for 玉.
