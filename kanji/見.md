# 見 — see, look

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 見 (no. 147) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

見 is a combination of 目 (eye, [目](目.md)) on top and 儿 (legs, a person) below: a person with a prominent eye, someone looking.
It is a standard example of an ideographic compound. It means "see", "look" and, in compounds, "opinion" (意見) and "view".

## Notes

- 見る is a regular ichidan verb: 見ます, 見ない, 見て, 見た. 見える (intransitive, "can be seen") and 見せる ("show") are separate verbs; 見つける みつける means "find".
- Writing: 見る is the general verb; 観る (to watch a film or play: 映画を観る) and 視る (to inspect, diagnose) are variants of the same word.
- Look-alikes: 貝 (shell, the base of [買](買.md)) is 目 over 八 (not 儿), and [目](目.md) (eye) is only the top of 見. Do not confuse 見 with 貝 or 具.
- 見 appears as the key of 親 (parent), 観 and 規 as well.
