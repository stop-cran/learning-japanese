# 犬 — dog

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (no. 94) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

犬 is a pictograph of a dog. The oldest forms show an animal in profile, with its head, legs and tail, and the form was simplified into a kanji that looks like [大](大.md) plus a dot.
Some people see the dot as an ear, which is a handy mnemonic only. As a radical it appears (as 犭, the "beast" form) in kanji for animals: 猫 (cat), 狼 おおかみ (wolf). 犭 also appears in non-animal kanji such as 犯 (crime), so it is not a sure sign of an animal.

## Notes

Compare [大](大.md) (big, three strokes) and [天](天.md) (sky): 犬 has the extra dot at the upper right of 大. 太 (thick) has its dot lower, under the crossing.
犬 is also a prefix meaning "useless, in vain" in some words, as in 犬死に.
