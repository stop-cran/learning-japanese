# 索 — rope

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

KANJIDIC2 gives 索 the senses "cord" and "rope," as well as "searching" and "inquiring." Digital Daijisen describes seeking by means of clues and separately lists a state of being scattered and disappearing in words such as 索然. JMdict's single-character entry reads 索 as さく for a rope or cord. In [索引](../words/索引.md) (さくいん), the established compound means an index used to locate information; the whole word supplies that meaning.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** see the compact upper strokes above 糸 at the bottom and imagine a cord gathered under a label so it can be found. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Common words

- [索引](../words/索引.md) さくいん - index in a book
- 捜索 そうさく - search, especially for someone or something missing
- 検索 けんさく - look up or search for information
- 索道 さくどう - cableway; ropeway

## Notes

- 索隠 is marked by JMdict as a search-only kanji form of 索引. It is not the spelling to imitate in ordinary writing.
- [緒](緒.md), whose title includes "cord," is excluded from meaning choices because that English label overlaps with 索's "rope." This is a quiz guard for the displayed meanings, not a claim that the two characters are interchangeable.
- [捜](捜.md), [探](探.md), and [求](求.md) overlap with searching or seeking; [問](問.md), [聞](聞.md), and [伺](伺.md) with asking or inquiring. [調](調.md) overlaps with investigation/inquiry; these targeted guards do not make those actions interchangeable with 索. [散](散.md) is excluded only for Daijisen's restricted scattered/disappearing sense (as in 索然), not as a general gloss of 索.
- [査](査.md) and [究](究.md) are also excluded as conservative guards because their fact-finding and investigation senses overlap with 索's broader search/inquiry senses. These guards do not make investigation synonymous with seeking by clues. [糸](糸.md) means thread, while 索 means a thick rope or cord; the material/size relationship is not a synonym.
- [偵](偵.md), spy/covert investigation, is guarded conservatively for the investigation overlap. 索's searching/inquiring sense does not by itself imply spying or secrecy.
- [頼](頼.md), request/rely, is guarded for the broad asking/request label near 索's inquiry/seeking sense, not for reliance. Asking for an action and seeking information remain different uses.
- [願](願.md), wish/petition, also has a sourced request sense, guarded conservatively near 索's inquiry/seeking sense. This does not make wishing, praying, or requesting an action the same as searching for information.
- [素](素.md), unadorned/element; [紙](紙.md), paper; and [経](経.md), pass through/sutra, share the 糸 component or radical but have distinct meanings.
