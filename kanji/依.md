# 依 — rely on; depend

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

依 concerns depending on or relying on something. 依頼 いらい is a request that entrusts a matter to someone, while 依存 いぞん or いそん concerns dependence. The character is useful for recognizing these compounds, not as a rule that all requests imply helplessness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a person 亻 relying on a coat-shaped 衣 on the right for shelter.

The canonical radical 人 has the left-hand form 亻.

- 依存 いぞん - dependence; reliance

## Notes

- 依存 has the dictionary readings いぞん and いそん. A compound's reading is a word-level fact; the character's イ reading does not settle the sound of 存.
- 依る よる is a recognized but uncommon kanji spelling for relevant よる uses. The everyday expression is often written in kana; do not substitute 依 for every よる homophone.
