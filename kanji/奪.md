# 奪 — snatch; rob; deprive

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

奪 is taking something away from someone by force: snatching, robbing, depriving. [奪う](../words/奪う.md) うばう is to take away or deprive; JMdict glosses it "to take (by force); to take away; to snatch; to steal" and, in a second sense, "to absorb (one's attention); to fascinate; to captivate."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ダツ: taking what belongs to someone else by force. Among its examples are 奪回 だっかい, "recovery; rescue; recapture"; 奪取 だっしゅ, "usurpation; taking back; dispossession"; 強奪 ごうだつ, "robbery; pillage; seizure; hijacking"; and 略奪 りゃくだつ, "pillage; plunder; looting; robbery." It marks ダツ as a customary reading and gives the kun reading うばう, the same as KANJIDIC2. A separate Daijisen headword treats だつ as a word element added before a noun for taking or winning it, as in 奪三振 だつさんしん, "striking a batter out," a baseball term. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [大](大.md), "big, large," at the top, a bird-like shape in the middle, the same shape as the top of [集](集.md), "gather," and [寸](寸.md), "sun (length unit); small," at the bottom. Imagine a big man who grabs a bird and holds it tight at the bottom so it cannot fly off. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 大, the bird-like shape or 寸 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ダツ | on | 争奪 そうだつ - struggle (for); scramble; contest; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| うば.う | kun | [奪う](../words/奪う.md) うばう — take away; deprive |

In 奪う, 奪 writes うば and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 争奪, 争 supplies ソウ and 奪 supplies ダツ. Before a following か or し, ダツ shortens to だっ, so the next sound is doubled: だっ + かい gives 奪回 だっかい, and だっ + しゅ gives 奪取 だっしゅ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ダツ, with 奪回, 奪取 and 争奪 as examples, and うばう, with 奪う and 奪い取る うばいとる, "to plunder; to grab; to snatch." Practice selects うば.う, the reading with a linked word article. ダツ is the reading of most 奪 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; it is illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 奪う is 奪 with okurigana only.
- [集](集.md), "gather," and [隼](隼.md), "falcon," have the same bird-like shape that sits in the middle of 奪, here at their top, and [雑](雑.md), "miscellaneous," has it at the right. All are different kanji.
- [盗](盗.md), "steal," [取](取.md), "take," and [捕](捕.md), "capture," overlap the senses "rob," "snatch," "take away" and "seize" that 奪 and its words carry, and [魅](魅.md), "charm; bewitch," overlaps the second sense of 奪う, "to fascinate; to captivate; to charm"; 取 appears with 奪 in 奪取, and JMdict also writes とる, "to steal; to rob; to take away," as 奪る. These four are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "take," "rob" or "charm" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ダツ sense: 奪還 だっかん, "recapture; retaking; recovery; taking back"; 簒奪 さんだつ, "usurpation"; 収奪 しゅうだつ, "plundering; exploitation"; 剝奪 はくだつ, "stripping (of rights, rank, etc.); deprivation; forfeit"; and 与奪 よだつ, "giving and taking away."
