# 暦 — calendar; almanac; year reckoning

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

暦 is a calendar or almanac: a record of days, months and seasons. [西暦](../words/西暦.md) せいれき names Common Era year numbering and the Western calendar. JMdict glosses 西暦 “Common Era; CE; Christian Era; anno domini; AD” and “Western (Gregorian) calendar.”

Daijisen gives two meanings for レキ: a record of the recurring days, months and seasons, and the calculation of celestial motion or one’s fortune. 暦数 れきすう, “calendar making; number of years; one’s fate,” illustrates the latter. The alternate reading リャク is supported by 延暦 えんりゃく, the Enryaku era; it has no declaring word article and is not selected for questions here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 日, “sun, day,” at the bottom, under the dense shapes above it. Picture dates arranged down a calendar page. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim about how the character was historically formed or what its upper parts mean. Historical formation is not established here.

| こよみ | kun | 暦 こよみ — calendar; almanac; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 西暦, 西 supplies セイ and 暦 supplies レキ. In 延暦, 延 supplies エン and 暦 is read リャク. The stand-alone word 暦 is read こよみ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows レキ with 暦年, 還暦 and 太陽暦, and こよみ with 暦 and 花暦. JMdict glosses 暦年 “calendar year; civil year,” 還暦 “kanreki; one’s 60th birthday,” 太陽暦 “solar calendar,” 暦 “calendar; almanac,” and 花暦 “flower calendar.” Practice selects レキ, the reading with a linked word article; リャク and こよみ remain sourced readings, not selected for questions here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid. The Joyo table gives 曆 in parentheses as a genuine older form.

## Notes

- The other kanji in 西暦 is [西](西.md), “west.”
- [木](木.md), “tree, wood,” and [林](林.md), “grove; woodland,” resemble the dense forms above 暦’s 日; [日](日.md), “sun, day,” is the clear shape at the bottom. These are the distractors. A day is not itself a calendar, and none of these words names a calendar or system of year reckoning.
- [年](年.md), “year,” [歳](歳.md), “years of age,” [齢](齢.md), “age; years lived,” [代](代.md), “era,” [歴](歴.md), “history; passage of time,” [史](史.md), “history; chronicle,” [紀](紀.md), “chronicle,” [曜](曜.md), “day of the week,” [季](季.md), “seasons,” and [期](期.md), “period; term,” overlap the year, time or calendar senses of 暦 and are kept out of its quiz options.
