# 決 — decide

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

決 marks deciding or settling something.
[決める](../words/決める.md) (きめる) describes making the decision; 決まる (きまる) describes something becoming settled.
That difference matters more in a sentence than the English gloss "decide" alone.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** treat the three water marks on the left as three possible routes before choosing one.
This is a deliberately invented memory scene, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

日を決める (ひをきめる) means "decide on a day"; 日が決まる (ひがきまる) means "the day is decided."
The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵, just as in [注](注.md) and [洋](洋.md).
Do not confuse the angular right side with the standalone character [欠](欠.md).
