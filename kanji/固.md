# 固 — firm; harden

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

固 connects firmness or hardness with becoming firm and making something firm. 固体 こたい is a solid, one of the states of matter; 固い かたい describes something hard or firm.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 古 held firmly inside a surrounding enclosure, unable to move.

The radical is 囗, the outer enclosure, not the smaller 口-shaped part inside 古. 囗 and 口 are different indexing radicals.

## Notes

- 固める is transitive: someone makes something firm. 固まる is intransitive: something becomes firm. The related noun 固まり names a lump or mass; it is not another verb ending.
- 固い, 硬い and 堅い can all be read かたい. Their spelling choices depend on the word and context; learning 固 does not make it the spelling for every English use of hard.
- Compare the enclosures in [因](因.md), cause, and [困](困.md), be in difficulty. Their similar outlines do not give them the firmness meaning.
