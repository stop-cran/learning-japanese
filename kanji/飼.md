# 飼 — keep (animals); rear

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 食 (no. 184) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

飼 is keeping and feeding animals. KANJIDIC2 lists “domesticate”, “raise”, “keep” and “feed”. Kanjipedia gives かう。やしなう。動物を育てる。 (keep; nourish; raise animals), and Digital Daijisen gives えさを与えて養う。 (give feed and nourish). [飼う](../words/飼う.md) かう is glossed in JMdict “to keep (a pet or other animal); to have; to own; to raise; to rear; to feed” (entry 1312970).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is 飠, a variant of [食](食.md), "eat, food," and on the right is [司](司.md), "take charge; official; preside." Imagine someone taking charge of a pet's food bowl, filling it every morning so the animal is well kept. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 飠 or 司 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- In 飼う, 飼 writes か and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji.
- Joyo lists シ, with 飼育 and 飼料 しりょう (“fodder; feed”, JMdict 1312990), and かう, with 飼う. Kanjipedia lists やしなう outside the table (外); it is not a KANJIDIC2 reading and has no table row. KANJIDIC2 gives grade 5, and Digital Daijisen marks 飼 as ［学習漢字］5年.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading かい, and Digital Daijisen gives かい. This is name use, not a quiz reading.

These four cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [養](養.md) (“nourish; raise; adopt”): KANJIDIC2 lists “rear” and the kun reading やしな.う for 養; Kanjipedia gives やしなう for 飼 outside the table (外), Digital Daijisen's sense is えさを与えて養う。, and JMdict's second sense of 養う やしなう is “to bring up; to raise; to rear; to feed” (1547090).
- [育](育.md) (“grow up; raise”): Kanjipedia's sense of 飼 includes 動物を育てる (raise animals); JMdict glosses 育てる そだてる “to raise; to rear; to bring up” (1160570) and 飼育 “breeding; raising; rearing; keeping”.
- [牧](牧.md) (“pasture; raise livestock; pastor”): KANJIDIC2 lists “breed”, “care for” and “feed” for 牧, which overlaps 飼う “to raise; to rear; to feed”, and JMdict glosses 牧畜 ぼくちく “stock-farming; livestock farming; cattle breeding” (1521820).
- [畜](畜.md) (“livestock; domestic animals”): KANJIDIC2 lists “domesticate” for 飼, and JMdict glosses 家畜 かちく “domestic animals; livestock; cattle” (1192240).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 捕 (“capture”), 捨 (“discard; abandon”), and 洗 (“wash”); none means keeping or rearing animals.
