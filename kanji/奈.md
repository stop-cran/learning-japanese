# 奈 — na in names

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

Learn 奈 first as な in the place name 奈良 (なら). In the formal question word 奈辺 (なへん, also written 那辺), it is also read な: the whole word means “where.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare [宗](宗.md), with a roof over 示, and [票](票.md), with a different top above 示. In 奈 the top is 大.

## Notes

如何 can be read いかん (“depending on how or what”) or いかが (“how; how about”). 奈何 is a rare spelling for the first of these words; the second is usually written いかが. Neither whole-word reading tells you how to divide up 奈良.

With [示](示.md) at the bottom, the top distinguishes 奈 (大), [宗](宗.md) (宀), and [票](票.md) (覀). Compare [何](何.md) (what): the question sense of 奈 does not make either character a substitute for the other in the name 奈良.
