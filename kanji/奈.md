# 奈 — na in names; how

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

Learn 奈 first as な in the place name 奈良. The character also has an interrogative use; it is not a standalone everyday translation for every English “what.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare [宗](宗.md), with a roof over 示, and [票](票.md), with a different top above 示. In 奈 the top is 大.

## Notes

In 奈良 the sound is the useful cue. In formal 奈辺 (なへん, “where”), the same な occurs in a question word rather than a place name.

With [示](示.md) at the bottom, the top distinguishes 奈 (大), [宗](宗.md) (宀), and [票](票.md) (覀). Compare [何](何.md) (what): the question sense of 奈 does not make either character a substitute for the other in the name 奈良.
