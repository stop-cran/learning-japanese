# 票 — ballot; voting slip

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 示 (no. 113), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

票 is a written slip, ticket or label, especially a ballot or vote.
[投票](../words/投票.md) とうひょう means voting or casting a ballot.
The character is not restricted to elections: 伝票 でんぴょう can be a
business slip or voucher. Nor does every modern vote require a paper slip.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 覀 over [示](示.md).
Imagine a marked voting slip held above a display. This is a memory scene
for the present shape, not a claim about ancient voting or the components'
historical functions.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヒョウ | on | [投票](../words/投票.md) とうひょう - voting; ballot |

Practice selects ヒョウ; 投 supplies トウ in 投票. Keep both long vowels.
The frozen source has no kun reading. Voiced or semi-voiced びょう/ぴょう
occur when 票 is counted or compounded; 伝票 is でんぴょう. These changes
do not add separate basic readings to the preserved metadata.

## Common words

- [投票](../words/投票.md) とうひょう - voting; casting a ballot
- 開票 かいひょう - counting ballots
- 得票 とくひょう - votes obtained
- 伝票 でんぴょう - slip; voucher

## Notes

- [札](札.md), tag/ticket, [券](券.md), ticket/coupon, and [符](符.md),
  token/sign, overlap the slip or ticket meanings. The sign/label branch
  also connects with [標](標.md) and [印](印.md), mark.
- [紙](紙.md) is paper, a possible material, not the vote itself.
  [表](表.md) can be a chart of results but does not therefore mean a ballot.
- [栗](栗.md), chestnut, has 木 below its upper block; [要](要.md),
  necessity/essentials, has 女. [祭](祭.md), festival/ritual/worship,
  shares 示 below but has a different top.
- 標 contains 票 beside 木 and also reads ヒョウ. A shared component
  or sound alone is not a rule that all their words mean the same thing.
