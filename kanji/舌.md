# 舌 — tongue; speech

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 舌 (no. 135) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

舌 is the tongue, the organ in the mouth. It can also name a tongue-shaped part, such as a bell’s clapper or a musical instrument’s reed. In compounds, its meaning extends to speaking or words: 毒舌 (どくぜつ) is sharp or abusive speech.

## Notes

The selected quiz reading is **した** because the whole single-character word 舌 is read した. Digital Daijisen records した（じた） for use in compounds; じた is a context-specific voiced form, not a separate standalone reading or this card’s quiz option. Its difficult whole-word reading 百舌 (もず) is likewise not a standalone reading for 舌.

Kanjipedia also lists セツ (on) and ことば (kun), outside the Jōyō and KANJIDIC2 reading lists. The reviewed JMdict entries provide no example aligned to either form, so they are noted as source-only rather than presented as ordinary readings or quiz choices. The “speech” sense is supported by Digital Daijisen; it overlaps the displayed titles of 弁 (“speech; discern; valve; bento”), 話 (“talk, speak”), 言 (“say; words”), 語 (“language, word”), and 詞 (“words; lyrics; part of speech”), so those cards are excluded from meaning choices.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 口 (“mouth”), 耳 (“ear”), and 足 (“foot; leg”); none names the tongue or speech.
