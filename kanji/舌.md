# 舌 — tongue

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 舌 (no. 135) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

舌 is the tongue, the organ in the mouth. It can also name a tongue-shaped part, such as a bell’s clapper or a musical instrument’s reed. In compounds, it can also refer to speech or words: 毒舌 (どくぜつ) is sharp or abusive speech, and 弁舌 (べんぜつ) is eloquence or a manner of speaking.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [千](千.md), "thousand," sits on top of [口](口.md), "mouth." Imagine a thousand words leaving one mouth, each shaped by the tongue. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 千 or 口 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

The selected quiz reading is **した** because the whole single-character word 舌 is read した. Digital Daijisen records した（じた） for use in compounds; じた is a context-specific voiced form, not a separate standalone reading or this card’s quiz option. Its difficult whole-word reading 百舌 (もず) is likewise not a standalone reading for 舌.

Kanjipedia also lists セツ (on) and ことば (kun), both marked as outside the Jōyō table; neither is in the KANJIDIC2 reading lists. The reviewed JMdict entries provide no example aligned to either form, so they are noted as source-only rather than presented as ordinary readings or quiz choices.

The title is limited to “tongue”, but speech remains a character-level sense of 舌: Kanjipedia's second meaning is ことば (舌禍, 筆舌), and Digital Daijisen's ゼツ entry gives speaking (舌戦, 饒舌, 毒舌, 弁舌). In English, “tongue” can also mean a language. Because a learner may reasonably connect 舌 with any card whose displayed title names speech, talk, words, or language, the meaning quiz excludes 弁 (“speech; discern; valve; bento”), 話 (“talk, speak”), 言 (“say; words”), 語 (“language, word”), 詞 (“words; lyrics; part of speech”), and 辞 (“words; resign”).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 口 (“mouth”), 耳 (“ear”), and 足 (“foot, leg”); none of these titles names the tongue, speech, or language.
