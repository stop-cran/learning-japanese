# 掛 — hang

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

Hanging something is a concrete starting point for 掛. [掛ける](../words/掛ける.md) かける can describe
hanging a picture; 掛かる かかる can describe its hanging there. These verbs have many other senses,
and JMdict marks them as usually written in kana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the hand-side 扌 hanging something beside the two stacked 土 shapes and the far-right 卜.
This is a modern visual aid, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

## Notes

絵を壁にかける (えをかべにかける) means "hang a picture on the wall."
Learn the whole phrase; the sound かける does not by itself identify a single meaning.
The linked article deliberately starts with hanging rather than treating every use as a physical suspension.

[指](指.md), [折](折.md), and [押](押.md) share 扌, but have fewer strokes at the right.
In 掛, remember both the stacked 土 shapes and the final 卜.
