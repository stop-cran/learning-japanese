# 親 — parent; close

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 見 (no. 147) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

親 means a parent and also expresses closeness or familiarity. The noun [親](../words/親.md) おや names a parent;
親しい したしい describes a close relationship.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a parent standing by a tree and looking out for a child:
spot [立](立.md)- and [木](木.md)-like shapes on the left and [見](見.md) on the right.
This familiar visual story is a mnemonic, not a verified account of the character's history.
