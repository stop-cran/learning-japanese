# 招 — invite

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

招 is associated with inviting or beckoning someone. [招く](../words/招く.md) まねく is a verb,
and 招待 しょうたい is the noun "invitation," also used with する.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture 扌 making an inviting gesture beside the stacked 刀 and 口 of 召.
The gesture is a modern memory aid, not a verified explanation of the character's historical parts.

## Notes

友達を家に招く (ともだちをいえにまねく) means "invite a friend to one's home."
The person takes を; the destination takes に.

Look for 口 beneath 刀 on the right. [切](切.md) contains 刀 without that lower box,
while [抱](抱.md) and [押](押.md) share only the hand-side layout.
Do not infer the reading from the hand component alone.
