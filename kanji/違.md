# 違 — differ

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

違 marks a difference or mismatch. [違う](../words/違う.md) ちがう can simply mean "be different," without judging either thing as bad.
In a correction it can also mean "be wrong"; context determines which interpretation fits.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the central 口 between the bars of 韋, all riding on 辶.
Picture the bars not lining up with a matching pattern. This is only a mnemonic.
