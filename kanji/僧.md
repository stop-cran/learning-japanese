# 僧 — Buddhist monk; priest

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

僧 usually refers to a Buddhist monk or priest.
[僧侶](../words/僧侶.md) そうりょ is a useful word for a member of the Buddhist clergy,
not simply anyone who believes in Buddhism.

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and 曽 on the right.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a person beside a stack of temple books.
This is a visual memory aid, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

The frozen character dictionary gives ソウ and no kun reading.
Learn the pronunciation of each compound rather than substituting ソウ
mechanically: 小僧 こぞう can mean a boy, a young monk or an apprentice.
It is not a neutral substitute for 僧侶 in every context.

## Common words

- [僧侶](../words/僧侶.md) そうりょ — Buddhist priest; monk
- 高僧 こうそう — a high-ranking or highly respected Buddhist priest

## Notes

- 僧 そう can also abbreviate the Buddhist community, or sangha, in religious usage.
  The context distinguishes this collective sense from an individual monk.
- The English gloss "priest" does not make 僧 a general translation for clergy
  of every religion. Compounds can have wider uses; learn the whole word.
- [増](増.md), "increase," has 土 on the left instead of 亻.
  [値](値.md), "value; price," and [倍](倍.md), "multiple; double,"
  share 亻 but have different right-hand shapes.
