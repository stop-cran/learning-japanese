# 病 — illness

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 疒 (no. 104) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

病 concerns sickness or disease. [病気](../words/病気.md) is a general word for illness, and [病院](../words/病院.md) names a hospital or clinic.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see 疒 wrapping around 丙. Imagine the outer strokes as a shelter around someone who is ill inside. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not an etymology.

## Notes

In びょう, the small ょ combines with び, and う lengthens the vowel.
The interior of 病 resembles [内](内.md) with an extra top line. Compare [広](広.md), which has 广 rather than 疒 around its inside part; do not lose the extra strokes on the left of 病.
