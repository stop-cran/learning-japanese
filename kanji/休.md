# 休 — rest

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

休 combines 亻 (person, [人](人.md)) and 木 (tree, [木](木.md)): a person resting against a tree. This is the standard explanation
and a good mnemonic, even though some details of its early history are debated.
It means "rest", "take a break" and "be off work".
