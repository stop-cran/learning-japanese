# 着 — wear; arrive

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

着 appears both in clothing words and in words about arriving. Learn those two useful branches through
[着る](../words/着る.md) (きる, put on clothes) and 着く (つく, arrive), rather than forcing one English translation into every word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** picture the many-stroked upper part as a coat hanging over
an [eye](目.md): you look to see whether you have put it on correctly.

## Common words

- [着る](../words/着る.md) きる — put on clothes covering the upper body
- 着物 きもの — kimono
- 着く つく — arrive
- 到着 とうちゃく — arrival; 到着する means "arrive"

## Notes

着る and 着く have different grammar as well as different readings: コートを着る means "put on a coat";
駅に着く (えきにつく) means "arrive at the station." 着ている (きている) commonly describes wearing something now.

The lower part is 目, not 日. Compare [者](者.md), which has a different top and a 日-shaped lower part.
