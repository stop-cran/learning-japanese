# 着 — wear; arrive

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

Two useful meanings of 着 are “wear,” as in [着る](../words/着る.md) (きる, put on clothes), and “arrive,” as in
[着く](../words/着く.md) (つく). 着ける (つける) can fit either meaning: you can put on a mask or bring a boat alongside the shore.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** picture the many-stroked upper part as a coat hanging over
an [eye](目.md): you look to see whether you have put it on correctly.

## Common words

- [着る](../words/着る.md) きる — put on clothes covering the upper body
- 着物 きもの — kimono
- 着く つく — arrive
- 到着 とうちゃく — arrival; 到着する means "arrive"
- [着せる](../words/着せる.md) きせる - put clothes on someone
- [着替え](../words/着替え.md) きがえ - changing clothes; a change of clothes

## Notes

- コートを着る (コートをきる) means “put on a coat.” 子どもにコートを着せる (こどもにコートをきせる) means “put a coat on a child.” 着ている (きている) describes the state of wearing it.
- マスクを着ける (マスクをつける) means “put on a mask.” For attaching one thing to another, use [付ける](../words/付ける.md) or kana つける: シャツにボタンを付ける means “attach a button to a shirt.”
- 船が岸に着く (ふねがきしにつく) means “the boat reaches the shore.” 船を岸に着ける (ふねをきしにつける) means “bring the boat alongside the shore.”

身に着ける (みにつける) means “wear.” For acquiring knowledge or a skill, write 身に付ける or 身につける; 身に着ける is also possible in this figurative sense.

The lower part is 目, not 日. Compare [者](者.md), which has a different top and a 日-shaped lower part.
