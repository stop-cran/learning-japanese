# 躍 — leap; jump up

**Strokes:** 21 · **Key (radical):** 足 (no. 157) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

躍 is leaping or springing up, and moving about with energy. KANJIDIC2 lists “leap”, “dance” and “skip”, and Kanjipedia gives おどる。おどりあがる。とびはねる。 (leap; jump up; jump about). Digital Daijisen gives two senses for ヤク: 高くはね上がる (to spring up high), as in 飛躍 and 跳躍, and 勢いよく動き回る (to move about vigorously), as in 躍動 and 活躍. [活躍](../words/活躍.md) かつやく is glossed in JMdict “activity (esp. energetic or successful); great efforts; active participation”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [足](足.md), "foot, leg," with [口](口.md), "mouth," at its top, stands on the left. On the right is the shape 翟: [羽](羽.md), "feather; bird counter," over the shape 隹, which begins with 亻, a variant of [人](人.md), "person." Picture a foot leaping up as lightly as a bird on its feathers. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 足 or 翟 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ヤク, with 躍動 やくどう (“lively motion; throb”, JMdict 1538470) and 飛躍 ひやく among its examples, and おどる, with 躍る and 躍り上がる, and pairs 躍る with 踊る (⇔).
- Kanjipedia lists テキ outside the table (外), for its second meaning group すばやい。はやく走る。. It is not a KANJIDIC2 reading, so it has no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading おどり; Digital Daijisen gives no name readings. This is a name-use form, not a quiz reading.

These four cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [踊](踊.md) (“dance; hop”): KANJIDIC2 lists “dance” for 躍; JMdict 躍る (entry 1538445), “to jump up; to spring up; to leap”, has a cross-reference to 踊る; the Joyo table pairs 躍る with 踊る; and the 踊 card lists おど.る, the same kun reading as 躍.
- [飛](飛.md) (“fly”): JMdict glosses 飛躍 ひやく “leap; jump” (entry 1485560, sense 1), Kanjipedia's sense of 躍 includes とびはねる (jump about), and JMdict 飛ぶ (entry 1429700) sense 2 is “to jump; to leap; to spring; to bound; to hop”.
- [舞](舞.md) (“dance”): KANJIDIC2 lists “dance” for 躍.
- [跳](跳.md) (“hop; spring up”): Kanjipedia gives 跳 the second sense おどる。おどりあがる。 (with おどる outside the Joyo table) and gives 躍 おどる。おどりあがる。, and JMdict glosses 跳躍 ちょうやく “jump; leap; skip; bound” (entry 1429710).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 転 (“turn; roll”), 倒 (“topple; collapse”), and 沈 (“sink; subside”); none means leaping or jumping up.
