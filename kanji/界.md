# 界 — world, boundary

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

界 can refer to a world or sphere of activity, or to a boundary or limit. [世界](../words/世界.md) names the world, while 業界 (ぎょうかい) names an industry or business sphere.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice [田](田.md) above 介. Imagine the lower strokes as markers dividing the field into separate regions. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical explanation.

- 限界 げんかい — limit, boundary

## Notes

Compare [男](男.md): both have 田 above another component, but 男 has 力 below, not 介.
In 世界, [世](世.md) and 界 belong to one word; 界 by itself is not the ordinary standalone Japanese word for "the world."
