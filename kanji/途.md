# 途 — route; course

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

途 is a way or course. [途中](../words/途中.md) とちゅう places something partway along a journey
or an activity; 用途 ようと concerns the purpose for which something is used.
The road idea helps with 途中, but does not mechanically translate every compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture 余 as a marker standing beside the long sweeping route.
This is a modern visual memory aid, not a proposed etymology.

## Notes

[路](路.md) and [道](道.md) also have way or route meanings; their words help distinguish how each is used.
The radical 辵 appears as 辶. Compare the inner 余 with the centers of [近](近.md), [迷](迷.md), and [追](追.md).
途中 does not require being exactly at the halfway point.
