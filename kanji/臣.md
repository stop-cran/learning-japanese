# 臣 — retainer; subject

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 臣 (no. 131) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

臣 concerns a retainer or subject in a relationship to a ruler or lord. 家臣 かしん illustrates the retainer sense; [大臣](../words/大臣.md) だいじん is the established modern title cabinet minister.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a person remaining within the squared-off outline, serving a ruler. This is a memory story about the modern shape, not a historical account.

## Notes

- The complete word decides between シン and ジン; 大臣 is not read だいしん.
- Subject here is a person under a ruler, not the grammatical subject of a sentence or a school subject.
- Compare the shorter [巨](巨.md), huge. Similar squared forms do not supply the same meaning.
