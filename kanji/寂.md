# 寂 — lonely; quiet; desolate

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寂 is stillness and the loneliness that comes with it: a quiet, empty place, or a person who misses company. [寂しい](../words/寂しい.md) さびしい is lonely or lonesome; JMdict glosses it "lonely (e.g. person, feeling); lonesome; forlorn," and in later senses "desolate; deserted; lonely (e.g. place); isolated; solitary," "sad" and "lacking; wanting; insufficient." JMdict also reads 寂しい as さみしい and lists the second spelling 淋しい.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ジャク. The first is being hushed and still, as in 静寂 せいじゃく, "silence; stillness; quietness," 閑寂 かんじゃく, "quiet; peaceful; tranquil," and 幽寂 ゆうじゃく, "quiet; sequestered." The second is the death of a Buddhist priest, as in 入寂 にゅうじゃく, "death of a priest; nirvana; spiritual liberty," and 示寂 じじゃく, "death of a high-ranking priest." For セキ it gives the first sense again, as in 寂寥 せきりょう, "loneliness; desolateness." Its kun readings are さび, さびしい and さびれる. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

Daijisen also has a headword さむしい spelled with 寂しい, marked as outside the official table; JMdict has no entry for さむしい. JMdict glosses the noun 寂 さび "patina; antique look," then "elegant simplicity" and "well-trained voice," and has a separate entry 寂 じゃく or せき, "(entering into) nirvana," "died" as a suffix, and "silent; tranquil."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the roof-like 宀 at the top; below it, at the left, a shape that looks like [上](上.md), "up, above," stacked on [小](小.md), "small, little," and at the right 又, a shape often seen as a hand. Imagine one small person sitting alone under a big roof, hand on knee, in a silent house. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 宀, 上, 小 or 又 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 寂しい, 寂 writes さび and しい is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. Read さみしい, the same spelling has 寂 write さみ. 寂れる uses さび with れる, and the noun 寂 is さび with no okurigana. In 静寂, 静 supplies セイ and 寂 supplies ジャク. In 寂然, 寂 supplies セキ and 然 supplies ゼン; JMdict also reads 寂然 じゃくねん and limits its second sense, "forlornness; desolation," to that reading.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジャク, with 寂滅 じゃくめつ, "achieving nirvana; death," 静寂 and 閑寂; セキ, with 寂然 and 寂として せきとして, "silently; hushed; in a still manner," noting that 寂然 is also read ジャクネン; さび, with 寂; さびしい, with 寂しい and 寂しがる さびしがる, "to miss someone; to miss something; to feel lonely"; and さびれる, with 寂れる. さみ.しい is not in the official table. Practice selects さび.しい, the reading with a linked word article; the other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 寂しい is 寂 with okurigana only.
- [督](督.md), "supervise; urge," has the same lower part as 寂, the 上-and-小 shape beside 又, here at its top. [宿](宿.md), "lodging," and [室](室.md), "room, chamber," have the same 宀 at the top. All are different kanji.
- [静](静.md), "quiet," [悲](悲.md), "sadness," [哀](哀.md), "sorrow; pity; pathos," [独](独.md), "alone; sole; Germany (abbreviation)," [乏](乏.md), "scarce; lacking," [死](死.md), "death," and [荒](荒.md), "rough; wild; laid waste," overlap the senses "quiet," "sad," "lonely," "lacking," "death of a priest" and "desolate" that 寂 and 寂しい carry; 静 appears with 寂 in 静寂, and JMdict lists 荒びれる as a rarely used spelling of 寂れる. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "quiet" or "lonely" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first ジャク sense: 寂寂 じゃくじゃく, "sad; lonesome; desolate," which JMdict also reads せきせき and さびさび; 寂然, read じゃくねん; and 寂寞 じゃくまく, "lonely; lonesome; dreary; desolate," which JMdict marks formal or literary and dated. Under セキ, Daijisen also gives 寂寞 read せきばく, which JMdict marks formal or literary.
