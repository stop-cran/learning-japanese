# 春 — spring season

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

春 means spring, the season. It can also suggest youth, as in 青春 せいしゅん.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the spreading upper strokes as new shoots above a warming [日](日.md), "sun," below. This is a mnemonic, not a reconstruction of the character's origin.

## Notes

The four seasons are 春, [夏](夏.md), [秋](秋.md), and [冬](冬.md). 春 names a season, not a water spring.
Compare [青](青.md): its upper and lower shapes differ from 春. [昼](昼.md) also contains 日, but has a 尺-shaped top and a bottom horizontal stroke.
