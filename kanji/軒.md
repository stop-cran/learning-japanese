# 軒 — eaves; building counter

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

軒 names the eaves of a building as のき and serves as a counter for buildings, especially houses, as ケン. The noun and counter are separate uses.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 車 on the left beside 干 on the right, and imagine a little counting board under the eaves of a house. This memory story is not etymology.

- 軒先 のきさき - edge of the eaves; house frontage

## Notes

- 一軒 is いっけん, with a small っ, not いちけん. This is a change within the whole counter expression, not an extra character reading.
- The linked 軒 word article teaches the counter けん. For the physical eaves, use のき; 軒先 shows that native reading inside a compound.
- The left-hand 車 distinguishes 軒 from [干](干.md). Compare [軟](軟.md), whose right side is 欠 rather than 干.
