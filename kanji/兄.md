# 兄 — older brother

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (no. 10) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

兄 means an older brother. The standalone word [兄](../words/兄.md) あに is commonly used for one's own older brother when speaking outside the family.
Japanese distinguishes older and younger siblings in its basic family words.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the [口](口.md) above two leg-like strokes is an older brother calling out.
This is an invented memory aid, not verified origin.

## Common words

- [兄](../words/兄.md) あに — older brother
- お兄さん おにいさん — older brother, as a respectful reference or family address
- 兄弟 きょうだい — siblings; brothers

## Notes

Learn お兄さん as a whole: its にい is not the あに reading with お and さん simply added.
Contrast [弟](弟.md) おとうと, "younger brother."
[元](元.md) has two lines above its legs; [見](見.md) has 目 rather than 口 above them.
