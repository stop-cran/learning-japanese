# 駐 — station; remain

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 馬 (no. 187) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

駐 concerns remaining or being stationed in a place. 駐車 is parking a vehicle, 駐車場 a car park, and 駐在 being stationed or posted somewhere.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the 馬-shaped left side stopped beside the 主-shaped post on the right. This is a modern-shape memory story, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical radical is 馬, no. 187, in its left-hand form.

## Notes

- 駐車 names parking, whereas 駐車場 names the parking place. The whole expression supplies the distinction between activity and location.
- Compare [住](住.md), live/reside: there is an area of residence-related meaning, so it is not a semantically clean wrong answer here. [柱](柱.md), pillar/post, and [注](注.md), pour/direct attention, have different whole meanings despite their 主-shaped parts.
