# 帳 — notebook; account book; register

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

帳 is a book for writing things down: a notebook, an account book, a register. An older sense, a hanging curtain, survives in a few words. [手帳](../words/手帳.md) てちょう is a pocket notebook or diary, and [通帳](../words/通帳.md) つうちょう is a passbook or bankbook. JMdict glosses 手帳 "notebook; memo pad; (pocket) diary" and 通帳 "passbook; bankbook"; 帳 on its own, read ちょう, is "book; register."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for チョウ. The first is a long hanging curtain, とばり, as in 緞帳 どんちょう, "thick curtain; drop curtain," 几帳 きちょう, "screen," 紙帳 しちょう, "paper mosquito net," 帳台 ちょうだい, "curtained sleeping platform in shinden-style residences (used by people of high rank)," and 開帳 かいちょう, "public exhibition (of a Buddhist image); displaying (a Buddhist image)," a sense JMdict restricts to the spelling 開帳. The second is a booklet for writing in, as in 帳簿 ちょうぼ, "account book; register; ledger," 帳面 ちょうめん, "notebook; account book; register," 記帳 きちょう, "registry; entry; book-keeping; signature," 台帳 だいちょう, "account book; ledger; register," and 大福帳 だいふくちょう, "(Edo-period) account book," as well as 通帳 and 手帳. Its kun reading is とばり, the same as KANJIDIC2, and it names 蚊帳 (かや) among words with a hard-to-guess reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 巾, the key, a shape like a hanging cloth, at the left, and [長](長.md), "long, chief," at the right. Imagine a long strip of cloth on which a shopkeeper writes every sale, the first account book. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 巾 or 長 contributes meaning or sound; 長 is also read チョウ. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チョウ | on | [手帳](../words/手帳.md) てちょう — pocket notebook; diary; [通帳](../words/通帳.md) つうちょう — passbook; bankbook |
| とばり | kun | 帳 とばり - curtain; hanging; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 手帳, 手 supplies て, its kun reading, and 帳 supplies チョウ, its on reading; this is a mixed reading, so 手帳 is not a purely native word. In 通帳, 通 supplies ツウ and 帳 supplies チョウ, with no sound change. As とばり, 帳 is written alone with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict lists 帳 first for とばり, with 帷, 幄 and 幌 as rarely used kanji forms, and its second sense, "veil (of darkness, mystery, etc.); mantle," is noted as used especially in 夜の帳 よるのとばり, "veil of darkness; curtain of night." 蚊帳 かや, "mosquito net," is a whole-word reading: it does not divide into a reading of 蚊 and a reading of 帳. JMdict also gives かちょう and ぶんちょう for 蚊帳, both marked as out-dated or obsolete kana usage.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows チョウ, with 帳面, 帳簿 and 通帳 as examples, and the special reading 蚊帳（かや）. とばり is not in the table. Practice selects チョウ, the reading of both linked word articles. とばり is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [手](手.md), "hand," in 手帳 and [通](通.md), "pass through; commute," in 通帳.
- [帽](帽.md), "hat; headgear," and [幅](幅.md), "width; breadth," have the same 巾 at the left, and [帯](帯.md), "sash; zone," has 巾 at the bottom. All are different kanji. [張](張.md), "stretch tight; assert," has the same right part 長 with a different left side and is also read チョウ.
- [録](録.md), "record; register," [籍](籍.md), "register; membership," [記](記.md), "record in writing," [誌](誌.md), "written record; journal," [冊](冊.md), "volume; book counter," [本](本.md), "book, origin," and [巻](巻.md), "roll; book volume," overlap the senses "register," "account book," "notebook" and "diary" that 帳 and its words carry; 記 appears with 帳 in 記帳. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "book" or "register" as exclusive to any one character.
- The curtain sense is shared with [幕](幕.md), "curtain; act of a play; shogunate," whose card covers it; this card's title keeps to the book sense, and 幕 is also kept out of this card's quiz options.
