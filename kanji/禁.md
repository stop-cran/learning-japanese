# 禁 — prohibit; ban

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 示 (no. 113) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

禁 concerns forbidding or restraining an action. In 禁止 it contributes prohibition; in 禁煙 it can concern a no-smoking rule or a person's abstaining from smoking. The full word and its subject identify the intended use.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Treat the 林-shaped top as two trees above a 示-shaped warning sign: imagine a notice forbidding entry. This invented scene is not the character's historical explanation.

示, no. 113, is the indexing key at the bottom. The 林-shaped upper part does not make 木 the canonical key.

## Notes

- With 禁止する, the prohibited action takes を; the passive 禁止されている means that it is prohibited. Inability to do something is not by itself a prohibition.
- 禁煙 on a sign means no smoking, while a person's 禁煙 can describe not smoking. Do not make the sign sense the only possible interpretation.
- Compare [林](林.md), grove, and [森](森.md), forest. Count the lower components rather than guessing the meaning from the trees alone.
