# 夢 — dream

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 夕 (no. 36) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

夢 is a dream during sleep, and also a hope for the future.
The word [夢](../words/夢.md) ゆめ covers both: 夢を見る (ゆめをみる) is "have a dream," while a person's 夢 may be an ambition.
悪夢 あくむ is a nightmare.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the layers above 夕 as images hovering over an evening scene. This is a present-shape memory aid, not a historical account.

## Notes

The indexing radical is 夕 at the bottom, not the grass-like strokes at the top.
[花](花.md) shares that upper outline, while [多](多.md) repeats an evening-shaped part.
[現実](../words/現実.md) げんじつ, "reality," contrasts with an imagined situation; a future dream need not be impossible to realize.
