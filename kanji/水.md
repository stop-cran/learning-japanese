# 水 — water

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

水 is a pictograph of flowing water: a central stream with droplets or currents on either side. It is one of the
most productive key components. On the left side of a kanji it shrinks to the three-drop form 氵 (さんずい), as in
海, 池, 泳, 洗. In 氷 (ice) the character is recognisably 水 with one extra stroke.

It is also one of the "five elements" in the weekday names: 水曜日 (Wednesday) goes with 火 ([火](火.md)), [木](木.md), 金 and 土.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| スイ | on | 水曜日 すいようび, [水中](../words/水中.md) すいちゅう, 水道 すいどう, 香水 こうすい |
| みず | kun | 水 みず (water), 水着 みずぎ, 水色 みずいろ |
| みず- | kun | prefix before a word, e.g. 水玉 みずたま |

## Common words

- [水中](../words/水中.md) すいちゅう — underwater
- [山水](../words/山水.md) さんすい — landscape (mountains and water)
- 水 みず — water (cold water; hot water is お湯 おゆ)

## Notes

Japanese distinguishes cold 水 from hot お湯 and from 氷 (ice); English "water" covers all three, so learn the
separate words. For rivers see [川](川.md).
