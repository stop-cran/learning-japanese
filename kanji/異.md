# 異 — different; unusual; strange

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

異 is being different from something else, and from there being out of the ordinary or strange. [異なる](../words/異なる.md) ことなる is to differ or be different, and [異常](../words/異常.md) いじょう is an abnormality or abnormal. JMdict glosses the noun 異 い, which has no word article here, "difference (of opinion)" and, as an adjective, "strange; odd; unusual."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for イ. One example of each:

- differing from others, being another one: 異論 いろん, "different opinion; objection," besides 異常;
- not being orthodox: 異端 いたん, "heresy; nonconformism; heathenism";
- being out of the ordinary or uncanny: 奇異 きい, "odd; strange; queer; peculiar";
- an unusual event: 天変地異 てんぺんちい, "natural disaster; cataclysm."

The entry also gives こと as a noun meaning another thing, and け as a classical adjective meaning out of the ordinary. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [田](田.md), "rice field," at the top, and a shape like [共](共.md), "together; shared," below it. Imagine one field held up apart from the others, plainly different from the rest. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 田 or 共 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| イ | on | [異常](../words/異常.md) いじょう — abnormality; abnormal |
| こと | kun | 異にする ことにする - to differ (in); to have different ...; to be different (in terms of ...); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| こと.なる | kun | [異なる](../words/異なる.md) ことなる — differ; be different |
| け | kun, source-only here | JMdict lists け beside こと for 異, "unusual; extraordinary," and Daijisen gives け only as a classical adjective with quotations from old texts; no ordinary modern example is verified here |

In 異常, 異 supplies イ and 常 supplies ジョウ. In 異なる, 異 writes こと and なる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 異にする, 異 also writes こと, followed by に and the verb する.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows イ, with 異論, 異同 いどう, "difference," and 奇異 as examples, and こと, with 異にする and 異なる. It has no separate row for こと.なる, which KANJIDIC2 lists apart from こと, and け is not in the table. Practice selects イ and こと.なる, the readings with linked word articles; こと is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary phrase, and け has no ordinary modern example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [常](常.md), "usual; constant," in 異常. The word 異なる contains only this kanji.
- [田](田.md), "rice field," is the key at the top, and [胃](胃.md), "stomach," has the same 田 at the top above a different shape; it is also read イ. [共](共.md), "together; shared," resembles the lower part of 異, and [囲](囲.md), "surround; enclose," is also read イ. All are different kanji.
- [違](違.md), "differ," [他](他.md), "other," [別](別.md), "separate, another," [差](差.md), "difference; gap," [変](変.md), "change; strange," [奇](奇.md), "strange; extraordinary," [妙](妙.md), "strange; exquisite," [珍](珍.md), "rare; unusual," and [怪](怪.md), "suspicious; uncanny," overlap the senses "different," "other," "difference," "unusual" and "strange" that 異 and its words carry; 違 is also read イ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "different" or "strange" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the イ senses: 異国 いこく, "foreign country," 異性 いせい, "the opposite sex," 異動 いどう, "(personnel) change; transfer; relocation; reassignment," 異例 いれい, "exceptional; unusual; extraordinary; unprecedented," and 差異 さい, "difference; disparity; gap"; 異教 いきょう, "religion other than one's own"; and 異様 いよう, "strange; odd; peculiar; weird," and 驚異 きょうい, "wonder; miracle; amazement."
