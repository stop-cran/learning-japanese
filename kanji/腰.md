# 腰 — waist; lower back

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

腰 concerns the lower back, waist or hip region. [腰](../words/腰.md) こし is not a precise English anatomical label in every context; 腰痛 ようつう normally concerns lower-back pain.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the body bending at the waist beneath the broad 要 shape on the right.

The key is 肉, no. 130, in the month-like 月 form on the left.

## Notes

- The separate texture use, often written コシ, concerns resilience or body, as in noodles. It does not describe a noodle's anatomical waist.
- [要](要.md) is the right-hand shape but has its own essential/need meanings. The visual comparison is not an etymological explanation.
- Do not automatically translate every painful 腰 as the hip joint; use the body region and context.
