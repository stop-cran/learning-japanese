# 刻 — carve; time

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (as 刂, no. 18) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

刻 has a cutting family and a time family. 刻む きざむ can mean chopping finely or engraving;
時刻 じこく means a time, especially a point on the clock.
In [遅刻](../words/遅刻.md) ちこく, the concern is arriving late.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine 刂 cutting small marks beside the slanting strokes on the left.
Use those marks to recall both carving and time markings, without treating the scene as historical evidence.

## Common words

- [遅刻](../words/遅刻.md) ちこく — lateness; arriving late
- 時刻 じこく — time; clock time
- 刻む きざむ — chop finely; engrave

## Notes

Time and engraving are separate meanings to recognize in context; 遅刻 does not mean "slow carving."
時刻 can specify a clock time, while [時間](../words/時間.md) じかん also covers an amount of time.
[列](列.md), [到](到.md), and [判](判.md) have the same right-hand 刂 but different left sides.
