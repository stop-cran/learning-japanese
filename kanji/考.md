# 考 — think over

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 老 (no. 125) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

考 concerns thinking about something, considering possibilities, and forming an idea or judgment.
[考える](../words/考える.md) (かんがえる) is the central everyday verb.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** picture the top leaning over a bent lower stroke like
a thinker leaning over a difficult problem. The classical radical classification is 老; the visible shape is not the full standalone 老.

## Notes

Contrast [思う](../words/思う.md) (おもう): 考える often emphasizes deliberation, while 思う commonly introduces
an opinion or feeling. This is a tendency, not a rule that one is always logical and the other always emotional.

[者](者.md) has a similar upper part but 日 below. [教](教.md) is more complex and adds a right-hand component;
do not read either as 考.
