# 互 — mutual

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 二 (no. 7) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

互 marks a relation going both ways. [互い](../words/互い.md) たがい means each other;
相互 そうご expresses mutuality or reciprocity.
The useful idea is not merely that two people are present, but that each is considered in relation to the other.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see the opposed turns between the top and bottom bars as two sides reaching toward one another.
This is a shape-based memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

互いに (たがいに) can describe an action directed toward one another; 互いの (たがいの) means "each other's."
The shape is particularly worth separating from [五](五.md).
[工](工.md) and [西](西.md) share only some of its broad horizontal structure, not its meaning.
The radical 二 is an indexing label; it is not a pronunciation instruction.
