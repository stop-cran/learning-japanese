# 略 — abbreviate; omit

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

略 concerns abbreviation, omission and a shortened outline. Its range also includes strategy and taking or plundering; the reading examples here focus on abbreviation and approximation rather than trying to turn every source gloss into an everyday standalone word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 田 beside 各 and imagine writing only a short outline in each box. This is a visual association, not a historical account or a phonetic analysis.

## Notes

- The approximate adverb [ほぼ](../words/ほぼ.md) is usually written in kana; JMdict flags 略 as a rare kanji spelling for that word. The spelling label does not make the pronunciation ほぼ rare.
- [略す](../words/略す.md) りゃくす is a transitive godan verb, abbreviate or omit. It illustrates リャク, not the separately retained はぶ.く lookup reading. The ordinary linked [省く](../words/省く.md) はぶく is a different written form.
- The noun 略 りゃく can mean abbreviation, outline or strategy. The linked [略語](../words/略語.md) specifically names a shortened word or expression.
