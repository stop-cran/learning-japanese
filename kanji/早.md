# 早 — early

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

早 concerns being early or doing something promptly. [早い](../words/早い.md) often compares a time with what is usual or expected.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see [日](日.md) above a [十](十.md)-shaped marker and imagine the sun already above the marker early in the day. This is not an origin claim.

## Common words

- 早朝 そうちょう — early morning
- 早起き はやおき — getting up early

## Notes

Compare 早い with 速い はやい: 早い usually concerns timing, while 速い emphasizes speed. Both become はやく in the adverbial form.
For shape, [古](古.md) has 十 above 口; 早 has 日 above 十.
