# 近 — near

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

近 means near or close: in space, time, or similarity. The ordinary adjective is [近い](../words/近い.md) ちかい.
最近 さいきん extends nearness to time: "recently; these days."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine following the sweeping lower path to the nearby 斤 shape.
This is a visual memory aid, not an etymology.
