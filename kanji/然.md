# 然 — so; in that state

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 火 (as 灬, no. 86) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

然 supplies an abstract idea of "so" or being in a particular state. It is best learned here
through whole words: [偶然](../words/偶然.md) ぐうぜん means "by chance," while 自然 (しぜん)
can mean nature or natural. Neither word should be translated by replacing 然 with "so."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the four bottom dots pinning down the scene above:
"it is so." This is a memory prompt, not a reconstruction of the character's origin.

## Notes

駅で偶然友達に会いました。 (えきでぐうぜんともだちにあいました。) — I happened to meet a friend at the station.
Here 偶然 describes how the meeting happened, without an added particle.
The upper right contains a [犬](犬.md) shape; unlike [熱](熱.md), it does not mean heat.
