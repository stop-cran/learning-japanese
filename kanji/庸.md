# 庸 — ordinary; commonplace

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

庸 expresses ordinariness in 凡庸. 中庸 (ちゅうよう) instead refers to the middle way or moderation.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look beneath 广 for the vertical strokes inside the lower box. Do not mistake that bottom for 車.

## Notes

[康](康.md) and [庫](庫.md) share the shelter shape; [周](周.md) has a different enclosing shape. [凡](凡.md), [常](常.md) and sometimes [並](並.md) can express the ordinary or usual. 凡庸 need not describe an average measured with numbers.
