# 徒 — on foot

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

徒 has several branches of meaning. 徒歩 とほ means travel on foot, while [生徒](../words/生徒.md)
せいと names a pupil. Learn these as different uses of the character, not as an assertion that a pupil
is someone who walks to school.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the 彳 left side stands beside a [走](走.md)-shaped right side.
Use that visible movement-like outline to remember 徒歩. The mnemonic is not etymology.

The source kun readings いたずら and あだ are preserved in metadata; they are not readings of 生徒.

## Common words

- [生徒](../words/生徒.md) せいと — pupil; student
- 徒歩 とほ — walking; going on foot

## Notes

徒歩 describes a way of travelling, not necessarily running, despite the visible 走 shape.
[待](待.md), [後](後.md), and [彼](彼.md) are useful 彳 look-alikes; attend to the entire right side.
