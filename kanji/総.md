# 総 — whole; overall; total

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

総 contributes the whole or overall total, as in 総額. Its native lookup readings include a word for everything and a different noun for a tuft or tassel; these do not all have the same spelling status.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 糸 beside a top shape over 心 and imagine gathering every thread into one whole bundle. This is a mnemonic for the modern shape, not a verified derivation of the meaning or sound.

The indexing key 糸, no. 120, appears on the left as ⺯.
