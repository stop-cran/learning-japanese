# 宗 — religion; sect

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宗 is a body of religious teaching and the group that follows it, and by extension what is central or held in the highest regard, such as the head house of a clan. [宗教](../words/宗教.md) しゅうきょう is glossed in JMdict "religion; religious affiliation; belief; faith; creed."

For シュウ, which it marks as a customary reading, the Daijisen character entry gives one sense, a school of doctrine or the body of people following it. Its examples include 宗派 しゅうは, "sect; denomination," and also "school (e.g. of poetry)"; 改宗 かいしゅう, "religious conversion"; 邪宗 じゃしゅう, "heresy; dangerous religion"; and 禅宗 ぜんしゅう, "Zen (Buddhism)."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ソウ. One example of each:

- a place where ancestors are worshipped: 宗廟 そうびょう, "ancestral shrine (temple); mausoleum (of one's ancestors); Imperial mausoleum";
- the center of a clan, the head house: 宗家 そうけ, "head of family; head house";
- revering as central, or a revered person: 宗匠 そうしょう, "master; teacher."

It also gives the kun readings むね and たっとぶ. KANJIDIC2 does not list たっとぶ, and JMdict's entry for たっとぶ, "to value; to prize; to esteem," is spelled 尊ぶ, 貴ぶ and 尚ぶ, not 宗. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the roof-shaped 宀 at the top and [示](示.md), "indicate," below it. Imagine a roof raised over a sign that indicates where the faithful of one sect gather. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 宀 or 示 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 宗教, 宗 supplies シュウ and 教 supplies キョウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シュウ, with 宗教, 宗派 and 改宗, and ソウ, with 宗家 and 宗匠. It does not list むね, which Kanjipedia marks as outside the table. Practice selects シュウ, the reading with a linked word article. ソウ is in the table, but no word article uses it yet; ソウ and むね are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [教](教.md), "teach, instruct," in 宗教.
- [示](示.md), "indicate," is the lower part of 宗. [宇](宇.md), "eaves; space," and [官](官.md), "government official," have the same 宀 at the top. All are different kanji.
- [信](信.md), "trust; belief," [仏](仏.md), "Buddha; Buddhism," [派](派.md), "faction; school of thought," [主](主.md), "master, principal," and [祖](祖.md), "ancestor," overlap the senses "religion" and "sect" and the Daijisen senses of ancestors and revered masters that 宗 and its words carry; JMdict's 宗教 includes "belief; faith," its 宗派 includes "school (e.g. of poetry)," and 主 is also read シュウ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "religion" or "sect" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ソウ senses: for the head of a clan, 宗族 そうぞく, "one's family or clan," and 祖宗 そそう, "ancestors"; for a revered person or what is held central, 宗主 そうしゅ, "suzerain," and 大宗 たいそう, "leading figure; foundation."
