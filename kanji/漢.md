# 漢 — Chinese, Sino-

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

漢 points to China or Chinese-related language and writing. In [漢字](../words/漢字.md), it identifies Chinese characters, including those used to write Japanese.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 氵 ([水](水.md)) beside a dense stack of horizontal strokes and a box. Imagine an elaborate sign marking a water route to China. This is a memory aid for the visible form, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

Do not confuse a character with a whole word: 漢字 names the writing units, whereas 漢語 can name vocabulary of Chinese origin.
Compare [洋](洋.md), which can mark Western style, and [海](海.md), "sea": all three share 氵, but their right-hand parts and meanings differ.
