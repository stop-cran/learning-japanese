# 屯 — garrison; gather in one place

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 屮 (no. 45) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

屯 occurs in words for troops staying in a place and people gathering. Friends hanging out and troops assembling are two different contexts for たむろする.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find the bent stroke extending to the lower right. Compare the whole four-stroke shape with the right side of 純.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| トン | on | [駐屯](../words/駐屯.md) ちゅうとん — being stationed (of troops) |
| たむろ | kun | 屯 (たむろ), a gathering or gathering place; outside the standard-use table. Usually written in kana, たむろする can mean that people gather or hang out, or that troops assemble or are quartered somewhere. |

## Notes

[純](純.md) adds thread on the left; [毛](毛.md) has different upper strokes and [申](申.md) has a box around the central stroke. [集](集.md) also means gather. [拾](拾.md) can mean gathering things by picking them up; 屯 is about people gathering. For the unit “ton,” use [トン](../words/トン.md) rather than treating 屯 as its normal spelling.
