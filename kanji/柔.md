# 柔 — soft; supple

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

柔 concerns softness, flexibility, or gentleness. 柔らかい やわらかい is an i-adjective, while 柔らか やわらか is a na-adjective. 柔道 じゅうどう is judo, and 柔和 にゅうわ means gentle or mild.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the 矛-like upper shape above 木 and imagine a supple branch bending rather than snapping. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified origin.

## Notes

- Before a noun, compare 柔らかな布 やわらかなぬの and 柔らかい布 やわらかいぬの, soft cloth. 柔らか takes な; the i-adjective 柔らかい attaches directly.
- The adjective 柔 やわ is usually written in kana as やわ; its meanings include weakness or fragility. It is not a rule that every soft thing is weak.
- 柔ら やわら is a separately listed noun for judo or jujutsu, not the inflectional stem of the linked i-adjective. This reading example provides recognition context without claiming it is the usual modern name for either martial art.
- The linked 柔道 article explains the sport. The character's association with softness is not a complete description of its techniques.
