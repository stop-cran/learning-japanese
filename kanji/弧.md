# 弧 — arc; curved line

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (no. 57), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弧 is an arc: a curved line shaped like a drawn bow, or a part of a circle's edge. [括弧](../words/括弧.md) かっこ, "brackets; parentheses," uses it for the curved shape of the marks. 円弧 えんこ means "circular arc," and 弧 こ alone is also a dictionary noun meaning "arc." The Daijisen character entry lists "bow" (ゆみ) as its first sense and the curved line as its second; that kun reading is not listed in KANJIDIC2, so it is not part of the metadata here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 弓, a bow, at the left and 瓜 at the right. Imagine the curve of a drawn bow. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 瓜 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| コ | on | [括弧](../words/括弧.md) かっこ — brackets; parentheses |

In 括弧, 弧 supplies コ and 括 supplies カツ; the sound is かっこ because カツ becomes かっ before コ. 円弧 えんこ, "circular arc," and 弧 こ also use コ. KANJIDIC2 lists no kun reading for 弧, so none is taught or selected here.

## Notes

- [括](括.md), "tie together; enclose," is the other kanji in 括弧.
- [引](引.md), "pull; draw," [弱](弱.md), "weak; frail," and [強](強.md), "strong," also have 弓 at the left, but their right-hand shapes differ and none of them means an arc.
- [曲](曲.md), "bend; musical piece," and [円](円.md), "yen, circle," overlap with 弧 in the ideas of curving and circles. An arc is a curved part of a circle, but 弧 names that curved line itself.
