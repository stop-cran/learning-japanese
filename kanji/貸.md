# 貸 — lend

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

貸 means lending: letting someone else use something temporarily. [貸す](../words/貸す.md) かす can also mean renting something out.
It describes the lender's side, unlike [借](借.md), which concerns borrowing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice [代](代.md) above 貝 and imagine a valuable item being placed in someone else's care.
This is an association for the modern components, not a claim about their historical roles.

## Notes

Keep the direction clear: 貸す is "lend," while [借りる](../words/借りる.md) かりる is "borrow."
[買](買.md), "buy," also has 貝 below, but its top is different and buying is not a temporary loan.
