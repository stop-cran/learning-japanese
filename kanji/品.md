# 品 — goods, refinement

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

品 often refers to goods or individual articles. It can also express refinement or dignity; not every use concerns merchandise.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the three [口](口.md)-shaped boxes as three items arranged on a display. This is a visual aid, not a claim about the character's historical origin.

- 商品 しょうひん — merchandise; goods for sale
- 食品 しょくひん — food products

## Notes

品物 uses two native readings, しな and もの. It is not read as a regular on-reading compound.

Count the three separate boxes in 品. [員](員.md), "member," has one 口 at the top and a different lower part, not two more boxes.
