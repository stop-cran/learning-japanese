# 礎 — cornerstone; foundation

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

礎 is the stone on which a building rests, and so the foundation of something. [基礎](../words/基礎.md) きそ is "foundation; basis," used for both physical and figurative foundations. The word 礎 いしずえ, JMdict entry 1396770, is "foundation stone; cornerstone"; it is the word itself and not one of the linked word articles.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ソ: the foundation stone on which a building's pillars rest, as in 礎石 そせき, "foundation stone; cornerstone," 定礎 ていそ, "laying a cornerstone (foundation stone)," and 基礎; and the root of something that makes it hold together, as in 国礎 こくそ, "pillar of state." It lists the kun reading いしずえ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [石](石.md), "stone," at the left. At the right, a pair of trees like [林](林.md), "grove; woodland," stands above a lower shape with no card here. Imagine a stone set under a post as the base of a wooden building. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that any part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 基礎, 基 supplies キ and 礎 supplies ソ. In 礎石, 礎 supplies ソ and 石 supplies セキ; in 定礎, 定 supplies テイ and 礎 supplies ソ. 礎 read いしずえ is a whole word written with this one kanji and no okurigana.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ソ, with 礎石, 基礎 and 定礎, and いしずえ, with 礎. Practice selects ソ, the reading of the linked word article. いしずえ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [基](基.md), "foundation; basis," in 基礎. It supplies キ and is also read もと. Its title overlaps this card's "foundation," so it is in the quiz exclusions. [根](根.md), "root; foundation," [底](底.md), "bottom; base," [台](台.md), "stand, machine counter," [本](本.md), "book, origin," [元](元.md), "origin, former," [源](源.md), "source; headwaters; origin," and [石](石.md), "stone," overlap the senses "foundation," "base," "root or origin" and "foundation stone" that 礎 carries. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- In the other words named above, [定](定.md) supplies テイ in 定礎 and 石 supplies セキ in 礎石.
- [研](研.md), "polish; study closely," [楽](楽.md), "music, enjoyment, ease," and [画](画.md), "picture, brushstroke," are crowded kanji, as 礎 is, and 研 also has 石 at the left, so they look plausible. They are read ケン and と.ぐ; ガク, ラク and たの.しい; and ガ, カク, エ and えが.く, and mean polish, music and picture. None shares a reading or a meaning with 礎.
- [祖](祖.md), [素](素.md), [粗](粗.md) and other kanji read ソ, like 礎, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
