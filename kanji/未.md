# 未 — not yet

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

未 often contributes "not yet": something has not reached a state or happened.
[未来](../words/未来.md) みらい is the future, what has not yet come.
未定 みてい means a matter has not yet been decided or fixed.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the upper horizontal is shorter, as though it has "not yet" grown
to the length of the line below. This is a visual reminder, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

[末](末.md) reverses the crucial proportions: its upper horizontal is longer.
[本](本.md) adds its short horizontal below the main crossing, and [木](木.md) lacks the extra horizontal.
未定 does not mean a proposal was rejected; it says that a decision is still pending.
