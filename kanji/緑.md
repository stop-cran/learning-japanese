# 緑 — green

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

緑 names the color green and can also mean greenery. The native noun みどり describes color with の before another noun; it is not an i-adjective.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 糸 beside the 彔-shaped right side and imagine green thread being gathered below its upper part. This is an invented visual association, not an etymology or a verified phonetic analysis.

The canonical key 糸, no. 120, appears on the left as ⺯.

## Notes

- Use 緑のシャツ みどりのシャツ, a green shirt, not an invented みどりい adjective. The linked native noun article also explains the greenery use.
- 緑青 also has the dictionary reading りょくしょう. The ロク row gives ろくしょう without claiming that the spelling has only one reading.
- [青](青.md) あお can cover green in conventional uses, especially plants, fruit and traffic lights. It is not freely interchangeable with 緑 in every color description, and it is not a safe curated wrong answer here.
