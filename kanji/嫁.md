# 嫁 — bride; daughter-in-law; marry into

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

嫁 is a woman who marries into another family: the bride, and the daughter-in-law she becomes in her husband's household. [嫁](../words/嫁.md) よめ is glossed in JMdict "bride" and "(one's) daughter-in-law"; its third sense, which JMdict marks as Kansai dialect, is "wife." [花嫁](../words/花嫁.md) はなよめ is "bride." The verb 嫁ぐ とつぐ is "to marry (of a woman); to get married (to); to marry into (a family)," a sense JMdict marks as dated.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for カ: a woman marrying into another family, as in 降嫁 こうか, "marriage of an Imperial princess to a subject," and 再嫁 さいか, "remarriage (of a woman)," which JMdict marks as formal or literary; and shifting blame onto others, as in 転嫁 てんか, "imputation; shifting (blame, responsibility, etc.); passing the buck." Its よめ examples include 兄嫁 あによめ, "elder brother's wife; sister-in-law," and 花嫁. Among hard-to-read words it lists 許嫁 いいなずけ, "fiancé; fiancée," which JMdict marks as a gikun reading, one given to the whole word rather than to each kanji. Its kun readings are よめ and とつぐ; it does not show い.く or ゆ.く.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [女](女.md), "woman, female," at the left and [家](家.md), "house, home," at the right. Imagine a woman stepping into a new house as its bride. 家 is also read カ; that overlap is stated only as a fact. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 女 or 家 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

The word 嫁 is this kanji alone, read よめ. In 花嫁, 花 supplies はな and 嫁 supplies よめ, with no sound change. In 嫁ぐ, 嫁 writes とつ and ぐ is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 嫁き遅れ いきおくれ or ゆきおくれ, 嫁 writes い or ゆ and the き after it is okurigana. JMdict has no entry spelled 嫁く.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カ, with 再嫁, 転嫁 and 嫁する かする, "to wed; to be married; to shift blame to someone else"; よめ, with 嫁 and 花嫁; and とつぐ, with 嫁ぐ and 嫁ぎ先 とつぎさき, "family a woman has married into." It does not list い.く or ゆ.く. Practice selects よめ, the reading with linked word articles. カ and とつ.ぐ are in the table, but no word article uses them yet; カ, とつ.ぐ, い.く and ゆ.く are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [花](花.md), "flower," in 花嫁. The word 嫁 contains only this kanji.
- [好](好.md), "liking; favorable," [妨](妨.md), "hinder; obstruct; interfere," and [姓](姓.md), "surname; family name," have the same 女 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [妻](妻.md), "wife," [婦](婦.md), "woman; wife," and [婚](婚.md), "marriage," overlap the senses "bride," "wife" and "marry into" that 嫁 and its words carry; 婦 is also read よめ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "wife" or "marriage" as exclusive to any one character.
