# 幅 — width; breadth

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

幅 names width or breadth and can extend to a range or difference. 幅 はば is the noun for that dimension; 増幅 ぞうふく means amplification. The noun is not the adjective 広い ひろい, wide.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 巾 beside a right-hand shape ending in 田; imagine measuring a wide piece of hanging cloth. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a verified origin or phonetic claim.
