# 土 — earth, soil

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

土 is usually explained as a pictograph of a mound of earth (or an altar mound) on the ground line. The earliest forms are debated, so take this as the standard reading and not as certain.
It means "earth, soil, ground" and also "land, country" (本土 ほんど, mainland) and Saturday (土曜日, Saturn day).
As a radical it appears in many kanji about ground and places: 地 (ground), 場 (place), 坂 (slope), 塩 (salt), 城 (castle).

## Notes

Look-alike: 士 (samurai, gentleman): in 土 the top horizontal is the shorter one, in 士 it is the longer one.
Also [上](上.md) (up): its short horizontal stroke is attached to the side of the vertical, above a long base; in 土 the short bar is above a long one and the vertical passes through both. Compare [金](金.md), which contains 土 in the lower part, and 在 (exist), which has 土 at the bottom.
The weekday series 火水木金土 ([火](火.md) [水](水.md) [木](木.md) [金](金.md) 土) ends the five elements.
