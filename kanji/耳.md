# 耳 — ear

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 耳 (no. 128) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

耳 is commonly explained as a pictograph of an ear; the earliest forms are more rounded and the modern shape is a stylised box with strokes inside.
It means "ear" and, as in English, "hearing" (耳がいい, good hearing).
As a component it appears in characters about hearing and listening: [聞](聞.md) (hear, ask), 取 (take), 恥 (shame), 職 (job).

## Notes

Expressions: 耳を傾ける みみをかたむける (to listen attentively), 耳にする みみにする (to hear (about)), 耳が痛い みみがいたい (to hear something uncomfortably true).

Look-alikes: [目](目.md) (eye) is a closed box; 耳 has its horizontal lines extending past the box. 取 (take) is 耳 with 又 added on the right, a classic confusion.
