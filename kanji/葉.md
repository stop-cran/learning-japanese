# 葉 — leaf

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

葉 is a leaf, including a blade of grass or a pine needle. The familiar word [言葉](../words/言葉.md) ことば
means a word, expression, or language: its meaning is not obtained by literally translating 葉 as "leaf."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture leaves above the 木 at the bottom. The plant-shaped top
is 艹, the visible form of the indexing radical 艸. This picture is a modern memory aid, not an origin claim.

- 紅葉 こうよう — autumn colors; reddening leaves

## Notes

In 言葉, the ending is ば, not は: learn the complete reading ことば.
The middle and bottom of 葉 distinguish it from [落](落.md) and [薬](薬.md).
[業](業.md) also has a dense upper half and spreading lower strokes, but lacks the same plant top.
The resemblance is only a handwriting reminder.
