# 偏 — lean to one side; biased

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

偏 means being weighted toward one side or departing from the center, and by extension being biased. A common example is [偏見](../words/偏見.md) へんけん, prejudice or bias. Digital Daijisen also lists the sense “narrow,” as in 偏狭, and the left-hand component of a kanji, as in 人偏.

## Common words

- [偏見](../words/偏見.md) へんけん — prejudice; bias
- 偏差値 へんさち — deviation value; standard score, often used for academic grades
- 偏り かたより — deviation; inclination; bias

## Notes

JMdict lists 偏る and [片寄る](../words/片寄る.md) as two spellings of the same かたよる; both spellings can describe physical imbalance or a biased view. 片 (“one-sided; sheet”) shares the one-sided idea, 傾 (“tilt; incline”) overlaps with leaning, and KANJIDIC2 lists “side” among the meanings of both 偏 and 傍 (“beside; nearby”). These characters overlap with part of 偏's meaning range without being interchangeable.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 魚 (“fish”), 雨 (“rain”), and 耳 (“ear”); none means leaning to one side or being biased.
