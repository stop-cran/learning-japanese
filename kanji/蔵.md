# 蔵 — storehouse; keep in storage

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

蔵 concerns a storehouse or things kept in storage. In 冷蔵庫 れいぞうこ, storage combines with cooling and a storage unit to name a refrigerator.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a storehouse concealed beneath the 艹 roof, with the inner 臣-shaped section standing guard over what is kept inside. This is a modern visual association, not a historical derivation.

The indexing radical 艸 has the visible form 艹 at the top.

## Notes

- The noun くら can also be written 倉 or [庫](庫.md). Compare the storehouse character 蔵 with [臓](臓.md), internal organs: the left-hand 肉 form 月 belongs to 臓, not 蔵.
