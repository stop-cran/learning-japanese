# 丁 — city block; even number; fourth

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

丁 is a character of counting and order: it marks even numbers, the fourth item in a series and a block of a town, and it counts leaves of a book and some objects. It also appears in words for courtesy. [丁寧](../words/丁寧.md) ていねい is polite or careful, and [包丁](../words/包丁.md) ほうちょう is a kitchen knife. JMdict glosses 丁寧 "polite; courteous; civil" and, in a second sense, "careful; close; thorough; conscientious."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for チョウ. One example of each:

- an even number: 丁半 ちょうはん, "even and odd numbers (on dice)";
- a leaf of bound paper: 落丁 らくちょう, "missing page";
- the sound of things striking: 丁丁発止 ちょうちょうはっし, "with (the sound of) clashing swords."

The Daijisen character entry gives six senses for テイ. One example of each:

- the fourth heavenly stem, ひのと: 丁酉 ていゆう, "Fire Rooster (34th term of the sexagenary cycle, e.g. 1957, 2017, 2077)";
- fourth in rank: 丁夜 ていや, "fourth division of the night (approx. 1am to 3am)," which JMdict marks archaic;
- a grown man: 丁年 ていねん, "adulthood (i.e. age 20 in Japan); one's majority";
- a man employed to work for others: 園丁 えんてい, "gardener";
- the shape of 丁: 丁字路 ていじろ, "T junction";
- courtesy: 丁重 ていちょう, "polite; courteous; hospitable; respectful," besides 丁寧.

A separate Daijisen entry for the noun ちょう adds a division of a town, as in 丁目 ちょうめ, "nth district of a town; nth city block," and uses 丁 as a counter, for example for blocks of tofu, as in 豆腐一丁 とうふいっちょう, "one cake of bean curd." The character entry lists no チン, トウ or チ reading, though it names 丁幾 チンキ among words with irregular readings. A separate Daijisen headword よほろ, people conscripted for public labour in ancient times, lists 丁 as a spelling outside the official table; KANJIDIC2 does not list よほろ, and JMdict has no standalone entry for it; it appears only in つかえのよほろ, one reading of 仕丁 しちょう, which JMdict marks archaic and glosses "men pressed into forced labor (ritsuryō system)." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [一](一.md), "one," as the stroke across the top, and below it a single vertical stroke ending in a hook. Imagine a straight street on a map with a side street meeting it from below: a T junction that marks off a city block. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 一 or the hooked stroke contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 丁寧, 丁 supplies テイ and 寧 supplies ネイ. In 包丁, 包 supplies ホウ and 丁 supplies チョウ. Neither word has okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 丁々, both kanji are read トウ; 々 repeats the kanji before it. The word 丁 ひのと is the kanji alone; JMdict also reads it てい in its "fourth rank" sense, and in 丁酉, 丁 is also read ひのと, giving ひのととり.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows チョウ, with 丁数 ちょうすう, "number of leaves (in a book, esp. one with traditional Japanese-style binding); number of sheets," 落丁 and 二丁目 にちょうめ, the second 丁目, as examples; and テイ, with 丁字路 and 甲乙丙丁 こうおつへいてい, "A, B, C and D; first, second, third and fourth." チン, トウ, チ and ひのと are not in the table. Practice selects チョウ and テイ, the readings with linked word articles; トウ and ひのと are illustrated with dictionary words, and チン and チ have no verified ordinary example. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [寧](寧.md), "polite; peaceful," in 丁寧 and [包](包.md), "wrap; enclose," in 包丁.
- [打](打.md), "strike," has 丁 at the right, after 扌. [丙](丙.md), "third rank; third heavenly stem," also has 一 at the top and is the heavenly stem before 丁. [了](了.md), "completion; settling," is a similar two-stroke shape that ends in a hooked vertical stroke. All are different kanji.
- [町](町.md), "town, neighborhood," [区](区.md), "ward; district," [偶](偶.md), "chance; even numbers," and [四](四.md), "four," overlap the senses "city block," "even number" and "fourth" that 丁 carries; JMdict also lists 横町 as a spelling of 横丁 よこちょう, "bystreet; side street; back street." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "city block" or "even number" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the チョウ and テイ senses: 乱丁 らんちょう, "incorrect collating; pages out of order"; 壮丁 そうてい, "youth; young man who has reached adulthood (conscription age)"; 使丁 してい, "servant; janitor; messenger," 廷丁 ていてい, "court attendant," and 馬丁 ばてい, "stable boy; stable hand; groom," which JMdict marks dated; and 丁字形 ていじけい, "T-shaped."
