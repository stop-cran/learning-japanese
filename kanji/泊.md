# 泊 — stay overnight; anchor

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

泊 concerns lodging overnight or a vessel staying at anchor. 泊まる とまる is intransitive, stay overnight, and 泊める とめる is transitive, put someone up. 宿泊 しゅくはく names lodging; 停泊 ていはく names anchoring or mooring.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside 白 and imagine a boat resting beside a pale-lit shore for the night. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not verified etymology.

## Notes

- Compare 友達が家に泊まる ともだちがいえにとまる, a friend stays at the house, with 友達を家に泊める ともだちをいえにとめる, put a friend up at the house.
- 泊まる is godan, with 泊まって とまって; 泊める is ichidan, with 泊めて とめて. The latter is a transitive verb, not the grammatical causative form of the former.
- 止まる とまる means stop. Identical pronunciation does not make a stopping train an overnight guest.
- 宿泊 and 泊 share a lodging meaning, so 宿 is a useful comparison rather than a reliable wrong meaning choice.
