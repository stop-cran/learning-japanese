# 徐 — slowly; gradually

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

徐 is moving slowly and calmly, a little at a time. [徐々に](../words/徐々に.md) じょじょに means "gradually; by degrees"; JMdict glosses it "gradually; steadily; slowly; little by little; step by step; by degrees."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ジョ, slow and unhurried, with examples such as 徐行 じょこう, "going slowly (esp. to enable a vehicle to stop immediately); going at a reduced speed; slowing down; reducing speed," 徐徐 じょじょ, "slow; gradual; steady," which JMdict marks archaic, and 緩徐 かんじょ, "gentle and quiet." It gives the kun reading おもむろ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 彳 at the left, a narrow shape of three strokes (it has no card here), and [余](余.md), "surplus," at the right. Imagine someone on the road with time to spare, walking slowly. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 彳 or 余 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジョ | on | [徐々に](../words/徐々に.md) じょじょに — gradually; by degrees |
| おもむ.ろに | kun | 徐に おもむろに - slowly; without haste; calmly; gently; deliberately; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 徐々に, 徐 supplies ジョ, 々 repeats the kanji, so 徐々 reads じょじょ, and に is the adverb ending. JMdict also spells the word 徐徐に, and lists 除々に and 除除に as irregular spellings. In 徐に, 徐 writes おもむろ and に is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict also spells it 徐ろに. JMdict says おもむろに is usually written in kana. The gloss above is its second sense, marked "original meaning"; its first sense is "suddenly; abruptly," with the note "sometimes considered non-standard."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ジョ, with 徐行 and 徐々に as examples. おもむ.ろに is not in the official table. Practice selects ジョ, the reading with a linked word article; おもむ.ろに is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- [余](余.md), "surplus," is the right part of 徐. [途](途.md), "route; course," has the same 余 inside it, and [徒](徒.md), "on foot," has the same 彳 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [除](除.md), "remove; exclude," also reads ジョ and has the same 余 at the right. It is a different kanji; quiz options for this card do not include it.
- [遅](遅.md), "late; slow," and [悠](悠.md), "leisurely; distant," overlap the sense "slow, unhurried" that 徐 carries. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "slow" as exclusive to any one character.
