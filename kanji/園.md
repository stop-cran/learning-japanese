# 園 — park; garden

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

園 appears in words for parks and gardens. In [公園](../words/公園.md) こうえん it is a public park; in 動物園 どうぶつえん it is a zoo.
The first part of the compound tells you what kind of place is meant.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** regard the outer enclosure as a park boundary around the detailed 袁 shape inside. It is a visual aid, not proof that the character originated as a plan of a park.

- 庭園 ていえん — garden
- 動物園 どうぶつえん — zoo

## Notes

The radical is the enclosing 囗, not the mouth-shaped part inside.
Compare [国](国.md), [困](困.md), and [回](回.md): the outside frame alone cannot identify the character.
[公演](../words/公演.md) also reads こうえん, but means a public performance, not a park.
