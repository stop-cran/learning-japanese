# 損 — loss; damage

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

損 concerns loss, damage or disadvantage. 損 そん can describe losing out, while 損害 そんがい names damage or loss. The native verbs 損なう そこなう and 損ねる そこねる can describe harm; attached to another verb stem, related forms can express failing to do something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside 員, including a lower 貝 shape, and imagine a hand counting something lost. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical explanation.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.

## Notes

- The source records both そこ.なう and そこな.う. The former corresponds to 損なう; JMdict flags 損う for the latter as irregular okurigana. These spellings do not establish two different verb pronunciations.
- The suffix -そこなう follows a verb's ます-stem: 取り とり plus 損なう gives 取り損なう. 見損ねる likewise combines 見 み, the stem of 見る みる, with 損ねる.
- 損なう is godan ending in う; 損ねる is ichidan. Compare 損なわない そこなわない with 損ねない そこねない.
- The suffix also has an almost-do sense in the dictionary. The selected compound examples above illustrate failing or missing, not a rule to translate every suffix occurrence the same way.
