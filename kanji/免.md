# 免 — exempt; be spared

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (no. 10) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

免 concerns exemption, release from an obligation, or being spared something
unwelcome. [免税](../words/免税.md) めんぜい is tax exemption;
[免れる](../words/免れる.md) まぬがれる means escaping harm or avoiding
an unwelcome outcome. In [免許](../words/免許.md) めんきょ, learn the
whole word as a license or official permission. The character also has
a dismissal-from-office sense; one English gloss does not fit every compound.

The indexing radical 儿 appears in the two leg-like lower strokes.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture those legs stepping clear of an obligation
under the hooked upper shape and box-like middle. This is an invented spelling
aid, not a historical derivation or a phonetic analysis.

Both まぬかれる and まぬがれる are valid. Practice selects まぬがれる
to avoid two variant questions for the same written verb; this does not
label まぬかれる rare or incorrect. All sourced readings remain in their
original metadata order. The dot marks where the written ending れる begins.

## Notes

- [兄](兄.md), "older brother," has 口 above its legs;
  [元](元.md), "origin, former," has two horizontal lines.
  [先](先.md), "previous; ahead," has a different upper shape.
- [許](許.md), "permit; forgive," overlaps in permission-related meanings.
  It is not a freely interchangeable spelling of 免.
- [逃](逃.md), "escape; flee," overlaps in escape contexts. 免れる does
  not require physical flight: 責任を免れる (せきにんをまぬがれる)
  means "evade responsibility."
