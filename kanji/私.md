# 私 — I; private

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

As the word [私](../words/私.md) (わたし), this character means "I" or "me." In compounds it often concerns
what is private or personal, as opposed to public.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** picture the grain-shaped 禾 beside the small ム shape
as a bundle someone has marked "mine."

## Common words

- [私](../words/私.md) わたし — I; me
- 私立 しりつ — privately established, as in a private school
- 私生活 しせいかつ — private life

## Notes

わたし is a useful choice in polite introductions for speakers of any gender. In casual conversation its social
feel depends on the speaker and setting; it is not the only Japanese first-person pronoun.

Contrast 私立 with [公立](../words/公立.md) (こうりつ, public institution). [公](公.md) is a useful
public/private comparison. [秋](秋.md) shares 禾 but has 火 on the right; [広](広.md) contains ム under a different top.
