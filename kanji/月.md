# 月 — moon, month

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 月 (no. 74) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

月 is a pictograph of a crescent moon. Old forms show the crescent with a mark inside it, usually explained as
shading on the moon or a way to tell it apart from 夕 (evening), which historically descends from the same picture.
Like 日 ([日](日.md)), it serves for both a celestial body and the time unit it measures: a lunar cycle gives "month", and
つき is both "moon" and "month" in Japanese.

Two things to keep apart:

- In 明 (bright) it is really the moon, paired with the sun: 日 + 月.
- In many body-part kanji (e.g. 腕, 脳) the 月 on the left is not the moon but the flesh radical 肉 (にくづき), which
  has a similar shape in modern print.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ゲツ | on | 月曜日 げつようび, 今月 こんげつ, 来月 らいげつ |
| ガツ | on | month names: 一月 いちがつ, 十二月 じゅうにがつ; 正月 しょうがつ |
| つき | kun | 月 つき (moon, month), 月日 つきひ, 三日月 みかづき (voiced and shortened) |

ガツ is used for the numbered month names (一月–十二月); ゲツ for "month" as a counted span (一ヶ月 いっかげつ) and the weekday.

## Common words

- [月日](../words/月日.md) つきひ／がっぴ — time passing; calendar date
- 月曜日 げつようび — Monday
- 毎月 まいつき／まいげつ — every month (both readings are used)

## Notes

For the weekday series see [日](日.md); 月 pairs with [日](日.md) as the "sun and moon" of 日月.
