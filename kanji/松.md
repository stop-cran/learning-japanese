# 松 — pine tree

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

松 writes **pine tree**, [松](../words/松.md) まつ. In the conventional three-tier 松・竹・梅 labels, it can also identify the highest option, not something made from pine.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside [公](公.md). Imagine a pine tree in a public garden. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical explanation of the character.

## Notes

- In 松竹梅, the on readings combine as ショウ + チク + バイ. The individual plant names are 松 まつ, [竹](../words/竹.md) たけ and [梅](../words/梅.md) うめ.
- A menu's 松 is a conventional rank label, not a universal promise about ingredients, price or quality. Read the actual options.
- Compare [公](公.md), public or official, without 木; [村](村.md), village, has 寸 on the right, while [相](相.md), mutual or aspect, has 目.
