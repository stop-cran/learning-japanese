# 匿 — hide; shelter

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 匸 (no. 23) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

匿 means to hide or conceal, and to keep someone hidden. A common example is [匿名](../words/匿名.md) とくめい, anonymity.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| トク | on | [匿名](../words/匿名.md) とくめい — anonymity; using an assumed name (JMdict entry 1454470); 隠匿 いんとく — concealment, especially of wrongdoing or criminals (JMdict entry 1170790) |
| かくま.う | kun | 匿う かくまう — shelter or harbor someone, such as a fugitive; usually written in kana (JMdict entry 1454460). Not selected for questions here. |

## Common words

- [匿名](../words/匿名.md) とくめい — anonymity; using an assumed name
- 隠匿 いんとく — concealment, especially of wrongdoing or criminals
- 匿う かくまう — shelter; harbor (usually written in kana)

## Notes

隠 (“hide; conceal”) appears with 匿 in 隠匿, and 秘 (“secret; conceal”) and 密 (“secret; dense; close”) share the idea of secrecy. 潜 (“dive; submerge; lurk”) and 伏 (“lie prostrate; lurk; yield”) can describe hiding or lurking. 皮 (“skin; hide”) shares only the English word “hide,” there meaning an animal skin.

JMdict lists 匿す as a rarely used spelling of 隠す かくす, “to hide; to conceal” (entry 1170650), and Kanjipedia gives ジョク and かく.れる for 匿, marked as outside the Joyo table (外). None of these readings is in this card's metadata, and none is quizzed.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 父 (“father”), 魚 (“fish”), and 年 (“year”); none means hiding or sheltering.
