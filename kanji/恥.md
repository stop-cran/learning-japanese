# 恥 — shame

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

恥 covers shame and embarrassment. The noun 恥 はじ names the feeling or disgrace;
[恥ずかしい](../words/恥ずかしい.md) はずかしい can describe embarrassment without implying a serious
moral fault.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** [耳](耳.md), "ear," stands beside [心](心.md); imagine hearing a remark
that makes you blush. This is a learner's memory aid, not historical evidence.
The radical is 心 even though 耳 is visually prominent on the left.

## Notes

Keep the spelling 恥ずかしい: the kana after 恥 are ずかしい, even though the noun is pronounced はじ.
[聞](聞.md) surrounds 耳 with a gate shape; 恥 puts 心 beside it.
[耳](耳.md) alone and [息](息.md) share only selected parts of the outline.
