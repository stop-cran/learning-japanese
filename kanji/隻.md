# 隻 — ship counter; one of a pair

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

隻 is used to count ships and can refer to one half of a pair. 一隻 counts one ship; the character is not the ordinary noun for a boat.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See a 隹-shaped bird above a 又-shaped hand holding just one item. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical account.

The canonical radical is 隹, no. 172, in the upper part.

## Notes

- In 一隻 いっせき, the 一 part takes いっ before セキ. Learn the whole numeral-counter form rather than pronouncing it いちせき.
- JMdict also records a counter for half of a pair, such as half of a folding screen. Its bundle-item counter for fish, birds or arrows is marked archaic; that does not make the ship-counter sense archaic.
- Compare [集](集.md), gather, which also contains 隹 but has a different lower part and whole-character meaning.
