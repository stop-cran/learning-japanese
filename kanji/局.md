# 局 — bureau; office

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

局 appears in names of offices and service organizations. 郵便局 ゆうびんきょく is a post office;
[薬局](../words/薬局.md) やっきょく is a pharmacy, not a generic office.
The first part of each word tells you what kind of organization or service is involved.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a little 口-shaped service window tucked below the 尸 top and inside the turning outer line.
This uses the current shape for memory, not historical evidence.

## Common words

- [薬局](../words/薬局.md) やっきょく — pharmacy
- 郵便局 ゆうびんきょく — post office
- 放送局 ほうそうきょく — broadcasting station; broadcaster

## Notes

薬局 is やっきょく, with a small っ before きょ.
[同](同.md) and [古](古.md) also contain a clear 口, but their upper and outer lines are different.
[写](写.md) is a looser visual comparison: it has a covering top and folded lower strokes, not the enclosed 口 of 局.
