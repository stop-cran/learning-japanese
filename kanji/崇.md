# 崇 — revere; worship

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

崇 expresses reverence. 崇拝 (すうはい) can be worship of a deity or deep adoration of a person.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 山 sits above 宗. Picture looking up at the mountain rather than looking down at an object.

## Notes

[山](山.md) is only the top part of 崇. [安](安.md) has 女 under its roof; [京](京.md) has a box above its lower strokes, not 示. Compare [尊](尊.md), [敬](敬.md) and [仰](仰.md) for reverence, [尚](尚.md) and [貴](貴.md) for esteem, and [拝](拝.md) and [祭](祭.md) for worship. A respectful greeting need not amount to worship.
