# 注 — pour, direct attention

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

注 can mean pouring liquid or directing attention toward something. It also occurs in words for notes and annotations. [注意](../words/注意.md) is the practical word for attention or caution.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** combine the visible water side 氵 ([水](水.md)) with [主](主.md) on the right. Imagine pouring water onto one main spot, then directing your attention to that spot. This is a mnemonic, not a claim about the character's origin.
