# 走 — run

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 走 (no. 156) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

走 means running. [走る](../words/走る.md) はしる describes a person running and can also describe a vehicle moving along.
It is not limited to movement on human feet.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper cross-like part as a runner's body and the long lower sweep as a leg
stretching forward. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a reconstruction of an ancient form.

- [走る](../words/走る.md) はしる — run; travel along
- 競走 きょうそう — race
- 走行 そうこう — running or travel of a vehicle

## Notes

[足](足.md) means "foot; leg" and has a box-shaped top; 走 does not.
[起](起.md) adds a component beside the 走 shape.
走る is a godan verb despite the i-sound before る: learn 走ります はしります and 走って はしって.
