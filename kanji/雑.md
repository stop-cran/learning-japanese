# 雑 — miscellaneous

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

雑 points to a mixture or assortment rather than one neatly separated kind.
[雑草](../words/雑草.md) ざっそう means weeds; 雑誌 (ざっし) is a magazine, and 雑音 (ざつおん)
is noise or interference. These established compounds need their own meanings, not a repeated translation of "miscellaneous."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 九-over-木 block on the left as a mixed pile beside the right-hand 隹.
This describes the modern outline only; it does not explain the character's historical construction.
