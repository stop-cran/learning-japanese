# 島 — island

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

島 means an island. 孤島 ことう is an isolated island; 半島 はんとう is a peninsula, not a completely separate island. Learn the whole compound rather than treating every occurrence of 島 as an isolated landmass.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the bird-like upper shape above 山 and imagine a bird looking down at an island mountain. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical account of the character.

## Notes

- The native reading can be voiced in a compound: 小島 こじま means small island. This example does not make every 島 in a name じま.
- Compare [鳥](鳥.md), bird, and [山](山.md), mountain: neither alone has the island meaning.
