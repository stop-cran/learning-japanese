# 妙 — strange; exquisite

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

妙 can suggest something unusual or something wonderfully skillful and fine.
[奇妙](../words/奇妙.md) きみょう is strange or curious; [妙](../words/妙.md)
みょう can describe strangeness, but also excellence or a clever touch.
The context determines whether the unusual quality is puzzling or admirable.

[微妙](../words/微妙.md) びみょう means subtle or delicate; in conversational
judgments it can also mean iffy or not very good. Those are meanings of the
complete word, not a rule that 妙 always expresses approval.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice [女](女.md) beside
[少](少.md). Imagine a tiny, exquisitely made ornament beside the 女-shaped
outline. This is an invented visual cue, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ミョウ | on | [妙](../words/妙.md) みょう - strange; exquisite; [奇妙](../words/奇妙.md) きみょう - strange; [微妙](../words/微妙.md) びみょう - subtle; delicate |
| ビョウ | on | 神妙 しんびょう - marvelous; meek or docile according to sense; an outdated reading, for recognition only |
| たえ | kun | 妙なる たえなる - exquisite; enchanting; no word article yet |

Practice selects ミョウ. The dictionary records しんびょう for 神妙 but marks
that reading as outdated or obsolete; the primary reading is しんみょう.
Do not treat ビョウ as freely interchangeable with ミョウ in the linked words.
The qualification concerns this example, not a claim that every ビョウ use is obsolete.

In 妙なる, 妙 supplies たえ and なる is written in kana. This dictionary
expression explains the retained kun reading without turning it into an
unsupported reading question.

## Notes

- [砂](砂.md), sand, and [秒](秒.md), second, also contain 少 but have
  different left components. [姉](姉.md), older sister, instead shares 女.
- Strangeness overlaps [奇](奇.md), [変](変.md) and [珍](珍.md); excellence
  overlaps [美](美.md) and [優](優.md). Subtlety connects 妙 with [微](微.md).
  Daijisen also records a youth sense, so [若](若.md) is not an unambiguous
  wrong meaning answer either.
- JMdict also gives the word 妙 みょう a skill or cleverness sense, which
  overlaps [技](技.md), skill/technique, and [腕](腕.md), arm/skill.
  These direct comparisons are excluded from meaning choices too.
- The obsolete reading みみょう of 微妙 belongs to a separate dictionary
  entry. It is not the reading used in the linked modern びみょう examples.
