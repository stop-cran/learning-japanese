# 覆 — cover; overturn

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 襾 (no. 146) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

覆 has two main meaning groups: covering something, and turning something over. In [覆う](../words/覆う.md) おおう, it is a transitive verb that JMdict glosses as “to cover; to hide; to conceal; to wrap; to disguise.” The overturning sense appears in 覆す くつがえす, “to overturn; to capsize; to upset,” which JMdict also glosses “to reverse (decision etc.).” Kanjipedia adds a third sense, くりかえす (to repeat).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the shape 覀 sits on top like a lid, and [復](復.md), "restore; repeat," sits below it. Imagine putting a lid over something, flipping it over, and putting it back again and again: cover, overturn, repeat. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 覀 or 復 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists フク, おおう, くつがえす and くつがえる. Kanjipedia marks くつがえす and くつがえる 高 (a school-level mark, not an outside-Joyo mark). KANJIDIC2 gives no name readings for 覆.
- 覆う (to cover) and 覆す (to overturn) differ only in okurigana; read the ending to tell them apart.

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [被](被.md) (“cover; incur”): it shares “cover” with the title, and JMdict lists 被う as a rarely used spelling of 覆う.
- [包](包.md) (“wrap; enclose”) and [秘](秘.md) (“secret; conceal”): JMdict glosses 覆う as “to cover; to hide; to conceal; to wrap; to disguise.”
- [翻](翻.md) (“turn over; reverse”), [反](反.md) (“oppose; reverse”) and [逆](逆.md) (“reverse; opposite”): JMdict glosses 覆す as “to overturn; to capsize; to upset” and “to reverse (decision etc.).”
- [倒](倒.md) (“topple; collapse”): JMdict glosses 覆る as “to topple over; to be overturned; to capsize.”
- [復](復.md) (“restore; repeat”): Kanjipedia's third sense for 覆 is くりかえす (to repeat), and it names 復 as a similar character.
- [殻](殻.md) (“shell; husk; empty outer covering”): its “covering” overlaps the title's “cover.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 開 (“open; unfold”), 拾 (“pick up; gather”), and 投 (“throw”); none means covering or overturning.
