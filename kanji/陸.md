# 陸 — land; six

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

陸 concerns land in contrast with water. It also writes the numeral six in legal documents, with a different reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the left 阝 beside the stacked earth-like blocks as land rising above water. This is a modern-shape memory story, not a historical derivation.

The left-hand 阝 is the shortened form of the canonical radical 阜, no. 170, not the right-hand 邑 radical.

## Notes

- The linked 陸 article teaches りく, land or shore. おか is another reading in that land family; neither should be given the six meaning merely because the written character is the same.
- The numeric ろく entry explicitly notes legal-document use of 陸. Compare ordinary [六](六.md), six. A separate usually-kana ろく entry means satisfactory or decent and is used with a negative verb; it is not the numeral.
