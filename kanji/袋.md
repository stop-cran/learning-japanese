# 袋 — bag; sack; pouch

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

袋 means a bag, sack or pouch. The native noun is ふくろ; its initial sound may become ぶ in particular compounds, as in 手袋 てぶくろ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The top resembles [代](代.md), replace, over [衣](衣.md), clothing. Imagine a cloth bag replacing an awkward armload of things; this is a mnemonic, not the character's history.

The indexing radical 衣 is at the bottom, in its full form rather than the left-hand 衤 variant.
