# 欧 — Europe (abbreviation)

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 欠 (no. 76) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

欧 represents Europe in compounds such as 欧州 おうしゅう, Europe, and 北欧 ほくおう, northern Europe or the Nordic region. Learn these established geographic terms rather than treating each dictionary reading as an ordinary independent word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the 区-like left side beside 欠 and imagine a small outline on a map marking Europe. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

- 欧州 names Europe; it is not by itself a statement that a country belongs to the European Union.
- 北欧 has conventional geographic uses. A bare north-plus-Europe mnemonic does not determine a unique country list for every context.
- Distinguish [区](区.md), ward or district, from 欧 by the added 欠 on the right. [吹](吹.md) has 口 on the left instead.
