# 筆 — brush; writing

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

筆 concerns a writing or painting brush and, by extension, writing or composing text. In compounds it can contribute a writing implement without saying that the object has traditional brush hairs.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the bamboo-shaped top as the handle above the 聿-shaped writing part. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified account of an ancient hand or brush.

The canonical key is [竹](竹.md), no. 118, visible as ⺮ at the top.

## Notes

- The linked [筆](../words/筆.md) article teaches ふで. In the linked [万年筆](../words/万年筆.md) article's まんねんひつ reading, 筆 is ヒツ, unlike ぴつ in [鉛筆](../words/鉛筆.md); learn each whole compound. JMdict also records まんねんぴつ and まんねんふで for 万年筆.
- The dictionary also records ひつ for the spelling 筆. The native noun ふで is still a valid reading; the on example does not replace it.
- In 執筆, シツ + ヒツ become しっぴつ, with a small っ and ヒ changing to ピ. Learn that whole compound rather than adding ピツ as an independent reading everywhere.
- In 筆記 and 筆者, ヒツ contracts before キ or シャ to give ひっき and ひっしゃ. Learn these whole compounds; ヒツ does not always contract.
- Compare [律](律.md), law or rhythm, and [建](建.md), build. [書](書.md), write, is also a useful comparison, but writing meanings overlap, so it is not a safe wrong answer merely because the shape differs.
