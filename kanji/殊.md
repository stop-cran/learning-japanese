# 殊 — especially; special; particular

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 歹 (no. 78), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

殊 is being different from the ordinary, and so "especially" or "particularly." The linked word [特殊](../words/特殊.md) とくしゅ is JMdict's "special; particular; peculiar; unique," used as 特殊な before a noun. Digital Daijisen's character entry glosses シュ as differing from the usual, especially, and gives 殊遇 しゅぐう, "special favour; special favor"; 殊勲 しゅくん, "distinguished services; meritorious deeds"; and 殊勝 しゅしょう, "admirable; laudable," beside 特殊.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 歹 at the left and 朱 at the right. Imagine something set apart from a row of ordinary things. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 歹 or 朱 contributes meaning or sound. [残](残.md) and [死](死.md) also have 歹 at the left. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シュ | on | [特殊](../words/特殊.md) とくしゅ — special; particular; peculiar |
| こと | kun | 殊に ことに — especially; particularly; unusually; above all; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 特殊, [特](特.md) supplies トク and 殊 supplies シュ. Daijisen also gives ジュ, marked as the Go-on (呉) reading, as in 文殊 もんじゅ, "Manjushri; Manjusri; Bodhisattva that represents transcendent wisdom"; it is not a separate KANJIDIC2 reading and is not taught here. For こと, JMdict lists 殊に ことに, "especially; particularly; unusually; above all," with 異に as another spelling, and 殊の外 ことのほか, "exceedingly; extremely; exceptionally; unusually," marked "word usually written using kana alone." 殊更 ことさら is "intentionally; deliberately; designedly; on purpose," also marked "word usually written using kana alone," and lists 故 as a "word containing out-dated kanji or kanji usage." In these adverbs 殊 writes こと, with the rest kana or other kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シュ with 殊勝, 殊勲 and 特殊, and こと with 殊に, 殊の外 and 殊更. Practice selects シュ, the reading with a linked word article. こと is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; being outside the practice set does not make it invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [特](特.md), "special," in 特殊.
- [残](残.md), "remain; leftover," and [死](死.md), "death," share the 歹 at the left of 殊; [殿](殿.md), "palace; lord," has 殳 at the right and is a similarly busy-looking kanji. All are different kanji.
- [特](特.md), "special," [珍](珍.md), "rare; unusual," [異](異.md), "different; unusual; strange," [奇](奇.md), "strange; extraordinary," [専](専.md), "specialize; exclusive," [別](別.md), "separate, another," and [格](格.md), "rank; character," overlap the senses of 殊 and its words: special in 特殊, unusual in 殊に and 殊の外. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. 異 also shares こと with 殊, as do [事](事.md), [琴](琴.md) and [言](言.md). Do not treat "special" or "especially" as exclusive to any one character.
