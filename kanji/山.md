# 山 — mountain

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

山 is a pictograph of a mountain with peaks, the middle one taller. As a key it appears in kanji about mountains and terrain:
岩, 岸, 峠, 島, 崖.

Don't confuse it with 出 ([出](出.md)): 出 looks like two 山 stacked, but its origin is a different picture (a foot leaving a pit) and
the character is not formed from 山.

| やま | kun | 山 やま, 山道 やまみち, 山登り やまのぼり |

## Common words

- [火山](../words/火山.md) かざん — volcano
- [山水](../words/山水.md) さんすい — landscape
- 山 やま — mountain (also a pile, or the crux of a matter, in idioms like 山場 やまば)

## Notes

山 and 川 ([川](川.md)) often go together as "mountains and rivers" for the natural landscape: 山川 やまかわ／さんせん (literary).
