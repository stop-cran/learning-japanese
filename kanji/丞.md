# 丞 — assist; aid

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

丞 has the meaning **help; assist**. Learn it through the historical title
[丞相](../words/丞相.md) じょうしょう, "grand chancellor" in ancient China.
This is not a general word for a modern workplace assistant.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the central hook and the strokes spreading to either side resting on a supporting bar.
That bottom horizontal stroke is the indexing radical 一. The picture is a memory aid for assistance, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

## Common words

- [丞相](../words/丞相.md) じょうしょう — grand chancellor (ancient China); also a Chinese-style historical ministerial title.

This is historical recognition vocabulary, not a claim of everyday frequency.

## Notes

[蒸](蒸.md), "steam; sultry heat," has extra strokes above and below an 丞-shaped middle.
That shape comparison does not establish historical derivation or a rule for transferring readings.
[乗](乗.md), "ride; board," and [水](水.md), "water," are separate characters, not alternative spellings of 丞.

For ordinary helping and rescue, compare [助ける](../words/助ける.md) たすける and
[救う](../words/救う.md) すくう. Their spellings do not demonstrate the kun readings of 丞.
