# 瀬 — shallows; rapids; position

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

瀬 is a shallow, fast-moving stretch of a river, and, by extension, the spot where one stands. The linked [瀬戸物](../words/瀬戸物.md) せともの is ceramic ware; JMdict glosses its first sense "earthenware; porcelain; china; pottery; crockery" and its second sense "Seto ware; ceramic ware from Seto (Aichi Prefecture)," with the note "orig. meaning." In the word, 瀬 is the first part of the place name 瀬戸 せと, which JMdict glosses "strait; channel." The word article does not treat the characters as "rapids" plus "door," so neither does this card.

JMdict gives 瀬 alone four senses: "shallows; shoal"; "rapids; current; torrent"; "position; place"; and "chance; opportunity." Daijisen's character entry gives, for ライ, a shallow place in the water or a fast-flowing place, with the example 急瀬 きゅうらい. For せ, Daijisen lists 瀬戸, 浅瀬 and 川瀬 かわせ and 早瀬 はやせ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The whole right-hand part is the kanji [頼](頼.md), "request; rely." Imagine a stretch of river running shallow and fast beside a clump of stones. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 瀬戸物, 瀬 writes せ, [戸](戸.md), "door; counter for households," writes と and [物](物.md), "thing, object," writes もの. JMdict lists 瀬戸もの as a "search-only kanji form" and セトモノ as a "search-only kana form." 瀬 by itself reads せ: 浅瀬 あさせ is "shoal; shallows; sand bar; ford," 川瀬 かわせ is "rapids; shallows of a river," 早瀬 はやせ is "swift current; rapids," and 立つ瀬 たつせ is "one's position; one's face; one's honour; one's reputation," with the note "usu. as 〜がない."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows せ with 瀬, 浅瀬 and 立つ瀬, and shows no on reading. Practice selects せ, the reading of the linked word article. ライ is shown from Daijisen and KANJIDIC2 and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. The table shows the older form 瀨 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word are [戸](戸.md), "door; counter for households," which writes と in せと, and [物](物.md), "thing, object," which writes もの. The Seto place association is JMdict's second sense of the word and no more.
- [湯](湯.md), "hot water," [湿](湿.md), "damp, moist," and [滴](滴.md), "drip; drop," have 氵 at the left like 瀬. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [浅](浅.md), "shallow," and [滝](滝.md), "waterfall; rapids," overlap the senses of 瀬 and its words 浅瀬 and 早瀬. [流](流.md), "flow," [川](川.md), "river," and [河](河.md), "river; watercourse," overlap running water. [位](位.md), "rank; position," overlaps the sense "position; place," as in 立つ瀬. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "shallows," "rapids" or "position" as exclusive to any one character.
- Other words that use 瀬 are 瀬戸 せと, "strait; channel," and 瀬戸際 せとぎわ, "brink; verge; critical moment; crucial moment."
