# 撮 — photograph; film

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

撮 concerns taking a picture or filming. [撮る](../words/撮る.md) とる is the verb for taking a photograph or shooting a film or video, and [撮影](../words/撮影.md) さつえい is photography or filming. 特撮 とくさつ, "special effects; tokusatsu," also uses 撮. The Daijisen character entry lists picking something up between the fingers (つまんで取る) as its first sense, which matches the kun reading つま.む.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the side form of [手](手.md), "hand," at the left and [最](最.md), "most," at the right, made of a wide 日-like box (曰) above 取. Imagine a hand lifting a camera to capture the best shot. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim about the historical formation of 撮.

In 撮影, 撮 supplies サツ and 影 supplies エイ. In 撮る, 撮 writes と and る is okurigana; this is the spelling for the photographic sense of とる, and other senses of とる use other kanji. Practice selects サツ and と.る; the other kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with unlinked dictionary words.

## Notes

- [影](影.md), "shadow; silhouette," is the other kanji in 撮影.
- [写](写.md), "copy, photograph," also concerns photographs. 写す can mean copying as well as photographing, whereas 撮る is for taking the picture itself. They overlap, so do not treat "photograph" as exclusive to either character.
- [最](最.md), "most," is the right-hand part but reads サイ or シュ, not サツ. [接](接.md), "touch; join," and [投](投.md), "throw," share 扌 at the left but have different right-hand shapes.
