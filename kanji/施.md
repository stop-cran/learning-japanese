# 施 — carry out; give; alms

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 方 (no. 70), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

施 can mean putting a plan into practice, giving something to someone, or almsgiving. In the linked word [実施](../words/実施.md) じっし, it means carrying out an activity; JMdict glosses 実施 as “enforcement; implementation; putting into practice.”

Daijisen uses シ for putting a plan into practice, as in 実施. Its セ reading is giving alms, as in 布施 ふせ, “alms-giving; charity.” The separate せ【施】 word entry describes Buddhist almsgiving and giving something to another person. The verb 施す ほどこす can mean “to give (time, money, goods); to donate.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice [方](方.md) at the left and the different curved strokes on the right. Imagine putting a plan into practice or giving something away. This is a memory aid only; the visible parts do not establish meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | [実施](../words/実施.md) じっし — implementation; 施設 しせつ — facility; 施政 しせい — administration |
| セ | on | 施主 せしゅ — donor; 施療 せりょう — free medical treatment; 布施 ふせ — alms-giving; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ほどこ.す | kun | 施す ほどこす — to give (time, money, goods); 施し ほどこし — charity; almsgiving |

In 実施, 実 is read じつ and shortens to じっ before シ; 施 supplies シ. 施設 and 施政 also use シ. In 施主, 施療 and 布施, 施 supplies セ. JMdict gives 施主 the senses “donor; benefactor; almsgiver,” “chief mourner,” and “client (of a builder, etc.).” In 施す, 施 supplies ほどこ and す is okurigana; 施し uses the same stem with し.

The official Joyo table, the standard-use kanji list, shows シ with 施設，施政，実施; セ with 施主，施療，布施; and ほどこす with 施す，施し. Practice selects シ because it is used in the linked article 実施. Readings outside this quiz selection are not invalid.

## Notes

- In 実施, [実](実.md) contributes actuality; the link article explains how 実施 describes a planned activity being carried out.
- The distractors [旋](旋.md), [旗](旗.md) and [旅](旅.md) share the left-side 方 shape, but their meanings are rotation, flag and travel. Their readings do not match シ.
- [執](執.md) and [履](履.md) have titles that include carrying out; [与](与.md), [贈](贈.md), [給](給.md), [授](授.md) and [恵](恵.md) overlap giving; [設](設.md) overlaps putting something in place. These titles are excluded so “carry out,” “give” and “alms” do not make more than one answer defensible.
