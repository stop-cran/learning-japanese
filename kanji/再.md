# 再 — again; repeat

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 冂 (no. 13) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

再 concerns something happening again or a second time. 再び ふたたび means again or once more. In 再来年 さらいねん, the サ reading forms a word for the year after next, not simply next year.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine tracing the rectangular upper shape and then passing across it again with the long lower horizontal stroke.

The classical indexing radical is 冂, represented by the enclosure-like outer shape.

## Notes

- Compare 来年 らいねん, next year, with 再来年 さらいねん, the year after next. The same contrast applies to 来月／再来月 and 来週／再来週.
- 再び is an adverb: 再び会う ふたたびあう means meet again. It is not a する verb by itself.
