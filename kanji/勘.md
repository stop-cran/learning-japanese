# 勘 — intuition; hunch

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19, at the right) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

As the noun [勘](../words/勘.md) (かん), this character means intuition or a hunch: sensing something or judging it without explicitly working through every step. That intuitive feel can develop through experience, but a hunch can also be wrong.

In compounds, 勘 also contributes examining or reckoning. [勘定](../words/勘定.md) (かんじょう), "reckoning; bill," is not a "settled hunch"; learn the whole word rather than translating every 勘 as intuition.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the stacked 甚-shaped section at the left and [力](力.md) at the right. Imagine effort spent noticing clues. This uses the present-day outline only, not a verified account of the character's historical formation.

## Notes

- Compare [勤](勤.md), "employment; diligence": both have 力 at the right, but their dense left sides differ. [助](助.md), "help; rescue," has a simpler 且-shaped left side. [期](期.md), "period; term," has 月 at the right instead of 力.
- 勘が当たる (かんがあたる) means "one's hunch proves right"; 勘が外れる (かんがはずれる) means "one's hunch proves wrong." Intuition does not guarantee correctness or imply supernatural powers.
- **Supplementary spelling recognition:** Digital Daijisen also lists the native reading かんがえる. JMdict records 勘える as a rare spelling of the usual [考える](../words/考える.md) (かんがえる), "think about; consider." The spelling 勘える is rare, not the pronunciation かんがえる. This is recognition information, not an additional reading question on this card.
