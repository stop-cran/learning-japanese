# 殿 — palace; lord

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 殳 (no. 79) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

殿 occurs in palace words such as 宮殿 きゅうでん and 御殿 ごてん. 殿 との is also a respectful word for a feudal lord. The bound form -どの has a separate use as a written address suffix.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the crowded left side beside 殳 and imagine an elaborate palace with a ceremonial object at its side. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

## Notes

- 仮殿 かりどの houses a shrine's sacred object while the main shrine is being repaired. This gives a lexical example of word-final どの in the building sense.
- Separately, the polite address suffix 殿 -どの attaches to a name or title: for example 部長殿 ぶちょうどの, To the department head, in a written address. JMdict identifies official/business letters and letters to inferiors; it is not a universal upgrade in respect over 様 さま.
- The palace or mansion sense of 殿 との is marked archaic in JMdict. That qualification does not make the separate modern word 御殿 ごてん archaic.
- 御殿 ごてん and 宮殿 きゅうでん illustrate different on readings. Learn the whole words instead of choosing デン or テン from the English gloss alone.
