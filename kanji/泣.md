# 泣 — cry; weep; sob

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

泣 is crying with tears: weeping, sobbing, shedding tears. The linked [泣く](../words/泣く.md) なく is JMdict's "to cry; to shed tears; to weep; to sob" in its first sense. Its later senses reach figurative uses, for example "to suffer (from); to be troubled (by); to face hardship" (usually as ...に泣く) and "to lament; to bemoan; to bewail."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for キュウ: shedding tears (なく), with 泣訴 きゅうそ, "imploring with tears in one's eyes," 感泣 かんきゅう, "being moved to tears," 号泣 ごうきゅう, "crying loudly; bawling; wailing; lamentation," 涕泣 ていきゅう, "weeping; sobbing" and 悲泣 ひきゅう, "tears of grief; crying with sadness"; and tears themselves (なみだ), with 垂泣.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left and [立](立.md), "stand; set up," at the right. Imagine someone standing still while tears run down like water. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or 立 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キュウ | on | 号泣 ごうきゅう — crying loudly; bawling; wailing; lamentation; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| な.く | kun | [泣く](../words/泣く.md) なく — cry; weep |

In 泣く, 泣 writes な and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same な appears in 泣き声 なきごえ, "cry (e.g. of a baby); sob; whine," 泣き出す なきだす, "to burst into tears; to burst out crying; to begin to cry," and 泣き沈む なきしずむ, "to abandon oneself to grief." In 号泣, 号 supplies ゴウ and 泣 supplies キュウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キュウ with 号泣 and 感泣, and なく with 泣く and 泣き沈む. Practice selects な.く, the reading of the linked word article. キュウ is shown with a dictionary example and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- 泣く is 泣 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji. In 号泣, [号](号.md), "number; call," is an existing card.
- [立](立.md), "stand; set up," is the right part of 泣; [注](注.md), "pour, direct attention," and [泊](泊.md), "stay overnight; anchor," also have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; 立, 注 and 泊 supply this card's wrong answers.
- [涙](涙.md), "tears," overlaps Daijisen's second sense, the tears themselves. [鳴](鳴.md), "animal call; ring," shares な.く: JMdict's 鳴く なく begins "to make sound (of an animal); to call; to cry; to whine; to sing; to chirp," so it is a different spelling of the same sound なく, and 鳴 is a card with its own title. [叫](叫.md), "shout; exclaim," and [号](号.md) (号泣 is crying out loud) overlap crying out; [悲](悲.md), "sadness," [哀](哀.md), "sorrow; pity; pathos," and [悔](悔.md), "regret; repent; mourn; frustration," overlap the feelings that bring tears. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "cry" or "weep" as exclusive to any one character.
