# 飛 — fly

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 飛 (no. 183) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

飛 expresses flying in [飛ぶ](../words/飛ぶ.md) とぶ and 飛行機 (ひこうき), an airplane.
鳥が飛んでいます。 (とりがとんでいます。) — A bird is flying.
The related verb 飛ばす (とばす) can mean making or sending something flying.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the long bent strokes and smaller strokes projecting outward like wings.
This is a mnemonic based on the modern outline, not a historical reconstruction.
