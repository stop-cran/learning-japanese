# 沢 — marsh; luster

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

沢 is a marsh or wetland, and also gloss, richness and blessing. KANJIDIC2 lists “swamp”, “marsh”, “brilliance” and “grace”. Kanjipedia's first sense is さわ。草木の生えている湿地帯。 (a wetland where plants grow), its second うるおう。うるおい。めぐみ。 (to be moistened; moisture; blessing) and its third つや。かがやき。 (gloss; brilliance). Digital Daijisen's second sense begins with 物が豊かにあること (having things in abundance), and 贅沢 is among its examples. In [贅沢](../words/贅沢.md) ぜいたく, JMdict's first sense is “luxury; extravagance”; the other senses are “to live in luxury; to indulge oneself”, “lavish (use of something); abundant; copious; wasteful” and “excessive (e.g. demands, expectations)” (entry 1573150).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 氵 stands on the left, a variant of [水](水.md), "water," and the shape 尺 stands on the right. Picture 尺 as the bent legs of a heron wading through a quiet marsh, its wet surface shining in the sun. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or 尺 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists タク, with 光沢 and 潤沢 じゅんたく, and さわ, with 沢, and gives 澤 as the old form. KANJIDIC2 adds うるお.い, うるお.す and つや; Kanjipedia marks うるおう and つや as outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen gives ［音］タク and ［訓］さわ.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings おも, さ, さわん and わさ, and Digital Daijisen gives the name reading ます; these are name uses, not quiz readings.

These thirteen cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [湿](湿.md) (“damp, moist”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 沢 is 草木の生えている湿地帯 (a wetland where plants grow) and Digital Daijisen's first is 湿地 (wetland); Kanjipedia's second sense is うるおう, KANJIDIC2 lists うるお.い and うるお.す for 沢, and the 湿 card lists うるお.う and うるお.す.
- [泥](泥.md) (“mud; mire”): KANJIDIC2 lists “swamp” and “marsh” for 沢, JMdict glosses 泥沼 どろぬま (1436950) as “bog; marsh; swamp; quagmire; morass”, and 沼沢 しょうたく (1350020) is “marsh; swamp; bog.”
- [谷](谷.md) (“valley; ravine”) and [川](川.md) (“river”): the first sense of 沢 さわ is “mountain stream; valley; dale” (JMdict 1415850), and JMdict glosses 川 かわ (1390020) as “river; stream.”
- [富](富.md) (“wealth; abundance”), [豊](豊.md) (“bountiful; abundant”) and [裕](裕.md) (“abundant; rich; fertile”): Digital Daijisen's second sense of 沢 begins with 物が豊かにあること (having things in abundance), JMdict glosses 潤沢 じゅんたく (1635870) as “abundant; ample; plentiful; affluent”, the third sense of 贅沢 includes “abundant”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “abundant” and “rich” for 裕.
- [多](多.md) (“many, much”): Digital Daijisen gives 沢山 as an example of its second sense, and the first sense of 沢山 たくさん (JMdict 1415870, usually written in kana) includes “a lot; lots; plenty; many.”
- [恵](恵.md) (“blessing; favor; grace”) and [恩](恩.md) (“favor; debt of gratitude”): KANJIDIC2 lists “grace” for 沢 and “favor”, “blessing” and “grace” for both 恵 and 恩; Kanjipedia's second sense of 沢 includes めぐみ (blessing), with 恩沢, and Digital Daijisen's third is 人に施す恵み (blessings bestowed on people); JMdict glosses 沢 たく (2843433, archaic) as “blessing; grace; favour; favor; benefit” and 恩沢 おんたく (1183240) as “favour; favor; benefit.”
- [艶](艶.md) (“gloss; luster; allure”): KANJIDIC2 gives the kun reading つや for both 沢 and 艶, Kanjipedia's third sense of 沢 begins with つや, and the first sense of 艶 つや (JMdict 1177680) is “gloss; luster; lustre; shine; sheen; polish.”
- [光](光.md) (“light; radiance”): KANJIDIC2 lists “brilliance” for 沢, and JMdict glosses 光沢 こうたく (1273050) as “brilliance; polish; lustre; luster; glossy finish.”
- [輝](輝.md) (“shine; sparkle”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 沢 is つや。かがやき。 (gloss; brilliance), JMdict glosses 輝き かがやき (1224010) as “brightness; brilliance; brilliancy; radiance; glitter”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “radiance” and “shine” for 輝.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 霜 (“frost”), 岩 (“rock; boulder”), and 丘 (“hill; knoll”); frost is weather and rock and hill are other landforms; none means a marsh or luster.
