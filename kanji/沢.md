# 沢 — marsh; luster

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

沢 is a marsh or wetland, or a mountain stream or valley. It also means moisture and blessing, richness and abundance, and gloss or brilliance. KANJIDIC2 lists “swamp”, “marsh”, “brilliance” and “grace”. The word [贅沢](../words/贅沢.md) ぜいたく first means “luxury; extravagance”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 氵 stands on the left, a variant of [水](水.md), "water," and the shape 尺 stands on the right. Picture 尺 as the bent legs of a heron wading through a quiet marsh, its wet surface shining in the sun. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or 尺 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists タク, with 光沢 and 潤沢 じゅんたく, and さわ, with 沢, and gives 澤 as the old form. KANJIDIC2 adds うるお.い, うるお.す and つや, and Kanjipedia lists うるおう and つや outside the table (外).
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings おも, さ, さわん and わさ, and Digital Daijisen gives the name reading ます; these are name uses, not quiz readings.

These thirteen cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [湿](湿.md) (“damp, moist”) and [泥](泥.md) (“mud; mire”): the wetland and moisture senses; 沼沢 しょうたく is “marsh; swamp; bog”, much like 泥沼 どろぬま, “bog; marsh; swamp; quagmire; morass”.
- [谷](谷.md) (“valley; ravine”) and [川](川.md) (“river”): 沢 さわ can also mean a mountain stream or valley.
- [富](富.md) (“wealth; abundance”), [豊](豊.md) (“bountiful; abundant”), [裕](裕.md) (“abundant; rich; fertile”) and [多](多.md) (“many, much”): the abundance sense, as in 潤沢, “abundant; ample; plentiful; affluent”, and 沢山 たくさん, usually written in kana, which can mean “a lot” or “plenty”.
- [恵](恵.md) (“blessing; favor; grace”) and [恩](恩.md) (“favor; debt of gratitude”): the blessing sense, as in 恩沢 おんたく, “favour; favor; benefit”.
- [艶](艶.md) (“gloss; luster; allure”), [光](光.md) (“light; radiance”) and [輝](輝.md) (“shine; sparkle”): the gloss and brilliance sense, as in 光沢 こうたく, “brilliance; polish; lustre; luster; glossy finish”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 霜 (“frost”), 岩 (“rock; boulder”), and 丘 (“hill; knoll”); frost is weather and rock and hill are other landforms; none means a marsh or luster.
