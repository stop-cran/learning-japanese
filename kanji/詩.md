# 詩 — poem; poetry

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

詩 means a poem or poetry. KANJIDIC2 lists “poem” and “poetry.” Kanjipedia's first sense begins し。うた。 and describes what one feels, put into words that fit a set rhythm and form. The noun [詩](../words/詩.md) し is glossed in JMdict as “poem; poetry; verse,” with a second sense, “Chinese poem.” In [詩人](../words/詩人.md) しじん, JMdict's gloss is “poet.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [言](言.md), "say; words," with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it, stands on the left. On the right is [寺](寺.md), "Buddhist temple," made of [土](土.md), "earth, soil," over [寸](寸.md), "sun (length unit); small." Imagine words spoken softly in a quiet temple, slowly shaped into a poem. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 寺 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists シ only. Among its examples is 詩歌, with the note 「詩歌」は，「シイカ」とも (詩歌 may also be read シイカ); JMdict entry 1579540 lists both しいか and しか for 詩歌.
- Kanjipedia marks うた as outside the Joyo table (外), and Digital Daijisen also gives うた as a kun reading. KANJIDIC2 records no name readings, and Digital Daijisen's character entry gives none.

These three cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [歌](歌.md) (“song, sing”): JMdict entry 1193180 うた lists 詩 as a spelling for its sense 3, “modern poetry,” and Kanjipedia's first sense of 詩 begins し。うた。
- [句](句.md) (“phrase; verse”): JMdict glosses 詩 し as “poem; poetry; verse.”
- [詞](詞.md) (“words; lyrics; part of speech”): JMdict entry 2266770 glosses 詞 し as “words; writing; lyrics” and “ci (form of Chinese poetry).”

The [謡](謡.md) card (“ballad; Noh chanting”) declares the 詩/謡 pair, because Kanjipedia gives うた for both characters.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 劇 (“drama; play”), 琴 (“koto; zither”), and 絵 (“drawing; painting”); none means a poem or poetry.
