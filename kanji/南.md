# 南 — south

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 十 (no. 24) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The origin of 南 is uncertain. Early forms are often described as a kind of bell or musical instrument hung in a frame, with
"south" borrowed by sound, but this is debated and the modern parts have no individual meaning. Treat any story as a mnemonic only. The key 十 is a classification by shape.

- [南口](../words/南口.md) みなみぐち — south exit
- 南北 なんぼく — north and south
- 南極 なんきょく — the South Pole, Antarctica

## Notes

- The pair is 南北 なんぼく (south-north), with 南 first, unlike English "north-south"; 東西 とうざい (east-west) also puts 東 first. See [北](北.md), where ホク voices to ボク in 南北 なんぼく.
- Look-alikes: 用 (use) has no 十 on top, and 角 (corner) has a different base. Compare also [北](北.md), [東](東.md) and [西](西.md).
- 南風 is normally みなみかぜ (south wind); 南 also appears in 南米 なんべい (South America).
