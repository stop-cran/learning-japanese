# 紹 — introduce; inherit

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

紹 contributes introducing or continuing/inheriting. Learn it first through 紹介, which can introduce a person, place or subject rather than only arrange a first face-to-face meeting.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 糸 beside 召 and imagine a thread connecting the two sides of an introduction. This is an invented modern-shape association, not verified historical or phonetic analysis.

The indexing key 糸, no. 120, is compressed at the left as ⺯.

## Notes

- 紹介する is transitive: AにBを紹介する means introduce B to A. The linked article also explains introductions in books and websites.
- The meaning of 紹介 is not obtained by assigning one immutable English word to each character. [介](介.md) contributes acting between parties in this compound.
- Compare [召](召.md), summon/respectful actions, and [照](照.md), illuminate/shine. Their similar right-hand shapes and ショウ readings do not make them synonyms of introducing.
