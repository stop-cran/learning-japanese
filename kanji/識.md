# 識 — knowledge; awareness

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

識 concerns knowing and awareness. [知識](../words/知識.md) ちしき is knowledge or information a person has;
意識 いしき concerns consciousness or awareness. The two compounds answer different questions:
what someone knows, and what they are aware of.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the dense right half as information to recognize,
with 言 on the left as its verbal label. This is a modern writing aid, not a verified origin
or a claim about a phonetic component.

## Notes

知識 is a noun: 知識がある (ちしきがある) means "have knowledge."
[試](試.md) shares a slanting stroke on the right as well as 言;
[調](調.md) and [訪](訪.md) are simpler speech-component contrasts.
None has the complete dense right-hand pattern of 識.
