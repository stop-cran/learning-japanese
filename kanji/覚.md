# 覚 — memorize; awaken

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 見 (no. 147) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

覚 connects with memory and becoming awake or aware. [覚える](../words/覚える.md) おぼえる is the word to learn
for committing something to memory. 覚める さめる belongs to waking, so the ending is essential
to deciding what the verb means.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the 見 at the bottom can suggest noticing something beneath
the small upper strokes and cover. This is a modern visual association, not a claim about origin.

## Notes

目が覚める (めがさめる) means "wake up"; it is not a phrase about memorizing an eye.
[字](字.md) offers a roof-and-lower-part contrast, but its lower part is 子.
In [規](規.md) and [親](親.md), 見 sits on the right rather than underneath.
