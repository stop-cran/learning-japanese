# 鳥 — bird

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 鳥 (no. 196) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鳥 is **bird**, the broad animal category. In food contexts, とり can also refer to chicken meat;
the more specific spelling 鶏（にわとり, chicken） helps distinguish chicken from birds in general.
鶏肉（とりにく） means chicken meat.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper box as a head with an eye, the bent middle as a body
and wing, and the lower marks as moving feet. This is a visual aid, not a verified account of early forms.

## Notes

In 白鳥, [白](白.md) means white, but the usual whole-word meaning is the specific bird "swan,"
not simply any white bird.

[魚](魚.md) also has four lower marks; compare the upper bodies.
The 馬 component in [駅](駅.md) has a different upper shape from 鳥.
