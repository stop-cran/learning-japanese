# 畔 — paddy boundary; waterside

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

畔 concerns a **field boundary or the side of water**. The native noun [畔](../words/畔.md) あぜ names a raised earth boundary between rice fields. In [湖畔](../words/湖畔.md) こはん, it instead identifies the lakeside.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [田](田.md) beside the [半](半.md)-shaped right side. Imagine a bank separating two portions of farmland. This is a shape-based memory scene, not an origin claim or a rule that 畔 means half.

## Common words

- [湖畔](../words/湖畔.md) こはん - lakeside; lakefront
- [畔](../words/畔.md) あぜ - paddy-field ridge; often written あぜ or 畦
- 河畔 かはん - riverside

## Notes

- [畝](畝.md) うね is a raised row for planting crops. Contrast **planting on a ridge** with **a ridge marking a field boundary**; not every raised strip is interchangeable in use.
- あぜ also names the ridge between grooves in a threshold or upper sliding-door frame, and can abbreviate あぜ道 (あぜみち), a field path.
- The dictionary family restricts its くろ reading to 畔, not its alternative spelling 畦. That is a restriction of this JMdict family, not a claim that other dictionaries never record 畦 as くろ.
- The ほとり spelling is recognition material, not a recommendation to replace kana with 畔 in ordinary writing. [辺](辺.md) has a separate source reading ほと.り; neither is the primary reading of the linked あぜ article.
- [半](半.md) means half; [判](判.md) concerns judgment or a seal; [畜](畜.md) concerns livestock. Similar components do not give them a field-boundary meaning.
