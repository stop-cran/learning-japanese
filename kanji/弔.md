# 弔 — mourn; condolence

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (no. 57) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弔 relates to mourning a death: 弔問 is a condolence call, 弔辞 (ちょうじ) a funeral address, and 弔う (とむらう) to mourn for someone.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 弔 is 弓 with one vertical line running through it. In 引 the line stands to the right of 弓 instead.

## Notes

[引](引.md) puts the line beside 弓, [弟](弟.md) has extra strokes on top, and [第](第.md) adds bamboo above. [悲](悲.md) and [哀](哀.md) express sorrow in general; [葬](葬.md) relates to burial and funerals, and [喪](喪.md) to mourning or loss.
