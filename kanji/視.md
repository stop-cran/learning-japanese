# 視 — look at; regard as

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 見 (no. 147) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

視 has two meaning groups. Kanjipedia's first sense is looking: みる, 目で見る (to see with the eyes) and じっと見る (to look steadily). Its second sense is みなす (to regard as), as in [重視](../words/重視.md) じゅうし, which JMdict glosses “regarding as important; attaching importance to; taking a serious view of; putting emphasis on.” Digital Daijisen lists [無視](../words/無視.md) むし, “disregarding; ignoring,” under the same regard-as sense.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is 礻, a variant of [示](示.md), "indicate," and on the right is [見](見.md), "see, look." Imagine someone pointing at something and saying "look at this": you look at it and decide what to regard it as. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 礻 or 見 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists シ only. KANJIDIC2 also lists み.る; Kanjipedia marks みる outside the Joyo table, and Digital Daijisen lists it as a kun reading.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings とも and み, and Digital Daijisen gives み and よし. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [見](見.md) (“see, look”): KANJIDIC2 lists “see” and “look at” for 視, and JMdict entry 1259290 lists 視る as a spelling of 見る.
- [観](観.md) (“view; observe”), [眺](眺.md) (“gaze; view; look out over”) and [察](察.md) (“observe; infer”): JMdict entry 1259290, which includes the spelling 視る, glosses みる as “to see; to look; to watch; to view; to observe.”
- [看](看.md) (“watch over; see”) and [監](監.md) (“oversee; watch over”): the same JMdict entry also glosses みる as “to look after; to attend to; to take care of; to keep an eye on,” and KANJIDIC2 lists “see” for both 看 and 視.
- [仰](仰.md) (“look up; revere”): to look up at something is itself to look at it, so its “look up” overlaps the title's “look at.”
- [査](査.md) (“investigate; inspect”) and [検](検.md) (“inspect; examine; check”): KANJIDIC2 lists “inspection” for 視, and JMdict entry 1259290 glosses みる as “to examine; to look over; to assess; to check; to judge.”

The [覧](覧.md) card (“look over; peruse”) declares the 視/覧 pair, because KANJIDIC2 gives both characters “see”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 聞 (“hear, ask”), 言 (“say; words”), and 音 (“sound”); none means looking at something or regarding it as something.
