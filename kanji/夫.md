# 夫 — husband

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

夫 names a husband in the word 夫 (おっと). In [夫婦](../words/夫婦.md) (ふうふ), it supplies the husband half of "husband and wife," meaning a married couple.
The character also has a broader man sense in compounds; do not use おっと as a general word for a man.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** add an extra horizontal line above the arms of a 大-shaped figure and imagine a husband wearing a broad hat.
This is a visual memory aid, not an etymology.

## Notes

Compare [失](失.md): its short slant at the top is an extra stroke. In [天](天.md), the upper horizontal line caps the character; in 夫, the central stroke rises above both horizontal lines.
[妻](妻.md) (つま), "wife," is the relationship counterpart. Learn the two standalone words as おっと and つま, not by borrowing their compound readings.
