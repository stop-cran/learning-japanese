# 泳 — swim

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

泳 is the swimming character. [泳ぐ](../words/泳ぐ.md) およぐ describes moving through water
by swimming, while 水泳 (すいえい) names the activity.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the spreading strokes of the right-hand 永 shape look like limbs
moving through the water on the left. This visual image is not an etymology.

## Notes

プールで泳ぎます。 (プールでおよぎます。) — I swim in the pool.
泳ぐ changes to 泳いで (およいで) in the て-form, not およって.
Keep swimming separate from [浮く](../words/浮く.md) うく, floating: something can float without swimming.
The three water strokes also occur in [流](流.md), [治](治.md), and [洗](洗.md); read their right sides carefully.
