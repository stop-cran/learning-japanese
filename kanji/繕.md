# 繕 — mend; darn; repair

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

繕 is mending something torn or damaged so that it can be used again. [修繕](../words/修繕.md) しゅうぜん is repair or mending of clothing, buildings, or other property; JMdict glosses it "repair; mending." The word article teaches "repair; mending."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ゼン: fixing torn places, as in 営繕 えいぜん, "maintenance and repair; upkeep (of equipment)," and 修繕. It marks ゼン as a Go-on reading (呉), lists the kun reading つくろう, and lists よし among the name readings.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left and [善](善.md), "good; virtue; handle well," at the right. Imagine a thread pulling a torn place back into good order. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 or 善 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ゼン | on | [修繕](../words/修繕.md) しゅうぜん — repair; mending |
| つくろ.う | kun | 繕う つくろう - to mend; to patch up; to repair; to fix; to darn; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 修繕, 修 supplies シュウ and 繕 supplies ゼン. In 営繕, 営 supplies エイ and 繕 supplies ゼン. In 繕う, 繕 writes つくろ and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict's 繕う has further senses, "to fix (hair, clothes, appearance, etc.); to adjust; to tidy up; to groom" and "to keep up appearances; to cover up (e.g. a mistake); to gloss over," and an archaic one, "to treat (illness, injury, etc.)." The noun 繕い つくろい, "mending; repair; darning; patching up," keeps つくろ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ゼン, with 修繕 and 営繕, and つくろう, with 繕う and 繕い. Practice selects ゼン, the reading of the linked word article. つくろ.う is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word; it has no word article yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [修](修.md), "repair; amend; cultivate," in 修繕, supplying シュウ. Its card links 修繕 too.
- 修, [直](直.md), "direct; repair," and [補](補.md), "supplement; make good," overlap "repair." [縫](縫.md), "sew; stitch," overlaps "darn" and mending cloth. [治](治.md), "govern; heal," overlaps the archaic sense of treating an injury. [復](復.md), "restore; repeat," and [整](整.md), "put in order; adjust," overlap restoring and tidying up. All are kept out of this card's quiz options. [継](継.md), "succeed to; continue; patch," also has a patching and mending sense (that card lists 繕 as an exclusion, and the app treats exclusions as symmetric): the difference is that 継 is joining or carrying on, with patching one sense among others, while 繕 is about mending and putting things in order.
- [紙](紙.md), "paper," has the same 糸 at the left; [鳥](鳥.md), "bird," and [雨](雨.md), "rain," are common simple kanji. They are read シ, かみ; チョウ, とり; and ウ, あめ, and share no reading or meaning with 繕.
- Many other kanji read ゼン, such as [全](全.md), "whole; complete," [前](前.md), "front, before," and 善, are never offered as wrong answers. Neither are kanji read セン, the Kanjipedia outside reading.
- For reference, Kanjipedia also lists セン as an outside reading of 繕; it is not a row here.
