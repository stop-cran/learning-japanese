# 入 — enter, put in

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 入 (no. 11) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

入 is often described as a pictograph of something pointed going inward (an entrance or tip), but the origin is debated.
Use it as a mnemonic: two strokes converging, opposite to the outward 出 ([出](出.md)).
It means "enter", "go in" and "put in".

It looks like 人 ([人](人.md)) but the strokes differ: the short left-falling stroke is written first, then the long right-falling stroke from the top (人 is the other way round).
