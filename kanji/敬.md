# 敬 — respect; honor

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (no. 66) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

敬 concerns respect or honoring someone. 敬意 けいい names respectful regard, while 尊敬 そんけい can form a verb with する. [敬う](../words/敬う.md) うやまう is a native verb for showing respect or reverence.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the right-hand 攵 beside the compact left-hand group containing 口 and imagine attending carefully to someone's words. This is a modern-shape story, not a verified origin.

The canonical radical is 攴; its visible right-hand form here is 攵.

## Notes

- 愛敬 あいきょう demonstrates キョウ; the same dictionary entry also spells this word 愛嬌. Do not replace ケイ with キョウ in 敬意 or 尊敬.
- 敬う is a transitive godan verb ending in う: 敬います うやまいます, 敬って うやまって, 敬わない うやまわない.
- 敬意 commonly works with a supporting verb, as in 敬意を払う けいいをはらう, pay respect. The linked word article explains that construction.
