# 日 — sun, day

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **Phonetic:** none (not a phonetic compound) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

日 is a pictograph of the sun. The oldest forms are round, often with a dot or short line inside; brush and carving
styles squared the circle. What the inner mark originally represented is uncertain, so treat any story about it
(a sunspot, a core) as a mnemonic rather than established fact.

The same word covers both "the sun" and "a day" (one sun-cycle), which is why one character serves for both
and for the days of the week and the month (日曜日, 十日). As a component, 日 usually hints at the sun, daylight or time,
as in 明 (日 + 月, bright), 時, 晴, 昨.

It is easy to confuse with 目 (eye, taller with two inner lines), 白 (white, one extra stroke on top) and [月](月.md) (moon, slimmer, with two inner strokes).

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ニチ | on | 日曜日 にちようび, 毎日 まいにち, 日本 にほん／にっぽん, 日記 にっき |
| ジツ | on | [休日](../words/休日.md) きゅうじつ, 平日 へいじつ, 祝日 しゅくじつ, 先日 せんじつ |
| ひ | kun | 日 ひ (sun, day), 日の出 ひので, 日当たり ひあたり |
| -び | kun | 誕生日 たんじょうび, 記念日 きねんび (voiced form of ひ in compounds) |
| -か | kun | day counting: 二日 ふつか, 三日 みっか, 十日 とおか; 一日 ついたち is special |

Special readings: 今日 きょう, 昨日 きのう, 明日 あした／あす.

## Common words

- [日中](../words/日中.md) にっちゅう — daytime; also Japan–China (日中関係)
- [月日](../words/月日.md) つきひ／がっぴ — time passing; calendar date
- [休日](../words/休日.md) きゅうじつ — day off, holiday
- 毎日 まいにち — every day

## Notes

The five-element kanji [火](火.md) [水](水.md) [木](木.md) (plus 金 土) combine with 日 and [月](月.md) in the weekday names:
日曜日, 月曜日, 火曜日, 水曜日, 木曜日, 金曜日, 土曜日.
