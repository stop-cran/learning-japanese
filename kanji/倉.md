# 倉 — granary; storehouse

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9), the spreading upper strokes · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

倉 concerns a building or space for keeping goods, especially grain.
[倉庫](../words/倉庫.md) そうこ means a warehouse or storehouse.
The native noun 倉 くら can also name a storehouse; the dictionary family
includes cellar and treasury senses. A treasury here is a place for storing
valuables, not the gold or money itself.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the spreading top as a
roof over shelves, with the lower [口](口.md)-shaped box as a doorway.
This is an invented scene using the present outline, not a historical
component analysis or a claim that 口 supplies the meaning or sound.
The indexing radical is [人](人.md), not the separate building radical 宀.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソウ | on | [倉庫](../words/倉庫.md) そうこ - warehouse; storehouse |
| くら | kun | 倉 くら - storehouse; warehouse; also written 蔵 or 庫 |

In 倉庫, 倉 supplies ソウ and 庫 supplies コ. Do not substitute くら
inside this compound. Practice selects ソウ, which has a linked word
article; くら remains valid dictionary information but has no standalone
word article here, so kun questions are not selected.

Kanjipedia additionally lists にわか and a sudden or hurried sense, as in
倉皇 そうこう, "in great haste," and 倉卒 そうそつ, "hurried; sudden."
JMdict marks 倉卒 as formal or literary; that flag does not apply to every
word containing 倉. にわか is source-specific lookup information, not an
extra reading inserted into the frozen KANJIDIC2 lists.

## Notes

- [庫](庫.md) and [蔵](蔵.md) overlap in storage meanings. The noun
  くら has all three spellings in JMdict; this does not make the
  characters interchangeable in every compound.
- [急](急.md), "urgent, sudden," and [突](突.md), "thrust; sudden,"
  overlap the sudden/hurried branch, not the warehouse sense.
- [府](府.md) has a dictionary storehouse sense, and [室](室.md)
  shares the cellar sense. They are guarded comparisons, not claims that
  every prefecture or room is a warehouse.
- The storing branch also motivates conservative guards for
  [収](収.md), "collect; store," [貯](貯.md), "save up; store,"
  [置](置.md), "put; place," and [入](入.md), "enter, put in."
  A storage place and putting something away are related, not identical.
- Further conservative guards for [囲](囲.md), [保](保.md), [預](預.md),
  [容](容.md), [留](留.md) and [持](持.md) prevent unresolved broad
  enclosing, preserving, depositing, containing, retaining or holding
  choices from competing with storing; they do not make every use synonymous.
- [忙](忙.md), "busy," also has a sourced hurried branch, and
  [速](速.md), "fast," is conservatively separated from hurried action.
  Being busy, being flustered and moving quickly are not identical.
- Digital Daijisen and Nihon Kokugo Daijiten also give くら a qualified
  kabuki/planned-matter cancellation use, motivating guards for
  [止](止.md) and [停](停.md), not a general meaning of outages or bus stops.
  [延](延.md), "prolong; postpone," is a conservative shelving/postponement
  guard: cancelling and delaying differ, and the whole-word senses of
  お倉入り おくらいり are not automatically meanings of the character.
- [金](金.md) has a similar spreading top but concerns gold or money;
  [全](全.md) has a 王-shaped lower part and means whole or complete;
  [合](合.md) has a much simpler lower outline and concerns fitting
  together. They are shape comparisons, not storage synonyms.
- The character dictionary Jitsu also records storing and a prison sense.
  Its quoted old-dictionary readings include ほろぶ and もろし.
  These are source-specific historical lookup information, not ordinary
  replacement readings for 倉庫; they are not added to practice metadata.
  [亡](亡.md), with a perishing branch, and [死](死.md), "death," are
  conservatively separated from the old ほろぶ lookup, whose ordinary
  currency is not established by that quotation. This is not a claim that
  everyday 倉 means "deceased." The modern-shape mnemonic does not explain
  these senses.
