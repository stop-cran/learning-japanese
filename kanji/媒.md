# 媒 — mediate; go-between

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

媒 conveys the idea of something or someone acting between two sides. The 媒 in 媒体 is what carries something across; in the marriage word なこうど it can write the matchmaker.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 女 on the left and a 木 shape at the foot of the right half. [梅](梅.md) and [棋](棋.md) have their 木 on the left instead.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| なこうど | kun | 媒 (なこうど), a matchmaker; also written 仲人. KANJIDIC2 lists this kun reading; the standard-use reading is バイ. The reading ちゅうにん in this dictionary family belongs to 仲人, not to 媒. |

## Common words

- [媒体](../words/媒体.md) ばいたい — medium for carrying information

## Notes

[妹](妹.md) begins with 女 but ends in 未. In 媒体 (ばいたい), 媒 supplies ばい; in 媒介 (ばいかい, mediation or transmission), the same reading again points to a link between two sides. In [梅](梅.md) and [棋](棋.md), look for 木 at the left rather than below. [仲](仲.md) and [介](介.md) can both suggest someone acting between others; in 媒体 the intermediary can be a thing rather than a person. [旋](旋.md) can also mean to mediate.
