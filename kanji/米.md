# 米 — rice grain

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 米 (no. 119) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

米 refers to rice as grain, as in [米](../words/米.md) こめ and 白米 はくまい, "polished white rice."
It also represents the United States in 米国 べいこく. That country name is not a description of rice.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine grains scattered around the central cross.
This is a modern memory aid; no historical reconstruction is claimed here.

## Notes

Compare [ご飯](../words/ご飯.md) ごはん, "cooked rice; meal," rather than assuming English "rice" always maps to the same Japanese word.
[来](来.md), [木](木.md), and [金](金.md) provide visual contrasts in the central and diagonal strokes.
[飯](飯.md) has a cooked-rice sense, so English "rice" can correspond to either character depending on the word.
