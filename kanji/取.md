# 取 — take

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 又 (no. 29) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

取 concerns taking or obtaining. Begin with [取る](../words/取る.md) とる for taking an object,
then recognize シュ in 取得 しゅとく, "acquisition."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine 又 reaching for the 耳-shaped object on the left.
This is a deliberately modern memory scene, not an account of historical practices or derivation.

## Common words

- [取る](../words/取る.md) とる — take; pick up
- 取得 しゅとく — acquisition; obtaining a qualification or right

## Notes

取って (とって) is the て-form, not とりて: ペンを取ってください means "Please take the pen."
[聞](聞.md) puts 耳 inside 門; 取 puts 又 beside it.
[受](受.md) concerns receiving and [収](収.md) collecting; both ideas can overlap with taking.
