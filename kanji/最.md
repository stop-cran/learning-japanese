# 最 — most

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 曰 (no. 73) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

最 marks an extreme: the most, the first, or the last within a comparison.
In [最近](../words/最近.md) さいきん, nearness is understood in time, giving "recently; these days."
最も もっとも can directly express "most."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the top box as a marker above the rest, showing the highest degree.
This is not an etymology. The top looks like 日 in modern type, but the dictionary indexes 最 under 曰.

## Notes

最初 and 最後 select opposite ends of a sequence; 最 alone does not mean just "first."
