# 猫 — cat

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (as 犭, no. 94) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

猫 names a cat. Start with the independent word [猫](../words/猫.md) ねこ, rather than trying to read it ビョウ in an ordinary sentence.
The indexing radical 犬 appears in its narrow left-side form 犭; this does not make the whole character mean "dog."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a cat beside the plant-like top and field-shaped bottom on the right.
Those visible shapes are a memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

猫が寝ています。 (ねこがねています。) — A cat is sleeping.

Distinguish [犬](犬.md), the radical's independent character, from 猫.
[理](理.md) has a different left side and 里 on the right; [番](番.md) also contains 田 but lacks 猫's left-hand 犭.
