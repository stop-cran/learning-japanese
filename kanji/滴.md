# 滴 — drip; drop

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

滴 concerns drops of liquid and the action of dripping. A drop need not be water; 水滴 specifically identifies a water droplet.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a drop falling beside the tall, enclosed-looking shape on the right, as though water were dripping from a narrow vessel.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.
