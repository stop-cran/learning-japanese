# 波 — wave; billow

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

波 concerns waves, including waves on water and figurative fluctuations. 波 なみ is the native noun. 波長 はちょう means wavelength and supplies ハ in a compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside 皮 and imagine successive ripples crossing the surface. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical radical 水, no. 85, is written as 氵 on the left.

## Notes

- The linked 波 article illustrates 波がある なみがある for uneven or fluctuating performance. That expression does not require literal water.
- Japanese 波 does not itself name the English hand gesture wave. The gesture is expressed with 手を振る てをふる.
- Compare [彼](彼.md), he, and [破](破.md), tear or break. Their left components differ from 氵 even though the right sides look alike.
