# 附 — affix; append

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

附 is about attaching or adding something, and about going along with something as part of it. KANJIDIC2 lists “affixed”, “attach”, “refer to” and “append”. Kanjipedia's senses are ①つく。つける。 (attach), ②したがう。つきしたがう。 (follow; go along with) and ③わたす。 (hand over), and Digital Daijisen notes that 附 is used interchangeably with 付 ([補説]「付」と通用する). In [附属](../words/附属.md) ふぞく, JMdict's first sense is “being attached (to); being affiliated (to); belonging (to); going with” and its second is “attached school; affiliated school”; JMdict files 附属 in the entry headed 付属 (entry 1602700).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the ⻖ shape stands on the left. On the right is [付](付.md), "attach," built from 亻, a variant of [人](人.md), "person," beside [寸](寸.md), "sun (length unit); small." Picture a person attaching a small extra note to the side of a wall. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that ⻖ or 付 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists only フ, with 附属 and 寄附; JMdict lists 寄附 as a spelling of 寄付 きふ, “contribution; donation” (entry 1591400). Kanjipedia lists ブ, つく, and つける outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen gives ブ（呉） beside フ（漢）.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings ずき and づけ. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings ちか, ます, より, and よる. These are name use, not quiz readings.

These twenty cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [付](付.md) (“attach”): KANJIDIC2 lists “attach” for 附, Digital Daijisen says 附 is used interchangeably with 付 (「付」と通用する), and JMdict files 附属 in the 付属 entry and lists 附ける and 附く under 付ける and 付く.
- [属](属.md) (“belong; affiliated; genus”): JMdict's first sense of 附属 is “being attached (to); being affiliated (to); belonging (to); going with”, and KANJIDIC2 gives 属 the kun reading つく.
- [着](着.md) (“wear; arrive”): JMdict entry 1495770 lists both 着ける and 附ける as spellings of つける, and the 着 card lists つ.く and つ.ける.
- [加](加.md) (“add”) and [接](接.md) (“touch; join”): the first sense of 付ける (also written 附ける) is “to attach; to join; to connect; to add; to append; to affix; to stick; to glue; to fasten; to sew on; to apply (ointment); to put against” (JMdict 1495770), and JMdict glosses 付加 ふか, also written 附加, “addition; annexation; appendage” (entry 1496170).
- [従](従.md) (“follow; obey; subordinate”), [守](守.md) (“guard; obey”), [則](則.md) (“rule; follow a model”) and [循](循.md) (“follow; cycle”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 附 is したがう。つきしたがう。 (follow; go along with); JMdict glosses つきしたがう, written 付き従う, “to follow; to accompany” and “to serve; to work under; to obey” (entry 1597810); KANJIDIC2 gives 従 the kun reading したが.う and 循 “follow”.
- [供](供.md) (“accompany, offer”) and [仕](仕.md) (“serve, attend”): the sixth sense of 付く (also written 附く) is “to accompany; to attend; to follow; to study with” (JMdict 1495740), and the second sense of 付き従う is “to serve; to work under; to obey”.
- [与](与.md) (“give; grant”) and [授](授.md) (“grant; impart; teach”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 附 is わたす。 (hand over), with the example 附与, and JMdict lists 附与 as a rarely used kanji form of 付与 ふよ, “grant; allowance; endowment; bestowal; assignment; conferment” (entry 1496430).
- [渡](渡.md) (“cross over”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 附 is わたす and Digital Daijisen's third is 手渡す (hand over); the third sense of 渡す わたす is “to hand over; to hand in; to pass; to give; to transfer” (JMdict 1444610), and the 渡 card lists わた.す.
- [譲](譲.md) (“hand over; yield”), [届](届.md) (“reach; deliver; notify”) and [納](納.md) (“pay; deliver; put away; accept”): Digital Daijisen's second and third senses of 附 are 届ける (deliver) and 手渡す (hand over).
- [任](任.md) (“responsibility; entrust”), [委](委.md) (“entrust; committee”) and [預](預.md) (“deposit; entrust”): Digital Daijisen's fourth sense of 附 is 任せる (entrust), with the example 附託, which JMdict lists as a spelling of 付託 ふたく, “committal; reference; submission (e.g. to committee); commitment” (entry 1496390); the 任 card lists まか.せる.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 外 (“outside”), 出 (“go out, exit”), and 間 (“interval, between”); 外 and 出 name being or going outside, and 間 names a gap between things, so none means affixing or appending.
