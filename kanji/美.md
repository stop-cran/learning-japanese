# 美 — beauty

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 羊 (no. 123) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

美 concerns beauty: something pleasing to look at, hear, or appreciate.
[美しい](../words/美しい.md) うつくしい is the adjective "beautiful."
美術 びじゅつ brings the same character into the vocabulary of art.

The indexing radical 羊 forms the horned upper portion above 大; it is not a separate full-height 羊 beside 大.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture that horned top and the broad 大 below as a frame around an impressive artwork.
This is a modern visual aid, not an origin story.
