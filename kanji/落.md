# 落 — fall down

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

落 connects a downward movement with something losing its position. In [落ちる](../words/落ちる.md) おちる,
the thing itself falls; 落とす おとす describes someone dropping it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** under the plant top 艹, imagine the water-shaped left part
carrying something downward past the 各 shape. This is a memory aid, not a historical derivation.
The indexing radical 艸 appears here as 艹.

## Notes

葉が落ちる (はがおちる) means "a leaf falls." 葉を落とす (はをおとす) instead presents
the leaf as the object being dropped or shed.
[葉](葉.md), [薬](薬.md), and [茶](茶.md) share the plant top, but their lower shapes differ.
Do not identify 落 from 艹 alone.
