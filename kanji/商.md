# 商 — commerce

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

商 points to trade and selling. A [商店](../words/商店.md) しょうてん is a shop; 商品 しょうひん is merchandise, the goods being sold.
The character helps connect the business and its stock, but it is not itself the everyday standalone word for a shop.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a shop beneath the top strokes, with goods above the small 口 near the bottom. This invented image is not a historical explanation.

## Notes

Compare [高](高.md): both have a cap and a lower framed area, but their interior strokes differ.
[南](南.md) and [同](同.md) are also useful shape comparisons, not trade-related meanings.
Do not infer that every word containing 商 names a physical shop; 商品 names what is sold.
