# 曇 — cloud over; cloudy weather

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

曇 concerns clouding over or becoming dim. 曇る くもる can describe an overcast sky or a surface fogging up, while 曇り くもり names cloudy weather. 曇天 どんてん is an overcast sky.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 日 above 雲 and imagine the sun obscured by a cloud. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified etymology.

## Notes

- 曇る is an intransitive godan verb: 曇ります くもります, 曇って くもって, 曇らない くもらない.
- Distinguish 曇り くもり, a weather condition, from [雲](../words/雲.md) くも, a cloud. The related ideas do not give the nouns the same reading.
- [晴](晴.md), clear skies, is a useful weather contrast.
