# 謙 — humble; modest

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

謙 is about humbling oneself and not putting oneself forward. KANJIDIC2 lists “self-effacing”, “humble oneself”, “condescend” and “be modest”, and Kanjipedia explains it as へりくだる (to humble oneself) and as keeping one's manner modest and respectful. [謙虚](../words/謙虚.md) けんきょ is glossed “modest; humble” in JMdict, and [謙遜](../words/謙遜.md) けんそん is glossed “modesty; humility; being humble”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [言](言.md), "say; words," with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it, stands on the left. On the right is [兼](兼.md), "combine; concurrent roles," with [八](八.md), "eight," at its top, the shape ⺕ and two vertical strokes 丨. Picture a person who holds many roles at once yet speaks about themselves quietly and modestly. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 兼 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ケン only, with 謙虚 and 謙譲 (けんじょう, “modesty; humility”, JMdict entry 1260210). KANJIDIC2 and Digital Daijisen give the kun reading へりくだる, and Kanjipedia marks it as outside the Joyo table.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings かね and ゆずる. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings あき, かた, かね, しず, のり, ゆずる, and よし. These are name use, not quiz readings.

These five cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [申](申.md) (“humbly say”), [伺](伺.md) (“ask humbly; visit humbly”) and [致](致.md) (“do humbly; bring about; send”): their titles' “humbly” overlaps this card's “humble” and KANJIDIC2's “humble oneself” for 謙.
- [慎](慎.md) (“careful; prudent; discreet”): KANJIDIC2 gives 慎 “humility”, and JMdict glosses 謙遜 “modesty; humility; being humble”.
- [敬](敬.md) (“respect; honor”): Kanjipedia's sense of 謙 includes うやうやしくする (to act respectfully), which overlaps “respect”.

The [譲](譲.md) card (“hand over; yield”) declares the 謙/譲 pair, because Kanjipedia gives へりくだる for both characters.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 強 (“strong”), 汚 (“dirty; defile”), and 怒 (“anger”); none means humble or modest.
