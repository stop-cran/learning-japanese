# 常 — usual; constant

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

常 covers what is usual, regular, or continuing. 日常 にちじょう concerns everyday life, while 常に つねに means
"always" or "constantly." In [非常口](../words/非常口.md) ひじょうぐち, 非 negates the ordinary:
非常 means an emergency, and the whole word is an emergency exit.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** remember the familiar upper shape of [堂](堂.md), but hang a 巾 below it instead of putting 土 there.
This is a comparison of modern shapes, not an account of their origins.

## Notes

The bottom component and indexing radical are 巾.
Distinguish 常 from [堂](堂.md), which ends in 土.
[帰](帰.md) and [席](席.md) also include 巾, but do not have the same top.
In 非常口, read 常 as じょう, not つね.
