# 膚 — skin; bodily surface

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

膚 concerns skin and the body's surface. [皮膚](../words/皮膚.md) ひふ is a useful anatomical noun. The character also occurs as an alternative spelling of はだ, but that spelling has a qualification below.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine an outer covering above the field-like 田 and flesh-shaped lower part.

The canonical key is 肉, no. 130. Here its month-like form is at the bottom, not a separate 月 key on the left.

## Notes

- JMdict marks 膚 as a rare kanji spelling in the 肌/膚 family. That does not make the pronunciation はだ rare; the linked everyday [肌](../words/肌.md) article uses 肌.
- [皮](皮.md) also covers skin and outer coverings. These characters do not divide neatly into nonoverlapping biological categories.
- The similar lower structure in [胃](胃.md) does not give 膚 the meaning stomach.
