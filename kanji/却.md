# 却 — step back; reject

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 卩 (no. 26) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

却 means stepping back or rejecting. Digital Daijisen gives four senses: retreating, as in 退却; rejecting, as in 却下; removing or disposing of something, as in 売却 and [返却](../words/返却.md); and doing something completely, as in 忘却 ぼうきゃく, which JMdict glosses as “forgetting completely; oblivion.”

## Common words

- [返却](../words/返却.md) へんきゃく — return of something borrowed or entrusted
- 却下 きゃっか — rejection; dismissal
- 売却 ばいきゃく — selling off
- 冷却 れいきゃく — cooling

## Notes

In 却下, キャク is pronounced きゃっ before か. Here “source-only” means that a source lists the reading but no checked word example uses it; it does not mean しりぞく is invalid for 却 in every dictionary or context.

退 (“withdraw; retreat”) appears with 却 in 退却 and is the main spelling of しりぞく and しりぞける. 断 (“sever; refuse”) and 否 (“deny; negate”) overlap with refusal, and 返 (“give back; repay”) is the other kanji in 返却. 逆 (“reverse; opposite”) and 反 (“oppose; reverse”) overlap with the “on the contrary” sense of かえって; JMdict also lists 反って as a spelling of かえって.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 女 (“woman, female”), 田 (“rice field”), and 花 (“flower”); none means stepping back or rejecting.
