# 泡 — bubble; foam; froth

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

泡 is a bubble, or a mass of bubbles: foam on soap or beer, froth on water. The linked [泡](../words/泡.md) あわ is JMdict's "bubble; foam; froth; suds; lather," and the word article reads it as one noun that covers one bubble or many.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ホウ: a ball of water that encloses air, an あわ. Its examples include 泡影 ほうえい, "bubbles and shadows; something transient," and 泡沫 ほうまつ, and 気泡, 水泡 and 発泡. Under あわ it lists 泡盛 あわもり, 泡雪 and 一泡. It also lists hard-to-read words: 泡銭 あぶくぜに, 泡沫 うたかた and 水泡 みなわ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left and [包](包.md), "wrap; enclose," at the right. Imagine water wrapped around a puff of air. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or 包 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ホウ | on | 気泡 きほう — (air) bubble (esp. in a liquid); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| あわ | kun | [泡](../words/泡.md) あわ — bubbles; foam |

The selected reading あわ is the reading of the linked word article, which is 泡 alone with no okurigana. JMdict also gives 泡 the reading あぶく, restricted to the spelling 泡; the Kanjipedia page lists it but KANJIDIC2 does not, so it is not part of this card's metadata or questions. 泡立つ あわだつ, "to bubble; to foam; to froth," uses the same あわ. 水泡 すいほう is "foam; bubble," and its second sense, "nothing," is the base of the idiom 水泡に帰する すいほうにきする, "to come to nothing; to go down the drain; to go up in smoke." In 気泡 and 水泡, 気 supplies キ and 水 supplies スイ, and 泡 supplies ほう. In 発泡 はっぽう, "foaming," the same ホウ is read ぽう after the small っ. 泡沫 is "bubble on surface of liquid" and "ephemeral; transient," read うたかた or ほうまつ: each is a reading of the whole word, so it is not split between its two kanji.

JMdict lists 泡雪 as a spelling of あわゆき, "light snow; light snowfall; thin layer of snow," beside the first-listed spelling 淡雪; the reading belongs to the whole word. 泡盛 あわもり, "awamori; strong Okinawan liquor distilled from rice or millet," is a whole word too.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ホウ with 気泡, 水泡 and 発泡, and あわ with 泡 and 泡立つ. Practice selects あわ, the reading explained by the linked article. ホウ is shown with a dictionary example and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- The common-word article is for the single character 泡, so it has no other kanji. In the examples above, [気](気.md), "spirit, air," [水](水.md), "water," and [発](発.md), "depart, emit," are existing cards.
- [池](池.md), "pond; reservoir," [河](河.md), "river; watercourse," and [泊](泊.md), "stay overnight; anchor," also have 氵 at the left, so they look alike at a glance. They are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [沸](沸.md), "boil; seethe," overlaps in meaning: boiling water forms bubbles and 泡立つ is to foam. It is kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "bubble" or "foam" as exclusive to any one character.
- 泡雪 あわゆき is also a spelling of the word 淡雪, so the later [淡](淡.md) card meets the same word. Daijisen's あわ entry for 淡 lists 淡雪, too.
- 一泡 ひとあわ, "blow; shock," and 泡銭 あぶくぜに, "easy money," are listed by Daijisen under あわ and the hard-to-read words; they are whole words.
