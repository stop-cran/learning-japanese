# 氏 — clan; family name

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 氏 (no. 83) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

氏 concerns a family name or lineage. It is also used after a person's name as a respectful suffix, normally read し in modern reporting. The suffix does not specify marital status.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a family label resting along the lower sweeping stroke. This is a memory aid for the whole shape, not a reconstruction of its origin.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | [氏](../words/氏.md) し - respectful name suffix; [氏名](../words/氏名.md) しめい - full name |
| うじ | kun | [氏](../words/氏.md) うじ - family name; lineage; clan (the article distinguishes this from its primary し) |
| -うじ | kun suffix | 山田氏 やまだうじ - Mr. Yamada (older name-suffix use; see below) |

The suffix example illustrates an older use documented by Japanese dictionaries. For a contemporary reference to Mr./Ms. Yamada, learn 山田氏 やまだし instead; do not replace modern name-suffix し with うじ mechanically.

## Common words

- [氏名](../words/氏名.md) しめい - full name
- [氏](../words/氏.md) し - respectful suffix after a name; also clan in the relevant context
- 氏 うじ - family name; lineage

## Notes

- The linked 氏 word article has primary reading **し**, not うじ. Both readings can have a clan-related meaning; the difference is not a strict split between "respectful title" and "clan."
- [姓](姓.md), surname, overlaps with 氏's family-name meaning. It is a useful comparison, not a safe wrong answer for this card.
- Distinguish [民](民.md), populace, and [低](低.md), low. 氏 is also visible within [紙](紙.md), paper, but 紙 is a different character.
- KANJIDIC2 lists both うじ and suffix -うじ. The dash marks attachment, not an extra sound.
