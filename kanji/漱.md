# 漱 — rinse the mouth; gargle

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

漱 concerns **rinsing the mouth or gargling**. The everyday action is usefully learned as [うがい](../words/うがい.md); 漱 is a rare kanji spelling of that noun, not its required written form.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Connect the water-shaped 氵 on the left with water moving around the mouth. The dense right side is a shape to recognize, not a literal diagram of gargling.

In 漱ぐ **くちすすぐ**, the one character covers **くちすす** and only ぐ is written outside it. Do not silently rewrite that spelling as 口漱ぐ. The separate reading **すすぐ** has a different-sized spoken stem despite the same written form.

## Common words

The four-character expression above is reading support, not everyday hygiene vocabulary.

## Notes

- 漱ぐ すすぐ is specifically mouth-rinsing in the selected dictionary family. Do not transfer every laundry or washing sense of the separate 濯ぐ family to it.
- Rinsing the mouth is not swallowing a drink. Compare [飲](飲.md), drink, and [歌](歌.md), song or sing; neither is a suitable translation of 漱.
- [速](速.md), fast, has 辶 surrounding 束 rather than 漱's left-hand 氵 and additional right-hand strokes.
- The linked うがい article preserves its kana headword and primary reading. No medical benefit is implied by these language examples.
