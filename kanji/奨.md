# 奨 — encourage; exhort; promote

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

奨 is urging someone on and backing them: encouraging, recommending, promoting a good effort. [奨学金](../words/奨学金.md) しょうがくきん is money that supports study, a scholarship or a student loan; JMdict glosses it "scholarship; stipend; bursary; grant-in-aid" and, in a second sense, "student loan."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ショウ: recommending, and helping and encouraging. Its examples are 奨学 しょうがく, "encouragement to study"; 奨励 しょうれい, "encouragement; promotion; inducement; incitement"; 勧奨 かんしょう, "encouragement; stimulation"; 推奨 すいしょう, "recommendation; endorsement"; and 報奨 ほうしょう, "bonus; bounty; reward; compensation." For the kun reading it gives すすめる, the same as KANJIDIC2.

The older form of 奨 is 奬; the Daijisen character entry and the official Joyo table show it in parentheses, and this card teaches the modern 13-stroke 奨.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a narrow upright shape, 丬, at the top left, a smaller shape beside it at the top right, and [大](大.md), "big, large," at the bottom. Imagine a big crowd at the bottom cheering on the small figure above them, urging it to keep going. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 丬 or 大 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ショウ | on | [奨学金](../words/奨学金.md) しょうがくきん — scholarship; student loan |
| ソウ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict has no entry that reads 奨 as そう, and the official table does not list it |
| すす.める | kun | 奨める すすめる - to recommend (someone to do); to advise; to encourage; to urge; JMdict marks the spelling 奨める as rarely used, beside the main spelling 勧める; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 奨学金, 奨 supplies ショウ, 学 supplies ガク and 金 supplies キン; JMdict also lists the variant reading しょうがっきん, in which がく shortens to がっ. In 奨める, 奨 writes すす and める is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ショウ, with 奨励, 奨学金 and 推奨 as examples; it gives no kun reading for 奨, so ソウ and すすめる are not in the table. Practice selects ショウ, the reading with a linked word article; すす.める is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, and ソウ has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word are [学](学.md), "study," and [金](金.md), "gold, money," in 奨学金.
- [美](美.md), "beauty," and [奥](奥.md), "interior; depths," also have 大 at the bottom, and [奇](奇.md), "strange; extraordinary," has 大 at the top. All are different kanji.
- [励](励.md), "encourage; be diligent," [勧](勧.md), "recommend; encourage," [促](促.md), "urge; prompt," [督](督.md), "supervise; urge," and [賞](賞.md), "prize; reward," overlap the senses "encourage," "urge," "recommend" and "reward" that 奨 and its words carry; 励 and 勧 appear with 奨 in 奨励 and 勧奨, 勧 is also read すす.める, and 賞 is also read ショウ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "encourage" as exclusive to any one character.
