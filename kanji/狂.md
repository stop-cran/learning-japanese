# 狂 — go mad; frenzied; go awry

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (as 犭, no. 94), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

狂 is being out of one's right mind or out of normal bounds. [狂う](../words/狂う.md) くるう has four JMdict senses: "to go mad; to lose one's mind; to go crazy; to go insane"; "to get out of order; to go amiss; to malfunction; to become imprecise"; "to go wrong (of a plan or expectation, etc.); to fall through; to get mixed up"; and "to go crazy (over someone or something); to get enthusiastic; to go wild."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for キョウ. One example of each:

- Mad: 狂気 きょうき, JMdict's "madness; insanity."
- Beyond the normal: 熱狂 ねっきょう, "wild enthusiasm; being crazy about."
- Comic: 狂歌 きょうか, "comic (satirical) tanka."
- Fierce: 狂風 きょうふう, "raging wind."

Other examples for the first two senses are in Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [犬](犬.md), "dog," in its left-hand form 犭, beside [王](王.md), "king." Imagine a dog that has lost its head and runs wild. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 狂う, 狂 writes くる and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 狂おしい, 狂 writes くる and おしい is okurigana. In 狂気, [気](気.md), "spirit, air," supplies キ. In 狂風, [風](風.md), "wind; style," supplies フウ. In 熱狂, [熱](熱.md), "heat; fever," supplies ネツ, sounding ねっ; 狂 is the second element.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キョウ with 狂気, 狂言 and 熱狂, くるう with 狂う, and くるおしい with 狂おしい. Practice selects くる.う, the reading of the linked word article. The other readings are shown with dictionary examples and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- 狂う has only okurigana, so it has no other kanji.
- [犬](犬.md), "dog," is this character's radical, which makes it a plausible wrong answer, but it is a different kanji with its own readings. [気](気.md), "spirit, air," appears in 狂気, and [風](風.md), "wind; style," in 狂風. Neither is read くる.う, and they supply this card's other wrong answers. No other キョウ card is used as a wrong answer.
- The first sense also covers 狂人, 狂乱 and 発狂; the second covers 狂喜, 狂態 and 酔狂. 狂言 is a theatre and comic-play word. Other kanji for disorder and intensity overlap with these senses and are kept out of this card's quiz options: [乱](乱.md), "disorder; disturbance," [荒](荒.md), "rough; wild; laid waste," [暴](暴.md), "violence; outburst," [激](激.md), "intense; excite," [騒](騒.md), "make noise; uproar," [熱](熱.md), "heat; fever," [変](変.md), "change; strange," and [異](異.md), "different; unusual; strange."
- 狂 also stands alone in JMdict as きょう, "maniac; enthusiast; fan" or "mania; craze"; this is a suffix use of the same kanji, not a separate reading.
