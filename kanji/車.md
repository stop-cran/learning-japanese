# 車 — car, vehicle

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

車 is a pictograph of a wheeled cart seen from above: the horizontal strokes at the top and bottom are the wheels, the vertical line the axle, and the box in the middle the carriage body.
Over the centuries it was squared off. The original object was a chariot or cart, and today it covers cars, trains' carriages, bicycles and any wheeled vehicle.

As a radical it appears in kanji connected with vehicles and wheels: 転 (turn), 輪 (wheel) and 軽 (light).
