# 技 — skill; technique

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

技 concerns skill or technique. 技 わざ is a skill or technique, 技術 ぎじゅつ names technical skill or technology, and 技師 ぎし identifies a technical professional. The shared character does not make the person and the expertise the same noun.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside 支 and imagine a hand supporting a practiced movement. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a verified origin or phonetic analysis.

The canonical radical is 手, visible here as the left-hand form 扌.
