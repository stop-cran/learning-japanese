# 蚊 — mosquito

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 虫 (no. 142), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

蚊 means **mosquito**, not an insect in general. The ordinary animal noun is a short **か**.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [虫](虫.md), insect, beside [文](文.md). Imagine a mosquito landing on a written page. This is a visual memory aid, not a claim about the character's historical formation or the origin of its pronunciation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ブン | on | 飛蚊症 ひぶんしょう - seeing floaters in one's vision; a medical term, retained for recognition rather than quiz practice |
| か | kun | [蚊](../words/蚊.md) か - mosquito |

Use **か** for the standalone noun. **ブン** occurs in compounds; Digital Daijisen explicitly lists 飛蚊症 under that character reading. The compound names a visual symptom, not an actual mosquito flying inside the eye. This card practices the animal noun without requiring a separate medical support article.

## Common words

- [蚊](../words/蚊.md) か - mosquito
- 蚊帳 かや - mosquito net
- 蚊取り線香 かとりせんこう - mosquito coil
- 蚊に刺される かにさされる - be bitten by a mosquito

## Notes

- The kana spelling **カ** also names the insect. It does not make the pronunciation long: ordinary modern か is not かあ.
- In 蚊に刺される, [刺す](../words/刺す.md) さす describes the biting or stinging action; 蚊 names its actor. The noun does not mean a bite, a net or a repellent.
- [虫](虫.md), insect/bug, is a broader category and can describe a mosquito. It is therefore not a good opposing meaning-quiz answer here.
- Compare [文](文.md), sentence/writing, with no left-hand 虫. [枚](枚.md), the counter for thin flat objects, has 木 at the left; [改](改.md), revise/reform, has 己. Those two characters share a four-stroke right-hand component with a slanting top stroke, unlike the dot-and-horizontal top of 文 in 蚊.
