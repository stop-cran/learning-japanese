# 倹 — frugal; thrifty

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as left-hand 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

倹 concerns thrift: avoiding waste and keeping expenditure modest.
[倹約](../words/倹約.md) (けんやく) means frugality, not the economy of a country.
[倹しい](../words/倹しい.md) (つましい), usually written つましい, describes frugal or modest living.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture the person-shaped 亻 beside the compact right-hand shape
as someone keeping a household's spending small. This is a memory aid, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

## Notes

Compare the similar right-hand shapes in [険](険.md), "steep; perilous," and [験](験.md), "test; verify."
Their left sides are 阝 and 馬, not 亻. [値](値.md), "value; price," shares 亻 but has 直 on the right:
a price is not the practice of frugality.

倹しい is つましい, not つつましい. The latter is a separate adjective with the kanji spelling 慎ましい;
their meanings can overlap, but the pronunciations are not interchangeable.
The older form of 倹 is 儉; this card's ten-stroke drawing teaches modern 倹.
