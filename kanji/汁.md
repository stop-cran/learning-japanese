# 汁 — juice; soup; sap

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

汁 is liquid that comes out of something, or liquid used for cooking: juice, sap, soup or broth. The linked word [汁](../words/汁.md) しる is JMdict's "juice; sap" and, as a second sense, "soup; broth." Digital Daijisen's しる entry lists liquid pressed or seeping out of something, as in レモンの汁; a seasoned cooking liquid; soups such as すまし汁 すましじる, "clear soup," and 味噌汁; and, figuratively, a profit gained from another's effort. Its character entry for ジュウ, marked as a customary reading with the older spelling ジフ, gives the plain meaning "juice."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the water radical 氵 (from [水](水.md)) at the left and [十](十.md), "ten," at the right. Imagine ten drops squeezed out of a fruit. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or 十 contributes meaning or sound, although 十 is read ジュウ and 氵 is a form of the key 水. Historical formation is not established here.

JMdict's entry 汁 lists the spellings 汁 and 液 and two readings: しる, restricted to 汁, and つゆ. Its senses are "juice; sap," "soup; broth" and "(dipping) sauce," the last restricted to つゆ, so the dipping-sauce sense is not a sense of しる. The linked article teaches しる; つゆ is supported but not selected. The row -しる stands for the ending form that attaches to another word: しる in 味噌汁 and voiced じる in 煮汁 and だし汁. In 果汁, [果](果.md), "fruit; fruition," supplies カ and 汁 supplies ジュウ; in 墨汁, [墨](墨.md), "ink; inkstick," supplies ボク. Whole words are not split: 灰汁 あく, "lye," which JMdict marks "word usually written using kana alone" and as a gikun or jukujikun (a special reading of the kanji as a whole word), and 苦汁 にがり, "bittern; concentrated solution of salts (esp. magnesium chloride) left over after the crystallization of seawater or brine," are Daijisen hard-to-read words.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジュウ with 果汁 and 墨汁, and しる with 汁 and 汁粉 しるこ, "shiruko; sweet red-bean soup with mochi," which JMdict marks "word usually written using kana alone." Practice selects しる, the reading with a linked word article. The other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; being outside the practice set does not make them invalid.

## Notes

- The common word is the single character 汁, so it has no other kanji.
- [水](水.md), "water," is the full form of the 氵 at the left of 汁; [十](十.md), "ten," is the shape at the right. [汚](汚.md), "dirty; defile," [池](池.md), "pond; reservoir," and [決](決.md), "decide," also have 氵 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [液](液.md), "liquid, fluid," [湯](湯.md), "hot water," [乳](乳.md), "milk; breast," [滴](滴.md), "drip; drop," [水](水.md), "water," [汗](汗.md), "sweat; perspire," and [涙](涙.md), "tears," overlap the liquid senses of 汁 and its words; 液 is also the second spelling JMdict lists for the word 汁, and 湯 overlaps soup and broth. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "juice," "soup" or "liquid" as exclusive to any one character.
