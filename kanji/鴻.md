# 鴻 — large bird; wild goose

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 鳥 (no. 196) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鴻 concerns **large birds**, including wild geese and swans in dictionary
explanations. The native noun [鴻](../words/鴻.md) (ひしくい) names the bean
goose. In compounds, the character can also convey greatness or large scale;
not every occurrence names the same bird.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 江 beside [鳥](鳥.md).
Picture a large waterbird beside the water-like strokes on the left.
This is a memory aid for the current shape, not a historical or phonetic rule.

## Common words

- [鴻](../words/鴻.md) (ひしくい) — bean goose, a bird name usually written in kana
- [鴻鵠](../words/鴻鵠.md) (こうこく) — large birds; a great person by metaphor

These anchors serve bird-name and literary recognition. They are not a claim
that every source reading or compound is everyday conversational vocabulary.

## Notes

- [紅](紅.md), "crimson; deep red," contains 糸 and 工, not 鳥.
  [鳴](鳴.md), "animal call; ring," places 口 beside 鳥.
  [島](島.md), "island," has a bird-like upper part above 山.
- The bird reading おおとり also occurs with other spellings, including 大鳥,
  鳳 and 鵬. The shared reading is not permission to substitute 鴻 freely for
  [鳳凰](../words/鳳凰.md) (ほうおう), the Chinese phoenix.
  大トリ (おおとり), the last performer, is a separate word.
- The grade-9/name-use classification does not make all uses personal names.
  The bird noun and literary compound above are independently documented.
