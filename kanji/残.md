# 残 — remain; leftover

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 歹 (no. 78) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

残 concerns what remains or is left behind. 残る (のこる) describes something remaining;
残す (のこす) describes someone leaving it.
The related-looking [残念](../words/残念.md) (ざんねん) is a whole word meaning regrettable or disappointing, not simply "leftover."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the short bars on the right as items left on a list after a slanting cancellation stroke.
This is a visual memory aid, not a derivation.

## Notes

ご飯が残った (ごはんがのこった) means "Some rice was left."
ご飯を残した (ごはんをのこした) means "I left some rice uneaten."
The shared 歹 in [死](死.md) is a shape clue, not a reason to translate 残 as "death."
