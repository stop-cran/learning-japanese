# 敵 — enemy; opponent; rival

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (as 攵, no. 66), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

敵 is the side you stand against: an enemy, an opponent in a contest, a rival, and, in compounds such as 匹敵, being a match for someone. [敵](../words/敵.md) てき is an opponent or enemy; JMdict glosses it "opponent; rival; adversary" and, in a second sense, "menace; danger; threat; enemy." [匹敵](../words/匹敵.md) ひってき is being a match for something; JMdict glosses it "to be a match for; to rival; to equal; to compare with; to be equivalent to."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for テキ. The first is facing someone on equal terms and competing, as in 敵対 てきたい, "hostility; antagonism; opposition," 匹敵, 無敵 むてき, "invincible; unrivaled," and 好敵手 こうてきしゅ, "worthy opponent; worthy rival; good match." The second is an opponent you compete with or hate, a foe, as in 敵意 てきい, "hostility; animosity; enmity," 強敵 きょうてき, "formidable enemy; strong enemy; tough enemy," 宿敵 しゅくてき, "old enemy; longtime enemy; arch-enemy," and 天敵 てんてき, "natural enemy." Under the reading かたき, which becomes がたき in compounds, it lists 敵役, 碁敵 ごがたき, "rival in (the game of) go," 恋敵 こいがたき, "one's rival in love," and 商売敵. In a usage note on the word てき【敵】, Daijisen says 敵 is the usual word, while かたき sounds somewhat old-fashioned and is used mostly in compounds such as 商売がたき or for a deeply hated enemy, as in 親のかたきを討つ. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a left part that has no card here and 攵 at the right, the same right part as in [敬](敬.md), "respect; honor," and [散](散.md), "scatter." The same left part is the right-hand part of [適](適.md), "suitable," and [滴](滴.md), "drip; drop," which are both read テキ. Imagine a stick at the right raised against whoever stands across from you. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that the left part or 攵 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| テキ | on | [敵](../words/敵.md) てき — opponent; enemy; [匹敵](../words/匹敵.md) ひってき — be a match for; rival |
| かたき | kun | 敵 かたき - rival; opponent; adversary; competitor; enemy (esp. one with which there is longstanding enmity); foe; also written 仇; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| あだ | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists あだ, but JMdict has no ordinary word that writes 敵 with this reading |
| かな.う | kun | 敵う かなう - to match; to be a match for; to rival; to equal; to compare with (with neg. verb); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 敵, the kanji alone is read テキ. In 匹敵, 匹 supplies ヒツ and 敵 supplies テキ; ヒツ shortens to ひっ before テキ, so the next sound is doubled: ひっ + てき gives ひってき. JMdict's かたき entry notes that the word is usually pronounced がたき when used as a suffix, as in 商売敵 しょうばいがたき, "business rival; professional jealousy"; its second sense is "revenge," and a third, archaic sense is "spouse." JMdict's entry for あだ is spelled 仇, 寇, 讐 and 賊, not 敵; in 仇を討つ, also spelled 敵を討つ, "to avenge (someone) by striking down their killer," the reading あだをうつ is restricted to the spelling 仇を討つ, and 敵を討つ is read かたきをうつ. In 敵う, 敵 writes かな and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict also lists 敵わない かなわない, "no match for; cannot compete with," usually written in kana and also spelled 適わない.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows テキ, with 敵, 敵意 and 匹敵 as examples, and かたき, with 敵, 敵役 and 商売敵. 敵役 is read かたきやく or てきやく, "role of the villain; the bad guy." あだ and かな.う are not in the table. Practice selects テキ, the reading with linked word articles. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [匹](匹.md), "small-animal counter; equal," in 匹敵.
- [敬](敬.md), "respect; honor," [救](救.md), "rescue; save; relief," and [散](散.md), "scatter," also have 攵 at the right. All are different kanji.
- [匹](匹.md), "small-animal counter; equal," overlaps the sense of 匹敵, "to be a match for; to rival; to equal"; [競](競.md), "compete; bid," and [争](争.md), "contend; dispute," overlap the rival you compete with; [対](対.md), "oppose; face," [抗](抗.md), "defy; oppose," and [反](反.md), "oppose; reverse," overlap the opponent who opposes you and appear in 敵対 and similar words; and [恨](恨.md), "resent; grudge," overlaps the hated foe of かたき. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "enemy," "opponent" or "rival" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the second テキ sense: 敵襲 てきしゅう, "enemy attack; enemy raid"; 敵陣 てきじん, "enemy camp; enemy line"; 敵愾心 てきがいしん, "hostility; enmity," for which JMdict lists 敵がい心 as a search-only kanji form; 仇敵 きゅうてき, "bitter enemy"; 残敵 ざんてき, "remnants of an enemy army"; 弱敵 じゃくてき, "weak enemy"; 政敵 せいてき, "political opponent"; 大敵 たいてき, "great rival; powerful enemy; archenemy"; and 論敵 ろんてき, "opponent in a debate or argument."
