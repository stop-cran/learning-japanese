# 探 — search

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

探 concerns looking for something or probing to learn more. [探す](../words/探す.md) さがす is useful
when looking for a desired thing; 探る さぐる can describe feeling around or investigating.
Searching does not itself say that the thing has been found.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the hand-side 扌 searching under the cover above the right-hand 木.
This is an image for the modern shape, not an etymological explanation.

## Notes

仕事を探す (しごとをさがす) means "look for work."
JMdict particularly associates 探す with something desired and the alternative spelling 捜す (さがす)
with something lost; this is a spelling tendency, not an absolute separation.

[持](持.md), [抱](抱.md), and [指](指.md) share the hand-side layout.
Their right sides are different: for 探, notice 木 at the bottom and the covering shape above it.
