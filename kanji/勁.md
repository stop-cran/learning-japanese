# 勁 — strong; taut

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

勁 conveys strength, including the idea of being stretched taut and strong. Learn it through [雄勁](../words/雄勁.md) ゆうけい, which describes vigorous, forceful expression in art or prose. It is not a general replacement for the everyday spelling [強い](../words/強い.md) つよい, "strong."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the 巠-shaped left part held taut beside [力](力.md), "strength," on the right. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical derivation or a claim about a phonetic component.

Reading questions select only ケイ, practiced as けい. In 雄勁, 雄 supplies ユウ and 勁 supplies ケイ. The full source reading つよ.い is preserved, but its indexed spelling is not recommended as ordinary usage or offered in reading questions. The link to the ordinary [強い](../words/強い.md) article is spelling guidance, not an example of a word written with 勁.

## Notes

- [功](功.md), "merit; achievement," and [劾](劾.md), "censure; impeach," also have 力 at the right, but their left parts and meanings differ.
- [剖](剖.md), "dissect; split open," has 刂 rather than 力 at the right. Keep that blade-shaped component distinct from the strength radical.
- [強](強.md), "strong," [剛](剛.md), "sturdy; unyielding strength," [力](力.md), "power," and [勢](勢.md), "force; momentum," have overlapping strength or force meanings. They are useful comparisons, not clearly wrong meaning choices.
- The source's grade-9 classification concerns name-use kanji. It does not mean every use is a personal name or that the character is ordinary everyday spelling. Personal-name readings are not taught here.
