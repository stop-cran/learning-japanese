# 映 — reflect, project

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

映 concerns an image being reflected, projected, or displayed. It appears in [映画](../words/映画.md), "movie," and also in words about something looking striking.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine light from the left-hand [日](日.md) projecting the figure-shaped 央 on the right onto a screen. This is a visual memory aid, not an etymology.

## Notes

映る describes what happens to an image; 映す describes making the image appear. Compare [写](写.md) in 写る うつる, "to appear in a photograph"; these are useful tendencies, not a claim that the meanings never overlap.
[英](英.md) also contains a 央-shaped lower part, but has a grass-shaped top rather than 日 at the left. [明](明.md) has 月 on the right.
