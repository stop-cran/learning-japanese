# 罰 — punishment; penalty; divine retribution

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 网 (no. 122) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

罰 is punishment given for breaking a rule or doing wrong, and in older religious use the retribution that gods or the Buddha bring on wrongdoers. [罰する](../words/罰する.md) ばっする is to punish or penalize; JMdict glosses it "to punish; to penalize; to penalise." The word article teaches "punish; penalize." JMdict has two noun entries for 罰: 罰 ばつ, "punishment; penalty" (with a note that it is "used as a suffix"), and 罰 ばち, "(divine) punishment; curse; retribution."

The Daijisen character entry gives 3 senses for バツ. One example of each:

- punishment for breaking a law or rule, as in 罰金 ばっきん, "fine; penalty";
- giving a punishment, as in 処罰 しょばつ, "punishment; penalty";
- divine retribution for evil deeds, as in 天罰 てんばつ, "divine punishment; wrath of God; justice of heaven; nemesis."

For バチ it gives divine retribution, with the example 仏罰 ぶつばち, "punishment by Buddha; divine retribution." It marks バツ and バチ as customary readings (慣). A note says 罸 is a variant form. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the key is 网, which appears at the top as 罒. Below it you can see [言](言.md), "say; words," and at the right [刀](刀.md), "sword; blade," in its standing form 刂. Imagine a verdict spoken aloud and then enforced. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that any part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| バツ | on | [罰する](../words/罰する.md) ばっする — punish; penalize |
| バチ | on | 罰 ばち - (divine) punishment; curse; retribution; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ハツ | on, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists ハツ, and Kanjipedia gives it as an outside reading, but no ordinary word was checked, so it has no example here |
| ばっ.する | kun | [罰する](../words/罰する.md) ばっする — punish; penalize |

罰する is read ばっする: 罰 supplies バツ, which becomes ばっ with a small っ (sokuon) before する, and する is the verb ending. ばっ is that sokuon form of バツ; it is not a separate kun reading, even though KANJIDIC2 lists it as ばっ.する. Compare the noun 罰 ばつ, "punishment; penalty," which keeps the full ばつ. The same small っ appears before き and そ in 罰金 and 罰則 ばっそく, "punishment; penalty" and "penal regulations; penal provisions; penal code," while 処罰, 天罰 and 刑罰 けいばつ, "(criminal) punishment; penalty; sentence," show ばつ. In 処罰, 処 supplies ショ; in 天罰, 天 supplies テン; in 刑罰, 刑 supplies ケイ. The compound 罰当たり ばちあたり, "damned; cursed; accursed," uses バチ; JMdict also lists バチ当たり as a spelling and 罰当り, 罰あたり as "search-only kanji form."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows バツ, with 罰金, 処罰 and 天罰, and バチ, with 罰当たり. Practice selects バツ, the reading of the linked word article 罰する, where it appears as ばっ. バチ is a Joyo reading with no word article of its own yet. ハツ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and is not used in any ordinary word checked here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 罰 is the only kanji in the linked word; する is the verb ending.
- [刑](刑.md), "criminal punishment," is a direct overlap with "punishment." [罪](罪.md), "crime; guilt," [犯](犯.md), "commit an offense," [償](償.md), "recompense; make amends," [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible," and [処](処.md), "deal with; dispose" (as in 処罰), overlap the wrongdoing and consequence ideas. All are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [四](四.md), "four," looks plausible because it resembles the 罒 shape at the top of 罰. [山](山.md), "mountain," and [歌](歌.md), "song, sing," are common starter kanji. They are read シ, よん; サン, セン, やま; and カ, うた, and share no reading or meaning with 罰.
- Other kanji read バツ, バチ or ハツ; none of them is ever offered as a wrong answer.
- For reference, Daijisen lists further examples of the senses above.
