# 政 — politics

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (as 攵, no. 66) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

政 concerns governing and public affairs. Start with [政治](../words/政治.md) せいじ,
"politics": the decisions and activities involved in governing, rather than simply a particular government office.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 正 on the left and 攵 on the right; imagine trying to put public
affairs in order. This is a memory aid, not a claim about the character's historical formation.
The indexing radical 攴 appears here in its compact right-side form 攵.

## Notes

[正](正.md) lacks the right-hand 攵. [教](教.md) and [敗](敗.md) share that right-hand form,
but neither means politics. 政府 names the governing administration; 政治 names the sphere of political activity.
