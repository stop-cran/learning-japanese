# 来 — come

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

来 is the modern form of 來. The old character is commonly explained as a pictograph of a wheat or barley plant, which was
borrowed for the unrelated word "come" because the sounds matched; this is the usual account, not a certainty. The key 木 (tree) in
dictionaries is a matter of shape, not meaning. The shinjitai form 来 simplified the inner strokes of 來.
It means "come" and, in compounds, "next" (来年, 来月) and "since/coming from" (以来).

- 来週 らいしゅう — next week

## Notes

- 来る is irregular (the "k-irregular" verb): the same kanji is read き, こ or く depending on the form: 来ます (き), 来ない (こ), 来る (く), 来て／来た (き).
- Opposite direction: [行く](../words/行く.md) (go away from the speaker) versus 来る (come toward the speaker).
- Look-alikes: [木](木.md) (tree) and [東](東.md) (east); also 未 (not yet) and 末 (end), which differ in bar length: 未 has the shorter top bar, 末 the longer one.
