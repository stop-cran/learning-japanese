# 燥 — parch; dry up

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

燥 concerns dryness or drying out, as in 乾燥. The native reading はしゃぐ belongs to a separate lexical use, being merry or in high spirits.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture [火](火.md) beside a [品](品.md)-like group of boxes over [木](木.md): imagine heat drying stacked wooden goods.

## Notes

- 燥ぐ is a rare kanji spelling; the word is normally written はしゃぐ. It is an intransitive godan verb: はしゃいで. Do not recommend this spelling as an ordinary way to write the verb.
- 乾燥 is a noun and also forms 乾燥する. That verb can describe something drying or someone drying it; the supporting article gives both particle patterns.
- Compare [湿度](../words/湿度.md), humidity. A dryness noun is not a measurement of moisture in the air.
