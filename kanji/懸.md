# 懸 — hang; suspend; stake

**Strokes:** 20 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

懸 is hanging something up or letting it hang, and, by extension, staking something on an outcome or keeping it on one's mind. [一生懸命](../words/一生懸命.md) いっしょうけんめい is doing something with utmost effort; JMdict marks it as a four-character idiom (yojijukugo) and glosses it "very hard; with utmost effort; as hard as one can; with all one's might; for dear life; eagerly."

The Daijisen character entry gives five senses for ケン. One example of each:

- hanging or dangling in the air: 懸垂 けんすい, "suspension; dangling; hanging," also "pull-up (exercise); chin-up";
- holding something up for all to see: 懸賞 けんしょう, "offering a prize; prize competition; prize; reward";
- entrusting or staking: 懸命 けんめい, "eager; earnest; strenuous; fervent";
- not yet settled: 懸案 けんあん, "pending question; unresolved problem";
- being far apart: 懸隔 けんかく, "difference; discrepancy."

For ケ, marked as a customary reading, Daijisen gives keeping something on one's mind, as in 懸念 けねん, "worry; fear; anxiety; concern," and 懸想 けそう, "falling in love; attachment." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [県](県.md), "prefecture," at the top left, [系](系.md), "lineage; system," at the top right and [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the bottom. Imagine a whole prefecture hanging by a thread over a heart that stakes everything on it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 県, 系 or 心 contributes meaning or sound, although 県 is read ケン and か.ける, readings KANJIDIC2 also lists for 懸. Historical formation is not established here.

| か.かる | kun | 懸かる かかる - to hang; JMdict heads the entry 掛かる and marks 懸かる a rarely used kanji form; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 一生懸命, 一 イチ becomes いっ before しょう (gemination), 生 supplies ショウ, 懸 supplies ケン and 命 supplies メイ. JMdict also lists 一生けんめい and 一生けん命 as search-only spellings. The older form of the phrase, 一所懸命 いっしょけんめい, has the same first sense, and JMdict marks a second sense as a historical term: "(of a samurai) devoting oneself to (the defence of) one's territory." 懸ける is a kanji form in the JMdict entry headed 掛ける かける, whose senses are usually written in kana and include "to hang up (e.g. a coat, a picture on the wall); to let hang; to suspend (from)" and "to wager; to bet; to risk; to stake." There 懸 writes か and ける is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 命懸け, 命 supplies いのち and かけ is voiced to がけ (rendaku); Daijisen's entry がけ gives 命― for staking one's life. In 懸念, 懸 supplies ケ and 念 supplies ネン; JMdict lists けねん first and also the reading けんねん, where 懸 supplies ケン.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケン, with 懸垂, 懸賞 and 懸命; ケ, with 懸念 and 懸想; かける, with 懸ける and 命懸け and the spelling contrast 懸ける ⇔ 掛ける, 架ける かける, "to suspend between two points; to build (a bridge, etc.)," and 賭ける かける, "to wager; to bet; to risk; to stake"; and かかる, with 懸かる and the contrast ⇔ 掛かる, 架かる かかる, "to span; to bridge; to cross." Practice selects ケン, the reading with a linked word article. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word are [一](一.md), "one," [生](生.md), "life, birth," and [命](命.md), "life; command," in 一生懸命.
- [系](系.md), "lineage; system," is the shape at the top right of 懸. [恩](恩.md), "favor; debt of gratitude," and [忘](忘.md), "forget," also have 心 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [掛](掛.md), "hang," [垂](垂.md), "hang down; droop; dangle," [努](努.md), "make an effort," [勉](勉.md), "strive, exert effort," [依](依.md), "rely on; depend," and [悩](悩.md), "worry; be troubled," overlap the senses of 懸 and its words: hanging in 懸ける and 懸垂, where 掛ける is the usual spelling, utmost effort in 一生懸命 and 懸命, entrusting in Daijisen's third sense, and worry in 懸念. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "hang" or "effort" as exclusive to any one character.
- [慮](慮.md), "consider; concern; forethought," shares worry and concern: JMdict glosses 懸念 "worry; fear; anxiety; concern" and 憂慮 "anxiety; concern; fear." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ケン senses: 懸崖 けんがい, "overhanging cliff"; and 懸絶 けんぜつ, "great difference." Daijisen lists 懸樋 かけひ, "open water pipe; conduit," which JMdict spells 筧 first, and 鈴懸 すずかけ, "plane tree," usually written in kana, with 篠懸 a rarely used kanji form, as hard-to-read words.
