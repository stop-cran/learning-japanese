# 層 — layer; stratum

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

層 concerns a layer or stratum, including a geological layer in 地層 ちそう. 一層 いっそう can be the noun one layer, but it is also an adverb meaning even more or all the more. The sentence determines which use is intended.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the rectangular levels stacked inside the 尸-shaped enclosure. This modern-shape image helps recall layers; it is not a historical derivation of the character.

## Notes

- The small っ in いっそう belongs to the combined pronunciation of 一層. It is not いちそう in this word.
- Do not translate every adverbial 一層 literally as one physical layer.
- Compare [増](増.md), increase. Its left side is 土; 層 has a 尸-shaped outer frame. An association with more in 一層 does not make the two characters freely interchangeable.
