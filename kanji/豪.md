# 豪 — powerful; excelling

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 豕 (no. 152) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

豪 describes great power and anything beyond the ordinary. KANJIDIC2 gives “overpowering,” “great,” “powerful,” “excelling” and “Australia.” Digital Daijisen gives four senses for ゴウ: strong and powerful, with great force (すぐれて力強い。勢いが盛ん。); a person who stands out in ability or wealth (能力・財力などがぬきん出た人。); beyond the ordinary, greatly (並み外れている。大いに。); and Australia. The declaring word [豪華](../words/豪華.md) ごうか, which Digital Daijisen lists under the third sense, is glossed in JMdict “extravagant; lavish; opulent; luxurious; magnificent; splendid; fancy; gorgeous.” A Joyo example word is 豪雨 ごうう, “torrential rain; heavy rain; cloudburst; downpour,” and the Australia sense appears in 豪州 ごうしゅう, “Australia” (JMdict 1285580).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 豪 is built from the shape 亠, then [口](口.md), "mouth," then the shape 冖, with the shape 豕 at the bottom. Picture a tall tower with a mouth in it, shouting so loudly that everyone below feels its power. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 口 or 豕 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ゴウ only, with 豪遊 ごうゆう, 豪雨 and 文豪 ぶんごう as examples. KANJIDIC2 adds えら.い, which Kanjipedia lists outside the table (外). Kanjipedia also lists コウ and つよい outside the table, and Digital Daijisen gives つよい as its kun; no checked JMdict word uses コウ or つよい for 豪, so they have no table row.
- Kanjipedia's fourth sense is an abbreviation for Australia, as in 豪州.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings こう, ご, すぐる, たけ, たけし, ひで and まさる; Digital Daijisen gives かた, かつ, すぐる, たけ, たけし, つよ, つよし, とし and ひで. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.

These twelve cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [偉](偉.md) (“admirable; great”): JMdict 偉い (1155780), “great; excellent; admirable,” lists 豪い as a rarely used kanji form, and KANJIDIC2 gives 豪 “great.”
- [傑](傑.md) (“outstanding; excellence; hero”): KANJIDIC2 gives 傑 “excellence” and 豪 “excelling,” and JMdict 豪傑 ごうけつ (1285560) is “hero; bold person; daring person; great person; extraordinary person.”
- [秀](秀.md) (“excel; outstanding”): its “excel” overlaps KANJIDIC2's “excelling” for 豪.
- [強](強.md) (“strong”) and [力](力.md) (“power”): KANJIDIC2 gives 豪 “powerful,” and Kanjipedia's first sense begins つよい (strong).
- [勇](勇.md) (“courage; bravery”): JMdict 豪傑 includes “bold person; daring person.”
- [剛](剛.md) (“sturdy; unyielding strength”): KANJIDIC2 gives 剛 “strength,” and JMdict entry 2080860, ごう, “strength; toughness; bravery; boldness,” is written 剛 or 豪.
- [華](華.md) (“flower; splendor; showiness”): both characters write the declaring word 豪華, which JMdict glosses with “splendid” and “gorgeous,” and KANJIDIC2 gives 華 “splendor” and “gorgeous.”
- [富](富.md) (“wealth; abundance”): Digital Daijisen's second sense for 豪 is a person who stands out in ability or wealth (能力・財力などがぬきん出た人), with 富豪 ふごう, “wealthy person; millionaire” (JMdict 1496780).
- [奇](奇.md) (“strange; extraordinary”): Kanjipedia's second sense is なみはずれる (to be out of the ordinary), and JMdict 豪傑 includes “extraordinary person.”
- [甚](甚.md) (“extreme; excessive; very”): KANJIDIC2 gives “great” for both 甚 and 豪, and Digital Daijisen's third sense includes 大いに (greatly).
- [盛](盛.md) (“thrive; heap up; serve food”): Kanjipedia's first sense includes 勢いがさかんである (full of vigor), and Digital Daijisen's first sense includes 勢いが盛ん.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 弱 (“weak; frail”), 静 (“quiet”), and 暗 (“dark”); none means powerful or excelling.
