# 恐 — fear; awe

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

恐 expresses fear, dread, or awe. [恐れる](../words/恐れる.md) おそれる means "to fear,"
and 恐ろしい おそろしい describes something terrifying.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** spot the [工](工.md) shape in the upper left and [心](心.md) underneath;
imagine a worried heart beneath an overhanging structure. This is a modern visual aid, not a history
of the components or a claim about their sounds.
