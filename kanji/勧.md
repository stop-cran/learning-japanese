# 勧 — recommend; encourage

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

勧 concerns recommending, advising or encouraging someone toward an action. [勧める](../words/勧める.md) すすめる can also recommend a person or item, or offer something for someone to accept. [勧告](../words/勧告.md) かんこく is advice or a recommendation to take an action. Neither recommending nor offering establishes that the recipient accepted.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice [力](力.md), "strength," at the right of a dense left-hand shape. Imagine putting strength into urging someone onward. The lower part of the left side resembles 隹; that is a visual comparison, not a claim that a bird contributes this meaning. Historical formation is not established here.

In 勧告, 勧 supplies カン and 告 supplies コク. In 勧める, 勧 writes すす and める is the kana ending. Practice selects カン and すす.める; the full source list also retains ケン. The absence of a linked ケン example here does not establish that the reading is invalid, obsolete or rare.

## Notes

- [観](観.md), "view; observe," has 見 at the right, where 勧 has 力.
- [確](確.md), "certain," has 石 at the left of its dense shape. Encouraging an action does not make its outcome certain.
- [権](権.md), "authority; rights," has 木 at the left. Having authority and making a recommendation are different ideas.
- [励](励.md) also has an encouragement meaning. 勧める can urge someone to do something, while 励ます (はげます) can cheer someone on or up. These uses can overlap; do not treat "encourage" as exclusive to either character.
- 勧める and [進める](../words/進める.md) share the reading すすめる, but the latter means to advance or move something forward. They are different verbs, not freely interchangeable spellings.
