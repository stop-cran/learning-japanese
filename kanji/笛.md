# 笛 — flute; whistle

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

笛 is a flute, and more widely any small wind instrument or blown signal. [笛](../words/笛.md) ふえ has two JMdict senses: "flute; fife; pipe; recorder; flageolet; shakuhachi; clarinet" and "whistle." 口笛 くちぶえ is "whistle (sound made with the lips)."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for テキ: ふえ, a wind instrument. Its examples include 汽笛 きてき, 警笛 けいてき, 牧笛 ぼくてき and 霧笛 むてき, JMdict "foghorn." Its kun reading is ふえ, also ぶえ in compounds such as 草笛 くさぶえ, "reed pipe," 口笛 and 角笛 つのぶえ, "horn (instrument; made of animal horn); bugle; hunting horn."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [竹](竹.md), "bamboo," at the top (written ⺮ when it sits on top) and [由](由.md), "reason; grounds," below it. Imagine a bamboo tube with a hole to blow across. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 汽笛, [汽](汽.md), "steam; vapor," supplies キ and 笛 supplies テキ. In 警笛, [警](警.md), "warn; be on guard," supplies ケイ and 笛 supplies テキ. In 口笛, [口](口.md), "mouth," supplies くち and 笛 supplies ぶえ, the voiced form of ふえ. 笛 alone is read ふえ as a whole native word.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows テキ, with 汽笛, 警笛 and 牧笛, and ふえ, with 笛 and 口笛. JMdict lists 牧笛 ぼくてき as "shepherds pipe." Practice selects ふえ, the reading of the linked word article. テキ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 笛 is a single-kanji word, so the linked word has no other kanji. In the examples above, 汽 supplies キ, 警 supplies ケイ and 口 supplies くち.
- Kanjipedia lists ジャク for 笛 as a reading outside its main set. No ordinary word with that reading was checked here, so it is not taught on this card. JMdict also tags the reading ちゃく for 笛 as "out-dated or obsolete kana usage."
- [笑](笑.md), "laugh," [答](答.md), "answer," and [筆](筆.md), "brush; writing," carry the same bamboo 竹 shape at the top as 笛, so they look plausible. They are read ショウ, わら.う and え.む; トウ, こた.える and こた.え; and ヒツ and ふで, and mean laugh, answer and brush. None shares a reading or a meaning with 笛.
- [管](管.md), "pipe; supervise," overlaps the "pipe" gloss that JMdict gives for 笛 and also names a wind instrument tube, so it is kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [敵](敵.md), [滴](滴.md), [的](的.md) and [適](適.md) share the reading テキ with 笛, and [寂](寂.md), [弱](弱.md), [着](着.md) and [若](若.md) share the Kanjipedia reading ジャク, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
