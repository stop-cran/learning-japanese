# 盛 — thrive; heap up; serve food

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 皿 (no. 108), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

盛 is fullness and vigor: food heaped in a dish, and things at their height or thriving. [盛る](../words/盛る.md) もる is "to serve (in a bowl, on a plate, etc.); to dish out; to dish up; to fill (a bowl) with" and "to pile up; to heap up; to fill up; to stack up." [盛り](../words/盛り.md) さかり is "height (e.g. of summer); peak (e.g. of cherry blossom season); (in) season; full bloom; full swing (of a party, etc.)" and "prime (of one's life); (one's) best days; bloom." [盛ん](../words/盛ん.md) さかん is "prosperous; flourishing; thriving; successful."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for セイ, strength and energy that are at their height, as in 盛大 せいだい, JMdict "grand; magnificent; lavish; large scale; prosperous; thriving; lively." For ジョウ it gives the same sense of thriving, as in 繁盛 はんじょう, JMdict "prosperity; flourishing; thriving." Its kun readings are もる, さかる and さかん. More Daijisen examples of セイ are listed in the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [成](成.md), "become," at the top and [皿](皿.md), "dish; plate," at the bottom. Imagine a dish being filled until it has "become" full, heaped and thriving. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 成 or 皿 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 盛る, 盛 writes も and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 盛り and 盛ん, 盛 writes さか; the り of 盛り and the ん of 盛ん complete the words. The repo labels the さか of both words as さか.る, so that is the listed reading linked in the table. The same さか.る reading also belongs to the verb 盛る read さかる, JMdict "to become vigorous; to gain force; to become more active," which has no word article yet. The さか.ん row has the same word 盛ん as its example; 盛ん is labeled さか.る in the repo's alignment, so no word is linked on that row. In 花盛り はなざかり, "flowers in full bloom; time of year in which flowers are in full bloom," 盛り is read ざかり, its さかり with a voiced sound.

JMdict also has a separate noun 盛り read もり, "serving (of food); helping," as in 大盛り おおもり, "large serving; large portion." That is a different word from the さかり taught here. In 盛大, 盛 supplies セイ and 大 supplies ダイ. In 繁盛, 繁 supplies ハン and 盛 supplies ジョウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セイ, with 盛大, 隆盛 and 全盛; ジョウ, with 繁盛; もる, with 盛る and 盛り上がる; さかる, with 燃え盛る, 盛り and 花盛り; and さかん, with 盛んだ and 盛んに. 隆盛 りゅうせい is JMdict "prosperity; flourishing; thriving," 全盛 ぜんせい is "height of prosperity," 盛り上がる もりあがる is "to swell; to rise; to bulge; to be piled up" and also "to rouse; to get excited," 燃え盛る もえさかる is "to blaze; to burn brightly," and 盛んに さかんに is "actively; energetically; heartily; enthusiastically; eagerly; frequently; repeatedly." Practice selects も.る and さか.る, the readings with linked word articles. セイ, ジョウ and さか.ん are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The words 盛る, 盛り and 盛ん are 盛 with okurigana only, so they have no other kanji. In the examples above, [大](大.md), "big, large," supplies ダイ in 盛大 and [花](花.md), "flower," supplies はな in 花盛り.
- [成](成.md), "become," is the top part of 盛 and is also read セイ and ジョウ, so it is never offered as a wrong answer. [山](山.md), "mountain," [海](海.md), "sea, ocean," and [店](店.md), "shop," are different kanji with different readings (サン and やま, カイ and うみ, and テン and みせ) and meanings. A heaped serving can look like a small mountain, a full dish like a sea of food, and a shop is where food is served and sold, so each looks plausible; none shares a reading or a meaning with 盛.
- [栄](栄.md), "flourish; glory," [富](富.md), "wealth; abundance," [豊](豊.md), "bountiful; abundant," [活](活.md), "lively," and [咲](咲.md), "blossom; bloom," overlap "thrive" and "full bloom." [旬](旬.md), "ten days; peak season," and [峠](峠.md), "mountain pass; peak; climax," overlap "peak." [積](積.md), "pile up," [重](重.md), "heavy; piled up," [充](充.md), "allot; fill," and [満](満.md), "full; satisfy," overlap "heap up" and "fill (a bowl) with." [注](注.md), "pour, direct attention," and [仕](仕.md), "serve, attend," overlap "serve food." They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of セイ: 盛況 せいきょう, "success; boom; being well-attended (of an event); prosperity," 全盛, 旺盛 おうせい, "lively; vigorous; energetic; healthy; avid (e.g. desire); rich (e.g. imagination)," and 隆盛.
- [益](益.md), "benefit; profit; gain," shares the 皿 at the bottom with 盛 and is a member of the same batch.
