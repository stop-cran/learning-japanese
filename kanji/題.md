# 題 — topic; question

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

題 concerns a **topic, subject, or question to work on**. It also appears in words for titles
and assignments; a 題-related word need not describe trouble.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the [日](日.md)-shaped box at the upper left of 是 as the label on
a topic folder, with 頁 forming its tall right edge. This is a mnemonic, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

[問](問.md) relates to asking or questioning; 題 identifies a subject or task.
問題 combines them, but 話題 is simply a topic and need not be a problem.

Compare [質](質.md), another dense character: its lower box is 貝, while 題 has 頁 on the right.
Neither the meaning nor the radical can be inferred from the number of box-like shapes alone.
