# 懲 — punish; learn a lesson

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

懲 means to punish or chastise, and, as in 懲りる, to learn one’s lesson from a bad experience. It appears in 懲役 (ちょうえき), “imprisonment with hard labor,” and in 懲罰 (ちょうばつ), “discipline; punishment.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [徴](徴.md), “sign; levy,” sits above [心](心.md), “heart”; 徴 is also read チョウ, like 懲.

## Notes

[徴](徴.md) forms the top and is also read チョウ, but means sign or levy; [微](微.md) and [徹](徹.md) share 彳 and mean minute and penetrate. [罰](罰.md), [刑](刑.md), [罪](罪.md) and [責](責.md) relate to punishment, crime or blame. [課](課.md) is titled “lesson,” as in a lesson in a textbook, and [習](習.md) and [学](学.md) relate to learning; they share only the English words “lesson” and “learn” with 懲’s “learn a lesson,” not its sense of being put off by a bad experience, and are kept out of its quiz choices so that the English does not mislead. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 111](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=111) lists チョウ, with 懲罰, 懲戒 and 懲役; こりる, with 懲りる and 性懲りもなく (しょうこりもなく); こらす, with 懲らす; and こらしめる, with 懲らしめる.
