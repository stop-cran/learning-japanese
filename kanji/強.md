# 強 — strong

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (bow, no. 57) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

強 expresses strength or intensity: a strong person, strong wind, or making something stronger. Strength can be physical, emotional, or practical.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 弓 bow on the left held firmly while a tiny 虫-shaped bug rests at the lower right. The firm bow reminds you of strength; this is not an etymology.

## Notes

弱い よわい (weak) is the usual opposite of 強い. [力](力.md) means power or strength as a noun; 強い describes something as strong.

[弟](弟.md) has a bow-like center rather than the left-right layout of 強. In 勉強, learn the whole word's meaning "study"; translating the two kanji separately does not give a natural English definition.
