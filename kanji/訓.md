# 訓 — instruction; kun reading

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

訓 concerns instruction, precepts and explaining or reading written characters. It also names a native Japanese kanji reading, a 訓 くん, in contrast with an on reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a teacher's words 言 flowing clearly like the right-hand 川, a river. This is a memory aid, not an assertion that either component had that historical role.

KANJIDIC2 records おし.える and よ.む. JMdict retains 訓える and 訓む as search-only kanji forms: the rows above are explicitly index evidence, not ordinary recommended spellings. For the teaching word, use 教える おしえる; 読む よむ is the non-index reading spelling in its family. The specific 訓む sense concerns reading a kanji with its native Japanese reading, not a recommendation to respell every occurrence of 読む.

## Notes

- The キン row uses the attested 家訓 かきん. The same entry also has かくん; dictionary order or a missing frequency flag does not by itself establish how frequent either reading is.
- JMdict marks 訓ずる くんずる as a rare term. It illustrates the source reading without making it an everyday verb recommendation.
- The noun 訓 くん uses an on reading even though it names the kun-reading category. A word's meaning does not determine whether its own pronunciation is on or kun.
