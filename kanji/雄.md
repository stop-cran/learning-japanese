# 雄 — male; hero

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

雄 is the male of animals and plants, and also manly strength and a person who stands out. KANJIDIC2 lists “masculine”, “male”, “hero”, “leader”, “superiority” and “excellence”. Kanjipedia's first sense is お。動植物のおす。 (male; the male of animals and plants), and its second is おおしい。いさましい。まさる。ひいでる。また、すぐれた人物。 (manly; brave; superior; excelling; also an outstanding person). Digital Daijisen's second ユウ sense is おおしい。勇ましく強い。 (manly; brave and strong), and its third is 規模が大きい。 (large in scale). [雄勁](../words/雄勁.md) ゆうけい is glossed in JMdict “vigorous; powerful” (entry 1839950).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is 厷, a shape that ends in 厶; on the right is 隹, a shape whose first two strokes are 亻, a variant of [人](人.md), "person." Picture the person on the right flexing a strong arm on the left: a male hero showing off his strength. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 厷 or 隹 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- The trailing dash in お- marks a use before another element; it is not pronounced. 雄しべ begins with it. JMdict also files お, written 雄, 男, 夫 or 牡, as a prefix and suffix whose first sense is “male” (entry 2742870).
- Joyo lists ユウ, with 雄大, 英雄 and 雌雄 as examples; お, with 雄しべ and 雄々しい among its examples; and おす. Kanjipedia lists おん and まさる outside the table (外).
- KANJIDIC2 records かつ, たけ, つよし, ゆ and よう as name readings; Digital Daijisen gives かず, かた, かつ, たか, たけ, たけし, のり and よし. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [男](男.md) (“man, male”): KANJIDIC2 lists “male” and “masculine” for 雄, the first sense of お, written 雄 or 男, is “male” (JMdict 2742870), the first sense of 雄 ゆう is “male; man” (2836662), and the 男 card lists お and おとこ.
- [鳳](鳳.md) (“male Chinese phoenix”): KANJIDIC2 lists “male mythical bird” for 鳳 and “male” for 雄.
- [傑](傑.md) (“outstanding; excellence; hero”): KANJIDIC2 lists “hero” and “excellence” for 雄 and “excellence” for 傑.
- [勇](勇.md) (“courage; bravery”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 雄 begins おおしい。いさましい。 (manly; brave), Digital Daijisen's second ユウ sense is おおしい。勇ましく強い。, the second sense of お is “manly; brave; heroic”, JMdict glosses 雄々しい おおしい “manly; brave; heroic” (1643720), and it glosses 勇 ゆう “bravery; courage; heroism” (1539650).
- [豪](豪.md) (“powerful; excelling”): JMdict glosses 雄勁 “vigorous; powerful”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “powerful” and “excelling” for 豪.
- [秀](秀.md) (“excel; outstanding”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 雄 includes ひいでる (excel), and KANJIDIC2 lists “excellence” for 雄 and “excel”, “excellence” and the kun reading ひい.でる for 秀.
- [優](優.md) (“gentle; superior”): KANJIDIC2 lists “superiority” for 雄; Kanjipedia's second sense of 雄 includes まさる (surpass), which it also lists as a reading outside the table (外); the 優 card lists まさ.る; and JMdict lists 優る as a kanji form of 勝る まさる (1603910), whose first sense is “to excel; to surpass; to exceed; to have an edge; to be superior; to outrival”.
- [英](英.md) (“English; outstanding”) and [偉](偉.md) (“admirable; great”): the second sense of 雄 ゆう is “excellence; greatness; best (of); great person; leading figure”, Digital Daijisen's ゆう headword includes 特にすぐれていること (being especially outstanding), and the first sense of 偉い えらい is “great; excellent; admirable; commendable; laudable; honorable; wonderful; outstanding; extraordinary” (JMdict 1155780).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 弱 (“weak; frail”), 鈍 (“dull; blunt”), and 柔 (“soft; supple”); none means male, heroic or excellent.
