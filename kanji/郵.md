# 郵 — mail; postal service

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 邑 (no. 163) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

郵 concerns the postal system and mail. 郵便 names the mail service or postal matter, 郵便局 a post office, and [郵送](../words/郵送.md) mailing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the 垂-shaped left block as stacked letters beside a town marked by the right-hand 阝. This is a modern-shape association, not a historical derivation.

The indexing radical is 邑, no. 163, in its right-hand form 阝.
