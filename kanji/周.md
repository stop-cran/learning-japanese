# 周 — circumference; circuit

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

周 concerns going around or the extent around something. 周り まわり can name a circumference or the surroundings, and 周囲 しゅうい also covers surroundings or circumference.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Follow the outer frame around the inner strokes and small 口, as though tracing a perimeter.

The radical is the small inner 口, not the outer frame. Do not replace its source radical with the enclosure radical 囗.

## Notes

- 周り is a noun here, not the dictionary form of a verb. Its ending り remains written in kana.
- 周 and 囲 occur together in 周囲, but they have different radicals: 口 for 周 and 囗 for 囲.
- Do not confuse 周 with 同, same, or assume that the larger character 調 has the meaning circumference merely because it contains 周.
