# 別 — separate, another

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

別 expresses separation, difference, or something distinct from the rest. 別れる is used when people part; 特別 describes something special.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the two narrow strokes on the right cutting one shape away from another. This is a mnemonic, not verified history. The right-hand 刂 is the side form of the radical 刀.

## Notes

Compare [分](分.md): 分かれる わかれる describes branching or splitting into groups, whereas 別れる commonly concerns people parting. The usual spelling for わける, "divide," is 分ける.

[切](切.md) also has the knife radical but focuses more directly on cutting.
