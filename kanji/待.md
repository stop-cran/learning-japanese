# 待 — wait, await

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

待 is the central kanji in "wait". Its compounds also concern anticipation or receiving someone, as in an invitation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a walker, represented by 彳, pausing beside the 寺-shaped right side to wait for a friend. This modern visual association is not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

待 has 彳 on the left. [持](持.md) has the hand shape 扌, and [時](時.md) has [日](日.md). All three share the modern right-hand shape 寺, so the left side is the key visual distinction.

In 友達を待つ ともだちをまつ (wait for a friend), the person you await takes を, not a literal equivalent of English "for".
