# 室 — room, chamber

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

室 means a room or chamber. It often follows a word describing the room's purpose, as in [教室](../words/教室.md), a classroom.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 宀 roof above a firmly stacked base as a room enclosed between ceiling and floor. This is a visual memory aid, not an account of the character's historical formation.

## Notes

For an ordinary room in conversation, use [部屋](../words/部屋.md) へや rather than treating bare 室 as its usual replacement.

室 and [屋](屋.md) share the lower shape 至, but their upper parts differ: 宀 in 室 and the wrapping 尸 in 屋. [家](家.md) names a house or home, not just one room.
