# 弦 — string (of a bow or instrument)

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (no. 57) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弦 is a string: the string of a bow, or a string of a musical instrument. In 上弦 (じょうげん) and 下弦 (かげん) it refers to the moon’s first and last quarter.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 弓 on the left and 玄 on the right; 玄 is also read ゲン.

## Notes

[弧](弧.md), [強](強.md) and [弱](弱.md) also start with 弓, and [玄](玄.md) alone means dark or profound. [糸](糸.md) is thread and [線](線.md) a line; [弾](弾.md) can mean to play a stringed instrument, and [琴](琴.md) is a koto.
