# 培 — cultivate; foster

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

培 means to cultivate or foster. The selected compound [培養](../words/培養.md) (ばいよう) is used for growing cells or microorganisms under managed conditions.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture soil beneath a carefully tended growth. This is a memory aid only; it makes no claim that the character's components supply a historical meaning or sound.
