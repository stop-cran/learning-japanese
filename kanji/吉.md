# 吉 — good luck; auspicious

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

吉 means good luck or auspicious. Digital Daijisen defines it as auspicious, or blessed with good luck, and gives the reading キチ in words such as 吉日 and 大吉 and キツ in words such as 吉報, 吉兆 and [不吉](../words/不吉.md). As a word, 吉 means good fortune, and Digital Daijisen gives 凶 (bad luck) as its opposite.

## Common words

- [不吉](../words/不吉.md) ふきつ — ominous; unlucky
- 吉日 きちじつ — lucky day
- 大吉 だいきち — excellent luck (in fortune-telling)
- 吉報 きっぽう — good news

## Notes

In 吉報 きっぽう, 吉兆 きっちょう and 吉凶 きっきょう, キツ becomes きっ. The Joyo table notes that 吉日 can also be read キツジツ. Kanjipedia also gives the kun reading よ.い, marked as outside the Joyo table (外); it is not in this card's metadata and is not quizzed.

福 (“good fortune”) and 幸 (“happiness; good fortune”) overlap with good fortune, 運 (“carry; luck”) overlaps with luck, and 良 (“good; favorable”) is the main spelling of the よい and よし adjectives noted above.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 下 (“down, below”), 事 (“matter, affair”), and 千 (“thousand”); none means good luck or auspicious.
