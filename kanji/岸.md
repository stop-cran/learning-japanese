# 岸 — shore; riverbank

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

岸 きし names a shore, coast or bank beside water. It is not restricted to the sea: 海岸 かいがん specifically means seashore or coast because 海 supplies the sea association.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 山 above a 厂-like middle shape and a 干-like base; imagine land rising beside water. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical explanation of the components.

## Notes

- The standalone noun is きし; 海岸 instead combines カイ + ガン.
- Compare [厚](厚.md), thick or cordial. Its lower shape is 子, not the 干-like strokes at the base of 岸.
- The indexing radical is the upper 山. The land-at-water meaning comes from the complete word, not a requirement to name a mountain.
