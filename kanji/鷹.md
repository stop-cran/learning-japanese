# 鷹 — hawk; falcon

**Strokes:** 24 · **Key (radical):** 鳥 (no. 196) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鷹 is the **hawk/falcon** character. The native word [鷹](../words/鷹.md) たか names a bird of prey; animal names are also written in kana, including タカ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 鳥 at the bottom, under the many strokes of the upper part. Picture a hawk sheltering under a broad roof. The roof image is only a memory aid.

## Notes

- This card has 24 strokes. Locate the upper enclosing part and the lower 鳥 before practicing the full stroke sequence.
- [鳴](鳴.md), an animal call or ringing sound, also contains 鳥 but adds 口 on the left. [島](島.md), island, ends in 山; [雲](雲.md), cloud, has 雨 on top. None means a hawk.
