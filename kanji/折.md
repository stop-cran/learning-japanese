# 折 — fold

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

折 connects folding with breaking by bending. [折る](../words/折る.md) おる can fold a sheet of paper
or snap something slender. The object determines which English verb fits.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the hand-side 扌 bending a sheet along the upright edge of 斤.
This is a modern visual prompt, not a verified explanation of the character's origin.

## Notes

紙を折る (かみをおる) is "fold paper"; 枝が折れる (えだがおれる) is "a branch breaks."
The を/が difference reflects doing something to an object versus the object undergoing a change.

[新](新.md) and [所](所.md) also contain 斤.
[投](投.md) shares 扌 but has a different right side. None supplies the folding meaning of 折.
