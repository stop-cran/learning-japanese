# 為 — do; act; for the sake of

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

為 is doing: carrying out an action or deed, and also the "for the sake of" that follows from it. The linked [為す](../words/為す.md) なす is a verb, and JMdict glosses its first sense "to do; to commit; to carry out; to bring about," marked "word usually written using kana alone," with 爲す as a "search-only kanji form." The linked [為替](../words/為替.md) かわせ is a different case: JMdict glosses it "money order; draft" and "exchange (e.g. foreign)," and tags its reading "gikun (meaning as reading) or jukujikun (special kanji reading)." Its reading is a whole-word reading, so it says nothing about what 為 reads alone.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for イ: doing or acting. Examples: 行為 こうい, "act; deed; conduct"; 作為 さくい, "artificiality; pretence; contrived act," with a second sense, "commission (of a crime); feasance," in law; 人為 じんい, "human work; human agency; art; artificiality"; 為政者 いせいしゃ, "statesman; administrator; politician; policymaker"; and 無為 むい, "idleness; inactivity." Daijisen also gives the kun readings なす, する, なる and ため for the same doing; its entry for ため gives "something that benefits," "cause; reason," "an aim," and "for someone."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** look at the bottom of 為: four dots, written 灬, the form that [火](火.md), "fire," often takes at the bottom of a kanji. [黒](黒.md), "black," and [魚](魚.md), "fish," end in the same four dots. Imagine four small embers glowing under the work being done. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that the dots contribute meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 為す, 為 writes な and す is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The repo's reading checker labels the な of 為す as な.る, a stem shared by な.る and な.す, so this card claims only what the linked word supports: 為す なす, JMdict 2861111, "word usually written using kana alone," and KANJIDIC2 lists な.す as a reading. 成す なす, "to form; to make; to constitute," is a separate JMdict entry whose fifth sense, "to do; to commit; to carry out; to bring about," is marked "word usually written using kana alone" with the note "also written as 為す." In 行為, [行](行.md), "go," supplies コウ and 為 supplies イ; in 作為, [作](作.md), "make, produce," supplies サク; in 人為, [人](人.md), "person," supplies ジン; in 為政者, [政](政.md), "politics," supplies セイ and [者](者.md), "person in a role," supplies シャ. 為 ため is also listed with the spellings 爲, marked "word containing out-dated kanji or kanji usage," and 為め, marked "irregular okurigana usage"; 為に ために is "for; for the sake of; to one's advantage; in favor of; in favour of; on behalf of." In 為替, the readings か, わ and せ do not belong to 為 or [替](替.md), "replace; substitute"; the whole word is read かわせ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows イ with 為政者, 行為 and 作為, followed by 為替（かわせ）, a special reading listed for the whole word. Practice selects な.す, the reading of the linked word article 為す. イ and the other readings are shown with dictionary examples or marked source-only and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. The table shows the older form 爲 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- 為す is 為 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji. 為替 has [替](替.md), "replace; substitute," but かわせ is a whole-word reading and is not split. For the other examples, see the Readings section.
- [黒](黒.md), "black," and [魚](魚.md), "fish," share the four dots at the bottom with 為, and [火](火.md), "fire," is its key (radical). They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [成](成.md), "become," [作](作.md), "make, produce," and [造](造.md), "make; construct," share readings with 為 (な.る, な.す and つく.る) and overlap the making and becoming senses. [行](行.md), "go," [致](致.md), "do humbly; bring about; send," [施](施.md), "carry out; give; alms," [執](執.md), "take hold; carry out; cling," [履](履.md), "footwear; put on shoes; carry out," [犯](犯.md), "commit an offense," [営](営.md), "conduct business; manage," [業](業.md), "work, occupation," [働](働.md), "work; function," [動](動.md), "move," and [演](演.md), "perform; stage," overlap doing, carrying out and acting. [幕](幕.md), "curtain; act of a play; shogunate," shares the word "act." [因](因.md), "cause; factor," [故](故.md), "reason; former; the late; mishap," [由](由.md), "reason; grounds," [理](理.md), "reason, logic," [利](利.md), "benefit; advantage," and [用](用.md), "use, purpose," overlap the "for the sake of" sense. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "do," "act" or "for the sake of" as exclusive to any one character.
- 無為 むい is "idleness; inactivity"; Daijisen lists it with 為 as イ.
