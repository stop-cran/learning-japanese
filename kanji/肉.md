# 肉 — meat; flesh

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

肉 means meat or flesh. In food words, the preceding element often tells you the animal:
[牛肉](../words/牛肉.md) (ぎゅうにく) is beef.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** picture the outer frame holding two stacked
person-like marks, like portions of meat arranged in a tray.

## Readings

The table focuses on the everyday reading ニク. Metadata also preserves the source kun reading しし;
it is not the reading to use when ordinarily asking for meat.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ニク | on | 肉 にく — meat; [牛肉](../words/牛肉.md) ぎゅうにく — beef |

## Common words

- 肉 にく — meat; flesh
- [牛肉](../words/牛肉.md) ぎゅうにく — beef
- 豚肉 ぶたにく — pork
- 鶏肉 とりにく — chicken meat
- 肉屋 にくや — butcher's shop

## Notes

肉 is not limited to food: its "flesh" meaning also describes bodily tissue.
The ニク in these words is an on reading even when it is the whole word 肉.

[内](内.md) has only one inner person-like shape. [同](同.md) instead has a horizontal line and a 口-shaped box
inside its outer frame.
