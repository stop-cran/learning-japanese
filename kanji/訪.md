# 訪 — visit; call on

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

訪 is the character for paying a visit. [訪ねる](../words/訪ねる.md) たずねる directs attention to going
to see a person or place. 訪れる おとずれる also has a separate arrival sense,
including the coming of a season.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** put 言 beside 方 and imagine heading in a direction to speak
with someone you are visiting. This is a modern association, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

友達を訪ねる (ともだちをたずねる) means "visit a friend": the person visited can take を.
[方](方.md) is the right-hand shape without 言.
[記](記.md) and [許](許.md) retain 言 but replace the part on the right.
