# 置 — put; place

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 网 (as 罒, no. 122) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

置 concerns putting something in a position or leaving it there.
In [置く](../words/置く.md) おく, an object is placed somewhere; in 位置 いち,
the word names the position itself.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine placing the upper 罒 neatly above 直.
This describes a visual arrangement, not the character's historical construction.

## Notes

本を机に置く (ほんをつくえにおく) means "put a book on the desk":
を marks the thing placed, and に marks its destination.
The top is the 网 radical in its 罒 form. Compare the lower parts of [罪](罪.md) and
[買](買.md); [目](目.md) has a related box-and-lines appearance but is not this radical.
