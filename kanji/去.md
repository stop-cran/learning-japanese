# 去 — leave, elapse

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 厶 (no. 28) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

去 concerns leaving, going away, or time passing. 去年 is the year that has gone by; 過去 is the past.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see [土](土.md) above 厶 and imagine a small shape moving away from a patch of ground. This is a visual story, not an etymology.

## Notes

去る describes departure, not simply motion toward a destination. Compare [来](来.md), "come," and [行](行.md), "go."

Distinguish 去 from [台](台.md): 去 has 土 on top and 厶 below; 台 has 厶 above a square 口.
