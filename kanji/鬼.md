# 鬼 — demon; ogre

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 鬼 (no. 194) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鬼 is a demon or ogre, and also the spirit of a dead person. KANJIDIC2 lists “ghost” and “devil”. Kanjipedia's first sense is おに。かいぶつ。ばけもの。もののけ。悪神。 (ogre; monster; goblin; evil spirit; evil god), and its second is 死者のたましい。 (the soul of a dead person). Digital Daijisen's first sense is 死者の霊魂。亡霊。 (the soul of a dead person; a ghost), and its third is この世のものとも思われない恐るべき存在。化け物。 (a fearsome being that seems not of this world; a monster). The word [鬼](../words/鬼.md) おに is glossed in JMdict as “ogre; demon; oni”, “spirit of a deceased person”, “ogre-like person (i.e. fierce, relentless, merciless, etc.)” and “it (in a game of tag, hide-and-seek, etc.); seeker; chaser; tagger; tigger” (entry 1224190). JMdict cross-refers that last sense to 鬼ごっこ おにごっこ, “tag (children's game); tig” (entry 1224200).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** at the top is 甶, a shape made of 丿 over [田](田.md), "rice field"; below it come 儿, a shape whose two strokes KanjiVG analyses as [八](八.md), "eight," and 厶, a small hooked shape. Picture an ogre with a big square head and a single horn on top, striding on two long legs with a little hooked tail behind. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 甶, 儿 or 厶 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- The trailing dash in おに- marks a use before another element; it is not pronounced. 鬼百合 begins with it.
- Joyo lists キ and おに, with 鬼 and 鬼ごっこ among the examples under おに. Kanjipedia lists no readings outside the table.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 鬼, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These five cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [魔](魔.md) (“demon; magic”): KANJIDIC2 lists “devil” for 鬼 and “demon” and “evil spirit” for 魔; the first sense of 鬼 is “ogre; demon; oni”, and JMdict glosses 魔 ま (1633990) as “demon; devil; evil spirit; evil influence”.
- [妖](妖.md) (“bewitching; supernatural”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 鬼 includes ばけもの。もののけ。 (goblin; evil spirit), and JMdict glosses 妖怪 ようかい (1545300) as “ghost; apparition; phantom; spectre; specter; demon; monster; goblin; yōkai”.
- [怪](怪.md) (“suspicious; uncanny”): KANJIDIC2 lists “ghost” for 鬼 and “apparition” for 怪. Kanjipedia's first sense of 鬼 includes かいぶつ。ばけもの。 (monster; goblin), Digital Daijisen's third sense ends 化け物。, and JMdict glosses 化け物 ばけもの (1601300) as “goblin; apparition; monster; ghost; phantom; spectre; specter”.
- [神](神.md) (“deity”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 鬼 includes 悪神 (evil god), JMdict glosses 鬼神 きしん (1577770) as “fierce god”, and the first sense of 神 かみ (JMdict 1364440) is “god; deity; divinity; spirit; kami”.
- [気](気.md) (“spirit, air”): the second sense of 鬼 is “spirit of a deceased person”, Kanjipedia's second sense is 死者のたましい。 and Digital Daijisen's first is 死者の霊魂。亡霊。; these overlap the title's “spirit”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 仏 (“Buddha; Buddhism”), 猿 (“monkey”), and 熊 (“bear (animal)”); none means a demon or ogre.
