# 共 — together; shared

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 八 (the spreading bottom strokes, no. 12) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

共 connects people or things through joint action or a shared feature.
[共同](../words/共同.md) きょうどう is cooperation or joint activity; 共通 きょうつう describes something held in common.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the upper horizontal bars as a shared platform supported by the
two spreading strokes below. This is a modern memory image, not a historical account.

## Common words

- [共同](../words/共同.md) きょうどう — cooperation; joint activity
- 共通 きょうつう — common; shared
- 共に ともに — together; usually ともに

## Notes

Shared activity and shared characteristics are different applications of the same general idea:
共同 concerns doing something jointly, while 共通 can describe what people have in common.
[具](具.md), [真](真.md), and [六](六.md) also have spreading lower strokes; 共 has no tall boxed stack above them.
