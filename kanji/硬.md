# 硬 — hard; stiff

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

硬 concerns physical hardness or stiffness. 硬い can describe a hard material, while 硬貨 names a coin. English hard also means difficult, but that does not make 硬い a general adjective for difficult questions.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 石 pressing against the 更-shaped panel and finding it too stiff to bend. This is an invented scene using the modern components.

## Notes

- 硬い is an i-adjective: 硬くない かたくない, not hard; 硬かった かたかった, was hard. The written い is part of its inflection.
- [固](固.md) also occurs in [固い](../words/固い.md) かたい, and [堅い](../words/堅い.md) is another spelling in the same dictionary family. Their uses overlap, but particular expressions and spelling notes matter; this is not a strict one-spelling/one-meaning division.
- The linked [硬貨](../words/硬貨.md) article divides the compound as コウ + カ and contrasts coins with banknotes.
