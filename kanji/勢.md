# 勢 — force; momentum

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

勢 concerns force, vigor, momentum, or the strength of a group. 勢い いきおい is force or momentum; 情勢 じょうせい describes a situation or state of affairs. The ゼイ reading occurs in 大勢 おおぜい, many people.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the densely packed upper strokes leaning on 力 at the bottom, like a crowd gathering momentum.

The canonical indexing radical 力 is the lower component.

## Notes

- 大勢 おおぜい, many people, combines native おお with ゼイ. The same spelling read たいせい means the general situation or trend, a different lexical entry. The linked 大勢 article teaches おおぜい.
- JMdict records the rare spelling 勢み はずみ, bounce or rebound, with written み. That related spelling does not establish 勢 alone as an ordinary spelling of the complete はずみ; the exact source row is retained as lookup information rather than assigned that example.
- In its force or momentum sense, 勢い is a noun, not an i-adjective despite its final い. 勢いよく いきおいよく means forcefully or vigorously.
