# 菓 — confectionery; sweets

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

菓 is useful in words for confectionery. [菓子](../words/菓子.md) かし and [お菓子](../words/お菓子.md) おかし cover sweets and snack foods rather than only one kind of cake.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine treats set above a fruit-shaped 果 base, using the plant canopy as a reminder of ingredients.

The canonical key is 艸, no. 140, appearing as 艹 above 果.

## Notes

- 菓子 is broader than pastry and does not require every item to taste sweet; the linked お菓子 article includes a nonsweet-snack example.
- Compare [果](果.md) in 果物 くだもの, fruit. A shared shape does not make fruit and confectionery interchangeable categories.
- The お in お菓子 precedes the compound. It is not part of 菓's reading カ.
