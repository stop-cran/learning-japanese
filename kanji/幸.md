# 幸 — happiness; good fortune

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 干 (no. 51) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

幸 concerns happiness and being fortunate. [幸福](../words/幸福.md) こうふく names happiness or well-being,
while 幸せ しあわせ can express happiness and good fortune.
The character is not restricted to a brief pleased reaction; it can concern how well life is going.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture good things stacked along the central vertical line, with a 土-shaped top.
This is an invented memory image for the modern form, not a claim about its historical meaning.

## Notes

The indexing radical is 干, despite the easily noticed 土-shaped top.
[土](土.md) is only a small part of the complete outline.
[年](年.md) and [立](立.md) share some crossing strokes, but neither has 幸's full stack of horizontals.
Do not treat the compound 不幸 as merely "not smiling": its meanings include misfortune.
