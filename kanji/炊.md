# 炊 — cook rice; boil

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

炊 is cooking rice or other grains over heat, and more generally doing the cooking. [炊く](../words/炊く.md) たく is to cook rice or grains, and [炊事](../words/炊事.md) すいじ is cooking or kitchen work. JMdict glosses the verb 炊く "to cook (grains, e.g. rice)" and, in a second sense that it marks as Kansai dialect, "to boil; to simmer; to stew; to seethe," where standard Japanese uses [煮る](../words/煮る.md) にる.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for スイ: cooking rice, as in 炊事, 炊飯 すいはん, "rice cooking; boiling rice," 自炊 じすい, "cooking for oneself; cooking one's own food; doing one's own cooking," and 雑炊 ぞうすい, "rice porridge made by simmering cooked rice in a seasoned broth, often containing vegetables, fish, etc. (usu. made from leftovers)." It lists the kun readings たく and かしぐ; JMdict marks the verb 炊ぐ かしぐ, "to cook (e.g. rice); to boil," as a rare term, and KANJIDIC2 does not list かしぐ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [火](火.md), "fire," at the left, and [欠](欠.md), "lack; gap," at the right, a shape that looks like a person with an open mouth. Imagine a hungry person waiting with an open mouth beside the fire while the rice cooks. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 火 or 欠 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| スイ | on | [炊事](../words/炊事.md) すいじ — cooking; kitchen work |
| た.く | kun | [炊く](../words/炊く.md) たく — cook rice or grains |
| -だ.き | kun suffix | 追い炊き おいだき - reheating bathwater; cooking more rice; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 炊事, 炊 supplies スイ and 事 supplies ジ. In 炊く, 炊 writes た and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the polite form 炊きます has たき before ます. The leading dash in -だ.き marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced. There the た of たき is voiced to だ (rendaku), as in 追い炊き, while 飯炊き めしたき, "cooking rice; kitchenmaid; cook," keeps た.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows スイ, with 炊事, 自炊 and 雑炊 as examples, and たく, with 炊く and 飯炊き. It has no separate row for だき. Practice selects スイ and た.く, the readings with linked word articles; -だ.き is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [事](事.md), "matter, affair," in 炊事. 炊く is 炊 with okurigana only.
- [吹](吹.md), "blow," also has 欠 at the right and is also read スイ. [次](次.md), "next," also has 欠 at the right. [灯](灯.md), "lamp; lamplight," also has 火 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [沸](沸.md), "boil; seethe," [飯](飯.md), "cooked rice; meal," and [蒸](蒸.md), "steam; sultry heat," overlap the senses "boil," "cooked rice" and cooking with heat that 炊 and its words carry; the Kansai-dialect sense of 炊く in JMdict also has "to seethe." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "boil" or "cook" as exclusive to any one character.
