# 皿 — dish; plate

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 皿 (no. 108) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

皿 names a plate or dish. It can also count a plateful or serving, as in 一皿 ひとさら. Separate the container from the portion of food rather than assuming that English dish always names tableware.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the rectangular outline and short vertical dividers as a sectioned serving plate. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a reconstructed ancient drawing.

## Notes

- The on-reading example 器皿 きべい does not replace the ordinary noun さら. [お皿](../words/お皿.md) adds polite お without changing which kind of object is meant.
- サラダを一皿注文しました。 (サラダをひとさらちゅうもんしました。) - I ordered one plate of salad. Here 一皿 counts a serving; physical plates can instead be counted with [枚](枚.md) まい.
- Compare the outlines of [目](目.md), eye, and [四](四.md), four. Similar enclosures do not make them dishes.
