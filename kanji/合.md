# 合 — fit together

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

合 relates to things joining, matching, or fitting. [合う](../words/合う.md) あう describes a fit or match;
合わせる あわせる can describe actively bringing things into line.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the two sloping top strokes coming together over 一 and 口.
This is a memory aid using the current outline, not a historical claim.

## Notes

[会う](../words/会う.md) あう means meeting someone; [合う](../words/合う.md) can instead describe matching sizes or tastes.
The wider idea of coming together links the meanings of 合 and [会](会.md).
