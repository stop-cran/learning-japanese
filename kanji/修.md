# 修 — repair; amend; cultivate

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

修 is improving something by working on it: repairing or amending a thing, or cultivating oneself. [修正](../words/修正.md) しゅうせい is correction or revision, [修理](../words/修理.md) しゅうり is repair, [修繕](../words/修繕.md) しゅうぜん is repair or mending and [研修](../words/研修.md) けんしゅう is in-service training.

The Daijisen character entry gives five senses for シュウ: making something look good with decoration, as in 修飾 しゅうしょく, "ornamentation; embellishment; decoration; adornment"; fixing something by working on it, as in 修正, 修理, 改修 かいしゅう, "repair; improvement," and 補修 ほしゅう, "repair; mending"; polishing character and mastering learning, as in 研修, 修養 しゅうよう, "self-improvement; (mental) training; self-discipline; cultivation," and 必修 ひっしゅう, "required (subject)"; keeping a relationship in good order, as in 修好 しゅうこう, "amity; friendship"; and compiling material into a book, as in 監修 かんしゅう, "(editorial) supervision; general editorship." For シュ it gives studying a discipline, especially the Buddhist way, as in 修行 しゅぎょう, "training; practice; discipline; study."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [人](人.md), "person," at the left, written 亻, with a vertical stroke beside it, then 攵 at the upper right and 彡 below it. Imagine a person tapping a worn post with a tool (攵) and then brushing a few finishing strokes (彡) over it until it looks as good as new. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that any part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 修正, 正 supplies セイ. In 修理, 理 supplies リ. In 修繕, 繕 supplies ゼン. In 研修, 研 supplies ケン. In 修める, 修 writes おさ and める is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same pattern gives 修まる with まる.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シュウ, with 修飾, 修養 and 改修 as examples; シュ, with 修行; おさめる, with 修める, and sets 治める beside it as a contrasting writing; and おさまる, with 修まる, and sets 治まる beside it as a contrasting writing. Practice selects シュウ, the only reading with linked word articles; シュ and the two kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [正](正.md), "correct, proper," in 修正, [理](理.md), "reason, logic," in 修理, [研](研.md), "polish; study closely," in 研修 and the 繕 in 修繕, which does not have a kanji card here.
- [人](人.md), "person," is the key at the left. [秋](秋.md), "autumn," [州](州.md), "state; province," and [周](周.md), "circumference; circuit," are also read シュウ. All are different kanji.
- [直](直.md), "direct; repair," [改](改.md), "revise; reform," [補](補.md), "supplement; make good," [正](正.md), "correct, proper," [研](研.md), "polish; study closely," [習](習.md), "learn; practise," [学](学.md), "study," and [治](治.md), "govern; heal," overlap the senses "repair," "amend" and "study" that 修 and its words carry; the Joyo table also sets 治める beside 修める. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "repair" or "study" as exclusive to any one character.
