# 叙 — narrate; describe; confer (rank or honor)

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 又 (no. 29) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

叙 means describing or narrating something in order. Digital Daijisen gives three senses: stating things in order, as in 叙述 and 叙事; conferring a court rank or a decoration, as in 叙勲; and, used in place of 抒, expressing one's feelings, as in [叙情](../words/叙情.md).

## Notes

The verb 叙する じょする covers both main senses: JMdict glosses it as “to relate; to narrate; to describe” and “to confer (a rank)” (entry 1344790). JMdict also lists 抒情 as another spelling of 叙情. Here “source-only” means that a source lists the reading but no checked word example uses it; it does not mean つい.ず or ついで is invalid for 叙 in every dictionary or context. Kanjipedia also gives the kun reading の.べる, marked as outside the Joyo table (外); it is not in this card's metadata and is not quizzed.

述 (“state; relate”) appears with 叙 in 叙述, and 描 (“draw; depict; sketch”) overlaps with describing. 贈 (“give as a gift; confer”) and 授 (“grant; impart; teach”) overlap with conferring a rank or honor.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 内 (“within, inner”), 食 (“eat, food”), and 八 (“eight”); none means narrating or conferring a rank.
