# 乃 — thereupon; possessive no

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 丿 (no. 4) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

乃 appears in connecting expressions such as [乃至](../words/乃至.md) ないし, "from ... to; or." Character dictionaries also give **すなわち**, "thereupon; namely," **なんじ**, "you," and use for the sound **の**. These are not instructions to replace ordinary hiragana の with 乃 throughout a modern sentence.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Follow the curved, hooked outline and the separate slanting stroke. Think of one phrase curving into the next. The shape association does not explain the character's history.

**Kanbun** is the Japanese reading tradition for texts written in Classical Chinese. Recognizing a character's kanbun gloss does not make it an everyday standalone word. The on ノ and kun の labels above preserve the source's separate classifications; identical kana do not establish two interchangeable modern uses.
