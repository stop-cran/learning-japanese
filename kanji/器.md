# 器 — vessel; instrument

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30), at the top left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

器 is a container or a tool, and by extension a bodily organ or a person's capacity. [容器](../words/容器.md) ようき is a container, [食器](../words/食器.md) しょっき is tableware, [楽器](../words/楽器.md) がっき is a musical instrument, [武器](../words/武器.md) ぶき is a weapon, [器具](../words/器具.md) きぐ is a utensil or tool, [受話器](../words/受話器.md) じゅわき is a telephone receiver, and [器用](../words/器用.md) きよう is being dexterous or skillful.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses: a container, or a tool made in a set shape, as in 楽器 and 食器; a part of a living body with a particular function and form, as in 器官 きかん, "organ," and 臓器 ぞうき, "internal organs; viscera"; and a person's ability or talent, as in 器用 and 器量 きりょう, "ability; capability; capacity." JMdict gives the standalone word 器 うつわ as "bowl; vessel; container" and "ability; capacity; calibre," and 大器 たいき as "large container" and "person of great talent."

The official Joyo table also prints an older form of 器 in round brackets, with a dot in its centre like 犬; this card teaches the standard form, whose centre is plain 大.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find four [口](口.md), "mouth," in the corners around a centre shape that is [大](大.md), "big, large," with no dot. Imagine four mouths crowded around one big serving bowl, and remember 器 as a bowl or vessel. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 口 or 大 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 容器, 容 supplies ヨウ. In 食器, 食 supplies ショク, shortened to しょっ before キ. In 楽器, 楽 supplies ガク, shortened to がっ. In 武器, 武 supplies ブ. In 器具, 具 supplies グ and 器 comes first. In 受話器, 受 supplies ジュ and 話 supplies ワ. In 器用, 用 supplies ヨウ. The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キ and うつわ for 器. Practice selects キ, which has linked word articles; うつわ is the standalone word 器, which has no linked article yet, so no kun reading is selected for questions.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [容](容.md), "contain; appearance," [食](食.md), "eat, food," [楽](楽.md), "music, enjoyment, ease," [武](武.md), "military; warrior," [具](具.md), "tools; ingredients," [受](受.md), "receive; undergo," [話](話.md), "talk, speak," and [用](用.md), "use, purpose."
- [品](品.md), "goods, refinement," stacks three 口 and looks like part of 器, but it has no centre shape and no fourth 口. [喜](喜.md), "rejoice," and [奇](奇.md), "strange; extraordinary," also read キ but do not mean vessel or instrument.
- [缶](缶.md), "tin can; container," [皿](皿.md), "dish; plate," [瓶](瓶.md), "bottle; jar," and [容](容.md), "contain; appearance," each overlap the "container" or "vessel" sense of 器. [具](具.md), "tools; ingredients," overlaps its "tool" sense, and [機](機.md), "mechanism; opportunity," and [械](械.md), "machine; fetter," overlap its "instrument" sense. [能](能.md), "ability; capacity," and [才](才.md), "talent," overlap the "ability" sense. All of them are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "vessel," "tool" or "ability" as exclusive to any one character.
