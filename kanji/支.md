# 支 — support

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 支 (no. 65) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

支 appears in support and branching vocabulary: 支える ささえる is "support," while 支店 してん is a branch office or shop.
Do not force either meaning into [支払う](../words/支払う.md) しはらう, "pay":
JMdict identifies 支 in that spelling as ateji, a character used for its sound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the upper cross supported by the spreading lower strokes.
This is a visual memory aid, not a historical analysis.

- [支払う](../words/支払う.md) しはらう — pay

## Notes

家族を支える (かぞくをささえる) means "support one's family"; support need not be a physical prop.
Learn 支払う as a whole word, not as "support + pay."

[友](友.md) also ends in 又, but its upper strokes differ.
[父](父.md) and [文](文.md) have crossing lower strokes with different tops.
These are useful small-shape comparisons, not synonyms.
