# 鎖 — chain; shut

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鎖 is a chain, and also shutting something off. On its own, [鎖](../words/鎖.md) くさり is glossed in JMdict as "chain; chains." KANJIDIC2 gives "chain," "irons" and "connection." Kanjipedia's senses are くさり。また、つなぐ。つなぎ合わせる。 (a chain; to link) and とざす。とじる。 (to shut), and 鎖国 さこく, "national isolation; closing the country (to foreigners)," belongs to the second. Digital Daijisen also gives 錠前。かぎ。 (a lock) and かぎをかける。とざす。 (to lock; to shut).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is [金](金.md), "gold, money," and on the right is ⺌, a variant of [小](小.md), "small, little," over [貝](貝.md), "shellfish; seashell," which is [目](目.md), "eye," over [八](八.md), "eight." Imagine small metal shells linked one after another into a long chain. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 金, ⺌ or 貝 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists サ, with 鎖国, 連鎖 れんさ and 封鎖 ふうさ, and くさり, with 鎖. KANJIDIC2 adds とざ.す, which Digital Daijisen also gives; Kanjipedia lists とざす and さす outside the table.
- Digital Daijisen also lists 鎖 under the headword じょう【錠／▽鎖／×鏁】, a lock that is opened and closed with a key, and its note says this sense was originally written 鎖, with 錠 applied later. JMdict entry 1633480, headed 錠 じょう (“lock; padlock”), lists 鎖 as a rarely used kanji form. じょう is not a KANJIDIC2 reading and has no table row here.
- KANJIDIC2 and Digital Daijisen give no name readings for 鎖.

These eleven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [閉](閉.md) (“shut”): Kanjipedia's second sense is とざす。とじる。, the 閉 card lists と.ざす, and JMdict's 閉ざす (1508540) lists 鎖す as a rarely used kanji form.
- [封](封.md) (“seal; close”): JMdict glosses 封鎖 (1499650) as “blockade; lockdown; sealing off (an area).”
- [連](連.md) (“link; take along”): JMdict glosses 連鎖 (1559490) as “chain; series; connection,” and Kanjipedia gives 連鎖 under its first sense.
- [係](係.md) (“connection; person in charge”) and [縁](縁.md) (“edge; connection; fate”): KANJIDIC2 lists “connection” for both 鎖 and 縁, matching the titles' “connection.”
- [結](結.md) (“tie; join; conclude”), [接](接.md) (“touch; join”), [括](括.md) (“tie together; enclose”) and [縛](縛.md) (“bind; tie up; restrain”): Kanjipedia's first sense includes つなぐ。つなぎ合わせる。, and JMdict glosses 繋ぐ つなぐ (1251900) as “to connect; to link together” and “to tie; to fasten; to restrain.”
- [締](締.md) (“tighten; fasten; conclude”): the first sense of 閉ざす, which lists 鎖す, is “to shut; to close; to fasten; to lock,” and KANJIDIC2 lists “shut,” “lock” and “fasten” for 締; Digital Daijisen's third sense of 鎖 is かぎをかける。とざす。
- [械](械.md) (“machine; fetter”): KANJIDIC2 lists “irons” for 鎖, close to the title's “fetter”; the 械 card lists かせ.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 縄 (“rope; cord”), 針 (“needle; pin”), and 帯 (“sash; zone”); none means a chain or shutting.
