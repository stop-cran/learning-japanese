# 帥 — commander; lead troops

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

帥 denotes a commander or someone at the head of a group. 元帥 is a title; 総帥 (そうすい) can also refer to the head of an organization.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare 帥 with 師: the right of 帥 is 巾, without the separate horizontal above it in 師.

## Notes

[師](師.md) can denote a teacher; [帰](帰.md) and [追](追.md) have different surrounding strokes. [将](将.md) can mean commander, and [率](率.md) can mean lead. Do not substitute 師 for 帥 in 元帥.
