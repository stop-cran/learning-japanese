# 堕 — degenerate; lapse

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

堕 can describe a decline or lapse into a worse state. [堕落](../words/堕落.md) (だらく) is the selected example and commonly concerns moral decline or corruption.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine a step sinking into loose earth. This is a memory aid only; no historical derivation is asserted.
