# 徹 — penetrate; be thorough

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

徹 concerns going all the way through something and carrying it through to
the end. [徹底](../words/徹底.md) てってい is thoroughness or the thorough
enforcement of something, and [徹夜](../words/徹夜.md) てつや is staying up
all night. 徹底的 てっていてき is thorough or exhaustive, 貫徹 かんてつ is
accomplishing or carrying something through, and 徹する てっする is to
penetrate, to devote oneself to something exclusively, or to do something
throughout a period such as the night.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives the sense of piercing through
and of reaching all the way to the limit, with 徹底, 徹夜, 貫徹, 透徹 and
冷徹 among its examples. It lists とおる and とおす as kun readings. Those
readings are not in KANJIDIC2 or the official Joyo table, so practice does
not select them.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 彳 at the left, [育](育.md) in
the middle and 攵 at the right. Imagine setting out along a road, raising a
task step by step with a firm hand until it is carried through to the very
end. This is a memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| テツ | on | [徹底](../words/徹底.md) てってい - thoroughness; thorough enforcement; [徹夜](../words/徹夜.md) てつや - staying up all night |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects テツ. In 徹底, テツ and
テイ combine as てってい with a small っ; in 徹夜 it stays てつ. The official
Joyo table lists テツ, with 徹底, 徹夜 and 貫徹 as examples.

## Notes

- [微](微.md), "minute; faint," shares both 彳 at the left and 攵 at the
  right, and [御](御.md), "honorific; govern," shares 彳. [育](育.md), "grow
  up; raise," is the middle component of 徹. Their meanings differ from
  徹's.
- Thoroughness overlaps [究](究.md), "investigate thoroughly," [完](完.md),
  "complete," [全](全.md), "whole; complete," and [了](了.md), "completion;
  settling." Daijisen defines [討](討.md), "attack; discuss," as probing a
  problem thoroughly, and JMdict's second sense of [丁寧](../words/丁寧.md),
  written with [寧](寧.md), "polite; peaceful," is "careful; close;
  thorough; conscientious." This card declares the pairs 徹–討 and 徹–寧.
- Piercing, passing through or reaching all the way overlaps
  [刺](刺.md), "pierce; prick," [通](通.md), "pass through; commute,"
  [経](経.md), "pass through; sutra," [至](至.md), "reach; attain,"
  [届](届.md), "reach; deliver; notify," and [達](達.md), "reach, plural
  suffix."
- Staying up all night overlaps [夜](夜.md), "night," [晩](晩.md),
  "evening; night," [座](座.md), "sit; seating," [泊](泊.md), "stay
  overnight; anchor," and [留](留.md), "retain; stay," because KANJIDIC2
  glosses 徹 "sit up (all night)."
- Clarity, as in the Daijisen examples 透徹 and 冷徹, overlaps [冴](冴.md),
  "clear; sharp-minded," [明](明.md), "bright, clear," and [晴](晴.md),
  "clear skies," because KANJIDIC2 also glosses 徹 "clear."
- KANJIDIC2's "strike home" is a figure of speech for hitting the mark. It
  overlaps [打](打.md), "strike," and [撃](撃.md), "shoot; strike," and its
  word "home" is shared by [家](家.md), "house, home," and [帰](帰.md),
  "return home," although 徹 never means a house. These direct meaning
  comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as identical in every
  compound.
