# 型 — mold; type

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

型 names a mold, pattern, model or type. 型 かた can be a mold used to shape something; 典型 てんけい is a typical example or model, and 小型 こがた describes a small size or small-sized model.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the upper 刑-shaped strokes pressing a repeatable pattern into 土 below.

## Notes

- The -がた row illustrates a suffix in a complete word. It does not mean that every occurrence of 型 is read がた.
- Compare [形](形.md), shape or form. 型 and 形 have overlapping uses, but a reusable mold or a type is not automatically the same thing as an object's visible outline.
- The radical is 土 at the bottom, not the upper 刑-shaped part. The mnemonic does not establish a historical phonetic component.
