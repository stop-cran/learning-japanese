# 費 — expense; spend

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

費 concerns what is spent or used up. [費用](../words/費用.md) ひよう is an expense,
and 学費 がくひ is money needed for schooling. 費やす ついやす also applies to spending time,
so the character is not confined to cash.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the looping upper part pulling resources out of the 貝 below.
This is a visual memory aid, not a reconstruction of the character's origin.

- 費やす ついやす — spend; consume

## Notes

時間を費やす (じかんをついやす) means "spend time."
Spending need not be wasteful; whether it is worthwhile comes from context.
Compare the distinctive upper strokes with the tops of [貸](貸.md), [貧](貧.md), and [賛](賛.md).
