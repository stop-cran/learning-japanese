# 立 — stand; set up

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 立 (no. 117) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

立 expresses standing, rising, or setting something up. Start with [立つ](../words/立つ.md) (たつ, stand)
and 立てる (たてる, stand something up). Compounds can extend this to an institution's establishment or a position taken.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** picture the middle strokes as legs planted on the long
bottom line. The figure stands upright.

## Notes

A person stands with 立つ; building a house is [建てる](../words/建てる.md) (たてる).
The shared sound does not make 立 and [建](建.md) interchangeable.

[音](音.md) has a 立-shaped top with 日 below. [土](土.md) has one central vertical stroke and lacks 立's upper dot
and paired middle strokes.
