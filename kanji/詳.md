# 詳 — detailed; well-informed

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

詳 is about detail and thorough knowledge. KANJIDIC2 lists “detailed,” “full,” “minute,” “accurate,” and “well-informed,” and Kanjipedia gives くわしい and つまびらか. In [詳しい](../words/詳しい.md) くわしい, JMdict's first sense describes information as “detailed; full; minute,” and its second describes a person as “knowing very well; knowledgeable (about)” a subject.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [言](言.md), "say; words," with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it, stands on the left, and [羊](羊.md), "sheep," stands on the right. Imagine a shepherd who can talk about every single sheep in the flock: that is knowing something in detail. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 羊 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ショウ and くわしい. Kanjipedia marks つまびらか as outside the Joyo table (外).
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading よし, and Digital Daijisen gives つま and みつ; these are name-use forms, not quiz readings.

These ten cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [細](細.md) (“slender; fine; detailed”): it shares “detailed” with the title.
- [精](精.md) (“refined; vitality”) and [委](委.md) (“entrust; committee”): JMdict entry 1351730 also writes くわしい as 精しい and 委しい, and the 精 card lists くわ.しい among its readings.
- [密](密.md) (“secret; dense; close”) and [微](微.md) (“minute; faint”): KANJIDIC2 gives 詳 “minute,” and gives both 密 and 微 “minuteness.”
- [審](審.md) (“examine; judge; trial”): JMdict entry 2106780 writes つまびらか as 詳らか or 審らか, and KANJIDIC2 gives つまび.らか as a kun reading of both 詳 and 審.
- [識](識.md) (“knowledge; awareness”), [知](知.md) (“know”) and [博](博.md) (“broad knowledge; doctor; expo”): KANJIDIC2 lists “well-informed” for 詳, and JMdict's second sense of 詳しい is “knowing very well; knowledgeable (about); well-acquainted (with); well-informed (about); familiar (with).”
- [満](満.md) (“full; satisfy”): KANJIDIC2 lists “full” for 詳, and JMdict's first sense of 詳しい is “detailed; full; minute.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 弱 (“weak; frail”), 暗 (“dark”), and 粗 (“coarse; rough; crude”); none means detailed or well-informed.
