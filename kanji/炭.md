# 炭 — charcoal; coal

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

炭 concerns charcoal or coal. The native noun 炭 すみ means charcoal; 石炭 names coal.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture [山](山.md), a mountain, above a sheltered [火](火.md), fire: imagine fuel being made or found under the mountain.

## Notes

- Stone plus 炭 gives 石炭, coal; do not assume standalone すみ and 石炭 are identical materials.
- [灰](灰.md) is ash, not the same thing as charcoal. The shared association with burning is not synonymy.
