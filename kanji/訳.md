# 訳 — translate; explanation

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

訳 concerns translating and explaining meaning. [翻訳](../words/翻訳.md) ほんやく is translation;
[訳](../words/訳.md) わけ is a reason, explanation, or sense. Learn the word and reading together.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine 言 supplying words beside the measuring-like 尺 shape.
Keep the final rightward stroke of 尺 distinct from the hooked shapes in similar 言 characters.

## Notes

The noun 訳 やく, "translation," is not the noun 訳 わけ, "reason." In explanatory わけだ,
the kana spelling is common: do not read it やくだ or translate it as "it is a translation."

[試](試.md), [記](記.md), and [計](計.md) share 言 but have 式, 己, and 十 on the right.

For わけ compared with こと and the distinction between わけだ, わけがない, and わけではない,
see [こと, わけ, and noun-based grammar](../articles/koto-wake-and-formal-nouns.md).
