# 簡 — simplicity; brevity

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

簡 contributes simplicity or brevity, as in 簡単. A character's contribution is not a rule that every compound containing it means easy.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for the bamboo-shaped top above 間 and imagine a brief message that fits into the available space. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not an explanation of the historical writing material or sound.
