# 在 — exist; be located

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

在 concerns existence or being present somewhere. [存在](../words/存在.md) そんざい names existence itself; 不在 ふざい means absence.
現在 げんざい shifts the focus to time: "the present; now."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture something located on the 土 at the lower right, under the upper strokes. This is a location cue, not an explanation of the ancient form.

## Notes

Distinguish [左](左.md), which has 工 at the bottom, from 在 with 土 and an additional left vertical stroke.
不在 means not being present at the relevant place; it does not mean that the person does not exist.
[有](有.md) has overlapping existence meanings and is a semantic comparison, not an opposite.
