# 岬 — cape; headland; promontory

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

岬 is a cape: a narrow strip of land that sticks out into the sea or a lake. On its own, [岬](../words/岬.md) みさき is the word for a cape; JMdict glosses it "cape (on coast)," and the same entry also lists the spelling 崎. The Daijisen word entry for みさき describes land that juts out, long and narrow, into the sea or a lake.

The Daijisen character entry for 岬 gives the on reading コウ, which it marks as outside the official table, the kun reading みさき, and one sense, みさき. Its example is 岬角 こうかく, which JMdict marks a rare term and glosses "promontory," and also, as an anatomy term, "promontory." JMdict also lists 岬湾 こうわん, a rare term, "capes and bays; indentation (of a coast)."

KANJIDIC2 also gives さき as a reading used in names. JMdict lists 岬 among the spellings of the noun さき, "small peninsula; cape; promontory; headland," after 崎; Daijisen's headword さき also lists 崎 first, and marks its spelling 岬 with ▽, meaning the official table does not give 岬 this reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [山](山.md), "mountain," as the narrow part on the left, and on the right the same part that forms the right side of [押](押.md), "push." Imagine a mountain ridge pushing out into the sea: the end of it is a cape. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 山 or the right part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| コウ | on | 岬湾 こうわん - capes and bays; indentation (of a coast); JMdict marks it a rare term; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| みさき | kun | [岬](../words/岬.md) みさき — cape; headland |

In the word 岬, the kanji alone is read みさき, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows a kanji. In 岬湾, 岬 supplies コウ and 湾 supplies ワン.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only みさき, with 岬 as its example. コウ is not in the official table. Practice selects みさき, the reading with a linked word article; コウ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a rare dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 岬 is used on its own as the word みさき, so it has no other kanji in its common word.
- [山](山.md), "mountain," is the part on the left of 岬. [田](田.md), "rice field," matches the right part of 岬 except that 岬's middle vertical stroke runs on below the box, and [中](中.md), "middle, inside," also has a vertical stroke that runs through a box and out below it. [押](押.md), "push," has the same right part as 岬, with 扌 on the left instead of 山. All are different kanji.
- [岸](岸.md), "shore; riverbank," [端](端.md), "edge; end; tip," and [末](末.md), "end; tip," overlap the coastline and "tip of land" senses that 岬 and its word carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "cape" or "tip" as exclusive to any one character.
- JMdict lists 崎 as a spelling of both みさき and さき; for さき it lists 崎 first.
