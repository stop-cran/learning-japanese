# 内 — within, inner

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 冂 (no. 13) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

内 is commonly explained as 冂 (an enclosure, frame) with [入](入.md) (enter) inside: something that has entered an enclosure, so "inside". Its meaning is "inside",
"within", "inner" and also one's own group (うち, "our house/company"). It is the opposite of [外](外.md).

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ナイ | on | [国内](../words/国内.md) こくない, 室内 しつない, 以内 いない, 内外 ないがい |
| ダイ | on | 内裏 だいり (the imperial palace, rare) |
| うち | kun | 内 うち (inside; one's own home or group), [内側](../words/内側.md) うちがわ, 今月の内に こんげつのうちに |

## Common words

- [国内](../words/国内.md) こくない — domestic, within the country
- [内側](../words/内側.md) うちがわ — inner side
- 以内 いない — within

## Notes

- **内 vs 外:** 内 (inside) and 外 (outside) pair up: 国内/海外, 内側/外側. In the sense of "my side", うち can also mean one's house, family or company.
- **Look-alikes:** 内 vs 肉 (meat, にく): 肉 has 人 twice inside; 内 vs [円](円.md) (yen, circle): 円 has a horizontal bar and a vertical stroke inside the frame; 内 has 入 inside.
- 内 vs 中: both mean "inside"; 中 is the middle of something, 内 is the interior of a range or group (三日以内 within three days).
