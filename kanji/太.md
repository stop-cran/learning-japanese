# 太 — thick; plump

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

太 describes thickness or being big around: 太い ふとい can describe a thick rope, and 太る ふとる means to gain weight.
In [太陽](../words/太陽.md) たいよう, "the sun," 太 instead has the greatness sense listed for this compound in Kanji Pedia. Do not translate it as "a thick sun."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the extra dot below the center of 大. Use it as a reminder of extra bulk; this is a memory device, not a historical claim.

## Notes

Compare [犬](犬.md): its dot is at the upper right, not low in the center.
[天](天.md) has another horizontal stroke instead of the dot.
For a rope, 太い contrasts with 細い ほそい, "thin; slender." Thickness of a book is instead 厚い あつい: English "thick" does not select one Japanese adjective in every context.
