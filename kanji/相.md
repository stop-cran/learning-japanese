# 相 — mutual; aspect

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

相 often concerns a relation between people or things. [相談](../words/相談.md) そうだん is consultation,
and 相手 あいて is the other party, partner, or opponent.
The character also has aspect and minister senses; 首相 しゅしょう means prime minister.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine an eye 目 facing a tree 木, with the two sides relating to one another.
This is a modern association, not a verified explanation of the character's history.

## Notes

先生に相談しました。 (せんせいにそうだんしました。) — I asked my teacher for advice.
The mutual association does not require equal roles: a learner can consult a teacher.

[休](休.md) combines a person-shaped left side with 木; 相 puts 木 on the left and 目 on the right.
[本](本.md) has an extra stroke across 木, while [目](目.md) is only the right-hand component of 相.
