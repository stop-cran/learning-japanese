# 紅 — crimson; deep red

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

紅 concerns crimson or deep red. Its words include color names and, with a particular reading, cosmetic rouge; do not assign every sense to every reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 糸 beside 工 and imagine working crimson-colored thread. This is an invented modern-shape scene, not a historical dyeing explanation or a verified sound-component claim.

The canonical key 糸, no. 120, is compressed at the left as ⺯.

## Notes

- 深紅 also has the dictionary reading しんこう. The ク row gives しんく without claiming it is the only pronunciation or using the start of くれない to prove an on reading.
- In the 紅 entry, rouge/lipstick is restricted to べに; くれない has the color sense. The readings are not a free license to transfer the cosmetic meaning.
- JMdict groups 紅い with [赤い](../words/赤い.md) あかい and notes especially 紅い for scarlet, not exclusively. [赤](赤.md) is therefore a useful color comparison, not a safe wrong answer. 紅茶 names black tea in English, not every red-colored drink.
