# 縮 — shrink; contract

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

縮 is getting smaller or shorter: shrinking, contracting, drawing in. [縮む](../words/縮む.md) ちぢむ is to shrink or contract, [縮める](../words/縮める.md) ちぢめる is to shorten or reduce, [縮れる](../words/縮れる.md) ちぢれる is to be wavy or curly, [縮小](../words/縮小.md) しゅくしょう is scaling down, [圧縮](../words/圧縮.md) あっしゅく is compression and [恐縮](../words/恐縮.md) きょうしゅく is feeling obliged or apologetic.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses: shrinking or shortening, as in 縮図 しゅくず, "reduced drawing; miniature copy," 短縮 たんしゅく, "shortening; contraction; reduction," 濃縮 のうしゅく, "concentration; enrichment; condensation," and 収縮 しゅうしゅく, "contraction; shrinking"; and a spirit that shrinks back, as in 畏縮 いしゅく, "wincing; cowering; shrinking," and 恐縮. JMdict lists "shrinking back in fear" as an archaic sense of 恐縮, next to the current "feeling obliged" and "feeling sorry (for troubling someone)."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left and [宿](宿.md), "lodging," at the right. Imagine a cloth woven of thread shrinking until it is small enough to fit inside a lodging. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 宿 contributes meaning or sound, although 宿 also happens to be read シュク. Historical formation is not established here.

In 圧縮, 圧 supplies アツ, which shortens to あっ before シュク. In 恐縮, 恐 supplies キョウ. In 縮小, 小 supplies ショウ. In 縮む, 縮 writes ちぢ and む is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same pattern gives ちぢめる and ちぢれる with the okurigana める and れる, and the dictionary words 縮まる and 縮らす with まる and らす.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シュク, with 縮小, 縮図 and 短縮 as examples, and all five kun readings: ちぢむ, ちぢまる, ちぢめる, ちぢれる and ちぢらす. JMdict also lists ちじむ as a variant reading of 縮む. Practice selects シュク, ちぢ.む, ちぢ.める and ちぢ.れる, the readings with linked word articles; the other two kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [圧](圧.md), "pressure; oppress," in 圧縮, [恐](恐.md), "fear; awe," in 恐縮 and [小](小.md), "small, little," in 縮小.
- 縮緬 ちりめん, "(silk) crepe; crape," is listed by the Daijisen among its hard-to-read words, and JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana alone. In this word 縮 is read ちり, which is not one of the readings above. 緬 does not have a kanji card.
- [宿](宿.md), "lodging," is the shape at the right and also reads シュク. [績](績.md), "achievements; results," and [組](組.md), "group; assemble," share the thread shape at the left. All three are different kanji.
- [減](減.md), "decrease, reduce," [短](短.md), "short," and [略](略.md), "abbreviate; omit," overlap the senses "reduce" and "shorten," and 短縮 is glossed in JMdict as "shortening; contraction; reduction; curtailment; abbreviation." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "reduce" or "shorten" as exclusive to any one character.
