# 団 — group; association

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

団 concerns a group or association in 団体 だんたい and 集団 しゅうだん. Its other on-reading トン is useful in 布団 ふとん, a word for bedding that must not be translated by assigning the group meaning to every component.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the 寸-like strokes gathered into one group inside the outer frame.

The outer 囗 is the enclosure radical (no. 31), not the smaller mouth radical 口.
