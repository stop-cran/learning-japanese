# 磨 — polish; grind

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

磨 concerns polishing, grinding or improving. 磨く is used for polishing a surface, brushing one's teeth and figuratively honing a skill. The intended action depends on the object rather than one universal English translation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the 麻-shaped upper structure over 石 and imagine a polishing stone under a workshop cover. This is a modern visual association, not a claim that every component supplied that meaning historically.

The canonical indexing key is 石, no. 112, at the bottom, not the larger upper structure.

## Notes

- 磨く is transitive godan: 歯を磨く (はをみがく), brush one's teeth; 磨きます みがきます and 磨いて みがいて. The object takes を.
- JMdict flags 磨る as a rare spelling of the transitive godan verb する, with forms such as すります and すって. This is not the irregular verb [する](../words/する.md), do, and the spelling flag does not by itself make the pronunciation rare.
- Compare the upper outlines of [歴](歴.md), history or passage of time, [庫](庫.md), storehouse, and [床](床.md), floor or bed. They are visual comparisons, not synonyms for polishing.
