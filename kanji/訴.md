# 訴 — sue; appeal to

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

訴 is about bringing a grievance before someone. Kanjipedia's first sense reads うったえる。さばきを求めて申し出る (coming forward to ask for a judgment), and its second is 同情を求める (seeking sympathy). KANJIDIC2 lists “accusation,” “sue,” “complain of pain,” and “appeal to.” In [訴える](../words/訴える.md) うったえる, JMdict's senses include “to raise; to bring to (someone's attention),” “to complain,” and “to sue (a person); to take someone to court.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [言](言.md), "say; words," with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it, stands on the left. On the right is 斥: the shape 斤 with one extra dot 丶. Imagine words pouring out of a mouth while that small dot points across at the person you are complaining about: you are making your appeal. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 斥 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ソ and うったえる, the same readings as KANJIDIC2, and also gives the noun 訴え うったえ (JMdict 1397710, “lawsuit; complaint”). KANJIDIC2 records no name readings, and Digital Daijisen's character entry gives none.

These eleven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [告](告.md) (“announce; inform”): Digital Daijisen's second sense of 訴 is 不満・苦痛などを告げ知らせる (making dissatisfaction or pain known), and JMdict glosses 訴える with “to raise; to bring to (someone's attention).”
- [痛](痛.md) (“pain; hurt”): KANJIDIC2 lists “complain of pain” for 訴.
- [裁](裁.md) (“judge; cut out cloth”), [判](判.md) (“judge; seal”) and [審](審.md) (“examine; judge; trial”): Kanjipedia's first sense asks for さばき (a judgment), KANJIDIC2 lists “sue,” and JMdict glosses 訴える with “to sue (a person); to take someone to court.”
- [求](求.md) (“seek; request”): both Kanjipedia senses use 求める もとめる (JMdict 1229350, which includes “to seek” and “to request”): さばきを求めて and 同情を求める.
- [請](請.md) (“request; beg; undertake”) and [願](願.md) (“wish; petition”): JMdict glosses 訴状 そじょう as “petition; complaint; (legal) brief” (entry 1397770), and KANJIDIC2 lists “appeal to” for 訴.
- [挙](挙.md) (“raise; cite; hold (an event)”): JMdict's first gloss for 訴える is “to raise.”
- [申](申.md) (“humbly say”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 訴 ends with 申し出る もうしでる, a word written with 申, and Digital Daijisen's first sense is 裁きを求めるため上に申し出る (coming forward to a superior to ask for a judgment); JMdict glosses 申し出る with “to request; to ask (for); to apply (for); to report (to); to notify” (entry 1362930).
- [責](責.md) (“blame; hold responsible”): KANJIDIC2 lists “accusation” for 訴.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 迎 (“welcome; greet”), 逃 (“escape; flee”), and 戦 (“battle”); none means suing someone or appealing to someone.
