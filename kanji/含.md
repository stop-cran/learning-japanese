# 含 — contain; include

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

含 connects containing something with including it in a group or scope. 含む ふくむ can also mean holding something in the mouth; 含める ふくめる can describe actively including something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 今 above 口 and imagine something being held inside the mouth. This scene uses the modern shape, not verified component history.

## Notes

- 含む and 含める are both transitive verbs; do not teach them as an intransitive/transitive pair. Their forms differ: 含みます ふくみます versus 含めます ふくめます.
- Containing liquid in the mouth is not the same as swallowing it. The mouth sense does not turn every abstract inclusion into a physical action.
- The lower 口 is the radical; 今 is a visible upper shape, not a proven phonetic component in this card.
