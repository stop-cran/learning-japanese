# 乏 — scarce; lacking

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 丿 (no. 4), the short upper slant · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

乏 concerns not having enough of something. [乏しい](../words/乏しい.md) とぼしい
describes a meagre or insufficient supply; [貧乏](../words/貧乏.md) びんぼう
concerns poverty. The adjective can also describe financial hardship, so these
are overlapping uses, not separate compartments.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a small upper slant above
a winding lower line, like a supply dwindling along a path. This is a memory
aid for the visible outline, not an account of the character's history.

## Notes

- [貧](貧.md), poverty, and [欠](欠.md), lack, have related meanings.
  [少](少.md) concerns small amounts; scarcity can overlap with that idea,
  but 乏しい emphasizes an insufficient supply.
- Compare the short outlines of [久](久.md), a long time, [反](反.md),
  opposition or reversal, and [乃](乃.md), connecting or particle-related uses.
  Similar-looking strokes do not give them the lack meaning of 乏.
