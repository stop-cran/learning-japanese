# 初 — first time; initial

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

初 marks a first occurrence or an initial stage. [初めて](../words/初めて.md) はじめて means "for the first time."
最初 さいしょ refers to the beginning or the first item; 初恋 はつこい is a first love.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the left side is 衤, the clothing form of 衣, beside 刀 on the right.
Imagine making the first cut in material for clothing. This is a memory aid, not a verified historical account.

## Notes

Use 初めて for the "first time" adverb when writing your own examples.
Distinguish that use from 始めて (はじめて), the linking form of [始める](../words/始める.md), "begin."
JMdict also records 始めて as a spelling of the adverb, so context matters: the written form alone does not always identify which word it is.
[切](切.md) also has a full 刀 on the right; [列](列.md) and [判](判.md) use its narrow form 刂 instead.
