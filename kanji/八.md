# 八 — eight

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 八 (no. 12) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

八 is commonly explained as two strokes pulling apart, the idea of "divide" or "separate"; the number eight was written with this shape by borrowing. This is the standard account, but it is not certain.
As a component 八 often appears at the bottom or sides of kanji as the "divide" shape (公, 分, 谷).
Because the shape widens downward, 八 is considered auspicious in Japan (末広がり すえひろがり, "widening toward the end").

## Notes

Sound changes: 八本 はっぽん, 八分 はっぷん, 八回 はっかい, 八百 はっぴゃく, 八千 はっせん. In these, はち shortens to はっ.
Look-alikes: [人](人.md) (person: the left stroke is longer and the right starts at its middle), [入](入.md) (enter: the left stroke is the shorter one), [六](六.md).
