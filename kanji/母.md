# 母 — mother

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 毋 (no. 80) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

母 is commonly explained as 女 ([女](女.md), woman) with two dots added for breasts: a woman who nurses a child, hence "mother".
The dots are enclosed in the shape, which is why the modern form looks like a box with a stroke and two dots. This is the usual explanation and a good mnemonic.
It means "mother".

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ボ | on | 母国 ぼこく, 母語 ぼご, 母校 ぼこう, 母性 ぼせい; with 父: 父母 ふぼ |
| はは | kun | 母 はは (my mother), 母親 ははおや, 母の日 ははのひ |
| も | kun | rare, e.g. 母屋 もや (main building; also おもや) |

## Common words

- [お母さん](../words/お母さん.md) おかあさん — mother
- 母 はは — (my) mother
- 母国 ぼこく — one's home country
- 母語 ぼご — mother tongue

## Notes

For your own mother to outsiders use 母 はは; for another's mother use お母さん or お母様. In お母さん the 母 is read かあ, a whole-word reading rather than a standard on or kun one; 母さん かあさん is the short form.

Look-alikes: 毎 (every), with a left-falling stroke on top of 母, and 每, the traditional form of 毎 and not used in Japanese text. [女](女.md) alone is "woman".
