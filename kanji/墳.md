# 墳 — burial mound; tomb

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

墳 points to a mound built as a tomb. In 古墳 it is the mound, not merely the fact that the grave is old, that matters.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 土 on the left; imagine earth heaped over a burial site.

## Notes

[塚](塚.md) can name other kinds of mounds too. Compare a shell mound with an ancient burial mound.

[噴](噴.md) has the same right-hand shape but starts with 口 rather than 土. [境](境.md) (boundary) and [塔](塔.md) (tower) start with 土 but lack the same right side. [墓](墓.md) (grave) identifies a burial place without specifying a mound.
