# 梓 — azusa tree; printing block

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

梓 writes [梓](../words/梓.md) あずさ, **azusa**, a tree name. In Japanese descriptions this usually identifies Japanese cherry birch, *Betula grossa*. Older sources and other naming traditions also associate the spelling with catalpa or Japanese mallotus. Do not assume that every occurrence identifies the same species.

梓 also concerns a **printing block**, the wooden surface used to print text or images. The printing connection survives in 上梓 (じょうし), publication.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside [辛](辛.md). Imagine a tree beside a sign warning of a painful prick. This is a memory aid for the visible parts, not a botanical property or an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

- This card asks あずさ. シ has a verified printing use; it is not source-only or a reading inferred from a personal name.
- The linked word article uses あずさ, not し. JMdict permits the printing-block sense without a reading restriction, and the Japanese dictionary also records it under あずさ.
- 梓弓 contains 弓 (ゆみ), bow. In older writing 梓 alone can abbreviate this bow or the word あずさみこ, a spirit medium; a passage about a ritual need not be about a tree.
- Compare [新](新.md), new, whose structure includes an axe-shaped right side, and [村](村.md), village, with 寸 instead of 辛. Their meanings are not meanings of 梓.
- Personal-name readings are outside this lesson; learn names separately.
