# 弁 — speech; discern; valve; bento

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 廾 (no. 55), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弁 stands for several older characters at once, so it has unrelated meanings: speaking and arguing, telling things apart and settling them, a petal or a valve, and, in everyday words, a packed meal. [弁当](../words/弁当.md) べんとう is a bento, a packed meal, and [お弁当](../words/お弁当.md) おべんとう is the same word with the polite prefix お; JMdict glosses both "bento; Japanese box lunch." On its own, 弁 べん is a noun; JMdict glosses it "speech; tongue; talk; eloquence," and, used as a suffix, "dialect; brogue; accent"; other senses include "petal" and "valve."

The Daijisen character entry gives eleven senses for ベン, in a few main groups: telling things apart and settling them, as in 弁償 べんしょう, "compensation; indemnification; reparation"; a packed meal, as in 駅弁 えきべん, "boxed lunch bought at a station (often a local specialty)"; speaking and arguing, as in 弁解 べんかい, "explanation (e.g. for one's actions); excuse; justification," and a dialect, as in 東北弁 とうほくべん, "Tōhoku dialect"; and a petal or a valve, as in 花弁 かべん, "petal," and 安全弁 あんぜんべん, "safety valve." A common everyday word from the speaking group is 弁護士 べんごし, which JMdict glosses "lawyer; attorney." The Daijisen supplementary note says the discerning, settling and packed-meal senses were originally written 辨, the speaking senses 辯 and the petal and valve senses 瓣. It gives only the kun reading わきまえる and lists 花弁 (はなびら) among words with a hard-to-guess reading. All eleven senses, with an example each, are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a shape like katakana ム at the top and 廾 at the bottom, two upright strokes joined by a horizontal bar. Imagine a speaker in a small pointed cap standing on a two-legged stand to give a speech. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that ム or 廾 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 弁当, 弁 supplies ベン and 当 supplies トウ, with no sound change; JMdict also lists 辨當, 弁當 and 辨当 as spellings with out-dated kanji. お弁当 adds the polite prefix お, written in kana, to 弁当. In 弁える, 弁 writes わきま and える is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana and lists 辨える as an out-dated spelling. 花弁 has two readings: as かべん, 花 supplies カ and 弁 supplies ベン; as はなびら, the reading belongs to the whole word.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ベン, with 弁償, 花弁 and 雄弁 as examples, and it gives 辨, 瓣 and 辯 as older forms of 弁. ヘン, かんむり, わ.ける, はなびら, あらそ.う and わきま.える are not in the official table. Practice selects ベン, the reading with linked word articles; わきま.える is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, and the other readings have no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [当](当.md), "hit; be on target," in 弁当 and お弁当.
- [台](台.md), "stand, machine counter," has the same ム-like shape at the top, over 口, and [去](去.md), "leave, elapse," has it at the bottom, under 土. [算](算.md), "calculate; reckon," ends in the same 廾 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [話](話.md), "talk, speak," [言](言.md), "say; words," [語](語.md), "language, word," [詞](詞.md), "words; lyrics; part of speech," [論](論.md), "argument; discourse," and [議](議.md), "deliberate; debate," overlap the senses "speech," "talk," "dialect" and "argument" that 弁 carries; 論 appears with 弁 in 弁論. [別](別.md), "separate, another," overlaps "discern" and "distinguish" and is also read わ.ける, and [飯](飯.md), "cooked rice; meal," overlaps the meal that 弁当 and お弁当 name. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "speech" or "meal" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, the eleven Daijisen senses of ベン, one example each: telling right from wrong, 弁別 べんべつ, "distinguishing; distinction; discrimination; telling apart"; settling a matter properly, 弁償; a packed meal, 駅弁; speaking with reasons, 弁解; argument, talk or a way of speaking, 雄弁 ゆうべん, "eloquence; fluency (of speech)"; a local way of speaking, a dialect, 東北弁; a petal, 花弁; a petal-like part at the opening of a pipe that controls the flow, 安全弁; a hood-shaped cap, 武弁 ぶべん, "soldier," a word JMdict marks formal or literary; standing in for 辨, handling business, 合弁 ごうべん, "joint management; pool"; and standing in for 辮, braiding, 弁髪 べんぱつ, "pigtail; queue; cue."
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ベン senses: 思弁 しべん, "speculation," a philosophy term; 弁済 べんさい, "repayment; settlement (of a debt); paying off," and 勘弁 かんべん, "pardon; forgiveness; forbearance"; 腰弁 こしべん, "lunchbox hanging from one's waist; low-paid office worker"; 弁護 べんご, "advocacy; defense; justification," 弁論 べんろん, "discussion; debate; argument," and 答弁 とうべん, "response; reply; answer; defence; defense"; 弁舌 べんぜつ, "speech; eloquence; manner of speaking," and 熱弁 ねつべん, "impassioned speech; fervent speech"; and 弁膜 べんまく, "valve (of the heart and the veins)," an anatomy term.
