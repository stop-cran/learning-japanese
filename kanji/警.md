# 警 — warn; be on guard

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

警 concerns warning people and keeping watch against trouble. In [警官](../words/警官.md) けいかん,
it belongs to the vocabulary of policing; in 警告 けいこく, it points to a warning.
Do not reduce every word containing 警 to the English noun "police."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the lower 言, "words," as a warning notice beneath the busy upper shape.
This is a memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.
