# 均 — level; average

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

均 concerns making things level or even, and averaging. 平均 へいきん names an average; 均す ならす can mean leveling something or averaging it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the ground 土 being made even beside the small strokes held within the right-hand curve.

## Notes

- JMdict marks 均す as usually written in kana. The verb is transitive: it describes leveling or averaging something, not simply being level.
- An average does not assert that all the individual values are equal. Keep the mathematical use of 平均 distinct from a claim that every item is identical.
