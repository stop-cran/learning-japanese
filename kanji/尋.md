# 尋 — inquire; search for; fathom

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

尋 is asking about something or searching for it, and also an old unit of length. [尋ねる](../words/尋ねる.md) たずねる is to ask; JMdict glosses it "to ask; to enquire; to inquire," then "to search; to look for; to look into; to investigate," and lists the second spelling 訊ねる. On its own, 尋 read ひろ or じん is JMdict's "fathom."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ジン. One example of each:

- a unit of length, the span of both outstretched arms: 千尋 せんじん, "great depth; bottomless";
- ordinary: 尋常 じんじょう, "common; ordinary; usual; average; normal";
- seeking out or visiting: one compound that JMdict does not list;
- used in place of 訊 for asking questions: 尋問 じんもん, "questioning; interrogation; examination (of a witness)."

It gives the kun readings たずねる and ひろ, the same as KANJIDIC2. Its separate headword ひろ describes a customary unit measured from fingertip to fingertip with both arms stretched out, taken as 5 shaku, about 1.515 m, or 6 shaku, about 1.816 m, and used for rope, fishing line and water depth. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a shape like the katakana ヨ at the top, [工](工.md), "craft, construction," at the left and [口](口.md), "mouth," at the right in the middle, and [寸](寸.md), "sun (length unit); small," at the bottom. Imagine a worker opening their mouth to ask how many measures deep the water is. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that ヨ, 工, 口 or 寸 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ジン | on | 尋常 じんじょう - common; ordinary; usual; average; normal; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| たず.ねる | kun | [尋ねる](../words/尋ねる.md) たずねる — ask; inquire |
| ひろ | kun | 尋 ひろ - fathom; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 尋ねる, 尋 writes たず and ねる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 尋常, 尋 supplies ジン and 常 supplies ジョウ. The word 尋 read ひろ is the kanji alone, with no okurigana; JMdict also reads it じん. In 千尋, JMdict gives せんじん, and ちひろ for this spelling only.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジン, with 尋問, 尋常 and 千尋 as examples, and たずねる, with 尋ねる and 尋ね人 たずねびと, "missing person; wanted person." It compares 尋ねる with 訪ねる たずねる, "to visit; to call on; to pay a visit to," which has the same reading. ひろ is not in the official table. Practice selects たず.ねる, the reading with a linked word article; the other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 尋ねる is 尋 with okurigana only.
- [寺](寺.md), "Buddhist temple," and [尊](尊.md), "revere; esteem," have the same 寸 at the bottom, and [射](射.md), "shoot; shine into," has it at the right. All are different kanji.
- [問](問.md), "ask, question," [聞](聞.md), "hear, ask," [伺](伺.md), "ask humbly; visit humbly," [訪](訪.md), "visit; call on," [探](探.md), "search," [捜](捜.md), "search; look for," [求](求.md), "seek; request," [調](調.md), "investigate; tune," and [査](査.md), "investigate; inspect," overlap the senses "ask," "search," "investigate," "seek" and "visit" that 尋 and 尋ねる carry; 問 appears with 尋 in 尋問, and 訪 is also read たず.ねる. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "ask" or "search" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, Daijisen also gives 審尋 しんじん, "hearing; interrogation," for the asking sense.
