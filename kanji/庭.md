# 庭 — garden; yard

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

庭 names a garden, yard, or courtyard. As a word, [庭](../words/庭.md) にわ does not require flower beds:
it can be the outdoor space by a building.
In 家庭 かてい, the meaning is the home or household, not a particular patch of ground.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a winding garden path in the 廷 shape beneath the 广 shelter.
The path is a visual association with the sweeping strokes; it is not a historical or phonetic analysis.

## Notes

[公園](../words/公園.md) こうえん is a public park; it is not a blanket replacement for someone's 庭.
[座](座.md), [度](度.md), and [店](店.md) share the outer 广, so use the lower sweeping form to recognize 庭.
Learn 家庭 as a whole word rather than translating it literally as "house garden."
