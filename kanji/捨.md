# 捨 — discard; abandon

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

捨 concerns discarding or giving something up. 捨てる すてる can describe disposing of an unwanted object, without any particular throwing motion. 取捨 しゅしゃ concerns choosing what to adopt or reject.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside 舎 and imagine a hand setting an unwanted object outside a building. This is a modern-shape story, not a historical derivation.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.

## Notes

- 捨てる is an ichidan verb. 捨てます すてます, 捨てて すてて and 捨てない すてない retain the stem 捨て.
- Compare [投](投.md), throw: tossing a ball need not mean discarding it, and putting rubbish carefully in a bin can still be 捨てる.
- 取捨 combines taking and discarding; it need not describe physically throwing objects away.
