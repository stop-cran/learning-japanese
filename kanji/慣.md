# 慣 — accustomed

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慣 concerns becoming accustomed to something through experience. In [慣れる](../words/慣れる.md) なれる,
something that once felt unfamiliar becomes easier to deal with. This is familiarity, not necessarily mastery.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the heart-side 忄 settling beside the dense 貫 shape as it becomes familiar.
This is a memory aid for the modern form, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

仕事に慣れる (しごとになれる) means "get used to the work": に marks what becomes familiar.
慣らす presents the action of getting someone or something accustomed instead.

[買](買.md) shares the lower 貝 shape but lacks 忄. [持](持.md) has 扌, not 忄;
[海](海.md) has 氵 and a different right side. These are partial visual comparisons, not related meanings.
