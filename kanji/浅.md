# 浅 — shallow

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

浅 means shallow, literally for limited depth and figuratively for limited knowledge or experience. It is the opposite of [深い](../words/深い.md) ふかい, deep.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the water at the left stopping at the short, stacked marks on the right: it is only a shallow pool.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- 浅い is an i-adjective; 浅く あさく is its adverbial form.
- The depth image also applies to knowledge, experience, or how recently something began. Do not limit 浅 to the physical depth of water.
