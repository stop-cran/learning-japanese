# 祈 — pray; wish

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 示 (no. 113) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

祈 concerns prayer and earnest wishes. 祈る can describe praying to a deity or wishing someone a good outcome, so not every use needs an explicitly religious setting.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the 礻-shaped left side beside 斤 as a person keeping an earnest wish close. This is only a visual memory scene, not an etymology or phonetic claim.

The canonical key 示, no. 113, appears as 礻 on the left. Keep 示 in the metadata rather than substituting the visible variant.

## Notes

- 祈る is godan despite ending in る: 祈ります いのります, 祈って いのって and 祈らない いのらない. Do not conjugate it as ichidan.
- 家族の健康を祈ります。 (かぞくのけんこうをいのります。) - I pray for my family's health. を marks the desired outcome; 神に祈る (かみにいのる), pray to a deity, marks the addressee with に.
- Compare [折](折.md), fold, [近](近.md), near, and [所](所.md), location. Sharing a 斤-shaped part does not give them 祈's meaning.
