# 嫡 — principal family line; heir

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

嫡 identifies the principal family line in words about descent and succession. In 嫡子 (ちゃくし), context tells you whether the writer is identifying an heir or a child by parentage.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare 嫡 with [滴](滴.md) and [適](適.md). Look to the left edge or lower left: only 嫡 starts with 女; 滴 starts with water and 適 has a movement stroke underneath.

## Notes

[商](商.md) has a similar outline to part of the right side but lacks the 女 at the left. The principal-line sense helps when reading family histories; it is not a rule about inheritance today. The shapes on the left distinguish 嫡 from [滴](滴.md) and [適](適.md). [嗣](嗣.md) also appears in a word for an heir, 嗣子 (しし); [継](継.md) concerns succeeding or carrying on, not who a child's mother was.
