# 鑑 — model; appraise

**Strokes:** 23 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鑑 has two meaning groups: a model or example to learn from, and looking closely at something to tell good from bad. KANJIDIC2 lists “specimen”, “take warning from” and “learn from”. Kanjipedia's first sense is かがみ。手本。模範。いましめ。 (model; example; warning), and its second is かんがみる。見わける。照らしあわせて見る。 (consider; tell apart; look at side by side). Digital Daijisen also gives 資料を並べて手本とする書物 (a book that sets out material as a model). JMdict glosses [図鑑](../words/図鑑.md) ずかん “pictorial book; picture book; illustrated reference book; identification manual; field guide” and [鑑賞](../words/鑑賞.md) かんしょう “appreciation (of art, music, poetry, etc.)”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [金](金.md), "gold, money," stands on the left, and [監](監.md), "oversee; watch over," stands on the right. Picture an expert watching over a piece of polished metal, judging whether it is a model piece. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 金 or 監 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists カン, with 鑑賞, 鑑定 かんてい (“judgement; judgment; expert opinion; appraisal”, JMdict 1215210) and 年鑑 ねんかん (“yearbook; almanac; annual”, JMdict 1468370), and かんがみる, with 鑑みる. Kanjipedia lists かがみ outside the table (外).
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings あき and あきら. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings あき, あきら, かた, かね, しげ, のり, み, and みる. These are name use, not quiz readings.

These twenty-five cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [鏡](鏡.md) (“mirror; lens”): KANJIDIC2 gives the kun reading かがみ for both 鑑 and 鏡, Digital Daijisen has the headword かがみ【鏡／▽鑑／×鑒】, and JMdict entry 2017840 lists 鏡 as a search-only kanji form of 鑑 かがみ.
- [範](範.md) (“model; standard”) and [模](模.md) (“imitate; model; pattern”): JMdict glosses 鑑 かがみ “model; pattern; paragon; exemplar” (entry 2017840), Kanjipedia's first sense of 鑑 includes 手本。模範。 (model; exemplar), and KANJIDIC2 gives 範 “pattern”, “example” and “model”.
- [例](例.md) (“example; precedent”): Digital Daijisen's first sense of 鑑 is 反省の資となる前例や手本 (a precedent or model that serves for reflection).
- [則](則.md) (“rule; follow a model”): JMdict's “model” for 鑑 かがみ and Kanjipedia's 手本 (model) overlap the title's “follow a model”.
- [評](評.md) (“appraise; reputation”) and [判](判.md) (“judge; seal”): JMdict glosses 鑑定 かんてい “judgement; judgment; expert opinion; appraisal” (entry 1215210), Digital Daijisen's third sense of 鑑 begins よしあしを見分ける (tell good from bad), and KANJIDIC2 gives 評 “evaluate”.
- [踏](踏.md) (“tread; trample”): KANJIDIC2 lists “appraise”, a word of this title, for 踏, and JMdict glosses 値踏み ねぶみ “appraisal; estimation; evaluation; setting prices” (entry 1600200).
- [弁](弁.md) (“speech; discern; valve; bento”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 鑑 includes 見わける (tell apart), and KANJIDIC2 gives 弁 “distinguish” and “discrimination”.
- [審](審.md) (“examine; judge; trial”), [裁](裁.md) (“judge; cut out cloth”), [査](査.md) (“investigate; inspect”) and [検](検.md) (“inspect; examine; check”): JMdict lists 鑑査 as a spelling of 監査 かんさ, “inspection; audit; judgement; judgment” (entry 1590860), and Digital Daijisen's third sense of 鑑 includes よく点検する (inspect carefully).
- [比](比.md) (“compare; ratio”) and [較](較.md) (“compare; contrast”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 鑑 includes 照らしあわせて見る, looking at things side by side to check them.
- [慮](慮.md) (“consider; concern; forethought”) and [習](習.md) (“learn; practise”): JMdict glosses 鑑みる かんがみる “to take into account; to bear in mind; to consider; to learn from; to take as a lesson” (entry 1215170), and KANJIDIC2 lists “learn from” for 鑑 and “consider” for 慮.
- [警](警.md) (“warn; be on guard”): KANJIDIC2 lists “take warning from” for 鑑, Kanjipedia's first sense of 鑑 includes いましめ (warning), JMdict lists 警め as a spelling of 戒め いましめ, whose first sense is “caution; admonition; warning; lesson” (entry 1609250), and the 警 card lists いまし.める.
- [典](典.md) (“reference texts; rules; ceremony”), [本](本.md) (“book, origin”), [冊](冊.md) (“volume; book counter”) and [巻](巻.md) (“roll; book volume”): Digital Daijisen's second sense of 鑑 is 資料を並べて手本とする書物 (a book that sets out material as a model), with 図鑑 and 年鑑 among its examples.
- [暦](暦.md) (“calendar; almanac; year reckoning”): JMdict glosses 年鑑 ねんかん “yearbook; almanac; annual” (entry 1468370), and KANJIDIC2 gives 暦 “almanac”.
- [証](証.md) (“evidence; proof”): Digital Daijisen's fourth sense of 鑑 is 身分・資格などを見分ける証拠 (evidence for telling someone's status or qualifications), and JMdict glosses 鑑札 かんさつ “licence; license; permit” (entry 1215180).
- [印](印.md) (“stamp; mark”): JMdict glosses 印鑑 いんかん “stamp; seal” (entry 1168120).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 層 (“layer; stratum”), 頂 (“summit; receive”), and 塔 (“tower; pagoda”); none means a model or judging the worth of something.
