# 房 — room; tassel; bunch

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 戶 (as 戸, no. 63), at the top left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

房 is a room, and also a tassel or a hanging bunch. [冷房](../words/冷房.md) れいぼう is air cooling, [女房](../words/女房.md) にょうぼう is a wife, [文房具](../words/文房具.md) ぶんぼうぐ is stationery or writing supplies and [暖房](../words/暖房.md) だんぼう is indoor heating.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ボウ: a side room or dwelling, as in 工房 こうぼう, "workshop; studio; atelier," 書房 しょぼう, "study; library," 僧房 そうぼう, "priests' temple quarters," 独房 どくぼう, "single-person cell; isolation cell; solitary cell; solitary confinement cell," and 暖房; a couple's bedroom, as in 閨房 けいぼう, "marital bedroom; conjugal bedroom"; a partitioned, room-like space, as in 子房 しぼう, "ovary," and 心房 しんぼう, "atrium"; and the old province of Awa, as in 安房 あわ, "Awa (former province located in the south of present-day Chiba Prefecture)." For ふさ it gives threads bundled with their ends hanging loose, such as the tassel on a hat; hanging clusters of flowers or fruit, such as wisteria or grapes; and bag-shaped parts, such as the inner skin of a mandarin orange.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [戸](戸.md), "door; counter for households," at the top left and [方](方.md), "direction, method," beneath it. Imagine a door opening onto a room, with a signpost pointing the way in. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 戸 or 方 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ボウ | on | [冷房](../words/冷房.md) れいぼう — air cooling; [女房](../words/女房.md) にょうぼう — wife; [文房具](../words/文房具.md) ぶんぼうぐ — stationery; writing supplies; [暖房](../words/暖房.md) だんぼう — indoor heating |
| ふさ | kun | 房 ふさ - tuft (of hair, threads, etc.); fringe; tassel; 乳房 ちぶさ - breast; udder; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 冷房, 冷 supplies レイ. In 女房, 女 supplies ニョウ. In 文房具, 文 supplies ブン and 具 supplies グ. In 暖房, 暖 supplies ダン. In the single-kanji word 房, 房 writes ふさ with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 一房 ひとふさ, 房 is still ふさ after ひと, and in 乳房, 乳 supplies ち while 房 takes the voiced form ぶさ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ボウ, with 独房, 冷房 and 僧房 as examples, and ふさ, with 房, 一房 and 乳房. Practice selects ボウ, the only reading with linked word articles; ふさ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

The Daijisen character entry also lists ホウ, which it labels 漢音, and the difficult reading 阿房 あほう, which JMdict marks as an ateji (phonetic) reading. KANJIDIC2 does not list ホウ, so it is not part of the metadata here.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [冷](冷.md), "cold; chill," in 冷房, [女](女.md), "woman, female," in 女房, [文](文.md), "sentence, writing," and [具](具.md), "tools; ingredients," in 文房具, and [暖](暖.md), "warm; warm up," in 暖房.
- [戸](戸.md), "door; counter for households," is the shape at the top left, and [戻](戻.md), "return; revert," has it too. [方](方.md), "direction, method," is the shape beneath it, and [防](防.md), "defend; ward off," has it too and is also read ボウ, as is [忙](忙.md), "busy." All are different kanji.
- [室](室.md), "room, chamber," [群](群.md), "flock; cluster; crowd," [部](部.md), "section; department," and [束](束.md), "bundle," overlap the senses "room," "cluster," "section" and "bunch" that 房 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "room" or "bunch" as exclusive to any one character.
