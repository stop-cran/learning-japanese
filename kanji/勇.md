# 勇 — courage; bravery

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

勇 concerns courage, bravery, or high spirits. 勇気 ゆうき means courage, while 勇む いさむ describes being in high spirits or eager to act. A word about courage need not describe someone who feels no fear.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the upper マ-shaped strokes above the 田-like middle and 力 at the bottom, as though gathering strength before acting bravely.

The canonical radical 力 is the lower component.

## Notes

- 勇気 is a noun. 勇敢 ゆうかん is used with な before a noun, as in 勇敢な人 ゆうかんなひと, a brave person.
- Compare [男](男.md), man or male: 勇 has the additional upper strokes. The visual resemblance is not a rule that bravery belongs to one gender.
