# 被 — cover; incur

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

被 connects covering or putting something over oneself with receiving an effect, including harm. Its native verbs have different readings and uses even when their written forms coincide.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a layer of clothing 衤 over [皮](皮.md), skin. It can cover a person while that person receives an outside effect; this is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

The indexing radical 衣 appears as left-hand 衤. Do not confuse it with 礻, the form associated with 示.

## Notes

- 被う おおう has a rare kanji-spelling flag; the same entry includes 覆う. That flag concerns this spelling, not the rarity of the pronunciation おおう.
- JMdict marks the head-wearing/blame uses of かぶる and the shown uses of かぶせる as usually written in kana. The separate verb こうむる can receive kindness as well as harm; it is not restricted to bad experiences.
- The linked 被る article teaches かぶる and explicitly contrasts こうむる. A shared filename is not a shared primary reading.
