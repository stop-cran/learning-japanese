# 多 — many, much

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 夕 (no. 36) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

多 is made of two stacked 夕: two of them together mean "lots", the way 林 and 森 use two and three 木 for many trees. In the earliest forms the component is thought to be a piece of meat rather than 夕 (evening),
and that is debated, so treat this as a mnemonic only.

## Common words

- [多い](../words/多い.md) おおい — many, a lot
- [多少](../words/多少.md) たしょう — more or less, somewhat
- 多分 たぶん — perhaps, probably

## Notes

Its opposite is [少](少.md): 多い and [少ない](../words/少ない.md) are the pair. Okurigana (the kana after the kanji, like い in 多い): 多い (おおい), and note it is a different word from 大きい ([大](大.md), big).
Do not confuse 多 with 夕 (evening) or with [外](外.md), which contains 夕 plus 卜.
