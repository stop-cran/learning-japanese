# 難 — difficult

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

難 concerns difficulty and trouble. [難しい](../words/難しい.md) むずかしい describes something hard to do
or understand: この問題は難しいです。 (このもんだいはむずかしいです。) — This problem is difficult.
It is not the material hardness of stone or metal.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** think of the dense left block as a difficult puzzle beside 隹 on the right.
That is a memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

The contrast for task difficulty is [易しい](../words/易しい.md) やさしい, "easy," usually written in kana as やさしい.
For hard stone, compare 硬い (かたい), not 難しい.
Visually, [雑](雑.md) has the same right 隹 but a simpler left block; [集](集.md) moves 隹 to the top.
[親](親.md) is a looser dense-shape comparison with 見 on the right.
