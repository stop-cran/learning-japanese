# 屈 — bend; yield; submit

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44), at the top and left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

屈 is bending: bending the body or a joint, and by extension bending to someone else's will, yielding or giving in. [退屈](../words/退屈.md) たいくつ is boredom or something tedious; JMdict glosses it "tedious; boring; dull; uninteresting; tiresome; monotonous" and, as a する verb, "to feel bored; to get bored (with); to get tired (of)." JMdict glosses 屈する くっする "to yield; to give in; to be daunted; to shrink" and, in a second sense, "to bend (knee, etc.)."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for クツ. One example of each:

- bending, or being bent: 屈折 くっせつ, "bending; curving; twisting; turning; winding";
- giving in, or breaking someone's spirit: 屈服 くっぷく, "yielding; submission; surrender; giving way; succumbing";
- being bent down and unable to stretch out: 窮屈 きゅうくつ, "narrow; tight; cramped," a group in which it also lists 退屈;
- used in place of 倔, strong: 屈強 くっきょう, "robust; brawny; muscular; strong; sturdy," which JMdict also spells 倔強, a rarely used kanji form.

Daijisen gives the kun readings かがむ, かがまる and かがめる. かがまる is not a KANJIDIC2 reading; JMdict writes it 屈まる, "to bow (of one's back, e.g. due to age); to bend." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 尸, the radical, as a roof-like top with a long stroke down the left, and under it [出](出.md), "go out, exit." Imagine someone going out through a door with a low roof: to get out, they have to bend down. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 尸 or 出 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| クツ | on | [退屈](../words/退屈.md) たいくつ — boredom; tedious |
| かが.む | kun | 屈む かがむ - to stoop; to lean over; to bend forward; JMdict marks it usually written in kana; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| かが.める | kun | 屈める かがめる - to stoop; to bend (e.g. one's knees); JMdict marks it usually written in kana; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 退屈, 退 supplies タイ and 屈 supplies クツ, with no sound change. In many compounds クツ shortens to くっ, so the next sound is doubled: くっ + せつ gives 屈折 くっせつ, and in 屈服 くっぷく the ふ of 服 also changes to ぷ. In 屈む, 屈 writes かが and む is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 屈める uses the same かが with める. JMdict also gives the readings こごむ and くぐむ, the latter an obsolete kana usage, for 屈む, and こごめる for 屈める.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only クツ, with 屈辱 くつじょく, "disgrace; humiliation," 屈伸 くっしん, "bending and stretching; flexing and extending," 不屈 ふくつ, "persistence; fortitude; indomitability," and 理屈 りくつ, "theory; reason; logic," as examples. かがむ and かがめる are not in the official table. Practice selects クツ, the reading with a linked word article; かが.む and かが.める are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [退](退.md), "withdraw; retreat," in 退屈.
- [出](出.md), "go out, exit," is the whole lower part of 屈. [届](届.md), "reach; deliver; notify," and [居](居.md), "be present; dwell," have the same 尸 at the top and left, with a different part under it. All are different kanji.
- [曲](曲.md), "bend; musical piece," [折](折.md), "fold," and [負](負.md), "lose; bear a burden," overlap the senses "bend" and "yield; give in" that 屈 and its words carry; 折 appears with 屈 in 屈折. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "bend" or "yield" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the クツ senses: bending, 屈曲 くっきょく, "crookedness; bending; indentation; curvature; flexion," 屈指 くっし, "leading; foremost; preeminent; outstanding; one of the best," 屈伸 and 後屈 こうくつ, "retroflexion"; giving in, 屈従 くつじゅう, "servile submission; subservience," 屈辱 and 不屈; and being bent down, 鬱屈 うっくつ, "gloomy," 卑屈 ひくつ, "servile; subservient; obsequious; slavish; self-abasing; grovelling," and 偏屈 へんくつ, "narrow-minded; obstinate; stubborn; perverse; unreasonable; warped."
