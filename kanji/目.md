# 目 — eye

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

目 is a pictograph of an eye. The oldest forms show a horizontal eye with pupil; when the character was turned upright for vertical writing it became the narrow box with two inner strokes.
It means "eye", and by extension "look, sight", "item, point" (項目) and, as a suffix, an ordinal ("-th").

## Notes

**Ordinal 目, [番](番.md), and [号](号.md):** In ordinal expressions, -目 (-め) follows a counter or counting expression to mark position in a sequence:
三番目 (さんばんめ), "third"; 三人目 (さんにんめ), "third person"; 三つ目 (みっつめ), "third item."
番 alone can mark either identification or order: 一番 (いちばん) can mean "number one" or "first," depending on context.
号 is used in conventional numbered designations such as 三号室 (さんごうしつ), "room number three."
That need not be 三番目の部屋 (さんばんめのへや), "the third room" in the order you encounter them.
号 can express order too; these are overlapping uses, not three interchangeable ordinal endings.

The kun reading め also means "experience" and "mesh" in expressions such as ひどい目にあう (have a terrible time) and 網の目 あみのめ.

Look-alikes: [日](日.md) (sun, day) is wider and has one inner stroke where 目 has two; 白 (white) is 日 with a small stroke on top; 自 (oneself) is 目 with a small stroke on top; 且 (moreover) has a longer bottom stroke.
[耳](耳.md) (ear) has a similar box but its lines extend beyond it.
