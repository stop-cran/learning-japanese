# 打 — strike

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

打 centers on striking or hitting. [打つ](../words/打つ.md) うつ can describe hitting a ball,
while 打撃 だげき names a blow or impact. The verb has other uses too; learn the object along with the verb.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the hand-side 扌 tapping the flat top of 丁.
This is an image for remembering the modern form, not a proven account of its construction.

## Notes

ボールを打つ means "hit the ball." The object takes を.
The past form is 打った (うった), not うつた.

[払](払.md), [折](折.md), and [持](持.md) share 扌.
For 打, look for the very small 丁 at the right, rather than ム, 斤, or 寺.
Sharing the hand component does not make the actions synonymous.
