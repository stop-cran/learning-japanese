# 伏 — lie prostrate; lurk; yield

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

伏 means lying face down on the ground, hiding out of sight, and following or giving in to someone. KANJIDIC2 lists “prostrated”, “bend down”, “bow”, “cover” and “lay (pipes)”. The word [降伏](../words/降伏.md) こうふく is “surrender; capitulation; submission”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 亻, a variant of [人](人.md), "person," stands on the left, and [犬](犬.md), "dog," stands on the right; KanjiVG builds 犬 from [大](大.md), "big, large," and the dot shape 丶. Picture a person and a big dog lying flat side by side, hiding in tall grass. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 亻 or 犬 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists フク, ふせる and ふす. Kanjipedia lists ブク and the kun かくれる and したがう outside the table (外).
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings ふし and ふせ, and Digital Daijisen gives the name readings ふし and やす; these are name uses, not quiz readings.

These thirteen cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [隠](隠.md) (“hide; conceal”), [潜](潜.md) (“dive; submerge; lurk”) and [秘](秘.md) (“secret; conceal”): hiding and lurking out of sight; 伏せる ふせる can mean “to conceal; to hide; to keep secret”.
- [屈](屈.md) (“bend; yield; submit”) and [譲](譲.md) (“hand over; yield”): 伏 can mean giving in to someone.
- [従](従.md) (“follow; obey; subordinate”), [随](随.md) (“follow; at will”) and [守](守.md) (“guard; obey”): following and obeying someone.
- [寝](寝.md) (“lie down; sleep”): lying down; 伏せる ふせる can mean “to lie down (and sleep); to go to bed (with an illness)”.
- [拝](拝.md) (“worship; bow in respect”): bowing down low; 伏す ふす can mean “to bend down (in submission); to bow down; to prostrate oneself”.
- [覆](覆.md) (“cover; overturn”) and [被](被.md) (“cover; incur”): 伏 can mean to cover.
- [礼](礼.md) (“bow; thanks”): 伏 can mean a bow.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 起 (“wake; rise”), 座 (“sit; seating”), and 伸 (“stretch; lengthen”); none means lying prostrate, lurking or yielding.
