# 半 — half

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 十 (no. 24) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

半 is commonly explained as 八 (divide) above 牛 (ox): dividing a large animal down the middle. The details are not certain, but the idea
"split into two equal parts" is the meaning: half, middle, partial.
The modern form shows two dots on top of a vertical stroke crossing bars.

## Notes

- 半 follows a number to mean "and a half": 三時半 さんじはん (half past three), 一年半 いちねんはん (one and a half years). It also follows a duration: 一時間半 いちじかんはん (an hour and a half).
- Look-alikes: [年](年.md) (year) and [午](午.md) (noon) both have a long vertical stroke; 半 has two dots on top and a horizontal pair underneath. Also compare 牛 (cow), which has only one stroke on top.
