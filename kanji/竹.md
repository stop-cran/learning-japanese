# 竹 — bamboo

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

竹 names bamboo. Its full form is also the canonical bamboo radical, while a compressed form appears at the top of many other characters. A shared bamboo-shaped top does not mean that every resulting word names a bamboo object.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the two matching sides as two bamboo stems with leaves branching outward. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a claim about the ancient drawing or historical character type.

The indexing key is 竹 itself, no. 118. Its top-position form is ⺮, visible in characters such as [符](符.md) and [筆](筆.md).

## Notes

- 竹林 also has the dictionary reading たけばやし. The チク row gives the reading ちくりん without claiming that it is the only reading of the spelling.
- In the conventional [松](../words/松.md) まつ, 竹 たけ, [梅](../words/梅.md) うめ three-tier labels, 竹 names the middle option. That rank sense does not mean that a course contains bamboo.
- Compare [笑](笑.md), laugh, with its bamboo-shaped top. [米](米.md) means rice grain; it is not another reading or spelling of bamboo.
