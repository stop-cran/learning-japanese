# 幾 — how many; how much

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 幺 (no. 52) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

幾 appears in questions about number and amount.
[幾つ](../words/幾つ.md) いくつ asks "how many?" or "how old?"; 幾ら いくら asks "how much?"
Both are usually written in kana. Knowing 幾 helps you recognize the kanji spellings without making them the default in your own writing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** look for two small 幺 shapes above the crossing lower strokes.
Imagine trying to count the little loops. This is a mnemonic, not a historical origin story.

## Notes

幾つか is also marked usually kana in the dictionary. Adding か changes a question about number into an indefinite amount.
For visual comparison, [紙](紙.md) and [後](後.md) also contain small angular, loop-like strokes.
[強](強.md) is only a loose resemblance: its bent left-hand component is 弓, not the paired 幺 at the top of 幾.
