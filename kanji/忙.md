# 忙 — busy

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

忙 describes being occupied or busy. [忙しい](../words/忙しい.md) いそがしい can describe a person
with much to do or a hectic period; it is not simply another word for working.

The indexing radical 心 appears as the narrow left-hand form 忄.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** fit too many tasks beside that narrow strip and the [亡](亡.md) shape on the right.
This visual story is not an etymology.
