# 球 — ball; sphere

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 玉 (as 王 on the left, no. 96) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

球 points to a ball or a three-dimensional round shape. [地球](../words/地球.md) ちきゅう names Earth as a globe,
while 野球 やきゅう names the sport baseball.
The canonical radical 玉 appears as the narrow 王-shaped part on the left.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** keep that left part separate from the spreading strokes of 求 on the right,
and imagine a ball bouncing across them. This is a visual aid, not etymology.

## Notes

弟は野球が好きです。 (おとうとはやきゅうがすきです。) — My younger brother likes baseball.
Do not translate 野球 by adding the English meanings "field" and "ball"; learn the established sport name.

[理](理.md) and [現](現.md) share the left side, but their right sides are 里 and 見.
[王](王.md) resembles that left component alone; it does not mean a sphere.
