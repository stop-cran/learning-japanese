# 上 — up, above

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

上 is an indicative character: a base line with a mark placed above it. Older forms are simpler, a short line over a longer one;
the modern shape adds a vertical stroke. Its mirror image is [下](下.md), with the mark below the line.
Because it is a position mark, it can mean "above", "upper", "up", "on top of" and "go up", and extends figuratively
to rank and quality (上手, 上司, 年上).

## Common words

- [上下](../words/上下.md) じょうげ — top and bottom, up and down
- [川上](../words/川上.md) かわかみ — upstream
- 上手 じょうず — skilled (the special pair with 下手 へた)

## Notes

The pair かみ／しも: 上 can read かみ ("upper") against しも for [下](下.md). They appear in 川上／川下, 上座／下座 and stage terms.
