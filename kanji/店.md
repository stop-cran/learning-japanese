# 店 — shop

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **Phonetic:** 占 (セン; the sound shifted to テン, hedged) · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

店 is a phonetic compound. 广 is a building with a roof (a shed or house), and 占 is usually taken as the sound component (占 is read only セン, as in 占う うらなう, "to tell fortunes"; the sound is thought to have shifted to テン).
The idea is "a building where goods are put out", that is, a shop. In older usage the meaning also included an inn or stall.
The sound テン is the standard on'yomi, and the kun reading みせ is the native word. たな is a rare reading found in 店賃 たなちん and 店子 たなこ (tenant).

## Notes

店 is not 広 (ひろい, wide): 広 is 广 + ム, while 店 contains 占 (卜 over 口) under the roof.
Compare nothing else here: a shop that you use as a place is 店, the person is 店員.
