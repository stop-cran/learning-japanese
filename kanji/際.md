# 際 — edge; occasion

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (as 阝) (no. 170) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

際 can mark an edge or the point at which something happens. 窓際 (まどぎわ) is the space beside a window.
For an occasion, その際 (そのさい) means "on that occasion."
The compound [実際](../words/実際.md) じっさい means "actually" or "reality"; learn that word as a unit,
not as a literal instruction to find an edge.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** let the left 阝 mark the edge of a crowded right-hand block.
阝 is the visible form of the radical 阜 here. This is a visual cue, not historical derivation.
