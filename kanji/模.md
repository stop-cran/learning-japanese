# 模 — imitate; model; pattern

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

模 is a model to copy from, and the copying itself: a template, an example to follow, an imitation, and the pattern or look that results. [模倣](../words/模倣.md) もほう is imitation or copying, and [模様](../words/模様.md) もよう is a pattern or the appearance of a situation.

The Daijisen character entry gives five senses for モ: a mould or template for making copies, or a model to follow, as in 模型 もけい, "model; dummy; maquette," and 模範 もはん, "exemplar; model; example; pattern"; form or appearance, as in 模様; copying exactly, as in 模擬 もぎ, "imitation," 模写 もしゃ, "copy (of the real thing); copying; reproduction; tracing," 模造 もぞう, "imitation; counterfeit; fake," and 模倣; groping with the hands, where 模 stands in for the character 摸, as in 模索 もさく, "groping (for); fumbling around (for); searching (for an answer, solution, etc.)"; and being hard to see clearly, as in 模糊 もこ, "dim; vague; indistinct; faint; obscure." For ボ it gives one sense, the overall shape and size of something, as in 規模 きぼ, "scale; scope; plan; structure."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md), "tree, wood," the key, at the left, and at the right the grass top 艹, [日](日.md), "sun, day," and [大](大.md), "big, large," stacked from top to bottom. Imagine carving a big wooden model of the sun to set out on the grass. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that these parts contribute meaning or sound, although [暮](暮.md) and [墓](墓.md), which contain the same stacked shape, are read ボ, as 模 is in 規模. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| モ | on | [模倣](../words/模倣.md) もほう — imitation; copying; [模様](../words/模様.md) もよう — pattern; appearance |
| ボ | on | 規模 きぼ - scale; scope; plan; structure; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 模倣, 模 supplies モ and 倣 supplies ホウ. In 模様, 模 supplies モ and 様 supplies ヨウ; 様 is not read さま here. In 規模, 規 supplies キ and 模 supplies ボ. The Daijisen character entry also lists two hard-to-read words: the place name 相模 さがみ, "Sagami (former province located in present-day Kanagawa Prefecture)," and the plant name 酸模 すかんぽ, "garden sorrel (Rumex acetosa)," which JMdict marks as usually written in kana alone. Both are learned here as whole words.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows モ, with 模範, 模型 and 模倣 as examples, and ボ, with 規模, and has no kun row for 模. Practice selects モ, the only reading with linked word articles; ボ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [倣](倣.md), "imitate; emulate," in 模倣 and [様](様.md), "manner; honorific suffix," in 模様.
- [暮](暮.md), "dusk; livelihood," and [墓](墓.md), "grave; tomb," contain the same 艹, 日 and 大 shape that forms the right side of 模 and are read ボ; [横](横.md), "horizontal," has the same 木 at the left with a different shape at the right. All are different kanji.
- [倣](倣.md), "imitate; emulate," [範](範.md), "model; standard," [則](則.md), "rule; follow a model," [型](型.md), "mold; type," [写](写.md), "copy, photograph," [似](似.md), "resemble," [形](形.md), "shape; form," and [漠](漠.md), "desert; vague," overlap the senses "imitate," "model," "copy," "mould," "form" and "vague" that 模 and its words carry; 倣 appears in 模倣, 範 in 模範, 型 in 模型 and 写 in 模写, and 模糊 means vague. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "imitate" or "model" as exclusive to any one character.
