# 皆 — all; everyone

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 白 (no. 106) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

皆 gathers the members of a group into "all" or "everyone."
The linked word article teaches [皆](../words/皆.md) みな.
みんな is also a valid reading; JMdict describes it as less formal and more emphatic, and marks the word as usually written in kana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine both upper halves joining one group above 白.
This is a shape-based memory aid, not an explanation of the character's origin.

## Common words

- [皆](../words/皆.md) みな — everyone; all
- みんな — everyone; all
- 皆さん みなさん — everyone, with an honorific suffix

## Notes

皆、元気です。 (みな、げんきです。) — Everyone is well.
Read 皆さん as みなさん, not by adding さん to みんな.

[白](白.md) is the bottom component alone. [習](習.md) has 羽 above 白 instead of 比.
[百](百.md) has a much simpler top; none of these means everyone.
