# 危 — danger

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 卩 (as 㔾, no. 26) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

危 signals danger or a precarious situation. [危険](../words/危険.md) きけん names danger,
and 危ない あぶない describes something dangerous or at risk.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the curled lower part sheltered beneath an overhang that feels unsafe.
This is a modern memory picture, not historical derivation. The lower-right 㔾 is the visible form
of the indexing radical 卩.

## Notes

危ない！ (あぶない！) can be a warning: "Look out!"
For a noun modifier, compare 危ない場所 (あぶないばしょ) and 危険な場所 (きけんなばしょ), both "a dangerous place."
[色](色.md) has a similar top but a different lower shape.
