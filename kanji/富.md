# 富 — wealth; abundance

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

富 concerns wealth or having an abundance of something. The noun [富](../words/富.md) (とみ) is wealth or riches.
富む (とむ) and 豊富 (ほうふ) also describe abundance, which need not be money.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** stack 一, 口, and 田 beneath a roof as a visual inventory of a well-stocked storehouse.
This is a modern memory aid, not a reconstruction of the character's origin.

## Notes

富 has a divided 田 at the bottom, not just a single box. [同](同.md) shares some box-and-line shapes but has a different enclosure.
[宿](宿.md) shares the roof, not the interior arrangement.
For 富む, learn the pattern ～に富む: "be rich in something," rather than treating it as a verb meaning "own money" only.
