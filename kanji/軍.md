# 軍 — army; troops

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

軍 concerns an army, armed forces or military matters. 軍隊 names the troops or military, while 軍事 concerns military affairs.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 車 beneath a 冖-shaped cover as vehicles gathered under one army shelter. This is a modern-shape story, not a historical account of military vehicles.

The canonical radical is 車, below the 冖-shaped top.

## Notes

- The いくさ family also lists 戦 and 兵. Its war/battle sense is unmarked in this snapshot; only its second troops/forces sense is marked archaic. Do not transfer that flag to the entire reading.
- The linked 軍 word article teaches ぐん. The same written character does not make its army definition a complete explanation of the separate いくさ entry.
- [運](運.md) adds 辶 around a 軍-shaped part; the complete character's carry/luck meaning is different from army.
