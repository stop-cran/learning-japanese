# 顔 — face; expression

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

顔 is the face and, by extension, the expression visible on it.
The ordinary noun is [顔](../words/顔.md) かお; 笑顔 (えがお) means a smiling face or smile.
顔を洗います。 (かおをあらいます。) — I wash my face.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** distinguish the left 彦-shaped block, with its three slanting strokes,
from the right 頁. Imagine the slanting strokes drawing attention to someone's face.
This is a modern visual cue, not historical derivation.
