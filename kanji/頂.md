# 頂 — summit; receive

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

頂 points to a top or summit in [頂上](../words/頂上.md) ちょうじょう.
It also writes 頂く (いただく), "receive" in humble language: wording that lowers the speaker's side
relative to the giver. These are meanings to learn through their words, not one English gloss that fits every occurrence.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the small 丁 block marking a high point beside the larger 頁 block.
This is a modern shape cue, not an etymology or a verified phonetic analysis.

## Notes

The dictionary marks the humble "receive" sense of 頂く as usually written in kana.
Do not infer that its kanji spelling is mandatory in polite writing.
[題](題.md), [類](類.md), and [頼](頼.md) share 頁 on the right; compare their much larger left components with 丁.
