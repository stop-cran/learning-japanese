# 航 — navigate; sail; fly

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 舟 (no. 137) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

航 concerns navigation and travel by vessel, including travel through the air. [航空](../words/航空.md) こうくう is aviation; 航海 こうかい concerns a sea voyage or navigation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a craft on the left passing the upright marker on the right as it follows its route.

## Notes

- The canonical key 舟, no. 137, is on the left. A boat-shaped component does not restrict 航空 to water travel.
- 航空 names aviation, not every possible use of the English verb fly. Learn the whole compound.
- Distinguish [般](般.md), used in 一般, from 航. Sharing a left side is not a meaning rule.
