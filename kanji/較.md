# 較 — compare; contrast

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

較 is setting things side by side to compare them. KANJIDIC2 lists “contrast” and “compare”, Kanjipedia's first sense is くらべる。つきあわせる。 (compare; set side by side), and Digital Daijisen gives カク the sense つき合わせて見比べる (to set side by side and compare). [比較](../words/比較.md) ひかく is glossed in JMdict “comparison”, and [比較的](../words/比較的.md) ひかくてき “comparatively; relatively”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [車](車.md), "car, vehicle," stands on the left. On the right is [交](交.md), "intersect; mingle," built from the shape 亠 over [父](父.md), "father." Picture two cars meeting at an intersection, side by side, so a father can compare them. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 車 or 交 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists カク only, with 比較. KANJIDIC2 adds コウ and くら.べる, which Kanjipedia lists outside the table (外).
- Kanjipedia's second sense is あきらか。いちじるしい。, and Digital Daijisen gives あきらか as a kun reading. It is not a KANJIDIC2 reading, so it has no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 較. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings あつ, とお and なお. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.

These two cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [比](比.md) (“compare; ratio”): its title's “compare” matches KANJIDIC2's “compare” for 較, both characters appear in the declaring word 比較, JMdict 比べる (entry 1483540) lists 較べる as a rarely used kanji form, and the 比 card lists くら.べる.
- [競](競.md) (“compete; bid”): JMdict entry 1483540 also lists 競べる as a search-only kanji form, and its second sense is “to compete (on); to measure (one's strength, ability, etc. against another's)”; the 競 card lists くら.べる.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 選 (“choose”), 証 (“evidence; proof”), and 抜 (“extract”); none means to compare or contrast.
