# 賞 — prize; reward

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

賞 concerns a prize, reward or appreciation of merit. 賞 names an award, while 賞与 names a bonus.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the upper 尚-like shape as a prize display, with 貝 below representing the reward. This is a visual memory story, not a historical derivation.

The indexing radical is 貝, the lower part of the character.

## Notes

- JMdict marks 賞める as a rare kanji spelling in the family headed by 褒める. The flag concerns the spelling 賞める, not a rare pronunciation of the ordinary verb ほめる.
- Compare [常](常.md), usual or constant: its bottom is 巾, not 貝. Similar upper strokes do not make the two characters share the award meaning.
