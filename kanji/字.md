# 字 — written character

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 子 (no. 39) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

字 names a written character or letter and can also refer to handwriting. [漢字](../words/漢字.md) are one kind of written character; 字 is not limited to kanji.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine [子](子.md), a child, practicing written characters under the 宀 roof. This is a mnemonic for the modern shape, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

[学](学.md) also has 子 below a covering shape, but its top is different. In 字, the classical radical is 子, even though 宀 is visible above it.

Do not confuse 字, a written symbol, with [語](語.md), which concerns words or language. One word can contain several written characters.
