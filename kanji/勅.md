# 勅 — imperial order

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

勅 concerns an imperial order or the words of a sovereign. Learn it through [勅令](../words/勅令.md) ちょくれい, "imperial edict," rather than as the everyday word for a boss's instruction. It can also mark an imperial connection in compounds: 勅使 (ちょくし) is an imperial envoy, not an order.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a [束](束.md), "bundle," of messages passed with the authority suggested by [力](力.md), "strength." 束 is on the left and the indexing radical 力 is on the right. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical derivation or a phonetic analysis.

Reading questions select only チョク, practiced as ちょく. In 勅令, 勅 supplies チョク and 令 supplies レイ. Both full kun readings remain in the reference metadata; this batch prioritizes the on-compound instead of adding another overlapping word article just for practice.

Digital Daijisen marks the みことのり reading of 勅 as outside the Joyo on/kun reading list. That mark describes the list's scope, not the reading's validity, frequency or obsolescence.

## Notes

- [束](束.md), "bundle," shares the left shape. [効](効.md), "efficacy; effectiveness," and [勤](勤.md), "employment; diligence," share the right-hand 力, but their left parts and meanings differ.
- [令](令.md), "command; decree," and [命](命.md), "life; command," overlap in their order/command meanings. They are useful comparisons, not clearly wrong meaning choices.
- The reference-only いまし.める entry is not a recommendation to invent a spelling from its kana. Matching another word's pronunciation does not establish a word written with 勅.
