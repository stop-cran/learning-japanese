# 羽 — feather; bird counter

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 羽 (no. 124) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

羽 writes words for a feather or wing and also a counter for birds or rabbits. The noun はね and the counter わ are separate lexical uses, not two ways to pronounce the same feather noun in every sentence.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the two similar panels and their short strokes as a pair of feathered wings. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified account of the earliest character.

羽 itself is the canonical key, no. 124. It also appears above other parts in [翌](翌.md) and [習](習.md).

## Notes

- The counter is illustrated by 二羽 にわ, two birds or rabbits. JMdict also records ば and ぱ counter readings; learn the complete counting form rather than reading the noun はね as a counter.
- JMdict permits はね with 羽 and [羽根](../words/羽.md), but restricts the reading は to 羽 in that entry. The linked article also teaches the はね shuttlecock uses, including the spelling-restricted hanetsuki use. Do not transfer は to 羽根 merely because the two spellings share some senses.
- [翼](../words/翼.md) つばさ overlaps with the wing sense, not simply every loose feather. 羽化 concerns an insect's emergence or growing wings; it is not the ordinary noun for a bird's feather.
