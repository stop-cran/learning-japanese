# 宣 — proclaim; announce; declare

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宣 is making something known widely: stating an intention in public, spreading a message, and, in older usage, an emperor's or a god's pronouncement. [宣伝](../words/宣伝.md) せんでん is glossed in JMdict "publicity; advertisement; advertising; propaganda."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for セン. One example of each:

- stating one's intentions widely: 宣言 せんげん, "declaration; proclamation; announcement";
- spreading widely: 宣教 せんきょう, "religious mission; religious proclamation";
- an emperor or a god stating intent: 託宣 たくせん, "oracle; revelation; divine message."

宣伝 belongs to the second sense. Of the kun readings in the character entry, only のたまう is a KANJIDIC2 reading. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the roof-shaped 宀 at the top and 亘 below it, the same shape that stands at the right of [垣](垣.md), "fence; hedge." Imagine a herald on a rooftop calling out an announcement across the fences. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 宀 or 亘 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| セン | on | [宣伝](../words/宣伝.md) せんでん — publicity; advertising; propaganda |
| のたま.う | kun | 宣う のたまう - to say; to be pleased to say (honorific; usually written in kana); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 宣伝, 宣 supplies セン and 伝 supplies デン. In 宣う, 宣 writes のたま and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict also gives the reading のたもう and the spelling 曰う.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only セン, with 宣言, 宣誓 せんせい, "oath; abjuration; pledge," and 宣伝. It does not list のたま.う. Practice selects セン, the reading with a linked word article; のたま.う is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [伝](伝.md), "transmit; convey," in 宣伝.
- [垣](垣.md), "fence; hedge," has the same 亘 at the right. [宙](宙.md), "space; midair," and [室](室.md), "room, chamber," have the same 宀 at the top. All are different kanji.
- [告](告.md), "announce; inform," [述](述.md), "state; relate," [言](言.md), "say; words," [伝](伝.md), "transmit; convey," [令](令.md), "command; decree," and [勅](勅.md), "imperial order," overlap the senses "proclaim," "announce" and "declare," spreading a message, and the emperor's pronouncements that 宣 and its words carry; JMdict's 宣う is "to say." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "announce" or "declare" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the セン senses: for stating intentions, 宣告 せんこく, "sentencing; pronouncement of a verdict; passing of sentence," a law term in JMdict, and 宣誓; for spreading widely, 宣伝 and 宣揚 せんよう, "enhancement (e.g. of national prestige); raising; heightening; promotion"; for an emperor or god, 宣旨 せんじ, "written imperial order (late Heian period)," 宣命 せんみょう, "imperial edict (written in a classical grandiose style)," 院宣 いんぜん, "imperial command (decree); decree of cloistered emperor," in which セン is voiced to ぜん (JMdict also gives いんせん), and 勅宣 ちょくせん, "imperial decree."
