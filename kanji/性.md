# 性 — nature; gender

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

性 can refer to a nature or characteristic, as in [性格](../words/性格.md) せいかく, "personality,"
or to sex/gender, as in 男性 だんせい, "man; male." Context selects the branch of meaning.

The indexing radical 心 takes the left-hand form 忄.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** pair that narrow heart form with [生](生.md) on the right and think of
a living thing's nature. This is an association for remembering the shape, not an etymology.

## Notes

[忙](忙.md) and [怖](怖.md) share the left radical.
[住](住.md) has a different left side and 主 rather than 生 on the right; that resemblance is only partial.
Do not read 性格 as a statement about someone's gender: its established meaning here is personality.
