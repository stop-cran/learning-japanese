# 布 — cloth; distribute

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

布 concerns cloth and, in other words, spreading or distribution. The native noun 布 ぬの is cloth or fabric, while 配布 はいふ means distribution. 毛布 もうふ means blanket and 財布 さいふ means wallet; their modern objects need not be made from one particular traditional material.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the crossing upper strokes over 巾 and imagine a piece of cloth hanging below them. This is a shape-based memory aid, not a verified origin.

## Notes

- 布衣 ほい is a historical clothing term, not a general recommendation for naming modern clothes. Here kariginu names a type of robe.
- The きれ row is demonstrated inside 布地 きれじ, also spelled 切れ地. 布地 also has the separate reading ぬのじ, fabric or cloth; do not assume that every 布地 is read きれじ. The linked standalone 布 article teaches ぬの.
- The 布く しく form is search-only in the inspected JMdict spelling record. The entry notes this spelling specifically for promulgating, laying railway tracks and deploying troops (senses 3, 5 and 6), not its futon example. 敷く is the entry's primary spelling. 布く remains lookup evidence, not an ordinary spelling recommendation.
- JMdict labels the whole spelling 布団 as ateji, a spelling in which ordinary character meanings need not explain the word. That label alone does not establish the historical role of each individual character.
