# 帝 — emperor; imperial

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

帝 concerns an emperor or imperial rule. 帝国 names an empire; 皇帝 (こうてい) names an emperor.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 巾 hanging beneath the broad middle stroke. In 締 the same shape sits beside thread.

## Notes

[締](締.md) adds thread on the left; [京](京.md) has a similar top but ends differently. [山](山.md) and [安](安.md) mean mountain and inexpensive or peaceful, not emperor. [王](王.md) denotes a king, and [君](君.md) can mean a ruler; those titles are not necessarily imperial.
