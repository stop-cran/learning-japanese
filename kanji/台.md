# 台 — stand, machine counter

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

台 can mean a stand or support. As a counter, it counts machines and vehicles. It also occurs in fixed words whose meanings cannot be guessed from "stand," notably 台風.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the square [口](口.md)-shaped bottom as a stand supporting the angular shape above it. This is not a historical explanation.

## Notes

一台 いちだい means "one machine/vehicle," with the object supplied by context. 台所 mixes on ダイ with native ところ, voiced to どころ.

Compare [古](古.md), whose top is 十, and [名](名.md), whose top is 夕. All three have a square lower part, but their upper strokes differ.
