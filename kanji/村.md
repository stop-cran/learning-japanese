# 村 — village

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

村 concerns a village, either a settlement or an administrative unit. The native noun 村 is read むら; 村長 そんちょう names a village head or mayor.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside 寸 and imagine a small settlement near a tree. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a claim about the character's historical construction.

## Notes

- Calling a place 村 does not establish that every resident farms or that the village lacks modern services.
- The ソン reading is illustrated by 村長; the linked independent noun is むら. Place-name pronunciations must be learned separately.
- Compare [材](材.md), material or timber: the right-hand component changes from 寸 to 才.
