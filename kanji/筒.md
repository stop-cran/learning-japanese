# 筒 — cylinder; tube

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

筒 concerns a cylinder, tube or hollow cylindrical object. It also occurs in 封筒, envelope; the established compound does not require an ordinary flat envelope to be shaped like a pipe.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the bamboo-shaped top above 同 and imagine a hollow cylinder with matching openings. This is a modern-shape association, not a historical explanation or a phonetic analysis.

The indexing key is [竹](竹.md), no. 118, in the top form ⺮.

## Notes

- [管](管.md) and its native noun [管](../words/管.md) くだ also concern a pipe or tube. Do not manufacture an absolute size or material distinction between 管 and 筒.
- In [封筒](../words/封筒.md), [封](封.md) contributes sealing or enclosing. The meaning of the whole word is not obtained by forcing every envelope into the standalone cylinder sense.
- Compare [同](同.md), same, and [答](答.md), answer. The similar lower box does not give 筒 either meaning.
