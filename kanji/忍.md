# 忍 — endure; act in secret

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

忍 means to bear something painful, and also to act in secret or stay out of sight. 忍耐 (にんたい) is endurance or patience, and 忍者 (にんじゃ) is a ninja, a person trained for covert work.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See [刃](刃.md), “blade,” above [心](心.md), “heart.” Picture a heart bearing the press of a blade.

## Notes

[忘](忘.md), [念](念.md) and [志](志.md) also have 心 at the bottom, but mean forget, thought and will. [堪](堪.md) and [凌](凌.md) can also mean endure; [隠](隠.md), [秘](秘.md), [匿](匿.md), [潜](潜.md) and [密](密.md) relate to hiding, secrecy or lurking, close to 忍ぶ in its sense “hide oneself.” 忍び足 (しのびあし) is stealthy steps, and 忍びない (しのびない) means “cannot bear to (do).” The [standard-use table, PDF p. 123](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=123) lists ニン, しのぶ and しのばせる.
