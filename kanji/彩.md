# 彩 — color; coloring

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 彡 (no. 59) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

彩 concerns color and coloring. It appears in 色彩 and 多彩 (たさい), “colorful; varied,” and in the verb 彩る (いろどる), “to color or decorate.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The left part 采 also appears in 採 and 菜; 彩 adds 彡, three slanted strokes, on the right.

## Notes

[採](採.md) and [菜](菜.md) also contain 采, but mean pick and vegetables. [彫](彫.md) also has 彡 on the right, but means carve. [色](色.md) is the general character for color, [絵](絵.md) a picture, [染](染.md) dye and [塗](塗.md) paint.
