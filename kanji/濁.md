# 濁 — muddy; cloudy; impure; voiced

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

濁 is losing clarity: water that turns muddy or cloudy, a heart or a world that becomes impure, and a consonant that becomes voiced. The linked [濁る](../words/濁る.md) にごる has four JMdict senses: "to become muddy; to become cloudy; to become turbid; to become impure (of a liquid or gas)"; "to become dull (of a sound, color, etc.); to become indistinct; to become fuzzy; to become hoarse"; "to become impure (of one's heart, a society, etc.); to be corrupted; to be polluted"; and "to become voiced (of a consonant); to be pronounced as a voiced sound."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ダク. One example of each:

- muddy water: 濁流 だくりゅう, "muddy stream";
- defiled conduct: 濁世 だくせ, "this corrupt or degenerate world; this world or life; the world of mankind," a Buddhism term in JMdict;
- a voiced sound: 濁音 だくおん, "syllable with a voiced consonant (in Japanese); voiced sound."

More examples are in the Notes. For ジョク, Daijisen gives the same defiled sense, with 濁世 じょくせ and 五濁 ごじょく, "five defilements; five corruptions; five impurities," also a Buddhism term.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part ends in a [虫](虫.md)-like shape at the bottom. Imagine a ditch with something crawling in the water until it turns cloudy. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ダク | on | 濁流 だくりゅう — muddy stream; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ジョク | on | 五濁 ごじょく — five defilements; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| にご.る | kun | [濁る](../words/濁る.md) にごる — become cloudy; become impure |
| にご.す | kun | 濁す にごす — to make muddy; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 濁る, 濁 writes にご and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 濁り にごり is "muddiness; murkiness; cloudiness," and 濁す にごす is "to make muddy (of a liquid); to make cloudy; to make turbid; to roil," with a second sense, "to make ambiguous; to evade (e.g. the point); to be noncommittal about." In 濁流, 濁音 and 濁点 だくてん, "dakuten; diacritic used with kana that turns an unvoiced consonant into a voiced consonant," 濁 supplies ダク. In 濁世 and 五濁 it supplies ジョク; JMdict lists 濁世 with the readings だくせ, だくせい and じょくせ, and 五濁 only as ごじょく. In 清濁 せいだく, "good and evil; purity and impurity," with a second sense "voiced and unvoiced consonants," 濁 supplies ダク.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ダク with 濁流, 濁音 and 清濁, にごる with 濁る and 濁り, and にごす with 濁す. Practice selects にご.る, the reading of the linked word article. ダク, ジョク and にご.す are shown with dictionary examples and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. The table gives no ジョク example, though KANJIDIC2 and Kanjipedia list it.

## Notes

- 濁る is 濁 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji. In the examples above, [流](流.md), "flow," supplies リュウ in 濁流, [世](世.md), "world, generation," supplies セ in 濁世, [音](音.md), "sound," supplies オン in 濁音, [五](五.md), "five," supplies ゴ in 五濁, [点](点.md), "point; dot," supplies テン in 濁点 and [清](清.md), "pure, clean," supplies セイ in 清濁. 汚濁 おだく, "pollution; contamination; corruption; graft," is read おだく in JMdict, and [汚](汚.md), "dirty; defile," supplies お there.
- Two Daijisen hard-to-read words are whole-word readings and are never split: 濁酒 どぶろく, "doburoku (unrefined sake)," and 濁声 だみごえ, "thick voice; husky voice; guttural voice; hoarse voice; gravelly voice." JMdict also lists 濁酒 as だくしゅ for the same sake, and 濁り酒 にごりざけ, "unrefined sake; unfiltered sake; cloudy sake," is a different entry.
- [触](触.md), "touch; contact," has a 虫-like shape at the right, and [渡](渡.md), "cross over," and [湯](湯.md), "hot water," have 氵 at the left like 濁. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [汚](汚.md), "dirty; defile," [混](混.md), "mix, mingle," [泥](泥.md), "mud; mire," [曇](曇.md), "cloud over; cloudy weather," [雲](雲.md), "cloud," [悪](悪.md), "bad, evil," [鈍](鈍.md), "dull; blunt," [音](音.md), "sound," and [声](声.md), "voice," overlap the senses of 濁 and its words: dirty or impure water, mud, cloudiness, a corrupt world, a dull sound and a voiced sound. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "muddy," "cloudy" or "impure" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, 混濁 こんだく is "becoming turbid; becoming muddy; becoming cloudy; becoming opaque," and 濁水 だくすい is "muddy water" in JMdict; Daijisen also lists 濁酒 and 濁声 among its hard-to-read words.
