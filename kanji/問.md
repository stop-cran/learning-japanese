# 問 — ask, question

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

問 concerns asking, questions, and matters to be examined. 質問 is a question asked of someone; 問題 can be a test question or a problem.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a [口](口.md), "mouth," asking a question at a gate, 門. This is a mnemonic, not a historical or phonetic claim.

## Notes

Inspect the inside: 問 has 口, [間](間.md) has 日, and [聞](聞.md) has 耳.

In everyday requests, [聞く](../words/聞く.md) きく and 質問する しつもんする are often more useful than the comparatively formal 問う.
