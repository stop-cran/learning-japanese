# 電 — electricity

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 雨 (no. 173) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

電 combines [雨](雨.md) (rain, weather from the sky) with 申, which in old forms is commonly explained as a bolt of lightning.
So the original meaning is "lightning", and it was later extended to "electricity" when the word was needed for the new science.
The details of the early forms of 申 are debated, so treat the picture of lightning in a rain sky as the usual explanation, not a certainty.

Everyday lightning is now 雷 かみなり or 稲妻 いなずま.
電 itself now mostly means electricity, and by extension electric devices and the electric train.

## Notes

Do not confuse 電 with 雷 (かみなり, thunder; 雨 over 田) or with [雨](雨.md) itself: 電 has the extra tail stroke at the bottom (the 申 part with its hook).
Some words are shortened to one kanji: 終電 しゅうでん (last train).
