# 充 — allot; fill

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (no. 10) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

充 concerns filling, making sufficient, or allotting something to a purpose.
In [拡充](../words/拡充.md) かくじゅう, it contributes making provision more
substantial; [充てる](../words/充てる.md) あてる means allocating a resource to a use.

The modern shape has an upper 亠 and 厶-like middle above the two leg-like
strokes 儿, the indexing radical.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture someone standing beneath a sign showing
where to allot supplies. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical
derivation or a phonetic analysis.

| み.たす | kun | source-only for this quiz: 充たす みたす — fill; satisfy; rare spelling of usual 満たす |

All three sourced readings remain in order. Reading questions select ジュウ
and あ.てる, not み.たす. The rarity concerns the spelling 充たす, not the
pronunciation みたす. For example, 条件を充たす (じょうけんをみたす) means
"satisfy the conditions"; normally write 条件を満たす.

## Common words

- [拡充](../words/拡充.md) かくじゅう — expansion; strengthening, as of facilities or services
- [充てる](../words/充てる.md) あてる — allocate time, money, or another resource to a purpose

## Notes

- [允](允.md) has 厶 directly above 儿; 充 has an additional upper 亠 shape.
- [元](元.md) has two horizontal lines above its legs, [兄](兄.md) a square 口,
  and [先](先.md) a different crossed upper shape. Compare the whole outline.
- [当てる](../words/当てる.md) あてる covers hitting, holding against, and guessing
  correctly. Use 充てる for the resource-allocation sense taught here, not as
  a replacement spelling for those contact or guessing uses. The broader
  あてる family also has allotting uses; this is not an absolute division
  of every sense between the spellings.
