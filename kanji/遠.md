# 遠 — far

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

遠 describes distance. [遠い](../words/遠い.md) とおい is the ordinary adjective for a place that is a long way away.
遠足 えんそく is a school trip or excursion; do not translate that established word merely as "far feet."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** follow 辶 past the central 口 toward the spreading lower strokes of 袁, as if the route continues far away.
This is a memory aid rather than etymology.
