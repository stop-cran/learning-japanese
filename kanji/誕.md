# 誕 — birth; be born

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

誕 appears in words about being born. [誕生](../words/誕生.md) たんじょう is
the birth of a person and, more widely, the coming into existence of
something new, such as a new city. [誕生日](../words/誕生日.md)
たんじょうび is a birthday. 生誕 せいたん is the birth of a famous person,
and 降誕 こうたん is the birth of a saint or monarch.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: telling great lies or
nonsense, in compounds such as 虚誕, 荒誕 and 妄誕 that lie outside this
card's practical vocabulary, and a child being born, as in 誕生, 降誕 and
生誕. KANJIDIC2 glosses 誕 as nativity, be born, declension, lie and be
arbitrary; its "lie" is a falsehood, not lying down.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [言](言.md) at the left beside
[延](延.md). Imagine words of announcement carried on from person to person
to say that a baby has arrived. This is a memory picture for the modern
shape, not a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| タン | on | [誕生](../words/誕生.md) たんじょう - birth; coming into existence; [誕生日](../words/誕生日.md) たんじょうび - birthday |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects タン. In 誕生日, 日 is
read び, a voiced form of ひ, after 誕生 たんじょう. The official Joyo table
lists タン, with 誕生 and 生誕 as examples.

## Notes

- [延](延.md), "prolong; postpone," is the right-hand component of 誕.
  [談](談.md), "converse; consult," and [誌](誌.md), "written record;
  journal," share 言 at the left but differ in meaning.
- Being born overlaps [生](生.md), "life, birth," and [産](産.md),
  "produce; give birth."
- The coming-into-existence sense of 誕生, which JMdict glosses as
  "creation; formation; birth; origin; making," overlaps [創](創.md),
  "originate; create," [作](作.md), "make, produce," [元](元.md), "origin,
  former," [本](本.md), "book, origin," and [原](原.md), "original; plain."
- The "lie" in KANJIDIC2's glosses is a falsehood, but the English word is
  spelled like the "lie" of [寝](寝.md), "lie down; sleep," which is
  excluded for that reason. These direct meaning comparisons are excluded
  from choices, not treated as identical in every compound.
