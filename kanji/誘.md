# 誘 — invite; tempt

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

誘 is about drawing someone toward something, either by inviting them or by tempting them. KANJIDIC2 lists “entice”, “lead”, “tempt”, “invite”, “ask”, “call for”, “seduce” and “allure”. In [誘う](../words/誘う.md) さそう, JMdict's first sense is “to invite; to ask (someone to do); to call (for); to take (someone) along”, its second is “to tempt; to lure; to entice; to seduce”, and its third is “to induce (tears, laughter, sleepiness, etc.); to arouse (e.g. sympathy); to provoke”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [言](言.md), "say; words," with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it, stands on the left. On the right is [秀](秀.md), "excel; outstanding," built from the shape 禾 above [乃](乃.md), "thereupon; possessive no." Picture sweet words from the mouth on the left inviting you toward something outstanding on the right. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 秀 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ユウ and さそう only. Kanjipedia lists いざなう and おびく outside the Joyo table; おびく is not a KANJIDIC2 reading.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 誘.

These fourteen cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [招](招.md) (“invite”) and [請](請.md) (“request; beg; undertake”): KANJIDIC2 lists “invite” for 誘 and also gives 請 “solicit”, “invite” and “ask”; JMdict's first gloss of 誘う is “to invite”.
- [惑](惑.md) (“bewilder; perplex; delude”): JMdict 誘う sense 2 is “to tempt; to lure; to entice; to seduce”, KANJIDIC2 gives 惑 “beguile”, and JMdict glosses 誘惑 ゆうわく “temptation; allurement; lure; enticement; seduction” (entry 1542040).
- [導](導.md) (“guide; conduct”) and [率](率.md) (“rate; lead; frank”): KANJIDIC2 lists “lead” for both 誘 and 率, and Kanjipedia's first sense of 誘 includes みちびく (to guide).
- [連](連.md) (“link; take along”): JMdict 誘う sense 1 includes “to take (someone) along”.
- [呼](呼.md) (“call out”) and [訪](訪.md) (“visit; call on”): JMdict 誘う includes “to call (for)”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “call for” for 誘; both titles also use “call”.
- [問](問.md) (“ask, question”), [聞](聞.md) (“hear, ask”) and [伺](伺.md) (“ask humbly; visit humbly”): JMdict 誘う includes “to ask (someone to do)”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “ask” for 誘.
- [促](促.md) (“urge; prompt”): JMdict 誘う sense 3 includes “to provoke”, and KANJIDIC2 gives 促 “stimulate”, “urge” and “incite”.
- [勧](勧.md) (“recommend; encourage”): JMdict glosses 勧誘 かんゆう “invitation; solicitation; canvassing; canvasing; inducement; persuasion; encouragement” (entry 1211120), and KANJIDIC2 gives 勧 “persuade” and “encourage”.
- [艶](艶.md) (“gloss; luster; allure”): KANJIDIC2 lists “allure” for 誘.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 攻 (“attack; go on the offensive”), 舞 (“dance”), and 握 (“grip; grasp”); none means to invite or tempt.
