# 妄 — unfounded; reckless

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

妄 points to what is unfounded or reckless. 妄想 is a wild fancy or delusion; 妄言 (もうげん or ぼうげん) is a rash or unfounded remark.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look under 亡: 妄 has 女; [忘](忘.md) has 心. [忙](忙.md) puts its heart element beside 亡 instead.

## Notes

A thought can be a plausible 想像 (そうぞう, an imagining). 妄想 adds the idea that it is a fantasy or without a sound basis.

[忘](忘.md) ends in 心, [忙](忙.md) begins with the side form of 心, and [妨](妨.md) puts 女 on the left rather than underneath. Compare [惑](惑.md) (confuse or delude): 妄 in 妄想 characterizes the thought as unfounded.
