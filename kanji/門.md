# 門 — gate; doorway

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 門 (no. 169) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

門 concerns a gate or entrance. Complete words extend the image to entering a school or field of learning, as in 入門.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the two sides of 門 as the leaves of a gate. This is a modern-shape memory aid; no historical structural classification is inferred.

The complete character is also the canonical radical 門, no. 169.

## Notes

- The linked 門 article teaches もん. Separate source entries support かど and と; their senses must not be assumed identical.
- In the inspected 門 と record, the unmarked applicable sense is shutter or window shutter; the entrance and narrows senses are archaic. Do not infer that 門 can replace 戸 in every ordinary と word.
- [間](間.md), [問](問.md) and [聞](聞.md) add inner components. The complete characters mean interval, asking and hearing rather than being interchangeable gate labels.
