# 椎 — vertebra; chinquapin

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

In anatomy, 椎 concerns the **vertebrae**, the bones making up the backbone: [椎骨](../words/椎骨.md) ついこつ means a vertebra. In plant names, 椎 (しい) names chinquapin trees. These are different uses of the same character, not interchangeable meanings in every compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [木](木.md) on the left and the bird-shaped component called ふるとり on the right. Imagine a bird beside a tree. This is a visual memory aid, not a verified historical explanation or a claim that the anatomical meaning comes from a bird.

## Common words

- [椎骨](../words/椎骨.md) ついこつ - vertebra
- 脊椎 せきつい - spine or vertebral column; the dictionary also records a vertebra sense
- 椎間板 ついかんばん - intervertebral disc, between vertebrae
- 椎茸 しいたけ - shiitake mushroom; usually written in kana according to JMdict

## Notes

- This card asks only ツイ. The source-only スイ and う.つ, and the rare 椎 spelling of つち, remain lookup information rather than reading questions. The noun つち itself is not labelled rare merely because this spelling is.
- [骨](骨.md) is the broader bone/skeleton character. A vertebra is a bone, so its broad title would not be an unambiguous wrong answer for 椎.
- [集](集.md), gather, puts that bird-shaped component above 木 rather than beside it. [難](難.md), difficult, has a denser left block beside the same component. [稲](稲.md) names the rice plant, not a chinquapin, and has 禾 rather than 木 on the left.
- 椎 (しい) is a tree name, not the anatomical pronunciation. Do not read 椎骨 as しいこつ or treat 椎茸 as an anatomical term.
