# 秒 — second as a time unit

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

秒 names a second as a unit of time: sixty seconds make one minute. This is not English second meaning the item after the first.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 禾 on the left and 少 on the right; imagine measuring a small amount of time beside a grain plant. The association with a small interval is a mnemonic, not an etymology.

The key 禾, no. 115, is on the left.

## Notes

- Useful expressions are 一秒 いちびょう, one second, and 三十秒 さんじゅうびょう, thirty seconds. Preserve びょう; do not turn the unit into an ordinal number.
- The dictionary also records an angular second. A measurement context distinguishes it from ordinary timekeeping; that sense does not make 秒 mean second place.
- Compare [秋](秋.md), autumn, and [科](科.md), department or course. Sharing 禾 does not give them the same meaning.
