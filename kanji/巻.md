# 巻 — roll; book volume

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 己 (no. 49) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

巻 concerns rolling or winding and also a volume of a book. 巻く まく means to wind, coil or roll something; 一巻 いっかん can mean one volume or the first volume. The reading and complete expression distinguish the action from a book volume.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the broad upper strokes over the curled lower component 己; imagine paper being gathered into a roll. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- 巻 and 巻き are documented spellings of the noun まき. The two source rows do not imply two different pronunciations.
- For a usable volume expression, learn 一巻 いっかん, with a small っ. 席巻 せっけん uses ケン, not カン.
- Compare [券](券.md), ticket or coupon: its lower shape is 刀 rather than 己.
