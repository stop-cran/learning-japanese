# 叫 — shout; exclaim

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

叫 expresses shouting or crying out. 叫ぶ さけぶ can describe a loud call or an insistent appeal; it does not necessarily describe tears or crying sadly.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Use the left-hand 口 as a mouth sending a shout toward the upright, hooked strokes on the right.

## Notes

- 叫ぶ is a godan verb ending in ぶ: 叫びます さけびます and 叫んだ さけんだ. The latter is past tense, not a different dictionary reading.
- Do not confuse 叫ぶ, crying out, with 泣く なく, crying or shedding tears. A person may do both, but the verbs do not assert the same action.
