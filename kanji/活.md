# 活 — lively

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

活 concerns living and being active. In [生活](../words/生活.md) せいかつ it is part of the word
for life or living; in 活動 (かつどう) it concerns activity.
"Lively" is a compact meaning cue, not a translation to insert into every compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture water refreshing the tongue-shaped 舌 on the right,
ready for a lively conversation. This image does not establish the character's history.

## Notes

日本で生活しています。 (にほんでせいかつしています。) — I live in Japan.
生活する concerns living one's life, not simply being biologically alive.
Compare [話](話.md): both characters have 舌 on the right, but 活 has 氵 and 話 has 言.
