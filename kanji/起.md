# 起 — wake; rise

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 走 (no. 156) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

起 concerns getting up, waking, or bringing something into action. In event words it can also concern something happening:
[起きる](../words/起きる.md) おきる can mean either getting up or an incident occurring.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the [走](走.md) shape getting moving beside the curled 己 shape,
as if someone is uncurling and getting out of bed. This is a visual mnemonic, not a historical explanation.

- 起立 きりつ — standing up

## Notes

起きる describes the person who wakes; 起こす takes the person being awakened as its object.
For standing up from a chair, also learn [立つ](../words/立つ.md) たつ.
Do not confuse 起 with the simpler [走](走.md) or [足](足.md).
