# 朝 — morning

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 月 (no. 74) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

朝 means morning in everyday time vocabulary. In historical words it can instead refer to a dynasty or court.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice [日](日.md) between two [十](十.md)-shaped parts on the left and [月](月.md) on the right. Imagine the sun appearing while the moon is still visible in the morning. This is a mnemonic, not a historical derivation.

## Common words

- [朝](../words/朝.md) あさ — morning
- 朝ご飯 あさごはん — breakfast
- 朝食 ちょうしょく — breakfast
- 毎朝 まいあさ — every morning

## Notes

[午前](../words/午前.md) ごぜん marks the period before noon and the a.m. part of clock times. 朝 is an everyday part of the day, not a strict label for every hour after midnight.
[明](明.md) has just 日 on its left; 朝 has additional strokes above and below 日. [服](服.md) puts 月 on the opposite side.
