# 仏 — Buddha; Buddhism

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

仏 concerns the Buddha and Buddhism. 仏 ほとけ can also refer to a deceased person in a religious or euphemistic context. The character is additionally an abbreviation for France, read フツ; that use must be learned separately from the Buddhist meaning.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a person beside a small enclosed meditation space, using the left-hand 亻 and right-hand ム as a visual cue.

The canonical radical 人 has the left-hand form 亻 here.

## Notes

- 仏教 is pronounced ぶっきょう, with a small っ: the compound does not retain a fully pronounced ぶつ before きょう.
- 仏語 ふつご means the French language. The same spelling read ぶつご is a different word meaning a Buddhist term or the Buddha's teachings; identify the word by its reading and context.
