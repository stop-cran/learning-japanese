# 逃 — escape; flee

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

逃 concerns getting away from pursuit, danger, or confinement.
[逃げる](../words/逃げる.md) にげる describes the escaping person's action;
逃がす にがす describes letting someone or something get away.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the strokes of the inner 兆 scattering toward an escape route along 辶.
This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

Compare 鳥が逃げる (とりがにげる), "a bird escapes," with 鳥を逃がす (とりをにがす), "let a bird go."
The radical 辵 appears as 辶. Identify 兆 within it rather than the inner parts of [近](近.md), [週](週.md), or [迎](迎.md).
