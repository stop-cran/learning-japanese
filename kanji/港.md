# 港 — harbor

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

港 names a harbor or port. The standalone noun [港](../words/港.md) is みなと.
In 空港 (くうこう), "airport," the character contributes a port or arrival-and-departure point,
not a claim that aircraft need a seaside harbor.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the curled lower-right shape as a sheltered docking space,
with water at the left. This uses the modern outline, not historical derivation.

## Notes

船が港に着きました。 (ふねがみなとにつきました。) — The ship arrived at the port.
港 is a place associated with vessels arriving and departing, not a synonym for [海](海.md), the sea.
Compare [満](満.md): its lower-right area is more enclosed, rather than the curled shape of 港.
