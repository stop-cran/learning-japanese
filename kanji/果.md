# 果 — fruit; fruition

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

果 connects fruit with completion or an achieved outcome. [果物](../words/果物.md) (くだもの) is edible fruit;
結果 (けっか) is a result, while 果たす (はたす) means to fulfil or carry something out.
English "fruition" is a useful meaning bridge, not evidence about Japanese word history.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the upper divided square as a fruit resting on the lower 木-like branches.
This describes the modern shape only.

## Notes

約束を果たす (やくそくをはたす) means "fulfil a promise."
Compare [東](東.md): its central vertical continues above the enclosure, unlike the top of 果.
Do not replace every occurrence of 果 with the English noun "fruit."
