# 霧 — fog; mist

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 雨 (no. 173) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

霧 is fog or mist. KANJIDIC2 lists “fog” and “mist”, and Kanjipedia gives きり。また、きりのようなもの。 (fog; also, something like fog). On its own, [霧](../words/霧.md) きり is glossed in JMdict as “fog; mist” and also “spray” (entry 1531110). Digital Daijisen's senses for ム are きり (fog), きりのように集まり、または消えるさま。 (gathering or vanishing like fog) and きりのように散らばる水滴。 (water drops scattered like fog), with 噴霧器 ふんむき, “sprayer; vaporizer; vaporiser; atomizer; atomiser; nebulizer” (JMdict 1504620), as its example of the last.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [雨](雨.md), "rain," sits over [務](務.md), "duties." Imagine rain so fine that it hangs in the air instead of falling, softly wrapping someone who goes about the day's duties: a fog. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 雨 or 務 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ム, with 霧笛 むてき (“foghorn”, JMdict 1531130), 濃霧 and 噴霧器, and きり. Kanjipedia lists ブ outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen gives ム as its only on reading. ボウ is not listed by the Joyo table, Kanjipedia or Digital Daijisen.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 霧, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

This card is excluded from meaning choices:

- [雰](雰.md) (“mist; atmosphere”): KANJIDIC2 lists “fog” for both 霧 and 雰, and the title's “mist” matches this card's “mist.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 氷 (“ice; freeze”), 嵐 (“storm; tempest”), and 星 (“star; celestial point”); none means fog or mist.
