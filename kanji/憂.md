# 憂 — worry; grief

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

憂 is worry and grief: a heavy heart over what may happen or what has happened. It appears in 憂慮, in 憂鬱 (ゆううつ), “depression; melancholy,” and in 一喜一憂 (いっきいちゆう), “swinging between joy and sorrow.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Put 亻, the narrow form of 人 “person,” before 憂 and you get [優](優.md), “gentle; superior,” which is also read ユウ. In the middle of 憂 is [心](心.md), “heart,” and 夂 is at the bottom, as in [夏](夏.md), “summer.”

## Notes

[優](優.md) contains 憂 and is also read ユウ, but means gentle or superior; [夏](夏.md) also ends in 夂, and [愛](愛.md) also has 心 above 夂; they mean summer and love. [愁](愁.md), [悩](悩.md), [哀](哀.md), [悲](悲.md), [嘆](嘆.md), [悼](悼.md) and [慨](慨.md) relate to sorrow, worry, lament or mourning; [慮](慮.md) is thinking something over, and [患](患.md) is to suffer or be ill. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 151](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=151) lists ユウ, with 憂愁 (ゆうしゅう), “melancholy,” 憂慮 and 一喜一憂; うれえる, with 憂える and 憂え; うれい, with 憂い; and うい, with 憂い, 憂き目 and 物憂い. It notes that 憂き is the classical attributive form, and cross-references うれえる and うれい with 愁.
