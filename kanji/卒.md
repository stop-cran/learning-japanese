# 卒 — graduate; finish

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 十 (no. 24) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

卒 is a useful anchor for completing a course of study in 卒業 そつぎょう, graduation. Other source-listed uses concern soldiers or death; those do not make every 卒 word an education word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the roof-like top above two person-like strokes and a 十-shaped base, as though students have reached the end of their course together.

The classical indexing radical is 十, visible at the bottom.

## Notes

- 卒去 しゅっきょ concerns the death of a noble or a person in a comparable position. 卒する そっする is marked formal or literary in JMdict; neither is offered as the general everyday way to say someone died.
- Kanjipedia explicitly lists 卒わる おわる. JMdict instead includes the rare spelling 卒る おわる, without written わ. The row uses the independently verified spelling that matches the source's お.わる boundary; ordinary [終わる](../words/終わる.md) おわる is the useful everyday comparison.
- JMdict flags 卒える おえる as a search-only kanji form, even though a sense note mentions occasional use for graduating. An indexed form is not a recommended everyday spelling; the お.える row remains lookup information.
- The source-only ついに and にわか readings correspond to finally and sudden in character-dictionary explanations. They do not make 卒 a normal everyday spelling for those words.
