# 塚 — mound; burial mound

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

塚 is a mound, including a mound over a grave. The shape alone does not establish its purpose.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 土 at the left. Imagine a low heap of earth.

## Notes

Compare [墳](墳.md), used for a burial mound. A 貝塚 (かいづか) can be a shell heap rather than a tomb.

[家](家.md) (house) has a roof above its lower strokes. Look instead for 土 on the left of 塚; [坊](坊.md) and [塀](塀.md) share that left side but have different right sides. Compare [丘](丘.md) (hill) and [墓](墓.md) (grave): a 塚 need not be either a natural hill or a burial place.
