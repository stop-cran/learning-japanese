# 操 — operate; manipulate; fidelity

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

操 is handling something skillfully with the hands, such as a machine, a vehicle or a puppet, and, by extension, controlling people from behind; it also names firm, unchanging loyalty. [操作](../words/操作.md) そうさ is operation or manipulation; JMdict glosses it "operation; management; handling," then "manipulating (to one's benefit); manipulation; influencing." [体操](../words/体操.md) たいそう is gymnastics or physical exercises.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ソウ. One example of each:

- handling something skillfully with the hands: 操縦 そうじゅう, "steering; piloting; flying; control; operation; handling";
- moving and training the body: 操練 そうれん, "drill (esp. military); training";
- holding firmly to one's resolve, a will that does not change lightly: 節操 せっそう, "integrity; fidelity; constancy; principle; faithfulness; honor."

Daijisen also has a separate entry そう【操】, another word for みさお, and an entry みさお【操】 with four senses: holding to one's will against temptation, a woman's chastity, and two older senses, elegance and constancy. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the left, written 扌, and at the right [品](品.md), "goods, refinement," on top of [木](木.md), "tree, wood." Imagine a hand working the strings of a wooden puppet whose three little boxes of goods bob above its head. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 扌, 品 or 木 contributes meaning or sound. [燥](燥.md), "parch; dry up," has the same right part and is read ソウ, a plain fact about readings. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソウ | on | [体操](../words/体操.md) たいそう — gymnastics; physical exercises; [操作](../words/操作.md) そうさ — operation; manipulation |
| サン | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; no JMdict entry writes 操 with this reading, and Daijisen gives only ソウ as the on reading |
| みさお | kun | 操 みさお - fidelity; honour; honor; constancy; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| あやつ.る | kun | 操る あやつる - to operate (e.g. a machine); to handle; to manage; to control; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 体操, 体 supplies タイ and 操 supplies ソウ; in 操作, 操 supplies ソウ and 作 supplies サ, with no sound change in either word. 操 read みさお stands alone with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict also gives the reading そう for this noun, and its second sense is "chastity (of a woman); faithfulness (e.g. to one's husband)." In 操る, 操 writes あやつ and る is okurigana. JMdict's later senses of 操る are "to have a good command of (a language)," "to work (a puppet); to pull the strings of a puppet" and "to manipulate (a person, public opinion, etc.); to pull the strings." In 操り人形 あやつりにんぎょう, "puppet; marionette," 操 writes あやつ with り after it and 人形 supplies にんぎょう; JMdict lists あやつり人形 and 操人形 as search-only kanji forms.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ソウ, with 操縦, 操作 and 節操 as examples; みさお, with 操; and あやつる, with 操る and 操り人形. サン is not in the table. Practice selects ソウ, the reading of the linked word articles. No word article uses みさお or あやつ.る yet, and サン has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [体](体.md), "body, form," in 体操 and [作](作.md), "make, produce," in 操作.
- [品](品.md), "goods, refinement," is the top of the right part of 操, and [木](木.md), "tree, wood," is its bottom. [拝](拝.md), "worship; bow in respect," also has 扌 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [扱](扱.md), "handle; deal with," [営](営.md), "conduct business; manage," [善](善.md), "good; virtue; handle well," [節](節.md), "joint; season; restraint," and [徳](徳.md), "virtue; benevolence," overlap the senses of 操 and its words: handling and managing in 操作 and 操る, running a factory or a machine in 操業, fidelity and principle in 節操, and virtue in 貞操 and 徳操. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "handle" or "fidelity" as exclusive to any one character.
- [揮](揮.md), "wield; command; display," and [掌](掌.md), "palm (of the hand); administer," overlap handling and control: JMdict glosses 操る "to operate (e.g. a machine); to handle; to manage; to control", 指揮 "command; direction; supervision", and 掌る, a rarely used kanji form of 司る, "to be in charge of; to manage; to direct; to administer". The three are kept out of one another's quiz options.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first ソウ sense, handling skillfully: 操業 そうぎょう, "operation (of a machine, factory, fishing boat, etc.); work," 操車 そうしゃ, "operation (of trains)," 操船 そうせん, "steering a ship; controlling; maneuvering; piloting," and 操舵 そうだ, "steering (of a boat); steerage." An example of the second sense is 体操.
- More examples of the third ソウ sense, firm resolve: 操守 そうしゅ, "constancy; fidelity," 志操 しそう, "principle," 情操 じょうそう, "sensibility (artistic, moral); (good) taste; sentiment," 貞操 ていそう, "chastity; virtue; fidelity," and 徳操 とくそう, "strong, immovable sense of morality; high moral character; chastity; virtue."
