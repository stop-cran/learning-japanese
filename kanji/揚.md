# 揚 — hoist; lift; deep-fry

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

揚 is raising something high, such as a flag or a kite, and, in the kitchen, deep-frying. [揚げる](../words/揚げる.md) あげる means to deep-fry or to hoist, and [鷹揚](../words/鷹揚.md) おうよう means magnanimous or composed. JMdict's entry for 揚げる lists "to deep-fry; to make deep-fried food" first and "to launch (fireworks, etc.); to hoist (e.g. a flag); to (let) fly (e.g. a kite)" second.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ヨウ. The first is rising or raising high, as in 揚力 ようりょく, "dynamic lift; lifting power," 掲揚 けいよう, "hoisting (e.g. a flag); raising; flying; putting up," 高揚 こうよう, "elevation (of spirits); raising (of morale); uplift; upsurge," 浮揚 ふよう, "floating (e.g. in air, water); floatation; buoyancy," 抑揚 よくよう, "intonation; accent; modulation; inflection," and 鷹揚. The second is declaring loudly or making widely known, as in 揚言 ようげん, "opening declaration; profession; declaration in public; proclamation," and 宣揚 せんよう, "enhancement (e.g. of national prestige); raising; heightening; promotion." The third is high spirits, as in 意気揚揚 いきようよう, "triumphant; exultant; in high and proud spirits." The fourth is praising, as in 称揚 しょうよう, "praise; admiration; exalting; extolling," also written 賞揚.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the left, written 扌, and at the right a shape with [日](日.md), "sun, day," above a horizontal line and 勿 below it. Imagine a hand hoisting the sun up over the horizon. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that these parts contribute meaning or sound, although [陽](陽.md), "sunshine," which has the same right-hand shape, is also read ヨウ. Historical formation is not established here.

In 鷹揚, 鷹 supplies オウ and 揚 supplies ヨウ. In 揚げる, 揚 writes あ and げる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 揚がる has the same あ with がる. In 荷揚げ, 荷 supplies に, and 揚 writes あ with げ after it. The leading dash in -あ.げ marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ヨウ, with 意気揚々, 抑揚 and 掲揚 as examples; あげる, with 揚げる and 荷揚げ; and あがる, with 揚がる. It also points from 揚げる to 上げる and 挙げる, and from 揚がる to 上がる and 挙がる, words with the same sounds written with other kanji. Practice selects ヨウ and あ.げる, the readings with linked word articles. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so あ.げる supplies the one question for that stem, shown as あげる; -あ.げ and あ.がる are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [鷹](鷹.md), "hawk; falcon," in 鷹揚. 揚げる is 揚 with okurigana only.
- [場](場.md), "place, site," [湯](湯.md), "hot water," and [陽](陽.md), "sunshine," have the same right-hand shape as 揚 with a different part at the left, and 陽 is also read ヨウ. All are different kanji.
- [上](上.md), "up, above," [掲](掲.md), "put up; display; publish," [昇](昇.md), "rise; ascend," [起](起.md), "wake; rise," [飛](飛.md), "fly," and [賛](賛.md), "approve; praise," overlap the senses "raise," "hoist," "rise," "fly" and "praise" that 揚 and its words carry; 上 also writes あげる and あがる, 掲 appears in 掲揚, 揚げる can mean letting a kite fly, and 称揚 means praise. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "raise" or "rise" as exclusive to any one character.
