# 央 — center; middle

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

央 expresses the center or middle. 中央 ちゅうおう names the center or a central part; 中 and 央 both contribute to that central meaning.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the large 大-shaped strokes reaching through the middle of the upper box-like outline.

KANJIDIC2 indexes 央 under 大 (no. 37), visible in the central and lower strokes. Do not replace it with 口 merely because there is a box-like shape.

## Notes

- This source inventory records オウ and no kun-reading. The reading of the whole word 中央 is not a kun-reading of 央.
- Compare [中](中.md), middle or inside. Their meanings overlap, but 中央 is read ちゅうおう, not by repeating 中's reading.
- Keep 央 distinct from [英](英.md), whose title includes English or outstanding, and [夫](夫.md), husband.
