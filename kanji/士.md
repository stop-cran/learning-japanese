# 士 — warrior; professional

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 士 (no. 33) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

士 occurs in words for warriors and people with a particular role or expertise. [武士](../words/武士.md)
ぶし means a samurai or warrior; 弁護士 べんごし is a lawyer. The ending alone does not establish
the qualifications or prestige of every occupation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a broad-shouldered figure with a shorter base.
In 士 the upper horizontal is longer than the lower one.

## Notes

In [土](土.md), the **lower** horizontal is longer. [工](工.md) has its vertical between the two
horizontals, without the upward projection; [上](上.md) has an asymmetric short upper stroke.

For 士 compared with [師](師.md), [者](者.md), and other person-denoting endings, see
[occupation and role endings](../articles/occupation-and-role-endings.md). Do not reduce 師 versus 士
to licensed versus unlicensed work.
