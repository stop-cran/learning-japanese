# 党 — political party; faction

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (no. 10) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

党 concerns a party, faction, or group aligned together. 政党 せいとう is a political party. 与党 よとう and 野党 やとう distinguish a party in power from an opposition party.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a group gathered beneath the shared roof-like top, with 口 in the middle and two spreading strokes below.

The classical indexing radical is 儿, the spreading lower pair of strokes. The central 口-shaped component is not the indexing radical of this character.
