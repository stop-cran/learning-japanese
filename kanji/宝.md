# 宝 — treasure; valuables

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宝 たから means treasure. 宝石 ほうせき is a gem or precious stone: 宝 contributes the precious or treasure association, while 石 supplies stone. Treasure need not be a gemstone.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a valuable 玉-shaped object kept under 宀, a roof. This modern-shape scene is a mnemonic, not a verified account of the character's earlier forms.
