# 護 — safeguard; protect

**Strokes:** 20 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

護 means guarding and protecting someone or something. KANJIDIC2 gives “safeguard” and “protect,” and Kanjipedia's first sense is to protect, to shelter and to help (まもる。まもり。かばう。たすける。). The example word [看護婦](../words/看護婦.md) かんごふ is labelled in JMdict as an obsolete term: it is glossed “(female) nurse,” with the note “now deprecated” and a cross-reference to 看護師 かんごし, “(hospital) nurse; registered nurse; RN” (JMdict 1928100). So the word to use for a nurse today is 看護師. A Joyo example word is 保護 ほご, “protection; safeguard; guardianship; custody; patronage.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [言](言.md), "say; words," stands at the left, with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it. At the right is the shape 蒦: the grass crown 艹, also at the top of [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," over [隻](隻.md), "ship counter; one of a pair." 隻 is the bird shape 隹 above the hand shape 又. Picture someone calling out words of warning to protect a bird held in a hand among the grass. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 蒦 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ゴ only, with 護衛 ごえい, 救護 きゅうご and 保護 as examples. KANJIDIC2 adds まも.る. Kanjipedia lists まもる and まもり outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen gives まもる and まもり as kun readings.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading もり; Digital Daijisen gives さね and もり. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.
- Kanjipedia's second sense is use in transcribing Sanskrit (梵語), as in 護摩 ごま, “homa; Buddhist rite of burning wooden sticks to ask a deity for blessings” (JMdict 1271590).

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [保](保.md) (“protect; preserve”): KANJIDIC2 gives “protect” for both 保 and 護, and both characters write 保護, “protection; safeguard.”
- [守](守.md) (“guard; obey”): JMdict 守る (1327120), “to protect; to guard; to defend,” lists 護る as a rarely used kanji form, and the 守 card lists the reading まも.る.
- [衛](衛.md) (“guard; defense”), [防](防.md) (“defend; ward off”) and [警](警.md) (“warn; be on guard”): 護る, as a spelling of 守る, means “to guard; to defend,” and KANJIDIC2 gives 衛 “defense” and “protection.”
- [助](助.md) (“help; rescue”), [救](救.md) (“rescue; save; relief”) and [援](援.md) (“aid; assistance”): Kanjipedia's first sense for 護 includes たすける (to help), and KANJIDIC2 gives “help” for both 救 and 援.
- [看](看.md) (“watch over; see”): both characters appear in the example word 看護婦, and “watch over” is close to “safeguard; protect.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 盗 (“steal”), 壊 (“break; destroy; demolish”), and 戦 (“battle”); none means guarding or protecting.
