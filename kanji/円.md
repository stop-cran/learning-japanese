# 円 — yen, circle

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 冂 (no. 13) · **Phonetic:** none (in the simplified form) · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

円 is the simplified (shinjitai) form of 圓, "round", which consists of 囗 (enclosure) and 員 (member), where 員 is commonly explained as giving the sound. The simplified form 円 is a different shape, not just a reduction.
円 means "circle, round" and, since the Meiji era (the New Currency Act of 1871), "yen", Japan's currency.

## Notes

In prices 円 is the counter for yen: 五円 ごえん, 十円 じゅうえん, 百円 ひゃくえん. 円 and 丸 (まる) both mean round; 円 is more geometric (円, circle), 丸 more physical, but this is a tendency rather than a rule.
Look-alikes: [日](日.md) (sun), [田](田.md) (rice field), and [月](月.md) (moon); 円 has a 冂 frame with a vertical stroke inside it.
