# 祭 — festival; ritual; worship

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 示 (no. 113) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

祭 concerns festivals, ritual observance and worship. The noun まつり names a festival; the verb まつる concerns enshrining or worshipping, not simply holding any kind of party.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the two upper parts bringing offerings toward the 示-shaped base below. This is a modern-shape memory scene, not a verified reconstruction of ancient offerings or hands.

The indexing key 示, no. 113, is visible at the bottom, unlike the left-side 礻 in [祝](祝.md).

## Notes

- The two noun rows, まつ.り and まつり, preserve the source's spelling distinction; they do not describe two different festivals. The linked [祭](../words/祭.md) article teaches the one-kanji spelling.
- 祭る is a transitive godan verb: 祭ります まつります and 祭って まつって. 祀る is another dictionary spelling of that verb, not of the festival noun.
- A festival is not necessarily a day off. 祭日 and 祝日 しゅくじつ have overlapping holiday glosses in the dictionary; do not infer a strict modern calendar distinction just from the two characters.
