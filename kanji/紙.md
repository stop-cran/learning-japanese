# 紙 — paper

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

紙 means paper, both as the word [紙](../words/紙.md) (かみ) and in words for paper products and written materials.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** picture the thread-shaped 糸 at the left tying up sheets
of paper marked with the angular 氏 shape on the right. No historical role for either component is asserted here.

## Common words

- [紙](../words/紙.md) かみ — paper
- 手紙 てがみ — letter
- 用紙 ようし — paper for a use; blank form
- 折り紙 おりがみ — origami; paper folding

## Notes

The がみ in 手紙 is a voiced form of かみ, not a separate on reading.
髪 (かみ, hair) and 神 (かみ, god or deity) have the same kana spelling but different meanings and kanji.

[終](終.md) shares the 糸 left side. [私](私.md) also has an on reading シ, but means "I" or "private," not paper.
