# 開 — open; unfold

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 門 (no. 169) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

開 means **open**, **unfold**, or **begin**. It appears in both actions that open something and
events in which something opens.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the outer 門 as double doors and the inner strokes as a bar
being lifted to open them. This is a memory aid for the current shape, not a historical reconstruction.

## Notes

ドアを開ける（ドアをあける）is "open the door"; ドアが開く（ドアがあく）is "the door opens."
The reading ひらく can be either transitive or intransitive, so do not extend the あける／あく pattern to every use.

[間](間.md) has [日](日.md) inside the gate, and [問](問.md) has [口](口.md).
The inner shape of 開 is neither of those boxes.
