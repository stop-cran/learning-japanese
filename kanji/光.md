# 光 — light; radiance

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (no. 10) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

光 is light: the brightness coming from a source or reaching your eyes.
The noun [光](../words/光.md) is ひかり; the verb 光る ひかる means "shine."
日光 にっこう makes the source explicit: sunlight.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the three upper strokes as rays above the two spreading lower
strokes. This picture describes a memory aid for the modern shape, not a proven origin.

## Notes

The noun ends in the sound り without a written り after the kanji; the verb needs the okurigana る.
In 日光, the whole reading is にっこう, with a small っ.
[先](先.md), [兄](兄.md), and [元](元.md) share the lower 儿 shape; inspect what sits above it.
