# 欲 — desire

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 欠 (no. 76) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

欲 expresses wanting, desire, or appetite. [欲しい](../words/欲しい.md) (ほしい) describes wanting to have something.
食欲 (しょくよく) narrows that desire to food; it means appetite, not a particular food one wants.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the open space above the small square in 谷 as room for something wanted, with 欠 beside it.
This is a modern visual association, not a historical analysis.

## Notes

欲しい is an い-adjective, not the Japanese equivalent of an English verb's grammar.
水が欲しい (みずがほしい) means "I want some water."
Compare the left sides of 欲 and [飲](飲.md); both have 欠 on the right, but only 欲 has 谷.
