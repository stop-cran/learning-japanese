# 掲 — put up; display; publish

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

掲 concerns raising something where people can see it, and by extension putting it into print. [掲示](../words/掲示.md) けいじ is posting a notice. 掲載 けいさい, "publication (e.g. of an article in a newspaper); carrying; running; insertion; printing; posting (e.g. on the web)," uses 掲 for the item that is printed or posted. The verb 掲げる かかげる covers both the physical sense ("to put up (a notice, sign, etc.); to hoist; to raise; to display") and the printed sense ("to publish; to print; to carry (e.g. an article)").

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the side form of [手](手.md), "hand," at the left and a variant of 曷 at the right, drawn as a wide 日-like box (曰) above 匂. Imagine a hand holding a sign up toward the sun. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 曷 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 掲示, 掲 supplies ケイ and 示 supplies ジ. In 掲げる, 掲 writes かか and げる is okurigana. Practice selects ケイ. かか.げる is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with 掲げる, but that word has no linked article yet, so the reading is not selected for questions. This is a teaching selection, not a claim that かか.げる is invalid, obsolete or rare.

## Notes

- [示](示.md), "indicate," is the other kanji in 掲示. 掲示 puts a notice up, and 示 contributes showing it.
- [景](景.md), "scenery," also reads ケイ, but it is only a sound-alike with a different shape and meaning. [損](損.md), "loss; damage," and [掘](掘.md), "dig; excavate," share 扌 at the left but have different right-hand shapes.
- [刊](刊.md), "publish; issue," [置](置.md), "put; place," and [刷](刷.md), "print; brush," overlap with 掲 in the ideas of publishing, printing and putting something somewhere: JMdict gives "to publish; to print; to carry" as a sense of 掲げる. 掲 is raising or posting something so that it is seen, so do not treat "publish," "print" or "put" as exclusive to any one character.
