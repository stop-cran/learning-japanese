# 差 — difference; gap

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 工 (no. 48) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

差 marks a difference or gap between things, often one that can be compared in size.
The word [差](../words/差.md) さ can stand alone: 差がある。 (さがある。) — There is a difference.
In 時差 じさ, the difference is specifically in time.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the sheep-like upper strokes descending toward 工.
Imagine checking a gap between two levels. This is a shape-based memory aid, not an explanation of the character's origin.

## Notes

The indexing radical is the 工 at the bottom, not the prominent upper shape.
[着](着.md) has a similar top but ends in 目.
[洋](洋.md) places 羊 beside a water component, while [左](左.md) also contains 工 but has a much simpler upper part.
違い ちがい is another word for a difference; the linked article compares their uses.
