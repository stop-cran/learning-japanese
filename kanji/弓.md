# 弓 — bow (for arrows)

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (no. 57) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弓 is a bow for shooting arrows. As a component it appears in 引 (pull), 弦 (bowstring) and 強 (strong).

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Trace the three bends as a bow seen from the side.

## Notes

[万](万.md), [九](九.md) and [力](力.md) also have a bent, hooked stroke like 弓 but mean ten thousand, nine and power. [引](引.md), [弟](弟.md) and [弱](弱.md) contain 弓 itself and mean pull, younger brother and weak. [矢](矢.md) is the arrow, [弧](弧.md) an arc and [射](射.md) shooting; they relate to a bow without meaning “bow.”
