# 速 — fast

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

速 concerns speed: how quickly something moves or happens. [速い](../words/速い.md) はやい describes a fast runner or vehicle.
Do not confuse speed with an early time: [早い](../words/早い.md) はやい supplies that time-related meaning.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 束-shaped center as a parcel speeding along the sweeping 辶 path.
This is a memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

[連](連.md) has 車 inside the shared 辶 outline; 速 has 束. [週](週.md) has a boxier center.
