# 業 — work, occupation

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

業 concerns work, an occupation, or an activity someone carries out. In [授業](../words/授業.md), it relates to the work of teaching and learning in class.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the many upper strokes as tasks stacked above a [木](木.md)-like support: a full load of work. This is a memory aid, not an etymology.

- 作業 さぎょう — work, task, operation
- 卒業 そつぎょう — graduation

## Notes

業 is not normally the standalone everyday word for "my job"; [仕事](../words/仕事.md) しごと fills that role.
Compare [楽](楽.md): both have a broad lower part, but 楽 has a 白-shaped center above 木, while 業 has a dense stack of upper strokes without that box.
