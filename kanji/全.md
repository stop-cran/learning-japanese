# 全 — whole; complete

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 入 (no. 11; the modern top looks 人-like) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

全 takes in the whole of something rather than one portion.
[全部](../words/全部.md) ぜんぶ means "all; the whole," while 全員 ぜんいん means all the members of a group.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the broad two-stroke roof covering the whole 王-shaped stack
below. This is a visual cue, not an etymology. The source indexes 全 under 入 even though its modern top looks like 人.

## Common words

- [全部](../words/全部.md) ぜんぶ — all; the whole
- 全員 ぜんいん — all members
- 全く まったく — completely; really
- 全て すべて — everything; usually すべて

## Notes

全員 answers a question about the members; 全部 can take in objects, contents, or an entire amount.
Learn 全く as its own word rather than substituting it for 全部 in every sentence.
[金](金.md), [会](会.md), and [公](公.md) have similar broad tops, but different lower structures.
