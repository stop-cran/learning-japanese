# 椋 — muku tree

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

椋 names the **muku tree**, [椋](../words/椋.md) むく, *Aphananthe aspera*. It is a deciduous tree, one that loses its leaves seasonally. 椋 can also refer to its fruit or abbreviate 椋鳥 (むくどり), starling; context matters.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside [京](京.md). Imagine a muku tree growing near a capital city. This labels the visible parts for memory, not their historical relationship.

## Notes

- This card asks むく. リョウ is retained with the attested 椋樹 compound, not treated as nonexistent or inferred from a person's name.
- 椋の木 is the longer plant name; do not replace the source reading むく with むくのき in this card's metadata.
- The bird word 椋鳥 contains [鳥](鳥.md), bird, with とり voiced to どり. The dictionary also records broader starling and figurative senses of the whole word; the example here selects the grey-starling sense.
- Compare [涼](涼.md), cool or refreshing: it has a water-shaped left side where 椋 has 木. [京](京.md) alone means capital, while [村](村.md), village, and [相](相.md), mutual or aspect, have different right-hand components.
