# 猿 — monkey

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (no. 94; visible as 犭 on the left) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

猿 writes the native animal noun **さる**, "monkey." Depending on context, the Japanese word can also cover apes and other non-human primates; do not assume it always matches a strict English monkey-versus-ape division.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the animal radical 犭 sits beside 袁 (えん), the detailed shape also inside [園](園.md), "park; garden." Picture a monkey beside a garden. This is a memory aid, not a historical derivation or a rule that every character with 犭 names an animal.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| エン | on | 類人猿 るいじんえん — ape; compound recognition, not selected for this animal-noun reading lesson |

Both full source readings are preserved. Only さる is selected for reading questions here; this does not make エン invalid or imply that all its compounds are rare.

## Common words

- [猿](../words/猿.md) さる — monkey; also written サル or さる
- 日本猿 にほんざる — Japanese macaque; also written ニホンザル
- 類人猿 るいじんえん — ape

## Notes

動物園でサルを見ました。 (どうぶつえんでサルをみました。) — I saw monkeys at the zoo.

日本猿 has voiced ざる in the compound; that does not replace the independent reading さる. The animal noun itself does not mark singular or plural.

The existing [申](申.md) card includes さる for the Monkey sign of the traditional zodiac. That is a different lexical use from the animal noun 猿. Compare the whole shapes: 申 has a vertical stroke through a box; 猿 has the animal radical beside a more detailed right side.

Applied to a person, 猿 can be derogatory, for example for someone regarded as sly or foolish. Those uses are not neutral personality descriptions. The animal example above does not carry that judgment.

[園](園.md) encloses the shared right-hand shape rather than putting 犭 beside it. [鳥](鳥.md), "bird," is a different animal category; [風](風.md), "wind; style," is not an animal.
