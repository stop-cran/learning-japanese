# 康 — health; well-being

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53), enclosing the upper left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

康 concerns being sound, well or at ease.
[健康](../words/健康.md) けんこう means health or healthy.
Small periods of relief or stability appear in 小康 しょうこう,
a lull or respite, including a stable or improved medical condition.
It does not by itself promise a permanent recovery.

KANJIDIC2 gives ease and peace; Daijisen also explicitly records bodily
soundness. Health is therefore a useful focus, not a claim that 康 only
concerns medicine.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the broad 广 enclosure over
stacked bars and spreading lower strokes. Imagine an untroubled person
resting comfortably beneath that cover. This is a modern memory aid, not
an account of the character's historical components.

## Notes

- [健](健.md) directly overlaps health. Peace or ease also connects 康 with
  [安](安.md), [平](平.md), [寧](寧.md), [楽](楽.md) and [和](和.md).
  This is not a claim that health means cheapness, flatness, music or Japanese style.
- Quiet or an undisturbed state offers a comparison with [静](静.md).
  The English *lull* in 小康 overlaps [凪](凪.md), although sea calm and
  medical stability are not the same situation.
- [庫](庫.md), storehouse, [床](床.md), floor/bed, and [庭](庭.md),
  garden/yard, share 广 but differ in their interiors and meanings.
