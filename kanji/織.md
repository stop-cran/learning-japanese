# 織 — weave; fabric; organization

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

織 is weaving: working threads across one another on a loom to make cloth. That gives woven cloth, and the idea of parts put together into a structured whole. [組織](../words/組織.md) そしき is an organization or the structure in which its parts work together; JMdict glosses it "organization; organisation; formation," and its sixth sense is "weave (of a fabric)." The word article teaches "organization; tissue."

The Daijisen character entry gives 2 senses for ショク. One example of each:

- weaving cloth on a loom, as in 織機 しょっき, "loom; weaving machine";
- the Oda clan, as in 織豊時代 しょくほうじだい, "historical term Shokuhō period (i.e. the Azuchi-Momoyama period, approx. 1568-1600)"; this is a name use and is not taught here.

For シキ it gives "weave; assemble," as in 組織. It marks ショク as a Sino-Japanese reading (漢) and シキ as a Go-on reading (呉), lists the kun reading おる, and lists おり among the name readings. More Daijisen examples of the first ショク sense are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left. In the right half you can see [音](音.md), "sound," with a hooked stroke beside it. Imagine the click of a loom as threads cross. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 or 音 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 組織, 組 supplies ソ and 織 supplies シキ. そしき is the whole-word reading of 組織: it is not itself a single on reading. ショク is a different on reading, as in 染織, where 染 supplies セン and 織 supplies ショク, and in 織機, where JMdict gives しょっき for the spelling 織機 and おりき for both spellings. In 織る, 織 writes お and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 織り, the り is okurigana; JMdict gives 織り おり "weave; weaving; woven item," with the spelling 織 also listed. In 織物, 織 writes おり and 物 writes もの, so the word is おりもの; JMdict glosses it "textile; fabric" and lists 織り物 and 織りもの as "search-only kanji form." In 羽織, 羽 supplies は and 織 supplies おり. The leading dash in -おり and -お.り marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ショク, with 織機, 染織 and 紡織; シキ, with 組織; and おる, with 織る and 織物. Practice selects シキ, the reading of the linked word article 組織 そしき, where 織 supplies シキ. ショク is the reading of most 織 compounds about cloth, and no word article uses it yet. 織物 おりもの is not a linked article. The other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [組](組.md), "group; assemble," in 組織, supplying ソ. The word article describes 組 as grouping or putting together and 織 as weaving.
- JMdict's 組織 also has "structure; construction; setup; constitution," "system (e.g. railroad, transport, party, etc.)," "tissue [field: biology]" and "texture (of a rock) [field: geology]." The spelling 組職 is tagged "search-only kanji form" and the reading そしょく is tagged "out-dated or obsolete kana usage."
- [編](編.md), "knit; compile," and [縫](縫.md), "sew; stitch," overlap "weave" and making cloth. [布](布.md), "cloth; distribute," overlaps "fabric." [団](団.md), "group; association," [系](系.md), "lineage; system," [制](制.md), "system; regulate," [構](構.md), "build; stance," [体](体.md), "body, form," and 組 overlap "organization," "structure" and "system." All are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [川](川.md), "river," [魚](魚.md), "fish," and [歌](歌.md), "song, sing," are common starter kanji with simple shapes; none looks like 織. They are read セン, かわ; ギョ, さかな, うお; and カ, うた, and share no reading or meaning with 織.
- Many other kanji read ショク or シキ, such as [職](職.md), "employment; post," [色](色.md), "color," and [式](式.md), "ceremony; formula," and [処](処.md), "deal with; dispose," [居](居.md), "be present; dwell," and [折](折.md), "fold," share お.る; they are never offered as wrong answers.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first ショク sense, weaving cloth: 織布 しょくふ, "woven fabric," 機織 はたおり, "weaving; weaver," 製織 せいしょく, "weaving," and 紡織 ぼうしょく, "spinning and weaving." Daijisen also lists 交織 こうしょく, "mixed weave," and 混織 and 染織.
