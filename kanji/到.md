# 到 — arrive; attain

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (as 刂, no. 18) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

到 emphasizes reaching an endpoint. [到着](../words/到着.md) とうちゃく is arrival at a destination,
while 到達 とうたつ can also be attaining a goal or level.
Focus on reaching, rather than merely moving toward something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the left-hand 至 shape reaching the upright finish marker 刂.
The finish marker is a visual invention, not evidence of the character's historical formation.

## Notes

駅に到着する (えきにとうちゃくする) means "arrive at the station": に marks the destination.
[着](着.md) and [達](達.md) also have reaching-related meanings; compare the whole words to see how each is used.
For shape practice, compare the different left halves of [利](利.md), [列](列.md), and [刻](刻.md).
