# 兆 — trillion; omen

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (no. 10) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

兆 has two important learning anchors: the large-number unit 10^12, one trillion, and a sign or indication of something. 兆候 ちょうこう and 兆し きざし concern signs or indications, not large quantities.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the two spreading curved sides and their small side strokes as two branches suggesting what may develop next.

The classical indexing radical is 儿, represented by the two spreading leg-like strokes.

## Notes

- 一兆 いっちょう is 1,000,000,000,000, or ten thousand 億. Notice the small っ in いっちょう.
- A 兆し need not be a bad omen: the word can describe a sign of recovery or another favorable development. Distinguish the sign sense from the numerical unit.
