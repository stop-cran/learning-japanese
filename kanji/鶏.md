# 鶏 — chicken

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 鳥 (no. 196), on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鶏 means **chicken**, the domestic bird. The native noun is [鶏](../words/鶏.md)
にわとり. In food vocabulary, [鶏肉](../words/鶏肉.md) とりにく means chicken meat.
The shorter reading とり also occurs, so do not assume that 鶏 always has four kana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** locate [鳥](鳥.md) on the right.
On the left, imagine the upper claw-like strokes reaching toward the
[夫](夫.md)-shaped lower part, then associate the whole picture with a chicken.
This is a memory aid for the modern form, not a verified historical or phonetic analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケイ | on | 養鶏 ようけい — chicken farming; useful compound recognition, not selected for this native-noun lesson |
| にわとり | kun | [鶏](../words/鶏.md) にわとり — chicken, the domestic bird |
| とり | kun | [鶏肉](../words/鶏肉.md) とりにく — chicken meat; native とり + on ニク |

The two selected native readings have different examples. にわとり names the bird;
とり in 鶏肉 combines with にく. It is not an instruction to read 鶏肉 as にわとりにく.
The [養鶏 headword](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%A4%8A%E9%B6%8F-145629)
confirms the complete reading ようけい; the full source on-reading remains ケイ.

## Notes

- [鳥](鳥.md) is the broader bird character. 鶏 adds an eight-stroke left side;
  keep it when writing chicken specifically. Their shared animal category makes
  鳥 unsuitable as a wrong meaning answer here.
- [魚](魚.md), fish, also has four lower marks, but a different upper body.
  [駅](駅.md), station, and [黒](黒.md), black, are other shape comparisons, not chicken meanings.
- The zodiac sign とり is written 酉, not a reading rule for the animal noun にわとり.
- Dictionary evidence also recognizes a meat sense for 鶏; a bird-focused explanation
  does not make that sense disappear. The word article distinguishes the food context.

Reading and lexical evidence: [Nikkoku's にわとり entry](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%B6%8F-460974)
and JMdict entries 1252990, 1253020 and 1547120. The same dictionary page includes
archaic headwords; those are not additional selected readings in this lesson.
