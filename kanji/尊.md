# 尊 — revere; esteem

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

尊 concerns valuing or regarding someone or something highly. 尊敬 そんけい means respect or esteem, and 尊重 そんちょう means respect or due regard. 尊い is an adjective meaning precious or noble; 尊ぶ is a verb meaning to value or respect.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine carefully holding the 酋-shaped upper object above 寸 as a reminder to treat something as valuable. This invented modern-shape image is not a reconstruction of a ritual or the character's historical origin.
