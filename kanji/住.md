# 住 — reside, dwell

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

住 concerns having a home or residence in a place. [住む](../words/住む.md) is "to live somewhere," not "to be alive."
It also appears in words for addresses and housing.

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and [主](主.md).
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** a person's main place is where they live. This is a mnemonic, not an account of the parts' historical roles.

## Notes

Use a place + に with 住む: 東京に住んでいます (とうきょうにすんでいます), "I live in Tokyo."
Compare [主](主.md), without 亻, and [仕](仕.md), whose right side lacks 主's dot and extra horizontal stroke.
