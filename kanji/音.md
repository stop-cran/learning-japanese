# 音 — sound

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 音 (no. 180) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

音 is **sound**: a noise, a musical tone, or a speech sound, depending on the word.
The ordinary standalone word is 音（おと）.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see [立](立.md) above a [日](日.md)-shaped box and imagine a speaker standing
on a sound box. This is a visual association, not the character's verified origin.

## Notes

声（こえ）usually draws attention to a voice or an animal's call; 音 is the broader everyday word for sound.
雨の音（あめのおと）means "the sound of rain."

[意](意.md) adds [心](心.md) below 音. [立](立.md) alone lacks the lower box.
Learn おと and ね in their words rather than exchanging them freely.
