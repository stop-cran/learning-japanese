# 椿 — camellia

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

椿 writes **camellia**, [椿](../words/椿.md) つばき: the flowering tree or shrub, particularly *Camellia japonica*. Context can focus on its flowers.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside [春](春.md), spring. Imagine a camellia flowering beside a tree in spring. The visible shapes are a memory aid, not evidence that the character was historically created to mean "spring tree."

## Notes

- 椿の花 (つばきのはな) specifies the flower. 椿の木 (つばきのき) specifies the tree.
- This card asks only つばき. The チン example preserves a separate compound use without adding an incident-vocabulary question.
- 山茶花 (さざんか), sasanqua, is a different plant name; its spelling is not a reason to pronounce 椿 さざんか.
- Compare [棒](棒.md), rod or stick: below its upper strokes, the right side does not end in 日 as 春 does. [村](村.md), village, has the much simpler 寸 on the right.
