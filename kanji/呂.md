# 呂 — spine; backbone; bass range

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

呂 has the dictionary meanings "spine" and "backbone," and in Japanese music it names a low range of tones, but in everyday Japanese it is met mainly as the second syllable of the bath word. [風呂](../words/風呂.md) ふろ is a bath or bathtub, [お風呂](../words/お風呂.md) おふろ is the everyday polite form, "bath," and [風呂敷](../words/風呂敷.md) ふろしき is a furoshiki, a wrapping cloth. JMdict glosses 風呂 "bath; bathing; bathtub; bathroom."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for each on reading. For リョ it gives the yin mode of tones in gagaku, Japanese court music, as in 律呂 りつりょ, "standard tones; Chinese system of musical sounds," 大呂 たいりょ, "second note of the ancient Chinese chromatic scale (approx. D sharp)," and 南呂 なんりょ, "10th note of the ancient chromatic scale (approx. B)." JMdict also has the word 呂 りょ, an abbreviation glossed "bass range (in Japanese music)."

For ロ the entry gives only 当て字 (ateji), kanji used for their sound, as in 語呂 ごろ, "sound (of a sentence); euphony," and 風呂. JMdict does not mark the spelling 風呂 as ateji, and the official Joyo table lists 風呂 under ロ. The entry also lists 呂律 ろれつ, "articulation," among words that are hard to read. It gives no kun reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find two [口](口.md), "mouth," shapes stacked one above the other, joined by a short stroke. Imagine two vertebrae of a spine, one sitting on the next. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 口 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ロ | on | [風呂](../words/風呂.md) ふろ — bath; bathtub; [お風呂](../words/お風呂.md) おふろ — bath; [風呂敷](../words/風呂敷.md) ふろしき — furoshiki; wrapping cloth |
| リョ | on | 律呂 りつりょ - standard tones; Chinese system of musical sounds; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| せぼね | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2 with the meanings "spine; backbone"; JMdict writes せぼね as 背骨, "spine; backbone; spinal column," with no spelling using 呂, and the Daijisen character entry gives no kun reading |

In 風呂, 風 supplies フ and 呂 supplies ロ, giving ふろ. お風呂 adds the polite prefix お, written in kana. In 風呂敷, 敷 adds しき. After another element ふろ often becomes ぶろ, a voicing called rendaku, as in 露天風呂 ろてんぶろ, "open-air bath; rotenburo; rotemburo." In 律呂, 律 supplies リツ and 呂 supplies リョ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ロ, with 風呂 as its example. リョ and せぼね are not in the official table. Practice selects ロ, the reading 呂 supplies in the linked articles 風呂, お風呂 and 風呂敷; the Daijisen labels this ロ as ateji, but JMdict does not mark 風呂 that way. リョ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, and せぼね has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [風](風.md), "wind; style," in all three words and [敷](敷.md), "spread; lay out," in 風呂敷. The お of お風呂 is kana.
- [口](口.md), "mouth," is the shape 呂 has twice. [品](品.md), "goods,refinement," stacks three 口, and [営](営.md), "conduct business; manage," has the same 呂 shape at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [背](背.md), "back; stature," [腰](腰.md), "waist; lower back," and [骨](骨.md), "bone; skeleton," overlap the senses "spine" and "backbone" that KANJIDIC2 gives for 呂; JMdict's 背骨 せぼね is written with 背 and 骨. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "spine" or "back" as exclusive to any one character.
