# 蚕 — silkworm

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 虫 (no. 142), below 天 · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

蚕 means **silkworm**, the insect raised for silk. In the ordinary rearing context, it names the larva that eats mulberry leaves and makes a [繭](../words/繭.md) まゆ, cocoon. The insect, its cocoon and the silk material are different things.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [天](天.md), sky, above [虫](虫.md), insect. Imagine an insect under a little sky-shaped roof. This describes the modern shape, not a verified historical explanation or a claim that 天 supplies the usual pronunciation.

| こ | kun | 春蚕 はるご - spring-reared silkworms; a bound use with こ voiced to ご, retained for recognition |

Practice **かいこ** for the standalone insect noun. **サン** occurs in compounds such as 養蚕; this card does not require a separate sericulture-vocabulary article.

**こ** is documented as an older standalone word and in compounds. Digital Daijisen gives 春蚕 as an example; that word also has the reading **しゅんさん**. Do not replace かいこ with こ in ordinary animal descriptions. The source-only **テン** entry is lookup information, not an additional pronunciation to guess for the examples above.

## Notes

- [繭](../words/繭.md) まゆ names the cocoon; [絹](../words/絹.md) きぬ names silk. Neither is another name for the larva.
- [虫](虫.md), insect/bug, is a broader category, so it is not an unambiguous wrong meaning choice for 蚕.
- Compare [天](天.md), heaven/sky, [春](春.md), spring season, and [蛍](蛍.md), firefly. 蚕 has 天 above 虫; 春 has 日 below its upper strokes, while 蛍 has a cover and small strokes above 虫.
- The animal name can also be written かいこ or カイコ. Kana spelling does not make it a foreign loanword. Read whole compounds separately rather than assigning every 蚕-containing word the reading かいこ.
