# 好 — liking; favorable

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

好 concerns liking, preference, or a favorable feeling. [好き](../words/好き.md) (すき) expresses being fond of something; 好む (このむ) is a verb meaning "like; prefer."
They share a character but do not fit the same sentence patterns.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** let the visible 女 and 子 stand beside something they both like.
This invented scene is a memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin or about family roles.

The source lists す.く; the useful related form here is 好き (すき), with き written outside the kanji.

## Common words

- [好き](../words/好き.md) (すき) — fond of; to one's liking
- 好む (このむ) — like; prefer
- 好意 (こうい) — kindness; goodwill; affection

## Notes

Despite ending in the kana き, 好き is a **な-adjective**: 好きな本 (すきなほん), "a book one likes."
Compare [妹](妹.md), [姉](姉.md), and [始](始.md): all keep 女 on the left, but none has 子 on the right.
The additional よ.い and い.い source readings are preserved in metadata; the table selects the readings used by the words shown.
