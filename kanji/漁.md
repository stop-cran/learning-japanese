# 漁 — fishing; fishery

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

漁 concerns fishing or gathering seafood. Its native verb 漁る also extends to searching or rummaging for things.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the water strokes beside [魚](魚.md), fish: someone searches the water for a catch.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- 漁業 reads ぎょぎょう, not りょうぎょう. Learn the ギョ and リョウ examples as distinct word readings.
- 漁る あさる is a transitive godan verb: 漁って あさって. Its searching use is not limited to fish or the sea.
- [釣り](../words/釣り.md) specifically concerns angling. Do not turn that distinction into a rule that all 釣り is recreational or that all fishing uses a hook.
