# 縄 — rope; cord

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

縄 is rope made by twisting fibre. [縄](../words/縄.md) なわ is the everyday word; JMdict glosses it "rope; cord" and "policeman's rope" (usually as お縄). The word article teaches "rope; cord."

The Daijisen character entry gives 2 senses for ジョウ:

- rope: 縄文 じょうもん, "straw-rope pattern pressed into earthenware" and "Jōmon period (ca. 14000-1000 BCE)";
- a carpenter's inkline, and by extension a standard of correctness: 準縄 じゅんじょう, "a level and inked string; norm; rule; standard."

Daijisen's なわ headword describes a long thin cord of twisted plant or chemical fibre used for tying, a rope for binding criminals, and a rope used to measure field area. Its つな usage note says 綱, 縄 and ひも run from thick to thin, that 縄 is mainly twisted rice straw, and that 縄 is used for tying while 綱 supports or pulls.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 糸, "thread," at the left; a rope is many threads twisted together. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 縄文, 縄 supplies ジョウ and 文 supplies モン. In 縄跳び なわとび, "skipping rope; jump rope," 縄 supplies なわ; the rest is a separate word part. In 縄張り なわばり, "stretching a rope around; roping off; cordoning off; demarcation," 縄 supplies なわ and 張り supplies ばり. In 自縄自縛 じじょうじばく, "being caught in one's own trap; being hoisted by one's own petard," 自 supplies ジ, 縄 supplies ジョウ, 自 supplies ジ and 縛 supplies バク. 結縄 けつじょう is "knotted cord or rope," 捕縄 ほじょう is "policeman's rope (used for restraining criminals)," 腰縄 こしなわ is "leash; rope tied round prisoner's waists," and 泥縄 どろなわ is "measure taken too late; measure taken in haste."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジョウ, with 縄文 and 自縄自縛, and なわ, with 縄 and 縄張; it prints the variant 繩 in round brackets. Practice selects なわ, the reading of the linked word article 縄. Readings outside the practice set are not invalid.

## Notes

- 縄 contains no other kanji. Among the compounds named above, [文](文.md), "sentence, writing," supplies モン in 縄文, [張](張.md), "stretch tight; assert," supplies ば in 縄張り, [自](自.md), "self, oneself," and [縛](縛.md), "bind; tie up; restrain," appear in 自縄自縛, and [捕](捕.md), "capture," appears in 捕縄.
- [綱](綱.md) is the thick rope or hawser (that card lists 縄 as an exclusion, and the app treats exclusions as symmetric); [索](索.md), [緒](緒.md) and [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," overlap cord or string. All are kept out of this card's quiz options. 縛 and 結 describe tying, not the rope itself.
- [紙](紙.md), "paper," also has 糸 at the left and is read シ and かみ. [練](練.md), "practice; refine," also has 糸 and is read レン and ね.る. [馬](馬.md), "horse," is read バ and うま. None shares a reading or a meaning with 縄. Cards [弾](弾.md), [正](正.md) and [質](質.md) share ただ.す and are never offered as wrong answers.
- Like the Joyo table, Daijisen prints the traditional form 繩 in brackets after 縄.
