# 暴 — violence; outburst

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

暴 concerns violent or uncontrolled behavior, as in 暴れる あばれる and 暴力 ぼうりょく, violence. In 暴く あばく and 暴露 ばくろ, it instead concerns exposing or disclosing something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 日 above the crowded lower strokes and imagine an outburst scattering things below. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

- 暴れる is intransitive and ichidan: 暴れます あばれます, 暴れて あばれて, 暴れない あばれない. It describes behavior, not simply having an angry feeling.
- 暴く is a transitive godan verb ending in く: 暴いて あばいて. A secret can be exposed without anyone acting violently.
- JMdict also lists 曝露 for ばくろ in the same disclosure/exposure entry. This spelling list alone does not establish frequency or make every specialized use of the two spellings interchangeable.
