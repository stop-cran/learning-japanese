# 幕 — curtain; act of a play; shogunate

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

幕 is a large hanging cloth: a curtain that hides or closes off a space, the stage curtain of a theatre and so an act of a play, and, read バク, the tented headquarters of a general and so the shogunate. [幕](../words/幕.md) まく is a curtain or an act in a play; JMdict glosses it "curtain," "act (in a play)," also used as a counter, "end; close," and "case; occasion; role (to play); part; place," and in sumo the top division, makuuchi.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for マク. One example of each:

- a cloth hung to hide or cover something: 暗幕 あんまく, "blackout curtain";
- a hanging cloth in theatre or film, or a division of a play: 字幕 じまく, "subtitles; captioning";
- the top division of sumo: 入幕 にゅうまく, "advancing to the first grade," that is, into 幕内 まくうち, "makuuchi; top division (of professional sumo)."

It gives four senses for バク. One example of each:

- a covering cloth or tent: 幕営 ばくえい, "camp; camping";
- a commander's tented headquarters: 幕僚 ばくりょう, "staff; staff officer";
- the place where the shogun governs: 幕府 ばくふ, "shogunate; bakufu," a historical term;
- short for 幕府, especially the Edo shogunate: 幕末 ばくまつ, "Bakumatsu period; closing days of the Tokugawa shogunate; end of the Edo period," a historical term.

It gives no kun reading. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a grass-like top, [日](日.md), "sun, day," under it, a flattened [大](大.md), "big, large," and 巾, the key, a shape like a hanging cloth, at the bottom. Imagine the sun sinking into the grass at the end of the day while a big cloth comes down: the curtain falls. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 日, 大 or 巾 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| マク | on | [幕](../words/幕.md) まく — curtain; act in a play |
| バク | on | 幕府 ばくふ - shogunate; bakufu; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| とばり | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; the Daijisen character entry gives no kun reading, and JMdict spells とばり 帳, "curtain; hanging," or rarely 帷, 幄 or 幌, and has no entry writing it with 幕 |

The word 幕 is the kanji alone, read with its on reading マク and used as a noun by itself, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 幕府, 幕 supplies バク and 府 supplies フ. In 幕切れ まくぎれ, "fall of the curtain; end of an act," 幕 supplies マク and 切 with the okurigana れ, read きれ, becomes ぎれ by rendaku, the voicing of the first sound of a following element.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows マク, with 幕切れ, 天幕 てんまく, "decorative curtain hung from the ceiling," also "tent; pavilion; marquee," and 暗幕 as examples; and バク, with 幕府, 幕末 and 幕僚. とばり is not in the table. Practice selects マク, the reading with a linked word article. バク is the reading of the shogunate words, but no word article uses it yet; it is illustrated with a dictionary word, and とばり has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The word 幕 contains only this kanji. [帳](帳.md), "notebook; account book; register," also has a hanging-curtain sense and is the usual spelling of とばり, so the two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- [墓](墓.md), "grave; tomb," [暮](暮.md), "dusk; livelihood," and [募](募.md), "recruit; solicit," share the upper part of 幕, the grass-like top over 日 over a flattened 大, with 土, 日 and 力 at the bottom where 幕 has 巾. All are different kanji.
- [劇](劇.md), "drama; play," [演](演.md), "perform; stage," [布](布.md), "cloth; distribute," [終](終.md), "end; finish," and [末](末.md), "end; tip," overlap the senses "act of a play" and "end; close" that 幕 and its word carry, and the covering-cloth sense in the Daijisen entry; 末 appears with 幕 in 幕末. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "curtain," "play" or "end" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the マク senses: 煙幕 えんまく, "smokescreen," and 除幕 じょまく, "unveiling (a statue, monument, etc.)"; 開幕 かいまく, "raising the curtain," also "opening (of an event); start; beginning," 序幕 じょまく, "curtain-raiser; prologue," and 閉幕 へいまく, "falling of the curtain," also "(coming to an) end; close." Of the バク senses: 帷幕 いばく, "curtain; field staff headquarters; secret meeting place"; 幕舎 ばくしゃ, "camp; barracks"; 幕政 ばくせい, "the shogunate (administration)"; and 討幕 とうばく, "attacking the shogunate."
