# 制 — system; regulate

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (as 刂, no. 18) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

制 belongs to the vocabulary of systems, rules, and control.
[制度](../words/制度.md) せいど names an institutional system; 規制 きせい is regulation or restriction.
The system can organize activity without being a physical machine.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the crossbars and hanging lower strokes on the left as an organized
framework, with 刂 marking its boundary. This is a present-shape memory aid, not an etymology.

## Notes

制度 concerns an established arrangement; 規制 highlights controls or restrictions.
Do not assume every English use of "system" translates as 制度.
[判](判.md), [利](利.md), and [列](列.md) share 刂, but 制 has its own crossbar-and-hanging-strokes pattern on the left.
