# 侮 — despise; scorn

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

侮 means to despise, scorn or make light of someone or something. The verb [侮る](../words/侮る.md) あなどる uses its kun reading.
