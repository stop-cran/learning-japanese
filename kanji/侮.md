# 侮 — despise; scorn

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

侮 means to despise, scorn or make light of someone or something. The verb [侮る](../words/侮る.md) あなどる uses its kun reading.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ブ | on | 侮辱 ぶじょく — insult; affront (JMdict entry 1498260). |
| あなど.る | kun | [侮る](../words/侮る.md) あなどる — look down on; make light of (JMdict entry 1583670) |
| あなず.る | kun | あなずる — older form of あなどる (Digital Daijisen via Weblio). |

## Common words

- [侮る](../words/侮る.md) あなどる — look down on; make light of
- 侮辱 ぶじょく — insult; affront

## Notes
