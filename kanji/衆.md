# 衆 — the masses; multitude

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 血 (no. 143) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

衆 refers to many people. KANJIDIC2 gives “masses,” “great numbers,” “multitude,” and “populace,” and Kanjipedia's senses are being many in number and many people or the people (多くの人・たみ). JMdict glosses 衆 on its own, しゅう, as “great numbers (of people); numerical superiority; masses.” In [公衆](../words/公衆.md) こうしゅう, JMdict gives “the public; general public.” The whole word reads こうしゅう, with シュウ for 衆.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [血](血.md), "blood," sits at the top, with [皿](皿.md), "dish; plate," inside it; below are strokes that include 亻, a variant of [人](人.md), "person." Imagine many people lining up beneath one huge serving dish at a festival: a multitude. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 血, 皿, or 人 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists シュウ and シュ, with 衆生 as its example for シュ, and no kun reading.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings とも, ひろ, もり, and もろ; these are name use, not quiz readings.

These seven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [民](民.md) (“populace”): KANJIDIC2 also gives 衆 “populace,” and Kanjipedia's second sense of 衆 includes たみ (the people).
- [群](群.md) (“flock; cluster; crowd”), [団](団.md) (“group; association”), and [組](組.md) (“group; assemble”): JMdict glosses 群衆 ぐんしゅう as “group (of people); crowd; horde; throng; mob; multitude,” and glosses 衆 itself with “people; folk; clique; bunch.”
- [多](多.md) (“many, much”) and [諸](諸.md) (“various; many; together”): KANJIDIC2 gives 衆 “great numbers,” and Kanjipedia's first sense is おおい。数が多い。もろもろ (many; numerous; all sorts).
- [公](公.md) (“public, official”): JMdict glosses 公衆 こうしゅう as “the public; general public.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 王 (“king”), 客 (“guest; customer”), and 父 (“father”); none means the masses or a multitude.
