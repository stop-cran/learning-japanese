# 項 — item; clause

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

項 is one item in a list or one clause in a text, and also the nape of the neck. KANJIDIC2 lists “paragraph”, “nape of neck”, “clause”, “item” and “term (expression)”. Kanjipedia's first sense is うなじ。くびすじ。 (nape; back of the neck) and its second is 小分けした一つ一つのことがら。 (each one of the matters in a subdivision), with 項目 and 事項 as examples. [項目](../words/項目.md) こうもく is glossed in JMdict “item; heading; category; clause” and “headword (in a dictionary, encyclopedia, etc.); entry” (entry 1283000). On its own, 項 こう is “clause; paragraph; item” (JMdict 1282980).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [工](工.md), "craft, construction," stands on the left, and 頁 stands on the right: a horizontal stroke and a left-falling stroke over [貝](貝.md), "shellfish; seashell," which is made of [目](目.md), "eye," over [八](八.md), "eight." Imagine a builder running an eye down a work plan, ticking off each item and clause in turn. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 工 or 頁 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists コウ only, with 項目, 事項 じこう and 条項 じょうこう (“clause; article; stipulations”, JMdict 1356550). Kanjipedia lists うなじ outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen gives the kun readings うなじ and うな.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 項; Digital Daijisen gives the name reading うじ. This is name use, not a quiz reading.

These five cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [条](条.md) (“clause; condition; item; line”): KANJIDIC2 lists “clause” and “item” for both 項 and 条.
- [件](件.md) (“case; item”): the title's “item” matches KANJIDIC2's “item” for 項, and Kanjipedia's second sense of 項 is 小分けした一つ一つのことがら (each one of the matters in a subdivision).
- [個](個.md) (“individual; item counter”): KANJIDIC2 lists “item” for 項, JMdict glosses 項 こう as “clause; paragraph; item” (1282980), and Digital Daijisen's second sense of 項 begins 小分けした一つ一つ。 (each one of the subdivisions); these overlap the title's “item counter”.
- [事](事.md) (“matter, affair”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 項 ends in ことがら and Digital Daijisen's second sense includes 事柄 (matter); JMdict glosses 事柄 ことがら “matter; thing; affair; circumstance” (1314240) and 事項 じこう “matter; item; facts” (1313910).
- [首](首.md) (“neck”): KANJIDIC2 lists “nape of neck” for 項, JMdict glosses 項 うなじ “nape (of the neck); nucha” (1282970), Kanjipedia's first sense is うなじ。くびすじ。, Digital Daijisen's first sense is 首筋。うなじ。, and the 首 card lists くび.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 額 (“forehead; frame; sum”), 頂 (“summit; receive”), and 預 (“deposit; entrust”); none means an item, a clause or the nape of the neck.
