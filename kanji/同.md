# 同 — same, equal

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

同 conveys sameness, equality, or agreement. 同じ is the everyday word for "the same"; 同時 means "the same time."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the inner line and [口](口.md) gathered under one frame because they belong to the same group. This is a memory aid, not a historical analysis.

## Common words

- [同じ](../words/同じ.md) おなじ — same
- 同時 どうじ — same time; 同時に どうじに — simultaneously
- 同意 どうい — agreement; consent

## Notes

Before an ordinary noun, use 同じ directly: 同じ本 おなじほん, "the same book," not 同じな本.

Distinguish the outer frame from the more detailed gate in [問](問.md). [何](何.md) has a separate person component on the left rather than an enclosing frame.
