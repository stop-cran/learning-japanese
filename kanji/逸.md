# 逸 — deviate; stray

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

逸 is about veering off course, away from a target, a path or a topic. KANJIDIC2 lists “deviate”, “idleness”, “leisure”, “miss the mark”, “evade”, “elude”, “parry” and “diverge”. In [逸れる](../words/逸れる.md) それる, JMdict's first sense is “to turn away; to bear off; to veer away; to swerve from; to miss (e.g. a target)” and its second is “to deviate (e.g. of a conversation); to digress; to go astray; to wander”; JMdict notes that the word is usually written in kana. Kanjipedia's third sense is ③それる。はずれる。わがまま。みだら。, and JMdict glosses 逸脱 いつだつ “deviation; departure” (entry 1167750).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [免](免.md), "exempt; be spared," sits on ⻌, the stroke group that wraps the left side and the bottom; KanjiVG shows 𠂊 at the top of 免 and 儿 at its bottom. Picture a traveller spared from the main road who veers off and strays down a side path. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 免 or ⻌ contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| イツ | on | 逸話 いつわ — anecdote; story; episode; JMdict entry 1167810. Not selected for questions here. |
| そ.れる | kun | [逸れる](../words/逸れる.md) それる — to veer off; to digress (usually written in kana). This word establishes the selected reading. |
| そ.らす | kun | 逸らす そらす — to turn away (one's eyes, face, etc.); to avert; to divert (e.g. one's attention); JMdict entry 1167650 notes that it is usually written in kana. Not selected for questions here. |
| はぐ.れる | kun | 逸れる はぐれる — to lose sight of (one's companions); to stray from; JMdict entry 2801130, a separate entry with the same spelling, notes that it is usually written in kana. Not selected for questions here. |

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only イツ, with 逸話, 逸品 and 逸する. そ.れる, そ.らす and はぐ.れる are outside the official table; Kanjipedia marks all three as outside it. Practice nevertheless selects そ.れる, because 逸れる is the word article that declares 逸; JMdict notes that the word is usually written in kana. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- JMdict glosses 逸品 いっぴん “excellent article; fine item; rare beauty; masterpiece; gem” (entry 1167760), and its first sense of 逸する is “to lose (a chance); to miss (a chance)” (entry 1587490).
- Kanjipedia also lists イチ and はしる, うしなう, すぐれる and はやる outside the Joyo table. The Daijisen character entry gives イチ as an on reading, and Daijisen has the headword いち【▽逸】.
- Kanjipedia's sixth sense is transliteration of foreign words, with the example 「独逸(ドイツ)」; JMdict entry 1599200 marks 独逸 as ateji and rarely used.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings いっ, はや and へん; the Daijisen character entry gives the names すぐる, とし, はつ, はや, まさ and やす.

These twelve cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [逃](逃.md) (“escape; flee”) and [避](避.md) (“avoid; evade”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 逸 is ①はしる。にげる。にがす。, KANJIDIC2 lists “evade” and “elude” for 逸, and it lists “evade” for 避 too.
- [走](走.md) (“run”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 逸 begins with はしる, and the 走 card lists はし.る.
- [失](失.md) (“lose; loss”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 逸 includes うしなう, and JMdict's first sense of 逸する is “to lose (a chance); to miss (a chance)” (entry 1587490).
- [脱](脱.md) (“undress; remove; escape”): JMdict glosses 逸脱 いつだつ “deviation; departure” (entry 1167750), and KANJIDIC2 gives 脱 “escape from”.
- [秀](秀.md) (“excel; outstanding”), [傑](傑.md) (“outstanding; excellence; hero”) and [優](優.md) (“gentle; superior”): Kanjipedia lists すぐれる for 逸 outside the Joyo table; KANJIDIC2 gives 傑 the kun reading すぐ.れる and 秀 “excel” and “excellence”, the 優 card lists すぐ.れる, and JMdict glosses 秀逸 しゅういつ “excellent; superb; first-rate” (entry 1332620).
- [暇](暇.md) (“free time; leisure; time off”), [悠](悠.md) (“leisurely; distant”) and [楽](楽.md) (“music, enjoyment, ease”): KANJIDIC2 lists “leisure” for 逸, 暇 and 悠; Kanjipedia's fifth sense of 逸 begins ⑤気楽に楽しむ。, and JMdict glosses 安逸 あんいつ “(idle) ease; idleness; leisureliness” (entry 1608240).
- [外](外.md) (“outside”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 逸 includes はずれる, and the 外 card lists はず.れる.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 会 (“meet”), 供 (“accompany, offer”), and 道 (“road, way”). Meeting and accompanying are the opposite of straying from one's companions, and 道 shares the radical 辵 with 逸 but names the road itself, not leaving it; none of them means to deviate or stray.
