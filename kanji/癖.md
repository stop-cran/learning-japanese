# 癖 — habit; quirk; peculiarity

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 疒 (no. 104) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

癖 is a habit or quirk of a person or thing. The linked [癖](../words/癖.md) くせ is JMdict's "habit (usu. a bad one); tendency"; "peculiarity; idiosyncrasy; mannerism; quirk"; "crease; wrinkle; curl; kink."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ヘキ, a biased habit, and lists くせ (ぐせ) in 口癖, 酒癖, 手癖 and 難癖.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the sickness radical 疒 (no card yet) sits at the upper left, over a part that looks like 辟. Think of a habit as a small ailment of behavior. This is a memory aid only and makes no claim about meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| くせ.に | kun | 癖に くせに — "and yet; though; when; in spite of," tagged "word usually written using kana alone"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

Joined to another word, くせ often becomes ぐせ: 口癖 くちぐせ, "pet saying; favorite phrase; stock phrase; catchphrase," and 酒癖 さけぐせ (JMdict also lists しゅへき). 手癖 can be read てくせ or てぐせ, and 難癖 なんくせ keeps くせ. In 潔癖 けっぺき, "fastidiousness; love of cleanliness," ヘキ becomes ペキ after the small っ; the [潔](潔.md) card links back here for it. 習癖, 病癖 and 奇癖 also use ヘキ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ヘキ with 習癖, 病癖 and 潔癖; くせ with 癖 and 口癖. Practice selects くせ. The other readings are shown with dictionary examples; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- [疲](疲.md), "grow tired," [痛](痛.md), "pain; hurt," and [療](療.md), "heal; cure," share the 疒 radical and supply this card's wrong answers.
- [習](習.md) and [性](性.md) appear in 習癖 and 性癖; with 慣, 常, 傾, 特, 殊, 異, 奇, 妙, 珍, 柄, 風 and 様 they overlap the habit, tendency and peculiarity senses and are kept out of this card's quiz options. 壁 also reads ヘキ and is never offered as a wrong answer.
- [口](口.md) and [酒](酒.md) supply くち and さけ in 口癖 and 酒癖.
