# 荒 — rough; wild; laid waste

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

荒 concerns roughness, wildness or a devastated state. [荒れる](../words/荒れる.md) あれる describes becoming rough or disordered; 荒らす あらす is transitive and can describe damaging or laying waste to something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine scattered plants over a broken-looking center and uneven marks at the bottom: a place no longer kept in order.

The canonical key 艸, no. 140, appears as 艹 at the top.

## Notes

- 荒ぶ すさぶ and 荒む すさむ are different godan verbs, ending in ぶ and む. The intensifying use can follow another verb's masu stem; the standalone words also have deterioration senses.
- 荒らし is a noun for an Internet troll/disturber and has suffix uses for vandalism or robbery. It is not the spelling 嵐 for a weather storm.
- 荒れる is intransitive, not the passive form of 荒らす. Match the complete construction and sense, rather than treating every English rough as one word.
