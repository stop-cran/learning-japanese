# 弾 — bullet; play strings; bounce

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (no. 57), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弾 is a bullet, playing an instrument by its strings or keys, and springing back; it also appears in words for censuring wrongdoing. [弾](../words/弾.md) たま is a bullet, [弾く](../words/弾く.md) ひく is to play a string or keyboard instrument and [弾劾](../words/弾劾.md) だんがい is impeachment or denunciation.

The Daijisen character entry gives five senses for ダン: playing an instrument by plucking its strings, as in 弾琴 だんきん, "playing on the koto," 弾奏 だんそう, "performance; playing on stringed instruments," and 連弾 れんだん, "four-handed performance (on the piano)"; flicking with a finger, as in 弾指 だんし, "snapping the fingers"; exposing wrongdoing and pressing hard, as in 弾圧 だんあつ, "oppression; suppression; pressure," 弾劾, 糾弾 きゅうだん, "censure; denunciation; (verbal) attack; blaming," and 指弾 しだん, "criticism; blame; rejection; disdain; contempt"; springing back, as in 弾性 だんせい, "elasticity," and 弾力 だんりょく, "elasticity; flexibility"; and the bullet of a gun, as in 弾丸 だんがん, "bullet; shot; shell," 弾薬 だんやく, "ammunition; ammo," 実弾 じつだん, "real bullet; live ammunition; live round," 銃弾 じゅうだん, "bullet (from a rifle)," 爆弾 ばくだん, "bomb," and 砲弾 ほうだん, "shell; cannonball." A separate entry uses だん to count bullets and installments, which JMdict glosses "counter for parts, stages, installments, etc. (of a story, series, project, campaign, etc.)" and "counter for bullets."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 弓, "bow," at the left, and [単](単.md), "single; simple," at the right. Imagine a bowstring snapping and a single shot springing off it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 弓 or 単 contributes meaning or sound, although 単 is read タン, a reading KANJIDIC2 also lists for 弾. Historical formation is not established here.

In 弾劾, 弾 supplies ダン and 劾 supplies ガイ. 弾 read たま stands alone as a word, with no okurigana; JMdict lists it as one spelling of たま beside 玉 and 球, with "bullet" as its fifth sense. In 弾く, 弾 writes ひ and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same spelling 弾く is also read はじく, a word JMdict marks as usually written in kana alone, so context decides between ひく for playing an instrument and はじく for flicking or repelling. 弾む gives はず with む, and 弾ける gives はじ with ける. After another element 弾き is read ひき, as in 三味線弾き, where 三味線 supplies しゃみせん; JMdict also lists 弾き ひき as a suffix, "player (of music, instrument, etc.)." The leading dash in -ひ.き marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ダン, with 弾力, 弾圧 and 爆弾 as examples; ひく, with 弾く and 弾き手 ひきて, "performer or player (of an instrument)," and the spelling contrast 弾く ⇔ 引く; はずむ, with 弾む and 弾み はずみ, "bounce; spring; rebound"; and たま, with 弾 and the spelling contrast 弾 ⇔ 玉, 球. タン, はじく, はじける, ただす and はじきゆみ are not in the official table, and it has no separate row for ひき. Practice selects ダン, ひ.く and たま, the readings with linked word articles; the other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words where an ordinary example exists. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid. The official Joyo table also prints a variant form of 弾 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [劾](劾.md), "censure; impeach," in 弾劾. The word 弾 is this kanji alone, and 弾く is 弾 with okurigana only.
- 弓, the key at the left, does not have a kanji card here. [引](引.md), "pull; draw," has the same 弓 at the left and is also read ひ.く; the official table contrasts 弾く with 引く ひく, "to pull; to tug; to lead (e.g. a horse)." [単](単.md), "single; simple," is the shape at the right of 弾 and is read タン. [段](段.md), "step; level," is read ダン and タン. All are different kanji.
- [玉](玉.md), "jewel; ball," [球](球.md), "ball; sphere," [爆](爆.md), "burst open; explode," [圧](圧.md), "pressure; oppress," [劾](劾.md), "censure; impeach," [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible," [批](批.md), "criticize; critique," [演](演.md), "perform; stage," and [遊](遊.md), "play," overlap the senses "bullet," "burst open," "oppression," "censure," "blame," "criticism," "performance" and "play" that 弾 and its words carry; 玉 and 球 are other spellings in the JMdict entry for たま, 爆 appears in 爆弾, 圧 in 弾圧 and 劾 in 弾劾. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "bullet," "play" or "censure" as exclusive to any one character.
