# 柳 — willow

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

柳 writes **willow**, [柳](../words/柳.md) やなぎ. It can name willows generally, not only trees with the familiar hanging branches of a weeping willow.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside the divided shape 卯. Imagine branches hanging on two sides of a willow. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not an account of its historical construction.

## Notes

- In the verse name 川柳, 川 supplies セン and 柳 supplies リュウ. Learn the whole word rather than reading the poem name かわやなぎ.
- The same written 川柳 also has the separate tree reading かわやなぎ, and an archaic かわやぎ. Those readings do not invalidate せんりゅう in a poetry context.
- Compare [卵](卵.md), egg, which lacks 木 and has interior dots. [印](印.md), stamp or mark, has a simpler left side; [相](相.md), mutual or aspect, has a closed 目 on the right.
- A separate astronomical noun 柳 りゅう names one of the traditional Chinese lunar mansions. It is not the reading used for an ordinary willow tree in the linked やなぎ article.
