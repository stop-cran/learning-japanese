# 診 — diagnose; examine medically

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

診 concerns examining a person's medical condition and assessing what is
wrong. [診察](../words/診察.md) しんさつ is a medical examination;
[診断](../words/診断.md) しんだん is diagnosis or diagnostic assessment.
The examination and the resulting assessment are related, not identical
stages or freely interchangeable nouns.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [言](言.md) on the left
and [人](人.md) above three slanting 彡 strokes on the right.
Imagine a clinician listening to a person and checking three marks in a
record. This is an invented scene for the modern shape, not a historical
or phonetic explanation.

| み.る | kun | [診る](../words/診る.md) みる - examine medically; diagnose |

Both readings are selected with linked examples. 診る is a transitive
ichidan verb: 診 supplies み and る is written in kana. Its spelling
specifies medical examination, not every ordinary act of seeing.

## Notes

- [珍](珍.md), rare/precious, shares the right-hand 人-over-彡 group
  but has a different left side. [参](参.md), participate/visit,
  also has lower slanting strokes; [記](記.md), record in writing,
  shares 言 but has a different right component.
- [察](察.md), [調](調.md) and [試](試.md) have examination or
  investigation meanings; [見](見.md) and [観](観.md) overlap looking,
  and [判](判.md) judging. Their broader meanings make them poor wrong
  answers for this card, without making all their compounds medical terms.
- [看](看.md) connects with watching over and caring for someone.
  The 看 card declares this direct exclusion pair. Looking after a patient
  is not necessarily diagnosing them; learn the specific verbs and compounds.
