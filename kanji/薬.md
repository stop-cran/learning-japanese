# 薬 — medicinal drug

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

薬 is medicine: something used as a drug or remedy. Its vocabulary also extends to chemicals,
so a compound containing 薬 does not by itself name something safe to take as medicine.
[薬局](../words/薬局.md) やっきょく names a pharmacy.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 艹 above [楽](楽.md). Imagine a plant being prepared
as a remedy. This is not a claim about the character's historical construction or the effectiveness
of a particular plant. 艹 is the visible form of radical 艸.

Compare [楽](楽.md), which has no plant top. [葉](葉.md) shares that top,
while [業](業.md) shares only a generally dense silhouette; inspect the interior strokes.
