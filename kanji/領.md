# 領 — territory; jurisdiction

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

領 concerns territory or jurisdiction and occurs in words involving leadership or receiving. 領土 is territory, 大統領 a president, and 領収 the receipt of money.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 令-shaped left part as a marker of authority beside 頁. This is a modern-shape memory story, not historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical radical is 頁, no. 181, on the right.

## Notes

- The えり example belongs to the collar family that also contains 襟 and 衿. The pinned source does not mark 領 rare; recognition does not establish how frequent or usual that spelling is.
- Do not transfer the collar gloss to every リョウ compound. Conversely, 領土's territory sense does not give the native collar noun a land meaning.
- Compare [冷](冷.md), cold/chill. Its 令-shaped part is similar, but its left component and whole-character meaning differ.
