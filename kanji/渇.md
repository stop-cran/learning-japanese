# 渇 — thirst; dry up; crave

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

渇 is thirst: a dry throat that wants water, and, figuratively, a strong wanting. The linked [渇く](../words/渇く.md) かわく has two JMdict senses: "to be thirsty; to feel thirsty," and "to thirst for; to crave," often as ～に渇いた. The word article keeps it apart from the ordinary drying verb 乾く かわく, "to get dry."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for カツ. One example of each:

- a throat that is dry: 飢渇 きかつ, "hunger and thirst; starvation";
- wanting something badly: 渇愛 かつあい, "thirst; craving; desire";
- water that runs dry: 渇水 かっすい, "water shortage."

More examples are in the Notes. Daijisen also gives a second on reading, カチ (呉), with 消渇 しょうかち, "diabetes (in traditional Chinese medicine)": a whole word, not asked here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part is the same shape that sits at the right of [掲](掲.md). Imagine a dry throat that wants water, with a wavy 氵 beside it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カツ | on | 渇望 かつぼう — craving; longing; thirsting; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| かわ.く | kun | [渇く](../words/渇く.md) かわく — be thirsty; crave |

In 渇く, 渇 writes かわ and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 渇き かわき is "thirst" and then "craving; thirst (for love, knowledge, etc.); hunger," and 喉が渇く のどがかわく is "to be thirsty"; JMdict's phrase entry lists 喉が渇く and 喉が乾く as spellings. In 渇水, 渇望 and 飢渇, 渇 supplies カツ; the small っ in かっすい is part of the same reading. 枯渇 こかつ, "drying up; running dry," is spelled 涸渇 in a form JMdict marks "rarely used kanji form"; 渇仰 かつごう, "adoration; reverence; esteem," is read かつごう or かつぎょう.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カツ with 渇望 and 渇水, and かわく with 渇く and 渇き, followed by ⇔ 乾く, which marks the contrast with the other かわく. Practice selects かわ.く, the reading of the linked word article. カツ is shown with a dictionary example and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. Kanjipedia also lists むさぼ.る, but KANJIDIC2 does not, so it is not in this card's metadata. The table shows the older form 渴 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- 渇く is 渇 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji. In the examples above, [飢](飢.md), "starve; be hungry," supplies キ in 飢渇, [枯](枯.md), "wither; dry up," supplies コ in 枯渇, [望](望.md), "hope," supplies ボウ in 渇望, [愛](愛.md), "love," supplies アイ in 渇愛, [仰](仰.md), "look up; revere," supplies ゴウ in 渇仰, and [消](消.md), "extinguish; erase," supplies ショウ in 消渇.
- [掲](掲.md), "put up; display; publish," shares the right-hand part with 渇; [湯](湯.md), "hot water," and [渡](渡.md), "cross over," also have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [乾](乾.md), "dry; desiccate," also reads かわ.く and is the spelling JMdict gives for 乾く, "to get dry"; the two かわく words are different. [干](干.md), "dry; ebb," [枯](枯.md), [燥](燥.md), "parch; dry up," overlap drying out. [欲](欲.md), "desire," [恋](恋.md), "romantic love; longing," [憧](憧.md), "long for; yearn; admire," and [求](求.md), "seek; request," overlap craving, and [飢](飢.md) overlaps hunger and thirst. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "thirst," "dry up" or "crave" as exclusive to any one character.
