# 瓶 — bottle; jar

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 瓦 (no. 98) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

瓶 names bottles, jars and related containers. The familiar standalone word is びん. A separate native reading かめ refers to an earthenware pot, so spelling alone does not identify the reading or the exact kind of container.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the left-hand upright framework beside the 瓦-shaped right side; imagine a bottle standing next to a pottery tile. This associates the modern parts with containers without claiming their historical roles.

The indexing key is 瓦, no. 98, visible on the right.
