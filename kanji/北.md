# 北 — north

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 匕 (no. 21) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

北 is commonly explained as two people standing back to back: the oldest forms show two figures turned away from each other.
The original meaning was "back, turn one's back", and the later sense "north" is explained by houses facing south, so that the
back is north. This account is the usual one, and the same character survives with the older meaning in 敗北 はいぼく (defeat, "turning
the back"). The key 匕 here is a classification of the right-hand shape and not a meaning.

## Notes

- ホク often becomes ほっ before a k-sound: 北海道 ほっかいどう (ホク + カイ), 北極 ほっきょく (ホク + キョク).
- The four directions: [東](東.md), [西](西.md), [南](南.md), 北, listed as 東西南北 とうざいなんぼく. Intercardinal words put north or south first: 北東 ほくとう, 南西 なんせい.
- Look-alikes: 化 (change) has 亻 and 匕, and 北 is two symmetrical halves. The two mirrored halves of 北 are a helpful way to tell them apart.
- 北京 is ペキン in Japanese (Beijing, a Chinese-based reading), not ほっきょう.
