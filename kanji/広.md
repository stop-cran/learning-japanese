# 広 — wide, spacious

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

広 concerns width, space or spreading. [広い](../words/広い.md) describes something spacious or wide; related verbs describe spreading something or its becoming widespread.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 广 shelter with only a small ム-shaped bundle inside, leaving plenty of space. This is a modern visual mnemonic, not an origin claim.

## Notes

[店](店.md) has the same enclosing 广 but a different interior. [公](公.md) has a similar lower ム shape without the enclosing stroke.

広い contrasts with 狭い せまい, "narrow, cramped." Unlike the general size word 大きい おおきい, it emphasizes breadth or available space. A 広い部屋 (ひろいへや) is a spacious [room](../words/部屋.md).
