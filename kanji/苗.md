# 苗 — seedling; young plant

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (as 艹, no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

苗 names a **seedling or young plant**, especially one being raised for planting out. [苗](../words/苗.md) なえ can refer to rice seedlings, but is not limited to rice. 苗木 (なえぎ) specifies a sapling or young tree.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the plant-shaped 艹 above a small [田](田.md)-shaped growing plot. Imagine young plants waiting to be moved into a field. This is a memory aid, not a historical derivation.

The canonical radical 艸 appears in its upper form 艹.

## Notes

- [芽](../words/芽.md) め is a sprout, shoot or bud; it can occur on a mature tree. 苗 presents a young plant, not every bud on an established plant.
- Do not translate 苗字 literally as "seedling character." It is the same family-name word as 名字, with a different spelling.
- The dash in なわ- marks a bound form, not a separately pronounced syllable or a standalone word なわ.
- [猫](猫.md), cat, adds 犭 to the left of 苗. [留](留.md), retain or stay, and [畜](畜.md), livestock, have different shapes above their lower 田.
