# 牛 — cattle, cow

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 牛 (no. 93) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

牛 names cattle. The word 牛 (うし) can refer to a cow, bull, or ox; it is not limited to an adult female animal.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper strokes as a cow's horns and the vertical as the center of its face. This is a mnemonic for the present shape, not a verified account of its origin.
