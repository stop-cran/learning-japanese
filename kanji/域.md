# 域 — region; range

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

域 concerns an area, region, range or limit. 地域 ちいき is an area or region; [区域](../words/区域.md) くいき is a designated area or zone.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 土 marking out a region beside the boxed and crossing strokes on the right.

## Notes

- This source inventory supplies イキ and no kun-reading. Do not invent a native reading to match the idea of land.
- Compare [城](城.md), castle. A castle may occupy an area, but castle and region are different meanings.
