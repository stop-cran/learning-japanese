# 講 — lecture; association

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

講 concerns lectures and organized associations. Learn it through complete words such as 講義, lecture, and 講堂, lecture hall.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 言 on the left delivering a lecture, while the stacked horizontal bars and lower box-like shape on the right hold rows of lecture notes. This is a visual mnemonic, not a claim about historical components or phonetics.

## Notes

- 講義 and 講演 name a lecture or speaking activity; 講堂 names the hall. Shared 講 does not make the three nouns interchangeable.
- The standalone noun 講 こう can name a Buddhist lecture meeting, a religious association or a mutual financial-assistance association. Do not force the school-lecture sense onto all uses.
