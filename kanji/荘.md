# 荘 — villa; manor; solemn

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

荘 has two useful meaning groups. It can describe solemn or imposing grandeur, as in 荘厳 そうごん, and it can refer to a country residence, villa, or historical estate. In [別荘](../words/別荘.md) べっそう, the word means a holiday home; the reading is ソウ for 荘 and the whole word has a small っ after ベツ. A 別荘 need not be a grand mansion.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ソウ only. KANJIDIC2 additionally records ショウ and チャン, while Digital Daijisen uses ショウ for words such as 荘園 and gives そう as a suffix for apartment or inn names. It gives チャン only as a Mahjong counter suffix. Do not merge these distinct scopes.
- KANJIDIC2 records そ as a name reading. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings たか, たかし, and まさ. Kanjipedia also lists the non-Joyo kun reading しもやしき; the word 下屋敷 is not written with 荘 and does not establish that reading in a common word.
- “Solemn” overlaps [厳](厳.md), “strict; severe; solemn.” “Villa” and “manor” overlap the broad residence labels [家](家.md), “house; home,” and [宅](宅.md), “residence.” These three kanji are excluded from meaning choices.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 犬 (“dog”), 日 (“sun; day”), and 文 (“sentence; writing”); none means a villa, manor, or solemn quality.
