# 荘 — villa; manor; solemn

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

荘 has two useful meaning groups. It can describe solemn or imposing grandeur, as in 荘厳 そうごん, and it can refer to a country residence, villa, or historical estate. In [別荘](../words/別荘.md) べっそう, the word means a holiday home; the reading is ソウ for 荘 and the whole word has a small っ after ベツ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the grass crown 艹, also at the top of [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," sits above 壮, whose right side is [士](士.md), "warrior; professional." Imagine a warrior's country house standing among grassy fields: a villa. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 艹 or 壮 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ソウ only. KANJIDIC2 additionally records ショウ and チャン, while Digital Daijisen uses ショウ for words such as 荘園 and gives そう as a suffix for apartment or inn names. It gives チャン only as a Mahjong counter suffix. Do not merge these distinct scopes.
- KANJIDIC2 records そ as a name reading. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings たか, たかし, and まさ. Kanjipedia also lists しもやしき outside the Joyo table, matching its third meaning, a temporary residence in the countryside (別宅); no checked JMdict word supports it as a reading of 荘, so it is not selected.
- “Solemn” overlaps [厳](厳.md), “strict; severe; solemn.” “Villa” and “manor” overlap the broad residence labels [家](家.md), “house, home,” and [宅](宅.md), “residence.” Digital Daijisen also describes 荘 as a lodging place, Kanjipedia gives やど・旅館 (inn) as its fourth meaning, and KANJIDIC2 lists “inn”, so [宿](宿.md), “lodging,” is excluded as well. KANJIDIC2 lists “dignified” for 荘, and Kanjipedia's first sense is おごそか・いかめしい (solemn; dignified), which overlaps [凜](凜.md), “dignified; bracing cold.” KANJIDIC2 lists “villa” for 寮 and “cottage” for both 荘 and 亭, and JMdict glosses 館 (やかた) as “mansion; palace; manor house; castle,” so [寮](寮.md), “dormitory; quarters,” [亭](亭.md), “pavilion; restaurant building,” and [館](館.md), “hall; large building,” could also be defended as answers. These eight kanji are excluded from meaning choices. The [舎](舎.md) card (“house; lodging; hut”) declares the 舎/荘 pair, because KANJIDIC2 gives both characters “inn” and “cottage”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 犬 (“dog”), 日 (“sun, day”), and 雨 (“rain”); none means a villa, manor, or solemn quality.
