# 想 — thought; conception

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

想 concerns thoughts and what one pictures or conceives. [予想](../words/予想.md) よそう is an
expectation or prediction; 想像 そうぞう is imagination. 感想 かんそう is an impression or reaction.
The whole word tells you what kind of thinking is involved.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** [相](相.md), with 木 and 目 shapes, sits over [心](心.md).
Imagine seeing a tree and keeping its image in mind. This is a mnemonic, not a derivation.
