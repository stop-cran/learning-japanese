# 棚 — shelf; ledge; trellis

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

棚 is a shelf: a board laid across to hold things. JMdict gives the word たな two senses: "shelf; ledge; rack" and "trellis." The linked [棚](../words/棚.md) たな is a shelf or rack, [戸棚](../words/戸棚.md) とだな is a cupboard with doors and [本棚](../words/本棚.md) ほんだな is a bookshelf.

The Digital Daijisen page for 棚 has no character entry with an on reading, only word entries. Its たな entry glosses 1) a horizontal board for holding things, 2) a frame of wood or bamboo that vines are trained over, as in 藤棚 ふじだな, "wisteria trellis; wisteria arbor (arbour); wisteria pergola," and 3) a step in a rock wall wide enough to stand on. A separate Daijisen entry たな【店／棚】 is a different word, "shop" or "rented house," written with 棚 as a non-standard form; this card does not teach it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md) at the left, wood, and two [月](月.md) shapes side by side at the right. Imagine boards fixed to a wooden frame. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ホウ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; the only JMdict word found with ほう is 氷棚 ひょうほう, "ice shelf," an unusual entry that gives no ordinary example |
| たな | kun | [棚](../words/棚.md) たな — shelf; ledge; rack |
| -だな | kun | [戸棚](../words/戸棚.md) とだな — cupboard; cabinet; [本棚](../words/本棚.md) ほんだな — bookshelf; bookcase |

In 戸棚, 戸 supplies と, "door," and 棚 is voiced to だな; in 本棚, 本 supplies ホン and 棚 again ends in だな. The dash in -だな marks a form that follows another element. It is the voiced form of たな, so the quiz lists たな only. 棚 read たな stands alone as a word, with no okurigana, the kana that follow a kanji. 大陸棚 たいりくだな, "continental shelf," is another word with だな. 棚機 たなばた is a rarely used kanji form of the Star Festival word 七夕, and 葡萄棚 ぶどうだな, "grapevine trellis," is a whole word: neither is split into per-kanji readings here.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows たな, with 棚, 戸棚 and 大陸棚. It has no row for ホウ. Practice selects たな, the reading explained by the linked articles; being outside the practice set does not make ホウ invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [戸](戸.md), "door," in 戸棚, and [本](本.md), "book," in 本棚. The word 棚 is this kanji alone.
- [架](架.md), "rack; bridge; overhead; imaginary," [台](台.md), "stand, machine counter," [板](板.md), "plank; board," [机](机.md), "desk; writing table," [卓](卓.md), "table; eminent," and [段](段.md), "step; level," overlap the shelf, rack, support and ledge senses. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. [店](店.md), "shop," is also read たな but is a different word, so it is never an option. Do not treat "shelf," "rack" or "ledge" as exclusive to any one character.
- [梅](梅.md), "ume; Japanese apricot," has the same 木 at the left, [明](明.md), "bright, clear," is built from 日 and 月 and is not the same part, and [構](構.md), "build; stance," is another kanji with 木 at the left. All are different kanji.
