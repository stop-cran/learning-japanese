# 甘 — sweet; pamper

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 甘 (no. 99) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

甘 connects sweetness with indulgence or leniency. 甘い あまい describes a sweet taste and can also concern being too lenient; 甘える and 甘やかす concern dependence or pampering. These related meanings do not make the words grammatically interchangeable.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Treat the box-like outline and its inner horizontal stroke as a small box holding a sweet. This is only a modern-shape association.

## Notes

- 甘える is an intransitive ichidan verb, with the person relied on often marked with に; 甘えます あまえます. 甘やかす is transitive godan, with the person pampered marked with を; 甘やかします あまやかします.
- The linked [甘い](../words/甘い.md) article teaches あまい, sweet-tasting. Its うまい homograph belongs to a separate usually-kana entry, with a tasty/delicious sense especially associated with 旨い, 美味い and 甘い.
- The same うまい entry also has a skilled sense especially associated with 上手い and 巧い. A shared entry does not establish that every spelling is equally suitable for every sense, or that sweet and tasty are the same judgment.
