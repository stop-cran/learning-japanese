# 絹 — silk

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[絹](../words/絹.md) きぬ names silk: the fibre and fabric woven from it.
The material is not defined merely by a smooth or shiny appearance.
Digital Daijisen distinguishes the fibre from the woven fabric; 日本国語大辞典
also records a broader cloth sense.
The 糸 radical is compressed at the left as ⺯.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the thread-shaped left
side beside a small 口-shaped box over a 月-shaped folded piece of silk.
This is an invented scene for the modern outline, not a historical claim
that the right side means a mouth and the moon.

## Readings

| Reading | Kind | Example or disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケン | on | 絹布 けんぷ, silk cloth, and 正絹 しょうけん, pure silk. These verified dictionary examples have no linked support article here; this reading is not practised. |
| きぬ | kun | [絹](../words/絹.md) きぬ, silk; the single-character noun directly supports this practice reading. |

Both full reading lists remain unchanged. 絹糸 has two dictionary readings,
きぬいと and けんし; its corn-silk sense is restricted to けんし.
Neither pronunciation licenses assigning all its senses to the other.

## Common words

- 絹布 けんぷ - silk cloth.
- 正絹 しょうけん - pure silk.

## Notes

- [衣](衣.md) きぬ is a separately attested clothing word. Sharing きぬ does not make clothing and silk identical. [綿](綿.md) めん names cotton, but the separate わた has a broader wadding sense that can include silk. This positive secondary sense motivates a direct guard, not a claim that cotton and silk are the same material.
- 生絹 has separate complete entries for すずし and きぎぬ/せいけん. 紅絹 もみ names red silk lining. These whole-word readings are not new general readings of 絹; JMdict also allows the single spelling [紅](紅.md) for もみ.
- 字通 includes きぎぬ among its character glosses, records historical かとり and かく lookup forms, and gives a use in place of 羂, a snare or setting a snare. These source-positive records remain qualified; they do not replace modern 絹 きぬ. The missing context does not justify declaring them nonexistent.
- [布](布.md), cloth, [糸](糸.md), thread/yarn, and [維](維.md), fibre, have broader ranges. [紅](紅.md) has the separate red-silk-lining noun. [捕](捕.md), capture, and [掛](掛.md), hang, are conservative precautions around the historical snare/set-a-snare gloss. These direct guards protect broad choices, not universal synonymy.
- [紙](紙.md), paper, [約](約.md), promise/approximately, and [級](級.md), grade/school class, share the left-hand thread shape but do not name silk.
