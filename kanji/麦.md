# 麦 — wheat; barley

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 麥 (no. 199) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

麦 names cereal grains such as wheat and barley. 小麦 identifies wheat, while 大麦 identifies barley; the whole crop name matters rather than translating every 麦 word as wheat.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the upper bars as rows in a field and the lower crossing strokes as harvested stalks. This is only a modern-shape memory story, not a historical explanation.

The canonical indexing radical is 麥, no. 199; the visible modern form on this card is 麦. Keep the source's indexing label distinct from the displayed glyph.

## Notes

- 麦むぎ is broader than the particular crop 小麦こむぎ. 大麦おおむぎ is barley, not a large variety of wheat.
- The バク example 麦芽ばくが is malt. It should not be confused with the native reading むぎ merely because both refer to cereal-related material.
- Compare [表](表.md), surface/chart, and [夏](夏.md), summer. Their upper or lower strokes can look similar, but their whole-character meanings differ.
