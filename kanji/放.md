# 放 — release

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (as 攵, no. 66) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

放 concerns letting something go or sending something out. 放す はなす can mean setting something free;
[放送](../words/放送.md) ほうそう means broadcasting. Light or a scent can also be given off with 放つ はなつ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the left-hand 方 opening outward toward 攵 as something is released.
This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not evidence for its origin or phonetic structure.

- 放す はなす — release; set free
- 放つ はなつ — let loose; emit

## Notes

鳥を放す (とりをはなす) means "release a bird."
The same sound はなす in [話す](../words/話す.md), "speak," belongs to a different verb and spelling.

[方](方.md) is the left part of 放, not the whole character.
[旅](旅.md) shares that left part, while [教](教.md) shares the right-side 攵.
Keep both sides in view when distinguishing these characters.
