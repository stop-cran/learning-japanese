# 空 — sky; empty

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 穴 (no. 116) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

空 has two especially useful branches: the sky above us and space that is empty or available.
The word [空](../words/空.md) (そら) means "sky"; 空 (から) means "empty." Choose the reading with the word and context.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** imagine the 穴-shaped top as an opening above an
[工](工.md)-shaped frame, with nothing filling the space.

## Common words

- [空](../words/空.md) そら — sky

- 空気 くうき — air; atmosphere
- 空港 くうこう — airport

## Notes

The same spelling has different readings: 席が空く (せきがあく) means "a seat becomes available";
おなかが空く (おなかがすく) means "get hungry." Learn these as phrases, not freely interchangeable readings.

[究](究.md) has 九 at the bottom; 空 has 工. [天](天.md) also relates to the sky or heavens,
but そら is the straightforward everyday word for the visible sky.
