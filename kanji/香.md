# 香 — fragrance; incense

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 香 (no. 186) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

香 concerns fragrance, scent or incense. 香り is an aroma, 香る means to be fragrant, and 香車 names the shogi lance piece.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 禾-shaped top as a scented plant over a 日-shaped container. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not an etymological account.

The complete character is the canonical radical 香, no. 186.

## Notes

- 香 か occurs especially in expressions of the form Nの香; 花の香 (はなのか) is the fragrance of flowers. The linked 香り article teaches the separate complete noun かおり.
- The キョウ row uses 香車 きょうしゃ, a shogi piece. The separate 香きょう entry is an abbreviation for that lance, not a recommendation to pronounce every incense word きょう.
- 香る is an intransitive godan verb. 香り is a noun; writing its final り or the verb's final る is part of the complete word spelling.
- Compare [季](季.md), seasons, which also has a 禾-shaped upper part but a different lower part and whole meaning.
