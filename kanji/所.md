# 所 — location

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 戶 (as 戸, no. 63) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

所 points to a place or location. [場所](../words/場所.md) ばしょ names a place,
住所 じゅうしょ an address, and 台所 だいどころ a kitchen. The surrounding word tells you what kind of place is meant.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use 戸 as a doorway marking a location and notice the separate 斤 shape to its right.
This is a visual memory aid, not an explanation of the historical combination.

## Notes

Read 台所 as だいどころ, not as a sequence of the two most familiar on readings.
Its 所 has the voiced どころ reading.

[戻](戻.md) places 大 below 戸; 所 instead has a right-hand 斤.
[新](新.md) and [近](近.md) also contain 斤, but their other parts and meanings differ.
[場](場.md) shares a place-related meaning with 所; both appear in 場所 (ばしょ), "place."
