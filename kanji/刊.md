# 刊 — publish; issue

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

刊 concerns publishing or issuing a publication. 刊行 かんこう is publication. 週刊 しゅうかん and 月刊 げっかん specify weekly and monthly publication schedules.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the left-hand 干 shape as a printed notice beside the thin right-hand blade shape. This picture is not an account of how the character developed.

The canonical radical 刀 appears as the right-hand form 刂.

## Notes

- 週刊 しゅうかん means weekly publication. Do not confuse its spelling and meaning with 週間 しゅうかん, a period of weeks.
- Publishing a work and [印刷](../words/印刷.md) いんさつ, printing it, are related but different activities. 刊行 does not simply mean producing ink marks.
