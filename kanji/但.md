# 但 — however; provided that

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

但 adds a condition or an exception to what has just been said: "however," "but," "provided that." [但し](../words/但し.md) ただし introduces such a qualification. JMdict glosses it "but; however; provided that" and marks it as usually written in kana alone. KANJIDIC2 gives 但 the meanings "however" and "but."

The Digital Daijisen has no character entry for 但. The only Daijisen block on its Weblio page is the word ただ, written 唯, 只 or 但, with 但 marked as a spelling outside the official table. Used as a conjunction, that ただ adds a condition to what was said or holds part of it back, like ただし. JMdict's entry for ただ lists 但 as a rarely used kanji form, marks the word as usually written in kana, and glosses the conjunction "but; however; nevertheless; except."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [人](人.md), "person," as 亻 at the left, and at the right [日](日.md), "sun,day," above a single horizontal line. Imagine a person watching the sun rise over the horizon and adding a condition: you may go out, but be back by noon. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 人 or 日 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| タン | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2 but not in the official table; JMdict has it only in the railway name 播但線 ばんたんせん, "Bantan Line (Hyogo Prefecture)" |
| ただ.し | kun | [但し](../words/但し.md) ただし — however; provided that |

In 但し, 但 writes ただ and し is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the dot in ただ.し marks where the okurigana begins. In 但し書き ただしがき, "proviso," 書き かき is voiced to がき; this voicing of the first sound of a later element is called rendaku. JMdict also lists shorter spellings of that word, such as 但書.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ただし, with 但し and 但し書き as examples. タン is not in the official table. Practice selects ただ.し, the reading of the linked word article 但し; タン has no verified ordinary example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 但し is 但 with okurigana only; it contains no other kanji.
- [昼](昼.md), "daytime,midday," has 日 above a horizontal line at the bottom, like the right side of 但. [伸](伸.md), "stretch; lengthen," and [他](他.md), "other," have the same 亻 at the left. All are different kanji.
