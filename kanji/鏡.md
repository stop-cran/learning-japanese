# 鏡 — mirror; lens

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鏡 is a mirror, a surface for seeing one's own reflection, and it is also the last part of many names for instruments built on lenses. [望遠鏡](../words/望遠鏡.md) ぼうえんきょう is a telescope, [眼鏡](../words/眼鏡.md) めがね is eyeglasses, [鏡](../words/鏡.md) かがみ on its own is a mirror and [顕微鏡](../words/顕微鏡.md) けんびきょう is a microscope.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for キョウ. The first is an instrument for seeing one's own reflection, as in 鏡台 きょうだい, "dresser; dressing table; vanity table," 鏡面 きょうめん, "mirror surface; lens surface," and 明鏡 めいきょう, "polished mirror; clear mirror." The second is an instrument that uses lenses, as in 眼鏡, 顕微鏡 and 望遠鏡, and the third is a model to learn from, as in 鏡鑑 きょうかん, "paragon; mirror."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [金](金.md), "gold, money," at the left and, at the right, 竟, which does not have a kanji card here and is built from [音](音.md), "sound," over 儿, which does not have a kanji card either. Imagine a polished metal plate that gives back whatever stands in front of it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 金 or 竟 contributes meaning or sound, although the same right-hand 竟 appears in [境](境.md), "boundary; border," which is also read キョウ and ケイ. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キョウ | on | [望遠鏡](../words/望遠鏡.md) ぼうえんきょう — telescope; [顕微鏡](../words/顕微鏡.md) けんびきょう — microscope |
| ケイ | on | 明鏡 めいけい - polished mirror; clear mirror; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| かがみ | kun | [鏡](../words/鏡.md) かがみ — mirror |

In 望遠鏡, 望 supplies ボウ and 遠 supplies エン, and in 顕微鏡, 顕 supplies ケン and 微 supplies ビ; 鏡 reads キョウ in both. In 鏡 on its own, 鏡 writes the whole of かがみ. The Daijisen entry also lists 手鏡 てかがみ, "hand-mirror," 水鏡 みずかがみ, "reflective water surface; reflecting water," and 鏡板 かがみいた, "panel; scene-panel," under かがみ, but none of them has a word article here. It labels キョウ, which it also spells キャウ in historical kana, as 呉音, the older Chinese-derived (Go-on) reading, and it gives no ケイ; that row rests on JMdict, which lists めいけい as the third reading of 明鏡 after めいきょう and みょうきょう.

In 眼鏡 めがね the pair is read as one unit: JMdict marks めがね as gikun or jukujikun, a reading assigned to the word as a whole, and がね is not one of the readings KANJIDIC2 lists for 鏡, so the word gives 鏡 no reading of its own.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キョウ, with 鏡台, 望遠鏡 and 反射鏡 as examples, and its remarks column lists 眼鏡 めがね as a special reading; it also shows かがみ, with 鏡. Practice selects キョウ and かがみ, the readings with linked word articles; ケイ, which the Joyo table does not list, is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [望](望.md), "hope," and [遠](遠.md), "far," in 望遠鏡, the 眼 in 眼鏡 and the 顕 and 微 in 顕微鏡, which do not have kanji cards here.
- [金](金.md), "gold, money," is the key at the left, and it is also the key at the left of [銀](銀.md), "silver," and [鈍](鈍.md), "dull; blunt." [境](境.md), "boundary; border," shares the right-hand 竟, and KANJIDIC2 lists キョウ and ケイ for 境 and for 竟 itself, the same two on readings as 鏡. All are different kanji.
- [映](映.md), "reflect, project," [照](照.md), "illuminate; shine," [像](像.md), "image; statue," [例](例.md), "example; precedent," and [則](則.md), "rule; follow a model," overlap the ideas of reflecting light, showing an image and serving as a model that 鏡 and its words involve. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "mirror" as exclusive to any one character.
