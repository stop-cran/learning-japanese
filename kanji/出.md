# 出 — go out, exit

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 凵 (no. 17, "open box") · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The common explanation is a foot (止) stepping out of a pit or cave (凵). The modern shape looks like two stacked
山 ([山](山.md)) but is not formed from them. It means "go out", "come out", "put out" and "emerge".
