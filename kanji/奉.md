# 奉 — offer respectfully; serve

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

奉 can mean to offer something respectfully or to serve. 奉仕 is service to others. The ブ in the historical title 奉行 (ぶぎょう) belongs to the same character.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare [春](春.md), which ends in 日, and [奏](奏.md), which has a different lower part. In 奉, follow the central vertical through the lower horizontal strokes.

## Notes

Compare [献](献.md) (offer or dedicate) and [仕](仕.md) (serve). In 奉仕 the idea is what one does for others. It need not mean giving them an object.

Follow the lower strokes: [春](春.md) ends in 日, and [奏](奏.md) ends differently again. [棒](棒.md) puts its wood element to the left of a shape like 奉. [供](供.md) can also suggest offering, but neither that gloss nor “serve” makes 奉仕 a word for an object placed on an altar.
