# 莉 — jasmine in compounds

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (as 艹, no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

莉 is a character used in **jasmine names**, especially 茉莉 まつり and [茉莉花](../words/茉莉花.md) まつりか, "Arabian jasmine." Learn it inside these words rather than treating 莉 as an ordinary standalone Japanese noun.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Spot the plant-shaped 艹 above [利](利.md). The plant top can remind you that this character belongs in a flower name; 利's "benefit" meaning is not a literal definition of jasmine.

## Notes

- The indexing radical is 艸, no. 140; its visible top form is 艹.
- [利](利.md), benefit or advantage, has no 艹. [荷](荷.md), load or baggage, and [苦](苦.md), bitter or suffering, share the plant top but have different lower components and meanings.
- The source's grade-9/name-use classification does not make the documented plant compounds personal names or authorize guessed name readings.
