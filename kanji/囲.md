# 囲 — surround; enclose

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

囲 connects surrounding something with an enclosure. 囲む かこむ and 囲う かこう can both mean enclosing or surrounding; the noun 囲い かこい can name an enclosure or fence. 範囲 はんい extends the idea to a range or scope.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the inner crossed strokes fenced in by the outer enclosure. This is an association with the current shape, not a historical explanation.

The outer frame is 囗 (enclosure, no. 31). It is not an oversized instance of the mouth radical 口.

## Notes

- 囲む and 囲う are both transitive verbs in the enclosing senses. Do not describe them as an intransitive/transitive pair just because their endings differ.
- 囲い かこい is a noun in the enclosure sense, not an i-adjective meaning enclosed.
- 範囲 may describe an abstract scope without any physical fence. A component mnemonic is not a restriction on every compound's meaning.
