# 傾 — tilt; incline

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

傾 concerns leaning, tilting, or an inclination. 傾く かたむく describes something tilting; 傾ける かたむける describes tilting something. 傾向 けいこう extends the inclination idea to a tendency or trend.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the person-shaped 亻 leaning toward the larger 頃 block on the right.

The canonical radical 人 takes the left-hand form 亻.

## Notes

- かたぶく is an outdated or obsolete reading in the source; it is not a recommended replacement for the modern かたむく.
- 傾げる can be read かしげる or かたげる. 首を傾げる くびをかしげる means tilt one's head, and can suggest puzzlement; do not infer that all 傾 verbs have the same conjugation.
