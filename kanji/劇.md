# 劇 — drama; play

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

劇 concerns drama or a play. 劇場 げきじょう is a theater, and 悲劇 ひげき is a tragedy. 劇的 げきてき means dramatic; the word need not describe a staged performance.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the 虍-shaped top and 豕-shaped lower block as an actor's elaborate costume beside a prop blade, 刂.

The classical indexing radical 刀 is written as 刂 on the right.

## Notes

- 劇場 names the venue; 劇 names a play or drama. They do not have the same grammatical role in every sentence.
- 悲劇 can describe tragedy, not only a staged play. A character association helps recall a word but does not restrict all its uses to a theater.
