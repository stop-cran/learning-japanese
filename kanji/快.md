# 快 — pleasant; agreeable

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

快 concerns a pleasant or agreeable feeling. 快適 かいてき means comfortable or pleasant, while 愉快 ゆかい can mean enjoyable or amusing. 快い こころよい is an adjective for something pleasant, agreeable or refreshing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the left-hand heart form 忄 and the open angular shape on the right; imagine a heart feeling pleasantly unburdened. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a verified origin.

The canonical radical is 心; its visible left-hand form here is 忄.

## Notes

- 快い is an い-adjective; the final い remains kana. Its complete reading is こころよい, not かいい.
- 快適 and 愉快 have their own grammar and uses; the shared character is not a rule making the words interchangeable.
- Compare [決](決.md), decide: it has the water form 氵 instead of 忄.
