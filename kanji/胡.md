# 胡 — foreign; dubious

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

胡 points to peoples and goods from beyond China, and to things that are dubious. KANJIDIC2 lists “barbarian” and “foreign”. Kanjipedia's first sense is えびす。中国の北方・西方に住む異民族。 (other peoples living to the north and west of China), and Digital Daijisen's first begins 中国で、北方または西方の異民族 (in China, the other peoples to the north or west). Digital Daijisen's second sense is 外国産の (produced abroad), with 胡椒 and 胡麻 as examples, and under the reading ウ it gives いいかげんな (careless; unreliable), with 胡散 and 胡乱. Kanjipedia's second sense begins でたらめ。うたがわしいこと。 (nonsense; something doubtful). [胡椒](../words/胡椒.md) こしょう is “pepper” in JMdict (entry 1267600), which marks the word as usually written in kana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [古](古.md), "old," made of [十](十.md), "ten," and [口](口.md), "mouth," stands on the left, and [月](月.md), "moon, month," a variant of [肉](肉.md), "meat; flesh," stands on the right. Picture an old traveller from a far-off land telling tales by moonlight: exotic, and a little dubious. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 古 or 月 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- 胡 is not in the 2010 Joyo table, and Digital Daijisen labels it ［人名用漢字］.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings えびす and くる, and Digital Daijisen gives the name reading ひさ; these are name uses, not quiz readings.
- Kanjipedia also gives the readings えびす, でたらめ, みだり, ながいき and いずくんぞ, and Digital Daijisen gives ［訓］えびす; these are not quiz readings here.
- The source's grade-9 classification concerns name-use kanji. It does not mean every use is a personal name or that the character is ordinary everyday spelling. Personal-name readings are not taught here.

These eleven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [外](外.md) (“outside”): KANJIDIC2 lists “foreign” for 胡, Digital Daijisen's second sense is 外国産の (produced abroad), and JMdict glosses 外国 がいこく (1203620) as “foreign country.”
- [異](異.md) (“different; unusual; strange”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 胡 includes 中国の北方・西方に住む異民族 and Digital Daijisen's first includes 北方または西方の異民族 (other peoples to the north or west); JMdict glosses 異民族 いみんぞく (1950140) as “different race; different ethnic group; different peoples”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “different” for 異.
- [洋](洋.md) (“ocean; Western style”): KANJIDIC2 lists “foreign” for 胡, and the third sense of 洋 よう (JMdict 2740960) is “foreign; Western; European.”
- [怪](怪.md) (“suspicious; uncanny”) and [嫌](嫌.md) (“dislike; suspicion”): JMdict glosses 胡散 うさん (2457840) as “suspicious” and 胡乱 うろん (1267590, usually written in kana) as “suspicious-looking; fishy”; KANJIDIC2 lists “suspicious” for 怪, and the title of 嫌 contains “suspicion.”
- [疑](疑.md) (“doubt; suspect”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 胡 includes うたがわしいこと (something doubtful), JMdict glosses 胡散臭い うさんくさい (1267540) as “suspicious-looking; shady; questionable; dubious; fishy”, and the 疑 card lists うたが.う.
- [何](何.md) (“what”): KANJIDIC2 gives the kun reading なんぞ for 胡, Kanjipedia's fifth sense includes なんぞ。なに。 (what), the second sense of 何ぞ なんぞ (JMdict 2270830) is “what”, and the 何 card lists なに.
- [乱](乱.md) (“disorder; disturbance”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 胡 includes みだり, and JMdict's みだり (2714830), marked archaic, lists 乱り among its kanji forms and glosses its first sense as “selfish; with disregard for order or rules”; the 乱 card lists みだ.る.
- [長](長.md) (“long, chief”) and [老](老.md) (“grow old”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 胡 is ながいき。老人。 (long life; an old person), JMdict glosses 長生き ながいき (1610940) as “longevity; long life”, and the 長 card lists なが.い.
- [蝶](蝶.md) (“butterfly”): Digital Daijisen's third sense says 「胡蝶」は昆虫の名。チョウ。 (胡蝶 is the name of an insect, the butterfly), JMdict glosses 胡蝶 こちょう (1572080, poetical term) as “butterfly”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “butterfly” for 蝶.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 甘 (“sweet; pamper”), 柔 (“soft; supple”), and 静 (“quiet”); sweetness, softness and quiet are qualities; none means foreign or dubious.
