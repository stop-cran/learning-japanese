# 鉢 — bowl; flowerpot

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鉢 is an open bowl, wider at the top, and a pot for growing a plant. The word [鉢](../words/鉢.md) はち, written with this kanji alone, is a bowl or a plant pot; JMdict glosses it "bowl; pot; basin," "(plant) pot; flowerpot" and "crown (of the head); skull."

The Daijisen character entry splits the readings. For ハチ it gives a container open at the top, as in 金鉢 かなばち, "metal bowl; iron helmet," 乳鉢 にゅうばち, "mortar," 火鉢 ひばち, "brazier; hibachi," and 手水鉢 ちょうずばち, "washbasin; stoup or font for cleaning hands." For ハツ it gives a monk's eating bowl, as in 衣鉢 いはつ, "mysteries of one's master's art; robes and a bowl," and 托鉢 たくはつ, "religious mendicancy; asking for alms; monk's begging."

A note says the character was first used to write a Sanskrit word by its sound, and the separate noun entry for はち traces the word to Sanskrit pātra through the transcription 鉢多羅, which has no JMdict entry. That noun entry lists a Buddhist monk's bowl, a dish deeper than a plate, a wooden rice tub, a plant pot, the skull, and the crown of a helmet.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [金](金.md), "gold, money," at the left, and [本](本.md), "book, origin," at the right, a tree with a mark at its root. Imagine a metal bowl with a small tree's roots planted in it: a flowerpot. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 金 or 本 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

The word 鉢 is this kanji alone, read はち with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows some kanji. This is the on reading ハチ used as a whole word, not a native kun reading: KANJIDIC2 lists no kun reading for 鉢, the Daijisen character entry gives ハチ as an on reading, and its noun entry describes はち as a shortening of the transcription 鉢多羅. After another element ハチ often becomes ばち, a sound change called rendaku, as in 火鉢, where 火 supplies ひ, and in 植木鉢 うえきばち, "flowerpot; plant pot," where 植木 supplies うえき. In 托鉢, 托 supplies タク and 鉢 supplies ハツ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ハチ, with 鉢 and 植木鉢 as examples, and ハツ, with 衣鉢. Practice selects ハチ, the reading of the linked word article; ハツ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The only common word, 鉢, is this kanji alone, so it contains no other kanji. 植木鉢, an example in the official table, adds [植](植.md), "plant; put in the ground," and [木](木.md), "tree, wood."
- [八](八.md), "eight," is also read ハチ and ハツ, and its はち sounds the same as the word 鉢. [鉄](鉄.md), "iron," and [鉛](鉛.md), "lead (metal)," have the same 金 at the left with a different shape beside it. All are different kanji.
- [器](器.md), "vessel; instrument," [皿](皿.md), "dish; plate," [盆](盆.md), "tray; basin," and [缶](缶.md), "tin can; container," overlap the senses "vessel," "dish," "basin" and "container" that 鉢 and its word carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "bowl" or "container" as exclusive to any one character.
