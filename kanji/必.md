# 必 — without fail

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

必 contributes certainty or necessity. 必ず かならず means "without fail," while
[必要](../words/必要.md) ひつよう describes what is needed.
Necessity and a prediction of what will happen are related ideas, but their grammar differs.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the long diagonal cutting decisively through the compact dotted
shape: it must go through. This is only a memory aid. The indexing radical is 心; the visible outline
is not a neatly detachable ordinary 心 plus one extra stroke.

## Notes

必ず modifies a statement or action: 必ず来ます (かならずきます), "I will definitely come."
必要 is used differently, as in 必要な物 (ひつようなもの), "necessary things."
[心](心.md), [少](少.md), and [母](母.md) offer dot-and-crossing-stroke comparisons, not equivalent meanings.
