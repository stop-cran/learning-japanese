# 昨 — yesterday; previous

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

昨 points back to the previous day or period. Its meaning is not limited to days:
昨年 さくねん means "last year."
Learn [昨日](../words/昨日.md) きのう, "yesterday," as a complete word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** pair the 日 at the left with the 乍 shape also seen in [作](作.md).
Imagine a mark beside yesterday's date. This is not a historical or phonetic analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| サク | on | 昨年 さくねん — last year; 昨夜 さくや — last night |

昨日 きのう is a special whole-word reading, not a separate kun reading of 昨.
昨日 can also be read さくじつ.

## Common words

- [昨日](../words/昨日.md) きのう — yesterday
- 昨年 さくねん — last year; marked formal in JMdict
- 昨夜 さくや — last night

## Notes

In [作](作.md), the left component is the person form 亻, not 日.
[晴](晴.md) and [時](時.md) keep 日 but change the right-hand shape.
Do not confuse "previous" with "early": an event yesterday could have happened late at night.
