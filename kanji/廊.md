# 廊 — corridor; covered passage

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53), wrapping the upper left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

廊 concerns a connecting corridor or roofed passage.
[廊下](../words/廊下.md) ろうか is a corridor or hallway; 回廊 かいろう
is a corridor or cloister. In 画廊 がろう, the complete word names an
art gallery.

English hall can mean a passage or a large room. The practical focus here
is the connecting passage, not a rule that every hall or lobby is 廊.
KANJIDIC2 also retains the gloss tower; the inspected modern examples
do not establish a general tower use, so none is invented here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice 广 covering a 郎-shaped
cluster. Imagine a covered passage along a building. The small
right-hand ⻏ belongs to the 邑-side form; this visual description is not
a historical explanation or a verified sound-component claim.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ロウ | on | [廊下](../words/廊下.md) ろうか - corridor; hallway |

Practice selects ロウ, also the Joyo reading. Keep the long ろう in
ろうか; 下 supplies カ. The compound does not mean downstairs or
literally under the corridor.

## Notes

- [庭](庭.md), garden/yard, [床](床.md), floor/bed, and [府](府.md),
  urban prefecture/administration, share 广 but have different interiors.
- [堂](堂.md) and [館](館.md) also display hall in their labels.
  This English ambiguity is protected in choices without treating
  a corridor and a large hall as the same Japanese word.
- Passage meanings connect with [路](路.md), [道](道.md) and
  [通](通.md). [塔](塔.md), tower/pagoda, is also excluded to protect
  the retained source gloss, not to assert an unverified modern example.
- The pinned canonical stroke file has twelve strokes. KANJIDIC2 also
  records eleven as an alternative count; this card uses twelve and
  does not present that annotation as an additional stroke-order variant.
