# 誤 — mistake; error

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

誤 marks an error or doing something incorrectly. In [誤解](../words/誤解.md) ごかい, the error lies in
the interpretation; in 誤字 ごじ, it lies in a written character or spelling.
The whole word tells you what went wrong.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** place 言 beside the distinctive 呉 shape and imagine checking
a statement for an error. This story is a memory aid, not an explanation of historical formation.

## Notes

誤解 is a mistaken understanding, not necessarily a lie or an intentional deception.
Compare [語](語.md): both right-hand parts contain a mouth-shaped 口, but the surrounding
strokes differ. [記](記.md) and [認](認.md) share 言, not the rest of the character.
