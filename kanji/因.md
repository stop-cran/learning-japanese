# 因 — cause; factor

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

因 concerns what brings something about or what something depends on.
Learn it first in [原因](../words/原因.md) げんいん, "cause," and 要因 よういん, "main cause; factor."
These words help identify what lies behind a result, rather than naming the result itself.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 大 inside the enclosure as the big factor behind a problem. This is an invented learning image, not an etymology.

## Notes

The outside is the enclosure radical 囗, not 口.
[困](困.md) contains 木 rather than 大; [回](回.md) contains another box.
理由 りゆう, "reason; grounds; motive," is a useful comparison with 原因, but explanations and causes do not always answer the same question.
