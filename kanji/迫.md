# 迫 — press; approach

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

迫 is about pressing in: drawing near, and pressing someone hard. KANJIDIC2 lists “urge”, “force”, “imminent” and “spur on”. In [迫る](../words/迫る.md) せまる, JMdict's first sense is “to approach; to draw near; to be imminent” and its second is “to press (someone for something); to urge; to compel”. Kanjipedia gives ①せまる。さしせまる。近づく。 and ②おいつめる。苦しめる。

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [白](白.md), "white," sits on ⻌, the stroke group that wraps the left side and the bottom. Picture a white car speeding along the road and pressing closer behind you. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 白 or ⻌ contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ハク, with 迫害 and 脅迫 among its examples, and せまる. JMdict glosses 脅迫 きょうはく “threat; menace; coercion; blackmail” (entry 1238110).
- Kanjipedia lists せる outside the Joyo table; JMdict entry 1475720 gives せる only as an out-dated or obsolete kana reading restricted to 迫る.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings さこ, せ, せこ, はさ, はさま and はざま; the Daijisen character entry gives the names せり and とお. That entry also gives さこ and せこ as kun readings, and Daijisen has the headword さこ【▽谷／▽迫】.

These seven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [寄](寄.md) (“draw near; stop by”) and [近](近.md) (“near”): JMdict 迫る sense 1 includes “to draw near”, and Kanjipedia's first sense of 迫 includes 近づく。
- [圧](圧.md) (“pressure; oppress”): JMdict glosses 圧迫 あっぱく “pressure” and “oppression; suppression” (entry 1153310).
- [促](促.md) (“urge; prompt”) and [督](督.md) (“supervise; urge”): KANJIDIC2 lists “urge” for 迫, 促 and 督.
- [詰](詰.md) (“pack tightly; press”) and [追](追.md) (“chase; pursue”): the 詰 card's title shares “press” with this card, and Kanjipedia's second sense of 迫 is ②おいつめる。苦しめる。, and JMdict glosses 追い詰める おいつめる “to corner; to drive to the wall; to run down; to track down; to chase after” (entry 1432300).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 出 (“go out, exit”), 入 (“enter, put in”), and 休 (“rest”). Going out moves away rather than drawing near, entering goes inside rather than closing in, and resting is the opposite of pressing hard; none of them means to press or approach.
