# 咲 — blossom; bloom

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

咲 is the character in 咲く さく, to bloom or flower. Learn that native verb for a flower opening. The dictionary example for ショウ below concerns laughter, a different lexical use.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a flower opening its branching strokes beside the small 口 on the left. The scene is for remembering the modern outline.

## Notes

- 咲く is intransitive: 花が咲く はながさく, flowers bloom. The flowers are the subject, not an object marked with を.
- 早咲き はやざき retains the written き. The source suffix reading -ざき does not instruct you to drop that kana.
- 巧咲 is included to document ショウ, not as an ordinary example of talking about flowers. Its dictionary entry does not establish that every modern use of 咲 means laugh.
- 咲いた さいた is the past form of 咲く; the sound change is a verb inflection, not another kanji reading to add to the metadata.
