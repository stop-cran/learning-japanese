# 困 — be in difficulty

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

困 is being in a difficult position: troubled, stuck, or unsure what to do.
[困る](../words/困る.md) こまる describes the person's difficulty; 困難 こんなん names difficulty or hardship more generally.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine 木 growing inside a frame with no room to spread. The cramped tree is a memory aid for being stuck, not a claim about the character's history.

## Common words

- [困る](../words/困る.md) こまる — be in difficulty
- 困難 こんなん — difficulty; difficult
- 困らせる こまらせる — put someone in difficulty; inconvenience

## Notes

The enclosure radical 囗 surrounds 木. In [因](因.md), the enclosed shape is 大 instead.
Keep the direction of the verbs separate: 困る is to be troubled; 困らせる is to put someone else in that position.
[難しい](../words/難しい.md) むずかしい can describe a difficult task without saying that a particular person is currently stuck.
