# 既 — already; previously

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 无 (no. 71), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

既 expresses that something has already happened or was true before a reference time. The linked adverb [既に](../words/既に.md) すでに means “already; previously; before”; JMdict marks it as usually written using kana alone.

Daijisen gives キ for “already,” as in 既成 きせい, and for “come to an end,” as in 皆既 かいき, “total eclipse; totality.” The kun reading すでに is “already.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice a compact, 日-like enclosure on the left and angular strokes to the right, where the radical 无 sits. Imagine looking back at something that has already happened. This is a memory aid only; the shape does not establish meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キ | on | 既成 きせい — established; existing; accomplished; 既婚 きこん — married; 既往症 きおうしょう — previous illness; 皆既 かいき — total eclipse; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| すで.に | kun | [既に](../words/既に.md) すでに — already; previously; before |

In 既成, 既婚 and 既往症, 既 supplies キ. In 皆既, it supplies キ in a word meaning “total eclipse; totality,” illustrating Daijisen's “come to an end” sense. In 既に, the whole adverb is すでに and に is written in kana.

The official Joyo table, the standard-use kanji list, shows キ with 既成，既婚，既往症 and すでに with 既に. It prints 既 in parentheses as a compatibility ideograph, not as a historical form. Practice selects すで.に because it is used in the linked article 既に. Readings outside this quiz selection are not invalid.

## Notes

- JMdict has a separate whole-word reading すんでに for the rarely used kanji spelling 既に; its glosses are “almost; nearly.” This is not a reading to split out of the selected adverb すでに.
- The distractors [良](良.md), [日](日.md) and [明](明.md) have familiar box-like or enclosure shapes that may look similar at a glance, but their meanings are good/favorable, sun/day and bright/clear. Their readings do not match すで.に.
- [前](前.md), [予](予.md), [先](先.md), [昨](昨.md), [旧](旧.md), [元](元.md) and [故](故.md) have titles related to what came before or is former; [完](完.md), [全](全.md), [終](終.md), [済](済.md) and [卒](卒.md) overlap completion; [在](在.md), [存](存.md) and [有](有.md) overlap existence. They are kept out of the quiz options so the time and completion senses do not make multiple answers defensible.
