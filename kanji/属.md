# 属 — belong; affiliated; genus

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44), at the top and left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

属 is belonging: being part of a group or class, being attached to something larger, or coming under it. [属する](../words/属する.md) ぞくする is to belong to or come under something, [金属](../words/金属.md) きんぞく is metal, and [附属](../words/附属.md) ふぞく is attachment or affiliation. JMdict glosses 属する "to belong to; to come under; to be affiliated with; to be subject to," and 附属 "being attached (to); being affiliated (to); belonging (to); going with"; that entry lists 付属 first and 附属 second. As a noun, 属 ぞく is "genus," a rank in biological classification.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ゾク. Following and coming within a range or group, as in 属国 ぞっこく, "vassal state; vassal nation; dependency," 帰属 きぞく, "belonging; ascription; attribution," 従属 じゅうぞく, "subordination; dependency," 所属 しょぞく, "belonging to (a group, organization, etc.); affiliation (with)," and 付属; and companions, kin and things of the same kind, as in 金属, 眷属 けんぞく, "(one's) family; dependents; household," and 尊属 そんぞく, "direct ancestors; ascendant." For ショク it gives two senses: joining or composing, as in 属文, a word JMdict does not list, and fixing one's eyes or hopes on something, as in 属望 しょくぼう, "having great hopes for (someone's future); expecting much of," and 属目 しょくもく, "paying attention to; taking note of; observing; watching." It notes that 属 in this second sense is used interchangeably with 嘱; JMdict lists 嘱望 and 嘱目 first. It gives the kun reading さかん but not つく or やから. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 尸, the radical, as a roof-like top with a long stroke down the left, and tucked under it the rest of the character, a part that has no card here. Imagine a small creature kept under the household's roof: it belongs to the house. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 尸 or the lower part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ゾク | on | [属する](../words/属する.md) ぞくする — belong to; come under; [金属](../words/金属.md) きんぞく — metal; [附属](../words/附属.md) ふぞく — attachment; affiliation; belonging to |
| ショク | on | 属する しょくする - to entrust (someone) with; to pin one's hopes on (someone's future); JMdict marks it a formal or literary term and lists 嘱する first; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| さかん | kun | 雅楽属 うたのさかん - alternate assistant court musician (ritsuryō system); JMdict marks it a historical term; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| つく | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict has no entry writing つく with 属; the everyday verb 付く つく, "to be attached; to be connected with; to adhere," is written with 付 |
| やから | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict spells やから 輩, a derogatory "group; bunch; lot; pack; set (of people); gang," or 族, a poetical "clan; family; one's kin," and has no entry writing it with 属 |

In 属する ぞくする, 属 supplies ゾク and する follows it as a verb ending. In 金属, 金 supplies キン and 属 supplies ゾク, and in 附属, 附 supplies フ and 属 supplies ゾク. In 属国, ゾク shortens to ぞっ, so the next sound is doubled: ぞっ + こく gives ぞっこく. The same spelling 属する is a separate, formal word when read しょくする, with 属 supplying ショク. 雅楽属 is read うたのさかん as a whole title, with 属 supplying さかん, the name of the lowest of four official ranks in the old ritsuryō system; Daijisen's word entry ぞく【属】 gives, as one of its senses, the さかん, written 主典, of certain ritsuryō offices; JMdict marks 主典 read さかん archaic, "secretary (lowest of the four administrative ranks of the ritsuryō system)." KANJIDIC2 writes さかん, つく and やから without a dot, so it does not mark which part would be okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ゾク, with 属性 ぞくせい, "attribute; property," 従属 and 金属 as examples. ショク, さかん, つく and やから are not in the official table. Practice selects ゾク, the reading with linked word articles; ショク and さかん are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words, and つく and やから have no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [金](金.md), "gold, money," in 金属, and [附](附.md), "affix; append," in 附属; 附属 is also written with [付](付.md), "attach." 属する is this kanji followed by する.
- The older form of 属 is 屬; the Daijisen character entry shows it in brackets after the headword, and the official table in parentheses.
- [層](層.md), "layer; stratum," [屋](屋.md), "roof, shop," and [履](履.md), "footwear; put on shoes; carry out," have the same 尸 at the top and left, with a different part under it. All are different kanji.
- [類](類.md), "kind; category," [種](種.md), "seed; kind," [族](族.md), "tribe, family group," [籍](籍.md), "register; membership," [員](員.md), "member, employee," [付](付.md), "attach," [係](係.md), "connection; person in charge," and [般](般.md), "general; kind; category," overlap the senses "genus," "kin," "membership," "attached" and "affiliated" that 属 and its words carry; 族 is also read ゾク, JMdict writes the poetical やから with 族, and 付 appears in the spelling 付属. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "belong" or "category" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first ゾク sense: 属性; 軍属 ぐんぞく, "civilian in military employ"; 係属 けいぞく, "connection; relation; association; affiliation"; 専属 せんぞく, "exclusive; attached to; specialist"; 直属 ちょくぞく, "direct control; direct supervision"; 転属 てんぞく, "changing assignments"; 配属 はいぞく, "assignment (of a person to somewhere); attachment (of a person to another unit, organization, etc.)"; and 隷属 れいぞく, "subordination; servitude; servility; vassalage; slavery." Of the second: 卑属 ひぞく, "lineal descendants."
