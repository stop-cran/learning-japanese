# 原 — original; plain

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 厂 (no. 27) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

原 has two useful threads: an original source or starting point, and an open plain or field.
[原因](../words/原因.md) げんいん belongs to the first thread: the cause from which an outcome arose.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a starting point under the 厂 overhang, with 白 above a 小-like base.
This labels visible shapes for memory, not the character's historical construction.

## Notes

The land sense of "plain" does not mean "unadorned."
[白](白.md) names just the white/白 shape, not the whole 原 character.
[元](元.md) also has an origin-related meaning; the surrounding word helps distinguish it from 原.
