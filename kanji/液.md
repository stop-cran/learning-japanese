# 液 — liquid, fluid

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

液 identifies liquid or fluid substances. It does not mean only water and need not describe a drink.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture drops of liquid at the left of 夜, night: a bottle still drips in the dark.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.
