# 準 — standard, conform

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

準 concerns a standard, level, or conformity to a model. As the prefix 準 じゅん, it can mean semi-, quasi-, or associate rather than fully the named status.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the upper-left water marks beside the bird-like shape and above 十 as a made-up instrument for checking a water level against a standard.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the upper left; the lower 十 extends beneath the upper structure.

## Notes

- The on/kun labels reproduce KANJIDIC2's fields, including its placement of じゅん.じる and じゅん.ずる. They are not a claim that the whole vocabulary item has a purely native origin.
- 準じる is an ichidan verb; 準ずる is its related -ずる form. Their meanings overlap; do not invent a strict semantic division between them.
- 準える is usually written in kana, なぞらえる; the written form is a recognition example, not a frequency recommendation.
- 準備 is preparation, while 水準 is a level or standard. The character contributes to these different compounds; they are not interchangeable words.
