# 泥 — mud; mire

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

泥 concerns mud or mire. 泥 どろ is the native substance noun, while 汚泥 おでい means sludge. 泥む なずむ is a dated verb for clinging or adhering; the religious word 泥洹 ないおん means nirvana, not a kind of mud.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside 尼 and imagine muddy water clinging to a surface. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not historical etymology.

The canonical radical 水, no. 85, appears as left-hand 氵.

## Notes

- 汚泥 おでい illustrates デイ. The initial でい in a word such as 泥水 でいすい does not independently establish the shorter デ reading.
- JMdict marks 泥む なずむ as dated in its cling/adhere sense. It also records 滞む as a rare alternative spelling; that spelling flag does not by itself mark the pronunciation rare.
- 泥棒 どろぼう is a thief. The linked article preserves that lexical meaning rather than translating the visible mud and stick components literally.
- Mud is wet soil; not every stain or dirty substance is 泥. Keep the native noun どろ separate from the specialized ナイ compound.
