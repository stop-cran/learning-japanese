# 列 — row; queue

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (as 刂, no. 18) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

列 is an ordered line or row of people or things. The standalone noun [列](../words/列.md) れつ
can name a queue, while 列車 れっしゃ names a train.
The "file" sense in dictionary glosses means a line of people or things, not a computer document.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the two upright strokes of 刂 as the straight edge along which a
queue lines up. This is a modern visual association, not the character's historical derivation.

## Notes

列車 is read れっしゃ, not れつしゃ: learn the small っ as part of the word.
[判](判.md), [利](利.md), and [到](到.md) also end in 刂.
Their shared radical is a visual clue, not a reason to treat their meanings as interchangeable.
