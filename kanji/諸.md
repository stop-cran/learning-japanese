# 諸 — various; many; together

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

諸 contributes various, many or several in compounds. The native もろ use can mean both or together; a single English gloss does not cover every compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture many people beside the 言 on the left, with the 者-shaped right side reminding you of people addressed together. This visual association is not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- 諸手 is もろて, both hands. Its entry also allows もろで; the spelling 双手 has an additional restricted reading そうしゅ. That restriction does not license そうしゅ with 諸手.
- The independent prefix entry 諸 もろ is marked usually written in kana. That flag is not attached to the separate 諸手 entry and should not be transferred to every compound.
- Compare [者](者.md), a person in a role, [著](著.md), writing or being remarkable, and [署](署.md), signing or a government office. The surrounding components change the character.
