# 首 — neck

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 首 (no. 185) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

首 is the neck in [首](../words/首.md) くび.
In compounds it need not name that body part: 首都 (しゅと) means a national capital.
首が痛いです。 (くびがいたいです。) — My neck hurts.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the [自](自.md)-like lower shape under the two short upper strokes and horizontal line.
Picture that whole stack above a neck. This is a modern memory aid, not an origin claim.

## Notes

手首 and 足首 are established names for joints; neither means the neck above your shoulders.
Compare [頭](頭.md) あたま for "head."
首 forms the inner block of [道](道.md), while [自](自.md) lacks its upper strokes.
[百](百.md) is a looser comparison of the enclosed lower shape.
