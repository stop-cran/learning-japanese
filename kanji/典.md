# 典 — reference texts; rules; ceremony

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 八 (the spreading bottom strokes, no. 12) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

典 appears in words for reference texts and established standards:
[辞典](../words/辞典.md) じてん is a dictionary, [古典](../words/古典.md)
こてん a classic work, and [典型](../words/典型.md) てんけい a typical
example or model. It also has a ceremony branch, as in 式典 (しきてん),
"ceremony." These are related uses, not one English translation that fits
every compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a reference text in the
upper divided enclosure, resting on two spreading supports below.
This remembers the modern outline; it does not establish a historical
origin or phonetic component.

## Notes

- 辞典 (じてん) explains words; 事典 (also じてん) is an encyclopedia
  or subject reference. The complete word 百科事典 can also be spelled
  百科辞典 without becoming merely a language dictionary.
- Learn デン in 香典 as part of こうでん. This example does not give a
  rule that changes every テン to デン after another kanji.
- Compare [具](具.md), which has a taller stacked box, and [曲](曲.md),
  which has a divided enclosure but no spreading feet. [共](共.md)
  has spreading lower strokes without 典's enclosed upper part.
