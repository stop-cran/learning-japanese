# 枚 — counter for thin flat objects

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

枚 is a counter used after a number for thin, flat objects such as sheets of paper, plates, or coins. 二枚 にまい is two such objects. Counting uses マイ; the separate バイ example below belongs to an archaic expression.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside 攵 and imagine tapping a stack of flat wooden pieces as you count them. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a verified origin.

## Notes

- Use 一枚 いちまい, 二枚 にまい and 三枚 さんまい. The linked counter article gives complete sentences; マイ does not change to バイ in these counts.
- 枚を含む ばいをふくむ is explicitly archaic in JMdict. It is retained to illustrate the source-listed バイ, not offered as ordinary conversation or an alternative counting pattern.
- The counter is not limited to paper. Conversely, an object being physically thin does not erase established counting conventions, such as [冊](冊.md) さつ for bound volumes.
