# 混 — mix, mingle

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

混 connects mixing and mingling with confusion or crowding. The verb family distinguishes something being mixed from someone mixing it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture two ingredients being compared beneath the upper box on the right, beside water, before they are mixed together.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- 混じる and 混ざる are intransitive godan verbs; 混ぜる is transitive ichidan. Do not teach 混じる and 混ざる as strictly separate kinds of mixture.
- The leading hyphen in -ま.じり marks a suffix use. 雨混じり あめまじり describes something mixed with rain; 混じり まじり contributes 'mixed with' after the preceding noun.
- 混む こむ means being crowded. Its reading is not inferred from まじる, and this spelling is not a blanket replacement for every use of [込む](../words/込む.md) こむ.
