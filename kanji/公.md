# 公 — public, official

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 八 (no. 12) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

公 concerns what is public or official rather than private. It appears in [公立](../words/公立.md), for public institutions,
and 公園, a public park.

The modern shape has [八](八.md) above ム.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper strokes opening out so that everyone can see what is below.
This is a mnemonic, not a historical derivation.
