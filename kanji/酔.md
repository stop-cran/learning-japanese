# 酔 — drunk; intoxicated

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 酉 (no. 164) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

酔 is being drunk, and also feeling sick from motion or being carried away by something. KANJIDIC2 lists “drunk”, “feel sick”, “poisoned”, “elated” and “spellbound”. Kanjipedia's senses are getting drunk or sick from alcohol, vehicles and the like; losing sensation through drugs, as in 麻酔 ますい; and 心を奪われる こころをうばわれる, which JMdict glosses “to be captivated by ...; to be fascinated with ...” (entry 1863390). In [酔う](../words/酔う.md) よう, JMdict's senses are “to get drunk; to become intoxicated”, “to feel sick (e.g. in a vehicle); to become nauseated” and “to be elated; to be exalted; to be spellbound; to be in raptures”. The noun [酔っ払い](../words/酔っ払い.md) よっぱらい is “drunk person; (a) drunk” or “being drunk; getting drunk”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [酉](酉.md), "Rooster zodiac sign," stands on the left; picture it as a squat jar. On the right is the shape 卆, which is [九](九.md), "nine," over [十](十.md), "ten." Picture someone beside the jar who loses count after nine cups, then ten, and is thoroughly drunk. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 酉 or 卆 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists スイ, with 酔漢 すいかん (“drunkard; drunken fellow”, JMdict entry 1653710), 麻酔 and 心酔 しんすい (“adoration; admiration”, JMdict entry 1360760), and よう, with 酔う, 酔い and 二日酔い ふつかよい (“hangover”, JMdict entry 1602760). It gives the old form 醉.
- Practice selects よ.う, the reading of the linked verb 酔う. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so よ.う supplies the one question for that stem, shown as よう; よ.い and よ are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with 酔い and the linked noun 酔っ払い. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading よい; Digital Daijisen gives no name readings for 酔. Name readings are not quiz readings.

These two cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [毒](毒.md) (“poison; venom”): KANJIDIC2 lists “poisoned” for 酔.
- [魅](魅.md) (“charm; bewitch”): KANJIDIC2 lists “spellbound” for 酔 and “fascination”, “charm” and “bewitch” for 魅; 酔う sense 3 includes “to be spellbound”, JMdict 酔わす よわす sense 2 is “to charm; to enchant” (entry 2634660), JMdict 陶酔 とうすい sense 2 is “being fascinated (by); being carried away (by); being enraptured (by); being intoxicated (with)” (entry 1450680), and Kanjipedia's third sense of 酔 is 心を奪われる.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 眠 (“sleep; sleepy”), 疲 (“grow tired”), and 泣 (“cry; weep; sob”); none means drunk or intoxicated.
