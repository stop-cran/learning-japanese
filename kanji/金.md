# 金 — gold, money

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **Phonetic:** often analysed with 今 (キン, コン) as the sound element · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

金 means "metal", "gold" and "money". The early form is usually explained as lumps of metal in the ground under a cover, and the modern form is sometimes read as a roof-like top (亼) over 土 (earth) plus two dots (nuggets in the earth), but the
history is debated, so take the nugget picture as a mnemonic. As a radical on the left side (釒, 金へん) it marks metals and metal objects: 銀 (silver), 鉄 (iron), 銅 (copper), 針 (needle).

## Notes

Weekday: 金曜日 is Venus day, from 金星 (Venus, "metal star") in the East Asian planet and five-element names: 日月火水木金土 ([日](日.md) [月](月.md) [火](火.md) [水](水.md) [木](木.md) 金 [土](土.md)).
Look-alike: 今 (now) resembles the top of 金, but the top of 金 is the roof-like 亼. 銀 ぎん is silver, and 銀行 ぎんこう is a bank.
