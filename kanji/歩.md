# 歩 — walk, steps

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 止 (no. 77) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

歩 concerns walking and steps. [歩く](../words/歩く.md) names the ordinary action of walking, while compounds can describe a walk or a place for pedestrians.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice [止](止.md) above the small spreading strokes. Imagine one foot planted above another taking a step. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not an account of its historical origin.

## Notes

In 散歩, ホ appears as ぽ; learn the compound's whole reading rather than reading each character unchanged.
Compare [止](止.md), which lacks the lower part, and [走](走.md), associated with running. 歩く identifies how someone moves; it does not by itself mean a leisurely outing.
