# 建 — build, construct

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 廴 (no. 54) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

建 concerns building or construction. It is the kanji to recognize in words about putting up a house or another building.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the upright, cross-barred center as a building's framework and the sweeping 廴 below it as a path around the foundations. This is a visual memory aid, not historical analysis.

## Notes

Compare 家を建てる いえをたてる (build a house) with 家が建つ いえがたつ (a house is built). [立](立.md) also has たつ and たてる, but is used for standing or setting things upright.

The lower outline is 廴, not the 辶 shape in [通](通.md); [起](起.md) has a different wrapping component, [走](走.md).
