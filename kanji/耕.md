# 耕 — till; plough

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 耒 (no. 127) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

耕 concerns tilling or cultivating agricultural land. 耕す describes working the soil, not every activity that may be part of farming.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 耒 at the left beside 井 and imagine working the soil along rows. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a verified historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical key 耒, no. 127, is the left-hand part. Do not replace it with the similar 木.

## Notes

- 耕す is a transitive godan verb ending in す: 耕します たがやします and 耕して たがやして. 土を耕す つちをたがやす identifies the soil being worked.
- 耕地 names land, while 耕作 names cultivation and can take する. The shared コウ does not make the complete words interchangeable.
- Compare the different left sides in [科](科.md), department/course, and [料](料.md), materials/fee. Their meanings do not follow from a general resemblance to 耕.
