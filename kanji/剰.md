# 剰 — remainder; surplus amount

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

剰 concerns a surplus or remainder. [過剰](../words/過剰.md) かじょう means excess: more than is needed or appropriate, not simply a large amount. Learn 剰 through compounds rather than treating it as an everyday standalone noun.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a compressed [乗](乗.md)-like block beside the blade-shaped 刂, as though cutting off a surplus. This invented scene is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジョウ | on | [過剰](../words/過剰.md) かじょう — excess; too much |
| あまつさえ | kun | 剰え あまつさえ — besides; to make matters worse. A rare kanji spelling of a dated adverb, usually written in kana and usually adding something negative. Recognition only. |
| あま.り | kun | KANJIDIC2 source-only lookup reading. JMdict lists 剰り あまり as a search-only spelling for lookup, not a recommended normal spelling; the noun means a remainder or surplus. |
| あま.る | kun | KANJIDIC2 source-only lookup reading. The character dictionary also lists あまる, but the ordinary verb taught here is [余る](../words/余る.md) あまる — be left over; its spelling does not establish a modern 剰る example. |

Only ジョウ is selected for reading questions. The other readings remain reference information, not alternative pronunciations of 過剰. Their exclusion does not mean that ordinary あまり or あまる is rare or invalid.

The source lists あまつさえ without an okurigana dot, but the rare lexical spelling above writes a final え. For the noun "remainder," learn 余り あまり or kana あまり, not the search-only spelling 剰り. This noun sense is read あまり, not あんまり; the related adverbial uses are a separate learning point.

## Common words

- [過剰](../words/過剰.md) かじょう — excess; too much
- 余剰 よじょう — surplus; remainder
- 剰余 じょうよ — surplus; remainder, including a mathematical remainder

## Notes

- The indexing radical is [刀](刀.md), visible here as right-hand 刂. [乗](乗.md), "ride; board," has a similar dense block without that extra right-hand blade. [剣](剣.md), "sword; double-edged blade," and [則](則.md), "rule; follow a model," share 刂 but have different left blocks.
- [余](余.md) also means surplus. The two characters' meanings overlap; do not invent a strict division between them. The useful distinction here is learning actual words and spellings.
- The older form 剩 has 12 strokes; this card teaches the modern 11-stroke 剰. Recognizing the older form does not change this card's drawing target.
