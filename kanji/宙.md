# 宙 — space; midair

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宙 concerns space or the air above the ground.
[宇宙](../words/宇宙.md) うちゅう means the universe or outer space.
宙に浮く ちゅうにうく can mean floating in the air and, figuratively,
being unsettled or undecided.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 宀 over [由](由.md).
Imagine the lower box suspended in the air under a roof. This is a shape
memory aid, not a claim that the word means "roof plus reason."

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チュウ | on | [宇宙](../words/宇宙.md) うちゅう - universe; outer space |

Practice selects チュウ. 宇 supplies ウ; the entire ちゅう belongs to 宙.
The small ゅ combines with ち, and the final う lengthens the vowel.
The frozen source has no kun reading.

## Common words

- [宇宙](../words/宇宙.md) うちゅう - universe; outer space
- 宙返り ちゅうがえり - somersault
- 宙に浮く ちゅうにうく - float in midair; remain unsettled
- 宙ぶらりん ちゅうぶらりん - suspended; in limbo

## Notes

- A separate dictionary use of 宙 concerns doing something from memory or
  by heart. Compare [覚](覚.md), memorize; this does not mean that memorizing
  and already knowing something by heart have the same grammar.
- Daijisen's 宇宙 supplement describes a classical account with
  [宇](宇.md) for spatial extent and 宙 for time extending from past to
  present. This explains the source's time gloss in that account; 宙 is
  not the ordinary replacement for [時](時.md) in a clock time.
  Because the source keeps that interval-of-time sense, 時 is still
  excluded from meaning choices.
- [空](空.md) and [天](天.md) also concern the sky. The English labels
  *air* on [気](気.md) and *space* associated with [間](間.md) can overlap,
  although air as a substance, an interval and outer space are different uses.
- [宅](宅.md), residence, [守](守.md), guard/obey, and [定](定.md),
  determine/settle, share 宀 but have different lower parts and meanings.
