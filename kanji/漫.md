# 漫 — aimless; rambling; comic

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

漫 is a loose, unfocused idea: doing something without a goal, or spreading out without a firm shape. It is also the first kanji of [漫画](../words/漫画.md) まんが, JMdict "cartoon; comic; comic strip; manga." The word article reads 漫 as "free or unrestrained expression" next to [画](画.md), which its card titles "picture, brushstroke."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for マン. One example of each:

- covering everything: 爛漫 らんまん, "in full bloom; in full glory; glorious; luxuriant; splendid";
- loose and without a grip: 散漫 さんまん, "vague; desultory; distracted; loose; half-hearted; diffuse; scattered";
- casual, as the mind goes: 漫画 まんが, "cartoon; comic; comic strip; manga";
- a character used to write a foreign word by sound: 浪漫 ろうまん, JMdict ateji (phonetic) reading, first sense "romance (e.g. Arthurian romances); heroic tale," a word JMdict marks "word usually written using kana alone."

More examples are in the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part is a taller shape with [日](日.md) at its top. Imagine water spreading out loosely with no goal. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| マン | on | [漫画](../words/漫画.md) まんが — cartoon; comic |
| みだり.に | kun | 漫りに みだりに — "without authority"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| そぞ.ろ | kun | 漫ろに そぞろに — "in spite of oneself; somehow; without knowing why; vaguely"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 漫画, 漫 supplies マン and 画 supplies ガ: まん + が. JMdict lists the spellings 漫画 and kana まんが, and also マンガ, tagged spec1. In 漫歩 まんぽ, "rambling; sauntering; strolling," 漫談 まんだん, "chat; desultory conversation," and 漫遊 まんゆう, "pleasure trip; tour," 漫 also supplies マン; the small ん in まんぽ and まんだん is part of the same reading.

Both kun readings are in JMdict as adverbs that are "word usually written using kana alone." 漫りに みだりに is written with 漫 only as "rarely used kanji form," among the spellings 妄りに, 濫りに, 猥りに and 乱りに, and its senses include "without reason; unnecessarily" and "recklessly; indiscriminately; arbitrarily." 漫ろに そぞろに writes 漫 as そぞ with ろに as okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The related 漫ろ そぞろ is "restless; on edge (and unable to concentrate); distracted," with すずろ and すぞろ recorded as "out-dated or obsolete kana usage." Daijisen's entry also lists すず and そぞろ as kun readings.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows マン with 漫画, 漫歩 and 散漫. It lists no kun reading for 漫. Practice selects マン, the reading of the linked word article. みだり.に and そぞ.ろ are shown with dictionary examples and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. Kanjipedia also lists バン, but KANJIDIC2 does not, and no ordinary word is verified for it here, so it is not in this card's metadata.

## Notes

- In 漫画, [画](画.md), "picture, brushstroke," supplies ガ. In the other examples, [散](散.md), "scatter," supplies サン in 散漫, [冗](冗.md), "superfluous; redundant," supplies ジョウ in 冗漫 じょうまん, "verbosity," [放](放.md), "release," supplies ホウ in 放漫 ほうまん, "whimsy; looseness; indiscretion; laxity," [歩](歩.md), "walk, steps," supplies ホ in 漫歩, [談](談.md), "converse; consult," supplies ダン in 漫談, and [遊](遊.md), "play," supplies ユウ in 漫遊. 漫然 まんぜん is "aimless; rambling; desultory; casual," with [然](然.md), "so; in that state," supplying ゼン. The kanji of 爛漫 and 浪漫 other than 漫 have no card linked here.
- [海](海.md), "sea, ocean," has 氵 at the left, and [水](水.md), "water," and [日](日.md), "sun, day," are the two parts named in the mnemonic above. They are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [散](散.md), [冗](冗.md) and [漠](漠.md), "desert; vague," overlap loose, vague or diffuse. [慢](慢.md), "arrogance; slackness," and [満](満.md), "full; satisfy," also read マン and are never offered as wrong answers; 慢 also overlaps looseness, and 満 overlaps filling. [画](画.md), [絵](絵.md), "drawing; painting," and [描](描.md), "draw; depict; sketch," overlap the comic sense, as does [滑](滑.md), "slide; slippery; smooth," in 滑稽 こっけい, "funny; comical"; [歩](歩.md), [旅](旅.md), "journey, travel," and [遊](遊.md) overlap rambling and touring. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
