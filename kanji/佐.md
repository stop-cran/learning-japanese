# 佐 — aide; assistant

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

佐 can mean an assistant or help. It appears in common compounds such as [補佐](../words/補佐.md), “aid; assistance; assistant.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 亻 stands beside 左; picture an aide standing beside a leader. This is only a visual cue. It does not claim that 左 contributes the historical meaning.

## Notes

左, 差 and 査 share the サ reading but mean “left,” “difference,” and “investigation.” 佐 overlaps with 助 in assistance-related glosses: 佐 usually points to an aide or assistant, especially in compounds such as 補佐, while 助 is the broader everyday character for helping or rescuing. 丞 (“assist; aid”) and 援 (“aid; assistance”) are also close in meaning. JMdict records 佐ける (たすける) as a rare spelling of 助ける; the usual spelling is 助ける (entry 1344410).
