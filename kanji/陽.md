# 陽 — sunshine

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (as 阝) (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

陽 connects sunlight with brightness and warmth. In [太陽](../words/太陽.md) たいよう it names the sun;
in 陽気 (ようき), brightness can describe a cheerful person rather than light.
太陽が出ています。 (たいようがでています。) — The sun is out.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture sunlight reaching the little 日-shaped window in the right-hand block.
The left 阝 is the visible form of the indexing radical 阜. This is a memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

- 陽光 ようこう — sunlight

## Notes

[院](院.md) and [際](際.md) share the left 阝 but have different right-hand blocks.
[場](場.md) shares 陽's right-hand shape, with 土 rather than 阝 on the left.
[日](日.md) can also mean "sun," so its meaning overlaps with 陽's sunlight-related meanings.
