# 街 — street; town

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 行 (no. 144) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

街 means a street or the town area along streets. KANJIDIC2 gives “boulevard,” “street,” and “town,” and Kanjipedia gives まち。まちすじ。まちなか (town; streets; town centre). JMdict lists 街 as a spelling of まち, “town; block; neighbourhood,” with “downtown; main street” restricted to the 街 spelling. In [街角](../words/街角.md) まちかど, JMdict gives “street corner”; the whole word reads まちかど, with まち for 街. JMdict also spells this word 町角.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [行](行.md), "go," is split into a left half and a right half, and between them stands 圭, two [土](土.md), "earth, soil," stacked one above the other. Imagine people going along a street, with plots of earth piled between the two sides of the road. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 行 or 土 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ガイ, カイ, and まち, and cross-references 町 for まち. Digital Daijisen labels ガイ 慣 and カイ 漢.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading また, a name-use form, not a quiz reading. Digital Daijisen's character entry gives no name readings.

These seven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [町](町.md) (“town, neighborhood”): KANJIDIC2 also gives 街 “town,” JMdict lists 街 as a spelling of まち (main spelling 町), “town; block; neighbourhood,” and 街角 is also spelled 町角.
- [道](道.md) (“road, way”), [路](路.md) (“path; route”), and [途](途.md) (“route; course”): JMdict glosses 街 read まち as “street; road,” and glosses みち, spelled 道, 途, or 路, as “road; path; street; lane; passage.”
- [市](市.md) (“city; market”): JMdict glosses 市街 しがい as “urban areas; the streets; town; city.”
- [区](区.md) (“ward; district”): JMdict glosses 街 read がい as “street; quarter; district; area.”
- [丁](丁.md) (“city block; even number; fourth”): JMdict's “block” for 街 read まち overlaps “city block.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 橋 (“bridge”), 庭 (“garden; yard”), and 池 (“pond; reservoir”); none means a street or a town.
