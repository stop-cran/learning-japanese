# 療 — heal; cure

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 疒 (no. 104) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

療 concerns treating or healing illness and injury. It appears in medical vocabulary such as 医療, medical care, and 治療, treatment. Mentioning treatment does not itself guarantee that the patient has been cured.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the layered strokes inside the 疒-shaped frame as the attention and equipment gathered around a patient. This is a modern visual memory scene, not an origin claim.

The canonical key is 疒, no. 104, forming the top-and-left frame.
