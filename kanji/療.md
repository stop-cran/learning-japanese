# 療 — heal; cure

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 疒 (no. 104) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

療 concerns treating or healing illness and injury. It appears in medical vocabulary such as 医療, medical care, and 治療, treatment. Mentioning treatment does not itself guarantee that the patient has been cured.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** use the [sickness radical 疒](https://www.japandict.com/radical/%E7%96%92)
at the top and left as an **illness/sickbed frame**. Inside it, turn the large
upper shape into a doctor with outstretched arms, the box in the middle into a
medicine tray, and the bottom strokes into instruments. Picture the doctor
actively treating the patient: this cues **treatment/healing**, not illness or
pain alone. This is an invented modern-shape scene, not an origin claim.

## Notes

- In [医療](../words/医療.md), the readings are イ + リョウ. Learn 療 in a whole word rather than treating isolated りょう as an ordinary independent verb meaning heal.
- Compare [病](病.md), illness, [痛](痛.md), pain, and [疲](疲.md), grow tired. They share a health context but do not mean medical treatment.
