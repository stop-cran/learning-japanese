# 聞 — hear, ask

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 耳 (ear, no. 128) · **Phonetic:** 門 (モン) · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

聞 combines 門 (gate) and 耳 (ear, [耳](耳.md)). Dictionaries commonly treat it as a phonetic compound in which 耳 gives the meaning
(hearing) and 門 gives the sound モン, which survives in the on reading. A mnemonic: an ear listening at a gate. It is a mnemonic,
not the etymology. It means "hear", "listen" and "ask" (hearing in order to find out).

## Notes

- 聞く covers three ideas: hear (音楽を聞く), listen to, and ask (先生に聞く). 聴く きく (listen attentively, music or a lecture) and 訊く きく (ask, a rarer spelling) are variants.
- Compare 聞こえる (a sound comes to your ears, involuntary) with 聞く (you listen or ask).
- Look-alikes: [間](間.md) (interval, 門 with 日 inside), 門 (gate) and 問 (question: 門 with 口), which share the 門 frame. Also [見](見.md) (see).
- In 聞 the 耳 sits inside the gate; in 問 (question) it is 口. 聞く きく is also a homophone of 効く きく (to be effective).
