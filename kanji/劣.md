# 劣 — inferior; worse

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

劣 expresses being inferior or worse in a comparison of ability, quality or another specified dimension. [劣る](../words/劣る.md) おとる means "be inferior; fall short." [優劣](../words/優劣.md) ゆうれつ covers both superiority and inferiority, not inferiority alone.

The comparison need not involve a contest or a completed defeat. 劣らない (おとらない), "not inferior; no worse," can make a favorable comparison without saying that two things are identical.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture [少](少.md)-shaped "less" above [力](力.md)-shaped strength as a reminder of falling short in one respect. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical derivation or a judgment of anyone's inherent worth.

## Notes

- [努](努.md), "make an effort," also has 力 below, but its upper part is 奴 rather than 少. Effort does not itself say whether the result is better or worse.
- [功](功.md), "merit; achievement," places [工](工.md) beside 力 rather than 少 above it. An achievement is not the same as inferiority.
- [省](省.md), "ministry; omit," has a similar upper 少-like outline but [目](目.md) below instead of 力.
- State the dimension of comparison when possible. A product can be inferior in one respect without being worse in every way; 劣 does not make every comparison a judgment of moral character.
