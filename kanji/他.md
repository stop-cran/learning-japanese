# 他 — other

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

他 directs attention to someone or something else.
[他人](../words/他人.md) たにん means another person or other people; 他の人 (ほかのひと) means "another person; other people."
The person or group being contrasted must come from context.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the left-hand person stands beside the broad, turning shape on the right: someone other than oneself.
This is an association with the modern layout, not a derivation.

## Notes

[地](地.md) has the same right-hand shape but 土 on the left.
[休](休.md) and [付](付.md) keep the person-shaped left side and change the right.
人 is the canonical radical label; its narrow left-side form here is 亻.
Do not read 他人 as ほかひと: its established reading is たにん.
