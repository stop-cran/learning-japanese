# 経 — pass through; sutra

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

経 appears in words about passing through time or experience, and also in the Buddhist word for a sutra, a religious text.
経る へる means passing or going through; 経験 けいけん means experience.
[経済](../words/経済.md) けいざい is the established word for economy or economics, not a literal description of time passing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** follow the left-hand 糸 as a thread through successive events.
This remembers one meaning of the modern character; it does not explain the history of all its uses.

## Notes

長い年月を経て (ながいねんげつをへて) means "after many years."
経て is the て-form of 経る, not a form ending in って.

[組](組.md), [紙](紙.md), and [約](約.md) share 糸.
For 経, compare the crossing strokes above the 土-shaped base on the right.
The Buddhist reading キョウ should not be substituted into 経済.
