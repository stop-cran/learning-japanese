# 植 — plant; put in the ground

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

植 concerns planting. 植える うえる is the transitive verb to plant something; 植わる うわる describes something being planted. 植物 しょくぶつ names plants or vegetation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside 直 and imagine setting a young tree straight in the soil. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified origin or phonetic claim.

## Notes

- Compare 木を植える きをうえる, plant a tree, with 木が植わっている きがうわっている, a tree is planted there. The object of the first construction is the subject of the second.
- 植える is ichidan, with 植えて うえて. 植わる is godan, with 植わって うわって; it is an intransitive verb, not the grammatical passive form of 植える.
- Compare [値](値.md), value or price: the left side changes from 木 to 亻.
