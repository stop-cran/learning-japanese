# 矛 — spear; polearm

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 矛 (no. 110) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

矛 names a **spear or polearm**, a weapon with a long shaft. It is familiar in [矛盾](../words/矛盾.md) むじゅん, "contradiction; inconsistency," whose conventional explanatory story contrasts a spear and a shield.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the long central stroke as a shaft and the angled strokes around it as the weapon's head and fittings. This is a drawing aid, not a reconstruction of an ancient weapon.

## Notes

- The ほこ dictionary family also records a festival-float sense, short for a float decorated with spears. That context-specific use does not mean that every float is a weapon.
- [予](予.md), beforehand, lacks 矛's lower-left diagonal. [柔](柔.md), soft or supple, places a related-looking upper shape above 木; [務](務.md), duties, has additional components to the right.
- A shield, 盾 たて, is a useful semantic contrast, not the meaning of 矛. The linked 矛盾 article explains the attributed story without treating it as an actual weapon test.
