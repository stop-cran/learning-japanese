# 枝 — branch; twig

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

枝 concerns a branch or twig. The native noun is 枝 えだ; 枝葉 しよう can name branches and leaves and also unimportant details.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside 支 and imagine a side branch supported by a tree. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a claim about etymology.

## Notes

- The linked article teaches えだ. The シ reading here is demonstrated in 枝葉, rather than treating the independent branch noun as if it were normally read し.
- 枝葉 can also be read えだは for branches and leaves. JMdict associates the figurative unimportant-details sense especially with しよう; these are not exclusive reading compartments.
- A branch office is normally [支店](../words/支店.md) してん, not simply the plant noun 枝; the English word branch spans different Japanese words.
- Compare [技](技.md), skill: its left-hand form is 扌, not 木.
