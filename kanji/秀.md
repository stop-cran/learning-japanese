# 秀 — excel; outstanding

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

秀 is standing out above the others: being excellent, or the best. [優秀](../words/優秀.md) ゆうしゅう is a な-adjective glossed in JMdict "superior; excellent; brilliant; outstanding," used for people, results and products. The word article glosses it "excellent; outstanding."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for シュウ, to stand out from the rest and be especially fine, as in 秀逸 しゅういつ, 秀才 しゅうさい, 秀作 しゅうさく, 優秀 and others listed in the Notes. It also lists ひいでる and ほ as kun readings. A separate Daijisen headword しゅう gives two senses: a person or thing that is excellent, and the top mark in a grading scale (秀, then 優, 良, 可).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 禾, the grain-plant key, at the left and [乃](乃.md), "thereupon; possessive no," at the right. Imagine one stalk of grain rising above the rest of the field. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 禾 or 乃 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シュウ | on | [優秀](../words/優秀.md) ゆうしゅう — excellent; outstanding |
| ひい.でる | kun | 秀でる ひいでる — "to excel; to surpass; to be superior; to be better (than others at something)"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 優秀, 優 supplies ユウ and 秀 supplies シュウ. For the kun reading, 秀でる is a JMdict Ichidan verb with a second sense, "to be conspicuous (esp. forehead and eyebrows); to be prominent; to stand out." There 秀 writes ひい and でる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. Daijisen also has a headword ほ, glossed as something that stands out or is better than the rest. It is a whole word, not a reading to split off, and KANJIDIC2 lists ほ only among name readings, as it does しゅ, しょう, ひで and ひでし.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シュウ, with 秀逸, 秀才 and 優秀, and ひいでる, with 秀でる. Practice selects シュウ, the reading of the linked word article. ひい.でる is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [優](優.md), "gentle; superior," in 優秀. 優 supplies ユウ and is also read すぐ.れる, so its title and reading overlap this card; it is in this card's quiz exclusions and is not offered as a wrong answer.
- [傑](傑.md), "outstanding; excellence; hero," [英](英.md), "English; outstanding," [卓](卓.md), "table; eminent," [凌](凌.md), "endure; surpass," [超](超.md), "exceed; surpass," and [越](越.md), "cross over; exceed," carry the senses "outstanding," "eminent," "surpass" and "exceed" that 秀 and 秀でる share. [才](才.md), "talent," and [最](最.md), "most," overlap 秀才, "bright person; talented student; prodigy," and Daijisen's "the best." The B147 card [粋](粋.md), "stylish; essence; pure," carries a similar sense "the best; cream," and is excluded on the 粋 card. All are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [木](木.md), "tree, wood," [私](私.md), "I; private," and [秒](秒.md), "second as a time unit," look plausible because 木 resembles the 禾 shape, 私 and 秒 are written with 禾 at the left. They are read ボク, モク and き; シ and わたくし; and ビョウ, and mean tree, I and second. None shares a reading or a meaning with 秀.
- Other kanji read シュウ, such as [秋](秋.md), "autumn," and [集](集.md), "gather," are never offered as wrong answers.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the シュウ sense: 秀抜 しゅうばつ, JMdict "outstanding; superb"; 閨秀 けいしゅう, "accomplished lady; talented woman; eminent woman"; and 俊秀 しゅんしゅう, "genius; prodigy; talented person." JMdict glosses 秀逸 "excellent; superb; first-rate," 秀才 "bright person; talented student; prodigy," and 秀作 "excellent (piece of) work." None of these has a word article here.
