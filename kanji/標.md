# 標 — mark; signpost; goal

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

標 is a mark that shows where to look or what to aim for. [標本](../words/標本.md) ひょうほん is a specimen or sample, [標準](../words/標準.md) ひょうじゅん is a standard or norm, [標識](../words/標識.md) ひょうしき is a sign or marker and [目標](../words/目標.md) もくひょう is a goal or target.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses: a mark or goal, as in 標的 ひょうてき, "target," 指標 しひょう, "index; indices; indicator," 商標 しょうひょう, "trademark," and 道標 どうひょう, "guidepost; signpost," besides the four words above; and showing something conspicuously, as in 標語 ひょうご, "motto; slogan; catchword," and 標榜 ひょうぼう, "standing for; advocating; championing; professing (to be)."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md), "tree, wood," at the left and 票 at the right, with 覀 above [示](示.md), "indicate," inside 票. Imagine a wooden post carrying a sign that people steer by. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 木 or 票 contributes meaning or sound, although 示 has a related meaning. Historical formation is not established here.

In 標本, 本 supplies ホン; in 標準, 準 supplies ジュン; in 標識, 識 supplies シキ; and in 目標, 目 supplies モク. 標 reads ヒョウ in all four. The spelling 道標 stands for two words: 道標 どうひょう reads 標 as ヒョウ, and 道標 みちしるべ reads it as しるべ; JMdict glosses both as "guidepost; signpost."

The Daijisen character entry also lists the difficult reading 標縄 しめなわ, which JMdict marks as a rarely used kanji form of しめなわ, "rope used to cordon off consecrated areas or as a talisman against evil," and the kun reading しるす, for which JMdict has 標す, "to leave (a mark, trace, etc.); to print; to stamp." KANJIDIC2 lists neither しめ nor しるす, so they are not part of the metadata here.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ヒョウ, with 標準, 標本 and 目標 as examples, and has no kun row for 標. Practice selects ヒョウ, the only reading with linked word articles; しるべ and しるし are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [本](本.md), "book, origin," in 標本, [準](準.md), "standard, conform," in 標準, [識](識.md), "knowledge; awareness," in 標識 and [目](目.md), "eye," in 目標.
- [木](木.md), "tree, wood," is the key at the left; 票 at the right has no kanji card here. [兵](兵.md), "soldier; troops," [氷](氷.md), "ice; freeze," [平](平.md), "flat; peaceful," and [表](表.md), "surface; chart," are also read ヒョウ, and [秒](秒.md), "second as a time unit," has the key 禾 at the left and is read ビョウ. All are different kanji.
- [印](印.md), "stamp; mark," [符](符.md), "token; sign; charm," [示](示.md), "indicate," [的](的.md), "target; adjectival suffix," [当](当.md), "hit; be on target," [準](準.md), "standard, conform," [規](規.md), "standard; rule," [導](導.md), "guide; conduct," and [栞](栞.md), "bookmark; guidebook," overlap the senses "mark," "sign," "target," "standard" and "guide" that 標 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "mark" or "sign" as exclusive to any one character.
