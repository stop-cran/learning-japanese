# 採 — pick; collect; adopt

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

採 concerns taking up or collecting something, including choosing a method or employing someone in particular words. 採る とる can mean adopting a proposal or picking and gathering things. 採用 さいよう can mean adoption or employment, while 採掘 さいくつ concerns mining.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside a tree-like lower shape with short strokes above it and imagine a hand gathering something from a tree. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.

## Notes

- 採る is a transitive godan verb: 採ります とります, 採って とって, 採らない とらない.
- [取る](../words/取る.md) とる is a broader taking verb. The shared pronunciation is not a rule to replace 取 with 採 in every expression.
- 採用's adoption and employment senses depend on what is taken up: a method, proposal or person. 採 alone does not mean that every compound concerns a job.
