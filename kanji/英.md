# 英 — English; outstanding

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (as 艹, no. 140) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

英 commonly points to English or Britain in words such as [英語](../words/英語.md).
It also has an "outstanding" or "excellent" sense; not every compound containing 英 is about Britain.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine an outstanding student wearing a leafy crown above the broad lower shape.
The crown-like top also appears in [花](花.md) and [茶](茶.md). This is a visual memory aid, not an origin claim.

## Notes

英語 means the language, including varieties spoken outside Britain. 英国 is the country name.
The lower part distinguishes 英 from [茶](茶.md); do not identify the character from its top alone.
