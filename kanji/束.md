# 束 — bundle

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

束 gathers separate things into a bundle. The noun 束 (たば) can describe a bunch of flowers or a bundle of papers.
In [約束](../words/約束.md) (やくそく), the whole word means a promise or agreement, not a physical bundle.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the central upright and spreading lower strokes tied together by the square in the middle.
This is a memory picture, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

[東](東.md) has a divided central enclosure; 束 has the simpler 口-like square.
束ねる is transitive: 花を束ねる (はなをたばねる), "tie flowers into a bunch."
The management sense extends beyond physically tying things.
