# 録 — record; register

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

録 concerns recording or keeping an account. 記録 names a record or recording, [録音](../words/録音.md) specifies audio recording, and 録る is a native verb for recording audio or video.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 金 beside the right-hand strokes as a durable record marked on a metal surface. This is a visual memory story, not a historical explanation.

The indexing radical is 金 in its narrow left-hand form.
