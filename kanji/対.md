# 対 — oppose; face

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

対 puts one side in relation to another: opposing it, facing it, or forming a pair.
[反対](../words/反対.md) はんたい expresses opposition, whereas 対話 たいわ is dialogue.
Thus the character does not make every word hostile: two sides may be talking rather than fighting.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see 文 on the left facing 寸 on the right.
The image of two facing sides is a memory aid, not a claim about how the character developed.

## Notes

一対 has a small っ: いっつい, not いちつい.
For recognition, [文](文.md) lacks the right-hand 寸 altogether.
[持](持.md) and [時](時.md) also contain 寸 within 寺, but their left sides and overall structures differ.
