# 武 — military; warrior

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 止 (no. 77) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

武 concerns military or martial matters. 武器 ぶき means a weapon, 武士 ぶし a warrior or samurai, and 武者 むしゃ a warrior. The unusual native adjective 武し たけし is retained for recognition with its archaic qualification.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the crossing upper strokes and the 止-shaped lower part, and imagine a martial emblem. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical explanation of the character.

## Notes

- 武し たけし is an archaic ku-adjective, a classical adjective class. JMdict also lists 猛し in that entry. This is recognition context, not a recommendation for ordinary modern conversation.
- The linked 武士 article teaches ぶし and explains its historical warrior context. Do not infer every warrior term's pronunciation from the ブ reading alone: 武者 uses ム.
- The visible 止 is the canonical indexing radical. The mnemonic does not establish a historical claim that 武 literally means stopping warfare.
