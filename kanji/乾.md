# 乾 — dry; desiccate

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 乙 (no. 5) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

乾 describes drying or a dry state. 乾く かわく means become dry, and 乾かす かわかす means dry something. The ケン reading belongs to a different group of traditional cosmological terms, not to an alternative pronunciation of these everyday verbs.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the stacked left-hand strokes as laundry hung in rows, and the curled right side as a drying breeze.

The indexing radical 乙 is the bent, hooked stroke at the lower right, not a separate word in this character.

## Notes

- 洗濯物が乾く せんたくものがかわく means the laundry dries; 洗濯物を乾かす せんたくものをかわかす means dry the laundry.
- Do not confuse 乾く with 渇く かわく, which concerns thirst. The traditional northwest reading いぬい is not the everyday word for drying.
