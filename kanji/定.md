# 定 — determine; settle

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

定 concerns something being decided, established, or fixed. In [予定](../words/予定.md) (よてい), the idea is something arranged in advance: a plan or schedule.
A plan can still change; 定 in this compound does not guarantee that the event will happen.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the long lower sweep leading to a fixed destination beneath the roof.
This is a visual prompt for "settled," not an origin claim.

- 定まる (さだまる) — become fixed or settled
- [定規](../words/定規.md) (じょうぎ) — measuring ruler

## Notes

定める takes an object; 定まる describes something becoming settled. Compare 日を定める (ひをさだめる), "set a date," with 日が定まる (ひがさだまる), "the date becomes settled."
[足](足.md) and [走](走.md) resemble parts of the lower outline, but 定 has 宀 at the top.
