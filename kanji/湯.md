# 湯 — hot water

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

湯 means hot water and is also associated with baths and bathing facilities. It is not a generic spelling for water at any temperature.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture water beside a sun-like upper box with trailing diagonal strokes: the water has become hot.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- お湯 おゆ is the usual polite everyday form for hot water; お does not change the reading of 湯.
- Compare [水](水.md), water. English 'water' can cover hot water too, but Japanese normally distinguishes 水 and お湯 in this temperature contrast.
