# 阪 — slope; Osaka in compounds

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170), appearing as left-side 阝 · **JLPT:** N3 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

阪 has a **slope** meaning and is important in the place name [大阪](../words/大阪.md) おおさか, Osaka. ハン can abbreviate Osaka, as in [阪神](../words/阪神.md) はんしん, Osaka-Kobe or Hanshin.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the left-side 阝 beside [反](反.md). Imagine a slope on the way to Osaka. This is a modern memory aid, not the history of the character or city name.

## Notes

- [坂](坂.md) is the ordinary spelling for a slope. 阪 is also recorded for さか, but learn 大阪 as the established current place-name spelling rather than freely replacing 坂 in every everyday word.
- Left-side 阝 here represents 阜. Right-side 阝 in other characters can instead represent 邑; position matters.
- [降](降.md), descend, [除](除.md), remove, and [険](険.md), steep or perilous, have different right-hand shapes.
