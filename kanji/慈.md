# 慈 — compassion; affection

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慈 is compassion: tender, caring love. It appears in 慈善, in 慈悲 (じひ), “mercy; compassion,” and in 慈愛 (じあい), “affection (esp. parental).”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The top part, 兹, is also the right side of [磁](磁.md), “magnetism,” which is likewise read ジ; [心](心.md), “heart,” is at the bottom. Picture a heart full of tender care.

## Notes

[磁](磁.md) shares the top part and the reading ジ, but means magnetism or porcelain; [総](総.md) and [態](態.md) also end in 心 and mean whole and attitude. [愛](愛.md), [恵](恵.md), [恩](恩.md), [仁](仁.md), [徳](徳.md) and [哀](哀.md) relate to love, blessing, favor, benevolence, virtue or pity; [恋](恋.md) and [慕](慕.md) express longing; [惜](惜.md) is to value something or find its loss a pity, and [慰](慰.md) is to console. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 68](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=68) lists ジ, with 慈愛, 慈善 and 慈悲, and いつくしむ, with 慈しむ.
