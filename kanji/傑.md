# 傑 — outstanding; excellence; hero

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

傑 is standing out above others, and a person who does. [傑作](../words/傑作.md) けっさく is a masterpiece. JMdict glosses it "masterpiece; masterwork; chef d'oeuvre; fine piece of work" and, in a second sense it marks jocular and dated, "(unintentionally) hilarious; absurd (e.g. blunder)."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ケツ. The first is being outstanding, as in 傑作 and 傑出 けっしゅつ, "being outstanding; excelling; being foremost." The second is an outstanding person, as in 傑物 けつぶつ, "great person; distinguished person; outstanding figure," 英傑 えいけつ, "great person; hero; mastermind," and 豪傑 ごうけつ, "hero; bold person; daring person." The entry lists no kun reading. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [人](人.md), "person," as 亻 at the left, and at the right the shape 舛, which has no card, above [木](木.md), "tree, wood." Imagine a person who has climbed to the very top of a tree and stands above the crowd, easy to pick out. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 人, 舛 or 木 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケツ | on | [傑作](../words/傑作.md) けっさく — masterpiece; hilarious absurdity (jocular, dated) |
| すぐ.れる | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict lists 傑れる only as a search-only spelling of 優れる すぐれる, "to be better (than); to be superior; to surpass" |

In 傑作, 傑 supplies ケツ and 作 supplies サク. ケツ is shortened to けっ before サク, and the small っ doubles the next sound (gemination): けつ + さく gives けっさく, as in 傑出, けっ + しゅつ. In 豪傑, nothing follows 傑, and it keeps けつ. In すぐ.れる, the dot marks れる as okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji.

For comparison with other compound sound changes, see [sound changes in compounds](../articles/compound-sound-changes.md).

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケツ, with 傑物, 傑作 and 豪傑 as examples. すぐ.れる is not in the official table. Practice selects ケツ, the reading of the linked word article 傑作; すぐ.れる has no verified ordinary example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [作](作.md), "make, produce," in 傑作.
- [舞](舞.md), "dance," has 舛 at the bottom, the shape at the upper right of 傑. [休](休.md), "rest," has 亻 beside 木, and [体](体.md), "body, form," has 亻 beside 本. All are different kanji.
- [優](優.md), "gentle; superior," [英](英.md), "English; outstanding," [偉](偉.md), "admirable; great," [卓](卓.md), "table; eminent," [超](超.md), "exceed; surpass," and [凌](凌.md), "endure; surpass," overlap the senses "outstanding," "excellence" and "surpass" that 傑 and its words carry; 優 is also read すぐ.れる and is the standard spelling of 優れる. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "outstanding" or "excellence" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the second ケツ sense, an outstanding person: 怪傑 かいけつ, "person of extraordinary talent; wonder; marvel," 俊傑 しゅんけつ, "hero; genius," and 女傑 じょけつ, "heroine; great woman; brave woman."
