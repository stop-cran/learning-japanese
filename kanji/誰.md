# 誰 — who; which person

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N3 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

誰 asks **who**, or which person is meant. The everyday word is [誰](../words/誰.md) だれ. [誰か](../words/誰か.md) だれか means "someone"; the added か changes the expression.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 言, speech, beside 隹. Imagine asking who is speaking beside a small bird. This is a memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| スイ | on | 誰何 (すいか) - asking an unknown person's identity; recognition only here |
| だれ | kun | [誰](../words/誰.md) だれ - who; [誰か](../words/誰か.md) だれか - someone |
| たれ | kun | 誰 (たれ) - who; an older form retained in literary language, not the ordinary modern question word |
| た | kun | Historical variant of "who," as in 誰が (たが), "whose"; retained for reference, not practice |

## Notes

- 誰が来ましたか (だれがきましたか) asks "Who came?" 誰の傘ですか (だれのかさですか) asks "Whose umbrella is it?" The particles show the person's role.
- 誰も with a negative predicate can mean "no one": 誰もいません (だれもいません), "Nobody is here." Do not treat 誰も as inherently negative in every construction.
- Practice selects だれ. The full metadata preserves スイ・たれ・た without presenting them as everyday substitutes.
- [認](認.md), recognize, [議](議.md), deliberate, and [記](記.md), record, share 言 but have different right-hand components.
