# 弊 — harm; bad practice; worn out

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 廾 (no. 55) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弊 expresses harm or a bad practice in 弊害 and 旧弊 (きゅうへい), exhaustion in 疲弊 (ひへい), and humility about one’s own side in 弊社 (へいしゃ), “our company.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The top is the same as in 幣, “currency; offering.” 弊 has 廾 at the bottom; 幣 has 巾 there instead.

## Notes

[敬](敬.md) and [散](散.md) share the right-hand 攵, and [弁](弁.md) also ends in 廾. [幣](幣.md) is read ヘイ too but relates to money or offerings. [害](害.md) means harm, [悪](悪.md) bad, [疲](疲.md) tired and [損](損.md) loss; 弊 overlaps them only in particular words.
