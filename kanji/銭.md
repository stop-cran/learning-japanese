# 銭 — coin; sen (1/100 yen)

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

銭 is money in coins, and also the sen, a unit worth one hundredth of a yen. [金銭](../words/金銭.md) きんせん is glossed in JMdict as "money; cash." KANJIDIC2 gives "coin," ".01 yen" and "money"; Kanjipedia's first two senses are ぜに。かね。貨幣。 (coins; money) and the unit worth 一円の一〇〇分の一 (one hundredth of a yen), and JMdict glosses 銭 read せん (1392410) as "sen (hundredth of a yen)."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is [金](金.md), "gold, money," and on the right is 戋, a shape of short strokes like [三](三.md), "three," crossed by 戈. Imagine three little coins stacked up, with one stroke slicing through them: small change. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 金 or 戋 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists セン, with 銭湯 せんとう ("public bath; bathhouse") and 金銭, and ぜに, with 銭, 銭入れ ぜにいれ ("till; purse; handbag") and 小銭, and gives 錢 as the old form.
- On its own, 銭 is read せん (JMdict 1392410), ぜに (JMdict 2175880, sense 3 "money") and ぜぜ (JMdict 2843310, marked children's language, "money"). Digital Daijisen also has the headwords ぜぜ and ちゃん for 銭.
- KANJIDIC2 and Digital Daijisen give no name readings for 銭.

These four cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [金](金.md) (“gold, money”): KANJIDIC2 lists “money” for 銭, Kanjipedia's first sense includes かね, which the 金 card lists as a reading, and JMdict glosses 金銭 as “money; cash.”
- [貨](貨.md) (“goods; freight; currency”) and [幣](幣.md) (“currency; Shinto offering”): Kanjipedia's first sense includes 貨幣 かへい, which JMdict (1195930) glosses as “money; currency; coin,” matching KANJIDIC2's “coin” for 銭; KANJIDIC2 also lists “cash” for 幣.
- [円](円.md) (“yen, circle”): KANJIDIC2 lists “.01 yen” for 銭, Kanjipedia's second sense is 一円の一〇〇分の一, and JMdict glosses 銭 せん (1392410) as “sen (hundredth of a yen).”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 鉢 (“bowl; flowerpot”), 針 (“needle; pin”), and 税 (“tax; fiscal duty”); none means a coin or the sen.
