# 単 — single; simple

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 十 (no. 24) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

単 presents something as single or simple. [単語](../words/単語.md) たんご is an individual word,
not necessarily an easy word. 単純 たんじゅん supplies the "uncomplicated" sense.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** follow the single central upright through the box and lower horizontal line.
This is a cue for remembering the modern shape, not its origin.
