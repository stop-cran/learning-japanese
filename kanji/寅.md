# 寅 — Tiger zodiac sign

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (うかんむり, no. 40) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寅 names the **third of the twelve zodiac signs**, the Tiger. The native
sign noun is [寅](../words/寅.md) とら. The ordinary animal noun
[虎](../words/虎.md), also とら, uses a different character.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a tiger sheltered under
the roof 宀, with a barred, boxed middle and two spreading lower strokes.
The roof is the indexing radical and takes the first three strokes.
This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| イン | on | 甲寅 こういん — Wood Tiger, term 51 of the sixty-term calendar cycle; also read きのえとら; recognition only |
| とら | kun | [寅](../words/寅.md) とら — Tiger zodiac sign; 寅年 とらどし — year of the Tiger |

Only とら is selected for practice. イン is verified calendar recognition,
not classified here as obsolete or name-only. Learn 甲寅's two complete
readings rather than freely exchanging character readings. The sixty-term
cycle combines stems and branches; it is not just the twelve-sign set.

## Notes

- [演](演.md), perform or stage, adds 氵 on the left of the 寅 shape.
  [黄](黄.md), yellow, has a different top instead of the roof.
  [申](申.md), humbly say, has a through-stroke in its box but no roof
  or spreading lower strokes; its separate zodiac use is the Monkey,
  not the Tiger. These are modern-shape comparisons, not etymologies.
- [虎](虎.md) names the associated animal. The shared reading とら
  does not make the two spellings unrestricted substitutes.
- Historical uses include an hour around 4 a.m., a traditional direction
  labelled east-northeast, and the first lunar month. Dictionary hour
  mappings include both 3–5 a.m. and 4–6 a.m.; no universal clock
  conversion is intended. These are not ordinary modern scheduling or
  navigation substitutes. The lunar month is not Gregorian January.
- [丑](丑.md)寅 (うしとら), northeast, names a direction between two branches;
  it is not the same direction as standalone 寅.
- The grade-9 tag classifies name-use kanji; it does not make the sign
  noun or every reading a personal name.
