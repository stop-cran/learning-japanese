# 離 — separate; detach

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172), on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

離 concerns separation, distance or leaving.
[離れる](../words/離れる.md) はなれる describes being apart, moving
away or leaving; [離す](../words/離す.md) はなす describes separating
or moving something away. [離婚](../words/離婚.md) りこん means divorce,
a particular legal and personal separation, not every kind of departure.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** identify the dense left-hand
离, with 亠 above 凶 and a lower 禸-shaped group, beside the right-hand 隹.
Imagine the two large blocks moving apart. This is a visual memory aid,
not a historical account or a claim that the parts independently mean separate.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| リ | on | [離婚](../words/離婚.md) りこん - divorce |
| はな.れる | kun | [離れる](../words/離れる.md) はなれる - be apart; move away; leave |
| はな.す | kun | [離す](../words/離す.md) はなす - separate; move something away |

All three raw readings are selected with their own linked examples.
離れる is intransitive and ichidan; 離す is transitive and godan.
The character writes はな in both, while れる or す supplies the written
kana ending. Their shared reading stem does not erase the difference in
grammar; stem-based practice may present only one はな reading question.

## Notes

- [雛](雛.md), chick/hina doll, [雑](雑.md), miscellaneous, and
  [難](難.md), difficult, share the right-hand 隹 but have different
  left blocks. Preserve the full nineteen-stroke shape of 離.
- Separation overlaps [別](別.md) and [分](分.md); release overlaps
  [放](放.md), and leaving overlaps [去](去.md). These are direct
  meaning comparisons, not instructions to exchange their spellings freely.
- In particular, [放す](../words/放す.md) はなす often concerns letting go or releasing,
  whereas the linked 離す article focuses on separation or distance.
  Learn the situation and spelling together; pronunciation alone cannot choose them.
