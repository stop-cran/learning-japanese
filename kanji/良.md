# 良 — good; favorable

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 艮 (no. 138) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

良 expresses a positive judgment of quality or condition. [良い](../words/良い.md) よい
means "good"; 良好 りょうこう describes a favorable or satisfactory condition.
What counts as good depends on the thing being evaluated.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the dot above the 艮-like form, as if marking something
with a small sign of approval. This is a modern visual association, not an etymology.

## Notes

Learn いい alongside よい, but form the basic past and negative with よ:
よかった, "was good," and よくない, "not good."
[食](食.md), [長](長.md), and [銀](銀.md) have related lower shapes;
pay attention to 良's single dot at the top.
