# 鯨 — whale

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 魚 (no. 195), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鯨 means **whale**. Whales are mammals, not fish: the dictionary's indexing radical [魚](魚.md), fish, is not a biological classification.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 魚 on the left and [京](京.md), capital, on the right. Imagine a whale swimming past a seaside capital. This links the visible shapes for memory; it is not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- **くじら** and **クジラ** also write the native animal name. JMdict labels it usually written in kana; katakana does not make it a foreign loanword.
- 鯨肉 has both げいにく and くじらにく. Do not assume that every compound containing 鯨 uses ゲイ.
- JMdict separately records archaic **いさな** and **いさ** for 鯨. These are historical word readings, not additional ordinary answers for the noun practised here; they are not added to the frozen character metadata.
- [京](京.md) is the right-hand shape alone. [涼](涼.md), cool/refreshing, has 氵 on the left; [景](景.md), scenery, adds 日 above 京. None means whale.
- [鯛](鯛.md) たい, sea bream, is a fish. Sharing the 魚 radical does not make 鯛 and 鯨 the same kind of animal.
