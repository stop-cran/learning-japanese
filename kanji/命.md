# 命 — life; command

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

命 has two important meanings to keep separate: life and a command.
The standalone noun [命](../words/命.md) いのち is life as something that can be protected or lost. 命令 めいれい means an order, not a kind of living.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the broad top shelters the small 口 below, as if protecting a life. This is only a memory image; it does not explain the historical connection between the meanings.

## Notes

[会](会.md) and [今](今.md) share a broad upper outline with 命; look for the separate small box and the lower-right strokes.
[生活](../words/生活.md) せいかつ concerns living and one's daily life. It is not interchangeable with 命 in "save a life."
