# 如 — like; as if

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

如 can express likeness, as in 如し (ごとし, “like; as if”). Do not expect that English gloss to make sense separately in every compound: 欠如 names a lack.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 女 on the left and 口 on the right. [加](加.md) and [知](知.md) also end in 口 but start with different shapes.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジョ | on | [欠如](../words/欠如.md) けつじょ — lack; absence of something needed |
| ニョ | on | 如来 (にょらい) — Tathagata, a Buddhist title. 如 is にょ in this word. |
| ごと.し | kun | 如し (ごとし) — like; as if. This kun-reading is outside the common-use table. |

## Common words

- [欠如](../words/欠如.md) けつじょ — lack; absence of something needed

## Notes

Compare [似](似.md) (resemble). In 如何 (いかが, “how”), the reading belongs to the whole spelling; do not use い or いか as a reading of 如.

Follow 口 to its left neighbor in [知](知.md), [加](加.md) and 如: only 如 has 女 immediately to its left. [始](始.md) also starts with 女 but has 台, not just 口, on the right.
