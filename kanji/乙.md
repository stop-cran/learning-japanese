# 乙 — second rank; stylish

**Strokes:** 1 · **Key (radical):** 乙 (no. 5) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

乙 marks second place in an order: the second of the ten calendar signs, the second rank, or grade B after 甲. Used like an adjective, it can mean stylish or tasteful, and also strange. Kanjipedia also gives a prefix use meaning young or lovable. KANJIDIC2 lists “the latter”, “duplicate”, “strange” and “witty”. As a word, 乙 おつ first means “(the) second (party, rank, etc.); B (grade); the latter” (JMdict), and [甲乙](../words/甲乙.md) こうおつ pairs it with 甲: “first and second; A and B”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 乙 is written with a single bending stroke. Picture a swan gliding along a pond, always the second one in a line of two, its neck bending and its body sweeping to the side. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that the single stroke of 乙 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists オツ only, with 乙種 and 甲乙, and gives 乙女 おとめ as a special reading; JMdict glosses 乙女 as “(young) girl; maiden; young lady”. Kanjipedia gives イツ, きのと and おと outside the table (外).
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 乙, and Digital Daijisen gives お, き, くに, たか, つぎ, と and とどむ; these are name uses, not quiz readings.
- 乙 is itself the key (radical no. 5); KANJIDIC2 also lists “fishhook radical (no. 5)” among its meanings.

These fifteen cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [副](副.md) (“vice-; secondary”): 乙 can mean a duplicate, a second copy.
- [位](位.md) (“rank; position”), [格](格.md) (“rank; character”) and [級](級.md) (“grade; school class”): 乙 names the second rank or grade, like B after A.
- [粋](粋.md) (“stylish; essence; pure”): 乙 can mean stylish or chic.
- [妙](妙.md) (“strange; exquisite”), [奇](奇.md) (“strange; extraordinary”), [変](変.md) (“change; strange”) and [異](異.md) (“different; unusual; strange”): 乙 can also mean strange or odd.
- [若](若.md) (“young”), [稚](稚.md) (“immature; childish; young”) and [幼](幼.md) (“early childhood; immature”): as a prefix, 乙 can mean young.
- [弟](弟.md) (“younger brother”) and [妹](妹.md) (“younger sister”): in old usage, 乙 read おと can mean a younger brother or sister.
- [児](児.md) (“child; youngster”): 乙 can name the youngest child, as in 乙子 おとご, “last child”.

The [亜](亜.md) card (“sub-; next in rank; Asia”) declares the 乙/亜 pair, because Kanjipedia gives a second-in-order sense for both characters.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 王 (“king”), 硬 (“hard; stiff”), and 粗 (“coarse; rough; crude”); none means a second rank or stylish.
