# 僚 — colleague

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

僚 concerns a colleague or associate sharing work or a role, as in
[同僚](../words/同僚.md) どうりょう, "coworker; colleague."
It also appears with an official sense in 官僚 かんりょう, "bureaucrat; government official."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the person-shaped 亻 beside
the layered strokes on the right as a colleague beside a busy work desk.
This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- 同僚 identifies a work relationship, not necessarily a personal friendship.
  Do not derive a rigid equal-rank requirement from 同.
- [療](療.md) shares the elaborate right-hand shape but has the illness frame 疒
  and concerns treatment or healing, not a colleague.
- [像](像.md) and [値](値.md) also have 亻 on the left; inspect the different
  right-hand components rather than relying on the radical alone.
