# 旧 — former; old

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

旧 concerns what is old, former or from an earlier period. As the prefix 旧 きゅう, it identifies a previous name, building, system or version. Its native readings are separate dictionary-listed uses, not a way to pronounce every 旧- compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the upright stroke beside 日 and imagine a mark for a day already past. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

- JMdict flags 旧い as a rare spelling of the adjective [古い](../words/古い.md) ふるい. Retain it for recognition rather than replacing the ordinary 古い spelling.
- The linked 旧 article teaches the prefix きゅう. The もと row records another dictionary reading; a shared written form does not make every example in that article a もと example.
- [新](新.md), new, is a useful contrast. A former building is not necessarily dilapidated: prior status and physical condition are different ideas.
