# 四 — four

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The oldest forms of four were four horizontal lines, continuing the pattern of [一](一.md), [二](二.md) and [三](三.md). The modern shape, a box with strokes inside, is a different, later form; its exact origin is debated
(it has been explained as a borrowed character for another word, and as a picture of breath or of a divided space). Treat any single story as a mnemonic only.

## Notes

Four is read よん, よ or シ. Because シ sounds like 死 (death), よん is preferred in speech: 四時 よじ, 四月 しがつ, 四人 よにん, but 四十 よんじゅう. 4 o'clock is よじ, never しじ; April is しがつ, never よんがつ.
Look-alikes: 西 (west, see [西](西.md)) and 田 ([田](田.md)) share the box shape, but 四 has two short strokes inside rather than a cross. 四 is the key 囗 (enclosure).
