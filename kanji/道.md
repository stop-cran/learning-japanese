# 道 — road, way

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **Phonetic:** 首 (sometimes proposed; debated) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

道 combines 辶 (a road with a foot, movement along a way) and 首 (head). The usual dictionary explanation takes 首 partly as a sound (シュ/ドウ are related in old Chinese) and the whole as
"walking along a way". An older explanation sees a head being carried along a road as a ritual, but this is debated, so treat it as a story and not as established.
From "road" the meaning widens to "way, method, principle", as in 柔道 じゅうどう, 茶道 さどう and 道徳 どうとく.

## Notes

The top part is 首 (with its own 自 and two dots), so compare 首 (くび, neck) and 道: 道 needs the 辶 underneath.
Do not mix up [道](道.md) with [行](行.md), which is also a way/going kanji, and with [出](出.md).
