# 優 — gentle; superior

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

優 links several vocabulary families: kindness in [優しい](../words/優しい.md) やさしい,
excellence in 優れる すぐれる, and performers in 俳優 はいゆう.
The whole word tells you which meaning matters; a 優しい person is being described as kind, not ranked first.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the small person-shaped 亻 making room for the large, elaborate
right-hand shape 憂. Giving someone room can cue gentleness. This is an invented modern-shape aid.

[夏](夏.md) has a somewhat similar top-and-sweeping-bottom outline, but no 亻.
