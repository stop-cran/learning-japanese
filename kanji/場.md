# 場 — place, site

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

場 is a place or site, often somewhere an activity happens. In [工場](../words/工場.md), it is a place for manufacturing; in [場所](../words/場所.md), it helps name a location.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the [土](土.md) on the left as ground and the tall stack of strokes on the right as a signpost marking a place. This is a memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Common words

- [場所](../words/場所.md) ばしょ — place, location
- [工場](../words/工場.md) こうじょう — factory
- 会場 かいじょう — venue, meeting place
- 売り場 うりば — sales area or counter

## Notes

Compare [地](地.md), which concerns land or ground, with 場, which often identifies a site or setting. They overlap in meaning but are not freely interchangeable within words.

場所 is mixed kun+on: ば + しょ. Do not read every compound with 場 as ジョウ.
