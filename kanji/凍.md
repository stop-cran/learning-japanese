# 凍 — freeze; frozen

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 冫 (no. 15) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凍 concerns freezing or a frozen state. 凍る こおる describes something becoming frozen; 凍える こごえる describes a body becoming chilled or numb with cold. [冷凍](../words/冷凍.md) れいとう is freezing or frozen storage.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the two cold marks of 冫 beside [東](東.md), as though the right-hand object were being frozen.

The canonical radical 冫 is the two-stroke ice radical on the left; distinguish it from the three strokes of 氵.

## Notes

- 水が凍る みずがこおる means water freezes. 寒さで凍える さむさでこごえる means become chilled or numb with the cold; it does not necessarily mean the person's body has literally frozen solid.
- 凍てる いてる and 凍みる しみる are additional words for freezing. The source-only reading こごる is kept for dictionary lookup; do not substitute it for こおる in the basic verb.
