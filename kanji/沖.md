# 沖 — open sea; offshore; rise high

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

沖 is water far from the shore: the open sea or the offing. The linked word [沖](../words/沖.md) おき is JMdict's "open sea," and 沖合 おきあい is "off the coast; offshore; out at sea." JMdict's second sense for the word 沖, restricted to the spelling 沖, is "abbreviation Okinawa," a shortening of 沖縄 おきなわ, "Okinawa (city, prefecture)." Digital Daijisen's character entry gives チュウ the meaning "rise straight up," as in 沖天 ちゅうてん, "rising into the heavens," and gives おき with 沖合 and 沖魚. Its supplementary note says that 冲 is a variant form.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the water radical 氵 (from [水](水.md)) at the left and [中](中.md), "middle, inside," at the right. Imagine the middle of the water, far from land. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or 中 contributes meaning or sound, although 中 is read チュウ, a reading KANJIDIC2 also lists for 沖. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| チュウ | on | 沖天 ちゅうてん — rising into the heavens; 沖積 ちゅうせき — alluvial; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| おき | kun | [沖](../words/沖.md) おき — open sea; 沖合 おきあい — off the coast; offshore; out at sea |
| おきつ | kun | 沖つ おきつ — poetical term meaning "of the ocean; in the open sea; oceanic"; JMdict also writes it 沖津; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ちゅう.する | kun | 沖する ちゅうする — to rise up into the air; to ascend into the sky; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| わく | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; no JMdict entry writes わく with 沖, and the Daijisen character entry does not give it |

沖 おき is a single character writing the whole native noun; JMdict lists 澳 as a "rarely used kanji form," which is not taught here. In 沖天 and 沖積 ちゅうせき, 沖 supplies チュウ; 沖天 is also spelled 冲天 in JMdict. In 沖する, 沖 writes ちゅう and する is the verb ending. 沖つ is a pre-noun adjectival (a word used only before a noun) whose おき is the same word as the noun. Okinawa, in 沖縄, is a whole-word name that this card does not use as reading evidence.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows チュウ with 沖積層 ちゅうせきそう, "alluvium; alluvial stratum," 沖天 and 沖する, and おき with 沖. Practice selects おき, the reading with a linked word article. The other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words where possible; being outside the practice set does not make them invalid.

## Notes

- The common word is the single character 沖, so it has no other kanji.
- [水](水.md), "water," is the full form of the 氵 at the left of 沖; [中](中.md), "middle, inside," is the shape at the right. [沈](沈.md), "sink; subside," [汽](汽.md), "steam; vapor," and [治](治.md), "govern; heal," also have 氵 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [海](海.md), "sea, ocean," [洋](洋.md), "ocean; Western style," [湖](湖.md), "lake," [岸](岸.md), "shore; riverbank," [港](港.md), "harbor," [波](波.md), "wave; billow," and [流](流.md), "flow," overlap the sea and water senses of 沖 and its words. [昇](昇.md), "rise; ascend," [上](上.md), "up, above," [起](起.md), "wake; rise," [天](天.md), "heaven, sky," and [空](空.md), "sky; empty," overlap the rising-into-the-sky sense of 沖天 and 沖する; 空 also shares むな.しい, a Kanjipedia reading that is outside KANJIDIC2. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "open sea" or "rise" as exclusive to any one character.
