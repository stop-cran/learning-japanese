# 寝 — lie down; sleep

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寝 connects sleep with lying down or going to bed. [寝る](../words/寝る.md) ねる can describe sleeping,
but also the decision to retire for the night: もう寝ます。 (もうねます。) — I am going to bed now.
The character also appears in the word for a bedroom, 寝室 しんしつ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a sleeping space under 宀, with the small left-hand strokes tucked beside a larger right-hand shape.
This is a visual aid only; no historical derivation is proposed here.

## Notes

[眠る](../words/眠る.md) ねむる focuses on sleeping; 寝る also has a lying-down sense, so the words are not identical.
[起きる](../words/起きる.md) おきる supplies the practical contrast of getting up or waking.
