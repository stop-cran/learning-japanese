# 己 — self; oneself

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 己 (no. 49) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

己 refers to the self. In 自己紹介 (じこしょうかい), the person introduced is oneself.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** In 己 the upper space stays open on the left; compare the closed top of 巳.

## Notes

[巳](巳.md) is a zodiac sign, [包](包.md) wraps around an inner shape and [記](記.md) adds a speech element. [自](自.md), [我](我.md), [身](身.md) and [私](私.md) also refer to the self in some contexts. Do not use おのれ as a neutral way to address someone.
