# 毎 — every, each

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 毋 (no. 80) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

毎 means "every", "each time". The shape is closely related to [母](母.md) (mother): the old form is commonly described as a woman (女 / 母) with a hair ornament on top.
How this gave "every" is unclear, so it is best treated as a mnemonic only: "a mother does it every day".
The key 毋 (no. 80) comes from the dictionary classification, not from the meaning.

## Notes

- Special sound changes: 毎月 まいつき (native reading of 月), 毎年 まいとし (also まいねん), while 毎日 is まいにち, not まいひ.
- Look-alike: [母](母.md) (mother) has no slanted stroke at the top; 毎 has it, plus the horizontal bar. Also compare 海 (sea) and 梅 (plum), which contain 毎 as a right-hand part.
