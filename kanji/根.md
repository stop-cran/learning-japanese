# 根 — root; foundation

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

根 concerns a root or underlying basis. 根 ね can be a plant's root, and 根気 こんき means patience or perseverance. In 屋根 やね, the complete word means a roof; do not infer that every compound containing 根 must name a plant part.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside 艮 and imagine a tree held firmly by its roots. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not verified etymology.
