# 急 — urgent, sudden

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 心 (heart, no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

急 connects hurrying, urgency, and sudden change. It can also describe a steep slope, so not every use is about a person rushing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the angular shapes above [心](心.md) pressing down on a heart that feels hurried. This is a modern visual memory aid, not an origin claim.

## Common words

- [急ぐ](../words/急ぐ.md) いそぐ — hurry
- 急に きゅうに — suddenly
- 急行 きゅうこう — express train
- 急用 きゅうよう — urgent business

## Notes

急な坂 きゅうなさか means "a steep slope"; 急な changes a following noun. 急に often describes how an event happens, as in 急に雨が降った きゅうにあめがふった (it suddenly started raining).

Compare the upper shapes of 急, [悪](悪.md), and [思](思.md): they all end in 心 but are separate characters with different readings.
