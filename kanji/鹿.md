# 鹿 — deer

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 鹿 (no. 198) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鹿 means deer. The standalone animal noun is [鹿](../words/鹿.md) しか.
The word does not by itself specify a male or female animal.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice the broad upper enclosure
and the 比-shaped lower part; imagine a deer standing beneath a shelter.
This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation of the character.
The indexing radical is the whole 鹿, not the upper 广 shape.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ロク | on | 神鹿 しんろく — deer kept at a shrine as messengers of the gods; recognition example |
| しか | kun | [鹿](../words/鹿.md) しか — deer; the selected animal-noun reading |
| か | kun | 鹿 か — archaic word for deer; 鹿の子 かのこ — dappled cloth or a spotted pattern, a compound to recognize |

か is archaic as a standalone deer noun; that does not make every compound
containing it archaic. 鹿の子 also names a kind of mochi, while its original
"fawn" sense is marked archaic in JMdict. Do not translate every 鹿の子 as a
young deer. Only しか is selected for reading questions here; ロク and か
remain in the full metadata for reference.

## Common words

- [鹿](../words/鹿.md) しか — deer
- [馬鹿](../words/馬鹿.md) ばか — fool; foolish. This is a whole-word **ateji**
  (sound-based spelling), not a deer compound whose meaning or reading can
  be worked out character by character.

## Notes

- [席](席.md), [庭](庭.md), and [底](底.md) have a similar upper enclosure
  but different lower parts. Their seat, garden, and bottom meanings do not
  mean deer.
- Do not use 馬鹿 ばか as evidence that 鹿 independently supplies か in
  that word. Learn its complete spelling and reading together.
- 神鹿 is a shrine-related recognition example, not a replacement for the
  ordinary standalone noun 鹿 しか.
