# 踏 — tread; trample

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 足 (no. 157) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

踏 is putting a foot down on something: stepping on it, treading on it or trampling it. KANJIDIC2 lists “step”, “trample”, “carry through”, “appraise” and “evade payment”, and Kanjipedia gives ふむ。ふまえる。あるく。ふみ行う。 (step on; be based on; walk; carry out). In [踏む](../words/踏む.md) ふむ, JMdict's first sense is “to step on; to tread on; to trample on”; the same entry also has “to follow (rules, principles, etc.); to go through (e.g. formalities); to complete” and “to estimate; to guess; to judge; to value; to appraise”. [踏切](../words/踏切.md) ふみきり is glossed in JMdict “railway crossing; railroad crossing; train crossing; level crossing”, and JMdict also spells it 踏み切り, showing ふみ written with 踏.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [足](足.md), "foot, leg," with [口](口.md), "mouth," at its top, stands on the left. On the right is the shape 沓, made of [水](水.md), "water," over [日](日.md), "sun, day." Picture a foot treading through shallow water under the sun. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 足 or 沓 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists トウ, with 踏破, 踏襲 and 高踏的, ふむ, with 踏む among its examples, and ふまえる, with 踏まえる. Kanjipedia lists no readings outside the table.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 踏, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These two cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [歩](歩.md) (“walk, steps”): its “steps” is close to KANJIDIC2's “step” for 踏, and Kanjipedia's sense of 踏 includes あるく (walk), while the 歩 card lists ある.く.
- [履](履.md) (“footwear; put on shoes; carry out”): JMdict entry 1450270 lists 履む as a spelling of ふむ, KANJIDIC2 gives 履 “perform” and “complete” and 踏 “carry through”, and Kanjipedia's sense of 踏 includes ふみ行う (carry out).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 握 (“grip; grasp”), 掘 (“dig; excavate”), and 拾 (“pick up; gather”); none means stepping on or trampling.
