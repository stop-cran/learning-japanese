# 焦 — scorch; char; impatient

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 火 (as 灬, no. 86), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

焦 is scorching: something burning dark at the surface, and from there a mind that burns with impatience. [焦げる](../words/焦げる.md) こげる is to get scorched or charred, [焦がす](../words/焦がす.md) こがす is to scorch or char something, and [焦点](../words/焦点.md) しょうてん is a focus or focal point. JMdict glosses the verb 焦る あせる "to be in a hurry; to be impatient; to be anxious (to do); to fret."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ショウ: scorching, as in 焦点, 焦土 しょうど, "scorched earth," 焦熱 しょうねつ, "scorching heat," and 焦眉 しょうび, "emergency; urgency; imminence"; and being irritated or impatient, as in 焦心 しょうしん, "impatience; anxiety," 焦躁 しょうそう, "impatience; irritation; frustration; fretfulness," a spelling JMdict marks as rarely used beside its main spelling 焦燥, and 焦慮 しょうりょ, "impatience; worry." The 焦点 word article explains how that compound's name comes from paper scorching where a lens focuses sunlight.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a bird-like shape at the top, the same top part as in [集](集.md), "gather," and [火](火.md), "fire," at the bottom, written as four dots 灬. Imagine a bird held over a fire until its feathers char, flapping impatiently to get away. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that the top shape or 火 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 焦点, 焦 supplies ショウ and 点 supplies テン. In 焦げる, 焦 writes こ and げる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same こ takes がす in 焦がす, the transitive partner, and がれる in 焦がれる. 焦る writes あせ with る, and 焦れる and 焦らす write じ with れる and らす.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ショウ, with 焦土, 焦慮 and 焦心 as examples; こげる, with 焦げる and 黒焦げ くろこげ, "charred; burnt black; scorched black"; こがす, with 焦がす; こがれる, with 焦がれる; and あせる, with 焦る and 焦り あせり, "impatience." じれる and じらす are not in the table. Practice selects ショウ, こ.げる and こ.がす, the readings with linked word articles; the other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [点](点.md), "point; dot," in 焦点. The words 焦がす and 焦げる contain only this kanji.
- [火](火.md), "fire," is the key at the bottom, written 灬, and [照](照.md), "illuminate; shine," and [熊](熊.md), "bear (animal)," have the same 灬 at the bottom below a different shape; 照 is also read ショウ. [集](集.md), "gather," has the same bird-like shape at the top. All are different kanji.
- [焼](焼.md), "bake; burn," [燃](燃.md), "burn; blaze," [炭](炭.md), "charcoal; coal," [燥](燥.md), "parch; dry up," [急](急.md), "urgent, sudden," and [悩](悩.md), "worry; be troubled," overlap the senses "burn," "char," "parch," "hurry" and "anxious" that 焦 and its words carry; 燥 appears in 焦燥 and 焼 is also read ショウ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "burn" or "impatient" as exclusive to any one character.
