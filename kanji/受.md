# 受 — receive; undergo

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 又 (no. 29) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

受 concerns receiving something or undergoing an experience. [受ける](../words/受ける.md) うける
can describe receiving help, but also taking an examination.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture something being passed down from the upper strokes to the 又 at the bottom.
This is a memory scene, not a historical analysis.

## Common words

- [受ける](../words/受ける.md) うける — receive; take a test
- 受かる うかる — pass a test
- 受験 じゅけん — sitting an examination

## Notes

試験を受ける (しけんをうける) means "take an exam"; 試験に受かる (しけんにうかる)
means "pass an exam." Notice both the verb ending and the particle.
[学](学.md) has a somewhat similar covered shape, but its lower part is 子 rather than 又.
