# 允 — permit; sincerity

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (no. 10) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

允 has permission and sincerity associations. In [允可](../words/允可.md)
いんか, it contributes permitting or assenting. This is a useful recognition
word for formal or older writing, not the default way to ask permission in conversation.
The source classifies 允 as a name-use character; that does not make every use a personal name.

The modern shape has 厶 above two leg-like strokes, 儿, the indexing radical.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture someone standing beneath a sign granting permission.
This is a visual memory aid, not a historical derivation or a phonetic analysis.

All four sourced readings remain in their original order, but reading questions
select only イン. The historical office-title use of じょう is real, not merely
a personal-name reading. The last two rows preserve dictionary lookup information
without inventing modern word spellings. The dot in まこと.に is the source's
okurigana notation; ゆるす is preserved without a dot.

For the everyday verb "allow; forgive," learn [許す](../words/許す.md) ゆるす.
Its written form and range of meanings are not automatically transferable to 允.

## Notes

- [元](元.md), "origin, former," has two horizontal lines above its legs;
  [兄](兄.md), "older brother," has a square 口. Neither has 允's upper 厶 shape.
