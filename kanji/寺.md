# 寺 — Buddhist temple

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寺 てら names a Buddhist temple. The on reading ジ occurs in 古寺 こじ, old temple, and 寺社 じしゃ, temples and shrines. These words do not make temple and shrine interchangeable categories.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See a 土-like upper shape over 寸 and imagine a small temple model resting on a hand. The scene is invented from the modern shape; it is not a claim about the historical role of either component.

## Notes

- Do not read the standalone word for temple じ merely because temple names can end in that reading. Learn the reading of each name separately.
- Compare [時](時.md), time, [待](待.md), wait, and [持](持.md), hold. Each adds a different left-hand component to the visible 寺 shape.
- The indexing radical is 寸, not the upper 土-like shape.
