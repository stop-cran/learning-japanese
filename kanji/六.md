# 六 — six

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 亠 (no. 8, "lid") · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The origin of 六 is debated. Early forms are sometimes explained as a small hut or roof with legs, borrowed for the number six by sound. There is no reliable picture-meaning, so treat any story as a mnemonic.
Its key is 亠 (lid), the top part.

## Notes

Sound changes with ロク before k/s/t/p: 六回 ろっかい, 六本 ろっぽん, 六分 ろっぷん, 六百 ろっぴゃく. Look-alikes: [大](大.md) and [八](八.md); 六 has a lid on top of 八.
