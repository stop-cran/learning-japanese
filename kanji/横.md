# 横 — horizontal

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

横 directs attention sideways or across rather than vertically.
The noun [横](../words/横.md) (よこ) can mean a horizontal direction or the side of something.
In 横書き (よこがき), it describes writing arranged horizontally.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the lower spread of the right-hand 黄 as a broad platform extending sideways from 木.
This is a modern-shape picture, not a claim about origin.

## Notes

縦 (たて), "vertical," is a useful directional contrast.
横 does not itself specify left or right: both can be beside something.
[東](東.md) has a central upright through a box but lacks the separate 木-left layout of 横.
