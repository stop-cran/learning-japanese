# 句 — phrase; verse

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30), inside 勹 · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

句 is a phrase or a verse unit: a short stretch of words that belongs together. [俳句](../words/俳句.md) はいく is a haiku, [句](../words/句.md) く is a phrase or verse, [句読点](../words/句読点.md) くとうてん are punctuation marks and [文句](../words/文句.md) もんく is a complaint or a phrase.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses: a break in a text, as in 句点 くてん, "period; full stop," and 句読 くとう, "breaks and pauses (in a sentence)"; a phrase, as in 禁句 きんく, "taboo word; forbidden topic," 語句 ごく, "words and phrases; words," 成句 せいく, "idiom; set phrase; idiomatic phrase," and 文句; a unit of a verse form such as waka, renga, haikai or kanshi, as in 句法 くほう, "conventions to be followed in composing Japanese poetry; phraseology; diction," and 俳句; and a haiku itself, as in 句集 くしゅう, "collection of haiku poems," and 句会 くかい, "gathering of haiku poets."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 勹, a shape that wraps around, with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it. Imagine a mouth held snugly in a wrapper: one short phrase kept together as a single unit. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 勹 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ク | on | [俳句](../words/俳句.md) はいく — haiku; [句](../words/句.md) く — phrase; verse; [句読点](../words/句読点.md) くとうてん — punctuation marks; [文句](../words/文句.md) もんく — complaint; phrase |

In 俳句, 俳 supplies ハイ. In 句読点, 読 supplies トウ, not the familiar ドク, and 点 supplies テン. In 文句, 文 supplies モン. The single-kanji word 句 writes ク on its own.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ク, with 句集, 字句 and 節句 as examples. Practice selects ク, the only reading.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [俳](俳.md), "haiku; actor," in 俳句, [読](読.md), "read," and [点](点.md), "point; dot," in 句読点, and [文](文.md), "sentence, writing," in 文句. The word 句 contains only 句 itself.
- [口](口.md), "mouth," is the key inside 勹. [旬](旬.md), "ten days; peak season," has 日 inside the same 勹, and [包](包.md), "wrap; enclose," wraps a smaller shape in it too. [久](久.md), "a long time," and [苦](苦.md), "bitter; suffering," are also read ク. All are different kanji.
- [文](文.md), "sentence, writing," [語](語.md), "language, word," [詞](詞.md), "words; lyrics; part of speech," [辞](辞.md), "words; resign," [言](言.md), "say; words," and [俳](俳.md), "haiku; actor," overlap the senses "phrase," "words" and "haiku" that 句 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "phrase" or "words" as exclusive to any one character.
