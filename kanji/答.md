# 答 — answer

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

答 concerns giving an answer or a solution. [答える](../words/答える.md) (こたえる) is the verb "answer";
答え (こたえ) is the noun.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** the bamboo-like clusters at the top sit above 合.
Imagine a [mouth](口.md) under that little roof giving an answer to questions written on bamboo slips.

## Common words

- [答える](../words/答える.md) こたえる — answer; reply
- 答え こたえ — answer
- 回答 かいとう — reply to an inquiry
- 解答 かいとう — answer or solution to a problem

## Notes

Use 質問に答える (しつもんにこたえる), "answer a question": the question takes に.
For an answer treated as an object, 答えを書く (こたえをかく) means "write the answer."

[会](会.md) has a similar roof-like shape but no bamboo top or 口 at the bottom.
[茶](茶.md) has a grass-like top rather than 答's bamboo-like clusters.
