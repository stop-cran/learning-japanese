# 選 — choose

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

選 concerns selecting one or more possibilities. [選ぶ](../words/選ぶ.md) えらぶ is the verb "choose,"
and 選択 せんたく names a choice or selection.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the two curled shapes at the top as two options, with 辶 leading toward the selected one.
This visual story is not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

Mark the chosen thing with を: 本を選ぶ (ほんをえらぶ), "choose a book."
The て-form is 選んで (えらんで).
[遊](遊.md) has a single 子 low on the right, unlike the paired curls and broad base inside 選.
