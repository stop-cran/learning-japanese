# 葬 — bury; funeral

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

葬 is about laying the dead to rest. KANJIDIC2 gives “interment” and “bury,” and Digital Daijisen explains it as burying the dead (死者をほうむる) and also the ceremony for that (その儀式). In [葬式](../words/葬式.md) そうしき, which JMdict glosses as “funeral,” 葬 supplies ソウ in the whole word. The verb 葬る ほうむる means “to bury; to inter; to entomb,” and JMdict also gives it the sense “to cover up; to hush up; to shelve.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the grass crown 艹, also at the top of [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," sits above [死](死.md), "death," with the shape 廾 at the bottom. Imagine grass drawn over a resting place that stands on a low base: a burial. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 艹, 死 or 廾 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ソウ and ほうむる. KANJIDIC2 and Digital Daijisen give the same two readings, and Kanjipedia marks neither as outside Joyo.
- KANJIDIC2 records はふり as a name reading; it is a name use, not a quiz reading.

These six cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [埋](埋.md) (“bury; embed”): it overlaps the title's “bury,” and KANJIDIC2 lists “bury” for 葬.
- [儀](儀.md) (“ceremony; formal conduct”), [式](式.md) (“ceremony; formula”), [典](典.md) (“reference texts; rules; ceremony”) and [祭](祭.md) (“festival; ritual; worship”): Kanjipedia and Digital Daijisen both give 葬 the sense その儀式 (that ceremony, the rite for the dead), so “ceremony” and “ritual” overlap the title's “funeral.”
- [悔](悔.md) (“regret; repent; mourn; frustration”): Kanjipedia also gives とむらい (mourning the dead) for 葬, which overlaps “mourn.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 土 (“earth, soil”), 花 (“flower”), and 寺 (“Buddhist temple”); none means to bury the dead or a funeral.
