# 姻 — marriage; kinship by marriage

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

姻 concerns marriage and the ties it creates. In 婚姻 the focus is the union; in 姻族 (いんぞく) it is the people related by marriage.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 女 on the left and [因](因.md) on the right. Look inside the frame: 因 has 大, unlike the tree in [困](困.md) or the crossing strokes in [囲](囲.md).

## Notes

[始](始.md) shares the left side but has 台 on the right. Both [婚](婚.md) and 姻 concern marriage; they are the first and second characters, respectively, of 婚姻. Inside the frame, compare [困](困.md) and [囲](囲.md) with the 大 in 姻. [嫁](嫁.md) can describe marrying into a family; neither that act nor a bride is the whole meaning of 婚姻.
