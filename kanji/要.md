# 要 — necessity; essentials

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 襾 (no. 146) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

要 points to what is needed or central. [必要](../words/必要.md) ひつよう concerns necessity,
while 重要 じゅうよう concerns importance. These ideas overlap, but "important" is not a reason
to translate every 要 word as "necessary."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the top part covering something you need to keep.
This is a memory aid only. The canonical radical 襾 appears in the upper 覀 shape,
above 女; the component picture does not establish a historical meaning.

## Common words

- [必要](../words/必要.md) ひつよう — necessary; needed
- 重要 じゅうよう — important; significant
- 要る いる — be needed; usually written in kana

## Notes

Use 必要な before a noun: 必要な物 (ひつようなもの), "things that are needed."
要る is a godan verb: its negative is いらない, not いない.
Compare the top with [西](西.md), and the bottom with [女](女.md).
[安](安.md) also contains 女 but has a different roof.
