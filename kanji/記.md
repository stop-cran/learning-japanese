# 記 — record in writing

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

記 is about putting something into a written account or record. [日記](../words/日記.md) にっき is a diary;
記録 きろく can be a record or the act of recording. Think of information retained in an account,
not simply any act of speaking.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine 言, words, being placed beside the curled 己 shape
as a written note. The scene is an invented aid; it does not identify the right part as a verified phonetic.

## Notes

日記 has a small っ: にっき. Do not pronounce it as にちき.
[起](起.md) also contains 己, but its other part is different.
[計](計.md) and [訪](訪.md) share the 言 on the left; check the entire right-hand component.
