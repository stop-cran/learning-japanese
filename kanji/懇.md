# 懇 — cordial; sincere

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

懇 is warm sincerity and friendliness. It appears in 懇談, in 懇切 (こんせつ), “kind and careful,” and in 懇親会 (こんしんかい), “a social gathering.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Above [心](心.md), “heart,” are 豸 on the left and 艮 on the right; 艮 is also the right side of [恨](恨.md), “resent,” and [根](根.md), “root,” which are read コン, like 懇. [墾](墾.md), “clear land,” has the same top over 土 and is also read コン.

## Notes

[墾](墾.md), [恨](恨.md) and [根](根.md) share parts and the reading コン, but mean clear land for farming, resent and root. [親](親.md) (close), [厚](厚.md) (cordial), [寧](寧.md) (polite), [密](密.md) (close), [真](真.md) (true), [忠](忠.md) (loyalty), [丹](丹.md) (sincerity) and [允](允.md) (sincerity) overlap with warmth, closeness or sincerity. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 58](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=58) lists コン, with 懇切 and 懇親会, and ねんごろ, with 懇ろだ.
