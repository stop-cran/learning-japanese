# 慰 — console; comfort; solace

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慰 is easing someone's sad or troubled heart: consoling, comforting, and also taking one's mind off things. [慰める](../words/慰める.md) なぐさめる is to comfort or console; JMdict glosses it "to comfort; to console; to amuse."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for イ: caring for someone's feelings and calming them. Among its examples are 慰安 いあん, "solace; relaxation"; 慰問 いもん, "visit to console someone; giving comfort (to the sick, injured, etc.); expressing condolences (sympathy, concern, etc.); sympathy call"; 慰霊 いれい, "consoling the spirits of the dead"; 慰労 いろう, "recognition of services"; and 弔慰 ちょうい, which JMdict marks as a formal or literary term, "condolence; sympathy." It gives the kun readings なぐさめる and なぐさむ, the same as KANJIDIC2. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the bottom, and above it a wide shape whose right side is [寸](寸.md), "sun (length unit); small." Imagine a small hand reaching down to pat a hurting heart until it calms. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 寸 or 心 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 慰める, 慰 writes なぐさ and める is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 慰む uses the same なぐさ with む; it is mostly intransitive, feeling comforted oneself, while 慰める comforts someone else. JMdict also gives 慰む a transitive sense, "to trifle with; to fool around with." In 慰問, 慰 supplies イ and 問 supplies モン.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows イ, with 慰安, 慰問 and 慰労 as examples; なぐさめる, with 慰める and 慰め なぐさめ, "comfort; consolation; diversion"; and なぐさむ, with 慰む and 慰み なぐさみ, "amusement; diversion (to pass time); pastime; recreation." Practice selects なぐさ.める, the reading with a linked word article. イ is the reading of most 慰 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; なぐさ.む is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 慰める is 慰 with okurigana only.
- [忘](忘.md), "forget," and [怒](怒.md), "anger," also have 心 at the bottom, and [封](封.md), "seal; close," has 寸 at the right. All are different kanji.
- [娯](娯.md), "recreation; amusement," and [楽](楽.md), "music, enjoyment, ease," overlap the senses "to amuse," "amusement," "recreation" and "relaxation" that 慰める, 慰み and 慰安 carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "comfort" or "amusement" as exclusive to any one character.
- [愉](愉.md), "cheerful; joyful; delight," shares amusement and good spirits: JMdict glosses 慰める "to comfort; to console; to amuse" and 慰む "to feel comforted; to be in good spirits," and 愉快 "pleasant; delightful; enjoyable; joyful; cheerful; amusing." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- [憩](憩.md), "rest; take a break; relax," shares relaxation: JMdict glosses 慰安 "solace; relaxation" and 憩い "rest; relaxation." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the イ sense: 慰撫 いぶ, "pacification; soothing," and 慰留 いりゅう, "dissuasion (from resigning, leaving, etc.); persuading (to stay)."
