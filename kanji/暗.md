# 暗 — dark

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

Start with darkness or lack of light: [暗い](../words/暗い.md) くらい describes a dim room
or a dark path. A compound may require its own meaning: 暗記 あんき means memorization,
not "a dark record."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 日 on the left and 音 on the right; imagine relying on sounds
when the light is low. This is an invented memory aid, not an etymology or a verified phonetic analysis.

## Notes

[音](音.md) is the right-hand shape by itself; [意](意.md) puts 心 under that shape.
[映](映.md) has 日 on the left but not 音 on the right.
For literal lighting, [明るい](../words/明るい.md) あかるい, "bright," is a useful opposite.
Darkness and black color are not the same property.
