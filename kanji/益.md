# 益 — benefit; profit; gain

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 皿 (no. 108), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

益 is the good that comes from something: benefit, use, advantage, and the money gained as profit. [利益](../words/利益.md) りえき is "profit; gains" and also "benefit; advantage; good; interests (e.g. of society)." JMdict also has 益 alone, read えき, as a noun: "benefit; use; good; advantage; gain."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for エキ. One example of each:

- to increase: 増益 ぞうえき, "increased (profit)";
- useful, or being of use: 有益 ゆうえき, "beneficial; profitable; useful; helpful; instructive";
- profit, gain: 利益 りえき, "profit; gains."

For ヤク it gives being of use, as in 益体 やくたい, "usefulness; utility; being orderly; being proper," and 御利益 ごりやく, "divine grace; divine favour; blessing; answer to a prayer." Its kun readings are ます and ますます. More Daijisen examples of the エキ senses are listed in the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [皿](皿.md), "dish; plate," at the bottom. Imagine a dish that holds more than it needs, so the extra is your gain. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 皿 or the part above it contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| エキ | on | [利益](../words/利益.md) りえき — profit; benefit |
| ヤク | on | 御利益 ごりやく — divine grace; divine favour; blessing; answer to a prayer; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ま.す | kun | 益す ます — "to increase; to grow"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 利益, 利 supplies リ and 益 supplies エキ. JMdict lists the second reading りやく for 利益 in a religious sense of "grace," usually as ご〜; that is a separate reading of the same spelling and is not taught here. In 御利益, 御 supplies ご, 利 supplies り and 益 supplies ヤク, so ヤク is a separate listed reading, not the reading エキ. In 益す, JMdict gives 増す as the main spelling and 益す as a "rarely used kanji form," so that spelling is rare. Kanjipedia also lists ますます, and JMdict has ますます, "increasingly; more and more," written 益々, a "word usually written using kana alone." In 益体 and 益荒男 ますらお, "hero; manly person; warrior," the whole word has its own reading, so they are not split into per-kanji readings.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows エキ, with 有益 and 利益, and also 益する えきする, "to benefit (the world, society, etc.); to be of use to; to be of service to," and ヤク, with 御利益. It has no kun-reading row for 益. Practice selects エキ, the reading of the linked word article. ヤク and ま.す are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [利](利.md), "benefit; advantage," in 利益. 利 supplies リ; 益 supplies エキ.
- 利 has the title "benefit; advantage," a direct overlap with this card's "benefit." This card cannot change the 利 card, so the separation is made here: 利 is listed in this card's quiz exclusions and is never offered as a wrong answer.
- [得](得.md), "gain; acquire," [増](増.md), "increase," [加](加.md), "add," [享](享.md), "enjoy; receive benefits," [用](用.md), "use, purpose," [功](功.md), "merit; achievement," [効](効.md), "efficacy; effectiveness," and [恵](恵.md), "blessing; favor; grace," overlap the senses "benefit," "gain," "increase," "use" and "blessing" that 益 and its words carry. 増 is also read ま.す. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [役](役.md), "role; duty," is read ヤク and エキ, and [駅](駅.md), "station," is read エキ. They are different kanji sharing a reading with 益, so they are never offered as wrong answers.
- [皿](皿.md), "dish; plate," is the bottom part of 益, so its shape looks plausible, but it is read ベイ or さら and means a dish. [雨](雨.md), "rain," and [魚](魚.md), "fish," are everyday kanji with strokes of the same general weight; they are read ウ, あめ and ギョ, うお and mean rain and fish. None of the three shares a reading or a meaning with 益.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples: of the sense "useful," 益鳥 えきちょう, "beneficial bird," and 益友 えきゆう, "good friend; useful friend"; of the sense "profit," 益金 えききん, "profit."
