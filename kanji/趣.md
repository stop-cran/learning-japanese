# 趣 — elegance; gist

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 走 (no. 156) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

趣 covers two groups of meaning: the flavour or charm of something, and the gist or aim behind it. KANJIDIC2 lists “purport”, “gist”, “elegance”, “interest”, “proceed to”, “tend” and “become”. Kanjipedia's first sense is おもむき。あじわい。ようす。 (charm; flavour; state of things), and its second is 心の向かうところ。ねらい。考え。わけ。 (where the heart is directed; aim; idea; reason). In [趣味](../words/趣味.md) しゅみ, JMdict's first sense is “hobby; pastime” and its second is “tastes; preference; liking”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [走](走.md), "run," built from [土](土.md), "earth, soil," over the shape 龰, wraps the left side and the bottom. At the upper right sits [取](取.md), "take," built from [耳](耳.md), "ear," and the shape 又. Picture someone running off to take up the hobby that has caught their ear. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 走 or 取 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists シュ, with 趣向 しゅこう (“plan; idea; design; plot”, JMdict 1328970), 趣味 and 興趣 as examples, and おもむき, with 趣. Kanjipedia lists おもむく outside the table (外).
- Kanjipedia also lists ソク and うながす outside the table, for its second meaning group うながす。せきたてる。 (urge; hurry). They are not KANJIDIC2 readings, so they have no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 趣. Digital Daijisen gives the name reading とし. This is a name-use form, not a quiz reading.

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [旨](旨.md) (“purport; gist; delicious”): KANJIDIC2 lists “purport” for both 旨 and 趣, and JMdict glosses 趣旨 しゅし “meaning; point (e.g. of a statement); gist; effect” (entry 1328990).
- [味](味.md) (“taste, flavor”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 趣 includes あじわい (flavour), and JMdict 趣 おもむき sense 4 includes “taste” (entry 1328960).
- [興](興.md) (“revival; interest; pleasure”): KANJIDIC2 lists “interest” for both 興 and 趣, and JMdict glosses 興趣 きょうしゅ “interest (in something)” (entry 1238310).
- [意](意.md) (“meaning, intention”): JMdict 趣 おもむき sense 1 is “meaning; tenor; gist”, and JMdict glosses 趣意 しゅい “opinion; view; purport; tenor; meaning; point; gist” (entry 2869123).
- [志](志.md) (“will; aspiration; intention”): KANJIDIC2 gives 志 “intention”, JMdict 趣旨 sense 2 is “goal; intent; object; aim; point”, and Kanjipedia's second sense of 趣 begins 心の向かうところ (where the heart is directed).
- [況](況.md) (“situation; circumstances”): the 況 card lists おもむき among its readings, and Kanjipedia's first sense of 趣 includes ようす (state of things).
- [姿](姿.md) (“figure; appearance; posture”) and [容](容.md) (“contain; appearance”): JMdict 趣 おもむき sense 3 is “appearance; aspect”, which overlaps the “appearance” in both titles.
- [粋](粋.md) (“stylish; essence; pure”): KANJIDIC2 gives 粋 “chic” and “style”, and JMdict 粋 いき sense 1, “chic; smart; stylish; tasteful; refined; sophisticated” (entry 1372410), is close to 趣 おもむき sense 4, “grace; charm; refinement; taste; elegance”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 嫌 (“dislike; suspicion”), 誇 (“pride; boast”), and 恥 (“shame”); none means elegance, charm or the gist of something.
