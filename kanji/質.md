# 質 — quality; substance

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

質 often concerns a thing's quality, substance, or nature. It also appears in [質問](../words/質問.md) しつもん,
"question; inquiry"; do not translate that word mechanically as "quality question."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the two 斤 shapes above 貝 can suggest two inspectors checking an item's quality.
This is a visual story for remembering the character, not a verified origin.

## Notes

Learn 質問 with [問](問.md), "ask," and 品質 with [品](品.md), "goods."
Compare [貸](貸.md) and [買](買.md): their lower 貝 shape is similar, but the tops differ.
The reading シチ belongs to particular words such as 質屋; it does not replace シツ freely.
