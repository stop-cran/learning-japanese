# 剛 — sturdy; unyielding strength

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

剛 concerns strength, sturdiness or an unyielding quality, physically or in character. [剛健](../words/剛健.md) ごうけん describes robust bodily or mental vigor; 剛性 ごうせい means stiffness or rigidity.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 岡-shaped left block, with an enclosing 冂 and a [山](山.md)-shaped part inside, holding firm beside the blade-shaped 刂 on the right. This invented scene is a memory aid, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

## Notes

- The indexing radical is [刀](刀.md); its visible form here is 刂. [削](削.md), "shave; sharpen; pare," [剖](剖.md), "dissect; split open," and [則](則.md), "rule; follow a model," share right-hand 刂 but have different left blocks and meanings.
- [強](強.md) also concerns strength, and [力](力.md) power. Their meanings overlap with 剛; they are not mutually exclusive categories. For the everyday adjective "strong," learn [強い](../words/強い.md) つよい rather than treating 剛 as a general standalone replacement.
- JMdict indexes 剛い under つよい as a search-only spelling. That is not a recommendation to write the ordinary adjective that way.
