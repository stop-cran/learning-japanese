# 梢 — treetop; branch tip

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[梢](../words/梢.md) こずえ names a **treetop or the tip of a branch**. It identifies an extremity, not the whole tree or every part of a branch. In compounds such as 末梢 (まっしょう), the meaning extends to an end or peripheral part.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) on the left and small separated strokes above a 月-shaped lower-right block. Imagine little tips at the outer edge of a tree. This describes the modern shape for memory, not its historical formation.

## Notes

- This card asks こずえ and preserves both full kun readings in their original order. The spelling is **こずえ**, with ず, not づ.
- [枝](枝.md) names a branch or twig; 梢 focuses on its tip. [末](末.md) has a broader end/tip meaning. They are useful comparisons, not unambiguous wrong choices.
- The source-only くすのき entry is not permission to replace [楠](楠.md) with 梢 in an ordinary camphor-tree reference. The linked 楠 article actually teaches くすのき.
- 末梢's figurative minor-details sense and its anatomical uses are senses of those compounds; they do not make the independent こずえ noun mean a trivial matter or a nerve.
- Compare [消](消.md), extinguish or erase, which shares the right-side shape but has the three-dot water form on the left. [晴](晴.md), clear skies, has 日 and a different upper-right shape; [深](深.md), deep, has a 木-shaped part at the lower right instead.
