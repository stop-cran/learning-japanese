# 偶 — chance; even numbers

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

偶 has two useful entry points: chance in [偶然](../words/偶然.md) ぐうぜん and even numbers in
偶数 ぐうすう. Learn these as separate word families rather than trying to translate both with "accident."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the person-shaped 亻 unexpectedly meeting the boxed, long-tailed
shape on the right. This is a modern visual association, not a historical explanation.

## Common words

- [偶然](../words/偶然.md) ぐうぜん — by chance; coincidence
- 偶数 ぐうすう — even number

## Notes

偶数 includes numbers such as two and four: numbers divisible by two without a remainder.
In 偶然, the point is that something happens by chance, not that it is even-numbered.
[候](候.md), [側](側.md), and [値](値.md) share 亻; check the whole right-hand shape.
