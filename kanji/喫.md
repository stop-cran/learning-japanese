# 喫 — consume; take in

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

喫 connects eating, drinking or smoking with taking something in. 喫茶店 きっさてん names a coffee shop or tearoom, while 喫煙 きつえん is tobacco smoking. Learn the whole words rather than translating every occurrence as eat.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 口 on the left taking something in beside the dense strokes on the right. This association is not a verified historical construction.

## Notes

- The キツ reading becomes きっ in 喫茶店, whose complete reading is きっさてん. Do not mechanically read it as きつちゃてん.
- The exact 喫む のむ entry used here means smoking tobacco; it is not evidence for substituting this spelling into every beverage use of 飲む.
- The right-hand part contains a 刀-like shape above 大; the indexing radical remains the left-hand 口.
