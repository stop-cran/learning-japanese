# 信 — trust; belief

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

信 concerns believing or trusting. [信じる](../words/信じる.md) しんじる can mean accepting a claim as true
or trusting a person; 信用 しんよう names trust or confidence.
What is believed must be supplied by the word or sentence around the character.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see a person beside [言](言.md), "words," and imagine trusting what that person says.
This is a modern mnemonic; it does not establish a historical classification or phonetic component.
