# 歴 — history; passage of time

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 止 (no. 77) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

歴 concerns history, a course through time, or what has been passed through. 歴史 れきし is history. The separately established expression 歴とした れっきとした means genuine or fully fledged and is usually written in kana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the 厂-like outer strokes, the pair of 木 shapes, and 止 below, and imagine stopping to trace a path through old woods. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology.

## Notes

- Keep the small っ in れっきとした. Its dictionary-listed spelling supplies a concrete レッキ example, but JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana.
- 歴とした qualifies something as genuine or fully fledged; its meaning is not obtained by substituting the English word history into a sentence.
- [史](史.md) also concerns history or records. This semantic overlap makes it a useful comparison, not a reliable wrong-answer meaning for 歴.
