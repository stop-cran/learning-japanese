# 災 — calamity; disaster; misfortune

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

災 is a calamity: something bad that strikes a person or a place, from a fire to a natural disaster to a run of bad luck. The linked [火災](../words/火災.md) かさい is a fire as a damaging event, and JMdict glosses it "conflagration; fire." The linked [災難](../words/災難.md) さいなん is "calamity; misfortune; disaster," a misfortune that befalls someone.

Daijisen gives サイ the sense of a bad event that happens naturally or harms daily life, with 災害 さいがい, "calamity; disaster; misfortune," 災難, 災厄 さいやく, "calamity; disaster; accident," 火災, 震災 しんさい, "earthquake disaster," 天災 てんさい, "natural disaster; natural calamity; act of God," 人災 じんさい, "man-made calamity," and 防災 ぼうさい, "disaster preparedness; prevention of damage resulting from a natural disaster; protection against disasters." Daijisen's わざわい entry gives "unhappiness brought on a person" and "something unpleasant," the latter used mostly in exclamations.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [火](火.md), "fire," at the bottom, with a three-stroke 巛 shape above it. Imagine floodwater pouring down over a fire: two kinds of calamity at once. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 火 or 巛 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

In 火災, [火](火.md) supplies カ and 災 supplies サイ. In 災難, 災 supplies サイ and [難](難.md) supplies ナン. In 災害, 震災, 天災, 人災 and 防災, 災 also supplies サイ. In 災い, 災 writes わざわ and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict glosses 災い "disaster; calamity; misfortune; trouble; woes" and lists the spellings 禍 and 殃, with 殃 marked "rarely used kanji form."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows サイ with 災害, 災難 and 火災, and わざわい with 災い. Practice selects サイ, the reading of the two linked word articles. わざわ.い is shown with a dictionary example and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [火](火.md), "fire," in 火災 and [難](難.md), "difficult," in 災難.
- [巡](巡.md), "go around; patrol; tour," has the same 巛 shape. [炊](炊.md), "cook rice; boil," and [灰](灰.md), "ashes," contain 火 as 災 does. All are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [厄](厄.md), "misfortune; calamity," [凶](凶.md), "ill fortune; evil," [害](害.md), "harm," [難](難.md), "difficult," and [損](損.md), "loss; damage," overlap the senses of 災 and its words: calamity, misfortune, damage and trouble. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "calamity" or "misfortune" as exclusive to any one character.
