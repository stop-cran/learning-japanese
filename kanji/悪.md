# 悪 — bad, evil

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 心 (heart, no. 61) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

悪 marks something bad, undesirable, or morally wrong. In everyday descriptions it need not mean "evil": bad weather or poor health also uses this family of meanings.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the flat, barred upper part as a heavy lid over [心](心.md), making the heart feel bad. This is a memory image, not a historical explanation.

## Common words

- [悪い](../words/悪い.md) わるい — bad, poor
- 悪口 わるぐち・わるくち — insulting remarks, bad-mouthing
- 悪意 あくい — ill will, malice
- 悪化 あっか — getting worse

## Notes

悪化 is あっか, not あくか: learn the small っ in this word. For ordinary "bad", start with わるい rather than treating every metadata reading as an interchangeable pronunciation.

[意](意.md) and [急](急.md) also have 心 below, but different upper portions. 良い よい (good), also commonly いい, contrasts with 悪い.
