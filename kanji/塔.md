# 塔 — tower; pagoda

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

塔 names a tower, steeple or pagoda. The standalone noun is 塔 とう; 仏塔 ぶっとう names a Buddhist stupa or pagoda.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 土 supporting the tall right-hand stack, with its upper 艹-like strokes above a 合-like shape.

## Notes

- Learn 仏塔 as ぶっとう, with a small っ. The complete word does not require adding a separate ットウ reading to 塔's metadata.
- This source inventory records トウ and no kun-reading. The native word for another kind of tall structure is not automatically a reading of 塔.
- The radical is 土 on the left, not the plant-like strokes at the top right.
