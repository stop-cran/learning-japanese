# 座 — sit; seating

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

座 belongs to the vocabulary of sitting and seats.
[座る](../words/座る.md) すわる is the action of sitting down or being seated;
座席 ざせき names seating or a seat.
You can sit on a chair or on the floor, so 座る does not itself specify furniture.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see two 人-like shapes over 土 inside 广, as if two people had taken their places under a shelter.
This describes a memory image of the current components, not verified etymology.

## Notes

In 椅子に座る (いすにすわる), "sit on a chair," に marks where the person sits.
[立つ](../words/立つ.md) たつ, "stand up," is a useful action contrast.
For the written shape, distinguish the inside of 座 from [庭](庭.md), [広](広.md), and [店](店.md);
all four have an outer 广.
