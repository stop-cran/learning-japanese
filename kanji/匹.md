# 匹 — small-animal counter; equal

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 匸 (no. 23) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

匹 is a counter for small animals when read ひき. In 匹敵 ひってき it instead contributes the idea of matching or equaling. A counter reading and a compound reading serve different purposes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the right-open outer enclosure around two small leg-like strokes, like a small animal in a pen.

The canonical indexing radical is 匸, the outer enclosure open on the right. The inner spreading strokes are not its radical.

## Common words

- [匹敵](../words/匹敵.md) ひってき - be a match for; equal

## Notes

- Counter pronunciations change with the number: 一匹 いっぴき, one small animal; 二匹 にひき, two; 三匹 さんびき, three. These illustrate ぴき, ひき and びき; do not simply append unchanged ひき to every number.
- 匹敵する ひってきする compares someone or something with a match or equal; it does not count animals. Learn the small っ in that whole word.
