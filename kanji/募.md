# 募 — recruit; solicit

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

募 concerns seeking people, applications, or contributions. 募集 ぼしゅう is recruitment or taking applications, and 募金 ぼきん is fundraising. The native verb 募る つのる also describes a feeling growing stronger.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 艹-shaped top, 日-shaped center, and spreading lower strokes above 力, as though gathering many people's efforts.

The canonical indexing radical is 力 at the bottom, not the conspicuous 艹-shaped top.

## Notes

- The recruiting side 募集する seeks applicants; the applicant 応募する responds. These compounds share 募 but describe different sides of the process.
- 募る has both intransitive and transitive uses: 不安が募る ふあんがつのる, anxiety grows, versus 参加者を募る さんかしゃをつのる, seek participants. The particle and context distinguish the uses.
