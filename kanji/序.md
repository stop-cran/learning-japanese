# 序 — order; preface; beginning

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53), wrapping the top and left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

序 is the order in which things are lined up, and the part that comes first: the opening of something, or the preface at the front of a book. [順序](../words/順序.md) じゅんじょ is the order or sequence of things, and also a procedure; JMdict glosses it "order; sequence" and, in a second sense, "procedure." On its own, 序 じょ is also a noun; JMdict glosses it "order; ordering," "beginning; start" and "foreword; preface; introduction."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ジョ. One example of each:

- a line-up that follows some standard: 序列 じょれつ, "rank; ranking order; hierarchy";
- the preface of a book, or adding one: 序文 じょぶん, "preface; foreword; introduction";
- the start of something, its first part: 序曲 じょきょく, "overture; prelude";
- a school in ancient China: 庠序 しょうじょ, "school," which JMdict marks archaic; here 庠 supplies しょう and 序 supplies じょ.

It gives the kun reading ついで. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 广, a roof shape with a dot on top that wraps the top and left side (it has no card here), and under it [予](予.md), "beforehand." Imagine everything prepared beforehand and laid out in order under one roof, with the preface placed first. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 广 or 予 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジョ | on | [順序](../words/順序.md) じゅんじょ — order; sequence; procedure |
| つい.で | kun | 序で ついで - opportunity; occasion; chance; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ついで | kun | 序 ついで - opportunity; occasion; chance; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 順序, 順 supplies ジュン and 序 supplies ジョ, with no sound change. The dot in つい.で marks okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji: in the spelling 序で, 序 writes つい and で is okurigana. KANJIDIC2 also lists ついで without a dot, which matches the spelling 序, where the kanji writes the whole word. JMdict has both spellings in one entry, 序で first; it marks the sense "opportunity; occasion; chance" as usually written in kana, and its other senses, "order; sequence" and "successor," as archaic. The related adverb ついでに, "while (one is at it); when; on the occasion of; at the same time (as)," is also usually written in kana; JMdict lists 序でに and 序に as rarely used kanji forms.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ジョ, with 序幕 じょまく, "curtain-raiser; prologue," 順序 and 秩序 ちつじょ, "order; discipline; regularity; system; method," as examples. つい.で and ついで are not in the official table. Practice selects ジョ, the reading with a linked word article; つい.で and ついで are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [順](順.md), "order; sequence," in 順序.
- [広](広.md), "wide, spacious," and [庁](庁.md), "government agency," have the same 广 wrapping the top and left, with a different shape inside. [預](預.md), "deposit; entrust," has 予, the shape inside 序, at its left. All are different kanji.
- [順](順.md), "order; sequence," [番](番.md), "turn in a series," and [整](整.md), "put in order; adjust," overlap the senses "order" and "sequence" that 序 and 順序 carry; 順 appears with 序 in 順序. [始](始.md), "begin, start," [緒](緒.md), "cord; beginning," and [初](初.md), "first time; initial," overlap the sense "beginning." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "order" or "beginning" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ジョ senses: 花序 かじょ, "inflorescence," 公序 こうじょ, "public order; public policy," 次序 じじょ, "order; system," and 秩序; 自序 じじょ, "authors preface"; and 序説 じょせつ, "introduction," 序奏 じょそう, "introduction," 序盤 じょばん, "early stages; initial phase; outset," 序幕 and 序論 じょろん, "introduction; preface."
