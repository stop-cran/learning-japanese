# 覧 — look over; peruse

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 見 (no. 147) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

覧 means looking over something carefully or widely. Kanjipedia gives みる, よくみる (to look closely) and 広くながめる (to view widely), and KANJIDIC2 lists “perusal” and “see.” In [展覧会](../words/展覧会.md) てんらんかい, JMdict's gloss is “exhibition.” Digital Daijisen also gives a respectful sense, 見ることを敬っていう語 (a respectful word for seeing), as in [御覧](../words/御覧.md) ごらん. JMdict lists ご覧 first and also 御覧, glossing the noun as respectful “seeing; looking; watching.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [臣](臣.md), "retainer; subject," stands at the upper left beside the small shape 𠂉 (a slanting stroke with a short horizontal stroke to its right) and one more horizontal stroke below it, and [見](見.md), "see, look," sits underneath. Imagine a retainer standing high above a hall, looking over everything below. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 臣 or 見 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ラン only, with the traditional form 覽 in parentheses. KANJIDIC2 gives no name readings for 覧; Digital Daijisen gives かた, ただ and み as name readings, not quiz readings.

These eleven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [視](視.md) (“look at; regard as”): KANJIDIC2 gives both characters “see”.
- [見](見.md) (“see, look”) and [看](看.md) (“watch over; see”): KANJIDIC2 lists “see” for 覧, and Kanjipedia's sense begins with みる (to see).
- [観](観.md) (“view; observe”), [眺](眺.md) (“gaze; view; look out over”) and [察](察.md) (“observe; infer”): Kanjipedia gives よくみる (to look closely) and 広くながめる (to view widely) for 覧.
- [読](読.md) (“read”): KANJIDIC2 lists “perusal” for 覧, and JMdict glosses 閲覧 えつらん as “inspection; reading; perusal.”
- [検](検.md) (“inspect; examine; check”) and [査](査.md) (“investigate; inspect”): JMdict's gloss of 閲覧 also includes “inspection.”
- [仰](仰.md) (“look up; revere”): looking up at something and looking over something are both ways of looking at it, so its “look up” overlaps the title's “look over.”
- [監](監.md) (“oversee; watch over”): Digital Daijisen's first sense, 全体を視野に収めて眺める (taking the whole in view), overlaps “oversee.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 書 (“write”), 歌 (“song, sing”), and 絵 (“drawing; painting”); none means looking over or perusing.
