# 宴 — banquet; feast; party

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宴 is a banquet or feast: people gathering to eat, drink and enjoy themselves together. [宴会](../words/宴会.md) えんかい is a banquet or party; JMdict glosses it "party; banquet; reception; feast; dinner." On its own, 宴 is read うたげ, which JMdict marks as a poetical term, "party; banquet; feast," and also えん, which it marks as formal or literary, "banquet; party; feast."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for エン. The first is a drinking party or feast, with examples such as 宴会, 宴席 えんせき, "banquet; dinner party," 賀宴 がえん, "banquet," 饗宴 きょうえん, "feast; banquet," 酒宴 しゅえん, "drinking party; banquet; feast," and 祝宴 しゅくえん, "celebratory banquet; feast." The second is enjoying oneself and relaxing, with one compound that JMdict does not list. It gives the kun reading うたげ, the same as KANJIDIC2. A separate Daijisen headword えん uses 宴 as a noun, as in 宴を張る えんをはる, "to give a dinner party; to hold a banquet." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the roof-like 宀 at the top, [日](日.md), "sun, day," in the middle, and [女](女.md), "woman, female," at the bottom. Imagine a house where the party under the roof goes on all day, and a host stands at the door to welcome every guest. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 宀, 日 or 女 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| エン | on | [宴会](../words/宴会.md) えんかい — banquet; party |
| うたげ | kun | 宴 うたげ - party; banquet; feast; JMdict marks it a poetical term; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 宴会, 宴 supplies エン and 会 supplies カイ, with no sound change. The word 宴 read うたげ is the kanji alone, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows a kanji in many words. JMdict also has 花見の宴 はなみのうたげ, "cherry blossom viewing party," where 宴 keeps うたげ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only エン, with 宴会, 宴席 and 酒宴 as examples. うたげ is not in the official table; the Daijisen headword うたげ also marks this reading of 宴 as outside it. Practice selects エン, the reading with a linked word article; うたげ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [会](会.md), "meet," in 宴会.
- [室](室.md), "room, chamber," and [宿](宿.md), "lodging," have the same 宀 at the top, and [要](要.md), "necessity; essentials," has the same 女 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [祭](祭.md), "festival; ritual; worship," [祝](祝.md), "celebrate; congratulate," [楽](楽.md), "music, enjoyment, ease," [享](享.md), "enjoy; receive benefits," [会](会.md), "meet," [酒](酒.md), "alcohol; sake," and [飯](飯.md), "cooked rice; meal," overlap the senses "feast," "party," "enjoying oneself" and "drinking party" that 宴 and 宴会 carry; 会 appears with 宴 in 宴会, 祝 in 祝宴 and 酒 in 酒宴. [党](党.md), "political party; faction," shares only the English word "party." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "party" or "feast" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first エン sense: 小宴 しょうえん, "small (dinner) party"; 招宴 しょうえん, "banquet; invitation to a party; party"; and 盛宴 せいえん, "grand banquet." JMdict also has 披露宴 ひろうえん, "reception (e.g. wedding); banquet; celebration; party."
