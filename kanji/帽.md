# 帽 — hat; headgear

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

帽 concerns a hat or head covering. 帽子 ぼうし means hat or cap; 子 is part of that established noun, not a separate child being described.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 巾 on the left and the stacked box-like shapes on the right; imagine cloth covering the top of a head. This is a visual mnemonic, not a historical or phonetic analysis.
