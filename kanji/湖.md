# 湖 — lake

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

湖 means lake, a body of inland water. The independent native word is みずうみ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the water strokes beside the 古-and-月 area: an old moon is reflected in a lake.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- Standalone 湖 is taught here as みずうみ. In lake names, 湖 can instead be the suffix こ.
- 湖 is the body of water; 湖岸 こがん specifically names its shore.
