# 児 — child; youngster

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (no. 10) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

児 concerns a child or young person. 児童 じどう means children, and [育児](../words/育児.md) いくじ is childcare or child-rearing. Some of the source's kun readings occur in specialized words or alternative spellings rather than providing a general spelling rule for こ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the box-like upper part standing on the two small lower legs of a child.

The classical radical 儿 is the pair of spreading strokes at the bottom. It is not the separate character 兄.
