# 囚 — prisoner; confine

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

囚 refers to a person held captive or confined. The ordinary compound [囚人](../words/囚人.md) (しゅうじん) means “prisoner”; it is not a claim that this is a current formal legal classification.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a person enclosed by the outer frame. This memory aid describes the visible shape only, not a verified historical derivation.

## Notes

- The English label “prisoner” is broader than any one legal status. The support article uses the general sense and notes the dictionary's historical usage warning.
- 囚 and [捕](捕.md), “catch; seize,” are related in meaning but are distinct characters and words; their meanings are kept apart in the quiz.
- Kanjipedia also lists the outside-table reading とらえる; it is not added to the KANJIDIC2 metadata or selected for questions.
