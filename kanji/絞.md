# 絞 — strangle; wring; squeeze

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

絞 is pulling something tight by twisting or pressing it: wringing out a cloth, squeezing out juice, or strangling. [絞る](../words/絞る.md) しぼる is the word taught here. JMdict glosses its first sense "to wring (towel, rag); to squeeze," with the note "esp. 絞る," and its second "to squeeze (fruit to extract juice); to press; to extract; to milk; to express milk," with the note "esp. 搾る." The word article teaches "wring; squeeze; narrow down."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for コウ: to tie tightly with a cord, to strangle, as in 絞罪 こうざい, "execution by hanging; hanging," 絞殺 and 絞首 こうしゅ, "hanging; strangling to death." It lists the kun readings しぼる, しめる and しまる. The JMdict entry 絞る has 13 senses; narrowing a range ("to narrow down (one's focus); to whittle down"), racking one's brains, stopping down a lens and scolding severely are extensions of wringing and are not the core taught here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left. Imagine a twisted thread being pulled tighter and tighter until water runs out of a cloth. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 contributes meaning or that the right part contributes sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| し.める | kun | 絞める しめる — "to strangle; to constrict"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| し.まる | kun | 絞まる しまる — "to be strangled; to be constricted"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 絞る, 絞 writes しぼ and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The noun 絞り しぼり has the same stem; JMdict glosses it "shibori; tie-dye; tie-dyeing," "aperture; aperture stop; stop," and "contraction; squeezing; choke." JMdict also lists 搾る as a second spelling of しぼる; 絞り上げる しぼりあげる, "to squeeze (to the utmost); to wring out," lists 搾り上げる as another spelling. In 絞殺, 絞 supplies コウ and 殺 supplies サツ; in 絞首刑, 絞 supplies コウ, [首](首.md), "neck," supplies シュ and 刑 supplies ケイ.

The verb 絞める しめる, "to strangle; to constrict," is a different word from 締める しめる, "to tie; to fasten; to tighten," which is taught on the 締 card. The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows コウ, with 絞殺 and 絞首刑; しぼる, with 絞る, 絞り上げる and 絞り, marked ⇔ 搾る; しめる, with 絞める, marked ⇔ 締める; and しまる, with 絞まる, marked ⇔ 締まる. 締まる しまる, among other glosses, is "to tighten; to be tightened." Practice selects しぼ.る, the reading of the linked word article 絞る しぼる. The other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 絞る is 絞 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji.
- Kanjipedia also lists くび.る for 絞 as a reading outside the official table (外). JMdict has 縊る くびる, "dated term to strangle to death," written with a different kanji, so this card does not claim that 絞 writes it.
- [締](締.md), "tighten; fasten; conclude," [緊](緊.md), "tense; tight; urgent," [縛](縛.md), "bind; tie up; restrain," [括](括.md), "tie together; enclose," [結](結.md), "tie; join; conclude," [束](束.md), "bundle," [圧](圧.md), "pressure; oppress," [押](押.md), "push," [詰](詰.md), "pack tightly; press," [握](握.md), "grip; grasp," [抽](抽.md), "pull out; extract; abstract," [殺](殺.md), "kill," and [刑](刑.md), "criminal punishment," overlap the senses of tightening, binding, pressing, extracting, strangling and execution by hanging that 絞 and its words carry. 絞, 締 and 緊 also share し.める (閉 and 占 do too), and 絞殺 is a way of killing. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [紙](紙.md), "paper," [終](終.md), "end; finish," and [夏](夏.md), "summer," look plausible because 紙 and 終 have the same thread key 糸 at the left, and 夏 is a common starter card. They are read シ and かみ; シュウ, お.わる and おわ.る; and カ, ガ, ゲ and なつ. None shares a reading or a meaning with 絞.
- Many other kanji read コウ, such as [稿](稿.md), "manuscript; draft," and [校](校.md), "school," so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
