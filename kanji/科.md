# 科 — department; course

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

科 helps label a division or branch, especially a subject of study or an institutional department.
In [科学](../words/科学.md) かがく, the established whole-word meaning is "science."
科 does not turn every compound containing it into the name of a science.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine sorting stalks represented by 禾 into separate departments beside the 斗 shape.
This is a learning image, not a verified origin story.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カ | on | [科学](../words/科学.md) かがく — science; 理科 りか — school science |
| カ | on | 学科 がっか — subject; department |

In 学科, the full reading is がっか, not がくか.

## Common words

- [科学](../words/科学.md) かがく — science
- 理科 りか — science, including the school subject
- 学科 がっか — subject of study; university department

## Notes

[料](料.md) shares the right-hand 斗 but has 米 rather than 禾 on the left.
[秋](秋.md) and [私](私.md) share 禾 and differ on the right.
Also distinguish 科学 from 化学 かがく, "chemistry": the pronunciation alone does not tell them apart.
