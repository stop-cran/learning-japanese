# 巡 — go around; patrol; tour

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 巛 (no. 47), at the top right, resting on 辶 · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

巡 is going around: making a circuit, touring from place to place, or patrolling an area. [巡る](../words/巡る.md) めぐる is to go around, recur or concern, and [巡査](../words/巡査.md) じゅんさ is a police officer or constable. JMdict's entry for 巡る lists, among its senses, "to go around; to make a circle around," "to come around (of a season, anniversary, turn, etc.); to return; to repeat; to circulate (of blood, money, etc.)" and "to travel around; to make a tour of."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ジュン: walking around from place to place, as in 巡業 じゅんぎょう, "provincial tour," 巡航 じゅんこう, "cruise; cruising," 巡礼 じゅんれい, "pilgrimage; pilgrim," and 一巡 いちじゅん, "one round; one tour; one circuit; one patrol"; going around to inspect, as in 巡査, 巡察 じゅんさつ, "(making) a round of inspection; patrolling," 巡視 じゅんし, "inspection tour," and 巡狩 じゅんしゅ, "Imperial visit"; and hesitating, as in 逡巡 しゅんじゅん, "hesitation; indecision." A separate Daijisen entry uses じゅん as a counter for complete rounds, as when a batting order goes around once.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 巛, the form of the key [川](川.md), "river," with three wavy strokes, resting on 辶, the stroke group that wraps the left side and the bottom. Imagine a winding stream running along a looping path that brings it back round to where it started. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 巛 or 辶 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

In 巡査, 巡 supplies ジュン and 査 supplies サ. In 巡る, 巡 writes めぐ and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The noun 巡り ends in り instead, which is why KANJIDIC2 lists めぐ.り as a separate reading; the same form begins 巡り歩く めぐりあるく, "to walk around; to travel around." The familiar word お巡りさん おまわりさん, "police officer," which JMdict marks as usually written in kana alone, reads 巡 as まわ; that reading is not among the KANJIDIC2 readings of 巡. [Kanjipedia's character entry](https://www.kanjipedia.jp/kanji/0003246000) does list まわる as a kun reading outside the official table, so this card's quiz does not offer まわる as a wrong answer.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジュン, with 巡回 じゅんかい, "going around; patrol; round; tour," 巡業 and 一巡 as examples, and めぐる, with 巡る and 巡り歩く, and prints お巡りさん with まわ in brackets as a special reading. It has no separate row for めぐり. Practice selects ジュン and めぐ.る, the readings with linked word articles. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so めぐ.る supplies the one question for that stem, shown as めぐる; めぐ.り is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [査](査.md), "investigate; inspect," in 巡査. 巡る is 巡 with okurigana only.
- 巛, the key at the top right, is a form of [川](川.md), "river," which has the same key, no. 47. [道](道.md), "road, way," and [達](達.md), "reach, plural suffix," have the same 辶 wrapping the left side and the bottom. [順](順.md), "order; sequence," has 川 at the left and is also read ジュン; JMdict lists 順 in the rarely used spellings 順礼 and 一順 of 巡礼 and 一巡. [純](純.md), "pure; genuine," is also read ジュン. All are different kanji.
- [回](回.md), "revolve; times," [周](周.md), "circumference; circuit," [循](循.md), "follow; cycle," [囲](囲.md), "surround; enclose," [旅](旅.md), "journey, travel," and [査](査.md), "investigate; inspect," overlap the senses "go around," "circuit," "circulate," "surround," "tour" and "inspect" that 巡 and its words carry; JMdict lists 回る as a rarely used spelling of 巡る, 循 is also read ジュン, and 査 appears in 巡査. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "go around" or "tour" as exclusive to any one character.
