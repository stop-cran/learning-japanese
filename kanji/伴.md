# 伴 — accompany; companion

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

伴 means a companion who goes along with someone, and going along with, accompanying or attending someone. KANJIDIC2 lists “consort”, “accompany”, “bring with” and “companion”. The word [同伴](../words/同伴.md) どうはん is “accompanying; being accompanied by; going with”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 亻, a variant of [人](人.md), "person," stands on the left, and [半](半.md), "half," stands on the right. Picture two friends walking side by side, each carrying half of a heavy load. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 亻 or 半 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ハン, バン and ともなう. Kanjipedia lists とも outside the table (外).
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading とも, and Digital Daijisen gives the name reading すけ; these are name uses, not quiz readings.

These eight cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [供](供.md) (“accompany, offer”), [連](連.md) (“link; take along”), [随](随.md) (“follow; at will”) and [輩](輩.md) (“fellows; companions”): 伴 can mean a companion or attendant, as in 伴 とも, “companion; follower; attendant; retinue”.
- [従](従.md) (“follow; obey; subordinate”): 伴 can mean following along with someone.
- [共](共.md) (“together; shared”): 伴 can mean going somewhere together.
- [友](友.md) (“friend”): 伴 can mean a companion, and 友 とも can mean “friend; companion; comrade; pal”.
- [送](送.md) (“send; escort”): accompanying someone; 送る おくる can mean “to see (someone) off; to escort; to accompany; to take”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 塩 (“salt”), 羊 (“sheep”), and 墨 (“ink; inkstick”); salt, sheep and ink are concrete things; none means accompanying or a companion.
