# 変 — change; strange

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 夂 (no. 34) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

変 has both change and strangeness uses. [変化](../words/変化.md) へんか is a change or transformation; 変な音 (へんなおと) is "a strange sound."
The adjective-like use needs な before a noun. It does not mean that every 変化 is strange.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the upper strokes being rearranged above the crossing lower strokes: a change of arrangement. This is not an explanation of the character's historical origin.

## Notes

The radical 夂 is the lower part. [夏](夏.md) shares a similar lower shape but has a different top.
Compare 色が変わる (いろがかわる), "the color changes," with 色を変える (いろをかえる), "change the color."
Do not confuse 変える with [帰る](帰.md) かえる, "return home," despite the matching pronunciation.

For 変な compared with 怪しい, 珍しい and 疑わしい, see [the article on 怪しい and related words](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
