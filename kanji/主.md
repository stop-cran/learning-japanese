# 主 — master, principal

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 丶 (no. 3) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

主 can identify the person in charge or the owner, and it can mean "main" or "principal."
[主人](../words/主人.md) has several person-related uses, while 主な means "main."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the top dot marking the most important position above the horizontal lines.
This is a mnemonic, not verified historical origin.

## Notes

[住](住.md) adds the person component 亻 to the left. Compare [生](生.md): its upper stroke arrangement is different from 主's dot.
The meaning of 主人 depends on context; it does not simply mean any important person.
