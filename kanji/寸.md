# 寸 — sun (length unit); small

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41), the whole character · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寸 names the Japanese length unit *sun* (すん): one tenth of a shaku,
about 3.03 cm in the standard measure. It also concerns measurement and
smallness or shortness. [寸法](../words/寸法.md) すんぽう means dimensions
or measurements, and has a separate plan/arrangement use.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine a short mark being measured
beside the hooked upright and its dot. This remembers the three-stroke
outline without claiming to reconstruct the unit's or character's origin.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| スン | on | [寸法](../words/寸法.md) すんぽう - dimensions; measurements |

Practice selects スン, the sole reading in the frozen source.
In 寸法, 寸 supplies すん and 法 supplies ぽう. 一寸 いっすん
means one sun or a little bit; learn its small っ.

The spelling 一寸 also belongs to the separate, usually-kana word
[ちょっと](../words/ちょっと.md), "a little; somewhat."
That rare whole-word spelling does not give 寸 a regular reading ちょっと.
Daijisen records an ancient 寸 き separately; it is outside this metadata.

## Common words

- [寸法](../words/寸法.md) すんぽう - dimensions; measurements; plan, by context
- 寸前 すんぜん - just before; on the verge of
- 寸暇 すんか - a brief moment of leisure
- 寸分 すんぶん / すんぷん - a very small amount

## Notes

- A 寸 is not an English inch. 寸法 itself does not require measurements
  in sun; the dimensions of a box may be stated in centimeters.
- [小](小.md), small, [少](少.md), a little, [短](短.md), short, and
  [細](細.md), fine/small in detail, overlap aspects of smallness.
  [計](計.md) and [測](測.md) also carry measuring meanings.
  These are comparisons, not interchangeable words in every sentence.
- [村](村.md), village, [付](付.md), attach, and [寺](寺.md),
  Buddhist temple, contain 寸 without inheriting its unit as their meaning.
