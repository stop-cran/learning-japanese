# 亥 — Boar zodiac sign

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 亠 (no. 8), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

亥 names the **twelfth zodiac sign**, the Boar, in the twelve-sign cycle.
The native sign noun is [亥](../words/亥.md) い. It is not the ordinary
name for a living wild boar, [猪](../words/猪.md) いのしし, or a domestic
pig, [豚](../words/豚.md) ぶた.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a zodiac label beneath
the two-stroke 亠 cap, with slanting strokes spreading below it.
This is a drawing memory aid, not a historical account of the character.

Only い is selected for practice. ガイ is independently documented in
calendar compounds; this does not make it the reading of the standalone
native noun. カイ remains in the full source list: the lack of a corroborated
example here does not establish that it is invalid or restricted to names.

## Notes

- [刻](刻.md), carve/time, has a similar left shape plus 刂.
  [交](交.md), intersect/mingle, and [亡](亡.md), deceased, share the
  亠 top but have different lower strokes. None names the Boar sign.
- In historical texts, 亥 can also identify an hour around 10 p.m.,
  a direction approximately north-northwest, or the tenth lunar month.
  These are not ordinary modern scheduling terms. The hour has differing
  dictionary mappings, including 9–11 p.m. and 10 p.m.–midnight;
  lunar month ten is not simply October.
- The twelve signs and the sixty combinations of calendar stems and
  branches are related systems, not identical lists.
- The source's grade-9 tag classifies name-use kanji; it does not make
  the sign noun a name-only word. Historical kana ゐ does not add another
  modern reading.
