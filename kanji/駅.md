# 駅 — station

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 馬 (no. 187) · **Phonetic:** 尺 (simplified from 睪) · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

駅 is a phonetic compound of 馬 (horse) and a sound component. The old form 驛 had 睪 as the phonetic; the new form replaced it with the simpler 尺.
The horse points to the original meaning: a relay post on a road where messengers changed horses (the old post-station system).
Modern Japanese used it for the railway station, and the meaning "post station" survives in historical words such as 宿駅 しゅくえき.

## Notes

The same phonetic part appears in 訳 (やく, translation; old form 譯) with a 言 on the left: do not confuse 駅 (horse) with 訳 (words).
It is also easy to mix up with [電](電.md) in train contexts, but they mean "station" and "electricity".
