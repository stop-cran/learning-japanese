# 国 — country

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **Phonetic:** none (the old form 國 contains 或, which is sometimes seen as a phonetic) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

国 is the Japanese simplified form of 國, in which an enclosure 囗 (borders) surrounds 或 (a territory guarded with a weapon: 戈 with 口). The newer form puts 玉 (jade, jewel) inside the border, a treasure within borders; this is easy to remember, but treat it as a mnemonic for the simplified shape.
It means "country, nation", and also "one's home region".

## Common words

- [外国](../words/外国.md) がいこく — foreign country
- 国 くに — country; hometown, home region
- 中国 ちゅうごく — China
- 外国人 がいこくじん — foreigner

## Notes

中国 is read ちゅうごく: the コク is voiced to ゴク in this word, while 韓国 かんこく, 英国 えいこく and 米国 べいこく keep コク.
国 can also mean one's home region: お国はどちらですか is a polite way to ask where someone is from.

Look-alikes: 玉 (jewel) is the inside part; [田](田.md) (rice field), 回 (times) and [四](四.md) (four) are other shapes inside a box. Characters with the same box include 園 (garden), 図 (diagram) and 団 (group).
