# 銅 — copper

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

銅 names copper. 銅貨 is a copper coin; the complete word specifies the material rather than every kind of money.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a copper object marked with the 同-shaped block beside 金. This is a modern-shape story, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

The indexing radical 金 is the narrow left-hand part.

## Notes

- The inspected family records both どう and あかがね for copper without a rarity flag. The bronze-medal abbreviation sense is restricted to どう; it is not automatically an あかがね sense.
- [同](同.md), same, is not the complete character 銅. [鉄](鉄.md) and [銀](銀.md) name different metals.
