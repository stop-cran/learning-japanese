# 迎 — welcome; greet

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

迎 concerns receiving someone who is coming toward you.
[迎える](../words/迎える.md) むかえる can mean going out to meet a visitor, not merely saying hello.
歓迎 かんげい emphasizes a welcoming reception.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the facing inner strokes as a host meeting a guest beside the sweeping path.
This is a modern visual memory aid, not a derivation.

## Notes

友達を迎える (ともだちをむかえる) is to welcome or go to meet a friend.
[会う](../words/会う.md) あう simply means meeting someone; it does not by itself assign you the role of receiver.
The radical 辵 appears as 辶. Compare the inner shapes of [近](近.md), [週](週.md), and [返](返.md).
