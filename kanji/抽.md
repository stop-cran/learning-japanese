# 抽 — pull out; extract; abstract

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

抽 is pulling something out from among others: extracting a part, drawing lots, or drawing out a general idea from particular things. [抽象](../words/抽象.md) ちゅうしょう is abstraction; JMdict glosses it "abstraction."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for チュウ: pulling out or drawing out, as in 抽出 ちゅうしゅつ, "extraction; abstraction," and in another sense "selection (from a group); sampling"; 抽象; and 抽籤 ちゅうせん, which JMdict lists as a rarely used kanji form of 抽選 ちゅうせん, "lottery; raffle; drawing (of lots); balloting." JMdict also has 抽象的 ちゅうしょうてき, "abstract." Daijisen gives the kun readings ぬきんでる and ぬく, which KANJIDIC2 does not list; JMdict has 抽んでる as a rarely used kanji form of 抜きん出る ぬきんでる, "to surpass; to outdo; to excel."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the form of 手, "hand," at the left, and [由](由.md), "reason; grounds," at the right. Imagine a hand pulling the one real reason out of a heap of excuses. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 手 or 由 contributes meaning or sound. [宙](宙.md), "space; midair," also contains 由 and is read チュウ, a plain fact about readings. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チュウ | on | [抽象](../words/抽象.md) ちゅうしょう — abstraction |
| ひき- | kun | 抽き出し ひきだし - drawer; JMdict heads the entry 引き出し and marks 抽き出し and 抽斗 as rarely used kanji forms, and the word article 引き出し uses the usual spelling; no word article uses this spelling yet, not selected for questions here |

In 抽象, 抽 supplies チュウ and 象 supplies ショウ, with no sound change; in 抽出, 出 supplies シュツ, and in 抽選, 選 supplies セン. The trailing dash in ひき- marks a prefix use, one that comes before another element; it is not pronounced. In 抽き出し, 抽 writes ひ, き is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji, and だし follows; JMdict also lists 抽出し as a search-only spelling, and Daijisen lists 抽斗 ひきだし as a hard-to-read word.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows チュウ, with 抽出 and 抽象 as examples. ひき- is not in the table. Practice selects チュウ, the reading with a linked word article. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [象](象.md), "elephant; image; shape," in 抽象.
- [油](油.md), "oil; grease," has the same 由 at the right with a different left side, and [届](届.md), "reach; deliver; notify," contains 由 under 尸. [押](押.md), "push," has the same 扌 at the left with 甲 at the right. All are different kanji.
- [抜](抜.md), "extract," [引](引.md), "pull; draw," [選](選.md), "choose," [択](択.md), "select; opt for," and [採](採.md), "pick; collect; adopt," overlap the senses of 抽 and its words: pulling out and extraction in 抽出, drawing lots in 抽選, and selection in the second sense of 抽出; 引 writes the usual spelling of ひきだし, 抜 that of ぬきんでる, and 選 appears with 抽 in 抽選. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "extract," "pull" or "select" as exclusive to any one character.
