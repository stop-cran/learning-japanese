# 丹 — red; sincerity

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 丶 (no. 3) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

丹 is red: a red mineral, a red pigment, or the color red. It can also mean a carefully prepared medicine, especially an elixir of immortality, and sincerity, a true heart. KANJIDIC2 lists “red”, “pills” and “sincerity”. The word [丹念](../words/丹念.md) たんねん means “painstaking; careful; meticulous”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 丹 starts with the frame shape 冂, whose first stroke is the slanting shape 丿. The dot shape 丶 comes next, and one last stroke finishes the character. Picture 冂 as a small clay pot, 丶 as one drop of red paint, and the last stroke as the brush that stirs it slowly and with care. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 冂, 丿 or 丶 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists タン only, with 丹念 and 丹精, and gives no kun reading. Kanjipedia gives に, あか and まごころ outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen gives the kun readings に and あかい.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings た, たみ and まこと, and Digital Daijisen gives あかし, あきら, に and まこと; these are name uses, not quiz readings.

These five cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [赤](赤.md) (“red”) and [紅](紅.md) (“crimson; deep red”): 丹 can mean red or a red pigment.
- [允](允.md) (“permit; sincerity”): 丹 can mean sincerity, a true heart.
- [薬](薬.md) (“medicinal drug”) and [剤](剤.md) (“medicinal preparation; chemical agent”): 丹 can mean a prepared medicine or pill, including an elixir, as in 仙丹 せんたん, “elixir (of life)”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 三 (“three”), 社 (“company, shrine”), and 旅 (“journey, travel”); they name a number, a company or shrine, and a journey; none means red or sincerity.
