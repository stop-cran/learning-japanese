# 滑 — slide; slippery; smooth

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

滑 is moving without catching: sliding or gliding across a surface, a surface so smooth that things slip on it, and, by extension, things that go without a hitch. The linked [滑る](../words/滑る.md) すべる has seven JMdict senses. The first three are "to slide; to glide; to skate; to ski," "to slip; to skid" and "to be slippery." Its fourth is "to fail (an examination)" and its fifth is "to lose (a position); to be demoted." The word article covers sliding and slipping.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for カツ. One example of each:

- smooth, with no hitch: 円滑 えんかつ, "smooth; undisturbed; uninterrupted; harmonious";
- to slide: 滑走 かっそう, "gliding; sliding; skating (on ice); skiing; glide; slide."

More examples are in the Notes. Daijisen also gives a second on reading, コツ, for upsetting order and joking around, with 滑稽 こっけい, JMdict's "funny; comical; humorous; laughable": a whole word, not asked here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part looks like the kanji [骨](骨.md), "bone; skeleton." Imagine a wet bone sliding out of your hand. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound, and 骨 sharing コツ with this card is not offered as an explanation. Historical formation is not established here.

In 滑る, 滑 writes すべ and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 滑る is a godan verb. JMdict marks 辷る as a "rarely used kanji form" of 滑る すべる. In 滑らか, 滑 writes なめ and らか is okurigana; JMdict marks 滑か as a "search-only kanji form" of 滑らか, and its second sense, "smooth (of an action, proceedings, etc.); fluent (speech); fluid; trouble-free," is the Daijisen "no hitch" sense. In 滑走, 滑降 かっこう, "descent (esp. in skiing); sliding down; skiing down," and 円滑, 滑 supplies カツ, and the small っ in かっそう and かっこう is part of the same reading.

滑る has a separate JMdict entry, ぬめる, "to be slippery; to be slimy," which marks 滑る as a "rarely used kanji form" and the word as "usually written using kana alone." Kanjipedia lists ぬめ.る outside KANJIDIC2, so it is not in this card's metadata. 滑子 なめこ, "nameko (var. of mushroom)," is a whole-word reading in Daijisen's list of hard-to-read compounds.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カツ with 滑走, 滑降 and 円滑, コツ with 滑稽, すべる with 滑る and 滑り すべり, "sliding; slipping," and なめらか with 滑らかだ, the adjectival-noun form of 滑らか. Practice selects すべ.る, the reading of the linked word article. The other readings are shown with dictionary examples and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- 滑る is 滑 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji. The "fail (an examination)" sense of 滑る is a figurative use of slipping; it does not make [落](落.md), "fall down," a second answer to this card.
- [湖](湖.md), "lake," [濃](濃.md), "concentrated; dark," and [涙](涙.md), "tears," also have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [平](平.md), "flat; peaceful," [磨](磨.md), "polish; grind," [擦](擦.md), "scrape; scrub," and [柔](柔.md), "soft; supple," overlap the smooth sense; [転](転.md), "turn; roll," [落](落.md), [降](降.md), "descend," and [流](流.md), "flow," overlap sliding and moving along. [滞](滞.md), "stagnate; stay; be delayed," is the opposite of the Daijisen sense of smooth, with no hitch. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "slide," "slippery" or "smooth" as exclusive to any one character.
