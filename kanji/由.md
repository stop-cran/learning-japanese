# 由 — reason; grounds

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

由 concerns a reason or what something comes from. 理由 りゆう is a reason, and 由来 ゆらい is an origin or derivation.
The whole word [自由](../words/自由.md) じゆう means freedom; "self-reason" is not a useful literal English translation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a reason sticking up above a box.
The distinguishing feature is the center stroke projecting above the top, not below the bottom. This is not an origin story.

## Notes

理由を教えてください。 (りゆうをおしえてください。) — Please tell me the reason.

[田](田.md) has no center stroke projecting above its top. [申](申.md) projects both above and below.
[目](目.md) divides its interior horizontally instead. These line endings matter more than the general box shape.
