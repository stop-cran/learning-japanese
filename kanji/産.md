# 産 — produce; give birth

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 生 (no. 100) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

産 covers bringing something into being: a birth, a yield, or manufactured output.
[生産](../words/生産.md) せいさん concerns production, whereas 出産 しゅっさん can name childbirth.
Do not give every compound the biological translation "birth."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** find 生 at the bottom and imagine new life or goods emerging from beneath the upper strokes.
This association is for the modern shape, not a reconstruction of its origin.

## Notes

この工場は車を生産しています。 (このこうじょうはくるまをせいさんしています。) — This factory produces cars.

Compare [立](立.md) and [音](音.md) for the upper outline, but retain 産's sloping stroke and lower 生.
[重](重.md) also stacks horizontal strokes, with a different center. These are shape comparisons, not related meanings.
