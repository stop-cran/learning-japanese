# 潔 — clean; pure; upright

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

潔 is being free of dirt, and, by extension, free of blemish in conduct. The linked [不潔](../words/不潔.md) ふけつ has two JMdict senses: "unclean; dirty; unsanitary; filthy," and "indecent; dirty (story, money, etc.); obscene; immoral." The linked [清潔](../words/清潔.md) せいけつ has "clean; hygienic; sanitary" and "pure; virtuous; immaculate."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ケツ. One example of each:

- free of dirt, pure: 清潔 せいけつ, "clean; hygienic; sanitary";
- with nothing extra, neat: 簡潔 かんけつ, "concise; brief; succinct; compact (style)";
- correct in heart and conduct: 潔白 けっぱく, "innocence; guiltlessness; purity; uprightness; integrity."

More examples are in the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part is a busy shape that ends with [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the bottom. Imagine a clean thread rinsed in clear water, with nothing stuck to it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 不潔, [不](不.md) supplies フ and 潔 supplies ケツ: ふ + けつ. In 清潔, [清](清.md) supplies セイ: せい + けつ. In 潔白, 潔癖 けっぺき, "fastidiousness; love of cleanliness," and 潔斎 けっさい, "religious abstinence; purification," 潔 also supplies ケツ; the small っ in けっぱく, けっぺき and けっさい is part of the same reading.

In 潔い, 潔 writes いさぎよ and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict gives 潔い 1254440 with いさぎ良い, 潔よい and 潔良い as "search-only kanji form." Its senses also include "upright; righteous; honest; pure (heart); innocent," tagged "dated term," and "unspoiled (scenery, etc.); pure; clean," tagged "archaic."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケツ with 潔白, 清潔 and 純潔, and いさぎよい with 潔い. Practice selects ケツ, the reading of the linked word articles. いさぎよ.い is shown with a dictionary example and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. Daijisen's character entry and Kanjipedia also list きよい, which KANJIDIC2 does not, so it is not in this card's metadata. The kanji is a grade 5 kanji.

## Notes

- In the linked words, [不](不.md), "not, non-," supplies フ in 不潔, and [清](清.md), "pure, clean," supplies セイ in 清潔. In the other examples, [簡](簡.md), "simplicity; brevity," supplies カン in 簡潔, [白](白.md), "white," supplies ハク in 潔白, [純](純.md), "pure; genuine," supplies ジュン in 純潔 じゅんけつ, "purity; chastity," [斎](斎.md), "purification; abstinence; study room," supplies サイ in 潔斎, and [高](高.md), "high, expensive," supplies コウ in 高潔 こうけつ, "noble; lofty; high-minded; virtuous; upright." [癖](癖.md) supplies ヘキ in 潔癖.
- [漁](漁.md), "fishing; fishery," [溶](溶.md), "melt; dissolve," and [渡](渡.md), "cross over," also have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but have different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [清](清.md) also reads きよ.い, the reading Daijisen lists for 潔 beside いさぎよい, and both overlap cleanness and purity. [純](純.md) overlaps purity, [簡](簡.md) overlaps the concise sense, [白](白.md) overlaps innocence, [正](正.md), "correct, proper," overlaps the upright sense, and [斎](斎.md) overlaps purification. [澄](澄.md), "clear; become transparent; serene," overlaps clear, pure water. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. 傑, 欠, 決, 結 and 血 also read ケツ and are never offered as wrong answers.
