# 畜 — livestock; domestic animals

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

畜 concerns domesticated animals and livestock. 家畜 is a useful whole word for animals kept by people, especially in farming; it is not limited to cattle.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the small twisted shape beneath the top cap as a bundle of feed above the 田-shaped enclosure of an animal pen. The pen is an invented memory scene, not the character's history.
