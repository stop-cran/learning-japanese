# 雇 — employ; hire

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

雇 concerns employing or hiring. 雇う is the verb to hire someone, 雇用 names employment, and 解雇 means dismissal from employment.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 戸-shaped top as a workplace entrance above the 隹-shaped worker. This is a modern-shape association, not a historical derivation.

The indexing radical is 隹, no. 172, in the lower part; the 戸-shaped top is not the canonical radical here.

## Notes

- 雇う is a transitive godan verb: 人を雇う (ひとをやとう), hire a person. Its negative is 雇わない.
- The inspected 雇う family also includes chartering a car or boat. The complete expression determines whether hiring a person or chartering a vehicle is meant.
- Compare [戸](戸.md), door, and [肩](肩.md), shoulder. Their shared upper shape does not give them the employment meaning.
