# 沈 — sink; subside

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

沈 concerns sinking, submerging, or becoming subdued. 沈む しずむ is intransitive, while 沈める しずめる is transitive. 沈香 じんこう is agarwood, a specialized noun rather than a word to translate as sinking incense.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside the 冘-shaped right side and imagine an object settling below the surface. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical radical 水, no. 85, has the left-side form 氵.

## Notes

- Compare 船が沈む ふねがしずむ, a ship sinks, with 船を沈める ふねをしずめる, sink a ship. The thing sinking is the subject of one verb and the object of the other.
- 沈む is godan, with 沈んで しずんで. 沈める is ichidan, with 沈めて しずめて; it is not the grammatical causative form of 沈む.
- JMdict lists 沈香 as both じんこう and じんこ. The former supplies the ジン example here; the spelling list alone does not rank their frequency.
- 気分が沈む きぶんがしずむ describes feeling down. The expression alone does not name a medical diagnosis.
