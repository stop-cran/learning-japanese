# 抗 — defy; oppose

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

抗 concerns standing against something: resisting, defying or opposing it. It appears in [抵抗](../words/抵抗.md) ていこう, "resistance; opposition," and [反抗](../words/反抗.md) はんこう, "defiance; rebellion." Other dictionary words include 抗議 こうぎ, "protest; objection," and 対抗 たいこう, "opposition; rivalry; competition." As a prefix it can mean "anti-," as in 抗がん剤 こうがんざい (also written 抗癌剤), "anticancer drug."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the side form of [手](手.md), "hand," at the left and 亢 at the right, made of 亠 above 几. Imagine a hand pushing back against pressure. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 亢 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 抵抗, 抗 supplies コウ and 抵 supplies テイ. In 反抗, 反 supplies ハン and 抗 supplies コウ. In 抗う, 抗 writes あらが and う is okurigana. Practice selects コウ. あらが.う is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with 抗う, but that word has no linked article yet, so the reading is not selected for questions. This is a teaching selection, not a claim that あらが.う is invalid, obsolete or rare.

## Notes

- [抵](抵.md), "resist; collide," is the other kanji in 抵抗, and both characters concern acting against something.
- [反](反.md), "oppose; reverse," and [対](対.md), "oppose; face," also give "oppose" meanings. 反抗 pairs 反 and 抗 for defiance; 対抗 pairs 対 and 抗 for rivalry. Their meanings overlap, so do not treat "oppose" as exclusive to any one character.
- [航](航.md), "navigate; sail; fly," also reads コウ and contains the same 亢 shape, but it has 舟 at the left. [抜](抜.md), "extract," and [技](技.md), "skill; technique," share 扌 at the left but have different right-hand shapes.
