# 陥 — fall into; cave in

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

陥 is about falling or sinking into something: ground caving in, a stronghold being taken, falling into a trap, and also falling short. KANJIDIC2 lists “collapse”, “fall into”, “cave in”, “fall (castle)” and “slide into”. Kanjipedia's first sense begins おちいる。おちこむ。攻めおとされる。 (fall into; sink in; be captured by attack), and its second is 欠ける。不足する。あやまち。 (be lacking; fall short; a mistake). In [欠陥](../words/欠陥.md) けっかん, JMdict glosses “defect; fault; flaw; deformity; deficiency; shortage; gap”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the ⻖ shape stands on the left. On the right, the shape 𠂊 sits over [旧](旧.md), "former; old," which is 丨 beside [日](日.md), "sun, day." Picture an old wall on the left and, beside it, old ground that has caved in under the sun. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that ⻖, 𠂊 or 旧 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists カン, with 陥落, 陥没, and 欠陥, おちいる, with 陥る, and おとしいれる, with 陥れる, and gives the old form 陷. Kanjipedia lists no readings outside the table.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 陥, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These twelve cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [落](落.md) (“fall down”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 陥 includes 攻めおとされる (be captured by attack), JMdict 陥落 かんらく sense 2 is “fall (of a city, fortress, etc.); surrender” (entry 1216150), and the 落 card lists お.ちる.
- [崩](崩.md) (“crumble; collapse; demolish”) and [倒](倒.md) (“topple; collapse”): KANJIDIC2 lists “collapse” for 陥, and the first sense of 陥落 is “subsidence; sinking; cave-in; collapse; falling in”.
- [沈](沈.md) (“sink; subside”) and [凹](凹.md) (“concave; sunken”): JMdict glosses 陥没 かんぼつ “cave-in; collapse; sinking; depression (e.g. of the skull); subsidence” (entry 1216140), Digital Daijisen's first sense of 陥 is 地面がへこむ (the ground sinks in), and KANJIDIC2 gives 凹 “sunken” and the kun reading へこ.む.
- [欠](欠.md) (“lack; gap”) and [乏](乏.md) (“scarce; lacking”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 陥 begins 欠ける。不足する。 (be lacking; fall short), Digital Daijisen's fourth sense is 不足する (fall short), with 欠陥, and the 欠 card lists か.ける.
- [誤](誤.md) (“mistake; error”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 陥 includes あやまち (a mistake), and the 誤 card lists あやま.る.
- [穴](穴.md) (“hole; pit; cavity”): Digital Daijisen's third sense of 陥 is おとし穴 (pitfall), with 陥穽 かんせい, which JMdict glosses “trap; pitfall” (entry 1216160); the first sense of 陥る is “to fall into (e.g. a hole)”.
- [敗](敗.md) (“failure; defeat”) and [負](負.md) (“lose; bear a burden”): the fourth sense of 陥る is “to fall; to surrender; to capitulate” (JMdict 1216120), and JMdict glosses 失陥 しっかん “surrender; fall” (entry 1319840).
- [滑](滑.md) (“slide; slippery; smooth”): KANJIDIC2 lists “slide into” for 陥 and “slide” for 滑.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 昇 (“rise; ascend”), 跳 (“hop; spring up”), and 浮 (“float”); each is about going or staying up, so none means falling into something or caving in.
