# 平 — flat; peaceful

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 干 (no. 51) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

平 can describe a level surface, equal treatment, or peace.
These are related learning associations, not a rule for translating every compound.
Compare 平ら たいら, "flat," [平等](../words/平等.md) びょうどう, "equality,"
and [平和](../words/平和.md) へいわ, "peace."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the long horizontal stroke as a level bar, with the small strokes balanced to either side.
This is a memory aid for the current shape, not a verified historical explanation.

## Notes

平ら is a な-adjective: 平らな道 (たいらなみち), "a level road."
Do not read 平等 as へいとう; learn びょうどう as a complete word.
The crossed horizontal and vertical strokes can resemble [半](半.md), [午](午.md), or [年](年.md).
Check the top horizontal line and the two short strokes on either side of the center.
