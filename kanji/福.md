# 福 — good fortune

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 示 (as 礻, no. 113) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

福 names favorable fortune or a blessing. It also contributes to words about happiness and people's well-being.
[幸福](../words/幸福.md) こうふく is happiness, while 福祉 ふくし concerns welfare.
Neither word needs to mean a lucky win.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 一, 口, and 田 stacked on the right; imagine good things filling those compartments.
This is a modern memory aid, not an explanation of the historical components.

## Notes

不幸 ふこう, "unhappiness; misfortune," is a useful contrast to 幸福.
Do not confuse the kanji with [神](神.md), [祖](祖.md), or [礼](礼.md): all have 礻,
but 福 has the large rectangular stack on the right.
