# 堀 — moat; ditch

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

堀 means a moat, ditch or canal. The reused [濠](../words/濠.md) article preserves its existing identity and level while explaining that 堀 is the everyday spelling for the same word ほり.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine an earthwork dug around a place. This is a memory aid only, not a historical account.
