# 話 — talk, speak

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 言 (as 訁, no. 149) · **Phonetic:** none in modern form (old 𠯑) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

話 is 言 (words, speech) plus 𠯑, an old sound component. In the modern form the right-hand part looks like 舌 (tongue) only because the shapes merged graphically.
As a labelled mnemonic only, "words from the tongue" is easy to remember.
It means "talk", "speak" and "a story, a conversation".

## Notes

- 話す takes the person with に or と: 友達と話す (talk with a friend), 先生に話す (tell the teacher). The noun 話 (はなし) is the same word without the final す.
- 話す is a regular godan verb: 話します, 話さない, 話して, 話した.
- Contrast: 言う いう (say), 話す (talk) and 語る かたる (narrate, a formal verb).
- Look-alikes: [読](読.md) and [書](書.md), which are also sound-and-sense kanji, and 活 (life; with 氵 and 舌). 話 ends in 舌 (a 千-like top over 口).
