# 遊 — play

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

遊 concerns play and recreation. [遊ぶ](../words/遊ぶ.md) あそぶ can describe children playing or friends spending time together for enjoyment.
遊園地 ゆうえんち is an amusement park.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** spot 子 low in the right-hand group and imagine a child playing beside the 辶 path.
The scene is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

[族](族.md) shares the 方-shaped area but has 矢 rather than the 子 visible in 遊, and no 辶.
English "play" also covers performing music; ピアノを弾く (ピアノをひく) means "play the piano."
遊ぶ does not supply that instrument-playing meaning.
