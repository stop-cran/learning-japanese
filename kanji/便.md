# 便 — convenience

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

In [便利](../words/便利.md) べんり, 便 concerns convenience: something makes a task or daily life easier.
The same character appears in 郵便 ゆうびん, "mail," where translating it simply as "convenience" would miss the word's meaning.
Learn the two words as distinct uses.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a person beside a crossed-over checklist, pleased that the task is easy to handle.
This is only a visual association with the right-hand [更](更.md) shape, not verified etymology.

## Notes

The radical 人 takes the visible left-side form 亻.
[体](体.md), [借](借.md), and [住](住.md) have different right-hand components.
便 also has a bodily-waste sense: the convenience gloss is not a safe translation in every context.
In 便利な道具 (べんりなどうぐ), "a handy tool," な connects 便利 to the following noun.
