# 展 — unfold; expand

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44), at the upper left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

展 concerns spreading out, unfolding or developing something.
[展開](../words/展開.md) てんかい can be an unfolding or development;
[発展](../words/発展.md) はってん emphasizes development or expansion.
An exhibition in [展覧会](../words/展覧会.md) てんらんかい puts things on view.
These related compounds are not interchangeable translations of every English
use of "development."

The character's dictionary senses also include looking over something and
unrolling or rolling it. The short title is not an exhaustive definition.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** see the enclosing 尸 above
horizontal bars and a long lower-right sweep. Imagine a map being unfolded
beneath a cover. The inner seven-stroke block is not simply a complete 衣
placed under 尸; follow the stroke reference rather than rebuilding the
character from that resemblance.

the complete reading is はってん, not はつてん. The frozen metadata

## Notes

- [屋](屋.md), house/shop, [居](居.md), reside, and [層](層.md), layer,
  share the enclosing 尸 shape. Check their different interiors rather than
  treating that shared part as the whole character.
- Unfolding overlaps [開](開.md); expansion and extension overlap
  [広](広.md), [伸](伸.md), [張](張.md) and [延](延.md).
  Showing or viewing connects with [示](示.md), [観](観.md) and [見](見.md);
  the rolling sense also overlaps [転](転.md). These are not suitable
  wrong meaning choices merely because their titles use different English words.
- Spreading something out flat also overlaps [敷](敷.md), spread/lay out.
  That direct meaning comparison is excluded too.
- In exhibition names, 展 can itself abbreviate an exhibition. This does
  not make every compound containing 展 the name of an exhibition.
