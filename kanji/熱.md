# 熱 — heat; fever

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 火 (as 灬, no. 86) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

熱 names heat and fever; it can also express enthusiasm or passion.
[熱い](../words/熱い.md) あつい is the adjective for something hot to the touch.
The standalone noun 熱 is read ねつ, so the presence or absence of い matters.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the dense upper shape being heated by the four fire-dots
below it. This is a visual aid, not a proposed historical construction.

## Notes

熱があります。 (ねつがあります。) — I have a fever.
For hot weather, compare 暑い (あつい), a different spelling from 熱い.
[点](点.md), [黒](黒.md), and [然](然.md) also have 灬, but their upper shapes and meanings differ.
