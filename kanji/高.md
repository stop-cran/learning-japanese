# 高 — high, expensive

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 高 (no. 189) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

高 is generally explained as a pictograph of a tall building or tower with a roof and a gate-like base. Details of the original object are not certain.
It means "high", "tall" and, for prices, "expensive" and "high (in amount)": 高い山 (a high mountain), 高い本 (an expensive book), 高い声 (a high voice).

## Common words

- [高い](../words/高い.md) たかい — high, tall; expensive
- 高校 こうこう — senior high school (short for 高等学校 こうとうがっこう)
- 高さ たかさ — height

## Notes

Its opposite for price is [安](安.md) (cheap), and for height 低い ひくい (low). For a person's height the usual phrase is 背が高い せがたかい.
Okurigana (the kana after the kanji, like い in 高い): 高い (adjective) versus 高さ (noun "height") and 高める (verb).
