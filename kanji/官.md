# 官 — government official

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

官 marks an official or government connection in words such as [警官](../words/警官.md) (けいかん), "police officer," and 官庁 (かんちょう), "government office."
It also has an organ sense in 器官 (きかん), so an official is not the only possible meaning.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the stacked boxlike forms under 宀 as an official's filing trays.
This office scene is a memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Common words

- [警官](../words/警官.md) (けいかん) — police officer
- 官庁 (かんちょう) — government office; agency
- 器官 (きかん) — organ of a living body

## Notes

Inspect the connected lower outline rather than remembering only "roof plus boxes."
[客](客.md) and [富](富.md) have different material beneath the roof; [同](同.md) has an outer enclosure rather than 宀.
The biology word 器官 is a separate lexical use, not a claim that a body organ is a government official.
