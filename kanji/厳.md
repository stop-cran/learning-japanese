# 厳 — strict; severe; solemn

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 厂 (no. 27), under the three short strokes at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

厳 is strictness and severity, and also a solemn, imposing dignity. [厳しい](../words/厳しい.md) きびしい is strict or harsh, and [厳重](../words/厳重.md) げんじゅう is strict, rigorous or secure.

The Daijisen character entry gives ゲン four senses. The first is solemn and imposing, as in 厳粛 げんしゅく, "grave; solemn; serious," 威厳 いげん, "dignity; majesty; solemnity; gravity," and 尊厳 そんげん, "dignity; majesty; sanctity." The second is unsparing or strict, as in 厳格 げんかく, "strict; severe; stern; rigid; rigorous," 厳密 げんみつ, "strict; close; precise; scrupulous," and 厳重. The third is intense or harsh, as in 厳寒 げんかん, "intense cold," and 厳冬 げんとう, "severe winter." The fourth is a respectful word for a father, one's own or another person's, as in 厳父 げんぷ, "your honored father." For ゴン it gives solemn, as in 荘厳, read そうごん, "solemn; sublime; grand; magnificent," or しょうごん, which JMdict glosses "decorating (a Buddhist statue or temple); adorning."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find three short strokes at the top, the key 厂, a cliff-like overhang, under them, and under the overhang 敢, which has no card here, with 攵 at its right. Imagine a stern guard standing under a cliff with three warning lights overhead, tapping a stick (攵) to keep everyone in strict order. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 厂, 敢 or 攵 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 厳重, 厳 supplies ゲン and 重 supplies ジュウ. In 厳しい, 厳 writes きび and しい is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the noun 厳しさ きびしさ, "severity; strictness; intensity," keeps the same きび. In 厳か, 厳 writes おごそ before the okurigana か, and in 厳めしい it writes いか before めしい. JMdict also accepts 厳しい as an irregular spelling of いかめしい, but 厳しい is normally read きびしい. In 荘厳, 荘 supplies ソウ and 厳 supplies ゴン.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ゲン, with 厳格, 厳重 and 威厳 as examples; ゴン, with 荘厳; おごそか, with 厳かだ; and きびしい, with 厳しい and 厳しさ. いかめしい and いつくし are not in the official table. Practice selects ゲン and きび.しい, the readings with linked word articles. ゴン, おごそ.か and いか.めしい are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; いつくし has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [重](重.md), "heavy; piled up," in 厳重. 厳しい is 厳 with okurigana only.
- [原](原.md), "original; plain," has the same 厂 key and is also read ゲン. [厚](厚.md), "thick; cordial," also has 厂, and [減](減.md), "decrease, reduce," is also read ゲン. All are different kanji.
- [凜](凜.md), "dignified; bracing cold," overlaps the dignity of 威厳 and the intense cold of 厳寒, and KANJIDIC2 also lists きびし.い for it. [激](激.md), "intense; excite," overlaps 厳しい, "intense (e.g. cold)." [硬](硬.md), "hard; stiff," and [固](固.md), "firm; harden," overlap "rigid" and "firm" in 厳しい and 厳重, and [難](難.md), "difficult," overlaps 厳しい, "hard (to do); difficult." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "strict" or "severe" as exclusive to any one character.
