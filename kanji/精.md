# 精 — refined; vitality

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 米 (no. 119) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

精 brings together refinement, concentrated quality, and vital energy.
精米 せいまい refers to rice polishing or polished rice; 精力 せいりょく is vigor.
In [精神](../words/精神.md) せいしん, it contributes to the whole word's sense of mind or spirit.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the visible 米 to recall the concrete rice-polishing word 精米,
then recognize 青 on the right. This is a learning association, not proof of a historical phonetic component.

## Notes

[青](青.md) is the right-hand shape, but its color meaning is not the meaning of 精.
[料](料.md) shares 米 and has 斗 on the right; [積](積.md) instead has 禾 and 責.
Do not translate 精神 by combining "rice" with "deity": written components and a word's meaning are different levels of analysis.
