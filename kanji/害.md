# 害 — harm

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

害 concerns harm, damage, or a harmful influence. [害](../words/害.md) (がい) names the harm itself; 有害 (ゆうがい) describes something harmful.
被害 (ひがい) brings attention to harm or damage suffered.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the stacked bars between the roof and 口 as a warning barrier against harm.
This modern image is a memory aid, not an etymology.

## Notes

Compare [客](客.md), which also has 宀 and a lower 口 but a different middle.
[言](言.md) and [書](書.md) are looser stacked-line comparisons; neither has the same roof-and-middle arrangement.
有害 is a な-adjective: 有害な物 (ゆうがいなもの), "a harmful thing."
