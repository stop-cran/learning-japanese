# 届 — reach; deliver; notify

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

届 connects reaching a destination with delivering something or formally notifying someone. 届く とどく describes something reaching or arriving; 届ける とどける describes delivering it, and can also mean reporting or giving formal notice.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a message reaching the 由-shaped space inside the 尸-shaped enclosure. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not historical analysis.

## Notes

- 届く is intransitive, while 届ける is transitive. 荷物が届く (にもつがとどく) means the parcel arrives; 荷物を届ける (にもつをとどける) means to deliver the parcel.
- The suffix reading -とどけ names a notification or registration. In 婚姻届 こんいんとどけ, marriage registration, the suffix is written 届 without a final kana け, but is still read とどけ. That is the suffix's reading, not the entire compound's.
- Compare [居](居.md), dwell, [屋](屋.md), roof or shop, and [由](由.md), reason. Similar enclosures or inner strokes do not establish the same lexical meaning.
