# 特 — special

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 牛 (as 牜, no. 93) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

特 marks something special, particular, or exceptional. It is useful in words that single something out from the ordinary.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** put 牜 ([牛](牛.md), cattle) beside 寺, "temple." Imagine one special cow standing beside a temple. This is a shape-based memory aid, not an etymology.

## Notes

In 特急, とく becomes とっ before きゅう; keep the small っ.
Compare [持](持.md) and [待](待.md). They also have 寺 on the right, but their left sides are 扌 and 彳 rather than 牜.
