# 反 — oppose; reverse

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 又 (no. 29) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

反 expresses opposition or a reversed direction in [反対](../words/反対.md) はんたい.
Its bending sense appears in 反る そる, "warp; bend backward."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 又 shape turning back beneath the sloping overhang.
This is a visual reminder of reversal, not a historical derivation.
