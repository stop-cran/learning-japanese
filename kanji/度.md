# 度 — degree, number of times

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

度 measures a degree or counts occasions: temperature in degrees, doing something once, or "this time".

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a tally board under the 广 roof, with the 又-shaped part below checking off each occurrence. This is a memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

## Common words

- [今度](../words/今度.md) こんど — this time; next time, depending on context
- 一度 いちど — once; one degree
- 温度 おんど — temperature
- 毎度 まいど — every time

## Notes
