# 喜 — rejoice

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

喜 is delight: being pleased by something or rejoicing at good news. The verb is [喜ぶ](../words/喜ぶ.md) よろこぶ; 喜び よろこび is the noun "joy."
It describes a positive feeling, not necessarily an audible laugh.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** let the two 口-shaped boxes suggest two delighted voices, one above the other. This is a modern memory aid, not the character's historical origin.

## Notes

喜 has a longer stack of strokes than [古](古.md) or [告](告.md). Count both box-shaped parts.
[笑う](../words/笑う.md) わらう means laugh or smile; someone can 喜ぶ without laughing.
The metadata also preserves よろこ.ばす; do not insert that extra ば into the dictionary form 喜ぶ.
