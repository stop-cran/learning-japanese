# 雰 — mist; atmosphere

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 雨 (no. 173), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

雰 suggests misty or hazy air. Its familiar word
[雰囲気](../words/雰囲気.md) ふんいき means atmosphere, mood or
ambience, including the air someone gives off. A room's 雰囲気 can
be welcoming or tense without containing physical mist.

JMdict marks the separate physical Earth-atmosphere sense of 雰囲気
obsolete. That flag does not apply to the ordinary mood/ambience
senses of the word or erase the character's mist association.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** see [雨](雨.md) above
[分](分.md). Imagine moisture divided into tiny drifting droplets.
This is a visual memory scene, not a reconstruction of its history
or a statement about a sound component.

Practice selects フン, also the Joyo reading. Keep the order
ふん・い・き: 雰 supplies ふん. JMdict recognizes the spoken form
ふいんき but marks its reading irregular; it is not the selected
pronunciation or a new 雰 reading for practice.

## Notes

- [分](分.md), minute/part, is the lower component, without 雨.
  [雪](雪.md), snow, has a different lower group under 雨.
  [晴](晴.md), clear skies, is a weather comparison with 日 beside 青,
  not the mist character.
- [気](気.md) overlaps air or atmosphere. [汽](汽.md) overlaps the
  vapor/mist cue, though its practical words concern steam. These
  direct comparisons are excluded; their compounds are not interchangeable.
- [雲](雲.md) names clouds and [煙](煙.md) smoke. Related visible
  weather or air effects are not automatically synonyms.
- Daijisen notes the related spelling 氛. The card teaches the current
  雰 spelling and the standard ふんいき word, not a free spelling swap.
