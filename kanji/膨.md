# 膨 — swell; expand; inflate

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (as 月, no. 130), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

膨 is swelling out: something filling up and growing round, like a balloon or rising bread, and from there growing very large. [膨らむ](../words/膨らむ.md) ふくらむ is to swell or expand, [膨らます](../words/膨らます.md) ふくらます is to inflate or expand something, and [膨大](../words/膨大.md) ぼうだい is huge or vast.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ボウ: being stretched tight and swelling out, as in 膨大, which JMdict glosses "huge; vast; enormous; colossal" and, in a second sense, "swelling; expansion," and 膨脹 ぼうちょう, "expansion; swelling; increase; growth," a spelling JMdict lists beside its main spelling 膨張. JMdict glosses the verb 膨れる ふくれる "to swell (out); to expand; to be inflated; to distend" in its first sense and "to get cross; to get sulky; to pout" in its second.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [肉](肉.md), "meat; flesh," at the left, written 月, then a tall stacked shape topped by [士](士.md), "warrior; professional," and three slanting strokes at the right. Imagine a warrior puffing out his chest beside his own body, with three lines of air puffing out to the right. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 肉 or 士 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| ボウ | on | [膨大](../words/膨大.md) ぼうだい — huge; vast |
| ふく.らむ | kun | [膨らむ](../words/膨らむ.md) ふくらむ — swell; expand; [膨らます](../words/膨らます.md) ふくらます — inflate; expand |
| ふく.れる | kun | 膨れる ふくれる - to swell (out); to expand; to be inflated; to distend; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 膨大, 膨 supplies ボウ and 大 supplies ダイ. In 膨らむ, 膨 writes ふく and らむ is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 膨らます, the transitive partner, uses the same ふく with the okurigana らます; KANJIDIC2 has no separate row for it. 膨れる writes ふく with れる. After another element ふく can voice to ぶく, as in 青膨れ あおぶくれ, "dropsical (blue-green) swelling," where 青 supplies あお.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ボウ, with 膨大 as its example; ふくらむ, with 膨らむ and 膨らみ ふくらみ, "swelling; bulge; puff"; and ふくれる, with 膨れる and 青膨れ. Practice selects ボウ and ふく.らむ, the readings with linked word articles; ふく.れる is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [大](大.md), "big, large," in 膨大. The words 膨らます and 膨らむ contain only this kanji.
- [肉](肉.md), "meat; flesh," is the key at the left, written 月, and [腕](腕.md), "arm; skill," has the same 月 at the left beside a different shape. [影](影.md), "shadow; silhouette," also ends in three slanting strokes at the right, and [帽](帽.md), "hat; headgear," is also read ボウ. All are different kanji.
- [展](展.md), "unfold; expand," [張](張.md), "stretch tight; assert," [伸](伸.md), "stretch; lengthen," [増](増.md), "increase," [太](太.md), "thick; plump," [巨](巨.md), "huge; gigantic," and [大](大.md), "big, large," overlap the senses "expand," "stretch tight," "increase" and "huge" that 膨 and its words carry, and 太 matches the KANJIDIC2 meanings "get fat" and "thick" given for 膨; 張 appears in 膨張 and 大 in 膨大. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "expand" or "swell" as exclusive to any one character.
