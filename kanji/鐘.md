# 鐘 — hanging bell; gong

**Strokes:** 20 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鐘 is a bell, especially a large bell that hangs and is struck. KANJIDIC2 lists “bell”, “gong” and “chimes”. Kanjipedia gives かね。つりがね。 (bell; hanging bell), and Digital Daijisen's first sense is つりがね (hanging bell), its second 打楽器の一。かね。 (a kind of percussion instrument). On its own, [鐘](../words/鐘.md) かね is a noun: JMdict glosses it “bell (often a large hanging bell); chime”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [金](金.md), "gold, money," stands on the left. On the right is [童](童.md), "child; juvenile," built from [立](立.md), "stand; set up," over the shape 里, with [日](日.md), "sun, day," inside 里. Picture a child standing beside a great metal bell, ready to strike it at sunrise. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 金 or 童 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ショウ, with 半鐘 はんしょう (“fire alarm; fire bell”, JMdict 1479440) and 警鐘, and かね, with 鐘. KANJIDIC2 gives only ショウ and かね; Kanjipedia lists シュ outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen gives シュ（呉） beside ショウ（漢）.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 鐘. Digital Daijisen gives the name reading あつむ. This is name use, not a quiz reading.

These two cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [時](時.md) (“time, hour”) and [刻](刻.md) (“carve; time”): Digital Daijisen's third sense of 鐘 is 時計。また、時刻。 (clock; also, the time of day), and JMdict glosses 時刻 じこく “time; (the) hour” (entry 1316220, sense 1), which overlaps both titles.

The [鈴](鈴.md) card (“small bell”) declares the 鈴/鐘 pair, because KANJIDIC2 gives 鈴 “small bell” and 鐘 “bell”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 旗 (“flag; banner; standard”), 柱 (“pillar; post”), and 瓶 (“bottle; jar”); none means a bell or a gong.
