# 弟 — younger brother

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (bow, no. 57) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弟 primarily means a younger brother. In words about a teacher and a pupil, it can instead refer to the junior member of that relationship.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a younger brother carrying a bow, suggested by the bent 弓-like middle. The short strokes at the top are his raised arms; this is only a memory picture.

## Notes

[兄](兄.md) is an older brother; [妹](妹.md) is a younger sister. These words distinguish age relative to the sibling, not whether the person is a child.

[強](強.md) also contains 弓, but places it on the left. Learn 兄弟 and 弟子 as complete readings rather than reading every occurrence of 弟 as おとうと.
