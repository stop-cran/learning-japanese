# 派 — faction; school of thought

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85), appearing as 氵 · **JLPT:** N2 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

派 can identify a **group, faction, school or sect**. The word [派](../words/派.md) は can stand as a noun or follow another expression to name a camp. Learn [派手](../words/派手.md) はで, flashy, as a separate established word rather than translating it as "faction hand."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 and the branching-looking strokes on the right. Imagine a group splitting into several streams. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- In 派手, ハ combines with native て, voiced to で. This is not an all-on reading.
- "School" here means a school of thought or artistic tradition, not ordinarily the building where pupils attend classes.
- [流](流.md) can also identify a style or school in compounds. Do not treat it as a clearly wrong meaning merely because it also means "flow."
- [泳](泳.md), swim, [洗](洗.md), wash, and [泊](泊.md), stay overnight, share 氵 but differ on the right.
