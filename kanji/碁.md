# 碁 — Go board game

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[碁](../words/碁.md) ご is Go, the board game played with black and
white stones. 囲碁 いご names the same game. 碁を打つ ごをうつ means
to play Go, not to go somewhere.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice 其 above [石](石.md).
Imagine a board raised above the stones used in the game. This is a
memory scene for the modern components, not a historical explanation
of the writing or of Go.

## Notes

- Compare the simpler closed-box shapes [目](目.md), eye,
  [日](日.md), sun/day, and [田](田.md), rice field. None has the
  complete 其-over-石 structure or names the Go board game.
- [甘](甘.md), sweet/pamper, resembles only part of the upper cluster.
- [基](基.md), foundation/basis, has 其 above 土 rather than 石;
  [期](期.md), period/term, has 其 beside 月. They are useful shape
  comparisons, but their キ reading is also recognized for 碁, so
  they are not selected as its wrong-answer cards.
- [盤](盤.md), board/disc, names the Go board itself, as in 碁盤 ごばん.
  Because it is the game's own equipment and the title also says board,
  this direct pair is excluded from choices; the game and its board
  remain different meanings. [板](板.md), plank, is not that equipment.
- JMdict also recognizes 棊 and 棋 as spellings of ご. This does not
  make every compound containing 棋 a Go term; learn each whole word.
- The label explicitly names the board game. Neither the English
  movement verb go nor generic [遊](遊.md), play, is the same meaning.
  A shared game-related topic alone is not an exclusion rule.
