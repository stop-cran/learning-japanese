# 煙 — smoke

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

煙 means smoke or fumes. The noun [煙](../words/煙.md) けむり can stand alone.
In 禁煙 (きんえん), the smoke character participates in a word about smoking or its prohibition.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the box-like top over 土 on the right as a chimney,
with the 火 on the left producing smoke. This is a modern visual invention, not etymology.

## Notes

煙が見えます。 (けむりがみえます。) — I can see smoke.
On a sign, 禁煙 means "No smoking"; it is not a statement that smoke is currently present.
[秋](秋.md) also contains 火, but on the right. In 煙 it is on the left.
