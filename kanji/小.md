# 小 — small, little

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 小 (no. 42) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The oldest forms show three small dots, usually read as tiny grains or bits. Accounts of the character's
formation differ (pictograph versus a "division of something small"), so treat the picture as a mnemonic.
The related 少 (few, a little) is generally explained as a development from the same idea.

## Notes

Opposite: [大](大.md). 小 is the key for 少 and 尖 (sharp, small on top). Compare 小さい (small) and 少ない すくない (few): size versus quantity.
