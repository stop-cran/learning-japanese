# 気 — spirit, air

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 气 (no. 84) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

気 is the simplified form of 氣, which has 气 (rising vapour) above 米 (rice): steam rising from cooked rice is the commonly explained image of 氣. The new form replaces 米 with ×.
From "vapour, breath" the meaning grew to "air", "atmosphere" and "spirit, feeling, mind", as in 元気, 天気 and 気持ち きもち.
Note that 気 does not mean "weather" by itself: it appears in 天気 together with 天 (sky).

Special readings: the ケ reading is an older Chinese-derived reading (Go-on) kept in a few words.

## Common words

- [天気](../words/天気.md) てんき — weather
- [電気](../words/電気.md) でんき — electricity
- 元気 げんき — healthy, lively
- 気持ち きもち — feeling

## Notes

Compare 汽 (き, steam: 氵 + 气), which is in 汽車 きしゃ and 汽船: 気 is the general kanji and 汽 is steam only. 気 has × inside, not 米 (that is the old 氣).
元気 (げんき), 人気 (にんき) and 本気 (ほんき) are common; 人気 can also be read ひとけ (signs of people).
