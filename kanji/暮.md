# 暮 — dusk; livelihood

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

暮 has two useful word families. 暮れる くれる describes daylight fading or a period coming to an end;
[暮らす](../words/暮らす.md) くらす concerns living one's life.
Do not turn the second word into "make it dark": its meaning must be learned as a whole.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** follow the stacked shapes 艹, 日, 大, 日, as if a day were sinking toward
the bottom of the page. This is a modern memory aid, not a reconstruction of the character's origin.
