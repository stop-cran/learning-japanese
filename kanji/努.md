# 努 — make an effort

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

努 points to deliberate effort toward a goal. [努力](../words/努力.md) どりょく names that effort;
it does not itself say that the person succeeded.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the 力 at the bottom as the effort holding up the 女-and-又 shapes.
This is a modern visual cue, not an explanation of the character's origin.

- 努める つとめる — endeavor; try hard

## Notes

努める, [勤める](../words/勤める.md), and 務める all read つとめる.
Their useful learning contrast is effort, employment, and carrying out a role.
[勉](勉.md) also expresses effort or exertion, so it is close in meaning to 努.
