# 症 — symptoms; illness

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 疒 (no. 104), wrapping the upper left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

症 concerns an illness's nature or condition and can also name a medical
condition with particular symptoms. [症状](../words/症状.md) しょうじょう
means symptoms or a patient's symptomatic condition. 花粉症 かふんしょう
is hay fever; 症 also works as a disease/condition suffix.

The English condition is much broader than this medical use. A symptom
does not itself establish a diagnosis, and 症状 does not grammatically
distinguish one symptom from several.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice [正](正.md) inside
疒. Imagine carefully recording the correct description of an illness.
This is an invented memory scene, not a historical explanation or a
claim that symptoms are morally right or wrong.

- 症候群 しょうこうぐん - syndrome

## Notes

- [正](正.md), correct/proper, is the interior shape; [政](政.md),
  politics, also contains 正. [床](床.md), floor/bed, has 广 over 木,
  not 疒 around 正. These do not mean symptoms.
- Illness overlaps [病](病.md) and [患](患.md); condition overlaps
  [状](状.md) and [態](態.md). These comparisons are excluded, but a
  broad state or condition is not automatically a medical symptom.
- [候](候.md)'s displayed weather-conditions label is not a translation
  of 症. Sharing a word in English or occurring in 症候群 does not make
  weather and medical conditions interchangeable.
