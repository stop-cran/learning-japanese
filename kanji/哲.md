# 哲 — philosophy; wise; sage

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

哲 is wisdom, a clear grasp of how things work, and the wise person who has it; in modern words it most often stands for philosophy. [哲学](../words/哲学.md) てつがく is philosophy, the academic field or a person's considered approach to life.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for テツ. One example of each:

- understanding reason and being wise: 明哲 めいてつ, "wisdom; sagacity; wise man";
- a person of virtue and wisdom: 先哲 せんてつ, "ancient wise men";
- short for 哲学: 印哲 いんてつ, "Indian philosophy."

It lists no kun reading. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [折](折.md), "fold," at the top and [口](口.md), "mouth," at the bottom. Imagine a thinker folding a hard question again and again until a clear answer comes out of the mouth below. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 折 or 口 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| あきらか | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict spells あきらか 明らか, "clear; obvious; evident; plain; definite," and has no entry writing it with 哲 |

In 哲学, 哲 supplies テツ and 学 supplies ガク, with no sound change. KANJIDIC2 writes さとい and あきらか without a dot, so it does not mark which part would be okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows テツ, with 哲学, 哲人 てつじん, "wise man; philosopher; sage," and 先哲 as examples. さとい and あきらか are not in the official table. Practice selects テツ, the reading with a linked word article; さとい and あきらか have no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [学](学.md), "study," in 哲学.
- [折](折.md), "fold," is the whole top of 哲. [近](近.md), "near," and [祈](祈.md), "pray; wish," contain 斤, the right half of 折. All are different kanji.
- [賢](賢.md), "wise; intelligent," [冴](冴.md), "clear; sharp-minded," [知](知.md), "know," [識](識.md), "knowledge; awareness," [理](理.md), "reason,logic," and [博](博.md), "broad knowledge; doctor; expo," overlap the senses "wise," "sage" and "philosophy" that 哲 and its words carry; 賢 and 理 appear with 哲 in 賢哲 and 哲理. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "wise" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the テツ senses: 哲人 and 哲理 てつり, "philosophic principles; philosophy"; 賢哲 けんてつ, "wise man," and 十哲 じってつ, "ten great disciples (e.g. of Basho, Confucius, etc.)"; and 中哲 ちゅうてつ, "Chinese philosophy."
