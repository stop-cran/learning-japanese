# 締 — tighten; fasten; conclude

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

締 is pulling a cord or knot tight so that it holds. [締める](../words/締める.md) しめる, [締め切り](../words/締め切り.md) しめきり and [締め切る](../words/締め切る.md) しめきる are the words taught here. 締める is しめる. JMdict glosses 締める "to tie; to fasten; to tighten," "to wear (necktie, belt); to put on," "to total; to sum," and "to be strict with," among other senses. 締め切り is "deadline; closing; cut-off," "closing (a door, window, etc.)," and "cofferdam." 締め切る is "to close up; to shut up (e.g. behind doors)" and "to cut off (e.g. because a deadline has expired); to close off (e.g. subscription list)."

The Daijisen character entry gives テイ for binding things together into one and for concluding an agreement, with 締結 ていけつ, "conclusion; execution (of a contract); entering (into treaty)," and 締約 ていやく, "conclusion of a treaty."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left. Imagine pulling a thread tight until the knot holds. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 contributes meaning or that the right part contributes sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 締め切り and 締め切る, 締 supplies し (め is okurigana) and [切](切.md), "cut," supplies き (り and る are okurigana). The linked articles teach しめる, whose reading is し.める with め as okurigana. The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows テイ, with 締結; しまる, with 締まる and 締まり (also 閉まる, 絞まる); and しめる, with 締める, 締め切る and 引き締め (also 閉める, 絞める). Practice selects し.める, the reading of the linked word articles. The other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [切](切.md), "cut," supplying き in 締め切り and き in 締め切る.
- 閉める is a separate word: しめる written with [閉](閉.md), "shut," means closing a door or window, and 締める means tightening or fastening. Joyo lists 閉める, 絞める and 締める together under しめる.
- [結](結.md), "tie; join; conclude," [約](約.md), "promise; approximately," [契](契.md), "pledge; vow; agreement," and [括](括.md), "tie together; enclose," overlap tying and concluding an agreement; [縛](縛.md) overlaps "bind"; [束](束.md), "bundle," [封](封.md), "seal; close," [切](切.md), "cut," [限](限.md), "limit," [期](期.md), "period; term," [終](終.md), "end; finish," [了](了.md), "completion; settling," and [済](済.md), "settle; finish," overlap the closing and deadline senses. [絞](絞.md), "strangle; wring; squeeze," and [緊](緊.md), "tense; tight; urgent," share し.める and し.まる and the sense of tight. They are kept out of this card's quiz options: 緊 is listed here, and 絞 lists 締 on its own card (the app treats exclusions as symmetric, so one side suffices).
- [紙](紙.md), "paper," [店](店.md), "shop," and [海](海.md), "sea, ocean," look plausible because 紙 has the same thread key 糸 at the left, and 店 and 海 are common starter cards with kun readings. They are read シ and かみ; テン and みせ; and カイ and うみ. None shares a reading or a meaning with 締.
- Kanjipedia lists むす.ぶ for 締 as outside the official table (外); it is not used here. Many other kanji read テイ, so none is offered as a wrong answer.
