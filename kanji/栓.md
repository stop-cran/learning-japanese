# 栓 — plug; stopper

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[栓](../words/栓.md) せん is something that plugs an opening, such as
a bottle stopper or bath plug. It can also name a tap, stopcock or
valve controlling a pipe. The material need not be cork or wood.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md) beside
[全](全.md). Imagine a wooden stopper completely filling an opening.
This is a memory picture, not evidence that these meanings historically
produced the character or that all stoppers are wooden.

## Notes

- [全](全.md), whole/complete, is the right-hand shape.
  [松](松.md), pine tree, and [村](村.md), village, share 木.
  None names a stopper.
- Closing or blocking an opening connects with [封](封.md), seal/close,
  [閉](閉.md), shut, [詰](詰.md), pack tightly/press, and [止](止.md),
  stop/halt. 栓 is the object or device; it is not a replacement for
  every closing verb.
- [判](判.md) also displays seal, but means a stamp rather than a
  bottle closure in that use. The ambiguous English label is excluded,
  not taught as an unrestricted Japanese synonym.
- A 栓 is not every lid or electrical switch. The separate archaic
  耳栓 じせん dictionary entry concerns ear ornaments, not an ordinary
  alternative pronunciation of modern みみせん earplugs.
