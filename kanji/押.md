# 押 — push

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

押 concerns pushing or applying pressure. [押す](../words/押す.md) おす is the verb to learn for pushing a door.
押さえる おさえる can mean holding something down or steady, so pressure need not produce movement.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture 扌 pressing against the box-shaped part of 甲 on the right.
The vertical line continues below the box. This is a visual memory aid, not etymology.

The source preserves two dot divisions for おさえる; they represent the same spoken word here.

## Common words

- [押す](../words/押す.md) おす — push
- 押さえる おさえる — hold down; hold in place

## Notes

ドアを押す means "push the door." The contrasting action is [引く](../words/引く.md) ひく, "pull."
That is a contrast of actions, not a claim that the two verbs oppose each other in every expression.

The right side of 押 is not simply [田](田.md): its central line extends below.
[指](指.md) and [招](招.md) share 扌 but arrange the right-hand strokes differently.
