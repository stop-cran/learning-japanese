# 童 — child; juvenile

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 立 (no. 117) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

童 concerns children. 児童 is a useful compound for children as a category, especially in school-related contexts; the native noun わらべ is a dated term rather than the default word to insert into everyday examples.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 立 above a 里-shaped block and imagine a child standing on a little platform. This scene is a mnemonic for the modern outline, not a historical explanation of 童.

立, no. 117, is the indexing key at the top.

## Notes

- In [児童](../words/児童.md), [児](児.md) and 童 both contribute the child idea; the compound does not mean two separate children.
- JMdict marks the standalone わらべ entry as dated. For the everyday word child, see [子供](../words/子供.md) こども; do not assume equal register just because the English gloss is the same.
- Compare [重](重.md), heavy or piled up, and [章](章.md), chapter or badge. The lower shapes, not only the shared upper strokes, help distinguish them.
