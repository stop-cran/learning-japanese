# 釈 — interpret; explain

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 釆 (no. 165) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

釈 is mainly about explaining and interpreting, and also about setting free, dissolving, and the name of the Buddha. KANJIDIC2 lists only “explanation”. Kanjipedia's first sense is とく。ときあかす。 (explain; make clear), and later senses cover making excuses, understanding, ときはなつ。ほどく。ゆるす。 (set free; untie; pardon), とかす。とける。うすめる。 (dissolve; dilute) and 釈迦 (Shakyamuni). Digital Daijisen's senses cover hard lumps coming loose, explaining hard-to-follow matters or texts by untangling them, releasing what was held, and Shakyamuni. In [解釈](../words/解釈.md) かいしゃく, JMdict gives “interpretation; explanation; reading; construction”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is the shape 釆: a slanting stroke 丿 over [米](米.md), "rice grain." On the right is the shape 尺. Picture someone sorting through grains of rice one at a time, working out what each one means: interpreting. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 釆 or 尺 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists only シャク, with 釈明 しゃくめい (“explanation; vindication”, JMdict entry 1324270), 釈放 and 解釈, and gives the old form 釋. Kanjipedia lists セキ outside the table (外).
- Kanjipedia also lists とかす and おく outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen gives the kun reading とける. These are not KANJIDIC2 readings and have no table rows here.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading しゃ. Digital Daijisen gives the name reading とき. These are name use, not quiz readings.

These thirteen cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [解](解.md) (“unravel; solve”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 釈 is とく。ときあかす。, with 解釈 as an example, and the 解 card lists と.く.
- [説](説.md) (“explanation; theory”) and [訳](訳.md) (“translate; explanation”): KANJIDIC2's only meaning for 釈 is “explanation”, which matches a word in both titles.
- [放](放.md) (“release”): Kanjipedia's fourth sense begins ときはなつ (set free), and JMdict glosses 釈放 しゃくほう “release; liberation; acquittal” (entry 1324260).
- [許](許.md) (“permit; forgive”): Kanjipedia's fourth sense includes ゆるす, KANJIDIC2 lists ゆる.す for 釈, and the 許 card lists ゆる.す.
- [允](允.md) (“permit; sincerity”): KANJIDIC2 gives 允 “permit” and the kun reading ゆるす, and lists ゆる.す for 釈.
- [緩](緩.md) (“loosen; slack; lenient”): Kanjipedia's fourth sense includes ほどく (untie); JMdict glosses 解く ほどく “to undo; to untie; to unfasten; to unlace; to unravel; to loosen; to unpack” (entry 1198900), and KANJIDIC2 gives 緩 “loosen”.
- [溶](溶.md) (“melt; dissolve”): Kanjipedia's fifth sense is とかす。とける。うすめる。, and the 溶 card lists と.ける and と.かす.
- [薄](薄.md) (“thin; dilute”): Kanjipedia's fifth sense includes うすめる (dilute), and JMdict glosses 希釈 きしゃく “dilution” (entry 1219870).
- [捨](捨.md) (“discard; abandon”): Kanjipedia's sixth sense is すてる, with 捨 given as a related character; KANJIDIC2 lists す.てる for 釈, and the 捨 card lists す.てる.
- [置](置.md) (“put; place”): Kanjipedia's seventh sense begins お（置）く (put), and the 置 card lists お.く.
- [仏](仏.md) (“Buddha; Buddhism”): Kanjipedia's eighth sense is 釈迦 (Shakyamuni), also used for the Buddha or Buddhism, and JMdict glosses 釈迦 しゃか “Gautama Buddha; Shakyamuni; the historical Buddha” (entry 1324230).
- [講](講.md) (“lecture; association”): JMdict 講釈 こうしゃく sense 1 is “explanation (of a text, phrase, etc.); lecture; exposition” (entry 1834200).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 書 (“write”), 聞 (“hear, ask”), and 学 (“study”); they are about writing, hearing and studying, not about interpreting or explaining what something means.
