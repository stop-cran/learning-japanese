# 郡 — county; district

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 邑 (no. 163) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

郡 names an administrative district, a county. KANJIDIC2 gives “county” and “district”, and Kanjipedia describes it as ぐん。こおり。行政区画の一つ。 (an administrative division), in Japan one below the prefecture (日本では都道府県の下にある行政区画). On its own, [郡](../words/郡.md) ぐん is a noun; JMdict glosses its first sense “district; county”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [君](君.md), "you; -kun," made of the shapes 尹 and [口](口.md), "mouth," sits on the left, and KanjiVG marks the ⻏ shape on the right as a variant of 邑. Picture calling out to you, -kun, across every village of a whole county. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 君 or ⻏ contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists グン only, with 郡部 ぐんぶ (“rural districts; counties”, JMdict entry 1249250) and ○○郡 as examples. Kanjipedia lists クン and こおり outside the Joyo table, and Digital Daijisen has the headword こおり for 郡. No matching JMdict example was found for クン, so it has no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 郡. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings くに, さと, and とも. These are name use, not quiz readings.

This card is excluded from meaning choices:

- [区](区.md) (“ward; district”): KANJIDIC2 lists “district” for 郡.

The [郷](郷.md) card (“hometown; village”) declares the 郡/郷 pair, because KANJIDIC2 gives "district" for both characters.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 湾 (“bay, gulf”), 丘 (“hill; knoll”), and 岸 (“shore; riverbank”); they name landforms, and none is an administrative district.
