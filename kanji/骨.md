# 骨 — bone; skeleton

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 骨 (no. 188) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

骨 names bone and can also concern a supporting frame. The native noun 骨ほね covers a body's bone or an object's structural rib; 骨折 names a bone fracture.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the boxed upper strokes over the 月-shaped lower part as a joint above a supporting bone. This is a modern-shape memory story, not an origin or phonetic claim.

The canonical indexing radical is the whole character 骨, no. 188. The lower 月-like shape does not change that source classification.

## Notes

- Learn the small っ in 骨折こっせつ and 骨格こっかく as part of those complete compounds; do not pronounce them こつせつ or こつかく.
- The separate 骨こつ entry includes knack or trick, which JMdict marks usually written in kana. Its bone and cremated-remains senses are separate and do not carry that same sense flag. The linked 骨 article teaches ほね, not every word written 骨.
- Compare [胃](胃.md), stomach, and [肩](肩.md), shoulder. Similar lower shapes and a bodily association do not make these words synonyms for bone.
