# 指 — finger

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

指 connects a body part with pointing or indicating. The noun [指](../words/指.md) ゆび means a finger or toe;
the verb 指す さす can mean "point." These are different words, not interchangeable readings of one sentence.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a finger from 扌 pointing toward the small upper shape above 日 in 旨.
This is a modern visual prompt rather than an origin claim.

## Notes

足の指 (あしのゆび) means "toe": Japanese uses 指 for digits of the feet as well as the hands.
指で指す (ゆびでさす), "point with a finger," shows the noun and verb together.

[持](持.md), [押](押.md), and [折](折.md) all have 扌.
For 指, locate 日 low on the right; do not mistake the right side for 寺, 甲, or 斤.
