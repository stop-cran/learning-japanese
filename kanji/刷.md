# 刷 — print; brush

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

刷 concerns printing, as in 印刷 いんさつ and 刷る する. A different source reading, 刷く はく, concerns lightly applying something with a brush; it is not the ordinary verb for printing a page.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the left-hand block and hanging 巾-like strokes as a printing brush beside the thin 刂 on the right.

The canonical radical 刀 takes its right-hand form 刂.

## Notes

- 色刷り and 色刷 are both source-listed spellings of いろずり. The two suffix notations retained in the metadata do not require two different pronunciations.
- The printing verb 刷る する is not the general-purpose verb する, do, despite the shared pronunciation. Its polite and te-forms are 刷ります すります and 刷って すって, compared with します and して for general-purpose する. 刷く はく belongs to the brush-application sense, not an unrestricted spelling of every verb pronounced はく.
