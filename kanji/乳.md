# 乳 — milk; breast

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 乙 (no. 5) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

乳 concerns milk and the breast that produces it. 牛乳 ぎゅうにゅう is cow's milk, and 乳児 にゅうじ refers to an infant. The independent noun 乳 ちち also occurs, though 牛乳 is the ordinary specific word for the drink.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the small strokes above the child-like 子 shape as drops of milk, with a long hook beside them. This is a memory picture of the modern form.

The classical radical is 乙; its visible form is the hook on the right, often represented by 乚.

## Notes

- The reading ち appears in compounds such as 乳首 ちくび, nipple; it is not a rule that every compound uses ち rather than ちち.
- 乳房 にゅうぼう is an anatomical word. 牛乳 and 母乳 ぼにゅう distinguish cow's milk from breast milk.
