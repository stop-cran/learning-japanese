# 庶 — common people; numerous

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

庶 refers to ordinary people in 庶民 and to a variety of things in 庶務 (しょむ), general affairs.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find the four dots at the bottom beneath the shelter-shaped 广.
