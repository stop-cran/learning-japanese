# 能 — ability; capacity

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (in the 月-shaped left part, no. 130) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

能 concerns ability or capacity. [才能](../words/才能.md) さいのう names talent,
while 可能 かのう describes something as possible or feasible. Those are related ideas,
but "a person has talent" and "an action is possible" are different claims.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the two curved 匕-like forms on the right as two "skills"
beside the left-hand shape. This is a visual memory aid, not a derivation.
