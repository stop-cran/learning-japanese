# 静 — quiet

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 靑 (as 青) (no. 174) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

静 expresses quietness or stillness. [静か](../words/静か.md) しずか describes a quiet place or person:
静かな部屋 (しずかなへや) means "a quiet room." 静止 (せいし) instead concerns being stationary.
Not every use of 静 is specifically about sound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine [青](青.md) on the left calming the busy [争](争.md) shape on the right.
This is not an etymology. The canonical indexing radical is 靑; its visible modern form here is 青.

## Notes

静まる describes a change that happens; 静める describes causing that change.
Before a noun, use 静かな, not 静かい.
The left side of 静 is 青. [持](持.md) and [帰](帰.md) are weaker comparisons of the compact horizontal strokes,
not characters built from the same pair of components.
