# 柱 — pillar; post

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

柱 concerns a pillar or supporting post. 柱 はしら can also name a figurative mainstay, such as an important person in a team. 支柱 しちゅう is a prop or support.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside 主 and imagine a main wooden post holding a structure up. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical account.

## Notes

- The independent noun is はしら; チュウ is illustrated by 支柱. Do not read every standalone pillar as ちゅう.
- The English post here is a structural support, not postal mail or a postbox.
- Compare [住](住.md), reside, and [注](注.md), pour or direct attention: their left-hand forms differ from the 木 in 柱.
