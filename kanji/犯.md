# 犯 — commit an offense

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (as 犭, no. 94) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

犯 concerns committing an offense or violating a rule.
[犯人](../words/犯人.md) はんにん is the culprit or offender; 犯罪 (はんざい) is a crime.
The character alone does not mean the person: 人 supplies that part in 犯人.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** treat the hooked shape beside 犭 as a boundary someone has crossed.
This is a memory device, not a historical explanation or a statement about the dog radical's meaning here.

## Notes

犯罪を犯す (はんざいをおかす) means "commit a crime"; を marks the offense committed.
犯人 is not merely "suspect": 容疑者 (ようぎしゃ) is the word for someone under suspicion.
In [状](状.md), the full 犬 shape is on the right; 犯 has its narrow 犭 form on the left.
