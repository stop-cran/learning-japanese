# 耗 — decrease; wear down through use

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 耒 (no. 127) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

耗 concerns decreasing, using up or wearing down. In
[消耗](../words/消耗.md) しょうもう, it contributes depletion through
use, including a person's reserves. This need not mean that everything
has disappeared or that the expenditure was useless. 摩耗 まもう
specifically illustrates wear through friction.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the 耒-shaped tool at
the left gradually wearing down the 毛-shaped strokes beside it.
This is an invented scene using the modern components, not a historical
claim that the character originally depicted wearing away fur.

## Readings

| Reading | Kind | Example or disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| モウ | on | [消耗](../words/消耗.md) しょうもう, consumption or exhaustion; the existing article supports this practice reading. |
| コウ | on | 消耗 also has the dictionary reading しょうこう; 心神耗弱 しんしんこうじゃく names diminished mental capacity in its legal context. Recognition examples do not establish another linked practice reading. |

The full ordered モウ・コウ inventory and empty kun list are preserved.
字通 additionally gives ボウ and へる・みだれる; 漢字ペディア gives
へる・たより. In the latter, たより concerns news or information, not
the depletion sense. These positive supplemental records remain distinct
from the frozen metadata and selected practice.

## Common words

- [消耗](../words/消耗.md) しょうもう - consumption; depletion;
  exhaustion. The alternative しょうこう remains recorded.
- 摩耗 まもう - wear through friction.
- 心神耗弱 しんしんこうじゃく - diminished mental capacity in a
  legal context; this whole term is not a general definition of 耗.

## Notes

- [減](減.md), [損](損.md), [失](失.md), [費](費.md), [消](消.md)
  and [使](使.md) overlap decreasing, loss, expenditure or using up.
  [古](古.md) also illustrates 使い古す, wearing something out through
  use. Its title "old" alone would miss that reason not to curate it.
- [擦](擦.md) and [摩](摩.md) overlap frictional wear, but rubbing
  need not wear something out. [磨](磨.md) also has rare 磨れる
  すれる, becoming worn; this is not just an inference from 磨耗.
  [研](研.md)'s sharpening or polishing names working an edge or
  surface, not depletion itself.
- [削](削.md) includes reducing an amount; [下](下.md) and
  [落](落.md) include a decrease, not only spatial movement.
  [退](退.md) also has rare 退く ひく, to lessen or subside.
  These receive direct guards without making every reduction consumption.
  [用](用.md)'s broad use label is guarded for using up, not because
  every use depletes something.
- [疲](疲.md) includes 疲れる つかれる for a well-used object
  becoming worn out, as well as tiredness. [欠](欠.md) includes the
  moon waning. Direct guards also cover [乏](乏.md)'s scarcity and
  [枯](枯.md)'s plant decline against the qualified impoverished-land
  and vegetation senses in 字通; this does not make all lack or
  withering ordinary interchangeable meanings of 耗.
- 字通 positively records depletion, damage, bad or impoverished
  condition, thinness, darkness and disorder. Some historical glyphs
  are images absent from the text extraction. Their identity and the
  allocation of every old gloss remain unresolved. Direct guards for
  broad damage, badness, thinness, dark and disorder labels are
  precautions, not a claim that ordinary 耗 means all their senses.
- 漢字ペディア's news/information sense gives 音耗 as an example.
  [報](報.md), [便](便.md), [信](信.md) and [音](音.md) have
  independently recorded news or tidings senses and direct guards.
  信's message counter alone would not establish that noun sense.
  Complete comparison entries also retain a letter under [書](書.md),
  a notification under [届](届.md) and a reply under [返](返.md).
  News/report and broad informing labels receive direct precautions,
  but a shared secondary topic does not automatically exclude every
  letter-related card. [文](文.md)'s composition or literary letter and
  書's writing, document or letter name the writing or written object,
  not the news itself; neither displayed writing label defines 耗.
  A letter of the alphabet,
  [字](字.md), is a different English sense.
- 字通's old-dictionary strings include タカヘス・トカ and
  ヲヂナシ・ソコナハル・ツイユ・ノゴフ・ヤブル・ホロブ・キヌ・ウツカス・
  ツク・オドロク・ソコナフ・ヘナル・カヘル・ニクム・ムナシ.
  These are quoted lookup forms, not a list of ordinary modern verbs
  interchangeable with 耗. Broad separation/change/return, hatred and
  emptiness choices are conservatively guarded while allocation remains
  unresolved; the presence of キヌ is not evidence that depletion means
  putting on clothes.
- [毛](毛.md) shares モウ and [耕](耕.md) shares コウ. Their related
  shapes do not make them safe reading distractors. The curated choices
  are [右](右.md), right, [川](川.md), river, and [本](本.md), book or
  origin; they do not mean depletion or news. [石](石.md), stone, and
  [未](未.md), not yet, are comparisons only, not curated choices.
  The curated cards' full ranges include source/base and counting uses, not just
  the first labels.
- Source accounts of the early structure differ. The present-shape
  mnemonic does not choose between them. The canonical radical is
  耒, U+8012, not the whole 耕 character.
