# 垂 — hang down; droop; dangle

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

垂 is something hanging down from above: drooping, dangling, or dripping downward. [垂直](../words/垂直.md) すいちょく is vertical or perpendicular. JMdict glosses the verb 垂れる たれる "to hang; to droop; to dangle; to sag" and, in a later sense, "to drip; to ooze; to trickle; to drop."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for スイ. One example of each:

- hanging down or letting hang: 懸垂 けんすい, "pull-up (exercise); chin-up; chinning," which JMdict also glosses "suspension; dangling; hanging";
- a superior showing teaching to those below: 垂訓 すいくん, "instruction";
- being on the point of something: 垂死 すいし, "on the verge of dying";
- the far edge of a country, with two compounds that JMdict does not list.

Its kun readings are たれる, たらす, しだれる, しずる and なんなんとする. KANJIDIC2 does not list しだれる or しずる; JMdict gives 垂れる, read しだれる, as a second spelling of 枝垂れる しだれる, "to droop; to hang down; to weep." Among words with a hard-to-guess reading, the entry names 垂木 たるき, "rafter," and 直垂 ひたたれ, "ancient ceremonial court robe," which JMdict marks as a historical term. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a short slanting stroke at the top, then horizontal strokes crossed by one central vertical, with a shorter vertical on each side of it, and a long horizontal at the bottom. Imagine a willow branch with twigs hanging down on both sides, drooping toward a ground line at the bottom. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that any of these strokes contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| スイ | on | [垂直](../words/垂直.md) すいちょく — vertical; perpendicular |
| た.れる | kun | 垂れる たれる - to hang; to droop; to dangle; to sag; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| た.らす | kun | 垂らす たらす - to dribble; to spill; also to suspend; to hang down; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| た.れ | kun | 垂れ たれ - hanging; something hanging (flap, lappet, etc.); JMdict's first sense, sauce, is usually written in kana alone; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| -た.れ | kun suffix | 雨垂れ あまだれ - raindrops (dripping from eaves, branches, etc.); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| なんなんと.す | kun | 垂んとす なんなんとす - to close in on (a certain age, etc.); to approach; to get close to; JMdict marks it archaic; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 垂直, 垂 supplies スイ and 直 supplies チョク, with no sound change. In 垂れる, 垂 writes た and れる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 垂らす uses the same た with らす, and the noun 垂れ keeps た with れ. The leading dash in -た.れ marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced. In 雨垂れ, 雨 is read あま and た becomes だ, a sound change called rendaku, the voicing of the first sound of a following element. JMdict writes 垂んとす with the kana んとす after the kanji, while KANJIDIC2 marks only す as okurigana.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows スイ, with 垂直, 懸垂 and 胃下垂 いかすい, "gastroptosis," a sagging of the stomach, as examples; たれる, with 垂れる and 雨垂れ; and たらす, with 垂らす. た.れ, -た.れ and なんなんと.す have no separate row in the table. Practice selects スイ, the reading with a linked word article; the kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [直](直.md), "direct; repair," in 垂直.
- [郵](郵.md), "mail; postal service," has the whole of 垂 at the left. [乗](乗.md), "ride; board," and [重](重.md), "heavy; piled up," also begin with a short slanting stroke above horizontal strokes crossed by a central vertical. All are different kanji.
- [掛](掛.md), "hang," [滴](滴.md), "drip; drop," [下](下.md), "down, below," and [低](低.md), "low; lower in height," overlap the senses "hang down," "droop" and "drip" that 垂 and its words carry; JMdict's 垂れる also includes "to lower," and 下 appears in 胃下垂. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "hang" or "droop" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the スイ senses: 垂涎 すいぜん, "avid desire; craving; thirst; envy," also glossed "watering at the mouth; drooling," and 胃下垂 for hanging down; and 垂示 すいし, "instruction; teaching; explanation," and 垂範 すいはん, "setting an example," for teaching those below.
