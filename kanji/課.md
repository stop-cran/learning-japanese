# 課 — lesson; organizational division

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

課 names a lesson or a section within an organization. It also counts lessons or chapters; the context distinguishes a unit in a textbook from a unit in an office.

Its imposing or assigning use appears in 課税 かぜい, taxation: imposing a tax rather than teaching a tax lesson.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 言 delivering the 果 on the right as the result of an assigned lesson. This is a memory story using modern shapes, not an etymological analysis.

## Notes

- The right side resembles [果](果.md), but the left-hand 言 distinguishes 課. [菓](菓.md) instead has 艹 above 果 and concerns sweets.
- 課長 heads an organizational section; it does not mean a teacher in charge of a textbook lesson. Learn which sense of 課 the whole expression selects.
