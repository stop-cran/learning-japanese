# 歯 — tooth

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 止 (no. 77) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

歯 means a tooth or teeth. The everyday noun is [歯](../words/歯.md) (は).
The same character helps identify dental care: 歯科 (しか) is dentistry and 歯医者 (はいしゃ) is a dentist.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the strokes inside the lower enclosure as teeth in a mouth, with 止 sitting above them.
This is a memory aid for the current form, not a reconstruction of its history.

## Notes

KANJIDIC2 indexes this modern form under the visible top component 止, radical 77.
Keep that separate from the character's meaning: 歯 does not mean "stop."
The noun は itself does not mark singular versus plural; context tells whether one tooth or several teeth are meant.
