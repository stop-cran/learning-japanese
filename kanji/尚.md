# 尚 — still; furthermore; esteem

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 小 (no. 42) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

尚 read なお can express continuation, a greater degree or an added point. In compounds it may instead suggest esteem or refinement; do not translate each 尚 as “still.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for the short upper strokes and the little 口 inside the lower frame. Imagine a notice with one more item in a box.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ショウ | on | 高尚 (こうしょう), refined or noble in character. The しょう belongs to 尚. |
| なお | kun | [尚](../words/尚.md) なお — still; even more; furthermore. Outside the standard-use table; usually written なお. |

## Notes

[堂](堂.md) adds 土 at the bottom, [賞](賞.md) ends with 貝 and [常](常.md) ends with 巾. Compare [尊](尊.md) and [貴](貴.md) for the idea of esteem and [更](更.md) for adding something further. In a notice, なお (usually in kana) may introduce an extra detail.
