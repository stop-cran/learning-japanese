# 匠 — artisan; master craftsman

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 匚 (no. 22) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

匠 refers to a craftsman or artisan and to a master of an art or skill, as in [師匠](../words/師匠.md) ししょう. Digital Daijisen also gives the sense of a creative idea or design, as in 意匠.

## Notes

In JMdict entry 1346260, the spelling 匠 is used only for the “craftsman; artisan” sense of たくみ; the senses of craft or skill and of a plot or trick are restricted to the spelling 工.

師 (“teacher; specialist”) is the other kanji in 師匠. 工 (“craft, construction”), 技 (“skill; technique”), 芸 (“art; performance; craft”) and 術 (“technique”) overlap with craft and skill, and 職 (“employment; post”) appears in [職人](../words/職人.md) しょくにん, another word for a craftsperson.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 雨 (“rain”), 犬 (“dog”), and 南 (“south”); none means an artisan or master craftsman.
