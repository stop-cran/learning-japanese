# 接 — touch; join

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

接 concerns touching, contact or joining things. 接触 せっしょく can refer to physical contact or dealings with people. 直接 ちょくせつ means direct or firsthand; 接ぐ つぐ is a verb for joining or piecing things together.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside the right-hand stack ending in 女 and imagine bringing two pieces into contact by hand. This is a modern-shape story, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.

## Notes

- 接ぐ is a transitive godan verb ending in ぐ: 接ぎます つぎます and 接いで ついで. Its dictionary senses include grafting onto a tree as well as joining pieces.
- 接触 せっしょく has a small っ in the complete word. It is not pronounced せつしょく.
- [接近](../words/接近.md) and [接する](../words/接する.md) likewise show セツ as せっ before きん and する. Learn their complete pronunciations rather than reading せつきん or せつする.
- [接続](../words/接続.md) retains せつ in せつぞく; do not import the small っ from 接近.
- 直接 need not mean right now: directness of contact and immediacy in time are different ideas.
