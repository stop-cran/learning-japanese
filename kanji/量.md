# 量 — quantity; amount

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 里 (no. 166) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

量 concerns quantity, amount or measurement. 量 is the noun りょう, while 量る is a verb for measuring or weighing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the upper 日-like block above a measuring container shaped like 里. This is a visual mnemonic, not a claim about the character's historical formation.

The canonical radical is 里, no. 166, in the lower part.
