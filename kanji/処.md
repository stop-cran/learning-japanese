# 処 — deal with; dispose

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 几 (no. 16) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

In [処理](../words/処理.md) しょり, 処 contributes dealing with or handling something.
Other useful compounds concern responding to a situation or disposing of something.
The character also has place-related senses, but "place" does not explain 処理.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the sweeping left part clearing things past the little 几-shaped
stand on the right. This is a visual cue for "deal with," not a claim about historical components.

## Notes

処理 is not automatically "throwing away": data can be processed without being discarded.
The right-hand outline distinguishes 処 from [外](外.md); [冬](冬.md) has two dots underneath instead.
