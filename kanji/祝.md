# 祝 — celebrate; congratulate

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 示 (no. 113) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

祝 concerns celebrating a happy occasion and congratulating someone. The native verb 祝う can take the occasion or achievement as its object; the character does not prescribe a party or one particular kind of present.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the 礻-shaped left side beside 兄 and imagine a person offering congratulations at a celebration. This is an invented scene for the modern shape, not an account of how the character developed.

The canonical key is 示, no. 113, appearing here in its left-side form 礻.

## Notes

- 祝う is transitive godan: 誕生日を祝う たんじょうびをいわう, celebrate a birthday; 祝います いわいます and 祝って いわって.
- Keep シュク in 祝日 and シュウ in 祝儀 distinct; the two on readings are not freely interchangeable within those words.
- [祭](祭.md) also concerns celebration and ritual, and [祈](祈.md) concerns praying. These are useful comparisons, not mutually exclusive meanings: the dictionary also records a wish/prayer sense of 祝う.
