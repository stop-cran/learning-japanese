# 疲 — grow tired

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 疒 (no. 104) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

疲 concerns tiredness or weariness. [疲れる](../words/疲れる.md) つかれる describes becoming tired;
疲労 ひろう names fatigue.
Being tired does not by itself say that someone is ill or in pain.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the inner 皮 shape slumping under the shelter of 疒 after a long day.
The components are visible, but the story is only a memory aid, not the character's historical derivation.

## Notes

今日はたくさん歩いて疲れました。 (きょうはたくさんあるいてつかれました。) — I walked a lot today and got tired.
眠い ねむい specifically means sleepy; fatigue and sleepiness can occur together without being identical.

[痛](痛.md) and [病](病.md) share 疒, but their inner components differ.
[破](破.md) shares 皮 and replaces the outer illness radical with 石.
