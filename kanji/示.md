# 示 — indicate

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 示 (no. 113) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

示 is about making something apparent: showing a direction, presenting information, or expressing an attitude.
The verb [示す](../words/示す.md) しめす can describe what a person shows or what a sign indicates.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the two top bars as a sign, with the lower strokes directing attention beneath it.
This is a visual reminder, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

In [礼](礼.md), [祖](祖.md), [神](神.md), and [福](福.md), the indexing radical 示 appears on the left as 礻.
That shared component does not make their meanings identical to 示す.

For shape recognition, [小](小.md) lacks the two top bars, [木](木.md) has a crossing central stem,
and [赤](赤.md) has a different lower arrangement.
