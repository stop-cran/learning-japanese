# 哀 — sorrow; pity; pathos

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30), in the middle · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

哀 is sorrow that tightens the chest, and the pity felt for someone in a sad state. [哀れ](../words/哀れ.md) あわれ is pity or sorrow, and as a na-adjective it means "pitiable." JMdict glosses the noun 哀れ "pity; sorrow; grief; misery; compassion" and the adjective "pitiable; pitiful; pathetic; miserable."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for アイ. One example of each:

- sorrow that wrings the heart: 哀愁 あいしゅう, "pathos; sorrow; grief";
- feeling pity: 哀憐 あいれん, "pity; compassion";
- pleading pitifully: 哀願 あいがん, "supplication; appeal; entreaty; petition."

Its kun readings are あわれ, あわれむ and かなしい, as in KANJIDIC2. Among words that are hard to read it lists 可哀相 かわいそう, "poor; pitiable; pathetic; pitiful," which JMdict marks as an ateji spelling, kanji chosen for their sounds, and as usually written in kana. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment," split into a top and a bottom, with [口](口.md), "mouth," set between them. Imagine someone hiding a sobbing mouth in the folds of a robe. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 衣 or 口 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| かな.しい | kun | 哀しい かなしい - sad; miserable; unhappy; sorrowful (JMdict's main spelling is 悲しい); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 哀れ, 哀 writes あわ and れ is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 哀れむ uses the same あわ with れむ, and 哀しい writes かな with しい. In 哀愁, 哀 supplies アイ and 愁 supplies シュウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows アイ, with 哀愁, 哀願 and 悲哀 ひあい, "sorrow; grief; sadness," as examples; あわれ, with 哀れ, 哀れな話 あわれなはなし, a pitiful story, and 哀れがる あわれがる, "to feel sorry for; to pity," which JMdict marks as archaic; and あわれむ, with 哀れむ and 哀れみ あわれみ, "pity; compassion." かなしい is not in the official table. Practice selects あわ.れ, the reading with a linked word article. アイ is the reading of most 哀 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; アイ, あわ.れむ and かな.しい are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The common word 哀れ contains no other kanji; it is 哀 with okurigana only.
- [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment," is the shape split around 口 in 哀. [裏](裏.md), "reverse side; lining," also has 衣 split around another shape, and [袋](袋.md), "bag; sack; pouch," has 衣 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [悲](悲.md), "sadness," and [苦](苦.md), "bitter; suffering," overlap the senses "sorrow" and "grief" that 哀 and its words carry; 悲 is also read かな.しい and appears with 哀 in 悲哀. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "sorrow" or "pity" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first アイ sense, sorrow that wrings the heart: 哀歓 あいかん, "joys and sorrows; happiness and sadness," 哀悼 あいとう, "condolence; regret; tribute; sorrow; sympathy," and 悲哀; and of the third, pleading: 哀訴 あいそ, "appeal; complaint."
