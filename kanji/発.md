# 発 — depart, emit

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 癶 (no. 105) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

発 can express setting out, sending out, or bringing something forth. [出発](../words/出発.md) concerns departure; 発音 (はつおん) concerns producing or pronouncing sounds.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the spread-out upper 癶 strokes as something bursting outward above the crossed lines and two lower legs. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a reconstruction of the character's origin.

## Notes

Learn the small っ and the p-sounds in しゅっぱつ and はっぴょう; simply joining the dictionary readings does not give their pronunciation.
