# 級 — grade; school class

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

級 is one step in an ordered series: a grade, a rank or a level, and also a school class. [上級](../words/上級.md) じょうきゅう is an advanced or upper level, [初級](../words/初級.md) しょきゅう is a beginner or elementary level, [学級](../words/学級.md) がっきゅう is a school class and [高級](../words/高級.md) こうきゅう is high-grade or luxury.

As a word on its own, [級](../words/級.md) きゅう is a class, rank or level; JMdict also lists it as a school grade or year, as a suffix meaning "class" or "level," and as a counter for kyu, the junior ranks in martial arts, go and shogi.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses: a step in an ordered series, as in 階級 かいきゅう, "rank; grade," 等級 とうきゅう, "grade; class; degree; rank; rating; order," and 進級 しんきゅう, "promotion (school, military, etc.)," besides 上級, 初級 and 高級; a school class, as in 学級, 級友 きゅうゆう, "classmate; friend in the same class," 級長 きゅうちょう, "class representative; head of class" (a dated term), and 同級 どうきゅう, "the same grade; same class"; and a head taken in battle, as in 首級 しゅきゅう, "decapitated head of an enemy," a rare sense that is not practised here.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left and 及 at the right. Imagine a thread tied with knots, each knot one step higher on a ladder of grades. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 or 及 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 上級, 上 supplies ジョウ. In 初級, 初 supplies ショ. In 学級, 学 supplies ガッ. In 高級, 高 supplies コウ. The word 級 is written with this one kanji.

KANJIDIC2 lists no kun reading for 級, so none is taught or selected here. KANJIDIC2 gives しな only among the readings used in names, and the Daijisen character entry lists しな as a kun reading; JMdict lists 級の木 among the spellings of しなのき, "Japanese linden (Tilia japonica)," a word usually written in kana. So しな is not part of the metadata here and is not practised.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キュウ, with 等級, 上級 and 階級 as examples, and no kun reading. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [上](上.md), "up, above," in 上級, [初](初.md), "first time; initial," in 初級, [学](学.md), "study," in 学級 and [高](高.md), "high, expensive," in 高級.
- 及 at the right does not have a kanji card here. [吸](吸.md), "inhale; suck in," and [扱](扱.md), "handle; deal with," share that right-hand shape, and [給](給.md), "pay; bestow," shares the thread shape at the left. All three also have the on reading キュウ, but they are different kanji.
- [位](位.md), "rank; position," [格](格.md), "rank; character," [段](段.md), "step; level," [等](等.md), "equal; and so on," [階](階.md), "storey; stair," and [組](組.md), "group; assemble," overlap the senses "rank," "level," "step," "grade" and "school class" that 級 carries. 階 is also the first kanji of 階級. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "grade" or "class" as exclusive to any one character.
- [校](校.md), "school," belongs to the same setting but is a contrast, not an overlap: [学校](../words/学校.md) がっこう is the school itself, while a 学級 がっきゅう is one class within it. A school and a school class are different things, so 校 is not kept out of this card's quiz options.
