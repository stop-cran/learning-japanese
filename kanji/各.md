# 各 — each; every

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

各 distributes attention over the members of a set: each or every. 各地 かくち refers to various places or all parts of an area, and 各 おのおの means each.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the upper strokes arriving at one 口 at a time: each opening gets its turn. This is an invented modern-shape scene.

## Notes

- [各々](../words/各々.md) おのおの is another written form of the each word; 々 repeats the character. The full word is おのおの, not a mechanical doubling of that entire reading.
- The カク reading occurs before another element in 各地. Do not read that compound as おのおのち.
- 各 emphasizes distribution over a set; the English word every does not make it interchangeable with 毎 in all compounds.
