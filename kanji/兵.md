# 兵 — soldier; troops

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 八 (no. 12) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

兵 concerns soldiers, troops, and military affairs. 兵士 へいし means a soldier. The ヒョウ reading occurs in such words as 兵糧 ひょうろう, army provisions, rather than replacing ヘイ in every military compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the upper block standing above two separate, spreading 八-like strokes, as though soldiers were standing in formation.

The canonical indexing radical is 八, visible as the two spreading strokes at the bottom.

## Notes

- 兵 つわもの is a separate dictionary reading meaning soldier or warrior, not the pronunciation of 兵士.
- 兵法, the art of war or military strategy, can be read へいほう or ひょうほう. Learn these as readings of this particular compound, not as a rule to change every へい to ひょう.
