# 眠 — sleep; sleepy

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

眠 concerns sleep and drowsiness. [眠る](../words/眠る.md) ねむる describes sleeping,
while 眠い ねむい describes feeling sleepy. Wanting sleep is not the same as already being asleep.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the eye-shaped 目 on the left getting heavy beside 民 on the right.
This is a recall image, not a claim that 民 historically meant sleep or supplied the reading.

## Notes

赤ちゃんが眠っています。 (あかちゃんがねむっています。) — The baby is sleeping.
Compare [寝る](../words/寝る.md) ねる: it can mean sleep, but also go to bed or lie down.

[目](目.md) alone lacks the right side. [銀](銀.md) has a superficially similar lower-right sweep, but 艮 is not 民.
[服](服.md) has 月 on the left rather than the eye radical. These are visual contrasts, not synonyms.
