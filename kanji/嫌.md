# 嫌 — dislike; suspicion

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

嫌 is disliking something, and in a few compounds suspecting it. [嫌う](../words/嫌う.md) きらう is to dislike or shun, [嫌い](../words/嫌い.md) きらい is a dislike, [嫌](../words/嫌.md) いや is unpleasant or unwanted, [嫌がる](../words/嫌がる.md) いやがる is to show dislike or seem unwilling, [好き嫌い](../words/好き嫌い.md) すききらい is likes and dislikes, and [機嫌](../words/機嫌.md) きげん is mood or temper.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ケン: disliking, as in 嫌悪 けんお, "disgust; hate; repugnance; loathing," and 嫌煙 けんえん, "dislike of smoking"; and suspecting, as in 嫌疑 けんぎ, "suspicion (of having committed a crime, wrongdoing)." For ゲン it gives disliking and doubting and lists 機嫌 as its example, although the modern word means mood or temper. For いや it lists 嫌気 いやき, "dislike; disgust; disinclination; tired of," and 嫌味 いやみ, "disagreeableness" or "cutting remark."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 女 at the left and [兼](兼.md), "combine; concurrent roles," at the right. Imagine being asked to combine two jobs at once and disliking the extra work. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 女 or 兼 contributes meaning or sound, although 兼 also happens to be read ケン. Historical formation is not established here.

In 機嫌, 機 supplies キ and 嫌 is read ゲン. In 嫌う, 嫌 writes きら and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 嫌い the okurigana is い. In 好き嫌い, 好 supplies す with the okurigana き, and 嫌い follows as the second half. In 嫌がる, 嫌 writes いや and がる follows as okurigana.

Standing alone, 嫌 writes いや; JMdict notes that its interjection use, "no!; quit it!; stop!," is usually written in kana alone. The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケン, with 嫌悪 and 嫌疑 as examples, ゲン, with 機嫌, きらう, with 嫌う and 嫌い, and いや, with 嫌だ, 嫌がる and 嫌気がさす.

Practice selects ゲン, きら.う, きら.い and いや, the readings with linked word articles. ケン is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words, but no word article for 嫌悪 or 嫌疑 exists yet, so it is not selected for questions here.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [機](機.md), "mechanism; opportunity," in 機嫌 and [好](好.md), "liking; favorable," in 好き嫌い, where 好 is the opposite word.
- [兼](兼.md), "combine; concurrent roles," is the shape at the right and also reads ケン. [験](験.md), "test; verify," reads ケン and ゲン too. Both are different kanji.
- [憎](憎.md), "hate; detest," shares the KANJIDIC2 glosses "hate" and "detest" with 嫌. [疑](疑.md), "doubt; suspect," and [怪](怪.md), "suspicious; uncanny," overlap the Daijisen sense of suspecting and the title word "suspicion." All three are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "dislike" or "suspicion" as exclusive to any one character.
