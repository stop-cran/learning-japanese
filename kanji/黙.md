# 黙 — be silent; silence

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 黑 (no. 203) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

黙 is silence: keeping quiet and saying nothing. KANJIDIC2 lists “silence”, “become silent”, “stop speaking” and “leave as is”. Kanjipedia gives だまる。口をつぐむ。もだす。 (be silent; shut one's mouth; keep silent), and Digital Daijisen gives 声・言葉を出さない。だまる。 (not uttering a voice or words; be silent). The verb [黙る](../words/黙る.md) だまる is glossed in JMdict as “to be silent; to say nothing” (entry 1534930).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [黒](黒.md), "black," marked as a variant of 黑, wraps around [犬](犬.md), "dog": the upper part of [黒](黒.md), with [日](日.md), "sun, day," inside 里, stands on the left, [犬](犬.md) stands on the right, and the bottom of [黒](黒.md), 灬, marked as a variant of [火](火.md), "fire," runs under both. Picture a black dog lying in the dark without making a sound. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 黒 or 犬 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists モク and だまる and gives 默 as the old form. Kanjipedia also lists だんまり outside the table (外); JMdict 黙り だんまり (1623650), usually written in kana, is “silence; keeping silent; taciturnity”. It is not a KANJIDIC2 reading and has no table row here.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 黙, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These two cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [静](静.md) (“quiet”): KANJIDIC2 lists “silence” for 黙, 黙る is “to be silent; to say nothing”, and the first sense of 静か しずか (JMdict 1381820) is “quiet; silent”; the 静 card lists しず.か.
- [寂](寂.md) (“lonely; quiet; desolate”): KANJIDIC2 lists “quietly” for 寂, and JMdict しじま (2849868), usually written in kana, lists 黙 and 静寂 among its rarely used kanji forms and is glossed “silence; stillness; quietness”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 叫 (“shout; exclaim”), 走 (“run”), and 踊 (“dance; hop”); none means silence.
