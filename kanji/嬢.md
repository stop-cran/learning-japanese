# 嬢 — young lady; daughter; unmarried woman

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

嬢 is a young, unmarried woman, often someone's daughter, and it is also added after a woman's name or job. [お嬢さん](../words/お嬢さん.md) おじょうさん is polite language in JMdict, "(another's) daughter" and "young lady." The word 嬢 じょう is "unmarried woman," and as a suffix "Miss" and "-ess; -ette."

The Daijisen character entry gives ジョウ two senses. The first is an unmarried woman or daughter, as in 令嬢 れいじょう, "(your) daughter; young woman," which JMdict marks as honorific; 愛嬢 あいじょう, "one's beloved daughter"; 貴嬢 きじょう, "you (esp. unmarried women)," an honorific pronoun; and 老嬢 ろうじょう, "elderly spinster." The second is mother, with the example 爺嬢 やじょう; JMdict has no entry with that spelling, but it lists 耶嬢 やじょう, "father and mother." The character entry gives no kun reading. A separate Daijisen headword, じょう【嬢／▽娘】, shows 娘 as another spelling and describes the suffix use after a woman's name or a word for her work, as in 受付嬢 うけつけじょう, "receptionist; reception woman."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [女](女.md), "woman, female," at the left and a dense, many-stroke part at the right. Imagine a young lady in a gown with many layers of trim. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 女 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジョウ | on | [お嬢さん](../words/お嬢さん.md) おじょうさん — daughter; young lady |
| むすめ | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict spells むすめ 娘, "daughter; girl (i.e. a young, unmarried woman)," and has no entry writing it with 嬢 |

In お嬢さん, お is the polite prefix, 嬢 supplies ジョウ, and さん is the suffix; 嬢 sits between them.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows 嬢 with its older form 孃 in parentheses and only ジョウ, with 令嬢, 愛嬢 and お嬢さん. It does not list むすめ, which Kanjipedia marks as outside the table. Practice selects ジョウ, the reading with a linked word article; むすめ is retained from KANJIDIC2 as lookup information. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- お嬢さん contains only this kanji, with kana. JMdict's 御嬢さん is a search-only spelling.
- Related forms in JMdict: お嬢様 おじょうさま, "(another's) daughter," marked polite language, and "young lady (of pampered upbringing)"; 嬢ちゃん じょうちゃん, "young girl; young lady"; and 嬢さん じょうさん, "(another's) daughter; young lady (esp. of respectable upbringing)." 嬢さん read とうさん is a Kansai-dialect honorific, "daughter (of a good family)."
- [娯](娯.md), "recreation; amusement," [妙](妙.md), "strange; exquisite," and [始](始.md), "begin, start," have the same 女 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [娘](娘.md), "daughter; girl," [女](女.md), "woman, female," and [婦](婦.md), "woman; wife," overlap the senses "young lady," "daughter" and "unmarried woman" that 嬢 and its words carry; 娘 is also read ジョウ and むすめ, and Daijisen gives 娘 as another spelling of the word じょう. [若](若.md), "young," shares the English word "young" with "young lady." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "daughter" or "woman" as exclusive to any one character.
