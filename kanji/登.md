# 登 — climb; ascend

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 癶 (no. 105) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

登 concerns going up, especially in [登る](../words/登る.md) のぼる, "climb."
Compounds extend beyond a literal climb: 登場 とうじょう is an entrance or appearance on a stage or scene.
Learn that word's meaning rather than imagining physical climbing every time.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the spreading top 癶 as a pair of feet stepping above the lower 豆 shape.
This is a modern memory aid, not a historical analysis.

## Notes

日曜日に山に登りました。 (にちようびにやまにのぼりました。) — I climbed a mountain on Sunday.
The reading のぼる also belongs to 上る in overlapping uses; this is not permission to use 登 for every sense of 上る.

[発](発.md) shares the top but not the lower 豆. [短](短.md) has 豆 on the right instead of below.
[足](足.md) is only a broad outline comparison and lacks 登's spreading top.
