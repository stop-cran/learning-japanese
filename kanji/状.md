# 状 — condition; form

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (no. 94) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

状 helps describe how something is: its condition, circumstances, or form.
[状態](../words/状態.md) じょうたい concerns a state or condition; 形状 (けいじょう) concerns shape.
These compounds choose different parts of the character's meaning.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the left-hand bars as a little chart recording the condition
of the 犬 shape on the right. This modern picture is not a derivation.

## Notes

車の状態を調べます。 (くるまのじょうたいをしらべます。) — I will check the car's condition.
The radical is the full [犬](犬.md) on the right, including its small extra stroke:
do not reduce it to [大](大.md). [犯](犯.md) instead has 犭 on the left.
