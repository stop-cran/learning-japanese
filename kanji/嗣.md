# 嗣 — heir; succeed

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

嗣 means an heir; it can also mean to succeed to a position or line. In [嗣子](../words/嗣子.md), it means the child who is to carry on the family line and is read シ.
