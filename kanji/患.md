# 患 — be ill; suffer

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

患 concerns illness or suffering. 患者 かんじゃ names a medical patient, not a person who is patient about waiting. 患う わずらう means to be ill or suffer from an illness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 串 over 心 and imagine the heart burdened by an ailment. This is a shape-based memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- 病気を患う びょうきをわずらう means to suffer from an illness. The dictionary records both transitive and intransitive uses of 患う; this example uses を for the illness.
- 患者 identifies the medical-care role, not a guarantee that the person is hospitalized.
- Compare [思](思.md) and [息](息.md), whose upper parts differ despite the shared 心 below.
