# 就 — take a position; undertake

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 尢 (no. 43), within the right-hand 尤 · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

就 concerns taking up a position or beginning an activity.
[就職](../words/就職.md) しゅうしょく is obtaining employment;
[就任](../words/就任.md) しゅうにん is assuming a particular post.
[就く](../words/就く.md) つく can describe taking a seat or position,
starting a journey, or studying under a teacher, according to context.

The character also has a completion or accomplishment side, illustrated by
成就 じょうじゅ, fulfillment or successful completion. Beginning an
undertaking and bringing it to completion are not the same event.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [京](京.md) on the left
and 尤 on the right. The latter is a 尢-shaped group plus a dot.
Imagine the dot marking the place where someone takes up a new post.
This is a memory aid, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シュウ | on | [就職](../words/就職.md) しゅうしょく - obtaining employment; [就任](../words/就任.md) しゅうにん - assuming office |
| ジュ | on | 成就 じょうじゅ - fulfillment; successful completion; no word article yet |
| つ.く | kun | [就く](../words/就く.md) つく - take a position; undertake an activity |
| つ.ける | kun | 就ける つける - appoint someone to a post; assign someone to study under a teacher; no word article yet |

就く is intransitive and godan; 就ける is transitive and ichidan.
The latter is dictionary-attested, not merely a counterpart guessed from
the ending. Both write つ with 就, followed by く or ける in kana.

All four readings appear in the official Joyo table. Practice selects
シュウ and つ.く because they have linked examples here; the other two
remain in the complete inventory.

## Notes

- [京](京.md), capital, is the left component, not the whole character.
  [秋](秋.md), autumn, and [州](州.md), state/province, share シュウ
  but not 就's meaning or shape.
- [着](着.md) and [付](付.md) also occur in words read つく, with
  overlapping but spelling-sensitive uses. Do not select a spelling from
  pronunciation alone; the linked 就く article explains this word's scope.
- Position-taking overlaps [任](任.md) and settling overlaps [定](定.md).
  The completion and beginning senses connect with [成](成.md) and
  [始](始.md). Departing on a journey overlaps [去](去.md) and [発](発.md);
  studying under someone overlaps [学](学.md). These direct comparisons
  are excluded from meaning choices, not asserted to be interchangeable words.
