# 看 — watch over; see

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109), in the lower part · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

看 concerns looking carefully or watching over someone.
[看病](../words/看病.md) かんびょう is looking after a sick person.
[看板](../words/看板.md) かんばん is a signboard; it is a separate
established word, not a kind of patient care. The character dictionary also
records guarding and reading senses.

[看護婦](../words/看護婦.md) かんごふ is an older, female-specific word
for nurse. Its linked article explains the current gender-neutral term
看護師 かんごし; the older word is included for recognition, not presented
as the general modern occupational label.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the four-stroke [手](手.md)-shaped
upper group stretches over [目](目.md). Imagine shading your eyes with
a hand to look carefully. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not
a verified account of its historical formation. The indexing radical is 目.

rejected as invalid; it is unselected because this lesson has no linked word
article for it. In that spelling, 看 supplies み and る is written in kana.

## Notes

- [着](着.md), wear/arrive, has a similar lower eye-shaped group but a
  different top. [県](県.md), prefecture, and [眠](眠.md), sleep,
  are partial shape comparisons, not words for watching over someone.
- [見](見.md) and [観](観.md) overlap seeing; [察](察.md) overlaps
  close observation, [守](守.md) guarding, and [読](読.md) reading.
  Their differing titles do not make those meanings exclusive to one character.
- [診](診.md) concerns medical examination and diagnosis. 看病 and
  [診察](../words/診察.md) しんさつ, medical examination, are related activities but not
  synonyms: caring for a sick person need not mean examining them to diagnose
  their condition. Both characters can nevertheless belong to care-related
  みる words, so they are excluded as a direct meaning-choice pair.
