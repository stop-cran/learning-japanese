# 灯 — lamp; lamplight

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

灯 concerns a light or lamp. The character can name illumination without implying that something is on fire.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture [火](火.md), fire, beside a 丁-shaped lamp stand: a small light sits on its support.

## Notes

- The existing 灯 word article specifically teaches ひ. The ともしび row is a separate dictionary-backed reading, not a correction of that article.
- The hyphen in ほ- marks a bound element. 灯影 ほかげ also has the spelling 火影; it supplies a concrete compound rather than an invented standalone noun ほ.
- 灯す ともす is transitive godan: 灯して ともして. It describes lighting or turning on a light, not the intransitive state of shining.
