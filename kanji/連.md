# 連 — link; take along

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

連 brings things or people together in a connected sequence. In [連休](../words/連休.md) れんきゅう, the days off follow one another;
in 連れる つれる, someone takes another person along.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the visible 車 inside 辶 to imagine a line of vehicles travelling together.
The connected line is a mnemonic, not an etymology.

## Notes

連れる is used with the person taken along: 子どもを連れる (こどもをつれる), "take a child along."
Its て-form is 連れて (つれて).
[速](速.md) replaces the 車-shaped center with 束; the outer outline alone does not identify the character.
