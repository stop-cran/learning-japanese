# 償 — recompense; make amends

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

償 concerns making up for a loss, recompensing someone or making amends.
[補償](../words/補償.md) ほしょう is compensation for loss;
[償う](../words/償う.md) つぐなう can describe compensating for a loss
or atoning for wrongdoing. Not every use implies that someone committed a crime.

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and [賞](賞.md) on the right.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a person handing over something valuable to make good a loss.
This is a visual memory aid, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

## Notes

- [賞](賞.md) also has ショウ, but its prize or reward meaning does not
  make 賞 and 償 interchangeable. Notice the extra 亻 in 償.
- [補](補.md), "supplement; make good," overlaps in supplying what is missing.
  Learn the whole word rather than dividing compensation and supplementation into absolute categories.
- [儀](儀.md), "ceremony; formal conduct," [僧](僧.md), "Buddhist monk; priest,"
  and [億](億.md), "hundred million," share 亻 but have different right-hand shapes.
