# 針 — needle; pin

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

針 concerns a needle, pin or slender pointer. 指針 can name an instrument pointer or a guiding principle; 方針 names a policy or course of action.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 金 on the left and a 十-shaped needle crossing on the right. This is a modern-shape story, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

The canonical radical 金 appears in its narrow left-hand form.

## Notes

- 針 can refer to a clock hand or instrument pointer, not only a sewing needle. The complete word and context identify the object.
- 指針 and 方針 have established abstract uses. Their policy or guideline meanings do not mean that a policy is literally made of metal.
