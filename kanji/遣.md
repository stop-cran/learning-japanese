# 遣 — dispatch; usage

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

遣 is about sending someone off and about the way something is used. KANJIDIC2's meanings include “dispatch”, “send”, “give” and “donate”, and Kanjipedia gives ①つかわす。さしむける。 (to send; to dispatch), ②つかう。使用する。 (to use) and ③しむ。せしむ。使役の助字。 (a causative helper character). The three linked words all end in づかい: [仮名遣い](../words/仮名遣い.md) かなづかい is “kana orthography; syllabary spelling”, [小遣い](../words/小遣い.md) こづかい is “pocket money; spending money; pin money; allowance”, and [言葉遣い](../words/言葉遣い.md) ことばづかい is “speech; expression; wording; language”. JMdict lists 遣い as a spelling of 使い つかい (entry 1305720), whose fourth sense, “use; usage; user; trainer; tamer; handler; charmer”, carries the note “oft. read づかい when used as a suffix”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** at the top is [中](中.md), "middle, inside," which KanjiVG splits into [口](口.md), "mouth," and a vertical stroke 丨. Strokes 5-10 below it have no KanjiVG label, and ⻌, the stroke group that wraps the left side and the bottom, runs underneath. Picture a messenger sent out from the middle of town down a long road, carefully choosing their words. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 中 or ⻌ contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 仮名遣い, 小遣い and 言葉遣い, 遣 writes づか and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In each whole word, づかい corresponds to つかい; see [sound changes in compounds](../articles/compound-sound-changes.md). The leading dash in -づか.い and -つか.い marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ケン (with 派遣 among its examples), つかう (遣う; 金遣い かねづかい, JMdict entry 1682140), paired with 使う (⇔), and つかわす (遣わす). It has no separate row for -つかい or -づかい. Kanjipedia lists やる outside the Joyo table.
- Digital Daijisen also gives the kun readings よこす and しむ, which are not KANJIDIC2 readings.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 遣, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [使](使.md) (“use, employ”): JMdict 使う is also written 遣う (entry 1305990), the Joyo table pairs 遣う with 使う (⇔), and the 使 card lists -づか.い, the reading selected here.
- [送](送.md) (“send; escort”) and [致](致.md) (“do humbly; bring about; send”): KANJIDIC2 lists “send” for 遣, and also for 致.
- [与](与.md) (“give; grant”) and [贈](贈.md) (“give as a gift; confer”): KANJIDIC2 lists “give” and “donate” for 遣.
- [授](授.md) (“grant; impart; teach”) and [施](施.md) (“carry out; give; alms”): JMdict 遣わす sense 2 is “to bestow (favour, etc.); to grant (e.g. pardon)” (entry 1260460), and KANJIDIC2 gives 授 “grant” and “confer” and 施 “bestow” and “give”.
- [用](用.md) (“use, purpose”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 遣 is つかう。使用する。 (to use).
- [費](費.md) (“expense; spend”): JMdict 使う sense 3 is “to use (time, money, etc.); to spend; to consume” (entry 1305990), and JMdict glosses 金遣い かねづかい “(way of) spending money; way with money; spending habits” (entry 1682140).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 迎 (“welcome; greet”), 呼 (“call out”), and 頼 (“request; rely”); none means sending someone off or a way of using something.
