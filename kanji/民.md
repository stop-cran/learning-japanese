# 民 — populace

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 氏 (no. 83) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

民 concerns people as a population or community.
[市民](../words/市民.md) (しみん) means citizens; 住民 (じゅうみん) focuses on residents.
It does not simply replace 人 for any individual person.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper box as a banner and the long lower stroke as its tail hanging over a community.
This is only an invented picture of the modern form.

## Common words

- [市民](../words/市民.md) しみん — citizens; city residents
- 国民 こくみん — people of a country; nationals
- 住民 じゅうみん — residents; inhabitants

## Notes

KANJIDIC2 uses 氏 as the indexing radical; the complete five-stroke character is 民, not standalone 氏.
市民 can concern a city's inhabitants or citizenry more broadly; context decides.
In shape comparison, [目](目.md) stays enclosed, while 民 continues into a long lower slant and hook.
