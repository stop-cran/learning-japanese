# 屋 — roof, shop

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

屋 occurs in words for buildings, roofs and shops. As や after a product, it can name a shop or the person running that business.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the top-and-left 尸 shape as a shelter wrapping around a compact foundation. This is a present-day memory aid, not a verified origin story.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| オク | on | 屋上 おくじょう — rooftop |
| や | kun | 本屋 ほんや — bookshop; [部屋](../words/部屋.md) へや — room |

## Common words

- [部屋](../words/部屋.md) へや — room
- 本屋 ほんや — bookshop
- 屋上 おくじょう — rooftop

## Notes

[室](室.md) shares the lower shape 至, but has 宀 above it instead of 尸. [店](店.md) means a shop; 屋 has broader uses, so 部屋 does not mean a shop.

Learn 部屋 as へや, not ぶや. The reading of the whole word is not a regular on+on combination. Compare [家](家.md) いえ for a house or home.
