# 究 — investigate thoroughly

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 穴 (no. 116) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

究 concerns research and pursuing a subject deeply. It is especially useful in
[研究](../words/研究.md) (けんきゅう, research).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** under the cave-like 穴 top sits [九](九.md), "nine."
Imagine nine questions to investigate inside a cave. This is a memory aid, not a claim that 九 supplies the sound.

## Notes

[研](研.md) and 究 work together in 研究: both contribute an idea of studying closely.
究める is useful for recognition, but 研究する is the more immediately useful research expression for this card.

[空](空.md) shares the 穴 top. Check the bottom: 究 has 九; 空 has 工.
