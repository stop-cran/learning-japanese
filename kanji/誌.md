# 誌 — written record; journal

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

誌 concerns a written record or journal. It appears in words for magazines, logs and other publications rather than naming an individual spoken statement.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 言 on the left beside 志, with 士 over 心, as thoughts being put into a written journal. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

- 雑誌 is ざっし and 日誌 is にっし. Their small っ is part of each complete word's pronunciation, not an extra character reading to add to the metadata.
- The left-hand radical is 言. Compare [持](持.md), with 扌, and [時](時.md), with 日; similar-looking right-side strokes do not give these characters the journal meaning.
