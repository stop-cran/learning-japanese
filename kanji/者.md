# 者 — person in a role

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 老 (no. 125) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

者 identifies a person, frequently through a role, activity, or characteristic. In
[医者](../words/医者.md) (いしゃ, doctor), it supplies the "person" part.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** imagine the lower [日](日.md) as a name badge
under the upper strokes: it identifies the person doing a job. The classical radical is 老, not the visible lower 日.

## Common words

- [医者](../words/医者.md) いしゃ — doctor; physician
- 学者 がくしゃ — scholar; academic
- 研究者 けんきゅうしゃ — researcher
- 若者 わかもの — young person; young people

## Notes

[人](人.md) (ひと) is a general word for a person. 者 (もの) usually comes with a description or qualifier;
it is not a neutral replacement for 人 in every sentence.
[物](物.md) can also be read もの, but refers to a thing rather than a person in its basic meaning.

[考](考.md) shares the upper shape but not the 日 below. [着](着.md) has a different top and a 目-shaped lower portion.

For the endings 者, 家, 師, 士, 手, 員 and 屋 compared, see [the article on occupation and role endings](../articles/occupation-and-role-endings.md).
