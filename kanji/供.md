# 供 — accompany, offer

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** 共 (キョウ) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

供 is 亻 (person) plus 共 (together, both hands holding something). 共 gives the sound キョウ, and it also seems to contribute meaning: a person who is with you, or who holds something out together with both hands.
It means "accompany, attendant" (お供) and "offer, supply" (供える, 提供). The sense "child" in 子供 is not the kanji's own meaning: see [子供](../words/子供.md), where 供 is treated as an ateji for the suffix ども.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キョウ | on | 供給 きょうきゅう (supply), 提供 ていきょう (provide), 供述 きょうじゅつ (statement) |
| ク / クウ / グ | on | 供養 くよう (memorial service), 供物 くもつ (offering), 供御 くご (rare) |
| とも | kun | お供 おとも (attendant, escort), 供をする (accompany) |
| そな.える | kun | 供える そなえる (to offer to a god or the dead), お供え おそなえ |
| -ども | kun | [子供](../words/子供.md) こども, 私ども わたくしども |

## Common words

- [子供](../words/子供.md) こども — child
- 提供 ていきょう — provision, offering
- 供給 きょうきゅう — supply (opposite: 需要 じゅよう)
- お供 おとも — attendant, companion on a trip

## Notes

- **供える vs 備える:** 供える offers something to a deity or the dead; 備える (そなえる) is "prepare, equip": 災害（さいがい）に備える. They are different words with the same sound.
- The very common 子供 uses 供 almost only for its reading こども; the spelling 子ども is also widely used.
- **Look-alikes:** 供 vs 共 (together; same right-hand part, no 亻), and 借 (borrow) or 倍 (double), which also share 亻.
