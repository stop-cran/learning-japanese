# 史 — history; chronicle

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

史 concerns history and historical records. 歴史 れきし is history, while 史学 しがく is the study of history rather than every kind of study.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the central box and sweeping crossed strokes as a compact record of past events. This is a visual reminder, not a verified picture of the original character.

The indexing radical is 口 (no. 30), although crossing strokes make the central shape less like an isolated 口.
