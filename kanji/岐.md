# 岐 — fork; branch off

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

岐 marks branching or a fork. In 分岐 (ぶんき), a route divides; in 岐路 (きろ), attention is on the fork or crossroads.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 山 at the left and 支 at the right. Picture a path dividing as it goes around a mountain.

## Notes

[技](技.md) has a hand at the left of 支. [岸](岸.md) and [岩](岩.md) put 山 on top, not to the left. [枝](枝.md) is a tree branch and [分](分.md) also suggests division. They are meaning comparisons, not interchangeable spellings.
