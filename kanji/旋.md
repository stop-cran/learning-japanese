# 旋 — rotate; return; mediate

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 方 (no. 70), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

旋 can describe turning around, returning after a circuit, or going around to mediate. In the linked word [凱旋](../words/凱旋.md) がいせん, 旋 contributes セン and the sense of returning; JMdict glosses 凱旋 “triumphant return; returning in triumph.”

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for セン. One example of each:

- turning around and around: 旋回 せんかい — “revolution; rotation; turning”;
- making a circuit and returning: 凱旋 がいせん — “triumphant return”;
- going around to mediate: 斡旋 あっせん — “mediation; intercession; conciliation.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice [方](方.md) at the left and the compact strokes to its right. Picture turning around a route and coming back. This is a memory aid only; the visible parts do not establish meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 凱旋, 旋 supplies セン; 凱 supplies ガイ. 旋回, 旋律 and 周旋 also contain the on-reading セン. The word 旋律 means “melody; tune”; that compound meaning should not be assigned to 旋 by itself.

The official Joyo table, the standard-use kanji list, shows セン with 旋回，旋律，周旋. Practice selects セン because it is used in the linked article 凱旋. Readings outside this quiz selection are not invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in 凱旋 is [凱](凱.md), “victory song; triumph.” The compound means a triumphant return, while 旋 has other senses in other words.
- 旋律 contains [律](律.md), “law; rhythm,” but “melody” is the meaning of the whole word, not a separate meaning established for 旋.
- The distractors [施](施.md), [旗](旗.md) and [旅](旅.md) share the left-side 方 shape, but their meanings are giving, flag and travel. Their readings do not match セン.
- [回](回.md), [転](転.md), [巡](巡.md), [周](周.md), [循](循.md), [円](円.md), [丸](丸.md), [巻](巻.md) and [輪](輪.md) overlap turning or circular motion; [帰](帰.md), [戻](戻.md) and [返](返.md) overlap returning; [介](介.md) overlaps mediation.
