# 運 — carry; luck

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

運 covers **carrying or moving things** and also **luck or fortune**. Learn these as related uses
of the character, not as a rule that every word containing it must refer to transport.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the road-shaped 辶 carries a covered load; inside 軍, the lower part
resembles [車](車.md), a vehicle. This is a visual memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

- [運転](../words/運転.md) うんてん — driving; operation of a machine
- 運動 うんどう — exercise; movement
- 運ぶ はこぶ — carry; transport
- 運 うん — luck; fortune

## Notes

運がいい（うんがいい）means "to be lucky"; it does not describe driving skill.
運動する（うんどうする）means "exercise," whereas 運転する（うんてんする）can mean "drive."

Compare [週](週.md) and [通](通.md): the surrounding 辶 is similar, but the inner shapes differ.
辵 is the classical radical name recorded in the metadata.
