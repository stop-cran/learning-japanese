# 版 — printing plate; edition

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 片 (no. 91) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

版 concerns a printing plate or block and, by extension, an edition or version. 出版 names publishing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture [片](片.md) beside [反](反.md): imagine turning a flat printing block over to press its image onto paper.

## Notes

- 出版 reads しゅっぱん. The small っ and following ぱ are part of this compound's sound pattern, not grounds for adding パン to the pinned character-reading metadata.
- 版 also reads ばん as an edition/version suffix. Learn that word use separately; the source character-reading field here remains ハン.
- [板](板.md), board, also occurs as a spelling for a plate/block in the inspected 版 entry. Do not teach an absolute ban on that overlap or use the two as universally unambiguous wrong answers.
