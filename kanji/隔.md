# 隔 — separate; partition; every other

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (as 阝, no. 170), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

隔 is putting something between two things so that they are apart, in space, in time or between people; in time words it also means skipping one unit, "every other." [間隔](../words/間隔.md) かんかく is spacing or an interval, and [隔てる](../words/隔てる.md) へだてる is to separate things or to have something between them.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for カク. One example of each:

- putting something in between, separating, distance: 隔離 かくり, "isolation; segregation; separation; quarantine," besides 間隔;
- keeping someone at a distance in feeling: 隔意 かくい, "reserve; reticence; unfriendliness; estrangement";
- skipping exactly one unit of a repeating time: 隔月 かくげつ, "every second month; every other month";
- standing in for another character meaning the part between chest and belly: 横隔膜 おうかくまく, "diaphragm."

More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 阝, the written form of the key 阜, at the left, and at the right a lid stroke over [口](口.md), "mouth," above a frame with marks inside it. Imagine a wall at the left and, beside it, a box with a divider inside: everything is kept apart. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 阝, 口 or the frame contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カク | on | [間隔](../words/間隔.md) かんかく — spacing; interval |
| へだ.てる | kun | [隔てる](../words/隔てる.md) へだてる — separate; have between |
| へだ.たる | kun | 隔たる へだたる - to be distant; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 間隔, 間 supplies カン and 隔 supplies カク. In 隔てる, 隔 writes へだ and てる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same pattern gives 隔たる with たる; 隔たる is a separate intransitive verb, not a form of 隔てる. In 隔月, 隔 supplies カク and 月 supplies ゲツ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カク, with 隔離, 隔月 and 間隔 as examples; へだてる, with 隔てる and 隔て へだて, "partition; division; barrier; separation"; and へだたる, with 隔たる and 隔たり へだたり, "distance; difference; gap; disparity." Practice selects カク and へだ.てる, the readings with linked word articles; へだ.たる is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [間](間.md), "interval, between," in 間隔. 隔てる is 隔 with okurigana only.
- [格](格.md), "rank; character," and [革](革.md), "leather; reform," are also read カク. [陽](陽.md), "sunshine," has the same 阝 at the left with a different shape beside it. All are different kanji.
- [間](間.md), "interval, between," [離](離.md), "separate; detach," [別](別.md), "separate, another," [遠](遠.md), "far," and [挟](挟.md), "pinch; hold between," overlap the senses "interval," "separate," "distant" and "have between" that 隔 and its words carry; 間 appears in 間隔. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "separate" or "interval" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the カク senses: 隔世 かくせい, "separation of ages; being of a different age," 隔絶 かくぜつ, "isolation; separation; remoteness; seclusion," 隔壁 かくへき, "barrier wall; bulkhead; partition," 遠隔 えんかく, "distant; remote; isolated," 懸隔 けんかく, "difference; discrepancy," and 隔靴掻痒 かっかそうよう, "being frustrated because something is not quite as hoped (just as one cannot scratch an itch from outside a shoe)," in which カク shortens to かっ before カ, the reading of 靴; 疎隔 そかく, "alienation"; and 隔日 かくじつ, "every other day; every second day," 隔週 かくしゅう, "every other week; every two weeks," and 隔年 かくねん, "biennially; every second year; every other year."
