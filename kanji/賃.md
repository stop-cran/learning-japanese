# 賃 — fee; rent

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

賃 can refer to a fee, fare, rent, wages or a charge for hiring. The complete word determines the particular payment: [家賃](../words/家賃.md) (やちん) is rent for housing, 運賃 (うんちん) is a passenger fare or freight charge, and 賃金 (ちんぎん) is wages or pay.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice 任 above 貝 and picture a receipt for a charge. This is a memory aid based on the modern shape, not a claim about the character's history.

## Common words

- [家賃](../words/家賃.md) やちん - rent for housing
- 運賃 うんちん - passenger fare; freight charge
- 賃金 ちんぎん - wages; pay
- 賃貸 ちんたい - leasing or renting out
- 賃借 ちんしゃく - renting or leasing from someone

## Notes

- 賃貸 describes renting or leasing something out; 賃借 describes renting or leasing it from someone. They are opposite perspectives, not interchangeable labels.
- JMdict lists ちんぎん for 賃金 and also records ちんきん restricted to that spelling. The ちんきん reading is attached to a rental-payment sense; it is not an extra reading of 賃 itself.
- [借](借.md), borrow/rent, and [料](料.md), materials/fee, are excluded from meaning choices because their displayed English titles overlap with 賃's "rent" and "fee." The guard preserves the distinction: 借 takes the borrower's/renter's perspective, while 料 can mean materials or a fee; neither makes the compounds interchangeable.
- [代](代.md) (fee/charge), [俸](俸.md) (salary/wages), [給](給.md) (pay/wages), [費](費.md) (expense/fee), [価](価.md) and [値](値.md) (price/value), and [払](払.md) (pay/payment) overlap with the character-level payment senses. [雇](雇.md) is excluded because 賃 can name a charge for hiring while 雇 concerns hiring or employing; the shared English label does not make employment itself a fee.
- [報](報.md) and [賞](賞.md) have reward meanings that can overlap with 賃's sourced remuneration sense; the guards do not make a prize or retribution a fee or wage. [償](償.md) is also excluded conservatively because its recompense/compensation vocabulary has a broad payment overlap; restitution for loss is not interchangeable with a fee, fare, rent or wages.
- [使](使.md), use/employ, is guarded conservatively at the English employment/hire-charge boundary. Using a tool, employing someone, and paying a hiring charge remain distinct.
- [補](補.md), supplement/make good, has compensation vocabulary that can overlap with payment. It is guarded conservatively without making covering a shortage or loss the same as a fee or wages.
- [任](任.md), responsibility/entrust; [住](住.md), reside; and [仕](仕.md), serve/attend, share a person-shaped left side or the upper shape but have different meanings.
