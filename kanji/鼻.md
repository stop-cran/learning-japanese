# 鼻 — nose; snout

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 鼻 (no. 209) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鼻 names the nose, including the snout in animals. The linked 鼻はな article also illustrates Japanese using this body-part noun for an elephant's trunk.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 自-shaped upper part above the lower strokes as the center of a face with a prominent nose. This is a modern visual memory aid, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical indexing radical is the whole character 鼻, no. 209, not just its 自-shaped top.

- 鼻水 はなみず - nasal mucus; runny nose

## Notes

- 鼻炎 names nasal inflammation, while 鼻水 names nasal mucus or a runny nose. These are related vocabulary, not identical meanings.
- The native はな reading and the ビ reading belong to complete words; 鼻炎 is びえん, not はなえん.
- Compare [自](自.md), self, [息](息.md), breath, and [首](首.md), neck. A similar upper shape or a connection with breathing does not make them words for the nose.
