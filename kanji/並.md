# 並 — line up; parallel

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

並 brings things into a line or puts them on a comparable level. 並ぶ ならぶ describes things lining up; 並べる ならべる describes arranging them. 並み なみ can instead mean an ordinary level or being on a par with something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine two upright objects standing side by side on the same long base line.

## Notes

- 並ぶ is intransitive; 並べる is transitive. 人が並ぶ ひとがならぶ means people line up; 椅子を並べる いすをならべる means arrange chairs.
- 並びに ならびに is a formal connector meaning 'and' or 'as well as', rather than an instruction to arrange something.
