# 摩 — rub; chafe

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

摩 concerns rubbing, chafing or brushing against something. [摩擦](../words/摩擦.md) まさつ is friction or rubbing, and figuratively friction or discord between people or groups. A second, less common sense is drawing so near as to touch, as in 摩天楼 まてんろう, "skyscraper." The Daijisen character entry also records 摩 as a character used to write Sanskrit sounds, as in 摩訶 まか and 護摩 ごま, where it is not about rubbing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 广 over [林](林.md), "grove; woodland," above [手](手.md), "hand," at the bottom. Imagine a hand rubbing against a rough stand of trees until it chafes. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim about the historical formation of 摩, and 手 is simply the key shape at the bottom.

In 摩擦, 摩 supplies マ and 擦 supplies サツ. Practice selects マ, the only reading of 摩 in the Joyo table, the official list of standard-use readings: the table has no kun reading for 摩. The three kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 for recognition only. 摩する まする is マ plus the verb する, 摩る さする is usually written in kana, and 摩る す.る is only a rarely used kanji form of 擦る する. None of those words has a linked article yet, so no kun reading is selected for questions. Being outside the table is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- [擦](擦.md), "scrape; scrub," is the other kanji in 摩擦. Both characters concern rubbing, so their meanings overlap and neither is a wrong description of rubbing.
- [磨](磨.md), "polish; grind," also reads マ and shares the shape of 广 over 林 at the top, but it has 石 at the bottom where 摩 has 手. The word 研磨 けんま, "grinding; polishing," is the usual spelling; 研摩 is only a rarely used form of it.
- [研](研.md), "polish; study closely," is the first kanji of 研磨 けんま, "grinding; polishing," which JMdict also lists with the rarely used spelling 研摩. KANJIDIC2 lists "polish" and "grind" among the meanings of 摩, so 研 is kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [馬](馬.md), "horse," also has マ among its readings, but it is only a sound-alike. [席](席.md), "seat; place," and [林](林.md), "grove; woodland," share parts of the shape of 摩 but are not rubbing words.
