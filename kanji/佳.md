# 佳 — beautiful; excellent

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

佳 means excellent, pleasing or beautiful. A common example is [佳作](../words/佳作.md), a fine work or a work receiving an honorable mention.

## Notes

佳作 can praise a fine work or name an honorable mention in a contest; the context tells which sense is intended. KANJIDIC2 lists カ for this card. JMdict also records 佳い (よい) as a rarely used spelling; it is not taught here as a usual kun reading.

美 centers on beauty; 良, 善 and 宜 can mean “good,” matching Digital Daijisen's よい gloss for 佳; 優 can mean superior; and 俊, 傑, 秀, 英, 卓 and 豪 have excellence-related senses. These characters share part of 佳’s meaning range without being interchangeable in every context.
