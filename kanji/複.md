# 複 — multiple; compound

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

複 concerns doubling, multiplicity and compound structure. 複数 ふくすう is multiple or plural; 複雑 ふくざつ describes a complex or intricate whole.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine clothing 衤 with another layer in the right-hand 复-shaped section: more than a single layer. This is a modern visual association, not a verified history.

The indexing radical 衣 has the left-hand form 衤.
