# 息 — breath

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The noun [息](../words/息.md) いき means breath or breathing. In 休息 きゅうそく,
the character participates in a word for rest: a useful connection, but not a reason to translate every
compound with "breath."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** [自](自.md) sits above [心](心.md); imagine pausing to notice your own
breathing. This uses the modern shape, without claiming a historical derivation.

## Notes

The upper part is 自, not the 田 in [思](思.md). [怒](怒.md) also has 心 below,
but its top is quite different. [自](自.md) alone lacks the lower heart shape.
息 is a noun; the word article shows the verbs used to breathe in and out.
