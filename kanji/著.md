# 著 — write; remarkable

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

著 concerns writing or authorship and conspicuous, remarkable qualities. [著者](../words/著者.md) ちょしゃ is an author; 著す あらわす means write or publish, and 著しい いちじるしい means striking or remarkable.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a writer below the plant-like canopy leaving marks that stand out from the page.

The canonical key is 艸, no. 140, appearing as 艹 above the 者-shaped lower part.

## Notes

- 著する ちゃくする is in the 着する/著する spelling family. Its arrival sense illustrates チャク; do not carry that pronunciation into 著者.
- JMdict also records 著る as a rare spelling of [着る](../words/着る.md) きる, wear. This is a spelling-specific fact, not a new reading of 著しい.
- 著す concerns authoring or publishing. The visible [者](者.md) shape alone does not turn every word containing 著 into a word for a person.
