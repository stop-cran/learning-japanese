# 厄 — misfortune; calamity

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 厂 (no. 27), enclosing the upper left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

厄 concerns misfortune, calamity or bad luck. In
[厄介](../words/厄介.md) やっかい, it contributes to a word for trouble
or a burden. That whole word also has a care/dependence use:
人の厄介になる means to depend on someone's care, not to call that person
a disaster.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine trouble caught beneath the
厂 overhang, beside the curled lower strokes. This is a memory aid for the
present outline, not a historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヤク | on | [厄介](../words/厄介.md) やっかい - troublesome; burden; care, by construction |

The frozen source supplies ヤク and no kun reading. Practice selects ヤク;
in 厄介, it changes to やっ before かい. Learn the complete word's small っ,
not a separate basic reading ヤッ for every compound.

## Common words

- [厄介](../words/厄介.md) やっかい - troublesome; burden; care
- 厄年 やくどし - a year traditionally considered unlucky; a bad year
- 厄払い やくばらい / やくはらい - a rite to ward off misfortune
- 厄災 やくさい - calamity; misfortune

## Notes

- 厄年 and 厄払い name cultural beliefs and practices, not evidence that
  a particular age causes illness or harm.
- [凶](凶.md) directly overlaps ill fortune. [難](難.md), difficulty,
  [困](困.md), being in difficulty, and [苦](苦.md), suffering, are useful
  comparisons for trouble and adversity; their individual words do not
  replace every 厄 expression.
- [労](労.md), labor/trouble, also displays trouble, as in
  [苦労](../words/苦労.md) くろう, hardship. That direct pair is excluded
  from meaning choices; work itself is not misfortune, and the care use
  of 厄介 is not labor.
- JMdict's bare 厄 entry also uses the English gloss *evil*, which overlaps
  [悪](悪.md), bad/evil, as an English quiz answer. This does not mean
  that having bad luck makes a person morally wicked.
- [厚](厚.md), thick/cordial, and [原](原.md), original/plain, share
  厂 but have different interiors. [印](印.md), stamp/mark, has a different
  arrangement of bent strokes.
