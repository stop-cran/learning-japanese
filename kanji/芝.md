# 芝 — turf; lawn

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

芝 means turf or lawn grass. Digital Daijisen describes it as a perennial grass whose stems creep along the ground and which is planted in gardens and on embankments to make lawns; it names lawn grasses, not plants in general. In [芝居](../words/芝居.md) しばい, 芝 supplies しば, but the complete word means “play; drama,” not “grass.” The related [芝生](../words/芝生.md) しばふ means a lawn; the Joyo table gives 芝生 (しばふ) as a special whole-word reading, so it is not the quiz evidence.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the grass crown 艹, also at the top of [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," sits above 之: a dot, then a bent stroke, then a long sweep. Imagine lawn grass creeping along a winding path across a garden. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 艹 or 之 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists しば, including 芝 and 芝居. The source on-reading シ appears in KANJIDIC2 and Digital Daijisen but is not in the Joyo table, and Kanjipedia marks it as outside Joyo. Digital Daijisen and Kanjipedia connect シ with a mushroom, the reishi (マンネンタケ; ひじりだけ), as in 霊芝. Its dictionary example is not a reason to quiz it.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings こげ and しは. Digital Daijisen also gives しば as a place name in Tokyo; these name/place uses do not add quiz readings.
- The title “turf; lawn” is close enough to [草](草.md), “grass; weeds,” that either label could be defended for a meaning question; 草 is excluded.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 犬 (“dog”), 石 (“stone”), and 山 (“mountain”); none means grass or lawn.
