# 芝 — turf; lawn

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

芝 means turf or lawn grass. The Digital Daijisen entry describes the low-growing grass planted on lawns and embankments; it is the grass itself, not a general word for every plant. In [芝居](../words/芝居.md) しばい, 芝 supplies しば, but the complete word means “play; drama,” not “grass.” The related [芝生](../words/芝生.md) しばふ means a lawn, but this article's reading is not aligned to a KANJIDIC2 reading and is not the quiz evidence.

## Notes

- Joyo lists しば, including 芝 and 芝居. The source on-reading シ appears in KANJIDIC2 and Digital Daijisen but is not in the Joyo table; its dictionary example is not a reason to quiz it.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings こげ and しは. Digital Daijisen also gives しば as a place name in Tokyo; these name/place uses do not add quiz readings.
- The title “turf; lawn” is close enough to [草](草.md), “grass; weeds,” that either label could be defended for a meaning question; 草 is excluded. 犬, 石, and 山 have no reading in common with 芝.
