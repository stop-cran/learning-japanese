# 類 — kind; category

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

類 groups things by kind. [種類](../words/種類.md) しゅるい is a kind or category;
分類 (ぶんるい) is classification, the act or system of sorting things into groups.
何種類ありますか。 (なんしゅるいありますか。) — How many kinds are there?

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the [米](米.md) shape above [大](大.md) on the left as items sorted into one large category.
頁 occupies the right. This is a modern shape story, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

Kinds and individual items are different counts: several objects can all belong to one 種類.
[頭](頭.md), [題](題.md), and [顔](顔.md) share the right-hand 頁, not the 米-over-大 block.
Use that left side to identify 類 before choosing its meaning.
