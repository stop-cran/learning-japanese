# 協 — cooperate; join forces

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 十 (no. 24) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

協 concerns cooperation or joining efforts. 協力 きょうりょく means cooperation or help toward a shared aim, while 協議 きょうぎ is consultation or discussion.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the left 十 as a meeting marker beside three 力-shaped pieces, with several efforts being coordinated.

The canonical indexing radical is 十 on the left, not one of the repeated 力 shapes.

## Notes

- Keep the small ょ and long vowel in キョウ. 協力 is きょうりょく, not きょりょく.
- 協力する and 協議する are not interchangeable just because they start with 協. The former concerns cooperation; the latter concerns discussing or consulting.
