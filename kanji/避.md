# 避 — avoid; evade

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

避 is about keeping clear of something: avoiding it, evading it or warding it off. KANJIDIC2's meanings are “evade”, “avoid”, “avert”, “ward off”, “shirk” and “shun”, and Kanjipedia gives さける。よける。のがれる。 (to avoid; to dodge; to escape). For [避ける](../words/避ける.md) さける, JMdict gives “to avoid (situation); to evade (question, subject); to shirk (one's responsibilities)” and “to ward off; to avert”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the shape 辟 rests on ⻌, the stroke group that wraps the left side and the bottom. In 辟, the shape 𡰪 (尸 over [口](口.md), "mouth,") stands beside [辛](辛.md), "spicy; painful," which is [立](立.md), "stand; set up," over [十](十.md), "ten." Picture something spicy and painful standing in the road, so you step around it and avoid it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 辟 or ⻌ contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヒ | on | 避難 ひなん — taking refuge; evacuation; JMdict entry 1484660. Not selected for questions here. |
| さ.ける | kun | [避ける](../words/避ける.md) さける — to avoid (a situation); to evade; to ward off. This word establishes the selected reading. |
| よ.ける | kun | 避ける よける — to avoid (physical contact with); to move out of the way; JMdict entry 1583260. Not selected for questions here. |

JMdict entry 1583260 gives 避ける both readings, with restrictions: sense 1, “to avoid (physical contact with)”, and sense 4, “to put aside; to move out of the way”, are for よける only; sense 2, “to avoid (situation); to evade (question, subject); to shirk (one's responsibilities)”, is for さける only; sense 3, “to ward off; to avert”, has no restriction. The entry also lists 除ける as a rarely used kanji form and restricts さける to the spelling 避ける.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ヒ (避難; 逃避 とうひ, JMdict entry 1450460; 不可避 ふかひ, JMdict entry 1491540) and さける (避ける). Kanjipedia lists よける outside the Joyo table.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 避, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These four cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [逃](逃.md) (“escape; flee”): Kanjipedia gives のがれる for 避, the 逃 card lists のが.れる, and JMdict glosses 逃避 とうひ “escape; evasion; flight” (entry 1450460).
- [防](防.md) (“defend; ward off”): KANJIDIC2 lists “ward off” for 避.
- [除](除.md) (“remove; exclude”): JMdict entry 1583260 lists 除ける as a rarely used kanji form of よける, and the 除 card lists -よ.け.
- [免](免.md) (“exempt; be spared”): JMdict 免れる まぬがれる sense 2 is “to avoid (e.g. punishment); to evade (e.g. responsibility); to avert; to elude; to be exempted from” (entry 1584670).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 求 (“seek; request”), 従 (“follow; obey; subordinate”), and 拾 (“pick up; gather”); none means avoiding or evading something.
