# 了 — completion; settling

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 亅 (no. 6) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

了 concerns completion or a matter being understood and settled. 完了 かんりょう is completion; 了解 りょうかい is understanding or acknowledgment. The character's contribution depends on the compound, rather than giving each word the same English translation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the curved upper stroke ending in a long hook: one final stroke brings the task to an end.

## Notes

- 了 has no horizontal crossbar through its lower hook. Adding the crossbar gives the different character [子](子.md) こ, child.
- 了解 and 了承 concern receiving or understanding a matter; they do not automatically mean that a physical task has been completed.
