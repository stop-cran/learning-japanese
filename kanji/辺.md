# 辺 — vicinity; edge

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

辺 concerns an area, vicinity or edge. 辺り あたり names the surroundings, while the separate ほとり family concerns a side, bank or shore.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the movement-shaped 辶 wrapping around a 刀-shaped part and trace the edge of a nearby area. This is a modern-shape association, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 辵, no. 162, visible here as 辶. The primary source stroke count is five; its alternate count of four is not substituted for that primary value.

## Notes

- 辺り ほとり is marked irregular okurigana usage and usually written in kana. It is lexical dictionary evidence, not a search-only index form. The linked 辺り article teaches the separate あたり reading.
- 野辺 のべ establishes -べ inside a complete word. It is not a rule to attach べ freely to any place noun.
