# 冗 — superfluous; redundant

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 冖 (the cover-like upper part, no. 14) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

冗 concerns what is superfluous or unnecessary, and wordiness. Learn the meaning of each compound:
[冗談](../words/冗談.md) (じょうだん) means "joke; jest," not a long speech.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a cover-like 冖 above a 几-shaped lower part, with more covering than needed.
This is an invented memory aid, not a historical explanation or a verified phonetic analysis.

## Notes

冗長性 (じょうちょうせい) means "redundancy," including in technical contexts.
Technical redundancy need not be a fault; do not translate every 冗 compound as "useless."
Likewise, calling something 冗談 does not guarantee that it is funny or harmless.

For shape comparison, [元](元.md) has two horizontal lines at the top, [兄](兄.md) has [口](口.md), and [見](見.md) has [目](目.md).
冗 instead has the cover-like 冖 above a 几-shaped outline. These are visual contrasts, not claims that all the lower parts are identical.
