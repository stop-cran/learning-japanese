# 圧 — pressure; oppress

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

圧 expresses pressure or pressing down and can also concern oppression. 圧力 あつりょく names pressure, both physical pressure and pressure exerted on people or organizations.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the 厂 overhang pressing down on 土 beneath it.

The indexing radical is 土, the lower part. The conspicuous 厂 overhang is not the canonical radical in this source.
