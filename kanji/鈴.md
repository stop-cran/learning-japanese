# 鈴 — small bell

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鈴 is a small bell that you shake to ring. On its own, [鈴](../words/鈴.md) すず is glossed in JMdict as "bell (often globular)." KANJIDIC2 gives "small bell" and "buzzer," and Kanjipedia describes it as すず。振って鳴らす器具。りん。ベル。 (a bell; a tool shaken to make a sound).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is [金](金.md), "gold, money," and on the right is [令](令.md), "command; decree," with [人](人.md), "person," at its top, then [一](一.md), "one," and マ. Imagine a person shaking a small metal bell once to give a command, and everyone turning to listen. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 金 or 令 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists レイ, with 電鈴 でんれい ("electric bell"), 振鈴 しんれい ("hand bell; ringing of a bell") and 予鈴; リン, with 風鈴; and すず, with 鈴. Digital Daijisen marks レイ as （漢） and リン as （唐）. Kanjipedia lists no readings outside the table.
- JMdict's entry for 鈴 also gives the readings りん and れい, and marks れい as out-dated or obsolete kana usage.
- KANJIDIC2 records ず as a name reading; Digital Daijisen gives no name readings.

This card is excluded from meaning choices:

- [鐘](鐘.md) (“hanging bell; gong”): KANJIDIC2 gives 鈴 “small bell” and 鐘 “bell”; JMdict glosses 鈴 (1557580) as “bell (often globular)” and 鐘 かね (1352030) as “bell (often a large hanging bell); chime”; Kanjipedia describes 鈴 as すず。振って鳴らす器具。りん。ベル。 and 鐘 as かね。つりがね。

The meaning-quiz distractors are 笛 (“flute; whistle”), 琴 (“koto; zither”), and 扇 (“fan; incite”); none means a bell.
