# 奮 — rouse; summon one's energy

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

奮 conveys rousing energy or courage. In 勇気を奮う (ゆうきをふるう), someone summons courage to act.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 田 at the bottom. [奪](奪.md) has a similar upper part but ends in 寸; [集](集.md) has 木 below its bird-like top.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| フン | on | 興奮 (こうふん) — excitement or agitation. Here 奮 provides ふん, not ふる. |
| ふる.う | kun | [奮う](../words/奮う.md) ふるう — muster one's courage or energy |

## Common words

- [奮う](../words/奮う.md) ふるう — muster one's courage or energy

## Notes

Distinguish 奮う (ふるう, muster) from 振る (ふる, wave or shake). The long う in the dictionary form and the choice of kanji matter.

Compare the bases of [奪](奪.md) (寸), [集](集.md) (木), [準](準.md) (十), and 奮 (田). [励](励.md) can mean to encourage: encouraging someone else is different from mustering your own courage.
