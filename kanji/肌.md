# 肌 — skin; texture

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

肌 concerns skin and can also concern a material's surface or grain. The native noun 肌 はだ is useful for skin condition or the feel of a surface; it is not restricted to one skin color or only to human skin.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the flesh-shaped left side beside 几 and imagine noticing the feel of a surface. This is an invented modern-shape mnemonic, not an etymology or a verified sound-component claim.

The canonical key 肉, no. 130, is written here in the compressed left-side form 月. The moon-shaped appearance does not change the indexing key to 月.

## Notes

- The キ row uses the verified dictionary compound 肌膚 きふ. It is not a claim that the ordinary standalone noun はだ can simply be pronounced き, or a frequency claim about that compound.
- [皮](../words/皮.md) かわ includes skin, hide, peel and an outer covering; 皮膚 ひふ names skin. These overlap with 肌 without creating a strict living-skin versus removed-skin rule.
- Distinguish [机](机.md), desk, with 木 on the left, and [服](服.md), clothing. [脂](脂.md), fat/grease, shares the flesh-shaped left part but is not a word for surface texture.
