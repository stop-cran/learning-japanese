# 慮 — consider; concern; forethought

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慮 is turning a matter over in your mind: weighing it, thinking ahead, and thinking of how others will be affected. [考慮](../words/考慮.md) こうりょ is consideration, taking something into account; JMdict glosses it "consideration; taking into account." [遠慮](../words/遠慮.md) えんりょ is holding back out of consideration for others; JMdict glosses it "reserve; constraint; restraint; modesty; diffidence; hesitation," then "declining; refraining," and last "forethought; foresight," which it notes is the original meaning.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for リョ: thinking things over this way and that; a thought or an idea. Among its examples are 配慮 はいりょ, "consideration; concern; attention; thoughtfulness"; 思慮 しりょ, "prudence; discretion; thought; consideration"; 熟慮 じゅくりょ, "deliberation; (thoughtful) consideration"; 苦慮 くりょ, "racking one's brains; worrying oneself; being anxious"; 憂慮 ゆうりょ, "anxiety; concern; fear"; and 不慮 ふりょ, "unforeseen; unexpected; sudden; accidental." It gives the kun reading おもんぱかる. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the tiger-like top 虍 and, under it, [思](思.md), "think, feel," which is [田](田.md), "rice field," over [心](心.md), "heart, mind." Imagine a farmer who sees a tiger at the edge of the field and stops to think ahead about every move. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 虍 or 思 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| リョ | on | [考慮](../words/考慮.md) こうりょ — consideration; taking into account; [遠慮](../words/遠慮.md) えんりょ — holding back out of consideration |
| おもんぱく.る | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict has no entry read おもんぱくる, and the Daijisen character entry gives only おもんぱかる |
| おもんぱか.る | kun | 慮る おもんばかる or おもんぱかる - to consider carefully; to deliberate thoroughly; to think over; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 考慮, 考 supplies コウ and 慮 supplies リョ; in 遠慮, 遠 supplies エン and 慮 supplies リョ, with no sound change in either. In 慮る, 慮 writes おもんぱか and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict lists おもんばかる, with ば, first and おもんぱかる second; KANJIDIC2's おもんぱか.る matches the second. The noun 慮り おもんぱかり or おもんばかり is glossed "thought; consideration; fears."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only リョ, with 遠慮, 考慮 and 無慮 むりょ, "approximately," as examples. おもんぱく.る and おもんぱか.る are not in the official table. Practice selects リョ, the reading with linked word articles; おもんぱか.る is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, and おもんぱく.る has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [考](考.md), "think over," in 考慮 and [遠](遠.md), "far," in 遠慮.
- [虎](虎.md), "tiger," has the same 虍 at the top, [劇](劇.md), "drama; play," has it at the top left, and [膚](膚.md), "skin; bodily surface," has it over 田 and 月. All are different kanji.
- [考](考.md), "think over," [思](思.md), "think, feel," [念](念.md), "thought; attention," [想](想.md), "thought; conception," and [議](議.md), "deliberate; debate," overlap the senses "consider," "thought" and "deliberate" that 慮 and its words carry; [節](節.md), "joint; season; restraint," overlaps the "restraint" of 遠慮; and [悩](悩.md), "worry; be troubled," overlaps "concern" in the sense of worry, as in 憂慮 and 苦慮. 考 and 思 appear with 慮 in 考慮 and 思慮. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "consider" or "concern" as exclusive to any one character.
- [慎](慎.md), "careful; prudent; discreet," shares prudence and restraint: JMdict glosses 慎重 "careful; cautious; prudent; discreet; deliberate," 思慮 "prudence; discretion; thought; consideration" and 遠慮 "reserve; constraint; restraint." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- [懸](懸.md), "hang; suspend; stake," shares worry and concern: JMdict glosses 懸念 "worry; fear; anxiety; concern" and 憂慮 "anxiety; concern; fear." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the リョ sense: 顧慮 こりょ, "concern; solicitude; consideration"; 叡慮 えいりょ, "the emperor's pleasure"; and 無慮.
