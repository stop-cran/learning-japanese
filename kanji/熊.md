# 熊 — bear (animal)

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 火 (as 灬, no. 86) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

熊 is the **bear** character: [熊](../words/熊.md) (くま) names the animal, not the English verb meaning "carry" or "endure."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [能](能.md) above four bottom dots. Imagine a sturdy bear standing on four paws. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a claim that "ability plus fire" explains the animal's name.

The indexing radical [火](火.md) appears as **灬** at the bottom. Its lookup label does not make 熊 a word for fire.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ユウ | on | 熊胆 (ゆうたん) — dried bear gallbladder used in traditional medicine; a specialized recognition example, not a reading question here. |
| くま | kun | [熊](../words/熊.md) (くま) — bear (animal) |

This card selects くま for questions while retaining both full source readings. ユウ is not a substitute pronunciation for the ordinary standalone animal noun.

## Common words

- [熊](../words/熊.md) (くま), also クマ — bear
- 小熊 (こぐま), also 子熊 — small bear; bear cub
- 熊手 (くまで) — rake

In 小熊, くま becomes ぐま. 熊手 is a tool name, not the name of another animal; learn each complete word rather than translating every compound literally.

## Notes

- [能](能.md), "ability; capacity," lacks 熊's four bottom dots. Do not confuse their modern shapes.
- [熱](熱.md), "heat; fever," [照](照.md), "illuminate; shine," and [点](点.md), "point; dot," also have 灬, but their upper parts and meanings differ.
- クマ is a kana spelling of the native noun くま, not evidence that ユウ is the animal's everyday reading.
