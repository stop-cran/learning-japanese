# 婿 — groom; son-in-law

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

婿 names a son-in-law or a groom. A parent talking about a daughter's spouse and a couple posing at their wedding give two perspectives on the same character.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 女 on the left and the 月-like shape low on the right. [望](望.md) has a 月-like part higher up; [掃](掃.md) begins with the hand element.

## Notes

[婦](婦.md) begins with 女 too, but its right half differs. Do not translate 婿 as the neutral word for every husband; [夫](夫.md) is the broader husband character. Compare the placement of the 月-like form in [望](望.md) and the hand element in [掃](掃.md).
