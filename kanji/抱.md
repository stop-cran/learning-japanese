# 抱 — embrace

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

抱 is associated with holding in the arms. [抱く](../words/抱く.md) だく can describe holding a baby or embracing someone.
The spelling also occurs in words about holding thoughts or carrying burdens, but the reading and phrase matter.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the hand-side 扌 reaching around the enclosing 包 shape.
This is a memory aid based on the modern form, not a claim that the components prove its history.

## Common words

- [抱く](../words/抱く.md) だく — embrace; hold in one's arms
- 抱く いだく — harbor a thought or feeling
- 抱える かかえる — carry in one's arms; have problems or responsibilities

## Notes

The linked article teaches だく. Do not automatically transfer all senses of いだく to that reading.
For example, 希望を抱く (きぼうをいだく) means "harbor hope."

[招](招.md), [折](折.md), and [払](払.md) share 扌, but lack the enclosing right side of 抱.
[持](持.md) also has a holding sense, which overlaps with 抱.
