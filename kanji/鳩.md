# 鳩 — pigeon; dove

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 鳥 (no. 196) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鳩 is the **pigeon or dove** character. The native noun [鳩](../words/鳩.md) (はと) covers both English bird names; do not learn "pigeon" and "dove" as separate readings.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [九](九.md), "nine," on the left and [鳥](鳥.md), "bird," on the right. Imagine nine pigeons together. This is a memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin or phonetic structure.

## Common words

- [鳩](../words/鳩.md) (はと) — pigeon; dove
- 伝書鳩 (でんしょばと) — carrier pigeon; homing pigeon. In this compound, はと becomes ばと.

## Notes

- [九](九.md), "nine," is only the left part of 鳩. [丸](丸.md), "round; circle," adds a dot to a similar small shape; [究](究.md), "investigate thoroughly," puts 穴 above 九. None is the pigeon/dove character.
- 鳩 can also express gathering in compounds: 鳩首 (きゅうしゅ) means people putting their heads together to confer. This does not turn the bird noun はと into a verb.
- English "dove" does not mean that the Japanese word must refer to a white bird. はと is a broader bird name, not a species identification.
