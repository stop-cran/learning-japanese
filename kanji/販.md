# 販 — sell; market

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

販 concerns selling or marketing goods. 販売 names selling or sales, and 販売する is a verb for selling.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the [貝](貝.md) on the left as goods being turned over for sale by the 反-shaped right side. The transaction is an invented memory scene, not etymology.

## Notes

- 販売する is transitive: what is sold can take を. 販 itself is a character, not a standalone replacement for the complete verb.
- Compare [版](版.md), printing plate or edition, [坂](坂.md), slope, and [飯](飯.md), cooked rice or a meal. Their shared 反-shaped part does not give them the meaning sell.
