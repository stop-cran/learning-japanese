# 億 — hundred million

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

億 is the Japanese large-number unit for 100,000,000, or 10^8. It is ten thousand 万, not the English million. Japanese number grouping uses units of ten thousand, which need not line up with English groups of three digits.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the person-shaped 亻 trying to hold an enormous number in mind beside [意](意.md).

The canonical radical 人 has the left-hand form 亻.

## Notes

- 一億 いちおく is 100,000,000; 十億 じゅうおく is 1,000,000,000, one billion in modern English usage.
- 億万長者 おくまんちょうじゃ is a word for a very rich person, not a statement that the person owns exactly one 億 of a specified currency.
