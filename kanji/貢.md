# 貢 — tribute; contribute

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

貢 is about offering tribute and, more broadly, contributing. KANJIDIC2 gives “tribute,” “support” and “finance.” Kanjipedia explains みつぐ as presenting local products to the imperial court, and also the tribute itself (朝廷に地方の産物を献上する。また、みつぎもの。). Digital Daijisen's second sense for コウ, devoting one's effort (力をささげる), is illustrated by 貢献. In the declaring word [貢献](../words/貢献.md) こうけん, JMdict glosses the first sense “contribution (furthering a goal or cause); services (to a cause)” and marks the second, “paying tribute; tribute,” as a historical term.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [工](工.md), "craft, construction," sits on top of [貝](貝.md), "shellfish; seashell," which is drawn as [目](目.md), "eye," over [八](八.md), "eight." Picture a craftsman setting fine work on top of a pile of seashells, a gift carried to the ruler. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 工 or 貝 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists コウ (貢献), ク (年貢) and みつぐ (貢ぐ, 貢ぎ物 みつぎもの). Kanjipedia marks ク and みつぐ with 高, a school-level mark, not 外 (outside the table).
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 貢; Digital Daijisen gives すすむ, つぐ and みつぎ. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.

These ten cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [献](献.md) (“offer; contribute; dedicate”): both characters write the declaring word 貢献, KANJIDIC2 gives 献 “offering” and “present,” and Digital Daijisen's first sense for 貢 uses 献じる (to present).
- [納](納.md) (“pay; deliver; put away; accept”): KANJIDIC2 gives 納 “pay” and “supply,” close to JMdict 年貢, “annual tribute; land tax,” and 貢ぐ, “to supply (money).”
- [供](供.md) (“accompany, offer”) and [贈](贈.md) (“give as a gift; confer”): JMdict's second sense of 貢ぐ is “to present (money or gifts) to a monarch (feudal lord, etc.).”
- [支](支.md) (“support”): KANJIDIC2 gives 貢 “support,” and JMdict 貢ぐ includes “to give (in support).”
- [推](推.md) (“infer; recommend; push forward”), [薦](薦.md) (“recommend; mat”) and [勧](勧.md) (“recommend; encourage”): Digital Daijisen's third sense for 貢 is 人材を推薦する (to recommend capable people), and JMdict 推薦 すいせん (1371170) is “recommendation; referral; endorsement.”
- [幣](幣.md) (“currency; Shinto offering”): KANJIDIC2 gives 幣 “gift” and “Shinto offerings of cloth,” and JMdict 幣貢 へいこう (1907230), written with both characters, is “offering; tribute.”
- [税](税.md) (“tax; fiscal duty”): JMdict glosses 年貢 “annual tribute; land tax.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 損 (“loss; damage”), 罰 (“punishment; penalty; divine retribution”), and 奪 (“snatch; rob; deprive”); none means paying tribute or contributing.
