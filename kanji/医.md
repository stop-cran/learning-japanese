# 医 — medicine, doctor

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 匸 (no. 23) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

医 relates to doctors and medical practice. In 医学 it means medicine as a field of study, not a dose of medicine.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the enclosing shape as an open medical kit holding an instrument. This is a memory aid for the present shape, not a historical derivation.

## Common words

- [医者](../words/医者.md) いしゃ — doctor
- 医学 いがく — medicine; medical science
- 医院 いいん — doctor's office; clinic

## Notes

Keep both い sounds in 医院 いいん. The classical radical is 匸, number 23; the similar-looking 匚 is a different radical.

Unlike the closed outer frame of [図](図.md), 医 is open on the right. [同](同.md) is another enclosed-looking character, but its shape and meaning are different.
