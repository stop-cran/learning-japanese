# 胃 — stomach

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

胃 names the stomach as a digestive organ. In words such as 胃炎 and 胃痛 it identifies the organ concerned, not every use of English stomach for the belly or abdominal area.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See a 田-shaped top above the flesh-shaped lower part and imagine food arriving at the stomach. This is a mnemonic for the modern shape, not a verified reconstruction of an early drawing.

The canonical key 肉, no. 130, appears at the bottom in its compact 月-shaped form. The visible resemblance to 月 does not make moon the indexing key.

## Notes

- The linked 胃 article explains 胃が痛い いがいたい, my stomach hurts, and the difference from a general belly-area expression. Keep the short reading い.
- In 胃炎, 炎 えん contributes inflammation; in 胃痛, 痛 つう contributes pain. The compounds do not by themselves establish a medical diagnosis for a particular person.
- Compare [思](思.md), think/feel, with 心 below its upper part, and [畳](畳.md), tatami/fold, with a different lower structure. Similar upper shapes do not make them stomach words.
