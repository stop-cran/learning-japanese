# 理 — reason, logic

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 玉 (in its 王-shaped side form, no. 96) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

理 concerns reason, principles, and arrangement. Its contribution depends on the word: [料理](../words/料理.md) means cooking or cuisine, not "food logic."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the 王-shaped side of 玉 and the 里-shaped right side. Imagine arranging gems into neat rows, putting everything in a logical order. This is a modern memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

The classical radical is 玉, even though its left-side shape here looks like 王.
Compare [野](野.md), which has 里 on the left rather than the right, and [重](重.md), with its dense central stack of strokes.
Learn the complete word: the broad idea of "reason" does not predict every compound's meaning.
