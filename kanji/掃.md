# 掃 — sweep; brush

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

掃 concerns sweeping or brushing something away. 掃く はく is a verb for sweeping, while 掃除 そうじ names cleaning more generally. The character does not mean that every activity called 掃除 must use a broom.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside a right-hand shape ending in 巾 and imagine a hand moving a cleaning tool. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified origin.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.
