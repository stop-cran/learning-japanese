# 響 — resound; echo

**Strokes:** 20 · **Key (radical):** 音 (no. 180), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

響 concerns sound resounding or carrying, and extends to an effect reaching
something else. [響く](../words/響く.md) ひびく can describe a sound
echoing or an event affecting something; [響き](../words/響き.md)
ひびき is a sound, resonance or impression in the relevant context.

[影響](../words/影響.md) えいきょう means influence or effect.
It need not concern any audible sound. Do not restrict the character to
literal echoes merely because that is the easiest image to remember.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 郷 above [音](音.md).
Imagine a sound rising from the lower 音 and reaching every corner of the
intricate upper block. This is a visual memory aid, not a verified historical
or sound-component analysis. 郷 has no kanji card here yet.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キョウ | on | [影響](../words/影響.md) えいきょう - influence; effect |
| ひび.く | kun | [響く](../words/響く.md) ひびく - resound; affect |

Both readings are selected with linked examples. 響く is an intransitive
godan verb: 響 writes ひび and く is written in kana.
The noun 響き ひびき is related, but its final き is part of that noun's
spelling, not a replacement ending to insert into every use of 響く.

## Common words

- [響く](../words/響く.md) ひびく - resound; have an effect
- [響き](../words/響き.md) ひびき - sound; resonance; impression

## Notes

- [境](境.md), boundary, [競](競.md), compete, and [鏡](鏡.md),
  mirror, have related upright shapes and readings, but different complete
  forms and meanings. 響 has 音 at the bottom, not the lower shapes of those cards.
- Sounding overlaps [音](音.md), [鳴](鳴.md) and [声](声.md);
  vibration overlaps [震](震.md), and effect overlaps [効](効.md).
  They are useful semantic comparisons, not clearly wrong meaning choices.
- JMdict separately records 響く どよめく and 響き どよめき in other
  lexical entries, with kana-preferred or rare-kanji qualifications.
  Those are not free alternate readings for the linked ひびく and ひびき
  examples. Bare 響 for ひびき is itself an irregular-okurigana spelling;
  use the full spelling 響き shown here.
