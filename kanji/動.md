# 動 — move

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **Phonetic:** 重 · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

動 is a phono-semantic compound: [力](力.md) (strength, effort) gives the sense and 重 gives the sound (重 is read ジュウ/チョウ, so the fit with ドウ is only approximate in Japanese). The meaning is "to move, to set in motion, to shake", and so "motion, action".
The same 力 appears as a component in 働 (work), a Japanese-made character built on 動 with 亻 added (the radical of 働 is 人/亻). Reading 重 as "heavy" and 力 as "strength" to get "using strength to move something heavy" is a mnemonic only; it happens to fit the sense but is not claimed as the origin.

## Common words

- [自動車](../words/自動車.md) じどうしゃ — automobile
- 動物 どうぶつ — animal
- 運動 うんどう — exercise, movement
- 動く うごく — to move

## Notes

- 動く is intransitive (the clock moves), 動かす is transitive (move something): 車が動く but 車を動かす.
- 動 in 動物 contrasts with 植物 (plant): "moving things" versus "growing things".
- Look-alikes: 働 (はたら.く, work) is 動 with 亻 on the left, and 重 (heavy) is the left part of 動.
