# 伎 — skill; performer

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

伎 means skill or fine technique, and it also means a performer or entertainer. KANJIDIC2 lists “deed” and “skill”. The word [歌舞伎](../words/歌舞伎.md) かぶき is “kabuki; traditional form of drama and music performed by male actors wearing makeup mainly in white and red”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 亻 stands on the left, a variant of [人](人.md), "person," and [支](支.md), "support," stands on the right, with [十](十.md), "ten," at its top and the shape 又 at its bottom. Picture a performer leaning on a support pole and showing the crowd ten practiced tricks. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 亻 or 支 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists キ only, with 歌舞伎. Kanjipedia lists ギ and the kun わざ and たくみ outside the table (外); Digital Daijisen gives キ, ギ and the kun わざ.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 伎, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These eight cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [技](技.md) (“skill; technique”), [術](術.md) (“technique”), [能](能.md) (“ability; capacity”), [腕](腕.md) (“arm; skill”) and [熟](熟.md) (“ripen; mature; skilled”): 伎 can mean skill or fine technique.
- [芸](芸.md) (“art; performance; craft”), [俳](俳.md) (“haiku; actor”) and [演](演.md) (“perform; stage”): 伎 can also mean a performer or entertainer.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 供 (“accompany, offer”), 食 (“eat, food”), and 自 (“self, oneself”); none means skill or a performer.
