# 面 — surface; mask

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 面 (no. 176) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

面 can mean a face, mask, or surface. 表面 (ひょうめん) names a surface or exterior.
In [面白い](../words/面白い.md) おもしろい, however, learn the whole adjective meaning "interesting";
"white face" is not a usable translation of it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the inner upright strokes as a face framed by the broad outer enclosure.
This is a visual memory aid, not a reconstruction of the character's origin.

## Notes

[顔](顔.md) focuses on the face in words such as 顔 (かお); 面 also covers surfaces and masks.
Both characters can mean "face," though their uses depend on the word.
Distinguish the inner arrangement from [目](目.md), the top and enclosure from [百](百.md),
and the inner 口 shape of [同](同.md).
