# 脂 — fat; grease

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

脂 concerns fat or grease and appears in compounds for sebum and resin. The whole word matters: 脂 あぶら and 樹脂 じゅし do not have identical lexical scope.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the flesh-shaped left side beside 旨 and imagine noticing fat in a piece of meat. This is an invented modern-shape mnemonic, not an explanation of the character's historical structure or sound.

The canonical key 肉, no. 130, appears in the compressed 月-shaped form at the left.

## Notes

- [油](../words/油.md) あぶら is the separate oil entry. Do not turn the contrast into an absolute solid/liquid or animal/plant rule. 油脂 ゆし groups fats and oils.
- 皮脂 names sebum, while 樹脂 names resin. A character used in the resin compound does not make every native 脂 あぶら a general plant-material noun.
- [石油](../words/石油.md) せきゆ concerns petroleum and also has a kerosene sense; the broad English oil label is not an unrestricted Japanese synonym for food fat.
- Compare [指](指.md), finger, with a hand-shaped left side, and [服](服.md), clothing. A matching right half or similar left side does not establish the same meaning.
