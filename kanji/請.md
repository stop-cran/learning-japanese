# 請 — request; beg; undertake

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

請 is asking someone for something, often formally, and, read うける, taking on a job someone asks you to do. [申請](../words/申請.md) しんせい is an application or formal request, [請求](../words/請求.md) せいきゅう is a claim, a bill or a request, and [請う](../words/請う.md) こう is to beg, request or invite.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for セイ: asking for something, wishing for it, begging, as in 請願 せいがん, "petition," 要請 ようせい, "appeal; call for something; request," 請求 and 申請. It gives シン and ショウ the same sense, with 普請 ふしん, "construction; building (e.g. a house)," for シン, and 勧請 かんじょう, "praying for the coming of a deity," and 起請 きしょう, "vow," for ショウ. More Daijisen examples of the セイ sense are listed at the end of the Notes.

Among hard-to-read words it lists 強請る, read ねだる, "to beg; to pester; to plead; to coax," or ゆする, "to solicit; to demand; to extort"; JMdict marks both as special whole-word readings usually written in kana alone.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [言](言.md), "say; words," at the left, and [青](青.md), "blue, green," at the right. Imagine saying your request out loud under a clear blue sky. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 青 contributes meaning or sound, although 青 is read セイ, a reading 請 also has. Historical formation is not established here.

| シン | on | 普請 ふしん - construction; building (e.g. a house); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ショウ | on | 起請 きしょう - vow; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| こ.う | kun | [請う](../words/請う.md) こう — beg; request; invite |
| う.ける | kun | 請け負う うけおう - to contract (to do); to undertake; to take on (work); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 申請, 申 supplies シン and 請 supplies セイ; the しん at the start belongs to 申, not to the シン reading of 請. In 請求, 請 supplies セイ and 求 supplies キュウ. In 請う, 請 writes こ and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 請け負う, 請 writes う, け is okurigana, and 負う おう follows. In 勧請 the ショウ of 請 is voiced to じょう after カン, the reading of 勧.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セイ, with 請求, 請願 and 申請 as examples; シン, with 普請; こう, with 請う and the spelling contrast 請う ⇔ 乞う; and うける, with 請ける, 請負 うけおい, "contract (for work); contracting; undertaking," and 下請け したうけ, "subcontract," and the spelling contrast 請ける ⇔ 受ける. JMdict lists 請ける as a spelling in its entry for うける headed by 受ける. ショウ is not in the official table. Practice selects セイ and こ.う, the readings with linked word articles; シン, ショウ and う.ける are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [申](申.md), "humbly say," in 申請 and [求](求.md), "seek; request," in 請求. 請う is 請 with okurigana only.
- [清](清.md), "pure, clean," and [晴](晴.md), "clear skies," have 青 at the right as 請 does, and [静](静.md), "quiet," has 青 at the left; all three are read セイ, and 清 also has the readings シン and ショウ. All are different kanji.
- [求](求.md), "seek; request," [頼](頼.md), "request; rely," [願](願.md), "wish; petition," [招](招.md), "invite," [問](問.md), "ask, question," [伺](伺.md), "ask humbly; visit humbly," [聞](聞.md), "hear, ask," [承](承.md), "accept; undertake," [企](企.md), "plan; undertake," [就](就.md), "take a position; undertake," and [受](受.md), "receive; undergo," overlap the senses "request," "petition," "ask," "invite," "undertake" and "receive" that 請 and its words carry; 求 appears in 請求, and the official table contrasts 請ける with 受ける, both read うける. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "request" or "undertake" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the セイ sense: 懇請 こんせい, "appeal; entreaty; request," 招請 しょうせい, "invitation," 奏請 そうせい, "petitioning the Emperor," and 強請 きょうせい or ごうせい, "blackmail; extortion; coercion."
