# 配 — distribute

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 酉 (no. 164) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

配 describes distributing or allocating things. 配る くばる means handing things out, and 配達 はいたつ means delivery.
[心配](../words/心配.md) しんぱい is a separate established compound for worry; a literal "distribute hearts" translation will mislead you.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine handing out items from the 酉-shaped container to the curled 己 beside it.
This is a modern-shape story, not an account of the character's history.

[酒](酒.md) also contains 酉, but pairs it with 氵 rather than 己.
