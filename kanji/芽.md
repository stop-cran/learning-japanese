# 芽 — bud; sprout

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

芽 is a plant's bud or sprout: the first stem and leaves emerging from a seed, or an undeveloped growing point on a plant. It can also describe something new that is about to grow, such as budding talent. The single-character noun [芽](../words/芽.md) is read め; the on-reading ガ appears in compounds such as 発芽 はつが, “germination.”

## Notes

- Joyo lists ガ and め. KANJIDIC2 also records the name readings じ and めぐ; Digital Daijisen lists めい as a name reading. Keep these name forms separate from the ordinary readings and quiz.
- The title overlaps [苗](苗.md), “seedling; young plant”: an emerging sprout can be described as a young plant, although 芽 focuses on the developing bud or shoot. 苗 is excluded from meaning choices.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 犬 (“dog”), 山 (“mountain”), and 水 (“water”); none means a bud or sprout.
