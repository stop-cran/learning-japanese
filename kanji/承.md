# 承 — accept; undertake

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

承 concerns receiving or accepting something, acknowledging it or undertaking it. 承知 しょうち can express awareness or acceptance; 承認 しょうにん is approval or recognition. These complete words do not mean exactly the same kind of acceptance.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the central hooked stroke with strokes spreading around it and imagine a message being received and passed on. This is a visual mnemonic, not a historical analysis.

KANJIDIC2 indexes 承 under 手. The full modern character does not display a separate left-hand 扌; the indexing key is not a claim that it does.

## Notes

- 承和 じょうわ is an era name, not an ordinary noun meaning acceptance. It illustrates ジョウ without changing the ショウ reading in 承知.
- The Joyo table, the official guide to standard-use kanji and readings, lists ショウ and うけたまわる for 承, not ジョウ or うける. Learn 承ける for recognition, not as a general replacement for 受ける; table status alone does not establish rarity.
- 承る is a humble verb in its hearing sense. It does not elevate the other person's hearing in the way a respectful verb would.
- JMdict lists 承ける alongside 受ける, 請ける and 享ける in the same entry. This row illustrates receive/get, not unrestricted spelling substitution across every sense. The entry notes ウケる especially for its colloquial amusement use, and 受ける/承ける especially for the linguistic sense be modified by; these notes are not exclusive restrictions.
