# 亭 — pavilion; restaurant building

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 亠 (the top dot and horizontal, no. 8) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

亭 can refer to a pavilion or to a building used for dining or entertainment. In [料亭](../words/料亭.md) りょうてい, it points to a restaurant, not overnight accommodation. The whole word determines the kind of establishment.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 亠 cap and [口](口.md)-shaped box above a second roof-like 冖 and a long 丁-shaped support: a small pavilion with a tall central post. This is a memory scene for the modern shape, not a historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| テイ | on | [料亭](../words/料亭.md) りょうてい - traditional Japanese restaurant, especially an upscale one |
| チン | on | 亭 ちん - a small pavilion for viewing or resting, for example in a garden; recognition example |

Practice selects テイ. チン is an attested reading of the pavilion noun, not a missing or name-only reading; it is retained for recognition here. The source supplies no kun readings.

The suffix 亭 can also end some restaurant names and writers' or performers' pseudonyms. Those suffix senses use てい, not an interchangeable ちん.

## Common words

- [料亭](../words/料亭.md) りょうてい - traditional Japanese restaurant, especially an upscale one

## Notes

- [停](停.md), "halt; stopping," adds the person-shaped 亻 on the left of 亭. A restaurant or pavilion is not the stopping action.
- [京](京.md), "capital," ends in 小, and [享](享.md), "enjoy; receive benefits," ends in 子. 亭 instead has the covered 丁-shaped lower part.
- 料亭 is not the lodging word [旅館](../words/旅館.md) りょかん. Nor does every restaurant use 亭 in its name.
