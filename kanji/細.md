# 細 — slender; fine; detailed

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

細 concerns slender shape, fineness or detail. Its native adjectives describe different dimensions: 細い is slender or narrow, while 細かい concerns small pieces or minute detail.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the thread-shaped left side beside 田 and imagine fine threads dividing a field into detailed sections. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not an etymology.

The canonical key 糸, no. 120, appears on the left as ⺯.

## Notes

- 細かな説明 こまかなせつめい and 細かい説明 こまかいせつめい both mean a detailed explanation, but use different adjective forms: na-adjectival 細か versus i-adjectival 細かい. The source preserves both okurigana boundaries.
- 細る is an intransitive godan verb: 細ります ほそります and 細って ほそって. It describes becoming thin or tapering off, not making an object thin.
- Compare [小](小.md), small/little. Small overall size, a slender shape and fine detail can overlap, but they are not one unrestricted adjective. The linked articles explain the dimensions in context.
