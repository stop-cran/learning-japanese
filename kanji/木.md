# 木 — tree, wood

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

木 is a pictograph of a tree: a trunk with branches above and roots below. It also means the material, "wood".
As a key, it marks trees and wooden things: 林 (grove, two trees), 森 (forest, three), 板, 橋, 机.

Repetition builds meaning here: one 木 is a tree, 林 a small wood, 森 a forest. This is a useful memory aid, not a
strict rule for all repeated kanji.

## Notes

Don't mix it up with 本 (book, origin), which is 木 with a mark at the base, or 末 (end, tip), with the mark at the top.
