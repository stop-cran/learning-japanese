# 臭 — foul smell; stink

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 自 (no. 132) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

臭 is about smell. It commonly points to an unpleasant odor, as in 悪臭 (あくしゅう), “foul smell,” and can also describe a suspicious or characteristic “air” about something. The native adjective 臭い (くさい) means “smelly” or “stinking”; the separate noun 臭い (におい) means “smell” or “odor,” often an unpleasant one.

## Notes

The selected quiz reading is **くさ.い**: in the whole-word adjective 臭い (くさい), 臭 supplies くさ. Do not use the noun 臭い (におい) to justify that selection. The noun’s spelling often uses 匂い for a neutral or pleasant scent and 臭い for an unpleasant odor; 臭う is likewise commonly used for a bad smell, while 匂う can mean “smell fragrant.” The JMdict entry for 臭う also records “glow; be bright,” a less usual sense.

Kanjipedia also lists キュウ as an outside-Jōyō on-reading. It is not in this card’s KANJIDIC2 reading list, and no checked JMdict example in the evidence set supports it; it is not a quiz reading.

Digital Daijisen prints シウ as the historical sound form for シュウ; this is not a separate modern reading or quiz option.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 雪 (“snow”), 糸 (“thread; yarn”), and 鳥 (“bird”); none concerns an unpleasant smell.
