# 臭 — foul smell; stink

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 自 (no. 132) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

臭 is about smell. It commonly points to an unpleasant odor, as in 悪臭 (あくしゅう), “foul smell,” and can also describe a suspicious or characteristic “air” about something. The native adjective 臭い (くさい) means “smelly” or “stinking”; the separate noun 臭い (におい) means “smell” or “odor,” often an unpleasant one.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [自](自.md), "self, oneself," sits on top of [大](大.md), "big, large." Imagine yourself catching a big, bad smell. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 自 or 大 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

The selected quiz reading is **くさ.い**: in the whole-word adjective 臭い (くさい), 臭 supplies くさ. The noun 臭い (におい) is a different word and does not show this reading. JMdict notes that the noun spelling 臭い is used for unpleasant smells. Its shared entry for the verb におう gives “be fragrant” as usually written 匂う and “stink” as usually written 臭う.

Kanjipedia also lists キュウ as an outside-Jōyō on-reading. It is not in this card’s KANJIDIC2 reading list, and no checked JMdict example in the evidence set supports it; it is not a quiz reading.

Digital Daijisen prints シウ as the historical sound form for シュウ; this is not a separate modern reading or quiz option.

KANJIDIC2 lists “suspicious looking” among the meanings of 臭, and JMdict gives “suspicious; fishy” as a sense of the quiz word 臭い (くさい). That overlaps with the displayed title of 怪 (“suspicious; uncanny”), so 怪 is excluded from this card's meaning quiz.

KANJIDIC2 also lists “fragrance,” “be fragrant,” “glow,” and “be bright” for 臭, and JMdict's shared におう entry includes “to be fragrant” and “to glow; to be bright.” JMdict marks the fragrant sense as usually written 匂う. Digital Daijisen defines 臭 as a smell, especially an unpleasant one (特に、いやなにおい), and neither it nor Kanjipedia gives a glowing or bright sense. 香 (“fragrance; incense”) and 明 (“bright, clear”) are therefore contrasts rather than alternative answers, and they are not excluded.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 雪 (“snow”), 糸 (“thread; yarn”), and 鳥 (“bird”); none concerns an unpleasant smell.
