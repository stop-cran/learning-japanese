# 法 — law; method

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

法 can concern a rule that governs conduct or a method for doing something.
[方法](../words/方法.md) ほうほう is a method; 法律 (ほうりつ) is law.
The shared character does not make an ordinary method a legal requirement.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** combine the visible water strokes with [去](去.md), "leave":
an orderly method makes a mess go away. This is not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

この方法でやります。 (このほうほうでやります。) — I will do it using this method.
Here で marks the method used. Visually, keep the lower 厶-shaped part of 去 separate from
the lower legs in [洗](洗.md) and the spreading right side of [泳](泳.md).
