# 章 — chapter; badge

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 立 (no. 117) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

章 can identify a chapter or section of a written work, and also a badge, medal or insignia. In 文章 it participates in the broader idea of written text, not a promise that the text contains several book chapters.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 立 above a 早-shaped lower part and imagine a chapter heading standing above the page. This uses the modern shape only, not an account of the character's origin.

The indexing key is 立, no. 117, at the top.

## Notes

- Learn numbered chapters as whole expressions: 第一章 だいいっしょう, chapter one, and 第三章 だいさんしょう, chapter three. The first has a small っ.
- The badge sense and the chapter sense are separate uses, not a statement that a medal is a section of a book. The linked [章](../words/章.md) article gives both.
