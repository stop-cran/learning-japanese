# 貴 — precious; esteem

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

貴 means high in value or rank, and treating something as precious. KANJIDIC2 gives “precious,” “value,” “prize,” “esteem” and “honor.” Kanjipedia gives three senses: high in rank or value (身分や価値が高い); to treat as important (大切にする); and a word expressing respect for the person addressed (相手への敬意を表す語), as in 貴下 きか and 貴殿 きでん, pronouns that JMdict glosses “you.” The declaring word [貴重](../words/貴重.md) きちょう is glossed in JMdict “precious; valuable.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [中](中.md), "middle, inside," made of [口](口.md), "mouth," and a vertical stroke 丨, sits at the top, and [貝](貝.md), "shellfish; seashell," drawn as [目](目.md), "eye," over [八](八.md), "eight," sits at the bottom. Picture a single seashell kept in the very middle of a display case: precious and admired. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 中 or 貝 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists キ, with 貴重, 貴下 and 騰貴 とうき as examples, and all four kun readings, pairing 貴い with 尊い and 貴ぶ with 尊ぶ (⇔). Kanjipedia marks the four kun with 中, a school-level mark, not 外 (outside the table).
- Kanjipedia lists たかい outside the table (外); no checked JMdict word uses たかい for 貴. Digital Daijisen also has headwords あて and むち written with 貴.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings きよ, ぎ, たか, たかし and よし; Digital Daijisen gives あつ, あて, たか, たかし, たけ, むち and よし. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.

These eleven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [尊](尊.md) (“revere; esteem”): KANJIDIC2 gives 貴 “esteem,” the Joyo table pairs 貴い and 貴ぶ with 尊い and 尊ぶ, and JMdict entries 1598620 and 1598640 write each word with either character.
- [宝](宝.md) (“treasure; valuables”): JMdict glosses 貴重 “precious; valuable,” and 貴重品 きちょうひん (1223530) “valuable article; valuables.”
- [価](価.md) (“worth; price”) and [値](値.md) (“value; price”): KANJIDIC2 gives “value” for both 貴 and 価, and the 値 card's title also has “value.”
- [高](高.md) (“high, expensive”): Kanjipedia's first sense is 身分や価値が高い (high in rank or value), and it lists たかい outside the table (外).
- [敬](敬.md) (“respect; honor”), [御](御.md) (“honorific; govern”) and [様](様.md) (“manner; honorific suffix”): KANJIDIC2 gives 貴 “honor,” and Kanjipedia's third sense is a word expressing respect for the person addressed (相手への敬意を表す語).
- [賞](賞.md) (“prize; reward”): KANJIDIC2 gives 貴 “prize,” and JMdict glosses 貴ぶ “to value; to prize; to esteem.”
- [惜](惜.md) (“pity; precious; begrudge”): its title shares “precious.”
- [珍](珍.md) (“rare; unusual”): JMdict 珍重 ちんちょう (1431900), “prizing; valuing highly; esteeming,” overlaps 貴's “prize” and “esteem.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 汚 (“dirty; defile”), 粗 (“coarse; rough; crude”), and 軽 (“lightweight; slight”); none means precious or esteemed.
