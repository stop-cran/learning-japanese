# 慶 — celebration; joyous occasion

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慶 is joy at a happy occasion, and the happy occasion itself. It appears in 慶弔, in 慶事 (けいじ), “a happy event,” and in 慶祝 (けいしゅく), “celebration.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 广 is at the top, like a roof; [鹿](鹿.md), “deer,” starts with the same top. Under it are [心](心.md), “heart,” and then 夂 at the bottom, the same order as in [愛](愛.md), “love.”

## Notes

[鹿](鹿.md) shares the top part and means deer; [愛](愛.md) and [憂](憂.md) also have 心 above 夂, but mean love and worry. [喜](喜.md), [祝](祝.md), [歓](歓.md), [悦](悦.md), [幸](幸.md), [福](福.md), [吉](吉.md), [愉](愉.md), [楽](楽.md) and [享](享.md) relate to joy, celebration, good fortune or enjoyment. The [実用日本語表現辞典](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E6%85%B6) entry notes that 慶 is often used in expressions such as 謹んでお慶び申し上げます (つつしんでおよろこびもうしあげます), a formal congratulation. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 45](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=45) gives 慶 only ケイ, with 慶弔, 慶祝 and 慶賀 (けいが), “congratulation.”
