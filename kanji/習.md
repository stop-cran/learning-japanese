# 習 — learn; practise

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 羽 (no. 124) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

習 concerns learning and practice. [習う](../words/習う.md) (ならう) commonly means learning from a teacher;
練習 (れんしゅう) is practice.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** imagine the paired 羽 shapes as wings practising above a
[white](白.md) training mat. This is an invented modern-shape association, not an account of the ancient character.

- 練習 れんしゅう — practice
- 復習 ふくしゅう — review of learned material
- 予習 よしゅう — preparation before a lesson

## Notes

習う and [教える](../words/教える.md) (おしえる, teach) describe different sides of instruction.
Someone can 習う from a teacher and then 練習する (れんしゅうする, practise) independently.

The bottom is 白, with its short upper stroke, not just 日. [終](終.md) also has the reading シュウ,
but is about finishing rather than learning.
