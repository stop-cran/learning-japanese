# 晩 — evening; night

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

晩 belongs to the evening and nighttime part of the day.
[今晩](../words/今晩.md) こんばん means "this evening; tonight," while 毎晩 まいばん
means "every night." The exact hour depends on context, not a fixed clock boundary in the kanji.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 日 beside the 免-shaped right side and imagine daylight leaving
for the night. This is only a visual memory aid.

- 毎晩 まいばん — every night
- 晩ご飯 ばんごはん — dinner; evening meal

## Notes

[勉](勉.md) shares the 免-shaped part but adds 力 rather than 日.
[映](映.md) and [晴](晴.md) have the same left-hand 日 with different right sides.
[夜](夜.md), "night," and [夕](夕.md), "evening," overlap with 晩's time-of-day meanings.
