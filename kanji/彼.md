# 彼 — he

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The main learner word is [彼](../words/彼.md) かれ, "he; him." The character also occurs in
彼女 かのじょ, "she; her": a character-level gloss is not a translation recipe for every compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 彳 on the left and the 皮 shape on the right; picture recognizing
the person by that distinctive right-hand outline. This is a visual aid, not historical derivation.

## Notes

In 彼女, かの belongs to 彼 and ジョ to [女](女.md). Do not read the compound as かれじょ.
Compare the right side carefully with [役](役.md), [待](待.md), and [後](後.md), which have the same
narrow 彳 on the left but do not mean "he."
