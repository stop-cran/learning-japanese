# 真 — true, genuine

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

真 concerns truth, reality, and genuineness. In some native words, ま- strengthens the following idea, as in 真っ白 (まっしろ), "pure white."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the [目](目.md)-shaped center as an eye looking directly at the truth, with [十](十.md)-like strokes above and a stand below. This is a mnemonic for the visible form, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

Compare [目](目.md) and [百](百.md): neither has the full top-and-bottom arrangement of 真.
In 真っ白, ま- appears as まっ before しろ. A kanji's meaning is not a guarantee about the real world: 写真 means a photograph, not necessarily an unaltered or truthful image.
