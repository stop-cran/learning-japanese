# 火 — fire

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

火 is a pictograph of a flame with sparks, later stylised. As a key it appears on the left as 火 or at the bottom as four dots 灬 (れっか), in words related to fire, heat and
cooking: 焼, 灯, 点, 照, 熱, 烈.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カ | on | 火曜日 かようび, [火山](../words/火山.md) かざん, 火事 かじ, 火災 かさい |
| ひ | kun | 火 ひ, 火花 ひばな, 火傷 やけど (special) |
| -び | kun | 花火 はなび, たき火 たきび |
| ほ- | kun | 火照る ほてる (be flushed) |

## Common words

- [火山](../words/火山.md) かざん — volcano
- 火事 かじ — a fire (accidental), not a bonfire
- 火曜日 かようび — Tuesday

## Notes

火 can be read ひ for "fire" and ひ for "day" ([日](日.md)). Pronunciation is the same; kanji and context tell them apart.
