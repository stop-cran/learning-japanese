# 通 — pass through; commute

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

通 concerns passage, connection, and traffic. [通う](../words/通う.md) かよう means making repeated trips,
such as attending school; 通る とおる means passing through or along a place.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the box-like inner 甬 shape as a passageway, with a route sweeping around it.
This is a visual association for the modern character, not a claim about its origin.

- 交通 こうつう — traffic; transportation

## Notes

Keep かよう and とおる separate: repeated attendance is not the same as passing a place on a route.
Write とおる and とおす with おお, not おう.
[送](送.md) and [道](道.md) have similar outer shapes but different centers.
