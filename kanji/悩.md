# 悩 — worry; be troubled

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

悩 concerns worry, distress or being troubled. 悩む なやむ describes experiencing trouble or struggling with a decision; 悩ます なやます describes troubling or tormenting someone. 苦悩 くのう is anguish or distress.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 忄 beside small upper marks over a boxed crossed shape and imagine a tangled concern occupying the heart. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 心, visible here as the left-hand form 忄.

## Notes

- 悩む is intransitive; 悩ます is transitive. Changing the written ending changes who experiences or causes the trouble.
- The noun なやみ is written 悩み, with final み in kana. The complete reading does not absorb that kana into a standalone 悩 spelling.
- 悩ましい also has seductive or enchanting and anxious senses in JMdict. The troubling/difficult gloss in the table is sense 2, not an exhaustive definition.
