# 術 — technique

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 行 (no. 144) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

術 concerns a technique, skill, or means of doing something. The field comes from the whole word:
[手術](../words/手術.md) しゅじゅつ is a surgical operation, whereas 美術 びじゅつ concerns the fine arts.
Do not translate every occurrence as either "surgery" or "art."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the two sides of 行 frame a narrow central shape with a dot.
Imagine following a carefully controlled route through the middle: a practiced technique.
This is a visual aid, not an etymology or identification of a phonetic component.

[行](行.md) supplies the recognizable outer frame, while [待](待.md) shares only its left side.
[特](特.md) is another compact left-right shape, but begins with a cattle component rather than 彳.
