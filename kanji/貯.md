# 貯 — save up; store

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

貯 concerns saving up or keeping something in store. The examples include money and water; not every use is restricted to a bank account.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the [貝](貝.md) on the left as saved valuables beside a peg-shaped 丁 marking where they are stored. The peg is a visual cue, not a historical meaning or phonetic claim.

## Notes

- 貯める is a transitive ichidan verb for saving up money. 貯える is also transitive and ichidan, but its dictionary family has broader senses, including accumulating knowledge or experience and growing a beard or moustache.
- JMdict lists 蓄える and 貯える in the same たくわえる entry. A shared entry does not establish that both spellings are equally frequent or equally preferred in every collocation.
- 貯 has 貝 on the left. [打](打.md) instead has 扌, while [頂](頂.md) has 頁 on the right; the shared 丁-shaped part does not make their meanings the same.
