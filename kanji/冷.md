# 冷 — cold; chill

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 冫 (no. 15) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

冷 concerns coldness and cooling. [冷たい](../words/冷たい.md) つめたい describes a cold drink or cold hands,
and can also describe emotionally cold behavior.
Different verbs distinguish becoming cold from making something cold.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture two icy flecks on the left of the angular 令 shape.
This associates the visible 冫 with coldness without claiming a historical analysis of the whole character.

## Notes

水が冷える (みずがひえる) means "the water gets cold"; 水を冷やす (みずをひやす) means "chill the water."
For cold weather or feeling cold, compare [寒い](../words/寒い.md) さむい.
The left radical has two strokes, not the three of 氵. [今](今.md), [公](公.md), and [会](会.md)
offer modest top-shape comparisons, but none has 冷's complete 冫 + 令 arrangement.
