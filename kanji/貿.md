# 貿 — trade; exchange

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

貿 concerns trade or exchange. 貿易 is the useful whole word for foreign or international trade, including importing and exporting.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the [貝](貝.md) at the bottom as goods passing between two stalls suggested by the upper strokes. This invented trading scene is not a reconstruction of the original character.

## Notes

- The abbreviation 内貿 ないぼう names domestic commerce. This keeps the character's broader trade meaning separate from the specifically foreign-trade meaning of 貿易.
- Compare [留](留.md), retain or stay, which has 田 at the bottom rather than 貝. The similar upper shapes do not make it a trade character.
