# 妻 — wife

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

妻 identifies a wife, including in the standalone noun [妻](../words/妻.md) (つま).
It specifies a marriage relationship, not someone's age, personality, or role in household work.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a name label marked "wife" below a tall stack of address lines.
Use the 女 shape at the bottom as the visual anchor; the scene is not an etymology.
