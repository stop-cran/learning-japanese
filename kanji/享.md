# 享 — enjoy; receive benefits

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 亠 (the top dot and horizontal, no. 8) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

享 connects receiving something with enjoying or benefiting from it. In [享受](../words/享受.md) きょうじゅ, it helps express enjoying benefits or rights, not simply having a good time.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a [子](子.md)-shaped child beneath the top cap and [口](口.md)-shaped box, receiving a welcome gift. This is a memory scene for the modern shape, not a historical component explanation.

| う.ける | kun | 享ける うける - receive (recognition spelling); 生を享ける せいをうける, "be born" |

Practice selects キョウ. The character dictionary 字通 also recognizes コウ, but that does not make it interchangeable with キョウ in 享受.

享ける is an attested spelling, not a missing word example. The broad verb is normally learned as [受ける](../words/受ける.md) うける. JMdict particularly associates 受ける/享ける with being given life or talent and with succession; this does not authorize replacing 受 with 享 in every receiving, examination or colloquial use. The spelling is retained for recognition, not selected for practice here.

## Common words

- [享受](../words/享受.md) きょうじゅ - enjoyment of benefits or rights
- 享年 きょうねん - age at death

## Notes

- Distinguish [京](京.md), "capital": its bottom is 小-shaped, whereas 享 ends in 子. [厚](厚.md) has a 厂 overhang and a 日-shaped box above 子; 享 instead starts with 亠 above 口.
- [受](受.md) is the broader receiving/undergoing character. Learn the whole word and spelling rather than substituting either character mechanically.
- 享年 names age at death, not someone's current age or a general wish for a long life.
