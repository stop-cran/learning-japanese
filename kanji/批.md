# 批 — criticize; critique

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

批 concerns examining something by comparison and giving a judgment on it. The judgment is not always negative: [批判](../words/批判.md) ひはん is criticism or critical judgment, and [批評](../words/批評.md) ひひょう is a critique or review. The Daijisen character entry gives a second sense of a ruler or authority approving something, seen in 批准 ひじゅん, "ratification."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the side form of [手](手.md), "hand," at the left and [比](比.md), "compare; ratio," at the right. Imagine a hand weighing two things side by side. 比 also reads ヒ, which makes a handy sound cue, but this lesson makes no claim about the historical formation of 批.

| ヒ | on | [批判](../words/批判.md) ひはん — criticism; critical judgment; [批評](../words/批評.md) ひひょう — critique; review |

In 批判, 批 supplies ヒ and 判 supplies ハン. In 批評, 批 supplies ヒ and 評 supplies ヒョウ. KANJIDIC2 lists no kun reading for 批, so none is taught or selected here.

## Notes

- [判](判.md), "judge; seal," is the other kanji in 批判. 批判 combines 批's sense of examining with 判's sense of judging.
- [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible," overlaps with 批 because 責める includes "to criticize" among its senses. 批 examines and judges, whereas 責 holds someone to account, so do not treat "criticize" as exclusive to either character.
- [悲](悲.md), "sadness," [疲](疲.md), "grow tired," and [被](被.md), "cover; incur," also read ヒ, but they are only sound-alikes: none of them shares the shape or the meaning of 批.
