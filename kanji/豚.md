# 豚 — pig; pork

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 豕 (no. 152), on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

豚 means **pig**, and also **pork** in food contexts. The native noun
[豚](../words/豚.md) ぶた names the animal; [豚肉](../words/豚肉.md)
ぶたにく explicitly names its meat.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the narrow
[月](月.md)-shaped outline on the left and the seven-stroke 豕 shape on the right.
Imagine a pig beside a small enclosure. This is a drawing memory aid, not
a claim that the moon or an enclosure explains the character's history.
The indexing radical is the right-hand 豕, not the similar-looking left side.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| トン | on | 養豚 ようとん — pig farming; compound recognition, not a reading question on this card |
| ぶた | kun | [豚](../words/豚.md) ぶた — pig; [豚肉](../words/豚肉.md) ぶたにく — pork |

豚肉 combines native ぶた with on にく. Its linked article teaches ぶたにく;
the dictionary also recognizes とんにく. Do not assume every compound uses
the animal noun's native reading: 養豚 uses トン.

## Common words

- [豚](../words/豚.md) ぶた — pig; pork in a food context
- [豚肉](../words/豚肉.md) ぶたにく — pork
- 養豚 ようとん — pig farming

## Notes

- [月](月.md), moon/month, is only a shape comparison here.
  [服](服.md), clothing, and [腹](腹.md), belly, also have a similar left outline,
  but different right sides. None means pig or pork.
- 豚 used about a person can be derogatory. It is not a polite description
  of someone's body; the animal and food uses do not carry that insult.
- Do not split the special whole-word reading 海豚 いるか, dolphin,
  into an invented reading of 豚. That kanji spelling is rare and the word
  is usually written in kana in the cited dictionary.
