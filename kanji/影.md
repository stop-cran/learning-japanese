# 影 — shadow; silhouette

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 彡 (no. 59), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

影 is a shadow, and by extension a silhouette, a reflected image or a trace of something. [影](../words/影.md) かげ is a shadow or silhouette, [影響](../words/影響.md) えいきょう is influence or effect, and [撮影](../words/撮影.md) さつえい is photography or filming, where 影 is the recorded image. The Daijisen character entry also gives a sense of light, as in 月影 つきかげ, "moonlight; moon; moonbeams." In 面影 おもかげ, "face; looks; countenance; vestige; trace; reminder," and 遺影 いえい, "portrait of a deceased person," 影 stands for a face or image that is kept in memory or on a photograph.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [景](景.md), "scenery," at the left, made of [日](日.md), "sun, day," above [京](京.md), "capital," and 彡 at the right. Imagine a scene whose slanting strokes trail off like a cast shadow. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 景 or 彡 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| エイ | on | [影響](../words/影響.md) えいきょう — influence; effect; [撮影](../words/撮影.md) さつえい — photography; filming |
| かげ | kun | [影](../words/影.md) かげ — shadow; silhouette |

In 影響, 影 supplies エイ and 響 supplies キョウ. In 撮影, 撮 supplies サツ and 影 supplies エイ. In 影, the single kanji writes the native noun かげ with no okurigana. Practice selects エイ and かげ. The Daijisen character entry also lists the Go-on reading ヨウ; KANJIDIC2 does not, so it is not part of the metadata here.

## Notes

- [景](景.md), "scenery," is the left-hand part and reads ケイ; [京](京.md), "capital," sits inside it. JMdict also lists 景 as an alternative kanji form of the noun かげ, but this lesson teaches 景 as "scenery" and not as a spelling of かげ.
- [形](形.md), "shape; form," also has 彡 at the right, but its left-hand part is different.
- [映](映.md), "reflect, project," also reads エイ and concerns reflected or projected images, so its meaning overlaps with 影. Do not treat "image" as exclusive to either character.
- [撮](撮.md), "photograph; film," is the other kanji in 撮影.
