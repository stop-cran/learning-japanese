# 捕 — capture

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

捕 concerns catching or capturing. [捕まえる](../words/捕まえる.md) つかまえる describes catching someone or something;
捕まる つかまる describes being caught. The two perspectives need different sentence patterns.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the hand-side 扌 catching something against the gridded 甫 shape.
The image uses the modern appearance and is not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

犯人を捕まえる (はんにんをつかまえる) means "catch the culprit";
犯人が捕まる (はんにんがつかまる) means "the culprit gets caught."
捕まる is a separate intransitive verb, not the conjugated passive form of 捕まえる.

[指](指.md), [掛](掛.md), and [折](折.md) share the left-hand component.
The long vertical through the right-hand 甫, with its small upper-right dot, distinguishes 捕.
