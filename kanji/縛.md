# 縛 — bind; tie up; restrain

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

縛 is binding: tying with rope, and by extension taking away freedom. [縛る](../words/縛る.md) しばる is the verb; JMdict glosses it "to tie; to bind; to fasten" and "to restrict (freedom); to tie down (with rules, regulations, etc.); to fetter." The word article teaches "tie; bind."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for バク: to tie with rope, to deprive of freedom. Examples are 緊縛, 繋縛, 呪縛, 就縛, 束縛, 捕縛 and 自縄自縛. Daijisen lists the kun readings しばる and いましめる, and a headword ばく meaning a bond, in Buddhism the earthly desires.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 糸, "thread," at the left and the shape 尃 at the right, which also appears in [博](博.md), "broad knowledge; doctor; expo," and [薄](薄.md), "thin; dilute." Imagine thread wound round a bundle. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 or 尃 contributes meaning or sound, although 博 is also read バク. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| バク | on | 束縛 そくばく - "restraint; restriction; fetters; yoke; shackles"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| しば.る | kun | [縛る](../words/縛る.md) しばる — tie; bind |

In 縛る, 縛 supplies しば and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 縛り しばり is "binding," "regulation; limit," and "binding period (e.g. phone or broadband contract); minimum contract term." In 金縛り かなしばり, "binding hand and foot" and "sleep paralysis; temporary feeling of paralysis," 金 supplies かな and 縛り supplies しばり. In 束縛, 束 supplies ソク and 縛 supplies バク; in 捕縛 ほばく, "arrest; apprehension; capture," 捕 supplies ホ and 縛 supplies バク. 緊縛 きんばく is "binding tightly; tight binding." 縛 alone is also a JMdict word ばく, "tying up; restraint; restriction; arrest," and 縛める いましめる is "to bind (with rope, etc.)." 自縄自縛 じじょうじばく is "being caught in one's own trap; being hoisted by one's own petard," a yojijukugo (four-kanji idiom) read as a whole.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows バク, with 束縛 and 捕縛, and しばる, with 縛る and 金縛り. Practice selects しば.る, the reading of the linked word article 縛る. Readings outside the practice set are not invalid. Kanjipedia also lists ハク and いまし.める as outside-the-table readings; these are not KANJIDIC2 rows here.

## Notes

- 縛る contains no other kanji. Among the compounds named above, [束](束.md), "bundle," supplies ソク in 束縛, [捕](捕.md), "capture," supplies ホ in 捕縛, [金](金.md), "gold, money," supplies かな in 金縛り, [緊](緊.md) supplies キン in 緊縛 and [縄](縄.md), "rope; cord," appears in 自縄自縛.
- [結](結.md), [束](束.md), [括](括.md) and [締](締.md) overlap tying and fastening; [絞](絞.md) is also a tight-cord kanji (絞 and 締 list 縛 as an exclusion on their own cards, and the app treats exclusions as symmetric). [逮](逮.md) and [捕](捕.md) overlap arrest; [制](制.md), [限](限.md), [禁](禁.md), [圧](圧.md), [械](械.md) and [節](節.md) overlap restriction and restraint. All are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [専](専.md), "specialize; exclusive," looks plausible because of the similar right-hand shape and is read セン and もっぱ.ら. [細](細.md), "slender; fine; detailed," also has 糸 at the left and is read サイ and ほそ.い. [駅](駅.md), "station," is read エキ. None shares a reading or a meaning with 縛. Cards [博](博.md), [拍](拍.md), [泊](泊.md), [白](白.md) and [薄](薄.md) share ハク and are never offered as wrong answers.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples: 繋縛, 呪縛 and 就縛.
