# 菊 — chrysanthemum

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (as 艹, no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

菊 names the **chrysanthemum**, a flowering plant cultivated in many varieties. It also appears in words about chrysanthemum flowers, displays and designs.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find the grass-shaped 艹 at the top and a [米](米.md)-shaped part inside the curved enclosure below. Imagine flower petals gathered inside a wrapping. This is a visual memory aid, not a claim that the character historically means rice wrapped in grass.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| キク | on | [菊](../words/菊.md) きく - chrysanthemum; 菊の花 きくのはな - chrysanthemum flower |

The standalone plant noun is **きく**, but this is an **on-reading**, not a kun-reading merely because it is a word on its own. The frozen character source lists no kun-readings.

- 白菊 しらぎく - white chrysanthemum
- 菊人形 きくにんぎょう - doll dressed in chrysanthemum flowers and branches

## Notes

- Learn each compound's full pronunciation: 白菊 has ぎく, and 菊花 can have the small っ in きっか. These do not require replacing the source reading キク.
- Not every plant name containing 菊 identifies the same plant. 春菊 しゅんぎく is crown daisy, an edible leafy vegetable, rather than simply a chrysanthemum that blooms in spring.
- [花](花.md), flower, and [草](草.md), grass/herbs, are broader comparisons, not unambiguous wrong answers for this flowering plant.
- [米](米.md), rice grain, has no grass-shaped top or surrounding enclosure. [薬](薬.md), medicinal drug, shares 艹 but has a different lower arrangement; ornamental or traditional medicinal uses do not make 菊 a general word for medicine. [秋](秋.md), autumn, names a season, not this flower.
