# 珍 — rare; unusual

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 玉 (as left-hand 王, no. 96) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

珍 concerns rarity, novelty, or something unusual enough to attract notice. Some compounds add the
idea of valuing something; [珍しい](../words/珍しい.md) めずらしい need not mean valuable or suspicious.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine an unusual jewel beside the three slanting strokes
on the right. The indexing radical is 玉, whose left-hand form here resembles [王](王.md).

| たから | kun | Dictionary reading meaning treasure; compare the ordinary word 宝 たから — treasure |

KANJIDIC2 records たから, but the inspected JMdict entry for 宝 does not list 珍 among its spellings.
Retain this as a character-dictionary reading for recognition, not a suggested everyday replacement
for 宝. The useful modern adjective is 珍しい.

## Notes

[理](理.md) shares the left-hand radical but has 里 on the right. [形](形.md) has the three strokes
彡 on the right but a different left half. Keep 珍's upper-right person-like shape as well as 彡.

For 珍しい compared with [変](../words/変.md) (へん) and [怪しい](../words/怪しい.md) (あやしい), see
[strange, suspicious, and rare](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
