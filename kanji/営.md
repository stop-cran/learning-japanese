# 営 — conduct business; manage

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

営 concerns running a business or carrying on an activity. 営業 えいぎょう is business activity or sales, while 経営 けいえい emphasizes management or running an enterprise. 営む いとなむ also extends to carrying on a life or conducting a ceremony.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the small top strokes and cover sheltering the two stacked boxes of a business being run.

The radical is 口 (no. 30); it is visible in the stacked lower boxes. The top is not metadata for a different radical.

## Notes

- 営む is a transitive godan verb ending in む. 営み いとなみ is a noun, not a second spelling of the dictionary form.
- 営業 and 経営 are related, but sales activity and managing a business are not automatically interchangeable. The linked articles explain the relevant constructions.
