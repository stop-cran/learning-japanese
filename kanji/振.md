# 振 — wave; swing; allocate

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

振 is moving something back and forth, as in waving a hand or swinging a bat, and also handing out roles or work. [振る](../words/振る.md) ふる means to wave, shake or allocate; [振り仮名](../words/振り仮名.md) ふりがな is furigana, the kana pronunciation guide added to kanji; and [振舞う](../words/振舞う.md) ふるまう means to behave or to treat someone to food or drink. JMdict's entry for 振る lists twelve senses, beginning with "to wave; to shake; to swing," "to sprinkle; to throw (dice)" and "to cast (actor); to allocate (work)," and including "to turn down (someone); to reject; to jilt; to dump" and "to add kana indicating a reading of a word."

JMdict glosses 振り仮名 "furigana; kana printed above or next to kanji to indicate pronunciation" and marks it "word usually written using kana alone"; besides 振り仮名 it lists the spellings ふり仮名 and 振仮名, and 振りがな and フリ仮名 as search-only kanji forms. It spells 振舞う both 振舞う and 振る舞う, glossed "to behave; to conduct oneself; to act" and "to treat (someone) to (food or drink); to entertain (e.g. to lunch)." It also has a separate suffix ぶる, written 振る, "to assume an air of ...; to act ...; to affect ...; to pose as ...; to behave like ...," marked "word usually written using kana alone."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for シン. The first is shaking or swinging, as in 振動 しんどう, "oscillation; vibration; swing (e.g. of a pendulum)," 振幅 しんぷく, "amplitude (of vibration)," a physics term in JMdict, 強振 きょうしん, "swinging hard," and 三振 さんしん, "strikeout," a baseball term. The second is rousing oneself or gaining momentum, as in 振興 しんこう, "promotion; encouragement," and 不振 ふしん, "dullness; slump; stagnation." It lists the kun readings ふる, ふるう and ふれる.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the left, written 扌, and [辰](辰.md), "Dragon zodiac sign," at the right. Imagine a hand waving at a dragon until the dragon swings its long tail back and forth. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that these parts contribute meaning or sound, although 辰 is also read シン. Historical formation is not established here.

In 振る, 振 writes ふ and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 振り仮名, 振 writes ふ with the okurigana り, and 仮名 is read がな: its usual かな becomes がな after another element, a sound change called rendaku. In 振舞う, 振 supplies ふる and 舞 supplies まう; the spelling 振る舞う writes the る as okurigana. 振れる and 振るう use the same ふ with れる and るう. JMdict also spells ふれる 震れる and 偏れる, restricting its swinging sense to 振れる and 震れる, and spells ふるう 揮う and 振う, the latter labelled "irregular okurigana usage."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シン, with 振動, 振興 and 不振 as examples; ふる, with 振る and 振り ふり, "swing; shake; sweep; wave; wag"; ふるう, with 振るう, compared (⇔) with 奮う ふるう, "to muster (e.g. one's courage); to call forth; to rouse up," and 震う ふるう, a dated term in JMdict, "to shiver; to shake; to quake; to tremble"; and ふれる, with 振れる.

Practice selects ふ.る, the reading with linked word articles. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so ふ.る supplies the one question for that stem, shown as ふる; シン, ふ.れる and ふ.るう are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [仮](仮.md), "temporary; assumed," and [名](名.md), "name," in 振り仮名, and [舞](舞.md), "dance," in 振舞う. 振る is 振 with okurigana only.
- [唇](唇.md), "lips," has the same 辰 shape at the top, and [拝](拝.md), "worship; bow in respect," and [掘](掘.md), "dig; excavate," have the same 扌 at the left. All are different kanji. [農](農.md), "agriculture; farming," also has 辰, at the bottom.
- [震](震.md), "quake; tremble," overlaps the shaking senses of 振る and 振れる and writes 震う; [投](投.md), "throw," [捨](捨.md), "discard; abandon," and [配](配.md), "distribute," overlap the senses "to throw (dice)," "to abandon; to give up" and "to allocate (work)" of 振る; [栄](栄.md), "flourish; glory," matches "to flourish; to prosper; to thrive," a sense of 振るう; and [奨](奨.md), "encourage; exhort; promote," matches 振興. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "shake" or "swing" as exclusive to any one character.
- Waving a hand or a flag is 振る, "to wave." A wave of water is a different idea, written 波 なみ, "wave; billow; ripple." English "wave" covers both ideas, so [波](波.md), "wave; billow," remains an ordinary wrong option for 振.
- [揺](揺.md), "shake; sway; waver," shares shaking: JMdict glosses 揺する "to shake; to jolt; to rock (cradle); to swing" and lists 揺さ振る as a rarely used kanji form of 揺さぶる. The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- [揮](揮.md), "wield; command; display," writes 揮う, which JMdict lists as a spelling of 振るう, "to swing; to wield (physically); to exert." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
