# 会 — meet

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

会 is the simplified form of the old character 會. The old form is commonly explained as a lid fitted over a vessel with its contents, the idea of things coming together.
The new form is much simplified (人 over 云), and these parts are not meaningful in the same way, so a story such as "people (人) say (云) something when they meet" is a mnemonic only.
The core meaning is "meet, gather", and from that "a meeting, an association" (as in 会社 and 会議).

## Notes

会う (meet a person) is different from 合う (あう, to match, fit): 友達に会う but サイズが合う.
Look-alikes: 全 (ぜん, all) and 合 (ごう, fit), which have 人 on top too; 会 has 云 below with a small hook.
Special reading: エ is used only in a few words such as 会釈 and 一期一会.
