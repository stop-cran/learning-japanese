# 往 — going; past

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

往 is going away from where you are, and by extension time that has gone by. [往復](../words/往復.md) おうふく is a round trip, going and coming back, and also a return ticket. JMdict glosses it "making a round trip; going and returning; coming and going," and in later senses "correspondence; exchanging (of letters)" and "keeping company (with); socializing; visiting each other."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for オウ. One example of each:

- setting out and going: 往来 おうらい, "coming and going; traffic";
- time going by, the past: 往年 おうねん, "years gone by; earlier years; former years; the past";
- from now on, the time ahead: 以往 いおう, "after; ... and later," which JMdict marks formal or literary; JMdict also gives it the opposite sense "before; prior to";
- now and then: 往往 おうおう, "sometimes; often," which JMdict spells 往々 first.

It gives the kun readings ゆく and いぬ. いぬ is not a KANJIDIC2 reading; JMdict's 往ぬ いぬ, "to leave; to go home," is marked Kansai dialect, and its other senses are archaic. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 彳 at the left, a narrow shape of three strokes (it has no card here), and [主](主.md), "master, principal," at the right. Imagine the master of the house setting off down the road; once he has gone, his visit belongs to the past. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 彳 or 主 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| オウ | on | [往復](../words/往復.md) おうふく — round trip; return ticket |
| い.く | kun | 往く いく - to go; to move (towards); to head (towards), a rarely used kanji form; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| いにしえ | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict spells いにしえ, "antiquity; ancient times; bygone days; former times," 古 and lists 往古 as a rarely used kanji form of the whole word, not a spelling with 往 alone |
| さき.に | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; no JMdict entry writes さきに with 往 |
| ゆ.く | kun | 往く ゆく - to go; to move (towards); to head (towards), a rarely used kanji form; 往き ゆき - the way there; outbound leg; outbound trip; departing leg; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 往復, 往 supplies オウ and 復 supplies フク, with no sound change. 往く is one entry with two readings, いく and ゆく: 往 writes い or ゆ, and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict's usual spelling of the word is 行く and it lists 往く as a rarely used kanji form. In 往き, 往 writes ゆ and き is okurigana; JMdict also reads it いき, and its first spelling is 行き. JMdict marks いにしえ as a poetical term. The same spelling 往古 also has a separate entry read おうこ, "ancient times," in which 往 supplies オウ and 古 supplies コ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only オウ, with 往復, 往来 and 既往症 きおうしょう, "illness that one had in the past; previous illness; medical history," as examples. い.く, いにしえ, さき.に and ゆ.く are not in the official table. Practice selects オウ, the reading with a linked word article; い.く and ゆ.く are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words, and いにしえ and さき.に have no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [復](復.md), "restore; repeat," in 往復.
- [住](住.md), "reside, dwell," and [注](注.md), "pour, direct attention," have the same 主 at the right, with a different shape at the left. [待](待.md), "wait, await," has the same 彳 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [行](行.md), "go," [出](出.md), "go out, exit," [去](去.md), "leave, elapse," and [旅](旅.md), "journey, travel," overlap the senses "going" and "making a round trip" that 往 and 往復 carry; 行 shares the readings い.く and ゆ.く and is the usual spelling of 行く. [昔](昔.md), "old times," [旧](旧.md), "former; old," and [古](古.md), "old," overlap the sense "past"; 古 is the usual spelling of いにしえ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "go" or "past" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the オウ senses: 往還 おうかん, "traffic; coming and going; highway," 往生 おうじょう, "passing on to the next life," a Buddhism term, 往診 おうしん, "doctor's visit; house call," 往路 おうろ, "outward journey," 勇往 ゆうおう, "spirited advance; energetically going forward," and 右往左往 うおうさおう, "moving about in confusion; going every which way; going this way and that"; and 往古, 往時 おうじ, "the past; old times; bygone times; days of yore," 往昔 おうせき, "ancient times," and 既往 きおう, "the past," which JMdict marks formal or literary.
