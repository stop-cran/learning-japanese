# 坑 — pit; mine

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

坑 means a pit or hole, especially one associated with mining. In [坑道](../words/坑道.md) (こうどう), 道 supplies the idea of a passage and 坑 identifies the mining context. JMdict also records 坑 as a noun meaning a pit, especially a mine (entry 2414450).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a cut in the ground beside 土. This is a memory aid only, not a claim about the character's history.
