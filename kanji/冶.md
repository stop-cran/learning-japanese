# 冶 — smelt; work metals

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 冫 (the two left-hand strokes, no. 15) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

冶 concerns smelting and working metals. Learn it through [冶金](../words/冶金.md)
(やきん), metallurgy: the science and technology of obtaining and working metals.
Not every use is literal metalworking: 陶冶 (とうや) can mean cultivation or training,
for example of character or abilities.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the two cold 冫 marks beside a [台](台.md)-shaped
work stand where metal is being shaped. This is an invented memory aid, not a
historical derivation or a verified explanation of the sound.

## Notes

Compare [治](治.md), "govern; heal": its left side has three water strokes, 氵,
rather than 冶's two strokes, 冫. [始](始.md), "begin," has [女](女.md) on the left.
Sharing the 台-shaped right side does not make these characters interchangeable.

鍛冶 (かじ), "blacksmithing; blacksmith," has a special whole-word reading.
Do not extract ジ from its final kana and add it to 冶's on readings.
The dictionaries also document the separate reading たんや for 鍛冶.

For general melting or dissolving, compare [溶](溶.md) and
[溶かす](../words/溶かす.md) (とかす). 冶 is not the ordinary spelling
to substitute into a sentence about melting butter or dissolving sugar.
