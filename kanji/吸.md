# 吸 — inhale; suck in

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

吸 directs attention inward: drawing in air, sucking in liquid, or absorbing something. Start with [吸う](../words/吸う.md) すう in 息を吸う (いきをすう), "breathe in."
呼吸 こきゅう names breathing as a whole, rather than only inhalation.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the small 口 is an opening drawing something toward the 及 shape beside it. The image is for remembering the character, not an etymology.

## Notes

Keep the direction clear: 息を吸う (いきをすう) is "breathe in"; 息を吐く (いきをはく) is "breathe out."
[吹](吹.md) also has 口 at the left, but means blowing, not drawing air inward. Compare the right-hand shapes in 吸・吹・[呼](呼.md).
