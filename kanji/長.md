# 長 — long, chief

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 長 (no. 168, a key in its own right) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

長 is commonly explained as a pictograph of an elder with long hair leaning on a staff. The picture produces two meanings:
"long" (hair, then lengths in space and time) and "elder, head, leader" (the senior person). Don't over-read the modern parts;
they are best learned as a whole.

## Notes

As a suffix チョウ means "head of": 部長 ぶちょう, 校長, 社長. The opposite of the "long" sense is 短 たん (みじかい, short), not yet a card here.
