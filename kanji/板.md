# 板 — plank; board

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

板 concerns a board, plank, or flat plate of material. 板 いた can name a wooden board or a pane of glass, and 黒板 こくばん is a blackboard. The character is not restricted to wood.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside 反 and imagine turning a wooden board over. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not etymology.

## Notes

- JMdict lists 板木 はんぎ alongside 版木 はんぎ for a printing woodblock. This gives a concrete recognition example of ハン; the spelling list alone does not rank their frequency.
- The native noun 板 is いた. Learn バン in 黒板 and ハン in 板木 as parts of complete words, not interchangeable pronunciations of every board.
- Compare [坂](坂.md), slope: the left-hand form changes from 木 to 土.
