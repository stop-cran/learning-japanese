# 城 — castle

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

城 names a castle. 城 しろ is the standalone castle word; 古城 こじょう is an old castle. The additional compound 傾城 けいせい has lexical meanings concerning a beauty or courtesan, not simply an old or leaning castle.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 土 as a castle's ground and 成 as the enclosing defensive strokes beside it.

## Notes

- The セイ row documents the reading in 傾城, a complete lexical item. It is not a direction to pronounce the standalone word for castle せい. The courtesan sense is historical: Nipponica notes that the term largely fell out of use from the Meiji period onward.
- Compare [成](成.md), become, and [域](域.md), region or range. Similar right-hand strokes do not make the characters interchangeable.
- The modern-shape mnemonic does not verify the historical role of 成.
