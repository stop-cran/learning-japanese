# 瞬 — blink; instant

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

瞬 is a blink of the eye, and so a very short span of time. [一瞬](../words/一瞬.md) いっしゅん is "instant; moment; for an instant," and [瞬間](../words/瞬間.md) しゅんかん is "moment; instant; second."

The Daijisen character entry gives シュン as a reading meaning to blink, and also such a very short time, as in 瞬間, 瞬時 and 一瞬. It also lists またたく, "to blink," as the kun reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [目](目.md), "eye," at the left. Imagine that eye blinking, and a moment passing in that blink. The right-hand part is a separate shape with no card here. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim about how 瞬 was formed. Historical formation is not established here.

| まじろ.ぐ | kun | 瞬ぐ まじろぐ — "to wink; to blink; to twinkle; to flicker"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 一瞬, 一 supplies イチ, and いっ is that word's sokuon form, the small っ that doubles the consonant before シュン; 瞬 supplies シュン. In 瞬間, 瞬 supplies シュン and 間 supplies カン. In 瞬く, 瞬 writes またた and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict gives 瞬く the readings またたく, まばたく and まだたく. A second entry, 瞬く めたたく, is marked archaic and a third, 瞬く しばたたく, is marked "word usually written using kana alone." Kanjipedia also lists まばた.く, しばた.く and しばたた.く for 瞬; they appear as readings of those JMdict words, with no word article here.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シュン, with 瞬間, 瞬時 しゅんじ, "instant; moment; (split) second; (in a) flash; (in the) blink of an eye," and 一瞬, and またたく, with 瞬く and 瞬き またたき, "blink (of eyes); wink." Practice selects シュン, the reading of the two linked word articles. またた.く and まじろ.ぐ are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [一](一.md), "one," in 一瞬 and [間](間.md), "interval, between," in 瞬間. The 瞬間 article contrasts 瞬間 with 時 and 秒, and 間 is in the quiz exclusions because "interval" is close to "moment."
- [目](目.md), "eye," is the radical of 瞬 and is excluded because a blink is an eye movement. [刻](刻.md), "carve; time," [時](時.md), "time, hour," [間](間.md) and [秒](秒.md), "second as a time unit," overlap the senses "time," "moment" and "second" that 瞬 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [親](親.md), "parent; close," [験](験.md), "test; verify," and [題](題.md), "topic; question," are crowded, many-stroke kanji, as 瞬 is, so they look plausible. They are read シン and おや; ケン and ゲン; and ダイ, and mean parent, test and topic. None shares a reading or a meaning with 瞬.
- [旬](旬.md), [春](春.md) and [隼](隼.md) share the reading シュン with 瞬, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
- 瞬 and the B147 card 瞳 both involve the eye: 瞬 is the blink and 瞳 is the pupil. Because 瞬 also means "to blink (one's eyes)" and 瞳 is titled "pupil of the eye; eyes," 瞳 is in this card's quiz exclusions and is not offered as a wrong answer.
