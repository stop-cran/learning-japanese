# 係 — connection; person in charge

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

係 appears in [関係](../words/関係.md) かんけい, a relationship or connection.
As the word 係 かかり, it can identify a duty or the person responsible for it.
These uses help connect involvement with having an assigned role, without making the words interchangeable.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the person on the left holding the thread-like lines on the right, keeping connections organized.
This is a visual learning story, not a verified historical analysis.

## Notes

係 can stand as a noun: 係の人 (かかりのひと), "the person in charge."
Its 亻 is the left-side form of radical 人.
Compare [休](休.md), [体](体.md), and [作](作.md): the shared person component is not enough to identify the character.
Preserve the distinction between the on reading ケイ in 関係 and the independent word かかり.
