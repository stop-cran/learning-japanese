# 世 — world, generation

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

世 concerns the world people live in, society, or a generation and its times. [世界](../words/世界.md) means "world";
世の中 often concerns society or life among people rather than the physical planet.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the joined upright strokes as successive generations connected along one line.
This is a visual memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.
