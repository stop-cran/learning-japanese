# 墾 — clear land for farming

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

墾 means to open up ground for cultivation. It is more specific than simply working the soil.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 土 below the rest of the character; picture new farmland being cleared.

## Notes

[耕](耕.md) refers to tilling. Already established fields can be tilled without clearing any new land.

Look below the busy top of 墾 for 土; [既](既.md) (already) has no such base. Compare [壁](壁.md), with 土 at the bottom, and [望](望.md), with 王 at the bottom. [培](培.md) is cultivating or fostering growth; 墾 is about bringing new land into use.
