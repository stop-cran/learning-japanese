# 悲 — sadness

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

悲 expresses sadness and grieving. [悲しい](../words/悲しい.md) かなしい describes feeling sad;
悲しむ かなしむ is a verb for grieving or mourning.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the opposed strokes of [非](非.md) above [心](心.md), and imagine
a heavy feeling pressing down on the heart. This is a visual memory aid, not an etymological claim.

## Notes

[想](想.md), [息](息.md), and [怒](怒.md) all have 心 underneath.
Distinguish their upper components instead of guessing the meaning from the lower heart alone.
悲しい is an adjective; 悲しむ is a verb, so their endings behave differently.
