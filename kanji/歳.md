# 歳 — years of age

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 止 (no. 77) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

For learners, a central use of 歳 is stating age. [歳](../words/歳.md) (さい) follows a number:
五歳 (ごさい), "five years old."
It labels years of age, not simply a calendar year.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the short strokes below 止 as a tally of years crossed by the long slanting stroke.
This is an invented counting picture, not an origin claim.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| サイ | on | [歳](../words/歳.md) さい — years old; 何歳 なんさい — how old |

The remaining source readings are retained in metadata.
二十歳 (はたち), "twenty years old," is learned as a whole expression, not as a reading はたち of 歳 alone.

## Common words

- [歳](../words/歳.md) さい — age counter
- 何歳 なんさい — how old; what age
- 二十歳 はたち — twenty years old

## Notes

[歩](歩.md) also has 止 above a lower part, but its lower strokes are simpler.
For an age answer, use the complete number-plus-counter expression: 五歳です (ごさいです), "I am five."
Use 歳 (さい), not [年](年.md) (ねん), after the number when stating an age.
