# 畳 — tatami; fold

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

畳 writes the noun たたみ, a tatami mat, and the verb たたむ, fold. With the on reading ジョウ it also counts tatami mats or expresses room size in tatami units. Do not read a room-size count as the independent noun たたみ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the upper box, the cover-like middle and the layered lower strokes as mats stacked after folding. This describes a memory picture of the modern shape.

## Notes

- 六畳の部屋です。 (ろくじょうのへやです。) - It is a six-tatami room. 六畳 describes the room in tatami units; it does not establish one universal area because actual mat dimensions vary.
- 畳む is transitive godan: 服を畳む (ふくをたたむ), fold clothes; 畳みます たたみます and 畳んで たたんで. JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana.
- The linked [畳](../words/畳.md) article teaches the mat noun たたみ, not the counter じょう. Neither the folding verb nor the mat noun licenses a new pronunciation for every compound containing this character.
