# 師 — teacher; specialist

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

師 points to a teacher, master, or practitioner with a particular skill.
Compare [教師](../words/教師.md) きょうし, a teacher, with 医師 いし, a physician.
These name different professions; the shared character does not mean that every specialist teaches a class.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** look for 巾 hanging below a short horizontal stroke on the right, beside the stacked left-hand shape.
Imagine a specialist standing beside a teaching chart. This is a visual aid, not verified etymology.

## Notes

[先生](../words/先生.md) せんせい can be a respectful form of address as well as a word for a teacher.
教師 identifies the occupation; the linked article explains why the labels serve different purposes.
[帰](帰.md) has a related-looking left side but a different right side.
[市](市.md) and [姉](姉.md) provide other 巾-containing shapes to distinguish from 師.
