# 恋 — romantic love; longing

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

恋 concerns romantic love or longing. 恋 こい is romantic love, while [恋しい](../words/恋しい.md) こいしい describes someone or something longed for or missed. The adjective's object of longing need not be a romantic partner.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 亦 above 心 and imagine a feeling held in the heart beneath the upper strokes. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not an account of the older character's formation.

## Notes

- 恋う こう is a literary verb meaning to love or miss someone or something. JMdict classifies it as a special-class godan verb ending in う; its special て and past forms are 恋うて こうて and 恋うた こうた. The noun 恋 こい is a different grammatical form.
- The linked 恋 article teaches the noun こい. 恋人 こいびと names a romantic partner, not every person one cares about.
- Compare [変](変.md): its lower component differs from 恋's 心.
