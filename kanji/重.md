# 重 — heavy; piled up

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 里 (no. 166) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

重 can indicate **weight**, **importance**, or **things layered on one another**.
A heavy bag and overlapping events use different words even though both contain 重.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the horizontal strokes as layers stacked on a central support,
making a heavy pile. This is a mnemonic for the modern shape, not verified history.

## Common words

- [重い](../words/重い.md) おもい — heavy; serious
- 体重 たいじゅう — body weight
- 重要 じゅうよう — important
- 重ねる かさねる — put one on top of another; repeat

## Notes

The weight opposite of 重い is 軽い（かるい）, "light." Do not substitute 重い for every English use
of "important": 重要な（じゅうような）is useful for importance without a sense of burden.

[車](車.md) also has a central vertical stroke and several horizontal strokes; check the top and bottom.
[動](動.md) contains the shape 重 with [力](力.md) on the right.
