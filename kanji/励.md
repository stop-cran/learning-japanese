# 励 — encourage; be diligent

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

励 covers both encouraging someone and diligently applying oneself. [励む](../words/励む.md) はげむ means "work hard; strive," while [励ます](../words/励ます.md) はげます means "encourage; cheer someone on." [激励](../words/激励.md) げきれい is encouragement that spurs someone on.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice a 厂-like cover over [万](万.md)-shaped strokes, beside [力](力.md), "strength." Imagine putting strength into effort or encouragement. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical derivation; the 万-like shape does not contribute "ten thousand" to this meaning.

All three sourced readings are retained in the practice selections. The app groups kun-readings with the same stem before the dot, so はげ.む supplies the はげむ question; はげ.ます remains in the reading table but does not create a separate question. The on-reading question is れい. In 激励, 激 supplies ゲキ and 励 supplies レイ.

励ます is a plain dictionary form, despite ending in ます. Its polite form is 励まします (はげまします). The polite form of the separate verb 励む is 励みます (はげみます).

## Notes

- [劣](劣.md), "inferior; worse," has 力 below [少](少.md) rather than at the right. Making an effort does not itself mean being inferior.
- [万](万.md), "ten thousand," resembles part of the left side, but lacks the cover and right-hand 力 of 励.
- [厚](厚.md), "thick; cordial," also has a 厂 overhang, but a different interior and no right-hand 力.
- [努](努.md), [勉](勉.md) and [勤](勤.md) share effort or diligence meanings. They are useful comparisons, not clearly wrong alternatives to that side of 励's meaning.
- Distinguish the roles: 勉強に励む (べんきょうにはげむ), "work hard at one's studies," versus 友達を励ます (ともだちをはげます), "encourage a friend." Neither effort nor encouragement guarantees success.
