# 宅 — residence

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宅 points to a residence: a place where someone lives. [自宅](../words/自宅.md) (じたく) is one's own home, while 住宅 (じゅうたく) describes a residential building or housing.
"Home" here need not mean a detached house; an apartment can be someone's 自宅.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** treat the hooked lower stroke beneath 宀 as a marker showing which roof is someone's residence.
This is a visual aid, not a historical account.
