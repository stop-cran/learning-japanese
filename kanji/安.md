# 安 — cheap, peaceful

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (roof, no. 40) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

安 is 宀 (roof) over [女](女.md) (woman): the traditional explanation is a woman safely indoors, hence "peace" and "calm". Modern scholars debate the original image,
so take it as a mnemonic. The core meanings are "peaceful, safe, at ease", and the sense "cheap" (low price, "easy on the purse") developed from there.

## Notes

Opposite of "cheap" is [高い](../words/高い.md) ([高](高.md)). Opposite of "peaceful" is 危ない あぶない (dangerous), as in 不安 and 危険 きけん.
やすい also appears as a suffix, as in 分かりやすい (easy to understand), which is normally written in kana (its kanji is the different 易い, not 安). Compare 休む ([休](休.md)), which shares the sound やす but has an unrelated kanji.
