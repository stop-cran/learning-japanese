# 身 — one's body; oneself

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 身 (no. 158) · **JLPT:** N3 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

身 concerns **one's body or person**, and by extension oneself. The native noun [身](../words/身.md) み can also name the main substance of something: 魚の身 (さかなのみ) is fish flesh as opposed to bones or skin.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a body seen from the side, with its outline and lower strokes. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical reconstruction.

## Notes

- [体](体.md) also means body. 身 additionally has personal and substantial-part uses; this is overlap, not an absolute division between physical and abstract meanings.
- 身体 is also read からだ. That whole-word reading does not give 身 a separate reading から; the linked article distinguishes it from しんたい.
- Do not confuse 身 with [目](目.md), eye, or [耳](耳.md), ear. Follow the seven-stroke reference rather than reducing the shape to a box.
