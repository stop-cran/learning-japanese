# 砕 — smash; crush; break into pieces

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

砕 is breaking something into small pieces. [砕く](../words/砕く.md) くだく is the transitive verb: JMdict's first sense is "to break (into pieces); to smash; to crush." [砕ける](../words/砕ける.md) くだける is the intransitive verb: "to break (into pieces); to be broken; to be smashed." Both words have figurative senses that are not the core meaning. JMdict also gives 砕く "to simplify; to make easy to understand," and 砕ける "to become less formal; to throw off reserve; to become affable."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for サイ: to break, as in 砕石 さいせき, "crushed stone; macadam," 玉砕 ぎょくさい, "honorable death; death without surrender; honorable defeat," and 粉砕 ふんさい, "pulverization; pulverisation"; and fine or small, as in 零砕 れいさい, "a tiny bit," marked "rare term." It lists the kun readings くだく and くだける.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [石](石.md), "stone," at the left. The part at the right looks like [九](九.md), "nine," above [十](十.md), "ten." Imagine a stone breaking into many small pieces. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 砕く, 砕 writes くだ and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 砕ける, 砕 writes くだ and ける is the okurigana. Practice selects くだ.く as the word's own reading in 砕く. In 粉砕, 粉 supplies フン and 砕 supplies サイ; in 砕石, 砕 supplies サイ and 石 supplies セキ; in 玉砕, 玉 supplies ギョク and 砕 supplies サイ. JMdict lists 摧く as a "rarely used kanji form" of 砕く, and 摧ける beside 砕ける with no tag.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows サイ, with 砕石, 砕氷 さいひょう, "ice breaking," and 粉砕; くだく, with 砕く; and くだける, with 砕ける. It also prints a variant form of 砕 in brackets, which this card does not teach. Practice selects くだ.く, the reading of the linked word articles. サイ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The words 砕く and 砕ける are 砕 with okurigana only, so they have no other kanji.
- [壊](壊.md), "break; destroy; demolish," [破](破.md), "tear; break," [割](割.md), "split; proportion," [崩](崩.md), "crumble; collapse; demolish," [倒](倒.md), "topple; collapse," [滅](滅.md), "perish; destroy; die out," [粉](粉.md), "flour; powder," [細](細.md), "slender; fine; detailed," and [磨](磨.md), "polish; grind," overlap the senses "break," "crumble," "destroy," "powder," "fine" and "grind" that 砕 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [究](究.md), "investigate thoroughly," [十](十.md), "ten," and [砂](砂.md), "sand," share parts or a stone image with 砕, so they look plausible: 究 has a 九 shape, 十 is the lower part at the right, and 砂 has 石 at the left. They are read キュウ, ク and きわ.める; ジュウ, ジッ and とお; and サ, シャ and すな, and mean investigate, ten and sand. None shares a reading or a meaning with 砕.
- [催](催.md), [再](再.md), [細](細.md) and many other kanji read サイ, like 砕, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
