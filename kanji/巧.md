# 巧 — skillful; ingenious

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 工 (no. 48) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

巧 concerns skill or ingenuity. 巧み (たくみ) can describe skill in doing something; 巧い (うまい) can praise a performance.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 工 at the left and the bent, hooked stroke at the right. Imagine a craftsperson making that bend precisely.

## Notes

[功](功.md) puts 力 to the right of 工; [攻](攻.md) has a different right part and [打](打.md) starts with a hand. [技](技.md), [伎](伎.md), [熟](熟.md), [腕](腕.md), [匠](匠.md) and [工](工.md) can suggest skill or craft. Choose the spelling 巧い for skill here, not as the default way to praise a meal.
