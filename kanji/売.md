# 売 — sell

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 士 (no. 33) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

売 concerns selling. [売る](../words/売る.md) describes someone selling something, while 売れる describes something selling or being popular.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper strokes as a shop counter and the two lower legs carrying sold goods away. This describes the modern shape, not its historical origin.

## Notes

The natural counterpart is [買](買.md), "buy": the same transaction seen from the other side. [読](読.md), "read," has a similar right-hand shape, but that resemblance is not a meaning rule.

The classical radical is 士, not [土](土.md). In the upper 士 shape, the top horizontal stroke is longer than the lower one.
