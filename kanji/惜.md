# 惜 — pity; precious; begrudge

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

惜 is not wanting to let something go: finding its loss a pity, holding it dear, and using it sparingly. [惜しい](../words/惜しい.md) おしい describes such a feeling; JMdict glosses it "regrettable; disappointing; unfortunate," "precious; dear; valuable," "too good for; deserving better" and "almost (but not quite); close (but no cigar)."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for セキ: being unable to let go, with the heart lingering, and not wanting to lose something, おしむ. Its examples are 惜春 せきしゅん, "lamenting the passing of spring; lamenting the passing of one's youth"; 惜敗 せきはい, "regrettable defeat; defeat by a narrow margin"; 惜別 せきべつ, "reluctance to part; sorrow at parting"; 哀惜 あいせき, "grief; sorrow"; 愛惜 あいせき, "missing someone; loathing to part," which JMdict also reads あいじゃく; and 痛惜 つうせき, "deep regret." Its kun readings are おしい and おしむ, the same as KANJIDIC2.

For シャク, which KANJIDIC2 does not list, Daijisen gives おしむ, as in 不惜身命 ふしゃくしんみょう, "devoting one's body and soul to Buddhist teachings; unsparing devotion to Buddhism," which JMdict marks as a Buddhist four-character idiom. Daijisen also names 可惜 あたら, "alas; regrettably; to my great regret; tragically," among words with a hard-to-guess reading. JMdict notes that this word is usually written in kana alone, also reads it あったら and also spells it 惜 alone; あたら belongs to the whole word and is not a KANJIDIC2 reading of 惜.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 忄, the narrow form of [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the left, and [昔](昔.md), "old times," at the right. Imagine a heart holding on to the old times, reluctant to let them go. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 忄 or 昔 contributes meaning or sound, although 昔 is read セキ, a reading 惜 also has. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| セキ | on | 惜敗 せきはい - regrettable defeat; defeat by a narrow margin; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| お.しい | kun | [惜しい](../words/惜しい.md) おしい — regrettable; precious; so close |
| お.しむ | kun | 惜しむ おしむ - to spare; to grudge; to be sparing (with); to be frugal; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 惜しい, 惜 writes お and しい is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 惜しむ uses the same お with しむ. JMdict glosses 惜しむ "to spare; to grudge; to be sparing (with); to be frugal; to be stingy," "to regret; to lament," "to be reluctant (to do); to be unwilling," "to value; to prize; to hold dear" and, in an archaic sense, "to love; to cherish." In 負け惜しみ まけおしみ, "being a poor loser; unwillingness to admit defeat; sour grapes," 負 is read ま with okurigana け, and 惜 keeps お before しみ. In 惜敗, 惜 supplies セキ and 敗 supplies ハイ, with no sound change.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セキ, with 惜敗, 痛惜 and 愛惜 as examples; おしい, with 惜しい; and おしむ, with 惜しむ and 負け惜しみ. Practice selects お.しい, the reading with a linked word article. セキ is the reading of most 惜 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; お.しむ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 惜しい is 惜 with okurigana only. JMdict also has 名残惜しい なごりおしい, "reluctant (to part)," and lists 口惜しい, written with 惜, as a rarely used kanji form of 悔しい くやしい, "frustrated (over a failure, humiliation or injustice); annoyed; chagrined."
- [憎](憎.md), "hate; detest," [性](性.md), "nature; gender," and [慣](慣.md), "accustomed," have the same 忄 at the left. All are different kanji. [借](借.md), "borrow, rent," has 昔 at the right and is read シャク, and [籍](籍.md), "register; membership," contains 昔 and is read セキ.
- [哀](哀.md), "sorrow; pity; pathos," [悲](悲.md), "sadness," [値](値.md), "value; price," [宝](宝.md), "treasure; valuables," [倹](倹.md), "frugal; thrifty," and [恨](恨.md), "resent; grudge," overlap the senses "pity," "grief; sorrow," "valuable," "precious," "frugal" and "grudge" that 惜 and its words carry: JMdict glosses 惜しい "precious; dear; valuable" and 惜しむ "to grudge," "to be frugal" and "to value"; 哀 appears with 惜 in 哀惜. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "pity" or "precious" as exclusive to any one character.
- [悔](悔.md), "regret; repent; mourn; frustration," shares the regret of 惜しむ, which JMdict glosses "to regret; to lament," and JMdict lists 口惜しい as a rarely used kanji form of 悔しい. The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- [懐](懐.md), "bosom; nostalgic; become attached," shares "dear": JMdict glosses 惜しい "precious; dear; valuable" and 懐かしい "dear (old); fondly remembered; beloved; missed; nostalgic." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
