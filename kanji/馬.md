# 馬 — horse

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 馬 (no. 187) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

馬 names a horse. The standalone word is [馬](../words/馬.md) うま,
while 乗馬 (じょうば) can name horse riding.
馬に乗りました。 (うまにのりました。) — I rode a horse.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a horse's body above the four short strokes along the bottom.
This is a way of remembering today's shape, not a reconstruction of an early written form.

## Notes

[鳥](鳥.md) and [魚](魚.md) also have short strokes along the bottom;
compare the upper parts rather than guessing the animal from those strokes alone.
