# 州 — state; province

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 巛 (no. 47) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

州 names an administrative region such as a state or province. 州都 しゅうと is a state or provincial capital. The same character also writes the separate native noun す, a sandbank or sandbar; do not transfer that meaning to every geographical name.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the three upright river-like strokes with small marks between them; imagine regions separated by waterways. This is a modern-shape story, not a verified derivation.

The canonical indexing key is 巛, the river radical. Here its shape is the straighter 川-like form, not the isolated 巛 outline.

## Notes

- KANJIDIC2 lists both the on reading ス and the native reading す. The sandbank word demonstrates the native reading; it is not independent evidence for an on-reading word.
- The linked 州 article teaches しゅう, not the sandbank noun. 九州 きゅうしゅう names Kyushu; the name is not a count of modern administrative states.
