# 延 — prolong; postpone

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 廴 (no. 54) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

延 concerns extending or prolonging something and putting an event off. 延期 えんき means postponement. In 延びる のびる an event or period is extended or delayed; 延ばす のばす describes someone extending or postponing it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the long sweeping 廴 around the inner upright strokes and imagine stretching a route farther along. This is a modern-shape story, not etymology.

## Notes

- The prefix 延べ のべ gives an aggregate total before a counter or unit: 延べ三日 のべみっか means three days in total. This is sense 4 of the dictionary entry, not its separate futures-trading noun.
- 延びる is intransitive; 延ばす and 延べる are transitive. The entry for 延ばす specifically associates postponement with this spelling, not its hair-growing example.
- The primary source stroke count is eight; an alternative count of seven is also recorded. The card and stroke drawing use the primary count.
