# 予 — beforehand

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 亅 (no. 6) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

予 points ahead of an event: something is arranged or thought about before it happens.
[予定](../words/予定.md) よてい concerns a plan or schedule, while [予想](../words/予想.md) よそう concerns a prediction.
Planning and predicting are related ways of looking ahead, not the same activity.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the lower hook holding a place for an event that has not arrived yet.
This use of the visible hook is a memory aid, not etymology.

## Notes

The lower hook is useful for distinguishing the outline from [子](子.md), [手](手.md), and [与](与.md),
though these are only modest visual resemblances.
Learn ヨ inside the two words above rather than reading an isolated 予 as an ordinary word for "before."
Other source readings are retained in metadata without being presented as interchangeable choices.
