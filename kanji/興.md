# 興 — revival; interest; pleasure

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 臼 (no. 134) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

興 has two useful on-reading patterns. コウ is associated with rising, starting or reviving something, as in 復興 (ふっこう), “revival,” and 興奮 (こうふん), “excitement.” キョウ is associated with interest, pleasure or amusement, as in 興味 (きょうみ), “interest; curiosity.” The meanings are related through a sense of activity or engagement, but the readings are not interchangeable.

## Notes

The selected quiz reading is **キョウ**: in the whole compound 興味 (きょうみ), 興 is read キョウ and 味 is read ミ. コウ is not the reading used in this word. The kun readings are verbs: 興る is intransitive (“rise; flourish”), while 興す is transitive (“promote; revive; establish”); distinguish them from the similarly read 起こる and 起こす.

KANJIDIC2 lists the name readings おき・おこっ・とも. Digital Daijisen also gives name readings おき・き・さかん・さき・とも・ふか・ふさ. These are name forms, not additional Jōyō readings or quiz options.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 空 (“sky; empty”), 水 (“water”), and 顔 (“face; expression”). 娯 (“recreation; amusement”) and 楽 (“music, enjoyment, ease”) are excluded because their displayed titles overlap 興’s “pleasure” sense.
