# 宿 — lodging

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宿 is associated with lodging or staying somewhere. 宿 (やど) is an inn or lodging, and 宿泊 (しゅくはく) is accommodation or staying overnight.
Learn [宿題](../words/宿題.md) (しゅくだい), "homework," as a separate whole-word use rather than translating it as "inn question."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 亻-shaped person checking in beside a 百-shaped room marker under the roof.
This is a modern memory scene, not an etymology or a statement about the historical components.

## Notes

Keep the narrow person-shaped portion inside the roof; [百](百.md) alone lacks both that part and the roof.
[客](客.md) is a guest, not the lodging itself, while [字](字.md) has a much simpler interior.
The longer やど.る and やど.す readings remain in metadata but are not needed for the words selected here.
