# 峰 — summit; mountain peak

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

峰 points to a mountain summit or ridge. 包丁の峰 (ほうちょうのみね) uses the same word for the back of a kitchen knife, not the sharp edge.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 山 is at the left; follow the strokes down the right-hand side to the vertical line crossing horizontal strokes. Imagine looking up at a summit.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ホウ | on | 高峰 (こうほう), a lofty peak. In 連峰 (れんぽう), a mountain range, the second element is pronounced ぽう. |
| みね | kun | [峰](../words/峰.md) みね — mountain peak; back of a blade |
| ね | kun | Daijisen also gives 峰 (ね), a mountaintop, as in its literary examples. Do not substitute ね for みね in the blade example. |

## Common words

## Notes

[降](降.md) has a different left part and means descend. [岸](岸.md) and [岩](岩.md) place 山 on top rather than at the side. [頂](頂.md), [山](山.md), [岳](岳.md) and [峠](峠.md) also occur in talk about mountains; a pass and a summit are not the same feature.
