# 進 — advance

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

進 describes moving forward or making progress. [進む](../words/進む.md) すすむ can describe a person moving ahead or work progressing;
進める すすめる describes someone moving something forward.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 隹-shaped figure moving forward along 辶.
This visual scene does not identify a historical phonetic component.

## Notes

Compare 仕事が進む (しごとがすすむ), "the work progresses," with 仕事を進める (しごとをすすめる),
"move the work forward." The が/を difference shows whether the work progresses or someone advances it.
[週](週.md), [道](道.md), and [遠](遠.md) share 辶 but not the center.
