# 描 — draw; depict; sketch

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

描 is putting the shape of something onto paper, as a drawing or a sketch, and, by extension, depicting it in words or picturing it in the mind. [描く](../words/描く.md) えがく is to draw or to depict. JMdict glosses it "to draw; to paint; to sketch," then "to depict; to describe," a sense it limits to the reading えがく, then "to picture in one's mind; to imagine," and "to form a certain shape (e.g. path of an action, appearance of an object, etc.)."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ビョウ: copying the shape or state of a thing, drawing it. Its examples include 描写 びょうしゃ, "depiction; description; portrayal," 素描 そびょう, "drawing; sketch," 点描 てんびょう, "sketching," also "pointillism; stippling," and 線描 せんびょう, "line drawing." It lists the kun readings えがく and かく, the same as KANJIDIC2. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the left, written 扌, and [苗](苗.md), "seedling; young plant," at the right. Imagine a hand with a pencil drawing young seedlings in a field. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 扌 or 苗 contributes meaning or sound, although 苗 is also read ビョウ, a plain fact about readings. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ビョウ | on | 描写 びょうしゃ - depiction; description; portrayal; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| えが.く | kun | [描く](../words/描く.md) えがく — draw; depict |
| か.く | kun | 絵描き えかき - painter; artist, marked familiar language, also "drawing pictures; doodling; painting"; JMdict lists 絵かき first; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 描く, 描 writes えが and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same JMdict entry also gives the reading かく, with 画く as a second spelling; there 描 writes か with く, and the word article explains both readings, but its headword reading is えがく, so it supports えが.く here. In 描き出す えがきだす, "to delineate; to draw; to express; to imagine," 描 writes えが with き before 出す. In 絵描き, 絵 supplies え and 描 writes か with き after it. JMdict also lists 絵描, 絵書き, marked as a rarely used kanji form, and 画描き, marked as a search-only kanji form. In 描写, 描 supplies ビョウ and 写 supplies シャ; JMdict marks the spelling 描寫 as a word containing out-dated kanji. 素描 is read そびょう; the separate JMdict entry すがき, "uncolored sketch; line sketch; rough sketch," also lists 素描 among its spellings, and there 描 is not read ビョウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ビョウ, with 描写, 素描 and 点描 as examples; えがく, with 描く and 描き出す; and かく, with 描く and 絵描き and the spelling contrast ⇔ 書く かく, "to write; to compose; to pen." Practice selects えが.く, the reading of the linked word article. No word article uses ビョウ or か.く yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 描く is 描 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji.
- [押](押.md), "push," [握](握.md), "grip; grasp," and [掘](掘.md), "dig; excavate," also have 扌 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [引](引.md), "pull; draw," [絵](絵.md), "drawing; painting," [画](画.md), "picture, brushstroke," [写](写.md), "copy, photograph," [塗](塗.md), "paint; coat," and [書](書.md), "write," overlap the senses of 描 and its words: drawing, painting and pictures in 描く, depicting in 描写, and writing in 書く, the spelling the Joyo table contrasts with 描く かく; 画 is also read えが.く, and 引 writes 引く, which JMdict glosses in one sense "to draw (a line, plan, etc.)." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "draw" or "paint" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ビョウ sense: 描画 びょうが, "drawing; painting," 描出 びょうしゅつ, "description; rendition," 描破 びょうは, "depicting thoroughly," 寸描 すんびょう, "thumbnail sketch; brief sketch," and 白描 はくびょう, "plain sketch."
