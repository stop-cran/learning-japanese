# 礼 — bow; thanks

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 示 (as 礻, no. 113) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

礼 belongs to the language of respect: a bow, good manners, or an expression of gratitude.
[御礼](../words/御礼.md) おれい means thanks; the same word can be written お礼.
The polite wording does not make 礼 itself an honorific prefix.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** beside 礻, imagine the bent stroke on the right as a person bending in a bow.
This is only a memory picture for the modern form.

## Common words

- 礼 れい — bow; manners; thanks
- 失礼 しつれい — discourtesy; excuse me

## Notes

礼をする (れいをする) means "make a bow."
失礼します (しつれいします), "excuse me," is a courtesy expression, not an announcement that one intends to be rude.

[神](神.md), [祖](祖.md), and [福](福.md) also begin with 礻.
Identify 礼 by its much simpler right-hand stroke rather than treating the whole group as one meaning.
