# 田 — rice field

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

田 is a pictograph of a field divided into plots by banks or paths, a rice paddy. It is also its own radical, and appears in
畑 はたけ (dry field, a Japanese-made kanji), 町 (town), 男 ([男](男.md), man: 田 plus 力, the power to work the fields) and 留.

| た | kun | 田 た, 田んぼ たんぼ, [田中](../words/田中.md) たなか, 田植え たうえ |

## Common words

- [田中](../words/田中.md) たなか — Tanaka (surname, "in the field")
- 田んぼ たんぼ — rice paddy
- 田舎 いなか — countryside (a special reading)

## Notes

- Look-alikes: 由 (reason), 甲 (the first of the ten stems), 申 (say, monkey sign) extend the vertical stroke below, above or both; 田 is a closed box with a cross and nothing sticking out. Also [日](日.md) (sun) and [口](口.md) (mouth) are simpler boxes.
- In names た often voices to だ: 山田 やまだ, 前田 まえだ, 吉田 よしだ. 田 as the surname element -だ (山田, 本田) is not a separate reading; it is the same た with voicing in names.
