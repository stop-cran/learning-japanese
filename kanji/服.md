# 服 — clothing

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 月 (no. 74) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

服 is central to words for clothes. In other vocabulary it also relates to taking medicine or obeying; do not translate it as "clothing" in every compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the left-hand [月](月.md) as a folded garment and the right-hand strokes as a hand arranging it. This is a visual memory aid, not verified history.

## Notes

A single-kanji word need not use a kun reading: 服 ふく uses the on reading.
Compare [朝](朝.md), which has 月 on the right, not the left. For the action of putting on clothes, learn [着](着.md) in [着る](../words/着る.md) きる.
