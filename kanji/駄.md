# 駄 — packhorse; horse load; worthless

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 馬 (no. 187), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

駄 is a horse that carries loads and the load it carries; as a prefix it marks something cheap or of little value. It also appears in names of traditional footwear. [下駄](../words/下駄.md) げた is a traditional Japanese wooden sandal, and [無駄](../words/無駄.md) むだ is waste, futility or pointlessness.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ダ. One example of each:

- loading a horse, or its load: 駄賃 だちん, which JMdict glosses "reward; recompense; tip" and "conveyance charge; packhorse charge";
- the packhorse itself: 駄馬 だば, "packhorse; workhorse; hack";
- something of low value: 駄作 ださく, "poor piece of work; rubbish; trash," besides 無駄.

For タ it gives footwear, as in 下駄 and 雪駄 せった, "leather-soled sandals (geta)." A separate entry for the word だ uses it as a prefix for worthless or cheap things, as in 駄菓子 だがし, "penny candy; cheap individually wrapped sweets," and as a counter for horse loads, as in 一駄 いちだ, "horse load." The character entry also lists the kun reading においうま, which KANJIDIC2 does not list and no JMdict entry has. More Daijisen examples of the ダ senses are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [馬](馬.md), "horse," at the left, and [太](太.md), "thick; plump," at the right. Imagine a plump horse loaded so heavily that it can barely walk. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 馬 or 太 contributes meaning or sound, although 太 is read タ, a reading 駄 also has. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ダ | on | [無駄](../words/無駄.md) むだ — waste; futility; pointlessness (an ateji spelling, not counted for this reading); 駄目 だめ - no good; not serving its purpose; useless; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| タ | on | [下駄](../words/下駄.md) げた — wooden sandal |

In 下駄, 下 supplies ゲ and 駄 supplies タ, unvoiced: げた uses the second on reading タ, which the Daijisen character entry gives for footwear words, not ダ with a sound change. Rendaku, the voicing of a sound at the start of a later element, would go the other way, from た to だ, and does not happen here. 雪駄 せった also has タ, after 雪 shortened to せっ.

無駄 is different: JMdict marks the spelling as ateji, kanji chosen to write the native word むだ for their sounds, so the whole word is read むだ. Its だ matches ダ, and the Daijisen and the official table list 無駄 under ダ, but an ateji spelling is not counted here as a word in which 駄 supplies a reading of its own. In 駄目, 駄 supplies ダ and 目 supplies め; JMdict marks 駄目 as usually written in kana alone.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ダ, with 駄菓子, 駄作 and 無駄 as examples. タ is not in the official table. Practice selects タ, the reading 駄 supplies in the linked article 下駄; ダ is the main reading, but its only linked word, 無駄, is an ateji spelling, and 駄目 has no word article yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [下](下.md), "down, below," in 下駄 and 無 in 無駄, which has no card yet.
- [太](太.md), "thick; plump," is the shape at the right of 駄 and is also read タ. [駅](駅.md), "station," has the same 馬 at the left with a different shape beside it. [打](打.md), "strike," is read ダ. All are different kanji.
- [馬](馬.md), "horse," [荷](荷.md), "load; baggage; burden," [劣](劣.md), "inferior; worse," and [履](履.md), "footwear; put on shoes; carry out," overlap the senses "packhorse," "load," "of low value" and "footwear" that 駄 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "load" or "worthless" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ダ senses: 荷駄 にだ, "pack; horseload"; and 駄犬 だけん, "mongrel; cur; mutt," and 駄文 だぶん, "poor piece of writing."
