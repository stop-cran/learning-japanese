# 具 — tools; ingredients

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 八 (the spreading bottom strokes, no. 12) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

具 appears in words for equipment and useful implements, especially [道具](../words/道具.md) どうぐ, "tools."
As the noun 具 ぐ, it can also mean the ingredients or fillings added to food, such as soup.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper divided box holding equipment on a stand with two
spread feet. The equipment picture is a learning device, not an explanation of the character's origin.

## Readings

グ is the focus of these words; the kun readings remain in the metadata without being taught as default alternatives.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| グ | on | [道具](../words/道具.md) どうぐ — tools; 家具 かぐ — furniture; 具 ぐ — ingredients |

## Common words

- [道具](../words/道具.md) どうぐ — tool; implement
- 家具 かぐ — furniture
- 具 ぐ — ingredients added to soup, rice, and similar foods

## Notes

Do not translate every 具 as a tool: soup's 具 are its added ingredients, not the spoon.
[真](真.md) has a similar stacked outline but adds a top cross.
[共](共.md) lacks the upper box, and [目](目.md) lacks the broad base and spreading lower strokes.
