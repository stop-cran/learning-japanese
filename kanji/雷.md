# 雷 — thunder; lightning

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 雨 (no. 173) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

雷 is thunder and lightning. KANJIDIC2 lists “thunder” and “lightning bolt”. Kanjipedia's first sense is かみなり。いかずち。 and Digital Daijisen's first is かみなり。; Kanjipedia also uses 雷 to describe things like thunder (かみなりのようなものの形容) and for a weapon rigged to explode (爆発するしかけの兵器。), as in 地雷 じらい, “land mine” (JMdict 1421520). On its own, [雷](../words/雷.md) かみなり is glossed in JMdict “lightning; thunder; thunderbolt”, “god of thunder; god of lightning” and “anger; fit of anger” (entry 1585060).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [雨](雨.md), "rain," sits over [田](田.md), "rice field." Picture a summer storm: rain pours down over the rice fields while thunder rolls and lightning flashes. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 雨 or 田 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ライ, with 雷雨 among its examples, and かみなり. Kanjipedia lists いかずち outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen also gives いかずち as a kun reading.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 雷; Digital Daijisen gives あずま. This is a name-use form, not a quiz reading.

These three cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [怒](怒.md) (“anger”): the third sense of 雷 かみなり is “anger; fit of anger”, and JMdict glosses 怒り いかり “anger; rage; fury; wrath; indignation” (1445670).
- [電](電.md) (“electricity”): KANJIDIC2 lists “lightning bolt” for 雷, and JMdict lists 電 as a rarely used kanji form of 稲妻 いなずま (1167860), “lightning; flash of lightning; lightning bolt”.
- [神](神.md) (“deity”): the second sense of 雷 かみなり is “god of thunder; god of lightning”, and JMdict's first sense of 神 かみ is “god; deity; divinity; spirit; kami” (1364440), whose fourth sense, marked archaic, is “thunder”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 霜 (“frost”), 虹 (“rainbow”), and 雲 (“cloud”); they are other weather words, and none means thunder or lightning.
