# 菜 — vegetables; greens

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

菜 concerns vegetables and greens. [野菜](../words/野菜.md) やさい names vegetables as a food category; 菜 な is also a noun for greens and can name the rapeseed plant.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine greens being gathered beneath the plant canopy; use the lower strokes as a reminder of a hand and a growing plant.

The canonical key is 艸, no. 140, visible as 艹 at the top.

- 菜の花 なのはな - rapeseed flowers

## Notes

- 菜の花 names rapeseed flowers, not every flower on any vegetable.
- 野菜 includes ordinary cultivated vegetables. 野 does not restrict the word to wild plants.
- Compare [採](採.md), pick/collect/adopt, and [葉](葉.md), leaf. An action, a plant part and a food category are different meanings.
