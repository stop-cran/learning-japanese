# 純 — pure; genuine

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

純 concerns purity, genuineness or being unmixed. In a compound such as 単純, the complete word can concern simplicity rather than the chemical purity of a material.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 糸 on the left and 屯 on the right; picture keeping one unmixed bundle of thread. This modern-shape association does not establish a historical or phonetic analysis.

糸, no. 120, appears in its compressed left-side form ⺯.

## Notes

- 単純 combines [単](単.md) with 純 and is a na-adjective: 単純な仕組み たんじゅんなしくみ, a simple mechanism. A simple task is not necessarily easy.
- 純粋 is useful for purity or genuineness; 不純 supplies a contrasting impure or mixed meaning. Do not force the same English translation into every compound.
- Compare [続](続.md), continue, and [組](組.md), group/assemble. Similar left sides do not make them purity words.
