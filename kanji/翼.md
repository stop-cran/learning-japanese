# 翼 — wing

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 羽 (no. 124) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

翼 is a wing: a bird's wing or an aircraft's wing. It can also mean a part that projects to the left or right, including the left or right flank of an army's formation. The native noun 翼 is read つばさ. In compounds such as 右翼 (うよく), “right wing,” the on-reading ヨク is used.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [羽](羽.md), "feather; bird counter," sits on top, and [異](異.md), "different; unusual; strange," fills the bottom. Imagine a strange creature that has grown feathers along its back: now it has wings. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 羽 or 異 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

The Jōyō table lists ヨク and つばさ. Kanjipedia marks たすける as outside the Jōyō readings; it is not included in the KANJIDIC2 reading metadata. Digital Daijisen also gives name readings すけ and たすく: these are name-only, not ordinary word readings or quiz choices. It records a suffix/counter use of ヨク for wings or birds, and historically ships; that specialized use does not change the reading of the standalone noun. 羽 (“feather; bird counter”) can also mean a wing, and both characters can count birds, so 羽 is excluded from this card's meaning quiz.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 鳥 (“bird”), 魚 (“fish”), and 虫 (“insect; bug”); none denotes a wing.
