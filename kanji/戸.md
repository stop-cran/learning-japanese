# 戸 — door; counter for households

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 戶 (no. 63) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

戸 concerns a door and also occurs in counting houses or households. 戸 と is the native door noun. 一戸 いっこ means one house or household and gives a usable example of the counting reading コ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the top stroke over the door-like lower outline and imagine a door panel under its lintel. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not an asserted historical derivation.

The source's canonical radical is 戶 (no. 63). 戸 is the modern Japanese shape used in this card; keep the canonical metadata rather than substituting a different radical.

## Notes

- 一戸 いっこ contains a small っ. The linked 戸 article teaches the door noun と, not a standalone pronunciation こ for that noun.
- Counting with 戸 concerns houses or households; it is not the ordinary counter for individual door panels.
- In 雨戸 あまど, the native と is voiced to ど; this is not the on reading コ.
- Compare [戻](戻.md), return or revert, which has extra lower strokes.
