# 靴 — shoes

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 革 (no. 177) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

靴 names shoes or boots. The standalone word is [靴](../words/靴.md) くつ.
These are things worn on the feet, not the feet themselves.
靴を脱いでください。 (くつをぬいでください。) — Please take off your shoes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the 革 block on the left and [化](化.md) on the right as two checkpoints
when remembering the outline. This is a shape aid, not evidence that every shoe is leather or an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

[花](花.md) also contains 化 but puts 艹 above it.
