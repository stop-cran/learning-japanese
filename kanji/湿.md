# 湿 — damp, moist

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

湿 expresses dampness, moisture, or humidity. The native verbs distinguish becoming damp from wetting something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture moisture collecting below the upper box and among the spreading lower strokes on the right, beside the water radical.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- [湿る](../words/湿る.md) しめる is intransitive godan, not ichidan: 湿って しめって. 湿す しめす is transitive godan.
- 湿度 measures moisture in the air; it is not another word for temperature.
- The source-only うるお.う and うるお.す rows do not justify changing the spelling of an everyday word merely because its pronunciation matches.
