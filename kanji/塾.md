# 塾 — private tutoring school

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

A 塾 provides privately run instruction. Someone who goes there for extra lessons can still be a pupil at another school.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare the bottoms of 塾 and 熟: 土 in the school character, four dots in the other.
