# 孫 — grandchild; descendants

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 子 (no. 39) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

孫 まご is a grandchild. In 子孫 しそん, the character participates in the broader word for descendants or offspring; that word is not restricted to grandchildren.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Use 子 at the left and the thread-like strokes at the right to picture a family connection continuing to a grandchild. This is an invented modern-shape association, not verified genealogy in the character's origin.

## Notes

- The standalone family-relationship word is まご. ソン is the on reading in 子孫, not an interchangeable pronunciation of that standalone word.
- Do not equate 子孫 with a count of grandchildren only; its dictionary sense is broader.
- The visible 子 component helps the mnemonic, but the entire character writes まご.
