# 語 — language, word

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **Phonetic:** 吾 (ゴ) · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

語 is a phonetic compound: 言 (speech, words) gives the meaning and 吾 gives the sound ゴ (吾 itself is an old word for "I", and also contains 五 and 口).
It means "word", "language" and "to tell". As a suffix it names languages: 日本語, 英語 えいご, 中国語 ちゅうごくご.

## Common words

- [日本語](../words/日本語.md) にほんご — the Japanese language
- 英語 えいご — English
- 単語 たんご — a word, vocabulary item
- 物語 ものがたり — story, tale

## Notes

語 is the character for a word or language as a system; 話 (talk, speak, story) is about speaking itself. Both have 言 on the left: 語 has 吾 on the right and 話 has 舌 (tongue).
Compare 言う いう and 読む よむ ([読](読.md)), which also use 言 but have other meanings; 語る and 話す differ in that 語る suggests telling at length or a story.

The 吾 part has the shape of [五](五.md) over [口](口.md), which helps in remembering the shape.
