# 昔 — old times

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[昔](../words/昔.md) むかし looks back to earlier times, often a period felt to be distant from the present.
It can introduce a recollection as well as a folk tale; it does not by itself mean the story is fictional.
昔の町 (むかしのまち) is "the town in the old days."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture an old date stamp, with a barred top sitting above 日.
This is a modern visual association, not evidence about the original character.

## Notes

[借](借.md) contains the same 昔 shape beside a person-shaped left side, but means borrowing.
[春](春.md) and [景](景.md) are other vertically arranged cards with 日.
Unlike [昨日](../words/昨日.md) きのう, "yesterday," 昔 does not specify an exact day.
