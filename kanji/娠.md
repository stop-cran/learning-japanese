# 娠 — being pregnant

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

娠 refers to being pregnant. The word 妊娠 (にんしん) puts two pregnancy-related characters together; it does not describe two separate events.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Both 妊 and 娠 begin with 女. Trace the long sloping strokes on the right of 娠; compare the shorter 壬 in [妊](妊.md).

## Notes

[唇](唇.md) has a similar upper shape but ends in 口. [妹](妹.md) and [妨](妨.md) have the same left side as 娠, not the same right side. 妊婦 (にんぷ) names the pregnant woman, whereas 妊娠 names pregnancy. The right half distinguishes 娠 from [妊](妊.md); do not use the shared pregnancy gloss to swap them in 妊娠.
