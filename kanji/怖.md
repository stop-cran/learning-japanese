# 怖 — frightening

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

怖 belongs to the fear family: [怖い](../words/怖い.md) こわい means frightening and can also express
being scared of something. 恐怖 きょうふ names fear or terror.

The indexing radical 心 appears as 忄 on the left.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine something startling hidden behind the 布-shaped right side.
This story uses the modern outline only; it is not historical derivation.
