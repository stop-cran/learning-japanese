# 奴 — fellow; servant

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

奴 can refer to a fellow or, in other words and readings, a servant. 奴 (やつ) in conversation may sound affectionate or dismissive; it is not a neutral label for a stranger.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 女 next to 又. [努](努.md) and [怒](怒.md) both contain this same upper shape, but add 力 and 心 below respectively.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ド | on | 奴隷 (どれい) — an enslaved person; slavery. The meaning belongs to the compound, not to every use of やつ. |
| やつ | kun | [奴](../words/奴.md) やつ — a guy; that one (familiar or rough) |
| やっこ | kun | 奴 (やっこ) — a servant, for example a samurai's attendant. An outside-table reading distinct from やつ. |

## Common words

- [奴](../words/奴.md) やつ — a guy; that one (familiar or rough)

## Notes

In 小さいやつ (ちいさいやつ, “the small one”), やつ can point to an object. In a historical account, 奴 read やっこ may instead identify a servant.

[努](努.md) and [怒](怒.md) add a lower component to 奴; [始](始.md) keeps 女 but changes the right side. [人](人.md) is neutral for a person; [物](物.md) is neutral for a thing. [僕](僕.md) is read ぼく when it means “I”; that is not a reading of 奴 just because the characters can appear in servant senses.
