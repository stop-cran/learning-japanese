# 吐 — spit out; vomit; breathe out

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

吐 is sending something out through the mouth: vomiting, spitting, breathing out, and putting thoughts into words. [吐く](../words/吐く.md) はく is to exhale, vomit or utter, and [吐き気](../words/吐き気.md) はきけ is nausea, the urge to vomit. JMdict glosses 吐く はく "to vomit; to throw up; to spit up," "to emit; to send forth; to breathe out" and, in later senses, "to give (an opinion)" and "to confess."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ト, putting something out of the mouth, with examples such as 吐血 とけつ, "vomiting blood; hematemesis," 吐露 とろ, "expressing one's mind; speaking out," and 嘔吐 おうと, "vomiting; emesis." It lists only はく as a kun reading and names 反吐 へど, "vomit; vomiting," among words with a hard-to-guess reading. A separate Daijisen headword つく lists 吐く as a spelling outside the official table. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [口](口.md), "mouth," at the left, and [土](土.md), "earth, soil," at the right. Imagine someone spitting from the mouth onto the ground. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 口 or 土 contributes meaning or sound, although 土 is read ト, a reading 吐 also has. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ト | on | 吐露 とろ - expressing one's mind; speaking out; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| は.く | kun | [吐く](../words/吐く.md) はく — to exhale; vomit; utter; [吐き気](../words/吐き気.md) はきけ — nausea; urge to vomit |
| つ.く | kun | 吐く つく - to breathe out; to breathe; to tell (a lie) (usually written in kana); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 吐く, 吐 writes は and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 吐き気, 吐き keeps はき and 気 supplies け. JMdict has two separate entries spelled 吐く: はく, the word the article teaches, and つく, usually written in kana alone, as in ため息をつく ためいきをつく, "to sigh; to breathe a sigh," also spelled 溜息を吐く. In 吐露, 吐 supplies ト and 露 supplies ロ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ト, with 吐露, 吐血 and 音吐朗々 おんとろうろう, "in a clear voice," as examples; and はく, with 吐く and 吐き気. つく is not in the official table. Practice selects は.く, the reading of the linked word articles 吐く and 吐き気. ト is the reading of 吐 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; ト and つ.く are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [気](気.md), "spirit, air," in 吐き気. 吐く is 吐 with okurigana only.
- [叫](叫.md), "shout; exclaim," and [吸](吸.md), "inhale; suck in," have the same 口 at the left. [社](社.md), "company, shrine," has the same 土 at the right. All are different kanji.
- [吹](吹.md), "blow," and [息](息.md), "breath," overlap the sense "breathe out" that 吐 and its words carry; JMdict's 吐く includes "to breathe out." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "breathe out" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ト sense: 吐瀉 としゃ, "vomiting and diarrhea (diarrhoea)," 音吐 おんと, "voice," and 呑吐 どんと, "swallowing and spitting out," a formal or literary term.
