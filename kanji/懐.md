# 懐 — bosom; nostalgic; become attached

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

懐 is the bosom, the space inside the front of one's clothing, and the feelings held close there: fondness for the past and attachment to someone. [懐かしい](../words/懐かしい.md) なつかしい is fondly remembered or nostalgic; JMdict glosses it "dear (old); fondly remembered; beloved; missed; nostalgic."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for カイ. One example of each:

- holding a thought in one's heart: 懐古 かいこ, "reminiscence; nostalgia; thinking fondly of the past";
- wrapping and holding something inside: 懐妊 かいにん, "pregnancy; conception";
- drawing someone in and winning them over: 懐柔 かいじゅう, "winning over; placation; gentle persuasion";
- the bosom of one's clothing: 懐中 かいちゅう, "one's pocket."

Daijisen also has a separate entry かい【懐】 for the thoughts in one's heart, an entry ふところ with seven senses, from the front fold of one's clothing to one's reach, the depths of a place, one's inner thoughts and the money one carries, and an archaic entry ほほ meaning ふところ; ほほ is not a KANJIDIC2 reading. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 忄, the narrow form of [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the left, and at the right [十](十.md), "ten," at the top, a box-like 罒 in the middle and the full shape of [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment," at the bottom. Imagine a heart tucked inside the folds of one's clothes. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 心, 十 or 衣 contributes meaning or sound. [壊](壊.md), "break; destroy; demolish," has the same right part and is read カイ and エ, a plain fact about readings. Historical formation is not established here.

In 懐かしい, 懐 writes なつ and かしい is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 懐かしむ uses the same なつ with かしむ, 懐く なつく uses なつ with く and 懐ける uses なつ with ける. The spelling 懐く is also read いだく, so context decides. In 手懐ける, 手 supplies て and 懐 supplies なず with ける. 懐 read ふところ stands alone with no okurigana; after 内 うち it becomes ぶところ, a voiced sound change (rendaku), in 内懐. In 懐中, 懐 supplies カイ and 中 supplies チュウ; in 述懐, 述 じゅつ becomes じゅっ before かい (gemination).

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カイ, with 懐中, 懐古 and 述懐 じゅっかい, "speaking about (one's thoughts, memories, etc.); relating (one's feelings, reminiscences, etc.)"; ふところ, with 懐, 懐手 ふところで, "with hands in pockets; idleness," and 内懐 うちぶところ, "inside pocket; bosom"; なつかしい, with 懐かしい; なつかしむ, with 懐かしむ; なつく, with 懐く; and なつける, with 懐ける. エ, なず.ける, いだ.く and おも.う are not in the table. Practice selects なつ.かしい, the reading with a linked word article. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid. The table also shows the older form 懷 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- 懐かしい is 懐 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji.
- [慢](慢.md), "arrogance; slackness," [憎](憎.md), "hate; detest," and [忙](忙.md), "busy," also have 忄 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [胸](胸.md), "chest; breast; feelings," [乳](乳.md), "milk; breast," [抱](抱.md), "embrace," [憶](憶.md), "remember; recall; guess," [恋](恋.md), "romantic love; longing," [付](付.md), "attach," and [心](心.md), "heart, mind," overlap the senses of 懐 and its words: the bosom and breast pocket of ふところ, its "mind; heart; inner thoughts" sense, embracing in いだく, where JMdict lists 抱く first, recalling the past in 懐古 and 追懐, yearning in 懐かしむ, and becoming attached in 懐く. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "bosom," "embrace" or "attached" as exclusive to any one character.
- [憧](憧.md), "long for; yearn; admire," shares yearning: JMdict glosses 懐かしむ "to yearn (for); to miss; to feel nostalgic (for)" and 憧れる "to long for; to yearn for." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- [惜](惜.md), "pity; precious; begrudge," shares "dear": JMdict glosses 惜しい "precious; dear; valuable" and 懐かしい "dear (old); fondly remembered; beloved; missed; nostalgic." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first カイ sense, holding a thought in one's heart: 懐疑 かいぎ, "doubt; skepticism," 懐旧 かいきゅう, "reminiscence; nostalgia," 感懐 かんかい, "impression," 所懐 しょかい, "one's opinion," 素懐 そかい, "a cherished hope," 坦懐 たんかい, "level emotion; frankness," 追懐 ついかい, "recollection; remembrance; reminiscence," 抱懐 ほうかい, "harboring; cherishing," and 本懐 ほんかい, "one's long-cherished desire."
- More examples of the second カイ sense, wrapping and holding something inside: 懐石 かいせき, "kaiseki; sophisticated traditional Japanese cuisine brought in courses," and 懐胎 かいたい, "conception; becoming pregnant; pregnancy."
- More examples of the fourth カイ sense, the bosom of one's clothing: 懐剣 かいけん, "dagger," 懐紙 かいし, "paper folded and tucked inside the front of one's kimono (esp. for use at the tea ceremony)," 懐炉 かいろ, "pocket heater; hand warmer," usually written in kana as カイロ, and 胸懐 きょうかい, "heart; feelings."
- A Daijisen example with ふところ is 懐刀 ふところがたな, "dagger; stiletto," and also "confidant; right-hand man."
