# 順 — order; sequence

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

順 concerns order, sequence or taking a turn. 順番 names an order or turn, while 順調 describes something proceeding favourably.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the 川-shaped strokes lined up in order beside 頁. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a claim about historical formation.

The canonical radical is 頁, no. 181, on the right.

## Notes

- The order noun 順 and 順調's favourable-state meaning are not interchangeable English labels. Learn the complete word and its grammatical use.
- The source also records an obedient/docile na-adjective sense for 順. That secondary sense does not change every sequence expression into a statement about obedience.
- Compare [川](川.md), river: the left-side resemblance is visual, not a reason to translate the whole character 順 as river.
