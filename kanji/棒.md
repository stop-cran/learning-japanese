# 棒 — rod; stick

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

棒 concerns a stick, rod, or pole. 棒 ぼう is the independent noun. 泥棒 どろぼう means a thief; its modern meaning is not derived by mechanically adding mud and stick.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside the crowded 奉-like right side and imagine a long wooden rod among the upright strokes. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not etymology.

## Notes

- The independent noun supplies the complete ボウ example. Learn 泥棒 as the whole reading どろぼう rather than trying to read each character independently.
- [柱](柱.md) concerns a pillar or post and its supporting role; 棒 emphasizes a rod-like object. A structural post can also be a pole, so these are useful comparisons rather than reliably wrong meanings for each other.
- The linked 泥棒 article explains its present-day thief meaning; the visible components alone do not establish its word history.
