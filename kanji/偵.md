# 偵 — spy; covert investigation

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

偵 concerns finding out hidden information, as in spying or covert investigation.
[探偵](../words/探偵.md) たんてい names a detective or investigator;
it does not automatically mean an enemy agent.
Learn 偵 through compounds rather than treating it as a standalone word for a detective.

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and 貞.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a person on the left keeping watch beside
the tall, boxed shape on the right. This is a visual memory aid, not an
etymology or an explanation based on 貞's meaning.

## Notes

- A 探偵 may be a private investigator; [刑事](../words/刑事.md) けいじ
  specifically names a police detective in its person sense.
  The shared English word "detective" does not make these identical job labels.
- [停](停.md), "halt; stopping," shares 亻 and テイ but has a different
  right side, with a roof-like top and a long lower hook.
- [低](低.md), "low," and [値](値.md), "value; price," also share 亻.
  Compare the entire right side, not just the radical or a boxed outline.
