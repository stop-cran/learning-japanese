# 省 — ministry; omit

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

省 appears in government-ministry vocabulary and in words about omitting, economizing or reflecting on oneself. Its separate readings belong to particular words; do not infer a single translation or pronunciation that works in all compounds.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 少 above 目 and imagine looking carefully to leave out an unnecessary part. This links visible modern shapes to an omission meaning, not to a historical derivation.

## Notes

- 省く is transitive godan: 手間を省く (てまをはぶく), save effort; 省きます はぶきます. It is not the same verb or conjugation as 省みる.
- 省みる is transitive ichidan: 自分の行動を省みる (じぶんのこうどうをかえりみる), reflect on one's own behavior; 省みます かえりみます. Its written みる is okurigana in the source reading かえり.みる.
- 帰省 uses セイ, while the ministry-related 省令 uses ショウ. These examples establish the readings, not an unrestricted rule for reading every new compound.
