# 隠 — hide; conceal

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

隠 is hiding: keeping something out of sight, or keeping oneself out of view. KANJIDIC2 lists “conceal”, “hide” and “cover”. Kanjipedia's first sense is かくれる。おおわれる。かくれ住む。 (hide; be covered; live in hiding), and its second is かくす。おおいかくす。 (hide something; cover it up). [隠す](../words/隠す.md) かくす is glossed in JMdict “to hide; to conceal” (entry 1170650). [隠れる](../words/隠れる.md) かくれる is “to hide; to conceal oneself; to take cover”, “to disappear (behind); to be hidden (by); to be covered; to go out of sight”, “to live in seclusion; to retire (from the world)” and “to be unknown (e.g. of a talent); to be undiscovered; to be hidden (e.g. of a meaning)” (entry 1170660).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the ⻖ shape stands on the left. On the right, ⺤ sits at the top, ⺕ in the middle, and [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the bottom. Picture a heart tucked away at the bottom behind a tall wall, with layers stacked over it so no one can see it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that ⻖, ⺤, ⺕ or 心 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- In 隠す, 隠 writes かく and す is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 隠し and 隠れる end in し and れる instead. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so かく.す supplies the one question for that stem, shown as かくす; かく.し and かく.れる are retained from KANJIDIC2.
- Joyo lists イン, with 隠居 and 隠語 いんご (“secret language; jargon; cant; argot; slang”, JMdict 1170720) among its examples, as well as かくす and かくれる, and shows 隱 as the older form in parentheses. Kanjipedia lists オン outside the table (外). Digital Daijisen gives オン（呉） beside イン（漢）, and also the kun reading こもる; JMdict lists 隠る as a rarely used kanji form of 籠もる こもる (entry 1593430).
- KANJIDIC2 records お and がくし as name readings; Digital Daijisen gives none. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.

These seven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [秘](秘.md) (“secret; conceal”): KANJIDIC2 lists “conceal” and the kun reading かく.す for both 隠 and 秘, and JMdict glosses 秘める ひめる “to hide; to keep to oneself” (1483980).
- [潜](潜.md) (“dive; submerge; lurk”): KANJIDIC2 lists “conceal”, “hide” and the kun reading かく.れる for both 隠 and 潜, and JMdict glosses 潜む ひそむ “to lurk; to be hidden; to be concealed; to lie dormant; to be latent” (1391240).
- [密](密.md) (“secret; dense; close”): the first sense of 隠密 おんみつ is “secret; clandestine; covert; stealthy” (JMdict 1170890), and Digital Daijisen's first オン sense of 隠 is 世間からかくれる。秘密にする。 (hide from the world; keep secret), with 隠密 as its example.
- [覆](覆.md) (“cover; overturn”) and [被](被.md) (“cover; incur”): KANJIDIC2 lists “cover” for both 隠 and 覆, Kanjipedia's second sense of 隠 includes おおいかくす (cover up), JMdict glosses 覆う おおう “to cover; to hide; to conceal; to wrap; to disguise” (1588840) and lists 被う as a rarely used kanji form of it, and the 被 card lists おお.う.
- [偵](偵.md) (“spy; covert investigation”): the second sense of 隠密, marked as a historical term, is “spy (for a daimyo, shogun, etc.); secret agent”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “spy” for 偵.
- [陰](陰.md) (“shade; yin”): KANJIDIC2 lists “secret” for 陰, and the third sense of 陰 いん is “hidden place; unseen part; private location” (JMdict 1170230).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 探 (“search”), 示 (“indicate”), and 捕 (“capture”); none means hiding or covering.
