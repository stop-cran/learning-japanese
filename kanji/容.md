# 容 — contain; appearance

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

容 connects to containing something, and also has form or appearance meanings.
The containing side is a useful anchor for [内容](../words/内容.md) (ないよう), "contents," and 容器 (ようき), "container."
A container and what it contains are related but different things.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the broad opening above the lower 口 as the mouth of a container sheltered by 宀.
This is a visual aid, not a historical derivation.

## Common words

## Notes

容 has 谷 beneath the roof. Compare [客](客.md), which has 各, and [宿](宿.md), whose interior has a person-shaped side and a 百-shaped block.
[空](空.md) is a looser comparison for the spreading upper interior, not an identical component.
Learn 内容 as "what is in it," not automatically "what it looks like."
