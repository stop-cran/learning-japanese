# 毛 — hair; fur; down

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 毛 (no. 82) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

毛 concerns hair, fur, wool, or down. The native noun 毛 け can refer to body hair or an animal's covering. 毛布 もうふ is a blanket; the material association does not require every modern blanket to be made of wool.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the short upper strokes and the long lower stroke curling upward, and imagine a tuft of hair. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not verified etymology.

## Notes

- [髪](髪.md) concerns hair on the human head. 毛 is broader, so the difference is not that 毛 can never refer to a head hair.
- The linked 毛 article teaches け. モウ is demonstrated by 毛布; do not substitute it for the native noun in an ordinary sentence about a cat's fur.
