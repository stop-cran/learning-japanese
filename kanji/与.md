# 与 — give; grant

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

与 concerns giving or granting something. [与える](../words/与える.md) あたえる can describe granting someone an opportunity;
給与 きゅうよ is pay received for work. This is a useful core, not a translation for every compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture an item resting on the short upper ledge, ready to be handed across the lower bar.
The bent outline is a memory cue, not a historical account.

## Notes

[写](写.md) has a similar lower outline but adds a cover above it. [五](五.md) and [予](予.md) are other compact shapes
worth distinguishing stroke by stroke.
For giving a present to a friend, do not automatically substitute 与える for あげる:
与える can frame the giver as granting something to a recipient.
