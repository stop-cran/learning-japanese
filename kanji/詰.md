# 詰 — pack tightly; press

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

詰 concerns packing or pressing things together and reaching a tight or final point. Its extensions include close questioning and being checkmated; they are not all literal acts of filling a container.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 言 on the left and the compact 吉-shaped right side, with 士 stacked tightly over 口. Imagine words squeezed into a small space; this is a mnemonic, not etymology.

## Notes

- Learn the whole verbs: 詰める is ichidan; 詰まる and 詰む are intransitive godan verbs in the senses shown. 詰む つむ is not the same verb as 詰める つめる.
- The noun 詰め つめ and suffix -づめ are separate uses. The suffix can follow a noun, as in 缶詰め; its dictionary entry also covers continuous action after a verb's -ます stem. It is not an independent noun pronounced づめ.
- 詰む also has a colloquial extension for being hard-pressed or out of options. The checkmate example does not make all uses of this verb colloquial.
