# 傘 — umbrella; parasol

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9), the broad top form · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

傘 names an umbrella or parasol. [傘](../words/傘.md) かさ can protect against rain or sun; 日傘 ひがさ specifically names a sun umbrella.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture the broad 人-shaped top as an open umbrella, the four smaller 人-like shapes beneath it as people, and the central vertical stroke as its handle. The 十-shaped crossing helps locate the bottom of the frame. This is a memory aid using the present outline, not a historical account.

This lesson practices かさ, the reading of the linked word article. サン remains in the full metadata and is a valid compound reading; its omission from practice is not a rarity claim.

## Common words

- [傘](../words/傘.md) かさ — umbrella; parasol
- 日傘 ひがさ — parasol; sunshade
- 雨傘 あまがさ — rain umbrella
- 傘下 さんか — affiliated with; under an organization's umbrella

## Notes

- In 日傘 and 雨傘, かさ is voiced to がさ. Learn the complete words rather than adding がさ as a separate source reading.
- JMdict also records 傘 as a spelling of からかさ, a paper umbrella, alongside から傘 and 唐傘. That separate whole-word reading is valid but is not selected for practice here.
- The native word 傘 can also name an umbrella-like object, such as a lampshade or mushroom cap. This physical "shade" is not the darkness of [暗](暗.md); a mushroom cap is not the ordinary headgear of [帽](帽.md).
- [被](被.md) has the broad meanings cover and shelter. Its "cover; incur" answer label can be too broad beside this protective object or a lampshade, so the direct meaning-quiz pair is conservatively excluded. This does not make the characters interchangeable.
- [金](金.md) means gold or money, [全](全.md) concerns the whole or completeness, and [合](合.md) concerns fitting or joining. Their broad tops can invite visual confusion, but none names an umbrella.
