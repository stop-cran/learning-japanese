# 野 — open field

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 里 (no. 166) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

野 refers to **open land or fields**. In compounds it can suggest the wild, or an abstract field
of activity. The meaning of a whole word still needs to be learned: 野菜 means vegetables, not only wild plants.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the left-hand 里 block, with shapes resembling [田](田.md)
and [土](土.md), beside an open stretch marked by the right-hand 予. This is a visual aid, not etymology.

## Notes

[田](田.md) is associated with rice fields; 野 often concerns open countryside or the wild.
That contrast is a learning guide, not a rule restricting 野 to uncultivated land in every compound.

[理](理.md) has 里 on the right, while 野 has it on the left. [重](重.md) is a single compact block,
not the side-by-side arrangement of 野.
