# 娘 — daughter; girl

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

Learn 娘 first through [娘](../words/娘.md) (むすめ), "daughter." A person's daughter can be an adult: the family relationship does not disappear with age.
The same word also has a young-woman sense, so context matters when no parent is identified.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture 女 beside a tall name tag shaped like 良, marking the daughter's place in a family photo.
This visual story is not a historical derivation.

## Notes

The right side is 良-shaped, with a top dot. Compare the related lower outlines in [銀](銀.md) and [食](食.md), without treating them as identical components.
一人娘 says there is only one daughter; it does not by itself say there are no sons.
