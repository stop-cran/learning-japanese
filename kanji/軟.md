# 軟 — soft; pliant

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

軟 concerns softness, pliancy or softening. 軟らかい describes something soft or flexible, while 軟化 names a softening.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 車 on the left beside 欠, and imagine the wheel yielding softly instead of remaining rigid. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- 軟らかい belongs to the same unrestricted spelling family as [柔らかい](../words/柔らかい.md). That entry does not establish a rigid rule assigning each spelling to only physical or only figurative softness.
- 軟らか is separately recorded as a na-adjective; 軟らかい is an i-adjective. Their separate entries support both source readings, not an instruction to turn the i-adjective into 軟らかいな.
- The left side is 車. [次](次.md) and [飲](飲.md) have the same 欠-shaped right side but different left sides and meanings.
