# 許 — permit; forgive

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

許 concerns permission, and in [許す](../words/許す.md) ゆるす it can also concern forgiveness.
Allowing someone to do something is not the same situation as forgiving something already done:
the sentence tells you which sense is intended.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** place a spoken permission, 言, beside the 午 shape.
The image helps with spelling but is not a historical explanation or a claim that 午 supplies the sound.

## Notes

辞書を使うことを許す (じしょをつかうことをゆるす) means "allow the use of a dictionary."
The whole action before ことを is what is permitted.
[午](午.md) lacks the speech component; [話](話.md) has 舌 on the right,
while [訪](訪.md) has 方.
