# 惨 — miserable; cruel; tragic

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

惨 describes something miserable, cruel or tragic. It appears in 惨め (みじめ), “miserable,” in 悲惨 (ひさん), “tragic; miserable,” and in 惨事 (さんじ), “disaster.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 忄, the narrow form of 心 “heart,” is on the left, and [参](参.md), “participate,” is on the right; 参 is also read サン.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| サン | on | 悲惨 (ひさん), tragic or miserable; 惨事 (さんじ), a disaster or tragic accident. |
| ザン | on | 惨殺 (ざんさつ), slaughter; 惨敗 (ざんぱい), a crushing defeat. |
| みじ.め | kun | [惨め](../words/みじめ.md) みじめ — miserable; pitiable; wretched |
| いた.む | kun | source-only here: KANJIDIC2 records いたむ, but JMdict has no 惨む, and the standard-use table does not list this reading for 惨. |
| むご.い | kun | 惨い (むごい), cruel or tragic; JMdict marks this word as usually written in kana. The standard-use table does not list this reading for 惨. |

## Notes

[診](診.md) and [珍](珍.md) also end in three slanted strokes, as 参 does, but mean diagnose and rare; [情](情.md) also starts with 忄 and means emotion. [苦](苦.md), [惜](惜.md), [哀](哀.md) and [悲](悲.md) relate to suffering, pity or sorrow. 惨 has two on readings: サン, as in 悲惨, and ザン, as in 惨殺 (ざんさつ), “slaughter.” 惨敗, “crushing defeat,” is read ざんぱい; JMdict also lists さんぱい. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 63](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=63) lists サン, ザン and みじめ, with 惨めだ; KANJIDIC2 records the kun reading as みじ.め, with め as okurigana.
