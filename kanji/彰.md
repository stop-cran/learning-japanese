# 彰 — make known; commend

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 彡 (no. 59) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

彰 means to make something clearly known, especially someone’s merit. It appears in 表彰 and 顕彰 (けんしょう), “public honoring.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 章, “chapter; badge,” is on the left and 彡 on the right. Picture a badge with rays shining out so that everyone sees it.
