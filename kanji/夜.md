# 夜 — night

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 夕 (no. 36) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

夜 means night and can cover what English calls evening. It contrasts with [昼](昼.md), daytime, rather than marking only the hours after midnight.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the top dot and bar as a dark cover over the moving strokes below: the world going about its business at night. This is a memory aid, not an etymology.

## Common words

- [夜](../words/夜.md) よる — night, evening
- 今夜 こんや — tonight, this evening
- 夜中 よなか — middle of the night
- 夜間 やかん — nighttime

## Notes

Learn the reading with each word: 今夜 is こんや, not a combination ending in よる. 夜中 means the middle of the night, not necessarily exactly twelve o'clock.

[夕](夕.md) points toward evening and fading daylight; [朝](朝.md) is morning. Night follows evening, but ordinary time expressions can overlap.
