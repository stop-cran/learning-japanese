# 臓 — internal organs; viscera

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

臓 concerns the body's internal organs. [心臓](../words/心臓.md) しんぞう is the heart, while 臓器 ぞうき names internal organs more generally.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine organs protected within a crowded enclosure to the right of the flesh sign.

The canonical key 肉, no. 130, appears as 月 on the left.

## Notes

- 心臓 is the anatomical heart and also has figurative extensions. [心](心.md) alone has a broader heart/mind range.
- 臓器 does not name only the intestines; the heart is one internal organ too.
- The シン in 心臓 belongs to 心 and ゾウ to 臓. Do not allocate the whole reading to either character.
