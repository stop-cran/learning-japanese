# 墨 — ink; inkstick

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

墨 is ink, above all the black ink of East Asian calligraphy and ink painting, made by grinding an inkstick with water. [墨](../words/墨.md) すみ is sumi ink or an inkstick. JMdict glosses 墨 すみ "sumi; India ink; Chinese ink; ink stick; ink-cake," then "squid ink; octopus ink" and "carpenter's inking string."

The Daijisen character entry gives eight senses for ボク. One example of each:

- black pigment for writing and painting: 墨汁 ぼくじゅう, "India ink; China ink";
- something written in ink: 遺墨 いぼく, "autographs (brushwork) of departed person";
- a tool for writing: 白墨 はくぼく, "(piece of) chalk";
- tattooing, or tattooing as a punishment: 墨刑 ぼっけい or ぼくけい, "tattooing (as a form of punishment in ancient China)," a historical term in JMdict;
- a carpenter's inking line: 縄墨 じょうぼく, "standard; inked timber marking string";
- Mozi, a thinker of ancient China: 墨家 ぼっか or ぼくか, "Mohism; Moism," a historical term in JMdict;
- the Sumida River: 墨堤 ぼくてい, "banks of the Sumida river";
- Mexico: 日墨 にちぼく, "Japan and Mexico."

For すみ, which becomes ずみ after another element, it gives 墨絵 すみえ, "ink painting," 靴墨 くつずみ, "shoe polish," and 眉墨 まゆずみ, "eyebrow makeup; eyebrow pencil." It names 墨西哥 メキシコ, "Mexico," among words with a hard-to-guess reading; JMdict marks that spelling as ateji and rarely used, and notes that the word is usually written in kana alone. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [黒](黒.md), "black," at the top and [土](土.md), "earth, soil," at the bottom. Imagine black soot pressed as hard as packed earth into a stick of ink. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 黒 or 土 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ボク | on | 水墨画 すいぼくが - India-ink painting; ink wash painting; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| すみ | kun | [墨](../words/墨.md) すみ — sumi ink; inkstick |

The word 墨 is the kanji alone, read すみ, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. After another element すみ often becomes ずみ, a sound change called rendaku, the voicing of the first sound, as in 靴墨 and 眉墨. In 水墨画, 水 supplies スイ, 墨 supplies ボク and 画 supplies ガ. ボク shortens to ぼっ in 墨痕 ぼっこん, "ink marks; handwriting," and in 墨家, where the small っ doubles the next sound.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ボク, with 筆墨 ひつぼく, "stationery; pen and ink; writing materials," 白墨 and 遺墨 as examples, and すみ, with 墨, 墨絵 and 眉墨. Practice selects すみ, the reading with a linked word article. ボク is the reading of most 墨 compounds, but no word article uses it yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The common word 墨 contains only this kanji.
- [墓](墓.md), "grave; tomb," [型](型.md), "mold; type," and [堂](堂.md), "hall," have the same 土 at the bottom under a different shape. All are different kanji.
- [黒](黒.md), "black," is the top part of 墨 and overlaps the black color that 墨 names in words such as 墨色 すみいろ, "ink black." It is kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "black" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ボク senses: 墨痕, 墨跡 ぼくせき, "writing (especially of a Zen monk); penmanship," 翰墨 かんぼく, "brush and ink," 古墨 こぼく, "old ink stick," 筆墨 and 水墨画 for black pigment; 墨守 ぼくしゅ, "adherence (to custom, tradition)," for Mozi; and 米墨戦争 べいぼくせんそう, "Mexican-American War (1846-1848)," a historical term in JMdict, for Mexico. For すみ it also gives 薄墨 うすずみ, "thin India ink; diluted ink," and 朱墨 しゅずみ, "vermilion ink stick; cinnabar ink stick."
