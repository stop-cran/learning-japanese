# 穀 — grain; cereals

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115), in the lower-left group · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

穀 concerns staple seed crops and grain. [穀物](../words/穀物.md) こくもつ
means grains or cereals, such as rice and wheat or barley. Daijisen's
noun entry also includes beans among food seed crops; do not turn this
vocabulary category into a strict botanical rule.

English corn in a grain gloss is not restricted to maize. Similarly,
cereals here need not be a prepared breakfast food.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 禾 below [士](士.md) and
a 冖-shaped cover on the left, with 殳 on the right. Imagine harvested
grain sheltered under a cover. This is a modern visual association,
not an account of historical component meanings or sounds.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| コク | on | [穀物](../words/穀物.md) こくもつ - grain; cereals |

Practice selects コク, also the Joyo reading. In 穀物, 物 supplies
モツ. 脱穀 だっこく and 雑穀 ざっこく retain こく after the small っ
in the preceding part. Name readings are not practice readings.

Daijisen also records classical たなつもの for rice or grains. It is
recognition information outside this frozen on/kun inventory, not an
alternative pronunciation for modern こくもつ.

- 脱穀 だっこく - threshing
- 雑穀 ざっこく - assorted grains; also millet in a separate dictionary sense

## Notes

- [殿](殿.md), palace/lord, [投](投.md), throw, and [段](段.md),
  step/level, also contain 殳 but do not mean grain.
- [米](米.md) and [麦](麦.md) name particular grains; [稲](稲.md)
  names the rice plant. The broader 穀 category and these particular
  crop meanings overlap without being identical categories.
- Grain as seed overlaps [種](種.md) and the seed/fruit sense of
  [実](実.md). [粒](粒.md) shares the English grain label but can name
  a particle of many materials. These comparisons are excluded;
  穀 does not mean every seed, fruit or small particle.
- [穂](穂.md) is the grain-bearing head, a part of a plant, not a
  synonym for all grain. The source's classical indexing key is 禾;
  its separate Nelson classification under 殳 does not replace that key.
