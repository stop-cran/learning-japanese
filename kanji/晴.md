# 晴 — clear skies

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

晴 is a useful weather character: [晴れる](../words/晴れる.md) はれる means that the sky
clears or the weather is sunny. 晴れ はれ names fine weather, while 晴天 せいてん
is another noun for a clear sky or fair weather.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** put 日, the sun, beside 青, a blue-green shape cue, and picture an open sky.
This is not an assertion that these parts historically combined in that way.

## Notes

[青](青.md) alone names a color, not sunny weather. [時](時.md) and [晩](晩.md) share 日
but have different right sides. [明](明.md), "bright; clear," has related meanings, so compare the whole words in context.
晴れる is a verb; 晴れ is a noun, as in 今日は晴れです (きょうははれです), "It is sunny today."
