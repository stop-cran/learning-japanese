# 設 — establish; provide

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

設 concerns setting something up, establishing it or providing what is needed. 設備 names equipment or facilities, while 建設 concerns construction or establishment.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 言 on the left announcing that a new facility is being set up, while the 殳-shaped right side supplies the tools. This is a visual story, not an explanation of the character's historical construction.

## Notes

- 設ける is a transitive ichidan verb. The spelling and reading belong together; do not substitute the sound of the on reading into this native verb.
- 設計 is せっけい, with a small っ from セツ + ケイ. Setting out a design is not the same activity as completing the construction.
