# 向 — face toward

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

向 concerns orientation: which way something faces or heads.
[向く](../words/向く.md) むく describes turning or facing, while 向かう むかう can describe heading toward a destination.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine looking through the inner 口 opening in one direction.
This is a picture suggested by the modern shape, not an account of its origin.

## Notes

右を向く (みぎをむく) means "turn to the right"; it does not by itself mean traveling rightward.
Compare [同](同.md): 向 has a short slanting stroke above its outer frame, rather than 同's inner horizontal line.
The other source readings are retained in metadata.
