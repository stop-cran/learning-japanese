# 箱 — box

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (as the bamboo top ⺮, no. 118) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

箱 is a box, case, or similar container.
The noun [箱](../words/箱.md) はこ does not specify what the box is made of.
Learn compounds as words in their own right: 本箱 ほんばこ means a bookcase, not just any box with a book inside.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the top bamboo marks as a lid over the 木 and 目 shapes below.
This is a memory image for the modern character, not a statement about its origin or a box's material.

## Notes

竹 appears here in its top form ⺮. Distinguish the 木-plus-目 lower section from the lower parts of
[笑](笑.md), [等](等.md), and [答](答.md).
箱に入れる (はこにいれる), "put into a box," uses に for the destination.
