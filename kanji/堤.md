# 堤 — dike; embankment

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

堤 is an embankment or bank. The compound [堤防](../words/堤防.md) (ていぼう) refers to a raised structure used to hold back water or protect land.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture earth raised into a bank beside water. This is a memory aid only, not a claim about historical formation.

## Notes

- 堤 and [岸](岸.md), “shore; riverbank,” can both describe a bank by water, but 堤 is a raised barrier. The direct overlap is excluded from meaning choices.
- 防波堤 is a breakwater and is not interchangeable with every river levee.
