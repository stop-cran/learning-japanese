# 絶 — sever; cease

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

絶 can express a connection or flow being cut off. It also has a "beyond comparison" side:
the useful word [絶対](../words/絶対.md) ぜったい means "absolutely; definitely."
Learn that word as a unit, not as a literal instruction to cut something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 糸 thread stopping at the 色-shaped part on the right.
This is a modern memory aid, not an etymology.

## Common words

- [絶対](../words/絶対.md) ぜったい — absolutely; definitely
- 絶える たえる — come to an end; cease
- 絶つ たつ — cut off; sever

## Notes

絶える describes something ceasing; 絶つ describes cutting something off.
In 絶対, ゼツ + タイ becomes ぜったい, with a small っ.
The right-hand 色 shape distinguishes 絶 from [絵](絵.md), [紙](紙.md), and [給](給.md).
