# 儒 — Confucian; Confucian scholar

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

儒 concerns Confucian thought and its scholars.
[儒教](../words/儒教.md) じゅきょう means Confucianism, the tradition of
teachings associated with 孔子（こうし）, Confucius.
儒 is not a general label for every teacher or religious believer.

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and 需 on the right.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a person beside layers of study notes.
This is an invented visual memory aid, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

## Notes

- 儒学（じゅがく）also means Confucianism, with emphasis on its scholarly
  aspect. It often overlaps with 儒教; do not impose an absolute
  religion-versus-philosophy divide.
- In older scholarly usage, 儒 can extend to a learned person more generally.
  Learn the whole word and context rather than treating "Confucian" as an
  exceptionless translation in every compound.
- [儀](儀.md), "ceremony; formal conduct," [僧](僧.md), "Buddhist monk; priest,"
  and [優](優.md), "gentle; superior," also have 亻 but different right-hand shapes.
  A shared component does not make their meanings interchangeable.
