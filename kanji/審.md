# 審 — examine; judge; trial

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

審 is looking into something closely in order to judge it, as a court or a referee does. [審判](../words/審判.md) しんぱん is a judgment or a referee; JMdict glosses it "judgement; judgment; decision; verdict; sentence," then "refereeing; umpiring; judging," "judgement (of God)" and "referee; umpire; judge."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for シン. One example of each:

- examining in detail and telling good from bad: 審査 しんさ, "judging; inspection; examination; investigation; review";
- a hearing of a case in court: 再審 さいしん, "retrial; reopening of a case";
- a referee: 主審 しゅしん, "chief umpire; referee (soccer, boxing, etc.)."

It gives the kun reading つまびらか, which KANJIDIC2 also has. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the roof-like 宀 at the top and [番](番.md), "turn in a series," below it; 番 itself has 釆 over [田](田.md), "rice field." Imagine people waiting their turn under one roof while a judge examines each case. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 宀, 番, 釆 or 田 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| シン | on | [審判](../words/審判.md) しんぱん — judgment; referee |
| つまび.らか | kun | 審らか つまびらか - detailed; clear; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| つぶさ.に | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict spells つぶさに 具に and in other ways, but not with 審 |

In 審判, 審 supplies シン and 判 supplies パン; JMdict also reads the word しんばん. In 審らか, 審 writes つまび and らか is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict lists 審らか after the spelling 詳らか, marks the word as usually written in kana, and gives the obsolete kana つばひらか. JMdict's entry つぶさに, "in detail; with great care; completely; fully," is spelled 具に and in other ways, but not with 審, so no 審 word with つぶさ.に is shown here; [具](具.md), "tools; ingredients," has that reading on its own card.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only シン, with 審判, 審議 しんぎ, "deliberation; discussion; consideration," and 不審 ふしん, "doubt; suspicion; question; strangeness," as examples. つまび.らか and つぶさ.に are not in the official table. Practice selects シン, the reading with a linked word article; the kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [判](判.md), "judge; seal," in 審判.
- [番](番.md), "turn in a series," is the lower part of 審 written on its own. [宙](宙.md), "space; midair," and [宿](宿.md), "lodging," have the same 宀 at the top. All are different kanji.
- [判](判.md), "judge; seal," [査](査.md), "investigate; inspect," [診](診.md), "diagnose; examine medically," [調](調.md), "investigate; tune," [究](究.md), "investigate thoroughly," [議](議.md), "deliberate; debate," [決](決.md), "decide," [試](試.md), "test; try," and [験](験.md), "test; verify," overlap the senses "examine," "judge," "inspect," "investigate," "deliberate," "decision" and "examination" that 審 and 審判 carry; 判 appears with 審 in 審判, 査 in 審査 and 議 in 審議. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "examine" or "judge" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples. Examining: 審議; 審理 しんり, "trial"; 審美的 しんびてき, "aesthetic"; 不審. Court hearings: 一審 いっしん, "first instance; first trial"; 結審 けっしん, "conclusion of trial; conclusion of a hearing"; 原審 げんしん, "original sentence"; 終審 しゅうしん, "final trial"; 陪審 ばいしん, "jury." Referees: 球審 きゅうしん, "umpire-in-chief; plate umpire"; 線審 せんしん, "line judge; linesman"; 副審 ふくしん, "assistant umpire; assistant referee."
