# 仰 — look up; revere

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

仰 is looking up, with the eyes and with respect: raising the face, revering someone above, and, in honorific words, what a superior says or orders. [信仰](../words/信仰.md) しんこう is religious faith. JMdict glosses the verb 仰ぐ あおぐ "to look up (at)," "to look up to; to respect; to revere," "to ask for (e.g. guidance); to seek" and "to depend on; to rely on."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ギョウ. One example of each:

- looking up: 仰角 ぎょうかく, "angle of elevation";
- looking up to someone with respect: 欽仰 きんぎょう, "reverence; adoration; veneration";
- exaggeration: 大仰 おおぎょう, "exaggerated; overblown; overdone."

For コウ and ゴウ it gives revering, as in 信仰, 景仰 けいこう, "adoration; admiration; reverence," and 渇仰 かつごう, "adoration; reverence; esteem." It marks コウ as a customary reading and also lists ゴウ, which KANJIDIC2 does not list. Its kun readings are あおぐ, おおせ and おっしゃる. More Daijisen examples of the ギョウ senses are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [人](人.md), "person," as 亻 at the left, and at the right a shape also found in [迎](迎.md), "welcome; greet." Imagine a person at the left tipping back his head to look up at someone he reveres. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 人 or the right-hand shape contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| お.っしゃる | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; it matches the spelling 仰っしゃる, which JMdict lists only as a search-only form of 仰る |
| おっしゃ.る | kun | 仰る おっしゃる - to say; to speak; to tell (honorific; usually written in kana); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 信仰, 信 supplies シン and 仰 supplies コウ. In 仰ぐ, 仰 writes あお and ぐ is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 仰せ is おお + せ in the same way. 仰る is おっしゃ + る; the KANJIDIC2 division お.っしゃる fits the longer spelling 仰っしゃる, where the kanji writes only お. JMdict marks 仰せ and 仰る as honorific language.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ギョウ, with 仰視 ぎょうし, "looking up to; revering," 仰天 and 仰角 as examples; コウ, with 信仰; あおぐ, with 仰ぐ; and おおせ, with 仰せ. お.っしゃる and おっしゃ.る are not in the official table. Practice selects コウ, the reading with a linked word article. ギョウ is the reading of most other 仰 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; it and the kun readings are illustrated with dictionary words, except お.っしゃる, which has only a search-only spelling. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [信](信.md), "trust; belief," in 信仰.
- [迎](迎.md), "welcome; greet," has the same right-hand shape as 仰. [印](印.md), "stamp; mark," has a similar shape. [仲](仲.md), "relations between people," has the same 亻 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [尊](尊.md), "revere; esteem," [敬](敬.md), "respect; honor," [拝](拝.md), "worship; bow in respect," [信](信.md), "trust; belief," [見](見.md), "see,look," and [上](上.md), "up,above," overlap the senses "look up," "revere" and "faith" that 仰 and its words carry; 信 also appears in 信仰. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "look up" or "revere" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ギョウ senses: 仰臥 ぎょうが, "lying on one's back; lying face upward; lying supine," 仰望 ぎょうぼう, "looking up; looking upwards," and 俯仰 ふぎょう, "looking up and down; actions; being obliging," besides 仰視 and 仰天; and 仰山 ぎょうさん, "a lot; plenty; abundant; great many," which JMdict marks as usually written in kana.
