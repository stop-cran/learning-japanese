# 農 — agriculture; farming

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 辰 (no. 161) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

農 concerns farming and agriculture. 農業 names the activity or industry, 農家 a farmer or farming family, and 農民 farmers or peasants.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See a 曲-shaped top above 辰 and imagine rows in a cultivated field above the ground. This is an invented modern-shape memory aid, not historical analysis.

KANJIDIC2 indexes 農 under 辰, no. 161, the lower component rather than the 曲-shaped top.
