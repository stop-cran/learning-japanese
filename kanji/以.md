# 以 — by means of

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

以 carries ideas such as "by means of" and "on the basis of." A useful learning connection is taking something as a reference point:
[以上](../words/以上.md) and 以下 mark numerical bounds, while 以前 concerns earlier time.
This is a guide to the words, not a claim that 以 has one English equivalent everywhere.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** the [人](人.md)-like shape on the right uses the marks on the left as a guide.
The association is a memory aid, not verified history.

## Common words

- [以上](../words/以上.md) いじょう — the stated amount or more; the above
- 以下 いか — the stated amount or less; the following
- 以前 いぜん — before; previously

## Notes

For precise numerical limits, 以上 and 以下 include the stated number. Ten or more includes ten.
Do not reduce the whole shape to [人](人.md) or [入](入.md): the separate marks on the left are essential.
