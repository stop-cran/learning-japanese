# 桑 — mulberry

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

桑 names the **mulberry tree**. The native plant name is [桑](../words/桑.md) くわ; it is not a general word for every tree or berry.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice three 又-like shapes over 木. Imagine hands reaching over a tree to gather leaves. This is a mnemonic for the modern shape, not an explanation of its historical construction.

## Notes

- [森](森.md), forest or woods, has three 木 shapes rather than the 又-like upper shapes of 桑.
- [友](友.md), friend, is much simpler and has no 木 base. [茶](茶.md), tea, has 艹 at the top and a different lower outline.
- The reading questions select くわ in the linked plant-name article. ソウ and the verified example 桑園 remain available for recognition; exclusion from questions is not a rarity or invalidity claim.
- Learn personal names separately rather than assuming this plant-name reading works for every name written with 桑.
