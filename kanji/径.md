# 径 — diameter; path

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

径 is the distance straight across a circle, and also a narrow path. [直径](../words/直径.md) ちょっけい is a diameter and [半径](../words/半径.md) はんけい, half of it, is a radius. On its own, 径 けい also means "diameter" in JMdict. The older form of the character is 徑.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ケイ. One example of each:

- the line straight across a circle: 口径 こうけい, "aperture; bore; calibre; caliber";
- a small path or a shortcut: 径庭 けいてい, "great difference," also written 逕庭;
- straight, directly: 直情径行 ちょくじょうけいこう, "impulsive and straightforward; guileless."

It gives the kun reading みち and notes that 逕 can replace 径 in the path sense. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 彳 at the left, a narrow shape of three strokes (it has no card here), and at the right a 又-like shape over [土](土.md), "earth, soil." Picture a straight path of earth cutting across a round field: the path is the field's diameter. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 彳, the 又-like shape or 土 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケイ | on | [直径](../words/直径.md) ちょっけい — diameter; [半径](../words/半径.md) はんけい — radius |
| みち | kun | 径 みち - road; path; street; lane; passage, the fourth spelling after 道, 途 and 路; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| こみち | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict's only entry reading 径 as こみち is the company name 径書房 こみちしょぼう, not a common word |
| さしわたし | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict spells さしわたし, "length; diameter; distance across," 差し渡し, not with 径 |
| ただちに | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict spells ただちに, "at once; immediately; right away; without delay," 直ちに, not with 径 |

In 直径, 直 reads チョク and the ク becomes a small っ before ケイ, so the word is ちょっけい; 径 supplies ケイ with no change. In 半径, 半 supplies ハン and 径 supplies ケイ. In the みち entry, 径 writes the whole word みち with no okurigana, the kana ending that can follow a kanji; Daijisen marks this spelling as outside the Joyo readings. The same 径 entry for "diameter" also lists the old kana reading わたり, marked out-dated.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ケイ, with 直径 and 直情径行 as examples. みち, こみち, さしわたし and ただちに are not in the official table. Practice selects ケイ, the reading with linked word articles; みち is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, and こみち, さしわたし and ただちに have no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [直](直.md), "direct; repair," in 直径, and [半](半.md), "half," in 半径.
- [役](役.md), "role; duty," and [彼](彼.md), "he," have the same 彳 at the left. [怪](怪.md), "suspicious; uncanny," has the same right part, with a different shape at the left. All are different kanji.
- [経](経.md), "pass through; sutra," and [軽](軽.md), "lightweight; slight," also read ケイ and have a similar right part. They are different kanji; quiz options for this card do not include them.
- [道](道.md), "road, way," [路](路.md), "path; route," and [途](途.md), "route; course," overlap the sense "path," and JMdict lists all three with 径 as spellings of みち. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "path" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ケイ senses: 径路 けいろ, a rarely used kanji form of 経路, "course; route; path; channel," 山径 さんけい, "mountain path," 小径 しょうけい, "path; lane," which JMdict marks formal or literary, and 捷径 しょうけい, "shortcut; expedient."
