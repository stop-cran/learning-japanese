# 克 — overcome

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 儿 (no. 10) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

克 expresses overcoming or mastering something. In [克服](../words/克服.md)
こくふく, the challenge can be a difficulty, illness, fear, or weakness.

The modern shape has a [古](古.md)-like top above the two leg-like strokes
儿, the indexing radical.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture that top standing firmly on two legs
to overcome a difficulty. This is a memory aid, not a historical derivation
or a phonetic analysis.

Both sourced readings remain in the metadata. Reading questions select
コク only. The rarity label concerns the standalone spelling 克つ, not
the pronunciation かつ. The self-control expression 己に克つ illustrates
the native reading without making every use of 勝つ interchangeable with 克つ.

## Notes

- [古](古.md) lacks the lower legs; [兄](兄.md) has 口 above its legs,
  while [元](元.md) has two horizontal lines above them.
- [勝](勝.md) also concerns prevailing. Learn [勝つ](../words/勝つ.md)
  かつ for winning a contest. 克つ appears in self-control uses such as
  己に克つ; this is a useful spelling association, not an absolute division
  of every meaning between 克 and 勝.
