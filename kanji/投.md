# 投 — throw

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

投 is the throwing character in [投げる](../words/投げる.md) なげる.
投票 とうひょう, "voting," offers another useful setting: English likewise speaks of casting a vote.
That comparison helps with the word's meaning, not its historical origin.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the hand-side 扌 sending an object past the right-hand 殳 shape.
The image is a modern memory aid.

## Notes

ボールを投げる is "throw a ball." 投げる names sending it through the air;
[打つ](../words/打つ.md) うつ names hitting it.

[抜](抜.md), [折](折.md), and [抱](抱.md) share 扌.
Distinguish their right-hand parts rather than guessing from the shared hand component.
The lower right of 投 has a 又 shape beneath its upper part.
