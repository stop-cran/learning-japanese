# 激 — intense; excite

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

激 is intensity, and by extension stirring something up. [激しい](../words/激しい.md) はげしい is intense or violent, [急激](../words/急激.md) きゅうげき is sudden or drastic, [刺激](../words/刺激.md) しげき is a stimulus, [感激](../words/感激.md) かんげき is being profoundly moved, [激励](../words/激励.md) げきれい is encouragement and [激増](../words/激増.md) げきぞう is a sharp increase.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses: strong momentum, as in 激戦 げきせん, "fierce battle; hard-fought battle," 激痛 げきつう, "sharp pain; acute pain," 激動 げきどう, "turbulence; turmoil," and 過激 かげき, "extreme; radical"; stirring up energy or feeling, as in 激励, 感激 and 憤激 ふんげき, "fury; outrage"; and, standing in for the character 戟, pricking, as in 刺激. JMdict lists 刺戟 as a rarely used spelling of 刺激. A separate Daijisen entry covers the informal prefix げき, "extremely; terrifically; super," as in 激うま, 激やせ and 激レア.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 氵, "water," at the left, [白](白.md), "white," above [方](方.md), "direction, method," in the middle, and 攵 standing at the far right; 方 and 攵 together make [放](放.md), "release." Imagine water released in a flash of white: a raging torrent. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 白, 方 or 放 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ゲキ | on | [刺激](../words/刺激.md) しげき — stimulus; stimulation; [急激](../words/急激.md) きゅうげき — sudden; sharp; drastic; [感激](../words/感激.md) かんげき — being profoundly moved; deep emotion; [激励](../words/激励.md) げきれい — encouragement; spurring on; [激増](../words/激増.md) げきぞう — sharp increase; sudden rise |
| はげ.しい | kun | [激しい](../words/激しい.md) はげしい — intense; violent |

In 刺激, 刺 supplies シ. In 急激, 急 supplies キュウ. In 感激, 感 supplies カン. In 激励, 励 supplies レイ. In 激増, 増 supplies ゾウ. In 激しい, 激 writes はげ and しい is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ゲキ, with 激動, 感激 and 激する げきする, "to intensify," as examples, and はげしい, with 激しい and 激しさ. The verb 激する has no word article yet. The Daijisen character entry marks ゲキ with （慣）, its label for a customary reading. The informal prefix げき, which JMdict lists with the meaning "extremely; terrifically; super," is not part of the practice set.

Practice selects ゲキ and はげ.しい, the two readings with linked word articles. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [刺](刺.md), "pierce; prick," in 刺激, [急](急.md), "urgent, sudden," in 急激, [感](感.md), "feeling; sensation," in 感激, [励](励.md), "encourage; be diligent," in 激励 and [増](増.md), "increase," in 激増.
- [撃](撃.md), "shoot; strike," also reads ゲキ. [滴](滴.md), "drip; drop," and [漱](漱.md), "rinse the mouth; gargle," share the water shape at the left. All three are different kanji.
- [劇](劇.md), "drama; play," reads ゲキ too, and JMdict records 劇 as a rarely used older spelling in 急劇 きゅうげき, 劇しい はげしい and 劇痛 げきつう, the old spellings of 急激, 激しい and 激痛. The Daijisen entry for 劇 also lists はげしい, as in 劇薬 げきやく, "powerful medicine; strong poison." 劇 is therefore kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [暴](暴.md), "violence; outburst," [強](強.md), "strong," [怒](怒.md), "anger," and [鋭](鋭.md), "sharp; keen," overlap the senses "violent," "strong," "enraged" and "sharp" in JMdict and KANJIDIC2. [刺](刺.md), "pierce; prick," overlaps the pricking sense, and [摩](摩.md), "rub; chafe," shares the KANJIDIC2 gloss "chafe." [励](励.md), "encourage; be diligent," and [勧](勧.md), "recommend; encourage," overlap the sense of spurring someone on. These are kept out of the quiz options. Do not treat "intense," "violent" or "encourage" as exclusive to any one character.
