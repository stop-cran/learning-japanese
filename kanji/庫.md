# 庫 — storehouse

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

庫 concerns storage or a place for keeping things. 金庫 きんこ is a safe or strongbox, and 冷蔵庫 れいぞうこ is a refrigerator. The complete word identifies what kind of storage is meant; 庫 does not make every example a separate warehouse building.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 广 over 車 and imagine vehicles sheltered in a storage building. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- 庫裏 くり gives the on reading ク. It does not demonstrate the separate native reading くら.
- The Joyo table, the official guide to standard-use kanji and readings, lists コ and ク for 庫 but not くら. JMdict also records 蔵 and 倉 for the word くら. Retain 庫 くら for recognition, not as an everyday spelling recommendation; being outside the table does not by itself establish rarity.
- 庫裏 belongs to temple vocabulary; its kitchen and living-quarters senses are not general names for every household kitchen.
- Compare [車](車.md), vehicle: the surrounding 广 changes both the complete shape and its meaning.
