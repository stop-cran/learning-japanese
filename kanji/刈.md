# 刈 — mow; reap

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

刈 concerns cutting growing things: mowing grass, clipping hair, or reaping crops.
The native verb [刈る](../words/刈る.md) かる retains a cutting action; it is not
a general word for every method of harvesting.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the two crossed strokes on the
left as growth to be clipped beside the upright knife form 刂 on the right.
The indexing radical is [刀](刀.md); 刂 is its right-hand form. This shape
association is a memory aid, not a reconstruction of the character's history.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ガイ | on | 刈除 がいじょ — removal; cutting off; mowing |
| カイ | on | 刈除 かいじょ — an alternative reading of the same word |
| か.る | kun | [刈る](../words/刈る.md) かる — mow; clip; reap |

Focus practice on かる. The two on readings are retained for recognition:
JMdict records both がいじょ and かいじょ for 刈除, without a spelling or sense
restriction separating them. This does not establish how often either is used.
In 刈る, 刈 supplies か and る is the written verb ending.

## Common words

- [刈る](../words/刈る.md) かる — mow; clip; reap
- 稲刈り いねかり — rice reaping; rice harvesting

The object helps with the English translation: 草を刈る (くさをかる) is mowing
grass, 髪を刈る (かみをかる) is clipping hair, and 稲を刈る (いねをかる) is
reaping rice plants. See the linked verb article for complete examples.

## Notes

- [切](切.md), "cut," has a broader range. Compare 刈's crossed left strokes
  and separate upright 刂 with 切's left part and bent 刀 on the right.
- [乃](乃.md), "thereupon; possessive no," [凹](凹.md), "concave; sunken," and
  [凸](凸.md), "convex; protruding," do not mean mowing or reaping.
- Learn 刈る as a godan verb: 刈ります (かります), 刈った (かった),
  刈らない (からない). These inflections do not add new dictionary readings.
