# 忘 — forget

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

忘 is the character for forgetting. [忘れる](../words/忘れる.md) わすれる can describe losing something
from memory or leaving an item behind. 忘れ物 わすれもの is an item left behind.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** [亡](亡.md) sits above [心](心.md); imagine a thought slipping out of the
heart-and-mind area. That story is a present-day memory aid, not a claim about how 忘 was created.

## Notes

[忙](忙.md) also contains 亡, but places the heart radical as 忄 on the left.
In 忘, 心 is underneath. [念](念.md) and [怒](怒.md) have the same lower 心 but different tops.
Do not infer "forget" from 心 alone.
