# 浮 — float

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

浮 concerns floating or coming to the surface. [浮く](../words/浮く.md) うく supplies a basic
physical example. 浮かぶ (うかぶ) also means float, and can describe an idea coming to mind.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 子-shaped lower right floating beneath the small
slanting strokes above it. This is only a picture suggested by the modern shape.

## Notes

木は水に浮きます。 (きはみずにうきます。) — Wood floats on water.
沈む (しずむ), "sink," is the useful physical contrast.
[学](学.md) and [字](字.md) also contain 子-shaped lower parts, but neither has the left-hand water strokes.
