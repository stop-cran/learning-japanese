# 輸 — transport; convey

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

輸 concerns transporting or conveying. 輸入 names importing, 輸出 exporting, and 輸送 transport itself.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 車 on the left beside the 俞-shaped right side as goods stacked for conveyance. This memory picture is not a historical derivation or evidence of a phonetic component.
