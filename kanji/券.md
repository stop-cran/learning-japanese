# 券 — ticket; coupon

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

券 identifies a ticket, coupon, or certificate. 入場券 にゅうじょうけん is an admission ticket, while 割引券 わりびきけん is a discount coupon. The preceding part of a compound tells you what the ticket is for.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the spreading upper strokes above the blade-shaped 刀 at the bottom, as though cutting out a ticket.

The classical radical 刀 appears in its full bottom form here, not the right-hand variant 刂.

## Notes

- 券 by itself does not tell you whether something is a travel ticket, an admission ticket, or a coupon. Read the whole label.
- The bottom 刀 is useful for recognizing the character, but the mnemonic is not a claim about how tickets were historically made.
