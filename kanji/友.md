# 友 — friend

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 又 (no. 29) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

友 is commonly explained as two hands (又 repeated, in the oldest forms) reaching out together, the idea being people who help each other or act in step.
The modern shape is a squashed version of that double hand, and the details are debated, so take the picture as the usual explanation rather than
a certain fact. It means "friend", "companion".

## Common words

- [友達](../words/友達.md) ともだち — friend
- 友人 ゆうじん — friend (more formal)
- 親友 しんゆう — close friend
- 友情 ゆうじょう — friendship

## Notes

In daily speech the kun reading appears almost only inside 友達; on its own とも is literary. Look-alikes are [右](右.md) and [左](左.md), which differ
in the lower part and mean "right" and "left", and 反 (opposite).
