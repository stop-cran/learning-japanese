# 拡 — expand; enlarge; widen

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

拡 is making something wider or larger: expanding a range, enlarging an image, widening a road. [拡充](../words/拡充.md) かくじゅう is expansion and strengthening; JMdict glosses it "expansion." [拡大](../words/拡大.md) かくだい is "expansion; extension," and also "magnification; enlargement" and "escalation; spread." [拡張](../words/拡張.md) かくちょう is "expansion; extension; enlargement"; JMdict also gives the computing sense "escape; ESC."

The Daijisen character entry, which marks 拡 as a sixth-grade school kanji, gives two senses for カク: making a range wider, as in 拡散 かくさん, "spreading; disseminating; scattering," 拡幅 かくふく, "widening (of a road)," and 拡声器 かくせいき, "megaphone; bullhorn; loudhailer," besides 拡充, 拡大 and 拡張; and short for 拡張, as in 軍拡 ぐんかく, which JMdict marks as an abbreviation, "military expansion; arms buildup." It gives the kun readings ひろげる and ひろがる.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the form of 手, "hand," at the left, and [広](広.md), "wide, spacious," at the right. Imagine two hands pushing the walls of a room to make it wider still. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 手 or 広 contributes meaning or sound, although 広 is read コウ, ひろ.がる, ひろ.げる and ひろ.める, readings KANJIDIC2 also lists for 拡. Historical formation is not established here.

In 拡充, 拡大 and 拡張, 拡 supplies カク, and 充 supplies ジュウ, 大 supplies ダイ and 張 supplies チョウ, with no sound change. In 拡がる, 拡 writes ひろ and がる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 拡げる uses the same ひろ with げる. JMdict lists these spellings without a tag after 広がる and 広げる.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カク, with 拡大, 拡張 and 拡声器 as examples. コウ, ひろ.がる, ひろ.げる and ひろ.める are not in the table. Practice selects カク, the reading with linked word articles. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid. The table also shows the older form 擴 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [充](充.md), "allot; fill," in 拡充, [大](大.md), "big, large," in 拡大, and [張](張.md), "stretch tight; assert," in 拡張.
- [払](払.md), "pay," and [拝](拝.md), "worship; bow in respect," have the same 扌 at the left. [底](底.md), "bottom; base," has 广 around its other strokes, as 広 does. All are different kanji.
- [展](展.md), "unfold; expand," [膨](膨.md), "swell; expand; inflate," [広](広.md), "wide, spacious," [張](張.md), "stretch tight; assert," [伸](伸.md), "stretch; lengthen," [増](増.md), "increase," [大](大.md), "big, large," [散](散.md), "scatter," [敷](敷.md), "spread; lay out," and [及](及.md), "reach; extend to; and," overlap the senses of 拡 and its words: expanding, enlarging and widening, stretching and reaching in 拡がる, spreading and scattering in 拡散 and 拡げる, and growth in 拡大; 広 writes the usual spelling of ひろがる and ひろげる, and 大 and 張 appear in 拡大 and 拡張. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "expand" or "spread" as exclusive to any one character.
