# 算 — calculate; reckon

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

算 concerns calculation, counting or reckoning. 計算 names calculation itself, while 予算 concerns a budget or advance cost estimate; a process and the planned amount are not the same noun.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the bamboo-shaped top, the 目-shaped middle and the open lower strokes; imagine looking over a calculation laid out on a board. This is a visual mnemonic, not an account of ancient calculating rods.

The indexing key is [竹](竹.md), no. 118, appearing as ⺮ at the top.

## Notes

- 計算する means calculate; for example, 費用を計算する ひようをけいさんする, calculate the cost. See the linked [計算](../words/計算.md) article for the distinction from counting individual items.
- JMdict marks 算盤 そろばん as usually written in kana. Kanjipedia's separate word entry also confirms the abacus and profit/loss-reckoning senses; it is evidence for that whole word, not for a new everyday standalone noun そろ.
- Compare [具](具.md), tools or ingredients, and [真](真.md), true or genuine. Similar stacked strokes do not make them alternative spellings of calculation.
