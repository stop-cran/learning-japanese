# 敷 — spread; lay out

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 攴 (as 攵, no. 66), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

敷 is spreading something out flat, and it turns up in words for places that are laid out, such as rooms and plots. [座敷](../words/座敷.md) ざしき is a tatami room or formal Japanese room, [敷く](../words/敷く.md) しく is to lay out or spread underneath, [敷地](../words/敷地.md) しきち is a site, plot or grounds and [風呂敷](../words/風呂敷.md) ふろしき is a furoshiki, a wrapping cloth.

The Daijisen character entry gives フ the sense of spreading something out flat so that it covers the whole, as in 敷設 ふせつ, "laying (a railroad, pipes, naval mines, etc.); construction," and 敷衍 ふえん, "expatiation; enlargement (e.g. on a point); elaboration; amplification." It also groups words in which 敷 is read しき, or じき after another element, such as 敷居 しきい, "threshold (esp. one with grooves for sliding doors); sill," 敷金 しききん, "(security) deposit; caution money," 屋敷 やしき, "residence; estate; grounds; premises; mansion," 河川敷 かせんじき, "flood plain; river terrace; riverside area; area between river and river bank," 座敷, 敷地 and 風呂敷.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 甫 above [方](方.md), "direction, method," in the left part, and 攵 at the right. Imagine spreading a cloth out flat and tapping it smooth with a tool (攵). This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that any part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| -し.き | kun, bound form | [座敷](../words/座敷.md) ざしき — tatami room; formal Japanese room; [敷地](../words/敷地.md) しきち — site; plot; grounds; [風呂敷](../words/風呂敷.md) ふろしき — furoshiki; wrapping cloth |

In 敷く, 敷 writes し and く is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 座敷, 座 supplies ザ and 敷 reads しき; in 風呂敷, 敷 reads しき after ふろ. In 敷地, 敷 reads しき and 地 supplies チ. After another element, 敷 can voice to じき, as in 桟敷 さじき, "dress circle (of a theatre); (first) balcony; box," and 河川敷. The dash in -し.き marks a bound form, one that appears inside compounds; it is not pronounced. The ふ at the start of 風呂敷 belongs to 風, which is read フ there, not to 敷. In 敷設, 敷 supplies フ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows フ, with 敷設 as an example, and しく, with 敷く, 敷石 and 屋敷, and prints 桟敷 with さじき in brackets. It has no separate row for しき. Practice selects し.く and -し.き, the kun readings with linked word articles. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so し.く supplies the しく question; -し.き stays in the reading table without a separate question. フ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [座](座.md), "sit; seating," in 座敷, [地](地.md), "ground, land," in 敷地, [風](風.md), "wind; style," in 風呂敷 and the 呂 in 風呂敷, which does not have a kanji card here. 敷く is 敷 with okurigana only.
- 攵, the key at the right, also appears in [放](放.md), "release," [敬](敬.md), "respect; honor," and [敗](敗.md), "failure; defeat." 放 has [方](方.md), "direction, method," too. All are different kanji.
- [布](布.md), "cloth; distribute," [普](普.md), "universal; widespread," [散](散.md), "scatter," and [床](床.md), "floor; bed," overlap the sense "spread" that 敷 and its words carry, and 床 also overlaps the futon in 敷く, "to spread out (e.g. a futon); to lay out"; 布 is also read し.く and shares フ with 敷. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "spread" as exclusive to any one character.
