# 率 — rate; lead; frank

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 玄 (no. 95) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

率 is a rate, read リツ, and also leading or being plain-spoken, read ソツ; the verb 率いる ひきいる means to lead. [率](../words/率.md) りつ is a rate or proportion, [率直](../words/率直.md) そっちょく is frank or candid, [確率](../words/確率.md) かくりつ is probability and [能率](../words/能率.md) のうりつ is efficiency. JMdict glosses the noun 率 りつ, which is also used as a suffix, "rate; ratio; proportion; percentage."

The Daijisen character entry splits the readings. For ソツ it gives four senses: leading a whole group, as in 率先 そっせん, "taking the initiative," 引率 いんそつ, "leading; guiding; commanding," and 統率 とうそつ, "command; lead; generalship; leadership"; following, as in 率由 そつゆう, "following (a precedent, former example, etc.)"; being as-is, as in 率直 and 真率 しんそつ, "honesty; sincerity; frankness"; and suddenness, as in 軽率 けいそつ, "rash; thoughtless; careless; hasty; imprudent." For リツ it gives one sense, the share of a part against the whole, as in 比率 ひりつ, "ratio; proportion; percentage," 勝率 しょうりつ, "winning percentage," 税率 ぜいりつ, "tax rate," and 百分率 ひゃくぶんりつ, "percentage."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 玄 at the top centre, a pair of small strokes on each side of it, and [十](十.md), "ten," at the bottom. Imagine a leader at the top with a small stroke on each side like followers, standing above a ten-mark that counts out the rate. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 玄 or 十 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソツ | on | [率直](../words/率直.md) そっちょく — frank; candid |
| リツ | on | [率](../words/率.md) りつ — rate; proportion; [確率](../words/確率.md) かくりつ — probability; likelihood; [能率](../words/能率.md) のうりつ — efficiency; work rate |
| シュツ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; the しゅつ of 出塁率 しゅつるいりつ belongs to 出 and does not establish this reading of 率 |
| ひき.いる | kun | 率いる ひきいる - to lead; to spearhead (a group); to command (troops); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 率直, 率 supplies ソツ, which shortens to そっ before チョク, the reading of 直. In 確率, 確 supplies カク, and in 能率, 能 supplies ノウ; 率 stays リツ in both, and the word 率 on its own is りつ. In 率いる, 率 writes ひき and いる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ソツ, with 率先, 引率 and 軽率 as examples; リツ, with 比率, 能率 and 百分率; and ひきいる, with 率いる. シュツ is not in the official table. Practice selects ソツ and リツ, the readings with linked word articles; ひき.いる is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, and シュツ has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [直](直.md), "direct; repair," in 率直, [確](確.md), "certain," in 確率 and [能](能.md), "ability; capacity," in 能率. The word 率 contains only this kanji.
- [卒](卒.md), "graduate; finish," also has 十 at the bottom and is also read ソツ and シュツ; JMdict lists 卒 in the rarely used spellings 軽卒 and 卒直 of 軽率 and 率直. [律](律.md), "law; rhythm," and [栗](栗.md), "chestnut," are also read リツ. All are different kanji.
- [比](比.md), "compare; ratio," [割](割.md), "split; proportion," [令](令.md), "command; decree," [命](命.md), "life; command," [将](将.md), "commander; impending," [導](導.md), "guide; conduct," and [直](直.md), "direct; repair," overlap the senses "ratio," "proportion," "command," "lead" and "direct" that 率 and its words carry; 将 also has the kun reading ひきい.る, and 直 appears in 率直. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "rate" or "lead" as exclusive to any one character.
