# 望 — hope

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 月 (no. 74) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

望 is useful for wishes, hopes and things someone wants to happen.
[望む](../words/望む.md) のぞむ is the verb "hope for; desire"; 希望 きぼう is "hope; wish."
The desired outcome is not a statement that it will definitely occur.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** find 亡 and 月 above 王 and imagine someone looking up with a wish.
This is an invented scene for remembering the written arrangement, not a historical analysis.
