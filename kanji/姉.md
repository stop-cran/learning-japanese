# 姉 — older sister

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

姉 means an older sister. The everyday native word is [姉](../words/姉.md) あね; the polite family expression お姉さん has its own reading, おねえさん.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture [女](女.md) on the left beside a tall hanging shape on the right: an older sister hanging up a coat. This is a mnemonic, not historical evidence.

## Common words

- [姉](../words/姉.md) あね — older sister
- お姉さん おねえさん — older sister, politely; also an address form
- 姉妹 しまい — sisters

## Notes

[妹](妹.md) is a younger sister. The difference is relative age within the sibling relationship, not adulthood versus childhood.

Use あね for your own older sister when speaking to someone outside the family; お姉さん is common for the listener's older sister or as a family address form. For the ordinary word "mother," use [母](母.md) はは, not 姉.
