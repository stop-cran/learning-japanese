# 銃 — gun; firearm

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

銃 is a gun. On its own, [銃](../words/銃.md) じゅう is glossed in JMdict as "gun; firearm; small arms (e.g. rifle, pistol, etc)." KANJIDIC2 gives "gun" and "arms," Kanjipedia describes 銃 as じゅう。つつ。武器の一つ。鉄砲。 (a gun; a kind of weapon), and Digital Daijisen as 弾丸を発射する小型の武器 (a small weapon that fires bullets).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is [金](金.md), "gold, money," and on the right is [充](充.md), "allot; fill," made of 亠 over [允](允.md), "permit; sincerity." Imagine a metal barrel being filled with a charge before it can fire. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 金 or 充 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ジュウ only, with 銃砲 じゅうほう ("guns; firearms"), 銃弾 じゅうだん ("bullet (from a rifle)") and 小銃 しょうじゅう ("rifle; small arms"). KANJIDIC2 and Digital Daijisen also give the kun reading つつ, which Kanjipedia lists outside the table.
- Digital Daijisen gives かね as a name reading; KANJIDIC2 gives no name readings.

This card is excluded from meaning choices:

- [砲](砲.md) (“cannon; gun”): KANJIDIC2 lists “gun” for both 銃 and 砲, the archaic word つつ (JMdict 2864475) is written 銃 or 砲, and Digital Daijisen defines 銃 by contrast with 砲, as the general term for guns of smaller calibre.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 剣 (“sword; double-edged blade”), 矢 (“arrow; wedge”), and 盾 (“shield; buckler”); none means a gun or firearm.
