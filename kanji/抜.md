# 抜 — extract

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

抜 concerns taking something out or something coming out. In [抜く](../words/抜く.md) ぬく,
an object is extracted, as when a plug is pulled from a socket. Other uses concern removal or omission.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture 扌 tugging something out through the crossing strokes on the right.
This illustrates the modern outline without claiming a historical derivation.

## Notes

プラグを抜く means "unplug it"; プラグが抜ける means "the plug comes out."
In this pair, を marks the object being removed and が marks what comes out.

[投](投.md), [押](押.md), and [抱](抱.md) all share 扌, but only the right side of 抜 has this
particular crossing outline. The resemblance is in the component and layout, not in the meanings.
