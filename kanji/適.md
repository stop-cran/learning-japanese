# 適 — suitable

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

適 points to fitting a purpose or situation. [適切](../words/適切.md) てきせつ means appropriate;
適度 てきど concerns a suitable degree or amount. 快適 かいてき instead describes comfort or pleasantness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine checking that the small cross-and-box shape fits inside the larger box above 辶.
The idea of a good fit is a mnemonic, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

適切 is a な-adjective: 適切な言葉 (てきせつなことば), "appropriate words."
Suitability is not the same as personal comfort, so do not replace 適切 with 快適 automatically.
[週](週.md) has 周, not the taller boxed shape in 適.
