# 毒 — poison; venom

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 毋 (no. 80) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

毒 concerns poison or something harmful. 毒 どく is the independent noun. 気の毒 きのどく is an established expression for pitiful or unfortunate and for feeling sorry for someone; it is not a literal description of poisoned air.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the layered upper strokes above the 母-like lower portion and imagine a warning label on a poisonous substance. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical derivation.

The source indexes 毒 under 毋, no. 80. Its lower portion looks 母-like in print; preserve the canonical indexing label rather than replacing it with a visible-shape guess.

## Notes

- The linked 毒 article distinguishes a poisonous substance from [害](害.md), harm or damage. Something harmful need not be a poison.
- 気の毒 is a complete expression. Its compassionate use should not be inferred by combining the broad English glosses spirit and poison.
