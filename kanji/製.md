# 製 — manufacture; made of

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

製 means to make or manufacture, and as a suffix it says what something is made of or where it was made. [作製](../words/作製.md) さくせい and [製作](../words/製作.md) せいさく are both manufacture or production, [製品](../words/製品.md) せいひん is a manufactured product and [製造](../words/製造.md) せいぞう is manufacturing.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense, making things, as in 製紙 せいし, "papermaking; paper manufacture," 製図 せいず, "drafting; drawing," 製鉄 せいてつ, "iron manufacture," 製粉 せいふん, "milling; grinding into flour," and 木製 もくせい, "wooden; made of wood." Its noun entry adds the suffix use: after a place name it shows where something was made, as in 日本製 にほんせい, "made in Japan; Japanese-made," and after a material or means it shows what something is made of, as in 鉄製 てっせい, "made of iron."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [制](制.md), "system; regulate," at the top and [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment," at the bottom. Imagine a garment cut to a fixed pattern and made the same way every time. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 制 or 衣 contributes meaning or sound, although 制 is also read セイ. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| セイ | on | [作製](../words/作製.md) さくせい — manufacture; production; [製作](../words/製作.md) せいさく — manufacture; production; [製品](../words/製品.md) せいひん — manufactured product; [製造](../words/製造.md) せいぞう — manufacturing |

In 作製, 作 supplies サク and 製 reads セイ; 製作 reverses the order, with 製 first and 作 supplying サク. In 製品, 品 supplies ヒン, and in 製造, 造 supplies ゾウ. [制作](../words/制作.md) せいさく is read like 製作 but is written with 制 instead; JMdict glosses it "work (film, book)" first, and it has its own word article.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セイ, with 製造, 製鉄 and 鉄製 as examples. Practice selects セイ, the only reading.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [作](作.md), "make, produce," in 作製 and 製作, [品](品.md), "goods, refinement," in 製品 and [造](造.md), "make; construct," in 製造.
- [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment," is the key at the bottom, and [袋](袋.md), "bag; sack; pouch," also has 衣 at the bottom, under 代. [制](制.md), "system; regulate," is the top part and is also read セイ, as are [世](世.md), "world, generation," and [声](声.md), "voice." All are different kanji.
- [作](作.md), "make, produce," [造](造.md), "make; construct," [産](産.md), "produce; give birth," and [創](創.md), "originate; create," overlap the senses of making and producing that 製 and its words carry, and [築](築.md), "build; construct," [建](建.md), "build, construct," [構](構.md), "build; stance," and [工](工.md), "craft, construction," are close neighbours in the sense of making things. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "manufacture" as exclusive to any one character.
