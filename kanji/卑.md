# 卑 — lowly; despicable

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 十 (no. 24) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

卑 is about being low: low in rank or status, low in character (mean or vulgar), and looking down on others. It can also be a humble word for oneself, and Kanjipedia adds the sense near or familiar, as in 卑近 ひきん, “common; simple”. KANJIDIC2 lists “lowly”, “base”, “vile”, “vulgar” and “despise”. The word [卑怯](../words/卑怯.md) ひきょう means “cowardly; craven” or “unfair; mean; sneaky; dirty; dastardly”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** in writing order, 卑 starts with a short 丿 stroke, then [田](田.md), "rice field," then a second 丿 stroke, and ends with [十](十.md), "ten," which supplies the last two strokes. Picture someone stooping low over a rice field, quietly counting to ten. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 田 or 十 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ヒ, with 卑近, 卑屈 and 卑下, and the kun readings いやしい, いやしむ and いやしめる. Kanjipedia also lists ひくい (low) outside the table (外); it is not a KANJIDIC2 reading, so it has no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 卑, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These eleven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [低](低.md) (“low; lower in height”), [下](下.md) (“down, below”) and [劣](劣.md) (“inferior; worse”): 卑 means low or inferior in rank, value or character; compare 下品 げひん, “vulgar; indecent; coarse; crude”.
- [汚](汚.md) (“dirty; defile”) and [悪](悪.md) (“bad, evil”): 卑しい いやしい and 卑怯 can both mean “dirty”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “vile” for 卑.
- [謙](謙.md) (“humble; modest”): 卑 can be a humble word for oneself, and 卑下 ひげ is “self-abasement; self-depreciation; humility”.
- [凡](凡.md) (“commonplace; mediocre”) and [近](近.md) (“near”): the near-and-familiar sense, as in 卑近, “common; simple”.
- [粗](粗.md) (“coarse; rough; crude”) and [貧](貧.md) (“poor; impoverished”): 卑しい can also mean “coarse; crude” or “shabby; poor-looking”.
- [軽](軽.md) (“lightweight; slight”): 卑しむ いやしむ can mean “to look down on”, much like 軽んじる かろんじる, “to look down on; to make light of”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 勇 (“courage; bravery”), 賢 (“wise; intelligent”), and 鋭 (“sharp; keen”); courage, wisdom and sharpness are positive qualities, and none means lowly or despicable.
