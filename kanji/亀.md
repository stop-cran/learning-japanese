# 亀 — turtle; tortoise

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 乙 (no. 5) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

亀 is the **turtle or tortoise** character. The native noun [亀](../words/亀.md) (かめ) is not limited to land-dwelling tortoises.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a small head above a divided shell, with a long hooked tail below. This is a way to remember the modern shape, not an account of its historical construction.

KANJIDIC2 indexes this form under **乙**, the bent-stroke radical. Use the long bent, hooked stroke as a lookup cue; the radical label is not the animal's meaning.

## Common words

- [亀](../words/亀.md) (かめ) — turtle; tortoise
- 海亀 (うみがめ), also ウミガメ — sea turtle
- 陸亀 (りくがめ), also リクガメ — tortoise

In these two compounds, かめ becomes がめ. Learn their complete readings, not うみかめ or りくかめ.

## Notes

- [電](電.md), "electricity," has 雨 above its lower part; [申](申.md), "humbly say," has a simpler box with a through-stroke; [角](角.md), "angle; corner; horn," has a different lower outline. None is the turtle/tortoise character.
- 亀裂 (きれつ) is not an animal name. Dictionaries explain the cracking through a turtle-shell-pattern comparison; do not translate every compound containing 亀 literally as a turtle.
- English "turtle" and "tortoise" do not require different readings of 亀. Use a more specific animal name when the distinction matters.
