# 体 — body, form

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

体 commonly means the body, and in other words it concerns a form or a substance.
Start with 体 からだ and [体力](../words/体力.md), the body's strength and stamina.

Its visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and [本](本.md).
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a person's body supported by the base-marked tree 本.
This describes a learning image, not the historical formation of 体.

## Notes

[休](休.md) has 木 on the right; 体 has 本, with an additional short horizontal stroke.
Without 亻, the right-hand part is [本](本.md), a different kanji.
The full reading of 体育 is たいいく; do not infer a reading merely from how quickly someone says it.
