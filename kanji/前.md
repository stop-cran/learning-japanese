# 前 — front, before

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (as 刂, no. 18) · **Phonetic:** none established · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

前 means "front", "before" and "previous" in space and time. The modern form has 䒑 on top, 月 below and 刂 (knife) on the right. In its
older form (歬) it is commonly explained as a foot (止) on a boat (舟), moving forward, and the knife part was added later. The history is debated, so treat
the boat-and-foot picture as a mnemonic-quality explanation. 前 is the opposite of [後](後.md).

## Notes

- **前 vs 先:** 前 is "front/before" in place and time (駅の前, 三年前); [先](先.md) is "ahead/previous in sequence" (先月, 先生).
- **前 vs 後:** 前 (before, front) and 後 (after, back) are opposites; 前 has the knife 刂 on the right, 後 has the walking radical 彳 on the left.
- Time words: 二時間前 にじかんまえ (two hours ago); 前 follows the time amount, unlike English "before" with a clause.
