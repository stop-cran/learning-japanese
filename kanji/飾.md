# 飾 — decorate; adorn

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 食 (no. 184) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

飾 is decorating: making something look fine by adding ornaments, and also putting things on show. KANJIDIC2 lists “decorate”, “ornament”, “adorn” and “embellish”. Kanjipedia's first sense is かざる。きれいにする。 (decorate; make pretty), and its second is とりつくろう。よそおう。 (keep up appearances; dress up). In JMdict, the first sense of the verb [飾る](../words/飾る.md) かざる is “to decorate; to ornament; to adorn”, its second is “to display; to exhibit; to put on show; to arrange”, and its fourth is “to affect (a manner); to keep up (appearances); to embellish; to dress up; to be showy; to be pretentious” (entry 1357210). The noun [飾り](../words/飾り.md) かざり is “decoration; ornament; trimmings” (entry 1357160).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is 飠, marked as a variant of [食](食.md), "eat, food," and on the right is 𠂉 over 巾, shapes that look like a little hook with a strip hanging from it. Imagine a feast table on the left and a ribbon hung on a hook beside it to decorate the room. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 飠, 𠂉 or 巾 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- In 飾る, 飾 writes かざ and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the noun 飾り ends in り instead. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so かざ.る supplies the one question for that stem, shown as かざる; かざ.り is retained from KANJIDIC2.
- Joyo lists ショク and かざる, with 飾り as an example under かざる. Kanjipedia lists no readings outside the table.
- KANJIDIC2 records しか as a name reading; Digital Daijisen gives the name readings あきら and よし.

These four cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [装](装.md) (“attire; outfit; pretend”): the first sense of 飾る is “to decorate; to ornament; to adorn”, the first sense of 装う よそおう (JMdict 1402280) includes “to adorn; to decorate”, and JMdict glosses 装飾 (1402340) as “ornament; decoration”. Kanjipedia's second sense of 飾 includes よそおう, and the 装 card lists よそお.う.
- [掲](掲.md) (“put up; display; publish”) and [揮](揮.md) (“wield; command; display”): the second sense of 飾る is “to display; to exhibit; to put on show; to arrange”, which overlaps “display” in both titles; KANJIDIC2 also lists “display” for 掲.
- [華](華.md) (“flower; splendor; showiness”): the fourth sense of 飾る includes “to be showy”, and KANJIDIC2 lists “showy” and “ostentatious” for 華.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 汚 (“dirty; defile”), 削 (“shave; sharpen; pare”), and 壊 (“break; destroy; demolish”); none means decorating or adorning.
