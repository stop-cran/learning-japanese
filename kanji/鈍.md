# 鈍 — dull; blunt

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鈍 concerns bluntness, dullness or slow responsiveness. 鈍い is an adjective, 鈍る a verb for becoming dull, and 鈍化 names a dulling or slowing process.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the rounded 屯-shaped strokes beside 金 as a tool whose edge is no longer sharp. This is only a modern-shape mnemonic.

The canonical radical 金 is the narrow left-hand part.

## Notes

- 鈍る has both にぶる and なまる in the inspected family without a rarity flag on either. Both are intransitive; 鈍らす にぶらす is a separate transitive verb. Dictionary recognition does not establish equal frequency.
- The source notation なまく.ら gives the complete word 鈍ら なまくら, with written ら after 鈍. JMdict marks its blunt-edge sense usually written in kana. It is a na-adjective, unlike 鈍い.
- にぶ- is retained as a bound source reading and illustrated inside the complete word 鈍らす, not taught as a free-standing word.
- A separate usually-kana entry records [のろい](../words/のろい.md), also written 鈍い. The [鈍い](../words/鈍い.md) article teaches にぶい; that additional word identity does not change the pinned character-reading list or add a new reading row here.
