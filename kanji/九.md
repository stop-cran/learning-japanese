# 九 — nine

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 乙 (no. 5) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The origin of 九 is debated. It is often explained as a picture of a bent arm or elbow, borrowed for the number nine by sound, but other explanations exist. Treat any such story as a mnemonic.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キュウ | on | 九 きゅう (spoken number), 九十 きゅうじゅう, 九人 きゅうにん, 九州 きゅうしゅう |
| ク | on | [九月](../words/九月.md) くがつ, 九時 くじ |
| ここの | kun | [九日](../words/九日.md) ここのか (the 9th day / nine days) |

## Common words

- [九月](../words/九月.md) くがつ — September
- [九日](../words/九日.md) ここのか — the 9th; nine days
- 九つ ここのつ — nine (things)
- 九時 くじ — nine o'clock

## Notes

Both きゅう and く are common. く is the go-on reading (九月 くがつ, 九時 くじ) and きゅう the kan-on reading (九十 きゅうじゅう, 九人 きゅうにん); each belongs to particular words.

The reading く sounds like 苦 (suffering), so it is sometimes avoided for its [inauspicious association](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%BF%8C%E6%95%B0-32261). This can reflect concern about luck, cultural convention or consideration for others, not necessarily personal superstition.

This is not a general rule for choosing readings. Learn each word's established reading: 九人 きゅうにん (nine people), but 九月 くがつ (September) and 九時 くじ (nine o'clock).

Look-alikes: 力 (strength; also 2 strokes, but its vertical stroke ends in a hook rather than bending right), [七](七.md) and 丸 (round, which is 九 plus a dot).
