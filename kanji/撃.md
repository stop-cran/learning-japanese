# 撃 — shoot; strike

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

撃 concerns hitting something hard, and especially firing at a target. [攻撃](../words/攻撃.md) こうげき is an attack or offensive. Other dictionary words include 反撃 はんげき, "counterattack; counteroffensive; counterblow," 衝撃 しょうげき, "impact; shock; impulse," and 目撃 もくげき, "witnessing; observing; sighting," where the Daijisen character entry describes an impression striking the senses. The verb 撃つ うつ, "to shoot (a gun, person, etc.); to fire," is the usual word for firing at something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [車](車.md), "car, vehicle," and 殳 across the top and [手](手.md), "hand," at the bottom. Imagine a hand striking a rolling cart. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 車 or 殳 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 攻撃, 撃 supplies ゲキ and 攻 supplies コウ. In 撃つ, 撃 writes う and つ is okurigana. Practice selects ゲキ. う.つ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with 撃つ, but that word has no linked article yet, so the reading is not selected for questions. [打つ](../words/打つ.md) うつ, "hit; strike," is a different word that sounds the same; it is written with 打. This is a teaching selection, not a claim that う.つ is invalid, obsolete or rare.

## Notes

- [攻](攻.md), "attack; go on the offensive," is the other kanji in 攻撃. Both characters concern hitting at a target, so their meanings overlap.
- [打](打.md), "strike," shares the reading う.つ, and 打つ and 撃つ are homophones. 打つ is to hit or strike, whereas 撃つ is to shoot or fire. They overlap in "strike," so do not treat the word as exclusive to either character.
- [投](投.md), "throw," [殺](殺.md), "kill," and [設](設.md), "establish; provide," also contain 殳, but each has a different remaining shape and a different meaning.
