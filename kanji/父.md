# 父 — father

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 父 (no. 88) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

父 is usually explained as a hand holding a stick or axe-like tool, an old symbol of the male head of the household who works and leads.
The exact original object is debated. It means "father", and it is the key of a few related characters.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| フ | on | 父母 ふぼ, 祖父 そふ, 父兄 ふけい |
| ちち | kun | 父 ちち (my father), 父親 ちちおや, 父の日 ちちのひ |

## Common words

- [お父さん](../words/お父さん.md) おとうさん — father (polite, also "dad")
- 父 ちち — (my) father
- 祖父 そふ — grandfather
- 父母 ふぼ — father and mother, parents

## Notes

For one's own father, speaking to outsiders, use 父 ちち; for someone else's father use お父さん or お父様. In お父さん the 父 is read とう, a
reading not listed among the standard on or kun readings: it belongs to the whole word. It is shortened to 父さん とうさん, and children say パパ.

The paired kanji is [母](母.md) (mother). Do not confuse 父 with 交 (mix) or 文 (writing); they share some strokes but are different characters.
