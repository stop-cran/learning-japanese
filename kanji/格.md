# 格 — rank; character

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

格 concerns rank, standing, or character. In [性格](../words/性格.md) (せいかく), it helps describe a person's personality.
In 資格 (しかく), the concern is qualifications, not whether someone has a pleasant personality.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a rank label in the small square at the bottom of the right-hand 各, attached beside 木.
This is a visual aid, not a proposed etymology.

## Notes

In ごうかく, ごう belongs to 合 and かく belongs to 格: do not infer a reading from the wrong half of a compound.
[校](校.md) also has 木 on the left, but its right side is 交 rather than 各.
English "character" here means qualities or nature, not a written letter.
