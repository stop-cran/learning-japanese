# 的 — target; adjectival suffix

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 白 (no. 106) · **JLPT:** N3 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

的 can mean a **target**, as in [的](../words/的.md) まと. It also supplies テキ in compounds such as [目的](../words/目的.md), purpose, and in an adjective-forming suffix. These uses are related learning associations, not a rule that every 的 means a physical target.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [白](白.md) beside 勺. Imagine a white target with a mark beside it. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not an account of its historical formation.

## Common words

- [的](../words/的.md) まと - a target, or the focus of attention
- [目的](../words/目的.md) もくてき - purpose; objective

## Notes

- The suffix often makes a な-adjective: 科学的な説明 (かがくてきなせつめい), "a scientific explanation," and 科学的に説明する (かがくてきにせつめいする), "explain scientifically." Learn the resulting word and construction; do not attach 的 indiscriminately to any noun.
- In 目的, 的 is part of the established noun, not a suffix that turns 目 into an adjective.
- [約](約.md), promise or approximately, has 糸 rather than 白 on the left. [均](均.md), even or equal, has 土 and a different right-hand shape.
