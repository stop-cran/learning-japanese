# 抵 — resist; collide

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

抵 concerns pushing against something or running into it. [抵抗](../words/抵抗.md) ていこう is resistance or opposition. Other dictionary words include 抵触 ていしょく, "infringement (of a law, treaty, etc.); contravention; running afoul," 抵当 ていとう, "mortgage; security; hypothec; pledge," and 大抵 たいてい, "mostly; ordinarily; usually," which is usually written in kana. The Daijisen character entry lists "equivalent to" for 抵当 and "roughly" for 大抵, so learn those two as whole words rather than from the resisting sense.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the side form of [手](手.md), "hand," at the left and 氐 at the right, which looks like [氏](氏.md), "clan; family name," with a stroke added below. Imagine a hand pushing against a wall. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氐 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- [抗](抗.md), "defy; oppose," is the other kanji in 抵抗. Both characters concern acting against something, so their meanings overlap.
- [低](低.md), "low; lower in height," and [底](底.md), "bottom; base," also read テイ and have a similar-looking right-hand part. Check the left side: 亻 in 低, 广 in 底, 扌 in 抵.
- [抱](抱.md), "embrace," shares 扌 at the left but has a different right-hand shape and reads ホウ.
