# 分 — minute, part

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

分 combines 八 (eight, here the shape of something splitting apart) and 刀 (sword, knife): cutting something into parts. This is the commonly
given explanation; as a mnemonic, "八 + 刀 = a knife dividing". The core idea is "divide", which gives "part", "share", "minute" (a part of an hour) and, via being separated clearly, "understand" (分かる).
