# 塗 — paint; coat

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

塗 concerns applying paint, plaster, cream or another coating. 塗る ぬる is the action of applying or spreading it; 塗装 とそう is painting or coating.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine liquid from the left-hand 氵 spreading across the 余-like strokes and coating 土 below.

The indexing radical is 土 at the bottom. The visible 氵 on the upper left is a water-shaped component, not this character's canonical indexing radical.

## Notes

- 塗る is a transitive godan verb despite ending in る: 塗ります ぬります and 塗った ぬった. It is not an ichidan verb with the form ぬます.
- The まみれる entry is intransitive and usually written in kana. The written example documents that reading; it is not presented as the preferred spelling for new sentences.
- 塗り is a noun for a coating or lacquering. Distinguish it from the complete dictionary form of the verb 塗る.
