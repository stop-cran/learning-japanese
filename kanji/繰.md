# 繰 — reel; turn pages; repeat

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

繰 is drawing thread off a reel, winding or spinning it, and by extension turning something over one piece after another: leafing through pages, counting off days, and doing something again and again. [繰り返す](../words/繰り返す.md) くりかえす is to repeat; JMdict glosses it "to repeat; to do over again." The word article teaches "repeat; do again." The verb 繰る くる alone is glossed "to reel; to wind; to spin (thread)," "to turn (pages); to flip through (a book); to leaf through (a book); to consult (a dictionary); to refer to (an encyclopedia)," "to count (e.g. the days)" and "to open one-by-one; to close one-by-one (e.g. shutters)."

Digital Daijisen has no character entry for 繰, so this card gives no Daijisen character senses. Daijisen does have headwords for the whole words く・る (繰る), くり (繰り) and くり‐かえ・す (繰り返す).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left. In the right half, [品](品.md), "goods, refinement," sits above [木](木.md), "tree, wood." Imagine thread wound off a reel in loops, again and again. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸, 品 or 木 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソウ | on, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 and Kanjipedia (as an outside reading) list ソウ. JMdict has 繰糸 そうし, "reeling (silk)," where そうし is restricted to the spelling 繰糸 (くりいと is given for both spellings), but it is not an ordinary word checked for practice, and the Joyo table has no on reading for 繰 |
| く.る | kun | [繰り返す](../words/繰り返す.md) くりかえす — repeat; do again |

In 繰り返す, 繰 supplies く, り is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji, and [返](返.md), "give back; repay," supplies かえ with す as okurigana. くりかえす is the whole-word reading of the verb, not a reading of 繰 alone. 繰る くる is the plain verb written with 繰 and る. 繰り出す くりだす, "to draw (a thread); to let out (e.g. a rope)," "to head out as a group; to set out in large numbers; to flock; to sally forth," "to send out one after another; to dispatch" and "to lunge (a spear); to unleash (a punch)," is a further compound. 繰り has no JMdict entry of its own.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only くる, with 繰る and 繰り返す, and no on reading. Practice selects く.る, the reading of the linked word article. ソウ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and is not used in any ordinary word checked here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [返](返.md), "give back; repay," in 繰り返す, supplying かえ.
- [復](復.md), "restore; repeat," and [再](再.md), "again; repeat," overlap "repeat." [返](返.md) is the other kanji in 繰り返す and is read かえ.す. [巻](巻.md), "roll; book volume," overlaps winding on a reel. [回](回.md), "revolve; times," [転](転.md), "turn; roll," [循](循.md), "follow; cycle," [環](環.md), "ring; loop; surround," [巡](巡.md), "go around; patrol; tour," [周](周.md), "circumference; circuit," and [度](度.md), "degree, number of times," overlap turning, circling, counting and recurring. All are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [犬](犬.md), "dog," [雨](雨.md), "rain," and [駅](駅.md), "station," are common starter kanji with simple shapes. They are read ケン, いぬ; ウ, あめ; and エキ, and share no reading or meaning with 繰. [来](来.md), "come," is also read く.る and is never offered as a wrong answer, nor are the many kanji read ソウ.
