# 材 — timber; material

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

材 concerns material available for use, including timber. 材料 ざいりょう means materials or ingredients, while [木材](../words/木材.md) もくざい specifically names lumber or timber. Materials need not be wooden.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside 才 and imagine selecting a piece of timber as material for a project. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

- 材料 can also concern material or evidence for a decision, as the linked article explains; not every use names a tangible ingredient.
- [木](木.md) can mean a tree or wood as a substance. 材 emphasizes material or timber, so the two characters are useful comparisons, not reliably wrong answers for one another.
- Keep 材 separate from [村](村.md), village: 才 and 寸 on the right help distinguish the written forms.
