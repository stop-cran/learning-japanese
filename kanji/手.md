# 手 — hand

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64; written 扌 at the left of characters) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

手 is a pictograph of a hand with fingers spread; the oldest forms show a palm with several fingers, and later forms simplified them into horizontal strokes with a vertical hook.
It means "hand", and also "person who does something" (歌手 singer, 運転手 driver) and "means, method" (手段 しゅだん).
As a key it takes the shape 扌 at the left, in verbs of hand actions such as 持つ, 打つ, 押す, 拾う.

- [手](../words/手.md) て — hand

- 手紙 てがみ — letter

## Notes

上手 じょうず and 下手 へた are special whole-word readings: 手 is ず in 上手 (ズ is listed as a rare reading) and た in 下手 (へ + た). The same spellings are also read うわて and したて ("upper hand", "lower position"), with different meanings. 

Look-alikes: 毛 (hair, fur) has a similar frame but its last stroke bends to the right, whereas 手 ends in a vertical hook; [千](千.md) (thousand), [年](年.md) (year) and [午](午.md) (noon) are other characters built from horizontals and a vertical stroke.
