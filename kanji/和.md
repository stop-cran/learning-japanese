# 和 — harmony; Japanese style

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

和 can signal harmony or peace, as in [平和](../words/平和.md) へいわ. It also marks Japanese style in words such as 和食 わしょく, "Japanese food."
These are different uses of the character: 和食 does not mean "peaceful food."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the left-hand 禾 and right-hand 口 fitting comfortably beside each other: harmony. This is a visual association, not a derivation.

## Notes

The indexing radical is 口 on the right, despite the prominent 禾 on the left.
[私](私.md) and [秋](秋.md) also begin with 禾; distinguish their right-hand parts.
For 和やか before a noun, use な: 和やかな会話 (なごやかなかいわ), "a friendly conversation."
