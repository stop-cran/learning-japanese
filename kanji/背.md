# 背 — back; stature

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (as the lower 月 shape, no. 130) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

背 refers to the back of the body and also to stature or height.
In [背中](../words/背中.md) せなか, it names the bodily back; in 背が高い
(せがたかい), "be tall," it concerns height. The full expression tells you which sense is intended.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use 北 above the 月-shaped body component as a visual label on
someone's back. This is a modern association, not a historical analysis.
