# 宜 — suitable; proper; good

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宜 is fitting well, being just right for the situation. [宜しく](../words/宜しく.md) よろしく is glossed in JMdict "well; properly; suitably," and, in its best-known use, "best regards; please remember me; please treat me favorably (favourably); please take care of; please do"; JMdict marks every sense of it as usually written in kana. The adjective 宜しい よろしい, "good; OK; all right; fine; very well," is also usually written in kana, and JMdict marks it as honorific.

For ギ, the Daijisen character entry gives one sense, fitting moderately well, with the examples 機宜 きぎ, "opportuneness; timeliness; opportunity; occasion"; 時宜 じぎ, "right time; appropriate time"; 辞宜 じぎ, "bow; bowing (and greeting)," which JMdict lists after 辞儀 as a rarely used kanji form; 適宜 てきぎ, "suitable; appropriate; fitting; proper"; and 便宜 べんぎ, "convenience; accommodation; advantage; benefit; expediency."

Its kun readings are むべ, うべ and よろしい; KANJIDIC2 does not list むべ or うべ, and JMdict's 宜 むべ, also read うべ (an obsolete kana usage), is archaic, "truly; indeed." Among hard-to-read words it lists 宜乎 うべなるかな; JMdict spells that expression 宜なるかな むべなるかな, gives うべなるかな as a rarely used kana form, and marks it formal or literary and usually written in kana, "quite right; indeed; truly; well said." It also lists 禰宜 ねぎ, "senior priest (serving under the chief priest and associate chief priest)," a Shinto term in which 宜 supplies ぎ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the roof-shaped 宀 at the top and [且](且.md), "moreover; also," below it. Imagine a stack of shelves that fits just right under a roof. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 宀 or 且 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ギ | on | 適宜 てきぎ - suitable; appropriate; fitting; proper; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| よろ.しい | kun | 宜しい よろしい - good; OK; all right; fine (honorific; usually written in kana); the word article よろしい is written in kana and does not declare 宜, so this reading is not selected for questions here |
| よろ.しく | kun | [宜しく](../words/宜しく.md) よろしく — best regards; please treat favorably |

In 宜しく, 宜 writes よろ and しく is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 宜しい uses the same よろ with しい. In 適宜 and 便宜, 宜 supplies ギ after 適 テキ and 便 ベン.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ギ, with 適宜 and 便宜. よろ.しい and よろ.しく are outside the official table; Kanjipedia marks よろ.しい as outside it. Practice nevertheless selects よろ.しく, because 宜しく is the word article that declares 宜; JMdict notes that the word is usually written in kana. ギ and よろ.しい are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 宜しく is 宜 with okurigana only. JMdict also lists 宜敷く, an ateji spelling marked as a rarely used kanji form, and 宣しく, a search-only form.
- [且](且.md), "moreover; also," is the lower part of 宜. [組](組.md), "group; assemble," has 且 at the right, and [査](査.md), "investigate; inspect," has 且 at the bottom. Compare [宣](宣.md), which has 亘 rather than 且 under the same 宀. All are different kanji.
- [良](良.md), "good; favorable," [善](善.md), "good; virtue; handle well," [適](適.md), "suitable," [妥](妥.md), "appropriate; settled," [正](正.md), "correct, proper," and [好](好.md), "liking; favorable," overlap the senses "suitable," "proper" and "good" that 宜 carries and 宜しく's "please treat me favorably"; 良, 善 and 好 are also read よ.い, and JMdict lists 宜い as a rarely used spelling of よい. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "good" or "suitable" as exclusive to any one character.
