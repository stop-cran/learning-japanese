# 輝 — shine; sparkle

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

輝 is about shining and sparkling. KANJIDIC2 lists “radiance”, “shine”, “sparkle”, “gleam” and “twinkle”. In [輝く](../words/輝く.md) かがやく, JMdict's first sense is “to shine; to sparkle; to twinkle; to glitter; to glisten” and its second is “to light up (with happiness, hope, etc.); to beam; to gleam; to glow”. Kanjipedia gives the sense かがやく。かがやき。かがやかしい。

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [光](光.md), "light; radiance," stands on the left, with ⺌, which KanjiVG marks as a form of [小](小.md), "small, little," over the shape 兀. On the right is [軍](軍.md), "army; troops," with 冖 over [車](車.md), "car, vehicle." Picture an army's vehicles on the right catching the light from the left until they sparkle. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 光 or 軍 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists キ, with 輝石 and 光輝, and かがやく, with 輝く among its examples. JMdict glosses 輝石 きせき “pyroxene; augite” (entry 1224030).
- Kanjipedia lists てる outside the Joyo table.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings あき, あきら, さき, てる, ひ and ひかる; the Daijisen character entry gives the names あきら, てる and ひかる.

These five cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [光](光.md) (“light; radiance”): KANJIDIC2 lists “radiance” for 輝, and JMdict glosses 光輝 こうき “brightness; splendour; splendor” (entry 1272940).
- [照](照.md) (“illuminate; shine”): KANJIDIC2 lists “shine” for 輝; Kanjipedia lists てる for 輝 outside the Joyo table, and the 照 card lists て.る.
- [射](射.md) (“shoot; shine into”): KANJIDIC2 gives 射 “shine into”, which overlaps 輝's “shine”.
- [艶](艶.md) (“gloss; luster; allure”): JMdict's first sense of 艶 つや is “gloss; luster; lustre; shine; sheen; polish” (entry 1177680).
- [華](華.md) (“flower; splendor; showiness”): KANJIDIC2 lists “shine” and “luster” for 華 as well as “shine” for 輝, and JMdict's 光輝 gloss includes “splendor”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 曇 (“cloud over; cloudy weather”), 汚 (“dirty; defile”), and 枯 (“wither; dry up”); none means to shine or sparkle.
