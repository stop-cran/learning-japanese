# 二 — two

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 二 (no. 7) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

二 is an indicative character: two horizontal lines for the number two. Between [一](一.md) and [三](三.md), it follows the same pattern of counting lines.
Write the top line shorter than the bottom one.
It is also radical no. 7, and appears in 五 (five), 云 and 元.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ニ | on | 二月 にがつ, 二回 にかい, 二十 にじゅう, 二階 にかい |
| ジ | on | rare; not needed at N5 |
| ふた | kun | [二人](../words/二人.md) ふたり, 二日 ふつか |
| ふた.つ | kun | [二つ](../words/二つ.md) ふたつ (two things) |

Special readings: 二人 ふたり, 二日 ふつか (2nd day / two days), 二十日 はつか (20th day), 二十歳 はたち (age 20).

## Common words

- [二つ](../words/二つ.md) ふたつ — two (things)
- [二人](../words/二人.md) ふたり — two people
- 二月 にがつ — February
- 二十 にじゅう — twenty

## Notes

Compare [一](一.md) and [三](三.md) (one and three lines).
The on reading ニ is the usual counting reading; ジ is rare. The kun reading is ふた-, never に, when counting things: ふたつ, ふたり, ふたたび.
