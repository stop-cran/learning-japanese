# 泉 — spring; water source

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

泉 concerns a spring or source of water. The native noun 泉 is いずみ. 温泉 おんせん means a hot spring and can also name a hot-spring resort.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 白 above 水 and imagine clear water emerging from the ground. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical derivation.

The source indexes 泉 under 水, no. 85. The water-shaped part is below 白, not a left-hand 氵.

## Notes

- 泉 does not say that the water is hot or safe to drink. 温泉 supplies the hot-spring word; do not infer those properties from 泉 alone.
- English spring also names the season, but [春](春.md) is a different character and is not a defensible river-source meaning for 泉.
- The linked noun article distinguishes a natural water source from an ornamental spraying fountain. English fountain is not a license to replace every 噴水 ふんすい with 泉.
