# 爆 — burst open; explode

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

爆 concerns bursting, popping, or exploding. Not every use describes a large or dangerous explosion.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture [火](火.md) beside [暴](暴.md): imagine the dense right-hand strokes suddenly bursting outward.

## Notes

- 爆ぜる is an intransitive ichidan verb: 爆ぜて はぜて, not はぜって.
- 爆発 can also describe an outburst of emotion. The character is not restricted to bombs or military contexts.
- The right-hand shape is [暴](暴.md), whose violence/outburst meaning can overlap in English. It is a useful visual comparison, not a safe wrong answer for every 爆 quiz.
