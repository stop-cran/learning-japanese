# 子 — child

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 子 (no. 39) · **Phonetic:** none (not a phonetic compound) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

子 is commonly explained as a pictograph of a small child, with a large head and arms stretched out. The oldest forms are
more pictorial; the modern shape has lost most of that detail. Its basic meaning is "child", but its role depends on the word:

- **Child, offspring, or person:** native [女の子](../words/女の子.md) (おんなのこ), "girl," and
  Sino-Japanese [王子](../words/王子.md) (おうじ), "prince," retain a child or family relationship.
  [女子](../words/女子.md) (じょし), "girls; women," can also describe adults.
- **Chinese-derived nominal ending:** in [帽子](../words/帽子.md) (ぼうし), "hat,"
  [椅子](../words/いす.md) (いす), "chair," and [様子](../words/様子.md) (ようす), "appearance; situation,"
  子 helps form an established noun rather than meaning a literal child.
- **Technical particle sense:** in [電子](../words/電子.md) (でんし), "electron," 子 contributes the idea of a small particle,
  not a child or merely a semantically light noun ending.

"Nominal ending" means a suffix used in a noun. The preceding element may already be a noun: 帽 itself denotes a head covering.
This use does not require the object to be small, does not supply a separate English word "thing," and is not a rule for
freely adding 子 to Japanese words. The established word determines the meaning and reading.

子 is also the first sign of the Chinese zodiac (the rat, ね), used in old time-keeping and in dates such as 子年 ねどし.
As a component it is the key of characters about children and learning, for example [学](学.md).

## Notes

ツ is listed as a rare on reading you will not need at this level. The on readings シ and ス appear mainly in fixed compounds,
while こ is the everyday reading of the word itself. ス is a later Sino-Japanese reading (唐音, Tō-on), not the native こ.
In women's names 子 is usually read こ (花子 はなこ).

Look-alikes: [学](学.md) contains 子 under its crown and means "study"; 孫 (grandchild) also contains 子. 女 ([女](女.md)) and 子 together make 好 (like).

Usage and readings: [Digital Daijisen: 子](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%AD%90-3235), especially the separate child/person,
small-particle and nominal-ending senses. The Chinese-derived suffix in 帽子 and 椅子 is also explained in
[Gogen-yurai: 帽子](https://gogen-yurai.jp/boushi/) and [椅子](https://gogen-yurai.jp/isu/).
