# 線 — line; track

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

線 concerns a drawn line, wire or route. Its compounds can name boundaries or railway lines; a line in a railway name is not one individual train.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 糸 beside 泉 and imagine a thread tracing a line away from a spring. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical explanation or a claim that 泉 supplies the sound.

The canonical key 糸, no. 120, appears on the left as ⺯.

## Notes

- 線を引く せんをひく means draw a line. For a queue or row, compare [列](列.md) れつ; English line covers several Japanese words rather than establishing free interchangeability.
- The existing [筋](../words/筋.md) すじ article explains streaks, sinews and the thread of a story. Those meanings are not all senses to transfer automatically to 線 せん.
