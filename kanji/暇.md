# 暇 — free time; leisure; time off

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

暇 is time with nothing that must be done: free time, leisure and time off from work. [暇](../words/暇.md) ひま is free time, or being not busy, and [休暇](../words/休暇.md) きゅうか is a vacation, leave or time off. JMdict's entry for 暇 ひま also lists "time (it takes to do something); time required" and "time off; leave; vacation; holiday."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for カ, time without work or a break between tasks, as in 閑暇 かんか, "leisure," 賜暇 しか, "furlough; leave of absence," 寸暇 すんか, "moment's leisure; free minute," 余暇 よか, "leisure; leisure time; spare time," and 休暇. For ひま it lists 暇人 ひまじん, "person with a lot of free time on their hands; person of leisure; idler; loafer," and 手間暇 てまひま, "time and effort; trouble." Its separate entry for the noun ひま adds a short gap between activities, the time a task needs, free time, time off, and the ending of a tie between master and servant or husband and wife.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [日](日.md), "sun, day," the key, at the left, and at the right a more complex shape with [又](又.md), "again; also; or," at its lower right; the whole right-hand shape has no card yet. Imagine the sun at the left shining on an empty deck chair at the right: a day with nothing to do. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that these parts contribute meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カ | on | [休暇](../words/休暇.md) きゅうか — vacation; leave; time off |
| ひま | kun | [暇](../words/暇.md) ひま — free time; not busy |
| いとま | kun | お暇 おいとま - taking leave (of a visited place); leaving; going home; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 休暇, 休 supplies キュウ and 暇 supplies カ. The word 暇 ひま is the kanji alone, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows some kanji. 暇 can also be read いとま on its own, "(spare) time; free time; leisure," which JMdict marks as usually written in kana alone; in お暇, the polite prefix お is written in kana before 暇 read いとま.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カ, with 余暇, 休暇 and 寸暇 as examples, and ひま, with 暇 and 暇な時. いとま is not in the official table. Practice selects カ and ひま, the readings with linked word articles; いとま is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [休](休.md), "rest," in 休暇. The word 暇 contains only this kanji.
- [暖](暖.md), "warm; warm up," and [映](映.md), "reflect, project," also have 日 at the left, and 暖 also has 13 strokes; [歌](歌.md), "song, sing," is also read カ. All are different kanji.
- [休](休.md), "rest," [時](時.md), "time, hour," [悠](悠.md), "leisurely; distant," and [去](去.md), "leave, elapse," overlap the senses "time off," "time," "leisure" and "leave" that 暇 and its words carry; 休 appears in 休暇, 暇 ひま can mean the time a task requires, and お暇 おいとま means taking one's leave. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "free time" or "leave" as exclusive to any one character.
