# 吹 — blow

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

吹 is blowing: wind moving, a person blowing air, or someone playing a wind instrument.
The subject and object show the use of [吹く](../words/吹く.md) ふく: 風が吹く (かぜがふく), "the wind blows," but フルートを吹く (フルートをふく), "play the flute."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine air blowing from the left-hand 口 toward the right-hand 欠. This labels a visual memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

[吸](吸.md) draws air inward; 吹 sends it outward or describes blowing wind.
The sound ふく also belongs to 拭く, "wipe," a different verb with a different written form.
Do not replace the right-hand 欠 with the 及 of 吸.
