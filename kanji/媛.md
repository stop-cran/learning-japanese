# 媛 — lady; beautiful woman

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

媛 can refer to a woman, traditionally a beautiful woman. In 才媛 (さいえん), the whole word praises her ability or learning, not her appearance.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare the right side with [暖](暖.md) and [緩](緩.md). The left side tells these forms apart: 女 in 媛, 日 in 暖 and thread in 緩.

## Notes

[授](授.md) begins with a hand rather than 女. The name 愛媛 (えひめ), Ehime Prefecture, preserves ひめ. For the standalone word “princess,” learn the spelling [姫](姫.md) rather than substituting 媛. Compare the left sides of [暖](暖.md), [緩](緩.md) and 媛 before reading. [美](美.md) expresses beauty, but 才媛 singles out ability. [女](女.md) and [婦](婦.md) can both refer to a woman without implying talent or noble rank.
