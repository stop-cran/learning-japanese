# 誓 — vow; swear

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

誓 means to vow or swear. KANJIDIC2 lists “vow”, “swear” and “pledge”, and Kanjipedia explains it as ちかう and ちぎる: promising something to gods, Buddhas or people (約束する). In [誓う](../words/誓う.md) ちかう, JMdict glosses the verb “to swear; to vow; to take an oath; to pledge”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [折](折.md), "fold," sits on top; its left part 扌 is a variant of [手](手.md), "hand," and its right part is the shape 斤. Below it is [言](言.md), "say; words," with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it. Picture someone folding their hands together and speaking the words of a promise aloud. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 折 or 言 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists セイ and ちかう only. ゼイ is not a KANJIDIC2 reading; Kanjipedia marks it as outside the Joyo table.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 誓. Digital Daijisen gives the name reading ちか; this is name use, not a quiz reading.

These two cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [契](契.md) (“pledge; vow; agreement”): KANJIDIC2 gives 契 “pledge”, “promise” and “vow”, and gives 誓 “vow” and “pledge”. Kanjipedia also explains 誓 with ちぎる, the kun reading KANJIDIC2 gives for 契 (ちぎ.る).
- [約](約.md) (“promise; approximately”): Kanjipedia explains 誓 as making a promise (約束する), and JMdict glosses 約束 やくそく as “promise” first (entry 1538130).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 断 (“sever; refuse”), 壊 (“break; destroy; demolish”), and 盗 (“steal”); none means to vow or swear.
