# 才 — talent

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

才 is useful for thinking about talent and gifted ability. [才能](../words/才能.md) さいのう
means talent or ability; 天才 てんさい can name a genius or prodigy.
KANJIDIC2 also records an age-counting use, so the talent meaning is not its only function.

The indexing radical is 手, but the written character is the three-stroke 才, not a visible full 手 or a left-side 扌.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a tiny three-stroke tool in the hands of someone talented.
This is a mnemonic, not a claim about derivation.

## Notes

才能 names an ability, whereas 天才 can label the gifted person.
Do not read 才 as て merely because 手 is its indexing radical.

[手](手.md) has more strokes. [木](木.md) has a rightward branch absent from 才,
and [千](千.md) arranges its short upper stroke differently. These simple shapes deserve a close look.
