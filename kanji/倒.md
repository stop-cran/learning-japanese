# 倒 — topple; collapse

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

倒 is useful for something losing its upright position.
[倒れる](../words/倒れる.md) たおれる describes something falling over; 倒す たおす describes bringing it down.
転倒 てんとう names a fall or toppling.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the person on the left leaning toward the densely packed right side and losing balance.
The right side has the visible shape [到](到.md); no phonetic role is established here.
The story is a memory aid, not an origin claim.

## Notes

Compare 木が倒れる (きがたおれる), "a tree falls over," with 木を倒す (きをたおす), "fell a tree."
The radical 人 appears as 亻.
[例](例.md) and [側](側.md) have similar outer components but different centers; [借](借.md) has a different right side altogether.
Not every English use of "fall" is expressed with 倒れる.
