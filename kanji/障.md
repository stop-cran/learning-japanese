# 障 — obstruct; hinder

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (as left-hand 阝, no. 170) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

障 concerns hindrance or something that stands between things.
[故障](../words/故障.md) こしょう is a breakdown or malfunction;
[障害](../words/障害.md) しょうがい can be an obstacle or hindrance,
or a disability or impairment in the relevant context.

[障子](../words/障子.md) しょうじ is a shoji sliding screen or partition.
A barrier can separate or protect, not only interfere; the character
dictionary includes prevention and protection senses too.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the left 阝 beside
[章](章.md). Imagine the left-side bank blocking a page-shaped 章 from
moving past it. This is an invented memory scene, not an origin account.
The left 阝 represents 阜, not the 邑 radical found on the right of
some other characters.

## Notes

- [章](章.md), chapter, is the right component, not another spelling of
  obstruction. [童](童.md), child, shares a similar upright upper shape.
  [陽](陽.md), sunshine, shares 阜 on the left but has a different right side.
- Harm or interference overlaps [害](害.md); prevention or guarding
  overlaps [防](防.md). These direct meaning comparisons are excluded
  from choices, not treated as identical in every compound.
- The disability or impairment sense belongs to a specific use of 障害.
  It does not make every occurrence of 障 a label for a person or turn
  the screen 障子 into a medical term. The linked article separates
  the applicable senses rather than imposing one gloss on all contexts.
