# 怪 — suspicious; uncanny

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

怪 concerns suspicion, unexplained strangeness, and uncanny beings. [怪しい](../words/怪しい.md)
あやしい can describe a doubtful story without suggesting anything supernatural.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** put an uneasy feeling in the left-hand 忄 beside the unfamiliar
又-over-土 shape on the right. The indexing radical is 心 in its side form 忄.

## Notes

[性](性.md) also starts with 忄, but has 生 on the right. [経](経.md) shares the 又-over-土 shape,
with 糸 instead of 忄. [忙](忙.md) has the much simpler 亡 on the right.

For 怪 compared with [妖](妖.md), [奇](奇.md), and [疑](疑.md), see
