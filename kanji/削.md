# 削 — shave; sharpen; pare

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

削 concerns shaving or cutting material away. [削る](../words/削る.md) けずる can mean sharpening a pencil by shaving it, reducing an amount, or deleting part of a text. In [削除](../words/削除.md) さくじょ, the focus is deletion or erasure, not necessarily a physical cut.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 肖-shaped left part as a block with small marks above it, being shaved by the blade radical 刂 on the right. This invented scene is a memory aid, not a historical explanation or a verified phonetic analysis.

This card selects サク and けず.る for reading questions. The other two readings remain useful recognition information, not invalid or obsolete pronunciations. The dots mark the written ending: 削る has る outside the kanji, while 削ぐ has ぐ.

## Common words

- [削る](../words/削る.md) けずる — shave; sharpen; cut down
- [削除](../words/削除.md) さくじょ — deletion; erasure
- 削減 さくげん — reduction; cut
- 添削 てんさく — correction of writing

Two practical uses of けずる are 鉛筆を削る (えんぴつをけずる), "sharpen a pencil," and 予算を削る (よさんをけずる), "cut the budget."

## Notes

- The indexing radical is [刀](刀.md); its visible form here is 刂. [消](消.md) also has a 肖-shaped part, but its left side is 氵 rather than the right-side 刂 of 削. Erasing and deleting meanings can overlap; these shapes do not create an absolute semantic boundary.
- The spelling 削る can represent the separate verb はつる as well as けずる. The linked word article teaches けずる. JMdict's "take a percentage" sense belongs to its separate はつる entry, not the けずる lesson.
- 削ぐ そぐ is another verb, not a different spelling of けずる. It also has figurative weakening or dampening uses; JMdict marks those senses as usually written in kana, not every physical use of 削ぐ.
- Compare the right-side 刂 in [前](前.md), [判](判.md) and [刑](刑.md). Their meanings are not shaving or sharpening; a shared radical does not make the words interchangeable.
