# 暑 — hot weather; summer heat; hot season

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

暑 is heat in the surrounding air and the hot season. [暑い](../words/暑い.md) あつい describes hot weather or surroundings; JMdict glosses it “hot; warm; sultry.” [蒸し暑い](../words/蒸し暑い.md) むしあつい adds uncomfortable humidity; JMdict glosses it “humid; sultry.”

Daijisen gives two senses for ショ: heat or hot weather, as in 残暑 ざんしょ, “late summer heat; lingering summer heat”; and the hot season, including the calendar period from 小暑 しょうしょ, “minor heat” solar term (approximately July 7), through 大暑 たいしょ, a “major heat” solar term (approximately July 23). 暑い is about hot surroundings; it is not the spelling for hot objects or liquids, which use 熱い あつい, “hot (to the touch).”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 日, “sun, day,” above 者, “person in a role.” Imagine a person under the summer sun. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either visible part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ショ | on | 残暑 ざんしょ — late summer heat; lingering summer heat; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| あつ.い | kun | [暑い](../words/暑い.md) あつい — hot weather; [蒸し暑い](../words/蒸し暑い.md) むしあつい — humid; sultry |

In 暑い, 暑 writes あつ and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 蒸し暑い, 蒸 writes む and し is okurigana, so 蒸し is むし; 暑 writes あつ, followed by い. The two words share the weather-heat reading, but 蒸し暑い specifically adds humidity. Do not equate 暑い with 熱い.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ショ with 暑気, 残暑 and 避暑, and あつい with 暑い and 暑さ, contrasted with 熱い. JMdict glosses 暑気 as “summer heat; hot weather,” 避暑 as “escaping the summer heat; going somewhere cooler during the summer; summering,” 暑さ as “heat (of the weather); hotness,” and 熱い as “hot (to the touch).” Practice selects あつ.い, the reading with linked word articles. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the linked word 蒸し暑い is [蒸](蒸.md), “steam; sultry heat.”
- [日](日.md), “sun, day,” is at the top of 暑; [者](者.md), “person in a role,” is its lower shape, and [早](早.md), “early,” also contains 日. These make the distractors visually plausible, but their titles are not weather heat and their readings do not match 暑.
- [熱](熱.md), “heat; fever,” can be confused with hot weather but is the spelling used for hot things or liquids; [夏](夏.md), “summer,” overlaps the season; [蒸](蒸.md), “steam; sultry heat,” overlaps 蒸し暑い; [暖](暖.md), “warm; warm up,” [温](温.md), “warm, mild,” and [湯](湯.md), “hot water,” overlap heat or warmth. These cards are excluded as meaning choices; 暑い and 熱い are not interchangeable. [炎](炎.md), “flame,” was checked; it names fire rather than weather heat.
