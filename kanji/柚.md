# 柚 — yuzu citrus

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

柚 writes **yuzu**, [柚](../words/柚.md) ゆず: the citrus tree or its aromatic fruit. 柚子 is another spelling of the same native noun. The English dictionary gloss "citron" should not make you equate yuzu with every fruit called citron.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside [由](由.md). Imagine a yuzu tree next to a box of its fruit. This is a memory aid, not a reconstruction of the character's history.

## Notes

- 柚の木 (ゆずのき) specifies the tree; 柚の皮 (ゆずのかわ) is yuzu peel, used for its aroma in cooking.
- The source's name-use classification does not make the plant noun name-only. This card asks only ゆず; the on-reading examples are recognition information, not additional questions.
- JMdict records older standalone ゆ and ゆう with an outdated-reading flag. Those noun forms do not by themselves establish the on/kun classification of a compound.
- Compare [油](油.md), oil: its left side is 氵, not 木. [相](相.md) has 目 on the right, whereas 柚 has 由, with its vertical stroke projecting above the box.
