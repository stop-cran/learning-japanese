# 滞 — stagnate; stay; be delayed

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

滞 is something held up and not moving on: traffic that does not flow, a payment that is late, and a person who stays on in a place. The linked [渋滞](../words/渋滞.md) じゅうたい is JMdict's "(traffic) congestion; traffic jam; gridlock," and its second sense is "delay; stagnation." The linked [滞在](../words/滞在.md) たいざい is "stay; sojourn."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for タイ. One example of each:

- things stuck in one place and not moving on: 渋滞, and also 滞納 たいのう, "falling behind (with a payment); being in arrears; non-payment";
- stopping in a place: 滞在, and also 滞日 たいにち, "staying in Japan."

More examples are in the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part is the same shape as the kanji [帯](帯.md), "sash; zone." Imagine a band across a stream holding the water back. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound, and 帯 sharing タイ with this card is not offered as an explanation. Historical formation is not established here.

In 渋滞, 渋 supplies ジュウ and 滞 supplies タイ. In 滞在, 滞 supplies タイ and 在 supplies ザイ. The same タイ is in 停滞, 滞納, 滞日, 滞貨 たいか, 滞留 たいりゅう, 沈滞 ちんたい, 延滞 えんたい and 遅滞 ちたい.

In 滞る, 滞 writes とどこお and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. Its second JMdict sense is "to be overdue (of a payment); to fall into arrears; to be outstanding," and the noun 滞り とどこおり is "stagnation; hindrance; delay."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows タイ with 滞在, 滞貨 and 沈滞, and とどこおる with 滞る. Practice selects タイ, the reading of the linked word articles. とどこお.る is shown with a dictionary example and テイ is source-only; neither is asked here, and being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. The table shows the older form 滯 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- In 渋滞, [渋](渋.md), "astringent; sluggish; reluctant," supplies ジュウ. In 滞在, [在](在.md), "exist; be located," supplies ザイ. Other examples above: 停滞 uses [停](停.md), "halt; stopping," 滞納 uses [納](納.md), "pay; deliver; put away; accept," 滞日 uses [日](日.md), "sun, day," 滞貨 uses [貨](貨.md), "goods; freight; currency," 沈滞, "stagnation; inactivity," uses [沈](沈.md), "sink; subside," 延滞, "arrears; (being) overdue; delay (e.g. in payment); procrastination," uses [延](延.md), "prolong; postpone," and 遅滞, "delay; procrastination," uses [遅](遅.md), "late; slow." 滞留 is "stagnation; standstill; accumulation; pile-up" and "stay; sojourn."
- [漁](漁.md), "fishing; fishery," [涼](涼.md), "cool, refreshing," and [湖](湖.md), "lake," also have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [停](停.md), [留](留.md), "retain; stay," [止](止.md), "stop, halt," [駐](駐.md), "station; remain," [残](残.md), "remain; leftover," [泊](泊.md), "stay overnight; anchor," [居](居.md), "be present; dwell," [住](住.md), "reside, dwell," and [在](在.md) overlap stopping and staying; [遅](遅.md), [延](延.md) and [渋](渋.md) overlap delay and sluggishness; 停 also shares テイ with 滞. [滑](滑.md), "slide; slippery; smooth," is the opposite of stagnating. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "stagnate," "stay" or "be delayed" as exclusive to any one character.
