# 証 — evidence; proof

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

証 is about evidence and proof. KANJIDIC2 lists “evidence,” “proof,” and “certificate.” Kanjipedia gives あかす and あかしをたてる (to prove; to establish proof), and also the nouns あかし and しるし (proof; a sign). In [証明](../words/証明.md) しょうめい, JMdict's gloss is “proof; testimony; demonstration; verification; certification.” In [保証](../words/保証.md) ほしょう, JMdict's first sense is “guarantee; security; assurance; pledge.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [言](言.md), "say; words," with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it, stands on the left. On the right is [正](正.md), "correct, proper," with [止](止.md), "stop, halt," inside it. Imagine words from the mouth that stop only once they are correct: what is left can stand as proof. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 正 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ショウ only, with the old form 證 in parentheses. Kanjipedia marks あかし as outside the Joyo table (外).
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 証; Digital Daijisen gives あきら, つく and み as name readings, not quiz readings.

These six cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [験](験.md) (“test; verify”): the 験 card lists あかし and しるし among its readings, and JMdict glosses 証明 with “verification.”
- [契](契.md) (“pledge; vow; agreement”) and [保](保.md) (“protect; preserve”): KANJIDIC2 gives 契 “pledge” and 保 “guarantee,” and JMdict's first sense of 保証 is “guarantee; security; assurance; pledge.”
- [徴](徴.md) (“sign; indication; levy”): JMdict entry 1168060 notes that しるし in the sense “symbol; emblem” is also written as 徴, and KANJIDIC2 gives 徴 the meaning “sign” and the kun reading しるし.
- [印](印.md) (“stamp; mark”) and [標](標.md) (“mark; signpost; goal”): JMdict entry 1168060 writes しるし with 印, 標 or 証, and Digital Daijisen's heading is しるし【印／▽標／▽証】; KANJIDIC2 also gives 標 “evidence.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 罪 (“crime; guilt”), 刑 (“criminal punishment”), and 損 (“loss; damage”); none means evidence or proof.
