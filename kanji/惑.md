# 惑 — bewilder; perplex; delude

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

惑 is a mind caught by something so that it cannot judge clearly: being bewildered or perplexed, or leading someone else astray. [迷惑](../words/迷惑.md) めいわく is trouble or a nuisance caused to others; JMdict glosses it "trouble; bother; annoyance; nuisance; inconvenience" and, used as a verb, "to be troubled (by); to be bothered (by); to be inconvenienced (by)."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ワク. The first is a mind so caught by something that it cannot judge correctly, まどう or まどわす, with examples such as 困惑 こんわく, "bewilderment; perplexity; embarrassment"; 疑惑 ぎわく, "doubt; misgivings; distrust; suspicion"; 幻惑 げんわく, "fascination; bewitchment; entrancement; dazzlement"; 魅惑 みわく, "fascination; captivation; charm"; 誘惑 ゆうわく, "temptation; allurement; lure"; and 迷惑. The second is moving around within a fixed area, as in 惑星 わくせい, "planet." Its kun reading is まどう, the same as KANJIDIC2. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

Daijisen names 思惑 おもわく, "expectation; anticipation; prediction," among words with a hard-to-guess reading; JMdict marks the spelling 思惑 as ateji, kanji chosen for their sound, and also lists 思わく. A separate JMdict entry, 思惑 しわく, is a Buddhist term, "perceptive mental disturbances." A separate Daijisen headword わく treats 惑 as a Buddhist term for what gives rise to delusion; JMdict has it as 惑 わく, "klesha," marked Buddhist.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 或 at the top, the same shape as at the right of [域](域.md), "region; range," and [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the bottom. Imagine a heart wandering in a strange region, not knowing which way to turn. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 或 or 心 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ワク | on | [迷惑](../words/迷惑.md) めいわく — nuisance; inconvenience |
| まど.う | kun | 惑う まどう - to get lost; to lose one's bearings; to be puzzled; to be perplexed; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 迷惑, 迷 supplies メイ and 惑 supplies ワク, with no sound change. In 惑う, 惑 writes まど and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict glosses 惑う "to get lost; to lose one's bearings," "to be puzzled; to be perplexed; to be confused; to be at a loss" and "to be tempted; to be seduced; to be captivated." 惑わす まどわす uses the same まど with わす: "to bewilder; to perplex; to puzzle; to delude; to mislead" and "to tempt; to seduce." In 戸惑う とまどう, "to be bewildered; to be perplexed," 戸 supplies と and 惑 keeps まど; JMdict also lists 途惑う as a rarely used kanji form.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ワク, with 惑星, 迷惑 and 誘惑 as examples, and まどう, with 惑う and 惑い まどい, "delusion; illusion; infatuation; bewilderment; perplexity." Practice selects ワク, the reading with a linked word article. No word article uses まど.う yet; it is illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [迷](迷.md), "get lost; be unsure," in 迷惑. 或, the top of 惑, has no card yet.
- [域](域.md), "region; range," has the same 或 at the right, with 土 at the left. [感](感.md), "feeling; sensation," and [忘](忘.md), "forget," have the same 心 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [迷](迷.md), "get lost; be unsure," [困](困.md), "be in difficulty," [悩](悩.md), "worry; be troubled," [労](労.md), "labor; trouble," [魅](魅.md), "charm; bewitch," and [妖](妖.md), "bewitching; supernatural," overlap the senses "lose one's bearings," "be at a loss," "perplexity," "be troubled," "trouble," "charm" and "bewitchment" that 惑 and its words carry; 迷 appears with 惑 in 迷惑, 困 in 困惑 and 魅 in 魅惑. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "bewilder" or "trouble" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first ワク sense: 惑乱 わくらん, "bewilderment; confusion"; 眩惑 げんわく, "dazzlement; bewilderment; daze"; 蠱惑 こわく, "fascination; glamour; enchantment; seduction"; 当惑 とうわく, "bewilderment; perplexity; embarrassment; confusion"; and 不惑 ふわく, "past forty; following right course." JMdict also glosses 惑星 "dark horse; surprise contender" in a second sense.
