# 隼 — falcon

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

隼 is the **falcon** character. The native bird noun
[隼](../words/隼.md) はやぶさ is usually written in kana.
It especially names the peregrine falcon, but dictionaries also record a
broader falcon use; it is not a name for every bird of prey.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find the bird-shaped radical 隹
above a cross like [十](十.md). Imagine a falcon perched above a cross-shaped
marker. This is a memory aid, not a historical claim that 隼 means "ten birds."
The indexing radical is 隹, not the independent [鳥](鳥.md) character.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シュン | on | 鷹隼 ようしゅん — hawk and falcon; archaic compound, for recognition rather than practice |
| ジュン | on | Qualified source-only reading: character dictionaries record it, but no suitable lexical practice example was established for this lesson |
| はやぶさ | kun | [隼](../words/隼.md) はやぶさ — falcon; usually written はやぶさ or ハヤブサ |

This lesson practices **はやぶさ** only. Both on-readings remain in the full
metadata. ジュン is a documented reading, not an error or an automatically
name-only form. The archaic label above belongs to 鷹隼, not to every possible
use of シュン.

## Common words

- [隼](../words/隼.md) はやぶさ — falcon

The heading is the standard card section, not a claim that the kanji spelling
is more usual than kana. Learn the native noun without substituting シュン or
ジュン for はやぶさ.

## Notes

- [準](準.md), "standard; conform," adds water marks beside the upper
  bird-shaped part and has a lower 十. Its ジュン reading does not by itself
  establish the reading of 隼.
- [集](集.md), "gather," has 木 below 隹, not the 十-like base of 隼.
  Independent [十](十.md) means "ten," not "falcon."
- [鷹](鷹.md) means "hawk; falcon" and has a much more complex shape,
  including 鳥 below. Do not invent an exclusive hawk-versus-falcon naming
  rule from the different kanji.
- Grade-9/name-use metadata does not make the bird noun name-only.
  [Kanjipedia](https://www.kanjipedia.jp/kanji/0003237400) also records a
  figurative brave-person meaning for the character; that is recognition
  information, not the animal word or this lesson's practice sense.
