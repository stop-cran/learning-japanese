# 久 — a long time

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 丿 (no. 4) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

久 points to a long stretch of time. 久しい ひさしい describes a long time having passed, while 久しぶり ひさしぶり describes doing or encountering something after a long interval.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a person leaning back for a very long wait, with one stroke stretching out to the right.

## Common words

- [久しぶり](../words/久しぶり.md) ひさしぶり - after a long time
- [永久](../words/永久.md) えいきゅう - permanence; eternity
- 久しい ひさしい - long in elapsed time

## Notes

- 久しぶり is not a statement that an activity itself lasts a long time: 久しぶりに会う ひさしぶりにあう means meet after a long interval.
- Distinguish 久 from [夕](夕.md) ゆう, evening: 久's final stroke extends to the lower right.
