# 告 — announce; inform

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

告 is about making something known. It appears in [報告](../words/報告.md) ほうこく, "report," and 広告 こうこく, "advertisement."
The verb 告げる つげる means to tell, inform, or announce; it directs attention to the information being conveyed.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the 口 at the bottom as the mouth making an announcement, with the strokes above as a sign attracting attention. This is not historical evidence.

## Notes

Compare the upper part with [古](古.md), which has only 十 above 口.
The upper strokes also resemble [牛](牛.md), but the whole 告 has a separate box below.
[報](報.md) is a useful meaning partner in 報告, not an opposite.
