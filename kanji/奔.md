# 奔 — rush; run about

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

奔 conveys rushing or running with energy. 奔走 can describe someone working hard to arrange something even if we never see the person run.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 大 at the top. Compare [奇](奇.md), where the lower part contains 口, and [突](突.md), where the arrangement above and below is different.

## Notes

[走](走.md) is the ordinary character in 走る (はしる). 奔る is a dictionary spelling to recognize, not the model spelling for going for a run.

[奇](奇.md) has 口 in the lower half; [突](突.md) carries its cover at the top; [茶](茶.md) begins with the grass element. In 奔, find 大 at the top and the spreading strokes below.
