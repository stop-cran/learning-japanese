# 装 — attire; outfit; pretend

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

装 concerns dressing, outfitting or presenting an appearance, including pretending. In 装置 そうち the equipping sense contributes to a word for a device or apparatus, not clothing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 衣 at the bottom and a 士-shaped figure in the upper right: imagine dressing a figure for a role. The upper left strokes complete the outline; this is not an etymological derivation.

The indexing radical 衣 remains visible at the bottom in its full form.

## Notes

- Keep ソウ in 服装 ふくそう and ショウ in 衣装 いしょう. A different on reading in another compound is not a correction to either word.
- 装う is a godan verb; 装い is a noun. The same 装う entry includes both dressing and pretending, so a clothing translation is not suitable in every context.
