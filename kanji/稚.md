# 稚 — immature; childish; young

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

稚 is being young and not yet mature. [幼稚](../words/幼稚.md) ようち has two JMdict senses: "infancy" and "childish; infantile; immature; primitive." The word article teaches "childish; immature; infancy." [幼稚園](../words/幼稚園.md) ようちえん is a "kindergarten (in Japan, non-compulsory education from age 3 until primary school); preschool," where 稚 means young age, not a criticism.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for チ: young, not yet grown up, as in 稚気 ちき, 稚魚 ちぎょ, 稚拙 ちせつ and 幼稚. It lists the kun readings わかい and いとけない. A note says 穉 is the 本字, the older standard form.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 禾, the grain-plant key, at the left and a 隹 shape at the right. Imagine a small bird among young shoots of grain. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 禾 or 隹 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| おさない | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists おさない, but JMdict writes 幼い おさない, "very young; little" and "childish; immature," with 幼 only, not 稚, so no ordinary word is checked here |
| おくて | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists おくて, but JMdict writes 奥手 おくて, "late developer (e.g. child who reaches puberty late); late bloomer," with 奥手, 晩生, 晩稲 and 晩熟, not 稚, so no ordinary word is checked here |
| おでる | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists おでる, but no JMdict entry for おでる was found here |

In 幼稚, 幼 supplies ヨウ and 稚 supplies チ. In 幼稚園, 幼 supplies ヨウ, 稚 supplies チ and 園 supplies エン. In 稚魚, "fry; juvenile fish; fingerling," 稚 supplies チ and 魚 supplies ギョ. In 稚い, 稚 writes いとけな and い is the okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; in the other JMdict spelling 稚けない, 稚 writes いとけ. Whole words keep their own readings: 稚児 ちご, "infant; child," and 丁稚 でっち, "apprentice; shop boy," are Daijisen hard-reading words, and JMdict also lists 稚児 as a spelling of ややこ, "baby; child"; none of them is split into per-kanji readings here.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows チ, with 稚魚, 稚拙 and 幼稚, and 稚児 (ちご) as a special reading; it lists no kun reading. Practice selects チ, the reading of the linked word articles. ジ, いとけない, おさない, おくて and おでる are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated or explained as above. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [幼](幼.md), "early childhood; immature," in 幼稚 and 幼稚園, supplying ヨウ, and [園](園.md), "park; garden," in 幼稚園, supplying エン. 幼 overlaps this card's title and is read おさな.い; it is in this card's quiz exclusions and is not offered as a wrong answer.
- [若](若.md), "young," [児](児.md), "child; youngster," [童](童.md), "child; juvenile," [子](子.md), "child," [苗](苗.md), "seedling; young plant," and [嬢](嬢.md), "young lady; daughter; unmarried woman," overlap "young," "child" and "immature," the senses 稚 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. [熟](熟.md), "ripen; mature; skilled," is the opposite of "immature," so it is not excluded.
- [木](木.md), "tree, wood," [私](私.md), "I; private," and [税](税.md), "tax; fiscal duty," look plausible because 木 resembles the 禾 shape, and 私 and 税 are written with 禾 at the left. They are read ボク, モク and き; シ and わたくし; and ゼイ, and mean tree, I and tax. None shares a reading or a meaning with 稚.
- Other kanji read チ or ジ, such as [知](知.md), "know," [地](地.md), "ground, land," and [字](字.md), "written character," are never offered as wrong answers.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the チ sense: 稚気 ちき, "childishness; naivete"; and 稚拙 ちせつ, "unskillful; childish; immature; naive; artless; clumsy; crude." JMdict also lists 稚気 with 穉気 as a "rarely used kanji form."
