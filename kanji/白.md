# 白 — white

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 白 (no. 106) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

白 is usually classed as a pictograph, but what it pictured is debated: proposed origins include an acorn or seed, a thumb, and a ray of light over the sun ([日](日.md)).
Do not rely on any one story. The meaning is "white", and by extension "clear, plain, blank" (白紙 はくし, a blank sheet) and "to tell, state" in older words (告白 こくはく, confession).

## Notes

Its opposite is [黒](黒.md), and 白黒 しろくろ means black and white. Look-alikes: [日](日.md) (sun) lacks the extra top stroke; 自 (self) has an extra horizontal; 百 (hundred, [百](百.md)) has 一 above 白.
Okurigana (the kana after the kanji, like い in 白い): 白い (しろい) versus the noun 白 (しろ).
