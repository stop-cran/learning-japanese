# 求 — seek; request

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

求 concerns seeking or asking for something.
[求める](../words/求める.md) (もとめる) can describe requesting help or seeking a solution.
求人 (きゅうじん) concerns recruiting workers, not an individual looking for a job.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the strokes branching from the central hook as arms reaching out in search of something.
This is a visual association, not an origin claim.

## Notes

The source indexes 求 under 水, but there is no separate three-mark 氵 component at the left.
Compare the whole seven-stroke shape with [水](水.md), especially the upper horizontal and the dot at the upper right.
助けを求める (たすけをもとめる) means "ask for help"; it need not mean searching for a lost object.
