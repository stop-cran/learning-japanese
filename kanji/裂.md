# 裂 — tear apart; split

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

裂 is about tearing or splitting something apart. In [裂く](../words/裂く.md) さく, it is a transitive verb: JMdict glosses it “to tear; to rip up,” “to cut up; to cleave; to cut open (esp. the abdomen),” and “to forcibly separate (e.g. two lovers).” Kanjipedia gives a second sense, ばらばらにわかれる (to break apart into pieces), with examples such as 決裂 けつれつ, a breakdown of talks, and 分裂 ぶんれつ, a split or division.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [列](列.md), "row; queue," sits on top, and its right side 刂 is a variant of [刀](刀.md), "sword; blade." Below it is [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment." Imagine a blade slashing a row of cuts across a garment until the cloth tears apart. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 列 or 衣 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists レツ, さく and さける. KANJIDIC2 also lists the suffix reading -ぎ.れ. KANJIDIC2 gives no name readings for 裂.
- JMdict entry 1207730 spells さく as both 裂く and 割く. The tearing sense and the forcible-separation sense belong to 裂く only; the sense “to spare (time, money, etc.)” belongs to 割く only. The Joyo table also cross-refers 裂く to 割く.

These twelve cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [裁](裁.md) (“judge; cut out cloth”): JMdict glosses 裂く as “to cut up; to cleave; to cut open,” which overlaps cutting out cloth.
- [切](切.md) (“cut”) and [剖](剖.md) (“dissect; split open”): they overlap the same cutting sense of 裂く, and 剖 also shares “split” with the title.
- [破](破.md) (“tear; break”): it shares “tear” with the title, and JMdict glosses 裂く as “to tear; to rip up.”
- [割](割.md) (“split; proportion”): it shares “split” with the title, and JMdict entry 1207730 also spells さく as 割く.
- [爆](爆.md) (“burst open; explode”): JMdict glosses 裂ける as “to split; to tear; to burst; to be separated; to be divided.”
- [別](別.md) (“separate, another”), [離](離.md) (“separate; detach”) and [隔](隔.md) (“separate; partition; every other”): JMdict glosses 裂く as “to forcibly separate” and 裂ける as “to be separated.”
- [断](断.md) (“sever; refuse”) and [絶](絶.md) (“sever; cease”): JMdict's “to forcibly separate” for 裂く overlaps “sever.”
- [砕](砕.md) (“smash; crush; break into pieces”): Kanjipedia's second sense for 裂, ばらばらにわかれる (to break apart into pieces), overlaps “break into pieces.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 縫 (“sew; stitch”), 編 (“knit; compile”), and 畳 (“tatami; fold”); none means tearing or splitting apart.
