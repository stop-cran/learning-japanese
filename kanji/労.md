# 労 — labor; trouble

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

労 connects work with the effort or trouble it costs. [労働](../words/労働.md) ろうどう means labor,
while 苦労 くろう draws attention to hardship.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine 力 working under a low cover and three short marks.
This is a memory picture of the current shape, not its derivation.

## Notes

The dictionary describes ねぎらう especially in relation to someone of equal or lower status;
do not treat it as a universal substitute for "thank."
Compare [学](学.md): a similar top sits above 子, not 力.
