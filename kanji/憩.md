# 憩 — rest; take a break; relax

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

憩 is pausing to rest: stopping for a breather in the middle of an activity and letting body and mind relax. [休憩](../words/休憩.md) きゅうけい is a rest break or intermission; JMdict glosses it "rest; break; recess; intermission."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ケイ: taking a breather, resting, relaxation. Its examples are 休憩, 少憩 しょうけい, "short break; breather; brief recess; rest," which JMdict lists after the spelling 小憩, and one compound that JMdict does not list. It gives the kun readings いこい and いこう, the same as KANJIDIC2, and notes that 憇 is a popular variant form of 憩.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [舌](舌.md), "tongue," at the left. At the right is [自](自.md), "self, oneself," over [心](心.md), "heart, mind," the same right side as [息](息.md), "breath." Imagine a runner who stops with tongue out, catching breath until the heart slows down. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 舌, 自 or 心 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| いこ.う | kun | 憩う いこう - to rest; to relax; to repose; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 休憩, 休 supplies キュウ and 憩 supplies ケイ, with no sound change; JMdict also lists 休けい as a search-only spelling. In 憩い, 憩 writes いこ and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict also spells this noun 憩 alone. The verb 憩う uses the same いこ with う; JMdict lists 息う as a rarely used kanji form of it.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケイ, with 休憩 as its example; いこい, with 憩い; and いこう, with 憩う. Practice selects ケイ, the reading with a linked word article; いこ.い and いこ.う are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [休](休.md), "rest," in 休憩.
- [話](話.md), "talk, speak," and [活](活.md), "lively," have 舌 at the right, and [鼻](鼻.md), "nose; snout," has 自 at the top. All are different kanji.
- [休](休.md), "rest," [息](息.md), "breath," and [暇](暇.md), "free time; leisure; time off," overlap the senses "rest," "relaxation" and "break" that 憩, 憩い and 休憩 carry; 休 appears with 憩 in 休憩, and JMdict spells いこう, "to rest," with 息 as a rarely used form. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "rest" as exclusive to any one character.
- [慰](慰.md), "console; comfort; solace," shares relaxation: JMdict glosses 慰安 "solace; relaxation" and 憩い "rest; relaxation." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
