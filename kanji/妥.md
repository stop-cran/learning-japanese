# 妥 — appropriate; settled

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38), below · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

妥 is associated with being appropriate, satisfactory, or settled. In [妥当](../words/妥当.md) だとう, the whole word evaluates something as reasonable or fitting the circumstances.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice the upper strokes above 女; imagine checking that the two parts sit comfortably together. This is a memory aid, not a claim about historical formation.

## Notes

- KANJIDIC2 records やす as a name reading, not a kun reading. Separate character sources record タ as a non-Joyo on-reading and やす, やすい or おだやか as kun readings; these source-specific classifications do not change the pinned metadata or the selected practice reading.
- [適](適.md) and [当](当.md) also express appropriateness; [正](正.md) includes what is proper or right, and [良](良.md) includes a satisfactory condition. These overlapping senses do not make their words interchangeable.
- KANJIDIC2 includes peace and gentleness for 妥. [安](安.md), [和](和.md), and [平](平.md) include peace; [温](温.md) includes mildness, and [静](静.md) includes becoming quiet. These comparisons concern particular senses, not universal substitutions.
- [女](女.md) shares the lower shape. [母](母.md) and [子](子.md) are looser visual contrasts, chosen to supply distinct on-reading choices in the starter pool.
