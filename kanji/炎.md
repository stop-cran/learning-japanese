# 炎 — flame; blaze; inflammation

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

炎 is a flame: the burning, glowing part of a fire, and, by extension, a blaze, a passion, or an inflamed body part. The linked [炎](../words/炎.md) ほのお is a noun, and JMdict gives two senses: "flame; blaze," and "flames (of intense emotion, e.g. love, jealousy, anger); passion."

The Daijisen character entry gives five senses for エン. One example of each:

- a flame: 火炎 かえん, "flame; blaze";
- blazing up: 炎上 えんじょう, "going up in flames; destruction by fire (esp. of a large building)," with a colloquial second sense, "stirring up a storm of criticism online";
- a feeling like a flame: 気炎 きえん, "high spirits; boastful talk";
- burning hot: 炎天 えんてん, "blazing heat; scorching sun";
- a body part with heat or pain: 胃炎 いえん, "gastritis; gastric catarrh."

More examples are in the Notes. Daijisen's ほのお entry says its first sense is the glowing, burning part of a burning gas, with 火炎 as a synonym, and its second sense is a fierce feeling such as envy, anger or love; it also lists ほむら, with the same two senses.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find two stacked copies of [火](火.md), "fire." Imagine one fire sitting on top of another until it grows into a blaze. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either 火 contributes sound or any other meaning. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| エン | on | 胃炎 いえん — gastritis; gastric catarrh; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ほのお | kun | [炎](../words/炎.md) ほのお — flame; blaze |

JMdict lists 炎 ほのお with the spelling 焔 and the readings ほのお and ほむら. The word 炎 is a whole word of one kanji, so 炎 writes the whole ほのお. In 胃炎, 炎 supplies エン and [胃](胃.md), "stomach," supplies イ. JMdict has a separate suffix entry, 炎 えん, "-itis (indicating an inflammatory disease)" [field: medicine], which is where the medical sense of エン is recorded; 肺炎 はいえん is "pneumonia" and 炎症 えんしょう is "inflammation; irritation." In 火炎, 炎上, 気炎 and 炎天, 炎 supplies エン.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows エン with 炎上, 炎天 and 火炎, and ほのお with 炎. Practice selects ほのお, the reading of the linked word article. エン is shown with a dictionary example and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. Kanjipedia also lists も.える, but KANJIDIC2 does not, so it is not in this card's metadata. ほむら is a word reading in Daijisen and JMdict, not in KANJIDIC2.

## Notes

- 炎 as a word has no other kanji. In the examples above, [火](火.md), "fire," supplies カ in 火炎, [上](上.md), "up, above," supplies ジョウ in 炎上, [気](気.md), "spirit, air," supplies キ in 気炎, [天](天.md), "heaven, sky," supplies テン in 炎天 and [胃](胃.md), "stomach," supplies イ in 胃炎.
- 陽炎 かげろう, "heat shimmer; heat haze; shimmering of hot air," is a Daijisen hard-to-read word whose かげろう is a whole-word reading, tagged "gikun (meaning as reading) or jukujikun (special kanji reading)" in JMdict, and is never split between 陽 and 炎. JMdict also lists 炎暑 えんしょ, "heat wave; intense heat."
- [淡](淡.md), "pale; faint; plain; unsalted," and [談](談.md), "converse; consult," both contain 炎 as a visible part, and [炊](炊.md), "cook rice; boil," has 火 at the left. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [火](火.md), "fire," [焼](焼.md), "bake; burn," [燃](燃.md), "burn; blaze," which also reads も.える, [焦](焦.md), "scorch; char; impatient," and [爆](爆.md), "burst open; explode," overlap flame and burning. [熱](熱.md), "heat; fever," and [暑](暑.md), "hot weather; summer heat; hot season," overlap heat, which is the sense of 炎暑 and 炎天; the [暑](暑.md) card notes that 炎 mainly names fire. [症](症.md), "symptoms; illness," [病](病.md), "illness," and [痛](痛.md), "pain; hurt," overlap inflammation. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "flame," "blaze" or "inflammation" as exclusive to any one character.
