# 表 — surface; chart

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

表 can name the outward-facing surface of something or a table that presents information.
In [発表](../words/発表.md) はっぴょう, it belongs to making something known to an audience.
Keep the whole word and its reading in view: a data table is not called おもて.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine information brought onto a visible front surface.
Use the stacked upper strokes and spreading lower strokes as a shape cue, not as a historical explanation.
The indexing radical is 衣; here its lower strokes are embedded in the character, not written as a separate 衤.

## Notes

The two nouns written 表 have separate readings and uses; do not swap them freely.
[青](青.md) and [生](生.md) help you compare the upper strokes; [長](長.md) is a useful
lower-stroke contrast. None has the same complete outline as 表.
