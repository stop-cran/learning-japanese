# 工 — craft, construction

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 工 (no. 48) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

工 concerns making things, skilled work or construction. It helps form words for factories, manufacturing and craftspeople.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a workbench with two horizontal rails joined by a support. This is a mnemonic based on the modern shape, not a claim about an ancient tool.

- 大工 だいく — carpenter

## Notes

In [土](土.md), the vertical stroke rises above the upper horizontal; in 工, it joins the two horizontals. [左](左.md) contains a 工-shaped part, but the whole character means "left."

Do not use bare 工 as a general translation of the English verb "work." Learn it in words such as 工場 or 大工.
