# 引 — pull; draw

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (no. 57) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

引 begins with pulling or drawing something: [引く](../words/引く.md) ひく.
ドアを引いてください。 (ドアをひいてください。) — Please pull the door.
The character also occurs in 引用 いんよう, quotation or citation; that word is not about physically dragging an object.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see the 弓 shape on the left and imagine pulling its string toward the separate vertical stroke on the right.
This is a modern memory aid, not a sourced historical explanation.

## Notes

[押す](../words/押す.md) おす, "push," is the useful physical contrast to 引く.
[弟](弟.md) and [強](強.md) also have bent, bow-like strokes, but 引 has only 弓 and the right-hand vertical.
[写](写.md) is a looser comparison with folded strokes under a top cover.
The full word 引く has several senses; the linked article starts with pulling an object.
