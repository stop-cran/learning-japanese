# 堅 — hard; solid; steady

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

堅 is hardness that holds firm: solid material, and people or plans that are steady and dependable. [堅い](../words/堅い.md) かたい is hard or solid, and also steady or reliable. JMdict lists 堅い in one entry with the spellings 硬い and 固い, glossed "hard; solid; tough" and, in a later sense, "safe; steady; honest; steadfast."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ケン: being tight and hard, as in 堅固 けんご, "solid; strong; firm," and 堅牢 けんろう, "solid; strong; sturdy; durable; stout"; and being steady and dependable, as in 堅持 けんじ, "adhering to (a view, policy, etc.); sticking to; holding fast to," 堅実 けんじつ, "steady; sound; stable; safe (e.g. business); reliable," 堅忍 けんにん, "perseverance," and 中堅 ちゅうけん, "nucleus; backbone; mainstay; key figure." Its kun reading is かたい.

Among words with a hard-to-guess reading, the entry names 堅魚 かつお, "skipjack tuna (Katsuwonus pelamis); oceanic bonito"; JMdict lists 堅魚 as a rarely used spelling of 鰹 and notes that the word is usually written in kana alone.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [臣](臣.md), "retainer; subject," at the top left, 又, a small hand-like shape with no card here, at the top right, and [土](土.md), "earth, soil," at the bottom. Imagine a loyal retainer pressing a hand down on packed earth to check that the ground is hard and solid. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 臣, 又 or 土 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケン | on | 堅実 けんじつ - steady; sound; stable; safe (e.g. business); reliable; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| かた.い | kun | [堅い](../words/堅い.md) かたい — hard; solid |
| -がた.い | kun suffix | 手堅い てがたい - sound; sure; safe; secure; trustworthy; reliable; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 堅い, 堅 writes かた and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The leading dash in -がた.い marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced. In 手堅い, 手 is read て and かた becomes がた, a sound change called rendaku, the voicing of the first sound of a following element; 義理堅い ぎりがたい, "possessed of a strong sense of duty," works the same way. In 堅実, 堅 supplies ケン and 実 supplies ジツ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケン, with 堅固, 堅実 and 中堅 as examples, and かたい, with 堅い, marked as a pair to compare with 硬い and 固い, two other spellings in JMdict's かたい entry; that entry also lists 緊い, marked as irregular kanji usage. It has no separate row for -がた.い. Practice selects かた.い, the reading with a linked word article. ケン is the reading of most 堅 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; ケン and -がた.い are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The word 堅い is 堅 with okurigana only.
- [臣](臣.md), "retainer; subject," is the top left part of 堅, and [監](監.md), "oversee; watch over," also has 臣 at the upper left. [基](基.md), "foundation; basis," has the same 土 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [硬](硬.md), "hard; stiff," [固](固.md), "firm; harden," [剛](剛.md), "sturdy; unyielding strength," [勁](勁.md), "strong; taut," [強](強.md), "strong," and [厳](厳.md), "strict; severe; solemn," overlap the senses "hard," "solid" and "steady" that 堅 and its words carry, and the KANJIDIC2 meaning "strict"; 硬 and 固 are also read かた.い, and the Joyo table pairs 堅い with 硬い and 固い. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "hard" or "firm" as exclusive to any one character.
