# 舎 — house; lodging; hut

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

舎 means a house, lodging place or building, especially in compounds for quarters or institutional buildings. 校舎 (こうしゃ) is a school building; 宿舎 (しゅくしゃ) is lodging or accommodation. The character is also used in historical and humble expressions, but the reading of a whole word must be checked rather than inferred from its parts.

## Notes

The selected quiz reading is **シャ**: in 校舎 (こうしゃ), 校 is コウ and 舎 is シャ. Do not use 田舎 (いなか) to justify a reading of 舎; it is a whole-word reading not aligned to the character’s listed readings.

セキ and やど.る are in the KANJIDIC2 metadata but outside the Jōyō table; no checked JMdict example in the reviewed set aligns to either. Digital Daijisen lists おく as a kun reading, and Kanjipedia also lists it outside Jōyō; it likewise has no checked JMdict example here. Kanjipedia’s other non-Jōyō forms いえ and やど, and Digital Daijisen’s name readings いえ・や・やどる, are not standalone quiz readings. KANJIDIC2 nanori さ・とり and Digital Daijisen name readings are names, not ordinary readings.

Digital Daijisen’s difficult forms 田舎 (いなか), 舎密 (セイミ), 舎人 (とねり) and 学舎 (まなびや) are whole-word readings, not readings of 舎 by itself; 精舎 (しょうじゃ) has a compound reading, not a separate on-reading. Digital Daijisen and Kanjipedia also record a historical sense, the distance an army marched in one day; JMdict's 三舎 (さんしゃ) is three days' march. Both sources also list humble terms for one's own family, such as 舎弟.

Digital Daijisen's first sense is a house or building, and Kanjipedia's is いえ・やど・たてもの (house, lodging, building), also used as やどる (to lodge). JMdict adds “boarding house; residence hall; dormitory”, and Digital Daijisen treats 舎 as short for 寄宿舎 (dormitory) in 舎監; 官舎 (かんしゃ) is an official residence. KANJIDIC2 gives 舎 “cottage; inn; hut; house; mansion”, which shares “inn” and “cottage” with 荘 and “cottage” and “mansion” with 亭. These senses overlap with the displayed titles of 家 (“house, home”), 宿 (“lodging”), 宅 (“residence”), 住 (“reside, dwell”), 寮 (“dormitory; quarters”), 荘 (“villa; manor; solemn”), 亭 (“pavilion; restaurant building”), and 館 (“hall; large building”). JMdict's “residence hall” and Kanjipedia's たてもの (building) also fit 堂 (“hall”), and 屋 (“roof, shop”) forms 小屋 (こや), which JMdict glosses as “hut; cabin; shed.” Those ten cards are excluded from meaning choices.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 雨 (“rain”), 木 (“tree, wood”), and 山 (“mountain”); none means a house or lodging.
