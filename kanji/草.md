# 草 — grass; weeds

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (as 艹, no. 140) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

草 concerns grass and other herbaceous plants. [雑草](../words/雑草.md) ざっそう
means weeds, while 草原 そうげん names a grassy area.
The character also has a draft-writing sense, as in 草案 そうあん, "draft."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture grass sprouting from the top 艹 above 早.
This is a visual cue for remembering the form, not its historical derivation.

## Notes

The canonical radical is 艸; the visible top is 艹.
In 雑草, learn the small っ in ざっそう.
[早](早.md) is the lower shape without 艹; [若](若.md) and [苦](苦.md) share the top
but have different lower parts.
