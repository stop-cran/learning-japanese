# 愁 — sorrow; melancholy

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

愁 is sorrow, grief or melancholy. It appears in 郷愁, in 哀愁 (あいしゅう), “pathos; sorrow,” and in 憂愁 (ゆうしゅう), “melancholy.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See [秋](秋.md), “autumn,” above [心](心.md), “heart”; 秋 is also read シュウ. Picture a heart heavy with autumn sadness.

| うれ.える | kun | 愁える (うれえる), to grieve or worry; JMdict lists it as a rarely used kanji form of 憂える. |
| うれ.い | kun | 愁い (うれい), sorrow or grief; JMdict lists it as a second spelling of 憂い. JMdict marks the sense “sorrow; grief” as especially 愁い and the sense “anxiety; worry” as especially 憂い; these are preferences, not strict rules. |

## Notes

[想](想.md), [意](意.md) and [息](息.md) also have 心 at the bottom, but mean thought, meaning and breath. [悩](悩.md), [寂](寂.md), [懐](懐.md), [哀](哀.md) and [悲](悲.md) relate to worry, loneliness, nostalgia or sorrow. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 74](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=74) lists シュウ, with 哀愁 and 憂愁; うれえる, with 愁える; and うれい, with 愁い. It cross-references them with 憂える and 憂い, which are written with a different kanji.
