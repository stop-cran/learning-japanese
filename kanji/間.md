# 間 — interval, between

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 門 (gate, no. 169) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

間 is 門 (gate) around 日 (sun): sunlight showing through the gap between the gate doors. The older form (閒) used 月 (moon) in the same position; this explanation is standard, though the
story is partly a picture explanation. It means "gap", "interval", "between", "space" and "period of time".

## Common words

- [時間](../words/時間.md) じかん — time, hour
- [人間](../words/人間.md) にんげん — human being
- 間に合う まにあう — be in time

## Notes

- **間 as a counter of duration:** かん follows a unit: 二時間 にじかん, 三日間 みっかかん, 一週間 いっしゅうかん. Not for clock time: that is 時 (三時 = 3 o'clock; 三時間 = three hours).
- Look-alikes: 門 (gate) has 一 in the middle; 問 (question) has 口; 聞 (hear) has 耳; 間 has 日.
- 間 can mean "room": 居間 いま (living room). 人間 is read にんげん (人 is にん here, not じん; 間 is the voiced ゲン of ケン, not かん) and means "human being".
