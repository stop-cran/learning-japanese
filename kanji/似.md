# 似 — resemble

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

似 expresses resemblance rather than strict identity.
With [似る](../words/似る.md) にる, learners will often need the form 似ている (にている), "be similar; look alike."
類似 るいじ is a noun for similarity and can also take する.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** find the person-shaped 亻 next to [以](以.md), then imagine comparing two people who look alike.
This is a memory story based on the modern shape, not etymology.

## Notes

Compare 母に似ている (ははににている), "resemble one's mother": に marks the person used for comparison.
[以](以.md) lacks the extra 亻; [代](代.md) and [住](住.md) have simpler right sides.
The canonical radical 人 appears here as 亻.
Similarity does not require every feature to be the same.
