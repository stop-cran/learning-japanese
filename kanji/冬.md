# 冬 — winter

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 冫 (no. 15) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

冬 means winter, the season contrasted with [夏](夏.md), summer.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper bent strokes as a scarf, with two small snowflakes below it. This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Common words

- [冬](../words/冬.md) ふゆ — winter
- 冬休み ふゆやすみ — winter vacation
- 冬季 とうき — winter season, often in schedules and notices

## Notes

[終](終.md), "finish," contains a similar right-hand shape but adds a component on the left. 冬 by itself names the season, not the act of finishing.

For the other seasons, see [春](春.md), [夏](夏.md), and [秋](秋.md).
