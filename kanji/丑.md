# 丑 — Ox zodiac sign

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 一 (no. 1) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

丑 names the **second of the twelve zodiac signs**, the Ox. The native
sign noun is [丑](../words/丑.md) うし. The ordinary animal noun
[牛](../words/牛.md), also うし, uses a different character.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a bent top-and-right
stroke and a descending inner stroke held together by a middle crossbar
and a bottom bar. The middle crossbar is the indexing radical
[一](一.md) in the stroke diagram. This is a memory aid for the modern
shape, not an account of the character's historical origin.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チュウ | on | 丁丑 ていちゅう — Fire Ox, term 14 of the sixty-term calendar cycle; recognition only |
| うし | kun | [丑](../words/丑.md) うし — Ox zodiac sign; 丑年 うしどし — year of the Ox |

Only うし is selected for practice. チュウ is verified calendar recognition,
not classified here as obsolete or name-only. Learn 丁丑's complete reading
ていちゅう rather than freely exchanging character readings. The sixty-term
cycle combines stems and branches; it is not just the twelve-sign set.

## Notes

- [五](五.md), five, and [互](互.md), mutual, also fit between top and
  bottom bars, but their inner turns differ from 丑's descending strokes.
  [土](土.md), earth or soil, has a single upright through two bars and
  no bent top-and-right stroke. These are modern-shape comparisons,
  not etymologies.
- [牛](牛.md) names the associated animal. The shared reading うし
  does not make the two spellings unrestricted substitutes.
- Historical uses include an hour around 2 a.m., a traditional direction
  labelled north-northeast, and the twelfth lunar month. Dictionary hour
  mappings include both 1–3 a.m. and 2–4 a.m.; no universal clock
  conversion is intended. These are not ordinary modern scheduling or
  navigation substitutes. The lunar month is not Gregorian December.
- 丑[寅](寅.md) (うしとら), northeast, names a direction between two
  branches; it is not the same direction as standalone 丑.
- The grade-9 tag classifies name-use kanji; it does not make the sign
  noun or every reading a personal name.
