# 険 — steep; perilous

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (as left-hand 阝, no. 170) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

険 occurs in steep or difficult terrain and in danger-related words.
険しい けわしい can describe a steep, rugged route; [危険](../words/危険.md) きけん means danger or dangerous.
冒険 ぼうけん names an adventure or venture.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the pointed upper shape and spread lower strokes beside 阝 as a difficult slope.
This image is a mnemonic, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

険しい is an い-adjective: 険しい道 (けわしいみち), "a steep, rugged path."
危険 is a な-adjective when it modifies a noun: 危険な道 (きけんなみち), "a dangerous path."
[験](験.md) has the same right-hand shape but 馬 on the left, not 阝.
