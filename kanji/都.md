# 都 — metropolis

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 邑 (as right-hand 阝, no. 163) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

都 appears in words for cities and capitals. [都会](../words/都会.md) とかい describes a city or urban setting,
while the native word 都 みやこ can mean a capital or seat of government.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see 者 beside the right-hand 阝 and imagine people gathered in a metropolis.
This is a learning image, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

Learn 京都 as きょうと; the 都 there is ト.
[都会](../words/都会.md) does not imply that the city is a national capital.
[部](部.md) shares the right-hand 邑 form 阝 but replaces 者 with a different left-hand cluster.
