# 猪 — wild boar

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 犬 (no. 94), appearing as 犭 on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

猪 writes **wild boar** in the native noun
[猪](../words/猪.md) いのしし. The animal name does not specify male or female.
イノシシ is also useful to recognize: JMdict marks the noun as usually written
in kana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture an animal beside the
[者](者.md)-shaped right side. The three-stroke 犭 on the left distinguishes
猪 from 者 alone. This is a drawing memory aid, not a claim that a person
or a dog explains the character's history.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チョ | on | 猪突 ちょとつ — recklessness; 野猪 やちょ — wild boar; compound recognition, not reading questions here |
| い | kun | 猪 い — an archaic noun for a hog, especially a wild boar; historical recognition, not a reading question |
| いのしし | kun | [猪](../words/猪.md) いのしし — wild boar |

The full source lists retain い before いのしし, but only いのしし is selected
for native-noun practice. The older い noun can include domestic pigs;
that does not make it a neutral modern replacement for [豚](../words/豚.md) ぶた.

## Common words

- [猪](../words/猪.md) いのしし — wild boar
- 猪突猛進 ちょとつもうしん — rushing recklessly; a four-character expression

## Notes

- [者](者.md) means a person in a role; [著](著.md) concerns writing or
  remarkable qualities; [諸](諸.md) means various, many or together.
  Similar surrounding shapes do not make their meanings interchangeable.
- The source's grade-9 tag is a name-use classification, not a claim that
  猪 occurs only in people's names. The animal noun is useful outside names;
  personal-name readings are outside this lesson.
- 猪口 ちょこ means a sake cup in its usually-kana sense, not a small boar.
  Learn such a word's complete reading and meaning rather than guessing
  them from the animal character.
