# 尼 — nun

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

尼 designates a nun. In a Buddhist setting it identifies a woman in religious life; the standalone word あま can have a Christian interpretation in the appropriate context.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for the enclosing top shape and the small hooked strokes below it. [泥](泥.md) puts water on the left of the entire 尼 shape.
