# 仮 — temporary; assumed

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

仮 describes something that is not the real or final version: temporary, provisional or assumed. [仮定](../words/仮定.md) かてい is an assumption or supposition. [仮名](../words/仮名.md) かな is kana, the Japanese syllabaries; the word article notes that this native term is a contraction of かりな, so 仮名 is learned as one unit. The same spelling 仮名 also writes a separate word かめい, "alias; pseudonym." The kana family also includes [仮名遣い](../words/仮名遣い.md) かなづかい, [平仮名](../words/平仮名.md) ひらがな, [片仮名](../words/片仮名.md) かたかな, [振り仮名](../words/振り仮名.md) ふりがな and [送り仮名](../words/送り仮名.md) おくりがな.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for カ: something not genuine or formal but a temporary stopgap or appearance, as in 仮称 かしょう, "temporary name; provisional name," 仮説 かせつ, "hypothesis; supposition; assumption; tentative theory," 仮定, 仮名 and 仮面 かめん, "mask" or "disguise"; temporarily, as in 仮設 かせつ, "temporary construction; temporary establishment; provisional construction," and 仮眠 かみん, "nap; doze"; borrowing, as in 仮借 かしゃ, "phonetic loan character; loangraph"; and forgiving, as in 仮借 かしゃく, "pardon; extenuation; excuse." The Daijisen heading also shows the older form 假, which this card does not teach.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the person shape 亻 at the left beside [反](反.md), "oppose; reverse," at the right. Imagine a person standing in for someone else for a while: a temporary stand-in, not the real one. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 反 contributes meaning or sound, and 亻 is simply the key shape at the left. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カ | on | [仮定](../words/仮定.md) かてい — assumption; supposition; 仮面 かめん - mask; disguise; 仮装 かそう - costume; fancy dress; masquerade; disguise; 仮設 かせつ - temporary construction; temporary establishment |
| ケ | on | 仮病 けびょう - feigned illness; 虚仮 こけ - folly; fool, usually written in kana alone; not selected for questions here |
| かり | kun | 仮 かり - temporary; provisional; interim; 仮に かりに - supposing; temporarily; for the time being; 仮処分 かりしょぶん - provisional disposition; temporary injunction; not selected for questions here |
| かり- | kun | prefix form: 仮住まい かりずまい - temporary residence; 仮寝 かりね - nap; catnap; not selected for questions here |

In 仮定, 仮 is read カ and 定 is read テイ. The kana words are different: in 仮名 かな, 仮 and 名 are learned as one unit, not read separately as か and な, and the unit changes to がな after another element, as in 平仮名 ひらがな, 振り仮名 ふりがな and 送り仮名 おくりがな. In 片仮名 かたかな and 仮名遣い かなづかい the same unit keeps its かな sound.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カ, with 仮面, 仮定 and 仮装 as examples; ケ, with 仮病; and かり, with 仮の住まい, 仮に and 仮処分; its remarks column lists 仮名 かな as a special reading. Practice selects カ, the only reading with a linked word article that reads 仮 on its own. The ケ and かり readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and the Daijisen character entry and are illustrated with unlinked dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

In 虚仮 こけ, 虚 supplies こ and 仮 supplies け; 虚 does not have a kanji card yet.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [定](定.md), "determine; settle," in 仮定, [名](名.md), "name," in the 仮名 family, [平](平.md), "flat; peaceful," in 平仮名, [片](片.md), "one-sided; sheet," in 片仮名 and [送](送.md), "send; escort," in 送り仮名. 振 in 振り仮名 and 遣 in 仮名遣い do not have kanji cards yet.
- [板](板.md), "plank; board," [坂](坂.md), "slope; incline," and [返](返.md), "give back; repay," share 反 at the right but are different kanji. [化](化.md), "transform," and [価](価.md), "worth; price," share the person shape and the on reading カ with 仮 but are different kanji.
- [借](借.md), "borrow, rent," sounds the same as the kun reading かり and overlaps the borrowing sense seen in 仮借. [装](装.md), "attire; outfit; pretend," overlaps 仮装, "disguise." [許](許.md), "permit; forgive," overlaps the forgiving sense of 仮借 かしゃく. All three are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "temporary" or "pretend" as exclusive to any one character.
