# 寛 — lenient; broad-minded

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寛 suggests giving someone room rather than treating them harshly; it also appears in a verb for relaxing. In 寛大 (かんだい) it is the response or judgment that is generous.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for the roof at the top and the 見-like shape low down. Imagine someone with room under that roof to put their feet up.

## Notes

[覚](覚.md) also ends with 見 but starts differently. [察](察.md) has a different bottom and [寄](寄.md) has a 口 shape below. [緩](緩.md) can likewise suggest leniency, [憩](憩.md) rest, and [広](広.md) breadth. A broad mind in 寛大 is not a room measurement.
