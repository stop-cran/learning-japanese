# 志 — will; aspiration; intention

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

志 is the mind set on a goal: what one resolves to do or become. [意志](../words/意志.md) いし is will or determination; JMdict glosses it "will; volition; intention; intent; determination."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for シ. One example of each:

- the mind turned toward a goal: 志望 しぼう, "wish; desire; ambition; choice";
- the will or intention itself, こころざし: 大志 たいし, "ambition; aspiration";
- writing down, or what is written: 三国志 さんごくし, "Records of the Three Kingdoms (Chinese historical text)";
- short for the old province of Shima: 志州; JMdict has no entry for it, so its reading is not given here.

Its kun readings are こころざす, こころざし, しるす and さかん; しるす and さかん are not KANJIDIC2 readings. JMdict's noun 志 こころざし has the senses "will; resolution; intention; ambition; aim; goal," "kindness; goodwill; kind offer" and "gift (as a token of gratitude)." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [士](士.md), "warrior; professional," on top and [心](心.md), "heart, mind," below. Imagine a warrior whose heart is fixed on one aim. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 士 or 心 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | [意志](../words/意志.md) いし — will; determination |
| シリング | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict's シリング, "shilling," notes that it was formerly written as 志 and lists 志 as a search-only spelling |
| こころざ.す | kun | 志す こころざす - to aspire to; to aim for; to intend; to resolve; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| こころざし | kun | 志 こころざし - will; resolution; intention; ambition; aim; goal; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 意志, 意 supplies イ and 志 supplies シ, with no sound change. In 志す, 志 writes こころざ and す is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the noun 志 こころざし is the kanji alone, with 志 writing the whole word. シリング is an English loanword, not a Japanese kun reading in the usual sense; KANJIDIC2 files it with the kun readings because 志 was once used to write it.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シ, with 志望, 有志 ゆうし, "interested person; volunteer; supporter," and 寸志 すんし, "small present; small token of appreciation; with compliments (e.g. written on small gift)," as examples; こころざす, with 志す; and こころざし, with 志. シリング is not in the table. Practice selects シ, the reading with a linked word article; こころざ.す and こころざし are illustrated with dictionary words, and シリング is kept as a source-only reading. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [意](意.md), "meaning, intention," in 意志. [誌](誌.md), "written record; journal," is 言 + 志 and also reads シ; it is a different kanji.
- [売](売.md), "sell," and [声](声.md), "voice," also have 士 on top. [忘](忘.md), "forget," has 心 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [意](意.md), "meaning, intention," [望](望.md), "hope," [希](希.md), "hope; rare," [願](願.md), "wish; petition," [欲](欲.md), "desire," [祈](祈.md), "pray; wish," [標](標.md), "mark; signpost; goal," [決](決.md), "decide," and [定](定.md), "determine; settle," overlap the senses "intention," "hope," "wish," "desire," "aim; goal" and "resolve" that 志 and its words carry: 志望 is "wish; desire; ambition; choice," the noun 志 includes "aim; goal," and 志す includes "to resolve." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "will" or "intention" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the シ senses: 志学 しがく, "age 15"; 志願 しがん, "aspiration; volunteering; desire; application"; 志向 しこう, "intention; aim; preference (for); orientation (towards a goal)"; 弱志 じゃくし, "weak will"; 初志 しょし, "one's original intention"; 闘志 とうし, "fighting spirit; (will to) fight"; 同志 どうし, "comrade; fellow; kindred soul" in its second sense; 篤志 とくし, "charity; benevolence; charitable spirit"; and 立志 りっし, "fixing one's aim in life; deciding one's life goal."
