# 測 — measure, gauge

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

測 concerns measuring, gauging, or surveying. It also appears in words for predicting or estimating, not only direct physical measurement.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture water beside 則, a rule to follow: compare the water with a marked scale to measure its level.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- 測る is a transitive godan verb; 測って はかって is its te-form.
- [計る](../words/計る.md) and 量る are also read はかる. Spelling depends on the use; pronunciation alone does not decide among them.
- Keep 測, measuring, separate from the similar-looking [則](則.md), rule, and [側](側.md), side.
