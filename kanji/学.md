# 学 — study

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 子 (no. 39) · **Phonetic:** none (the old form 學 is sometimes analysed as having one; debated) · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

学 is the simplified form of the older 學. In 學, a child ([子](子.md)) sits under a roof (冖), and the upper part, with its two pairs of hands,
is commonly explained as the teaching or the building where learning takes place. The details of the upper part are debated.
It means "study, learning", and is used as a suffix for fields ("-ology": 数学, 文学) and in the words for school and student.

## Notes

学 usually means studying as a field or a long-term activity; the everyday verb for homework-type study is 勉強する べんきょうする, and 習う ならう is "to learn (from someone)".
In 学校 the reading shifts to がっこう: ガク + コウ becomes ガッコウ (the final く doubles into a small っ before a k sound).

Look-alikes: 字 (character, letter) has 宀 on top and 子 below, with no crown; 学 has an extra ⺍ crown over 冖. [校](校.md) ("school", the institution or building) and 学 ("study") are different notions that appear together in 学校.
