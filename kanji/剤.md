# 剤 — medicinal preparation; chemical agent

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

剤 is useful as a compound element for a medicinal preparation or chemical agent. [洗剤](../words/洗剤.md) せんざい means detergent or cleaning agent: this use is not limited to medicine that people take. Learn 剤 inside words rather than using it as a general standalone noun for any substance.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the crossed strokes and lower bars of the 斉-shaped left block beside the blade-shaped 刂, as though a preparation were being carefully divided. This invented scene helps remember the modern shape; it is not a historical or phonetic explanation.

Only ザイ is selected for reading questions. The other four readings remain dictionary reference information, not interchangeable pronunciations of 洗剤. Not selecting a word example does not establish that a reading is invalid or obsolete.

For ordinary かる, "mow; trim," learn [刈る](../words/刈る.md); for けずる, "shave; sharpen; reduce," learn [削る](../words/削る.md). Those words do not establish 剤 as an alternative spelling.

## Common words

- [洗剤](../words/洗剤.md) せんざい — detergent; cleaning agent
- 薬剤 やくざい — medicine; chemical agent
- 錠剤 じょうざい — tablet; pill
- 調剤 ちょうざい — preparing or dispensing a prescription

These are recognition examples, not instructions about using a product or medicine.

## Notes

- The indexing radical is [刀](刀.md), visible here as right-hand 刂. [済](済.md), "settle; finish," has a similar 斉-shaped block but adds left-hand 氵 instead. Check the side and shape of the extra strokes, not just the shared block.
- [剛](剛.md), "sturdy; unyielding strength," and [則](則.md), "rule; follow a model," also have right-hand 刂 but different left blocks and meanings.
- [薬](薬.md) also concerns medicine and chemicals. Its meanings overlap with 剤; do not treat them as mutually exclusive categories or assume that a compound containing either names something safe to ingest.
- 製剤 せいざい, "pharmaceutical formulation," does not demonstrate 剤's セイ: 剤 supplies ざい there. Likewise, in 推進剤 すいしんざい, "propellant," the initial すい belongs to 推, not 剤.
- JMdict also records the whole word 粘剤 ねり, a material used in making Japanese paper. That whole-word spelling does not establish an independent ねり or り reading for 剤.
