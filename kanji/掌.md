# 掌 — palm (of the hand); administer

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

掌 is the palm of the hand and, by extension, holding something in hand as one's charge. [車掌](../words/車掌.md) しゃしょう is a train conductor, the person in charge on a train; JMdict glosses it "(train) conductor." JMdict's entry headed 手のひら, "palm (of the hand)," lists 掌 as one of its spellings, along with 手のひら and 手の平, and restricts its readings たなごころ and しょう to the spelling 掌.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ショウ. One example of each:

- the palm of the hand: 掌中 しょうちゅう, "in the hand; (something) easily manipulated";
- taking charge of something as one's duty: 職掌 しょくしょう, "official duties; office; function";
- holding in the hand: 鞅掌 おうしょう, "being busy with."

It lists the kun readings たなごころ, たなうら, てのひら and つかさどる. KANJIDIC2 does not list たなうら or つかさど.る; JMdict gives 掌る as a rarely used kanji form of 司る つかさどる, "to be in charge of; to manage; to direct; to administer; to preside over." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

Daijisen also gives 仙人掌 サボテン, "cactus," as a word with a hard-to-guess reading. JMdict marks 仙人掌 as a rarely used kanji form and サボテン as "word usually written using kana alone." サボテン belongs to the whole word and is not divided between its kanji.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the bottom, under a top part with three short strokes, a cover and a small square, the same top that [常](常.md), "usual; constant," and [賞](賞.md), "prize; reward," have. Imagine an open hand held palm up, with a small box resting on it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that these parts contribute meaning or sound, although 賞 is also read ショウ. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ショウ | on | [車掌](../words/車掌.md) しゃしょう — train conductor |
| てのひら | kun | 掌 てのひら - palm (of the hand); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| たなごころ | kun | 掌 たなごころ - palm (of the hand); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 車掌, 車 supplies シャ and 掌 supplies ショウ, with no sound change. てのひら and たなごころ are readings of 掌 written alone, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict's 掌を指す, read たなごころをさす or しょうをさす, means "to be obvious; to be obviously correct." Its 掌を返す, headed 手のひらを返す, "to flip-flop; to do an about-face," restricts the readings たなごころをかえす and しょうをかえす to the spelling 掌を返す. JMdict marks both as "idiomatic expression."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ショウ, with 掌中, 職掌 and 車掌 as examples, and has no kun row, so てのひら and たなごころ are outside the table. Practice selects ショウ, the reading with a linked word article; てのひら and たなごころ are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [車](車.md), "car, vehicle," in 車掌.
- [堂](堂.md), "hall," and [党](党.md), "political party; faction," have the same three short strokes over a cover at the top as 掌, and [撃](撃.md), "shoot; strike," also has 手 at the bottom. All are different kanji. 常 has the whole top part of 掌 and is read ジョウ.
- [手](手.md), "hand," matches the palm sense, and 手のひら is the usual spelling of the palm word; [司](司.md), "take charge; official; preside," writes 司る, which JMdict also spells 掌る; [係](係.md), "connection; person in charge," [管](管.md), "pipe; supervise," [営](営.md), "conduct business; manage," [督](督.md), "supervise; urge," [府](府.md), "urban prefecture; administration," [担](担.md), "shoulder; take on," and [務](務.md), "duties," overlap administering and taking charge, as in 職掌; and [握](握.md), "grip; grasp," matches 掌握 しょうあく, "grasping; seizing; holding; commanding; having control over." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "palm" or "administer" as exclusive to any one character.
- [揮](揮.md), "wield; command; display," and [操](操.md), "operate; manipulate; fidelity," overlap administering: JMdict glosses 掌る, a rarely used kanji form of 司る, "to be in charge of; to manage; to direct; to administer", 指揮 "command; direction; supervision", and 操る "to operate (e.g. a machine); to handle; to manage; to control". The three are kept out of one another's quiz options.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first ショウ sense, the palm: 掌握; 合掌 がっしょう, "pressing one's hands together in prayer," which JMdict also glosses "triangular frame of a thatched roof" and "yours sincerely; yours truly"; 熊掌 ゆうしょう, "bear paw (meat treasured in ancient China); bear's palm"; and 落掌 らくしょう, "receive."
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the second ショウ sense, taking charge: 管掌 かんしょう, "taking charge; management," 兼掌, which has no JMdict entry, 車掌, and 分掌 ぶんしょう, "division of duties; taking charge of (one's allotted duty)."
