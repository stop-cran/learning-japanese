# 浴 — bathe, be exposed to

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

浴 connects bathing with receiving water, light, or attention over oneself. The related verb 浴びせる instead directs something over a target.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the water strokes beside the valley-shaped 谷 area: water showers down into a valley.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- The verb 浴びる is taught in the existing [あびる](../words/あびる.md) article, whose headword is kana.
- 浴びる and 浴びせる are both transitive ichidan verbs: 水を浴びる receives water, while 人に水を浴びせる directs water at someone. They are not an intransitive/transitive pair.
- The exposure image extends to attention and questions, not only washing. The material received or directed takes を.
