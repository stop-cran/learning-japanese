# 巳 — Snake zodiac sign

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 己 (no. 49), here in the 巳 form · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

巳 names the **sixth zodiac sign**, the Snake, in the twelve-sign cycle.
The native sign noun is [巳](../words/巳.md) み. For a living snake,
use [蛇](../words/蛇.md) へび instead; the sign and the animal noun
are not interchangeable.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a small box with a
curled tail, labelled for the Snake sign. This is a memory aid for the
present shape, not an account of its historical origin.
The indexing radical is 己, but the character to learn and write is 巳.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | 上巳 じょうし — the seasonal festival associated with the Doll's Festival; recognition only |
| み | kun | [巳](../words/巳.md) み — Snake zodiac sign; 巳年 みどし — year of the Snake |

Only み is selected for practice here. シ is a real dictionary-supported
reading, not an obsolete or name-only reading. 上巳 also has the documented
whole-word reading じょうみ; this does not allow swapping し and み in
every compound.

## Common words

- [巳](../words/巳.md) み — Snake zodiac sign
- 巳年 みどし — year of the Snake

## Notes

- Distinguish **巳**, the Snake sign, from **己** おのれ, self,
  and **已** イ, already/cease. In their standard modern shapes, the
  third stroke of 巳 starts up at the top, closing the upper-left.
  己 leaves that upper-left open; 已 starts above the middle but short
  of the top. These are different characters, not spelling alternatives.
- [包](包.md), wrap/enclose, surrounds a similar shape;
  [巻](巻.md), roll/book volume, has a curled lower part;
  [記](記.md), record in writing, has 言 beside 己. None means the Snake sign.
- 己 also names the sixth of the **ten calendar stems**, read つちのと
  in that use. It is not 巳, the sixth of the **twelve branches**.
  The stems and branches combine into a sixty-term cycle.
- Historical texts also use 巳 for an hour around 10 a.m., a direction
  approximately south-southeast, or the fourth lunar month.
  Dictionary hour conventions include 9–11 a.m. and 10 a.m.–noon;
  no universal modern-clock conversion is asserted here. Lunar month
  four is not simply April.
- The source's grade-9 tag classifies name-use kanji; it does not make
  the sign noun a name-only word.
