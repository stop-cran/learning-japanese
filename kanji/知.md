# 知 — know

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 矢 (no. 111) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

知 concerns knowing, becoming aware of something, and knowledge. Its central verb is
[知る](../words/知る.md) (しる). 知っている (しっている) normally expresses the resulting state, "know."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** beside the arrow-shaped 矢, a [mouth](口.md) gives an answer
that hits the target: the speaker knows.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| し.る | kun | [知る](../words/知る.md) しる — know; find out |
| し.らせる | kun | 知らせる しらせる — inform; notify |

## Common words

- [知る](../words/知る.md) しる — learn of; know
- 知らせる しらせる — let someone know
- 知識 ちしき — knowledge
- 知人 ちじん — acquaintance

## Notes

The ordinary negative of 知っています (しっています, "I know") is 知りません (しりません, "I don't know").
[分かる](../words/分かる.md) (わかる) often means understanding something; knowing a name and understanding an explanation
are useful contrasting situations, not an absolute division of all meanings.

[短](短.md) shares the 矢-shaped left side but has a different right side. [右](右.md) also contains 口,
but its upper strokes do not make 矢.
