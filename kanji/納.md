# 納 — pay; deliver; put away; accept

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

納 is putting something where it belongs: handing in what is due, delivering goods, putting things away, and taking something in and accepting it. [納める](../words/納める.md) おさめる is to pay taxes or deliver goods, and [納得](../words/納得.md) なっとく is acceptance or being convinced. JMdict glosses one sense of 納める "to pay (fees, taxes, etc.); to deliver; to supply," a sense it restricts to the spelling 納める, and another "to accept (a gift or money)."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ノウ. One example of each:

- handing in to an authority or offering: 納入 のうにゅう, "payment (of taxes, fees, etc.)";
- putting away: 収納 しゅうのう, "storage; putting away";
- accepting: 納涼 のうりょう, "escaping the heat to enjoy the cool breeze (by the river, etc.)";
- bringing to a close: 納会 のうかい, "last meeting (of the year, term, etc.); year-end gathering (e.g. party)."

For ナッ, ナ and ナン it gives putting away, as in 納豆 なっとう, "natto (fermented soybeans)," 納屋 なや, "barn (for storage); shed; outbuilding," and 納戸 なんど, "storage room; storeroom; closet," and accepting, as in 納得. For トウ it gives putting in, as in 出納 すいとう, "receipts and expenditure (disbursements)." It marks ナッ, ナ, ナン and トウ as customary readings and also lists the kun reading いれる, which KANJIDIC2 does not list. More Daijisen examples of the ノウ senses are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the left, and [内](内.md), "within, inner," at the right. Imagine bolts of thread handed in as payment and put away inside a storehouse. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 or 内 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 納得, 納 supplies ナッ and 得 supplies トク. ナッ is a separate listed reading that ends in a small っ, so the next sound is doubled: なっ + とく gives なっとく, as in 納豆, なっ + とう. It is not the reading ノウ, which 納得 does not use. In 納める, 納 writes おさ and める is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The leading dash in -おさ.める marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced. In 御用納め, 御用 is read ごよう and 納め keeps おさめ. 納まる uses the same おさ with まる. In 出納, 出 supplies スイ and 納 supplies トウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ノウ, with 納入, 納涼 and 収納 as examples; ナッ, with 納得 and 納豆; ナ, with 納屋; ナン, with 納戸; トウ, with 出納; おさめる, with 納める and 御用納め, marked as a pair to compare with 収める; and おさまる, with 納まる and 納まり おさまり, "conclusion; end; settlement," marked as a pair to compare with 収まる. It has no separate row for -おさ.める. Practice selects ナッ and おさ.める, the readings with linked word articles. ノウ is the reading of most 納 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; ノウ, ナ, ナン, トウ, -おさ.める and おさ.まる are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [得](得.md), "gain; acquire," in 納得. 納める is 納 with okurigana only.
- [紙](紙.md), "paper," has the same 糸 at the left. [脳](脳.md), "brain; mind," is also read ノウ, and [南](南.md), "south," is also read ナ and ナン. All are different kanji.
- [払](払.md), "pay," [給](給.md), "pay; bestow," [届](届.md), "reach; deliver; notify," [収](収.md), "collect; store," [蔵](蔵.md), "storehouse; keep in storage," [貯](貯.md), "save up; store," [承](承.md), "accept; undertake," and [入](入.md), "enter, put in," overlap the senses "pay," "deliver," "put away," "store" and "accept" that 納 and its words carry; 収 and 蔵 are also read おさ.める, and JMdict's 納める includes "to put (into)." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "pay" or "put away" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the first ノウ sense, handing in to an authority or offering: 納税 のうぜい, "payment of taxes," 滞納 たいのう, "falling behind (with a payment); being in arrears; non-payment," and 奉納 ほうのう, "dedication; offering; presentation."
