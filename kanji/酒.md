# 酒 — alcohol; sake

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 酉 (no. 164) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

酒 names alcoholic drink, and context can narrow it to sake. [酒](../words/酒.md) さけ is broader than the English borrowed word "sake":
it can refer to alcohol generally. 日本酒 にほんしゅ specifies Japanese sake.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice the liquid-like 氵 beside 酉 and imagine a drink in a container.
This is a visual mnemonic, not an etymological claim.
