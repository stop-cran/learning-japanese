# 枯 — wither; dry up

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

枯 concerns withering, dying vegetation, or drying up. 枯れる かれる describes a plant withering; 枯らす からす can describe causing vegetation to wither. 枯死 こし names withering or dying.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside 古 and imagine an old, withered tree. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

- 枯れる is intransitive and ichidan: 花が枯れる はながかれる, the flowers wither. 枯らす is transitive and godan: 花を枯らす はなをからす, let the flowers wither.
- Their negative forms differ: 枯れない かれない versus 枯らさない からさない. The written る ending does not make both verbs ichidan.
- For ordinary drying of laundry, use [乾く](../words/乾く.md) かわく. Do not extend the basic plant use of 枯れる to every loss of moisture.
