# 妨 — hinder; obstruct; interfere

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

妨 is getting in the way: hindering, obstructing, interfering with what someone else is doing. [妨げる](../words/妨げる.md) さまたげる is to hinder or obstruct; JMdict glosses it "to disturb; to prevent; to obstruct; to hinder."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ボウ: getting in the way, hindering. Its examples are 妨害 ぼうがい, "obstruction; hindrance; blocking; disturbance; interference," and 押妨 おうぼう, a historical term for breaking into another's domain to commit violence or impose unjust taxes. It marks ボウ as a customary reading and gives the kun reading さまたげる, the same as KANJIDIC2. JMdict's 業務妨害 ぎょうむぼうがい, "obstruction of business; interference with business," is a legal term.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [女](女.md), "woman, female," at the left, and [方](方.md), "direction, method," at the right. Imagine a woman standing in exactly the direction you want to go, blocking your way. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 女 or 方 contributes meaning or sound, although 方 is read ホウ, close to the ボウ of 妨. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ボウ | on | 妨害 ぼうがい - obstruction; hindrance; blocking; disturbance; interference; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| さまた.げる | kun | [妨げる](../words/妨げる.md) さまたげる — hinder; obstruct |

In 妨げる, 妨 writes さまた and げる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The noun 妨げ さまたげ, "obstruction; hindrance," keeps the same さまた with げ. In 妨害, 妨 supplies ボウ and 害 supplies ガイ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ボウ, with 妨害 as its example, and さまたげる, with 妨げる and 妨げ. Practice selects さまた.げる, the reading with a linked word article. ボウ is the reading of most 妨 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; it is illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 妨げる is 妨 with okurigana only.
- [姉](姉.md), "older sister," and [始](始.md), "begin, start," have the same 女 at the left. [旅](旅.md), "journey, travel," has 方 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [障](障.md), "obstruct; hinder," [防](防.md), "defend; ward off," [乱](乱.md), "disorder; disturbance," and [害](害.md), "harm," overlap the senses "hinder," "obstruct," "prevent" and "disturb" that 妨 and its words carry; 害 appears with 妨 in 妨害, and 防 also has 方 at the right and is also read ボウ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "hinder" or "obstruct" as exclusive to any one character.
