# 粒 — grain; small particle

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 米 (no. 119) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

粒 names a small grain, bead or drop and also counts small round objects. It identifies an individual unit or its shape, not one particular material.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 米 on the left and 立 on the right; picture one grain standing apart from the rest. This is an invented modern-shape memory aid.

The canonical key 米, no. 119, appears on the left.

## Notes

- 一粒 ひとつぶ provides a usable native count. The dictionary also attests a リュウ counter reading; the リュウ row uses the unambiguous compound 粒子 instead of presenting the noun つぶ as freely pronounced りゅう.
- Compare [粉](粉.md), powder, with a single 粒. Finely divided material and an individual grain are related ideas, not a rule that each visible particle is called flour.
- [立](立.md), stand/set up, lacks 米. [位](位.md), rank/position, has a person-shaped left side rather than 米.
