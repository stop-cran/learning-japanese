# 歓 — delight; joyful welcome; cheer

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 欠 (no. 76), at the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

歓 is open delight: rejoicing and enjoying oneself, often together with other people. The linked word [歓迎](../words/歓迎.md) かんげい is JMdict's "welcome; (warm) reception," a reception given gladly. Digital Daijisen's character entry glosses カン as rejoicing and enjoying, with the examples 歓喜 かんき, "delight; great joy"; 歓声 かんせい, "cheer; shout of joy"; 歓待 かんたい, "warm welcome; friendly reception; hospitality; entertainment"; 歓楽 かんらく, "pleasure; fun; enjoyment"; and 交歓 こうかん, "exchange of courtesies (cordialities); fraternization; fraternisation."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [欠](欠.md), "lack; gap," at the right. Imagine a wide-open mouth cheering beside a more complex shape at the left. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 欠 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カン | on | [歓迎](../words/歓迎.md) かんげい — welcome; warm reception |
| よろこ.ぶ | kun | 歓ぶ よろこぶ — to be delighted; to be glad; to be pleased; JMdict marks 歓ぶ a rarely used kanji form of 喜ぶ; no word article yet for this spelling, not selected for questions here |

In 歓迎, 歓 supplies カン (read かん) and [迎](迎.md) supplies ゲイ (read げい). JMdict lists 歓ぶ, with 歓 writing よろこ and ぶ as okurigana (the kana ending that follows the kanji), only as a "rarely used kanji form" of the verb 喜ぶ よろこぶ; the ordinary spelling is 喜ぶ, which belongs to the card [喜](喜.md). 歓び よろこび, "joy; delight; rapture; pleasure," has the same status beside 喜び. KANJIDIC2 also lists ぶ as a name reading; it is not a kun reading and is not taught here. 合歓木 ねむのき, "silk tree (Albizia julibrissin)," which JMdict marks "word usually written using kana alone," is a Daijisen hard-to-read word; its pronunciation is not split between its kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カン with 歓迎, 歓声 and 交歓, and writes the character 歓 beside its older form 歡. Practice selects カン, the reading with a linked word article. よろこ.ぶ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word; being outside the practice set does not make it invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [迎](迎.md), "welcome; greet," in 歓迎.
- [欠](欠.md) is the shape at the right of 歓. [権](権.md), "authority; rights," has a part at its right that looks like the left part of 歓; [次](次.md), "next," and [欲](欲.md), "desire," also have 欠 at the right. All are different kanji.
- [喜](喜.md), "rejoice," [愉](愉.md), "cheerful; joyful; delight," [快](快.md), "pleasant; agreeable," [楽](楽.md), "music, enjoyment, ease," [享](享.md), "enjoy; receive benefits," [朗](朗.md), "cheerful; clear; resonant," [祝](祝.md), "celebrate; congratulate," [笑](笑.md), "laugh," [幸](幸.md), "happiness; good fortune," and [福](福.md), "good fortune," overlap delight, joy or enjoyment. [迎](迎.md), "welcome; greet," overlaps 歓迎 and the welcome in the title. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. 喜 also shares よろこ.ぶ. Do not treat "delight," "joy" or "welcome" as exclusive to any one character.
