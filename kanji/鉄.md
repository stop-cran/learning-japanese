# 鉄 — iron

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鉄 concerns iron, with established uses for steel and railways in complete words. 鉄道 is a railway and 地下鉄 an underground railway.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 金 beside the 失-shaped block as a lost iron tool. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not the character's historical derivation.

The radical is 金 in its narrow left-hand form.
