# 漬 — soak; pickle; steep

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

漬 is putting something into a liquid and leaving it there, and, in food, pickling. The linked [漬ける](../words/漬ける.md) つける has two JMdict senses: "to soak (in); to steep; to dip; to dunk," and "to pickle; to preserve (in salt, vinegar, etc.)." JMdict limits the pickling sense to the spelling 漬ける; the other spelling, 浸ける, covers the soaking sense only. The word article keeps it apart from 付ける つける, "attach."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for シ: dipping in a liquid, as in 浸漬 しんし, JMdict "dipping; soaking; immersing." For the kun side it lists つける, つかる and ひたす, and the compounding form つけ (づけ) in 漬物 and 粕漬 かすづけ, "fish or vegetables pickled in sake lees."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part is the whole kanji [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible." Imagine a vegetable lying under the water of 氵 in a tub. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 漬ける, 漬 writes つ and ける is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the verb is ichidan, so 漬けます, 漬けて. In 漬かる, JMdict lists 浸かる and 漬かる; its senses are "to be submerged; to be soaked," "to be pickled; to be well seasoned," with the note "esp. 漬かる," and "to be totally immersed (in a condition, e.g. laziness)." In the compounding form, 漬 changes to づ: in 塩漬け, 漬 writes づ and け is okurigana, and 茶漬け ちゃづけ, "chazuke; cooked rice with green tea poured on it," works the same way. In 粕漬 JMdict gives the spellings 粕漬け and 粕漬, plus 糟漬け and 糟漬 as "rarely used kanji form" and かす漬け and かす漬 as "search-only kanji form"; 漬 there writes づけ, with the け inside the kanji reading. In 浸漬, 漬 supplies シ.

漬物 つけもの, "tsukemono (pickled vegetables)," is JMdict entry 漬物, with 漬け物 as another spelling and 漬けもの as "search-only kanji form"; it has no word article yet. The related 漬す ひたす, "to soak; to dip; to steep; to immerse," is listed in JMdict as 漬す "rarely used kanji form" under the usual spelling 浸す.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows つける with 漬ける and 漬物, and つかる with 漬かる. It lists no on reading for 漬. Practice selects つ.ける, the reading of the linked word article. The other readings are shown with dictionary examples and are not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. Kanjipedia also lists シ, which KANJIDIC2 has too.

## Notes

- 漬ける is 漬 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji. In the examples above, [塩](塩.md), "salt," supplies しお in 塩漬け, [茶](茶.md), "tea," supplies ちゃ in 茶漬け, and 粕 in 粕漬 has no card linked here.
- [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible," is the right-hand part of 漬; [漁](漁.md), "fishing; fishery," and [渡](渡.md), "cross over," also have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but have different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [付](付.md), "attach," also reads つ.ける, so it is never offered as a wrong answer; the word article warns that 付ける is a different verb. [染](染.md), "dye; stain; infect; permeate," [湿](湿.md), "damp, moist," [沈](沈.md), "sink; subside," [浴](浴.md), "bathe, be exposed to," and [溶](溶.md), "melt; dissolve," overlap soaking in liquid. [潜](潜.md), "dive; submerge; lurk," overlaps being submerged, the 漬かる sense. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
