# 圏 — sphere; range

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

圏 names a sphere, circle or range. In [圏内](../words/圏内.md) (けんない), it can refer to being inside a physical area or an abstract range, such as the area affected by a storm or a passing range.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the enclosure around a defined area. This is a memory aid from the visible frame, not a historical explanation.

圏外 (けんがい; entry 1257090) is the corresponding “outside the range” expression. 圏 can also be used as a suffix meaning “sphere” or “range” (entry 1257080).

## Common words

- [圏内](../words/圏内.md) けんない — within an area; within range
- 圏外 けんがい — outside an area; out of range
