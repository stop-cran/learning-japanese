# 丘 — hill; knoll

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 一 (the long bottom horizontal, no. 1) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

丘 means a hill or knoll: land rising above its surroundings. The native noun is [丘](../words/丘.md) おか. A hill is typically lower and gentler than a mountain, but these everyday labels do not impose a universal numerical height boundary.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the short slant and upright strokes as a small rise standing on the long ground line. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation of the character.

## Notes

- [山](山.md) means mountain and can also describe a hill in context. [坂](坂.md) focuses on a slope or inclined road; it can be part of a hill.
- 岡 is another dictionary spelling of the native noun おか. Do not assume that every name containing either spelling is read おか.
- Distinguish the five-stroke 丘 from [兵](兵.md), soldier or troops: 兵 has two spreading strokes below its upper part, rather than 丘's long bottom horizontal.
