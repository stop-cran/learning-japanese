# 裸 — naked; bare

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

裸 describes being naked, or being bare and uncovered. Kanjipedia gives はだか and むきだしの (exposed; bare). On its own, [裸](../words/裸.md) はだか is a noun: JMdict glosses it “nakedness; nudity,” and also “bareness; nakedness; baldness; being uncovered,” “being penniless,” and “concealing nothing; openness.”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is 衤, a variant of [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment," and on the right is [果](果.md), "fruit; fruition." Imagine a fruit whose clothing, its peel, has been stripped away until it stands bare. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 衤 or 果 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ラ and はだか. KANJIDIC2 gives no name readings for 裸, and Kanjipedia lists no readings outside the Joyo table.
- Digital Daijisen lists 裸足 はだし (barefoot; JMdict entry 1547660) under 難読 (hard-to-read words). はだし is the reading of the whole word, not a reading of 裸 alone.

This card is excluded from meaning choices:

- [素](素.md) (“unadorned; element”): KANJIDIC2 lists “naked” and “uncovered” for both 素 and 裸.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 靴 (“shoes”), 帯 (“sash; zone”), and 傘 (“umbrella; parasol”); none means naked or bare.
