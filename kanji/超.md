# 超 — exceed; surpass

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 走 (no. 156) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

超 concerns exceeding an amount, limit or standard. 超過 names an excess or going over a limit; 超える and 超す are native verbs for exceeding or going beyond.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 走-shaped part carrying someone past a marker beside the 召-shaped upper-right block. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not an etymological account.

## Notes

- The inspected families include both 超える/越える and 超す/越す. Their sense notes favor 超 for exceeding and 越 for crossing, but shared entries do not establish a rigid spelling boundary for every use.
- 超える is ichidan and 超す is godan. Learn each complete verb rather than treating える and す as interchangeable endings.
