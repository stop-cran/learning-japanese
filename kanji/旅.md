# 旅 — journey, travel

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 方 (no. 70) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The old form of 旅 shows a flag (㫃, the part that became 方 on the left) over two people (从), usually explained as a troop gathered under a banner. That is the original sense of "army company"; the meaning "to travel, journey" is usually explained as a later development.
The modern right-hand part is a worn-down version of the two people, so the picture is only a mnemonic for today's shape.
It means "journey, trip", often with a hint of a long or lonely trip.

## Common words

- [旅行](../words/旅行.md) りょこう — trip, travel
- 旅館 りょかん — Japanese-style inn
- 旅 たび — journey (more poetic than 旅行)

## Notes

- 旅行 (on-reading noun, a suru-verb) is everyday "travel", while 旅 (たび) is the more literary "journey" and also appears in 旅に出る (set out on a journey).
- Contrast with [行](行.md) (go), which is the second half of 旅行, and [道](道.md) (road).
- Look-alikes: 族 (family, tribe) and 旗 (flag) also contain the same flag part (方 with 𠂉 on top).
