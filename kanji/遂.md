# 遂 — accomplish; at last

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

遂 is about carrying something through to its end. KANJIDIC2's meanings include “consummate”, “accomplish” and “attain”, and Kanjipedia gives two senses: ①とげる。なしとげる。やりとげる。 (to accomplish; to carry through) and ②ついに。とうとう。 (at last; finally). In [遂に](../words/遂に.md) ついに, JMdict's first sense is “finally; in the end; at last”; with a negative verb, its second sense is “in the end; after all; never (happened)”. JMdict marks the word as usually written in kana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** two short strokes sit at the top, then the shape 豕, and ⻌, the stroke group that wraps the left side and the bottom, carries them along. Picture something travelling the whole long road until, at last, it arrives. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 豕 or ⻌ contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists スイ (遂行; 未遂 みすい, JMdict entry 1527650; 完遂 かんすい, JMdict entry 1211480) and とげる (遂げる). つい.に is outside the official table; Kanjipedia marks ついに, and also おおせる, as outside it. Practice nevertheless selects つい.に, because 遂に is the word article that declares 遂; JMdict notes that the word is usually written in kana.
- Digital Daijisen has a headword つい written 終, 遂 or 竟. おお.せる is not a KANJIDIC2 reading.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 遂. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings かつ, つく, なり, なる, みち, もろ, やす, ゆき, and より. These are name use, not quiz readings.

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [完](完.md) (“complete”) and [了](了.md) (“completion; settling”): JMdict glosses 完遂 かんすい “successful execution; accomplishment; completion; fulfillment; carrying through” (entry 1211480).
- [終](終.md) (“end; finish”): JMdict entry 1372630 also writes ついに as 終に, a rarely used kanji form, and the 終 card lists つい.に.
- [卒](卒.md) (“graduate; finish”): the 卒 card lists ついに.
- [果](果.md) (“fruit; fruition”): JMdict 果たす はたす begins “to accomplish; to achieve; to carry out; to fulfill; to fulfil; to realize; to execute; to perform; to do” (entry 1192850), and JMdict writes おおせる as 果せる or 遂せる (entry 1192830).
- [達](達.md) (“reach, plural suffix”), [到](到.md) (“arrive; attain”) and [至](至.md) (“reach; attain”): KANJIDIC2 lists “attain” for 遂 and “attain”, “arrive” and “reach” for 至, and JMdict 遂げる sense 2 is “to arrive at (a certain outcome); to come to; to end with” (entry 1372620).
- [成](成.md) (“become”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 遂 includes なしとげる, JMdict glosses 成し遂げる なしとげる “to accomplish; to achieve; to complete; to carry out; to carry through” (entry 1375550), and JMdict 成す sense 3 is “to accomplish; to achieve; to win (fame); to gain” (entry 1157130); the 成 card lists な.す.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 既 (“already; previously”), 更 (“renew; grow late”), and 必 (“without fail”); none means accomplishing something or reaching an end at last.
