# 孤 — alone; orphan

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 子 (no. 39) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

孤 indicates being alone, as in 孤独, or being without parents, as in 孤児 (こじ), an orphan. Being lonely does not itself make someone an orphan.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare [弧](弧.md): the right half looks the same, but 弧 has a bow on the left and means an arc. 孤 has 子 on the left.

## Notes

[孫](孫.md) also begins with 子 but has thread-like strokes at the right. [幼](幼.md) has a compact left half without 子. The コ in 孤独 belongs to 孤; the following どく belongs to [独](独.md). [弧](弧.md) is an arc, not a state of isolation. [寂](寂.md) can evoke loneliness, but neither being lonely nor living alone implies having lost one's parents.
