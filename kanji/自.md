# 自 — self, oneself

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 自 (no. 132, itself a radical) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

自 is traditionally explained as a picture of a nose: in the oldest forms it looks like a nose. The story that people point to their nose when they say "I" is a folk etymology. This is why it came to mean "oneself", and from there "from, since" (in classical use). The same nose picture is the origin of 鼻 (はな, nose), where 自 appears as a component. The form has since become hard to tell from 目 (eye) with a stroke on top, but the two are historically separate characters.
In compounds 自 means "by oneself, of itself", and so "automatic, spontaneous" (自動, 自然).

## Notes

- Do not confuse 自 (ジ) with 字 (ジ, character): different shape and meaning.
- Look-alikes: [目](目.md) (eye) has no top stroke; [白](白.md) (white) has one fewer horizontal; 百 (hundred) has 一 on top. 自 is 目 with a small stroke on top.
- シ is rare and best learned as part of 自然 しぜん (nature); ジ is the usual on reading. 自然 contains 自 as "of itself": "as it is by itself".
