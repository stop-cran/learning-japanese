# 断 — sever; refuse

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 斤 (no. 69) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

断 appears in cutting something off, refusing something, and reaching a judgment.
Compare 断つ たつ, "sever," with 断る ことわる, "decline"; [判断](../words/判断.md)
はんだん names a judgment or decision, not necessarily a refusal.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** find 米 inside the open corner on the left, then the distinct 斤 on the right.
Imagine drawing a firm cutoff after weighing the options. This is a memory aid rather than an origin claim.

## Notes

断る has additional uses involving giving notice or obtaining permission; do not translate it as
"refuse" automatically in every sentence.
[新](新.md) and [近](近.md) also contain 斤. [料](料.md) shares 米 but has a different right side.
Here, "sever" means "cut off"; the two English glosses overlap.
