# 砂 — sand

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

砂 names sand, grit or fine gravel. It also appears in compounds for materials, ingredients and landscapes; the complete word need not name sand itself.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 石 beside 少 and imagine stone broken down to a few tiny grains. This visual association is not a claim about the historical formation.

## Notes

- The linked [砂](../words/砂.md) noun is すな. [砂糖](../words/砂糖.md) さとう supplies an on-reading example but names sugar, not sand used as an ingredient.
- [砂漠](../words/砂漠.md) さばく names a desert. The spelling's sand association does not mean every desert must be sandy or hot.
- 土砂 どしゃ demonstrates シャ. Do not infer this character's reading simply by finding a similar sound in an unrelated part of a compound.
