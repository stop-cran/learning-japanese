# 続 — continue

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

続 expresses something going on, following on, or forming a series. The important pair is
続く つづく, "continue," and [続ける](../words/続ける.md) つづける, "keep something going."
The difference is who or what the sentence presents as continuing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** follow the left-hand 糸 as a thread that keeps going past the 売-shaped
right side. This is a visual cue, not a derivation.

## Notes

Compare 雨が続く (あめがつづく), "the rain continues," with 勉強を続ける
(べんきょうをつづける), "continue studying."
[読](読.md) has 言 instead of 糸; [売](売.md) is a shorter visual comparison.
[終](終.md) shares 糸 but means ending rather than continuing.
