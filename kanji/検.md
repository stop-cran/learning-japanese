# 検 — inspect; examine; check

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

検 means to look into something closely to see whether it is correct, safe or true. The linked [検査](../words/検査.md) けんさ is an inspection, examination or test, and [検討](../words/検討.md) けんとう is examining or considering something before a judgment.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ケン. One example of each:

- to look into: 検査 けんさ, "inspection (e.g. customs, factory); examination; test; check; scan (e.g. MRI, PET); audit";
- to control or crack down: 検束 けんそく, "arrest; custody; detention";
- an inspection: 車検 しゃけん, "vehicle inspection";
- short for 検察庁, the prosecutors' office: 地検 ちけん, "District Public Prosecutor's Office," marked in JMdict as an abbreviation.

More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md) at the left and a crowded part at the right. Imagine checking a wooden post for cracks. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケン | on | [検査](../words/検査.md) けんさ — inspection; examination; test; [検討](../words/検討.md) けんとう — consideration; examination; investigation |
| しら.べる | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2 and shown by Daijisen as the kun reading しらべる; JMdict has no 検べる entry, and the ordinary word 調べる しらべる is written with [調](調.md) |

In 検査, 検 supplies ケン and [査](査.md) supplies サ; in 検討, 検 supplies ケン and [討](討.md) supplies トウ. Kanjipedia also lists あらためる for 検. JMdict lists 検める as a "search-only kanji form" of 改める あらためる, whose sense 3 is "to examine; to check; to inspect" with the note "occ. 検める". It is a supported spelling, but not a KANJIDIC2 reading, so this card does not add it. 検非違使 けびいし, "police and judicial chief (Heian and Kamakura periods)," and 検見 けみ, "Edo-period inspection of rice plants," are whole words with readings not split among their kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケン with 検査, 検討 and 点検; 点検 てんけん means "detailed inspection; thorough examination; checking." It prints 檢 in round brackets after 検, an older form of the kanji that this card does not teach. Practice selects ケン, the reading with linked word articles; being outside the practice set does not make しら.べる invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [査](査.md), "investigate; inspect," in 検査, and [討](討.md), "attack; discuss," in 検討.
- [験](験.md), "test; verify," [診](診.md), "diagnose; examine medically," [調](調.md), "investigate; tune," [審](審.md), "examine; judge; trial," [試](試.md), "test; try," [研](研.md), "polish; study closely," [究](究.md), "investigate thoroughly," [探](探.md), "search," [捜](捜.md), "search; look for," [尋](尋.md), "inquire; search for; fathom," [察](察.md), "observe; infer," [点](点.md), "point; dot," [監](監.md), "oversee; watch over," [改](改.md), "revise; reform," [偵](偵.md), "spy; covert investigation," and [剖](剖.md), "dissect; split open," overlap the senses of examining, testing and searching that 検 and its words carry. 験 is also read ケン, 調 is the ordinary kanji for しらべる, and 改 is the ordinary kanji for あらためる. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "inspect," "examine" or "check" as exclusive to any one character.
- [横](横.md), "horizontal," [植](植.md), "plant; put in the ground," and [橋](橋.md), "bridge," are different kanji with 木 at the left, like 検. None is read ケン.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ケン senses: 検閲 けんえつ, "censorship" and "inspection; examination"; 検診 けんしん, "medical examination (for a specific condition or disease); health screening"; 受検 じゅけん, "undergoing an inspection; undergoing an examination"; and 送検 そうけん, "committing a suspect to trial; referring a case to a public prosecutor."
