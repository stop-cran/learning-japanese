# 酌 — pour sake; ladle

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 酉 (no. 164) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

酌 is about pouring or serving sake, and also about taking in someone's feelings or circumstances. KANJIDIC2 lists “bar-tending”, “serving sake”, “the host”, “draw (water)”, “ladle”, “scoop” and “pump”. Kanjipedia's first sense is 酒をくむ。しゃくをする。 (pouring sake; serving it), and its second is about taking in another person's feelings and situation. In [酌む](../words/酌む.md) くむ, JMdict's first sense is “to pour (sake); to serve; to drink (together)”, and its second is “to consider (feelings, the situation, etc.); to sympathize with; to intuit; to understand”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [酉](酉.md), "Rooster zodiac sign," stands on the left; picture it as a squat jar. On the right is the shape 勺: a hook 勹 cupping a single dot 丶. Picture a little ladle lifting one drop out of the jar to pour for a guest. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 酉 or 勺 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists シャク, with 酌量 しゃくりょう (“taking into consideration; making allowances”, JMdict entry 1324220) and 晩酌, and くむ, with 酌む and 酌み交わす くみかわす. Kanjipedia marks くむ with 高, a school-level mark, not 外 (outside the table).
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 酌, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These eight cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [酒](酒.md) (“alcohol; sake”): KANJIDIC2 lists “serving sake” for 酌, and Kanjipedia's first sense begins 酒をくむ (pouring sake).
- [注](注.md) (“pour, direct attention”): 酌む sense 1 begins “to pour (sake)”; JMdict glosses 注ぐ つぐ “to pour (into a vessel); to fill (a cup, bowl, etc.) with; to dish out (food or drink)” (entry 2145240), the 注 card lists つ.ぐ, and Digital Daijisen's first sense of 酌 is 酒をつぐ (pouring sake).
- [飲](飲.md) (“drink”): 酌む sense 1 includes “to drink (together)”, and JMdict glosses 酌み交わす “to pour drinks for each other; to drink together” (entry 1324200).
- [盛](盛.md) (“thrive; heap up; serve food”) and [仕](仕.md) (“serve, attend”): 酌む sense 1 includes “to serve”, which overlaps “serve” in both titles.
- [慮](慮.md) (“consider; concern; forethought”): 酌む sense 2 begins “to consider (feelings, the situation, etc.)”, KANJIDIC2 gives 慮 “consider”, JMdict glosses 酌量 “taking into consideration; making allowances”, and Digital Daijisen's second sense of 酌 is about taking in the other party's views and circumstances.
- [察](察.md) (“observe; infer”): 酌む sense 2 includes “to sympathize with; to intuit; to understand”, Kanjipedia's second sense is about taking in a person's feelings and situation, and JMdict 察する さっする sense 2 is “to sympathize with; to sympathise with” (entry 1298740).
- [勘](勘.md) (“intuition; hunch”): 酌む sense 2 includes “to intuit”, and KANJIDIC2 gives 勘 “intuition”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 煮 (“boil; simmer; stew”), 焼 (“bake; burn”), and 絞 (“strangle; wring; squeeze”); none means pouring or serving sake, or ladling.
