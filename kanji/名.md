# 名 — name

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

名 combines 夕 (evening) and [口](口.md) (mouth). The commonly given explanation is that in the dark of evening one cannot see who is who, so people
announce their name aloud. Some scholars read 夕 differently, so treat the story as the usual explanation and a mnemonic.
It means "name", and by extension "fame, reputation" (有名 ゆうめい, famous).

## Notes

メイ is by far the commonest on reading; ミョウ appears in a few older or Buddhist-flavoured words (名字, 名代).
名字 and 苗字 are two spellings of the same word for a family name; 氏名 しめい is a full name as written on a form.

Look-alikes: [多](多.md) ("many") is 夕 stacked twice; 名 is 夕 over [口](口.md). 右 ([右](右.md)) also has 口 at the bottom but a different top.
