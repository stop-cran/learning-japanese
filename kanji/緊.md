# 緊 — tense; tight; urgent

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

緊 is being drawn tight: a rope, a muscle, a mood or a situation pulled taut. [緊張](../words/緊張.md) きんちょう is the word taught here. JMdict glosses it "tension; strain; nervousness; stress," "tensions (between countries, groups, etc.)" and "tonus; muscle tone." The word article teaches "tension; nervousness."

The Daijisen character entry gives 2 senses for キン. One example of each:

- Sense 1, firmly tightened: 緊縮 きんしゅく, "tightening; shrinkage; contraction; constriction," and "(economic) curtailment; retrenchment; austerity."
- Sense 2, pressing: 緊急 きんきゅう, "urgency; emergency."

It lists no kun reading in the character entry. More examples of both senses are given below the readings table.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," at the bottom, with other parts above it. Imagine a thread pulled so tight that it hums. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 contributes meaning or that the upper part contributes sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キン | on | [緊張](../words/緊張.md) きんちょう — tension; nervousness |
| し.める | kun | 緊める しめる — "to be strict with"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| し.まる | kun | 緊まる しまる — a spelling of 締まる しまる, "to tighten; to be tightened," tagged "rarely used kanji form"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 緊張, 緊 supplies キン and [張](張.md), "stretch tight; assert," supplies チョウ. The ordinary spelling of the verb しめる, "to tie; to fasten; to tighten," is 締める, which is taught on the 締 card; JMdict lists 緊める しめる, "to be strict with," as a separate entry that points to 締める. The ordinary spelling of しまる is 閉まる or 締まる, with 緊まる only as the "rarely used kanji form" in JMdict entry 1436560. Daijisen's compounds also give キン: 緊密 きんみつ, "rigour; rigor; closeness; compactness; tightly knit," 緊迫 きんぱく, "tension; strain," 緊縛 きんばく, "binding tightly; tight binding," 緊要 きんよう, "momentous; exigent; urgent; important; vital," and 喫緊 きっきん, "urgent; pressing; exigent."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キン, with 緊張, 緊密 and 緊急, and no kun reading. Practice selects キン, the reading of the linked word article 緊張. し.める and し.まる are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words; being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [張](張.md), "stretch tight; assert," supplying チョウ in 緊張; its card links back to this one.
- Kanjipedia lists the kun readings かた.い, ちぢ.む and きび.しい (and し.める) for 緊 as outside the official table (外). JMdict writes かたい with 緊い only as "word containing irregular kanji usage" (ordinary spellings 硬い, 固い and 堅い), so 緊 is not a normal writing of かたい. The cards 硬, 固 and 堅 carry かた.い, 縮 carries ちぢ.む and 厳 carries きび.しい, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
- [張](張.md), "stretch tight; assert," [急](急.md), "urgent, sudden," [締](締.md), "tighten; fasten; conclude," [絞](絞.md), "strangle; wring; squeeze," [密](密.md), "secret; dense; close," [詰](詰.md), "pack tightly; press," [勁](勁.md), "strong; taut," [硬](硬.md), "hard; stiff," [固](固.md), "firm; harden," [堅](堅.md), "hard; solid; steady," [縮](縮.md), "shrink; contract," [圧](圧.md), "pressure; oppress," [要](要.md), "necessity; essentials," and [縛](縛.md), "bind; tie up; restrain," overlap the senses of tension, tightness, urgency, pressure and binding that 緊 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. 緊, 締 and 絞 also share し.める; the cards 締 and 絞 list the pair with 緊 as an exclusion on their side, and the app treats exclusions as symmetric.
- [紙](紙.md), "paper," [話](話.md), "talk, speak," and [雨](雨.md), "rain," look plausible because 紙 has the same thread key 糸, here at the left instead of the bottom, and 話 and 雨 are common starter cards. They are read シ and かみ; ワ and はな.す; and ウ and あめ. None shares a reading or a meaning with 緊.
- Many other kanji read キン, such as [近](近.md), "near," [金](金.md), "gold, money," and [筋](筋.md), so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
