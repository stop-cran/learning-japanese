# 込 — crowded; included

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

込 can suggest things crowded together or going into something.
In verb compounds, -こむ can add an inward or thorough sense, but the resulting word must still be learned.
[申し込む](../words/申し込む.md) もうしこむ means to apply or make a request, not simply "say inside."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture 入 entering the sweeping 辶 enclosure and taking up space.
This is an invented modern-shape aid, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

The canonical radical 辵 appears as 辶. The accepted count is five strokes;
KANJIDIC2 records four as a common miscount, not an equally valid alternative.
Inside the shared 辶 outline, 入 distinguishes 込 from [近](近.md), [週](週.md), and [逃](逃.md).
