# 十 — ten

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 十 (no. 24) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

十 is a cross of a horizontal and a vertical stroke. It is commonly explained that the earliest form was a single vertical line, later given a swelling or a bar; the modern cross is the result. As a component, 十 means "ten" or "complete" (千, 半, 古).
Compare [七](七.md): in the ancient writing 七 was a cross, and the modern 七 bends the vertical stroke to separate the two. In 十 the vertical stays straight; in 七 it curls to the right at the bottom.

## Common words

- [十日](../words/十日.md) とおか — the 10th; ten days
- [十月](../words/十月.md) じゅうがつ — October
- 十分 じゅうぶん — enough (but 十分 じゅっぷん is ten minutes)
- 二十 にじゅう — twenty

## Notes

Sound changes before k/s/t/p/h: 十回 じゅっかい／じっかい, 十本 じゅっぽん／じっぽん; both ジッ and ジュッ are accepted. Larger numbers stack 十: 十一 じゅういち, 二十 にじゅう, 三十 さんじゅう.
Look-alikes: [千](千.md) (thousand, with a tilted top stroke), [七](七.md) (seven; its vertical stroke curls right at the bottom, while 十 stays straight), 干 (dry) and 才 (talent).
