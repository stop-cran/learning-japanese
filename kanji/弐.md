# 弐 — two (formal numeral)

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 弋 (no. 56) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弐 is a formal way to write the number [二](二.md), "two." It can appear in monetary certificates and similar documents instead of 二; it does not name a different number. This is a spelling to recognize, not a replacement for 二 in all ordinary writing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** spot the inner 二 below the short top stroke, with a longer crossing stroke, a sweeping hooked stroke on the right and a dot above it. Imagine extra strokes dressing "two" formally. The crossing stroke, right hook and dot make up the indexing radical 弋; this is a shape aid, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ニ | on | 弐（に）— two, the formal spelling of [二](../words/二.md) に; the linked article explains both spellings |
| ジ | on | 弐心（じしん; also にしん）— disloyalty; treachery. JMdict marks the pronunciation じしん obsolete, not every reading or use of this word. Recognition only. |
| ふた.つ | kun | 弐つ（ふたつ）— two. JMdict records this spelling for old or legal documents; use ordinary [二つ](../words/二つ.md) ふたつ for everyday counting. Recognition only. |
| そえ | kun | source-only reading retained from KANJIDIC2; no verified word example is established here, and it is not selected for practice |

Only ニ is selected for reading practice. The other readings remain for lookup. The character dictionary *Jitsu* discusses supplement/secondary senses under related そえる, but that does not establish a modern word 弐（そえ）or a spelling such as 弐える.

## Common words

- [二](../words/二.md) に — two; 弐 is its formal documentary spelling, not a separate numeral word.

## Notes

- Compare [式](式.md), "ceremony; formula": its inner [工](工.md) has a vertical connecting stroke, unlike 弐's inner 二. [武](武.md), "military; warrior," has a lower [止](止.md) shape. These are modern-shape comparisons, not shared-origin claims.
- The obsolete-pronunciation label belongs specifically to じしん in 弐心. Do not apply it to に, to every 弐 spelling, or to all pronunciations of 弐心.
- The old/documentary spelling 弐つ is verified in JMdict; the sources inspected here do not include an independent Japanese whole-word heading for it. Kanjipedia separately lists the character reading ふたつ.
