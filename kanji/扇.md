# 扇 — fan; incite

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 戶 (as 戸, no. 63), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

扇 is a fan, the act of fanning, and, by extension, fanning people into action. [扇子](../words/扇子.md) せんす is a folding fan, [扇風機](../words/扇風機.md) せんぷうき is an electric fan and [扇ぐ](../words/扇ぐ.md) あおぐ is to fan; JMdict gives 扇ぐ a second sense, "to incite; to instigate," and also spells it 煽ぐ.

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for セン. One example of each:

- a fan, folding or flat: 扇状地 せんじょうち, "alluvial fan or delta";
- sending air with a fan: 扇風機, "electric fan";
- stirring people up to act: 扇動 せんどう, "incitement; sedition; agitation; abetting," also spelled 煽動.

Daijisen notes that in the third sense 扇 can be replaced by 煽, which has no card here. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [戸](戸.md), "door; counter for households," at the top, and [羽](羽.md), "feather; bird counter," under it. Imagine feathers behind a door, waving to fan the room. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 戸 or 羽 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| セン | on | [扇子](../words/扇子.md) せんす — folding fan; [扇風機](../words/扇風機.md) せんぷうき — electric fan |

In 扇子, 扇 supplies セン and 子 supplies ス, a reading of 子 found in fixed nouns like this one rather than the standalone こ. In 扇風機, 扇 supplies セン, 風 フウ becomes ぷう after ん, and 機 supplies キ; JMdict also lists 扇風器 as a search-only spelling. 扇 read おうぎ stands alone with no okurigana; in 舞扇 まいおうぎ, "dancer's fan," 舞 supplies まい. In 扇ぐ, 扇 writes あお and ぐ is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji, but あお.ぐ is not a KANJIDIC2 reading, so this word supports no reading here. Daijisen lists あおぐ and あおる among the kun readings of 扇; the Joyo table does not. JMdict lists おおぎ for 扇 as irregular kana.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セン, with 扇子, 扇風機 and 扇状地, and おうぎ, with 扇 and 舞扇. Practice selects セン, the reading with linked word articles. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [子](子.md), "child," in 扇子, and [風](風.md), "wind; style," and [機](機.md), "mechanism; opportunity," in 扇風機. 扇ぐ is 扇 with okurigana only.
- [房](房.md), "room; tassel; bunch," [肩](肩.md), "shoulder," and [雇](雇.md), "employ; hire," also have 戸 at the top. All are different kanji.
- [促](促.md), "urge; prompt," and [督](督.md), "supervise; urge," overlap the sense of stirring people to act in 扇動, 扇情 and the second sense of 扇ぐ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "urge" or "incite" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the セン senses: 銀扇 ぎんせん, "silver-colored folding fan," 軍扇 ぐんせん, "war fan; commander's war fan," 秋扇 しゅうせん, "fan in autumn (esp. one that is no longer used); outdated item that has fallen into disuse," and 換気扇 かんきせん, "ventilation fan; extractor fan"; and 扇情 せんじょう, "stirring up strong emotions; sensationalism," also spelled 煽情.
- Under おうぎ, Daijisen gives 扇形 おうぎがた, "fan shape," which JMdict also reads せんけい, with the sense "sector" in mathematics restricted to せんけい. It lists 団扇 うちわ, "uchiwa; type of traditional Japanese handheld fan," usually written in kana, as a hard-to-read word; this is a reading of the whole word, not of either kanji.
