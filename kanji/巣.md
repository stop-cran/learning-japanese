# 巣 — nest; hive; den

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 巛 (no. 47), drawn in the modern shape as three short strokes at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

巣 is the home an animal builds: a bird's nest, a beehive, a spider's web, and by extension a den or haunt where people gather. [巣](../words/巣.md) す is a nest, hive or web; JMdict glosses it "nest; rookery; breeding place; hive," then "den," "haunt" and "(spider's) web."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ソウ. One example of each:

- a bird's nest: 営巣 えいそう, "building a nest";
- a place where something gathers: 卵巣 らんそう, "ovary," an anatomy term;
- a hideout: 巣窟 そうくつ, "den; haunt; hangout; nest; lair."

For す it gives 巣箱 すばこ, "birdhouse; nest box; bird box," and 古巣 ふるす, "old haunts; former homes." Its only kun reading is す; it does not list すくう. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find three short strokes at the top and [果](果.md), "fruit; fruition," below them, itself [田](田.md), "rice field," over [木](木.md), "tree, wood." Imagine three chicks poking their heads out of a nest built in a fruit tree. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that the short strokes or 果 contribute meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

The word 巣 is the kanji alone, read す, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 卵巣, 卵 supplies ラン and 巣 supplies ソウ. In 巣くう, 巣 writes す and くう is okurigana; JMdict also lists the spelling 巣食う, which it marks as ateji, kanji used for their sound, and 巣喰う as a search-only spelling. JMdict's second sense of 巣くう is "to hang out (of a bad group); to be based (in); to haunt." In 巣箱, 巣 keeps す and 箱 supplies はこ, which becomes ばこ by rendaku, the voicing of the first sound of a following element.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ソウ, with 営巣, 卵巣 and 病巣 びょうそう, "focus; nidus; lesion," a medical term, as examples; and す, with 巣, 巣箱 and 巣立つ すだつ, "to leave the nest," which JMdict also uses idiomatically for "to go out into the world; to become independent (of one's parents)." す.くう is not in the table. Practice selects す, the reading with a linked word article. ソウ is the reading of most 巣 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; ソウ and す.くう are illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The word 巣 contains only this kanji. The older form of 巣 is 巢, with 巛 visible at the top; the official table shows it in parentheses.
- [果](果.md), "fruit; fruition," is the whole lower part of 巣. [単](単.md), "single; simple," and [栄](栄.md), "flourish; glory," also begin with three short strokes at the top, and 栄 also has 木 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [家](家.md), "house, home," overlaps the senses "nest" and "den," an animal's or a group's home, that 巣 and its word carry; JMdict glosses 古巣 "old haunts; former homes." It is kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "home" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ソウ senses: 燕巣 えんそう, "swift's nest (used to make bird's nest soup); edible bird's nest," which JMdict marks as a rare term, and 帰巣性 きそうせい, "homing instinct; homing ability," a zoology term; and 精巣 せいそう, "testicle," an anatomy term, and 病巣.
