# 人 — person

**Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9; as a left-hand component 亻, にんべん) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

人 is a pictograph of a person seen from the side: a body with an arm or leg leaning forward. Compare [大](大.md), a
frontal figure with arms spread. As 亻 on the left it marks people and human activity: 休 ([休](休.md)), 住, 作, 体, 何, 他.

Because 人 and 入 ([入](入.md)) are nearly mirror images, they are a classic confusion. In 人 the long left-falling stroke is written first from the top and
the right-falling stroke starts from its middle; in 入 the short left-falling stroke comes first and the long right-falling stroke starts at the top, to its upper left.
