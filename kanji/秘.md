# 秘 — secret; conceal

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

秘 is keeping something secret or hidden from others. [秘密](../words/秘密.md) ひみつ is the everyday word: JMdict glosses its first sense "secret; secrecy; confidentiality; privacy" and also lists "mystery," "secret (e.g. to success)" and, as a Buddhism term, "esoteric teachings." The word article teaches "secret; secrecy."

The Daijisen character entry gives 3 senses for ヒ. One example of each:

- too deep for people to know: 神秘 しんぴ, "mystery; mysteriousness; secret";
- keeping something hidden so that its contents are not known (ひめる): 秘密;
- poor passage, as of the bowels: 便秘 べんぴ, "constipation."

Its kun reading is ひめる. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 禾, the grain-plant key, at the left and [必](必.md), "without fail," at the right. Imagine grain that must be kept hidden without fail. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 禾 or 必 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| かく.す | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 and Kanjipedia list かく.す, but no JMdict word checked here writes かくす with 秘; 隠す かくす, "to hide; to conceal," uses 隠. JMdict has the literary 秘す ひす, "to keep secret; to conceal," read ひす, not かくす |

In 秘密, 秘 supplies ヒ and 密 supplies ミツ. In 神秘, ぴ is the handakuten form of ヒ (ヒ becomes ピ after シン), and 便秘 べんぴ is read the same way (ぴ after ベン). In 秘める, 秘 writes ひ and める is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 秘か, 秘 writes ひそ and か is okurigana.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ヒ, with 秘密, 秘書 and 神秘, and ひめる, with 秘める. Practice selects ヒ, the reading of the linked word article. ひ.める, ひそ.か and かく.す are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated or explained as above. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [密](密.md), "secret; dense; close," in 秘密. 密 supplies ミツ and is also read ひそ.か, like 秘. Its title and reading overlap this card, so it is in this card's quiz exclusions and is not offered as a wrong answer. Its card links 秘 as the other kanji in 秘密.
- [玄](玄.md), "mysterious; profound; dark," overlaps 秘密 in JMdict's "mystery" sense. [私](私.md), "I; private," overlaps "privacy." [偵](偵.md), "spy; covert investigation," and [潜](潜.md), "dive; submerge; lurk," overlap "secret" and "hide." [皮](皮.md), "skin; hide," shares the English word "hide," and [被](被.md), "cover; incur," is read ヒ and means "cover." [奥](奥.md), "interior; depths," overlaps the Daijisen sense of something too deep to know. All are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [木](木.md), "tree, wood," [秒](秒.md), "second as a time unit," and [税](税.md), "tax; fiscal duty," look plausible because 木 resembles the 禾 shape, and 秒 and 税 are written with 禾 at the left. They are read ボク, モク and き; ビョウ; and ゼイ, and mean tree, second and tax. None shares a reading or a meaning with 秘.
- Other kanji read ヒ, such as [皮](皮.md), [彼](彼.md), "he; that," and [飛](飛.md), "fly," are never offered as wrong answers.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ヒ senses: 秘奥 ひおう, "secrets; mysteries"; 秘境 ひきょう, "unexplored region; secluded region; place off the beaten track"; 秘書 ひしょ, "(private) secretary"; 秘匿 ひとく, "hiding; concealment"; 黙秘 もくひ, "remaining silent; keeping secret"; and 極秘 ごくひ, "absolute secrecy." Daijisen lists 秘訣 ひけつ, "secret (method, trick, etc.); key (e.g. to success)," which JMdict also writes 秘けつ as a search-only kanji form, and 秘蔵 ひぞう, "treasuring; cherishing; prizing."
