# 捜 — search; look for

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

捜 concerns searching or looking for something. 捜す さがす is particularly associated with looking for something lost. 捜査 そうさ concerns investigation, especially criminal investigation; 捜索 そうさく can concern a search for a missing person or thing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside the enclosed upper strokes and 又-like lower shape; imagine hands searching through a container. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.

## Notes

- JMdict groups 捜す and [探す](../words/探す.md) さがす together. Its preference for 捜す with something lost and 探す with something desired is a tendency, not an exclusive division of all searches.
- Searching does not guarantee finding: 捜した さがした reports a search, not necessarily a successful discovery.
- 捜査 and 捜索 are complete words with different emphases; their shared first character does not make them interchangeable in every context.
