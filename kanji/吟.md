# 吟 — recite poetry; chant

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

吟 means reciting or chanting poetry. Digital Daijisen gives four senses: groaning, as in 呻吟; reciting or composing poems, as in 吟詠, 詩吟 and 苦吟; a poem, as in 名吟; and examining something closely, as in [吟味](../words/吟味.md) and 吟醸.

## Notes

JMdict marks reciting and appreciating traditional poetry as the original meaning of 吟味 (entry 1243390, sense 4, archaic), and Digital Daijisen also marks that sense as the original one. Kanjipedia also gives the kun readings うた.う and うめ.く, marked as outside the Joyo table (外); they are not in this card's metadata and are not quizzed.

詩 (“poem; poetry”) appears with 吟 in 詩吟, 歌 (“song, sing”) and 謡 (“ballad; Noh chanting”) overlap with chanting or singing verse, and 句 (“phrase; verse”) names a verse.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 二 (“two”), 自 (“self, oneself”), and 社 (“company, shrine”); none means reciting poetry.
