# 客 — guest; customer

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

客 describes someone in the role of guest, visitor, or customer. [客](../words/客.md) (きゃく) may be a visitor to a home or a customer in a shop.
Compounds can specify the activity: 観客 (かんきゃく) are spectators, and 旅客 (りょかく) are passengers or travelers.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** see the small 口 beneath a roof as a guest speaking at the door.
This is an invented scene, not an account of the character's history.

## Notes

The lower shape is 各, not the bars over 口 found in [害](害.md).
[名](名.md) shares a lower 口 but has no roof; [字](字.md) has 子 beneath the roof.
Translate the person's role in context rather than assuming that every 客 is buying something.
