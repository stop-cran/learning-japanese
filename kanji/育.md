# 育 — grow up; raise

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (in the 月-shaped lower part, no. 130) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

育 concerns growing up and bringing something up. [育つ](../words/育つ.md) そだつ
presents the child or plant as growing; 育てる そだてる presents someone as raising or nurturing it.
教育 きょういく connects the character with education and upbringing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture growth taking place under the small upper "cap."
The lower 月-shaped part belongs to the 肉 radical here; this memory picture is not an origin claim.

## Notes

子供が育つ (こどもがそだつ) means "a child grows up"; 子供を育てる
(こどもをそだてる) means "raise a child."
The upper parts distinguish 育 from [背](背.md), [青](青.md), and [有](有.md).
Their 月-like shapes do not make their meanings or radical classifications identical.
