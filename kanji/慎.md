# 慎 — careful; prudent; discreet

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慎 is care in what you do and say: taking pains not to make a slip, and holding yourself back. [慎重](../words/慎重.md) しんちょう means cautious or prudent; JMdict glosses it "careful; cautious; prudent; discreet; deliberate."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for シン: taking care that nothing is overlooked, being restrained. Its examples are 慎重, 戒慎 かいしん, "caution; discretion," and 謹慎 きんしん, "self restraint; moderating one's own behavior; penitence; discipline"; later senses of 謹慎 are "confinement (esp. to one's home); house arrest" and "suspension (from work or school)." It gives the kun reading つつしむ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [心](心.md), "heart, mind," as 忄 at the left, and [真](真.md), "true, genuine," at the right. Imagine a true heart that weighs every step before taking it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 忄 or 真 contributes meaning or sound, although 真 is read シン, a reading 慎 also has. Historical formation is not established here.

In 慎重, 慎 supplies シン and 重 supplies チョウ, with no sound change. In 慎む, 慎 writes つつし and む is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the noun 慎み uses the same つつし with み, and JMdict also lists 慎しみ as an irregular okurigana spelling. In 慎ましい, 慎 writes only つつ and ましい follows. JMdict also glosses 慎む "to do in moderation; to refrain (from overdoing); to abstain," and gives 慎ましい a second sense, "frugal; simple; plain."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シン, with 慎重 and 謹慎 as examples, and つつしむ, with 慎む and 慎み and the spelling contrast 慎む ⇔ 謹む. JMdict lists 謹む in the same entry as 慎む and limits its sense "to be reverent" to that spelling; 謹 has no card yet. つつましい and the bare stem つつし have no row in the table. Practice selects シン, the reading with a linked word article; つつし.む, つつ.ましい and つつし.み are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words, and つつし has no separate example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [重](重.md), "heavy; piled up," in 慎重. The older form of 慎 is 愼; the Daijisen character entry and the official table show it in parentheses.
- [慣](慣.md), "accustomed," [忙](忙.md), "busy," and [性](性.md), "nature; gender," have the same 忄 at the left with a different right side. All are different kanji.
- [警](警.md), "warn; be on guard," overlaps the sense "cautious" of 慎重; [節](節.md), "joint; season; restraint," overlaps the self-restraint of 慎む, 慎み and 謹慎; and [議](議.md), "deliberate; debate," shares the English word "deliberate" with JMdict's gloss of 慎重. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "careful" or "restraint" as exclusive to any one character.
- [慮](慮.md), "consider; concern; forethought," shares prudence and restraint: JMdict glosses 慎重 "careful; cautious; prudent; discreet; deliberate," 思慮 "prudence; discretion; thought; consideration" and 遠慮 "reserve; constraint; restraint." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
