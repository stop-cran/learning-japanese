# 写 — copy, photograph

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 冖 (no. 14) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

写 concerns copying or representing something, including photography.
[写す](../words/写す.md) is an action such as copying text or photographing a subject;
写る describes appearing in a photograph.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the bent lower strokes as a sheet being copied under the cover 冖.
This is a modern visual aid, not a historical explanation.
