# 涼 — cool, refreshing

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

涼 describes coolness or a refreshing impression. 涼しい is the useful everyday adjective for pleasantly cool conditions.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Water stands beside 京. Imagine water cooling a hot capital city and making it feel refreshing.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- 涼しい is an i-adjective; 涼やか is a na-adjective, as in 涼やかな すずやかな.
- 涼む is an intransitive godan verb meaning to cool off. Do not derive its conjugation from the adjective 涼しい.
- 涼風 also has the reading すずかぜ; the りょうふう example above specifically illustrates リョウ.
