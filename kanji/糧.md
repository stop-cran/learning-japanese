# 糧 — provisions; food supply

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 米 (no. 119), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

糧 is food carried or stored to live on: provisions. [食糧](../words/食糧.md) しょくりょう is "food (esp. staple food such as rice or wheat); provisions; rations; food supply." Read かて, 糧 alone is JMdict "food; provisions" and also "nourishment (mental, spiritual, etc.); sustenance (e.g. of one's life); source of encouragement."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for リョウ: food carried on a journey or march, or a staple food, as in 糧食 りょうしょく, JMdict "provisions," and 糧道 りょうどう, JMdict "supply line; supply of provisions." For ロウ it gives かて, with 兵糧 ひょうろう. Its kun reading is かて. 粮 is noted as a variant form.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [米](米.md), "rice grain," at the left (the radical) and [量](量.md), "quantity; amount," at the right. Imagine measuring out rice for a long march. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 米 or 量 contributes meaning or sound, although 量 is read リョウ, a reading KANJIDIC2 also lists for 糧. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| リョウ | on | [食糧](../words/食糧.md) しょくりょう — food supply; provisions |
| ロウ | on | 兵糧 ひょうろう — "(army) provisions; food"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| かて | kun | 糧 かて — "food; provisions"; 心の糧 こころのかて — "food for thought"; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 食糧, [食](食.md) supplies ショク and 糧 supplies リョウ. In 糧食, 糧 supplies リョウ and 食 supplies ショク. In 糧道, 糧 supplies リョウ and [道](道.md), "road, way," supplies ドウ. In 兵糧, [兵](兵.md), "soldier; troops," supplies ヒョウ and 糧 supplies ロウ, so the word is read ひょうろう. JMdict also lists 兵粮 for 兵糧, tagged "word containing out-dated kanji or kanji usage." 糧 alone is read かて as a whole word; the same JMdict entry (2017850) lists りょう and, tagged "out-dated or obsolete kana usage," かりて. Its spelling 粮 carries no tag.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows リョウ, with 糧食 and 糧道, ロウ, with 兵糧, and かて, with 糧. Practice selects リョウ, the reading of the linked word article. ロウ and かて are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- In 食糧, [食](食.md), "eat, food," supplies ショク. Its title overlaps this card's "provisions," so it is in this card's quiz exclusions.
- [粗](粗.md), "coarse; rough; crude," [重](重.md), "heavy; piled up," and [野](野.md), "open field," look plausible: 粗 has the same 米 shape at the left, and 重 and 野 have a 里-like shape that resembles part of 量. They are read ソ and あら.い; ジュウ, チョウ and おも.い; and ヤ, ショ and の, and mean coarse, heavy and field. None shares a reading or a meaning with 糧.
- [飯](飯.md), "cooked rice; meal," [穀](穀.md), "grain; cereals," [養](養.md), "nourish; raise; adopt," [給](給.md), "pay; bestow," [貯](貯.md), "save up; store," and [備](備.md), "prepare; equip," overlap the senses "food," "nourishment," "supply" and "stored provisions" that 糧 and its words carry. 養 also shares the reading リョウ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [両](両.md), [了](了.md), [僚](僚.md), [凌](凌.md), [寮](寮.md), [料](料.md), [椋](椋.md), [涼](涼.md), [漁](漁.md), [療](療.md), [良](良.md), [量](量.md), [領](領.md) and [養](養.md) share the reading リョウ with 糧, and [労](労.md), [廊](廊.md), [朗](朗.md), [滝](滝.md) and [老](老.md) share ロウ, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
