# 契 — pledge; vow; agreement

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

契 is a promise that binds two sides: a pledge, a vow, an agreement. [契約](../words/契約.md) けいやく is a contract or agreement, and JMdict glosses it "contract; compact; agreement." JMdict glosses the verb 契る ちぎる "to pledge; to vow; to promise; to swear."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ケイ. One example of each:

- making a promise: 黙契 もっけい, "implicit agreement; tacit understanding";
- a tally, a token split in two whose halves are matched later: 契印 けいいん, "impression of a seal over the joint of two papers; tally";
- fitting together well: 契機 けいき, "opportunity; chance," and in a second sense "trigger; cause";
- carving signs or letters, or written characters: 書契 しょけい, "script; text; characters."

For the kun reading it gives ちぎる, the same as KANJIDIC2. It also names 契丹 きったん, "Khitan people; Khitai; Kitan; Kidan," among words with a hard-to-guess reading. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** at the top left find three short horizontal strokes crossed by a vertical one, at the top right [刀](刀.md), "sword; blade," and at the bottom [大](大.md), "big, large." Imagine two people cutting notches into a stick with a blade to mark a big promise they both must keep. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 刀 or 大 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケイ | on | [契約](../words/契約.md) けいやく — contract; agreement |
| ちぎ.る | kun | 契る ちぎる - to pledge; to vow; to promise; to swear; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 契約, 契 supplies ケイ and 約 supplies ヤク, with no sound change. In 契る, 契 writes ちぎ and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 契丹 きったん, 契 supplies きっ: キツ shortens to きっ, with a small っ that doubles the next sound (gemination). [Kanjipedia's character entry](https://www.kanjipedia.jp/kanji/0001765100) marks キツ as a reading outside the official table, used in this country name, and also lists ケツ outside the table, as in 契闊 けっかつ, not seeing someone for a long time. KANJIDIC2 lists neither reading, and JMdict also reads 契丹 as けいたん, with ケイ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケイ, with 契約, 契機 and 黙契 as examples, and ちぎる, with 契る and 契り ちぎり, "pledge; vow; promise." Practice selects ケイ, the reading with a linked word article; ちぎ.る is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, but no word article uses it yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [約](約.md), "promise; approximately," in 契約.
- [大](大.md), "big, large," is the bottom part of 契, and [天](天.md), "heaven, sky," is 大 with one more horizontal stroke on top. [分](分.md), "minute, part," has at the bottom the same 刀 that 契 has at the top right. All are different kanji.
- [約](約.md), "promise; approximately," overlaps the senses "pledge," "vow" and "agreement" that 契 and its words carry, and it writes the second half of 契約. It is kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "promise" or "agreement" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, another Daijisen example of the tally sense: 契合 けいごう, "coincidence of opinions, etc.; agreement."
