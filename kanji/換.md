# 換 — exchange; replace

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

換 concerns exchanging or replacing something. 換える かえる describes making an exchange; 換わる かわる can describe something being exchanged or changing places. 乗り換える のりかえる describes changing what one rides, such as transferring between trains.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside the stacked right-hand shape spreading into 大 and imagine a hand exchanging one object for another. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified origin.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.

## Notes

- 換える and 乗り換える are transitive ichidan verbs; 換わる is intransitive and godan. The suffix row illustrates 換える inside a compound, not another independent pronunciation.
- JMdict associates the exchange sense especially with 換える and 換わる. Those are spelling tendencies, not claims that all senses of 替える/代える or 替わる/代わる belong exclusively to different spellings.
