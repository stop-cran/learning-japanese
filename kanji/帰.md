# 帰 — return home

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

帰 concerns returning home or to a place one belongs. In [帰る](../words/帰る.md), it can be translated as either "go home" or "come home," depending on the viewpoint.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the short strokes on the left as a traveler approaching the layered doorway on the right, with 巾 hanging below. This is a memory story, not a historical analysis.

## Notes

[行](行.md) in 行く いく describes going; [来](来.md) in 来る くる describes coming. 帰る adds the idea of returning to a home or base, not just motion in either direction.

Despite ending in える, 帰る is a godan verb: 帰ります かえります, 帰って かえって. Keep the classical radical 巾 distinct from the other visible parts of the character.
