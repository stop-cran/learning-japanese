# 認 — recognize; acknowledge

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

認 concerns recognizing or acknowledging something. [認める](../words/認める.md) みとめる can describe
noticing a fact or admitting it; those are distinct uses, not a promise that the fact is welcome.
確認 かくにん concerns checking or confirming.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice 言 on the left and 忍 on the right, with 心 at its base.
Imagine putting a recognition into words. This is a memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

間違いを認める (まちがいをみとめる) is "admit a mistake," not "approve of the mistake."
In [誤](誤.md), [訪](訪.md), and [語](語.md), the same 言 is followed by a different component.
Keep the 心 at the bottom right of 認 visible.
