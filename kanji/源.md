# 源 — source; headwaters; origin

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

源 is the place where something starts: where a river or spring begins, and then where anything comes from. The linked [資源](../words/資源.md) しげん is JMdict's "resources." JMdict's word 源 みなもと has two senses: "source (of a river); fountainhead" and "source; origin; root."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ゲン. One example of each:

- where a stream starts: 源泉 げんせん, "source (of a spring, etc.)";
- where things come from: 起源 きげん, "origin; beginning; source";
- one of the four clan names: 源氏 げんじ, JMdict's "the Minamoto family" (its first sense is "Genji (the character in the Genji Monogatari)"). This is a family-name use, not a reading to learn.

More examples are in the Notes. For みなもと, Daijisen's own note on the word says the み is 水 (み, "water"), the な is the particle の and もと is "origin," so みなもと is "origin of the water." That is Daijisen's note on the Japanese word, not a claim about the kanji's history.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. The right-hand part is the same shape as the kanji [原](原.md), "original; plain." Imagine water coming from an original place. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ゲン | on | [資源](../words/資源.md) しげん — resources |
| みなもと | kun | 源 みなもと — source (of a river); fountainhead; source; origin; root; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 資源, 資 supplies シ and 源 supplies ゲン. The same ゲン is in 源泉, 起源, 源氏 and 源流 げんりゅう, "source (e.g. of a river); headwaters." 水源 すいげん is "source of river; fountainhead," and 語源 ごげん is "origin of a word; derivation of a word; etymology."

JMdict also has 源 げん as a suffix, "source; origin," as in 資源. The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ゲン with 源泉, 水源 and 資源, and みなもと with 源. Practice selects ゲン, the reading of the linked word article. みなもと is shown with a dictionary example and is not asked here; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. The names はら, みな and もと that KANJIDIC2 lists for 源 are not readings taught here.

## Notes

- In 資源, [資](資.md), "resources; capital," supplies シ. Other examples above use [泉](泉.md), "spring; water source," in 源泉, [起](起.md), "wake; rise," in 起源, [氏](氏.md), "clan; family name," in 源氏, [流](流.md), "flow," in 源流, [水](水.md), "water," in 水源 and [語](語.md), "language, word," in 語源.
- [漠](漠.md), "desert; vague," [湿](湿.md), "damp, moist," and [港](港.md), "harbor," also have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [元](元.md), "origin, former," [原](原.md), [本](本.md), "book, origin," [根](根.md), "root; foundation," [基](基.md), "foundation; basis," [因](因.md), "cause; factor," [緒](緒.md), "cord; beginning," [初](初.md), "first time; initial," [起](起.md), [由](由.md), "reason; grounds," [創](創.md), "originate; create," and [資](資.md) overlap origin and source; 元 and 原 also share ゲン. [泉](泉.md) overlaps the spring sense, and [湧](湧.md), "well up; surge; arise," is the verb for water coming up. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "source," "headwaters" or "origin" as exclusive to any one character.
