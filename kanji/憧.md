# 憧 — long for; yearn; admire

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

憧 is the heart reaching out toward something it does not have: longing for a way of life, yearning for a place, admiring a person you would like to be like. [憧れる](../words/憧れる.md) あこがれる is to yearn for or admire; JMdict glosses it "to long for; to yearn for; to hanker after; to be attracted by; to be drawn to; to aspire to."

The Daijisen character entry gives ショウ, marked as a Han reading, and ドウ, marked as a customary reading, with the kun reading あこがれる, and one sense: yearning. Its only examples are one word read two ways, 憧憬 しょうけい and 憧憬 どうけい, "longing; yearning; aspiration; adoration."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [心](心.md), "heart, mind," as 忄 at the left, and [童](童.md), "child; juvenile," at the right. Imagine a child pressing a hand to the heart while watching a grown-up hero and longing to be like them one day. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 忄 or 童 contributes meaning or sound, although 童 is read ドウ, a reading 憧 also has. Historical formation is not established here.

| あこが.れる | kun | [憧れる](../words/憧れる.md) あこがれる — yearn for; admire |

In 憧れる, 憧 writes あこが and れる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the noun 憧れ あこがれ, "yearning; longing; aspiration; adoration; admiration," uses the same あこが with れ. JMdict also lists 憬れる as a rarely used kanji form of あこがれる and 憧憬れる as a search-only form. In 憧憬, 憧 supplies ドウ or ショウ and 憬, which has no card yet, supplies ケイ; JMdict lists どうけい first and しょうけい second.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ショウ, with 憧憬 as its example and the remark that 憧憬 is also read ドウケイ, and あこがれる, with 憧れる and 憧れ. トウ and ドウ have no reading row of their own in the table. Practice selects あこが.れる, the reading with a linked word article; ショウ and ドウ are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, and トウ has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 憧れる is 憧 with okurigana only.
- [憎](憎.md), "hate; detest," [怪](怪.md), "suspicious; uncanny," and [慢](慢.md), "arrogance; slackness," have the same 忄 at the left with a different right side. All are different kanji.
- [恋](恋.md), "romantic love; longing," [偉](偉.md), "admirable; great," [志](志.md), "will; aspiration; intention," [望](望.md), "hope," [希](希.md), "hope; rare," [欲](欲.md), "desire," [尊](尊.md), "revere; esteem," and [敬](敬.md), "respect; honor," overlap the senses "longing," "admire," "aspire to," "hanker after" and "adoration" that 憧 and its words carry; the word article 憧れる compares it with 望む and 尊敬. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "long for" or "admire" as exclusive to any one character.
- [懐](懐.md), "bosom; nostalgic; become attached," shares yearning: JMdict glosses 懐かしむ "to yearn (for); to miss; to feel nostalgic (for)" and 憧れる "to long for; to yearn for." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
