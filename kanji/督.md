# 督 — supervise; urge

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 目 (no. 109), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

督 concerns supervising or directing people and urging action.
[監督](../words/監督.md) かんとく is supervision, or a director,
supervisor or coach. In 督促 とくそく, the emphasis is pressing
someone to carry out an action rather than letting it be delayed.
督励 とくれい is encouragement or urging.

Daijisen separates watching/regulating from pressing for timely action.
KANJIDIC2's coach, command, urge, lead and supervise glosses belong to
this range; they do not make every 督 compound a sports term.
総督 そうとく, governor-general or governor, illustrates an
administrative title.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the eight-stroke
叔-shaped group above [目](目.md). Imagine a supervisor keeping an
eye on what is happening. This is a memory aid, not a verified historical
derivation or claim about a sound component.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| トク | on | [監督](../words/監督.md) かんとく - supervision; director; coach |

Practice selects トク, also the Joyo reading. In 監督, [監](監.md)
supplies カン and 督 supplies トク. The two characters reinforce
related supervision ideas; they do not divide every use into separate
English job titles.

Daijisen also lists かみ as a kun reading, outside the KANJIDIC2
reading list used here. It is not selected for practice. The difficult
spelling 基督 キリスト, Christ, is a complete-word reading:
JMdict marks the kanji spelling rare and phonetic, with kana usual.
It does not establish a general reading スト for 督.

## Common words

- [監督](../words/監督.md) かんとく - supervision; director; coach
- 督促 とくそく - urging; demand for action
- 督励 とくれい - encouragement; urging
- 総督 そうとく - governor-general; governor

## Notes

- [目](目.md) is only the lower eye; [眠](眠.md), sleep/sleepy,
  puts 目 on the left. [且](且.md), moreover/also, has an extended
  bottom bar and no upper 叔 group.
- Supervision and control overlap [監](監.md), [管](管.md),
  [御](御.md), [治](治.md), [制](制.md), [統](統.md) and
  [握](握.md). The regulatory branch also connects with [憲](憲.md).
- Leading or commanding overlaps [導](導.md), [将](将.md),
  [率](率.md), [命](命.md) and [令](令.md). Role or authority
  uses connect with [領](領.md), [長](長.md), [主](主.md) and
  [官](官.md); actual job titles and ranks still differ.
- Urging overlaps [促](促.md), [勧](勧.md), [励](励.md) and
  the prompting sense of [催](催.md). [責](責.md) usually concerns
  blame, but 責める せめる also has a separately recorded
  urge/press/pester sense. Urging someone does not necessarily mean
  blaming them.
- A demand for action overlaps the requesting in [求](求.md) and
  [頼](頼.md). [需](需.md) also displays English "demand," but
  economic demand or need is not the same activity as urging someone.
- English "direct" can mean supervise, whereas [直](直.md)
  concerns directness or putting something right. These comparisons are
  excluded from meaning choices without equating the Japanese senses
  or licensing arbitrary substitution between complete words.
