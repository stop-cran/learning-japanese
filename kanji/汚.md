# 汚 — dirty; defile

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

汚 concerns dirt, pollution, or defilement. 汚い きたない is an adjective. 汚す よごす is transitive, make something dirty, whereas 汚れる よごれる is intransitive, become dirty. The けがす and けがれる readings also occur in senses concerning defilement or corruption.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside the angular right-hand strokes and imagine a stain in otherwise clear water. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not verified etymology.

The canonical radical 水, no. 85, is written in its left-side form 氵.

## Notes

- Compare 服を汚す ふくをよごす, get clothes dirty, with 服が汚れる ふくがよごれる, clothes get dirty. Transitive and intransitive are different verb constructions, not merely different translations.
- JMdict places よごす and けがす in one entry and says the disgrace/defile sense is especially けがす. Especially is not an absolute rule that every physical use must be よごす or every figurative use must be けがす.
- The linked 汚れる article teaches よごれる, not the separately indexed けがれる. 汚らわしい is the i-adjective けがらわしい, not a conjugated form of 汚れる.
- 汚い ends in ない, but that is part of the adjective, not a grammatical negative. Its negative is 汚くない きたなくない.
