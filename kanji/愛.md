# 愛 — love

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

愛 names love, affection, or care. The noun [愛](../words/愛.md) あい is not restricted to romance:
love for family is one useful example. 愛する あいする supplies a verb, "to love."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** find [心](心.md) among the middle strokes and imagine it being held
carefully within the larger shape. This is a modern memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin.
