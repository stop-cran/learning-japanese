# 戻 — return; revert

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 戶 (as 戸, no. 63) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

戻 brings something back to an earlier place or state. [戻る](../words/戻る.md) もどる describes
the return; 戻す もどす describes putting something back or restoring it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a 大-shaped figure returning beneath the 戸-shaped top.
This uses the visible modern parts, not a verified origin story.

## Notes

部屋に戻る (へやにもどる) is "go back to the room"; 本を戻す (ほんをもどす) is "put the book back."
The first names the returning subject, the second an object being returned.

[所](所.md) also contains 戸 but adds 斤 at the side. In 戻, the bottom is
[大](大.md), not [犬](犬.md): do not add a dot.
[帰](帰.md) also appears in return-related words; learn each word's use rather than assuming the characters are interchangeable.
