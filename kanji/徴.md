# 徴 — sign; indication; levy

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

徴 is a sign that shows something hidden, and also an authority calling people up or collecting money from them. [特徴](../words/特徴.md) とくちょう is a characteristic or distinguishing feature; JMdict glosses it "feature; trait; characteristic; peculiarity; distinction."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for チョウ. One example of each:

- calling people up: 徴兵 ちょうへい, "conscription; (military) draft; (compulsory) enlistment";
- collecting: 徴収 ちょうしゅう, "collection (of fees, taxes, etc.); levy";
- the visible sign of a hidden meaning: 象徴 しょうちょう, "symbol (of something abstract); emblem; (symbolic) representation."

For チ it gives one of the five tones of Chinese and Japanese music, as in 変徴 へんち, which JMdict labels a music term, "note a semitone below the fourth degree of the Chinese and Japanese pentatonic scale." Its kun readings are めす and しるし; めす is not a KANJIDIC2 reading. A separate Daijisen headword treats ちょう as a noun for an omen, a summons or levying goods; JMdict's noun 徴 ちょう has the senses "sign; indication; symptom," "call; summons" and "requisition; expropriation." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 彳 at the left, [山](山.md), "mountain," over [王](王.md), "king," in the middle, and 攵 at the right. Imagine a king on a mountain raising a sign that calls people in from the roads to pay their levy. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 彳, 山, 王 or 攵 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| チョウ | on | [特徴](../words/特徴.md) とくちょう — characteristic; distinguishing feature |
| チ | on | 徴 ち - fourth degree (of the Japanese and Chinese pentatonic scale); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| しるし | kun | 徴 しるし - sign; indication; omen; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 特徴, 特 supplies トク and 徴 supplies チョウ, with no sound change. The words 徴 ち and 徴 しるし are the kanji alone, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; JMdict spells しるし in this sense 徴 or 験. In 変徴, 変 supplies ヘン and 徴 supplies チ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only チョウ, with 徴収, 特徴 and 象徴 as examples. チ and しるし are not in the table. Practice selects チョウ, the reading with a linked word article; チ and しるし are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [特](特.md), "special," in 特徴. The older form of 徴 is 徵; the official table shows it in parentheses.
- [微](微.md), "minute; faint," has 彳 at the left, 山 at the top of the middle and 攵 at the right, as 徴 does, but a different shape under 山; JMdict even lists 微候 as a search-only spelling of 徴候 ちょうこう, "sign; indication; omen; symptom." [徹](徹.md), "penetrate; be thorough," also has 彳 at the left and 攵 at the right, and [徳](徳.md), "virtue; benevolence," has 彳 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [兆](兆.md), "trillion; omen," [符](符.md), "token; sign; charm," [印](印.md), "stamp; mark," [標](標.md), "mark; signpost; goal," [症](症.md), "symptoms; illness," [収](収.md), "collect; store," [採](採.md), "pick; collect; adopt," [税](税.md), "tax; fiscal duty," [召](召.md), "summon; respectful actions," and [募](募.md), "recruit; solicit," overlap the senses "sign," "omen," "symptom," "mark," "collect," "levy," "summon" and "recruit" that 徴 and its words carry: 徴候 is also spelled 兆候, 印 and 標 are also read しるし, 徴収 is "collection (of fees, taxes, etc.); levy," 徴税 ちょうぜい is "tax collection; taxation," the noun 徴 includes "call; summons," Daijisen's めす is the kun reading of 召, and 徴募 ちょうぼ is "enlistment; recruitment." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "sign" or "levy" as exclusive to any one character.
- 徴 is a sign in the sense of an indication or omen. Signing one's name is a different idea, as in 署名 しょめい, "signature; autograph." English "sign" covers both ideas, so [署](署.md), "sign; government office," remains an ordinary wrong option for 徴.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the チョウ senses: 徴募 and 徴用 ちょうよう, "drafting; requisition; impressment; commandeering; expropriation"; 徴税, 徴発 ちょうはつ, "requisition; commandeering," and 追徴 ついちょう, "supplementary charge; additional collection"; and 徴候, 性徴 せいちょう, "sexual characteristics," a biology term, and 明徴 めいちょう, "clarification."
