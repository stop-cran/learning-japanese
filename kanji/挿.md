# 挿 — insert; plant a cutting

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

挿 is pushing one thing into or between others: slipping an object into place, sticking a cut stem into soil so that it roots, or putting flowers in a vase. [挿す](../words/挿す.md) さす means to insert or to plant a cutting. JMdict's entry for 挿す lists five senses: "to insert; to put in," "to plant (a cutting); to strike," "to arrange (flowers)," "to wear (a sword) in one's belt" and "to shut; to close; to lock; to fasten."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ソウ, putting something in between other things, as in 挿花 そうか, "flower arrangement," which JMdict notes as literary, 挿画 そうが, "(book) illustration," 挿入 そうにゅう, "insertion; interposition; interpolation," and 挿話 そうわ, "episode; side story; story within a story." It lists the kun readings さす and はさむ, and gives 挿頭 かざし, "flower fastened in the hair," as a word with a hard-to-guess reading. かざし belongs to the whole word and is not divided between its two kanji.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the left, written 扌, and at the right a part that has no card of its own. Imagine a hand pushing a flower stem down into a narrow vase. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that these parts contribute meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 挿す, 挿 writes さ and す is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 挿入, 挿 supplies ソウ and 入 supplies ニュウ; JMdict lists 插入 as a search-only kanji form. JMdict's entry for はさむ is headed 挟む and lists 挿む among its spellings with no label, alongside 挾む, labelled "word containing out-dated kanji or kanji usage." In 挿む, 挿 writes はさ and む is okurigana.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows 挿 with its older form 插 in parentheses. It lists ソウ, with 挿入 and 挿話 as examples, and さす, with 挿す, 挿絵 さしえ, "illustration (in a book, newspaper, etc.); picture," and 挿し木 さしき, "cutting; planting (a cutting)," compared (⇔) with 差す さす, one of whose senses is "to insert; to put in," 刺す さす, "to pierce; to stab; to prick," and 指す さす, "to point." It has no row for はさむ. JMdict also spells さしえ 挿し絵 and さし絵, and さしき さし木 and 挿木.

Practice selects さ.す, the reading with a linked word article; ソウ and はさ.む are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 挿す is 挿 with okurigana only, so the common words contain no other kanji.
- [捨](捨.md), "discard; abandon," [拝](拝.md), "worship; bow in respect," and [握](握.md), "grip; grasp," have the same 扌 at the left. All are different kanji. [括](括.md), "tie together; enclose," also has 扌 at the left.
- [入](入.md), "enter, put in," shares "put in," the first sense of 挿す, and writes the second kanji of 挿入; [植](植.md), "plant; put in the ground," and [苗](苗.md), "seedling; young plant," overlap planting a cutting; [挟](挟.md), "pinch; hold between," writes 挟む はさむ, which JMdict also spells 挿む; and [刺](刺.md), "pierce; prick," writes 刺す, close to sticking something in. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "insert" or "plant" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, JMdict's 一輪挿し いちりんざし, "small vase for one or two flowers," uses 挿 with its kun reading, where さし becomes ざし by rendaku.
