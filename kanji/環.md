# 環 — ring; loop; surround

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 玉 (as 王, no. 96), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

環 is a ring: a loop that comes back round to where it began, and so something that surrounds or goes around. The linked [環境](../words/環境.md) かんきょう is JMdict's "environment; surroundings; circumstances." The linked [循環](../words/循環.md) じゅんかん is "circulation; rotation; cycle; loop."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for カン. One example of each:

- a ring or ring shape: 環状 かんじょう, "annulation; ring shape; loop shape," and 一環 いっかん, "link (e.g. in a chain of events); part (of a plan, campaign, activities, etc.)";
- surrounding, going round: 環境, 循環, and 環視 かんし, "observation (by a crowd); (public) viewing; spectating; watching."

Daijisen's first sense also lists 金環 きんかん, "gold ring."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [王](王.md), "king," at the left, which is the form the jewel key 玉 takes in this position. Imagine a jewel ring on the left of a busy shape that circles round it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 王 or the right-hand part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 環境, 環 supplies カン and [境](境.md) supplies キョウ: かん + きょう. In 循環, [循](循.md) supplies ジュン and 環 supplies カン: じゅん + かん. The same カン is in 環状, 一環, 金環 and 環視.

In 環 わ, 環 is the whole word. JMdict 1555710 lists 輪 with its priority tags and 環 as a second spelling; its first sense is "ring; circle; loop." 指環 ゆびわ is "(finger) ring" and has the more usual spelling 指輪. Daijisen and Kanjipedia also list たまき and めぐる for the character; these are not in this card's metadata and have no word example here.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カン with 環状, 環境 and 循環, and no kun reading. Practice selects カン, the reading of the linked word articles. The kun reading is shown with a dictionary example; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid.

## Notes

- In the linked words, [境](境.md), "boundary; border," supplies キョウ in 環境 and [循](循.md), "follow; cycle," supplies ジュン in 循環. [指](指.md), "finger," writes ゆび in 指環.
- [現](現.md), "actual; present," [理](理.md), "reason, logic," and [琴](琴.md), "koto; zither," also have the jewel shape 王 at the left or top. They look alike at a glance but have different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [輪](輪.md), "wheel; ring," also reads わ and is a direct overlap. [円](円.md), "yen, circle," [丸](丸.md), "round; circle," [囲](囲.md), "surround; enclose," and [包](包.md), "wrap; enclose," overlap the ring and surround senses. [周](周.md), "circumference; circuit," [巡](巡.md), "go around; patrol; tour," which also reads めぐ.る, [循](循.md), [回](回.md), "revolve; times," and [旋](旋.md), "rotate; return; mediate," overlap the circulation sense. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Many other kanji also read カン, such as 関, 間, 感, 観 and 館, and are never offered as wrong answers.
