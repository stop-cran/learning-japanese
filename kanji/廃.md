# 廃 — abolish; fall into disuse

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 广 (no. 53) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

廃 appears both when someone abolishes a practice and when something falls into disuse without anyone deciding to end it.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 発 tucked under 广. Picture a notice saying an old rule is no longer in force.

## Notes

[発](発.md) lacks the shelter; [庭](庭.md) and [庫](庫.md) put other elements under it. [止](止.md) means stop, [捨](捨.md) means discard, and [絶](絶.md) can mean cease. 廃止 targets an existing practice, rule or service.
