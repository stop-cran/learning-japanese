# 灰 — ashes

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 火 (no. 86) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

灰 means ash, the powdery residue of burning. Compounds also use it in material names, such as 石灰, lime.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture [火](火.md), fire, beneath the overhanging upper strokes; ash remains after it burns out.

## Notes

- 灰皿 reads はいざら: the 皿 element changes from さら to ざら in this compound.
- Keep ash distinct from [炭](炭.md), charcoal or coal. Related materials are not interchangeable names.
