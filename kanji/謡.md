# 謡 — ballad; Noh chanting

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

謡 covers songs sung to a melody and the chanting of Noh plays. KANJIDIC2 lists “song”, “sing”, “ballad” and “noh chanting”. Kanjipedia's first sense is うたう。うた。はやりうた (to sing; a song; a popular song), and its third is 能のうたい (the chanting of Noh). [歌謡](../words/歌謡.md) かよう is glossed “song; ballad” in JMdict, and [民謡](../words/民謡.md) みんよう “folk song; popular song”. The Noh sense appears in 謡曲 ようきょく, “noh song” (JMdict entry 1546870).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [言](言.md), "say; words," with [口](口.md), "mouth," inside it, stands on the left. On the right is the shape 䍃: ⺤ sits above [缶](缶.md), "tin can; container," which has [山](山.md), "mountain," inside it. Picture someone singing words across the mountains while tapping out the beat on a tin can. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 言 or 䍃 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ヨウ, うたい (謡, 素謡 すうたい, JMdict entry 2829730) and うたう (謡う, paired with 歌う, JMdict entry 1588120), and gives the old form 謠. Kanjipedia marks うたい and うたう with 高, a school-level mark; both are in the Joyo table.
- Digital Daijisen also gives うた as a kun reading; no checked JMdict word writes うた with 謡. Kanjipedia's second sense is うわさ (rumour).
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 謡.

These six cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [歌](歌.md) (“song, sing”): KANJIDIC2 gives 謡 “song” and “sing”, and JMdict glosses 歌謡 “song; ballad”.
- [詩](詩.md) (“poem; poetry”): Kanjipedia gives 詩 ① し。うた and 謡 ① うたう。うた, so both characters are glossed うた.
- [曲](曲.md) (“bend; musical piece”): JMdict glosses 謡曲 “noh song”, and a song is a musical piece.
- [凱](凱.md) (“victory song; triumph”): its “victory song” overlaps KANJIDIC2's “song” for 謡.
- [楽](楽.md) (“music, enjoyment, ease”): its “music” overlaps the songs and chanting named by KANJIDIC2's “song” and “noh chanting”.
- [詞](詞.md) (“words; lyrics; part of speech”): its “lyrics” are the words of a song, overlapping KANJIDIC2's “song” and “ballad”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 舞 (“dance”), 笛 (“flute; whistle”), and 茶 (“tea”); none means a ballad or Noh chanting.
