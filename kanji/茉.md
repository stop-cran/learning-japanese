# 茉 — jasmine

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

茉 occurs in jasmine names, especially [茉莉花](../words/茉莉花.md)
まつりか, Arabian jasmine. Learn 茉莉 as the plant-name unit rather
than treating 茉 and [莉](莉.md) as two independent everyday nouns.
The longer name identifies a plant, not just a cut flower.
The 艸 radical is visible as 艹 above 末.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine a jasmine plant under
the 艹 canopy, with a 末-shaped label below it. The lower shape is
[末](末.md), with the longer upper horizontal, not [未](未.md).
This is an invented memory scene, not a historical derivation.

The complete KANJIDIC2 on list stays in source order. Its kun list is empty.
字通 separately gives the character gloss/kun label まつり; that does not
change the frozen metadata or make this an ordinary standalone noun.
An inspected Wiktionary extract lists マチ and treats み as kun.
KANJIDIC2 instead records み separately as nanori, not in its kun list;
Daijisen does not corroborate マチ. These supplemental classifications
are not promoted to practice or silently inserted into the full lists.

## Common words

- [茉莉花](../words/茉莉花.md) まつりか - Arabian jasmine (Jasminum sambac).
- 茉莉 まつり - the same plant; JMdict labels this shorter name usually written in kana.

## Notes

- Grade 9 is the source's name-use classification. The verified plant names are non-name uses; personal-name readings are not guessed here.
- 野茉莉 えごのき is a dictionary-listed rare spelling of Japanese snowbell, a different plant. Its whole-word reading does not establish エゴ or ノキ as readings of 茉.
- 茉莉 まつり and 祭り まつり, festival, are different words.
- [莉](莉.md) overlaps the jasmine meaning. [花](花.md), flower, [咲](咲.md), blossom/bloom, [植](植.md), plant/put in the ground, [木](木.md), tree/wood, and [樹](樹.md), tree/establish, have broader or different uses. Their direct guards protect unresolved broad English choices, not universal Japanese synonymy.
- [本](本.md), book/origin, [古](古.md), old, and [石](石.md), stone, are shape comparisons, not jasmine meanings. 本 has its short mark below the crossing; 古 and 石 have a 口-shaped bottom. 未 is not a curated wrong choice: its ミ conflicts with the conservatively supplied み claim for 茉.
