# 穏 — calm; gentle; peaceful

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

穏 is calm and peaceful: no agitation, no roughness. [穏やか](../words/穏やか.md) おだやか has two JMdict senses: "calm; quiet; gentle; peaceful; mild" and "moderate; reasonable; amicable." It describes a calm sea, mild weather, a gentle person or a reasonable, non-extreme attitude.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for オン: settled, calm, おだやか. Its examples include 穏健 おんけん, 穏和 おんわ, 安穏, 静穏, 不穏 ふおん and 平穏 へいおん. Its kun reading is おだやか. 〔穩〕 is given as the old form.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a grain-plant shape, 禾, at the left (the radical) and [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the lower right. Imagine a heart at rest beside a calm field of grain. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

穏やか is read おだやか as one native word. The card records that reading as おだ.やか: 穏 is written for おだ, and やか is the kana ending that follows it. The quiz selection rests on this linked word alone. In 平穏, 平 supplies ヘイ and 穏 supplies オン. Daijisen's other examples also give オン: 穏健 "moderate (view, politics, etc.); temperate; sensible; sober; non-radical" and 不穏 "disquieting; threatening; turbulent; restless; unsettled; disturbing."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows オン, with 穏和, 穏当 and 平穏, and おだやか, with 穏やかだ. It adds a note: 安穏 is read アンノン. JMdict lists 穏和 おんわ ("gentle (nature, personality, etc.); mild; quiet; pleasant" and "moderate (statement, measure, etc.); mild; temperate"; its sense "mild (climate); temperate; clement; pleasant; agreeable" is written 温和 only) and 穏当 おんとう ("proper; reasonable; right; appropriate"); it lists 安穏 as あんのん and あんおん ("peace; quiet; tranquility; tranquillity"). Practice selects おだ.やか, the reading of the linked word article. オン is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 穏やか is 穏 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji. In 平穏, [平](平.md), "flat; peaceful," supplies ヘイ. Kanjipedia also lists やす.らか for 穏, which this card does not teach; [安](安.md) is the card that carries やす.らか.
- [木](木.md), "tree, wood," [田](田.md), "rice field," and [花](花.md), "flower," look plausible because 木 resembles the grain-plant 禾 shape at the left of 穏, and 田 and 花 belong to the same field-and-plants picture as the mnemonic. All three are Starter cards with kun readings, so they fill every wrong slot of the おだ.やか reading question in all pools, which keeps the reading やす.らか of 安 from appearing as a wrong option; keep Starter cards here if a distractor is ever replaced. They are read ボク, モク and き; デン and た; and カ, ケ and はな, and mean tree, rice field and flower. None shares a reading or a meaning with 穏.
- [安](安.md), "cheap, peaceful," [平](平.md), [静](静.md), "quiet," [寧](寧.md), "polite; peaceful," [凪](凪.md), "calm seas; lull," [寂](寂.md), "lonely; quiet; desolate," [優](優.md), "gentle; superior," [和](和.md), "harmony; Japanese style," [温](温.md), "warm, mild," [柔](柔.md), "soft; supple," [澄](澄.md), "clear; become transparent; serene," and [妥](妥.md), "appropriate; settled," overlap the senses "peaceful," "quiet," "gentle," "mild," "calm," "serene" or "settled" that 穏 and its words carry. 安 also shares the Kanjipedia reading やす.らか. They are kept out of this card's quiz options.
- [恩](恩.md), [温](温.md), [遠](遠.md), [音](音.md) and [飲](飲.md) share the reading オン with 穏, so none of them is offered as a wrong answer.
