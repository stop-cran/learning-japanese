# 黒 — black

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 黒 (no. 203) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

黒 is the shinjitai (modern simplified) form of 黑. The old form is usually explained as a fire (the bottom, 灬) beneath a chimney or window, with soot on top, hence "black". The upper part
of the modern form looks like 里 (village), but it has no connection with that kanji; the history of the shape is debated, so the soot story is a mnemonic.

## Notes

Its opposite is [白](白.md). Okurigana (the kana after the kanji, like い in 黒い): 黒い (adjective) versus 黒 (noun). Beware of 里 (village), which is not the same as the top part. The compounds 黒字 くろじ (a profit, "in the black")
and 赤字 あかじ (a loss, "in the red") pair with [赤](赤.md).
