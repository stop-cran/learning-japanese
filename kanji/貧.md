# 貧 — poor; impoverished

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

貧 expresses poverty or a shortage of resources. [貧しい](../words/貧しい.md) まずしい can describe
someone who lacks the means to live comfortably. The focus is insufficiency, not the low price of an item.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper 分 dividing up the lower 貝 until very little is left.
This is an invented memory aid based on the visible components, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

The adjective 貧しい can go directly before a noun; 貧乏 uses な when modifying one.
Recognize the whole upper [分](分.md), not just the small cap found in [負](負.md).
[買](買.md) has a wider, net-shaped top over the same lower 貝.
