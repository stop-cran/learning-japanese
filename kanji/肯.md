# 肯 — affirm; consent

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

肯 contributes affirming, accepting or consenting. 肯定 concerns affirmation; that is not automatically a statement that something is liked or that formal permission has been granted.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the 止-shaped top above the flesh-shaped lower part and imagine pausing before saying yes. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical explanation of those components.

The canonical key 肉, no. 130, is shown below the top part in a compact 月-shaped form.

## Notes

- The metadata preserves KANJIDIC2's boundary がえんじ.る, but JMdict spells the complete word 肯んじる がえんじる. Do not mechanically replace everything before the dot with one kanji and invent 肯る. 肯んじる is ichidan: 肯んじます がえんじます.
- JMdict also lists 肯じる for がえんじる but marks that spelling irregular okurigana. The reading row uses the unflagged 肯んじる spelling; the source boundary does not override the word dictionary.
- [否定](../words/否定.md) ひてい, denial/negation, supplies a useful contrast to 肯定. [賛成](../words/賛成.md) さんせい can express agreement or support, but the complete expressions are not unrestricted substitutes.
- Distinguish [止](止.md), stop, and [育](育.md), grow up/raise, by their complete shapes. The flesh-shaped lower part alone does not make 肯 an anatomical word.
