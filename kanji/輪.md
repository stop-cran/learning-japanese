# 輪 — wheel; ring

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

輪 concerns a wheel, ring, circle or loop. 車輪 is a vehicle wheel, while 指輪 is a ring worn on a finger.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 車 on the left beside the 侖-shaped block on the right as a stack of circular wheels. This is a visual association, not a historical or phonetic claim.

## Notes

- 輪 also counts wheels and flowers: 一輪 いちりん can mean one wheel or one flower. The counter use does not make every リン compound a flower word.
- 指輪 is ゆびわ, using the native わ, while 車輪 is しゃりん, using リン. Learn the complete combinations.
- Compare [論](論.md), with 言, and [輸](輸.md), with a different right-hand shape. Shared 車 does not make wheel and transport interchangeable meanings.
