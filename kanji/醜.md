# 醜 — ugly; unsightly

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 酉 (no. 164) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

醜 is ugliness, in looks or in conduct. KANJIDIC2 lists “ugly”, “unclean”, “shame” and “bad looking”. Kanjipedia's first sense is みにくい。みっともない。けがらわしい。わるい。 (ugly; unseemly; foul; bad), and it gives 美 as the opposite. Digital Daijisen describes looks or behaviour bad enough to give others an unpleasant feeling. In [醜い](../words/醜い.md) みにくい, JMdict's first sense is “ugly; unattractive; bad-looking”, and its second is “unsightly; unseemly; disgraceful; shameful; dishonorable”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [酉](酉.md), "Rooster zodiac sign," stands on the left; picture it as a squat jar. On the right is the shape 鬼: at its top 甶, with [田](田.md), "rice field," inside, then 儿, which KanjiVG marks as a variant of [八](八.md), "eight," and 厶. Picture a lumpy, boxy head on spindly legs crouching beside the jar: not a pretty sight. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 酉 or 鬼 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists シュウ, with 醜悪 しゅうあく (“ugly; unsightly; hideous; mean; repulsive; disgraceful”, JMdict entry 1333820), 醜態 and 美醜 びしゅう (“beauty or ugliness”, JMdict entry 1486430), and みにくい, with 醜い and 醜さ みにくさ. Digital Daijisen gives the kun readings みにくい and しこ.
- Kanjipedia lists にくむ, たぐい and しこ outside the table (外); にくむ and たぐい go with its second and third senses. They are not KANJIDIC2 readings and have no table rows here.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 醜. Digital Daijisen gives the name reading むね; this is name use, not a quiz reading.

These thirteen cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [恥](恥.md) (“shame”): KANJIDIC2 lists “shame” for 醜; Digital Daijisen's headword しゅう【醜】 has the second sense はじ (shame), JMdict 醜 しゅう sense 2 is “shame; disgrace” (entry 2588860), and the 恥 card lists はじ.
- [汚](汚.md) (“dirty; defile”): KANJIDIC2 lists “unclean” for 醜, Kanjipedia's first sense includes けがらわしい (foul), and the 汚 card lists けが.らわしい.
- [悪](悪.md) (“bad, evil”): Kanjipedia's first sense includes わるい (bad) and its second begins にくむ (hate); the 悪 card lists わる.い and にく.む, and JMdict glosses 醜悪 “ugly; unsightly; hideous; mean; repulsive; disgraceful”.
- [憎](憎.md) (“hate; detest”): Kanjipedia's second sense begins にくむ, and the 憎 card lists にく.む.
- [嫌](嫌.md) (“dislike; suspicion”): Kanjipedia's second sense includes きらう (dislike), and KANJIDIC2 gives 嫌 the kun reading きら.う.
- [似](似.md) (“resemble”): Kanjipedia's third sense begins 似ている (resembling).
- [等](等.md) (“equal; and so on”), [同](同.md) (“same, equal”) and [匹](匹.md) (“small-animal counter; equal”): Kanjipedia's third sense includes ひとしい (equal); the 等 card lists ひと.しい, and JMdict 等しい ひとしい sense 1 is “equal; identical; the same” (entry 1449330).
- [類](類.md) (“kind; category”), [種](種.md) (“seed; kind”) and [般](般.md) (“general; kind; category”): Kanjipedia's third sense includes たぐい (kind); the 類 card lists たぐ.い, and JMdict 類い たぐい sense 1 is “kind; sort; type” (entry 1596870).
- [謙](謙.md) (“humble; modest”): JMdict 醜 しこ sense 2 is “unworthy; insignificant; humble” (entry 2843271).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 短 (“short”), 赤 (“red”), and 新 (“new”); each names a plain quality such as length, colour or newness, and none means ugly or unsightly.
