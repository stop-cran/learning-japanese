# 愉 — cheerful; joyful; delight

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

愉 is a heart that is clear and happy: cheerfulness, joy and delight. [愉快](../words/愉快.md) ゆかい is the everyday word; JMdict glosses it "pleasant; delightful; enjoyable; joyful; cheerful; amusing."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ユ: the heart is bright and clear and one feels happy. Its examples are 愉悦 ゆえつ, "joy; pleasure; delight"; 愉快; and 愉楽 ゆらく, "pleasure." Its kun readings are たのしい and たのしむ, the same as KANJIDIC2. JMdict also has 不愉快 ふゆかい, "unpleasant; disagreeable; displeasing; uncomfortable; unhappy," with the prefix [不](不.md), "not, non-."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 忄, the narrow form of [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the left, and 兪 at the right. Imagine a heart lifted up and carried along by good news. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 忄 or 兪 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| たの.しい | kun | 楽しい たのしい - enjoyable; fun; pleasant; happy; JMdict lists 愉しい as a rarely used kanji form; no word article uses this spelling yet, not selected for questions here |
| たの.しむ | kun | 楽しむ たのしむ - to enjoy; to take pleasure in; JMdict lists 愉しむ as a rarely used kanji form; no word article uses this spelling yet, not selected for questions here |

In 愉快, 愉 supplies ユ and 快 supplies カイ, with no sound change. In the spelling 愉しい, 愉 writes たの and しい is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 愉しむ uses the same たの with しむ. JMdict lists these spellings, and 愉しみ for 楽しみ たのしみ, as rarely used kanji forms; the usual spellings use [楽](楽.md).

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ユ, with 愉快 and 愉悦 as examples. Practice selects ユ, the reading with a linked word article. たの.しい and たの.しむ are retained from KANJIDIC2; they are not Joyo readings. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [快](快.md), "pleasant; agreeable," in 愉快. 兪, the right part of 愉, has no card yet; [輸](輸.md), "transport; convey," has the same right part and is also read ユ.
- [忙](忙.md), "busy," [怪](怪.md), "suspicious; uncanny," and [慣](慣.md), "accustomed," have the same 忄 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [快](快.md), "pleasant; agreeable," [楽](楽.md), "music, enjoyment, ease," [喜](喜.md), "rejoice," [享](享.md), "enjoy; receive benefits," [娯](娯.md), "recreation; amusement," and [幸](幸.md), "happiness; good fortune," overlap the senses "pleasant," "enjoyable," "joy," "pleasure" and "happy" that 愉 and its words carry; 快 appears with 愉 in 愉快, and 楽 in 愉楽 and the usual spellings of 楽しい and 楽しむ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "joy" or "pleasure" as exclusive to any one character.
- [慰](慰.md), "console; comfort; solace," shares amusement and good spirits: JMdict glosses 慰める "to comfort; to console; to amuse" and 慰む "to feel comforted; to be in good spirits," and 愉快 "pleasant; delightful; enjoyable; joyful; cheerful; amusing." The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
