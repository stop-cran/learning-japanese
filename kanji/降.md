# 降 — descend

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (as left-hand 阝, no. 170) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

降 covers coming down. [降る](../words/降る.md) ふる is used for rain, snow, and similar falling material;
降りる おりる can mean getting off a vehicle. These are different words, not polite and casual readings of the same verb.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the steps suggested by left-hand 阝 and the low right-hand strokes to imagine descending.
This is a visual reminder, not etymology.

## Notes

雨が降る (あめがふる) means "rain falls"; バスを降りる (バスをおりる) means "get off the bus."
For an ordinary object dropping, use [落ちる](../words/落ちる.md) おちる, "fall"; 降る is not the general verb for every falling object.
[限](限.md) shares left-hand 阜, while [部](部.md) has right-hand 邑.
