# 欠 — lack; gap

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 欠 (no. 76) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

欠 marks something missing or lacking. [欠席](../words/欠席.md) (けっせき) is non-attendance.
欠ける (かける) can describe a chipped object, a missing member, or a quality that is lacking.
The English translation depends on what is incomplete.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper hooked stroke reaching toward a missing piece above the two spreading lower strokes.
This is a memory aid, not the radical's historical origin.

- 欠く かく — lack something

## Notes

欠ける is intransitive: it describes the incomplete thing. 欠く is transitive and can take what is lacking with を.
Compare [次](次.md), which adds two marks on the left.
出席 (しゅっせき), "attendance," is the useful contrast to 欠席.
