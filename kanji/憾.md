# 憾 — regret; dissatisfaction

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

憾 is the feeling that something is unsatisfactory and regrettable. It appears in 遺憾, the only example the standard-use table gives for this kanji.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 忄, the narrow form of 心 “heart,” is on the left, and [感](感.md), “feeling,” is on the right; 感 is also read カン, like 憾. 感 already has 心 at the bottom, so 憾 has a heart in two places.

## Notes

[感](感.md) forms the right part and is also read カン; [減](減.md) shares 咸, the top of 感, and means decrease; [憶](憶.md) also starts with 忄 and means remember. [恨](恨.md) and [悔](悔.md) relate to resentment and regret, [惜](惜.md) to finding something a pity, and [嘆](嘆.md) to lament. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 30](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=30) gives 憾 only カン, with 遺憾.
