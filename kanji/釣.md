# 釣 — angling; lure

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

釣 is fishing with a hook and line, and by extension luring someone in. [釣る](../words/釣る.md) つる is glossed in JMdict as "to fish; to angle; to catch" and also "to lure in; to tempt; to attract; to entice; to allure." The noun [釣り](../words/釣り.md) つり is "fishing; angling" and, usually written in kana, "change (for a purchase)"; Kanjipedia's second sense of 釣 is likewise つりせん, the change handed back. [釣り合う](../words/釣り合う.md) つりあう is "to balance; to be in harmony; to be in equilibrium" and "to suit; to go well together; to be a good match."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is [金](金.md), "gold, money," and on the right is 勺, a shape made of 勹 with 丶 tucked inside. Imagine a little metal hook curling round a single drop of bait, dangling in the water to catch a fish. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 金 or 勺 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- In 釣る, 釣 writes つ and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the noun 釣り ends in り instead. The trailing dash in つ.り- marks a use before another element, as in 釣り合う; it is not pronounced. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so つ.る supplies the one question for that stem, shown as つる; つ.り and つ.り- are retained from KANJIDIC2.
- Joyo lists チョウ, with 釣果, 釣魚 ちょうぎょ ("fishing; angling") and 釣艇 ちょうてい (marked archaic in JMdict, "fishing boat"), and つる, with 釣る, 釣り and 釣り合い つりあい ("balance; equilibrium"). Kanjipedia lists no readings outside the table.
- KANJIDIC2 records つり as a name reading; Digital Daijisen gives no name readings.

These five cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [魚](魚.md) (“fish”): KANJIDIC2 lists “fish” for 釣, Kanjipedia's first sense is 魚をつる (to catch fish), and JMdict glosses 魚釣り さかなつり (1233180) as “fishing.”
- [漁](漁.md) (“fishing; fishery”): JMdict glosses 釣り as “fishing; angling,” which overlaps the title's “fishing.”
- [捕](捕.md) (“capture”): KANJIDIC2 lists “catch” for 釣, and the first sense of 釣る includes “to catch.”
- [誘](誘.md) (“invite; tempt”): KANJIDIC2 lists “allure” for both 釣 and 誘, and the second sense of 釣る is “to lure in; to tempt; to attract; to entice; to allure.”
- [艶](艶.md) (“gloss; luster; allure”): KANJIDIC2 lists “allure” for 釣, which matches the title's “allure.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 刈 (“mow; reap”), 掘 (“dig; excavate”), and 潜 (“dive; submerge; lurk”); none means angling or luring.
