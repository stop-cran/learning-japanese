# 融 — melt; circulate

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 虫 (no. 142) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

融 has two useful meaning groups. KANJIDIC2 gives “dissolve” and “melt,” and Kanjipedia's first sense is a solid becoming liquid. Kanjipedia's third sense is passing through and circulating (とおる・通じる・流通する), and Digital Daijisen lists [金融](../words/金融.md) under its sense of passing through without obstruction. JMdict glosses 金融 きんゆう as “finance; financing; credit transacting; loaning of money; circulation of money.” The whole word reads きんゆう, with ユウ for 融.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the tall shape 鬲 (a flat stroke over a small box, which sits on a taller frame with a few strokes inside) stands at the left, and [虫](虫.md), "insect; bug," sits at the right. Imagine a bug perched beside that tall boxy stack while the ice under it melts and trickles away. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 鬲 or 虫 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ユウ only. Kanjipedia marks とける and とおる outside the Joyo table.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name reading あきら. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings あき, あきら, すけ, とお, ながし, みち, and よし. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.

These four cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [溶](溶.md) (“melt; dissolve”): KANJIDIC2 gives 融 “dissolve” and “melt,” and JMdict lists 融ける and 融かす as spellings of 溶ける and 溶かす.
- [通](通.md) (“pass through; commute”) and [流](流.md) (“flow”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 融 is とおる・通じる・流通する (pass through; circulate), and JMdict's gloss of 金融 includes “circulation of money.”
- [和](和.md) (“harmony; Japanese style”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 融 is やわらぐ (soften; become harmonious), and JMdict glosses 融和 ゆうわ as “harmony; reconciliation.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 水 (“water”), 雪 (“snow”), and 氷 (“ice; freeze”); none means to melt or to circulate.
