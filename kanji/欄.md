# 欄 — column; form field; railing

**Strokes:** 20 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

欄 is a marked-off section: a column of a page, a field in a form, and, in its original sense, a railing. The linked [欄](../words/欄.md) らん names a section or column of printed material, or a space or field in a form or web page. JMdict gives らん three senses: "section (e.g. in a newspaper); column; page," "field (in a form, web page, etc.); blank," and "handrail; railing; banister; balustrade," the last with the note "orig. meaning."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ラン. One example of each:

- a handrail: 欄干 らんかん, "railing; guard rail; handrail; banister; balustrade; parapet";
- an enclosure made of wood laid across: Daijisen names one example, but JMdict has no ordinary word for it, so this card names none;
- a marked-off part of a page: 欄外 らんがい, "margin."

More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md) at the left and [門](門.md) enclosing parts at the right. Imagine a wooden railing in front of a gate. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 木 or 門 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ラン | on | [欄](../words/欄.md) らん — section; column; field; blank |
| てすり | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict's ordinary word 手すり てすり, "handrail; railing; banister," is written 手すり or 手摺り, not with 欄. Daijisen glosses a sense of 欄 as てすり |

欄 read らん stands alone as a word. In 欄干, 欄 supplies ラン and 干 supplies カン; in 欄外 and 空欄 くうらん, "blank column; blank space," 欄 supplies ラン. The handrail sense also has the separate word 欄 おばしま, "handrail; railing; banister; balustrade," which JMdict marks archaic and lists as its own entry; it is not a KANJIDIC2 reading and this card does not add it. Kanjipedia also lists わく and おり for 欄, which this card does not add either.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ラン with 欄干, 欄外 and 空欄. It prints a parenthesized form after 欄, a compatibility form of the same kanji that this card does not teach separately. Practice selects ラン, the reading with a linked word article; てすり comes from KANJIDIC2, and being outside the practice set does not make it invalid.

## Notes

- The common word is the single character 欄, so it has no other kanji.
- [部](部.md), "section; department," [額](額.md), "forehead; frame; sum," [井](井.md), "well; well frame," [空](空.md), "sky; empty," [帳](帳.md), "notebook; account book; register," [区](区.md), "ward; district," [垣](垣.md), "fence; hedge," [囲](囲.md), "surround; enclose," [列](列.md), "row; queue," [件](件.md), "case; item," [条](条.md), "clause; condition; item; line," [表](表.md), "surface; chart," and [段](段.md), "step; level," overlap the senses of a section, a field, a frame and a railing. 空 appears in 空欄. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "column," "section" or "field" as exclusive to any one character.
- [門](門.md), "gate; doorway," is part of the right side of 欄. [横](横.md), "horizontal," and [模](模.md), "imitate; model; pattern," have 木 at the left, like 欄. None is read ラン.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples: 高欄 こうらん, "railing; handrail; balustrade" [field: architecture], for the handrail sense; and 上欄 じょうらん, "top or preceding horizontal column," and 本欄 ほんらん, "this column," for the page sense.
