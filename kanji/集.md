# 集 — gather

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

集 means **gather** or **collect**. It can describe people coming together, someone collecting objects,
or attention being concentrated on something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a bird-shaped 隹 above a [木](木.md), gathering with other birds in a tree.
This is a mnemonic, not a verified explanation of the historical composition.

## Common words

- [集まる](../words/集まる.md) あつまる — gather; assemble
- 集める あつめる — collect; assemble something
- 集合 しゅうごう — gathering; assembly
- 集中 しゅうちゅう — concentration

## Notes

人が集まる（ひとがあつまる）means "people gather"; 人を集める（ひとをあつめる）means
"gather people together." The が／を contrast follows who or what is gathering versus being gathered.

The upper 隹 is not written like the independent kanji [鳥](鳥.md). Keep the [木](木.md) base:
without it, the shape is not 集.
