# 集 — gather

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

集 means **gather** or **collect**. It can describe people coming together, someone collecting objects,
or attention being concentrated on something.

**Origin:** 集 is commonly explained as an ideographic compound of 隹 (a bird) over [木](木.md) (tree): birds settling
together in a tree. The classical dictionary *Shuowen Jiezi* records an older form 雧, with three birds above 木, and defines
it as "a flock of birds on a tree"; 集 is its abbreviated form
([MOE variant dictionary](https://dict.variants.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=49013&la=0),
[Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E9%9B%86)).

隹 is traditionally glossed as a **short-tailed** bird, in contrast with [鳥](鳥.md), a **long-tailed** bird. In Japanese the
component is called ふるとり ("the bird in 舊, old"), which distinguishes it from 鳥 used as a component
([Kotobank: 隹](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%9A%B9-621949)).

## Notes

人が集まる（ひとがあつまる）means "people gather"; 人を集める（ひとをあつめる）means
"gather people together." The が／を contrast follows who or what is gathering versus being gathered.

The upper 隹 is not written like the independent kanji [鳥](鳥.md). Keep the [木](木.md) base:
without it, the shape is not 集.

You may see 隹 and 鳥 described as "small bird" and "big bird." That is a loose learner shortcut, not the classical
distinction, which is about tail length. Even tail length is a traditional classification, not a rule that predicts which
component appears in a given bird kanji.
