# 集 — gather

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

集 means **gather** or **collect**. It can describe people coming together, someone collecting objects,
or attention being concentrated on something.

**Origin:** 集 is traditionally explained as birds gathering in a tree: 隹 (bird) above [木](木.md) (tree)
([Kotobank: 集](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%9B%86-526135)).

In Japanese, 隹 is called ふるとり. The classical Chinese dictionary *Shuowen Jiezi* describes 隹 as a
**short-tailed** bird and [鳥](鳥.md) as a **long-tailed** bird
([Kotobank: 隹](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%9A%B9-621949), [鳥](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%B3%A5-106314)).
This is a traditional distinction, not a rule for every character: 雉 きじ (pheasant) contains 隹 even though male
pheasants have long tails.

- [集まる](../words/集まる.md) あつまる — gather; assemble
- 集める あつめる — collect; assemble something
- 集合 しゅうごう — gathering; assembly
- 集中 しゅうちゅう — concentration

## Notes

人が集まる（ひとがあつまる）means "people gather"; 人を集める（ひとをあつめる）means
"gather people together." The が／を contrast follows who or what is gathering versus being gathered.

In ordinary Japanese, 鳥 とり means "bird" in general, not "big bird"; [小鳥](../words/小鳥.md) ことり
means "small bird".

The upper 隹 is not written like 鳥. Keep the [木](木.md) base: without it, the shape is not 集.
