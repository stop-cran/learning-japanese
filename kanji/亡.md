# 亡 — deceased

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 亠 (no. 8) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

亡 concerns death or perishing. [死亡](../words/死亡.md) しぼう names death;
亡くなる なくなる is a gentler way to say that someone dies.
Learn the whole expression, since sensitivity depends on the wording, not just the character.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the open-sided lower shape as a place left empty beneath a cap.
This association with absence is a memory aid, not the character's verified origin.

## Notes

Learn 亡くなる as the complete reading なくなる; do not read it with ボウ.
[方](方.md), [六](六.md), and [立](立.md) share a cap-like top but have different lower strokes.
[死](死.md) is a useful meaning comparison: both characters can concern death, but their shapes and word readings differ.
All source readings, including the hyphen in な.き-, are preserved in metadata.
