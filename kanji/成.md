# 成 — become

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 戈 (no. 62) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

成 connects becoming something with reaching a result. [完成](../words/完成.md) かんせい is completion;
成長 せいちょう is growth. The result need not be a physical object.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the long slant and hook closing around a result that is taking shape.
The image is a way to remember the outline, not an account of the character's origin.

- 成長 せいちょう — growth; development

## Notes

Completion and success are different results: 完成 says something is finished, while 成功 says it succeeds.
The spelling 成る is recognized, but JMdict marks the ordinary "become" use as usually written in kana.

Compare the slanting strokes of [戦](戦.md), [代](代.md), and [試](試.md).
Their larger outlines differ; similar strokes do not imply similar meanings.
