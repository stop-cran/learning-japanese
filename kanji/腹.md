# 腹 — belly

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (as 月 on the left, no. 130) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

腹 names the belly or abdomen. The word [腹](../words/腹.md) はら concerns a body region,
not just the internal stomach organ. 空腹 くうふく connects that region with hunger.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** locate the left-hand body radical, then trace the right side from
its upper strokes through 日 to the crossing strokes below. Imagine layers behind the belly wall;
this is a memory aid, not anatomy encoded in the character's origin.

## Common words

- [腹](../words/腹.md) はら — belly
- 空腹 くうふく — hunger; empty stomach
- お腹 おなか — belly; stomach, a polite expression

## Notes

Learn お腹 おなか as a complete expression; it is not read おはら in this use.
The visible 月 on the left represents the 肉 radical.
[服](服.md) and [朝](朝.md) also have 月-like shapes, while [背](背.md) places its body
component below. Similar outlines do not imply the same body part.
