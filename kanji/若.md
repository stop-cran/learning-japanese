# 若 — young

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (as 艹, no. 140) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

若 expresses youth in [若い](../words/若い.md) わかい and 若者 わかもの,
"a young person." Youth is relative to a context: a person can be young for a particular role
without being a child.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture new shoots at the top 艹 and notice the 右-shaped part
below. The shoots are a memory cue, not evidence for the character's historical derivation.
