# 械 — machine; fetter

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

械 occurs in words about machinery or implements, such as 機械 きかい, machine. The separate native word 械 かせ names shackles or fetters; this written form is marked rare in the inspected dictionary.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside the 戒-shaped right side and imagine a wooden restraint. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

## Notes

- JMdict lists 枷 and 械 in the かせ entry and marks 械 as a rare kanji spelling. That is a spelling qualification, not evidence that かせ is a rare pronunciation.
- The linked 機械 article distinguishes a machine or mechanism from 器械 きかい, an instrument or apparatus. An identical reading does not make their usual contexts identical.
- Compare [校](校.md), school, and [枚](枚.md), a flat-object counter. The right-hand strokes distinguish these 木-left characters.
