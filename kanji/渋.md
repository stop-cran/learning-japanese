# 渋 — astringent; sluggish; reluctant

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

渋 is things that do not go smoothly: an astringent, puckery taste, traffic or work that moves sluggishly, a person who hangs back. The linked [渋滞](../words/渋滞.md) じゅうたい is JMdict's "(traffic) congestion; traffic jam; gridlock," and its second sense is "delay; stagnation."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ジュウ. One example of each:

- not proceeding smoothly, しぶる: 渋滞, and also 晦渋 かいじゅう, "ambiguous; obscure; equivocal," and 難渋 なんじゅう, "suffering; distress; difficulty; hardship; misery; being bogged down; hurdle";
- bitter-looking, sour: 渋面 じゅうめん, "grimace; sullen face," and 苦渋 くじゅう, "bitterness; distress; pain; anguish; affliction."

Under しぶ it lists 渋柿 しぶがき, "astringent persimmon," 渋紙 しぶがみ, "paper treated with astringent persimmon juice," 渋渋 and 茶渋 ちゃしぶ, "tea incrustation; tea stains (on teeth, cups, etc.)."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [水](水.md), written 氵, at the left. Imagine water that will not flow freely and clogs a road. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 氵 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 渋滞, 渋 supplies ジュウ and 滞 supplies タイ. The same ジュウ is in 苦渋, 渋面 and 難渋 above, and in 晦渋.

In 渋い and 渋る, 渋 writes しぶ and い or る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 渋い has four senses in JMdict; after "astringent; bitter; puckery; rough; harsh; tart" come "austere; elegant (and unobtrusive); refined; quiet (and simple); sober; sombre; subdued; tasteful (in a quiet way); understated," "sour (look); glum; grim; sullen; sulky," and "stingy; tight-fisted." The first two senses of 渋る are "to be reluctant (to do); to be unwilling (to do); to hesitate (to do); to grudge; to be tardy (in doing); to put off (doing)" and "to falter; to slacken; to slow down."

渋々 しぶしぶ is "reluctantly; unwillingly" and 渋み しぶみ is "astringency; tartness" and "austere elegance; elegant simplicity; subdued style; quiet taste; refinement." JMdict marks 澁い as a "search-only kanji form" of 渋い.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジュウ with 渋滞 and 苦渋; しぶ with 渋 and 渋紙; しぶい with 渋い, 渋さ and 渋み; and しぶる with 渋る. JMdict has no entry for 渋さ. Practice selects ジュウ, the reading of the linked word article. The kun readings and シュウ are shown with dictionary examples or flagged as source-only; being outside the practice set does not make a reading invalid. The table shows the older form 澁 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- In 渋滞, [滞](滞.md) supplies タイ. Other words above use [苦](苦.md), "bitter; suffering," in 苦渋 and [難](難.md), "difficult," in 難渋, and [面](面.md), "surface; mask," in 渋面.
- [池](池.md), "pond; reservoir," [河](河.md), "river; watercourse," and [治](治.md), "govern; heal," also have 氵 at the left. They look alike at a glance but are different kanji with different readings and meanings; they supply this card's wrong answers.
- [苦](苦.md), [酸](酸.md), "acid; sour," [味](味.md), "taste, flavor," and [辛](辛.md), "spicy; painful," overlap the tastes and unpleasant looks of 渋い and 苦渋. [詰](詰.md), "pack tightly; press," [停](停.md), "halt; stopping," [鈍](鈍.md), "dull; blunt," [遅](遅.md), "late; slow," and [難](難.md) overlap sluggishness and difficulty in 渋滞, 渋る and 難渋. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "astringent," "sluggish" or "reluctant" as exclusive to any one character.
