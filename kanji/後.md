# 後 — behind, after

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60) · **Phonetic:** none established · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

後 combines 彳 (step, going) with 幺 (thread, small) and 夂 (a foot pointing down): a person who takes small steps and drags the foot, therefore lagging behind.
This is the commonly given explanation. The idea gives "behind", "after" and "later", and is the opposite of [前](前.md).

## Notes

- **後 vs 前:** 後 is "after/behind", 前 is "before/front". Compare 前に (before) and 後で (afterwards).
- **後 vs 先:** 後 is later in time; [先](先.md) is earlier or ahead.
- Look-alike: 彼 (he) also has 彳 on the left; 後 is distinguished by 幺 and 夂 on the right.
- Time words: 三年後 さんねんご (three years later); 後 follows the time amount.
