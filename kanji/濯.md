# 濯 — wash; rinse

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

濯 appears in words for washing and laundry. The native verb 濯ぐ means to rinse or wash out.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture water beside a feather-and-bird-like cluster: imagine rinsing dust from a bird's feathers.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- Both すすぐ and ゆすぐ are transitive godan verbs, usually written in kana. Their te-forms are すすいで and ゆすいで.
- Learn 洗濯 as the whole compound せんたく. Its reading does not come from either native verb.
- The rinsing readings above do not license every reading or sense of another dictionary spelling, [雪](雪.md)ぐ. In the inspected entry, ゆすぐ is restricted to 濯ぐ and does not carry the revenge/wiping-out-disgrace sense.
