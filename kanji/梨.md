# 梨 — pear tree

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

梨 writes **pear**, [梨](../words/梨.md) なし, naming the tree or its fruit. In food vocabulary, なし often refers to Japanese or Asian pears; the word can also have a broader botanical use.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [利](利.md) above [木](木.md). Imagine a pear tree supporting the upper block. This is a shape memory aid, not a claim about historical construction or a compound meaning "beneficial tree."

## Notes

- 梨の木 (なしのき) specifies the tree; 梨の実 (なしのみ) specifies the fruit.
- 梨園 combines リ with [園](園.md)'s エン. Its established theatrical sense is not an instruction to translate every occurrence literally as a pear garden.
- This card asks only the useful native reading なし. The recorded on reading is retained with a verified compound, not labelled nonexistent or automatically made a quiz target.
- Compare [利](利.md), benefit or advantage, without the bottom 木. [村](村.md) and [相](相.md) put 木 on the left instead.
