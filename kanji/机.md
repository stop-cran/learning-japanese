# 机 — desk; writing table

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

机 concerns a desk or table used for activities such as writing or studying. The independent native noun is 机 つくえ. 机上 きじょう literally concerns being on a desk and also has theoretical or not-yet-implemented uses.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside the 几-like right-hand shape and imagine a wooden writing desk. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified origin.

## Notes

- The standalone noun 机 is read つくえ; キ is illustrated here by the compound 机上, not offered as a replacement for that noun's ordinary pronunciation.
- The English word table is broader than a writing desk. Use the established Japanese name for a particular kind of furniture rather than translating every table as 机.
- 机上の きじょうの can qualify something theoretical; an abstract proposal need not be physically sitting on a desk.
