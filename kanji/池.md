# 池 — pond; reservoir

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

池 concerns a pond or pool of water. The native noun is 池 いけ. 電池 でんち means a battery or cell; learn that complete technical word rather than interpreting it as an ordinary pond.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 beside 也 and imagine a pool spreading beside the curved lower stroke. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology.

The canonical 水 radical, no. 85, appears on the left as 氵.

## Notes

- A 池 can be natural or artificial. Learn the actual water feature rather than imposing an exact size rule or assuming that every 池 was made by people.
- Compare [地](地.md), ground or land, with 土 on the left, and [他](他.md), other, with 亻. The shared 也-like right side does not make their meanings identical.
- チ is illustrated inside 電池. It is not the reading of the standalone native noun taught in the linked 池 article.
