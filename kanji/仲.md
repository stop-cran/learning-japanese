# 仲 — relations between people

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

仲 concerns relationships between people. 仲 なか can describe how well people get along, and 仲間 なかま names a companion or fellow member. In 仲介 ちゅうかい, the character participates in the idea of an intermediary.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a person 亻 standing beside [中](中.md), as if in the middle of a group of companions.

The canonical radical 人 appears as the left-hand form 亻.

## Notes

- 仲がいい なかがいい means people get along well. It describes a relationship, not their physical distance from one another.
- 仲間 なかま uses the kun reading なか. Do not read that everyday companion word as ちゅうげん; that reading belongs to a different historical word.
