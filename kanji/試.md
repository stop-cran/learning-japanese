# 試 — test; try

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

試 means testing or trying something. [試験](../words/試験.md) しけん can be an exam or a test of how something works;
試す ためす is the everyday verb "try out; test."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture [言](言.md) as spoken test instructions, beside the angled 式 shape.
Imagine reading the instructions before trying an experiment. This is a memory aid, not a historical analysis.
