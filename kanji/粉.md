# 粉 — flour; powder

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 米 (no. 119) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

粉 concerns powder or finely divided material. In cooking it can mean flour, but the character does not require wheat or even an edible substance.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 米 beside 分 and imagine dividing grains into powder. This modern-shape association is not a verified account of the character's origin.

## Notes

- 小麦粉 uses こ for its final 粉; do not replace the whole word's こむぎこ with a mechanically constructed こむぎこな. The linked 粉 article has こな as its primary reading.
- The pinned source places デシメートル in its kun-reading field, despite its katakana loan-unit form. JMdict separately attests 粉 for decimetre, marks that kanji spelling rare and the word usually written in kana. This is not a native powder reading or a recommended replacement for ordinary unit notation.
- A [粒](粒.md) is an individual grain or particle; 粉 describes finely divided material. [米](米.md) names rice, not flour of every kind.
