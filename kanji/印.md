# 印 — stamp; mark

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 卩 (no. 26) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

印 connects a seal or its impression with a mark or sign. The compound 印刷 いんさつ means printing, while 印象 いんしょう is an impression left on someone, not necessarily a physical stamp.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the left-hand strokes as a stamp pressed beside the bent shape on the right, leaving a mark.

The indexing radical is 卩, the small bent shape on the right. This shape cue does not establish the historical origin of 印.

## Notes

- Standalone 印 can be しるし, a mark or sign, or いん, a seal or stamp. The linked 印 article teaches しるし; do not silently transfer its reading to every use.
- In 矢印 やじるし, 印 has the voiced suffix reading じるし. The complete word names an arrow symbol, not a physical arrow.
- The 印す example concerns leaving a mark or stamping. Do not infer that this spelling replaces 記す in every use of しるす.
