# 転 — turn; roll

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

転 concerns turning, rolling, or changing position or situation. It appears in [自転車](../words/自転車.md) じてんしゃ,
"bicycle," and in 転校 てんこう, changing schools.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** notice [車](車.md) on the left and imagine its wheels turning beside the curled
lower stroke of 云 on the right. This is a modern visual association, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

[運](運.md) also contains 車, but inside 軍 and surrounded by the sweeping 辶 shape.
In 転, 車 stands on the left beside 云.
Compare 自転車 with [自動車](../words/自動車.md) じどうしゃ, "car."
The standard bicycle reading is じてんしゃ, with てん, not でん.
