# 絡 — entwine; get entangled

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

絡 concerns entwining or becoming involved. The physical tangle and the connection in 連絡 are related learning associations, not a requirement that communication involve an actual knot.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 糸 beside 各 and imagine threads reaching each side and becoming tangled. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical explanation or a claim that 各 supplies the pronunciation.

The canonical key 糸, no. 120, appears at the left as ⺯.

## Notes

- 絡む and 絡まる are both intransitive godan verbs, not a transitive/intransitive pair. Compare 絡みます からみます / 絡んで からんで with 絡まります からまります / 絡まって からまって.
- 糸が絡む いとがからむ and 糸が絡まる いとがからまる describe thread becoming tangled. 人に絡む ひとにからむ can instead mean pester or pick a quarrel with someone; context matters.
- Compare [各](各.md), each, and [格](格.md), status or standard. Check the left side rather than assigning the same meaning to every 各-shaped character.
