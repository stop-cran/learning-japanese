# 荷 — load; baggage; burden

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

荷 concerns a load, baggage or burden. [荷物](../words/荷物.md) にもつ concerns luggage or a package, while 負荷 ふか also names a technical load or workload.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the lower 何-shaped part bent beneath a bundle placed across the top.

The canonical key is 艸, no. 140, visible as 艹 at the top.

## Common words

- 荷 に - load; baggage
- 負荷 ふか - load; workload

## Notes

- The noun 荷 に is not the grammatical particle に, despite the shared sound.
- 荷物 has the mixed native-plus-on reading に + もつ. カ is not substituted into that established word.
- 負荷's noun load/workload sense is distinct from its formal or literary suru use for bearing or imposing a burden.
