# 薦 — recommend; mat

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

薦 has two meaning groups. Most often it means recommending someone or something: KANJIDIC2 gives “recommend,” “advise,” and “encourage,” and Kanjipedia's first meaning is すすめる, picking out a person and putting them forward (人を選びだす). In [推薦](../words/推薦.md) すいせん, which JMdict glosses as “recommendation; referral; endorsement,” 薦 supplies セン in the whole word. The other meaning is a mat: KANJIDIC2 lists “mat,” Kanjipedia's second meaning is こも, a mat or floor covering (敷物), and JMdict glosses 薦 こも as “woven straw mat (orig. made of wild rice leaves).”

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the grass crown 艹, also at the top of [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," sits above the shape 广, which runs down the left like the edge of a roof over the middle strokes, and 灬, a variant of [火](火.md), "fire," lines the bottom. Imagine recommending the best place in the house to a guest: a straw mat under the roof, close to the fire. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 艹, 广 or 灬 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists セン and すすめる, and sets 薦める beside the same-sounding 進める and 勧める. Kanjipedia also lists こも, しく, and しきりに as readings outside Joyo; Digital Daijisen gives the kun readings すすめる and こも.
- KANJIDIC2 records こも as a name reading, and Digital Daijisen gives the name readings しげ and のぶ. These are name uses, not quiz readings.

These seven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [勧](勧.md) (“recommend; encourage”) and [推](推.md) (“infer; recommend; push forward”): both titles share “recommend,” and JMdict lists 勧める and 薦める as spellings of one verb すすめる.
- [奨](奨.md) (“encourage; exhort; promote”) and [励](励.md) (“encourage; be diligent”): KANJIDIC2 lists “encourage” for 薦, and JMdict's entry for 推薦 has a see-also cross-reference to 推奨.
- [献](献.md) (“offer; contribute; dedicate”) and [供](供.md) (“accompany, offer”): KANJIDIC2 lists “offer” for 薦.
- [畳](畳.md) (“tatami; fold”): the title's “mat” rests on KANJIDIC2's “mat” and on Kanjipedia's second sense, こも。敷物。しとね。 (straw mat; floor covering; a mat to sit or lie on), which overlaps a tatami mat.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 話 (“talk, speak”), 書 (“write”), and 教 (“teach, instruct”); none means to recommend or a mat.
