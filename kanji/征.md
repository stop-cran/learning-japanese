# 征 — conquer; military expedition

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

征 means to go on a military expedition or to subdue by force. It appears in 征服, 遠征 (えんせい), “expedition,” and 出征 (しゅっせい), “going to war.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 彳 on the left and 正 on the right; 正 is also read セイ.

## Notes

[径](径.md), [待](待.md) and [徒](徒.md) also start with 彳. [伐](伐.md) and [討](討.md) can mean to attack or punish, [攻](攻.md) to attack and [撃](撃.md) to strike. [克](克.md) means to overcome, close to 征服 in its sense of overcoming a difficulty. 遠征 is also used for a sports team’s or performer’s tour.
