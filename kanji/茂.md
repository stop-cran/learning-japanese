# 茂 — overgrown; lush

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

茂 describes vegetation growing thickly or luxuriantly. In [茂る](../words/茂る.md) しげる, it is an intransitive verb: plants or foliage grow dense, or are in full leaf. The character's sense is specifically about abundant growth, not simply becoming larger.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the grass crown 艹, also at the top of [草](草.md), "grass; weeds," sits above 戊, which contains the shape 戈, also at the right of [戦](戦.md), "battle." Imagine grass growing so thick over an old battlefield that the weapons disappear beneath it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 艹 or 戊 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists モ and しげる. KANJIDIC2 records the name readings うむさ, き, し, つとむ, む, and もて. Digital Daijisen gives further name readings: あり, いかし, し, しげ, しげい, しげみ, たか, とお, とも, とよ, もち, もと, and ゆた. These are name-use forms, not additions to the ordinary reading quiz.
- Kanjipedia also lists the non-Joyo readings ボウ and すぐれる. The checked JMdict examples for this character use モ or しげる; no JMdict example was found for those extra readings, so they are retained as source-only and not selected.
- The linked verb takes が for the vegetation that grows thickly. It is not a transitive “make lush” verb.
- JMdict glosses 茂る as “to grow thickly; to be in full leaf; to be rampant; to luxuriate; to be luxurious,” and KANJIDIC2 lists “grow thick” and “be luxuriant” for 茂. Thick growth overlaps 密 (“secret; dense; close”), and luxuriant growth overlaps 盛 (“thrive; heap up; serve food”) and 豊 (“bountiful; abundant”). These three are excluded from meaning choices.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 山 (“mountain”), 川 (“river”), and 土 (“earth, soil”); none describes luxuriant plant growth.
