# 茂 — overgrown; lush

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

茂 describes vegetation growing thickly or luxuriantly. In [茂る](../words/茂る.md) しげる, it is an intransitive verb: plants or foliage grow dense, or are in full leaf. The character's sense is specifically about abundant growth, not simply becoming larger.

## Notes

- Joyo lists モ and しげる. KANJIDIC2 records the name readings うむさ, き, し, つとむ, む, and もて. Digital Daijisen gives further name readings: あり, いかし, し, しげ, しげい, しげみ, たか, とお, とも, とよ, もち, もと, and ゆた. These are name-use forms, not additions to the ordinary reading quiz.
- Kanjipedia also lists the non-Joyo readings ボウ and すぐれる. The checked JMdict examples for this character use モ or しげる; no JMdict example was found for those extra readings, so they are retained as source-only and not selected.
- The linked verb takes が for the vegetation that grows thickly. It is not a transitive “make lush” verb.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 犬 (“dog”), 山 (“mountain”), and 水 (“water”); none describes luxuriant plant growth.
