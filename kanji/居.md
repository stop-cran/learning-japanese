# 居 — be present; dwell

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

居 concerns being somewhere or living there. In [居間](../words/居間.md) いま, it helps name the living room.
居住 きょじゅう is residence or living at a place.
The verb 居る いる means to be present or exist for animate beings, but is usually written simply いる.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine someone staying beneath the 尸 covering beside the 古 shape.
This is not a derivation of 居 from the meaning "old."

## Notes

Do not use this card as a reason to replace every kana いる with 居る.
The dictionary marks the kanji spelling as rare and the relevant senses as usually kana.
[局](局.md) shares the upper 尸 but has a different lower enclosure.
Compare the inner 古 with [古](古.md) itself; [右](右.md) has a different shape above its 口.
