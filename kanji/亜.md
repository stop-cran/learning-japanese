# 亜 — sub-; next in rank; Asia

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 二 (no. 7) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

亜 means coming next after something higher or more important: second in rank, like the English prefix sub-. In chemistry it marks a compound with a lower degree of oxidation, and it can also stand for Asia. KANJIDIC2 lists “Asia”, “rank next” and “come after”. The word [亜熱帯](../words/亜熱帯.md) あねったい means “subtropics”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 亜 is written in this order: first one stroke of [二](二.md), "two," a stroke that is also [一](一.md), "one"; then [口](口.md), "mouth"; then two more strokes; and last the second stroke of 二. Picture a race for second place: one line starts it, a mouth cheers, two quick strokes run, and the second line of 二 ends it, second of two. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 二, 一 or 口 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ア only, with 亜流, 亜麻 and 亜熱帯, and gives 亞 as the old form. Kanjipedia gives つぐ outside the table (外).
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings や, つぎ and つぐ, and Digital Daijisen gives つぎ; these are name uses, not quiz readings.

These nine cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [次](次.md) (“next”), [翌](翌.md) (“next; subsequent”) and [後](後.md) (“behind, after”): 亜 can mean next, coming after something.
- [副](副.md) (“vice-; secondary”) and [従](従.md) (“follow; obey; subordinate”): 亜 marks what is secondary, ranking below the main thing.
- [准](准.md) (“quasi-; associate rank”): 亜 can mean quasi-, ranking just below.
- [位](位.md) (“rank; position”) and [格](格.md) (“rank; character”): 亜 can mean the next rank.
- [乙](乙.md) (“second rank; stylish”): both characters can mark the second place in an order.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 母 (“mother”), 多 (“many, much”), and 事 (“matter, affair”); they name a person, a large amount and a matter; none means sub-, next in rank or Asia.
