# 曲 — bend; musical piece

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 曰 (no. 73) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

Learn two practical branches: bending or turning in 曲がる まがる, and a piece of music in
[曲](../words/曲.md) きょく. A musical 曲 may be a song or an instrumental piece.
The musical sense is not an instruction to bend something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** trace an imaginary turning route through the divided box.
This is a visual memory aid, not a claim about historical development. The dictionary's indexing
radical is 曰, distinct from 日.

## Notes

道が曲がる (みちがまがる), "the road curves," describes its course.
指を曲げる (ゆびをまげる), "bend a finger," names what someone bends.
[田](田.md), [用](用.md) and [画](画.md) also have divided enclosures; count the vertical lines
and notice which project beyond the top.
