# 橋 — bridge

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

橋 concerns a bridge. The independent native noun is 橋 はし, while 鉄橋 てっきょう names an iron or railway bridge.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside the tall, layered right side and imagine a wooden bridge spanning an opening. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified historical derivation.

## Notes

- 鉄橋 てっきょう has a small っ in its complete reading. The on reading キョウ is not a replacement for はし when using the ordinary independent noun.
- The 木 radical does not mean every bridge is wooden; 鉄橋 is a useful counterexample.
- Compare [高](高.md) and [塔](塔.md) as written forms. Height or a tower can be associated with a bridge without being its meaning.
