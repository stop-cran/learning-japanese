# 悼 — mourn; grieve

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

悼 means to grieve or mourn, especially over a death. It appears in 追悼, in 哀悼 (あいとう), “condolence,” and in the verb 悼む (いたむ), “to mourn.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 忄, the narrow form of 心 “heart,” is on the left, and [卓](卓.md), “table; eminent,” is on the right.

## Notes

[卓](卓.md) alone means table or eminent, and [草](草.md) has the same 早 at the bottom as 卓; [悟](悟.md) also starts with 忄 and means realize. [弔](弔.md), [喪](喪.md), [忌](忌.md), [悔](悔.md), [哀](哀.md), [嘆](嘆.md), [悲](悲.md) and [葬](葬.md) relate to mourning, sorrow or funerals. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 118](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=118) lists トウ, with 哀悼 and 追悼, and いたむ, with 悼む; it cross-references 悼む with [痛](痛.md)む (“hurt”) and 傷む (“be damaged”), two other verbs read いたむ that are written with different kanji.
