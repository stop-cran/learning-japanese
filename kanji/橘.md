# 橘 — tachibana orange

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

橘 names [橘](../words/橘.md) たちばな, the **tachibana orange**, *Citrus tachibana*, and its tree. In compounds such as 柑橘類 (かんきつるい), citrus, the character participates in a broader citrus category; not every compound names this one plant.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) on the left and a crowded right side with [矛](矛.md) above a divided box. Imagine a citrus tree beside a patterned sign. This describes a visible shape for memory, not the character's historical formation.

## Notes

- This card asks たちばな. キツ is retained with verified citrus compounds, not treated as nonexistent or justified only by a name.
- In older material 橘 can name edible mandarins, while the particular wild tachibana described here has bitter, sour fruit. It is not a general substitute for みかん in a shopping conversation.
- 橘 also occurs as a family name and as a crest name. A name or crest is a separate use, not a claim that a person is a citrus fruit.
- からたち, trifoliate orange, and 酢橘 (すだち), sudachi, have whole-word readings; dictionaries also list a spelling containing 橘 for からたち. Do not extract new freely reusable readings for 橘 from their kana.
- Compare [橋](橋.md), bridge, with a different layered right side; [商](商.md), commerce, has no 木 on the left. [相](相.md), mutual or aspect, has 目 on the right.
