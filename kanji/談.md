# 談 — converse; consult

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

談 belongs to talking things over. [相談](../words/相談.md) そうだん is consultation or asking for advice;
会談 かいだん is a meeting for talks. These words do not require the same relationship
between speakers: consulting someone and holding a conference are different situations.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** two 火 shapes make 炎 beside 言. Imagine a lively conversation,
while remembering that real 談 words need not imply anger. This is a mnemonic, not etymology.

## Notes

先生に相談する (せんせいにそうだんする) means "consult a teacher."
The teacher is marked with に in this pattern.
[許](許.md), [調](調.md), and [記](記.md) share the speech component, but none has
the two stacked 火 shapes on the right.
