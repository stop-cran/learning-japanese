# 揺 — shake; sway; waver

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

揺 is moving back and forth: shaking, swaying or rocking, and, figuratively, a mind or a situation that wavers. [揺れる](../words/揺れる.md) ゆれる is to shake or sway; JMdict glosses it "to shake; to sway; to rock; to swing; to tremble; to jolt," then "to become unstable; to become unsettled; to be shaken; to waver."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ヨウ: swaying to and fro. Its examples include 動揺 どうよう, "shaking; trembling; pitching; rolling; oscillation," also "disturbance; unrest; agitation; excitement," 揺曳 ようえい, "flutter; linger," and 揺籃 ようらん, "cradle." It lists the kun readings ゆれる, ゆる, ゆらぐ, ゆるぐ, ゆする, ゆさぶる and ゆすぶる.

Among words with a hard-to-guess reading it names 揺蕩う (たゆたう), which JMdict glosses "to sway to and fro; to drift about; to flutter; to flicker," usually written in kana, with たゆたう marked as a gikun or jukujikun reading, a special reading that belongs to the whole word; and 揺籃 read ゆりかご, a search-only kanji form in the JMdict entry ゆりかご, "cradle," usually written in kana.

Separate Daijisen headwords give four more readings, each marked ▽, meaning that the official table does not list the reading for 揺: ゆ, written ▽揺, a technique on the koto and similar instruments of touching a string lightly with the left fingertips so that the lingering sound wavers; よる, written ▽揺る, an old verb for ゆれる; and あゆく and あよく, both written ▽揺く, old verbs for ゆれる and ゆらぐ. JMdict has 揺る only as ゆる and has no entry for 揺く. None of these readings is asked here, and none of the three cards chosen to supply this card's wrong answers, 入, 見 and 休, is read ゆ, よる, あゆく or あよく.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the left, written 扌, and at the right a claw-like top over a shape like [缶](缶.md), "tin can; container," with one stroke fewer. Imagine a hand reaching down and shaking a tin can to hear what rattles inside. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 扌 or 缶 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 揺れる, 揺 writes ゆ and れる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. Every kun word above uses the same ゆ with a different ending: 揺れ ゆれ, "shaking; shake; jolt; jolting; vibration; tremor," 揺る, 揺らぐ, 揺るぐ, 揺する, 揺さぶる and 揺すぶる. JMdict marks 淘る and 汰る, other spellings of ゆる, as words containing out-dated kanji, 揺さ振る as a rarely used kanji form of 揺さぶる, and also lists 揺す振る for 揺すぶる. The second sense of 揺する, "to blackmail; to extort; to shake down," is usually written in kana. In 動揺, 動 supplies ドウ and 揺 supplies ヨウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ヨウ, with 動揺; ゆれる, with 揺れる and 揺れ; ゆる, with 揺り返し ゆりかえし, "aftershock; afterquake," and 揺り籠 ゆりかご; ゆらぐ, with 揺らぐ; ゆるぐ, with 揺るぐ and 揺るぎない ゆるぎない, "firm; solid; steady; secure; unshakeable; unwavering"; ゆする, with 揺する and 貧乏揺すり びんぼうゆすり, "shaking one's legs (usu. unconsciously); tapping one's foot; fidgeting (of the legs)," which JMdict spells 貧乏ゆすり first; ゆさぶる, with 揺さぶる; and ゆすぶる, with 揺すぶる. JMdict also lists 揺るぎ無い and 揺ぎ無い, marked as irregular okurigana usage. うご.く is not in the table.

Practice selects ゆ.れる, the reading of the linked word article. No word article uses ヨウ or the other kun readings yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid. The table also shows the older form 搖 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- 揺れる is 揺 with okurigana only, so it has no other kanji.
- [缶](缶.md), "tin can; container," has six strokes; the bottom of the right part of 揺 is a five-stroke shape like it. [採](採.md), "pick; collect; adopt," also has 扌 at the left and a claw-like top at the right, and [稲](稲.md), "rice plant," has a claw-like top over a different bottom at the right. [入](入.md), "enter, put in," [見](見.md), "see, look," and [休](休.md), "rest," are not similar in shape; they write the verbs 入れる いれる, 見える みえる and 休める やすめる, which end in る like 揺れる. These three cards supply this card's wrong answers. All are different kanji.
- [震](震.md), "quake; tremble," [動](動.md), "move," and [乱](乱.md), "disorder; disturbance," overlap the senses of 揺 and its words: trembling in 揺れる, moving in 動揺 and 揺り動かす, where 動 also writes うご, and disturbance or unrest in 動揺. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "shake" or "tremble" as exclusive to any one character.
- [振](振.md), "wave; swing; allocate," shares shaking and swinging: JMdict glosses 振る "to wave; to shake; to swing" and lists 揺さ振る as a rarely used kanji form of 揺さぶる. The two are kept out of each other's quiz options.
