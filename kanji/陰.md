# 陰 — shade; yin

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

陰 is shade: a place the light does not reach, and also what is hidden or behind something. KANJIDIC2 lists “shade”, “yin”, “negative”, “secret” and “shadow”. Kanjipedia's first sense is かげ。日かげ。物におおわれているところ。 (shade; shade from the sun; a place covered by something), and it gives 陽 as the opposite (対) of its first four senses. In [日陰](../words/日陰.md) ひかげ, JMdict's first sense is “shade; shadow” (entry 1463840), and [陰](../words/陰.md) かげ is glossed “shade; shadow”, “behind (something); other side; back”, “the background; behind the scenes; behind someone's back” and “gloom (in someone's expression, nature, etc.); darkness” (entry 1590150). The title's “yin” is KANJIDIC2's “yin” and the second sense of 陰 いん, “yin (in Chinese divination)” (JMdict 1170230).

## Notes

- Joyo lists イン, かげ, and かげる, and pairs 陰 and 日陰 with 影 (⇔). Kanjipedia lists オン and アン, and くらい and ひそか, outside the table (外), and Digital Daijisen gives オン（呉） beside イン（漢）. These are not KANJIDIC2 readings and have no table rows here.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 陰, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These sixteen cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [影](影.md) (“shadow; silhouette”): KANJIDIC2 gives “shadow” and the kun reading かげ for both 陰 and 影, the Joyo table pairs 陰 and 日陰 with 影 (⇔), and the first sense of 陰 かげ is “shade; shadow” (JMdict 1590150).
- [覆](覆.md) (“cover; overturn”): KANJIDIC2 lists “shade” for both 陰 and 覆, and Kanjipedia's first sense of 陰 includes 物におおわれているところ (a place covered by something).
- [暗](暗.md) (“dark”), [濃](濃.md) (“concentrated; dark”) and [玄](玄.md) (“mysterious; profound; dark”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 陰 is くらい (dark), the fourth sense of 陰 かげ is “gloom (in someone's expression, nature, etc.); darkness”, and the 暗 card lists くら.い.
- [秘](秘.md) (“secret; conceal”) and [密](密.md) (“secret; dense; close”): KANJIDIC2 lists “secret” for 陰 and gives the kun reading ひそ.か for 秘 and 密, and Kanjipedia's second sense of 陰 includes ひそか (secret).
- [私](私.md) (“I; private”): the third sense of 陰 いん is “hidden place; unseen part; private location” (JMdict 1170230).
- [裏](裏.md) (“reverse side; lining”), [後](後.md) (“behind, after”) and [背](背.md) (“back; stature”): the second sense of 陰 かげ is “behind (something); other side; back”, and its third is “the background; behind the scenes; behind someone's back”.
- [曇](曇.md) (“cloud over; cloudy weather”): Digital Daijisen's fourth sense of 陰 is 暗い。曇る。 (dark; clouded over), and the first sense of 陰る かげる is “to become clouded; to darken; to get dark; to become hidden (behind clouds)” (JMdict 1590180).
- [時](時.md) (“time, hour”), [刻](刻.md) (“carve; time”) and [歴](歴.md) (“history; passage of time”): Kanjipedia's fifth sense of 陰 is 時間 (time), with 光陰, Digital Daijisen's third is 移りゆく日かげ。時間。 (the passing sunlight; time), and JMdict glosses 光陰 こういん “time; Father Time” (entry 1272850).
- [北](北.md) (“north”): Digital Daijisen's second sense of 陰 is 山の北側 (the north side of a mountain).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 陽 (“sunshine”), 光 (“light; radiance”), and 炎 (“flame; blaze; inflammation”); Kanjipedia gives 陽 as the opposite of 陰's first four senses, and 光 and 炎 name light and fire, so none means shade or yin.
