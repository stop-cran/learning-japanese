# 収 — collect; store

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 又 (no. 29) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

収 brings together ideas of obtaining something and putting it within a place or limit.
[収入](../words/収入.md) しゅうにゅう is money coming in; 収める おさめる can mean putting something away.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the right-hand 又 gathering things toward the narrow left side.
This is a memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

収める describes putting something in place; 収まる can describe its fitting there.
In [反](反.md) the 又 sits under an overhang, unlike 収's side-by-side arrangement.
KANJIDIC2 gives four as the accepted stroke count and records five as a common miscount.
