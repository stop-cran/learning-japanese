# 凱 — victory song; triumph

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 几 (no. 16) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凱 concerns the celebration of victory: a victory song or cry, and triumph.
Learn it in [凱旋](../words/凱旋.md) がいせん, "triumphant return."
The character also has a separate dictionary sense of becoming gentle or
calm, seen in 凱風 (がいふう), "southerly wind"; do not force the victory
meaning into that compound.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the left side 豈 resembles
[山](山.md), "mountain," above 豆 (まめ), "bean." Imagine a victory
celebration beside that stacked shape. The right-hand 几 is the indexing
radical. This is a memory aid, not a historical or phonetic derivation.

In particular, 凱風 is read **がいふう**, not やわらぐ. The native verb
和らぐ (やわらぐ), "soften; subside," is a separate spelling; do not
construct an example such as 凱ぐ from this metadata.

## Common words

- [凱旋](../words/凱旋.md) がいせん — triumphant return
- 凱歌 (がいか) — victory song; victory

The second word can concern victory itself, not necessarily an actual song.

## Notes

- [勝](勝.md) concerns winning; 凱 emphasizes victory celebration in the
  compounds above. [歌](歌.md) means a song or singing more generally.
- [凡](凡.md) concerns the commonplace or general, while [凸](凸.md) and
  [凹](凹.md) concern projecting and sunken shapes, not triumph.
- Grade 9 is the source's name-use classification, not a claim that 凱
  occurs only in names. 凱旋 is a non-name example; personal-name readings
  are outside this card's scope.
