# 穫 — harvest; reap

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

穫 concerns reaping or gathering crops. [収穫](../words/収穫.md)
しゅうかく is a harvest: gathering crops or what is gathered.
作物を収穫する means to harvest crops.

The whole noun 収穫 also describes useful gains or results, as in the
linked article's productive-trip example. JMdict additionally records
a fishing or hunting catch sense. These are attested compound uses,
not a claim that bare 穫 means every act of getting or catching.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** see 禾 beside 蒦, whose
lower part has the shape [隻](隻.md) under 艹. Imagine gathering a
crop beside a tall plant-like cluster. This is a memory scene for
the modern shape, not an etymology or a verified phonetic analysis.

## Notes

- [程](程.md), extent, [秋](秋.md), autumn, and [科](科.md),
  department/course, share 禾 but have different right sides.
  A season associated with harvesting is not the harvesting action.
- [刈](刈.md) overlaps reaping; [収](収.md), [採](採.md) and
  [取](取.md) overlap gathering or taking in. 刈 retains a cutting
  action, so it is not every method of harvesting.
- In the extended uses of 収穫, gains connect with [得](得.md), a
  catch with [捕](捕.md), and fruitful results with [果](果.md) and
  [実](実.md). These compound-context comparisons are excluded from
  choices without licensing arbitrary substitutions of the characters.
- Figurative 収穫 is a noun in the linked examples. Do not mechanically
  turn every English get results phrase into 収穫する.
