# 像 — image; statue

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

像 concerns an image, likeness, or statue. 映像 えいぞう is an image on a screen; 銅像 どうぞう is a bronze statue. 想像 そうぞう instead concerns imagination, so the image need not be a physical object.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the person-shaped 亻 standing beside the large 象 shape, as though posing beside a statue.

The canonical radical 人 is written as 亻 on the left.

## Notes

- 想像する そうぞうする means imagine. It is different from merely seeing an image that is already on a screen.
- The left-hand 亻 distinguishes 像 from 象 ぞう, elephant. Their shared ゾウ reading does not make the words interchangeable.
