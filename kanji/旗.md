# 旗 — flag; banner; standard

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 方 (no. 70), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

旗 is a flag or banner: cloth or paper raised as a sign or decoration. The linked word [旗](../words/旗.md) はた is the standalone noun “flag.” A flag can identify a group, mark a place or send a signal; the situation determines what it means.

Daijisen gives キ for a flag and also records a historically specific sense for a Qing military banner unit. Its はた entry describes a flag raised as a sign or decoration. JMdict's 旗 entry has the gloss “flag”; its separate Buddhist sense is “pataka (banner)” and is restricted to 幡, while its administrative-division sense is restricted to 旗.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice [方](方.md) at the left and the grid-like strokes on the right. Imagine raising a banner to mark a place. This is a memory aid only; the visible parts do not establish meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キ | on | 国旗 こっき — national flag; 旗手 きしゅ — standard-bearer; 旗艦 きかん — flagship; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| はた | kun | [旗](../words/旗.md) はた — flag; 旗色 はたいろ — situation; outlook; 手旗 てばた — semaphore flag |

In 国旗, 旗 supplies キ, which sounds like き in こっき after the preceding consonant changes; in 旗手 and 旗艦 it supplies き. In the selected standalone word 旗, the whole word is はた. The Joyo example 旗色 uses はた; JMdict also lists the whole word 旗色 as きしょく.

The official Joyo table, the standard-use kanji list, shows キ with 旗手，旗艦，国旗 and はた with 旗，旗色，手旗. Practice selects はた because it is used in the linked article 旗. Readings outside this quiz selection are not invalid.

## Notes

- The linked article is a single-kanji word, so it has no other kanji to list. Do not confuse 旗 with the related but distinct 幡 or 旌.
- JMdict marks 旌 as “rarely used kanji form”; its Buddhist “pataka (banner)” sense is restricted to 幡 [field: Buddhism], and the administrative-division sense is restricted to 旗.
- The distractors [施](施.md), [旋](旋.md) and [旅](旅.md) share the left-side 方 shape, but their meanings are carrying out/giving, rotation/return and travel. Their readings do not match はた.
- [標](標.md), “mark; signpost; goal,” and [印](印.md), “stamp; mark,” can describe a sign or mark that a flag may serve as, but neither means a flag itself; both are excluded to avoid more than one defensible answer.
