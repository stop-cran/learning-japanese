# 飽 — tire of; satiate

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 食 (no. 184) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

飽 is having had enough: eating one's fill, being satisfied, and growing tired of something. KANJIDIC2 lists “sated”, “tired of”, “bored” and “satiate”. Kanjipedia gives あきる。腹いっぱい食べる。満たされる。 (tire of; eat one's fill; be satisfied). [飽きる](../words/飽きる.md) あきる is glossed in JMdict “to get tired of; to tire of; to lose interest in; to grow weary of; to get fed up with; to get sick of; to become bored with” and “to become sated; to become satiated; to have one's fill” (entry 1586250). [飽くまで](../words/飽くまで.md) あくまで is an adverb: “to the end; to the bitter end; to the last; stubbornly; persistently; consistently; to the utmost” and “after all; it must be remembered; only; purely; simply” (entry 1518320). JMdict marks both of its kanji spellings as rarely used and the word as usually written in kana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** on the left is 飠, a variant of [食](食.md), "eat, food," and on the right is [包](包.md), "wrap; enclose." Imagine so much food wrapped up and packed inside you that you are full and cannot face another bite. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 飠 or 包 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- In 飽きる, 飽 writes あ and きる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so あ.きる supplies the one question for that stem, shown as あきる; あ.かす and あ.く are retained from KANJIDIC2.
- Joyo lists ホウ, with 飽和 and 飽食 ほうしょく (“eating until one is full; eating one's fill; gorging (on); satiation”, JMdict 1518340), and the kun readings あきる and あかす (……に飽かして). Kanjipedia lists no readings outside the table.
- KANJIDIC2 records the name readings あき and あく; Digital Daijisen gives あき, あきら and あく. These are name use, not quiz readings.

These four cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [疲](疲.md) (“grow tired”): the first sense of 飽きる includes “to grow weary of”, and JMdict glosses 疲れる つかれる “to get tired; to tire; to get fatigued; to become exhausted; to grow weary” (1483740).
- [嫌](嫌.md) (“dislike; suspicion”): the first sense of 飽きる includes “to get fed up with; to get sick of”, KANJIDIC2 lists “dislike” for 嫌, and JMdict's first sense of 嫌 いや is “disliking; not wanting; unwilling (to do); reluctant” (1587610).
- [満](満.md) (“full; satisfy”): the second sense of 飽きる is “to become sated; to become satiated; to have one's fill”, Kanjipedia gives 満たされる (be satisfied), Digital Daijisen's second sense is 十分に満たされる。, and JMdict glosses 飽和 “saturation; satiation”; the 満 card lists み.たす, and JMdict's first sense of 満たす みたす is “to satisfy (conditions, one's appetite, etc.); to meet (e.g. demands); to fulfill; to gratify” (1526670).
- [充](充.md) (“allot; fill”): KANJIDIC2 lists “fill” and the kun reading み.たす for 充, and JMdict lists 充たす as a rarely used kanji form of 満たす, whose second sense is “to fill (e.g. a cup); to pack; to supply”; Digital Daijisen's second sense of 飽 is 十分に満たされる (be fully filled).

The meaning-quiz distractors are 迷 (“get lost; be unsure”), 恥 (“shame”), and 祝 (“celebrate; congratulate”); none means tiring of something or being sated.
