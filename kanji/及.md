# 及 — reach; extend to; and

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 又 (no. 29) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

及 is reaching: catching up with something, or extending as far as it. Written 及び および, it is also the formal conjunction "and; as well as." [及ぼす](../words/及ぼす.md) およぼす is to exert an influence or cause an effect, and [普及](../words/普及.md) ふきゅう is spread or popularization.

The Daijisen character entry gives a single sense: catching up to a line, or reaching as far as a range. Its compounds include 及第 きゅうだい, "passing (an examination); making the grade," 言及 げんきゅう, "reference; allusion," 追及 ついきゅう, "investigation (e.g. into someone's guilt); questioning; pressing," 波及 はきゅう, "spread; extension; influence; aftereffect; ripple," and 普及. Among hard-to-read words it lists 埃及 エジプト, "Egypt," a spelling chosen for its sounds; JMdict marks it as rare, and the name is usually written in katakana.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 及 is only three strokes: a long slant down to the left, a stroke that bends as it goes, and a sweep down to the right. Its key is 又, which has no card here. Imagine a runner leaning forward with one arm stretched out, just reaching the person ahead. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 又 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キュウ | on | [普及](../words/普及.md) ふきゅう — spread; popularization |
| およ.ぶ | kun | 及ぶ およぶ - to reach; to amount to; to befall; to happen to; to extend; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| およ.び | kun | 及び および - and; as well as; 及び腰 およびごし - bent back; indecisive attitude; timidity; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| および | kun | 及 written alone for the conjunction および, a spelling JMdict marks as irregular okurigana; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| およ.ぼす | kun | [及ぼす](../words/及ぼす.md) およぼす — exert an influence; cause an effect |

In 普及, 普 supplies フ and 及 supplies キュウ, a long vowel. In 及ぼす, 及 writes およ and ぼす is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 及ぶ and 及び share the same およ, followed by ぶ or び. KANJIDIC2's separate reading および, with no okurigana mark, matches 及 written alone for the conjunction; 及び is the usual spelling.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キュウ, with 及第, 追及 and 普及 as examples; およぶ, with 及ぶ and 及び腰; および, with 及び marked as a conjunction; and およぼす, with 及ぼす. Practice selects キュウ and およ.ぼす, the readings with linked word articles. およ.ぶ, およ.び and および are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [普](普.md), "universal; widespread," in 普及. 及ぼす is 及 with okurigana only.
- [吸](吸.md), "inhale; suck in," and [級](級.md), "grade; school class," contain 及 at the right and are also read キュウ. [扱](扱.md), "handle; deal with," also contains 及 at the right, and KANJIDIC2 lists キュウ among its readings too. All are different kanji.
- [届](届.md), "reach; deliver; notify," [至](至.md), "reach; attain," [到](到.md), "arrive; attain," and [達](達.md), "reach, plural suffix," overlap the reaching sense of 及 and of 及ぶ, "to reach." [且](且.md), "moreover; also," overlaps the "and" of 及び. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "reach" or "and" as exclusive to any one character.
