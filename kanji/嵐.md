# 嵐 — storm; tempest

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 山 (no. 46), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

嵐 is a storm: violent weather with strong wind, and by extension a violent uproar. [嵐](../words/嵐.md) あらし is a storm or tempest; JMdict glosses it "storm; tempest" and, in a second sense marked as an idiomatic expression, "uproar; hullabaloo; storm (e.g. of protest); winds (e.g. of change)."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ラン: the fresh, moist air of the mountains. Its examples include 嵐気 らんき, "mountain mist; mountain air"; 翠嵐 すいらん, "the sense of being engulfed in a green, mountainous atmosphere"; and 晴嵐 せいらん, "mountain vapor; mountain vapour." It also lists 青嵐, which JMdict reads あおあらし, "wind blowing through fresh verdure," or せいらん, and whose second sense, "mountain air," it restricts to the reading せいらん. For あらし it gives 花嵐 はなあらし, "gale during cherry blossom season"; 山嵐 やまあらし, "mountain storm"; and 夜嵐 よあらし, "night storm." JMdict's separate entry for やまあらし, "porcupine; hedgehog," lists 山嵐 only as a search-only spelling. The Daijisen heading marks the character as outside the official table for the reading ラン, and its kun reading is あらし, the same as KANJIDIC2.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [山](山.md), "mountain," at the top, and [風](風.md), "wind; style," filling the bottom. Imagine wind roaring down from the mountain and turning into a storm in the valley below. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 山 or 風 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ラン | on | 嵐気 らんき - mountain mist; mountain air; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| あらし | kun | [嵐](../words/嵐.md) あらし — storm; tempest |

The word 嵐 is the kanji alone, read あらし, with no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. In 砂嵐 すなあらし, "sandstorm," 砂 supplies すな and 嵐 keeps あらし; JMdict's second sense of 砂嵐 is "noise (video); snow." In 嵐気, 嵐 supplies ラン and 気 supplies キ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only あらし, with 嵐 and 砂嵐 as examples. ラン is not in the table. Practice selects あらし, the reading with a linked word article. ラン appears in a few literary compounds, and no word article uses it yet; it is illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The word 嵐 contains only this kanji.
- [岩](岩.md), "rock; boulder," [岸](岸.md), "shore; riverbank," and [崩](崩.md), "crumble; collapse; demolish," have the same 山 at the top over a different lower part. All are different kanji.
- [風](風.md), "wind; style," [騒](騒.md), "make noise; uproar," and [乱](乱.md), "disorder; disturbance," overlap the senses "storm," "winds (e.g. of change)" and "uproar" that 嵐 and its word carry; 風 is the lower part of 嵐, and 乱 is also read ラン. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "storm" or "uproar" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, JMdict also has 春嵐 しゅんらん, "spring storm," with 嵐 read ラン, and 大嵐 おおあらし, "raging storm," with 嵐 read あらし.
