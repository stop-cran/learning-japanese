# 冴 — clear; sharp-minded

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 冫 (the two left-hand strokes, no. 15) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

Learn 冴 through [冴える](../words/冴える.md) (さえる): to be clear-headed or alert,
or vivid and clear in appearance or sound. The verb can also describe deft performance.
An older sense concerns intense cold; coldness is not implied whenever someone is clear-headed.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the cold 冫 beside the fang-like 牙 (きば) shape
as a reminder of a crisp, keen mind. This is an invented memory aid, not a historical
derivation or verified phonetic analysis.

## Common words

- [冴える](../words/冴える.md) さえる — be clear-headed; be vivid
- 頭が冴える (あたまがさえる) — be clear-headed
- 目が冴える (めがさえる) — be wide awake; be unable to sleep

## Notes

目が冴える concerns wakefulness, not eyes visibly shining. 冴えない (さえない)
can describe something lacklustre, disappointing or unappealing; it is not always
a literal absence of visual clarity or a judgement about intelligence.

Compare the two left-hand marks with [次](次.md), whose right side is different.
[汗](汗.md) and [河](河.md) have three left-hand water dots, 氵, rather than 冫.
The grade-9 source classification concerns name-use kanji, but 冴える is not a name-only word.
Personal-name readings are outside this card's scope.
