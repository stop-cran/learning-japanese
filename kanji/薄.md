# 薄 — thin; dilute

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 艸 (no. 140) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

薄 describes little thickness or concentration, and extends to faint color, weak flavor and diminishing intensity. It also writes the plant name すすき, a separate word rather than a kind of thinness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture water 氵 thinning tea made from the plants under 艹. The right-hand block, with 寸 at its foot, can be the careful hand measuring how much water to add.

The indexing radical is 艸, visible as the upper 艹; the left-hand 氵 is a separate component.

## Notes

- The bound forms need complete words: 薄着 illustrates initial うす-, 品薄 final -うす, and 薄ら寒い the うす.ら- form. In the last spelling, 薄 supplies うす and ら remains written in kana. The prefix 薄ら is usually written in kana; it adds a slight or faint degree.
- 薄める is transitive; 薄まる, 薄らぐ and 薄れる are intransitive in the senses shown. Their meanings overlap, but their endings are not freely interchangeable.
- For the grass, JMdict gives 薄 and 芒 with すすき and marks the word as usually written in kana. Its katakana ススキ reading is kana-only in that record; the spelling list does not itself license every reading with every kanji.
