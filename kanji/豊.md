# 豊 — bountiful; abundant

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 豆 (no. 151) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

豊 concerns abundance, richness or a plentiful supply. 豊か and 豊富 describe richness or abundance, while 豊作 names an abundant harvest.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See a 曲-shaped top above 豆, and imagine a serving stand piled with a bountiful harvest. The feast is a memory aid, not an account of the character's historical formation.

The canonical indexing radical is 豆, the lower part, not the 曲-shaped top.

## Notes

- 豊前 ぶぜん is the name of a former Japanese province. This historical place-name example establishes ブ here, not a rule to read ordinary 豊 compounds with ブ.
- 豊の秋 とよのあき is a complete dictionary expression for an autumn with plentiful harvests. It illustrates とよ within that expression, not a standalone modern adjective to substitute for 豊か.
- 豊か is a na-adjective: the final か is part of the word, and 豊かな supplies な before a noun. 豊富 is also a na-adjective in adjectival use.
