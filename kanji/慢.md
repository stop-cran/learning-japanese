# 慢 — arrogance; slackness

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慢 concerns an attitude that is either too pleased with itself or too loose
and slow. [自慢](../words/自慢.md) じまん is pride in one's own achievements
or possessions, or boasting about them. 高慢 こうまん is haughty or
arrogant, and 慢心 まんしん is self-conceit.

[我慢](../words/我慢.md) がまん is endurance or self-restraint in current
dictionary usage. The Digital Daijisen character entry files both 我慢 and
自慢 under its conceit sense, so keep two facts apart: the character
evidence for 慢 and the modern meaning of the complete word 我慢.

The same entry also gives a slack or negligent attitude, as in 怠慢
たいまん, and a sluggish pace, as in 緩慢 かんまん, slow or sluggish, and
慢性 まんせい, chronic (of an illness).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 忄, the left-hand form of
[心](心.md), beside 曼. Inside 曼, a [日](日.md) sits above 罒 and 又.
Imagine a relaxed heart watching the sun idle above a net-like 罒:
swaggering or slack, depending on the scene. This is a memory picture for
the modern shape, not a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

| マン | on | [自慢](../words/自慢.md) じまん - pride; boasting; [我慢](../words/我慢.md) がまん - endurance; self-restraint |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects マン. In 自慢, 自
supplies ジ; in 我慢, 我 supplies ガ; 慢 is まん in both words. The
official Joyo table lists マン only, with 慢性, 怠慢 and 自慢 as examples.

## Common words

- [自慢](../words/自慢.md) じまん - pride; boasting
- [我慢](../words/我慢.md) がまん - endurance; self-restraint
- 慢性 まんせい - chronic (of an illness)
- 怠慢 たいまん - negligence; neglect
- 緩慢 かんまん - slow; sluggish; lax

## Notes

- [慣](慣.md), "accustomed," [憎](憎.md), "hate; detest," and
  [怪](怪.md), "suspicious; uncanny," share the left-hand 忄 but have
  different right-hand parts and different meanings.
- The slow and dull side, as in 緩慢, overlaps [遅](遅.md), "late; slow,"
  and [鈍](鈍.md), "dull; blunt."
- The endurance and self-restraint of 我慢 overlaps [凌](凌.md),
  "endure; surpass," [負](負.md), "lose; bear a burden," and
  [節](節.md), "joint; season; restraint." [永](永.md), "eternity;
  enduring duration," shares the English stem "endur-" but concerns
  lasting, not putting up with something. These direct comparisons are
  excluded from choices, not treated as identical in every compound.
- Learn 我慢 as a complete word. The dictionary grouping of 我慢 with the
  conceit sense of 慢 does not make the modern word mean "arrogance," and
  the pride in 自慢 is not endurance.
- 怠 in 怠慢 and 緩 in 緩慢 have no kanji cards here yet; this does not
  change the complete words' readings.
