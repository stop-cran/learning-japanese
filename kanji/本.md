# 本 — book, origin

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

本 is [木](木.md) (tree) with a short mark across the bottom of the trunk, pointing at the roots: an indicative character for "root, base".
From "root" come "origin", "main", "true, real" and, through the sense of a basic or written source, "book". The step to "book" is explained in different ways; what is clear is that it
is the standard word for a book in Japanese today. 本 is also a counter for long, thin objects.

## Notes

**Counter:** 本 counts long, thin things (pencils, bottles, trains, films): the reading changes with the preceding number.
一本 いっぽん, 二本 にほん, 三本 さんぼん, 四本 よんほん, 五本 ごほん, 六本 ろっぽん, 七本 ななほん, 八本 はっぽん／はちほん, 九本 きゅうほん, 十本 じゅっぽん／じっぽん, 何本 なんぼん.
So the reading cycles between ほん, ぽん and ぼん.

Look-alikes: [木](木.md) (tree) has no mark at the root; 末 (end, tip) has a longer upper stroke; 未 (not yet) has a longer lower stroke.
休 ([休](休.md)) and 体 (body) are 亻 beside 木 or 本.
