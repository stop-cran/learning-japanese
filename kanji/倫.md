# 倫 — ethics; moral principles

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

倫 refers to the path or principles people should follow. Digital Daijisen also gives a second sense, companions of the same standing, seen in 絶倫 ぜつりん, “matchless; peerless”; KANJIDIC2 lists “companion” among its meanings. Its reading appears in [倫理](../words/倫理.md) りんり, “ethics; morals.”

- 不倫 ふりん — infidelity; an extramarital affair

## Notes

倫理 and 不倫 are common compounds using リン with distinct senses: ethics versus infidelity. 倫 also appears in 五倫 ごりん, “the five Confucian filial-piety relationships.” 伴 and 輩 share the companion sense, while 徳 (“virtue”) and 義 (“righteousness”) are related moral concepts.
