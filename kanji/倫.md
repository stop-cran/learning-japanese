# 倫 — ethics; moral principles

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

倫 refers to the principles people should follow and to relations among people. Its reading appears in [倫理](../words/倫理.md) りんり, “ethics; morals.”

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| リン | on | [倫理](../words/倫理.md) りんり — ethics; morals (JMdict entry 1555390) |

## Common words

- [倫理](../words/倫理.md) りんり — ethics; morals
- 不倫 ふりん — infidelity; an extramarital affair

## Notes

倫理 and 不倫 are common compounds using リン with distinct senses: ethics versus infidelity. 倫 also appears in the classical term 五倫 for human relationships and ethical principles.
