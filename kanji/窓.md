# 窓 — window

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 穴 (no. 116) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

窓 names a window, including the window of a room or vehicle.
The standalone word is [窓](../words/窓.md) まど.
窓口 まどぐち extends the idea to a service window or point of contact.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** find 穴 at the top, 厶 in the middle, and 心 at the bottom;
imagine a window above a heart enjoying the view. This is a memory aid, not an etymology.

## Notes

窓を開ける (まどをあける) means "open a window"; 窓を閉める (まどをしめる) means "close a window."
The verbs describe what someone does to it, so 窓 takes を here.

[突](突.md), [究](究.md), and [空](空.md) share 穴.
For 窓, look for the bottom 心 instead of guessing from the roof alone.
