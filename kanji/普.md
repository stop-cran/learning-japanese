# 普 — universal; widespread

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

普 conveys general or widespread applicability. 普通 ふつう means ordinary or usual, while 普く あまねく is an adverb meaning widely or everywhere. Learn the established words rather than translating every occurrence as the English word universal.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the upper strokes spread across the space above 日 and imagine daylight reaching widely. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

- 普く あまねく is dictionary-listed, but the word is usually written in kana as あまねく; 遍く is another listed spelling. This is recognition context, not a recommendation to prefer 普く in everyday writing.
- JMdict marks 普段 ふだん as ateji and records its earlier spelling 不断. Its meaning should not be derived mechanically from the modern characters 普 and 段.
- The linked 普通 article also explains its local-train use; that is a vocabulary sense, not a general meaning of the character 普.
