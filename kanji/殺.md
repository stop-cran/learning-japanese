# 殺 — kill

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 殳 (no. 79) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

殺 expresses causing death, as in [殺す](../words/殺す.md) (ころす), "kill."
It also occurs in 殺菌 (さっきん), "disinfection," where the concern is microorganisms rather than people.
The character is not limited to the legal meaning of murder.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the crossed strokes at the upper left as a warning mark for the character's serious meaning.
This is a modern association, not a reconstruction of the original form.

## Notes

[死ぬ](../words/死ぬ.md) (しぬ), "die," describes the being that dies.
殺す describes causing that death and takes an object with を.
The right-hand 殳 also occurs in [段](段.md); compare the left halves to tell them apart.
