# 乗 — ride; board

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 丿 (no. 4) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

乗 is the character to recognize when someone boards or rides in a vehicle.
[乗る](../words/乗る.md) のる describes the rider's action; 乗せる のせる can describe giving someone a ride.
乗車 じょうしゃ packages boarding into a noun that can also take する.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the dense middle riding across the long central upright.
This is a visual reminder, not an explanation of the character's historical construction.

## Notes

Use a vehicle with に in 電車に乗る (でんしゃにのる), "take a train."
The branching shapes [来](来.md), [東](東.md), and [重](重.md) have different central details.
乗 also has mathematical uses involving multiplication and powers; those do not change the reading of 乗る.
