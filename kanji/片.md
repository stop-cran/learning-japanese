# 片 — one-sided; sheet

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 片 (no. 91) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

片 concerns one side, one member of a pair, or a thin piece or sheet. In 破片 it identifies a broken fragment.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a single upright with short strokes extending to one side: only one side of a larger shape remains.

## Notes

- KANJIDIC2 lists both かた- and かた. The inspected JMdict 片/かた entry is a prefix, not an ordinary standalone noun. Both native-reading rows therefore use an actual compound, 片手.
- Do not derive [片付ける](../words/片付ける.md) mechanically as 'attach one side'. Learn the established verb for tidying or putting things in order.
- 片 is also the canonical indexing radical, no. 91. Compare [版](版.md), which contains 片 but concerns printing and editions.
