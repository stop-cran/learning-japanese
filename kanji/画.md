# 画 — picture, brushstroke

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 田 (no. 102) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

画 concerns pictures and drawn strokes. In compounds it also occurs in planning, as in 計画 (けいかく).

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the [田](田.md)-shaped center as a picture, with a line above and a frame around its lower sides. This is a modern memory aid, not a verified origin story.

## Notes

ガ and カク are particularly useful readings to learn through whole words.
Compare the bare box of [田](田.md) and the enclosing shape of [国](国.md). Keep the extra upper line and lower frame of 画 distinct.
For the ordinary standalone noun "picture" or "drawing," learn 絵 (え); do not assume every listed reading can be used freely as a word.
