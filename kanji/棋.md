# 棋 — shogi; go; go stone

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

棋 belongs to the two traditional board games, shogi and go, and to the pieces they are played with. The linked [将棋](../words/将棋.md) しょうぎ is shogi, "Japanese chess." The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for キ: (1) go stones, and (2) go or shogi, as in 棋士 きし, "professional shogi player; professional go player," and 棋譜 きふ, "record of a game of go, shogi, chess, etc."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [木](木.md) at the left, a wooden board or piece, and a separate part at the right. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キ | on | [将棋](../words/将棋.md) しょうぎ — shogi; Japanese chess |
| ご | kun | 棋 ご — go (board game); KANJIDIC2 files it under kun, but Kanjipedia lists ゴ as an on reading; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 将棋, 将 supplies ショウ and 棋 is voiced to ぎ: the ギ is rendaku, the voicing of a following sound, so it is the on reading キ and not a separate reading to learn. 棋 read ご is a single-kanji word that JMdict lists with 碁 and 棊 as kanji forms for ご, "go (board game)"; 碁 is the ordinary spelling and carries the priority tags, so this card does not teach 棋 ご for questions.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows キ only, with 棋士, 棋譜 and 将棋. 棋士 きし means "professional shogi player; professional go player," and 棋譜 きふ means "record of a game of go, shogi, chess, etc." Practice selects キ, the reading with a linked word article. The kun reading ご is retained from KANJIDIC2; being outside the practice set does not make it invalid. Daijisen notes that 棊 is a variant form of 棋.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [将](将.md), "commander; impending," read ショウ in 将棋.
- [碁](碁.md), "Go board game," is the other game kanji; it is read ご, and JMdict's ご entry, written 碁, 棊 or 棋, is the same word as 棋 ご, so the card keeps 碁 out of the options. [盤](盤.md), "board; disc," [石](石.md), "stone," and [遊](遊.md), "play," overlap the board, stone and game senses. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "go" or "game" as exclusive to any one character.
- [模](模.md), "imitate; model; pattern," [横](横.md), "horizontal," and [構](構.md), "build; stance," are different kanji with 木 at the left, like 棋. They are read モ/ボ, オウ and コウ, not キ.
