# 升 — measuring box; shō (volume unit)

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 十 (no. 24) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

升 names the traditional volume unit shō, equal to ten 合 and about 1.8 litres, and, read ます, a measuring box ([升](../words/升.md)). Digital Daijisen also gives the sense “rise,” in which 升 is used interchangeably with 昇.

- 一升瓶 いっしょうびん — one-shō bottle (about 1.8 litres)
- 升目 ますめ — square of graph or manuscript paper; box on a form

## Notes

In the inspected JMdict snapshot, no entry written with 升 carries the priority tags news1, ichi1, spec1 or gai1; 升 ます (entry 1346290) carries only news2 and spec2. JMdict also lists 枡 and 桝 as spellings of ます, and Digital Daijisen notes that 枡 and 桝 are kokuji (characters created in Japan).

箱 (“box”) overlaps with the box shape. 計 (“measure; plan”), 測 (“measure, gauge”) and 量 (“quantity; amount”) overlap with measuring. 寸 (“sun (length unit); small”) is another traditional unit, of length rather than volume, and 昇 (“rise; ascend”) shares the “rise” sense noted above.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 犬 (“dog”), 川 (“river”), and 耳 (“ear”); none means a measuring box or the shō unit.
