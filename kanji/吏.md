# 吏 — official; civil servant

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

吏 means an official, a person in government service. Digital Daijisen defines it as [役人](../words/役人.md) やくにん, a government official, with examples such as 吏員, [官吏](../words/官吏.md), 公吏 and 能吏.

## Notes

All three examples in the Joyo table, 吏員, 官吏 and 能吏, use リ. Kanjipedia also gives the kun reading つかさ, marked as outside the Joyo table (外); it is not in this card's metadata and is not quizzed.

官 (“government official”) is the other kanji in 官吏 and shares its meaning, and 司 (“take charge; official; preside”) also refers to an official. 公 (“public, official”) appears in 公吏 and in [公務員](../words/公務員.md) こうむいん, “public employee.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 今 (“now”), 六 (“six”), and 赤 (“red”); none means an official.
