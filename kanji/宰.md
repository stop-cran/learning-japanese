# 宰 — preside; direct affairs

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宰 concerns directing affairs or the person in charge. In 主宰 (しゅさい) it is the leadership of a group, not a job title that every group must use.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look below the roof for the shape 辛. Imagine a leader standing at the center beneath a roof: a memory cue, not a history of the character.

## Notes

[害](害.md) has a 口 low down; [客](客.md) ends with 各 and [実](実.md) has a different set of crossing strokes. [司](司.md), [督](督.md), [管](管.md), [営](営.md) and [主](主.md) can also concern overseeing or running something. In 主宰 the two characters work together: 宰 supplies さい.
