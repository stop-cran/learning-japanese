# 掘 — dig; excavate

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

掘 concerns digging or excavating. [掘る](../words/掘る.md) ほる can describe digging a hole or digging something out of the ground. 採掘 さいくつ names mining or extraction by digging.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside 屈 and imagine a hand bending down to dig. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.

## Notes

- [掘る](../words/掘る.md) is a transitive godan verb: 掘ります ほります, 掘って ほって, 掘らない ほらない. Its final る does not make it ichidan.
- 掘削 くっさく has a small っ. Learn the complete pronunciation rather than joining クツ and サク without the sound change.
- Compare [届](届.md), reach or deliver: its right-hand interior is not the same as 掘's.
