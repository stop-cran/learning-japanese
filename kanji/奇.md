# 奇 — strange; extraordinary

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

奇 concerns what is unusual, surprising, or extraordinary. Its compounds do not all express distrust:
奇跡 きせき is a miracle, while 奇数 きすう is an odd number.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine [大](大.md) balanced unusually over 可.
Keep the lower 口 and the long hooked vertical distinct.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キ | on | 奇跡 きせき — miracle; 奇数 きすう — odd number |
| く.しき | kun | 奇しき くしき — mysterious; a literary noun-modifying form, as in 奇しき縁 くしきえにし — a mysterious bond |
| あや.しい | kun | [奇しい](../words/奇しい.md) あやしい — an uncommon spelling of 怪しい; recognize rather than prefer it |
| くし | kun | 奇し くし — mysterious, a classical adjective; 奇しくも くしくも — by a strange coincidence |
| めずら.しい | kun | 奇しい めずらしい — an outside-the-joyo-table reading recorded by Kanjipedia; use [珍しい](../words/珍しい.md) めずらしい in ordinary writing |

The metadata preserves KANJIDIC2's spelling `くし`; the classical word is written 奇し, not necessarily
as a bare 奇 in a sentence. The めずらしい reading is character-dictionary evidence, not evidence
that an invented modern phrase with 奇しい would be natural.

## Common words

- 奇跡 きせき — miracle
- 奇数 きすう — odd number
- 奇しくも くしくも — by a strange coincidence
- [奇しい](../words/奇しい.md) あやしい — rare spelling to recognize; ordinary spelling [怪しい](../words/怪しい.md)

## Notes

[寄](寄.md) adds 宀 above this whole shape. [何](何.md) has 亻 beside 可 rather than 大 above it.
The shared shapes do not make these words interchangeable.

For 奇しい compared with 怪しい and 妖しい, and the separate classical くし／くすし words,
see [strange, suspicious, and rare](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
