# 善 — good; virtue; handle well

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

善 is goodness in the moral sense, doing what is right, and also handling something well. [善](../words/善.md) ぜん is moral good or virtue, and [改善](../words/改善.md) かいぜん is improvement or betterment. JMdict glosses the noun 善 "good; goodness; right; virtue."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ゼン. One example of each:

- good in conduct or nature: 善人 ぜんにん, "good person; virtuous person," besides 改善;
- dealing with things well: 善処 ぜんしょ, "dealing appropriately (with a situation); taking proper measures; handling (an issue) carefully";
- being on good terms: 親善 しんぜん, "friendship; goodwill; friendly relations; amity."

Its kun readings are よい, よく and よくする; KANJIDIC2 does not list よくする. Daijisen also has a headword えい, glossed simply as よい, that lists 善い as a spelling outside the official table; JMdict records えい only as an out-dated kana reading of 良い, and KANJIDIC2 does not list it. A separate entry for the word ぜん pairs it with 悪 あく, "evil." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a shortened [羊](羊.md), "sheep," at the top and [口](口.md), "mouth," at the bottom, with two short strokes and a horizontal line between them. Imagine a gentle sheep that speaks only kind words. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 羊 or 口 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| よし.とする | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; JMdict spells よしとする 良しとする, "to deem acceptable; to approve of," and has no entry writing it with 善 |

The word 善 is the kanji alone, read ぜん. In 改善, 改 supplies カイ and 善 supplies ゼン. In 善い, 善 writes よ and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 善く uses the same よ with く. In 善い人, 善い is read いい: 善 writes い, followed by the okurigana い, and 人 is ひと.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ゼン, with 善悪 ぜんあく, "right and wrong; good and evil," 善処 and 慈善 じぜん, "charity; benevolence; philanthropy," as examples; and よい, with 善い, marked as a pair to compare with 良い. い.い, よ.く and よし.とする have no separate row in the table. Practice selects ゼン, the reading with linked word articles; よ.い, い.い and よ.く are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words, and よし.とする has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [改](改.md), "revise; reform," in 改善. The word 善 contains only this kanji.
- [羊](羊.md), "sheep," is the shape shortened at the top of 善. [着](着.md), "wear; arrive," and [差](差.md), "difference; gap," start with a similar 羊 shape, but there its vertical becomes a long stroke sweeping down to the lower left. All are different kanji.
- [良](良.md), "good; favorable," [好](好.md), "liking; favorable," [徳](徳.md), "virtue; benevolence," [義](義.md), "righteousness; duty; meaning," and [正](正.md), "correct,proper," overlap the senses "good," "virtue" and "right" that 善 and its words carry; 良 and 好 are also read よ.い and い.い, and JMdict lists 良い, 好い and 善い as spellings of one word よい. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "good" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ゼン senses: 善行 ぜんこう, "good deed; good conduct; benevolence," 最善 さいぜん, "the very best; utmost," 慈善 and 偽善 ぎぜん, "hypocrisy"; 善戦 ぜんせん, "fighting a good fight; putting up a good fight; fighting bravely," and 善後策 ぜんごさく, "remedial measure; relief measure; corrective measure"; and 善隣 ぜんりん, "good neighbour; good neighbor."
