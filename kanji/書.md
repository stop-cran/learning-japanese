# 書 — write

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 曰 (no. 73) · **Phonetic:** none in the modern form (the old form is commonly analysed with 者 as a sound component) · **JLPT:** N5 (unofficial list)

## Meaning and origin

The upper part 聿 is a hand holding a brush (as in 筆 ふで, brush). Below it, the old form 書 had 者 (the sound shi/sho), which was reshaped
into 日 in the modern shape. The 日 at the bottom is therefore a shape, not "sun". It means "write" and also "book, document, letter" (本, 書類).
The key 曰 (no. 73) is a classification, not the sense.

## Notes

- かく has several spellings: 書く (write letters, characters, text), 描く えがく／かく (draw a picture; 絵を描く) and 欠く (lack). Write the kanji by its meaning.
- Look-alikes: 画 (picture) and 昼 (daytime); don't confuse 書 with them. 書 has 聿 on top of 日.
- 書 is also a suffix meaning "document": 説明書 せつめいしょ (manual), 証明書 しょうめいしょ (certificate).
