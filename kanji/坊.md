# 坊 — Buddhist priest; boy

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N2 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

坊 occurs in words concerning **Buddhist priests or boys**, such as 坊主 (ぼうず), a Buddhist priest and, in other contexts, a boy or a person with closely cropped hair. It also occurs in established words such as [寝坊](../words/寝坊.md), oversleeping; do not translate every occurrence literally as "priest."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [土](土.md) beside [方](方.md). Imagine a boy pointing out the direction beside a patch of ground. This is a modern shape association, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

- ボッ contains a small ッ. 坊ちゃん is ぼっちゃん, not ぼうちゃん; 坊っちゃん is another recorded spelling of the same word.
- 坊主 used for a boy can sound familiar or derogatory. The respectful son use and sheltered-young-man use of 坊ちゃん also depend on context; neither is a neutral label for every male child.
- [坂](坂.md), slope, has 反 rather than 方. [地](地.md), ground, and [城](城.md), castle, have different right-hand components.
