# 宵 — early evening; nightfall

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宵 is the early part of the night. The evening is already under way in 宵の口 (よいのくち); the phrase does not mean the deepest hours before dawn.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Under the roof is a shape with small strokes above a 月-like lower part. [消](消.md) puts water, not a roof, beside a similar shape.

## Notes

[宙](宙.md) and [宿](宿.md) also start with a roof; neither has this lower shape. Compare [夕](夕.md), [晩](晩.md) and [夜](夜.md): each can point to nearby times of day. 宵 looks at the beginning of night, not a fixed hour on a clock. Compare the water at the left of [消](消.md).
