# 帆 — sail (of a boat)

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

A 帆 is a sail. Wind catches it and helps move the boat.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 巾 on the left and 凡 on the right. Picture a piece of cloth stretched to catch the wind.

## Notes

[布](布.md) means cloth, [肌](肌.md) has flesh rather than 巾 on the left, and [凡](凡.md) is the component at the right. [航](航.md) concerns navigation or sailing; a 帆 is the object, not the action.
