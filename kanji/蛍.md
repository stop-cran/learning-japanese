# 蛍 — firefly

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 虫 (no. 142) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

蛍 names a **firefly**, also called a lightning bug. The insect is associated with light, and the character also appears in technical words about fluorescence. Not every species called ホタル produces conspicuous light.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find [虫](虫.md) below a cover and three small upper strokes. Imagine an insect sheltered under a cover, with little points of light above it. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation of the character.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケイ | on | [蛍光灯](../words/蛍光灯.md) けいこうとう - fluorescent lamp; 蛍光 けいこう - fluorescence |
| ほたる | kun | [蛍](../words/蛍.md) ほたる - firefly |

Use **ほたる** for the standalone insect noun. **ケイ** occurs in compounds such as 蛍光灯; it is not the pronunciation of that ordinary standalone noun.

## Notes

- The older character form is **螢**. The insect noun is also written ほたる or ホタル; kana spelling does not change its reading or identify another species.
- A compound containing 蛍 need not name the insect itself. 蛍光灯 names lighting equipment; 蛍石 names a mineral. Learn the whole word rather than translating every occurrence as "firefly."
- [虫](虫.md), insect/bug, is a broader category, not an unambiguous wrong answer for a firefly.
- [学](学.md), study, has 子 below the cover. [栄](栄.md), flourish/glory, has 木 below it. [営](営.md), conduct business/manage, has stacked boxes. In 蛍, inspect the lower 虫 shape instead.
