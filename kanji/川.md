# 川 — river

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 川 (no. 47) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

川 is a pictograph of a stream: lines of water flowing between banks. The exact reading of the three lines varies
between accounts; the learning point is "flowing water".
The related 州 (province, state) is explained as land (sandbars, islets) in a river, so it keeps the three flows with extra marks.
Compare 水 ([水](水.md)), the general word for water, and 巛, an old variant of the river key.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| セン | on | 河川 かせん, 山川 さんせん (literary) |
| かわ | kun | 川 かわ, [川上](../words/川上.md) かわかみ, 川下 かわしも, 川辺 かわべ |
| -がわ | kun (voiced) | [小川](../words/小川.md) おがわ, 隅田川 すみだがわ |

In compounds 川 is often voiced (小川, 隅田川), but not always: 石川 いしかわ.

## Common words

- [小川](../words/小川.md) おがわ — brook
- [川上](../words/川上.md) かわかみ — upstream
- 川 かわ — river

## Notes

川 vs 河: 河 is a different character, also read かわ, mostly seen in compounds and names (黄河 こうが, 河川 かせん). Treat 川 as the
everyday spelling; "大きい川 = 河" is not a reliable rule.
