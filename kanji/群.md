# 群 — flock; cluster; crowd

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 羊 (no. 123) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

群 concerns a group, flock, crowd or cluster. Its words can concern people, animals or things; 群れ is not an animal-only noun.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the 君-shaped left part beside 羊 and imagine gathering several sheep into one group. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical or phonetic claim.

The canonical key 羊, no. 123, is the right-hand part.

## Notes

- 群れる is intransitive ichidan: 群れて むれて. 群がる is intransitive godan: 群がって むらがって. They are not a transitive/intransitive pair, and 群れ is a noun rather than either verb's dictionary form.
- For むら, JMdict lists 群 with 叢 and 簇 in a gathering/group entry. A dictionary spelling family does not establish frequency or make every reading of one spelling available to all the others.
- 群れ overlaps with several group/crowd words, but a flock, an organized association and a social event are not universally interchangeable. Compare [君](君.md), you/-kun, and [洋](洋.md), ocean/Western style, by their shapes.
