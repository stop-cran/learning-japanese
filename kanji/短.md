# 短 — short

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 矢 (no. 111) · **Phonetic:** 豆 (traditional analysis) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

短 is 矢 (arrow) plus 豆 (bean, also an old stand/vessel). Traditional dictionaries treat 豆 as the sound part (see タン and トウ/ズ) and 矢 as the sense, because an arrow was an old measure of a short length. This is the standard analysis, but the sense link to the arrow is a tradition rather than something firmly proven.
It means "short" in length and time, and by extension "lacking, a fault" (短所).

## Notes

- Opposite: [長](長.md) (long): 長い ながい and 短い みじかい; in compounds 長所 ちょうしょ and 短所 たんしょ.
- The adjective 短い is an い-adjective: 短くない (not short), 短かった (was short). Note that the okurigana is い, not しい.
- Look-alike: 知 (know) also has 矢 on the left but 口 on the right.
