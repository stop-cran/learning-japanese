# 僕 — I (male); servant

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

僕 is a word for "I" used mainly by boys and men, and in older use a male servant. [僕](../words/僕.md) ぼく means "I; me" in masculine speech. JMdict glosses 僕 ぼく "I; me" as male language, adds a colloquial sense "you," and keeps an archaic sense "manservant."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ボク, a male servant, with the kun reading しもべ. Examples include 下僕 げぼく, "manservant," 公僕 こうぼく, "public servant," and 忠僕 ちゅうぼく, "faithful servant." A separate Daijisen entry for the word ぼく explains the pronoun: a man uses it for himself with equals or juniors, and it is also used to address a small boy. It adds that the spelling was once read やつがれ, a humble "I," and that students began reading it ぼく in the Meiji era.

Daijisen also has headwords やつがれ and しもべ that list 僕 as a spelling outside the official table. KANJIDIC2 does not list やつがれ; JMdict glosses 僕 やつがれ "I; me" as archaic humble language, and 僕 しもべ "servant; manservant; menial," a word usually written in kana alone. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [人](人.md), "person," as 亻 at the left. The upper part of the right side looks like the top of [業](業.md), "work, occupation." Imagine a person bent under a heavy load of work, a servant who calls himself 僕. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 亻 or the 業-like shape contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ボク | on | [僕](../words/僕.md) ぼく — I; me (masculine) |
| しもべ | kun | 僕 しもべ - servant; manservant; menial (usually written in kana); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

The word 僕 is the kanji alone, read with the on reading ボク and no okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same spelling read しもべ is a different word, the native word for a servant, and the spelling read やつがれ is a third one. In 公僕, 公 supplies コウ and 僕 supplies ボク; in 下僕, 下 supplies ゲ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ボク, with 僕 and 公僕 as examples. しもべ is not in the official table. Practice selects ボク, the reading of the linked word article 僕; しもべ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The word 僕 contains only this kanji.
- [業](業.md), "work, occupation," has the same upper part as the right side of 僕. [億](億.md), "hundred million," and [偉](偉.md), "admirable; great," have the same 亻 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [私](私.md), "I; private," [我](我.md), "self; I; ego," [臣](臣.md), "retainer; subject," and [仕](仕.md), "serve, attend," overlap the senses "I" and "servant" that 僕 and its word carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "I" or "servant" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the servant sense: 家僕 かぼく, "houseboy; manservant," 従僕 じゅうぼく, "manservant; male attendant; valet" (a formal or literary term), and 老僕 ろうぼく, "elderly servant."
