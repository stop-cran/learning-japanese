# 路 — path; route

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 足 (no. 157) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

路 is a path, road, or route. [道路](../words/道路.md) どうろ names a road,
while 路線 ろせん can be the route or line of a transport service.
Think of a way along which movement is organized, not only a strip of pavement.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** let the left foot-shaped part suggest feet following a path past 各 on the right.
The indexing radical is 足, compressed and reshaped on the left here.
The memory picture is not an etymology.

## Notes

[道](道.md) and [途](途.md) also have road or way meanings; learn their uses through the words that contain them.
For shape recognition, compare [足](足.md) by itself and the lower-right 口 with the 口 in [名](名.md) or [語](語.md).
The last two are partial visual resemblances, not the same component arrangement.
