# 債 — debt; bond

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

債 relates to debt. Digital Daijisen gives three senses: owing something, as in [負債](../words/負債.md) and 債務; collecting what one has lent, as in 債権; and a short form of 債券 (bond), as in 国債 and 社債.

## Notes

債務 and 債権 name the two sides of one relationship: the debt or obligation owed, and the claim held by the lender.

借 (“borrow, rent”) and 貸 (“lend”) name the acts of borrowing and lending, and 負 (“lose; bear a burden”) is the other kanji in 負債. 恩 (“favor; debt of gratitude”) is a debt of gratitude rather than a financial one.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 花 (“flower”), 月 (“moon, month”), and 水 (“water”); none means debt or a bond.
