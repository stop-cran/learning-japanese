# 債 — debt; bond

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

債 relates to debt. Digital Daijisen gives three senses: owing something, as in [負債](../words/負債.md) ふさい and 債務 さいむ; collecting what one has lent, as in 債権 さいけん, a claim; and a short form of 債券 さいけん, a bond, as in 国債 and 社債. 債権 and 債券 sound the same but are different words.

## Notes

JMdict marks 債務 (“debt; liabilities; obligation”) and 債権 (“credit; claim”) as antonyms: the debt owed by one side and the claim held by the other.

Kanjipedia also gives the kun readings か.り and か.し, both marked as outside the Joyo table (外); neither is in this card's metadata, and neither is quizzed.

借 (“borrow, rent”) and 貸 (“lend”) name the acts of borrowing and lending, and 負 (“lose; bear a burden”) is the other kanji in 負債. 恩 (“favor; debt of gratitude”) is a debt of gratitude rather than a financial one.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 花 (“flower”), 月 (“moon, month”), and 水 (“water”); none means debt or a bond.
