# 缶 — tin can; container

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 缶 (no. 121) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

缶 is useful for cans or tins, such as a drink can or an empty can. The separate dictionary reading かま identifies a boiler, not a second pronunciation to substitute freely in an ordinary can word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the upper strokes above the open-topped lower outline as a container with its rim. This modern-shape memory aid is not a claim about the earliest shape or the origin of the loanword かん.

缶 itself is the canonical indexing key, no. 121.

- The linked 缶 article has the container reading かん. JMdict marks that spelling as ateji and records loan sources; this is not evidence that the modern container word arose from the character's pictured shape.

- Distinguish [山](山.md), mountain, [出](出.md), go out/exit, and [午](午.md), noon. Do not replace かん with かま in 空き缶 あきかん.
