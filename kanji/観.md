# 観 — view; observe

**Strokes:** 18 · **Key (radical):** 見 (no. 147) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

観 concerns looking, observation, or a view of something. [観察](../words/観察.md) かんさつ focuses on watching
and observing; 観光 かんこう names sightseeing. The same character participates in quite
different purposes for looking.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** let the clearly visible 見 on the right remind you to look closely
at the dense strokes on the left. This is a writing aid, not a reconstruction of the character's origin.

## Notes

観察する means "observe"; it does not by itself claim that the observer has explained
what they saw. Compare [親](親.md) and [規](規.md), which also end in 見.
[覚](覚.md) has 見 at the bottom, so preserve the left-right layout of 観.
