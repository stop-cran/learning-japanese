# 従 — follow; obey; subordinate

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

従 is following: going along after someone, doing what one is told, and holding the second place beside a main person or thing. [従う](../words/従う.md) したがう is to obey or follow; JMdict glosses it "to obey (an order, law, etc.); to abide by (a rule, custom, etc.); to follow; to observe" and, in a second sense, "to follow (a person); to accompany; to go with." [従って](../words/従って.md) したがって is the conjunction "therefore; consequently; accordingly," which JMdict marks as usually written in kana. [従兄弟](../words/従兄弟.md) いとこ is a male cousin and [従姉妹](../words/従姉妹.md) いとこ a female cousin; JMdict glosses them "cousin (male)" and "cousin (female)" and marks both as usually written in kana.

The Daijisen character entry gives six senses for ジュウ, among them obeying, as in 服従 ふくじゅう, "obedience; submission; resignation"; being engaged in a task, as in 従事 じゅうじ, "engaging in (work); pursuing (e.g. studies); following (a profession)"; and a starting point in time, as in 従来 じゅうらい, "up to now; so far." For ショウ its senses include following along, as in 追従 ついしょう, "flattery; sycophancy; adulation," and for ジュ it gives a court rank just below the main one, as in 従三位 じゅさんみ, "second grade of the third rank of honor (honour)." A separate headword じゅう uses 従 as a noun for what is secondary; JMdict glosses it "subordinate; secondary; junior; incidental." The other senses, the Daijisen kun readings and more examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 彳 at the left, the same narrow shape as at the left of [後](後.md), "behind, after," and at the right two short strokes and a horizontal above the same lower shape as in [走](走.md), "run." Imagine two small followers running along the road behind a leader. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 彳 or the shape from 走 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

| したが.える | kun | 従える したがえる - to be accompanied by; to be attended by; to take along (someone); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| より | kun, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2 and among the Daijisen kun readings; JMdict has no entry writing より with 従 |

In 従う, 従 writes したが and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 従って is the te-form of 従う used as a conjunction: 従 still writes したが, followed by って. 従える uses the same したが with える. In 従事, 従 supplies ジュウ and 事 supplies ジ; in 従容, 従 supplies ショウ and 容 supplies ヨウ; in 従三位, 従 supplies ジュ, 三 supplies サン and 位 is read み after ん. 従兄弟 and 従姉妹 are different: JMdict marks いとこ as a gikun or jukujikun reading, a special reading that belongs to the whole word and that no single kanji supplies, so they support none of 従's readings. JMdict also reads them じゅうけいてい and じゅうしまい, with 従 supplying ジュウ. In 追従, 従 supplies ショウ; JMdict also has a separate entry 追従 ついじゅう, "following (e.g. someone's opinion); being servile to; adherence; compliance," with ジュウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジュウ, with 従事, 従順 じゅうじゅん, "obedient; submissive; docile; meek; pliant," and 服従 as examples; ショウ, with 従容; ジュ, with 従○位, that is 従 + a rank number + 位, as in 従三位; したがう, with 従う; and したがえる, with 従える. より is not in the table. Practice selects したが.う, the reading with linked word articles. ジュウ is the reading of most 従 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; ジュウ, ショウ, ジュ and したが.える are illustrated with dictionary words, and より has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [兄](兄.md), "older brother," and [弟](弟.md), "younger brother," in 従兄弟, and [姉](姉.md), "older sister," and [妹](妹.md), "younger sister," in 従姉妹. 従う is 従 with okurigana only, and 従って is 従 followed by kana only. The Daijisen character entry also names 従弟 among six cousin spellings read いとこ. The older form of 従 is 從; the Daijisen character entry and the official table show it in brackets.
- [徒](徒.md), "on foot," has 彳 at the left and the whole of 走 at the right, whose lower part matches the lower right of 従. [律](律.md), "law; rhythm," and [待](待.md), "wait, await," also have 彳 at the left. All are different kanji.
- [循](循.md), "follow; cycle," [則](則.md), "rule; follow a model," [守](守.md), "guard; obey," [応](応.md), "respond; comply," [準](準.md), "standard, conform," [屈](屈.md), "bend; yield; submit," [追](追.md), "chase; pursue," [供](供.md), "accompany, offer," [仕](仕.md), "serve, attend," and [副](副.md), "vice-; secondary," overlap the senses "follow," "obey," "comply," "conform," "yield," "pursue," "accompany," "serve" and "subordinate" that 従 and its words carry: JMdict's 従う also includes "to conform to; to yield to" and "to serve (as)," 従事 includes "pursuing (e.g. studies)," and the noun 従 is "subordinate; secondary." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "follow" or "obey" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, the other Daijisen senses: for ジュウ, going along after someone, 従軍 じゅうぐん, "military service; serving in a war; taking part in a campaign"; a follower, or a place secondary to the main one, 主従 しゅうじゅう, "master and servant; lord and retainer; employer and employee"; and, added to kinship terms, a more distant relative, 従弟 じゅうてい, "cousin (younger male)." For ショウ, vertical, 合従 がっしょう, "alliance (esp. originally of the Six Kingdoms against the Qin dynasty)," and being at ease, 従容 しょうよう, "calm; composed; tranquil." Its kun readings are したがう, したがえる and より, the same as KANJIDIC2. Daijisen also has a prefix ひろい written 従 for the lower of two equal court ranks, which JMdict marks archaic; KANJIDIC2 does not list ひろい.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ジュウ senses: 従者 じゅうしゃ, "attendant; valet; servant; follower," and 侍従 じじゅう, "chamberlain"; 従犯 じゅうはん, "accomplice; complicity"; 従順 and 従属 じゅうぞく, "subordination; dependency"; 従業員 じゅうぎょういん, "employee; worker"; and 従前 じゅうぜん, "previous; former." For the first ショウ sense it also gives 扈従 こしょう, "attendance (on one's superior); accompanying," which JMdict also reads こじゅう.
