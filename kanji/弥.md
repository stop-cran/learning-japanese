# 弥 — all the more; far-reaching; patch up

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (no. 57) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

弥 carries senses such as “spreading widely,” “lasting long,” “patching up” and “all the more.” In 弥縫 (びほう), “patching up,” it combines with 縫, “sew.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 弓 on the left and 尓 on the right. The traditional form 彌 has a more complex right-hand side.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ミ | on | 弥勒 (みろく), Maitreya, a figure in Buddhism. |
| ビ | on | [弥縫策](../words/弥縫策.md) びほうさく — stopgap measure; makeshift fix |
| や | kun | 弥生 (やよい), an old name for the third lunar month and the name of the Yayoi period; the reading belongs to the whole word, so learn やよい as a unit. |
| いや | kun | 弥 (いや), an archaic adverb meaning “more and more.” |
| いよ.いよ | kun | 弥々 is a rarely used spelling of いよいよ, “more and more; at last,” which is usually written in kana. |
| わた.る | kun | source-only here: KANJIDIC2 records わたる, but JMdict lists 弥る only as a search-only form of 渡る (わたる); write 渡る for crossing, or kana わたる for spanning or extending over. |

## Notes

The right part 尓 ends in a shape like [小](小.md), as does [京](京.md), and [水](水.md) has a similar bottom; they mean small, capital and water. [弧](弧.md), [張](張.md) and [強](強.md) also start with 弓. [縫](縫.md) means sew, [補](補.md) make up for and [修](修.md) repair; [継](継.md) can also mean to patch. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 149](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=149) gives 弥 only the reading や and cites 弥生 (やよい), which is also in its appendix of special readings; ビ in 弥縫策 is outside the table.
