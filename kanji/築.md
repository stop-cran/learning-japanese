# 築 — build; construct

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

築 concerns building or constructing, including building up a relationship or reputation. The native verb 築く is transitive: what is built or established takes を.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the bamboo-shaped top above a middle section and 木 at the bottom. Imagine gathering building materials above a wooden foundation. This is an invented scene for the modern shape, not a historical account of construction.

The canonical key is 竹, no. 118, compressed into the top-position form ⺮. The bottom 木 does not replace it as the indexing key.

## Notes

- 築く is godan: 築きます きずきます and 築いて きずいて. A relationship can be the object: 関係を築く かんけいをきずく, build a relationship.
- [建](建.md) and 築 overlap in building meanings. [建てる](../words/建てる.md) たてる is a useful verb for putting up a building; this is not an absolute physical-versus-figurative division.
- Compare the bamboo-topped [筆](筆.md), brush/writing, [筒](筒.md), tube, and [算](算.md), calculate. A shared top does not make their words interchangeable.
