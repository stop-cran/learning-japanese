# 張 — stretch tight; assert

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 弓 (no. 57), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

張 is pulling something tight or spreading it out, and by extension putting an opinion forward strongly. [緊張](../words/緊張.md) きんちょう is tension or nervousness, [拡張](../words/拡張.md) かくちょう is expansion or extension, [主張](../words/主張.md) しゅちょう is a claim or assertion, and [出張](../words/出張.md) しゅっちょう is a business trip. The Daijisen character entry gives two senses: pulled tight without slack or spreading out sideways, as in 張力 ちょうりょく, "tension; tensile force," and putting an opinion forward strongly, as in 誇張 こちょう, "exaggeration," and 主張. It also lists 張 as a counter for strung things such as bows and koto, for hung things such as curtains and mosquito nets, and for sheets of paper or hide. The verb 張る はる, "to stretch; to spread; to strain; to tighten; to put up (e.g. a tent)," has no word article yet; [張り切る](../words/張り切る.md) はりきる, "to be in high spirits; to be full of vigor; to be eager," is built on it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 弓, "bow," at the left and [長](長.md), "long, chief," at the right. Imagine a bow string pulled long and taut. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 長 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 緊張, 緊 supplies キン and 張 supplies チョウ. In 拡張, 拡 supplies カク. In 主張, 主 supplies シュ. In 出張, 出 supplies シュツ, shortened to しゅっ before チョウ. In 張り切る, 張り is the stem of the verb 張る, so 張 writes は and り is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; 切る supplies きる. In 欲張り, 欲 supplies よく and 張り is read ばり, the voiced form of はり. The same voiced ば appears in [威張る](../words/威張る.md) いばる, "to put on airs; to throw one's weight about; to swagger." In [引っ張る](../words/引っ張る.md) ひっぱる, "to pull; to draw; to pull tight," 張る follows the small っ and is pronounced ぱる. The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows チョウ, with 張力, 拡張 and 主張 as examples, and はる, with 張る, 欲張る and 引っ張る, and sets 貼る beside はる as a contrasting writing. Practice selects チョウ and は.る; the other kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. JMdict also lists ばる as a separate suffix entry for the voiced form.

## Notes

- [主](主.md), "master, principal," is the other kanji in 主張, and [出](出.md), "go out, exit," is the other kanji in 出張. 緊 in 緊張, 拡 in 拡張 and 威 in 威張る do not have kanji cards yet.
- [長](長.md), "long, chief," is the right-hand part and also reads チョウ, but it means "long" or "chief," not "stretch tight." [弧](弧.md), "arc; curved line," and [弱](弱.md), "weak; frail," also contain a 弓 shape but are not written with 長 at the right.
- [伸](伸.md), "stretch; lengthen," [引](引.md), "pull; draw," [延](延.md), "prolong; postpone," and [広](広.md), "wide, spacious," each overlap a sense of 張: JMdict glosses 張る as "to stretch; to spread," 引っ張る as "to pull; to draw," and 拡張 as "expansion; extension; enlargement." [述](述.md), "state; relate," overlaps the "assert" sense of 主張. All five are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "stretch" or "assert" as exclusive to any one character.
