# 希 — hope; rare

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

希 has a hoping or wishing association in 希望 きぼう, hope or wish, and a rarity association in 希有 けう, rare or uncommon. These associations belong to particular words; 希望 does not mean a rare view.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the crossing strokes above the 布-like lower shape and imagine hoping to find a rare piece of cloth. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology.

## Notes

- The 希 まれ entry is usually written in kana. Dictionary recognition of this kanji spelling is not a recommendation to use it everywhere.
- 希う こいねがう expresses an earnest wish or entreaty; it is not a general substitute for every casual request. The inspected Japanese dictionary defines it as wishing very strongly.
- Compare [布](布.md): 希 has additional upper strokes.
