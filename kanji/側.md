# 側 — side

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** 則 (ソク) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

側 is 亻 (person) plus 則, which supplies the sound ソク. It is a phonetic-semantic compound: a person standing "at the side" of something is the usual way to picture it.
The semantic link between 則 (rule, pattern) and the meaning "side" is not clear, so treat the picture as a mnemonic only.
It means "side", "the part next to something", and also "leaning to one side".

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソク | on | 側面 そくめん (side, flank), 側近 そっきん (close aide), 両側 りょうそく (both sides, formal) |
| かわ / がわ | kun | [内側](../words/内側.md) うちがわ, 外側 そとがわ, 右側 みぎがわ, 左側 ひだりがわ, 川の向こう側 むこうがわ |
| そば | kun | 側 そば (beside, near), 側にいる そばにいる; 傍 is a common alternative spelling |

## Common words

- [内側](../words/内側.md) うちがわ — inner side, inside
- 外側 そとがわ — outer side, outside
- 右側 みぎがわ / 左側 ひだりがわ — the right / left side
- 両側 りょうがわ — both sides

## Notes

- As the second element of a compound, かわ usually voices to がわ (内側, 右側); the standalone word 側 かわ is also common: 側が違う.
- 側 marks a side or a party: 客の側 きゃくのがわ (the customer's side), 学校側 がっこうがわ (the school's side).
- **Look-alikes:** 則 (rule) is the right-hand part of 側, without the 亻, and 測 (measure), which has 氵 instead. [内](内.md), [右](右.md) and [左](左.md) are the usual partners in 内側, 右側 and 左側.
