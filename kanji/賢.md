# 賢 — wise; intelligent

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

賢 concerns wisdom, intelligence and good judgment. 賢い describes someone or something as clever or wise; 賢明 emphasizes sensible judgment.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 臣 and 又 above 貝, and imagine a wise person carefully checking a valuable object. This modern-shape story is not etymology.

The canonical radical is 貝 at the bottom, not 臣 in the upper-left part.

## Notes

- 賢い is an i-adjective, whereas 賢明 takes な before a noun in adjectival use. Shared kanji do not give the two words the same inflection.
- [貨](貨.md) and [貸](貸.md) also have 貝 below, but their upper shapes and whole-character meanings differ.
