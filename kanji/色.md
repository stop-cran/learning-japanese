# 色 — color

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 色 (no. 139) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

色 names color, rather than one particular color. In some words it extends to appearance or a distinctive character.
For the everyday noun, learn [色](../words/色.md) いろ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper strokes as a paintbrush poised above a curled pot of color.
This is a memory aid for the modern outline, not a historical explanation.

## Common words

- [色](../words/色.md) いろ — color
- 茶色 ちゃいろ — brown
- 景色 けしき — scenery
- 特色 とくしょく — distinctive feature

## Notes

[白](白.md) and [青](青.md) name particular colors; 色 is the category.
茶色 combines [茶](茶.md) チャ with native いろ, so it is not an on+on reading.
Learn 景色 as けしき without treating け as a freely reusable reading of 景.
