# 奏 — play music; report to a ruler

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 大 (no. 37) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

奏 is playing music on an instrument, and in older and formal words it is presenting a report to the emperor or a ruler. [演奏](../words/演奏.md) えんそう is a musical performance, playing music for listeners. JMdict glosses 演奏 "musical performance."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for ソウ. One example of each:

- gathering one's views and presenting them to a ruler: 奏上 そうじょう, "report to the throne; reporting to the emperor; reporting to the monarch";
- playing an instrument: 合奏 がっそう, "ensemble (e.g. orchestra, chamber group, etc.); concert";
- achieving a result: 奏功 そうこう, "success; achievement; fruition."

Its kun readings are かなでる and もうす. かなでる is the verb 奏でる, which JMdict glosses "to play an instrument (esp. string instruments)." KANJIDIC2 does not list もうす, and JMdict writes it 申す, "to say; to be called," a humble verb. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the same top as in [春](春.md), "spring season," and [天](天.md), "heaven, sky," at the bottom. Imagine music played in spring rising up to the sky. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 春 or 天 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソウ | on | [演奏](../words/演奏.md) えんそう — musical performance |
| かな.でる | kun | 奏でる かなでる - to play an instrument (esp. string instruments); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 演奏, 演 supplies エン and 奏 supplies ソウ, with no sound change. In 合奏, 合 supplies がっ, the small っ doubling the next sound, and 奏 keeps ソウ. In 奏でる, 奏 writes かな and でる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ソウ, with 奏楽 そうがく, "musical performance," 演奏 and 合奏 as examples, and かなでる, with 奏でる. もうす is not in the table. Practice selects ソウ, the reading with a linked word article. かなでる is a common reading, but no word article uses it yet. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common word is [演](演.md), "perform; stage," in 演奏.
- [春](春.md), "spring season," has the same top as 奏, [天](天.md), "heaven, sky," is the bottom of 奏, and [実](実.md), "reality; fruit," has that same top shape under its roof. All are different kanji.
- [演](演.md), "perform; stage," [弾](弾.md), "bullet; play strings; bounce," [楽](楽.md), "music, enjoyment, ease," and [遊](遊.md), "play," overlap the senses "play" and "music" that 奏 and its words carry; 演 and 弾 appear with 奏 in 演奏 and 弾奏 だんそう, "performance; playing on stringed instruments," and 楽 in 奏楽. [申](申.md), "humbly say," [報](報.md), "report; news," and [告](告.md), "announce; inform," overlap the sense "report to a ruler." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "play" or "report" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the ソウ senses: 上奏 じょうそう, "report to the emperor," 奏聞 そうもん, "reporting to the Emperor," and 内奏 ないそう, "secret report to the emperor," for reporting to a ruler; 吹奏 すいそう, "playing (a wind instrument); blowing," 独奏 どくそう, "(instrumental) solo; recital," 伴奏 ばんそう, "accompaniment," and 前奏 ぜんそう, "prelude; overture," for playing; and 奏効 そうこう, "efficacy," for achieving a result.
