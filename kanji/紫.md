# 紫 — purple; violet

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[紫](../words/紫.md) むらさき names purple or violet. The same noun also
names purple gromwell, whose root supplied a dye, and can name soy sauce.
The plant's flowers are white; its name is not a rule that its flowers are purple.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the 此-shaped upper part
as two marks over a bundle of 糸-shaped threads dyed purple. This is an
invented memory picture, not an account of the character's formation.

KANJIDIC2 records さい and ゆかり as name readings, not ordinary readings
selected for this card. 字通 cites ツツム in an older dictionary; its scope
is unresolved and it is not selected for practice. 紫陽花 あじさい is a
whole-word special reading; do not allocate あじ to 紫.

## Notes

- The pinned KANJIDIC2 record and KanjiVG geometry have 12 strokes. The
  captured 字通 entry labels the character 11 strokes; the discrepancy is
  retained rather than used to alter the pinned metadata or geometry.
- 日本国語大辞典 also records 紫 for purple paper or cloth, sardine,
  woman and the rank 検校. It records specific abbreviated references to
  Edo, Murasaki Shikibu and The Tale of Genji. These source-positive uses
  do not mean every woman, fish, rank, city or book is called 紫.
- 字通 describes uses of 紫 in words for beautiful or sacred things. Its
  historical ツツム lookup has unresolved meaning and scope. Broad-label precautions
  protect against those unresolved uses, not universal synonymy.
- [書](書.md) can mean a book or writings in formal or literary use;
  紫's reference is specifically to The Tale of Genji, not books generally.
  [紺](紺.md) is a deep blue described as having a purplish cast; it is
  not interchangeable with every shade called 紫.
- Purple cloth may be silk or cotton, but the color alone does not determine
  its material. The historical sardine and woman uses are particular
  recorded uses, not general replacements for fish or woman.
- The precaution against [包](包.md) does not establish that the unresolved
  historical ツツム means "wrap". Meaning-choice exclusions do not control
  reading choices.
- [濃](濃.md) has a recorded historical use for deep purple; its ordinary
  concentration or darkness senses do not make 濃 a general word for purple.
- The broad food/foodstuff sense of [食](食.md) can overlap 紫's recorded
  soy-sauce referent; this does not make 食 a specific word for soy sauce.
- [古](古.md) concerns oldness or antiquity, [右](右.md) a right side or
  direction, and [川](川.md) a river. None denotes the sourced color,
  plant, condiment or qualified secondary uses of 紫.
