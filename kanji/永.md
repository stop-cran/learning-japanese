# 永 — eternity; enduring duration

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

永 concerns lasting or enduring time. 永遠 えいえん and 永久 えいきゅう express eternity or permanence. The native adjective spelling 永い ながい belongs to the long-time sense, not a rule for describing every long physical object.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the top dot and the strokes branching around the central line, and imagine a stream continuing into the distance. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not historical etymology.

The source indexes 永 under 水, no. 85; retain that radical even though the whole shape is not an ordinary left-side 氵 component.
