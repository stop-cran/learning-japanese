# 徳 — virtue; benevolence

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

徳 concerns virtue or benevolence. In [道徳](../words/道徳.md) どうとく, the whole word means morals or morality.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine a person walking beside a carefully stacked set of lines and a heart, mindful of their conduct. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- JMdict also records 徳 とく as profit or benefit, with a cross-reference to 得 とく. [利](利.md) means benefit or advantage and [得](得.md) concerns gain; the financial sense is distinct from moral virtue.
- [義](義.md) includes righteousness, [良](良.md) good, [恩](恩.md) benevolence or favor, and [功](功.md) merit. They overlap aspects of 徳 without making every compound interchangeable.
- [優](優.md) includes kindness and [正](正.md) can describe proper conduct; benevolence and virtuous conduct overlap these labels without making the characters interchangeable.
- The character source also describes a virtuous person, blessings and wealth; compare [人](人.md), [福](福.md), [富](富.md), and [享](享.md). [尊](尊.md) and [敬](敬.md) concern esteem or respect. These are limited meaning comparisons, not synonyms in every use.
- A separate character dictionary lists おしえ as a kun reading; KANJIDIC2 does not. Compare [教](教.md), "teach; instruct"; this source-specific reading is not added to practice metadata.
- 徳行 とっこう means a virtuous act, with a shortened sound in the compound. JMdict marks 徳利 とっくり, a word for a sake bottle, as ateji: do not treat とっくり as a separate reading supplied by 徳 alone.
- [休](休.md), [本](本.md), and [木](木.md) are loose contrasts, not the closest shapes.
