# 徳 — virtue; benevolence

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

徳 concerns virtue or benevolence. In [道徳](../words/道徳.md) どうとく, the whole word means morals or morality.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine a person walking beside a carefully stacked set of lines and a heart, mindful of their conduct. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

The Joyo table assigns トク in 道徳. KANJIDIC2 records no kun reading; its five name readings are not selected for practice.

## Notes

- JMdict also records 徳 とく as profit or benefit, with a cross-reference to 得 とく. [利](利.md) means benefit or advantage and [得](得.md) concerns gain; the financial sense is distinct from moral virtue.
- [義](義.md) includes righteousness, [良](良.md) good, [恩](恩.md) benevolence or favor, and [功](功.md) merit. They overlap aspects of 徳 without making every compound interchangeable.
- [優](優.md) includes kindness and [正](正.md) can describe proper conduct; benevolence and virtuous conduct overlap these labels without making the characters interchangeable.
- The character source also describes an excellent person, blessings, wealth and innate nature or ability. Compare [人](人.md), [者](者.md), [偉](偉.md), [幸](幸.md), [福](福.md), [財](財.md), [富](富.md), [資](資.md), [金](金.md), [宝](宝.md), [豊](豊.md), [性](性.md), [能](能.md) and [格](格.md). These are limited comparisons, not synonyms in every use; the source's old examples do not establish present-day frequency.
- [厚](厚.md) and [愛](愛.md) can convey kindness or care; [助](助.md) and [援](援.md) concern help. These are related to benevolence or protection, not interchangeable with 徳. [礼](礼.md) includes a gift or reward; [与](与.md), [給](給.md), [贈](贈.md), [授](授.md), [受](受.md), and [享](享.md) concern giving or receiving. These are cautious comparisons with benefit or grace.
- [尊](尊.md), [敬](敬.md) and [拝](拝.md) concern esteem or respect; [品](品.md) includes refinement, [儀](儀.md) formal conduct, and [師](師.md) an exemplar or teacher. [道](道.md) also has a moral-principles sense. These differ from virtue itself but may invite related choices.
- A separate character dictionary lists おしえ as a kun reading; KANJIDIC2 does not. Compare [教](教.md), "teach, instruct", and [訓](訓.md), instruction; this source-specific reading is not added to practice metadata.
- 徳行 とっこう means a virtuous act, with a shortened sound in the compound. JMdict marks 徳利 とっくり, a word for a sake bottle, as ateji: do not treat とっくり as a separate reading supplied by 徳 alone.
- [後](後.md) shares 彳, while [側](側.md) has a similar left-side 亻 and a dense right side. [達](達.md) has a movement-shaped left side and stacked strokes on the right. Check those parts against 徳's 彳 and stacked right side; their meanings differ.
