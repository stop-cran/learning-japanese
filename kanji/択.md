# 択 — select; opt for

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

択 concerns picking one thing out from several. [選択](../words/選択.md) せんたく is choice or selection. Other dictionary words include 採択 さいたく, "adoption; selection; choice," and 択一 たくいつ, "choosing one from among several; multiple choice." The Daijisen character entry gives 擇 as the older form of 択.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the side form of [手](手.md), "hand," at the left and 尺 at the right. Imagine a hand reaching out to pick one item from a row. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 尺 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 選択, 択 supplies タク and 選 supplies セン. Practice selects タク. The verb えらぶ is normally written with 選, as in [選ぶ](../words/選ぶ.md), and JMdict lists 択ぶ only as a rarely used kanji form of it. For that reason えら.ぶ is retained from KANJIDIC2 but not selected for questions here.

## Notes

- [選](選.md), "choose," is the other kanji in 選択. Both characters concern choosing, so their meanings overlap.
- [採](採.md), "pick; collect; adopt," is the other kanji in 採択 and also concerns taking one option. 択 and 採 overlap, so do not treat "choose" or "pick" as exclusive to any one character.
- [駅](駅.md), "station," and [訳](訳.md), "translate; explanation," share the right-hand 尺 but have different left-hand parts and different meanings. [宅](宅.md), "residence," also reads タク, but it is only a sound-alike.
