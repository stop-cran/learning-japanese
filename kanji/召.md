# 召 — summon; respectful actions

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

召 connects summoning with a family of respectful expressions. 召喚 しょうかん means a summons or summoning; 召す めす can respectfully describe calling for someone and, in other contexts, actions such as eating, wearing or riding.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 刀 above 口 and imagine a mouth calling someone forward. The little scene is a memory cue, not an explanation of the character's origin.

## Notes

- The linked 召し上がる article teaches respect toward the person eating or drinking. It is not simply a more polite way to describe your own meal.
- A respectful meaning of 召す is chosen by its expression and context; do not translate every occurrence as summon.
- The shape contains 刀 over 口, but its radical is 口. Compare 券, whose lower component is 刀 rather than 口.
