# 桂 — katsura tree

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[桂](../words/桂.md) かつら names the **katsura tree**, *Cercidiphyllum japonicum*. In other words 桂 can refer to different aromatic trees, including cinnamon, or to a shogi piece. Do not identify every compound containing 桂 as the katsura species.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [木](木.md) beside two stacked 土 shapes. Imagine a katsura tree growing beside two layers of earth. This is a memory aid for the visible form, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

## Notes

- This card asks かつら. ケイ is a verified non-name reading, not erased from metadata or treated as nonexistent.
- 桂 also occurs in place and family names. Names must be identified in context; they are not an additional personal-name course here.
- Literary compounds can refer to the moon. Other plant compounds include cinnamon and osmanthus; the character does not name one fixed species in every setting.
- The homophone かつら meaning **wig** is a different word, often written in kana. It is not a meaning of the katsura-tree noun.
- Compare [柱](柱.md), pillar, with 主 on the right; [村](村.md), village, with 寸; and [掛](掛.md), hang, with a hand-side form and an extra far-right stroke.
