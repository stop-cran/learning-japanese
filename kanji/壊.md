# 壊 — break; destroy; demolish

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

壊 is breaking: something falling apart, being destroyed or no longer working, or someone breaking it. [壊す](../words/壊す.md) こわす is to break or damage something, and [壊れる](../words/壊れる.md) こわれる is to break or stop working. JMdict glosses 壊す "to break; to destroy; to demolish" and 壊れる "to be broken; to break; to fall apart; to collapse; to be destroyed; to be damaged"; a later sense of 壊れる covers plans and talks that fall through.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for カイ, breaking down, with examples such as 壊滅 かいめつ, "(complete) destruction; annihilation; devastation," 損壊 そんかい, "damage; destruction," 倒壊 とうかい, "destruction; collapse; crumbling," 破壊 はかい, "destruction; disruption," and 崩壊 ほうかい, "collapse; crumbling; breaking down; caving in." For エ it gives the same meaning, with 壊死 えし, "necrosis," 壊疽 えそ, "gangrene," and 金剛不壊 こんごうふえ, "firm and solid; sturdy and indestructible; unshakable; adamantine," which JMdict marks as a four-character idiom. Its kun readings are こわす and こわれる; it does not list やぶる. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [土](土.md), "earth, soil," at the left, and at the right [十](十.md), "ten," above a box-like 罒 and a shape like the lower part of [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment." Imagine ten nets stuffed with clothes heaped on the ground until the pile breaks apart. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 土, 十 or 衣 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 壊す, 壊 writes こわ and す is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 壊れる uses the same こわ with れる: something breaks by itself, while 壊す means breaking something. In 破壊, 破 supplies ハ and 壊 supplies カイ. In 壊死, 壊 supplies エ and 死 supplies シ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カイ, with 壊滅, 破壊 and 決壊 けっかい, "burst (e.g. dam, embankment, levee); breach; collapse," as examples; こわす, with 壊す; and こわれる, with 壊れる. エ and やぶる are not in the table. Practice selects こわ.す and こわ.れる, the readings with linked word articles. カイ is the reading of most 壊 compounds, but no word article uses it yet; エ is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, and やぶ.る has no verified example here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 壊す and 壊れる are 壊 with okurigana only. The older form of 壊 is 壞; the Daijisen character entry and the official table show it in parentheses.
- [均](均.md), "level; average," [埋](埋.md), "bury; embed," and [塔](塔.md), "tower; pagoda," have the same 土 at the left with a different right side. All are different kanji.
- [破](破.md), "tear; break," [損](損.md), "loss; damage," [倒](倒.md), "topple; collapse," [割](割.md), "split; proportion," and [傷](傷.md), "wound; injury; damage," overlap the senses "break," "destroy," "damage" and "collapse" that 壊 and its words carry; 破, 損 and 倒 appear with 壊 in 破壊, 損壊 and 倒壊, and 破 is also read やぶ.る. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "break" or "destroy" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the カイ sense: 壊乱 かいらん, "corruption (of order, public morals, etc.); subversion; degradation"; 壊血病 かいけつびょう, "scurvy"; 自壊 じかい, "disintegration; self-destruction"; and 全壊 ぜんかい, "complete destruction."
