# 恵 — blessing; favor; grace

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

恵 is kindness shown to others and the good that comes from it: a favor, a blessing, grace. [恩恵](../words/恩恵.md) おんけい is a benefit or blessing, [恵まれる](../words/恵まれる.md) めぐまれる is to be blessed with or to abound in something, and [知恵](../words/知恵.md) ちえ is wisdom or practical ingenuity. JMdict glosses the related verb 恵む めぐむ "to bless; to show mercy to" and, in a second sense, "to give (money, etc.)."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses for ケイ: showing kindness or compassion, as in 恩恵, 互恵 ごけい, "reciprocity; mutual benefit," 仁恵 じんけい, "graciousness; mercy; charity," and 特恵 とっけい, "preferential treatment; special favour; special benefit"; and a word that expresses respect for a gift one has received, as in 恵贈 けいぞう, "presenting (a gift, etc.); giving; bestowing," 恵投 けいとう, "presentation; giving; bestowing," and 恵与 けいよ, "presentation; giving; bestowing," all three marked honorific in JMdict. For エ it gives two senses: clever and wise, where the entry notes that 恵 is used interchangeably with 慧, as in 知恵; and auspicious, as in 恵方 えほう, "favourable direction; favorable direction; lucky direction." It gives めぐむ as the kun reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the upper part, a box divided like 田 whose center stroke rises through a horizontal stroke on top, set over [心](心.md), "heart, mind," the key at the bottom. Imagine a parcel tied with crossed string, its knot on top, resting on a heart: a gift received as a blessing. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that the upper part or 心 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 恩恵, 恩 supplies オン and 恵 supplies ケイ. In 知恵, 知 supplies チ and 恵 supplies エ; JMdict also lists the spellings 智恵 and 智慧 for this word. In 恵まれる, 恵 writes めぐ and まれる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; the word article traces 恵まれる to a passive form of 恵む. The noun 恵み ends in み instead, which is why KANJIDIC2 lists めぐ.み as a separate reading.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ケイ, with 恵贈, 恵与 and 恩恵 as examples; エ, with 恵方参り えほうまいり, "New Year's visit to a shrine or temple which lies in a favorable (favourable) or lucky direction," and 知恵; and めぐむ, with 恵む and 恵み. It has no separate row for めぐみ; it lists 恵み under めぐむ. Practice selects ケイ, エ and めぐ.む, the readings with linked word articles. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so めぐ.む supplies the めぐ question; めぐ.み is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid. The official Joyo table also prints a variant form of 恵 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [恩](恩.md), "favor; debt of gratitude," in 恩恵 and [知](知.md), "know," in 知恵. 恵まれる is 恵 with okurigana only.
- [専](専.md), "specialize; exclusive," has an upper part much like that of 恵, with 寸 below instead of 心. [思](思.md), "think, feel," has a 田 above the same 心, the key at the bottom. [絵](絵.md), "drawing; painting," is also read エ. All are different kanji.
- [恩](恩.md), "favor; debt of gratitude," [福](福.md), "good fortune," [幸](幸.md), "happiness; good fortune," [利](利.md), "benefit; advantage," [享](享.md), "enjoy; receive benefits," [与](与.md), "give; grant," [贈](贈.md), "give as a gift; confer," [徳](徳.md), "virtue; benevolence," and [賢](賢.md), "wise; intelligent," overlap the senses "favor," "blessing," "benefit," "give," "mercy" and "wisdom" that 恵 and its words carry; 恩 appears in 恩恵, 贈 in 恵贈 and 与 in 恵与. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "blessing" or "favor" as exclusive to any one character.
