# 清 — pure, clean

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

清 expresses cleanliness, clarity, or purity, including an untainted character. Its reading シン also occurs in the historical name of China's Qing dynasty.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture water next to 青, blue or green: imagine a clear, pure-looking pool. This is a visual association, not proof that pure water has a particular color.

The indexing radical 水 appears as 氵 at the left.

## Notes

- 清浄 also reads せいじょう. The しょうじょう row establishes ショウ; it is not a claim that the other reading is wrong.
- The Qing-dynasty sense of 清朝 is specifically しんちょう. The same spelling also has a typeface sense, especially read せいちょう; do not transfer the historical gloss to every reading.
- 清まる is intransitive godan; 清める is transitive ichidan. 清い is an i-adjective.
- JMdict and Digital Daijisen also record 清む すむ in the [澄む](../words/澄む.md) family. This is spelling-recognition evidence; the pinned KANJIDIC2 record does not list す.む, so it is not added to this card's source reading table or metadata.
