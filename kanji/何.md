# 何 — what

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **Phonetic:** 可 (カ) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

何 is a phonetic compound: 亻 ([人](人.md), person) and 可 (カ, "can, possible"). In classical Chinese it was an interrogative "what?", "which?",
and the original sense "carry" (later written 荷) is commonly cited for the character. How the "what" use arose from it is not firmly settled, so treat the history as debated.
In Japanese it is the basic question word "what", used also for "how many" before counters.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カ | on | 幾何 きか (geometry), rare in everyday words |
| なに | kun | 何 なに, [何か](../words/何か.md) なにか, 何も なにも, 何色 なにいろ, 何人 なにじん (what nationality) |
| なん | kun | [何時](../words/何時.md) なんじ, [何人](../words/何人.md) なんにん, 何分 なんぷん, 何歳 なんさい, 何年 なんねん |

## Common words

- [何時](../words/何時.md) なんじ — what time
- [何人](../words/何人.md) なんにん — how many people
- [何か](../words/何か.md) なにか — something

## Notes

- **なに vs なん:** 何 is read なん before the sounds t, d, n (なんでも, なんだ, なんの) and before most counters (何時, 何人, 何分); otherwise なに (何を, 何が, 何か). This is a tendency with exceptions, so learn common words as units.
- 何人 is なんにん "how many people" but なにじん "what nationality".
- Look-alike: 河 (river) and 荷 (load) share the 可 component; 何 has 亻 on the left, and is not [今](今.md).
