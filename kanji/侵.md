# 侵 — invade; encroach

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9), appearing as 亻 on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

侵 concerns invading, encroaching, or infringing. [侵入](../words/侵入.md) しんにゅう describes intrusion or trespass; 侵害 しんがい can describe an infringement of rights.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the left-hand 亻 approaching the shape to its right. This is a visual memory aid, not a claim about historical formation.

## Notes

- [犯](犯.md) also has 犯す おかす, including breaking or violating a rule. [冒](冒.md) has 冒す おかす and a harm or affliction sense shared with 侵す. [害](害.md) concerns harm; these pairs are excluded to avoid broad English-choice ambiguity, not to assert the words are interchangeable.
- [攻](攻.md) and [撃](撃.md) include attack or aggression; an invasion can involve attack, but their meanings are not identical. [入](入.md) means enter or put in: entry alone does not imply intrusion. These direct quiz exclusions are conservative, not universal synonym claims.
- [寝](寝.md) means lie down or sleep, [進](進.md) means advance or progress, and [掃](掃.md) means sweep or brush. Their shapes or readings may invite comparison, but those meanings do not specify invading or encroaching.
- 進入 しんにゅう means entry or approach; the homophone 侵入 implies an intrusion. The whole-word meanings are not automatically meanings of each component.
