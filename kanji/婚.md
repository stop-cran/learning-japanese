# 婚 — marriage

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

婚 marks the marriage domain. [結婚](../words/結婚.md) (けっこん) is marriage or getting married; 離婚 (りこん) is divorce.
Thus the character alone does not tell you whether a marriage is being formed or ended.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a wedding photograph with the sun, represented by the visible 日 on the right, low in the picture.
This is a memory scene, not evidence about historical wedding customs or the character's origin.

## Notes

婚 has 女 on the left and 昏 on the right; identifying the visible shapes does not establish a historical phonetic analysis.
[始](始.md) shares 女, whereas [借](借.md) and [曜](曜.md) provide different arrangements involving 日.
Remember the small っ in けっこん.
