# 拾 — pick up; gather

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

拾 concerns picking things up or gathering them. 拾う ひろう can describe picking up litter or finding a dropped object. In a separate numerical use, 拾 じゅう represents ten in legal documents.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the hand form 扌 beside 合 and imagine a hand gathering scattered items together. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.

## Notes

- 拾 じゅう is a special written numeral. Ordinary [十](十.md) じゅう also means ten; do not confuse this numerical use with the verb 拾う.
- 拾う is a transitive godan verb ending in う: 拾います ひろいます, 拾って ひろって, 拾わない ひろわない.
- [捨](捨.md), discard, provides a useful practical contrast when deciding whether to pick an object up or throw it away.
