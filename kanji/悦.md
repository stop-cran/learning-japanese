# 悦 — delight; pleasure

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

悦 is delight or pleasure. It appears in 満悦, in 悦楽 (えつらく), “joy; pleasure,” and in 喜悦 (きえつ), “joy.”

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** 忄, the narrow form of 心 “heart,” is on the left; the right part is the same as in 説, 脱 and 税.

## Notes

[説](説.md), [脱](脱.md) and [税](税.md) share the right part but mean explanation, undress and tax. [喜](喜.md) is the everyday character for rejoicing; [歓](歓.md), [愉](愉.md), [快](快.md), [楽](楽.md), [幸](幸.md), [享](享.md) and [興](興.md) also express joy, pleasure or enjoyment. The [standard-use table, PDF p. 16](https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/pdf/joyokanjihyo_20101130.pdf#page=16) gives 悦 only エツ, with 悦楽 and 喜悦.
