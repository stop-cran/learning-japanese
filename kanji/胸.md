# 胸 — chest; breast; feelings

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (no. 130) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

胸 concerns the chest or breast and also the heart, mind or feelings in expressions. A bodily and an emotional translation can therefore belong to the same native noun むね.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the flesh-shaped left side beside the enclosing strokes around 凶 and imagine holding a feeling in the chest. This is an invented modern-shape scene, not historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical key 肉, no. 130, is compressed at the left into a 月-shaped form.

## Notes

- The bound むな- row is illustrated by 胸元 むなもと. JMdict notes むな especially in compounds; do not freely replace standalone むね with むな in every sentence.
- The 胸 entry also has organ-related senses, including heart, lungs and stomach. Neither the chest gloss nor this lesson establishes an absolute anatomical boundary for every expression.
- 度胸 concerns courage or nerve, not simply the dimensions of a chest. 胸がいっぱい むねがいっぱい can describe being overwhelmed with emotion.
- Compare [肌](肌.md), skin/texture, [脂](脂.md), fat/grease, and [服](服.md), clothing. The shared left-side appearance is not a rule that every resulting character names the chest.
