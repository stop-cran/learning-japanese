# 伸 — stretch; lengthen

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

伸 concerns extending, stretching, or growing in length. 伸びる のびる describes something extending or developing; 伸ばす のばす describes causing an extension. The English translation changes with the object: hair, an arm, and an ability do not all 'stretch' in the same way.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a person 亻 beside a long vertical line running through [申](申.md), as if stretching upright.

The canonical radical 人 has the left-hand form 亻.

## Notes

- 髪が伸びる かみがのびる means hair grows longer; 髪を伸ばす かみをのばす means let one's hair grow.
- The reading のす in 伸す has several uses and is usually written in kana. Learning it does not replace the basic 伸びる／伸ばす pair.
