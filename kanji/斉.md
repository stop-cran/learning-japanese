# 斉 — uniform; all together; simultaneous

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 文 (no. 67), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

斉 is things lined up evenly with nothing out of step: uniform, all together, at the same moment. [一斉](../words/一斉.md) いっせい is doing something all at once; JMdict glosses it "simultaneous; all at once," and 一斉に いっせいに, "simultaneously; all at once; in unison."

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses. The first is being even and uniform with no unevenness, or making things so, as in 斉一 せいいつ, "uniformity; equality"; 斉唱 せいしょう, "singing in unison; chanting in unison"; 一斉; 均斉 きんせい, "symmetry; balance; proportion; uniformity," which JMdict spells 均整 first; and 整斉 せいせい, "arranged; organized; orderly; neat," a formal or literary term that JMdict also spells 斉整. The second sense is the name of the ancient Chinese state of Qi; JMdict has 斉 せい, also spelled 齊, as a historical term, "Qi (kingdom in China during the Spring and Autumn Period and the Period of the Warring States)." Daijisen marks セイ as the kan-on reading and サイ as a customary reading, and gives the kun readings ととのえる, ととのう and ひとしい. ととの.える and ととの.う are not KANJIDIC2 readings; JMdict lists 斉える as a rarely used kanji form of 整える, "to put in order; to arrange; to tidy up," and 斉う as a rarely used kanji form of 整う, "to be ready; to be prepared; to be arranged."

斉 and [斎](斎.md) look alike and are sometimes mixed up: JMdict lists 書斉 as a search-only kanji form of 書斎 しょさい, "study; library; den; home office; reading room," and gives 斎行 and 斉行 as spellings of the same word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [文](文.md), "sentence, writing," at the top, with a short stroke at each side below it and two short horizontal strokes between them. Imagine a row of letters printed above two straight, even lines: everything is lined up together. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 文 or the lower strokes contribute meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 一斉, 一 supplies イチ and 斉 supplies セイ; イチ shortens to いっ before セイ, so the next sound is doubled: いっ + せい gives いっせい. In 斉しい, 斉 writes ひと and しい is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji; in 斉しく, しく follows it. In 斉行, 斉 supplies サイ and 行 supplies コウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セイ, with 斉唱 and 一斉 as examples, and gives the older form 齊 in parentheses. It lists no サイ reading and no kun reading. Practice selects セイ, the reading with a linked word article. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [一](一.md), "one," in 一斉.
- [文](文.md), "sentence, writing," sits at the top of 斉, [対](対.md), "oppose; face," has 文 at the left, and [交](交.md), "intersect; mingle," begins with the same dot and horizontal stroke. All are different kanji.
- [済](済.md), "settle; finish," and [剤](剤.md), "medicinal preparation; chemical agent," contain 斉; 済 is read サイ and セイ, like 斉, and 剤 is read ザイ. [蚊](蚊.md), "mosquito," has 文 at the right.
- [等](等.md), "equal; and so on," and [同](同.md), "same, equal," overlap the equality of 斉一 and 斉しい; [均](均.md), "level; average," overlaps "uniform" and appears in 均斉; [整](整.md), "put in order; adjust," overlaps the evenly arranged sense and appears in 整斉; [共](共.md), "together; shared," and [合](合.md), "fit together," overlap "all together"; and [皆](皆.md), "all; everyone," [全](全.md), "whole; complete," [総](総.md), "whole; overall; total," and [諸](諸.md), "various; many; together," overlap the "all" of 一斉. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "uniform," "together" or "all" as exclusive to any one character.
- 斉 is many people or things acting alike at the same moment, as in 一斉. A pair of two is a different idea, the sense of 双 in 双子 ふたご, "twins," and 双方 そうほう, "both parties; both sides." The English word "together" appears in both titles, so [双](双.md), "pair; two together," remains an ordinary wrong option for 斉.
