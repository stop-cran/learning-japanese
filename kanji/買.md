# 買 — buy

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (shell, no. 154) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

買 is commonly explained as 罒 (a net, a form of 网) over 貝 (a shell): shells were an early form of money, so the picture
is of "gathering in" goods with money. As the details of the early forms are debated, take the net image as a mnemonic.
The key 貝 appears in many money and trade words: 財, 貯 and 貨. (売 looks similar but has no 貝 in its modern form; its radical is 士.)

## Notes

- Opposite: 売る うる (sell), written 売; the two kanji look alike at a glance, but 買 has 罒 over 貝 while 売 has 士 over a different base. かう is also 飼う (keep an animal), a homophone with another spelling.
- Look-alikes: [見](見.md) (see) is 目 over 儿 (legs); 買 has 罒 over 貝, and 貝 itself contains 目 over 八. 貝 is also a part of 貸 (lend) and 貯.
- 買う is a regular godan verb: 買います, 買わない (note the わ, not あ), 買って, 買った.
