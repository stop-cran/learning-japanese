# 旦 — dawn; daybreak; morning

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

旦 means dawn, daybreak or morning. In the linked word [一旦](../words/一旦.md) いったん, JMdict gives “once,” “for a short time; briefly; temporarily,” and the archaic “one morning”; it marks the word as usually written using kana alone. Those are whole-word meanings, not a literal rule for translating 旦.

Daijisen gives タン for morning or daybreak. It also lists ダン as an abbreviation of 檀, used in 旦那 だんな; this is a different use from “dawn.” Kanjipedia lists あした as an outside-table reading, but no ordinary JMdict word was found that uses it, and 一旦 is read いっ + タン.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine [日](日.md) just rising above a horizontal line like a horizon, with [一](一.md) as the line. This is a memory aid from the visible shape only. Historical formation is not established here.

In 一旦, 一 is read イチ and shortens to いっ before タン; 旦 supplies タン. Learn いったん as a whole word. In 旦那, the reading is だんな; JMdict also lists 檀那 as a rarely used kanji form.

The official Joyo table, the standard-use kanji list, shows タン with 一旦，元旦 and ダン with 旦那. Practice selects タン because it is used in the linked article 一旦. Readings outside this quiz selection are not invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in 一旦 is [一](一.md), “one.” In this compound, 一 supplies いっ and 旦 supplies タン; together they form the whole-word reading いったん.
- The five KANJIDIC2 kun readings shown above have no ordinary JMdict word example. Kanjipedia lists あした outside the official table; 一旦 is still いっ + タン and contains no あした.
- The distractors [且](且.md), [目](目.md) and [旧](旧.md) have simple box-and-line shapes that may be confused at a glance, but their meanings are moreover/also, eye and former/old. Their readings do not match タン.
- [朝](朝.md), “morning,” and [日](日.md), “sun; day,” overlap the time-of-day sense and are excluded so “dawn” or “morning” does not have multiple defensible answers.
