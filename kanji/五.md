# 五 — five

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 二 (no. 7) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The oldest form of 五 is commonly explained as a cross × between two horizontal lines (the lines being heaven and earth, or simply a counting device); this is not certain. The modern shape keeps the lines at the top and bottom.
Its key is 二 ([二](二.md)).

## Notes

Look-alikes: [四](四.md) (four, boxed), [六](六.md) (six, with a top dot) and 吾 (I, rare). The five yen coin (五円玉 ごえんだま) is a lucky coin because ごえん sounds like 御縁 (ご縁, "fate/connection").
