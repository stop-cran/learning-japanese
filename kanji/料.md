# 料 — materials, fee

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 斗 (no. 68) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

料 has two especially useful associations: materials or ingredients, and fees or charges. The surrounding word determines which meaning is relevant.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 米 on the left as rice being measured beside the 斗 on the right, then priced. This links ingredients and a fee in a memory scene, without claiming a verified historical derivation.

## Common words

- 料金 りょうきん — fee, charge
- 材料 ざいりょう — materials, ingredients
- 無料 むりょう — free of charge

## Notes

料理 is not "a cooking fee": learn its whole-word meaning. Compare 料金, where [金](金.md) makes the money context clear.

Do not mistake the left-side 米 for 禾, the grain-like component in [秋](秋.md) and [私](私.md). The right-hand 斗 also helps distinguish 料 from those cards.
