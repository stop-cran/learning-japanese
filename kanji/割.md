# 割 — split; proportion

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (as 刂, no. 18) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

割 concerns dividing or splitting something, and shares that idea with proportions.
[割る](../words/割る.md) わる can mean breaking or splitting an object; 割合 わりあい means a proportion or ratio.
The related verb 割れる われる describes something breaking or splitting.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the long 刂 separating a piece from the large [害](害.md) shape
on the left. This is a memory picture of the modern outline, not a historical derivation.

## Notes

コップを割る (コップをわる) means "break a glass"; コップが割れる (コップがわれる) means "a glass breaks."
The first describes acting on the glass, while the second describes what happens to it.
[到](到.md), [利](利.md), and [判](判.md) share 刂 but lack 割's large roofed left side.
