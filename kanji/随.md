# 随 — follow; at will

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

随 is following: going along after someone, or letting things take their course and acting as one pleases. KANJIDIC2 lists “follow”, “obey”, “submit to” and “comply”, among other senses. Kanjipedia's first sense is したがう。ついていく。ともにする。 (follow; go along after; accompany), and its second is 思いのまま。言いなりになる。 (as one pleases; doing as one is told), with 随意 and 随筆 as examples. Digital Daijisen's second sense is 成り行きにまかせる。 (leave things to take their course), and its examples also include 随筆. [随筆](../words/随筆.md) ずいひつ is glossed in JMdict “essays; miscellaneous writings; literary jottings” (entry 1372790). The title's “at will” is Kanjipedia's 思いのまま, and JMdict glosses 随意 ずいい “voluntary; optional; free; elective” (entry 1372720).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** the ⻖ shape stands on the left; to its right, [有](有.md), "have, exist," with [月](月.md), "moon, month," inside it, sits on ⻌, a shape that sweeps along the bottom. Picture someone who has the moon to light the road and simply follows wherever the path sweeps, at will. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that ⻖, 有 or ⻌ contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ズイ only, with 随行 and 追随 among its examples, and shows 隨 as the older form in parentheses. Kanjipedia lists したがう outside the table (外). Digital Daijisen also gives the kun reading まま; JMdict lists 随に as a rarely used kanji form of ままに (entry 2409100), so this is a dictionary spelling, not a KANJIDIC2 reading.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 随; Digital Daijisen gives あや, みち, ゆき and より. These are name-use forms, not quiz readings.

These eleven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [従](従.md) (“follow; obey; subordinate”): KANJIDIC2 gives “follow”, “obey”, “submit to” and “comply” and the kun reading したが.う for both 随 and 従, and JMdict lists 随う as a rarely used kanji form of 従う したがう (1335210), whose first sense is “to obey (an order, law, etc.); to abide by (a rule, custom, etc.); to follow; to observe; to conform to; to yield to” and whose second is “to follow (a person); to accompany; to go with”.
- [循](循.md) (“follow; cycle”): KANJIDIC2 lists “follow” for both 随 and 循.
- [則](則.md) (“rule; follow a model”) and [守](守.md) (“guard; obey”): KANJIDIC2 lists “follow” and “obey” for 随, which overlap these titles' “follow a model” and “obey”.
- [屈](屈.md) (“bend; yield; submit”): KANJIDIC2 lists “submit to” for 随 and “submit” and “yield” for 屈.
- [応](応.md) (“respond; comply”): KANJIDIC2 lists “comply” for 随, which matches the title's “comply”.
- [供](供.md) (“accompany, offer”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 随 includes ともにする (accompany), the first sense of 随伴 ずいはん is “attendance; accompanying; following” (JMdict 1620260), the second sense of 従う includes “to accompany”, and the 供 card lists とも.
- [縦](縦.md) (“vertical; lengthwise; at will”): the title shares “at will” with this card's title; KANJIDIC2 lists “self-indulgent” and “wayward” for 縦, close to Kanjipedia's 思いのまま (as one pleases) for 随, and JMdict lists 縦 among the kanji forms of ほしいまま (1908340), “selfish; self-indulgent; arbitrary”, a word usually written in kana.
- [準](準.md) (“standard, conform”): the first sense of 従う includes “to conform to”, and the first sense of 準じる じゅんじる is “to follow (a rule, precedent, etc.); to conform to (the law, standards, etc.); to be based on; to apply correspondingly” (JMdict 1341510).
- [仕](仕.md) (“serve, attend”): JMdict glosses 随行 ずいこう “attendant; follower” (1372740) and 仕える つかえる “to serve; to work for; to attend” (1304760).
- [追](追.md) (“chase; pursue”): the first sense of 追う おう is “to chase; to run after; to pursue; to follow after” (JMdict 1432410), which overlaps this card's “follow”; the 追 card lists お.う.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 逆 (“reverse; opposite”), 離 (“separate; detach”), and 導 (“guide; conduct”); none means following or acting at will.
