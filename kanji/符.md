# 符 — token; sign; charm

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

符 concerns a token, sign, mark or charm. 切符 gives a familiar ticket example; [符号](../words/符号.md) concerns signs, symbols or codes. The whole word identifies which kind of token or sign is intended.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the bamboo-shaped top above 付 and imagine a little token attached to a bamboo tag. This invented scene is not a historical claim about the parts or their sounds.

The canonical key is [竹](竹.md), no. 118, appearing as the top form ⺮.

## Notes

- The established reading of [切符](../words/切符.md) is きっぷ, with a small っ and ぷ. The フ row does not instruct you to pronounce that compound mechanically as きりふ.
- Compare [付](付.md), attach, and [府](府.md), urban prefecture or administration. They share a useful visible element with 符 but have different indexing keys and meanings.
- The English word sign is broad: 符号 can be a symbol or code, not necessarily a physical roadside sign.
