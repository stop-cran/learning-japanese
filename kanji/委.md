# 委 — entrust; committee

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

委 connects entrusting a matter to someone with the committee role in 委員 いいん, committee member. 委ねる ゆだねる means to entrust or leave a matter to someone; it is a complete native verb, not the on reading イ with an ending attached.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 禾 above 女 and imagine entrusting the care of a plant to another person. This invented scene is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical account or a claim about gender roles.

## Notes

- Keep both い sounds in いいん: イ + イン. The committee member is 委員, not 委 alone.
- Compare [季](季.md), seasons: its lower component is 子 rather than 女.
- 委ねる is an ichidan verb: 委ねます ゆだねます and 委ねた ゆだねた.
