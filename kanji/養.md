# 養 — nourish; raise; adopt

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 食 (no. 184), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

養 means to nourish and look after something so that it grows, whether a body, a child or the mind, and also to take in a child as one's own. [休養](../words/休養.md) きゅうよう is recuperative rest, [教養](../words/教養.md) きょうよう is cultivated knowledge, [栄養](../words/栄養.md) えいよう is nutrition and [養分](../words/養分.md) ようぶん is nutrients.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses. The first is feeding and strengthening the body, as in 養生 ようじょう, "taking care of one's health." The second is looking after someone by providing food and care, as in 養育 よういく, "bringing up; rearing; upbringing," and 養殖 ようしょく, "aquaculture; culture; cultivation; raising."

The third is nourishing the mind to raise a person well, as in 養成 ようせい, "training; education; development; cultivation." The fourth is taking another person's child as one's own, as in 養子 ようし, "adopted child (usu. male); son-in-law," and 養女 ようじょ, "adopted daughter; foster daughter." The verb 養う やしなう covers the same range, and JMdict lists the senses "to support; to maintain; to provide for," "to bring up; to raise; to rear; to feed" and "to adopt (a child)."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 羊, which does not have a kanji card here, at the top and [食](食.md), "eat, food," at the bottom. Imagine a sheep standing over a meal while it is fed and raised. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 羊 or 食 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヨウ | on | [休養](../words/休養.md) きゅうよう — recuperative rest; relaxation; [教養](../words/教養.md) きょうよう — culture; cultivated knowledge; [栄養](../words/栄養.md) えいよう — nutrition; nourishment; [養分](../words/養分.md) ようぶん — nutrients; nourishment |
| リョウ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; the りょう of 療養 りょうよう belongs to 療 and does not establish this reading of 養 |
| やしな.う | kun | 養う やしなう - to support; to maintain; to provide for; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 休養, 休 supplies キュウ and 養 reads ヨウ. In 教養, 教 supplies キョウ, in 栄養, 栄 supplies エイ, and in 養分, 分 supplies ブン. In 療養, "recuperation; medical treatment," the りょう belongs to 療 and 養 reads ヨウ again, so the word is not an example of リョウ for 養; this card supplies no verified example of that reading. In 養う, 養 writes やしな and う is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The Daijisen entry labels ヨウ, which it also spells ヤウ in historical kana, as both 呉音, the older Chinese-derived (Go-on) layer, and 漢音, a later Chinese-derived layer, so the two layers agree.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ヨウ, with 養育, 養子 and 休養 as examples, and やしなう, with 養う. Practice selects ヨウ, the only reading with linked word articles; やしな.う is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word, and リョウ, which the Joyo table does not list, is source-only here. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [休](休.md), "rest," in 休養, [教](教.md), "teach, instruct," in 教養, [栄](栄.md), "flourish; glory," in 栄養 and [分](分.md), "minute, part," in 養分.
- [食](食.md), "eat, food," is the key at the bottom. 羊, the top of 養, is also the top of [義](義.md), "righteousness; duty; meaning," and a part of [洋](洋.md), "ocean; Western style," and [様](様.md), "manner; honorific suffix," which are also read ヨウ, as is [葉](葉.md), "leaf." All are different kanji.
- [育](育.md), "grow up; raise," [採](採.md), "pick; collect; adopt," [支](支.md), "support," [修](修.md), "repair; amend; cultivate," and [休](休.md), "rest," overlap the senses of raising, adopting, supporting, cultivating and resting that 養 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "raise" or "nourish" as exclusive to any one character.
