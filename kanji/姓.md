# 姓 — surname; family name

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

姓 names a surname or family name. The standalone noun is 姓 せい; 姓名 せいめい is a full name, combining the family-name and personal-name ideas.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine 女 beside 生 as a reminder of a name shared through a family. This is an invented modern-shape scene, not a claim about the history of surnames.

## Notes

- JMdict marks the farmer sense of 百姓 ひゃくしょう as sensitive and gives a separate derogatory yokel sense. The reading example is not a neutral label to apply indiscriminately to a farmer.
- 名字, also written 苗字, is read みょうじ and means family name. By contrast, 姓名 せいめい includes more than the surname alone.
- Compare [性](性.md), nature or gender. Both can be セイ in compounds, but the left-hand component and meaning differ.
