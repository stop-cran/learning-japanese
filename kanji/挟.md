# 挟 — pinch; hold between

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

挟 concerns holding or placing something between two sides. 挟む はさむ is transitive; 挟まる はさまる describes something getting between or being caught. The idea also appears in 挟撃 きょうげき, an attack from both sides.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside the balanced central shape on the right and imagine a hand holding an object between two sides. This is a visual mnemonic, not a historical explanation.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.

## Notes

- The linked 挟む article teaches はさむ. The dictionary also records 挟む さしはさむ under the same entry as 差し挟む さしはさむ; retain the shorter spelling for recognition rather than reading every 挟む that way.
- 挟む はさむ takes the object with を; 挟まる はさまる presents the thing caught with が. They do not conjugate alike: 挟んで はさんで versus 挟まって はさまって.
