# 挙 — raise; cite; hold (an event)

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

挙 is lifting something up for all to see: raising a hand, citing an example, holding an event. [挙げる](../words/挙げる.md) あげる means to raise or to cite an example. JMdict's entry for 挙げる lists eleven senses. The first is "to give (an example); to list; to enumerate; to show; to display; to cite," and others include "to hold (an event, ceremony, etc.); to conduct (a wedding)," "to arrest (a criminal)," "to nominate (e.g. for a position); to recommend; to propose (something)," "to raise (an army)" and "to raise; to elevate." It marks one sense, "to have (a child); to bear (a child); to raise (children)," as a dated term.

The Daijisen character entry gives six senses for キョ. One example of each:

- lifting high: 挙手 きょしゅ, "raising one's hand; show of hands (e.g. for a vote)";
- picking out from among many: 列挙 れっきょ, "enumeration; list";
- starting an undertaking, or the undertaking itself: 挙式 きょしき, "holding a ceremony; wedding ceremony";
- behavior: 挙動 きょどう, "conduct; behavior";
- all of something, all together: 挙国 きょこく, "the whole nation";
- catching: 検挙 けんきょ, "arrest; roundup."

It lists the kun readings あげる, あがる and こぞる, and has a separate noun entry きょ【挙】 for an action or undertaking; JMdict's 挙 きょ is glossed "action; behavior; behaviour; move." More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the bottom, under a top part made of short strokes and horizontal lines. Imagine a hand at the bottom pushing a bundle up into the air so that everyone can see it, as when you raise a hand in class. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that these parts contribute meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 挙げる, 挙 writes あ and げる is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 挙がる has the same あ with がる; JMdict also lists 挙る as a search-only kanji form of this あがる. In 挙る こぞる, 挙 writes こぞ and る is okurigana. JMdict's 挙って こぞって, "all; all together; unanimously," is marked "word usually written using kana alone." In 挙手, 挙 supplies キョ and 手 supplies シュ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows 挙 with its older form 擧 in parentheses. It lists キョ, with 挙手, 挙国 and 壮挙 そうきょ, "ambitious (heroic) undertaking; daring enterprise; grand scheme," as examples; あげる, with 挙げる and 挙げて〔副〕 あげて, "all; whole; en masse," where 〔副〕 marks an adverb, compared (⇔) with 上げる あげる, "to raise; to elevate," and 揚げる あげる, "to deep-fry; to make deep-fried food"; and あがる, with 挙がる, compared with 上がる あがる, "to rise; to go up," and 揚がる あがる, "to be deep fried." It has no row for こぞる.

Practice selects あ.げる, the reading with a linked word article. The app groups kun readings with the same stem before the dot, so あ.げる supplies the one question for that stem, shown as あげる; キョ, あ.がる and こぞ.る are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- 挙げる is 挙 with okurigana only, so the common words contain no other kanji.
- [巻](巻.md), "roll; book volume," has a similar top part of short strokes over horizontal lines, and [探](探.md), "search," and [掃](掃.md), "sweep; brush," have 手 at the left, written 扌. All are different kanji.
- [撃](撃.md), "shoot; strike," and [摩](摩.md), "rub; chafe," also have 手 at the bottom.
- [上](上.md), "up, above," and [揚](揚.md), "hoist; lift; deep-fry," also write あげる and あがる; [例](例.md), "example; precedent," matches citing an example; [催](催.md), "host an event; sponsor," [式](式.md), "ceremony; formula," [儀](儀.md), "ceremony; formal conduct," [持](持.md), "hold, have," and [執](執.md), "take hold; carry out; cling," overlap holding an event; [逮](逮.md), "arrest; chase," and [捕](捕.md), "capture," match the arrest sense of 挙げる; and [勧](勧.md), "recommend; encourage," matches its recommend sense. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "raise" or "hold" as exclusive to any one character.
- Raising a hand or lifting something up for all to see is 挙. Raising a child is a different idea, expressed by 育てる そだてる, "to raise; to rear; to bring up," and 養う やしなう, "to bring up; to raise; to rear; to feed." English "raise" covers both ideas, so [育](育.md), "grow up; raise," and [養](養.md), "nourish; raise; adopt," remain ordinary wrong options for 挙.
- [提](提.md), "submit; propose; carry in hand," and [推](推.md), "infer; recommend; push forward," share proposing and recommending: JMdict glosses one sense of 挙げる "to nominate (e.g. for a position); to recommend; to propose (something)", and 推 appears with 挙 in 推挙, "recommendation (of a person for a position); nomination." The three are kept out of one another's quiz options.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the second キョ sense, picking out: 挙用 きょよう, "appointment; promotion," 科挙 かきょ, marked archaic in JMdict, "Chinese higher civil service examinations," 推挙 すいきょ, "recommendation (of a person for a position); nomination," 選挙 せんきょ, "election," and 枚挙 まいきょ, "enumeration."
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the third キョ sense, an undertaking: 挙行 きょこう, "celebration (of ceremony); solemnization (e.g. of a marriage)," 挙兵 きょへい, "raising an army," 一挙 いっきょ, "one effort; one action," 快挙 かいきょ, "brilliant achievement; spectacular feat," 義挙 ぎきょ, "noble undertaking; heroic deed," 再挙 さいきょ, "another attempt; another try," 壮挙 and 暴挙 ぼうきょ, "violence; reckless action; (an) outrage."
- For reference, a further Daijisen example of the fourth キョ sense is 挙止 きょし, "bearing; deportment," and of the fifth 挙党 きょとう, "whole party; party as a whole."
