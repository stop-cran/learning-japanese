# 緩 — loosen; slack; lenient

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

緩 is looseness and ease: a knot or garment that is not tight, rules that are not strict, a slope that is not steep. [緩い](../words/緩い.md) ゆるい is the adjective; JMdict glosses it "loose," "lenient; lax," "gentle (curve, slope, etc.)," "slow; weak" and "soft; not firm." The word article teaches "loose; lax; gentle."

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for カン, having room to spare, being relaxed, and making something loose, with 緩急 かんきゅう, "high and low speed; fast and slow; pace; tempo," 緩慢 かんまん, "slow; sluggish; dull; slack; inactive," and 緩和 かんわ among its examples. It lists the kun readings ゆるい, ゆるやか, ゆるむ and ゆるめる; its name readings のぶ, ひろ, ふさ and やす are not treated here. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 糸, "thread," at the left and the shape 爰 at the right, which also appears in [援](援.md), "aid; assistance," and [暖](暖.md), "warm; warm up." Imagine a thread going slack. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 糸 or 爰 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

In 緩い, 緩 writes ゆる and い is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. 緩やか ゆるやか, 緩む ゆるむ and 緩める ゆるめる likewise take ゆる from 緩, with やか, む and める as okurigana; 緩み ゆるみ, "slack; looseness; play," is the noun form of 緩む. In 緩和, 緩 supplies カン and 和 supplies ワ; in 緩慢, 慢 supplies マン; in 緩急, 急 supplies キュウ.

JMdict also has a different word 緩い ぬるい, "lukewarm; tepid" and "lenient," marked "word usually written using kana alone," in which 緩い is a "search-only kanji form" and 温い is the listed spelling. It is not ゆるい, and the ぬる.い that Kanjipedia lists for 緩 as an outside-the-standard-table reading is not taught or selected here.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows カン, with 緩和, 緩慢 and 緩急; ゆるい, with 緩い; ゆるやか, with 緩やかだ; ゆるむ, with 緩む and 緩み; and ゆるめる, with 緩める. Practice selects ゆる.い, the reading of the linked word article 緩い ゆるい; the other readings are illustrated with dictionary words and are not invalid for being outside the practice set.

## Notes

- 緩い contains no other kanji; the い is okurigana. Among the compounds named above, [和](和.md), "harmony; Japanese style," supplies ワ in 緩和, [慢](慢.md), "arrogance; slackness," supplies マン in 緩慢 (its card links 緩) and [急](急.md), "urgent, sudden," supplies キュウ in 緩急. JMdict also glosses 緩急 "lenience and severity" and "emergency; crisis; dire situation."
- [穏](穏.md), "calm; gentle; peaceful," and [優](優.md), "gentle; superior," overlap the "gentle" of 緩い and 緩やか. [柔](柔.md), "soft; supple," and [軟](軟.md), "soft; pliant," overlap "soft; not firm." [慢](慢.md), "arrogance; slackness," overlaps "slack" and 緩慢. [遅](遅.md), "late; slow," [鈍](鈍.md), "dull; blunt," [弱](弱.md), "weak; frail," [徐](徐.md), "slowly; gradually," and [悠](悠.md), "leisurely; distant," overlap "slow; weak" and the unhurried sense. [甘](甘.md), "sweet; pamper," and [怠](怠.md), "neglect; be lazy," overlap "lenient; lax" and JMdict's 緩怠 "laxity; negligence." [憩](憩.md), "rest; take a break; relax," [和](和.md), "harmony; Japanese style," and [楽](楽.md), "music, enjoyment, ease," overlap the relaxation and relief of 緩和. [渋](渋.md), "astringent; sluggish; reluctant," overlaps "sluggish." All are kept out of this card's quiz options. [緊](緊.md) and [締](締.md) mean tightening, the opposite of loosening, and are separate cards.
- [援](援.md), "aid; assistance," looks plausible because it contains the same right-hand shape as 緩, but it is read エン and means aid. [絵](絵.md), "drawing; painting," looks plausible because it also has 糸 at the left, but it is read カイ and エ and means a picture. [犬](犬.md), "dog," is a common beginner kanji read ケン and いぬ. None shares a reading or a meaning with 緩. [縦](縦.md), "vertical; lengthwise; at will," also lists ゆる.める as a Kanjipedia reading outside the standard table, and is a separate card.
- Many other kanji read カン, such as [間](間.md), [関](関.md) and [感](感.md), and are never offered as wrong answers.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples of the カン sense: 緩衝 かんしょう, "shock absorption; buffer action; cushioning; buffer"; 緩怠 かんたい, "laxity; negligence"; 緩下剤 かんげざい, "laxatives"; and 弛緩 しかん, "relaxation (e.g. of muscles); becoming flaccid." Daijisen also has literary headwords ゆる and かん, marked as forms of adjectival nouns in the older language, which are not taught here.
