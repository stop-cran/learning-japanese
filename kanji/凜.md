# 凜 — dignified; bracing cold

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 冫 (the two left-hand marks, no. 15) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凜 can describe a dignified, tightly composed bearing or bracing, severe cold.
Learn it through [凜](../words/凜.md) (りん), especially 凜とした before a noun.
The word also has a clear, ringing sound sense; these meanings are not
interchangeable descriptions of everything that is "strict."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the two icy 冫 marks beside
the tall right-hand 稟 (りん) block as someone standing straight in bracing air.
The bottom of that block has the 禾 shape. This is an invented memory aid,
not a verified historical or phonetic explanation.

## Notes

凛 is a recognized variant of 凜, with the same りん reading in the linked
word's dictionary family. Compare the lowest right-hand part: 凜 has 禾,
while 凛 has a [示](示.md)-shaped bottom. They are different characters for
typing and this card's identity; practice writing the displayed 凜.
No separate variant card or personal-name reading course is supplied here.
The grade-9 source classification concerns name-use kanji, not name-only usage.

For other visual contrasts, [准](准.md) has a bird-shaped right side,
[冗](冗.md) has a cover-like top, and [凹](凹.md) has a sunken outline.
Their meanings are not dignified bearing or bracing cold.
