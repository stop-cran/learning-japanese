# 位 — rank; position

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

位 identifies a position in an ordering or hierarchy.
[地位](../words/地位.md) ちい concerns social standing; 一位 いちい is first place in a ranking.
Position here need not be a physical spot on the ground.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the person on the left standing at a ranked position beside [立](立.md).
The visible combination helps memory; no historical structural classification is asserted.

## Notes

Radical 人 is visible as 亻. Removing it leaves [立](立.md); [住](住.md) and [体](体.md) have different right sides.
There is also an approximate-amount expression くらい, as in 三分くらい (さんぷんくらい), "about three minutes."
JMdict lists 位 only as a lookup spelling for that expression; use the kana form in this example.
Do not confuse this with the noun 位 meaning rank.
