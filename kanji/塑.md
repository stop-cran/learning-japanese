# 塑 — model in clay

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

塑 is shaping pliable material to make a figure. It is the modeling side of sculpture.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The character rests on 土. Picture a soft lump of clay that can be shaped.

## Notes

A 型 is a mold or pattern; 塑 describes the shaping rather than the mold as a tool.

Look at the bottom: [望](望.md) (hope) ends in 王, while 塑 ends in 土. [塔](塔.md) (tower) and [塀](塀.md) (boundary wall) put 土 on the left. [彫](彫.md) (carve) suggests cutting into the material; 塑 suggests building up a form.
