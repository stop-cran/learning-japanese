# 密 — secret; dense; close

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

密 has two main ideas: something hidden from others, and things packed closely together with no gaps. [秘密](../words/秘密.md) ひみつ is a secret; JMdict glosses it "secret; secrecy; confidentiality; privacy," then "mystery," "secret (e.g. to success)" and "esoteric teachings." The word [密](../words/密.md) みつ means dense or close; JMdict glosses it "dense; thick; crowded," "close (relationship); intimate," "minute; fine; careful," "secret," and, as an abbreviation, "esoteric Buddhism."

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses for ミツ. One example of each:

- hidden and unknown to others: 密約 みつやく, "secret agreement";
- having no gaps, closely joined, or a close relationship: 密集 みっしゅう, "crowding together; clustering together; close formation; swarm";
- fine and thorough: 精密 せいみつ, "precise; exact; detailed; accurate; minute; close";
- esoteric Buddhism: 台密 たいみつ, "esoteric Buddhism of the Japanese Tendai sect."

It gives the kun readings ひそか, the same as KANJIDIC2, and みそか, which Daijisen also lists as a separate headword with this spelling; JMdict has no entry for みそか spelled 密か. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the roof-like 宀 at the top, [必](必.md), "without fail," in the middle, and [山](山.md), "mountain," at the bottom. Imagine a house hidden deep in the mountains, where a secret is kept under the roof without fail. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 宀, 必 or 山 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ミツ | on | [秘密](../words/秘密.md) ひみつ — secret; [密](../words/密.md) みつ — dense; close |
| ひそ.か | kun | 密か ひそか - secret; private; surreptitious; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 秘密, 秘 supplies ヒ and 密 supplies ミツ, with no sound change. The word 密 is the kanji alone, read みつ. Before a k, s, t or p sound, ミツ often becomes みっ, a small っ that doubles the next consonant, as in 密集 みっしゅう and 密閉 みっぺい, "shutting tightly; making airtight." In 密か, 密 writes ひそ and か is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict lists other spellings of ひそか, such as 秘か and 窃か, and the related word 密やか ひそやか, "quiet; still; surreptitious."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows only ミツ, with 密約, 厳密 げんみつ, "strict; close; precise; scrupulous," and 秘密 as examples. ひそ.か is not in the official table. Practice selects ミツ, the reading with linked word articles; ひそ.か is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [秘](秘.md), "secret," in 秘密. The word 密 contains only this kanji.
- [必](必.md), "without fail," is the middle part of 密 written on its own. [宝](宝.md), "treasure; valuables," has the same 宀 at the top, and [島](島.md), "island," has the same 山 at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [親](親.md), "parent; close," [封](封.md), "seal; close," [近](近.md), "near," [込](込.md), "crowded; included," [詰](詰.md), "pack tightly; press," [細](細.md), "slender; fine; detailed," [微](微.md), "minute; faint," [濃](濃.md), "concentrated; dark," [厚](厚.md), "thick; cordial," and [私](私.md), "I; private," overlap the senses "close," "dense," "crowded," "fine," "minute," "thick," "private" and "shut tightly" that 密, 秘密 and 密か carry; 親 appears with 密 in 親密. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "secret" or "close" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples. Hidden: 密会 みっかい, "secret meeting; clandestine meeting"; 密航 みっこう, "stowing away; smuggling oneself; clandestine travel abroad"; 密室 みっしつ, "locked room," then "secret room"; 密輸 みつゆ, "smuggling; contraband trade"; 隠密 おんみつ, "secret; clandestine; covert; stealthy," then, historically, "spy (for a daimyo, shogun, etc.); secret agent"; 機密 きみつ, "secrecy; highly classified information"; 内密 ないみつ, "privacy; secrecy; confidentiality." No gaps or close: 密着 みっちゃく, "close adhesion; sticking firmly (to); being glued (to)"; 密度 みつど, "density"; 密林 みつりん, "close thicket; dense forest; jungle"; 過密 かみつ, "overcrowding; congestion; overpopulation"; 緊密 きんみつ, "rigour; rigor; closeness; compactness; tightly knit"; 親密 しんみつ, "intimacy; friendship." Fine: 厳密; 綿密 めんみつ, "minute; detailed; careful; scrupulous; thorough." Esoteric Buddhism: 東密 とうみつ, "esoteric Buddhism of the Japanese Shingon sect."
