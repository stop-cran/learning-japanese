# 干 — dry; ebb

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 干 (no. 51) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

干 is associated with drying and with water ebbing or receding. 干す ほす is a transitive drying or airing action; 干る ひる is an intransitive verb for drying up or ebbing. 干拓 かんたく means land reclamation from the sea.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the two horizontal strokes crossed by one vertical stroke; imagine an outdoor drying stand. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not etymology.

## Notes

- The prefix 干し- ほし- contributes dried, as in 干し芋 ほしいも. The suffix -ぼし is also written 干し, as in 日干し ひぼし. In both spellings the final し remains kana.
- The transitive action 干す is not the same grammatical construction as [乾く](../words/乾く.md) かわく, become dry.
- Compare [千](千.md), thousand, and [午](午.md), noon: their extra or differently shaped strokes matter.
