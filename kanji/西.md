# 西 — west

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 襾 (no. 146) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

The oldest forms of 西 look like a basket or a bird's nest. A common account says the character first meant "nest" and was used for
"west" because birds return to the nest at sunset, when the sun is in the west; others think "west" was borrowed purely for
its sound. Both are debated, so treat the image as a mnemonic. 襾 is a rare key, listed by shape.

- 関西 かんさい — the Kansai region

## Notes

- Both on readings occur: セイ in 西洋 せいよう, サイ in 関西 かんさい. 東西 とうざい (east and west) uses the on reading ザイ (voiced サイ).
- Look-alikes: 酉 (the rooster sign, with a full-height frame and a 一 across the middle) and [四](四.md) (four, a box with two legs inside).
- The four directions: [東](東.md), 西, [南](南.md), [北](北.md).
