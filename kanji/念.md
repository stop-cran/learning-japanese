# 念 — thought; attention

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

念 concerns thought, attention, or a feeling held in mind. Its contribution changes with the word:
[残念](../words/残念.md) ざんねん expresses disappointment, while 念のため ねんのため means
"just to make sure" or "as a precaution."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** [今](今.md), "now," sits above [心](心.md); keep the matter in mind now.
This is a memory aid based on the modern shape, not a historical explanation.

## Common words

- [残念](../words/残念.md) ざんねん — disappointing; unfortunate
- 念のため ねんのため — just to be sure; as a precaution

## Notes

念のため is a useful whole expression; "for the sake of a thought" is not a useful literal translation.
Keep the 今 top distinct from 亡 in [忘](忘.md), 自 in [息](息.md), and the upper part of [怒](怒.md).
The shared 心 does not give these four characters the same meaning.
