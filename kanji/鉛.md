# 鉛 — lead (metal)

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 金 (no. 167) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

鉛 なまり is lead, the metallic element Pb. It is not the English
verb "lead," meaning guide. [鉛筆](../words/鉛筆.md) えんぴつ is a
pencil, but a modern pencil's writing core is graphite-based, not
metallic lead.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 金 at the left and
an 八-like shape above 口 at the right; imagine a heavy metal block
below two lifting handles. This is an invented modern-shape memory aid,
not an explanation of the historical components or pronunciation.

In えんぴつ, 鉛 supplies エン; 筆 has the form ぴつ.
字通 also cites the old dictionary form くろなまり. It is retained
as supplemental evidence, not added to practice.

## Common words

- [鉛筆](../words/鉛筆.md) えんぴつ - pencil.
- 鉛 なまり - lead (Pb).
- 黒鉛 こくえん - graphite, not metallic lead.

## Notes

- 日本国語大辞典 also records なまり for counterfeit money and for
  things felt to be heavy or dull. Digital Daijisen's character entry
  includes white face powder and lead-like substances in compounds
  such as 黒鉛, graphite, 亜鉛 あえん, zinc, and 蒼鉛 そうえん,
  bismuth. These are not all the same chemical element.
- Direct precautions with [金](金.md), [素](素.md) and [鉱](鉱.md)
  protect broad metal/element labels, including ore or unrefined metal.
  鉛鉱 means lead mine/deposits, not lead ore (JMdict 1736440).
  Those with [銀](銀.md), [両](両.md), [札](札.md), [財](財.md),
  [貨](貨.md) and [円](円.md) protect money labels: 日本国語大辞典's
  まろ【円・丸】 records 銭. This does not transfer every 丸 sense
  to 円 or equate every coin, banknote or possession with counterfeit money.
- [粉](粉.md), [末](末.md) and [顔](顔.md) receive precautions for
  powder/cosmetics: 顔 also has a sourced makeup sense, not merely
  the body part to which powder is applied.
- [重](重.md), [鈍](鈍.md), [遅](遅.md), [厚](厚.md) and [量](量.md) receive
  conservative precautions for unresolved heavy/dull/weight labels.
  Lead is not a synonym for every weight or quantity.
- [古](古.md), old, [右](右.md), right, and [川](川.md), river,
  remain independently distinct from the sourced metal, powder,
  money and heavy/dull senses.
