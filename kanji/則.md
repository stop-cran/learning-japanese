# 則 — rule; follow a model

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

則 concerns a rule, standard, or following a model. 規則 きそく means rules or regulations; 原則 げんそく is a principle. 則る のっとる means to follow a rule, precedent, or model.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 貝-shaped left block beside a blade-shaped 刂, as though making a clear mark for a rule.

The canonical indexing radical is 刀, visible as 刂 on the right. The larger left block does not replace that metadata.

## Notes

- 則る uses に for the rule or precedent being followed: 規則に則る きそくにのっとる, follow the rules.
- The source lists すなわち without an okurigana dot; the attested word spelling here is 則ち, with written ち. JMdict marks that kanji spelling rare and the word usually written in kana. Learn ordinary すなわち rather than treating 則ち as the preferred modern spelling.
- The single-character のり is a dictionary reading for a rule or standard, not a reason to change the pronunciation of 規則.
