# 古 — old

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **Phonetic:** none given · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

古 is 十 over 口. A frequently told explanation is "what has been passed down by mouth through ten generations", hence "old, ancient". This is a mnemonic;
the earliest form is debated, and some scholars read the character differently (as a phonetic compound).
古 means "old" for things and times past, not for a person's age.

## Notes

Its opposite is [新](新.md). 古い is for things and ideas; for a person's age use 年を取った としをとった or 年寄り としより (elderly person); 年上 としうえ means "older than me".
Look-alikes: 右 ([右](右.md)), which has a diagonal stroke instead of the vertical; 石 (stone), which has 厂 on top; 苦 (bitter), which has 艹 on top.
