# 案 — proposal; draft plan

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

案 commonly concerns a **proposal, idea or draft**: [案](../words/案.md) あん is a proposal or draft, and [提案](../words/提案.md) ていあん is proposing or a suggestion. The established compound [案内](../words/案内.md) あんない means guidance or information, not literally "a plan inside."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice [安](安.md) above [木](木.md). Imagine calmly drafting a proposal at a wooden desk. This is a modern memory aid, not a claim about the character's origin.

## Notes

- A proposed 案 need not have been adopted. [計](計.md), plan or measure, and [策](策.md), strategy or policy, overlap in planning meanings; they are useful comparisons, not unambiguously wrong meaning choices.
- The source-listed つくえ does not make ordinary 案内 or 提案 native-reading words. Practice selects アン.
- Unlike [安](安.md), 案 has 木 below. Keep all ten strokes rather than treating the lower part as optional.
