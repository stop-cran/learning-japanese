# 尉 — officer rank; old man in Noh

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

尉 occurs in officer ranks, as in 大尉 (たいい). The standalone reading じょう belongs to a different setting, including the old-man role in Noh theater.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Look for 寸 on the right. [慰](慰.md) contains the shape of 尉 above 心; the extra heart at the bottom changes the character.

## Readings

## Common words

## Notes

[射](射.md) and [討](討.md) also end in 寸 but begin differently. Compare [慰](慰.md) before reading. [官](官.md) can mean an official and [位](位.md) a rank; neither specifies this rank group. [老](老.md) concerns old age; reading 尉 as じょう does not turn 大尉 into a word for an old man.
