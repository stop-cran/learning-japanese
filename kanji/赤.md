# 赤 — red

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 赤 (no. 155) · **Phonetic:** none · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

赤 is traditionally analysed as 大 over 火, a big fire, hence "red". The modern shape has been simplified and looks like 土 over a changed 火, so the story is only a mnemonic;
scholars disagree about the original form. It means "red" and, in compounds, "bare, complete" (赤の他人 あかのたにん, a total stranger) and "baby" (赤ちゃん).

## Notes

Colour siblings: [青](青.md), [白](白.md), [黒](黒.md).
Okurigana (the kana after the kanji, like い in 赤い): 赤い (adjective) versus 赤 (noun) and 赤らむ (verb). Compare 真っ赤 まっか (bright red), with a sound change (small っ) before か.
