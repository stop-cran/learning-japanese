# 司 — take charge; official; preside

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30), inside the frame · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

司 is taking charge of a duty or of proceedings, and the person or office that does so. [司会](../words/司会.md) しかい is presiding over a meeting or event, or the host or moderator who does it. JMdict glosses the verb 司る つかさどる "to be in charge of; to manage; to direct; to administer; to preside over."

The Daijisen character entry gives three senses for シ. One example of each:

- taking charge of a duty: 司書 ししょ, "librarian," besides 司会;
- a person in charge: 上司 じょうし, "(one's) superior; (one's) boss; the higher-ups";
- a government office: 写経司, a historical office name that JMdict does not list.

It also gives ス as a reading and lists the kun readings つかさどる and つかさ; KANJIDIC2 does not list ス, and lists つかさ only as a name reading. Among words with a hard-to-guess reading it names 下司 げす, "vulgar person; low-life; sleazebag; boor," and 公司 コンス, "company (in China); firm," which JMdict also reads こうし. More Daijisen examples are listed at the end of the Notes.

Daijisen also has headwords つかさ and みこともち that list 司 as a spelling outside the official table. JMdict glosses つかさ "office; official"; みこともち, an official sent to a province under imperial orders in ancient times, has no JMdict entry.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [口](口.md), "mouth," inside a frame that hooks down at the right, with a short stroke above it. Imagine an official under a roof, mouth open, directing the meeting. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 口 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | [司会](../words/司会.md) しかい — presiding; host or moderator |
| つかさど.る | kun | 司る つかさどる - to be in charge of; to manage; to direct; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 司会, 司 supplies シ and 会 supplies カイ. In 司る, 司 writes つかさど and る is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. After another kanji, シ can be voiced to じ (rendaku, the voicing of a sound at the start of a later element), as in 行司 ぎょうじ, "referee," and 宮司 ぐうじ, "chief priest."

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シ, with 司会, 司令 しれい, "command; commander; commandant," and 上司 as examples. つかさどる is not in the official table. Practice selects シ, the reading of the linked word article 司会; つかさど.る is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with a dictionary word. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words is [会](会.md), "meet," in 司会.
- [句](句.md), "phrase; verse," also has 口 inside a hooked frame. [同](同.md), "same, equal," and [向](向.md), "face toward," have 口 inside a frame open at the bottom. All are different kanji.
- [係](係.md), "connection; person in charge," [官](官.md), "government official," [公](公.md), "public, official," [管](管.md), "pipe; supervise," [監](監.md), "oversee; watch over," [督](督.md), "supervise; urge," [営](営.md), "conduct business; manage," [治](治.md), "govern; heal," and [御](御.md), "honorific; govern," overlap the senses "in charge," "official," "manage" and "govern" that 司 and its words carry; JMdict's 司る includes "to rule; to govern; to control." They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "take charge" or "official" as exclusive to any one character.
- For reference, more Daijisen examples: 司令 for taking charge, and 国司 こくし, "provincial official (under the ritsuryō system; esp. a governor)," a historical term, and 保護司 ほごし, "probation officer," for a person in charge.
