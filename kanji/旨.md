# 旨 — purport; gist; delicious

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

旨 is the central point or purport of what is said, and can also describe something tasty. [要旨](../words/要旨.md) ようし is the gist or main points of a text; JMdict glosses it “(main) point; gist; key points.” In 要旨, 旨 is read シ, not うま.い.

Daijisen gives two meanings for シ: the content or intention of what is said, and something delicious. The kun reading むね also refers to a main purpose or the central point of a statement; うま.い is “delicious; tasty”; JMdict spells its “skillful; skilful; skilled” sense 上手い or 巧い.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 日, “sun, day,” at the bottom, with a compact angled shape above it. Picture the key point sitting above a page. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that either visible part contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | [要旨](../words/要旨.md) ようし — (main) point; gist; key points |
| むね | kun | 旨 むね — principle; aim; main purpose; central part; pillar; purport; gist; drift; meaning; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| うま.い | kun | 旨い うまい — delicious; tasty; good; nice; JMdict also lists 旨い as a spelling but marks the word usually written using kana alone; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 要旨, 要 supplies ヨウ and 旨 supplies シ. The stand-alone word 旨 むね means a principle, aim or the central point. JMdict lists 旨い as a spelling of うまい and labels the word “usually written using kana alone.” A separate JMdict entry for うまみ lists 旨み and 旨味, and tags 旨味 as ateji (kanji chosen for sound or sense); 旨 is read うま in both.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows シ with 要旨, 趣旨 and 本旨, and むね with 旨. JMdict glosses 趣旨 as “meaning; point (e.g. of a statement); gist; effect” and “goal; intent; object; aim; point”; 本旨 is “main object; principal object; true aim.” The reading うま.い is not in the table. Practice selects シ, the reading with a linked word article. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.
