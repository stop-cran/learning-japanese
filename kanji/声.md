# 声 — voice

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 士 (no. 33) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

声 centers on voice, especially a sound produced by a speaker or calling animal.
The standalone word is [声](../words/声.md) こえ. 大声 おおごえ is a loud voice, while 音声 おんせい can refer to voice, speech, or recorded audio.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a voice emerging from under the broad top bar. This is a visual memory aid; no historical reconstruction is claimed here.

## Notes

The upper radical is 士: its top horizontal is longer than the lower one. The metadata follows that indexing, not 土.
[売](売.md), [古](古.md), and [告](告.md) offer stacked-shape contrasts.
[音](音.md) means sound more broadly and can overlap with 声; it is not a clean opposite.
Notice the voiced ごえ in 大声, rather than おおこえ.
