# 微 — minute; faint

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

微 concerns something very small, slight or difficult to notice.
[顕微鏡](../words/顕微鏡.md) けんびきょう is a microscope, used to see
minute things. [微妙](../words/微妙.md) びみょう concerns subtle or delicate
differences; a conversational judgment can instead be iffy or not very good.
Smallness alone does not predict every meaning of the complete word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 彳 on the left, a small
[山](山.md) above a 兀-shaped group in the middle, and 攵 on the right.
Imagine zooming in on a tiny mountain between the two side groups.
This is a memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ビ | on | [微妙](../words/微妙.md) びみょう - subtle; delicate; [顕微鏡](../words/顕微鏡.md) けんびきょう - microscope |
| かす.か | kun | 微か かすか - faint; slight; usually written in kana; no word article yet |

Practice selects ビ. 微か writes かす with 微 and the final か in kana;
it explains the preserved kun reading but is not selected without a linked
word article.

[微笑む](../words/微笑む.md) ほほえむ means smile. Learn the complete
native reading: it is not evidence that 微 independently supplies ほほ.
The linked article also records ほおえむ. Neither reading is obtained by
substituting ビ into part of the word.

## Notes

- [復](復.md), return/repeat, [御](御.md), honorific prefix/control,
  and [律](律.md), rule/rhythm, share the left 彳. Their right-hand
  groups and meanings differ.
- Smallness overlaps [細](細.md), [小](小.md) and [少](少.md);
  faintness or weakness overlaps [薄](薄.md) and [弱](弱.md).
  Subtlety also overlaps [妙](妙.md), whose direct exclusion names 微.
- A slight degree also overlaps [軽](軽.md), lightweight/slight; both
  characters appear in 軽微 けいび, slight or insignificant. That direct
  comparison is excluded too, without claiming that 微 means light in weight.
- Here English "minute" means tiny, not the unit of time associated with
  [分](分.md). That card's title is "minute, part"; the shared English
  spelling is excluded from meaning choices without treating the Japanese
  meanings as interchangeable.
- The obsolete 微妙 reading みみょう is a separate dictionary entry with
  sublime or exquisite senses, not a replacement for modern びみょう.
- In 顕微鏡, [鏡](鏡.md) supplies きょう. The character 顕 has no card
  here yet; this does not change the complete word's reading.
