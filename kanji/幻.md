# 幻 — illusion; apparition

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 幺 (no. 52) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

幻 is something that appears real but may vanish. 幻の鳥 (まぼろしのとり) can be a real bird so rarely seen that it seems legendary.

**Shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare the right-hand hook of 幻 with 力 on the right in 幼.
