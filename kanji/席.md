# 席 — seat; place

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 巾 (no. 50) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

席 is a seat or a place at a gathering, and it also appears in words about attending.
[欠席](../words/欠席.md) けっせき means absence or non-attendance; 出席 しゅっせき means attendance.
Here the issue is whether someone takes part, not simply whether a chair exists.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a 巾-shaped mat under the 广 shelter, with a place reserved above it.
This is a modern visual mnemonic, not a statement about the character's historical construction.

## Notes

The indexing radical is 巾 at the bottom, although 广 is conspicuous around the outside.
[度](度.md) has a similar upper arrangement but a different bottom.
[庭](庭.md) also has 广, while [市](市.md) is a simpler 巾-containing shape.
椅子 いす names a chair; 席 can instead identify someone's assigned place.
