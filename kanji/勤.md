# 勤 — employment; diligence

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 力 (no. 19) · **Phonetic:** not identified here · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

勤 appears in words about employment, service, and diligent activity.
[勤める](../words/勤める.md) つとめる describes working for an employer;
通勤 つうきん is the journey to work.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** think of 力 reporting for work beside the densely stacked left side.
This uses the modern outline only, not a verified origin.

## Notes

会社に勤める (かいしゃにつとめる) means "work for a company."
The employer takes に in this pattern.
Compare [動](動.md), whose left side is 重 rather than 勤's stacked shape.
