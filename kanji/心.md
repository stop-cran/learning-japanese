# 心 — heart, mind

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 心 (heart, no. 61) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

心 covers the heart, mind, feelings, and inner attitude. Japanese does not always divide "heart" and "mind" as English does. In some compounds, it also means a center or core.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the curved stroke as a bowl holding the surrounding dots like thoughts and feelings. This describes a way to remember the modern shape, not its historical development.

- 中心 ちゅうしん — center, core
- 真心 まごころ — sincerity

## Notes

心 appears at the bottom of [思](思.md), [急](急.md), [悪](悪.md), and [意](意.md). Recognizing it can help you remember those shapes, but it does not determine each whole character's meaning.

Do not confuse the curved stroke and three dots of 心 with the simpler arrangement of [小](小.md).
