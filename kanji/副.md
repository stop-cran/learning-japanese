# 副 — vice-; secondary

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

副 concerns an assistant, deputy, or secondary role. 副社長 ふくしゃちょう is an executive vice-president; 副作用 ふくさよう is a side effect. These are whole compounds, not a promise that adding 副 to any noun creates a normal Japanese title.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the stacked 畐-shaped block beside a blade-shaped 刂, as though separating off a secondary copy.

The canonical radical 刀 appears as the right-hand variant 刂.

## Notes

- 副詞 ふくし is the grammatical term adverb; it does not mean an assistant person.
- Distinguish [福](福.md), good fortune, and [富](富.md), wealth. Similar central shapes do not give these characters the same meaning.
