# 悠 — leisurely; distant

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), below · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

悠 can suggest an unhurried manner or something extending far in space or time. In [悠々](../words/悠々.md) ゆうゆう, it is repeated to express a leisurely or composed manner, or doing something easily.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the upper strokes stretching out above [心](心.md), a heart at leisure. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical explanation.

## Notes

- Weblio's character-dictionary section lists はるか and とおい as kun readings, which the pinned KANJIDIC2 record does not. Digital Daijisen separately lists はるか and the name readings ちか, ひさ and ひさし; these name readings are not added to practice metadata.
- [遠](遠.md) means far; [久](久.md), [永](永.md), and [長](長.md) concern long duration or lasting time. [常](常.md) includes constant or long-lasting, and [続](続.md) means continue; these overlap 悠's continuing extent, not every sense of either character. 悠久 and 悠遠 illustrate related senses, not a rule that their components can be exchanged freely.
- [静](静.md) includes quietness; [安](安.md), [平](平.md), [和](和.md), and [寧](寧.md) include peace or calm. [凪](凪.md) concerns a lull in wind or calm at sea. These overlap aspects of calmness, not every use of 悠.
- [楽](楽.md) includes ease, [休](休.md) rest, and [息](息.md) occurs in 休息, rest; [遅](遅.md) includes slowness. 悠々 can describe ease or a leisurely manner, but these are not interchangeable in every phrase. [広](広.md) describes width or space; 悠々 also has a distant or boundless sense.
- [易](易.md) means easy; [快](快.md) includes comfort. 悠々 can describe doing something easily or comfortably. [慢](慢.md) includes sluggishness, while 悠長 can mean slow or leisurely: neither contrast implies that every unhurried action is negligent.
- [遊](遊.md) concerns play, and [娯](娯.md) recreation; leisure can involve recreation, but 悠's unhurried manner need not mean playing. [延](延.md) and [伸](伸.md) include extending or lengthening, and [幅](幅.md) includes breadth or range; these are cautious comparisons with 悠's spatial or temporal extent, not interchangeable translations.
- [側](側.md), side, has 亻 on the left and a dense right side. [後](後.md), behind or after, and [達](達.md), reach or a plural suffix, have compact strokes beside a left-side component. These are loose shape comparisons, not synonyms of 悠.
