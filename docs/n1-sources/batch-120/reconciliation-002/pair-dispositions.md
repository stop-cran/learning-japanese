# Direct pair: 逮 and 押

The direct safeguard excludes the pair in both prompt directions. It preserves
the original comparison card and does not claim that every sense overlaps.

- 押 prompt → `arrest; chase`: a defensible arrest answer is supported by
  JMdict 1589080, whose third sense for おさえる includes obtain, seize, catch,
  and arrest without spelling or reading restrictions
  (`jmdict-records.xml:104-150`). The exact 押 card displays `push` and teaches
  押さえる for holding down or steady (`comparison-cards/押.md:3,21,33`).
  KANJIDIC2 independently lists “seize” for 押
  (`kanjidic2-records.xml:59-66`).
- 逮 prompt → `push`: general pushing is not established as a meaning of 逮.
  The shared direct guard is justified by the opposite direction alone, not
  by equating pushing with arrest or through another character's exclusions.

JMdict 1180470 preserves 押す pushing, pressing, and other senses, with 圧す
and 捺す marked search-only in its spelling list. JMdict 1589080 separately
marks 圧さえる search-only, and its hold-down sense notes that spelling
without restricting the later arrest sense. JMdict 2011120 independently
gives seize/capture/arrest for 取り押さえる; 取り抑える is rare and the
other two shortened spellings are search-only. The conclusion does not depend
on allocating every meaning of a compound to an individual character.

The complete records are parsed and reserialized extracts, not byte-identical
raw dictionary slices. `receipt.json` binds the pinned compressed inputs and
the exact extracted records. This is a source-backed direct-pair disposition,
