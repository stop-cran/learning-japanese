## Pair dispositions

Each decision is direct, not a transitive closure. The selected title and additional source senses were compared against actual baseline card bodies as well as titles, in both prompt directions. In particular:

- 利 and 得: benefit, profit, advantage and gain overlap the JMdict 徳 とく financial sense. 恩: its card gives benevolence and blessing, overlapping KANJIDIC2 and Daijisen. 享 and 福: the 徳 character source includes blessings or benefits; their cards have receiving benefits and good fortune. 富: the noun source includes wealth, with literary examples, so this is a qualified precaution, not an assertion that the sense is current in every context.
- 義 and 正: right conduct and righteousness overlap moral virtue. 良 and 優: goodness and kindness overlap goodness and benevolence. 功: JMdict 1275080 gives 功徳 religious merit and a virtuous deed, while the 功 card treats merit; this shared compound does not make the individual characters interchangeable.
- 尊 and 敬: KANJIDIC2 includes commanding respect and the character source includes a virtuous person held in regard; the comparison is limited to esteem. 人: Daijisen includes an outstanding virtuous person; the very broad label person is unsuitable as a wrong option. 教: the separate dictionary's source-only おしえ is instruction; this is a qualified precaution and does not add a kun reading to KANJIDIC2.
- 礼 was considered and rejected as an exclusion: manners and gratitude are associated with virtue but its displayed label “bow; thanks” is not established as a direct synonym of 徳. 悪 is an antonym in 悪徳, not a meaning of 徳. These dispositions are not propagated through other excluded cards.
- 休 “rest”, 本 “book, origin”, 木 “tree, wood” are deliberately loose contrasts rather than close shapes. Neither the target's cited virtue, profit, person, blessing or teaching senses nor its cited compound readings establishes these three labels as 徳 meanings; the baseline 休, 本 and 木 articles do not establish virtue or benevolence as meanings of their respective characters. Likewise, the literal tree component in 休 and the root pictured in 本 do not make those labels synonyms. This check is bounded by the preserved source records and card bodies, not an exhaustive semantic guarantee.

The reading-model wrapper includes the dictionary-only おしえ and compound sound とっ, and separately checks the captured source's additional name readings without reclassifying them as ordinary readings. The model's host pools and single-holder checks do not prove safety for arbitrary custom pools, multiple borrowed cards, native rendering or installed devices.
