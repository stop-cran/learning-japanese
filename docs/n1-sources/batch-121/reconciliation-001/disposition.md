# B121 neutral source dispositions

The comparison snapshot is the complete card at parent `b5302617bf4c7232bab5bcd1e76b8ae6b938bc3a`; each is preserved under `comparison-cards/`. All listed decisions are direct 徳 pairs, not inherited from a third card. The displayed 徳 label is **virtue; benevolence**. For every row, the first direction considers the other card's displayed label as a wrong answer to 徳; the reverse considers that 徳 label as a wrong answer to the other card. A guard prevents both directions; it is a conservative quiz decision, not a claim that every sense is interchangeable.

The original `../source-receipt.json` binds the 徳 sources. The additive `source-bindings.json` binds these snapshots and the complete parsed-and-reserialized records in this directory to the same compressed sources. `jmdict-records.xml` contains all entries matching the explicitly listed exact spellings, not all entries containing each comparison character; `kanjidic2-records.xml` contains the complete selected character records. `starter-*` and `roads-*` contain additional complete records. References below are JMdict entry IDs unless labelled KD (the complete KANJIDIC2 record) or Daijisen (the captured Weblio source in the original receipt). No frequency conclusion is drawn from absent priority flags.

Daijisen 徳 lists excellent character/person, 恩恵・加護, 富・財産 and 生まれつきの能力・性質. Its old quotations attest uses but prove neither exclusive obsolescence nor current frequency. KD 徳 gives benevolence, virtue, goodness and commanding respect; JMdict 2080970 とく gives virtue, benevolence and profit/benefit/advantage, the latter cross-referencing 得. The older character dictionary lists おしえ as a reading; neither that reading nor 教え alone proves an independently established ordinary 徳 teaching sense. All these senses remain separately attributed.

- 功: KD merit/achievement and standalone 2411590 功 こう “merit, success, meritorious deed” provide independent 功 meanings. 2411570 いさお is dated, and 1560230's sympathy sense is restricted to 労り, not 功; 1275080 功徳's merit is specifically Buddhist or Christian, not proof both kanji independently mean every compound sense. - 富: KD wealth and 1496730 富 とみ riches/wealth/fortune independently match Daijisen 徳 富・財産, with the same unknown frequency qualification. 1496740 富む is wealthy or abundant; no claim that virtue itself is abundance.
- 享: KD enjoy/receive and 1329590's receive/be given sense (especially 享ける/受ける) support a limited comparison with 徳 benefit or grace; 1233280 享受 is a whole-word enjoyment/reception, not an independent allocation of every compound meaning.

## Visual choices and reading behavior

The options are 後, 側 and 達. 後 shares the exact left 彳 with 徳 but has 幺/夂 rather than the crowded 十/罒/心 right side. 側 is a similar vertically divided, dense shape: its left 亻 is not 彳 and its 則/貝 bars and right 刂 differ from 徳's stacked right half. 達 is a twelve-stroke movement-form contrast: 辶 runs around the left/bottom rather than 彳, and its vertically stacked horizontal strokes end differently from 徳's 罒/心. These are practical form contrasts, not claims of a common etymology or validated perceptual-confusability experiment; 側 and 達 are less exact than an 彳 match. They are stronger structural contrasts than bare 木/本 and the simple 休, while remaining in the Starter pool.

Full cards and `starter-*`/`roads-*` supply both-direction screening: 後's behind/after/remainder/descendant senses (1269320, 1269330, 1269410) are not virtue/benevolence or the 徳 source's blessing, wealth or innate nature; neither 徳 source establishes behind/after. 側's side, vicinity, position and leaning (1403830, 1581310, KD) do not mean virtue/benevolence, and 徳 does not mean side. 達's reach/arrival, suffix for people and progress (1416220, 1416230, 1353850, KD) are not moral virtue or benevolence, and 徳 does not mean reaching or a plural suffix. KD 達 “accomplished” and progress in skill are not the same as Daijisen 徳's innate ability; their conceptual proximity is acknowledged, not dismissed because a third card is guarded. These are bounded source-based non-collision judgments, not proof of every possible usage.
