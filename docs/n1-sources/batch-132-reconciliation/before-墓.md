# 墓 — grave; tomb

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 土 (no. 32) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[墓](../words/墓.md) はか is a grave or tomb: a burial place or
associated structure. 日本国語大辞典 also includes the grave marker
or gravestone among the things called はか.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find the 莫-shaped upper
part above [土](土.md), earth, and imagine a marker on the ground.
This is an invented scene for the present shape, not an account of
how the character originated.

## Notes

- [暮](暮.md) shares much of the upper shape but concerns dusk or
  livelihood; [募](募.md) concerns recruitment or solicitation.
  Their readings and meanings must be learned in their own words.
- [川](川.md) means a river or stream, not a burial place.
- [埋](埋.md), bury or embed, receives a direct precaution for its
  burial sense. A grave is a place or structure, not every act of
  burying or filling.
- [石](石.md), stone, receives a precaution because 墓 can also
  refer to a grave marker or gravestone. Not every stone is a grave.
- 浅墓 is a spelling of 浅はか あさはか, "thoughtless" or
  "shallow"; JMdict marks this spelling as ateji. That whole-word
  meaning is not an ordinary independent meaning of 墓.
