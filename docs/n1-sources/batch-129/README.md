絹 includes fibre and silk fabric, plus Nikkoku's broader cloth sense.

use. These are qualified character-source uses, not erased or promoted to

entries establish **silk wadding under native わた** despite the displayed
cotton label; this positive reverse overlap is directly guarded.
衣 きぬ is clothing, not automatically silk.

虹 includes the ordinary rainbow family and the rare spelling 霓.
The separate 二次 family has search-only 虹 and is not the weather noun.
Nikkoku のじ/ぬじ and Jitsu ヲニジ remain qualified lookup records.
Jitsu additionally records rainbow-coloured flag, bridge, oil dish and
disorder/dispute/せめる uses. Missing image glyphs in the captured text
are unresolved, never reconstructed. The old serpent/dragon conception is
cultural/historical evidence, not universal modern lexical synonymy.
The compound 虹の橋を渡る does not make standalone 虹 mean death.
