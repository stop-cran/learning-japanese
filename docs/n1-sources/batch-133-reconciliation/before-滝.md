# 滝 — waterfall; rapids

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

[滝](../words/滝.md) たき is a waterfall. The complete word family
also records rapids, marked archaic in JMdict; the monolingual sources
likewise distinguish older fast-current use from the usual modern
waterfall focus. 日本国語大辞典 additionally records an abbreviation
of 滝水, water from a waterfall.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the three water
strokes 氵 beside a 竜-shaped dragon watching falling water.
This invented scene is not a historical derivation. 瀧, an older
spelling, has 龍 on the right and 19 strokes; this card's modern
滝 and its stroke animation have 13.

字通 also gives リョウ and ソウ（サウ）, with はやせ and ひたす
among its kun glosses. Weblio records らき outside the standard list;
KANJIDIC2 places らき among name readings. 日本国語大辞典 discusses
older たるみ and a possible voiced たぎ; that discussion does not
establish unrestricted modern alternatives. None is added to practice.

## Common words

- [滝](../words/滝.md) たき - waterfall.
- 滝川 たきがわ - rapids.
- 滝口 たきぐち - top of a waterfall.

## Notes

- 字通's character senses also include rain sound, rapids, and
  soaking/moistening (ひたす・うるおす). Preserve this source layer
  separately from ordinary modern たき; absence from the practice
  list is not evidence that the source senses are invalid.
- [川](川.md), [河](河.md), [流](流.md), [水](水.md),
  [急](急.md) and [注](注.md) receive direct precautions for
  broad water/current/rapid/pouring labels. [湿](湿.md) receives
  one for the source moistening sense.
- [雨](雨.md), [音](音.md), [響](響.md), [騒](騒.md) and
  [鳴](鳴.md) receive precautions for the supplemental rain/sound
  range; [落](落.md) and [降](降.md) for broad falling labels.
  These protect against unresolved labels, not universal synonymy:
  not every sound, rain shower, descent or wet thing is a waterfall.
- [古](古.md), old, [右](右.md), right, and [寺](寺.md),
  Buddhist temple, do not name the water feature or the supplemental
  rain/sound/moistening senses.
- 白滝 しらたき can mean shirataki noodles or a white-sheet-like
  waterfall. The noodle sense belongs to that word, not bare 滝.
