# 刃 — blade; cutting edge

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

刃 concerns a blade or cutting edge. The standalone word [刃](../words/刃.md) は names the cutting part of a knife or sword, not automatically the whole tool. The character also appears in words concerning swords.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Compare [刀](刀.md), "sword; blade," and notice the extra short inner mark, shaped like 丶. Let that mark remind you of the cutting edge. This is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジン | on | 白刃 はくじん — drawn sword; the same word is also read しらは |
| ニン | on | 刃傷 にんじょう — injury or bloodshed involving a blade; recognize the whole word |
| は | kun | [刃](../words/刃.md) は — blade; cutting edge |
| やいば | kun | 刃 やいば — blade; sword; a separate documented word reading |
| き.る | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this reading; no independently verified word example is established here |

This small lesson practices **は**. ジン, ニン and やいば remain recognition material here; leaving them out of practice does not make them invalid or uniformly rare. The source-only き.る is not a recommendation to replace the spelling of [切る](../words/切る.md) きる, "cut," with an invented spelling.

## Common words

- [刃](../words/刃.md) は — blade; cutting edge
- 刃物 はもの — edged tool; cutting instrument

## Notes

- 刀 かたな names a sword; 刃 は can name its cutting part. Learn the whole word and context rather than treating the two characters as freely interchangeable.
- [乃](乃.md), with connecting or particle-related uses, [凹](凹.md), "concave; sunken," and [凸](凸.md), "convex; protruding," do not share the blade/cutting-edge meaning.
- The same spelling 刃 also has the reading やいば. Its dictionary senses are not all automatically senses of the noun は.

# 刃 (は) — blade; cutting edge

## Meaning

刃 is a noun naming the blade or cutting edge of a knife, sword or similar tool. This article teaches the reading **は** and the physical cutting-part sense.

- この包丁の刃は鋭いです。 (このほうちょうのははするどいです。) — This kitchen knife's blade is sharp.
- 写真では、刃がよく見えます。 (しゃしんでは、はがよくみえます。) — The blade is clearly visible in the photograph.

In 包丁の刃 (ほうちょうのは), "the blade of a kitchen knife," の connects the tool with its cutting part. In 刃が見える (はがみえる), "the blade is visible," が marks what is visible.

## How the kanji combine

[刃](../kanji/刃.md) represents the whole native noun は here. There is no compound to split. The same written character also has the separate word reading やいば, "blade; sword"; that does not make every sense under やいば a sense of は.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[ナイフ](ナイフ.md), "knife," and [包丁](包丁.md) ほうちょう, "kitchen knife," name whole tools. 刃 は focuses on the cutting part. Likewise, [刀](刀.md) かたな and [日本刀](日本刀.md) にほんとう name swords rather than just their cutting edges.

[端](端.md) はし, "edge; end; tip," and [縁](縁.md) ふち, "rim; edge," describe boundaries more broadly. Not every edge is a cutting edge. There is no single lexical antonym for 刃.

## Distinctive meaning

English "edge" can mean the edge of a desk or the boundary of an area. 刃 は does not generally replace 端 or 縁 for those spatial meanings. Learn it with a cutting tool and keep the distinction between the tool and its blade.

For figurative English "cutting edge" meaning the forefront of a field, use words such as [先端](先端.md) せんたん, not 刃 は.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1369820, sense 1; separate やいば family 1369830. Physical は sense: [Digital Daijisen and Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, 刃](https://kotobank.jp/word/刃-536578).
