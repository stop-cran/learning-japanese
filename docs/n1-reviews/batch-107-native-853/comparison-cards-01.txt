21:

24: [凡例](../words/凡例.md) はんれい, a book's explanatory conventions or a map's

16: # 凶 — ill fortune; evil
17:
18: **Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 凵 (no. 17) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
19:
20: ## Meaning and origin
21:
22: 凶 concerns ill fortune, an unfavorable outcome, or evil. In
23: [凶作](../words/凶作.md) きょうさく, it describes a poor harvest; in
24: 凶悪 (きょうあく), it concerns heinousness rather than agricultural failure.
25: Learn the whole compound rather than translating every 凶 as "bad luck."
26:
27: **Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the crossing strokes 乂
28: inside the open-topped frame 凵 as a warning mark for an unfavorable outcome.
29: This is a memory aid for the current shape, not a historical derivation or
30: a claim about a phonetic component.

45: - 凶器 きょうき — dangerous or lethal weapon

47: ## Notes
48:
49: - 凶 (きょう) can itself mean bad luck. In fortune-telling, 大凶
50:   (だいきょう) means very bad luck; 凶兆 (きょうちょう) is an ill omen.
51: - [悪](悪.md) also concerns badness or evil, but 凶 is not a replacement
52:   for the ordinary adjective 悪い (わるい).
53: - [凡](凡.md) concerns being ordinary; [凸](凸.md) and [凹](凹.md)
54:   concern raised and sunken shapes. Similar outlines do not make their
55:   meanings interchangeable with 凶.
