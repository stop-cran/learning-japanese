51: - Compare the right-side 刂 in [前](前.md), [判](判.md) and [刑](刑.md). Their meanings are not shaving or sharpening; a shared radical does not make the words interchangeable.

45:
46: - This card asks only ツイ. The source-only スイ and う.つ, and the rare 椎 spelling of つち, remain lookup information rather than reading questions. The noun つち itself is not labelled rare merely because this spelling is.
47: - [骨](骨.md) is the broader bone/skeleton character. A vertebra is a bone, so its broad title would not be an unambiguous wrong answer for 椎.
48: - [集](集.md), gather, puts that bird-shaped component above 木 rather than beside it. [難](難.md), difficult, has a denser left block beside the same component. [稲](稲.md) names the rice plant, not a chinquapin, and has 禾 rather than 木 on the left.
49: - 椎 (しい) is a tree name, not the anatomical pronunciation. Do not read 椎骨 as しいこつ or treat 椎茸 as an anatomical term.
