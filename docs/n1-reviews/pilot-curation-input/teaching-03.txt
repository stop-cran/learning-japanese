L16:# 漱 — rinse the mouth; gargle
L17:
L18:**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
L19:
L20:## Meaning and origin
L21:
L22:漱 concerns **rinsing the mouth or gargling**. The everyday action is usefully learned as [うがい](../words/うがい.md); 漱 is a rare kanji spelling of that noun, not its required written form.
L23:
L24:**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Connect the water-shaped 氵 on the left with water moving around the mouth. The dense right side is a shape to recognize, not a literal diagram of gargling.
L25:

L27:
L28:| Reading | Type | Typical words |
L29:| --- | --- | --- |
L30:| ソウ | on | 漱石枕流 そうせきちんりゅう - a four-character expression for stubbornly refusing to admit being wrong |
L31:| シュウ | on, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this reading; no word example is established here |
L32:| ス | on, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this reading; no word example is established here |
L33:| くちすす.ぐ | kun | 漱ぐ くちすすぐ - rinse the mouth; gargle; Kanjipedia explicitly gives this written boundary |
L34:| くちそそ.ぐ | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this form; a separate verified lexical example with this pronunciation has not been established here |
L35:| うがい | kun | 漱 うがい - gargling; rinsing the mouth, a rare spelling of [うがい](../words/うがい.md) |
L36:| すす.ぐ | kun | 漱ぐ すすぐ - rinse the mouth; usually written in kana in this dictionary family |
L37:
L38:In 漱ぐ **くちすすぐ**, the one character covers **くちすす** and only ぐ is written outside it. Do not silently rewrite that spelling as 口漱ぐ. The separate reading **すすぐ** has a different-sized spoken stem despite the same written form.
L39:
L40:## Common words
L41:
L42:- [うがい](../words/うがい.md) うがい - gargling; rinsing the mouth; the useful kana spelling
L43:
L44:The four-character expression above is reading support, not everyday hygiene vocabulary.
L45:
L46:## Notes
L47:
L48:- 漱ぐ すすぐ is specifically mouth-rinsing in the selected dictionary family. Do not transfer every laundry or washing sense of the separate 濯ぐ family to it.
L49:- Rinsing the mouth is not swallowing a drink. Compare [飲](飲.md), drink, and [歌](歌.md), song or sing; neither is a suitable translation of 漱.
L50:- [速](速.md), fast, has 辶 surrounding 束 rather than 漱's left-hand 氵 and additional right-hand strokes.
L51:- The linked うがい article preserves its kana headword and primary reading. No medical benefit is implied by these language examples.

===== words/幹.md =====

L3:reading: みき
L4:title: tree trunk; backbone
L5:type: wago
L6:kanji: [幹]
L7:tags: [wago, noun, trees]
L8:quiz_exclusions: [基, 基本, 基礎, 基盤]
L9:---
L10:
L11:# 幹 (みき) - tree trunk; backbone
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:幹 is the trunk of a tree, as distinct from its branches. It can also mean a backbone
L16:or foundation figuratively. It is a noun.
L17:
L18:- この木は幹が太いです。 (このきはみきがふといです。) - This tree has a thick trunk.
L19:- 木の幹に触れました。 (きのみきにふれました。) - I touched the tree's trunk.
L20:
L21:## How the kanji combine
L22:
L23:[幹](../kanji/幹.md) is a single character read みき in this native noun.
L24:Its on reading カン occurs in compounds such as [新幹線](新幹線.md) (しんかんせん).
L25:Those compounds do not change the reading of the standalone noun to かん.
L26:
L27:## Synonyms and antonyms
L28:
L29:[枝](枝.md) (えだ) is a branch; [木](木.md) (き) names a tree or wood more generally.
L30:For a figurative foundation, [基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) and [基盤](基盤.md) (きばん)
L31:overlap in meaning, but do not ordinarily name a tree's trunk.
L32:
L33:## Distinctive meaning
L34:
L35:English "trunk" can also mean luggage or an elephant's trunk. 幹 does not name those things.
L36:The dictionary separately records 幹/柄 read から, "trunk; stem; stalk."
L37:That separate word is not a reason to pronounce every 幹 either way.
L38:
L39:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
L40:entry 1577640, senses 1-2; separate から family 2396230.

===== words/凹凸.md =====

L2:word: 凹凸
L3:reading: おうとつ
L4:title: unevenness; disparity
L5:type: kango
L6:kanji: [凹, 凸]
L7:tags: [kango, noun, surfaces, differences]
L8:quiz_exclusions: [でこぼこ, ごつごつ, ざらざら]
L9:---
L10:
L11:# 凹凸 (おうとつ) - unevenness; disparity
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:凹凸 names unevenness: raised and sunken parts of a surface. It also names an
L16:imbalance or disparity. It is a noun, not an ordinary な-adjective.
L17:
L18:- この壁には凹凸があります。 (このかべにはおうとつがあります。) - This wall has an uneven surface.
L19:- 表面の凹凸を調べました。 (ひょうめんのおうとつをしらべました。) - We examined the surface's unevenness.
L20:
L21:凹凸のある表面 (おうとつのあるひょうめん) means "a surface with bumps and hollows."
L22:
L23:## How the kanji combine
L24:
L25:[凹](../kanji/凹.md) contributes the sunken part and [凸](../kanji/凸.md) the raised part.
L26:The contrasting parts together name the uneven whole. The on readings are オウ + トツ.
L27:
L28:## Synonyms and antonyms
L29:
L30:[でこぼこ](でこぼこ.md), also written 凸凹, overlaps in physical unevenness.
L31:Notice the reversed character order and learn each whole word with its reading.
L32:[ごつごつ](ごつごつ.md) emphasizes hard, angular irregularities;
L33:[ざらざら](ざらざら.md) can describe finer-grained roughness.
L34:平ら (たいら), "flat; level," provides a useful contrast for a surface.
L35:
L36:## Distinctive meaning
L37:
L38:Unevenness need not feel gritty: a smooth, wavy surface can still have raised and sunken parts.
L39:The abstract disparity sense is not literal roughness under the fingers.
L40:Do not turn every でこぼこ expression into 凹凸 merely by reversing the characters.
L41:
L42:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
L43:entry 1179280, senses 1-2; comparison 1582410.

L5:type: wago
L6:kanji: [矛]
L7:tags: [wago, noun, historical-equipment]
L8:quiz_exclusions: [武器]
L9:---
L10:
L11:# 矛 (ほこ) - spear; polearm
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:矛 names a spear or polearm: a weapon with a long shaft.
L16:The dictionary also records broader "weapons; arms" and a context-specific
L17:abbreviation for a parade float decorated with spears.
L18:
L19:- 博物館で矛を見ました。 (はくぶつかんでほこをみました。) - I saw a spear at the museum.
L20:- この矛は長いです。 (このほこはながいです。) - This spear is long.
L21:
L22:## How the kanji combine
L23:
L24:[矛](../kanji/矛.md) is a single character read ほこ in this native noun.
L25:In [矛盾](矛盾.md) (むじゅん), "contradiction," the same character instead has the on reading ム.
L26:Do not replace ほこ with む when reading the standalone noun.
L27:
L28:## Synonyms and antonyms
L29:
L30:[武器](武器.md) (ぶき) is a weapon more generally.
L31:盾 (たて), "shield," provides a contrast in the conventional spear-and-shield story
L32:explained in [矛盾](矛盾.md); it is not the word for the spear.
L33:
L34:## Distinctive meaning
L35:
L36:The full dictionary family includes 鉾, 戈 and other spellings; sharing a family
L37:does not make their historical shapes or every contextual use identical.
L38:Its alternative とかり is restricted to 鋒, not 矛.
L39:In a festival context, the abbreviated float sense does not mean every float is a spear.
L40:The noun is not an instruction for using a weapon.
L41:
L42:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
L43:entry 1603450; teaching focuses on the polearm sense, with qualified recognition of senses 2 and 4.
