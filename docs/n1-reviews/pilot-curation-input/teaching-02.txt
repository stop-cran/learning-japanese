L16:# 凹 — concave; sunken
L17:
L18:**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 凵 (no. 17) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
L19:
L20:## Meaning and origin
L21:
L22:凹 describes a hollow or a surface that sinks inward: **concave**. Contrast [凸](凸.md), convex or projecting outward.
L23:
L24:**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The dip in the top of 凹 looks like a dent. Use that visible hollow to remember the inward direction.
L25:
L26:## Readings
L27:
L28:| Reading | Type | Typical words |
L29:| --- | --- | --- |
L30:| オウ | on | 凹レンズ おうレンズ - concave lens; [凹凸](../words/凹凸.md) おうとつ - unevenness; disparity |
L31:| くぼ.む | kun | 凹む くぼむ, also written 窪む - cave in; become hollow or sunken |
L32:| へこ.む | kun | [凹む](../words/凹む.md) へこむ - become dented; colloquially feel down |
L33:| ぼこ | kun, bound use | [凸凹](../words/でこぼこ.md) でこぼこ - unevenness; raised and sunken parts together |
L34:
L35:The linked 凹む article teaches **へこむ** as its primary reading. The same written form has a separate **くぼむ** dictionary family; do not transfer every emotional or defeat sense of へこむ to くぼむ.
L36:
L37:## Common words
L38:
L39:- [凹む](../words/凹む.md) へこむ - become dented; feel down
L40:- [凸凹](../words/でこぼこ.md) でこぼこ - uneven and bumpy
L41:- [凹凸](../words/凹凸.md) おうとつ - unevenness; disparity
L42:- 凹レンズ おうレンズ - concave lens
L43:
L44:## Notes
L45:
L46:- 凸凹 でこぼこ and 凹凸 おうとつ reverse the character order. They are related words, not freely interchangeable readings of one spelling.
L47:- In 凸凹 でこぼこ, 凹 supplies the sunken-part **ぼこ**. The separately indexed 穴ぼこ あなぼこ, "hole; pothole," has search-only spelling 穴凹 in JMdict; that index spelling is not recommended here as an ordinary written example.
L48:- Contrast [回](回.md), revolve or times, and [同](同.md), same. Their enclosures lack 凹's open-topped dip.

L20:## Meaning and origin
L21:
L22:凸 describes a surface projecting outward: **convex**. Its native reading でこ also occurs for a forehead or bump. Contrast [凹](凹.md), which points inward or downward.
L23:
L24:**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The raised middle of 凸 looks like a bump rising above the surrounding surface.

L33:## Common words
L34:
L35:- [凸凹](../words/でこぼこ.md) でこぼこ - uneven and bumpy
L36:- 凸レンズ とつレンズ - convex lens
L37:- [凹凸](../words/凹凸.md) おうとつ - unevenness; disparity
L38:
L39:## Notes
L40:
L41:- 凸凹 でこぼこ combines raised and sunken parts. Its dictionary family also records とつおう for physical unevenness, but restricts the imbalance/difference sense to **でこぼこ**. The linked kana-headed article retains its primary でこぼこ identity.
L42:- 凹凸 おうとつ has the reverse written order. Learn each whole spelling with its reading rather than reversing sounds by analogy.
L43:- [回](回.md), revolve or times, and [同](同.md), same, are visual comparisons, not words for a projecting surface.

L16:# 矛 — spear; polearm
L17:
L18:**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 矛 (no. 110) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
L19:
L20:## Meaning and origin
L21:
L22:矛 names a **spear or polearm**, a weapon with a long shaft. It is familiar in [矛盾](../words/矛盾.md) むじゅん, "contradiction; inconsistency," whose conventional explanatory story contrasts a spear and a shield.
L23:
L24:**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the long central stroke as a shaft and the angled strokes around it as the weapon's head and fittings. This is a drawing aid, not a reconstruction of an ancient weapon.
L25:

L27:
L28:| Reading | Type | Typical words |
L29:| --- | --- | --- |
L30:| ム | on | [矛盾](../words/矛盾.md) むじゅん - contradiction; inconsistency |
L31:| ボウ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2; no independently established word example is supplied here |
L32:| ほこ | kun | [矛](../words/矛.md) ほこ - spear; polearm; also weapons collectively in the applicable dictionary sense |
L33:
L34:## Common words
L35:
L36:- [矛盾](../words/矛盾.md) むじゅん - contradiction; inconsistency
L37:- [矛](../words/矛.md) ほこ - spear; polearm
L38:
L39:## Notes
L40:
L41:- The ほこ dictionary family also records a festival-float sense, short for a float decorated with spears. That context-specific use does not mean that every float is a weapon.
L42:- [予](予.md), beforehand, lacks 矛's lower-left diagonal. [柔](柔.md), soft or supple, places a related-looking upper shape above 木; [務](務.md), duties, has additional components to the right.
L43:- A shield, 盾 たて, is a useful semantic contrast, not the meaning of 矛. The linked 矛盾 article explains the attributed story without treating it as an actual weapon test.

L17:
L18:**Strokes:** 24 · **Key (radical):** 鳥 (no. 196) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
L19:
L20:## Meaning and origin
L21:
L22:鷹 is the **hawk/falcon** character. The native word [鷹](../words/鷹.md) たか names a bird of prey; animal names are also written in kana, including タカ.
L23:
L24:**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 鳥 at the bottom, under the many strokes of the upper part. Picture a hawk sheltering under a broad roof. The roof image is only a memory aid.
L25:
L26:## Readings
L27:
L28:| Reading | Type | Typical words |
L29:| --- | --- | --- |
L30:| ヨウ | on | 放鷹 ほうよう - falconry; hawking |
L31:| オウ | on | [鷹揚](../words/鷹揚.md) おうよう - magnanimous; composed |
L32:| たか | kun | [鷹](../words/鷹.md) たか - hawk; falcon |
L33:
L34:鷹揚 has other recorded whole-word readings, including ようよう and おおよう. Learn the specific word and reading rather than replacing たか with オウ or ヨウ in any expression.
L35:
L36:## Common words
L37:
L38:- [鷹](../words/鷹.md) たか - hawk; falcon
L39:- [鷹揚](../words/鷹揚.md) おうよう - magnanimous; composed
L40:
L41:## Notes
L42:
L43:- This card has 24 strokes. Locate the upper enclosing part and the lower 鳥 before practicing the full stroke sequence.
L44:- [鳴](鳴.md), an animal call or ringing sound, also contains 鳥 but adds 口 on the left. [島](島.md), island, ends in 山; [雲](雲.md), cloud, has 雨 on top. None means a hawk.
L45:- The source's grade-9/name-use classification is not a claim that 鷹 occurs only in personal names. The ordinary bird word is independently documented.

L16:# 彗 — broom; comet
L17:
L18:**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 彐 (no. 58) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
L19:
L20:## Meaning and origin
L21:
L22:彗 has **broom** and **comet** senses. For useful modern vocabulary, start with [彗星](../words/彗星.md) すいせい, "comet." The character's broom reading is recognition material, not a reason to replace ordinary ほうき with 彗 in all writing.
L23:
L24:**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the paired upper shapes as broom bristles and the lower part as the grip. A comet's tail can remind you of those bristles. This memory aid is not an etymological claim.
L25:

L35:
L36:## Common words

L39:
L40:## Notes
L41:
L42:- Keep 彗星 separate from a meteor or "shooting star": they name different astronomical phenomena.
L43:- [雪](雪.md), snow, has 雨 across the top. [帰](帰.md), return, has additional left-hand strokes; [習](習.md), learn or practice, ends in 白.
L44:- The indexing radical is 彐, no. 58; locate it at the bottom of the character rather than mistaking the paired upper shapes for two separate characters.
L45:- A font may draw the small lower strokes differently. Use the generated stroke guide for practice; typeface differences alone do not establish different characters.
