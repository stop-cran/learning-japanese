L11:
L12:# 落ち着く (おちつく) - calm down; settle
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:落ち着く is an intransitive godan verb meaning to calm down or settle. It can describe regaining composure, a situation becoming stable, or settling into a place.
L17:
L18:- 少し休んだら、気持ちが落ち着きました。 (すこしやすんだら、きもちがおちつきました。) - After resting a little, I felt calmer.
L19:- 引っ越しが終わり、やっと新しい家に落ち着きました。 (ひっこしがおわり、やっとあたらしいいえにおちつきました。) - The move is over, and I have finally settled into the new house.
L20:
L21:The forms include 落ち着きます (おちつきます), 落ち着いて (おちついて), and 落ち着かない (おちつかない).
L22:
L23:It can also mean that an arrangement is settled: 結局、この案に落ち着きました (けっきょく、このあんにおちつきました), "In the end, we settled on this proposal."
L24:
L25:## How the kanji combine
L26:
L27:[落](../kanji/落.md) writes お in 落ち, and [着](../kanji/着.md) writes つ in 着く. The native combination suggests coming to rest in a settled state, not necessarily physically falling. 落着く and 落ちつく are other spellings of おちつく.
L28:
L29:## Synonyms and antonyms
L30:
L31:[あわてる](あわてる.md), "become flustered," is a useful contrast for composure. [安定](安定.md)する (あんていする) overlaps in becoming stable, and [決まる](決まる.md) (きまる) in an arrangement becoming settled.
L32:
L33:[静か](静か.md) (しずか) describes quietness; [安心](安心.md) (あんしん) emphasizes relief or freedom from worry. Regaining composure does not always mean becoming silent or resolving every worry.
L34:
L35:## Distinctive meaning
L36:
L37:落ち着いた before a noun can describe a calm person or subdued colors: 落ち着いた色 (おちついたいろ), "a subdued color." The dictionary also recognizes fitting or harmonizing with something, overlapping with [似合う](似合う.md) (にあう), but this is not the only meaning of the verb.
L38:
L39:Do not replace every English "settle" with 落ち着く. Settling a dispute actively can be [治める](治める.md) or [収める](収める.md), both read おさめる; paying an amount due can be [納める](納める.md) (おさめる). The complete construction determines the intended sense.
L40:
L41:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1589220, senses 1-6.

L11:
L12:# 平気 (へいき) - untroubled; all right
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:平気 describes being untroubled, unconcerned or composed. In conversation it can
L17:also mean all right, fine or able to cope. Use 平気だ/です, 平気な + noun,
L18:and 平気で + action.
L19:
L20:- 怖い話を聞いても、彼は平気な顔をしていました。 (こわいはなしをきいても、かれはへいきなかおをしていました。) - Even after hearing the scary story, he looked untroubled.
L21:- 「痛くない？」「うん、平気。」 (「いたくない？」「うん、へいき。」) - "Doesn't it hurt?" "No, I'm all right."
L22:
L23:## How the kanji combine
L24:
L25:[平](../kanji/平.md) contributes levelness or calm; [気](../kanji/気.md)
L26:contributes a person's spirit or state of mind. The compound evokes an undisturbed
L27:state: ヘイ + キ, not a literal measurement of flatness.
L28:
L29:## Synonyms and antonyms
L30:
L31:[冷静](冷静.md) (れいせい) and [落ち着く](落ち着く.md) (おちつく) overlap in
L32:composure. [大丈夫](大丈夫.md) (だいじょうぶ) overlaps in reassuring someone
L33:that things are all right. [安心](安心.md) (あんしん) concerns freedom from worry;
L34:平気 can also imply simply not caring about something.
L35:
L36:## Distinctive meaning
L37:
L38:平気でうそをつく (へいきでうそをつく) means lie without compunction or concern,
L39:not necessarily praise for remaining calm. Context decides whether the lack of concern
L40:is admirable, reassuring or objectionable.
L41:Being 平気 about an injury reports the person's reaction; it is not a medical
L42:guarantee that no injury exists.
L43:
L44:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1507180, senses 1-2.
