L11:
L12:# 冷静 (れいせい) - calm; composed
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:冷静 is a noun for composure and a na-adjective describing someone or something as calm
L17:and clear-headed. Use 冷静な before a noun, 冷静に before an action, and 冷静です as a predicate.
L18:
L19:- 問題を冷静に考えましょう。 (もんだいをれいせいにかんがえましょう。) - Let's think about the problem calmly.
L20:- 彼女は事故の後も冷静でした。 (かのじょはじこのあともれいせいでした。) - She remained composed even after the accident.
L21:
L22:冷静な判断 (れいせいなはんだん) means "a calm, clear-headed judgment."
L23:冷静になる means "become calm; regain composure."
L24:
L25:## How the kanji combine
L26:
L27:[冷](../kanji/冷.md) contributes coolness, and [静](../kanji/静.md) contributes quietness or stillness.
L28:The two qualities combine figuratively in mental composure.
L29:The on readings are レイ + セイ. The word does not describe low body temperature.
L30:
L31:## Synonyms and antonyms
L32:
L33:落ち着く (おちつく), "calm down; settle," overlaps in descriptions of composure but is a verb.
L34:[冷たい](冷たい.md) (つめたい) can describe physical coldness or an unfriendly manner;
L35:冷静 does not by itself mean either.
L36:Panic or agitation contrasts with composure, depending on the situation.
L37:
L38:## Distinctive meaning
L39:
L40:冷静 emphasizes maintaining perspective and judgment rather than being controlled by an immediate
L41:emotional reaction. A person can be concerned and compassionate while remaining 冷静.
L42:Conversely, the word alone does not guarantee that their judgment is correct.
L43:Keep the な and に forms distinct: 冷静な人 describes a person, while 冷静に話す describes speaking calmly.
L44:
L45:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1557050, sense 1.

L10:---
L11:
L12:# 悠々 (ゆうゆう) - leisurely; composed; easily
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:悠々 describes a leisurely, unhurried or composed manner.
L17:It also means doing something easily, with room to spare.
L18:悠々と is the practical adverbial pattern.
L19:
L20:- 退職後は田舎で悠々と暮らしています。 (たいしょくごはいなかでゆうゆうとくらしています。) - After retiring, I live leisurely in the countryside.
L21:- 今から出れば、電車に悠々と間に合います。 (いまからでれば、でんしゃにゆうゆうとまにあいます。) - If we leave now, we can easily catch the train.
L22:
L23:Before a noun, the literary pattern is 悠々たる:
L24:悠々たる態度 (ゆうゆうたるたいど), "a composed manner."
L25:Do not replace たる with an ordinary na-adjective's な.
L26:
L27:## How the kanji combine
L28:
L29:悠 is associated with remoteness and an unhurried
L30:extent or manner. 々 repeats the character, including its on reading
L31:ユウ: ゆうゆう. It is not a separately pronounced kanji.
L32:
L33:## Synonyms and antonyms
L34:
L35:[のんびり](のんびり.md) and [ゆっくり](ゆっくり.md) overlap in taking
L36:one's time. [冷静](冷静.md) (れいせい) emphasizes composure;
L37:[余裕](余裕.md) (よゆう) is leeway or room to spare.
L38:[容易](容易.md) (ようい) overlaps in ease, but has different grammar.
L39:慌てて (あわてて), "in a fluster," contrasts with a composed manner.
L40:
L41:## Distinctive meaning
L42:
L43:Leisureliness and easy achievement are different uses: catching the
L44:train 悠々と does not mean walking slowly enough to miss it.
L45:The dictionary also records distant, boundless or enduring extent;
L46:this is recognition-level literary scope, not a claim that 悠々
L47:replaces every use of "wide," "far" or "forever."
L48:悠悠 is another spelling. 悠々 and ゆうゆう are forms of one entry,
L49:not two N2 assignments.
L50:
L51:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1605700.
L52:Usage and attachment: [Digital Daijisen, 悠々](https://www.weblio.jp/content/悠々).

L11:
L12:# 穏やか (おだやか) - calm; gentle; peaceful
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:穏やか is a な-adjective for calm conditions or a gentle, unagitated manner. Use 穏やかな before a noun, 穏やかに before a verb, and 穏やかです as a predicate. It can describe the sea, weather, a person's character, or how someone speaks.
L17:
L18:- 今日は海が穏やかです。 (きょうはうみがおだやかです。) - The sea is calm today.
L19:- 彼女は穏やかに話した。 (かのじょはおだやかにはなした。) - She spoke calmly and gently.
L20:
L21:穏やかな話し合い (おだやかなはなしあい), "an amicable discussion," highlights the absence of an aggressive confrontation.
L22:
L23:## How the kanji combine
L24:
L25:穏 carries the idea of calmness or peacefulness. The whole native adjective is read おだやか; やか is part of its written ending. There is no second kanji to combine with it.
L26:
L27:## Synonyms and antonyms
L28:
L29:[静か](静か.md) (しずか) often concerns quietness or little activity. 穏やか can instead focus on gentleness even when someone is speaking. [優しい](優しい.md) (やさしい) overlaps in descriptions of people but can emphasize kindness. [激しい](激しい.md) (はげしい), "intense/violent," provides a useful contrast for waves or behavior.
L30:
L31:## Distinctive meaning
L32:
L33:Do not translate every "quiet person" as 穏やかな人. The phrase describes temperament, not simply how many words someone says. Likewise, 穏やかな天気 suggests mild, settled conditions rather than an absence of sound.
L34:
L35:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1183590, senses 1, 2.

L10:
L11:# しっとり — pleasantly moist; softly composed
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:Lightly moist or damp, often attractively so. It also describes a calm, soft, gracefully restrained atmosphere or manner.
L16:
L17:- このケーキは、次の日もしっとりしている。 — This cake is still moist the next day.
L18:- 雨上がりの土はしっとりしていた。 — The soil was moist after the rain.
L19:- 彼女は静かな曲をしっとりと歌った。 — She sang the quiet song with gentle composure.
L20:
L21:## Synonyms and antonyms
L22:
L23:[ぱさぱさ](ぱさぱさ.md) is dried-out; [びしょびしょ](びしょびしょ.md) is thoroughly wet. [じめじめ](じめじめ.md) usually emphasizes unwelcome dampness. しっとり does not imply that all moisture is pleasant.
L24:
L25:## Distinctive meaning
L26:
L27:しっとりした肌（はだ）, しっとりする, and しっとりと歌う are useful. A cake's moisture and a singer's restrained mood are separate contextual senses. Do not automatically translate it as “wet,” or use it as a general praise word meaning stylish in every setting.
L28:
L29:See [Japanese onomatopoeia](../articles/japanese-onomatopoeia.md) and [texture and warmth](../articles/onomatopoeia-texture-and-warmth.md).
L30:
L31:Source: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 2121280, senses 1–2.

L5:jlpt: 3
L6:type: kango
L7:kanji: [余, 裕]
L8:tags: [kango, noun, capacity, feelings]
L9:---
L10:
L11:# 余裕 (よゆう) - leeway; room to spare; composure
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:余裕 is spare capacity or leeway in time, money, space, or effort. It can also mean mental composure. Use 余裕がある, "have room to spare," and 余裕がない, "have no leeway."
L16:
L17:- 出発までまだ時間に余裕があります。 (しゅっぱつまでまだじかんによゆうがあります。) - There is still plenty of time before departure.
L18:- 忙しくて、気持ちに余裕がありません。 (いそがしくて、きもちによゆうがありません。) - I am so busy that I have no mental breathing room.
L19:
L20:Verb dictionary form + 余裕 gives the capacity to do something: 休む余裕がない (やすむよゆうがない), "have no room to take a break."
L21:
L22:## How the kanji combine
L23:
L24:[余](../kanji/余.md) contributes what remains or is in excess. 裕 contributes plenty or roominess. Together they express having more capacity than the immediate demand consumes. The on readings are ヨ + ユウ.
L25:
L26:## Synonyms and antonyms
L27:
L28:[暇](暇.md) (ひま) concerns free time. 余裕 is broader: someone may have financial leeway but little free time. 不足 (ふそく), "shortage," contrasts with enough capacity to spare in a particular resource.
L29:
L30:## Distinctive meaning
L31:
L32:余裕 does not always mean an idle period or unused money. It can describe calmness under pressure: 余裕のある態度 (よゆうのあるたいど), "a composed manner." Conversely, 余裕がない may explain emotional strain even when the sentence gives no measured shortage of minutes or money.
L33:
L34:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1544590, senses 1-2.

L6:type: kango
L7:kanji: []

L9:quiz_exclusions: [のんびり, 適当, 粗末, そそっかしい, おおざっぱ]
L10:---
L11:
L12:# のんき (のんき) - carefree; easygoing
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:のんき describes an easygoing, carefree, or optimistic attitude. Depending on the situation, the same lack of worry can be welcome or can imply carelessness about something that deserves attention.
L17:
L18:- 兄はのんきな性格です。 (あにはのんきなせいかくです。) - My older brother has an easygoing personality.
L19:- 締め切りは明日なのに、のんきに構えています。 (しめきりはあしたなのに、のんきにかまえています。) - Despite the deadline being tomorrow, they are taking things very casually.
L20:
L21:Use **のんきな + noun**, **のんきに + predicate**, and **のんきだ／です**. The word is usually written in kana. **呑気** and **暢気** are recognized spellings, also read のんき.
L22:
L23:## How the kanji combine
L24:
L25:The dictionary treats 呑気 and 暢気 as ateji spellings. Do not explain the word by combining modern "swallow" and "[気](../kanji/気.md), spirit." Digital Daijisen traces のん to a historical Chinese-derived reading of 暖; the present word is best learned as a whole, not as a freely predictable modern reading of either written variant.
L26:
L27:## Synonyms and antonyms
L28:
L29:[のんびり](のんびり.md) overlaps in relaxed, unhurried behavior. のんき particularly evaluates the lack of concern or worry; the context determines whether that is reassuring or irresponsible. [心配](心配.md) (しんぱい), "worry," provides a useful contrast, not an automatic replacement.
L30:
L31:## Distinctive meaning
L32:
L33:のんき does not guarantee good judgment or a favorable outcome. Do not translate it as "reckless" in every sentence, or assume that an easygoing person never takes care over anything. The second example criticizes a relaxed attitude in that situation, not the person's physical speed.
L34:
L35:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1600560, sense 1. Spelling and origin note: [Digital Daijisen via Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E5%91%91%E6%B0%97).
