L10:
L11:# 鷹揚 (おうよう) - magnanimous; composed
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:鷹揚 describes a large-hearted, easygoing or composed manner, without fussing over
L16:small things. It is a な-adjective: 鷹揚な + noun, 鷹揚に + action, and 鷹揚だ/です.
L17:
L18:- 彼は小さな失敗にも鷹揚です。 (かれはちいさなしっぱいにもおうようです。) - He is magnanimous even about small mistakes.
L19:- 彼女は鷹揚な態度で話を聞きました。 (かのじょはおうようなたいどではなしをききました。) - She listened with an unruffled manner.
L20:
L21:## How the kanji combine
L22:
L23:[鷹](../kanji/鷹.md) names a hawk, and 揚 contributes rising or lifting.
L24:Japanese dictionary explanations associate the expression with a hawk flying broadly in the sky.
L25:That image helps with the unhurried, dignified manner; the modern adjective does not
L26:literally describe a bird. In おうよう, the on readings are オウ + ヨウ.
L27:
L28:## Synonyms and antonyms
L29:
L30:[冷静](冷静.md) (れいせい) overlaps in composure, with an emphasis on clear-headedness.
L31:[のんき](のんき.md) overlaps in an easygoing attitude, sometimes with a criticism of insufficient concern.
L32:鷹揚 need not imply carelessness. 慌てる (あわてる), "become flustered," provides a contextual contrast.
L33:
L34:## Distinctive meaning
L35:
L36:Learn the whole word: the noun [鷹](鷹.md) remains たか, not おう.
L37:JMdict also recognizes 鷹揚 read おおよう and ようよう.
L38:The alternative spelling 大様 shares おおよう, but the entry restricts おうよう and
L39:ようよう to 鷹揚. Do not freely substitute every reading into every spelling.
L40:Being 鷹揚 does not guarantee sound judgment or generosity with money in every situation.
L41:
L42:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
L43:entry 1608970, senses 1-2. Image and manner: [精選版 日本国語大辞典, 鷹揚](https://kotobank.jp/word/鷹揚-449377),
L44:おうよう sense 1 and ようよう heading.

L10:
L11:# 氏 (し) - respectful name suffix
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:氏 (し) is a respectful suffix attached directly to a person's name, as in 田中氏 (たなかし).
L16:English may translate it as Mr., Ms., or a similar title, or omit the title when natural.
L17:It does not itself specify marital status.
L18:
L19:- 田中氏は会議に出席しました。 (たなかしはかいぎにしゅっせきしました。) - Mr./Ms. Tanaka attended the meeting.
L20:- これは山田氏の意見です。 (これはやまだしのいけんです。) - This is Mr./Ms. Yamada's opinion.
L21:
L22:The suffix forms part of the name expression: attach は or の after it, not between the name and 氏.
L23:
L24:氏 (し) can also mean **clan**. In a historical clan name such as **藤原氏** (ふじわらし), "the Fujiwara clan," it identifies the lineage group rather than addressing one person as Mr. or Ms. Fujiwara.
L25:
L26:## How the kanji combine
L27:
L28:[氏](../kanji/氏.md) is a single character read シ in this suffix.
L29:The same character read **うじ** is a separate dictionary identity meaning family name, lineage, or birth.
L30:Sharing the character does not make those definitions the ordinary meaning of the name suffix し.
L31:However, the clan meaning above is also recorded under し; lineage-related uses are not confined to the reading うじ.
L32:
L33:For reading recognition, the separate noun **氏 (うじ)** means a family name or lineage,
L34:and can also name a clan. **氏を継ぐ (うじをつぐ)** means "carry on the family name."
L35:This is not a second pronunciation to substitute into every modern name suffix:
L36:田中氏 in the reporting examples above remains たなかし.
L37:
L38:## Synonyms and antonyms
L39:
L40:さん is another respectful name suffix and is useful in everyday address.
L41:氏 fits reference to named people in reports and other relatively formal descriptions; it is not a default replacement for さん when calling to a friend.
L42:[名前](名前.md) (なまえ) is the name itself, not a respectful suffix.
L43:
L44:## Distinctive meaning
L45:
L46:Read the complete expression and context: 田中氏 in the examples identifies a person respectfully, while 藤原氏 in a historical discussion can identify a clan.
L47:The dictionary's respectful label does not impose an absolute rule about the speaker's gender.
L48:Do not add both 氏 and さん mechanically to the same name.
L49:
L50:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 2101130, senses 1-2; contrasting うじ: entry 1311010, senses 1-2. Clan-name example: [Digital Daijisen, 氏](https://www.weblio.jp/content/氏), the し entry. Family-name/lineage recognition: [精選版 日本国語大辞典, 氏](https://kotobank.jp/word/氏-34455), うじ [1] senses 2-3, including the attested 氏をつがぬ.

L10:
L11:# でこぼこ — uneven and bumpy
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:An uneven arrangement of raised and sunken parts. [凸](../kanji/凸.md)[凹](../kanji/凹.md) is a dictionary spelling of でこぼこ: 凸 is the raised part and 凹 the sunken part. It also describes an imbalance or mismatch, such as people of markedly different heights.
L16:
L17:- この道はでこぼこしていて、自転車で走りにくい。 — This road is bumpy and hard to cycle along.
L18:- パテで壁（かべ）のでこぼこを埋めた（うめた）。 — I filled the uneven spots in the wall with putty.
L19:- 背の高い兄と小さい弟は、でこぼこコンビだ。 — The tall older brother and small younger brother make a mismatched pair.
L20:
L21:## Synonyms and antonyms
L22:
L23:[ごつごつ](ごつごつ.md) emphasizes hard, angular projections. [ざらざら](ざらざら.md) is roughness at a finer scale. 平ら（たいら）means flat or level.
L24:
L25:## Distinctive meaning
L26:
L27:でこぼこの道, でこぼこした道, and でこぼこな道 occur; as a noun, でこぼこを直す means to fix unevenness. The mismatch sense is not automatically a criticism. [凹凸](凹凸.md)（おうとつ）is a related conventional term with the characters reversed, not another reading to substitute mechanically into でこぼこコンビ.
L28:
L29:See [Japanese onomatopoeia](../articles/japanese-onomatopoeia.md) and [texture and warmth](../articles/onomatopoeia-texture-and-warmth.md).
L30:
L31:Source: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1582410, senses 1–2; sense 2 is restricted to でこぼこ.
