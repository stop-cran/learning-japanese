L12:# 基 (もと) - basis; foundation
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:基 (もと) is a basis, base, or foundation.
L17:A particularly useful construction is Xを基に, "on the basis of X" or "using X as a basis."
L18:Use Xを基にしたY to describe a Y based on X.
L19:
L20:- この映画は実際の出来事を基にしています。 (このえいがはじっさいのできごとをもとにしています。) - This film is based on actual events.
L21:- 調査の結果を基に、計画を立てました。 (ちょうさのけっかをもとに、けいかくをたてました。) - We made a plan based on the survey results.
L22:
L23:In both, を marks what serves as the basis. The complete phrase を基に then connects that basis to the resulting work or decision.
L24:
L25:## How the kanji combine
L26:
L27:基 expresses a foundation, using the native reading もと here.
L28:JMdict groups several spellings under もと, but specifically notes 基 as a spelling used for the basis/foundation sense.
L29:That preference is not a rule that every occurrence of もと must use 基.
L30:
L31:## Synonyms and antonyms
L32:
L33:基礎 (きそ) also means a foundation or basic groundwork.
L34:[原因](原因.md) (げんいん) is a cause; the basis for a plan need not be the cause of an event.
L35:[元](元.md) (もと) overlaps in source/origin uses, but spelling depends on the sense.
L36:
L37:## Distinctive meaning
L38:
L39:Sharing a dictionary entry does not make all spellings interchangeable.
L40:In particular, the entry restricts some senses to 元 or 本; this card does not assign those senses to 基.
L41:A film based on events can still adapt them: を基に states a foundation, not an exact reproduction.
L42:
L43:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1260670, sense 2.

L11:# 基本 (きほん) - basics; foundation
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:基本 names the basics, fundamentals, or underlying basis of something. It is a noun that can modify another noun with の: 基本の動き (きほんのうごき), "basic movements."
L16:
L17:- まず文法の基本を学びましょう。 (まずぶんぽうのきほんをまなびましょう。) - First, let's learn the basics of grammar.
L18:- 相手の話を聞くことが会話の基本です。 (あいてのはなしをきくことがかいわのきほんです。) - Listening to the other person is a fundamental part of conversation.
L19:
L20:Nを基本にする means "make N the basis." The related form 基本的な (きほんてきな) means "basic; fundamental."
L21:
L22:## How the kanji combine
L23:
L24:基 contributes a base or foundation, and [本](../kanji/本.md) contributes the root or underlying part. Their related meanings reinforce what supports the rest. The on readings are キ + ホン.
L25:
L26:## Synonyms and antonyms
L27:
L28:基礎 (きそ) also means foundation or basics and overlaps in learning contexts. 応用 (おうよう), "application," is a practical contrast: applying principles is not their logical opposite.
L29:
L30:## Distinctive meaning
L31:
L32:Basic does not necessarily mean easy. A fundamental principle can take time to master.
L33:
L34:In colloquial speech, 基本 can also work adverbially: 基本、毎日来ます (きほん、まいにちきます), "Basically, I come every day." It states the normal pattern, allowing contextual exceptions. 基本的に (きほんてきに) is another useful form for "basically; in principle."
L35:
L36:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1219190, senses 1-2.

L11:
L12:# 基礎 (きそ) - foundation; basis
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:基礎 is a foundation or basis, either physical or figurative. It is a noun and can modify another noun with の.
L17:
L18:- 建物の基礎を調べました。
L19:  (たてもののきそをしらべました。) - We examined the building's foundations.
L20:- 日本語の基礎をしっかり学びたいです。
L21:  (にほんごのきそをしっかりまなびたいです。) - I want to learn the basics of Japanese thoroughly.
L22:
L23:## How the kanji combine
L24:
L25:基 contributes a base, and 礎 a foundation or foundation stone. The related meanings reinforce one another. The on readings are キ + ソ. This association helps with meaning; it is not a claim about the historical origin of either character.
L26:
L27:## Synonyms and antonyms
L28:
L29:[基本](基本.md) (きほん) also concerns basics or a foundation; [基](基.md) (もと) is another basis or foundation word. [基準](基準.md) (きじゅん) is a standard or criterion used for judgment. A foundation supports something; a criterion provides a reference for assessing it.
L30:
L31:## Distinctive meaning
L32:
L33:In learning, 基礎 points to knowledge or skills that support later work, not necessarily knowledge that is easy for everyone. 基礎を固める (きそをかためる) means "strengthen the foundations"; context determines whether the foundations are concrete or figurative.
L34:
L35:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1219060, sense 1.

L11:
L12:# 基盤 (きばん) - foundation; underlying infrastructure
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:基盤 is an underlying base or foundation that supports an activity, organization, or system. Depending on context, "infrastructure" is a useful translation. It is a noun.
L17:
L18:- 地域の産業を支える基盤を整えます。
L19:  (ちいきのさんぎょうをささえるきばんをととのえます。) - We will develop the foundation supporting the region's industries.
L20:- 新しいサービスは、共通のソフトウェア基盤を使っています。
L21:  (あたらしいサービスは、きょうつうのソフトウェアきばんをつかっています。) - The new services use a shared software platform.
L22:
L23:The second sentence illustrates the computing use: a platform on which other work or services operate.
L24:
L25:## How the kanji combine
L26:
L27:基 contributes a base, and 盤 a board or supporting surface. Their related contributions suggest underlying support. The on readings are キ + バン. This is a meaning aid, not a historical derivation.
L28:
L29:## Synonyms and antonyms
L30:
L31:[基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) and [基本](基本.md) (きほん) also concern foundations or basics. 基盤 is especially useful for the supporting framework or conditions on which something operates; this is an emphasis, not an absolute prohibition on overlapping uses.
L32:
L33:## Distinctive meaning
L34:
L35:基盤 need not be a concrete slab. Financial resources, institutions, or technical systems can provide a foundation. An electronics circuit board or substrate is ordinarily written 基板 (きばん), a separate same-reading word; do not infer a circuit board from every occurrence of 基盤.
L36:
L37:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1219170, senses 1 and 3; comparisons 1219060, 1219160 and 1591210.
L38:Usage: [Kotobank: 基盤, Seisenban definition](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%9F%BA%E7%9B%A4-475382).

L11:
L12:# 武器 (ぶき) - weapon; asset
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:武器 is a weapon or weaponry. It is a noun. It also has a figurative meaning: an ability or advantage that helps someone succeed.
L17:
L18:- 博物館には昔の武器が展示されています。 (はくぶつかんにはむかしのぶきがてんじされています。) - Old weapons are displayed in the museum.
L19:- 語学力は仕事で大きな武器になります。 (ごがくりょくはしごとでおおきなぶきになります。) - Language skills can be a major asset at work.
L20:
L21:武器になる means "become or serve as an asset" in the second example. It does not suggest using a physical weapon at work.
L22:
L23:## How the kanji combine
L24:
L25:[武](../kanji/武.md) contributes military or martial activity, and 器 contributes an implement or instrument. Together they name an implement for armed conflict. The on readings are ブ + キ. This component relationship explains the compound's sense, not the historical shape of either character.
L26:
L27:## Synonyms and antonyms
L28:
L29:[道具](道具.md) (どうぐ) is a tool or implement more generally. 武器 narrows the physical use to weapons. Figuratively, [能力](能力.md) (のうりょく), "ability," may be the asset being described.
L30:
L31:## Distinctive meaning
L32:
L33:The source's English "ordinance" is a spelling error: an ordinance is a law or regulation. The relevant English word is "ordnance," weaponry. 武器 does not mean [法律](法律.md) (ほうりつ), "law." In figurative use, identify what gives the advantage rather than interpreting every 武器 as military equipment.
L34:
L35:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1498460, senses 1-2.

L10:
L11:# ごつごつ — hard and knobbly
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:Hard, angular, rugged irregularities, such as protruding rocks, tree roots, or bony joints.
L16:
L17:- ごつごつした岩（いわ）の上に腰（こし）を下ろした（おろした）。 — I sat down on a rugged rock.
L18:- 祖父（そふ）の手は、長年の仕事でごつごつしている。 — My grandfather's hands are knobbly from years of work.
L19:
L20:## Synonyms and antonyms
L21:
L22:[ざらざら](ざらざら.md) is finer-grained roughness. [でこぼこ](でこぼこ.md) concerns uneven highs and lows without necessarily being hard or angular. [つるつる](つるつる.md) suggests a smooth surface.
L23:
L24:## Distinctive meaning
L25:
L26:Use ごつごつした岩, ごつごつの手, or ごつごつしている. A surface can be both ごつごつ in its overall form and ざらざら at a smaller scale. The word can suggest robust, work-worn hands; it is not inherently a diagnosis or an insult. Do not replace every instance of English “rough” with it.
L27:
L28:See [Japanese onomatopoeia](../articles/japanese-onomatopoeia.md) and [texture and warmth](../articles/onomatopoeia-texture-and-warmth.md).
L29:
L30:Source: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1631900, sense 1.

L12:
L13:# ざらざら — rough and gritty
L14:
L15:## Meaning
L16:
L17:Fine roughness or a sandy, granular feel; also a rattling sound made by many small hard objects.
L18:
L19:- この紙は、触るとざらざらしている。 — This paper feels rough to the touch.
L20:- 砂が入って、口の中がざらざらする。 — I got sand in my mouth, and it feels gritty.
L21:- 袋（ふくろ）から豆（まめ）をざらざらと出した。 — I poured the beans out of the bag with a rattling sound.
L22:
L23:## Synonyms and antonyms
L24:
L25:[ごつごつ](ごつごつ.md) suggests larger, harder protrusions. [でこぼこ](でこぼこ.md) describes raised and lowered parts of a surface. [すべすべ](すべすべ.md) is smooth under the fingers.
L26:
L27:## Distinctive meaning
L28:
L29:ざらざらした紙, ざらざらの表面（ひょうめん）, and ざらざらする describe texture. With 出す or 落ちる, ざらざらと can foreground the sound of grains or beans moving. A rough voice can also be called ざらざらした声; this is a sensory extension, not literal sand in the speaker's throat.
L30:
L31:See [Japanese onomatopoeia](../articles/japanese-onomatopoeia.md) and [texture and warmth](../articles/onomatopoeia-texture-and-warmth.md).

L33:Source: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1005420, senses 1–2.
