12: # 冗談 (じょうだん) - joke; jest
13: 
14: ## Meaning
15: 
16: 冗談 is a joke or remark not meant seriously. It is a noun.
17: Use 冗談を言う (じょうだんをいう), "tell a joke; joke," rather than adding する
18: as the default verb. 冗談です means "I'm joking" or "It's a joke."
19: 
20: - 兄はよく冗談を言って、みんなを笑わせます。
21:   (あにはよくじょうだんをいって、みんなをわらわせます。)
22:   - My older brother often makes jokes and makes everyone laugh.
23: - 「本当に引っ越すの？」「ううん、冗談だよ。」
24:   (「ほんとうにひっこすの？」「ううん、じょうだんだよ。」)
25:   - "Are you really moving?" "No, I'm joking."
26: 
27: 冗談で言った (じょうだんでいった) means "said it as a joke."
28: 
29: ## How the kanji combine
30: 
31: [冗](../kanji/冗.md) contributes something superfluous or not serious here, and [談](../kanji/談.md)
32: contributes talk. The compound names joking talk, not necessarily a long or wordy speech.
33: The on readings are ジョウ + ダン.
34: 
35: ## Synonyms and antonyms
36: 
37: ジョーク overlaps with "joke." [嘘](嘘.md) (うそ) is a lie: a joking statement
38: may be untrue, but the words describe different aspects of the communication.
39: [本当](本当.md) (ほんとう), "true; real," contrasts with the pretended move in the exchange.
40: 
41: ## Distinctive meaning
42: 
43: Calling a remark 冗談 describes it as a joke, but does not guarantee that listeners
44: find it funny or harmless. Tone, relationship, and context matter.
45: In its literal use, 冗談ではありません says "I'm not joking / This is not a joke."
46: Conversational 冗談じゃない (じょうだんじゃない) can also express indignant rejection:
47: "Give me a break!"
48: 
49: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1355540, sense 1; entry 2403910.
