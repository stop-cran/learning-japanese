11: # 凜 (りん) — dignified; bracing cold
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 凜 describes a dignified, firmly composed bearing, or cold that feels sharp
16: and bracing. It can also describe a clear, ringing voice or sound.
17: Learn the constructions **凜と** and **凜とした + noun**, rather than adding
18: な as if this were an ordinary な-adjective.
19: 
20: - 凜とした声がひびく。
21:   (りんとしたこえがひびく。) — A clear, firm voice rings out.
22: - 凜とした朝の寒気
23:   (りんとしたあさのかんき) — The bracing chill of the morning.
24: 
25: These short examples come from Daijisen. The second is a noun phrase,
26: not a complete sentence. In both, とした describes the following noun;
27: it does not necessarily report an earlier change into that state.
28: 凜と can modify an action. JMdict labels the word a *taru adjective*
29: and an adverb taking と; that classification does not make 凜 an い-adjective.
30: 
31: ## How the kanji combine
32: 
33: This is a single-character Sino-Japanese word, not a compound.
34: [凜](../kanji/凜.md) supplies the whole on reading リン and the dignity/coldness
35: meaning. JMdict also recognizes 凛 (りん) in the same lexical family.
36: The variant spelling does not introduce another pronunciation or word.
37: 
38: ## Synonyms and antonyms
39: 
40: [寒い](寒い.md) (さむい) is the broader everyday adjective for cold weather
41: or surroundings. 凜とした adds the sharp, bracing quality in the morning example;
42: it is not a neutral substitute for every occurrence of 寒い.
43: [厳しい](厳しい.md) (きびしい) can describe severe cold, but also strict
44: standards and harsh circumstances; do not transfer all those uses to 凜.
45: 
46: [澄む](澄む.md) (すむ) overlaps for a clear, resonant voice.
47: [冷静](冷静.md) (れいせい) emphasizes mental composure and clear-headedness,
48: not necessarily the dignified outward bearing conveyed by 凜とした.
49: Warmth contrasts with the cold sense, but is not an opposite of dignity.
50: 
51: ## Distinctive meaning
52: 
53: A 凜とした声 can sound clear and firm without being loud, angry or cruel.
54: Likewise, a dignified manner does not establish someone's social rank or moral
55: superiority. Select the translation from what is being described.
56: 
57: The full dictionary family also records a tense atmosphere sense.
58: This article focuses on bearing, sound and bracing cold; it is not an
59: exhaustive account of every literary or historical construction.
60: Neither dictionary inclusion nor the variant spelling establishes a frequency
61: ranking. Personal-name usage is outside this article's scope.
62: 
63: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1564320, all four senses.
64: Constructions and short example bases: [Digital Daijisen: 凜](https://kotobank.jp/word/凜-659857);
65: adverbial senses: [Seisen: 凜と](https://kotobank.jp/word/凜と-2093032).
