# 苦労 (くろう) - hardship; trouble

## Meaning

苦労 is hardship, difficulty, or the trouble taken in doing something. 苦労する means "have a hard time; struggle." Mark the difficulty with に, or use a plain verb plus のに to describe what was hard to accomplish.

- 仕事を探すのに苦労しました。 (しごとをさがすのにくろうしました。) - I had a hard time finding a job.
- 若いころはお金に苦労しました。 (わかいころはおかねにくろうしました。) - I struggled financially when I was young.

苦労をする is also possible, with the noun itself marked by を. Do not turn the job search into a direct object merely by copying English "struggle with."

## How the kanji combine

[苦](../kanji/苦.md) contributes suffering or difficulty, and [労](../kanji/労.md) contributes labor or toil. Together they highlight effort accompanied by difficulty. The reading is ク + ロウ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[努力](努力.md) (どりょく) focuses on effort; it need not imply the same hardship. [困難](困難.md) (こんなん) names difficulty, while 苦労 often presents what someone goes through.

[楽](楽.md) (らく), "ease; comfortable," provides a contextual contrast: 苦労せずに means "without difficulty."

## Distinctive meaning

苦労 can describe years of hardship or one troublesome task. It does not guarantee success or imply that the person chose the difficulty. A sentence about 苦労 often invites attention to the experience behind a result, rather than measuring how many hours were spent working.
