separately, using CLEAN, NEEDS FIX or UNVERIFIED with evidence, severity, confidence,
source-line citations and explicit limits. CLEAN is bounded absence of an established

distinct title strings alone do not settle an ambiguous English answer. Distinguish
source readings from quiz selections and mnemonics from historical/phonetic evidence.
