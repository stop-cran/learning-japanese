## Findings
### F1: 耗 lacks direct exclusions for 摩 and 擦
- Facet: Consistency/quiz safety
- Severity: low
- Confidence: medium
- Citations: snapshot/kanji/耗.md:14; snapshot/kanji/耗.md:26-27; repo/kanji/摩.md:3; repo/kanji/摩.md:14; repo/kanji/擦.md:14; repo/kanji/擦.md:33; evidence/jmdict-families.xml:243; evidence/kanjidic2-records.xml:97; evidence/external-snapshots/04-extract.txt:11
- Evidence: 耗 is titled "decrease; wear down through use", and its own meaning section uses 摩耗 to illustrate wear through friction. JMdict glosses 摩耗/磨耗 as "wear; abrasion", and Daijisen glosses 耗 as すりへる. 摩 is displayed as "rub; chafe" (KANJIDIC2: chafe, rub, polish, grind, scrape), and the 擦 card records 擦れる す.れる "to rub; to chafe; to wear out". Neither direct pair is declared on 耗 (54 precautionary guards, including weaker ones such as 暮 and 帰) or on 摩/擦. The extra guards on 拍 do not cover 耗. The curated 右/川/本 choices do not expose either pair today. However, 摩, 擦 and 耗 all carry jlpt-n1, grade-8 and structure-unclassified tags, so tag-preferring fallback choices in reduced or custom pools could offer "rub; chafe" or "scrape; scrub" with a wear-down target, or the reverse. The answers are not interchangeable, but friction wear makes the pair defensible enough to need a guard, to the same standard the card applies elsewhere.
- Required change: Add 摩 and 擦 to 耗's quiz_exclusions and mention them briefly in 耗's Notes as friction-wear comparisons. Then rerun validate, quiz-safety and manifest.
