# 企 — plan; undertake

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9), the two-stroke top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

企 concerns planning or undertaking something. [企てる](../words/企てる.md)
くわだてる means to plan or attempt; [企業](../words/企業.md) きぎょう
names a business enterprise, not just any plan.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture [人](人.md), a person,
above [止](止.md), stopping to think before undertaking a plan.
This is a memory aid for the visible parts, not a historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キ | on | [企業](../words/企業.md) きぎょう — business enterprise; company |
| くわだ.てる | kun | [企てる](../words/企てる.md) くわだてる — plan; attempt |
| たくら.む | kun, recognition here | 企む たくらむ — scheme; often written たくらむ |

This card practices キ and くわだ.てる. The separate verb たくらむ is
dictionary-attested, not an obsolete or invented pronunciation. Its usual
kana spelling makes it a recognition example here rather than an additional
kanji-reading question. It often suggests a dubious or secret scheme;
that tendency is not a rule that every use must concern wrongdoing.

## Common words

- [企業](../words/企業.md) きぎょう — business enterprise; company
- [企てる](../words/企てる.md) くわだてる — plan; attempt

## Notes

- 企てる is an ichidan verb: 企てます, 企てた. 企む is a different,
  godan verb: たくらみます, たくらんだ. They are not two interchangeable
  endings on one verb.
- Compare [全](全.md): its lower part is 王-shaped, while 企 has 止 below.
  The source indexes 全 under 入, but 企 under 人; a similar top does not
  give every character the same indexing radical.
- [危](危.md), danger, and [休](休.md), rest, also have six strokes,
  but neither has the planning meaning of 企.

# 企てる (くわだてる) — plan; attempt

## Meaning

企てる means to devise a plan or attempt to carry something out.
It is a transitive ichidan verb: the planned or attempted activity takes を.
Forms include 企てます (くわだてます), 企てない (くわだてない),
企てた (くわだてた) and 企てている (くわだてている).

- 新しい事業を企てています。 (あたらしいじぎょうをくわだてています。) — We are planning a new business venture.
- 彼らは何かを企てているようです。 (かれらはなにかをくわだてているようです。) — They seem to be plotting something.

The second sentence suggests suspicion about an unspecified plan.
The verb also covers undertaking or attempting an activity, not only
thinking about it. 企てた alone does not establish that the attempt succeeded.

## How the kanji combine

[企](../kanji/企.md) supplies くわだ, the planning or undertaking element
of this native verb; てる is the written ending.
Keep the whole reading くわだてる rather than using the on reading キ.
Learn くわだてる as one ichidan verb meaning "plan; attempt."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[計画](計画.md)する (けいかくする) is a more neutral way to say "plan,"
including ordinary travel arrangements. 企てる can give a plan a more
deliberate or weighty tone and can suggest a plot; it is not restricted
to bad intentions.

[引き受ける](引き受ける.md) (ひきうける) overlaps with "undertake"
when accepting responsibility. Accepting an assigned task and devising
an undertaking emphasize different things, though their English labels overlap.

## Distinctive meaning

企む (たくらむ), usually written たくらむ, is a separate godan verb
often used for scheming. It is not the contracted form of 企てる.
For an ordinary schedule, [予定](予定.md) (よてい) or 計画 is usually
a better starting point than a word that may sound like plotting.

The dictionary also records an archaic tiptoe sense of 企てる.
That label belongs to that sense, not to the planning verb taught here.
The planning use taught here does not describe physically standing on tiptoe.

# 企業 (きぎょう) - business enterprise; company

## Meaning

企業 is a noun for a business enterprise or company. It is useful when discussing businesses as economic organizations, their activities, or their sizes.

- この企業は医療機器を作っています。 (このきぎょうはいりょうききをつくっています。) - This company makes medical equipment.
- 地元の企業で働きたいです。 (じもとのきぎょうではたらきたいです。) - I want to work for a local business.

企業で働く uses で for the organization where someone works. 企業の活動 (きぎょうのかつどう) means "a company's activities."

## How the kanji combine

[企](../kanji/企.md) carries a planning or undertaking association; [業](../kanji/業.md) concerns work or business. Together they name an organized business undertaking. The readings are キ + ギョウ; keep the long vowel in ぎょう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[会社](会社.md) (かいしゃ), "company," overlaps strongly. 企業 is useful for discussing the business entity or sector without focusing on a particular workplace.

[工場](工場.md) (こうじょう), "factory," names a production facility. A business can operate several factories.

## Distinctive meaning

大企業 (だいきぎょう) means a large enterprise; 中小企業 (ちゅうしょうきぎょう) refers to small and medium-sized enterprises. 企業 does not inherently mean a giant corporation.

Do not confuse it with 起業 (きぎょう), "starting a business." That same-sounding word names the startup act; 企業 in this card names the enterprise itself, not any undertaking such as a household chore.
