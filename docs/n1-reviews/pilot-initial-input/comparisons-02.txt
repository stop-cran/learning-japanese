L12: # 図書 (としょ) - books
L13: 
L14: ## Meaning
L15: 
L16: 図書 means books, especially as reading materials or a collection being
L17: selected, kept, or lent. It is a noun: 図書を借りる (としょをかりる),
L18: "borrow books," and 図書の返却 (としょのへんきゃく), "returning books."
L19: 
L20: - この図書は来週まで借りられます。 (このとしょはらいしゅうまでかりられます。) - This book can be borrowed until next week.
L21: - 学校で新しい図書を購入しました。 (がっこうであたらしいとしょをこうにゅうしました。) - The school purchased new books.
L22: 
L23: The first example refers to one item. The English gloss "books" does not
L24: make the Japanese noun obligatorily plural.
L25: 
L26: ## How the kanji combine
L27: 
L28: [図](../kanji/図.md) contributes diagrams or pictorial material, and
L29: [書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing or books. Together they name books
L30: as written materials; they do not require every book to contain diagrams.
L31: The on readings here are と + しょ, not ず + しょ.
L32: 
L33: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L34: 
L35: [本](本.md) (ほん) is the broad everyday word for a book.
L36: [図書館](図書館.md) (としょかん) is a library, the place or institution,
L37: not the books themselves. There is no useful antonym of 図書; another medium
L38: is a comparison, not an opposite.
L39: 
L40: ## Distinctive meaning
L41: 
L42: 図書 is useful in school and library contexts, including labels and descriptions
L43: of collections. That does not make it restricted to official documents.
L44: Distinguish 図書を借りる, borrowing a book, from 図書館で借りる,
L45: borrowing at a library: one names the object and the other the location.
L46: 
L47: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1370410.

L11: # 鳥 (とり) — bird
L12: 
L13: ## Meaning
L14: 
L15: 鳥 means **bird**. It can refer to one bird or to birds generally; the noun does not change form for plural.
L16: 
L17: - 鳥が木の上にいます。（とりがきのうえにいます。）— There is a bird in the tree.
L18: - あの白い鳥を見てください。（あのしろいとりをみてください。）— Please look at that white bird.
L19: 
L20: The first example uses います because it describes the presence of a living animal.
L21: 
L22: ## How the kanji combine
L23: 
L24: This is a single-kanji native noun: [鳥](../kanji/鳥.md) is read とり as a whole.
L25: It has no okurigana and no compound parts.
L26: The on reading チョウ appears in other words, such as 野鳥（やちょう）, "wild bird."
L27: 
L28: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L29: 
L30: - 小鳥（ことり）means "small bird," a narrower description rather than an exact synonym.
L31: - 鶏（にわとり）means "chicken," a particular kind of bird.
L32: - 野鳥（やちょう）means "wild bird."
L33: - [魚](魚.md)（さかな）, "fish," names another animal category, not an antonym.
L34: 
L35: There is no natural opposite of "bird."
L36: 
L37: ## Distinctive meaning
L38: 
L39: Outside a food context, 鳥 normally refers to the animal category.
L40: In food contexts, とり can mean chicken meat; 鶏肉（とりにく）makes that meaning explicit,
L41: and 鶏 is the usual kanji for chicken specifically.
L42: Do not assume that every use of 鳥 means chicken or that every bird mentioned is food.

L10: 
L11: # 花 (はな) — flower
L12: 
L13: ## Meaning
L14: 
L15: 花 is a flower or blossom: 花が咲く さく (the flowers bloom), 花を買う かう (buy flowers), 花を飾る かざる (decorate with flowers), 花屋 はなや (flower shop), 花見 はなみ (cherry-blossom viewing).
L16: 
L17: ## How the kanji combine
L18: 
L19: [花](../kanji/花.md) alone with the kun reading はな. In a few words the on reading カ is used: 花瓶 かびん (vase), 花壇 かだん (flower bed). [花火](花火.md)（はなび）, "fireworks," uses はな as its first element; 火 is read び here.
L20: 
L21: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L22: 
L23: - Close: 桜 さくら (cherry blossom, often meant by 花見), 草花 くさばな (flowering plants).
L24: - Same kana reading: 鼻 はな (nose), 端 はな (edge, tip); distinguish these words by their spelling and meaning.
L25: 
L26: ## Distinctive meaning
L27: 
L28: 花 can mean "(the) best" in set expressions such as 言わぬが花（いわぬがはな）, "some things are better left unsaid." 花形（はながた） means "a star; a leading figure."
L29: 
L30: In 花より団子（はなよりだんご）, literally "dumplings over flowers," 花 represents blossoms and aesthetic enjoyment, not "the best part." The proverb contrasts practical benefit with beauty. In spring, 花 often means cherry blossoms specifically.
L31: 
L32: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1194500, senses 1–3 and 7; entries 1264370 (言わぬが花), 1194640, sense 2 (花形), and 1194530 (花より団子).
