L12: # 矛盾 (むじゅん) -- contradiction; inconsistency
L13: 
L14: ## Meaning
L15: 
L16: 矛盾 is a contradiction or inconsistency: claims, facts or actions do
L17: not fit together. As an intransitive suru verb, 矛盾する means to
L18: contradict or be inconsistent with something.
L19: 
L20: - 二つの説明は矛盾しています。 (ふたつのせつめいはむじゅんしています。) -- The two explanations contradict each other.
L21: - 彼の発言は事実と矛盾しています。 (かれのはつげんはじじつとむじゅんしています。) -- His statement is inconsistent with the facts.
L22: 
L23: Use AがBと矛盾する for A being inconsistent with B. Forms include
L24: 矛盾しない, 矛盾して and 矛盾した (むじゅんしない・むじゅんして・むじゅんした).
L25: 
L26: ## How the kanji combine
L27: 
L28: [矛](../kanji/矛.md) means spear and 盾 shield; the readings here are ム + ジュン.
L29: The dictionary traces the expression to a story in 韓非子 (かんぴし):
L30: a seller praises a spear that pierces anything and a shield that nothing
L31: can pierce, then cannot answer what would happen if the spear struck
L32: that shield. This is an attributed explanatory story, not a report of
L33: an actual tested weapon.
L34: 
L35: 矛楯 is a recorded rare spelling.
L36: 
L37: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L38: 
L39: - [相違](相違.md) (そうい) and [違い](違い.md) (ちがい) concern differences or discrepancies; not every difference is contradictory.
L40: - [対立](対立.md) (たいりつ) and [衝突](衝突.md) (しょうとつ) include opposition or conflict, not necessarily a logical inconsistency.
L41: - [一致](一致.md) (いっち) concerns agreement or correspondence.
L42: 
L43: ## Distinctive meaning
L44: 
L45: Two opinions may differ without being mutually incompatible. 矛盾
L46: points to their failure to fit together, not merely different tastes,
L47: a quarrel between people or any isolated mistake. It is also not a
L48: transitive template *Bを矛盾する for refuting an opponent.
