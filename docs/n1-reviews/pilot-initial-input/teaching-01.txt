L14: # 幹 — tree trunk; main part
L15: 
L16: **Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 干 (no. 51) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
L17: 
L18: ## Meaning and origin
L19: 
L20: 幹 means a tree's trunk and, by extension, a main or central part. In 幹線 かんせん, "main line; trunk line," it identifies a principal route rather than a branch.
L21: 
L22: **Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Use the tall left-hand stack and the right-hand [干](干.md) shape as a reminder of an upright trunk supporting the main structure. This is a visual memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

L37: ## Notes
L38: 
L39: - [木](木.md) names a tree or wood more generally; 幹 singles out its trunk. 幹部 refers to an organization's leadership, not its wooden parts.

L41: - The dictionary also records 幹 から, "trunk; stem; stalk," in a separate word family. That word does not change this card's preserved KANJIDIC2 reading list or make every written 幹 interchangeable between readings.

L14: # 氏 — clan; family name
L15: 
L16: **Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 氏 (no. 83) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
L17: 
L18: ## Meaning and origin
L19: 
L20: 氏 concerns a family name or lineage. It is also used after a person's name as a respectful suffix, normally read し in modern reporting. The suffix does not specify marital status.
L21: 
L22: **Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a family label resting along the lower sweeping stroke. This is a memory aid for the whole shape, not a reconstruction of its origin.
L23: 
L24: ## Readings
L25: 
L26: | Reading | Type | Typical words |
L27: | --- | --- | --- |
L28: | シ | on | [氏](../words/氏.md) し - respectful name suffix; [氏名](../words/氏名.md) しめい - full name |
L29: | うじ | kun | 氏 うじ - family name; lineage; clan |
L30: | -うじ | kun suffix | 山田氏 やまだうじ - Mr. Yamada (older name-suffix use; see below) |
L31: 
L32: The suffix example illustrates an older use documented by Japanese dictionaries. For a contemporary reference to Mr./Ms. Yamada, learn 山田氏 やまだし instead; do not replace modern name-suffix し with うじ mechanically.
L33: 
L34: ## Common words
L35: 
L36: - [氏名](../words/氏名.md) しめい - full name
L37: - [氏](../words/氏.md) し - respectful suffix after a name; also clan in the relevant context
L38: - 氏 うじ - family name; lineage
L39: 
L40: ## Notes
L41: 
L42: - The linked 氏 word article has primary reading **し**, not うじ. Both readings can have a clan-related meaning; the difference is not a strict split between "respectful title" and "clan."
L43: - [姓](姓.md), surname, overlaps with 氏's family-name meaning. It is a useful comparison, not a safe wrong answer for this card.
L44: - Distinguish [民](民.md), populace, and [低](低.md), low. 氏 is also visible within [紙](紙.md), paper, but 紙 is a different character.
L45: - KANJIDIC2 lists both うじ and suffix -うじ. The dash marks attachment, not an extra sound.
L46:

L14: # 乃 — thereupon; possessive no
L15: 
L16: **Strokes:** 2 · **Key (radical):** 丿 (no. 4) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
L17: 
L18: ## Meaning and origin
L19: 
L20: 乃 appears in connecting expressions such as [乃至](../words/乃至.md) ないし, "from ... to; or." Character dictionaries also give **すなわち**, "thereupon; namely," **なんじ**, "you," and use for the sound **の**. These are not instructions to replace ordinary hiragana の with 乃 throughout a modern sentence.
L21: 
L22: **Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Follow the curved, hooked outline and the separate slanting stroke. Think of one phrase curving into the next. The shape association does not explain the character's history.
L23: 
L24: ## Readings
L25: 
L26: | Reading | Type | Typical words |
L27: | --- | --- | --- |
L28: | ナイ | on | [乃至](../words/乃至.md) ないし - from ... to; or, usually written ないし |
L29: | ダイ | on | 乃公 だいこう - dated, rough or arrogant male "I; me" |
L30: | ノ | on, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this as an on reading; no separate on-reading word example is established here |
L31: | アイ | on, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this reading; no word example is established here |
L32: | の | kun | 乃 の - a historical spelling associated with the possessive particle の; JMdict treats 乃 in this family as search-only |
L33: | すなわ.ち | kun | 乃ち すなわち - namely; thereupon, a rare spelling in the すなわち family |
L34: | なんじ | kun | 乃 なんじ - "you" in character-dictionary/kanbun usage; not a modern conversational form of address |
L35: 
L36: **Kanbun** is the Japanese reading tradition for texts written in Classical Chinese. Recognizing a character's kanbun gloss does not make it an everyday standalone word. The on ノ and kun の labels above preserve the source's separate classifications; identical kana do not establish two interchangeable modern uses.
L37: 
L38: ## Common words
L39: 
L40: - [乃至](../words/乃至.md) ないし - from ... to; or
L41: 
L42: The support article teaches the usual kana spelling as well as the kanji spelling. The other readings above are recognition or source-lookup material, not a list of common compounds.
L43: 
L44: ## Notes
L45: 
L46: - 乃公 is not a neutral substitute for 私 わたし, "I." Its whole-word alternative おれ is a special reading, not evidence that 乃 alone reads お.
L47: - Do not infer a person's name reading from this table. The source's grade-9/name-use classification does not mean every listed reading is a usable name.
L48: - Contrast [九](九.md), nine, [万](万.md), ten thousand, and [力](力.md), power. Similar short outlines do not give them 乃's connecting or particle-related uses.
