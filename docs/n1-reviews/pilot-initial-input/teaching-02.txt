L14: # 凸 — convex; protruding
L15: 
L16: **Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 凵 (no. 17) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
L17: 
L18: ## Meaning and origin
L19: 
L20: 凸 describes a surface projecting outward: **convex**. Its native reading でこ also occurs for a forehead or bump. Contrast [凹](凹.md), which points inward or downward.
L21: 
L22: **Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** The raised middle of 凸 looks like a bump rising above the surrounding surface.

L37: ## Notes
L38: 
L39: - 凸凹 でこぼこ combines raised and sunken parts. Its dictionary family also records とつおう for physical unevenness, but restricts the imbalance/difference sense to **でこぼこ**. The linked kana-headed article retains its primary でこぼこ identity.
L40: - 凹凸 おうとつ has the reverse written order. Learn each whole spelling with its reading rather than reversing sounds by analogy.
L41: - [回](回.md), revolve or times, and [同](同.md), same, are visual comparisons, not words for a projecting surface.

L18: ## Meaning and origin
L19: 
L20: 矛 names a **spear or polearm**, a weapon with a long shaft. It is familiar in [矛盾](../words/矛盾.md) むじゅん, "contradiction; inconsistency," whose conventional explanatory story contrasts a spear and a shield.
L21: 
L22: **Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the long central stroke as a shaft and the angled strokes around it as the weapon's head and fittings. This is a drawing aid, not a reconstruction of an ancient weapon.

L37: ## Notes

L41: - A shield, 盾 たて, is a useful semantic contrast, not the meaning of 矛. The linked 矛盾 article explains the attributed story without treating it as an actual weapon test.

L17: 
L18: ## Meaning and origin
L19: 
L20: 漱 concerns **rinsing the mouth or gargling**. The everyday action is usefully learned as [うがい](../words/うがい.md); 漱 is a rare kanji spelling of that noun, not its required written form.
L21: 
L22: **Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Connect the water-shaped 氵 on the left with water moving around the mouth. The dense right side is a shape to recognize, not a literal diagram of gargling.
L23:

L26: | Reading | Type | Typical words |
L27: | --- | --- | --- |
L28: | ソウ | on | 漱石枕流 そうせきちんりゅう - a four-character expression for stubbornly refusing to admit being wrong |
L29: | シュウ | on, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this reading; no word example is established here |
L30: | ス | on, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this reading; no word example is established here |
L31: | くちすす.ぐ | kun | 漱ぐ くちすすぐ - rinse the mouth; gargle; Kanjipedia explicitly gives this written boundary |
L32: | くちそそ.ぐ | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this form; a separate verified lexical example with this pronunciation has not been established here |
L33: | うがい | kun | 漱 うがい - gargling; rinsing the mouth, a rare spelling of [うがい](../words/うがい.md) |
L34: | すす.ぐ | kun | 漱ぐ すすぐ - rinse the mouth; usually written in kana in this dictionary family |
L35: 
L36: In 漱ぐ **くちすすぐ**, the one character covers **くちすす** and only ぐ is written outside it. Do not silently rewrite that spelling as 口漱ぐ. The separate reading **すすぐ** has a different-sized spoken stem despite the same written form.
L37: 
L38: ## Common words
L39: 
L40: - [うがい](../words/うがい.md) うがい - gargling; rinsing the mouth; the useful kana spelling
L41: 
L42: The four-character expression above is reading support, not everyday hygiene vocabulary.
L43: 
L44: ## Notes
L45: 
L46: - 漱ぐ すすぐ is specifically mouth-rinsing in the selected dictionary family. Do not transfer every laundry or washing sense of the separate 濯ぐ family to it.
L47: - Rinsing the mouth is not swallowing a drink. Compare [飲](飲.md), drink, and [歌](歌.md), song or sing; neither is a suitable translation of 漱.
L48: - [速](速.md), fast, has 辶 surrounding 束 rather than 漱's left-hand 氵 and additional right-hand strokes.
L49: - The linked うがい article preserves its kana headword and primary reading. No medical benefit is implied by these language examples.
