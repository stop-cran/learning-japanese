L11: # でこぼこ — uneven and bumpy
L12: 
L13: ## Meaning
L14: 
L15: An uneven arrangement of raised and sunken parts. [凸](../kanji/凸.md)[凹](../kanji/凹.md) is a dictionary spelling of でこぼこ: 凸 is the raised part and 凹 the sunken part. It also describes an imbalance or mismatch, such as people of markedly different heights.
L16: 
L17: - この道はでこぼこしていて、自転車で走りにくい。 — This road is bumpy and hard to cycle along.
L18: - パテで壁（かべ）のでこぼこを埋めた（うめた）。 — I filled the uneven spots in the wall with putty.
L19: - 背の高い兄と小さい弟は、でこぼこコンビだ。 — The tall older brother and small younger brother make a mismatched pair.
L20: 
L21: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L22: 
L23: [ごつごつ](ごつごつ.md) emphasizes hard, angular projections. [ざらざら](ざらざら.md) is roughness at a finer scale. 平ら（たいら）means flat or level.
L24: 
L25: ## Distinctive meaning
L26: 
L27: でこぼこの道, でこぼこした道, and でこぼこな道 occur; as a noun, でこぼこを直す means to fix unevenness. The mismatch sense is not automatically a criticism. 凹凸（おうとつ）is a related conventional term with the characters reversed, not another reading to substitute mechanically into でこぼこコンビ.
L28: 
L29: See [Japanese onomatopoeia](../articles/japanese-onomatopoeia.md) and [texture and warmth](../articles/onomatopoeia-texture-and-warmth.md).
L30: 
L31: Source: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1582410, senses 1–2; sense 2 is restricted to でこぼこ.

L11: 
L12: # 凹む (へこむ) - become dented; feel down
L13: 
L14: ## Meaning
L15: 
L16: 凹む is an intransitive godan verb: become dented, sink in or develop a depression.
L17: It also means be beaten or give in, feel down in colloquial speech, or suffer a loss.
L18: Use the affected thing or person as the subject, not a direct object being dented.
L19: 
L20: - 箱の角がへこみました。 (はこのかどがへこみました。) - The corner of the box became dented.
L21: - 試験に落ちて、少しへこんでいます。 (しけんにおちて、すこしへこんでいます。) - I failed the examination and am feeling a little down.
L22: 
L23: ## How the kanji combine
L24: 
L25: [凹](../kanji/凹.md) has a concave or sunken-in meaning; the native verb is へこむ, often written
L26: in kana. Associating the visible hollow in 凹 with a dent is a modern-shape
L27: memory aid, not a historical explanation of the word's emotional sense.
L28: 
L29: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L30: 
L31: [沈む](沈む.md) (しずむ) overlaps in sinking and low spirits.
L32: [しょんぼり](しょんぼり.md) describes a downcast state;
L33: [がっかり](がっかり.md) emphasizes disappointment.
L34: [負ける](負ける.md) (まける) and [損](損.md) (そん) help with the defeat
L35: and loss senses. Separate 窪む (くぼむ), also written 凹む, concerns a hollow;
L36: do not transfer every emotional へこむ use to that reading.
L37: 
L38: ## Distinctive meaning
L39: 
L40: Conjugate へこまない, へこみます and へこんだ, not へこみた.
L41: The emotional use can describe a temporary setback, not a medical diagnosis.
L42: The defeat and loss senses depend on context; the word does not require a literal
L43: dent whenever a person feels discouraged or suffers a setback.
L44: 
L45: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1179200, senses 1-4; compare separate 1246840 for くぼむ.

L11: # 扱う (あつかう) - handle; deal with; treat
L12: 
L13: ## Meaning
L14: 
L15: 扱う is a transitive godan verb for handling something, dealing with a matter, or treating a person. The person or thing takes を. Its forms include 扱います, 扱って, and 扱わない.
L16: 
L17: - この機械は丁寧に扱ってください。 (このきかいはていねいにあつかってください。) - Please handle this machine carefully.
L18: - 私を子供として扱わないでください。 (わたしをこどもとしてあつかわないでください。) - Please do not treat me as a child.
L19: 
L20: In the second example, AをBとして扱う means "treat A as B." Problems and topics can also be objects: この問題を扱う (このもんだいをあつかう), "deal with this problem."
L21: 
L22: ## How the kanji combine
L23: 
L24: [扱](../kanji/扱.md) carries the handling meaning and is read あつか before the ending う. This is one native verb, not a compound of several kanji. Learn its changing endings with the whole word.
L25: 
L26: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L27: 
L28: [使う](使う.md) (つかう) means "use": using a machine and handling it carefully emphasize different things. [処理](処理.md) (しょり) concerns processing or disposing of a matter; 扱う need not imply that the matter has been resolved.
L29: 
L30: ## Distinctive meaning
L31: 
L32: The object and surrounding phrase determine the English translation. A shop that 商品を扱う (しょうひんをあつかう) deals in those goods, while an article that 問題を扱う covers a problem. The core idea is how someone engages with the person or thing, not simply physical contact.
L33: 
L34: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1153440, senses 1-6.

L12: # 新幹線 (しんかんせん) - Shinkansen; bullet train
L13: 
L14: ## Meaning
L15: 
L16: 新幹線 names Japan's high-speed railway system and its trains.
L17: English uses "Shinkansen" or "bullet train." It is a noun.
L18: 
L19: - 東京から京都まで新幹線で行きました。 (とうきょうからきょうとまでしんかんせんでいきました。) - I went from Tokyo to Kyoto by Shinkansen.
L20: - 新幹線の切符を予約しました。 (しんかんせんのきっぷをよやくしました。) - I booked a Shinkansen ticket.
L21: 
L22: 新幹線で marks the means of travel; 新幹線に乗る (しんかんせんにのる)
L23: means to board or ride a Shinkansen train.
L24: 
L25: ## How the kanji combine
L26: 
L27: [新](../kanji/新.md), new, qualifies 幹線 (かんせん), a trunk or main line.
L28: [幹](../kanji/幹.md) contributes a trunk or main-part association, and [線](../kanji/線.md)
L29: contributes a line or railway route. The on readings are シン + カン + セン.
L30: 
L31: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L32: 
L33: [鉄道](鉄道.md) (てつどう) is railway or rail transport more generally.
L34: [列車](列車.md) (れっしゃ) names a railway train without specifying this
L35: system. [特急](特急.md) (とっきゅう), limited express, is a service label,
L36: not an interchangeable name for the Shinkansen network.
L37: 
L38: ## Distinctive meaning
L39: 
L40: The established word names a particular Japanese railway system and its
L41: services, not every fast train anywhere in the world. Its component image,
L42: "new trunk line," should not be used to call every newly built railway
L43: a 新幹線. A sentence about 新幹線 can concern the railway or a train on it.
L44: 
L45: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1361590; 幹線, entry 1212140.
L46: Usage: [Digital Daijisen: 新幹線](https://www.weblio.jp/content/新幹線).

L10: 
L11: # 氏 (し) - respectful name suffix
L12: 
L13: ## Meaning
L14: 
L15: 氏 (し) is a respectful suffix attached directly to a person's name, as in 田中氏 (たなかし).
L16: English may translate it as Mr., Ms., or a similar title, or omit the title when natural.
L17: It does not itself specify marital status.
L18: 
L19: - 田中氏は会議に出席しました。 (たなかしはかいぎにしゅっせきしました。) - Mr./Ms. Tanaka attended the meeting.
L20: - これは山田氏の意見です。 (これはやまだしのいけんです。) - This is Mr./Ms. Yamada's opinion.
L21: 
L22: The suffix forms part of the name expression: attach は or の after it, not between the name and 氏.
L23: 
L24: 氏 (し) can also mean **clan**. In a historical clan name such as **藤原氏** (ふじわらし), "the Fujiwara clan," it identifies the lineage group rather than addressing one person as Mr. or Ms. Fujiwara.
L25: 
L26: ## How the kanji combine
L27: 
L28: [氏](../kanji/氏.md) is a single character read シ in this suffix.
L29: The same character read **うじ** is a separate dictionary identity meaning family name, lineage, or birth.
L30: Sharing the character does not make those definitions the ordinary meaning of the name suffix し.
L31: However, the clan meaning above is also recorded under し; lineage-related uses are not confined to the reading うじ.
L32: 
L33: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L34: 
L35: さん is another respectful name suffix and is useful in everyday address.
L36: 氏 fits reference to named people in reports and other relatively formal descriptions; it is not a default replacement for さん when calling to a friend.
L37: [名前](名前.md) (なまえ) is the name itself, not a respectful suffix.
L38: 
L39: ## Distinctive meaning
L40: 
L41: Read the complete expression and context: 田中氏 in the examples identifies a person respectfully, while 藤原氏 in a historical discussion can identify a clan.
L42: The dictionary's respectful label does not impose an absolute rule about the speaker's gender.
L43: Do not add both 氏 and さん mechanically to the same name.
L44: 
L45: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 2101130, senses 1-2; contrasting うじ: entry 1311010, sense 1. Clan-name example: [Digital Daijisen, 氏](https://www.weblio.jp/content/氏), the し entry.

L12: # 氏名 (しめい) - full name
L13: 
L14: ## Meaning
L15: 
L16: 氏名 means a person's full name. It is useful on forms and in requests for identifying information: **氏名を記入する** (しめいをきにゅうする), "enter one's full name."
L17: 
L18: - ここに氏名を書いてください。
L19:   (ここにしめいをかいてください。) - Please write your full name here.
L20: - 申込書には氏名と住所が必要です。
L21:   (もうしこみしょにはしめいとじゅうしょがひつようです。) - The application form requires your full name and address.
L22: 
L23: The dictionary gloss "identity" concerns identifying a person by name. 氏名 does not mean an identity card, personality, or every aspect of someone's identity.
L24: 
L25: ## How the kanji combine
L26: 
L27: [氏](../kanji/氏.md) contributes the family or clan name, and [名](../kanji/名.md) the name. The compound brings together the family and personal name: シ + メイ becomes しめい.
L28: 
L29: JMdict also records うじな. Learn **しめい** for the full-name use here; the separate public-dictionary うじな heading concerns a surname, so its explanation must not be pooled with this one.
L30: 
L31: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L32: 
L33: [名前](名前.md) (なまえ) is broader and less administrative: it can name a person, animal, or thing, and context can make it a full name. [名](名.md) (な) also overlaps in "name." [姓](姓.md) (せい), "surname," identifies only the family-name part, not the entire 氏名.
L34: 
L35: ## Distinctive meaning
L36: 
L37: 氏名 is a noun, not a verb meaning "introduce yourself." A request for 氏名 does not by itself specify a particular writing system or name order. Follow the form's separate instructions, including any フリガナ field for the pronunciation.
L38: 
L39: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1311060, sense 1.
L40: Usage: [Digital Daijisen: 氏名](https://www.weblio.jp/content/氏名), しめい heading; うじな distinguished.
