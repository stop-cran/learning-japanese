L11: # または - or; either alternative
L12: 
L13: ## Meaning
L14: 
L15: または connects alternatives: "or" or "either ... or ..."
L16: It is especially useful in notices, instructions, and clearly stated choices.
L17: 
L18: - 名前はペンまたは鉛筆で書いてください。 (なまえはペンまたはえんぴつでかいてください。) - Please write your name with a pen or pencil.
L19: 
L20: The two nouns form one alternative phrase; で then marks the writing tool.
L21: 
L22: ## How the kanji combine
L23: 
L24: Hiragana is usual; 又は is another spelling. The final は is pronounced わ,
L25: as in the particle は. The written kana reading remains または, not またわ.
L26: Do not interpret it as merely adding "also" to an unrelated sentence.
L27: 
L28: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L29: 
L30: か can also join alternatives, as in ペンか鉛筆 (ペンかえんぴつ).
L31: それとも often introduces another possibility in a question.
L32: そして means "and then; and," connecting rather than selecting alternatives.
L33: 
L34: ## Distinctive meaning
L35: 
L36: または does not automatically mean "otherwise, if not" between any two
L37: sentences. That conditional use may need そうでなければ.
L38: Whether one or several choices are allowed depends on the surrounding
L39: instructions; the word alone is not a complete selection rule.
L40: 
L41: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1524990, sense 1.

L11: 
L12: # あるいは (あるいは) - or; possibly
L13: 
L14: ## Meaning
L15: 
L16: あるいは connects alternatives as "or." It also works as an adverb meaning
L17: "perhaps" or "possibly." These uses need different English translations:
L18: one presents choices, while the other presents an uncertain possibility.
L19: 
L20: - 電話、あるいはメールで連絡してください。 (でんわ、あるいはメールでれんらくしてください。) - Please contact us by telephone or email.
L21: - あるいは、彼はもう帰ったのかもしれません。 (あるいは、かれはもうかえったのかもしれません。) - Perhaps he has already gone home.
L22: 
L23: In the first example, で belongs to the means of contact. In the second,
L24: かもしれません makes the speaker's uncertainty explicit.
L25: 
L26: ## How the kanji combine
L27: 
L28: This card uses the usual kana spelling. 或いは is a dictionary-listed kanji
L29: spelling, but there is no kanji compound to divide here. The final written は
L30: is pronounced わ: the spoken word is aru-i-wa, despite its spelling あるいは.
L31: 
L32: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L33: 
L34: [または](または.md) also connects alternatives. [それとも](それとも.md)
L35: is useful when asking which of two possibilities applies. [多分](多分.md)
L36: (たぶん) overlaps with the uncertainty use, not with the conjunction "or."
L37: There is no useful single-word antonym.
L38: 
L39: ## Distinctive meaning
L40: 
L41: Check what follows the word. A list such as 電話あるいはメール presents
L42: alternatives; a whole uncertain statement may instead mean "possibly."
L43: Do not mechanically insert "or" whenever you see あるいは.
L44: 
L45: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1586850, senses 1-2.

L12: # それとも (それとも) - or; or alternatively
L13: 
L14: ## Meaning
L15: 
L16: それとも is a conjunction introducing an alternative, especially in a question. It asks the listener to choose between possibilities: **Aですか。それともBですか。**
L17: 
L18: - お茶にしますか。それともコーヒーにしますか。 (おちゃにしますか。それともコーヒーにしますか。) - Would you like tea, or coffee?
L19: - 歩いて行きますか、それともバスに乗りますか。 (あるいていきますか、それともバスにのりますか。) - Shall we walk, or take the bus?
L20: 
L21: The alternatives can be whole actions, not just individual nouns. A speaker can also use the pattern when wondering which explanation is correct.
L22: 
L23: ## How the kanji combine
L24: 
L25: The word is usually written in kana. 其れとも is a recorded but rare kanji spelling. Learn それとも as a linking expression; there is no useful kanji decomposition in the spelling used here.
L26: 
L27: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L28: 
L29: [または](または.md) also means "or" and can list alternatives in statements or instructions. それとも particularly suits setting one possibility against another in a question. [それで](それで.md), "so; and then," expresses a different connection.
L30: 
L31: ## Distinctive meaning
L32: 
L33: Do not translate every English "or else" as それとも. In the examples it offers alternatives; it is not a threat meaning "otherwise something bad will happen." In a choice question, preserve the question endings or context that make the alternatives clear.
L34: 
L35: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1007010, sense 1.

L11: # 案内 (あんない) — guidance
L12: 
L13: ## Meaning
L14: 
L15: 案内 is guidance or information. With する, it means to guide someone or show them around. In a person-and-destination pattern, を marks the person guided and に or へ marks the destination. When showing someone around an area, を can instead mark that area; the person being guided may be left understood.
L16: 
L17: お客さんを部屋に案内します。（おきゃくさんをへやにあんないします。） — I will show the guest to the room.
L18: 
L19: 学校の中を案内してください。（がっこうのなかをあんないしてください。） — Please show me around the school.
L20: 
L21: ## How the kanji combine
L22: 
L23: 案 has meanings such as a plan or proposal; [内](../kanji/内.md) means inside. Both use on readings. Treat "guidance" as the established compound meaning, not a literal instruction to go inside.
L24: 
L25: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L26: 
L27: 説明（せつめい） is an explanation. 案内 often concerns finding one's way or using a facility; an explanation can concern any subject.
L28: 
L29: ## Distinctive meaning
L30: 
L31: 案内 can also be written information, as in 利用案内（りようあんない）, "instructions for use." A person need not physically lead you anywhere.
L32: 
L33: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1154860, senses 1–2.
L34: Usage: [Digital Daijisen, 案内](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%A1%88%E5%86%85-29545), sense 1.

L10: 
L11: # 冊子 (さっし) - booklet; pamphlet
L12: 
L13: ## Meaning
L14: 
L15: 冊子 is a book or booklet, including a pamphlet. The "booklet" gloss is useful,
L16: but it does not establish a fixed page count or require a particular binding method.
L17: 
L18: - 受付で案内の冊子をもらった。
L19:   (うけつけであんないのさっしをもらった。) - I received an information booklet at reception.
L20: - この冊子には地図も載っている。
L21:   (このさっしにはちずものっている。) - This booklet also contains a map.
L22: 
L23: ## How the kanji combine
L24: 
L25: [冊](../kanji/冊.md) supplies the book or bound-volume idea.
L26: [子](../kanji/子.md) functions as a noun-forming ending here, not as a literal child.
L27: The resulting compound names a book or booklet. Read it さっし, with a small っ:
L28: the 冊 reading サツ combines with シ in this word, rather than remaining さつし.
L29: 
L30: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L31: 
L32: [本](本.md) (ほん), [書物](書物.md) (しょもつ) and [図書](図書.md) (としょ) are broader words for books.
L33: Their meanings overlap with 冊子; a different English label does not make them mutually
L34: exclusive categories. A booklet can also be a book.
L35: 
L36: ## Distinctive meaning
L37: 
L38: 冊子 names an object, not the act of publishing or printing it.
L39: It is useful for information booklets and similar publications, but the dictionary
L40: also allows the broader book sense. Do not make "small" or "few pages" an absolute rule.
L41: 
L42: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1298530.

L11: # 箒 (ほうき) - broom
L12: 
L13: ## Meaning
L14: 
L15: 箒 is a broom, used for sweeping. The word is usually written in kana as ほうき;
L16: 帚 is another kanji spelling to recognize.
L17: 
L18: - ほうきで庭を掃きました。 (ほうきでにわをはきました。) - I swept the garden with a broom.
L19: - 掃除の後、ほうきを片付けました。 (そうじのあと、ほうきをかたづけました。) - After cleaning, I put the broom away.
L20: 
L21: ## How the kanji combine
L22: 
L23: This is a single native word, ほうき, not a multi-kanji compound.
L24: The kanji spelling is 箒. Its written shape is not a recipe for how every modern
L25: broom is made.
L26: 
L27: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L28: 
L29: [掃く](掃く.md) (はく) is the sweeping action; 箒 names the tool.
L30: [ブラシ](ブラシ.md) is a brush, and [掃除](掃除.md) (そうじ) cleaning more generally.
L31: Not every cleaning tool is a broom.
L32: 
L33: ## Distinctive meaning
L34: 
L35: In ほうきで掃く, で marks the instrument. Do not replace it with を merely
L36: because a broom is involved: 床をほうきで掃く (ゆかをほうきではく) means
L37: "sweep the floor with a broom," with を marking the floor being swept.
L38: 
L39: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1566500, sense 1.

L10: 
L11: # 本 (ほん) — book
L12: 
L13: ## Meaning
L14: 
L15: 本 is a book: 本を読む (read a book), 本屋 ほんや (bookshop), 絵本 えほん (picture book), 本棚 ほんだな (bookcase), 一冊の本 (a book).
L16: It is also a counter for long, thin things (see below).
L17: 
L18: ## How the kanji combine
L19: 
L20: A single kanji, [本](../kanji/本.md), read with its on reading ホン. The kanji pictures a tree with its root marked, the base of something; "book" is a later sense.
L21: The same character gives 日本 にほん and 本当 ほんとう (truth), where it means "origin, true".
L22: Counter: 一本 いっぽん, 二本 にほん, 三本 さんぼん; books themselves are counted with 冊 さつ, not 本.
L23: 
L24: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L25: 
L26: - Close: 書物 しょもつ (book, formal), 図書 としょ (books, as in a library).
L27: - Related: 雑誌 ざっし (magazine), 辞書 じしょ (dictionary).
L28: 
L29: ## Distinctive meaning
L30: 
L31: 本 is the ordinary word for a book. Count books with 冊 (一冊, 二冊), and count long thin things such as pencils and bottles with 本; mixing them up is a common learner mistake.

L11: 
L12: # 書物 (しょもつ) - book; written volume
L13: 
L14: ## Meaning
L15: 
L16: 書物 is a noun meaning a book or volume. It is useful when discussing books as written works, sources of knowledge, or objects to collect and read. It does not grammatically mark singular versus plural.
L17: 
L18: - 歴史に関する書物を読んでいます。 (れきしにかんするしょもつをよんでいます。) - I am reading a book about history.
L19: - この図書館には古い書物がたくさんあります。 (このとしょかんにはふるいしょもつがたくさんあります。) - This library has many old books.
L20: 
L21: In the first sentence, 書物を is the object of reading. In the second, たくさん and the setting make the plural interpretation clear.
L22: 
L23: ## How the kanji combine
L24: 
L25: [書](../kanji/書.md) contributes writing or written works, and [物](../kanji/物.md) contributes a thing. The writing-related first component specifies the kind of object. The whole word means "book," not every possible thing with writing on it.
L26: 
L27: ## Synonyms and antonyms
L28: 
L29: [本](本.md) (ほん) is the straightforward word for a book and can replace 書物 in many book-related contexts. [書類](書類.md) (しょるい) refers to documents or paperwork, which need not be bound books.
L30: 
L31: ## Distinctive meaning
L32: 
L33: 書物 fits discussion of written works, but does not establish that a book is old, scholarly, or physically large. In the library example, 古い supplies "old." Nor does the English plural gloss "books" make the Japanese noun inherently plural: 一冊の書物 (いっさつのしょもつ) is one volume.
L34: 
L35: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1344150, sense 1.
