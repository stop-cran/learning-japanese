17: # 剰 — remainder; surplus amount
18:
19: **Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
20:
21: ## Meaning and origin
22:
23: 剰 concerns a surplus or remainder. [過剰](../words/過剰.md) かじょう means excess: more than is needed or appropriate, not simply a large amount. Learn 剰 through compounds rather than treating it as an everyday standalone noun.
24:
25: **Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture a compressed [乗](乗.md)-like block beside the blade-shaped 刂, as though cutting off a surplus. This invented scene is a memory aid for the modern shape, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

32: | あまつさえ | kun | 剰え あまつさえ — besides; to make matters worse. A rare kanji spelling of a dated adverb, usually written in kana and usually adding something negative. Recognition only. |
33: | あま.り | kun | KANJIDIC2 source-only lookup reading. JMdict lists 剰り あまり as a search-only spelling for lookup, not a recommended normal spelling; the noun means a remainder or surplus. |
34: | あま.る | kun | KANJIDIC2 source-only lookup reading. The character dictionary also lists あまる, but the ordinary verb taught here is [余る](../words/余る.md) あまる — be left over; its spelling does not establish a modern 剰る example. |
35:
36: Only ジョウ is selected for reading questions. The other readings remain reference information, not alternative pronunciations of 過剰. Their exclusion does not mean that ordinary あまり or あまる is rare or invalid.

47:

49: - [余](余.md) also means surplus. The two characters' meanings overlap; do not invent a strict division between them. The useful distinction here is learning actual words and spellings.

10:
11: # あまり (あまり) - not very; not much
12:
13: ## Meaning
14:
15: あまり with a negative predicate means "not very" or "not much." The negative ending is essential to this beginner pattern; あまり alone does not supply "not."
16:
17: - この料理はあまり辛くないです。 (このりょうりはあまりからくないです。) - This dish is not very spicy.
18: - テレビはあまり見ません。 (テレビはあまりみません。) - I do not watch much television.
19:
20: With a noun or na-adjective predicate, the same pattern works: あまり静かではありません (あまりしずかではありません), "it is not very quiet."
21:
22: ## How the kanji combine
23:
24: This adverb is usually written in kana. 余り is another spelling; the related noun can mean a remainder or surplus. Do not substitute that noun meaning into every sentence.
25: The remainder noun is read あまり; that sense is not assigned to the conversational adverb あんまり in the inspected dictionary entry.
26:
27: ## Synonyms and antonyms
28:
29: [あんまり](あんまり.md) is a conversational variant of the adverb. [全然](全然.md) (ぜんぜん) with a negative can mean "not at all," a stronger denial than "not very."
30:
31: ## Distinctive meaning
32:
33: Affirmative expressions such as あまりにも can mean "excessively." Always inspect the whole construction: the negative pattern taught here limits degree or frequency rather than asserting excess.
34:
35: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1584930, especially senses 2-3.

12: # あんまり (あんまり) - not very; excessively
13:
14: ## Meaning
15:
16: あんまり is a conversational form related to [あまり](あまり.md). With a negative predicate, it means "not very" or "not much." With a positive expression of degree, especially あんまりに or あんまりにも, it can mean "excessively."
17:
18: - この料理はあんまり辛くないです。 (このりょうりはあんまりからくないです。) - This dish is not very spicy.
19: - あんまりにも暑くて、眠れません。 (あんまりにもあつくて、ねむれません。) - It is so terribly hot that I cannot sleep.
20:
21: In それはあんまりだ, "That is too much," it expresses that something is excessive or unreasonable.
22:
23: ## How the kanji combine
24:
25: This article uses kana. Although JMdict groups あまり and あんまり together under 余り, not every sense belongs to both readings. The noun meaning "remainder; leftovers" belongs to あまり, not the あんまり taught here.
26:
27: ## Synonyms and antonyms
28:
29: [全然](全然.md) (ぜんぜん) with a negative predicate expresses "not at all," a stronger denial than "not very." あまり provides a close alternative in the degree uses.
30:
31: ## Distinctive meaning
32:
33: The negative ending matters: あんまり辛くない does not say that the dish is excessively spicy. Conversely, あんまりにも暑い has no negative predicate and expresses excess. Learn the complete pattern rather than assigning a single positive or negative meaning to the adverb alone.
34:
35: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1584930, senses 2-4; sense 1 is restricted to あまり.

10:
11: # 余る (あまる) — be left over
12:
13: ## Meaning
14:
15: 余る describes an amount left beyond what was used or needed.
16: It is intransitive: the leftover thing takes が.
17: The surplus may be food, objects, or time; the verb does not by itself say whether having extra is good or bad.
18:
19: - 料理がたくさん余りました。 (りょうりがたくさんあまりました。) — A lot of food was left over.
20: - 切符が二枚余っています。 (きっぷがにまいあまっています。) — There are two tickets left over.
21:
22: 余る is a godan verb: 余ります (あまります), 余った (あまった), and 余って (あまって).
23:
24: ## How the kanji combine
25:
26: [余](../kanji/余.md) carries the surplus meaning and is read あま; る completes this native verb.
27: It is not a multi-kanji compound.
28: The on reading ヨ appears in 余分 (よぶん), "extra; surplus."
29:
30: ## Synonyms and antonyms
31:
32: 残る (のこる), "remain," is broader: it does not by itself say that the remainder exceeds what is needed.
33: 足りる (たりる) means "be enough"; its negative 足りない (たりない), "not be enough," is a useful contrast with having a surplus.
34: These comparisons concern the available amount, not who owns it.
35:
36: ## Distinctive meaning
37:
38: If time is left after finishing a task, 時間が余った (じかんがあまった) focuses on the unused time.
39: Simply saying that time remains need not imply a surplus: there may still be plenty of work to do.
40: Use 余る when the left-over or excess aspect matters.
41:

11: # 扱う (あつかう) - handle; deal with; treat
12:
13: ## Meaning
14:
15: 扱う is a transitive godan verb for handling something, dealing with a matter, or treating a person. The person or thing takes を. Its forms include 扱います, 扱って, and 扱わない.
16:
17: - この機械は丁寧に扱ってください。 (このきかいはていねいにあつかってください。) - Please handle this machine carefully.
18: - 私を子供として扱わないでください。 (わたしをこどもとしてあつかわないでください。) - Please do not treat me as a child.
19:
20: In the second example, AをBとして扱う means "treat A as B." Problems and topics can also be objects: この問題を扱う (このもんだいをあつかう), "deal with this problem."
21:
22: ## How the kanji combine
23:
24: [扱](../kanji/扱.md) carries the handling meaning and is read あつか before the ending う. This is one native verb, not a compound of several kanji. Learn its changing endings with the whole word.
25:
26: ## Synonyms and antonyms
27:
28: [使う](使う.md) (つかう) means "use": using a machine and handling it carefully emphasize different things. [処理](処理.md) (しょり) concerns processing or disposing of a matter; 扱う need not imply that the matter has been resolved.
29:
30: ## Distinctive meaning
31:
32: The object and surrounding phrase determine the English translation. A shop that 商品を扱う (しょうひんをあつかう) deals in those goods, while an article that 問題を扱う covers a problem. The core idea is how someone engages with the person or thing, not simply physical contact.
33:
34: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1153440, senses 1-6.

11: # 獣 (けもの) — beast
12:
13: ## Meaning
14:
15: 獣 means a **beast or animal**, especially with mammals in mind rather than
16: the whole biological animal kingdom. The noun does not itself specify the
17: species, sex or number.
18:
19: - 山で獣を見ました。 (やまでけものをみました。) — I saw a beast in the mountains.
20: - この山には獣がいます。 (このやまにはけものがいます。) — There are beasts in these mountains.
21:
22: で marks where the sighting took place; に marks where the animals exist.
23: The second sentence uses いる for living animals.
24:
25: **けだもの** is another dictionary-supported reading of 獣 with an overlapping
26: animal sense. Applied to a person, it can be an abusive, contemptuous word
27: for someone regarded as inhumane. This is a usage warning, not a claim that
28: けだもの can only refer to people or is an obsolete animal word.
29: The word-reading question for this article uses the primary けもの.
30:
31: Writing 獣 for けだもの uses a reading outside the standard Joyo kanji
32: reading table. Daijisen's ▽ mark identifies that spelling distinction,
33: not a rare or obsolete pronunciation.
34:
35: ## How the kanji combine
36:
37: The single character [獣](../kanji/獣.md) writes the whole native noun
38: けもの, without okurigana. Its on-reading ジュウ appears in compounds such
39: as [獣医](獣医.md) じゅうい, veterinarian; do not replace けもの with that
40: reading in the animal examples above.
41:
42: ## Synonyms and antonyms
43:
44: [動物](動物.md) どうぶつ is the broad neutral category "animal."
45: [熊](熊.md) くま, bear, and [鹿](鹿.md) しか, deer, specify kinds of animals;
46: 獣 does not identify either species. [家畜](家畜.md) かちく emphasizes
47: domesticated animals or livestock, a different way of categorizing them.
48: These categories can overlap; they are not mutually exclusive meanings
49: or a set of antonyms.
50:
51: ## Distinctive meaning
52:
53: Do not use 獣 as an automatic substitute for 動物 in every scientific or
54: everyday category statement. Conversely, the character does not make every
55: compound wild-animal-only: 獣医 names the veterinary profession.
56: Calling a person a "beast" can be dehumanizing; the animal examples do not
57: license using the word as a neutral description of someone's character.
58:
59: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
60: entry 1335590, sense 1. Its separate subculture sense is restricted to ケモノ,
61: not a general meaning assigned to every reading and spelling of 獣.
62: [Digital Daijisen, けもの](https://www.weblio.jp/content/けもの) and
63: [けだもの](https://www.weblio.jp/content/けだもの) corroborate the animal
64: overlap and the latter's abusive person use;
65: the [notation guide](https://www.weblio.jp/shogakukan.jsp#5) explains ▽.

10: # 獣医 (じゅうい) — veterinarian
11:
12: ## Meaning
13:
14: 獣医 is a **veterinarian**, a medical practitioner who treats animals.
15: It names a person or profession, not a veterinary clinic or the field
16: of study itself.
17:
18: - 私は獣医です。 (わたしはじゅういです。) — I am a veterinarian.
19: - 犬の病気について獣医に相談しました。 (いぬのびょうきについてじゅういにそうだんしました。) — I consulted a veterinarian about my dog's illness.
20:
21: In the second sentence, について marks the topic and に marks the person
22: consulted. [相談](相談.md) そうだんする means to consult or ask for advice.
23:
24: ## How the kanji combine
25:
26: [獣](../kanji/獣.md) contributes animal, and [医](../kanji/医.md) contributes
27: medical practitioner. The first component specifies whom the practitioner
28: treats: an animal doctor. Both use on readings, ジュウ + イ.
29: Keep the final い: the complete word is じゅうい, not じゅう.
30:
31: ## Synonyms and antonyms
32:
33: 獣医師 (じゅういし) is another name for the same profession.
34: [医者](医者.md) いしゃ and [医師](医師.md) いし ordinarily identify a
35: medical doctor treating people; the 獣 component makes the animal field
36: explicit. These are related professions, not antonyms.
37:
38: 獣医学 (じゅういがく) means veterinary medicine as a field of study.
39: It does not name the veterinarian.
40:
41: ## Distinctive meaning
42:
43: The animal component does not restrict the word to treating wild beasts:
44: the dog example concerns a familiar domestic animal. Learn the complete
45: professional term rather than translating each character as a separate noun.
46: English **vet** here is short for veterinarian, not veteran.
47:
48: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
49: entries 1335610 and 2095820, sense 1; 1699880 for the field of study.
50: [Digital Daijisen's 獣医 entry](https://www.weblio.jp/content/獣医)
51: corroborates the reading and equivalent professional name 獣医師.

11: # 矛 (ほこ) - spear; polearm
12:
13: ## Meaning
14:
15: 矛 names a spear or polearm: a weapon with a long shaft.
16: The dictionary also records broader "weapons; arms" and a context-specific
17: abbreviation for a parade float decorated with spears.
18:
19: - 博物館で矛を見ました。 (はくぶつかんでほこをみました。) - I saw a spear at the museum.
20: - この矛は長いです。 (このほこはながいです。) - This spear is long.
21:
22: ## How the kanji combine
23:
24: [矛](../kanji/矛.md) is a single character read ほこ in this native noun.
25: In [矛盾](矛盾.md) (むじゅん), "contradiction," the same character instead has the on reading ム.
26: Do not replace ほこ with む when reading the standalone noun.
27:
28: ## Synonyms and antonyms
29:
30: [武器](武器.md) (ぶき) is a weapon more generally.
31: 盾 (たて), "shield," provides a contrast in the conventional spear-and-shield story
32: explained in [矛盾](矛盾.md); it is not the word for the spear.
33:
34: ## Distinctive meaning
35:
36: The full dictionary family includes 鉾, 戈 and other spellings; sharing a family
37: does not make their historical shapes or every contextual use identical.
38: Its alternative とかり is restricted to 鋒, not 矛.
39: In a festival context, the abbreviated float sense does not mean every float is a spear.
40: The noun is not an instruction for using a weapon.
41:
42: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
43: entry 1603450; teaching focuses on the polearm sense, with qualified recognition of senses 2 and 4.

11:
12: # 矛盾 (むじゅん) -- contradiction; inconsistency
13:
14: ## Meaning
15:
16: 矛盾 is a contradiction or inconsistency: claims, facts or actions do
17: not fit together. As an intransitive suru verb, 矛盾する means to
18: contradict or be inconsistent with something.
19:
20: - 二つの説明は矛盾しています。 (ふたつのせつめいはむじゅんしています。) -- The two explanations contradict each other.
21: - 彼の発言は事実と矛盾しています。 (かれのはつげんはじじつとむじゅんしています。) -- His statement is inconsistent with the facts.
22:
23: Use AがBと矛盾する for A being inconsistent with B. Forms include
24: 矛盾しない, 矛盾して and 矛盾した (むじゅんしない・むじゅんして・むじゅんした).
25:
26: ## How the kanji combine
27:
28: [矛](../kanji/矛.md) means spear and 盾 shield; the readings here are ム + ジュン.
29: The dictionary traces the expression to a story in 韓非子 (かんぴし):
30: a seller praises a spear that pierces anything and a shield that nothing
31: can pierce, then cannot answer what would happen if the spear struck
32: that shield. This is an attributed explanatory story, not a report of
33: an actual tested weapon.
34:
35: 矛楯 is a recorded rare spelling.
36:
37: ## Synonyms and antonyms
38:
39: - [相違](相違.md) (そうい) and [違い](違い.md) (ちがい) concern differences or discrepancies; not every difference is contradictory.
40: - [対立](対立.md) (たいりつ) and [衝突](衝突.md) (しょうとつ) include opposition or conflict, not necessarily a logical inconsistency.
41: - [一致](一致.md) (いっち) concerns agreement or correspondence.
42:
43: ## Distinctive meaning
44:
45: Two opinions may differ without being mutually incompatible. 矛盾
46: points to their failure to fit together, not merely different tastes,
47: a quarrel between people or any isolated mistake. It is also not a
48: transitive template *Bを矛盾する for refuting an opponent.
49:
50: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
51: entry 1531090, sense 1.
52: Origin account: [Digital Daijisen via Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/矛盾).

11: # 芋 (いも) — tuber
12:
13: ## Meaning
14:
15: 芋 is a **broad food word for tubers and similar underground crops**, including potatoes, taro and yams. The whole word or situation identifies the kind; this is not a strict botanical classification.
16:
17: - 芋をゆでます。（いもをゆでます。）— I will boil the tubers.
18: - この料理には里芋を使います。（このりょうりにはさといもをつかいます。）— We use taro in this dish.
19:
20: In the first example, the context must tell you which kind is being cooked. In the second, 里芋 (さといも) specifies taro.
21:
22: ## How the kanji combine
23:
24: The single character [芋](../kanji/芋.md) writes the native noun いも. It is not a compound of separately pronounced parts.
25:
26: In じゃが芋 (じゃがいも), potato, and 里芋 (さといも), taro, 芋 supplies いも. 薩摩芋 (さつまいも), sweet potato, is another specific food name. These words are usually written in kana.
27:
28: ## Synonyms and antonyms
29:
30: じゃがいも, さといも and さつまいも identify different foods; do not use them as unrestricted substitutes for one another. 芋 is less specific. It has no direct food antonym.
31:
32: ## Distinctive meaning
33:
34: The food sense is the teaching focus. JMdict also records derogatory **yokel or bumpkin** and dismissive **dud or worthless thing** senses. These do not make いも a neutral label for a person; avoid using the insult as ordinary descriptive vocabulary.
35:
36: Do not assume the English translation must always be "potato." A recipe naming 里芋 calls for taro, not whichever tuber happens to be available.
37:
38: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1167960; comparison entries 1005930, 1550780 and 1299220.

11:
12: # 過剰 (かじょう) - excess; too much
13:
14: ## Meaning
15:
16: 過剰 means excess: more than is needed, appropriate, or allowed by a relevant standard. It is a noun and na-adjective: 過剰な + noun, or 過剰に + a verb or adjective.
17:
18: - 過剰な心配は、体によくありません。 (かじょうなしんぱいは、からだによくありません。) - Excessive worry is not good for your health.
19: - 商品を過剰に生産してしまいました。 (しょうひんをかじょうにせいさんしてしまいました。) - We ended up producing too many goods.
20:
21: 供給過剰 (きょうきゅうかじょう) means oversupply. The relevant standard may concern need rather than a legally fixed numerical limit.
22:
23: ## How the kanji combine
24:
25: [過](../kanji/過.md) contributes going beyond; [剰](../kanji/剰.md) contributes a remainder or surplus. The two ideas reinforce being beyond what is required. Their on readings are カ + ジョウ.
26:
27: ## Synonyms and antonyms
28:
29: [余分](余分.md) (よぶん) can mean extra or surplus, including a useful spare. [超過](超過.md) (ちょうか) emphasizes exceeding a limit or estimate. [不足](不足.md) (ふそく), shortage, provides a useful contrast with excess.
30:
31: ## Distinctive meaning
32:
33: 過剰 is not simply "a large amount": the amount is excessive relative to something. 過剰な反応 (かじょうなはんのう), an excessive reaction, is possible without a countable stock of objects. Do not attach it like an i-adjective.
34:
35: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1196170, sense 1; comparisons 1544520, 1429410, 1032390, 1584930, 1543910, 1544590 and 1493700.
36: Usage: [Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten on Kotobank](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%81%8E%E5%89%B0-161855), the actual 過剰 definition.
