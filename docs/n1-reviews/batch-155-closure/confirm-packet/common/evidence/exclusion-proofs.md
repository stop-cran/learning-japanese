## 卑

### Meaning paragraph at 97c49eb2

卑 is about being low: low in status, low in character, and looking down on others. KANJIDIC2 lists “lowly”, “base”, “vile”, “vulgar” and “despise”. Kanjipedia's first sense is ひくい。いやしい。, covering low rank or status (地位や身分が低い) and a mean heart (心がいやしい。下品な。); its second is いやしめる。さげすむ。 (to look down on), its third is a humble word for oneself (自分のことをいうときの謙称), and its fourth is ちかい。身近な。 (near; familiar). Digital Daijisen covers Kanjipedia's first three senses but has no ちかい sense, and adds へりくだる (to humble oneself) and 土地が低い (low-lying land). In [卑怯](../words/卑怯.md) ひきょう, JMdict's first sense is “cowardly; craven” and its second is “unfair; mean; sneaky; dirty; dastardly” (entry 1482710).

### Notes at 97c49eb2

- Joyo lists ヒ, with 卑近, 卑屈 and 卑下, いやしい, with 卑しい, いやしむ, with 卑しむ, and いやしめる, with 卑しめる. Kanjipedia marks the three kun readings with 高, a school-level mark, and lists ひくい outside the table (外); ひくい is not a KANJIDIC2 reading, so it has no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 卑, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These twelve cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [低](低.md) (“low; lower in height”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 卑 begins ひくい, and Kanjipedia lists ひくい outside the Joyo table (外); Digital Daijisen's first sense begins 身分や地位が低い (low in status or rank); JMdict's first sense of 低い ひくい is “low (rank, degree, value, content, quality, etc.)” (entry 1434180); and the 低 card lists ひく.い.
- [下](下.md) (“down, below”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 卑 includes 心がいやしい。下品な。, Digital Daijisen's second is 品性が劣る。下品だ。, and JMdict glosses 下品 げひん “vulgar; indecent; coarse; crude” (entry 1186230); JMdict also glosses 下 げ “lowness (of degree, value, etc.); inferiority” (entry 2080200), beside Digital Daijisen's 価値が劣る.
- [劣](劣.md) (“inferior; worse”): Digital Daijisen describes 卑 with 価値が劣る and 品性が劣る (inferior in value; inferior in character), and KANJIDIC2 gives 劣 “inferiority”, “be inferior to” and “be worse”.

- [謙](謙.md) (“humble; modest”): JMdict's first sense of 卑しい includes “humble”, and JMdict glosses 卑下 ひげ “self-abasement; self-depreciation; humility” (entry 1482700); Digital Daijisen gives へりくだる and 自分のことをへりくだっていう語, Kanjipedia's third sense is 自分のことをいうときの謙称 (a humble word for oneself), and KANJIDIC2 gives 謙 “humble oneself” and “be modest”.
- [凡](凡.md) (“commonplace; mediocre”): JMdict glosses 卑近 ひきん “common; simple” (entry 1482720), and KANJIDIC2 gives 凡 “commonplace” and “ordinary”.
- [底](底.md) (“bottom; base”): KANJIDIC2's “base” for 卑 and JMdict's “base” for 卑劣 share the word “base” with this title.

- [悪](悪.md) (“bad, evil”): KANJIDIC2 lists “vile” for 卑, and JMdict's second sense of 悪い わるい is “poor (quality); inferior; insufficient” and its third “evil; sinful” (entry 1151260).

## 汰

### Meaning paragraph at 97c49eb2

汰 is about sifting: washing things in water to sort the good from the bad, and washing away what is not needed. KANJIDIC2 lists “washing”, “sieving”, “filtering”, “weeding out” and “luxury”. Kanjipedia's first sense is よなげる。よりわける。水で洗ってえらびわける。 (to sift; to sort out; to wash in water and pick out), and Digital Daijisen describes 汰 as 不要のものを流し去る。良いものと悪いものをより分ける。 (washing away what is not needed; sorting good things from bad). [無沙汰](../words/無沙汰.md) ぶさた is glossed in JMdict as “not writing or contacting for a while; neglecting to write (call, visit, etc.); failing to write (call, visit, etc.); long silence” (entry 1672130), and [御無沙汰](../words/御無沙汰.md) ごぶさた as “not writing or contacting for a while; neglecting (failing) to write (call, visit, etc.); long silence” (entry 1270650).

### Notes at 97c49eb2

- Joyo lists タ only, with 沙汰 さた (JMdict entry 1291480, first sense “affair; state; incident; issue; matter”). Digital Daijisen gives only the on reading タ.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 汰, and Digital Daijisen gives none.

These eleven cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [洗](洗.md) (“wash”) and [濯](濯.md) (“wash; rinse”): KANJIDIC2 lists “washing” for 汰, Kanjipedia's first sense includes 水で洗って (washing in water), and the 洗 card lists あら.う.
- [捨](捨.md) (“discard; abandon”) and [除](除.md) (“remove; exclude”): Digital Daijisen gives 不要のものを流し去る (washing away what is not needed), and JMdict's first sense of 淘汰 is “weeding out; elimination (e.g. of unneeded employees); culling; selection”.
- [抜](抜.md) (“extract”): KANJIDIC2 lists “weeding out” for 汰, JMdict's first sense of 抜く ぬく includes “to weed” and its fifth is “to pick out; to choose; to select; to extract” (entry 1478190), and the 抜 card lists ぬ.く.
- [抽](抽.md) (“pull out; extract; abstract”): JMdict's second sense of 抽出 ちゅうしゅつ is “selection (from a group); sampling” (entry 1426180), beside the “selection” of 淘汰, and KANJIDIC2 gives 抽 “pluck” and “extract”.
- [濁](濁.md) (“muddy; cloudy; impure; voiced”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 汰 begins with にご（濁）る, with にごる listed outside the Joyo table (外); KANJIDIC2 gives にご.る for both 汰 and 濁; and JMdict's third sense of 濁る にごる is “to become impure (of one's heart, a society, etc.); to be corrupted; to be polluted” (entry 1415960).
- [汚](汚.md) (“dirty; defile”): Kanjipedia's second sense of 汰 continues 行いや志が清潔でない (conduct or aims that are not clean), which overlaps this title's “dirty”.
- [誇](誇.md) (“pride; boast”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 汰 is おご（奢）る。ほこる。 (to live in luxury; to boast), and KANJIDIC2 gives 誇 the kun reading ほこ.る.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 縫 (“sew; stitch”), 磨 (“polish; grind”), and 塗 (“paint; coat”); sewing, polishing and painting are crafts or surface work, and none means sifting or weeding out.

## 沙

### Meaning paragraph at 97c49eb2

沙 means sand, and also sifting things by washing them in water. KANJIDIC2 lists “sand”. Kanjipedia's first sense is すな。いさご。 (sand), its second is みぎわ。水辺の砂地。 (the water's edge; sandy ground by the water), and its third is よなげる。水中で洗ってよりわける。 (to wash in water and sort out), with 沙汰 as its example. Under サ, Digital Daijisen likewise gives すな and 水で洗って適否をより分ける (washing in water to sort the suitable from the unsuitable). [無沙汰](../words/無沙汰.md) ぶさた is glossed in JMdict as “not writing or contacting for a while; neglecting to write (call, visit, etc.); failing to write (call, visit, etc.); long silence” (entry 1672130), and [御無沙汰](../words/御無沙汰.md) ごぶさた as “not writing or contacting for a while; neglecting (failing) to write (call, visit, etc.); long silence” (entry 1270650).

### Notes at 97c49eb2

- Joyo lists サ only, with 沙汰 さた (JMdict entry 1291480). Kanjipedia lists シャ, すな, みぎわ and よなげる outside the table (外); みぎわ is neither a KANJIDIC2 nor a Digital Daijisen reading, so it has no table row.
- KANJIDIC2 writes this reading よなげる, without a dot, but writes よな.げる for 汰; each card copies its own form.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 沙. Digital Daijisen gives the name readings いさ and す; these are name uses, not quiz readings.

These ten cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [漠](漠.md) (“desert; vague”): JMdict entry 1593800, 砂漠 さばく “desert”, lists 沙漠 as a rarely used kanji form, Digital Daijisen gives 沙漠 as its example of 沙 meaning sand, and KANJIDIC2 gives 漠 “desert”.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 鉄 (“iron”), 銅 (“copper”), and 塩 (“salt”); iron, copper and salt are other materials, and none means sand or sifting.

## 胡

Kanjipedia's first sense is えびす。中国の北方・西方に住む異民族。 (other peoples living to the north and west of China), and Digital Daijisen's first begins 中国で、北方または西方の異民族 (in China, the other peoples to the north or west). Digital Daijisen's second sense is 外国産の (produced abroad), with 胡椒 and 胡麻 as examples, and under the reading ウ it gives いいかげんな (careless; unreliable), with 胡散 and 胡乱. Kanjipedia's second sense begins でたらめ。うたがわしいこと。 (nonsense; something doubtful). [胡椒](../words/胡椒.md) こしょう is “pepper” in JMdict (entry 1267600), which marks the word as usually written in kana.

- Kanjipedia also gives the readings えびす, でたらめ, みだり, ながいき and いずくんぞ, and Digital Daijisen gives ［訓］えびす; these are not quiz readings here.

- [励](励.md) (“encourage; be diligent”), [勧](勧.md) (“recommend; encourage”) and [奨](奨.md) (“encourage; exhort; promote”): Kanjipedia's third sense of 鼓 is ふるいたたせる。はげます。, Digital Daijisen's third is 奮い立たせる。励ます。 (to rouse; to encourage), JMdict glosses 鼓舞 こぶ (1268020) as “encouragement; inspiration; rousing; stirring up; raising (e.g. morale)”, and KANJIDIC2 gives “encourage” for 励, 勧 and 奨.
- [激](激.md) (“intense; excite”): KANJIDIC2 lists “rouse” for 鼓 and “get excited” and “incite” for 激, and JMdict glosses 激励 げきれい (1253760) as “encouragement; spurring (on); cheering (on)”, beside the “encouragement” of 鼓舞.
- [扇](扇.md) (“fan; incite”): the title's “incite” sits beside the “stirring up” of 鼓舞, and JMdict glosses the second sense of 扇ぐ あおぐ (1609000) as “to incite; to instigate” and 扇動 せんどう (1390750) as “incitement; sedition; agitation; abetting.”

The meaning-quiz distractors are 笛 (“flute; whistle”), 琴 (“koto; zither”), and 筆 (“brush; writing”); the flute and koto are other instruments, not drums, and a brush is not an instrument; none means drum or rouse.

## Exclusions removed by the fix

- 卑/底 (G1): the only link was the English word "base" (moral baseness in KANJIDIC2's 卑 and JMdict's 卑劣, versus the physical bottom in 底's title). The meanings are not the same, so the pair is no longer excluded.
- 沙/漠 (G2): the link transferred the whole word 沙漠 (a rarely used kanji form of 砂漠 さばく, "desert", JMdict 1593800) onto 沙. 沙 names sand, a material; 漠 names a desert, a region (as the 漠 card's Notes already explain), so the pair is no longer excluded.
