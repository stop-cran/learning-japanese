11: # 凝らす (こらす) — concentrate; apply effort
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 凝らす directs concentrated attention or effort. Learn it with established
16: objects marked by **を**, rather than using it for every English "focus."
17: 
18: - 目を凝らして見ました。（めをこらしてみました。）— I looked closely, straining my eyes.
19: - 工夫を凝らしました。（くふうをこらしました。）— I exercised my ingenuity.
20: 
21: 目を凝らす（めをこらす）concerns looking intently. 工夫を凝らす
22: （くふうをこらす）concerns inventive effort, not staring at an idea.
23: The verb is transitive and godan: 凝らします（こらします）,
24: 凝らして（こらして）, 凝らさない（こらさない）.
25: 
26: ## How the kanji combine
27: 
28: [凝](../kanji/凝.md) writes こ here, with らす as the written ending.
29: This is one native verb, not a compound of separate words for "concentration"
30: and "doing."
31: 
32: ## Synonyms and antonyms
33: 
34: [集中](集中.md)（しゅうちゅう）overlaps in focusing attention.
35: [注ぐ](注ぐ.md)（そそぐ）can describe devoting energy or attention.
36: Their objects and particles must still fit the particular expression.
37: 
38: [凝る](凝る.md)（こる）instead describes stiffness, absorption, or elaborate
39: care: compare ゴルフに凝る, "be really into golf," with 工夫を凝らす,
40: "exercise one's ingenuity." The latter takes 工夫 as its object.
41: 
42: ## Distinctive meaning
43: 
44: The object affects the translation. 息を凝らす（いきをこらす）means
45: "hold one's breath," not "stare at one's breath."
46: 
47: The same spelling has a separately recorded reading **こごらす**, including
48: "cause to congeal; freeze." This article teaches **こらす**.
49: 凝らせる can be the potential form こらせる of this godan verb; the dictionary
50: verb 凝らせる **こごらせる**, "cause to congeal," is a different reading.
51: The homophone 懲らす（こらす）means "punish; chastise," not concentrate.
52: 
53: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entries 1239010, 1239000, 1239020, 1309500, 2572530, 2835904 and 1428180.
54: Usage: [Digital Daijisen: 凝らす](https://kotobank.jp/word/凝らす-500443).

11: # 努力 (どりょく) — effort
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 努力 is the effort someone puts toward a goal. The intransitive する verb 努力する means "make an effort; strive."
16: It can describe sustained work, not just a single attempt.
17: 
18: - 日本語が上手になるように努力しています。 (にほんごがじょうずになるようにどりょくしています。) — I am working hard to improve my Japanese.
19: - みんなの努力が実を結びました。 (みんなのどりょくがみをむすびました。) — Everyone's efforts bore fruit.
20: 
21: In this example, a plain verb phrase followed by ように states the result the person is aiming for.
22: The noun also fits 努力が必要です (どりょくがひつようです), "Effort is needed."
23: 
24: ## How the kanji combine
25: 
26: [努](../kanji/努.md) means making an effort, and [力](../kanji/力.md) adds the idea of strength or exertion.
27: Together they name applied effort, not merely physical strength. The on readings are ド + リョク.
28: 
29: ## Synonyms and antonyms
30: 
31: 努める (つとめる) is a related native verb meaning "endeavor."
32: 苦労 (くろう), "hardship; trouble," highlights difficulty experienced rather than simply the effort someone chooses to make.
33: A task can involve both 努力 and 苦労, but the words do not describe exactly the same aspect.
34: 
35: ## Distinctive meaning
36: 
37: 努力 says that someone is trying; it does not guarantee success. It also need not be physical:
38: practicing a language or changing a habit can involve 努力.
39: Avoid translating every English "try" with it when only one tentative action is intended.
40: 
41: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1445130, sense 1 (努力); comparison entries 1240820 (努める) and 1244680 (苦労).
