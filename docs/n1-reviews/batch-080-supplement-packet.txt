11: # はっきり — clearly; distinctly
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: はっきり means clearly or distinctly, whether something is easy to hear, easy to see, or unambiguous in meaning.
16: 
17: - もう少しはっきり話してください。 (もうすこしはっきりはなしてください。) — Please speak a little more clearly.
18: - 山がはっきり見えます。 (やまがはっきりみえます。) — I can see the mountain clearly.
19: 
20: It modifies a verb directly, or with optional と: はっきりと話す. はっきりする means to be or become clear; はっきりした答え (はっきりしたこたえ) is a definite answer.
21: 
22: ## How the kanji combine
23: 
24: This mimetic expression is written in kana. Keep the small っ in はっきり; there is no kanji compound to decompose.
25: 
26: ## Synonyms and antonyms
27: 
28: 明確 (めいかく) means clear or definite, especially in statements and decisions. ぼんやり can describe a vague or blurred impression. Quiet speech can still be clear, so 大きな声 (おおきなこえ), "a loud voice," is not a synonym.
29: 
30: ## Distinctive meaning
31: 
32: はっきり言う can mean to speak frankly, not just pronounce each sound distinctly. The object and situation tell you whether the issue is sensory clarity or directness of expression.
33: 
34: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1010150.

11: # 晴れる (はれる) — clear up; be sunny
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 晴れる describes the sky clearing or sunny weather. It is intransitive: the sky or weather clears,
16: rather than someone "clearing" an object with を.
17: 晴れています (はれています) describes the resulting sunny or clear condition.
18: 
19: - 空が晴れました。 (そらがはれました。) — The sky cleared.
20: - 午後は晴れるでしょう。 (ごごははれるでしょう。) — It will probably be sunny in the afternoon.
21: 
22: ## How the kanji combine
23: 
24: [晴](../kanji/晴.md) carries the clear-weather meaning; れる completes the native verb.
25: It is an ichidan verb, meaning its endings attach after dropping the final る:
26: 晴れます (はれます), "is sunny; clears up," and 晴れない (はれない), "does not clear up."
27: 
28: ## Synonyms and antonyms
29: 
30: 晴れ (はれ), "fine weather," is the related noun: 今日は晴れです (きょうははれです),
31: "It is sunny today." 晴天 (せいてん) also names fair weather.
32: 曇る (くもる), "become cloudy," gives a useful weather contrast.
33: The nouns and verbs describe related conditions but do not attach to the rest of a sentence in the same way.
34: 
35: ## Distinctive meaning
36: 
37: 晴れる can report a change, as in 空が晴れました, or predict sunny weather without telling us
38: whether rain came first. It is not restricted to "the rain has just stopped."
39: This article concentrates on weather rather than treating every figurative use of 晴れる as identical.
40: 
41: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
42: entries 1376470 (晴れる, weather sense), 1376460 (晴れ), 1376550 (晴天) and 1457560 (曇る).

11: # 冴える (さえる) — be clear-headed; be vivid
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 冴える describes mental clarity or alertness, and a crisp, vivid quality
16: in sights, colours or sounds. Learn the whole expression with its subject:
17: 頭が冴える (あたまがさえる) means to be clear-headed; 目が冴える
18: (めがさえる) means to be wide awake, often when you would rather sleep.
19: 
20: - 今日は頭が冴えています。
21:   (きょうはあたまがさえています。) — I feel clear-headed today.
22: - 目が冴えて眠れません。
23:   (めがさえてねむれません。) — I'm wide awake and can't sleep.
24: 
25: This is an intransitive ichidan verb: the subject takes が.
26: Forms include 冴えます (さえます), 冴えた (さえた), 冴えて (さえて)
27: and 冴えない (さえない). 冴えている can describe an existing alert state.
28: The examples adapt dictionary examples to polite endings.
29: 
30: ## How the kanji combine
31: 
32: [冴](../kanji/冴.md) supplies the clarity meaning in this single-kanji native verb.
33: In the written form 冴える, 冴 represents さ and the following える completes
34: さえる. The conjugation stem is さえ, including the written え.
35: It is not a compound assembled from on readings.
36: 
37: JMdict also records 冱える (さえる) as a rarely used kanji spelling.
38: That spelling flag does not make the pronunciation さえる rare.
39: 
40: ## Synonyms and antonyms
41: 
42: [覚める](覚める.md) (さめる) focuses on waking or regaining awareness:
43: 目が覚める (めがさめる) means "wake up," whereas 目が冴える describes wakefulness.
44: [澄む](澄む.md) (すむ) and [透き通る](透き通る.md) (すきとおる)
45: overlap for clear sound, but physical transparency is not a universal meaning of 冴える.
46: 
47: For an opposite evaluation, 冴えない can mean "lacklustre" or "unsatisfactory,"
48: depending on what is being described; it is not a single antonym for every sense.
49: 
50: ## Distinctive meaning
51: 
52: 冴える can describe deft technique or a well-executed performance, not only
53: mental alertness. It does not generally mean a permanent high intelligence score,
54: nor does 目が冴える mean eyes lighting up with happiness.
55: 
56: The negative 冴えない often concerns a disappointing result, an unappealing
57: appearance or a subdued condition rather than literal blur.
58: An older, somewhat old-fashioned sense of 冴える is "become intensely cold."
59: For ordinary cooling, learn [冷える](冷える.md) (ひえる);
60: for freezing, [凍る](凍る.md) (こおる). Those verbs are not alternative
61: pronunciations of the 冴える taught here.
62: 
63: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
64: entries 1297050, 1911790, 2576510 and 2679180.
65: Usage and example bases: [Digital Daijisen: 冴える](https://kotobank.jp/word/冴える-508792),
66: especially sense 5; clarity, performance and negative uses in senses 2–7.
