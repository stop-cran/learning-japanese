26: the same moment.

29: by several crossbars, sharing one frame. This recalls the modern shape,
30: not the character's historical origin or a verified phonetic component.

32: the spreading bottom strokes seen in [典](典.md).

11: # 兼任 (けんにん) — holding concurrent posts
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 兼任 means one person holding two or more posts or duties concurrently.
16: Use 兼任する as a verb, marking the post or posts with **を**:
17: an additional post, or two posts joined with **と**.
18: 
19: - 首相が外相を兼任しています。
20:   (しゅしょうががいしょうをけんにんしています。) — The prime minister also serves as foreign minister.
21: - 彼女は社長と会長を兼任しています。
22:   (かのじょはしゃちょうとかいちょうをけんにんしています。) — She holds both the president's and the chairperson's positions.
23: 
24: These are overlapping appointments, not necessarily two tasks performed
25: at the same instant.
26: 
27: ## How the kanji combine
28: 
29: [兼](../kanji/兼.md) contributes combining or serving concurrently;
30: [任](../kanji/任.md) contributes a post or duty. The modifier 兼 describes
31: how the posts are held. The on readings are ケン + ニン.
32: This explains the compound's meaning, not the historical origin of either character.
33: 
34: ## Synonyms and antonyms
35: 
36: [兼ねる](兼ねる.md) (かねる) also describes combining posts, but extends
37: to combined functions, such as a room serving two purposes.
38: 兼務 (けんむ) is a close synonym for carrying out concurrent duties.
39: 専任 (せんにん), dedicated service in a particular post or duty, is the contrast.
40: 
41: [就任](就任.md) (しゅうにん) means taking office. It marks entry into a
42: post, not the continued holding of two posts. Compare the particles:
43: 社長に就任する (しゃちょうにしゅうにんする), "take office as president,"
44: but 外相を兼任する (がいしょうをけんにんする), "also serve as foreign minister."
45: 
46: ## Distinctive meaning
47: 
48: The point is that **one person has multiple roles**. Two colleagues sharing
49: one job do not establish 兼任 merely by sharing it. Nor does ordinary employment
50: by itself imply concurrent posts: [勤める](勤める.md) (つとめる) can simply
51: mean working for an employer.
52: 
53: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
54: entry 1256670; comparisons 1256520, 1256710, 1331780 and 1389840.
55: Usage: [Digital Daijisen, 兼任](https://www.weblio.jp/content/兼任).

12: # 兼ねる (かねる) - combine roles; cannot readily do
13: 
14: ## Meaning
15: 
16: 兼ねる is an ichidan verb with a combined-functions use and a concurrent-posts use. As a suffix after another verb's masu stem, ～かねる means to be unable or find it difficult to do something.
17: 
18: - この部屋は事務所と寝室を兼ねています。 (このへやはじむしょとしんしつをかねています。) - This room serves as both an office and a bedroom.
19: - 彼は社長と会長を兼ねています。 (かれはしゃちょうとかいちょうをかねています。) - He holds both the president's and the chairperson's positions.
20: - その条件には同意しかねます。 (そのじょうけんにはどういしかねます。) - I cannot agree to those terms.
21: 
22: In the last example, 同意する becomes 同意し + かねる. Similarly, [答える](答える.md) (こたえる) gives 答えかねる, find it difficult to answer. Attach to the masu stem, not to the dictionary form or the て-form.
23: 
24: ## How the kanji combine
25: 
26: [兼](../kanji/兼.md) concerns combining or serving in more than one capacity; ねる completes the native verb. The ordinary combined-role verb is written 兼ねる. The difficulty suffix is usually written in kana as ～かねる; its meaning is not obtained by translating the kanji literally.
27: 
28: ## Synonyms and antonyms
29: 
30: [兼任](兼任.md)する (けんにんする) describes holding concurrent posts. [勤める](勤める.md) (つとめる) means to work for or be employed, not specifically to combine positions. ～できない also expresses inability, but ～かねる is useful for difficulty or reluctance in formal responses; it is not a universal replacement in every inability sentence.
31: 
32: ## Distinctive meaning
33: 
34: The suffix is negative in meaning even without ない. Conversely, ～かねない expresses possibility: 事故につながりかねない (じこにつながりかねない), "might lead to an accident." Do not treat it as a simple positive "can do" counterpart. The combined-role verb and the difficulty suffix need separate constructions.
35: 
36: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1256520, senses 1-3; compare 1922120.
37: Usage: [Digital Daijisen and Seisenban Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, 兼ねる](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%85%BC%E3%81%AD%E3%82%8B-465451), concurrent functions and verb-stem suffix definitions.
