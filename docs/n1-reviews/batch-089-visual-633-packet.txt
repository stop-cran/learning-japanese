# 凶 — ill fortune; evil

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 凵 (no. 17) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凶 concerns ill fortune, an unfavorable outcome, or evil. In
[凶作](../words/凶作.md) きょうさく, it describes a poor harvest; in
凶悪 (きょうあく), it concerns heinousness rather than agricultural failure.
Learn the whole compound rather than translating every 凶 as "bad luck."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the crossing strokes 乂
inside the open-topped frame 凵 as a warning mark for an unfavorable outcome.
This is a memory aid for the current shape, not a historical derivation or
a claim about a phonetic component.

## Notes

- 凶 (きょう) can itself mean bad luck. In fortune-telling, 大凶
  (だいきょう) means very bad luck; 凶兆 (きょうちょう) is an ill omen.
- [悪](悪.md) also concerns badness or evil, but 凶 is not a replacement
  for the ordinary adjective 悪い (わるい).
- [凡](凡.md) concerns being ordinary; [凸](凸.md) and [凹](凹.md)
  concern raised and sunken shapes. Similar outlines do not make their
  meanings interchangeable with 凶.

# 凶作 (きょうさく) — poor harvest; crop failure

## Meaning

凶作 is a very poor harvest or crop failure. It is a noun describing
an agricultural outcome, not a verb meaning "harvest."

- 今年は米が凶作でした。 (ことしはこめがきょうさくでした。) — This year's rice harvest was very poor.
- 冷害による凶作が二年続きました。 (れいがいによるきょうさくがにねんつづきました。) — Crop failures caused by cold-weather damage continued for two years.

凶作 does not necessarily mean that nothing at all could be harvested.
It can describe a severely reduced yield.

## How the kanji combine

[凶](../kanji/凶.md) contributes an unfavorable outcome;
[作](../kanji/作.md) refers to the crop or yield in this compound.
The relationship is modifier + head: a poor crop. The on-readings are
キョウ + サク; 作's broader "make" meaning does not turn 凶作 into
a general word for bad workmanship.

## Synonyms and antonyms

不作 (ふさく) overlaps in the poor-harvest sense, but also has a broader
sense of low-quality work, including literary works. 豊作 (ほうさく),
a bumper harvest, is the useful opposite.

[作物](作物.md) (さくもつ) names the crops themselves.
[収穫](収穫.md) (しゅうかく) concerns gathering a crop or the resulting
yield; it does not by itself say that the harvest was poor.

## Distinctive meaning

凶作 focuses on a very poor crop outcome, not every disappointing result
or an unlucky event in general. Crop failure can contribute to food
shortages, but 凶作 is not itself a statement that a famine occurred.

entry 1235440; 作's crop/yield sense: entry 1297240, sense 2.
