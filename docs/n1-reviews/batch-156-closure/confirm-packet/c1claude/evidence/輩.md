# 輩 — fellows; companions

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 車 (no. 159) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

輩 is about people who belong together as fellows and companions. KANJIDIC2 lists “comrade”, “fellow”, “people” and “companions”, and Kanjipedia's first sense begins ①ともがら。やから。なかま。たぐい。 The word articles [先輩](../words/先輩.md) せんぱい and [後輩](../words/後輩.md) こうはい place people within such a group: JMdict glosses 先輩 “senior (at school, work, etc.); superior; elder; older person; predecessor” and 後輩 “junior (at work, school, etc.); younger people; younger student”.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** [非](非.md), "non-; wrong," sits on top of [車](車.md), "car, vehicle." Picture a row of fellows lined up two by two on top of a cart. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 非 or 車 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Notes

- Joyo lists ハイ only, with 輩出, 同輩 and 先輩. JMdict glosses 輩出 はいしゅつ “producing (people) in great numbers; appearing one after the other” (entry 1472980) and 同輩 どうはい “fellows; comrade; colleague; one's equal” (entry 1453260).
- Kanjipedia lists ともがら and やから outside the Joyo table. Daijisen has the headwords やから【▽族／▽輩】, ともがら【▽輩／×儕】 and ばら【▽輩／▽原／×儕】.
- KANJIDIC2 records no name readings for 輩; the Daijisen character entry gives the name とも.

These eight cards are excluded from meaning choices:

- [僚](僚.md) (“colleague”): KANJIDIC2 gives 僚 “companion”, which overlaps 輩's “companions”, and JMdict glosses 同輩 どうはい “fellows; comrade; colleague; one's equal” (entry 1453260).
- [仲](仲.md) (“relations between people”), [友](友.md) (“friend”) and [党](党.md) (“political party; faction”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 輩 includes なかま, the 党 card lists なかま, and JMdict 仲間 なかま sense 1 is “companion; fellow; friend; mate; comrade; partner; colleague; coworker; associate” (entry 1425790).
- [属](属.md) (“belong; affiliated; genus”): KANJIDIC2 gives やから as a kun reading of both 属 and 輩.
- [達](達.md) (“reach, plural suffix”): JMdict ばら, written 輩, is “suffixed to words indicating a class of people to create a plural (esp. in impolite contexts)” (entry 1472965).
- [人](人.md) (“person”): KANJIDIC2 lists “people” for 輩, and JMdict 輩 やから is “group; bunch; lot; pack; set (of people); gang” (entry 1472970).
- [類](類.md) (“kind; category”): Kanjipedia's first sense of 輩 includes たぐい, and the 類 card lists たぐ.い.

The meaning-quiz distractors are 敵 (“enemy; opponent; rival”), 臣 (“retainer; subject”), and 師 (“teacher; specialist”); none means fellows or companions.
