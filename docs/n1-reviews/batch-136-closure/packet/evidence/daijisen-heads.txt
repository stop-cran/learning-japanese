Digital Daijisen (デジタル大辞泉) headwords on Weblio, collected by daijisen_heads.py (coordinator retrieval).
For each queried form, the script fetches https://www.weblio.jp/content/<form>, records the raw-HTML sha256
prefix, and lists every kana headword whose bracketed form list contains the query. "D" marks a headword
found inside the Digital Daijisen block. "▽" before a form means Daijisen marks that reading of that form as
outside the official Joyo table; "×" marks a non-Joyo character. A ▽ applies only to the form it precedes.
