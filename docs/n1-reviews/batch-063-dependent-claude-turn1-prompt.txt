+# 模倣 (もほう) - imitation; copying
+
+## Meaning
+
+模倣 is imitation or copying of an existing action, style or work.
+模倣する is a transitive verb: the model being imitated takes を.
+
+- 子供は大人の話し方を模倣します。 (こどもはおとなのはなしかたをもほうします。) - Children imitate adults' ways of speaking.
+- この絵は、有名な作品を模倣したものです。 (このえは、ゆうめいなさくひんをもほうしたものです。) - This picture is an imitation of a famous work.
+
+Forms include 模倣します, 模倣しない and 模倣して.
+
+## How the kanji combine
+
+模 contributes modeling or copying, and [倣](../kanji/倣.md) imitation.
+The two reinforce the copying sense rather than naming the person doing it.
+The on readings here are モ + ホウ: もほう.
+
+## Synonyms and antonyms
+
+[真似](真似.md) (まね) also means imitation, and [真似る](真似る.md)
+(まねる) means to imitate or mimic.
+[倣う](倣う.md) (ならう) follows a model or precedent, normally marked
+with に; do not transfer that construction to 模倣する.
+[コピー](コピー.md), [複写](複写.md) (ふくしゃ) and [写す](写す.md)
+(うつす) overlap in copying, but copying a sheet or file is not necessarily
+imitating someone's behavior or creative style.
+Original creation can contrast with imitation; there is no single opposite
+that fits every activity.
+
+## Distinctive meaning
+
+模倣 names imitation, not simply resemblance.
+Two people can look alike without either copying the other.
+It does not by itself say whether the imitation is useful practice,
+entertainment or something being criticized. Context supplies that judgment;
+do not translate every occurrence as fraud or plagiarism.
+

+entry 1533700, sense 1; [Selected Edition of the Japanese Language Dictionary,
+模倣](https://kotobank.jp/word/模倣-142729).
