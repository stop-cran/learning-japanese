L11:
L12:# 粗い (あらい) - coarse; rough
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:粗い describes something that is not fine, closely spaced or carefully finished:
L17:large grains, an open mesh, or rough workmanship. It is an i-adjective;
L18:粗く (あらく) modifies an action.
L19:
L20:- この砂は粒が粗いです。 (このすなはつぶがあらいです。) - This sand has coarse grains.
L21:- この網は目が粗い。 (このあみはめがあらい。) - This net has a coarse mesh.
L22:- この仕事は仕上がりが粗い。 (このしごとはしあがりがあらい。) - The finish on this work is rough.
L23:
L24:## How the kanji combine
L25:
L26:粗 contributes coarseness or lack of fineness. The native reading is あらい;
L27:do not use the on reading ソ to read this adjective.
L28:
L29:## Synonyms and antonyms
L30:
L31:[ざらざら](ざらざら.md) describes a rough, gritty texture felt on a surface.
L32:[粗末](粗末.md) (そまつ) can describe poor quality or careless treatment.
L33:[細かい](細かい.md) (こまかい), "fine; detailed," contrasts with coarse grains or broad detail;
L34:[丁寧](丁寧.md) (ていねい), "careful," contrasts with rough workmanship.
L35:
L36:## Distinctive meaning
L37:
L38:[荒い](荒い.md), also あらい, concerns rough force or behavior in examples such as
L39:波が荒い (なみがあらい), "the waves are rough." Official spelling guidance instead
L40:uses 粗い for 網の目が粗い and 仕事が粗い. Do not assume that every rough surface,
L41:rough action and rough personality takes the same character.
L42:
L43:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1396910, sense 1.
L44:Spelling guidance: [Council for Cultural Affairs, 2014 report](https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/bunkashingikai/kokugo/hokoku/pdf/ijidokun_140221.pdf), entry 014, printed p.6.

L10:
L11:# 柱 (はしら) - pillar; mainstay
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:柱 is a pillar or structural post. It can also be a figurative mainstay:
L16:a person or thing that others depend on.
L17:
L18:- この柱が屋根を支えています。 (このはしらがやねをささえています。) - This pillar supports the roof.
L19:- 彼女はチームの柱です。 (かのじょはチームのはしらです。) - She is a mainstay of the team.
L20:
L21:The first example names a physical support; the second does not mean that
L22:the person is literally a building component.
L23:
L24:## How the kanji combine
L25:
L26:[柱](../kanji/柱.md) writes the whole native noun はしら without okurigana.
L27:In 支柱 (しちゅう), prop or support, the same character uses the on reading
L28:チュウ. Do not split はしら into separate readings for the visible shapes.
L29:
L30:## Synonyms and antonyms
L31:
L32:[支える](支える.md) (ささえる) is the verb support or hold up, as in the roof
L33:example. The English support also translates [援助](援助.md) (えんじょ),
L34:aid or assistance, and [賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい), agreement with a proposal.
L35:Those uses are not all names for physical posts.
L36:
L37:[棒](棒.md) (ぼう) names a stick, rod, or pole by its shape. A pole may serve
L38:as a structural post, but 棒 alone does not say that it supports anything.
L39:
L40:## Distinctive meaning
L41:
L42:The English post here means a structural support, not mail or a postbox.
L43:The figurative use emphasizes a main source of strength or stability;
L44:calling someone チームの柱 is not merely saying that the person agrees
L45:with the team's opinions.
L46:
L47:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1426480, senses 1-2; 棒, entry 1519750, sense 1.

L9:quiz_exclusions: [地, 土地, 基盤, 基礎, 台, 基地, 基, 基本]
L10:---
L11:
L12:# 地盤 (じばん) - ground; supporting base
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:地盤 is the ground, especially the underlying ground that supports a building or other structure. It is a noun. **地盤が弱い** (じばんがよわい) means that the ground is weak.
L17:
L18:- 雨で地盤が緩みました。
L19:  (あめでじばんがゆるみました。) - The rain loosened the ground.
L20:- 家を建てる前に、地盤を調べます。
L21:  (いえをたてるまえに、じばんをしらべます。) - We will investigate the ground before building a house.
L22:
L23:It also means a foundation or base, including a political support base or constituency. その候補者は、この地域に強い地盤を持っています (そのこうほしゃは、このちいきにつよいじばんをもっています) means "That candidate has a strong support base in this area." This is about support, not unusually solid soil.
L24:
L25:## How the kanji combine
L26:
L27:[地](../kanji/地.md) contributes ground and 盤 a board or supporting surface. Together they provide a meaning aid for the ground as a supporting base, not a geological derivation from the shapes. The on readings here are ジ + バン: じばん.
L28:
L29:JMdict also records ちばん for the same entry. The primary reading taught here is the source's じばん; the alternate is not evidence that this reading is wrong.
L30:
L31:## Synonyms and antonyms
L32:
L33:地面 (じめん) names the ground surface. 地盤 particularly invites attention to the ground's supporting condition. [土地](土地.md) (とち) is land or a plot.
L34:
L35:[基盤](基盤.md) (きばん) and [基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) also concern a supporting foundation. Their figurative uses overlap with 地盤; do not turn the soil association into an absolute physical-versus-abstract division.
L36:
L37:## Distinctive meaning
L38:
L39:地盤 is not simply a synonym for a room's [床](床.md) (ゆか), floor. Nor does an electoral 地盤 mean every resident agrees with a candidate. A strong support base describes established support or influence, not a guaranteed election result.
L40:
L41:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1421420, senses 1-3.
L42:Usage: [Digital Daijisen: 地盤](https://www.weblio.jp/content/地盤).
