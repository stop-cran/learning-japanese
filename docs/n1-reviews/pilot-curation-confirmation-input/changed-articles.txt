L10:
L11:# 幹 (みき) - tree trunk; backbone
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:幹 is the trunk of a tree, as distinct from its branches. It can also mean a backbone
L16:or foundation figuratively. It is a noun.
L17:
L18:- この木は幹が太いです。 (このきはみきがふといです。) - This tree has a thick trunk.
L19:- 木の幹に触れました。 (きのみきにふれました。) - I touched the tree's trunk.
L20:
L21:## How the kanji combine
L22:
L23:[幹](../kanji/幹.md) is a single character read みき in this native noun.
L24:Its on reading カン occurs in compounds such as [新幹線](新幹線.md) (しんかんせん).
L25:Those compounds do not change the reading of the standalone noun to かん.
L26:
L27:## Synonyms and antonyms
L28:
L29:[枝](枝.md) (えだ) is a branch; [木](木.md) (き) names a tree or wood more generally.
L30:For a figurative foundation, [基礎](基礎.md) (きそ) and [基盤](基盤.md) (きばん)
L31:overlap in meaning, but do not ordinarily name a tree's trunk.
L32:
L33:## Distinctive meaning
L34:
L35:English "trunk" can also mean luggage or an elephant's trunk. 幹 does not name those things.
L36:The dictionary separately records 幹/柄 read から, "trunk; stem; stalk."
L37:That separate word is not a reason to pronounce every 幹 either way.
L38:
L39:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
L40:entry 1577640, senses 1-2; separate から family 2396230.

L9:---
L10:
L11:# 凹凸 (おうとつ) - unevenness; disparity
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:凹凸 names unevenness: raised and sunken parts of a surface. It also names an
L16:imbalance or disparity. It is a noun, not an ordinary な-adjective.
L17:
L18:- この壁には凹凸があります。 (このかべにはおうとつがあります。) - This wall has an uneven surface.
L19:- 表面の凹凸を調べました。 (ひょうめんのおうとつをしらべました。) - We examined the surface's unevenness.
L20:
L21:凹凸のある表面 (おうとつのあるひょうめん) means "a surface with bumps and hollows."
L22:
L23:## How the kanji combine
L24:
L25:[凹](../kanji/凹.md) contributes the sunken part and [凸](../kanji/凸.md) the raised part.
L26:The contrasting parts together name the uneven whole. The on readings are オウ + トツ.
L27:
L28:## Synonyms and antonyms
L29:
L30:[でこぼこ](でこぼこ.md), also written 凸凹, overlaps in physical unevenness.
L31:It also has an imbalance or mismatch sense.
L32:Notice the reversed character order and learn each whole word with its reading.
L33:[ごつごつ](ごつごつ.md) emphasizes hard, angular irregularities;
L34:[ざらざら](ざらざら.md) can describe finer-grained roughness.
L35:平ら (たいら), "flat; level," provides a useful contrast for a surface.
L36:
L37:## Distinctive meaning
L38:
L39:Unevenness need not feel gritty: a smooth, wavy surface can still have raised and sunken parts.
L40:The abstract disparity sense is not literal roughness under the fingers.
L41:Do not turn every でこぼこ expression into 凹凸 merely by reversing the characters.
L42:
L43:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
L44:entry 1179280, senses 1-2; comparison 1582410.

===== words/鷹揚.md =====

L4:title: magnanimous; composed
L5:type: kango
L6:kanji: [鷹, 揚]
L7:tags: [kango, na-adjective, temperament]
L8:quiz_exclusions: [冷静, 穏やか, 悠々, しっとり, 余裕, のんき, 落ち着く, 平気]
L9:---
L10:
L11:# 鷹揚 (おうよう) - magnanimous; composed
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:鷹揚 describes a large-hearted, easygoing or composed manner, without fussing over
L16:small things. It is a な-adjective: 鷹揚な + noun, 鷹揚に + action, and 鷹揚だ/です.
L17:
L18:- 彼は小さな失敗には鷹揚です。 (かれはちいさなしっぱいにはおうようです。) - He is magnanimous about small mistakes.
L19:- 彼女は鷹揚な態度で話を聞きました。 (かのじょはおうようなたいどではなしをききました。) - She listened with an unruffled manner.
L20:
L21:## How the kanji combine
L22:
L23:[鷹](../kanji/鷹.md) names a hawk, and 揚 contributes rising or lifting.
L24:Japanese dictionary explanations associate the expression with a hawk flying broadly in the sky.
L25:That image helps with the unhurried, dignified manner; the modern adjective does not
L26:literally describe a bird. In おうよう, the on readings are オウ + ヨウ.
L27:
L28:## Synonyms and antonyms
L29:
L30:[冷静](冷静.md) (れいせい) overlaps in composure, with an emphasis on clear-headedness.
L31:[のんき](のんき.md) overlaps in an easygoing attitude, sometimes with a criticism of insufficient concern.
L32:鷹揚 need not imply carelessness. 慌てる (あわてる), "become flustered," provides a contextual contrast.
L33:
L34:## Distinctive meaning
L35:
L36:Learn the whole word: the noun [鷹](鷹.md) remains たか, not おう.
L37:JMdict also recognizes 鷹揚 read おおよう and ようよう.
L38:The alternative spelling 大様 shares おおよう, but the entry restricts おうよう and
L39:ようよう to 鷹揚. Do not freely substitute every reading into every spelling.
L40:Being 鷹揚 does not guarantee sound judgment or generosity with money in every situation.
L41:

L44:おうよう sense 1 and ようよう heading.
