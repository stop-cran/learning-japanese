L11:# 差 (さ) — difference; gap
L12:
L13:## Meaning
L14:
L15:差 is a difference between things, often a gap whose size matters.
L16:Use AとBの差 for "the difference between A and B," or 差がある (さがある) for "there is a difference."
L17:
L18:- 二人の身長には差があります。 (ふたりのしんちょうにはさがあります。) — There is a difference in height between the two people.
L19:- 値段の差は百円です。 (ねだんのさはひゃくえんです。) — The price difference is one hundred yen.
L20:
L21:## How the kanji combine
L22:
L23:There is only one kanji, [差](../kanji/差.md), read with the on reading サ.
L24:It functions as a noun by itself and also as a final element in other words.
L25:For example, 時差 (じさ), "time difference," specifies the area in which the difference occurs.
L26:
L27:## Synonyms and antonyms
L28:
L29:[違い](違い.md) (ちがい) also means "difference" and can draw attention to how things differ.
L30:差 is particularly useful when stating the size of a gap, as in the price example.
L31:This is a useful focus, not a ban on using either word in other kinds of comparison.
L32:差がない (さがない), "there is no difference," directly negates the comparison.
L33:
L34:## Distinctive meaning
L35:
L36:A 差 does not by itself tell you which side is better.
L37:A height difference is a measurable contrast, not automatically an advantage or a fault.
L38:Add the relevant quantity or context rather than assuming that "difference" means a problem.
L39:
L40:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1291070, sense 1;
L41:comparison entries 1316240 and 1158870.

L10:---
L11:
L12:# 違い (ちがい) - difference; discrepancy
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:違い is a noun for a difference or distinction. AとBの違い means "the difference between A and B."
L17:Use 違いがある for "there is a difference" and 違いが分かる for "understand or notice the difference."
L18:
L19:- この二つの言葉の違いが分かりますか。 (このふたつのことばのちがいがわかりますか。) - Do you understand the difference between these two words?
L20:- 値段には大きな違いがあります。 (ねだんにはおおきなちがいがあります。) - There is a large difference in price.
L21:
L22:An adjective can modify the noun: 大きな違い is a large difference, and 小さな違い
L23:(ちいさなちがい) is a small one.
L24:
L25:## How the kanji combine
L26:
L27:[違](../kanji/違.md) supplies the idea of differing or diverging.
L28:違い is related to the verb [違う](違う.md) (ちがう), "differ; be different."
L29:The final い is part of a noun form here, not an i-adjective ending:
L30:use 違いがある rather than conjugating it like 高い.
L31:
L32:## Synonyms and antonyms
L33:
L34:[共通](共通.md) (きょうつう), "shared; in common," gives the contrasting viewpoint.
L35:[間違い](間違い.md) (まちがい) usually identifies a mistake. A 違い can be neutral:
L36:two colors or prices need not involve anyone making an error.
L37:
L38:## Distinctive meaning
L39:
L40:The noun identifies a distinction without necessarily judging one side correct or superior.
L41:When comparing language forms, specify which aspect differs: meaning, usage, pronunciation, or politeness.
L42:AとBの違い names the comparison itself; the sentence still needs to explain what the difference is.
L43:
L44:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1158870, sense 1.

===== words/相違.md =====

L7:kanji: [相, 違]
L8:tags: [kango, noun, suru-verb, comparison]

L10:---
L11:
L12:# 相違 (そうい) - difference; discrepancy
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:相違 is a difference or discrepancy, useful in formal comparisons of
L17:opinions, accounts, or details. AとBの相違 names a difference between
L18:A and B. 相違する is intransitive: it describes things differing.
L19:
L20:- 二つの報告には相違があります。 (ふたつのほうこくにはそういがあります。) - There are discrepancies between the two reports.
L21:- 私たちの意見は相違しています。 (わたしたちのいけんはそういしています。) - Our opinions differ.
L22:
L23:相違点 (そういてん) is a point of difference.
L24:A difference need not mean that either person is dishonest or mistaken.
L25:
L26:## How the kanji combine
L27:
L28:[相](../kanji/相.md) contributes a relationship between things, and
L29:[違](../kanji/違.md) differing.
L30:Together they suggest things differing in relation to one another.
L31:The on readings are ソウ + イ.
L32:
L33:## Synonyms and antonyms
L34:
L35:[違い](違い.md) (ちがい) is an everyday noun for a difference.
L36:[差](差.md) (さ) can name a difference or gap;
L37:[異なる](異なる.md) (ことなる) is a verb meaning differ.
L38:一致 (いっち), agreement or matching, contrasts in relevant details.
L39:
L40:## Distinctive meaning
L41:
L42:相違 is not a transitive verb for deliberately changing something:
L43:相違する describes a difference rather than making an object different.
L44:Its formal tone is useful in reports, but does not require that the
L45:difference be numerically measurable.
L46:
L47:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
L48:entry 1400820, sense 1; 相異 is a rare spelling.
L49:Usage: [Digital Daijisen, 相違](https://www.weblio.jp/content/相違).

L10:---
L11:
L12:# ぶつぶつ - grumbling; muttering; small bumps
L13:
L14:## Meaning
L15:
L16:ぶつぶつ describes low muttering or grumbling. As a noun it can also refer to small bumps or spots on the skin; this is a different use, not a sound made by a rash.
L17:
L18:- 彼はぶつぶつ文句を言っています。 (かれはぶつぶつもんくをいっています。) - He is grumbling complaints.
L19:- 腕にぶつぶつができました。 (うでにぶつぶつができました。) - Small bumps appeared on my arm.
L20:
L21:## Synonyms and antonyms
L22:
L23:[ぼそぼそ](ぼそぼそ.md) overlaps in subdued speech, while [ぶうぶう](ぶうぶう.md) also has a complaining use. [文句](文句.md) (もんく) and [不平](不平.md) (ふへい) name complaints rather than necessarily their quiet delivery.
L24:
L25:## Distinctive meaning
L26:
L27:Learn ぶつぶつ言う (いう), mutter or grumble, and ぶつぶつができる, develop bumps. The dictionary also records simmering and cutting into small pieces: 鍋がぶつぶつ煮えている (なべがぶつぶつにえている), the pot is simmering, and ぶつぶつと切る (きる), cut into pieces. Do not force the complaint translation into those constructions. ブツブツ is another recorded spelling.
L28:
L29:Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1011200, senses 1-2; senses 3-4 for the additional cutting/simmering uses; [Digital Daijisen](https://www.weblio.jp/content/ぶつぶつ).
