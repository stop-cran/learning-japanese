# 儀 — ceremony; formal conduct

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

儀 appears in words for ceremonies and formal conduct:
[儀式](../words/儀式.md) ぎしき is a ceremony or ritual,
while [礼儀](../words/礼儀.md) れいぎ concerns manners or etiquette.
Learn the whole word rather than translating every 儀 as "ceremony."

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and 義 on the right.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a person standing beside an elaborate ceremonial pattern.
This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation of the character.

## Notes

- 儀 also has a "matter; affair; case" branch. In formal letters and notices,
  this suffix typically follows a noun referring to oneself or one's own side,
  adding a humble nuance: 私儀（わたくしぎ）, "as for me."
  This is a formal written use, not an ordinary conversational suffix.
- Not all compounds concern conduct: 地球儀（ちきゅうぎ）is an Earth globe,
  a model of the Earth, not a ceremony.
- [式](式.md) overlaps in the ceremony sense; [礼](礼.md) overlaps in manners
  and also names a bow. These are useful comparisons, not interchangeable characters.
- 義 alone lacks the left-hand 亻. It also has the reading ギ, but its meanings
  include righteousness and meaning; a shared sound does not make the spellings interchangeable.
- [億](億.md), "hundred million," [優](優.md), "gentle; superior," and
  [倍](倍.md), "multiple; double," share 亻 but have different right-hand shapes.
