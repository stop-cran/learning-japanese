11: # 冒す (おかす) — brave danger; take a risk
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 冒す means braving danger or proceeding despite a known difficulty. In 危険を冒す (きけんをおかす), "take a risk," を marks the danger being faced.
16: It is a transitive verb, not a noun meaning an adventure.
17: 
18: - 危険を冒して山に登りました。 (きけんをおかしてやまにのぼりました。) — I climbed the mountain despite the danger.
19: - 危険を冒す必要はありません。 (きけんをおかすひつようはありません。) — There is no need to take a risk.
20: 
21: The second sentence says that taking the risk is unnecessary; it does not say that the situation is safe.
22: 
23: ## How the kanji combine
24: 
25: [冒](../kanji/冒.md) writes おか, with す as okurigana. There is no second kanji to read as a compound.
26: This is a godan verb: 冒します (おかします), 冒して (おかして), 冒さない (おかさない).
27: 
28: ## Synonyms and antonyms
29: 
30: [冒険](冒険.md) (ぼうけん) is the related noun "adventure; risky venture"; 冒険する means to venture or have an adventure.
31: [危険](危険.md) (きけん) names danger, whether or not anyone chooses to face it.
32: Avoiding danger contrasts with braving it, but 冒す has other senses too, so no single opposite covers every use.
33: 
34: ## Distinctive meaning
35: 
36: Do not translate every occurrence as voluntarily taking a risk. 病に冒される (やまいにおかされる), "be afflicted by a disease," uses the passive of the harm/affliction sense.
37: Here the person is affected by the illness, not choosing to brave it. This sense can also concern harmful chemicals.
38: The dictionary also records desecrating something sacred and assuming someone else's surname; these are separate senses, not explanations of 危険を冒す.
39: 
40: Two other verbs share the reading おかす. 犯す, written with [犯](../kanji/犯.md), is used in 罪を犯す (つみをおかす), "commit a crime."
41: 侵す concerns invading or infringing and also has a harm/affliction use overlapping with 冒す.
42: Choose the spelling from the expression and meaning, not from the sound alone; this comparison is not an absolute division of every use.
43: 
44: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entries 1519820 (senses 1–4), 1868850, 1855070, 1519830, 1218560, 1359800 and 1481550.
45: Spelling and usage: [Digital Daijisen: 冒す](https://www.weblio.jp/content/冒す).

11: # 冒険 (ぼうけん) - adventure; risky venture
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 冒険 is an adventure or venture into something uncertain. 冒険する means to venture or have an adventure. It can also describe a risky attempt whose success is doubtful.
16: 
17: - 子供のころ、冒険の物語が好きでした。 (こどものころ、ぼうけんのものがたりがすきでした。) - As a child, I liked adventure stories.
18: - 準備なしで店を開くのは冒険です。 (じゅんびなしでみせをひらくのはぼうけんです。) - Opening a shop without preparation is a risky venture.
19: 
20: The first example suggests exploration and excitement; the second emphasizes risk. Calling something 冒険 does not promise an enjoyable outcome.
21: 
22: ## How the kanji combine
23: 
24: [冒](../kanji/冒.md) contributes braving or venturing, while [険](../kanji/険.md) contributes danger or difficulty. The relationship is roughly venturing into danger. The on readings are ボウ + ケン.
25: 
26: ## Synonyms and antonyms
27: 
28: [旅行](旅行.md) (りょこう) is travel, which need not involve the uncertainty associated with an adventure. [危険](危険.md) (きけん) means danger; it can exist without anyone undertaking a venture. Safety can contrast with the risk, but it is not a direct opposite of every adventure story.
29: 
30: ## Distinctive meaning
31: 
32: 冒険 can be literal or figurative. It may concern a journey, a business decision, or trying something outside one's usual experience. Check whether the speaker is presenting the uncertainty positively as adventure or negatively as a gamble. The noun itself does not settle that evaluation.
33: 
34: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1519830, senses 1-2.
