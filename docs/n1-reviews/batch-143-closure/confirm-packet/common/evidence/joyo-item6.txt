Gloss (author's): the table says the parenthesised forms are the traditional Kangxi-dictionary forms. Where the packet's text extract (evidence\joyo-pages.md in the original packet) shows a CJK compatibility ideograph in parentheses, for example U+FA42 after 既 and U+F929 after 朗, that is how the PDF text layer encodes the parenthesised form; the table does not call these forms "compatibility ideographs".
