12: # 勘定 (かんじょう) - reckoning; bill
13: 
14: ## Meaning
15: 
16: 勘定 is counting or reckoning, and 勘定する means to count or calculate something. It also names a bill or the settlement of a bill, especially in expressions with お.
17: 
18: - 人数を勘定しました。 (にんずうをかんじょうしました。) - I counted the people.
19: - お勘定をお願いします。 (おかんじょうをおねがいします。) - The bill, please.
20: 
21: In the first example, を marks what is counted. In the second, the speaker is asking to settle the bill, not requesting an abstract mathematics calculation.
22: 
23: ## How the kanji combine
24: 
25: [勘](../kanji/勘.md) contributes examining or reckoning, and [定](../kanji/定.md) contributes settling or determining. Together they suggest determining an amount or account. The on readings are カン + ジョウ.
26: 
27: ## Synonyms and antonyms
28: 
29: [計算](計算.md) (けいさん) covers calculation more broadly. [会計](会計.md) (かいけい) overlaps in payment and accounting contexts. [支払う](支払う.md) (しはらう) directly means "pay," whereas 勘定 can name the amount or the counting process.
30: 
31: ## Distinctive meaning
32: 
33: 勘定に入れる (かんじょうにいれる) means "take into account"; 勘定に入れない means "leave out of the reckoning." This is the consideration sense, not a general replacement for every use of "think." Context distinguishes counting participants, paying a restaurant bill, and allowing for a factor in a decision.
34: 
35: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1210750, senses 1-3.

11: # 料金 (りょうきん) - fee; charge; fare
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 料金 is a noun for the charge for a service, facility, or use of something. Depending on the setting, English "fee," "charge," or "fare" fits.
16: 
17: - 駐車場の料金を払いました。 (ちゅうしゃじょうのりょうきんをはらいました。) - I paid the parking charge.
18: - 子供の料金はいくらですか。 (こどものりょうきんはいくらですか。) - How much is the charge for children?
19: 
20: Nの料金 identifies the service or category being charged for. 料金がかかる (りょうきんがかかる) means "a fee is charged/it costs money," and 料金に含まれる (りょうきんにふくまれる) means "be included in the charge."
21: 
22: ## How the kanji combine
23: 
24: [料](../kanji/料.md) contributes a fee or charge, and [金](../kanji/金.md) contributes money. Together they specify money charged for something. Their on readings are リョウ + キン.
25: 
26: ## Synonyms and antonyms
27: 
28: [値段](値段.md) (ねだん) and [価格](価格.md) (かかく) concern price more broadly. 運賃 (うんちん) specifically names a transportation fare. [無料](無料.md) (むりょう), "free of charge," contrasts with having to pay, rather than being an opposite kind of fee.
29: 
30: ## Distinctive meaning
31: 
32: The word does not state what a charge includes. Ask about additional charges or included services rather than assuming a quoted 料金 is the entire final cost. This is especially useful when reading a hotel's, parking facility's, or rental service's price information.
33: 
34: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1554280, sense 1.
