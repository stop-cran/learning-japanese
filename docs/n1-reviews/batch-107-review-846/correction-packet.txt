12: # 勘 (かん) - intuition; hunch
13: 
14: ## Meaning
15: 
16: 勘 is a noun for intuition, a hunch, or an intuitive feel for something. It can concern a quick guess or an ability developed through experience. Use **勘で** for doing something by intuition rather than through an explicit calculation.
17: 
18: - 答えが分からなかったので、勘で選びました。 (こたえがわからなかったので、かんでえらびました。) - I did not know the answer, so I chose by intuition.
19: - 私の勘が当たりました。 (わたしのかんがあたりました。) - My hunch was right.
20: 
21: 勘が当たる (かんがあたる) says the hunch proves right; 勘が外れる (かんがはずれる) says it proves wrong.
22: 
23: ## How the kanji combine
24: 
25: [勘](../kanji/勘.md) is a single kanji used as the whole noun, with the on reading カン. There is no multi-kanji compound to break down.
26: 
27: ## Synonyms and antonyms
28: 
29: [予想](予想.md) (よそう) is an expectation or prediction and need not come from intuition. [判断](判断.md) (はんだん) names a judgment; 勘 can be one basis for making it. Neither word is an exact opposite.
30: 
31: ## Distinctive meaning
32: 
33: 勘がいい, "have good intuition," and 勘が鋭い (かんがするどい), "have sharp intuition," describe sensitivity to clues. They do not assert supernatural powers or guarantee correctness. 久しぶりで勘が鈍った (ひさしぶりでかんがにぶった) means one's feel for an activity has become rusty after a long break.
34: 
35: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1210590, sense 1.

12: # 感覚 (かんかく) - sense; sensation
13: 
14: ## Meaning
15: 
16: 感覚 names a bodily sensation or a sense for perceiving and judging something. Useful noun patterns are Nの感覚, "the sensation/sense of N," and 感覚がある, "have sensation/a sense."
17: 
18: - 寒くて指の感覚がなくなりました。 (さむくてゆびのかんかくがなくなりました。) - My fingers lost feeling because of the cold.
19: - 暗い部屋では時間の感覚が分からなくなります。 (くらいへやではじかんのかんかくがわからなくなります。) - In a dark room, I lose my sense of time.
20: 
21: The first concerns physical feeling; the second concerns one's awareness of time. The word does not by itself mean an emotion.
22: 
23: ## How the kanji combine
24: 
25: [感](../kanji/感.md) contributes feeling, and [覚](../kanji/覚.md) contributes perceiving or becoming aware. The related meanings reinforce each other in カン + カク.
26: 
27: ## Synonyms and antonyms
28: 
29: [気持ち](気持ち.md) (きもち) can describe feelings or how something feels, but 指の感覚 specifically concerns sensation in the fingers. [気分](気分.md) (きぶん) concerns mood or how one feels overall. These words overlap in English translation without being substitutes in every phrase.
30: 
31: ## Distinctive meaning
32: 
33: 感覚 can be bodily or abstract: 距離の感覚 (きょりのかんかく) means a sense of distance. Read the attached noun to identify what is being perceived. Do not confuse it with [間隔](間隔.md) (かんかく), a different word meaning an interval or gap.
34:

12: # 勘定 (かんじょう) - reckoning; bill
13: 
14: ## Meaning
15: 
16: 勘定 is counting or reckoning, and 勘定する means to count or calculate something. It also names a bill or the settlement of a bill, especially in expressions with お.
17: 
18: - 人数を勘定しました。 (にんずうをかんじょうしました。) - I counted the people.
19: - お勘定をお願いします。 (おかんじょうをおねがいします。) - The bill, please.
20: 
21: In the first example, を marks what is counted. In the second, the speaker is asking to settle the bill, not requesting an abstract mathematics calculation.
22: 
23: ## How the kanji combine
24: 
25: [勘](../kanji/勘.md) contributes examining or reckoning, and [定](../kanji/定.md) contributes settling or determining. Together they suggest determining an amount or account. The on readings are カン + ジョウ.
26: 
27: ## Synonyms and antonyms
28: 
29: [計算](計算.md) (けいさん) covers calculation more broadly. [会計](会計.md) (かいけい) overlaps in payment and accounting contexts. [支払う](支払う.md) (しはらう) directly means "pay," whereas 勘定 can name the amount or the counting process.
30: 
31: ## Distinctive meaning
32: 
33: 勘定に入れる (かんじょうにいれる) means "take into account"; 勘定に入れない means "leave out of the reckoning." This is the consideration sense, not a general replacement for every use of "think." Context distinguishes counting participants, paying a restaurant bill, and allowing for a factor in a decision.
34:

11: # 支払う (しはらう) — pay
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 支払う means to pay. The bill, fee, or amount paid takes を; the means of payment can take で.
16: It is a verb, not a noun requiring する.
17: 
18: - 料金をカードで支払います。 (りょうきんをカードでしはらいます。) — I will pay the fee by card.
19: - 家賃はもう支払いました。 (やちんはもうしはらいました。) — I have already paid the rent.
20: 
21: The past form is 支払った (しはらった); the negative is 支払わない (しはらわない).
22: 
23: ## How the kanji combine
24: 
25: JMdict explicitly identifies [支](../kanji/支.md) as **ateji** here: a character used for its sound,
26: not a reliable literal contribution such as "support."
27: [払](../kanji/払.md) corresponds to はらう, "pay." Do not explain the word as "support + pay."
28: 
29: The spelling mixes し with the native reading はらう. Under this repository's on-first mixed-reading convention
30: it is filed as `kango`; that is a display classification, not a claim that this is an ordinary two-on-reading compound
31: or proof of its historical derivation.
32: 
33: ## Synonyms and antonyms
34: 
35: [払う](払う.md) (はらう) also means "pay": 料金を払う (りょうきんをはらう) is another way to say "pay the fee."
36: But 払う also has nonfinancial uses, such as ほこりを払う, "brush off the dust."
37: 支払う is not a replacement for 払う in that dusting expression.
38: 
39: ## Distinctive meaning
40: 
41: Learn 支払う as a payment verb with its own spelling. Its extra character does not make it a combination
42: of providing support and making a payment. もう支払いました (もうしはらいました), "I have already paid,"
43: reports that the payment was made; it is not a general expression for handing any object to somebody.
44:

12: # 給与 (きゅうよ) - pay; provision of money or goods
13: 
14: ## Meaning
15: 
16: 給与 commonly names pay received for work. It can include salary and allowances. It also means providing money or goods; in that use, 給与する is a transitive verb.
17: 
18: - 毎月の給与は銀行口座に振り込まれます。
19:   (まいつきのきゅうよはぎんこうこうざにふりこまれます。) - The monthly pay is deposited into a bank account.
20: - 会社は従業員に制服を給与しています。
21:   (かいしゃはじゅうぎょういんにせいふくをきゅうよしています。) - The company provides uniforms to its employees.
22: 
23: The second sentence illustrates the provision sense, not wages made out of uniforms. A recipient can take に and the money or goods を.
24: 
25: ## How the kanji combine
26: 
27: [給](../kanji/給.md) and [与](../kanji/与.md) both contribute giving or providing. Their related meanings reinforce each other. The on readings are キュウ + ヨ; keep the long vowel in きゅう.
28: 
29: ## Synonyms and antonyms
30: 
31: [給料](給料.md) (きゅうりょう) closely overlaps the employment-pay use. [支給](支給.md) (しきゅう) focuses on supplying or paying something to recipients; [供給](供給.md) (きょうきゅう) is another supply/provision word.
32: 
33: ## Distinctive meaning
34: 
35: Do not enforce an exceptionless rule that 給料 always excludes allowances while 給与 always includes every possible benefit. Ordinary meanings overlap, and a document's payroll definitions require its own context. The provision sense is useful in formal descriptions of what an organization furnishes, not only cash payments.
36:

12: # 支払 (しはらい) - payment
13: 
14: ## Meaning
15: 
16: 支払 is a noun meaning payment. 支払い is another spelling of the same word, with the final い written explicitly. Both are read しはらい.
17: 
18: - 支払期限は来週の月曜日です。 (しはらいきげんはらいしゅうのげつようびです。) - The payment deadline is next Monday.
19: - 支払いは現金でお願いします。 (しはらいはげんきんでおねがいします。) - Please pay in cash.
20: 
21: 支払方法 (しはらいほうほう) is the payment method. 支払いを済ませる (しはらいをすませる) means to finish making a payment. The related verb is [支払う](支払う.md) (しはらう), "pay," with the amount or charge marked by を.
22: 
23: ## How the kanji combine
24: 
25: This noun is formed from 支払う. JMdict identifies [支](../kanji/支.md) as ateji in that verb: a character used for its sound, not a literal "support" component. [払](../kanji/払.md) corresponds to はらい, from 払う (はらう), "pay."
26: 
27: The spelling combines シ with the native はらい. Like 支払う, this mixed reading is grouped under `kango` here; that display category does not make it an ordinary all-on-reading compound or establish its historical origin. Learn 支払 and 支払い as alternative written forms of one noun.
28: 
29: ## Synonyms and antonyms
30: 
31: [会計](会計.md) (かいけい) can refer to settling a restaurant bill, but also to accounting. [料金](料金.md) (りょうきん) is the charge to be paid, not the payment itself. [収入](収入.md) (しゅうにゅう), income, describes money coming in rather than directly negating 支払.
32: 
33: ## Distinctive meaning
34: 
35: 支払 identifies payment, not a promise that it has already happened. 支払予定 (しはらいよてい) is a payment schedule or plan, while 支払済み (しはらいずみ) means paid. The surrounding expression tells you the status.
36: 
37: Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1594480, sense 1; entry 1310300, spelling note on the related verb.

12: # 勘違い (かんちがい) - mistaken assumption; misunderstanding
13: 
14: ## Meaning
15: 
16: 勘違い is a mistaken understanding, assumption, or guess.
17: 勘違いする means to get the wrong idea.
18: Use clause + と勘違いする to state what someone mistakenly believed.
19: 
20: - 会議は明日だと勘違いしていました。
21:   (かいぎはあしただとかんちがいしていました。) - I had mistakenly thought the meeting was tomorrow.
22: - 私の勘違いでした。
23:   (わたしのかんちがいでした。) - I had the wrong idea.
24: 
25: In the first example, と follows the full mistaken claim, 明日だ.
26: XをYと勘違いする means mistaking X for Y.
27: 
28: ## How the kanji combine
29: 
30: [勘](../kanji/勘.md) contributes judgment or intuition, and
31: [違](../kanji/違.md) contributes being different or mistaken.
32: The word combines カン with native 違い (ちがい), related to
33: [違う](違う.md). This is a mixed on-first compound, not two on readings.
34: 
35: ## Synonyms and antonyms
36: 
37: [誤解](誤解.md) (ごかい) overlaps in a misunderstanding or wrong
38: interpretation. [間違い](間違い.md) (まちがい) is broader:
39: a copying error need not involve a mistaken belief about the situation.
40: These distinctions do not make every use mutually exclusive.
41: 
42: ## Distinctive meaning
43: 
44: 勘違い does not necessarily imply deliberate deception.
45: The speaker may genuinely have assumed the wrong date, person, or meaning.
46: Do not infer that 勘違い is always harmless or only concerns another
47: person's words; context determines the consequences and what was mistaken.
48:
