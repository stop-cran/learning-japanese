1. Moderate; comprehensibility; kanji-4.txt:37-38. The `-づ.く`/`-ず.く` rows can mis-pair spellings and hide the usual force sense. JMdict 1585205: 力尽く is both ちからづく and ちからずく, glossed “(brute) force” and “using all one’s might,” often 〜で. JMdict 2065420: 納得尽く is only なっとくずく; なっとくづく is only 納得づく. JMdict 2436480: ずく/づく is a noun suffix, usually kana, “relying entirely on …”. “The family” and “the hyphen marks a suffix” do not separate this from 食べ尽くす. Remedy: replace both rows with: suffix after a noun, usually kana, “relying entirely on …”. 力ずく is ちからずく and often means “by force” (力ずくで); 力尽く may be ちからずく or ちからづく. 納得ずく／納得尽く is なっとくずく (“with consent”); なっとくづく is written 納得づく, not 納得尽く. The hyphen means “not word-initial,” not the verb suffix in 食べ尽くす. Confidence: high.
