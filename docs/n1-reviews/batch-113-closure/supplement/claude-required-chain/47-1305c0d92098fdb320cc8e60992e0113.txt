# 屋 — roof, shop

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 尸 (no. 44) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

屋 occurs in words for buildings, roofs and shops. As や after a product, it can name a shop or the person running that business.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the top-and-left 尸 shape as a shelter wrapping around a compact foundation. This is a present-day memory aid, not a verified origin story.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| オク | on | 屋上 おくじょう — rooftop |
| や | kun | 本屋 ほんや — bookshop; [部屋](../words/部屋.md) へや — room |

## Common words

- [部屋](../words/部屋.md) へや — room
- 本屋 ほんや — bookshop
- 屋上 おくじょう — rooftop

## Notes

[室](室.md) shares the lower shape 至, but has 宀 above it instead of 尸. [店](店.md) means a shop; 屋 has broader uses, so 部屋 does not mean a shop.

Learn 部屋 as へや, not ぶや. The reading of the whole word is not a regular on+on combination. Compare [家](家.md) いえ for a house or home.

For the endings 者, 家, 師, 士, 手, 員 and 屋 compared, see [the article on occupation and role endings](../articles/occupation-and-role-endings.md).

# 掘 — dig; excavate

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

掘 concerns digging or excavating. [掘る](../words/掘る.md) ほる can describe digging a hole or digging something out of the ground. 採掘 さいくつ names mining or extraction by digging.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside 屈 and imagine a hand bending down to dig. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical radical is 手; its visible left-hand form here is 扌.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| クツ | on | [採掘](../words/採掘.md) さいくつ - mining; digging |
| ほ.る | kun | [掘る](../words/掘る.md) ほる - dig; excavate |

## Common words

- [採掘](../words/採掘.md) さいくつ - mining; digging
- [掘る](../words/掘る.md) ほる - dig; excavate
- 掘削 くっさく - digging out; excavation

## Notes

- [掘る](../words/掘る.md) is a transitive godan verb: 掘ります ほります, 掘って ほって, 掘らない ほらない. Its final る does not make it ichidan.
- 掘削 くっさく has a small っ. Learn the complete pronunciation rather than joining クツ and サク without the sound change.
- Compare [届](届.md), reach or deliver: its right-hand interior is not the same as 掘's.

# 掲 — put up; display; publish

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

掲 concerns raising something where people can see it, and by extension putting it into print. [掲示](../words/掲示.md) けいじ is posting a notice. 掲載 けいさい, "publication (e.g. of an article in a newspaper); carrying; running; insertion; printing; posting (e.g. on the web)," uses 掲 for the item that is printed or posted. The verb 掲げる かかげる covers both the physical sense ("to put up (a notice, sign, etc.); to hoist; to raise; to display") and the printed sense ("to publish; to print; to carry (e.g. an article)").

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the side form of [手](手.md), "hand," at the left and a variant of 曷 at the right, drawn as a wide 日-like box (曰) above 匂. Imagine a hand holding a sign up toward the sun. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 曷 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケイ | on | [掲示](../words/掲示.md) けいじ — posting a notice; notice |
| かか.げる | kun | 掲げる かかげる - to put up (a notice, sign, etc.); to hoist; to raise; to display; to publish; to print; not selected for questions here |

In 掲示, 掲 supplies ケイ and 示 supplies ジ. In 掲げる, 掲 writes かか and げる is okurigana. Practice selects ケイ. かか.げる is retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with 掲げる, but that word has no linked article yet, so the reading is not selected for questions. This is a teaching selection, not a claim that かか.げる is invalid, obsolete or rare.

## Common words

- [掲示](../words/掲示.md) けいじ — posting a notice; notice

## Notes

- [示](示.md), "indicate," is the other kanji in 掲示. 掲示 puts a notice up, and 示 contributes showing it.
- [景](景.md), "scenery," also reads ケイ, but it is only a sound-alike with a different shape and meaning. [損](損.md), "loss; damage," and [掘](掘.md), "dig; excavate," share 扌 at the left but have different right-hand shapes.
- [刊](刊.md), "publish; issue," [置](置.md), "put; place," and [刷](刷.md), "print; brush," overlap with 掲 in the ideas of publishing, printing and putting something somewhere: JMdict gives "to publish; to print; to carry" as a sense of 掲げる. 掲 is raising or posting something so that it is seen, so do not treat "publish," "print" or "put" as exclusive to any one character.

# 寝 — lie down; sleep

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寝 connects sleep with lying down or going to bed. [寝る](../words/寝る.md) ねる can describe sleeping,
but also the decision to retire for the night: もう寝ます。 (もうねます。) — I am going to bed now.
The character also appears in the word for a bedroom, 寝室 しんしつ.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a sleeping space under 宀, with the small left-hand strokes tucked beside a larger right-hand shape.
This is a visual aid only; no historical derivation is proposed here.

## Readings

The table focuses on two useful readings. The metadata retains the other KANJIDIC2 readings.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ね.る | kun | [寝る](../words/寝る.md) ねる — sleep; go to bed |
| シン | on | 寝室 しんしつ — bedroom |

## Common words

- [寝る](../words/寝る.md) ねる — sleep; lie down; go to bed
- 寝室 しんしつ — bedroom

## Notes

[眠る](../words/眠る.md) ねむる focuses on sleeping; 寝る also has a lying-down sense, so the words are not identical.
[起きる](../words/起きる.md) おきる supplies the practical contrast of getting up or waking.
For the shape, compare the contents under the roofs of [寒](寒.md), [察](察.md), and [字](字.md).

# 宝 — treasure; valuables

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宝 たから means treasure. 宝石 ほうせき is a gem or precious stone: 宝 contributes the precious or treasure association, while 石 supplies stone. Treasure need not be a gemstone.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine a valuable 玉-shaped object kept under 宀, a roof. This modern-shape scene is a mnemonic, not a verified account of the character's earlier forms.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ホウ | on | [宝石](../words/宝石.md) ほうせき - gem; precious stone |
| たから | kun | [宝](../words/宝.md) たから - treasure |

## Common words

- [宝](../words/宝.md) たから - treasure
- [宝石](../words/宝石.md) ほうせき - gem; precious stone

## Notes

- The standalone native noun is たから; ホウ appears in the compound 宝石.
- The lower shape includes the dot of 玉. Compare [王](王.md), king, without turning the shape comparison into an origin claim.
