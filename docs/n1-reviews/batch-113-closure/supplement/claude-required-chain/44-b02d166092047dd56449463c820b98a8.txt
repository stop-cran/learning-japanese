# 震 — quake; tremble

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 雨 (no. 173) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

震 concerns quaking, shaking or trembling. 地震 is an earthquake; 震える describes shivering or trembling.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the [辰](辰.md)-shaped lower strokes shaking under the 雨-shaped top. This is a modern-shape association, not a historical or phonetic account.

The canonical radical 雨, no. 173, is the upper part.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シン | on | [地震](../words/地震.md) じしん - earthquake |
| ふる.う | kun | 震う ふるう - shiver; shake (dated) |
| ふる.える | kun | [震える](../words/震える.md) ふるえる - shiver; tremble |
| ふる.わせる | kun | 震わせる ふるわせる - make something quiver; shake |
| ふる.わす | kun | 震わす ふるわす - make something quiver; shake |

## Common words

- [地震](../words/地震.md) じしん - earthquake
- [震える](../words/震える.md) ふるえる - shiver; tremble

## Notes

- 震う ふるう is marked dated in the pinned source. 震える is a separate intransitive ichidan verb; the source does not give it that dated flag.
- 震わせる is transitive and ichidan, while 震わす is transitive and godan. They can describe making something shake; do not infer their conjugations from an English shake gloss alone.
- Compare [農](農.md), agriculture, whose lower part is also 辰-shaped. Its upper strokes and complete meaning differ.

# 零 — zero; spill

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 雨 (no. 173) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

零 writes zero and also occurs in words for spilling or overflowing. The number reading and the spilling verbs are separate vocabulary uses.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the 雨-shaped top over 令 as drops spilling down until nothing remains. This is only a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical explanation.

The canonical radical is 雨, no. 173, at the top.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| レイ | on | [零](../words/零.md) れい - zero |
| ぜろ | kun | 零 ゼロ - zero; the word source writes the reading in katakana |
| こぼ.す | kun | [零す](../words/こぼす.md) こぼす - spill; shed tears (usually written in kana) |
| こぼ.れる | kun | [零れる](../words/零れる.md) こぼれる - spill; overflow (usually written in kana) |

## Common words

- [零](../words/零.md) れい - zero
- [零点](../words/零点.md) れいてん - zero marks; a mathematical zero
- 零下 れいか - below zero
- [零れる](../words/零れる.md) こぼれる - contents spill out; the word article contrasts 水がこぼれる with 水をこぼす

## Notes

- KANJIDIC2 puts ぜろ in its kun-reading list; JMdict writes the corresponding zero reading ゼロ in katakana. The table preserves both source notations. A character dictionary's grouping is not an etymological classification.
- The linked 零 article teaches れい; the separate [ゼロ](../words/ゼロ.md) article explains that number word. Do not assume every expression permits either reading merely because both word identities mean zero.
- 零す こぼす is transitive and godan, while 零れる こぼれる is intransitive and ichidan. JMdict marks their displayed spilling senses usually written in kana: こぼす and こぼれる. Do not infer that every source sense has that same flag.
- Compare [冷](冷.md), cold/chill, which also has a 令-shaped part. Below zero can describe a temperature, but cold is not the meaning of every 零 word.

# 糸 — thread; yarn

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

糸 concerns thread, yarn or string. The character by itself does not identify whether the fiber is silk, cotton or wool.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the upper turns and lower spreading strokes as a small bundle of thread. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a claim about the earliest drawing or historical structural type.

糸 itself is the canonical key, no. 120. Its narrower left-side form, often written ⺯, appears in [細](細.md), [絡](絡.md) and [線](線.md).

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | 絹糸 けんし - silk thread |
| いと | kun | [糸](../words/糸.md) いと - thread; yarn |

## Common words

- [糸](../words/糸.md) いと - thread; yarn
- [毛糸](../words/毛糸.md) けいと - wool yarn
- 絹糸 けんし - silk thread

## Notes

- 毛糸 preserves いと after 毛's kun reading け; the spelling is read けいと, not もうし. 絹糸 also has the dictionary reading きぬいと, so けんし is not the only reading of that spelling.
- The separate dictionary entry 糸 し includes thread and a numerical one-ten-thousandth sense. It is not a rule to pronounce the linked native noun いと as し in any sentence.
- Compare [紙](紙.md), paper, and [線](線.md), line/track. Sharing the thread key is not proof that every resulting word denotes a textile.

# 約 — promise; approximately

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

約 has two particularly useful jobs: agreements and approximate amounts.
[約束](../words/約束.md) やくそく is a promise or arrangement; 約一時間 (やくいちじかん) means "about one hour."
The second use does not describe a promise.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the left-hand 糸 tying an agreement to the small 勺 shape on the right.
This recalls the promise meaning only; it is not an etymology explaining every sense.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヤク | on | [約束](../words/約束.md) やくそく — promise; 予約 よやく — reservation |
| ヤク | on | 約 やく — approximately |

The source's kun readings remain in the metadata; these examples all use ヤク.

## Common words

- [約束](../words/約束.md) やくそく — promise; arrangement
- [倹約](../words/倹約.md) けんやく — thrift; frugality
- 予約 よやく — reservation; booking
- 約 やく — about; approximately, before a quantity

## Notes

約束を守る (やくそくをまもる) means "keep a promise."
約 does not mark an exact measurement when it appears before a number.

[紙](紙.md), [組](組.md), and [終](終.md) share the compact left-hand 糸.
Identify 約 by its relatively simple 勺 on the right.
