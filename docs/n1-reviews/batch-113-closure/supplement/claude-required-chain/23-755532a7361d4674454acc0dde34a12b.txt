# 誕生日 (たんじょうび) - birthday

## Meaning

誕生日 is a birthday, the annual date celebrating a person's birth.

- 誕生日はいつですか。 (たんじょうびはいつですか。) - When is your birthday?
- 誕生日に本をもらいました。 (たんじょうびにほんをもらいました。) - I received a book for my birthday.

Use a person's name or relationship + の + 誕生日: 母の誕生日 (ははのたんじょうび), "my mother's birthday."

## How the kanji combine

[誕](../kanji/誕.md) and [生](../kanji/生.md) form 誕生 (たんじょう), "birth." [日](../kanji/日.md), "day," adds the native reading ひ, voiced to び. This is an on-first mixed reading, not three on readings.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[生年月日](生年月日.md) (せいねんがっぴ) is a full date of birth, including the year. 記念日 (きねんび) is an anniversary or commemorative day more generally.

## Distinctive meaning

A birthday is not necessarily a party. 誕生日おめでとう means "Happy birthday"; 誕生日 names the occasion, not the act of congratulating someone.

# 謝る (あやまる) — apologize

## Meaning

謝る means "apologize". Mark the person receiving the apology with に. The mistake or conduct being apologized for can be marked with を.

- 友達に謝りました。  
  ともだちにあやまりました。  
  "I apologized to my friend."
- 遅れたことを先生に謝りました。  
  おくれたことをせんせいにあやまりました。  
  "I apologized to the teacher for being late."

## How the kanji combine

[謝](../kanji/謝.md) represents apologizing here, with the native reading あやま; る is okurigana. This is a godan verb despite ending in る: 謝ります (あやまります), 謝って (あやまって), 謝らない (あやまらない).

## Synonyms and antonyms

すみません and ごめんなさい are things people say to apologize; 謝る names the action. 許す (ゆるす), "forgive", describes the other person's possible response, not an opposite action.

## Distinctive meaning

謝る reports an apology without specifying its exact words or whether it was accepted. Do not confuse it with the identically pronounced 誤る (あやまる), "make a mistake": making a mistake and apologizing are separate actions.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1323010, sense 1.

# 軍隊 (ぐんたい) - armed forces; troops

## Meaning

軍隊 is a noun for organized armed forces or troops. It can refer to a country's military organization or to troops acting as a group. Useful patterns include 軍隊に入る (ぐんたいにはいる), "join the armed forces," and 軍隊を送る (ぐんたいをおくる), "send troops."

- 兄は軍隊に入りました。 (あにはぐんたいにはいりました。) - My older brother joined the armed forces.
- 政府は被災地に軍隊を送りました。 (せいふはひさいちにぐんたいをおくりました。) - The government sent troops to the disaster area.

The second example shows that the word names the organization, not necessarily a combat action.

## How the kanji combine

[軍](../kanji/軍.md) supplies the military meaning; [隊](../kanji/隊.md) is an organized body or unit. 軍 modifies 隊: an organized military force. The on readings combine as グン + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[軍](軍.md) (ぐん) also names an army or military force and appears in many compounds. 兵隊 (へいたい) can focus on soldiers rather than the organization. These are related terms, not interchangeable in every title or compound.

## Distinctive meaning

English "army" may mean specifically land forces. 軍隊 is broader and does not itself identify a military branch. It also is not the ordinary word for any organized team: the military component matters.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1248710, sense 1.

# 需要 (じゅよう) - demand; need

## Meaning

需要 is a noun for demand or need, including demand for goods or services in economics. Use **X の需要** or **X に対する需要** to identify what is wanted. **需要がある** means "there is demand."

- この地域では保育サービスの需要が高まっています。 (このちいきではほいくサービスのじゅようがたかまっています。) - Demand for childcare services is growing in this area.
- 需要が供給を上回りました。 (じゅようがきょうきゅうをうわまわりました。) - Demand exceeded supply.

Useful combinations include 需要が増える (じゅようがふえる), "demand increases," and 需要が減る (じゅようがへる), "demand decreases."

## How the kanji combine

[需](../kanji/需.md) contributes needing or seeking; [要](../kanji/要.md) contributes need or requirement. The components reinforce the idea of something being wanted or required. The readings are ジュ + ヨウ: じゅよう, not じゅうよう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[供給](供給.md) (きょうきゅう), "supply," is the economic counterpart. [必要](必要.md) (ひつよう) describes necessity or need more broadly. [依頼](依頼.md) (いらい) is a request or commission addressed to someone.

## Distinctive meaning

需要 is not the act of saying "please do this." It describes the demand that exists, whether or not a particular person has voiced a request. This distinction prevents confusing market demand with requesting help. The homophone 受容 (じゅよう), "acceptance," has different kanji and meaning.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1330450, senses 1-2.

# 飢える (うえる) - starve; hunger for

## Meaning

飢える means to be hungry, famished, or starving. It can also describe
being starved of something figuratively, such as affection or knowledge.
It is an intransitive ichidan verb.

- 食べ物がなく、人々は飢えていました。 (たべものがなく、ひとびとはうえていました。) - There was no food, and the people were starving.
- その子は愛情に飢えています。 (そのこはあいじょうにうえています。) - That child is starved of affection.

In the figurative construction, に marks what is keenly lacking.
Forms include 飢えます, 飢えて, and 飢えない.

## How the kanji combine

[飢](../kanji/飢.md) has hunger associations and writes う in this verb;
える is the written ending. For conjugation, drop the final る and
keep うえ-, as in うえます. 餓える is another dictionary spelling,
and 饑える is marked rare. The dictionary also lists かつえる,
restricted to 飢える and 餓える, not 饑える.

## Synonyms and antonyms

お腹が[空く](空く.md) (おなかがすく) is useful for everyday
"become hungry." お腹が[ぺこぺこ](ぺこぺこ.md) describes strong hunger
in a conversational way. 満腹 (まんぷく), "a full stomach," contrasts
with the physical hunger use, not every figurative lack.

## Distinctive meaning

The examples illustrate serious deprivation and strong figurative need.
The dictionary's broader "be hungry" gloss does not mean that every
ordinary lunchtime statement should use this word.
[植える](植える.md) also reads うえる but means "plant something";
the spelling and meaning are unrelated to hunger.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
entry 1224080, senses 1-2.

# 飢饉 (ききん) - famine; chronic shortage

## Meaning

飢饉 names a famine or crop failure. It concerns a serious lack of food, not simply feeling hungry before a meal. The word can also describe a chronic shortage of another resource.

- その地域では、干ばつの後に飢饉が起きました。
  (そのちいきでは、かんばつのあとにききんがおきました。) - A famine occurred in that region after the drought.
- 雨が少なく、水飢饉が続いています。
  (あめがすくなく、みずききんがつづいています。) - There has been little rain, and the water shortage continues.

## How the kanji combine

[飢](../kanji/飢.md) concerns hunger or starvation, while 饉 concerns famine and crop failure. The related meanings reinforce each other. The on readings are キ + キン.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[不足](不足.md) (ふそく) is shortage or insufficiency more generally. [飢える](飢える.md) (うえる) is the verb "starve; hunger for"; 飢饉 names the famine or shortage rather than an individual's action or state.

## Distinctive meaning

水飢饉 (みずききん), "water shortage; drought," illustrates the extended resource-shortage use. Do not infer that every use concerns food alone. The dictionary also recognizes 飢きん and marks 饑饉 as a rare kanji spelling; this card uses 飢饉.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1591080, senses 1-2; compare 1737360.
