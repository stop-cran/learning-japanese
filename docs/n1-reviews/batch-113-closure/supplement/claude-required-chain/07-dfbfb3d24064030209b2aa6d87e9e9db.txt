# 恩 — favor; debt of gratitude

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

恩 concerns a kindness received and the gratitude or obligation that goes
with it. [恩](../words/恩.md) おん is a favor or a debt of gratitude, and
[恩恵](../words/恩恵.md) おんけい is the benefit or blessing that comes from
something. 恩人 おんじん is a benefactor, 恩返し おんがえし is repaying a
favor, and 恩師 おんし is a teacher to whom one owes a debt of gratitude.

The Digital Daijisen character entry glosses 恩 as めぐみ (grace;
blessing), いつくしみ (affection; benevolence) and 情け (compassion).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [因](因.md) above
[心](心.md). Imagine a reason or cause resting on the heart: a kindness you
keep in mind until you repay it. This is a memory picture for the modern
shape, not a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| オン | on | [恩](../words/恩.md) おん - favor; debt of gratitude; [恩恵](../words/恩恵.md) おんけい - benefit; blessing |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading; めぐみ appears there only as a name
reading. Practice selects オン. The official Joyo table lists オン, with
恩情, 恩人 and 謝恩 as examples.

## Common words

- [恩](../words/恩.md) おん - favor; debt of gratitude
- [恩恵](../words/恩恵.md) おんけい - benefit; blessing
- 恩人 おんじん - benefactor
- 恩返し おんがえし - repaying a favor
- 恩師 おんし - mentor; one's former teacher

## Notes

- [因](因.md), "cause; factor," is the upper component of 恩, so the two
  can look alike at a glance. [思](思.md), "think, feel," and
  [患](患.md), "be ill; suffer," share 心 at the bottom but differ in
  meaning.
- A benefit or blessing, as in 恩恵, overlaps [利](利.md), "benefit;
  advantage," [享](享.md), "enjoy; receive benefits," [福](福.md), "good
  fortune," and [幸](幸.md), "happiness; good fortune."
- The English stem "favor" is shared with [好](好.md), "liking;
  favorable," and [良](良.md), "good; favorable," although those kanji
  describe preference or quality, not a kindness received.
- Thanks and gratitude overlap [礼](礼.md), "bow; thanks," and
  [謝](謝.md), "apologize; thank." This card declares the pair 恩–謝.
- An obligation or debt of gratitude overlaps [義](義.md), "righteousness;
  duty; meaning," and [役](役.md), "role; duty." These direct meaning
  comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as identical in every
  compound.

# 盆 — tray; basin

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 皿 (no. 108), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

盆 names a shallow, dish-like shape. [盆](../words/盆.md) ぼん is a tray, and
the same word is also the Obon festival, the mid-summer observance for
ancestors' spirits. [盆地](../words/盆地.md) ぼんち is a basin, such as a
plain surrounded by mountains. Polite お盆 おぼん can mean either the
festival or a tray, and 盆栽 ぼんさい is a bonsai, a miniature potted plant.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives four senses: a shallow vessel
with a wide mouth, as in 盆栽; a hollow shape, as in 盆地; a flat implement
for carrying tableware; and the Urabon festival, as in 旧盆.

JMdict's entry for 盆 also lists an untagged sense, "family; household,"
and a slang sense, "gambler's den." Both are beyond this card's practical
tray-and-basin focus. JMdict marks the festival sense as an abbreviation of
盂蘭盆 うらぼん.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [分](分.md) above
[皿](皿.md). Imagine a dish with food divided into portions on it: a tray.
This is a memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ボン | on | [盆](../words/盆.md) ぼん - tray; Obon festival; [盆地](../words/盆地.md) ぼんち - basin surrounded by mountains |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects ボン. In お盆, お is a
polite prefix and 盆 is ぼん. The official Joyo table lists ボン, with 盆栽,
盆地 and 旧盆 as examples.

## Common words

- [盆](../words/盆.md) ぼん - tray; Obon festival
- [盆地](../words/盆地.md) ぼんち - basin surrounded by mountains
- お盆 おぼん - Obon festival; tray (polite)
- 盆栽 ぼんさい - bonsai; miniature potted plant

## Notes

- [分](分.md), "minute, part," is the upper component of 盆.
  [盗](盗.md), "steal," shares 皿 at the bottom, and [貧](貧.md), "poor;
  impoverished," shares 分 at the top; both differ in meaning.
- [皿](皿.md), "dish; plate," is the lower component, and a tray carries
  dishes. [器](器.md), "vessel; instrument," overlaps the shallow-vessel
  sense.
- A basin is a hollow shape, so 盆地 overlaps [凹](凹.md), "concave;
  sunken," and [谷](谷.md), "valley; ravine," as a landform.
- The Obon festival overlaps [祭](祭.md), "festival; ritual; worship."
- JMdict's untagged "family; household" sense overlaps [家](家.md), "house,
  home," [戸](戸.md), "door; counter for households," [族](族.md), "tribe,
  family group," [氏](氏.md), "clan; family name," and [姓](姓.md),
  "surname; family name." These direct meaning comparisons are excluded
  from choices, not treated as identical in every compound.

# 誕 — birth; be born

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

誕 appears in words about being born. [誕生](../words/誕生.md) たんじょう is
the birth of a person and, more widely, the coming into existence of
something new, such as a new city. [誕生日](../words/誕生日.md)
たんじょうび is a birthday. 生誕 せいたん is the birth of a famous person,
and 降誕 こうたん is the birth of a saint or monarch.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: telling great lies or
nonsense, in compounds such as 虚誕, 荒誕 and 妄誕 that lie outside this
card's practical vocabulary, and a child being born, as in 誕生, 降誕 and
生誕. KANJIDIC2 glosses 誕 as nativity, be born, declension, lie and be
arbitrary; its "lie" is a falsehood, not lying down.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [言](言.md) at the left beside
[延](延.md). Imagine words of announcement carried on from person to person
to say that a baby has arrived. This is a memory picture for the modern
shape, not a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| タン | on | [誕生](../words/誕生.md) たんじょう - birth; coming into existence; [誕生日](../words/誕生日.md) たんじょうび - birthday |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects タン. In 誕生日, 日 is
read び, a voiced form of ひ, after 誕生 たんじょう. The official Joyo table
lists タン, with 誕生 and 生誕 as examples.

## Common words

- [誕生](../words/誕生.md) たんじょう - birth; coming into existence
- [誕生日](../words/誕生日.md) たんじょうび - birthday
- 生誕 せいたん - birth (of a famous person)
- 降誕 こうたん - birth (of a saint or monarch)

## Notes

- [延](延.md), "prolong; postpone," is the right-hand component of 誕.
  [談](談.md), "converse; consult," and [誌](誌.md), "written record;
  journal," share 言 at the left but differ in meaning.
- Being born overlaps [生](生.md), "life, birth," and [産](産.md),
  "produce; give birth."
- The coming-into-existence sense of 誕生, which JMdict glosses as
  "creation; formation; birth; origin; making," overlaps [創](創.md),
  "originate; create," [作](作.md), "make, produce," [元](元.md), "origin,
  former," [本](本.md), "book, origin," and [原](原.md), "original; plain."
- The "lie" in KANJIDIC2's glosses is a falsehood, but the English word is
  spelled like the "lie" of [寝](寝.md), "lie down; sleep," which is
  excluded for that reason. These direct meaning comparisons are excluded
  from choices, not treated as identical in every compound.
