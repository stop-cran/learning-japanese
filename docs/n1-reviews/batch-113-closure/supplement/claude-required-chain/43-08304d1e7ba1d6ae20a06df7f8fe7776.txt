# 談 — converse; consult

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

談 belongs to talking things over. [相談](../words/相談.md) そうだん is consultation or asking for advice;
会談 かいだん is a meeting for talks. These words do not require the same relationship
between speakers: consulting someone and holding a conference are different situations.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** two 火 shapes make 炎 beside 言. Imagine a lively conversation,
while remembering that real 談 words need not imply anger. This is a mnemonic, not etymology.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ダン | on | [相談](../words/相談.md) そうだん — consultation; 会談 かいだん — talks; conference |

## Common words

- [相談](../words/相談.md) そうだん — consultation; asking for advice
- 会談 かいだん — talks; conference
- [冗談](../words/冗談.md) じょうだん — joke; jest

## Notes

先生に相談する (せんせいにそうだんする) means "consult a teacher."
The teacher is marked with に in this pattern.
[許](許.md), [調](調.md), and [記](記.md) share the speech component, but none has
the two stacked 火 shapes on the right.

# 誌 — written record; journal

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

誌 concerns a written record or journal. It appears in words for magazines, logs and other publications rather than naming an individual spoken statement.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture 言 on the left beside 志, with 士 over 心, as thoughts being put into a written journal. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | [雑誌](../words/雑誌.md) ざっし - magazine; periodical |

## Common words

- [雑誌](../words/雑誌.md) ざっし - magazine; periodical
- 日誌 にっし - journal; log
- 週刊誌 しゅうかんし - weekly magazine; weekly journal

## Notes

- 雑誌 is ざっし and 日誌 is にっし. Their small っ is part of each complete word's pronunciation, not an extra character reading to add to the metadata.
- The left-hand radical is 言. Compare [持](持.md), with 扌, and [時](時.md), with 日; similar-looking right-side strokes do not give these characters the journal meaning.

# 除 — remove; exclude

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (as left-hand 阝, no. 170) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

除 concerns taking something away or leaving it out. [除く](../words/除く.md) のぞく can describe physical removal,
but also an exception: 日曜日を除く (にちようびをのぞく), "excluding Sundays."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine clearing the 余-shaped piece away from the left-hand 阝.
This is a visual reminder of removal, not an etymology.

## Readings

The table selects useful readings; the metadata preserves the complete source reading list.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| のぞ.く | kun | [除く](../words/除く.md) のぞく — remove; exclude |
| ジョ | on | 除外 じょがい — exclusion |
| ジ | on | 掃除 そうじ — cleaning |

## Common words

- [除く](../words/除く.md) のぞく — remove; exclude
- 除外 じょがい — exclusion
- 掃除 そうじ — cleaning
- [削除](../words/削除.md) さくじょ — deletion; erasure

## Notes

掃除 uses ジ, not ジョ; learn its whole reading そうじ.
Excluding a day from a schedule does not remove that day from the calendar: the scope of the statement changes.
[降](降.md) shares left-hand 阝 but not the 余 on its right.

# 象 — elephant; image; shape

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 豕 (no. 152) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

象 names an elephant but also concerns an image, form or appearance. The ショウ compounds below are not about literal elephants.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the long sweeping lower strokes as an elephant's trunk and legs below its compact upper body. This modern-shape picture is not a historical derivation.

KANJIDIC2 indexes 象 under 豕, no. 152. The complete character looks different from that standalone radical; do not replace the canonical key with the animal word 象 itself.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ショウ | on | [印象](../words/印象.md) いんしょう - impression; [抽象](../words/抽象.md) ちゅうしょう - abstraction |
| ゾウ | on | [象](../words/象.md) ぞう - elephant |
| かたど.る | kun | 象る かたどる - model on; take the shape of; represent (usually written in kana) |

## Common words

- [象](../words/象.md) ぞう - elephant
- [印象](../words/印象.md) いんしょう - impression
- [現象](../words/現象.md) げんしょう - phenomenon
- [抽象](../words/抽象.md) ちゅうしょう - abstraction

## Notes

- 象 ぞう, elephant, and 象 しょう, form or appearance, have separate dictionary entries. The linked word article 象 teaches the animal reading.
- 象る is a godan verb with transitive and intransitive uses; JMdict marks it usually written in kana as かたどる. The same family also lists 模る and 形取る, but that does not make every word containing those kanji interchangeable.

# 陽 — sunshine

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (as 阝) (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

陽 connects sunlight with brightness and warmth. In [太陽](../words/太陽.md) たいよう it names the sun;
in 陽気 (ようき), brightness can describe a cheerful person rather than light.
太陽が出ています。 (たいようがでています。) — The sun is out.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture sunlight reaching the little 日-shaped window in the right-hand block.
The left 阝 is the visible form of the indexing radical 阜. This is a memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヨウ | on | [太陽](../words/太陽.md) たいよう — sun; 陽光 ようこう — sunlight |

The metadata also records the kun reading ひ; the compounds here use ヨウ.

## Common words

- [太陽](../words/太陽.md) たいよう — the sun
- 陽光 ようこう — sunlight
- 陽気 ようき — cheerful; as a noun, also weather or season

## Notes

[院](院.md) and [際](際.md) share the left 阝 but have different right-hand blocks.
[場](場.md) shares 陽's right-hand shape, with 土 rather than 阝 on the left.
[日](日.md) can also mean "sun," so its meaning overlaps with 陽's sunlight-related meanings.
