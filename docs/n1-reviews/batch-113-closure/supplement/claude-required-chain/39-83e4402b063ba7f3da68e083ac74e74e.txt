# 慣 — accustomed

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慣 concerns becoming accustomed to something through experience. In [慣れる](../words/慣れる.md) なれる,
something that once felt unfamiliar becomes easier to deal with. This is familiarity, not necessarily mastery.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the heart-side 忄 settling beside the dense 貫 shape as it becomes familiar.
This is a memory aid for the modern form, not a historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| な.れる | kun | [慣れる](../words/慣れる.md) なれる — get used to |
| な.らす | kun | 慣らす ならす — accustom; train |
| カン | on | 習慣 しゅうかん — habit; custom |

## Common words

- [慣れる](../words/慣れる.md) なれる — become accustomed to
- 慣らす ならす — accustom someone or something
- 習慣 しゅうかん — habit; customary practice

## Notes

仕事に慣れる (しごとになれる) means "get used to the work": に marks what becomes familiar.
慣らす presents the action of getting someone or something accustomed instead.

[買](買.md) shares the lower 貝 shape but lacks 忄. [持](持.md) has 扌, not 忄;
[海](海.md) has 氵 and a different right side. These are partial visual comparisons, not related meanings.

# 憎 — hate; detest

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

憎 concerns hatred or detestation. 憎む にくむ means to hate, and 憎しみ にくしみ is the noun hatred. 憎悪 ぞうお names hatred or abhorrence.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 忄 beside 曽 and imagine a repeated grievance beside the heart. This is a modern-shape memory story, not a verified historical or phonetic account.

The canonical radical is 心; the left-hand shape in 憎 is 忄.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ゾウ | on | 憎悪 ぞうお - hatred; abhorrence |
| にく.む | kun | [憎む](../words/憎む.md) にくむ - hate; detest |
| にく.い | kun | [憎い](../words/憎い.md) にくい - hateful; detestable |
| にく.らしい | kun | [憎らしい](../words/憎らしい.md) にくらしい - hateful; odious |
| にく.しみ | kun | 憎しみ にくしみ - hatred |

## Common words

- [憎む](../words/憎む.md) にくむ - hate; detest
- [憎い](../words/憎い.md) にくい - hateful; also ironically admirable
- [憎らしい](../words/憎らしい.md) にくらしい - hateful; also ironically darling
- 憎しみ にくしみ - hatred
- 憎悪 ぞうお - hatred; abhorrence

## Notes

- Keep the long vowel in 憎悪 ぞうお. It is not ぞお.
- 憎い can also express admiration in another sense, and 憎らしい has an ironic darling sense. Their negative table glosses do not exhaust every contextual use.
- Compare [増](増.md), increase: it has 土 instead of 忄. The similar right side does not make their meanings interchangeable.

# 怪 — suspicious; uncanny

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

怪 concerns suspicion, unexplained strangeness, and uncanny beings. [怪しい](../words/怪しい.md)
あやしい can describe a doubtful story without suggesting anything supernatural.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** put an uneasy feeling in the left-hand 忄 beside the unfamiliar
又-over-土 shape on the right. The indexing radical is 心 in its side form 忄.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カイ | on | 怪物 かいぶつ — monster; 妖怪 ようかい — supernatural being |
| ケ | on | 怪 け — an uncanny occurrence or apparition, a historical dictionary use; [怪我](../words/怪我.md) けが — injury, an ateji spelling |
| あや.しい | kun | [怪しい](../words/怪しい.md) あやしい — suspicious; doubtful |
| あや.しむ | kun | 怪しむ あやしむ — suspect; regard as suspicious |

## Common words

- [怪しい](../words/怪しい.md) あやしい — suspicious; doubtful
- 怪しむ あやしむ — suspect
- 怪物 かいぶつ — monster
- [怪我](../words/怪我.md) けが — injury; do not translate its characters literally

## Notes

[性](性.md) also starts with 忄, but has 生 on the right. [経](経.md) shares the 又-over-土 shape,
with 糸 instead of 忄. [忙](忙.md) has the much simpler 亡 on the right.

For 怪 compared with [妖](妖.md), [奇](奇.md), and [疑](疑.md), see
[strange, suspicious, and rare](../articles/ayashii-and-related-words.md).
