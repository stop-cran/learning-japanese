# 担 — shoulder; take on

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

担 concerns carrying on the shoulder or taking on a burden or responsibility. [担ぐ](../words/担ぐ.md) かつぐ and 担う になう can both describe shouldering something. 担当 たんとう concerns being in charge of an assigned task or area.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside 旦 and imagine a hand helping lift a load at the start of the day. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a verified historical account.

The canonical radical is 手, with the visible left-hand form 扌.

## Common words

- [担ぐ](../words/担ぐ.md) かつぐ - carry on one's shoulder
- 担う になう - shoulder; bear a responsibility

## Notes

- 担う includes figuratively bearing a responsibility; its shoulder meaning is not limited to that figurative use.
- 担ぐ has additional senses, including nominating a representative and deceiving someone. The shoulder gloss is a starting sense, not an exhaustive rule.
- 担当 does not by itself mean being the highest-ranking manager. The existing article explains the assigned-role use.
