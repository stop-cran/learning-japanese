# 縫う (ぬう) - sew; weave one's way through

## Meaning

縫う means sewing or stitching. It is a transitive godan verb:
縫います (ぬいます), 縫って (ぬって), 縫った (ぬった),
縫わない (ぬわない). The potential is 縫える (ぬえる), "can sew."

- 破れたシャツを縫いました。
  (やぶれたシャツをぬいました。) - I stitched the torn shirt.
- ボタンをしっかり縫い付けてください。
  (ボタンをしっかりぬいつけてください。) - Please sew the button on securely.

The shirt takes を in the first example.
縫い付ける (ぬいつける) combines sewing with attaching something.

## How the kanji combine

[縫](../kanji/縫.md) supplies ぬ and う is okurigana.
The って form is the godan change ぬう to ぬって, not ぬいて.
This is a whole native verb, not a multi-kanji compound.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[裁縫](裁縫.md) (さいほう) names sewing or needlework as an activity.
[針](針.md) (はり) is a needle; the dictionary also records a sewing/needlework
sense, although the existing article emphasizes needles and clock hands.
[編む](編む.md) (あむ), "knit; weave," uses a different construction method from stitching.

## Distinctive meaning

An additional sense is making one's way through gaps:
人ごみを縫って進みました。
(ひとごみをぬってすすみました。) - I threaded my way through the crowd.
Here を marks the space traversed, not people being sewn.
[通る](通る.md) (とおる), "pass through," and
[抜ける](抜ける.md) (ぬける), "get through," overlap without necessarily
depicting this winding route.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
entry 1517700, source sense 1 and additional recognition sense 2.
Usage: [Digital Daijisen: 縫う](https://www.weblio.jp/content/縫う).

# 自慢 (じまん) - pride; boasting

## Meaning

自慢 is pride in something connected with oneself, or boasting about it. 自慢する is a transitive suru verb: **thing を自慢する**. 自慢の + noun describes something one is proud of.

- 彼は新しい車を自慢しています。 (かれはあたらしいくるまをじまんしています。) - He is showing off his new car.
- この庭は祖母の自慢です。 (このにわはそぼのじまんです。) - This garden is my grandmother's pride and joy.

The first example describes behavior; the second names a source of pride. Neither construction needs な before a following noun.

## How the kanji combine

[自](../kanji/自.md) contributes self, and [慢](../kanji/慢.md) contributes self-satisfaction or boastfulness here. Together they concern pride associated with oneself. The on readings are ジ + マン; the components are a meaning aid, not a claim about the word's historical origin.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[得意](得意.md) (とくい) can describe being good at something or feeling proud. 自信 (じしん) is confidence in one's ability; it need not involve telling others about an achievement. Neither is a mechanical replacement for 自慢する.

## Distinctive meaning

自慢 can sound critical when someone repeatedly boasts, but it also appears positively: 自慢の料理 (じまんのりょうり), "a dish one is proud of." Let the construction and speaker's attitude guide the English translation rather than translating every occurrence as objectionable bragging.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1318680, sense 1.

# 裁縫 (さいほう) - sewing; needlework

## Meaning

裁縫 is sewing or needlework. 裁縫する names doing that activity; it is classified as intransitive in the cited dictionary.

- 母に裁縫を教わりました。 (ははにさいほうをおそわりました。) - I learned sewing from my mother.
- 週末は家で裁縫をしています。 (しゅうまつはいえでさいほうをしています。) - I do sewing at home on weekends.

In 裁縫をする, を marks the activity noun itself. It does not make 裁縫 an object-taking verb for every item you sew.

## How the kanji combine

裁 has a cutting-out or tailoring association, and [縫](../kanji/縫.md) contributes sewing or stitching. Together they cover preparing and sewing cloth into clothing or other sewn items. The on readings are サイ + ホウ; the final ほう has a long vowel.

## Synonyms and antonyms

縫う (ぬう) is the verb "sew": ボタンを縫い付ける (ボタンをぬいつける) means sew on a button. [針](針.md) (はり) normally names a needle; its dictionary family also records a sewing/needlework use. The tool and the activity should still be distinguished in an actual sentence.

## Distinctive meaning

裁縫 names the activity or skill, not the needle, thread, or sewing machine individually. 裁縫が得意です (さいほうがとくいです) means "I am good at sewing." The word alone does not say whether the work is done by hand or by machine.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1296200, sense 1; 針 comparison 1366210, sense 6. Activity scope: [Digital Daijisen](https://www.weblio.jp/content/裁縫).

# 討つ (うつ) - attack; defeat; avenge

## Meaning

討つ is a transitive godan verb meaning to attack or defeat an enemy. It also occurs in expressions for avenging someone. The target takes を. Learn 討ちます (うちます), 討った (うった), and 討たない (うたない).

- 王は敵を討つために軍を送りました。 (おうはてきをうつためにぐんをおくりました。) - The king sent an army to defeat the enemy.
- 物語の主人公は父の仇を討ちました。 (ものがたりのしゅじんこうはちちのかたきをうちました。) - The story's main character avenged his father.

The second sentence uses 仇を討つ (かたきをうつ), "avenge." These examples concern stories and conflict, not an ordinary way to say "win a game."

## How the kanji combine

[討](../kanji/討.md) writes the native stem う before つ. There is one written kanji, so no compound relationship to unpack. The dictionary also records 撃つ and 伐つ for this attack/defeat entry.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[打つ](打つ.md), also read うつ, covers hitting and many other actions. 撃つ is associated with shooting, but the dictionary also admits that spelling for the attack/defeat identity taught here; the spellings do not form an absolute boundary.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn 討つ through its target and context. 敵を討つ names confronting or defeating an enemy; 仇を討つ describes revenge. A dictionary gloss such as "destroy" is not a license to use this verb for deleting a file or breaking an everyday object.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1478010, sense 1.

# 誕生 (たんじょう) - birth; coming into existence

## Meaning

誕生 is a noun and intransitive する verb meaning birth or coming into existence. It applies to a person's birth and can also describe the creation of something new.

- 昨日、赤ちゃんが誕生しました。 (きのう、あかちゃんがたんじょうしました。) - A baby was born yesterday.
- 二つの町が一つになり、新しい市が誕生しました。 (ふたつのまちがひとつになり、あたらしいしがたんじょうしました。) - Two towns joined together, and a new city came into existence.

The person or thing coming into being takes が. 赤ちゃんの誕生 (あかちゃんのたんじょう) means "the baby's birth."

## How the kanji combine

[誕](../kanji/誕.md) contributes birth in this compound, and [生](../kanji/生.md) contributes life or coming into being. Together they reinforce the beginning-of-existence meaning. The reading is タン + ジョウ; do not substitute 生's reading セイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[生まれる](生まれる.md) (うまれる) is the native verb for being born and also has figurative uses. 出産 (しゅっさん) focuses on giving birth. [誕生日](誕生日.md) (たんじょうび) identifies a birthday rather than simply naming the birth event.

## Distinctive meaning

誕生 presents an event as the start of a new existence and suits announcements or descriptions of origins. It does not imply that the new entity is biological: a city or organization can 誕生する. Nor does it specify the agent who created it. If someone founded an organization, that person's action requires a different construction.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1419080, senses 1-2.
