# 徹夜 (てつや) - staying up all night

## Meaning

徹夜 is staying awake throughout the night. It is a noun and an intransitive する verb: **徹夜する**, "stay up all night." Use **徹夜で + verb** to say what someone does through the night.

- 昨日は仕事で徹夜しました。 (きのうはしごとでてつやしました。) - I stayed up all night yesterday because of work.
- 徹夜でレポートを書きました。 (てつやでレポートをかきました。) - I stayed up all night writing my report.

The past is 徹夜した, and the negative is 徹夜しない. **徹夜の作業** (てつやのさぎょう) is "all-night work"; の connects the noun to the activity.

## How the kanji combine

[徹](../kanji/徹.md) contributes going through or carrying through; [夜](../kanji/夜.md) contributes night. The compound expresses continuing through the night. Its on readings are テツ + ヤ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

夜更かし (よふかし) means staying up late and does not necessarily extend through the entire night. 一晩 (ひとばん) means "one night" or "all night" without saying whether anyone remained awake.

## Distinctive meaning

徹夜 identifies wakefulness across the night, not merely poor-quality sleep. A train running all night is not itself "staying awake"; choose a time expression appropriate to its operation. The word describes what happened and does not imply that missing sleep is desirable or productive.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1437700, sense 1.

# 徹底 (てってい) - thoroughness

## Meaning

徹底 is thoroughness or completeness, and also ensuring that a policy or practice
is carried out consistently. In Nを徹底する, the object is what is thoroughly
implemented. 徹底した before a noun means "thorough."

- 安全確認を徹底してください。 (あんぜんかくにんをてっていしてください。) - Please make sure safety checks are carried out thoroughly.
- 徹底した調査が必要です。 (てっていしたちょうさがひつようです。) - A thorough investigation is necessary.

徹底的に (てっていてきに), "thoroughly," is another useful form:
徹底的に調べる means investigating something thoroughly.

## How the kanji combine

[徹](../kanji/徹.md) contributes going through or penetrating, and [底](../kanji/底.md) means bottom. Together they
suggest reaching all the way through to the bottom, a helpful image for
thoroughness rather than a requirement for physical depth.
テツ + テイ becomes テッテイ, with a small っ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[完全](完全.md) (かんぜん) concerns completeness or perfection.
徹底 emphasizes thorough execution or consistent application, not simply a
finished object with no missing parts. 中途半端 (ちゅうとはんぱ), "half-done;
halfhearted," is a useful contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

徹底 can describe both the depth of an investigation and the extent to which
people follow a practice. A request for 安全確認の徹底 is not merely a request
to announce the rules: it asks for thorough implementation. Neither use says
by itself whether the chosen policy is a good one.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1437670, senses 1-2.

# 必需品 (ひつじゅひん) - necessities; essential items

## Meaning

必需品 names necessary articles or essentials.
What counts as essential depends on the person, activity or situation.

- 旅行の必需品をかばんに入れました。 (りょこうのひつじゅひんをかばんにいれました。) - I put the essentials for the trip into my bag.
- この仕事では、パソコンが必需品です。 (このしごとでは、パソコンがひつじゅひんです。) - A computer is an essential item for this job.

The noun can refer to one item or several; Japanese does not require
a plural ending to name a set of necessities.

## How the kanji combine

[必](../kanji/必.md) contributes necessity, [需](../kanji/需.md) need or demand,
and [品](../kanji/品.md) an article or item.
必需 modifies 品: articles that are needed.
The readings are ヒツ + ジュ + ヒン; keep じゅ distinct from じゅう.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[日用品](日用品.md) (にちようひん) names everyday items.
The categories overlap, but a special-purpose essential need not be used daily.
[必要](必要.md) (ひつよう) concerns being necessary or a need;
必需品 names the actual item.
ぜいたく品 (ぜいたくひん), luxury goods, provides a contextual contrast.

## Distinctive meaning

Calling something 必需品 states that it is essential for the relevant purpose,
not that every person must own it or buy it every day.
It is not merely a name for any [品物](品物.md) (しなもの), item or goods.
Keep the requirement and its context with the noun.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
entry 1487500, sense 1.
Usage: [Digital Daijisen](https://www.weblio.jp/content/必需品).

# 応援 (おうえん) - support; cheering

## Meaning

応援 is support or help, including cheering for someone.
応援する is transitive: the person or team supported can take を.
The noun can also concern practical backup or reinforcement.

- スタジアムで選手を応援しました。
  (スタジアムでせんしゅをおうえんしました。) - We cheered for the players at the stadium.
- 人手が足りないので、応援を頼みました。
  (ひとでがたりないので、おうえんをたのみました。) - We were short-staffed, so we asked for extra help.

In the second example, を marks the support being requested,
not a team being cheered. The context determines the kind of help.

## How the kanji combine

[応](../kanji/応.md) contributes responding, and [援](../kanji/援.md) helping or supporting.
The related ideas give a memory aid of responding with support:
オウ + エン. Both written characters belong in the word.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[援助](援助.md) (えんじょ) concerns aid or assistance.
[手伝う](手伝う.md) (てつだう) is helping with a task, and
[助ける](助ける.md) (たすける) is helping or saving someone.
[励ます](励ます.md) (はげます) is encouraging or cheering someone on.
These meanings overlap without making every kind of help a sports cheer.

## Distinctive meaning

応援 is not limited to shouting from the stands, nor does it
automatically mean giving money. Compare
[賛成](賛成.md) (さんせい), agreement or support for a proposal:
応援 foregrounds backing or assisting someone rather than merely
stating agreement. Neither word guarantees that the supported effort succeeds.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
entry 1179840, senses 1-2; comparisons 1176660, 1328190, 1344410
and 1557350.

# 恩 (おん) - favor; debt of gratitude

## Meaning

恩 is a benefit or kindness received from someone, considered as a reason for gratitude or a sense of obligation. Useful combinations include 恩を受ける (おんをうける), "receive a favor," and 恩を返す (おんをかえす), "repay a kindness."

- 先生には恩があります。 (せんせいにはおんがあります。) - I owe my teacher a debt of gratitude.
- 助けてくれた人の恩を忘れません。 (たすけてくれたひとのおんをわすれません。) - I will not forget the kindness of the person who helped me.

Here 人の恩 names kindness received from that person; it does not mean that the person owes the speaker something.

## How the kanji combine

[恩](../kanji/恩.md) is a single noun with the on reading オン. The whole character represents favor or kindness and the gratitude associated with receiving it. There is no compound relationship to analyze.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[親切](親切.md) (しんせつ) describes kindness or a kind act. [感謝](感謝.md) (かんしゃ) focuses on gratitude or expressing thanks. 恩 directs attention to the benefit received and the relationship it creates.

## Distinctive meaning

A "debt" of 恩 is not necessarily a financial debt or a precisely measurable obligation. It may remain important long after the original help. There is no simple opposite that fits all these uses.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1183090, senses 1 and 2.
