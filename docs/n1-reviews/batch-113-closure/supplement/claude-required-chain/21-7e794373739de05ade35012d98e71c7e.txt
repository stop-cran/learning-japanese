# 援助 (えんじょ) - aid; assistance; support

## Meaning

援助 is a noun for aid or support, including money, supplies, and practical assistance. 援助する is a transitive verb: 人を援助する means to assist someone. With the noun, 人への援助 identifies aid directed to that person.

- 両親が留学の費用を援助してくれました。 (りょうしんがりゅうがくのひようをえんじょしてくれました。) - My parents helped pay for my studies abroad.
- 被災地への援助が必要です。 (ひさいちへのえんじょがひつようです。) - Aid for the disaster-hit area is needed.

援助を受ける (えんじょをうける) means "receive assistance"; the receiver need not be the person who performs 援助する.

## How the kanji combine

[援](../kanji/援.md) contributes support, and [助](../kanji/助.md) contributes help. The two related meanings reinforce one another in a noun read えんじょ. This component explanation is a guide to the modern word, not a historical derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[手伝う](手伝う.md) (てつだう) describes helping with an activity. 援助 can instead supply the resources that make it possible. [救助](救助.md) (きゅうじょ) focuses on rescue or relief from danger.

## Distinctive meaning

援助 is not limited to governments, charities, or emergencies: family financial support also fits. Conversely, it is not automatically a loan. If repayment matters, the surrounding explanation must say so. The word names assistance, not its precise financial conditions.

# 検討 (けんとう) - consideration; examination

## Meaning

検討 means examining or considering something before reaching a judgment. It is a noun and a transitive する verb: 案を検討する (あんをけんとうする), "consider a proposal." It can involve one person's thought or a group's discussion.

- 新しい計画を検討しています。 (あたらしいけいかくをけんとうしています。) - We are considering the new plan.
- 費用について、もう少し検討しましょう。 (ひようについて、もうすこしけんとうしましょう。) - Let's examine the costs a little further.

検討中 (けんとうちゅう) means "under consideration." It describes an unfinished decision process, not approval.

## How the kanji combine

検 contributes examining or checking. [討](../kanji/討.md) contributes investigating or discussing. The two related elements reinforce looking into a question carefully; neither supplies a claim that the outcome will be favorable.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[考える](考える.md) (かんがえる) is broader "think/consider." 検討する directs attention to examining a particular option or issue. [相談](相談.md) (そうだん) involves consultation with someone; 検討 does not itself require another participant.

## Distinctive meaning

検討します means "I/we will consider it." In a request or negotiation it leaves the result open. It may postpone an answer, but treating it as an automatic hidden refusal would also overread the word. Look for the actual follow-up decision and any stated conditions.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1258000, sense 1.

# 盆 (ぼん) - tray; Obon festival

## Meaning

盆 is a tray, and also names Obon, the Japanese observance associated with honoring ancestors and the spirits of the dead. お盆 (おぼん) can likewise mean a tray or Obon.

- 盆にお茶を載せて運びました。 (ぼんにおちゃをのせてはこびました。) - I carried the tea on a tray.
- お盆には実家に帰ります。 (おぼんにはじっかにかえります。) - I go back to my family home for Obon.

Obon dates and customs vary by region. In the second sentence, the seasonal and travel context selects the observance, not a tray.

## How the kanji combine

[盆](../kanji/盆.md) is a single character read ボン here, so there is no multi-kanji relationship to divide. The festival use is associated with the shortened name of 盂蘭盆 (うらぼん).

## Synonyms and antonyms

トレー is another word for a tray. [皿](皿.md) (さら) is a plate or dish: a tray used for carrying dishes is not automatically the dish itself.

[祭](祭.md) (まつり), "festival," is broader than the named Obon observance. There is no single ordinary antonym covering both the tray and festival senses.

## Distinctive meaning

Learn tray and Obon as separate uses of the same written word. English source labels such as "Lantern Festival" do not identify this with the Chinese Lantern Festival. JMdict additionally records family/household and a slang gambling-den sense; those are recognition beyond this practical tray-and-Obon focus.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1523700, senses 1-4; お盆, 1002690.

# 盆地 (ぼんち) - basin surrounded by mountains

## Meaning

盆地 is a geographical basin: an area of lower land surrounded by mountains or higher ground. It names the landform, not a household bowl or a river's entire drainage area.

- この町は盆地にあります。 (このまちはぼんちにあります。) - This town is in a basin.
- 山に囲まれた盆地に霧が出た。 (やまにかこまれたぼんちにきりがでた。) - Fog formed in the basin surrounded by mountains.

## How the kanji combine

[盆](../kanji/盆.md) evokes a tray or basin; [地](../kanji/地.md) contributes land. The compound presents the terrain as basin-shaped. Its reading is ぼん + ち, not a reading inferred from English "basin."

## Synonyms and antonyms

[平野](平野.md) (へいや) is a plain, emphasizing relatively flat land. A 盆地 is identified by the higher ground around it; the terms are not interchangeable names for every flat area.

[盆](盆.md) (ぼん) can mean a tray. Sharing the character does not make the household object and the geographical feature the same word.

## Distinctive meaning

盆地 can occur inland. Do not interpret it as necessarily a lake bed, or assume that every English use of "basin," including a washbasin, translates as 盆地.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1523760, sense 1.

# 系統 (けいとう) - system; lineage

## Meaning

系統 can mean a system of connected parts or a line of descent.
The connection between members matters: it can be functional, ancestral, or a shared type.

- 電気系統に異常があります。 (でんきけいとうにいじょうがあります。) - There is a fault in the electrical system.
- この二つの品種は同じ系統です。 (このふたつのひんしゅはおなじけいとうです。) - These two varieties belong to the same line.

A related grouping use includes 青系統の色 (あおけいとうのいろ), "colors in the blue family."
This groups related colors; it does not refer to a human household.

## How the kanji combine

[系](../kanji/系.md) contributes connection or lineage, and [統](../kanji/統.md) contributes uniting or a connected line.
Their related associations help recall connected systems and lines of descent.
The on readings are ケイ + トウ; the character associations are not proof of the compound's history.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[制度](制度.md) (せいど) concerns an institutional system;
[組織](組織.md) (そしき) can mean an organization or organized structure.
These overlap with some English uses of "system" but are not replacements for every electrical system.
[家族](家族.md) (かぞく), family members, is not simply another word for any ancestral or linguistic family.

## Distinctive meaning

Ask what connects the members: equipment in a system, descent from a source, or a related category such as a color family.
The raw study list also says "geological formation," but the inspected dictionary definitions do not establish that as a general translation.
This article therefore does not teach that unsupported gloss as a separate meaning.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1251030, selected senses 1-2 and additional sense 3;
definitions compared with [Digital Daijisen](https://www.weblio.jp/content/系統).
