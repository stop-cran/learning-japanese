# 村 — village

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

村 concerns a village, either a settlement or an administrative unit. The native noun 村 is read むら; 村長 そんちょう names a village head or mayor.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 木 beside 寸 and imagine a small settlement near a tree. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a claim about the character's historical construction.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソン | on | 村長 そんちょう - village head; village mayor; [農村](../words/農村.md) のうそん - farming village |
| むら | kun | [村](../words/村.md) むら - village |

## Common words

- [村](../words/村.md) むら - village
- 村長 そんちょう - village head; village mayor
- [農村](../words/農村.md) のうそん - farming village; rural community

## Notes

- Calling a place 村 does not establish that every resident farms or that the village lacks modern services.
- The ソン reading is illustrated by 村長; the linked independent noun is むら. Place-name pronunciations must be learned separately.
- Compare [材](材.md), material or timber: the right-hand component changes from 寸 to 才.

# 寺 — Buddhist temple

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寺 てら names a Buddhist temple. The on reading ジ occurs in 古寺 こじ, old temple, and 寺社 じしゃ, temples and shrines. These words do not make temple and shrine interchangeable categories.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See a 土-like upper shape over 寸 and imagine a small temple model resting on a hand. The scene is invented from the modern shape; it is not a claim about the historical role of either component.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジ | on | 古寺 こじ - old temple |
| てら | kun | [寺](../words/寺.md) てら - Buddhist temple |

## Common words

- [寺](../words/寺.md) てら - Buddhist temple
- 古寺 こじ - old temple
- 寺社 じしゃ - temples and shrines
- [寺院](../words/寺院.md) じいん - temple; religious building

## Notes

- Do not read the standalone word for temple じ merely because temple names can end in that reading. Learn the reading of each name separately.
- Compare [時](時.md), time, [待](待.md), wait, and [持](持.md), hold. Each adds a different left-hand component to the visible 寺 shape.
- The indexing radical is 寸, not the upper 土-like shape.

# 診 — diagnose; examine medically

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

診 concerns examining a person's medical condition and assessing what is
wrong. [診察](../words/診察.md) しんさつ is a medical examination;
[診断](../words/診断.md) しんだん is diagnosis or diagnostic assessment.
The examination and the resulting assessment are related, not identical
stages or freely interchangeable nouns.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [言](言.md) on the left
and [人](人.md) above three slanting 彡 strokes on the right.
Imagine a clinician listening to a person and checking three marks in a
record. This is an invented scene for the modern shape, not a historical
or phonetic explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シン | on | [診察](../words/診察.md) しんさつ - medical examination; [診断](../words/診断.md) しんだん - diagnosis |
| み.る | kun | [診る](../words/診る.md) みる - examine medically; diagnose |

Both readings are selected with linked examples. 診る is a transitive
ichidan verb: 診 supplies み and る is written in kana. Its spelling
specifies medical examination, not every ordinary act of seeing.

## Common words

- [診る](../words/診る.md) みる - examine medically
- [診察](../words/診察.md) しんさつ - medical examination
- [診断](../words/診断.md) しんだん - diagnosis; diagnostic assessment

## Notes

- [珍](珍.md), rare/precious, shares the right-hand 人-over-彡 group
  but has a different left side. [参](参.md), participate/visit,
  also has lower slanting strokes; [記](記.md), record in writing,
  shares 言 but has a different right component.
- [察](察.md), [調](調.md), [試](試.md) and [査](査.md) have examination
  or investigation meanings; [見](見.md) and [観](観.md) overlap looking,
  and [判](判.md) judging. Their broader meanings make them poor wrong
  answers for this card, without making all their compounds medical terms.
- [看](看.md) connects with watching over and caring for someone.
  The 看 card declares this direct exclusion pair. Looking after a patient
  is not necessarily diagnosing them; learn the specific verbs and compounds.

# 訪 — visit; call on

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

訪 is the character for paying a visit. [訪ねる](../words/訪ねる.md) たずねる directs attention to going
to see a person or place. 訪れる おとずれる also has a separate arrival sense,
including the coming of a season.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** put 言 beside 方 and imagine heading in a direction to speak
with someone you are visiting. This is a modern association, not an account of the character's origin.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| たず.ねる | kun | [訪ねる](../words/訪ねる.md) たずねる — visit |
| おとず.れる | kun | 訪れる おとずれる — visit; arrive |
| ホウ | on | 訪問 ほうもん — visit |

## Common words

- [訪ねる](../words/訪ねる.md) たずねる — visit; call on
- 訪問 ほうもん — a visit; visiting
- 訪れる おとずれる — visit; come

## Notes

友達を訪ねる (ともだちをたずねる) means "visit a friend": the person visited can take を.
[方](方.md) is the right-hand shape without 言.
[記](記.md) and [許](許.md) retain 言 but replace the part on the right.

# 守 — guard; obey

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

守 can mean guarding a person or place, or keeping a rule or promise. [守る](../words/守る.md) (まもる) connects these uses: protect what matters, or keep an obligation intact.
The English translation depends on what is being 守る.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a small guard stationed by the 寸 shape beneath a roof, 宀.
This is an invented memory aid, not a claim about the character's construction history.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| まも.る | kun | [守る](../words/守る.md) (まもる) — protect; obey |
| シュ | on | 守備 (しゅび) — defense |
| ス | on | 留守 (るす) — absence from home |

## Common words

- [守る](../words/守る.md) (まもる) — protect; keep a promise; follow a rule
- 守備 (しゅび) — defense; fielding in baseball
- 留守 (るす) — absence from home; also house-sitting in another sense

## Notes

The lower part is 寸, not the hooked shape in [宅](宅.md) or the 子 in [字](字.md).
Learn 留守 as a whole word: its "away from home" meaning cannot be obtained by translating 守 as "guard" in isolation.
守る is transitive: the protected person or the promise being kept can be marked with を.
