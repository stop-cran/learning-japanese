# 徹 — penetrate; be thorough

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

徹 concerns going all the way through something and carrying it through to
the end. [徹底](../words/徹底.md) てってい is thoroughness or the thorough
enforcement of something, and [徹夜](../words/徹夜.md) てつや is staying up
all night. 徹底的 てっていてき is thorough or exhaustive, 貫徹 かんてつ is
accomplishing or carrying something through, and 徹する てっする is to
penetrate, to devote oneself to something exclusively, or to do something
throughout a period such as the night.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives the sense of piercing through
and of reaching all the way to the limit, with 徹底, 徹夜, 貫徹, 透徹 and
冷徹 among its examples. It lists とおる and とおす as kun readings. Those
readings are not in KANJIDIC2 or the official Joyo table, so practice does
not select them.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 彳 at the left, [育](育.md) in
the middle and 攵 at the right. Imagine setting out along a road, raising a
task step by step with a firm hand until it is carried through to the very
end. This is a memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| テツ | on | [徹底](../words/徹底.md) てってい - thoroughness; thorough enforcement; [徹夜](../words/徹夜.md) てつや - staying up all night |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects テツ. In 徹底, テツ and
テイ combine as てってい with a small っ; in 徹夜 it stays てつ. The official
Joyo table lists テツ, with 徹底, 徹夜 and 貫徹 as examples.

## Common words

- [徹底](../words/徹底.md) てってい - thoroughness; thorough enforcement
- [徹夜](../words/徹夜.md) てつや - staying up all night
- 徹底的 てっていてき - thorough; exhaustive; complete
- 徹する てっする - penetrate; devote oneself to; do throughout
- 貫徹 かんてつ - accomplishment; carrying something through

## Notes

- [微](微.md), "minute; faint," shares both 彳 at the left and 攵 at the
  right, and [御](御.md), "honorific; govern," shares 彳. [育](育.md), "grow
  up; raise," is the middle component of 徹. Their meanings differ from
  徹's.
- Thoroughness overlaps [究](究.md), "investigate thoroughly," [完](完.md),
  "complete," [全](全.md), "whole; complete," and [了](了.md), "completion;
  settling." Daijisen defines [討](討.md), "attack; discuss," as probing a
  problem thoroughly, and JMdict's second sense of [丁寧](../words/丁寧.md),
  written with [寧](寧.md), "polite; peaceful," is "careful; close;
  thorough; conscientious." This card declares the pairs 徹–討 and 徹–寧.
- Piercing, passing through or reaching all the way overlaps
  [刺](刺.md), "pierce; prick," [通](通.md), "pass through; commute,"
  [経](経.md), "pass through; sutra," [至](至.md), "reach; attain,"
  [届](届.md), "reach; deliver; notify," and [達](達.md), "reach, plural
  suffix."
- Staying up all night overlaps [夜](夜.md), "night," [晩](晩.md),
  "evening; night," [座](座.md), "sit; seating," [泊](泊.md), "stay
  overnight; anchor," and [留](留.md), "retain; stay," because KANJIDIC2
  glosses 徹 "sit up (all night)."
- Clarity, as in the Daijisen examples 透徹 and 冷徹, overlaps [冴](冴.md),
  "clear; sharp-minded," [明](明.md), "bright, clear," and [晴](晴.md),
  "clear skies," because KANJIDIC2 also glosses 徹 "clear."
- KANJIDIC2's "strike home" is a figure of speech for hitting the mark. It
  overlaps [打](打.md), "strike," and [撃](撃.md), "shoot; strike," and its
  word "home" is shared by [家](家.md), "house, home," and [帰](帰.md),
  "return home," although 徹 never means a house. These direct meaning
  comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as identical in every
  compound.

# 討 — attack; discuss

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

討 concerns going after something, either an enemy or a problem.
[討つ](../words/討つ.md) うつ is the native verb for attacking, defeating,
conquering or avenging, and [検討](../words/検討.md) けんとう is
consideration, examination or review. 討伐 とうばつ is subjugation by
military force, 討議 とうぎ is a debate or discussion, and 討論 とうろん is
also a debate or discussion.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: attacking and
subduing an enemy, as in 討伐, and probing a problem thoroughly, as in 討議
and 検討. It lists うつ and たずねる as kun readings. たずねる is not in
KANJIDIC2 or the official Joyo table, so practice does not select it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [言](言.md) on the left and 寸
on the right. Imagine words delivered in exact small measures against an
enemy or a problem until it is dealt with. This is a memory picture for the
modern shape, not a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| トウ | on | [検討](../words/検討.md) けんとう - consideration; examination |
| う.つ | kun | [討つ](../words/討つ.md) うつ - attack; defeat; avenge |

Practice selects トウ and う.つ, each with a linked example. 討つ writes う
with 討 and つ in kana; it is a transitive godan verb. The official Joyo
table lists トウ, with 討伐, 討論 and 検討 as examples, and うつ, with 討つ
and 敵討ち, where it contrasts 討つ with 打つ and 撃つ.

## Common words

- [討つ](../words/討つ.md) うつ - attack; defeat; avenge
- [検討](../words/検討.md) けんとう - consideration; examination
- 討伐 とうばつ - subjugation; suppression
- 討議 とうぎ - debate; discussion
- 討論 とうろん - debate; discussion

## Notes

- [計](計.md), "measure; plan," shares 言 at the left, and [村](村.md),
  "village," and [寺](寺.md), "Buddhist temple," share 寸 at the right.
  Their meanings differ from 討's.
- Attacking and striking overlaps [攻](攻.md), "attack; go on the
  offensive," [撃](撃.md), "shoot; strike," [打](打.md), "strike," and
  [戦](戦.md), "battle." JMdict lists 撃つ among the written forms of the
  same entry as 討つ, and the Joyo table sets 討つ against both 打つ and
  撃つ.
- Defeating and overcoming overlaps [敗](敗.md), "failure; defeat,"
  [克](克.md), "overcome," [勝](勝.md), "victory," and [殺](殺.md), "kill,"
  because 討つ is glossed "defeat," "conquer" and "destroy."
- Punishing overlaps [責](責.md), "blame; hold responsible," and
  [刑](刑.md), "criminal punishment," because KANJIDIC2 glosses 討
  "chastise."
- Discussion and consideration, as in 討議, 討論 and 検討, overlap
  [批](批.md), "criticize; critique," [議](議.md), "deliberate; debate,"
  [論](論.md), "argument; discourse," [談](談.md), "converse; consult,"
  [考](考.md), "think over," [学](学.md), "study," and [研](研.md), "polish;
  study closely."
- Investigation overlaps [査](査.md), "investigate; inspect," [調](調.md),
  "investigate; tune," [究](究.md), "investigate thoroughly," [診](診.md),
  "diagnose; examine medically," and [偵](偵.md), "spy; covert
  investigation," because JMdict glosses 検討 "examination; investigation;
  study." These direct meaning comparisons are excluded from choices, not
  treated as identical in every compound.
- [徹](徹.md), "penetrate; be thorough," declares the pair 徹–討, so this
  card does not repeat it.
- In 検討, 検 supplies ケン. It has no kanji card here yet; this does not
  change the complete word's reading.
