# 謝 — apologize; thank

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

謝 concerns apologizing and giving thanks.
[感謝](../words/感謝.md) かんしゃ is gratitude or appreciation, and
[謝る](../words/謝る.md) あやまる is the native verb for apologizing. 謝罪
しゃざい is an apology, 謝礼 しゃれい is a reward or honorarium given as
thanks, 謝絶 しゃぜつ is refusal or declining, 月謝 げっしゃ is a monthly
tuition fee, and 代謝 たいしゃ is metabolism, or renewal by replacing the
old with the new.

謝る has two other senses that JMdict marks as dated: to refuse or decline,
and to be unable to bear something, be defeated by it or be stumped by it.

The Digital Daijisen character entry lists six senses: apologizing, as in
謝罪; declining, as in 謝絶; saying thanks, as in 謝礼 and 感謝; money or
goods that express thanks or an apology, as in 月謝; replacing, as in
新陳代謝; and, as a substitute character for 藉, consoling, as in 慰謝料.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [言](言.md) on the left and
[射](射.md) on the right, with [身](身.md) and 寸 inside 射. Imagine words
fired straight at someone to say sorry or thank you. This is a memory
picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or sound-component
analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シャ | on | [感謝](../words/感謝.md) かんしゃ - gratitude; appreciation |
| あやま.る | kun | [謝る](../words/謝る.md) あやまる - apologize |

Practice selects シャ and あやま.る, each with a linked example. 謝る writes
あやま with 謝 and る in kana; it is a godan verb despite ending in る: 謝ります,
謝って, 謝らない. The official Joyo table lists シャ, with 謝絶, 感謝 and
陳謝 as examples, and あやまる, with 謝る and 平謝り.

## Common words

- [感謝](../words/感謝.md) かんしゃ - gratitude; appreciation
- [謝る](../words/謝る.md) あやまる - apologize
- 謝罪 しゃざい - apology
- 謝礼 しゃれい - reward; honorarium
- 謝絶 しゃぜつ - refusal; declining
- 月謝 げっしゃ - monthly tuition fee
- 代謝 たいしゃ - metabolism; renewal

## Notes

- [診](診.md), "diagnose; examine medically," and [訪](訪.md), "visit; call
  on," share 言 at the left. [射](射.md), "shoot; shine into," is the
  right-hand component of 謝, and [守](守.md), "guard; obey," shares the 寸
  inside it. Their meanings differ from 謝's.
- Thanks overlaps [礼](礼.md), "bow; thanks." Refusing overlaps
  [断](断.md), "sever; refuse," because 謝絶 means declining and KANJIDIC2
  glosses 謝 "refuse."
- Making amends overlaps [償](償.md), "recompense; make amends." A reward
  or fee overlaps [賞](賞.md), "prize; reward," and [料](料.md),
  "materials, fee," because of 謝礼 and 月謝.
- Replacing and renewing, as in 代謝 and 新陳代謝, overlap [代](代.md),
  "replace, era," [換](換.md), "exchange; replace," [替](替.md), "replace;
  substitute," and [更](更.md), "renew; grow late."
- The dated senses of 謝る overlap [敗](敗.md), "failure; defeat,"
  [負](負.md), "lose; bear a burden," [失](失.md), "lose; loss," and
  [損](損.md), "loss; damage," through "be defeated," "be unable to bear"
  and "be at a loss." These direct meaning comparisons are excluded from
  choices, not treated as identical in every compound.
- [恩](恩.md), "favor; debt of gratitude," declares the pair 恩–謝, so this
  card does not repeat it.

# 握 — grip; grasp

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

握 concerns closing the hand around something. [握る](../words/握る.md)
にぎる is the native verb for gripping or grasping, and it also covers
holding the answer to something, seizing power, and shaping rice balls or
nigirizushi by hand. [握手](../words/握手.md) あくしゅ is a handshake, and
JMdict's second sense adds reconciliation or joining hands. 握力
あくりょく is grip strength, and 把握 はあく is grasping a situation or its
meaning.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: gripping with the
hand, as in 握手, 握力 and 一握, and firmly making something one's own, as
in 掌握 and 把握. It lists にぎる as the kun reading.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the left-hand form of
[手](手.md), beside [屋](屋.md). Imagine a hand closing tightly around a
small house. This is a memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified
origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| アク | on | [握手](../words/握手.md) あくしゅ - handshake |
| にぎ.る | kun | [握る](../words/握る.md) にぎる - grasp; grip |

Practice selects アク and にぎ.る, each with a linked example. 握る writes
にぎ with 握 and る in kana; it is a transitive godan verb: 握ります, 握った,
握らない. In 握手, 握 is あく and 手 is しゅ, not て. The official Joyo
table lists アク, with 握手, 握力 and 掌握 as examples, and にぎる, with 握る,
握り and 一握り.

## Common words

- [握る](../words/握る.md) にぎる - grasp; grip
- [握手](../words/握手.md) あくしゅ - handshake
- 握り にぎり - grip; handful; handle
- 握力 あくりょく - grip strength
- 把握 はあく - grasp; understanding

## Notes

- [屋](屋.md), "roof, shop," is the right-hand component of 握, and
  [掘](掘.md), "dig; excavate," and [掲](掲.md), "put up; display;
  publish," share 扌 at the left. Their meanings differ from 握's.
- Holding and gripping overlap [持](持.md), "hold, have," [挟](挟.md),
  "pinch; hold between," and [抱](抱.md), "embrace," because JMdict glosses
  握る "clasp; grasp; grip; clutch" and KANJIDIC2 glosses 握 "hold."
  Seizing, as in JMdict's "seize (power)" for 握る, overlaps [取](取.md),
  "take," and [捕](捕.md), "capture." [責](責.md), "blame; hold
  responsible," shares the English word "hold" with KANJIDIC2's gloss and
  with JMdict's "hold (the answer)" and "hold (the reins)," although it
  concerns blame, not gripping.
- Controlling and dominating, from JMdict's sense "seize (power); hold (the
  reins); dominate; control," overlaps [御](御.md), "honorific; govern,"
  [治](治.md), "govern; heal," and [圧](圧.md), "pressure; oppress."
- Forming something by hand, from JMdict's sense "make (nigirizushi, rice
  ball, etc.); form (with one's hands); press into shape; mold" and
  KANJIDIC2's "mould sushi," overlaps [型](型.md), "mold; type,"
  [形](形.md), "shape; form," [詰](詰.md), "pack tightly; press," and
  [作](作.md), "make, produce." These direct meaning comparisons are
  excluded from choices, not treated as identical in every compound.

# 寧 — polite; peaceful

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寧 concerns calm, settled peace and, in 丁寧, careful attention.
[丁寧](../words/丁寧.md) ていねい is polite or courteous, and also careful or
thorough. 安寧 あんねい is peace, stability or tranquility.
[寧ろ](../words/寧ろ.md) むしろ is the adverb "rather; if anything." Its
article warns that 寧's calm and careful associations do not directly
explain the adverb's comparison, so learn the complete word.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: being calm and
settled, as in 寧日 and 安寧, and being cordial, as in 丁寧. It lists むしろ
as a kun reading, which KANJIDIC2 also has but the official Joyo table does
not. It also lists ニョウ as a Go-on reading, which is outside both
KANJIDIC2 and the Joyo table.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 宀 on top, [心](心.md) and a
net-like 罒 in the middle, and 丁 at the bottom. Imagine a quiet household
with a settled heart under its roof and everything kept in careful order.
This is a memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ネイ | on | [丁寧](../words/丁寧.md) ていねい - polite; careful |
| むし.ろ | kun | [寧ろ](../words/寧ろ.md) むしろ - rather; if anything; usually written in kana |

Practice selects ネイ. 寧ろ writes むし with 寧 and ろ in kana, but JMdict
marks the word as usually written in kana alone and the official Joyo
table lists only ネイ, with 安寧 and 丁寧 as examples, so むし.ろ is
recorded here without being selected for practice.

## Common words

- [丁寧](../words/丁寧.md) ていねい - polite; careful
- [寧ろ](../words/寧ろ.md) むしろ - rather; if anything (usually written in kana)
- 安寧 あんねい - peace; stability; tranquility

## Notes

- [寝](寝.md), "lie down; sleep," [宝](宝.md), "treasure; valuables," and
  [寒](寒.md), "cold weather," share 宀 on top but differ in meaning.
- Peace and calm overlap [安](安.md), "cheap, peaceful," [平](平.md),
  "flat; peaceful," [静](静.md), "quiet," and [凪](凪.md), "calm seas; lull,"
  because KANJIDIC2 glosses 寧 "peaceful," "quiet" and "tranquility."
- Courtesy overlaps [敬](敬.md), "respect; honor," [厚](厚.md), "thick;
  cordial," [儀](儀.md), "ceremony; formal conduct," and [礼](礼.md), "bow;
  thanks," because 丁寧 means polite or courteous and Daijisen calls the
  sense cordial.
- Care and closeness overlap [細](細.md), "slender; fine; detailed,"
  [親](親.md), "parent; close," and [封](封.md), "seal; close," through
  JMdict's "careful; close; thorough; conscientious" for 丁寧. Thoroughness
  also overlaps [研](研.md), "polish; study closely," and [究](究.md),
  "investigate thoroughly." These direct meaning comparisons are excluded
  from choices, not treated as identical in every compound.
- [徹](徹.md), "penetrate; be thorough," declares the pair 徹–寧, so this
  card does not repeat it.
- In 丁寧, 丁 supplies テイ. It has no kanji card here yet; this does not
  change the complete word's reading.
