# 患 — be ill; suffer

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

患 concerns illness or suffering. 患者 かんじゃ names a medical patient, not a person who is patient about waiting. 患う わずらう means to be ill or suffer from an illness.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 串 over 心 and imagine the heart burdened by an ailment. This is a shape-based memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カン | on | [患者](../words/患者.md) かんじゃ - medical patient |
| わずら.う | kun | 患う わずらう - be ill; suffer from |

## Common words

- [患者](../words/患者.md) かんじゃ - medical patient
- 患う わずらう - be ill; suffer from

## Notes

- 病気を患う びょうきをわずらう means to suffer from an illness. The dictionary records both transitive and intransitive uses of 患う; this example uses を for the illness.
- 患者 identifies the medical-care role, not a guarantee that the person is hospitalized.
- Compare [思](思.md) and [息](息.md), whose upper parts differ despite the shared 心 below.

# 分 — minute, part

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **Phonetic:** none (an ideographic compound) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

分 combines 八 (eight, here the shape of something splitting apart) and 刀 (sword, knife): cutting something into parts. This is the commonly
given explanation; as a mnemonic, "八 + 刀 = a knife dividing". The core idea is "divide", which gives "part", "share", "minute" (a part of an hour) and, via being separated clearly, "understand" (分かる).

## Readings

| --- | --- | --- |
| ブン | on | [半分](../words/半分.md) はんぶん, 自分 じぶん, 気分 きぶん, 十分 じゅうぶん (enough) |
| フン／プン | on | minutes: 五分 ごふん, 三分 さんぷん, 十分 じゅっぷん／じっぷん, 何分 なんぷん |
| ブ | on | 五分五分 ごぶごぶ (fifty-fifty), 七分袖 しちぶそで (three-quarter sleeve) |
| わ.ける | kun | 分ける わける (divide, separate) |
| わ.かれる | kun | 分かれる わかれる (split, branch off) |
| わ.かる | kun | [分かる](../words/分かる.md) わかる (understand) |
| わ.かつ | kun | 分かつ わかつ (literary: divide, share), 袂を分かつ たもとをわかつ (part ways) |

## Common words

- [半分](../words/半分.md) はんぶん — half
- [分かる](../words/分かる.md) わかる — to understand
- 自分 じぶん — oneself

## Notes

- **Level source:** kanjiapi.dev has no JLPT value for 分; the N5 label follows [Kanshudo](https://www.kanshudo.com/kanji/%E5%88%86)
  and [JLPT Sensei](https://jlptsensei.com/jlpt-n5-kanji-list/), not an official examination list.

- **Minute counter:** the 分 of minutes changes with the number before it (十分 じゅっぷん／じっぷん; 何分 なんぷん):

# 盗 — steal

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 皿 (no. 108) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

盗 identifies stealing rather than simply picking something up.
[盗む](../words/盗む.md) ぬすむ is the verb; 盗難 とうなん names theft.
The meaning concerns taking something that is not rightfully yours.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture someone reaching toward the dish-shaped 皿 at the bottom without permission.
This is an invented scene for remembering the modern shape, not its historical origin.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ぬす.む | kun | [盗む](../words/盗む.md) ぬすむ — steal |

## Common words

- [盗む](../words/盗む.md) ぬすむ — steal
- 盗難 とうなん — theft; robbery

## Notes

自転車を盗まれました。 (じてんしゃをぬすまれました。) — My bicycle was stolen.
盗まれる is the passive form: the sentence presents the theft from the affected person's side.

The bottom is 皿, not the 田 in [留](留.md) or [番](番.md).
[買](買.md) has another stacked, box-like outline, but its lower part is 貝. Buying and stealing are different actions.

# 貧 — poor; impoverished

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

貧 expresses poverty or a shortage of resources. [貧しい](../words/貧しい.md) まずしい can describe
someone who lacks the means to live comfortably. The focus is insufficiency, not the low price of an item.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the upper 分 dividing up the lower 貝 until very little is left.
This is an invented memory aid based on the visible components, not a historical derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |

| まず.しい | kun | 貧しい まずしい — poor |
| ヒン | on | 貧困 ひんこん — poverty |

## Common words

- [貧しい](../words/貧しい.md) まずしい — poor; needy
- 貧困 ひんこん — poverty
- 貧乏 びんぼう — poverty; being poor

## Notes

The adjective 貧しい can go directly before a noun; 貧乏 uses な when modifying one.
Recognize the whole upper [分](分.md), not just the small cap found in [負](負.md).
[買](買.md) has a wider, net-shaped top over the same lower 貝.

# 延 — prolong; postpone

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 廴 (no. 54) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

延 concerns extending or prolonging something and putting an event off. 延期 えんき means postponement. In 延びる のびる an event or period is extended or delayed; 延ばす のばす describes someone extending or postponing it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice the long sweeping 廴 around the inner upright strokes and imagine stretching a route farther along. This is a modern-shape story, not etymology.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| エン | on | [延期](../words/延期.md) えんき - postponement |
| の.びる | kun | [延びる](../words/伸びる.md) のびる - be prolonged; be postponed |
| の.べる | kun | 延べる のべる - spread out; extend; postpone |
| の.べ | kun | 延べ のべ - total; aggregate (prefix before a counter or unit) |
| の.ばす | kun | [延ばす](../words/伸ばす.md) のばす - prolong; postpone |

## Common words

- [延期](../words/延期.md) えんき - postponement
- [延長](../words/延長.md) えんちょう - extension; prolongation
- [延びる](../words/伸びる.md) のびる - be prolonged; be postponed
- [延ばす](../words/伸ばす.md) のばす - prolong; postpone

## Notes

- The prefix 延べ のべ gives an aggregate total before a counter or unit: 延べ三日 のべみっか means three days in total. This is sense 4 of the dictionary entry, not its separate futures-trading noun.
- 延びる is intransitive; 延ばす and 延べる are transitive. JMdict notes 延ばす especially for postponement; especially is a usage tendency, not an exclusive spelling restriction.
- The primary source stroke count is eight; an alternative count of seven is also recorded. The card and stroke drawing use the primary count.
