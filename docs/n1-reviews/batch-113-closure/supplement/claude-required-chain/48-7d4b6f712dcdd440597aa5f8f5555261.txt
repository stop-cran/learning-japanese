# 寒 — cold weather

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

寒 is the cold you meet in the weather or feel around you. Start with [寒い](../words/寒い.md) さむい:
今日は寒いです。 (きょうはさむいです。) — It is cold today.
For something cold to the touch, such as water, compare [冷たい](../words/冷たい.md) つめたい.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine shivering beneath the 宀 roof, with two small marks below the crowded middle.
This is a way to remember the visible arrangement, not an account of the character's history.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| さむ.い | kun | [寒い](../words/寒い.md) さむい — cold |
| カン | on | 寒冷 かんれい — cold; chilliness |

## Common words

- [寒い](../words/寒い.md) さむい — cold, especially weather
- 寒さ さむさ — coldness
- 寒冷 かんれい — cold; chilliness

## Notes

[察](察.md), [寝](寝.md), and [家](家.md) share the roof but have different material underneath it.
The two lowest marks help distinguish 寒. 暑い あつい is the weather adjective for the opposite condition, hot.

# 暖 — warm; warm up

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 日 (no. 72), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

暖 is warmth: being warm, or making something warm. [暖かい](../words/暖かい.md) あたたかい is warm or mild, [暖まる](../words/暖まる.md) あたたまる is to get warm, [暖める](../words/暖める.md) あたためる is to warm or heat something up, [暖房](../words/暖房.md) だんぼう is indoor heating and [温暖](../words/温暖.md) おんだん is a warm, mild climate. The Daijisen character entry gives two senses: being warm, as in 暖気 だんき, "warmth; warm weather," 暖冬 だんとう, "mild winter; warm winter," 暖流 だんりゅう, "warm current," 温暖, 寒暖 かんだん, "cold and heat; (degree of) temperature," and 春暖 しゅんだん, "warm spring weather; spring warmth"; and making something warm, as in 暖房 and 暖炉 だんろ, "fireplace; hearth; stove."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [日](日.md), "sun, day," at the left and 爰 at the right, whose lower part looks like [友](友.md), "friend." Imagine a friend sitting in the sun, feeling warm. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 日 or 爰 contributes meaning or sound. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ダン | on | [暖房](../words/暖房.md) だんぼう — indoor heating; [温暖](../words/温暖.md) おんだん — warm; mild in climate |
| ノン | on | 暖気 のんき - easy; easygoing; carefree; a rarely used kanji form of a word usually written in kana; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| あたた.か | kun | 暖か あたたか - warm; mild; genial; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| あたた.かい | kun | [暖かい](../words/暖かい.md) あたたかい — warm; mild |
| あたた.まる | kun | [暖まる](../words/暖まる.md) あたたまる — get warm; warm oneself |
| あたた.める | kun | [暖める](../words/暖める.md) あたためる — warm; heat up |

In 暖房, 房 supplies ボウ. In 温暖, 温 supplies オン. In 暖か, 暖かい, 暖まる and 暖める, 暖 writes あたた and か, かい, まる or める is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. JMdict also writes 暖か, 暖かい, 暖まる and 暖める with 温.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ダン, with 暖流, 暖房 and 温暖 as examples; あたたか, with 暖かだ, and sets 温か beside it as a contrasting writing; あたたかい, with 暖かい; あたたまる, with 暖まる; and あたためる, with 暖める. The Joyo table does not list ノン.

KANJIDIC2 lists it and the Daijisen labels it 唐音, one of the later Chinese-derived readings, with 暖簾 のれん, "(short) curtain hung at shop entrance; split curtain used to divide spaces in a house," and 暖気 のんき among its difficult readings; both words are usually written in kana. Practice selects ダン and the three kun readings with linked word articles; ノン and あたた.か are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Common words

- [暖房](../words/暖房.md) だんぼう — indoor heating
- [温暖](../words/温暖.md) おんだん — warm; mild in climate
- [暖かい](../words/暖かい.md) あたたかい — warm; mild
- [暖まる](../words/暖まる.md) あたたまる — get warm; warm oneself
- [暖める](../words/暖める.md) あたためる — warm; heat up

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [温](温.md), "warm, mild," in 温暖 and [房](房.md), "room; tassel; bunch," in 暖房.
- [日](日.md), "sun, day," is the shape at the left, and [暗](暗.md), "dark," has it too. [段](段.md), "step; level," and [談](談.md), "converse; consult," are also read ダン. All are different kanji.
- [温](温.md), "warm, mild," and [熱](熱.md), "heat; fever," overlap the senses "warm" and "heat" that 暖 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "warm" as exclusive to any one character.

# 授 — grant; impart; teach

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

授 is handing something over to another person: granting an award, or imparting knowledge. [准教授](../words/准教授.md) じゅんきょうじゅ is an associate professor, [助教授](../words/助教授.md) じょきょうじゅ is the former Japanese title for an associate professor, [授業](../words/授業.md) じゅぎょう is a class or lesson and [教授](../words/教授.md) きょうじゅ is a professor or instruction.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense for ジュ: handing something over and bestowing it, as in 授与 じゅよ, "awarding; conferment; granting; presentation," 授賞 じゅしょう, "awarding a prize; giving an award," 授受 じゅじゅ, "giving and receiving; transferring; transfer; changing hands," 授戒 じゅかい, "handing down the precepts," 口授 こうじゅ, "oral instruction or teaching or method," 伝授 でんじゅ, "(giving) instruction (esp. in the secrets of an art, discipline, etc.)," 授業 and 教授. JMdict's entry for the verb 授ける さずける lists both halves of the meaning: "to grant; to give; to confer; to award" and "to teach; to instruct; to impart (knowledge)."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [手](手.md), "hand," at the left, written 扌, and [受](受.md), "receive; undergo," at the right. Imagine a hand passing something across to the person who receives it. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 受 contributes meaning or sound, although 授 and 受 are both read ジュ. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジュ | on | [准教授](../words/准教授.md) じゅんきょうじゅ — associate professor; [助教授](../words/助教授.md) じょきょうじゅ — associate professor (former Japanese title); [授業](../words/授業.md) じゅぎょう — class, lesson; [教授](../words/教授.md) きょうじゅ — professor; instruction |
| さず.ける | kun | 授ける さずける - to grant; to give; to confer; to award; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| さず.かる | kun | 授かる さずかる - to be granted (e.g. a title); to be awarded (e.g. a prize); to be gifted with (a talent); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 准教授, 准 supplies ジュン, 教 supplies キョウ and 授 supplies ジュ. In 助教授, 助 supplies ジョ. In 授業, 業 supplies ギョウ. In 教授, 教 supplies キョウ. In 授ける, 授 writes さず and ける is okurigana, the kana ending that follows the kanji. The same pattern gives 授かる with かる.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ジュ, with 授与, 伝授 and 教授 as examples; さずける, with 授ける; and さずかる, with 授かる. Practice selects ジュ, the only reading with linked word articles; the two kun readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words.

## Common words

- [准教授](../words/准教授.md) じゅんきょうじゅ — associate professor
- [助教授](../words/助教授.md) じょきょうじゅ — associate professor (former Japanese title)
- [授業](../words/授業.md) じゅぎょう — class, lesson
- [教授](../words/教授.md) きょうじゅ — professor; instruction

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [准](准.md), "quasi-; associate rank," and [教](教.md), "teach, instruct," in 准教授, [助](助.md), "help; rescue," in 助教授, and [業](業.md), "work, occupation," in 授業. 教授 repeats 教.
- [手](手.md), "hand," is the key at the left, written 扌, and [受](受.md), "receive; undergo," is the shape at the right; it is also read ジュ. [投](投.md), "throw," and [押](押.md), "push," have the same 扌 at the left with a different shape beside it, and [樹](樹.md), "tree; establish," is also read ジュ. All are different kanji.
- [与](与.md), "give; grant," [贈](贈.md), "give as a gift; confer," [教](教.md), "teach, instruct," [給](給.md), "pay; bestow," [受](受.md), "receive; undergo," and [訓](訓.md), "instruction; kun reading," overlap the senses "give," "grant," "teach" and "receive" that 授 and its words carry; the verb 授かる covers both being granted something and being taught it. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "give" or "teach" as exclusive to any one character.
