# 凡 — commonplace; mediocre

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 几 (no. 16) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凡 can express being ordinary or unremarkable, as in [平凡](../words/平凡.md)
へいぼん. It also has a broader general or overall association, used in
[凡例](../words/凡例.md) はんれい, a book's explanatory conventions or a map's
legend. Do not translate every occurrence as "mediocre."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the 几-shaped frame with one
ordinary dot 丶 inside. This is a memory aid for the current shape, not a
historical derivation or a claim about a phonetic component.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ボン | on | [平凡](../words/平凡.md) へいぼん — ordinary; unremarkable |
| ハン | on | [凡例](../words/凡例.md) はんれい — explanatory notes; legend |
| およ.そ | kun | 凡そ [およそ](../words/およそ.md) — approximately; generally; usually written in kana |
| おうよ.そ | kun, source-only | Preserved exactly from KANJIDIC2; no verified modern word example is supplied here. |
| すべ.て | kun | 凡て すべて — everything; a rare kanji spelling of the word taught under [全て](../words/全て.md) |

The two on-readings are selected for reading practice. The native readings
remain recognition or lookup information here. In particular, the source-only
おうよ.そ is not silently changed to おおよ.そ or treated as a verified word.
In 凡そ and 凡て, the final そ and て are written outside the kanji.

## Common words

- [平凡](../words/平凡.md) へいぼん — ordinary; unremarkable
- [凡例](../words/凡例.md) はんれい — explanatory notes; legend

For recognition, 凡そ corresponds to usually-kana [およそ](../words/およそ.md).
The rare spelling 凡て corresponds to [全て](../words/全て.md) すべて; the
pronunciation and the ordinary kana word are not thereby rare.

## Notes

- Read 凡例 as はんれい, not ぼんれい. The same pronunciation also belongs to
  判例 (はんれい), "judicial precedent," which is a different word.
- [丸](丸.md) concerns roundness or a circle, and [九](九.md) means nine.
  Compare the position of the dot and the long outer stroke rather than
  treating these similar shapes as interchangeable.
- [凹](凹.md) describes a concave or sunken shape, not mediocrity.

# 処 — deal with; dispose

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 几 (no. 16) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

In [処理](../words/処理.md) しょり, 処 contributes dealing with or handling something.
Other useful compounds concern responding to a situation or disposing of something.
The character also has place-related senses, but "place" does not explain 処理.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the sweeping left part clearing things past the little 几-shaped
stand on the right. This is a visual cue for "deal with," not a claim about historical components.

## Readings

The table focuses on ショ in compounds. The source's kun readings remain in the metadata.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ショ | on | [処理](../words/処理.md) しょり — processing; 対処 たいしょ — dealing with |

## Common words

- [処理](../words/処理.md) しょり — processing; handling
- 対処 たいしょ — dealing with; coping with
- 処分 しょぶん — disposal; also punishment, depending on context

## Notes

処理 is not automatically "throwing away": data can be processed without being discarded.
The right-hand outline distinguishes 処 from [外](外.md); [冬](冬.md) has two dots underneath instead.
[風](風.md) offers a looser comparison through its enclosing hooked outline.

# 館 — hall; large building

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 食 (no. 184) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

館 often names a **building with a particular purpose**, such as a library, cinema, or inn.
It is not a word you can freely add to any noun to name a building.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the roofed right-hand 官 as a large hall with stacked rooms,
and the [食](食.md)-shaped left side as a meal served inside. This is a visual aid, not historical evidence.

## Readings

This table is selective; metadata also retains たて.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カン | on | [図書館](../words/図書館.md) としょかん (library); 映画館 えいがかん (cinema) |
| やかた | kun | 館 やかた (mansion; manor, literary) |

## Common words

- [図書館](../words/図書館.md) としょかん — library
- 映画館 えいがかん — cinema; movie theater
- 旅館 りょかん — traditional Japanese inn

## Notes

館（やかた）is not the everyday word for any ordinary building. For beginner vocabulary, prioritize
the カン compounds above.

[院](院.md) also appears in institution names, but the two endings are not interchangeable.
[飯](飯.md) and [飲](飲.md) have similar left sides; recognize 官 on the right to identify 館.

# 因 — cause; factor

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

因 concerns what brings something about or what something depends on.
Learn it first in [原因](../words/原因.md) げんいん, "cause," and 要因 よういん, "main cause; factor."
These words help identify what lies behind a result, rather than naming the result itself.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 大 inside the enclosure as the big factor behind a problem. This is an invented learning image, not an etymology.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| イン | on | [原因](../words/原因.md) げんいん — cause; 要因 よういん — main factor |

The kun readings よ.る and ちな.む remain in the metadata; the examples here focus on the on-reading compounds.

## Common words

- [原因](../words/原因.md) げんいん — cause; source
- 要因 よういん — main cause; primary factor

## Notes

The outside is the enclosure radical 囗, not 口.
[困](困.md) contains 木 rather than 大; [回](回.md) contains another box.
理由 りゆう, "reason; grounds; motive," is a useful comparison with 原因, but explanations and causes do not always answer the same question.

# 思 — think, feel

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 心 (heart, no. 61) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

思 concerns thinking, believing, or having something in mind. In 思う, it can express both an opinion and a feeling.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** take the visible [田](田.md) above [心](心.md) as a "field of thoughts in the heart". This is a mnemonic for the modern outline, not a claim that a rice field explains the historical form.

## Readings

This table is selective; metadata preserves all source on/kun readings.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | 思考 しこう (thinking; thought) |
| おも.う | kun | [思う](../words/思う.md) おもう (think; believe) |
| おもえら.く | kun | classical, kanbun-style: 思えらく おもえらく ("I think that…"); not used in modern speech |
| おぼ.す | kun | 思し召し おぼしめし (honorific: will; opinion), 神の思し召し かみのおぼしめし (God's will) |

## Common words

- [思う](../words/思う.md) おもう — think, believe, feel
- 思い出 おもいで — memory, recollection
- 思考 しこう — thinking, thought

## Notes

[考える](../words/考える.md) かんがえる often emphasizes considering something or working it out; 思う often presents a view or impression. Their uses overlap, so this is not a strict logic-versus-emotion division.

[男](男.md) also has 田 above another part, but its lower part is [力](力.md), not 心. [意](意.md) has [音](音.md) above 心.
