# 絶 — sever; cease

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

絶 can express a connection or flow being cut off. It also has a "beyond comparison" side:
the useful word [絶対](../words/絶対.md) ぜったい means "absolutely; definitely."
Learn that word as a unit, not as a literal instruction to cut something.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the 糸 thread stopping at the 色-shaped part on the right.
This is a modern memory aid, not an etymology.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ゼツ | on | [絶対](../words/絶対.md) ぜったい — absolutely |
| た.える | kun | 絶える たえる — cease; die out |
| た.つ | kun | 絶つ たつ — sever; cut off |

## Common words

- [絶対](../words/絶対.md) ぜったい — absolutely; definitely
- 絶える たえる — come to an end; cease
- 絶つ たつ — cut off; sever

## Notes

絶える describes something ceasing; 絶つ describes cutting something off.
In 絶対, ゼツ + タイ becomes ぜったい, with a small っ.
The right-hand 色 shape distinguishes 絶 from [絵](絵.md), [紙](紙.md), and [給](給.md).

# 微 — minute; faint

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

微 concerns something very small, slight or difficult to notice.
[顕微鏡](../words/顕微鏡.md) けんびきょう is a microscope, used to see
minute things. [微妙](../words/微妙.md) びみょう concerns subtle or delicate
differences; a conversational judgment can instead be iffy or not very good.
Smallness alone does not predict every meaning of the complete word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 彳 on the left, a small
[山](山.md) above a 兀-shaped group in the middle, and 攵 on the right.
Imagine zooming in on a tiny mountain between the two side groups.
This is a memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ビ | on | [微妙](../words/微妙.md) びみょう - subtle; delicate; [顕微鏡](../words/顕微鏡.md) けんびきょう - microscope |
| かす.か | kun | 微か かすか - faint; slight; usually written in kana; no word article yet |

Practice selects ビ. 微か writes かす with 微 and the final か in kana;
it explains the preserved kun reading but is not selected without a linked
word article.

[微笑む](../words/微笑む.md) ほほえむ means smile. Learn the complete
native reading: it is not evidence that 微 independently supplies ほほ.
The linked article also records ほおえむ. Neither reading is obtained by
substituting ビ into part of the word.

## Common words

- [微妙](../words/微妙.md) びみょう - subtle; delicate; contextually iffy
- [顕微鏡](../words/顕微鏡.md) けんびきょう - microscope
- [微笑む](../words/微笑む.md) ほほえむ - smile

## Notes

- [復](復.md), return/repeat, [御](御.md), honorific prefix/control,
  and [律](律.md), rule/rhythm, share the left 彳. Their right-hand
  groups and meanings differ.
- Smallness overlaps [細](細.md), [小](小.md) and [少](少.md);
  faintness or weakness overlaps [薄](薄.md) and [弱](弱.md).
  Subtlety also overlaps [妙](妙.md), whose direct exclusion names 微.
- A slight degree also overlaps [軽](軽.md), lightweight/slight; both
  characters appear in 軽微 けいび, slight or insignificant. That direct
  comparison is excluded too, without claiming that 微 means light in weight.
- Here English "minute" means tiny, not the unit of time associated with
  [分](分.md). That card's title is "minute, part"; the shared English
  spelling is excluded from meaning choices without treating the Japanese
  meanings as interchangeable.
- The obsolete 微妙 reading みみょう is a separate dictionary entry with
  sublime or exquisite senses, not a replacement for modern びみょう.
- In 顕微鏡, [鏡](鏡.md) supplies きょう. The character 顕 has no card
  here yet; this does not change the complete word's reading.

# 育 — grow up; raise

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 肉 (in the 月-shaped lower part, no. 130) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

育 concerns growing up and bringing something up. [育つ](../words/育つ.md) そだつ
presents the child or plant as growing; 育てる そだてる presents someone as raising or nurturing it.
教育 きょういく connects the character with education and upbringing.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture growth taking place under the small upper "cap."
The lower 月-shaped part belongs to the 肉 radical here; this memory picture is not an origin claim.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| そだ.つ | kun | [育つ](../words/育つ.md) そだつ — grow up |
| そだ.てる | kun | 育てる そだてる — raise; nurture |
| イク | on | 教育 きょういく — education |

## Common words

- [育つ](../words/育つ.md) そだつ — grow; be brought up
- 育てる そだてる — raise; bring up
- 教育 きょういく — education; upbringing

## Notes

子供が育つ (こどもがそだつ) means "a child grows up"; 子供を育てる
(こどもをそだてる) means "raise a child."
The upper parts distinguish 育 from [背](背.md), [青](青.md), and [有](有.md).
Their 月-like shapes do not make their meanings or radical classifications identical.

# 御 — honorific; govern

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 彳 (no. 60) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

御 can mark respectful or polite wording. An honorific is an element that adds respect or politeness:
[御礼](../words/御礼.md) おれい uses 御 for お before 礼, "thanks."
The character also has control or governing meanings, so the polite-prefix use is not its whole range.

For shape recognition, separate the narrow 彳 on the left from the larger right-hand group, including
the small 卩 shape at the far right. No historical classification is proposed here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| お- | kun prefix | [御礼](../words/御礼.md) おれい — thanks |
| ゴ | on | 御飯 ごはん — cooked rice; meal |

Metadata preserves ギョ, おん-, and み- as well. Prefix readings are learned with their words.

## Common words

- [御礼](../words/御礼.md) おれい — thanks; gratitude
- 御飯 ごはん — cooked rice; meal; also written [ご飯](../words/ご飯.md)

## Notes

The spellings お礼 and ご飯 show that a polite prefix can be written in kana. Do not mechanically add
御 to every noun, or assume that the same prefix reading fits every word.
[彼](彼.md), [徒](徒.md), and [後](後.md) share only part of 御's outline: their right sides are different.

Metadata: [KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project).
Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entries 1270810, 1270590.

# 計 — measure; plan

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

計 concerns measuring, calculating, or planning. It appears in a time-measuring device,
[時計](../words/時計.md) とけい, and in 計画 けいかく, "plan."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** combine [言](言.md), words, with [十](十.md), ten:
imagine counting aloud while making a plan. This is a modern memory aid, not an etymology.

## Readings

This is a selective table; metadata also preserves はか.らう.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケイ | on | 計画 けいかく — plan; 合計 ごうけい — total |
| はか.る | kun | 計る はかる — measure; time |

## Common words

- [時計](../words/時計.md) とけい — clock; watch
- 計画 けいかく — plan
- 合計 ごうけい — total amount
- 計る はかる — measure; time

## Notes

時計 is not a regular on+on reading of [時](時.md) and 計. Learn the word as とけい.
計 has a simple 十 on the right; compare the more complex right side of [試](試.md).
For timing something, 時間を計る (じかんをはかる) uses 計る.
