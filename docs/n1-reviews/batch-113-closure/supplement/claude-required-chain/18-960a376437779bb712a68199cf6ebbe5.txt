# 丁寧 (ていねい) — polite; careful

## Meaning

丁寧 describes courteous behavior or careful, thorough work. It is a na-adjective: 丁寧な + noun, 丁寧に + verb, and 丁寧です at the end of a sentence.

- 店員は丁寧に説明してくれました。 (てんいんはていねいにせつめいしてくれました。) — The shop assistant explained it politely and carefully.
- 字を丁寧に書いてください。 (じをていねいにかいてください。) — Please write the characters carefully.

## How the kanji combine

丁 and [寧](../kanji/寧.md) form the established Sino-Japanese word ていねい. Learn the whole expression; separate character glosses do not reliably predict both its courtesy and carefulness senses.

## Synonyms and antonyms

失礼 (しつれい), "rude," contrasts with the politeness sense. 雑 (ざつ), "rough; careless," contrasts with careful work. No single opposite covers both equally well.

## Distinctive meaning

丁寧 does not simply mean "slow." Work can be quick and still 丁寧 if it is attentive and well done. In language study, 丁寧語 (ていねいご) refers to polite language such as です and ます; that is a narrower grammatical term.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1427360, senses 1 and 2.

# 体系 (たいけい) - system; organized whole

## Meaning

体系 is a system in which related parts form an organized whole. It often
concerns a body of knowledge or theory, but it is not limited to ideas.
It is a noun and can form compounds such as 法体系 (ほうたいけい), "legal system."

- この本で日本語の文法体系を学びます。 (このほんでにほんごのぶんぽうたいけいをまなびます。) - We will study the system of Japanese grammar in this book.
- 知識を一つの体系にまとめました。 (ちしきをひとつのたいけいにまとめました。) - I organized the knowledge into a single system.

体系的に (たいけいてきに) means systematically; 体系的な説明
(たいけいてきなせつめい) is a systematic explanation. The 的 form supplies
the adjective-like construction.

## How the kanji combine

[体](../kanji/体.md) contributes a body or whole; [系](../kanji/系.md) contributes connected
parts or a system. The relation highlights components organized into a
coherent whole. タイ + ケイ gives たいけい; it does not describe a person's
physical build simply because it begins with 体.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[体制](体制.md) (たいせい) often emphasizes an operating or institutional
setup. [組織](組織.md) (そしき) and [系統](系統.md) (けいとう) also have
system or organization uses. [構造](構造.md) (こうぞう) emphasizes structure.
These meanings overlap; they do not create an absolute ideas-versus-people rule.

## Distinctive meaning

体系 concerns the relations that make parts into a system, not just a
random list of related items. A 体系的な説明 has an organized framework;
it is not automatically correct or complete merely because it is systematic.
Keep the homophone 体形 (たいけい), "bodily form," separate.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project),
entry 1409390, sense 1.

# 兵隊 (へいたい) - soldier; troops

## Meaning

兵隊 can name troops or the military collectively, or an individual soldier,
especially a rank-and-file one. It is a noun.
The dictionary also records a colloquial figurative use for an ordinary employee
or underling, viewed as one of the people carrying out orders.

- 兵隊が橋を渡っていきました。 (へいたいがはしをわたっていきました。) - The soldiers went across the bridge.
- 祖父は若い頃、兵隊でした。 (そふはわかいころ、へいたいでした。) - My grandfather was a soldier when he was young.

## How the kanji combine

[兵](../kanji/兵.md) contributes soldiers; [隊](../kanji/隊.md) (タイ) contributes
a unit or organized group. The compound names a group of soldiers and can also
identify a member of it: ヘイ + タイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[兵士](兵士.md) (へいし) also names an individual soldier.
[軍隊](軍隊.md) (ぐんたい) and [軍](軍.md) (ぐん) overlap with the collective
troops/armed-forces sense. [武士](武士.md) (ぶし) is a samurai or historical
warrior, not the general name for everyone serving in a modern military.

## Distinctive meaning

Do not reduce every occurrence to an entire army: 一人の兵隊 (ひとりのへいたい)
can be one soldier. Conversely, a collective reading need not specify a single person.
The figurative employee sense can sound dismissive and is not a neutral default
job title. Although a broad source gloss includes "sailor," the military setting
does not make 兵隊 a general word for a civilian sailor or seafarer.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1506590, senses 1-3.

# 寧ろ (むしろ) - rather; if anything

## Meaning

寧ろ is an adverb that selects a better description or alternative: "rather,"
"instead," or "if anything." It is usually written むしろ. A useful pattern is
Aというより、むしろB, "rather than A, it is more accurately B."

- 今日は寒いというより、むしろ涼しいです。 (きょうはさむいというより、むしろすずしいです。) - Today is cool rather than cold.
- 迷惑ではありません。むしろ助かります。 (めいわくではありません。むしろたすかります。) - It is not a bother. If anything, it helps.

The first example refines a description. The second replaces an expected
negative evaluation with a positive one.

## How the kanji combine

[寧](../kanji/寧.md)ろ is an established spelling of the native adverb むしろ; ろ is written in
kana. The character 寧 has associations with calmness or carefulness elsewhere,
but those component glosses do not directly explain the adverb's comparison.
Learn the complete expression rather than inventing a literal derivation.

## Synonyms and antonyms

どちらかといえば (どちらかといえば), "if I had to choose," can introduce a
qualified preference. 寧ろ can make a firmer correction to an earlier description.
[でも](でも.md), "but," signals contrast without necessarily choosing the more
appropriate alternative.

## Distinctive meaning

English "rather" sometimes merely intensifies an adjective, as in "rather cold."
むしろ is not that degree adverb: it compares alternatives, even when one is
understood from context. It does not mean "better" in every sense of quality;
the sentence must establish what is being preferred or corrected.
