# 慢 — arrogance; slackness

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 心 (as 忄, no. 61), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

慢 concerns an attitude that is either too pleased with itself or too loose
and slow. [自慢](../words/自慢.md) じまん is pride in one's own achievements
or possessions, or boasting about them. 高慢 こうまん is haughty or
arrogant, and 慢心 まんしん is self-conceit.

[我慢](../words/我慢.md) がまん is endurance or self-restraint in current
dictionary usage. The Digital Daijisen character entry files both 我慢 and
自慢 under its conceit sense, so keep two facts apart: the character
evidence for 慢 and the modern meaning of the complete word 我慢.

The same entry also gives a slack or negligent attitude, as in 怠慢
たいまん, and a sluggish pace, as in 緩慢 かんまん, slow or sluggish, and
慢性 まんせい, chronic (of an illness).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 忄, the left-hand form of
[心](心.md), beside 曼. Inside 曼, a [日](日.md) sits above 罒 and 又.
Imagine a relaxed heart watching the sun idle above a net-like 罒:
swaggering or slack, depending on the scene. This is a memory picture for
the modern shape, not a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| マン | on | [自慢](../words/自慢.md) じまん - pride; boasting; [我慢](../words/我慢.md) がまん - endurance; self-restraint |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects マン. In 自慢, 自
supplies ジ; in 我慢, 我 supplies ガ; 慢 is まん in both words. The
official Joyo table lists マン only, with 慢性, 怠慢 and 自慢 as examples.

## Common words

- [自慢](../words/自慢.md) じまん - pride; boasting
- [我慢](../words/我慢.md) がまん - endurance; self-restraint
- 慢性 まんせい - chronic (of an illness)
- 怠慢 たいまん - negligence; neglect
- 緩慢 かんまん - slow; sluggish; lax

## Notes

- [慣](慣.md), "accustomed," [憎](憎.md), "hate; detest," and
  [怪](怪.md), "suspicious; uncanny," share the left-hand 忄 but have
  different right-hand parts and different meanings.
- The slow and dull side, as in 緩慢, overlaps [遅](遅.md), "late; slow,"
  and [鈍](鈍.md), "dull; blunt."
- The endurance and self-restraint of 我慢 overlaps [凌](凌.md),
  "endure; surpass," [負](負.md), "lose; bear a burden," and
  [節](節.md), "joint; season; restraint." [永](永.md), "eternity;
  enduring duration," shares the English stem "endur-" but concerns
  lasting, not putting up with something. These direct comparisons are
  excluded from choices, not treated as identical in every compound.
- Learn 我慢 as a complete word. The dictionary grouping of 我慢 with the
  conceit sense of 慢 does not make the modern word mean "arrogance," and
  the pride in 自慢 is not endurance.
- 怠 in 怠慢 and 緩 in 緩慢 have no kanji cards here yet; this does not
  change the complete words' readings.

# 縫 — sew; stitch

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

縫 concerns sewing: passing thread through cloth to join or mend it.
[縫う](../words/縫う.md) ぬう is the native verb for sewing or stitching,
and [裁縫](../words/裁縫.md) さいほう is sewing or needlework. 縫合
ほうごう is sewing together, as when a wound is stitched up, and 縫い目
ぬいめ is a seam or stitch.

縫う has a second, figurative sense: to weave one's way through something,
such as a crowd. That sense moves between obstacles; it does not mean
sewing anything.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [糸](糸.md) on the left
and 逢 on the right. Inside 逢, 夂 and 丰 are carried along by the 辶-shaped
sweep underneath. Imagine a thread running along a winding path. This is a
memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ホウ | on | [裁縫](../words/裁縫.md) さいほう - sewing; needlework |
| ぬ.う | kun | [縫う](../words/縫う.md) ぬう - sew; stitch; weave one's way through |

Practice selects ホウ and ぬ.う, each with a linked example. 縫う writes ぬ
with 縫 and う in kana; it is a transitive godan verb. The official Joyo
table lists ホウ, with 縫合, 縫製 and 裁縫 as examples, and ぬう, with 縫う
and 縫い目.

## Common words

- [縫う](../words/縫う.md) ぬう - sew; stitch
- [裁縫](../words/裁縫.md) さいほう - sewing; needlework
- 縫合 ほうごう - stitching up a wound; suture
- 縫製 ほうせい - sewing (by machine)
- 縫い目 ぬいめ - seam; stitch

## Notes

- [縮](縮.md), "shrink; contract," [練](練.md), "practice; refine," and
  [緒](緒.md), "cord; beginning," share the left-hand 糸 but have different
  right-hand parts and different meanings.
- A needle is the sewing tool, not the act: [針](針.md), "needle; pin,"
  overlaps the sewing words 縫う and 裁縫 and is excluded from meaning
  choices for that reason.
- The weaving-through sense of 縫う overlaps [通](通.md), "pass through;
  commute," and [経](経.md), "pass through; sutra." These direct
  comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as identical in every
  compound.
- In 裁縫, 裁 supplies サイ. It has no kanji card here yet; this does not
  change the complete word's reading.

# 飢 — starve; be hungry

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 食 (as 飠, no. 184), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

飢 concerns hunger severe enough to amount to starvation.
[飢える](../words/飢える.md) うえる means to starve or be famished, and it
also describes being starved of something, such as affection, or hungry for
knowledge. [飢饉](../words/飢饉.md) ききん is a famine or crop failure and
also a chronic shortage, as in 水飢饉, a water shortage. 飢餓 きが is
starvation, and 飢え うえ is hunger.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: being hungry for lack
of food, as in 飢餓, and a shortage of food after a poor harvest, as in
飢饉. It adds that 饑 can be used interchangeably with 飢.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 飠, the left-hand form of
[食](食.md), beside 几. Imagine food set out beside a small table-like 几
with nothing left on it. This is a memory picture for the modern shape, not
a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キ | on | [飢饉](../words/飢饉.md) ききん - famine; chronic shortage |
| う.える | kun | [飢える](../words/飢える.md) うえる - starve; hunger for |

Practice selects キ and う.える, each with a linked example. 飢える writes
う with 飢 and える in kana; it is an intransitive ichidan verb, so える
stays in forms such as 飢えて and 飢えない. JMdict also lists かつえる,
restricted to the spellings 飢える and 餓える; it is not a KANJIDIC2 or
Joyo reading, so practice does not select it. The official Joyo table lists
キ, with 飢餓, and うえる, with 飢える and 飢え.

## Common words

- [飢える](../words/飢える.md) うえる - starve; hunger for
- [飢饉](../words/飢饉.md) ききん - famine; chronic shortage
- 飢餓 きが - starvation; famine
- 飢え うえ - hunger; starvation

## Notes

- [凡](凡.md), "commonplace; mediocre," and [処](処.md), "deal with;
  dispose," share 几 with 飢 but differ in meaning. [館](館.md), "hall;
  large building," shares the 食 component, written 飠 at the left, but
  names a building.
- [食](食.md), "eat, food," supplies the 飠 form and the hunger
  association, but eating and food are not meanings of 飢.
- A chronic shortage, as in 飢饉, overlaps [乏](乏.md), "scarce; lacking,"
  [欠](欠.md), "lack; gap," and [貧](貧.md), "poor; impoverished." The
  crop-failure sense shares the word "failure" with [敗](敗.md), "failure;
  defeat."
- Being starved of or hungry for something, as with 飢える, overlaps
  [欲](欲.md), "desire," and [恋](恋.md), "romantic love; longing." These
  direct meaning comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as
  identical in every compound.
