# 援 — aid; assistance

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

援 concerns reaching out to help someone. [援助](../words/援助.md) えんじょ
is aid or assistance, and [応援](../words/応援.md) おうえん is support,
including cheering for someone. 声援 せいえん is cheering or shouted
encouragement, 支援 しえん is support or backing, 救援 きゅうえん is relief
or rescue, and 援軍 えんぐん is reinforcement.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: reaching out a hand
to help, as in 援護, 援助 and 応援, and drawing in an example from
elsewhere, as in 援引 and 援用. It lists たすける and ひく as kun readings.
Those readings are not in KANJIDIC2 or the official Joyo table, so practice
does not select them. Its supplementary note says the 援 in 義援金 and
援護射撃 stands in for 捐 and 掩, respectively.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 扌, the left-hand form of
[手](手.md), beside 爰. Inside 爰, ⺤ sits above a part that contains
[友](友.md). Imagine a hand reaching out to a friend to lend aid. This is a
memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| エン | on | [援助](../words/援助.md) えんじょ - aid; assistance; [応援](../words/応援.md) おうえん - support; cheering |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects エン. In 援助, 援 is
えん and 助 is じょ; in 応援, [応](応.md) is おう and 援 is えん. The official
Joyo table lists エン only, with 援助, 応援 and 声援 as examples.

## Common words

- [援助](../words/援助.md) えんじょ - aid; assistance
- [応援](../words/応援.md) おうえん - support; cheering
- 声援 せいえん - cheering; encouragement
- 支援 しえん - support; backing
- 救援 きゅうえん - relief; rescue
- 援軍 えんぐん - reinforcement

## Notes

- [暖](暖.md), "warm; warm up," shares the right-hand 爰 with 援, and
  [授](授.md), "grant; impart; teach," and [担](担.md), "shoulder; take on,"
  share 扌 at the left. Their meanings differ from 援's.
- Helping and rescuing overlap [助](助.md), "help; rescue," [丞](丞.md),
  "assist; aid," and [支](支.md), "support," because KANJIDIC2 glosses 援
  "help" and "save," JMdict glosses 援助 "assistance; aid; support," and the
  応援, 声援 and 支援 entries also include "support." [保](保.md),
  "protect; preserve," overlaps the "save" sense, and [貯](貯.md), "save up;
  store," shares its English word although it concerns putting money or
  goods aside.
- Encouraging and cheering, as in 声援 and the second sense of 応援, overlap
  [励](励.md), "encourage; be diligent," and [勧](勧.md), "recommend;
  encourage." [根](根.md), "root; foundation," shares the stem "root" with
  the "rooting (for)" glosses of 応援 and 声援, although it never concerns
  cheering.
- Reinforcement, as in 援軍 and JMdict's first sense of 応援, overlaps
  [補](補.md), "supplement; make good," and [協](協.md), "cooperate; join
  forces."
- Drawing in an example from elsewhere, the second Daijisen sense with 援引
  and 援用, overlaps [例](例.md), "example; precedent," [典](典.md),
  "reference texts; rules; ceremony," and [引](引.md), "pull; draw." These
  direct meaning comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as
  identical in every compound.

# 応 — respond; comply

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

応 is answering a call or demand and acting in line with it. [応じる](../words/応じる.md) おうじる and [応ずる](../words/応ずる.md) おうずる mean to respond or comply, [応対](../words/応対.md) おうたい is attending to people who come to you, [応接](../words/応接.md) おうせつ is receiving visitors, [応援](../words/応援.md) おうえん is support or cheering, [応用](../words/応用.md) おうよう is practical application, and [一応](../words/一応.md) いちおう is a hedge: tentatively, roughly or just in case.

The Daijisen character entry gives four senses: answering a question or call, as in 応答 おうとう, "reply; answer; response"; consenting; moving in response to an outside request or action, as in 応募 おうぼ, "application; entry," 反応 はんのう, "reaction; response," and 対応 たいおう, "dealing with; coping with; handling"; and being suitable or well matched, as in 相応 そうおう, "suitable; appropriate." The Daijisen heading also shows the older form 應, which this card does not teach.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find a roof-like 广 cover at the top left over [心](心.md), "heart, mind," at the bottom. Imagine a heart under a shelter, ready to answer whenever someone calls. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 心 contributes meaning or sound, and 心 is simply the key shape at the bottom. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| オウ | on | [一応](../words/一応.md) いちおう — tentatively; roughly; [応じる](../words/応じる.md) おうじる — to respond; comply; [応ずる](../words/応ずる.md) おうずる — respond; comply; accept; [応対](../words/応対.md) おうたい — attending to people; handling callers; [応接](../words/応接.md) おうせつ — receiving visitors; reception; [応援](../words/応援.md) おうえん — support; cheering; [応用](../words/応用.md) おうよう — practical application |
| ヨウ | on | no common word reads 応 as ヨウ; the ヨウ of 応用 おうよう belongs to 用; not selected for questions here |
| -ノウ | on | 反応 はんのう - reaction; response; 順応 じゅんのう - adaptation; accommodation; the ノ sound occurs inside compounds after ん; not selected for questions here |
| こた.える | kun | 応える こたえる - to respond; to answer; to meet (expectations); not selected for questions here |
| あた.る | kun | retained from KANJIDIC2 only; no word was found that uses it; not selected for questions here |
| まさに | kun | retained from KANJIDIC2 only; JMdict gives 正に, 将に and 当に as spellings of まさに, which it marks as usually written in kana alone; not selected for questions here |

In 一応, 一 supplies イチ. In 応じる and 応ずる, 応 supplies おう and じる or ずる are verb endings. In 応対, 対 supplies タイ. In 応接, 接 supplies セツ. In 応援, 援 supplies エン. In 応用, 応 is read オウ and 用 supplies ヨウ.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows オウ, with 応答, 応用 and 呼応 as examples, and こたえる, with the spelling contrast 応える ⇔ 答える; its remarks column notes that 反応 and 順応 are read ハンノウ and ジュンノウ, so the ノ is a sound change after ん, not a separate reading. The table does not list ヨウ, あた.る or まさに.

KANJIDIC2 and the Daijisen character entry list ヨウ, the 漢 reading, alongside オウ, the 呉 reading, two historical layers of borrowed Chinese readings. The Daijisen character entry also lists いらえる; KANJIDIC2 does not, so it is not part of the metadata here. Practice selects オウ, the only reading with linked word articles. Being outside the table is not proof that a reading is invalid.

## Common words

- [一応](../words/一応.md) いちおう — tentatively; roughly; just in case
- [応じる](../words/応じる.md) おうじる — to respond; comply
- [応ずる](../words/応ずる.md) おうずる — respond; comply; accept
- [応対](../words/応対.md) おうたい — attending to people; handling callers
- [応接](../words/応接.md) おうせつ — receiving visitors; reception
- [応援](../words/応援.md) おうえん — support; cheering
- [応用](../words/応用.md) おうよう — practical application

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [一](一.md), "one," in 一応, [対](対.md), "oppose; face," in 応対, [接](接.md), "touch; join," in 応接, [援](援.md), "aid; assistance," in 応援 and [用](用.md), "use, purpose," in 応用.
- [広](広.md), "wide, spacious," and [庁](庁.md), "government agency," share the 广 cover at the top left but have different insides, and [欧](欧.md), "Europe (abbreviation)," also reads オウ.
- [答](答.md), "answer," [承](承.md), "accept; undertake," [受](受.md), "receive; undergo," [適](適.md), "suitable," and [対](対.md) each overlap a sense of 応: JMdict glosses 応える as "to respond; to answer," 応じる includes "to accept (an invitation, offer, etc.)," and 相応 is "suitable; appropriate." All of them are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "answer" or "accept" as exclusive to any one character.
- [募](募.md), "recruit; solicit," is a contrast, not an overlap. In 応募 おうぼ, 募 is the recruiting side and 応 the responding side, and JMdict lists "to apply (in response to recruitment)" among the senses of 応じる. Applying is a response to a recruitment, not the same act, so 募 is not kept out of this card's quiz options.
