# 隊 — squad; troop

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (as left-hand 阝, no. 170) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

隊 is an organized group that acts as one body, most often a unit of
soldiers. [軍隊](../words/軍隊.md) ぐんたい is the armed forces or troops,
and [兵隊](../words/兵隊.md) へいたい is troops or a rank-and-file soldier.
隊 can also stand alone, or serve as a suffix, for a party, crew, team or
squad. 部隊 ぶたい is a unit or force, 楽隊 がくたい is a band, 隊員
たいいん is a member of such a group, and 隊長 たいちょう is its captain or
commander.

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: an organized group
that acts as one body, as in 隊員 and 隊長, and especially a group of
soldiers, as in 軍隊 and 部隊.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 阝, the left-hand form of 阜,
beside a right-hand part containing 豕. Imagine animals filing up a slope in
formation, like a squad on a hillside. This is a memory picture for the
modern shape, not a verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| タイ | on | [軍隊](../words/軍隊.md) ぐんたい - armed forces; troops; [兵隊](../words/兵隊.md) へいたい - soldier; troops |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects タイ, which stays たい
in 軍隊 and 兵隊 with no sound change. The official Joyo table lists タイ,
with 隊列, 軍隊 and 部隊 as examples.

## Common words

- [軍隊](../words/軍隊.md) ぐんたい - armed forces; troops
- [兵隊](../words/兵隊.md) へいたい - soldier; troops
- 部隊 ぶたい - unit; force; troops
- 隊員 たいいん - member of a unit or team
- 隊長 たいちょう - captain; commander
- 楽隊 がくたい - band; orchestra

## Notes

- [除](除.md), "remove; exclude," and [陽](陽.md), "sunshine," share 阝 at
  the left but differ in meaning. [象](象.md), "elephant; image; shape," is
  keyed under 豕, the pig-like shape inside 隊's right-hand part.
- Soldiers and troops overlap [軍](軍.md), "army; troops," [兵](兵.md),
  "soldier; troops," [武](武.md), "military; warrior," and [士](士.md),
  "warrior; professional."
- A group or party overlaps [団](団.md), "group; association," [組](組.md),
  "group; assemble," [党](党.md), "political party; faction," [族](族.md),
  "tribe, family group," and [群](群.md), "flock; cluster; crowd."
  [社](社.md), "company, shrine," shares the word "company" with JMdict's
  "company (of troops)."
- Members and divisions, as in 隊員 and 部隊, overlap [員](員.md), "member,
  employee," [部](部.md), "section; department," [科](科.md), "department;
  course," and [課](課.md), "lesson; organizational division."
- JMdict glosses 隊 itself with "body" and "unit," and 部隊 with "force."
  These share words with [体](体.md), "body, form," [身](身.md), "one's
  body; oneself," [秒](秒.md), "second as a time unit," [勢](勢.md),
  "force; momentum," and [協](協.md), "cooperate; join forces." These
  direct meaning comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as
  identical in every compound.
- [系](系.md), "lineage; system," declares the pair 系–隊, so this card does

# 需 — demand; need

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 雨 (no. 173), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

需 concerns something wanted or required. [需要](../words/需要.md)
じゅよう is demand or need, including economic demand, and
[必需品](../words/必需品.md) ひつじゅひん are necessities or essential
items. 需給 じゅきゅう is supply and demand, 内需 ないじゅ is domestic
demand, and 特需 とくじゅ is emergency demand or special procurement,
particularly in wartime.

The Digital Daijisen character entry glosses 需 as needing something, and
as a demand (もとめ), and it lists もとめる as a kun reading. That reading is
not in KANJIDIC2 or the official Joyo table, so practice does not select
it.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [雨](雨.md) above 而. Imagine
a figure standing under the rain, waiting for something it needs. This is a
memory picture for the modern shape, not a verified origin or
sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジュ | on | [需要](../words/需要.md) じゅよう - demand; need; [必需品](../words/必需品.md) ひつじゅひん - necessities; essential items |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects ジュ. Keep じゅ
distinct from じゅう: 需要 is じゅよう, not じゅうよう. The official Joyo
table lists ジュ, with 需要, 需給 and 必需品 as examples.

## Common words

- [需要](../words/需要.md) じゅよう - demand; need
- [必需品](../words/必需品.md) ひつじゅひん - necessities; essential items
- 需給 じゅきゅう - supply and demand
- 内需 ないじゅ - domestic demand
- 特需 とくじゅ - emergency demand; special procurement

## Notes

- [震](震.md), "quake; tremble," [零](零.md), "zero; spill," and
  [雲](雲.md), "cloud," share 雨 at the top but differ in meaning.
- Need and necessity overlap [要](要.md), "necessity; essentials," and
  the wanting in 需要 overlaps [欲](欲.md), "desire."
- Asking for something overlaps [求](求.md), "seek; request," and
  [頼](頼.md), "request; rely." These direct meaning comparisons are
  excluded from choices, not treated as identical in every compound.

# 系 — lineage; system

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

系 concerns things connected in a line, whether a line of descent or a
connected system. [系統](../words/系統.md) けいとう is a system or a
lineage, and also a group, family, party or school of thought.
[体系](../words/体系.md) たいけい is an organized system or whole. 家系
かけい is a family lineage, 系譜 けいふ is a genealogy or family tree, and
系列 けいれつ is a series, or a keiretsu group of affiliated businesses. On
its own or as a suffix, 系 means a system, lineage or group; colloquially it
also marks a type, which JMdict glosses "-type; -ish."

The Digital Daijisen character entry gives two senses: things connected
one after another like a thread, as in 系統 and 家系, and an organized
whole, as in 体系. JMdict also lists special senses for mathematics,
geology and taxonomy.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find 丿 above [糸](糸.md).
Imagine one stroke starting a thread that runs on and on, linking one
generation to the next. This is a memory picture for the modern shape, not a
verified origin or sound-component analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケイ | on | [系統](../words/系統.md) けいとう - system; lineage; [体系](../words/体系.md) たいけい - system; organized whole |

KANJIDIC2 supplies no kun reading. Practice selects ケイ. In 家系, the
word meaning a family lineage is かけい; the same spelling also names a
ramen style, which is not taught here. The official Joyo table lists ケイ,
with 系統, 系列 and 体系 as examples.

## Common words

- [系統](../words/系統.md) けいとう - system; lineage
- [体系](../words/体系.md) たいけい - system; organized whole
- 系列 けいれつ - series; group of affiliated businesses
- 系譜 けいふ - genealogy; family tree
- 家系 かけい - family lineage

## Notes

- [糸](糸.md), "thread; yarn," is the lower component of 系.
  [約](約.md), "promise; approximately," and [絶](絶.md), "sever; cease,"
  share 糸 but at the left, and they differ in meaning.
- A line of descent overlaps [統](統.md), "unify; lineage," [孫](孫.md),
  "grandchild; descendants," [祖](祖.md), "ancestor," [族](族.md), "tribe,
  family group," [親](親.md), "parent; close," [姓](姓.md), "surname;
  family name," and [氏](氏.md), "clan; family name."
- A system or group overlaps [制](制.md), "system; regulate," [型](型.md),
  "mold; type," [類](類.md), "kind; category," [派](派.md), "faction;
  school of thought," [党](党.md), "political party; faction,"
  [団](団.md), "group; association," [組](組.md), "group; assemble," and
  [隊](隊.md), "squad; troop." This card declares the pair 系–隊.
- A relation, series or line overlaps [係](係.md), "connection; person in
  charge," [仲](仲.md), "relations between people," [並](並.md), "line up;
  parallel," [番](番.md), "turn in a series," [線](線.md), "line; track,"
  and [弧](弧.md), "arc; curved line."
- A school of thought shares the word "school" with [校](校.md),
  "school," and [級](級.md), "grade; school class," though those kanji do
  not mean a school of thought. An organization shares its word with
  [課](課.md), "lesson; organizational division," because JMdict glosses
  体系 as "system; organization."
- An organized whole or a series overlaps [総](総.md), "whole; overall;
  total," [全](全.md), "whole; complete," [連](連.md), "link; take along,"
  and [順](順.md), "order; sequence."
- JMdict's 系統 also glosses a close evolutionary relationship, which
  shares the word "close" with [封](封.md), "seal; close." These direct
  meaning comparisons are excluded from choices, not treated as identical
