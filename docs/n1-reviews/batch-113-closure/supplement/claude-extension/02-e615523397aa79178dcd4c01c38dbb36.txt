# 評 — appraise; reputation

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149), at the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

評 means to weigh how good or bad something is and, from that, the reputation it earns. [批評](../words/批評.md) ひひょう is critique or review, [評価](../words/評価.md) ひょうか is assessment or appraisal, [評判](../words/評判.md) ひょうばん is reputation and [評論](../words/評論.md) ひょうろん is critical writing.

The Daijisen character entry gives two senses. The first is weighing the good and bad of something, as in 評議 ひょうぎ, "conference; discussion," 書評 しょひょう, "book review," and 論評 ろんぴょう, "comment; criticism; critique; review." The second is the reputation that follows, as in 好評 こうひょう, "favorable reception; good reputation; popularity," 定評 ていひょう, "established reputation," and 不評 ふひょう, "bad reputation; poor reception; unpopularity; disrepute." Its noun entry defines 評 ひょう as discussing the worth or the good and bad points of something, or the piece written about it, and JMdict glosses the noun "criticism; commentary; review."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [言](言.md), "say; words," at the left and [平](平.md), "flat; peaceful," at the right. Imagine words spoken to level out the good and bad points of something until a fair verdict remains. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 平 contributes meaning or sound, although 平 also has ヒョウ among its on readings. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヒョウ | on | [批評](../words/批評.md) ひひょう — critique; review; [評価](../words/評価.md) ひょうか — assessment; appraisal; [評判](../words/評判.md) ひょうばん — reputation; public estimation; [評論](../words/評論.md) ひょうろん — critique; critical essay |

In 批評, 批 supplies ヒ and 評 reads ヒョウ; in 評価, 価 supplies カ; in 評判, 判 supplies バン, an on reading of 判 besides ハン; and in 評論, 論 supplies ロン. In 論評 ろんぴょう, the sound changes to ぴょう after the ン of 論. The Daijisen character entry marks ヒョウ, which it also spells ヒャウ in historical kana, with （慣）, its label for a customary reading.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows ヒョウ, with 評価, 評判 and 定評 as examples. Practice selects ヒョウ, the only reading.

## Common words

- [批評](../words/批評.md) ひひょう — critique; review
- [評価](../words/評価.md) ひょうか — assessment; appraisal
- [評判](../words/評判.md) ひょうばん — reputation; public estimation
- [評論](../words/評論.md) ひょうろん — critique; critical essay

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [批](批.md), "criticize; critique," in 批評, [価](価.md), "worth; price," in 評価, [判](判.md), "judge; seal," in 評判 and [論](論.md), "argument; discourse," in 評論.
- [言](言.md), "say; words," is the key at the left, and [平](平.md), "flat; peaceful," is the part at the right. [氷](氷.md), "ice; freeze," [兵](兵.md), "soldier; troops," [表](表.md), "surface; chart," and [標](標.md), "mark; signpost; goal," are also read ヒョウ. All are different kanji.
- [批](批.md), "criticize; critique," [判](判.md), "judge; seal," [価](価.md), "worth; price," [値](値.md), "value; price," [論](論.md), "argument; discourse," [議](議.md), "deliberate; debate," and [賛](賛.md), "approve; praise," overlap the senses of critiquing, judging, valuing, discussing and praising that 評 and its words carry. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "appraise" or "reputation" as exclusive to any one character.

# 試 — test; try

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 言 (no. 149) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

試 means testing or trying something. [試験](../words/試験.md) しけん can be an exam or a test of how something works;
試す ためす is the everyday verb "try out; test."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture [言](言.md) as spoken test instructions, beside the angled 式 shape.
Imagine reading the instructions before trying an experiment. This is a memory aid, not a historical analysis.

| こころ.みる | kun | 試みる こころみる — attempt |

## Common words

- 試す ためす — try out
- 試みる こころみる — attempt
- 試合 しあい — match; game

## Notes

試 and [験](験.md) occur together in 試験, but they are different characters.
試合 is しあい, not a regular on+on combination; remember the word's established reading.

# 想 — thought; conception

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 心 (no. 61) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

想 concerns thoughts and what one pictures or conceives. [予想](../words/予想.md) よそう is an
expectation or prediction; 想像 そうぞう is imagination. 感想 かんそう is an impression or reaction.
The whole word tells you what kind of thinking is involved.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** [相](相.md), with 木 and 目 shapes, sits over [心](心.md).
Imagine seeing a tree and keeping its image in mind. This is a mnemonic, not a derivation.

# 察 — observe; infer

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

察 concerns finding things out: looking closely in [観察](../words/観察.md) かんさつ, or judging from clues in
察する さっする. Observation and inference are connected, but observing something is not the same as guessing what it means.
Learn the complete word to know which activity is intended.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** look beneath 宀 for the 祭 shape, with 示 at the bottom.
Imagine inspecting all the details under a roof. This is a modern visual association, not etymology.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| サツ | on | [観察](../words/観察.md) かんさつ — observation; 警察 けいさつ — police |

## Common words

- [観察](../words/観察.md) かんさつ — observation; 観察する means to observe
- 警察 けいさつ — police
- 察する さっする — infer; sense; judge

## Notes

In 察する, サツ combines with する as さっする, with a small っ.
警察 names the police; do not try to translate every compound as the separate verb "guess."
Unlike [寒](寒.md), 察 ends in a 示-shaped part. [寝](寝.md) and [家](家.md) have different forms beneath the same roof.

[JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entries 1214900, 1298740, 1252390.
