# 種 — seed; kind

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

種 can be a physical seed or the kind to which something belongs.
The noun 種 たね is useful for plants; [種類](../words/種類.md) しゅるい classifies things into types.
Do not translate 種類 as "seeds" merely because you recognize the first character.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine a plant marked by 禾 beside the dense, layered 重 shape, holding many seeds.
This is a memory picture only, not a claim about why the character was created.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| たね | kun | 種 たね — seed |
| シュ | on | [種類](../words/種類.md) しゅるい — kind; variety |

The metadata also preserves the suffix reading -ぐさ.

## Common words

- 種 たね — seed; kernel
- [種類](../words/種類.md) しゅるい — kind; type; variety

## Notes

三種類 (さんしゅるい) counts three kinds, not necessarily three individual objects.
[重](重.md) supplies a close visual comparison on the right, while [秋](秋.md) and [私](私.md) share the left.
The meaning "heavy" belongs to 重, not to 種.

Word evidence: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entries 1328820, 1328890.

# 般 — general; kind; category

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 舟 (no. 137) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

般 appears in words about kinds, scope or generality. [一般](../words/一般.md) いっぱん means general or ordinary; [全般](../words/全般.md) ぜんぱん concerns the whole scope, while 諸般 しょはん means various.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the boat-shaped left side carrying an assortment, or a whole range, of things.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ハン | on | 諸般 しょはん - various; several; [全般](../words/全般.md) ぜんぱん - the whole; 般 appears as パン |

## Common words

- [一般](../words/一般.md) いっぱん - general; ordinary
- [全般](../words/全般.md) ぜんぱん - the whole; as a whole
- 諸般 しょはん - various; several

## Notes

- The canonical key is 舟, no. 137, on the left. The boat mnemonic is not a claim that 一般 means a type of boat.
- KANJIDIC2 lists the on reading ハン. It appears as パン in 一般 いっぱん and 全般 ぜんぱん, while 諸般 しょはん keeps ハン. Learn each complete word rather than substituting いちはん for 一般.
- 一般に means generally, not without a single exception. The compound supplies a usage that the character alone does not explain.

# 列 — row; queue

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (as 刂, no. 18) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

列 is an ordered line or row of people or things. The standalone noun [列](../words/列.md) れつ
can name a queue, while 列車 れっしゃ names a train.
The "file" sense in dictionary glosses means a line of people or things, not a computer document.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** use the two upright strokes of 刂 as the straight edge along which a
queue lines up. This is a modern visual association, not the character's historical derivation.

## Readings

The table selects レツ; metadata also preserves レ.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| レツ | on | [列](../words/列.md) れつ — row; 行列 ぎょうれつ — queue |

## Common words

- [列](../words/列.md) れつ — row; line; queue
- 行列 ぎょうれつ — queue; procession
- 列車 れっしゃ — train

## Notes

列車 is read れっしゃ, not れつしゃ: learn the small っ as part of the word.
[判](判.md), [利](利.md), and [到](到.md) also end in 刂.
Their shared radical is a visual clue, not a reason to treat their meanings as interchangeable.

# 機 — mechanism; opportunity

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 木 (no. 75) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

機 appears both in words for machines and in words for an opportunity.
[機会](../words/機会.md) (きかい) is a chance to do something; 機械 (きかい) is a machine.
Identical pronunciation does not make those words interchangeable.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the small loops high on the right as moving threads in a mechanism held by a 木-shaped frame.
This is only a visual memory aid.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キ | on | [機会](../words/機会.md) きかい — opportunity; 飛行機 ひこうき — airplane |

Metadata also records the kun reading はた; this card concentrates on the compounds above.

## Common words

- [機会](../words/機会.md) きかい — opportunity; occasion
- 機械 きかい — machine; mechanism
- 飛行機 ひこうき — airplane

## Notes

Look at the second character in きかい: 会 signals the word for an opportunity, while 械 gives the machine word.
[楽](楽.md) also has small upper strokes and a 木 shape, but its 木 is below, not a separate left-hand component.
The examples illustrate different meanings, not a proposed historical path from machinery to opportunity.

# 話 — talk, speak

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 言 (as 訁, no. 149) · **Phonetic:** none in modern form (old 𠯑) · **JLPT:** N5 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

話 is 言 (words, speech) plus 𠯑, an old sound component. In the modern form the right-hand part looks like 舌 (tongue) only because the shapes merged graphically.
As a labelled mnemonic only, "words from the tongue" is easy to remember.
It means "talk", "speak" and "a story, a conversation".

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ワ | on | [会話](../words/会話.md) かいわ, 電話 でんわ, 話題 わだい, 神話 しんわ |
| はな.す | kun | [話す](../words/話す.md) はなす, 話し方 はなしかた, 話し合う はなしあう |
| はなし | kun | 話 はなし (a talk, story): 話を聞く, 面白い話 おもしろいはなし |

## Common words

- [話す](../words/話す.md) はなす — to speak, to talk
- [会話](../words/会話.md) かいわ — conversation
- 電話 でんわ — telephone

## Notes

- 話す takes the person with に or と: 友達と話す (talk with a friend), 先生に話す (tell the teacher). The noun 話 (はなし) is the same word without the final す.
- 話す is a regular godan verb: 話します, 話さない, 話して, 話した.
- Contrast: 言う いう (say), 話す (talk) and 語る かたる (narrate, a formal verb).
- Look-alikes: [読](読.md) and [書](書.md), which are also sound-and-sense kanji, and 活 (life; with 氵 and 舌). 話 ends in 舌 (a 千-like top over 口).

# 争 — contend; dispute

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 亅 (no. 6) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

争 involves competing claims or opposition. [争う](../words/争う.md) あらそう can mean compete or argue;
[戦争](../words/戦争.md) せんそう is the more specific word for war.
The character's presence does not make every disagreement an armed conflict.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the stacked middle lines competing for space around the descending hook.
This is a modern visual association, not a claim about the original drawing.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソウ | on | [戦争](../words/戦争.md) せんそう — war |
| あらそ.う | kun | [争う](../words/争う.md) あらそう — compete; dispute |

## Common words

- [戦争](../words/戦争.md) せんそう — war
- [争う](../words/争う.md) あらそう — contend; argue

## Notes

[急](急.md) has a similar upper area but a 心-shaped base; 争 ends with a hooked vertical.
[書](書.md) and [予](予.md) also reward attention to the lower part.
争う is broader than 戦争: a contest for first place need not involve violence.
The less immediately useful source reading いか.でか is kept in metadata, not used to read 戦争.
