# 造 — make; construct

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (no. 162) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

造 concerns making, manufacturing or construction. 製造 is manufacture, and the native family 造る includes making or building.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the 告-shaped block as a finished object carried along by the 辶-shaped part. This is a modern-shape memory aid, not a historical or phonetic analysis.

The canonical radical is 辵, shown as 辶.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ゾウ | on | [製造](../words/製造.md) せいぞう - manufacture; production; [人造](../words/人造.md) じんぞう - artificial; man-made; [造船](../words/造船.md) ぞうせん - shipbuilding |
| つく.る | kun | [造る](../words/作る.md) つくる - make; build; spelling taught with 作る |
| つく.り | kun | 造り つくり - construction; structure |
| -づく.り | kun | 酒造り さけづくり - sake brewing; 造り supplies bound -making |

## Common words

- [製造](../words/製造.md) せいぞう - manufacture; production
- [構造](../words/構造.md) こうぞう - structure; framework
- [造る](../words/作る.md) つくる - make; build; larger-scale making or brewing spelling in the 作る article
- 造り つくり - construction; structure
- [改造](../words/改造.md) かいぞう - remodeling; modification; reorganization
- [人造](../words/人造.md) じんぞう - artificial; man-made
- [造船](../words/造船.md) ぞうせん - shipbuilding

## Notes

- JMdict places 作る and 造る in one family, with a note that 造る is usually used for large-scale building or manufacturing. That is a spelling tendency, not an absolute division of every sense.
- The bound -づくり reading is illustrated inside 酒造り さけづくり. It is not a claim that every compound containing 造 takes づくり.
- The final written る or り belongs to the complete native word. The character-dictionary dot marks the spelling boundary, not the whole grammatical analysis of the verb.

# 製 — manufacture; made of

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 衣 (no. 145), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

製 means to make or manufacture, and as a suffix it says what something is made of or where it was made. [作製](../words/作製.md) さくせい and [製作](../words/製作.md) せいさく are both manufacture or production, [製品](../words/製品.md) せいひん is a manufactured product and [製造](../words/製造.md) せいぞう is manufacturing.

The Daijisen character entry gives one sense, making things, as in 製紙 せいし, "papermaking; paper manufacture," 製図 せいず, "drafting; drawing," 製鉄 せいてつ, "iron manufacture," 製粉 せいふん, "milling; grinding into flour," and 木製 もくせい, "wooden; made of wood." Its noun entry adds the suffix use: after a place name it shows where something was made, as in 日本製 にほんせい, "made in Japan; Japanese-made," and after a material or means it shows what something is made of, as in 鉄製 てっせい, "made of iron."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [制](制.md), "system; regulate," at the top and [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment," at the bottom. Imagine a garment cut to a fixed pattern and made the same way every time. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 制 or 衣 contributes meaning or sound, although 制 is also read セイ. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| セイ | on | [作製](../words/作製.md) さくせい — manufacture; production; [製作](../words/製作.md) せいさく — manufacture; production; [製品](../words/製品.md) せいひん — manufactured product; [製造](../words/製造.md) せいぞう — manufacturing |

In 作製, 作 supplies サク and 製 reads セイ; 製作 reverses the order, with 製 first and 作 supplying サク. In 製品, 品 supplies ヒン, and in 製造, 造 supplies ゾウ. [制作](../words/制作.md) せいさく is read like 製作 but is written with 制 instead; JMdict glosses it "work (film, book)" first, and it has its own word article.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セイ, with 製造, 製鉄 and 鉄製 as examples. Practice selects セイ, the only reading.

## Common words

- [作製](../words/作製.md) さくせい — manufacture; production
- [製作](../words/製作.md) せいさく — manufacture; production
- [製品](../words/製品.md) せいひん — manufactured product
- [製造](../words/製造.md) せいぞう — manufacturing

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [作](作.md), "make, produce," in 作製 and 製作, [品](品.md), "goods, refinement," in 製品 and [造](造.md), "make; construct," in 製造.
- [衣](衣.md), "clothing; garment," is the key at the bottom, and [袋](袋.md), "bag; sack; pouch," also has 衣 at the bottom, under 代. [制](制.md), "system; regulate," is the top part and is also read セイ, as are [世](世.md), "world, generation," and [声](声.md), "voice." All are different kanji.
- [作](作.md), "make, produce," [造](造.md), "make; construct," [産](産.md), "produce; give birth," and [創](創.md), "originate; create," overlap the senses of making and producing that 製 and its words carry, and [築](築.md), "build; construct," [建](建.md), "build, construct," [構](構.md), "build; stance," and [工](工.md), "craft, construction," are close neighbours in the sense of making things. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "manufacture" as exclusive to any one character.

# 始 — begin, start

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

始 concerns beginning. [始める](../words/始める.md) means to start something; 始まる means that something starts.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture [女](女.md) beside [台](台.md), a platform: a performer is about to begin. This is a memory story about the modern components, not a verified origin or phonetic analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | 開始 かいし — start, commencement |
| はじ.める | kun | [始める](../words/始める.md) はじめる — start something |
| -はじ.める | kun suffix | 読み始める よみはじめる — begin to read |
| はじ.まる | kun | 始まる はじまる — begin, start |

## Common words

- [始める](../words/始める.md) はじめる — start something
- 始まる はじまる — something starts
- 開始 かいし — start, commencement

## Notes

Contrast 授業を始める (じゅぎょうをはじめる), "start the lesson," with 授業が始まる (じゅぎょうがはじまる), "the lesson starts." を marks what someone starts; が marks what begins.

The suffix attaches to the verb's ます-stem: 読む → 読みます → 読み + 始める. 読み始める above is a productive combination, not a separate compound whose reading must be guessed. Compare [終](終.md), "end."

# 和 — harmony; Japanese style

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

和 can signal harmony or peace, as in [平和](../words/平和.md) へいわ. It also marks Japanese style in words such as 和食 わしょく, "Japanese food."
These are different uses of the character: 和食 does not mean "peaceful food."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the left-hand 禾 and right-hand 口 fitting comfortably beside each other: harmony. This is a visual association, not a derivation.

## Readings

The table selects ワ and なご.やか; the metadata retains the other dictionary readings.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ワ | on | [平和](../words/平和.md) へいわ — peace; 和食 わしょく — Japanese food |
| なご.やか | kun | 和やか なごやか — friendly; harmonious |

## Common words

- [平和](../words/平和.md) へいわ — peace; harmony
- 和食 わしょく — Japanese food
- 和やか なごやか — congenial; harmonious

## Notes

The indexing radical is 口 on the right, despite the prominent 禾 on the left.
[私](私.md) and [秋](秋.md) also begin with 禾; distinguish their right-hand parts.
For 和やか before a noun, use な: 和やかな会話 (なごやかなかいわ), "a friendly conversation."
