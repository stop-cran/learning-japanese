# 破 — tear; break

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 石 (no. 112) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

破 concerns tearing, breaking, or destruction. [破る](../words/破る.md) やぶる describes an action on something;
破れる やぶれる describes something tearing or getting torn.
The broad English word "break" does not mean that 破る fits every broken object.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the stone-shaped 石 catching and tearing the 皮-shaped material on the right.
This is a deliberately invented visual aid, not a historical account or phonetic analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| やぶ.る | kun | [破る](../words/破る.md) やぶる — tear something |
| やぶ.れる | kun | 破れる やぶれる — get torn |
| ハ | on | 破壊 はかい — destruction |

## Common words

- [破る](../words/破る.md) やぶる — tear; rip
- 破れる やぶれる — tear; get torn
- 破壊 はかい — destruction

## Notes

紙を破りました。 (かみをやぶりました。) — I tore the paper.
紙が破れました。 (かみがやぶれました。) — The paper tore.
The を／が contrast helps show which verb describes the action and which describes the change.

[疲](疲.md) shares 皮 but has 疒 around it. [研](研.md) shares 石 but has a different right side.
[石](石.md) alone is only the left component.

# 当 — hit; be on target

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 彐 (no. 58) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

当 connects hitting a target with being right or appropriate.
[当たる](../words/当たる.md) あたる can describe a ball hitting a wall or a prediction turning out right.
当てる あてる, by contrast, can describe making something hit a target.
In 本当 ほんとう, the character belongs to the word for truth or reality.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the three upper strokes arriving at the open-sided 彐 target below.
This is a picture for remembering today's shape, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| あ.たる | kun | [当たる](../words/当たる.md) あたる — hit; be right |
| あ.てる | kun | 当てる あてる — hit a target; guess |
| トウ | on | 本当 ほんとう — truth; real |

The remaining source readings are retained in metadata.

## Common words

- [当たる](../words/当たる.md) あたる — hit; strike; be correct
- 当てる あてる — make contact with; hit a target
- 本当 ほんとう — truth; reality

## Notes

For physical contact, learn ボールが壁に当たる (ボールがかべにあたる), "the ball hits the wall."
The ball is the subject; the wall is marked by に.
[帰](帰.md) has a related-looking horizontal stack within a larger character.
[白](白.md) and [目](目.md) have closed outlines; 当's lower part is open on the left.

[JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entries 1448810, 1448860, 1523060.

# 射 — shoot; shine into

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41), on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

射 concerns shooting or directing something outward, including light.
[発射](../words/発射.md) はっしゃ is firing or launching, and
[注射](../words/注射.md) ちゅうしゃ is an injection.
The native verb [射す](../words/射す.md) さす describes light shining in;
it does not mean giving someone an injection.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [身](身.md) beside 寸.
Imagine the tall 身-shaped block lined up with a small target marked by
寸. This is a visual memory scene, not an account of the character's origin.
The canonical radical here is 寸, not the larger 身 component.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シャ | on | [注射](../words/注射.md) ちゅうしゃ - injection; [発射](../words/発射.md) はっしゃ - firing; launching |
| い.る | kun | 射る いる - shoot an arrow, bolt or dart; no word article yet |
| さ.す | kun | [射す](../words/射す.md) さす - shine in, of light |
| う.つ | kun | 射つ うつ - shoot; a rare kanji spelling in the 撃つ family, not the ordinary spelling recommended here |

Practice selects シャ and さ.す, each with a linked example. The official
Joyo table lists シャ and いる; the separate dictionary evidence for 射す
supports teaching it here too. A reading's absence from that table does
not make the word invalid.

射す is intransitive: the light shines. 射る is an ichidan verb, whereas
射す and the うつ verb are godan verbs; their endings do not form one
interchangeable conjugation pattern. In 発射, 発 is はっ and 射 is しゃ.

## Common words

- [射す](../words/射す.md) さす - shine in
- [注射](../words/注射.md) ちゅうしゃ - injection
- [発射](../words/発射.md) はっしゃ - firing; launching

## Notes

- [身](身.md), one's body, lacks the right-hand 寸. [時](時.md), time,
  and [寺](寺.md), temple, also contain 寸-shaped strokes but are different
  complete characters.
- Shooting overlaps [撃](撃.md); the light sense overlaps [照](照.md),
  [映](映.md) and the light-related use of [差](差.md).
  Emission and launching also connect with [放](放.md) and [発](発.md).
  Different spellings or English titles do not make those safe wrong answers.
- Learn 射す as a whole spelling-and-meaning pair. The many other words
  pronounced さす are not automatically written 射す.

# 突 — thrust; sudden

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 穴 (no. 116) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

突 combines meanings of thrusting, poking, collision, and suddenness.
In [突然](../words/突然.md) とつぜん, it describes an abrupt event, not necessarily a physical impact.
突く つく is the verb for poking or thrusting.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the lower 大 pushing out from under the opening-shaped 穴 above.
This uses the present written form; it is not a historical origin claim.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| トツ | on | [突然](../words/突然.md) とつぜん — suddenly; 衝突 しょうとつ — collision |
| つ.く | kun | 突く つく — poke; thrust |

The source also records カ; these examples use トツ or つく.

## Common words

- [突然](../words/突然.md) とつぜん — sudden; suddenly
- 突く つく — poke; thrust
- 衝突 しょうとつ — collision; clash

## Notes

The eight-stroke form taught here has 大 below 穴.
Compare the lower portions of [究](究.md), [空](空.md), and [窓](窓.md); their shared upper component is not enough to identify 突.

突然雨が降った (とつぜんあめがふった), "It suddenly rained," illustrates suddenness without a collision.

Word evidence: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entries 1457040, 1456890, 1351560.

# 済 — settle; finish

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

済 is useful for something being settled, finished, or taken care of.
済む (すむ) describes completion; 済ます (すます) describes getting something finished.
Also learn [経済](../words/経済.md) けいざい, "economy; economics," as a whole word rather than
translating it from these verbs.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** view the bars and upright strokes of the right-hand 斉 shape as
items lined up and settled. The water strokes distinguish 済; the image is not an etymology.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| サイ | on | [経済](../words/経済.md) けいざい — economy; サイ is voiced to ざい here |
| す.む / す.ます | kun | 済む すむ — be finished; 済ます すます — finish something |

## Common words

- [経済](../words/経済.md) けいざい — economy; economics
- 済む すむ — finish; be resolved
- 済ます すます — finish; settle

## Notes

支払いが済みました。 (しはらいがすみました。) — The payment is taken care of.
With the other verb, 支払いを済ます (しはらいをすます) means "settle the payment."
The many source readings in the metadata are a reference list, not interchangeable choices for these words.
