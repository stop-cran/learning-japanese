# 終 — end; finish

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

終 marks an ending or completion. [終わる](../words/終わる.md) (おわる) is a basic way to say that something
ends; 終える (おえる) commonly means bringing an activity to an end.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not verified etymology:** imagine tying off the last thread, 糸, as
[winter](冬.md) arrives at the end of the year. The visible 冬 is used only as a memory cue here.

## Readings

The table is selective; metadata preserves all source forms, including both お.わる and おわ.る.
The dot indicates an okurigana boundary, not a pause in pronunciation.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シュウ | on | 終了 しゅうりょう — ending; 最終 さいしゅう — final |
| お.わる | kun | [終わる](../words/終わる.md) おわる — end; finish |
| お.える | kun | 終える おえる — finish something |
| つい | kun | 終の住処 ついのすみか (one's final home; literary) |
| つい.に | kun | 終に ついに (at last; finally, normally written in kana or 遂に) |

## Common words

- [終わる](../words/終わる.md) おわる — end; finish
- 終わり おわり — the end
- 終了 しゅうりょう — conclusion; completion
- 最終 さいしゅう — final; last

## Notes

授業が終わる (じゅぎょうがおわる) means "class ends." 終わる also has transitive uses and appears after a verb stem,
as in 読み終わる (よみおわる, finish reading); it is not restricted to the pattern "something が ends."
Compare [始](始.md), used in words for beginning.

[紙](紙.md) has the same left side, but a different right side. 冬 alone means winter, not "finish."

# 夕 — evening

**Strokes:** 3 · **Key (radical):** 夕 (no. 36) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

夕 concerns evening, especially the time as daylight fades. Learn it first in [夕方](../words/夕方.md), rather than assuming that the bare character is the usual conversational word.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the curved outline holding a final small stroke of evening light. This is a mnemonic for the present shape, not a verified historical explanation.

## Readings

This table focuses on ゆう; the source's on-reading セキ remains in metadata.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ゆう | kun | [夕方](../words/夕方.md) ゆうがた — early evening; 夕日 ゆうひ — setting sun; 夕食 ゆうしょく — evening meal |

## Common words

- [夕方](../words/夕方.md) ゆうがた — late afternoon, early evening
- 夕日 ゆうひ — evening sun, setting sun
- 夕食 ゆうしょく — evening meal, dinner

## Notes

[外](外.md) adds strokes to the right of a 夕-shaped part; [多](多.md) stacks two such shapes. Keep the whole characters distinct.

Compare 夕方 with [夜](夜.md) よる, the night or darker part of the evening. Their everyday boundaries depend on context, not a fixed clock time. 夕食 mixes kun ゆう with on ショク from [食](食.md).

# 起 — wake; rise

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 走 (no. 156) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

起 concerns getting up, waking, or bringing something into action. In event words it can also concern something happening:
[起きる](../words/起きる.md) おきる can mean either getting up or an incident occurring.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the [走](走.md) shape getting moving beside the curled 己 shape,
as if someone is uncurling and getting out of bed. This is a visual mnemonic, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

This is a selective table; the metadata preserves all source readings and their exact okurigana notation.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| お.きる | kun | [起きる](../words/起きる.md) おきる — get up; wake up |
| お.こす | kun | 起こす おこす — wake someone up; raise something |
| お.こる | kun | 起こる おこる — happen |
| キ | on | 起立 きりつ — standing up |

## Common words

- [起きる](../words/起きる.md) おきる — get up; wake up; occur
- 起こす おこす — wake someone up; raise something
- 起こる おこる — happen
- 起立 きりつ — standing up

## Notes

起きる describes the person who wakes; 起こす takes the person being awakened as its object.
For standing up from a chair, also learn [立つ](../words/立つ.md) たつ.
Do not confuse 起 with the simpler [走](走.md) or [足](足.md).

# 宿 — lodging

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 宀 (no. 40) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

宿 is associated with lodging or staying somewhere. 宿 (やど) is an inn or lodging, and 宿泊 (しゅくはく) is accommodation or staying overnight.
Learn [宿題](../words/宿題.md) (しゅくだい), "homework," as a separate whole-word use rather than translating it as "inn question."

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the 亻-shaped person checking in beside a 百-shaped room marker under the roof.
This is a modern memory scene, not an etymology or a statement about the historical components.

## Common words

- [宿題](../words/宿題.md) (しゅくだい) — homework; assignment

# 過 — exceed; go beyond

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

過 is useful for passing beyond a point or exceeding an amount. [過ぎる](../words/過ぎる.md) すぎる can mean passing a place or the passage of time.
過去 かこ names the past, while 過ごす すごす describes spending time.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** treat the box-like center as a marker that the sweeping 辶 path goes past.
This is a visual memory aid, not an origin story.

## Readings

The table selects useful readings; the metadata preserves the complete source reading list.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| す.ぎる | kun | [過ぎる](../words/過ぎる.md) すぎる — pass; go beyond |
| す.ごす | kun | 過ごす すごす — spend time |
| カ | on | 過去 かこ — the past |

## Common words

- [過ぎる](../words/過ぎる.md) すぎる — pass; go beyond
- 過ごす すごす — spend time
- 過去 かこ — the past
- [過剰](../words/過剰.md) かじょう — excess; too much

## Notes

The excessive-use suffix is usually written in kana: 食べすぎる (たべすぎる), "eat too much."
It attaches to the verb's ます-stem: 食べます → 食べ + すぎる.
Compare the box-like center of 過 with 周 inside [週](週.md); they are not the same component.
