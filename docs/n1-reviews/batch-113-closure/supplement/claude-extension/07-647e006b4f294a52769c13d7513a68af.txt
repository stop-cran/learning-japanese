# 辞 — words; resign

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 辛 (no. 160) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

辞 has a word-related branch, seen in [辞書](../words/辞書.md) じしょ, "dictionary,"
and a leaving-a-position branch, seen in 辞める やめる, "resign."
Keep both meanings available instead of trying to translate every compound with one English gloss.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the left 舌-shaped part delivering words beside the tall 辛 on the right.
This labels visible shapes for memory only; it does not establish their historical roles.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジ | on | 辞書 じしょ — dictionary; 辞任 じにん — resignation |
| や.める | kun | 辞める やめる — resign; quit a job |

## Common words

- [辞書](../words/辞書.md) じしょ — dictionary
- [辞典](../words/辞典.md) じてん — dictionary; lexicon
- 辞める やめる — resign; leave a job
- 辞任 じにん — resignation from a position
- [お辞儀](../words/お辞儀.md) おじぎ — bow; bowing

## Notes

辞める concerns giving up a position, not every action translated "stop."
The left 舌 begins with strokes resembling [千](千.md); the right 辛 includes a [立](立.md)-like upper shape.
[新](新.md) has a partially similar upright arrangement but a different right side.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entries 1318970, 1318950, 1319000.

# 否 — deny; negate

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 口 (no. 30) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

否 expresses a negative answer or denial. In [否定](../words/否定.md) ひてい, it concerns rejecting or negating something said, not simply putting a negative ending on any verb.
可否 かひ pairs the positive and negative possibilities: whether something is acceptable or possible.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture 不 above 口 as a mouth giving a negative answer. This is a memory aid using the present shape, not a claim about its historical formation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヒ | on | [否定](../words/否定.md) ひてい — denial; 可否 かひ — advisability; yes or no |

The metadata also records the kun readings いな and いや; learn their uses as whole expressions rather than treating 否 as the ordinary spelling of every spoken "no."

## Common words

- [否定](../words/否定.md) ひてい — denial; negation
- 可否 かひ — whether something is appropriate or possible; ayes and noes

## Notes

[古](古.md) has 十 above its box; [告](告.md) has a different upper structure. Neither means denial.
[不](不.md) is a useful meaning comparison, but 否 has the additional mouth-shaped box below it.

# 返 — give back; repay

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 辵 (as 辶, no. 162) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

返 concerns returning something or responding back.
[返す](../words/返す.md) かえす can describe giving a borrowed object back; 返事 へんじ is a reply.
The thing that comes back need not be a person returning home.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the inner 反 turning something back along the sweeping 辶 path.
This is a mnemonic based on the modern shape, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| かえ.す | kun | 返す かえす — give back |
| かえ.る | kun | 返る かえる — come back |
| ヘン | on | 返事 へんじ — reply |

## Common words

- [返す](../words/返す.md) かえす — return something
- 返る かえる — return; come back
- 返事 へんじ — reply; response

## Notes

[帰る](../words/帰る.md) かえる is the word to learn for going home.
返す has an object: 本を返す (ほんをかえす), "return a book."
The radical 辵 is written as 辶 here. Distinguish 反 inside it from the centers of [近](近.md), [週](週.md), and [迷](迷.md).

For 返る compared with 帰る and 還る, and 返す compared with the returning subject, see [Kaeru: change, replace, exchange, and return](../articles/kaeru-change-replace-and-return.md).

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entries 1512130, 1512150, 1512220, 1221270.

# 盤 — board; disc

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 皿 (no. 108), at the bottom · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

盤 can name a board, disc or flat supporting surface.
[地盤](../words/地盤.md) じばん is the ground or a supporting base;
[基盤](../words/基盤.md) きばん is a foundation, infrastructure or platform.
The support can be figurative, not necessarily a physical board.

The character dictionary also records vessel, flat-rock, game-stage and
coiling senses. Learn the particular word rather than translating every
occurrence as a disc.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [般](般.md) above
[皿](皿.md). Within 般, [舟](舟.md) is on the left and 殳 on the right.
Imagine the broad lower dish as a board supporting the more intricate
upper block. This is a memory picture, not a claim about historical formation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| バン | on | [地盤](../words/地盤.md) じばん - ground; supporting base; [基盤](../words/基盤.md) きばん - foundation; infrastructure |

Practice selects バン. [算盤](../words/算盤.md) そろばん, abacus, is
learned as a complete word and is usually written in kana. Do not invent
a general reading そろ for 算 from that spelling or use the word as proof
that each character's contribution can be recovered mechanically.

## Common words

- [地盤](../words/地盤.md) じばん - ground; supporting base
- [基盤](../words/基盤.md) きばん - foundation; infrastructure
- [算盤](../words/算盤.md) そろばん - abacus; usually written そろばん

## Notes

- [船](船.md), ship, contains 舟 but lacks the lower 皿.
  [番](番.md), turn/number, and [晩](晩.md), evening, share バン
  but have different shapes and meanings.
- A board or supporting surface overlaps [板](板.md), [皿](皿.md)
  and [台](台.md). Foundation overlaps [基](基.md), and the flat-rock
  sense overlaps [岩](岩.md). These are direct meaning comparisons,
  not safe distractors for this card.
- English "board" here is a flat object, not boarding a vehicle as in
  [乗](乗.md), ride/board. The shared English spelling is excluded from
  meaning choices; it is not a shared Japanese meaning.
- Distinguish foundation 基盤 from the electronics word 基板 きばん,
  circuit board. The linked 基盤 article explains why the identical
  pronunciation does not license unrestricted spelling substitution.
- JMdict also records 盤 as a rare spelling in the 皿 さら family.
  That separate lexical entry does not change the バン examples or the
  frozen metadata, whose kun list is empty.

# 編 — knit; compile

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

編 concerns knitting or braiding and also compiling or editing written material. Learn the complete word: 編集 concerns editing, while 編む can concern either textile work or compiling a book.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See 糸 beside the 扁-shaped right half and imagine threads being arranged like the material in a compiled book. This is an invented modern-shape association, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

The canonical key 糸, no. 120, appears in its compressed left-side form ⺯.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ヘン | on | [編集](../words/編集.md) へんしゅう - editing; compilation; [短編](../words/短編.md) たんぺん - short story; short film (ヘン becomes ぺん in this word) |
| あ.む | kun | [編む](../words/編む.md) あむ - knit; braid; compile |
| -あ.み | kun | 手編み てあみ - hand knitting |

## Common words

- [編む](../words/編む.md) あむ - knit; braid; compile
- [編物](../words/編物.md) あみもの - knitting; knitted work, also written 編み物
- [編集](../words/編集.md) へんしゅう - editing; compilation
- [短編](../words/短編.md) たんぺん - short story; short film
- 手編み てあみ - hand knitting

## Notes

- 編む is a transitive godan verb ending in む: 編みます あみます and 編んで あんで. The object takes を, whether it is something knitted or a book being compiled.
- The leading hyphen in -あ.み marks a bound use, illustrated by 手編み てあみ. It is not a hyphen to insert in the Japanese spelling. The separate noun 編 へん can concern compilation or a volume.
- [組む](../words/組む.md) くむ also has a dictionary braiding sense, besides assembling and teaming up. It is not an unrestricted opposite of 編む. Compare the shapes of [紙](紙.md), [約](約.md) and [絡](絡.md) without assigning them all the same meaning.
