# 望 — hope

**Strokes:** 11 · **Key (radical):** 月 (no. 74) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

望 is useful for wishes, hopes and things someone wants to happen.
[望む](../words/望む.md) のぞむ is the verb "hope for; desire"; 希望 きぼう is "hope; wish."
The desired outcome is not a statement that it will definitely occur.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** find 亡 and 月 above 王 and imagine someone looking up with a wish.
This is an invented scene for remembering the written arrangement, not a historical analysis.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| のぞ.む | kun | [望む](../words/望む.md) のぞむ — hope for; desire |
| ボウ | on | 希望 きぼう — hope; aspiration |

## Common words

- [望む](../words/望む.md) のぞむ — desire; wish for
- 希望 きぼう — hope; wish

## Notes

[願う](../words/願う.md) ねがう also means "wish for"; its meaning overlaps with 望む (のぞむ).
[服](服.md) and [朝](朝.md) share a 月 component in different positions.
[青](青.md) also has a vertically stacked outline, but does not have 亡 beside its upper section.

Vocabulary: JMdict entries 1519630, 1219910 and 1217950.

# 派 — faction; school of thought

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 水 (no. 85), appearing as 氵 · **JLPT:** N2 (supplementary community placement)

## Meaning and origin

派 can identify a **group, faction, school or sect**. The word [派](../words/派.md) は can stand as a noun or follow another expression to name a camp. Learn [派手](../words/派手.md) はで, flashy, as a separate established word rather than translating it as "faction hand."

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 氵 and the branching-looking strokes on the right. Imagine a group splitting into several streams. This is a visual memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ハ | on | [派](../words/派.md) は - faction; school; [派手](../words/派手.md) はで - flashy |

## Common words

- [派](../words/派.md) は - a faction, school or camp
- [派手](../words/派手.md) はで - flashy; showy

## Notes

- In 派手, ハ combines with native て, voiced to で. This is not an all-on reading.
- "School" here means a school of thought or artistic tradition, not ordinarily the building where pupils attend classes.
- [流](流.md) can also identify a style or school in compounds. Do not treat it as a clearly wrong meaning merely because it also means "flow."
- [泳](泳.md), swim, [洗](洗.md), wash, and [泊](泊.md), stay overnight, share 氵 but differ on the right.

# 制 — system; regulate

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (as 刂, no. 18) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

制 belongs to the vocabulary of systems, rules, and control.
[制度](../words/制度.md) せいど names an institutional system; 規制 きせい is regulation or restriction.
The system can organize activity without being a physical machine.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the crossbars and hanging lower strokes on the left as an organized
framework, with 刂 marking its boundary. This is a present-shape memory aid, not an etymology.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| セイ | on | [制度](../words/制度.md) せいど — institutional system; 規制 きせい — regulation |

## Common words

- [制度](../words/制度.md) せいど — system; institution
- 規制 きせい — regulation; restriction
- 体制 たいせい — system; organizational structure

## Notes

制度 concerns an established arrangement; 規制 highlights controls or restrictions.
Do not assume every English use of "system" translates as 制度.
[判](判.md), [利](利.md), and [列](列.md) share 刂, but 制 has its own crossbar-and-hanging-strokes pattern on the left.

# 刺 — pierce; prick

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

刺 concerns piercing, pricking, or a thorn. [刺す](../words/刺す.md) さす describes pricking or piercing something; [刺さる](../words/刺さる.md) ささる describes something sharp sticking into something. In 刺激 しげき, the response can be mental as well as physical.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine the pointed, tree-like left block beside the upright blade-shaped 刂, ready to prick something.

The canonical radical 刀 takes its right-hand form 刂.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | [刺激](../words/刺激.md) しげき - stimulus; stimulation |
| さ.す | kun | [刺す](../words/刺す.md) さす - pierce; prick |
| さ.さる | kun | [刺さる](../words/刺さる.md) ささる - stick into; pierce (intransitive) |
| さ.し | kun | 刺し さし - stabbing; piercing (noun) |
| さし | kun | [刺身](../words/刺身.md) さしみ - sashimi; 刺 is さし in this spelling |
| とげ | kun | 刺 とげ - thorn; splinter |

## Common words

- [刺激](../words/刺激.md) しげき - stimulus; stimulation
- [刺す](../words/刺す.md) さす - pierce; prick
- [刺さる](../words/刺さる.md) ささる - stick into; become lodged
- [刺身](../words/刺身.md) さしみ - sashimi; sliced raw seafood
- [名刺](../words/名刺.md) めいし - business card

## Notes

- 刺す is transitive; 刺さる is intransitive. Compare 針を刺す はりをさす, stick in a needle, with 針が刺さる はりがささる, a needle sticks into something.
- 刺身 and 刺し身 are both dictionary spellings of さしみ. The written し differs, but the whole word has the same pronunciation. The noun 刺し also has other dictionary senses; the reading row uses the piercing sense.
- 名刺 めいし means a business card, not something sharp. Learn the whole compound instead of forcing the piercing meaning into every word.

# 凌 — endure; surpass

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 冫 (the two left-hand strokes, no. 15) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

凌 has two useful branches: **enduring or getting through a difficulty**, and
**surpassing something else**. In [凌ぐ](../words/凌ぐ.md) (しのぐ), the
context selects between these branches. [凌駕](../words/凌駕.md) (りょうが)
concerns surpassing, not sheltering from bad weather.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** imagine the two 冫 marks as drops
beside the right-hand 夌 shape, pictured as a barrier. Think of weathering
the drops or getting beyond the barrier. This is an invented memory scene,
not a historical derivation or a verified explanation of the sound.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| リョウ | on | [凌駕](../words/凌駕.md) りょうが — surpassing; outdoing |
| しの.ぐ | kun | [凌ぐ](../words/凌ぐ.md) しのぐ — endure; surpass |

Both readings in the frozen KANJIDIC2 record are selected for practice.
Its grade-9/name-use classification does not make these ordinary word
readings personal-name readings; names are not taught here.

## Common words

- [凌ぐ](../words/凌ぐ.md) しのぐ — endure or pull through; surpass
- [凌駕](../words/凌駕.md) りょうが — surpassing; outdoing, also with する

## Notes

- 凌ぐ is a ぐ-ending verb: しのぎます, しのいで, しのいだ.
  Do not confuse it with the separate verb 忍ぶ (しのぶ), which ends in ぶ.
- 陵 (りょう; みささぎ) has 阝 on the left rather than 凌's two 冫 strokes.
  Recognizing the rarer spelling 陵駕 (りょうが) does not make these
  characters freely interchangeable in other words.
- Compare [准](准.md), "quasi-; associate rank," with 隹 on the right;
  [冶](冶.md), "smelt; work metals," with [台](台.md); and [次](次.md),
  "next," with [欠](欠.md). Similar left-hand marks do not imply the same meaning.
