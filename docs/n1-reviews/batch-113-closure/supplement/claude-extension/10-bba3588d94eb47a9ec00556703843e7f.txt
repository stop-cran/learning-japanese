# 節 — joint; season; restraint

**Strokes:** 13 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (as the bamboo top ⺮, no. 118), at the top · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

節 is a joint, a point where something divides, and from there a season or a restraint. [季節](../words/季節.md) きせつ is a season or time of year, [節](../words/節.md) ふし is a joint, a tune or a notable point, [節約](../words/節約.md) せつやく is economizing and [調節](../words/調節.md) ちょうせつ is regulation or adjustment.

The Daijisen character entry gives nine senses for セツ: the joint of a bamboo stem; a joint or a break in a text or in music, as in 関節 かんせつ, "joint," and 音節 おんせつ, "syllable"; a melody; the turn of the seasons, as in 季節; a point in time, as in 時節 じせつ, "season"; a holiday, as in 節句 せっく, "seasonal festival"; holding something to a moderate level, as in 節約 and 節減 せつげん, "retrenchment; curtailment; economy"; integrity, as in 節操 せっそう, "integrity; fidelity; constancy; principle; faithfulness; honor"; and an envoy's tally, as in 使節 しせつ, "envoy; ambassador; delegate." The separate noun 節 せつ, which has no word article here, is glossed "occasion; time" in its first JMdict sense and "section (of a literary work or piece of music); passage; paragraph; verse; stanza" in its second.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** find [竹](竹.md), "bamboo," at the top, in its top form ⺮, with 即 below it. Imagine a bamboo stalk whose joints divide it into sections, standing on a base. This is a memory aid only: it makes no claim that 竹 or 即 contributes meaning or sound, although the Daijisen entry lists the joint of a bamboo stem among the senses of 節. Historical formation is not established here.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| セツ | on | [季節](../words/季節.md) きせつ — season; time of year; [節約](../words/節約.md) せつやく — economizing; conserving; [調節](../words/調節.md) ちょうせつ — regulation; adjustment; control |
| セチ | on | お節 おせち - osechi; food eaten during the New Year's Holidays; 節会 せちえ - seasonal court banquet; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| ふし | kun | [節](../words/節.md) ふし — joint; tune; notable point |
| -ぶし | kun suffix | 削り節 けずりぶし - flaked bonito; no word article yet, not selected for questions here |
| のっと | kun | 節 ノット - knot (nautical mile per hour); no word article yet, not selected for questions here |

In 季節, 季 supplies キ and 節 reads セツ; in 節約, 約 supplies ヤク; and in 調節, 調 supplies チョウ. 節 read ふし stands alone as a word, with no okurigana, and is also the ふし of 節穴 ふしあな, "knothole; peep-hole," and 節目 ふしめ, "turning point; critical juncture." After another element ふし voices to ぶし, as in 削り節, where 削り supplies けずり. The leading dash in -ぶし marks a suffix use, one that follows another element; it is not pronounced.

セチ is the older Chinese-derived (Go-on) reading, which the Daijisen entry labels 呉音, beside セツ, which it labels 漢音, a later Chinese-derived reading. セチ appears in words about festivals and feasts: JMdict marks お節 as an abbreviation usually written in kana alone, and 節会 as a rare term. JMdict also lists 節 as an ateji, a kanji chosen for sound, for ノット, "knot (nautical mile per hour)," a word usually written in kana alone; KANJIDIC2 gives this use as のっと.

The official Joyo table, the list of standard-use readings, shows セツ, with 節約, 季節 and 関節 as examples; セチ, with お節料理; and ふし, with 節 and 節穴. It has no separate row for ぶし or のっと. Practice selects セツ and ふし, the readings with linked word articles; the other readings are retained from KANJIDIC2 and illustrated with dictionary words. Being outside the practice set is not proof that a reading is invalid. The official Joyo table also prints a variant form of 節 in round brackets, which this card does not teach.

## Common words

- [季節](../words/季節.md) きせつ — season; time of year
- [節](../words/節.md) ふし — joint; tune; notable point
- [節約](../words/節約.md) せつやく — economizing; conserving
- [調節](../words/調節.md) ちょうせつ — regulation; adjustment; control

## Notes

- The other kanji in the common words are [季](季.md), "seasons," in 季節, [約](約.md), "promise; approximately," in 節約 and [調](調.md), "investigate; tune," in 調節. The word 節 contains only this kanji.
- [竹](竹.md), "bamboo," is the key at the top, and [笑](笑.md), "laugh," has the same ⺮ at the top above a different shape. The 即 below the bamboo does not have a kanji card here. [雪](雪.md), "snow," and [殺](殺.md), "kill," are also read セツ. All are different kanji.
- [季](季.md), "seasons," [春](春.md), "spring season," [期](期.md), "period; term," [接](接.md), "touch; join," [制](制.md), "system; regulate," [倹](倹.md), "frugal; thrifty," [限](限.md), "limit," [際](際.md), "edge; occasion," [祭](祭.md), "festival; ritual; worship," [部](部.md), "section; department," [句](句.md), "phrase; verse," and [旬](旬.md), "ten days; peak season," overlap the senses "season," "period," "joint," "regulation," "economizing," "occasion," "festival," "section" and "verse" that 節 and its words carry, and 限 is close to holding something to a moderate level, as in 節約 and 節減; 接 is also read セツ. They are kept out of this card's quiz options. Do not treat "season," "verse" or "restraint" as exclusive to any one character.

# 負 — lose; bear a burden

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 貝 (no. 154) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

負 has two useful starting points: losing a contest and carrying a burden.
[負ける](../words/負ける.md) まける is "to lose," but 負う おう means to bear or shoulder something,
including responsibility. The second meaning is not a kind of defeat.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine the small angled cap carrying the large 貝 below it like a load.
This is a modern visual association, not an etymology.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ま.ける | kun | 負ける まける — lose a contest |
| お.う | kun | 負う おう — bear; shoulder |
| フ | on | 負担 ふたん — burden |

## Common words

- [負ける](../words/負ける.md) まける — lose; be defeated
- 負う おう — bear a load or responsibility
- 負担 ふたん — burden; bearing a cost

## Notes

責任を負う (せきにんをおう) means "take responsibility"; see [責任](../words/責任.md).
For the contest meaning, [勝つ](../words/勝つ.md) かつ, "win," is the useful opposite.
[買](買.md), [貧](貧.md), and [賛](賛.md) share the lower 貝 but have different tops and meanings.

# 乏 — scarce; lacking

**Strokes:** 4 · **Key (radical):** 丿 (no. 4), the short upper slant · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

乏 concerns not having enough of something. [乏しい](../words/乏しい.md) とぼしい
describes a meagre or insufficient supply; [貧乏](../words/貧乏.md) びんぼう
concerns poverty. The adjective can also describe financial hardship, so these
are overlapping uses, not separate compartments.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** picture a small upper slant above
a winding lower line, like a supply dwindling along a path. This is a memory
aid for the visible outline, not an account of the character's history.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ボウ | on | [貧乏](../words/貧乏.md) びんぼう — poverty; being poor |
| とぼ.しい | kun | [乏しい](../words/乏しい.md) とぼしい — scarce; meagre |
| とも.しい | kun, recognition here | 乏しい ともしい — an alternate reading of the adjective with insufficiency and poverty senses |

This card practices ボウ and とぼ.しい. The linked adjective article's primary
reading is とぼしい; the separate ともしい row records a dictionary-recognized
alternative, not a second linked article or a claim about its frequency.

## Common words

- [乏しい](../words/乏しい.md) とぼしい — scarce; meagre
- [貧乏](../words/貧乏.md) びんぼう — poverty; being poor

## Notes

- [貧](貧.md), poverty, and [欠](欠.md), lack, have related meanings.
  [少](少.md) concerns small amounts; scarcity can overlap with that idea,
  but 乏しい emphasizes an insufficient supply.
- Compare the short outlines of [久](久.md), a long time, [反](反.md),
  opposition or reversal, and [乃](乃.md), connecting or particle-related uses.
  Similar-looking strokes do not give them the lack meaning of 乏.
- This drawing uses the pinned four-stroke KanjiVG form, agreeing with the
  primary KANJIDIC2 count. The dictionary also records a three-stroke count;
  that annotation does not change this card's drawing model.
