# 管 — pipe; supervise

**Strokes:** 14 · **Key (radical):** 竹 (no. 118) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

管 concerns pipes or tubes and also supervision or management. The concrete native noun is くだ; 管理 uses the on reading カン for overseeing something, not for a literal pipe.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** See the bamboo-shaped top over 官 and imagine an official checking a row of pipes. This is an invented memory scene based on the modern components, not their historical or phonetic roles.

The canonical key is [竹](竹.md), no. 118, in its top form ⺮.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カン | on | [管理](../words/管理.md) かんり - management; control |
| くだ | kun | [管](../words/管.md) くだ - pipe; tube |

## Common words

- [管](../words/管.md) くだ - pipe; tube
- [管理](../words/管理.md) かんり - management; control

## Notes

- 管 かん is also a documented noun for a pipe or tube, with further wind-instrument and anatomical senses. The linked [管](../words/管.md) article specifically teaches くだ; the two records should not be merged into one freely interchangeable pronunciation rule.
- 管理する is transitive: mark what is managed with を. Management does not by itself establish ownership; see [管理](../words/管理.md).
- Compare [筒](筒.md), cylinder or tube, as a genuinely overlapping meaning, not a wrong quiz answer. [官](官.md) means government official; the extra bamboo-shaped top distinguishes 管.

# 扱 — handle; deal with

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 手 (as 扌, no. 64) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

扱 is useful for **handling something, dealing with a matter, or treating someone**. In [扱う](../words/扱う.md) あつかう, the object and context decide which English translation fits.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the hand-shaped 扌 on the left reaching toward the visible 及 on the right to handle something. This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ソウ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2 and Kanjipedia; no independently established word example is supplied here |
| キュウ | on, source-only here | Listed in KANJIDIC2 and Kanjipedia; do not use it to pronounce 扱う |
| あつか.い | kun | 扱い あつかい - handling; treatment |
| あつか.う | kun | [扱う](../words/扱う.md) あつかう - handle; deal with; treat |
| あつか.る | kun, source-only here | KANJIDIC2 lists this form; a separate dictionary-verified word and its usage have not been established for this lesson |
| こ.く | kun | 扱く こく - strip off; thresh, as in 稲を扱く いねをこく, strip grain from rice stalks |

The source-only rows preserve character-dictionary information, not recommended everyday substitutes for **あつかう**. They are not conjugations of that verb.

## Common words

- [扱う](../words/扱う.md) あつかう - handle; deal with; treat
- 扱い あつかい - handling; treatment

## Notes

- 扱い can follow a noun to mean treating someone or something as that category. It is not a second on-reading compound.
- 扱く also has the separate reading **しごく**, "draw through the hand; work someone hard," usually written in kana. That dictionary word does not establish the metadata form あつか.る.
- Compare [吸](吸.md), inhale or suck in: its left side is 口, not 扌. [坂](坂.md), slope, has 土 on the left; [投](投.md), throw, has a different right side.

# 防 — defend; ward off

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 阜 (no. 170) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

防 concerns protection against something or preventing it. 防ぐ means to defend against or prevent; 予防 is prevention and 消防 fire fighting.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the left-hand 阝 as a bank guarding the direction marked by 方. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not a historical or phonetic explanation.

The left-hand 阝 is the canonical radical 阜, no. 170. It is different from the right-hand 邑 radical in 郊 and 郵.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ボウ | on | [予防](../words/予防.md) よぼう - prevention; precaution; [防止](../words/防止.md) ぼうし - prevention; [消防署](../words/消防署.md) しょうぼうしょ - fire station; [防犯](../words/防犯.md) ぼうはん - crime prevention |
| ふせ.ぐ | kun | [防ぐ](../words/防ぐ.md) ふせぐ - defend against; prevent |

## Common words

- [防ぐ](../words/防ぐ.md) ふせぐ - defend against; prevent
- [予防](../words/予防.md) よぼう - prevention; precaution
- [防止](../words/防止.md) ぼうし - prevention
- [防犯](../words/防犯.md) ぼうはん - crime prevention

- [消防署](../words/消防署.md) しょうぼうしょ - fire station

## Notes

- 防ぐ is a transitive godan verb. Learn the complete verb and the thing prevented rather than treating the character alone as a grammatical rule.
- The right-hand 方 shape also occurs in [放](放.md) and [訪](訪.md). Those complete characters mean release and visit, not defend.

# 仕 — serve, attend

**Strokes:** 5 · **Key (radical):** 人 (no. 9) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

仕 appears in words about serving, work, and getting something done. In 仕える it means to serve or work for someone;
[仕事](../words/仕事.md) is the everyday word for work or a job.

The visible parts are 亻, the side form of [人](人.md), and 士.
**Modern-shape mnemonic:** a person stands beside a signboard 士, reporting for duty. This is not an etymology.

## Readings

This table is selective; metadata also preserves ジ.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| シ | on | 仕事 しごと (work), 仕上げる しあげる (finish up) |
| つか.える | kun | 仕える つかえる (serve; work for) |

## Common words

- [仕事](../words/仕事.md) しごと — work; job
- 仕上げる しあげる — finish up; complete
- 仕える つかえる — serve; work for someone

## Notes

In the right-hand 士, the upper horizontal is longer; in [土](土.md), the lower one is longer.
[住](住.md) has a different right-hand part, 主.
In 仕事, ごと comes from the native reading こと of [事](事.md); the word is not a regular on+on combination.

# 願 — wish; petition

**Strokes:** 19 · **Key (radical):** 頁 (no. 181) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

願 concerns wishing for an outcome or making a request.
[願う](../words/願う.md) ねがう is the verb; 願い (ねがい) is a wish or request.
みんなの幸せを願っています。 (みんなのしあわせをねがっています。) — I hope everyone will be happy.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture a wish written across the broad [原](原.md) shape on the left, beside 頁.
This gives you a way to remember the modern arrangement, not the character's historical origin.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ねが.う | kun | [願う](../words/願う.md) ねがう — wish; request |
| ガン | on | 願望 がんぼう — desire; aspiration |

The noun 願い (ねがい) belongs to the same native word family.

## Common words

- [願う](../words/願う.md) ねがう — wish for; request
- 願い ねがい — wish; request
- お願い おねがい — request; please

## Notes

お願い is a polite request expression; recognize it without assuming every occurrence of 願 has the same level of politeness.
[頼](頼.md) also concerns requests; compare the whole words to see whether they express a wish, a request, or reliance.
For shape practice, compare 原 here with the left blocks of [顔](顔.md), [題](題.md), and [類](類.md).
