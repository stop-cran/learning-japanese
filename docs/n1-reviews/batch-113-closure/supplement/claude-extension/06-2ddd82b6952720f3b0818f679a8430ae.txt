# 尊 — revere; esteem

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 寸 (no. 41) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

尊 concerns valuing or regarding someone or something highly. 尊敬 そんけい means respect or esteem, and 尊重 そんちょう means respect or due regard. 尊い is an adjective meaning precious or noble; 尊ぶ is a verb meaning to value or respect.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Imagine carefully holding the 酋-shaped upper object above 寸 as a reminder to treat something as valuable. This invented modern-shape image is not a reconstruction of a ritual or the character's historical origin.

## Common words

- [尊敬](../words/尊敬.md) そんけい - respect; esteem
- [尊重](../words/尊重.md) そんちょう - respect; due regard

## Notes

- Both たっとい and とうとい are documented readings of 尊い; both たっとぶ and とうとぶ are documented for 尊ぶ. The different endings also distinguish adjective from verb.
- Do not assign fixed, mutually exclusive meanings to the two pronunciation variants merely because they have separate rows.
- Compare [導](導.md), guide, and [専](専.md), concentrate on or specialize. A shared 寸 radical does not make their meanings interchangeable.

# 拝 — worship; bow in respect

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 手 (no. 64) · **JLPT:** N2 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

拝 concerns worship, reverence or a humble attitude in certain expressions. 拝む おがむ can mean to pay respects or assume a praying posture. 拝見 はいけん is humble seeing, not a claim that looking at a document is an act of religious worship.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Notice 扌 beside several horizontal strokes crossed by an upright stroke and imagine hands brought together respectfully. This is a modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology.

The canonical radical is 手; the left-hand shape is 扌.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ハイ | on | [拝見](../words/拝見.md) はいけん - seeing or looking at (humble) |
| おが.む | kun | [拝む](../words/拝む.md) おがむ - pay respects; assume a praying posture |
| おろが.む | kun | Source-only reading; see the qualification below. |

KANJIDIC2 also records `おろが.む`. No suitable word example was established in the inspected JMdict snapshot. These are character-dictionary readings for lookup, not recommendations to use this kanji as an everyday spelling of a word.

## Common words

- [拝見](../words/拝見.md) はいけん - seeing or looking at (humble)
- [拝む](../words/拝む.md) おがむ - pay respects; assume a praying posture

## Notes

- The inspected JMdict entry for 拝む gives おがむ. It is not independent evidence that the same example is pronounced おろがむ; that source reading remains qualified lookup information.
- 拝見する describes the speaker's own side's seeing humbly, not an honorific description of the listener's action.
- 拝む also has a begging sense and a humble seeing sense; JMdict notes that the latter is sometimes sarcastic in modern Japanese. Do not assume that every occurrence means religious worship.

# 優 — gentle; superior

**Strokes:** 17 · **Key (radical):** 人 (as 亻, no. 9) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

優 links several vocabulary families: kindness in [優しい](../words/優しい.md) やさしい,
excellence in 優れる すぐれる, and performers in 俳優 はいゆう.
The whole word tells you which meaning matters; a 優しい person is being described as kind, not ranked first.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the small person-shaped 亻 making room for the large, elaborate
right-hand shape 憂. Giving someone room can cue gentleness. This is an invented modern-shape aid.

## Readings

The table selects useful words; metadata also preserves ウ and まさ.る.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| やさ.しい | kun | [優しい](../words/優しい.md) やさしい — kind; gentle |
| すぐ.れる | kun | 優れる すぐれる — excel; be superior |
| ユウ | on | 俳優 はいゆう — actor |

## Common words

- [優しい](../words/優しい.md) やさしい — kind; gentle
- 優れる すぐれる — excel; be superior
- [俳優](../words/俳優.md) はいゆう — actor
- [優劣](../words/優劣.md) ゆうれつ — superiority or inferiority; relative merits

## Notes

[夏](夏.md) has a somewhat similar top-and-sweeping-bottom outline, but no 亻.
[借](借.md) and [側](側.md) share 亻 without 優's complex right side.

# 困 — be in difficulty

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 囗 (no. 31) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

困 is being in a difficult position: troubled, stuck, or unsure what to do.
[困る](../words/困る.md) こまる describes the person's difficulty; 困難 こんなん names difficulty or hardship more generally.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** imagine 木 growing inside a frame with no room to spread. The cramped tree is a memory aid for being stuck, not a claim about the character's history.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| こま.る | kun | [困る](../words/困る.md) こまる — be troubled; be at a loss |
| コン | on | 困難 こんなん — difficulty; hardship |

## Common words

- [困る](../words/困る.md) こまる — be in difficulty
- 困難 こんなん — difficulty; difficult
- 困らせる こまらせる — put someone in difficulty; inconvenience

## Notes

The enclosure radical 囗 surrounds 木. In [因](因.md), the enclosed shape is 大 instead.
Keep the direction of the verbs separate: 困る is to be troubled; 困らせる is to put someone else in that position.
[難しい](../words/難しい.md) むずかしい can describe a difficult task without saying that a particular person is currently stuck.

# 給 — pay; bestow

**Strokes:** 12 · **Key (radical):** 糸 (no. 120) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

給 concerns giving or supplying something, including money paid for work. In [給料](../words/給料.md)
きゅうりょう, it points to pay; 給食 きゅうしょく concerns providing a meal instead.
Do not assume that every word containing 給 refers to wages.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** picture the narrow 糸 on the left tying together a package to hand over;
the right side has the shape 合. This is a memory aid, not a historical explanation.

## Readings

Focus on キュウ in these compounds. The source's kun readings remain in the metadata.

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| キュウ | on | [給料](../words/給料.md) きゅうりょう — pay; 給食 きゅうしょく — meal provision |

## Common words

- [給料](../words/給料.md) きゅうりょう — salary; wages
- 月給 げっきゅう — monthly salary
- 給食 きゅうしょく — meal service, such as a school lunch service
- [俸給](../words/俸給.md) ほうきゅう — salary; pay, especially for public employees

## Notes

Distinguish [絵](絵.md), [紙](紙.md), and [終](終.md) by the part to the right of 糸.
給料 is money received for work, not the general word for everything one earns or receives.
