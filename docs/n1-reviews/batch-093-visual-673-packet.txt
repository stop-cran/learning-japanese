# 刑 — criminal punishment

**Strokes:** 6 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

刑 concerns criminal punishment or a legal sentence. Here "sentence" means a penalty imposed for an offense, not a grammatical sentence.
In [刑事](../words/刑事.md) けいじ, it contributes the criminal-law association: the whole word can name a police detective or concern criminal matters.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the squared-off left part as a rule board beside the blade radical 刂, a reminder of a legal penalty. This invented association does not establish the character's history or a phonetic component.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ケイ | on | [刑事](../words/刑事.md) けいじ — police detective; criminal matters; 刑罰 けいばつ — criminal punishment; penalty |

The frozen KANJIDIC2 metadata lists ケイ and no kun readings. Other dictionaries have broader reading coverage: Digital Daijisen also records ギョウ, as in the historical ministry name 刑部省 ぎょうぶしょう. That does not change the reading of 刑事 to ぎょうじ.

## Common words

- [刑事](../words/刑事.md) けいじ — police detective; criminal matters
- 刑 けい — penalty; sentence
- 刑罰 けいばつ — criminal punishment; penalty
- 刑法 けいほう — criminal law; penal code

## Notes

- [刀](刀.md) is the dictionary radical; its visible right-side form here is 刂. Compare [形](形.md), with three slanting strokes 彡 on the right, and [型](型.md), with 土 underneath the 刑-shaped upper part.
- The main lesson here is punishment. Dictionaries also record older model/form-related senses of 刑; the shape comparisons above are not a claim that 刑, 型 and 形 have never overlapped in meaning.
- 刑事 also has the slang whole-word reading でか. Learn it as a separate reading of the whole word, not as a kun reading of 刑 alone. The linked article's primary reading remains けいじ.
- In legal expressions, 刑事 does not mean "criminal" as a noun naming an offender. Use the surrounding noun and sentence role to distinguish criminal matters from the detective.

# 刑事 (けいじ) - police detective; criminal matters

## Meaning

刑事 has two related uses: a noun for a police detective, and "criminal" in legal expressions concerning criminal matters.

- 刑事が目撃者に話を聞いています。 (けいじがもくげきしゃにはなしをきいています。) - A detective is talking to a witness.
- これは刑事事件です。 (これはけいじじけんです。) - This is a criminal case.

In the first example, 刑事 is a person. In 刑事事件, it qualifies 事件 (じけん), "case/incident"; it does not mean "a detective's incident." Another useful combination is 刑事裁判 (けいじさいばん), "criminal trial."

## How the kanji combine

[刑](../kanji/刑.md) contributes criminal punishment, and [事](../kanji/事.md) contributes a matter or affair. The legal meaning is therefore a helpful guide to the compound. The on readings are ケイ + ジ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[警官](警官.md) (けいかん) means a police officer, a broader role than detective. [探偵](探偵.md) (たんてい) can refer to a detective such as a private investigator. For legal matters, 民事 (みんじ), "civil," contrasts with 刑事; it is not an opposite kind of police officer.

## Distinctive meaning

Use the surrounding noun and sentence role to separate the two senses. 刑事が来た (けいじがきた) means a detective came. 刑事事件 concerns criminal law. Neither use means "criminal" as a noun naming the person who committed an offense.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1249660, senses 1-2.

# 事 — matter, affair

**Strokes:** 8 · **Key (radical):** 亅 (no. 6) · **JLPT:** N4 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

事 means "matter, affair, thing to be done", and from there "business", "incident" and "fact". The early form is usually explained as a hand holding a tool or a pennant-like object, in the sense of "to serve, to do a task", but scholars disagree on the details, so treat the picture as uncertain.
It is filed under 亅 (the hook stroke) only for dictionary purposes; the hook does not carry the meaning.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ジ | on | [食事](../words/食事.md) しょくじ, 大事 だいじ, 火事 かじ, 事故 じこ, 事件 じけん |
| ズ | on | rare, e.g. 好事家 こうずか (a dilettante, an enthusiast) |
| こと | kun | 事 こと (a matter), 大事なこと (an important matter), 仕事 しごと (voiced: ごと) |

## Common words

- [食事](../words/食事.md) しょくじ — a meal
- 仕事 しごと — work, job
- 火事 かじ — fire (a blaze)
- [百科事典](../words/百科事典.md) ひゃっかじてん — encyclopedia
- [刑事](../words/刑事.md) けいじ — police detective; criminal matters

## Notes

- 事 (こと) is an abstract matter or an act, while [物](物.md) (もの) is a concrete thing. 事 also serves as a formal noun (the こと-nominalizer) but is usually written in kana in that role; see the [koto and wake article](../articles/koto-wake-and-formal-nouns.md).
- 仕事 しごと is read with the kun reading (voiced ごと), while 食事 and 火事 use ジ.
- Look-alikes: 事 has a vertical stroke with a hook running through the whole character, with 一 and a box-like part above it; compare [力](力.md) and [話](話.md), which are not related in shape.
