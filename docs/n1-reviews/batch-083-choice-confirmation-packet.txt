11: # 凌ぐ (しのぐ) — endure; surpass
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 凌ぐ can mean **endure, keep out, or get through** something difficult.
16: It can also mean **surpass or outdo** someone or something in ability,
17: degree or quality. Learn the whole phrase rather than assigning one
18: English equivalent to every occurrence.
19: 
20: - 風雨を凌ぐ (ふううをしのぐ) — shelter from wind and rain
21: - 前作を凌ぐ傑作 (ぜんさくをしのぐけっさく) — a masterpiece surpassing the previous work
22: 
23: These short phrases illustrate the two branches. The difficulty faced or
24: the thing surpassed takes を. 凌ぐ is a transitive godan verb ending in
25: ぐ: しのぎます, しのいで, しのいだ, しのがない.
26: 
27: ## How the kanji combine
28: 
29: [凌](../kanji/凌.md) supplies the enduring or surpassing idea. The native
30: verb is しのぐ: ぐ is written as okurigana, while 凌 represents しの-.
31: The same word, not a different reading, has both meaning branches.
32: 
33: ## Synonyms and antonyms
34: 
35: [こらえる](こらえる.md) and [我慢](我慢.md) (がまん), with する, overlap in
36: endurance. Their restraint uses do not all transfer to 凌ぐ.
37: [凌駕](凌駕.md) (りょうが), with する, overlaps with the surpassing branch,
38: not with sheltering from the weather.
39: 
40: 忍ぶ (しのぶ) is a separate verb that can also mean endure.
41: Its ending is ぶ, not 凌ぐ's ぐ: do not confuse しのいで with しのんで.
42: 
43: ## Distinctive meaning
44: 
45: Getting through a difficulty does not by itself promise a permanent
46: solution. Conversely, 凌ぐ is not restricted to brief emergencies.
47: In a comparison, it means going above the other thing, not merely
48: matching it. The weather phrase above expresses protection or endurance;
49: the masterpiece phrase expresses comparative quality.
50:

11: # 凌駕 (りょうが) — surpass; outdo
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 凌駕 names surpassing or outdoing another thing. It is a noun and a
16: する-verb: 凌駕する, 凌駕します, 凌駕した, 凌駕しない.
17: The thing surpassed takes を.
18: 
19: - 前作を凌駕する売れ行き (ぜんさくをりょうがするうれゆき) — sales surpassing those of the previous work
20: 
21: Here the comparison concerns sales, not a claim that the new work is
22: artistically better. The context determines the dimension being compared.
23: 
24: ## How the kanji combine
25: 
26: [凌](../kanji/凌.md) contributes surpassing, read リョウ here.
27: 駕 (ガ) also has a rising-above-others sense, documented with this
28: compound. The two reinforce the surpassing idea; do not force 駕's
29: separate vehicle meaning into a literal story about this word.
30: 
31: ## Synonyms and antonyms
32: 
33: [凌ぐ](凌ぐ.md) (しのぐ) overlaps in surpassing, but also has endurance and
34: weather-protection uses that 凌駕 does not share.
35: [優れる](優れる.md) (すぐれる) describes excellence or superiority; it is
36: an intransitive verb, so its grammar is not 凌駕する's object + を pattern.
37: 
38: 上回る (うわまわる), "exceed; surpass," also suits numerical comparisons.
39: These related words are not unrestricted substitutes in every phrase.
40: 
41: ## Distinctive meaning
42: 
43: 凌駕する means going above the comparison target, not simply reaching
44: the same level. Identify what is surpassed and in what respect.
45: It is not a general expression for surviving a hardship.
46: 
47: 陵駕 (りょうが) is also recognized, but JMdict marks that spelling as
48: rarely used. This does not make the pronunciation りょうが rare or make
49: 凌 and 陵 freely interchangeable elsewhere.

10: 
11: # 冴える (さえる) — be clear-headed; be vivid
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 冴える describes mental clarity or alertness, and a crisp, vivid quality
16: in sights, colours or sounds. Learn the whole expression with its subject:
17: 頭が冴える (あたまがさえる) means to be clear-headed; 目が冴える
18: (めがさえる) means to be wide awake, often when you would rather sleep.
19: 
20: - 今日は頭が冴えています。
21:   (きょうはあたまがさえています。) — I feel clear-headed today.
22: - 目が冴えて眠れません。
23:   (めがさえてねむれません。) — I'm wide awake and can't sleep.
24: 
25: This is an intransitive ichidan verb: the subject takes が.
26: Forms include 冴えます (さえます), 冴えた (さえた), 冴えて (さえて)
27: and 冴えない (さえない). 冴えている can describe an existing alert state.
28: The examples adapt dictionary examples to polite endings.
29: 
30: ## How the kanji combine
31: 
32: [冴](../kanji/冴.md) supplies the clarity meaning in this single-kanji native verb.
33: In the written form 冴える, 冴 represents さ and the following える completes
34: さえる. The conjugation stem is さえ, including the written え.
35: It is not a compound assembled from on readings.
36: 
37: JMdict also records 冱える (さえる) as a rarely used kanji spelling.
38: That spelling flag does not make the pronunciation さえる rare.
39: 
40: ## Synonyms and antonyms
41: 
42: [覚める](覚める.md) (さめる) focuses on waking or regaining awareness:
43: 目が覚める (めがさめる) means "wake up," whereas 目が冴える describes wakefulness.
44: [澄む](澄む.md) (すむ) and [透き通る](透き通る.md) (すきとおる)
45: overlap for clear sound, but physical transparency is not a universal meaning of 冴える.
46: 
47: For an opposite evaluation, 冴えない can mean "lacklustre" or "unsatisfactory,"
48: depending on what is being described; it is not a single antonym for every sense.
49: 
50: ## Distinctive meaning
51: 
52: 冴える can describe deft technique or a well-executed performance, not only
53: mental alertness. It does not generally mean a permanent high intelligence score,
54: nor does 目が冴える mean eyes lighting up with happiness.
55: 
56: The negative 冴えない often concerns a disappointing result, an unappealing
57: appearance or a subdued condition rather than literal blur.
58: An older, somewhat old-fashioned sense of 冴える is "become intensely cold."
59: For ordinary cooling, learn [冷える](冷える.md) (ひえる);
60: for freezing, [凍る](凍る.md) (こおる). Those verbs are not alternative
61: pronunciations of the 冴える taught here.
62:

65: Usage and example bases: [Digital Daijisen: 冴える](https://kotobank.jp/word/冴える-508792),
66: especially sense 5; clarity, performance and negative uses in senses 2–7.

16:         val byKanji = all.associateBy { it.kanji }
17:         val chosen = LinkedHashMap<String, QuizCard>()
18:         val usedTitles = hashSetOf(target.title)
19:
