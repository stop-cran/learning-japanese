# 娯 — recreation; amusement

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 女 (no. 38), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

娯 concerns enjoyment and recreation. [娯楽](../words/娯楽.md) ごらく
means entertainment, amusement or recreation: something people enjoy,
not necessarily something childish or frivolous.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice [女](女.md) beside 呉.
Imagine the figure taking a break for an enjoyable activity. This is a
memory scene for the modern shape, not a historical explanation of its
parts or a claim about a sound component.

## Notes

- [誤](誤.md), mistake/error, shares 呉 but has 言 on the left.
  [妹](妹.md), younger sister, and [妙](妙.md), strange/exquisite,
  share 女 but have different right sides and meanings.
- Enjoyment overlaps [楽](楽.md), [遊](遊.md) and [享](享.md).
  享 often concerns enjoying benefits or rights, whereas 娯楽 focuses on
  recreation; the shared English enjoyment cue is not a substitution rule.
- Pleasure also connects with [喜](喜.md), rejoice, [快](快.md),
  pleasant/agreeable, and [幸](幸.md), happiness/good fortune. Those
  comparisons are excluded from meaning choices without claiming that
  amusement, delight, comfort and lasting happiness are identical.
- Learn the linked article's noun uses. A dictionary's recognition of
  娯楽する does not make every English entertainment phrase a natural
  Japanese する construction.
