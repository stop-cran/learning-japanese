# 汽 — steam; vapor

**Strokes:** 7 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

汽 concerns steam or vapor. In [汽車](../words/汽車.md) きしゃ, it
points to the steam association of a steam train. 汽船 きせん is a
steamship, and 汽笛 きてき a steam whistle.

Learn the whole word as well: 汽車 has dated or regional uses for
long-distance or non-electric trains that do not require a steam engine.
汽水 きすい means brackish water, not steam water.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** see the water form 氵 beside
气 and imagine vapor rising from water. This is an association with the
modern shape, not a verified account of its historical formation.

## Notes

- [河](河.md), river/watercourse, [池](池.md), pond/reservoir, and
  [海](海.md), sea/ocean, share 氵 but do not mean steam.
- [蒸](蒸.md) directly overlaps steam; [気](気.md) has broader air
  and vapor associations. 気 has extra crossing strokes inside 气,
  whereas 汽 has 氵 at the left. Both comparisons are excluded.
- [雰](雰.md) declares the vapor/mist comparison with 汽; the pair is
  not repeated here. [煙](煙.md) is smoke or fumes, not a rule that
  every visible plume is steam.
- The canonical radical is [水](水.md), not the separate right-hand 气.
  School grade and a community JLPT placement are different classifications.
