# 糖 — sugar

**Strokes:** 16 · **Key (radical):** 米 (no. 119), on the left · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

糖 concerns sugar: the sweetening substance in
[砂糖](../words/砂糖.md) さとう and sugars in terms such as
糖類 とうるい, saccharides. 糖分 とうぶん concerns sugar content
or the amount of sugar.

This is a substance meaning, not the adjective sweet. A food can taste
sweet without added table sugar, and the word 糖 by itself does not
describe how sweet a finished dish tastes.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** notice [米](米.md) beside
the 唐-shaped cluster. Imagine a container of sugar beside rice in
a cupboard. This is a visual memory scene, not a claim that all sugar
is made from rice or an explanation of the character's history.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| トウ | on | [砂糖](../words/砂糖.md) さとう - sugar |

Practice selects トウ, also the Joyo reading. In 砂糖, 砂 supplies
サ and 糖 supplies トウ, with a long vowel. Daijisen additionally
lists あめ as a kun reading; it is outside this frozen metadata and
practice selection, not an alternative to トウ in さとう.

## Common words

- [砂糖](../words/砂糖.md) さとう - sugar
- 糖分 とうぶん - sugar content
- 糖類 とうるい - saccharides; sugars
- 糖尿病 とうにょうびょう - diabetes

## Notes

- [米](米.md), rice grain, is the left component.
  [数](数.md), number, and [類](類.md), kind/category, also contain
  米-shaped strokes but do not mean sugar.
- [甘](甘.md) concerns sweet taste or indulgence, whereas 糖 names
  a substance. [菓](菓.md) concerns confectionery and snacks; it does
  not name the sugar ingredient itself. [粉](粉.md) is powder, not
  necessarily sugar or even food.
- In 砂糖, [砂](砂.md) provides a useful granular appearance
  association; sugar does not contain sand.
- 糖尿病 is a disease name. Recognizing its characters is not a
  medical explanation of its causes or a diagnostic rule.
