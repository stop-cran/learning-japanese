# 穂 — grain-bearing head

**Strokes:** 15 · **Key (radical):** 禾 (no. 115) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

穂 is an **ear or head of grain**: the grain-bearing part, not the entire plant. In [稲穂](../words/稲穂.md) いなほ, it is the head of a rice plant. English "ear" here is not the body part [耳](耳.md).

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find 禾 on the left and 心 at the bottom right. Imagine the weight of a grain head bending toward the heart-shaped lower strokes. This is a memory aid, not an account of the character's origin.

## Notes

- A dictionary also gives 穂 ほ the senses **tip or point** of a brush or spear, **crest** of a wave, and a **scion**, a shoot used in grafting. Context matters; not every occurrence concerns grain.
- [稲](稲.md) names the rice plant. 穂 is not a synonym for rice as food.
- Compare [積](積.md), pile up: it has 責 on the right. [想](想.md), thought or conception, has 相 above 心; [息](息.md), breath, has 自 above 心. Neither has 禾 on the left.
- The on reading スイ is retained and illustrated, but the selected reading question uses the transparent ほ in 稲穂. This is a teaching selection, not a claim that スイ is invalid or that 出穂 is obsolete.
