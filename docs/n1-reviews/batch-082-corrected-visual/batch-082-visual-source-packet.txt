11: # 准教授 (じゅんきょうじゅ) — associate professor
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 准教授 names an **associate professor**. In the Japanese university system,
16: this is an established academic rank below [教授](教授.md) きょうじゅ
17: (professor), with teaching and research responsibilities. It does not mean
18: a professor's personal helper.
19: 
20: - 彼女は大学の准教授です。 (かのじょはだいがくのじゅんきょうじゅです。) — She is an associate professor at a university.
21: - 田中准教授の講義を聞きました。 (たなかじゅんきょうじゅのこうぎをききました。) — I listened to Associate Professor Tanaka's lecture.
22: 
23: The second example places the rank after a name. For respectful direct
24: address, 田中先生 (たなかせんせい) can be appropriate; [先生](先生.md) is not
25: itself a statement of this particular academic rank.
26: 
27: ## How the kanji combine
28: 
29: [准](../kanji/准.md) modifies the rank head [教授](教授.md), marking the
30: associate rank. Within 教授, [教](../kanji/教.md) contributes teaching and
31: 授 contributes imparting knowledge. The on-readings are ジュン + キョウ + ジュ.
32: Learn the whole institutional title rather than translating it as
33: "half a professor."
34: 
35: ## Synonyms and antonyms
36: 
37: - [助教授](助教授.md) じょきょうじゅ is the former Japanese title replaced
38:   by 准教授 in April 2007. It remains relevant when reading older documents.
39: - 助教 じょきょう, often translated "assistant professor," is a separate rank,
40:   not an abbreviation of 准教授.
41: - [助手](助手.md) じょしゅ means assistant and has its own academic uses.
42:   It does not by itself identify someone as a 准教授.
43: - [教授](教授.md) きょうじゅ names the professor rank; [先生](先生.md)
44:   せんせい is a broader designation or respectful form of address.
45: 
46: These are role and title comparisons, not strict antonyms.
47: 
48: ## Distinctive meaning
49:

11: # 助教授 (じょきょうじゅ) - associate professor (former Japanese title)
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 助教授 names a former Japanese university or college rank below 教授,
16: professor. The Japanese rank was renamed [准教授](准教授.md) (じゅんきょうじゅ),
17: associate professor, in April 2007. Read 助教授 in its historical or
18: institutional context.
19: 
20: - 彼は当時、その大学の助教授でした。 (かれはとうじ、そのだいがくのじょきょうじゅでした。) - At that time, he held the former associate-professor rank at that university.
21: - 古い資料には「助教授」と書かれています。 (ふるいしりょうには「じょきょうじゅ」とかかれています。) - The old document uses the title jokyoju.
22: 
23: 当時 means "at that time." The examples deliberately concern an earlier
24: title, not a claim about a current appointment.
25: 
26: ## How the kanji combine
27: 
28: [助](../kanji/助.md) contributes assisting; [教](../kanji/教.md) and 授
29: contribute teaching and imparting knowledge. 助 qualifies [教授](教授.md)
30: (きょうじゅ). The established job title has its own institutional history:
31: it is not just any helper who assists a professor.
32: The on readings are ジョ + キョウ + ジュ.
33: 
34: ## Synonyms and antonyms
35: 
36: 准教授 is the successor Japanese title. 助教 (じょきょう), often translated
37: assistant professor, is a separate rank. [助手](助手.md) (じょしゅ) means
38: assistant and has its own academic uses; it is not another spelling of 助教授.
39: 
40: ## Distinctive meaning
41: 
42: The dictionary records both assistant professor and associate professor as
43: English glosses. Do not automatically equate the former Japanese rank with
44: the current US rank called assistant professor. The reform also distinguished
45: 助教 from 助手; changing 助教授 to 准教授 did not merely give all these
46: roles one interchangeable name.
