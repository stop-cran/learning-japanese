10: 
11: # 冴える (さえる) — be clear-headed; be vivid
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 冴える describes mental clarity or alertness, and a crisp, vivid quality
16: in sights, colours or sounds. Learn the whole expression with its subject:
17: 頭が冴える (あたまがさえる) means to be clear-headed; 目が冴える
18: (めがさえる) means to be wide awake, often when you would rather sleep.
19: 
20: - 今日は頭が冴えています。
21:   (きょうはあたまがさえています。) — I feel clear-headed today.
22: - 目が冴えて眠れません。
23:   (めがさえてねむれません。) — I'm wide awake and can't sleep.
24: 
25: This is an intransitive ichidan verb: the subject takes が.
26: Forms include 冴えます (さえます), 冴えた (さえた), 冴えて (さえて)
27: and 冴えない (さえない). 冴えている can describe an existing alert state.
28: The examples adapt dictionary examples to polite endings.
29: 
30: ## How the kanji combine
31: 
32: [冴](../kanji/冴.md) supplies the clarity meaning in this single-kanji native verb.
33: In the written form 冴える, 冴 represents さ and the following える completes
34: さえる. The conjugation stem is さえ, including the written え.
35: It is not a compound assembled from on readings.
36: 
37: JMdict also records 冱える (さえる) as a rarely used kanji spelling.
38: That spelling flag does not make the pronunciation さえる rare.
39: 
40: ## Synonyms and antonyms
41: 
42: [覚める](覚める.md) (さめる) focuses on waking or regaining awareness:
43: 目が覚める (めがさめる) means "wake up," whereas 目が冴える describes wakefulness.
44: [澄む](澄む.md) (すむ) and [透き通る](透き通る.md) (すきとおる)
45: overlap for clear sound, but physical transparency is not a universal meaning of 冴える.
46: 
47: For an opposite evaluation, 冴えない can mean "lacklustre" or "unsatisfactory,"
48: depending on what is being described; it is not a single antonym for every sense.
49: 
50: ## Distinctive meaning
51: 
52: 冴える can describe deft technique or a well-executed performance, not only
53: mental alertness. It does not generally mean a permanent high intelligence score,
54: nor does 目が冴える mean eyes lighting up with happiness.
55: 
56: The negative 冴えない often concerns a disappointing result, an unappealing
57: appearance or a subdued condition rather than literal blur.
58: An older, somewhat old-fashioned sense of 冴える is "become intensely cold."
59: For ordinary cooling, learn [冷える](冷える.md) (ひえる);
60: for freezing, [凍る](凍る.md) (こおる). Those verbs are not alternative
61: pronunciations of the 冴える taught here.

26: ## How the kanji combine
27: 
28: [冶](../kanji/冶.md) (ヤ) contributes smelting or working metal.
29: [金](../kanji/金.md) (キン) means metal here, not money or gold alone.
30: The relationship is action plus object: working metal. The whole noun names
31: the technical field, not just one operation. This is a meaning aid, not an
32: account of the word's historical formation.
33: 
34: ## Synonyms and antonyms
35: 
36: 冶金学 (やきんがく) specifically names metallurgy as a science.
37: [金属](金属.md) (きんぞく) means the material "metal," not the field that studies
38: and processes it. 製錬 and 精錬 (both せいれん) concern smelting or refining:
39: related processes rather than the whole discipline.
40: There is no useful everyday opposite of the field's name.
41: 
42: ## Distinctive meaning
43: 
44: 冶金 belongs in scientific, educational and industrial contexts. It does not
45: mean making money, and it is not limited to gold. 錬金術 (れんきんじゅつ),
46: "alchemy," is a different term, not a synonym for modern metallurgy.
47: 
48: Use [溶かす](溶かす.md) (とかす) when describing an action such as melting
49: butter or dissolving sugar; 冶金 names the metalworking field and its technology,
50: not any act of making something liquid.

11: # 隼 (はやぶさ) — falcon
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 隼 means **falcon**, especially the peregrine falcon. Dictionaries also
16: record a broader falcon use, so not every occurrence is an exact species
17: identification. The native noun is usually written **はやぶさ** or **ハヤブサ**;
18: 隼 is its kanji spelling.
19: 
20: - 空を隼が飛んでいます。 (そらをはやぶさがとんでいます。) — A falcon is flying through the sky.
21: - 隼の写真を撮りました。 (はやぶさのしゃしんをとりました。) — I took a photograph of a falcon.
22: 
23: These examples name the bird; they do not teach how to identify a species
24: from its appearance.
25: 
26: ## How the kanji combine
27: 
28: This is a single native noun, not a multi-kanji compound.
29: The whole character [隼](../kanji/隼.md) is read **はやぶさ** here.
30: Its on-readings シュン and ジュン are not alternative pronunciations to insert
31: into these sentences.
32: 
33: ## Synonyms and antonyms
34: 
35: - [鷹](鷹.md) (たか), "hawk; falcon," is an overlapping bird-of-prey word,
36:   not a strictly exclusive opposite of はやぶさ.
37: - [鳥](鳥.md) (とり), "bird," is the broader category.
38:   [動物](動物.md) (どうぶつ), "animal," and
39:   [生き物](生き物.md) (いきもの), "living thing; creature," are broader still.
40: - 鷲 (わし), "eagle," is a related bird name, not an unrestricted synonym.
41: 
42: There is no useful antonym.
43: 
44: ## Distinctive meaning
45: 
46: Use はやぶさ for the documented falcon noun, not every bird of prey.
47: English bird names and Japanese common names do not form a simple
48: one-to-one species key. Katakana animal spelling does not make this native
49: word a foreign loanword.
50: 
51: This article teaches the bird, not the separate Hayabusa spacecraft name
52: or a personal-name reading. The kanji's grade-9 classification does not
53: erase its non-name lexical use.
