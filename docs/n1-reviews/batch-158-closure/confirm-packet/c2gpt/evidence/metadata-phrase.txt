348c4f70:kanji/泡.md:36:The selected reading あわ is the reading of the linked word article, which is 泡 alone with no okurigana. JMdict also gives 泡 the reading あぶく, restricted to the spelling 泡; the Kanjipedia page lists it but KANJIDIC2 does not, so it is not part of this card's metadata or questions. 泡立つ あわだつ, "to bubble; to foam; to froth," uses the same あわ. 水泡 すいほう is "foam; bubble," and its second sense, "nothing," is the base of the idiom 水泡に帰する すいほうにきする, "to come to nothing; to go down the drain; to go up in smoke."
