2. **Choice of word.** I first check whether an existing article, including a kana-headed one, already teaches a suitable word, checking its spelling, reading and senses; if so, it is linked instead of adding one. Otherwise I take a word from the pinned JMdict, preferring a spelling with one of JMdict's common-word tags (news1, ichi1, spec1, gai1); where none exists, an untagged word may be used with a justification reviewed for that row. In either case the spelling and reading must have no irregular, rare, outdated, ateji or gikun mark. Its reading must use one KANJIDIC2 reading of the kanji, and its other kanji must already have cards or join the same batch. A word that shows the kanji's main meaning is preferred. The tags guide the choice; they are not evidence of frequency. A survey with my script (not in the repository) found, at `415eb687`:
   - a tagged word whose other kanji have cards: 401 Joyo and 27 name-use rows;
   - a tagged word only with another kanji that has no card yet: 16 Joyo and 3 name-use rows;
   - only untagged words: 38 Joyo and 154 name-use rows;
   - no word: 11 name-use rows.

   Rows outside the first group are decided one by one when they come up; a row with no defensible word is set aside and reported.
