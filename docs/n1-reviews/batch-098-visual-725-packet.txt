# 剤 — medicinal preparation; chemical agent

**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

剤 is useful as a compound element for a medicinal preparation or chemical agent. [洗剤](../words/洗剤.md) せんざい means detergent or cleaning agent: this use is not limited to medicine that people take. Learn 剤 inside words rather than using it as a general standalone noun for any substance.

**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the crossed strokes and lower bars of the 斉-shaped left block beside the blade-shaped 刂, as though a preparation were being carefully divided. This invented scene helps remember the modern shape; it is not a historical or phonetic explanation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ザイ | on | [洗剤](../words/洗剤.md) せんざい — detergent; cleaning agent |
| スイ | on | KANJIDIC2 source-only lookup reading; no verified modern word example is taught here. |
| セイ | on | KANJIDIC2 source-only lookup reading; no verified modern word example is taught here. |
| かる | kun | KANJIDIC2 source-only lookup reading, retained without an okurigana dot; no verified word spelling is taught here. |
| けず.る | kun | KANJIDIC2 source-only lookup reading; no verified modern word example is taught here. |

Only ザイ is selected for reading questions. The other four readings remain dictionary reference information, not interchangeable pronunciations of 洗剤. Not selecting a word example does not establish that a reading is invalid or obsolete.

For ordinary かる, "mow; trim," learn [刈る](../words/刈る.md); for けずる, "shave; sharpen; reduce," learn [削る](../words/削る.md). Those words do not establish 剤 as an alternative spelling.

## Common words

- [洗剤](../words/洗剤.md) せんざい — detergent; cleaning agent
- 薬剤 やくざい — medicine; chemical agent
- 錠剤 じょうざい — tablet; pill
- 調剤 ちょうざい — preparing or dispensing a prescription

These are recognition examples, not instructions about using a product or medicine.

## Notes

- The indexing radical is [刀](刀.md), visible here as right-hand 刂. [済](済.md), "settle; finish," has a similar 斉-shaped block but adds left-hand 氵 instead. Check the side and shape of the extra strokes, not just the shared block.
- [剛](剛.md), "sturdy; unyielding strength," and [則](則.md), "rule; follow a model," also have right-hand 刂 but different left blocks and meanings.
- [薬](薬.md) also concerns medicine and chemicals. Its meanings overlap with 剤; do not treat them as mutually exclusive categories or assume that a compound containing either names something safe to ingest.
- 製剤 せいざい, "pharmaceutical formulation," does not demonstrate 剤's セイ: 剤 supplies ざい there. Likewise, in 推進剤 すいしんざい, "propellant," the initial すい belongs to 推, not 剤.
- JMdict also records the whole word 粘剤 ねり, a material used in making Japanese paper. That whole-word spelling does not establish an independent ねり or り reading for 剤.

# 洗剤 (せんざい) - detergent; cleaning agent

## Meaning

洗剤 is a detergent or cleaning agent used to remove dirt.
The noun names the substance, not the action of washing.
It can concern laundry, dishes and other cleaning purposes.

- この洗剤で食器を洗います。 (このせんざいでしょっきをあらいます。) - I wash dishes with this detergent.
- 洗濯用の洗剤を買いました。 (せんたくようのせんざいをかいました。) - I bought laundry detergent.

で marks the substance used as the means in the first example.
洗濯用 (せんたくよう) specifies "for laundry" in the second.

## How the kanji combine

[洗](../kanji/洗.md) contributes washing, and [剤](../kanji/剤.md) an agent or preparation.
The first character identifies the purpose of the second.
The on readings are セン + ザイ.

## Synonyms and antonyms

[せっけん](せっけん.md), soap, is a more specific cleaning substance.
The public dictionary includes soap as well as synthetic detergents within 洗剤;
do not impose a rule that soap can never be 洗剤.
[洗う](洗う.md) (あらう) is the verb "wash."

## Distinctive meaning

The exact product and its intended use matter.
洗剤 does not mean every cleaning tool, and a product intended for dishes
is not automatically suitable for washing the body.
The distinction from せっけん is useful but not an absolute exclusion of soap
from the broader cleaning-agent category.

Dictionary: [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), entry 1390950, sense 1.
Category scope: [Digital Daijisen: 洗剤](https://www.weblio.jp/content/洗剤).

# 洗 — wash

**Strokes:** 9 · **Key (radical):** 水 (as 氵, no. 85) · **JLPT:** N3 (community list)

## Meaning and origin

洗 is useful for washing hands, dishes, clothes, and other things.
[洗う](../words/洗う.md) あらう names the action; 洗濯 (せんたく) is washing or laundry.
Start with this concrete washing sense rather than treating every kind of cleaning as 洗う.

**Modern-shape mnemonic:** water plus the visible [先](先.md), "before," reminds you to
wash your hands before eating. This is a modern memory aid, not a derivation.

## Readings

| Reading | Type | Typical words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| あら.う | kun | [洗う](../words/洗う.md) あらう — wash |
| セン | on | 洗濯 せんたく — laundry; washing |

## Common words

- [洗う](../words/洗う.md) あらう — wash; rinse
- 洗濯 せんたく — laundry; washing
- [洗剤](../words/洗剤.md) せんざい — detergent; cleaning agent

## Notes

手を洗ってください。 (てをあらってください。) — Please wash your hands.
The thing washed takes を, and 洗う has the て-form 洗って (あらって).
The split legs at the lower right distinguish 洗 from [活](活.md), whose right side ends in a box.

+# 剤 — medicinal preparation; chemical agent
+
+**Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 刀 (no. 18), appearing as 刂 on the right · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
+
+## Meaning and origin
+
+剤 is useful as a compound element for a medicinal preparation or chemical agent. [洗剤](../words/洗剤.md) せんざい means detergent or cleaning agent: this use is not limited to medicine that people take. Learn 剤 inside words rather than using it as a general standalone noun for any substance.
+
+**Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Picture the crossed strokes and lower bars of the 斉-shaped left block beside the blade-shaped 刂, as though a preparation were being carefully divided. This invented scene helps remember the modern shape; it is not a historical or phonetic explanation.
+

+
+Only ザイ is selected for reading questions. The other four readings remain dictionary reference information, not interchangeable pronunciations of 洗剤. Not selecting a word example does not establish that a reading is invalid or obsolete.
+
+For ordinary かる, "mow; trim," learn [刈る](../words/刈る.md); for けずる, "shave; sharpen; reduce," learn [削る](../words/削る.md). Those words do not establish 剤 as an alternative spelling.
+
+## Common words
+
+- [洗剤](../words/洗剤.md) せんざい — detergent; cleaning agent
+- 薬剤 やくざい — medicine; chemical agent
+- 錠剤 じょうざい — tablet; pill
+- 調剤 ちょうざい — preparing or dispensing a prescription
+
+These are recognition examples, not instructions about using a product or medicine.
+
+## Notes
+
+- The indexing radical is [刀](刀.md), visible here as right-hand 刂. [済](済.md), "settle; finish," has a similar 斉-shaped block but adds left-hand 氵 instead. Check the side and shape of the extra strokes, not just the shared block.
+- [剛](剛.md), "sturdy; unyielding strength," and [則](則.md), "rule; follow a model," also have right-hand 刂 but different left blocks and meanings.
+- [薬](薬.md) also concerns medicine and chemicals. Its meanings overlap with 剤; do not treat them as mutually exclusive categories or assume that a compound containing either names something safe to ingest.
+- 製剤 せいざい, "pharmaceutical formulation," does not demonstrate 剤's セイ: 剤 supplies ざい there. Likewise, in 推進剤 すいしんざい, "propellant," the initial すい belongs to 推, not 剤.
+- JMdict also records the whole word 粘剤 ねり, a material used in making Japanese paper. That whole-word spelling does not establish an independent ねり or り reading for 剤.
