17: 
18: **Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 冫 (no. 15) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
19: 
20: ## Meaning and origin
21: 
22: 准 can mark a status corresponding to, but not fully the same as, a named rank.
23: In [准教授](../words/准教授.md) じゅんきょうじゅ, it helps name the established

31: the two marks of 冫 as a bird standing beside a rank sign. This is an invented

16: # 隼 — falcon
17: 
18: **Strokes:** 10 · **Key (radical):** 隹 (no. 172) · **JLPT:** N1 (community list)
19: 
20: ## Meaning and origin
21: 
22: 隼 is the **falcon** character. The native bird noun
23: [隼](../words/隼.md) はやぶさ is usually written in kana.
24: It especially names the peregrine falcon, but dictionaries also record a
25: broader falcon use; it is not a name for every bird of prey.
26: 
27: **Modern-shape mnemonic, not etymology:** Find the bird-shaped radical 隹
28: above a cross like [十](十.md). Imagine a falcon perched above a cross-shaped
29: marker. This is a memory aid, not a historical claim that 隼 means "ten birds."
30: The indexing radical is 隹, not the independent [鳥](鳥.md) character.
31: 
32: ## Readings

34: | Reading | Type | Typical words |

38: | はやぶさ | kun | [隼](../words/隼.md) はやぶさ — falcon; usually written はやぶさ or ハヤブサ |
39: 
40: This lesson practices **はやぶさ** only. Both on-readings remain in the full
41: metadata. ジュン is a documented reading, not an error or an automatically
42: name-only form. The archaic label above belongs to 鷹隼, not to every possible
43: use of シュン.
44:

59:   Independent [十](十.md) means "ten," not "falcon."
60: - [鷹](鷹.md) means "hawk; falcon" and has a much more complex shape,
61:   including 鳥 below. Do not invent an exclusive hawk-versus-falcon naming
