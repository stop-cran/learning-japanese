For each D/E row, supply a source-line citation supporting the semantic decision in the Reason or Disposition cell. Briefly identify the potentially overlapping sense, then state the evidence that supports the decision. A short explanation with a citation is enough; do not add columns or change the report skeleton.

Read all applicable senses in each supplied comparison article, not just its title or first paragraph. Do not infer that a sense is exclusively physical, exclusively abstract, confined to a particular domain, or absent merely because another sense comes first. If that restriction is material to a harmless disposition, it needs positive support in the opened evidence. Explicitly address secondary meanings that the article itself includes.

A whole-word meaning does not automatically establish a constituent character meaning. Conversely, saying "only a compound" does not refute a possible ordinary secondary sense either. Decide from the actual character and word evidence, preserving the distinction without using it as a blanket reason to dismiss a comparison. Likewise, a nominal role versus an action can be a relevant distinction but is not by itself a proof that ordinary meaning choices cannot overlap.

Do not assert an exhaustive negative merely because one source lacks a gloss. If the opened evidence cannot settle a consequential semantic decision, state the uncertainty and propagate UNVERIFIED to the affected assessment. Do not force agreement or a particular verdict. A supported NEEDS FIX is a valid review outcome; a CLEAN outcome is not the goal.

In the Related new card(s) column, write only the literal new-card character(s) whose direct pair that row assesses, separated by ", " when there is more than one. Do not put parentheses, exclusion provenance, or explanatory text in that cell. Put relevant cross-card context in Reason or Disposition instead. This does not change the scope: only direct pairs involving each of the three new cards are adjudicated.

Before the one authorized write, check every harmless disposition against its cited source: does the explanation address the strongest plausible overlap found there, or merely a different title, first sense, part of speech, compound status or current runtime exposure? Correct the reasoning or disclose uncertainty in the draft before writing. Do not inspect any earlier run or create a second report.

CITATION FIELD GRAMMAR (form only). In every Findings or Optional points "Citations:" bullet, each semicolon-delimited citation must repeat the complete source path followed by a colon and exactly one line number or one contiguous range. Do not put comma-separated line numbers or ranges after a source path. To cite noncontiguous locations in the same source, repeat the full source path after each semicolon. The literal layout is "<source>:<line>; <source>:<first>-<last>". Replace placeholders with your own opened sources and line numbers; the layout supplies no source, claim or verdict. Check every such bullet against this grammar before the sole report write.

Finish with exactly one report write, then one complete view readback (forceReadLargeFiles: true). Do not hash it: the coordinator will. Send ONE phase-2 send_session_message to the coordinator whose entire message value is exactly CLEAN, NEEDS FIX or UNVERIFIED, with at most one trailing LF or CRLF newline. No report path, explanation, recommendations, extra spaces, extra lines, hash or other text is allowed in that coordinator message; its findings and evidence must be in the report. An ordinary final assistant response in this isolated reviewer session may summarize its report, but is not review evidence. No further investigation or tool calls are authorized after the completion message except the existing optional task_complete. Then stop; do not start further work.
