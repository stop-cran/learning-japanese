しん【臣】
［音］シン（漢）　ジン（呉）　［訓］おみ
［学習漢字］4年
〈シン〉主君に仕える人。家来。
The entry marks ジン as the same sense and lists 大臣（だいじん）.

The separately opened noun entry しん【臣】 also defines a person serving a ruler.
It additionally gives a humble first-person use by a retainer addressing the ruler.
The おみ entry has a person-serving-a-lord sense and an ancient hereditary-title sense.
These summaries are coordinator translations; the short character definition above is quoted.
The complete modern word 大臣 is a cabinet minister; this excerpt does not relabel
every contemporary minister, public official or supervisor as a feudal retainer.
