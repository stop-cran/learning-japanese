17:

20: ## Meaning and origin
21:

28: [疑](疑.md) as someone pausing so intently that they seem frozen in place.

11: # 凝固 (ぎょうこ) — coagulation; solidification
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 凝固 names coagulation or solidification. With する, it describes a material
16: congealing or becoming solid; the material takes が.
17: 
18: - 血液が凝固する。（けつえきがぎょうこする。）— Blood coagulates.
19: 
20: It also names a change to the solid state, including water becoming ice.
21: The English translation depends on the material: blood **coagulates**;
22: a liquid can **solidify**. Not every 凝固 is water freezing.
23: 
24: ## How the kanji combine
25: 
26: [凝](../kanji/凝.md) contributes congealing and [固](../kanji/固.md) firmness
27: or hardening. Their related meanings reinforce the solidifying idea.
28: Read ギョウ + コ; learn the established scientific uses as whole-word meanings.
29: 
30: ## Synonyms and antonyms
31: 
32: [固まる](固まる.md)（かたまる）, "harden; solidify," overlaps in a material
33: becoming firm. [凍る](凍る.md)（こおる）, "freeze," is useful for water or a
34: surface freezing over. [冷凍](冷凍.md)（れいとう）concerns freezing or frozen
35: storage, rather than every kind of coagulation.
36: 
37: ## Distinctive meaning
38: 
39: 凝固 is useful in descriptions of materials and blood. It is not the term for
40: mental concentration: compare [集中](集中.md)（しゅうちゅう）.
41: The physical process is not necessarily a change to ice, and this vocabulary
42: explanation does not provide medical advice.

11: # 凝る (こる) — become stiff; get absorbed
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 凝る has three useful branches: muscles becoming stiff, becoming absorbed
16: in an activity, and putting elaborate care into details.
17: 
18: - 肩が凝る。（かたがこる。）— My shoulders get stiff.
19: - ゴルフに凝っています。（ゴルフにこっています。）— I'm really into golf.
20: - 凝った衣装（こったいしょう）— an elaborate outfit
21: 
22: In the first pattern, the stiff body part takes が. In the hobby pattern,
23: the activity takes **に**. This is an intransitive godan verb despite ending
24: in る: 凝ります（こります）, 凝って（こって）, 凝らない（こらない）.
25: 凝った is its た-form; before a noun it can mean "elaborate."
26: Do not add な as if 凝った were a na-adjective.
27: 
28: ## How the kanji combine
29: 
30: [凝](../kanji/凝.md) writes こ in this native verb; る is the written ending.
31: Its stiffness and absorption associations help connect the uses, but one
32: English gloss does not predict every construction.
33: 
34: ## Synonyms and antonyms
35: 
36: [熱中](熱中.md)（ねっちゅう）and [夢中](夢中.md)（むちゅう）overlap in
37: absorption. [集中](集中.md)（しゅうちゅう）focuses on concentration and need
38: not imply enthusiasm for a hobby.
39: 
40: [凝らす](凝らす.md)（こらす）is transitive in expressions such as
41: 工夫を凝らす（くふうをこらす）, "exercise one's ingenuity."
42: Do not simply replace the に in ゴルフに凝る with を.
43: 
44: ## Distinctive meaning
45: 
46: 凝る is not the general adjective "hard": [硬い](硬い.md)（かたい）describes
47: hardness or stiffness. Nor must getting absorbed in a hobby be a problem.
48: The elaborate-detail use can praise care and craftsmanship.
49: 
50: This article's primary reading is **こる**. Dictionaries separately record
51: 凝る **こごる**, "congeal; jell," usually written in kana, and **しこる**,
52: "stiffen; harden." Do not read the shoulder and golf examples as こごる or しこる.
53: Daijisen also records gathering or cold-induced solidification under こる;
54: the three practical branches above are not an exhaustive definition.
55:

11: # 凝らす (こらす) — concentrate; apply effort
12: 
13: ## Meaning
14: 
15: 凝らす directs concentrated attention or effort. Learn it with established
16: objects marked by **を**, rather than using it for every English "focus."
17: 
18: - 目を凝らして見ました。（めをこらしてみました。）— I looked closely, straining my eyes.
19: - 工夫を凝らしました。（くふうをこらしました。）— I exercised my ingenuity.
20: 
21: 目を凝らす（めをこらす）concerns looking intently. 工夫を凝らす
22: （くふうをこらす）concerns inventive effort, not staring at an idea.
23: The verb is transitive and godan: 凝らします（こらします）,
24: 凝らして（こらして）, 凝らさない（こらさない）.
25: 
26: ## How the kanji combine
27: 
28: [凝](../kanji/凝.md) writes こ here, with らす as the written ending.
29: This is one native verb, not a compound of separate words for "concentration"
30: and "doing."
31: 
32: ## Synonyms and antonyms
33: 
34: [集中](集中.md)（しゅうちゅう）overlaps in focusing attention.
35: [注ぐ](注ぐ.md)（そそぐ）can describe devoting energy or attention.
36: Their objects and particles must still fit the particular expression.
37: 
38: [凝る](凝る.md)（こる）instead describes stiffness, absorption, or elaborate
39: care: compare ゴルフに凝る, "be really into golf," with 工夫を凝らす,
40: "exercise one's ingenuity." The latter takes 工夫 as its object.
41: 
42: ## Distinctive meaning
43: 
44: The object affects the translation. 息を凝らす（いきをこらす）means
45: "hold one's breath," not "stare at one's breath."
46: 
47: The same spelling has a separately recorded reading **こごらす**, including
48: "cause to congeal; freeze." This article teaches **こらす**.
49: 凝らせる can be the potential form こらせる of this godan verb; the dictionary
50: verb 凝らせる **こごらせる**, "cause to congeal," is a different reading.
51: The homophone 懲らす（こらす）means "punish; chastise," not concentrate.
52:
