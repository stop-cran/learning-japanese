12: # 省く (はぶく) - omit; cut down on
13: 
14: ## Meaning
15: 
16: 省く means to omit or leave something out, or to save effort or expense by
17: cutting it down. It is a transitive godan verb: mark what is omitted or saved
18: with を.
19: 
20: - 時間がないので、詳しい説明は省きます。 (じかんがないので、くわしいせつめいははぶきます。) - Since there is no time, I will omit the detailed explanation.
21: - この道具を使うと、手間を省けます。 (このどうぐをつかうと、てまをはぶけます。) - Using this tool saves effort.
22: 
23: 省きます (はぶきます) is polite nonpast; 省いて (はぶいて) is the て-form.
24: 省ける (はぶける) is the potential form, "can omit/save."
25: 
26: ## How the kanji combine
27: 
28: [省](../kanji/省.md) contributes omitting or economizing in this word. The native verb is
29: はぶく, not the on reading ショウ used in some compounds. The final く
30: changes with conjugation.
31: 
32: ## Synonyms and antonyms
33: 
34: [除く](除く.md) (のぞく) overlaps in excluding or removing.
35: 省略 (しょうりゃく) means omission or abbreviation and overlaps when leaving
36: part of an explanation out. Adding information is a contextual opposite,
37: not the meaning of one fixed antonym.
38: 
39: ## Distinctive meaning
40: 
41: 省く often concerns reducing what a process requires, as in 手間を省く.
42: It does not automatically mean careless omission: the result may be an
43: efficient procedure. Conversely, omitting something does not prove that
44: it was unimportant; the surrounding explanation supplies the reason.
