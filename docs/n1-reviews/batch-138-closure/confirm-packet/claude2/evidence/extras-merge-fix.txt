Batch 138: why the earlier reading audits left out some --extra readings, and what the corrected audits show
=============================================================================================================

Written by the author on 2026-11-18. The two audit tools (reading_exposure.py and reading_options.py) and the
author's gate runner are not in any packet; this file states what changed in them and why.

1. The defect
-------------
- Both tools take any number of `--extra KANJI=reading,reading,...` arguments. Each tool parsed them with
      extra[k] = [x for x in v.split(",") if x]
  (reading_exposure.py line 179, reading_options.py line 188), so a second --extra for the same kanji REPLACED the
  first one instead of adding to it.
- For batch 138 the author's gate runner passed two --extra arguments for five kanji (垂, 執, 壊, 奏, 奪): first the
  readings Kanjipedia marks as outside the official table and the readings from the Digital Daijisen character entries
  and headwords, then, after all other --extra arguments, the JMdict kanji-plus-okurigana readings listed in
  okurigana-scan.txt. Line 1 of every earlier reading-exposure.txt and reading-options.txt shows both arguments, but
  only the second took effect.
- Both tools label every --extra reading "(card text)", whatever its source; the label does not mean the card names
  the reading. Line 2 of each output states the sources.
- This affects the original packet (batch-138-b6020f9e, evidence\audits\reading-exposure.txt and
  reading-options.txt) and both first-round confirmation packets (confirm-138-claude and confirm-138-gpt,
  checks\reading-exposure.txt and checks\reading-options.txt).
- The published audits of batches 135 to 137 pass one --extra per kanji (checked by counting the --extra keys on
  line 1 of every reading-exposure.txt and reading-options.txt in their closure folders), so they are not affected.

2. The fix
----------
- Both tools now add to the list instead of replacing it, skipping duplicates:
      extra.setdefault(k, []).extend(x for x in v.split(",") if x and x not in extra.get(k, []))
- The gate runner now merges the two lists per kanji and passes one --extra per kanji, keeping the order. Line 1 of
  the corrected outputs shows the merged arguments, for example
      --extra 壊=エ,やぶ.る,やぶ.れる,こわ.る,こほ.つ,こぼ.つ

3. Readings the earlier audits did not apply (the first argument, minus readings also in the second)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  垂  しで, なんなん.とする, しず.る, しずれ
  執  とら.える
  壊  エ, やぶ.る, やぶ.れる, こわ.る
  奏  すす.める, もう.す
  奪  タツ

  - Three of these 12 cannot matter. The tools ignore every reading in the card's own front matter and every
    sound-change variant of one (`excluded_keys`, which applies the same variants as ReadingOptions.mutations in
    evidence\Words.kt). エ and やぶる (壊) are in 壊's front matter, and たつ (奪) is a voicing variant of 奪's だつ.
  - しで (垂) was still checked, because JMdict entry 1767000 also gives it; the corrected outputs list it as
    "(card text, JMdict 1767000)".
  - The other eight were not checked at all: なんなんとする, しずる, しずれ (垂); とらえる (執); やぶれる, こわる (壊);
    すすめる, もうす (奏).
