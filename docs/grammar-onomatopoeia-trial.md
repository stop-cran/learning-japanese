# Onomatopoeia Grammar pilot: owner-trial protocol

Work item: [#58](https://github.com/stop-cran/learning-japanese/issues/58).
Content scope and source limitations: [the pilot ledger](grammar-onomatopoeia-sources.json).

**Status: not run. Learning effectiveness: unverified. Expansion: deferred.**
This document is a protocol, not invented owner feedback, a completed learner study or a
claim that the app has automatic holdout management.

## Participants and materials

The owner is the initial participant. Confirm familiarity with the selected meanings of
ぐっすり, そっと, きらきら, にこにこ, しっかり, わくわく, どきどき, いらいら,
ふわふわ, ぶかぶか, へとへと and ぺこぺこ. Record known/uncertain/unknown per word and
selected sense. Supply a neutral meaning if needed, and report that support rather than
treating vocabulary recall as the grammar result.

Record content version, app revision, device, input method, study dates, and prior familiarity
with each construction family. Do not alter word or Grammar schedules merely to obtain a
convenient score. Use targeted practice or manually administered prompts.

A facilitator prepares **two matched six-task manual forms** before the first session:
one adverb-with-ordinary-verb task, one する reaction, two noun modifiers with different
lexical behavior, one state/change task, and one descriptive-した interpretation.
Use different situations and wording on the two forms. Inspect sources and all fitting
answers before administration; the form is not generated from a Cartesian suffix table.

Match the elicited operation and the amount of supplied material, not only the family label.
A bare-word state completion and a word-plus-に completion before なる, for example, are not
interchangeable baseline/delayed probes.

Keep the delayed form out of the learner-facing article, runtime package and practice pool.
The facilitator may keep it outside the synced source. Merely calling a published item a
"holdout" does not prevent exposure. The forms are not yet administered or certified.

## Baseline before practising

1. Before reading the companion guide or doing the pilot, ask which relevant cards, guides,
   examples and situations the owner has already seen. Record prior exposure by task, not
   just whether the exercise ID is new.
2. Administer the six baseline tasks without answer hints or correction between tasks.
   Present free typing/handwriting first. Only then show a choice version, if desired.
   Report that choice result as **after a production attempt**, not an independent sample.
3. Save the exact response, assistance, perceived clarity and confidence. Score construction
   appropriateness separately from spelling/input mistakes. An unlisted but fitting manual
   response requires adjudication, not automatic labeling as bad Japanese.
4. Record source/guide exposure and prior construction knowledge. A high baseline may leave
   little measurable room for improvement; do not reinterpret it as a benefit of later practice.

## Practice

Use Grammar with All or Unclassified and the **Onomatopoeia in sentences** chapter/set.
Record the actual navigation path and any discovery difficulty. Use a manageable session
of around 10-15 minutes on two separate days rather than requiring completion of every item.
This duration is a practical trial plan, not a research-backed optimum.

Record which exercise IDs and modes appeared, retries, hints/card/guide lookups, and any
answer judged wrong despite fitting the visible task. Choice and typing share an exercise
schedule; they are not independent mastery tracks. Additional correct buttons appear only
occasionally, so their absence in a short session is not evidence that variants are unsupported.

Do not practise, preview, explain or add the delayed form to the app during this period.
Trained-item accuracy and ease of navigation are useful observations, but not transfer scores.

## Delayed check

Aim for **seven days after the final practice session**, recording the actual interval.
Do not enforce this as an app gate or an optimal forgetting-curve claim.

Before each delayed task, check whether its wording or scenario has since appeared in a
card, guide, app exercise or outside study. Mark exposed tasks and do not count them as
clean holdouts. If an independently reviewed replacement is unavailable, report the smaller
unexposed sample rather than silently calling it equivalent coverage.

Administer the matched delayed form with the same response-order and assistance rules as
the baseline. Record intervening Japanese study, missing sessions, hints and any input issues.
Compare unassisted production with production, and choice-after-production with the same
baseline condition. Never combine their numerators into one "mastery" percentage.

## Recording and interpretation

For each observation record:

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Task and family | Exact manual prompt or runtime ID; selected word/sense/construction |
| Exposure | Previously seen wording/scenario, source, approximate date, or unknown |
| Phase and mode | Baseline, practice or delayed; typing/handwriting or choice |
| Response | Exact response, accepted alternative, omission, retry or timeout |
| Assistance | Supplied meaning, reading hint, dictionary/guide lookup or none |
| Judgment | Construction fit, input/spelling issue, uncertainty needing review |
| Experience | Confusing instructions, navigation trouble, useful explanation, confidence |

Report counts as small descriptive observations. Compare matched families and separate
unexposed delayed responses from memorized-item performance. Even an improvement after
practice is **not causal or population-wide proof**: this is one uncontrolled owner trial
with potentially different prompts, practice effects and outside study.

Revise content when a supported answer is rejected, a distractor fits, or instructions hide
the intended response boundary. File an app follow-up only for a reproduced runtime defect.
Any expansion beyond the bounded pilot is a separate decision after reviewing feedback;
without results, retain **effectiveness unverified** and **expansion deferred**.
