### B23-GPT-01 — Medium; correctness — Missing 系統／筋 quiz exclusion

**Claim and impact:** 系統 has the answer label **“system; lineage”**, but neither endpoint excludes 筋. 筋 has an ordinary secondary lineage/family-line meaning, even though its current article concentrates on other senses. Consequently, “system; lineage” is a defensible answer for 筋, and 筋 is a defensible Japanese choice for the lineage part of that English prompt. This can produce false grading in context-free meaning quizzes.

This is actual lexical overlap, not merely a shared “line” metaphor. Excluding 家族 or 一家 does not protect this pair.

**Minimal remedy:** Add one direct `系統`／`筋` exclusion at either endpoint, preserving existing exclusions and titles.

**Precise evidence:** The complete principal family **JMdict 1251030**, selected senses 1–2, supports 系統’s system/lineage teaching. Independently inspected [Digital Daijisen, 筋](https://www.weblio.jp/content/筋), **すじ noun sense 7**, gives the family-line meaning, illustrated by descent from nobility. The actual two metadata declarations contain no direct pair. `docs\content-format.md:79–100` establishes semantic checking and symmetric, non-transitive exclusions.

**Confidence:** High.

**Claim and impact:** The following differently worded titles remain eligible together:

- 形式 — **form; format**
- 形 — **shape; form**
- 型 — **mold; type; pattern**

In particular, 形式 and 形 explicitly share form as opposed to substance; 型 includes prescribed/standard form; 式 includes form and type. 型 also has a formula/convention sense. Exact-title deduplication therefore does not resolve the ambiguity.

The established missing pairs are **形式／形, 形式／型, 形式／式, 形／型, 形／式, and 型／式**. The last three matter when these cards appear as other choices together; protecting only pairs involving 形式 would not protect them transitively.

These are not claims that their Japanese constructions are freely interchangeable, or that mathematical 形式 means every formula. The problem is the breadth of the context-free English answer labels. The gaps are inherited rather than all newly introduced, but fall within the requested reuse and quiz-choice review.

**Minimal remedy:** Declare those six direct pairs, distributed across endpoints as convenient. Preserve current exclusions, stable identities, levels, titles, and the useful distinctions in the prose.

**Precise evidence:** Complete allowed comparison families: **JMdict 1250220, sense 3**; **1250090, senses 1, 3–4**; **1319060, sense 1**; and principal **1250310, senses 1–2**.
