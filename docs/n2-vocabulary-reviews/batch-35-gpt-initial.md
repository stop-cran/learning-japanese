**B35-GPT-01 — しっぽ / 仕舞い**

Location: `words\しっぽ.md:4,9,16,23`; supporting card `words\仕舞い.md:4,9,16`.

The expressly taught “tail end” extension overlaps 仕舞い’s final-part meaning. Its title, **“end; finish,”** can therefore be accepted for that extension. This is not an assertion that an animal’s tail means finishing an activity: [Seisen’s 仕舞い entry, sense ②](https://kotobank.jp/word/仕舞い-3137845), explicitly covers the last portion of an order, ranking or time sequence. The supplied しっぽ entry separately licenses its end extension.

Minimal remedy: add **仕舞い** to しっぽ’s exclusions. Existing protection through 終わり or 最後 is insufficient.

**B35-GPT-02 — 実用 / 用いる**

Location: `words\実用.md:4,9,16`; supporting card `words\用いる.md:4,9,16`.

**“Practical use; utility”** and **“use; make use of”** leave an overlapping “use” answer unprotected. 用いる’s actual card teaches using tools, materials or methods; this is not merely a shared usefulness topic. Independently retrieved Seisen entries confirm modern [実用](https://kotobank.jp/word/実用-520994) as actual/practical use and [用いる](https://kotobank.jp/word/用いる-645695), sense ③, as putting something to use. Their different grammatical classes do not remove the context-free English-label overlap.

Minimal remedy: add **用いる** to 実用’s exclusions.

**B35-GPT-03 — 実力 / 能**

Location: `words\実力.md:4,9,16`; supporting card `words\能.md:4,9,16,33`.

実力’s actual-ability sense overlaps 能’s **talent** meaning. 能’s unrelated noh meaning does not make its first title alternative safe as an opposing answer. The complete [Jisho/JMdict 能 record](https://jisho.org/api/v1/search/words?keyword=能) identifies **のう**, noun, with talent/gift/function as sense 1; the existing card also explicitly teaches ability in 能がない. This is the same relevant overlap already recognized for 才能 and 能力, but their exclusions do not propagate.

Minimal remedy: add **能** to 実力’s exclusions.

**B35-GPT-04 — 実例 / ケース**

Location: `words\実例.md:4,9,16`; supporting card `words\ケース.md:4,9,16`.

**“Actual example; real case”** is not safely opposed to **“case; container; instance.”** ケース has an ordinary abstract instance/situation sense, not only a physical container. Its complete [Jisho/JMdict record](https://jisho.org/api/v1/search/words?keyword=ケース), sense 2, confirms that identity; 実例’s supplied principal sense explicitly includes an actual instance.

Minimal remedy: add **ケース** to 実例’s exclusions. Their separate exclusions involving 例 do not protect this pair.

**B35-GPT-05 — 地盤 / 基 and 基本**

Location: `words\地盤.md:4,9,23`; supporting cards `words\基.md:4,9,16` and `words\基本.md:4,15`.

The taught supporting-foundation sense overlaps both **“basis; foundation”** and **“basics; foundation.”** These are not merely words concerning construction. [Seisen’s 地盤 entry](https://kotobank.jp/word/地盤-522987), sense ④, explicitly identifies an underlying basis/basic foundation. The complete [Jisho/JMdict results](https://jisho.org/api/v1/search/words?keyword=基) confirm the foundation sense associated with **基・もと**, and 基本’s noun sense includes basis/foundation.

Minimal remedy: add **基** and **基本** to 地盤’s exclusions. Existing 基盤/基礎 guards, and 基’s guard against 基本, do not cover these two pairs.

**B35-GPT-06 — しびれる / 凍える**

Location: `words\しびれる.md:4,9,16`; supporting card `words\凍える.md:4,9,16–18`.

凍える’s title explicitly offers **“become numb with cold,”** overlapping しびれる’s **“become numb.”** The cause-specific qualification does not eliminate that ordinary overlap. Independently retrieved Seisen entries support loss of sensation for [痺れる](https://kotobank.jp/word/痺れる-523102), sense ①, and loss of bodily sensation through cold for [凍える](https://kotobank.jp/word/凍える-498854).

Minimal remedy: add **凍える** to しびれる’s exclusions. This does not imply that their causes and full usage ranges are identical.

**B35-GPT-07 — 紙幣 / 金銭**

Location: `words\紙幣.md:4,9,16`; supporting card `words\金銭.md:4,9,16,33`.

金銭’s title supplies the broad answer **“money,”** which remains defensible for 紙幣’s paper-money identity. The existing card expressly rejects a coin-only interpretation. [Seisen’s 金銭 entry](https://kotobank.jp/word/金銭-481384), modern sense ②, confirms money/currency generally. The problem is that broad answer label—not merely both words belonging to finance.

Minimal remedy: add **金銭** to 紙幣’s exclusions, consistently with the existing お金 and 金 protections. Those intermediary guards are not transitive.

**B35-GPT-08 — 絞る / ぎゅうぎゅう**

Location: `words\絞る.md:4,9,16`; supporting card `words\ぎゅうぎゅう.md:4,8,17,28`.

ぎゅうぎゅう’s actual title includes **“squeezing hard,”** an acceptable description of 絞る’s squeezing action. Its complete [Jisho/JMdict record](https://jisho.org/api/v1/search/words?keyword=ぎゅうぎゅう) confirms squeezing in sense 2 and forceful pressing/tying/twisting in sense 3. The supplied 絞る principal senses and independently retrieved [Daijisen entry](https://kotobank.jp/word/絞る-523544) confirm the literal wringing/squeezing use. Mimetic manner versus verb is a useful distinction, but the displayed titles do not reliably enforce it.

Minimal remedy: add **ぎゅうぎゅう** to 絞る’s exclusions.
