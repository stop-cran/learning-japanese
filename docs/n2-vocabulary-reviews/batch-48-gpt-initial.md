**B48-GPT-01 — Medium — Correctness: missing 属する / 入る guard.**

**Location:** `words\属する.md:1–9,31–35`; `words\入る.md:1–9,15–18,34–36`. Neither endpoint declares the pair. The comparison correctly identifies an act-of-joining use of 入る, but that does not exhaust its ordinary meanings: complete pinned **1465590 sense 3**, specifically at reading **はいる**, includes “come under (a heading)” and “belong (in a category).” Those match 属する’s displayed **“belong to; come under”**, supported by 1405710:1. Thus that new title can be a defensible answer for 入る despite its primary title being “to enter; be inside.”

**Minimal remedy:** add 入る to 属する’s direct exclusions; do not remove either word’s valid teaching. **Confidence: high.**

**B48-GPT-02 — Medium — Correctness: missing 続々 / ばたばた guard.**

**Location:** `words\続々.md:4–9`; `words\ばたばた.md:4–8,15–19,29`. The older card explicitly teaches quick succession and translates its falling-signs example as **“one after another.”** Consequently 続々’s complete title **“in succession; one after another”** can correctly describe that ordinary secondary use of ばたばた. The existing ばたばた/次々 and ばたばた/どんどん guards do not protect this new pair transitively. The freshly retrieved [日本国語大辞典 entry](https://kotobank.jp/word/ばたばた-601814), sense `[1]①(ニ)`, independently supports repeated successive falling.

**Minimal remedy:** append ばたばた to 続々’s exclusions. **Confidence: high.**

**B48-GPT-03 — Medium — Correctness: additive guards omit そして and そうして.**

**Location:** `words\そのうえ.md:4–9`; `words\そのほか.md:4–9`; `words\そして.md:4–9,15,29`; `words\そうして.md:4–9,15–19`. Four direct pairs are unprotected: each new additive card against each older connective. The older complete titles—**“and then”** and **“and then; in that way”**—do not establish safety: both bodies explicitly teach addition. For those uses, **“moreover; on top of that”** or **“the rest; besides that; etc.”** supplies a defensible additive answer. Pinned 1006880:1 and 1006900:2 establish the new additive senses. Fresh [Daijisen そして](https://www.weblio.jp/content/そして) refers to そうして, and the complete [日本国語大辞典 然うして entry](https://kotobank.jp/word/然うして-552446) explicitly gives additive そのうえ/それに加えて. Existing guards through それと or other connectors do not close these pairs.

**Minimal remedy:** append そして and そうして to both new cards’ exclusions. **Confidence: high.**

**B48-GPT-04 — Medium — Correctness: unqualified English “survey” remains a competing answer for 測量.**

**Location:** `words\測量.md:4–9,16–22,34–37`; `words\調査.md:4–9,15–20,39–40`. 測量’s title is **“surveying; measurement”**; 調査’s is **“investigation; survey.”** The latter’s unqualified “survey” is also a normal English noun for the land-measuring operation taught and translated in 測量. Their informative body comparison is not displayed in a title-only quiz. Complete pinned **1404590:1** and **1429120:1** support these actual glosses, and neither endpoint excludes the other. This is an English-label collision, not a claim that every 調査 is 測量.

**Minimal remedy:** append 調査 to 測量’s exclusions, retaining the useful distinction in the prose. **Confidence: medium-high.**

**B48-GPT-05 — Medium — Correctness: the new 備える possession sense lacks direct possession guards.**

**Location:** `words\備える.md:1–9,25–32`; `words\持つ.md:1–9,15–22,34`; `words\ある.md:1–9,15–18`. This finding concerns the new possession addition, not a request to redesign the original preparation lesson.

**Minimal remedy:** declare 備える/持つ and 備える/ある directly on one endpoint each. If 備える’s metadata must remain unchanged, declare them on the two comparison cards. **Confidence: high.**
