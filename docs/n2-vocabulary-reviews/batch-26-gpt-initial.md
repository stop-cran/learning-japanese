## Findings: three direct quiz-exclusion omissions

**N2-26-GPT-01 — Moderate; correctness: 公衆/一般 remains an ambiguous permitted pair.**

**Location:** `words\公衆.md:4–9`; comparison `words\一般.md:4–9,16–18,29`.

公衆 displays **“the general public”**, while 一般 displays **“general; ordinary.”** 一般 has an ordinary public-related noun use: its actual article explicitly teaches 一般の人 as “ordinary people; the general public,” and independently inspected [Daijisen 一般](https://www.weblio.jp/content/一般), noun sense **2㋑**, records ordinary people/the public, including 一般に公開する. Consequently, 公衆’s displayed title is defensible for 一般 despite not being its primary title. Neither card directly excludes the other. Any protection through 公共 is nontransitive.

**Learning impact:** a quiz can offer a legitimate secondary-sense answer as a supposedly wrong choice.

**Minimal remedy:** add `一般` to 公衆’s `quiz_exclusions`; preserve both existing identities, titles and levels.

**Confidence:** high.

**N2-26-GPT-02 — Moderate; correctness: こうして lacks guards against ambiguous directional English labels.**

**Location:** `words\こうして.md:4–9,16–21,45–46`; comparisons `words\こちら.md:4,15–19` and `words\こっち.md:4,15–19`.

こうして displays **“thus; in this way”**; こっち displays **“this way”**, and こちら displays **“this way; this person.”** English “this way” can naturally describe manner—“Fold the paper this way”—as well as direction. Thus, こっち’s unqualified English label is a defensible answer for the manner use actually exemplified by こうして, and an English→Japanese “this way” prompt lacks the context needed to separate them.

The Japanese distinction in the body is correct. Complete evidence families **2008040:1** and **1004500:1–5** support manner/result versus direction/side/person; this finding is **English-label ambiguity, not Japanese synonymy**. Neither directional card declares an exclusion, and こうして excludes neither.

**Learning impact:** correct prose distinctions do not prevent an ambiguous context-free quiz question.

**Minimal remedy:** add both `こちら` and `こっち` to こうして’s direct exclusions, retaining the useful body comparison and stable titles.

**Confidence:** high for the label collision.

**N2-26-GPT-03 — Moderate; correctness: 功績/働き omits an ordinary secondary-sense guard.**

**Location:** `words\功績.md:4–9`; comparison `words\働き.md:4–9,16–21,29–33`.

功績 displays **“achievement; meritorious contribution.”** 働き’s primary title is **“work; functioning,”** but its ordinary secondary meaning includes achievement/performance. The actual existing article teaches a person’s performance or contribution. Independently retrieved [Jisho’s complete 働き record](https://jisho.org/api/v1/search/words?keyword=働き) gives **“achievement”** in sense 2 without a rare/archaic restriction; independently inspected [Daijisen 働き](https://www.weblio.jp/content/働き), sense 2, likewise covers accomplishment/results of work.

Accordingly, “achievement” is a legitimate answer for 働き, not merely a shared work-related category. The differing primary titles do not deduplicate, and neither direct exclusion list protects this pair.

**Learning impact:** a learner recognizing 働き’s achievement sense can be penalized for accepting 功績’s English label.

**Minimal remedy:** add `働き` to 功績’s `quiz_exclusions`; no releveling or title change is necessary.

**Confidence:** high.

All three findings apply the documented title-based, symmetric but **pairwise/nontransitive** exclusion contract in `docs\content-format.md:78–106`.
