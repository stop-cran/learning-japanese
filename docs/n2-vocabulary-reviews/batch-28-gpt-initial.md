**B28-GPT-01 — Moderate severity · Correctness: missing direct quiz exclusion**

**Location:** `words\腰掛ける.md:4,9`; comparison `words\掛ける.md:1–9`.

The new answer label is “sit down; take a seat,” but its exclusions contain only 座る and おかけください. Existing 掛ける has no reciprocal exclusion. Its narrower article title, “hang something up,” does not eliminate the ordinary **掛ける／かける = take a seat** sense from a context-free Japanese answer.

**Learning impact:** a quiz can present two defensible Japanese answers for “sit down; take a seat,” or offer both hanging and sitting meanings for 掛ける. Different titles do not deduplicate, and exclusions through another card are nontransitive.

**Minimal remedy:** add **掛ける** directly to 腰掛ける’s `quiz_exclusions`. No expansion or retitling of the existing 掛ける article is necessary.

**Precise evidence:** independently inspected [Jisho’s 掛ける entry](https://jisho.org/search/%E6%8E%9B%E3%81%91%E3%82%8B), sense 11, explicitly includes taking a seat/sitting. Independently inspected [Digital Daijisen’s 掛ける entry](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E6%8E%9B%E3%81%91%E3%82%8B), heading か・ける【掛ける／懸ける】, sense 16㋐, includes the chair-taking construction. This finding does not depend on treating a selected-evidence omission as dictionary absence.

**Confidence: high.**
