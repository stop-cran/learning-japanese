**B70-01 — Medium; Correctness — `words\ピストル.md:26`**

**Claim/impact:** “This is a katakana loanword from English ‘pistol’” misattributes the borrowing source as written. Being a loanword, having an English equivalent, and historically entering Japanese through English are different claims.

**Exact evidence:** The complete JMdict **1106660** record establishes the noun meaning but supplies no loan-source attribution. The frozen Daijisen roman heading `pistol` does not independently establish a historical borrowing route. 精選版 日本国語大辞典 explicitly identifies **Dutch `pistool`** in its [ピストル entry](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E3%81%B4%E3%81%99%E3%81%A8%E3%82%8B-3166237).

**Minimal remedy:** Remove “from English ‘pistol’,” leaving the safe katakana-loanword explanation, or replace it with a sourced Dutch attribution. Retain `gairaigo`, the primary reading, title and identity.

**Confidence:** High.

**B70-02 — Medium; Scoped coverage / quiz ambiguity — `words\引き出す.md:4,9,16–21`; `words\引き出し.md:4–9,15–24`**

**Claim/impact:** 引き出す and 引き出し remain eligible together despite directly overlapping financial meanings. Their different titles—“withdraw money; pull out” and “drawer; cash withdrawal”—do not trigger exact-title deduplication. A learner can reasonably associate the withdrawal label with the verb’s ordinary banking meaning; the noun/verb distinction alone does not eliminate the documented ambiguity.

**Exact evidence:** 引き出す explicitly teaches withdrawing money from an account. 引き出し explicitly teaches the corresponding financial transaction and gives `現金の引き出しにはカードが必要です。` The complete families independently support **1601660 sense 3** and **1601650 sense 2**, the latter applicable to the actual 引き出し spelling. 引き出す excludes only 下ろす, 降ろす and 出す; 引き出し declares no exclusion. Neither endpoint therefore protects this pair.

**Minimal remedy:** Add `引き出し` to 引き出す’s exclusions, or declare the reciprocal pair on 引き出し. One declaration suffices symmetrically. Preserve the existing three additions and N4 level. Existing protection involving 取り出す or 抜く cannot supply this missing pair transitively.

This is an **existing unprotected overlap remaining in the reviewed scope**, not a newly authored error in the retained banking examples.

**Confidence:** High.
